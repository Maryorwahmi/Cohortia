Risk Acceptance: Conscious Decision Making

Acknowledging risk and deciding to take no action

What is Risk Acceptance?

Risk acceptance involves acknowledging a risk and making a deliberate decision to take no action to reduce its likelihood or impact. It is a conscious, documented choice.

  • No action taken to reduce risk.
  • Requires explicit management approval.
  • Based on understanding residual risk.
Example Scenario

An organization accepts the risk of a brief outage for a non-critical system, as the cost of implementing high availability is prohibitive and the business impact is minimal.

When to Choose Acceptance

  • Cost of mitigation/transfer outweighs potential impact.
  • Likelihood of risk occurring is extremely low.
  • Impact of the risk is manageable and within risk appetite.
  • When no other viable response strategy exists.
Key Takeaway

Risk acceptance is a deliberate, documented decision, not a passive stance, chosen when the cost of action outweighs the potential impact or when risk is inherently low.
