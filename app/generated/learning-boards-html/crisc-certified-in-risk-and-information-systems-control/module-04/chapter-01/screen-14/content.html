Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Risk Avoidance

Eliminating a risk by discontinuing the activity that generates it.

Risk Acceptance

Consciously deciding to take no action to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk.

Risk Mitigation

Implementing controls to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk to an acceptable level.

Summary Takeaway

These three strategies—Avoidance, Acceptance, and Mitigation—provide fundamental approaches to managing IT risks, each with distinct implications for an organization's operations and security posture.
