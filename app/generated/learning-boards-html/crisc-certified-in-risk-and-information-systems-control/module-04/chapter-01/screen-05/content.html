Risk Avoidance: Eliminating the Source

The most direct way to deal with a risk

What is Risk Avoidance?

Risk avoidance involves completely eliminating a risk by discontinuing the activity or process that gives rise to it. It's about removing the source of the risk.

  • Cease the risky activity.
  • Removes the risk entirely.
  • Often the most straightforward solution.
Example Scenario

A company identifies a legacy application with critical, unpatchable vulnerabilities. To avoid the risk, they decommission the application and migrate its functionality to a new, secure platform.

When to Consider Avoidance

  • When the risk impact is unacceptable and cannot be mitigated.
  • If the cost of mitigation or transfer is too high.
  • When the activity is not core to business operations.
  • If alternative, less risky solutions are readily available.
Key Takeaway

Risk avoidance is a powerful strategy for eliminating severe risks, but it requires careful consideration of potential business impacts and alternative solutions.
