Risk Transfer: Due Diligence and Accountability

Understanding the limits and responsibilities when transferring risk

Ultimate Accountability

While risk transfer shifts the burden, the ultimate accountability for the risk often remains with the originating organization. It's not a complete abdication of responsibility.

  • Risk is reallocated, not eliminated.
  • Organization remains responsible for oversight.
  • Requires ongoing management of third-party relationships.
Example Scenario

A company outsources data storage to a cloud provider. While the provider manages infrastructure security, the company is still accountable for data privacy and compliance with regulations.

Common Pitfall: Lack of Due Diligence

A critical mistake is failing to conduct thorough due diligence on the third party or neglecting to monitor their performance. This can turn transferred risk into unmanaged risk.

  • Insufficient vetting of third-party capabilities.
  • Poorly defined contractual terms and SLAs.
  • Lack of ongoing monitoring and audits.
Key Takeaway

Always perform thorough due diligence and maintain oversight when transferring risk, as ultimate accountability often remains with your organization.
