Introduction to Risk Response Strategies

Choosing the right path after risk assessment

The Risk Response Phase

After identifying and assessing IT risks, organizations must decide how to address them. This phase is crucial for aligning risk management with business objectives and available resources.

  • Strategic decisions based on risk profile.
  • Aims to manage risk to an acceptable level.
  • Not about eliminating all risks, but optimizing response.
Key Takeaway

Effective risk response involves selecting the most appropriate strategy or combination of strategies to align with organizational goals and risk appetite.

Real-world Connection

Imagine a company deciding whether to launch a new product with unproven security features. The risk response strategy chosen will directly impact its market entry and long-term viability.
