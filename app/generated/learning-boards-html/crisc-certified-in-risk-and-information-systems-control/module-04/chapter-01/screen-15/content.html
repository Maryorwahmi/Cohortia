Key Concepts

Advanced terms for strategic risk management

Risk Transfer

Shifting the financial burden or responsibility of a risk to a third party.

Cost-Benefit Analysis

Evaluating the financial implications of a risk response strategy by comparing its cost against potential benefits.

Residual Risk

The risk that remains after risk responses have been implemented.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding Risk Transfer, Cost-Benefit Analysis, and Residual Risk is vital for making informed, strategic decisions that balance security needs with financial prudence and operational realities.
