Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from This Chapter

Preventive Controls

Safeguards designed to stop a risk event from occurring (e.g., firewalls, MFA, encryption).

Detective Controls

Safeguards designed to identify and alert when a risk event has occurred or is in progress (e.g., IDS, SIEM).

Corrective Controls

Safeguards designed to minimize the impact of an incident and restore systems to normal (e.g., backups, IR plans).

Chapter Summary

Understanding the distinct roles of preventive, detective, and corrective controls is fundamental to building a comprehensive and layered security defense strategy.
