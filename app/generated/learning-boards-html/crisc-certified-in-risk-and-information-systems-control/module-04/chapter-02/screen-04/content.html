Core Concept

What are Controls?

Controls are safeguards or countermeasures designed to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk event. They are critical for building a resilient and secure information environment.

Key Characteristics
  • Reduce risk likelihood or impact.
  • Can be technical, administrative, or physical.
  • Essential for a strong security posture.
Key Takeaway

Controls are the practical implementation of risk mitigation strategies, acting as vital safeguards against identified threats.

Understanding Risk Controls

Safeguards for a Resilient Information Environment

Technical Controls

Implemented through hardware or software (e.g., firewalls, encryption).

Administrative Controls

Policies, procedures, and guidelines (e.g., security policies, training).

Physical Controls

Tangible barriers and safeguards (e.g., locked doors, security cameras).

Example Scenario

Imagine protecting a data center. A locked door (physical) restricts entry, a security policy (administrative) dictates who has access, and multi-factor authentication (technical) secures login to servers.
