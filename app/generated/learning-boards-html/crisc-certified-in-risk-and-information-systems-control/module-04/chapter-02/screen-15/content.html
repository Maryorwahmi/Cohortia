Key Concepts

Expanding Your Understanding

Control Frameworks

Structured guidelines and catalogs of controls (e.g., NIST SP 800-53, ISO 27002, COBIT).

Control Selection Criteria

Factors considered when choosing controls: effectiveness, cost-effectiveness, feasibility, and compliance.

Implementation Challenges

Common obstacles like lack of resources, resistance to change, and legacy systems, and strategies to overcome them.

Chapter Summary

Mastering control frameworks, understanding selection criteria, and addressing implementation challenges are vital for effective risk mitigation and a resilient security posture.
