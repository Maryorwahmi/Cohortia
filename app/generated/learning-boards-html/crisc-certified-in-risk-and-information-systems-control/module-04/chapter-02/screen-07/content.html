Control Type

Corrective Controls

These controls minimize the impact of a security incident and restore systems to their normal state after an event has occurred.

Key Examples
  • Data Backup & Recovery
  • Incident Response Plans
  • System Patching & Remediation
Common Mistake

Failing to regularly test corrective controls, leading to surprises and inefficiencies during an actual incident.

Incident Response: Corrective Controls

Minimizing Impact and Restoring Operations

How They Work

Backups restore data after loss.

IR plans guide incident handling.

DR plans ensure business continuity.

Patching fixes vulnerabilities.

Scenario Example

After a critical server crash, the IT team activates the disaster recovery plan, restoring services from a secondary data center within the defined recovery time objective (RTO).

Key Takeaway

Corrective controls are vital for post-incident recovery, minimizing damage, and ensuring business continuity.
