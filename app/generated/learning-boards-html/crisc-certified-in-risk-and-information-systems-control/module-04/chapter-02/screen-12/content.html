Navigating Implementation Challenges

Common Obstacles in Deploying Security Controls

Key Challenges

Obstacles to Effective Control Deployment

  • • Lack of Resources: Insufficient budget, skilled personnel, or time.
  • • Resistance to Change: Employees resisting new security procedures.
  • • Legacy Systems: Older systems not supporting modern controls.
  • • Complexity: Overly complex controls prone to misconfiguration.
  • • Lack of Management Support: Without executive buy-in, efforts can falter.
Key Takeaway

Anticipating and understanding common implementation challenges is crucial for developing effective mitigation strategies.

Impact of Challenges

Why These Matter

Resource Constraints: Can lead to incomplete or poorly implemented controls, leaving gaps in security.

User Resistance: May result in workarounds or non-compliance, undermining control effectiveness.

Technical Debt: Legacy systems create vulnerabilities and hinder the adoption of modern security solutions.

Operational Burden: Complex controls increase management overhead and risk of human error.

Scenario Example

A company tries to implement a new data loss prevention (DLP) system, but employees find it too restrictive and time-consuming, leading to widespread resistance and attempts to bypass the system.
