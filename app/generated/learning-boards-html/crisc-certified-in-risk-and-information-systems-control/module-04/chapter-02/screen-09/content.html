Core Concept

What are Control Frameworks?

Control frameworks are structured guidelines and comprehensive catalogs of controls that help organizations systematically select, implement, and manage security measures.

Key Benefits
  • Structured approach to security.
  • Ensures comprehensive control coverage.
  • Aids in demonstrating compliance.
Key Takeaway

Control frameworks provide a vital blueprint for building and maintaining a robust and compliant security posture.

Guiding Principles: Control Frameworks

Structured Approaches to Risk Mitigation

Why Use a Framework?

Provides a structured, systematic approach.

Offers a comprehensive catalog of controls.

Helps ensure systematic implementation.

Facilitates compliance and governance.

Real-world Connection

A startup uses a control framework to build its security program from scratch, ensuring all critical areas like access control, incident response, and data protection are covered systematically.
