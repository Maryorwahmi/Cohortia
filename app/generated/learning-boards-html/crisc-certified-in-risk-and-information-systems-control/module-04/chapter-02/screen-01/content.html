Implementing Risk Mitigation Controls

Safeguarding Your Information Environment with Effective Controls

This chapter will guide you through the process of selecting, implementing, and managing various types of controls to build a resilient and secure information environment. You'll learn to differentiate control types, choose appropriate safeguards, and navigate common implementation challenges.
