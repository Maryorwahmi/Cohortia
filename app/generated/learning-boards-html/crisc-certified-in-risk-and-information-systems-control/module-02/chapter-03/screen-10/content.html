Leadership Imperative

Leadership's Indispensable Role

Championing and Embedding Risk Management Principles

Key Leadership Actions

  • •Visible Support: Consistent backing from senior management and the board.
  • •Resource Allocation: Providing adequate budget, personnel, and technology.
  • •Risk-Informed Decisions: Demonstrating risk consideration in their own choices.
  • •Accountability: Holding self and others responsible for risk performance.
Key Takeaway

Leadership's active championship, resource allocation, and accountability are crucial for embedding risk management as a strategic imperative.

Impact of Leadership

Positive Impact

Fosters trust, accountability, and proactive risk culture.

Negative Impact

Undermines efforts, creates fear, leads to ignored warnings.

Role of CISO/CIO

These leaders bridge technical risks with business implications, advocating for necessary controls and investments to protect the organization.
