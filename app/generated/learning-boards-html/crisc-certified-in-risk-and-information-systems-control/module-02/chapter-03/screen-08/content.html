Effective Communication Channels

Ensuring Risk Information Reaches the Right People, Right Time

Key Communication Directions

  • •Upward Reporting: To senior management and the board for strategic decisions.
  • •Downward Communication: Dissemination of policies and risk appetite to employees.
  • •Horizontal Sharing: Between departments to foster collaboration and intelligence sharing.
  • •Tailored Information: Content adapted to the specific needs and context of the audience.
Key Takeaway

Robust risk communication involves multi-directional flow (up, down, across) and tailored reporting to ensure all stakeholders are informed and aligned.
