Quantitative Risk Assessment: Numerical Analysis

Calculating Expected Monetary Loss

What is Quantitative Assessment?

Assigns numerical values to likelihood and impact, allowing for calculations of expected monetary loss (e.g., ALE).

  • Requires more data and sophisticated analysis.
  • Provides a precise basis for decision-making.
  • Enables cost-benefit analysis of controls.
Key Takeaway

Quantitative risk assessment offers precise, data-driven insights into financial risk, enabling optimized decision-making for risk treatment and control investments.

Example & Benefits

Example: System outage probability = 0.01/year, cost = $1,000,000. ALE = $10,000.

Benefits: More precise, supports cost-benefit analysis, better resource allocation, objective decision-making.

Common Pitfall

Relying solely on qualitative assessments for critical risks where quantitative data is available can lead to less informed decisions and suboptimal risk treatment.
