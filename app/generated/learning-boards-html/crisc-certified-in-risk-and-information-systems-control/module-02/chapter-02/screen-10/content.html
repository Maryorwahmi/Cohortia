Involving Key Stakeholders in Risk Strategy

Diverse Perspectives for Comprehensive Plans

Key Stakeholders & Roles

  • Board of Directors: Strategic direction, oversight, shareholder alignment.
  • Executive Management (CEO, CFO, CIO, CISO): Operational strategy, resource allocation.
  • Business Unit Leaders: Operational risk insights, impact assessment.
  • Legal & Compliance: Regulatory adherence, legal implications.
  • Internal Audit: Independent assessment of strategy effectiveness.
  • External Partners/Regulators: Broader ecosystem insights, compliance.

Effective Engagement Methods

  • Steering Committee Meetings: Regular forums for strategic discussions.
  • Workshops: Collaborative sessions to build shared understanding and consensus.
  • Surveys: Efficiently gather broad input from a large group.
  • One-on-One Interviews: Obtain detailed perspectives and address specific concerns.
  • Communication Plans: Ensure consistent messaging and updates to all stakeholders.
Example Scenario

When defining data loss tolerance, input from the CISO (technical), legal counsel (regulatory), and business unit leaders (operational impact) is essential for a holistic view.

Key Takeaway

Broad stakeholder engagement ensures the risk management strategy is comprehensive, relevant, and has the necessary organizational buy-in for successful implementation.
