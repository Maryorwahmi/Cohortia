Key Concepts: Foundational Definitions

Essential Terms for Risk Management Strategy

Risk Appetite

The broad amount of risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives.

Risk Tolerance

The acceptable deviation from the risk appetite, often expressed in specific, measurable terms.

Risk Capacity

The maximum amount of risk an organization can bear without threatening its existence.

Summary Takeaway

Distinguishing between risk appetite, tolerance, and capacity is fundamental for establishing clear boundaries and making informed strategic decisions about risk-taking.
