Strategic Process

Crafting Your Risk Appetite Statement

A Guide for All Risk-Related Decisions

Key Steps in the Process

  1. Understand Objectives: Begin with the organization's strategic goals, operating environment, and financial health.
  2. Engage Leadership: Senior leadership and the board define the strategic risk stance.
  3. Conduct Workshops/Interviews: Gather executive perspectives on acceptable risk levels across categories.
  4. Draft Statement: Create a clear, concise, and actionable statement that guides decisions.
  5. Review and Approve: Ensure alignment and formal endorsement by the board.
Key Takeaway

A well-defined risk appetite statement, developed collaboratively, provides essential guidance for all risk-related decisions and ensures strategic alignment.

Example Statement Structure

"Our organization has a low appetite for risks leading to data breaches impacting customer privacy."

"We have a moderate appetite for market volatility risks in new product development."

"We maintain a high appetite for technological innovation risks that could provide significant competitive advantage."

Safety Note

An ill-defined or overly aggressive risk appetite can expose the organization to unacceptable risks, while an overly conservative one can stifle innovation and growth.
