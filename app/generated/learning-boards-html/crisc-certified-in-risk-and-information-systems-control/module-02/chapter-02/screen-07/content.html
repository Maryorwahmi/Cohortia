The Absolute Limit

Risk Capacity: The Ultimate Boundary

Maximum Risk an Organization Can Bear

What is Risk Capacity?

It's the maximum amount of risk an organization can bear without violating laws, regulations, or solvency requirements.

  • Absolute upper limit.
  • Exceeding it leads to catastrophic consequences.
  • A non-negotiable survival threshold.
Key Takeaway

Risk capacity defines the ultimate boundary of acceptable risk, ensuring the organization's fundamental viability is never threatened.

Consequences of Exceeding

Financial: Insolvency, bankruptcy, significant monetary losses.

Legal/Regulatory: Heavy fines, sanctions, loss of operating licenses.

Reputational: Irreparable damage to brand, loss of customer trust.

Critical Relationship

Risk appetite and tolerance must always be set well within the organization's risk capacity to ensure long-term viability and avoid catastrophic outcomes.
