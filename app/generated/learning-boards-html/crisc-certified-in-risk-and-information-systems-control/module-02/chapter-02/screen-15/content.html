Key Concepts: Strategic Implementation

Methods and Approaches for Risk Management

Stakeholder Engagement

Involving individuals or groups interested in the risk management strategy.

Qualitative Risk Assessment

Uses descriptive terms (e.g., High, Medium, Low) for likelihood and impact.

Quantitative Risk Assessment

Assigns numerical values to likelihood and impact for precise calculations.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management relies on collaborative stakeholder engagement and the judicious application of both qualitative and quantitative assessment methods to inform strategic decisions.
