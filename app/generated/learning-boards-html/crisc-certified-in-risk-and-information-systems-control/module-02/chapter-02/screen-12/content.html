Qualitative Risk Assessment: Descriptive Evaluation

Understanding Likelihood and Impact with Categories

What is Qualitative Assessment?

Involves using descriptive terms to evaluate the likelihood and impact of risks.

  • Uses categories like High/Medium/Low.
  • Ideal for initial screening or when data is scarce.
  • Subjective but quick and cost-effective.
Key Takeaway

Qualitative risk assessment provides a quick, cost-effective way to prioritize risks using descriptive categories, especially useful in early stages or with limited data.

Example & Benefits

Example: Rating a cyberattack likelihood as 'High' and its impact as 'Severe' to prioritize it for immediate attention.

Benefits: Quick to implement, cost-effective, helps prioritize risks, useful for non-quantifiable risks (e.g., reputational damage).

Limitation

Its subjectivity can lead to inconsistencies, and it may not provide sufficient detail for critical risks where precise data is available.
