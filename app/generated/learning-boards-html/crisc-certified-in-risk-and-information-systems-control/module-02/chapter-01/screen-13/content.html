Framework Comparison

COSO ERM, ISO 31000, and NIST RMF

Choosing the Right Guidance for Your Organization

FeatureCOSO ERMISO 31000NIST RMF
ScopeEnterprise-wide risk management, integrating with strategy and performance.Generic guidelines for all types of risk management, adaptable to any organization.Managing risk to information systems and organizations, strong cybersecurity focus.
FocusValue creation and preservation, holistic view of all risk types.Principles and framework for effective risk management, less prescriptive.Structured process for security and privacy risk management in IT environments.
PrescriptivenessMore prescriptive, outlines specific components and principles.Less prescriptive, provides flexible guidelines.Highly prescriptive, detailed steps for implementation.
Target AudienceBoards, senior management, enterprise risk professionals.Any organization or individual involved in managing risks.Federal agencies, organizations with significant IT/cybersecurity risks.
Key Takeaway

These frameworks offer distinct strengths and can be used individually or in combination to build a comprehensive risk governance program tailored to an organization's specific needs.
