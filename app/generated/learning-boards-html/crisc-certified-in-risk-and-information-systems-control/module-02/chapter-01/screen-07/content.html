Core Principle

Integration of Risk Considerations

Embedding Risk into All Levels of Decision-Making

What is Integration?

Integration means that risk considerations are not treated as a separate function but are woven into the fabric of all organizational processes, from strategic planning to daily operations.

Example

When developing a new product, potential market, operational, and cybersecurity risks are assessed from the initial design phase.

Why it Matters

Ensures risk is proactively managed, prevents siloed efforts, and leads to more informed and resilient decisions.

Achieving Effective Integration

  • •Strategic Alignment: Risk management supports and informs strategic objectives.
  • •Process Embedding: Risk assessments are built into existing business processes.
  • •Cultural Shift: Fostering a mindset where everyone considers risk in their daily tasks.
  • •Information Flow: Ensuring risk data flows seamlessly across departments.
Common Pitfall

Treating risk management as a separate, isolated function, leading to a disconnect from core business activities.

Key Takeaway

Integrated risk governance ensures that risk is a fundamental consideration in all organizational decisions, enhancing overall resilience.
