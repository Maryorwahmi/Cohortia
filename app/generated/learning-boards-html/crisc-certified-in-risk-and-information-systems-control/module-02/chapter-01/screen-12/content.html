NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)

Structured Process for Information System Risk

NIST RMF Overview

The NIST RMF provides a structured, flexible, and repeatable process for managing risk to information systems and organizations. It is particularly relevant for cybersecurity and IT risk management.

Key Focus

Specific, actionable steps for IT and cybersecurity risk, making it highly valuable for CRISC professionals.

Benefit

Ensures systematic management of security and privacy risks for information systems.

Seven Steps of NIST RMF

  1. •Prepare: Establish context and priorities.
  2. •Categorize: Classify information systems based on impact.
  3. •Select: Choose appropriate security controls.
  4. •Implement: Put controls into practice.
  5. •Assess: Evaluate control effectiveness.
  6. •Authorize: Make a risk-based decision to operate the system.
  7. •Monitor: Continuously track control effectiveness and changes.
Misconception

NIST RMF is not exclusive to government agencies; it's widely adopted by private sector organizations for robust cybersecurity.

Key Takeaway

NIST RMF provides a detailed, seven-step process for managing information system risks, making it indispensable for cybersecurity professionals.
