Essential Definitions

Core Ideas to Remember (Part 1)

Risk Governance

The overarching framework of policies, structures, and processes guiding an organization's approach to managing risks, aligned with strategic objectives.

Risk Management

The systematic application of policies and procedures to identify, assess, treat, monitor, and review specific risks.

Accountability

The obligation of an individual or organization to account for its activities, accept responsibility, and disclose results transparently.

Summary Takeaway

Risk governance provides the strategic blueprint, risk management is the operational execution, and accountability ensures ownership of risk-related outcomes.
