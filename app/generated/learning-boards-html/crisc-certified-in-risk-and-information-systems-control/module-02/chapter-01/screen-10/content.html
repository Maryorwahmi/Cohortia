COSO Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)

Integrating with Strategy and Performance

COSO ERM Overview

The COSO ERM framework provides a comprehensive approach to enterprise risk management, focusing on how ERM can enhance value creation and preservation by integrating with an organization's strategy and performance.

Key Focus

Beyond compliance, it emphasizes strategic alignment and value enhancement through effective risk management.

Benefit

Helps organizations make better decisions by considering risks and opportunities in a holistic manner.

Five Interrelated Components

1. Governance & Culture

2. Strategy & Objective-Setting

3. Performance

4. Review & Revision

5. Information, Communication, & Reporting

Misconception

COSO ERM is not just for large corporations; its principles are scalable and adaptable for organizations of all sizes.

Key Takeaway

COSO ERM provides a holistic framework for integrating risk management with strategy to enhance organizational value and performance.
