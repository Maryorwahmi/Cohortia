Core Concept

Risk Governance vs. Risk Management

Architecting the Approach vs. Building the Solution

Risk Governance

  • •The overarching framework, policies, and structures.
  • •Sets the strategic tone from the top.
  • •Ensures alignment with strategic objectives.
  • •Defines roles, responsibilities, and risk appetite.
Analogy

The architect designing the house: creating the blueprint and overall vision.

Risk Management

  • •The processes and activities to manage specific risks.
  • •Operational execution of risk strategies.
  • •Involves identification, assessment, treatment, monitoring.
  • •Ensures consistent application of policies.
Analogy

The builder constructing the house: executing plans and managing specific tasks.

Key Takeaway

Risk governance provides the strategic direction and framework, while risk management is the operational execution of risk-related activities.
