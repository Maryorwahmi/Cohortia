Key Principles and Frameworks

Expanding Your Understanding (Part 2)

Transparency

The principle of openly communicating risk-related information, decisions, and performance to relevant stakeholders.

Integration

Embedding risk considerations into all levels of organizational decision-making and processes, rather than treating them separately.

COSO ERM

A comprehensive framework for enterprise risk management that helps organizations enhance value creation and preservation by integrating with strategy and performance.

Summary Takeaway

Transparency and integration are core principles, while COSO ERM provides a comprehensive framework for enterprise-wide risk management and value enhancement.
