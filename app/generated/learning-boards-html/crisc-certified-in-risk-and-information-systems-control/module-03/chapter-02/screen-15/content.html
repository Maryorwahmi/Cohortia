Key Concepts: Measuring and Evaluating Risk

Critical Metrics and Decision-Making Frameworks

Risk Matrix (Heat Map)

A visual tool used in qualitative analysis to plot likelihood against impact, resulting in a risk rating.

Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)

The monetary loss expected each time a specific threat materializes (AV x EF).

Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

The expected monetary loss from a risk over a one-year period (SLE x ARO).

Summary Takeaway

These metrics and evaluation processes provide the quantitative and qualitative data needed to make informed decisions about risk treatment and resource allocation.
