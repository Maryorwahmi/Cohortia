Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)

The estimated frequency with which a specific threat is expected to occur within a one-year period. It's expressed as a decimal (e.g., 0.5 for once every two years).

Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

The expected monetary loss from a specific risk over a one-year period. It combines the impact of a single event with its expected frequency.

Formula: ALE = SLE × ARO

Key Takeaway

ALE provides a critical annual financial figure for risk, enabling cost-benefit analysis for security investments.

Calculating ALE: Example

Scenario: Server Fire Risk

  • Single Loss Expectancy (SLE): $10,000 (from previous example)
  • Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO): 0.1 (fire expected once every 10 years)
ALE Calculation:$10,000 × 0.1
Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE):$1,000

Why ALE Matters

ALE allows organizations to compare the cost of a control (e.g., fire suppression system) against the potential financial loss it prevents, justifying security investments.
