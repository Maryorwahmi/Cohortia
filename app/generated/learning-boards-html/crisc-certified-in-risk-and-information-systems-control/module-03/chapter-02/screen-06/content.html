What is a Risk Matrix?

A visual tool (also called a heat map) that plots the likelihood of a risk occurring against its potential impact. It helps in assigning a qualitative risk rating (e.g., Low, Medium, High).

How it Works

  • Likelihood: Categorized (e.g., Very Low, Low, Medium, High, Very High).
  • Impact: Categorized (e.g., Negligible, Minor, Moderate, Major, Catastrophic).
  • Rating: The intersection determines the risk level (e.g., High Likelihood + Major Impact = High Risk).
Key Takeaway

Risk matrices simplify complex risk data into an easily digestible visual format, aiding rapid prioritization.
