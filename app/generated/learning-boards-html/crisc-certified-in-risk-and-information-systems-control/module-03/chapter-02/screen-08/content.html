Definition

An objective, numerical method that assigns monetary values to the likelihood and impact of risks, allowing for precise assessment and cost-benefit analysis.

When to Use It

  • For risks with significant financial implications.
  • When detailed cost-benefit analysis of controls is needed.
  • To provide objective, defensible data for decision-making.
  • When historical data or reliable benchmarks are available.
Key Takeaway

Quantitative analysis provides concrete financial figures for risks, essential for strategic budgeting and control investments.

Key Metrics in Quantitative Analysis

Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)

The monetary loss expected each time a specific threat materializes.

Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)

The estimated frequency with which a threat is expected to occur in one year.

Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

The expected monetary loss from a risk over a one-year period.
