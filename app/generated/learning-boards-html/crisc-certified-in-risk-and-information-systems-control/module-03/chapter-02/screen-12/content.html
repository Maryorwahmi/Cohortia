What is Risk Evaluation?

The process of comparing analyzed risk levels against the organization's predefined risk criteria and risk appetite to decide whether a risk is acceptable or requires treatment.

Risk Criteria

  • Terms of reference for evaluating risk significance.
  • Includes cost, legal/regulatory, reputational, operational impact.
  • Example: High legal impact for data breaches.
Key Takeaway

Risk evaluation uses criteria and appetite to determine if a risk aligns with organizational tolerance, guiding treatment decisions.

Understanding Risk Appetite

Definition

The amount and type of risk an organization is willing to take to achieve its strategic objectives. It's the 'comfort zone' for risk.

Example: Financial Institution

Very low risk appetite for data breaches due to regulatory compliance and customer trust.

Example: Tech Startup

Higher risk appetite for innovative, unproven technologies to gain market advantage.

Why it's Crucial

Without a defined risk appetite, risk evaluation can become arbitrary, leading to inconsistent decisions and inefficient resource allocation.
