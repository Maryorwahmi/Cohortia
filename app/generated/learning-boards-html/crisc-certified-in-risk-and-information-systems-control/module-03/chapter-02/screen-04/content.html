Risk Analysis

The process of understanding a risk's nature and determining its level by assessing the likelihood of occurrence and potential impact. It answers 'What could happen?' and 'How bad could it be?'.

Risk Evaluation

The subsequent step where analyzed risk levels are compared against established risk criteria and the organization's risk appetite to decide if the risk is acceptable or requires treatment. It answers 'Is this risk acceptable to us?'.

Key Takeaway

Analysis quantifies or qualifies risks; evaluation determines their strategic importance and required response.

The Risk Management Cycle

1. Identify Risks

2. Analyze Risks

3. Evaluate Risks

4. Treat Risks

5. Monitor & Review
