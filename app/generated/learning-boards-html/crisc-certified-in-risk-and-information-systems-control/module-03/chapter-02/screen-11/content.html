Data-Intensive & Complex

Accurately determining AV, EF, and ARO is challenging, requiring historical data, industry benchmarks, and expert estimations that may not always be available or precise.

Common Mistakes

  • Over-reliance on precise numbers without understanding assumptions.
  • Using inaccurate or outdated data, leading to misleading results.
  • Ignoring the inherent uncertainties in input variables.
Key Takeaway

Quantitative analysis requires careful data validation and an understanding of its limitations to avoid misleading conclusions.

Tools for Managing Uncertainty

Monte Carlo Simulations

Models various outcomes and probabilities, providing a range of possible ALEs rather than a single point estimate, accounting for uncertainties.

Sensitivity Analysis

Examines how changes in input variables (AV, EF, ARO) affect the output (ALE), identifying the most influential factors.

Scenario Analysis

Evaluates risks under different plausible future conditions, providing insights into potential impacts across various scenarios.
