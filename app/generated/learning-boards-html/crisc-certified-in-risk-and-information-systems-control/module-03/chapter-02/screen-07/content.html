The Challenge of Subjectivity

Qualitative analysis can suffer from inconsistencies due to varying individual perceptions of likelihood and impact. This can lead to unreliable risk prioritization.

Role of Expert Judgment

  • Gathers input from experienced individuals.
  • Uses methods like Delphi technique for consensus.
  • Ensures consistent understanding of definitions.
  • Mitigates individual biases for more reliable assessments.
Key Takeaway

Expert judgment is essential to enhance the reliability and consistency of qualitative risk assessments.

Techniques for Expert Consensus

Delphi Method

Anonymous expert feedback rounds to converge on a consensus without direct confrontation.

Structured Interviews

Standardized questions and definitions ensure all experts assess risks using the same framework.

Facilitated Workshops

Guided discussions among experts to openly share perspectives and reach collective agreement on risk ratings.

Common Mistake

Over-reliance on a single expert's opinion without cross-validation, leading to biased risk assessments.
