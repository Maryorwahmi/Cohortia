Key Concepts: Understanding Risk

Essential Definitions for Risk Analysis and Evaluation

Risk Analysis

The process of understanding a risk's nature and determining its level by assessing likelihood and impact.

Qualitative Risk Analysis

A subjective, non-numerical method for prioritizing risks based on perceived likelihood and impact, often using risk matrices.

Quantitative Risk Analysis

An objective, numerical method that assigns monetary values to risk components (SLE, ARO, ALE) for precise assessment.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering both qualitative and quantitative analysis methods is crucial for comprehensive and effective risk management, enabling informed decision-making.
