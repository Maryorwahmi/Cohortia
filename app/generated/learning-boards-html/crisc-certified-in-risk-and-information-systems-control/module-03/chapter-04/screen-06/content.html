Quantifying and Managing Risk: Key Metrics

Likelihood, Impact, and Risk Scores in the register.

Common Mistake

Confusing inherent risk (before controls) with residual risk (after controls). Both are important for a complete picture.

Example

A 'High' likelihood of a phishing attack combined with 'Major' impact from data loss results in a 'High' inherent risk score.

06. Likelihood

Probability of the risk occurring (e.g., Low, Medium, High).

07. Impact

Severity of consequences if the risk materializes (e.g., Minor, Moderate, Major).

08. Current Risk Score/Level

Inherent risk (before controls) and residual risk (after controls).

09. Existing Controls

Description of current measures in place to mitigate the risk.
