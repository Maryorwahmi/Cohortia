Building Your Risk Register: Key Fields

Understanding the fundamental elements for comprehensive risk tracking.

Key Takeaway

Each component of the risk register serves a specific purpose, from unique identification to assigning clear ownership, all contributing to a holistic risk view.

Pro-Tip

Ensure risk owners are clearly defined and understand their responsibilities; this is critical for driving action and accountability.

01. Risk ID

A unique identifier for easy reference and tracking.

02. Risk Description/Statement

Clear, concise statement outlining cause, event, and impact.

03. Risk Category

Classification (e.g., technical, operational, compliance).

04. Asset(s) at Risk

Specific assets that would be impacted (e.g., customer data).

05. Risk Owner

Individual or department accountable for managing the risk.
