Clear and Concise Risk Statements

Avoiding jargon and focusing on business impact.

Common Mistake

Using overly technical jargon that non-technical stakeholders cannot understand, hindering effective communication and decision-making.

Pro-Tip

Always ask: 'What is the business consequence if this risk materializes?' This helps frame the statement effectively.

Ineffective Risk Statement

"CVE-2023-1234 on Apache Struts."

Too technical, lacks business context.

Effective Risk Statement

"Due to an unpatched vulnerability in the Apache Struts framework used by the customer portal, a remote attacker could gain control of the web server, leading to a data breach of customer PII and significant reputational damage."

Clear cause, event, and business impact.
