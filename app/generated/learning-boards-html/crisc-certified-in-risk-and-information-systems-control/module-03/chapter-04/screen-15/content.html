Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms to solidify your understanding

Risk Reporting

The process of communicating risk information to various stakeholders, tailored to their specific needs.

Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)

Forward-looking metrics that provide an early signal of increasing risk exposure.

Inherent Risk

The level of risk before any controls or mitigation actions are applied.

Residual Risk

The level of risk remaining after controls have been implemented.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive monitoring with KRIs and a clear understanding of inherent vs. residual risk are crucial for effective risk posture assessment and communication.
