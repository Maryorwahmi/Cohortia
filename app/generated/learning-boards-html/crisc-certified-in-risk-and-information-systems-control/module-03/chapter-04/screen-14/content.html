Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Risk Register

A central repository for all identified risks, tracking their status and associated information throughout their lifecycle.

Risk Owner

The individual or department accountable for managing a specific risk and its associated controls.

Risk Statement

A clear, concise description of a risk, including its cause, event, and business impact.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management begins with clear documentation and defined accountability, ensuring risks are understood and owned.
