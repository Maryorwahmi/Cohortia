Actioning Risk: Mitigation and Review

Planning treatment, setting targets, and maintaining currency.

Why it Matters

Regular review dates prevent the risk register from becoming outdated, ensuring continuous relevance and effectiveness of risk management efforts.

Pro-Tip

Ensure mitigation plans are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) to maximize their effectiveness.

10. Mitigation/Treatment Plan

Proposed actions to reduce risk to an acceptable level (e.g., patching program).

11. Target Risk Score/Level

Desired risk level after mitigation (e.g., Medium, Low).

12. Status

Current state of the risk (e.g., Open, In Progress, Mitigated, Closed).

13. Review Date

Date of last review and next scheduled review, ensuring currency.
