Module 4›Chapter 3.4›Stakeholder Reporting

Audience-Specific Risk Communication

Delivering relevant risk insights to Board, Operational, and Technical teams.

Key Takeaway

Effective risk communication ensures that each stakeholder receives information in a format and language that is most useful for their decision-making.

Check Your Understanding

Why would a detailed list of CVEs be inappropriate for a Board of Directors report?

Board of Directors/Executive Management

High-level, strategic reports focusing on aggregated risk posture, alignment with risk appetite, and financial impact. Uses business language and visuals (heat maps).

Operational Management

Reports on departmental/project-specific risks, progress on mitigation plans, resource allocation, and operational impact.

Technical Teams

Highly detailed reports with specific technical vulnerabilities, remediation steps, and performance metrics of security controls.
