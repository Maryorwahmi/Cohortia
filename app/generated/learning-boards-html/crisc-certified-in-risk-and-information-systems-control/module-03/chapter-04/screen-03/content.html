Describe Role of Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)

Identify and explain how KRIs function as forward-looking metrics, providing early signals of increasing risk exposure. This competency enables proactive risk management and timely intervention before risks escalate into incidents.

Integrate Risk into Decision-Making
