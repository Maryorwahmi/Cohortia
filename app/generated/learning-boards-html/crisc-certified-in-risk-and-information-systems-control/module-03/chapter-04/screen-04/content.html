The Risk Register: A Central Repository

Documenting and tracking IT risks throughout their lifecycle.

What is a Risk Register?

A risk register is a central repository for all identified risks, serving as a living document that tracks the status of each risk throughout its lifecycle. It's an indispensable tool for maintaining an organized, current, and comprehensive view of the organization's risk landscape.

Key Benefit

Ensures consistency and prevents fragmented risk management efforts.

Why it Matters

Provides a single source of truth for all risk information, crucial for accountability and informed decision-making.
