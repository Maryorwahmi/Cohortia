Examples of Effective IT KRIs

Metrics that provide actionable insights into IT risk.

Why it Matters

Monitoring KRIs helps identify trends and potential control weaknesses, enabling proactive investigation and mitigation before risks escalate.

Pro-Tip

Define clear thresholds for each KRI. When a threshold is crossed, it should trigger a predefined action or investigation.

Unpatched Vulnerabilities

Number of critical vulnerabilities in production systems.

Patching Time

Average time to patch critical vulnerabilities.

Failed Login Attempts

Number of failed login attempts per day.

Phishing Training Failure

Percentage of employees failing phishing awareness training.

Privileged Access Requests

Increase in privileged user access requests.
