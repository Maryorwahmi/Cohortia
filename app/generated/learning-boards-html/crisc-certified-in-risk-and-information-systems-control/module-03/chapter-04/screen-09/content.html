Risk Reporting: Communicating Insights

Tailoring risk information for various organizational stakeholders.

Key Takeaway

Effective risk reporting is not about presenting all data, but about delivering relevant, actionable insights tailored to each stakeholder's role and understanding.

Example Scenario

An executive summary for the board might use a heat map, while a technical team receives a detailed vulnerability report with remediation steps.

The Risk Reporting Cycle
1. Identify Stakeholders
2. Determine Information Needs
3. Tailor Report Content & Format
4. Communicate & Review
