Key Concepts to Remember

Assessment Techniques and Methodologies

Threat Modeling

A structured approach to identifying potential threats, vulnerabilities, and attack vectors against a system.

Threat Intelligence

Actionable knowledge about existing or emerging threats, used for proactive defense and informed decision-making.

Vulnerability Scanning

Automated process to identify known vulnerabilities in systems, networks, and applications.

Chapter Summary

This chapter covered essential methodologies like Threat Modeling (including STRIDE) and the critical role of Threat Intelligence. We also differentiated between automated Vulnerability Scanning and in-depth Penetration Testing, alongside the importance of Security Audits, Code Reviews, and Configuration Reviews for a holistic security posture.
