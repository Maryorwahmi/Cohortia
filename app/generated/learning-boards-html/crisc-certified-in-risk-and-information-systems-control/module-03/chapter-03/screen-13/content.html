Key Concepts to Remember

Foundational Terms in Risk Assessment

Threat

A potential cause of an unwanted incident that may result in harm to a system or organization.

Vulnerability

A weakness in an asset or control that could be exploited by one or more threats.

Exploit

A piece of software or data that takes advantage of a vulnerability to cause unintended behavior.

Chapter Summary

This chapter laid the groundwork for understanding the critical components of IT risk: the adversaries (threats), the weaknesses they target (vulnerabilities), and the methods they use (exploits). Mastering these distinctions is essential for effective risk identification and mitigation strategies.
