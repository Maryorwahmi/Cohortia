Understanding Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Exploits

The Foundation of Proactive Security

Threat

Any potential cause of an unwanted incident that may result in harm to a system or organization.

  • Malicious actors (hackers, insiders)
  • Environmental events (flood, fire)
  • System failures (hardware malfunction)

Vulnerability

A weakness in an asset or control that could be exploited by one or more threats.

  • Software flaws, misconfigurations
  • Weak processes, lack of training

Exploit

Software, data, or commands that take advantage of a vulnerability to cause unintended behavior.

  • Malware, specific attack scripts
  • Social engineering techniques

Threat (Adversary)

Vulnerability (Weakness)

Exploit (Action)

Key Takeaway

Effective risk management starts with a clear understanding of threats, vulnerabilities, and the exploits that link them.
