What is Threat Modeling?

Systematically Identifying Attack Vectors

Threat Modeling
What are we trying to protect?
Who are we protecting it from?
What are the most likely attacks?
What are the impacts if an attack succeeds?

Purpose

Identify potential threats and vulnerabilities early in the system lifecycle.

Benefits

Reduces security flaws, improves design, and prioritizes mitigation efforts.

Key Takeaway

Threat modeling is a proactive security practice that helps anticipate and prevent attacks by systematically analyzing system weaknesses.
