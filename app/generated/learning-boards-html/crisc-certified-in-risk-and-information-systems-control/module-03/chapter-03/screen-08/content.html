Leveraging Threat Intelligence

Evidence-Based Knowledge for Evolving Threats

Threat Intelligence
OSINT: Publicly available info (news, blogs, social media).
Commercial Feeds: Subscription services with real-time data (malware, IOCs).
ISACs/ISAOs: Industry-specific sharing organizations for collective defense.
Government Agencies: Alerts and advisories on critical vulnerabilities (e.g., CISA).

Importance

Enables proactive defense, strengthens security posture, and prioritizes remediation.

Benefit

Helps organizations anticipate attacks and make informed decisions about security investments.

Key Takeaway

Integrating threat intelligence from diverse sources is crucial for staying informed and building a proactive, adaptive security defense.
