Methodology

Top-Down Risk Identification

Aligning Risk with Strategic Objectives

How it Works

  • •Starts with Strategy: Begins with organizational strategic objectives.
  • •Senior Management Focus: Involves business leaders defining critical processes and assets.
  • •Impact-Driven: Identifies risks that could impede strategic goal achievement.
Key Takeaway

The top-down approach ensures risk identification is strategically relevant, focusing on high-level impacts to business objectives.

Example Scenario

A company aims to launch a new e-commerce platform. A top-down approach identifies risks like data breaches or system downtime that could directly prevent successful market entry and revenue generation.
