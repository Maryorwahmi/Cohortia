Structured Techniques

Leveraging Standards and Scenario Planning: Checklists & Workshops

Checklists

  • •Industry Standards: Based on ISO 27001, NIST CSF, etc.
  • •Regulatory Compliance: Ensures adherence to GDPR, HIPAA, etc.
  • •Comprehensive Coverage: Helps cover common and known risks.
Key Takeaway

Structured techniques provide a systematic baseline for risk identification, but require dynamic methods to address unique organizational contexts.

Workshops

Interactive Scenario Planning

Engage participants in 'what-if' exercises to explore potential risk events and their consequences.

Creative Risk Discovery

Encourages out-of-the-box thinking to identify novel threats and vulnerabilities.

Pro-Tip

Combine checklists with workshops to ensure both foundational coverage and discovery of unique, context-specific risks.
