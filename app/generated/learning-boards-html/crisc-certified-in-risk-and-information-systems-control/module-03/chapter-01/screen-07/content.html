Collaborative Techniques

Leveraging Collective Knowledge: Brainstorming & Interviews

Brainstorming Sessions

  • •Diverse Stakeholders: Brings together IT, legal, operations, finance.
  • •Structured Prompts: Uses questions like 'What could go wrong if...?'
  • •Collective Insight: Uncovers risks from varied perspectives.
Key Takeaway

Collaborative techniques like brainstorming and interviews are essential for gathering broad and deep insights into potential risks across an organization.

Questionnaires & Interviews

Targeted Insights

Uncover risks specific to individual roles and responsibilities, providing granular operational details.

Key Personnel Focus

Gain insights from subject matter experts on their daily challenges and potential failure points.

Common Pitfall

Allowing brainstorming sessions to lack structure or be dominated by a few voices can lead to an incomplete or biased view of risks.
