Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Risk Identification

The process of finding, recognizing, and describing potential risks that could affect an organization's objectives.

Top-Down Approach

A risk identification methodology starting from strategic objectives and working downwards to identify related risks.

Bottom-Up Approach

A risk identification methodology starting from operational assets, systems, or processes to identify specific risks.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk identification combines strategic oversight with granular operational detail to uncover all potential threats.
