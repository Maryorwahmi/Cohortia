Proactive & Reactive Techniques

Learning from the Past and Anticipating the Future

Analysis of Past Incidents

  • •Historical Data: Reviews incident logs, security reports, near-misses.
  • •Reveals Weaknesses: Highlights vulnerabilities that have materialized.
  • •Concrete Evidence: Provides real-world examples of threats.
Key Takeaway

A balanced approach combining historical incident analysis with proactive threat intelligence provides the most robust risk identification.

Threat Intelligence & Data Flow Analysis

Threat Intelligence Feeds

Offers insights into emerging threats, attack vectors, and actor capabilities for proactive risk identification.

Data Flow Analysis

Maps information movement to identify points vulnerable to interception, alteration, or destruction.

Example Scenario

A review of past security incidents reveals repeated attempts to exploit a specific web application vulnerability. Threat intelligence then warns of a new, more sophisticated exploit targeting that same vulnerability, prompting immediate action.
