Avoiding Common Pitfalls

Navigating Challenges in the Risk Identification Process

Common Mistakes

  • •Incomplete Scope: Focusing only on technical risks, neglecting operational, compliance, or strategic.
  • •Single Source Reliance: Relying on limited individuals or information, leading to bias.
  • •Lack of Expertise: Team members unable to recognize certain risks or their impact.
Key Takeaway

A broad, inclusive, and expert-driven approach is essential to avoid common pitfalls and ensure comprehensive risk identification.

Impact of Pitfalls

Overlooked Critical Risks

Failure to identify significant threats that could severely impact business objectives.

Ineffective Risk Response

Inability to develop appropriate mitigation strategies for unidentified or misunderstood risks.

Pro-Tip

Always involve subject matter experts from various domains (IT, legal, HR, operations) to ensure a holistic view and prevent blind spots.
