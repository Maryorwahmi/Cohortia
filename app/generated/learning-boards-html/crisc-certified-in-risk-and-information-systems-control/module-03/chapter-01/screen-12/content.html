Crafting Effective Risk Statements

Clearly Articulating Identified IT Risks

Components of a Risk Statement

  • •Asset: The item of value being protected (e.g., customer data).
  • •Threat: A potential cause of an unwanted incident (e.g., remote attacker).
  • •Vulnerability: A weakness that can be exploited by a threat (e.g., unpatched software).
  • •Impact: The consequence if the risk materializes (e.g., unauthorized access).
Key Takeaway

Clear, structured risk statements are essential for effective communication and actionable risk management.

Risk Statement Formula & Example

Formula

"Due to (vulnerability), a (threat) could exploit (asset), resulting in (impact)."

Concrete Example

"Due to unpatched software on the customer-facing web server, a remote attacker could exploit a known vulnerability, leading to unauthorized access to customer data and a potential data breach."

Common Pitfall

Vague or incomplete risk statements hinder accurate risk assessment and the development of effective mitigation controls.
