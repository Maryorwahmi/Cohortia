The Core: Asset-Based Risk Identification

Building Risk Identification on a Solid Asset Inventory

What is an Asset Inventory?

  • •Comprehensive List: All IT assets, including hardware, software, data, IP, personnel, facilities.
  • •Criticality Assessment: Understanding each asset's importance to business operations.
  • •Continuous Update: Must be regularly maintained and updated.
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive and current asset inventory is the indispensable starting point for any meaningful risk identification process.

Types of Assets & Their Risks

Hardware & Software
  • •Servers, workstations
  • •Applications, OS
  • •Risks: Downtime, vulnerabilities
Data & IP
  • •Customer data, trade secrets
  • •Source code, patents
  • •Risks: Breach, theft, loss
Personnel & Facilities
  • •Employees, contractors
  • •Data centers, offices
  • •Risks: Human error, physical damage
Network & Cloud
  • •Routers, firewalls
  • •Cloud instances, SaaS
  • •Risks: Misconfiguration, unauthorized access

Example Scenario

An organization identifies its customer relationship management (CRM) database as a critical asset. This immediately highlights risks related to data confidentiality, integrity, and availability, prompting a deeper dive into its security controls.
