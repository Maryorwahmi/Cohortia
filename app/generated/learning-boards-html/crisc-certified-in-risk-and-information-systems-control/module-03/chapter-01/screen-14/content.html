Key Concepts

Critical Elements for Risk Identification

Asset Inventory

A comprehensive list of all IT assets (hardware, software, data, personnel, facilities) crucial for effective risk identification.

Threat Intelligence

Information about current and emerging threats, vulnerabilities, and attack methods used to inform risk identification.

Risk Statement

A clear, concise description of an identified risk, typically including the asset, threat, vulnerability, and potential impact.

Summary Takeaway

A robust asset inventory, informed by threat intelligence, and documented with clear risk statements, forms the backbone of proactive risk management.
