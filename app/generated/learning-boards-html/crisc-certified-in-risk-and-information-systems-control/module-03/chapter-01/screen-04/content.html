The Foundation of Risk Management

Understanding the Importance of Risk Identification

What is Risk Identification?

It's the systematic process of discovering, recognizing, and describing potential risks that could affect an organization's objectives.

  • •Ongoing Process: Not a one-time event, but continuous and adaptive.
  • •Proactive: Identifies risks before they materialize.
  • •Comprehensive: Requires understanding assets, operations, goals, and environment.
Key Takeaway

Risk identification is the essential first step in managing IT risks, ensuring an organization can proactively address potential threats to its objectives.

Why It Matters: The Risk Cycle

Identify Risks
Assess Risks
Respond to Risks
Monitor Risks

Real-World Connection

Consider a financial institution launching a new mobile banking app. Without identifying risks like data breaches, unauthorized access, or system downtime, they risk severe financial and reputational damage. Proactive identification allows them to build security from the ground up.
