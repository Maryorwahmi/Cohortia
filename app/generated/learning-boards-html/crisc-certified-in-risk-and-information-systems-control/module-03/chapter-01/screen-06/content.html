Bottom-Up Risk Identification

Uncovering Risks from the Operational Level

How it Works

  • •Starts with Operations: Focuses on specific assets, systems, and processes.
  • •Technical Team Driven: Involves examining individual components for vulnerabilities.
  • •Granular Detail: Uncovers specific technical risks like unpatched software.
Key Takeaway

The bottom-up approach provides crucial granular detail on operational risks, best combined with a top-down view for comprehensive coverage.

Top-Down vs. Bottom-Up

Top-Down
  • •Strategic Objectives
  • •Business Leaders
  • •High-level Impact
Bottom-Up
  • •Operational Assets
  • •Technical Teams
  • •Granular Vulnerabilities
Most Effective: Combine Both Approaches

Example Scenario

A bottom-up assessment might reveal an unpatched web server running an outdated operating system, posing a direct vulnerability to the organization's online services.
