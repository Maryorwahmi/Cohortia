Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Understanding Risk Assessment

A quick recap of the foundational ideas in risk assessment methodologies.

Risk Assessment

The process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating risks to an organization's assets to inform decision-making.

Qualitative Risk Assessment

A subjective, descriptive approach using categories like 'High,' 'Medium,' 'Low' for likelihood and impact.

Quantitative Risk Assessment

An objective, numerical approach assigning monetary values to assets, losses, and probabilities.

Summary Insight

Choosing between qualitative and quantitative methods depends on data availability and the required precision for risk prioritization and treatment decisions.
