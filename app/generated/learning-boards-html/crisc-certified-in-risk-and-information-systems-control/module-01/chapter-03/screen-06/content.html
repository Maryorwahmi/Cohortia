The Risk Matrix in Practice

Visualizing likelihood and impact

How it Works

Plots likelihood (e.g., Rare, Likely) against impact (e.g., Minor, Catastrophic) to determine a risk level (e.g., Critical, Minor).

Example

A data breach with 'Likely' occurrence and 'Major' impact would be classified as 'Critical' on the matrix.

Strengths & Weaknesses

Strength: Simplicity, quick overview. Weakness: Subjectivity, potential for inconsistency without clear criteria.

Key Takeaway

The risk matrix offers a clear visual for qualitative risk prioritization, but its effectiveness hinges on consistent, predefined criteria for likelihood and impact.
