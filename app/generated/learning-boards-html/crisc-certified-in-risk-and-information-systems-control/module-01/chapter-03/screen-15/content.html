Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Tools and Frameworks

A recap of essential metrics, frameworks, and management tools for risk assessment.

Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

The expected monetary loss from a specific risk over a one-year period, calculated as SLE × ARO.

NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)

A U.S. federal framework providing a structured approach to managing security and privacy risks across a system's lifecycle.

Risk Register

A central repository for documenting, tracking, and managing all identified risks throughout their lifecycle.

Summary Insight

Effective risk management combines quantitative metrics for financial insight, structured frameworks for process guidance, and comprehensive registers for ongoing tracking.
