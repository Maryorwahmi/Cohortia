NIST RMF: A Structured Approach to Risk Management

Understand how the NIST Risk Management Framework guides security and privacy risk assessment.

The NIST RMF Process

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Risk Management Framework (RMF) provides a comprehensive, lifecycle-based approach to managing security and privacy risks for information systems and organizations.

1

Categorize: Define system boundaries and information types.

2

Select: Choose appropriate security controls.

3

Implement: Put controls into practice.

4

Assess: Evaluate control effectiveness and identify risks.

5

Authorize: Make risk-based decisions to operate the system.

6

Monitor: Continuously track controls and risks.

Key Takeaway

NIST RMF provides a systematic, lifecycle-based approach to managing security and privacy risks, with the 'Assess' step being central to identifying and evaluating risks.

Focus on the 'Assess' Step

The 'Assess' step is where the core risk assessment activities take place, ensuring a thorough understanding of an organization's risk posture.

1. Conduct Assessments

Perform security and privacy control assessments to determine if controls are implemented correctly, operating as intended, and producing the desired outcome.

2. Analyze Results

Analyze the assessment results to identify vulnerabilities, threats, and the likelihood and impact of potential risk events.

3. Report Findings

Document and report the risk assessment findings to organizational officials to support risk-based decision-making.

4. Inform Decisions

Use the assessment results to inform risk response decisions and prioritize mitigation efforts.
