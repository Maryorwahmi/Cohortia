Quantitative Risk Assessment

Objective, numerical, and financially focused

Definition

An objective, numerical approach assigning monetary values to assets, potential losses, and risk event probabilities to estimate financial impact.

Key Metric: ALE

Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) calculates the expected monetary loss from a risk over a one-year period, central to financial risk analysis.

Benefits

Offers greater precision, supports better cost-benefit analysis for risk treatment, and provides a clear financial perspective on risk exposure.

Key Takeaway

Quantitative risk assessment provides a precise, financial understanding of risk, enabling data-driven decisions for resource allocation and risk mitigation strategies.

Components of Quantitative Risk

Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)

Monetary loss expected each time a specific threat exploits a vulnerability.

Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)

Estimated frequency of a specific risk event happening in a year.

Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

The expected monetary loss from a risk over a one-year period.

Common Pitfall

Difficulty in obtaining accurate data for SLE and ARO. Rely on historical data, industry benchmarks, and expert estimates.
