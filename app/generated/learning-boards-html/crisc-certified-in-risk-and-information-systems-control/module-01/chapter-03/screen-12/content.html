The Comprehensive Risk Register: Tracking and Managing Risks

Learn about the essential components of a risk register for effective risk tracking and management.

Purpose of a Risk Register

A risk register serves as a critical tool for centralizing risk information, facilitating consistent tracking, communication, and decision-making across the organization.

  • Centralized Documentation: Single source of truth for all risks.
  • Lifecycle Tracking: Monitors risks from identification to closure.
  • Accountability: Clearly assigns ownership for risk management.
  • Informed Prioritization: Supports decisions on risk treatment.
Key Takeaway

A well-maintained risk register is indispensable for proactive risk management, ensuring risks are consistently monitored and addressed.

Typical Risk Register Entries

Each entry provides a detailed snapshot of a specific risk, enabling comprehensive management.

Risk ID & Description

Unique identifier and a clear statement of the risk.

Asset(s) Affected & Threat(s)

The assets at risk and the source of potential harm.

Vulnerability(ies) & Likelihood

The weakness being exploited and its probability.

Impact & Risk Score/Level

Consequence of the risk and its calculated severity.

Risk Owner & Mitigation Strategy

Individual responsible and planned actions to reduce risk.

Current Status & Review Date

Progress of mitigation and last review timestamp.
