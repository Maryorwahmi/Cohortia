Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) Formula

ALE = SLE × ARO

Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)

Monetary loss per incident. Example: $50,000 for a data breach.

Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)

Expected frequency per year. Example: 0.2 (once every 5 years).

Calculation Example

If SLE = $50,000 and ARO = 0.2, then ALE = $50,000 × 0.2 = $10,000

Quantitative Metric

Calculating Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

Quantifying financial risk over time

Understanding SLE

The direct and indirect costs incurred each time a specific risk event occurs. This includes recovery, fines, and reputational damage.

Understanding ARO

The probability of a specific risk event happening within a one-year period. It can be a fraction if the event is expected less than once a year.

Why ALE is Powerful

Provides a clear financial figure for budgeting, prioritizing security investments, and communicating risk to stakeholders in monetary terms.

Key Takeaway

ALE translates abstract risks into concrete annual financial impacts, enabling data-driven decisions for risk treatment and resource allocation.
