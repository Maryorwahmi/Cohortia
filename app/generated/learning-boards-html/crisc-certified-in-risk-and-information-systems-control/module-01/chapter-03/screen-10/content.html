FAIR: Quantifying Information Risk in Financial Terms

Explore the Factor Analysis of Information Risk (FAIR) methodology for financial risk quantification.

Understanding FAIR's Core

FAIR provides a model for understanding, analyzing, and quantifying information risk in financial terms, moving beyond subjective ratings.

  • Focus on Loss: Quantifies the probable financial impact of risk events.
  • Component-Based: Breaks down risk into measurable factors.
  • Objective Analysis: Reduces subjectivity in risk assessment.
  • Decision Support: Enables better cost-benefit analysis for risk treatment.
Key Takeaway

FAIR offers a rigorous, financially-oriented approach to risk assessment, providing objective data for strategic risk management decisions.

FAIR's Key Components

FAIR dissects risk into primary and secondary factors to calculate Loss Event Frequency and Magnitude.

Threat Event Frequency

How often a threat agent is likely to act against an asset.

Vulnerability

The probability that a threat event will result in a loss event.

Threat Capability

The probable level of force or sophistication a threat agent can apply.

Loss Event Frequency

The probable frequency, within a given timeframe, that a loss event will occur.
