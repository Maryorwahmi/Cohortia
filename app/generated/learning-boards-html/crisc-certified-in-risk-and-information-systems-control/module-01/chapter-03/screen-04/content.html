Core Concept

Understanding Risk Assessment

The foundation of effective IT risk management

What is Risk Assessment?

The systematic process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating risks to an organization's assets. It helps understand risk nature, potential impact, and likelihood.

Why it Matters

Enables prioritization and informed decision-making on risk treatment. Crucial for business continuity and security posture.

Iterative Process

Not a one-time event; it evolves with the threat landscape, vulnerabilities, and business objectives. Continuous monitoring is key.

Key Takeaway

Risk assessment is a continuous, dynamic process that informs strategic decisions by clarifying risk nature, impact, and likelihood.

The Risk Assessment Cycle

1. Identify
2. Analyze
3. Evaluate
4. Treat

The continuous loop of risk management ensures adaptability.
