Methodologies and Techniques for Effective IT Risk Management

This chapter will equip you with the knowledge to analyze and evaluate risks effectively, enabling you to make informed decisions about how to prioritize and treat them. Key Takeaway

Risk assessment is the systematic process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating risks to an organization's assets, crucial for informed decision-making and proactive risk treatment.
