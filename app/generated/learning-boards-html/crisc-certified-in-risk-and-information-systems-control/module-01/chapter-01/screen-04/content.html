Definition

IT Risk Explained

The potential for an unwanted outcome or event that could negatively impact an organization's ability to achieve its strategic objectives.

Financial Loss

Direct monetary costs, fines, or lost revenue.

Reputational Damage

Erosion of trust and public image.

Operational Disruption

Interruption of critical business processes.

Regulatory Non-Compliance

Failure to meet legal and industry standards.

Risk Impact Flow
Unwanted Event
Negative Impact
Failure to Achieve Objectives
Key Takeaway

IT risk is the potential for negative business outcomes, encompassing financial, reputational, operational, and compliance impacts, not just technical failures.
