Definition

What is a Threat?

Any potential cause of an unwanted incident that may result in harm to a system or organization.

Threat Categories
  • Intentional: Malicious actors, cyberattacks, insider fraud
  • Unintentional: Human error, system failure, natural disaster
Examples of Threats
Ransomware Attack
Employee Error
Natural Disaster (e.g., Flood)
System Failure
Key Takeaway

Threats are diverse, ranging from malicious attacks to accidental errors and natural events, all capable of causing harm to organizational assets.
