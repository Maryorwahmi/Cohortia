Category 1

Technical Risks

Risks directly related to technology infrastructure, software, and cybersecurity.

  • Software bugs and vulnerabilities
  • Hardware failures and system outages
  • Network connectivity issues
  • Cybersecurity breaches (e.g., malware, hacking)
Category 2

Operational Risks

Risks arising from failures in internal processes, people, and systems.

  • Inadequate change management procedures
  • Insufficient staffing or skill gaps
  • Poor incident response or recovery plans
  • Lack of employee security awareness training
Key Takeaway

Categorizing risks into technical and operational helps in understanding their diverse origins and developing targeted mitigation strategies.
