Definition

What is a Vulnerability?

A weakness in an asset, system, or process that can be exploited by one or more threats.

Types of Weaknesses
  • Technical: Software bugs, unpatched systems, misconfigurations
  • Process: Inadequate security policies, poor change management
  • Human: Lack of training, weak password practices
Examples of Vulnerabilities
Unpatched Operating System
Weak Password Policy
Misconfigured Firewall
Lack of Employee Training
Key Takeaway

Vulnerabilities are weaknesses that, when exploited by a threat, can lead to negative impacts on an organization's assets.
