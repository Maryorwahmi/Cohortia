The Interplay of Risk Components

Asset

Something of value.

Threat

Potential for harm.

Vulnerability

A weakness.

RISK
Scenario Example

A customer database (Asset) is targeted by a hacker (Threat) who exploits an unpatched server (Vulnerability), leading to data theft (Risk).

Key Takeaway

Risk materializes when a threat successfully exploits a vulnerability on a valuable asset, highlighting the interconnectedness of these components.
