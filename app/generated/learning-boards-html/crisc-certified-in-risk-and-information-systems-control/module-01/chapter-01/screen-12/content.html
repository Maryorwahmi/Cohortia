Category 3

Financial Risks

Potential monetary losses due to fraud, system downtime, or costly fines.

  • Direct financial losses from cyberattacks
  • Loss of revenue due to system downtime
  • Costs of incident response and recovery
Category 4

Compliance Risks

Risks from failing to adhere to laws, regulations, and industry standards.

  • Fines for data privacy violations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA)
  • Non-compliance with industry standards (e.g., PCI DSS)
Category 5

Strategic Risks

Risks impacting the organization's ability to achieve its long-term goals.

  • Erosion of customer trust and market share
  • Failure to innovate due to security concerns
Category 6

Reputational Risks

Risks stemming from events that damage the organization's public image.

  • Negative media coverage after a breach
  • Loss of customer trust and loyalty
Key Takeaway

IT risks can cascade across financial, compliance, strategic, and reputational domains, requiring a holistic and business-centric management approach.
