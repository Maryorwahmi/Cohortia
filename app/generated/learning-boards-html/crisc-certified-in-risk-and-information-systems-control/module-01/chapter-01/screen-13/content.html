Chapter Summary

Core Principles of IT Risk

Essential Definitions for CRISC Professionals

IT Risk

The potential for an unwanted outcome or event to occur, impacting organizational objectives.

Asset

Anything of value to the organization that requires protection, including information, hardware, and reputation.

Threat

Any potential cause of an unwanted incident that may result in harm to a system or organization.

Summary Takeaway

IT risk is the potential for negative impact on objectives, arising from a threat exploiting a vulnerability on an asset.
