Definition

What Constitutes an Asset?

An asset is anything of value to the organization that needs protection. This includes both tangible and intangible items.

Asset Types
  • Physical: Servers, laptops, network devices
  • Data: Customer lists, intellectual property, financial records
  • Software: Applications, operating systems
  • Services: Online banking, email, web presence
  • Reputation: Brand image, customer trust
Examples of Assets
Customer Database
Intellectual Property

Key Takeaway

A thorough asset inventory, including both tangible and intangible items, is the foundational step for effective IT risk identification.
