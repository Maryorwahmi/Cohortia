Chapter Summary

Analyzing Risk Components

Critical Terms for Holistic Risk Management

Vulnerability

A weakness in an asset, system, or process that can be exploited by one or more threats.

Impact

The magnitude of harm or loss that can be expected if a risk materializes.

Likelihood

The probability or frequency of a specific threat exploiting a particular vulnerability.

Summary Takeaway

Assessing vulnerabilities, impact, and likelihood is crucial for prioritizing risks and developing effective mitigation strategies.
