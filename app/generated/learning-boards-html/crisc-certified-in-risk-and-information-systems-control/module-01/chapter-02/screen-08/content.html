Roles and Reporting for Risk

Ensuring transparent risk communication and decision-making

CRISC Professional: Identify & Assess Risks
Risk Management / IT Steering Committee: Analyze & Advise
Board of Directors: Strategic Risk Decisions
Key Takeaway

Transparent risk reporting from operational teams to strategic committees and the board ensures that risk considerations are integrated into all levels of IT governance and decision-making.
