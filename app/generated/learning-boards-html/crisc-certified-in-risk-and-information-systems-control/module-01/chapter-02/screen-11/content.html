Definition

What is a Procedure?

Procedures are detailed, step-by-step instructions on how to perform specific tasks to meet standards and policies.

  • Highly specific and granular, guiding individual actions.
  • Ensure operational consistency and reduce human error.
  • Must be regularly reviewed, updated, and communicated.
Example

Data Backup Procedure

A procedure would detail steps like: "1. Log in to backup system. 2. Select 'Confidential Data' job. 3. Verify log file. 4. Store media offsite."

This provides exact instructions to ensure data is backed up according to standards and policies.

Key Takeaway

Procedures are the practical, step-by-step guides that ensure policies and standards are consistently and correctly implemented in daily operations.
