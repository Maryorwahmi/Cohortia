Key Concept

Risk Appetite

The amount of risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives. This is a strategic decision set by top leadership.

Key Takeaway

Risk management is an embedded component of good governance, with ultimate responsibility residing with the board and senior management who define the organization's risk appetite.
