Definition

What is a Standard?

Standards provide mandatory requirements for how policies are to be implemented, often specifying specific technologies, configurations, or methods.

  • More prescriptive and detailed than policies.
  • Ensure consistency and enforceability across the organization.
  • Translate high-level policy intent into specific requirements.
Example

Data Encryption Standard

A standard might dictate: "All data classified as 'Confidential' must be encrypted using AES-256 encryption before storage or transmission."

This standard specifies the exact technology and method to meet the data protection policy.

Key Takeaway

Standards provide the mandatory 'how' for policies, ensuring specific, consistent, and enforceable implementation of governance directives.
