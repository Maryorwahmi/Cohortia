Definition

What is IT Governance?

IT governance is a comprehensive framework of leadership, organizational structures, and processes that ensures the organization's IT sustains and extends its strategies and objectives.

  • Ensures IT delivers value to the business.
  • Manages IT resources responsibly.
  • Measures performance effectively.
  • Aligns with overall business strategy.
  • Manages IT-related risks.
Analogy

The Steering Wheel & Navigation System

Think of IT governance as the steering wheel and navigation system for the IT department. It guides IT to align with the overall business direction, ensuring it stays on course and reaches its strategic destinations.

Key Takeaway

Strong IT governance is essential to prevent siloed IT initiatives, ensure alignment with business goals, and protect the organization from unacceptable levels of risk.
