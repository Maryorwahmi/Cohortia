Governance Directives

The PSP Hierarchy

Policy: WHAT to do (High-level intent)
Standard: HOW to do it (Mandatory requirements)
Procedure: HOW to do it (Step-by-step instructions)
Common Mistakes
  • Vague policies lack clear direction.
  • Unenforceable standards lead to inconsistent practices.
  • Outdated procedures increase risk exposure.
Key Takeaway

A clear and well-maintained hierarchy of policies, standards, and procedures is fundamental for consistent practices, clear responsibilities, and effective risk mitigation within IT governance.
