Key Frameworks

COBIT: Guiding Enterprise IT

Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies

Meet Stakeholder Needs

Ensuring IT delivers value aligned with business objectives.

Cover Enterprise End-to-End

Addressing all IT-related functions and processes across the organization.

Apply Single Integrated Framework

Providing a consistent and comprehensive governance approach.

Enable Holistic Approach

Considering all relevant enablers like information, people, and culture.

Separate Governance from Management

Clearly defining distinct responsibilities for each function.

Key Takeaway

COBIT's principles provide a structured and integrated approach to IT governance, making it an essential tool for CRISC professionals to embed risk management effectively.
