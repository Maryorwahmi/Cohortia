Steering IT Towards Success

Understanding IT Governance and Risk Integration

IT governance is the strategic framework that ensures an organization's technology investments and operations align with its overall business objectives, deliver value, and effectively manage associated risks. It's about leadership, structure, and processes that guide IT to support and extend business strategies.
