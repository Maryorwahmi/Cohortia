Essential Takeaways

Key terms for risk management integration

Risk Appetite

The amount of risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives.

Policies

High-level statements of management's intent, outlining what is to be done.

Standards

Mandatory requirements specifying how policies are to be implemented (technologies, configurations).

Procedures

Detailed, step-by-step instructions on how to perform specific tasks to meet standards and policies.

These terms define the practical application of IT governance, ensuring that strategic intent is translated into actionable and controlled operations.
