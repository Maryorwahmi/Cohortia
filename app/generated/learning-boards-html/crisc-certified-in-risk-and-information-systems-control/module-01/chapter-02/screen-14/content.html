Essential Takeaways

Reinforcing core IT governance principles

IT Governance

Framework of leadership, structures, and processes ensuring IT aligns with business strategies and objectives.

COBIT

Comprehensive IT governance framework by ISACA, guiding enterprise IT management and risk integration.

ISO 27001

International standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS), focusing on systematic security risk management.

These concepts form the bedrock of effective IT governance, ensuring strategic alignment, value delivery, and robust risk management within any organization.
