Effective Governance

Engaging Stakeholders

The critical role of executive buy-in and collaboration

Board of Directors & Executive Leadership

Set the tone from the top, champion governance, and provide strategic direction.

IT Staff & Business Unit Managers

Implement directives, communicate risks, and ensure operational compliance.

Effective Communication & Collaboration

Translate technical risks into business impact to foster shared understanding and secure resources.

Key Takeaway

Successful IT governance relies heavily on active participation from all stakeholders, especially executive leadership, to ensure alignment and effective risk management.
