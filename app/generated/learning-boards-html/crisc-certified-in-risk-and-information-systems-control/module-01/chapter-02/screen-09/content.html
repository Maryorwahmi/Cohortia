Definition

What is a Policy?

A policy is a high-level statement of management's intent and expectations, outlining what is to be done.

  • Reflects strategic goals and organizational values.
  • Broad, principle-based, and generally enduring.
  • Provides the authority for all subsequent standards and procedures.
Example

Data Classification Policy

A policy might state: "All organizational data must be classified according to its sensitivity (e.g., Public, Internal, Confidential)."

This policy establishes the requirement for data classification without detailing the specific methods or tools to be used.

Key Takeaway

Policies define the high-level 'what' and strategic intent, forming the essential foundation for all subsequent governance and risk management directives.
