Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Understanding FHitResult
Key Fields
  • GetActor / HitActor: The actor that was hit.
  • HitComponent: The primitive component impacted.
  • Location: World position of the impact.
  • Normal: Surface normal at the hit point.
  • BoneName: For skeletal meshes when complex collision is used.
Practical Guidance
Always check bBlockingHit or IsValidBlockingHit before using fields. When you need limb-level detail use bTraceComplex true so BoneName becomes reliable. Use these fields to decide interactions, play effects at hit location, or orient decals with the normal.
Example
On hit, spawn an impact decal at HitResult.Location aligned to HitResult.Normal to visually communicate the collision.
