Tracing and Line of Sight
Cast rays and swept shapes from C++ to detect, highlight, and interact with actors.
In this lesson

You will learn line traces, shape traces, reading FHitResult, configuring collision channels and query params, plus debugging and performance recommendations for reliable interactions.

Why it matters

Tracing is the backbone of look-based interaction, hitscan weapons, and spatial queries. Correct setup prevents false hits and costly frame spikes.
