Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Performance Considerations
Mitigations
  • Run traces on input or at a capped frequency instead of every frame.
  • Prefer simple collision over complex unless accuracy demands it.
  • Use single shape sweeps instead of many discrete traces for area checks.
Practical Example
Switch an interaction trace from every-tick to a 10 Hz timer and measure whether perceived responsiveness remains acceptable while CPU cost drops.
Performance Tip
Measure first. Only optimize if traces appear on your profiler as a hotspot.
