Comparison
DebugGame vs Development vs Shipping
DebugGame
Full debugging symbols and instrumentation for deep troubleshooting. Slow and large.
When to use: step-through debugging and deep inspection.
Development
Balanced: keeps some diagnostics and profiling hooks while improving performance. Good for internal testing.
When to use: iterative testing and profiling before release.
Shipping
Optimized build with debugging removed. Smallest and fastest; intended for public release.
When to use: final release to players.
Example scenario
If a performance issue appears in QA, build in Development to profile. If the issue remains unclear at that stage, reproduce in DebugGame to step through code. Only produce Shipping builds for distribution.
Pitfall
Shipping removes logs and debug hooks. Avoid shipping when debugging or you will lose diagnostic context.
