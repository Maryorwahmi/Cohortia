Networking & Multiplayer Basics
Understand authority, replication, and RPCs to synchronize gameplay across machines.

Why this matters: Multiplayer introduces authority boundaries. Making the right replication and RPC choices prevents desyncs and cheating.

Concrete outcome: You will implement a replicated health component and a multicast damage effect that logs on both server and clients.

In this lesson
Client-server model, replication, RPCs, implementing replicated variables, testing with editor instances.
