Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Testing & Best Practices
Test with instances
Use editor + standalone to confirm RPCs and replication.
Log & verify
Place UE_LOGs in server, OnRep, and multicast functions to observe ordering.
Limit replication
Use conditions and compact types to reduce bandwidth and protect private data.
Final Note
Make authority explicit, test early, and keep replication minimal. These practices make multiplayer features reliable and maintainable.
