Ownership visual
Three small cards show Unique, Shared, Weak ownership relationships and their lifecycle behaviors.
TUniquePtr
Exclusive owner; automatic destroy at scope exit.
TSharedPtr
Reference counted shared ownership; destroyed when last owner releases.
TWeakPtr
Non-owning observer; lock to access if still alive.
