Troubleshooting checklist
Step-by-step
  1. Confirm IMC is added via AddMappingContext in BeginPlay.
  2. Verify UInputAction and IMC pointers are assigned in Defaults or Blueprint.
  3. Cast PlayerInputComponent to UEnhancedInputComponent before BindAction.
  4. Check Swizzle/Negate if axes are inverted or swapped.
  5. Log FInputActionValue inside handlers to validate payloads.
Common symptoms
  • No log entries from Move/Look — likely IMC not added or actions unassigned.
  • Movement axes reversed — check Negate or Swizzle modifiers.
  • Compile errors when binding — probably missing cast to EnhancedInputComponent.
Pro tip
Instrument BeginPlay with logs to confirm AddMappingContext executes and which priority you passed.
