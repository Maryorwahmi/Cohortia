Quick Reference
What to Remember
Activate IMC
Call AddMappingContext on the Enhanced Input Local Player Subsystem during BeginPlay.
Cast & Bind
Cast to UEnhancedInputComponent and use BindAction with UInputAction assets.
Match Types
Ensure action Value Type aligns with Value.Get<Type> in your handlers.
Final tip
When something fails, follow the checklist: assets assigned, IMC added, component cast, bind called, and logged values appear.
