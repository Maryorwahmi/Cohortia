What to Remember
Three concise reminders to guide your implementation and debugging.
Prefer Primitives
Use sphere/box/capsule for collision to keep costs low and behavior simple.
Inspect Responses
Check the Block/Overlap/Ignore matrix for your custom channels and presets.
Enable Correct Flags
Generate Overlap Events for triggers; Notify Rigid Body Collision or Simulate Physics for hits.
Closing Thought
Design collisions to express intent: notification or physical response. That choice simplifies configuration and leads to fewer surprises at runtime.
