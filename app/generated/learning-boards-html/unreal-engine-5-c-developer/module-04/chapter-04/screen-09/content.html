Pro Tip
Use log messages during development to confirm which event fired and what OtherActor actually is before adding game effects.
Concrete Example
Spawn the projectile in the editor, let it pass through a trigger volume and then hit a wall. Observe distinct log entries for overlap and hit to validate your configuration.
Key Takeaway

Handlers should validate inputs, log for debugging, and perform narrowly scoped actions or forward work to safe systems.
