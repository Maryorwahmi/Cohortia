Core Concept
Collision Primitives
Simple geometric components that provide the collision geometry used for overlap and hit detection.
USphereComponent
Good for projectiles and radius checks.
UBoxComponent
Works well for triggers and simple world geometry.
UCapsuleComponent
Ideal for character collision and smooth sliding.
Key Takeaway

Prefer simple primitives (sphere, box, capsule) for runtime collision. They are fast, predictable, and easier to debug.

Concrete Example
A projectile uses a USphereComponent with a small radius to detect hits and overlaps reliably. The player's UCapsuleComponent handles movement and prevents clipping on steps.
Common Mistake
Using per-poly collision for every object. This increases queries and can harm frame rate. Simplify collision where you do not need exact visual fidelity.
