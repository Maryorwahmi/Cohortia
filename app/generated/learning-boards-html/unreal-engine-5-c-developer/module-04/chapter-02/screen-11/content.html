Process Flow
Tuning movement and safety
Step 1: Start values
MovementSpeed = 500, FloatingPawnMovement.MaxSpeed = 1000.
Step 2: Play and observe
Check sliding, collisions, and feel. Test on slow and fast machines or simulate low FPS.
Step 3: Adjust
Lower speeds if tunneling occurs; tweak collision and physics settings as needed.
Practical tip

Tune movement with example environments: open spaces, narrow corridors, and slopes to ensure consistent behavior across contexts.
