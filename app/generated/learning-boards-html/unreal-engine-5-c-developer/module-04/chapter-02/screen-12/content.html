Hands-on Activity
Implement and test movement
Starter checklist
  1. Include FloatingPawnMovement header.
  2. Create UFloatingPawnMovement subobject in constructor and set MaxSpeed.
  3. Add MovementSpeed UPROPERTY to pawn.
  4. Implement Move using controller yaw, AddMovementInput, and delta seconds.
  5. Implement Look with controller yaw and pitch inputs.
Testing checklist
  • Place pawn in level and possess it.
  • Confirm W/A/S/D and mouse move the pawn and camera.
  • Test high and low FPS to verify consistent movement.
  • Adjust MovementSpeed and MaxSpeed if tunneling or sluggishness appears.
Example outcome

After these steps your pawn should move and look smoothly, respond to input consistently, and respect collisions in simple test environments.
