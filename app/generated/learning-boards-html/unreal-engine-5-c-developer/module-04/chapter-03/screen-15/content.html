Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Mechanics & Pitfalls
Sprint safety
Capture DefaultWalkSpeed in BeginPlay and restore it when sprint ends to avoid speed drift.
Input scaling
Do not multiply AddMovementInput by delta seconds when using CharacterMovementComponent; it integrates motion for you.
Rotation conflict
Avoid enabling both bUseControllerRotationYaw and bOrientRotationToMovement to prevent jerky rotation.
Final Tip
Test movement features early in the editor and in multiplayer when applicable to catch prediction and replication issues.
