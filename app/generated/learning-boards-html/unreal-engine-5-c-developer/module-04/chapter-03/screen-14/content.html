Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
ACharacter
A pawn subclass with capsule and CharacterMovement, ideal for bipedal player characters.
UCharacterMovementComponent
Handles physics-aware movement, jumping, falling, slopes, and network prediction for characters.
UCapsuleComponent
Common collision primitive for characters, defining physical bounds for stepping and collision.
Takeaway
These three components together provide the most reliable foundation for humanoid movement in Unreal Engine.
