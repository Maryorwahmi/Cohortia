Jump implementation

Bind the jump input to call Jump on start and StopJumping on completion so CharacterMovement manages motion.

Binding pattern
Bind JumpAction Started -> JumpStarted -> Jump(); Completed -> JumpCompleted -> StopJumping().
Tuning
Adjust JumpZVelocity and AirControl for desired arc and mid-air control. Test on slopes and during networked play if applicable.
Common Mistake
Calling Jump without StopJumping on completion can lead to inconsistent or extended jump behavior, especially with variable-height jumps.
