Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Best Practices
Small focused classes
Split responsibilities and prefer composition over one monolithic Actor.
Narrow UPROPERTY exposure
Expose only what designers need and document the reason for exposure next to the field.
Pointer safety
Prefer weak or owning UPROPERTY references to avoid dangling pointers and GC surprises.
Key Takeaway
Adopt these patterns early. They save debugging time and make your C++ types behave predictably in the Editor and at runtime.
