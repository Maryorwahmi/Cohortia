Unit Testing in Android

Learn how to test the smallest parts of your app quickly on the JVM, isolate dependencies with mocks, and make LiveData and coroutines deterministic in tests.

In this lesson
  • Set up JUnit and Mockito for local unit tests.
  • Isolate ViewModel logic with mocked repositories.
  • Control coroutines and LiveData in tests for determinism.
