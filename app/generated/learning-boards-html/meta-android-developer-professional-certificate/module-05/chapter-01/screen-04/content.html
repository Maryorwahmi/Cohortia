Core Concept

The Blueprint for Success

Avoiding 'God Activities' and Unwieldy Codebases

Without Architecture

  • Code becomes tightly coupled and hard to manage.
  • Increased risk of bugs and unexpected side effects.
  • Difficult to scale with new features.
  • Testing becomes complex and time-consuming.

With Architecture

  • Clear responsibilities for each component.
  • Improved testability and easier debugging.
  • Enhanced maintainability and scalability.
  • Smoother integration of new features.
Analogy

Think of building a house. Without blueprints, you might end up with a 'God Room' that's a kitchen, bedroom, and bathroom all in one, making it impossible to use or repair.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined app architecture is the blueprint that guides development, preventing common pitfalls and ensuring long-term success.

Common Mistake

Allowing Activities or Fragments to contain too much logic (UI, data, business rules) leads to 'God Activities' that are hard to test and maintain.
