Services and Foreground Services

Enabling long-running background operations in Android

Android Services are essential components for executing operations that need to persist without a user interface. This chapter will guide you through their creation, management, and the critical role of Foreground Services for user-visible tasks.

Key Takeaway

Services are fundamental for continuous background operations, ensuring your app remains functional even when not actively used by the user.

Background Tasks

Execute operations without UI.

No User Interface

Operate independently of screens.

Continuous Operation

Run even when app is closed.

System Priority

Less likely to be killed by OS.
