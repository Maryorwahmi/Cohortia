Key Concepts

Reinforcing your understanding of Android Services

Foreground Service

A service for user-visible tasks, requiring a persistent notification.

startForeground()

Method to promote a service to foreground, requiring a unique notification ID and object.

Notification Channel

(Android 8.0+) A categorization system for notifications, allowing user control.

Chapter Summary

Foreground Services are for critical, user-visible tasks, requiring startForeground() and a Notification Channel for proper implementation.
