Bound Services

Enabling client-server interaction and IPC

What is a Bound Service?

A Bound Service allows other application components (clients) to bind to it, send requests, receive responses, and perform IPC.

  • •Initiated by bindService().
  • •Returns an IBinder for interaction.
  • •Lifecycle tied to its clients.

Interaction Model

Clients bind to the service, establishing a two-way communication channel. The service remains active as long as at least one client is bound.

  • •Client calls bindService().
  • •Service's onBind() returns an IBinder.
  • •Clients interact via the IBinder interface.
Key Takeaway

Bound Services enable direct, two-way communication between components and the service, with their lifecycle managed by the binding clients.
