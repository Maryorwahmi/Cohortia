Key Concepts

Essential terms from this chapter

Service

An Android component for long-running background operations without a UI.

Started Service

Initiated by startService() for independent, one-way background tasks.

Bound Service

Initiated by bindService(), allowing clients to interact with it directly.

Chapter Summary

Services are crucial for background operations, with Started Services for independent tasks and Bound Services for client-server interactions.
