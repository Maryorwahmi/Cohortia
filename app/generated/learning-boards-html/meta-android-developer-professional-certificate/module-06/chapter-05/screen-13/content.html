Comparison

Testing tracks at a glance

Internal testing: fastest, for a handful of testers.
Closed testing: curated groups for staged QA.
Open testing: public beta before production.
Production: staged rollout percentages for cautious delivery.
Staged rollout example
  1. Upload signed AAB to internal track for smoke tests.
  2. Move to closed testing for a broader QA group.
  3. Start a 5% staged rollout in production and monitor crash rate.
  4. Increase rollout if metrics are healthy, or halt and fix if not.
Localization tips
  • Translate store listing and screenshots for target locales.
  • Test locale-specific behavior on devices configured for that language.
  • Align privacy policy and content declarations across locales.
Key Takeaway

Use test tracks and staged rollouts to protect users, and localize store assets for better reach.
