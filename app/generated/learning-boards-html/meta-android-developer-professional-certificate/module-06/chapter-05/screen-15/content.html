Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › What to Remember
Backup keystore
Store at least two encrypted backups outside the project and document recovery steps.
Increment versionCode
Play Console rejects updates without a higher versionCode than your previous release.
Test signed release
Install a signed AAB or APK on devices and verify functionality under release optimizations.
Final Takeaway

A careful pre-publish checklist and staged rollout are your best defenses against release-time surprises. Verify keys, builds, and listings before upload.
