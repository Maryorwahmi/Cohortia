Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Play Console
Definition

What the Play Console manages

Store listing
App name, short & full descriptions, screenshots, icon, feature graphic.
App releases
Upload signed AAB to tracks: internal, closed, open, or production.
App content
Privacy policy, target audience, content rating, ads declaration.
Concrete steps
  1. 1. Create app in Play Console and set default language.
  2. 2. Fill Store Listing with high-quality screenshots and a descriptive summary.
  3. 3. Upload signed AAB under App Releases and pick a track for rollout.
  4. 4. Complete Content Rating and Pricing & Distribution sections.
Example

For your first public test, upload the AAB to an internal testing track and invite testers. After verification, promote the release to production or use staged rollout.

Key Takeaway

Play Console enforces metadata and policy checks. Use testing tracks first and ensure versionCode increments for updates.
