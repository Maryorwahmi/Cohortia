App Deployment for Android

Prepare, sign, and publish Android apps with best practices for native and React Native workflows.

Terminal demo
Duration: ~20 min walkthrough
Why this matters

Signed bundles guarantee update authenticity and smaller downloads when distributed as AAB. Stores require proper assets and metadata to list your app.

Who benefits

Developers releasing production apps, team leads reviewing release pipelines, and learners preparing capstone apps for publication.
