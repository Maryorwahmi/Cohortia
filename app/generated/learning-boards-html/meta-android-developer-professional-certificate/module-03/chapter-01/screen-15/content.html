Always set a LayoutManager
No LayoutManager means no rendered items.
Cache views in ViewHolder
Avoid repeated findViewById calls to reduce binding cost.
Use targeted updates
Prefer notifyItem* or DiffUtil over notifyDataSetChanged for efficient animations.
Final takeaway

Small architectural choices in RecyclerView implementations have large effects on memory, smoothness, and maintainability. Use these three reminders as a checklist when you build or review list code.
