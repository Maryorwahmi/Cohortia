Common Pitfalls

Quick debugging checklist

Use this checklist to diagnose missing items, incorrect bindings, and performance issues.

No items visible?
Confirm you set a LayoutManager. Without it RecyclerView won't arrange children.
Clicks not firing?
Ensure itemView.setOnClickListener is called in the ViewHolder and the host lambda is non-null.
Wrong or flashing images?
Use Glide or Coil which cancel previous requests for recycled views to prevent incorrect images.
List updates look wrong?
Call notifyItemInserted/Removed/Changed or use DiffUtil to inform the adapter of changes.
Scrolling jank?
Profile and move expensive work out of onBindViewHolder. Precompute or use background threads.
Pro tip

Add logging in onCreateViewHolder and onBindViewHolder to understand how often views are created and bound during scrolls.
