Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › ImageView and scaleType
Definition

How images scale

ImageView shows images from drawables or mipmaps. Choose android:scaleType to control scaling and cropping behavior.

centerCrop
Scales uniformly and crops so the view is completely filled.
fitCenter
Scales uniformly so the whole image fits inside the view.
center
Places the image centered without scaling.
Practical example
Profile photos often use centerCrop so image fills the avatar circle while product thumbnails use fitCenter to show the whole product.
Key Takeaway

Choose the scaleType that preserves aspect ratio and matches whether fill or full-image display is more important for the UI.

XML sample
<ImageView
  android:id="@+id/avatar"
  android:layout_width="72dp"
  android:layout_height="72dp"
  android:src="@drawable/profile"
  android:scaleType="centerCrop" />
