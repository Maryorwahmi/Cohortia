TextWatcher & live validation
Attach a TextWatcher to react to text changes and provide immediate feedback or enable controls based on input content.
Mnemonic: watch the text, then validate.

06
Safe conversion & inputType
Use inputType like number and convert with toIntOrNull to avoid NumberFormatException on empty or invalid input.
Mnemonic: prefer toIntOrNull over direct toInt.
Verified competency milestone
07
ImageView & scaleType
Choose centerCrop, fitCenter or center to control how images are scaled and avoid distortion.
Mnemonic: crop to fill, fit to show all.
Verified competency milestone
08
Form wiring & Toasts
Find views by ID in onCreate/onViewCreated, set listeners, and show concise Toasts for brief confirmations.
Mnemonic: wire, validate, confirm.
Verified competency milestone
