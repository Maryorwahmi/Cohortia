View and ViewGroup — clear definitions

A View draws content and handles events. A ViewGroup contains children and controls measurement and layout. Together they form the view hierarchy that defines a screen.

Concrete example
A toolbar ViewGroup contains a title TextView and a navigation Button. The toolbar measures children and positions them horizontally.
Misconception
Treating ViewGroup purely as a wrapper ignores its impact on measurement and event routing. Choice of container affects performance and layout behavior.
Key Takeaway

Distinguish elements that draw from containers that layout; prefer flatter hierarchies when possible to reduce layout work.
