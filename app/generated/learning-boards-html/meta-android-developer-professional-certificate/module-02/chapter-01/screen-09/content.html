Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Comparison
LinearLayout
Sequential arrangement, simple and readable. Good for stacks, lists, and rows of controls.
Pros: simple, predictable, easy to read.
Cons: excessive nesting for alignment can hurt performance.
RelativeLayout
Position by relation to siblings or parent. Useful for overlays and pinned elements.
Pros: flexible positioning and overlays.
Cons: many interdependencies can make XML hard to maintain.
Key Takeaway

Match container choice to layout needs: sequence → LinearLayout, relative positioning → RelativeLayout, and prefer flat hierarchies for performance.
