Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › What to Remember
Keep bind light
Avoid synchronous image decoding or heavy calculations during bind; use caching and background work.
Notify precisely
Prefer notifyItemInserted/Removed/Changed and DiffUtil so RecyclerView can update minimally and animate changes.
Choose LayoutManager
Select Linear, Grid, or StaggeredGrid based on visual needs and test performance with real data.
Final checklist
Item XML, data class, ViewHolder caching, lightweight bind, precise notifications, and consider DiffUtil for frequent updates.
