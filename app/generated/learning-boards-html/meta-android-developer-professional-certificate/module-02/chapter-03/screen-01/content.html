Visual concept
How the pieces fit
Adapter
Creates ViewHolders, binds data
ViewHolder
Caches subview references for fast reuse
LayoutManager
Positions items and triggers recycling
As items scroll off-screen, RecyclerView reclaims their views and hands them back to the Adapter to be bound with new data for incoming items.
Why this matters
Large lists become responsive and memory-friendly when view creation is limited and binding work is minimized.
