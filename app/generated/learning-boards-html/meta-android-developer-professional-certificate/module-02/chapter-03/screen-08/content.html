Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › LayoutManager
LinearLayoutManager
One-dimensional list

Vertical or horizontal lists, commonly used for feeds and simple lists. Low layout complexity and predictable recycling.

GridLayoutManager
Uniform grid

Tiles of similar size, ideal for galleries or product grids. Requires span calculations for arrangement.

StaggeredGridLayoutManager
Variable-size grid

Supports differing item heights or widths. Visually dynamic but may increase layout complexity.

Practical note

Choose the manager based on your visual design and dataset characteristics. LayoutManager controls arrangement but does not replace good binding and update practices.

Check

If items have widely varying heights and you need masonry visuals, consider StaggeredGrid but test layout performance carefully.
