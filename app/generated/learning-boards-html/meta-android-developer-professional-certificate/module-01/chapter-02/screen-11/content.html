Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › String Templates
Examples
fun main() {
  val userName = "Jane"
  val userScore = 12
  val message = "Welcome, $userName! Your score is ${userScore * 10}."
  println(message)

  val price = 99.99
  println("Get the course for $${price * 0.8} today!")
}
Note: Place braces around expressions and escape a literal dollar sign by using another dollar when needed, or prefer formatting utilities for currency.
Analogy & Tip

Think of string templates as inline calculations inside a printed sentence. They keep the narrative intact and the computation next to its presentation.

Check Your Understanding

What happens if you put 10 / 3 inside a template and expect 3.33? Remember operand types determine the numeric result.
