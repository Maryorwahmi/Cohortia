Key Concepts (Continued)

Expanding your CI/CD vocabulary.

DevOps

A set of practices combining software development and IT operations to shorten the development life cycle and provide continuous delivery with high quality.

Feedback Loop

The process of quickly getting information back to developers about code quality and functionality, enabling rapid iteration and problem-solving.

Automated Tests

Scripts and tools that automatically verify the functionality, performance, and security of software components or the entire application.

Chapter Summary

These supporting concepts are integral to building and maintaining effective CI/CD pipelines, fostering a culture of quality and continuous improvement.
