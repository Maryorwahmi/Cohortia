The Single Application Advantage

GitLab consolidates version control, CI/CD, security, and more into one platform, eliminating the need for multiple tools.

Reduced Context Switching

Developers stay within a single interface, reducing mental overhead and improving focus on coding tasks.

Enhanced Collaboration

Teams share a unified data store and workflow, fostering better communication and alignment across roles.
