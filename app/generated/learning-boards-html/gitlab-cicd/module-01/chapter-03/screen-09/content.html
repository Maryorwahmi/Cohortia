Branching: Isolating Your Development Work

Working Concurrently Without Interference

Why Branching?

  • Isolate new features or bug fixes.
  • Enable parallel development by multiple team members.
  • Prevent unstable code from affecting the main codebase.
  • Facilitate experimentation without risk.
Key Takeaway

Branching is crucial for managing concurrent development, ensuring stability and enabling safe experimentation.

Pro-Tip

Use descriptive branch names like `feature/user-profile` or `bugfix/login-error`.
