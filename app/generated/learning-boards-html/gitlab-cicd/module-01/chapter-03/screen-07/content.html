Push vs. Pull

  • Push: Sends your local commits to the remote.
  • Pull: Fetches and merges remote changes into your local branch.
Common Mistake

Forgetting to `git pull` before starting new work or pushing, leading to merge conflicts.

Key Takeaway

`git push` and `git pull` are essential for keeping your local repository synchronized with the remote and collaborating effectively.
