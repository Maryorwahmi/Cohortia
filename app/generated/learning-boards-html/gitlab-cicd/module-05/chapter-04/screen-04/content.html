Dynamic Environments and Review Apps Defined

Isolated, Temporary Deployments for Feature Branches

What are Dynamic Environments?

On-demand, temporary environments created for specific purposes, such as feature branches. They exist only as long as needed.

What are Review Apps?

A specific type of dynamic environment, automatically deployed for each new merge request, providing a live preview of changes.

Key Takeaway

Review Apps eliminate shared staging bottlenecks by providing unique, isolated environments for every feature branch, accelerating feedback.
