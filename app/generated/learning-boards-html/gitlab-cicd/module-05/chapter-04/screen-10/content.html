Seamless Integration with Merge Requests

The 'View App' Button and Automated Cleanup

'View App' Button

GitLab automatically adds a 'View app' button to the merge request widget, providing direct access to the live Review App.

Automated Cleanup

When an MR is merged or closed, GitLab can automatically trigger the `on_stop` job to tear down the Review App resources.
