GitLab CI/CD for Blue/Green & Canary

Leveraging GitLab for advanced deployment patterns

Implementing Strategies with GitLab

  • Blue/Green: Define `production-blue` and `production-green` environments.
  • Blue/Green: Use manual jobs to switch load balancer targets after testing.
  • Canary: Deploy to small, specific environments or use service mesh integration.
  • GitLab's 'Deployments' view provides a single pane of glass for visibility.
Essential Components

Beyond the Strategy

  • Comprehensive Automated Testing
  • Robust Monitoring and Alerting
  • Clear Rollback Procedures
Key Takeaway

GitLab CI/CD provides the flexibility and visibility needed to implement sophisticated Blue/Green and Canary deployment strategies, ensuring controlled and observable releases for complex applications.
