Chapter Summary

Key Concepts (Continued)

Additional essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Backward Compatibility

The ability of a new software version to work with data, configurations, or systems created by an older version.

Load Balancer

A device or software that distributes network traffic across multiple servers, crucial for managing deployment traffic.

Rollback

The process of reverting an application to a previous, stable version in case of deployment failure or issues.

These supporting concepts are vital for building resilient and reliable deployment pipelines.
