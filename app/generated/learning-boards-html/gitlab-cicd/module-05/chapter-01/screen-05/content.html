Rolling Deployment: The Gradual Update

Understanding the incremental approach to software releases

How it Works: Step-by-Step

  1. 1.New version deployed to a small subset of instances.
  2. 2.Old version continues to serve traffic on remaining instances.
  3. 3.New version confirmed stable on updated instances.
  4. 4.Deployment proceeds to the next subset until all are updated.
Key Benefit

Minimal Downtime

Traffic is always served by at least some instances, ensuring continuous availability during the update process.

Key Takeaway

Rolling deployments offer a balanced approach to updates, prioritizing continuous service and controlled risk exposure through incremental changes.

Example Scenario

Updating a Microservice

A microservice with 10 instances. A rolling deployment updates 2 instances at a time. If issues arise, only 2 instances are affected, and traffic can be rerouted to the 8 stable ones.
