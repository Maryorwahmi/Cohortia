Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Rolling Deployment

New versions are gradually rolled out to a subset of servers, replacing old versions incrementally.

Blue/Green Deployment

Two identical environments; new versions deployed to inactive, then traffic switched instantaneously for zero downtime.

Canary Deployment

New version released to a small subset of users, monitored, then gradually rolled out if stable.

These strategies are fundamental for managing risk and ensuring continuous delivery in modern software development.
