Deployment Strategy

Canary Deployment: Gradual Exposure

Minimizing risk by releasing to a small subset of users

How it Works: Phased Rollout

  1. 1.New version deployed to a very small percentage (e.g., 1-5%) of user traffic.
  2. 2.This 'canary' group's experience is closely monitored for issues.
  3. 3.If stable, gradually roll out to larger percentages of users.
  4. 4.If issues detected, immediately halt rollout and revert traffic for the canary group.
Key Benefits

Risk Mitigation & Real-World Testing

Allows for real-world testing with actual user traffic, providing early warning signals and minimizing impact on the majority of users.

Key Takeaway

Canary deployments are excellent for critical applications, enabling cautious, data-driven releases by testing new versions with a small user subset before a full rollout.

Example Scenario

New Social Media Feature

A social media app releases a new 'Stories' feature to 2% of users. Monitoring shows high engagement and no errors. The feature is then gradually rolled out to all users.
