Mastering Environments in GitLab CI/CD

Streamline your deployment workflows with powerful environment management.

In this chapter, you'll learn how to configure and manage deployment environments within your GitLab CI/CD pipelines. We'll explore how to track deployments, access environment-specific URLs, implement cleanup actions for dynamic environments, and understand the full lifecycle management of your applications, ensuring robust and secure delivery.

Key Takeaway

GitLab environments provide structured, controlled stages for software delivery, enhancing visibility and control over your deployment pipelines.
