Critical Parameter

The Critical action: stop Parameter

Understand how action: stop explicitly signals environment teardown to GitLab.

Why it's Essential
  • • Explicitly tells GitLab that the job is stopping the environment.
  • • Updates the environment's status in the GitLab UI to 'stopped'.
  • • Ensures GitLab's internal records accurately reflect the environment's state.
Key Takeaway

Always include environment: action: stop in your cleanup jobs to ensure GitLab correctly updates the environment status, preventing confusion and maintaining accurate records.

.gitlab-ci.yml Snippet for Cleanup Job
stop_review_app:
  stage: cleanup
  script:
    - echo "De-provisioning resources..."
  environment:
    name: review/$CI_COMMIT_REF_SLUG
    action: stop # Crucial for GitLab to mark as stopped
  when: manual

Common Mistake

Forgetting action: stop means GitLab won't update the environment status. The environment might appear 'active' in the UI even after resources are deleted, leading to operational confusion and potential false alarms.
