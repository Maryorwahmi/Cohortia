Key Benefits of GitLab Environments

Recap the advantages of defining and managing environments in your CI/CD pipelines.

Enhanced Visibility

Centralized view of all deployments across various environments.

Robust Tracking

Clear history of who deployed what, and when, for auditing and debugging.

Granular Control

Manual deployments, environment protection, and lifecycle management.

Seamless Integration

Direct linking to deployed apps and integration with merge requests for Review Apps.

Key Takeaway

GitLab environments are essential for building robust, observable, and secure deployment pipelines, providing clarity and control throughout the software delivery process.
