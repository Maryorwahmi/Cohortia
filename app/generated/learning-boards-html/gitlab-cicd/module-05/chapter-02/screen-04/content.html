Core Concept

The Role of Environments in CI/CD

Understand why distinct environments are crucial for a robust software delivery pipeline.

Isolated Stages

Each environment (dev, test, staging, production) provides a dedicated, isolated space for specific development phases.

Risk Mitigation

Testing in non-production environments helps catch bugs and issues early, preventing critical failures in live systems.

Quality Assurance

Environments enable thorough validation and quality checks, ensuring software meets standards before release.

Key Takeaway

Environments are fundamental for structured, safe, and reliable software delivery, reducing risks and improving quality.
