Key Concepts: Core Environment Keywords

Review the fundamental terms for defining and linking environments.

Environment Keyword

A .gitlab-ci.yml keyword used in a job to declare that it deploys to a specific environment, enabling GitLab's features.

Environment URL

A URL specified with the environment keyword that GitLab uses to provide a direct link to the deployed application in the UI.

Dynamic Environments

Environments that are created on demand, typically for feature branches or merge requests, and later torn down.

Chapter Summary

The environment keyword, its associated url, and the concept of dynamic environments are foundational for effective deployment tracking and management in GitLab CI/CD.
