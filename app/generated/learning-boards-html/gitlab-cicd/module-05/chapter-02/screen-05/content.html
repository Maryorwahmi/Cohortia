Definition

Introducing the environment Keyword

Unlock GitLab's environment management features by declaring deployment jobs.

What it Does
  • • Declares a job as a deployment to a specific environment.
  • • Activates GitLab's dedicated 'Environments' dashboard.
  • • Enables deployment history tracking and environment-specific variables.
Key Takeaway

The environment keyword is essential for GitLab to recognize and manage your deployment stages, providing crucial visibility and control.

Basic .gitlab-ci.yml Snippet
deploy_job:
  stage: deploy
  script:
    - echo "Deploying..."
  environment:
    name: staging # Declares the environment

Why it Matters

Without environment, GitLab treats your deployment job like any other, missing out on critical tracking and management features. It's the bridge to full CI/CD visibility.
