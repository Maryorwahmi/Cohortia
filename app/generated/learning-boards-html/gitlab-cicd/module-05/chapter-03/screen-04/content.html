Why Manual or Scheduled?

Continuous deployment is powerful, but certain scenarios demand a more controlled approach. This ensures stability and compliance.

Critical Production Deployments

Require final human review or approval before pushing changes live to prevent high-impact errors.

Scheduled Maintenance Windows

Tasks like data synchronization, backups, or system cleanups that need to run at specific, regular intervals.

Key Takeaway

Manual and scheduled deployments provide essential flexibility, allowing teams to balance automation with necessary human oversight and precise timing for critical or routine operations.

When to Choose Controlled Deployments

Manual Trigger

For final approvals, sensitive production releases, or coordinated rollouts.

Scheduled Run

For recurring tasks like backups, reports, or nightly staging deployments.

Environment Protection

To restrict who can deploy to critical environments, adding a vital security layer.
