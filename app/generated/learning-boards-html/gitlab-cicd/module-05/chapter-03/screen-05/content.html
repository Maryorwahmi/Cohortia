The `when: manual` Keyword

The when: manual keyword in your .gitlab-ci.yml job definition pauses a job, requiring a user to explicitly trigger its execution.

Purpose: Human Approval

Ideal for critical deployments (e.g., production) where a final human review or approval is mandatory.

Pipeline Behavior

The job appears paused in the pipeline with a 'play' button, awaiting manual trigger by an authorized user.

Key Takeaway

Manual jobs act as essential gates, ensuring human oversight for sensitive operations and preventing unintended deployments.

Manual Deployment Flow

Automated Stages Complete

Build and test jobs run successfully.

Manual Job Paused

Deployment job awaits human trigger.

User Triggers Job

Authorized user clicks 'play' button.

Deployment Initiated

Job executes, deploying changes.
