Key Concepts (Continued)

Further essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Protected Branches

GitLab feature restricting who can push to or merge into specific branches, often used for main or release.

Protected Environments

GitLab feature restricting which users or groups can deploy to specific environments (e.g., production).

Deployment Gates

Strategic points in the pipeline (manual triggers, permissions) where conditions must be met before deployment.

Summary Insight

Protected branches and environments, combined with deployment gates, create a robust, layered security model for controlling access and ensuring the integrity of your CI/CD process.
