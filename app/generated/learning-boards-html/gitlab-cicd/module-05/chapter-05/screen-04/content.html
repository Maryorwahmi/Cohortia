Kubernetes: The Orchestrator

Automating container deployment, scaling, and management.

Why Kubernetes?

  • •Automates container deployment and updates.
  • •Manages scaling of applications based on demand.
  • •Ensures high availability and self-healing.
  • •Simplifies management of complex microservices.
Analogy

Think of Kubernetes as the 'operating system for your data center,' managing resources and workloads across a cluster of machines.

Key Takeaway

Kubernetes is essential for automating the lifecycle of containerized applications, providing scalability and resilience.
