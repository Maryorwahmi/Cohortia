Vulnerability Triage and Remediation Strategies

Establishing a process to review, prioritize, and fix security findings effectively.

Review

Understand the vulnerability, its description, severity, and potential impact.

Prioritize

Rank findings based on severity, exploitability, and business impact.

Remediate

Fix code, update dependencies, or adjust configurations to resolve the vulnerability.

Dismiss/Justify

Document false positives or acceptable risks with clear justifications in GitLab.

Key Takeaway

A structured triage process is essential for efficiently managing and resolving security vulnerabilities, ensuring that critical issues are addressed promptly and all findings are properly documented.

The Vulnerability Triage Loop

A continuous cycle of identification and resolution.

1. Detect

Scans identify vulnerabilities.

2. Triage

Review, prioritize, and plan action.

3. Remediate

Implement fixes or justify dismissal.
