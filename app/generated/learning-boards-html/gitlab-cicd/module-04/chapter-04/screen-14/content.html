Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Performance Testing and Quality Gates (Part 2)

Quality Gate (Advanced)

Criteria that must be met before a pipeline can proceed to the next stage.

Manual Job

A CI/CD job that requires a user to manually trigger its execution.

Protected Branch

Restricts who can push to or merge into specific branches.

Summary

Advanced quality gates, manual approvals, and environment protections are crucial for secure and controlled deployments in GitLab CI/CD.
