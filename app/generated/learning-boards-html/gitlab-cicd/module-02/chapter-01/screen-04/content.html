Core Concept

The Heart of CI/CD: .gitlab-ci.yml

Your Project's Automation Blueprint

What is it?

  • A single YAML file at the root of your project repository.
  • Defines your entire CI/CD pipeline, from build to deploy.
  • Instructs GitLab Runner on tasks, timing, and environment.

Why it Matters

It's the cornerstone of effective CI/CD, ensuring consistent, automated, and reliable software delivery. It standardizes processes across your team.

Real-World Connection

Imagine a team of developers. Without this file, each developer might use different build commands or deployment steps, leading to 'it works on my machine' issues. The .gitlab-ci.yml ensures everyone follows the same automated process.

Key Takeaway

The .gitlab-ci.yml file is the single, authoritative source for defining and automating your entire CI/CD pipeline in GitLab.
