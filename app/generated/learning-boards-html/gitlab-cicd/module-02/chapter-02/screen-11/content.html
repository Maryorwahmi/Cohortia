Flexible Job Control with the `rules` Keyword

Defining complex conditional logic for job inclusion in a pipeline.

`rules` Components

  • •

    `if`: A condition using CI/CD variables and logical operators (e.g., `$CI_COMMIT_BRANCH == "main"`).

  • •

    `when`: The action to take if the `if` condition matches (e.g., `on_success`, `manual`, `never`).

  • •

    Ordered Evaluation: Rules are evaluated sequentially; the first matching rule determines the job's behavior.

`rules` vs. `only`/`except`

`only`/`except`: Simple, single-condition filtering.

  • Less flexible for complex logic.
  • Can become cumbersome with many conditions.

`rules`: Powerful, multi-condition logic with explicit actions.

  • Combines `if` and `when` for precise control.
  • Preferred for complex and maintainable pipelines.
Key Takeaway

`rules` offers a highly flexible and expressive way to define complex conditional logic for job execution, combining `if` conditions with `when` actions.
