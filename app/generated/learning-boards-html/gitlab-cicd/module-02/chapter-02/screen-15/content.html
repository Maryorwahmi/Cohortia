Key Concepts: Job Control & Conditions

Advanced keywords for precise job execution and conditional logic.

`needs` keyword

Allows explicit definition of job dependencies, overriding default stage-based sequential execution.

`only` keyword

Specifies the conditions (e.g., branches, tags) under which a job *should* be included in the pipeline.

`except` keyword

Specifies the conditions under which a job *should not* be included in the pipeline.

`rules` keyword

A more flexible and powerful alternative to `only`/`except` for defining complex conditional logic (`if`, `when`) for job inclusion.

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging `needs`, `only`, `except`, and `rules` enables precise control over job execution, optimizing pipeline efficiency and reliability.
