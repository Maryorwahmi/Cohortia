Introduction to Multi-Stage Pipelines

Organizing complex CI/CD workflows into manageable, sequential phases.

Why Multi-Stage?

  • •

    Complexity Management: Break down large, intricate processes into smaller, more digestible parts.

  • •

    Sequential Flow: Ensure tasks like 'build' complete before 'test', and 'test' before 'deploy'.

  • •

    Improved Debugging: Isolate issues to specific stages, making troubleshooting faster.

  • •

    Clearer Overview: Provides a high-level view of the entire CI/CD process, enhancing team understanding.

Pipeline Stages: A Conceptual Flow

Build
Test
Deploy

Each stage groups related jobs and executes sequentially.

Key Takeaway

Multi-stage pipelines are crucial for organizing complex CI/CD workflows into logical, sequential phases, improving clarity and manageability.
