Granular Control with the `needs` Keyword

Defining explicit job dependencies to override default stage-based execution.

Why use `needs`?

  • •

    Override Stage Order: Allows jobs to start even if their stage hasn't officially begun, as long as their dependencies are met.

  • •

    Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG): Creates a custom dependency graph, optimizing execution flow.

  • •

    Faster Pipelines: Reduces unnecessary waiting by only depending on specific prerequisite jobs, not entire stages.

  • •

    Complex Workflows: Essential for scenarios where jobs have intricate interdependencies across stages.

Key Takeaway

The `needs` keyword allows you to define explicit job dependencies, optimizing pipeline flow by starting jobs as soon as their specific prerequisites are met, regardless of stage order.
