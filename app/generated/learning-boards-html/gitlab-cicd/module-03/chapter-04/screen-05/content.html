Key Feature

Modularizing with `include`

Breaking down `.gitlab-ci.yml` into reusable parts.

The `include` Keyword

Imports external YAML files into your main `.gitlab-ci.yml`, allowing for a modular pipeline structure.

Benefits of Modularity

  • Cleaner, more readable main pipeline file.
  • Promotes consistency by reusing common patterns.
  • Simplifies maintenance with single-source updates.
Key Takeaway

`include` is the foundation for building scalable and maintainable CI/CD pipelines by enabling the breakdown of configurations into logical, reusable components.
