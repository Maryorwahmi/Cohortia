Core Concept

The Challenge of Pipeline Duplication

Why repeating configuration is a maintenance headache.

The Problem: Repetitive Code

Copying and pasting similar job definitions or entire stages across multiple projects or within the same `.gitlab-ci.yml` file.

The Consequence: Maintenance Nightmares

Updates become cumbersome, requiring changes in multiple locations, leading to inconsistencies and errors.

Key Takeaway

Duplicated CI/CD configurations violate the DRY principle, making pipelines fragile and difficult to scale. Modularization is key.

Duplication Impact

Increased Error Risk: More places to change means more opportunities for mistakes.

Time-Consuming Updates: Every change requires modifying multiple files, slowing down development.

Inconsistent Pipelines: Different versions of the 'same' logic can lead to unpredictable behavior.

Scalability Issues: Hard to manage as the number of projects and pipelines grows.
