Structuring for Reusability

Common patterns for effective modular pipeline design.

Shared Stages/Jobs

Abstract common `lint`, `test`, or `deploy` stages into shared files for consistency across projects.

Environment Variables

Define variables for different environments (dev, prod) in separate included files and include them conditionally.

Job Templates

Create hidden jobs (e.g., `.docker_build_template`) with common setup, then `extend` them in specific jobs.

Key Takeaway

By abstracting common patterns into reusable components, you enforce the DRY principle, making your pipelines more adaptable, maintainable, and scalable.
