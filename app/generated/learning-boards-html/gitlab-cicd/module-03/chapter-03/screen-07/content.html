What are `rules`?

Flexible Conditional Logic

A list of expressions that evaluate to `true` or `false`, dictating job execution. Far more powerful than `only`/`except`.

Key Mechanism

Sequential Evaluation

Rules are processed from top to bottom. The first rule that matches (evaluates to `true`) determines the job's behavior, and subsequent rules are ignored.

Key Takeaway

`rules` provide granular control, and understanding their top-to-bottom evaluation order is crucial for predictable pipeline behavior.
