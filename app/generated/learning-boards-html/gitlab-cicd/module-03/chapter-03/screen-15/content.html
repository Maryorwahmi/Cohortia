Key Concepts Continued

Advanced terms for robust pipelines

`rules:changes`

Triggers a job if specific files or directories have been modified in the commit, optimizing resource usage.

`rules:exists`

Triggers a job if specific files or directories are present in the repository, adapting to project structure.

`rules:when` & `allow_failure`

`when` defines job behavior (e.g., `manual`), and `allow_failure` allows jobs to fail without blocking the pipeline.

Chapter Summary

By combining these advanced rule conditions and attributes, you can construct highly resilient, efficient, and context-aware CI/CD pipelines that meet complex project requirements.
