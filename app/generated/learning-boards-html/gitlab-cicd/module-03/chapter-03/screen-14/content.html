Key Concepts Revisited

Essential terms for conditional job execution

`only` / `except`

Legacy keywords for basic conditional job execution based on branches, tags, or file paths. Mutually exclusive and less flexible.

`rules`

Modern and powerful keyword for defining complex conditional logic. Rules are evaluated sequentially from top to bottom.

`rules:if`

A versatile condition within `rules` that evaluates boolean expressions using CI/CD variables, comparison, and logical operators.

Chapter Summary

Mastering conditional job execution with `rules` and understanding the limitations of `only`/`except` is key to building efficient, flexible, and robust GitLab CI/CD pipelines.
