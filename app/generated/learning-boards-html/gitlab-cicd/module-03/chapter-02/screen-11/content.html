Troubleshooting Artifacts and Dependencies

Best practices for robust and efficient pipelines

Common Pitfalls

  • •Missing expire_in: Leads to excessive storage usage and potential cost increases.
  • •dependencies vs. needs Misuse: Using dependencies when needs would provide better parallelization, or vice-versa.
  • •Large Artifacts: Transferring unnecessarily large files can significantly slow down pipelines.
  • •Over-complication: Overusing needs in simple pipelines where implicit stage order is clearer.
Key Takeaway

Proactively manage artifact expiration, choose the correct dependency keyword, and optimize artifact size to prevent common pipeline inefficiencies and errors.

Best Practices

Define Expiration

Always include expire_in for artifacts to manage storage and keep your GitLab instance lean.

Choose Wisely

Evaluate whether dependencies or needs is appropriate for your specific job relationships and desired parallelization.

Optimize Artifact Size

Only save essential files as artifacts. Minimize their size to reduce transfer times and improve pipeline speed.
