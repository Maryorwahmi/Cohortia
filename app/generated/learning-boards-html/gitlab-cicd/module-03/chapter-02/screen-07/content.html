When dependencies Limits Parallelization

The challenge of strict stage order in complex pipelines

The Stage Order Constraint

The dependencies keyword operates within the strict, sequential execution of pipeline stages. All jobs in a stage must complete before any job in the next stage can begin.

  • •Linear Progression: Jobs are forced to wait for previous stages, even if their specific dependencies are met earlier.
  • •Limited Parallelization: This strict order prevents jobs in different stages from running concurrently, even if they are logically independent.
  • •Inefficiency: Can lead to longer overall pipeline execution times, especially in complex projects.
Key Takeaway

While dependencies refines artifact transfer, it cannot overcome the inherent sequential nature of stage-based execution, limiting true parallelization.

Scenario: Linear Bottleneck

Jobs A, B, C across Stages

Job_C (Stage C) needs Job_A's (Stage A) artifact. Job_B (Stage B) also needs Job_A's artifact. Even if Job_C could run in parallel with Job_B, it must wait for Stage B to complete first.

Impact: Slower Feedback

This forced sequential execution means developers receive feedback on later stages much slower than necessary, hindering rapid iteration and deployment.
