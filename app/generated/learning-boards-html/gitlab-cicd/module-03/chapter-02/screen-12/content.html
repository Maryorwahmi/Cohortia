Key Concepts: Data Flow & Configuration

Essential terms from this chapter

Artifacts

Files or directories generated by a job, saved and made available for subsequent jobs or download.

artifacts keyword

Used in .gitlab-ci.yml to define which files/directories to save as artifacts and their properties.

expire_in

An artifact setting that defines how long artifacts should be retained before automatic deletion.

Chapter Insight

Mastering artifact management is fundamental for efficient data flow and storage optimization in your CI/CD pipelines.
