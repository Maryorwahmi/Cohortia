Variable Precedence: Who Wins?

Understanding the Order of Overrides

Precedence Order (Highest to Lowest)

  1. API Variables: Passed when triggering a pipeline.
  2. Job-level `.gitlab-ci.yml` Variables: Defined within a specific job.
  3. Global `.gitlab-ci.yml` Variables: Defined at the top level of the CI/CD file.
  4. Project Variables: Configured in Project Settings > CI/CD > Variables.
  5. Group Variables: Configured in Group Settings > CI/CD > Variables.
  6. Predefined CI/CD Variables: Automatically provided by GitLab (Lowest).

Common Mistake: Overriding Confusion

A frequent pitfall is defining a variable at a higher level (e.g., project) and expecting it to take precedence over a more specific definition (e.g., job-level). Remember, the most specific definition always wins.

If `DB_HOST` is 'dev.db' at project level, but 'prod.db' at job level, the job will use 'prod.db'.

Key Takeaway

Always consider the scope of your variable definitions; a variable defined at a more granular level, such as within a specific job, will override any broader definitions like project or group variables.
