Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from This Chapter

`variables` Keyword

Used in `.gitlab-ci.yml` to define variables globally or per job, providing contextual configuration.

`environment` Keyword

Defines a deployment environment for a job, enabling tracking, dynamic environments, and review apps.

Dynamic Environments

Temporary environments created for specific branches (e.g., feature branches) that can be automatically provisioned and de-provisioned.

Summary Takeaway

The `variables` and `environment` keywords are central to creating dynamic, trackable, and reviewable deployment processes, enabling flexible and efficient CI/CD workflows.
