Runner Optimization

Dedicated Runners and GitLab.com Resilience

Balancing control, security, and managed resilience.

Dedicated Runners

Always available for critical pipelines, preventing resource contention.

GitLab.com Shared Runners

Managed by GitLab, offering inherent resilience and reduced operational overhead.

Control vs. Convenience

Self-managed offers control; shared offers convenience. Choose based on needs.

Security & Compliance

Self-managed runners are preferred for strict environment and security control.

Key Takeaway

The choice between dedicated self-managed runners and GitLab.com shared runners depends on your project's criticality, security requirements, and desired level of operational control, each offering distinct resilience benefits.

Example: Production Deployment

For a critical production deployment, you might configure a specific tag for dedicated runners (e.g., `production-deploy`). Only runners with this tag would pick up the production deployment job, ensuring that these high-priority tasks are executed on isolated, well-resourced, and consistently available infrastructure, preventing delays from other, less critical pipeline jobs.

Trade-off: Operational Overhead

While self-managed runners offer greater control and customization for resilience, they also introduce operational overhead. You are responsible for their provisioning, maintenance, scaling, and security. GitLab.com shared runners, conversely, offload this responsibility, but you trade some control for convenience and managed resilience.
