Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The process of preparing for and recovering from a disaster that affects IT systems, ensuring business continuity.

GitLab Backup

Backing up critical GitLab components (database, repositories, artifacts, configurations) for full recovery.

GitLab Runners Resilience

Ensuring continuous availability and fault tolerance of the agents that execute CI/CD jobs.

Summary Takeaway

A resilient CI/CD system relies on comprehensive disaster recovery planning, robust GitLab instance backups, and highly available GitLab Runners to ensure uninterrupted software delivery.
