Core Concept

The Importance of Disaster Recovery for CI/CD

Minimizing downtime and ensuring business continuity.

Unforeseen Issues

Infrastructure failures or accidental misconfigurations can halt pipelines.

Minimize Downtime

A robust DR plan ensures quick recovery and reduces operational impact.

Prevent Data Loss

Safeguard critical CI/CD history, configurations, and artifacts.

Ensure Continuity

Maintain uninterrupted development and deployment processes.

Key Takeaway

Disaster recovery for CI/CD is not optional; it's a fundamental requirement for stable and reliable software delivery, protecting against both minor glitches and major outages.

Scenario: GitLab Server Failure

Imagine your primary GitLab server experiences a critical hardware failure. Without a DR plan, your entire CI/CD workflow halts, impacting all projects and deployments. A well-defined plan ensures you can quickly restore service from backups, minimizing the impact on your team and business operations.

Common Misconception

Many believe disaster recovery is only for production applications. However, the systems that build and deploy those applications (like GitLab CI/CD) are equally critical. Their failure can have a cascading effect on your entire software delivery lifecycle, making their resilience paramount.
