Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

High Availability (HA)

A system design approach ensuring high operational performance and uptime for a given period.

Idempotency

The property of an operation that produces the same result regardless of how many times it is executed.

Rollback Strategy

A plan and mechanism to revert a deployed application to a previous, stable state in case of issues.

Summary Takeaway

Implementing high availability, designing idempotent deployment scripts, and establishing clear rollback strategies are crucial for building resilient CI/CD pipelines that ensure continuous and safe software delivery.
