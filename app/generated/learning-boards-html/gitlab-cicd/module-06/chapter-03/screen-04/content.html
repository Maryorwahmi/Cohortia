The Need for Pipeline Best Practices

Ensuring CI/CD Scales with Your Project

Why Pipelines Become Complex

  • •Growing project size and codebase.
  • •Expanding development teams and diverse workflows.
  • •Increased number of stages, jobs, and dependencies.
  • •Integration with more external services and tools.
Common Pitfalls

Without best practices, pipelines become unwieldy, slow, error-prone, and difficult to maintain, hindering development velocity.

Key Takeaway

Proactive adoption of best practices is crucial for building scalable, maintainable, and efficient CI/CD pipelines that support project growth.

Initial Pipeline

Simple, clear, easy to manage.

Project Growth

More features, more developers, more complexity.

Unmanaged Pipeline

Unwieldy, slow, error-prone, maintenance nightmare.
