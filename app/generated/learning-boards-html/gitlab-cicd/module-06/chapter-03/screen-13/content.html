Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways for Pipeline Optimization

DRY Principle

A core development principle to reduce repetition of information and code, crucial for maintainable pipelines.

extends Keyword

Allows a job to inherit properties from another job, centralizing common configurations and promoting reuse within a pipeline.

include Keyword

Imports external .gitlab-ci.yml files from other projects, local paths, or GitLab templates, enabling widespread configuration reuse.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering DRY principles with extends and include is fundamental for creating clean, consistent, and easily maintainable GitLab CI/CD pipelines.
