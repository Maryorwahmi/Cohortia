The Importance of Runner Security

GitLab Runners are the agents that execute your CI/CD jobs. A compromised runner can provide attackers with direct access to your code, credentials, and infrastructure.

Runner as a Target

Attackers can exploit vulnerabilities in runners to gain access to the execution environment and sensitive data.

Access to Secrets

If a runner is compromised, any credentials or resources available to it become vulnerable.

Why Runner Security is Critical

The integrity of your entire CI/CD pipeline hinges on the security of your runners. They are the last line of defense before deployment.

  • •
    Code Integrity: Prevents malicious code injection during the build process.
  • •
    Credential Protection: Safeguards sensitive API keys and deployment credentials.
  • •
    Production Environment Safety: Protects against unauthorized deployments or access to production systems.
Key Takeaway

Secure your GitLab Runners with the same vigilance as your production servers; they are a critical component of your security posture.
