The Challenge of Secrets in CI/CD

Automated pipelines often require access to sensitive credentials. Storing these insecurely is a major vulnerability.

Don't Commit Secrets!

Never hardcode API keys, passwords, or tokens directly into your .gitlab-ci.yml or commit them to your repository.

Risk of Exposure

Secrets committed to Git history are permanently exposed, even if later removed, and can be accessed by anyone with repository access.

GitLab CI/CD Variables: The Solution

GitLab CI/CD variables provide a secure, out-of-band mechanism to store and manage sensitive data for your pipelines.

  • •
    Centralized Storage: Define variables at the project or group level, accessible to all pipelines within that scope.
  • •
    Separation of Concerns: Keep secrets distinct from your codebase, improving security and maintainability.
  • •
    Environment Injection: Variables are securely injected into the runner's environment at job execution time.
Key Takeaway

Always use GitLab CI/CD variables for any sensitive data required by your pipeline jobs to prevent direct exposure in your repository.
