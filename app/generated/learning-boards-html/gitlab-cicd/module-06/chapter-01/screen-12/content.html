Best Practices

A Systematic Approach to Troubleshooting

Incremental Changes for Efficient Problem Solving

1

Small, Incremental Changes

Modify one aspect of your .gitlab-ci.yml at a time to isolate variables.

2

Commit Frequently

Create a clear history of changes, making it easy to track and revert if necessary.

3

Observe & Evaluate

Run the pipeline after each change and carefully analyze the job logs for new insights.

4

Isolate the Problem

Use the information gained from each step to narrow down the potential causes of the failure.

Key Takeaway

A systematic, iterative approach with small changes and frequent commits is the most efficient way to diagnose and resolve complex pipeline issues.
