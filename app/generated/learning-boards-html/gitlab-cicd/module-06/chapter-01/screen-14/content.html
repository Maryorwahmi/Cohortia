Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

What to Remember from This Chapter

Job Artifacts

Files or directories produced by a job and stored by GitLab, useful for inspection, reporting, or passing data between jobs.

Retry Mechanism

The ability to re-run a failed job or pipeline, useful for transient issues but not a substitute for root cause analysis.

Diagnostic Commands

Commands like `pwd`, `ls`, `env` added to scripts to output environment information for debugging purposes.

Chapter Takeaway

Leveraging job artifacts, understanding the retry mechanism, and employing diagnostic commands are crucial for advanced troubleshooting and maintaining robust CI/CD pipelines.
