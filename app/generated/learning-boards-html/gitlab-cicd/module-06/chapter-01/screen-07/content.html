Leveraging Job Artifacts

Critical Context for Debugging and Reporting

What are Artifacts?

Files or directories generated by a job and stored by GitLab for later access.

Examples

Compiled binaries, test reports (e.g., JUnit XML), coverage reports, logs, screenshots.

Debugging Aid

Download and inspect artifacts to get granular details, like specific test failures, without re-running jobs.

Artifacts: Your Job's Output Repository

Configure your .gitlab-ci.yml to save critical files like test reports or compiled assets. These become invaluable for post-execution analysis and debugging.

Key Takeaway

Job artifacts provide a persistent record of job outputs, offering deep insights for debugging and ensuring data availability across the pipeline.
