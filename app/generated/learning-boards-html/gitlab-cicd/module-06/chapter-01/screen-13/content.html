Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

What to Remember from This Chapter

Pipeline Statuses

Indicators like `pending`, `running`, `success`, `failed` that show the current state of a pipeline or job.

Job Logs

Detailed output generated by a CI/CD job, containing all executed commands and their results, crucial for debugging.

Pipeline Graph

A visual representation of jobs, stages, and their dependencies, providing an overview of pipeline execution flow.

Chapter Takeaway

Mastering pipeline statuses, job logs, and the pipeline graph provides the foundational understanding needed to effectively monitor and diagnose CI/CD issues.
