Core Concept

Decoding Pipeline Statuses

Instant Feedback on Your CI/CD Workflow

PendingWaiting

Job is queued, awaiting an available runner.

RunningIn Progress

Job is actively executing on a runner.

SuccessCompleted

All commands executed successfully.

FailedError

A command exited with a non-zero code.

Pending / Running

Pipeline in progress.

Success

All tasks completed.

Failed

Issue detected, requires action.

Canceled / Skipped / Manual

Other states with specific meanings.

Key Takeaway

Pipeline statuses are your first line of defense, providing immediate feedback to guide your troubleshooting efforts.
