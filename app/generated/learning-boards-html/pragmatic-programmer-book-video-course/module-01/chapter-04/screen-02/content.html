Learning Objectives
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Recognize communication as a skill
You will see communication as an engineering practice that affects maintenance and velocity. Practice: treat a README like an interface contract.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Know your audience
Choose vocabulary and depth based on who will read or hear your message. Example: explain impact and timeline to product owners, internals to engineers.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Write meaningful comments and commits
Learn to capture the "why" and context for decisions, and to format commit messages that tell the story of change.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Practice active listening
Use paraphrasing and clarifying questions to reduce assumptions and focus work where it matters most.
Verified Competency Milestone
