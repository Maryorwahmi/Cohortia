DRY
One authoritative representation for each piece of knowledge.
Duplication types
Code, logical, data, knowledge, and process duplication require different fixes.
Refactor safely
Use tests and incremental extraction to keep behavior identical while improving design.
Summary Takeaway

Apply DRY pragmatically: centralize knowledge when it reduces maintenance cost, and support changes with tests and clear naming.
