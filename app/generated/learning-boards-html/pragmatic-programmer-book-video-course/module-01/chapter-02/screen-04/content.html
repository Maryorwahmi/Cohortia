What DRY means

Every piece of knowledge must have a single, unambiguous, authoritative representation within a system.

Authoritative representation: a constant, function, or service that carries the concept.
Example: TAX_RATE and calculate_tax used by both checkout and invoices.
Misconception: DRY is not just about avoiding copy-paste; it covers knowledge and intent.
Concrete example
# Centralized tax
TAX_RATE = 0.08

def calculate_tax(amount):
  return amount * TAX_RATE

# Now checkout and invoice call calculate_tax
Key Takeaway

Pick clear, testable locations for authoritative knowledge so changes are predictable.
