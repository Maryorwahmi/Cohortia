Learn

Understand
Identify different forms of duplication

Recognize code, logical, data, knowledge, and process duplication; learn practical signs to look for during code review.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Apply
Refactor duplication safely

Practice extracting functions or centralizing configuration with tests that keep behavior identical while improving maintainability.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Master
Design for adaptability and reduce technical debt

Learn when to centralize logic into services or configuration, and when simpler local solutions are acceptable to avoid premature abstraction.
