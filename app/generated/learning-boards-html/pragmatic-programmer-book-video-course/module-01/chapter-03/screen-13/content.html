Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Assessment: Choose the Pragmatic Approach
Scenario 1

Integrating a complex third-party API with unknown real-time performance and deployment quirks.

Best pragmatic approach

Implement a tracer bullet inside your app and deploy to staging to validate integration, latency, and failure modes.

Scenario 2

A quick script proves CSV export is possible but lacks error handling and UI integration.

Best pragmatic approach

Treat the script as a prototype, extract lessons, and reimplement a robust exporter with tests and integration points.

Reflective question

For your next risky integration, would a prototype or a tracer bullet reduce the most uncertainty? Why?
