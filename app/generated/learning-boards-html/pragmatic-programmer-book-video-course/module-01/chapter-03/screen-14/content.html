Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Tracer Bullet

Minimal end-to-end slice used to validate architecture, integrations, and deployment.

Prototype

A throwaway experiment to learn about feasibility. Reimplement findings for production.

Modularity

Design components with clear interfaces so they can be replaced or extended independently.

Final takeaway

Validate the riskiest assumptions earliest, choose the smallest experiment that answers them, and design components to accept future change.
