Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Broken Windows
Claim

Broken windows cause cultural decline in codebases

Small visible problems change expectations. Developers adapt to the visible norm and assume imperfect code is acceptable.

Concrete example
A utility file with inconsistent formatting, unclear names, and no docstring invites quick fixes that accumulate into technical debt.
Common misconception
Small issues are harmless if they don't crash production. In reality they change team norms and raise maintenance cost.
How to respond
  1. Notice a small issue while working in a file.
  2. Evaluate the cost: simple rename or small refactor often wins.
  3. Make a focused change, test, and write a clear commit message.
Key Takeaway

Repair small, visible defects when cost is low to prevent cultural decay and technical debt.
