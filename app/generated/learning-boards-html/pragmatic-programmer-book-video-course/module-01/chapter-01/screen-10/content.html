Identify and Repair a "Broken Window"

Practice a small, focused refactor and communicate it clearly.

Step 1 — Inspect
List three issues you see in the starter script.
Step 2 — Pick one to fix
Choose the smallest safe change that improves clarity or robustness.
Step 3 — Commit & explain
Write a commit message that explains the why and the expected benefit.
Example commit
"Introduce PRICE_MULTIPLIER constant to replace literal 1.15 and clarify pricing rule; update numeric parsing and add docstring."
Avoid
Do not bundle unrelated cleanups in the same commit; keep changes focused for easier review.
