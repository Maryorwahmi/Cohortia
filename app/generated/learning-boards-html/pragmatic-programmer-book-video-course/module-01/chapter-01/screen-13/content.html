Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
Repair small things
Small fixes compound and change team expectations for the better.
Own & propose fixes
Name the impact and suggest a safe path to resolve it, offering help when needed.
Prefer small refactors
Many focused changes are less risky and more likely to improve the codebase than rare large rewrites.
Final Action

Pick one small repair in your recent work, implement it, and write a short commit message explaining the why.
