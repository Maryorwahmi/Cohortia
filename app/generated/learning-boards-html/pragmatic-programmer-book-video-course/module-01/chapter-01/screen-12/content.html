Broken Windows
Small visible defects encourage more defects; repair early when cost is low.
Ownership
Take responsibility for code quality and team outcomes; own fixes and learning.
Proactive Practices
Small refactors, named constants, and tests stop debt and make change safer.
Takeaway

Practical responsibility is small, consistent, and communicative: one fix today avoids many tomorrow.
