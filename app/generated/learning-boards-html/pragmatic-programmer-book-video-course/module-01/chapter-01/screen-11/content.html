Review scenario

Choose a response that protects code health and preserves team momentum.

Best practice answer
Communicate the issue with facts, show impact, propose a quick fix, and offer to implement or pair on it. Decide with the author whether to fix before merge or add a follow-up commit.
Why not ignore or block?
Ignoring allows decay. Blocking without collaboration causes friction. A constructive offer balances quality and delivery needs.
Key Takeaway
Be pragmatic: protect system health through focused help and collaborative fixes rather than blame or silence.
