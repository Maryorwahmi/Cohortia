Ownership language

State the symptom, describe the impact, and suggest a fix. Offer to help implement or investigate further.

Blaming
"You broke the build."
Ownership
"Test test_login_failure failed after the recent commit to feature-X. It appears authenticate_user now expects a different parameter type. I can help investigate or propose a small fix to adapt the call."
Why it matters
  • Reduces defensiveness and speeds resolution.
  • Keeps focus on system health rather than personal fault.
  • Builds trust and collaborative norms over time.
Pro tip
When in doubt, include the failing test name and a brief suggested next step to make feedback actionable.
