Continuous Integration & Deployment
Automate builds, tests, and deployments so releases are frequent and low risk.

This chapter focuses on automating the software delivery lifecycle so teams get rapid feedback and fewer surprises. We emphasize small changes, reliable tests, and reproducible builds.

You will see a minimal GitHub Actions workflow, a hands-on Node.js lab, and practical safeguards such as environment isolation and rollbacks.
