Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Delivery vs Deployment
Continuous Delivery
Software is always in a deployable state; production deployment is a manual step or single click.
  • Automated builds and tests prepare artifacts for release.
  • Manual approval provides a human gate for coordination.
  • Useful when business timing or extra checks are required.
Example
Deploy to staging automatically; production requires a release click by the manager.
Continuous Deployment
Every change that passes automated pipelines is automatically deployed to production without human action.
  • Requires strong automated tests and monitoring.
  • Makes releases frequent and nearly invisible.
  • Best for low-risk, high-velocity teams with strong safety nets.
Example
Passing changes flow through staging and then to production automatically.
Practical guidance
Choose Continuous Delivery when you need human coordination or extra business checks. Choose Continuous Deployment when automated tests, feature flags, and monitoring give you confidence to release automatically. A hybrid strategy is common: automate everything up to a promotion, and automate production later once confidence grows.
