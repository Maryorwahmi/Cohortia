Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Key Concepts
Continuous Integration (CI)
Automated builds and tests on frequent merges to find integration issues early.
Continuous Delivery (CD)
Every change is prepared and ready to deploy; production promotion is a manual click.
Continuous Deployment (CDep)
Passing changes are automatically deployed to production without human intervention.
Summary Takeaway

These terms describe increasing automation and confidence. Choose the level that matches your testing maturity and business risk tolerance.
