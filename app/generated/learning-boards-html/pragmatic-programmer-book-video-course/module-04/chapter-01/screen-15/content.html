Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › What to Remember
Staging
Prepare exact changes for each commit so history is meaningful and revertable.
Branching
Use short-lived feature branches to isolate work and enable safe reviews.
Remotes
Push and pull to share and receive work. Coordinate before rewriting remote history.
Quick checklist

Before pushing: stage deliberately, write a descriptive message, pull the latest, and ensure your branch is ready to share.
