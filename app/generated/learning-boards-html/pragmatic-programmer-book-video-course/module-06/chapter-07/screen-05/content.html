Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Translating technical concepts
Process

Four-step translation

  1. Outcome sentence: State the practical change.
  2. Analogy: Use a relatable comparison to build intuition.
  3. Metric/impact: One measurable benefit or risk.
  4. Call to action: What you want the audience to decide or do next.
Example
Outcome: move to cloud to reduce costs and improve reliability. Analogy: old engine vs. modern engine. Metric: reduce idle server cost and downtime. Action: approve migration pilot.
Supporting tips
  • Drop acronyms or explain them once in plain terms.
  • Prefer one strong analogy over multiple weak ones.
  • Always end with a clear ask or next step for that audience.
Check your understanding
Ask a stakeholder to paraphrase the decision or action you proposed. If they can, your translation worked.
