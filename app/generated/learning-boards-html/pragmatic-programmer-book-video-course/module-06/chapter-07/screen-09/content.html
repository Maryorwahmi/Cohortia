Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Presentations
Preparation
  • Know your audience and their decisions.
  • Define a single clear goal for the talk.
  • Build agenda → story → summary → ask.
Practice
Rehearse transitions and opening sentences; test with a stakeholder proxy.
Handling Q&A
Anticipate questions and prepare concise answers. If unsure, promise and commit to a follow-up. Keep answers focused on business impact and decisions whenever possible.
Common mistake
Overloading slides with details instead of preparing a small set of clear decision points.
