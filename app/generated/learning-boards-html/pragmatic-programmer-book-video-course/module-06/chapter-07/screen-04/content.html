Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Why tailor your communication?
Definition

Audience Segmentation

Identify groups who will receive your message and choose the right level of detail and motivation for each.

Why it matters
Targeted messages reduce follow-ups and align decisions quickly.
Quick exercise
Write one sentence: what action should each audience take after your message?
Audience → Decision matrix
Engineers
Implementation details, constraints, APIs.
Product
Scope, user impact, trade-offs.
Executives
Business impact, cost, and ask.
Common mistake
Assuming everyone values the same detail level; this leads to either overload or missing information.
