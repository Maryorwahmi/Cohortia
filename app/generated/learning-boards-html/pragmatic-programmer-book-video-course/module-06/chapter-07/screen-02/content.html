Learning Objectives

Each objective pairs a skill with a small verification milestone.

01
Audience Tailoring
Select detail and tone for different stakeholders.

Skill: classify audiences and choose one communication goal. Why it matters: helps stakeholders act without overload.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Translate Technical Concepts
Use analogies and outcome-focused language.

Skill: convert a technical description into a one-paragraph business explanation. Why it matters: decisions are driven by business benefits.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Active Listening
Paraphrase, ask clarifying questions, and avoid premature solutions.

Skill: practice paraphrase and one clarifying question. Why it matters: reveals the real problem behind suggested solutions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Concise Written Communication
Structure emails, reports, and documentation for decisions.

Skill: write an executive summary and an action-oriented email. Why it matters: clarity saves time and reduces misunderstandings.
