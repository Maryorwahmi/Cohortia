Technique

Paraphrase & Clarify

  1. Listen fully without interrupting.
  2. Paraphrase what you heard to confirm.
  3. Ask one clarifying question.
  4. Summarize the decision criteria.
Example scenario
Request: 'We need a new dashboard.'
Paraphrase: 'You want clearer insight into performance to make staffing decisions.' Clarifying question: 'Which decision would change if you had that view?'
Common mistake
Answering with a technical solution before understanding the decision criteria.
