Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Written Communication
Email best-practices
  • Subject: decision or ask in 8 words.
  • First sentence: summary of action requested.
  • Bullets: facts, impact, timeline, decision needed.
Example subject
"Approve migration pilot: cost, timeline, risk"
Report & docs
Structure reports with an executive summary, decisions, supporting analysis, and an appendix for technical details. Documentation should record context, options considered, chosen solution, and rationale.
Tip
Proofread and remove one paragraph to check for unnecessary detail.
