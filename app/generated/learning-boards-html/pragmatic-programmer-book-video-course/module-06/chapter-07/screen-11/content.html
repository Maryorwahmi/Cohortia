Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › CI Explanation Activity
Scenario
Explain Continuous Integration to a marketing team in 150–200 words, focusing on what it is and why it benefits marketing and the business.
Analogy
Continuous Integration is like checking each puzzle piece as it's made so pieces always fit when assembled.
Model explanation (summary)
CI automatically combines small changes and runs checks so new features integrate smoothly. For marketing this means faster, more reliable releases, fewer last-minute bugs, and predictable timelines for campaigns. As a result, launches are less risky and coordination between teams becomes simpler.
Practice prompt
Draft your 150–200 word explanation using the model: one-line summary, analogy, three benefits tied to marketing, and a closing reassurance about predictability.
