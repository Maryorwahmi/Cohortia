Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › NFRs and Architecture
NFR examples and architectural impacts
Performance target: adds caching layer, database tuning, CDN
Security: enforces authentication, encryption, access control
Scalability: triggers stateless service design and horizontal scaling
Pragmatic checklist
  1. Record NFRs as measurable targets.
  2. Evaluate architecture options against NFR metrics.
  3. Document trade-offs and revisit them when requirements change.
Misconception
Treating NFRs as optional details. In reality, they constrain choices and must be planned early.
Key Takeaway

Make NFRs explicit and measurable, then use them as criteria in architecture decisions.
