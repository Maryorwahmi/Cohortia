Non-Functional Requirements
Performance, security, reliability—write them as measurable targets.
Scope Creep
Prevent unapproved feature growth with a transparent change process and clear trade-offs.
Iterative Refinement
Deliver small increments, learn quickly, and refine requirements with real feedback.
Final takeaway
Use these concepts as a practical checklist during planning, design, and sprint reviews to keep work aligned with real user value.
