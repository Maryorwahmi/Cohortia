Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Common Pitfalls
Frequent mistakes
  • Assuming stakeholder intent instead of verifying it.
  • Not involving all relevant stakeholders early enough.
  • Overly detailed upfront specs that become obsolete.
  • Analysis paralysis—waiting for perfect requirements.
Pragmatic remedies
Teach-back: restate and confirm requirements with stakeholders.
Living docs: concise, updateable notes near the work.
Rapid prototypes to validate assumptions quickly.
Key Takeaway

Validate assumptions early with teach-back and prototypes; keep documentation concise and living.
