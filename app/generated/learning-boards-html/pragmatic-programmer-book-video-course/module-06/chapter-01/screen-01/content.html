Why this matters

Starting without the big picture is like building a house without blueprints. The work may look impressive but fail to house the real need. This lesson emphasizes the value of early discovery—asking why, observing users, and building quick prototypes—to align team decisions with business outcomes. A shared vision reduces rework and keeps the product focused on measurable benefits.
