Change evaluation process
Document the request with rationale and stakeholder.
Assess impact on schedule, budget, and scope.
Decide: approve, defer to backlog, or reject with explanation.
Key Takeaway

A simple, visible change process preserves focus and prevents cumulative feature expansion.

Practical tips
  • Say "not now" with a reason and add the request to a prioritized backlog.
  • Explain the trade-offs: new features cost time and increase risk.
  • Have a single touchpoint or owner who routes and evaluates new requests.
Example scenario
A marketing ask to add a promotional carousel arrives mid-sprint. Log it, estimate impact, and either schedule for the next sprint or get approval for scope change now.
