Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Why the 'Big Picture' Matters
Definition

The big picture is a concise, shared statement of who we serve, the problem to solve, and how success is measured.

One-sentence goal
Two measurable outcomes
Observation & prototype validation
Key Takeaway

Keep the big picture concise and testable so it guides priorities and reduces costly rework.

Concrete Example
A team thinks the problem is slow search. After observing users, they discover users instead struggle to discover new items. The new goal becomes increasing discovery events by 25 percent. A prototype of curated lists reveals faster success and avoids costly full-text indexing work.
Common misconception
Mistake: Treating a long spec as understanding. Reality: Specs become obsolete without discovery and validation.
Try this
Write one-sentence goal and two measurable outcomes. Run a 30-minute observation session to test the hypothesis.
