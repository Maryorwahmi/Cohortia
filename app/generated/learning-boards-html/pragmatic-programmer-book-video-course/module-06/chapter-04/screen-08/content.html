Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Delivering Difficult News
Process Flow

Structured conversation steps

A reliable script to share setbacks while keeping stakeholders engaged and informed.

Step 1: State the facts
What happened, when you noticed it, and the verified facts.
Step 2: Assess impact
Explain the effect on timeline, budget, and features honestly.
Step 3: Propose options
Offer two or three mitigation options with trade-offs and a recommendation.
Step 4: Request input
Invite stakeholders to choose or adjust an option and confirm next steps.
Key Takeaway

Bring options and a recommendation when delivering bad news; that keeps conversations productive.

Scripted example
"We've encountered complexity with the X API that increases integration time from five days to ten. Impact: target release is at risk. Options: extend release by five days; defer Feature Z; explore a simpler API for two days. We recommend deferring Feature Z to keep the release date. We'd like your input on that option."
Common mistake
Blaming individuals or hiding uncertainty. Focus on facts and options.
Pro tip
Prepare a short slide or one-page summary with the facts and options before the meeting to keep the discussion focused.
Check your ask
End with a clear decision request or next meeting to confirm the chosen path.
