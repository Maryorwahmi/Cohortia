Comparison

Burn-down vs Traffic-light

Choose the right visual for the audience and add a concise interpretation.

Burn-down chart (team)
Shows remaining work over time and predicts completion; useful for planning adjustments when the curve deviates from expectations.
Traffic-light (executive)
Green/yellow/red indicator with one-sentence context and recommended action keeps sponsors informed without technical detail.
Kanban board (flow)
Visualizes work in progress and identifies bottlenecks for operational improvements.
Key Takeaway

Always pair the visual with a short interpretation and an explicit ask or next step.

Example: progress update

"Burn-down shows remaining work is 30% higher than planned at sprint midpoint. We recommend adding a focused pair of engineers for two days, or deferring feature X to the next sprint."

Common mistake
Sharing raw charts without interpretation leaves decision-makers guessing what to do next.
Pro tip
Standardize your report cadence and format so stakeholders know what to expect.
Quick template
Status: Green/Yellow/Red. Impact: one sentence. Recommendation: one-option or three short options.
