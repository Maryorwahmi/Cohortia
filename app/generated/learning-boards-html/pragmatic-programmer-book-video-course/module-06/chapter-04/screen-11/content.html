Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Tailoring Communication
Comparison

Audience-specific reporting

Layer your communication for different stakeholder needs and end with a clear ask.

Executives
One-line status, impact, and recommended decision. Minimal technical detail.
Project managers
Schedule, dependencies, and suggested resource adjustments.
Technical team
Root cause, logs, and concrete remediation steps they can take immediately.
Key Takeaway

Layered communication keeps everyone informed at the level they need and speeds decision-making.

Layered message example

Top line: Release at risk due to integration delay; Recommend deferring Feature Z. Appendix: technical notes and estimated remediation tasks for engineers.

Common mistake
Sending a single detailed engineering report to executives without a short summary and a decision request.
Pro tip
Always include an explicit decision or clarification question at the end of your update.
Check your format
One-line status plus one recommended action fits nearly every sponsor update.
