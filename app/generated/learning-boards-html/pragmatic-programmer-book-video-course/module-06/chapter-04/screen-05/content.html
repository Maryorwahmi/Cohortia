Process Flow

Collaborative goal setting

A repeatable sequence to convert requests into agreed, measurable deliverables.

1. Clarify requirements
Remove ambiguity before estimating; unclear requirements create optimistic guesses.
2. Prioritize with stakeholders
Use MoSCoW to highlight must-haves and acceptable deferrals.
3. Record assumptions
Explicitly state constraints like team size or third-party dependencies.
4. Present trade-offs
Show options: defer, add resources, or extend timeline.
5. Agree and record
Get stakeholder buy-in and document the decision to avoid misunderstandings.
Key Takeaway

Jointly created goals and explicit assumptions reduce rework and disagreement later.

Concrete scenario

Sponsor requests full feature set in one sprint. Team runs a five-minute MoSCoW exercise, identifies three must-haves for the sprint, moves other items to should/could, and documents the decision in the sprint brief.

Quick mnemonic
MoSCoW: Must, Should, Could, Won't — used to make scope trade-offs explicit.
Pro tip
Keep prioritization time-boxed so decisions happen quickly and stakeholders focus on trade-offs.
Common mistake
Not recording assumptions; future changes to those assumptions invalidate the original estimate.
