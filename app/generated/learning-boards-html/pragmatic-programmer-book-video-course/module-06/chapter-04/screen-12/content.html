Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Cadence & Consistency
Best Practice

Schedule predictable updates

A regular schedule and stable format reduce surprises and speed decisions.

Weekly summary
Traffic-light status, one-line progress, top risk, recommended action.
Sprint demo
Show working software regularly to align expectations with delivered value.
Ad-hoc alerts
For urgent issues, send a focused alert with facts and options outside the regular cadence.
Key Takeaway

A stable cadence and concise format make updates reliable tools for decision-making.

Example cadence

Every Friday: one-paragraph email with traffic light and one recommended action. Bi-weekly: working demo and sprint review for feedback.

Common mistake
Irregular updates or changing formats frequently — both reduce stakeholder confidence in reports.
Pro tip
Make reports short and repeatable; templates help maintain format over time.
Check-in
Ask stakeholders if the cadence and format meet their needs and adjust only with agreement.
