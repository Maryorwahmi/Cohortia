Skills You Will Practice

A practical checklist to build trust in your project communications.

Facilitation
Structured prioritization

Lead stakeholder discussions to select must-haves, using methods like MoSCoW to surface trade-offs.

Reporting
Concise, consistent updates

Deliver the right level of detail for each audience — a high-level traffic light for sponsors and deeper notes for engineers.

Conversation
Delivering difficult news

Communicate early, present facts, offer options, and ask for input to maintain control and trust.

Practice: craft a one-paragraph status email that states the impact and two mitigation options.
