Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Team Environment
Psychological safety
Encourage questions and experiments. Avoid public shaming. Reward learning from failures.
Blameless postmortem
After an incident, document the timeline, identify contributing factors, and agree on process changes to prevent recurrence.
Retrospectives
Regularly reflect on what worked and what to try next. Pick one small experiment for the next sprint and measure its effect.
Shared standards
Agree on linters, CI checks, and code conventions so tools catch basic issues and humans focus on design.
Key Takeaway

Trust and simple rituals produce better outcomes than blaming. Make learning public and process changes small and measurable.
