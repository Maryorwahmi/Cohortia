Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Pair & Mob Programming
Pair roles
Driver: implements the code. Navigator: thinks strategically, spots edge cases, and suggests tests. Rotate often so both learn.
Mob programming
The team collaborates on one task with a single person driving and others guiding. Use when alignment and shared design are priorities.
Practical tips
  • Timebox sessions to maintain focus.
  • Use pairing for onboarding and complex design decisions.
  • Rotate roles to spread experience across the team.
Concrete scenario
Pair a senior and junior dev to fix an elusive bug. The senior suggests test cases the junior drives; both leave with shared understanding and a test that prevents regressions.
Key Takeaway

Use pairing strategically to transfer knowledge and prevent defects, timeboxed and with rotating roles.
