PR Checklist
  • Short summary and ticket reference.
  • Tests added or updated, and manual verification steps.
  • Automated checks passing: lint, format, and CI tests.
  • Call out any design trade-offs or areas you want focused feedback on.
Automation-first reduces noise in reviews and frees reviewers to focus on architecture and correctness rather than style issues.
Concrete example
PR body example
Summary: Add dark-mode toggle. Tests: added snapshot for theme. Manual: click toggle in header. Ticket: #456. Areas for review: accessibility of the button.
Reviewer guidance
Look for correctness, test coverage, performance or security implications, and whether the change follows team conventions. Ask clarifying questions rather than demanding rewrites.
Key Takeaway

Treat PRs as learning opportunities and integration checkpoints — automate checks and keep human feedback focused and timely.
