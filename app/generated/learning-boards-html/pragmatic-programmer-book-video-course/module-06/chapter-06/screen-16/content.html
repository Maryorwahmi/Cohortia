Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › What to Remember
Commit often
Small commits make reviews and rollbacks straightforward.
Review constructively
Be timely, specific, and explain why suggestions help the code.
Invest in team health
Retros, blameless postmortems, and shared tools reduce friction and build trust.
Final takeaway

Start small: pick one habit to try this sprint — a commit guideline, a PR checklist, or a pairing session — and measure its effect on your team's flow.
