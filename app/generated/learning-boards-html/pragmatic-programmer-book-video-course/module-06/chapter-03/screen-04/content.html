Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Estimates Are Educated Guesses
Core Concept

Estimates are educated guesses

Present estimates as hypotheses: include a range and list the key unknowns you would resolve to improve accuracy.

Why this matters

Ranges preserve honesty, reduce pressure, and allow stakeholders to weigh trade-offs under uncertainty.

Common misconception

Precision does not equal accuracy — a single-day promise often misleads when unknowns exist.

Key Takeaway

Label your estimate with a range and the top two unknowns to make it actionable and honest.

Concrete example

Request: "How long to add a new report?" Answer: "3–7 days with moderate confidence. Primary unknowns: data model complexity and required aggregations. I recommend a 1-day spike to confirm data shape."

Check your understanding
  • Can you state two unknowns that would change your estimate?
  • Would a spike reduce your top unknown quickly?
