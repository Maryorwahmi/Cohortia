Planning Poker / Wideband Delphi

Structured rounds of independent estimates followed by discussion reveal hidden complexity and reduce anchors.

Step 1
Present story and constraints; name a reference story.
Step 2
Everyone estimates privately, then reveal simultaneously to avoid anchoring.
Step 3
Discuss outliers, surface assumptions, and re-vote until convergence.
Outcome

A defensible estimate and a recorded list of assumptions and spikes, if needed.

Key Takeaway

Structured, anonymous estimates followed by discussion reduce bias and improve shared understanding.
