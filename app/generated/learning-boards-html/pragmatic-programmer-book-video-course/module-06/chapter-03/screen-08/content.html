Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Ranges & Confidence
Comparison

Give ranges and explain drivers

Ranges show variability; confidence levels attach an explicit belief to your estimate. Both help stakeholders make trade-offs.

Example

"Estimate: 3–7 days. Confidence: 60%. Main risk: unknown data aggregations in reports."

Pitfall

Don't treat the lower bound as a committed deadline for release planning.

Practice prompt

When you next estimate, state: the range, a confidence percentage, and the single biggest factor that would move the work to the high end.

Key Takeaway

Ranges and clear risk notes keep planning grounded in reality and support better decisions.
