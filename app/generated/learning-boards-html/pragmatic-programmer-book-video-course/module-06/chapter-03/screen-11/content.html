Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Padding vs Target

Avoid secret padding; be explicit about buffers

Padding erodes trust. Present honest ranges and separate contingency items so stakeholders understand trade-offs.

Estimate

A prediction of effort based on current knowledge. Expect refinement as unknowns are resolved.

Target

A desired outcome or deadline. If different from the estimate, make trade-offs and risks explicit.

Key Takeaway

Separate contingency openly and explain why it might be needed instead of hiding padded estimates.
