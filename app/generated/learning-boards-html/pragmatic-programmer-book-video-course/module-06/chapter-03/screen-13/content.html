Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Velocity & History

Use historical velocity, not magical conversion

Aggregate completed points over past iterations to form a realistic throughput range and use that for forecasts.

Measure

Sum completed points per sprint for several sprints to see variability.

Apply

Convert a backlog size to weeks using a velocity range, e.g., 30 points / (10–15 points/week) = 2–3 weeks.

Adjust

Call out factors that change velocity: team changes, parallel work, or new tech learnings.

Key Takeaway

Historical velocity makes point totals meaningful and supports realistic delivery windows when presented with conditions and confidence ranges.

Reflect

Review past forecasts in retrospectives to improve future estimation practices.
