Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › What to Remember
Decompose
Split big work into tasks that can be sized reliably.
Communicate ranges
Offer min–max or a value with confidence and name the driver of uncertainty.
Collaborate & refine
Use planning poker, spikes, and historic velocity to make estimates defensible.
Final Thought

Estimation is a practice. Make uncertainty visible, learn from outcomes, and continuously improve your planning conversations.
