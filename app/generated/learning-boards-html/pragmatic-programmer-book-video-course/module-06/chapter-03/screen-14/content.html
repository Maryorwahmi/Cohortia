Estimation
Predict effort as an informed guess and communicate uncertainty openly.
Uncertainty
Recognize unknowns and surface them before committing to tight deadlines.
Story Points
Relative sizing of effort, complexity, and risk; use references and historical mapping.
Takeaway

Adopt ranges, decompose work, use spikes when needed, and improve estimates with historical data and team collaboration.
