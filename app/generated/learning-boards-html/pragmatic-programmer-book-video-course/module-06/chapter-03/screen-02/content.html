Learning Objectives

Concrete skills to plan with realism and clarity.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain uncertainty in estimates

Describe why estimates are educated guesses and why precision early on is misleading. This skill helps teams set realistic expectations.

Mnemonic: Venn of knowledge vs unknown
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Decompose work for accuracy

Break features into smaller tasks and stories so estimates become easier to reason about and compare.

Mnemonic: Timeline of decreasing size
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use relative sizing and ranges

Apply story points and provide ranges or confidence levels instead of single-day commitments to clarify risk.

Mnemonic: Range slider icon
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Mitigate bias and refine continuously

Recognize optimism, anchoring, and planning fallacy. Use team estimates and historical data to improve accuracy over time.

Mnemonic: Team circle icon
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
