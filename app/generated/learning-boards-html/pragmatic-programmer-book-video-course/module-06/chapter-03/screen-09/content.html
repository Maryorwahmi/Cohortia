Common biases that affect estimation

Name the bias, see how it shows up, and take a practical step to mitigate it.

Optimism bias

We focus on the happy path and ignore failures. Mitigation: explicit risk listing and pessimistic scenarios.

Anchoring

An early number skews later estimates. Mitigation: independent initial estimates before group discussion.

Planning fallacy

We underestimate even with contrary evidence. Mitigation: use historical velocity and similar-task data.

Team technique

Planning poker or Wideband Delphi gathers independent views then converges, reducing individual bias influence.

Key Takeaway

Biases are natural. Use structured, evidence-based practices to make estimates more reliable.
