Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Story Points
Definition

Story points: relative sizing

Use a Fibonacci-like scale to capture differences in effort, complexity, and uncertainty. Compare against known stories to stay consistent.

Practice

Pick a reference story you know well. Size new stories by asking if they are smaller, similar, or larger than that reference.

Misuse to avoid

Do not rigidly convert points to days without team-specific historical data.

Key Takeaway

Points are comparative tools. Always name a reference and the confidence when you share sizes.
