Effective Estimation

Learn to convert uncertainty into useful planning choices by breaking work down, sizing relatively, and communicating ranges.

Duration: ~11 min explainer
In this lesson
Why "I don't know" is valid and how to shrink uncertainty.
Coming up
Story points, spikes, and biases to watch for.
Outcome
Better conversations and estimates that reflect uncertainty.
Overview
Why it matters

Estimates shape planning and trust. When they hide uncertainty they cause missed expectations; when transparent, they inform choices.

Common trap

Treating an early single-point estimate as a commitment leads to pressure, blame, and poor decisions.

Concept Map
Decompose → Size → Communicate
Decompose
Split large features into stories and tasks until estimates become manageable.
Size
Use story points for relative effort and include uncertainty in the sizing conversation.
Communicate
Prefer ranges, confidence levels, and spikes to single-day promises.
Explore Key Topics
Story Points — Relative sizing for complexity and uncertainty.
Spikes — Time-boxed research to reduce unknowns before estimating.
Biases — Watch optimism and anchoring when you estimate.
