Iterative Development

A proven approach to reduce risk by delivering working pieces of software often and learning from real feedback.

Why this matters: Short cycles lower the cost of change and surface real priorities.
How we learn: Each increment teaches us about users and requirements we couldn’t foresee.
Working increments

Each piece is coded, tested, and potentially deployable.

Short feedback

Feedback after each cycle guides the next change.
