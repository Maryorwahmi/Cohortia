10-minute lab: To-Do List increments

2-minute overview

Contrast building piece-by-piece to waiting for a single big release. Use a LEGO metaphor to make it concrete.

8-minute coding demo

Increment 1: add & view tasks. Increment 2: mark complete. Increment 3: delete & persist. Commit each increment with tests.

Mini-quiz

Ask: which increment will give the most actionable feedback and why? Expect answers about visibility of user behavior.

Practical checklist for the demo
  • Keep commits small and focused.
  • Attach at least one automated test per increment.
  • Demonstrate the working increment in the browser or runner.
