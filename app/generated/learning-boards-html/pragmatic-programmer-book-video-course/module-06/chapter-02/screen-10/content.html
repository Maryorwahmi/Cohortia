Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Feedback Loops
Core Concept

Feedback is the engine of improvement

Short loops let you test assumptions with minimal cost and redirect effort to what matters.

Types of feedback
  • User observations and usability signals.
  • Stakeholder priorities and acceptance feedback.
  • Automated telemetry and analytics.
Key Takeaway

Design increments as experiments with clear measures so feedback produces actionable decisions.

Concrete example: checkout experiment

Ship a simple checkout and measure where users drop off. Use those signals to improve the flow before adding payment complexity.

Misconception

Feedback is not noise; treat it as data. Distinguish single anecdotes from reproducible signals before redesigning the product.
