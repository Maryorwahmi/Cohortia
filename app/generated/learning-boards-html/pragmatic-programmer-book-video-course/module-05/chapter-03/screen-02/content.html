How to use these objectives

Work through the code examples and the hands-on design task. Practice creating a small adapter for a provider and toggling a feature with a runtime flag. Discuss trade-offs with your team before committing to risky decisions.

Mnemonic
Think: Defer, Decouple, Deliver.
Quick check
Can you swap a provider with a one-line change? If not, you may be tightly coupled.
