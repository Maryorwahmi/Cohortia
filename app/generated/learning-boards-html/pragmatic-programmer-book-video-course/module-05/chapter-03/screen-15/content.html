Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › What to Remember
Indirection
Insert a small layer to decouple components and ease replacement.
Feature Flags
Make rollouts reversible at runtime, but manage flags carefully.
Vendor Lock-in
Dependence on a single provider that is costly to leave; mitigate with adapters and prototypes.
Final takeaway

Use minimal, targeted abstractions and runtime controls. Prototype risky choices and remove temporary mechanisms when stability is proven.
