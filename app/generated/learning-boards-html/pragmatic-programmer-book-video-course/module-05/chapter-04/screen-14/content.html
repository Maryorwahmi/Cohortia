Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts — Part 2
1. Build a tracer bullet
Prove your stack and pipeline with a minimal, deployable slice before expanding functionality.
2. Prototype intentionally
Use prototypes for narrow questions and clearly mark them as disposable.
3. Require reimplementation
Do not promote prototype implementations directly; reimplement with tests and documentation if retained.
Final takeaway
These practices reduce risk and technical debt by making learning explicit and repeatable. Choose the right tool and label your intent.
