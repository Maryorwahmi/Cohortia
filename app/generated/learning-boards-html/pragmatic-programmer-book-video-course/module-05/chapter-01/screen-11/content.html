Premature Abstraction vs Practical DRY

Premature abstraction

Happens when an abstraction bundles unrelated behavior or anticipates future differences. It can create complexity and conditional logic.

Warning

Clever abstractions that save a few lines today may cost clarity tomorrow.

Practical DRY

Favor small, named helpers and tests. Wait for evidence of repeated patterns or use incremental refactors you can split later.

Checklist

Is the repetition the same knowledge? Are tests in place? Can the abstraction be small and clear?
