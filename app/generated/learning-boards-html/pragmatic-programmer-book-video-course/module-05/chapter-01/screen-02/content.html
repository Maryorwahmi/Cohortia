Learning Objectives

Gain practical skills to find and eliminate duplicated knowledge and to balance abstraction with clarity.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain DRY

Describe the principle and why a single representation reduces bugs and maintenance cost.

02
Identify duplication

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Refactor safely

Apply extraction, encapsulation and shared utilities to centralize knowledge without breaking behavior.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Balance abstraction
