Quick DRY Reminders

1. Name it
Describe the repeated knowledge in one clear sentence.
2. Test it
Capture behavior so refactors are safe and verifiable.
3. Keep it small
Prefer focused helpers and avoid heavy generalization early.
Final Thought

DRY is not an aesthetic rule; it's a tool to keep systems consistent and maintainable. Use it with judgment and tests.
