Key Concepts

DRY
Every piece of knowledge should have one authoritative representation.
Knowledge vs Syntax
Distinguish between syntactic similarity and shared knowledge before abstracting.
Refactor Safely
Detect, test, extract, replace, and review — testing makes DRY refactors safe.
Takeaway

Centralize knowledge where it belongs. Prefer small, tested abstractions and avoid premature generalization.
