Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Coupling vs Cohesion
Low Coupling
Minimal, intentional dependencies through stable interfaces.
Signs
Few direct calls to internals, use of interfaces, and clear contracts.
Benefit
Easier to swap implementations and to test in isolation.
High Cohesion
Elements inside a module work toward a single responsibility.
Signs
Single reason to change, focused public API, predictable internals.
Benefit
Clear ownership and easier maintenance.
Concrete example
A UserService that handles validation, persistence, and email sending should be split into distinct services to improve cohesion and lower coupling.
Key Takeaway
Design for one responsibility per module and keep external dependencies minimal and intentional.
