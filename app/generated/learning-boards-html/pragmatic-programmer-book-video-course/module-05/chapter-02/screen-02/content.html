Learning Objectives
Bloom progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define orthogonality
Explain how independent components reduce system-wide risk. Verified competency milestone: explain the stereo metaphor and map it to a codebase.
Venn: independence vs change
02
Understand coupling & cohesion
Identify low coupling and high cohesion in modules. Verified competency milestone: label three modules in a sample app as low/high coupling and cohesion.
Mnemonic: interdependence map
03
Apply SRP to refactoring
Refactor a monolithic class into focused services. Verified competency milestone: split three responsibilities into distinct classes and wire them by injection.
Timeline: before → refactor → after
04
Identify anti-patterns
Spot globals, god objects, and direct instantiation. Verified competency milestone: propose concrete DI-based fixes for two anti-patterns.
Mnemonic: red flags list
