Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › What is Orthogonality?
Orthogonality explained
Independent components that change without breaking others.
Mathematical metaphor
Orthogonal vectors do not influence each other — same idea for modules.
Stereo analogy
Amplifier, tuner, and speakers: swap or upgrade one without rebuilding the rest.
Key Takeaway

Orthogonality reduces risk by making change localized to one component.

Common misconception
Orthogonality is not just fewer dependencies; it is about independent behavior and change boundaries.
Check: Blast radius
If a change touches more than two modules, consider improving interfaces and responsibilities.
Pro tip
Favor clear contracts over fragile shared state.
Example question
Can you replace one implementation with a mock in tests without touching other modules?
