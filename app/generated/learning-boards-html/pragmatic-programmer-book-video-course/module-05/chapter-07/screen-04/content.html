Core Concept Definition

What pragmatic testing is

A disciplined approach where tests are chosen and written to reduce the highest risks while keeping developer workflow fast.

Fast: run in seconds to encourage frequent use.
Isolated: avoid shared state and flaky interactions.
Actionable: failures point to cause, not implementation details.

Analogy: safety net

Think of tests as a safety net under a trapeze act. The net need not cover every square inch of the stage; it must catch the most dangerous falls and let performers move quickly.

Example
A unit test that asserts a service rejects negative prices protects a critical business rule and runs instantly in a developer workflow.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize tests that reduce the most risk and keep them fast and isolated so they remain useful.
