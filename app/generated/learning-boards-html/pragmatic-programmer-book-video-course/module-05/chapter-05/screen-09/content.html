Module 05 › Chapter 5.5 › Re-estimating
Concept

Cone of Uncertainty

Early estimates cover a wide range that narrows as you learn more. Plan iterations that allow you to re-estimate and reduce that range.

  • Re-estimate at the start of each iteration or after spikes.
  • Record why numbers changed and which assumptions shifted.
  • Offer ranges and confidence levels rather than single points.
Key Takeaway

Make re-estimation a regular habit so plans remain aligned with reality.

Example Scenario
You estimate an integration will take 3–5 days. After a spike you discover authentication is more complex, so you update pessimistic estimate and re-calculate totals. Share a short note with the original estimate, what changed, new estimate range, and suggested trade-offs.
Misconception
Re-estimating means failure.
Reality: re-estimation is planning; it reduces risk and preserves trust.
Pro Tip
Attach confidence (e.g., 80% confidence) and assumptions to any estimate you report.
Check Your Understanding
