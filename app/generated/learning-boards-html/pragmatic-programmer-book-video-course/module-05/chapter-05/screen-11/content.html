Module 05 › Chapter 5.5 › Mistakes & Responses
Common Mistakes
  • Treating estimates as promises instead of hypotheses.
  • Estimating large, vague tasks instead of decomposing.
  • Giving single-point estimates without assumptions.
Warning
Accepting impossible deadlines without stating trade-offs damages delivery and trust.
Pragmatic Responses
Break tasks down, use appropriate estimation techniques, record assumptions, present ranges, and re-estimate regularly. If you discover new facts, explain options: reduce scope, accept lower polish, or shift schedule.
Action
Run a short spike to reduce uncertainty before giving a firm date.
Result
Better decisions and fewer surprises because estimates become evidence-based.
Reflection
When was the last time an unknown doubled your work? How would you surface that fact earlier next time?
