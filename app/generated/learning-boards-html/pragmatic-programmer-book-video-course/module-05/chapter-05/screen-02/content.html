Learning Objectives

Each objective is paired with a competency milestone you can verify in a short demo or artifact.

01 Understand uncertainty
Explain why precise predictions are elusive and how uncertainty changes over time. This skill helps you set realistic expectations with stakeholders.
Verified Competency Milestone
02 Break down tasks
Create a Work Breakdown Structure so tasks are small, testable, and estimable. Smaller units reduce variance in your estimates.
Verified Competency Milestone
03 Apply estimation techniques
Use analogy, expert opinion, planning poker, and three-point estimation to capture team knowledge and uncertainty.
Verified Competency Milestone
04 Re-estimate & communicate
Update estimates regularly and present ranges and assumptions so stakeholders can make trade-offs with full context.
Verified Competency Milestone
