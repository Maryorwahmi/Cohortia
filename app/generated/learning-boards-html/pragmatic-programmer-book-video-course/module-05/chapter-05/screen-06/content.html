Module 05 › Chapter 5.5 › Estimation Techniques
Overview

Choose techniques intentionally

Each estimation technique captures different information. Pick the one that reduces your largest uncertainties.

  • Analogy: fast, needs good historical match.
  • Expert Opinion: precise if expert is unbiased.
  • Planning Poker: surfaces team assumptions and consensus.
  • Three-Point / PERT: quantifies uncertainty with a weighted average.
Key Takeaway

Record which technique you used and why; that context helps later re-estimation.

Technique Cards
Analogy
Use time from a similar completed task as the estimate.
Expert Opinion
Ask the assignee for their realistic estimate, document why.
Planning Poker
Team members vote and discuss until consensus emerges.
Three-Point / PERT
Provide O, M, P estimates to compute a weighted average that includes uncertainty.
Check Your Understanding
For a task that is similar to one finished last month, which technique would you use first and why? What assumption must you check?
