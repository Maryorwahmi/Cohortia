Module 05 › Chapter 5.6 › Identifying Automation Candidates
Checklist
  1. Step 1: Inventory repeating tasks across build, test, deploy, and setup.
  2. Step 2: Rate frequency and error-proneness. High frequency + high pain = priority.
  3. Step 3: Prototype a minimal script and verify it reduces steps.
  4. Step 4: Add tests and integrate into CI when stable.
Concrete scenario
If deployment takes 30 minutes and is error-prone, automate it with a script and a CI job to reduce time and mistakes.
Visual prioritization
High freq / High pain
Top priority: tests, builds, deployment scripts.
Low freq / High pain
Consider scripting but weigh maintenance cost.
Misconception
Automating every possible task is tempting but costly. Focus on repeatable pain that automation will reduce reliably.
