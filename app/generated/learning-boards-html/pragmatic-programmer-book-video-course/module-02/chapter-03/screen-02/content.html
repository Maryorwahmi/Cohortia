Learning Objectives
Master the practices that keep decisions reversible and avoid expensive vendor lock-in.
01
Define Reversibility
Explain what makes a decision cheap to undo and why that reduces risk. Visual mnemonic: branching path.

Identify Lock-in Sources

03
Apply Mitigations
Use abstraction layers, open standards, and feature flags to reduce coupling. Mnemonic: interface box with plugs.

04
Evaluate Trade-offs
Estimate the cost of change and decide when a vendor feature justifies lock-in. Mnemonic: scale balance icon.
