Reversibility
Design so decisions are cheap to change and undo.
Vendor Lock-in
Dependence on a single vendor that makes switching costly.
Abstraction Layer
A boundary that hides implementation details and enables substitution.
Takeaway

Prioritize reversibility where it reduces significant future cost: core data flows, authentication, and critical business integrations.
