Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them
Pitfall: Blind adoption
Adopting a new vendor without exit analysis can lock you into expensive migration later. Document expected exit cost at the time of adoption.
Pitfall: Over-abstraction
Abstracting everything creates indirection and slows progress. Focus on high-impact boundaries.
Pitfall: No migration plan
Integrating proprietary services without documenting export paths or rollback steps creates technical debt.
Key Takeaway

Ask the cost-of-change question in every design review and record the outcome to guide reversible design investments.
