Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › When to Accept Vendor Trade-offs
Checklist for pragmatic vendor choice
  • Estimate cost of exit: data, code, and operations.
  • Confirm data export options and formats.
  • Decide if an adapter can contain vendor specifics.
  • Assess vendor maturity and support risks.
  • Compare business value versus migration risk.
Architecture pattern
Keep vendor-specific services at the edges of your architecture behind adapters; core business logic depends on stable interfaces.
Example
Use a managed search service if search is a differentiator, but isolate it through a SearchService interface so you can replace the provider if necessary.
Key Takeaway

Accept vendor trade-offs when they yield clear business advantage, and isolate them to keep future options open.
