Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Anti-patterns
Pitfalls
Recognize and correct common mistakes
Copy-paste
Quick but dangerous. Replace with extraction and tests.
Shotgun surgery
Changes that require edits in many files indicate duplicated knowledge and high risk.
Premature abstraction
Avoid creating complex abstractions before the need is clear. Refactor incrementally.
Pro tip
When you centralize, add clear documentation and examples so callers know the intended usage and don't accidentally reimplement logic.
Quick repair
If you find slightly different copies, either parameterize the differences or keep separate functions with clear names — do not force a single, confusing API.
