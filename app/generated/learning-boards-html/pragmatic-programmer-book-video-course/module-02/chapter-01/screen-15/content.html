Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
DRY Principle
Every piece of knowledge should have a single authoritative representation.
Duplication Types
Code, data, and knowledge duplication need different strategies: reuse, normalization, and central services respectively.
Test before refactor
Capture behavior with tests, then extract and replace callers incrementally to preserve correctness.
Takeaway

DRY reduces maintenance cost and inconsistency when applied to knowledge. Use tests, clear ownership, and small refactors to centralize intent safely.
