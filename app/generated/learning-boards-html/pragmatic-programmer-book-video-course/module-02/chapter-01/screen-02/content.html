Balance refactor risk
Decide when to refactor by weighing maintenance savings against immediate complexity costs. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic
Think of DRY as a single label on a filing cabinet. If the same paper is kept in multiple folders, updates get missed. Centralize the paper and reference it from one place.
