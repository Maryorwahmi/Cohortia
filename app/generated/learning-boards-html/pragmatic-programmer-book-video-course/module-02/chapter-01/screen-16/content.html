Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What to Remember
Measure impact
Prioritize refactors where duplication causes many edits or bugs.
Document ownership
Make it clear who owns each piece of data or rule and where to update it.
Protect with tests
Use tests as a safety net while centralizing and evolving rules.
Final thought

DRY is about preserving intent. Centralize meaningful knowledge, keep APIs small and documented, and refactor with tests to make your codebase resilient and easier to change.
