Process Balancing Quality and Delivery
How to choose where to invest effort now
Decision Signals
  • Value: who benefits immediately?
  • Risk: what does failure cost?
  • Cost: implementation, tests, maintenance.
  • Evidence: can we measure impact quickly?
Pattern
Prefer shims and clear tests; generalize only when concrete reuse appears.
Misconception
Speed and quality are not opposed when you rely on tests and feedback loops.
Key Takeaway

Use evidence-based decisions to invest effort where it reduces risk or increases value now.
