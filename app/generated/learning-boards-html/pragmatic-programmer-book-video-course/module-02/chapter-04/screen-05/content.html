Comparison Perfectionism vs Pragmatism
How seeking perfection harms delivery and how to balance quality
Perfectionism
  • Design for every hypothetical future.
  • Large upfront architecture and delayed releases.
  • High maintenance burden from unused code.
Common Harm: Analysis Paralysis
Pragmatism
  • Ship functionality that solves current problems.
  • Iterate with feedback and measure impact.
  • Refactor when evidence shows it's needed.
Healthy Practice: Short iterations with measurement
Practical Question
Will this speed up or slow down measurable value?
Misconception
Pragmatism is not low quality; it's targeted quality.
