Good Enough Software and Eliminating Irrelevancy
Balance quality, avoid overengineering, and remove code that no longer provides value.

This chapter shows how to choose the right scope for features, avoid premature optimization, and apply YAGNI. It also previews a hands-on refactor where we remove an unused option, delete an irrelevant function, and simplify validation to make the codebase easier to maintain.
