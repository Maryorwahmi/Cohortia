Definition Premature Optimization
Why optimizing early is costly and how to know when to optimize
What it is
Spending effort on performance improvements before profiling or user evidence justifies them.
  • Often targets parts that are not bottlenecks.
  • Increases complexity and maintenance burden.
  • May introduce subtle bugs.
When to optimize
Profile first, target real hotspots, and test thoroughly. Optimize incrementally and measure user impact.
Concrete Signal
Measured latency affecting users or a clear CPU/memory hotspot.
