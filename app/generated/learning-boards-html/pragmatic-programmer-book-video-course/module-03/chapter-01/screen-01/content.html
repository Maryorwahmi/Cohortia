Don't Repeat Yourself

Every piece of knowledge must have one single, authoritative representation.

DRY prevents scattered knowledge that causes inconsistent behavior when business rules change. We will focus on practical refactoring and the trade-offs to avoid over-abstraction.

Example to watch for: duplicated discount logic in checkout, invoice generation, and reporting that should be centralized into one function or service.

In this lesson
Refactor duplicated logic, choose an authoritative representation, avoid over-abstraction.
Single Source of Truth
Functions & Methods: Encapsulate rules once and call them from many places.
Configuration: Centralize endpoints and connection strings to avoid scattered literals.
Shared Libraries: Provide one interface for common behavior across services.
