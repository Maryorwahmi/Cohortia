Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Key Concepts
Resource Consumption
CPU, memory, disk I/O, network, and time are the axes to measure.
Profiling & Pareto
Measure to find the small portion of code that causes most cost and focus there.
Safe Strategies
Use algorithms, caching, lazy loading, concurrency, and DB tuning where profiling supports the change.
Summary
Measure, focus, apply the right tool, and verify — that sequence keeps optimizations effective and maintainable.
