Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Contract Clauses
Precondition
Requirement the caller must satisfy. Example: deposit amount > 0.
Postcondition
Guarantee from the supplier after execution. Example: balance increased by amount.
Invariant
A property that must always hold for the object. Example: balance >= 0.
How to check
  • Assert preconditions at method start to detect caller errors.
  • Verify postconditions before returning to detect supplier mistakes.
  • Run invariant checks after mutations to ensure internal consistency.
