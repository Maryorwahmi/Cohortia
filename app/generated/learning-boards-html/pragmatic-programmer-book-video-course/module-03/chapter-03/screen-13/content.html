Decision Checklist
When to Use a Tracer Bullet
Checklist
  • Are integration points uncertain?
  • Do stakeholders need a working artifact?
  • Will early discovery reduce major risks?
  • Can you build a tiny slice quickly?
If yes

Prioritize a tracer bullet as the first deliverable; it will shape design and reduce rework.

Misconception

A tracer bullet isn't a substitute for later design; it informs design with concrete evidence.
