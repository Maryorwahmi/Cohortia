Hands-on Activity
Build a Blog Tracer Bullet
Checklist to complete a minimal blog post creator that proves your stack.
1. Scaffold app and DB
Add a DB helper, init function, and a small posts table.
2. Implement form and routes
Create POST to insert and GET to display posts.
3. Test full flow
Submit a post and verify it appears after refresh.
4. Commit & reflect
Record one validated assumption and one change for the next iteration.
Starter hints
Use SQLite for simplicity, keep validation minimal, and focus on a single, demonstrable workflow.
Key Takeaway

A short, focused tracer bullet gives you concrete evidence for decision making — scope to learn, not to impress.

Reflection prompt

After building, what integration surprise did you encounter, and how will it change your next step?
