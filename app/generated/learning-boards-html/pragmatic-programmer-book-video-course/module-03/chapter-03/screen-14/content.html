Key Concepts
Tracer Bullet Development
Build a minimal, end-to-end slice to validate your system quickly.
Early Feedback
Expose stakeholders to working behavior to guide requirements and priorities.
Living Skeleton
Treat the slice as a maintainable foundation to be extended, not tossed away.
Summary
Use tracer bullets to reduce risk and inform design with concrete, running code that stakeholders can interact with.
