Common Mistakes
Pitfalls and Misconceptions
Recognize and avoid frequent errors so your tracer bullet delivers meaningful learning.
UI-only mocks

They do not validate backend integration or persistence behavior.

Throwaway mentality

Treating the slice as disposable loses long-term value and increases debt.

Over-scoping

Trying to validate too many assumptions slows feedback; keep the first slice tiny.

Quick remedies
  • Ensure the slice exercises the full path and includes minimal tests.
  • Write down assumptions and acceptance criteria before building.
  • Scope narrowly to finish quickly and iterate.
Key Takeaway

Avoid these mistakes so your tracer bullet gives real, actionable insight into your project.
