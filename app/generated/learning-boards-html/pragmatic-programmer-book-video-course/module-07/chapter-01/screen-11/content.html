Personal Learning Plan
A minimal template to make learning repeatable and measurable.
Template
1) Core area to maintain. 2) Two adjacent topics to explore. 3) Weekly schedule with two blocks. 4) Micro-deliverables per block. 5) Two- and six-week checkpoints.
Example
Core: Backend systems. Adjacent: Functional paradigms, DevOps basics. Monday: 60m reading. Thursday: 60m experiment. Week 2: one-page summary + demo.
Review rules
If you miss blocks twice, shorten sessions or move them to a less busy week. Rebalance topics quarterly.
Concrete plan card
Core: Backend. Adjacent: Testing patterns, observability. Mon 7–8pm: read and note. Thu 7–8pm: implement a failing test and make it pass. Week 2: demo in 5 minutes.
Key Takeaway
A living plan with small deliverables turns vague goals into measurable progress you can adjust and repeat.
