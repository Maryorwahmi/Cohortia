Measure and Rebalance
Signals and simple actions to maintain momentum.
Keep a short log
Date, focus, deliverable, one-sentence outcome.
Monthly check
Look for repeated avoidance or stagnation and schedule a micro-sprint to correct course.
Rebalance rule
Shift time away from areas with diminishing returns toward gaps limiting your impact.
Example signal
If you take twice as long to integrate with a frontend team as others, invest in two small frontend exercises to close the collaboration gap.
Key Takeaway
Simple, repeatable signals let you keep the portfolio aligned with evolving team and career needs.
