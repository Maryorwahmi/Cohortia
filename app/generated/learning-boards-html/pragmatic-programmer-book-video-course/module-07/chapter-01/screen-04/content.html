Knowledge Portfolio
Treat skills as assets that require regular deposits and occasional rebalancing.
Core definition
A deliberate collection of skills, experiences, and mental models that you manage over time to stay adaptable and valuable.
Metaphor example
Small weekly learning sessions are like regular deposits; occasional study of unfamiliar domains is like rebalancing to reduce risk.
Common mistake
Waiting for project pressure to force learning leads to brittle skills and late surprises.
Key Takeaway

Plan small, repeatable learning deposits and intentionally expose gaps to rebalance your portfolio.
