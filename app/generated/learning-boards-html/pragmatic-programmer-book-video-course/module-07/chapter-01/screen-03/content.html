Turn Objectives into Weekly Actions
Each objective maps to a small experiment or scheduled block you can repeat.
01 — Explain
Write a one-paragraph explanation of the knowledge portfolio and one concrete example from your own work.
02 — Pick a Strategy
Choose reading, experiment, or course. Schedule two sessions and commit to building a tiny artifact.
03 — Draft a Plan
Create a plan with time slots, a deliverable, and a one-week review to capture lessons learned.
04 — Avoid Pitfalls
If you find yourself endlessly following tutorials, stop and build an original small project to test comprehension.
VERIFICATION TIP
Prefer official docs, solid books, and short experiments you can run in a real environment to confirm claims.
