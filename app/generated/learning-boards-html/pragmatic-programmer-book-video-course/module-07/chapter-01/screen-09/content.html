Escape Tutorial Hell
Convert passive following into active construction.
One tutorial, one twist
After finishing, implement the same idea with a constraint or twist that forces adaptation.
Use timeboxes
Short, focused sessions encourage completion and reflection rather than endless extension.
Reflect and record
Write one paragraph on what changed in your understanding after the twist.
Scenario
A tutorial builds a to-do app. Your twist: add a sync simulation that merges changes from two devices. Implement a merge rule and tests to see how the logic behaves under conflict.
Key Takeaway
Apply every tutorial idea in your own context with a small twist to deepen understanding.
