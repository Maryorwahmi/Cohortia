Portfolio Structure
  • Deep investments: areas you will master over months (language, framework).
  • Exploratory bets: adjacent topics to broaden your T‑shape.
  • Artifacts: projects, talks, blog posts that prove progress.
  • Metrics: measurable milestones and feedback plans.
Concrete Template
Area: Deep - Distributed Systems
Goal: Deploy one fault‑tolerant service in 3 months.
Activities: Read two chapters, build a small service, add health checks.
Feedback: Monthly mentor check‑in; automated acceptance tests.
Why It Matters
A portfolio makes tradeoffs explicit and helps you choose projects that change your career trajectory.
Try This
Add one deep item and one exploratory item to your portfolio today. Define a measurable outcome for each and schedule a review date.
Key Takeaway
Treat your skills like investments. Rebalance often to keep your career resilient and relevant.
