Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Mentorship & Community
Hierarchy of Contribution
Small: Code Reviews & Docs
Frequent, low-cost contributions that sharpen clarity and reduce friction.
Medium: Tutorials & Talks
Explain patterns and trade-offs publicly to solidify your own reasoning.
Large: Open Source & Mentoring Programs
Sustained contribution that multiplies impact across the community.
Practical Routine
  1. Schedule one short mentorship touchpoint or code review each week.
  2. Publish a short tutorial or a how-to note every quarter.
  3. Join or contribute to one community project or internal initiative annually.
Key Takeaway

Regular, small contributions compound into deep expertise and stronger teams.
