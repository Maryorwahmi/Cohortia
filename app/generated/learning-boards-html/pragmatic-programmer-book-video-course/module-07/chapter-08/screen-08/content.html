Activity

Create a 6–12 month roadmap

Pick deep dives and exploration topics, define actions, set success metrics, and schedule review points with a mentor or peer.

Practical Tips
  • Timebox experiments so you get results quickly and avoid sunk-cost fallacy.
  • Choose measurable, observable outcomes for each goal.
  • Schedule regular reviews and feedback to update your roadmap.
Key Takeaway

A roadmap is a sequence of small experiments with feedback loops; it should change as you learn.
