Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Actions that Reinforce
Mentorship
Teach to learn. Structured mentorship forces you to explain reasoning and discover gaps in your own knowledge.
Community Contribution
Share small artifacts—docs, PRs, short posts—to receive feedback and help others learn faster.
Responsibility
Own your career and code: manage your learning portfolio, set goals, and automate toil to focus on growth.
Summary: Small, consistent actions compound. Mentor once, contribute once, and automate once each month to build momentum for continuous improvement.
