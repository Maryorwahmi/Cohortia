Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Key Concepts
Lifelong Learning
A continuous, self‑motivated pursuit of skills and knowledge that sustains long‑term career growth.
Curiosity
A driving desire to understand why things work and how they can be improved, fueling deeper insight.
Adaptability
The ability to evaluate change carefully and incorporate useful practices incrementally and thoughtfully.
Takeaway: Combine these habits with small, measurable experiments and a living knowledge portfolio to make steady career progress.
