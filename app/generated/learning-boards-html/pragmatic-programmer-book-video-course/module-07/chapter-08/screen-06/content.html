Process

Research → Prototype → Measure → Decide

1. Research
Clarify the problem and potential benefits of the new approach.
2. Prototype
Build a tracer bullet: a minimal, end-to-end slice you can keep and evolve.
3. Measure
Define success metrics up front and gather evidence from the prototype.
4. Decide
Adopt incrementally when metrics justify the change, or learn and discard the idea if not.
Concrete Scenario
A team debates microservices. They prototype extracting one bounded context, measure latency, operational cost, and deployment complexity, and then choose an incremental plan informed by real data rather than by opinion.
Pitfall
Either migrating everything at once or rejecting change outright both risk long-term harm. The pragmatic middle path is measured experiments.
Key Takeaway

Adopt new tools by proving value with a small, measurable slice before broad rollout.
