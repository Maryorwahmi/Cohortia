Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Learning Objectives — Implementation
Objective Checkpoints
Synthesis
Weekly review: pick one recurring bug and design a systemic fix. Measure before/after incidence.
Curiosity
Keep a short log of 'why' questions and insights. Review monthly for patterns.
Adaptability
Prototype one new tool or pattern before committing team-wide. Timebox the experiment.
Mentorship
Publish a short tutorial or review a peer's code monthly to solidify your learning.
Verification
Each checkpoint becomes a verified competency milestone: measurable activity, demonstrable output, and a short reflection. These are the building blocks of your learning roadmap.
