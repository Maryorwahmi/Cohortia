Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Software as an evolving system
What it means

Software evolves when we plan for change: modular interfaces, feature toggles, and small, reversible steps keep systems healthy. Evolution reduces risk by making changes incremental and observable.

Practical rules
  • Design replaceable interfaces, not rigid implementations.
  • Deliver in small increments with measurable criteria.
  • Automate tests to guard behavioral contracts.
Analogy

Like pruning a tree: remove or replace branches gradually to guide growth.

Example

Route 10% of orders to a new payment provider behind a flag and increase after validation.

Common misconception

Evolution is not continuous hacking. It is controlled change with tests, rollouts, and clear ownership for each step.

Key takeaway

Adopt small, verifiable steps for every change. That habit converts risk into routine work you can measure and control.
