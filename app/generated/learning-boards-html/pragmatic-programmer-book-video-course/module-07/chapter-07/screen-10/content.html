Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Feature Flags
Use cases
  • Dark launching for performance and reliability validation.
  • A/B testing to measure user impact before full release.
  • Gradual rollout to manage load and risk.
  • Kill switches for instant rollback of problematic features.
Operational rules
  1. Record flag owner and lifecycle.
  2. Automate flag status change with auditing.
  3. Remove flags when no longer needed to avoid complexity.
Key takeaway

Flags reduce deployment risk but require governance, monitoring, and cleanup.
