Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Key Concepts
Evolutionary Design
Architect to adapt over time, not to be perfect up front.
Strangler Fig Pattern
Replace legacy parts incrementally behind a facade or proxy.
Feature Flags
Turn features on and off at runtime to control exposure and risk.
Final takeaway

Use small, measurable steps and the right controls to evolve systems while preserving value and minimizing disruption.
