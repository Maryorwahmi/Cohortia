Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Learning Objectives — Application
Mnemonic
Small Steps, Visible Results

Write three concrete steps for a migration and a pass/fail criterion for each. This becomes a repeatable habit for evolution.

Checklist
  • Identify a small seam to extract
  • Build the replacement with tests
  • Route traffic with a proxy or flag
  • Monitor and iterate
Success Criteria

Every step should be verifiable: feature parity, error rates under threshold, and measurable user impact before proceeding.

Who verifies

Assign owners for each step: developer for implementation, QA for parity checks, and product for user impact validation.
