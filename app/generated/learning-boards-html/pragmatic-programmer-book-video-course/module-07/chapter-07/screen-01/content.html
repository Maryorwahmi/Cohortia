Orchestrating Change: Managing Evolution

Learn to guide a living codebase forward with low-risk, incremental techniques. Understand the Strangler Fig pattern, feature flags, and how to manage technical debt so change stays sustainable.

In this lesson

You will learn patterns to introduce change safely, examples of gradual migration, and practical ways to control risk with flags and debt management.

Key Takeaway

Treat systems as evolving. Plan small, reversible steps that deliver value while you migrate.
