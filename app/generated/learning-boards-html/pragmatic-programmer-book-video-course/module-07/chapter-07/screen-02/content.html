Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Learning Objectives
01
Embrace Evolutionary Design
Understand systems change over time

You will learn to plan architecture that adapts. This reduces cost of future change and keeps options open as requirements clarify.

Verified competency milestone
02
Introduce Change Incrementally
Break large migrations into small steps

You will practice slicing migrations into safe, testable increments that deliver value and allow feedback after each step.

Verified competency milestone
03
Use Feature Flags Safely
Control rollouts and experiments

You will learn patterns for dark launches, gradual rollouts, and kill switches so production risk is controlled without redeploys.

Verified competency milestone
04
Manage Technical Debt
Prioritize and pay down debt deliberately

You will adopt practices for identifying high-interest debt, allocating time to refactor, and keeping systems flexible for future change.

Verified competency milestone
