Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Avoiding the big-bang rewrite
Big-bang rewrite
  • Large upfront cost and schedule risk
  • Loss of accumulated business logic and bug fixes
  • Stops regular feature delivery during rewrite
Common trap

Believing a rewrite is the fastest route to modern code. Often it is the most expensive and slows your team down.

Incremental evolution
  • Small, verifiable extractions or enhancements
  • Continuous delivery and feedback
  • Lower blast radius and reversible steps
Pro tip

Start by extracting a single seam and proving parity before scaling the migration.
