Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Incremental evolution steps
Repeatable steps
1. Identify a seam with clear inputs/outputs.
2. Implement replacement component with isolated tests.
3. Introduce a facade/proxy or flag to route traffic.
4. Gradually increase traffic while monitoring metrics.
5. Retire old component when parity and stability are proven.
Supporting cards
Example
Extract the order creation endpoint and route 5% of traffic to the new service for validation.
Monitoring checklist
  • Error rate under threshold
  • Latency within SLA
  • Functional parity checks pass
Takeaway
Incremental steps reduce risk by making every change reversible and observable.
