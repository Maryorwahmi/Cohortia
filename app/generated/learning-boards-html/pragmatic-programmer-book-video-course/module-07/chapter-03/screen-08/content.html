Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Cargo Culting
Definition

Cargo Culting

Adopting tools or practices without understanding why they work or whether they fit your needs.

Example
Using a complex framework because it is trending, even though a simpler approach would solve your immediate problem.
Red Flag
No clear problem statement, only a desire to mimic others' stack choices.
Practical guardrails
  • Demand a problem-first justification.
  • Time-box a POC that tests integration and operations.
  • Document results for team learning.
Key Takeaway
Adopt tools because they solve a specific, measured problem for your team, not because they are fashionable.
