Learning Objectives

Learn, Understand, Apply, Master

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Evaluate tools critically
Learn to ask what problem a tool solves and what trade-offs it brings. This prevents blind adoption.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Curate information sources
Subscribe to reputable newsletters, follow selected experts, and avoid information overload by filtering sources.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Run time-boxed POCs
Validate a tool with a small, focused experiment to discover integration and operational costs before migrating.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Prune your toolbelt
Remove outdated or unused tools to reduce complexity and technical debt in your workflow.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
