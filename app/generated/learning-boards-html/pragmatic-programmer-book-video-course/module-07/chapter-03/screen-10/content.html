Architecture

Staged Migration Pattern

Stage 1: Pick a small service
Choose a low-risk component to implement CI and automated builds.
Stage 2: Implement POC
Add pipeline steps, manage secrets, and ensure tests run in CI similarly to local runs.
Stage 3: Measure
Track test feedback time, deployment errors, and developer time spent on releases.
Stage 4: Iterate
Refine steps, add caching, and expand to other services based on evidence.
Concrete Concerns
  • Where will secrets be stored and who manages them?
  • How will caches and dependencies be handled in CI?
  • Who owns pipeline maintenance and reviews?
Key Takeaway

A staged migration surfaces hidden costs early and keeps changes reversible until the pipeline proves its value.
