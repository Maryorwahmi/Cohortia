Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Staying Informed
Strategy

Curate sources and schedule experiments

Curation
Subscribe to a few high-quality newsletters, follow selected community voices, and prefer in-depth posts over constant feeds.
Scheduled Practice
Block regular time for short POCs, experiments, or tooling spikes to gain practical knowledge.
Rotate and Prune
Periodically remove subscriptions or tools that no longer add value to avoid noise and clutter.
Document Learnings
Share POC results so the team benefits from experiments and decisions are evidence-based.
Why it matters

Combining curation with scheduled practice turns passive knowledge into actionable understanding while preventing overload.
