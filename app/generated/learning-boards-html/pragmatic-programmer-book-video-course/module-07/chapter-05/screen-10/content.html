Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Common Mistakes
Anti-patterns
  • Spike turned feature: skipping refactor causes messy code.
  • Broad tests: slow to run and hard to diagnose failures.
  • Slow CI: reduces iteration frequency and learning speed.
  • No reflection: loses context and intent of the experiment.
Good practices
  • Time-box spikes: keep experiments short and focused.
  • Narrow tests: one assertion per behavior tested.
  • Fast CI: short jobs encourage frequent runs.
  • Short reflections: capture why and what next.
Key Takeaway
Deliberate constraints keep experiments useful and fast. Constraints are a feature, not a bug.
