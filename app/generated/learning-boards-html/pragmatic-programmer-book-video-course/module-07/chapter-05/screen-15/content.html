Continuous Integration (CI)
Automate test runs on push to get objective feedback without manual steps.
Metacognition
Record short notes after experiments to preserve intent and reasoning.
Red-Green-Refactor
An iteration rhythm that clarifies expectations and preserves code quality.
Final takeaway
Combine experiments, automated checks, CI, and reflection into a routine. Small, frequent cycles beat occasional, large efforts when learning complex skills.
