Automate Your Learning Feedback Loop
Turn experiments, tests, and CI into fast, repeatable feedback for learning
Orientation

You will learn to design time-boxed experiments, use tests as validation, and integrate simple CI to automate feedback. This reduces friction and turns curiosity into measurable progress.

Why it matters

Automated feedback keeps you honest about what you actually know. It replaces vague confidence with concrete passing checks and repeatable practice.

In this lesson
Design, test, automate, reflect
Practice a red-green-refactor cycle with small spikes and CI checks.
