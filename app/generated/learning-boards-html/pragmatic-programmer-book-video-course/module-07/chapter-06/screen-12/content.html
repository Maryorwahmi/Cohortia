Stakeholder Management

Turn refusal into collaboration

Acknowledge the stakeholder's goal, explain the trade-offs in business terms, and present concrete options so the stakeholder participates in the prioritization decision.

Example choices to present
  • Delay the release and include the feature.
  • Cut or defer lower-priority features to keep the date.
  • Run a short spike to provide a better estimate.
Communication tip
Use neutral, impact-focused language: describe work, time, and consequences rather than personalities or blame.
Action
Always end with a decision request so the stakeholder chooses the trade-off that matters to them.
