Module 7›Chapter 7.6›Common mistakes
Definition

Mistakes that undermine refusals

  • Fear-driven yes: Accepting work to avoid conflict, which creates long-term costs.
  • Blunt no: Refusing without offering alternatives or rationale, which blocks collaboration.
  • Vague alternatives: Suggesting undefined follow-ups that don't solve the immediate decision problem.
Fixes
Use objective impact statements, offer concrete alternatives, and if uncertain, propose a time-boxed spike to gather facts.
Red flags
  • You're saying yes to avoid a difficult conversation.
  • Your alternative is "we'll see" or "maybe later".
Pro tip
Use concrete timeboxes and clearly named alternatives—these are easier for stakeholders to accept or reject.
