Measurement and Practice

Turn refusal into a recorded, repeatable craft by using impact statements and alternatives.

Practice 1
Write an impact statement
Describe the technical work required, the schedule impact, and quality risk in one paragraph.
Practice 2
Propose pragmatic alternatives
Defer, propose an interim solution, or suggest a separate project/team.
Practice 3
Draft a professional refusal
Acknowledge value, state impact, and offer next steps without emotional language.
Practice 4
Record and review outcomes
Track what you deferred and whether the alternative met the need to improve future decisions.
Micro-mnemonic
Ask: Why? Propose: Alternatives. Quantify: Impact. Repeat as needed.
Verified milestone
Produce one impact statement and two alternatives for a real request and discuss with a peer.
