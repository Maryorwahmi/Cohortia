Module 7›Chapter 7.6›Cost of always saying yes
Comparison

What happens when you say yes

  • Short-term: Stakeholder appears satisfied, feature is delivered.
  • Medium-term: Deadlines slip, bugs appear, and developers context-switch.
  • Long-term: Technical debt accumulates and team morale drops.
Comparison

What happens with a pragmatic no

  • Short-term: Core deliverables stay on track, expectations are set.
  • Medium-term: Alternatives are evaluated and a proper solution is scheduled.
  • Long-term: Fewer surprises, lower maintenance burden, sustainable pace.
Real question

Which approach will most improve your product quality and team health over the next six months: immediate feature additions or disciplined prioritization?
