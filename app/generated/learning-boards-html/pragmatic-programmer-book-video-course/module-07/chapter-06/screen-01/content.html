Know When to Say "No"

Learn a pragmatic approach to declining requests so you protect project focus, avoid scope creep, and keep your team sustainable.

Orientation

You will learn to identify risky requests, communicate a constructive refusal, and offer alternatives that preserve value and timelines. The lesson uses real-case examples and an activity to draft a pragmatic message.

Why this matters

Saying "yes" without assessment causes scope creep, technical debt, and missed deadlines. A clear, empathetic "no" protects quality and demonstrates professional judgment.
