Module 7›Chapter 7.6›Key Concepts
Trade-offs
Choices that balance time, cost, and quality; make these explicit when deciding.
Stakeholder Management
Communicate options and consequences so stakeholders can prioritize with you.
Opportunity Cost
Always ask: what are we giving up by accepting this request?
Final takeaway

Use objective impact statements, concrete alternatives, and shared prioritization to say no constructively and keep projects healthy.
