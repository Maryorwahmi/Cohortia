Module 7›Chapter 7.6›Key Concepts
Scope Creep
Uncontrolled expansion of requirements beyond the agreed objectives.
Opportunity Cost
The next-best alternative you forgo when choosing one option over another.
Sustainable Pace
A consistent workload the team can maintain long-term without burnout.
Summary takeaway

Apply these lenses to every new request to decide whether to accept, defer, or propose an alternative.
