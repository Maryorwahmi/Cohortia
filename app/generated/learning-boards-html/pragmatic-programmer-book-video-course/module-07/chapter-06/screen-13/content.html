Module 7›Chapter 7.6›Long-term consequences
Definition

Costs of saying yes to everything

  • Developer cost: Burnout and reduced accuracy under sustained overtime.
  • Project cost: Growing technical debt and unstable release schedules.
  • Stakeholder expectation: Normalized unrealistic requests that are harder to manage later.
Monitor scope changes and post-release defects. If both increase after mid-cycle additions, that is evidence of harmful over-commitment.
Mitigation
Use objective impact statements, time-boxed spikes, and a clear prioritization process to avoid accumulating long-term costs.
Reflection

Which project metric will you track to detect harmful over-commitment: scope changes, defects, or team overtime?
