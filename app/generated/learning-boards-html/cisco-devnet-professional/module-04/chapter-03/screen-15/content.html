Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from RESTCONF Protocol

Data Resources

Represent configuration and operational data defined by YANG modules, accessed via /restconf/data.

Operations Resources

Represent RPCs or actions defined in YANG modules, accessed via /restconf/operations for device-specific functions.

URL Structure

RESTCONF URLs mirror the hierarchical structure of YANG modules, with path segments corresponding to containers, lists, and leaves.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding RESTCONF's resource types and how its URLs map to YANG models is fundamental for precise and effective network automation.
