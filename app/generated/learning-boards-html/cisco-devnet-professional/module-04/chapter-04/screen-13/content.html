Ensuring Consistency: Idempotency and State Management

Designing automation scripts that are reliable and predictable, even when re-run.

Idempotency Defined

An operation that produces the same result regardless of how many times it is executed.

State Management

Understanding and accounting for the current configuration and operational state of a device.

Reliability Through Consistency

Idempotent scripts and careful state management prevent unintended changes and ensure predictable outcomes.
