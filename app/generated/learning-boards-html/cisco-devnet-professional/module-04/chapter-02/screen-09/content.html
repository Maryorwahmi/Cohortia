Ensuring Configuration Integrity with Locks
Preventing conflicts using `lock` and `unlock` operations.

The `lock` Operation

Acquires an exclusive lock on a target configuration datastore (e.g., `running`, `candidate`).

The `unlock` Operation

Releases the lock on a datastore, allowing other clients to make changes.

Importance

Prevents race conditions and ensures transactional integrity in multi-client environments.

Pro Tip

Always ensure to `unlock` the datastore after completing your configuration changes to avoid blocking other clients.
