The Future of Network Management
The Shift to Data-Centric Networking
Embracing YANG for Modern Network Management

Paradigm Shift: From imperative CLIs to declarative data models.

Key Enabler: YANG provides the structured language.

Benefits: Consistency, predictability, interoperability.

Foundation for: Scalable and resilient network automation.

Essential for: Modern network engineers and DevNet Professionals.

Why It Matters

Embracing Data-Centricity:

  • Enables true automation by providing a machine-readable source of truth.
  • Reduces human error and configuration drift.
  • Facilitates integration with broader IT automation ecosystems.
  • Prepares you for advanced network programmability concepts.

This shift is fundamental to managing complex, dynamic networks efficiently.
