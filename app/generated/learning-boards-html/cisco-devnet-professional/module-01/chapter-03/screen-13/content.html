Module 2›Chapter 1.3›Sandbox Types

Always-On Sandboxes

  • **Continuously Available:** Always accessible for immediate use.
  • **Shared Environment:** Used by multiple users, typically with basic configurations.
  • **Periodic Resets:** Changes are not persistent; ideal for quick exploration and simple API calls.

Reservable Sandboxes

  • **Dedicated & Isolated:** Private environment for a specific period (e.g., 4-8 hours).
  • **In-depth Development:** Suitable for complex automation scripts and configuration changes.
  • **Persistent Changes:** Configurations remain for the reservation duration; remember to clean up.
Safety Note

Always respect sandbox environments. Avoid persistent changes in Always-On, and clean up significant configurations in Reservable sandboxes before your reservation ends.

Key Takeaway

Choose between Always-On and Reservable Sandboxes based on your need for quick exploration versus dedicated, in-depth development and testing.
