What are DevNet Sandboxes?

Cisco DevNet Sandboxes are pre-configured, virtualized environments providing access to various Cisco platforms for hands-on practice.

  • **Virtualized Environments:** Access real Cisco devices and controllers without physical hardware.
  • **Pre-configured:** Ready-to-use setups for various Cisco platforms (IOS XE, Meraki, DNA Center, Webex).
  • **Safe Playground:** Experiment with APIs and configurations in an isolated, risk-free environment.
Access Requirement

You'll need a Cisco.com account to access the DevNet Sandbox portal.

Benefits of Using Sandboxes

Real-World Experience

Practice with actual Cisco platforms and APIs.

Cost-Effective Learning

No need to purchase expensive hardware for practice.

Risk-Free Experimentation

Test scripts and configurations without impacting production networks.

Key Takeaway

Cisco DevNet Sandboxes are crucial for gaining practical, hands-on experience with Cisco network automation in a safe, virtualized environment.
