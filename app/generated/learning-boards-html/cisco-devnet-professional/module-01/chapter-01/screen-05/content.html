Problem Statement

Challenges of Traditional Networks

Why Manual Management Fails Modern Demands

Human Error

Manual input leads to frequent mistakes, causing outages, security vulnerabilities, and prolonged troubleshooting.

Slow Scaling

Expanding operations requires more engineers and time, making network growth a significant bottleneck for businesses.

Lack of Consistency

Variations in configurations across devices complicate auditing, compliance, and overall network reliability.

Resource Intensive

Managing large networks manually consumes significant human and financial resources, diverting focus from innovation.

Compliance Burden

Ensuring and proving compliance with regulatory standards becomes a monumental task due to varied configurations.

Slow Service Delivery

New services and applications cannot be deployed rapidly, creating bottlenecks for business agility and innovation.

Key Takeaway

Manual network management is inherently limited by human error, scalability issues, and inconsistency, making it unsuitable for modern, agile IT demands.
