Accessing DevNet Sandboxes

Follow these steps to successfully connect to your chosen DevNet Sandbox environment and retrieve necessary credentials.

01

Log In to DevNet

Use your Cisco.com account to log in to the Cisco DevNet portal.

02

Navigate to Sandboxes

Find the 'Sandboxes' section to browse available environments.

03

Select Your Sandbox

Choose between Always-On or Reservable based on your project needs.

04

Access Credentials

For Always-On, click 'Access' to get API keys. For Reservable, complete the booking process.

05

Review Instructions

Always check specific instructions or limitations for each sandbox environment.

Key Takeaway

Access DevNet Sandboxes by logging into DevNet, navigating to the Sandboxes section, and following specific instructions for Always-On or Reservable environments.
