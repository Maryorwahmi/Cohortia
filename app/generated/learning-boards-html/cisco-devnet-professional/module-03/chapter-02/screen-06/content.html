Always-On Sandboxes

These environments are continuously available and shared among multiple users, making them ideal for rapid, low-stakes experimentation.

Instant Access: No reservation needed, jump in immediately.

Shared Resources: Environment is used by multiple developers.

Ephemeral Changes: Configurations may be overwritten or reset periodically.

Key Takeaway

Always-On Sandboxes are continuously available, shared environments ideal for quick experimentation and testing small code snippets, though changes may not persist.
