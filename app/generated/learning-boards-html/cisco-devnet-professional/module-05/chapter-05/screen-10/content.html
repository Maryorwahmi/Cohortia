Exploring how jobs are executed and the role of specific runners in network automation.

Job Execution

Runners are agents that pick up and execute jobs defined in your .gitlab-ci.yml file.

Specific Runners

Self-managed runners essential for network automation due to required network access and custom environments.

Tagging for Matching

Jobs are assigned tags to specify which registered runner should execute them.

Key Takeaway

Specific GitLab Runners are vital for network automation, providing the necessary connectivity and customized environments to interact with network devices securely and efficiently.
