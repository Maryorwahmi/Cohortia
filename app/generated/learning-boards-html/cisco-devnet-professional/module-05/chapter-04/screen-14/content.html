CI/CD for Network Changes: A Scenario

Choosing the right practice for automated deployments.

Scenario: Network team wants CI/CD but fears fully automated production deployments.

Question: Which CI/CD practice best addresses their concern while leveraging automation?

Answer: Continuous Delivery (CD).

Reasoning: CD ensures changes are always deployable but requires a manual approval gate before production deployment, providing the needed safety net.

This balances automation benefits with human oversight for critical network changes.

Key Takeaway

Continuous Delivery is often the preferred starting point for network automation CI/CD due to its built-in manual approval step for production deployments.

Scenario Focus: Safety First

For critical network infrastructure, maintaining human oversight through a manual approval gate in Continuous Delivery is often paramount.
