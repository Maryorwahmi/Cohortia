Core Concept

Continuous Deployment

Automatically deploying every validated change to production.

Takes Continuous Delivery a step further: every change passing all automated tests is automatically deployed to production.

Requires a very high level of confidence in automated testing and rollback mechanisms.

Less common for critical network infrastructure due to potential for widespread impact.

Offers maximum speed and efficiency but demands robust validation and safety nets.

Key Takeaway

Fully automated production deployment requires extreme confidence in testing and rollback procedures.
