Starting with CI/CD: Common Mistakes

Avoiding pitfalls for a smoother adoption.

Mistake 1: Automating Everything at Once - Leads to overwhelm and scope creep. Start small with simple tasks like linting.

Mistake 2: Skipping or Underestimating Testing - Automated testing is crucial for trust and reliability. Include functional and integration tests.

Mistake 3: Neglecting Rollback Procedures - Always have a tested plan to revert changes if issues arise.

Mistake 4: Rushing Production Deployments - Prioritize safety and validation over speed to build confidence.

Recommendation: Adopt CI/CD incrementally, build confidence, and prioritize safety.

Key Takeaway

Incremental adoption, robust testing, and clear rollback plans are key to successfully implementing CI/CD for network automation.

Common Mistake Alert!

Trying to automate everything at once or skipping crucial testing phases can lead to instability and erode trust in your automation efforts.
