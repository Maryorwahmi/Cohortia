Understanding Ansible's Core Components

Inventory, Playbooks, and Modules drive automation.

The Automation Flow

  • Inventory: Defines target devices.
  • Playbooks: Orchestrate tasks in YAML.
  • Modules: Execute specific actions on devices.
  • Collections: Group vendor-specific modules (e.g., `cisco.ios`).

Why This Structure Works

This modular design allows for clear separation of concerns: the inventory defines 'who' to manage, playbooks define 'what' to do, and modules define 'how' to do it. This makes Ansible highly flexible, scalable, and easy to maintain, as changes to one component don't necessarily impact others.

Key Takeaway

Ansible's architecture relies on a clear separation of inventory, playbooks, and modules to define, orchestrate, and execute automation tasks on managed devices.
