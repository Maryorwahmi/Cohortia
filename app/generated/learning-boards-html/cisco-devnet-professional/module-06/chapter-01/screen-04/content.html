Ansible: The Agentless Automation Engine

Define desired states for your network devices.

What is Agentless Automation?

  • No software agents needed on managed devices.
  • Connects via standard protocols like SSH, NETCONF, RESTCONF.
  • Simplifies deployment and reduces operational overhead.
  • Leverages existing network infrastructure security.

Why it Matters for Networks

Ansible's agentless nature makes it incredibly easy to adopt in diverse network environments. It allows engineers to quickly automate tasks without the complexities of agent installation, maintenance, or compatibility issues across various vendor platforms. This accelerates automation adoption and reduces the barrier to entry for network teams.
