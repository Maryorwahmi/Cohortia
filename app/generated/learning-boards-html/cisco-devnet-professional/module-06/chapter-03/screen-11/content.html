Monitoring Post-Change Impact

Observing KPIs for regressions or performance issues

Why Monitor Impact?

  • •Detect Regressions: Identify unintended negative effects on performance or stability.
  • •Ensure Performance: Confirm the network segment or service performs optimally post-change.
  • •Validate Automation: Gain confidence that automated changes are truly beneficial.
  • •Rapid Analysis: Leverage granular data from modern telemetry for quick issue detection.

Key Areas to Observe

Network KPIs

Latency, packet loss, throughput, jitter.

Device Health

CPU, memory, interface errors, temperature.

Service Performance

Application response times, user experience metrics.

Key Takeaway

Continuous monitoring of KPIs and health metrics after automated changes is crucial for detecting subtle regressions and ensuring the long-term stability and performance of the network.
