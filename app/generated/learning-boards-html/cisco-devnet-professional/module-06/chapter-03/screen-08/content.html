Automation and Monitoring Integration

Ensuring changes are verified and impacts are understood

1. Log the Change

Record what change was made, when, by whom, and on which device to provide a complete audit trail.

2. Verify the Change

Immediately after an automated change, perform post-change verification to confirm the intended outcome.

3. Monitor the Impact

Observe key performance indicators (KPIs) and health metrics to ensure no regressions or performance issues were introduced.

Key Takeaway

Integrating automated changes with logging, verification, and impact monitoring is crucial for maintaining network stability and operational confidence.
