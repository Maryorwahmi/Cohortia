Centralized Logging and Best Practices

Aggregating and analyzing logs for production environments

Benefits of Centralized Logging

  • •Aggregation: Collects logs from all sources into one place.
  • •Analysis: Enables powerful searching, filtering, and trend analysis.
  • •Correlation: Links events across different systems to understand complex issues.
  • •Alerting: Triggers notifications based on predefined log patterns or errors.
  • •Long-term Storage: Provides historical data for auditing and compliance.

Common Centralized Logging Systems

SplunkELK Stack (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana)Grafana Loki

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • •Relying solely on manual verification after automation.
  • •Having insufficient or unstructured logging in scripts.
  • •Not centralizing logs, making troubleshooting difficult.
Key Takeaway

Centralized, structured logging combined with automated verification is a critical best practice for production, enabling proactive problem detection and efficient incident response.
