Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Monitoring

Collecting and tracking data to understand system performance and health, often with predefined alerts.

Observability

Inferring internal system state by examining external outputs (logs, metrics, traces) to understand *why* issues occur.

Telemetry

The process of collecting and transmitting data from remote sources to receiving equipment for monitoring and analysis.

Summary Takeaway

Monitoring, observability, and telemetry are foundational for gaining deep insights into automated network environments, enabling proactive management and troubleshooting.
