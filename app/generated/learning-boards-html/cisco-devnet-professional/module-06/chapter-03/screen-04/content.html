Monitoring vs. Observability

Knowing If vs. Understanding Why

Monitoring

Focuses on collecting predefined metrics and alerts when thresholds are breached. It tells you if something is wrong.

  • •Reactive: Responds to known issues.
  • •Focuses on known unknowns (e.g., CPU > 80%).
  • •Answers questions like: Is the server up? Is bandwidth high?
  • •Tools: SNMP, Syslog, basic dashboards.
Example

An alert fires when a router's interface utilization exceeds 90% for 5 minutes.

Observability

About understanding why something is wrong by asking arbitrary questions about system's internal state.

  • •Proactive: Explores unknown unknowns.
  • •Focuses on deep contextual understanding.
  • •Answers questions like: Why is latency spiking after this change?
  • •Tools: Logs, metrics, traces, advanced analytics.
Example

Analyzing traces and logs to pinpoint a specific automated configuration change that caused a latency spike.

Key Takeaway

Monitoring provides alerts on known issues, while observability offers the tools to deeply investigate and understand the root cause of any system behavior.
