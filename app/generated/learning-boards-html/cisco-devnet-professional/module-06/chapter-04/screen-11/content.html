Operational Security

Comprehensive Auditing and Logging

Tracking every automation action for compliance and threat detection.

What to Log

  • Who initiated the action
  • What changes were made
  • On which devices
  • When the action occurred

Log Management Best Practices

Logs should be immutable, centrally collected, and regularly reviewed for suspicious activity. Integrate with a SIEM system.

Benefits

Provides a comprehensive audit trail for compliance, crucial for forensic analysis, and detects unauthorized changes or breaches.

Key Takeaway

Robust logging is your eyes and ears in an automated environment, essential for security, compliance, and incident response.
