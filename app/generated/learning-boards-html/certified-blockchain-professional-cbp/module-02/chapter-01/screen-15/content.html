Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Symmetric Encryption
Fast confidentiality using a single shared secret key for both encryption and decryption.
Asymmetric Encryption
Public and private key pairs solve key exchange and enable verification without sharing secrets.
Digital Signatures
Hash the data and encrypt the digest with a private key to prove origin and integrity.
Final takeaway

Match primitives to goals: confidentiality — encryption; integrity — hashing; authentication/non-repudiation — signatures. Use canonical formats and protect private keys to maintain security across a distributed system.
