Foundations of Cryptography for Blockchain
Learn why hashing, encryption, and signatures are essential to ledger security, and how they work together to make tampering detectable and actions verifiable.
Integrity: Hashes fingerprint data so any change is obvious.
Authentication: Signatures prove who authorized a transaction.
Verification: Merkle roots let clients prove inclusion efficiently.
In this lesson
You will explore hashing properties, understand symmetric vs asymmetric encryption, build intuition for digital signatures, and try a small Python hashing example to see the avalanche effect.
Topic: Hashing
One-way fingerprinting that secures blocks and detects tampering.
Topic: Signatures
Private-key signatures authorize transactions and enable verification with a public key.
Topic: Merkle Trees
A tree of hashes that allows compact proofs of transaction inclusion.
Concept map

Quick analogy
Hash = fingerprint, Symmetric = single locked box, Asymmetric = matched locked box and key pair, Signature = wax seal proving who signed it.
