Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Digital Signatures
Signing steps
  1. Hash the message or transaction to create a digest.
  2. Encrypt the digest with the signer's private key to produce the signature.
  3. Attach the signature to the message for distribution.
Why sign the hash? Because signing a short fixed digest is efficient and preserves integrity guarantees.
Verification steps
  1. Recompute the hash of the received message.
  2. Decrypt the signature with the signer's public key to recover the signed digest.
  3. Compare the decrypted digest with the recomputed hash; match means valid.
Key Takeaway

Digital signatures provide both integrity and proof of origin. They do not by themselves provide confidentiality.
