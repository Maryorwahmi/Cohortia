Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Hash vs Encrypt vs Sign
Hashing
One-way fingerprint for integrity; irreversible and deterministic.
Use when you need tamper detection and compact identifiers.
Encryption
Two-way secrecy: reversible with the correct key; symmetric for speed, asymmetric for key exchange.
Use when confidentiality is required for data storage or transport.
Signing
Authenticates origin and ensures integrity by signing a hash with a private key and verifying with the public key.
Use to authorize transactions and prove non-repudiation.
Check your choice

Ask: Do I need secrecy, integrity, or proof of origin? The answer points to encryption, hashing, or signing respectively.
