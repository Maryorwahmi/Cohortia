Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Private Key Safety
Why keys matter
A private key is proof you control an address. Anyone holding it can authorize transactions and access funds associated with that address.
Do not store private keys in plain text on connected devices. Prefer hardware wallets or offline cold storage for high-value keys.
Practical protections
  • Use hardware wallets for signing when possible.
  • Keep backups encrypted and offline.
  • Never share your private key or paste it into untrusted sites.
Warning

If your private key is compromised, an attacker can spend your funds immediately. There is usually no recovery mechanism on public chains.
