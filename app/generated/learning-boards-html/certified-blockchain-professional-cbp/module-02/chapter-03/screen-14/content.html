Key Concepts

Three operational terms to remember.

Nonce
A variable miners change to alter a block's hash output during PoW search.
Staking
Locking tokens as collateral to participate as a validator in PoS systems.
Slashing
Penalty that reduces a validator's stake for malicious or negligent actions.
Final takeaway

These operational terms connect the abstract consensus goals to the concrete mechanisms used by PoW and PoS networks.
