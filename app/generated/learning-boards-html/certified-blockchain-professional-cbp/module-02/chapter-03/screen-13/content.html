Key Concepts

Three compact concept cards to remember.

Consensus Mechanism
Protocol that ensures all honest nodes share one ledger state.
Byzantine Generals Problem
A thought experiment modeling unreliable or malicious participants in distributed agreement.
Proof of Work
Miner competition to find a nonce producing a hash under a difficulty target.
Summary

These foundational terms will help you compare protocol choices and reason about ledger security.
