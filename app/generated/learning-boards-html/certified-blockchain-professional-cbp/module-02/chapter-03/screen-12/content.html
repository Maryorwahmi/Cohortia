Process Flow

Practical trade-offs

Match consensus properties to application needs: energy, finality, decentralization and incentives.

Energy considerations
PoW's ongoing power draw may be unacceptable for some projects; PoS reduces these operational costs.
Finality
Faster finality simplifies UX for payments; probabilistic finality requires waiting for confirmations.
Incentives and decentralization
Reward structure, staking distribution, and hardware economies shape who controls consensus.
Guided scenario
Design a small payments chain: list your priority between energy use, transaction latency, and censorship resistance, then choose features that support it.
Misconception check
No single consensus mechanism optimizes every property; trade-offs are unavoidable and should be explicit.
Takeaway
Choose consensus by prioritizing the properties most critical to your application and use incentives to encourage healthy participation.
