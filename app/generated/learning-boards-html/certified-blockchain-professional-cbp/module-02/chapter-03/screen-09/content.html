Core Concept

Proof of Stake mechanics

Validators stake funds and are selected to propose or attest to blocks; slashing penalizes misbehavior.

Staking
Locking funds gives validators the right to participate and aligns incentives with network health.
Selection & rewards
Probability of proposing is often proportional to stake; honest behavior yields rewards and fees.
Slashing
Financial penalties remove misaligned incentives and deter equivocation or downtime.
Concrete example
A validator proposing conflicting blocks can be detected by other validators and lose part of their stake through slashing rules, creating a direct economic cost for misbehavior.
Misconception check
PoS reduces energy but does not automatically remove centralization or governance concentration; stake distribution matters.
Key takeaway
PoS secures chains by risking real value, shifting the defense from hardware costs to economic exposure.
