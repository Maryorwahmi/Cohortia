Process Flow

Proof of Work mechanics

Miners iterate nonces and compute hashes until they meet a difficulty target; the first valid block wins.

Step 1: Assemble block
Collect pending transactions, include previous block hash and timestamp.
Step 2: Search nonce
Try different nonce values until the resulting hash meets the difficulty target.
Step 3: Broadcast and verify
Other nodes verify the hash quickly and accept the block if valid, moving the chain forward.
Key Takeaway

PoW secures the chain by making block creation expensive while keeping verification cheap.

Annotated example
Block fields: index, timestamp, transactions, previous_hash, nonce. The miner changes nonce until the hash passes the difficulty check.
Energy trade-off
Higher difficulty increases expected work exponentially, increasing energy consumption but improving resistance to tampering.
Misconception check
Finding a nonce is random and probabilistic; speed depends on hash rate and difficulty, not on clever algebraic shortcuts.
