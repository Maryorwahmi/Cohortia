PoW vs PoS

Security, energy, and decentralization are shaped by whether the protocol relies on computation or economic stake.

Security model
PoW: cost in hardware and energy. PoS: cost in staked currency and penalties.
Energy
PoW consumes significant electricity. PoS uses far less operational energy.
Decentralization pressure
PoW favors hardware-rich miners; PoS favors large stakeholders — both can centralize without careful design.
Concrete example
Ethereum's transition from PoW to PoS prioritized lower energy consumption and different scaling properties, illustrating a design trade-off rather than a universal improvement.
Misconception check
Neither model is a silver bullet; they reflect different threat models and priorities.
Reflective question
