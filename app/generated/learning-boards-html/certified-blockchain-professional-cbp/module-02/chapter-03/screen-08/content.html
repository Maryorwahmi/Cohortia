PoW: security vs cost

PoW secures the network by making attacks computationally expensive, but this comes with real operational costs.

Security model
Attackers must control more hashing power than honest miners to rewrite history, which is costly.
Energy cost
Continuous mining consumes large amounts of electricity and favors specialized hardware and economies of scale.
Misconception
The work is not pointless; it is the economic barrier that secures the ledger.
Concrete scenario
If a miner pools resources to dominate hash power, they could temporarily reorganize recent blocks. The solution is decentralization of mining and high cumulative difficulty across many blocks to reduce risk.
Why it matters
Understanding PoW trade-offs helps you evaluate networks prioritizing censorship resistance versus sustainability.
