Consensus Mechanisms

Understand how decentralized networks achieve agreement and why that agreement is secure or vulnerable depending on the mechanism used.

In this lesson

We will define consensus, study the Byzantine Generals Problem, compare Proof of Work and Proof of Stake, and run a simplified mining simulation to see difficulty in action.

Coming up

Practical trade-offs: security, energy, and decentralization — and how network incentives shape participant behavior.

Proof of Work

Proof of Stake
