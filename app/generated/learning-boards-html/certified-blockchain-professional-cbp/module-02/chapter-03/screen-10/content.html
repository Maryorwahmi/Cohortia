PoS challenges and mitigations

Addressing nothing at stake and centralization requires penalties, stake distribution, and good protocol incentives.

Nothing at stake
If voting on multiple chains is free, validators could support conflicting histories without immediate cost; slashing deters this.
Centralization risk
Large stakers can concentrate influence; delegation and reward design encourage broader participation.
Protocol mitigations
Slashing, randomized selection, and reward structures are combined to reduce incentives for misbehavior and centralization.
Concrete scenario
A validator caught signing two conflicting blocks can be slashed and removed for a period, directly making equivocation costly and protecting finality.
