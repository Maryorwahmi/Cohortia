Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Hot vs Cold
Hot Wallets

Connected, convenient

Examples: mobile wallets, web wallets, desktop wallets. They are ideal for frequent use but expose keys to online threats.

Pros: immediate access, easy dApp integration, user-friendly.
Cons: online attack surface, phishing and malware risks.
Example use
Daily spending and quick interactions with decentralized applications.
Cold Wallets

Offline, secure

Examples: hardware wallets and paper wallets. Best for long-term storage of large amounts because keys are isolated from networked devices.

Pros: minimal remote attack surface, strong protection for holdings.
Cons: less convenient for frequent use, requires physical custody and backups.
Example use
Long-term holdings or assets you cannot risk losing to remote compromise.
Practical rule
Keep only small, spendable balances in hot wallets. Move bulk funds to cold storage and keep mnemonic backups offline and physically separated.
