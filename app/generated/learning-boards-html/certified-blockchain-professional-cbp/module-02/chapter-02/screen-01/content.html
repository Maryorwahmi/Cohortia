Public Key Infrastructure and Wallet Management

Learn why keys are identity on-chain, how wallets work as key managers, and what practices protect your assets. This chapter focuses on concepts and sound habits rather than implementation detail.

In this lesson
  • How key pairs establish ownership and sign transactions.
  • Wallet types and trade-offs: hot vs cold, hardware vs software.
  • Mnemonic phrases for deterministic recovery and best practices.
Why it matters

Your private key is the single point of control for funds. Understanding wallet choices and recovery methods protects you from loss and theft.
