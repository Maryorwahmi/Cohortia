Practical checklist to secure private keys

Adopt repeatable protections to reduce risk of theft or loss.

Minimize online exposure
Never store mnemonics or private keys in cloud drives, email, or as phone photos.
Use hardware wallets
Prefer hardware devices for significant funds and confirm firmware and vendor trust.
Create durable backups
Write seed phrases on paper or metal and store copies in separate secure locations.
Test recovery
Practice restoring a wallet from a test mnemonic so you know the steps before an emergency.
Final advice
Split responsibilities: keep a small hot wallet for daily use and move long-term reserves to cold storage guarded by a hardware wallet and physical backups.
