What to Remember

PKI & Keys
Private keys sign; public keys verify and derive addresses.
Wallets
Wallets manage keys; funds remain on-chain at addresses.
Recovery
Mnemonic phrases deterministically recreate master seeds and keys; guard them offline.
Final takeaway

Design custody around risk: convenience for small amounts, cold storage and strong backups for significant value.
