Cold Storage

Hardware wallets

Devices that store private keys securely and sign transactions internally. They require explicit physical confirmation for operations, reducing remote threats.

Benefits
Hardware isolation, resilience to malware, clear UX for signing.
Limitations
Cost, need for safe physical storage, and possible supply-chain risks if not purchased securely.

Paper wallets

Paper wallets are printed private keys or seed phrases stored offline. They are inexpensive but fragile and require careful generation and environmental protection.

Handling tips
  • Generate on an air-gapped device when possible.
  • Store backups in fireproof, waterproof safes in separate locations.
  • Use hardware wallets for significant balances and paper for secondary backups.
Common mistake

Creating a single paper copy and keeping it in an obvious place is risky. Use separated backups and durable storage.
