Definition

Mnemonic / Seed Phrase

A sequence of common words that represents a master seed. The seed deterministically generates private keys in HD wallets.

12, 18, or 24 words are common. More words increase entropy and the difficulty of guessability.
If you lose your device but have the mnemonic, you can recover all accounts. If you lose the mnemonic, you lose access permanently.
Key Takeaway

A mnemonic is the recovery master key. Do not store it digitally and keep multiple secure physical copies.

Example & Guidance
Concrete example
A 12-word phrase written on paper and stored in two separate safes lets you recover accounts even if one copy is destroyed. Never photograph or store it in cloud backups.
Security guidance
  • Write the phrase in ink on paper or metal backup.
  • Store copies in physically separate, secure locations.
  • Consider using hardware wallets that display and manage the mnemonic securely.
