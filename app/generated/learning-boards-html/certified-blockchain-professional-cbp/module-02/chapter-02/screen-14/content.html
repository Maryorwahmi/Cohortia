Match wallets to needs

Choose a wallet based on transaction frequency, value at risk, and tolerance for inconvenience.

Everyday use
Mobile or browser wallets for low-value, high-frequency payments and dApp interaction.
Long-term savings
Hardware wallets and cold storage for large balances and long-term holdings.
Institutional custody
Multi-signature and geographically separated keys to distribute control and reduce single-point failure risk.
Key Takeaway

A layered approach—keep a small hot wallet for spending and a cold wallet for reserves—balances usability with security.
