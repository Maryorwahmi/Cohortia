What to Remember

Quick Reference

On-chain guarantees
Only put logic and state on-chain when you need tamper-proof execution and public verification.
Plan upgrades
Immutability is powerful but prepare governance or upgrade patterns to address bugs or feature changes.
Storage strategy
Use IPFS or Arweave for large files and ensure availability with pinning or distributed hosting plans.
Final Thought

Use the architecture checklist and activity to practice mapping requirements to on-chain and off-chain solutions before building.
