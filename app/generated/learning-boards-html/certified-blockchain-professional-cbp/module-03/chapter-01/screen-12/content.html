What to Remember

Key Concepts

Architecture Layers
Frontend, web3 bridge, smart contracts, ledger, decentralized storage.
Immutability
On-chain rules are permanent unless explicit upgrade patterns exist.
Decentralized Storage
Store large files off-chain and reference them on-chain with content hashes.
Summary

Design DApps by anchoring critical guarantees on-chain and using decentralized storage for bulky data to balance trust, cost, and performance.
