Use Cases

Where DApps are used today

DeFi
Lending, borrowing, swaps, and insurance run via smart contracts so users interact without intermediaries.
Example: decentralized exchanges and lending protocols.
NFTs
Marketplaces and token standards prove ownership and provenance for unique digital items.
Example: marketplaces that store art off-chain and ownership on-chain.
DAOs
Organizations governed by token-based voting and smart contract enforced decisions.
Example: community governance for protocol upgrades.
Gaming
On-chain item ownership and provable scarcity for play-to-own experiences.
Example: in-game assets represented as tokens.
Supply Chain
Tamper-evident tracking and provenance across parties using on-chain records.
Example: immutable audit trails for shipments and goods.
Design note
Not every domain benefits equally from decentralization; evaluate throughput, privacy, and governance trade-offs before committing to on-chain designs.
