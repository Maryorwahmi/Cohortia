Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Common Use Cases
Escrow
Hold funds until conditions are met, then release automatically to the designated recipient.
Supply chain
Record checkpoints and trigger payments when items reach milestones to improve traceability.
Voting
Immutable vote records and verifiable tallies, with careful attention to privacy and identity.
DeFi
Lending, exchanges, and composable financial primitives running without central custodians.
Tokens & NFTs
Standards codify behavior so wallets and other contracts can interact reliably with tokens and unique assets.
Key takeaway
Choose minimal, auditable logic for each use case and consider how off-chain data and costs affect feasibility.
