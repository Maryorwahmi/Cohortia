Why oracles are needed
Contracts are isolated from the outside world. Oracles feed verified external data into on-chain logic so contracts can respond to real events.
Design considerations
  • Trust model: single-source vs decentralized feed.
  • Latency and availability requirements.
  • Verification and redundancy to reduce manipulation risk.
Concrete example
A lending platform depends on reliable price feeds to value collateral. Use of a decentralized oracle network reduces risk of a single manipulated feed causing incorrect liquidations.
Misconception
An oracle does not remove all trust requirements; it shifts trust to the oracle provider or network design.
