Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Gas & Costs
What is gas?
A fee model that charges for computation and storage to prevent abuse and allocate resources.
Design tips
  • Minimize storage writes; they are expensive.
  • Avoid large on-chain loops; prefer indexing or batching.
  • Push heavy computation off-chain and verify results on-chain when safe.
Concrete example
An on-chain loop over many items can be replaced with periodic batched updates that spread gas across multiple transactions, reducing per-user cost spikes.
Why it matters

Gas affects usability and adoption. Costly interactions discourage users, so optimize for common paths and consider off-chain work when appropriate.
