Comparison

Full node vs Light client

Full nodes store and verify entire blocks; light clients verify inclusion with Merkle proofs and a header, saving resources.

Full node
Downloads full blocks, validates transactions and headers independently.
Light client
Keeps headers and Merkle proofs; verifies inclusion without full transaction sets.
Practical use
Mobile wallets often use light-client techniques to confirm transactions without requiring full-node resources.
Key Takeaway

Merkle proofs reduce verification cost dramatically, enabling secure participation from constrained devices.
