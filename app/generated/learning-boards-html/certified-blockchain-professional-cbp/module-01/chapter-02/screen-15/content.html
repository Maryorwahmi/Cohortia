Previous Block Hash
The literal hash of the preceding block that forms the chain link.
Nonce & Difficulty
Parameters in the header used to meet Proof of Work constraints for block acceptance.
Genesis Block
The initial block with a placeholder previous-hash that anchors the entire chain.
Final takeaway

Together, these components let distributed participants detect tampering, validate blocks, and confirm transactions efficiently across diverse devices.
