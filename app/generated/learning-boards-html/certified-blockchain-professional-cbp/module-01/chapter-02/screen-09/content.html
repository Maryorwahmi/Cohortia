How it works

Nonce searches and difficulty

Miners iterate the nonce until the block header hash meets a difficulty requirement, which secures block production by requiring computational work.

Nonce
A trial value adjusted to change the header hash in repeated attempts to meet difficulty.
Difficulty target
A threshold that the header hash must satisfy, determining how hard it is to find a valid hash.
Analogy
Think of miners trying random nonces like rolling a die until you get a specific rare result; difficulty makes that rare result rarer.
Why it matters

Difficulty and nonce together make rewriting history costly, because recomputing many valid headers requires enormous work.
