DLT: a family of approaches

Blockchain is a type of DLT, but other architectures exist with different ordering and performance trade-offs.

Blockchain
Append-only blocks linked by hashes; strong history guarantees and widely used in public networks.
DAGs & alternatives
Directed acyclic graphs and other approaches aim for higher throughput with different ordering models.
Permissioned DLT
Enterprise-focused implementations prioritize governance, privacy, and performance over public openness.
Design prompt
Match the architecture to requirements: auditability and censorship resistance favor public blockchains; privacy and throughput favor permissioned or hybrid systems.
Key Takeaway

Treat DLT as a toolbox. Select the right tool within the family to match privacy, governance, and performance needs.
