Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Public vs Private DLTs
Public DLT

Open participation

Anyone can read and often propose entries. Great for transparency and censorship resistance.

Pros
High auditability and decentralization.
Cons
Potential privacy exposure and scaling challenges.
Permissioned DLT

Controlled participation

Only approved organizations validate and view certain data, useful for enterprise workflows.

Pros
Better privacy controls and potentially higher throughput.
Cons
Requires governance agreements among participants and careful trust assumptions.
Concrete decision guide
  • Choose public when auditability and openness are primary goals.
  • Choose permissioned when privacy, regulatory compliance, or performance are essential.
  • Remember: permissioned systems can still distribute validation across independent organizations.
Key Takeaway

Pick the DLT model that aligns with privacy, governance, and performance needs rather than following labels alone.
