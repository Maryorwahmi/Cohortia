Applied Competency

Turn each objective into a small, testable task you can demonstrate.

Practical checks
01
Explain core properties
Say what decentralization, immutability, transparency and cryptography mean, and give a one-line concrete example for each.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Contrast architectures
Describe a centralized bank ledger and a distributed ledger, and pick a scenario where DLT improves trust.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Weigh trade-offs
List benefits and name one mitigation for a challenge like scale or energy cost in your chosen example.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply to a use case
Pick supply chain or payments and explain how a shared immutable record improves verification and reduces intermediaries.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
