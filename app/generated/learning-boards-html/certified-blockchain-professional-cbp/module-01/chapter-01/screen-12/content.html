Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Benefits & Challenges

Benefits

Resilience
No single point of failure because many nodes hold copies.
Auditability
Immutable history simplifies verification and compliance checks.
Reduced intermediaries
Shared truth can enable direct peer-to-peer settlement in some workflows.

Challenges

Scalability & latency
Coordinating many nodes can slow throughput compared to centralized systems.
Energy & consensus cost
Some consensus mechanisms consume significant resources; design choices matter.
Governance & interoperability
Agreeing governance and connecting different ledgers requires careful standards and legal work.
Mitigations
Use permissioned networks, hybrid on/off-chain designs, or layer solutions to manage performance and privacy while keeping shared validation where it matters.
Key Takeaway

DLT delivers strong assurances but requires deliberate design choices to balance performance, privacy, and governance.
