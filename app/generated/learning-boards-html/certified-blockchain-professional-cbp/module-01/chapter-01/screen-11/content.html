How DLT helps
Immutable, shared entries reduce disputes and allow auditors to verify provenance without relying on any single party's internal logs.
Practical constraint
On-chain records depend on reliable off-chain inputs; use tamper-evident seals or IoT sensors to ensure real-world integrity.
Key Takeaway

DLT provides an auditable provenance trail, but trust in physical events requires reliable off-chain verification methods.
