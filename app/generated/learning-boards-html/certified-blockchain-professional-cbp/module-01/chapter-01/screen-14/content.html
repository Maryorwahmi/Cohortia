Distributed Ledger Technology
A decentralized, append-only system where multiple participants validate and store records.
Immutability
Entries are linked cryptographically so tampering is immediately evident.
Transparency & Pseudonymity
Participants can verify transactions while identities are often represented by cryptographic addresses.
Summary

DLT combines distributed copies, shared validation, cryptographic integrity, and flexible visibility settings to produce tamper-evident systems suited to auditability and trust minimization.
