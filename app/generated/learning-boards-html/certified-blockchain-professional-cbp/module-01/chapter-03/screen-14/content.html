Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Decentralization
Distribution of control and data across independent nodes.
Public-Key Cryptography
A private-public key pair used for signing and verification.
Digital Signature
A proof that a message was authored by a private key holder and was not altered.
Summary

These concepts together allow many untrusted participants to exchange value securely by relying on protocol rules and cryptographic proofs rather than a central intermediary.
