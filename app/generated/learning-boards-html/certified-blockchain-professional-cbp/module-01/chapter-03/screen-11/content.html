Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › PoW vs PoS
Proof of Work
  • Selection by computational race; requires energy and hardware
  • Security comes from cost of computation and difficulty of controlling majority hashing
  • Main downside: high energy use and potential mining centralization
Proof of Stake
  • Selection often weighted by amount staked and randomness
  • Security comes from economic penalties and the cost of losing stake if validators misbehave
  • Main downside: staking concentration and design of slashing rules affect decentralization
Concrete trade-off
PoW makes attacks expensive through continuing operational cost; PoS ties security to locked capital, which attackers would risk losing if detected. Both rely on broad distribution to remain resilient.
Key Takeaway

Choose consensus by weighing energy, throughput, economic incentives, and decentralization goals.
