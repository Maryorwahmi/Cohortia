Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Pillars in Concert
Architecture view

Security is the emergent property when decentralization, cryptographic proofs, and consensus constraints operate together. Each pillar covers gaps the others leave open.

Decentralization
Provides redundancy and independent verification.
Cryptography
Ensures messages are authentic and tamper-evident.
Consensus
Orders transactions and makes reorganization costly.
Trade-off example
If you relax decentralization to gain speed, you may reduce the work or stake required for consensus to be effective, making cryptographic guarantees easier to subvert with fewer compromised nodes.
Key Takeaway

Design choices in one pillar affect the security properties offered by the others; treat them as an integrated system.
