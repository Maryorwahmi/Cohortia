Decentralization, Cryptography, and Consensus

Explore the three pillars that secure blockchain networks and enable trustless interaction between participants.

16:9 HD Classroom
Orientation

You will understand what decentralization does for resilience, how public-key cryptography proves who created a transaction, and why consensus protocols are necessary so many independent nodes can agree on a single ledger. Practical examples and a small Python demo will make each idea concrete.

Explore Key Topics
Decentralization
Multiple nodes hold full ledger copies, removing single points of control and censorship.
Cryptography
Private and public keys create signatures that prove ownership and prevent tampering.
Consensus
Protocols like Proof of Work and Proof of Stake let nodes agree on the next block by economic or computational rules.
Visual concept: the three pillars

Nodes & Replication
Every full node verifies and stores the full ledger to prevent single points of failure.
Cryptography
Keys & Signatures

Consensus
Agreement Rules
