Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Explain decentralization

Describe how multiple independent nodes store and verify ledger copies, why that removes single points of failure, and how it supports censorship resistance. Practice with an example of a government trying to block transactions on a centralized service versus a distributed network.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain public-key cryptography

Explain private and public keys, how signatures provide authenticity and integrity, and why losing a private key means losing control of assets. Relate this to real ownership of a cryptocurrency address.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Distinguish PoW and PoS

Show the selection and security differences between Proof of Work and Proof of Stake, note energy impacts, and explain attacks like a 51% attack in PoW or slashing incentives in PoS.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Link the three pillars

Explain how decentralization, cryptography, and consensus combine to create a secure and immutable ledger, and reason about trade-offs between security, scalability, and decentralization.
