Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Implementation Hurdles
Key hurdles
  • Data format standardization across participants
  • Legacy system integration and middleware
  • Transaction volume and aggregation strategies
  • Ensuring off-chain data integrity (secure sensors, tag application)
Practical mitigations
Agree on event schemas, use signed IoT snapshots, employ aggregators for high-frequency data, and certify physical-to-digital processes.
Contrast
Permissioned frameworks support enterprise privacy and identity controls, which often fit supply chain needs better than public chains.
