Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Cold Chain Monitoring
Architecture components
  • IoT sensors capturing temperature and humidity snapshots
  • Secure edge gateway that signs or aggregates readings
  • On-chain digest or hash of a batch of readings for immutability
  • Off-chain store for detailed logs retrievable for audits
Design note
Aggregate and sign readings before writing to the ledger to control transaction volume and cost.
Concrete scenario
A pharma company writes hourly digests of temperature readings for a refrigerated truck. If a threshold is breached, the system flags the shipment and the on-chain digest proves when the event occurred and who signed the snapshot.
Key Takeaway

Balance evidentiary needs and scalability: signed digests with off-chain logs give verifiable proof without overwhelming the ledger.
