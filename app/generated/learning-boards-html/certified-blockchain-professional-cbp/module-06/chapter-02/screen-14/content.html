Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Supply Chain Management
Management of the flow of goods and processes from raw materials to final products.
Product Provenance
The recorded history of a product’s origin, custody, and certifications.
Anti-Counterfeiting
Measures that prevent fake or unauthorized copies by verifying authenticity against a ledger.
Summary Takeaway
These concepts form the vocabulary you will use to design pilots: map problems to these solutions before choosing technology.
