Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Off-chain Integrity
Problem statement
The ledger preserves whatever data is written. If initial capture is compromised, records will be accurate but false.
Mitigation patterns
Tamper-evident tags, device attestation, signed snapshots, audited application procedures, and dual-record verification.
Operational control
Record who applied identifiers and under what conditions so the physical-to-digital link is auditable.
Concrete example
If a counterfeiter can replicate NFC tags, you must combine tamper-evidence and secure application to prevent false registrations. Record application events on-chain with signer identity to deter fraud.
Key Takeaway

Design capture and signing controls first. Treat the ledger as a recorder of proven inputs, not a validator of raw truth.
