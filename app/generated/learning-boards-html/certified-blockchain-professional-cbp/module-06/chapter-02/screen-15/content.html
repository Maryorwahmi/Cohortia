Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Cold Chain Monitoring
Ensuring and proving that temperature-sensitive products remain within defined ranges.
Shared Ledger
A distributed, verifiable database that multiple participants can read and—where permitted—write to.
Immutability
A property ensuring recorded data cannot be altered without detection, creating reliable audit trails.
Final summary
Combine secure capture, agreed schemas, and on-chain digests to design supply chain pilots that reduce recall time, cut waste, and protect brand trust.
