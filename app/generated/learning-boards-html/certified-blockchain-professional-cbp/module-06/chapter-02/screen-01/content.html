Supply Chain Management and Logistics with Blockchain
Create verifiable product histories, prevent counterfeits, and add accountable IoT data to the record.
In this lesson
You will learn the blockchain properties that map to supply chain problems, inspect practical use cases, and walk through a conceptual traceability simulation that validates immutability.
Why it matters
Faster recalls, verified provenance, and accountability for temperature-sensitive goods can reduce waste, financial risk, and customer distrust.
