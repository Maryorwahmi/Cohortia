Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Benefits for Stakeholders
Consumers
Verified provenance and authenticity lead to greater trust and safer purchases.
Businesses
Faster recalls, fewer disputes, and lower fraud losses improve margins and reputation.
Regulators
Immutable records simplify compliance checks and incident investigations.
Concrete metric
Measure pilot success by reduction in time-to-trace and the shrinkage of recall scope — these directly map to cost savings and public safety improvements.
