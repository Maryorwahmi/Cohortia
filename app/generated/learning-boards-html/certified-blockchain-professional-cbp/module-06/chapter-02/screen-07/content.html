Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Provenance vs Anti-Counterfeiting
Product Provenance
A complete, timestamped history of a product from origin to sale. Useful for regulatory compliance and consumer trust.
Example
Trace coffee beans from farm certification through roasting and retail receipts.
Benefit
Fast recalls and verified origin claims.
Anti-Counterfeiting
Bind a physical identifier to a ledger record so scanning the identifier verifies authenticity.
Example
Luxury watch with an NFC tag recorded at manufacture and verifiable by a consumer scan.
Vulnerability
Copying or reprogramming a physical identifier if physical application and packaging are not secured.
