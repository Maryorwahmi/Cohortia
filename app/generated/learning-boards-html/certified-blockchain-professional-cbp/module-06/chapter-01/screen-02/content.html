Learning Objectives

Learn, understand, apply, and master core DeFi and CBDC concepts.

Bloom Band: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain DeFi fundamentals

Define permissionless protocols, smart contracts, and why DeFi replaces intermediaries. You will describe common components and why transparency matters.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify DeFi protocols

Recognize DEXs, lending markets, and stablecoins and explain their role in trading, liquidity, and collateralized lending.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Analyze DeFi risks

Assess smart contract vulnerabilities, impermanent loss, and fees; learn mitigation patterns and how to reason about protocol safety.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Describe CBDC trade-offs

Explain central-bank motivations, compare retail and wholesale CBDC designs, and discuss privacy and compliance trade-offs.
