Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Liquidity Pool
Funds held in a smart contract that provide liquidity for trading and other operations.
Stablecoin
A token designed to maintain a stable value for use as a medium of exchange inside DeFi.
Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC)
Digital fiat issued by a central bank, designed for retail or wholesale uses with policy-driven design choices.
Final Takeaway

Use these concept cards as a checklist: what rails a system uses, how value is preserved, and who controls change determine real-world behavior and risk.
