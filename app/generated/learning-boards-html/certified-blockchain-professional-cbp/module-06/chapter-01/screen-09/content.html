Hierarchy

Protocols as composable primitives

One protocol’s output can be another’s input, enabling layered financial strategies and automation.

Example stack

Supply assets to a lending market, receive supply tokens, deposit those into a yield aggregator to increase returns.

Systemic risk

A bug or depeg in one component can cascade through dependent contracts, amplifying losses.

Pro Tip

Favor minimal, audited stacks for high-value strategies and explicitly plan rollback or migration paths for complex deployments.

Concrete question

Before composing protocols, ask: which contracts can update their logic, and who has the authority to do so? Design decisions here affect trust assumptions.

Key Takeaway

Composability accelerates innovation but requires explicit dependency and failure modeling to remain safe.
