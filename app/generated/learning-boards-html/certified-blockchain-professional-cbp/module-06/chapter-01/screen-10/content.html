Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › DeFi Risks
Process

Identify and mitigate key hazards

Technical

Smart contract bugs, reentrancy, and oracle manipulation are primary concerns.

Economic

Impermanent loss, liquidity crunches, and volatile collateral values drive economic risk.

Operational

High fees and UX mistakes can lead to failed transactions or unintended exposure.

Regulatory

Legal changes can alter protocol viability or user access, creating compliance risk.

Mitigation

Use audits, diversified exposure, position limits, and prefer time-locked upgrades and clear governance to reduce systemic exposure.

Concrete example

An oracle attack that reports stale prices can trigger mass liquidations in a lending market, cascading through composable positions held as collateral elsewhere.

Key Takeaway

Mitigate with layered defenses: audits, oracles redundancy, governance transparency, and cautious economic sizing.
