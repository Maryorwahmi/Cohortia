Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Stablecoins
Definition

Stablecoins as settlement rails

They provide predictable value to enable trading, lending, and accounting in DeFi ecosystems.

Fiat-backed

Backed by reserves held by an issuer and redeemable for fiat under certain terms.

Crypto-collateralized

Over-collateralized with crypto to absorb volatility and maintain the peg.

Algorithmic

Use on-chain mechanisms to expand or contract supply to preserve peg, with varied stability properties.

Concrete scenario

A borrower prefers to borrow a stablecoin to keep debt predictable. If the stablecoin loses its peg, liquidation mechanics become more complex and risk increases.

Key Takeaway

Stablecoins are essential in DeFi but must be evaluated on backing quality and peg robustness before use as collateral.

Check

When would you prefer a fiat-backed stablecoin over an algorithmic one? Consider reserve transparency and redemption options.
