Architecture

Supply, borrow, collateral, and liquidation

Smart contracts track supplied assets, calculate borrowing power, and enforce liquidation when collateral falls short.

Component: Supply

Deposit asset into pool and receive a tokenized representation that accrues interest.

Component: Borrow

Borrow against collateral up to the protocol-defined limit; interest accrues on the borrowed amount.

Common Mistake

Ignoring liquidation thresholds. Borrowing near the maximum increases liquidation risk when collateral prices fall.

Concrete walkthrough

Supply testnet ETH, observe supply balance increase, and then borrow a stable asset well under your borrowing power. Confirm transactions and note gas fees and wait times.

Key Takeaway

Treat the protocol as the counterparty: check collateral factors, interest model, and liquidation mechanics before acting.
