Lab Walkthrough

Simulate supply and borrow on a testnet

Step 1 — Prepare

Configure a wallet for the test network and obtain a small amount of test ETH from a faucet to cover transactions.

Step 2 — Connect

Connect the wallet to the testnet deployment of a lending protocol and inspect the contract address and market parameters before proceeding.

Step 3 — Supply & Borrow

Supply a small amount of ETH, confirm the transaction, then borrow a conservative amount of a stable token, watching the borrow balance and gas used.

Reflection

Record observed gas fees, how long confirmations took, and whether UI feedback matched on-chain events. Consider what would change on mainnet and which safeguards you would add.

Concrete scenario

Supply 0.1 testnet ETH, then borrow a small stable amount well under your borrowing power and monitor the supply and borrow balances after confirmation.

Key Takeaway

Practice on testnets to learn transaction flow and smart contract effects without risking real funds; use conservative amounts and observe gas behavior.
