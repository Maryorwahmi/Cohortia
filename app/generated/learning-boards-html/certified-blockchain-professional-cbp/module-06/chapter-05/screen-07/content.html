Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Layer-0 & Atomic Swaps
Layer-0 Protocols
Foundations that connect multiple layer-1 chains to enable shared messaging and security guarantees.
Benefit: broad connectivity and the potential for unified cross-chain primitives.
Trade-off: requires cross-chain governance and coordination, and can introduce systemic risk across connected chains.
Use case: applications that require composability and shared state across many chains.
Analogy
Think of a layer-0 as a regional transit hub connecting many local networks; it simplifies travel but requires centralized schedule coordination.
Atomic Swaps
Direct, peer-to-peer exchanges that complete atomically without intermediaries.
Benefit: low-trust exchange suitable for two-party swaps.
Trade-off: limited to exchange scenarios and often requires compatible script or contract capabilities on both chains.
Use case: direct token swaps between users who do not want to rely on a bridge operator.

Would you choose a layer-0 connection or an atomic swap for enabling a cross-chain lending protocol? Consider required features: shared state, composability, and speed.
