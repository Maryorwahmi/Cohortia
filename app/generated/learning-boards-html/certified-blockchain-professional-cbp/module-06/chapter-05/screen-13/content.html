Compliance & design roadmap
01
Legal assessment

Obtain token classification opinions and map obligations across target jurisdictions.

02
Modular architecture

Separate compliance layers (KYC, reporting) from core protocol contracts.

03
Operational controls

Implement monitoring, audits, and incident response plans to satisfy regulators and users.

Societal implications

Blockchain can increase transparency and reduce intermediaries, but it can also concentrate power if governance or infrastructure is unevenly distributed.

Concrete scenario

A payments dApp may restrict certain features in regions without clear rules, while enabling full functionality where legal clarity exists, balancing compliance and user value.

Key Takeaway

Plan for multiple legal outcomes. Build adaptable systems that protect users, meet regulators, and preserve decentralization where possible.
