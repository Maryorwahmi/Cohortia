Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Regulatory Landscape
Overview
Global regulatory focus areas
Fragmented rules, shared goals
Consumer protection

Disclosure, fraud prevention, and clear risk communication for retail users.

AML / KYC

Transaction monitoring and identity checks to counter illicit finance, guided by FATF standards.

Taxation

How gains, income, and token transfers are reported and taxed per jurisdiction.

Regulatory fragmentation and arbitrage

Because rules differ, projects may register or operate where rules are favorable. This can spur innovation but also produces legal uncertainty and uneven consumer protections.

FATF

Global AML guidance that many jurisdictions implement into local rules.

SEC

A regulator focused on securities classification and investor protection in the U.S.

MiCA

An EU framework aimed at clarifying rules for crypto-assets and consumer safeguards.

Key Takeaway

Plan for multiple regulatory outcomes. Clear compliance and transparency reduce legal and operational risk when operating across borders.
