Architecture
Onboarding flow: reduce cognitive load
  1. Enable fiat on-ramps with clear confirmations and receipts.
  2. Offer social recovery or custodial-recovery choices with explicit trade-offs.
  3. Abstract gas fee estimation and present human-friendly labels for actions.
  4. Provide safe defaults and reversible steps when possible.
Concrete example

A wallet that explains a gas fee as "priority cost: faster confirmation" and offers a single recommended option reduces hesitation and costly mistakes.

Design patterns
Account Abstraction

Allow wallets to behave like regular web accounts, supporting recovery and fee sponsorship.

Social Recovery

Enable trusted contacts to help recover access without exposing private keys.

Fee Abstraction

Let dApps sponsor or simplify fee payments so users do not need native tokens for every action.

Pitfall

Hiding cryptography without explaining trade-offs can reduce user control; always present clear choices and risks.

Key Takeaway

Design for human mental models: protect users from irreversible errors and explain trade-offs in plain language.
