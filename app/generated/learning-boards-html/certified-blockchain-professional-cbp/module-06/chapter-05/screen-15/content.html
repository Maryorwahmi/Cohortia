Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Scalability
The ability of a network to handle growing transaction volume without degradation.
Sustainability
Environmental footprint of consensus and infrastructure, critical to perception and policy.
MiCA
An EU regulatory framework providing legal clarity and consumer protections for crypto-assets.
Summary Takeaway

Technical design, environmental impact, and legal clarity together determine whether blockchain systems achieve sustainable, mainstream use.
