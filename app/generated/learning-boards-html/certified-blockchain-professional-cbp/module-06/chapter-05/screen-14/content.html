Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Quantum-Resistant Cryptography
Algorithms designed to remain secure against quantum attacks, protecting keys and signatures.
Interoperability
The ability for blockchains to exchange data and assets, enabling a unified Web3 economy.
Cross-Chain Bridge
Mechanisms that move tokens or data between chains, via locking/minting or swap primitives.
Summary Takeaway

These concepts determine technical choices and risk models; evaluate them together when designing multi-chain systems.
