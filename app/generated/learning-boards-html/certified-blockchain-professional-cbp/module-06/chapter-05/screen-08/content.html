Scalability: Layer-2 Rollups
Layer-2 rollups batch transactions off-chain and post succinct proofs or data to layer-1 to reduce fees and increase throughput.
Benefit: higher transaction throughput and lower per-user fees.
Consideration: withdrawal delays, data availability strategies, and prover infrastructure are essential design choices.
Example: rollups can enable many small transactions cheaply, but teams must maintain strong monitoring of fraud or validity proofs.
Consensus: Proof-of-Stake
Proof-of-Stake replaces energy-intensive mining with validator selection based on stake, reducing electricity use and changing economic incentives.
Benefit: significantly lower energy consumption and potential for faster finality.
Trade-off: requires careful design of staking economics, slashing, and decentralization incentives.
Example: a chain moving to Proof-of-Stake reduces environmental impact but must manage validator distribution to avoid concentration.
Key Takeaway

Scaling and consensus choices reduce cost and energy but introduce new operational and economic design requirements; balance performance with security and user experience.
