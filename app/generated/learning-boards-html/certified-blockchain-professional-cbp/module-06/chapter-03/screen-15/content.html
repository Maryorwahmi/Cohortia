Key Concepts: Credentials & Governance
Essential terms and ideas from this chapter, focusing on credentials and governance.
Verifiable Credential (VC)

A tamper-evident digital credential issued by an issuer, held by a holder, and presented to a verifier.

Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO)

An organization governed by rules encoded as a transparent computer program, controlled by its members.

Governance Token

A cryptocurrency token that grants its holders voting rights and influence over a blockchain protocol or DAO.

Chapter Summary

Blockchain enables secure and private digital identity through Verifiable Credentials and empowers decentralized governance via DAOs and governance tokens.
