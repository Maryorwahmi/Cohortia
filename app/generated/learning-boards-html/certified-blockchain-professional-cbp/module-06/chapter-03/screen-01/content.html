Blockchain in Healthcare, Identity, and Governance
Revolutionizing Trust and Control in Critical Sectors

This chapter explores how blockchain's core principles can address critical challenges in healthcare, identity management, and organizational governance. We'll examine its potential to enhance data security, interoperability, and patient control, while also introducing new models for digital identity and decentralized decision-making.

Key Idea

Blockchain offers a robust framework to address critical challenges in healthcare, identity, and governance by enhancing security, transparency, and individual control.
