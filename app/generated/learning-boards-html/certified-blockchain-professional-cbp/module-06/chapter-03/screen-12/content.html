Organizations Governed by Code and Community: Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs)
Exploring the structure and function of DAOs.
How DAOs Operate

1. Rules as Smart Contracts

Organizational rules are encoded in self-executing blockchain code.

2. Governance Tokens

Members hold tokens that grant voting rights on proposals.

3. Community Consensus

Decisions are made through transparent, on-chain voting by token holders.

Transparent & Auditable

All proposals and voting results are immutably recorded on the blockchain.

Example

A DAO managing a decentralized finance (DeFi) protocol votes on a proposal to adjust lending interest rates.

Challenge

Voter apathy or low engagement can lead to decisions being made by a small, active minority.

Key Takeaway

DAOs offer a transparent, community-driven governance model using blockchain and smart contracts, with voting power tied to governance tokens.
