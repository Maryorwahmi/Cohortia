Ensuring Drug Authenticity and Safety
Preventing counterfeiting and diversion in the pharmaceutical supply chain.
Blockchain in Pharmaceutical Supply Chain

1. Manufacturer

Assigns unique ID to drug batch, records on blockchain.

2. Distributor

Records transfer of custody, verifies authenticity.

3. Pharmacy

Verifies drug origin before dispensing to patient.

4. Patient

Can verify drug authenticity using its blockchain record.

Real-World Impact

Combats the global issue of counterfeit drugs, which can be ineffective or harmful, saving lives and protecting public health.

Misconception Check

Blockchain doesn't store the physical drugs; it stores immutable records of their movement and authenticity.

Key Takeaway

Blockchain provides end-to-end traceability in the pharmaceutical supply chain, ensuring drug authenticity and preventing counterfeiting.
