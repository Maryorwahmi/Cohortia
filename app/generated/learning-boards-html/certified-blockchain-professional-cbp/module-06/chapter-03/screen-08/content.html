Centralized vs. Self-Sovereign Identity
A paradigm shift in how we manage our digital identities.
Traditional (Centralized) Identity

Third-Party Control: Governments or corporations issue and manage credentials.

Single Points of Failure: Central databases are vulnerable to breaches.

Privacy Risks: Over-sharing of personal data for verification.

Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI)

Individual Ownership: You own and control your digital identity.

Decentralized Control: No single entity holds all your identity data.

Enhanced Privacy: Selective disclosure of only necessary information.

Key Takeaway

SSI shifts identity control from centralized authorities to individuals, enhancing privacy, security, and user empowerment.
