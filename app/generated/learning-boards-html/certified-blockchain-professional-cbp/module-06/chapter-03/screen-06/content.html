Seamless Data Sharing and Immutable Audit Trails
How blockchain fosters secure interoperability and accountability.
Blockchain's Role in Healthcare Data Flow

Standardized Data Structures

Healthcare providers agree on common formats for patient data.

Permissioned Blockchain Networks

Securely share access to records without central intermediaries.

Immutable Audit Trail

Every data access or modification is permanently recorded, ensuring accountability.

Concrete Example

A patient's primary care physician, specialist, and pharmacy can all access relevant, up-to-date records securely and instantly, with the patient's permission.

Why it Matters

Improved interoperability leads to better coordinated care, reduced medical errors, and a more efficient healthcare system overall.

Key Takeaway

Blockchain facilitates secure interoperability and creates an immutable audit trail for healthcare data, enhancing transparency and accountability.
