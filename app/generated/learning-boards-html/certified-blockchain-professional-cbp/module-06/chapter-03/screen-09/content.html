Your Unique Digital Fingerprint: Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs)
Understanding the core component of self-sovereign identity.
Key Characteristics of DIDs

Globally Unique

Each DID is distinct and identifiable across all decentralized networks.

Persistent

Once created, DIDs cannot be revoked or altered by a central authority.

Cryptographically Verifiable

Secured by cryptographic keys, ensuring authenticity and integrity.

User-Controlled

Individuals have full ownership and management of their DIDs.

Analogy

Think of a DID as a permanent, unchangeable street address for your digital identity, which you own, unlike a rented apartment address.

Why it Matters

DIDs eliminate reliance on central authorities for identity, reducing censorship risks and enhancing individual autonomy online.

Key Takeaway

DIDs are the foundational, user-controlled identifiers in SSI, providing a globally unique and cryptographically secure anchor for digital identity.
