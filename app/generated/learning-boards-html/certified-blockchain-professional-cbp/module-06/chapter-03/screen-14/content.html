Key Concepts: Healthcare & Identity
Essential terms and ideas from this chapter, focusing on healthcare and identity.
Electronic Health Record (EHR)

A digital version of a patient's medical chart, containing comprehensive health information.

Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI)

A digital identity model where individuals own and control their digital identities and personal data.

Decentralized Identifier (DID)

A globally unique, persistent, and cryptographically verifiable identifier managed by the individual.

Chapter Summary

Blockchain offers transformative solutions for healthcare data security, interoperability, and patient control, while enabling self-sovereign digital identities for individuals.
