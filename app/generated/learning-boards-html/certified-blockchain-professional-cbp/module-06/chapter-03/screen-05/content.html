Patient-Controlled Electronic Health Records
Empowering individuals with self-sovereign control over their medical data.
How Blockchain Secures EHR Access

1. EHRs Stored Off-Chain

Actual medical records are kept in secure, decentralized storage.

2. Encrypted Hashes On-Chain

Cryptographic hashes of EHRs are recorded on the blockchain for integrity.

3. Patient Holds Cryptographic Keys

Patients use private keys to grant and revoke access to their data.

4. Selective & Revocable Access

Patients control who sees what information and for how long, enhancing privacy.

Example Scenario

A patient grants a new specialist temporary access to specific medical records for a consultation, revoking it after the visit.

Why it Matters

This model shifts control from institutions to individuals, ensuring greater privacy and compliance with data protection regulations.

Key Takeaway

Blockchain empowers patients with cryptographic keys to control access to their EHRs, enhancing privacy and data sovereignty.
