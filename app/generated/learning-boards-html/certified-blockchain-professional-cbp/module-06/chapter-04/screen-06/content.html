Use Cases

Beyond digital art

Real-world utility grows as NFTs represent rights, access, and tradable digital property.

Platforms: Decentraland, The Sandbox
Tickets
Provenance, resale rules, and royalty flows for event organizers.
Virtual land
Ownership of parcels in persistent virtual worlds, tradable across marketplaces.
Music royalties
Tokenized rights that pay creators from future revenue streams.
In-game items
Weapons, skins, and land as tradable, player-owned assets.
Design note

Metadata often points to off-chain storage to avoid bloating the blockchain. Contracts define transfer rules and can include royalties or access conditions. When designing a use case, specify what the token conveys: access, ownership, or a revenue share.

Common mistake: Assume an NFT automatically grants all IP rights.
Pro tip: Explicitly state rights and royalties in metadata or sale terms.
