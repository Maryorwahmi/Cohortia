Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › What is the Metaverse?
Definition

The Metaverse explained

A persistent, immersive set of virtual spaces where users interact and transact over time.

Role of blockchain

NFTs record ownership of land, avatars and items; cryptocurrencies enable in-world transactions and marketplaces.

Concrete example

Platforms such as Decentraland allow users to buy parcels as NFTs, build experiences, and monetize traffic or services on their land.

Misconception

The Metaverse is not a single centralized platform; it can be a network of interoperable virtual spaces, each with its own rules.

How ownership works
When a virtual land parcel is an NFT, the tokenId and contract record who owns the parcel. That owner can sell or transfer the token without going through a central platform's internal account system.
Design note: Interoperability requires shared standards and community agreements about metadata and usage.
Real-world connection: Ownership enables creators to monetize experiences and services inside persistent worlds.
Key Takeaway

Blockchains provide the ownership and economic rails that make the Metaverse a place for verifiable property and commerce.
