Analysis

Benefits and risks

Consider real-world economic incentives alongside legal and environmental trade-offs.

Benefits
  • Creator revenue and royalties
  • Player ownership and secondary markets
  • New digital entrepreneurship
Risks
  • Market speculation and scams
  • IP and rights ambiguity
  • Environmental and UX barriers
Design considerations
Tokenomics, clear rights statements, accessible wallets, and monitoring systems are essential to healthy ecosystems.
Concrete scenario

A play-to-earn economy can lift creators and players but may collapse if token issuance outpaces demand or if rare items concentrate wealth and undermine gameplay balance.

Challenge

Design tokenomics to align player incentives with sustainable demand and fair play.
