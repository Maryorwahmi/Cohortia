Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › From Mint to Marketplace
Process Flow

Mint → Transfer → Trade

Follow events and ownerOf to verify provenance and custody across marketplace activities.

1. Minting
Contract creates tokenId and emits a Transfer event from zero address to the creator.
2. Transfer
Calls to transferFrom or safeTransferFrom move tokenId and emit Transfer events reflecting new owner.
3. Marketplace
Approval and marketplace interactions allow buying and selling; events still record each transfer.
Concrete walkthrough

Mint tokenId 100 → Transfer event records origin → buyer calls safeTransferFrom → ownerOf(100) now returns buyer address. A public ledger preserves this chain.

Key Takeaway

Events plus owner queries give an auditable provenance trail for each NFT.
