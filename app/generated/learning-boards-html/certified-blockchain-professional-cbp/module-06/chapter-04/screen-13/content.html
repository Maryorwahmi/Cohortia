Architecture

Tokenomics & Fair Play

Design incentives that reward genuine participation while preserving fair gameplay and token value.

Decisions to make
  • Which items are NFTs and which are consumables
  • How scarcity and mint caps are set
  • Reward schedules and inflation controls
Balance example

Mix earned progression with marketplace purchases and include mechanisms to prevent item concentration that breaks gameplay balance.

Monitoring & adjustments
Implement analytics to watch token flows, marketplace liquidity, and concentration of ownership so you can iterate on rewards and supply rules.
Common mistake: Letting secondary markets set the game's progression balance without safeguards.
Pro tip: Use limited-time drops and controlled minting to manage scarcity and player expectations.
Key Takeaway

Good tokenomics align game goals with sustainable economic incentives and clear player experience.
