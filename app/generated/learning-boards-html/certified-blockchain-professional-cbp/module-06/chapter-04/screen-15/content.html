Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › What to Remember

Quick Reference

Three compact checks when reviewing NFTs and Metaverse items.

Digital Scarcity
Verify tokenId and totalSupply to confirm uniqueness.
Digital Property Rights
Read metadata and sale terms to know what rights transfer with the NFT.
Smart Contract
Inspect read functions and events to audit behavior and history.
Parting Advice

Adopt a verification habit: check name, totalSupply, ownerOf for sample tokenIds, and Events to build confidence in ownership claims.
