Core Concept

Web3 Gaming & Play-to-Earn

When game assets are NFTs, players gain transferable ownership and real economic agency.

How P2E works

Players earn tokens or NFTs by playing. Those rewards can enter the wider economy via exchanges or marketplaces.

Concrete example

Axie Infinity rewarded players with tokens for in-game actions, creating a player-driven economy where assets could be traded externally.

Design challenge

Maintaining fair gameplay while allowing tradable NFTs requires careful balancing of rewards and rarity to avoid pay-to-win dynamics.

Player responsibilities
Because assets are on-chain, players manage wallets and keys. This raises security and UX concerns that designers must address.
Common mistake: Expecting steady incomes. Token markets are volatile and linked to demand.
Pro tip: Build onboarding and marketplace flows that clearly explain ownership and trading risks.
Key Takeaway

Web3 gaming gives players asset ownership and income opportunities, but tokenomics and UX must be designed responsibly.
