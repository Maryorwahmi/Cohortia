Key Concepts

Three compact cards to keep as mental anchors.

Non-Fungible Token
A unique on-chain token representing one specific item and its provenance.
Metaverse
Persistent virtual spaces where NFTs and tokens support ownership and commerce.
Web3 Gaming
Games where players truly own assets as NFTs and can earn value through gameplay.
Summary

Ownership, place, and economy: use these lenses to evaluate NFT projects, Metaverse platforms, and Web3 games.
