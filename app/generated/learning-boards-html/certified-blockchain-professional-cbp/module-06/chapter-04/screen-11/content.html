Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Inspecting a Contract
Hands-on Activity

Conceptual Etherscan walkthrough

Practice reading contract state and events to confirm ownership and provenance without executing writes.

Step 1: Choose a contract
Find a known ERC-721 address for a collection such as Bored Ape Yacht Club or CryptoPunks.
Step 2: Read functions
Use name(), symbol(), totalSupply(), ownerOf(tokenId), and balanceOf(owner) to inspect state.
Step 3: Events
Open the Events tab and trace Transfer events to build a provenance timeline for a token.
Safety note
Review Write Contract signatures conceptually; do not execute on mainnet unless you control funds and keys.
Reflection

Seeing ownerOf and Transfer events makes verifiable ownership visible and auditable. This transparency underpins trust in NFT markets.

Key Takeaway

A block explorer exposes contract functions and events so you can verify claims without changing state.
