Mitigation Reminders

A short checklist to use during code review.

Order checks
Implement Checks-Effects-Interactions to prevent reentrancy.
Use pull payments
Let recipients withdraw to avoid blocking and unexpected failures.
Verify thoroughly
Combine tests, fuzzing, static checks, and audits to reduce residual risk.
Final Takeaway

Adopt patterns and verification as habits — they are the most reliable way to keep assets safe on immutable platforms.
