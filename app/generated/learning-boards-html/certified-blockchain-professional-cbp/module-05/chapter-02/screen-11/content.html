Core Concept

Timestamp dependence

Miners can tweak block timestamps slightly, which can affect time-based logic.

Why it's risky
Even small timestamp shifts can alter outcomes in high-value games or auctions.
Alternatives
Use commit-reveal or an external randomness oracle for secure, unbiased randomness.
Reviewer checklist
  • Find uses of block.timestamp in critical logic.
  • Replace with commit-reveal or oracle-based randomness where necessary.
  • Consider economic disincentives for manipulation.
Key Takeaway

Avoid relying on timestamps for critical randomness; use commit-reveal or reputable oracles instead.
