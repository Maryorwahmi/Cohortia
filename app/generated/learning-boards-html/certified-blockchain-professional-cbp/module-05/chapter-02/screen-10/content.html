Architecture

Ownership, initialization, and multisig

Design clear role responsibilities and protect initialization to avoid privilege escalation.

Initialization guards
Prevent public or repeated initialization that could let an attacker become owner. Use constructors or protected initializer modifiers.
Multisig & timelocks
Require multiple approvals and a delay for critical actions to reduce abuse risk.
Parity case lesson
Incorrect or missing initialization and privilege checks allowed ownership to be claimed or the library contract to be killed, freezing funds or enabling abuse.
Key Takeaway

Design roles and initialization carefully; prefer multisig and timelocks for high-value or upgrade operations.
