Key Concepts — Part 1
Blockchain Trilemma
A blockchain typically prioritizes two of decentralization, security, and scalability.
On-chain Scaling
Sharding and block-parameter changes increase TPS but affect node costs and coordination.
Layer 2 Overview
Move execution off-chain, anchor settlement on-chain; different Layer 2s change finality and trust models.
Final takeaway

When choosing a scaling path, list the property priorities and explain the shifted assumptions to users and operators.
