How Plasma works
Child chains run independent execution and commit periodic state roots to the root chain to anchor security.
Child chain processes transactions at high speed with its own consensus.
Periodic root-hash commits anchor child state to the root chain.
Exit and challenge procedures protect users if child chains misbehave.
Example & caveats
Example: A payments-focused child chain batches thousands of transfers and submits a single root to the main chain periodically. Caveat: during mass exit events, the root chain can be overwhelmed and users need reliable, well-designed withdrawal windows.
Key Takeaway

Plasma trades on-chain work for child-chain speed but requires clear exit protocols and dispute handling to protect funds.
