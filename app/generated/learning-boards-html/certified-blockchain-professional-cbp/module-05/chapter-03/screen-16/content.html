Key Concepts — Part 2
Payment Channels
Best for frequent small payments between the same parties; requires routing for wide connectivity.
Plasma & Sidechains
High throughput options with independent security; exits and bridge trust matter for safety.
Rollups
Bundle execution off-chain and use fraud or validity proofs for settlement; affects finality and operational cost.
Closing thought

Match DApp requirements to a solution's trust and finality model, and document the shifted security assumptions for stakeholders.
