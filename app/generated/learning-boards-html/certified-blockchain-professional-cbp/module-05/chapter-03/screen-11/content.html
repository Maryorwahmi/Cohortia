Rollups explained
Rollups execute off-chain and submit compressed batches to Layer 1. The verification model determines finality and latency.
Optimistic Rollups
Assume validity and allow fraud proofs during a challenge period. Lower on-chain cost, higher withdrawal latency.
ZK-Rollups
Submit cryptographic validity proofs that Layer 1 verifies quickly. Faster finality, more complex proof generation.
Example

Rollups amortize Layer 1 fees across many transactions, making per-transaction cost much lower while keeping settlement anchored to the main chain.
