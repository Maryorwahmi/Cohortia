Property definitions
Decentralization: node diversity, permissionless access, governance distribution.
Security: resistance to tampering, cryptographic guarantees, consensus robustness.
Scalability: throughput, latency, cost per transaction.
Design examples
Decentralized PoW chain
High decentralization and strong security, low TPS.
Permissioned consortium
High throughput and operational control, limited permissionless decentralization.
Layer 2 anchored to Layer 1
High throughput with Layer 1 security for settlement, but different liveness and dispute assumptions.
Key Takeaway

Use concrete metrics and examples to make trade-offs explicit and avoid assuming that more nodes equals full decentralization.
