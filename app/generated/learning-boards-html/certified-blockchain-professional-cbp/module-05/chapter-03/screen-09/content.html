Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Payment Channels
Process steps
  1. Open: on-chain funding transaction locks funds into a channel contract.
  2. Update: parties exchange signed off-chain balance updates for each transfer.
  3. Route (multi-hop): payments may traverse intermediary channels to reach distant parties.
  4. Close: final state is posted on-chain and funds are settled.
Example
Alice and Bob open a channel and perform many micro-payments off-chain, later settling the net balance on Layer 1.
When to use
Best for repeated, low-value transfers between known or routable parties — gaming, streaming payments, or frequent trading between two nodes.
Common mistake
Expecting payment channels to replace general-purpose on-chain transfers; they are specialized for frequent bilateral flows.
Key Takeaway

Payment channels minimize on-chain activity for repeated payments but require routing and liquidity management for broad connectivity.
