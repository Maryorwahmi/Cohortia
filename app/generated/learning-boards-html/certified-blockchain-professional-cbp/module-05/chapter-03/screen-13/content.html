Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › ZK-Rollups
How ZK-rollups work
Operator executes transactions off-chain, generates a succinct validity proof, and posts it to Layer 1 where it is quickly verified.
Fast finality for withdrawals because proofs are verified before settlement.
Higher off-chain computation and engineering complexity to generate proofs.
Example & caution
Example: a ZK-rollup batches token transfers and posts a SNARK that proves all state transitions were valid. Caution: proof generation can be costly and might require special circuits for new contract types.
Key Takeaway

Choose ZK-rollups when immediate finality and strong settlement guarantees outweigh proof-generation complexity.
