Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Layer 2 Overview
Layer 2 — key idea
Move execution off-chain, anchor results on-chain. Different Layer 2s make different security and finality trade-offs.
Payment Channels
Peer-to-peer off-chain transfers, settle open/close on Layer 1.
Plasma
Child chains committing periodic state roots to the main chain.
Rollups
Execute off-chain and submit compressed data or proofs to Layer 1.
Example

A payment channel handles many small transfers instantly and settles only the final net on-chain.

Misconception
Layer 2s are not a security replacement for Layer 1; they depend on Layer 1 to finalise and resolve disputes.
