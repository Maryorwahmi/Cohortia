Practice & Assessment
Small tasks to show competence
Try: 15–30 minute sketches
Task A
Explain the trilemma for a chosen chain and what it trades off.
Task B
Sketch sharding or describe how block-size change affects node costs.
Task C
Match DApp requirements to Layer 2s and justify your choice.
Assessment hint

Focus on trade-offs: security anchors, validator trust, finality delays, and user experience when selecting a solution.
