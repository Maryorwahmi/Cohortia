Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Decentralization vs Centralization
Centralized

Single operator model

Control, simple governance, fast decisions; vulnerability: single point of failure or insider manipulation.

Example
A centralized ledger operated by a single company; privacy may be easier but trust is concentrated.
Risk
Compromise of the operator can change records or censor users.
Decentralized

Distributed node model

Reduces single-point failures, makes tampering visible, and relies on consensus and incentives to maintain correctness.

Example
A public blockchain maintained by many independent miners or validators.
Caveat
Requires careful protocol design; small networks may still be vulnerable to centralization of resources.
