Process

How a 51% attack unfolds

1
Gain majority hash power
Either by owning, renting, or coordinating miners to control >50% of total work.
2
Privately build alternate chain
Mine a chain that omits or reverses targeted transactions.
3
Publish longer chain
When the attacker's chain is longer, honest nodes adopt it, invalidating some prior confirmations.
Concrete example
On a small chain, an attacker could rent sufficient hash power for a short time, perform a double-spend against a merchant that accepts few confirmations, then cease activity.
Mitigations
  • Require many confirmations for high-value transfers
  • Increase and diversify miner/validator participation
  • Monitor chain reorganization patterns for anomalies
Common mistake
Assuming a 51% attacker can break cryptography or create unlimited funds — they can only reorganize recent history and block or reorder transactions.
