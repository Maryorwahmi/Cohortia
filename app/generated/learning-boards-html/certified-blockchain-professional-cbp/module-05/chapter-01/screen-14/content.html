Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Immutability
Recorded data becomes tamper-evident due to linked cryptographic hashes.
Decentralization
Control and validation are distributed across many nodes, reducing single points of failure.
Cryptographic Hashing
One-way digests provide compact, tamper-evident fingerprints for data and blocks.
Summary takeaway
These three concepts pair structurally: hashes create evidence, decentralization amplifies resistance to tampering, and confirmations measure the increasing cost to reverse history.
