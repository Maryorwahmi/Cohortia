Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Verify a Bitcoin Transaction

Step-by-step verification

  1. Obtain the TxID (transaction hash) for the transfer you want to check.
  2. Paste the TxID into a reputable block explorer search field and open the transaction page.
  3. Confirm the Transaction Hash, Inputs, Outputs, Block Height, Timestamp, Fees, and Confirmations.
  4. Decide if the number of confirmations is sufficient for your risk tolerance before treating the transfer as final.
Example
A sample TxID used in exercises can be pasted into an explorer to observe these fields and the confirmation count in real-time.
What to check
  • Does the TxID on the explorer match the one you received?
  • Are inputs and outputs reasonable and expected?
  • How many confirmations have passed since the block that contains the transaction?
Common mistake
Relying on third-party screenshots or trusting unverified TxIDs. Always paste the TxID yourself and confirm details directly on the explorer.
Key takeaway
Verifying on a block explorer gives independent evidence of a transaction's inclusion and growing immutability measured by confirmations.
