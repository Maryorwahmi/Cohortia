Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Digital Signature
Authenticates origin and prevents repudiation when private keys remain secure.
51% Attack
Majority mining control lets an attacker reorganize recent blocks and enable double-spends on PoW chains.
Sybil Attack
Fake identities increase influence; resistant protocols make influence costly.
Closing takeaway
Combine cryptographic guarantees with operational discipline: verify TxIDs and confirmations, protect private keys, monitor resource concentration, and educate users to reduce the greatest real-world risks.
