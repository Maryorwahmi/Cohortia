Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Common Mistakes & Best Practices

User-level best practices

  • Store private keys in hardware wallets or offline environments.
  • Never share seed phrases or private keys, even if prompted.
  • Enable two-factor authentication and verify recipients before signing.
Operator practices
Diversify node peers, monitor hash power distribution, and set alerts for unusual chain reorganizations or latency spikes.
Scenario
Testing on small, low-security networks can mask real production risks; treat test environments separately and increase confirmations and monitoring in production.
Key takeaway
Good operational hygiene and education block the majority of real-world attacks, even when protocol guarantees are strong.
