What is a Sybil attack?

An attacker creates many fake identities to influence a network that counts identities rather than resources.

Risk scenario
A small network that votes per-node is vulnerable if an attacker spins up many validators.
Mitigation
Tie influence to costly resources: compute power or stake.
PoW vs PoS
  • PoW: Influence proportional to mining hash power (expensive hardware and energy).
  • PoS: Influence proportional to stake held or bonded (costs capital).
Misconception
Neither PoW nor PoS eliminates centralization risks; both can concentrate if a few actors control most resources.
Pro tip
Monitor resource distribution and voting power to detect emerging concentration early.
