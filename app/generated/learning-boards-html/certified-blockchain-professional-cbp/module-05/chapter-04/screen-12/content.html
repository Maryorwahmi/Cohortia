Blockchain Trilemma
A ledger can usually optimize for two of decentralization, security, and scalability; the third becomes the trade-off.
Layer 2 Pattern
Open on L1, execute off-chain, commit a concise state or proof to L1 for settlement.
Security Trade-offs
State channels, sidechains, Optimistic and ZK-Rollups each balance latency, cost, and the source of security differently.
Summary Takeaway

Choose the Layer 2 that matches your application's need for immediate finality, throughput, participant model, and acceptable trust assumptions.
