Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Rollups
What makes a rollup?
Execute off-chain, batch many transactions, and post a compact commitment to Layer 1 for settlement and dispute resolution.
Concrete example
A rollup bundles thousands of user transfers into a single Layer 1 transaction that updates the state root or verifies a proof.
Distinguishing factor
The assurance method: fraud proofs and challenge windows versus cryptographic validity proofs yield different withdrawal latencies and trust assumptions.
Misconception
Assuming all rollups give instant withdrawals; only ZK-Rollups provide immediate validity guarantees on L1, while Optimistic Rollups use a challenge window.
Key Takeaway

Rollups scale by batching and committing; choose based on whether immediate finality or easier EVM compatibility matters more.
