State Channels — Reminder
Fast and cheap for fixed parties; require watchers and are not ideal for open networks.
Sidechains — Reminder
Significant performance gains but a separate security model tied to the sidechain's validators and bridge code.
Rollups — Reminder
Optimistic: easier compatibility but slow withdrawals. ZK: instant finality but harder proof generation and integration.
Final takeaway

Use the selection checklist: required finality, participant model, acceptable withdrawal delay, and trust assumptions to pick the right Layer 2 approach.
