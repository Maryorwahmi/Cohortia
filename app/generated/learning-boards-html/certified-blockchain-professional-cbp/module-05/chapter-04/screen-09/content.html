How Optimistic Rollups secure batches
They post batch data and a state root to L1 and rely on a challenge period where fraud proofs can be submitted.
Concrete example
A sequencer posts a thousand transfers; users must wait through the challenge window before final withdrawals are permitted on L1.
Common mistake

Assuming no one needs to monitor for fraud. The system depends on at least one honest watcher to submit a fraud proof if something is wrong.

Pros & Cons
  • Pros: EVM compatibility, easier porting of existing contracts.
  • Cons: withdrawal delay, dependence on fraud challenge participants.
Key Takeaway

Optimistic Rollups are pragmatic for many DApps but plan for challenge windows and user expectations about withdrawal latency.
