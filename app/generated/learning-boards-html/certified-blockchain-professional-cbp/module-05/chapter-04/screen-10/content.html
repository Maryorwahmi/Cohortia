Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › ZK-Rollups
How ZK-Rollups secure batches
They generate a cryptographic validity proof for each batch and post that proof to Layer 1 for immediate verification and acceptance.
Concrete example
A rollup computes a new state and a SNARK proving the correctness; L1 verifies the proof and finalizes the state without a challenge delay.
Trade-offs
Stronger on-chain guarantees and faster withdrawals versus heavier computation and more complex development pipelines.
Misconception
Assuming ZK-Rollups are trivial to integrate; they require cryptographic tooling, and integration complexity can be significant today.
Key Takeaway

ZK-Rollups provide immediate finality through proofs, trading engineering complexity for stronger security and faster withdrawals.
