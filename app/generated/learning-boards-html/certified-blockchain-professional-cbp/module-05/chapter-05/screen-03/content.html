Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Learning Objectives
Verification Milestones
Short, demonstrable tasks that prove your understanding.
A
Explain a lock-and-mint transfer
Walk through approval, deposit, relayer verification, mint, burn, and unlock steps for a token transfer.
B
Categorize an interoperability need
Given a scenario, decide if it needs data, asset, or logic interoperability and justify the choice.
C
Identify bridge risks
Name at least three vulnerabilities and recommend mitigations like audits, multisig, or decentralized validator sets.
Assessment Support
Short checklist and examples to prepare your answers.
Sample Prompt
Choose a bridge for high-value NFTs and justify the trust model and mitigations.
Common Mistake
Assuming all bridges offer the same security; trust assumptions differ significantly.
