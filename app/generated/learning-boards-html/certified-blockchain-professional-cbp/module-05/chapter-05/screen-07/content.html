Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Bridge Components
Core Components
Validators / Relayers
Monitor events, sign or submit proofs, and relay them to the destination chain.
Smart Contracts
Handle locking, minting, burning and enforce protocol rules on each chain.
Oracles
Provide external data or aid in state verification when needed; they add extra trust surfaces.
Checklist
Identify key custody points, signature thresholds, and whether there are timelocks or dispute windows for transfers.
Concrete scenario
A bridge may require N-of-M validator signatures to authorize a mint. If those keys are stolen or validators collude, unauthorized mints can occur.
Risk
Key compromise, contract bug, or oracle manipulation can lead to asset loss.
Mitigation
Use multisig, threshold cryptography, audits, timelocks, and clear economic bonds for validators.
