Blockchain Interoperability and Cross-Chain Communication
Understand why multiple blockchains must connect, and the primary patterns that make secure cross-chain workflows possible.
In this lesson
What interoperability types exist and how bridges, swaps, and protocols enable them.
Why it matters
Cross-chain connections unlock liquidity, composability and broader DApp capabilities while raising new security trade-offs.
Key Takeaway

Interoperability increases utility but shifts security responsibility to the chosen bridge or protocol. Understand trust models before moving value across chains.
