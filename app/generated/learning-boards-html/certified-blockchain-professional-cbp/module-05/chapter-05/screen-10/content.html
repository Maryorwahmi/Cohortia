Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Bridge Vulnerabilities
Attack Vectors
  • Smart contract bugs in lock, mint, or burn logic.
  • Validator key compromise or collusion allowing unauthorized mints.
  • Oracle manipulation feeding false state or price data.
  • Economic attacks against liquidity pools backing wrapped assets.
  • Complex upgrade paths that leave legacy state vulnerable.
Risk analysis tip
Map the minimum compromise needed for loss and prioritize defenses at those custody points.
Mitigations
Technical
Use multisig, threshold signatures, audits and contestation windows.
Operational
Strong key management, validator incentives, and transparent governance.
Economic
Bonds or insurance to cover potential losses and disincentivize misbehavior.
Key Takeaway

Effective bridge security combines code correctness, robust key management, and economic incentives for honest behavior.
