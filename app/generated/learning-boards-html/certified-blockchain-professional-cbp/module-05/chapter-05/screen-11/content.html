HTLC Process
1. Secret and Hash
A shares hash of a secret with B.
2. Create HTLCs
Both parties fund contracts that can be claimed with the secret or refunded after a timeout.
3. Reveal and Claim
When one party claims, the secret is revealed and the other can claim too.
Example
Alice wants Bitcoin and Bob wants Ether. They use HTLCs on both chains so that either both receive assets or both can refund after timeouts.
Limitation
Atomic swaps are peer-to-peer and do not provide general cross-chain data or logic transfer.
When to use
Use for direct asset exchanges between two users when both chains support the required scripting or contract functionality.
