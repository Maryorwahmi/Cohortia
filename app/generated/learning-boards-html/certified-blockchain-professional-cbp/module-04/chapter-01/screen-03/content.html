Apply and verify

These objectives are focused on safe application: compile reliably, choose Gas sensibly, and confirm transactions when finality matters.

Compile & Deploy
Turn Solidity into bytecode and deploy to create a Contract Account that stores code and persistent state.
Read receipts
Interpret gas used, transaction hash, and block number to understand cost and inclusion.
Wait for confirmations
Reduce reorg risk by waiting multiple block confirmations for critical operations.
Audit essentials
Look for reentrancy, unchecked calls, and storage-heavy patterns that increase Gas costs and risk.
Verified Competency
Run a local Hardhat deployment of the sample Storage contract and confirm the stored value changes as expected. Inspect Gas used in the terminal output and compare to your estimate.
