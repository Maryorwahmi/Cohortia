Transaction finality

A transaction is included in a block quickly, but confirmations reduce the risk of a chain reorganisation reversing it. For critical operations wait multiple confirmations.

Practical rule
Wait 6–12 confirmations for high-value or irreversible operations to lower reorg risk.
Why it matters

Confirmations trade speed for assurance; plan your application UX to account for waiting where finality matters.

Common security pitfalls
Reentrancy, unchecked external calls, and arithmetic issues are recurring causes of exploits. Auditing and testing are essential defenses.
Example vulnerability
A contract that transfers Ether before updating internal balances can allow reentrancy attacks. Always update state before external calls when appropriate.
Checklist
  • Write unit tests and integration tests.
  • Run local simulations to estimate Gas and detect reverts.
  • Get independent audits before mainnet deployment.
Reflection
Does your contract require immediate finality? If so, bake in confirmation waits and stronger checks around external interactions.
