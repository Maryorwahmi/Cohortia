Module 04 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Ethereum
A decentralized, account-based platform where transactions change a shared global state.
Gas
A unit of computation cost. Fee = Gas used × Gas price; prevents infinite loops and compensates validators.
EVM
The isolated, deterministic runtime that executes compiled bytecode and enforces stack, memory, and storage separation.
Summary takeaway
Design with Gas in mind, test locally, wait confirmations for critical operations, and audit contracts to reduce security risk.
