Execution lifecycle when out of Gas
The EVM executes until Gas is exhausted, then reverts state changes but charges Gas for the work performed.
Step 1
Transaction executes, consuming Gas per EVM opcode.
Step 2
If Gas limit reached, the EVM halts and reverts state writes from that transaction.
Step 3
Gas consumed is still paid to the block proposer; revert does not refund spent Gas.
Concrete scenario
A loop runs due to unexpected input. If it exceeds the Gas limit the transaction reverts. The state is unchanged, but the sender loses the Gas paid while the loop executed.
Defensive checklist
  • Estimate Gas locally and add a small buffer to Gas limit.
  • Validate inputs to avoid unbounded loops or heavy computations.
  • Test on a local network to observe Gas consumption before mainnet deployment.
