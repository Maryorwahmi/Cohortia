Initiator vs Reactor
EOAs sign and start transactions; contracts run only when invoked.
Gas Mindset
Estimate Gas, set an appropriate limit, and expect to pay for consumed Gas even on revert.
EVM Separation
Stack and memory are ephemeral; storage is persistent and expensive. Plan writes accordingly.
Final quick tip
Test and audit locally, inspect Gas used in receipts, and wait confirmations for important transactions.
