Gas explained

Gas quantifies computation and storage cost on Ethereum. It prevents runaway execution and compensates validators.

Gas parameters
Gas limit: maximum payable Gas. Gas price: Ether per Gas unit. Fee = Gas used × Gas price.
Why Gas matters
It prevents infinite loops and DoS attacks by making heavy computation costly, and it provides an economic incentive for validators.
Common mistakes
Setting gas limit too low causes reversion and still consumes Gas. Overpaying gas price wastes funds when the network is not congested.
Example: fee calculation
Estimated Gas used: 50,000
Gas price: user-selected (expressed in Gwei)
Total fee = Gas used × Gas price
Pro tip

Use wallet or explorer estimates and add a small buffer to the gas limit to prevent accidental reverts.
