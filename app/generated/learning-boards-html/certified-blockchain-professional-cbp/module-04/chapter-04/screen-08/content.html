What is chaincode?

Chaincode implements the business rules that transform ledger state. It runs on endorsing peers in a simulated context and produces signed endorsements used for validation.

Example
A loan chaincode that enforces required approvers and computes balances before producing an endorsement.
Operational notes
  • Design endorsement policies to match organizational workflows and availability.
  • Plan chaincode versioning, approvals, and governance to avoid unexpected behavior changes.
  • Use language choices that suit your development and testing pipelines.
