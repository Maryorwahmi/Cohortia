Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Learning Objectives
Skill-to-task examples
Identity & Access
Define CAs and certificate renewal policies so that nodes and users are authenticated and auditable.
Privacy Controls
Choose channels or selective sharing in Corda when counterparty confidentiality is required for transactions.
Throughput & Ordering
Specify ordering service type and resources to meet peak transaction rates and maintain deterministic finality.
Smart Contract Lifecycle
Plan chaincode or CorDapp deployment, versioning, and endorsement or signature policies before production rollout.
Common mistakes
  • Misconfiguring channel policies and exposing data.
  • Conflating ordering service responsibility with endorsement rules.
  • Expecting global replication in Corda without adapting to its privacy model.
