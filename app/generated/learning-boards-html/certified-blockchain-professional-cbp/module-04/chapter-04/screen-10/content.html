Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › R3 Corda Architecture
Key elements
  • Peer-to-peer transaction sharing—no global broadcast.
  • Notaries for uniqueness checks to prevent double-spends.
  • CorDapps implement contracts and workflows in Kotlin/Java.
  • Vault stores local states relevant to each node.
Example
In a loan syndication, only the lender, borrower, and relevant notary see the transaction. Other banks remain unaware of the transaction details unless they are a party to the agreement.
Design question
When privacy is the highest priority, how will you model role-based access and auditing without a global ledger?
