Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Identity & CAs
Role of CAs
CAs issue and manage certificates for nodes and users, enabling authentication, TLS, and cryptographic signatures used in endorsements and access control.
Operational tip
Plan certificate rotation and revocation processes to match organizational identity lifecycles so access changes are auditable.
Example
A new peer requests credentials from its organization CA during onboarding. Those credentials are later used to sign proposals and authenticate to other peers and orderers.
Common mistake

Treating certificate issuance as a one-off rather than a lifecycle activity leads to stale credentials and governance gaps.
