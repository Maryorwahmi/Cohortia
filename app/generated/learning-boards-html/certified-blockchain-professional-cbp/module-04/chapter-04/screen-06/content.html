Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Execute → Order → Validate
Process
1. Execute (Simulate & Endorse)
Client proposal is simulated by endorsing peers. Results are signed as endorsements without changing the ledger.
2. Order
Endorsements and proposals are sent to the ordering service which sequences transactions for the channel.
3. Validate & Commit
Committing peers validate endorsements against policy and state, then append blocks to the ledger if valid.
Why it matters

Separation lets you optimize ordering for throughput and execution for privacy. It also reduces the need to broadcast sensitive execution data to the entire channel until validation.

Key Takeaway

This architecture delivers deterministic finality while keeping private simulation results restricted to endorsers.
