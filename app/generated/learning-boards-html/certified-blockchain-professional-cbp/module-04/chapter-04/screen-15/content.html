Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Channels
Private sub-ledgers for selected participants to ensure data isolation.
Execute-Order-Validate
Fabric’s lifecycle: simulate and endorse, order transactions, then validate and commit for deterministic finality.
Notaries (Corda)
Validate uniqueness without broadcasting full transaction content, preventing double-spends while preserving privacy.
Final takeaway

Design around who must see each fact, then choose the platform and components that enforce that model while meeting governance and performance needs.
