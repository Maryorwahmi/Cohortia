Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Fabric — Core Components
Component list
Peers
Maintain ledger and run chaincode. Endorsers simulate and sign; committers validate and append.
Ordering Service
Provides total ordering for transactions. Separates ordering from execution to boost performance.
Certificate Authorities
Issue and manage identities for nodes and users to support access control and auditing.
Channels
Private sub-ledgers shared only among channel members for transaction confidentiality.
Chaincode
Smart contracts implementing business logic; executed by endorsing peers involved in a transaction.
Operational flow — concept
Clients propose transactions to endorsing peers, endorsers simulate and sign, signed proposals go to ordering service which sequences them, and committing peers validate endorsements and write to the ledger.
Example
A trade finance transaction requires endorsements from importer and bank defined by an endorsement policy before ordering and commit.
Common mistake
Assuming the ordering service enforces endorsement rules; orderers order only, they do not check business logic endorsements.
