Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Fabric vs Corda
Fabric — Strengths
  • Channel-based privacy for shared ledgers
  • Modular ordering and high throughput
  • Chaincode in common enterprise languages
Corda — Strengths
  • Privacy by default with peer-to-peer sharing
  • Notaries for uniqueness without full content visibility
  • Efficient for bilateral financial workflows
Decision prompt
List the parties that must see each fact, then choose the platform matching that sharing model and compliance needs.
