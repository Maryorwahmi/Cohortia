Enterprise Blockchains & Permissioned Ledgers

Understand how permissioning, identity, and privacy change blockchain architecture for businesses, and why Fabric and Corda follow different design rules to meet enterprise requirements.

In this lesson
Permissioning basics, Fabric components, Corda privacy model, hands-on Fabric test network steps.
Coming up
Execution flow: execute-order-validate and notary uniqueness checks.
Why it matters
Enterprises require controlled visibility, compliance-ready audit trails, and performance tuned for known participants.
Concept Map
Permissioned vs Permissionless
Trusted participants / Identity
Fabric
Modular components, channels for privacy, ordering service separates consensus.
Corda
Peer-to-peer transactions, notary for uniqueness, strong privacy by default.
Quick takeaway
Permissioned designs trade universal transparency for selective visibility and governance. That trade lets enterprises meet privacy, compliance, and performance needs.
