Trilemma defined
A useful analytic lens for comparing L1 designs and their trade-offs.
Decentralization
Many independent validators, low barrier to entry.
Scalability
High throughput, low latency, low fees.
Security
Resistance to attacks, strong economic incentives.
Concrete example
Bitcoin prioritizes decentralization and security; Alt-L1s often trade validator accessibility or openness to reach high throughput.
Key Takeaway

There is no universal best — pick the balance that fits your application's trust and performance needs.

Common mistakes
  • Evaluating only TPS without considering validator accessibility.
  • Assuming protocol upgrades alone resolve fundamental trade-offs.
  • Mistaking permissioned control for equivalent security to broad, decentralized networks.
