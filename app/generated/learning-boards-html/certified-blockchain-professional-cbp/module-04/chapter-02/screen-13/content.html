Step 1: Prioritize requirements
  • Throughput / latency
  • Privacy / permissioning
  • Validator accessibility
  • Interoperability
  • Operational cost
Step 2: Map to platform features
Solana
Choose for latency-sensitive workloads; accept higher validator requirements.
Avalanche
Choose for custom, permissioned subnets and enterprise control.
Polkadot
Choose for shared security, parallelization, and native interoperability.
Operational checklist
Estimate validator and maintenance costs, governance burden, and integration complexity before final selection.
