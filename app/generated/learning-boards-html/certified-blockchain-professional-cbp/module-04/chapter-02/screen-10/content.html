Side-by-side trade-offs
A compact decision grid to match requirements to platforms.
Solana
Speed-first, PoH for ordering, high validator requirements, suited for low-latency apps.
Avalanche
Sampling consensus, rapid finality, subnets for custom governance and enterprise solutions.
Polkadot
Relay Chain secures parachains, strong cross-chain messaging and shared security model.
When to choose
Solana: trading, gaming. Avalanche: enterprise subnets, custom chains. Polkadot: interoperable multi-chain apps.
Key risk
Solana: validator centralization. Avalanche: subnet security design. Polkadot: Relay Chain dependence.
