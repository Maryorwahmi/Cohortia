Avalanche consensus
Probabilistic sampling yields fast finality without global synchronous agreement every round.
How sampling works
Nodes poll small random peer sets repeatedly; if a supermajority favors a choice across samples, preferences converge and finality is achieved probabilistically.
Concrete example
Rapid finality helps applications that require near-instant settlement while preserving decentralization through many participants in the sampling pool.
Subnets explained
Custom blockchains within Avalanche that define their own rules, validators, and virtual machines.
Enterprise subnet
Permissioned validators, strict governance, private operations.
Public subnet
Open validators, tokenized incentives, tuned for DeFi or gaming.
Why it matters

Subnets make Avalanche highly modular. Teams get full control over the environment without sacrificing the ability to interoperate when needed.
