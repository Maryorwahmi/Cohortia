Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Solana Trade-offs
Practical impact
What teams gain and what they accept when choosing Solana.
Benefits
  • High throughput and low fees
  • Low-latency confirmations
  • Parallel contract execution
Costs
  • Higher validator hardware requirements
  • Potentially fewer full-node operators
  • Centralization concerns in some governance aspects
Scenario
A trading DApp needs sub-second settlement for market microstructure. Solana delivers that, but the team must accept a smaller validator footprint and design for auditability through alternative monitoring.
Checklist for evaluation
  • Does your app require very low latency?
  • Can you accept fewer independent validators?
  • Is operational cost for validator-grade hardware manageable?
Key Takeaway

Match Solana to applications where latency and throughput are primary, and decentralization can be traded for performance.
