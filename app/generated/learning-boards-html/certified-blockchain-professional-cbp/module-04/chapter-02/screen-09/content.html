Relay Chain and Parachains
Shared security enables many specialized chains to run in parallel under a central authority for finality.
Parachain example
A DeFi parachain uses deterministic execution and benefits from Relay Chain validators securing its state.
Parathread example
A small project pays per-block usage with parathreads instead of leasing a full parachain slot.
XCMP
Cross-Chain Message Passing lets parachains exchange messages and assets directly, enabling composable multi-chain applications.
Common Misconception

Parachains are not isolated from shared security; their safety depends on the Relay Chain’s validators and staking model.
