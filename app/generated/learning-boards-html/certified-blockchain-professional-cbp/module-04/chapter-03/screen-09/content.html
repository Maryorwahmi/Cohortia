Two-way bridge pattern
Assets are locked on L1 and an equivalent representation is minted on the sidechain. When returning, the representation is burned and L1 unlocks the original asset.
Pros
High throughput and low fees; flexible validator design.
Cons
Security depends on the sidechain's validators; bridge design matters for safety.
Example: Polygon PoS is a popular sidechain connected to Ethereum via bridges.
Common mistake
Assuming sidechains inherit full L1 security. In fact, trust rests on the sidechain's validators and the bridge implementation.
Why it matters

For significant capital movement, analyze the sidechain's validator security and bridge guarantees before relying on it for custody.
