Sequencer responsibilities
Collect transactions, decide ordering, execute off-chain logic, and submit batches to L1 for settlement or verification.
Latency
A fast sequencer improves UX by quickly including transactions in rollup state.
Censorship
If sequencers can withhold or reorder txs, on-chain remedies and governance matter for users.
Recovery & governance
Look for on-chain data posting, challenge windows, and penalty mechanisms that allow the community to detect and revert malicious behavior even if the sequencer misbehaves.
Pro tip: prefer rollups that post sufficient data to L1 so state can be reconstructed if a sequencer fails or acts maliciously.
