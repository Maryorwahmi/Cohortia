Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Optimistic vs ZK
Optimistic Rollups
Assume correctness and allow fraud proofs during a challenge window. Lower off-chain proof cost, but withdrawals to L1 require waiting for the window to close.
Security: depends on challenge mechanism and watchers
Finality: delayed due to challenge period
Use cases: high-throughput apps that can tolerate withdrawal delay
ZK-Rollups
Provide cryptographic proofs for batch validity. The L1 verifies proofs and grants immediate finality. Higher off-chain computation cost but faster withdrawals.
Security: cryptographic verification on L1
Finality: near-instant for L1 withdrawals
Use cases: applications needing immediate settlement to L1
