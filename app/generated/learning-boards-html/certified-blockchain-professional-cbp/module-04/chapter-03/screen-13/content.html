Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Bridge Exercise
Step-by-step checklist
1. Prepare your wallet and switch to the L1 test network (Goerli or equivalent).
2. Obtain test Ether from a faucet to fund small deposits.
3. Add the Optimism testnet network entry to your wallet via a network registry if required.
4. Use the official bridge interface to deposit a small amount from L1 testnet to Optimism testnet and confirm the deposit.
5. Switch to Optimism testnet, verify funds, and connect to a simple test DApp to perform a small interaction and observe fees.
Reflection prompt

Record your deposit hash, approximate deposit time, and the gas fee observed on the testnet swap to compare with mainnet expectations.

Notes & common pitfalls
On optimistic testnets you will often see short deposit times, but remember that mainnet challenge windows are longer. Do not assume testnet timing mirrors production withdrawal delays.
