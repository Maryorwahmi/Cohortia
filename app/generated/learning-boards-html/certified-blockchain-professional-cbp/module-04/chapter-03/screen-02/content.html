01 Explain L2 necessity
01
You will explain why blockchains need L2s: to relieve L1 congestion and lower fees while keeping settlement anchored on the L1. This skill matters when evaluating scalability paths for a DApp.
Mnemonic: highway + express lane

02 Differentiate rollups
02
You will distinguish optimistic rollups from ZK-rollups by mechanism: fraud proofs and a challenge window versus cryptographic validity proofs. This helps you pick the right finality guarantees.
Mnemonic: challenge window vs immediate proof
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Describe sidechains
03
You will describe sidechain architecture, two-way bridges, and the separate security model. This matters to evaluate trust assumptions for funds moved off L1.
Mnemonic: bridge locks and mints
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 State channels basics
04
You will explain how state channels let participants transact off-chain with only open and close transactions on L1, and why they suit limited participant sets with high-frequency exchanges.
Mnemonic: open, trade, close
