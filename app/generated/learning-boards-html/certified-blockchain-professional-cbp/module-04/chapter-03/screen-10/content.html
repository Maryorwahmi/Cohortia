Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › State Channels
Channel lifecycle
Open: lock funds in an on-chain contract to create the channel.
Trade: participants exchange off-chain signed updates instantly.
Close: submit final state to L1 to settle balances, or resolve disputes on-chain.
Key Takeaway

Channels are perfect for many rapid, low-cost interactions between a fixed set of participants, but they do not generalize to open systems without extra routing designs.

Examples and limits
Lightning and Raiden show how channels enable micropayments and instant transfers between parties. The limit is participant scope: channels need participants to be online and typically operate between pairs or small groups.
Misconception
Channels are not a universal replacement for L2s; they are specialized for repeated exchanges among known participants.
Practical note
For a DApp that requires many users and shared global state, channels are typically insufficient without additional routing or hub structures.
