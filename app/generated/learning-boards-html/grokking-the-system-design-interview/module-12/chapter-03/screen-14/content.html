Key Concepts
Quick references to keep in mind
Edge Server (PoP)
Local cache that serves segments close to users.
Cache Hit Ratio
Share of requests served from cache vs origin; key performance metric.
Cache Invalidation
Remove or update cached content to preserve freshness for viewers.
Summary
These three levers — where content is served, how often it is cached, and how it is invalidated — determine playback experience, cost, and operational complexity.
