Definition
Cache-Control and Origin Access
Headers and access policies determine caching behavior and routing to origin.
• Set Cache-Control: max-age for static segments to increase hit ratios.
• For live, use short TTLs or invalidation mechanisms to preserve freshness.
• Restrict direct origin access so the CDN is the only allowed fetcher.
Operational Tip
Audit origin object headers after upload and enforce standard header policies in ingestion pipelines.
Checklist
  • • Verify Cache-Control and Expires on newly uploaded assets.
  • • Ensure CDN is configured as primary delivery endpoint.
  • • Lock down origin bucket policy to accept requests only from CDN IPs or origin access identity.
Key Takeaway

Small header mistakes can cause large increases in origin egress. Treat headers as a first-line optimization for cost and performance.
