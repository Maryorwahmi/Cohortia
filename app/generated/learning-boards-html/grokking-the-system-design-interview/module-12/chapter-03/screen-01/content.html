Content Delivery Networks for Video Streaming
Edge caches, cache strategies, multi-CDN trade-offs, and signed URLs for secure delivery.

A CDN places copies of transcoded video segments at edge servers near viewers. This reduces latency, lowers origin egress, and improves availability during traffic spikes or regional outages. Understanding caching behavior, invalidation needs for live vs VOD, and security features like signed URLs is essential for designing scalable streaming systems.
