Key Concepts — Security & Origin
Short, operational definitions to keep handy
Multi-CDN Strategy
Multiple providers for resilience and regional performance tuning.
Signed URLs / Tokens
Time-limited HMAC signatures that authorize CDN edge access.
Origin Server
Authoritative storage from which edges fetch on cache misses.
Final Takeaway
Design for both performance and security: edge caching and hit ratios lower cost, while signed URLs and origin restrictions protect content and enforce delivery through the CDN.
