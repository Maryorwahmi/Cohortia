Assessment Idea
Diagnose and Remediate CDN Issues
A practical troubleshooting checklist for common CDN problems.
Problem
Low cache hit ratio
Likely Causes
Short TTLs, many unique keys, direct origin access
Remedy
Adjust headers, consolidate keys, enforce origin restrictions
Stale Content
Shorten TTLs for live or issue explicit invalidations for VOD updates.
Regional Outage
Failover to another CDN or route traffic away from affected PoPs and monitor recovery.
Key Takeaway

Use metric correlation and targeted configuration changes, then re-measure to ensure fixes reduce egress and improve playback.
