Operational Practice
Pre-warming for Launches
Populate PoP caches in advance to reduce origin load and improve early playback.
Steps to Pre-warm
  1. Identify top N segments likely to be requested.
  2. Script requests to target PoPs across regions.
  3. Verify cache hits in each PoP and adjust as needed.
Verification
Use CDN logs or hit-ratio queries to confirm that pre-warmed objects are present and serving from edges.
Warning

Pre-warming should be targeted. Pre-warming everything increases cost and may impact other cached content due to eviction policies.

Key Takeaway

Include pre-warming in launch plans for major events and validate success with per-PoP hit ratio checks.
