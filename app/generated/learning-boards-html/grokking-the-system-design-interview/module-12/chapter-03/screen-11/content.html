Monitoring CDN Health
Which metrics reveal problems, and what to do about them.
Cache Hit Ratio
Primary measure of caching efficiency. Drops mean more origin traffic and higher cost.
Origin Egress
Dollar impact of cache misses. Correlate with hit ratio to find root causes.
Edge Error Rates
Indicates PoP or routing issues; monitor per-region and per-CDN.
Operational Playbook
  1. Alert on sudden hit-ratio drops.
  2. Correlate with egress and edge errors.
  3. Identify hot objects and consider pre-warming or TTL changes.
Pre-warming
Programmatically request popular segments before a launch so PoPs have cached copies for initial viewers.
Key Takeaway

Use per-region, per-CDN metrics to detect anomalies quickly and to steer traffic for multi-CDN systems based on real performance data.
