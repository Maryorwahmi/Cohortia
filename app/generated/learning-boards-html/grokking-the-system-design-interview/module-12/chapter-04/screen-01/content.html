Storage and Database Design for Video Streaming

Design decisions that determine cost, performance, and availability across petabytes of media and billions of metadata records.

In this lesson

Why object storage is the default for video, how to split metadata responsibilities between RDBMS and NoSQL, and approaches for scaling, consistency, and lifecycle management.

Key Takeaway

Match storage technology to data shape: immutable large blobs in object storage, structured catalog in an RDBMS, and user-scoped high-throughput records in NoSQL.
