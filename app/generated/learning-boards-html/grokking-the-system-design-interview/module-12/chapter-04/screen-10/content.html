Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Consistency & Availability
When each consistency model fits
Eventual consistency fits immutable objects. Strong consistency fits subscription and authorization checks that must reflect the latest state.
Practical patterns
  • Replicate relational data across zones with logical replication for DR.
  • Use NoSQL multi-region replication for user-scoped data where low latency matters.
  • Cache read-mostly metadata with short TTLs to preserve correctness while improving performance.
Concrete example

A canceled subscription must be honored immediately by the authorization service; use strongly-consistent storage paths for entitlements to avoid accidental access.

Failover & DR

Design cross-zone replication and tested failover procedures so availability is preserved during regional outages without losing correctness of critical metadata.

Key Takeaway

Be deliberate: accept eventual consistency where immutability and tolerance allow it, but use strong consistency for entitlements and control data that affect user access.
