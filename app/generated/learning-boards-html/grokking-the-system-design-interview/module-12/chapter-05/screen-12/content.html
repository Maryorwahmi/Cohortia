Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Scaling Strategy
Ingest
Geo-distributed PoPs, health checks, and failover to backups; autoscale edge workers as needed.
Transcoding
GPU-accelerated pools, autoscaling with warm capacity, and queue-based scale triggers to avoid backlog.
CDN
Multi-CDN with regional steering, origin shielding, and pre-warm policies for expected traffic.
Example plan
For 5M viewers: shard chat by region and channel, autoscale transcoding by queue depth, and use multi-CDN with DNS-based user steering. Rehearse with load tests mimicking peak concurrency.
