CDN responsibilities
  • Serve rapidly changing playlist parts with minimal additional hops.
  • Cache shield and regional origins to limit origin request rates.
  • Support multi-CDN failover and intelligent routing per region.
Operational tip
Coordinate TTLs and cache-control headers for parts, and validate CDN behavior during a dress rehearsal.
Why multi-CDN
Multi-CDN reduces vendor-specific outages and allows directing traffic to the best-performing network under load.
