Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › LL-HLS & LL-DASH
LL-HLS
  • Publishes partial segments (parts) as they are encoded.
  • Player can request parts immediately for lower latency.
  • Requires blocking playlist updates and CDN tuning.
LL-DASH
  • Uses fragmented MP4 parts and shorter availability windows.
  • Enables similar low-latency playback with DASH-compatible players.
Example
A global product launch uses LL-HLS so product demos appear to viewers within a few seconds of the live presenter, balancing broad device compatibility and lower latency.
