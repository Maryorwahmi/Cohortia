Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Ingest
What ingest does
  • Accepts incoming encoder connections and validates stream metadata and credentials.
  • Performs lightweight packet normalization and forwards to transcoding clusters or storage.
  • Maintains session health and offers redundant endpoints for failover.
Concrete example
A broadcaster uses an encoder configured to push via RTMP to a regional ingest point, which then hands off to a transcoding fleet in the same region to minimize latency.
