Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Monitoring & Reliability
Key SLIs to track
  • Ingest success rate and uplink bitrate stability.
  • Transcoding latency and queue depth.
  • CDN cache hit ratio and viewer-side rebuffer events.
Redundancy & alerts
Configure multi-region ingest, multi-CDN, and warm transcoding pools. Automate scale and notify operators on SLI breaches.
Runbook excerpt
If incoming RTMP bitrate drops >30% for 15 seconds: 1) attempt automatic reconnect, 2) route encoder to backup ingest, 3) page on-call and escalate if failover fails.
