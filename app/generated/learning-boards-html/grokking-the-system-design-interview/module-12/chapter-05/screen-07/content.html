Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › WebRTC
What WebRTC provides
  • Browser-native peer connections with sub-second latency.
  • Built-in congestion control, codec negotiation, and data channels for chat or signaling.
  • Requires signaling and TURN servers to handle NAT and relays.
Example
A live interview platform uses WebRTC for panelist audio/video and falls back to LL-HLS for large audience viewers.
Misconception
WebRTC is not always the best choice for millions of passive viewers because of persistent session and TURN costs.
