Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Key Concepts
Live vs VOD
Real-time pipelines need different trade-offs than pre-recorded content.
Protocols
Choose WebRTC for interactivity, LL-HLS/LL-DASH for low-second latency at scale.
Scale & Monitor
Autoscale ingestion, transcoding, and delivery; instrument SLIs and automate failover.
Takeaway

Design live systems by translating latency and scale requirements into protocol, provisioning, and operational choices; then rehearse them under load.
