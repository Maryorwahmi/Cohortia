Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Live Chat
Connection front doors
Persistent WebSocket or WebRTC data channels accept user connections and route messages to brokers.
Message brokers
Use Kafka or Redis Pub/Sub for durable, scalable fan-out and to support replay or moderation pipelines.
Sharding & moderation
Shard by channel or region and implement stream processors for filtering, rate limiting, and tagging.
Concrete scenario
Shard chat by language and region; ensure moderation pipeline can process messages with low latency to avoid toxic content reaching viewers.
