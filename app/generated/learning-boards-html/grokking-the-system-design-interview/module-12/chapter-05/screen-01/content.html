Live Streaming and Real-time Aspects
Understand end-to-end concerns for ingest, real-time transcoding, low-latency delivery, synchronization, scaling, and monitoring of live events.
In this lesson
You'll map the live streaming pipeline, compare protocols, learn scaling patterns, and prepare monitoring and redundancy plans for high-profile events.
Why it matters
Live events are unforgiving: a single failure or large latency can ruin engagement. Proper choices keep experiences responsive and reliable.
