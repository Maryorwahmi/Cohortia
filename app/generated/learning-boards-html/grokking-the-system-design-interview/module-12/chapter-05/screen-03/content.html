Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Demo & Monitoring Objectives
Terminal Demo Plan
  • Set up a local RTMP endpoint and push with FFmpeg to observe ingest.
  • Simulate live transcoding by creating 1-2 second segments and updating a live playlist.
  • Compare playlist latency and cache behavior between standard HLS and LL-HLS.
Monitoring Checklist
  • Ingest health: bitrate, keyframe intervals, and packet loss alerts.
  • Transcoding latency: end-to-end segment latency and queue depth.
  • Delivery: CDN cache hit ratio and viewer buffer rates.
Why practice matters
Hands-on demos reveal fragile assumptions, like relying on stable uplink or underprovisioned encoders.
Outcome
You will leave with a runnable plan and a monitoring playbook to support live events reliably.
