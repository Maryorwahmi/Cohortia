Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Operational Reminders
Format by content
Use JPEG for photos, PNG for crisp linework, and prune vector attributes.
Overlay strategy
Prefer vector streams for real-time overlays; raster overlays for rare updates.
Secure & monitor
Enforce origin access, sign URLs, and instrument for hotspots and abuse.
Final takeaway
Design tile generation and serving with format, caching, and operational controls in mind to deliver performant and cost-effective interactive maps.
