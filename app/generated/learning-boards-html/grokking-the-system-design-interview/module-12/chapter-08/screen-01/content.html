Tile Generation and Serving for Interactive Maps

Understand how maps are split into tiles, why tiles are pre-generated, and how CDNs and overlays fit into a scalable architecture.

Maps are composed of many small square tiles produced at multiple zoom levels. That design makes panning and zooming fast because clients only request visible tiles. Choosing raster or vector tiles and arranging CDN caching are the central design decisions for scale and flexibility.

Why this matters: tile design affects latency, device CPU usage, styling flexibility, and recurring costs for storage and egress.
