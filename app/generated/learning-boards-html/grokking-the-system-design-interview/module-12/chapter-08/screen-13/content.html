Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Security & Ops
Operational checklist
  • Validate and sanitize incoming geodata before tiling to prevent corruption or injection.
  • Restrict origin access with IAM, signed URLs, or edge-only origins to prevent hotlinking.
  • Instrument telemetry to detect unusual request patterns and high egress costs.
  • Use cache TTLs and CDN rate-limiting to stabilize load and control bills.
Example
Use signed, time-limited URLs for a premium tile style to prevent unauthorized access and unexpected egress.
Key Takeaway

Secure origins, monitor requests, and tune caching to keep tiles fast and costs under control.
