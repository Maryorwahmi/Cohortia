Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Mistakes & Optimizations
Common mistakes
  • Pre-generating unnecessary zooms without usage data.
  • Using lossless formats where lossy is acceptable, inflating size.
  • Skipping simplification or pruning for vector tiles.
  • Leaving origins public leading to hotlinking and egress costs.
Optimizations
  • Choose JPEG for photo tiles and PNG for linework where needed.
  • Prune attributes and simplify geometries by zoom for vector tiles.
  • Version tile paths, set cache headers, and warm hotspots on the CDN.
Try this
Audit your current tile sizes and top-requested tiles. Consider switching imagery to JPEG and pruning vector attributes to observe size improvements.
