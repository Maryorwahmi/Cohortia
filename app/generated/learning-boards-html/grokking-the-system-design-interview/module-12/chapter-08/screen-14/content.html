Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Key Concepts
Map Tiles
Small, cacheable square assets used to build interactive maps efficiently.
Raster vs Vector
Raster = images, Vector = geometry and attributes rendered client-side.
Origin + CDN
Store tiles by z/x/y at origin storage and serve them from a CDN edge for low latency.
Summary
Design tile formats, zoom coverage, and CDN strategies together to meet latency, cost, and flexibility goals.
