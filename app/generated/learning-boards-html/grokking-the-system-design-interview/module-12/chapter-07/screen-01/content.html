Orientation

We focus on three complementary approaches: spatial databases for exact geometry and topology, tree-based indexes for hierarchical spatial pruning, and coordinate encodings for fast prefix-based filtering and sharding. Each solves different problems at different cost points.

In this lesson
How indexes work, storage and sharding, and practical trade-offs for real systems.

Geohashing

Partitioning
Shard by region or geohash prefixes to scale reads and writes horizontally.
Key Takeaway

Combine tools: use spatial databases for exact geometry, use geohash for fast filtering and sharding, and maintain indexes to avoid performance degradation.
