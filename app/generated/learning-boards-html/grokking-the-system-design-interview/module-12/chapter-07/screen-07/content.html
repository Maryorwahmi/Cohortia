Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Quadtrees: recursive spatial subdivision
Process

Subdivide until leaves are manageable

Repeat splitting quadrants that exceed a point threshold to create an adaptive spatial index.

When to split

Define a capacity per node; if a node exceeds that capacity, split into four children and redistribute points.

Concrete scenario

A mapping tile with high POI density will subdivide more deeply than a rural tile, keeping point queries local and fast.

Advantages
  • Adaptive to data density
  • Straightforward implementation for point queries
  • Locality-friendly for caching and tiles
Limitation
Not ideal alone for complex polygon topology; needs additional geometric verification.
Takeaway
Use Quadtrees when adaptive spatial subdivision and fast point-in-region queries are central to your workload.
