Trade-offs explained

Index choice affects write performance, storage, and query latency; measure workload properties to decide.

High accuracy (R-tree/PostGIS)
Best for complex geometry and topological correctness; more expensive to maintain under heavy writes.
High scale (Geohash/NoSQL)
Scales writes and shards easily, but requires secondary filters for exactness and careful neighbor handling.
Design pattern
Two-stage search: 1) prefix or tree-based coarse filter. 2) exact geometric verification for the small candidate set.
Takeaway
Measure your write and read patterns, then choose or combine indexes to get the right balance of speed, accuracy, and cost.
