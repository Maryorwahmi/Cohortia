Learning Objectives — Applied Practice

What you should be able to build and defend

Bloom: Apply → Evaluate → Design
PostGIS & R-tree proficiency

Use spatial data types and functions for exact queries and rely on R-tree indexes to prune large candidate sets before geometric checks.

Geohash-based filtering

Generate geohashes for points, use prefix queries for quick candidate retrieval, and then apply precise distance checks when needed.

Sharding strategies

Design shards by geographic region or geohash prefix to balance load and keep locality for queries.

Trade-off analysis

Choose techniques that meet the system's accuracy, latency, and cost constraints rather than a single universal solution.
