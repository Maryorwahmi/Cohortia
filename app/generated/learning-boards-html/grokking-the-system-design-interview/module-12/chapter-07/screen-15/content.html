Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Key Concepts
Spatial Database
Databases with geometry types and spatial functions for exact topology, e.g., PostGIS.
R-tree
Hierarchical MBRs that prune spatial searches efficiently but need exact checks for candidates.
Quadtree
Recursive subdivision of space into quadrants, adaptive to data density for point queries.
Summary
Use the right tool for the dominant workload, combine approximate filters with exact checks for scale and accuracy, and automate maintenance for healthy indexes.
