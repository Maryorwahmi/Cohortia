Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Designing partitioning and distribution

Partitioning strategies

Decide between region, geohash prefix, or hybrid sharding based on the access patterns and balance requirements.

Region-based
Good for coarse locality when users rarely cross regional boundaries, but watch for uneven load across regions.
Geohash-prefix
Fine-grained locality and easy prefix computation; requires neighbor-aware queries near boundaries.
Operational considerations
  • Plan automated splitting of hot prefixes
  • Monitor distribution and rebalance proactively
  • Replicate critical shards for availability
Takeaway
Design partitions around locality, but include mechanisms to split, rebalance, and replicate as load changes.
