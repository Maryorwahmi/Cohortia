Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Using geohash for partitioning and sharding
Pattern

Prefix-based shard assignment

Map geohash prefixes to storage nodes to keep nearby points together and reduce query fan-out.

Shard mapping
Assign prefixes or prefix ranges to shards. Adjust prefix length to control shard granularity and load.
Hotspot handling
Detect heavy prefixes and split them into finer prefixes or rebalance ranges to avoid overloaded nodes.
Neighbor queries
Always query adjacent prefixes near cell borders and apply exact filters to avoid misses across geohash boundaries.
Takeaway
Geohash sharding is simple and effective for locality, but requires boundary-aware queries and dynamic handling of hot prefixes.
