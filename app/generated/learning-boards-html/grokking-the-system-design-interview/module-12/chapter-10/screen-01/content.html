Location Services, Search, and Real-time Updates

Understand how addresses become coordinates, how POIs are indexed and searched with spatial filters, and how streams of live GPS data are ingested and delivered with low latency and high freshness.

In this lesson

Walk through geocoding and reverse geocoding, design a scalable POI search index, and build a pipeline for real-time location updates with streams and WebSockets.

Why it matters

Low-latency, accurate location services are fundamental to routing, discovery, and live experiences such as ride-sharing and delivery tracking.

Explore Key Topics
Geocoding

Address parsing and database lookup with ambiguity resolution and normalization.

POI Search

Indexing billions of POIs and combining full-text search with spatial filters like geo_distance.

Real-time Updates

High-throughput ingestion, stream processing, geohashing, and WebSocket pushes for live location views.
