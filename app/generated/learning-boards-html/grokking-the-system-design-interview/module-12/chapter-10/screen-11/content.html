Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › WebSockets vs Polling
Quick contrast
Polling
Simple but inefficient at scale; high repeated requests.
WebSockets
Persistent, server push, lower overhead per update.
Operational concerns
  • Connection brokers and sharding to spread sockets across servers.
  • Heartbeat and liveness checks to detect dead clients.
  • Backpressure: avoid flooding clients with updates; aggregate or throttle as needed.
Subscription patterns
By Entity
Clients subscribe to a driver id to receive only that driver's updates.
By Region / Tile
Clients subscribe to tiles around their viewport to receive nearby updates.
Example: reducing churn

On reconnect storms, stagger reconnection with exponential backoff and random jitter. Use thin control channels for presence and heavier channels for location updates.
