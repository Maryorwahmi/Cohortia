Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Best Practices
Common mistakes
No spatial indexing
Leads to extremely slow geospatial queries under realistic loads.
Treating data as static
Updates become stale; user trust erodes without continuous pipelines.
Poor privacy controls
User location data requires strong privacy and access controls.
Mitigations & monitoring
Automated validation in ingestion, audit logs for POI changes, and anomaly detectors for traffic or tile generation issues.
Graceful degradation: fallback to recent estimates when real-time feeds are lost and inform clients about freshness constraints.
Key Takeaway

Scale is necessary but insufficient; robust validation, privacy, and monitoring keep the map useful and safe.
