Stream processing patterns
Ingest & validate
Buffer events, drop malformed items, and enforce privacy controls on user data.
Aggregate & smooth
Combine multiple device traces to estimate per-segment speeds and reduce noise.
Publish with safety
Expose derived traffic overlays while supporting rollback and anomaly detection.
Concrete example
GPS traces arrive as frequent events. The pipeline filters duplicates, groups by road segment, computes median speed across samples, smooths outliers, and publishes a traffic estimate that routing can consume.
Key Takeaway

Real-time overlays need stream processing, validation, and monitoring to produce reliable, timely data for routing and display.
