Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Key Concepts
Geocoding
Convert between addresses and coordinates to enable search and routing.
Routing Engine
Graph-based path computation that uses dynamic weights from traffic to determine optimal routes.
Spatial Indexing
Techniques like quadtrees, R-trees, and geohashing make spatial queries efficient and scalable.
Final Thought
Connect these concepts to concrete design choices: storage, indexing, caching, update frequency, and monitoring determine whether your map is fast, fresh, and correct.
