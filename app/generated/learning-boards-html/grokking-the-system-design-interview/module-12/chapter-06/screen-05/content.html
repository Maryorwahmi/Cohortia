Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Process Flow
Pipeline stages
1
Collection & validation
Receive imagery, GPS streams, business feeds; validate formats and coordinates.
2
Transformation & enrichment
Stitch imagery, extract vectors, attach attributes like hours or speed limits.
3
Indexing & packaging
Prepare data for spatial DBs, object stores, and search indices.
Concrete example
Aggregating vehicle GPS traces: ingest streaming coordinates, filter noise and duplicates, aggregate across devices to estimate speeds per road segment, and publish these estimates as traffic overlays for routing and tile rendering.
Key Takeaway

Treat ingestion as streaming ETL with validation and monitoring to avoid stale or corrupt map features.

Common Mistake
Assuming bulk imports are enough; without continuous validation, dynamic layers like traffic will be inaccurate.
