Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Key Concepts
Geospatial Data
Describes location and shape of features; requires specialized storage and indexing for efficient queries.
Map Tiles
Small raster or vector files representing map areas at zoom levels; cached at edges for fast delivery.
Points of Interest
Searchable entities like businesses and landmarks with attributes used for ranking and display.
Summary
Design choices for storage, indexing, caching, and update frequency follow directly from the data type and freshness requirements.
