A* Search
Combine actual cost and heuristic estimate to guide search toward the goal.
Order by f(n)=g(n)+h(n)
Admissible heuristic never overestimates remaining cost — guarantees optimality.
Practical heuristic straight-line distance converted to time is common for road routing.
Example

A* on a city map will pick nodes nearer to the destination first. This reduces explored area when the heuristic is informative and admissible.

Misconception

A stronger, non-admissible heuristic can be faster but may return a suboptimal route; never sacrifice correctness without understanding the cost.
