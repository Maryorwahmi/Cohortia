Process Flow
Scaling Challenges
Size: hundreds of millions of nodes; single-machine searches are infeasible.
Latency: user-facing queries need ms–100ms responses.
Dynamics: traffic changes mean weights are time-varying and must be integrated safely.
Distribute graph across machines and use locality-aware sharding.
Cache popular origin-destination pairs for instant responses.
Pre-compute highway-level routes to avoid full-graph searches.
Pitfall

Applying basic algorithms without pre-computation or caching leads to unacceptable latency in production systems.

Scaling strategy cards
Sharding: partition graph so queries touch few machines.
Caching: store frequent routes or route fragments in fast stores like Redis.
Pre-computation: build multi-level abstractions so long trips use a shrunk graph.
