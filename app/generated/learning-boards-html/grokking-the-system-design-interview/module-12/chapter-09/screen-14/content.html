Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Key Concepts
Graph Theory
Nodes and edges with weights represent a road network; pick semantics that match routing goals.
Dijkstra & A*
Dijkstra is optimal for non-negative weights; A* adds a heuristic to significantly reduce search for goal-driven queries.
Pre-computation & Traffic
Contraction Hierarchies and Transit Node Routing enable microsecond queries; streaming traffic updates keep ETAs relevant.
Summary

Modeling, algorithm choice, and pre-computation plus streaming are the three levers you use to trade optimality, latency, and freshness in routing systems.
