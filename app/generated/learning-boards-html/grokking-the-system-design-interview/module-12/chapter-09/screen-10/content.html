Process Flow
Pre-computation & Hierarchy
Build a multi-level abstraction so long-distance routing happens on a smaller graph.
Identify important nodes and edges that form a highway-level graph.
Pre-compute connectivity between high-level nodes to avoid exploring full local detail.
At query time, stitch origin-to-highway, highway-level path, and highway-to-destination segments.
Example

For inter-city trips, a route mostly traverses highways. Using a hierarchy means only local search near endpoints plus a compact highway graph in the middle.

Trade-offs
Pros: much faster long-distance queries, fewer nodes explored.
Cons: expensive preprocessing, harder to apply frequent dynamic weight updates.
Tip: combine hierarchy with incremental updates or dynamic deltas layered on top.
