Process Flow
Global Message Routing & Cross-Region Replication
Mirror message streams or route messages across regions to keep local consumers responsive.
Local write path
Client writes to the local message queue. Local consumers persist to the local shard and deliver to connected clients immediately.
Cross-region mirroring
Use queue mirroring to replicate partitions to other regions; remote consumers subscribe and deliver messages to remote clients.
Example trade-offs
Mirroring improves local latency but introduces eventual delivery and potential ordering differences. For chats, eventual consistency for message visibility is often tolerable; pick a UX that shows "delivered" vs "received" clearly.
Key Takeaway
Mirror streams to local regions to reduce critical-path latency, but design UX for replication lag.
Misconception
Assuming cross-region mirroring makes delivery instantaneous; it will not and ordering can be affected.
