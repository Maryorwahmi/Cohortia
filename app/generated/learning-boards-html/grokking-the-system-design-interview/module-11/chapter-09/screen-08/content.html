Core Concept
Replication & Durability in Message Queues
Replicas reduce data loss and keep partitions available after broker failures.
How it works
Each partition has a leader and followers. Producers write to the leader; the leader replicates to followers. If leader dies, a follower becomes leader.
Choose replication factor for durability. Higher replication reduces data loss risk but can increase write latency and resource cost.
Example
Replication factor 3 is common: messages survive one or two broker failures depending on sync settings.
Note: replication is not a substitute for backups.
Operational Tip
Distribute replicas across racks and regions to minimize correlated failures.
Misconception
Assuming replication alone handles logical errors; you still need backups and versioned deletes.
