Core Concept
Geographical Distribution & Replication Modes
Deploy regions near users and pick a replication mode based on availability and consistency needs.
• Active-passive: single primary handles writes; others are backups. Simpler consistency, slower failover.
• Active-active: multiple regions accept writes; lower latency and better availability, requires conflict resolution.
• Client routing: use DNS-based latency routing to direct clients to the nearest region for best latency.
Misconception: active-active always increases user experience. It can if conflicts are well handled, but poor conflict logic causes surprises.
Example decision
If your chat writes are append-only messages and eventual consistency is acceptable, active-active across regions lowers read/write latency for global users while keeping conflict risk low.
Operational Tip
Plan conflict resolution and replication lag tolerance before enabling active-active.
Warning
Neglecting conflict policies in active-active setups leads to inconsistent user experiences.
