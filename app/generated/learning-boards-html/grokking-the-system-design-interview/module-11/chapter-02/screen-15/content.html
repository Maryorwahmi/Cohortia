Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Key Concepts
Data Model
Structure users, posts, and follows to reflect the queries you will run most.
Storage Choice
Use SQL for integrity and NoSQL for denormalized, partitioned feeds; combine as needed.
Denormalization
Duplicate for speed but design reconciliation and retention to manage consistency and growth.
Summary Takeaway
Align keys, indexes, and storage types to your feed's access patterns and accept trade-offs as part of a robust, scalable design.
