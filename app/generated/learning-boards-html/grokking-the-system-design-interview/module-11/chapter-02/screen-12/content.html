Architecture
Polyglot Persistence
Assign each data type to the storage that matches its access and consistency needs.
SQL
Users and follows for integrity and transactional operations.
NoSQL
Denormalized user_feed tables for fast, partitioned reads.
Cache
Redis or similar for hot content and rate-limiting read pressure.
Operational clarity
Define authoritative data owners and background jobs for feed population and reconciliation.
Key Takeaway

A mixed storage approach lets you optimize for both correctness and scale; keep responsibilities and sync paths explicit.
