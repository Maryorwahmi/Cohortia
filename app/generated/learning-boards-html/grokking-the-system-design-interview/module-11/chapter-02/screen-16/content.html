Indexing
Create indexes on user_id and created_at to enable efficient author and time-ordered queries.
Partitioning
Partition by user_id and cluster by timestamp for per-user feeds.
Denormalize carefully
Duplicate content for reads but design background reconciliation and retention.
Final thought
Treat each feed component independently when choosing storage; the right mix delivers both correctness and scale.
