Fanout: Push vs Pull
Step through cost and latency trade-offs to pick the right model.
Fanout-on-write (Push)
Write a post into each follower's inbox at creation time. Fast reads, higher write cost.
Fanout-on-read (Pull)
Store posts once and assemble feeds on read by merging followees' posts. Lower write cost, slower reads.
Hybrid
Push for typical users, pull for high-fanout accounts; or cache hot authors separately.
Concrete scenario
A user with 100 followers triggers 100 inserts on push — manageable. A celebrity with 10 million followers makes push impractical without batching, async processing, or a hybrid plan.
Key Takeaway

Match fanout strategy to the distribution of follower counts and the read/write ratio of your application.
