Common Mistakes
Safety Notes for Feed Design
Avoid predictable pitfalls by planning for reads, writes, and growth.
Over-normalization
Joins at feed-read time are expensive at scale.
Ignoring access patterns
Pick keys and indexes that match your queries.
Monolithic DB
One database for everything makes scaling and ops harder.
No retention plan
Plan archiving and compaction to control storage costs.
Key Takeaway

Think ahead: document queries, choose keys that fit patterns, and separate stores to reduce operational complexity as the feed grows.
