Definition
Consistency, Durability & Fault Tolerance
How to choose replication, acknowledgement, and recovery strategies.
Principles
  • Consistency vs Availability: choose based on user-facing expectations during partitions.
  • Durability: persist critical data to durable storage or replicated brokers before acknowledging.
  • Fault Tolerance: design to keep serving degraded but useful functionality when components fail.
Concrete Examples
Feed: serve cached timelines when upstream data is unavailable, and update when fresh data arrives. Chat: buffer outgoing messages locally, persist to broker, and retry until acknowledged to avoid loss.
Misconception
Eventual consistency is not careless; it requires clear freshness targets and compensating UX design.
Key Takeaway

Match consistency and durability to product expectations; design recovery and retry pathways for reliability.
