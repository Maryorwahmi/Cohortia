Quick Design Reminders
Short prompts to use during design conversations.
Fanout Decision
State whether you push, pull, or use hybrid, and why based on follower sizes and freshness needs.
Connection Model
Declare whether you use WebSockets, server-sent events, or HTTP polling and how you route connections at scale.
Consistency & Durability
Explain where you accept eventual consistency and where you require durable, replicated writes.
Final Prompt
Before any design sketch, write a one-paragraph assumption summary: traffic patterns, freshness expectations, and failure tolerance. Use it to justify your architecture.
