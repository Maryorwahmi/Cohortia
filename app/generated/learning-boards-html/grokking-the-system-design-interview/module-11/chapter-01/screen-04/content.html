Core Concept
What is a News Feed?
A personalized, reverse-chronological or ranked stream that favors reads.
Definition & Behavior
  • Personalized aggregation of posts from followed entities.
  • Read-heavy: far more reads than writes per user.
  • Often uses ranking, caching, and precomputed timelines to reduce latency.
Common Mistake
Fetching all followees' posts and sorting per request causes N+1 collapse under scale.
Illustrative Example
User A follows 1,000 accounts. Naively querying each account and merging per request causes huge latency spikes. Better: precompute a timeline for heavy users or use selective pull for light-following users to balance cost and freshness.
Push (Precompute)
Push new posts into followers' timelines for fast reads; higher write cost and storage.
Pull (On-demand)
Fetch recent posts from followees on request; lower storage but higher read latency.
Key Takeaway

Treat feeds as read-optimized services and choose push or pull fanout based on follower counts and freshness needs.
