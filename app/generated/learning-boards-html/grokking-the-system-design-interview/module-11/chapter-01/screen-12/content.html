Key Concepts
Three cards to remember as you design.
News Feed
Personalized, read-heavy streams; optimized by caching and fanout choices.
Chat System
Stateful, low-latency messaging with durable delivery and presence management.
Scalability
Sharding, replication, load balancing, and hotspot mitigation are central to scale strategies.
Summary Takeaway
Match architecture to the user expectations for freshness, latency, and durability. Use fanout, persistent connections, and partitioning thoughtfully.
