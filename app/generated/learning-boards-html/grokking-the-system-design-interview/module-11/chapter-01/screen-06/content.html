Read-Heavy vs Read/Write Heavy
How dominant access patterns shape architecture.
News Feed (Read-Heavy)
  • Optimize for fast reads: caches, precomputed timelines, CDN.
  • Accept controlled eventual consistency for ranking freshness.
  • Fanout-heavy writes for popular users are a write cost concern.
Example
Precompute timelines for users with many followers to serve reads in milliseconds.
Chat (Read/Write Heavy)
  • Frequent writes require durable brokers and efficient routing.
  • Persistent connections and in-memory presence reduce latency.
  • Delivery semantics determine storage and retry strategies.
Example
Use message brokers for group chats and persist messages to durable stores for history and recovery.
Misconception

Caching alone fixes latency. In reality, caching shifts complexity to invalidation and freshness trade-offs.

Key Takeaway

Detect dominant access patterns early and prioritize scaling strategies that address the hottest operations.
