Learning Objectives
Map learning outcomes to practical system design skills.
Bloom
Learn Understand Apply Master
01
Identify core system requirements
You will list and explain latency, availability, scalability, personalization, and durability needs and why they matter for user experience.
Mnemonic: LAPS (Latency, Availability, Personalization, Scalability)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Recognize real-time scaling challenges
You will explain throughput, concurrency, and the N+1 problem and how they create hotspots and bottlenecks in feed and chat systems.
Mnemonic: N+1 alert
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Differentiate feed and chat components
You will map components such as fanout, caching, and ranking for feeds, and presence, message broker, and persistence for chat.
Mnemonic: F-C-R vs P-M-P
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Relate user patterns to design choices
You will translate patterns like read-heavy access or persistent chats into caching, replication, and protocol decisions.
Mnemonic: Pattern→Practice
