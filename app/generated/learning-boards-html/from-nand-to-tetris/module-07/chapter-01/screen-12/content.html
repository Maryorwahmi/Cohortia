Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › What Jack Omits
Missing features
  • Floating-point types and arithmetic
  • Advanced control flow: for, switch, break/continue
  • Inheritance, polymorphism, and dynamic dispatch
Why omitted
Each omitted feature adds significant compiler and runtime complexity. Omitting them keeps the language and compiler tractable for learning while preserving the ability to express algorithms.
