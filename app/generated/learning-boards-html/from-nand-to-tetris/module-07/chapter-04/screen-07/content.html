Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Operator Precedence
Why precedence matters
Precedence determines grouping in the parse tree, which in turn determines the order of pushes and operator emissions.
1. The parser groups higher-precedence operators into subtrees.
2. The generator compiles each subtree to leave a value on the stack.
3. Parent nodes combine values with operator commands.
Quick test
Construct expressions mixing addition and multiplication and compare your VM output to the reference to confirm correct ordering.
Takeaway
Always compile by tree structure; never by raw token order.
