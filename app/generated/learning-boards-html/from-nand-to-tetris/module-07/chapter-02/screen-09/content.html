Process Flow Comments & Whitespace
Line comments (//)
When seeing two slashes, discard until end of line and continue tokenizing after the newline.
Block comments (/* */)
When seeing slash star, read until the next star slash. Ignore everything in between, even newlines, then resume tokenizing.
Slash handling
If next character after slash is slash → line comment. If star → block comment. Otherwise emit slash as symbol token.
Common mistake
Treating comment markers as separate tokens or failing to look ahead after a slash leads to wrong token streams.
Key Takeaway

Remove comments and whitespace in the lexer so the parser receives only meaningful tokens.
