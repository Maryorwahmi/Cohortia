Reading quoted strings
When the lexer sees a double quote, switch to a mode that copies characters until the next double quote. Do not interpret symbols inside the string.
Examples
"Hello"
"He said: Hello, world!"
"x + y = 10"
Common mistake
Stopping at punctuation or failing to detect a missing closing quote leads to tokenization errors and parser confusion.
Key Takeaway

Treat the entire quoted sequence as one atomic token and ensure the closing quote is present.
