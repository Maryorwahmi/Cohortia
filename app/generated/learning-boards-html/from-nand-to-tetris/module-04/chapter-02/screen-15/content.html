Quick Reference › Cheat sheet
Labels
(NAME) → ROM address of next instruction. Used for jumps and references.
Predefined & variables
R0–R15 reserved; variables begin at 16; SCREEN and KBD map to fixed IO addresses.
Two-pass rule
Pass one: record labels. Pass two: translate instructions and assign variable addresses.
Final thought
When you translate programs, always validate the symbol table before trusting the binary output; that is where most errors hide.
