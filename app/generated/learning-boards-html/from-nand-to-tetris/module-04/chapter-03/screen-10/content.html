Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Off-by-One
Comparison
Common causes and fixes
Compare initialization, decrement timing, and the jump condition to locate the off-by-one fault.
Checklist to debug
  • Verify initial counter value matches iteration count intent.
  • Inspect whether decrement happens before or after the conditional check.
  • Confirm the jump type (JGT, JGE, JEQ) matches desired logic.
Concrete debugging step
Set a breakpoint at the jump instruction. Step until the breakpoint and record the counter value on each hit. If the counter reaches the terminal value one iteration later than expected, move the decrement or adjust initialization accordingly.
Mistake to avoid

Avoid guessing. Use concrete recorded values from the emulator to choose the correct fix, not intuition alone.
