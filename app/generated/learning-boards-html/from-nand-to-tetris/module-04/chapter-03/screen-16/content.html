Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Quick Checklist
Initialize everything
Set counters, pointers and temporary variables before loops execute.
Test position matters
Decide whether to check before or after the loop body to avoid off-by-one behaviors.
Pick the right jump
JGT, JEQ, and JLT have distinct semantics; match them to the comparison you compute in the ALU.
Final note
Treat I/O as memory operations and use the CPU Emulator for targeted stepping and breakpoint-driven inspection when programs misbehave.
