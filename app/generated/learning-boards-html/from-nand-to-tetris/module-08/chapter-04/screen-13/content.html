Hands-on Activity › Implement and test Math & Sys
Step 1 — Implement
Write Math.multiply, Math.divide, Math.sqrt and Sys.init, Sys.halt, Sys.error, Sys.wait. Keep functions small and testable.
Step 2 — Test
Create unit tests for edge cases: zeros, ones, negatives, division by zero, perfect and non-perfect squares. Log outputs and verify expected numeric results or error codes.
Step 3 — Compare
Implement Fibonacci recursively and iteratively. Count Math operations or time steps to compare performance and document findings.
Why it matters

Testing and measurement turn abstract algorithms into reliable code for constrained hardware. The Fibonacci comparison demonstrates how algorithm choice matters in practice.
