Insertion strategies
  • Sorted-by-address insertion: easier coalescing of neighbors.
  • Front insertion: simple pointer update, harder to coalesce.
  • Best-fit aware insertion: try to place freed block where it optimizes future allocations.
Double-free check
Verify an address was allocated and not already freed. In small systems, a simple guard-word or flag can help detect double frees during testing.
Coalescing example
Free list: [A(size=5)]->[B(size=8)]->[C(size=10)]. If block between A and B is freed and addresses align, merge to form A' with size 5+header+8. Tests: free neighboring blocks and ensure resulting free list has merged blocks and not two separate entries.
Key Takeaway

Plan deAlloc insertion so coalescing is efficient. Include validation checks to prevent double-free and invalid-free errors during development.
