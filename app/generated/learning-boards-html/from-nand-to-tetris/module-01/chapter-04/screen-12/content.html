Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Gate counting
Counting example
One 1‑bit And uses two Nand gates. Therefore And16 uses 16 × 2 = 32 Nand gates. This follows directly from replication: each bit gets its own hardware.
Contrast

Time‑multiplexing a single And reduces area but increases latency because you must sequence bit operations across cycles.

Takeaway

Parallel replication gives word‑wide single‑cycle behavior at the predictable cost of linear gate count increase.
