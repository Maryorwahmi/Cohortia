Zero detection architecture
Split the 16‑bit input into high[8] and low[8]. Compute two Or8Way outputs, Or them, then invert to produce IsZero.
// Logical expression:
// IsZero = Not(Or(Or8Way(high[8]), Or8Way(low[8])))

// HDL idea: instantiate two Or8Way parts and a final Or and Not.
Example

For input 0x0000 both Or8Way outputs are 0, so IsZero becomes 1. For input 0x0001 a low half Or8Way yields 1, IsZero becomes 0.

Pitfall

Do not attempt to replace the final Not with inverted Or8Way outputs; be explicit to avoid inversion logic mistakes.
