Not16 HDL
CHIP Not16 {
  IN in[16];
  OUT out[16];

  PARTS:
  Not(in=in[0], out=out[0]);
  Not(in=in[1], out=out[1]);
  // ... repeat through index 15
  Not(in=in[15], out=out[15]);
}
Example

Not16(0x0000) = 0xFFFF; Not16(0xAAAA) = 0x5555. Use these to sanity‑check wiring and bit ordering.

Misconception

Do not confuse bitwise inversion with arithmetic two's complement negation; they are different operations and implemented differently.
