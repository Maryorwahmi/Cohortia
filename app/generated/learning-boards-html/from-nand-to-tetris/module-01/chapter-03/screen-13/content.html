Why three Muxes implement a 4-way selector

Hierarchical reduction is the explanation: two Muxes reduce four inputs to two, the third reduces two to one.

Structure
left = Mux(a,b,sel0) right = Mux(c,d,sel0) out = Mux(left,right,sel1)
Efficiency
Three Muxes avoid redundancy and map selection bits cleanly. Using four Muxes would require extra combining logic.
Reflect

Can you generalize how many two-way Muxes are needed for 8 inputs? Think about the binary tree node count.
