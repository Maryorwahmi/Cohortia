Learning Objectives

From simple selectors to multi-bit routing

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Design 2-way Mux
Implement the boolean expression and HDL for a 2-to-1 multiplexer and explain why it forwards one input unchanged.
Mnemonic: simple switch
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Compose wider Muxes
Build 4-way and 8-way selectors by combining 2-way Muxes and explain the hierarchical selection pattern.
Mnemonic: binary tree
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement DMux variants
Create 1-to-2, 1-to-4, and 1-to-8 demultiplexers and describe when a DMux is used instead of a Mux.
Mnemonic: fork in the road
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Scale to multi-bit buses
Replicate 1-bit Muxes across a bus to make Mux16 and DMux16 and explain why selection bits broadcast across the bus.
Mnemonic: synchronized tracks
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
