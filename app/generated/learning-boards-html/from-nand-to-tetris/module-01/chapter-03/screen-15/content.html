Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › What to Remember
Broadcast selection
Ensure the same sel line reaches every parallel 1-bit Mux in a bus.
Compose recursively
Build wide selectors from smaller ones to keep designs modular.
Replicate for buses
Multi-bit chips are parallel copies of 1-bit logic operating synchronously.
One-line summary

Mux and DMux are the routing primitives of a CPU: compose, broadcast, and test thoroughly.
