Multiplexers & Demultiplexers

Build digital switches to route single-bit and multi-bit data using selection bits.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson
Design Mux/DMux and scale them to buses

Learn the Boolean forms, HDL implementations, and recursive construction patterns that let you compose wider selectors from 2-way components.

Why it matters
Routing is everywhere in a CPU

Registers, ALU inputs, and memory buses rely on many small Muxes working in parallel to choose data paths at clock boundaries.

Outcome
Write and test HDL for Mux family

By the end you will implement Mux, DMux, Mux16 and their wider variants, and explain the selection bit strategy used across all bits.

How we'll learn
Small examples, composition, and bus parallelism

We will combine gates to build a Mux, then compose Muxes into wider selectors and replicate them across buses for multi-bit operation.
