Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Recursive Mux
Pattern

2^n-way by composition

Use two smaller selectors and a top-level Mux to choose between their outputs. Selection bits are partitioned by level.

8-way example
left = Mux4Way(inputs[0..3], sel[0..1]) right = Mux4Way(inputs[4..7], sel[0..1]) out = Mux(left, right, sel[2])
Why it helps
Modularity simplifies HDL and reduces chances of wiring errors. It also mirrors efficient hardware practice.
Checklist
  • Partition selection bits by level (low-order for inner Muxes).
  • Instantiate smaller chips, wire intermediate outputs to top-level Mux.
  • Test every selection vector to validate the hierarchical mapping.
Common misconception

You do not need unique gate formulas for each wider Mux; composition reuses smaller, tested building blocks.
