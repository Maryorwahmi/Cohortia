Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › 4-way Mux
Process

Compose 4-way from 2-way parts

Build two Muxes to select pairs, then a third to choose between those results using hierarchical selection bits.

Step 1
Create left = Mux(a,b,sel0) and right = Mux(c,d,sel0).
Step 2
Output = Mux(left, right, sel1). Sel1 chooses the pair, sel0 chooses inside each pair.
Example
For sel[1..0] = 10, sel1=1 picks the right pair, and sel0=0 picks c within that pair.
Visual Steps
1
First-level pairwise selection reduces the candidate set from four to two.
2
Second-level selection uses the high-order select bit to pick between intermediate results.
3
This pattern recurses: an 8-way uses two 4-way Muxes plus a 2-way Mux to choose between them.
Check your understanding

Why are three Muxes sufficient for 4 inputs? Answer: two reduce 4→2, the third reduces 2→1.
