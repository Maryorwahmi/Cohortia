Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Canonical Forms: SOP vs POS
Sum of Products (SOP)
An Or of And terms; each minterm corresponds to a truth table row where output is 1 and includes every input or its negation.
Product of Sums (POS)
An And of Or terms; each maxterm corresponds to a row where output is 0 and includes every input or its negation.
Key Takeaway

Use canonical forms to get a correct expression from a truth table, then simplify algebraically or with K-maps to reduce gates.

Example: Majority
<SOP from majority truth table>
Majority(a,b,c) = (¬a ∧ b ∧ c) ∨ (a ∧ ¬b ∧ c)
  ∨ (a ∧ b ∧ ¬c) ∨ (a ∧ b ∧ c)
This SOP is correct but not minimal. A later simplification uses absorption and distributive identities to reduce it to three two-input And terms Or'ed together.
