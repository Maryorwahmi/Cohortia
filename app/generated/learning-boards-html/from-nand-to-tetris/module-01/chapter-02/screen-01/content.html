Boolean Algebra and Truth Tables
A compact system for reasoning about true/false signals and designing digital circuits.

Boolean values are 0 or 1. Operations And, Or, Not form every digital logic expression.

Truth tables list all 2^n input combinations so you can precisely define a function.

Algebraic laws let you transform and simplify expressions; De Morgan's laws let you swap And and Or under negation.
