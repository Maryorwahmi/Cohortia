Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Majority and Parity Functions
Majority (3 inputs)
Majority(a,b,c) = (a ∧ b) ∨ (a ∧ c) ∨ (b ∧ c)
This simplified form follows directly from combining minterms and applying absorption and distributive laws.
Odd / Parity (3 inputs)
Outputs 1 when an odd number of inputs are 1; its SOP is produced from rows with a single 1 or all three being 1 where odd count applies.
Misconception

Parity may resist the same simplification as majority; recognizing function type helps choose an efficient representation, such as using XOR equivalents when available.
