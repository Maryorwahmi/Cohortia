Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Boolean basics
Variables are 0/1 and operations And, Or, Not are defined by truth tables.
De Morgan
Negation distributes and flips connectives: ¬(x∧y)=¬x∨¬y and ¬(x∨y)=¬x∧¬y.
Canonical forms
SOP lists minterms for output-1 rows; POS lists maxterms for output-0 rows.
One-sentence summary

Use truth tables to get a correct expression, apply laws or K-maps to simplify, and then map to gates using De Morgan when necessary.
