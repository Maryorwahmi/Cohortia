Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Nand truth table
Only inputs 1 and 1 produce 0; all other pairs produce 1.
Universality
Not, And, and Or can be constructed from Nand, so any Boolean function is possible.
Design trade-offs
Equivalent logic can differ in gate count and delay; choose efficient decompositions when possible.
Summary

Keep these concepts front of mind as you write HDL and scale from gates to chips: the Nand truth table is the atomic fact, universality gives you a toolkit, and implementation choices determine cost.
