Definition

Nand = NOT (AND)

A two-input gate that inverts the And result. The single exceptional case is both inputs true.

Common misconception

Do not assume Nand is symmetric to Nor; Nand negates And, not Or. Confusing the operators leads to wrong wiring when composing gates.

Concrete example
Example scenario

If two sensors must both read true to disable a safety output, wiring them through Nand preserves the safety output as true unless both sensors are true, which then disables the output.

Why this matters

The Nand truth table is the atomic truth for proofs of universality and for reasoning about small combinational circuits before moving to HDL.

Key Takeaway

Memorize the four-row table: only 1,1 produces 0. This single fact unlocks constructive gate designs using Nand.
