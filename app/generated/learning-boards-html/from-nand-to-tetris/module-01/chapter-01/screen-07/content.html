Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › And & Or from Nand
Comparison

From Nand to And, then Or

Logical manipulation and De Morgan show how to compose Nand wiring into common gates.

And construction

t = Nand(a,b); And = Nand(t,t). Gate count: 2 Nands.

Or construction

na = Nand(a,a); nb = Nand(b,b); Or = Nand(na,nb). Gate count: 3 Nands.

Counting gates matters

Different equivalent logical formulas can require more or fewer Nand primitives. Gate count influences chip area and delay; always consider alternative decompositions when optimizing.

Concrete example
Evaluate Or(1,0)

na = Nand(1,1) = 0; nb = Nand(0,0) = 1; final = Nand(0,1) = 1. Result matches expected Or = 1.

Key Takeaway

Use inversion plus De Morgan transforms to build And and Or from Nand and count gates for efficiency.
