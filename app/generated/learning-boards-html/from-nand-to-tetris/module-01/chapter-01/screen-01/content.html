The Nand Gate: A Universal Primitive

A two-input logical gate whose output is false only when both inputs are true. Its universality means every Boolean function can be made from many Nands.

In this lesson

Learn truth table, build Not/And/Or/Xor from Nand, and express gates in simple HDL.

Why it matters

Universality simplifies fabrication and clarifies abstraction layers from gates to CPUs.

Key Takeaway

A single, two-input Nand gate is enough to construct every other gate and thereby any Boolean function.
