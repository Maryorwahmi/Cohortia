Architecture

High-level transistor view

CMOS Nand arranges two PMOS in parallel for pull-up and two NMOS in series for pull-down. This yields the required logical inversion of And.

  • PMOS network pulls output high unless both inputs are high.
  • NMOS network pulls output low only when both inputs are high.
  • Stacking transistors affects resistance, delay, and area — practical trade-offs for designers.
Why understand this

Knowing the physical mapping explains why logical choices matter for speed, power, and manufacturability when you scale from gates to chips.

Concrete note
Physical insight

Transistors are physical switches. Universality at the logic level becomes repeated physical switches arranged across silicon to implement complex functions.

Key Takeaway

Logical gates correspond to transistor networks; physical costs explain why equivalent logic can have different implementation trade-offs.
