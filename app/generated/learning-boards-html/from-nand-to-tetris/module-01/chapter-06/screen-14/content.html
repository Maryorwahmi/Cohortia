Module 1 › Chapter 1.6 › Key Concepts
Nand universality
All elementary gates can be composed from Nand, which enforces constructional understanding.
Modular hierarchy
Verify small components, then compose them into 16-bit and multi-way chips.
Design & verify loop
Understand → design → write HDL → test → verify is the recurring workflow.
Summary

Project 1 gives you a tested, composable standard library built from a single primitive. These ideas make larger systems tractable and verifiable.
