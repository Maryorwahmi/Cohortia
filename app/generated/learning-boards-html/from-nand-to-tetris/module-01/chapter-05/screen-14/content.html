Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Key Concepts
HDL Structure
CHIP, IN, OUT, PARTS define the interface and composition of a chip.
Named Signals
Outputs assigned to names become internal wires when reused in other parts.
Buses & Ranges
Declare widths with [n] and slice with [i..j] when parts accept vectors.
Simulator Workflow
load, set, eval, output, compare-to automates verification and points to failing cases.
Common Mistakes
Swapped pins, missing wires, wrong parts, width mismatches, or forgetting eval.
Modularity
Test components, then use them as black boxes to manage complexity and enable reuse.
Summary

HDL describes circuits as networks of named parts and wires. Combine careful wiring with automated tests to build reliable, modular hardware.
