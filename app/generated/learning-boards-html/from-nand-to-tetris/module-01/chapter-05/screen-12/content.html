Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Abstraction & Modularity
Principle

Test small, compose large

Verify each chip separately. Then use it as a black box in larger chips without exposing internal details.

Benefits
  • Reduces complexity when designing higher-level chips.
  • Makes debugging localized to the failing component.
  • Allows internal optimization without changing interfaces.
Practice
• Build Not, And, Or and test them.
• Use them to implement Mux and verify the Mux with a test script.
• Treat Mux as a black box when composing larger buses or adders.
Key Takeaway

Abstraction in HDL means verified components become reliable building blocks; test boundaries clearly and reuse with confidence.
