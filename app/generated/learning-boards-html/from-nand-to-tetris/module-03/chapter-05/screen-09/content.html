Chapter 3.5 › Conditional Branching

Conditional branching and PC updates

Jump bits consult ALU flags; PC is loaded from A on true conditions, otherwise it increments.

Jump sequence
1. @TARGET — load 15-bit address into A.
2. D;JLT — compute condition, if true load PC ← A at update.
Concrete example
@100
D=D-A
@END
D;JLT
If D - 100 is negative, ALU ng=1 and the JLT condition causes PC to load END.
Why two instructions?

A C-instruction cannot encode a full 15-bit target address because its format reserves bits for comp, dest and jump; the A-instruction provides the full 15-bit address instead.

Key Takeaway

Always ensure the A register holds the intended jump target before executing a conditional jump C-instruction.
