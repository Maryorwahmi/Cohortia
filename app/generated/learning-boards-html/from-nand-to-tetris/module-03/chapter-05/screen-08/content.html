Chapter 3.5 › C-instruction & ALU

C-instruction execution

How comp, a, dest, and jump drive ALU behavior and writes.

Architecture view
ALU inputs
a=0 → inputs D and A. a=1 → inputs D and M (RAM[A]).
comp
Selects the operation the ALU performs, producing zr and ng flags.
dest
Determines which of A, D, or M receives the result on update.
jump
Jump bits consult ALU flags to decide whether to load PC from A.
Concrete examples
D=A
comp=A, dest=D, no jump → D gets A, PC++
D=D+A
ALU computes D + A, dest=D, PC++
Pitfall

Assuming ALU reads M before A is set. A must already hold the address if a=1 and M is to be used.

Key Takeaway

Decode comp and a-bit first so the ALU selects the correct inputs; dest writes are committed at update.
