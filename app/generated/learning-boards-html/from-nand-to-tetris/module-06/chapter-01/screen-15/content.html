Return labels
Create a fresh label per call site; duplicates break nested returns.
Save & restore
Always push and later restore LCL, ARG, THIS, THAT in the specified order.
Use FRAME offsets
Compute RET = *(FRAME - 5) and restore segments from FRAME-1..FRAME-4.
