Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Two's Complement
Encoding rule
To negate: invert all bits, then add 1. +5 => 00000101 ~(+5) => 11111010 +1 => 11111011 (-5)
Note: the MSB contributes a negative weight of -2^(n-1) when interpreting the signed sum. This is why ranges are asymmetric.
Range example
8-bit two's complement range: -128 to 127. 16-bit range: -32,768 to 32,767. The most negative number has no positive negation in the same width.
Common misconception
Don't treat the sign bit as a separate magnitude field. Two's complement is not sign-magnitude; the bits form a single weighted sum including a negative high weight.
