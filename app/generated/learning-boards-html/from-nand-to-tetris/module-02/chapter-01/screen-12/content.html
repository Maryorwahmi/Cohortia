Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Advantages
Two's complement
  • Single representation of zero.
  • Same adder for signed and unsigned arithmetic.
  • Negation by invert and add one.
Alternatives
  • Sign-magnitude: two zeros, more complex arithmetic.
  • One's complement: requires end-around carry correction.
Design trade-off: two's complement sacrifices symmetric range for simpler circuitry and unified arithmetic, which is usually the right choice for general-purpose processors.
