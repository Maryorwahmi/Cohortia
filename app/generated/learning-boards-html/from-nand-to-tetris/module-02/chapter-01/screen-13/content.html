Hex boundaries
0xFFFF = 1111111111111111 → signed: -1, unsigned: 65535 0x8000 = 1000000000000000 → signed: -32768
When you see these patterns in logs or registers, treat them as boundary indicators and confirm the intended signedness before acting.
Overflow examples
8-bit signed: 01111111 (127) + 00000001 (1) = 10000000 (-128 interpreted signed) → signed overflow. Detect by checking carry into sign bit versus carry out.
