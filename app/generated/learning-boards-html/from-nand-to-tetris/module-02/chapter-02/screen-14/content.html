What to Remember
Half Adder
Sum = a XOR b; Carry = a AND b. No carry-in support.
Full Adder
Handles carry-in: Sum = a XOR b XOR c; Carry-out = (a&b) OR (c&(a XOR b)).
Ripple-Carry
Chain 16 full adders. Simple to build, but carry delay is linear in bit width.
Summary
Master these adder building blocks before moving on to the ALU; they determine correctness and timing for arithmetic instructions.
