What to Remember
Control Bits
zx,nx,zy,ny,f,no form preprocessing, function, and postprocessing stages for versatile behavior.
Functions
Add, subtract, bitwise AND/OR via combinations of control bits and the function stage.
Flags
zr computed by OR-ing and NOT-ing out bits; ng is the MSB. Both must reflect the final out after all steps.
Final takeaway
Implement the ALU in clear stages, test thoroughly, and rely on flags for correct CPU control flow. These components are the heart of the processor.
