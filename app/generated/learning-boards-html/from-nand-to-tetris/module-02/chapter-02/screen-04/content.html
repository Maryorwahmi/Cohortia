Half Adder
A single-bit adder producing a sum and a carry. It cannot accept a carry-in.
Boolean formulas
Sum   = a XOR b
Carry = a AND b
Truth table
abSumCarry
0000
0110
1010
1101
Concrete example
Add bits a=1 and b=1. XOR yields 0 for the sum, AND yields 1 as a carry. That carry is what a full adder would accept as carry-in for the next bit.
Common misconception
Confusing the sum with carry. The sum is not the arithmetic total when a carry exists; the carry represents the overflow that must travel to a higher bit.
Key Takeaway

The half adder gives you XOR and AND outputs; use these to compose larger adders that handle carry-in.
