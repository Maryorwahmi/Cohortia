Process Flow Full Adder from Half Adders
Step-by-step
  1. HalfAdder(a, b) → sum1, carry1
  2. HalfAdder(sum1, c) → sum, carry2
  3. carry-out = carry1 OR carry2
Worked example
a=1 b=1 c=0 → First half: sum1=0 carry1=1. Second half: sum=0 carry2=0. OR → carry-out=1.
Why reuse matters
Smaller verified blocks reduce engineering errors. If each half adder is correct, the composition is easier to trust and debug.
Common pitfall
Omitting the final OR step loses carry-out cases, causing wrong results when both lower bits produce carries.
Key Takeaway

Compose verified half adders and OR their carries to build a correct full adder with minimal new logic.
