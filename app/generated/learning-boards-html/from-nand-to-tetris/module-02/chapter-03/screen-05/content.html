What the clock does

It creates discrete clock cycles and synchronizes sampling. On the chosen edge, state elements update their outputs based on previously stable inputs, avoiding race conditions caused by arbitrary propagation order.

Concrete scenario
To write a register: apply address and data, assert load, wait for combinational logic to settle, then let the clock edge capture the data into the DFF.
Misconception
You cannot safely rely on disabling the clock signal to implement enable behavior at this HDL level; clock gating is a hardware optimization with timing pitfalls.
Quick checklist
  • Drive stable inputs before the sampling edge.
  • Allow combinational logic time to settle between edges.
  • Design control signals (load, inc, reset) to be synchronized with the clock.
Key Takeaway

Use the clock as the coordination point: preparations happen between edges; actual state change happens on the edge.
