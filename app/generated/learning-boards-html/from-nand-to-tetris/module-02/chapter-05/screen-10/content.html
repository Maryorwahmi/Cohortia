Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Hack vs Real Chips
Simplified Hack timing
The Hardware Simulator assumes combinational logic settles between clock cycles, so timing isn't modeled. This helps focus on architecture and correctness of logic.
Reality
Physical chips require that T ≥ t_cq + t_comb + t_setup and also consider clock skew and manufacturing variations.
Clock skew note
Clock arrival differences can reduce available setup margin at one flip-flop and increase hold risk at another. Real designs use clock trees and PLLs to minimize skew.
Practical point
Simulate for functionality, but do timing analysis or synthesis tools for physical implementation.
