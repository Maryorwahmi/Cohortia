Comparison
Why three commands are enough
Unconditional and conditional transfers express all control-flow graphs.
If-then / If-else
Conditional jump to one label, and an unconditional jump to skip the alternate branch.
While / For
A test that conditionally jumps to the end and a goto that returns to the start implement iteration.
Switch / Break / Continue
Lower to nested tests and jumps; break becomes a goto to the loop's end label, continue becomes a goto to the loop-start label.
Practical reflection
Compilers like the Jack compiler generate VM patterns that reflect these simple primitives. Your translator only needs to implement these primitives correctly and the rest of the language's constructs will be supported through the compiler's lowering steps.
Key Takeaway

Label, goto, and if-goto form a minimal, complete basis for control flow; reliable translation and label mangling lets you support any compiler-generated control structure.
