Three test programs

1. Simple addition
push constant 3
push constant 5
add
Expected: top of stack = 8.
2. Subtraction and negation
push constant 10
push constant 7
sub
neg
Expected: after sub top = 3; after neg top = -3.
3. Comparison
push constant 5
push constant 3
gt
push constant 5
push constant 5
eq
Expected: first comparison pushes -1 (true); second eq pushes -1 because 5 equals 5.
Verification steps
  1. Translate the .vm to .asm with your translator.
  2. Assemble and run in the Computer Simulator.
  3. Inspect RAM at the stack address and SP to confirm expected values.
Debug tips
Step through assembly and check SP after each command.
Verify each comparison label has a unique numeric suffix.
Confirm that boolean pushes use M=-1 for true and M=0 for false.
