Why widen fetch and decode?
To supply multiple instructions per cycle so several functional units can work in parallel and increase IPC.
Where the complexity appears
Dependency checking among simultaneously issued instructions and allocation of functional units becomes a critical path.
Real-world trade-offs
Area, power, and port pressure on the register file grow with issue width; practical IPC is usually below the theoretical maximum.
Key Takeaway

Superscalar widening can increase throughput, but the hardware to check dependencies and provide functional units often limits real IPC.
