Track tables
Instruction, functional unit, and register result tables are the scoreboard’s state.
Gate stages
Issue, Read, Execute, Write each has checks that may stall.
Remember renaming
Register renaming is the mechanism that Tomasulo uses to remove WAW and WAR stalls.
Final Thought

Scoreboarding is historically important: it shows how careful tracking and gating can improve throughput with modest hardware, and it motivates the renaming step that followed.
