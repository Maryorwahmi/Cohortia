Separate names & storage
Allocate a new physical register for each write so names do not collide.
Save old mappings
Use saved old RAT entries or checkpoints to restore precise state after squashes.
Balance PRF costs
Consider banking, clock gating, and hierarchy to trade area and power against latency and conflicts.
Final thought
Renaming is the enabler of out-of-order execution, but its hardware cost must be carefully managed with allocator and PRF design choices.
