Free-list allocator options
Queue and bitmap each have strengths and weaknesses.
Queue-based free list
Simple FIFO, easy to implement and to return registers at commit. Needs wider pop interface for multi-issue renames.
Bitmap allocator
Vector of free bits and priority logic to select multiple free entries. Supports parallel allocation but increases combinational area and power.
Checkpointing vs reconstruction
Checkpointing the free list enables fast recovery but consumes area. Reconstructing from in-use registers is slower but area-efficient.
Designers choose based on pipeline width, expected misprediction frequency, and area budget.
Key Takeaway

Choose allocators with your rename bandwidth and recovery latency in mind: queue simplicity vs bitmap parallelism.
