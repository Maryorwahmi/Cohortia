Simulation assumptions
Clear initial RAT and free list make the trace deterministic.
Initial mapping
R0→P0 (hardwired). RAT[R1]=P1 ... RAT[R7]=P7. Free list = P8..P15.
Instruction stream
ADD R1, R2, R3
MUL R4, R1, R5
SUB R1, R6, R7
AND R2, R1, R4
OR R3, R2, R1
ADD R5, R3, R4
Trace task
For each instruction: show RAT before and after, which physical register was allocated from the free list, and when an old physical register is freed at commit.
We will simulate the first three instructions in the next screens and then show the branch misprediction recovery scenario.
Why it matters

This hands-on trace makes the abstract rename flow concrete and reveals how free-list exhaustion and saved mappings affect correctness.
