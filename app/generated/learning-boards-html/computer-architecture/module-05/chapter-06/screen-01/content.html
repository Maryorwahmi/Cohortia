Limits of Instruction-Level Parallelism
Why dependencies and resource limits prevent wide sustained IPC
In this lesson

Identify dependency types that stop parallel execution, compute theoretical IPC from a sequence, and reason about the instruction window and real workloads.

Why it matters

Design choices such as issue width and window size have steep hardware costs. Understanding ILP limits explains why the industry shifted to multicore and accelerators.

Dependency Types
