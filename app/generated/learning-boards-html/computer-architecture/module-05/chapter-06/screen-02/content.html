Mnemonic
Think of ILP limits as three fences: data fences (true dependencies), decision gates (control), and name tangles (register reuse). Removing name tangles is cheap with renaming; the others are structural.
Quick check
Can you point to a RAW dependency in a short sequence? If so, that pair cannot be executed in parallel.
