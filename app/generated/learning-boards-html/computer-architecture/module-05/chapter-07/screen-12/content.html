Trade-off

Accuracy × Gain vs. Mispredict penalty

Accuracy
Higher accuracy raises expected gain but often at higher predictor complexity and power.
Recovery cost
Includes wasted cycles, refill latency, and lost speculative side-effects; lowering it improves profitability.
Performance gain
How much earlier the useful work completes when speculation is correct; only critical-path gains translate to overall speedup.
A practical design lesson: improving recovery cost or ensuring speculations affect the critical path can be more valuable than marginal accuracy improvements. Consider optimizations that reduce replay latency or forward data instead of solely improving the predictor.
Key takeaway
Treat accuracy, cost, and gain as a single optimization target — the best changes depend on workload and hardware constraints.
