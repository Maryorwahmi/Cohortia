Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Speculative Lifecycle
Process Flow

Fetch → Execute(speculative) → ROB store → Commit/Flush

1. Fetch & predict
Predict branch and fetch from predicted path.
2. Execute speculatively
Instructions execute out of order; results go to ROB entries.
3. Resolve & commit
If prediction correct, entries clear speculation and commit in order.
Concrete scenario
A misprediction causes flushing of ROB entries after the branch. The pipeline must discard speculative results and refill from the correct path, incurring cycles lost and possibly warming caches for the wrong data.
Misconception
Flushing seems cheap but includes refill and lost speculative side-effects such as prefetches, making recovery cost meaningful.
Key Takeaway
The lifecycle enforces correctness but also determines the recovery penalty on misprediction; architects design predictors and ROB behavior to minimize net loss.
