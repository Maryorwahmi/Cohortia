Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Key Concepts
Flush on misprediction
Speculative results after a wrong branch are discarded and the pipeline refilled from the correct path.
Trade-offs
Accuracy, recovery cost, and gain form the decision basis for speculation strategies.
Security & power limits
Speculation creates security risks and uses power; sometimes it must be curtailed despite performance benefits.
Final takeaway

Designers balance performance, security, and energy; speculation is a powerful tool but not an unconditional win for every design point.
