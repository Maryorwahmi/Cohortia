Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Limits of Speculation
Security
Speculative side-effects can leak sensitive information. Industry responses include hardware microcode updates, software mitigations, and new designs that separate speculative from architectural state.
Power
Speculation uses power even when wrong, which is costly for mobile processors where energy budget matters as much as peak throughput.
Diminishing returns
Beyond a point, additional speculation reduces accuracy and increases recovery cost, turning aggressive techniques into liabilities rather than assets.
Key takeaway
Architects must trade performance for security and power efficiency; a balanced approach often yields the best overall system design.
