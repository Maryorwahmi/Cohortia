What to Remember › Quick Reference

Three short concept cards

Cache & Boundaries
Instructions that span lines require multi-line fetch and alignment logic, not just wider reads.
Branch Effects
Taken branches and mispredictions reduce effective fetch IPC; accuracy and penalty matter.
Queues & Bypass
Instruction queues smooth timing; μop caches and loop detectors turn hot code into cheap fetches.
Summary takeaway
When analyzing fetch bandwidth, quantify branch frequency and predictor accuracy, consider misprediction penalty, and size buffers so the decode stage remains fed during common stalls.
