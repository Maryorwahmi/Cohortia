Estimate effective fetch throughput

Model derivation
Effective IPC = 1 / (1/4 + B + B*(1-A)*P)
where:
  B = branch frequency (taken branches per instruction)
  A = predictor accuracy
  P = misprediction penalty in cycles
Example: B=0.2, A=0.9, P=4
Effective IPC = 1 / (0.25 + 0.2 + 0.02*4) = 1 / 0.53 ≈ 1.89
This algebraic form shows why both low B and high A are important, and why P (the penalty) dramatically affects throughput when mispredictions occur.
Concrete example & question
Using the numeric example above, the effective fetch supply drops well below the ideal four instructions per cycle. If you change P to 8 cycles, recompute the IPC to see how misprediction penalties amplify the cost of only a small misprediction rate.
Key Takeaway

Expressing effective IPC with a small formula clarifies how branch frequency, accuracy, and penalty interact to determine fetch throughput.
