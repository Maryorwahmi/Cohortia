Common mistakes and pro tips for fetch reasoning

Three common mistakes
  1. Counting bytes instead of instructions when instructions cross boundaries.
  2. Ignoring the wasted cycle from correct redirects.
  3. Underestimating the impact of misprediction penalty on sustained bandwidth.
Pro tips
  • Model bandwidth with local windows sized to the instruction queue rather than global averages.
  • Use sensitivity analysis on misprediction penalty to prioritize predictor improvements or penalty reduction.
  • Favor μop caches and loop detectors for hot loops to convert repeated work into cheap fetches.
Key Takeaway

Model locally, size buffers for common stalls, and invest in prediction or bypass structures where they yield the most sustained throughput.
