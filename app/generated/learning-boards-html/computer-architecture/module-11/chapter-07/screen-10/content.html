Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Communication Overhead
Shared vs Distributed
Shared memory: cache coherence traffic and false sharing. Distributed memory: message latency and bandwidth.
Strategies
  • Minimize sharing and false sharing by padding or aligning data.
  • Batch messages and reduce message frequency and size.
  • Overlap communication with computation when possible.
Example scenario
A distributed matrix multiplication that exchanges halo regions on every iteration may pay a large communication cost. Reducing exchange frequency, compressing halo data, or increasing local computation per exchange are practical levers.
False sharing: small independent variables placed on the same cache line cause unnecessary coherence traffic.
Batching: combine small messages into larger, less frequent transfers to amortize latency.
Key Takeaway

Treat data movement as a first-class cost. Reducing or hiding communication often yields larger gains than micro-optimizing compute kernels.
