Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Key Concepts
Measure first
Raw timings and S/E curves drive decisions.
Watch the tail
Long-running workers indicate imbalance or serialization.
Profile deeply
Classify time into compute, memory, sync, and communication.
Pick the right law
Amdahl for fixed-size, Gustafson for scaled-size reasoning.
Reduce movement
Improve locality, batch messages, and avoid false sharing.
Validate changes
Always re-measure after an optimization to confirm real improvement.
Summary strip

Measure, pick the right model, diagnose with profiling, apply targeted mitigations, and re-measure to confirm scaling improvements.
