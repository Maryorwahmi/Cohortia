Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Synchronization Overhead
What it is
Time spent waiting for locks, barriers, or atomic operations instead of doing productive computation.
Symptoms
  • High time attributed to lock or barrier routines in the profiler.
  • Strong contention when multiple threads try to update shared state.
Mitigation strategies
Coarse-grained locking: reduce lock frequency by grouping updates.
Lock-free structures: use atomics or algorithms that eliminate global locks when safe.
Reduce barriers: avoid unnecessary global synchronization; prefer asynchronous progress where possible.
Overlap: schedule computation to occur while waiting for communication or other events.
Key Takeaway

Frequent synchronization serializes parallel work. Reduce frequency or change algorithmic structure to lessen contention and improve scaling.
