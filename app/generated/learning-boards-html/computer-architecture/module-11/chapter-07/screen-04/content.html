Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Speedup & Efficiency
Definitions
Speedup (S): ratio of sequential time to parallel time, S = T1 / Tp.
Efficiency (E): S divided by number of processors P, E = S / P.
Scalability: how well E is maintained as P increases.
Concrete example
T1 = 100s, T4 = 30s → S = 100 / 30 ≈ 3.33, E = 3.33 / 4 ≈ 0.83 (83%)
Common misconception
A speedup greater than one does not guarantee efficient scaling; check efficiency to see whether extra processors are well used.
Visual: measurement panel
Measured times
Record raw runtimes for 1, 2, 4, 8, ... processors. Then compute S and E for each point.
# Example calculation
T1 = 100.0  # seconds on 1 processor
T4 = 30.0   # seconds on 4 processors
S = T1 / T4
E = S / 4
# S ≈ 3.33, E ≈ 0.83
Key takeaway
Always compute both S and E and plot them to see where returns diminish.
