Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Key Concepts
Metrics
Speedup, efficiency, scalability — always compute and plot them.
Scaling laws
Use Amdahl for fixed-size limits, Gustafson when workload scales with P.
Diagnose
Profile to identify whether load imbalance, sync, communication, or memory bandwidth is dominant.
Bottom line

Measure, reason with the right model, and optimize the dominant bottleneck — then re-measure to confirm improvement.
