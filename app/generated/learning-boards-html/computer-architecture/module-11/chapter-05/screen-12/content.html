Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Sharer Overflow
Problem
Pointer list full
When P pointers are used and another cache requests a shared copy, the directory must fall back to a chosen strategy to maintain correctness while balancing traffic and storage.
Broadcast fallback: simplest, highest bandwidth cost.
Evict and invalidate a pointer: lowers sharing but adds miss risk.
Coarse group bits: compress information at the cost of precision.
Concrete example
P=4. P1,P2,P3,P4 share block 0. P5 requests Read. Options: broadcast to all, or invalidate one of P1..P4 to free a pointer. Each choice creates different future traffic.
Design trade-off
Choose the fallback that matches expected sharing locality: broadcast if overflows are rare, evict if sharing is transient, group bits if many caches share but storage is limited.
