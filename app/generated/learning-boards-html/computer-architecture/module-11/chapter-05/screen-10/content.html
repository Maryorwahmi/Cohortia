Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Directory Storage
Options
Sharer representations
  • Full bit vector: precise, O(N×M) storage.
  • Pointer lists: up to P pointers per entry, compact for low sharing.
  • Coarse group bits: one bit per group of caches to compress information.
Overflow handling
When pointer lists are full the directory can: 1) broadcast the request, 2) evict a sharer pointer and invalidate it, or 3) transition the block into a broadcast state. Each option trades increased bandwidth or future misses for storage reduction.
Design question
Choose P based on workload sharing distribution. Low P suits workloads with few sharers; high P or bitmaps help highly-shared data.
