Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Key Concepts
MESI States
Modified, Exclusive, Shared, Invalid describe ownership and cleanliness for cache lines.
Bus Actions
BusRd, BusRdX and BusUpgr coordinate data supply and invalidation between caches.
Optimizations
Silent evictions, cache-to-cache transfer, MESIF forwarder, and MOESI Owned reduce memory traffic in different ways.
Final summary

Analyze your workload's sharing pattern to choose the most effective protocol or optimization. Forwarding helps read-heavy sharing; Owned helps dirty-then-read sharing. Count bus events and writebacks to make decisions based on evidence.
