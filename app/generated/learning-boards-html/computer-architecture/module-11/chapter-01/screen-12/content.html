Definition

Big vs Little cores

Combine high-performance cores with power-efficient cores to run tasks in the right power/performance envelope.
Examples: ARM big.LITTLE, Intel P-cores and E-cores.
Benefit: power efficiency and flexible performance scaling.
Challenge: scheduler complexity and thread migration overhead.
Scheduler impact
OS schedulers use heuristics and hardware feedback to place threads. The goal is to match thread demand to core capability while minimizing costly migrations and cache misses.
Key takeaway
Heterogeneous designs trade scheduling complexity for energy efficiency; measure both power and performance when evaluating such processors.
