Activity: analyze a recent CPU

Extract specs, compute per-core bandwidth and cache per core, and compare to an older generation.

Step 1
Collect cores, L1/L2/L3 sizes, channels, and bandwidth.
Step 2
Compute per-core bandwidth = total bandwidth ÷ cores.
Step 3
Compute cache capacity per core and compare generations.
Deliverable
A short report with numbers, comparison, and a recommendation on whether to use more channels, HBM, or larger caches for the workload.
