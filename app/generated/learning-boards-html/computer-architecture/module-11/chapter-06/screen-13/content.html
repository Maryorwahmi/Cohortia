Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Practical rules
Rule 1
Identify publish-consume edges and apply release on publish and acquire on consume.
Rule 2
Use relaxed only for operations that do not require ordering, such as independent counters.
Rule 3
Prefer acquire/release over seq_cst when global ordering is not required; measure if unsure.
Rule 4
Test on target hardware: ARM may pay a larger penalty for fences than x86.
Rule 5
Avoid memory_order_consume due to portability and implementation difficulties.
Rule 6
When in doubt, write an acquire/release pair and prove correctness with a small test.
