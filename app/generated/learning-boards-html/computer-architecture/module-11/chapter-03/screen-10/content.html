Module 11 › Chapter 11.3 › SC Implementation
Hardware actions to enforce SC
Stall pipeline
Prevent issuing later memory ops until previous ones complete, avoiding reorder visibility to other cores.
Flush write buffers
Write buffered stores must be made globally visible before later operations proceed to preserve order.
Coherence traffic and invalidations
Stores often require broadcasting invalidations to other caches. Waiting for acknowledgements yields tens of cycles of latency per store in large systems.
Analogy: requiring every post office to confirm receipt of each letter before the next letter is sent keeps everyone perfectly synchronized but makes delivery slow. Relaxed models let local offices operate faster and only coordinate at lock or fence points.
Key takeaway
The global agreement requirement makes SC expensive; this motivates weaker models and programmer-visible fences.
