Relaxed consistency models
Processor Consistency
Stores and loads each preserve per-processor order, but loads can bypass earlier stores to different addresses. Good for many systems where store order matters but independent addresses can proceed.
Weak Ordering
Ordinary loads and stores can be reordered freely. Synchronization ops form ordering points. The programmer must place fences or locks to provide necessary ordering.
Release Consistency
Synchronization is split into acquire and release. Acquire prevents later ops from moving before it; release ensures prior ops complete before it becomes visible.
Example contrast
Dekker’s algorithm needs ordering between a store and a later load. Under weak ordering, without a fence the store may not be visible before the other thread's load, breaking mutual exclusion. Release consistency lets you place an acquire at entry and a release at exit to restore correctness without full fences around every memory op.
Common misconception
Relaxed means random. In fact, relaxations are specific allowed reorderings; correctness depends on using synchronization at the right points.
