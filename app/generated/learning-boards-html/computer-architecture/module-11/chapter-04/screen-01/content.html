Synchronization and Locking Mechanisms

Understand atomic operations and practical lock designs to build correct, scalable concurrent systems.
16:9 HD Classroom
Orientation

You will trace how hardware primitives map to software locks, evaluate spin versus block trade-offs, and learn cache-aware patterns like test-and-test-and-set, ticket locks, and MCS locks. Practical examples include timing a spinlock under contention and comparing throughput.

Explore Key Topics
Atomic Primitives
TAS, CAS, LL/SC are the building blocks for locks and lock-free code.
Cache-aware Locks
Techniques like test-and-test-and-set and MCS avoid cache line bouncing under contention.
Granularity & Performance
Choose coarse, fine, or lock-free approaches depending on contention and complexity trade-offs.

Why start here

Choosing the wrong locking approach causes wasted CPU, unpredictable latency, and poor scalability. This chapter equips you to reason about both correctness and performance when coordinating concurrent accesses.
