Module 11 › Chapter 11.4 › Spinlocks vs Mutexes
Spinlock
  • Busy-waits in a loop until the lock is free.
  • Low latency for short critical sections because no context switch.
  • Wastes CPU cycles and power if the wait is long.
Misconception

Spinlocks are not always faster; on long waits or heavy contention they degrade throughput.

Mutex
  • Blocks the waiting thread and yields CPU to the scheduler.
  • Efficient when the lock holds for longer durations.
  • Involves context-switch overhead and wake-up latency.
Pro Tip

For mixed workloads, consider adaptive locks that spin briefly then block if not acquired.

Scenario
Short critical section: spinlock is likely best.
Scenario
Long critical section or IO: use mutex to avoid wasting CPU.
Experiment
Measure acquisitions per second with different critical section lengths to choose appropriately.
