Module 11 › Chapter 11.4 › Ticket Locks
How it works
  1. Thread atomically fetches-and-increments a ticket counter.
  2. Thread spins until served counter equals its ticket.
  3. On release, the served counter increments to hand off the lock.
Why fairness helps

FIFO service prevents repeated collisions and improves predictability under heavy contention.

Example
Many threads arrive together: each gets a unique ticket and waits. Only one thread needs to observe the served counter at a time, which reduces simultaneous write attempts.
Trade-off

Ticket locks add bookkeeping but provide stable fairness and reduce repeated contention on the lock variable.
