Module 11 › Chapter 11.4 › Granularity & Lock-free
Coarse-grained
Single lock for a large structure. Simple, safe, but limits concurrency.
Fine-grained
Multiple locks protecting parts of a structure. Higher parallelism, more complexity and deadlock risk.
Lock-free
No locks; use CAS or LL/SC. Avoids locking bottlenecks but increases algorithmic and memory-management complexity.
Hands-on activity
Implement a TAS spinlock and measure one million acquisitions on one core, then with 2, 4, and 8 threads. Implement test-and-test-and-set and a ticket lock, compare throughput, and analyze cache line bouncing as contention rises.
