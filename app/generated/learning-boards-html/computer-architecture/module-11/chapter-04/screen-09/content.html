Module 11 › Chapter 11.4 › Cache-aware Locking
What is bouncing?

When a lock's cache line is repeatedly transferred between cores due to writes, latency and bus traffic increase dramatically.

Techniques to reduce
  • Test-and-test-and-set: spin on reads, issue TAS only when read sees free.
  • Backoff: randomized or exponential wait before retrying.
  • Ticket locks: assign tickets so only the next ticket holder needs to acquire.
Concrete scenario

In a tight TAS loop with many contending cores, the cache line migrates each time someone writes. Replace immediate TAS with a read-only spin loop and TAS only when the read reports zero. Add a small randomized pause when TAS fails repeatedly to reduce simultaneous retries.

Why it matters
Less coherence traffic means higher acquisition throughput and lower power use.
Misconception
A single improvement solves all contention — in reality you often combine techniques for best results.
