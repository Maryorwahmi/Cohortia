Module 11 › Chapter 11.4 › Learning Objectives
Apply & Verify
Example task
Write a TAS spinlock, run a million acquires/releases on one core, then repeat with multiple threads and compare throughput.
Measurement
Collect total time and compute acquisitions per second. Plot as threads increase to reveal contention effects.
Optimization
Add test-and-test-and-set and randomized backoff; measure improvements and annotate where coherence traffic decreased.
Reflection
Compare a simple spinlock, ticket lock, and MCS lock under high contention and report latency and throughput trade-offs.
