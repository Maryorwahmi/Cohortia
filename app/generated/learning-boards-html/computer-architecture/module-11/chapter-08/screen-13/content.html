Process Flow

Benchmarks → Architecture

SPEC rate shows throughput scaling with cores. HPCG highlights memory bandwidth. MLPerf reveals impact of accelerators like AMX and SVE.

HPCG
Memory-bound; favors EPYC and Neoverse with high bandwidth.
MLPerf
INT8 inference often benefits from Intel AMX; FP workloads depend on SVE or FP units.
Concrete application
For a distributed database, measure both throughput and tail latency. EPYC may lead throughput; Xeon may reduce latency for transaction-critical paths.
Key Takeaway
Match the benchmark to your bottleneck and read vendor architecture features to explain differences in scores.
Common mistake
Treating a single benchmark as a universal measure of performance rather than a workload-specific indicator.
