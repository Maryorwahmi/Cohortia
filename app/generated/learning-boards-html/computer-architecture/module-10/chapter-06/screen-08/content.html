General-Purpose vs Specialized

Use a five-dimension comparison to decide which suits your product.

Dimensions
  • Flexibility — CPU: high, Accelerator: low
  • Performance per watt — CPU: lower, Accelerator: higher
  • Programmability — CPU: easy, Accelerator: harder
  • Cost & Time to market — vary with volume
Concrete Example
For a high-volume training cluster, TPUs can provide better throughput per watt than general GPUs. For a phone, an integrated NPU improves battery life and latency for inference compared to sending data to the cloud.
Key Takeaway

Choose specialization when workload is stable and volume justifies the engineering cost; otherwise prefer general-purpose or reconfigurable options.

Misconception check
Specialized hardware is not inherently cheaper in low volume. Consider amortizing design costs, toolchain maturity, and expected kernel stability before committing.
