Parallel Architecture Trends

Explore heterogeneous computing, domain-specific accelerators, and chiplets that make modern SoCs efficient and modular.

16:9 HD Classroom
Heterogeneous Computing

Mix CPUs, GPUs, NPUs, and accelerators to match each workload to the best engine for latency, throughput, and power.

Chiplets & Packaging

Use smaller dies connected in a package for better yield, flexible product SKUs, and node-optimized manufacturing.

Domain Accelerators

Specialized hardware such as TPUs, NPUs, and video encoders deliver superior performance per watt for narrow tasks.

Future Trends

3D stacking, photonics, and neuromorphic ideas will further shift where computation happens and how components interact.
