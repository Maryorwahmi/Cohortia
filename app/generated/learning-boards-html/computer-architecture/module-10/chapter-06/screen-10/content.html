Timeline

Monolithic → Chiplets → Heterogeneous Packaging

Key stages and motivations behind the industry shift.

Monolithic Dies
Everything on one die; high on-die bandwidth but lower yield as die area grows.
Chiplets & Tiles
Smaller dies assembled into packages to improve yield and enable node mixing.
Heterogeneous SoCs
Accelerators and specialized blocks integrated to reduce data movement and power for common tasks.
Examples
  • AMD: CCD + IOD chiplets
  • Intel: Meteor Lake tiles
  • Apple: M1 Ultra UltraFusion
Future directions
3D stacking, photonics and neuromorphic designs aim to further reduce communication costs and enable new workloads.
Key Takeaway

Packaging innovations often unlock more practical gains than raw transistor scaling; design trade-offs determine the best path forward.
