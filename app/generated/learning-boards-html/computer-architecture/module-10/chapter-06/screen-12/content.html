Future Trends

3D Stacking, Photonics, Neuromorphic

Emerging approaches that change communication and computation trade-offs.

3D Stacking
Vertical dies reduce link length and increase bandwidth for memory-heavy workloads.
Photonics
Optical links can provide high bandwidth at lower power for certain interconnects across packages.
Neuromorphic
Architectures inspired by brains that may excel at sparse, event-driven workloads.
Practical note
Each approach has software and packaging challenges. Adoption depends on demonstrated system-level gains and ecosystem maturity.
Key Takeaway

Watch for technologies that reduce the cost of moving data; these can change the balance between specialization and integration.
