Turn objectives into practice

Concrete exercises and heuristics to verify each competency.

Exercise: System Categorization

List CPU, GPU, NPU, and accelerators present in one product and explain workload mapping in two sentences.

Milestone: short diagram + 3-sentence justification
Exercise: Accelerator Mapping

Choose an AI workload and explain whether a TPU, GPU or NPU is optimal, focusing on throughput, latency, and power.

Milestone: table comparing two options
Exercise: Chiplet Sketch

Draw a modular package with compute chiplets and an I/O die and note which node each chiplet would use and why.

Milestone: annotated sketch
Exercise: Trade-off Defense

Write a 150–250 word justification for choosing specialization or a general-purpose design for a product.

Milestone: written rationale
These exercises map directly to the verified competency milestones and prepare you for design conversations with engineers and product managers.
