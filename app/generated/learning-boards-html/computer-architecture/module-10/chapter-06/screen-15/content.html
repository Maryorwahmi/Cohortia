Quick Reference Cards

Three one-line concept cards to recall quickly.

Heterogeneity: assign each workload to the engine that minimizes energy and latency.
Chiplets: smaller dies improve yield and flexibility but require careful interconnect design.
Trade-offs: weigh flexibility, programmability, and costs versus raw efficiency per watt.
Closing question
Which of these three ideas most changes how you would design a new product: heterogeneity, chiplets, or trade-offs? Reflect briefly and note one implication.
