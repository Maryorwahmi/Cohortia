Key Concepts

Three compact cards to remember the chapter’s essentials.

Heterogeneous Computing
Use CPUs, GPUs, and accelerators together to match hardware to workload demands for efficiency.
Chiplets & Packaging
Split large dies into smaller chiplets to improve yield, cost, and product flexibility.
Trade-offs
Weigh flexibility, performance per watt, programmability, cost, and time to market when choosing hardware.
Summary
Architectural choices hinge on workload characteristics and economics; specialized accelerators and modular packaging are tools to optimize those trade-offs.
