Learning Objectives

Build practical skills for evaluating heterogeneous systems and chiplet designs.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Describe trends

Explain heterogeneous computing, chiplets, and accelerators and why they replace monolithic CPU scaling.

Mnemonic: Toolbox (different tools for different jobs)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify accelerators

Name NPUs, TPUs, DSPs, FPGAs, and ASICs and match them to typical workloads like inference, video, and crypto.

Mnemonic: Swiss Army → Drill → Screwdriver
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain chiplets

Describe modular dies, yield advantages, node mixing, and examples like AMD CCD/IOD and Apple UltraFusion.

Mnemonic: Modular cake — smaller perfect pieces
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Analyze trade-offs

Compare flexibility, performance per watt, programmability, and cost between CPUs and accelerators for given workloads.

Mnemonic: Scale vs. Fit — generalist vs. specialist
