Process Flow

From partition to integration

Five main stages show where yield and cost are affected.

1. Partition
Split SoC into chiplets and choose node per function.
2. Fabricate
Manufacture chiplets separately; higher yield per chiplet area.
3. Test
Identify good chiplets before packaging to avoid costly wasted packages.
4. Package
Assemble using interposers or die-to-die interconnects; watch latency and power.
5. Integrate
Place package in system and validate thermal and board-level behavior.
Concrete scenario
A vendor makes compute chiplets on 5nm for speed and an I/O die on 7nm to cut costs. Each good compute chiplet is tested and paired with a passing I/O die, improving effective yield against a single large 5nm die.
Key Takeaway

Manufacturing and packaging choices determine the economic advantage of chiplets; interconnect design determines performance overhead.
