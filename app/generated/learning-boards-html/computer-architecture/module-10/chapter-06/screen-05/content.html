Comparison

Accelerator Types

Compare function, example products, and typical uses.

TPU
Matrix ops, high throughput training and inference. Example: Google TPU v4 for large models.
NPU
On-device inference, low-power. Example: Apple Neural Engine.
DSP
Streaming transforms for audio/video. Often built into SoCs.
FPGA / ASIC
Reconfigurable vs fixed; FPGAs for iteration, ASICs for production efficiency.
Guiding points
  • Performance per watt often improves when removing general-purpose circuitry.
  • Programmability cost rises as specialization increases; toolchains vary widely.
  • Time to market matters: FPGAs shorten iteration; ASICs minimize unit cost at scale.
Concrete example
Data centers may deploy GPUs for mixed workloads and TPUs for large-scale model training because TPUs deliver higher throughput for tensor math while GPUs remain versatile for other tasks.
Key Takeaway

Match hardware to workload stability and development timelines to choose the right accelerator.
