Design Exercise

Research a recent chiplet-enabled processor or heterogeneous SoC and produce a short analysis and diagram.

Step 1: Component list
Identify CPU chiplets, GPU, NPU, memory, and I/O tiles; note process nodes if available.
Step 2: Block diagram
Sketch connections and label likely high-bandwidth paths and bottlenecks.
Step 3: Defense
Explain why the vendor chose chiplets/accelerators and recommend one improvement.
Deliverable
A simple annotated diagram plus a two-paragraph justification that covers trade-offs and one suggested optimization.
Key Takeaway

Practical analysis builds intuition about when chiplets and accelerators are worth the added complexity.
