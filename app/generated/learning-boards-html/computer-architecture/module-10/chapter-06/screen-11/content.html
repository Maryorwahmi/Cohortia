Interconnects and Data Movement

Communication cost often dominates runtime and energy when components are modularized.

On-die vs Package vs Off-package
  • On-die: lowest latency, highest bandwidth.
  • Package (interposer/EMIB): higher latency than on-die, lower than off-package.
  • Off-package: highest latency and energy cost.
Mitigations
  • Local scratchpads for accelerators to reduce repeated transfers.
  • High-bandwidth memory channels and stacked dies for limited hot data.
  • Efficient coherence and cache strategies to reduce unnecessary traffic.
Concrete example
AMD uses an internal fabric to move cache lines between CCDs and the I/O die. This fabric is optimized for speed but still creates extra hops compared to a single monolithic cache plane.
Key Takeaway

Treat interconnect design as central: minimizing hot-path data movement often yields larger gains than raw compute improvements.
