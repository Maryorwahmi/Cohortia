Module 11 › Chapter 10.6 › Chiplets
Definition

Chiplets and Modular Packaging

Small dies combined in a package to improve yield and flexibility.

Yield
Smaller dies have higher manufacturing yield than a single large die.
Node mixing
Different chiplets can be manufactured on different process nodes to balance cost and performance.
Scalability
Add compute chiplets to scale core counts without redesigning all functions.
Examples
  • AMD Zen CCD + IOD
  • Intel Meteor Lake tiles (compute, graphics, I/O)
  • Apple M1 Ultra using UltraFusion to join two dies
Trade-offs
  • Pro: Higher yield and lower average cost per good die.
  • Con: Inter-chiplet latency and packaging complexity increase design challenges.
  • Con: Interconnect power and area may offset some gains.
Key Takeaway

Chiplets trade manufacturing risk for interconnect complexity — they are a practical way to scale cores and mix process nodes.
