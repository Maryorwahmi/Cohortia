Summary Key Concepts
Thread-level parallelism
Run independent threads to overlap stalls and raise throughput.
Multithreading styles
CGMT, FGMT, and SMT trade latency hiding, implementation cost, and contention differently.
SMT trade-offs
High throughput per area, but shared resources can cause contention and side channels.
Final takeaway

Match multithreading and scheduling to your workload: balance throughput, per-thread performance, and security.
