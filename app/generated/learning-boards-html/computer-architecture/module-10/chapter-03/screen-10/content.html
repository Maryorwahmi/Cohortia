Comparison Intel Hyper-Threading (two-way SMT)
What Hyper-Threading does
Each physical core exposes two logical processors; threads share caches, predictors, and execution units.
Typical benefit
10–30% performance improvement in many multithreaded workloads.
When Hyper-Threading helps
  • I/O-bound or mixed workloads where one thread stalls and the other uses idle units.
  • Throughput-oriented server tasks with many lightweight threads.
When to be cautious
Two compute-bound threads sharing a core can harm per-thread performance due to cache and unit contention.
Key Takeaway

Hyper-Threading is a pragmatic SMT that often improves throughput; validate with benchmarks and schedule threads to avoid contention.
