Thread-level parallelism

Analogy
A chef serving multiple customers uses idle time to prepare another order instead of waiting.
Quick fact
SMT can often give 10–30% speedup on multithreaded workloads; two-way SMT can approach 70–90% of two physical cores' throughput while adding only 5–10% area.
