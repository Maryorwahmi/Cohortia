Comparison SMT vs additional cores (area & throughput)
SMT benefit
Two-way SMT can approach 70–90% of the throughput of two separate cores while adding only 5–10% area.
When to choose SMT
Throughput-oriented servers with many threads and limited chip area.
When to prefer more cores
Latency-sensitive or isolation-critical workloads that cannot tolerate resource contention between threads.
Concrete example
If adding a second physical core doubles area and power, SMT delivers a large fraction of that throughput for far less silicon. That makes SMT attractive in cost-constrained designs.
Key Takeaway

SMT is an efficient way to raise throughput per area, but it is not a replacement for more cores when isolation or peak per-thread performance matters.
