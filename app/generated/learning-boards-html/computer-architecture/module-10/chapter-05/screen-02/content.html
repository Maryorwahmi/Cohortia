Learning Objectives

Learn the skills you will demonstrate after studying vectorization.

01
Explain Auto-vectorization
Describe how compilers convert scalar loops into SIMD and why it improves throughput.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify Blocking Conditions
Recognize loop-carried dependencies, aliasing, control flow, and alignment issues that prevent vectorization.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use Compiler Hints
Apply __restrict__, assume_aligned, pragma omp simd, or ivdep to guide vectorization safely.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Analyze Performance
Compare SIMD width, memory bandwidth, alignment costs, and remainder loops when evaluating speedup.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
