Alignment matters
Vector loads do best when data are aligned to vector-width boundaries; misalignment can cause extra memory operations and lower throughput.
Memory bandwidth must supply enough data per cycle to fill vector lanes. If not, the vector unit stalls and theoretical speedups are unreachable.
Optimization strategies: align allocations, use contiguous arrays, and employ blocking or prefetching when working with large data sets.
Measurement checklist
  • Check alignment of arrays (16-, 32-, or 64-byte boundaries).
  • Measure memory bandwidth utilization to ensure lanes are fed.
  • Test aligned vs unaligned versions to evaluate overhead.
Key Takeaway: Without aligned, high-bandwidth memory, wide SIMD lanes cannot deliver their full potential.
