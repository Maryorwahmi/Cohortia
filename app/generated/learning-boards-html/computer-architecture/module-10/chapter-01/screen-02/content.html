Learning Objectives
Learn the concepts and why they matter
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain SIMD vs MIMD
You will articulate how one-instruction, many-data differs from multiple-instruction designs and why SIMD reduces control overhead for regular loops.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Analyze SIMD trade-offs
You will reason about width, vector length, masking, alignment, and memory bandwidth when estimating speedup.

04
Compare SIMD implementations
You will compare SSE/AVX, NEON, SVE and vector processors to understand portability and scalability trade-offs.
