Comparison

OpenMP scheduling policies

Static
Evenly divides iterations; minimal overhead; best for uniform work.
Dynamic
Threads grab work as they finish; good for irregular work but higher overhead.
Guided
Starts with large chunks and reduces size; hybrid approach for many cases.
Concrete scenario
Processing a set of tasks where early tasks take much longer than later ones: guided scheduling hands large chunks first then smaller ones, reducing imbalance while keeping overhead moderate.
Pro tip
Experiment with chunk size. For dynamic and guided schedules, tune chunk size to trade off overhead and balance.
Key Takeaway
Select scheduling by workload variability and verify with measurements.
