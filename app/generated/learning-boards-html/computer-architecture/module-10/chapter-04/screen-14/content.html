Key Concepts — Part 1

A compact collection of three essential ideas.

Shared-memory
Threads share an address space; synchronization prevents races but can limit scalability.
OpenMP directives
Use parallel, for, reduction, critical, and barrier to express parallel regions and synchronize safely.
Fork-join
Master forks workers at parallel regions and rejoins them at the end; region granularity matters for overhead.
Summary Takeaway

Use the shared-memory model and OpenMP for convenient multicore parallelism, but plan synchronization and region sizes to preserve correctness and performance.
