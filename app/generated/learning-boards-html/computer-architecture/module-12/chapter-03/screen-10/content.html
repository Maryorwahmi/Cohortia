Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Optimizing Compute-Bound Workloads
Primary levers
  • Enable and verify vectorization (SIMD).
  • Use fused multiply-add (FMA) where possible to double effective ops per instruction.
  • Increase instruction-level parallelism and reduce data dependencies.
  • Consider hand-tuned kernels or intrinsics if the compiler cannot reach the peak.
Diagnosing gaps
If intensity > ridge but achieved GFLOPS ≪ peak, inspect compiler reports, assembly, and performance counters to find vectorization or pipeline limits.
Common pitfall
Assuming parallel threads automatically increase per-core throughput. Per-core vector utilization matters more for compute-bound kernels.
