Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Drawing the Roofline
Step 1 — Peak compute
Use vendor numbers or a compute-heavy benchmark. Record in GFLOPS for floating arithmetic.
Step 2 — Memory bandwidth
Use STREAM-like measurements or published GB/s. Use sustainable bandwidth for your workload.
Step 3 — Compute ridge
Ridge = peak_compute / bandwidth. Units are FLOPs per byte. Example: 100 GFLOPS / 25 GB/s = 4 FLOPs/byte.
Step 4 — Plot kernels
Compute operational intensity for each kernel and find predicted performance with performance = min(bandwidth × intensity, peak_compute).
