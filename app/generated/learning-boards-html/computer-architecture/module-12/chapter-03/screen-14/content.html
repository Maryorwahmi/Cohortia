Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Pitfalls and Misconceptions
Frequent errors
  • Mixing units or forgetting to convert element size to bytes.
  • Ignoring writes and temporary traffic in byte counts.
  • Assuming theoretical peak equals measured peak without tuning.
  • Adding cores without addressing memory bandwidth limits.
Subtle observation
A kernel can be memory-bound overall but limited by cache-level bandwidth; sub-rooflines help isolate that case.
Best practice
Always record assumptions, measure where possible, and compare predicted performance to measured numbers to refine your model.
Warning
Misapplied roofline can lead to wasted optimization effort. Confirm with measurements before large refactors.
