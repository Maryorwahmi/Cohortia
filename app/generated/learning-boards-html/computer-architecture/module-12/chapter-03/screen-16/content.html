Sub-rooflines

Optimization mapping
Move right to increase reuse (blocking), move up to increase compute use (vectorize, FMA).
Measurement discipline
Record precision, reads vs writes, and use sustained bandwidth and achieved GFLOPS for accurate plots.
Final takeaway
