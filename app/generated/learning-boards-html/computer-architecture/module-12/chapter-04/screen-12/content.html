Compute and compare
Given:
Mode A: 3.0 GHz, 1.2 V
Mode B: 1.5 GHz, 0.8 V
Power ratio = (1.2² × 3.0) / (0.8² × 1.5) = 4.5
Time ratio = 0.5 (fast mode finishes twice as fast)
Energy ratio = 4.5 × 0.5 = 2.25
Conclusion: Mode B uses less energy for this CPU-bound task. Always add leakage and other terms for a full evaluation.
