Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Race vs Pace
Numeric example
Assume:
Mode A: 3.0 GHz, 1.2 V
Mode B: 1.5 GHz, 0.8 V
Power ratio = (1.2² × 3.0) / (0.8² × 1.5) = 4.5
Time ratio = 0.5 (fast mode finishes twice as fast)
Energy ratio = 4.5 × 0.5 = 2.25 → fast mode uses 2.25× more energy
When to choose which
  • Race-to-halt: good for short, CPU-bound tasks and when idle power is low.
  • Pace-to-halt: good for energy-critical tasks or where voltage reductions give large savings.
Check your understanding
Given a latency constraint, compute both energy and deadline adherence to decide the correct strategy; don't assume lower power always wins.
