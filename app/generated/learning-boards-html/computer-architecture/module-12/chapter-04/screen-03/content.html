Skills & Tools
From measurement setup to governor behavior and plotting, these skills make the theory actionable.
Measure
Capture frequency, voltage, and power under idle and workload conditions.
Plot
Create power vs frequency and performance vs power graphs to reveal efficient operating points.
Interpret
Relate measurements to DVFS policies and thermal constraints; identify measurement artifacts.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Run the hands-on activity and justify the chosen operating point for two workload types.
