Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Hands-on Activity
Step-by-step workflow
  1. Record idle baseline (freq, voltage, power).
  2. Run representative workloads and record sustained metrics.
  3. Compute energy = power × time for each task.
  4. Force frequency states, repeat, and plot results.
Practical tips
  • Control background tasks and keep ambient temperature stable.
  • Use sufficiently long runs to reveal sustained power and throttling behavior.
  • Plot power vs frequency and performance vs power to find efficient operating points.
Recommended tools mentioned in the chapter: platform power meters or OS utilities. Ensure you know how to read voltage, frequency, and power from your chosen tool.
