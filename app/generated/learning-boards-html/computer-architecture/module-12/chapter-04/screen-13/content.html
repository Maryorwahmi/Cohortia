Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Power vs Energy Decision
Decision checklist
  • Identify whether the workload is CPU or I/O bound.
  • Measure energy per task and sustained power under representative loads.
  • Compare against battery limits or TDP and cooling capacity.
  • Choose race-to-halt or pace-to-halt based on energy, latency, and thermal constraints.
Reflect: For a Mars rover with limited solar energy, would you target minimum energy per task or lowest instantaneous power? The right answer depends on mission constraints and duty cycles.
