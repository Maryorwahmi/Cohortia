Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Key Concepts
Power vs Energy
Rate versus total cost; use the right metric for the problem.
V² Dominance
Dynamic power scales with V² × f, so voltage is the most powerful lever.
Mitigation techniques
DVFS, power gating, body biasing, and multi-threshold design address dynamic and static components.
Summary

Combine theoretical estimates from the CMOS equations with careful measurements to choose optimizations that match your workload and platform constraints.
