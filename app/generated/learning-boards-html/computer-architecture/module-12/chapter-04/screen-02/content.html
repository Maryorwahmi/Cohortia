Learning Objectives
Build calculation skill, conceptual clarity, and evaluation techniques for power-aware design.
01
Calculate CMOS power
Use the CMOS equations to estimate dynamic and static components from activity, C, V, and f. Practice with numeric examples to gain intuition.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Distinguish dynamic vs static
Explain why dynamic scales with V² and f while leakage is roughly V×Ileakage and grows at small nodes; know mitigation techniques.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Understand the power wall
Relate TDP, thermal throttling, and cooling constraints to sustainable performance and system design decisions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Analyze energy efficiency
Compute performance per watt, compare race-to-halt and pace-to-halt strategies, and choose the right trade-off for your workload.
