DVFS policy examples
  • Performance: always max frequency.
  • Powersave: always minimum frequency.
  • Ondemand: scale up on high utilization.
  • Conservative: scale slowly to avoid oscillation.
Common pitfall
Rapid DVFS transitions can waste energy; tune governor thresholds and avoid thrashing for best results.
Visual: DVFS effect on power
Because P_dynamic ∝ V² × f and f roughly ∝ V in the operating region, reducing voltage by half can reduce dynamic power by several times when frequency scales down with voltage. Use governors to schedule those states responsibly.
Key Takeaway

DVFS is powerful for dynamic savings but must be combined with good policies and awareness of leakage to be effective in modern nodes.
