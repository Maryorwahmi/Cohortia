Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Power vs. Energy
Definitions and formulas
P = dE/dt
E = P × t

P: watts (rate)
E: joules (total)
Why it matters: energy per task determines battery life or electricity cost; power determines cooling and thermal safety. Both drive design decisions.
Concrete example
If a CPU at high power finishes a task in half the time, compute energy by multiplying power by runtime to compare alternatives.
Common misconception
Lower steady-state power isn't always lower energy. Measure energy per task to decide.
Key Takeaway

Use power for thermal planning and energy for task cost; compute energy as power times time for apples-to-apples comparisons.
