Dynamic power expression
P_dynamic = α × C × V² × f
Alpha is activity factor; C is switched capacitance; V is supply voltage; f is clock frequency. Use numeric estimates to compare CPU modes.
Static power expression
P_static = V × I_leakage
I_leakage grows with smaller process nodes and with increased temperature; it is present even when logic is idle.
Concrete example
Estimate dynamic power by choosing alpha (e.g., 0.1), C (in farads scaled to logic), V and f. Even coarse numbers reveal whether voltage or capacitance reduction is most effective.
