What TDP means
TDP is the sustained heat dissipation budget a cooling solution must handle. It guides heatsink and system design.
Systems may allow short power spikes above TDP, but continuous operation above TDP typically triggers thermal actions.
Thermal throttling
When thermal limits are reached, the processor reduces voltage or frequency to lower power and temperature, preserving hardware integrity.
Design note: ensure representative benchmarks to estimate sustained power; otherwise throttling surprises users in production.
Practical check
Measure sustained power with a long-running benchmark and compare to TDP. If sustained power exceeds TDP, expect throttling and plan cooling or software limits accordingly.
