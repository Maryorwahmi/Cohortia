Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Quantitative Models
Definition

Iron Law, AMAT, and 3C model

Show step-by-step calculations that convert cache and pipeline choices into CPI and throughput projections using representative workload numbers.

  • Iron Law: Performance ∝ Instruction count × CPI × clock period. Use it to compare alternatives.
  • AMAT: L1_hit_time + L1_miss_rate × (L2_hit_time + L2_miss_rate × DRAM_penalty).
  • 3C model: Classify misses as compulsory, capacity, or conflict to motivate cache changes.
Concrete walkthrough
Compute memory CPI = memory_accesses_per_instruction × AMAT. Use measured or estimated access rates from your workload to produce a numeric CPI term and add it to base CPI to get projected CPI.
Takeaway: Present calculations with assumptions and units so reviewers can reproduce your numbers.
