Learning Objectives

Skills to demonstrate and milestones to verify

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Design a target microarchitecture
Produce a 2-page spec describing ISA, pipeline depth, issue width, and cache hierarchy. This milestone ensures you can translate domain requirements into concrete design parameters.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Quantitative trade-offs
Calculate trade-offs between pipeline depth, cache sizes, associativity and branch prediction. You will learn to prioritize changes with measurable impact on CPI or energy per instruction.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Evaluate with models
Use the Iron Law, AMAT, Amdahl's Law and roofline model to project performance and identify bottlenecks. The mnemonic is: measure, model, modify, re-measure.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Present and defend
Prepare diagrams, projections, and a short presentation. Be ready to explain trade-offs and feasibility, and to show sensitivity analyses.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
