Capstone Project: Design and Analysis of a Modern Processor

Design a microarchitecture for a selected domain, produce quantitative justifications, and project realistic performance within area and power constraints.

In this lesson
Choose domain, pick ISA, design pipeline, caches, and predictors, then analyze with Iron Law, Amdahl, AMAT, and roofline models.
Deliverables
Architecture spec, quantitative analysis, area & power estimates, performance projection, diagrams, and a short presentation.
