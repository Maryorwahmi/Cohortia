System trade-offs
Design is balancing power, area, and performance under domain constraints.
Quantitative models
Iron Law, AMAT, Amdahl, and roofline are essential for justification.
Feasibility & presentation
Clear diagrams, explicit assumptions, and sensitivity analysis make a defendable design.
Summary takeaway

Ground your design in measurable targets, show step-by-step calculations, and present sensitivity results so your architecture is both realistic and persuasive.
