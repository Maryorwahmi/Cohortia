Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Assessment Focus

Skills to verify

Deliver correct calculations, realistic estimates, and a clear narrative linking assumptions to conclusions.

Architecture
ISA choice and microarchitecture must match the target workload and constraints.
Quantitative Analysis
AMAT, CPI breakdowns, Amdahl's Law, and roofline justification are required.
Feasibility
Area and power estimates must be reasonable and compared to existing processors in the domain.
Tip: Put assumptions in a single table so reviewers can check your math quickly.
Milestone: one-page summary and five-minute presentation defend the core trade-offs.
