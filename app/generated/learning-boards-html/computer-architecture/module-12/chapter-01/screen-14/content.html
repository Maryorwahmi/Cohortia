Key Concepts
Three compact reminders to carry forward.
Router Components
Input buffers, route computation, switch allocator and crossbar define per-hop behavior.
Virtual Channels
Multiple logical buffers per link prevent deadlock and reduce head-of-line blocking, at area cost.
Topology Trade-offs
Mesh scales throughput; ring is simple for small N; crossbar suits small clusters; hierarchical mixes local/global needs.
Takeaway

Use the chapter’s formulas and reasoning to balance latency, throughput, area and power when choosing router microarchitecture and topology.
