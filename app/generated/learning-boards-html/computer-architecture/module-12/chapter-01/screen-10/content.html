Flow Control: Credit-Based and On/Off
Prevent buffer overflow and regulate link usage.
Credit-based flow control
Sender tracks available buffer slots at receiver. A flit is sent only if sender has credit. Precise and efficient under high utilization.
Good for
High-performance NoCs and per-VC accounting to avoid interference across flows.
On/off flow control
Receiver signals sender to stop or continue when buffer fullness crosses a threshold. Simple but less efficient under sustained load.
Good for
Simple designs with low traffic or where control complexity must be minimal.
Example scenario
If VC0 is full and uses credit-based flow control, only VC0's sender stalls; other VCs with credits continue. With on/off, the whole link may pause, reducing pipeline utilization.
