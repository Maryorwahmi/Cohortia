NoC Power Management Techniques
Reduce energy while preserving performance when possible.
Clock gating
Gate clocks to router logic when idle to cut dynamic power with minimal state impact.
DVFS
Scale voltage and frequency to match traffic demand; saves dynamic and some static power at lower performance modes.
Power gating
Power off unused routers or clusters to eliminate leakage, but manage state retention and wakeup latency.
Design considerations
  • Balance energy saved with wakeup costs and added control logic.
  • Set gating thresholds using real traffic traces to avoid frequent transitions.
  • Plan for flow-control and state preservation when gating routers or ports.
