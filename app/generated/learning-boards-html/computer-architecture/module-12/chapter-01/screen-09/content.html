Latency, Throughput, Power and Area Trade-offs
Make trade-offs explicit with simple formulas and counts.
Latency
Depends on hops and router pipeline cycles: latency ≈ hops × (router cycles + link cycles). Example: mesh average hops ≈ O(√N).
Throughput
Driven by bisection bandwidth. Mesh offers O(√N) links across the bisection, ring offers constant bandwidth.
Power & Area
Grow with number of routers, buffer entries and link lengths. More VCs increase buffer area and dynamic power.
Design checklist
  • Estimate average hops for your topology and N.
  • Count links across the bisection to approximate throughput scaling.
  • Compute total buffer bits to estimate area and power impact.
