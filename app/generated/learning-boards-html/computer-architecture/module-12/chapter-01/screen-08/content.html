NoC Topologies: Ring, Mesh, Crossbar, Hierarchical
Choose a layout based on scale and traffic patterns.
Ring
Simple, low router count and low area for small systems. Limited bisection bandwidth makes it unsuitable for manycore throughput needs.
Mesh
Regular layout, good scalability, multiple paths and improved bisection bandwidth. Average hop count grows with the square root of N in an N-node mesh arrangement.
Crossbar
Full bandwidth within a small cluster; area and wiring explode with core count so crossbars are used for small groups, not whole-chip interconnects.
Hierarchical
Combine local rings or crossbars with a global mesh to balance local latency and global throughput. Useful when workloads show locality clustered in small groups.
Design lens
Ask: what is the expected core count and communication pattern? If local traffic dominates, a hierarchical or clustered design can yield low latency and scalable bandwidth. If many-to-many traffic dominates, prefer mesh-like structures.
