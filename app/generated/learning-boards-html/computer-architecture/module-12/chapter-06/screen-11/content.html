Surrogate models

Train a neural network or Gaussian process on a limited set of simulation results to predict IPC, power, or other metrics for untested configurations. Surrogates are fast to evaluate and enable broad searches.

Best practice
  • Use a diverse training set that covers the intended search region.
  • Hold out validation points and use them to estimate model error before trusting predictions.
Bayesian optimization & RL

Bayesian optimization uses uncertainty to pick informative samples; reinforcement learning frames search as sequential decision-making and can learn policies that find good designs quickly.

Warning
Models extrapolate poorly outside the training distribution. Validate candidate predictions with detailed simulations before committing to design changes.
