Design Space Exploration
Systematic evaluation of architectural configurations to meet constraints

We will use simulation, statistical sampling and machine learning to narrow millions of possible processor designs into a small set of practical candidates. Expect concrete examples using cache and issue-width variants and a Gem5 hands-on activity later in the lesson.

In this lesson
Scope of the design space and common parameters such as pipeline depth, cache size and ROB entries.
Simulation, sampling, and machine learning strategies that reduce runtime and guide decisions.
A hands-on Gem5 exercise to compare cache and issue-width variants and measure IPC changes.
