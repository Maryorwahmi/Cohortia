Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Architectural Simulators
Detailed simulators
Gem5
Detailed modeling for x86, ARM, RISC-V; supports full-system and syscall-emulation modes. Use for final validation and when microarchitectural detail matters.
Marss-x86
Detailed x86 simulation derived from PTLSim; useful for x86-specific studies.
Faster, approximate simulators
Sniper
Interval modeling for much faster exploration with acceptable trend accuracy.
ZSim
Parallel simulation techniques to scale exploration; good for large sweeps.
SimpleScalar
An earlier simulator useful for MIPS-like baseline studies and pedagogical experiments.
Practical tip
Plan your exploration stages: fast simulators for wide scans, detailed simulators for final validation and corner cases.
