Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Quick Reminders
1. Start focused
Collect cycles, instructions, and one cache counter first.
2. Use ratios
Compute CPI/IPC and miss rates to interpret raw counts.
3. Verify
Change one variable at a time and re-measure to confirm gains.
Final thought
A small, repeatable set of counters plus the Top-Down Method gives a high signal-to-noise path to real optimizations.
