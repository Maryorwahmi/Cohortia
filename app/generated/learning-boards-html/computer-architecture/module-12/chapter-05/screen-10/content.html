Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Speculation & Retiring
Bad Speculation
Wasted work from mispredictions or incorrect memory speculation.
Indicators
High BR_MISP_RETIRED, high fraction of pipeline assigned to bad speculation.
Fixes
Improve branch predictability, reduce unpredictable branches, modify data structures or algorithm to avoid mispredicted patterns.
Retiring
Instructions that complete and contribute to program output.
Interpretation
High retiring is good. If IPC is low despite high retiring, consider algorithmic or instruction-efficiency improvements.
