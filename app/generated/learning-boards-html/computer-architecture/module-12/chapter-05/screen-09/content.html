Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Back-End Bound
Memory-bound
Stalls caused by cache misses or insufficient bandwidth.
Indicators
High LLC_MISSES relative to LLC_REFERENCES, high L2 miss rates, long memory stall cycles.
Fixes
Cache blocking (tiling), loop interchange, prefetching, reduce working set, choose smaller data types.
Core-bound
Stalls caused by execution resource limitations or low ILP.
Indicators
Low IPC with low cache-miss rates, high port utilization or uops contention counters.
Fixes
Loop unrolling, instruction scheduling, reduce dependency chains, use vectorization to increase ILP.
