Amdahl's Law and Speedup Analysis
Measure, compute, and prioritize: learn the arithmetic of speedup and the principles that make caches and parallelism effective.

In this chapter we treat architecture as a quantitative discipline. Use the Amdahl formula to calculate how improving a subsystem affects whole-program runtime. Pair that calculation with locality and common-case reasoning to choose where to invest transistor budget for the best return.
