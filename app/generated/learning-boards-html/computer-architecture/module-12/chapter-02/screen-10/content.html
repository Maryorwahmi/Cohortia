Module 12›Chapter 12.2›Parallelism Levels
Instruction-level parallelism (ILP)
Find independent instructions within a thread. Effective for short critical paths but limited by dependencies.
Data-level parallelism (DLP)
Apply operations across many data items using vector units or SIMD. Excellent for regular data-parallel workloads.
Thread-level parallelism (TLP)
Use multiple threads/cores. Gains depend on software parallelism and synchronization overhead.
Request-level parallelism (RLP)
Independent requests handled concurrently in servers; scales well when requests are independent and resources permit.
Key Takeaway

Match the level of parallelism to workload characteristics and calculate the serial fraction to estimate realistic speedup limits.
