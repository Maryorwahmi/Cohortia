Module 12›Chapter 12.2›Key Concepts
Amdahl's Law
Speedup limited by fraction affected; compute before optimizing.
Principle of locality
Temporal and spatial locality justify caches and cache-line fetching.
Common-case-fast
Optimize frequent operations for average-case gains.
Takeaway
Measure first, compute expected gains with Amdahl, and use locality and cost metrics to make balanced architectural choices.
