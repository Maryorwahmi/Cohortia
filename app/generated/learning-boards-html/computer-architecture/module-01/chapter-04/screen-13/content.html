Design checklist
A prioritized sequence to choose policies and mitigations.
1. Characterize workload
CPU-bound, I/O-bound, latency-sensitive, or batch affects policy choice.
2. Measure leakage
High idle leakage favors race-to-halt with deep sleep; low leakage reduces the advantage.
3. Select mitigations
Combine DVFS with selective power gating and threshold tuning where beneficial.
Practical profile example
Use ondemand for bursting interactive work, conservative for mixed workloads to avoid oscillation, and powersave for long non-interactive batch jobs where latency is not critical.
Key Takeaway
No single solution fits all cases; synthesis of measurement, architecture, and thermal engineering yields the best results.
