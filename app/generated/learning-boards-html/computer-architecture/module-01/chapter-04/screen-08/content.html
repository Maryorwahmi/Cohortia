Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Race vs Pace
Compare strategies
Assess energy per task, idle power, and wake latency before choosing.
Race-to-halt (sprinter)
Run fast, finish early, and enter deep sleep. Best for CPU-bound tasks with low idle leakage.
Pace-to-halt (marathon)
Run at a lower frequency to meet deadlines while keeping power low. Best for tasks sensitive to latency or with high leakage penalties.
Decision checklist
  • Is the task CPU-bound? If yes, race-to-halt often reduces energy.
  • Is idle leakage significant? If yes, racing benefits grow if deep idle reduces leakage.
  • Is latency critical? If yes, racing may be preferred despite higher instantaneous power.
Key Takeaway
There is no universal winner; analyze workload type, idle power, and timing constraints to choose the right strategy.
