What to Remember
Three compact concept cards for quick recall.
Energy per task
Integrate power over runtime to compare operating points.
Voltage squared
Because of V², small voltage drops yield large power savings.
Control leakage
Use gating and threshold techniques where leakage dominates.
Final reflection

For each workload, ask: does racing fast then sleeping or pacing at low voltage minimize energy per task? Measure to answer that question.
