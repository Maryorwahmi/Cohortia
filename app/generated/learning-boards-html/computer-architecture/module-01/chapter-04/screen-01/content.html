Power vs Energy
Rate versus total — the guide to design trade-offs

Power is watts, the rate of energy use. Energy is joules, the accumulation over time. This difference explains why fast execution can reduce energy even when power is high, and why slow, low-power operation may burn more energy when deadlines push runtime long.
