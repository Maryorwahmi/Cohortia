Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Energy Example
Numeric walkthrough
Compare energy for two operating points using the dynamic power relation.
P1 ∝ V1² × f1 = 1.2² × 3.0 = 1.44 × 3.0 = 4.32
P2 ∝ V2² × f2 = 0.8² × 1.5 = 0.64 × 1.5 = 0.96
Power ratio = 4.32 / 0.96 = 4.5
If time ratio is 0.5 (fast mode finishes in half the time):
Energy ratio = 4.5 × 0.5 = 2.25
=> Slower mode uses 2.25× less energy per task.
Interpretation
For CPU-bound tasks where runtime scales with frequency, lower voltage and frequency can be more energy-efficient despite longer runtime because P scales with V² and f.
Key Takeaway
Always combine power equations with runtime estimates to compute energy per task for policy decisions.
