Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Measurement Plan
Hands-on plan
A sequence to collect reproducible power and energy data.
1. Select workloads
Idle, browsing, video, and CPU benchmark.
2. Record
Log instantaneous power, voltage, and frequency at steady sampling.
3. Compute
Energy per task = integral(power) over task duration; normalize by work done.
Analysis checklist
  • Repeat runs and average to reduce noise.
  • Force different frequencies to measure DVFS effects.
  • Plot power vs frequency and performance vs power to find operating sweet spots.
Key Takeaway
Empirical measurements reveal the actual energy trade-offs of DVFS and strategy choices for your platform and workload.
