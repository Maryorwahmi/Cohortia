Thermal design and limits
TDP sets a sustained power target for cooling and platform design, not peak bursts.
  • Cooling systems are sized to dissipate TDP under typical sustained workloads.
  • Processors throttle frequency or voltage when temperatures exceed safe limits to prevent damage.
  • Sustained high-power operation may reduce long-term reliability if thermal design is inadequate.
Practical example
A laptop with a 15 W TDP will not maintain a 45 W CPU peak. Under sustained heavy load, firmware reduces operating points to keep average power near the TDP and avoid overheating.
Key Takeaway
TDP anchors system design choices; expect reduced performance when workloads exceed the cooling budget over time.
