DVFS step-by-step
A practical control loop for adapting performance and power.
1. Monitor
Read utilization, voltage, frequency, and temperature to assess demand.
2. Decide
Governor or hardware policy chooses a target operating point based on goals.
3. Transition
Adjust frequency and request matching voltage from regulators, observing safe timing.
4. Verify
Confirm stability and monitor effects; rollback if errors or overshoot occur.
Governor examples
  • Performance: max frequency, best latency, worst energy.
  • Powersave: min frequency, best energy in long tasks but poor latency.
  • Ondemand: immediate scaling under load.
  • Conservative: gradual scaling to avoid oscillation.
Key Takeaway
DVFS must balance rapid responsiveness against transition overhead and energy goals; choose governors based on workload characteristics.
