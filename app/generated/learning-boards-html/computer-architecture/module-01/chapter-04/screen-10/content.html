What drives α and C?
Alpha and capacitance reflect workload toggles and physical transistor/wire properties.
Reduce alpha: clock gating, optimize software to avoid unnecessary state changes.
Reduce C: smaller transistors where safe, shorter wires, and layout optimizations.
Concrete example
A microarchitecture that widens the datapath may increase toggling and wire length, raising both alpha and C. The net effect can negate voltage-based savings if not addressed.
Key Takeaway
Optimization must consider alpha and C; reducing V and f alone can be insufficient if activity or capacitance increase.
