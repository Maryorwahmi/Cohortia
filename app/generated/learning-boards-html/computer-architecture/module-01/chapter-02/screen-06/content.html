Formal decomposition
CPI = CPI_base + CPI_stalls
CPI_stalls = CPI_branch + CPI_load + CPI_structural + ...
Measure each component by estimating the fraction of instructions in a class, the misprediction or miss rate, and the penalty in cycles. Multiply and sum to get the stall contribution.
Branch penalty
Contribution = fraction_of_branches × mispredict_rate × penalty_cycles
Load penalty
Contribution = fraction_of_loads × miss_rate × memory_penalty
Worked example
CPI_base = 1.0
Branches = 20% of instr, mispredict = 5%, penalty = 10 cycles
CPI_branch = 0.20 * 0.05 * 10 = 0.1
Loads = 25% of instr, miss = 4%, penalty = 20 cycles
CPI_load = 0.25 * 0.04 * 20 = 0.2
CPI_total = 1.0 + 0.1 + 0.2 = 1.3
Misconception: Not all stalls are permanent; some are amortized over instruction windows. Attribution still helps prioritize fixes.
