Popular suites
  • SPEC CPU: CPU-bound integer and floating workloads.
  • Geekbench: Cross-platform single- and multi-core tests.
  • MLPerf: Machine learning training and inference measurement.
  • STREAM: Memory bandwidth tests.
  • CoreMark: Embedded CPU benchmark.
Practical tip
Run the benchmark that matches your dominant workload and verify configuration details before trusting scores.
Interpreting scores
Benchmark scores are comparisons to references or normalized baselines. Look for single-core vs multi-core differences and whether the workload is memory- or CPU-bound. Vendors may optimize for a specific suite; real-world performance can diverge.
