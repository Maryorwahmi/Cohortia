CPI decomposition
CPI = CPI_base + CPI_stalls, where stalls include branch, load, and structural penalties. Decomposing helps prioritize fixes.
Measure IC reliably
Use hardware performance counters or instruction-set simulators with matched inputs and warm-up runs.
Interpret Benchmarks
Choose SPEC for CPU-bound workloads, MLPerf for machine learning, and STREAM for bandwidth.
Mnemonic
Think I-C-P: Instructions, Cycles per instruction, Period. Multiply to get time.
