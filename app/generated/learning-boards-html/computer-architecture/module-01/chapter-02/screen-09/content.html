Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Pipeline Depth
Why depth matters
  • More stages = shorter work per stage → higher possible frequency.
  • More stages = longer flushes and larger window for hazards → higher CPI penalties.
  • Mitigations like prediction and forwarding help but add complexity and power cost.
Design question
Is the workload branch- or memory-dominated? That answer guides whether deeper pipelines will help.
Scenario
Processor A doubles pipeline stages and raises frequency by 50% but sees CPI increase by 30%. Use the Iron Law to compute net effect for your workload. This is the canonical trade-off architects analyze numerically.
Pro tip: Pair pipeline depth changes with improved predictors to reduce the CPI impact of mispredictions.
