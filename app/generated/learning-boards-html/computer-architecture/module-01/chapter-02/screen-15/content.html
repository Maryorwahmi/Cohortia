Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Iron Law
Execution Time = IC × CPI × Cycle Time. Use consistent units and measure each term.
CPI Decomposition
CPI = CPI_base + CPI_stalls. Attribute stalls to branch, load, and structural causes.
Benchmarks & Measurement
Use SPEC, MLPerf, STREAM as appropriate and follow strict measurement methodology for reproducible results.
Summary Takeaway

Always compute execution time with IC, CPI, and cycle time, decompose causes of stalls, and choose benchmarks that reflect your real workloads.
