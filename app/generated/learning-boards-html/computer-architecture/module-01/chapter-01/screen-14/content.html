ISA is the contract
Defines instructions, registers, and memory model visible to software.
Microarchitecture
Pipelines, caches, and execution engines that determine CPI and throughput.
Logic & Technology
Transistors and layout determine achievable clock, area, and power characteristics.
Takeaway

Use the Iron Law and the five metrics to evaluate designs. Distinguish ISA from implementation when comparing processors.
