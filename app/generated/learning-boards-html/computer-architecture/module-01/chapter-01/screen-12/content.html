Hands-on Activity

Compare Three Processors

Collect structured specs and explain microarchitectural trade-offs in a one-page analysis.

Required table fields: ISA version, microarchitecture name, cores, clock, node, in/out-of-order, caches, TDP.
Analysis tips: map features to CPI, power, and area; compute Iron Law products when possible.
Deliverable: a filled table and a one-page analysis explaining how microarchitectures differ and why those differences make sense for their target market.
Key Takeaway

Practice reading specs and mapping them to stack-level consequences; this skill is central to architectural reasoning.
