Definition

Logic and Circuit Design

Turning microarchitectural structures into gates, cells, and layout while balancing timing, power, and area.

Process node
Smaller nodes can reduce cycle time but change leakage and design cost.
Circuit trade-offs
Faster arithmetic units may consume more area and power; cache array layout impacts access latency.
Practical consequences
  • Fabrication node affects maximum clock and thermal limits.
  • Designers often retarget microarchitecture to better suit a node's strengths.
  • Area reductions lower cost per chip and enable more cores or larger caches.
Warning

Assuming smaller nodes automatically solve thermal issues. Power density and leakage must be managed with design choices.

Reflect: When looking at a processor spec, always check both microarchitecture and process node to understand the published frequency and power numbers.
