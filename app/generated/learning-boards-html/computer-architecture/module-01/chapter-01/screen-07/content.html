Implementation Stack

ISA → Microarchitecture → Logic. Use this chain to reason about how a change at one level affects the rest.
ISA change
May require new decoders and affect instruction count.
Microarchitecture trade
Adding out-of-order logic reduces CPI but increases area and power.
Circuit impact
Larger structures change die area and timing closure complexity.
Case study

Imagine adding a complex addressing mode to an ISA to reduce instruction count. Microarchitecture must add decode and address-generation logic, which uses area and can lengthen critical paths. The logic team must then re-evaluate timing and might increase pipeline depth to meet frequency targets. Each change ripples across the stack.
