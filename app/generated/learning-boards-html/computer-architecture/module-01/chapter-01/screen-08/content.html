Core Concept

Key Evaluation Metrics

Five primary metrics guide design choices and trade-offs.

Performance
IPC, CPI, frequency
Power
Watts, energy per instruction
Area
Die mm²
Cost
Design and fab cost
Reliability
Fault tolerance
Practical note

Identify which metrics your product must prioritize, then evaluate microarchitectural changes against those priorities with measured workloads.

Common trade-off

Lowering CPI by adding hardware often increases area and power; shrinking area by removing features can increase CPI or reduce reliability.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize metrics for your product, measure effects with representative workloads, and reason about cross-level trade-offs before design changes.
