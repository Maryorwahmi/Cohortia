Trade-offs: Frequency, CPI, Pipeline Depth

Deeper pipelines can increase max frequency but may worsen CPI through larger misprediction costs.

Benefit: higher clock improves cycle time
Cost: deeper pipelines increase branch penalty and potential CPI
Worked estimate

If cycle time improves by 10% but CPI worsens by 15%, compute the net product. If the product increases, performance dropped. This quantitative habit avoids misleading conclusions based solely on frequency.

Check your understanding

Estimate the net performance change when CPI increases by 20% and cycle time reduces by 15%.
