Geometric mean
Reduces sensitivity to outliers and reflects multiplicative improvements across benchmarks.
Profile essentials
Collect instructions, cycles, L1/L2/L3 misses, and branch mispredictions to compute CPI and miss rates.
Avoid benchmarketing
Require documented methodology and reproducible base-mode runs to ensure results reflect real-world performance.
Final takeaway

Critical measurement plus clear methodology prevents misleading claims and leads to architectures tuned for real workloads.
