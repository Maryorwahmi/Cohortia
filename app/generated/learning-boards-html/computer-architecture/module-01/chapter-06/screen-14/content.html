SPEC CPU & geometric mean
Real applications and geometric mean aggregation give robust, comparable CPU scores.
Workload metrics
Instruction mix, ILP, cache misses, and branch behavior determine architectural trade-offs.
Benchmark selection
Use synthetic kernels for isolation, suites for representativeness, and traces for controlled study.
Summary takeaway

Measure carefully, choose the right benchmarks, and demand reproducible methodology to ensure benchmark results guide correct architectural decisions.
