Branch frequency & predictability
Collect branch counts and misprediction rates to measure pipeline penalties and guide predictor design.
Metrics to collect
  • Branch frequency (branches per instruction)
  • Branch misprediction rate
  • Refill penalty in cycles
Concrete example
A loop with a well-predicted exit is cheap to handle. A data-dependent indirect branch that jumps across many targets causes higher misprediction rates and large pipeline cost.
Common mistake

Assuming a single predictor improves all workloads. Predictor design must be matched to measured branch behavior and misprediction cost.

Key Takeaway

Measure branch frequency and misprediction cost to decide predictor complexity or software refactoring to reduce branches.
