Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Key Concepts
Amdahl's Law
Speedup limited by fraction affected; compute overall and max gains.
Principle of Locality
Temporal and spatial reuse justify caches and lines.
Common-case & Cost
Make frequent operations fast and evaluate improvements per dollar or watt.
Summary takeaway
Measure first, compute expected returns with Amdahl, exploit locality for caches, and choose changes that maximize performance per cost or power.
