Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Cost-Performance
Metrics
Performance per dollar = measured speedup / incremental cost. Performance per watt similarly measures return under power constraints.
Example: 10% faster at 50% higher cost → improvement per dollar is low; compute both absolute gain and normalized gain.
Decision factors
  • Target market willingness to pay for speed
  • Power constraints and thermal envelopes
  • Manufacturability and yield impacts of added complexity
Practical exercise
Compute speedup for two options with Amdahl's Law, then divide by cost delta to rank them by performance per dollar. Discuss which aligns with product goals.
Key Takeaway
Speed alone is not the full story—normalize gains by cost or power to decide on practical, market-aligned designs.
