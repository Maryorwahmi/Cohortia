Hands-on Activity
Create an annotated timeline of processor metrics
Collect transistor count, node, frequency, and TDP; plot and annotate major events.
Steps
  1. Gather metrics for the suggested processors and years.
  2. Plot transistor count on a log scale and overlay frequency and TDP.
  3. Annotate where Dennard scaling ends, multicore appears, and heterogeneity begins.
  4. Write a short analysis linking annotations to architectural changes.
Concrete guidance

Use log scale for counts, align time horizontally, and prefer normalized axes when comparing frequency versus transistor counts. Highlight TDP changes as a separate band.

Common mistake

Plotting raw numbers on mismatched scales makes trends unclear. Normalize or use multiple aligned axes.

Key Takeaway

Quantitative timelines reveal when transistor growth outpaced frequency and justify architectural shifts.
