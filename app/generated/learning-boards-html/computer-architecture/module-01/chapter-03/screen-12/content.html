Definition
Reliability risks increase with scaling
Key risks
  • Process variation affects timing and yield across the die.
  • Soft errors cause occasional bit flips in memories.
  • Aging mechanisms change transistor behavior over time.
  • Thermal stress accelerates failures in hot spots.
Mitigations
  • Error-correcting codes and parity for memory.
  • Redundancy and graceful degradation for critical units.
  • Adaptive voltage/frequency scaling and thermal management.
Concrete example

Large caches often include ECC to correct single-bit errors, protecting correctness without large performance cost.

Key Takeaway

Designers must include architectural and system-level techniques to maintain reliability as device dimensions shrink.
