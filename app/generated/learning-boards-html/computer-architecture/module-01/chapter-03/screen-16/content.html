Key Concepts — Part 1
Three short cards to lock the terms.
Moore's Law
Transistor count doubles roughly every two years; an economic observation.
Dennard scaling
Power density stayed constant as transistors shrank, enabling higher frequencies until leakage rose.
Power wall
Leakage and thermal limits that stopped safe frequency scaling.
Summary
These three concepts explain why hardware progress moved from frequency increases to multicore, heterogeneity, and accelerators.
