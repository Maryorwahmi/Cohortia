Timeline
Costs rise as nodes advance, shaping industry structure
Economic pattern
  • Cost per transistor falls initially with scaling.
  • Capital expenditures for fabs rise substantially at leading-edge nodes.
  • Only a few companies can fund the most advanced fabs, causing consolidation.
Practical consequence

Architects must factor foundry choice into cost, yield, and time-to-market trade-offs; some designs favor mature nodes for lower cost and higher reliability.
