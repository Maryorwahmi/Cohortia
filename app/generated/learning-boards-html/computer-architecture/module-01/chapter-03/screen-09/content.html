Comparison Heterogeneous computing
Big cores, little cores, and accelerators
B
Big core
High single-thread performance, higher power per core.
L
Little core
Lower performance but much better energy efficiency for light tasks.
Scheduler role

The OS or runtime must assign threads to the appropriate core type to reap efficiency benefits.

Concrete example

A phone uses little cores for background sync and big cores for interactive rendering. This balances battery life and responsiveness.

Trade-offs
  • Pros: improved energy efficiency and tailored performance.
  • Cons: more complex scheduling, verification, and software support.
Key Takeaway

Heterogeneous designs help manage dark silicon by matching work to the most efficient hardware unit.
