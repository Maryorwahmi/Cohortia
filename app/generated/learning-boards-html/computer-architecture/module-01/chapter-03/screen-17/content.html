Key Concepts — Part 2
Architectural and industry impacts to keep front of mind.
Dark silicon
Not all transistors can be active simultaneously; power gating is required.
Heterogeneous & accelerators
Mixing core types and accelerators increases energy-efficient throughput for specific tasks.
Cost & reliability
Fab costs and reliability challenges constrain node choices and design trade-offs.
Final summary
Turning transistor budgets into usable performance requires managing power, cost, and reliability through architecture, software, and manufacturing choices.
