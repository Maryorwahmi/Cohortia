Architecture Specialized accelerators
Hardware tuned for specific tasks yields better energy efficiency
Why they help
  • Datapaths and memory tailored to the computation reduce wasted work.
  • Lower-precision arithmetic and specialized interconnects cut energy per operation.
  • Offloading reduces contention on general-purpose cores.
Concrete example

An NPU performing neural inference can run many multiply-accumulate operations using less energy than the same operations on a CPU by using optimized dataflow and lower precision.

Cost trade-offs

Accelerators consume area and require verification and software support. They are justified when their workload share and energy savings offset these costs.

Misconception

Thinking accelerators simply 'speed up' tasks ignores the need for integration, drivers, and the cost of silicon area reserved for rarely used features.

Key Takeaway

Specialization trades area and complexity for large gains in energy efficiency for domain-specific workloads.
