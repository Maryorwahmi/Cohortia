PLA: AND plane + OR plane
Program product terms in the AND plane and map them to outputs in the OR plane using crosspoints.
Design steps
  1. List all product terms required by the truth table.
  2. Program the AND plane to produce those terms.
  3. Connect product terms to control outputs via the OR plane crosspoints.
When to pick a PLA
Choose PLA for small, dense control functions where regularity and verification simplicity outweigh slightly higher power or area for sparse logic.
Key Takeaway

A PLA is a structured, programmable realization of sums-of-products ideal for compact control logic in RISC processors.
