Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Speed vs Flexibility
Comparison at a glance
Hardwired control
Very low-latency outputs, efficient for stable, small ISAs; harder to extend.
Microcoded control
Flexible and easier to implement complex instructions, but adds microcode cycles and can be slower per instruction.
Design questions
  • How often will the ISA change?
  • Is single-instruction latency critical?
  • How many instruction types and addressing modes must be supported?
Reflection
Prefer hardwired for small stable ISAs and microcode when instruction complexity or growth is expected.
