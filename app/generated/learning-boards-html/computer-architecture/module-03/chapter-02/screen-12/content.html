Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Gate Delay Calculation
Estimate the critical path
Assume 1 ns per gate. Count gate levels from opcode to final control signal.
Worked approximation
Decoder: 2 ns, AND plane: 1 ns, OR plane: 1 ns, buffer/inverter: 1 ns → total ≈ 5 ns for a worst-case signal.
Practical tips
  • Factor expressions to reduce gate count and depth.
  • Balance large ORs into trees to avoid single long-delay gates.
  • Include inverters and buffering when summing delays.
Common mistake
Ignoring gate fan-in or physical implementation details leads to optimistic delay estimates. Always add margin.
