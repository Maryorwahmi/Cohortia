Hands-on checklist

  1. Design the single-cycle datapath and label control signals for the RISC subset.
  2. Compute critical path delays and maximum clock frequency (example total = 8.5 ns → ~117.6 MHz).
  3. Redesign as multi-cycle with 2 ns clock, count cycles per instruction, and compute execution times for a short sequence.
Assessment idea
Compare which processor is faster for a load and which is faster for an ADD, and explain why. Show arithmetic and control reasoning.
Solution hint
Single-cycle 10 ns vs multi-cycle 2 ns × 5 cycles → same for one load. For simple instructions, multi-cycle often yields lower total time because fewer cycles × short clock < long single-cycle clock.
