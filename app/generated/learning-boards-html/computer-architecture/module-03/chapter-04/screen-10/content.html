Decode vs μop width
Decode width: x86 instructions decoded/cycle. μop width: μops generated/cycle. Average μops per instruction determines effective μop supply.
If decode width = 4 and average μops/instr = 1.5, μop supply ≈ 6 μops/cycle.
If expansion exceeds μop width, the engine starves or front-end becomes bottleneck.
Fusion
Macro-op fusion combines two x86 instructions into one μop at decode time. Micro-op fusion merges two μops for execution efficiency. Both reduce μop pressure.
