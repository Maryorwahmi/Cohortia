x86 front-end
Fetch, length decode, decode, μop generation, queue.
Microcode ROM
Implements very complex instructions as μop sequences.
Decode vs μop bandwidth
Decode width, μop width, μop expansion, and fusion determine throughput.
Summary takeaway

Think in μops: they are the unit that the engine consumes. The front-end’s job is to supply μops efficiently while balancing complexity, area, and power.
