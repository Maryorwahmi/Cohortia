Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Trace Cache Comparison
Three approaches
Trace cache: store decoded μops for traces; pros: bypass decode; cons: area and complexity
μop cache: compact decoded μops stored for reuse; more predictable and lighter-weight
Fast decode: invest in parallel decoders and larger instruction cache, avoid storing μops
Example and takeaway
Example: a tight loop seen frequently can be served faster from a μop store. However, maintaining that storage and correctness across many paths uses die area and energy. Intel’s later choice favored larger instruction caches and faster decoders over a large trace cache.
