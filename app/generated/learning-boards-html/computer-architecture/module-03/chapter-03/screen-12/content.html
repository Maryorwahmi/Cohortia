Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Control unit
Generates control signals that drive the datapath; microcode stores these signals as micro-instructions.
Trade-offs
Microcode gives flexibility and patchability; hardwired control gives raw speed. Choose based on frequency and complexity.
Hybrid approach
Modern CPUs hardwire the hot path and microcode the complex or patchable behaviors like exceptions and system management.
Summary
Microcode is a strategic tool: it simplifies control logic and enables post-fabrication updates, but you must quantify its cycle cost and limit its use on performance-critical paths.
