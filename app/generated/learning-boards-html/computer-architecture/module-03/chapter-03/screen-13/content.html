Micro-instruction fields
ALU op, register enables, memory controls, multiplexer selects, next address and condition flags.
Vertical vs Horizontal
Narrow & compact versus wide & parallel; choose based on storage and parallelism needs.
Hybrid design
Hardwire the common path; microcode the complex, rare, or patchable cases.
Reflection
Which instructions in your target ISA would you hardwire and which would you microcode? Use frequency, complexity, and the need for updates to decide.
