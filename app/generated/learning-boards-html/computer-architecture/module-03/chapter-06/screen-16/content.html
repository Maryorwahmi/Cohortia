Module 4 › Chapter 3.6 › Quick Reference
Execution formula
Always compute clock × CPI × IC when comparing designs.
Area & power order
Single-cycle highest; multi-cycle lowest; pipelined moderate area, higher power than multi-cycle.
Minimize control delay
Use hardwired control, pre-decoding, or μop caches to keep control off the critical path.
Final takeaway
Quantify performance with execution time, and balance area and power constraints when choosing control implementations.
