Control Unit Performance and Trade-offs

Quantitative comparison of single-cycle, multi-cycle, and pipelined control. Formulas, examples, and design trade-offs you can compute and discuss.

In this lesson
Clock period formulas • CPI calculations • Area & power trade-offs
Single-cycle
One instruction per cycle. Clock = sum of all stage delays.
Multi-cycle
Instructions take multiple cycles; functional units are shared.
Pipelined
Multiple instructions overlap; CPI ≈ 1 but overhead and stalls apply.

Quick formulas
T_sc = sum(stage delays) • T_mc = max(stage delay) • T_pipe = T_mc + overhead
Why this matters
Small control delays avoid making the control unit the bottleneck on the critical path.
