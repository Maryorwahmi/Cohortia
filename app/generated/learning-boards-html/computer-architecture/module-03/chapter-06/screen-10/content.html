Comparison

Area and power across designs

Single-cycle
Highest area and power: every stage's hardware is present and active every cycle.
Multi-cycle
Lowest area and power: sharing and selective activation reduce silicon and switching.
Pipelined
Moderate area with higher power than multi-cycle due to registers, forwarding, and hazard logic.
Design rule

Choose multi-cycle for minimum silicon and energy, pipelining for throughput where area and energy budgets allow.

Scenario cards
Embedded sensor
Area and power constrained → multi-cycle often preferred.
High-performance server
Throughput dominates → pipelined or out-of-order despite larger area/power.
Teaching lab CPU
Single-cycle may be used for simplicity and clarity despite inefficiency.
