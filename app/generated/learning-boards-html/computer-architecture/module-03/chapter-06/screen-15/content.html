Module 4 › Chapter 3.6 › Key Concepts
Single-cycle
CPI = 1.0; T_sc is the sum of stage delays; high area and power.
Multi-cycle
T_mc = max(stage); area and power are lower; CPI depends on mix.
Pipelined
T_pipe = T_mc + overhead; CPI≈1 but stalls raise it; high throughput.
Summary takeaway
Compare designs using execution time and include area/power constraints; minimize control delay with hardwired or cached control for best clock periods.
