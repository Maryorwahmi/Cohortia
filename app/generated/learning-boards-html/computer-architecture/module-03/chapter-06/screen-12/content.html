Clock gating and power gating

Control units use gating to save energy. Clock gating blocks clock edges to idle registers; power gating turns off entire blocks to reduce leakage.

Clock gating
Fine-grained, reduces dynamic switching. Requires safe control sequences to avoid missing clocks.
Power gating
Coarse-grained, reduces leakage. Adds wake-up latency and state retention challenges.
Practical guideline
Use clock gating broadly; use power gating where long idle periods justify wake latency and extra control logic.
Example scenario
A control unit gates the ALU clock when executing a sequence of loads; this avoids toggling ALU registers and saves dynamic power while the ALU is unused.
