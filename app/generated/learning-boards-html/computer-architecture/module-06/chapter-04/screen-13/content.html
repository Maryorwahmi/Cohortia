Assessment: PRF vs ROB-only

Explain why PRF designs typically yield better read performance than ROB-only approaches, and discuss costs.

Make the argument
Highlight that a PRF read is a single register-file access after a map-table lookup, while a ROB-only read may require searching the ROB for the newest pending write.
Discuss trade-offs
Note allocation/free-list complexity, map-table checkpointing for recovery, and why these costs are often worth the read-speed gains.
Conclude with a reasoned judgment: for high-performance cores where read latency and multi-issue bandwidth matter, PRF plus ROB for control is usually the winning design.
