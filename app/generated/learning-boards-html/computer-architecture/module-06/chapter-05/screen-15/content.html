Check all prior stores
A load must locate the most recent store to its address among earlier stores.
Use prediction judiciously
Store-set predictors reduce stalls for repeated conflicts but require recovery support.
Balance scale and cost
Wide issue and large windows increase LSQ demands; consider clustering and fusion to control cost.
Reflective question
What single change would you make to a processor's memory dependence system if misprediction cost were unusually high for your workload?
