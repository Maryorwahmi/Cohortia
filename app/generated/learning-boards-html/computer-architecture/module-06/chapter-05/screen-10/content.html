Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Scheduling Trade-offs
Conservative scheduling
Hold loads until prior store addresses are known.
Pros: simple hardware, no replays. Cons: stalls that reduce ILP, especially costly in wide-issue cores with large windows.
Aggressive speculation
Issue loads early guided by prediction and recover on violations.
Pros: higher throughput when accurate. Cons: requires recovery logic, larger LSQ bookkeeping, and can suffer penalties on frequent mispredictions.
Choosing a policy
Designers often mix strategies: use predictors to speculatively issue most loads while conservatively scheduling loads with high conflict risk. Measure workload conflict behavior to guide the choice.
Key Takeaway

There is no free lunch: balancing correctness, hardware cost, and throughput requires targeted prediction and selective conservatism.
