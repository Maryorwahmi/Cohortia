Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Load‑Store Queue (LSQ)
LSQ entry fields
Typical contents of each LSQ entry and the role they play.
Virtual address
Stored when computed; used for early checks before translation.
Physical address
Available after translation; used for final associative address comparison.
Data
For stores: held until commit. For loads: may hold loaded value for bookkeeping.
ROB tag / status bits
Identify the instruction and record valid/address-known bits for safe issuance and commit.
Because loads must check all earlier stores, the LSQ implements associative address comparisons. That hardware cost is justified by the ability to forward data and avoid unnecessary stalls. To maintain correctness, the LSQ also records ordering so that on commit it writes stores to memory in program order.
Example entry table
(After three instructions issue)
# | VA | PA | Data | ROB | AddrKnown
1 | 0x1000 | 0x1000 | 0xABCD | 12 | yes
2 | 0x2000 | 0x2000 | 0xEF01 | 13 | yes
3 | 0x1000 | - | - | 14 | no
Common mistake

Assuming address-known is always true. Some stores compute addresses later, forcing loads to wait or speculate.
