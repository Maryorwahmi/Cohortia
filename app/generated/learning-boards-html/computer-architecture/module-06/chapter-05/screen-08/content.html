Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Disambiguation Strategies
Address‑based disambiguation
Wait for addresses, then compare precisely.
Pros: exact, no mis-speculation. Cons: delays loads until store addresses are ready, reducing parallelism.
Store‑set prediction
Remember which stores have conflicted with which loads and use that to guide scheduling.
Pros: reduces unnecessary stalls by focusing on risky loads. Cons: mispredictions require replay and recovery.
Combined approach
Use store-set prediction to decide which loads to hold back or tag as risky, and use address-based checks for final correctness and recovery. Prediction provides performance gains; address checks ensure correctness.
Key Takeaway

Prediction and address comparison are complementary: one improves scheduling, the other guarantees correctness.
