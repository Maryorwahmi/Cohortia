Step 1 — Station area

Assume 167 bits per station (example from earlier). For 8 stations: 167 × 8 = 1,336 bits (~0.17 KB). This shows how small the raw storage is compared with caches.

// Example calculation
per_station = 167 bits
stations = 8
total_bits = per_station * stations  // 1336 bits
Step 2 — Arbitration policy

Choose integer > FP > load priority or oldest-first. If three complete (integer, FP, load) and integer has top priority, integer broadcasts now, FP waits one cycle, load waits longer.

Step 3 — Comparator counts

With 1 CDB: comparators = 8. With 2 CDBs: comparators = 16. Each comparator per station per bus increases area and power linearly.

Step 4 — Bandwidth requirement

If FUs can produce up to 4 results in a busy cycle, provide at least 3–4 effective broadcast slots via multiple CDBs or segmentation to avoid queuing. Consider local bypass for nearby dependencies to reduce global load.
