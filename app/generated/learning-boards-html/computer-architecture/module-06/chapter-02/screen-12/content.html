Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Hierarchical CDBs
Driver-tree concept

Use staged drivers so each driver only handles a local fanout. Top-level drivers handle few mid-level drivers, and these drive local clusters of comparators and register inputs.

Coordination overhead

Staged broadcasts require drivers and timing coordination; drivers re-drive signals, adding control logic and area, but reduce local wire delays significantly.

When to use hierarchy
  • Large floorplans with long distances between stations.
  • When segmentation alone fails to meet timing goals.
  • When driver area is acceptable in exchange for timing improvements.
Design tip

Model worst-case path delay through the driver tree to ensure staged broadcasts meet your clock targets before committing to the area cost.
