Why the CDB is slow

The bus is long and drives many comparators and register inputs. This increases capacitance and wire RC delay. Comparator evaluation and latching add to the timing budget.

Mitigation techniques
  • Segmented CDB: divide the bus into shorter segments with repeaters or local drivers.
  • Hierarchical CDB: driver tree that limits how much each driver must drive.
  • Local bypass: direct forwarding between nearby stations without the global bus.
Practical trade-offs

Segmentation and hierarchy reduce delay but add drivers and control. Local bypass reduces global traffic but increases point-to-point routing. The decision depends on the physical floorplan and expected traffic patterns.

Key takeaway

Reduce global fanout to shorten the critical path, but account for driver area and routing cost when choosing a technique.
