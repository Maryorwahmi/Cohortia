Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Learning Objectives — Extended
Estimate reservation station area

Compute per-station bits from documented fields and multiply by station count. Compare the result to cache and register file area to judge practicality.

Milestone: Produce a one-paragraph area estimate for a 50-station system.
Simulate arbitration behavior

Define a priority scheme and step through a case where 3 units finish at once. Decide who broadcasts now and who waits next cycle.

Milestone: Trace three completions and justify the selected broadcaster.
Count comparators and buses

For M CDBs and N stations, compute comparators = N × M and explain area/power scaling.

Milestone: Show comparator counts for 2 and 4 CDB configurations.
