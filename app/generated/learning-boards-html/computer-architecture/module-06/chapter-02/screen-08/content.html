Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Multiple CDBs
How additional CDBs help

Each extra CDB allows another result to broadcast in the same cycle, reducing stalls when multiple FUs finish simultaneously. This directly increases potential IPC up to the limit of FU completions per cycle.

Comparator scaling

For N reservation stations and M CDBs you need N × M comparators. Example: 50 stations and 2 CDBs require 100 comparators.

Partitioning strategies
  • By type: give integer units one bus and FP units another to reduce cross-type contention.
  • Static mapping: assign each FU to a particular bus to simplify routing.
  • Dynamic mapping: schedule broadcasts to different buses based on load, more complex but flexible.
Design trade-off

Adding buses reduces contention but increases routing, drivers, and comparator area. Use simulations to match bus count to expected FU parallelism.
