Local bypassing

Directly forward results to nearby stations to avoid the global bus. Best for short dependency chains and physically proximate stations.

Segmented CDB

Break the bus into segments with repeaters or local drivers. Each driver sees lower capacitance, reducing RC delay and improving timing.

Hybrid approach

Combine local bypass for the common fast path and segmented/hierarchical CDBs for global coverage to optimize both latency and broadcast completeness.

Design caveat
Local bypass adds routing and control complexity; segmentation needs repeaters and logic to manage multi-segment broadcasts.
