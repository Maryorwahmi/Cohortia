Types of Instruction Fusion
Micro-op, Macro-op, and Compound Instructions

Micro-op Fusion

Combines a load and an ALU operation into a single micro-op, common in x86 processors to reduce pipeline pressure.

Macro-op Fusion

Fuses two x86 instructions, like a compare and branch, into a single micro-op at the front-end, reducing execution engine pressure.

Compound Instructions

RISC processors may support single instructions that perform multiple operations, achieving similar efficiency gains.

Key Takeaway

Instruction fusion comes in various forms, all aiming to optimize instruction throughput by reducing the effective number of operations processed by the pipeline.
