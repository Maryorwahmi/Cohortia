Value Prediction: Benefits and Risks
Performance Gains vs. Misprediction Penalties

Potential Benefits

  • Breaks dependency chains, allowing dependent instructions to execute earlier.
  • Increases Instruction-Level Parallelism (ILP).
  • Particularly effective for predictable values like loop counters and certain loads.

Significant Risks

  • High misprediction penalty: wrong guesses require expensive pipeline flushes and replays.
  • Increased complexity: prediction tables consume area and power.
  • Limited performance gains (5-15%) often don't justify the costs for general-purpose CPUs.
Key Takeaway

Value prediction offers ILP benefits but faces challenges from misprediction penalties, hardware complexity, and often modest performance gains in practice.
