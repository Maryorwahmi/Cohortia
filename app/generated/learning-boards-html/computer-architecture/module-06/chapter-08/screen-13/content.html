Advanced Reorder Buffer Designs
Enhancing Recovery and Efficiency

Virtual ROB

Implements the Reorder Buffer as a linked list or hierarchical structure to reduce area and complexity of commit logic.

Selective Replay

Instead of flushing the entire pipeline on a misprediction, only affected dependent instructions are replayed, reducing penalty.

Checkpoint Repair

Maintains multiple architectural state checkpoints for faster recovery from mispredictions, improving overall efficiency.

Key Takeaway

Advanced ROB designs like Virtual ROB, Selective Replay, and Checkpoint Repair are crucial for minimizing misprediction penalties and enhancing processor recovery efficiency.
