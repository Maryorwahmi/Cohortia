Key Concepts
Understanding Trade-offs and Speculation

Value Prediction

Speculatively predicts instruction results (last-value, stride, context-based) to break data dependencies.

Benefits and Risks

Value prediction offers ILP gains but carries high misprediction penalties and significant hardware complexity.

Advanced ROB Designs

Techniques like Virtual ROB, Selective Replay, and Checkpoint Repair enhance recovery from mispredictions.

Summary Takeaway

Value prediction and advanced ROB designs represent sophisticated efforts to optimize OoO execution, balancing speculative gains against complexity and recovery costs.
