Challenges of Clustering
Navigating Communication and Load Balancing

Inter-Cluster Communication

Data transfer between clusters adds latency, potentially stalling dependent instructions and impacting performance.

Load Balancing Issues

Uneven distribution of instructions can lead to underutilized clusters, reducing overall processor efficiency.

Increased Complexity

Sophisticated steering logic is required to efficiently direct instructions and minimize inter-cluster communication.

Key Takeaway

The benefits of clustering must be weighed against the costs of inter-cluster communication latency, load balancing challenges, and increased design complexity.
