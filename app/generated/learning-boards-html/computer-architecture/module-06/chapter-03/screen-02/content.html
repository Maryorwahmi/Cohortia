Learning Objectives

Each objective pairs an implementable skill with why it matters for out-of-order processors.

01
Implement RAT + Free List

Build the mapping table and a free-list allocator. This is essential so destinations get unique physical registers and source operands resolve to the correct storage.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Distinguish architectural vs physical

Explain why ISA-visible registers are names and why hardware needs more physical storage to support parallel in-flight writes and speculation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Allocate at issue, free at commit

Describe the lifecycle of a physical register so old mappings remain available until commit, avoiding hazards and preserving precise state.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Assess PRF area and power

Analyze why multi-ported files are expensive and how banking, hierarchy, and bypassing reduce cost and energy consumption.
