Register Map Table

Maps each architectural register to its current physical register; critical for reads and for allocating new destinations.

Free List

Tracks unused physical registers. Allocation at issue, freeing at commit; empty free list stalls issue.

Physical Register File

Large, multi-ported storage holding values. Area and power are a dominant design concern; banking and hierarchy are common mitigations.
