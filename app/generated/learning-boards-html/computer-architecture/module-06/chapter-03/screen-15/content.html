Quick Reference

What to Remember

Allocate on issue

New physical registers are allocated at issue; old mappings remain until commit.

Free at commit

Commit-time frees return old physical registers to the free list and avoid premature reuse.

Mitigate PRF cost

Banking, hierarchy, and bypassing reduce porting and energy while supporting capacity.

Final Takeaway

Register renaming is a small set of clear mechanisms with outsized impact on processor throughput and complexity; mastering their interactions is key to understanding modern CPU design.
