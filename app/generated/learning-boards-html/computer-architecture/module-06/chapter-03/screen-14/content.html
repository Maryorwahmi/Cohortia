What to Remember

Key Concepts

Architectural vs Physical

ISA names are stable; physical registers are dynamic storage entries assigned per write to enable parallelism.

RAT and Checkpointing

RAT maps names to physical registers and must be checkpointed or shadowed to recover from mispredictions.

Free list & PRF

Free list allocates on issue, reclaims on commit; PRF stores values and multi-porting drives area and power costs.

Summary Takeaway

Renaming decouples ISA names from storage so true dependencies are preserved while false dependencies are removed, enabling safe out-of-order execution at the cost of PRF complexity and checkpointing logic.
