Explore Key Topics
Intel Core

Microarchitectures from Sandy Bridge to Raptor Lake: µop cache, 4-wide decode, 6-wide issue and mid-sized ROBs.

AMD Zen

Zen 3–5: wider issue, larger ROBs, more execution ports, AVX-512 support on recent models.

Apple & ARM

Apple M-series: massive ROBs and wide issue; ARM cores balance fetch width, NEON/SVE, and efficiency.
