Family summary

Cortex-A7x and X-series

ARM scales fetch and issue widths while keeping power and area efficiency in mind. SIMD support and cache sizing are central to performance balance.

Front-end & Issue

Fetch width 4–6, issue 4–6 μops typical; higher peaks in X-series variants.

ROB & Registers

ROB from ~160 to ~288 entries, physical registers 128–256 depending on core.

Spec highlights
SIMD: NEON mandatory, SVE optional
L1: 32–64KB, L2: 256KB–1MB, L3: shared up to 8MB
Ports: ~6–10 execution ports
Example

An X4 core with high ROB and wider issue targets workloads that need single-thread throughput while retaining ARM efficiency benefits.

Misconception check

Comparing ROB sizes across ISAs without considering ISA-level micro-op expansion, decode width, or power budget can mislead conclusions.

Key Takeaway

ARM cores balance window size and fetch bandwidth to fit power-constrained and mobile targets while scaling for performance when needed.
