Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts
ROB = instruction window
Larger ROBs allow more speculative instructions and outstanding loads but cost area and power.
Issue width & ports
Issue width sets potential throughput; port specialization and front-end bandwidth determine actual gains.
Caches must scale
Large windows and wide issue demand larger L2/L3 and bandwidth to avoid stalls from cache misses.
Summary

When assessing a CPU, examine ROB, issue width, ports, and cache together to understand architecture strengths and trade-offs.
