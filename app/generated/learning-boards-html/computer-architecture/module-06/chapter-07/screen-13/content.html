Five design trends
Wider Issue
From 3-wide to 8–10-wide to increase peak throughput for ILP-rich code.
Larger ROB
Window sizes grew from ~40 to 900+ entries to hold more speculative work.
More Units
More execution ports and specialized units to support mixed workloads.
Larger Caches
Growing L2/L3 to feed wider cores and reduce off-chip misses.
Specialized Accelerators
NPUs, matrix units, and video engines relieve the core for domain-specific tasks.
Practical rule

Look for balanced scaling across these five trends. Over-investing in one without supporting resources produces limited returns.
