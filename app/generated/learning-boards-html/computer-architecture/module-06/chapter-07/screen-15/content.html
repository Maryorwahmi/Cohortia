Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts
Unified schedulers
Unified queues reduce idle functional units and improve throughput, at higher implementation cost.
Vendor trade-offs
Apple chooses very large windows and caches; AMD favors wider issue and per-core L2; ARM balances efficiency and throughput.
Specialized accelerators
On-chip NPUs, matrix units, and media engines improve system throughput and energy for domain workloads.
Closing summary

Assess CPU designs by reading ROB, issue width, ports, caches, and accelerators together to match architecture to workload needs and constraints.
