How to use these objectives
  • Refer to ROB, issue width, and port counts when comparing single-threaded performance.
  • Cross-check cache sizes and physical registers to understand memory pressure and renaming limits.
  • Use the hands-on activity to measure real processors against these objectives.
Tip: When reading a spec sheet, note both the numbers and the design philosophy to predict behavior on your workload.
