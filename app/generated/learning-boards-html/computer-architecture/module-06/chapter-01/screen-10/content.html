Latency Analysis

How latencies affect execution

Assumed latencies
MUL: 3 cycles · ADD/SUB: 1 cycle · DIV: 6 cycles
Fast completions produce early broadcasts that let dependents start sooner.
Dependency chain
A chain of dependent instructions serializes progress regardless of out-of-order features.
Independent instructions benefit most from extra stations or units.
Station count
More stations allow more in-flight independent instructions if there are enough functional units.
CDB bandwidth
Single-bus writes can serialize results even when units finish in parallel.
Design trade-off
Adding buses or units reduces stalls but costs area and power.
