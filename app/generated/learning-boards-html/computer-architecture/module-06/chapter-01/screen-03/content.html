Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Learning Objectives

Why these objectives matter

Implementation reveals hazards
Coding a simulator makes tags, Qi updates, and CDB arbitration explicit and testable.
Stations are scheduling units
Knowing station fields helps predict when an instruction becomes executable.
CDB is the forwarder
Broadcasts update waiting stations and registers by matching tags to Qi, Qj, or Qk.
Analyze to choose resources
Measure cycles vs. station counts to understand trade-offs between hardware and performance.
