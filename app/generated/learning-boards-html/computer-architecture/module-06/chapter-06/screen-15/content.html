Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Key Concepts
Microarchitectural leaks

Speculation leaves cache and predictor state that can be measured even after architectural rollback.

Spectre vs Meltdown

Spectre manipulates prediction or disambiguation; Meltdown exploits speculative privileged reads when kernel mappings exist in user space.

Layered mitigations

Use KPTI, retpoline, barriers, and hardware controls together. Evaluate both effectiveness and performance cost for your environment.

Summary

Identify the changed microarchitectural state, determine the observable channel, and select mitigations that interrupt that specific step while balancing performance.
