Skills & Assessment

Concrete tasks that demonstrate the objectives: code reading, timing analysis, and mitigation verification.

Code analysis

Walk through the Spectre-v1 pattern, identify training, speculative access, and cache probe steps, and explain why timing reveals the secret.

Safe lab

Run or inspect a local proof-of-concept. Use only your machine and owned data. Discuss mitigation flags such as kpti, ibrs, ibpb, ssbd where present.

Explain & evaluate

Answer why microarchitectural state leaks remain after rollback and compare KPTI effectiveness vs. Spectre mitigations.

Assessment idea

Explain why speculative cache effects leak secrets, and why KPTI prevents Meltdown but does not address Spectre-style branch predictor attacks.
