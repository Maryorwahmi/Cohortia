Performance considerations

Measure real workloads; mitigations affect CPU-bound, I/O-bound, and indirect-branch-heavy workloads differently. Decide whether to enable a mitigation by balancing measured cost against risk profile.

Example impacts
  • KPTI: 1–30% slowdowns, worst for syscalls and I/O.
  • Retpoline: usually minimal, but hurts indirect-branch-heavy code.
  • Barriers: can cause large slowdowns where speculation is critical.
Decision framework

Identify attacker capabilities, decide which mitigations are necessary, and run benchmarks to quantify impact. Consider layered defenses: combine KPTI, retpoline, and selective barriers with process isolation.

Reflection

Can you accept slightly lower throughput to eliminate the risk of local attackers stealing secrets? Or does your environment require disabling some mitigations because local threat is controlled?
