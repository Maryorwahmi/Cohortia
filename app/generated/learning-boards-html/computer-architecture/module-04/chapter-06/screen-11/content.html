Checklist for timing diagram
  • Label cycles for IF, ID, EX, MEM, WB for each instruction.
  • Mark flushed instructions when branch resolves taken.
  • Count total cycles and divide by instruction count to get CPI.
Key Takeaway

Careful timing diagrams and correct counting of flushed instructions are essential to compute CPI and compare designs accurately.
