Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Quick reference
Penalty table
ID: 1 cycle, EX: 2 cycles, MEM: 3 cycles (no prediction)
Delayed branch
Works only when compiler can fill the slot; otherwise NOPs waste cycles.
BTB behaviour
On hit with correct prediction, fetch target immediately; on miss or wrong prediction, penalty applies.
Final thought
Always use CPI times clock period to compare designs and measure BTB hit rates on representative workloads to size caches appropriately.
