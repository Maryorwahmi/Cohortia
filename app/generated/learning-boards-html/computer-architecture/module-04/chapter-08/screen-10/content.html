Module 5 › Chapter 4.8 › Deep Pipelines
Effects of depth

More stages mean more registers to flush and a longer time until the excepting instruction reaches commit. This increases exception latency and the length of stalls for following instructions.

Design consequence
Ensure write-disable signals and flush paths cover every stage. Conservative gating reduces risk of transient updates during long flushes.
Performance trade-off
Deeper pipelines can improve clock frequency but increase the cost when an exception forces a long stall and flush.
Practical advice
  • Make flush and gating logic simple and conservative in deep designs.
  • Test corner cases where flags propagate slowly across many registers.
Key takeaway

Deep pipelines increase flush cost and require robust gating across more stages.
