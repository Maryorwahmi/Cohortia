Why this matters

Precise exceptions let software rely on predictable failure points. Without them, state may be corrupted, debugging becomes unreliable, and recovery routines cannot resume execution cleanly.

Concept snapshot
  • Exception flags move with instruction through pipeline registers.
  • Earliest instruction takes priority regardless of detection stage.
  • Flush and write-disable stop later instructions from changing state.
Key takeaway

Ensuring exceptions are precise preserves a serializable program order and enables reliable recovery and debugging.

Teaching metaphor

Think of the pipeline as a highway of cars. A red car that needs service must stop the cars behind it and let cars ahead continue. Precise exceptions are the checkpoint that enforces that order.
