Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Pipeline Hazards
Hazard types
  • Data hazard: dependency on a register not yet written back; may require stalls or forwarding.
  • Control hazard: branch decisions that change the PC and force instruction fetch redirection.
  • Structural hazard: resource conflict when two stages require the same hardware simultaneously.
Common misbelief
Forwarding removes many data stalls but cannot fix control hazards or structural hazards without additional mechanisms.
Mitigations
Forwarding, pipeline interlocks, branch prediction, and duplicating resources are standard techniques to reduce stall penalties.
Key Takeaway

Catalog hazards and plan mitigations; assume some residual stalls that reduce ideal IPC.
