Identify where each dependent instruction must wait for R1; without forwarding, many will stall until the producer reaches WB.
Guided steps
  1. Draw cycles across the top and instructions on the left.
  2. Place IF, ID, EX, MEM, WB boxes diagonally for each instruction.
  3. Mark stalls where a source register is not yet available.
  4. Redraw with forwarding: forward ALU output from EX/MEM to dependent EX to remove stalls where possible.
Key Takeaway

Forwarding typically recovers several cycles of stalls by supplying the ALU result before it reaches WB, but it cannot fix hazards that require a value not yet computed.
