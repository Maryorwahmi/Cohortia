IPC vs Latency
Throughput rises with overlap; latency per instruction remains sum of stage delays.
Clock Estimation
T_pipe depends on the slowest stage plus register overhead; compute T_single/T_pipe to estimate speedup.
Hazard Planning
Identify data, control, and structural hazards and plan forwarding, prediction, or resource duplication accordingly.
Final thought
Design decisions should match the workload: deeper pipelines for branch-light workloads, stronger branch handling for branch-heavy code.
