Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Fill & Drain
Formula and intuition
Total cycles ≈ N + M − 1
N cycles to fill the pipeline, then M − 1 additional cycles to finish the remaining instructions after steady state begins.
Example
For N=5 stages and M=4 loads in the laundry analogy, total time corresponds to 5 + 4 − 1 = 8 cycles of the stage duration; compare that to serial time to see throughput improvement.
Key Takeaway

Include fill and drain when calculating cycles for short programs; for long programs the N overhead is amortized away.
