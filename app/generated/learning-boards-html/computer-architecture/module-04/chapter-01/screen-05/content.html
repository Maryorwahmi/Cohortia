Sequential vs Overlapped
Sequential (no pipeline)
Wash 30m → Dry 30m → Fold 30m per load. Four loads take 360 minutes total.
Pipelined (overlap)
Start wash 2 while load 1 dries. After filling, one load completes every 30 minutes; four loads take 180 minutes total.
Concrete numbers
Serial: 4 × 3 × 30 = 360 minutes. Pipelined steady state: initial fill 90 minutes, then one every 30 minutes; total 180 minutes.
Learning checklist
  • Identify latency (time for one load) and throughput (loads per unit time).
  • Compute pipeline fill time: N stages → first completion after N steps.
  • Apply the idea to instruction pipelines: overlapping stages increases IPC.
