What to Remember
A short checklist for implementation and tests.
1. Three paths
EX/MEM→EX, MEM/WB→EX, MEM/WB→MEM.
2. Three-way selects
Per-operand selects: regfile, EX/MEM, MEM/WB.
3. Comparator checks
Check RegWrite and Rd != 0 when matching registers.
4. Priority
EX/MEM has priority over MEM/WB.
5. Load-use stall
Detect MemRead in ID/EX and insert a one-cycle bubble when it matches sources.
6. Measure CPI
Compute CPI with forwarding and add one cycle per load-use pair to estimate real cost.
Final reflection

Forwarding is the single most important in-order optimization for reducing RAW stalls, but correct detection and handling of load-use hazards and priorities are essential for correctness.
