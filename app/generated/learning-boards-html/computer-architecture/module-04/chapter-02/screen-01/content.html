Pipeline Datapath and Control

Understand how control signals are produced, carried, and reduced across IF, ID, EX, MEM, and WB stages so each stage acts at the correct time.

In this lesson

Trace instructions and their control flags through the five stages. Learn which signals are retained, which are dropped, and how hazard detection injects stalls and flushes.

Why it matters

Correct control propagation keeps later stages deterministic. Dropping unused signals reduces register width and saves power without changing behavior.
