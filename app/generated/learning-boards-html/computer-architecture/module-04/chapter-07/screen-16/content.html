Quick Reference
What to Remember
Three one-line reminders to guide analysis and design.
1. Quantify branch cost
Convert cycles to time and include mispredict CPI in comparisons.
2. Measure IPC & memory
High frequency needs matching IPC and memory performance to pay off.
3. Account for overhead
Register, power, and verification costs reduce the net benefit of extra stages.
Summary
A balanced design wins: evaluate depth only after considering prediction, IPC, memory, and power together.
