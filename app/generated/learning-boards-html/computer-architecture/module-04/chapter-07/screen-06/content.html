Cost Analysis
Costs of Deeper Pipelines
Identify the practical penalties that grow with pipeline depth.
Branch penalty
Mispredicted branches waste many in-flight cycles. In a 20-stage pipeline, penalties of 15–20 cycles are common.
Pipeline fill time
A pipeline takes roughly N cycles to fill; short programs pay a large startup cost with deeper pipelines.
Register overhead
Each new stage boundary needs registers that add area, power, and timing overhead that reduce net speedup.
Hazard complexity
More stages require more forwarding, more checks, and more corner cases, increasing stalls and verification effort.
Diminishing returns
As stages shrink, register and clock overhead dominate, and the cycle time stops improving proportionally.
Practical advice
Measure branch frequency and mispredict rate, then compute CPI impact before choosing deeper pipelines.
