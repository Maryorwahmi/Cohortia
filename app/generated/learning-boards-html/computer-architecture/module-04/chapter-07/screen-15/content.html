Summary
Key Concepts
Three compact cards summarize core points to remember.
Superpipelining
More stages than the classic five to shorten per-stage work and raise clock frequency.
Costs
Increased branch penalty, pipeline fill time, register overhead, power, and complexity.
Net lesson
Modern CPUs prefer moderate depths and invest in IPC, prediction, and caches for the best real performance.
Final takeaway
Depth matters, but only in the context of prediction accuracy, IPC, memory hierarchy, and power. Balance these factors for real gains.
