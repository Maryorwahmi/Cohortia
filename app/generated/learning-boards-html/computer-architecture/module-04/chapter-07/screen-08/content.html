Case Study
Pentium 4 NetBurst Overview
Design goals, pipeline depth, and why the approach ran into limits.
Design choices
  • Very deep pipeline (≈20, later 31 stages) to chase higher clock speed.
  • Focus on frequency over IPC; relatively narrow issue width.
  • High-power operation and significant register overhead.
Observed problems
  • Large branch misprediction penalty reduced effective throughput.
  • High power consumption and heat made scaling impractical.
  • Memory latency did not scale with clock frequency, causing stalls.
Analogy
A factory with many tiny fast workers wastes more when a supervisor gives the wrong order; clearing wasted work is expensive.
