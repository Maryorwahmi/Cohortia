Measuring Cache Efficiency

Understanding hit rate, miss rate, hit time, and miss penalty

Hit Rate (H)

The fraction of memory accesses that find the data in the cache. Calculated as `Hits / (Hits + Misses)`.

Miss Rate (M)

The fraction of accesses that do not find the data in the cache. Calculated as `1 - H`.

Hit Time

The time to access the cache on a hit. Typically 1-4 CPU cycles for L1 cache.

Key Metrics Summary

These metrics are crucial for evaluating cache performance and understanding its impact on overall system speed.

Key Takeaway

Hit rate, miss rate, hit time, and miss penalty are essential metrics for quantifying cache efficiency and identifying performance bottlenecks in memory systems.
