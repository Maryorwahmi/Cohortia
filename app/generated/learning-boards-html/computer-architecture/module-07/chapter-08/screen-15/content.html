What to Remember
Quick reference for implementation
MSHRs
Size to match concurrent miss capacity, and expect merges to save bandwidth.
Victim / Skewed
Use small victims or per-way hashing to fix conflict-driven misses cheaply.
Prefetch metrics
Collect hit rate, prefetches, useful prefetches, pollution, and bandwidth for tuning.
Final Note
Measure and tune. The right set of mechanisms depends on your application's memory behavior.
