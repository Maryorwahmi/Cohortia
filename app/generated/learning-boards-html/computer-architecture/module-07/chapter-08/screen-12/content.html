Trade-Off Analysis Prefetching metrics
Four dimensions to measure
Bandwidth, pollution, timeliness, and accuracy together determine benefit.
Bandwidth
Prefetch traffic can saturate memory and hurt demand requests.
Pollution
Unused prefetched lines may evict useful data in small caches.
Timeliness
Data must arrive just before demand to be useful; arrival window matters.
Accuracy
Useful prefetches divided by total prefetches; low accuracy wastes resources.
Mitigations
  • Confidence counters to vote on issuing prefetches.
  • Limited prefetch windows or throttling to protect bandwidth.
  • Separate buffers to delay cache insertion and reduce pollution.
Key Takeaway

Measure prefetchers holistically. High raw hit-rate gains can be offset by bandwidth or pollution costs.
