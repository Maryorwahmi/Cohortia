Advanced Topic Correlation & GHB
History-based prefetching for irregular patterns
Use miss history to predict likely next misses when patterns are non-arithmetic.
Correlation prefetching
When miss A often precedes miss B, prefetch B upon seeing A. Requires storing pair relationships and updating counts to estimate confidence.
Global History Buffer (GHB)
Store a stream of recent miss addresses in a buffer and search it for repeated subsequences to compute prefetch targets. Good for repeating but non-regular patterns.
Caveat
These approaches add storage and matching logic. In workloads with shifting behavior, history can lead to inaccurate prefetches, so confidence and throttling are essential.
Key Takeaway

History-based prefetchers broaden captureable patterns but require extra state and careful confidence controls to avoid wasting bandwidth.
