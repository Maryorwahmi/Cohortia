Key Concepts Summary cards
What to remember
Non-blocking caches
MSHRs track concurrent misses and support miss merging to avoid duplicate fetches.
Victim & skewed caches
Use small fully-associative victims or per-way hashing to reduce conflict misses with low area overhead.
Prefetchers & trade-offs
Next-line, stride, stream, correlation and GHB each balance accuracy, timeliness, and bandwidth differently.
Takeaway: pick structures and prefetch policies based on measured workload behavior — there is no universal best choice.
