Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › TLB & Cache
Parallel probes
Common designs probe the TLB and L1 cache together so a TLB hit and cache hit both resolve quickly. On a TLB miss, the cache probe may be wasted, increasing effective latency.
Optimization
Place hot data within a few pages to improve both TLB and cache locality.
Separation of concerns
  • TLB: caches translations, not data.
  • Data cache: caches actual bytes accessed from physical addresses.
  • Improving one does not automatically fix the other; measure both independently.
Key Takeaway

Treat translation and caching layers separately when diagnosing performance; improving locality helps both.
