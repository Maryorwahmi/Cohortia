Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Practice Tips
Measure
Always compute Effective Access Time from measured hit rates and penalties to justify changes.
Prioritize
Address frequent TLB misses before rare page faults; the former are cheaper to fix and often more common.
Locality
Lay out data to improve spatial locality so hot items fall into fewer pages, aiding both cache and TLB performance.
Final thought

Good measurements plus simple simulations enable effective, low-risk optimizations for translation performance.
