These objectives prepare you to explain structure, compute access times, sketch a page-walk, and implement a small translator with TLB simulation. They focus on core mechanisms and measurable competency.

Describe isolation and protection, and why swapping and sharing matter.

03
Use the TLB

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Analyze performance

Verified Competency Milestone
