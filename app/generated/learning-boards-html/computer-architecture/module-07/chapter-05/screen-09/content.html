Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › TLB Performance
Formula
Effective Memory Access Time = TLB_Hit_Time + TLB_Miss_Rate × TLB_Miss_Penalty
Example 1: hit time=1 cycle, miss rate=1%, penalty=50 cycles → 1 + 0.01×50 = 1.5 cycles.
Example 2: with miss rate=5% → 1 + 0.05×50 = 3.5 cycles, showing big sensitivity to miss rate.
Interpretation
  • Even a small miss rate adds the miss penalty multiplied by that rate to every access.
  • Reducing miss rate is often more valuable than shaving a cycle off hit time when penalty is large.
  • Measure hit rate and penalty to prioritize TLB size, associativity, or algorithm changes.
Key Takeaway

Quantify TLB behavior with the effective time formula; small miss-rate changes can yield large performance differences.
