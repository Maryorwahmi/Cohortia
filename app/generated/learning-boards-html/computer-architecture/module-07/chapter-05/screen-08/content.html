Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › TLB Organization
Entry components
  • VPN: tag used to match virtual pages.
  • PFN: the physical frame number to form physical address.
  • Valid and protection bits: indicate entry validity and permissions.
  • ASID: identifies the process to avoid flushes on context switch.
  • Dirty/accessed bits: assist in page replacement and write-back decisions.
Associativity trade-offs
Fully-associative
Maximizes hit rate for small tables; simpler replacement logic like LRU is feasible.
Set-associative
Scales better to larger sizes with indexed access and controlled hardware complexity.
Practical tip

If ASIDs are absent, context switches force TLB flushes that temporarily raise miss rates; ASIDs reduce this overhead.
