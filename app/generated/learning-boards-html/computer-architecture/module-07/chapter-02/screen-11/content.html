Policy overview
LRU: best for temporal locality, higher tracking cost
Random: lowest hardware cost, sometimes close in quality
Pseudo-LRU: compromises tracking complexity and hit-rate quality
Write considerations

Write-back requires dirty bits and write-back on eviction which increases bandwidth needs and energy; include these when evaluating replacement costs.

Common implementation mistakes
  • Forgetting valid or dirty bits in tag storage calculations.
  • Assuming LRU is always worth its cost; measuring on traces is essential.
  • Neglecting the multiplexer delay when many comparators are used.
Pro tip

Start with random or pseudo‑LRU in small caches; profile with real traces before implementing full LRU in hardware.
