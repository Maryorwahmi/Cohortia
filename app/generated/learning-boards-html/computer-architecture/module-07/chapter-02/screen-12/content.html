Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Associativity Guidance
Practical checklist
Determine latency budget: can extra comparators extend critical path?
Measure on traces: does 4‑way already remove most conflict misses?
Calculate area/power cost for additional comparators and multiplexers.
Rule of thumb: prioritize low associativity for L1, allow higher associativity for L2 when access time is less critical. Always verify with representative traces before committing to hardware complexity.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Pick the lowest associativity that achieves near‑optimal hit rate for your workload to minimize added comparators, area, and power.
