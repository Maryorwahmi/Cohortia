Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
Direct‑mapped
One line per block, simplest and fastest, but conflict‑prone.
Set‑associative
N ways per set, parallel tag checks, replacement policy required on miss.
Fully‑associative
Any line can hold any block; highest hit rate, highest hardware cost.
Summary takeaway

Use bit math and representative traces to choose associativity. Target the smallest associativity that reaches near‑optimal hit rate while respecting latency and area constraints.
