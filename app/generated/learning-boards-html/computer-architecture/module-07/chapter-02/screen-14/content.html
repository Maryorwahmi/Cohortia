Formula: address bits
offset = log2(L) • index = log2(S/(L×A)) • tag = W − offset − index
Formula: tag storage
Tag bits total = (S / L) × (T + 1 valid + 1 dirty) • compare vs S×8 for overhead
Heuristic
L1: 2–8 ways for low latency. L2: higher associativity possible. Validate with traces.
Final thought

Quantify every choice. Associativity reduces conflict misses but adds hardware cost; use bit math and traces to find the sweet spot for your system.
