Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Skills & Assessment
Assessment tasks
Calculation
Index, offset, and tag bits from S, L, A and address width; compute total tag bits including valid and dirty.
Replacement policy
Explain LRU vs random and show when hardware complexity outweighs hit gains.
Design choice
Pick associativity for L1 or L2 and justify with tradeoffs in access time and area.
Hands‑on
Compare direct, 4‑way, 8‑way for 32 KB cache and estimate hit rates qualitatively.
Grading tips
Show each numeric step, state your assumptions (address width, line size), and explain whether remaining misses are capacity or conflict misses.
