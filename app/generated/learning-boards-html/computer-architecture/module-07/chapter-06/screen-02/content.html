Learning Objectives
Learn the skills, justify the trade-offs, and compute the limits for TLB and cache design.
01
Design a configurable TLB

Choose size, associativity and replacement policy to balance hit rate and hardware cost. Practice sizing L1 vs L2 for latency and reach.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain TLB-cache interaction

Understand serial vs parallel access, and when page-offset indexing permits simultaneous lookup to reduce latency.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe virtual caches

Explain the synonym problem, coherence challenges, and how VIPT uses physical tags to avoid inconsistency.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Analyze TLB hit rate impact

Quantify how TLB size, associativity and page size choices change miss rates and end-to-end memory latency for workloads.
