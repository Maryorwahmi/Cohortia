TLB Design and Virtual Caches

Design choices for the TLB and cache indexing directly affect latency, coherence, and correctness. This chapter shows the trade-offs and calculations you need to decide between VIPT and PIPT, or to size a multi-level TLB for your workload.

Visual concept
TLB: reception desk card catalog — quick lookups of virtual→physical.
VIPT cache: index with virtual offset, tag with physical address for coherence.
Trade-off: latency vs. aliasing and cache size limits.
Explore Key Topics
TLB Parameters

Size, associativity, pages, replacement and ASIDs determine hit rate and context-switch cost.

VIPT vs PIPT

VIPT lowers latency but forces cache size limits tied to page offset and associativity.

Performance Techniques

Large pages, TLB prefetching, multi-level TLBs and hardware walkers reduce miss penalties.
