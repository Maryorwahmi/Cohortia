Replacement & Associativity

Choose a replacement policy that fits hardware constraints and pairing it with appropriate associativity reduces conflict misses without excessive complexity.

LRU
Good for predictable locality; expensive to implement in high associativity.
Random / pseudo-LRU
Cheaper hardware; often nearly as effective for many workloads.
Associativity
Higher associativity reduces conflict misses, enabling larger VIPT capacity without more index bits.
Concrete scenario
If you have area to increase associativity, you might double associativity rather than doubling index bits. That increases capacity per set and helps VIPT limits without changing address indexing.
Key Takeaway

Match replacement complexity to TLB size. For small L1 TLBs choose fully-associative with simple LRU; for larger structures use pseudo-LRU or random to save hardware cost.
