What to Remember
Key Concepts — Part 2
VIPT limit
Maximum size = page size × associativity when index bits are confined to the page offset.
Large pages
2 MB or 1 GB pages reduce TLB entries required for big footprints but increase fragmentation risk.
Multi-level & walkers
Combine a tiny fast L1 with a larger L2 and use hardware page walkers to reduce OS interference on misses.
Final reflection: Which single change — larger pages, bigger L2, more associativity, or ASIDs — would give you the most latency reduction for your application profile?
