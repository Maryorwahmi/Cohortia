Cache Write Policies and Replacement Strategies
How caches handle writes, why lines are evicted, and practical optimizations you can simulate
In this lesson

Learn the 3C model of misses, contrast write-through vs write-back, implement write allocation choices, and compare replacement strategies including LRU, FIFO, Random, and PLRU.

Why it matters

Cache behavior drives memory bandwidth and latency. Choosing the right policy and replacement reduces misses and system stalls under real workloads.
