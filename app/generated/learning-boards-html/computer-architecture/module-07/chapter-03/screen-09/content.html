Optimization
Victim cache and pseudo-associative cache
Small additions that reduce conflict misses with modest area and latency costs.
Victim Cache
A tiny fully-associative buffer of recently-evicted lines. On miss, check victim cache and swap on hit.
Pseudo-Associative
Primary fast mapping, plus a secondary alternative mapping check to capture lines that conflict in the primary mapping.
Pro tip: Victim caches are especially effective when conflict misses are frequent but a full increase in associativity is too costly.
Lab check
Add a 4–16 line victim cache to your simulator and re-run the cyclic trace. Count how many misses convert into victim hits and how the conflict miss component drops.
Key Takeaway

Small buffers and secondary lookups often recover hot lines and dramatically reduce conflict misses with little area cost.
