Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Prefetching
Technique
Hardware and software prefetching
Prefetchers predict upcoming accesses and fetch blocks early to hide memory latency.
Hardware
Next-line, stride prefetchers detect patterns and fetch ahead automatically.
Software
Compiler or programmer inserts prefetch instructions where future access is known.
Measure both saved misses and extra bandwidth. Prefetching helps Trace A (sequential) and hurts Trace B (random) — verify that in your simulator.
Experiment idea
Vary prefetch distance and throttling. Plot saved misses versus extra memory traffic. Identify the sweet spot for your workload where net miss reduction is highest.
Key Takeaway

Prefetching is powerful for predictable patterns but must be tuned to avoid wasting bandwidth and polluting cache lines.
