Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Replacement Strategies
Overview
LRU, FIFO, Random, Pseudo-LRU
Understand how each choice balances effectiveness and implementation cost.
LRU
Evict least recently used. Best for temporal locality but expensive at high associativity.
FIFO
Evict oldest by insertion. Simple, can evict hot lines.
Random
Evict a random line. Low overhead and avoids some pathological patterns.
Pseudo-LRU
Approximate LRU with a bit tree. Lower cost and reasonably effective.
Misconception: believing LRU always wins. In cyclic or streaming access patterns, random or FIFO may perform as well or better.
Concrete scenario
Trace C: addresses 0, 64, 128, 0, 64, 128 shows cyclic conflict behavior. LRU can repeatedly evict the line that will be used next, whereas random avoids deterministic thrashing. Testing reveals these differences clearly in miss-rate plots.
Key Takeaway

Pick the replacement that balances your hardware budget and expected access patterns; PLRU is a common practical compromise.
