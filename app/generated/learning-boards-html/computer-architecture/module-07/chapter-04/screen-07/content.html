Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Hit Under Miss
Core Concept

Hit under miss

A hit that can be served immediately despite an outstanding miss to a different block.

Concrete example

Load A misses and issues an MSHR. Load B, to a different cached block, hits and completes, enabling dependent instructions to proceed.

Misconception check

Not all accesses during a miss can proceed; only those that do not depend on the missing block and when MSHR resources permit.

Key Takeaway

Hit under miss preserves forward progress for independent instructions and is basic to non-blocking performance gains.

Comparison — blocking vs non-blocking
Blocking

All accesses stall until miss completes, so hits cannot complete under a miss.

Non-blocking

Hits to different blocks proceed immediately, keeping the pipeline active.
