Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Key Concepts
Non-blocking cache

Serves hits while misses are in progress, preventing whole-cache stalls.

MSHR

Tracks an outstanding miss: address, valid, issued, waiting instructions, and returned data.

Merging

Misses to the same block merge into one MSHR, reducing memory traffic and MSHR pressure.

Bottom-line

MSHR count, merging policy, and store-handling shape the practical benefits of non-blocking caches; size them to match your workload’s parallelism and bandwidth constraints.
