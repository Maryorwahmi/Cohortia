Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › MSHR Count

MSHR capacity trade-offs

Weigh latency hiding against area, power, and diminishing returns when adding MSHRs.

When more MSHRs help

High-ILP workloads with many independent loads that can be issued in parallel benefit most from additional MSHRs.

When they don’t

Pointer-chasing and bandwidth-bound workloads show limited improvement from extra MSHRs.

Design checklist

Estimate expected parallel misses, check merging rates, and measure area/power before selecting an MSHR count.

Scenario matrix
4 MSHRs

Small area cost, limited outstanding misses. Good for simple cores or heavy merging patterns.

8–16 MSHRs

Supports more parallel misses and hides longer latencies, but increases area and power.

Reflect: For your target core, what is the expected degree of independent loads? That answer drives MSHR sizing.
