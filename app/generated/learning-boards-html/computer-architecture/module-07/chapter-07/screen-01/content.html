Cache Coherence Basics and Snooping
Understand conflicts, protocols, and when broadcasts fail to scale.

This chapter focuses on the coherence problem in shared-memory multiprocessors and practical solutions. We'll start with the simple MSI snooping protocol, extend it to MESI, note further enhancements like MOESI and MESIF, and finish with directory-based approaches for scalability.

Concept Map
Caches act like independent book readers; coherence protocols are the rules and messages that keep pages aligned. Snooping uses broadcasts, directories use a librarian lookup.
Protocol Highlights
  • MSI: simple snoop states (M, S, I)
  • MESI: adds Exclusive to avoid needless invalidations
  • Directory: scales with point-to-point messages
