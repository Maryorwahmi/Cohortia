Definition

What drives the ISA design

RISC-V emphasizes a tiny, frozen base plus optional extensions and reserved space for vendor features.

Simplicity
Small base eases design and verification.
Modularity
Optional extensions let you tailor feature sets.
Extensibility
Reserved opcodes for custom accelerators.
Stability & Openness
Frozen base and public specification.
Key Takeaway

A minimal, modular base plus open extension space enables rapid, targeted innovation while preserving compatibility.

Analogy
LEGO foundation with add-ons

Think of the base ISA as a small LEGO foundation. Extensions are colored add-ons you attach only if your build needs them. This keeps the foundation simple while still enabling complex structures.

Common mistake
Assuming openness means no governance

Openness coexists with a standards process. RISC-V International governs standard extensions to reduce fragmentation.
