Module 2 › Chapter 2.6 › RISC-V Impact
Academia
Teaching and research

Open cores and a small base ISA make RISC-V ideal for courses and experiments in architecture and verification.

Industry
Products and differentiation

Companies can ship RISC-V silicon with custom extensions to accelerate workloads without licensing constraints.

Government
Strategic independence

Open ISA adoption can reduce external IP dependence for strategic or sovereign projects.

Example cores
Rocket Chip, BOOM, CVA6, SiFive P550, XiangShan

These cores illustrate a range from educational designs to competitive, high-performance implementations.
