Compressed instruction sets

RISC-V C / ARM Thumb
Common instructions encoded in 16 bits. Decoder expands into full internal form before execution.
Improves code density and cache behavior for typical workloads with minimal pipeline disruption.
Limitations
Not every instruction is available in compressed form. Immediate ranges are smaller and compilers must use full-size encodings for larger constants.
Why it matters
Compressed sets strike a practical balance: better density without fully variable-length decoding costs.
