Fixed-length instruction formats

Benefits
  • Simple decode: fixed fields at fixed offsets.
  • Predictable pipeline and fast instruction fetch.
  • Friendly to instruction caches and alignment.
Limitations
Limited immediate and displacement size. Compilers may need extra instructions to construct large constants.
[ 31:0 ] opcode | rd | rs1 | rs2 | imm ; fixed 32-bit layout
Design note
Decide how many register bits and immediate bits you need. More registers requires more opcode space or a wider instruction.
