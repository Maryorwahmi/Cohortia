Architecture
Verification activities
  • • Formal modeling of instruction semantics where possible.
  • • Exhaustive test suites for encodings, edge cases, and exception flows.
  • • Cross-stack testing with compilers and OSes to detect contract mismatches.
Consequences of errors
Hardware bugs that violate the ISA cause errata. Fixes may require microcode patches, OS workarounds, or hardware replacement, all costly for ecosystems.
Practical tip
Treat the ISA as a legal spec: precisely document edge behaviors and provide a test harness to catch divergence early in implementation.
