Comparison
Rich ISA
Provides high-level instructions; simpler compiler output but harder hardware and verification.
Simple ISA
Fewer primitives; compiler must synthesize complex operations, easing hardware design.
Decision factors
Workloads, compiler maturity, power/area constraints, and verification effort determine the balance.
Misconception
Assuming compilers can always shoulder complexity overlooks maintenance, portability, and cases where hardware atomicity is necessary.
