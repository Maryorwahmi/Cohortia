Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Internal RISCification

Execution hierarchy

μop queue → renamer → reservation station → scheduler → execution units → retire.

Key mechanisms
  • Register renaming to eliminate false dependencies.
  • Reservation stations that hold μops until ready.
  • Out-of-order scheduling and speculative execution.
Why this matters

Although x86 is CISC at the ISA level, execution resembles RISC: simple μops executed by a sophisticated scheduler yield strong performance without breaking compatibility.

Misconception

Believing x86 is executed as complex CISC microcode alone—execution is largely μop-driven and optimized.

Pro tip

When analyzing performance, look at μop throughput, rename pressure, and scheduler bottlenecks rather than ISA-level instruction counts alone.
