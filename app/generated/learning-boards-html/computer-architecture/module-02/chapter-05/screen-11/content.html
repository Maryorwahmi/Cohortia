Compatibility vs simplicity

Market dynamics, not just technical merit, determine ISA longevity. x86 illustrates this interplay.

Compatibility

Legacy binaries, drivers, and OS support keep x86 deployed in desktops and servers.

Investment

Massive R&D by vendors improved microarchitectures so x86 competes effectively on performance and efficiency.

Ecosystem

Compilers, OSes, and application stacks create a self-reinforcing environment for x86 adoption.

Translation

Binary translation or emulation is possible but costly; it illustrates the inertia of legacy software.

Key takeaway

Market factors and ecosystem lock-in are as important as ISA cleanliness when predicting long-term dominance.
