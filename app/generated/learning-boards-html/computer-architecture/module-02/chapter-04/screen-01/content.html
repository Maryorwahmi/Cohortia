ARM Architecture and ARMv8-A
Explore the evolution, AArch64 design, and why ARM scales from microcontrollers to servers.

This chapter explains ARM’s progression from early 32-bit RISCs to ARMv8-A’s 64-bit clean-slate design. You’ll learn concrete hardware features and system-level consequences: register design, instruction encoding, exception levels, and SIMD extensions.

Why it matters: these choices determine instruction density, pipeline complexity, power use, and the ability to support virtualization and secure execution.
