Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Key Concepts
Evolution
ARMv1→ARMv8-A shows progressive feature additions and removals to meet new workloads.
AArch64
A clean 64-bit ISA with 31 regs, fixed 32-bit encodings, and simpler condition semantics.
Ecosystem & Licensing
Licensing and a mature toolchain let many vendors build diverse ARM implementations.
Summary
ARMv8-A’s architectural reset enabled efficient, scalable hardware while the licensing model multiplied commercial adoption across device classes.
