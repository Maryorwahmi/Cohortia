What is register pressure?

It's the number of live temporaries that must be held in registers simultaneously during execution.

Spilling
When pressure exceeds registers, values are stored to and reloaded from memory, adding instructions and latency.
Mitigations
  • Register reuse and renaming to shorten live ranges.
  • Partial unrolling to control live values.
  • Scheduling to reduce overlapping lifetimes.
Concrete scenario
Unrolled by 4: t1,t2,t3,t4 live simultaneously.
Available registers: 3 → spill one temporary to memory.
Spill cost: extra store + later reload per spilled value.
Warning

Spilling often erases gains from increased ILP. Measure spill counts when evaluating transforms.
