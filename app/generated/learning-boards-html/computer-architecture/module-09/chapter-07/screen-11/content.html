Module 9 › Chapter 9.7 › Loop Unrolling
Definition

Duplicate the body to expose ILP

Unrolling gives more independent instructions to schedule and reduces branch overhead, at the cost of larger code and more simultaneous live values.

Example: unroll by 4
for (i=0; i<N; i+=4) {
  sum += a[i]*b[i];
  sum += a[i+1]*b[i+1];
  sum += a[i+2]*b[i+2];
  sum += a[i+3]*b[i+3];
}
Impact details
Benefit
More instructions available to fill bundles and fewer loop-control instructions per element.
Cost
Increased code size and register pressure that may force spills to memory.
Mitigation
Partial unrolling, better register allocation, and scheduling to shorten live ranges.
Check

Measure cycles per iteration and spill counts for unroll factors of 1, 2, and 4 to find the sweet spot for your target.
