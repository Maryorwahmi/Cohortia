Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › 1-bit vs 2-bit

Loop accuracies

100 iterations
1-bit: 98% (2 mispredicts). 2-bit: 99% (1 mispredict).
10 iterations
1-bit: 80%. 2-bit: 90%.
2 iterations
1-bit: 0%. 2-bit: 50%.

Why these numbers matter

Even a 1% improvement in prediction accuracy for hot branches can reduce pipeline flushes and improve overall throughput. For short loops the relative improvement is large, which is why two-bit counters are favored in hardware.

Key Takeaway

Numbers show the practical benefit: a single extra bit often meaningfully reduces misprediction rates on loops.
