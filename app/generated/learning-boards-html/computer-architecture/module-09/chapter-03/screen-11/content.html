Techniques to reduce aliasing

XOR-based indexing
XOR the PC with a history value or other bits to spread indices more uniformly and reduce collisions.
Tagged BHT
Store a small tag per entry and verify it matches the branch; on mismatch treat entry as cold.
Multiple BHTs
Use different indexing functions or tables and select the best prediction to reduce the effect of any single collision.
Design note

Combine small tags or XOR hashing with a moderate table size to get most of the accuracy benefit without a large area increase.
