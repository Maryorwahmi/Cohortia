Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › 2-bit Predictor
State machine

Four saturating states

00 — Strongly Not Taken
01 — Weakly Not Taken
10 — Weakly Taken
11 — Strongly Taken
Prediction uses the most significant bit. Increment on taken, decrement on not-taken, with saturation at 00 and 11.
Concrete example

Loop with 100 iterations

After a few taken iterations the counter reaches strongly taken; on the single not-taken exit it mispredicts once and decays to weakly taken, which predicts taken correctly on the next entry.

Key Takeaway

Two bits prevent flip-flop behaviour at loop boundaries, reducing the misprediction count per loop.
