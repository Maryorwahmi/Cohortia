1-bit behavior
Flips on wrong guess; two mispredictions per loop exit/re-entry pattern.
2-bit benefit
Need two opposing outcomes to flip strong bias, reducing loop mispredictions to one.
Anti-aliasing
XOR indexing and tags reduce collisions so BHT entries reflect the correct branch history.
Quick summary
Small state and smart indexing often deliver most of the prediction benefit with limited hardware cost.
