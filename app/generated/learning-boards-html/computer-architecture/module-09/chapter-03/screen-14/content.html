Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Key Concepts
1-bit predictor
Single bit per entry that flips on misprediction; two mispredictions per loop exit/re-entry.
2-bit saturating counter
Four states (00,01,10,11); prediction from MSB; resists flip-flop behavior at loop boundaries.
BHT & aliasing
BHT indexed by PC bits; aliasing happens when branches share an index and corrupt each other's predictor state.
Summary takeaway
Small hardware changes like a second bit or smarter indexing yield disproportionate benefits for loop-heavy code and are a reason why 2-bit predictors are standard.
