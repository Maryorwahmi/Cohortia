Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Which branches are predictable?
Highly predictable
  • Loop branches (backward): taken repeatedly.
  • Error checks: usually not taken.
  • Induction-variable branches: regular counter-driven patterns.
Difficult to predict
  • Data-dependent branches: outcomes vary with input.
  • Indirect branches: variable targets like function pointers.
  • Correlated branches: outcome depends on other branches.
Concrete guidance
Profile hot code and categorize branches. Focus hardware or compiler efforts on the most common and least predictable hot branches. Consider software restructuring for data-dependent hotspots.
