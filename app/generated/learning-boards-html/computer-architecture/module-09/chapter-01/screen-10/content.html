Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Profiling branches in real programs
What to collect
  • Total branches executed
  • Total branch mispredictions
  • Branch misprediction rate
  • Distribution by branch type (conditional, indirect, return)
Practical tip
Compute per-hotspot CPI impact to prioritize optimization where it yields the largest runtime benefit.
Interpretation steps
  1. Compute branch frequency = branches / instructions.
  2. Compute mispredict rate = mispredictions / branches.
  3. Plug into CPI formula with assumed penalty to estimate impact.
  4. Rank hotspots by their CPI contribution and focus optimization there.
Example
If a hotspot has 40% of branches and mispredict rate 8%, with penalty 15 cycles, its CPI cost = 0.40 × 0.08 × 15 = 0.48. That hotspot dominates runtime.
