Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Profiling workflow timeline
Timeline
A five-step workflow to turn counters into decisions.
  1. Collect: Run benchmarks and record instructions, branches, mispredictions.
  2. Compute: Branch frequency and mispredict rate per hotspot.
  3. Model: Use CPI formula across assumed penalties to see sensitivity.
  4. Classify: Determine branch types and predictability per hotspot.
  5. Decide: Compare hardware cost vs. CPI savings and choose strategy.
Concrete estimate
A focused profiling pass and CPI modeling can turn into a design recommendation in a single workday for small projects.
Pitfall
Ignoring hotspot impact distribution leads to wasted effort on low-payoff branches.
