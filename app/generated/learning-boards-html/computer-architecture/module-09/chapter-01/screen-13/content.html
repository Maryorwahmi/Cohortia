Assessment: numerical CPI example
Walk through the math to see why accuracy matters.
Given:
Branch penalty = 10 cycles
Branch frequency = 0.15
Accuracy = 92% → mispredict = 0.08

CPI impact = 0.15 × 0.08 × 10 = 0.12 → CPI = 1.12

Improved accuracy = 98% → mispredict = 0.02
CPI impact = 0.15 × 0.02 × 10 = 0.03 → CPI = 1.03
Result
Improving accuracy from 92% to 98% reduces CPI by 0.09 (about 8%).

Recompute with penalty 15 cycles or frequency 20% to see sensitivity.
