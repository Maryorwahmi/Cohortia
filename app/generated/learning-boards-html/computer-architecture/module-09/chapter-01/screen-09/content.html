Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Pipeline depth and branch penalty
Pipeline depth ↔ Branch penalty
More stages usually mean larger mispredict penalties.

Designers increase depth to raise clock frequency but must account for higher branch cost.

Use CPI modeling to compare a deep pipeline with better frequency vs. a shallow pipeline with lower penalty.

Example sensitivity: doubling penalty doubles its contribution to CPI for the same frequency and mispredict rate.

Design question
Would a shallower pipeline outperform a deeper one on your workload if mispredicts are common?
Concrete effect
With branch frequency 20% and mispredict 5%: penalty 10 cycles → extra CPI 0.10. Penalty 20 cycles → extra CPI 0.20. That difference can overwhelm a modest clock improvement.
Penalty=10
Extra CPI = 0.20 × 0.05 × 10 = 0.10
Penalty=20
Extra CPI = 0.20 × 0.05 × 20 = 0.20
