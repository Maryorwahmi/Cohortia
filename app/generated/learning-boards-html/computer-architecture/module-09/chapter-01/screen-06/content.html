CPI formula
Translate profiler numbers into CPI impact.
CPI = Base CPI + Branch_Frequency × Mispredict_Rate × Branch_Penalty

Example:
Base CPI = 1.0
Branch_Frequency = 0.20  (1 branch every 5 instructions)
Mispredict_Rate = 0.05  (95% accuracy)
Branch_Penalty = 20 cycles

CPI = 1.0 + 0.20 × 0.05 × 20 = 1.2

Interpretation: a 5% mispredict rate costs an extra 0.2 CPI in this example, a 20% relative slowdown. Reducing mispredict rate to 2% cuts the extra CPI to 0.08.

Practical step: plug your profiler's branch frequency and mispredict numbers into this formula and vary the penalty to model different pipeline depths.

Key Takeaway
Use the formula to quantify trade-offs and justify predictor complexity numerically.
Worked calculation
We compare 5% vs 2% mispredict with the same frequency and penalty to show relative gains. This demonstrates why designers target even small accuracy improvements for branch-heavy workloads.
