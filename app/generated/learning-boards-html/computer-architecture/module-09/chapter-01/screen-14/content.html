In-order vs Out-of-order
Choose in-order for simplicity and predictable code; choose out-of-order when latency hiding and instruction-level parallelism matter.

In-order: lower area and power, better fit for static prediction and shallow pipelines.

Out-of-order: can hide some stalls but still flushes on misprediction; combines well with dynamic prediction.

Pipeline depth decision
Deeper pipelines raise clock but increase branch penalty; balance depends on mispredict exposure.

Model CPI under expected mispredict rates when choosing depth. If mispredict costs dominate, reducing depth may beat higher frequency.

Concrete step
Compute energy-delay or performance-per-area for alternative microarchitectures using measured branch metrics to choose the best trade-off.
