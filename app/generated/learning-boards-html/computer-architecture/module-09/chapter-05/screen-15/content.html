Indirect branch techniques
Target caches, path-history indexing, and virtual call speculation supply multi-target selection logic.
Return Address Stack
Push on call, pop on return, handle overflow/underflow and speculative updates with checkpointing.
Limits of prediction
Some branches are inherently unpredictable; aim to minimize mispredictions on the predictable subset.
Final takeaway
Designers balance accuracy, latency, area, and power. Understand workload characteristics to pick the right predictor complexity.
