Key Concepts › Part 1
TAGE predictor
Multiple tagged tables with geometric history lengths and a base predictor.
Tag matching & usefulness
Tagged entries and useful counters guide selection and replacement to preserve helpful history.
Perceptron predictors
Weighted sums of history bits capture linear correlations at higher compute and storage cost.
Summary takeaway
High accuracy requires carefully matched hardware: choose predictor style based on workload predictability, latency and area constraints.
