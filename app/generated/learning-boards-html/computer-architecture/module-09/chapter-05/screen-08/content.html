Research › Neural Predictors
What researchers try
• Multi-layer perceptrons to capture non-linear patterns.
• Recurrent networks to model long-range sequential dependencies.
• Hybrid schemes that combine fast tables with occasional heavy models offline.
Concrete contrast
Perceptron: one dot product. LSTM: gates and sequential state updates. The latter is expensive in latency and power for fetch-stage prediction.
Practical barriers
• Latency: fetch-stage predictions must be fast.
• Power: complex networks increase energy per prediction.
• Area: storage for weights and compute units grows quickly.
Key Takeaway
Neural predictors can model complex patterns but are limited by hardware cost; they remain mainly in research and simulation studies.
Misconception
Assuming neural nets are the obvious next step; practical constraints still favor lighter predictors.
Pro tip
Use neural models in simulation to explore trade-offs before committing to hardware designs.
Check
Name one reason why a complex neural predictor might not be feasible at fetch time.
