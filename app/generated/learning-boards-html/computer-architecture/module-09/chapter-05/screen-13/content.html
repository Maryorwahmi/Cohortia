Assessment › Why TAGE & RAS
Question 1
Why multiple geometric history lengths instead of a single long history?
• Geometric tables give near-optimal depth per branch without enormous storage.
• A single long table wastes entries and increases aliasing for short-history branches.
Question 2
Why is RAS better than BTB for returns?
• Returns depend on the caller's address, not the return instruction address.
• RAS pushes call-site return addresses and pops them on return, matching LIFO behavior and providing correct targets.
