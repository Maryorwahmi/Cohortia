Predication
Execute both paths and use predicates to choose the result.
Compiler speculation
Speculation in EPIC is explicit and validated by checks, unlike hardware speculation in OoO cores.
IA-64 lesson
Architecture and compiler must align with workloads; Itanium's mismatch limited adoption.
One-line summary

Use predication selectively: it removes mispredictions when both paths are short and compilers can manage the register and scheduling costs.
