Module 10 › Chapter 9.8 › Key Concepts
Predication
Replace branches with conditional instructions guarded by predicates.
Predicate registers
Small Boolean registers control instruction execution; IA-64 provided 64 of them.
Speculation & checks
VLIW speculation is compiler-driven and validated by check instructions.
IA-64 features
Bundles, template bits, rotating registers, large register files for software pipelining.
Failure causes
Itanium struggled due to compiler limits, x86 incompatibility, memory-latency, and branch-heavy workloads.
Design lesson
Shifting complexity between hardware and compiler must align with ecosystem and workloads.
Summary

Predication is a useful tool but must be chosen carefully; IA-64 shows the limits of relying primarily on compilers for parallelism.
