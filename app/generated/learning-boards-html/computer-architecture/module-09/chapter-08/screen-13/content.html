Module 10 › Chapter 9.8 › EPIC vs VLIW vs OoO

Where the complexity lives

EPIC/VLIW: complexity in the compiler for scheduling, speculation and register allocation. OoO: complexity in hardware for dynamic scheduling, speculation, and recovery.
EPIC advantages
Precise compiler control, potential for high ILP on regular code.
EPIC risks
Requires advanced compilers and compatible workloads to succeed.

Comparison cards

VLIW
Fixed slots; compiler must pack instructions. Simpler hardware, heavy compiler burden.
EPIC (IA-64)
Adds templates, predicates, rotating registers and checks to help compilers express parallelism and latency.
Out-of-order
Hardware dynamically extracts ILP and recovers from mis-speculation; more silicon complexity, less compiler burden.
Key Takeaway

Choose architecture by matching workload regularity and compiler capability: EPIC for predictable, OoO for diverse and branchy code.
