What to Remember
Global History (GHR)
Records recent branch outcomes to provide context for correlated predictions.
Gshare
Index = PC XOR GHR; uses 2-bit counters to capture patterns conditioned on recent history.
Tournament
Combine local and global predictors with a meta-selector that learns per branch.
Summary: GHR, table design, selector behavior, BTB and RAS sizing are the levers you can tune to improve accuracy versus cost.
