Definition Indirect Branches & BTB Limits
Why indirects are tricky
  • • Target comes from a register or memory, not a PC-relative offset.
  • • Many possible targets lead to BTB thrash and increased misses.
  • • BTB misses cause fetch delays since the target must be resolved.
Practical scenario
A virtual dispatch site with many derived classes produces diversified targets. A small BTB will capture only the hottest targets and will miss on less-common ones, increasing misprediction penalties.
Measure: track BTB hit rate per indirect branch and inspect the distribution of targets to decide if a larger BTB or specialized indirect predictor is warranted.
