Example bundles

Concrete bundle examples

Bundles show how the compiler filled slots and where it inserted NOPs when independent operations weren't available.

Bundle 1: ADD R1,R2,R3  | MUL F4,F5,F6  | LW R7,0(R8)  | NOP  | NOP
Bundle 2: SUB R9,R1,R10  | NOP            | NOP          | NOP  | BEQ R1,R2,target
Reading the example

Bundle 1 uses three slots with two empty. Bundle 2 schedules a branch in the final slot and leaves other slots empty, perhaps because there were no independent ops available.

Checklist when you inspect bundles
  • Mark producer-consumer dependencies.
  • Verify latencies to avoid early consumers.
  • Count functional unit usage per cycle.
Key Takeaway

Example bundles reveal utilization and scheduling choices; NOPs are the clearest sign of limited ILP for that code.
