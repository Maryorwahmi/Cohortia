Bundle anatomy
Count slots and unit types; schedule only independent ops into each.
Fill slots
Measure utilization and apply pipelining, unrolling, predication to reduce NOPs.
Trade-offs
VLIW: simpler, power-efficient, but compiler-heavy. OoO: complex, adaptive, compatible.
Final reflection

When would you choose VLIW over a superscalar design? Favor VLIW for predictable, high-ILP workloads and constrained power/area budgets; otherwise prefer out-of-order cores.
