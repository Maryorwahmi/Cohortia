Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › TSO Outcome Reasoning
Step-by-step comparison
Key constraints
  • Store-store ordering preserved globally.
  • Load may bypass earlier local stores to other addresses.
Implication
If S2 is visible, S1 must also be visible because S1 precedes S2 on P1.
Consequence

R1=1,R2=0 cannot occur on TSO; store-store ordering prevents that weak outcome.

Misconception
Store buffering means everything can reorder — not true for store-store ordering under TSO.
Check
Enumerate the global order preserving per-thread orders; you will see S1 cannot be after S2.
Takeaway
TSO gives strong intuitive guarantees while enabling buffering optimizations.
