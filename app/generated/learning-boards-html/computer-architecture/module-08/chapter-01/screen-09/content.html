Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Weak / Relaxed Models
Relaxed ordering for performance
ARM, Power and many RISC-V variants expose weak guarantees to enable hardware optimizations.
Allowed reorderings
  • Load-load reordering may occur.
  • Store-store reordering may occur.
  • Load-store and store-load reorderings are commonly allowed.
Only same-address and data-dependency orderings are commonly preserved without fences.
Programming consequences
  • Fences are required to prevent reordering in synchronization code.
  • Compilers and libraries must map higher-level synchronization to correct fences and atomics.
Lock-free algorithms often need careful fence placement on weak models.
Analogy
Intersections with no lights: drivers can pass unless a cop (fence) stops them.
Example caution
Double-checked locking requires fences to avoid publication before construction finishes.
Takeaway
Weak models give speed but make correctness rely on explicit synchronization.
