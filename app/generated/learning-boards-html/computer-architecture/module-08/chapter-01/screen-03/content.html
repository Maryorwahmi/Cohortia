Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Learning Objectives — Applied
How you will practice
A focused set of tasks that exercise tracing, simulation, and fence placement.
Trace examples
Step through interleavings and label when stores become globally visible.
Write a simulator
Enumerate outcomes for SC, TSO and a weak model and compare results.
Fences and fixes
Insert store or full fences to remove unsafe outcomes and confirm with the simulator.
Concurrency patterns
Analyze double-checked locking and show where ordering matters for safety.
Key Takeaway

Active practice with tracing and a simulator reveals which reorderings matter and how fences restore correctness.
