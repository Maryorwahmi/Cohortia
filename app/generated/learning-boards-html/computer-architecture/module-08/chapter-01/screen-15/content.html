Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Quick Reference
Store buffers
Buffers allow local progress but change visibility timing to other cores.
Dependency & same-address
These are common preserved orderings on weak ISAs; rely on them cautiously.
Fences & atomics
Use atomic acquire/release or fences to enforce the minimal ordering needed for correctness.
Actionable question

Before assuming ordering in concurrent code, ask: which model applies and which fences or atomics do I need to ensure safety?
