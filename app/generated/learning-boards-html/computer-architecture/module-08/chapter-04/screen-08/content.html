Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › DRAM Access Timing
Step timeline
Activate (ACT)
Open row into row buffer. ~15 ns.
Read/Write (CAS)
Access column data from row buffer. ~15 ns.
Precharge (PRE)
Close row and prepare bank for next ACT. ~15 ns.
Illustrative comparison
A row hit: single CAS (~15 ns). A row miss in same bank: PRE + ACT + CAS (~45 ns). This gap explains why locality and controller scheduling have outsized effects on observed latency and throughput.
