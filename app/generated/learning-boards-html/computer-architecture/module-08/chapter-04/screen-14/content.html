Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › DDR vs HBM vs LPDDR
DDR
Lower per-byte cost, higher capacity, moderate bandwidth, used for general-purpose systems.
HBM
3D-stacked high bandwidth via TSVs. Much higher GB/s per stack, higher cost and thermal density, used for GPUs and accelerators.
LPDDR
Low-power DDR for mobile devices, balancing bandwidth with energy efficiency and thermal constraints.
Real-world decision
Choose HBM when sustained, very high bandwidth is critical and cost/thermal constraints are acceptable. Choose DDR when capacity and cost matter more. Use LPDDR for power-limited mobile designs.
