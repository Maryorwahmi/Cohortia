Components

Compression, decompression, and management

  • Compression unit: encodes lines on write, may add write latency.
  • Decompression unit: reconstructs data on read within tight cycles.
  • Variable-size block management: tracks sizes, offsets and free fragments.
Why it matters

These elements change replacement, coherence, and the cache's critical path; design choices determine performance wins or losses.

Concrete cards
Variable-size example

A set holds compressed entries of different byte lengths; the controller tracks offsets and free fragments for allocation.

Common mistake

Ignoring metadata overhead; size bits and management tables consume area and must be counted in the design.

Pro tip

Favor encoders that decode in a few ALU ops or table lookups to stay within the decompression budget.
