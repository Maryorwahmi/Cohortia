Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Dictionary-based Compression
Core Idea

Replace repeated substrings with dictionary references

Maintain a dictionary of common substrings and encode references to them, yielding strong compression for repeated sequences.

Why powerful

Captures repeated substrings across a line, not just whole-line templates or numeric deltas.

Key Takeaway

High compression but often too costly for ultra-low-latency cache paths due to matching and maintenance overhead.

Concrete cards
Example

Text with repeated words or binary blobs with repeated substrings compress well with a dictionary.

Pitfall

Dictionary lookups and updates add hardware complexity and latency.

Where to use

Better suited for main-memory or software compression where latency budgets are larger.
