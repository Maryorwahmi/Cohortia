Compress only compressible data
Measure compressibility before enabling heavy encoders.
Match encoding to pattern
ZVC, FPC, or BDI pick different data shapes; choose accordingly.
Place data for locality
First-touch for regular locality; round-robin for bandwidth balance.
Final reflection

Before adding cache compression, profile compressibility and decompression cost. For NUMA, profile access locality and pick the placement policy that yields the most local hits for your workload.
