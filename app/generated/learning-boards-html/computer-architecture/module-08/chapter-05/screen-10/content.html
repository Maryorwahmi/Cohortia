Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Compression Trade-offs
Comparison

Ratio · Latency · Complexity

ZVC

Low complexity, minimal latency, limited ratio.

BDI

Good for numeric arrays, low decode cost, moderate ratio.

Dictionary

High ratio potential but higher latency and complexity.

Practical rule

Prefer the simplest encoder that captures the majority of your workload's compressible patterns.

Concrete example
1–2 cycle budget

If decompression exceeds a couple cycles, the cache hit time rises and benefits may vanish.

Misconception

Higher compression ratio alone guarantees better performance — not true when latency and area are factored in.

Decision tip

Profile compressibility and measure decoding cycles before selecting an encoder for hardware delivery.
