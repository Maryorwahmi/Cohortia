Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Key Concepts
Cache compression
Store compressed lines to increase effective capacity.
Key techniques
ZVC, FPC, BDI and dictionary-based methods with different costs and benefits.
Trade-offs
Balance compression ratio against decompression latency and hardware complexity.
Hardware units
Compression and decompression units plus variable-size block tracking.
Data-dependent
Encrypted or random data will not compress; detect and fallback gracefully.
NUMA placement
First-touch, round-robin, interleave and migration shape locality and bandwidth.
Summary takeaway

Match compression algorithms to your workload's patterns and keep decompression cost within the cache latency budget; choose NUMA placement based on access locality and bandwidth needs.
