Comparing MPX and MTE
Look beyond coverage and inspect the costs of adoption: runtime overhead, compiler and OS changes, and compatibility with existing binaries.
Intel MPX
Provided explicit bounds registers and checks but required extensive toolchain work and produced noticeable overhead.
ARM MTE
Uses small tags and allocator cooperation to catch common bugs with very low overhead and better backward compatibility.
Assessment idea
Ask learners why MPX failed to reach wide deployment and have them argue for MTE given a target use case such as a mobile OS.
