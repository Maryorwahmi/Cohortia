NoC design trade-offs
Router latency, link width, buffers and power must be balanced for target workloads
Router microarchitecture
Aim for 1–2 cycle routing latency, efficient arbitration and minimum buffer cost to meet area limits.
Power and area
Routers and links consume silicon area and power. Techniques like clock gating and voltage scaling reduce dynamic consumption.
Practical hint
Profile traffic and match router/link resources to realistic use cases rather than peak theoretical loads.
Design checklist
  • Choose topology that maps to floorplan.
  • Pick routing that avoids deadlock with minimal complexity.
  • Decide flit size and link width for expected traffic.
  • Balance buffers vs area; consider virtual channels for performance.
