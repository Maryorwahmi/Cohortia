Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Key Concepts
Packet switching
Packets with headers enable hop-by-hop routing and concurrent flows.
Wormhole & virtual channels
Wormhole reduces buffers; virtual channels reduce head-of-line blocking and help avoid deadlock.
Deadlock avoidance
Use acyclic routing or channel separation to prevent cycles of waiting that stall the network.
Final reflection

As you choose topology and routing, weigh hop counts, bisection bandwidth and buffer costs. Ask: what topology fits a 256-core design given your area and power constraints?
