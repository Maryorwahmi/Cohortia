Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Key Concepts
Interconnect role
Connects cores, caches and memory; often the system bottleneck.
Topology trade-offs
Bus: simple; ring: moderate; mesh/torus: scalable; crossbar: high cost.
Bisection bandwidth
Minimum capacity across a cut; key metric for aggregate throughput.
Summary takeaway

Match topology and routing to workload scale and locality. Consider link width, buffers and router latency when balancing performance and silicon cost.
