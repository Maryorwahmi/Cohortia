Interconnect Topologies and Routing
How choices in topology and routing shape latency, bandwidth and scalability
What you'll understand

Why the interconnect is often the system bottleneck, how common topologies trade off cost and performance, and why routing and flow control matter for on-chip networks.

In this lesson
Topologies, packet routing, flow control, deadlock avoidance, and NoC trade-offs for manycore chips.
