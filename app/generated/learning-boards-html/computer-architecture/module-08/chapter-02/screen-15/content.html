Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › What to Remember
Per-block metadata
State, sharers, owner are sufficient to route coherence traffic without broadcast.
Compression matters
Coarse vectors, limited pointers, hierarchical directories reduce storage at a messaging cost.
Hybrid approach
Snooping inside sockets, directory between sockets is often the practical compromise.
Action

Run the hands-on simulation and count messages for your target system size — that empirical check decides the best coherence approach.
