Safety & Planning

Best Practices for EOS Upgrades

Mitigate risks and ensure a smooth upgrade process with careful planning.

Schedule Maintenance Window

Perform upgrades during periods of low network activity to minimize impact.

Inform Stakeholders

Communicate planned downtime and potential impact to all relevant parties.

Ensure Console Access

Maintain direct console access for troubleshooting in case of network connectivity loss.

Consequences of Interruption

  • •Corrupted System: Interrupting an upgrade can lead to an unbootable or unstable device.
  • •Extended Downtime: Recovery from a corrupted system can be complex and time-consuming.
  • •Data Loss: Potential loss of configuration or operational data if not properly backed up.
Key Takeaway

Execute EOS upgrades during maintenance windows with stakeholder notification and console access to mitigate risks and ensure recovery options.
