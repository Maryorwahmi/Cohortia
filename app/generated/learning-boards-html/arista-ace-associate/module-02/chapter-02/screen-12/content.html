Memory Management

Running-config vs. Startup-config

Understanding active vs. persistent configurations on your switch.

Running-config

  • Active configuration in RAM.
  • Changes apply immediately.
  • Lost on reboot/power loss if not saved.
  • Temporary.

Startup-config

  • Configuration stored in flash memory.
  • Loaded upon switch reboot.
  • Persistent across reboots.
  • Permanent.
Key Takeaway

Always save your running-config to startup-config to prevent losing changes after a reboot.

Analogy

Running-config is like an open document you're editing; startup-config is the saved version on your hard drive.

Common Mistake

Forgetting to save changes to startup-config is a frequent and frustrating error, leading to lost configurations after a power cycle.

Why it Matters

Ensures that your network device consistently boots up with the desired configuration, maintaining network stability and functionality.
