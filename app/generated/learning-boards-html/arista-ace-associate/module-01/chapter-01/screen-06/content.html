Scalability and Operational Complexities

Oversubscription and East-West Traffic Challenges

Oversubscription Explained

When the total bandwidth demand from connected devices exceeds the available uplink bandwidth, creating a bottleneck. This was common in aggregation and core layers.

Impacts performance

East-West Traffic Growth

Increased communication between servers within the data center, driven by virtualization and distributed applications. Traditional designs were optimized for North-South traffic.

Bottlenecked by hierarchy

Operational Headaches

  • Complex Layer 2 domain management across multiple switches.
  • Cumbersome network segmentation and troubleshooting.
  • Static designs struggled with dynamic application requirements.
Key Takeaway

Traditional networks were ill-equipped to handle the demands of modern virtualization, east-west traffic, and the need for rapid, agile deployments.
