Industry Shift

Driving Forces for Network Evolution

The Rise of Cloud Computing and Agility Demands

Cloud Computing Adoption

The move to public, private, and hybrid cloud models required networks that could scale horizontally and handle dynamic workloads.

Elasticity & Scale

Demand for Agility

Businesses needed faster application deployment, quicker resource provisioning, and the ability to adapt to market changes rapidly.

Rapid Deployment

Automation Imperative

Reducing manual configuration errors and accelerating operational tasks became critical for efficiency and reliability.

Operational Efficiency

East-West Traffic Dominance

With virtualization, server-to-server communication became the dominant traffic pattern, requiring optimized internal data center paths.

Internal Communication
Key Takeaway

The demands of cloud computing and agile business operations rendered traditional network designs obsolete, paving the way for new architectures.
