Equal-Cost Multi-Pathing (ECMP)

Utilizing All Available Paths for High Performance

ECMP Advantages

  • Full Bandwidth Utilization: All links are active and carry traffic simultaneously.
  • Inherent Redundancy: Traffic automatically reroutes upon link failure without delay.
  • Load Balancing: Distributes traffic evenly across multiple paths, preventing congestion.
  • Simplified Design: Eliminates the need for complex Layer 2 redundancy protocols.
Key Takeaway

ECMP is vital for modern data centers, providing superior bandwidth utilization and fault tolerance by actively using all available network paths.
