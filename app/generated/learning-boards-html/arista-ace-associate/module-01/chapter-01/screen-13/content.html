Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Modern Networking

Innovations driving cloud-scale data centers

East-West Traffic

Network traffic flowing between servers within the same data center, dominant in virtualized environments.

Leaf-Spine Architecture

A modern, non-blocking topology where leaf switches connect to servers and spine switches.

Layer 3 Everywhere

A design principle where routing (Layer 3) is enabled on all switches, including the access layer.

Equal-Cost Multi-Pathing (ECMP)

A routing feature that uses multiple paths of equal cost to reach a destination, distributing traffic and providing redundancy.

Chapter Takeaway

Modern data center networks leverage leaf-spine architectures, Layer 3 everywhere, and ECMP to achieve the scalability, resilience, and agility required by cloud-scale environments.
