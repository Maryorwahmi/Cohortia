Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) Limitations

Blocking Redundant Paths and Wasting Bandwidth

How STP Impacts Networks

  • Loop Prevention: STP's primary role is to create a loop-free Layer 2 topology.
  • Blocked Paths: Achieves loop prevention by disabling redundant physical links.
  • Wasted Bandwidth: Active links are underutilized, as redundant paths sit idle.
  • Slow Convergence: Network changes or failures can lead to significant downtime during STP recalculations.
Key Takeaway

STP ensures Layer 2 stability but severely limits bandwidth utilization and network resilience, making it unsuitable for modern, high-demand data centers.
