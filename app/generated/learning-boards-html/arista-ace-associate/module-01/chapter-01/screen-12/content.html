Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Traditional Networking

Foundational ideas from the evolution of data centers

Three-Tier Architecture

A traditional hierarchical network design with Core, Aggregation, and Access layers.

Spanning Tree Protocol (STP)

Prevents loops in Layer 2 networks by blocking redundant paths, leading to underutilized bandwidth.

Oversubscription

When total bandwidth demand exceeds available uplink bandwidth, creating a potential bottleneck.

Chapter Takeaway

Traditional data center networks, while structured, were limited by hierarchical designs, STP's inefficiencies, and oversubscription challenges, paving the way for modern solutions.
