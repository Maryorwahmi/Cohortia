Introducing the Leaf-Spine Architecture

A Flat, Non-Blocking Fabric for Data Centers

Key Characteristics

  • Leaf Switches: Connect directly to servers (access layer).
  • Spine Switches: Interconnect all leaf switches (aggregation/core layer).
  • No Direct Connections: No leaf-to-leaf or spine-to-spine links.
  • Non-Blocking: Ensures predictable latency and high bandwidth between any two points.
Key Takeaway

The leaf-spine architecture creates a highly efficient, scalable, and resilient network fabric by connecting every leaf to every spine switch.
