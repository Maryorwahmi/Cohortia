Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember from This Chapter

Arista Networks

A networking vendor specializing in high-performance, low-latency, cloud-scale data center and campus networking solutions.

Extensible Operating System (EOS)

Arista's Linux-based, modular network operating system known for its stability, programmability, and open APIs.

Sysdb

The multi-process state-sharing architecture within EOS that enhances fault isolation and system resilience.

Chapter Summary

Arista's specialized focus on high-performance data centers, powered by the modular and resilient EOS with its Sysdb architecture, forms the core of their innovative networking solutions.
