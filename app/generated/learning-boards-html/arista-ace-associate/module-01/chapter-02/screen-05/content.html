Introducing Arista's Extensible Operating System (EOS)

A Groundbreaking Linux-Based Network OS

Linux Kernel Foundation

EOS is built on a standard Linux kernel, providing inherent stability, a familiar environment for developers, and access to a vast ecosystem of Linux tools directly on the switch.

Designed for Modern Networks

EOS was engineered from the ground up for resilience, programmability, and automation, addressing the demanding requirements of cloud-scale environments.

Open and Extensible

Its open architecture allows for rapid innovation and seamless integration with other technologies, transforming network devices into programmable infrastructure components.

Why Linux Matters

The Linux kernel provides a robust, well-understood, and highly stable foundation. This means fewer bugs, better security, and a wealth of existing tools and expertise that can be directly applied to network operations, accelerating development and troubleshooting.

Traditional OS vs. EOS

Traditional network OSes often use monolithic codebases, where a single fault can bring down the entire system. EOS's Linux foundation and modularity prevent this, allowing individual processes to fail and restart without impacting data forwarding.

Key Takeaway

EOS is a Linux-based, modular operating system that provides a stable, programmable, and open foundation for high-performance networking, differentiating Arista from traditional vendors.
