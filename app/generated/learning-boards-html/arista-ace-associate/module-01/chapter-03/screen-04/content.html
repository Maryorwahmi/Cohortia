Core Concept

Cloud-Scale Networking

A fundamental shift in network design, operation, and management to meet the demands of modern, distributed applications.

Key Characteristics
  • Massive horizontal scalability
  • Pervasive automation & programmability
  • Robust resiliency & predictable performance
  • Dynamic accommodation of fluctuating demands
Key Takeaway

Cloud networking is built for agility and dynamic growth, contrasting with the static nature of traditional networks.

Traditional vs. Cloud Networks
Traditional Networks
  • Static, predictable workloads
  • Manual configuration
  • Limited horizontal scalability
  • Slower deployments
Cloud Networks
  • Dynamic, distributed applications
  • API-first, automated
  • Massive horizontal scalability
  • Rapid, agile deployments
