Ethernet VPN (EVPN)

A control plane technology, often used with VXLAN, that uses BGP to distribute MAC and IP address information for virtual networks.

EVPN's Role
  • Provides an efficient control plane for VXLAN.
  • Uses BGP to distribute MAC and IP reachability.
  • Enables scalable multi-tenancy and VM mobility.
  • Avoids traditional flood-and-learn mechanisms.
Key Takeaway

EVPN, combined with VXLAN, offers a highly scalable and flexible solution for Layer 2 extension over a Layer 3 underlay, essential for cloud environments.

EVPN Control Plane Process
1. VM Connects to Leaf

Leaf learns VM's MAC/IP.

2. Leaf Advertises via BGP

Distributes MAC/IP to other VTEPs.

3. Other VTEPs Learn

VTEPs update their forwarding tables.
