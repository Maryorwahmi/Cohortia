Critical Considerations

Visibility and Microsegmentation

Essential for securing and monitoring highly dynamic and distributed cloud workloads.

Visibility Needs
  • Real-time telemetry and advanced analytics.
  • Granular insight into traffic flows.
  • Rapid identification of performance issues.
  • Detection of security threats.
Key Takeaway

Deep visibility and microsegmentation are vital for maintaining performance and limiting the blast radius of security breaches in cloud networks.

Microsegmentation in Action
Granular Policy Enforcement

Security policies applied at the individual workload level (VMs, containers).

Isolation of Workloads

Creates isolated security zones, limiting lateral movement of threats.

Dynamic Policy Application

Integrates with orchestration platforms to apply rules based on workload identity.
