Accelerating Convergence with RSTP

Overcoming traditional STP's slow transition times.

RSTP vs. Traditional STP

  • ●
    Faster Convergence: RSTP transitions ports to forwarding in milliseconds, compared to ~50 seconds for traditional STP.
  • ●
    New Port Roles: Introduces Alternate and Backup roles for quicker failover.
  • ●
    Simplified States: Uses Discarding, Learning, Forwarding states, streamlining the process.
  • ●
    Rapid PVST+: Arista EOS defaults to this enhancement, running per-VLAN STP instances.
Why it Matters

In today's always-on world, network downtime is costly. RSTP's rapid convergence ensures applications remain available and user experience is minimally impacted during topology changes.
