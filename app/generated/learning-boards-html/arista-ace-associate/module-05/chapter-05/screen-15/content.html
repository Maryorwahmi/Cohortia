Key Concepts
Essential ideas to remember from this chapter
Equal-Cost Multi-Path (ECMP)

A Layer 3 routing technique for load balancing and redundancy across multiple equal-cost paths to a destination.

Bidirectional Forwarding Detection (BFD)

A lightweight protocol for rapid, millisecond-level detection of failures in the forwarding path between adjacent network devices.

Physical Redundancy

Redundancy at the hardware level, including redundant power supplies, fans, and multiple physical links, forming the HA foundation.

Chapter Takeaway

Mastering ECMP, BFD, and physical redundancy is fundamental for building and maintaining highly available network infrastructures.
