Designing Highly Available Networks
A holistic approach to resilience
Holistic Approach

HA design requires considering all layers of the network, from physical infrastructure to logical configurations and operational processes. It's not just about one protocol, but a comprehensive strategy.

Physical Redundancy Pillars
  • Redundant Power Supplies: Ensures device operation if one PSU fails.
  • Dual Uplinks: Connects devices to multiple upstream switches/routers.
  • Geographically Diverse Paths: Protects against localized physical damage (e.g., cable cuts).
Why Physical Redundancy is Paramount

No amount of software redundancy can overcome a single point of physical failure. If a device loses power or its only uplink is cut, the network segment will go down regardless of configured protocols. It's the first line of defense.

Common Mistake: Overlooking power or cabling redundancy.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize physical redundancy in all critical network components and paths to establish a robust foundation for high availability.
