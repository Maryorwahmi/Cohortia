Internal HA Features in Arista EOS
Beyond protocols: hardware and service redundancy
Hardware Redundancy
  • Supervisor Card Redundancy: In modular chassis, active/standby supervisor engines ensure control plane continuity.
  • Power Supply/Fan Redundancy: Standard across many devices to prevent single points of failure.
Service Redundancy

Arista EOS can provide stateful firewall HA for security services. This ensures that if one firewall instance fails, another can seamlessly take over, maintaining active connections and security policies.

Integrated Approach to HA

These internal features demonstrate that HA is not just a collection of protocols, but a deeply integrated philosophy within Arista EOS, encompassing both hardware and software layers to maximize uptime and resilience.

Example: A modular switch with dual supervisors and redundant power supplies.
Key Takeaway

Arista EOS provides robust internal HA features that complement external protocols, offering comprehensive resilience from hardware to services.
