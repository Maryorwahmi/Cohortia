Bidirectional Forwarding Detection (BFD)
Accelerating failure detection in network paths
What is BFD?

BFD is a lightweight, low-overhead protocol designed for rapid detection of failures in the forwarding path between two adjacent network devices. It operates independently of the media and routing protocols.

Why it's Critical
  • Millisecond Detection: Detects failures much faster than traditional routing protocol hello timers.
  • Minimizes Downtime: Rapid notification allows associated protocols to converge quickly.
  • Protocol Agnostic: Can be integrated with OSPF, BGP, VRRP, and other routing/FHRPs.
Traditional vs. BFD

Traditional routing protocols often have hello timers in the seconds range (e.g., 10-30 seconds). BFD, by contrast, can be configured to send hello packets and detect failures in as little as 50 milliseconds, drastically reducing outage duration.

Example: OSPF hello timer is 10s, BFD can detect failure in 50ms.
Key Takeaway

BFD is essential for mission-critical networks requiring sub-second convergence and minimal service disruption.
