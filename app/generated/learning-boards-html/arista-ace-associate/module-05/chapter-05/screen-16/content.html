Key Concepts Continued
Further essential ideas for resilient networks
Logical Redundancy

Redundancy achieved through software configurations and protocols like LACP, MLAG, STP, and VRRP.

Proactive Monitoring

Continuously observing network health and protocol states to identify and address potential issues before they cause outages.

Change Management

A structured process for introducing changes to a network, including planning, testing, implementation, and rollback procedures.

Chapter Takeaway

A holistic HA strategy integrates logical redundancy, proactive monitoring, and disciplined change management for robust network operations.
