High Availability: A Holistic View
Combining design, protocols, monitoring, and operations
Key Pillars of HA
  • Robust Design: Eliminating single points of failure at all layers.
  • Intelligent Protocols: LACP, MLAG, VRRP, ECMP, BFD for redundancy and fast convergence.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Proactive detection of issues before they cause outages.
  • Disciplined Operations: Change management, backups, and clear documentation.
Why it's a Strategy

Relying on a single HA feature is insufficient. A truly resilient network integrates multiple layers of redundancy and operational best practices to ensure maximum uptime and business continuity.

The Goal: Uninterrupted Service

The ultimate objective of high availability is to provide uninterrupted network services, even in the face of component failures or unexpected events. This directly translates to improved user experience and business productivity.

Example: A data center network designed for 'five nines' (99.999%) availability.
Key Takeaway

High availability is a holistic, multi-layered strategy that combines technology and operational discipline to build resilient, performant networks.
