Change Management in HA Environments
Minimizing risk during network modifications
The Importance of Change Control

Any modification, however small, can potentially destabilize a highly available system. Strict change control procedures are essential to mitigate risks and prevent unintended outages.

Key Change Management Steps
  • Plan Meticulously: Detail every step and expected outcome.
  • Test in Lab: Validate changes in a non-production environment.
  • Have Rollback Plans: Be prepared to revert if issues arise.
Impact Awareness

Understand the potential consequences of changes. Modifying an MLAG peer link can affect synchronization, while adjusting VRRP priorities can cause unexpected traffic shifts. Always consider the ripple effect.

Example: Changing VRRP priority without understanding current active router.
Key Takeaway

Rigorous change management is a cornerstone of HA operations, preventing disruptions and ensuring network stability during modifications.
