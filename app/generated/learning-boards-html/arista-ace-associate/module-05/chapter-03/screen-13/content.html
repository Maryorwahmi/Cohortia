Common MLAG Misconfigurations and Troubleshooting Techniques

Diagnosing and resolving MLAG issues

Key Troubleshooting Areas

Identifying and resolving MLAG issues requires a systematic approach, focusing on the core components and their interactions.

  • •Peer Link Issues: Verify peer link formation, allowed VLANs, and physical connectivity.
  • •Mismatched MLAG IDs: Ensure corresponding port-channels on both peers have identical MLAG IDs.
  • •IP Reachability: Confirm IP connectivity between the `peer-address` loopback interfaces.
  • •Split-Brain Scenario: Understand the risks of peer link failure and ensure robust design.
Key Takeaway

Proactive monitoring and systematic troubleshooting of MLAG components are vital to prevent network disruptions and ensure high availability.

Troubleshooting Flow
Check show mlag output

Look for 'MLAG State: Up' and 'Peer Status: Active'.

Verify Peer Link & Peer Address

Ensure IP reachability between peer addresses and peer link is up and configured correctly.

Inspect MLAG Interfaces

Confirm `mlag ` is present and IDs match on both peers for downstream port-channels.

Example Scenario

If `show mlag` shows 'Peer Status: Inactive', check `ping 10.0.0.2` from Arista-SW1 to verify peer address reachability.
