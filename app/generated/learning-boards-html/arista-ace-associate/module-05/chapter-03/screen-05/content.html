Introducing Multi-Chassis Link Aggregation (MLAG)

Achieving switch-level redundancy with a logical switch

Two Switches, One Logical Entity

MLAG allows two separate physical Arista switches to appear as a single logical switch to a downstream device. This enables a server or another switch to form a single LAG across both, providing superior redundancy.

  • •Switch-Level Redundancy: Eliminates the single point of failure present in traditional LAGs.
  • •Seamless Failover: If one MLAG peer fails, the other takes over traffic forwarding without disruption.
  • •Active-Active Forwarding: All links in the bundle can actively forward traffic, maximizing bandwidth utilization.
Key Takeaway

MLAG provides both link-level and switch-level redundancy, ensuring continuous availability and efficient bandwidth use.
