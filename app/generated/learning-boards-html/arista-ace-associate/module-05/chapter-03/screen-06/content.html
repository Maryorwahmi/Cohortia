How MLAG Works: Control and Data Plane Synchronization

The synchronized operation between MLAG peers

Peer Synchronization

MLAG operates by forming a control plane and data plane synchronization between two Arista switches, known as MLAG peers. This coordination is vital for maintaining a consistent network state.

  • •Control Plane Sync: Shares MAC address learning, ARP entries, and IGMP snooping states.
  • •Data Plane Sync: Carries data traffic between peers, especially during certain failure scenarios.
  • •Unified View: Ensures downstream devices see a single logical switch, simplifying network topology.
Key Takeaway

Robust control and data plane synchronization between MLAG peers is fundamental to achieving seamless redundancy and active-active forwarding.
