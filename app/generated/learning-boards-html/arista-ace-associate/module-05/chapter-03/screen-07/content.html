Key Component: The MLAG Peer Link

The dedicated backbone for inter-peer communication

Purpose of the Peer Link

The peer link is a dedicated high-bandwidth link (or LAG) between the two MLAG peer switches. It's fundamental for maintaining MLAG domain integrity and functionality.

  • •Control Plane Synchronization: Carries MAC, ARP, and IGMP snooping state updates.
  • •Data Plane Forwarding: Essential for traffic forwarding between peers in specific failure scenarios.
  • •Heartbeat: Acts as a heartbeat to detect peer failures and prevent split-brain conditions.
Key Takeaway

A robust and redundant peer link is critical for MLAG stability, preventing split-brain scenarios and ensuring continuous operation.
