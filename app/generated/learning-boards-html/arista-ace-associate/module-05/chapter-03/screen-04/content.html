Problem Statement

Limitations of Traditional Link Aggregation Groups (LAGs)

Understanding the single point of failure in conventional designs

The Single Switch Vulnerability

While LAGs offer excellent link-level redundancy and increased bandwidth, they inherently suffer from a critical single point of failure: the switch itself. If the single switch hosting the LAG fails, all connectivity through that LAG is lost.

Risk

Total connectivity loss if the host switch fails.

Impact

Disruption in data center and core network designs where continuous availability is paramount.

Key Takeaway

Traditional LAGs provide link redundancy but not switch redundancy, leaving a critical single point of failure.
