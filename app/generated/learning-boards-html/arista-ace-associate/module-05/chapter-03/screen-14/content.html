Key Concepts: MLAG Fundamentals

Essential terms and definitions to remember

Multi-Chassis Link Aggregation (MLAG)

A proprietary Arista technology allowing two switches to act as a single logical switch for link aggregation, providing switch-level redundancy.

MLAG Peer

One of the two switches participating in an MLAG domain, working together to present a unified logical switch to downstream devices.

Peer Link

A dedicated high-bandwidth link (or LAG) between MLAG peers, used for control plane synchronization and data forwarding during failures.

Summary Takeaway

MLAG fundamentally enhances network resilience by allowing two switches to operate as one, with the peer link being the critical backbone for their synchronized operation.
