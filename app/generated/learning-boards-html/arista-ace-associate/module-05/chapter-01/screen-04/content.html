Core Concept

Addressing Single Points of Failure

Bundling physical links for enhanced reliability and bandwidth.

The Problem: Single Link Vulnerability

  • A single physical link failure leads to complete service disruption.
  • No backup path means downtime for critical connections.
  • Limited bandwidth capacity of a single link.
Key Takeaway

Link aggregation is vital for modern networks, providing both increased bandwidth and critical redundancy to prevent service disruptions.
