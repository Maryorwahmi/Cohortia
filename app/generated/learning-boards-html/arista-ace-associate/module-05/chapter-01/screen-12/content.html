Confirming LAG Operational Status

Essential commands to check your LACP setup.

Key Verification Commands

  • `show port-channel summary`: High-level overview of all LAGs.
  • `show port-channel detail`: Granular info on member interfaces.
  • `show interfaces Port-Channel`: Operational status and statistics of the logical interface.
Key Takeaway

Regularly use `show` commands to ensure your LACP LAGs are formed correctly and operating efficiently.

Arista EOS CLI Output Examples
Arista-SW1#show port-channel summary

Look for 'UP' status on the Port-Channel and 'P' (Up in port-channel) for member interfaces in `show port-channel summary`.
