LACP Modes: Active and Passive

Understanding active and passive modes for LACP negotiation.

LACP Active Mode

  • Actively initiates LACP negotiation.
  • Sends LACP PDUs to the peer.
  • Always tries to form a LAG.
  • Recommended for robust connections.
Key Takeaway

For a LAG to form, at least one side must be in active mode. Active/Active is the most reliable configuration.

LACP Passive Mode

  • Waits for the peer to initiate negotiation.
  • Responds to LACP PDUs, but does not send them first.
  • Will only form a LAG if the peer is active.
  • Less common for critical links.

Common Misconception

A common mistake is configuring both sides of a link in passive mode. This will prevent the LACP LAG from forming because neither device will initiate the necessary negotiation. Always ensure at least one side is active, or ideally, both for maximum reliability.
