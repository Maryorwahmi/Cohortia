VRRP Roles: Master and Backup

How routers coordinate to provide continuous gateway service.

Master Router (Active)

  • •Forwards all traffic sent to the virtual IP address.
  • •Responds to ARP requests for the virtual MAC address.
  • •Sends periodic VRRP advertisements to signal its active status.
Role Insight

The Master is the primary gateway, handling all traffic for the virtual IP.

Backup Router (Standby)

  • •Monitors the Master's status by listening for advertisements.
  • •Does not forward traffic for the virtual IP unless it becomes Master.
  • •Takes over the Master role if the active Master fails to send advertisements.
Role Insight

Backups are ready to assume the gateway role instantly upon Master failure.

Real-World Connection

This Master/Backup model ensures that end devices always have a functional default gateway, even during physical router failures, providing seamless network operation.
