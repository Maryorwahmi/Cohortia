Key Concepts

Understanding VRRP Roles and Election

Master Router

The router in a VRRP group that actively forwards traffic for the virtual IP and sends VRRP advertisements.

Backup Router

A router in a VRRP group that monitors the Master and takes over if the Master fails.

Priority

A configurable value (1-254) used to elect the Master router; higher priority wins.

Preempt

A setting that allows a higher-priority router to immediately take over the Master role if it becomes available.

Chapter Summary

VRRP's Master/Backup roles, priority-based election, and preemption mechanism work together to provide robust and dynamic first-hop redundancy.
