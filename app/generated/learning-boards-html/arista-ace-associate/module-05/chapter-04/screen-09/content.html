VRRP Configuration: Understanding Preemption

Controlling failback behavior for VRRP groups.

Preemption Explained

  • •When enabled (default in Arista EOS), a higher-priority router immediately takes Master role if it comes online.
  • •Ensures the preferred Master is always active when available.
  • •When disabled, a lower-priority Master remains Master until it fails, even if a higher-priority router becomes available.
  • •Preemption can cause transient traffic disruption during failback.
Key Takeaway

Preemption ensures the highest-priority router is always Master, but requires careful consideration due to potential brief traffic disruptions during failback.

Arista EOS CLI Example
interface Vlan10
   vrrp 1 ip 192.168.10.1
   vrrp 1 priority 150
   vrrp 1 preempt
Explanation:
  • •vrrp 1 preempt: Explicitly enables preemption for VRRP group 1.
  • •This command is often not strictly necessary in Arista EOS as preemption is enabled by default.
Consideration

While preemption ensures the preferred Master is always active, disabling it can be beneficial in scenarios where minimizing failback disruptions is prioritized over always having the highest-priority device as Master.
