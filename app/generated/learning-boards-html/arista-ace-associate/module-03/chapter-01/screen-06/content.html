Unicast Forwarding: Targeted Delivery

How Switches Send Frames to Known Destinations

When a switch receives a frame with a destination MAC address, it consults its MAC address table. If an entry exists for that destination, the switch forwards the frame only out the associated interface. This is called unicast forwarding.

Efficiency Benefit

Unicast forwarding is highly efficient because it prevents unnecessary traffic from being sent to other ports, conserving bandwidth and reducing network congestion.

Key Takeaway

Unicast forwarding ensures efficient, direct communication by sending frames only to the specific port where the destination device is known to reside.

Analogy

Think of unicast forwarding like a direct phone call to a specific person, rather than shouting your message to everyone in a room.
