Understanding MAC Address Aging

How Switches Maintain a Current Network Map

Dynamic entries in the MAC address table have an aging timer (e.g., 300 seconds). If no frames are received from a MAC address within this period, the entry is removed from the table.

Purpose of Aging
  • Network Hygiene: Prevents stale entries from accumulating.
  • Adaptability: Allows the table to adjust to device movements or removals.
  • Resource Management: Frees up CAM table resources.
Key Takeaway

MAC address aging is vital for maintaining an accurate and efficient MAC table, but it means silent devices may experience temporary flooding until re-learned.

MAC Aging Timeline
Device Active
MAC learned, timer resets.
Device Silent
Aging timer starts counting down.
Timer Expires
MAC entry removed from table.
Misconception

A device's MAC address being removed from the table doesn't mean it's disconnected; it simply means the switch has no current knowledge of its location, leading to temporary flooding until it communicates again.
