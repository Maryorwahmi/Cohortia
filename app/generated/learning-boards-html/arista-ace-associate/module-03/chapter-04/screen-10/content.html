Load Balancing Options

Exploring Arista EOS Load Balancing Algorithms

Choosing the right method for your network traffic

Common Methods Explained

  • •`src-mac` / `dst-mac`: Based on source or destination MAC address.
  • •`src-ip` / `dst-ip`: Based on source or destination IP address.
  • •`src-dst-mac`: Combines source and destination MAC addresses.
  • •`src-dst-ip`: Combines source and destination IP addresses.
  • •`src-dst-ip-port`: Combines source/destination IP and TCP/UDP ports (highest granularity).
Key Takeaway

The choice of load balancing method is critical; `src-dst-ip-port` often provides the best distribution for diverse traffic patterns due to its higher granularity.

Choosing the Right Algorithm

Scenario: Single Client to Single Server

`src-mac` or `src-ip` might use only one link. Recommend `src-dst-ip-port` for better distribution if multiple sessions/ports are used.

Scenario: Many Clients to Many Servers

`src-dst-ip` or `src-dst-ip-port` generally provide good distribution due to varied source/destination combinations.

Pro Tip

Always analyze your network's specific traffic patterns before deciding on a load balancing method. What works for one environment might not be optimal for another.
