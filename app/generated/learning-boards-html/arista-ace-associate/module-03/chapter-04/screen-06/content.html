LACP Modes: Active and Passive Explained

How switches negotiate aggregated links

Active Mode

  • •Port actively sends LACP PDUs.
  • •Initiates negotiation to form a LAG.
  • •Proactively seeks a partner to bundle links.

Passive Mode

  • •Port waits to receive LACP PDUs.
  • •Responds to active LACP requests but does not initiate.
  • •Will not form a bundle if both sides are passive.
Key Takeaway

For LACP to form, at least one side must be in 'Active' mode to initiate the negotiation process.

LACP Mode Combinations

Active + Active

Successful LACP bundle formation.

Active + Passive

Successful LACP bundle formation.

Passive + Passive

No LACP bundle will form.

Common Misconception

Many assume 'passive' means it will eventually become active. Remember, passive only responds; it never initiates. Both passive sides will wait indefinitely.
