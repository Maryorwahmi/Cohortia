Verification

Confirming LAG Operation and Health

Essential commands for checking Port-Channel status

Why Verify?

  • •Ensures LAG is functioning as intended.
  • •Confirms increased bandwidth and redundancy are active.
  • •Helps identify and resolve issues proactively.
Key Takeaway

Always verify your LAG configurations using specific `show` commands to confirm proper operation and ensure network stability and performance.

Arista EOS: Verification Commands

Arista# show port-channel summary
# Displays a summary of all Port-Channels and their member interfaces.

Arista# show lacp neighbor
# Shows detailed LACP negotiation status with the remote device.

Arista# show interfaces Port-Channel 1
# Provides status and configuration details for the logical Port-Channel interface.

Expected Output

Look for `(SU)` in `show port-channel summary` to confirm the Port-Channel is up and bundled, and `(P)` for member interfaces indicating they are part of the bundle.
