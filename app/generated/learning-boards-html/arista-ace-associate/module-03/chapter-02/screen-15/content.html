Key Concepts

Essential Terms and Ideas from This Chapter

Broadcast Domain

A logical division of a network where all nodes can reach each other by broadcast; VLANs segment these domains.

Native VLAN

The VLAN on an 802.1Q trunk whose traffic is sent untagged; must match on both ends of a trunk link.

Native VLAN Mismatch

A common configuration error where native VLANs on connected trunk ports do not match, leading to communication issues.

Summary Takeaway

Ensuring consistent native VLAN configurations across trunk links is paramount for preventing network disruptions and maintaining the integrity of your VLAN segmentation.
