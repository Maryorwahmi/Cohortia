Trunk Ports: Carrying Multiple VLANs Across Links

Enabling inter-switch and switch-to-router communication for multiple VLANs.

Trunk Port Definition

A switch port configured to carry traffic for multiple VLANs simultaneously, typically connecting switches to other switches or routers.

Key Mechanism: 802.1Q Tagging

Uses a tagging standard (IEEE 802.1Q) to insert VLAN identification into frame headers, allowing multiple VLANs to share a single physical link.

Key Takeaway

Trunk ports are vital for extending VLANs across your network infrastructure, enabling seamless communication between devices in different VLANs residing on separate switches.

Arista EOS CLI: Configuring Trunk Ports
Arista(config)# interface Ethernet1
Arista(config-if-Et1)# switchport mode trunk
Arista(config-if-Et1)# end
