The Native VLAN: Untagged Traffic on Trunks

Understanding how untagged frames are handled on 802.1Q trunk links.

Native VLAN Definition

The VLAN whose traffic is sent untagged over an 802.1Q trunk link. All other VLANs on the trunk are tagged.

Critical Requirement: Match!

The native VLAN must be identical on both ends of a trunk link to avoid communication failures and potential security vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaway

The native VLAN is crucial for proper trunk operation; a mismatch can lead to severe connectivity issues and is a common troubleshooting challenge.

Arista EOS CLI: Configuring Native VLAN
Arista(config)# interface Ethernet1
Arista(config-if-Et1)# switchport mode trunk
Arista(config-if-Et1)# switchport trunk native vlan 99
Arista(config-if-Et1)# end
