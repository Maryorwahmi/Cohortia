Troubleshooting STP
Common STP Misconfigurations
Avoiding pitfalls for network stability

Why Misconfigurations Occur:

STP's complexity and the critical nature of its functions make it prone to errors if not carefully planned and implemented.

Verification is Key:

Always use show spanning-tree and show spanning-tree active to verify root bridge, port roles, and states.

Key Takeaway

Proactive configuration and diligent verification are essential to prevent STP misconfigurations and maintain network health.

Common STP Mistakes

PortFast on Trunk Ports

Creates temporary loops during topology changes.

No PortFast on Access Ports

Leads to slow device boot times and connectivity delays.

Suboptimal Root Bridge

Inefficient traffic paths and network performance.

No BPDU Guard on Access Ports

Security risk; allows unauthorized switches or loops.
