Why IPv6? Addressing the Future

From IPv4 Exhaustion to a Vast New Address Space

The Problem: IPv4 Exhaustion

IPv4's 32-bit address space (approx. 4.3 billion addresses) is largely depleted, limiting new device connectivity.

The Solution: IPv6 Expansion

IPv6 offers a 128-bit address space (340 undecillion addresses), ensuring unique IPs for all future devices.

Key Takeaway

IPv6 is essential for the internet's continued growth, providing a virtually limitless supply of unique addresses for a connected world.
