Understanding Hexadecimal Notation and Segments

Hexadecimal Notation

Uses 0-9 and A-F, making addresses more compact than binary but longer than IPv4's dotted-decimal.

Eight 16-bit Segments

Addresses are divided into 8 groups of 4 hexadecimal digits, separated by colons.

Key Takeaway

Mastering hexadecimal and segment identification is the first step to confidently working with IPv6 addresses.

IPv6 Address Structure Example

2001:0db8:85a3:0000:0000:8a2e:0370:7334
