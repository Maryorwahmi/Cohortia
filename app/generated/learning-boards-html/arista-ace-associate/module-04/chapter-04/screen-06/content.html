How SVIs Facilitate Inter-VLAN Communication

The Layer 3 switch routes packets between SVIs by consulting its routing table, enabling seamless communication across VLANs.

Packet to Gateway

A device sends a packet to its default gateway (the SVI for its VLAN) when the destination is in another subnet.

Routing Table Lookup

The Layer 3 switch receives the packet on the SVI and consults its routing table for the destination network.

Forwarding to Destination

The switch forwards the packet out the SVI corresponding to the destination VLAN's network.

Key Takeaway

The Layer 3 switch acts as an intermediary, receiving packets on the source VLAN's SVI, performing a routing lookup, and forwarding them out the destination VLAN's SVI.
