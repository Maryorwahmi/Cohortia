Core Concept

The Necessity of Inter-VLAN Routing

VLANs segment networks into distinct broadcast domains and IP subnets, preventing direct Layer 2 communication between them.

Segmentation Benefits

VLANs improve security, performance, and management by isolating traffic within logical groups.

The Isolation Problem

Devices in different VLANs are in separate IP subnets and cannot communicate directly at Layer 2.

Routing is the Solution

A Layer 3 device (router or Layer 3 switch) is required to route packets between these distinct IP subnets.

Key Takeaway

Without inter-VLAN routing, VLANs are isolated broadcast domains, preventing communication between devices in different IP subnets.
