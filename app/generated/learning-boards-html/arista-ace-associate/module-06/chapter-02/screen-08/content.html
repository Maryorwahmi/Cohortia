Core Concept

Understanding Monitor Session

Port mirroring, or monitor session, duplicates network traffic from source interfaces or VLANs to a designated destination interface for external monitoring and analysis.

  • •
    Passive Observation: Traffic is duplicated, not redirected, ensuring no impact on production.
  • •
    External Analysis: Destination typically connects to a network analyzer or IDS.
  • •
    Sustained Monitoring: Ideal for long-term traffic capture and analysis.
Key Takeaway

`Monitor session` is essential for non-intrusive, sustained traffic analysis of switched data, providing deep insights for troubleshooting and security.
