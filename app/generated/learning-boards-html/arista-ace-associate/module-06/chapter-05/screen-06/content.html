Setting Up Your Ansible Inventory

Define your Arista EOS devices and connection parameters in a structured inventory file.

What is an Inventory?

  • •A list of managed hosts (network devices).
  • •Can be INI or YAML format.
  • •Organizes devices into logical groups.

Why it Matters for Arista EOS

  • •Specifies IP address, connection method (eAPI), and credentials.
  • •`ansible_network_os=arista.eos.eos` is crucial for module loading.
  • •Ensures Ansible knows how to communicate with your Arista switches.
Key Takeaway

A well-structured inventory is essential for Ansible to correctly identify, group, and connect to your Arista EOS devices, enabling targeted and efficient automation.

Pro Tip

Use `ansible-inventory --list` to verify your inventory file's structure and ensure all hosts and variables are correctly parsed by Ansible.
