Crafting the Rules of Engagement (RoE)

Defining the scope and boundaries of your test

What is an RoE?

A legally binding document that defines the scope, limits, and expectations of a penetration test, serving as a critical blueprint and legal protection.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined RoE is essential for a successful, compliant, and legally sound penetration testing engagement.

Key RoE Components

Scope: What's in/out of bounds.

Duration: Start and end times.

Communication: Contacts & emergency plan.

Methods: Permitted attack types.

Data Handling: Sensitive data protocols.

Legal Disclaimers: Liabilities & risks.

Pro-Tip

Always review the RoE thoroughly with the client to ensure mutual understanding and agreement before starting any work.
