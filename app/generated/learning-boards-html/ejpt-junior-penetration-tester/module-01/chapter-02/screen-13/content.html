Key Concepts

Solidifying your understanding

CFAA

A United States federal law that prohibits unauthorized access to computer systems.

GDPR

A comprehensive data privacy law in the European Union that regulates the processing of personal data.

Rules of Engagement (RoE)

A legally binding document defining the scope, duration, methods, and responsibilities for a penetration test.

Chapter Summary

Legal frameworks and operational documents like the RoE and 'Get Out of Jail Free' card are indispensable for conducting ethical and legally compliant penetration tests.
