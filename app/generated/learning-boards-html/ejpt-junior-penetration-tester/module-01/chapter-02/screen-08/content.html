The Cornerstone: Explicit Written Authorization

Your legal shield in penetration testing

Why it's Non-Negotiable

Without explicit, written authorization, any penetration testing activity, regardless of intent, is considered illegal and can lead to severe legal consequences.

Key Takeaway

Always secure a legally binding, written authorization document before initiating any penetration testing activities to protect yourself and your client.

Authorization Benefits

Legal Protection: Shields you from charges of unauthorized access.

Clear Scope: Defines what systems and actions are permitted.

Professionalism: Establishes trust and accountability with clients.

Common Mistake

Relying on verbal permission or assuming good intent is enough. Always get it in writing!
