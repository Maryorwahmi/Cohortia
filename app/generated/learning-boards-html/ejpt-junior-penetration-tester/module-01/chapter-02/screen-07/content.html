General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

Protecting personal data across borders

What is GDPR?

A comprehensive data privacy law in the European Union that imposes strict requirements on how personal data is collected, processed, and stored, with global reach.

Key Takeaway

Even if your client is not in the EU, if they handle data belonging to EU citizens, GDPR applies, requiring careful consideration during penetration tests.

GDPR's Impact on Pen Testing

Data Breach Notification: Mandates specific procedures if EU data is compromised.

Severe Penalties: Non-compliance can result in hefty fines (up to 4% global turnover).

Global Reach: Applies to any organization processing data of EU residents, regardless of location.

Misconception

GDPR only applies to companies located within the European Union. (False: it applies to any entity processing EU citizens' data).
