The 'Get Out of Jail Free' Card

Irrefutable proof of your legitimate purpose

What is it?

A formal letter, signed by a high-ranking client official, explicitly authorizing the penetration testing team to perform agreed-upon activities.

Key Takeaway

Always have your 'Get Out of Jail Free' card readily accessible, both physically and digitally, during any active testing to avoid legal issues.

Purpose & Importance

Proof of Authorization: Demonstrates legitimate purpose to authorities.

Prevents Arrest: Can prevent legal repercussions during unexpected incidents.

Legal Safeguard: Protects both the tester and the client from misunderstandings.

Scenario

An IDS flags your activity during a test, leading to a police call. Presenting the GOJF card immediately clarifies your legitimate purpose.
