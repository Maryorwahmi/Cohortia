Avoiding Common Pitfalls

Protecting yourself and your client

The Cost of Oversight

Failing to adhere to strict legal and ethical guidelines can lead to severe penalties, including fines, imprisonment, and significant damage to professional reputation.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize meticulous documentation, strict adherence to scope, and immediate accessibility of authorization documents to prevent legal and professional issues.

Common Mistakes & Consequences

No Signed RoE: Leads to legal vulnerability, lack of clear boundaries.

Operating Out of Scope: Unauthorized access, potential CFAA violation.

GOJF Card Inaccessible: Inability to prove legitimacy to authorities, potential arrest.

Ignoring Local Laws: Unintended legal breaches due to jurisdictional differences.

Pro-Tip

Before every test, conduct a quick checklist: Is the RoE signed? Is the scope clear? Is the GOJF card ready? This simple routine can save you from major headaches.
