Key Concepts: Methodologies & Reconnaissance

Essential terms from the initial phases of penetration testing.

PTES

A comprehensive, industry-recognized methodology for penetration testing, comprising seven distinct phases.

Intelligence Gathering

The initial phase of collecting information about the target, both passively and actively, before direct attacks.

Passive Reconnaissance

Gathering information without directly interacting with the target, minimizing detection risk (e.g., Google Dorking, WHOIS).

Summary Takeaway

The PTES framework provides a structured approach to penetration testing, starting with intelligence gathering, where passive reconnaissance is key for covert information collection.
