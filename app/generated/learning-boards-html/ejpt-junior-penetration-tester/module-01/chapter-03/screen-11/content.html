Technical Phase 6

Reporting: Communicating Findings Effectively

The essential components of a professional penetration test report.

Executive Summary

A high-level, non-technical overview for management, summarizing key findings and overall risk posture.

Technical Details

In-depth descriptions of each vulnerability, including affected systems, proof-of-concept steps, and evidence.

Risk Ratings

Assessment of vulnerability severity (e.g., using CVSS) to help clients prioritize remediation efforts.

Remediation Recommendations

Clear, actionable steps clients can take to fix each vulnerability, including patches or configuration changes.

Appendices

Supporting documentation such as the Rules of Engagement, detailed logs, or tool outputs.

Why a Good Report Matters

  • Translates technical findings into business risk.
  • Guides remediation efforts effectively.
  • Provides a record for compliance and audit.
  • Demonstrates the value of the penetration test.

Example: Tailored Communication

The Executive Summary focuses on business impact for leadership, while Technical Details provide granular steps for IT teams, ensuring relevance for all stakeholders.

Key Takeaway

A professional penetration test report is the critical deliverable, transforming technical findings into actionable intelligence for effective security improvement.
