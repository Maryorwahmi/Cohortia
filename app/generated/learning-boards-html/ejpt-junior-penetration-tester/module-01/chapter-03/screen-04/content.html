The PTES Framework: A Structured Approach

Understanding the seven phases of a professional penetration test.

Phase 1Pre-engagement
Phase 2Intelligence Gathering
Phase 3Threat Modeling
Phase 4Vulnerability Analysis
Phase 5Exploitation
Phase 6Post-Exploitation
Phase 7Reporting

Key Aspects of PTES

  • Ensures comprehensive coverage.
  • Provides a repeatable methodology.
  • Guides professional execution.
  • Focuses on technical phases.

Why it Matters

A structured approach prevents critical steps from being missed, leading to more effective and reliable security assessments. It transforms testing from ad-hoc attacks into a strategic process.

Key Takeaway

The PTES framework is essential for conducting thorough, professional, and repeatable penetration tests, ensuring all critical security aspects are systematically evaluated.
