What is Penetration Testing?

Simulating Attacks to Fortify Defenses

Core Definition

Penetration testing is a simulated cyberattack against your own computer system, network, or web application.

Primary Purpose

Its goal is to proactively identify security weaknesses and vulnerabilities that a real attacker could exploit, without causing actual harm.

Key Takeaway

Penetration testing is a crucial proactive measure, acting as an ethical 'stress test' for an organization's digital defenses.

Analogy: Professional Burglar for Home Security

Imagine hiring a professional burglar to test your home security. They'll try every entry point, not to steal, but to show you where your locks are weak. This allows you to reinforce your home before a real threat.

Common Misconception

Penetration testing is NOT about causing damage or stealing data; it's about authorized, controlled discovery and validation of vulnerabilities.
