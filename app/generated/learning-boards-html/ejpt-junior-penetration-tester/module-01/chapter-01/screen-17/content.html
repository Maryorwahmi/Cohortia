Key Concepts

Expanding Your Cybersecurity Vocabulary

White Box Testing

A pen test where the tester has full knowledge of the target system.

Grey Box Testing

A pen test with partial knowledge of the target system, a hybrid approach.

PTES

Penetration Testing Execution Standard: a comprehensive framework for pen tests.

Summary Takeaway

White Box and Grey Box testing offer varied insights, while PTES provides a structured methodology, and CVEs catalog known vulnerabilities.
