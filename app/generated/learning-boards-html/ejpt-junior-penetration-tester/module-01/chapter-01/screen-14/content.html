Structured Approaches to Penetration Testing

Ensuring Consistency, Thoroughness, and Replicability

Importance of Structure

Methodologies provide a roadmap, ensuring comprehensive and systematic security assessments.

Common Frameworks

Examples include OSSTMM, OWASP Testing Guide, and NIST SP 800-115, all guiding systematic approaches.

Key Takeaway

Adhering to a recognized methodology is crucial for conducting effective, thorough, and repeatable penetration tests.

Analogy: Building with a Blueprint

Just as a builder uses a blueprint to ensure a house is constructed correctly and completely, a pen tester uses a methodology to ensure a thorough and consistent security assessment.

Common Mistake

Beginners often jump straight into using tools without understanding the underlying methodology, leading to incomplete and ineffective assessments.
