PTES: The Penetration Testing Execution Standard

A Comprehensive Framework for Professional Pen Tests

Widely Recognized Framework

PTES is a comprehensive standard outlining seven key phases for conducting professional penetration tests.

Ensures Thoroughness

Its structured phases ensure all critical aspects of a penetration test are covered, from planning to reporting.

Key Takeaway

PTES is essential for aspiring pen testers, providing the foundational structure to conduct effective and professional security assessments.

2Intelligence Gathering

7Reporting

PTES Phases: A Step-by-Step Guide

PTES breaks down a penetration test into seven distinct phases, ensuring a methodical and comprehensive approach from initial planning to final documentation.

Common Mistake

Ignoring the methodology and jumping straight to tools can lead to incomplete assessments and missed critical vulnerabilities.
