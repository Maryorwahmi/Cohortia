Grey Box Testing: A Hybrid Approach

Partial Knowledge for Targeted Insider or Breached Scenarios

Partial Knowledge

Tester receives limited internal information, such as user credentials or network segment details.

Simulates Targeted Attacks

Ideal for simulating attacks by privileged insiders or scenarios where an attacker has gained an initial foothold.

Key Takeaway

Grey Box testing offers a balanced approach, providing efficiency while maintaining a realistic attacker perspective with some internal access.

Target System
Privileged Insider
Targeted Assessment

Scenario: Suspected Former Employee Backdoor

A company suspects a former employee left a backdoor. A Grey Box test, with some internal network info and a non-privileged account, simulates this scenario effectively.

Pro Tip

Grey Box testing is excellent for evaluating privilege escalation vulnerabilities from a standard user account within an internal network.
