Understanding Penetration Test Categories

Defining Scope and Approach for Security Assessments

Black Box

No prior knowledge of the target system, simulating an external attacker.

Grey Box

Partial knowledge of internal systems, simulating a privileged insider or targeted attack.

White Box

Full knowledge of the target environment, simulating an insider threat or highly informed attacker.

Why it Matters

The choice of test type directly impacts the scope, depth, and perspective of the security assessment, aligning it with specific organizational security goals.

Key Takeaway

Selecting the appropriate penetration test type is a strategic decision that determines the effectiveness of the security assessment against specific threat models.
