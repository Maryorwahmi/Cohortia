The Importance of a Structured Approach

Maintaining Clarity in Complex Penetration Tests

Why Document?

In complex engagements, meticulous documentation is paramount for staying organized, avoiding mistakes, and forming the basis of your final penetration test report.

What to Track

  • Compromised systems (IPs, hostnames, OS, access method).
  • Credentials obtained (usernames, hashes, passwords).
  • Persistence mechanisms deployed.
  • Pivots established.
  • Data exfiltrated.
  • Cleanup actions performed.
Common Mistake

Losing track of compromised systems or credentials, or failing to clean up temporary files, which can lead to detection or confusion.

Structured Engagement Flow

Planning & Reconnaissance

Define scope, gather initial intel

Execution & Exploitation

Gain access, escalate privileges

Documentation & Reporting

Record findings, generate report

Key Takeaway

A structured approach with meticulous documentation is vital for managing complex engagements, ensuring clarity, and producing comprehensive reports.
