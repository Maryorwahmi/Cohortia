Maintaining Persistence & Backdoors

Ensuring continuous access to compromised systems.

Gaining initial access is a significant step, but maintaining that access is paramount. This chapter will equip you with the techniques to establish and sustain control over compromised systems, even after reboots or security interventions. You'll learn how to create 'backdoors' that blend into normal operations, ensuring your foothold remains secure and undetected.

Key Idea

Initial access is temporary; persistence ensures long-term control over compromised systems.
