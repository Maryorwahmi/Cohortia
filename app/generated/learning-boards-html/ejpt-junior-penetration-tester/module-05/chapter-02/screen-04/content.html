Core Concept

Why Persistence Matters

Maintaining access after initial compromise is crucial for successful penetration testing.

The Challenge of Temporary Access

  • Initial access is often fleeting, easily lost.
  • Systems reboot, user sessions expire, and security tools may detect your presence.
  • Without persistence, you risk losing your hard-won foothold.

What is a Backdoor?

A backdoor is a hidden method of access that bypasses normal authentication or security controls. It's designed to blend in with legitimate system operations, making it difficult to detect while ensuring you can regain control.

Key Takeaway

Persistence is the ability to maintain access to a compromised system across reboots, disconnections, and other system changes, ensuring continued control.
