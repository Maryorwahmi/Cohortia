How it Works

  • ICMP Packets: Data is hidden within the payload of ICMP echo requests/replies.
  • Firewall Bypass: ICMP traffic is often permitted outbound for network diagnostics.
  • Tools: ptunnel, icmpshell create a tunnel over ICMP.
  • Two-way: Can establish a full duplex communication channel.

Advantages & Disadvantages

  • Stealth: High, as ICMP is common.
  • Speed: Slow, not suitable for large files.
  • Detection: Large volumes of ICMP traffic can be anomalous and trigger alerts.
  • Use Case: Best for small data payloads or C2 channels.
Key Takeaway

ICMP tunneling offers a stealthy way to exfiltrate data through restricted networks, but its low bandwidth and potential for volume-based detection limit its use to smaller data transfers.
