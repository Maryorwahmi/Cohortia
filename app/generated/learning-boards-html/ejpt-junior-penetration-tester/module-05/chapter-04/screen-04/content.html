What is Data Exfiltration?

Data exfiltration is the unauthorized transfer of sensitive information from a compromised system or network to an attacker-controlled location. It's a critical phase in penetration testing to demonstrate the tangible impact of a breach.

Why It Matters

  • Proof of Impact: Provides concrete evidence of a successful breach.
  • Risk Assessment: Quantifies potential damage (financial, reputational).
  • Scope Validation: Confirms ability to access and extract valuable assets.
  • Real-world Scenario: Mimics actual attacker objectives.

Common Data Types

  • Credentials (usernames, passwords, hashes)
  • Configuration files (network, application settings)
  • Intellectual property (source code, designs)
  • Customer data (PII, financial records)
  • Internal documents (strategic plans, emails)

The Exfiltration Lifecycle
