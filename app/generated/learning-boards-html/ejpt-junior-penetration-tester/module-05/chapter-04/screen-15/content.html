Key Concepts

A quick review of the most important terms and ideas.

Data Exfiltration

The unauthorized transfer of data from a compromised system or network to an attacker-controlled location.

Egress Filtering

Network security measures that control outbound traffic, often restricting certain protocols or ports.

DNS Tunneling

Encapsulating other network protocols within DNS queries and responses to bypass firewalls.

Chapter Summary

Data exfiltration is key to demonstrating breach impact, requiring an understanding of network egress filtering and the use of covert channels like DNS and ICMP tunneling to bypass defenses.
