What is Covering Tracks?

Covering tracks involves removing or altering forensic evidence of an intrusion to prevent detection, attribution, and maintain stealth on a compromised system.

Why It's Critical

  • Maintain Stealth: Avoid detection by security teams.
  • Prevent Attribution: Make it harder to trace activities back to you.
  • Forensic Evasion: Remove evidence crucial for incident response.
  • Operational Security: Ensure a clean exit and potential future access.

Key Areas of Evidence

  • Command history (shell, PowerShell)
  • System and application log files
  • File and directory timestamps
  • Temporary files and uploaded tools
  • Modified configurations (e.g., persistence)
