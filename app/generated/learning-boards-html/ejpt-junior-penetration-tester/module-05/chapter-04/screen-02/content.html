Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

Bloom's Taxonomy:LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Understand Exfiltration Importance

Grasp why data exfiltration is a critical objective in penetration tests, demonstrating real-world impact and risk. This skill helps you articulate the tangible consequences of a security breach to stakeholders.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Identify Exfiltration Methods

Recognize and differentiate various techniques for extracting sensitive data from both Linux and Windows systems. This enables you to choose the most effective method based on the target's operating system and network configuration.
