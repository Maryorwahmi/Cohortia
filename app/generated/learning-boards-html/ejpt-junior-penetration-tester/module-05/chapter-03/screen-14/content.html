Stealth and Detection

Minimizing Your Footprint During Lateral Movement

Key Considerations

  • EDR Solutions: Many tools are detected by Endpoint Detection and Response.
  • Mimikatz Example: A common tool for credential extraction, often flagged.
  • Minimize Footprint: Reduce traces and logs left behind.
  • Prioritize Stealth: Choose methods less likely to trigger alerts.
Key Takeaway

Always be mindful of EDR solutions and detection risks; prioritize stealth and minimize your footprint to avoid early discovery during lateral movement.

Balancing Impact and Stealth

Detection Risk

Using aggressive tools or noisy techniques increases the chance of being caught by security systems.

Stealthy Alternatives

Prefer built-in OS functionalities or less common tools to reduce detection likelihood.
