Pivoting: The Stepping Stone

Using a Compromised System as a Network Bridge

Key Characteristics of Pivoting

  • Indirect Access: Reach targets not directly accessible from your machine.
  • Intermediary Role: The compromised system acts as a relay for your traffic.
  • Network Tunneling: Often involves creating a tunnel or proxy through the pivot.
  • Expanded Reach: Extends your attack surface into internal network segments.
Key Takeaway

Pivoting is about leveraging a compromised host's network access to reach deeper into a target environment.
