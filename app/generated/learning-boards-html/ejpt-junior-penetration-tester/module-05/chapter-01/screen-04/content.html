Core Concept

The Privilege Escalation Journey

Moving from limited access to full system control.

Why Escalate Privileges?

  • •Initial access often means low-level user privileges.
  • •Limited access restricts system control and data access.
  • •Higher privileges (root/admin) are needed for full control and deeper network movement.
  • •It's a critical step in a penetration test to achieve objectives.

The Escalation Process

1. Initial Foothold: Gain basic, low-privileged access to the system.

2. Enumerate: Discover vulnerabilities or misconfigurations.

3. Exploit: Leverage findings to execute commands with higher privileges.

Common Misconception

Privilege escalation is not always about finding complex zero-day exploits. Often, it's about exploiting simple misconfigurations or known vulnerabilities.
