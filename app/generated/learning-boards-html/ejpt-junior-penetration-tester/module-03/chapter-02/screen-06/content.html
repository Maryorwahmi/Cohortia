Practical Steps

Setting Up Your First OpenVAS Scan

Defining targets, selecting scan policies, and launching an assessment.

1. Define Target

Specify the IP address, range, or hostname of the systems to be scanned (e.g., 192.168.1.105).

2. Select Scan Configuration

Choose a scan policy like "Full and fast ultimate" or "Full and very deep" to dictate scan intensity and checks.

3. Launch Scan

Initiate the scan task after target and policy are set. Monitor its progress through the web interface.

Key Takeaway

Always ensure vulnerability feeds are updated before scanning to avoid outdated results and missed vulnerabilities.
