Activity: Launching the Scan and Reviewing Results

Executing your OpenVAS task and interpreting the generated report.

1. Start the Scan

In the 'Tasks' list, click the 'play' icon next to your scan task to begin the assessment.

2. Monitor Status

Observe the task status change from 'Requested' to 'Running' and finally 'Done'. Be patient, as this can take time.

3. Review the Report

Once 'Done', click the date/time under 'Last Report' to view detailed findings, severity, and remediation advice.

Key Takeaway

Thoroughly reviewing the scan report, focusing on severity and details, is crucial for identifying and understanding vulnerabilities.
