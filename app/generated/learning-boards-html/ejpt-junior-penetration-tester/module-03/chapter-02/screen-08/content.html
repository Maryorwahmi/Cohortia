Unlocking Deeper Insights with Credentialed Scans

Why providing login credentials leads to more comprehensive vulnerability detection.

What They Are

Scans performed with valid login credentials (e.g., SSH, SMB) for target systems.

How They Work

The scanner logs in to inspect internal configurations, installed software, patch levels, and user permissions.

Key Takeaway

Credentialed scans provide a significantly more accurate and comprehensive assessment by gaining internal visibility into target systems.

Credentialed vs. Uncredentialed
Credentialed Scan

Internal system inspection, deep analysis.

Uncredentialed Scan

External, network-based checks only.

Higher Accuracy

Detects hidden and internal vulnerabilities.

Limited Visibility

Misses vulnerabilities behind authentication.
