Best Practices

Ethical Considerations and Impact of Credentialed Scans

Minimizing risks and ensuring responsible use of privileged access.

Minimum Privileges

Use credentials with the lowest necessary permissions to reduce risk if compromised.

Secure Handling

Handle all credentials securely, avoiding hardcoding or insecure storage.

Network Impact

Be aware that intensive scans can consume significant bandwidth and CPU, potentially affecting system performance.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize security and operational stability by using least privilege and communicating scan schedules to stakeholders.

Responsible Scanning Checklist

Use Least Privilege Credentials

Handle Credentials Securely

Schedule During Off-Peak Hours

Inform Stakeholders
