Staged vs. Stageless Payloads

Optimizing payload delivery for stealth and reliability

Staged Payloads

Delivered in multiple parts. A small initial payload (stager) downloads the rest of the larger payload.

  • More stealthy due to smaller initial size.
  • Greater flexibility for dynamic payload selection.
  • Requires two successful connections.
Benefit

Can bypass some network defenses by being less conspicuous initially.

Stageless Payloads

Delivered entirely in one go. The full payload is sent at once, making it larger but potentially more reliable.

  • Larger in size, potentially easier to detect.
  • More reliable in single-shot delivery scenarios.
  • Requires only one successful connection.
Benefit

Can be more robust in unstable network conditions or when a quick, direct delivery is needed.

Key Takeaway

The choice between staged and stageless payloads depends on balancing stealth, reliability, and network environment.
