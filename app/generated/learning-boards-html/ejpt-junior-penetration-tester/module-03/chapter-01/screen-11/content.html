Ensuring Safety and Avoiding Common Mistakes

Critical considerations for responsible security testing.

Safety Note: Always Get Permission!

Always, and without exception, ensure you have explicit, written permission from the asset owner before conducting any form of vulnerability assessment or penetration test. Unauthorized testing is illegal and unethical, and can lead to severe legal consequences. This is the most fundamental rule in ethical hacking.

Key Takeaway

Ethical and legal compliance, starting with explicit permission, is non-negotiable for any security assessment.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Misunderstanding Scope: Testing systems outside the agreed-upon boundaries.
  • No Communication Plan: Lacking clear protocols for reporting issues or emergencies.
  • Lack of Written Permission: Proceeding without explicit, documented authorization.
  • Ignoring RoE: Disregarding agreed-upon testing hours or forbidden actions.

Best Practice: Confirm Go/Stop

Always confirm the 'go-ahead' to start the assessment and the 'stop' conditions (e.g., if a critical system is impacted) with the client before you begin. This proactive approach minimizes risks and ensures a controlled testing environment.
