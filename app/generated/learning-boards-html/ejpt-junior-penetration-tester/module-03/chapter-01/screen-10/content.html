Rules of Engagement (RoE)

The agreed-upon conditions for conducting security assessments.

What are RoE?

Rules of Engagement (RoE) are a document outlining the specific conditions, limitations, and procedures for conducting a security assessment. They ensure ethical, legal, and controlled testing.

Key Components
  • Permitted Hours: Specific times when testing is allowed.
  • Communication Protocols: Who to contact and how during the assessment.
  • Acceptable Tools/Techniques: List of approved methods and tools.
  • Forbidden Actions: Activities strictly prohibited (e.g., DoS attacks).
Key Takeaway

RoE are crucial for defining the 'how' of an assessment, ensuring it's conducted ethically, legally, and without unintended consequences.

Example: Communication Plan

An RoE might specify that all critical findings or unexpected system impacts must be reported immediately via a dedicated secure chat channel to a specific client contact, even outside of normal business hours. For non-critical findings, a daily email summary might be sufficient. This ensures rapid response to any serious issues and maintains transparency.

Misconception: RoE as a Restriction

Some might view RoE as overly restrictive, but they are designed to protect everyone involved. They prevent legal issues, maintain client trust, and ensure the assessment focuses on agreed-upon objectives without causing unintended harm. A well-defined RoE empowers testers by providing clear boundaries.
