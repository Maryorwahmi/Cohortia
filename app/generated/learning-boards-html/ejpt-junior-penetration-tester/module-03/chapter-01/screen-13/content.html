Key Frameworks and Principles

Standardizing and guiding your assessments.

Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE)

A list of publicly disclosed cybersecurity vulnerabilities, each assigned a unique identifier for standardization.

Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS)

An open industry standard for assessing the severity of computer system security vulnerabilities with a numerical score.

Scope & Rules of Engagement (RoE)

The defined boundaries and conditions for a security assessment, ensuring ethical, legal, and controlled testing.

Summary Takeaway

CVE and CVSS standardize vulnerability identification and severity, while clear Scope and RoE ensure ethical and effective assessment execution.
