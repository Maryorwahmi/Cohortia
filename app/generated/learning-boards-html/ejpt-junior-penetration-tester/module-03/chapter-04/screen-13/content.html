Common Mistakes & Best Practices

  • •Being Noisy: Leaving temporary files, custom tools, or generating excessive log entries that alert defenders.
  • •Not Verifying: Failing to confirm privilege escalation attempts with commands like whoami or id.
  • •Ignoring Rules of Engagement: Performing disruptive actions (e.g., modifying system files, creating users, establishing persistence) without explicit permission.
  • •Lack of Documentation: Not documenting all actions taken, which is crucial for reporting and cleanup.
Safety Note

Always prioritize the integrity and availability of the client's systems. Any action in post-exploitation must be carefully considered against the rules of engagement to avoid unintended disruption.

Why Stealth Matters

Minimizing your footprint on a compromised system is essential for avoiding detection by security monitoring tools and incident response teams, making your assessment more realistic.

Ethical Responsibility

As a penetration tester, you are entrusted with sensitive access. Adhering to ethical guidelines and the scope of work protects both you and your client from legal and operational repercussions.

Key Takeaway

Stealth and strict adherence to ethical guidelines and rules of engagement are non-negotiable for professional and effective post-exploitation activities.
