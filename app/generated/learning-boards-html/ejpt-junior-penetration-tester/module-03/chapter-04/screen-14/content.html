Key Concepts

Reinforce your understanding of fundamental post-exploitation terminology.

Post-Exploitation

The phase after initial access, focused on maximizing control and achieving test objectives.

Enumeration

Gathering detailed information about a compromised system and its network environment.

Privilege Escalation

Gaining higher-level access permissions on a system than initially obtained.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts represent the core cycle of post-exploitation: understanding the goals, gathering intelligence, and elevating access to achieve full control.
