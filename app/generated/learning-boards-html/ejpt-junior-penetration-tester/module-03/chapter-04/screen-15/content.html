Key Concepts

Deepen your understanding of advanced post-exploitation terminology.

Persistence

Techniques used to maintain access to a compromised system across reboots or connection loss.

SUID (Set User ID) Bit

A special permission on Linux allowing an executable to run with the owner's permissions.

Stealth

The practice of performing actions on a target system without detection, minimizing traces.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering persistence, understanding SUID, and practicing stealth are crucial for advanced post-exploitation and maintaining covert control.
