Beyond eJPT: Advanced Certifications

Explore higher-level certifications that build upon your foundational eJPT skills for specialized expertise.

eCPPTv2

Advanced practical certification, building on eJPT skills with more complex scenarios.

OSCP

Highly respected, challenging hands-on certification with a rigorous 24-hour lab exam.

PNPT

Practical Network Penetration Tester, strong focus on real-world network scenarios.

CompTIA PenTest+

Vendor-neutral, intermediate-level, covering technical skills alongside management and compliance.

GIAC Certs

Industry-leading certifications from SANS, offering deep dives into specific areas.

Your Next Milestones

Advanced certifications validate specialized expertise and are critical for career progression in penetration testing.

Key Takeaway

Advanced certifications like OSCP, eCPPTv2, and GIAC are essential for validating specialized skills and advancing your penetration testing career beyond the foundational eJPT.
