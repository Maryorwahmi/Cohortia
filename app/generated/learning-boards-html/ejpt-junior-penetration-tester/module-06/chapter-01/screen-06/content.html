The Executive Summary: Speaking to Leadership

Provide high-level insights and business impact for non-technical stakeholders.

Audience Focus

Primarily for senior management and board members; avoid technical jargon.

Key Content

Engagement scope, critical findings, overall risk posture, strategic recommendations.

Impact Emphasis

Translate technical risks into business consequences (e.g., data breaches, service disruption).

Executive Summary: Key Elements
Engagement Scope
Critical Findings (High-Level)
Overall Risk Posture
Strategic Recommendations
Common Mistake

Including excessive technical jargon or granular details, which can alienate non-technical readers.
