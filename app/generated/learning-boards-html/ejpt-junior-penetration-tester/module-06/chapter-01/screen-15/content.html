Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Remediation Recommendations

Specific, actionable advice provided to the client on how to fix identified vulnerabilities.

Severity Rating

A classification (e.g., Critical, High, Medium, Low) assigned to vulnerabilities based on their potential impact and exploitability.

Audience Tailoring

Adapting the language and depth of content in a report to suit the technical understanding of its intended readers.

Summary Takeaway

Effective reports provide clear, prioritized remediation advice and are carefully tailored to meet the specific needs of diverse audiences.
