Tailoring Content and Actionable Recommendations

Reinforcing key principles for effective report communication and impact.

Audience Tailoring

Executive summaries need high-level risks and business impact, not granular technical data.

Actionable Recommendations

Vague advice is unhelpful; provide specific code examples or detailed steps for remediation.

Key Principles in Practice
Executive Summary Content

Appropriate: Top 3 critical risks and their potential business impact.

Inappropriate: Detailed Nmap output or specific exploit code.

Improving Vague Recommendations

Problem: "Implementing parameterized queries is too vague."

Solution: Provide a specific code example in their language demonstrating a parameterized query.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize clarity, audience relevance, and specificity to maximize the impact and utility of your penetration test reports.
