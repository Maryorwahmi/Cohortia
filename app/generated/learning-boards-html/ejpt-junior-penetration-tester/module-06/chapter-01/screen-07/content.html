Defining Boundaries: Scope and Methodology

Ensure transparency and set clear expectations for the penetration test assessment.

What was Tested

Clearly state the systems, applications, or networks included in the assessment.

What was Excluded

Explicitly list any areas or techniques that were out of scope (e.g., social engineering).

Timeframes & Techniques

Detail the duration of the test and the specific tools and methodologies used.

Pitfall to Avoid
