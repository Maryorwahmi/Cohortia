Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from This Chapter

Stakeholder Management

Identifying, engaging, and communicating with individuals or groups affected by the penetration test.

Audience Tailoring

Adapting communication content, language, and depth to suit different stakeholders' understanding and priorities.

Business Impact

The potential negative consequences of a security incident on an organization's operations, finances, reputation, or compliance.

Summary Takeaway

Effective communication is a strategic asset, enabling you to translate technical findings into actionable business decisions and build trust with clients.
