Translating Technical Risks to Business Impact

Quantifying the 'Why' for Leadership

Executive Questions

What is the overall risk? How much will it cost? What is the priority? How does it affect compliance/reputation?

Focus on 'Why'

Explain the strategic implications, not just the technical details of the vulnerability.

Jargon-Free Language

Use terms like 'data breaches,' 'service outages,' or 'regulatory fines' instead of technical acronyms.

Risk Translation Framework
Technical Vulnerability

SQL Injection in customer portal

Business Impact

Data breach, regulatory fines, reputational damage

Key Takeaway

Quantifying impact helps leadership prioritize and allocate resources effectively.
