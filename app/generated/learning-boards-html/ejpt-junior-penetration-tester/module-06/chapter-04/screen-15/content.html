Key Concepts Continued

More Essential Takeaways

Jargon-Free Communication

Explaining technical concepts in plain language, avoiding industry-specific terms for non-technical audiences.

Expectation Management

Proactively setting realistic understandings of the penetration test's scope, limitations, and potential outcomes.

Pushback

Resistance or disagreement from stakeholders regarding findings or recommendations, requiring professional handling.

Summary Takeaway

Clear, proactive communication and a resilient approach to challenges are hallmarks of a trusted security advisor.
