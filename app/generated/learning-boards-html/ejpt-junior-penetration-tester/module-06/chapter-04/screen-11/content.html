Strategies for Objective Communication

Focusing on Facts, Not Accusations

Stay Objective

Focus on the facts and evidence, not personal opinions or blame. Describe system behavior.

Emphasize Collaboration

Frame findings as opportunities for improvement, fostering a 'we're in this together' mindset.

Avoid Defensiveness

Resist the urge to become defensive or overly technical when challenged; maintain a calm, professional demeanor.

Objective Communication in Practice
Instead of:

"Your developers made a mistake in the authentication logic."

Say:

"Our tests showed that this vulnerability allows unauthenticated access to the system."

Instead of:

"You need to fix this immediately, it's critical!"

Say:

"Working together, we can implement these recommendations to significantly enhance your security posture."

Key Takeaway

Focus on factual evidence and collaborative language to ensure findings are addressed constructively.
