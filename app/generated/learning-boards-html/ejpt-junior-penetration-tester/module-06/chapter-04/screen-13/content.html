Active Listening and Building Credibility

Understanding Concerns to Guide Towards Solutions

Listen Actively

Understand stakeholder concerns regarding resources, feasibility, or perceived severity.

Offer Flexibility

Propose phased remediation plans or alternative controls when initial recommendations face resistance.

Build Credibility

Your role is to advise and inform, guiding clients to practical solutions, not to dictate.
