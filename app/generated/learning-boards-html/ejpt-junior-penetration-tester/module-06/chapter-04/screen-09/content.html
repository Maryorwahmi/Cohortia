Timely Updates and Out-of-Band Findings

Keeping Stakeholders Informed Throughout the Test

Regular Updates

Provide brief, consistent updates to keep stakeholders informed without overwhelming them.

Out-of-Band Findings

Immediately report critical vulnerabilities that require urgent attention outside the regular reporting schedule.

Build Trust

Proactive communication demonstrates commitment to security and allows for rapid mitigation.

Key Takeaway

Consistent communication and immediate reporting of critical issues are vital for effective security management.
