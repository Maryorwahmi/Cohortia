Quantifying Impact and Offering Solutions

Moving Beyond Problems to Practical Recommendations

Quantify Impact

Translate technical risks into tangible business impacts (e.g., financial losses, operational downtime).

Offer Solutions

Always accompany findings with clear, actionable recommendations, not just problems.

Empower Action

Provide both the 'what if' and the 'what to do' to enable informed decision-making and effective remediation.

From Problem to Solution
Problem:

"Unpatched server with critical vulnerability."

Quantified Impact:

"Could lead to a 72-hour outage of your e-commerce platform, potentially costing $X in lost revenue."

Solution:

"Implement a phased patching strategy, starting with non-production environments, and ensure regular vulnerability scanning."

Key Takeaway

Always pair a quantified risk with a clear, actionable recommendation to drive effective security improvements.
