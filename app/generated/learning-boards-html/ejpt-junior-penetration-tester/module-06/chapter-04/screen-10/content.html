Handling Difficult Conversations and Pushback

Navigating Resistance with Professionalism

Expect Resistance

Be prepared for pushback from teams feeling criticized or management viewing findings as unnecessary expenses.

Maintain Professionalism

Approach these conversations with empathy, objectivity, and a data-driven mindset.

Focus on Solutions

Shift the conversation from blame to collaborative problem-solving and improvement.

Common Sources of Pushback
Perceived Criticism of Work
Resource Constraints / Cost Concerns
Skepticism about Severity / Feasibility
Key Takeaway

Your credibility is built on your ability to guide clients to practical solutions, even amidst resistance.
