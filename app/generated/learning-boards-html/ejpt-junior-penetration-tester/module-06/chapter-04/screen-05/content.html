Communicating with Technical Teams

Providing Granular Details for Remediation

What They Need

Exact vulnerability locations, proof-of-concept steps, specific code snippets, and precise remediation instructions.

Focus

The 'how' and 'where' to fix the problem. They are the implementers.

Your Tone

Collaborative, offering solutions rather than merely pointing out flaws.

Technical Communication Essentials
Exact Locations & PoC
Code Snippets & Fixes
Timely & Collaborative
Actionable Recommendations
Key Takeaway

For technical teams, focus on 'how' and 'where' to fix, with precise details and a collaborative tone.
