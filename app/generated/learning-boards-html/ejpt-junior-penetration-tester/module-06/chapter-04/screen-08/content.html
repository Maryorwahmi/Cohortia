Proactive Expectation Management

Setting the Stage for a Successful Engagement

Start Early

Begin managing expectations even before the test commences, during initial discussions.

Define Clearly

Explicitly outline the scope, methodology, and crucial limitations of the penetration test.

Prevent Misunderstandings

Transparency upfront minimizes unrealistic expectations and potential pushback later.

Key Elements of Expectation Management
Clear Scope Definition
Detailed Methodology
Explicit Limitations
Key Takeaway

Proactive communication of scope and limitations builds trust and prevents future conflicts.
