Chapter Summary

Key Terms to Remember

Reinforcing Your Understanding

CVSS Temporal Metrics

Characteristics that change over time (e.g., Exploit Code Maturity).

CVSS Environmental Metrics

Characteristics unique to a user's environment (e.g., Confidentiality Requirement).

Prioritization

Ranking vulnerabilities based on severity, business impact, and feasibility.

Compensating Controls

Alternative security measures to mitigate risk when primary controls are unavailable.

Key Takeaway

A solid grasp of these terms will enable you to confidently discuss and implement robust cybersecurity remediation strategies.
