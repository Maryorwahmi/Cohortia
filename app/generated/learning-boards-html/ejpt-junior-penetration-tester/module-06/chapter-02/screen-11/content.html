Prioritizing Remediation: Beyond the Score

Considering Business Impact and Feasibility

Effective remediation goes beyond technical severity. It requires a strategic approach that weighs CVSS scores against an organization's unique business context and operational realities.

Key Takeaway

Effective prioritization combines technical severity (CVSS) with business impact and the practical feasibility of remediation, ensuring resources are allocated wisely.

Common Mistake

Prioritizing solely based on CVSS score without considering the asset's criticality or the business impact can lead to misallocated resources.

CVSS Score

Technical severity of the vulnerability (e.g., Critical, High, Medium).

  • Objective technical rating
  • Standardized communication

Business Impact & Feasibility

Organizational context and practicality of fixing.

  • Asset criticality
  • Cost & complexity of fix

Prioritization

The combined assessment to determine remediation order.
