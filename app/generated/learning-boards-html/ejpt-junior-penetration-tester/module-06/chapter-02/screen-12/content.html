Crafting Effective Remediation Recommendations

Clarity, Specificity, and Practicality

Your recommendations are the bridge between identifying a vulnerability and fixing it. They must be precise, actionable, and tailored to the client's environment.

Key Takeaway

Recommendations must be clear, specific, and practical, guiding clients with actionable steps rather than generic advice to ensure successful remediation.

Common Mistake

Recommending solutions that are overly complex, prohibitively expensive, or disrupt critical business operations without sufficient justification.

1. Be Clear and Specific

Avoid vague statements. State exactly what needs to be done.

  • Example: "Enforce 12-character password length."

2. Provide Actionable Steps

Outline the 'how-to' for implementing the fix.

  • Example: "Upgrade Apache HTTP Server to version X.Y.Z."

3. Consider Practicality

Tailor recommendations to the client's environment and resources.

  • Suggest compensating controls if a full fix isn't immediate.

4. Include Supporting Information

Reference vendor advisories, CVEs, or official patches.

  • Helps clients find detailed information.
