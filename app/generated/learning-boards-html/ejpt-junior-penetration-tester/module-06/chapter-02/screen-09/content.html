Environmental Metrics: Tailoring Scores to Your Context

Adjusting Severity Based on Business Importance and Controls

These metrics are unique to a user's environment, allowing organizations to reflect the importance of affected systems and the presence of compensating controls.

Key Takeaway

Environmental metrics allow organizations to customize CVSS scores, reflecting the unique importance of affected systems and the presence of existing security controls.

Example

A vulnerability on a critical production server will have a higher environmental score than the same vulnerability on a non-critical test server.

Confidentiality Requirement (CR)

Importance of confidentiality to the organization (Low, Medium, High).

Integrity Requirement (IR)

Importance of integrity to the organization (Low, Medium, High).

Availability Requirement (AR)

Importance of availability to the organization (Low, Medium, High).

Compensating Controls

Presence of existing security measures that mitigate risk (e.g., WAF, IPS).
