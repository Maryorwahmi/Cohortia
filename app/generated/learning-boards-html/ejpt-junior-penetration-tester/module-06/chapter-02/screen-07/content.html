Base Metrics: Impact Factors

Quantifying the Damage of a Successful Exploit

These metrics describe the consequences to the affected system if the vulnerability is successfully exploited, focusing on the core security properties.

Key Takeaway

Impact metrics assess the potential harm to confidentiality, integrity, and availability, directly reflecting the severity of a successful attack.

Example

A remote code execution (RCE) leading to complete compromise of C, I, and A would result in a Critical score.

Confidentiality (C)

Impact on the secrecy of information (None, Low, High).

Integrity (I)

Impact on the trustworthiness and accuracy of data (None, Low, High).

Availability (A)

Impact on access to the system or its resources (None, Low, High).
