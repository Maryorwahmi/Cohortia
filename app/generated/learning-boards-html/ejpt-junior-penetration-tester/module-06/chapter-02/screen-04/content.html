Defining Cybersecurity Risk

Understanding Likelihood, Impact, and Vulnerability

Cybersecurity risk is the potential for loss or damage resulting from a cyber attack. It's a combination of three key elements that help us quantify the danger a vulnerability poses.

Key Takeaway

Risk is the product of a threat's likelihood to exploit a vulnerability and the resulting impact, guiding where to focus remediation efforts.

Pro Tip

Don't just list vulnerabilities; assess their true risk by considering the context of the asset and potential business consequences.

Risk = Likelihood × Impact

The Risk Equation

A foundational model for understanding cybersecurity risk.

Likelihood

Probability of a threat exploiting a vulnerability.

Impact

Consequences of a successful exploitation.
