Navigating Common Ethical and Legal Traps

Prioritizing caution, clarification, and ethical conduct to build trust.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overlooking SOW Details: Missing critical scope boundaries.
  • Testing Beyond Scope: Unauthorized exploration due to curiosity.
  • Public Disclosure: Discussing client findings in unauthorized forums.

Best Practices for Ethical Conduct

  • Err on Caution: Always choose the safest, most authorized path.
  • Seek Clarification: If unsure, ask the client's point of contact.
  • Prioritize Ethics: Uphold integrity above all technical excitement.
Check Your Understanding

Why is it crucial to seek clarification from the client, rather than making assumptions, when encountering an ambiguous scope boundary?

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize caution, seek explicit clarification, and uphold ethical conduct to avoid legal traps and build a strong professional reputation.
