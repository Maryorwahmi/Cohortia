Key Concepts to Remember

Essential principles for ethical and legal penetration testing.

"Get Permission First"

The fundamental principle requiring explicit, written authorization before any testing activities.

Scope of Work (SOW)

A formal document outlining the precise boundaries, objectives, and deliverables of an engagement.

Going Out of Scope

Performing testing activities on targets not explicitly authorized, which can have legal consequences.

Summary Takeaway

Adhering to explicit permission and the defined scope is paramount to avoid legal issues and maintain professional integrity in penetration testing.
