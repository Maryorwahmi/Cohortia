Data Protection

Ethical and Legal Obligations for Sensitive Data

Protecting PII, intellectual property, and confidential client data.

Types of Sensitive Information

PII

Personally Identifiable Information (e.g., names, addresses, SSN).

Intellectual Property

Proprietary code, designs, algorithms, or business strategies.

Key Data Handling Principles

  • Confidentiality: Never share client data with unauthorized parties.
  • Data Minimization: Only collect data absolutely necessary for the test.
  • Secure Storage: Encrypt and control access to all collected data.
  • Proper Disposal: Securely erase all client data after the engagement.
Key Takeaway

Protecting sensitive client data is a non-negotiable ethical and legal obligation, requiring strict adherence to confidentiality, minimization, secure storage, and proper disposal.
