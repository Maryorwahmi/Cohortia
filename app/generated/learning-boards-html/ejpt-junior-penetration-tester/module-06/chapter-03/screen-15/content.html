More Key Concepts

Further essential principles for ethical and legal penetration testing.

Unauthorized Access

Gaining entry to a computer system or network without explicit permission, typically a criminal offense.

Responsible Disclosure

Reporting vulnerabilities privately to vendors/owners, allowing time for remediation before public disclosure.

Sensitive Information

Confidential data, PII, or intellectual property encountered during a test, requiring strict protection.

Summary Takeaway

Ethical handling of vulnerabilities and sensitive data, coupled with legal compliance, is crucial for maintaining trust and professional integrity.
