Professional Conduct

When You Stumble Upon a Flaw Outside Scope

The ethical approach to reporting unexpected vulnerabilities.

Ethical Steps for Out-of-Scope Findings

  • 1Immediately Stop: Cease all interaction with the out-of-scope system.
  • 2Document Discovery: Record details without further exploitation.
  • 3Inform Client: Notify the client's point of contact promptly.
  • 4Seek Permission: Request explicit authorization before any further action.

Why This Approach is Essential

  • Legal Protection: Avoids charges of unauthorized access.
  • Maintains Trust: Demonstrates integrity and adherence to agreements.
  • Professionalism: Upholds ethical standards in complex situations.
Ethical Dilemma Example

You're testing clientA.com and find a critical flaw in clientB.com (a separate entity). You must report to Client A's contact, not directly to Client B, and await instructions.

Key Takeaway

Always stop, document, and inform your client about out-of-scope discoveries, seeking explicit permission before any further action.
