Understanding the Statement of Work (SOW)

How the SOW precisely outlines what, where, and when you can test.

Key Elements of an SOW

  • Scope Definition: Specific systems, networks, applications, and functionalities.
  • Testing Types: Black-box, white-box, internal, external, etc.
  • Timeframes: Start and end dates for the engagement.
  • Deliverables: What reports or findings are expected.

SOW: Your Legal & Ethical Compass

  • Prevents Misunderstandings: Clearly defines expectations for both parties.
  • Legal Protection: Serves as proof of authorized activities.
  • Scope Enforcement: Establishes clear boundaries for testing.
Pro Tip

Always read the SOW thoroughly and ask for clarification on any ambiguous points before starting. Never assume implied permission.

Key Takeaway

The SOW is your definitive guide, legally and ethically, for every penetration test; understand and adhere to its every detail.
