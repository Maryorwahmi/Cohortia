The Principles of Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure

How to report vulnerabilities to vendors to ensure timely remediation.

The Responsible Disclosure Timeline

  • 1Discovery: Identify a vulnerability in a product or service.
  • 2Private Contact: Report details to the vendor/owner privately.
  • 3Remediation Period: Allow 60-90 days for the vendor to patch.
  • 4Public Disclosure: Only after patch release or reasonable timeframe.

Why Responsible Disclosure Matters

  • Protects Users: Prevents widespread exploitation of unpatched flaws.
  • Builds Trust: Fosters collaboration between researchers and vendors.
  • Maintains Reputation: Establishes you as a responsible security professional.
Risk of Premature Disclosure

Publicly disclosing a zero-day without vendor remediation can lead to widespread attacks and severe damage to your professional standing.

Key Takeaway

Always follow responsible disclosure guidelines: private vendor notification, a reasonable remediation period, and then public disclosure.
