All-in-One Output with -oA

The -oA <basename> flag is a powerful option that saves Nmap scan results in all three major formats (normal, XML, and greppable) simultaneously, using a specified filename prefix.

  • •Generates .nmap (normal), .xml (XML), and .gnmap (greppable) files.
  • •Ensures all data is available for different analysis needs.
  • •Highly efficient, avoiding multiple scan commands for different outputs.
  • •Recommended for primary scans to maximize data utility.
Practical Tip

Always use -oA for your main reconnaissance scans to ensure you have a complete and versatile record of your findings.

Key Takeaway

-oA simplifies Nmap output management, providing a robust and flexible dataset for all stages of a penetration test, from reporting to automated processing.

Terminal Demo: Using -oA for All Formats
$nmap -sS -sV -O -sC -p 1-1000 -oA full_scan_report <target_IP>
