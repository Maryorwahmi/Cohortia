Combined Scan Strategy

Comprehensive Scanning: -sS, -sV, -O

Combining the SYN scan (-sS) with service/version detection (-sV) and OS detection (-O) is a standard practice for thorough reconnaissance, providing a rich dataset in a single command.

  • •-sS: Fast and stealthy port scanning.
  • •-sV: Identifies specific services and their versions.
  • •-O: Detects the target's operating system.
  • •Maximizes information gathered in one efficient pass.
Pro Tip

Always start with a comprehensive scan like nmap -sS -sV -O to establish a strong baseline of information before diving into specific vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaway

Orchestrating Nmap flags like -sS, -sV, and -O provides a powerful, multi-layered approach to target reconnaissance, yielding actionable intelligence.
