Responsible Scanning: Ethics and Impact

Authorization, Documentation, and Progressive Aggression

Authorization is Key

Never perform active scanning without explicit, written permission from the target owner. This is non-negotiable.

Document Everything

Meticulously record all scans, commands used, and results. This provides an audit trail and forms the basis for reports.

Progressive Aggression

Start with less intrusive scans and only increase aggression when necessary and justified, minimizing potential impact.

Key Takeaway

Ethical conduct, authorization, and careful planning are as important as technical skills in penetration testing.
