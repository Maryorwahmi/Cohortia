Uncovering Services with Port Scanning

Why Open Ports Matter for Attack Vectors

What is a Port?

A communication endpoint on a host, identified by a number, where services listen for connections.

Why it Matters

Open ports indicate running services, which are potential entry points or attack vectors for exploitation.

Key Takeaway

Port scanning is crucial for identifying active services and understanding a target's attack surface.
