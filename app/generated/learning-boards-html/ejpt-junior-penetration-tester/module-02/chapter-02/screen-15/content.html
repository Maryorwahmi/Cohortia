Key Concepts

Advanced Techniques and Ethical Considerations

TCP SYN Scan (-sS)

A 'half-open' scan that sends SYN and receives SYN/ACK without completing the handshake, offering stealth.

TCP Connect Scan (-sT)

Performs a full TCP three-way handshake, reliable but less stealthy, often used without raw packet privileges.

UDP Scan (-sU)

Scans for UDP ports, essential for services like DNS/DHCP, but slower and less reliable due to connectionless nature.

Port States

Nmap reports ports as Open (service listening), Closed (no service, accessible), or Filtered (blocked by firewall).

Summary Takeaway

Mastering Nmap's diverse scanning techniques and understanding port states, coupled with strict ethical adherence, are critical for effective and responsible penetration testing.
