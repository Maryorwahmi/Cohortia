Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

OSINT (Open Source Intelligence)

Information gathered from publicly available sources, a key component of passive reconnaissance.

Google Dorking

Using advanced search operators in Google to find specific, often sensitive, information exposed online.

DNS Enumeration

The process of discovering information about a target's DNS records, including subdomains and mail servers.

Chapter Summary

OSINT, including Google Dorking, leverages public data for stealthy information gathering, while DNS enumeration actively maps target infrastructure through DNS record queries.
