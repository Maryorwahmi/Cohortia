Gathering Clues with Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)

Leveraging publicly available information without direct interaction.

What is OSINT?

Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) is information gathered from publicly available sources. This includes public records, social media, company websites, and general search engines. It's a non-intrusive way to build a target profile.

Examples of OSINT Sources

  • •Public Records: Company registrations, domain ownership.
  • •Social Media: Employee profiles, company announcements.
  • •Company Websites: Technologies used, organizational structure.
  • •News Articles: Recent events, partnerships, security incidents.

Google Dorking Explained

Google Dorking uses specific search operators to find sensitive information inadvertently exposed online. It's a powerful technique for uncovering files, directories, or data that shouldn't be public.

site:target.com filetype:pdf confidential

Example: Finding confidential PDF documents on a target's domain.

Key Takeaway

OSINT and Google Dorking are essential passive reconnaissance techniques that provide a wealth of information without risking detection.
