Shodan: The Search Engine for Internet-Connected Devices

Discovering external footprints without direct scanning.

What is Shodan?

Shodan is a specialized search engine that indexes internet-connected devices, not just websites. It continuously scans the internet to collect information about servers, routers, IoT devices, and more.

Information Shodan Reveals

  • •Open Ports: Services running on exposed devices.
  • •Service Banners: Software versions, configurations.
  • •Geographic Location: Physical location of devices.
  • •Potential Vulnerabilities: Outdated software, default credentials.

How Shodan Works (Passively)

When you search Shodan, you are querying its pre-collected database, not directly interacting with the target. This makes it a passive reconnaissance tool, leaving no trace on the target's network logs.

Key Takeaway

Shodan provides a global view of exposed services and devices, offering critical insights for reconnaissance without direct interaction.
