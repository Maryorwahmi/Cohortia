Avoiding Pitfalls: Common Reconnaissance Errors

Identifying and preventing mistakes that can compromise your penetration test.

Mistake 1: Being Too Noisy Too Early

Launching aggressive active scans or queries prematurely can trigger Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) or firewalls. This alerts the target to your presence, potentially leading to detection and countermeasures.

Impact: Compromised stealth, blocked access, or early termination of the pen test.
Pro Tip

Always prioritize passive reconnaissance first to gather foundational intelligence before considering any active methods.

Mistake 2: Not Documenting Findings Meticulously

Failing to keep detailed notes of all discovered information (IPs, domains, open ports, software versions, etc.) can lead to lost context, missed connections, and wasted effort later in the test.

Best Practice: Maintain a structured log or database for all reconnaissance data.
Consequence

Poor documentation can lead to repeating steps, overlooking critical clues, and ultimately an incomplete or ineffective penetration test.
