Ethical Hacking

The Ethical Compass: Legal and Moral Reconnaissance

Ensuring all information gathering adheres to legal frameworks and explicit permissions.

Explicit Permission is Non-Negotiable

Always obtain clear, written permission from the target organization before initiating any reconnaissance activities. Without it, even passive information gathering can be illegal.

Legal Consequence: Unauthorized reconnaissance can lead to severe legal penalties, including fines and imprisonment.
Ethical Principle

The primary goal is to test and improve security, not to cause harm or violate laws. Your actions must always be constructive.

Adhere to Scope of Engagement

Strictly limit your information gathering to the systems and networks explicitly defined in your engagement contract. Going beyond this scope is unauthorized and unethical.

Professional Responsibility: Maintain integrity and trust by operating within agreed-upon boundaries.
Misconception

'Publicly available' does not automatically mean 'legal to collect without permission' in the context of a penetration test. Always get explicit consent.
