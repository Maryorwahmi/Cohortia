Information Disclosure

Analyzing robots.txt and sitemap.xml

These files, intended for search engine crawlers, often inadvertently reveal hidden directories or sensitive areas.

robots.txt: The 'Don't Look Here' Sign

Located at http://example.com/robots.txt, it instructs benevolent crawlers which parts of a site to avoid.

User-agent: *
Disallow: /admin/
Disallow: /private/backups/

This explicitly points out sensitive directories to an attacker.

Key Takeaway

Always manually check robots.txt and sitemap.xml; they often contain direct pointers to sensitive or hidden resources.

sitemap.xml: The 'Here's Everything' List

Found at http://example.com/sitemap.xml, it lists all pages and files a website owner wants search engines to crawl.

<url>
  <loc>http://example.com/secret-report.pdf</loc>
</url>
<url>
  <loc>http://example.com/old-api-docs/</loc>
</url>

Can expose paths to interesting or forgotten resources.

Common Mistake

Assuming these files are only for SEO and contain no valuable information for a penetration tester.
