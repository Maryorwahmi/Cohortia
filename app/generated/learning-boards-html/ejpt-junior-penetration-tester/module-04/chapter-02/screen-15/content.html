Key Concepts: Advanced Reconnaissance

Expanding Your Reach with Files and Open-Source Intelligence

Subfinder/Amass

Command-line tools for automated subdomain discovery from public data sources.

robots.txt

A file that instructs web robots which pages or files they should not request, often revealing hidden paths.

sitemap.xml

A file listing pages and files a website owner wants search engines to crawl, sometimes exposing interesting resources.

OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence)

The practice of collecting and analyzing information from publicly available sources to gain insights about a target.

Google Dorking

Using advanced search operators in Google to find specific, often sensitive, information or hidden pages on websites.

Chapter Summary

These advanced reconnaissance techniques provide a holistic view of your target, uncovering critical details for effective penetration testing.
