Module 02 › Chapter 2.2 › Final Reminders
Encapsulation
Keep variables private unless external access is required; use [SerializeField] where useful.
Casting
Cast explicitly when converting types that could lose information.
Logging
Write concise, descriptive Debug.Log messages to make Console output immediately useful.
Final takeaway
Small, deliberate experiments in the Editor plus clear logging are the quickest route to mastering variables, types, and operators in Unity.
