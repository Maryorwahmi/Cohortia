Hands-on: Rigidbody vs CharacterController
Step-by-step setup and what to observe in runtime.
Setup
  • Create a Ground plane tagged Ground.
  • Create two Cubes: one named PlayerRigidbody with a Rigidbody, one named PlayerCharacterController with a CharacterController.
  • Attach the respective scripts and tune Height/Radius on the controller.
What to test
  • Compare jump arcs and landing behavior.
  • Observe pushing interactions with crates and wind forces.
  • Intentionally cause sliding and floating and fix them.
Observation
Rigidbody reacts to forces and pushes objects; CharacterController handles collisions but needs explicit code to affect rigidbodies.
Key Takeaway
Running both in parallel is the fastest way to learn the differences and pick the right approach for your game.
