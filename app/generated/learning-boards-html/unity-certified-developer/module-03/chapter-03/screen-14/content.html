Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Rigidbody Movement
Force, velocity, or MovePosition in FixedUpdate gives full physics interaction.
Transform Movement
Direct position updates are fast but ignore physics and collisions.
CharacterController
Collision-aware Move with manual gravity and controller-specific callbacks.
Summary

Choose the controller that best matches whether you need physics interactions, deterministic control, or collision-aware player movement.
