Common issues and how to fix them
Sticky movement
Reset vertical velocity when grounded and ensure ground checks are reliable.
Sliding
Increase Rigidbody drag or use PhysicMaterials with friction to stop indefinite sliding.
Floating jumps
Implement manual gravity for CharacterController and apply it every Update.
Update loop mismatch
Use FixedUpdate for physics and Update for input-driven Move calls; mixing causes inconsistent behavior.
Mixing controllers
Never place a Rigidbody and CharacterController on the same GameObject; choose one approach per actor.
Quick diagnostic
Ask: which update loop? is gravity applied? is ground check reliable? These reveal most issues quickly.
