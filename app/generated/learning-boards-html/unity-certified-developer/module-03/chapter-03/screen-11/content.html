Ground check patterns
Choose a method that matches your movement speed and environment complexity.
controller.isGrounded
Simple and built-in for CharacterController; reliable after Move() updates.
Raycast
Cast a short ray downward from the feet; tune distance to avoid false positives on slopes.
Collision callbacks
Use OnCollisionEnter/Stay for Rigidbody players and check contact tags or normals for ground detection.
Practical tip
Combine a short raycast with controller.isGrounded or collision flags to handle moving platforms and quick hops robustly.
Pitfall
Too-large raycasts pick up distant geometry and create false grounded states; tune distances conservatively.
Key Takeaway
Reliable ground checks prevent unintended double jumps and ensure consistent landing behavior.
