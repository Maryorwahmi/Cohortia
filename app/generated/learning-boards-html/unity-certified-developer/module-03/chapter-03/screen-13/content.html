Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Tuning & Performance
Tune for feel, not just numbers
Small parameter changes can meaningfully alter perceived responsiveness.
Drag & friction
Use Rigidbody drag and PhysicMaterials to control slide and stopping behavior rather than clamping velocities abruptly.
Acceleration
Acceleration factor determines how quickly you approach target speed. Tune it with the intended game feel in mind.
Frame-rate stability
Keep physics updates in FixedUpdate and scale movements with the appropriate delta time to avoid inconsistent behavior across frame rates.
Performance tip
Prefer a single raycast or contact flag for ground checks rather than many overlapping geometry queries every frame.
Playtest loop
Iterate with small changes and test across platforms to ensure movement feels consistent and performant.
Key Takeaway
Tune physics and controller parameters iteratively; subtle adjustments often produce the best improvements in feel.
