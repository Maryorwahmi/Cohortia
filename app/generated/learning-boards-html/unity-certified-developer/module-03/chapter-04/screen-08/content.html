When to use triggers
  • Collectibles that vanish when touched.
  • Area audio or dialogue triggers.
  • Checkpoints and safe zones that don't block movement.
Example
A collectible checks other.CompareTag("Player") on OnTriggerEnter and destroys itself after granting score.
Inspector checklist
  • Is Trigger is checked on the collectible collider.
  • The player has a Collider and a Rigidbody (can be kinematic).
  • Tags and layers are correctly set for your checks.
Key Takeaway

Triggers are overlap detectors. Ensure the right Rigidbody and collider settings and avoid heavy per-frame work in Stay callbacks.
