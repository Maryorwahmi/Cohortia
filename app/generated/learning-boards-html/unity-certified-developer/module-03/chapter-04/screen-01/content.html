Raycasting, Collision Callbacks & Event Handling
Detect objects along paths, respond to collisions and triggers, and wire lightweight event-driven interactions between GameObjects.
In this lesson
You will practice raycasts and sphere casts, filter results with LayerMasks, and implement collision and trigger callbacks that call methods on hit objects.
  • Raycasts and SphereCasts: line and volume queries.
  • Collision vs Trigger callbacks and the need for Rigidbody.
  • FixedUpdate: where physics runs and why it matters.
  • Event-driven communication: modular reactions across objects.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Follow the hands-on activity to build a camera-centered shooter, a destructible target, and a collectible trigger—each demonstrating one concept.
