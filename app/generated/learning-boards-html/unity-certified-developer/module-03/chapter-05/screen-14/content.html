Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Key Concepts
Joints

Components that define relative motion between rigidbodies such as Hinge, Fixed, Spring, and Configurable.

ConfigurableJoint

Most versatile joint, allowing fine control over linear and angular motion, limits, and springs along each axis.

Ragdoll Physics

Use Rigidbody and ConfigurableJoint on bones to simulate realistic collapse; toggle physics by disabling Animator and enabling rigidbodies.

Summary: Use joints intentionally, tune masses and limits, and switch control cleanly between animator and physics.
