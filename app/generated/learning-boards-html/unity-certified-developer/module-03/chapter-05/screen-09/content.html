Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Sleeping Rigidbodies
Definition

What sleeping means

Sleeping excludes resting rigidbodies from per-step simulation until a wake event occurs, saving CPU cycles in complex scenes.
Example
A crate comes to rest and sleeps. When the player pushes it, it wakes and resumes simulation, then may sleep again when settled.
Best practices
Avoid forced wakes
Do not write velocity = zero every frame to prevent sleeping; let physics manage rest.
Wake intentionally
Use WakeUp or apply a single impulse when a real event should reactivate the object.
Misconception
Sleeping objects are not broken; they simply pause until a meaningful interaction occurs.
Key Takeaway

Design interactions so objects can sleep and only wake for genuine events. That keeps physics affordable at scale.
