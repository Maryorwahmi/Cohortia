Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Joints Overview
Definition

Joints control relative motion

Joints connect two rigidbodies and constrain linear or angular movement to simulate mechanical links or anatomy.

HingeJoint
Single-axis rotation, supports limits and motors for doors and wheels.
FixedJoint
Locks position and rotation so two bodies move as one.
SpringJoint
Spring-like force between objects, useful for elastic links and suspension.
Key Takeaway

Use simple joints for predictable mechanics and ConfigurableJoint when you need per-axis control.

ConfigurableJoint demo card
Capabilities
  • Lock or free linear X/Y/Z motion
  • Limit angular X/Y/Z motion and set springs
  • Attach motors on axes for active movement
Concrete example
Torso mass = 10, Limb mass = 1. Use ConfigurableJoint angular limits to allow elbow bend while locking twist.
Common mistake
Adding a joint without Rigidbody on both objects or misplacing colliders leads to jitter and unexpected constraints.
Pro tip
Tune mass and limit springs together. A light limb with strong springs will snap back unnaturally.
