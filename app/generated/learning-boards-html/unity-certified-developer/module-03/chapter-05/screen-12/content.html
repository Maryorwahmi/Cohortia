Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Bottlenecks & Fixes
Common bottlenecks
  • Large numbers of active Rigidbody objects
  • Non-convex MeshColliders on dynamic objects
  • Excessive raycasts or overlap checks without masks
  • Unfiltered collision checks between layers
Mitigations
Profile first
Use the Unity profiler to find the heaviest physics cost before changing systems.
Use primitives
Replace mesh colliders on moving parts with boxes, spheres, or capsules.
Layer filtering
Disable unnecessary layer pairs in the Collision Matrix to cut costly checks.
Misconception
Avoid changing many variables at once; iterate and re-profile to confirm improvements.
Key Takeaway

Target the biggest physics costs first: primitives, fewer dynamic bodies, and layer filtering give the largest wins.
