PhysicMaterial basics
Adjust static friction to control when sliding starts. Adjust dynamic friction to control behavior while sliding. Bounciness controls energy returned on impact.
Low friction
Simulates ice; objects slide further and are harder to control.
High bounciness
Makes objects rebound strongly; ideal for rubbery gameplay elements.
Key Takeaway

Assign PhysicMaterials to colliders to control sliding and bounce. Test with representative masses and velocities for correct feel.

Concrete example
Bowling lane: very low friction PhysicMaterial. Rubber ball: high bounciness and moderate friction. Test collisions to tune bounce heights.
Misconception
Expecting bounciness alone to dictate rebound. Mass, approach velocity, and combined materials determine final bounce response.
