Rigidbody: the physics identity

When attached, a Rigidbody gives an object mass and makes it responsive to gravity, forces, and collisions. Colliders alone only declare shape.

Properties: mass, drag, angularDrag, useGravity, isKinematic.
Movement APIs: AddForce, AddTorque, MovePosition, MoveRotation.
Best practice: perform physics calls inside FixedUpdate for consistent results.
Key Takeaway

Attach a Rigidbody when you want authentic physics behaviour. Move via Rigidbody APIs to avoid unpredictable collisions.

Concrete example
Player cube with Rigidbody: mass 1, drag 0. When you call AddForce(Vector3.up * jumpForce, ForceMode.Impulse) inside FixedUpdate, the Rigidbody gains upward velocity and the engine integrates gravity and collisions reliably.
Common mistake
Moving a Rigidbody by changing transform.position or rotation causes physics to be bypassed, producing tunnelling, jitter, or missed collision events.
