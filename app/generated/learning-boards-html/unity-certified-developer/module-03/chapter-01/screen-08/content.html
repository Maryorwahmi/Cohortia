Collision vs Trigger
Collisions block movement and produce contact details available through the Collision parameter. Triggers detect overlap without blocking and supply a Collider parameter.
Collision callbacks
OnCollisionEnter, OnCollisionStay, OnCollisionExit. Use Collision.contacts for impact points and normals.
Trigger callbacks
OnTriggerEnter, OnTriggerStay, OnTriggerExit. Use for pickups, zones, and detection without physical blocking.
Key Takeaway

Choose collision when you need physics response and contact data; choose trigger for non-blocking detection. Ensure at least one Rigidbody is present for callbacks.

Concrete example
A pickup marked Is Trigger: true inside a PickupZone will be detected by OnTriggerEnter if either the pickup or the player has a Rigidbody. If you need the exact hit location use a solid collider and OnCollisionEnter instead.
Misconception
Expecting trigger callbacks to include contact points. Triggers only provide the overlapping collider; no contact data is available.
