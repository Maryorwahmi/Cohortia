Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Pitfalls
Common mistakes
Moving Rigidbody with Transform: causes jitter and missed collision responses.
Missing Rigidbody: OnCollision/OnTrigger callbacks won't fire if neither object has one.
Non-convex MeshColliders on dynamic bodies: performance and correctness issues.
Overlapping colliders at start: Enter events might not trigger until movement occurs.
Safety notes & checks
  • Use MovePosition/MoveRotation or AddForce for Rigidbody movement.
  • Ensure at least one Rigidbody on interacting objects.
  • Prefer primitive colliders and convex meshes for moving objects.
  • Place physics calls in FixedUpdate and test under variable frame rates.
Check your understanding

Before playtesting, run this checklist: Rigidbody present, no transform-based movement, collider types reviewed, FixedUpdate for physics.
