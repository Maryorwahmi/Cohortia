Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Configuring Rigidbody
Tuning steps
1
Set mass relatively
Choose mass values that reflect relative sizes; use larger forces for heavier objects.
2
Adjust drag
Increase drag to reduce sliding; angular drag to reduce unwanted spin.
3
Test with forces
Apply a known force and observe displacement; iterate on mass and drag until behavior matches design.
Key Takeaway

Mass and drag determine how objects respond to AddForce. Tune them together and test under realistic collisions.

Concrete example
If a player feels too floaty when jumping, increase mass or reduce jumpForce. If they slide too long on landing, raise drag slightly to shorten stopping distance.
Misconception
Reducing mass to make an object 'easier to push' then using the same forces often causes instability. Use ForceMode options or increase forces to match mass instead.
Try this
Apply a horizontal AddForce and measure how far an object travels. Adjust drag until travel distance matches your design constraint.
