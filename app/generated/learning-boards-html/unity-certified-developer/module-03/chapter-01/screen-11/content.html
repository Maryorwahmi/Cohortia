ForceMode options
Force
Continuous force that respects mass; good for steady pushes.
Acceleration
Continuous acceleration that ignores mass; use for consistent movement across masses.
Impulse
Instantaneous force for impacts and jumps; applied once.
VelocityChange
Immediate velocity change ignoring mass; use sparingly for direct control.
Key Takeaway

Select ForceMode based on whether you want mass to influence motion and whether the effect is instantaneous or continuous.

Concrete examples
  • Jump: AddForce(up * jumpForce, Impulse)
  • Thrust: AddForce(forward * thrust, Force)
  • Uniform acceleration across masses: ForceMode.Acceleration
Misconception
Using VelocityChange frequently creates unrealistic, non-physical motion. Reserve it for specific gameplay requirements.
