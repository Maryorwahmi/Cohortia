Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Exam Structure and Question Types
Definition
Multiple-choice with scenario prompts that test applied knowledge across weighted domains. Expect short code or inspector excerpts and questions about best practices or behavior.
Scenario Example
A moving kinematic rigidbody enters a trigger. Which callback fires?
Study Tip
Turn factual review into short scenario drills. Explain why each wrong option is incorrect.
Example Question
Which method detects a GameObject entering a trigger volume?
Correct answer: OnTriggerEnter. Distractors often include collision callbacks to test your lifecycle knowledge.
Key Takeaway

Practice tracing short scenarios and allocate study time based on domain frequency to avoid surprises on exam day.
