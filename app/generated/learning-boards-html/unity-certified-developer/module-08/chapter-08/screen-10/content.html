Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Profiling & Optimization
Flow

Measure before you change

  • Capture profiler baseline for minimal loop.
  • Add enemies and addressable loads, capture another snapshot.
  • Identify spikes and high-alloc paths, then apply targeted fixes.
Key Takeaway

Small measurable changes and repeated snapshots show whether an optimization truly helps.

Common problems & fixes
Synchronous Addressables loads during spawn → preload or pool assets.
High physics CPU → simplify colliders, reduce active rigidbodies.
Excessive GC allocs in Update → remove allocations from per-frame code and reuse buffers.
