Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Undo & Persistence
Patterns
  • Undo.RecordObject(object, "Action") — use before property changes.
  • Undo.RegisterCreatedObjectUndo(newObj, "Create") — use when instantiating objects.
  • EditorUtility.SetDirty(object) — call after changes to ensure saving.
Quick test
Perform an edit, undo, and then restart the editor to confirm persistence and undo behavior.
Prefab note
When editing prefab properties, prefer serializedObject to correctly maintain overrides and prefab connections rather than directly changing nested values.
Common mistake

Forgetting EditorUtility.SetDirty after Undo.RecordObject can lead to non-persistent changes.
