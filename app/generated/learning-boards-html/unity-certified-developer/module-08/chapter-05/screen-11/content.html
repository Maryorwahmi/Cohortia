Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Physics & Fixed Timestep
Profile Physics
Look for Physics.Simulate in the profiler. If it's a large consumer, inspect number of active colliders and complexity of colliders.
  • Simplify complex mesh colliders to primitives where possible.
  • Use Layer Collision Matrix to prevent unnecessary pair checks.
  • Tune Fixed Timestep for less frequent simulation if gameplay allows.
Example
Switching mesh colliders to capsule or box colliders for many dynamic objects often reduces physics cost dramatically with minimal gameplay impact.
Misconception: Higher physics frequency always improves feel. Often interpolation allows a larger timestep while preserving smoothness and saving CPU.
