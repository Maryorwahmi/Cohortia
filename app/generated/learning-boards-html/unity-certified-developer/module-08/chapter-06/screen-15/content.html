Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Practical Tips
Apply daily
Track Handles
Keep AsyncOperationHandles in fields or lists and release them when finished.
Prefer AssetReference
Expose AssetReference in scripts to assign Addressables in the Inspector without direct references.
Group by Update Cadence
Put frequently updated content in its own group so you can rebuild and host it independently.
Final Takeaway

Small operational habits—tracking handles, using AssetReference, and planning groups—prevent leaks and make updates safe and efficient.
