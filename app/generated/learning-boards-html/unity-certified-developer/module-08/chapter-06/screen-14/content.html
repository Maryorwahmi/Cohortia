Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Key Concepts
Quick recall
Addressable Asset
An asset referenced by a stable address or label for on-demand loading.
AsyncOperationHandle
Handle returned by async loads; check status and call Release when done.
Addressable Group
A packaging unit that controls bundling, compression, and hosting rules.
Summary

Remember addresses, manage handles, and design groups. Together they let you load on demand, control memory, and deliver updates remotely.
