Build & test the managers
A step-by-step checklist to implement, wire, and verify the patterns in Unity.
Step 1
Create _Managers GameObject and add AudioManager and SettingsManager scripts.
Step 2
Implement Singleton in AudioManager with Awake checks and DontDestroyOnLoad.
Step 3
Implement SettingsManager.MasterVolume and invoke OnMasterVolumeChanged when set.
Step 4
Subscribe in AudioManager.OnEnable and update AudioSource.volume in the handler.
Step 5
Wire a UI slider to set SettingsManager.MasterVolume and test in Play mode.
Step 6
Verify persistence and absence of duplicates across scene loads and confirm event delivery via logs.

If everything works, extend the system by making AudioManager respond to a mute toggle published by SettingsManager, demonstrating additional decoupled behavior.
