Module 07 › Chapter 7.5 › Mistakes & Tips
Avoid
  • Ignoring console warnings.
  • Flooding the Console with unfiltered logs.
  • Catching exceptions without logging context.
Prefer
  • Targeted logs with identifiers and values.
  • Conditional breakpoints for intermittent issues.
  • Device testing with script debugging enabled.
Quick checklist
  1. Form hypothesis before instrumenting.
  2. Use Clear on Play and collapse duplicates.
  3. Attach debugger when logs are insufficient.
