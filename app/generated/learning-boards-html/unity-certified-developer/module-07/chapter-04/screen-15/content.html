Key Concepts — What to Remember (continued)
Asset Bundles
Package and load assets on demand, then unload bundles to free memory for large or modular games.
Memory Profiler
The authoritative tool to inspect Textures, Audio, Meshes and scripting allocations on your target device.
Garbage Collection
Frequent GC indicates allocation churn; reduce allocations with pooling and reuse to avoid hitches.
Final takeaway
Measure, optimize one thing at a time, and verify on the target device. That cycle prevents surprises and keeps your game reliable across devices.
