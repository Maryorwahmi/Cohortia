Post-Deployment Monitoring & Analytics
Collect crash reports, record gameplay events, and use data to prioritize fixes and new content.
Why this matters: Crash and usage data identify real problems that only appear at scale or on specific hardware, helping you target limited engineering time.
Scope: stability (Cloud Diagnostics) and behavior (Analytics). Together they close the loop from release to improvement.
