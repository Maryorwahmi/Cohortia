Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Privacy & Ethics
Data minimization
Only collect fields required to answer your questions. Avoid storing personal identifiers unless necessary and consented.
  • Prefer anonymous session IDs over personal data.
  • Aggregate or hash sensitive fields if needed for analysis.
Operational advice
Document your events and retention policies, and train your team on what is allowed to be collected and shared.
