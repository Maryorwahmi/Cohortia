Batching and simplifying AI

Spread heavy calculations over time and favor cheaper approximations when possible.

Strategies
  • Stagger updates: run expensive tasks for subsets of agents each frame.
  • Use coroutines to yield between steps and spread load across frames.
  • Simplify decision logic to cheap heuristics where high fidelity isn't needed.
  • Avoid per-agent raycast storms; consider spatial partitioning or centralized checks.
Concrete scenario
If 100 agents perform pathfinding every frame, instead schedule 10 agents per frame to update paths, cycling through the list over ten frames. Players rarely notice a 0.1s delay in path updates, but the CPU load becomes much steadier.
Pitfall: spreading work increases perceived latency; test to maintain acceptable responsiveness while reducing spikes.
Check

Profile CPU per-frame spikes before and after batching to confirm smoother distribution of work.

Guide

Prefer central managers for heavy shared tasks rather than repeating identical work in many agents.

Warning

Over-simplifying AI can make behavior feel broken; balance cost and believability through testing.
