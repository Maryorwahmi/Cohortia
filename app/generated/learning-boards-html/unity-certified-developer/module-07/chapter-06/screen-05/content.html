Module 07 › Chapter 7.6 › Switching Target Platforms
Step-by-step
1. Open Build Settings
Choose the platform you intend to build for and prepare to switch.
2. Click Switch Platform
Wait for Unity to reimport assets and apply platform import settings.
3. Validate Player Settings
Confirm bundle id, keystore, scripting backend, and resolution for the active platform.
Pitfall
Switching and building immediately can produce unsigned or incompatible packages; always review Player Settings after switching.
Visual: Platform cards

Desktop executables and app bundles.
