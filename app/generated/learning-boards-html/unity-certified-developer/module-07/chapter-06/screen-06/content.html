Module 07 › Chapter 7.6 › Player Settings: Identity
Identity fields
Company Name
Used in metadata and store listings.
Product Name & Version
Displayed to users; increment version when publishing updates.
Icon
Set platform-specific icon sizes for device home screens or desktop icons.
Splash Screen
Customize or use Unity splash; affects first impression.
Concrete example
Set Company to Cohortia, Product to MyOptimizedGame, pick an icon, and set version 1.0 before building a PC standalone for distribution.
Why this matters
These fields determine how your app is presented to users and how updates are matched to previous installs. Mistakes here can break update paths or make your app look unprofessional.
Misconception
These settings are more than cosmetic; bundle identifiers and product names are used by stores and OS update systems to identify your app.
