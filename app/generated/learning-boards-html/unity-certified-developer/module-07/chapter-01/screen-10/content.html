Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › CPU vs GPU
Comparison

CPU-bound

Symptoms: Scripts or Physics dominate CPU Usage. Lowering resolution has little effect.

Example
Many AI agents each doing heavy per-frame work in Update.
Avoid this mistake

Don’t immediately reduce visual fidelity if scripts are the true cost.

Comparison

GPU-bound

Symptoms: Rendering or GPU Usage dominates. Lowering resolution or quality shows clear FPS improvement.

Example
Many unique materials, complex shaders, and heavy post-processing effects.
Simple test

Lower resolution or disable post-processing. Large FPS gains indicate GPU-bound behavior.

Quick diagnostic checklist
  • Lower resolution: big improvement → GPU-bound.
  • No change → investigate Scripts and Physics for CPU hotspots.
  • Use Profiler hierarchy to confirm before optimizing.
