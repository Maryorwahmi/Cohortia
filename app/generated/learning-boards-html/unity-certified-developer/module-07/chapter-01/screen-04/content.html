Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › What is a Performance Bottleneck?
Definition

Bottleneck explained

A bottleneck limits overall throughput. Use the traffic-jam analogy: overall flow equals the slowest link.

Concrete example

Hundreds of enemies with complex pathfinding in Update saturate the CPU and lower frame rate.

Common misconception

Appearance can be misleading: a visible frame drop doesn’t always mean the GPU is at fault; gather profiler evidence first.

Analogy

A narrow bridge limits how many cars can pass per minute. Improving upstream or downstream flow won’t help until the bridge is widened.

Check your understanding

If the Profiler shows Rendering dominating frame time, is the bottleneck necessarily GPU? Explain why or why not.

Key Takeaway

Identify which subsystem is limiting throughput before optimizing; a single visible symptom can have multiple causes.
