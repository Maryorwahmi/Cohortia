Practice tasks you will complete

Hands-on exploration will teach you to spot and report bottlenecks accurately.

Profiler exploration

Open Window > Analysis > Profiler, run Play Mode, and watch CPU, GPU, and Memory graphs react in real time.

Scene stress test

Duplicate many cubes or objects and capture a Profiler screenshot when a clear spike appears.

Interpretation

Identify the dominant Profiler section and recommend an initial diagnostic step such as lowering quality or instrumenting scripts.

Reflection

Explain why profiling early reduces refactor costs and keeps a stable performance baseline during development.

Assessment tip
When in doubt, reduce resolution
If frame rate improves after lowering resolution, suspect GPU-bound issues.
Milestone

Capture a Profiler screenshot showing a spike and write one diagnostic sentence naming the likely bottleneck and the first test you would run.
