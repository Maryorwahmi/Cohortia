Pro Tip

Choose fixes with measurable impact

Select targets revealed by profiling and aim for the largest performance gains per unit of effort.

High-impact examples
  • Batching meshes to reduce draw calls.
  • Moving heavy per-frame work out of Update into less frequent ticks.
Low-impact traps
  • Micro-optimizing a line without profiling it as a hotspot.
  • Blindly reducing texture size where rendering isn’t the issue.
Checklist before optimizing
  1. Measure with the Profiler and find hotspots.
  2. Estimate expected impact and effort.
  3. Make minimal change and re-profile to verify.
Key Takeaway

Optimize evidence-first. Prioritize fixes that reduce a measurable Profiler cost and verify after each change.
