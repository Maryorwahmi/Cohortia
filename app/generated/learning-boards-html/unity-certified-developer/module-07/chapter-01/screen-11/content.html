Activity: Initial Profiler Exploration

Follow a clear provoke-observe-capture cycle to gather evidence of bottlenecks.

Step 1 — Prepare
Open a simple scene, add a camera and a light, and open Window → Analysis → Profiler.
Step 2 — Provoke
Duplicate a cube until 100–200 objects exist or enable a heavy behaviour to create visible load.
Step 3 — Observe
Watch CPU, GPU and Memory graphs. Pause on a spike and inspect the frame’s hierarchy.
Step 4 — Capture & Report
Take a Profiler screenshot, note which module spiked, and recommend the next diagnostic like lowering quality or instrumenting scripts.
Example report

"After adding 150 cubes, Rendering increased dramatically. I captured a frame showing high draw calls. Next step: combine meshes and reduce material variants."

Key Takeaway

Collect evidence before acting. Provoke, observe, capture, and then choose a focused fix based on the Profiler data.
