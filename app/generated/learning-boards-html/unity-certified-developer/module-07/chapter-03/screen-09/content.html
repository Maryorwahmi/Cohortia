Definition
Occlusion culling basics
Occlusion culling uses baked visibility to skip rendering objects blocked by occluders. It is computed ahead of time and consulted at runtime to avoid unnecessary draw calls and triangles.
Implementation steps
  1. Mark static occluders and occludees in the Inspector.
  2. Open Window > Rendering > Occlusion Culling and adjust settings.
  3. Click Bake and test visibility during play while profiling.
Key Takeaway

Occlusion culling can dramatically reduce render cost in scenes with many occluders, but it requires static geometry and a bake step.

Concrete Scenario
In a multi-room interior, objects behind closed doors or walls are excluded after baking, preventing unnecessary triangles from reaching the GPU when the player cannot see them.
Adjustment
Tweak smallest occluder and hole settings for accuracy versus bake time.
Common Mistake
Expecting occlusion to handle dynamic small props. The bake assumes static layout; dynamic content may need runtime culling or manual toggling.
Check
After baking, profile the GPU triangle count and verify previously hidden rooms are no longer drawn when out of view.
