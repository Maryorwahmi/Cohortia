Texture Optimization
Atlas, compress, and scale textures per-platform to reduce bandwidth and allow batching.
Avoid Overuse
Do not overuse real-time lights or heavy post effects without profiling — they can quickly overwhelm the GPU.
Profile Often
Use Frame Debugger and Profiler after each change. Metrics and visuals both matter for a balanced result.
Final Tip
Optimization is a measurement-driven craft. One change at a time and test on the hardware your players use.
