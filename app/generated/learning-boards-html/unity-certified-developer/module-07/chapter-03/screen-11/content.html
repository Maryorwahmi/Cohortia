Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Shader Complexity & Simplification
Definition
What shader complexity means
Complex shaders cost more per pixel and per vertex. Reducing passes, texture lookups, and heavy math reduces GPU time.
Strategies
  • Use simpler shaders for distant or numerous objects.
  • Avoid multiple passes unless necessary for the visual effect.
  • Bake lighting where possible instead of expensive real-time calculations.
Key Takeaway

Simpler shaders reduce per-pixel cost and often yield the largest GPU savings when applied to many objects or expensive full-screen effects.

Concrete Example
Replace a layered character shader with a single-pass mobile shader for distant NPCs, or bake their lighting into a diffuse texture to avoid per-pixel expensive operations.
Frame Debugger Tip
Use the Frame Debugger to examine shader passes for a draw call and identify high-cost draws for replacement or reduction.
Common Mistake
Assuming all visual detail must be live. Baking and cheaper shading often preserve perceived quality at much lower cost.
