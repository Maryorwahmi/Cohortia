Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Lighting & Post-processing Costs
Definition
Why lighting and post-processing matter
Real-time lights, shadows, and full-screen post effects can be expensive on the GPU. Baking lights and choosing cheaper post-processing often recovers much of the lost performance.
Strategies
  • Bake static lighting for non-dynamic scenes.
  • Limit shadow-casting real-time lights.
  • Use cheaper anti-aliasing and reduced post effect quality on mobile.
Key Takeaway

Baked lighting and judicious post-processing choices can dramatically reduce per-frame GPU cost while keeping visual quality for players.

Concrete Example
Bake global illumination for a static level and replace expensive anti-aliasing with FXAA on mobile. Measure GPU frame time before and after to confirm improvements.
Pro tip
Test lighting decisions on the target hardware; desktop tolerances differ from mobile where bandwidth and compute are constrained.
Common Mistake
Assuming a single post-processing profile is acceptable across all platforms. Tune settings per platform to maintain frame rate targets.
