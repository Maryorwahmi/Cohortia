Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Debugging IK
Top mistakes
  • • IK calls outside OnAnimatorIK.
  • • Position/rotation weights left at zero.
  • • Wrong AvatarIKGoal or missing Animator reference.
  • • Targets placed out of reach or hints mispositioned.
Fixes and checks
• Ensure OnAnimatorIK gets called and contains your SetIK calls.
• Set both position and rotation weights when you want full control.
• Test reachable targets and adjust hint placements to remove twisting.
• Blend weights to avoid fights with complex base animations.
Pro Tip

Log the Animator reference and IK weights during Play to quickly spot whether your code is executing and with what values.
