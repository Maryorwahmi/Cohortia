Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Learning Objectives
01 Explain FK vs IK
Describe how FK rotates joints directly and IK computes joint rotations from a target position, and why that difference matters for gameplay.
Mnemonic: think of FK as a chain of rotations, IK as aiming the chain's tip.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Configure an IK rig
Ensure the Animator and humanoid rig are set, add targets and hints in the scene, and confirm Apply Root Motion when needed.
Mnemonic: visual checklist—Animator, Target, Hint, Weights.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Implement OnAnimatorIK
Write a controller that sets IK position, rotation, and weights inside OnAnimatorIK so the Animator applies your targets correctly.
Mnemonic: IK calls belong in the IK callback, not Update.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Apply IK for procedural effects
Use IK for look-at behavior, foot placement on slopes, and reactive adjustments to moving objects while blending weights smoothly.
Mnemonic: blend weights to keep poses stable under animation conflicts.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
