Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › End Effector & Hints
Key terms
• End Effector: the limb tip you want to control, e.g. a hand or foot.
• IK Goal: Mecanim's identifier for an end effector, such as RightHand.
• Hint: an optional position guiding elbow or knee bending.
Concrete: place RightElbowHint slightly behind the elbow to encourage a natural fold when reaching forward.
Placement rules
  • • Place hints relative to joint orientation, not the target.
  • • Avoid placing hints too close to the joint to prevent erratic bending.
  • • Use hints when the solver has multiple valid bend solutions.
Quick Check

Move a hint and observe the elbow orientation; the hint should produce predictable, natural bend direction.
