Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Weight Blending
Blend pattern
// Example lerp in Update
float targetWeight = targetInRange ? 1f : 0f;
ikWeight = Mathf.MoveTowards(ikWeight, targetWeight, Time.deltaTime * 2f);
Use a time-based interpolation to move weight smoothly. Tune the speed to match animation timing and avoid visible pops.
Practical tips
  • • Only enable IK for limbs that must contact objects or follow dynamic targets.
  • • Gradually blend to maintain authored animation character and detail.
  • • Lower weight can produce subtle corrections rather than full overrides.
Misconception

Assuming ikWeight 1 is always best will often destroy subtle hand or foot animations; blend thoughtfully.
