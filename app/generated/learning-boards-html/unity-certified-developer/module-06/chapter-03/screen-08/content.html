Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Inspector & Scene Setup
Step-by-step
  1. 1. Assign a Humanoid avatar and Animator component with an idle state.
  2. 2. Create IKTargetSphere and position it within reachable range of the right hand.
  3. 3. Create RightElbowHint slightly behind the elbow to guide the bend direction.
  4. 4. Attach HandIKController to character and drag references into the Inspector.
  5. 5. Run Play and adjust ikWeight to observe blending.
Common adjustments
If elbow collapses inward, move the hint further behind or to the side.
If hand overshoots, reduce the distance between target and shoulder or lower ikWeight.
If jitter occurs, smooth the target motion or tween the ikWeight instead of snapping.
Why it matters

A correct scene setup avoids solver artifacts and gives you repeatable, editable IK behavior during testing.
