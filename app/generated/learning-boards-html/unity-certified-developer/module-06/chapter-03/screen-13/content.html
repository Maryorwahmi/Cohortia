Patterns and examples
• Look-at: SetLookAtWeight and SetLookAtPosition to make the head and eyes track a moving object.
• Feet on slopes: raycast ground height and call SetIKPosition for each foot inside OnAnimatorIK.
• Spine tweaks: apply small weighted adjustments to upper-body joints for slope response.
Performance
  • • Limit the number of limbs updated each frame.
  • • Cache raycasts and reuse results within the frame.
  • • Tween weights instead of toggling to reduce solver instability.
Takeaway

Extend IK thoughtfully for gaze and grounding, and always profile to find tradeoffs between realism and runtime cost.
