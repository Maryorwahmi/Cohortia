Sub-Emitters
Spawn child systems on Birth, Collision, or Death for layered effects.
Collision Module
Enable physical interactions and tune Dampen, Bounce, and Lifetime Loss.
Triggers Module
Detect presence in volumes and drive state changes or spawns without physics.
Summary

Combine these systems to build interactions that read well in gameplay: staged visuals, physical reactions, and zone-based behavior.
