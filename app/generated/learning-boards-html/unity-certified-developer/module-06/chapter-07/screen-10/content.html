Profiling checklist
  1. Capture a frame with Unity Profiler and inspect GPU post-processing timings.
  2. Identify top-cost effects, such as AO, SSR, or high-res DoF.
  3. Tune effect parameters and test again to measure savings.
Example: if SSR costs too much on a target device, lower its resolution or disable it for that quality level.
Optimization techniques
Reduce sample counts for AO and lower SSR resolution.
Lower Depth of Field resolution or use a cheaper bokeh implementation.
Disable or reduce effects on low-end quality settings or when the camera is far from detail.
Tip

Measure cost first. Small parameter changes often yield large performance wins without losing artistic intent.
