Post-Processing Effects & Camera Enhancements
Apply full-screen image effects to define mood, clarity, and performance trade-offs.
In this lesson

You will set up URP post-processing, create a global volume and profile, and tune Bloom, Vignette, and Color Grading for a simple scene. We'll also cover optimization and common mistakes.

Why it matters

A few well-chosen effects can dramatically improve readability and emotional impact. Misused effects can hide gameplay cues or harm performance.

Visual Concept
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Bloom
Glow from bright sources that guides attention.
Color Grading
Controls overall color, contrast, and tone mapping.
Vignette
Darkens edges to focus the player on the center.
Depth of Field
Simulates camera focus to separate foreground and background.
