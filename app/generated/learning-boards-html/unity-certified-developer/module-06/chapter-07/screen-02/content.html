Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Learning Objectives
01
Understand post-processing
Explain how full-screen effects change perceived depth, focus, and mood.
Mnemonic: picture a photo filter that the camera applies after the scene renders. Verified Competency Milestone: describe two cases where post-processing improves gameplay readability.
02
Configure URP post-processing
Enable the pipeline setting and attach a Post Process Layer to the camera.
Mnemonic: settings first, then camera. Verified Competency Milestone: show a camera that renders a simple Bloom effect in Play mode.
03
Implement common effects
Add and tune Bloom, DoF, Vignette, and Color Grading for mood and clarity.
Mnemonic: one effect, one problem. Verified Competency Milestone: demonstrate the scene before and after Color Grading adjustments.
04
Optimize and profile
Tune quality settings, reduce sample counts, and disable costly effects when needed.
Mnemonic: measure then reduce. Verified Competency Milestone: use the Profiler to identify a post-process effect consuming GPU time.
