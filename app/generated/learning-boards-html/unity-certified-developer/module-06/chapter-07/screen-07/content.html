Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › DoF vs Vignette
Depth of Field (DoF)
Simulates camera focus using Focus Distance, Aperture, and Focal Length. Use for cinematic emphasis and to hide distant detail.
Example: Fix focus on a character in close-up; blur the background to hide unimportant geometry.
Misconception: DoF is always subtle — aggressive DoF can make gameplay feel sluggish or cause motion sickness.
Vignette
Darkens the image edges to center attention. Cheap and effective for mood and framing; works well in horror and cinematic scenes.
Example: Increase Vignette intensity slightly in a dark corridor to focus the player's sightline.
Performance: Minimal cost; safe to enable on most target devices.
When to choose DoF
Cinematic shots, portrait close-ups, or when depth separation improves narrative clarity.
When to choose Vignette
Quick framing, horror mood, inexpensive focus emphasis.
Key Takeaway
Use Vignette as an inexpensive framing tool and DoF for optical realism; test DoF performance carefully.
