Full-screen finishing pass
Post-processing operates on the camera's final rendered image to set tone, focus, and readability.
URP Volumes & Layers
Enable post-processing in the URP Asset, add Post Process Layer to the camera, and use Volumes for profile-driven effects.
Measure & optimize
Profile to find costly effects and tune resolution, samples, or quality levels instead of blind removal.
Takeaway

Use post-processing intentionally: configure URP correctly, pick high-impact effects, and profile to keep visuals performant and clear.
