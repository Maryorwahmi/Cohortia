Enable the pipeline
Confirm the post-processing option is enabled in your UniversalRenderPipelineAsset before doing any other setup.
Match layers
Set the camera's Volume Layer to the same layer used by your Global and Local Volumes to ensure effects apply.
Profile changes
Measure GPU cost and prefer parameter tuning or conditional disabling over blunt removal of effects.
Final thought

A little post-processing goes a long way. Configure carefully, tune deliberately, and measure impact to produce beautiful, playable scenes.
