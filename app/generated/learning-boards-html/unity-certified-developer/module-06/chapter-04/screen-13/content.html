Key Concepts

A compact set of reminders to carry into your projects.

Particle System
Many small sprites simulated together to create VFX like smoke and fire.
Main Module
Sets lifetime, speed, size, color, gravity, and caps overall particle counts.
Emission Module
Controls Rate over Time, Rate over Distance, and Bursts for flow and events.
Takeaway
Build effects by combining module controls with proper materials and iterative optimization to balance visual quality and performance.
