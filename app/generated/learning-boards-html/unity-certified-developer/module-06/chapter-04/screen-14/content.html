What to Remember

Quick checks and fixes when building effects.

Material
Assign particle shaders (Additive / Alpha Blended).
Shape
Pick Cone, Sphere, Box, or Edge to match the emitter source.
Over Lifetime
Use gradients and curves to fade and scale particles as they age.
Final reminder
If particles are invisible or look wrong, check Renderer material first, then Main and Emission settings, and finally Max Particles.
