Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › What is a Particle System?
Definition

Particle System

A compact simulation that emits many lightweight textured sprites, evolving their properties over time to create complex visual effects.

Why use particles?
  • Efficiently represent fluid, fuzzy, or transient visual phenomena.
  • Flexible control via modular properties for rapid iteration.
  • Scalable fidelity by adjusting particle counts and lifetime.
Common misconception

Particles are not single textures. They are many sprites whose collective motion and blending produce the effect.

Key Takeaway

Think in terms of many small primitives evolving over time, controlled by modular settings.

Concrete example

A looping campfire smoke: use a Cone shape, Rate over Time ~12, Start Lifetime 2-4s, Color over Lifetime fading to transparent white.

Analogy

Imagine sprinkling many tiny pieces of tissue paper into a gentle upward breeze; each piece is a particle and their collective motion forms the effect.

Check your understanding

Change only Start Lifetime and observe how persistence changes without altering emission origin.
