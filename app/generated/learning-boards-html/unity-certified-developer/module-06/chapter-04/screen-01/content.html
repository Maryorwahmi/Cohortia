Introduction to Particle Systems

Learn how Unity emits and renders particles, configure core modules, and create practical effects like fire and explosions.

In this lesson
What a particle system is
Many small textured sprites combined to simulate complex phenomena.
Core modules
Main, Emission, Shape, Color over Lifetime, Size over Lifetime, Renderer.
Performance
Limit max particles, use simple textures, and cull off-screen systems.
Hands-on
Build a one-shot explosion and a looping smoke effect during the chapter.
Explore Key Topics
Modules
Configure Main, Emission, Shape to sculpt behavior over life and space.
Materials
Choose additive or alpha blended shaders and a soft texture for proper blending.
Optimization
Limit particle counts and disable systems when not visible to save performance.
Visual Concept
Particle cards & connectors

A particle effect is assembled from emission, evolution, and rendering. Think of modules as connected cards that shape behavior over the particle's life.

Modules flow from emission to renderer. Adjust each to refine the final look.
Why this matters

Good particle systems amplify player feedback and mood. Small changes in color, lifetime, or emission rate create very different impressions. Learning these parameters helps you create believable, optimized effects.
