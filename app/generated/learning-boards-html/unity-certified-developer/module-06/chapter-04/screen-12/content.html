Best Practices

Keep effects cheap and readable

Limit Max Particles
Set a cap to prevent runaway particle counts in heavy scenes.
Use simple textures
Small, feathered circular textures render faster than large atlases.
Cull off-screen
Enable culling or disable emitters when not visible to save GPU work.
Prefer short lifetimes
Shorter lifetimes reduce total simultaneous particles for the same visual density.
Check

Profile with and without systems enabled and iterate by reducing counts until you meet performance targets while preserving readable visuals.

Practical tip

Group effects and disable groups when players move far from the action to save resources.

Common pitfall

Leaving many small looping emitters active across a scene can be costlier than a few well-tuned bursts.

Try this

Reduce particle count by 25% and observe if the visual quality remains acceptable; repeat until performance is stable.
