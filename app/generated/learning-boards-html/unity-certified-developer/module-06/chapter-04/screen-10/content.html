Comparison

Additive vs Alpha Blended

Additive: Brightness adds up when particles overlap. Great for fire and magic glows.
Alpha Blended: Standard transparency, better for soft smoke and dust.
Render Modes: Billboard, Stretch, Mesh control orientation and appearance.
Common mistake

Using a standard PBR shader for particles often prevents correct blending and transparency.

Example

Fire: Particles with Additive shader and soft circular texture. Smoke: Alpha Blended with feathered gray texture.

Practical tip

Test materials against both dark and bright backgrounds since additive blending can wash out on bright backdrops.

Key Takeaway

Always assign a particle material; choose Additive for glow and Alpha Blended for soft transparency, then inspect in-scene.
