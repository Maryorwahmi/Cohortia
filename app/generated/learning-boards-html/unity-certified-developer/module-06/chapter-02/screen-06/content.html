Comparison
1D Blend

Best when a single numeric factor drives motion. Example: Speed controls Idle → Walk → Run.

  • Pros: Simple to author, predictable blending.
  • Cons: Not suitable for directional nuance.
2D Blend

Use when two parameters control motion, typically horizontal and vertical input for strafing and diagonal motion.

  • Pros: Rich directional control, smoother diagonal blends.
  • Cons: More clips to author and position.
Authoring tip
Map clips on the parameter plane to match their actual directional influence. Preview corner cases: full forward, full right, and diagonals to ensure natural blends.
