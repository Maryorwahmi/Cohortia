Process Flow
Create UpperBodyMask
  1. Project window → Create → Avatar Mask. Name it UpperBodyMask.
  2. In the Inspector, open Humanoid and deselect all body parts.
  3. Manually enable Spine, Chest, UpperChest, Shoulders, Arms, Forearms, Hands.
  4. Assign the mask to the UpperBody layer in the Animator Layers tab.
  5. Play the Wave animation and verify only upper-body bones move.
Checklist
  • Mask assigned to the correct layer.
  • Layer Weight > 0 when testing.
  • Bone selection matches your rig's naming.
Common Mistake

Including legs in the mask or forgetting to assign it will let the layer override lower-body motion unexpectedly.
