Concrete example

Make a scrolling texture by sending Time into Tiling And Offset, then use the changed UV to sample a Texture 2D Asset. The texture will appear to shift across the model.

Space conventions & pitfalls
  • Position in object space moves with the object; in world space it’s fixed to the scene.
  • Time increases continuously; combine it with Multiply or Sine to control amplitude and frequency.
Key Takeaway

Choose the right input node to provide the data you need, and pay attention to coordinate spaces when using Position or UV.
