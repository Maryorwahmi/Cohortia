Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Unlit vs Lit
Unlit Shader

Outputs color and alpha without lighting. Great for emissive effects, HUD elements, and stylized visuals where you want consistent appearance.

Use when
  • You want exact color control independent of scene lights.
  • Creating glowing or stylized VFX without lighting interaction.
Lit Shader

Includes lighting and material parameters for realistic shading. Use for materials that must reflect or respond to scene lights.

Use when
  • You require realistic lighting, shadows, or reflections.
  • Surface response to lights impacts gameplay or visibility.
Key Takeaway

Choose shader type by whether you need lighting interaction. Avoid unnecessary math and texture samples regardless of type to keep shaders efficient.
