Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Mistakes & Performance
Compatibility checks
  • Confirm the project uses URP or HDRP before creating Shader Graph assets.
  • Save graphs after edits so materials update in the scene.
Performance checklist
  • Avoid repeated texture samples; cache and reuse samples in nodes.
  • Limit heavy math per pixel and avoid unnecessary trigonometry at high frequencies.
Key Takeaway

Check pipeline, keep graphs connected, and profile shader cost early. Efficient shaders preserve frame rate while delivering strong visuals.
