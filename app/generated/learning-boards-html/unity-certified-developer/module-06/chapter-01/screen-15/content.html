Assign the controller
No controller assigned in the Animator component means no animation will play even if a default state exists in the asset.
Drive with parameters
Change parameters from scripts and avoid direct clip control for better blending and centralized logic.
Tune transition timing
For gameplay responsiveness, uncheck Has Exit Time and use short transition durations; use exit time for cinematic sequences.
One final tip
During Play testing, keep the Animator window open to watch active states and parameter values. It’s the fastest way to find mismatches and verify your animations are responding to code.
