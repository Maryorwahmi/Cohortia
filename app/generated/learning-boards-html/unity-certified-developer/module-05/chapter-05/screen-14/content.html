Assessment question
Describe how to build a Pause Menu that slides in from the top and fades in. Include the role of RectTransform, CanvasGroup, and a coroutine in your answer.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • Profile UI.Canvas.Build to detect rebuild costs.
  • Use CanvasGroup for fades; avoid per-child color animation.
  • Reduce overlapping transparency to lower overdraw.
Key Takeaway

Assessments should verify both how you implement animations and how you optimize them; use profiling to inform fixes.
