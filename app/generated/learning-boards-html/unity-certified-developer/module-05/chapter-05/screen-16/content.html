Keep it responsive
Short durations and snappy easing make feedback feel immediate.
Select the right tool
Animator for states, coroutines for sequences, tweening for concise chains.
Profile and optimize
Use CanvasGroup, batching, and profiling to identify real bottlenecks.
Final takeaway
Design animations to communicate, then validate with testing and profiling to ensure they help rather than hinder play.
