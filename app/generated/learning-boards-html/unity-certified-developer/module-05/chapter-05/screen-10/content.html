Common causes
  • Frequent RectTransform updates forcing Canvas rebuilds.
  • Many overlapping semi-transparent images increasing overdraw.
  • Too many Animator components one each small element.
Optimization tips
  • Use CanvasGroup.alpha to fade groups efficiently.
  • Keep animations short and avoid animating dozens of RectTransforms at once.
  • Consider DOTween for many concurrent simple animations.
Profiler question
If UI feels slow, check the UI.Canvas.Build and overdraw metrics in the Unity Profiler to determine whether CPU geometry rebuilds or GPU fill rate is the source of the issue.
