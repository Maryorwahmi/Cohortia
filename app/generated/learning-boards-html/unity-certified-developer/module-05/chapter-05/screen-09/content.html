Concept

Lerp computes a straight-line interpolation between two values; easing remaps time to make motion start or end more naturally.

Example easing
EaseOutQuad: eased = 1 - (1 - t)^2; use before Lerp to slow toward the target.
Practical test
  • Animate a panel with linear Lerp and then with an ease-out. Compare perceived completion.
  • For quick feedback, reduce duration first; apply easing second.
Key Takeaway

Easing transforms motion from mechanical to natural and should be selected to match interaction intent.
