Common Mistake

Setting scroll position before layout rebuilds completes results in no visible scroll. Use ForceUpdateCanvases or a coroutine yield to allow layout to settle.

Exercise Prompt
Add five more unique quest entries to your starter loop. Verify that after adding them the ScrollRect shows the newest entries and the scrollbar matches content height. If it doesn't, ensure Content Size Fitter is set and force a layout update before adjusting scroll position.
Check your understanding
  • Did you set Vertical Fit to Preferred Size on the Content?
  • Did you wait or force a rebuild before setting verticalNormalizedPosition?
  • Are your prefab heights fixed to stabilize layout?
