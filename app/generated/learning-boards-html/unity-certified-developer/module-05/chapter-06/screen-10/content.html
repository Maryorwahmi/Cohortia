Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Canvas Render Modes
Screen Space - Overlay
Renders on top of everything. Best for static HUDs, simplest to use. Watch out for rebuilds when elements change often.
Screen Space - Camera
Renders with a camera reference. Useful for UI that needs camera effects or layered rendering. Can be performant when organized with dedicated UI cameras.
World Space
Places UI in 3D space for signs, floating labels, or interactive world panels. Be mindful of many canvases or distant elements increasing cost.
Practical strategy
Split canvases by how often they change. Put static elements on one canvas, dynamic menus on another, and world elements on separate canvases. Use CanvasGroup to fade sections instead of animating many individual elements.
