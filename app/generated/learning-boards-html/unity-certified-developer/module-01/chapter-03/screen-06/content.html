Architecture

Folder structure that scales

Use top-level folders with underscore prefixes and consistent naming to make assets discoverable and portable across machines.

Recommended top-level folders
_Scenes, _Scripts, _Models, _Textures, _Materials, _Audio, _Prefabs, _Animations
Team tip
Add a small text README in the Assets root to document naming conventions for your team.
Concrete example
A player character: put the model at _Models/Player/hero.fbx, textures at _Textures/Player/face.png, and animations at _Animations/Player/walk.anim. This keeps all player-related files grouped but still searchable at the top level.
Common mistake

Scattering files across many nested folders or keeping raw local paths makes it hard for others to find assets.

Why it matters: a clear structure speeds development, reduces merge conflicts, and simplifies asset reuse.
