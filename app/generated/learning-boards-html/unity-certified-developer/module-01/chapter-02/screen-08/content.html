Physics Component

Rigidbody

Enables simulation: gravity, forces, collisions, and physical response. Tune mass, drag, and gravity settings to affect motion.

Mass
Resistance to acceleration.
Drag
Air resistance, slows motion over time.
useGravity
Enable to let gravity apply automatically.
Practice & Warnings
Example
Add a Rigidbody to a cube with a Box Collider and press Play to observe gravity pulling it down and collision stopping it against the plane.
Common mistake
Using Transform to move dynamic rigidbodies. This can break collision detection and physics stability.
Key takeaway
Use Rigidbody APIs for physical motion and reserve Transform for static positioning or parenting changes.
