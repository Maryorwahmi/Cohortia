Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Physics Scene Activity
Step 1 — Ground
Create a Plane. Set Transform position to (0,0,0) and Scale to (5,1,5).
Step 2 — Cube
Create a Cube at position (0,5,0). Ensure a Box Collider is present. Add a Rigidbody component.
Observe & Experiment
Press Play to watch the Cube fall. If it passes through, check colliders and Rigidbody. Tweak mass and collider size, then retest to learn how properties affect behavior.
