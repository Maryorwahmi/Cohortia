Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › What to Remember
Collider choice
Prefer box, sphere, or capsule colliders for moving objects; use mesh colliders sparingly.
Rigidbody motion
Use forces, velocity, or MovePosition for physical movement to keep collisions stable.
Composition
Assemble small, single-purpose components for clarity, reuse, and easier debugging.
Final takeaway

Think in components: attach the minimum set needed for the behavior you want, test early, and prefer simple colliders and Rigidbody APIs for reliable physics.
