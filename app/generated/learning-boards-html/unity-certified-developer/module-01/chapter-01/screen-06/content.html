Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Hierarchy Window and Parent-Child Relationships
What the Hierarchy does
It shows every GameObject in the scene as a nested tree and controls parent-child relationships that influence transforms and organization.
Parenting Example
Attach a weapon as a child to a character hand so the weapon inherits movement and rotation during animations, saving you from synchronizing motion manually.
Pitfall
Parenting can change local vs global transforms. If a child jumps position when parented, reset local transform or choose to parent without changing world position.
Quick exercise
Create an empty GameObject called 'EnemyRoot', parent several enemy models beneath it, then move EnemyRoot to reposition the entire group. This demonstrates grouped transforms and simplifies level adjustments.
Key Takeaway

Hierarchy controls grouping and transform inheritance; use it to move sets of objects together and keep the scene tidy.

Pro tip
Name parents with intent, for example 'Props_Trees' or 'Enemies_Forest', to make search and selection faster later.
