Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Normals & Tangents
Concept comparison
Compare the roles and failure modes of Normals and Tangents.
Normals
Direction vectors for lighting calculations. Incorrect normals = wrong shading.
Tangents
Provide tangent space for Normal Maps. Incorrect tangents = warped normal mapping.
Check your model
If shading or normal maps look wrong, toggle Normal import mode (Import / Calculate) and Tangent options in the Model tab, and re-import if necessary.
Concrete scenario
A character's cloth Normal Map looks twisted when rotating under a light. Cause: incorrect tangent space from export. Fix by recalculating tangents in the modeling tool or change Tangent options in Unity's importer.
Misconception
Normal Maps do not create geometry. They only change lighting direction to fake depth. If you need real silhouette changes, add geometry.
