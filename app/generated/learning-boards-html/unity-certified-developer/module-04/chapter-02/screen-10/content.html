Texture import steps
Adjust Max Size, Compression, and texture type based on use.
Step 1: Select texture
Open Inspector and set Max Size to the smallest acceptable resolution.
Step 2: Compression
Choose compressed formats and quality to reduce VRAM usage.
Step 3: Texture Type
Set Normal textures to Normal Map type and accept conversion when prompted.
Why it matters

Properly sized and compressed textures drastically reduce load times and VRAM without sacrificing perceived quality when chosen well.

Concrete example
An Albedo at 2048 for a distant prop can be reduced to 512. The Normal map should retain enough detail for perceived bump height; consider 1024 or 512 depending on distance. Keep originals externally so you can re-import different sizes.
Misconception
Higher Max Size always equals better visuals. In practice, perceived quality often plateaus while memory cost increases—measure and downscale where acceptable.
