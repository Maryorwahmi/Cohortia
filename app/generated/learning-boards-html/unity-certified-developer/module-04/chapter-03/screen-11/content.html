Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › 3D Spatial Audio
Core terms
  • Spatial Blend — mix between 2D and 3D.
  • Min Distance — full volume radius.
  • Max Distance — outer falloff limit.
  • Rolloff Curve — defines how volume decreases with distance.
Example
Place an ambient source with Min Distance 2 and Max Distance 30 to make it feel present nearby but subtle at a distance.
Testing tips
While playing the scene, move the listener object around the source and observe changes. Use the Audio window’s profiler to watch sample rates and CPU. If spatialization feels off, adjust Min/Max values, rolloff curve, or Spatialize settings.
Key Takeaway

Tune distances and spatial blend by role: close interactions need tight Min Distance, ambience needs broad Max Distance.
