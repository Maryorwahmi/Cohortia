Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › AudioListener
Role
Receives audio from all AudioSources. It determines what the player hears and how spatial audio is perceived.
Common Pitfall
Imported prefabs may include their own AudioListener. Having more than one causes warnings and breaks expected audio behavior.
Quick fix
Remove or disable extra AudioListeners on prefabs. Keep a single listener on the main camera or active player object.
Practical checks
  • Open the scene and search for AudioListener components.
  • If multiple exist, remove extras from prefabs or scene objects not intended to be the ears.
  • Test during camera cuts: move the listener if listening should follow the active view.
Key Takeaway

Ensure a single active AudioListener that follows the player or active camera to preserve spatial consistency.
