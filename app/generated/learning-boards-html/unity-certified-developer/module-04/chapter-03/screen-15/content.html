AudioListener
The scene's ears. Keep exactly one active to avoid audio conflicts.
3D Spatial Audio
Spatial Blend and distance settings that create immersion through attenuation and panning.
AudioMixer
Centralized routing and effects. Use groups for Music and SFX to control many sources at once.
Final Summary
Combine these operational concepts to craft a consistent sound system: one listener, grouped routing, and tuned spatialization.
