Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Audio Format
Encoding method like WAV, MP3, OGG that affects quality and size.
Load Type
Decompress On Load, Compressed In Memory, or Streaming; choose by clip role.
AudioSource
Component that plays a clip and controls looping, spatialization, and output routing.
Summary
These three concepts determine how audio is stored, how it plays back, and how it is controlled at runtime. Use them as your import and design checklist.
