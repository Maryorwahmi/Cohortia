Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › AudioMixer Workflow
Mixer hierarchy
Master
› Music
› SFX
Create the AudioMixer asset, then add groups under Master. Use the inspector to add effects per group and expose volume parameters for runtime control.
Practical example
Create GameMixer, add SFX and Music groups, set AudioSource Output to the chosen group, and use the mixer faders to balance in play mode. Apply a soft reverb to ambient SFX group to glue world sounds together.
Misconception
Applying effects to individual sources is less maintainable than applying effects at the group level for consistent treatment.
Key Takeaway

Use grouped routing to simplify mixing and to enable rapid adjustments during playtests and polish passes.
