Managing Sprites & 2D Assets

Import images as Sprites, slice sheets, control draw order, and create simple sprite animations.

What you'll do

Configure textures as Sprites, slice sheets with the Sprite Editor, place sprites in scenes, manage Sorting Layers, and build a short walk animation.

Why it matters
Efficient asset slicing and ordering keeps your scenes correct and performant as your 2D project grows.
Sprite

Sprite Editor
