Troubleshooting scenarios
Short diagnosis tasks to test understanding of .meta and import settings.
Scenario A
You moved a model via the OS and scenes show missing references. Explain the role of .meta and how to fix it.
Scenario B
A short SFX is using 50MB of memory. Which import settings should you change and why?
Key Takeaway
Design assessments that check both conceptual knowledge of .meta/GUID and practical skill in changing import settings to meet performance goals.
