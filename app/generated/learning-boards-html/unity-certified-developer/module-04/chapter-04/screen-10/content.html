Inspector wiring

Drag a GameObject with a public method into OnClick and select the method. This is quick and easy for static UI elements.

Tip
Ensure the method is public and visible in the Inspector. For TextMeshPro buttons use the TMP button prefab variant for consistent styling.
Programmatic listeners

Use button.onClick.AddListener(MyMethod) and slider.onValueChanged.AddListener(MyFloatMethod) to connect behavior at runtime. Remember to avoid adding listeners repeatedly in Update or multiple Start invocations.

Common mistake

Adding listeners multiple times leads to repeated calls. Remove or guard additions to prevent duplicates.
