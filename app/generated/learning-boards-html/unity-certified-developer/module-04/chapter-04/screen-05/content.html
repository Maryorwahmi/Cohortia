Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Render Modes
Screen Space - Overlay
Renders UI directly on the screen. Not affected by camera transforms.
  • Best for HUD, menus, popups.
  • Pixel-based sizing and consistent placement.
  • Example: always-on minimap or score display.
Screen Space - Camera
UI is rendered by a specific camera, allowing post-processing and depth control.
  • Useful for multi-camera setups or UI that must share camera effects.
  • Adjust canvas plane distance to control occlusion and parallax.
World Space
Canvas behaves like a 3D object in the scene and is subject to lighting and perspective.
  • Ideal for in-world labels, terminals, or interactable panels.
  • Requires sizing in world units and careful scaling for legibility.
Practical contrast
Overlay gives pixel-stable UI. Camera ties visual style to a camera. World Space integrates UI into gameplay space. Pick based on whether UI must be a physical object or consistent overlay.
Misconception check: World Space does not guarantee readability—scale and font selection matter.
