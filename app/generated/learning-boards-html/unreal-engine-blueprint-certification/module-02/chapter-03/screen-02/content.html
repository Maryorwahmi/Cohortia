Learning Objectives
What you'll be able to do and why it matters for Blueprints.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Manage ordered collections
Create Arrays and use Add, Remove Index, and Get to keep ordered data you can iterate. Verified competency milestone
02
Use Maps for key lookups
Define key-value pairs and use Find/Contains Key for fast retrieval. Verified competency milestone
03
Design custom Structs
Group different typed fields into a single record with Make and Break nodes. Verified competency milestone
04
Choose the right structure
Combine Arrays, Maps, and Structs to design inventories, high scores, and settings. Verified competency milestone
