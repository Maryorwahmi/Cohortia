Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
Performance Bottleneck
The part of a program that determines overall speed; focus first on the largest consumers of frame time.
Event Tick
Fires every frame; avoid doing heavy work here unless you need per-frame precision.
Profiling
Use Stat commands for quick checks and Session Frontend for detailed node-level traces.
Blueprint Debugger
Breakpoints, watch variables, and stepping provide precise inspection of runtime state.
Debug Overhead
Remove or gate prints and debug draws to avoid contaminating profiler data and shipping performance.
Caching & Algorithms
Cache frequent references, avoid repeated lookups, and choose data structures that match access patterns.
Summary Takeaway

Measure first, change one thing, and verify with profiler traces and the debugger — this habit prevents regressions and leads to effective optimizations.
