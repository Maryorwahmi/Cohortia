Certification Exam Preparation and Best Practices

This session turns your Blueprint knowledge into exam-ready skills and repeatable professional workflows. We focus on what examiners look for and the practical habits that keep projects healthy.

In this lesson

Understand exam scope, practice scenario and debugging techniques, and apply project-level best practices like folder structure, naming conventions, version control, and Blueprint comments.

Why it matters

Clear structure and documented Blueprints reduce mistakes, speed debugging, and demonstrate professional competence both in exams and team projects.

Getting started

Begin by reviewing core Blueprint systems you used most: logic, interfaces, AI, UMG, and debugging. Then practice timed scenario questions and a short project refactor.

Exam Scope Overview
Core Blueprints, UI, AI, performance, and debugging.
Exam Types
Multiple-choice, scenario analysis, and debugging tasks.
Practical Refactor Preview
You will see a messy Content Browser refactored into logical folders and consistent names, plus examples of comment boxes and function descriptions.
Explore Key Topics
Exam Scope

Workspace Habits
