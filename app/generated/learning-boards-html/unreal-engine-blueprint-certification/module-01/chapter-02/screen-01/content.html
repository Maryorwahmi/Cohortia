Actors, Components, and the Level Editor

Learn to compose scene objects with Actors and Components, and place them precisely using the editor’s transform tools.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
What you'll master
Actors vs Components, placing Actors, parenting, snapping, and pivot tricks.

This lesson focuses on building reusable scene objects and positioning them with editor precision so your levels look intentional and behave predictably.

Why it matters
A solid grasp of Actors and Components saves development time and avoids runtime bugs caused by misplaced or orphaned parts.
Scene example
A lamp Actor contains a StaticMeshComponent for the model and a PointLightComponent for illumination. Move the lamp Actor and both parts move together.
Precision tools
Grid snapping and pivot adjustments let you align tiles and hinge doors without visual gaps.
