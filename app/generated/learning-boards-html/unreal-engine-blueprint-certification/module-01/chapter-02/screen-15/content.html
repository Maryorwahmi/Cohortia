Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Actor
The placeable container with a transform that holds Components and scripts.
Component
A reusable module you attach to an Actor to provide mesh, light, collision, camera, or movement.
Transforms & Snapping
Gizmo, grid and angle snapping, and pivot controls let you position and animate precisely.
Bottom line
Plan Actors as containers, attach focused Components, and use transform tools so your scenes are robust, editable, and easy to extend.
