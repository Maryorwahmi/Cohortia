Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Parent-Child Relationship
How transforms flow through the tree
A parent’s transform applies to all children; children maintain local offsets.
Example: Car Actor (root) with wheel Components. Moving the car translates every wheel automatically.
Misconception: Child rotation is in world space — actually it is relative to the parent unless you explicitly use world transform operations.
Practice: Drag one Actor on top of another in the World Outliner to make it a child. Move the parent to confirm inheritance.
Visual checklist
  • Child inherits translate, rotate, scale.
  • Rename children for clarity (Wheel_FL, Door_R).
  • Use Outliner to reparent Actors quickly.
Key Takeaway

Plan hierarchy to make group movement and targeted animation straightforward; avoid accidental detachment by confirming parentage after drag-and-drop operations.
