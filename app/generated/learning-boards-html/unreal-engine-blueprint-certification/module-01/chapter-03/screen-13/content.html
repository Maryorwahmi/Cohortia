Sample Question 1
You need a coin to play a sound when the player overlaps and then disappear. Which event and Blueprint do you use?
Answer
Use OnComponentBeginOverlap in the Actor Blueprint for the coin. Connect it to Play Sound and then Destroy Actor.
Sample Question 2
After editing a Blueprint the old message still appears when you press Play. What did you forget?
Answer
You likely forgot to Compile the Blueprint after making changes. Compile and test again.
Quick Practice
Write down the steps for a small feature: create Actor Blueprint, add mesh, add variable, wire BeginPlay to Print String, compile, and test. Rehearse this sequence until the workflow is familiar.
