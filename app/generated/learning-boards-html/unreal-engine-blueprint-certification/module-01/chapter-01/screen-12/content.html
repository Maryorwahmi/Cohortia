Blank vs. Genre Templates
Choose a template based on how much scaffolding you want.
Blank
Minimal setup, ideal to learn the Editor from the ground up or to build custom systems.
When to use: learning fundamentals, custom projects.
First Person / Third Person
Come with playable character, input mappings, and basic game mode to speed gameplay prototyping.
When to use: quickly test character interactions and level design.
Practical tip
Start with a genre template when your learning goal is interaction; use Blank if you want full control and are comfortable doing more setup.
