Content Browser Workflow
Find, organize, and place assets efficiently.
Core actions
  • Use search and folders to locate assets like SM_Chair.
  • Drag a mesh into the Viewport to create an Actor instance.
  • Inspect and edit its properties in the Details Panel.
Organization tips
Create scene folders for Models, Blueprints, Materials, and move imports into them. Avoid duplicated assets.
Key Takeaway

Good folder structure and search habits in the Content Browser speed development and reduce errors from missing or duplicated assets.

Quick Exercise
Locate SM_Chair in StarterContent → Props, drag it to the Level, and check its material in Details. Then move it into a Scene/Props folder in the Content Browser.
Misconception
Assets are cheap to duplicate — duplications create maintenance overhead. Prefer referencing and organizing instead.
