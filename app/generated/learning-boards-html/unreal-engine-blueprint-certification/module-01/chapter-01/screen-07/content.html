Viewport Navigation Controls
Practice the core camera controls to position objects accurately.
Core Controls
  • Hold Right Mouse Button + move mouse to look around.
  • While holding RMB: W forward, S back, A left, D right.
  • Q = move down, E = move up. Scroll wheel changes camera speed.
Practice Drill
Fast travel to a distant object by increasing speed with the scroll, then slow down to place a mesh precisely.
Common Mistake
Trying WASD without holding the right mouse button moves Actors or triggers other tools instead of moving the camera.
Quick Steps
1) Hold RMB and look. 2) Use WASD while holding RMB. 3) Use Q/E for vertical movement. 4) Scroll wheel to adjust speed.
Key Takeaway

Combining look and movement gives you a fluid, controllable camera for placing and inspecting objects accurately.
