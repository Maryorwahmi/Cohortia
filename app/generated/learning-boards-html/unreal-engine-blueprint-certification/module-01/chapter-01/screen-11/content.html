Move, Rotate, Scale
Practice the transform tools and save your Level.
Translate — W
Drag colored arrows to move along axes. Hold Shift for finer increments. Use numeric fields in Details for exact positions.
Rotate — E
Drag colored circles to rotate. Use angle snapping for precise rotations if needed.
Scale — R
Drag cubes to scale uniformly or per-axis. Numeric scale values ensure repeatability.
Key Takeaway

Practice W, E, R with numeric checks in the Details Panel to gain precision and speed in Level building.

Concrete Example
Place two chairs, rotate one 30 degrees with E, and scale the other to 0.9 with R. Save with Ctrl + S.
Common Mistake
Dragging without checking the active tool leads to accidental scaling or rotation. Confirm W/E/R before dragging.
