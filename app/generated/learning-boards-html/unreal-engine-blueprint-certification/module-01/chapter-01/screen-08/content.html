World Outliner and Actors
Understand Actors and how the Outliner helps manage them.
What is an Actor?
An Actor is any placeable or spawnable object in the Level: meshes, lights, cameras, and more.
Outliner usage
  • Find Actors by name, hide or lock them during editing.
  • Drag Actors into folders to group related items.
  • Select an Actor to populate the Details Panel with properties.
Concrete scenario
Place three chairs and rename them Chair_Left, Chair_Center, Chair_Right so you can select and edit them quickly later.
Key Takeaway

Treat the Outliner like your scene index: clear names and folders save time when levels grow.

Tip
Use folders in the Outliner early, even in practice scenes. It prevents accidental edits and speeds lookups.
Example
An Actor named SM_Chair appears in the Outliner when you place the mesh. Rename it to reflect purpose.
