How to verify competency
Small tasks that prove you understand each objective.
Panel Identification
  • Point to the Viewport and explain what a Level is.
  • Open World Outliner and show an Actor selected.
  • Confirm details appear in the Details Panel.
Navigation Demonstration
  • Hold right mouse and move mouse while pressing WASD.
  • Use Q and E for vertical moves, adjust speed with scroll wheel.
  • Common mistake: not holding right mouse before WASD; you’ll move objects instead of the camera.
Project Creation
Task
Create a Blank Blueprint project with Starter Content and explain why Blueprint is faster for iteration.
Transform Practice
Task
Place SM_Chair, then use W, E, R to move, rotate, and scale. Save the Level with Ctrl + S.
