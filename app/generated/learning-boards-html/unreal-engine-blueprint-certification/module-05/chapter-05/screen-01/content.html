Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Project Management & Optimization
Why this chapter matters

Small habits — consistent names, folders, and comments — compound into large savings when a project grows. Paired with basic optimization and safety checks, they reduce editor crashes and runtime slowdowns.

In this lesson
  • Establish a naming convention for Blueprints and UMG widgets.
  • Design logical folders in the Content Browser for discoverability.
  • Document Blueprint intent with comment boxes and descriptions.
  • Identify Event Tick misuse and replace it with timers or events.
