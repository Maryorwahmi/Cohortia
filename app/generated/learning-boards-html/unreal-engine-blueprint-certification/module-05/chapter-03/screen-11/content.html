Note: selecting the animation from the Play Animation node ensures you play the correct timeline. Use custom events when you need to coordinate multiple steps before showing the UI.
Practical checks
  • Ensure the widget is added to the viewport before relying on visual results.
  • Test different playback speeds to get the timing right for user perception.
  • Use reverse playback or Play Animation Reverse to hide the widget smoothly.
Misconception: Play Animation on a widget not added to viewport may not produce visible results during testing; always add the widget first.
