Parent controls rules
Whenever a widget becomes a child of a panel, its positioning and sizing are governed by that panel’s logic. Re-parenting moves responsibility and can change visual results immediately.
  • Anchors: define how a child scales relative to a parent.
  • Offsets: used with anchors to add pixel padding.
  • Alignment: controls placement inside parent containers.
Misconception: Anchors alone will fix layout—anchors must match your intended resizing behavior and often require appropriate offsets and parent settings.
Quick checks
After moving a widget, inspect the Hierarchy, check the parent’s alignment, and preview different resolutions to confirm behavior. Use Stretch anchors when you want a child to fill its parent.
Why it matters
Correct hierarchy prevents late-stage layout regressions as the UI scales across devices or windows.
