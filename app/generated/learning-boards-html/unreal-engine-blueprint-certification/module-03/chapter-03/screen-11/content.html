Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Finding Actors vs References
Comparison

Get All Actors vs Direct References

Choose initialization-time retrieval or cached references over frequent global searches.

When Get All Actors is OK

Level setup, one-off scripts, or debugging where performance is not critical.

When to avoid it

High-frequency logic like spawning many projectiles or per-frame checks; prefer cached references or dispatcher events.

Practical pattern

At BeginPlay, find required actors once and store their references. Use those stored references for future spawning and Owner assignment.

Key Takeaway

Prefer references established at BeginPlay or via dispatchers over repeated Get All Actors calls.
