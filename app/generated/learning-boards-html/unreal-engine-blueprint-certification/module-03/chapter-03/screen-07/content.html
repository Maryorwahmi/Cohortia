Definition

Owner and collision behavior

Owner ties a spawned Actor to its creator. Collision handling defines how to place the new Actor if the spawn point is occupied.

Owner uses
  • Damage attribution and friendly-fire checks.
  • Cleanup policies and ownership-based permissions.
  • Replication logic in networked games.
Collision handling choices

Pick Ignore Collisions for VFX, Try To Adjust for NPC placement, or Block spawn when exact placement is required.

Concrete scenario

Spawn a projectile with Owner set to the player. The projectile checks Owner to avoid damaging its creator. Use Try To Adjust for enemy spawn points in crowded waves to prevent overlap.

Pitfall

Ignoring collisions for physical Actors can cause immediate penetration or unexpected physics. Choose the override based on whether the Actor needs valid collision state on spawn.

Key Takeaway

Assign Owner for clear logic and pick collision handling to match gameplay reliability vs. cosmetic behavior.
