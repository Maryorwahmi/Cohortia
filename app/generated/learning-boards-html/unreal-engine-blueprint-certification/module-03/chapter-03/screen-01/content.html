You will implement a projectile that spawns from the player and self-destructs on impact or after a timeout.

Chapter orientation

Spawning and destruction are the runtime lifecycle operations for Actors. Treat them as paired actions: how you bring an Actor into the world determines how you should manage references and teardown later.
