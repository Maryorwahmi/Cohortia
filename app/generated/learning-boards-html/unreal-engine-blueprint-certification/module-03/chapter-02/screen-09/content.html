Enable Simulate Physics
Simulate Physics: ON

Check the Static Mesh component's Physics section and enable Simulate Physics. Ensure collision preset allows blocking or interaction with other physics bodies.

Gravity and contact forces automatically affect the object.
Apply forces or impulses via Blueprint to make objects move or react.
Avoid moving physics objects with SetActorLocation; use physics nodes for stable motion.
Key Takeaway

Enable simulation for realistic dynamics, and tune mass and damping to avoid jitter and tunneling.

Stability considerations
  • Set realistic mass values; very light objects accelerate too quickly and become unstable.
  • Use collision shapes that approximate the visual mesh; avoid tiny triangles or overly complex per-poly collisions.
  • Introduce damping to reduce oscillation for small or fast-moving objects.
  • Test interactions at gameplay speeds to catch tunneling or missed collisions.
Quick test
Spawn the physics object, apply a moderate impulse, and observe behavior. If it jitters, increase mass or damping or simplify the collision shape.
