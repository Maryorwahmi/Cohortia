Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Blackboard
Shared key-value store used by Behavior Trees to store state like TargetActor and PatrolLocation.
Behavior Tree
Hierarchical decision structure made of Root, Composites, Decorators, Tasks, and Services.
Navigation & Debug
Nav Areas, Crowd Manager, and the Behavior Tree Monitor are essential for reliable movement and diagnosing behavior.
Summary
Combine clear Blackboard keys, intentionally structured trees, and tuned navigation plus communication patterns to make AI that is both predictable and performant.
