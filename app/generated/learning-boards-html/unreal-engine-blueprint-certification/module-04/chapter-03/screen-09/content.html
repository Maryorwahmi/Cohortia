Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Move To Comparison
Move To Actor

Follow a live target

Directly references a target actor; path is recalculated to follow the actor as it moves.

Pros
  • Automatic re-evaluation for moving actors
  • Simpler setup for chasing behavior
Cons
Less control over offsets and prediction; may need extra logic for physics-driven targets.
Move To Location

Go to a position

Uses a vector location from the Blackboard; ideal for waypoints, patrols, or predicted positions.

Pros
More control for offsets, prediction, and smoothing. Good for patrols and precomputed destinations.
Cons
Requires updating vectors for moving targets; adds a Service or logic to refresh positions.
