Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Crowd Manager
Concept

Separation, Avoidance, Cohesion

Crowd Manager computes steering to reduce collisions and create natural group motion on top of NavMesh pathfinding.

When to use
Whenever many agents move in the same spaces and you need realistic spacing without manual collision handling.
Key Takeaway

Tune crowd parameters and test at scale; what looks fine with a few pawns may need different settings at larger scale.

Practical Tip
Use Separation to prevent overlap, Avoidance for dynamic obstacle steering, and Cohesion to keep platoons together. Combine with decorator logic to avoid deadlocks.
Common Mistake
Not testing with high agent counts and assuming per-agent steering scales linearly; always validate at target population levels.
