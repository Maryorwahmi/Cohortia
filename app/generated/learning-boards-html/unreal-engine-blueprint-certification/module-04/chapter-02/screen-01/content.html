Why it matters
Clean separation of logic helps reuse and makes debugging perception and pathfinding easier.
AI System Overview
Controller → Perception → Movement → State
AI Controller
Holds decision logic, binds to AIPerceptionComponent, issues Move To requests, and manages state variables like current waypoint index.
AIPerception
Configurable senses such as Sight and Hearing. Fires OnTargetPerceptionUpdated when actors appear or disappear from senses.
Movement
AI Move To uses the NavMesh to pathfind to a location or actor. Requires NavMesh Bounds Volume to be built and cover travel areas.
State Machine
Use an Enum (Idle, Patrol, Chase) and Switch on Enum to organize behavior and transitions cleanly.
