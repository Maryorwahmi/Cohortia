Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Debugging Perception & Navigation
Perception checks
  • Confirm Detection by Affiliation includes the player.
  • Verify Peripheral Vision Angle so the player is inside the vision cone.
  • Use ShowDebug AI Perception to see traces and which sense fired during runtime.
Visibility issue
An invisible blocking volume or wrong collision channel can prevent sight traces; inspect trace hits in the debug view to find the blocker.
Navigation checks
  • Press 'P' to visualize NavMesh; ensure patrol and chase areas are covered.
  • Use ShowDebug AI Navigation to inspect current path and look for invalid nodes.
  • Check Agent Radius and capsule collision to avoid narrow passage issues.
Common fix
Resize NavMesh Bounds Volume or rebuild NavMesh so AI has valid pathing surfaces for its patrol and chase routes.
