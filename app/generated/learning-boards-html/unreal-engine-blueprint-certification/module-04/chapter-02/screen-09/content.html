Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Move To Location or Actor & NavMesh
Architecture
• AI Move To(Target Actor) updates path as the actor moves; use retriggering to refresh frequently.
• AI Move To(Target Location) moves to a fixed vector; useful for waypoints or patrol points.
• NavMesh Bounds Volume defines where the NavMesh will be generated; press 'P' to visualize it in the editor.
Debug checklist
  • Is NavMesh present and covering the route? (Press 'P')
  • Does agent radius and step height match the pawn collision?
  • Use ShowDebug AI Navigation to inspect path and blocked nodes during runtime.
Common fix
Add or resize NavMesh Bounds Volume and rebuild the NavMesh. Verify the walkable area includes your TargetPoints and patrol paths.
Symptom
AI stands still or walks straight through obstacles—likely missing NavMesh or incorrect agent settings.
