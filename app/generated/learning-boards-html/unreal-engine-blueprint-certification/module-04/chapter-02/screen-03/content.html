Four Testable Checks
  1. Controller assignment: Confirm BP_EnemyAIController is set on BP_EnemyAI and BeginPlay events run in the controller.
  2. Perception events: Bind OnTargetPerceptionUpdated and validate sensing when player enters Sight Radius and peripheral angle.
  3. Patrol loop: Verify AI Move To reaches waypoints and increments CurrentWaypointIndex with wrap-around.
  4. State transitions: Change E_AI_State to Chase on successful sense, and back to Patrol when the player is lost.
Debug tools
Press 'P' to visualize NavMesh and use ShowDebug AI Perception during runtime to inspect traces and which sense fired.
Common pitfalls
Putting AI logic in the Pawn instead of the Controller, incorrect affiliation settings, or an incomplete NavMesh are frequent causes of nonresponsive AI.
