Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Retention & Tuning
S3 retention
Use lifecycle rules to move older logs to cheaper storage tiers or retain them as required by your compliance policy. Keep versioning on to recover deleted objects.
CloudWatch retention
Set retention periods per log group to bound costs and align with investigation windows.
Alarm tuning
Aggregate events and prefer high-confidence conditions. Revisit thresholds periodically to reduce false positives and maintain trust in alerts.
Key Takeaway
Retention and alarm tuning are continuous tasks: automate lifecycle policies and periodically review filters and thresholds to balance cost with security needs.
