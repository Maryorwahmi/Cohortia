Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Common Mistakes
Frequent Errors
Small misconfigurations cause denied access or weak auditability.
Assuming encryption is enabled
Verify each resource's encryption flag; older resources may be unencrypted.
Misconfigured KMS policies
IAM allowed but key policy blocks the service or role, causing AccessDenied.
Losing SSE-C keys
You are fully responsible for SSE-C keys. Losing a key renders data inaccessible.
Avoidance checklist
Practical checks to include in operational runbooks.
Verify encryption state after deployment and on target resources.
Test downloads with minimally privileged users to ensure kms:Decrypt is required and working.
Review CloudTrail KMS events to confirm GenerateDataKey and Decrypt activity aligns with expectations.
Key Takeaway

Run verification tests and include key policy reviews in deployments to prevent accidental access failures or weak controls.
