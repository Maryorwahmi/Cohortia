Learning Objectives
Part 2 — replication, keys, and mistakes to avoid
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Plan multi-region replication
Decide whether to use the same CMK in a destination region, or re-encrypt objects with a region-specific key and adjust ownership and policies accordingly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Create KMS policies & grants
Grant minimal kms:GenerateDataKey and kms:Decrypt to roles and services that need encryption functions while keeping auditability and control.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Avoid common mistakes
Verify encryption state; do not assume defaults are sufficient. Ensure upload roles and the s3.amazonaws.com principal have required key permissions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Test permission boundaries
Perform an upload as the intended role, then attempt a download with blocked kms:Decrypt to observe enforced AccessDenied behavior and refine policies.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
These objectives emphasize operational verification, not just configuration. Successful completion means encrypted assets remain usable by authorized systems and blocked for unauthorized actors.
