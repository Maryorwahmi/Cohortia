Cross-Region Replication — Key choices
Decide whether to replicate key ownership or re-encrypt objects in destination regions.
Option: same CMK
Requires an equivalent CMK in destination region — often not possible automatically because CMKs are regional.
Option: re-encrypt at destination
Destination bucket uses its own CMK and replicated objects are re-encrypted on arrival.
Permissions to check
Ensure replication role and s3.amazonaws.com are allowed in both CMK policies for required actions.
Concrete example
Replicate from us-east-1 to eu-west-1 and re-encrypt with eu-west-1 CMK.
Action: set up CRR, configure destination bucket default encryption with a eu-west-1 CMK, ensure the replication IAM role has kms:Encrypt permission on the destination key, and verify replicated objects show the destination CMK in their properties.
Common Mistake

Assuming KMS keys replicate automatically across regions. They do not; plan region-specific keys or re-encryption.
