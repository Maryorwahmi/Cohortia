DynamoDB Encryption — Options
Select key ownership according to compliance and audit needs.
AWS owned key
Default, zero management. AWS owns and rotates the key; minimal visibility.
AWS managed key
Visible in your account but policy managed by AWS; you can view key usage but not change policies.
Customer Managed Key
You manage the CMK policy, grants, and rotation. Best for compliance and audit tracking.
Concrete example
Your compliance team requires audit logs for key usage.
Action: choose a Customer Managed Key for the table and ensure the key policy grants your application role kms:Decrypt. Verify CloudTrail shows KMS Decrypt events when your application reads items.
Common Mistake

Selecting an AWS owned key when auditability is required, then discovering you cannot obtain key usage logs needed for compliance.
