Operational Rules
Quick references for daily operations.
EBS & RDS
Snapshots inherit encryption; copy to re-encrypt or change key ownership.
DynamoDB
Encrypted by default; choose CMKs for ownership and auditability.
Multi-Region
KMS keys are regional; plan re-encryption or matching keys in destination regions.
Final Takeaway
Encryption is a design and operations discipline: configure keys, verify policies, test access, and track key usage for audit and continuity.
