Key Concepts
Essential definitions to keep handy.
Server-Side Encryption (SSE)
Encryption performed by the storage service at the point of storage.
SSE-S3
S3 manages keys and rotation; low operational overhead but limited audit detail.
SSE-KMS
Uses KMS data keys and CMKs for control and CloudTrail-audited key usage.
Summary
Select the SSE option that matches your control, audit, and operational needs. Verify policies and test access after configuration.
