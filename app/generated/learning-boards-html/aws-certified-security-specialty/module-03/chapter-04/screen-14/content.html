Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
SSL/TLS
Cryptographic protocol that secures communication and ensures integrity and confidentiality.
ACM (Public & Private)
Centralized certificate provisioning, renewal, and deployment; Private CA for internal trust chains.
VPN & Direct Connect
Use IPsec for encrypted tunnels; Direct Connect requires layering IPsec for confidentiality.
Summary Takeaway

Choose TLS termination and certificate types based on trust boundaries and operational needs. Automate certificate lifecycle and protect keys when using client-side encryption.
