Comparison

Direct Connect vs VPN

Direct Connect
Dedicated private line, predictable throughput, lower latency. Does not provide encryption by default.
Site-to-Site VPN
Runs over the internet with IPsec encryption. Subject to variable network performance.
Hybrid approach

When you need both performance and confidentiality, layer IPsec VPN over Direct Connect. This gives you a dedicated path with encrypted payloads. Ensure routing and keys are managed and monitor both physical connectivity and tunnel state.

Key Takeaway

Direct Connect provides performance; add IPsec if your threat model requires encryption across the private link.
