Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › ACM: Public vs Private
Definition

ACM responsibilities and limits

ACM public certificates: issuance from a public CA, automated renewal, domain validation, and deployment to integrated AWS services. Private CA: create your own CA hierarchy for internal trust and certificate issuance.

Example: Use ACM public cert for CloudFront distribution to serve HTTPS for a custom domain.
Misconception: Public ACM certs are exportable — they are not. For external, non-integrated uses, request a certificate using a CSR from a public CA or use Private CA.
When to use Private CA
  • Internal microservices that require mutual TLS and are not public-facing.
  • IoT devices or internal servers that require a private trust chain.
  • When you need the ability to export or distribute private certificates under your own control.
Key Takeaway

ACM reduces operational burden for public TLS; choose Private CA when you must run an internal PKI and issue certificates under your trust chain.
