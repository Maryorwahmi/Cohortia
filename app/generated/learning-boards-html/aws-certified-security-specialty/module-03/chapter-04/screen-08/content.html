Architecture

Site-to-Site IPsec VPN

VPN creates encrypted tunnels from your on-premises gateway to AWS, protecting all traffic across the public internet. The AWS side typically terminates at a virtual private gateway or transit gateway.

Example: Corporate datacenter ↔ IPsec tunnel ↔ VPC; routing configured to send application subnets through the tunnel.
Misconception: VPN equals predictable performance — it does not; expect variable latency and throughput due to traversing the public internet.
Operational checklist
  • Configure strong cipher suites and lifetime settings for IPsec.
  • Monitor VPN tunnel health and automate failover to a secondary tunnel.
  • Ensure correct security group and route table settings to accept tunneled traffic.
Key Takeaway

Use Site-to-Site VPN to protect hybrid traffic over the internet. Plan for redundancy and monitor the tunnel for availability and performance.
