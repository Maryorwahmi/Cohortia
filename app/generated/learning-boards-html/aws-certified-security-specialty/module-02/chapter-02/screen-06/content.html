Comparison

Shield Standard vs Shield Advanced

Understand the scope and when to invest in Shield Advanced for business-critical protections.

Shield Standard
Automatic baseline mitigation for common network and transport DDoS attacks; enabled for all customers.
Shield Advanced
Enhanced mitigation for larger or complex attacks, near real-time visibility, DDoS cost protection, and 24/7 DRT access.
When to use Advanced

Choose Advanced for high-value applications where downtime or scaling costs pose significant business risk, such as critical e-commerce or financial services.

Example
An e-commerce platform experiencing frequent large volumetric attacks would use Shield Advanced to gain cost protection and access to DRT for coordinated mitigation.
Misconception
Shield Advanced is not a substitute for WAF. Use WAF for application-layer filtering and Shield for network/transport protections.
Key Takeaway
Combine WAF for application threats with Shield Advanced for stronger DDoS protections when required by risk tolerance.
