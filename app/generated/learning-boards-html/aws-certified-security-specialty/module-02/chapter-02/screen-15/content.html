Key Concepts — Part 2

Three compact cards on visibility and detection to remember.

VPC Flow Logs
Session records of network traffic published to CloudWatch or S3 for analysis.
GuardDuty Findings
ML and threat intelligence generate prioritized alerts like PortScan or C&CActivity.
Query Tools
CloudWatch Logs Insights and Athena let you parse flow logs and investigate findings.
Summary

Visibility plus prioritized detection equals faster, more accurate incident response.
