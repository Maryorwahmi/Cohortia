Introduction to Error Handling: Exceptions

Learn the why and how of C++ exceptions, use standard types, design custom exceptions, and avoid common mistakes that lead to crashes or sliced objects.

Orientation

We will start with mechanics, inspect standard exceptions, build a custom exception class, and finish with practical advice for resource-safe code.

Why this matters

Unhandled errors can crash your application or corrupt data. Exceptions let you centralize error handling and keep business logic readable.
