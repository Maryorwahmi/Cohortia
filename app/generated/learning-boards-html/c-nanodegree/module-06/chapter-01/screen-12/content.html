Comparison

Specific vs General handlers

Correct order
Place derived handlers first, then base class handlers, then catch(...).
When to use catch(...)
At top-level only: log diagnostic data, release critical resources, and perform controlled shutdown or restart logic.
Example implication
If you catch a broad type early, you may mask actionable details that a more specific handler could use to recover or retry safely.
Key Takeaway

Order handlers from specific to general and keep catch-all handlers limited to boundary-level logging and shutdown responsibilities.
