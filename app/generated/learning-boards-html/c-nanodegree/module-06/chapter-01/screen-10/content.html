Definition

Destructors and exceptions

If a destructor throws during stack unwinding while another exception is active, the runtime will call terminate. To prevent this, destructors should not allow exceptions to escape.

Safe patterns
Make destructors noexcept and perform fallible cleanup in explicit methods that the caller can call where exceptions are acceptable.
RAII note
RAII still applies: prefer simple resource wrappers whose destructors do not throw.
Concrete advice
  • Catch and handle errors inside destructors rather than letting them propagate.
  • Prefer explicit close or flush methods that report errors to callers before destruction.
  • Make destructors noexcept to document the intent and avoid implicit termination on failures.
Key Takeaway

Design destructors to avoid throwing; surface fallible cleanup through explicit, testable operations.
