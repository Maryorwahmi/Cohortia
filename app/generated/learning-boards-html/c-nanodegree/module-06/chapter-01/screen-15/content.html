Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What to Remember
Throw clear types
Prefer std exceptions or custom exceptions with messages and context over raw values.
Catch by const reference
Preserve type information and avoid unnecessary copies in your handlers.
Keep destructors noexcept
Avoid throwing from destructors; surface errors via explicit methods or safe logging.
Final tip
Test both happy and exceptional paths so your handlers and RAII patterns behave correctly under real failures.
