What to Remember
Pitfalls and best practices to avoid bugs
Validate input
Check fail() and handle stoi/stod exceptions when parsing numbers from strings.
Reset both buffer & flags
Call str("") and clear() before reusing a stringstream to avoid leftover error states.
Restore rdbuf
Always restore the original streambuf after redirection; prefer RAII to ensure it happens even on error.
Final thought
String streams and stream buffers let you reason about I/O at two levels: formatted operations in memory and the buffer mechanics that make redirection and extension possible.
