Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › What to Remember
Smart pointers
Use unique_ptr for exclusive ownership; shared_ptr when multiple owners exist.
Destructors
Do not propagate exceptions from destructors; handle failures internally.
noexcept on moves
Mark move constructors noexcept when they truly cannot throw to enable container optimizations.
Closing takeaway

These practical checks help you maintain exception safety and resource correctness across your codebase.
