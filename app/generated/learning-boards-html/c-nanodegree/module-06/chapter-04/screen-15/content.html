Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
Streams
Abstract flows connecting program and devices; streams carry characters and maintain state.
Extraction & insertion
Use >> for tokens and << for output; check stream state after reading.
Manipulators
Control precision, width, base, and boolean text. Some manipulators persist, others apply next item only.
Summary
Master these small, repeatable patterns—validation loops, buffer clearing, intentional formatting, and choosing the right stream—to make console programs that behave predictably for users.
