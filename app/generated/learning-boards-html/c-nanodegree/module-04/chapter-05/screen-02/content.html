These objectives focus you on reading and writing packs, processing via recursion or folds, and handling edge cases like empty packs. Practice by implementing the concat fold and by refactoring a recursive print to a fold-based print. This trains both your syntax fluency and your design judgment.
Verified competency is achieved when your concat handles strings, character arrays, and numeric types converted to strings with correct empty-pack behavior.
