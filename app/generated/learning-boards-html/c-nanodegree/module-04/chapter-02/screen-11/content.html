Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Common Mistakes
Missing template prefix
Always begin external definitions with template <...> or the compiler will reject the member definition.
Wrong qualification
Use Pair<T1,T2>::member rather than Pair::member. The template parameters are required to match the declaration.
Hidden definitions
Definitions in .cpp files often cause undefined references at link time. Keep templates in headers.
Quick Fix
Add template prefix and qualified name, then ensure the definition is included in the header visible at instantiation.
Verification
Recompile a minimal test that instantiates the template to confirm the fix. Linker errors should disappear when definitions are visible.
