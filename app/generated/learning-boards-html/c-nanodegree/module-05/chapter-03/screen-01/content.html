Iterator abstraction, iterator categories, and the STL algorithms that operate on ranges.

Why it matters

Quick win

Concept Map
Containers manage storage, iterators provide access, algorithms operate on ranges defined by iterator pairs. This separation keeps algorithms generic and reusable.
Container
std::vector, std::list, std::map
Iterator
begin(), end(), operator++
Algorithm
std::sort, std::find, std::transform
Range
[begin, end) half-open
