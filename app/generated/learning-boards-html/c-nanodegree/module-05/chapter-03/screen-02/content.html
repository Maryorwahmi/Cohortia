Learning Objectives
Skills, why they matter, and verified milestones.
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain iterators
Describe iterators as pointer-like accessors and why they enable generic algorithms.
Verified competency milestone
02
Differentiate categories
Identify Input, Output, Forward, Bidirectional, and Random Access and map to containers.
Verified competency milestone
03
Use algorithms on ranges
Apply std::for_each, std::find, std::sort, std::transform using begin/end ranges.
Verified competency milestone
04
Avoid iterator pitfalls
Recognize invalidation, end() off-by-one, and performance differences across iterator types.
Verified competency milestone
