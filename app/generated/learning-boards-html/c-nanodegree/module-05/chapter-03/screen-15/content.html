Use [begin, end)
Half-open ranges avoid off-by-one and compose well with algorithms.
Know the category
Match algorithm needs to iterator capabilities to ensure compilation and performance.
Prevent invalidation
Avoid using saved iterators after operations that reallocate or erase elements.
Final quick tip

When in doubt, favor explicit, documented container operations or copy to a vector for random-access algorithms, and always validate iterators before dereferencing.
