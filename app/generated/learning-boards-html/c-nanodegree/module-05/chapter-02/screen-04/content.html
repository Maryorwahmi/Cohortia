Core Concept
What are associative containers?
Containers optimized for key-based retrieval and uniqueness rules, used where position-based indexing is poor fit.
When to use
Lookups by identifier, dictionaries, symbol tables, caches, unique item tracking.
Misconception
Unordered is not random; it simply does not maintain sorted order and may vary with operations.
Key Takeaway

Choose ordered containers when sorted iteration or worst-case guarantees matter; choose unordered for fastest average-case lookup when order does not matter.

Concrete examples
Thesaurus (map): words to synonyms, iterated alphabetically. Word counter (unordered_map): fast frequency lookup when order isn't needed. Unique word list (set): sorted distinct words for reports.
Analogy
Think of ordered containers as a dictionary with alphabetical tabs, and unordered containers as a hash-indexed filing system where you jump directly to a bucket.
Check your understanding
  • Do you require sorted iteration? Then favor ordered containers.
  • Do you require fastest average lookups and don't care about order? Favor unordered containers.
  • Are you using custom key types? Remember comparator vs hash requirements.
