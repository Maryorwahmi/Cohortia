Quick Reference
Three rules you can apply immediately.
Ordered
map/set: sorted iteration and O(log N) guarantees.
Unordered
unordered_map/set: fastest average lookup; ensure good hash for custom keys.
Insertion
Use insert/emplace to detect duplicates; operator[] creates or overwrites intentionally.
Bottom Line

Match container semantics to your requirements: ordering, performance, memory, and key-type support are the deciding factors.
