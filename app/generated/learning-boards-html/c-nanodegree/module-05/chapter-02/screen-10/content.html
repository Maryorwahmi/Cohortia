Architecture
Custom types as keys
Ordered containers need comparators; unordered containers need hashes and equality checks.
Ordered: comparator
Provide operator< or a comparator that establishes a strict weak ordering for your key type.
Unordered: hash + equality
Provide std::hash specialization or a custom hasher and operator== for correct grouping and comparisons in buckets.
Test your custom key by inserting examples and verifying lookups and iteration behave as expected. Mistakes in comparator or equality semantics produce incorrect ordering or failed lookups.
Analogy & Example
Think of operator< as a rule that orders entries on the shelf. Think of the hash as the shelf label and operator== as checking the exact item when two items share a shelf label.
Common mistake
Providing only a hash without correct operator== leads to incorrect equality checks. Providing a non-strict comparator breaks tree invariants.
