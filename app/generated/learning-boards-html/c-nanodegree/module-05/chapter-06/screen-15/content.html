Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Concepts
std::optional
Represents optional presence; avoid dereferencing without checks.
std::variant / std::any
variant for known types and exhaustive handling; any for dynamic, runtime-typed storage.
chrono & thread
Chrono gives typed time; thread runs tasks but needs join/detach and synchronization.
Final takeaway

Choose the right utility for clarity and safety. Prefer type-safe alternatives and explicit checks to reduce runtime surprises.
