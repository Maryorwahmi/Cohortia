Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › What to Remember
Use make_unique
Creates and returns a unique_ptr safely, avoiding raw new usage.
Move to transfer
Ownership moves with std::move; the source becomes null and must not be used.
Be careful with get()
get() yields a raw pointer for interop; do not let it outlive the unique_ptr owner or call delete on it.
Final action

Refactor one allocation to unique_ptr, run the program, and observe deterministic destructor calls as immediate feedback of correct ownership.
