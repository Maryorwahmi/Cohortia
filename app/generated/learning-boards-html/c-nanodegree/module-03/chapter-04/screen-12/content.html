Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Common mistakes
Three frequent errors
Attempting to copy a unique_ptr — results in compile-time error and indicates wrong ownership model.
Manually deleting a pointer owned by unique_ptr — leads to double delete and undefined behavior.
Exposing raw pointer with get() without guarding lifetime — can produce dangling references.
Compile-time safety
One advantage is the compiler enforces the no-copy rule. Use that feedback to change your design instead of forcing copies.
Key Takeaway

Treat unique_ptr as an exclusive owner. Use moves for transfer, and keep raw pointers short-lived observations only.
