Constructing unique_ptr safely
Prefer the make_unique helper to avoid raw new and improve exception safety.
#include <iostream>
#include <memory>

auto r = std::make_unique<MyResource>("FileHandle");
if (r) r->doSomething();
Checking r for truthiness is the usual way to test whether it owns an object. When r goes out of scope, its destructor deletes the owned object.
Caution with get()
get() returns the raw pointer. Use it for interop but do not take ownership or call delete on the returned pointer; unique_ptr still owns it.
Key Takeaway

Construct with make_unique. Use pointer operations naturally and avoid manual delete when unique_ptr owns the resource.
