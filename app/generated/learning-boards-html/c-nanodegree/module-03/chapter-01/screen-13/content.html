Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Safer Memory Patterns
Practical habits
  • Prefer stack allocation for fixed-size local data.
  • When using the heap, prefer containers or RAII to manage lifetime.
  • Always match new with delete and new[] with delete[] and nullify pointers.
  • Keep allocation and deallocation responsibility close together in code.
Pro-tip
Use container abstractions and smart pointers where available. They reduce human error and express ownership intent clearly. We will explore RAII and smart pointers in subsequent chapters.
Key Takeaway

Small, consistent habits and RAII-based patterns prevent most manual memory-management bugs.
