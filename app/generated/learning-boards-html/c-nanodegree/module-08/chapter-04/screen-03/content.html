Practical Outcomes & Safety

These outcomes ensure you make measurable, correct, and portable performance improvements rather than risky micro-optimizations.

Measure
Profile in production-like environments
Tiny tests mislead. Use realistic inputs and system settings when measuring.
Verify
Confirm correctness after changes
Run tests to ensure optimizations do not change behavior or create vulnerabilities.
Document
Explain why a change was made
Record the benchmark evidence and platform details for maintainers.
