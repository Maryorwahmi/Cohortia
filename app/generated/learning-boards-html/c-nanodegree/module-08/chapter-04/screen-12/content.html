Core Concept

Branch prediction

Predictable branches keep the pipeline busy. Mispredictions inside hot loops are costly and show up in hardware counters.

Symptoms
High branch-misprediction counts for a hot function indicate unpredictable control flow.
Mitigations
Simplify conditionals, move checks out of tight loops, or use table-driven approaches to avoid unpredictable branching.
Practical tip

Measure branch-misprediction counters with perf to decide whether to refactor. Avoid blind branch elimination; test the performance impact in your environment.

Key Takeaway

Branch behavior matters: reduce unpredictability in hot loops and validate the effect with hardware counters.
