Orientation

This lesson emphasizes patterns you will use continuously: declare interfaces early, keep implementations private, and prefer small, pure functions where possible. We'll practice writing prototypes, defining functions, organizing .h and .cpp files, and refactoring a simple calculator into modular components so compilation and testing are easier.
