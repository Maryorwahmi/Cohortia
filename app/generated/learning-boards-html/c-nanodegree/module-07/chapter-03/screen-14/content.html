Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Key Concepts
std::condition_variable
Efficient waiting and notification; pair with predicate-protected waits to be correct.
std::promise / std::future
One‑time result channel; promise sets the value, future retrieves it once.
std::async
Launch a task and get a future; pick async or deferred policy to control behavior.
std::shared_mutex
Shared locks for many readers, exclusive locks for one writer — ideal for read-heavy data.
Predicate
Guard your waits. Predicates protect against spurious wakeups and ensure correctness.
Avoid busy waits
Prefer blocking primitives like condition variables over polling to conserve CPU.
Summary takeaway
Pick the right tool: condition variables for coordination, futures for one-time results, async for quick tasks, and shared_mutex for read-heavy performance.
