Protecting Shared Data with Mutexes
Prevent race conditions by using mutexes, lock_guard, and unique_lock for safe multithreading in C++.
In this lesson you will recognize race conditions, identify critical sections, and apply RAII-based locking to make concurrent code correct and exception safe.

Why this matters
Correctness under concurrency is nondeterministic without synchronization. Small bugs hide until they fail in production.
