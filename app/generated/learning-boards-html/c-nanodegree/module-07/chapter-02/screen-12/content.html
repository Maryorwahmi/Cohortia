Locking Patterns
Minimal locks: protect only what must be atomic.
Ownership: place mutex next to the data it protects.
Defaults: use lock_guard unless you need unique_lock features.
Multiple locks: document order or use std::lock with adopt_lock.
Testing and validation
Write stress tests that spawn many threads and insert small sleeps to amplify race windows. Use these tests when you change locking to ensure behavior remains correct.
Misconception: atomics replace locks. Atomics help single-step operations but do not make multi-step invariants atomic without careful composition.
Key Takeaway

Design locks to protect invariants, keep them small, and verify behavior with stress tests under contention.
