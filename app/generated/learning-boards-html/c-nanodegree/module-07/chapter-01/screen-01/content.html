Introduction to Concurrency and Threads
Why concurrent code matters and how std::thread lets C++ programs do more at once.

This chapter explains threads, how to launch them with std::thread, the difference between join and detach, and how race conditions arise when shared data is accessed without synchronization. You will see a practical worker example and a hands-on activity to compare join and detach behavior.
