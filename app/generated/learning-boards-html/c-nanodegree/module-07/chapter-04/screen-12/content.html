Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Pitfalls & Best Practices
Pitfall
Assuming atomic operations automatically make related non-atomic data visible.
Rule of thumb
Document and justify any use of weaker ordering; default to acquire-release for safety and seq_cst for simplicity.
CAS guidance
Make CAS loop side effects idempotent to prevent repeated actions on retries.
Reflection

Before replacing a mutex with atomics, prove the correctness of ordering and run targeted tests that increase contention to reveal hidden races.
