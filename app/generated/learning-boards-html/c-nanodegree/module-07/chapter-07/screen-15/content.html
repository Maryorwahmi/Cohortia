Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Final Checklist
Factory safety
Prefer std::make_unique and std::make_shared for exception-safety and allocation efficiency.
Ownership first
Choose unique_ptr by default; only use shared_ptr when multiple owners exist.
Break cycles
Use weak_ptr for back-references and observers so objects are destroyed when owners go out of scope.
Reflect

Which ownership pattern will you adopt in your next code change to reduce leaks and clarify lifetime?
