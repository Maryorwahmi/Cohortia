Smart Pointers and Resource Management

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass — revisit RAII, ownership models, and safe cleanup patterns.
In this lesson

You will learn RAII, unique and shared ownership, weak pointers to break cycles, and custom deleters for non-memory resources.

Why it matters

In concurrent programs, proper ownership eliminates leaks and double-frees and makes object lifetime predictable across threads.

Key Takeaway

Prefer unique ownership by default, use shared ownership only when necessary, and use weak references to avoid cycles.
