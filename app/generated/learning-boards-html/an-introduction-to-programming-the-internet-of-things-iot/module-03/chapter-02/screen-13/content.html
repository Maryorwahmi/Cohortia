Process Flow Deployment considerations
Power
Estimate energy per transmit and duty cycle; Wi‑Fi usually higher, LoRa designed for low energy.
Cost
Consider one‑time hardware costs and recurring costs like cellular data plans or gateway maintenance.
Maintenance
Plan for firmware updates, physical access, and how long devices must operate unattended.
Deployment checklist
  1. Define transmit frequency and expected battery life.
  2. Choose a network that meets range and power constraints.
  3. Schedule firmware update and fallback strategy for connectivity loss.
