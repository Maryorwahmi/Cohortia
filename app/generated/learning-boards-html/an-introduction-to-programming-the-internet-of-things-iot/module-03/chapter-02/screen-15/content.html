What to Remember
Ethernet Shield
W5100/W5500 via SPI for wired, reliable connections and optional microSD logging.
ESP8266 / ESP32
Low‑cost modules with built‑in Wi‑Fi, usable as standalone controllers or network adapters.
HTTP GET requests
A quick way to send telemetry in query parameters; simple but not encrypted and limited for complex payloads.
Summary

Match technology to requirements: wired reliability, wireless convenience, long range or low power—each has its place.
