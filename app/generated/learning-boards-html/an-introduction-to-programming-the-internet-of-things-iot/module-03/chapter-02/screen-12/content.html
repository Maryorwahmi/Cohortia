Comparison Network choices
Ethernet
Reliable, fast, requires cable. Best for stationary devices near a router.
Wi‑Fi (ESP modules)
Wireless, low-cost, easy to deploy where Wi‑Fi is available and power is sufficient.
Cellular
Works where cellular coverage exists; needs SIM and data plan and is costlier.
LoRa / LoRaWAN
Very long range and very low power for sensors spread across large areas with gateway support.
Assessment scenario
For a battery‑powered soil moisture sensor on a farm with no Wi‑Fi, LoRaWAN or NB‑IoT is preferred due to range and low power; Wi‑Fi would not be suitable without coverage and would drain batteries quickly.
