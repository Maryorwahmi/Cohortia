What to Remember
Web server limits
Serve tiny pages and parse minimally to avoid exhausting resources on Arduino-based servers.
Network tradeoffs
Match coverage, power, and cost to your application: cellular for coverage, LoRa for low power long range, Ethernet for local reliability.
Verify addresses
Confirm unique MACs and correct IP assignment to avoid collisions and connection failures in the field.
Closing Takeaway

Design for the full stack: hardware wiring, addressing, networking, and operational constraints determine whether your IoT device will work reliably in the real world.
