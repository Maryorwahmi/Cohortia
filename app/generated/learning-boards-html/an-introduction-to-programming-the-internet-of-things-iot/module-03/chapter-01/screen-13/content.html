Assessment prompt
Explain why a relay module between an Arduino and a 120V lamp is safer than wiring the lamp directly to the Arduino.
Key points to include: coil/contact isolation, optocoupler use, enclosure, and common-ground practice for low-voltage sides.
Explain: Arduino energizes coil at low voltage; contacts carry mains current separately. A fault on the mains side does not apply mains to the Arduino circuit directly when properly isolated and enclosed.
Misconception to avoid: Do not assume a relay removes the need for safe wiring — it reduces risk but does not eliminate it.
Answer outline
Isolation: coil and contacts separate circuits, so the Arduino never carries mains current.
Optocoupling: reduces electrical coupling and noise between the control and load sides.
Enclosure: prevents accidental contact with live terminals and improves user safety.
