Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Motor control
H-bridges + PWM for direction and speed; external supply and flyback protection required.
Servos & positioning
Pulse width maps to angle; use the Servo library and external power for movement peaks.
Relays & displays
Relays isolate mains; displays give local feedback — choose I2C or drivers to reduce pin use.
Summary takeaway

When controlling actuators, design for adequate power, shared grounds, and proper isolation; the Arduino should always control signals, not carry heavy currents.
