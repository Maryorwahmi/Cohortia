Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Power & Grounding
Power planning steps
  1. Estimate peak current for each actuator, including startup/stall.
  2. Choose an external supply that covers the combined peak current.
  3. Connect actuator power directly to the external supply; do not run heavy loads through Arduino pins.
  4. Connect the external supply ground to the Arduino ground so signals share a reference.
  5. Add protection: flyback diodes, decoupling capacitors, and fuses where appropriate.
Key Takeaway

Plan for peak currents and always common the grounds to maintain reliable control signals between Arduino and driver modules.

Concrete scenario
If a servo and a motor are both connected, estimate their combined startup draw. Select a 5V supply rated above that peak, power both actuators from it, and connect its ground to the Arduino ground pin.
Common mistakes
  • Powering moving servos from Arduino 5V and causing resets.
  • Not connecting ground lines together so signals have no reference.
  • Underestimating startup current and selecting an undersized supply.
