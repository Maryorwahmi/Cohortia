Top troubleshooting steps
  • Confirm external supply voltage and that it can deliver peak current.
  • Verify grounds are common between Arduino and external supplies.
  • Test each actuator with a minimal sketch to isolate faults.
  • Watch driver and relay module LEDs for status and errors.
  • For I2C displays scan the bus to confirm device address before assuming code issues.
Common fix list
Use a bench supply to reproduce the problem and watch for voltage drops.
Swap the actuator with a confirmed working unit to rule out mechanical faults.
Test relay switching with a low-voltage lamp or LED before connecting mains.
Safety check

Stop and re-evaluate if you smell burning, see smoke, or experience unexpected heating — disconnect power and inspect wiring.
