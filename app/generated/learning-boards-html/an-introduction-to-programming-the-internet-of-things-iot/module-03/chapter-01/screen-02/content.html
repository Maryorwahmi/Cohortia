Learning Objectives
Learn the skills, why they matter, and how to apply them safely.
01
Control motors
Drive DC and stepper motors through an H-bridge and use PWM for speed. Verified competency milestone.
02
Use relays safely
Operate AC devices with relay modules that provide isolation and protective enclosures. Verified competency milestone.
03
Interface displays
Connect character LCDs or OLEDs for local feedback and status messages. Verified competency milestone.
04
Manage power & grounds
Choose external supplies and common grounds to prevent resets and damage. Verified competency milestone.

How to use these objectives
  • Follow the code examples to exercise each actuator in isolation.
  • Then combine a servo, a relay, and an I2C LCD in the hands-on activity to test integration.
  • Practice power separation and always confirm grounds are common between supplies and the Arduino.
