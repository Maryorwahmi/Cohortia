Signal vs Power
Arduino provides signals; drivers and supplies provide the energy the actuator needs.
Common Ground
Connect grounds between Arduino and external supplies so control pulses have a stable reference.
Isolation & Safety
Use opto-isolated relay modules, enclosures, and fuses when switching mains to reduce risk to users and hardware.
Final thought

A few simple wiring and power rules make actuator systems reliable and safe. Apply them consistently and your projects will scale from prototypes to dependable devices.
