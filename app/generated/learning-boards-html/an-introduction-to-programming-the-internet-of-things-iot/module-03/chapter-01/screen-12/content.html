Layered architecture
Control signal
Low-voltage outputs from the microcontroller that command drivers or modules.
Driver
H-bridge, servo input stage, or relay coil interface which handles larger currents.
Power source
External supply sized for peak currents; decoupling and fuses applied here.
Actuator
The motor, servo, relay contacts, or display that performs the work or shows output.
Practical wiring checklist
  • Label control pins and note driver input logic levels.
  • Choose driver modules that include flyback protection for motors.
  • Place fuses and decoupling caps close to the actuator power input.
  • Keep high-voltage wiring segregated and in enclosures.
