Actuator definition
A device that converts electrical signals into motion, switching, or visible output.
Example: A smart lock: signal from the Arduino commands a servo to rotate to 90 degrees to unlock.
Contrast / misconception: The Arduino does not supply the motor's heavy current; it controls a driver that supplies power.
Design check: For every actuator list the control pin, driver module, and required external supply voltage and current.
Why this matters
Understanding the separation of control and power prevents damage and keeps devices predictable under load.
Common actuator types
DC motors
Rotational motion; needs an H-bridge for direction and PWM for speed.
Servo motors
Position control using timed PWM pulses; good for doors or valves.
Relays
Electromechanical switching of higher-voltage circuits while isolating the control side.
Displays
Character LCDs, OLEDs, and addressable LEDs provide local output and status indicators.
