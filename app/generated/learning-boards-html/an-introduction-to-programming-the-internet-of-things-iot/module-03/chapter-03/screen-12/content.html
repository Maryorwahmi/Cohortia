Comparison
USB / UART
Easy prototyping, two-way commands, works with pyserial. Best for initial builds and when cable length is short to moderate.
I2C
Low overhead for polling, Pi as master, Arduino as slave. Best inside the same enclosure or PCB; not for long distances.
Bluetooth (HC-05)
Wireless serial after pairing. Good when you must avoid cables, but introduces pairing and interference considerations.
Key Takeaway

Match transport to physical and latency constraints; design the same robust protocol on top regardless of link.

Misconception
Wireless is always better — not true. It increases setup steps and possible interference compared to wired links.
