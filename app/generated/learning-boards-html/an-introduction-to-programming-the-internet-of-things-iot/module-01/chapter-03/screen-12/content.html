Activity: View connected devices

Open your router admin panel and find the list of connected devices. Note IP, MAC, and connection type for each device, and mark which are IoT devices.

What to record
Device name, IP address, MAC address, Wi‑Fi or Ethernet.
What to draw
A simple topology with router as gateway and devices placed by connection type.
Example notes
Device: Smart Speaker IP: 192.168.1.23 MAC: 00:11:22:33:44:55 Connection: Wi‑Fi
Key Takeaway

This inspection reveals addressing and gateway constraints that inform protocol and topology choices for your devices.

Misconception to avoid
Device names may be generic; confirm identity by matching MAC addresses or unplugging devices to see which entry disappears.
