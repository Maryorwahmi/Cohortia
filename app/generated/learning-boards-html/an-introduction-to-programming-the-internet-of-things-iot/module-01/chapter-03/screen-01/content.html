Why this chapter

IoT projects fail when devices cannot address each other or when the wrong protocol wastes power. Learning these basics prevents common design mistakes and helps you pick efficient protocols for constrained hardware.

Key Takeaway

Networking decisions trade reliability, latency, and resource use. Choose addressing, transport, and topology to match constraints.
