Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
IP addressing
Private ranges and NAT let many devices share a public IP while keeping unique local identities.
TCP vs UDP
Use TCP for ordered, reliable delivery; use UDP for low-latency, low-overhead streaming when loss is acceptable.
Gateways & topologies
Gateways bridge local radios to the internet; topologies (star, mesh, point-to-point) trade simplicity for resilience and range.
Summary

These key concepts are the tools you will use to design reliable and efficient IoT networks. Refer back to them when you choose addresses, protocols, topologies, and gateway responsibilities.
