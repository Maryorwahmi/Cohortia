Scenario: 100 sensors across 10 acres

Sensors are too far for direct Wi‑Fi to the farmhouse. Select topology, protocol, and gateway placement and justify your choice.

Recommended topology
Mesh to relay across the farm and provide multiple paths around failures.
Recommended protocol
Use a low-power mesh radio such as ZigBee or LoRaWAN with a gateway to the farmhouse.
Key Takeaway
Match physical range and power constraints; gateways bridge local mesh to cloud backhauls.
Misconception
Choosing Wi‑Fi for convenience can fail when devices are out of range; mesh or long-range radios fit distributed deployments better.
