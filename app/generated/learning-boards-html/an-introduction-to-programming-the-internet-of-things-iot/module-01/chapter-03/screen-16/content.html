Addressing & DNS
IP, private ranges, and DNS resolution determine reachability to services.
Transport: TCP / UDP
Use TCP for reliability, UDP for low overhead and latency when loss is acceptable.
Topology & Gateway
Pick star, mesh, or point-to-point for range and resilience; use gateways to bridge to the cloud.
Final takeaway

Design choices should balance range, reliability, and device constraints. Test assumptions with simple inspections of IPs, MACs, and ports before committing to a topology or protocol.
