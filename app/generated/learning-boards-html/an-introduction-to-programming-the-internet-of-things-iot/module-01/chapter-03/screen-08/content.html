Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Network Topologies
Star
All devices connect to a central gateway. Simple management but single point of failure.
Common in Wi-Fi networks; ideal where a reliable gateway exists.
Mesh
Devices relay data peer-to-peer, creating multiple paths. Resilient to device failures but more complex and higher latency.
Used by ZigBee, Z-Wave, Thread for distributed sensor networks.
Point-to-Point
A direct link between two devices for simple data exchange. Used by Bluetooth and long-range links.
Good where only two endpoints need reliable communication.
Example decision
For 100 soil moisture sensors across a 10-acre farm, use a mesh topology with LoRaWAN or ZigBee and a gateway at the farmhouse to collect and forward data. Mesh extends reach and routes around failed nodes.
Key Takeaway
Match topology to coverage and reliability needs; expect trade-offs in latency and complexity.
Misconception
Mesh is not always lower-latency; extra hops can increase delay.
