Skills & Evidence

Each objective includes a short checklist you can complete to verify competency.

01 Inspect addresses
View connected devices, record IP and MAC, and note private vs public addressing ranges to prove understanding.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Identify transports
Observe whether a device uses a handshake and ordered delivery (TCP) or one-way datagrams (UDP).
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Map communication model
Draw a simple diagram showing requests and responses or publisher, broker, and subscribers for an example device.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Describe gateway role
Explain how a gateway translates local radio messages to TCP/IP and whether it filters or aggregates packets before forwarding.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
These skill checks are lightweight but concrete. If you complete them you can confidently choose protocols and topologies for small IoT deployments.
