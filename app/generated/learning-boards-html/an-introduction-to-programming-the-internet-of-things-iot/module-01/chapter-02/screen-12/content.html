Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Hands-on Activity
Activity instructions
For each given scenario, do the following: 1) State the top two constraints, 2) Choose processor class and wireless option, 3) Explain two trade-offs justifying the choice, and 4) Estimate impact on power, cost, or response time.
Scenario A: Wearable fitness tracker
Prioritize power and size. Choose a low-power SoC with BLE and deep-sleep to meet seven-day life.
Scenario B: Smart home gateway
Prioritize capability and scalability. Choose an SBC to run a local dashboard and bridge many ZigBee nodes.
Scenario C: Industrial solar monitor
Prioritize power and range. Choose an MCU with long-range low-power radio and plan for energy harvesting constraints.
Scenario D: Smart door lock
Prioritize response time and predictability. Use simple local processing and fast actuation; test end-to-end latency.
Assessment idea
Design a buried soil sensor requiring two-year battery life and 6-hour reporting. Choose a processor and wireless protocol, and explain why a Raspberry Pi is unsuitable due to power and cost.
Pro tip
Always estimate a daily energy budget and test with real hardware early to avoid late design surprises.
