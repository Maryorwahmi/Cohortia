Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Power vs Performance
Step-by-step tactics
  • Estimate active and sleep power.
  • Duty-cycle sensors and radios.
  • Use deep sleep and wake on event.
  • Batch transmissions and compress data.
  • Offload heavy processing to a gateway.
Concrete example: 7-day wearable
Reduce sampling rate during rest, summarize data hourly, use BLE for sync, and put main MCU in deep sleep between samples to meet a multi-day battery life.
Common-mistake
Assuming hardware sleep modes are automatic. Sleep behavior is implemented in firmware and must be validated with real measurements.
Key Takeaway

Quantify energy, reduce active duty, and validate with measurements. Firmware strategies are as important as hardware selection.
