Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Wired vs Wireless
Wired
  • Reliable and low-latency.
  • Lower exposure to RF interference.
  • Requires physical cabling and often mains power.
Use when uptime and latency are critical, or when devices are stationary with power available.
Wireless
  • Flexible installation and mobility.
  • Consumes more power and is subject to interference.
  • Range varies: Bluetooth short, Wi‑Fi medium, cellular and LoRa long.
Use when cabling is impractical or devices must be battery-operated or mobile.
Concrete example
Factory vibration sensor: choose wired Ethernet for reliable, continuous streaming. Soil sensors: choose low-power wireless with infrequent transmissions.
Common-mistake
Assuming wireless always lowers total cost. Consider battery size, replacement frequency, and radio costs before deciding.
