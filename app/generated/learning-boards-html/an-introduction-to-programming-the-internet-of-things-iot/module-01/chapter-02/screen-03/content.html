Learning Objectives — Applied Practice

Each objective becomes a real decision in a hands-on scenario or assessment.

Objective practice: Battery-powered sensor
Choose a low-power microcontroller and explain deep-sleep and infrequent transmit to meet a multi-month battery life. Milestone: recommend hardware and two power-saving tactics.
Objective practice: Home gateway
Select a single-board computer for dashboard and protocol bridging. Milestone: justify choice with capability vs unit-cost trade-offs.
Objective practice: Connectivity decision
Compare wired and wireless choices using range, interference, and power impact. Milestone: list when to pick wired vs wireless.
Objective practice: Response time
Measure segments: sensor read, processing, network, actuation. Milestone: propose optimizations to meet a 100 ms target.
