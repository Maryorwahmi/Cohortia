Trade-offs
Design decisions require explicit trade-offs; list and rank them early.
Response time
Measure sensor, processing, network, and actuation times to meet latency goals.
Design metrics
Turn needs into numbers: cost, power, size, reliability, security, scalability.
Final takeaway

Document requirements quantitatively, choose hardware that meets them, and validate with prototypes and measurements.
