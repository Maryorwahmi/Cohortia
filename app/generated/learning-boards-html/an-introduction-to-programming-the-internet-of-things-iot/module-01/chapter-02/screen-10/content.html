Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Design Metrics
Unit cost
Affects feasibility for large deployments. Set a maximum per device early.
Power consumption
Express as average energy per day or expected battery life for the chosen battery.
Size & weight
Critical for wearables and embedded installations.
Reliability
Use MTBF estimates and field test data when possible.
Security
Measure the device's ability to authenticate, encrypt, and receive secure updates.
Scalability
Can the design be deployed at scale without redesign? Consider network load and provisioning.
Key Takeaway

Convert qualitative needs into numeric targets for cost, power, reliability, security, and scalability to guide hardware and firmware choices.
