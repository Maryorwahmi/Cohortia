Five-step decision flow
  1. List non-negotiable requirements and metrics.
  2. Rank requirements by priority.
  3. Map processor and radio options to priorities.
  4. Choose tactics: sleep modes, batching, offload processing.
  5. Estimate metrics and validate choices.
Concrete scenario
Soil moisture sensor: requirement — two-year battery life, transmit every six hours. Prioritize power and size. Choose a low-power microcontroller, use deep-sleep between readings, and a low-duty-cycle long-range radio to minimize transmit energy.
Common-mistake
Optimizing for capability first leads to selecting processors that consume too much power. Always test the estimated daily energy budget before choosing an SBC for a battery design.
Key Takeaway

Follow a stepwise process: requirements → ranking → hardware mapping → tactics → validation. That converts trade-offs into engineering choices.
