What to Remember
Three compact reminders to carry forward.
Definition
Objects with sensors and connectivity that collect and exchange data to act or inform decisions.
Enablers
Cheap sensors, wireless, cloud, and open-source hardware lowered barriers to IoT projects.
Five-layer stack
Device → Edge/Gateway → Network → Cloud → Application is the checklist for design decisions.
Final prompt
Sketch one device you use and label its five layers. Which trade-off would you change? That single practice builds practical design judgment.
