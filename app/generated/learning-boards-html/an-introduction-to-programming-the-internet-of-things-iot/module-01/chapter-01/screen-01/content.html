What Is the Internet of Things?
Connecting physical objects to sense, exchange data, and act with cloud services and applications.
This chapter explains why IoT exists now, the layered architecture used to design systems, and examples across homes, healthcare, agriculture, industry, and cities. As you follow the lesson, notice how the same five-layer pattern repeats in very different devices. Keep one device in mind — a smart thermostat, fitness tracker, or smart light — and map parts of it to each layer as we progress.
