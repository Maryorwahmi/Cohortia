Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Device Layer
What lives on the device
Sensors, actuators and microcontrollers with choices around sampling, filtering, and power.
Constraints to consider
CPU, memory, battery life, temperature range, and physical durability shape design decisions.
Local processing
Smoothing, thresholding and encryption can run on the device to reduce traffic and improve privacy.
Concrete example — battery motion sensor
Sample motion briefly, wake the MCU, evaluate movement, send a short packet only on significant events, then sleep. This conserves power while still providing useful alerts.
Misconception
Assuming devices should stream all raw data. In many cases, summarizing or thresholding locally saves bandwidth and power without losing value.
Key Takeaway

Treat the device as a decision point: sample, filter, encrypt and transmit thoughtfully to meet constraints and use cases.
