Where IoT creates value
Applications turn sensor data into actionable insights, alerts, and automation across many domains.
Smart Home
Comfort, security and energy savings through coordinated devices and apps.
Healthcare
Wearables and remote monitoring with strict privacy and reliability needs.
Agriculture
Sensors and automation to optimize water and crop yields.
Industry
Predictive maintenance and asset tracking to reduce downtime.
Smart Cities
Traffic, air quality and waste sensors to improve urban services.
Exercise
Pick a device you own and draw the five-layer flow. Note one trade-off you would change and why.
Common constraint
Different domains prioritize latency, privacy, or reliability; choose architecture and processing location accordingly.
