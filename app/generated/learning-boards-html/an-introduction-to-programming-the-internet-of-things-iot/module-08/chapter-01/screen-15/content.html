Hardware Safety
Use proper drivers, separate power where needed, and respect relay ratings.
Security Basics
Use HTTPS, TLS, and SSH keys to protect local and cloud communications.
Testing & Documentation
Collect logs, screenshots, and a clear demo video showing both normal and fault recovery flows.
Final Takeaway

A well-documented, safely wired, and secure system with fault-tolerance evidence is the difference between a demo and a professional project.
