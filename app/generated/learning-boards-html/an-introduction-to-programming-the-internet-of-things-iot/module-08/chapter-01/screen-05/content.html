Wiring Plan (example)
  • DHT22 data → Arduino D2; Vcc 5V, GND common.
  • LDR voltage divider → Arduino A0 with documented resistor value.
  • Relay signal → Arduino D8; relay Vcc from 5V supply, common ground with Arduino.
  • Fan control → Raspberry Pi GPIO → transistor/motor driver → external 5–12V supply; include flyback diode and common ground.
Pro tip: use separate power supplies for motors to avoid injecting noise into the microcontroller power rails.
Testing & Safety Cards
Sensor Test: Read DHT22 values for 60 seconds and record plausible ranges before connecting to Pi.
Actuator Test: Use a multimeter to verify relay switching voltage and motor driver current without connecting mains loads.
Common Mistake: Directly driving motors from GPIO pins. This can damage the board; always use a driver or transistor with proper current handling.
