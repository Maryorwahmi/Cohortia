Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Smart Fridge

Requirements recap

  • Arduino reads temp (analog) and door (digital) every 5 seconds.
  • If door open > 30 seconds, Arduino turns on buzzer.
  • Arduino sends JSON over serial: {"temp":4.5,"door":"open"}.
  • Raspberry Pi stores readings in SQLite and uploads every 2 minutes.
  • Pi sends email alert if temp > 8°C for more than 5 minutes and runs a Flask dashboard.
Roles
Arduino: sensing, local alerts, and JSON serial output. Raspberry Pi: storage, uploads, alerting, and dashboard.
Key Takeaway

Partition time-critical control to the microcontroller and give the Pi responsibility for logging, uploads, and user-facing services.
