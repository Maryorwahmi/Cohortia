The sketch prints readings as fast as the CPU allows. Add a delay to control sampling and avoid flooding the serial channel.
Suggested fix
Add delay(1000); to loop or use millis-based timing to sample at a controlled rate.
Key Takeaway

Match sampling to sensor dynamics and avoid continuous tight loops that flood I/O channels.
