Why SQLite?

It runs in-process with your application and requires no server to manage.
SQL queries allow filtering and summarizing readings before upload.
Adding a sent flag supports batched uploads and retry logic on flaky networks.
Schema hint
Include timestamp, temp, door, and a sent integer defaulting to zero for upload tracking.
Key Takeaway

For prototype and small deployments, SQLite offers a low-friction, queryable store on the Pi.
