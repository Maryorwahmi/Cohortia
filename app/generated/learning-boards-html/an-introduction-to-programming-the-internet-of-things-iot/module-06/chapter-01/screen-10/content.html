Decision prompt
List your constraints: battery, range, latency, and message size. Eliminate protocols that cannot meet the strictest constraint.
Misconception
Low latency does not guarantee low energy. WebSockets may be low latency but not the best for coin-cell devices.
Key takeaway
Choose the protocol that aligns with the dominant constraint rather than personal preference.
