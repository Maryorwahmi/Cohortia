Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
CoAP
UDP-based, compact, HTTP-like protocol for constrained devices and LPWAN use cases.
WebSockets
Persistent, full-duplex TCP channel for realtime updates and direct device control.
Bluetooth Low Energy
Short-range, very-low-power radio using GATT for services and characteristics in wearables and beacons.
Key Takeaway

Match protocol transport and messaging model to device constraints: bytes, battery, latency, and range determine the best fit.
