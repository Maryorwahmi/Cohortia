Scenario

Design a fitness tracker that lasts six months on a coin-cell battery and sends data to a smartphone. Which protocol do you choose?

Recommended protocol: Bluetooth Low Energy
Reason: BLE optimizes for ultra-low power radio use and supports GATT services and characteristics suitable for heart rate and activity data. It lets the wearable advertise and keep connections brief to conserve battery.
Contrast
WebSockets and CoAP are not ideal for direct phone connections from coin-cell wearables because they require heavier stacks or radio profiles that consume more energy.
Extension
If cloud upload is needed, have the phone act as a gateway that relays BLE data to the cloud using HTTP or WebSockets.
Reflection question
What would change if the tracker needed to send data directly to a remote server without a phone present?
