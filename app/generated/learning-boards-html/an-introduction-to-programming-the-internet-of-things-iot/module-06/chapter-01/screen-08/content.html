Peripheral vs Central

A peripheral advertises and waits for connections. A central scans and initiates connections. Smartphones often act as centrals when reading wearable data.

GATT structure
  • Service: a collection of related characteristics
  • Characteristic: holds a single data value
  • Descriptor: metadata describing a characteristic
Practical notes

BLE excels when devices need multi-month battery life on a small battery. Use it for heart rate monitors, beacons, and proximity sensors. Treat BLE as a local link that a phone or gateway bridges to cloud services.

Common misconception
Low energy does not mean high range. BLE trades off range for power efficiency and is best used for short distances.
