Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Protocol Selection
Four-question flow
1. Battery or memory constrained?
Prefer CoAP for IP-constrained devices or BLE for local wearables.
2. Need publish-subscribe distribution?
MQTT fits brokered pub-sub for many subscribers.
3. Require realtime two-way control?
Choose WebSockets for persistent bidirectional channels.
4. Is range limited and local?
Use BLE for phone-to-device links and short-range sensors.
Practical test
Prototype a single use case and measure actual packet sizes, latency, and power draw to validate your theoretical choice.
