Process Flow
Smart garden: an end-to-end design walkthrough
Concrete mapping of sensors to dashboards with buffering and retries.
Sensors
Soil analog, DHT22 digital, light analog. Sample every 10s, average 5 samples.
Edge
Arduino formats CSV and sends serial every 30s.
Gateway & Cloud
Pi parses, timestamps, writes SQLite; uploader batches to ThingSpeak every 2min; Flask shows local dashboard.
Why this design works
Local buffering handles intermittent Wi‑Fi. Batching reduces cloud calls. Local dashboard ensures visibility even when cloud is down.
