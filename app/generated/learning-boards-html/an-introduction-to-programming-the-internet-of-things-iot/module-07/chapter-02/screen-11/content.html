Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Deployment & Maintenance

Deployment

Enclosures, network configuration, installation and documentation are essential.

Physical protection
Select housings and mounting that protect against environment and tampering.
Network & security
Configure secure connectivity, authentication, and encryption for device communications.

Maintenance

Monitor devices, plan secure firmware updates, and document procedures for replacement and rollback.

Key Takeaway

Design update paths and monitoring from day one to keep systems secure and resilient.

Common Mistake
Failing to plan for remote updates leads to expensive field visits and higher downtime.
