Cloud vs Edge vs Hybrid

A direct comparison helps choose the right pattern for your requirements.

Cloud-only
Simple, centralized analytics, but high latency, high bandwidth, and reliance on connectivity.
Best for non-time-sensitive analytics.
Edge-only
Local autonomy and privacy with minimal cloud interaction, but limited processing and remote access.
Best for isolated or security-critical deployments.
Hybrid
Time-critical processing at the edge; long-term analytics and remote access in the cloud. Most flexible.
Commonly preferred for production systems.
Key Takeaway

Choose architecture based on latency demands, privacy needs, and cost constraints; hybrid often balances these factors.
