Signed updates and update reliability
Follow a simple process: sign, distribute, verify, apply, and roll back as needed.
1. Sign firmware
Manufacturer signs the firmware image with a private key retained in a secure build environment.
2. Distribute via secure channel
Deliver images from trusted servers over TLS to avoid tampering in transit.
3. Verify on device
Device checks the signature against a stored public key and verifies integrity before flashing.
4. Apply and rollback safeguards
Support rollback and validate updates under power/network failure scenarios.
Practical tip

Prefer signed updates and keep the signing key in a protected environment. For critical devices, use hardware-backed signing and HSMs to guard keys.

Concrete example
An OTA update server signs firmware images. Devices download the image over TLS and verify the signature before writing flash. A verified rollback partition keeps a previous working image in case of failure.
Misconception
Assuming updates are optional. Insecure devices that cannot be patched remain a continuing risk for users and the wider internet.
