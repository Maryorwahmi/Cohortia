IoT Security: Threats, Encryption, Best Practices
Understand the risks and practical defenses for connected devices used in homes, industry, and health.
Why this matters
Compromised devices can cause physical harm, data theft, and large-scale internet attacks.
What you will do
Identify threats, apply TLS and certificates, secure firmware updates, and reduce attack surface for devices.

Threats
Eavesdropping, MitM, firmware extraction, DoS, replay.
Encryption
TLS, AES, and certificates to protect data and identity.
Best practices
Strong passwords, signed updates, HSMs, minimize services.
