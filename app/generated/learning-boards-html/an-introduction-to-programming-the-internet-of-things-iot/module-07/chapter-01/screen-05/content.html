Threats and brief mitigations
Review each threat and a practical mitigation you can apply.
Eavesdropping
Intercepting unencrypted traffic. Mitigation: enable TLS or use encrypted tunnels.
Man-in-the-Middle
Altering messages in transit. Mitigation: certificate validation, certificate pinning if required.
Firmware Extraction
Reading flash to recover secrets. Mitigation: remove hardcoded keys, use secure key storage or HSM.
Denial of Service
Flooding device or network. Mitigation: rate limiting and DDoS protection at network edge.
Replay Attacks
Reusing valid messages later. Mitigation: include nonces or timestamps and verify freshness.
Credential Stuffing
Reusing leaked credentials. Mitigation: enforce unique passwords and rate-limit login attempts.
Key Takeaway

Map threats to realistic scenarios on your device and pick the smallest set of mitigations that materially reduce risk.

Concrete example
If an IP camera sends snapshots over HTTP, an attacker on the same network can eavesdrop and stream the camera feed. Enabling HTTPS or a VPN prevents that exposure.
Misconception
Assuming that low-value devices won't be attacked. In reality attackers build botnets from many low-cost devices to amplify attacks.
