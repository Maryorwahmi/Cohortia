Protecting secrets from physical attack
Practical steps to reduce the risk of firmware extraction and key theft.
Remove debug interfaces
Disable or physically remove JTAG and serial consoles in production units.
Avoid hardcoded secrets
Provision unique credentials at manufacture or first boot instead of embedding keys in firmware.
Use an HSM
Store keys in tamper-resistant hardware and perform cryptographic operations inside the module.
Supply chain
Verify components and protect build and signing environments to prevent counterfeit or tampered devices entering production.
Concrete example
A device uses an HSM to store its device certificate private key so even if flash is read, the private key cannot be extracted and used elsewhere.
Key Takeaway

Plan for physical protections and supply chain verification when device secrets matter to safety or service integrity.
