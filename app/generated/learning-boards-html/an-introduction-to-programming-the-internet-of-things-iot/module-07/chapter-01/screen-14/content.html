Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Key Concepts
Threats
Eavesdropping, MitM, firmware extraction, DoS, replay, credential stuffing.
Principles
Confidentiality, integrity, availability, authentication, authorization.
Best practices
Strong passwords, TLS, certificate pinning, signed updates, HSMs, minimize services.
Takeaway

Design security around device constraints and build simple, verifiable controls: encryption, validation, updateability, and minimized attack surface.
