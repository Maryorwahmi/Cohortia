Learning Objectives
Four competency milestones to guide study and practice.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify threats
Recognize eavesdropping, MitM, firmware extraction, DoS and replay attacks and why each matters to device risk.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand encryption
Explain TLS, AES, and public key cryptography and when to use each on constrained devices.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply best practices
Use strong passwords, TLS, certificate pinning, signed firmware updates and reduce exposed services on devices.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Explain IoT constraints
Describe how limited resources, physical access, heterogeneity and long lifespans change threat models and defenses.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
