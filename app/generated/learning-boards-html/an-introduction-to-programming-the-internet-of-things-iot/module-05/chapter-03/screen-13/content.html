Module 6 › Chapter 5.3 › Platform selection
Cost & Ease
Free maker tiers lower up-front cost but impose rate and retention limits. Commercial platforms cost more but include management and integrations.
Scalability
Large fleets require device registries, certificate rotation, and routing to storage and analytics — features in AWS and Azure.
Privacy & Control
Self-hosting with Mosquitto, InfluxDB, and Grafana gives full data control but increases maintenance responsibility.
Assessment prompt

You are building a commercial product shipping 10,000 units sending data every minute. Which platform do you choose and why? Consider device management, security, and integration when you answer.
