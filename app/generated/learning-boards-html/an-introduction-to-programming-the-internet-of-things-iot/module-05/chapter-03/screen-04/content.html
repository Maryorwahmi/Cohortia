Module 6 › Chapter 5.3 › What is an IoT cloud platform?
Definition & analogy

An IoT cloud platform ingests telemetry from devices, indexes it by time, stores it, and exposes analytics, dashboards, and automation rules. Imagine a postal hub that receives packages, sorts them, and hands them to services that act on their contents.

Core roles
  • Device connectivity and authentication
  • Time-series storage and indexing
  • Visualization and rule-based automation
Common misconception
Not all platforms are interchangeable: APIs, limits, and device management differ and affect long-term operations.
Concrete example
A temperature sensor publishes a value every minute. The platform timestamps the value, stores it as a series, draws a live chart, and triggers an email when the value exceeds 30°C. That sequence converts raw telemetry into an actionable alert.
Key Takeaway

Pick a platform based on who manages devices, how data is routed, and whether built-in visualization meets your needs.
