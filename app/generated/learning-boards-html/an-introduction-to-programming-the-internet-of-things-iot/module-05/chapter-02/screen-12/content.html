Module 05 › Chapter 5.2 › Pi as Gateway
Gateway responsibilities
  • Subscribe to local sensors and devices
  • Buffer and preprocess data (aggregation, filtering)
  • Publish to cloud brokers or databases
Example: collect ten rapid readings, compute an average, and publish the average with QoS 1 to reduce cloud traffic.
Design considerations
Avoid making the gateway the only single point of failure; plan for persistence or reconnection strategies.
Use local retained messages and LWT to inform local systems of device state during connectivity loss.
Misconception: Gateways add unavoidable latency. In reality, they let you reduce cloud traffic and perform time-sensitive local decisions when needed.
