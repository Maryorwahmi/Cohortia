Module 05 › Chapter 5.2 › QoS Levels
QoS at a glance
QoS 0
At most once. Fast, may be lost.
QoS 1
At least once. Acknowledged, duplicates possible.
QoS 2
Exactly once. Four-step handshake.
Examples & guidance
Use QoS 0 for high-frequency telemetry like temperature where missing a reading is acceptable. Use QoS 1 for important sensor events where loss is unacceptable but duplicates can be deduplicated. Use QoS 2 only for commands that must not be lost or duplicated, such as critical actuator instructions.
Concrete example
Send 'FIRE DETECTED' with QoS 2 to guarantee a single delivery to the monitoring station.
Misconception
Higher QoS always means better system behavior. It increases latency and bandwidth; choose based on requirement.
When in doubt, design subscribers to tolerate duplicates and use QoS 1 unless exact-once is mandatory.
