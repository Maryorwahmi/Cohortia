Activity overview
Read TMP36 on A0, LDR divider on A1, pushbutton on pin 2. Apply a 10-sample average to temperature and light, convert and calibrate, then print CSV every second with temperature, light%, button.
Verification
  • Compare TMP36 output to a room thermometer.
  • Cover the LDR and observe light percentage change.
  • Press the button and confirm Serial shows the state change.
