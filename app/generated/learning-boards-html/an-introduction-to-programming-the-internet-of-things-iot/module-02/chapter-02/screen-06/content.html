Sensor cards
PIR motion: HIGH on motion, needs warm-up; good for occupancy detection.
Hall effect: solid-state magnetic sensor; reliable for repeated triggers.
Tilt sensor: mechanical switch changes with angle; simple orientation detection.
Reed switch: magnet-activated contact; common in door sensors and low-power designs.
Wiring and caveats
  • Confirm whether the sensor needs pull-up or pull-down. Not all outputs are the same type.
  • Watch for noise and false triggers from environmental changes, especially on PIR sensors.
  • Mechanical sensors may wear; solid-state sensors last longer for frequent events.
Example exercise
Connect a PIR, observe the HIGH pulse when walking by, and adjust sensitivity to learn false positive behavior.
