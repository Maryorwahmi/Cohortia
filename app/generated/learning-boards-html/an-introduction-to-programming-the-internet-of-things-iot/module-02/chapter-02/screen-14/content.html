Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Digital sensors
Use pull-ups and debounce so binary signals are stable and reliable for event detection.
Analog conversion
Convert analogRead counts to voltage, then apply sensor formulas or calibration to get physical units.
Filtering & hysteresis
Reduce noise with averaging, median, or EMA, and use hysteresis to avoid rapid on/off when detecting thresholds.
Summary

Accurate sensor readings come from correct wiring, proper conversion and calibration, and thoughtful noise management—together they make sensor-driven decisions dependable.
