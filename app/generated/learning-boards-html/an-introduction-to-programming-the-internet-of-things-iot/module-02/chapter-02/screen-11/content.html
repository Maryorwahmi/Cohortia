Averaging
Take N samples, sum them, divide by N. Reduces random noise by sqrt(N) but increases latency.
Median filter example
Samples: [102, 105, 300, 107, 103] → sorted [102,103,105,107,300] → median 105. Outliers are ignored.
Exponential moving average
filtered = alpha * current + (1 - alpha) * filtered. Low memory, tunable response. Use alpha between 0 and 1.
Pitfall: too large a smoothing factor delays real changes. Pick alpha to balance noise reduction and responsiveness.
Key Takeaway

Match your filter to the noise type: use median for spikes, averaging for random jitter, and EMA for efficient smoothing with tunable speed.
