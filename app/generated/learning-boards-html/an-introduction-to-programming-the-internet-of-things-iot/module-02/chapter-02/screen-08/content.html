Conversion formula
// Convert analog reading to voltage and degrees C
int raw = analogRead(A0);
float voltage = (raw / 1023.0) * 5.0;
float temperatureC = (voltage - 0.5) * 100.0;
Example: with voltage 0.73, temperature = (0.73 - 0.5) * 100 = 23°C. Remember the 0.5V offset is specific to TMP36.
Practice tip
Take several readings and average them before conversion to reduce random jitter. Compare the final value to a known thermometer for calibration.
Misconception
Do not forget to convert counts to voltage first. Directly scaling counts without accounting for Vref and offset yields wrong temperatures.
