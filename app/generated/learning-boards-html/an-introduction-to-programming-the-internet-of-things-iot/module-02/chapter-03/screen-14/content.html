Summary Key Concepts
I2C
Two wires, addresses, pull‑ups required for shared sensor networks.
SPI
MOSI/MISO/SCK + CS per device; choose for speed and bandwidth.
Modules & libraries
Libraries speed development; inspect raw registers if behavior is unexpected.
Takeaway

Use the right bus for your devices, rely on libraries for convenience, and keep bus‑level debugging skills to resolve conflicts and electrical issues.
