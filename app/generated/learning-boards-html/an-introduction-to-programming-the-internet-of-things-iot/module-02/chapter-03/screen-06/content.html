Definition Common I2C sensors
BME280
Temperature, humidity, pressure. Good for weather stations; commonly at address 0x76.
DHT22 / AM2302
Simpler temperature and humidity sensor. Easier to read but less precise than BME280.
MPU6050
6‑axis IMU: accelerometer & gyroscope for motion and orientation sensing.
BH1750
Digital light sensor that measures lux for ambient light detection.
SSD1306
OLED display driver accessible over I2C for quick visual feedback of sensor readings.
Practical tip
Confirm all modules use compatible voltage (often 3.3V). If a device expects 3.3V and you supply 5V, protect it with a level shifter.
