What to Remember Quick Reference
Power & ground
Confirm module voltage (3.3V vs 5V) and tie grounds together before testing communication.
I2C scan
Run a scanner to list addresses after wiring; catch duplicates early and document addresses.
Pull‑ups
Use external 4.7k pull‑ups on SDA and SCL when wires are long or many devices are present.
Final takeaway

Small wiring and address checks prevent most sensor communication problems and make your IoT projects more reliable.
