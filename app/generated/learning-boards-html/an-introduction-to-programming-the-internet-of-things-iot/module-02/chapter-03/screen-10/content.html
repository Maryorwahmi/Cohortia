Comparison I2C vs SPI

I2C

  • 2 wires (SDA, SCL), many devices via addresses.
  • Lower pin usage, good for many sensors.
  • Requires pull‑ups and careful address management.

SPI

  • 4 wires (MOSI, MISO, SCK, CS per device).
  • Faster and full‑duplex, better for SD cards and displays.
  • Needs extra CS pins and careful CS control to avoid collisions.
Decision rule: list your peripherals, note their bus and data needs, then match by pin budget and throughput. If you need many small sensors choose I2C; if you need streaming or large transfers choose SPI.
