Module 5 › Chapter 4.3 › Lab Walkthrough
Step sequence
  1. Create iot_data.db and sensor_readings(timestamp, sensor_id, value, sent).
  2. Implement a read loop to insert readings every 10 seconds.
  3. Implement process_unsent to POST unsent rows and update sent on success.
  4. Start Flask to serve a dashboard and /api/data for charting.
Simulation
Disconnect the Pi from the internet, let it collect data, then reconnect and observe buffered uploads and sent flag updates.
Concrete checks
  • Confirm new rows appear in sqlite3 after each sample.
  • While offline, confirm rows accumulate with sent = 0.
  • On reconnect, confirm rows become sent = 1 and the cloud receives them.
  • Access the Flask dashboard from another device and view recent readings.
Key Tip

Prune or archive old rows after successful upload to keep the local database small and performant on the Pi.
