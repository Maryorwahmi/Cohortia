3.3V GPIO
GPIO pins are digital only and use 3.3 volts. Exceeding this can damage the Pi.
gpiozero
High-level Python library that reduces boilerplate for common devices like LEDs and buttons.
MCP3008 ADC
An 8-channel 10-bit ADC used over SPI to read analog sensors on the Pi.
Summary

Keep voltage limits and ADC requirements in mind, and choose the right library for your development speed or control needs.
