Lab Walkthrough
Step 1 — LED
Wire LED to GPIO 17 with a 330Ω resistor and test a blink script.
Step 2 — Button
Wire button to GPIO 18 with 10kΩ pull-up and verify callback prints a timestamp.
Step 3 — ADC
Connect MCP3008 to SPI, wire pot to channel 0, and log scaled voltage readings.
Integration
Read ADC continuously; if value > 0.5 then led.on() else led.off(). On button press, append timestamp and value to CSV and optionally capture an image.
Safety reminder
Verify all wiring and voltage levels before powering. Never feed 5V signals to GPIO pins; use level shifters when necessary.
Key Takeaway
Integrate incrementally: test LED, button, ADC, then combine with logging and camera capture for a robust IoT demo.
