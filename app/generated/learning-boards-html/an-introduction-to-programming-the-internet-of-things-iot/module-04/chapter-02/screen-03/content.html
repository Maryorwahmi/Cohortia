Checklist
  • LED on GPIO 17 with 330Ω resistor — blink test
  • Button on GPIO 18 — callback prints timestamp
  • MCP3008 channel 0 reads potentiometer — value scaled to 0–3.3V
  • Camera captures a still image at default resolution
Quick tips
Power the Pi only after double-checking the wiring. Test components one at a time. Use pull-up or pull-down resistors for clean button readings.
Common Mistake
Connecting 5V signals to GPIO. Always verify voltage levels and use level shifters when needed.
Outcome
A reproducible lab where software reacts to analog and digital inputs and saves results including captured images.
