Actors, Components, and the World Outliner
How the Actor root, modular Components, and the World Outliner work together to build and manage your level.
In this lesson
Define Actors and Components, explore common Component types, learn the Actor-Component hierarchy, and practice using the World Outliner to organize a scene.
Why it matters
Understanding this model is foundational. It keeps scenes modular, reusable, and easier to debug as your project scales.

Key Takeaway

Actors are the top-level entities in a level. Attach Components to add visuals, collision, lights, and behaviors. Use the World Outliner to keep everything findable and organized.
