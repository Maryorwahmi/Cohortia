Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › What is an Actor?
Definition
Actor: any placeable or spawnable object

Every Actor has a transform: location, rotation, and scale. The Actor is the identity you move and reference in Blueprints.

Examples
  • Player character Actor with movement and mesh components.
  • Static prop Actor like a chair with a StaticMeshComponent.
  • Light Actor that provides scene lighting.
Misconception
Components cannot exist independently in the level; they attach to an Actor.

Key Takeaway

Treat the Actor as the container and handle in the level. Use Components to add what the Actor does or shows.
