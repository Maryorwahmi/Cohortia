Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Organizing a Level
Folder Strategy
Create top-level folders like Props, Lights, Characters. Use subfolders for scenes or levels. Move Actors into folders right after placement to avoid clutter.
Naming
Adopt predictable prefixes like PL_ for PointLight, BP_ for Blueprints, or Prop_Chair_01 to make searching reliable.
Locking
Lock Actors to prevent accidental edits. Lock selectively — don't lock everything. If you must lock many Actors, consider grouping them and locking the group instead.
Misconception
Organization is optional early in development. In reality, early habits prevent technical debt as the level scales.
Key Takeaway

A consistent folder and naming strategy makes searching and bulk editing fast. Organize as you build to avoid future overhead.
