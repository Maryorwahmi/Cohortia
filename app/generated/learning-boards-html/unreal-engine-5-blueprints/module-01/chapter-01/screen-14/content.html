Practice routine
Run this 6-step routine until it feels natural.
  1. Create a project from Third Person and make top-level folders.
  2. Drag an asset from Content into the Viewport.
  3. Use RMB+W/Alt combos to position the camera.
  4. Select the asset in Outliner and press F to frame it.
  5. Use W/E/R and Details numeric edits to finalize placement.
  6. Save All and save your editor layout.
Why it matters
A concise routine prevents lost time and builds the foundation for fast iteration.
Try this
Repeat the loop with three different assets to generalize skills across types.
Challenge
Frame PlayerStart, place a chair beside it, and save your scene under a descriptive name.
