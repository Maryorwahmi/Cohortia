Visual mnemonics
Use the workshop metaphor to remember roles: bench (Viewport), parts rack (Outliner), dial panel (Details), and supply cabinet (Content Browser). That association helps when you feel lost in the interface.
Practice tip
After creating a project, immediately locate a known Actor in the Outliner and press F to frame it. This builds muscle memory for navigation.
Common mistake
Saving projects with vague names or placing assets in the root Content folder will cost time later. Use clear names and folder structure.
