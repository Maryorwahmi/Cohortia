Practical checkpoints
Small tasks that confirm a working setup and good habits.
  • •Create a Third Person project named MyFirstUE5Project in a known folder.
  • •Locate and frame an Actor via the World Outliner and the F key.
  • •Drag SM_Chair from StarterContent into the Viewport and place it.
  • •Use W, E, R keys to move, rotate, and scale, and save your level.
Why organization matters
Organized projects are faster to navigate, easier for teams to understand, and prevent duplicated or misplaced assets that slow development.
Folder suggestion
Create folders like Blueprints, Meshes, Materials, and Audio immediately after project creation.
Pro tip
Save a custom layout once you prefer where panels dock. It restores your workflow across sessions.
