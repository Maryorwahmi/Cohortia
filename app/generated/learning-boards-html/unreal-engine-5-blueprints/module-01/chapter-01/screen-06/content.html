Camera Controls
Practice these combinations until they feel natural; they are how you traverse levels quickly.
Hold Right Mouse Button + WASD: fly camera forward/back/strafe.
Q/E move camera up and down.
Left Mouse Button drag: rotate or orbit around focus depending on mode.
Select an Actor and press F: frame selected to reset the camera focus.
Concrete example
After dragging SM_Chair into the scene, press F with the chair selected to center it in the Viewport for precision adjustment.
Common mistake and fix
Beginners often try to find objects by panning alone. Instead, search in the World Outliner, select the Actor, and press F to frame it instantly. This saves time in large levels.
Practice drill
1. Place a mesh in the Viewport.
2. Hold RMB and press W to approach it.
3. Select it in the Outliner and press F to frame.
4. Use W/E/R to move, rotate, and scale for fine-tuning.
Key Takeaway

Learn a consistent camera sequence: approach, select, frame, and adjust. It prevents disorientation and speeds editing.
