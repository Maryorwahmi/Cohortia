Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Content Browser
Purpose
An asset hub where you import, organize, and drag assets into levels.
Drag and drop assets from folders into the Viewport to place instances in the level.
Use filters to narrow results to Blueprints, Meshes, or Materials.
Maintain folder structure like Blueprints, Meshes, Materials, Audio for clarity.
Example
Navigate to Content/StarterContent/Props and drag SM_Chair into the level to create a placed instance.
Common mistake
Placing all assets in root Content makes later discovery hard. Create folders early and move assets into them immediately after import.
Pro tip
Adopt a naming convention and folder layout up front to reduce confusion later when collaborating.
Key Takeaway

Treat the Content Browser as the single source of truth for assets and maintain a clear folder structure from day one.
