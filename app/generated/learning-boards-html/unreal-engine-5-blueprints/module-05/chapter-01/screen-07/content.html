Process Flow

Anchor, Offset, Test

Step 1 — Anchor
Select your widget and pick an anchor preset (top-right for a score HUD).
Step 2 — Offset
Adjust Position X/Y to fine-tune distance from the anchor point; negative X values often nudge left from the right anchor.
Step 3 — Test
Preview the widget at multiple aspect ratios in Designer or PIE to confirm consistency.

Common mistake

Using absolute pixel coordinates without anchors causes misalignment across resolutions; anchors plus offsets is the correct pattern.
