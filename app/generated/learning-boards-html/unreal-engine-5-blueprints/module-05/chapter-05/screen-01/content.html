AI Tasks and Blackboard

Make patrol AI responsive by using a shared Blackboard and custom Behavior Tree tasks authored in Blueprint.

In this lesson
Create BB_EnemyAI and keys; build a custom FindPlayer task; wire a chase selector.
Key artifact
BTT_FindPlayer (BTTask_BlueprintBase) that sets TargetActor and TargetLocation.
Outcome
AI that falls back to patrol but prioritizes chasing when target is set.
Why this matters

Using a Blackboard decouples detection, planning, and actions so systems can share facts without hard wiring. It makes AI modular and easier to debug.
