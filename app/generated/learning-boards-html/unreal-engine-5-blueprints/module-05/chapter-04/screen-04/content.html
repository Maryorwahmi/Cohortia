Definition

AI Controller vs AI Pawn

Controller: invisible logic that runs Behavior Trees. Pawn: visible actor with mesh and movement. Keep them separate for modularity.

Checks after setup
  • AI Controller Class assigned on the Pawn.
  • Pawn has Character Movement or compatible component.
  • Controller runs Run Behavior Tree in BeginPlay.
Concrete example
Use BP_PatrolController for multiple BP_PatrolBot Pawns with different meshes; Controller logic remains unchanged while visuals differ.
Key Takeaway

Design controllers for behavior, Pawns for presentation. This reduces duplication and simplifies swapping visuals.

Analogy
Think of the Controller as a director and the Pawn as an actor. The director calls actions and the actor performs them on stage. Changing the actor doesn't change the script.
Common mistake
Attaching behavior to the Pawn duplicates logic and makes it harder to reuse controllers across different Pawn types. Prefer central controllers for shared AI patterns.
Why it matters
Modularity enables rapid iteration: change visuals or tweak controller logic independently, and debug the brain without touching the mesh.
