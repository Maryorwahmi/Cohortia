Definition

Behavior Trees explained

Hierarchical nodes evaluated repeatedly. Use composites to control flow, tasks to act, and decorators as guards.

Composites
Selector acts like OR: stops when a child succeeds. Sequence acts like AND: fails if any child fails.
Tasks & Decorators
Tasks perform actions such as Move To and Wait. Decorators check conditions and prevent execution when false.
Concrete example
Create a Selector with children: chase player, flee when low health, otherwise patrol. The Selector tries chase first, then flee, then patrol — giving natural priority handling.
Misconception check
Behavior Trees are not a one-size-fits-all magic; they require thoughtful node organization and good blackboard data. But they scale better than ad-hoc state spaghetti in most gameplay cases.
Key Takeaway

Use Behavior Trees to express priorities and ordered steps; they make complex AI more readable and debuggable.
