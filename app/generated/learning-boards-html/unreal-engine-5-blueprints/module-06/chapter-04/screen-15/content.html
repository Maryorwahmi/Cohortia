Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Final Summary
Ignore generated files
Use .gitignore to keep the repo small and avoid unnecessary conflicts.
Commit small & often
Focused commits make history readable and make rollbacks safe.
Coordinate binary edits
Use locking or team coordination; binaries don’t merge automatically.
Final takeaway
These three practices give you a reliable, collaborative workflow for Unreal Engine projects. They reduce surprises and keep your repository healthy as the project grows.
