Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Cleaning Before Archive
Step-by-step
  1. Confirm your packaged build runs and default map loads.
  2. Close Unreal Editor to avoid locked files.
  3. Make a staging copy of the project folder.
  4. Delete: Binaries, Build, DerivedDataCache, Intermediate, Saved.
  5. Compress the cleaned folder to .zip or .7z with date-stamped name.
Common mistake

Deleting files while the editor is open can corrupt caches or leave partial deletes. Always close the editor first.

Paths you can safely remove
ProjectRoot/ Binaries/
ProjectRoot/ Build/
ProjectRoot/ DerivedDataCache/
ProjectRoot/ Intermediate/
ProjectRoot/ Saved/
These are regenerable by the editor and reduce archive size dramatically.
Final steps
  • Compress and verify archive checksum.
  • Store at least one copy offsite or on an external drive.
  • Record name, date, engine version, and included platform builds.
