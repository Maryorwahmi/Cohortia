Label prints
Include event and variable names so printed messages are actionable during playtests.
Focus breakpoints
A single well-placed breakpoint beats many scattered ones for clear investigation.
Watch selectively
Monitor only variables that are critical to your hypothesis to keep focus during stepping.
Final thought

Debugging becomes fast when you form small experiments, observe with the right tools, and change only one variable per test.
