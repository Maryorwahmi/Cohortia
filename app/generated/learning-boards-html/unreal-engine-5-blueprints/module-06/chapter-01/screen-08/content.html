Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Watch Window
What to watch
  • Variables that should change during the sequence, like Score or Health.
  • Input and output pins on nodes in the current scope.
  • Objects and their properties when an instance-specific issue appears.
How to add
Right-click a variable in My Blueprint and choose Add to Watch. While paused, add pins by right-clicking them on the node and choosing Watch this value. Use the Debug Object dropdown to pick the correct instance.
Scenario
Score goes to -1 after firing. Add PlayerAmmo or Score to Watch and step through decrement logic to find missing conditional checks.
Tip
Focus your Watch list to 3–5 variables for the current hypothesis to keep your attention on the most relevant data.
