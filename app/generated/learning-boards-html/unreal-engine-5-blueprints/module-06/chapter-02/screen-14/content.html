What to Remember
Performance & FPS
Frame rate is the visible symptom; use stat unit to find which subsystem is the cause.
Event Tick
Tick is per-frame; avoid heavy per-frame operations across many actors.
Timers & Events
Use Set Timer by Event or event-driven updates instead of Tick for periodic or conditional work.
Summary
Profile to form a hypothesis, apply a targeted Blueprint or rendering fix, then re-profile to verify a measurable improvement.
