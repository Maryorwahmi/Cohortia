BeginPlay Pre-population
Spawn N projectiles, hide and deactivate them, and store references in ProjectilePool Array. This moves allocation cost to load time.
Design choices
  • Fixed pool: predictable memory; no expansion.
  • Expandable pool: avoids misses but may allocate later.
  • Pool ownership: GameMode, Manager actor, or subsystem.
Test by firing many shots and comparing profiler timelines between spawn/destroy and pooling.
