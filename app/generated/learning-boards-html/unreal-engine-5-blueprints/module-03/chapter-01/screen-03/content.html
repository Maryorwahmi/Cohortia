Verification Steps
  • Run BeginPlay with Print String to confirm single firing on spawn or level start.
  • Add Tick with Delta Seconds printed to observe per-frame updates, then disable to compare.
  • Create a Custom Event and call it from at least two places to show reuse.
  • Bind a handler to an Event Dispatcher at BeginPlay and toggle the broadcaster to observe the listener reacting.
Common Misconceptions
Tick is always necessary. No — prefer targeted events or timers when possible.
Dispatchers remove the need for references. They decouple behavior but listeners still need a way to bind to the dispatcher.
