Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Practical Rules
Array Safety
Do not remove items from an array while iterating with For Each; collect and remove after Completed.
While Guards
Always ensure a While loop has logic that will make its condition false to avoid infinite loops.
Sequence & Debug
Use Sequence to order independent tasks and Print Strings / breakpoints to trace flows and inspect variables.
Final Takeaway

Apply the right flow node to match your data and control type, test with prints and breakpoints, and design loops with termination in mind to keep your game stable.
