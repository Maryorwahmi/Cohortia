Final Notes › Three Reminders
Design Signatures
Decide inputs like Interactor early so implementations have the needed context.
Guard & Call
Use Does Implement Interface then OnInteract (Message) to safely dispatch behavior.
Pick the Right Tool
Casting for specific access, dispatchers for broadcasts, interfaces for shared targeted commands.
Final Takeaway
Interfaces help you decouple systems and add new interactables without changing caller code—design them deliberately and test implementations thoroughly.
