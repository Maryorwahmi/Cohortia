Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Tick & Performance
Analogy
Many actors polling every frame is like hundreds of cooks checking a stove every second — too expensive at scale.
  • Profile how many actors use Tick and what they do each frame.
  • Replace polling with events or timers when possible.
  • Use the engine profiler or simple Print String checks while developing.
Quick Checklist
Are you doing heavy work in Tick? Can it be evented? Is it per-frame necessary? If not, change it.
Key Takeaway

Move periodic or change-driven work off Tick to keep performance predictable and scalable.
