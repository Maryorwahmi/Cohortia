Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Event Tick
What Tick does
Executes every frame; use it for smooth continuous updates but avoid heavy computations inside it.
  • Good for per-frame interpolation, procedural animation, or reading continuous axis input.
  • Bad for expensive loops, per-actor heavy checks, or logic that can be event-driven.
Alternatives
Use Timers, Timelines, delegates, or physics systems instead of Tick when possible.
Performance Warning

Putting many heavy operations in Tick across many actors reduces frame rate. Profile and replace with event-driven triggers when appropriate.
