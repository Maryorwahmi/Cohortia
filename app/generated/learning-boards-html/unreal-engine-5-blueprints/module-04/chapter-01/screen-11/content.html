Interfaces vs Event Dispatchers vs Casting
Choose the right communication pattern for your use case
Interfaces
One-to-one commands. Define messages and let implementers decide how to respond. Best for targeted actions like Interact or ToggleLight.
Event Dispatchers
One-to-many notifications where many listeners should react to a single event. Good for global signals like OnLevelLoaded or OnPlayerDamaged broadcasting to systems.
Casting
Access class-specific APIs or data. Use when the caller legitimately needs the callee's unique methods or properties, but accept tighter coupling as a trade-off.
Decision reminder

Ask: targeted command, broadcast notification, or class-specific API? The answer determines interfaces, dispatchers, or casting respectively.
