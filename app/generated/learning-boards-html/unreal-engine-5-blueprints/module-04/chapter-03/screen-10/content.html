Serialization & Deserialization
How SaveGame data becomes files and is restored back into values.
What gets serialized
Variables declared on the SaveGame Blueprint and referenced serializable structs and enums.
What doesn't
Transient locals, engine runtime pointers, and direct actor references are not serialized automatically.
Design step
Convert complex runtime objects into serializable primitives or structs before saving; rehydrate them after loading.
Example
Serialize inventory as an array of item IDs and counts rather than saving full item actor instances.
