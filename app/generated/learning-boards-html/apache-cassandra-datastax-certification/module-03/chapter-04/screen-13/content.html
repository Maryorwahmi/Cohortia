Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › What to Remember
Target partitions
Provide the full partition key for efficient reads and lower latency.
Order carefully
ORDER BY works only on clustering columns in their defined order.
Avoid ad-hoc filtering
Use ALLOW FILTERING only for small, ad-hoc checks; prefer denormalization for production queries.
Final thought

Model your data around the queries your application actually needs. That is Cassandra's design principle and your path to predictable performance.
