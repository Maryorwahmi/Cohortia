Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts — Part 2
DELETE
Removes rows or columns by writing tombstones that remain until compaction.
Lightweight Transactions
IF clauses implement LWTs for single-row atomicity at the cost of latency.
TTL
Automatically expires data; expiry writes tombstones similar to DELETE.
Final takeaway
Plan deletes, TTL, and LWTs thoughtfully. They provide useful guarantees and automation, but each has operational costs you must manage.
