Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Mistakes & Best Practices
Common mistakes to avoid
Practical guidance to keep reads healthy and writes efficient.
Many small deletes
Generates tombstones that slow reads; prefer TTL or batched deletions.
Overusing LWT
IF conditions hurt throughput; reserve for critical uniqueness or transitions.
Large collections
Avoid rewriting large SETs/MAPs; model as separate rows if unbounded.
Action
Load-test delete-heavy and LWT-heavy paths, tune compaction, and consider partitioning strategies to limit tombstone impact.
Key Takeaway

Design for write patterns: minimize deletes that create many tombstones and keep LWT usage minimal to preserve throughput.
