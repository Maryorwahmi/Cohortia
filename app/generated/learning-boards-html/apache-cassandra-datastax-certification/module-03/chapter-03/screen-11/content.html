Step 1 — Insert a blog post
Generate UUIDs for post_id and author_id and use toTimestamp(now()) for publication_date.

Step 4 — Delete a comment
Delete the chosen comment using its comment_id and the post's post_id; note that this writes a tombstone.

Key Takeaway

Follow the steps to practice inserts, collection updates, and deletes; observe tombstones and consider TTL for ephemeral comments.
