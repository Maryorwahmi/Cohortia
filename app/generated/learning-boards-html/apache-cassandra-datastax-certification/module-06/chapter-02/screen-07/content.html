What DSE Graph provides
A native graph database on Cassandra using TinkerPop and Gremlin for traversals and modeling connected data efficiently.
Use cases
  • Social networks and recommendation filters where relationships drive results.
  • Fraud detection that analyzes chains of linked accounts and devices.
  • Master data management where relationships between entities must be tracked.
Model example
Nodes: user, device, account. Edges: owns, logs-in-from, transacts-with. Query traversals can follow edges to identify suspicious chains.
Concrete example
Find friends of friends in a social graph and filter by location to produce local recommendations or to detect clusters of suspicious connections.
Misconception check
Graphs are not a replacement for all tables. Use them when traversals and connected queries are central to the application.
