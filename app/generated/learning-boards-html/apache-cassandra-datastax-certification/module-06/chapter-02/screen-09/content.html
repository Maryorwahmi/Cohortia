Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Integrated vs Separate
Integrated DSE
  • Single platform: Cassandra storage + search, graph, analytics engines.
  • Reduces data movement and simplifies consistency across workloads.
  • Lower operational overhead when workloads are moderate and balanced.
Separate Stacks
  • Isolate heavy workloads to dedicated clusters for predictable performance.
  • More operational complexity due to maintaining ETL and synchronization.
  • May be appropriate for extreme scale or strict isolation requirements.
Decision checklist
Workload pattern
Are queries balanced between operational reads, search, and analytics?
Resource needs
Will Spark or Solr require dedicated CPU/memory beyond a shared cluster's capacity?
Operational capacity
Can your team manage multiple clusters or prefer a single integrated platform?
