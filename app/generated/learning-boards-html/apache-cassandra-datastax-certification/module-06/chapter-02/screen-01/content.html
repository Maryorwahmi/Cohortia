DSE Search
Full-text and faceted search via Solr directly on Cassandra tables.
DSE Graph
Native graph model using TinkerPop and Gremlin for traversals and relationships.
DSE Analytics
Spark integrated for real-time and batch analytics on operational data.
Concept Map
Unified Data Platform
Search, graph and analytics share the same storage layer and avoid ETL.
Operational Simplicity
Fewer clusters to manage, but careful planning is required for resource sharing and topology.
Why this matters
Integrated engines let you support search-driven user experiences, relationship-aware features, and analytics without replicating your dataset into separate stacks. That lowers latency and operational cost when planned correctly.
