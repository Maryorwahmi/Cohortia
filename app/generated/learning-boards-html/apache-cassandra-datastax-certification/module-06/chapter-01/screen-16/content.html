What to Remember

Three final checks to guide your lab work and assessments.

Managed operations
Astra handles infra tasks so your team can focus on application logic.
Protect credentials
Store secure connect bundles and secrets in a vault and avoid sharing them in code.
Model & monitor
Design for queries and monitor usage to avoid unexpected costs and performance issues.
Final takeaway

Astra DB reduces operational burden but keeps key developer responsibilities: secure connections, good data models, and cost monitoring.
