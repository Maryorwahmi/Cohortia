Decision Guide

When Astra DB fits

  • Small operations team or desire to reduce maintenance work.
  • Variable or unpredictable traffic patterns that benefit from automatic scaling.
  • Faster time-to-market is a priority over full infrastructure control.
When to reconsider
Sustained massive throughput with cost optimization needs, specialized hardware or strict in-house compliance may favor self-management.
Scenario
A social media startup with viral traffic and a small ops staff is an ideal Astra DB candidate due to automatic scaling and reduced operations.
Key Takeaway

Evaluate team capacity, expected traffic patterns, and cost model to choose between Astra DB and self-managed Cassandra.
