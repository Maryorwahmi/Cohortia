Operational Overhead

What Astra removes

  • Node provisioning, OS and JVM patching, and cluster upgrades.
  • Manual capacity planning and node replacement during failures.
  • Direct management of cross-zone replication and node health.
What remains yours
Data modeling, query design, and monitoring usage to control cost and performance.
Common misconception
Managed does not mean automatic schema tuning. Your queries and table designs determine runtime behavior.
Key Takeaway

Astra removes infra overhead; keep responsibility for schema, queries, and cost monitoring.
