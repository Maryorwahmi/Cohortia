Architecture

Serverless behavior

Astra auto-scales across availability zones, handles patching, and manages node health so teams do not perform manual capacity management.

Automatic provisioning on demand.
High availability across zones is built in.
Patching and upgrades are handled by the provider.
Important nuance
Cassandra concepts like replication and consistency still apply and must be set appropriately for your application.
Monitoring
Regularly monitor usage and patterns to detect cost spikes caused by sustained scaling during heavy traffic.
Key Takeaway

Serverless abstracts infrastructure but preserves data semantics; plan replication and monitor usage.
