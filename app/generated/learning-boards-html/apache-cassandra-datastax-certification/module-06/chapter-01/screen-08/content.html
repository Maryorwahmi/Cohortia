Comparison

Astra DB (Serverless)

  • Automatic scaling and maintenance across zones.
  • Pay-for-what-you-use billing model.
  • Reduced operational staffing and faster launch time.

Self-managed Cassandra

  • Full control over hardware, replication, and upgrades.
  • Higher operational overhead: patching, capacity planning, node health.
  • Potential cost benefits at massive, predictable scale.
Scenario
Startup with unpredictable traffic: Astra DB; Large enterprise with custom compliance: self-managed.
Key Takeaway

Match the offering to team size, traffic patterns, and the need for operational control.
