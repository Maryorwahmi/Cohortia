Key Concepts

Three one-sentence reminders to guide your Astra DB usage.

Astra DB
A managed, serverless Cassandra service that handles infrastructure and scaling across cloud zones.
Secure Connect Bundle
ZIP file with connection certs; pair it with Client ID and Secret and protect them as sensitive credentials.
Modeling & Monitoring
You still design tables for queries and monitor usage to control performance and serverless costs.
Summary

Astra DB simplifies operations and preserves Cassandra semantics. Secure connections and active modeling remain essential.
