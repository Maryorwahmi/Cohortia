Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Security Recap
Authentication
Verify identities before granting access; enable and secure superuser accounts.
Authorization
Grant precise permissions via roles and use NOSUPERUSER for application accounts.
Encryption & Auditing
Encrypt in transit and at rest; enable auditing to capture changes and access events for compliance.
Final Thought

Security and recoverability require configuration, testing, monitoring, and review. Put ownership, runbooks, and regular drills in place to ensure your cluster stays resilient.
