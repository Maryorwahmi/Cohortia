In Transit

TLS/SSL Configuration

Enable client and server encryption options and provide keystore and truststore paths. Rotate certificates as part of regular maintenance.

# In cassandra.yaml
client_encryption_options:
    enabled: true
    optional: false
    require_client_auth: true
    keystore: conf/cassandra_keystore.jks
    keystore_password: my_keystore_password
    truststore: conf/cassandra_truststore.jks
    truststore_password: my_truststore_password
Remember to configure client drivers to trust the cluster certificate and, where required, present a client certificate if mutual authentication is enabled.
At Rest

Disk and Table Protection

Encrypt disks or filesystems that hold SSTables. In managed enterprise products, table-level encryption can provide additional controls. Also ensure your backup targets use encryption at rest.

• Keystore holds private key and certificate for the node.
• Truststore contains certificates the node trusts, like CA certs or peer certificates.
• Test certificate rotation in staging before rolling to production.
Operational Reminder

TLS adds CPU load and may require tuning. Schedule upgrades and certificate rollouts with a rollback plan and driver updates included.
