Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › SSTables & Tombstones
SSTable (Sorted String Table)

Immutable on-disk files produced when memtables flush. New writes create new SSTables rather than modifying existing ones. Reads may consult multiple SSTables to reconstruct the latest row state.

Tombstones

Markers for deleted data that remain until compaction removes them during merging of SSTables. Tombstones increase read work until they are cleared.

Concrete Example

A table with frequent deletes shows increased disk usage and read latency. nodetool cfstats reveals high tombstone counts and many small SSTables. The fix is to pick a compaction strategy and schedule that will merge those SSTables and remove tombstones efficiently.

Key Takeaway

Deletes do not instantly free space. Monitor tombstone counts and plan compaction strategy accordingly to avoid read amplification.
