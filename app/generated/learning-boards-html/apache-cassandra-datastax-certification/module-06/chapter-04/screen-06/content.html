Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Compaction Strategies
SizeTieredCompactionStrategy (STCS)
Groups similarly sized SSTables and merges them; good for write-heavy workloads but can increase read amplification and disk usage.
LeveledCompactionStrategy (LCS)
Organizes SSTables into levels to minimize overlap; reduces reads touching many files, ideal for read-heavy tables but more CPU-intensive for writes.
TimeWindowCompactionStrategy (TWCS)
Groups SSTables by time window; excellent for time-series where older data is stable and can be compacted less frequently.
Common mistake

Choosing STCS for read-heavy tables leads to many small SSTables and poor read latency. Analyze access patterns before selecting a strategy.
