Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › What to Remember
Choose by access pattern
Identify the dominant queries before selecting the database type.
Sketch examples
Write small data sketches (keys, docs, rows) and validate hot queries with a prototype.
Polyglot when needed
Combine databases only when each adds clear value and boundaries are well-defined.
Final takeaway
Match model to queries, prototype quickly, and document your choices. These habits keep your architecture performant and maintainable.
