Scenarios
  1. Session data for high-traffic e-commerce: Best fit: Key-Value. Justification: direct key lookup with expiration.
  2. Product catalog with evolving attributes: Best fit: Document. Justification: flexible JSON records and nested fields.
  3. Billions of sensor readings with timestamps: Best fit: Column-Family. Justification: wide rows and efficient time-range retrieval.
  4. Recommendation engine for connections and interests: Best fit: Graph. Justification: traversals and relationship analysis.
Why justify
A short justification forces you to consider access patterns and reveals mismatches early. This habit reduces costly rework later.
Key Takeaway

Make quick sketches of keys, documents, or row keys for each scenario to validate your decision before implementation.
