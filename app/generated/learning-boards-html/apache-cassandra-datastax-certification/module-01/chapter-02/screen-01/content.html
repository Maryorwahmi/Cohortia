Exploring NoSQL Database Categories
A concise orientation to key-value, document, column-family, and graph databases and why they matter.
In this lesson

You will learn each NoSQL category's data model, see short concrete examples, and practice choosing a category for common scenarios.

Why it matters

Selecting the right model affects query performance, storage efficiency, and how easy it is to evolve your application as requirements change.

Explore Key Topics
Key-Value
Fast lookups; ideal for caching and sessions.
Document
Flexible JSON documents for evolving schemas like product catalogs.
Column-Family
Wide rows and high write throughput for time-series and events.
