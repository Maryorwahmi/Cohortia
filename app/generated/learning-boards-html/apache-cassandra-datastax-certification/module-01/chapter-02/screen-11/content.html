Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Common Mistakes
Frequent errors
  • Assuming a single NoSQL category solves every workload.
  • Using Graph for cache-like needs or Key-Value for complex relationship queries.
  • Confusing column-family stores with analytics columnar systems.
  • Neglecting partitioning and column family design in wide-row stores.
Quick check
For each system you design, list three example queries and one expected throughput constraint. If your chosen DB handles them naturally, proceed; otherwise, reconsider.
Pro tip

Prototype the hot paths with sample data. Real behavior exposes model mismatches quickly.
