Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Graph Databases
Definition
Data is modeled as nodes (entities) and edges (relationships), both with properties. Traversal queries follow edges to discover related nodes.
Example
Node
user: {id: 1, name: "Alice", interests: ["hiking"]}
Edge
friendship: {since: 2020, strength: 0.8}
Key Takeaway

Choose graph databases when relationship traversal and network queries are core to your application.

When to avoid graphs
  • If your primary need is simple key lookups or caching.
  • For large-scale columnar analytics where relationships are not central.
  • For simple document-style records that rarely require traversals.
Vendors
Neo4j, Amazon Neptune, ArangoDB — examples that optimize relationship queries and traversals.
