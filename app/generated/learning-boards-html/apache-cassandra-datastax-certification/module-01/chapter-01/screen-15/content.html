Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
RDBMS
Tables, SQL, and ACID for transactional correctness.
NoSQL
Flexible schemas and horizontal scale; trade-offs around consistency.
Eventual consistency
Replicas converge to the same value over time after writes stop.
Final takeaway
Use the vocabulary to align architecture choices with application requirements. Words map to trade-offs; trade-offs determine good designs.
