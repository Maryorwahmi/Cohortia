Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Why RDBMS Struggled at Web Scale
Core limits
  • Rigid schema slows rapid evolution and heterogeneous data.
  • Vertical scaling hits physical and cost limits.
  • Horizontal distribution requires sharding and coordination logic.
Concrete example
A global social feed needs to accept writes from many regions and serve millions of reads per second; strict global consistency would hurt availability and latency.
Practical pitfall
Attempting to normalize and join across sharded tables often forces cross-node coordination. That coordination undermines the very scalability you sought and can increase latency and failure modes.
Misconception

Assuming you can shard without redesigning your data model. Sharding often requires rethinking access patterns and denormalizing data.
