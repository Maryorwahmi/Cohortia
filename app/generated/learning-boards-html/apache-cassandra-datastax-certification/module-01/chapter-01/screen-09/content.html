Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Trade-offs: ACID vs BASE
ACID (RDBMS)
  • Strong consistency and transactional integrity
  • Good for financial and inventory systems
  • Can be harder to scale horizontally
BASE (NoSQL)
  • Prioritizes availability and scale
  • Flexible schema; good for varied data and streams
  • Eventual consistency requires design attention
Design rule
Match your data model and consistency choice to the application's correctness requirements and access patterns. Don’t force relational modeling on a distributed write-heavy workload.
