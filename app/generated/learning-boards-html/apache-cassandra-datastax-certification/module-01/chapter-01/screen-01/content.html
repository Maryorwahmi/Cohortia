Library vs. Distributed Hubs
Imagine a highly organized single-location library where each book has a fixed spot and a strict catalog. That represents an RDBMS: reliable, indexed, and tightly structured. Now imagine many neighborhood hubs each holding curated copies and accepting local updates — that represents NoSQL’s distributed hubs optimized for availability and scale.
