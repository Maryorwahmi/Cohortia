Definition

Relational model & ACID

Tables with fixed schemas, SQL as the interface, and ACID properties ensure dependable transactional behavior.

Example
Processing an online order where stock decrement and payment record must both succeed or both rollback.
Common mistake
Assuming schema rigidity is always negative; for many transactional systems it prevents class of errors and aids reporting.
Key Takeaway

RDBMS are best when you need predictable structure, strong consistency, and complex relational queries.

ACID quick reference
  • Atomicity: all-or-nothing transactions
  • Consistency: rules and constraints kept
  • Isolation: concurrent safety
  • Durability: committed changes survive failures
