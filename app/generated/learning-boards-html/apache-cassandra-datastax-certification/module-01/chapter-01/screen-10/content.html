Vertical Scaling

Increase resources on one server. Easy to adopt but limited by hardware and cost.

Horizontal Scaling

Add more servers and distribute data. Enables high capacity but needs partitioning and coordination.

Illustration
When vertical works
Small to moderate workloads with heavy transaction guarantees and predictable growth.
When horizontal is needed
Global systems, massive ingest, or multi-region availability requirements.
Pro tip
Design your data model for the scaling strategy. If you plan to shard, think early about partition keys and access patterns.
