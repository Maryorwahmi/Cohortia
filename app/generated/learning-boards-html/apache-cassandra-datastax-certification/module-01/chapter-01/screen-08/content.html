Definition

Basically Available, Soft state, Eventual consistency

  • Basically Available: serve requests despite partial failures.
  • Soft state: system state may be in flux until replicas synchronize.
  • Eventual consistency: replicas converge if updates stop.
Example
A distributed comment feed may show a new comment in one region immediately while another region reflects it a few seconds later. That temporary inconsistency is acceptable to keep the system responsive globally.
Design note
You can combine stronger and weaker guarantees within the same architecture: use stronger consistency for critical operations and eventual consistency for analytics.
