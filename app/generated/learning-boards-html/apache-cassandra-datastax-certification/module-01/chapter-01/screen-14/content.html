Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
RDBMS
Tables, schema, SQL, and ACID for transactional integrity.
NoSQL
A family of systems emphasizing horizontal scale and schema flexibility.
BASE
Basically Available, Soft state, Eventual consistency — a trade-off for scale.
Summary takeaway
Choose the database based on the dominant requirement. Use RDBMS for immediate consistency, NoSQL for scale and availability, and combine them when the application benefits from both.
