Practice and Pitfalls
What to practice and common traps to avoid
Apply → Master
Read a CQL schema
Identify PRIMARY KEY parts and explain how they map to partitioning and clustering.
Model for queries
Design tables around your expected lookups and sort orders instead of normalizing.
Avoid hot spots
Prefer partition keys that spread traffic; avoid time-based or low-cardinality keys alone.
Common misconception
Treating Cassandra like a relational DB and expecting joins and normalized schemas will lead to performance and design issues. Denormalize and accept duplication to meet query patterns.
Milestone
Create and explain a user_profiles table, identify the partition key, and state how that determines node placement for rows.
