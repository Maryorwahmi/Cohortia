Consistency levels
ONE, QUORUM, ALL are common examples that trade latency and availability against data staleness and guarantees.
Pick a low consistency for high write throughput and availability, and a higher consistency when correctness matters. QUORUM gives a majority of replicas which often balances correctness and latency. ALL is safest but requires every replica and is sensitive to node outages.
ONE
Fast, available, possibly stale
QUORUM
Majority-based, stronger guarantees
ALL
Strongest, highest latency
Concrete scenario
An IoT ingestion path might use ONE or LOCAL_ONE for writes to prioritize availability. Analytical queries that combine recent data for billing might use QUORUM to reduce the chance of reading stale values.
Key Takeaway

Use tunable consistency to make explicit tradeoffs: lower levels for availability, higher levels for correctness where required.
