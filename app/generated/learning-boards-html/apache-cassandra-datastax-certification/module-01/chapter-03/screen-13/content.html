Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Pitfalls: Hot Spots and Misusing Cassandra
Common mistakes
Treating Cassandra like a relational DB and picking poor partition keys are the top errors.
Relational mindset
Expecting joins and normalization leads to slow or impossible queries. Design tables per-query and accept duplication for speed.
Hot spots
Low-cardinality keys or single-bucket time keys concentrate load. Use compound keys or bucketing strategies to spread writes.
Pro tip

Map your expected traffic and table growth. If a partition could grow without bound, redesign the key or shard the partition to keep operations efficient.
