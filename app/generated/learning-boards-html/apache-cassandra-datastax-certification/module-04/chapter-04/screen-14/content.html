Question 1
Why is storing comments as a frozen LIST an anti-pattern? Provide a better schema.
Expected answer: unbounded collections require rewrites; use comments_by_article with clustering keys for ordering.
Question 2
A few user_id values generate millions of events. What anti-pattern is this, and how do you mitigate it?
Expected answer: hot partitions/wide rows. Mitigate with bucketing or salting; consider schema redesign for long-term fix.
