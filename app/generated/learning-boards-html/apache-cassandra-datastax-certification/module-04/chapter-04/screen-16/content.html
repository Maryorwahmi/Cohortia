Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Wide Partition

A partition with excessive rows; causes compaction and memory issues.

Hot Partition

A key receiving disproportionate traffic; distribute with salting or bucketing.

Secondary Indexes

Good for low-cardinality lookups; avoid for high-cardinality or range queries.

Final takeaway

Model for queries, monitor partition and compaction metrics, and fix anti-patterns with bucketing, salting, or denormalization as appropriate.
