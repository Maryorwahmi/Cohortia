Learning Objectives
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Model time-series efficiently
You will learn to bucket and cluster using timestamps so queries by time range are fast and partitions stay bounded.
Mnemonic: timeline with buckets
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Handle relationships with denormalization
You will create dedicated tables for queries such as users-by-group and groups-by-user, and manage consistency at the application layer.
Mnemonic: mirrored index cards
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Recognize and avoid anti-patterns
You will identify unbounded collections, ALLOW FILTERING usage, and inefficient secondary indexes and apply correct alternatives.
Mnemonic: red flag list
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Balance read/write and operations
You will evaluate trade-offs that impact storage, query flexibility, and operational overhead, and make design trade-offs intentionally.
Mnemonic: scale triangle
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
