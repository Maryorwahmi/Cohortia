Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Quick Reference
Design for queries
Make your partition and clustering choices based on frequent WHERE clauses and ordering needs.
Balance cardinality
Avoid low-cardinality partition keys and unbounded partitions—use composites or buckets to distribute load.
Order intentionally
Set clustering order to match the sorts and ranges you will request, such as newest-first for feeds.
Final takeaway
Design small, test early, and match your primary key to the real queries you must serve.
