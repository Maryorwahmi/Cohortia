Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › JMX Security
Why secure JMX?
JMX provides management access to the JVM and application internals; unsecured JMX can leak metrics or allow management operations.
Hardening steps
  • Enable authentication and configure jmxremote.password and jmxremote.access.
  • Enable SSL and provide keystore/truststore paths.
  • Restrict access to trusted hosts via firewall rules and bind JMX to internal interfaces.
Lab vs production
Lab
Disable auth/SSL only in isolated labs for ease of connection.
Production
Always enable auth and SSL, and restrict network access to management hosts.
