nodetool essentials
tpstats, tablestats, netstats and ring provide quick operational signals.
JMX MBeans
org.apache.cassandra.metrics and org.apache.cassandra.db expose attributes for deep inspection.
Security
Enable authentication, SSL and network restrictions for JMX in production; lab-only exceptions are allowed for learning.
Summary takeaway

Use nodetool to spot symptoms quickly, use JMX to confirm causes, and always correlate metrics across MBeans and system telemetry before making changes.
