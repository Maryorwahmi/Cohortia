Monitoring Cassandra with nodetool and JMX
Practical commands and MBean inspection for cluster health and performance.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
This chapter teaches two complementary monitoring approaches. Use nodetool for quick CLI checks like status, tpstats, tablestats and netstats. Use JMX clients to drill into MBeans and see metrics like ReadLatency, PendingTasks, LiveDiskSpaceUsed and more. Together they let you distinguish disk, CPU, network, or data-model problems and find hot spots in your cluster.
In this lesson
nodetool deep commands and MBean inspection.
Learn to spot thread pool bottlenecks, table hotspots, and network issues.
Coming up

Why it matters
Faster root-cause analysis reduces downtime and improves SLA adherence.
Pinpoint whether a slow node is saturated by CPU, disk, network, or a bad data model.
Start with nodetool for quick checks, then confirm with JMX MBeans when you need granular metrics.
