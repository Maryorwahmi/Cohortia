Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Diagnose MutationStage
Collect evidence
  • tpstats: confirm PendingTasks and ActiveTasks on MutationStage.
  • tablestats: check per-table write counts and LiveDiskSpaceUsed.
  • netstats: look for pending messages or streaming to peers.
  • system metrics: disk I/O latency, CPU, and GC pauses.
Remediation options
Short-term
Throttle clients, restart long-lived GC-causing processes, or temporarily move traffic away from the node.
Long-term
Upgrade disks, tune GC, add nodes, or redesign hot partitions to spread write load.
