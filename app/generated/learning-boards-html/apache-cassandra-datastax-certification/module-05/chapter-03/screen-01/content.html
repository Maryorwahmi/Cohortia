Why this chapter matters

Logs are the single most actionable data source during incidents. They tell you what Cassandra attempted, where timeouts happened, and whether JVM pauses or disk pressure contributed. Learning targeted searches and safe practices for verbose logs keeps you efficient and avoids creating new problems.
