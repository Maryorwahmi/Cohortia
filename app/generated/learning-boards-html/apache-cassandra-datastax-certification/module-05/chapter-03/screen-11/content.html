Architecture

Log rotation basics

Use log4j2 rolling appenders to rotate by size or time. This prevents logs from filling disks and causing node instability.

Operational checklist
  • Configure rotation in log4j2.xml for system.log, debug.log, and gc.log.
  • Place logs on dedicated partitions or mount points when possible.
  • Centralize logs to an aggregator for longer retention without node disk growth.
  • Test rotation and monitor free disk space with alerts.
Common mistake

Enabling verbose logging without adjusting retention or storage planning. This often leads to compaction failures and node crashes.

Key Takeaway

Treat rotation and storage planning as part of your Cassandra deployment checklist; logs must be durable but bounded.
