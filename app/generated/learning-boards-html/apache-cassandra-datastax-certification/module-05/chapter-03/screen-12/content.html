Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Interpreting compaction and disk errors
Definition

Out of space for compaction

Indicates compaction could not run because there was insufficient disk space for temporary files and merged SSTables.

Immediate actions
  • Free disk space by removing non-essential files and rotating logs.
  • Consider stopping Cassandra if the node is unstable to prevent data corruption.
  • As a last resort, temporarily disable autocompaction (use with caution).
Long-term fixes
  • Increase disk capacity or migrate to larger drives.
  • Review compaction strategy and tune for space and performance.
  • Configure disk_failure_policy appropriately and set proactive alerts for disk usage.
Key Takeaway

Treat disk space errors as urgent: resolve immediately and follow with capacity planning and monitoring to avoid recurrence.
