Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Comparing the three logs
Comparison

When to use each log

system.log
High-level events, startup/shutdown, compaction errors, and explicit timeouts. First stop for incidents.
debug.log
Verbose internals for deep investigation. Use briefly and narrowly to avoid disk growth.
gc.log
JVM GC cycles and pause durations. Use when unexplained latency or timeouts may be JVM-related.
Concrete mapping
  • Compaction failures → system.log → check disk and compaction strategy.
  • Poor query performance → system.log then debug.log for execution details.
Key Takeaway

Choose the log that answers your question and correlate across logs and metrics for a full picture.
