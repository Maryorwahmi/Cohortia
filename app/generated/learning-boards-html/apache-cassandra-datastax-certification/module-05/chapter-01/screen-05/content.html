nodetool status output
A quick terminal snapshot that shows node flags and distribution.
UN  192.0.2.1  256.71 GB  rack1  dc1
UN  192.0.2.2  260.12 GB  rack1  dc1
DN  192.0.2.3  0.00 KB    rack2  dc1
The first two letters are status and state: U/D and N/J/L. Load and ownership indicate data distribution and potential hotspots.
Checklist
  • All nodes should be UN under normal operation.
  • Check for sustained DN or prolonged J/L states.
  • Compare load across nodes to detect imbalance.
Interpretation cards
UN — Good
Node is up and in normal operation.
UJ / UL — Transitional
Node is joining or leaving; check for expected bootstrapping activity.
DN — Down
Node has gone down; inspect logs and resources for causes.
Common mistake: ignoring load skew. Even if all nodes are UN, uneven ownership can cause hotspots and high latency.
