Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › cfstats & compactionstats
Sample cfstats snippet
Keyspace: myapp
  Table: users
    SSTable count: 8
    Read Latency: 0.012 ms
    Write Latency: 0.310 ms
    Tombstone count: 42
    Tombstone ratio: 0.15
These per-table numbers help you identify hot tables with high tombstone ratios or many SSTables.
compactionstats snapshot
pending tasks: 12
bytes pending: 12.4 GB
Throughput (KB/sec): 0
A non-zero pending backlog with many SSTables indicates compaction is falling behind and can cause read amplification.
Pro-tip

Collect cfstats and compactionstats at regular intervals to spot trends rather than relying on a single snapshot.
