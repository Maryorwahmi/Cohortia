Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Tombstones
What is a tombstone?
A marker representing deleted or superseded data used for eventual consistency.
Effect on reads
Reads must scan tombstones, increasing I/O and CPU and raising latency, especially with many SSTables.
Causes
  • Frequent deletes or updates to wide rows.
  • Long gc_grace_seconds delaying tombstone purging.
  • Insufficient compaction or repair cycles.
Analogy

Tombstones are like faded sticky notes you must check before reading the current file; too many slow you down.

Monitoring signals
Use nodetool cfstats to read the tombstone count and tombstone ratio for tables. Watch ratios for frequently queried tables closely.
Remediation checklist
  • Review data model and query patterns that cause deletions.
  • Consider TTLs for short-lived data.
  • Run repair and schedule compaction to purge tombstones after gc_grace_seconds.
