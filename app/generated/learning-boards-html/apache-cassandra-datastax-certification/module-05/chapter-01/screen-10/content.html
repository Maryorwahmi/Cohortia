Comparing strategies
Understand how each strategy affects SSTables, read latency, and write amplification.
SizeTiered
Simple, write-friendly. Can accumulate many SSTables and increase read amplification.
Leveled
Keeps a bounded number of files per level for predictable reads; higher write amplification.
TimeWindow
Groups data by time for efficient TTL and time-series workloads.
Monitoring signals
Watch SSTable count, compaction backlog, read latency, and bytes pending to understand strategy effects in production.
Pro tip: don't force manual compactions in peak traffic times; schedule heavy compaction during lower load to avoid I/O contention.
