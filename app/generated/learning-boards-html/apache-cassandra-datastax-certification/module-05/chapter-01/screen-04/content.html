What 'healthy' means
A working definition with practical signals you can measure and verify.
Node availability
All nodes should report Up and Normal. Persistent Down, Joining, or Leaving states require investigation.
Performance signals
Read and write latency percentiles, tombstone ratios, and compaction backlogs indicate whether the cluster serves requests efficiently.
Resource check
Disk I/O, CPU, memory, and network must be within capacity; otherwise nodes appear up but respond slowly.
Key Takeaway

Health is both availability and performance; use status flags plus latency and tombstone metrics to form a reliable view.

Analogy
Think of status as the car's engine light and metrics as the speedometer and oil pressure; one tells you it's running, the others tell you how well it runs.
Quick checklist
  • nodetool status shows UN for all nodes.
  • Median and tail latencies are within expected ranges.
  • Tombstone ratio is low for frequently queried tables.
  • Compaction backlog is not persistently large.
Common misconception
Assuming UN equals good performance. Always pair status with latency and compaction metrics to avoid false assurances.
