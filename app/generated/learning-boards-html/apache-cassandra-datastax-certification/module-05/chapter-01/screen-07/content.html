Median vs Tail
Understand central tendency and percentile behavior for both reads and writes.
Median (50th)
Represents typical latency for most requests; useful for capacity planning.
95th / 99th percentiles
Reveal tail latency that affects a minority of requests but often drives user complaints.
Key Takeaway

Tail percentiles are early indicators of problems; prioritize investigating 95th/99th increases even when medians look fine.

Practical checks
  • Correlate tail spikes with cfstats to see if many SSTables or tombstones are present.
  • Check compaction backlog — falling behind increases SSTable count and read amplification.
  • For writes, inspect commit log flush and memtable flush behavior when tail latency rises.
Common misconception: averages hide variability. Use percentile-based alerts for latency-sensitive applications.
