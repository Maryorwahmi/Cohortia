Collecting metrics
Schedule regular captures of nodetool outputs and ingest them into a time-series store.
  1. Capture nodetool status, info, cfstats, compactionstats on a schedule.
  2. Compute baseline percentiles and typical tombstone ratios per table.
  3. Set alerts for sustained deviations and tail percentile increases.
Avoid alert noise by focusing on percentiles and sustained trends; revisit thresholds periodically as workloads evolve.
Alert suggestions
  • 95th/99th read latency sustained above baseline.
  • Rapid rise in tombstone ratio for frequently queried tables.
  • Compaction backlog growing over multiple collection intervals.
Correlation tip: pair metric alerts with node-level logs and OS-level disk I/O alerts for confident diagnosis.
