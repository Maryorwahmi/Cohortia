Repair: flags, sequencing, and monitoring

A step-by-step maintenance pattern to repair safely without overloading the cluster.

Step 1 — Scope
Decide whether to use -pr for primary ranges, -dc for a single data center, or incremental repair when supported.
Step 2 — Schedule
Plan repairs during low traffic windows and stagger across nodes to reduce peak load.
Step 3 — Monitor
Watch CPU, I/O, network, and request latencies. Be prepared to pause or stop if application impact is severe.
Common mistake

Running repairs cluster-wide simultaneously can flood the network with streaming traffic and harm availability. Always scope and sequence.

Check your understanding
If your cluster is multi-datacenter and you need to avoid cross-DC streaming, which flag helps isolate repairs? Answer: the -dc flag.
