Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Incremental backups
Definition

Continuous SSTable capture

When enabled, new SSTables are hard-linked into backups directories after flush, providing a stream of incremental files to copy off-cluster.

Usage pattern
Take a base snapshot first, enable incremental backups, then copy backup SSTables periodically to off-cluster storage.
Limitations & risks
  • Incremental backups capture only new SSTables, not modifications inside existing SSTables.
  • Deletions (tombstones) may not be present unless properly propagated and repaired.
  • A base snapshot is required to apply incrementals during restore.
Best practice
Combine a full snapshot with periodic copying of incremental SSTables, and include repair in your restore plan.
