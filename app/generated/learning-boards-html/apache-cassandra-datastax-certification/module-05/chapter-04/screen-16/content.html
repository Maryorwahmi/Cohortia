Off-cluster Storage
Store snapshot and incremental files off the node to survive host failure.
nodetool refresh
Registers newly copied SSTables with Cassandra so they are available without heavy rebuilds.
nodetool repair
Synchronizes the restored node with other replicas to ensure deletions and missing writes are reconciled.
Final takeaway
Always combine snapshots and incrementals with off-cluster copies, verify restores, and run refresh then repair to complete recovery.
