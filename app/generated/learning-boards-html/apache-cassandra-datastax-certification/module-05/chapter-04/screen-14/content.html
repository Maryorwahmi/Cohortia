Common backup and restore mistakes

Not copying off-cluster
Snapshots kept only on local disk are lost on disk or host failure.
Snapshot retention
Old snapshots block SSTable deletion and can fill disks; schedule cleanup after verification.
Skipping repair
Omitting repair after a restore leads to inconsistent replicas and resurrected data.
Tombstone handling
Ensure gc_grace_seconds and regular repairs preserve tombstones long enough to propagate deletions.
Test restores
Regularly simulate restores to confirm scripts and off-cluster copies are valid.
Plan for space
Monitor disk usage because snapshots and backups prevent SSTable deletions during retention windows.
