Why this matters

When you design a Cassandra deployment you decide how many copies of data live where, and how the system tolerates rack or data center failures. These choices determine latency, durability, and recovery behavior for your application.

Key Takeaway

Cassandra's logical grouping of nodes into racks and data centers lets you tune replication for locality, availability, and resilience without a single master node.
