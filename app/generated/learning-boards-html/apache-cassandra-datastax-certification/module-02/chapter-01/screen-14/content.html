Node & Cluster
Peer nodes form a cluster with no single master; any node can coordinate requests.
Racks & Data Centers
Logical groupings used to distribute replicas across physical failure domains and regions.
nodetool & Status
Use nodetool status and ring to verify Datacenter, Rack, and node Status codes like UN or DN.
Summary Takeaway

Design topology to match failure domains, pick replication per DC for your SLAs, and use nodetool as your first line of inspection.
