Map failure domains to topology

Document what failures you must tolerate, then design data centers and racks to match. Use replication factors to control how many local copies exist in each DC.

Example mapping
East-US → DC_East with rack1/2/3; West-US → DC_West with rack1/2/3. NetworkTopologyStrategy: DC_East:3, DC_West:2.
Validation steps
  • Run nodetool status to confirm rack and DC labels.
  • Simulate node or rack failures and observe availability.
  • Ensure repairs or streaming complete after topology changes.
Why it matters
A topology aligned with real failure domains ensures you meet SLAs without unnecessary cost. Poorly planned topology can leave you vulnerable during outages despite replication settings.
Pro-tip

Keep topology documentation with deployment manifests so rack and DC names map to actual hardware and locations.
