Process Flow

Coordinator routing

Client connects
A client may connect to any node, which becomes the temporary coordinator for that request.
Locate replicas
Coordinator uses partitioner and replication strategy to find responsible replicas across racks and DCs.
Orchestrate request
Send reads or writes to replicas and return results once the chosen consistency level is satisfied.
Example
Client writes to node A. Node A identifies replicas in DC1 and DC2 and sends write requests. If consistency is ONE, it returns success after one replica acknowledges; if QUORUM, it waits until a quorum acknowledges.
Misconception

Coordinators are not masters or single points of control; they are temporary orchestrators for individual requests.
