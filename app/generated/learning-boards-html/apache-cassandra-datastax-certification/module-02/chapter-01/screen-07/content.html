Concept

Data Center — logical grouping

A data center groups racks; it can represent a geographic region or a logical separation of workloads within the same site. Multi-DC deployments enable disaster recovery and lower latency for geographically distributed users.

Example topology
DC1 (3 replicas) in us-east with three racks; DC2 (2 replicas) in europe-west for read locality and DR.
Design note
Choose replication per DC based on regional traffic and recovery goals. More replicas increase durability but also write cost.
Why it matters
Geographic users benefit from a nearby data center to reduce latency. In disaster scenarios, another DC can continue serving traffic. Logical DCs let you separate transactional and analytical workloads so heavy analytical queries don't impact transactional latency.
Key Takeaway

Treat data centers as both a resilience and latency tool: replication per DC gives you control over where and how many copies of data exist.
