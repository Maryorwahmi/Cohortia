DN Troubleshooting

Immediate checklist

1. Check Cassandra process
Confirm the JVM process is running; restart if necessary after logs review.
2. Review logs
Look for system.log errors: startup exceptions, out of memory, or streaming failures.
3. Verify network
Ensure gossip and transport ports are reachable from peers; DNS or IP changes can break connectivity.
4. Check resources
Inspect CPU, memory, disk I/O, and disk capacity for starvation or throttling.
Operational notes
DN often indicates a recoverable outage. Use decommission, replace, or bootstrap only after confirming the node cannot be brought back cleanly. Repairs may be needed after rejoin.
