Learning Objectives
Build mental models that let you diagnose durability, performance, and consistency issues in Cassandra.
Bloom's: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Trace write lifecycle
Follow a write from client to coordinator to commit log and memtable, and finally to SSTable on disk.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain storage components
Define commit log, memtable and SSTable and why each is important for durability and performance.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe read process
Identify how memtable, caches, Bloom filters and SSTables are consulted during a read and how results are reconciled.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify mistakes
Recognize risks like disabling durable writes or misplacing the commit log, and how Bloom filter misconfiguration affects reads.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
