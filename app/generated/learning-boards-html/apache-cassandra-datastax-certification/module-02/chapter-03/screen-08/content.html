Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Coordinator & Hinted Handoff
Coordinator responsibilities
  • Determine replicas for a partition key.
  • Forward reads and writes to replicas and collect responses.
  • Enforce the configured consistency level before client acknowledgement.
Practical effect
The choice of coordinator affects where client connections go and how latency accumulates across the path to replicas.
Hinted Handoff
If a replica is unavailable, the coordinator stores a small hint recording the missed write. When the replica returns, the hint is delivered so the replica receives the missed mutation.
When it helps
Good for short outages to maintain write availability without requiring manual repair.
Misconception

Hinted handoff is not a replacement for repair. Hints are temporary and not intended for long-term consistency across wide outages.
