Per-DC control and rack awareness
Specify replication factors per data center so replicas are placed with awareness of racks and failure domains inside each DC.
How to think about it
  • Define RF per DC based on capacity and availability requirements.
  • Cassandra will attempt to distribute replicas across racks to limit correlated failures.
  • Per-DC RF lets you balance cost and resilience across regions.
Concrete example
CREATE KEYSPACE my_app_data WITH replication = {'class':'NetworkTopologyStrategy','DC_East':3,'DC_West':2} creates 3 replicas in DC_East and 2 in DC_West.
Misconception
Automatic perfect balancing
NetworkTopologyStrategy tries to be rack-aware but cannot create nodes or racks; topology and node counts limit placement options.
Key Takeaway

Use NetworkTopologyStrategy to express per-DC resilience goals and to keep replicas spread across racks within each data center.
