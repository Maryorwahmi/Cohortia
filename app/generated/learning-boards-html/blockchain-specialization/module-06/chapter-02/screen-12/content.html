Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Mitigations
Checks-Effects-Interactions
Perform checks, update state, then do external interactions. This ordering closes the reentrancy window by ensuring state reflects the action before control leaves the contract.
Safe arithmetic
Use compiler versions that revert on overflow or explicit checked math. Audit multipliers and other user-controlled arithmetic inputs for bounds.
RBAC and require checks
Ensure functions that change sensitive state enforce identity checks through require or role modifiers. Confirm initialization sets owner correctly.
Order-sensitivity mitigations
Commit-reveal, batching, or delay reduce front-running exposure by hiding immediate intentions or grouping transactions.
Practical checklist for reviewers
  • Point to any external call and confirm state changed prior to that call.
  • Verify arithmetic on critical paths or require compiler-level checks.
  • List functions that modify control or money and confirm access checks exist and are consistent.
  • Flag loops over user-controlled arrays and suggest pagination or push patterns.
