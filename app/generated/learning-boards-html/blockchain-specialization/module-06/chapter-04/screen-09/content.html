Designing a Comprehensive Security Strategy
A practical sequence to reduce risk across development and deployment.
1. Developer Testing
Unit and integration tests that assert expected behavior and guardrails; the foundation for later checks.
2. CI with Automated Tooling
Run static analysis and fuzzing on every commit to catch regressions early.
3. Peer Review
Internal review to reason about business logic and interaction patterns.
4. Third-party Audit
Professional manual review producing ranked findings and remediation guidance.
5. Bug Bounty
Crowdsourced testing after initial audit to find residual issues before mainnet launch.
6. Formal Verification (where justified)
Apply mathematical proofs to mission-critical contracts handling the highest value.
Checklist
  • Acceptance criteria for tests and audits.
  • CI pass gating rules for merge policy.
  • Bug bounty timing and scope after audit.
Why this order?
Early tests and CI reduce trivial issues; audits and bounties find logic and real-world misuse; formal proofs protect the highest-value invariants.
