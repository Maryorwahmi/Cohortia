Bug Bounties and Continuous Security
Crowdsourced testing plus active monitoring keep contracts safe after deployment.
Bug Bounty Design
Define scope, networks, and reward tiers. Prioritize critical contracts and clearly state disclosure rules and triage steps.
Monitoring
Set alerts for abnormal transactions, large transfers, or unusual gas patterns. Combine with incident response playbooks.
How they work together
Audits reduce obvious risk, bounties find remaining edge cases, and monitoring catches live exploitation. Do not rely on one layer alone.
Key Takeaway
Plan bug bounties after audits and implement monitoring thresholds to detect live anomalies and respond quickly.
