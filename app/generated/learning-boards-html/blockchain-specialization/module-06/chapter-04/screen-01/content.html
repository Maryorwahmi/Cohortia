Auditing and Formal Verification of Smart Contracts
Why careful review, automated tools, and mathematical proofs together form a robust defense for on-chain code.

What you'll understand: the stages of a security audit, capabilities and limits of static analysis and fuzzing, and when to invest in formal verification.

Why it matters: deployed contracts are immutable; a missed vulnerability can cause irreversible loss. A layered approach balances speed and assurance.

In this lesson
  • How manual audits complement automated tools.
  • Static analysis, fuzzing, and symbolic execution explained with examples.
  • What formal verification proves and when to use it.

Quick takeaway
Treat auditing and verification as ongoing processes. Start with fast automated checks, follow with expert audits, and reserve formal proofs for the highest-risk components.
