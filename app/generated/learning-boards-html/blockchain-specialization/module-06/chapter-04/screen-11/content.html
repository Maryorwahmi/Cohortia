Audit Lifecycle
Milestones from scoping to re-audit and final deliverables.
Scoping
Define architecture, critical flows, and coverage goals.
Initial Review
Auditor reads code and outlines threat surface.
Findings
Ranked vulnerabilities with severity and suggested fixes.
Remediation
Developer implements fixes and provides tests and explanations.
Re-audit
Auditor confirms fixes and issues final report and statement of coverage.
Deliverables
Ranked findings report, remediation checklist, tests added, and an executive summary for stakeholders.
Key Takeaway
Plan audits with time for remediation cycles and clear acceptance criteria so re-audits confirm root-cause fixes, not just surface changes.
