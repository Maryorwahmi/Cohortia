Architecture

Multi-layered security approach

Design defenses across network, contract, interface, and human layers to avoid single points of failure.

Network

Validator diversity, monitoring for concentration, and economic disincentives for centralization.

Contract

Checks-effects-interactions, audits, tests, formal methods where feasible, and restricted privileges.

Interface

Front-end integrity, content security policies, transaction previews and minimal client-side trust.

Human

Key custody, multi-signature, training, and documented emergency response with acceptance of immutability limits.

Practical exercise

Map your system controls to each layer. Identify the highest-impact gaps where a single compromise could cause irreversible loss, then prioritize mitigations that reduce blast radius such as multi-sig and timelocks.

Key Takeaway

Security in blockchain requires layered controls that jointly reduce the chance that any single compromise leads to catastrophic, irreversible loss.
