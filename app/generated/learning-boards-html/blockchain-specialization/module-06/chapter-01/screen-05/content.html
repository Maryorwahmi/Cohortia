Consensus threats and the 51% attack

How majority control can affect finality and censor transactions.

Example • Mitigations • Checks
Attack steps
1
Acquire majority power

Rent or accumulate mining/staking power on a small chain.

2
Censor or reorganize

Omit transactions or build an alternate chain segment.

3
Double-spend

Spend funds on one branch and then publish a longer branch that reverses the spending.

Mitigations
  • Increase validator diversity and make majority control expensive.
  • Monitor validator concentration and set alerting thresholds for sudden power shifts.
  • Use checkpointing or economic defenses on small chains where needed.
Concrete example

A small PoW chain lost decentralization when a miner rented hash power and reversed recent blocks, causing financial loss and trust damage.

Misconception
Believing major chains cannot be attacked overlooks risks to smaller networks with concentrated mining or staking.
Key Takeaway

Measure validator distribution and economic cost to acquire majority control as primary indicators of network risk.
