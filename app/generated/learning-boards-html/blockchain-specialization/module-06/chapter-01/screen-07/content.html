DApp front-end vs smart contract risks

Each layer — UI, wallet, contract — offers a different path to loss and needs tailored mitigations.

Front-end
Threats
  • Phishing and UI spoofing that trick users to sign malicious transactions.
  • Supply chain or CDN compromises that alter served code.
Mitigations

Content security policies, integrity checks, clear transaction previews, and minimal client-side trust.

Smart Contract
Threats
  • Logic bugs, reentrancy, incorrect access controls, and deterministic exploits.
Mitigations

Audits, tests, cautious upgrade patterns, minimal privileges and multi-sig for high-risk functions.

Scenario

A compromised front-end swaps a withdrawal contract address. The user signs a transaction and funds leave their account even though the on-chain contract was secure. The easiest attack path can be the front-end that users rely on.

Key Takeaway

Protect the entire DApp stack: user protections and front-end integrity are as important as contract correctness.
