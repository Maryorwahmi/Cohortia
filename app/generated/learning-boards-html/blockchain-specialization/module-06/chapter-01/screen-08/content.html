Core Concept

Private key management and the human element

Users and operators, not just code, create risk vectors that can cause permanent loss.

Risk
Seed phrase theft

Storing seeds on connected devices or sharing them enables attackers to fully control funds.

Risk
Single owner keys

Privileged keys on contracts create a single point of catastrophic failure if compromised.

Mitigation
Multi-signature

Require multiple approvals for sensitive operations to reduce single-key risk.

Education

Train users to recognise phishing attempts, never enter seed phrases into websites, and prefer hardware wallets for custody. Operational procedures should document key storage, rotation, and emergency plans that accept immutability limits.

Key Takeaway

Human factors are primary; design custody and operational controls that assume some human mistakes will occur and reduce their impact.
