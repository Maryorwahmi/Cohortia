Analyzing withdraw and emergencyWithdraw risks

Examine state ordering and privileged functions for potential abuse when keys are compromised.

Withdraw

Sequence: require → balances decrement → transfer. This follows checks-effects-interactions and reduces reentrancy risk.

Check

Simulate a recipient contract attempting to re-enter during transfer. Confirm balances prevent double withdrawals.

emergencyWithdraw

Privileged owner-only function that sends full contract balance to owner. If owner key is compromised, attacker can drain funds.

Mitigation

Use multi-signature, timelocks, and minimize owner-only powers. Consider upgrade patterns that limit emergency action scope.

Key Takeaway

Design for the realistic failure: state-ordering prevents reentrancy, and privileged functions require operational controls like multi-sig and timelock to limit impact of key compromise.
