Incident response under immutability

Design plans to detect, contain, and compensate when reversals are not feasible.

Detect

Monitor balances, unusual transaction patterns, and validator metrics to identify incidents quickly.

Contain

Use pausable contract functions, emergency multi-sig actions, or front-end freezes to limit ongoing damage.

Compensate

Prepare off-chain remediation plans and transparent communication; consider insured or community-based compensation where possible.

Example playbook
  1. Alert on abnormal drain
  2. Trigger pause or multisig hold
  3. Assess, notify users, and coordinate compensation
Misconception
Expecting forks or rollbacks as normal incident responses is unrealistic for most projects; plan for alternatives.
Key Takeaway

Build incident procedures that assume immutability, emphasizing fast detection, containment and user communication rather than relying on reversal.
