Core Concept

Common misconceptions and how to avoid them

Recognize false assumptions that lead to under-protected systems.

Misconception
Decentralization = invulnerability

Reality: decentralization changes failure modes; it does not remove bugs or human errors.

Misconception
Audits are permanent guarantees

Reality: audits reduce risk but do not guarantee absence of vulnerabilities; continuous testing and monitoring are required.

Misconception
Rollbacks are routine fixes

Reality: forks are costly and contentious; design incident plans that do not assume reversal.

Misconception
Front-end and human risks are secondary

Reality: attackers often exploit the easiest path — user interfaces and people — so these must be first-class defenses.

Key Takeaway

Be explicit about assumptions, and design compensating controls for every mistaken assumption. Prioritize minimal privileges, multi-sig, monitoring, and user protections.
