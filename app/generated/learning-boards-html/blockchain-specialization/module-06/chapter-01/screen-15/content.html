What to Remember

Key Concepts

Three compact ideas to retain and apply.

51% Attack

Majority control enables censorship and reorgs; decentralization and monitoring reduce feasibility.

Smart Contract Vulnerabilities

Immutable code controlling value must be rigorously tested and privilege-minimized.

Immutability

Permanent ledger entries change response options; focus on prevention and compensating controls.

Takeaway

Measure risk across layers and invest where exposure and impact overlap: network metrics, code quality, front-end integrity, and key custody.
