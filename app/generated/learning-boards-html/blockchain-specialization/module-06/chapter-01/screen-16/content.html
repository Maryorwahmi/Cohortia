Final Reminders

Key Concepts — Quick Reference

Three short, actionable reminders to keep in your workflow.

Monitor decentralization

Validator concentration metrics inform consensus risk and need ongoing monitoring.

Harden contract code

Prioritize safe patterns, audits and minimal privileges for any code that controls value.

Protect users and keys

Front-end integrity, hardware wallets and multi-sig reduce exposure from human errors and phishing.

Parting thought

Security is ongoing: make these checks routine and adjust them as your architecture and threat landscape evolve.
