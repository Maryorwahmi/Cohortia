Core Concept

What makes blockchain security unique?

Decentralization and immutability change what prevention and response look like.

Definition • Example • Misconception
Definition
Security covers network, code, interface, and human risks in a shared environment.
Concrete example

A deployed contract bug that allows unauthorized withdrawals illustrates immutability: the ledger records exploit transactions and reversal may require contentious forks.

Common mistake
Assuming decentralization eliminates the need for careful code and operational controls is dangerous; decentralization shifts where failures impact users.
Analogy
Digital concrete

Immutability is like pouring concrete for your ledger and contracts: once set, changes are difficult and expensive. That means prevention is more important than cure.

Key Takeaway

Design with the expectation that some incidents cannot be reversed; prioritize audits, safety patterns and minimal privileged power.
