Process Flow

Assessing attack surface across layers

Use a hierarchy to prioritize where security effort yields the greatest reduction in risk.

Layer
Network

Validator distribution and risk of majority control.

Layer
Contract

Logic, external calls, and privileged functions.

Layer
Interface & Human

Front-end integrity and key custody practices that expose users to scams.

Exercise

List your high-value assets and map which layer most directly exposes them. Prioritize reviews, monitoring, and operational controls accordingly.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize security effort where high impact and high exposure meet, and revisit the assessment as code and infrastructure change.
