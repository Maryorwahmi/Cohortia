Module 06 › Chapter 6.6 › Common Mistakes & Risk
Common mistakes and outcomes
User-side errors, not cryptography, are the leading cause of loss.
Sharing seeds
Anyone with the seed can regenerate keys and spend funds.
Insecure backups
Cloud notes or plain files can be accessed by attackers or leaked.
Phishing
Fake wallet sites or social engineering can trick users into revealing seeds.
Concrete contrast
Hardware wallet + offline backup vs. mnemonic saved in cloud: the former isolates signing and reduces remote theft risk; the latter dramatically increases exposure.
Key Takeaway
No matter how strong the protocol cryptography, user key compromise defeats it. Protect the private key first.
