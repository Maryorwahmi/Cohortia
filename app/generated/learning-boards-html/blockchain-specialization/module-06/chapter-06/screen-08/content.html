Module 06 › Chapter 6.6 › SHA-256 vs Keccak-256
SHA-256 and Keccak-256
Both are cryptographic hashes used by major chains for integrity and addressing.
Role in network
Used to compute block header digests, Merkle summaries, and address derivation depending on protocol.
Misconception
Algorithm choice is not a security label on its own; security depends on appropriate cryptographic hardness and proper use.
SHA-256
Used in Bitcoin for block hashing and proof-of-work loops.
Keccak-256
Used in Ethereum for keccak-based hashing and address derivation.
Key Takeaway
Both algorithms provide digest properties that support integrity and addressing; protocols choose one based on design history and ecosystem needs.
