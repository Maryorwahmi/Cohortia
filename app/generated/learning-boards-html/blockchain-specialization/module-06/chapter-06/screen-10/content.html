Module 06 › Chapter 6.6 › Digital Signatures
Sign and verify
Signing proves who authorized a transaction and that it was not altered.
Step A: Sign
Create signature over transaction using private key.
Step B: Verify
Anyone uses the public key to confirm the signature matches the data and signer.
Misconception
Signing is not encryption. It proves origin and integrity but does not conceal transaction contents.
Concrete example
When you sign a transfer, the resulting signature plus the transaction payload is broadcast. Nodes verify the signature before including the transaction in a block.
Key Takeaway
Signatures enable trustless verification: no central authority is needed to confirm who authorized a transaction.
