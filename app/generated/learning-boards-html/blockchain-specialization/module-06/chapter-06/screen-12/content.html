Hardware wallets and offline backups
Practical steps to reduce private key exposure and accidental loss.
Hardware wallet
Stores keys in a secure chip and requires physical confirmation for transactions, mitigating malware risk.
Offline generation
Create keys on an air-gapped device to avoid exposing seeds to networked environments.
Physical backups
Store seed phrases on paper or metal, in secure locations, to survive device loss.
Warnings
Never enter your full seed phrase into a website. Avoid cloud storage of plain seed backups. Beware phishing sites that mimic wallet UIs.
Key Takeaway
Defend private keys by keeping them offline when possible and using hardware wallets for signing critical transactions.
