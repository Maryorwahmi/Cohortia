Learning Objectives
A practical taxonomy of what you will be able to do and explain.
01
Explain hash properties
Describe determinism, pre-image and collision resistance and why they matter for tamper evidence.
Mnemonic: fingerprint
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Describe Merkle roots & block linkage
Explain how transaction summaries and previous-hash fields create efficient verification and immutability.
Mnemonic: tree root
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Explain public/private keys
Define the roles of private and public keys and how signatures prove authorization and integrity.
Mnemonic: lock and key
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Manage keys safely
Describe secure generation, mnemonic seed phrases, offline backups and hardware wallets to reduce theft risk.
Mnemonic: vault
Verified Competency Milestone
