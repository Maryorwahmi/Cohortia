Key Safety Concepts
Three concise practices to adopt now.
Protect your seed
Store the mnemonic offline and in secure physical backups only.
Use hardware wallets
Isolate signing operations from network-attacked environments.
Avoid phishing
Never paste your seed into websites and double-check URL legitimacy.
Final takeaway

Cryptographic primitives provide guarantees, but human practice determines whether those guarantees are preserved. Keep keys safe.
