Phishing scenarios

Fake front-ends that mimic legitimate sites and ask for signatures, compromised social accounts advertising malicious links, and ads that lead users to lookalike DApps.

Example: A site prompts to approve a token allowance; the UI says approve 100 but the wallet shows a different, larger allowance to an unknown address.

Defensive measures
  • Educate users to never enter seed phrases and to verify URLs carefully.
  • Recommend hardware wallets for high-value interactions.
  • Provide official security advisories and a way to report suspected phishing quickly.
Key Takeaway
Phishing exploits human trust; technical controls plus ongoing user education reduce its impact.
