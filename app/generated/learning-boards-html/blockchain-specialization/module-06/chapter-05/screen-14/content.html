Module X › Chapter Y › Assessment: Spot the Discrepancy
Scenario
A DApp shows an approval for 100 tokens to the known exchange router. The wallet prompt, however, requests approval for 1,000 tokens to an unfamiliar address. This mismatch is the red flag.
Check 1
Compare wallet recipient and amount to the DApp display; always trust the wallet UI.
Check 2
Verify the page origin and URL for authenticity; look for typosquat domains or unexpected redirects.
Check 3
Inspect included third-party scripts or recent front-end changes that could indicate compromise.
Assessment conclusion
A mismatch most likely indicates phishing or a compromised front-end. Do not sign and follow the verification checklist before proceeding.
