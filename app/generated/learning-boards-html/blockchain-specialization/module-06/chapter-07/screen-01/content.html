Emerging Threats and the Future of Blockchain Security

Anticipate cryptographic, privacy, regulatory, and cross-chain risks so you can design resilient systems. This chapter connects research, protocol practice, and developer responsibilities.

Orientation

We focus on quantum-era cryptographic threats, post-quantum defenses, zero-knowledge privacy advances, bridge and interoperability risks, and the security impact of regulatory change.

Who should care

Developers, auditors, protocol designers, and compliance leads who need to plan upgrades and balance privacy with legal obligations.
