Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Bridge Risks
Comparison

Bridge attack surfaces

  • Validator or signer compromise
  • Smart contract logic errors in locking/minting flows
  • Assumptions about finality and replay protection across chains
Mitigations and examples
Use multi-party computation and threshold signatures to avoid single custody. Keep bridge logic minimal and rely on verifiable cryptographic proofs where possible. Require independent audits before mainnet deployment.
Concrete scenario

A bridge that mints assets on the destination chain after a small committee signs a message is vulnerable if that committee is compromised; threshold signing increases robustness.

Takeaway

Treat bridges as first-class security targets with explicit threat models and rigorous safeguards.
