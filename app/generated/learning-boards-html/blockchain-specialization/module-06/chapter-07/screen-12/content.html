Key Concepts — Part 1

Three compact cards to remember core terms.

Quantum Computing

A computing model that can run algorithms capable of breaking certain classical cryptographic assumptions.

Post‑Quantum Cryptography

Algorithms designed to be resistant to quantum attacks, often with differing performance and size trade-offs.

Zero‑Knowledge Proofs

Methods to prove statements without revealing underlying information, enabling privacy and succinct verification.

Summary

Hold these definitions as you evaluate design choices: they map directly to threats, defenses, and practical trade-offs for your projects.
