Timeline

Immediate, mid-term, and long-term actions

Immediate
  • Inventory crypto uses
  • Avoid unnecessary key exposure
  • Run audits on critical contracts and bridges
Mid-term
  • Prototype PQC hybrids and ZKP flows
  • Measure costs and client impacts
  • Establish upgrade governance
Long-term
  • Plan full PQC migration when standards allow
  • Maintain cross-chain audit programs
  • Invest in privacy-preserving compliance
Key Takeaway

Start with inventory and incremental prototypes; build governance and testing gates so you can migrate safely when standards mature.
