Ongoing Research

ZKPs and PQC in real systems

  • zk-Rollups: compress many transactions into one on-chain proof to scale while preserving integrity.
  • PQC candidates: often lattice-based schemes providing quantum resistance with trade-offs in size or CPU.
  • Hybrid designs: combine classical and PQC algorithms during transition windows.
Practical metrics
When evaluating schemes, measure prover time, proof size, verifier cost, signature footprint, and the impact on wallets and nodes.
Example

A rollup operator compares prover cost and latencies to decide whether to batch more transactions per proof or accept more frequent proofs with higher overhead.

Takeaway

Prototype and measure; migration to PQC or ZKP systems is driven by measured operational trade-offs, not by theory alone.
