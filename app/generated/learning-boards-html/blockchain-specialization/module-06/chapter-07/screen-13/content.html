Key Concepts — Part 2

Three practical terms to guide engineering decisions.

Interoperability Security

Security concerns when connecting chains; bridges require cryptographic rigor, multi-party custody, and audits.

Regulatory Landscape

Laws like KYC and AML shape identity and data decisions; use minimal on-chain data and privacy-preserving attestations where possible.

Language & VM Evolution

Toolchain changes affect semantics and safety; revalidation and audits are needed after updates.

Final takeaway

Security is continuous: combine cryptographic readiness, privacy-preserving design, interoperability safeguards, and active governance to stay resilient.
