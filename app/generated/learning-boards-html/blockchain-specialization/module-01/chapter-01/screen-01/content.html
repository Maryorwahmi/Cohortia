Introduction to Blockchain: The Genesis

Understand how a ledger shared across many participants uses cryptographic hashing and consensus to create a tamper-evident history. This chapter frames blockchain’s promise and its first real-world application: Bitcoin.

In this lesson
Define blockchain and its parts, trace origins to Bitcoin, and explore cryptography’s role.
Coming up
Hands-on hashing activity with a Python example to show how small changes break hashes.
Why it matters
Replaces single-point trust with verifiable, distributed records useful across finance, contracts, and records.
