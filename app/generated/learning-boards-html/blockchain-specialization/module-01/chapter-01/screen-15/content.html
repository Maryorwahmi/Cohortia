Immutability?
If you need irreversible audit logs, immutability is beneficial; otherwise it may add unnecessary constraints.
Who runs nodes?
Node governance affects resilience and privacy; specify participants early to shape the architecture.
Key management
Signatures depend on private keys. Good custody practices are critical to prevent unauthorized actions.
Final takeaway
Use the three pointers as a short decision checklist before selecting blockchain as a technical approach.
