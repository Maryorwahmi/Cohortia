What signatures do

Prove the signer’s authority by using a private key to generate a signature that can be verified by the public key, confirming both origin and integrity.

Example
Alice signs a transaction; nodes verify the signature before they accept it for the block.
Misconception
A signature proves possession of a key pair, not a legal identity by itself.
Key Takeaway

Digital signatures authenticate and protect transactions, but they depend on secure private key custody and correct verification by the network.

Practical notes
  • Signatures bind the signer to the transaction content.
  • Nodes reject transactions with invalid signatures.
  • Signature validity relies on key management practices outside the protocol.
