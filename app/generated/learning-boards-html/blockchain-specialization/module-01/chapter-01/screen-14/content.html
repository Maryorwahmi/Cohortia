Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Blockchain
A distributed, immutable ledger secured by cryptography that records transactions across a peer-to-peer network.
Block & Chain
Blocks contain transactions, timestamps, and a previous-hash pointer that links the ledger chronologically.
Cryptography
Hash functions and signatures secure integrity and authenticity, enabling trust without a central intermediary.
Summary
Distribution, cryptographic links, and consensus together create a tamper-evident history that supports verifiability and resilience.
