Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Centralized
Single authority, single server. Fast management, single point of failure.
Decentralized
Multiple authoritative parties; power spread but can collude.
Distributed
Many independent nodes, shared ledger copies, peer-to-peer validation.
Notice how the distributed card emphasizes many independent nodes keeping ledger copies. That property underpins security, censorship resistance, and resilience.
