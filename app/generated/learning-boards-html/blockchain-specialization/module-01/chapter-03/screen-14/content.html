Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Peer-to-Peer (P2P)
Nodes act as both clients and servers to relay and validate messages across the network.
Distributed Ledger Technology
Replicated ledgers across participants, without a central administrator.
Scalability Trilemma
Trade-off among decentralization, security, and scalability; improving two often impacts the third.
Final takeaway
Use these quick definitions to assess whether a design meets the practical needs for censorship resistance, resilience, or high throughput.
