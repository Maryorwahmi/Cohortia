Learning Objectives — Applied Outcomes

These outcomes describe what you should do with the knowledge: analyze systems, describe P2P flows, and judge trade-offs.

01 — Diagnose architecture
Identify evidence for centralized vs decentralized vs distributed designs and give a short justification.
02 — Explain P2P propagation
Walk through a transaction's broadcast and explain why nodes track seen messages to prevent loops.
03 — Articulate benefits
Connect ledger replication to resilience, auditability, and censorship resistance with a real scenario.
04 — Weigh trade-offs
Describe when centralization is acceptable and when decentralization is required despite added complexity.
