Benefits: Security and Resilience

Replicated ledgers and independent validation make distributed networks harder to attack and more tolerant of failures.

Security
  • Eliminates a single point of compromise.
  • Altering history requires controlling a majority of validating power.
  • Independent verification by many nodes deters tampering.
Example: an attacker must gain majority control of validators to rewrite blocks — a costly and often impractical attack on mature networks.
Resilience
  • Network continues when some nodes fail or go offline.
  • Corrupted nodes can be repaired by copying state from honest nodes.
  • Transparency supports independent audit and recovery.
Example: during partial outages, nodes on different networks or geographies keep confirming transactions, preserving service availability.
Key takeaway
Replication and independent validation raise the bar for attackers and keep the network running under failure.
Misconception
Many nodes do not guarantee security if they are centrally controlled by the same actor.
Question
Who operates the nodes, and are their incentives aligned with honest behavior?
