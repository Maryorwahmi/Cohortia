Comparison › Hashing vs Signing
Hashing (Integrity)
  • Purpose: detect changes to data.
  • How: fixed-size digest of content.
  • Property: deterministic, one-way, avalanche.
Use case: comparing stored block hashes to detect tampering.
Signing (Authenticity)
  • Purpose: prove origin and authorization.
  • How: private-key signature over a hash.
  • Property: verifiable with public key; non-repudiation.
Use case: confirming that a transfer was authorized by the account owner.
Common mistake
Treating either primitive as a substitute for the other. Use both for full security.
Check
When auditing a transaction ensure the hash matches the data and the signature verifies with the public key.
