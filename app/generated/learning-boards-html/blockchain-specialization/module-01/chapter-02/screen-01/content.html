Cryptographic Foundations: Hashing & Digital Signatures
Understand the mathematical tools that secure data integrity and authorization in blockchains.
In this lesson
You will learn how hashing produces fingerprints, how signatures prove origin, and why their interaction creates an immutable ledger.
Why it matters
These primitives let a distributed network detect tampering and trust transactions without a central authority.
Explore Key Topics
Hashes
Fixed-size fingerprints that detect any change to data through the avalanche effect.
Signatures
Proof of who authorized a transaction using private/public key pairs.
Key Safety
Losing or exposing a private key leads to irreversible loss or compromise.
