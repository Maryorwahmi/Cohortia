Real uses beyond digital art

Design patterns change by domain, but the primitives are ownership and verifiable provenance.

Gaming
Players own items, trade them, and carry value across marketplaces; ERC‑1155 is popular for mixed inventories.
Identity & Credentials
Certificates, badges, and credentials as verifiable tokens that a user controls without relying on a central authority.
Real‑world tokenization
Represent deeds or high‑value goods as tokens, but pair them with legal agreements and verifiable off‑chain records.
Music, Media & DAOs
Enable royalties, fan ownership, membership roles, and governance via NFTs tied to rights encoded off‑chain.
Key Takeaway
Match the legal and off‑chain data model to the on‑chain token for meaningful, enforceable ownership.
