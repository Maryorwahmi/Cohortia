Safety Notes

Avoid common NFT errors

Durability, contract security, and buyer verification are essential safeguards.

Decentralized storage
Pin media and metadata to IPFS or Arweave; don't rely on single servers.
Contract security
Use audited libraries, limit minting permissions, and test thoroughly on testnets.
Buyer verification
Verify contract addresses and collection provenance; high prices are not proof of authenticity.
Common Mistake
Storing metadata centrally or publishing unreviewed contracts leads to broken assets or exploits.
Key Takeaway
Plan storage and security before minting; verify contract provenance when buying.
