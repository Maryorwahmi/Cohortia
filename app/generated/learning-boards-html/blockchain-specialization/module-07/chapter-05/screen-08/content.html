Process Flow

Minting an NFT

Follow a repeatable sequence to ensure metadata permanence and secure minting.

Step 1
Create media & metadata
Prepare your image and a JSON metadata file with name, description, image, and attributes.
Step 2
Pin to IPFS
Upload and pin both media and JSON to a pinning service to obtain stable CIDs.
Step 3
Set tokenURI
Use ipfs://CID as the tokenURI in your deployment or mint call.
Step 4
Mint & verify
Call safeMint or mint on your contract, then check ownerOf and tokenURI on the testnet explorer.
Example
Pin my-nft-image.png and my-nft-metadata.json, then mint with tokenURI = ipfs://Qm...metadata_cid.
Common Pitfall
Minting before pinning risks broken or changed assets if the original host is altered.
Key Takeaway
Always pin content to decentralized storage first, then mint and verify.
