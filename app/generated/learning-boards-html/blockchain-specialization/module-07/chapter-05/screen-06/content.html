Comparison

ERC‑1155 vs ERC‑721

Match the standard to your asset mix and operational constraints.

ERC‑1155 strengths
  • Single contract manages many tokenIds.
  • Supports both fungible balances and unique items.
  • Batch transfers reduce gas costs.
ERC‑721 strengths
  • Simpler model for strictly unique assets.
  • Widespread tooling and marketplace support.
  • Clear per‑token ownership semantics.
Scenario
A blockchain RPG with currency, stacks of consumables, and unique legendary gear benefits from ERC‑1155 efficiency and flexibility.
Key Takeaway

Use ERC‑1155 for mixed asset collections and efficiency; use ERC‑721 for one‑off, highly curated items.
