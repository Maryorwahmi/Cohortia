Concept

NFTs power virtual property

Ownership of avatars, land, and items creates user assets and markets inside immersive platforms.

Virtual land
Parcels tokenized as NFTs represent scarce locations where users build and monetize experiences.
Wearables & Avatars
Avatar items are NFTs that can be owned and traded across marketplaces that support the same standards.
Economies & Markets
Scarcity and tradability create secondary markets and incentives for creators and users.
Interoperability
Cross‑platform portability requires shared standards and agreements; without them assets can be locked to one world.
Key Takeaway
NFTs enable virtual ownership, but platform cooperation and economic design determine real portability and user value.
