Hands‑On Activity

Deploy ERC‑721 on Sepolia

Follow this checklist to complete the lab and confirm your minted NFT.

Prerequisites
Node, Hardhat, MetaMask on Sepolia, testnet funds, and a pinning service account.
Steps
  1. Initialize project and install OpenZeppelin and Hardhat plugins.
  2. Prepare media and metadata, pin to IPFS.
  3. Configure .env and deploy with Hardhat to Sepolia.
  4. Call mint with tokenURI and verify token on a testnet explorer.
Verification checklist
  • Confirm contract address and owner match deployment output.
  • Check tokenURI resolves to metadata JSON pinned on IPFS.
  • Ensure token image CID appears in the metadata image field and renders in marketplaces once indexed.
Pro Tip
Use a separate testnet account for deployments and never reuse mainnet keys during development.
