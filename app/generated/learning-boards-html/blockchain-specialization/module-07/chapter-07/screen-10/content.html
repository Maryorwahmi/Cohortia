Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Sustainability
Proof-of-Work
Energy-intensive mining; security emerges from computational cost but consumes high electricity.
Proof-of-Stake
Validators stake tokens instead of mining; major energy savings but potential stake centralization risks.
Scaling & Layer 2
Layer 2 solutions offload transactions to reduce mainnet energy per transaction while preserving security through batch settlement.
Key Takeaway

Choose consensus and scaling solutions that balance energy efficiency with decentralization and economic incentives.
