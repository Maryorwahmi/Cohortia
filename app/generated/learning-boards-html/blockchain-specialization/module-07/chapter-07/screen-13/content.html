Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Safety Notes
Common mistakes
  • Poor governance thresholds and reward structures.
  • On-chain exposure of personal data for SBTs without revocation.
  • Ignoring compliance and legal obligations across jurisdictions.
  • Premature adoption of unvetted cryptography.
  • Switching consensus without modeling centralization risk.
Practical checklist
  1. Minimum proposal stake and quorum rules.
  2. Privacy-first SBT designs and off-chain proofs.
  3. Legal review for token classification and KYC obligations.
  4. Plan hybrid cryptography and staged upgrades.
Example
Before launching governance, run simulations of token distributions and proposal outcomes, and require a minimum quorum to validate the governance model.
Key Takeaway

Combine design, legal, and cryptographic safeguards and deploy upgrades incrementally after testing.
