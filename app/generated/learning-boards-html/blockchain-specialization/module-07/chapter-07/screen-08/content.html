Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Quantum Risk
Key algorithms
Shor's algorithm threatens public-key cryptography; Grover's algorithm reduces the security margin of hash functions.
Practical implications
  • Potential recovery of private keys from public keys.
  • Faster collision or preimage attacks on hashes.
  • Need for post-quantum signature and hash schemes.
Practical preparation
Monitor standards for quantum-resistant algorithms, plan upgrade mechanisms, and avoid untested custom cryptography.
Common Mistake

Rushing custom cryptographic changes without standards or interoperability can create more risk than waiting for vetted post-quantum solutions.
