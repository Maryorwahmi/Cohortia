Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › DAO Process
Proposal Lifecycle
Create proposal (eligibility threshold)
Publish and open voting window
Vote (token-weighted, delegated, or quadratic)
Quorum & tally check
Execute or schedule execution
Mitigations
  • Minimum stake to propose, proposal costs to deter spam.
  • Quorums and delay windows for review.
  • Delegation with transparent accountability.
Concrete example
Imagine a DAO requiring 20% quorum and majority weighted vote to change fees. If a single holder has 40% of tokens, they can pass changes alone; mitigation could be quadratic voting or multi-sig safelocks for critical changes.
Common Mistake

Equating token distribution with decentralization ignores turnout and delegation mechanics that determine real influence.
