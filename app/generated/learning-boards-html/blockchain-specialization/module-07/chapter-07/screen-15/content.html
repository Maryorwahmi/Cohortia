Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Key Concepts
Quantum Computing
A computing model that could break certain classical cryptographic assumptions.
Quantum-Resistant Cryptography
Algorithms designed to remain secure in the presence of quantum attacks.
Proof-of-Stake (PoS)
A consensus mechanism where validators are selected based on staked tokens, reducing energy use.
Final takeaway
Combine standards-guided cryptographic planning, governance safeguards, and sustainable consensus choices to build resilient, long-lived blockchain systems.
