What is an SBT?

A non-transferable token permanently linked to a wallet, intended to represent credentials, reputation, or membership.

Use cases
  • Certification and academic credentials.
  • Reputation or contribution badges for DAOs.
  • Access and moderation privileges tied to identity.
Privacy considerations
Sensitive attributes may be stored off-chain with on-chain proofs; issuers must support revocation and consent models.
Analogy
Think of SBTs like certificates pinned to your identity that cannot be transferred, unlike collectible trophies that can be traded.
Common Mistake

Putting sensitive personal data directly on-chain without consent or revocation is risky and hard to correct.

Key Takeaway

SBTs can enable reputation systems, but require issuer trust models and privacy protections to be safe.
