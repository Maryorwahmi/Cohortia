Core compliance areas
  • Consumer protection from fraud and manipulation.
  • AML/KYC measures to prevent illicit finance.
  • Tax reporting and classification of tokens.
  • Securities rules for token offerings.
  • Stablecoin transparency and reserves.
DAO legal questions
DAOs face uncertain legal status: whether they are recognized as entities, who bears liability, and how contracts are enforced varies widely.
Scenario
A start-up issuing governance tokens must decide if tokens are securities, implement KYC for investors, and design disclosures to meet local rules. Legal advice is required before public launches.
Why this matters

Regulatory uncertainty can block institutional participation and create legal risk for projects that ignore compliance requirements.
