Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Research Trends
Active research areas
  • Verifiable delay functions for randomness and leader election.
  • Fully homomorphic encryption for private computation.
  • Post-quantum cryptographic algorithms and standards.
  • New consensus mechanisms balancing throughput and decentralization.
Integration path
Adopt modular designs: Layer 2 for scale, hybrid signatures for quantum transitions, and privacy modules where needed. Test on testnets before mainnet rollout.
Design note

Prefer incremental upgrades and community-tested primitives when adopting cutting-edge research.
