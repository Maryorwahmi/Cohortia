Practical Applications
Concrete domains where permissioned ledgers provide measurable benefit.
Supply chain
Track provenance, certify authenticity, and speed recalls while controlling who sees sensitive commercial data.
Trade finance
Streamline letters of credit and multi-party settlement by sharing verified documents and state.
Healthcare
Share patient records and manage drug provenance with strict access controls for privacy and compliance.
Digital identity
Create verifiable credentials to simplify KYC and reduce repeated identity verification processes.
Case note
IBM Food Trust is an example of using Fabric channels and ledgers to share provenance among trusted parties while preserving necessary confidentiality.
Integration reminder
Applications rely on data quality, IoT instrumentation, identity systems, and governance to realize benefits beyond the ledger itself.
Key Takeaway
Focus on multi-party coordination problems where immutability, provenance, or shared auditability solve a real pain point.
