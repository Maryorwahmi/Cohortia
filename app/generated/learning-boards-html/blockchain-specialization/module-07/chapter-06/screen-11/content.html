Read → Write → Own
How ownership and decentralization reshape digital experiences.

Web3 gives users cryptographic ownership of assets and greater control over identity and data. DApps interact with blockchains and decentralized storage to enable these experiences.

For enterprises, Web3 can unlock tokenized business models and transparent provenance, but it requires careful design for compliance and data control.

Example: a digital asset issued as a token can be traded on external marketplaces, unlike a platform-locked license in Web2.

DApp note
DApps provide a user interface for interactions with decentralized protocols; their backend logic may call both public and permissioned networks depending on privacy needs.
Common misconception
Web3 does not mean everything must be public; hybrid architectures often combine permissioned ledgers for sensitive data with public attestations for verification.
Key Takeaway
Web3 introduces verifiable ownership and decentralized primitives; enterprises should evaluate hybrid approaches to balance privacy and public verification.
