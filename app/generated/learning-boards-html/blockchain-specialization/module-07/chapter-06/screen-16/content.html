What to carry forward
Permissioned models
Private for single org control; consortium for shared governance and privacy among peers.
Fabric vs Corda
Fabric: channels and chaincode. Corda: need-to-know sharing and notaries for uniqueness.
Web3 hybrid patterns
Combine permissioned ledgers for private operations with public attestations where verifiability is needed.
Reflective question
Which network model best preserves confidentiality and auditability for your use case, and what governance steps would you take first?
