Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Opportunities
Where Web3 Adds Value
Automation, transparency, tokenization, and trusted interactions.
Transparency & Audit
Immutable records simplify audits and regulatory reporting across parties.
Automation
Smart contracts can automate conditional workflows and reduce reconciliation.
Tokenization
Represent assets digitally for fractional ownership and new liquidity models.
Business example
Automated payment release in trade finance once verified shipment events are recorded on a shared ledger reduces disputes and settlement time.
Key Takeaway
Prioritize pilots that show measurable efficiency, improved auditability, or new revenue possibilities before broad rollouts.
