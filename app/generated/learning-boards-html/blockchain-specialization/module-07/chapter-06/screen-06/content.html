Hyperledger Fabric Architecture
Modularity for identity, consensus, privacy, and chaincode execution.
Permissioned Membership
All participants are authenticated, which supports regulatory and audit requirements.
Pluggable Consensus
Choose algorithms that match performance and fault-tolerance needs.
Channels
Private communication lanes that confine transaction visibility to authorized participants.
Chaincode
Smart contracts written in general-purpose languages to define business rules.
Example
An international supply chain uses channels to let manufacturers, shippers, and retailers share provenance and compliance records only with relevant partners, preserving pricing confidentiality.
Design note
Plan channel topology and chaincode ownership early, because privacy boundaries are baked into network configuration and upgrade processes.
Key Takeaway
Fabric is built for enterprise governance: channels and modular components let organizations tune privacy and performance to business needs.
