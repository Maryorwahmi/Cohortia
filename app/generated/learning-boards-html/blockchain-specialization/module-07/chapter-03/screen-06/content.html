Comparison › SNARKs vs STARKs
ZK-SNARKs
  • Succinct proofs: small, fast to verify on-chain.
  • Non-interactive: single proof generation.
  • Requires a trusted setup; security depends on destroying secret parameters.
ZK-STARKs
  • Transparent: no trusted setup ceremony required.
  • Scalable: proof costs grow favorably with big computations.
  • Proofs are typically larger, increasing on-chain verification cost.
Decision checklist
  1. Is gas cost critical? If yes, prefer succinct proofs.
  2. Can you perform a secure trusted setup? If not, prefer transparent proofs.
  3. Does the proof need to cover very large computation batches? STARKs scale better.
Key Takeaway

Match proof primitive to your trust model and cost constraints: succinctness versus transparency and scale.
