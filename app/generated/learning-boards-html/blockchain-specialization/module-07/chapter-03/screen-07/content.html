How CTs work

Commitments bind to an amount without revealing it. Range proofs demonstrate that the hidden amount lies within a valid range so arithmetic checks remain meaningful.

Components
  • Pedersen commitments to hide values.
  • Range proofs to show non-negativity and bounds.
  • Verification ensures sum(inputs) = sum(outputs) even when values are hidden.
Example
A private payment publishes commitments and range proofs; anyone can verify totals match but cannot see individual amounts.
Trade-off
Confidential transactions increase transaction size and computational overhead due to commitments and range proofs.
Privacy note
CTs hide amounts but must be combined with address or routing protections to achieve stronger anonymity.
Key Takeaway

Use commitments plus range proofs to hide amounts while keeping monetary conservation provable.
