Process Flow › ZKP Applications
ZK-Rollups
Batch many off-chain transactions and post a single proof to Layer 1 to confirm correctness, saving on-chain capacity while retaining security.
Private Smart Contracts
Keep certain inputs or computations private, such as sealed bids or private balances, while still enabling public verification of outcomes.
Identity & Compliance
Prove attributes like age or residency without revealing identity documents, useful for privacy-preserving KYC checks.
Verifiable Computation
Prove that a computation was performed correctly off-chain without revealing inputs or intermediate steps, useful for heavy data processing.
Key Takeaway

ZKPs are a versatile primitive: they enable privacy, scale, and verifiable off-chain work when circuits and proof systems are designed for the intended task.
