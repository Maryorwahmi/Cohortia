Privacy-Preserving Blockchains
Zero-Knowledge Proofs and Confidential Transactions explained for practical DApps and finance.
In this lesson

Why public ledgers leak sensitive data, how ZKPs let you prove statements without disclosure, trade-offs between SNARKs and STARKs, and how confidential transactions hide amounts while preserving balance checks.

Coming up

Hands-on workflow for a simple ZK-SNARK proof, real-world use cases, and common mistakes to avoid when designing circuits or running a trusted setup.

Key Takeaway

Privacy on chain is possible by proving properties instead of publishing data. Choose the proof system that fits your performance and trust constraints.

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Confidential Transactions

Applications
ZK-rollups, private contracts, identity checks
Trade-offs
Proof size, transparency, verification cost
Explore Key Topics
ZKPs
Prove statements without disclosing the underlying secret, useful for private voting or compliance checks.
SNARKs vs STARKs
SNARKs are succinct and small; STARKs avoid trusted setup and scale differently. Choose by trust and cost trade-offs.
Confidential Tx
Commitments and range proofs hide amounts while ensuring inputs equal outputs.
