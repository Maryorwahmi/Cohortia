Master
Progress from recognizing privacy needs to building and evaluating privacy-preserving designs, using proofs and commitments where appropriate.
Milestone actions
  • Explain a ZKP with an everyday analogy.
  • Sketch a circuit for a simple arithmetic relation.
  • Choose SNARK or STARK and justify based on setup and gas costs.
  • Design a confidential transaction flow using commitments and range proofs.
How you will apply
Use proofs for private auctions, attribute checks for KYC, batch verification in rollups, or to protect payment amounts in finance and supply chain scenarios.
Verification
Demonstrate understanding by describing a ZKP workflow and explaining the trusted setup trade-offs and confidentiality guarantees for your chosen design.
