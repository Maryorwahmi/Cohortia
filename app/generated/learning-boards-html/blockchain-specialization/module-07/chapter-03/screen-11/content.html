Safety Notes › Common Mistakes
Circuit correctness
Ensure constraints exactly encode your claim. Use unit tests and auditors to catch mistakes that can leak data or permit forgery.
Trusted setup care
For SNARKs, treat the setup ceremony as a high-risk event. Use multi-party contributions and publish proofs of correct execution.
Understand guarantees
Catalog what your system keeps private and what remains public to avoid accidental exposure through assumptions.
Metadata risks
Even with hidden values, timing, address reuse, and other metadata can be correlated to deanonymize users. Plan mitigations.
Key Takeaway

Privacy depends on correct circuits, secure setup, and attention to metadata — build those safeguards first.
