How ZK-rollups work

Aggregate off-chain transactions and produce a zero-knowledge proof that the state transition is valid. Submit the proof to L1 for immediate verification.

Concrete example
zkSync and StarkNet verify state transitions with ZK proofs, enabling faster withdrawals without a challenge window.
Trade-off
Proof generation is computationally heavy; engineering complexity increases for non-trivial contract logic.
Why choose ZK
Fast finality and strong cryptographic assurance make ZK-rollups ideal for applications needing immediate settlement.
Key Takeaway

ZK-rollups trade higher computational cost for near-instant finality and cryptographic certainty of state transitions.

Developer implications
Assess proof-generation cost and tooling: complex contracts may require specialized proving pipelines.
Plan for node resources or third-party proving services to avoid long delays in producing proofs.
Validate that the rollup supports your contract patterns or consider porting logic to supported primitives.
