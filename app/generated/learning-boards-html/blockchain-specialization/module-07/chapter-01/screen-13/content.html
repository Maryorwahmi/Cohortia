Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Common Mistakes
Mistake: Security Assumptions
Assuming sidechains inherit parent-chain security. Confirm whether the solution posts data to L1 or uses independent validators.
Mistake: Unverified Bridges
Use only official, audited bridge contracts. Bridges are high-risk targets for exploits and social engineering.
Operational Safety
Log transaction hashes, monitor confirmations, and build UX that explains expected withdrawal windows to users.
Key Takeaway

Treat bridges and validator trust models as first-class parts of your threat model. Test and monitor continuously.
