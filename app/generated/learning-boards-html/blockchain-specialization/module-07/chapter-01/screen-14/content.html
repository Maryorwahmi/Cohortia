Blockchain Trilemma
A system can optimize for two of decentralization, security, and scalability; design accordingly.
Layer 1 Scaling
Sharding divides the base layer into parallel segments to increase throughput.
Layer 2 Scaling
Rollups, channels, and sidechains move execution off-chain and reduce Layer 1 load.
Summary

Anchor design decisions to these concepts to avoid costly architecture mismatches in production.
