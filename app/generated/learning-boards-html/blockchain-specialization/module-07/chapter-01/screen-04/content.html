Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › The Blockchain Trilemma
Definition

A network can aim for decentralization, security, or scalability, but maximizing all three is extremely difficult. Designers must prioritize.

Example
Bitcoin focuses on decentralization and security, yielding about 7 TPS. Ethereum 1.0 prioritized flexibility and security, reaching 15–30 TPS.
Misconception
The trilemma is not a fatal law; it is a design constraint that guides where to accept trade-offs when choosing architecture.
Why it matters
Selecting the wrong vertex causes UX, security, or cost failures in production systems.
Key Takeaway

Use the trilemma as an engineering checklist to map app requirements to protocol or layer choices.

Visual mnemonic
Reflect: For your app, which two properties are non-negotiable? That choice narrows the feasible architectures.
