Decision process
  1. List must-have properties (security, latency, cost).
  2. Eliminate options that violate any must-have.
  3. Rank remaining options by integration cost and user impact.
  4. Plan mitigations for known weaknesses (bridges, withdrawal windows).
Use-case examples
High-frequency trading → ZK-rollups for fast finality and provable correctness.
Gaming with many micro-transactions → sidechain or optimistic rollup for low fees and high throughput.
Public audit-heavy contracts → prioritize L1-based approaches like sharding to maintain decentralization.
Key Takeaway

Map requirements to trade-offs and include safety checks for bridges and validator sets before deployment.
