Safety Notes › Bridge Risks
Why bridges are targeted
Bridges often hold large pooled assets and mediate minting logic, creating concentrated risk surfaces attractive to attackers.
Typical vulnerabilities
Smart contract bugs, compromised multisigs, and flawed relayer or oracle logic.
Mitigations
Audits, distributed validator sets, nonce checks, multisig hardening, timelocks, and monitoring.
Developer checklist
Limit exposure, require audits, verify decentralization, and implement operational controls before integrating a bridge.
Scenario
If a bridge operator’s keys are stolen, attackers can bypass lock verification and withdraw or mint assets on the destination chain.
Key Takeaway
Treat bridges as complex systems; use layered defenses rather than a single safety control.
