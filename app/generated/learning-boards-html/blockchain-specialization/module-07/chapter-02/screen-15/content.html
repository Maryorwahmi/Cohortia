Key Concepts › Polkadot & Cosmos
Polkadot
A multi-chain network with a Relay Chain that provides shared security for parachains.
Relay Chain
The central chain that coordinates consensus and shared security in Polkadot.
IBC
Inter-Blockchain Communication protocol used by Cosmos to connect sovereign Zones.
Summary
Polkadot centralizes security; Cosmos centralizes a standard for messaging while preserving Zone sovereignty.
