Comparison › Trusted vs Trustless
Trusted Bridge
Relies on a central operator or small committee. Often faster and cheaper.
  • Single point of failure is the main risk.
  • Good for low-value or high-speed transfers with explicit trust.
  • Requires strong operational security from operators.
Trustless Bridge
Uses validator sets, proofs, or MPC to reduce reliance on single parties.
  • Lower trust assumptions but increased complexity.
  • May be vulnerable to smart contract bugs or oracle weaknesses.
  • Better suited when moving high-value assets across chains.
Concrete example
A gaming sidechain may choose a trusted bridge for speed, while a cross-chain DeFi protocol should prefer a trustless design or integration into a network that offers shared security.
Key Takeaway
Evaluate trust assumptions against your application’s financial exposure and user expectations.
