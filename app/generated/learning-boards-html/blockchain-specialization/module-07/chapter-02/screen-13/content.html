Integration pitfalls
Avoid sending funds to the wrong chain, trusting marketing over audits, and under-testing relayer failures.
Checklist
Confirm chain IDs, contract addresses, nonces, timelocks, and multisig controls before live usage.
Test scenarios
Simulate relayer outage, double deposits, and incorrect nonce replay to observe defense behavior.
Example
In a local Hardhat simulation, verify that deposit events emit the expected nonce and that withdraw calls on the destination reject already-processed nonces.
Key Takeaway
Operational discipline and conservative defaults protect users more than optimistic assumptions about third-party infrastructure.
