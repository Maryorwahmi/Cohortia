Comparison › Polkadot vs Cosmos
Polkadot
Shared security via Relay Chain.
  • Parachains inherit protection from a strong validator set.
  • Good when network-level defense is critical.
  • Less per-chain operational burden for validators.
Cosmos
Sovereign Zones connected by IBC.
  • Each Zone controls its own validators and governance.
  • Failures are localized to the compromised Zone.
  • Great for custom consensus and governance needs.
Developer question
Does your DApp prioritize inheriting strong network security, or controlling validator selection and governance for bespoke rules?
Key Takeaway
Match architecture to your primary threats and operational preferences.
