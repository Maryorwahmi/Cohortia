Blockchain Interoperability
How bridges, Polkadot, and Cosmos let chains exchange assets and messages.
This lesson will guide you through lock-and-mint bridges, trusted vs trustless designs, Polkadot's shared security with parachains and XCMP, and Cosmos's Zones and IBC approach to a decentralized Internet of Blockchains.
Why this matters
Interoperability allows DApps to combine strengths: secure value transfer, complex logic, and high throughput. Without it, each chain is a silo and composition is limited. Understanding models helps you pick safe, performant integration paths.
