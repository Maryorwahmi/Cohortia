Hands-on › Bridge Simulation
Conceptual steps
  1. Deploy a mock ERC-20 and Bridge on Chain A.
  2. Deploy a wrapped-token contract and Bridge on Chain B.
  3. Deposit tokens to Bridge A to lock funds and emit a deposit event.
  4. Relayer reads the event and calls withdraw on Bridge B to mint wrapped tokens.
  5. To return, burn wrapped tokens on Chain B and trigger unlock on Chain A.
Checks and cautions
  • Verify contract addresses and chain IDs before calling scripts.
  • Use nonces to prevent replay attacks and confirm event indexing.
  • Keep withdraw privileges under multisig or owner controls in the simulation to mirror production safety.
Example command snippets
// deployChainA.js: deploy token and bridge // deployChainB.js: deploy wrapped token and bridge // simulateBridge.js: read deposit event, call withdraw on Chain B
Key Takeaway
A local simulation clarifies sequence and trust assumptions without risking real funds; use it to validate your off-chain relayer logic and nonces.
