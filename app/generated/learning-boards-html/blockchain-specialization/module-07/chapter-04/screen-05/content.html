Process Flow
Automated Market Makers
Pools, reserves, and the constant product rule.
Step-by-step
  1. User wants to swap token A for B and sends token A to the pool.
  2. Pool increases token A reserve and decreases token B reserve.
  3. Price shifts to maintain x·y = k, creating slippage for larger trades.
  4. LPs collect a portion of fees proportional to share of pool.
Example
An ETH/DAI pool reduces ETH reserves as traders buy ETH, pushing price up relative to DAI.
Misconception
AMMs do not guarantee lowest price for large orders because slippage increases with trade size.

Key Takeaway

AMMs provide continuous liquidity algorithmically, but reserve sizes determine slippage and execution quality.
