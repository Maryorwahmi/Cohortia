Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Key Concepts
Automated Market Maker
Liquidity pools use a formula to provide continuous trading without an order book.
Decentralized Lending
Overcollateralized loans and algorithmic rates enable permissionless borrowing while maintaining solvency.
Oracles
Oracles feed off-chain data on-chain; decentralization of feeds is vital to resist manipulation.
Summary

Evaluate DeFi by economic incentives, smart contract quality, and the integrity of oracle data. These three factors determine protocol safety and utility.
