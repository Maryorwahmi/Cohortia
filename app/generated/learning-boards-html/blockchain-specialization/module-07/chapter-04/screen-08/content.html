Process Flow
Interest rate dynamics
Rates increase with borrowing pressure and fall as supply grows.
  • Borrow demand up → rates rise.
  • Supply up → rates fall.
  • Rates aim to clear the market by balancing incentives.
Liquidation lifecycle
Oracle reports price → protocol re-evaluates collateral → positions below threshold are liquidated to protect lenders.
Liquidation penalties and auction or direct sale mechanisms vary by protocol.
Example and caution
If a borrower’s collateral drops due to price moves, a fast oracle update can trigger liquidation. Slow or manipulable oracles can either delay needed liquidations or enable attacks that force unnecessary ones.
Pro tip
Check oracle refresh intervals and liquidation penalty parameters when auditing a market.
Common mistake
Assuming slow oracle updates always protect borrowers; they can delay protection for lenders or allow stale price exploitation.
