Liquidity Providers vs Holding

Comparison cards
Holding
Keep token A and B outside pool; value tracks market price directly.
Providing liquidity
Earn fees but reserves rebalance, exposing you to impermanent loss if prices diverge.
Example
Deposit equal value of two tokens. If one token rises sharply, your pool share will contain less of the appreciated token and more of the depreciated token.
Visual: holding vs LP outcome
When fees offset IL
High trading volume and fees can make LPing net profitable even with price divergence.
When IL dominates
Volatile tokens with low trade volume can leave LPs worse off than simple holding.
Key Takeaway

Impermanent loss is intrinsic to AMMs; compare expected fees to volatility before becoming an LP.
