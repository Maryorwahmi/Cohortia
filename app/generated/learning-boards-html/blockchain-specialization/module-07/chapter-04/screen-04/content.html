Core Concept
What is DeFi?
An ecosystem of permissionless financial contracts.
Principles
  • Permissionless access: no centralized approval needed.
  • Composability: protocols can call one another like building blocks.
  • Open-source: code and rules are inspectable.
Analogy
A decentralized bank built from code: rules replace human intermediaries.
Common mistake
Decentralized does not mean risk-free; code and composition create new failure modes.
Concrete example
Supply tokens to a lending pool and earn interest without a bank. Your funds are secured by smart contract rules and overcollateralization.
Why it matters
DeFi reduces gatekeeping and enables global access to financial services, but requires strong design and audits to manage risk.
Key Takeaway

DeFi swaps intermediaries for code; judge systems by economic incentives and code quality.
