Definition
Oracles: The Data Bridge
How off-chain data reaches on-chain contracts.
Why oracles matter
  • Provide price feeds for lending collateral valuation.
  • Supply event outcomes for insurance and derivatives.
  • Enable cross-domain integrations that depend on real-world inputs.
Centralized vs decentralized
Centralized oracles are a single point of failure. Decentralized networks aggregate many sources to improve integrity and resistance to manipulation.
Example
Chainlink-style networks pull from many providers and aggregate to provide a robust price feed.
Common risk
Relying on a single oracle can enable price manipulation and severe protocol damage.
Key Takeaway

Secure DeFi depends on reliable, decentralized oracle design; always vet data sources and aggregation methods.
