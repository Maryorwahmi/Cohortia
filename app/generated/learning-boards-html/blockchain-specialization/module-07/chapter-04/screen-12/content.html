Common Risks & Safety Notes
Technical and economic failure modes to watch for.
Risk list
  • Smart contract bugs (reentrancy, unchecked transfers).
  • Oracle manipulation and stale feeds.
  • Rug pulls and privileged admin keys.
  • Composability: cascading failures from integrated protocols.
Practical mitigations
Use audits, decentralized oracle feeds, conservative collateral factors, and staged governance changes. For users, start with small allocations and prefer protocols with transparent operations.
Checklist
Has the protocol been audited? Does it use decentralized oracles? Are admin keys timelocked or renounced?
Red flag
Exceptional APY promises with opaque reward mechanics and single-author control.
Key Takeaway

Risk management in DeFi is multidisciplinary: combine secure engineering, robust oracles, and prudent economic design.
