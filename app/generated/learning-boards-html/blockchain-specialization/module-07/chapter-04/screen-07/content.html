Lending & Borrowing Protocols
Supply, borrow, collateral, and liquidations.
Core components
  • Supply pool: where lenders deposit assets.
  • Borrowers: lock collateral to take loans.
  • Oracle feed: supplies asset prices to compute collateral value.
  • Liquidation mechanism: enforces solvency when thresholds break.
Concrete example
Aave or Compound-style markets let you supply DAI to earn interest while others borrow DAI by posting ETH. Overcollateralization and oracle price feeds determine safe borrowing limits and liquidation points.
Why it matters
Algorithmic rates and collateral rules make permissionless lending possible, but require accurate price feeds and sound liquidation logic.
Key Takeaway

Overcollateralization ensures lender safety, but accurate prices and secure liquidation paths are crucial to avoid cascading failures.
