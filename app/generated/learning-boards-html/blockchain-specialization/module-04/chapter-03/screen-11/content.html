Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Unbounded Loops
Risk
Denial-of-Service by gas exhaustion
A loop over a dynamically growing collection may eventually require more gas than allowed per block, causing permanent reverts.
Mitigation: pull payments let each user claim funds separately.
Mitigation: batch processing with explicit limits to control per-transaction work.
Example Scenario
A function that distributes rewards by iterating the entire registeredUsers array works fine at small scale but fails as the array grows. A safer design is to let users call claimReward(), or provide an owner-driven batched process that handles N users per call.
Check: when you design a loop, estimate worst-case gas and ensure it stays below block limits or use an alternative pattern.
