Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Conditionals
Use if/else to gate actions and pair with explicit errors for clarity.
Loops & Gas
For and while cost gas per iteration; avoid unbounded loops.
Error Handling
Prefer require/revert for preconditions; use custom errors to save gas on revert.
Summary

Design defensively: validate inputs, minimize on-chain work per transaction, and communicate failures in the most appropriate and efficient way.
