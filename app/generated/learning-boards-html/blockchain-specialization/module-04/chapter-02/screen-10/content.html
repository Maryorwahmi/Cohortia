Common mistakes

Public by default and unchecked calls

  • Defaulting to public exposes internal helpers and data; prefer private/internal.
  • Not checking low-level call results can mask failed transfers and leave inconsistent state.
  • Relying on block.timestamp for secure randomness is unsafe.
Warning

Avoid public unless necessary and always assert that transfers succeeded. Small checks prevent costly exploits.

Concrete fix
(bool success, ) = payable(owner).call{value: amount}('');
require(success, 'Transfer failed');
Try this: review your contract for public declarations and transfer code paths. Can you make visibility more restrictive and check every external call?
