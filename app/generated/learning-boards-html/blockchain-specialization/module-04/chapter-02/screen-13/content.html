Assessment tasks

Quiz (concept)
Question: Which visibility makes a function accessible to the contract and inheritors but not to external callers? Options: a) public b) private c) internal d) external. Correct: internal. Explain why in one sentence.
Practical (Remix)
Deploy SimpleWallet, deposit from one account, attempt withdraw from another, then withdraw from owner. Record the revert and successful transfer, and explain the role of msg.sender and msg.value.
Submission tip: include concise reasoning and the observed revert message to show mastery.
