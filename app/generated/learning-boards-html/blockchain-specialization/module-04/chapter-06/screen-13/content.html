Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Pull vs Push Payments
Push payments
Contract sends funds automatically, often in loops. Simple but can fail at scale or be blocked by failing external calls.
Pull payments
Users claim funds themselves. Keeps per-transaction gas bounded and avoids large loops that can cause DoS.
Concrete UI integration
Emit events on deposits so the frontend can notify users their balance is available and instruct them to call withdraw. This pairs observability with a safe payment flow.
