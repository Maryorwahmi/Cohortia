Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Front-running
What it is
An attacker observes a pending transaction and submits their own with higher gas to get mined first, exploiting info in the original transaction.
Mitigations
Use commit-reveal schemes or designs that do not expose sensitive values until after ordering is finalized.
Concrete design tip
If an action can be monetized by ordering, require a commit phase where users submit a hash, then reveal later. This prevents a miner or attacker from reacting to a visible value in the mempool.
Test idea

Simulate competing transactions with varying fees to observe whether ordering gives an attacker profit and adjust design accordingly.
