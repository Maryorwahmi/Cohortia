Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › What to Remember
Constructor args
Always forward required base constructor arguments to initialize state like owner or baseValue.
Use override
Explicitly mark overriding functions with override to satisfy the compiler and avoid ambiguity.
Pick the right tool
Interface = signatures only. Abstract = blueprint with state. Library = stateless utilities.
Final takeaway

Design your contract hierarchy intentionally: initialize bases, mark overrides, and choose interfaces or abstract contracts based on the need for state and helpers.
