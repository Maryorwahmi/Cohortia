Module 08 › Chapter 8.2 › Access Control
Comparison
Ownable

Single owner account. Simpler to implement and audit. Good for small teams or when a single admin is appropriate.

AccessControl

Defines roles and grants them to accounts. Better for multi-role projects and delegation of responsibilities.

Best practice
Emit events on grant/revoke, minimize privileged accounts, and consider timelocks for critical operations.
Concrete Example
Use Ownable to restrict a pause or upgrade function to a single multisig owner. Use AccessControl when separate teams manage pausing, upgrades, or other operational tasks.
Misconception
Role-based systems are always better. In truth, added complexity can introduce misconfigurations unless roles and grants are carefully audited.
Key Takeaway

Choose simplicity when possible and role-based permissions when responsibilities require separation and auditability.
