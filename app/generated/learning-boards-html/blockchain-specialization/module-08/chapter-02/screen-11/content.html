Why Natspec
Structured comments aid maintainers, auditors, and tools. They can describe side effects, permission requirements, and initialization behavior.
Example
/// @notice Sets the counter value. /// @dev Only the owner may call this. /// @param newValue The new counter value to set.
Best practices
  • Document permission model and initialization expectations.
  • Keep comments concise and intention-focused, not implementation repetition.
  • Use tags for params, returns, and custom notes about upgrades.
Key Takeaway

Good Natspec lowers onboarding cost, improves audits, and ensures upgradeers understand storage and initialization constraints.
