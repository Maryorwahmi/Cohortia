Scaling Options Overview
Understand the purpose and trade-offs of each scaling family to make an informed choice.
Rollups (Optimistic & ZK)
Bundle transactions off-chain and post proofs or state roots to L1 to reduce fees and increase throughput.
Sidechains
Independent chains with their own consensus offering lower fees but separate security assumptions.
State Channels
Private off-chain interactions between parties that settle final state on-chain, ideal for micropayments.
Selection Guide
If you need low fees
Consider rollups or sidechains.
If you need fast finality
ZK-rollups or sidechains with strong security assumptions.
If participants are fixed
State channels provide instant, private transactions.
Long-term network scaling
Sharding increases base-layer throughput but is a larger protocol-level change.
Key Takeaway

No one-size-fits-all: prioritize the security, cost, and UX properties most important for your DApp when choosing a scaling approach.
