Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts
Proxy Patterns
Upgrade logic while preserving address and state; secure upgrade authority.
Off-Chain Maintenance
Indexers, nodes, and front-ends need active monitoring and graceful degradation paths.
Security & Response
Combine monitoring, a practiced incident plan, and bug bounties to reduce risk.
Scaling Choices
Rollups, sidechains, and channels each balance security, cost, and complexity.
Governance
Timelocks, multisigs, and clear proposal practices protect users while enabling participation.
Operational Cadence
Scheduled audits, releases, and clear changelogs help teams iterate safely.
Summary

Sustainability requires both secure on-chain design and disciplined off-chain operations. Use monitoring, controls, and transparent governance to maintain trust.
