Process

Release Cadence, Audits, and Community Updates

Operational predictability reduces risk. Automate releases, schedule audits, and keep your community informed with concise updates.

Weekly
  • Health checks for nodes and subgraphs
  • Alert triage and backlog grooming
Monthly
  • Dependency audits and security reviews
  • Update documentation and release notes
Quarterly
  • External audits or third-party security reviews
  • DAO treasury reviews and governance health checks
Release Template

Include contract commit hash, front-end version, changelog, security notes, and whether a timelock is used. Keep the language clear for non-technical members.

Key Takeaway

A documented cadence and transparent updates reduce surprises and support healthy governance and user trust.
