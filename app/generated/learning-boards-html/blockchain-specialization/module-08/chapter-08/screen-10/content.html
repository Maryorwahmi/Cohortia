Security

Monitoring, Bounties & Incident Playbooks

Detect anomalies, triage reports, and practice your response so you can act confidently under pressure.

Monitoring Signals
  • Unexpected owner or upgrade calls
  • Large transfers or sudden balance drains
  • Subgraph indexing errors and RPC timeouts
  • Spike in failed transactions or reverts
Incident Playbook (example)
  1. Verify alert and gather transaction IDs.
  2. Activate emergency multisig or timelock pause.
  3. Notify stakeholders with a clear status update.
  4. Apply fixes or roll back via upgrade path if safe.
  5. Post-incident report and adjust monitoring rules.
Bug Bounty Setup

Define severity tiers, reproducibility criteria, and secure report channels. Include clear payout ranges and legal terms.

Triage Dashboard
Alert: Large transfer detected — tx 0xabc...123
Status: Investigating • Actions: freeze upgrades, notify multisig
Common Mistake

Delayed public communication increases speculation. Use a short, factual status and commit to regular updates.

Key Takeaway

Combine monitoring, practiced response, and community incentives. Preparation shortens mean time to recovery and preserves user trust.
