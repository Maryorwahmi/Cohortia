Governance

DAOs, Timelocks, and Token Incentives

Design governance so token holders can steer the protocol while protective controls prevent immediate risky changes.

Governance Components
  • Proposal creation rules and templates
  • Voting period and quorum thresholds
  • Execution guards: timelock, multisig, and veto mechanisms
Tokenomics Patterns

Reserve a treasury, define vesting for team tokens, and allocate community incentives to bootstrap participation and rewards.

Scenario

If a DAO votes to upgrade a contract, a timelock window lets users exit or raise objections, reducing the risk of rushed governance actions.

Common Misconception

On-chain voting alone doesn't guarantee good outcomes. Clear proposal framing, off-chain discussion, and transparent budgets are necessary for informed decisions.

Key Takeaway

Align incentives via vesting and treasury funds, and protect users with execution delays and multisig controls while empowering governance participation.
