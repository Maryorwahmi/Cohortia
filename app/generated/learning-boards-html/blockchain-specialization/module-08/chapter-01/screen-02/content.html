Evaluate and choose a blockchain

Design smart contract responsibilities

Define what must be immutable and what checks must run on-chain to protect value and users.

04
Plan off-chain and storage choices

Use IPFS for large assets and indexers for fast queries while keeping hashes and proofs on-chain.

Mnemonic
Remember the four-step checklist: Scope, Network, Contracts, Storage. Visualize them as concentric rings where only the center needs immutability.
Common misconception: More decentralization is always better. Reality: Apply decentralization where it adds value and use pragmatic off-chain services where needed.
