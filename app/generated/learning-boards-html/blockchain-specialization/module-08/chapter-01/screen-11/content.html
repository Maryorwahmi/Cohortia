Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Crowdfunding Activity
Activity instructions
  1. Choose a blockchain and justify why it fits the transaction model.
  2. List components: frontend, wallet, CampaignFactory, Campaign, IPFS, indexer.
  3. Sketch data placement: what is on-chain vs off-chain.
  4. Document persistence: who pins IPFS and who operates the indexer.
Starter topology
User Browser/Wallet → Frontend (React) → Smart Contracts (CampaignFactory, Campaign) → Blockchain. Off-chain: IPFS for descriptions and images, Indexer for listings and search.
Concrete example
Choose Polygon for low-cost pledges. Store campaign text and images on IPFS, pin them, and write hashes to contracts. Use an indexer to render campaign lists and totals quickly.
Misconception: Letting the indexer be the authoritative source. Always reconcile critical balance and payout decisions with on-chain state.
Try this: Annotate your sketch with who is responsible for persistence and how the frontend verifies on-chain state.
Common pitfall: Not planning for pinning costs; include pinning in your operating budget.
