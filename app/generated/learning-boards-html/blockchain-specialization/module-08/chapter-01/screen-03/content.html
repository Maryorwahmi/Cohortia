Learning Objectives — Skills in Action
Practical milestones that show you can plan and defend DApp design choices.
Milestone: Scoped requirements

Write a problem statement and list the exact on-chain guarantees needed. Example: tamper-proof voting tallies and censorship resistance.

Milestone: Network justification

Compare gas cost, throughput, and ecosystem tools. Example: choose Polygon for many low-cost interactions.

Milestone: Architecture sketch

Produce a diagram or list showing frontend, contracts, off-chain storage, and data flow for a sample app.

Milestone: Risk mitigation

Identify key risks like gas spikes and private key exposure and document mitigation strategies.

Visual mnemonic
Picture a layered cake: base is network choice, middle is smart contracts, top is the user-facing frontend, and side-channels represent off-chain storage and indexing.
Common mistake: Ignoring user experience around transaction finality. Solution: design retries, explain delays, and minimize non-critical on-chain actions.
