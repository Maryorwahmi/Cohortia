DApp Architecture
Layered design: contracts enforce value, frontends handle UX, off-chain services scale reads and storage.
Blockchain Selection
Balance throughput, fees, and ecosystem when picking a network for your transaction patterns.
IPFS Storage
Store large files in IPFS, keep content hashes on-chain, and ensure persistence via pinning.
Summary Takeaway

Design with purpose: isolate trust boundaries, document trade-offs, and keep on-chain code minimal and auditable.
