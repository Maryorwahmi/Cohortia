Practical checklist
  • Estimate gas per core flow and multiply by expected volume.
  • Check wallet and tooling support for your target audience.
  • Consider security history and how upgrades are handled.
Concrete exercise
Calculate monthly gas costs for pledge, refund, and payout flows on two networks to see which fits your budget and UX goals.
Example
A crowdfunding DApp with frequent small pledges benefits from a Layer 2 with low fees so users are not priced out. If your app is settlement of large value transfers, a secure mainnet may be preferable despite higher fees.
Misconception: Picking a fast chain always improves UX. Contrast: if wallets are scarce or tooling is immature, onboarding and maintenance costs may outweigh raw transaction speed.
Pro tip: Prototype a single transaction flow and measure actual latency and cost before committing.
Warning: Ignoring developer ecosystem size can increase time to market due to missing libraries or docs.
