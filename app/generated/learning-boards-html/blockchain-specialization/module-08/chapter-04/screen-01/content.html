Testing Smart Contracts and DApps
Strategies, frameworks, and practices to ensure safe, efficient, and maintainable on-chain code.
Why this matters

Smart contracts are immutable and often custody value. Tests are the primary safety net against logic errors, access flaws, and unexpected edge cases.

What you'll learn
  • Unit tests with Hardhat and Mocha/Chai for isolated functions.
  • Integration tests for contract-to-contract and frontend scenarios.
  • Gas measurement and TDD workflow for smart contracts.
