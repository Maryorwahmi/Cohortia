Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Objectives — Continued
Positive Cases
Assert expected successful operations like transfers or increments. These confirm intended behavior.
Negative & Edge Cases
Test reverts, boundary values, zero states, and unauthorized calls to avoid catastrophic failures.
Mocking & Isolation
Replace oracles and external contracts with deterministic mocks and reset the chain between tests for consistency.
Example Milestone
A token test suite that verifies initial supply, transfer success and failure, approval flows, transferFrom behavior, and event emission.
