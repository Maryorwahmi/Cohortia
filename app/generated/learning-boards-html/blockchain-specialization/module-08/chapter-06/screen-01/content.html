Deployment Strategies for Smart Contracts and DApps
Practical steps for moving from development to a public network, verifying your source, and publishing a frontend with secure practices.
Why this chapter matters
A secure, verified deployment makes your contract auditable and your DApp reachable. Mistakes at this stage can be irreversible on mainnet.
What you will do
Configure Hardhat, write a deployment script, manage keys with environment variables, verify on Etherscan, and publish a frontend to IPFS or a host.
In this lesson
Smart Contract Deploy
Signer, factory, and deploy flow for on-chain contracts.
Verification
Use Hardhat plugins to publish source to explorers.
Frontend Hosting
IPFS for decentralization or traditional hosts for convenience.
