Community & Documentation

Make community your safety net

Consistent communication, accessible docs and public timelines reduce panic and speed recovery.

Engagement pillars
  • Channels: Chat, governance forum, public issue tracker.
  • Docs: Clear upgrade policy, FAQs, and changelogs.
  • Transparency: Release notes, risk statements, and timelines.
Scenario
Publish a short upgrade summary with the rationale, the multisig threshold, and the timelock window. This guides auditors and gives users a decision window.
Documentation checklist
  1. Governance process description and voting thresholds.
  2. Multisig custody policy and signer rotation plan.
  3. Incident response playbook with contact points and actions.
  4. Upgrade compatibility and storage layout notes.
Key Takeaway
Clear, public documentation turns governance events from surprises into expected, auditable processes.
