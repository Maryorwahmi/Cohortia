Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Key Concepts

What to Remember

Three compact concept cards to anchor your post-deployment decisions.

Decentralized Autonomous Organization
A community-led body where token holders vote on proposals that shape protocol evolution.
Multisig & Timelock
Multi-signer approvals and enforced delays reduce the risk of reckless or compromised upgrades.
Incident Response Plan
A documented, rehearsed set of steps for detection, containment, communication and recovery during incidents.
Summary

These concepts work together: governance chooses direction, authorization enforces safety, and response plans preserve user trust.
