Maintenance & Analytics

Choose tools that cover alerts, indexing, and debugging

A layered toolset gives early warnings and deep post-incident insights.

Side-by-side comparison
Tenderly
Transaction simulation, state debugging, and rich failure traces for developers to reproduce and fix issues.
Blocknative
Real-time transaction monitoring and mempool visibility to catch pending issues before they confirm.
Web Analytics
Google Analytics or Mixpanel help track frontend usage, but consider privacy-preserving options for Web3 users.
Maintenance cadence
  • Daily: Alert triage, failed transaction review, and health checks.
  • Weekly: Dependency updates, monitoring rule reviews, and index maintenance.
  • Monthly: Audit scheduling, test coverage checks, and community reports.
Common Mistake
Treat monitoring as one-off. Without maintenance, alert rules drift and you miss regressions.
