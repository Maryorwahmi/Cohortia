Incident Response

Containment, communication, and recovery

Prepare roles and scripted actions so your team can move fast when seconds matter.

Incident playbook steps
  1. Detect: Automated alerts identify anomalies for human review.
  2. Triage: Assign leads and classify severity.
  3. Contain: Use pause mechanisms or circuit breakers where available.
  4. Communicate: Publish an initial notice with verified facts and what users should do.
  5. Recover: Test fixes, propose upgrades, and restore services once safe.
Example checklist for a critical bug
Immediate: Notify multisig signers, trigger pause if supported, gather logs and block numbers.
Short-term: Open incident thread, engage auditors, and prepare hotfix branches.
Medium-term: Create upgrade proposal, test on testnet, and coordinate governance vote if needed.
Pro-tip
Keep a list of trusted auditors and a pre-approved multisig quorum list so execution is rapid when time is critical.
