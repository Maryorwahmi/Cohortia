What to Remember

Three practical reminders to operationalize the chapter's lessons.

Monitoring & Analytics
Continuous telemetry and alerts let you detect failures and performance regressions quickly.
Proxy Pattern
Swap logic contracts while keeping state and addresses stable, enabling safe upgrades when paired with checks.
Community Engagement
Transparent communication and clear docs allow the community to participate and trust governance outcomes.
Final Thought

Combine monitoring, upgrade patterns, and community processes to create a DApp that can evolve securely and transparently.
