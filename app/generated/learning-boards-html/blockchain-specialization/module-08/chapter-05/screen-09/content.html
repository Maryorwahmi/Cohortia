Comparison
Front-running vs Denial of Service
Both exploit execution semantics: one by ordering, the other by forcing failures or gas exhaustion.
Front-running
Attackers reorder transactions to profit. Mitigations include commit-reveal, batch processing, and on-chain time locks.
Denial of Service
A participant or external call causes operations to fail or become prohibitively expensive. Use pull payments, gas-safe loops, and handle external failures gracefully.
Concrete change
Replace loops that push funds to many addresses with a withdrawal pattern where recipients claim funds. This avoids a failing recipient halting the whole process.
Misconception check
Do not assume honest participants or stable gas conditions. Design as if the worst ordering and failing external calls will happen frequently.
Key Takeaway

Prefer pull payments, add defenses against ordering manipulation, and make external calls non-critical to availability.
