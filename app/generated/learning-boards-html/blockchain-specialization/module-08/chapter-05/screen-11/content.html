Process Flow
Designing a Vulnerability Program
A continuous loop: discover, triage, fix, disclose, and monitor.
Bug bounty essentials
  • Clearly defined scope and in-scope contracts.
  • Severity tiers with example impacts and reward ranges.
  • Safe harbor and disclosure rules.
Monitoring & incident playbook
Set alerts for abnormal activity, prepare multisig pause or upgrade actions, and rehearse communication steps with stakeholders and reporters.
Triage example
When a researcher reports a reentrancy vector, confirm it with a test case, assign severity, and schedule a patch. Coordinate a disclosure timeline and optionally offer a temporary mitigation such as pausing functions via multisig until a full fix is deployed.
Misconception check
Bounties are not a substitute for audits or tests; they widen review coverage but depend on clear scopes and incentives to be effective.
Key Takeaway

A mature program combines incentives, clear reporting, and active monitoring so you detect and respond quickly to issues in production.
