How fees are determined
Fees are set by the sender as inputs minus outputs. Miners prefer transactions paying higher fee rates per byte. The transaction byte-size depends on number of inputs, outputs, and script complexity.
Small tx
Fewer inputs, lower byte size, lower fee for same priority.
Large tx
Many inputs raise byte size and required fee.
Congestion
High demand pushes required fee rate up.
Practical advice
Consolidate small UTXOs during quiet periods, and check wallet fee suggestions. If you need fast confirmation, raise the satoshis-per-byte rate rather than just the absolute fee.
Mempool behavior
The mempool holds unconfirmed transactions. Miners select transactions from it based on fee rate; low-fee txs may be delayed or dropped during congestion.
Check your understanding
If two transactions both pay the same absolute fee but one is much larger in bytes, which will likely confirm faster? (Consider fee rate per byte.)
