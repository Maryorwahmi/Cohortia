Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
UTXO model
Discrete outputs are spent whole; include change outputs and verify balances.
Digital signatures
Private keys sign inputs; signatures validate ownership without exposing the key.
Fees & mempool
Fee rate per byte and network demand determine confirmation priority; many inputs mean higher fees.
Final takeaway

Inspect UTXOs, check signatures, and verify arithmetic before broadcasting. These three checks catch most construction errors.
