Understanding Bitcoin Transactions and UTXOs
How Bitcoin moves value: inputs, outputs, signatures, fees, and change
In this lesson

You will learn the UTXO analogy, how to build a valid transaction, how signatures authorize spending, and how fees and change outputs work in practice.

Why it matters

Understanding these mechanics prevents accidental loss, helps optimize fees, and is essential when reading raw transactions or designing wallets and contracts.

Common pitfall

Forgetting change outputs can turn leftover funds into extra fees. We'll show how to avoid that and verify your math.

Start here

Treat each UTXO like a bill. Spend specific bills, create change, and sign with your private key to authorize spending.
