Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Quick Reference
Verify arithmetic
Confirm inputs = outputs + fee before signing or broadcasting.
Check signatures
Every input needs a signature from the private key controlling the UTXO.
Manage fees
Consider satoshis-per-byte and mempool conditions; consolidate UTXOs during low-fee times if needed.
One-line summary

Treat UTXOs like bills, sign inputs, and pay fees appropriate to byte-size and urgency.
