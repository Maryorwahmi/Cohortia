Altcoin
Any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin; can be a fork, platform, or novel architecture.
Categories
Utility, stable, privacy, governance, and platform tokens each serve distinct needs and risks.
Fork
A protocol split that can create an independent chain and a new token; outcome depends on backing and usage.
Summary Takeaway

Use the checklist: type, security/backing, and community support to quickly evaluate any altcoin's purpose and likely resilience.
