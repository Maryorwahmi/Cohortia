Concrete Example
Rollups move computation off-chain to increase throughput while settling final state on the main chain. This reduces on-chain congestion but requires secure bridging and fraud proofs.
Common Pitfall
Assuming faster or newer always means better. New designs need audits, economic modeling, and a healthy developer community to succeed.
