Consensus models and trade-offs
Security, energy, and decentralization vary by design.
Proof-of-Work (PoW)
Security via computational cost. Attack cost depends on hardware and energy expenditure.
Proof-of-Stake (PoS)
Validators stake tokens as collateral. Security derives from economic penalties and slashing for misbehavior.
Delegated PoS and Variants
Delegated models elect a smaller set of validators to improve throughput, changing decentralization dynamics and governance needs.
Concrete Example
The Merge: Ethereum moved from PoW to PoS to lower energy use and open scaling paths. This changed validator economics and network upgrade governance.
Misconception
PoS is not inherently insecure; it requires different economic assumptions and governance to ensure honest participation and low centralization risk.
