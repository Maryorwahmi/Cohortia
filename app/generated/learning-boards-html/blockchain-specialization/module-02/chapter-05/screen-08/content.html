Stablecoin types
Understand backing, trust assumptions, and failure modes.
Fiat-collateralized
Reserves held in fiat; trust and audits determine reliability. Example: USDC-style model.
Crypto-collateralized
Backed by crypto with overcollateralization and automated liquidation. Example: DAI uses collateralized debt positions.
Algorithmic
Peg maintained by on-chain monetary policy. Success depends on strong economic design and market confidence.
Concrete Comparison
Assess: trust model, reserve transparency, collateral volatility, liquidation mechanisms, and governance that manages reserves and policy.
Misconception
A peg is not a guarantee; market stress can reveal hidden vulnerabilities if reserves or mechanisms fail.
Reflection
Ask: what happens to the peg if collateral falls sharply in value? The answer highlights systemic risk and the need for clear governance rules.
