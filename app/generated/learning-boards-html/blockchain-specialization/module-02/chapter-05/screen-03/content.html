Practice: Researching an Altcoin
A short, structured approach to evaluate any altcoin before forming an opinion or investment view.
Step 1
Find official docs and whitepaper to confirm purpose and claims.
Step 2
Identify consensus, token use, and whether smart contracts are supported.
Step 3
Compare to Bitcoin: speed, privacy, programmability, and governance.
Step 4
Note risks: liquidity, volatility, community strength, and potential pump-and-dump behavior.
