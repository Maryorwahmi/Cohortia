Altcoins and the Evolution of Cryptocurrencies
Explore how alternative cryptocurrencies extend Bitcoin's ideas into new functions and systems.
In this lesson
Define altcoin, learn major categories like utility and stablecoins, and see how forks and new consensus models led to fresh features.
Why it matters
Altcoins drove practical innovations—smart contracts, privacy tools, and faster confirmations—that shaped the broader crypto ecosystem.
Key Takeaway

Altcoins broadened blockchain use cases; understanding categories and trade-offs is essential before evaluating any project.
