Module 02 › Chapter 2.4 › Scams & Risks
Common scams
  • Giveaway scams that request sending funds first.
  • Phishing sites mimicking wallets or exchanges.
  • SIM swaps to intercept SMS 2FA.
  • Clipboard address swapping malware.
Practical defenses
  • Never reveal seed words to anyone or enter them on websites.
  • Use authenticator apps, not SMS, for two-factor authentication.
  • Verify addresses by copy-checking and sending small test transactions.
Concrete scenario
If you receive a message promising double your bitcoin for a small deposit, treat it as a scam. Independent verification will reveal it as fraudulent.
Misconception
Believing unsolicited support messages are genuine. Legitimate support will never ask for your seed phrase.
Warning

Treat any request for seed words or private keys as a security emergency. Do not comply and move funds if you suspect compromise.
