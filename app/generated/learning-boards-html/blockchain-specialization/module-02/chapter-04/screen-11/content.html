Module 02 › Chapter 2.4 › Seed Backup
Create and verify
Generate the seed on the device, write words in order, and verify by restoring to a test wallet. Confirm you can reproduce derived addresses before relying on the backup.
Avoid
Do not store the seed in cloud storage, photos, or password managers that can be breached.
Store
Use multiple physical locations like a safe and a safety deposit box to protect against single-point failures.
Advanced options
Consider splitting the seed into shares or using metal plates for fire resistance. Always document a recovery plan with trusted parties without revealing words.
Practical test
Use a dummy seed to practice recovery steps so you know the exact process before depending on a real backup.
Misconception
Assuming one copy is enough. Single backups create single points of failure; distribute securely to increase resilience.
Key Takeaway

Record your seed offline, store multiple secure copies, and run a dummy restore to prove your backup works without risking real funds.
