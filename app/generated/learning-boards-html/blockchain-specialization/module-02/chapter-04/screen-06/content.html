Custodial Wallets
Third-party services store and manage private keys for you. They offer convenience for trading but introduce risk if the service is hacked or freezes withdrawals.
Risk
Counterparty risk, limited recourse, possible regulatory restrictions.
When to use
Short-term trading, convenience, and services requiring custody.
Non‑Custodial Wallets
You hold the private keys or seed phrase. This gives full control and responsibility. If you lose keys, there is no central recovery mechanism.
Benefit
Complete ownership and lower counterparty risk; suitable for long-term holdings.
Practical tip
Use hardware wallets and offline seed backups for significant amounts.
Scenario
You plan to hold for years: non-custodial cold storage reduces dependency on a third party.
Misconception
Insurance equals full protection — not always. Policies vary and access may still be restricted.
Checkpoint
List who controls keys for each account you use, and decide an action for high-value holdings.
