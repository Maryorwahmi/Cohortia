Architecture

Seed phrase regenerates a whole key tree

A 12 or 24-word mnemonic encodes the master seed. Wallets use standards to derive a master key and then child keys deterministically. Backing up the seed backs up all derived keys.
Concrete example
Restore a 12-word dummy phrase in test mode to observe the same addresses appear, confirming the backup works.
Compatibility note
Different wallets may use different derivation paths; ensure compatibility if switching software.
Misconception
A seed phrase is not just a password. It is the deterministic origin of all your private keys and must be protected accordingly.
Practical exercise
Use a dummy seed to test recovery steps offline; verify derived addresses without revealing any real funds.
Key Takeaway
Securely writing and storing your seed phrase is the most important backup you can make for an HD wallet.
