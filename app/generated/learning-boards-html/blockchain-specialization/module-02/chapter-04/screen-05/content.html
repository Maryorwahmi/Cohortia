Module 02 › Chapter 2.4 › Hot Wallets
Definition

Hot wallets are internet-connected wallets

They make transactions fast but increase exposure to malware, phishing, and account compromise when private keys are accessible on connected devices.
Software wallets
Desktop and mobile apps that store keys on the device. Good for frequent use; vulnerable if device is compromised.
Web and exchange wallets
Hosted by third parties. Very convenient, but they control your keys and are subject to custodial risk.
Risk management
  • Keep only small sums in hot wallets for daily transactions.
  • Enable authenticator 2FA on all accounts interacting with your funds.
  • Regularly update wallet apps to apply security patches.
Concrete scenario
If a mobile wallet is used for coffee payments, keep just the amount you expect to spend that week on the device; the rest should be moved to cold storage.
Key takeaway
Use hot wallets for convenience, but accept the trade-off: increased attack surface. Separate funds by use-case to limit exposure.
