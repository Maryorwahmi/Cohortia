Module 02 › Chapter 2.4 › Key Concepts
Wallet = keys
A wallet manages private keys that prove spending authority; the blockchain stores the funds.
Hot vs Cold
Hot = internet-connected (convenient). Cold = offline (more secure). Match storage to risk and use-case.
Seed phrase
A 12 or 24-word mnemonic is the master backup for HD wallets. Protect it offline and test recovery with dummy data.
One-line summary
Control the private keys, protect the seed, separate hot and cold funds, and verify before you send — these four habits preserve access and reduce theft risk.
