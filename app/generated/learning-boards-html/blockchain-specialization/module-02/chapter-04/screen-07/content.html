Module 02 › Chapter 2.4 › Cold Wallets
Hardware wallets
Purpose-built devices that store private keys offline and sign transactions internally. They provide a strong balance of security and practical use for high-value storage.
Why use one
Private keys never leave the device, preventing exposure to malware on a connected computer.
Practical tip
Always verify device prompts and use official firmware updates installed from the manufacturer channel.
Paper wallets
A printed private key and address stored offline. While immune to remote hacks, paper is fragile and must be created and stored securely.
Risks
Loss, theft, environmental damage, or insecure generation can make paper wallets unsafe in practice.
Storage advice
Keep multiple offline copies in secure, separate physical locations like a safe and a safety deposit box.
Scenario
Long-term investor moves majority of holdings to a hardware wallet and keeps a paper backup of the seed in a safe deposit box.
Misconception
Assuming any offline copy is enough. Durability and secure generation are crucial to preserve access.
