Bitcoin Wallets, Keys, and Security Best Practices

Understand how wallets manage cryptographic keys, why seed phrases are the ultimate backup, and how to choose storage based on risk and convenience.

In this lesson
Learn wallet types, how private and public keys relate to addresses, seed phrase recovery, hardware security, and common scams.
Why it matters
Losing a private key or exposing a seed phrase means permanent loss. Good practices preserve access and reduce theft risk.
Key Takeaway

A wallet does not hold coins; it holds keys. Protect the seed phrase and prefer hardware wallets for large holdings.
