Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Coinbase & Rewards
What the coinbase does
Creates the block subsidy and assigns it to the miner's address, and aggregates transaction fees that miners collect.
Example
A coinbase might award 6.25 BTC plus 0.003 BTC in fees to the miner who produced the valid block.
Why protocol rules matter
The coinbase follows consensus rules for subsidy and maturity. Newly minted coins often require confirmations before they enter general spending to avoid chain reorg issues.
Misconception
Coinbase outputs are not arbitrary payouts; they are constrained by consensus subsidy rules and maturity periods.
Key takeaway
Coinbase ties monetary issuance to mined work, making new coin creation conditional on valid PoW.
