Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Mining Hardware
Hardware progression
Miners evolved from CPUs to GPUs to FPGAs and now ASICs, each step increasing hashes per second and improving energy efficiency.
Why it matters
Higher efficiency lowers the cost of producing valid blocks and increases total network hashing, affecting difficulty.
Concrete contrast
A single ASIC rack can generate far more valid-hash attempts per second than dozens of consumer GPUs, making industrial operations dominant where electricity is cheap.
Misconception
Hashing is not proportional to time online; it's proportional to raw hashing throughput and energy efficiency.
Key takeaway
Hardware efficiency determines competitiveness and thus affects the network's total work and difficulty.
