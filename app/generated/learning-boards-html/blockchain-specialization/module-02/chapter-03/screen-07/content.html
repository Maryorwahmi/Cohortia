Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Rewards Comparison
Block reward
A predetermined amount of new bitcoins issued to the miner via the coinbase transaction. It halves roughly every four years, reducing issuance over time.
Example
A miner today receives 6.25 bitcoins in the coinbase when they successfully mine a block, plus fees.
Transaction fees
Fees are supplied by users to prioritize their transactions. Miners include higher-fee transactions first to maximize revenue in each block.
Scenario
During periods of high demand, fees can exceed the block reward portion for some miners, changing short-term incentives.
Misconception
Assuming fees alone will always replace block rewards ignores demand elasticity and long-term fee market evolution.
Key takeaway
Both block reward and fees together sustain miners; their relative importance shifts as issuance declines.
