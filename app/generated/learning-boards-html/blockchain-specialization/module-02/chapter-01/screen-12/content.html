Process Exploring the Blockchain

Guided explorer activity

Follow this checklist when you visit a public blockchain explorer to connect concepts to real data.

Checklist
  1. Open recent blocks and note the block height and hash.
  2. Inspect one transaction ID and list its inputs and outputs.
  3. Observe amounts, transaction fee, and the confirmation count.
  4. Reflect on how the record is public yet uses addresses instead of names.
Concrete observation
A transaction with multiple confirmations is harder to reverse and shows exactly which addresses participated and how much moved, illustrating both immutability and pseudonymity.
Reflection prompts
  • How many confirmations would you require for a high-value payment and why?
  • What operational mistake could make your addresses easy to link to you?
  • How does seeing a transaction in a block change your trust in its finality?
Key Takeaway

Exploring real transactions helps you see how public records support verifiability while revealing the limits of pseudonymity.
