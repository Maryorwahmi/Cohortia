Key Concepts Summary
Decentralization
No single administrator; network resilience comes from many independent participants.
Immutability
Blocks reference previous hashes so history becomes increasingly costly to alter as confirmations grow.
Pseudonymity
Addresses obscure direct identity but can be linked with external data; privacy is operational, not automatic.
Summary

These three concepts explain how Bitcoin secures and publishes a shared financial history while creating trade-offs around privacy and performance.
