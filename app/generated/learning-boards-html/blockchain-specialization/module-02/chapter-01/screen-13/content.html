What Bitcoin enabled and what it traded off

Enabled: Trustless settlement
Open verifiability and a shared ledger let participants settle value without a central authority.
Trade-off: Privacy
Public records aid auditability but expose transaction flows unless users take special precautions.
Trade-off: Performance
Decentralized consensus introduces latency and resource costs compared to centralized systems.
Key Takeaway

Bitcoin established a durable paradigm for decentralized money, and recognizing its trade-offs is essential when designing or evaluating alternative systems.
