Key Concepts Flashcards
Satoshi Nakamoto
The anonymous author of the Bitcoin whitepaper who proposed the peer-to-peer electronic cash design.
Bitcoin Whitepaper
The foundational document that defines Bitcoin's design, goals, and protocol rules.
Double-spending problem
The risk that a digital unit is copied and spent more than once; Bitcoin prevents this through shared ordering and consensus.
