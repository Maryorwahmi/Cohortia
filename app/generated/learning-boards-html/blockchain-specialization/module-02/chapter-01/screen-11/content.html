Comparison Pseudonymity vs Anonymity

What addresses show and what they hide

Pseudonymity (Bitcoin)
  • Transactions are public and tied to addresses.
  • Addresses are not human-readable identities.
  • Linking requires additional data or pattern analysis.
Example
Viewing a TX shows inputs, outputs, amounts, and fees—useful for audits but not a direct name mapping without extra information.
True anonymity
True anonymity means transactions cannot be linked back to an identity even with external data. Bitcoin does not provide this by default; additional privacy tools are required for anonymity and even they have limits.
Misconception
Believing that addresses equal anonymity leads users to reuse addresses or publicly associate them with accounts, which weakens privacy.
Key Takeaway

Bitcoin is pseudonymous; privacy depends on operational choices and external information that can link addresses to identities.
