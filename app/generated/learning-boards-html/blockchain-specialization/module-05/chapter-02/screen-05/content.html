Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › web3.js vs ethers.js
web3.js

An older, feature-rich library created early in the Ethereum ecosystem with broad API coverage.

Strength
Comprehensive API surface for many node interactions.
Consideration
Larger bundle and older patterns may not align with modern TypeScript usage.
ethers.js

Modern, modular, and often preferred for frontend DApp development for its smaller footprint and TypeScript friendliness.

Strength
Cleaner API, better modularization, and improved developer experience.
Consideration
Some very specialized utilities may be more mature in web3.js depending on use case.
Decision tip

If you prioritize bundle size, clarity, and TypeScript support in the browser, ethers.js is often the better fit. If you need exhaustive legacy functionality, web3.js remains usable.
