Module 05 › Chapter 5.4 › Quick Reminders
Do
Use signer.signMessage for ownership proofs, and verify signatures both client-side and server-side when needed.
Don't
Never ask users to share private keys or paste them into your application.
Watch out
Handle accountsChanged and chainChanged and clear signatures when appropriate to avoid stale trust.
Final takeaway

Design authentication flows that respect user custody, provide transparent prompts, and verify signatures in a secure, auditable way.
