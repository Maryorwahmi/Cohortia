What to Remember

CID permanence
A CID ties directly to content bytes; change the content and you get a new CID.
Pinning vs Payment
Pinning keeps content available; Filecoin pays providers for verifiable, time-bound storage.
Encrypt before upload
Anyone with a CID can fetch content, so encrypt private data client-side before uploading.
Final takeaway

Design: on-chain for proofs, IPFS for distribution, pin or pay for persistence, and encrypt for privacy.
