Encryption, public CIDs, and trade-offs

CIDs reveal nothing about content by themselves, but anyone with the CID can fetch the file. Encrypt sensitive content before upload and manage keys carefully.

  • Encrypt on the client; store decryption keys separately and securely.
  • Use pinning providers or Filecoin to meet retention requirements.
  • Audit and log who requests CIDs where privacy matters.
Checklist
  • Does the data require encryption before upload?
  • Is pinning enough, or is a Filecoin contract required?
  • How will you store and rotate encryption keys safely?
Example
Encrypt metadata client-side, upload encrypted blob to IPFS, pin it, and store the CID and key reference separately.
