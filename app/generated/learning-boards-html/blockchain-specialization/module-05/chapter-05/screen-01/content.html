Decentralized Storage (IPFS & Filecoin)

Understand content addressing, CIDs, and how an economic layer ensures persistent off-chain storage for DApps.

Why this matters

Blockchains are expensive for large files. Using content-addressed storage lets you keep data integrity while avoiding huge gas costs and blockchain bloat.

Orientation

You will learn practical integration, how to generate CIDs, and when to rely on pinning or Filecoin for persistence.

Quick takeaway

Keep large content off-chain, save immutable CIDs on-chain, and use an economic layer when persistence matters.

Concept snapshot

On-chain reference
Store the CID in your smart contract to link immutable data without the gas cost of the file.
Content ID
A CID is a cryptographic hash of the file. Any content change produces a new CID.
Persistence
Use pinning or Filecoin to make sure nodes keep hosting the content long-term.
