Key Concepts

Decentralized Storage
Off-chain storage distributed across peers, offering resilience and censorship resistance.
IPFS & CIDs
Content addressing returns a cryptographic CID that uniquely identifies the uploaded bytes.
Filecoin
An economic storage network that pays providers to store data for specified durations.
Summary takeaway

Design your DApp to store small proofs on-chain, put bulk content on IPFS, pin or pay for persistence, and encrypt private data before upload.
