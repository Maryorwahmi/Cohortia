Core Concept

Why pinning matters and how services help

Pinning tells a node to keep a copy of a CID's blocks and prevents garbage collection. Without pinning, content availability is opportunistic.

  • Pin locally if you manage your own node, or use a pinning provider for convenience.
  • Pinning ensures availability but is not an economic guarantee against content loss if a provider fails.
  • Combine pinning with replication across providers to reduce risk.
Workflow
  1. Upload file via client → receive CID.
  2. Pin CID to your node or a provider to retain content.
  3. Optionally, purchase Filecoin storage for contractual persistence.
Concrete example
Use web3.storage to upload and automatically pin files for a managed retention period, simplifying availability management.
Misconception
Uploading through a gateway is not the same as pinning; gateways may not retain content indefinitely for you.
