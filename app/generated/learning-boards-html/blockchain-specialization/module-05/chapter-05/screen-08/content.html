Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › IPFS vs Filecoin
IPFS

Content addressing & distribution

  • Addresses files by content hash (CID).
  • Peers that have content can serve it; resilient and decentralized.
  • Does not guarantee long-term hosting by itself.
Example use
Share immutable metadata for an NFT and let collectors fetch the content by CID.
Filecoin

Incentive layer for persistence

  • Market-based storage where providers accept payment to store data.
  • Offers verifiable proofs of storage for the duration of the deal.
  • Introduces cost and contract management to ensure longevity.
Example use
Pay storage providers to keep archival data available for a guaranteed period.
Key Takeaway

Choose IPFS for integrity and distribution, and add Filecoin when you require paid, verifiable persistence.
