Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Concepts
On-chain vs Off-chain
Keep authoritative values on-chain and heavy assets off-chain referenced by CIDs.
Gas-aware design
Minimize SSTORE, pack types, and favor events for off-chain reads.
Automate & Verify
Run CI tests, deploy to testnets, verify contracts, and pin frontends for reliable releases.
Summary takeaway

Prioritize cost, security, and automation when preparing DApps for real-world use. Small changes in each area compound into a robust product.
