Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Gas Optimization
Contract-level techniques
  • Minimize SSTORE writes; store hashes for bulky assets instead of the assets themselves.
  • Pack small data types into single slots when ranges allow.
  • Avoid expensive loops in transactions; prefer off-chain computation or batched updates.
  • Use events for off-chain logging instead of state variables when user-facing reads are sufficient.
Examples & notes
Example

Store the IPFS CID for an image on-chain and fetch the image off-chain. This reduces SSTORE cost dramatically compared to storing large byte arrays.

Pitfall

Overpacking without careful range checks risks overflow or loss of clarity. Document packing decisions and limits in the contract comments.
