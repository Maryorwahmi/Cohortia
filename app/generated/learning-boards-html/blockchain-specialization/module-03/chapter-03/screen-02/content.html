Learning Objectives

Learn the vocabulary and skills for Solidity and smart contracts.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define Smart Contracts

Explain immutability, determinism, transparency and trustlessness, and why they shape design decisions.

Verified Competency Milestone
02
Understand Solidity

Describe the contract keyword, compilation to EVM bytecode, and why Solidity is contract-oriented and statically typed.

Verified Competency Milestone
03
Identify Data Types

Recognize uint, int, address, bool, string, and bytes, and discuss gas tradeoffs for storage choices.

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Write Simple Contracts

Create a contract with state variables, a constructor, and read/write functions with correct visibility and simple checks.

Verified Competency Milestone
