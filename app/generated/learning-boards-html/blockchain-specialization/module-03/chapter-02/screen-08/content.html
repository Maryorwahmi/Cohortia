Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Gas & Fee Calculation
What gas measures
Gas quantifies EVM operations. Simple operations cost little; storage and complex logic cost more. The EVM adds up gas for every executed opcode to compute gasUsed.
Fee math
Total fee = gasUsed × gasPrice. gasLimit limits execution; unused gas (gasLimit minus gasUsed) is refunded.
Concrete example
If gasUsed=45,000 and gasPrice=20 Gwei then fee = 45,000 × 20 Gwei = 900,000 Gwei = 0.0009 ETH (after unit conversion).
Optimization hint
Reducing gasUsed through efficient code lowers fees directly. Small changes can compound across many transactions.

Misconception check
Unused gas from the gasLimit is returned, so setting gasLimit high does not mean you pay for all of it. You pay for what is actually consumed.
