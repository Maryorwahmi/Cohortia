Mini-Quiz & Reflection
Three quick prompts to test understanding and prepare for hands-on work.
Question 1
If gasLimit is too low for a contract function, what is the result and what cost is incurred? (Answer: revert and pay gasLimit consumed.)
Question 2
If a transaction is pending for long, which parameter should you suspect? (Answer: gasPrice is likely too low.)
Reflection
Run the provided Ganache script, change gasLimit and gasPrice, and observe how gasUsed and inclusion change. Learning by measurement builds reliable intuition.
