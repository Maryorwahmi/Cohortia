Key Concepts
Transaction
A signed message that changes blockchain state including transfers and contract calls.
Nonce
Sequential counter preventing replays and enforcing per-account ordering.
Gas & Fees
Gas measures computation; fee = gasUsed × gasPrice. gasLimit caps execution and protects the sender.
Key Takeaway

Understand fields, estimate gas locally, set conservative gas limits, and pick a competitive gas price to avoid failures and delays.
