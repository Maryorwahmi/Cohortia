Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Lab Workflow
Step-by-step
  1. Confirm your AdvancedStorage contract address and ABI from Remix or your deployment logs.
  2. Open the demo page and detect window.ethereum; create a provider.
  3. Request accounts and get a signer; instantiate the contract with address and ABI.
  4. Call getData to display the current value.
  5. Enter a new value, call setData, approve the MetaMask prompt, and wait for confirmation.
  6. Re-call getData to verify the update and listen for DataChanged events if desired.
Debug checklist
If something fails, check network selection in MetaMask, the contract address, ABI format, and whether the provider supports signing. Use console logs to inspect provider and signer objects.
