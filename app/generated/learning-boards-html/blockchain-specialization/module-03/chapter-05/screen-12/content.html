Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Benefits & Challenges
Benefits
  • Censorship resistance: No single operator can take down the DApp.
  • Transparency: Transactions and logic are auditable on-chain.
  • User ownership: Users control keys and assets directly.
Challenges
  • Scalability & gas: Transactions cost money and take time to confirm.
  • UX complexity: Wallets, gas, and confirmations are friction points.
  • Immutability: Bugs require planned upgrade or migration strategies.
Key Takeaway

Decide what must be on-chain for trust and design UX to manage cost and latency. Plan for upgrades, testing, and clear user communication about gas and confirmations.
