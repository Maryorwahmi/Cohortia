Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Deploy: Sepolia
Deployment steps
  1. Install MetaMask and switch to the Sepolia Test Network.
  2. Request Sepolia test ETH from a faucet and confirm receipt in MetaMask.
  3. In Remix, set Environment to "Injected Provider - MetaMask" and connect the wallet.
  4. Provide constructor argument and click Deploy; confirm the transaction in MetaMask.
  5. Monitor the transaction hash in Remix and verify on Sepolia explorers after confirmation.
Common mistake
Deploying on the wrong network or lacking faucet funds will cause the transaction to fail or be rejected by MetaMask.
Concrete example
Provide initialData 101, click Deploy, and confirm the MetaMask dialog showing gas estimate. After mining, interaction with setData will also require MetaMask confirmations and consume test ETH.
Why it matters
Deploying on a testnet simulates mainnet behavior and helps you verify gas usage, transaction latency, and real wallet interactions before production deployments.
