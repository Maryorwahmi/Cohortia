Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › JS VM vs Testnet
JavaScript VM
  • In-browser simulated blockchain with instant mining.
  • Pre-funded accounts for free testing.
  • State is transient and tied to the browser session.
Sepolia Testnet
  • Real distributed network behavior and real test ETH gas costs.
  • Transactions require confirmations and can be inspected on explorers.
  • Suitable for final verification of wallet integration and gas usage.
Practical advice
Iterate quickly in JS VM and then deploy to Sepolia to verify cost, latency, and integration with MetaMask. Expect timing and gas cost differences, and always confirm constructor inputs before deploying publicly.
