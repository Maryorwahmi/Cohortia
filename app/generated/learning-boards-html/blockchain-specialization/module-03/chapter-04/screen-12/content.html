Error checklist
  • Missing constructor arg: Provide correct type and value at deploy time.
  • Compiler mismatch: Align pragma and compiler selection before compiling.
  • Wrong MetaMask network: Confirm network and wallet balance to avoid failed transactions.
  • Access control confusion: Switch accounts to verify require behavior produces expected reverts.
  • Emit or variable misspellings: Check declaration and use of variable names carefully.
Quick fixes
Reproduce the issue in JS VM, fix the source, recompile, and test interactions locally before deploying to Sepolia. This minimizes wasted testnet ETH and clarifies the root cause of failures.
