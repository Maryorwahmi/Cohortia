What a contract account holds
  • Bytecode compiled from a high-level language.
  • Persistent storage for variables, mappings, and balances.
  • An address that other accounts can call.
Concrete example
A token contract stores a mapping of balances and exposes transfer functions. When a user calls transfer, the contract's bytecode runs and updates storage across the network.
Explorer cues
Contract pages include a "Code" section and interactive "Read/Write Contract" panels, indicating deployed bytecode and callable functions.
Key Takeaway

Contract accounts are code-bearing addresses that react to calls and store state; they do not initiate transactions on their own.
