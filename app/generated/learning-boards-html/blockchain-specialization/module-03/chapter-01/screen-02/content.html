Learning Objectives
Targets for comprehension and applied skills
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Distinguish Ethereum from Bitcoin
Explain differences in purpose and programmable capability.
Why it matters: Being able to explain this difference shows you understand why some applications require programmability, not just a currency. Mnemonic: Cash vs Calculator — one stores value, the other runs logic.
Verified competency milestone
02
Explain the EVM
Describe deterministic execution and isolation of smart contracts.
Why it matters: The EVM makes sure every node reaches the same result when contract code runs. Mnemonic: a sandboxed CPU everyone runs locally.
Verified competency milestone
03
Identify EOAs and Contract Accounts
Recognize account characteristics and who initiates transactions.
Why it matters: Differentiating accounts helps you know when code executes by itself versus when a user triggers it. Mnemonic: Person with key vs Vending machine with code.
Verified competency milestone
04
Understand Ether and Gas
Explain how ETH is used for value and for paying computation costs.
Why it matters: Gas prevents abuse by attaching a cost to computation, and it compensates nodes for work. Mnemonic: Fuel for the world computer.
Verified competency milestone
