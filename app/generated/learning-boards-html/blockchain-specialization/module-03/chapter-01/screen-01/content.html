Introduction to Ethereum
A programmable, decentralized runtime for smart contracts and DApps
In this lesson

You will learn the EVM's purpose, the difference between EOAs and Contract Accounts, how Ether becomes gas, and the idea of Ethereum as a world computer enabling DApps.

Why it matters

Understanding these foundations helps you reason about who can initiate transactions, how computation is paid for, and why decentralized apps can run trustlessly on a shared platform.

Key Takeaway

Ethereum extends blockchain into a universal, sandboxed runtime where code and accounts interact through gas-paid execution to enable DApps.
