Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Ether (ETH)
Native asset used for value transfer and to pay for computation on the network.
Gas
Unit measuring computational effort; paying gas prevents abuse and compensates nodes.
Smart Contract / DApp
Self-executing code deployed on-chain that, combined with off-chain parts, forms decentralized applications.
Final takeaway
Remember these operational rules when building or interacting with Ethereum: who initiates, what pays for computation, and where logic runs.
