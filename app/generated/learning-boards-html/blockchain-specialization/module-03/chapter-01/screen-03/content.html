Applied Objectives
Inspect accounts on a block explorer
Identify the presence of code and distinguish EOA from Contract Account pages.
Explain bytecode and storage
Relate deployed bytecode to contract behavior and persistent storage.
Compute gas costs
Use gas limit and gas price to estimate transaction fees and spot potential cost issues.
Verification Mnemonics
Person vs Machine: EOAs sign transactions; Contracts run when called.
Fuel: Ether pays gas; gas prevents unlimited computation.
Sandbox: The EVM is isolated so contracts can't access external systems directly.
