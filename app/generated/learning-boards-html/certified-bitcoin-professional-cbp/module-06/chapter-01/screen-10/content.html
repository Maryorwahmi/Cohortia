Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Financial Inclusion
Opportunity
Permissionless financial rails can reach unbanked users and reduce dependence on costly intermediaries.
Concrete Benefits
  • Lower-cost cross-border transfers.
  • Ability to receive and store value without a bank account.
  • Potential for new financial services built on open rails.
Limits
Connectivity, device access, key custody, and local liquidity are required to translate permissionless access into real inclusion.
Design Response
Combine simple wallets, on-ramps, customer support, and education to reduce exclusion risks.
Example
A sender in one country uses a remittance provider that delivers Bitcoin to a recipient's mobile wallet, enabling a recipient with no bank account to access funds immediately.
