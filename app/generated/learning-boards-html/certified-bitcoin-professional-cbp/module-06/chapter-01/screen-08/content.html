Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Monetary Policy
Architecture Impact
Bitcoin's algorithmic issuance prevents central banks from adjusting base money, changing how fiscal and monetary responses to crises could work.
Central Bank Tools
Interest rates, reserve requirements, and quantitative easing provide macro adjustments.
Bitcoin's Schedule
Preprogrammed issuance limits discretionary supply manipulation.
Consideration
If a nation seeks monetary independence from external pressures, adopting a fixed-supply money changes policy tools and requires alternative stabilizers.
Reflection
What are the political and distributional consequences when a country gives up discretionary monetary tools in favor of algorithmic money?
