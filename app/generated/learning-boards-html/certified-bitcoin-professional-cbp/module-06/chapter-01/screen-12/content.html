Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Energy & Environment
Security Cost
Proof-of-work secures the network by requiring energy expenditure; that energy secures ledger immutability and consensus.
Key Points
  • Mining energy is part of the security model.
  • Environmental impact depends on the electricity mix miners use.
  • Shifts to renewables are possible and observed in some contexts.
Practical Response
For product teams, track miner energy sourcing if environmental claims matter to customers; consider layer-2 to reduce on-chain activity when appropriate.
Common Misconception
Energy use does not equal pollution automatically; the source of electricity determines environmental outcome.
