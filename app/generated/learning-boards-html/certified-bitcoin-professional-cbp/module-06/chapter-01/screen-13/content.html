Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Societal Impact
Sovereignty
Control of private keys gives users direct control of funds, reducing dependence on intermediaries and enabling financial autonomy.
Risk
No central recovery mechanisms means mistakes or lost keys typically lead to permanent loss.
Digital Divide
Without devices, connectivity, or education, many cannot benefit from Bitcoin, which can perpetuate or worsen exclusion.
Design Guidance
Offer custody choices, clear backups, localized onboarding, and human support to bridge gaps.
Reflection
How will your product balance personal custody with accessible recovery and usability for diverse users?
