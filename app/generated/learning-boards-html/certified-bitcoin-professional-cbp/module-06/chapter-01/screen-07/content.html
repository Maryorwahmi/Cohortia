Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Store of Value
Concept
Scarcity from a fixed supply can support long-term value preservation, similar to other hard assets.
Why people compare Bitcoin to gold
Both are limited in supply and are used by some as a hedge against local currency depreciation.
Practical Reality
  • Volatility can negate short-term stability needed for everyday pricing.
  • Liquidity matters: converting to fiat reliably is critical for usability.
  • Mitigation: instant conversion services can preserve fiat-equivalent revenue for businesses.
Example
In countries with rapid currency depreciation, some users store value in Bitcoin to avoid local currency loss. This preserves value only if they can access reliable liquidity and accept volatility risk.
