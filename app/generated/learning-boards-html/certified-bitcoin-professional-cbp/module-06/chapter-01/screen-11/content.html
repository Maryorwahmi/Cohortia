Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Remittance Scenario
Scenario Tasks
  1. Identify benefits: lower fees, faster settlement using layer-2, inclusion for unbanked recipients.
  2. Identify risks: price volatility, regulatory uncertainty, user education and custody errors.
  3. Mitigations: instant conversion, compliant local partners, simple onboarding and support.
Key Tip
Map each risk to an operational control and a monitoring metric before launch.
Simplified $100 Workflow
1) Sender gives $100 USD to remittance business
2) Business converts USD to Bitcoin (exchange or liquidity pool)
3) Business sends Bitcoin via layer-2 to recipient's Lightning-enabled wallet
4) Recipient receives Bitcoin; option A: convert to fiat via local partner; option B: hold or spend directly
Mitigation strategies: instant conversion to fiat on receipt to limit volatility exposure; partner with compliant local off-ramps to ensure liquidity; provide onboarding materials for wallet setup.
