Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Volatility & Mitigation
Problem
Rapid price variation undermines predictable valuation for payments and receipts.
Business Impact
  • Revenue unpredictability when holding Bitcoin between receipt and conversion.
  • Difficulty setting stable prices in Bitcoin-denominated terms.
  • Accounting and tax complexity from frequent valuation changes.
Mitigations
Instant conversion to fiat or stablecoins, using payment processors, or requiring recipients to convert immediately can limit exposure.
Trade-offs
Mitigation often reintroduces custodial risk and reliance on intermediaries; balance is required based on business goals.
Key Takeaway
For payments use cases, protect predictable fiat flows first; allow speculative exposure only when it aligns with business appetite.
