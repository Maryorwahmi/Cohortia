Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Remittance Flow
Step-by-step
1
On-ramp: Sender pays fiat
Provider converts fiat to Bitcoin or prepares internal liquidity.
2
Transfer: Network or layer-2
Layer-2 reduces fees and latency compared to on-chain settlement.
3
Off-ramp: Recipient receives or converts
Recipient can hold Bitcoin or convert to fiat via local partner.
Common Mistake
Assuming lower fees everywhere — savings depend on chosen rails, liquidity, and conversion timing.
Example
Sending $100 through a traditional corridor might cost 5–10% in fees. Using Bitcoin with layer-2, fees and settlement time can be much lower, increasing the net amount the recipient receives.
Key Considerations
  • Latency and cost of the chosen rails (on-chain vs layer-2).
  • Exchange spreads and off-ramp liquidity affecting final fiat amount.
  • Volatility exposure and mitigation via instant conversion.
Key Takeaway
Layer-2 payments can dramatically reduce cost and time, but operational design determines actual savings.
