Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Operational Concepts
VASP
Entities that operate exchanges, wallets, or custody and must implement compliance measures.
Travel Rule
Requires VASPs to share sender and recipient identity for certain transfers above thresholds.
Capital Gains
Disposal of Bitcoin may trigger tax events; maintain cost-basis and transaction logs.
Final takeaway
These operational concepts determine how you collect data, monitor activity, and report for legal and tax purposes. Use them as checklists when designing services or advising users.
