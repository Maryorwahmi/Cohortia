Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › China vs El Salvador
Permissive example

When a country adopts Bitcoin as legal tender it actively incorporates it into public payments and may provide a supportive framework for exchanges and wallets.

Effect
Greater legal certainty for businesses, potential for broader retail adoption, and clearer tax and accounting rules.
Restrictive example

When a country bans mining and trading, it removes local operational capacity and forces users and businesses into non-compliant behavior or offshore solutions.

Effect
Displacement of economic activity, higher compliance burden for cross-border services, and potential enforcement actions against local operators.
