Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Jurisdiction Comparison
Case types
Legal tender / permissive
Authorities integrate Bitcoin into payments and may encourage use; businesses gain clarity but must align product offerings with national programs.
Restrictive / prohibitive
Some jurisdictions restrict mining and trading to manage capital flows and financial stability; this creates operational and legal barriers for service providers.
Practical consequences
  • Services must adapt onboarding and tax reporting to local rules.
  • Operating across borders requires mapping conflicting requirements and designing for compliance in the strictest applicable jurisdiction.
  • Businesses may need to restrict offerings in certain markets or implement geofencing to comply with local laws.
Misconception

Regulatory clarity in one country does not guarantee the same approach elsewhere; plan for variance and change.
