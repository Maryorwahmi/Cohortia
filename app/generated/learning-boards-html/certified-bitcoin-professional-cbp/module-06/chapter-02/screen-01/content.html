Overview

Regulations vary widely. This fragmentation affects custody, taxation, and how exchanges collect data. We will connect practical requirements to the standards that drive them.

Why it matters

Compliance enables institutional access and reduces legal risk, while non-compliance can lead to bans, fines, or reputational damage.

Key takeaway
Regulatory rules translate technical features into legal responsibilities.
