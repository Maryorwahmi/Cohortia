Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › United States
Regulatory split
Tax authorities treat Bitcoin as property for capital gains reporting and recordkeeping.
Other agencies oversee commodity or securities aspects depending on use cases.
Financial crime units expect AML/KYC programs and suspicious activity reporting.
Example
When a user sells Bitcoin at a gain, that event is taxable as a capital gain and must be recorded for reporting to tax authorities.
Practical guidance
  • Maintain accurate transaction histories and cost basis records for users.
  • Coordinate AML, tax, and other compliance functions within the organization.
  • Be prepared for overlapping inquiries or audits from different regulators.
Misconception

Treatment as property only affects tax; other regulatory obligations such as AML remain separate and must be addressed independently.
