Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Risks & Safety
Common issues
  • Phishing and social-engineering scams targeting credentials.
  • Irreversible transfers to incorrect addresses or fraudulent recipients.
  • Investment scams and fraudulent token offerings that result in loss.
Protective practices
Two-factor authentication, address verification routines, reputable custodians, and user education reduce exposure.
Operational measures
  • Implement scam-pattern detection and flag suspicious flows for manual review.
  • Keep incident response plans and ensure compliance teams can cooperate with authorities when required.
  • Provide users with clear warnings about irreversible actions and how to verify recipients.
Misconception

Privacy protections do not automatically prevent fraud; transparency in regulated channels helps trace illicit flows while security protects users from scams.
