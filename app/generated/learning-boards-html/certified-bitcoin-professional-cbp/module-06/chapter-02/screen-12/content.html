Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Tax Treatment
What to record
  • Acquisition date and acquisition cost (cost basis).
  • Dates and proceeds of disposals or trades.
  • Fees and other adjustments that affect gain/loss calculations.
Example
Buying Bitcoin at one price and later selling it creates a capital gain or loss equal to the sale proceeds minus the cost basis and allowable fees.
Design implications
Provide users with downloadable transaction histories and cost-basis calculations.
Ensure timestamps and fiat conversions at time of transaction are recorded to compute gains.
Key Takeaway

Accurate bookkeeping of acquisition and disposal details is essential to meet capital gains reporting obligations.
