Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
FATF
Sets international standards to prevent money laundering and terrorist financing.
KYC
Process of verifying client identity to prevent fraud and illicit activity.
AML
Monitoring and controls to detect and prevent the laundering of illicit funds.
Summary
These concepts form the backbone of compliance: standards, identity, and ongoing monitoring that connect on-chain activity to legal systems.
