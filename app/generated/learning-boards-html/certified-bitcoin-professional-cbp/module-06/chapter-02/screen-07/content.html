Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › AML Process
Monitoring steps
1) Define thresholds and suspicious indicators.
2) Apply analytics to score transactions and users.
3) Route high-risk alerts to trained investigators for context evaluation.
4) Report confirmed suspicious activity to authorities as required.
Example alert
A sudden large outgoing transfer to several new addresses from an account with previously low volume should trigger manual review.
Design notes
  • Combine rule-based thresholds with behavioral analysis to detect complex laundering patterns.
  • Ensure logs and evidence are retained to support any reporting or investigations.
  • Balance sensitivity to illegal activity with controls to reduce false positives and customer friction.
Misconception

Alerting alone is not sufficient; human review, clear escalation, and reporting workflows are essential to effective AML.
