Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › KYC
Core fields collected
  • Full legal name, date of birth, and nationality.
  • Residential address and proof of address (utility bill, bank statement).
  • Government-issued ID details and verification of document authenticity.
Example
Accepting a passport photo and a recent utility bill reduces impersonation risk compared with accepting only one document.
Operational notes
  • Define acceptable document types and verification confidence levels.
  • Log verification outcomes and retention periods in line with local rules.
  • Use multiple data points to reduce false positives and identity fraud.
Common mistake

Relying on a single document without corroboration increases risk of fraudulent accounts.
