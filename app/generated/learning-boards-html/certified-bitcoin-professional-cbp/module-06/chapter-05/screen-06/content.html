Utility tokens vs Stablecoins

Understand the practical function each token type serves and why that matters for users and protocols.

Utility Tokens
Grant access to services or reward participation. Example: a token used to pay for decentralized storage or to stake for certain privileges.
  • Often used inside an ecosystem for payments or incentives.
  • Value often tied to utility and demand for the service.
Misconception
Market price alone doesn't prove utility; study how tokens are used and consumed.
Stablecoins
Designed to minimize volatility by pegging to a stable asset. They enable trading and lending without constant price swings.
  • Provide a fiat-like medium inside crypto markets.
  • Can be collateralized by reserves or managed algorithmically.
Why it matters

Stablecoins reduce settlement friction and enable lending markets by lowering exposure to short-term volatility.
