Definition

Ecosystem risks and trade-offs

Identify the primary risks a project accepts to achieve performance, privacy, or programmability objectives.

Execution risk
Smart contract bugs and protocol vulnerabilities that can cause direct loss of funds.
Economic risk
Volatility, liquidity shortages, and poor collateral choices that impact value.
Governance risk
Who can change the rules and how upgrades are decided, especially in permissioned systems.
Key Takeaway

Always map a project's claimed benefits to the risks it accepts; that mapping reveals fit for purpose.
