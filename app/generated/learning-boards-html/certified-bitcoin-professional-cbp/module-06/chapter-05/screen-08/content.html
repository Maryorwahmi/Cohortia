Decentralized Exchanges and liquidity

Automated market makers price trades using smart contract reserves rather than matching orders in a book.

How an AMM works (simple)
  1. User deposits Token A and Token B into a pool.
  2. Pool holds reserves and uses a pricing formula to set exchange rates.
  3. Traders swap with the pool; liquidity providers earn a share of fees.
Example
Providing equal-value tokens to a pool and earning fees while accepting exposure to each token's price movement.
Impermanent loss
Occurs when token price divergence reduces a provider's holdings' dollar value relative to simply holding the tokens.
Mitigation
  • Choose pools with balanced exposure or stablecoin pairs to lower exposure.
  • Factor in expected fees versus potential loss when deciding to provide liquidity.
Check your understanding

If two tokens diverge in price, will a liquidity provider always profit? Consider trading fees vs impermanent loss.
