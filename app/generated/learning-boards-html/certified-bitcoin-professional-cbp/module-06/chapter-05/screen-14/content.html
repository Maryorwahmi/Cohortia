What to Remember

Key Concepts

Altcoins
Cryptocurrencies other than Bitcoin designed for different use cases or technical trade-offs.
DeFi
Smart-contract based financial services enabling exchanges, lending, and synthetic assets without central intermediaries.
Enterprise Blockchains
Permissioned ledgers favor privacy, governance, and controlled access for business use cases.
Takeaway

Purpose, mechanism, and risk form a practical framework for evaluating blockchain projects.
