Hands-on Activity

Altcoin Comparison Research

Use a short report or table to compare Bitcoin with a Layer 1 altcoin across standard criteria to surface meaningful trade-offs.

Template fields
  • Primary design goal / purpose.
  • Consensus mechanism and its implications.
  • Programmability / smart contract support.
  • Transaction speed & cost characteristics.
  • Scalability approach and decentralization philosophy.
Example prompt
Write a short paragraph for each template field comparing Bitcoin and your chosen altcoin. Conclude with which use cases each network is best suited for.
Key Takeaway

A structured comparison reveals where each network succeeds and what trade-offs drive that success.
