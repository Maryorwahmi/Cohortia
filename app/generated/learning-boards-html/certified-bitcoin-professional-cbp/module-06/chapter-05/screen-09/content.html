Definition

Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs)

Unique digital assets on-chain used to prove ownership, provenance, and rights for individual items.

Token standards
Standards define how uniqueness is encoded and handled. Some standards support strictly unique items, others support mixed fungible and non-fungible types for efficiency.
Example
A gaming asset NFT that represents a sword with verifiable ownership, transferable outside the game.
Practical cautions
  • Don't confuse the media file with the token; ownership is about the token's recorded rights and metadata.
  • Markets can be speculative; provenance and utility often drive long-term value.
Key Takeaway

NFTs are a tool for verifiable, transferable digital ownership; evaluate the contract and metadata to understand rights conveyed.
