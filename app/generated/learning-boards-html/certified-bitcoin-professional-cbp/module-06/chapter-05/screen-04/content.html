Core Concept

Why altcoins emerged

Bitcoin proved decentralized digital money. Altcoins explore different protocol choices to enable new features or improve performance.

Key idea
  • Bitcoin prioritizes security and scarcity over programmability and high throughput.
  • Altcoins change protocol rules to offer smart contracts, faster confirmations, or different governance.
  • Evaluate altcoins by their stated design goal, not by hype alone.
Example
A chain that adds smart contracts so developers can build dApps; it accepts trade-offs in language complexity and runtime safety.
Key Takeaway

Altcoins are experiments in protocol design; understand their goals before judging success or failure.

Common misconception

That altcoins necessarily replace Bitcoin. In practice many coexist: Bitcoin as a store of value, others for programmable features.

Compare & contrast
Bitcoin
Security, scarcity, limited scripting.
Altcoins
Programmability, varied consensus, experimental features.
