Core Concept

Enterprise and permissioned blockchains

Permissioned ledgers give organizations control over who participates and what data is visible while using distributed ledger primitives.

Typical features
  • Controlled node participation and access rights.
  • Privacy controls for sensitive business data.
  • Governance structures managed by consortiums or companies.
Use case
Tracking high-value goods across a supply chain while limiting who can add or view records.
Design contrast with Bitcoin
Bitcoin emphasizes permissionless access and censorship resistance. Enterprise chains emphasize privacy and compliance, often sacrificing some decentralization.
Misconception
That enterprise chains are 'not blockchains' — they are ledger systems using similar primitives but with different governance goals.
