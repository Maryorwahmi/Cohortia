Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Blockchain Forks
Step 1
Proposal and debate
A technical change is proposed and discussed by developers, miners, stakers, and users.
Step 2
Adoption by nodes
Nodes upgrade software to implement the new rule; incompatible nodes may continue on the old rules.
Step 3
Outcome
If most of the network uses the new rule, the chain continues with the change; otherwise a split may produce two chains.
Notes
  • Not all forks create new coins; many are cooperative upgrades.
  • Forks can be planned upgrades or the result of governance disputes.
  • Coordination matters: network fragmentation weakens security and economic finality.
Key Takeaway

Forks are political and technical events; evaluate compatibility and community support when judging their significance.
