Why new coins appear

Drivers
  • Experimentation with technical designs and consensus.
  • Governance disagreements prompting forks.
  • Targeted applications requiring different features.
  • Speculative capital seeking high returns.
Ecosystem impact
Some altcoins succeed and contribute useful techniques; many do not. Successful projects attract builders and sustained activity and can influence best practices elsewhere.
Key Takeaway

Proliferation accelerates innovation but increases the burden on evaluators to separate useful designs from short-lived experiments.
