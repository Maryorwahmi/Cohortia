Why it split

A disagreement over increasing block size to support more transactions led to a non-backward-compatible rule change and a new chain.

Proponents' goal
Lower fees and faster payment settlement by enlarging blocks.
Opponents' concern
Potential centralization and security trade-offs from larger block validation costs.
Evaluation checklist
  • Did the fork attract users and economic activity?
  • Were long-term decentralization and security considered?
  • Is the fork's value proposition still relevant after the split?
Key Takeaway

Forks born from governance disputes show how technical and social choices together shape cryptocurrency outcomes.
