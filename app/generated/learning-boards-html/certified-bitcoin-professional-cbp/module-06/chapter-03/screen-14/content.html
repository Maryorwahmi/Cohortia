Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
Altcoin
Any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin, representing diverse design experiments.
Smart Contract Platform
A blockchain that runs on-chain code to enable dApps and composable services.
Stablecoin
A token designed to keep value stable, often backed by reserves or algorithms.
Summary takeaway

Use the problem-solution-risk checklist when evaluating any altcoin: what problem does it solve, how is it secured, who governs it, and what trade-offs are made?
