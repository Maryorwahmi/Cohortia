Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Utility & Governance Tokens

Utility tokens

Tokens that provide access to services or resources within a blockchain ecosystem rather than representing equity.

Example
A token used to pay fees on a decentralized storage platform.
Misconception
Not all tokens confer ownership; utility depends on adoption and demand for the service.

Governance tokens

Tokens that grant voting rights over protocol changes or fund allocation decisions. They formalize community control but can concentrate influence.

Practical note

Evaluate the governance process: quorum, proposal pathways, and whether few holders displace broad community control.
