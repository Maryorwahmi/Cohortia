Mechanisms

How stablecoins achieve a peg

Fiat-backed
Reserves in banks or custodians back each token unit.
Crypto-collateralized
Other crypto assets are held as collateral, often over-collateralized.
Algorithmic
Supply rules expand or contract supply to maintain peg.
Use case

Traders and services use stablecoins to move quickly between assets without converting to bank-held fiat, reducing friction and settlement time.

Risks & Checks
  • Reserves not fully backed or not independently audited can hide shortfalls.
  • Algorithmic designs can fail under market stress and lose the peg.
  • Regulatory action can limit redemption or change the peg dynamics.
Key Takeaway

A stablecoin eases volatility risk but shifts trust into reserves or algorithms — verify how the peg is maintained.
