Exploring Drivechains

A more decentralized proposal for linking Bitcoin to sidechains.

Drivechain Principles
Miner-Voted Pegging

Miners on the Bitcoin main chain vote on transfers to and from sidechains.

Enhanced Decentralization

Aims to reduce reliance on a federated group for sidechain security.

Research & Development

Currently in the experimental phase, not yet widely adopted.

Offloading Transactions

Contributes to reducing load on the main Bitcoin blockchain.

Core Idea

Drivechains aim to provide a more trust-minimized and decentralized way to connect sidechains to Bitcoin, leveraging the security of the main chain's miners.

Contrast

Unlike federated sidechains which rely on a fixed group of signers, drivechains propose a mechanism where Bitcoin miners themselves secure the peg, potentially increasing decentralization.

Key Takeaway

Drivechains represent an ongoing effort to enhance the decentralization and flexibility of Bitcoin's layered scalability solutions.
