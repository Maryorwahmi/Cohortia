Core Concept

Channel liquidity refers to the available funds within a payment channel to facilitate transactions in a particular direction. If one side of a channel sends too many payments, it can become 'dry' on that side, limiting future outgoing payments.

Scenario

Alice opens a channel with Bob, funding it with 0.01 BTC. If Alice sends 0.009 BTC to Bob, her side of the channel now only has 0.001 BTC. She can't send another 0.005 BTC to Bob until the channel is rebalanced or new funds are added.

Key Takeaway

Managing channel liquidity is essential for reliable Lightning Network transactions, ensuring funds are available for routing.
