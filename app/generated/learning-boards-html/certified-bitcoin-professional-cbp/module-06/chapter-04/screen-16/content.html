Essential Takeaways (Part 2)

Reinforcing the core ideas of Bitcoin scalability solutions.

Payment Channel

A two-party smart contract allowing multiple off-chain transactions, settling the net result on the main blockchain.

Off-chain Transaction

A transaction that occurs outside the main blockchain, typically on a Layer-2 network, not immediately recorded on the main ledger.

Channel Liquidity

The amount of funds available within a payment channel to facilitate transactions in a particular direction.

Summary Insight

Efficient off-chain transactions via payment channels, managed with sufficient liquidity, and specialized sidechains like Liquid, are all crucial components of Bitcoin's evolving scalability strategy.
