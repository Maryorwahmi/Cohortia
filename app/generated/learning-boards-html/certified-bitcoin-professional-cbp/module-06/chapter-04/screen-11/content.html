Definition

A sidechain is a separate blockchain that is cryptographically linked to the Bitcoin main chain, allowing assets to be transferred between the two chains.

Analogy

Think of Bitcoin as a secure vault. A sidechain is like a separate, specialized room connected to the vault, where you can move some of your assets to perform specific, faster operations, then move them back to the main vault when done.

Key Takeaway

Sidechains enhance Bitcoin's utility by enabling diverse functionalities and higher throughput without compromising the main chain's core principles.
