Core Concept
The Bitcoin Bottleneck
Understanding On-Chain Limitations
Fixed Block Size

Bitcoin blocks are limited to 1MB, restricting the number of transactions per block.

Slow Block Time

An average of 10 minutes per block means slower transaction confirmations.

Limited Throughput

Only ~7 transactions per second (tps), insufficient for global demand.

Key Takeaway

Bitcoin's core design prioritizes security and decentralization, but this leads to inherent on-chain scalability limits that hinder its use for everyday payments.

Real-World Impact

Limited tps leads to network congestion, higher fees, and slower confirmation times, making Bitcoin less suitable for micro-payments compared to traditional systems.
