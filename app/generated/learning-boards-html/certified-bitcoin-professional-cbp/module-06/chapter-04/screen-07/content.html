Inside a Payment Channel
Opening, Transacting, and Closing
01

Open Channel (On-chain)

Users commit Bitcoin to a 2-of-2 multisig address. This initial transaction is recorded on the main blockchain.

02

Transact (Off-chain)

Unlimited, instant transactions occur between users, updating balances within the channel without touching the main chain.

03

Close Channel (On-chain)

The final net balance is broadcast and recorded on the main Bitcoin blockchain, settling the channel.

Key Takeaway

Payment channels drastically reduce on-chain transactions by allowing frequent, instant, and low-cost transfers off-chain, with only the opening and final settlement recorded on Bitcoin's main ledger.
