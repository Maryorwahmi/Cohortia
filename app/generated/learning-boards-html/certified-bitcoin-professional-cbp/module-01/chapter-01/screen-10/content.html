Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Digital Scarcity
Definition
Protocol-enforced supply cap
Bitcoin encodes a maximum supply of 21 million coins and reduces new issuance via scheduled halving events.
Total supply: 21,000,000 coins hard-coded into the protocol.
Halving: miner rewards are cut by half approximately every four years, slowing issuance.
Example
When a halving occurs, miners receive half the previous reward. This predictable reduction lowers new coin flow over time.
Important note
Scarcity is structural, not a price guarantee. Limited supply can support value if demand exists; without demand scarcity alone does not determine price.
Key takeaway
Bitcoin’s scarcity is visible and enforced by the network rules, unlike discretionary monetary policy of centralized issuers.
