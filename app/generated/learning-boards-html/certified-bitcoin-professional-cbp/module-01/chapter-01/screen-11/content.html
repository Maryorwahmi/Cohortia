Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Halving
Process
Scheduled reduction of miner rewards
Halving occurs roughly every four years and cuts the block reward by half.
This reduces issuance rate and gradually approaches the 21 million cap.
Miners and market participants can plan for this predictable change; it is encoded in the protocol.
Concrete example
If a block reward is 12.5 coins before halving, after the event it becomes 6.25. This reduces the flow of new coins but does not by itself set market price.
Misconception check
Halving changes issuance, not guaranteed market outcomes. Price depends on supply and demand interaction.
