Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Learning Objectives
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain double-spending
Describe the problem and why copying digital money breaks trust.
You will use a concrete thought experiment: a single digital coin sent to two recipients, and explain how a ledger must prevent both spends. Verified competency milestone: explain the failure mode and why a ledger must make spends unique.
02
Describe historical context
Summarize earlier projects and why centralization was a problem.
You will name example systems from the past and explain their reliance on trusted authorities and the resulting trade-offs. Verified competency milestone: list at least one drawback of centralized digital cash designs.
03
Identify Satoshi’s innovation
Explain how broadcast, blocks, and proofs replace a trusted third party.
You will state the whitepaper’s central claim and be able to explain how a distributed ledger prevents double-spend without a single verifier. Verified competency milestone: summarize the whitepaper’s solution in one sentence.
04
Define digital scarcity
Explain the 21 million cap and halving schedule and why scarcity matters.
You will explain the protocol-enforced supply cap and the halving mechanism, and why this contrasts with centrally printed currencies. Verified competency milestone: describe what halving means and its effect on supply rate.
