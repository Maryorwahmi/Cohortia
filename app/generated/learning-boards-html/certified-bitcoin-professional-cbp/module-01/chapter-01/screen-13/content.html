Misconceptions
Bitcoin is a company — No. It is an open protocol and a distributed network.
Bitcoin is fully anonymous — No. Addresses are pseudonymous; transactions are public and linkable.
Transactions are reversible — No. Once confirmed, the protocol does not reverse transactions.
Practical safety tips
  • Always double-check recipient addresses and send a small test amount first.
  • Never share private keys; legitimate services will never ask for them.
  • Be skeptical of promises that sound too good to be true; scams exploit the irreversible nature of transfers.
Key takeaway
Understanding protocol properties helps you choose safe practices and realistic expectations when interacting with Bitcoin.
