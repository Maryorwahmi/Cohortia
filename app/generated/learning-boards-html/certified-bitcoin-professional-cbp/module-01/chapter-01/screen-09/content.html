Timeline
Oct 31, 2008 — Bitcoin whitepaper
A concise design proposing broadcast transactions, blocks, and cryptographic proofs to avoid a trusted third party.
Jan 3, 2009 — Genesis block
The very first block mined and the network began maintaining a public, chronological ledger.
Why these matter
The whitepaper is the blueprint; the genesis block is proof of deployment. Real networks require both design and functioning participants to secure value and prevent double-spend.
