Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Double-Spending
A digital token can be duplicated unless a ledger enforces uniqueness.
Satoshi Nakamoto
The pseudonymous author of the Bitcoin whitepaper and initial software that implemented the design.
Digital Scarcity
A fixed supply and halving schedule encoded into the protocol that limits new issuance.
Summary takeaway

Bitcoin solved the fundamental double-spend problem by combining public records, cryptographic proofs, and distributed agreement, and it enforces scarcity through a predictable protocol schedule.
