Verified Competency Milestones
Skills to practice
  • Analyze Alice’s double-spend and determine which transaction wins under different systems.
  • Explain how a bank ledger enforces uniqueness using account balances and logs.
  • Describe the failure modes of a peer-to-peer network without a consensus ordering.
Common misconceptions
Bitcoin is a company — No: it is an open protocol and network.
Bitcoin is fully anonymous — No: addresses are pseudonymous; linking is possible.
Transactions are reversible — No: once confirmed they cannot be undone by the protocol.
