Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
SHA-256
A deterministic, one-way 256-bit digest used for integrity and linking.
Public-key cryptography
Private keys sign; public keys verify — enabling ownership proof without revealing secrets.
Key custody
Protect private keys with secure backups and hardware wallets; exposure equals loss of control.
Summary Takeaway

SHA-256 and asymmetric signatures together create verifiable, tamper-evident transactions — but human practices around private keys determine whether cryptography actually protects funds.
