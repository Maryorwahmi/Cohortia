Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Signing and verification
Steps to sign
  1. Select UTXOs you control and create transaction outputs.
  2. Create the transaction data to be authorized (inputs, outputs, amounts).
  3. Use your private key to produce a digital signature over that transaction data.
  4. Broadcast the transaction with your public key and signature to the network.
Verification
Nodes verify the signature with the public key and confirm the UTXOs are unspent and sufficient.
Concrete scenario
Imagine you sign a 0.5 BTC payment. Your wallet uses your private key to sign that exact payment. When broadcast, every node can confirm you authorized that specific transfer without ever seeing your private key. If verification passes and the UTXOs exist, the network accepts the transaction.
Misconception check
A signature proves authorization for that transaction only; it does not reveal the private key or allow new signatures to be forged without the private key.
