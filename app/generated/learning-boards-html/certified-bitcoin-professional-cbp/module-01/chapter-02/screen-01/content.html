Cryptography Fundamentals for Bitcoin

Learn how SHA-256 preserves integrity, how key pairs prove ownership, and how digital signatures authorize transactions without revealing secrets.

In this lesson
Hash properties, SHA-256 examples, signatures, and private key safety.
Why it matters
These primitives let the network verify funds and builds trust without a central authority.
Core tools
SHA-256 for integrity and linking; ECDSA for signatures and ownership proof.
Explore Key Topics
SHA-256
Fixed-size digest with avalanche and collision-resistant behavior used for IDs and block linking.
Key pairs
A private key generates a public key; the public key is used to verify signatures and derive addresses.
Signatures
Digital signatures prove authorization to spend funds while keeping the private key secret.

Safety note
Your private key is the secret to control funds. Store it securely; losing it means losing access forever.
