Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Private key management
Best practices
  • Use hardware wallets for significant balances so signing happens on-device.
  • Keep backups of recovery seeds in secure, geographically separated locations.
  • Avoid digital copies of private keys on cloud storage or email.
  • Test your recovery procedure with small amounts before transferring large funds.
Consequence
Losing your private key is irreversible: there is no central authority to restore access to funds.
Practical checklist
Prefer hardware wallets for signing and keep the seed phrase offline.
Don’t reuse single backups in multiple insecure places; spread risk across safe locations.
Never share private keys; treat them like the single secret to your holdings.
Key Takeaway
Good key custody practices are as important as cryptographic strength — human choices determine whether cryptography protects your funds.
