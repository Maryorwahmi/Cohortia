What signing achieves
A digital signature proves that the spender controls the private key associated with the UTXO being spent.
Definition
Use the private key to sign transaction data; anyone with the public key can verify the signature.
Concrete example
Signing an input proves you are authorized to consume the referenced UTXO; nodes reject transactions with invalid signatures.
Misconception
A signature does not reveal your private key, but losing your private key allows others to sign as you.
Key Takeaway

Protect private keys. Signatures enable trustless verification that inputs are authorized without central validation.
