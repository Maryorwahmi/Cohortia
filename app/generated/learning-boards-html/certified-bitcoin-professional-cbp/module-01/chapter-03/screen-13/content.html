Confirmations and why they matter
Each confirmation increases the difficulty of reversing a transaction by requiring an attacker to replace more proof-of-work.
1 confirmation
Transaction included in a block — initial acceptance by nodes.
2–5 confirmations
Increasingly secure; risk of reorganization drops as more blocks accumulate.
6 confirmations
Common industry convention for high-value transfers: much stronger assurance against reversal.
Misconception check: Confirmations are not an absolute 'undo' proof. They are a growing statistical guarantee — more confirmations makes reversal less likely but never impossible in a purely probabilistic sense.
