Peer discovery steps
1. Seed nodes
Contact known addresses to obtain an initial peer list.
2. Address exchange
Peers exchange addresses so each node learns of more peers to connect to.
3. Maintain connections
Nodes open a limited number of outbound and may accept inbound connections to balance load and reachability.
4. Block download
After connecting, a node downloads and validates blocks to build its own copy of the blockchain.
Visual: Connection cards
Outbound peers
Initiated by the node to connect and fetch data.
Inbound peers
Other nodes that connect to this node, increasing reachability.
Rotation
Connections rotate over time to discover different parts of the network.
Sync progress
Nodes may take time to download and validate the full blockchain after joining.
Misconception check: Connecting does not equal trust. Connections let you talk to peers, but you still must validate blocks and transactions locally to trust them.
