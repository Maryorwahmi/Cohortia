Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Mempool & Fees
What the mempool does
A temporary store of valid, unconfirmed transactions waiting for inclusion in a block.
Selection metric
Miners prioritize by fee per byte to maximize reward given limited block space.
Example
Two transactions: small high-fee and large low-fee — miners usually pick the small high-fee to get more fee per byte.
Misconception
A large absolute fee isn't always best; fee relative to size matters most for prioritization.
Practical guidance
  • Estimate fee relative to transaction size to balance cost and confirmation speed.
  • During congestion, expect low-fee transactions to be delayed or require replacement.
  • Wallets may offer fee bumping mechanisms; understand how your wallet handles unconfirmed transactions.
Check your understanding
If a transaction is large but low-fee and you need fast confirmation, what should you change? (Answer: increase fee per byte or reduce transaction size by consolidating UTXOs beforehand.)
