Why this chapter matters
Grasping the P2P network and lifecycle of transactions explains how Bitcoin achieves decentralization, how security emerges from confirmations, and how UTXOs change the mental model from an account balance to spendable outputs. This understanding is essential for safely sending, receiving, and auditing transactions.
