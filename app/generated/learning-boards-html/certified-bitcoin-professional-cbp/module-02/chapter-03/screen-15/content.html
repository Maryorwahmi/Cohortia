Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › What to Remember
Verification

Public keys let nodes check signatures against the transaction hash to accept the transaction.

Authenticity & Integrity

A valid signature shows the signer authorized the transaction and that the data was unchanged after signing.

Non-repudiation

Once a signature verifies, the cryptographic proof links the transaction to the private key holder.

Quick summary

Signatures authenticate and preserve integrity of transactions. ECDSA creates those signatures. Keep private keys secure so these guarantees remain meaningful in practice.
