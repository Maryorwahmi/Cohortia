Hash Function
Maps arbitrary-size data to a fixed-size digest for identity and integrity checks.
Cryptographic Properties
Determinism, one-wayness, and collision resistance make hashes secure for Bitcoin.
SHA-256 in Bitcoin
Used for block headers and Merkle roots to secure transaction integrity and chain immutability.
Final Takeaway

Hashing provides compact, reliable proofs of identity and integrity. In Bitcoin, these proofs are the foundation of block linking and transaction verification.
