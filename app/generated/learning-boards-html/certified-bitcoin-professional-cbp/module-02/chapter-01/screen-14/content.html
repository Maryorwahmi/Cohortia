Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What to Remember
Deterministic
Same input and encoding always yield the same digest.
One-way
You cannot reconstruct original data from a digest in practice.
Not encryption
Hashing verifies integrity; encryption preserves confidentiality — they are not interchangeable.
Final Tip

When you verify hashes, ensure digest sources are trusted and input bytes are canonical. This keeps your verification meaningful.
