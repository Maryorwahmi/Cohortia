Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Asymmetric Cryptography
A pair of linked keys: private signs, public verifies. This enables verifiable ownership without sharing secrets.
Random Number Generation
High-quality entropy is critical. Weak RNG can produce predictable keys and catastrophic loss of funds.
Bitcoin Address
A hashed and Base58Check encoded form of the public key that includes a checksum for error detection.
Summary Takeaway

Control of funds is controlled by private keys; protect them with secure generation, storage, and backup practices. Addresses help with usability and safety but do not replace key security.
