Learning Objectives

These objectives focus your attention on the mechanics and security implications of key pairs and address derivation in Bitcoin.

01
Explain asymmetric cryptography

Describe the paired-key model and why signatures verify ownership without revealing secrets. Verified competency milestone: explain with the mailbox analogy.

02
Differentiate public vs private keys

Explain what each key can and cannot do, and why sharing a public key is safe. Verified competency milestone: give two examples of safe sharing and one unsafe action.

03
Describe key-pair generation

Name the role of randomness and how secure RNG prevents predictable keys. Verified competency milestone: explain why hardware wallets matter for entropy.

04
Trace address derivation

Follow ECC -> SHA-256 -> RIPEMD-160 -> Base58Check and explain checksums. Verified competency milestone: write the correct sequence from memory.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Demonstrate each objective in a short explanation or by completing the hands-on activity without exposing real private keys.
