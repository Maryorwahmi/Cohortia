Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Mempool Lifecycle
Timeline
  1. Broadcast: Transaction is sent and received by peers.
  2. Validate: Node checks format, inputs, and policy then adds to mempool.
  3. Wait: Transaction sits in mempools until a miner includes it.
  4. Include & Confirm: Miner mines a block containing the transaction; nodes validate block and remove transaction from mempools.
Example and misconception
A low-fee transaction may remain in mempools for many blocks or be dropped during congestion. Being in a mempool is not a confirmation; only inclusion in a valid block begins confirmation count.
Key Takeaway

Mempools are holding areas for validated transactions and play a crucial role in propagation and miner selection but are not finality.
