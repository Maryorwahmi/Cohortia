Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Node Interaction
Check connections
bitcoin-cli getconnectioncount
bitcoin-cli getpeerinfo
These commands show how many peers are connected and details about each peer. Use them to confirm peer diversity and diagnose connectivity issues.
Example interpretation
  • Low connection count may slow propagation; consider opening outbound connections.
  • Many peers from a single hosting provider means less decentralization than a set of geographically distributed peers.
Key Takeaway

Use these diagnostic commands to assess whether your node is well connected and whether peers are diverse enough to support resilient propagation.
