Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Full Node
Validates and stores the full blockchain independently.
SPV / Light Client
Uses headers and Merkle proofs to verify inclusion, trading trustlessness for efficiency.
Propagation
Transactions and blocks spread by gossip; validation before relay maintains consensus.
Summary
Choose a client based on resources and threat model. Encourage diversity of full nodes to strengthen censorship resistance and network health.
