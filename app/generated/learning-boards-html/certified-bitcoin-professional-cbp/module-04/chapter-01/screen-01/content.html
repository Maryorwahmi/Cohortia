Bitcoin Transactions Explained
Learn the UTXO model, how inputs and outputs create new spendable coins, why fees exist, and how digital signatures authorize spending.
In this lesson
Deconstruct a transaction, compute fee and change, and understand how signatures prevent unauthorized spending.
Coming up
A concrete JSON transaction example, wallet coin selection, common mistakes, and a hands-on block-explorer activity.
Why this matters

If you understand UTXOs and fees you can predict transaction behavior, reduce wasted fees, and avoid accidentally losing change to miners.
