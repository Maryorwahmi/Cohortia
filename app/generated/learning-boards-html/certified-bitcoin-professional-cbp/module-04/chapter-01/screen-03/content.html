Practice checklist
  • Find a simple transaction in a block explorer. Label each input's previous TxID and vOut index.
  • Sum the inputs and outputs. Confirm the difference equals the explorer's fee.
  • Identify which output is likely change by comparing address patterns and sizes.
  • Consider how wallet coin selection would choose inputs to cover the payment and fee.
Quick mnemonic
Think: Inputs are 'consumed', outputs are 'created', fee = inputs − outputs, signatures = permission slip.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Complete one live transaction inspection and a hand-calculation of fee and change to confirm mastery of the practical skills.
