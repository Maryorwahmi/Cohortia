Change Output
An output returning leftover value to the sender as a new UTXO; omission increases fee.
Transaction Fee
Calculated as total inputs minus total outputs. Miners prefer higher fee per byte for inclusion priority.
Digital Signature (ECDSA)
Cryptographic proof that the transaction spender controls the private key and is authorized to spend each input.
Final takeaway
When inspecting or constructing transactions, compute fees, confirm change, and verify signatures to ensure correct, authorized transfers of value.
