Coin selection strategies
Different wallets use different heuristics: minimize fee, minimize change, preserve privacy, or compact many UTXOs offline.
Greedy: pick smallest number of UTXOs totaling at least the payment.
Branch and bound: search for an exact match to avoid change.
Privacy-aware: avoid combining UTXOs that would link unrelated funds.
Concrete trade-offs
  • Many small inputs → larger byte size → higher fee.
  • Consolidation reduces fee later but costs a fee now.
  • Using a large input may reveal linkages across payments.
Assess your priorities: limit fees, maintain privacy, or reduce UTXO fragmentation. Wallets make trade-offs for you, but understanding them helps you choose when to consolidate or which UTXOs to spend.
Pro tip
If you have many tiny UTXOs, consider consolidating during a low-fee window to lower long-term costs.
