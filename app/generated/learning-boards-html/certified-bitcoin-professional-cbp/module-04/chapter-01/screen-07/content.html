Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Transaction fees — calculation and incentives
Fee mechanics
Fee = sum(inputs) − sum(outputs). Miners prefer transactions offering higher fee per byte.
Why size matters
Each input and output adds bytes. More inputs means a larger transaction, increasing the fee needed to reach a given fee per byte.
Practical effect
If you consolidate many small UTXOs during low-fee periods, future transactions will be cheaper. If you spend many fragments during peak times, expect higher fees.
Concrete calculation
Inputs: 1.1 BTC total. Outputs: 0.9001 BTC total. Fee = 0.1999 BTC. Compare fee per byte to current network rates to estimate inclusion probability.
Misconception check
Paying a larger total fee does not guarantee faster confirmation if the fee per byte is low. Miners rank by fee density, so compact transactions with higher density get priority.
