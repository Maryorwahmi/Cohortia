UTXO — definition and metaphor
A UTXO is an amount of Bitcoin created by a prior transaction and not yet spent. Wallets show totals by summing UTXOs you control.
Metaphor: cash bills
  • Each UTXO is like a separate bill in your wallet.
  • Spending consumes whole bills; change recreates a bill to you.
  • Wallet totals are the sum of your bills, not a single mutable balance.
Key Takeaway

Think coins, not accounts. Each UTXO is consumed when spent and becomes history in the chain of transactions.

Scenario
You received a 0.5 BTC payment and a 0.3 BTC payment. Your wallet shows 0.8 BTC. If you send 0.6 BTC, which UTXOs are consumed and what happens to the remainder?
Inputs selected
0.5 BTC + 0.3 BTC = 0.8 BTC consumed
Outputs created
0.6 BTC to recipient, 0.2 BTC to your change address (unless omitted)
A common misconception is to treat UTXOs like an account you can partially spend. Remember: inputs are fully consumed. Any remainder must be explicitly returned as change.
