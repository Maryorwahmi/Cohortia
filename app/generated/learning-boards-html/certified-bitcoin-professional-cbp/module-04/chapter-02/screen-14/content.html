Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Broadcast
Transactions are gossiped across nodes rather than sent to a central server.
Mempool
A node-local waiting area for validated but unconfirmed transactions; mempools vary between nodes.
Validation
Nodes check signatures, unspent inputs, and format before allowing transactions to propagate.
Takeaway

Propagation, local mempools, and strict validation together ensure decentralization and ledger integrity.
