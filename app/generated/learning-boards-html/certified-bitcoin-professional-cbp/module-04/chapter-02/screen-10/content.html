Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Low vs High Fee

Head-to-head

High-fee transaction

Higher fee per byte → prioritized by miners → fast confirmation during congestion.

Low-fee transaction

Lower fee per byte → may wait longer or be evicted when mempools fill → unpredictable delay.

Example

During a busy period, sending with a fee well below the recommended sats/vB may leave your transaction unconfirmed for hours. In contrast, a competitive fee often results in inclusion in the next few blocks.

Key Takeaway

Match fee per byte to urgency: cost savings matter, but so does time-to-confirmation.
