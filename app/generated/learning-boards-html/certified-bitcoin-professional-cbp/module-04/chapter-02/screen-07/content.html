Core Concept

Fee per byte drives inclusion

Miners maximize revenue by selecting transactions that pay the highest fee per byte. Limited block space creates competition and a dynamic fee market.

During congestion: users outbid each other and fee rates rise.
When quiet: lower fees can be confirmed quickly.
Old transactions: miners may include older low-fee txs occasionally to clear mempools.
Example comparison
Tx A
Fee: 200 sats total
Size: 250 vB
Fee/vB: 0.8 sats/vB
Tx B
Fee: 150 sats total
Size: 100 vB
Fee/vB: 1.5 sats/vB

Even though Tx A pays a higher total fee, Tx B offers a higher fee per byte and is more attractive to miners.

Key Takeaway

Always consider fee per byte, not just total fee, when estimating inclusion speed.
