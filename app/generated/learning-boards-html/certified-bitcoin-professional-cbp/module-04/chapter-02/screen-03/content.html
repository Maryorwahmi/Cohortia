Practical milestone

Send a tiny test transaction, find its TxID, and track it through a mempool-aware explorer. Record fee rate, time to first confirmation, and the number of confirmations over the next hour.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Policy milestone

Explain when a merchant should require 1, 3, or 6 confirmations and why those thresholds trade off speed and security. Provide a reasoned recommendation for small-value vs high-value transfers.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Explain mempool differences

Describe why two nodes might show different mempool contents at a moment in time and how that affects propagation and inclusion timing.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Predict miner behavior

Given a list of transactions with fees per byte, rank which are most likely to be selected by miners and explain how network congestion changes those priorities.
