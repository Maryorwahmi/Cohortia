Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Mempool (Memory Pool)
Definition

What a mempool is

A mempool holds validated, unconfirmed transactions for a node. It is not global and may vary by node due to timing and policy differences.

Nodes apply validation before adding transactions to their mempool: signature checks, input availability, and format rules.
Mempool policies such as minimum fee rates and eviction rules cause divergence between nodes.
Transactions can be dropped or evicted from mempools if they are low-fee during congestion.
Scenario

You broadcast a low-fee payment. Some nodes accept it; some evict it later when mempools fill. A miner connected to nodes that kept it may include it, but other miners may never see it.

Why it matters

Different mempool visibility changes how quickly miners can select your transaction and whether explorers show it as unconfirmed.

Key Takeaway

Treat mempool inclusion as probabilistic and choose fees accordingly if you need timely confirmation.
