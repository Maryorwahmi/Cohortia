Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Merchant Scenario
Step 1 — Broadcast

Customer pays; transaction is broadcast and may show as 'pending' in the merchant's interface.

Step 2 — 1 confirmation

Merchant sees the first confirmation and may tentatively accept payment but often waits for additional confirmations depending on value.

Step 3 — Multiple confirmations

Each added block reduces reversal probability; many merchants require 3–6 confirmations for higher-value transactions.

Key takeaway

Set confirmation thresholds by risk appetite and communicate expected wait times to customers to align expectations.
