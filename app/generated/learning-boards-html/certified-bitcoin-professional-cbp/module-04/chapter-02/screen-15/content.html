Fee market
Miners select by fee per byte, so fee rate determines inclusion priority.
Confirmations
Each block on top increases the cost of reversal; six confirmations are a common standard for finality.
Monitor
Use mempool-aware explorers to observe status, fee rates, and confirmations in real time.
Final takeaway

Combine fee strategy, confirmation policy, and monitoring to manage timeliness and risk when using Bitcoin.
