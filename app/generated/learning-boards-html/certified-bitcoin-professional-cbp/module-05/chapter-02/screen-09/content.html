Receiving Bitcoin with Your Wallet

How to get funds into your software wallet.

The 'Receive' Process

To receive Bitcoin, you need to provide a sender with your unique Bitcoin address. This address is generated by your wallet and is safe to share publicly.

  • •Navigate: Go to the 'Receive' section in your wallet app.
  • •Find Address: Your wallet displays a Bitcoin address (alphanumeric string).
  • •Share: Provide this address or its QR code to the sender.
Misconception

While addresses may change for privacy, they all derive from your single seed phrase, so you don't need to back up each new address.
