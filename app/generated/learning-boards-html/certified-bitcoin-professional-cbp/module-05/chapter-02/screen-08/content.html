The Critical Seed Phrase: Secure Backup

Best practices for safeguarding your ultimate wallet recovery tool.

Best Practices for Storage

The security of your seed phrase directly correlates to the security of your funds. Proper storage is non-negotiable.

  • •Physical Medium: Write it down on paper or engrave it on metal.
  • •Offline Storage: Keep it completely disconnected from the internet.
  • •Multiple Locations: Store copies in geographically separate, secure places.
Common Mistake

Never store your seed phrase digitally (e.g., text file, email, cloud, screenshot), as this exposes it to online theft.

Example: Secure Storage

Handwritten on paper, laminated.
Stored in a fireproof safe at home.
Another copy in a bank safe deposit box.
Key Takeaway

Physical, offline, and geographically distributed storage of your seed phrase is paramount for security.
