Creating a Strong Wallet Password

Encrypting your wallet file for local device security.

The Role of Your Password

Your wallet password encrypts the wallet file on your device, adding a crucial layer of protection against unauthorized local access. It's not for network security but for device-level protection.

  • •Local Encryption: Protects your wallet data if your device is compromised physically.
  • •Access Control: Prevents others from opening the wallet app on your device.
Misconception

A password encrypts your local wallet file, but it does not protect your funds if your seed phrase is compromised.
