Enhancing Wallet Security with 2FA

Adding an extra layer of protection to your funds.

What is Two-Factor Authentication?

2FA requires two distinct forms of verification to access your wallet, making it significantly harder for unauthorized users to gain entry, even if they have your password.

  • •Something You Know: Your password.
  • •Something You Have: A code from an authenticator app or SMS.
Misconception

While 2FA adds a step, it's a minimal inconvenience for a massive boost in security against password theft.

How 2FA Works

Enter your password (Factor 1).
Enter a unique code from your authenticator app (Factor 2).
Key Takeaway

Always enable 2FA on your wallet and associated services for a robust defense against unauthorized access.
