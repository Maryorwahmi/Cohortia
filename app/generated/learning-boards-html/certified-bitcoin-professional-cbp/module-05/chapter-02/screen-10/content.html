Sending Bitcoin from Your Wallet

Executing transactions and understanding fees.

The 'Send' Process

Initiating a Bitcoin transfer involves specifying the recipient, amount, and a transaction fee. Accuracy is paramount due to the irreversible nature of blockchain transactions.

  • •Recipient Address: The Bitcoin address of the person or service you're sending to.
  • •Amount: The quantity of Bitcoin you wish to transfer.
  • •Transaction Fee: A small payment to miners for processing your transaction.
Critical Warning

Bitcoin transactions are irreversible. Always double-check the recipient's address to avoid permanent loss of funds.

Understanding Transaction Fees

Paid to Miners: Compensates network validators.
Impacts Confirmation Speed: Higher fees often mean faster processing.
Key Takeaway

Always verify recipient addresses and consider transaction fees for desired confirmation times when sending Bitcoin.
