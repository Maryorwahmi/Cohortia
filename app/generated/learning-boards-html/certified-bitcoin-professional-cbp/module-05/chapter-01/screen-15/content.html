Final Reminders
Three quick prompts to guide action.
Who controls the key?
If you do, you own the funds; if a third party does, you trust them.
Match wallet to purpose
Use hot for daily needs and cold for long-term custody.
Protect backups
Keep seed phrases offline and distributed for resilience.
Closing Thought

Secure key management is the foundation of responsible Bitcoin use. Start small, practice on testnet, and build robust backups.
