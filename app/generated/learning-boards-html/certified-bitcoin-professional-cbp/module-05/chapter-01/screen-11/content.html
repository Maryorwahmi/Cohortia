Security Best Practices for Wallets
Practical rules to reduce the risk of loss or theft.
Actionable steps
  • Use cold storage for large, long-term holdings.
  • Keep seed phrases offline and backed up in multiple secure places.
  • Limit hot wallet balances and maintain device hygiene.
  • Prefer hardware signing for significant transactions where possible.
Concrete example
Store a hardware wallet in a home safe and keep an encrypted metal backup of the seed phrase in a separate secure location.
Common mistakes & mitigation
Avoid photographing or cloud-storing your seed phrase; these are high-risk behaviors.
Be skeptical of phishing attempts that request seed phrases or private keys; legitimate wallets never ask for them online.
Regularly review your backup plan and test recovery using a testnet or a disposable wallet scenario.
Key Takeaway

Secure private keys by minimizing internet exposure, using cold storage for high-value assets, and protecting seed phrases offline.
