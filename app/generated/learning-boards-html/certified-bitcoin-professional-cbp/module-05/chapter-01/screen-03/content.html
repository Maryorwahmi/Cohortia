Learning Objectives — Practical Skills
Concrete behaviors you will demonstrate in the activity.
Locate Addresses & History
Find the receive address, transaction history, and security settings in a wallet UI during a testnet run-through.
Practice Signing
Create and sign a testnet transaction, observe the fee, and note confirmation behavior without risking real funds.
Verify Backup Procedures
Understand seed phrases and proper offline storage methods to safeguard recovery options.
Identify Threats
Recognize phishing, malware, and device compromise risks that affect hot wallets and take mitigations.
