Testnet Simulation Walkthrough
A safe way to practice wallet operations without real funds.
Step-by-step checklist
  1. Create a new testnet wallet with a practice password and note the seed for study only.
  2. Find your testnet receive address in the wallet UI.
  3. Use a testnet faucet to request coins to that address and wait for confirmation.
  4. Send a small amount to another testnet address and watch fee and confirmation behavior.
Example observation
Note how the wallet shows pending vs confirmed states and how the fee affects confirmation speed.
Reflection & safety
Do not reuse practice passwords or seed phrases for real wallets.
Test whether the wallet exposes any recovery options and how they work in a restore scenario.
Compare the wallet UI with a bank app: look for explicit signing steps and seed management features.
Key Takeaway

Testnet practice builds confidence in key generation, signing, and transaction flows without financial risk.
