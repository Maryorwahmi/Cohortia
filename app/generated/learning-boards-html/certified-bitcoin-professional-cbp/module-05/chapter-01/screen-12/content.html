Combining Hot and Cold Wallets
Match accessibility to the amount at risk and define transfer workflows.
Pattern
Maintain a small hot wallet balance for spending and a larger cold wallet for savings.
When more spending funds are needed, sign a transfer from cold to hot using secure signing procedures.
Automate nothing that requires exposing the private key to an online environment during normal storage.
Concrete example
Keep one month's spending in a mobile wallet and the remainder on a hardware wallet; replenish the hot wallet from cold storage on a scheduled cadence.
Operational guidance
Document your transfer steps and rehearse on testnet to avoid mistakes during real operations.
Keep transaction limits and approvals to avoid frequent cold wallet access.
Verify device and seed integrity before every transfer from cold storage.
Key Takeaway

A hybrid approach preserves convenience while protecting large holdings; plan and rehearse secure transfer workflows.
