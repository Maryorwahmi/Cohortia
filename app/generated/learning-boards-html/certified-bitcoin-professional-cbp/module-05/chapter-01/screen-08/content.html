Seed Phrases and Wallet Recovery
A mnemonic that restores private keys if you lose your device.
What is a seed phrase?
Sequence of 12, 18, or 24 words generated by the wallet representing your key data.
Used to recreate private keys and recover wallet access on new devices.
Acts as a single portable backup; its secrecy is critical.
Concrete example
When a hardware device fails, you can recreate the private keys by entering the original seed phrase into a compatible wallet.
Storage guidance
Prefer offline storage: paper in a safe, metal backup, or secure physical vaults.
Avoid digital copies on internet-connected devices or cloud services.
Consider geographically separated backups for disaster resilience.
Key Takeaway

Seed phrases enable recovery, but they are extremely sensitive. Protect them offline with physical backups and access controls.
