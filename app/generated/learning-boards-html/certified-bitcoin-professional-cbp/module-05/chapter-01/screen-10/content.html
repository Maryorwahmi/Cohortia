Definition Wallet Responsibilities
Wallet Software Responsibilities
Derive addresses, scan the ledger, compute balances, and surface history.
Core tasks
  • Derive addresses from the seed or private key.
  • Scan the blockchain for transactions that involve those addresses.
  • Calculate spendable balances by aggregating unspent outputs.
  • Present transaction history and status to the user.
Concrete example
A wallet that queries a node or service finds incoming outputs to your addresses and updates the displayed balance when confirmations occur.
Trust & privacy trade-offs
Full-node wallets validate the blockchain locally and offer stronger privacy.
Light wallets may query third-party servers, which simplifies use but may leak address linkage.
Understand your wallet's mode to assess privacy and trust implications.
Key Takeaway

Wallets map your private keys to ledger entries by scanning for related transactions. Know whether your wallet validates locally or relies on external services.
