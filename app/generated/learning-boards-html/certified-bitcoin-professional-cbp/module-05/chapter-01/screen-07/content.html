Cold Wallets — Offline Security for Long-Term Storage
Offline devices and media that keep keys away from internet threats.
Forms & properties
  • Hardware wallets: dedicated devices that sign transactions without exposing keys.
  • Paper wallets: printed keys kept physically, very low online risk but high physical fragility.
  • Cold storage reduces online attack vectors but requires physical protections and backups.
Concrete example
A hardware device stores a seed phrase internally and signs transactions after user confirmation, keeping the private key isolated from the host computer.
Risks & mitigations
Physical loss or damage: store backup copies in secure, separate locations.
Seed compromise through poor storage: avoid digital photos and cloud backups of seed phrases.
Human error during setup: verify seeds and use trusted setup procedures.
Key Takeaway

Cold storage is the recommended pattern for large, long-term holdings; plan physical backups and secure storage carefully.
