Hot Wallets — Convenience vs Risk
Connected wallets suited for frequent transactions with higher online exposure.
Characteristics
  • Connected to the internet: desktop, mobile, or web-based.
  • Fast access to funds and easy transaction creation.
  • Exposed to malware, phishing, and remote exploits.
Concrete example
A mobile app that stores keys on a smartphone and lets you scan QR codes to pay for coffee.
Common mistake
Keeping large holdings in a hot wallet out of convenience increases the chance of irreversible loss.
Guidance & takeaway
Use hot wallets for day-to-day transactions and small balances.
Ensure device hygiene, verified wallet software, and enable any available security features.
Consider keeping most funds in cold storage and moving smaller amounts into a hot wallet when needed.
Key Takeaway

Hot wallets are practical for frequent use, but you must accept and mitigate higher online risk.
