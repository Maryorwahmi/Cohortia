Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Sending BTC Securely
Flow
Compose → Send to device → Verify → Sign
Compose transaction in companion software with recipient, amount, and fee.
Receive the unsigned transaction on the hardware wallet for review.
Carefully read recipient and amount on the device before pressing confirm.
Pro tip: if the device screen truncates the address, compare start and end segments or use the device's full-address view before confirming.
Scenario
If your app shows address A but the device shows address B, cancel immediately. This is the classic sign of malware attempting address substitution. Do not proceed until you can confirm the device displays the expected address.
Check
  • Did you verify both recipient and amount on-device?
  • Is the fee acceptable and shown on-device?
