Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Hardware Wallet
A device that stores private keys offline and signs transactions without exposing keys to a computer.
Seed Phrase
12/18/24 words that are the master backup for all funds; protect them offline and redundantly.
On-device Verification
Always confirm recipient, amount, and fees on the device screen; the device is the trusted source.
Final Takeaway

Layer these practices — secure device, protected seed, and verification habit — to maintain both security and recoverability for your Bitcoin holdings.
