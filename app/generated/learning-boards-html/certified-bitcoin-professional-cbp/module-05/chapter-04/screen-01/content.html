Hardware Wallets: Setup, Usage, and Security

Learn how these purpose-built devices generate and protect your seed phrase, enable trusted transaction signing, and reduce exposure to online threats.

In this lesson

A correctly used hardware wallet prevents many common attacks and preserves recoverability through the seed phrase.
