Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Quick Reference
Buy official
Reduce tampering risk by purchasing from the manufacturer or authorized resellers.
Protect seed offline
Write the seed on paper or steel and store copies in separate locations; never digitize.
Verify every tx
Read and confirm recipient, amount, and fee on the device before signing to prevent redirection.
Remember

Use these three rules as your everyday checklist when setting up, storing, or transacting with a hardware wallet.
