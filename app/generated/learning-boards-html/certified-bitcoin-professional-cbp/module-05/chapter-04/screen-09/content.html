Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Passphrase (25th Word)
Definition

Optional extra word or phrase

The passphrase augments the seed to derive a distinct wallet. It functions like a second secret that must be remembered or securely stored separately from the seed.
Misconception: Storing the passphrase together with the seed negates its protective value. Keep them separate.
Example and Guidance
If you use a passphrase, choose a phrase you can reliably reproduce or store it in a secure, offline location distinct from the seed. Consider writing a recovery plan so an heir can access funds if appropriate, while still preserving secrecy.
Caution

Forgetting the passphrase means permanent loss of the hidden wallet, even if you have the seed phrase.
