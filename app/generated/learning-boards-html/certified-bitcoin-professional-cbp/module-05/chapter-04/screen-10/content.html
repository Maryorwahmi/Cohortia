Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Firmware & Supply Chain
Firmware
Patch management through official channels
Apply firmware updates via the official companion software to receive verified patches. These address vulnerabilities and improve device resilience.
Mistake: Installing firmware from an unknown source can introduce malicious code. Always follow manufacturer guidance.
Supply chain
Purchase only from trusted channels
Buying direct from the manufacturer or authorized resellers reduces the risk of receiving a tampered or pre-initialized device. Unofficial marketplaces can be risky.
Action

Verify reseller authorization and inspect the device for seals before setup. If in doubt, reset and initialize the device yourself before using.
