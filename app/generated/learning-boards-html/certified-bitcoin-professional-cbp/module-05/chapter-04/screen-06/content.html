Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Seed Phrase Protection
Comparison

Paper vs Digital vs Steel

Paper
Easy to write and copy, vulnerable to fire, water, and theft. Use sealed storage.
Digital (photo/file)
Strongly discouraged — exposes the phrase to malware and cloud compromise.
Stamped steel
Highly durable against water and fire and recommended for long-term storage of critical words.
Practical example
Store one paper copy at home in a fireproof container and a second copy in a safety deposit box or secure off-site location. Ensure they are separate so a single disaster cannot destroy both.
Misconception check
Encrypting a seed file on your laptop still exposes it to local malware or cloud sync. Treat any digital copy as insecure.
Action

Create at least two offline backups on different media and in different physical locations; never digitize the seed.
