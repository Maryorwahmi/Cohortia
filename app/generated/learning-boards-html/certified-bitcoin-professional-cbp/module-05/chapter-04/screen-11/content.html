Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Mistakes & Recovery
Top mistakes
  • Not writing the seed down accurately.
  • Keeping the seed as a photo or cloud file.
  • Sharing the seed or PIN with others.
Recovery scenarios
If the device is lost but you have the seed, you can restore on a new device. If you lose both device and seed, funds are permanently inaccessible. Manufacturer support cannot recreate your private keys for you.
Check: do you have at least two offline seed backups in separate locations?
