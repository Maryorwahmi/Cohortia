Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Operational Security
Daily habits
  • Always verify recipient and amount on-device before approving.
  • Do not store seed digitally or photograph it.
  • Use a unique, non-obvious PIN and change if compromised.
Long-term storage
Choose fire- and water-resistant materials for seed backups, keep multiple copies in separate locations, and review your backup plan periodically. Consider steel stamping for the highest durability.
Practice recovery mentally or with a controlled test restore to ensure backups are correct and complete.
