Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What is a Hardware Wallet?
Definition

Dedicated device that signs transactions securely

Stores private keys in a tamper-resistant secure element and performs signing internally so keys never touch an internet-connected computer.

Secure Element
Isolated chip that holds keys and executes cryptography.
Contrast: Paper Wallets
Paper is fragile and exposes keys during generation; hardware wallets perform signing without exposing keys.
Key Takeaway

Treat the device as the secure vault and the seed phrase as the ultimate recovery key. Both must be protected.

Example & Misconception
Example: You initiate a send in companion software on your computer, but the device displays final recipient and amount and only after you press buttons will it sign. Misconception: People sometimes think a device alone is enough; in reality, buying a tampered device or failing to back up the seed makes the setup fragile.
