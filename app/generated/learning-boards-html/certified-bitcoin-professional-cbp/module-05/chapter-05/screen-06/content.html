Definition

BIP39: Mnemonic Seed Phrases

A human-readable list of words that encodes entropy; used to generate the master seed for HD wallets.

Example (illustrative)
'zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo' — sample only, not secure.
Never reuse sample or weak mnemonics in production.
Passphrase note
An optional extra secret appended to the mnemonic. It strengthens security but must be backed up carefully.
Misconception
The mnemonic is not the derivation algorithm — it is the input. Losing the mnemonic or its passphrase makes recovery impossible.
Key Takeaway

Treat the mnemonic and any passphrase as the single most critical backup item. Secure generation and storage protect all derived funds.
