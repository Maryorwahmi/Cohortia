Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Multisig Use Cases
Personal: 2-of-3
Two hardware wallets in separate locations plus a third secure backup provide resilience to device loss.
Business treasury
Require multiple executives to sign large payments to enforce internal controls and auditing.
Escrow (2-of-3)
Buyer, seller, and a trusted arbitrator share keys; two-party agreement releases funds to prevent cheating.
Geographic separation
Distribute keys across locations to prevent local disasters from causing total key loss.
Operational caution
Plan signing workflows, backups, and key rotation. Complexity is the main trade-off for added security.
Key Takeaway

Match the multisig structure to your risk model: redundancy for individuals, governance for companies, and impartial resolution for escrow.
