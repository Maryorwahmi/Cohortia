Learning Objectives

Master the standards and design choices that shape HD and multisig wallets.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain HD wallets

Describe deterministic key trees and why one seed replaces many backups.

Mnemonic: imagine a single root tree feeding many branches.

02
Understand BIP32, BIP39, BIP44

Name each standard's role: derivation, mnemonic generation, and standardized paths for interoperability.

Mnemonic: think BIP32 = tree, BIP39 = words, BIP44 = map.

03
Describe multisig wallets

Explain M-of-N schemes and how multiple signatures authorize a spend.

Mnemonic: locks and keys — multiple keys required to open the lock.

04
Outline multisig flows

Step through generating keys, creating the multisig address, signing, co-signing, and broadcasting.

Mnemonic: think prepare → sign → co-sign → broadcast.
