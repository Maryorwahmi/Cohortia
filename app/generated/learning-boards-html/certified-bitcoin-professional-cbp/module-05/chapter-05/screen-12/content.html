Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts

What to Remember

HD Wallet
One mnemonic regenerates a deterministic tree of keys.
BIP32 / BIP39 / BIP44
Standards that provide mnemonic generation, key derivation, and path conventions.
Multisig
M-of-N thresholds distribute signing authority and reduce single points of failure.
Summary

Combine HD backups with clear BIP-compatible paths and a documented multisig policy to achieve both recoverability and robust custody controls.
