Process Flow

Creating & Transacting: 6 Steps

Step 1: Generate public keys
Each participant creates a private key and shares its public key only.
Step 2: Create multisig address
Combine public keys into an M-of-N script to produce a multisig address.
Step 3: Fund the address
Send the intended funds to the multisig address and confirm on-chain.
Step 4: Initiate transaction
A participant constructs a raw transaction and signs their part, producing a partially signed transaction.
Step 5: Co-sign
Pass the partial transaction to required signers who add signatures until the threshold is met.
Step 6: Broadcast
Once M signatures are present, broadcast the fully signed transaction to the network.
Concrete scenario
Alice signs a raw 1 BTC transaction, sends it to Bob who co-signs. With two signatures the transaction is broadcast.
Common Mistakes

Not backing up each participant's seed, unclear signing handoff, and failing to test with small amounts are frequent errors.
