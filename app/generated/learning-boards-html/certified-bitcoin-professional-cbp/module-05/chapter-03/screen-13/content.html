Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Assessing Choices
Scenario
Sarah stores life savings but needs occasional spending. Recommend a hardware wallet for the majority and a mobile wallet for small transactions — a classic hybrid.
Questions to ask
  • How often do you need access?
  • Are you comfortable managing offline backups?
  • Who can recover funds if something happens to you?
Reflection
Decide your own split: how much for spending, trading, and savings? Write this plan down and specify backup locations so choices are actionable during an emergency.
Key Takeaway

Translate risk tolerance into a concrete split and a documented backup plan to make your strategy resilient and repeatable.
