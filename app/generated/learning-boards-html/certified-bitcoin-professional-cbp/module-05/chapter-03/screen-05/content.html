Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Types of Hot Wallets
Wallet comparison
Desktop wallet
Good for management and larger transfers when your computer is secure.
Mobile wallet
Fast payments and QR scanning; protect the device from loss and malware.
Web wallet
Accessible across devices but exposed to browser risks and phishing.
Exchange wallet
Custodial and convenient for trading; requires trust in the provider.
Scenario card
Scenario
You travel and need quick payments. A mobile wallet with a small balance is ideal, while your savings stay on a hardware device.
Misconception check
Having many hot wallets increases risk if you reuse passwords or backups; centralize your recovery plan even for multiple hot apps.
Key Takeaway

Pick a hot wallet type based on access needs, then limit the balance to what you can afford to lose.
