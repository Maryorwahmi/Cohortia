Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Memory Aids
Exchange Wallet
Custodial and convenient for trading; not ideal for large, long‑term holdings.
Hardware Wallet
Preferred cold storage device; signs offline and keeps keys isolated.
Hybrid Strategy
Split funds by access needs, set replenishment rules, and document backups.
Final Thought

Small, simple protections reduce risk dramatically. Start with one action: secure a seed physically, move a major holding offline, or test recovery today.
