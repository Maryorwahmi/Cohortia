Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Common Errors
Top mistakes
  • Keeping large holdings on exchanges for convenience.
  • Storing seed phrases digitally or as photos.
  • Generating paper wallets on internet‑connected machines.
  • Failing to test a recovery before moving real funds.
Remedies
Custody plan
Use exchanges for trading only; move long‑term holdings to cold storage promptly.
Backup hygiene
Write seeds by hand, store multiple copies, and consider metal plates for durability.
Recovery drill
Practice restoring a wallet from a simulated seed to confirm your process.
Key Takeaway

Most losses come from preventable mistakes. Use clear processes and test them before trusting real funds.
