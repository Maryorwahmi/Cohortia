Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Practice Outcomes
Practice tasks
  • Simulate creating a wallet and writing a seed phrase on paper, then plan secure storage locations.
  • Consider how to split holdings between a mobile wallet for daily use and a hardware wallet for savings.
  • Identify three concrete steps to reduce hot wallet risk: device hygiene, strong passphrases, and two‑factor authentication.
  • Design a backup scheme with geographically separated copies of recovery information.
Why this helps

Applied practice builds habits. Writing simulated seed phrases and planning where to store them prepares you for real secure setups and reduces the chance of irreversible loss.
