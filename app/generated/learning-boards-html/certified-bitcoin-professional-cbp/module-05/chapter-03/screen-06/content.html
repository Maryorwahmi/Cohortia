Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › What is a Cold Wallet?
Definition

Offline storage for long‑term holdings

Cold wallets keep private keys away from networks, making online attacks like malware and phishing ineffective.

Concrete example
A hardware device that stores a 12‑ or 24‑word seed and signs transactions offline.
Analogy
Think of cold storage as placing the keys in a physical vault rather than carrying them on your person.
Risks & Protections
Paper wallets
Offline but vulnerable to damage and risky during generation if not air‑gapped.
Hardware wallets
Designed to sign offline; protect the device and store recovery seeds separately.
Key Takeaway

Cold storage minimizes online attack vectors but demands careful backup, generation, and physical security practices.
