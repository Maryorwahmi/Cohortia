Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Trade-offs
Decision framework
Frequency of access
High frequency favors hot wallets; rare access favors cold.
Technical comfort
If you can manage backups and recovery, cold storage becomes practical.
Loss tolerance
The higher the consequence of loss, the more you should favor cold storage.
Practical rule
Rule of thumb
Keep only what you are comfortable losing in hot wallets; protect the rest cold.
Scenario
If you check prices daily and trade often, you may keep a larger exchange balance temporarily, but plan withdrawal and cold storage as soon as possible.
Key Takeaway

Define tiers and rules for when to move funds between hot and cold to keep access predictable and secure.
