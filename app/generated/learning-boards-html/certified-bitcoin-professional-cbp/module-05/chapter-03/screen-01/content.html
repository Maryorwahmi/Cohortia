Hot vs. Cold Wallets

Learn the security trade-offs and plan a hybrid strategy to protect the bulk of your Bitcoin while keeping spending agile.

Duration: ~12 minutes lesson
Hot Wallets
Desktop, mobile, web, and exchange wallets for daily use.
Cold Wallets
Hardware devices and offline backups for long‑term security.
Hybrid Strategy
Split holdings and backups to balance access and protection.
In this lesson
Understand risks, catalog wallet types, and design secure backups.
You'll practice a simulated backup and compare the pros and cons of keeping funds online versus offline.
Visual Concept
Hot: Fast spending, online access
Cold: Offline keys, stronger protection
Hybrid: Best of both when planned
Coming up
Detailed profiles of hot and cold wallets, threats and mitigations, a hands‑on simulation, and practical backup guidance.
