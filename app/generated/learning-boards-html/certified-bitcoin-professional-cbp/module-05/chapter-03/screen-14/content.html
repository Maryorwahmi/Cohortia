Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Hot Wallet
Internet‑connected, convenient, suitable for small balances.
Cold Wallet
Offline keys for long‑term security; hardware wallets are common.
Seed Phrase
12–24 words that recover your wallet; keep physical backups and test recovery.
Summary Takeaway

Adopt a tiered hybrid approach: small hot balances for spending, large cold holdings protected by proper generation and multiple backups.
