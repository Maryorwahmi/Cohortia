Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Seed Phrases & Recovery
Definition

A backup that rebuilds keys

The seed phrase is the canonical method to recover wallets across devices. It must be copied exactly and protected physically.

Do
Write seeds by hand, store multiple copies in different physical locations, and prefer durable media.
Don't
Take screenshots, store seeds in cloud storage, or email them to yourself.
Example & Test
Practice writing a simulated 12‑word phrase and run a non‑funded restore on a spare wallet to confirm the words were captured correctly.
Misconception
Memorizing a passphrase is not a reliable sole backup unless you can demonstrate exact recall under stress or loss scenarios.
Key Takeaway

Treat seed phrases as the single most important recovery item; protect them like physical currency in a vault.
