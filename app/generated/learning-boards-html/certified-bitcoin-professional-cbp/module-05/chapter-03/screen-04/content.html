Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › What is a Hot Wallet?
Definition

Connected wallets for everyday use

A hot wallet stores keys on devices or services that touch the internet, enabling quick sending and receiving of Bitcoin.

Concrete example
A mobile wallet used to pay a friend or a web wallet for trading small amounts.
Common misconception
Being online doesn't automatically mean total insecurity; small balances with protections can be practical.
Warning
Do not keep large, long‑term savings on internet‑connected wallets without additional safeguards.
Types & Use
Desktop & Mobile
Apps that store keys on your device for frequent transactions.
Web & Exchange Wallets
Browser or custodial services that may hold keys for you for convenience or trading.
Key takeaway
Use hot wallets for amounts you need quick access to and apply strong device hygiene and two‑factor protections.
