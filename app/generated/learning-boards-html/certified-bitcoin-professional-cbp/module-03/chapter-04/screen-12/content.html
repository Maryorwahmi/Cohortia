Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Fork Implications
Security and economics
A fork changes hash power distribution and can alter how costly it is to attack a chain. New assets emerge with separate market values and adoption trajectories.
User impact
Holders at the fork point may control coins on both chains; wallets and exchanges must decide how to handle both histories.
Governance impact
Forks reflect unresolved community choices about protocol trade-offs and can influence future coordination costs.
Operational preparations
Service operators should plan for replay protection, decide whether to support multiple chains, and communicate clearly with users about custody and upgrade paths.
Reflection

Consider the trade-offs between innovation and compatibility. How would your organization balance them during a proposed protocol change?
