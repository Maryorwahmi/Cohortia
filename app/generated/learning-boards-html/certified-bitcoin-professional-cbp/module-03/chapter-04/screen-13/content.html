Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Research Activity
Activity instructions
  • Choose SegWit, Bitcoin Cash, or Bitcoin SV as your case study.
  • Explain the primary technical debate that caused the fork.
  • State whether it was a soft fork or hard fork and why.
  • Describe how consensus was achieved or not and the fork's immediate and long-term impacts.
Assessment prompt
Write a 200–300 word summary addressing the points above and add a brief reflection on decentralized governance challenges illustrated by the fork.
Example research checklist
Identify the cause, classify the fork type, describe miner and node responses, and note resulting changes to the ecosystem such as new chains or compatibility shifts.
Key Takeaway

This exercise reinforces how protocol design, network incentives, and coordination determine whether an upgrade preserves a single chain or creates a split.
