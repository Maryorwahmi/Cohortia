Counting confirmations
A confirmation is a block on top of the block that includes your transaction. Each additional block increases difficulty for reversal by requiring more re-mining work.
Example
If your transaction is in Block N and Blocks N+1 through N+5 are added, you have five confirmations. Reversing it requires re-mining Blocks N through N+5 faster than the honest network.
Practical rule
Merchants typically wait 3 to 6 confirmations for high-value transactions to reduce reversal risk.

Key Takeaway

Set confirmation thresholds proportional to value. Confirmations reduce risk probabilistically, not absolutely.
