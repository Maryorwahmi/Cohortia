Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Orphan Block
A valid block that is not part of the longest chain after a competing branch wins.
Soft Fork
A backward-compatible protocol change where old nodes still accept new blocks.
Hard Fork
A non-backward-compatible change that can split the chain into two incompatible ledgers.
Quick Takeaway

Keep these concise definitions ready: they explain why confirmations, compatibility, and coordination are central to Bitcoin's reliability.
