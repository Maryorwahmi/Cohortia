Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Miner Selection & Fees
Comparison
High fee per byte vs Low fee per byte
Miners rank transactions by expected reward per byte. A higher fee per byte typically yields earlier inclusion.
Higher fee per byte → faster inclusion
Larger transactions need higher fees to be competitive
Low-fee txs may be delayed or dropped during congestion
Concrete example
Two transactions enter a mempool: a compact one paying a moderate fee per byte, and a large one paying the same total fee but lower fee per byte. Miners will usually include the compact higher-fee-per-byte transaction first because it maximizes fee income per block space.
Misconception
Assuming miners include transactions in chronological order is incorrect; miners maximize fee revenue when choosing transactions.
Key Takeaway

Fee per byte is the main priority signal; choose fees according to current mempool pressure to control confirmation time.
