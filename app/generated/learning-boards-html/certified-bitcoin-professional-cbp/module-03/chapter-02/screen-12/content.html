Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Block Size Limit & Throughput
Definition
Limits → throughput → fee market
Block size or weight limits restrict total transaction data per block. The ten-minute average block time together with this limit sets maximum network throughput, shaping the fee market during demand spikes.
Limited space means competition when demand rises
SegWit changes counting rules and increases effective capacity
Throughput limits explain why fees spike under heavy usage
Concrete insight
Because block space is scarce over each ten-minute interval, fee bidding emerges: users attach higher fees to reduce waiting. This mechanism aligns miner incentives with demand for confirmation.
Misconception
Higher fees do not expand block capacity; they only change ordering of transactions competing for limited space.
Key Takeaway

Block limits plus average block time set throughput; fees coordinate user demand for that limited space.
