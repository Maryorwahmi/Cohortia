Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Assessment Questions
Question 1
Role of the mempool and fees
Explain how the mempool stores unconfirmed transactions and why miners prioritize by fee per byte. Include what happens during high congestion.
Correct answers mention that miners select transactions to maximize fee income and that low-fee transactions can be delayed or dropped when mempools are full.
Question 2
Merkle Tree purpose
Describe how a Merkle Tree summarizes transactions and how a Merkle Root allows compact inclusion proofs for light clients.
A strong answer explains hashing leaves, combining pairs upward, and using Merkle paths to prove inclusion without full block download.
Key Takeaway

Answering process and rationale questions shows deeper understanding than simple definitions; connect steps to why they exist.
