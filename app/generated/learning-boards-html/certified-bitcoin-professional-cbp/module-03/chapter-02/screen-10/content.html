Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Sending vs Ownership Transfer
Comparison
Sending coins vs Changing ownership
Instead of moving coins, transactions reassign which public key can spend a UTXO. New outputs represent newly owned UTXOs.
Recipient output ≈ new UTXO controlled by recipient
Change output ≈ new UTXO controlled by sender
Inputs are spent and no longer available
Practical pointer
When reading a TxID, the largest output is not always the recipient; sometimes the change output looks similar in size. Identifying change often requires reasoning about where the sender controls an address.
Misconception
Don't assume addresses are account ledgers. Instead, track UTXOs and the addresses that control them to understand where funds moved.
Key Takeaway

Transactions update ownership of UTXOs; reading outputs carefully helps you identify recipients and change addresses.
