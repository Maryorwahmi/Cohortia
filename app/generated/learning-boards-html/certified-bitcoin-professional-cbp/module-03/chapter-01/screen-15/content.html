Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Blockchain
A distributed, immutable ledger of linked blocks recording transactions across many nodes.
Immutability
Cryptographic hashes and chaining ensure changes to history are visible and costly to conceal.
Decentralization & Transparency
Many nodes hold copies, making the ledger resilient and publicly verifiable while addresses remain pseudonymous.
Takeaway
Linked blocks, replicated nodes, and public verifiability together create a tamper-resistant ledger you can verify yourself.
