Definition

Transparency vs Pseudonymity

Every transaction is public and can be examined; addresses act as pseudonyms rather than direct personal identifiers.

Public audit
Anyone can follow amounts and addresses to verify that transactions occurred as recorded.
Pseudonymous addresses
Addresses represent control over funds but are not direct proofs of identity without external linkage.
Example & Implications
Concrete example
You can inspect a transaction and verify that 2 BTC moved from address A to address B. Without off-chain data, you cannot conclude who controls addresses A and B.
Common misconception
Public visibility is not the same as public identification. Addresses are pseudonymous placeholders that only reveal identity with additional external information.
Key Takeaway

Transparency allows anyone to verify transactions; pseudonymity preserves privacy of identity unless external linkage is available.
