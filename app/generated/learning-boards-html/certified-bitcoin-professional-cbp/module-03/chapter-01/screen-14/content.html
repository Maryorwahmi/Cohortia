Assessment Idea

Two short prompts

Q1 — Immutability
Explain how cryptographic hashing and previous_hash fields make historic tampering practically infeasible. Use the hashing experiment as an example.
Q2 — Decentralization
Describe the difference between a central bank ledger and Bitcoin's distributed ledger and list key advantages of Bitcoin's approach.
Scoring hints
  • Q1: Mention hash sensitivity, previous_hash mismatch, and cost to recompute work across blocks.
  • Q2: Mention single point of failure versus many nodes, censorship resistance, and public verifiability.
Pro tip

When answering, include a brief concrete scenario to show you can apply the concept, not just recite it.
