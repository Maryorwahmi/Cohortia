The Bitcoin Blockchain: A Distributed Ledger

Understand how blocks are formed, linked with cryptographic hashes, and maintained by a global network of nodes. Learn why this design creates immutability and public verifiability without a central authority.

In this lesson
Explore blocks, block headers, hashes, decentralization, and a hands-on hashing example that shows how tampering breaks a chain.
Why it matters
A tamper-resistant public ledger changes how we trust records: verification replaces blind trust in a single custodian.
Explore Key Topics
Blocks & Headers
What each block stores and why the header is critical for linking.
Decentralization
How many nodes holding the ledger remove single points of control.
Immutability
Why cryptographic linking makes historical tampering infeasible.

Quick note
Nodes hold identical copies of this chain. If one copy is changed by an attacker, honest nodes will reject it because the hashes no longer match.
