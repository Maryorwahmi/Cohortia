Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › What to Remember
Block
A container of transactions plus header metadata that identifies it cryptographically.
Link (Hash)
A cryptographic fingerprint stored in the next block to ensure continuity and integrity.
Node
An independent participant that stores and validates the ledger, sustaining decentralization.
Summary strip
Keep these three words in mind: blocks, links, nodes. Together they define Bitcoin's distributed and tamper-resistant ledger.
