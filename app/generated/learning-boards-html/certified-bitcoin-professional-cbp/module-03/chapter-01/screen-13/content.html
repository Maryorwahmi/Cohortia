Common Mistake

Blockchain ≠ Bitcoin

Blockchain is a general design; Bitcoin is a specific public ledger that implements that design using proof-of-work and open participation.

Differences to highlight
Consensus mechanism, permission model, and the degree of decentralization can vary widely across blockchains.
Implication
Properties like censorship resistance and public verifiability depend on implementation choices, not the abstract term 'blockchain' alone.
Scenario & Reflection
Scenario
If a ledger restricted who could write blocks to a small committee, it would be more centralized and lose some censorship resistance even if it used block linking.
Key Takeaway

Always distinguish the abstract pattern 'blockchain' from the particular rules and properties of Bitcoin's implementation.
