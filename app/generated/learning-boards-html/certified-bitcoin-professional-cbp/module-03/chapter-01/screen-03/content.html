Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Learning Objectives
01 • Explain
Describe a blockchain as a distributed ledger
Practice: Explain with the 'page and stamp' analogy and name the properties that differ from a central ledger.
02 • Differentiate
Contrast centralized versus decentralized ledgers
Practice: List advantages such as resilience, censorship resistance, and public verifiability.
03 • Describe
Identify block header and body components
Practice: Point out previous_hash, timestamp, Merkle root, and nonce and explain their roles.
04 • Understand
Explain immutability and transparency
Practice: Run the hashing experiment that changes one transaction and observe the broken link.
