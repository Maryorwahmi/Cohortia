Comparison

Block reward vs transaction fees

Block reward is deterministic and halves periodically. Fees fluctuate with network demand and miner selection strategies.
Block reward
Fixed subsidy included by the miner, currently 3.125 BTC as noted in this chapter.
Transaction fees
Variable payments users include to prioritize confirmation; chosen by miners when selecting transactions.
Scenario
If fees fall and become insufficient to cover costs after repeated halvings, some miners may stop mining, reducing hash rate and triggering a difficulty decrease at the next adjustment.
Practical impact
Users set fees to influence inclusion; miners select higher-fee transactions to maximize revenue per block.
Key Takeaway

Block reward and fees together determine miner revenue. As block reward declines, fees become relatively more important for miner economics.
