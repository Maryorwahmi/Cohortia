Comparison

Solo vs pooled mining

Pools combine hash power to increase the chance of finding blocks and distribute rewards, reducing variance for individual miners.

Solo mining
Rare but full block rewards when successful; typically unprofitable for most individuals due to high difficulty.
Pool mining
Frequent, smaller payouts distributed according to contributed hash power; reduces income variance.
Practical note
Most individual miners join pools because solo chances are negligible at current difficulty levels. Pools add operational fees and potential centralization concerns if a pool dominates hash rate share.
Common mistake
Assuming pools change protocol rules — they only aggregate miners and distribute rewards according to their chosen scheme.
Key Takeaway

Pools smooth miner income and improve predictability for small participants, but they create trade-offs around fees and concentration.
