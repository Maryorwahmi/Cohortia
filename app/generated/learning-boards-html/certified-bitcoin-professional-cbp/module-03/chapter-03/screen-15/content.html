Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › What to Remember
Nonce & target
The adjustable nonce and the protocol target define the brute-force challenge miners solve.
Difficulty & timing
Difficulty adjusts every 2016 blocks to hold average block time near ten minutes.
Incentives & pools
Block rewards and fees pay miners; pools help small miners smooth payouts but trade off fees and concentration risk.
Final takeaway

Mining combines brute-force computation, automatic difficulty adjustment, and economic incentives to secure Bitcoin and maintain a predictable issuance schedule.
