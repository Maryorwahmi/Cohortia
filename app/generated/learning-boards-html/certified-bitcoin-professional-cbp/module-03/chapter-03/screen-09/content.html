Monetary Policy

Block reward and halving

Each mined block awards a block reward to the miner. This reward halves approximately every 210,000 blocks, reducing new issuance over time toward a capped supply.

Current reward
As stated in this chapter, the block reward is 3.125 BTC.
Halving rhythm
Halving occurs every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years, shrinking supply growth geometrically.
Example
A miner who mines a valid block includes the block reward in the coinbase transaction and collects transaction fees included in the block.
Economic effect
As rewards fall, transaction fees become a larger share of miner revenue, which affects long-term incentives.
Key Takeaway

Halvings are deterministic reductions in block reward that gradually shift mining revenue toward fees and enforce scarcity.
