NIST Essential Characteristics
A practical checklist for evaluating cloud services.
On-demand self-service
Users can provision compute, storage, or other services automatically without provider intervention.
Broad network access
Services are accessible over standard networks from a variety of client platforms.
Resource pooling
Provider resources are pooled and dynamically assigned to multiple consumers.
Rapid elasticity
Capabilities can be scaled rapidly to meet demand and released when no longer needed.
Measured service
Resource usage is monitored, controlled, and reported for transparency and billing.
Key Takeaway

These characteristics explain why cloud enables fast iteration, multi-client access, pooled efficiency, elasticity, and measurable cost—use them to compare services.

Example scenario
A load test where virtual servers are automatically added and removed demonstrates rapid elasticity; billing reports reflect measured service so teams can correlate cost to test duration.
Misconception check
Not every provider offers identical automation or metering granularity; verify the level of self-service and reporting when selecting a service.
