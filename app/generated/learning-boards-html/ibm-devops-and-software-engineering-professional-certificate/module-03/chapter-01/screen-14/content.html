Key Concepts
Three compact ideas to carry forward.
Cloud Computing
On-demand delivery of compute, storage, and services over the Internet, paid per use.
Service Models
IaaS, PaaS, SaaS describe who manages which layer of the stack.
Deployment Models
Public, private, hybrid are choices balancing control, compliance, and scale.
Summary takeaway
Use these concepts to map real systems to cloud models and to make trade-off decisions for architecture and operations.
