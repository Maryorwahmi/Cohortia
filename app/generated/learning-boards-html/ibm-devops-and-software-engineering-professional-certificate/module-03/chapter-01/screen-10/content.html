Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Platform as a Service (PaaS)
PaaS — platform for developers
Managed runtimes, middleware, and developer tools let teams deploy faster.
What PaaS provides
Application runtimes, buildpacks, managed databases, and deployment tooling.
Developer benefit
Focus on application code rather than OS patches or server maintenance.
Concrete example
Deploying to Cloud Foundry or OpenShift where the platform handles scaling and lifecycle of app instances.
Key Takeaway

PaaS speeds delivery by removing platform chores. Choose it when platform constraints are compatible with your app needs.

When PaaS may not fit
If you need custom OS kernels, device drivers, or system-level tweaks, PaaS may be too constrained.
