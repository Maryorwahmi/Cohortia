Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Software as a Service (SaaS)
SaaS — fully managed applications
Access software over the web while the provider manages everything behind it.
What SaaS offers
Complete applications delivered via browser or app with provider-managed updates and uptime.
Consumer benefit
Low operational overhead and rapid access to business functionality.
Concrete example
Email as a service or a CRM where the provider handles maintenance, security updates, and scaling.
Key Takeaway

SaaS is right when you want ready-to-use functionality and prefer to offload operational responsibilities to the provider.

Trade-offs
You trade operational simplicity for less control and potentially limited customization.
