In this lesson
We will compare VMs and containers, inspect Dockerfile structure, build and run a sample Python app, and review commands and best practices. Expect hands-on commands and a small lab to solidify learning.
Context
Containers virtualize at the OS level, not the hardware level. This saves resources and speeds up startup times compared to virtual machines.
Why it matters
Consistent environments reduce "works on my machine" issues and enable reliable CI/CD pipelines and microservice deployments.
