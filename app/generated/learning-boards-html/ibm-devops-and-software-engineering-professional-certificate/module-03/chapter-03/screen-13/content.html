Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Distributed System Challenges and Trade-offs
Challenges
  • Network latency and partial failure require retries, circuit breakers, and graceful degradation.
  • Eventual consistency replaces immediate consistency for many cross-service updates.
  • Distributed tracing and centralized metrics are needed to debug flows that cross services.
Concrete Scenario
If the payment gateway is down, Orders can accept requests to a queue and mark them pending. When payments resume, the queued messages are processed and reconciled.
Design Tip
Design workflows and compensating transactions to handle temporary inconsistencies and partial failures.
Misconception
Microservices do not remove race conditions or transaction complexity; they require architecture patterns to manage them across boundaries.
