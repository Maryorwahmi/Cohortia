Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Monolithic Architecture
A single, tightly coupled deployable unit that is simple initially but can become hard to evolve.
Microservices Architecture
An application composed of small, independently deployable services organized around business capabilities.
Independent Deployability
Deploy a single service without redeploying others, enabling faster releases and smaller blast radius.
Summary

Microservices provide autonomy, scalability, and technology flexibility, but require investment in service discovery, communication patterns, data ownership, CI/CD, and observability.
