Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Hands-on Activity: Designing E-commerce Services
Task Steps
  1. Identify at least five services by business capability.
  2. For each service, describe primary responsibilities and owned data.
  3. Pick communication styles for two service interactions and justify them.
  4. Outline CI/CD and observability measures required for the services.
Example Structure
Microservice: User Management — Responsibilities: register, authenticate. Data: user credentials and profile. Communication: REST for login flows.
Reflection Prompts
  • Which service would you scale independently during a flash sale and why?
  • Where would you choose asynchronous messaging instead of REST?
  • What observability metrics would indicate a service needs more instances?
Why it matters
This exercise builds your ability to make architecture decisions that balance autonomy, scalability, and operational cost.
