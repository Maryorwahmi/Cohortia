Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Scalability and Resource Optimization
Concept
Scale the service that needs it

When only one business capability has increased demand, run additional instances of that service rather than scaling the entire application. This reduces resource waste and cost in cloud environments.

Order Service
Scale instances during peak buying traffic.
Catalog Service
May need caching strategy rather than many pods.
Payment Service
Requires careful scaling when tied to transactional databases.
Design Note
Stateless services scale more easily. For stateful services, consider database sharding, caching, or specialized managed services that provide elastic capacity.
Key Takeaway

Plan scaling per service and design services for statelessness when possible to simplify elastic scaling.

Misconception
Scaling microservices always reduces cost; in some cases, many small services can increase overhead without proper automation.
