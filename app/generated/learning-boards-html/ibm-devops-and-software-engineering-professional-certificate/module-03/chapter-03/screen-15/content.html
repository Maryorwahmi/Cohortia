Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › What to Remember
Design around capabilities
Give each service a clear business role and the data it owns.
Choose communication patterns
Use REST for direct calls and message queues for decoupled workflows.
Invest in operations
CI/CD, discovery, and observability are prerequisites for scaling services reliably.
Closing Thought

When you evaluate an architecture change, match the operational cost of microservices to the real benefits in deployability and scalability for your organization.
