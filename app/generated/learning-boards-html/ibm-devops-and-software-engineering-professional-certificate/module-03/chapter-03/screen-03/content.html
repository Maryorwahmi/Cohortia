Practice & Patterns

Design service boundaries, choose communication methods, and plan CI/CD and cloud operations.

Identify service boundaries
List services by business capability and state what data each owns. This reduces coupling and clarifies responsibilities.
Choose communication patterns
Select synchronous REST for request/response paths or message queues for asynchronous, resilient interactions.
Plan CI/CD
Automate builds, tests, and deployments so teams can release services independently and safely.
Cloud alignment
Use managed infrastructure to scale individual services and rely on orchestration to manage many instances.
