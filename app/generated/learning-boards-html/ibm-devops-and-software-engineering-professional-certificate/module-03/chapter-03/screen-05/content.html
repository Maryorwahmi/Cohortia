Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Independent Deployability and CI/CD
Process
1
Code & Test
Unit and contract tests in the service repo validate behavior.
2
Build & Package
Artifacts or containers are produced for deployment.
3
Deploy & Observe
Automated deployment, health checks, and monitoring verify the release.
Concrete Example
A fix to the Payment service runs its pipeline only; automated contract tests ensure it still satisfies the Orders service expectations before production rollout.
Supporting Notes
  • Contract tests validate inter-service agreements without full integration runs.
  • Automated rollback and health checks reduce blast radius of faulty releases.
  • CI/CD pipelines must be reliable and fast to realize independent deployability benefits.
Misconception
Independent deployability does not mean services are independent of all testing; integration or contract testing remains necessary to ensure correct interactions.
