Process Flow
A short timeline from Agile to DevOps
Agile
Iterative development, rapid feedback within dev teams.
Integration
Frequent merges highlighted integration issues.
Operational Gap
Deployment and stability remain siloed and slow.
DevOps
Automation, shared ownership, and measurement close the loop.
Concrete example
A team that released monthly added automation and cross-training, reducing deployment time from hours to predictable minutes.
Misconception
Agile alone does not guarantee smooth operations; DevOps explicitly includes deployment and run-time concerns.
Why it matters
Extending iteration through operations enables faster feedback from production and better product outcomes.
