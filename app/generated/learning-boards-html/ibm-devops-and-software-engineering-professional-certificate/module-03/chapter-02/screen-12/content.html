Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Continuous Delivery vs Deployment
Comparison
Continuous Delivery
Automated builds and tests keep the codebase in a deployable state. Final deployment is a manual decision.
When to use
Teams that need manual checks before customer-facing releases or operate under strict compliance can adopt Continuous Delivery first.
Comparison
Continuous Deployment
Every change that passes automated validation is deployed to production automatically, requiring strong automated testing and monitoring.
When to use
Teams with mature automation and monitoring, and a tolerance for rapid customer-facing change, can adopt Continuous Deployment.
Misconception

Continuous Deployment is not universally superior; it requires mature automated tests and monitoring and may not suit all risk contexts.

Key Takeaway

Choose the automation level that matches your confidence in tests and your customer impact tolerance; work incrementally toward more automation.
