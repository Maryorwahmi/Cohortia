Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Sharing
Definition
Sharing practices that stick
Documentation, runbooks, incident reviews, and open channels all help teams learn faster and avoid repeat errors.
Concrete example
A team publishes a short runbook for a frequent alert. New engineers use it to reduce mean time to recovery.
Practical rule
Keep runbooks small, version-controlled, and easy to execute under stress.
Why it matters
Sharing reduces repeat troubleshooting, shortens recovery time, and spreads improvements across the organization.
Key Takeaway

Make learning accessible and operationally useful. The best documentation helps someone act under pressure.
