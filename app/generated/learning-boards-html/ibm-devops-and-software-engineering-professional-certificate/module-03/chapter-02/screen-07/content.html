Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Culture & Responsibility
Definition
Culture shifts needed for DevOps
Trust, shared responsibility, blameless post-mortems, and cross-training are key cultural practices.
Concrete example
Developers join on-call rotations and work with operations to improve deploy scripts, reducing time to resolve incidents.
Common mistake
Changing tools but not incentives: teams must be rewarded for shared outcomes, not isolated metrics.
Practical steps
  • • Hold blameless post-mortems focused on process improvements.
  • • Cross-train through rotations and paired work.
  • • Align team incentives around service health and delivery speed.
Key Takeaway

Cultural change amplifies technical improvements; without it, automation alone cannot deliver sustained benefits.
