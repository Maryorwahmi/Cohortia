Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Waterfall vs Iterative
Waterfall (sequential)
  • Phases complete in order with formal handoffs.
  • Best when requirements are stable and heavily regulated.
  • Risk: late discovery of flaws is costly.
Iterative (cycles)
  • Work in repeated smaller cycles that include design, build, and test.
  • Enables early feedback and adapts to changing requirements.
  • Risk: requires discipline to manage scope across iterations.
Scenario 1
Embedded flight control: Waterfall recommended due to stable requirements and high verification needs.
Scenario 2
Social media feature: Iterative recommended to gather user feedback quickly and evolve the feature.
