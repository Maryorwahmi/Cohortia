Introduction to Software Engineering and the SDLC
A structured approach to building, delivering, and maintaining reliable software.
Orientation

Software engineering applies disciplined methods to software development so teams can deliver predictable, maintainable solutions. You will learn common SDLC phases, why each matters, and how to select between Waterfall and Iterative approaches depending on project risk and change.

Deployment

Maintenance

Quick takeaway
Software engineering organizes work so teams can build dependable products and manage change across a system's lifetime.
