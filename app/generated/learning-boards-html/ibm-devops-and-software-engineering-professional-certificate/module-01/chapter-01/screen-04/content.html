Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What is Software Engineering?
Definition

Software engineering is a systematic, disciplined, and quantifiable approach to developing, operating, and maintaining software. It goes beyond coding to include planning, quality assurance, documentation, and maintainability.

Metaphor: treat software like a building with blueprints and inspection.
Common misconception

Software engineering is not just writing cleaner code; it includes processes, artifacts, and planning that support long-term reliability.

Concrete example

For an embedded flight control system, teams create detailed requirements and verification plans before coding. This ensures compliance with safety rules and lowers the chance of catastrophic failures.

Key Takeaway

Engineering practices make complex software predictable and maintainable across its lifecycle.
