Process Flow
Choose and sequence requirement-gathering techniques
Practical workflow to discover accurate, testable requirements.
Interviews
Deep insights from stakeholders; uncover goals and constraints.
Surveys
Quantify preferences and prioritize features across many users.
Observation
See real workflows and pain points users may not articulate.
Prototyping
Validate ideas quickly with mock-ups and early user feedback.
Example Sequence
Interview product owner → run a short survey → observe checkout flow → prototype the top change.
Trade-offs
Interviews are rich but slow; surveys are fast but shallow; combine them to balance speed and depth.
Tip
Document decisions and link each requirement to its source and acceptance criteria for traceability.
Key Takeaway

Use a mix of techniques to capture clear, testable requirements and to reduce blind spots that lead to rework.
