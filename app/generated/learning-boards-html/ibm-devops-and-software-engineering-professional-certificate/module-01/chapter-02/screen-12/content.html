Comparison
Tight vs Loose Coupling
Aim for well-defined interfaces to reduce the blast radius of changes.
Tight Coupling
Modules depend on each other's internals or share schema; changes require coordinated updates and risk regression.
Loose Coupling
Interactions occur via stable interfaces or APIs; modules can evolve and be deployed independently.
Example & Checklist
Example
If the cart relies only on an order API, a payment provider change does not require cart changes. That is loose coupling.
Coupling Checklist
  • Does the module expose a minimal API?
  • Is shared state avoided?
  • Can the dependency be swapped with a mock in tests?
Key Takeaway

Minimize knowledge about other modules’ internals and design stable contracts to reduce the cost and risk of change.
