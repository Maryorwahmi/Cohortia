Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Functional Requirements
Describe what the system does; write user stories with acceptance criteria.
Non-Functional Requirements
Quality attributes like performance and security; make them measurable and testable.
Design Principles
Modularity, cohesion, and coupling guide maintainable architecture and safe evolution.
Summary

Clear, measurable requirements plus disciplined design lead to systems that meet user needs and adapt over time.
