Definition of Done and the Increment
Quality gates that make Increments releasable and trustworthy.
  • The Increment is the sum of completed backlog items and previous Increments.
  • Definition of Done can include automated tests, integration, documentation, and deployable artifacts.
  • Without Done, reported progress may be misleading and releases risky.
Example
A watchlist card that displays correctly but lacks persistent storage and tests is not Done. Add persistence and tests to meet the Definition of Done.
Tip
Start with a minimal Definition of Done and grow it as automation reduces manual effort. Keep it visible to the team.
Key Takeaway

A clear Definition of Done is essential to deliver frequent, high-quality releases and avoid hidden work.
