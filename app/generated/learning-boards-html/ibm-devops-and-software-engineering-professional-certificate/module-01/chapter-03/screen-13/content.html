Fake Agile and sprint anti-patterns
Rituals without values lead to poor outcomes; focus on purpose and small improvements.
Anti-pattern: Mini-waterfall
Locking requirements and performing sequential handoffs inside a Sprint.
Anti-pattern: Scope creep
Adding work mid-Sprint without re-evaluating the Sprint Goal.
Anti-pattern: Check-the-box ceremonies
Holding stand-ups or retrospectives without action or reflection.
Corrective action
Run small experiments, protect the Sprint Goal, and teach the purpose of each ceremony.
Example workshop
Simulate adding a mid-Sprint request and show its impact on commitments; practice moving the item to the Product Backlog instead.
Role
The Scrum Master protects the team’s process and coaches stakeholders about why boundaries matter.
Key Takeaway

Ceremonies without values are ineffective; teach the purpose behind practices and run small experiments to improve.
