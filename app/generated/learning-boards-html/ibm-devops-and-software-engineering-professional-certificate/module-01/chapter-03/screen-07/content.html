Scrum events — a short process flow
Planning, Daily Scrum, Review, Retrospective — each has a clear purpose.
Sprint Planning
Select backlog items, create Sprint Backlog, set Sprint Goal.
Daily Scrum
15 minutes to synchronize and surface impediments; not a management status update.
Sprint Review
Inspect the Increment, gather stakeholder feedback, adapt the Product Backlog.
Sprint Retrospective
Reflect on process and plan improvements for the next Sprint.
Example
During a Daily Scrum the team highlights failing integration tests; they schedule a pairing session after the stand-up to fix the root cause rather than resolving it in the stand-up itself.
Tips
Keep timeboxes strict, protect the team from interruptions, and use Review feedback to re-order the Product Backlog. Retrospectives should produce a small, actionable improvement, not a long laundry list.
Key Takeaway

Events create rhythm and clear moments to inspect product and process, enabling continuous learning.
