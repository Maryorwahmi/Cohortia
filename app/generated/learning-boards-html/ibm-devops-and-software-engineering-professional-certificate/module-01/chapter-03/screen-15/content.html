Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Benefits of Agile
Practical benefits
Agile improves delivery speed, product quality, and team engagement when combined with good practices.
Faster delivery through short iterations and frequent releases.
Higher quality via early testing and incremental integration.
Greater customer satisfaction through continuous collaboration.
Improved team morale from empowerment and self-organization.
Implementation note
Pair Agile with DevOps automation and active stakeholder engagement to realize benefits and measure improvement with cycle time or feedback.
Pitfall
Introducing rituals without changing decision-making or tooling will not deliver the expected benefits.
Key Takeaway

Agile yields measurable improvements when practices, automation, and culture change align with its values.
