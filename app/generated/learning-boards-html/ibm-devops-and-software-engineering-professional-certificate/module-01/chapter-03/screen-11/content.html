Product Backlog
An ordered list of features, fixes, and enhancements reflecting product priorities.
  • Maintained by the Product Owner and refined over time.
  • Contains items of varying size and priority; higher items are more detailed.
  • Items can be added or reprioritized as new information arises.
Sprint Backlog
The Development Team's plan for the Sprint: selected Product Backlog items and the tasks to implement them.
  • Owned by the Development Team during the Sprint.
  • Should remain stable to protect the Sprint Goal; new work is usually deferred.
  • Broken into tasks that can be implemented and verified within the Sprint.
Example decision
When a disruptive bug appears mid-Sprint, the Scrum Master facilitates a trade-off discussion. If it must be addressed immediately, the team adapts; otherwise the item goes into the Product Backlog for future planning.
Key Takeaway

Backlogs balance long-term product vision with short-term focus; protect the Sprint Backlog to maintain predictability.
