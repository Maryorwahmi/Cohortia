Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Scrum vs Kanban
Scrum
Time-boxed Sprints, defined roles, and scheduled events for inspection.
Good when teams need structure and regular planning checkpoints.
Supports predictable increments and stakeholder reviews at Sprint end.
Kanban
Flow-focused, no required roles or Sprints, emphasis on WIP limits and continuous delivery.
Good when incoming work is variable and fast response is required.
Encourages finishing work before starting new items to improve throughput.
Hybrid approach
Teams often combine Scrum cadence with Kanban boards and WIP limits to get predictable planning and improved flow for day-to-day work.
Key Takeaway

Choose the framework that best matches team coordination needs and the nature of the work; hybrid models are common and practical.
