Introduction to Agile Methodologies
Why teams shift from rigid plans to iterative, collaborative delivery

In this lesson
Agile values, Scrum and Kanban basics, and how they connect to DevOps practices.
Learning outcome
Describe core Agile principles and explain Scrum roles and events.
Why it matters
Enables faster delivery, better quality, and closer alignment with customers.
Explore Key Topics
Agile Manifesto
Four values and twelve principles that guide iterative development and collaboration.
Scrum
A framework with Sprints, roles, and events to deliver increments regularly.
Kanban
A flow-based method using visual boards and WIP limits to reduce bottlenecks.
Concept map
Values → Frameworks → Practices → DevOps
This visual shows how values in the Agile Manifesto are expressed as frameworks like Scrum and Kanban, and how they enable DevOps practices such as CI/CD and close collaboration between teams.
Quick example
A streaming service team splits a watchlist feature into three small increments, releases them across two sprints, and uses customer feedback to adjust priorities—reducing wasted effort and delivering value sooner.
Key Takeaway

Agile replaces rigid plans with continuous delivery and learning so teams can adapt to real user needs quickly.
