Connecting Agile with DevOps
Agile needs DevOps automation to make frequent delivery safe and repeatable.
Agile offers
Short cycles, feedback, and incremental delivery.
DevOps provides
Automation, CI/CD, and collaboration between dev and ops.
Concrete Example
Automated tests and pipelines verify each increment so Scrum teams can claim a potentially releasable product at Sprint end.
Misconception
Agile alone doesn't ensure frequent releases are safe; DevOps automation is needed to reduce release risk.
Key Takeaway

Combine Agile's short cycles with DevOps automation to speed delivery while preserving quality and reliability.
