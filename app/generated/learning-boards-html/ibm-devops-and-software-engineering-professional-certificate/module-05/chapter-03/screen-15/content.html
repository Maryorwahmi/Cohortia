Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › What to Remember
Check state
docker ps and docker ps -a to see running and exited containers.
Run intentionally
Use -d for background services and -p to expose ports you need.
Debug & clean
Use logs, exec, inspect, then stop and remove or prune when done.
Final takeaway

Small, consistent habits — check, run, debug, and clean — keep your containers reliable and your workspace tidy.
