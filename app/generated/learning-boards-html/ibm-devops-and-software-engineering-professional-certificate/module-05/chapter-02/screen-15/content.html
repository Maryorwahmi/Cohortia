Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Quick Reference
Pick a slim base
Specific tags like python:3.9-slim-buster yield smaller, predictable images than generic tags.
Order to cache
COPY dependencies and RUN installs before copying source to keep caches effective.
Use .dockerignore & multi-stage
Exclude unnecessary files from the context and separate build/runtime to shrink final images.
Quick Takeaway

Small, well-ordered, and minimal images are faster to build, easier to test, and safer to deploy. Practice the pattern and watch cached layers in action.
