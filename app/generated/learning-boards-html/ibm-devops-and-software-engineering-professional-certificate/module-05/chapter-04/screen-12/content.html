Volume cleanup and bind mount safety
Prevent resource bloat and reduce risk by pruning unused volumes and limiting host mounts to safe directories.
Prune unused volumes
Inspect volumes, confirm they’re unused, then prune. Remember deletion is permanent.
Bind mount safety
Mount only project folders. Avoid mounting root or system directories into untrusted containers.
Warning

Removing a container does not delete the attached volume unless explicitly requested. Use pruning deliberately and with confirmation.
