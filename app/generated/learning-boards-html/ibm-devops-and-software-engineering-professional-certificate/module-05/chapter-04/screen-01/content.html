Docker Networking and Data Management
Understand how containers connect, discover each other, and keep their data safe across restarts.

Why this matters: Modern apps are made of multiple containers. Networking lets those services locate and call each other. Persistent storage ensures that databases and logs survive container replacement.

Outcome: Create custom networks for service name resolution and choose between volumes and bind mounts based on portability, performance, and security.

Automatic DNS by container name and better isolation.
Volume
Managed, portable persistence for databases and logs.
Bind Mount
Direct host folder mount; great for development, less portable.
