Definition depends_on and readiness
What depends_on does
It orders container startup so that dependency containers are started before dependents.
What it doesn't do
It does not wait for the internal application state or health. Containers may be up but not ready.
Practical strategies
  • Add health checks to services to detect readiness.
  • Use a simple wait-for-it script in the dependent service to poll the dependency until it responds.
  • Log and inspect startup sequences with docker compose logs to find timing-related failures.
Common misconception

Assuming depends_on guarantees service readiness can lead to intermittent connectivity errors at startup.
