Safety Notes

Common Mistakes and Mitigations

Recognize common pitfalls and their remedies to keep CDP safe.

Top mistakes
  • Insufficient testing — remedy: expand automated coverage and include performance/security checks.
  • Lack of monitoring — remedy: instrument metrics, logs, and traces and wire alerts into the pipeline.
  • No rollback plan — remedy: automate and rehearse rollback commands and traffic switches.
  • Ignoring alerts — remedy: tune alerts and define incident response ownership and runbooks.
  • Big-bang deployments — remedy: prefer small incremental changes and use canaries or feature flags.
  • Pipeline insecurity — remedy: manage secrets, scan artifacts, and secure the build environment.
Safety practices
Run rollback drills and post-mortems to learn from incidents.
Keep change sizes small so automated tests can isolate regressions.
Use feature toggles for risky features to decouple deployment from activation.
Key takeaway
Safety is a continuous program: prevent, detect, and rehearse recovery.
