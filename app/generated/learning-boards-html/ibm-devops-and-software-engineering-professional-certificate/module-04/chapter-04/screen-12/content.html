Cultural and Technical Prerequisites

Successful CDP rests on both people and systems being prepared.

Cultural
  • Shared ownership for quality
  • Practice rollback drills
  • Prefer small, incremental changes
Technical
  • Mature, fast CI/CD pipelines
  • Comprehensive automated testing
  • Automated rollback mechanisms
Support tools
  • Feature toggles for controlled releases
  • Observability stack tied into the pipeline
  • Tuned alerts and runbooks
