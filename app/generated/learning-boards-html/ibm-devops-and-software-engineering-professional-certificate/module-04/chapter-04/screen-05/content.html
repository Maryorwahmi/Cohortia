Continuous Delivery vs Continuous Deployment

Understand the operational and technical consequences of removing the manual gate.

Continuous Delivery
  • Automated build and tests produce deployable artifacts.
  • Human approval decides production promotion timing.
  • Good for organizations needing coordinated releases.
Example
Operations team schedules releases after manual validation and stakeholder sign-off.
Continuous Deployment
  • Every passing change is deployed automatically to production.
  • Requires strong trust in automated tests and monitoring.
  • Enables rapid feedback from real users.
Example
A bug fix merged to main flows to production as soon as tests and gates pass, with automatic observation after deploy.
