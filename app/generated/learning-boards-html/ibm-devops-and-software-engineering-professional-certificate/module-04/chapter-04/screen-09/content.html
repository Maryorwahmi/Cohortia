Definition

Monitoring and Observability

Collecting and acting on metrics, logs, and traces to validate production behavior after deploys.

What to collect
  • Metrics: error rates, latency, throughput, resource usage.
  • Logs: structured events and stack traces for incidents.
  • Traces: request paths to find bottlenecks across services.
Tool examples
Use metrics (Prometheus), dashboards (Grafana), logs (ELK), and APM for traces as part of a combined observability strategy.
Actionability
Convert signals into automated responses: health checks that fail should surface to the pipeline, and alerts should map to runbooks and rollback actions.
Misconception
Dashboards without automated alerts do not protect CDP; the pipeline needs programmatic checks to act immediately.
