Canary Deployments

Real-user validation with controlled traffic percentages.

Steps
  1. Deploy new version as canary serving small traffic share.
  2. Monitor performance, errors, and resource impact closely.
  3. If stable, incrementally increase traffic percentage.
  4. Complete rollout once confidence is achieved.
Example
Route 5% of users to the new feature to collect metrics and customer feedback before full rollout.
Common pitfall
Ramping too quickly or lacking observability turns a canary into a mini outage. Always pair canaries with feature flags and tuned alerts.
Key takeaway
Canaries let you test in production safely if you increment traffic and observe carefully.
