Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Manual Approval Gates
Core Concept

Manual approval gates

Add a controlled human checkpoint after staging validation to allow stakeholders to confirm release readiness and timing.
Who approves?
Product managers, QA leads, or release managers with context on business impact.
What to check
UAT sign-off, performance baselines, security scan results, and monitoring readiness.
Auditability
Record who approved and why; use notifications to keep stakeholders informed.
Key Takeaway

Use manual gates to add business and quality judgement to automated pipelines, while keeping rollback and override paths defined for emergencies.
