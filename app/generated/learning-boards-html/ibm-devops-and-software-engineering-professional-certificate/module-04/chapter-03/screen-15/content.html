Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › What to Remember
Immutable artifact
Deploy the exact CI-built artifact to maintain consistency.
Secure config
Inject environment variables and use secret managers for credentials.
Reproducible infra
Provision staging with IaC and validate after creation.
When you design a CD pipeline, use these three checks as a checklist: artifact identity, secure configuration, and reproducible infrastructure with validation and rollback plans.
