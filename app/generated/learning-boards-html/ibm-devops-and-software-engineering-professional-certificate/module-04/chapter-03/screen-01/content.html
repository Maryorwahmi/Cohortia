Why this chapter matters

Staging is the final automated stop before production. Using the exact artifact and scripted infrastructure reduces surprises, improves repeatability, and keeps sensitive data out of code. Manual approval gates let teams balance automation with business oversight.

Example
A Docker image tagged in CI is pulled by the CD pipeline and deployed unchanged to staging for UAT.
Common risk
Hardcoded configs or missing rollback plans can turn staging validation into production outages.
