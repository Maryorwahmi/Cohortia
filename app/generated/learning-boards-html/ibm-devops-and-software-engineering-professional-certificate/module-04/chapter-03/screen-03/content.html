Verification checklist

Concrete checks to confirm your staging pipeline is reliable.

Artifact identity
Confirm the CD pipeline pulls the exact tag or digest produced by CI and does not rebuild artifacts.
IaC reproducibility
Destroy and recreate staging from code to validate templates and drift detection.
Secrets and config
Ensure secrets are injected at deployment and not baked into images; test rotated secrets.
Approval gates
Test that the manual approval pauses the pipeline and triggers notifications and a safe rollback path.
