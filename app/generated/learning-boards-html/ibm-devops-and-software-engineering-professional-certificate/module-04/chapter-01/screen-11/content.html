Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Monitoring & Rollback

Observability signals

  • Errors and exceptions
  • Latency and throughput
  • Key business metrics (purchases, signups)
Rollback patterns
  • Redeploy previous artifact from registry
  • Feature toggles to disable new behavior
  • Progressive traffic shift or canary rollback
Concrete example
Canary deployment
Release to a small percentage of users. Monitor errors and business metrics; rollback slowly if problems appear to limit impact.
Key Takeaway

Observability plus artifact-based rollbacks let you automate deployments while minimizing user impact when failures occur.
