Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Delivery vs Deployment
Continuous Delivery

Ready for production with a manual gate

CD automates packaging and staging but keeps human approval before production. This balances speed with business control.

When to choose CD
  • When releases require legal approval or a business window.
  • When your tests are strong but you want a last human check.
Continuous Deployment

Fully automated production deployment

CDP deploys every passing change automatically. It requires rigorous tests, monitoring, and rollback strategies to be safe.

When to choose CDP
  • When you can trust automated tests and observability to catch regressions quickly.
  • When delivering small changes rapidly is core to your product strategy.
Concrete contrast

Example: a bug fix under CD waits for release manager approval; under CDP it reaches users automatically after passing tests. The choice depends on risk tolerance and testing maturity.
