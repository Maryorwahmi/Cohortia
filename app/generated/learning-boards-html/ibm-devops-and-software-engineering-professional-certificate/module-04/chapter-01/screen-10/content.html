Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Anti-patterns
Broken Pipeline
Frequent failures that block progress. Fix by identifying flaky tests, stabilizing environments, and restoring green builds.
Over-automation
Automating poor processes accelerates failure. Automate incrementally and ensure each stage is stable before adding more.
Security Gaps
Missing secret management or vulnerability scanning lets risks spread. Integrate security checks into the pipeline early.
Concrete fix strategy
  1. Measure pipeline health and identify top failure sources.
  2. Quarantine flaky tests and restore determinism.
  3. Automate only after stages are stable; integrate security and monitoring.
