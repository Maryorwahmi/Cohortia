Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › StatefulSets
Key guarantees
  • Stable, unique network identifiers for each Pod (ordered names).
  • Stable, persistent storage via per-Pod PVCs.
  • Ordered, graceful deployment, scaling, and deletion.
When to choose

Choose StatefulSet for databases and other services that require identity and stable storage per instance. For stateless services, keep using Deployments.

Example
A MySQL cluster with db-0, db-1 each gets its own PVC so data remains tied to that Pod identity across restarts.
Misconception
StatefulSets are not required just because an app writes files; they are for when unique identity and per-replica storage are necessary.
