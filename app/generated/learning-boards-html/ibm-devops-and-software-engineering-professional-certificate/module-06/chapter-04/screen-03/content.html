Skills & Verification
Inspect PV/PVC binding
Use kubectl get pvc and kubectl get pv to confirm Bound state. This ensures your claim maps to persistent storage.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Verify persistence
Deploy a logger writing to /var/log, delete its Pod, and confirm logs remain in the new Pod instance.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Read reclaim policy
Check PV.reclaimPolicy (Retain, Delete) to know whether storage is cleaned up after PVC deletion.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Identify StatefulSet needs
Confirm the app requires stable hostnames and dedicated volumes per Pod before choosing StatefulSet.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Terminal demo checklist
  1. kubectl apply my-app-pvc.yaml
  2. kubectl apply my-app-deployment.yaml
  3. kubectl exec into Pod and cat log
  4. kubectl delete pod <name> and re-check log
