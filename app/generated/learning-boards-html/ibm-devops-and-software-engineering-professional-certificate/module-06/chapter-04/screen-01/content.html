Managing State in Kubernetes

Understand how to persist application data reliably across Pod restarts and rescheduling using Volumes, Persistent Volumes, Persistent Volume Claims, StorageClasses, and StatefulSets.

Storage at a glance
Volume types, PV/PVC binding, StorageClasses
Transient
emptyDir: Pod-lifetime scratch space for caches and inter-container sharing.
Node-bound
hostPath: mounts host filesystem; ties Pods to nodes and risks node failure.
Durable
PVs + PVCs: cluster-level storage decoupled from Pod lifecycle; ideal for databases and long-lived logs.
Quick note
Think first: does your app need temporary scratch space, node-local access, or durable cluster storage? Your choice affects scheduling, availability, and backup strategy.
