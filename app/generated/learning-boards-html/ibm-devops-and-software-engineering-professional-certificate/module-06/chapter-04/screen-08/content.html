Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Persistent Volume (PV)
PV example

A PV is defined by capacity, access modes, and reclaim policy. It is a cluster object that can be bound to PVCs for durable storage.

apiVersion: v1
kind: PersistentVolume
metadata:
  name: my-pv
spec:
  capacity:
    storage: 1Gi
  accessModes:
    - ReadWriteOnce
  persistentVolumeReclaimPolicy: Retain
  storageClassName: standard
  hostPath:
    path: "/mnt/data"
Reclaim policy note
Retain keeps data after PVC deletion, Delete removes it, and Recycle is deprecated. Choose based on backup and lifecycle needs.
Access modes
Examples: ReadWriteOnce (single node RW), ReadOnlyMany, ReadWriteMany — ensure your PV supports your application's needs.
Key Takeaway
PVs represent durable cluster storage. Match capacity, access modes, and reclaim policy to your use case.
