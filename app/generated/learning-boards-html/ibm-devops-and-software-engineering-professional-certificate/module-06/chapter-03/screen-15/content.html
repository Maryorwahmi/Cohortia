Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Quick Reference
Selector
Ensure selector labels match Pod labels exactly so endpoints are populated.
port vs targetPort
port is the Service port; targetPort must match the container's listening port.
Ingress
Use Ingress with a controller for host/path routing and centralized TLS; the resource alone is inactive.
Final tip

When in doubt, test inside the cluster first, confirm selectors and ports, then expose externally with caution.
