Inspect and debug Services
A short checklist to find common networking problems.
Quick checklist
  1. kubectl get services — check ClusterIP/EXTERNAL-IP.
  2. kubectl describe service <name> — inspect endpoints and events.
  3. kubectl get pods --show-labels — ensure labels match the Service selector.
  4. Confirm targetPort equals the container port the app listens on.
  5. For NodePort, ensure port is within 30000-32767 and test with node IP.
Common pitfall
A selector mismatch results in zero endpoints. This is the most frequent cause of non-routing Services.
Example commands
kubectl get services
kubectl describe service my-web-app-clusterip-service
kubectl get pods --show-labels
kubectl get nodes -o wide
Testing inside cluster
Create a temporary busybox Pod and use wget or curl to reach the Service DNS name, confirming internal connectivity before exposing externally.
