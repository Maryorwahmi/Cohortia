Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Skills You'll Practice
Hands-on tasks
A sequence of tasks to practice creating Services, testing connectivity, and deploying Ingress rules.
Write a Service manifest
Add selector, port, and targetPort that match your Deployment's labels and container port.
Verify with kubectl
Use kubectl get services and kubectl describe service to confirm ClusterIP and endpoints.
Test internally and externally
Exec into a busybox Pod to curl a ClusterIP, or use NodePort and the node IP to test externally.
Checkpoints
  • Selector labels match Pod labels exactly.
  • targetPort equals the container port your app listens on.
  • Ingress requires a running controller to take effect.
