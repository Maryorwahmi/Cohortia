Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Rollbacks & History
Rollout commands
kubectl rollout history deployment/nginx-deployment
kubectl rollout undo deployment/nginx-deployment
kubectl rollout status deployment/nginx-deployment
After undo, validate that Pods become ready and perform logs/events checks. Remember rollback affects Pod templates and images, not external data or in-memory state.
Pro Tip

Use staged rollouts in staging first and keep rollout history shallow to simplify rollbacks when necessary.
