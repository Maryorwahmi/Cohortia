Deploying Apps with Pods & Deployments

Understand Pods as the smallest deployable unit, and use Deployments to declare and manage replica sets of Pods. Learn how to author YAML, apply resources, scale, update, and roll back safely.

Hands-on terminal demo style
Pod

Smallest deployable unit, holds one or more tightly coupled containers.

Deployment

Declarative controller that manages ReplicaSets and Pod replicas for availability and updates.

Rolling Update

Gradual replacement of Pods to update an app without downtime.

In this lesson
  • Write and apply a Deployment YAML for Nginx
  • Inspect ReplicaSets and Pods with kubectl
  • Scale, update, and roll back safely
