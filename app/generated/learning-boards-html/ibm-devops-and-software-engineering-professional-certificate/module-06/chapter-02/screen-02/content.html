Learning Objectives

Learn, Understand, Apply, Master — practical milestones for deployments.

Bloom
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define a Pod

Explain what a Pod represents, why containers may share a Pod, and why Pods are ephemeral. Understanding this avoids misusing local storage for persistence.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand Deployments

Describe how Deployments manage ReplicaSets and ensure the desired number of Pods. Recognize why Declarative control matters for production reliability.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Create YAML Manifests

Write and validate Deployment YAML, including metadata, selectors, template, and container specs. This skill ensures predictable resource creation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Operate Deployments

Scale, update, inspect rollout status, and roll back Deployments. These operations keep an application responsive to traffic and changes.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
