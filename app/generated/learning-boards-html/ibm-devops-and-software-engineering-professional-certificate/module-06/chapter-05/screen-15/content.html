Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Remember
Container Registry
A central repository for images. Control access, scan images, and clean up old tags regularly.
Principle of Least Privilege
Grant only necessary permissions to users and service accounts. Review and tighten them over time.
Vendor Lock-in
Relying heavily on provider-specific services increases migration effort; isolate and document such dependencies.
Bottom Line

Secure registries, enforce least privilege, and keep provider-specific integrations minimal to maintain optionality and safety.
