Process Flow

How to evaluate providers

  1. Inventory existing cloud accounts, IAM, and integrations you depend on.
  2. List the features you need: autoscaling, managed control plane, OpenShift, etc.
  3. Estimate costs: control-plane fees plus worker and storage costs.
  4. Verify compliance posture and support SLAs.
  5. Prototype a minimal deployment and test integration points.
Cost & Vendor Lock-in

Managed services cost money for the control plane but can reduce labor cost. Consider resource quotas, autoscaling policies, and cleanup processes to avoid cost overruns. To reduce lock-in risk, design manifests and images with open standards and keep provider-specific features isolated behind clear interfaces.

Compliance & Support

Confirm the provider meets your regulatory needs and offers support levels appropriate for production. Enterprise platforms like OpenShift may simplify compliance through built-in policies.

Prototype step

Deploy a minimal app, integrate logging and IAM, and run a short load test. Use this prototype to validate assumptions before migrating full workloads.
