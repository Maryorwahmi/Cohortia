Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Controlling Cloud Costs
Strategy
Create budgets, tag resources, and define ResourceQuotas per namespace to cap consumption. Combine quotas with limit ranges to enforce per-pod requests and limits, preventing noisy neighbors from inflating bills.
Practical Example
Development namespace: quota 4 CPU, 16 Gi memory. Production: autoscaler min 3, max 15 replicas and PV reclaim policies for unused disks. Schedule non-critical workloads to scale down at night.
Common Mistake
Leaving large node pools running for low-traffic apps. Or attaching large persistent volumes and forgetting to delete them.
Tip: audit storage and images monthly.
Cost Control Process
A repeatable checklist to reduce surprises
01 • Set budgets
Create alerts for spend thresholds tied to teams and namespaces.
02 • Enforce quotas
Use ResourceQuota and LimitRange to bound resource usage per namespace.
03 • Automate cleanup
Scheduled jobs to remove old images, unused PVCs, and test clusters.
04 • Smart autoscaling
Define safe min/max and use horizontal autoscaling for stateless workloads.
05 • Monitor & alert
Trigger alerts when spend trends exceed forecasted budgets.
06 • Tagging & accountability
Tag cluster resources to track cost per team or project.
Key Takeaway

Guardrails like quotas and automated cleanup reduce the risk of unexpected bills while preserving developer agility.
