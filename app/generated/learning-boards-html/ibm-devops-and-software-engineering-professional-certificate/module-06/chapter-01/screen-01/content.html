Introduction to Kubernetes

Understand the why, meet the main components, and prepare to explore a real cluster with kubectl.

Container orchestration automates deployment, scaling, networking, and health management for containers across many machines.

Kubernetes groups containers into logical units, continuously works to maintain desired state, and recovers from failures with minimal human intervention.

In this lesson
Build a mental model of Kubernetes, learn control plane vs worker responsibilities, and get ready to run kubectl to inspect a cluster.
