Shard sizing
Balance shard counts to expected index size and concurrency; avoid many tiny shards or few oversized ones.
Replica strategy
Replicas provide availability and read scaling; place them across failure domains for resilience.
Analyzer & mapping
Select analyzers and mapping types to match search and aggregation needs; reindex when mappings change.
Final takeaway

Use shards, replicas, and mappings as your primary levers to balance performance, availability, and correctness for search workloads.
