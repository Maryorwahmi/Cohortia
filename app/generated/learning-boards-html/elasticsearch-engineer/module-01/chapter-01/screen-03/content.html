Quick tips
  • Keep the sketch simple: show only nodes and shard labels to reason about failure modes.
  • When comparing databases, focus on guarantees you need: write durability, consistency, and search latency.
  • For inverted index inspection, treat punctuation and tokenization as real concerns for search results.
