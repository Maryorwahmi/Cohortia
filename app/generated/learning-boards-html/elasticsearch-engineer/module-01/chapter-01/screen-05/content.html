Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Database Comparison
Relational
Transactions & structured schema

Use when ACID guarantees and complex relationships are primary concerns.

Example: order processing with strict inventory updates and rollback requirements.
NoSQL
High throughput & flexible models

Use when write scale and simple access patterns dominate, often with eventual consistency.

Example: telemetry ingestion where writes are frequent and single-key reads are common.
Elasticsearch
Optimized for search, full-text, and aggregations across diverse fields.
Note
Document updates are handled by delete-and-reindex behavior; plan for that in heavy update flows.
Concrete example
A slow relational text scan for fuzzy product names becomes fast when products are tokenized and indexed into Elasticsearch, returning matches and facet counts in milliseconds.
