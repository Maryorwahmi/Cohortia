Key Mechanism

Influencing Search Order with Relevance Boosting

Giving More Weight to Important Query Parts

What is `boost`?

A floating-point number (default 1.0) applied to a query clause to increase or decrease its contribution to the overall `_score`.

Impact on `_score`

`boost > 1.0` increases importance, `0 < boost < 1.0` decreases importance, `boost = 1.0` is neutral.

Key Takeaway

Boosting allows explicit control over relevance, making specific query terms or fields more impactful on search results.

Default Relevance

All clauses contribute equally (boost 1.0).

Score: Normal

Boosted Relevance

Specific clauses have increased influence (boost > 1.0).

Score: Higher
Analogy

Think of boosting like adjusting the volume on different instruments in an orchestra. You can make the violins louder to emphasize a melody, or quiet the percussion to highlight a softer passage. The overall sound is still there, but the emphasis shifts.
