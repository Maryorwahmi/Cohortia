Decoding `_explain`: Key Factors in Relevance

Term Frequency, Inverse Document Frequency, and Field Length Norms

Term Frequency (tf)

How often a term appears in a document's field. More occurrences = higher relevance.

Inverse Document Frequency (idf)

How rare a term is across all documents. Rarer terms = more significant.

Field Length Norms

Shorter fields are generally more relevant for a given term than longer ones.

Key Takeaway

`tf`, `idf`, and Field Length Norms are fundamental to Elasticsearch's default scoring, each contributing uniquely to a document's relevance.
