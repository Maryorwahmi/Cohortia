Expert Tips

Effective Boosting: Strategies and Common Mistakes

Avoiding Skewed Results and Maximizing Precision

Pro-Tip: Start Small

Begin with conservative `boost` values (e.g., 1.5 or 2.0) and gradually increase as needed, observing the impact.

Experiment & Iterate

Continuously test different boosting strategies with real data and refine based on observed search result quality.

Key Takeaway

Judicious boosting requires careful experimentation and validation to ensure it enhances, rather than degrades, search relevance.

Common Mistake: Over-boosting

Applying excessively high `boost` values can lead to less relevant documents with high boosts overshadowing truly relevant ones.

Common Mistake: Blind Boosting

Boosting without understanding your data or validating with `_explain` can lead to unpredictable and suboptimal results.
