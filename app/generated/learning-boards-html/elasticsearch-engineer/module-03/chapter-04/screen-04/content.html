The Foundation of Search: Relevance and `_score`

How Elasticsearch Ranks Your Results by Default

Definition: `_score`

The numerical value Elasticsearch assigns to each document, representing its relevance to a given query. Higher scores mean more relevant documents.

Why it Matters

A well-tuned `_score` ensures users find the most pertinent information quickly, directly impacting user satisfaction and business goals.

Key Takeaway

`_score` is the core metric for relevance, calculated by Elasticsearch to rank search results from most to least relevant.
