Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter.

`bool` Query

A compound query that combines multiple query clauses using Boolean logic (AND, OR, NOT) for precise control.

`must` Clause

Queries that *must* match; all sub-queries must be true, and they contribute to the relevance score.

`filter` Clause

Queries that *must* match; do *not* contribute to relevance score and are often cached for performance.

Summary Takeaway

The `bool` query is the cornerstone of complex search, allowing precise control over document inclusion and relevance through its `must` and `filter` clauses, with `filter` offering significant performance benefits.
