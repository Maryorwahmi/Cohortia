Key Concepts

Reinforce your understanding of term-level queries and their applications.

Term-Level Queries

Queries that operate on exact terms in the inverted index without performing any text analysis on the query string.

term Query

Finds documents where a specific field contains an exact, unanalyzed term. Best used with `keyword`, `numeric`, `date`, or `boolean` fields.

terms Query

Finds documents where a specific field contains any of a list of exact, unanalyzed terms.

Chapter Summary

Term-level queries are essential for precise, exact-value filtering, especially on non-analyzed fields. Understanding their behavior versus full-text queries is a cornerstone of effective Elasticsearch engineering.
