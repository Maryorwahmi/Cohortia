Case Sensitivity and Stemming in match Queries

The analyzer is a silent partner in your search results.

Case Insensitivity

If your analyzer lowercases text, searching for "Running" will match "running". This is generally desired for user-friendly search.

Stemming Effects

If your analyzer stems words, searching for "ran" might match documents containing "running" because both stem to "run".

Key Takeaway

Always be aware of the analyzer configured for your fields, as it dictates how your query terms are processed and matched against indexed content.
