How Text Analysis Powers match Queries

The silent partner transforming text for effective search.

Normalization for Search

Text analysis processes text into a normalized form (tokens, lowercase, stems) for efficient and relevant matching, both at index time and query time.

Key Steps
  • Tokenization: Breaking text into individual words.
  • Lowercasing: Converting all characters to lowercase.
  • Stemming: Reducing words to their root form (e.g., 'running' to 'run').
Key Takeaway

Consistent text analysis ensures that your search queries effectively match variations of words stored in your documents.
