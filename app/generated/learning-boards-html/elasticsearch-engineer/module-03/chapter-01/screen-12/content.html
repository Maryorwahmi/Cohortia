Precision with operator: "AND"

Switching from OR to AND for precise results.

The `operator` Parameter

This parameter controls the boolean logic between the analyzed terms of your match query.

`AND` vs. `OR`
  • `OR` (Default): Any analyzed term can match.
  • `AND`: All analyzed terms must be present for a match.
Key Takeaway

Using operator: "AND" is crucial for narrowing down search results and achieving higher precision when all query terms are equally important.

`operator` Logic

Query: "apple orange"
`operator: "OR"`

Matches "apple" OR "orange"

  • "apple pie"
  • "orange juice"
  • "apple and orange"
`operator: "AND"`

Matches "apple" AND "orange"

  • "apple and orange"
  • "apple juice, orange slices" (No)
  • "apple pie" (No)
Choose `AND` for stricter, more focused results.
