Common Pitfall: Expecting Exact Phrases

Don't confuse match with exact phrase searching.

Misconception

Many beginners expect match to find documents containing an exact sequence of words, like a literal phrase.

Reality: Term-Based OR

By default, match breaks your query into individual terms (after analysis) and searches for documents containing any of those terms, not necessarily in order or as a phrase.

Key Takeaway

For exact phrase matching, you need to use the match_phrase query, which is distinct from the default behavior of the match query.

`match` vs. `match_phrase`

Query: "fast car"
match

Matches "fast" OR "car"

  • "A fast driver"
  • "My new car"
  • "Fast red car"
match_phrase

Matches "fast car" (exact)

  • "A fast car drove by"
  • "The car was fast" (No)
  • "Fast and furious car" (No)
Choose your query based on whether you need broad relevance or exact sequence.
