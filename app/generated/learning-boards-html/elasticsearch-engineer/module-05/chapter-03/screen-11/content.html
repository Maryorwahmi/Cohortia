Nesting Aggregations: Complex Queries

Combine multiple aggregations to perform multi-dimensional analysis on your data.

Bucket within Bucket: Group by category, then by date within each category.

Metric within Bucket: Calculate `sum` or `avg` for each group, as seen in previous examples.

Order Matters: Inner aggregations operate on the documents filtered by the outer aggregation's bucket.

Key Takeaway

Nesting aggregations allows you to build sophisticated queries, enabling multi-dimensional analysis and answering complex business questions by segmenting data at multiple levels.

Conceptual Example: Sales by Category & Month
Outer Agg: `terms` on `product_category.keyword`
Bucket: Electronics
Inner Agg: `date_histogram` on `order_date`
Bucket: Apparel
Inner Agg: `date_histogram` on `order_date`
Result: Monthly Sales for Each Category
