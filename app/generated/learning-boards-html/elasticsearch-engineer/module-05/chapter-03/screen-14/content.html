Advanced Aggregation Concepts

Reinforcing nesting, field types, and configuration for powerful analysis.

Nesting Aggregations

Placing one aggregation inside another to perform calculations on grouped data (e.g., metric within bucket).

`field.keyword` for `terms`

Essential for accurate grouping of string fields, treating the entire string as a single token.

`size` and `order` Parameters

Configure the number of top buckets returned and their sorting criteria for `terms` aggregation.

Chapter Summary

By combining nesting with careful field selection and configuration, you can unlock advanced analytical capabilities to derive precise and meaningful insights from your Elasticsearch data.
