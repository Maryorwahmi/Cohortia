Core Concept

What are Bucket Aggregations?

Bucket aggregations categorize documents into logical groups based on field values, ranges, or time intervals.

Categorization: Group documents by shared characteristics, like product categories or customer segments.

Nested Metrics: Apply metric aggregations (e.g., sum, avg) *within* each bucket to get per-group statistics.

Dashboard Foundation: Essential for creating dynamic charts and reports that visualize segmented data.

Key Takeaway

Bucket aggregations are the analytical backbone, allowing you to group documents and calculate statistics for each group, providing deep, segmented insights.
