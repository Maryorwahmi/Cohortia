Essential Bucket Aggregations

Recap of core concepts for grouping and analyzing data.

`terms` Aggregation

Groups documents by unique values of a field, typically a `keyword` field, for categorical analysis.

`range` Aggregation

Groups documents into custom, user-defined numeric or date ranges for segmented analysis.

`date_histogram` Aggregation

Groups documents into fixed time intervals based on a `date` field, crucial for trend analysis.

Chapter Summary

Bucket aggregations are the foundation for deep data analysis in Elasticsearch, allowing you to categorize, segment, and derive statistics from your documents based on various criteria.
