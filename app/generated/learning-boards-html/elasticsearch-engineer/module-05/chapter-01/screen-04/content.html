Search: Individual Documents

Focuses on retrieving documents that match specific criteria.

Returns raw document data.

Best for finding specific items.

Inefficient for large-scale summaries.

Aggregations: Summarized Insights

Processes data across many documents to derive statistics and groups.

Returns calculated summaries.

Ideal for trends, statistics, and grouping.

Highly efficient and scalable.

Key Takeaway

Aggregations are Elasticsearch's answer to complex analytical questions, providing efficient, server-side data summarization and insights.
