The keyword Field: Exact Matching

Precision for Identifiers, Tags, and Aggregations

Keyword Characteristics

Unanalyzed and Precise

  • No Analysis: Indexed as a single, raw token.
  • Exact Matching: Ideal for precise filters.
  • Sorting & Aggregations: Essential for grouping and ordering data.
  • Case-Sensitive: By default, matches must be exact, including case.
Concrete Example

If product_sku is a keyword, a search for "ABC-123" will only return documents with that exact SKU. It won't match "abc-123" or "ABC-12".

Key Takeaway

Use keyword fields when you need to filter, sort, or aggregate data based on its exact, unanalyzed value, ensuring precision in your operations.
