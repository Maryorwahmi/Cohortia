The date Field: Managing Timestamps

Flexible Formats and Efficient Range Queries

Date Handling

Formats & Internal Storage

  • Parses many ISO 8601 formats by default.
  • Supports custom date formats.
  • Internally stored as long (milliseconds since Unix epoch).
  • Enables efficient range queries and sorting.
Common Mistake

Inconsistent date formatting can lead to mapper_parsing_exception errors. Always define all expected formats.

Date Format Examples

"2023-01-15" (ISO 8601)

Standard date format

"2023/03/10" (Custom)

Requires explicit format definition

"1678704000000" (Epoch Millis)

Internal representation, also parsable

Key Takeaway

Always ensure consistent date formats or provide a comprehensive list of formats in your mapping to prevent parsing errors and enable powerful time-based queries.
