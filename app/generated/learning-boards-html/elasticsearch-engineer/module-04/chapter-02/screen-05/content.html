Text vs. Keyword: The Core Difference

Understanding Analyzed vs. Unanalyzed Data

Text Field

Designed for Full-Text Search

  • Undergoes analysis (tokenization, lowercasing, stemming).
  • Suitable for large blocks of content (e.g., article bodies, descriptions).
  • Matches variations of words (e.g., "running" matches "ran").
Example Use Case

Searching for keywords within a product description or a user comment.

Keyword Field

Designed for Exact-Value Matching

  • Indexed as a single, unanalyzed token.
  • Ideal for identifiers, tags, categories, email addresses.
  • Performs exact matches, sorting, and aggregations.
Example Use Case

Filtering by a specific product_id or aggregating data by category.

Key Takeaway

The choice between text and keyword fundamentally determines how your data is processed for search and whether you get fuzzy, relevance-based results or precise, exact matches.
