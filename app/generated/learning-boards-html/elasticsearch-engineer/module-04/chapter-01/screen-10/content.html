Dynamic Mapping Pitfalls

Pitfall 3: Limited Control Over Indexing Behavior

Customization Constraints with Dynamic Mapping

The Problem: Default Settings

Dynamic mapping applies default settings for inferred field types, which often lack the specific optimizations needed for complex search requirements.

  • Cannot specify custom analyzers for `text` fields.
  • No control over `doc_values` or `norms` settings.
  • Limits fine-tuning for search relevance and storage.

Consequences for Performance

Suboptimal Search Relevance

Without custom analyzers, search results might not be as accurate or relevant as desired for specific text fields.

Inefficient Storage & Queries

Default settings might consume more disk space or lead to slower queries than a carefully optimized explicit mapping.

Key Takeaway

For critical fields and production systems, explicit mapping is essential to gain the necessary control over indexing behavior and optimize performance.
