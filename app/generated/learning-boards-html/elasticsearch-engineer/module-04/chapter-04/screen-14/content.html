Avoiding Mapping Pitfalls and Cleaning Up

Understanding re-indexing requirements and maintaining a clean environment.

Common Mistake: Modifying Existing Mappings

You cannot change the type or core properties of an existing field. If a change is needed, you must re-index your data into a new index with the updated mapping. This is a costly operation, emphasizing the importance of careful initial mapping design.

Why Cleanup Matters

Deleting temporary indices and templates after a lab ensures your Elasticsearch cluster remains clean and avoids potential conflicts with future exercises or production data.

Key Takeaway

Always plan your mappings carefully to avoid re-indexing. Remember to clean up lab resources to maintain a tidy Elasticsearch environment.

Kibana Dev Tools: Clean up index
DELETE /user_logs-2023-10-27
Kibana Dev Tools: Clean up index template
DELETE /_index_template/user_logs_template
