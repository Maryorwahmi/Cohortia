Step 4 & 5: Verifying Mappings and Testing Queries

Confirming custom behaviors and understanding search limitations.

Verification Checklist

  • my_custom_analyzer on message field.
  • case_insensitive_keyword normalizer on user_id.
  • all_search_content field exists and contains copied data.
  • user_agent and ip_address have "index": false.
  • session_id is mapped as keyword (dynamic template).
Expected Query Outcomes

Searching all_search_content should succeed. Searching ip_address should fail. Searching user_id with 'userabc' should match 'UserABC'.

Key Takeaway

Thorough verification and testing are essential to ensure your custom mappings and templates are functioning correctly and delivering the desired search and storage behaviors.
