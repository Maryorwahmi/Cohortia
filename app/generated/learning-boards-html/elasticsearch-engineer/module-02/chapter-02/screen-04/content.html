Core Concept
What is Indexing in Elasticsearch?
The foundational step to making your data searchable.
Definition: Indexing

Indexing is the process of adding JSON documents to an Elasticsearch index. This makes the data stored, analyzed according to its mapping, and ultimately searchable.

Why it Matters
  • Transforms raw data into a queryable format.
  • Enables fast full-text search and complex aggregations.
  • Applies defined mappings for data type and analysis.
  • Essential for populating and maintaining your search cluster.
