Understanding _id and _source
The unique identifier and the original document content.
The _id Field

The _id is the unique identifier for a document within a specific index. It acts as a primary key, allowing direct access to a document for retrieval, updates, or deletions.

  • Can be explicit (user-defined) or automatic (Elasticsearch-generated).
  • Ensures uniqueness within an index.
  • Crucial for direct document referencing.
The _source Field

The _source field stores the complete original JSON document that was sent during indexing. It's the raw data returned upon retrieval.

  • Contains the entire document as indexed.
  • Used for reindexing and other internal operations.
  • Can be disabled to save space, but often not recommended.
Pro Tip

Always ensure your _id strategy aligns with your data's natural identifiers and your application's retrieval needs. The _source field is invaluable for debugging and data recovery.
