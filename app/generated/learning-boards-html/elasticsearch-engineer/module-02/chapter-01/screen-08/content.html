Text and Keyword: The Foundation of Search

Understanding the difference for full-text vs. exact matching.

Text Field Characteristics
  • Full-Text Search: Designed for human-readable text.
  • Analyzed: Content is tokenized, lowercased, stemmed.
  • Relevance-Based: Matches based on terms, not exact phrases.
Key Takeaway

Choose 'text' for searchable content and 'keyword' for exact values, sorting, and aggregations to ensure accurate search behavior.

Keyword Field Characteristics
  • Exact Matching: Content treated as a single, unanalyzed token.
  • Sorting & Aggregations: Ideal for filtering, grouping, and exact lookups.
  • Case-Sensitive (by default): Matches require exact value.
Common Mistake

Allowing dynamic mapping to infer a field like product_code (e.g., "P-1001") as text instead of keyword. This can lead to partial matches or no results for exact searches.

Example Usage

For a blog post title, use text. For a unique user ID or product SKU, use keyword. For a user's name, you might use both (multi-field).
