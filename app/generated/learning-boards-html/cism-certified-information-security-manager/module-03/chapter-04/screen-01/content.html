Key Information Security Policies
Policies translate risk decisions into consistent actions. This chapter focuses on the policies that form a practical policy portfolio, what each requires, and how to spot gaps that increase organizational exposure.
In this lesson
Identify critical policies, read policy content for enforcement value, and apply them to real scenarios such as healthcare data leaks.
