Applied Milestones
Checklist-style skills you can use immediately when reviewing policies.
Scope
Is the policy scoped to the right systems and people?
A narrow scope improves enforceability and avoids stifling business activities.
Ownership
Are roles assigned for review and enforcement?
Policies without owners rarely survive beyond their first draft.
Operational detail
Does the policy include procedures or references for provisioning and de-provisioning?
Operational statements are the bridge from policy to control.
Enforcement & review
Is enforcement defined and is there a review cadence?
Regular review keeps policies aligned with technology and threats.
Mnemonic
SCOPE: Scope, Ownership, Controls, Procedures, Enforcement
Quick Win
If a policy is missing scope or owner, assign a steward and schedule a 30-minute review.
