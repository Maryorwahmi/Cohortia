Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Enforcement & Review
Enforcement and review make policies effective
Ensure consequences, logging, and a review cadence are explicit and measurable.
Enforcement table (example)
Violation: storing Restricted data on personal device → Action: immediate data removal, incident report, retraining, potential disciplinary steps.
Review triggers
Annual review, after major incidents, or when regulatory requirements change.
Practical step
Add an enforcement matrix to every policy and record review dates and owners in the policy header.
Key Takeaway

Enforcement must be measurable and reviews must be scheduled; without both policies become obsolete or ignored.
