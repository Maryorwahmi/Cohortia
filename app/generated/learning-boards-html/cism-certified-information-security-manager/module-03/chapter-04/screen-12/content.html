Common mistakes in policy drafting
Learn to spot vagueness, missing owners, and absent enforcement that make policies ineffective.
Vagueness
Broad statements without examples or responsibilities are hard to enforce.
Over-prescriptive
Directives that block business operations become obsolete quickly.
Missing enforcement
Without defined consequences, policies are ignored.
No review cycle
Policies must be reviewed as technology and threats evolve.
Practical check
Can you name the policy owner and the last review date in less than a minute? If not, flag the policy for revision.
Key Takeaway

Policies must be actionable, owned, and regularly reviewed; otherwise they provide false comfort rather than protection.
