Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Policy Gap Analysis
Scenario: MediCare Solutions
Apply the checklist to identify missing or deficient policies in a healthcare provider after a patient-data leak.
Excerpt analysis
  • Excerpt A: Employee handbook forbids personal devices for patient data — suggests AUP/BYOD mention but may lack operational BYOD controls.
  • Excerpt B: Patching and new account approval exist, but no access review cadence — implies least privilege may be weak.
  • Excerpt C: No Data Classification Policy — critical gap given patient records involved.
  • Excerpt D: "Contact IT Helpdesk" is not a full incident response process; roles and escalation are missing.
Prioritized recommendations
1) Create Data Classification Policy to label patient records and set handling rules. 2) Draft an Incident Response Policy with roles and communication paths. 3) Add scheduled access reviews to the Access Control Policy.
Check your understanding
Which missing policy from Excerpt C most directly explains why patient data leaked? (Hint: classification determines handling and storage.)
Key Takeaway

Use prioritized, specific fixes: classification first, then incident response and access lifecycle controls to reduce exposure quickly.
