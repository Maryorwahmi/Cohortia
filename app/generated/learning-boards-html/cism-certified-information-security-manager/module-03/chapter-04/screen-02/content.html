Learning Objectives
Bloom progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify purpose and scope
Name core policies and explain what each is meant to control and why that matters to the organization.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Recognize critical content
List the essential elements a policy must include: purpose, scope, roles, statements, enforcement, and review cadence.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply policy principles
Use policy guidance to analyze simple incidents and suggest which policy governs a response or control change.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Relate policies holistically
Explain how classification, access control, and incident response interact to protect sensitive assets and maintain compliance.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
