Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Information Asset Management
Definition
Information Asset Management Policy
Ensures assets are identified, inventoried, classified, owned, and tracked through their lifecycle so protection can be applied where needed.
Asset register example
Columns: Asset name, owner, classification, storage location, last review.
Operational link
Asset records must feed access control and incident response so teams can quickly locate affected systems and owners.
Common mistake
Treating the register as a one-time inventory rather than an integrated, maintained source of truth.
Key Takeaway

Make asset ownership explicit and keep inventories current; these records enable targeted controls and faster incident response.
