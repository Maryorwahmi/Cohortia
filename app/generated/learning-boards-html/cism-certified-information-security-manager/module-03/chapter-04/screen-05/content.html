Core Concept
Access Control Policy
Governs who may access systems and data, under what conditions, and how access is managed through provisioning and de-provisioning.
Principles
Least privilege, separation of duties.
Lifecycle
Provisioning, review, de-provisioning, and authentication requirements.
Example
A clinician role is granted access to patient records. A separate billing role can adjust invoices. Approval is required for role changes, and multi-factor authentication is mandatory for remote access.
Common mistake
No scheduled access reviews. Permissions accumulate and expose sensitive functions.
Key Takeaway

Document role definitions, approval flows, and review cadence so access is auditable and limited to need.
