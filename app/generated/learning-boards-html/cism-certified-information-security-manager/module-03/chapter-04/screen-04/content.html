Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Acceptable Use Policy
Definition
Acceptable Use Policy
A set of rules that defines acceptable and unacceptable use of IT resources, sets privacy expectations, and explains consequences for violations.
Prohibited activities
Illegal downloads, harassment, unauthorized sharing.
Privacy expectations
Limited privacy on corporate devices; clearly state monitoring practices.
Concrete example
"Employees must not download copyrighted material to corporate systems. Personal devices may not store patient data."
Common mistake
Vague language like "use resources responsibly" — gives little guidance for enforcement.
Pro tip
Include examples of prohibited actions and reference related controls and training programs.
Key Takeaway

An AUP must be specific enough to be enforceable and linked to technical controls and awareness activities.
