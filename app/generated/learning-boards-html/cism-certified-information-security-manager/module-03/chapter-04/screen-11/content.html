Comparison
What every good policy contains
Compare clear, enforceable policy structure with vague or overprescriptive alternatives.
Purpose & Scope
Why the policy exists and what it covers; narrow scope aids enforceability.
Roles & Responsibilities
Owners, approvers, and operational roles must be explicit.
Policy statements
Specific, actionable requirements such as authentication, encryption, or retention rules.
Enforcement & Review
Consequences for violations and a scheduled review cadence to keep policies current.
Key Takeaway

Use a consistent structure so policies are readable, enforceable, and maintainable over time.
