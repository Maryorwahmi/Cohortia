Incident Response Policy
Defines roles, communications, decision points, and review steps used when security incidents occur.
Prepare
Detect
Contain
Eradicate
Review
Example
Ransomware detection triggers containment playbook: isolate affected networks, notify leadership, and begin forensic capture per defined roles.
Common mistake
No clear escalation thresholds or communication channels, leading to duplicated efforts and delayed containment.
Key Takeaway

A precise incident response policy shortens reaction time and reduces harm by making roles and steps explicit.
