Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › BYOD Policy
Core
Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
Covers acceptable personal device use, data segregation, remote wipe policies, and required device controls like encryption and authentication.
Example requirement
Corporate email must reside in a managed container; personal photos must not include patient data.
User controls
Define what device management is permitted and obtain consent for remote actions like wiping corporate containers.
Common mistake
Not specifying how corporate and personal data are separated, leading to privacy disputes and compliance risk.
Key Takeaway

Make BYOD rules clear about data separation, required protections, and user consent for device management actions.
