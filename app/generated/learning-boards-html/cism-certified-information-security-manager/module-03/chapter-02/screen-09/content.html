Process

Governance Components

Executive Sponsor / Board Owner
Provides strategic endorsement and secures funding commitments.
Steering Committee
Prioritizes roadmap items and resolves cross-functional trade-offs.
Program Manager & Owners
Tracks progress, assigns owners, and reports to governance bodies.
Cadences & Decisions
  • Quarterly steering committee for prioritization and funding decisions.
  • Monthly program updates for status and risk reporting.
  • Annual strategy refresh to adjust the target state and roadmap.
RACI Example

Each initiative should list: Responsible owner, Accountable sponsor, Consulted stakeholders, and Informed parties to avoid ambiguity.

Key Takeaway

Governance turns plans into funded action. Without definitions for approval and ownership, even the best roadmaps stall.
