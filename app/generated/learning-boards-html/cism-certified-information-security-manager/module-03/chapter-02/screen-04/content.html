Core Concept

Components of a Security Strategy

Each component connects to business outcomes and ensures security decisions are prioritized and measurable.

Vision & Alignment
Statement of where security needs to be and how it supports business objectives.
Current & Target State
Baseline from assessments and a target profile for the 3–5 year horizon.
Gap Analysis & Roadmap
Identify initiatives, prioritize by business impact, and estimate timelines and resources.
Key Takeaway

Each strategy component must be tied to a measurable business outcome to guide investment and governance.

Roles & Governance

Define executive approval, owners for initiatives, steering committees, and review cadences so the strategy remains active and funded.

Metric Examples
  • % critical assets with MFA
  • Mean time to detect high-severity incidents
  • % of roadmap milestones completed on time
Common Mistake

Confusing a list of technologies with strategy. Strategy explains why those technologies are selected and how they reduce business risk.

Concrete Example

An objective might be: implement multi-factor authentication for all critical systems within 12 months to reduce account compromise risk and support enterprise client requirements.

Check Your Understanding

Ask: which metric will show the initiative reduced business risk, and who must sign the budget to fund it?
