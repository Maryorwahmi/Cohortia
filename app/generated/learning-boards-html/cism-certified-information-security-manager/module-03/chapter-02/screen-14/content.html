Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
Strategy Components
Vision, assessments, target state, gap analysis, roadmap, governance, metrics.
Frameworks
Use NIST CSF for function-based objectives and ISO 27001 for an auditable ISMS governance model.
Plan Levels
Strategy directs tactics; tactics convert to operational tasks. Keep the hierarchy clear and traceable.
Summary Takeaway

Create a gap-driven, framework-informed roadmap with owners, metrics, and governance to ensure security investments reduce business risk and deliver measurable outcomes.
