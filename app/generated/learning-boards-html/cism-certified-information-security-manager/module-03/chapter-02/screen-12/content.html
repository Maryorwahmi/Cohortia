Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Metrics & Continuous Improvement
Process

Measure to Manage

Outcome Metrics
Examples: % critical assets with MFA, mean time to detect high-severity incidents.
Process Metrics
Examples: % roadmap milestones completed on time, number of tabletop exercises executed.
Reporting

Embed metrics in steering and board updates and use them to decide whether to accelerate, delay, or stop initiatives.

PDCA Cycle
  1. Plan: Define initiatives and success metrics.
  2. Do: Execute initiatives and collect data.
  3. Check: Review metrics and progress.
  4. Act: Adjust strategy and roadmap based on findings.
Key Takeaway

Metrics that reflect business risk enable governance to make informed decisions and continuously improve the strategy.
