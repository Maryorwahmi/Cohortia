Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › NIST CSF vs ISO/IEC 27001
NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
Flexible, function-based, risk-driven
  • Five core functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
  • Use to assess current vs target profiles and to generate prioritized initiatives.
  • Good for translating risks into concrete strategic objectives.
Example Strategic Objective

Develop a comprehensive inventory of critical information assets and assign owners to support risk-based prioritization.

ISO/IEC 27001
Management system standard for ISMS
  • Specifies requirements to establish, implement, maintain, and improve an ISMS.
  • Suited to organizations seeking a systematic, auditable approach and certifications.
  • Follows PDCA: Plan, Do, Check, Act for continual improvement.
Example Strategic Benefit

Use ISO 27001 as the governance anchor to demonstrate commitment and manage continuous improvement across initiatives.

Recommendation

Map ISO 27001 as the ISMS foundation and use NIST CSF to create measurable strategic objectives and maturity profiles. This dual approach ensures both governance and functional clarity.
