Why this matters

A clear strategy prevents fragmented investments, secures executive resources, and ensures security work directly reduces business risk. Use this chapter to prepare a concise, board-ready security strategy summary.

Strategic Blueprint
Vision → Assessment → Roadmap → Governance
Vision
Describe desired long-term security posture aligned to business goals.
Roadmap
Prioritized initiatives with timeline and metrics for success.
Governance
Defined roles, approvals, and executive oversight for resources.
Quick starter

Begin with a current-state assessment, map to a 3–5 year target, then build a gap-driven roadmap that cites risk and business impact.
