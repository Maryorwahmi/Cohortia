Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Creating a 3–5 Year Roadmap
Process

Roadmap: Prioritize and Plan

Quick Wins
Low-cost, high-impact items for year 1.
Medium Projects
18–36 month initiatives with measurable outcomes.
Transformational
Large, multi-year investments that change the security posture.
Measureable Example

Reduce mean time to detect high-severity incidents by 40% in 24 months; achieve 90% MFA on critical systems in 12 months.

Owner & Resources

Assign a senior owner, required FTE, and budget estimate for each initiative to enable approval discussions.

Common Mistake

Publishing a list without owners, timelines, or metrics; such items fail to attract resources and attention.

Roadmap Scoring
  1. Score business impact (high/med/low)
  2. Score risk reduction potential
  3. Estimate cost and feasibility
  4. Rank by combined score
Key Takeaway

A prioritized, measurable roadmap with owners and resources is the operational heart of a deliverable strategy.
