Hands-on Activity

CloudSecure Solutions: One Initiative per NIST Function

Identify

Initiative: Enterprise asset management program to inventory cloud resources and assign owners. Benefit: supports risk-based prioritization and compliance.

Protect

Initiative: Enforce multi-factor authentication for all critical systems with policy and monitoring. Benefit: reduces account compromise and aligns with enterprise client requirements.

Detect

Initiative: Centralized logging, detection engineering, and threat hunting program with detection SLAs. Benefit: faster identification of advanced threats and better incident context.

Respond

Initiative: Formal incident response playbooks, cross-team drills, and escalation paths tied to business objectives. Benefit: coordinated response and reduced business impact.

Recover

Initiative: Strengthen backup and restoration testing for critical services with defined recovery time objectives and regular exercises. Benefit: resilience and faster recovery after disruptions.

Owner
Assign accountable owners and program managers for each initiative.
Metrics
Define measurable outcomes, such as % MFA coverage or mean time to detect reductions.
Timeline
Set phased targets and deliverables for year 1, 2, and 3 to show staged progress.
