Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Gap Analysis & Prioritization
Definition

From Findings to Priorities

Use a scoring matrix to compare candidate initiatives. Common scoring axes include business impact, risk reduction, cost, and feasibility. Document assumptions for each score to make prioritization transparent.
Identify gaps
Map assessment findings to required changes.
Create initiatives
Design programs that close gaps with owners and outcomes.
Score & prioritize
Use consistent criteria to rank items for the roadmap.
Example

Finding: undocumented cloud assets. Initiatives: discovery program (high impact, low cost), asset ownership assignment (medium impact), automated prevention (high cost). Prioritize discovery and ownership first.

Scoring Template
  • Business impact: 1–5
  • Risk reduction: 1–5
  • Cost estimate: 1–5 (lower is better)
  • Feasibility: 1–5
Pro Tip

Make scoring visible to stakeholders so prioritization decisions are defensible to executives and auditors.
