Stakeholder Engagement
Engaging Key Stakeholders
Collaboration is crucial for effective policy development
Senior Management

Provides authority, resources, and strategic alignment. Ensures policies are taken seriously.

Legal Counsel

Ensures compliance with laws and regulations, and legal defensibility.

Human Resources (HR)

Integrates policies into HR processes, manages employee communication and disciplinary actions.

IT Operations & Technical Teams

Provide input on technical feasibility, implementation, and operational impact.

Business Unit Leaders

Offer insights into operational needs and ensure policies don't hinder productivity.

Why Collaboration Matters
Prevents policies from being unrealistic or overly restrictive.
Ensures legal and regulatory compliance.
Fosters buy-in and reduces resistance to policy changes.
