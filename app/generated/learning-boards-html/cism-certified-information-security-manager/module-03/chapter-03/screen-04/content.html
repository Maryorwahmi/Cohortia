Core Concept
The Policy Hierarchy
From broad intent to specific action
Policy

High-level, management-approved statement of intent and direction. Defines *what* must be achieved. Mandatory and broad.

Standard

Mandatory technical specifications or methods for implementing policies. Defines *how* to achieve objectives consistently. Less flexible.

Guideline

Recommended actions, best practices, and non-mandatory advice. Suggests *how* to achieve policy/standard with discretion.

Procedure

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for carrying out specific tasks or processes. Ensures consistency and repeatability.

Example Flow
Policy:
Protect sensitive data from unauthorized access.
Standard:
Use AES-256 full disk encryption on all laptops.
Guideline:
Consider passphrases of 16+ characters for encryption keys.
Procedure:
Steps to enable BitLocker on Windows 10.
