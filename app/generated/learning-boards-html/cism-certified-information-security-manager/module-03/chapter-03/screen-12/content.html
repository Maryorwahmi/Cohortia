Policy Review
Keeping Policies Current
The importance of regular review and revision
Alignment with Business

Ensure policies align with current business objectives, risk appetite, and organizational changes.

Effectiveness Assessment

Evaluate practical effectiveness, identify recurring violations, and gather feedback for improvements.

External Environment Changes

Adapt to new laws, regulations, evolving threats, and advancements in security technology.

Defined Review Cycle

Establish and adhere to a regular review schedule (e.g., annually or biennially) as specified in the policy.

Why Regular Updates Matter
Maintains relevance in a changing threat landscape.
Ensures continued compliance with laws and regulations.
Maintains operational efficiency and security effectiveness.
