Policy Lifecycle
The Policy Development Lifecycle
A continuous process for relevance and effectiveness
Initiation

Driven by business needs, regulations, or risk assessments. Identifies the need for a policy.

Development

Drafting the policy with clear, concise language, involving subject matter experts.

Approval

Formal sign-off by senior management, legal, HR, and business units to ensure alignment and feasibility.

Publication & Dissemination

Making policies accessible to all relevant personnel through appropriate channels.

Enforcement

Consistent application of policies and addressing violations appropriately.

Review & Revision

Regular updates to keep policies current with technology, threats, and organizational changes.
