Policy vs. Standard
Policy vs. Standard: Defining the Difference
Understanding the mandatory requirements at different levels
Policy

A high-level statement of intent and direction. Defines *what* must be achieved. Broad scope, mandatory.

Standard

Mandatory technical specifications or methods for implementation. Defines *how* to achieve objectives consistently. Specific scope, mandatory.

Example Scenario
Policy Statement:
"All sensitive customer data must be protected against unauthorized access and disclosure."
Supporting Standard:
"Sensitive customer data stored on servers must be encrypted using AES-256 encryption. Data transmitted over public networks must use TLS 1.2 or higher."
