Guideline vs. Procedure
Guideline vs. Procedure: Recommended vs. Required Steps
Navigating best practices and detailed instructions
Guideline

Recommended actions and best practices. Offers flexibility and discretion. Suggests *how* to achieve objectives.

Procedure

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for specific tasks. Ensures consistency and repeatability. Mandatory for the task.

Example Comparison
Guideline Example:
"When selecting a password, consider using a passphrase of at least 16 characters, including a mix of upper/lower case letters, numbers, and symbols for enhanced security."
Procedure Example:
"To enable full disk encryption on a Windows 10 laptop: 1. Open Control Panel. 2. Navigate to BitLocker Drive Encryption. 3. Select the drive to encrypt. 4. Click 'Turn on BitLocker' and follow the on-screen prompts..."
