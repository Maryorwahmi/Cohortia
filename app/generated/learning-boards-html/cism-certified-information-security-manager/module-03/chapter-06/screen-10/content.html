Module 3›Chapter 3.6›Procurement Due Diligence

Key Due Diligence Steps

Thoroughly evaluate potential vendors across several security dimensions to ensure their practices align with your organization's standards.

  • **Security Certifications:** Review ISO 27001, SOC 2 reports, etc.
  • **Incident Response Capabilities:** Assess their plan for handling breaches.
  • **Data Handling Practices:** Understand data residency, encryption, retention.
  • **Supply Chain Security:** Examine how they secure their own third-party vendors.
  • **Audit Rights:** Ensure your organization has the right to audit their security.

Mistake: Vague Contractual Language

Using vague or generic security clauses in contracts can leave your organization exposed. Specific, measurable, and enforceable terms are essential for clear accountability and legal recourse.

Contractual Security Safeguards

Explicitly include security clauses in all vendor contracts to define responsibilities, expectations, and legal protections.

  • **Data Protection Responsibilities:** Clearly define who is responsible for what data security aspects.
  • **Incident Notification:** Mandate timely and detailed notification of security incidents.
  • **Right to Audit:** Secure the right to conduct or request security audits.
  • **Service Level Agreements (SLAs):** Include security performance metrics and penalties.
  • **Liability & Indemnification:** Define liability in case of security breaches.
Key Takeaway

Robust due diligence and explicit contractual security clauses are indispensable for managing third-party risks and protecting your organization's assets during procurement.
