Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Reporting and Impact

Understanding how security performance is communicated and its broader organizational effects.

Security Reporting

The formal process of communicating security performance, risk posture, and strategic recommendations to stakeholders.

Risk Posture

An organization's overall state of security and its ability to manage and mitigate risks effectively.

Business Impact

The effect of a security event or control on the organization's business operations, revenue, or reputation.

Summary Takeaway

Effective security reporting, a clear understanding of risk posture, and the ability to articulate business impact are vital for strategic security management.
