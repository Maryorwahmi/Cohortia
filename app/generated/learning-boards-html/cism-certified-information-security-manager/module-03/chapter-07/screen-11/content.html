Reporting Cadence

Establishing Effective Reporting Strategies and Cadence

Defining the frequency and channels for communicating security performance to various stakeholders.

1

Operational Reports

Daily/Weekly: Granular metrics for technical teams (e.g., SIEM alerts, patch status).

2

Management Reports

Monthly: KPIs, risk posture, program effectiveness for senior management.

3

Board Reports

Quarterly: High-level risk, compliance, financial impact for board of directors.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined reporting cadence ensures that all stakeholders receive timely, relevant, and appropriately detailed security performance information.
