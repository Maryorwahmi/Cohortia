Security Metrics vs. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Understanding the distinct roles of raw data and strategic measurements.

Security Metrics

Quantitative measurements of security-related activities or states. Often raw and lack business context.

  • •Number of vulnerabilities found
  • •Average time to patch systems
  • •Number of phishing emails reported
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Metrics directly tied to strategic objectives, providing insight into critical security functions. SMART criteria apply.

  • •Reduce critical vulnerabilities by 20% in Q3
  • •Achieve 95% patch compliance for critical systems
  • •Mean time to recover from critical incident < 4 hours
Key Takeaway

Metrics provide data; KPIs provide strategic context and answer if security goals are being met, making them essential for executive communication.
