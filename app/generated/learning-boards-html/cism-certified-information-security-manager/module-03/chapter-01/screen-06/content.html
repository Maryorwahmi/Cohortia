Hierarchy template
Use this sequence to identify and prioritize:
Business Function
Order fulfillment — generates revenue and customer satisfaction.
Supporting Process
Inventory reconciliation and shipping orchestration.
Critical Asset
Customer database, inventory system, payment gateway.
Attach an owner and an impact note to each asset: financial loss, regulatory exposure, or reputational harm.
Example inventory entry
Asset: Customer database
Owner: VP Product
Business impact: High — loss of customer trust and legal exposure
Controls: Access logging, encryption, backup SLAs
Common mistake

Protecting low-impact assets with expensive controls while leaving high-impact assets under-protected.
