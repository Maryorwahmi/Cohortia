Practical deliverables
Deliver these artifacts to show alignment:
  • •Prioritized list of critical business functions and their supporting information assets.
  • •Risk assessment summaries tied to business impact and risk appetite.
  • •SMART security objectives with measurable metrics and timelines.
Verification tips
Show stakeholders how each security objective reduces measurable business risk. Use simple metrics and a short executive summary.
