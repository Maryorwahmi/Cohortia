Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Business-IT Alignment
Ensure IT and security initiatives support strategic objectives and deliver measurable value.
Critical Business Functions
The essential activities the organization must perform to achieve its mission and objectives.
Critical Information Assets
Data, systems, applications, and infrastructure whose compromise would significantly impact business goals.
Risk Appetite
The level and type of risk the organization is willing to accept when pursuing objectives.
Security as Enabler
When aligned, security facilitates innovation and protects the ability to meet strategic goals.
SMART Objectives
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound objectives that link controls to business impact.
Summary
Use these concepts together to prioritize resources and show leaders how security protects what matters.
