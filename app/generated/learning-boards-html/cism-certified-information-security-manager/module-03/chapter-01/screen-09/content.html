Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › SMART Objectives
SMART example
Objective: Reduce average time to detect a critical security incident by 20% within 12 months to minimize business disruption.
Specific: Time-to-detect metric defined
Measurable: 20% reduction target
Achievable: Resources allocated
Time-bound: 12 months
Implementation notes
Define data sources (SIEM alerts, mean time to detect calculations), owner, reporting frequency, and acceptance criteria tied to business impact.
Misconception: Avoid objectives that track effort rather than outcome. Measure the risk reduction, not the number of tasks completed.
