CEO Goals
  • 1. 1M active users in the first year
  • 2. 99.99% platform availability
  • 3. Industry-leading data privacy for enterprise clients
Critical assets identified from goals inform which controls to prioritize and how to measure success.
Example asset-objective pairs
Customer Documents → Objective: Achieve encryption in transit and at rest; zero unauthorized access incidents for 12 months.
Authentication System → Objective: Reduce account takeover incidents by 30% in 9 months via MFA adoption and monitoring.
Payment Platform → Objective: Maintain 99.99% availability with defined SLA monitoring and failover testing every quarter.
Misalignment example: Overly strict onboarding that blocks sign-ups undermines the growth goal. Balance security with user experience.
