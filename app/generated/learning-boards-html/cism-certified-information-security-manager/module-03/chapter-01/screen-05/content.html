Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Stakeholder Engagement
Four-step engagement process
  1. Prepare — craft short, business-focused questions tied to business goals and KPIs.
  2. Listen — learn mission-critical processes and tolerance for downtime or change.
  3. Map — connect answers to critical functions and supporting assets.
  4. Validate — confirm with KPIs, SLAs, or incident history and prioritize.
Pro tip: bring a one-page asset-impact template to each meeting so you can quickly record and rank what you learn.
Example conversation outcome
From a product VP: top priority is rapid feature delivery; acceptable downtime is low during business hours; customer trust is critical for enterprise sales.
Mapped CBF
Continuous delivery and release pipeline
Critical Asset
CI/CD pipeline credentials and artifact repository
Misconception check: These talks are not security checklists; they are evidence-gathering sessions for business priorities.
