Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Legal & Communication
Why involve legal early?
They identify mandatory constraints, data residency rules, and contractual obligations that shape acceptable controls and risk tolerances.
Tip: Request a short legal impact summary for any control that touches regulated data.
Communicating in business terms
Translate technical metrics into business impact: potential revenue loss, customer churn, or SLA penalties avoided by the recommended control.
Misconception: Compliance is not the whole picture. Use compliance as a minimum baseline and layer impact-based prioritization on top.
