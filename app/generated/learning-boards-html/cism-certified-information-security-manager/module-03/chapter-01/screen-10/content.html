Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Misalignment Mistakes
Over-engineering
Controls added without mapping to business impact. Results: user friction, cost overruns, and blurred priorities.
Example
Blocking internal collaboration tools aggressively, slowing innovation and harming user experience.
Under-engineering
Insufficient controls for high-impact assets due to lack of business context. Results: breaches with high cost.
Example
Leaving customer PII unencrypted, exposing the company to regulatory fines and reputation loss.
Check: Does every control have a documented business rationale and owner?
