Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Security as Enabler
What it means
Treat security as protecting business outcomes, not as a standalone technical function.

Definition: security decisions are evaluated by how they protect revenue, reputation, and continuity.

Example: Prioritize payment gateway integrity and availability for an online retailer to prevent revenue loss.

Misconception: More controls always help. Over-control can hinder growth and productivity.

Key Takeaway

Link every control to a specific business value it preserves; justify cost by measurable risk reduction.

Analogy
Think of security as a building foundation. It should support the structure you intend to build, not block the doors or add unnecessary weight to an unimportant wing.
Business Outcome
Customer trust and revenue
Security Action
Protect payment processing and customer data
