Simple risk statement format
Use this template: "If [threat] exploits [vulnerability], then [business impact]." Attach likelihood and estimated financial or operational impact.
Threat
Credential theft from developers' machines
Vulnerability
Unrotated secrets in CI/CD pipelines
Business impact
Potential large-scale data exfiltration and loss of enterprise contracts
Risk appetite guidance
Engage leadership to define acceptable thresholds: financial loss per year, acceptable downtime hours, or reputational exposure boundaries.
Misconception check: Risk appetite is not indifference to risk. It is a deliberate tolerance level that guides investment decisions.
