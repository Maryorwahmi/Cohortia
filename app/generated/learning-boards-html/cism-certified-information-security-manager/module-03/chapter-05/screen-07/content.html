Best Practices

Building Engaging Training Programs

From Checkbox to Behavioral Change

  • Real-world examples: Use current threats and incidents to make training relatable and impactful.

  • Interactive modules: Incorporate quizzes, scenarios, and gamification to boost engagement and retention.

  • Simulated phishing: Provide practical experience in identifying and reporting suspicious communications.

Key Takeaway

Engaging training moves beyond passive learning, using interactive elements and real-world simulations to foster active vigilance and secure behaviors.

Interactive Modules

Quizzes, drag-and-drop, decision-making scenarios.

Gamification

Leaderboards, badges, points for completion and performance.

Regular Refreshers

Short, frequent updates to combat 'security fatigue' and address new threats.

Avoid Over-Technicality

Keep language accessible for non-technical staff to ensure broad understanding.
