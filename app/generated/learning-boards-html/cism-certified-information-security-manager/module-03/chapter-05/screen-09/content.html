Administrative Controls

Monitoring Compliance: Administrative Controls

Human Oversight and Review for Policy Effectiveness

  • Independent verification: Audits and assessments provide objective checks on policy adherence.

  • Feedback for improvement: Findings highlight areas where policies or controls need adjustment.

  • Management accountability: Reviews ensure leadership is informed and takes responsibility for security posture.

Key Takeaway

Administrative controls provide crucial human oversight through audits, assessments, and management reviews, ensuring policies are effective and continuously improved.

Internal & External Audits

Verify policy adherence and identify control weaknesses.

Security Assessments

Vulnerability scans, penetration tests to evaluate posture.

Management Reviews

Leadership review of compliance reports and findings.
