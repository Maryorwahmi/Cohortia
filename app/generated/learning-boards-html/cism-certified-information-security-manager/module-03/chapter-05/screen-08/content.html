Technical Controls

Monitoring Compliance: Technical Controls

Leveraging Technology to Ensure Policy Adherence

  • Automated detection: Tools continuously scan for deviations from established security policies.

  • Real-time alerts: Provide immediate notification of potential policy violations or security incidents.

  • Data-driven insights: Collect metrics to assess policy effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

Key Takeaway

Technical controls automate the monitoring of policy compliance, providing real-time visibility and proactive protection against violations and threats.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Prevents sensitive data from unauthorized exfiltration.

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)

Collects and analyzes logs for security incidents and policy violations.

Network Access Control (NAC)

Enforces access policies for devices and users connecting to the network.
