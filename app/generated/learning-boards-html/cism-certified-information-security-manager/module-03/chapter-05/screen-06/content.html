Core Concept

Tailoring Security Awareness Training

Beyond Generic: Role-Based Education for Maximum Impact

  • Targeted content: Training should address specific risks and responsibilities relevant to each employee's role.

  • Behavioral change: Move beyond mere information delivery to actively shaping secure habits and decision-making.

  • Increased engagement: Relevant training is more engaging, leading to better retention and application of security principles.

Key Takeaway

Generic training is often ineffective; customize content to specific roles and responsibilities to maximize relevance, engagement, and behavioral impact.

Developers:

Secure coding practices, vulnerability identification.

Finance Personnel:

Phishing, social engineering, fraud detection.

Executives:

Targeted attacks, governance responsibilities.

All Employees:

Password hygiene, clean desk policy, incident reporting.
