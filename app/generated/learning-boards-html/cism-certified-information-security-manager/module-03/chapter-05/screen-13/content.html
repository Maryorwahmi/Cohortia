The Continuous Cycle of Policy Management

Adapting to Evolving Threats and Organizational Needs

  • Dynamic process: Policies are not static; they must evolve with the threat landscape and business changes.

  • Feedback loop: Monitoring and enforcement findings directly inform policy review and updates.

  • Resilience: Continuous adaptation ensures the security program remains robust and effective against new challenges.

Key Takeaway

Effective policy management is an ongoing, iterative cycle driven by feedback from monitoring and enforcement, ensuring policies remain relevant and protective.
