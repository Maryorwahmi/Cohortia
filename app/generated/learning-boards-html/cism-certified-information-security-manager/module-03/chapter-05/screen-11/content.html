Ensuring Fair and Consistent Enforcement

The Role of HR and Legal in Disciplinary Actions

  • HR & Legal involvement: Essential for ensuring compliance with labor laws and ethical standards.

  • Thorough documentation: Protects the organization legally and provides data for policy improvement.

  • Reinforce security culture: Consistent application builds trust and accountability, strengthening overall security.

Key Takeaway

Fair and consistent enforcement, supported by HR and Legal, is crucial for maintaining employee trust, legal compliance, and a strong security culture.

Human Resources (HR)

Ensures compliance with labor laws, employment contracts, and ethical guidelines.

Legal Counsel

Reviews procedures to ensure legal defensibility and mitigate organizational risk.

Documentation

Records violations and actions for legal protection and continuous improvement.
