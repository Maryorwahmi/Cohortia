Adapting to Challenges: Silos & Evolving Threats

Building an Agile and Collaborative Security Posture

Challenge: Organizational Silos

  • •Conflicting departmental priorities and lack of communication.
  • •Hinders cross-functional security collaboration.

Strategy: Foster Collaboration

  • •Establish cross-functional committees (e.g., Steering Committee).
  • •Promote a culture of shared security responsibility.

Challenge: Rapidly Evolving Threat Landscape

  • •Constant emergence of new vulnerabilities and attack techniques.
  • •Difficulty for security programs to keep pace.

Strategy: Agile & Adaptive Governance

  • •Regularly review and update policies and strategies.
  • •Ensure governance framework is flexible and responsive.
Key Takeaway

Fostering collaboration and maintaining an agile governance framework are essential for navigating organizational silos and the dynamic threat landscape.
