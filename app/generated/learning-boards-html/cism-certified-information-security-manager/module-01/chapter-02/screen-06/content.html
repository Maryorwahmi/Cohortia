CISO & Supporting Roles

Bridging Strategy and Operational Execution

CISO (Chief Information Security Officer)

  • •Primary liaison between technical teams and executive leadership.
  • •Translates security risks into business terms.
  • •Drives security strategy implementation.

Legal Counsel

  • •Ensures compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
  • •Advises on legal risks related to information security.

Human Resources

  • •Manages security awareness training programs.
  • •Enforces security policies related to personnel.

Business Unit Leaders

  • •Own the information assets and associated risks.
  • •Ensure security aligns with operational needs.
Key Takeaway

The CISO's role is crucial for translating technical risks into business impacts, supported by legal, HR, and business unit leaders for comprehensive governance.
