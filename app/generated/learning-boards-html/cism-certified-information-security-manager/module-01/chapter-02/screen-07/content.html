Strategic Alignment

Integrating Security with Enterprise Governance

Security as a Core Business Enabler

Integration Points
Strategic Planning
Risk Management
Compliance Programs
Enterprise Governance
Performance Measurement
Strategic Decision-Making
Why it Matters

Ensures security is considered in every major business decision, from new product development to vendor selection.

Benefits

Prevents security from being an afterthought, reduces friction, and ensures security investments support mission objectives.

Common Pitfall

Treating security as a purely technical function, isolated from business operations, often leads to resistance and underfunding.

Key Takeaway

Integrated security governance ensures security is a strategic business enabler, not just a technical cost center.
