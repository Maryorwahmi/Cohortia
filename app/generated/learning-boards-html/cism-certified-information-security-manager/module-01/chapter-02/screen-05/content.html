Key Stakeholders

The Board & Senior Management

Ultimate Oversight and Strategic Leadership

Board of Directors

Ultimate fiduciary responsibility and strategic oversight.

  • •Oversees strategic direction of information security.
  • •Ensures alignment with enterprise risk appetite.
  • •Delegates responsibility but retains accountability.

Senior Management

Defines, implements, and monitors security strategy.

  • •Includes CEO, CFO, CIO, CISO.
  • •Translates strategic vision into actionable plans.
  • •Ensures security supports business goals.
Key Takeaway

Strong leadership from the Board and Senior Management is essential for embedding security into the organizational culture and strategic decision-making.
