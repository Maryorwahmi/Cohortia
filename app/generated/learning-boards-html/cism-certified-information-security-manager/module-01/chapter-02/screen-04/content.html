Core Concept

Governance vs. Management

Setting Direction vs. Executing the Journey

Information Security Governance

  • •Defines what needs to be done and why.
  • •Sets strategic direction, objectives, and oversight.
  • •Responsibility of Board and Senior Management.
  • •Analogy: The steering wheel and navigation system.
Key Takeaway

Governance provides the strategic framework and mandate, while management executes the operational activities within that framework.

Information Security Management

  • •Focuses on how it gets done.
  • •Operational activities, controls, and processes.
  • •Executed by security teams and IT.
  • •Analogy: The engine and the driver.
Common Pitfall

Treating security as purely technical, isolated from business operations, leads to resistance and underfunding.
