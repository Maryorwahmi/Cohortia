Policy Catalog

Common enterprise policies and their purpose

Acceptable Use Policy

Defines permitted use of IT resources and prevents abuse.

Data Classification Policy

Categorizes information by sensitivity and prescribes handling controls.

Access Control Policy

Specifies how access is granted, reviewed, and revoked to enforce least privilege.

Incident Response Policy

Outlines detection, reporting, containment and recovery steps during incidents.

Remote Access Policy

Governs secure access methods for users connecting from outside the corporate network.

Vendor Security Policy

Sets security and contractual requirements for third-party service providers.

Key Takeaway

Each policy targets a specific risk; together they form a coherent control set linked by standards and procedures.

Practical mapping

Map each policy to standards and procedures so expectations for technology, configuration, and daily tasks are traceable and auditable.
