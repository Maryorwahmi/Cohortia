Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Policy Lifecycle: Review and Update
Timeline

Review cadence and triggers

Schedule annual reviews and add triggers for incidents, regulatory changes, or major architecture updates. Record the review date and maintain version history.

Review checklist
  • Is the purpose still relevant?
  • Does the scope still match systems and users?
  • Are owners still appropriate?
  • Do linked standards and procedures need updates?
Practical note

Document review outcomes, approvals, and action items. Without records, auditors cannot verify that management oversight occurred.

Key Takeaway

A formal, recorded review process keeps policies current and defensible during audits or incidents.
