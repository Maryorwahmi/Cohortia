Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Common Mistakes in Policy Development
Don'ts
  • Do not write policies that are overly technical or prescriptive.
  • Do not develop policies in isolation without stakeholders.
  • Do not omit executive sponsorship or a named owner.
  • Do not publish without communication and training.
  • Do not neglect enforcement and regular review.
Do's
  • Write in plain language: policy = what and why; procedure = how.
  • Engage legal, HR, and business units during drafting.
  • Secure visible executive approval and a named owner.
  • Plan communication, training, and acknowledgements.
  • Establish monitoring, audits, and scheduled reviews.
Key Takeaway

A short checklist of do's and don'ts prevents common failure modes and preserves policy value.
