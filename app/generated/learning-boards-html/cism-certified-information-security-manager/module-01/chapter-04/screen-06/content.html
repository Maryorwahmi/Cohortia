Core Elements

What makes a policy enforceable

Purpose
Explains why the policy exists and links to business risk.
Scope
Specifies who, what, and where the policy applies.
Owner
Identifies who maintains and enforces the policy.
Policy Statements
Clear, mandatory statements about required behavior.
Enforcement & Consequences
How compliance is monitored and what actions follow violations.
Review Date
A scheduled cadence or trigger for updates to remain current.
Example

A policy stating "All employees must complete annual security awareness training" includes owner, scope, enforcement, and a review date to be enforceable.

Key Takeaway

Explicit components and plain language make policies auditable and actionable.

Quick checklist
  • Does the policy state purpose and scope?
  • Is an owner named?
  • Are statements clear and mandatory where required?
  • Is enforcement and consequence described?
  • Is there a review date?
