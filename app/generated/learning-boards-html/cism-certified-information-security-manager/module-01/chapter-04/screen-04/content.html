Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Policy vs Standard vs Procedure vs Guideline
Definition

What each document means

  • Policy: High-level mandatory statement of intent and obligation.
  • Standard: Specific mandatory requirements to meet a policy.
  • Procedure: Step-by-step instructions to implement a standard.
  • Guideline: Recommended practices for better outcomes.
Common misconception

Treating policies as technical how-to documents makes them unreadable to managers and unmanageable in practice. Keep policy high level; move detail to standards and procedures.

Key Takeaway

Clear separation improves clarity, compliance, and maintainability.

Concrete example

Encryption requirement mapped across the hierarchy

Policy

"All sensitive customer data must be encrypted."

Standard

"AES-256 must be used for data at rest."

Procedure

"Steps to configure encryption on the database server and verification tests."

Guideline

"Tips for key management and rotation frequency when feasible."

Key Takeaway

Assign each document a single role: intent, requirement, execution, or advice.

Pro tip

Write policies in plain language for managers; include technical citations in linked standards.
