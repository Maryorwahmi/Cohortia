Applied Objectives

These objectives guide the hands-on drafting and evaluation exercises you will complete to demonstrate competency.

01
Develop critical policy examples

Draft concise high-level statements for AUP, Data Classification, Access Control, Incident Response, and Vendor Security.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Draft a Data Classification statement

Include purpose, at least three classification levels, responsibilities, and review period so it can be enforced and audited.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Evaluate enforceability

Check for owner, scope, enforcement, review date and clarity; these criteria determine whether a policy will be effective.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Spot and fix common mistakes

Identify poor sponsorship, complex language, isolated development, and weak enforcement, and suggest corrective steps.
