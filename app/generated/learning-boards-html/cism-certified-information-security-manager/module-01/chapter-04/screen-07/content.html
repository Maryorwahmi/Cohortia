Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Communication and Enforcement
Communication

Ensure awareness and comprehension

  • Publish policy in a central repository and include in onboarding.
  • Deliver role-based training and collect acknowledgements.
  • Use concise summaries for non-technical audiences and link to standards for technical teams.
Scenario

If remote workers don't receive the Remote Access Policy during onboarding, they may use insecure configurations, increasing exposure. Communication prevents this gap.

Enforcement

Monitor, audit, remediate

  • Monitor compliance with technical controls and access reviews.
  • Perform periodic audits and document findings.
  • Apply documented consequences and corrective actions for violations.
Common mistake

Publishing a policy without training or enforcement makes it ineffective.

Key Takeaway

Training, monitoring, and consistent remediation make policies real.
