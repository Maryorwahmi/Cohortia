Learning Objectives

These objectives prepare you to design, assess, and maintain information security policies that work in real organizations.

01
Explain the policy hierarchy

Describe how policies, standards, procedures, and guidelines relate and why each layer is needed for consistency.

Mnemonic: Pyramid — strategic top, operational base
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe essential policy components

Identify purpose, scope, owner, statements, enforcement, and review date so policies are actionable and enforceable.

Mnemonic: SPORE — Scope, Purpose, Owner, Rules, Evaluation
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Outline the policy lifecycle

Explain development, review and approval, communication, enforcement, and periodic update to keep policies current.

Mnemonic: D-ARC — Develop, Approve, Reinforce, Cycle
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify common mistakes

Recognize pitfalls like overly technical language, isolated development, and weak enforcement, and learn how to prevent them.

Mnemonic: BUNK — Beware Unclear, Noninclusive, Kitchen-sink rules
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
