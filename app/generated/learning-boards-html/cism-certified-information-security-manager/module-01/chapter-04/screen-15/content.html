Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › What to Remember
Separation of concerns
Policy = intent; Standard = required choices; Procedure = how; Guideline = advice.
Clear components
Purpose, Scope, Owner, Enforcement, Review date make policies enforceable and auditable.
Continuous management
Develop with stakeholders, approve, communicate, enforce, and review regularly.
Final takeaway

Practical policy programs connect strategy to operations through clear documents, traceability to controls, and a disciplined lifecycle.
