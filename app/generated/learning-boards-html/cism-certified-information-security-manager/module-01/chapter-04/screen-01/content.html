Information Security Policy Framework

Learn the hierarchy that moves board-level security intent into operational practice, the essential policy components, the continuous lifecycle, common pitfalls, and concrete enterprise policy examples.

In this lesson

Understand Policies, Standards, Procedures, and Guidelines. See how a data classification policy is structured. Learn lifecycle steps and common mistakes to avoid.

Why it matters

A clear policy framework ensures consistent decisions, enforceable controls, and traceable ownership — essential for risk management and regulatory compliance.

Explore Key Topics
Policy Hierarchy

Why policies set what and why, standards set required technology choices, procedures define steps.

Policy Lifecycle

A continuous cycle: develop, approve, communicate, enforce, review and update.

Critical Policies

Acceptable Use, Data Classification, Access Control, Incident Response and vendor policies.

Policy Pyramid

A visual metaphor: Policies form the trunk and standards, procedures, and guidelines branch outward to operationalize intent.

Policy — high level, mandatory
Standard — specific mandatory requirement
Procedure — step-by-step how-to
Guideline — recommended best practice
Getting started

As you proceed, compare a policy you know to this hierarchy and note where it needs clearer owner, scope, or review cadence.
