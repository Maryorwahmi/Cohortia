Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Assessment Example — Policy vs Standard
Question

Which statement is a policy and which is a standard?

Statement A

"All sensitive customer data must be encrypted." (Answer: Policy)

Statement B

"AES-256 must be used for all sensitive customer data at rest." (Answer: Standard)

Why it matters

Placing requirements in the correct layer prevents policy churn when technical choices change and clarifies who is responsible for updates — business leaders for policy and technical teams for standards.

Key Takeaway

Use the policy for intent and standards for firm technical requirements; this separation simplifies governance and change management.
