Policy Framework
Hierarchy: Policy → Standard → Procedure → Guideline.
Essential Components
Purpose, Scope, Owner, Statements, Enforcement, Review Date.
Lifecycle
Develop, Approve, Communicate, Enforce, Review.
Summary

A clear framework, granular traceability to standards and procedures, and a disciplined lifecycle make policies effective and auditable.
