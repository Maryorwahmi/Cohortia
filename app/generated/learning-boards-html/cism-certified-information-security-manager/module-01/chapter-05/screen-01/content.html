Legal, Regulatory, and Compliance Issues

Learn how major regulations shape controls, why compliance is a strategic risk function, and how ethics guides difficult trade-offs.

You will connect regulatory requirements to operational controls, practice a HIPAA gap analysis scenario, and explore the CISM responsibilities that keep programs aligned with law and values.

Keep an eye out for practical checks: which data is covered, who is accountable, what monitoring is required, and where legal counsel must be engaged.
