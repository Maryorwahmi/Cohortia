Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › CISM Responsibilities
Six responsibilities
  1. Identify applicable regulations.
  2. Conduct gap analyses.
  3. Develop and implement controls.
  4. Establish monitoring and reporting.
  5. Liaise with legal counsel and auditors.
  6. Foster a culture of compliance.
Example deliverable
A compliance dashboard that lists applicable regulations, current control status, gaps, owners, remediation deadlines, and evidence links for auditors to inspect.
Common misconception
Thinking that compliance is solely a legal issue. In reality, legal defines obligations, while the CISM operationalizes controls, monitoring, and evidence to meet those obligations.
Key takeaway

Combine legal interpretation with technical controls and a culture of stewardship to maintain continuous compliance.
