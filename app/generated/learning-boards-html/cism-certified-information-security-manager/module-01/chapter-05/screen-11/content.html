Company: MediData Solutions
Regulation: HIPAA
New System: Cloud-based EHR

HIPAA Requirement 1: Encryption and Decryption (ePHI at rest and in transit)
- Potential Compliance Gap: Default storage lacks encryption at rest or data transfers lack enforced TLS.
- High-Level Mitigation Strategy:
  1. Verify provider supports AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit.
  2. Configure client-side encryption where applicable.
  3. Include encryption requirements in the Business Associate Agreement.

HIPAA Requirement 2: Access Control
- Potential Compliance Gap: Broad administrative access and lack of RBAC or MFA.
- High-Level Mitigation Strategy:
  1. Implement RBAC and enforce MFA for all accounts.
  2. Regularly review permissions and remove unnecessary access.
  3. Document access approval and offboarding processes.

HIPAA Requirement 3: Audit Controls
- Potential Compliance Gap: Insufficient logging and no regular review process.
- High-Level Mitigation Strategy:
  1. Enable comprehensive audit logging for ePHI systems.
  2. Integrate logs into the SIEM for analysis and alerting.
  3. Define log retention and regular review procedures.
Example evidence
  • Encryption configuration screenshots and provider attestations.
  • RBAC policy documents and access review logs.
  • SIEM ingestion proof, alert records, and review meeting notes.
Common misconception
BAA alone is insufficient; you must verify technical implementation and collect evidence for audits.
Key takeaway

Document gaps, assign owners, and collect concrete evidence to demonstrate each mitigation is in place.
