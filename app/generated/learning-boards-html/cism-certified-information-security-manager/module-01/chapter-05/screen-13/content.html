Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Ethical Considerations
Core ethical duties
  • Act with integrity and prioritize the greater good over personal or short-term gain.
  • Be transparent about risks and avoid silent compromises to security.
  • Consult legal counsel when obligations are unclear or when disclosure may carry legal consequences.
Concrete example
If pressured to cut security testing for a launch, require a documented risk acceptance with senior executive sign-off and mitigation plan rather than silently proceeding.
Governance actions
Create a formal risk acceptance process, require legal review for privacy-impacting changes, and maintain a whistleblower or escalation path for ethical concerns.
Key takeaway

Ethics must be operationalized through policies, approvals, and culture — not left as informal expectations.
