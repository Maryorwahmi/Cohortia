Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Compliance Roadmap
Roadmap steps
  1. Discovery: data mapping and inventory.
  2. Gap analysis: score deficiencies by risk and impact.
  3. Remediation: implement prioritized controls and gather evidence.
  4. Monitoring: enable logs, dashboards, and reporting.
  5. Continuous assurance: periodic reassessments and auditor liaison.
Quick wins
Identify high-impact controls like encryption and MFA that produce immediate risk reduction and visible audit evidence.
Example milestone plan
Sprint 1: Data mapping (2 weeks). Sprint 2: Gap scoring workshop (1 week). Sprint 3: Remediation sprints for encryption/MFA (4–6 weeks). Sprint 4: SIEM integration and reporting (2–3 weeks). Ongoing: quarterly reassessment and auditor engagement.
Common misconception
Believing roadmap milestones replace evidence collection; instead, each milestone should produce documented artifacts for auditors.
