Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Consequences of Non-Compliance
Process: cascade of impacts
1
Financial penalties and remediation costs.
2
Legal action and required corrective orders.
3
Operational disruption, e.g., halted processing or revoked services.
4
Reputational harm and lost business.
How to communicate to leaders
  • Translate a regulation gap into direct costs and time-to-fix estimates.
  • Show scenarios for operational loss, e.g., payment processing downtime impact on revenue.
  • Present reputational metrics: customer churn, partner risk, and contract losses.
Concrete example
A payment processor finds PCI gaps. The acquirer imposes remediation and sets milestones. If milestones are missed, the processor may stop card acceptance, immediately affecting revenue. The organization must then budget for remediation, communicate with customers, and restore processing to avoid long-term losses.
Common misconception
Treating compliance as a one-time audit rather than an ongoing program with continuous monitoring and evidence collection.
Key takeaway

Quantify and scenario-plan impacts so leadership can fund remediation that prevents larger losses.
