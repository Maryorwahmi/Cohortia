Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › SOX & PCI DSS
SOX essentials
Focus on internal controls for financial reporting, change control, segregation of duties, and retention of audit evidence to support accuracy of reports.
PCI DSS essentials
Prescriptive controls for cardholder data, including network segmentation, encryption, access restrictions, and regular assessments.
Operational risk
Non-compliance can lead to loss of processing privileges from acquiring banks, immediately impacting revenue flow.
Practical checklist for auditors
  • Retention of logs and access reviews (SOX)
  • Network segmentation and encryption evidence (PCI)
  • Change control records and segregation of duties evidence
  • Third-party attestation and assessment reports
Key takeaway

Prepare concrete artifacts: logs, access lists, change tickets, segmentation diagrams, and vendor attestations to demonstrate compliance.
