Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Key Concepts
Regulation scope
Different laws protect different data and impose distinct obligations; identify scope before designing controls.
Frameworks & evidence
Use ISO, NIST, or COBIT to structure controls and create auditable evidence for multiple regulations.
Ethics & governance
Ethical decisions require governance, consultation, and transparency; technical fixes alone are not enough.
Takeaway
Regulatory compliance is a continuous, cross-functional program requiring identified scope, prioritized controls, documented evidence, monitoring, legal liaison, and an ethical culture.
