Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › HIPAA
Technical safeguards
  • Encryption for ePHI at rest and in transit
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Audit controls that record and examine activity
Common misconfiguration
Assuming default cloud storage settings provide HIPAA compliance. The CISM must verify encryption, MFA, RBAC, and logging.
Key takeaway

Verify controls and contractual responsibilities with vendors; technical safeguards and BAAs together form HIPAA compliance.

Concrete example: cloud EHR checklist
Company: MediData Solutions
Regulation: HIPAA
New System: Cloud-based EHR

HIPAA Requirement 1: Encryption (ePHI at rest and in transit)
- Potential gap: Provider defaults do not enable encryption or client-side encryption not configured.
- Mitigations: Verify AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit, and include encryption requirements in BAA.

HIPAA Requirement 2: Access Control
- Potential gap: Broad admin access or lack of RBAC and MFA.
- Mitigations: Enforce RBAC, MFA, and regular access reviews.

HIPAA Requirement 3: Audit Controls
- Potential gap: Insufficient logging or lack of regular review.
- Mitigations: Ensure comprehensive logs, integrate with SIEM, and schedule reviews.
