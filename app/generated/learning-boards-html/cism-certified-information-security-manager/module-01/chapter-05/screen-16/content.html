Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › What to Remember
Map scope
Identify applicable laws by data type, geography, and industry.
Use frameworks
ISO, NIST, or COBIT give structure and evidence for audits.
Lead ethically
Document trade-offs, involve counsel, and prioritize transparency and stewardship.
Final takeaway
A mature compliance program integrates legal requirements, technical controls, monitoring, evidence, and ethical governance to protect people and sustain operations.
