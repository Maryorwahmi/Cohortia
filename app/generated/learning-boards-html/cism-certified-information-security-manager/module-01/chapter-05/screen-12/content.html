Question 1
A company processing credit card transactions is not PCI DSS compliant. What is the most immediate and significant business consequence?
Correct answer
Inability to process credit card transactions, leading to direct loss of revenue. Remediation should prioritize restoring processing capability and preventing future downtime.
Question 2
Marketing proposes extensive personal data collection that raises GDPR concerns. What is the most appropriate CISM response?
Correct approach
Collaborate with marketing and legal to design a privacy-by-design solution that balances business objectives with regulatory obligations, while clearly articulating the risks of non-compliance.
Why this matters
Answers show how you translate regulatory issues into prioritized, risk-based actions that the business can accept and fund.
Key takeaway

Use collaboration and clear risk communication rather than dominance or avoidance when resolving ethical and compliance conflicts.
