Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Engaging delivery: methods and best practices
Methods
  • Interactive e-learning with short scenarios and quizzes.
  • Gamification: points, badges, and leaderboards.
  • Short storytelling videos with practical lessons.
  • Phishing simulations with immediate feedback.
  • Role-specific hands-on exercises for high-risk functions.
Best Practices
- Keep modules short and focused to avoid fatigue.
- Use scenarios tied to actual job tasks for relevance.
- Provide immediate, constructive feedback after simulations.
- Mix formats to reach diverse learners and reinforce messages.
Concrete Example

A two-minute animated vignette followed by a three-question scenario for targeted practice.
