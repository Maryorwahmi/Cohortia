Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Security champions and leadership buy-in
Champion Role
  • Local advocate who coaches peers and surfaces concerns.
  • Translates central guidance into team workflows.
  • Collects realistic scenarios for training content.
Champion Support
Provide lightweight training, a clear role description, and a feedback channel to the security team.
Leadership
Visible, consistent messaging and resource allocation signal that security matters and enable champions to act without friction.
Concrete Example

A director who shares a short note praising a team member for reporting a suspicious email encourages others to do the same.
