Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Measuring effectiveness: key metrics
Primary Metrics
  • Phishing click-through rate (trend over time).
  • Reporting rate of suspicious emails.
  • Training module completion and quiz scores.
  • Incidents attributable to human error.
Measurement Best Practices
- Establish baseline measures before interventions.
- Use multiple indicators to avoid misleading conclusions.
- Report aggregated results and recommended actions to leaders.
Key Takeaway

Use both behavior and completion metrics to assess real learning and incident reduction.
