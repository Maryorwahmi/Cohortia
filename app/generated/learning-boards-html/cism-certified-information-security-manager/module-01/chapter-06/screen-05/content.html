CISM Change Process
1
Assess
Collect baseline metrics and employee feedback to locate human risk areas.
2
Design
Create role-relevant training and policy reinforcements tied to identified risks.
3
Engage
Use leadership messaging and security champions to make training stick.
4
Measure
Track phishing rates, reporting, completion, quiz scores, and incident trends.
5
Adapt
Refine content and cadence based on evidence to reduce incidents over time.
Concrete Example
After finding credential reuse in one team, deploy a short module, enable second-factor for the group, and run a follow-up simulation to measure improvement.
Key Takeaway

Change is iterative: combine evidence and local engagement to build lasting behavior change.
