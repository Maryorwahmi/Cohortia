Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Hands-on: Phishing simulation and follow-up plan
Plan Template (Use as guide)
1. Scenario
Realistic lure like an HR benefits update asking to click a link to review details.
2. Target audience
All staff except security and notified leadership to obtain a representative vulnerability baseline.
3. Click action and landing
Redirect to an educational page explaining the simulation and providing a short tip.
4. Follow-up training
Mandatory 15-minute interactive module for clickers within 24 hours covering tactics and reporting steps.
Evaluation
Choose metrics: phishing click rate, reporting rate, completion, and incident trends. Baseline before testing and re-measure after training.
Key Takeaway

Pair simulations with immediate education and supportive communication to maximize learning and trust.
