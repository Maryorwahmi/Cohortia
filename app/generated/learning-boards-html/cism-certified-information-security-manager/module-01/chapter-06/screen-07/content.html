Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Core components of an effective awareness program
Program Components
Objectives
Concrete skills and behaviors expected after training.
Content
Role‑specific scenarios like phishing for finance staff.
Delivery
Interactive modules, videos, gamification, and simulations.
Cadence
Regular refreshers reduce knowledge decay and respond to new threats.
Policy
Link training to procedures so employees can act on guidance.
Leadership
Visible endorsement and enforcement from management.
Concrete Example
Quarterly microlearning for customer service that walks through identity checks and reporting procedures, tied to policy and reinforced by team leads.
Key Takeaway

Design training as an ongoing program that aligns with daily work, not a single event.
