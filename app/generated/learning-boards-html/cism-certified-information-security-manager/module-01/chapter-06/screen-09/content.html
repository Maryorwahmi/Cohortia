Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Phishing simulations: design and follow-up
Design Checklist
  • Scenario: believable internal message relevant to the audience.
  • Sender: realistic but controlled address; avoid targeted leaders who are aware.
  • Call to action: plausible request that encourages a decision.
  • Landing page: immediate education and logging for follow-up.
Follow-up Plan
- Immediate tip on the landing page explaining the simulation.
- Mandatory 15-minute interactive module for clickers within 24 hours.
- Aggregate communications to all employees summarizing lessons without naming individuals.
Key Takeaway

The educational moment immediately after a simulated click is the most powerful opportunity to change behavior.
