Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Organizational Culture
Shared values and practices that determine whether security is routine or ignored.
Human Element
Individuals can be vulnerabilities or defenders depending on training and context.
Security Awareness Training
Continuous, role-relevant, and engaging learning that translates to safer behavior.
Summary
Use culture, people, and continuous training as a framework to design programs that reduce human-related incidents and increase reporting.
