Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Phishing Simulations
Controlled exercises that reveal behavior and create immediate learning opportunities.
Security Champions
Departmental advocates who localize training and surface real issues to the security team.
Metrics for Awareness
Quantitative measures like click rates, reporting, and incidents used to guide improvements.
Takeaway
Pair realistic testing, local advocates, and clear metrics to build trust and demonstrate continuous improvement.
