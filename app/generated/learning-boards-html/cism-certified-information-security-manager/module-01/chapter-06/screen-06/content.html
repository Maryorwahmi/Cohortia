Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › People as vulnerabilities and assets
Common Failure Modes
  • Clicking phishing links due to urgency or curiosity.
  • Reusing weak passwords across accounts.
  • Accidentally sharing sensitive files or misconfiguring access.
  • Not reporting suspicious activity for fear of blame.
Misconception
Blaming users ignores process design and lack of training that make risky choices likely.
Proactive Behaviors
  • Reporting suspected phishing instead of clicking.
  • Using approved tools and following access procedures.
  • Participating in simulations and learning from feedback.
  • Acting as a local security champion to coach peers.
Takeaway

Transforming people into defenders requires removing friction and recognizing positive actions.
