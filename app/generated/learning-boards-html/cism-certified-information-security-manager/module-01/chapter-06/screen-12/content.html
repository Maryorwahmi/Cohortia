Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Driving continuous improvement in awareness programs
Improve Cycle
  1. Measure baseline and identify gaps.
  2. Design targeted interventions for the gap.
  3. Implement and communicate changes.
  4. Re-measure and compare to baseline.
Practical Example
If a department shows high click rates, create a short tailored module, run a follow-up simulation, and compare click rates over the next quarter.
Key Takeaway

Small experiments, measured and communicated, drive sustained improvement and buy-in.
