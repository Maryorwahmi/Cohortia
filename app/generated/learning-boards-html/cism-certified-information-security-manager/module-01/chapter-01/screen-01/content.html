Introduction to Information Security Management

Protecting Your Organization's Most Valuable Assets

This chapter lays the groundwork for understanding information security management as a strategic imperative. We'll explore its purpose, key drivers, and how it differs from related terms like cybersecurity. You'll also be introduced to the four core domains of the CISM job practice, setting the stage for your journey to becoming a certified information security manager.

Key Takeaway

Effective information security management is a holistic, strategic imperative that protects an organization's most valuable assets and ensures business continuity.

The Core Challenge

Protecting information assets from evolving threats.

Ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA).
