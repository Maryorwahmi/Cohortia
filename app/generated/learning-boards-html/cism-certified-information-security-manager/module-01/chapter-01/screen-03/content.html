03

Distinguish InfoSec, Cyber, IA

Differentiate between information security, cybersecurity, and information assurance, understanding their distinct scopes and applications. This clarity is essential for precise communication and strategy.

Verified Competency Milestone
04

Recognize CISM Job Practice Domains

Identify the four core domains of the CISM job practice and understand their interrelation. This provides a framework for managing information security effectively across an organization.

Verified Competency Milestone
05

Articulate Value to Stakeholders

Learn to communicate the strategic value of effective information security management to business leaders. This involves translating technical risks into business impacts and demonstrating ROI.
