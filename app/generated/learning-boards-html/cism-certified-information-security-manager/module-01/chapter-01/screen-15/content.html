Key Concepts: Frameworks & Compliance

Understanding CISM Domains and Regulations

Information Assurance (IA)

Broader term (often gov/military) encompassing CIA plus non-repudiation, authentication, and reliability.

CISM Domains

Four main areas: Governance, Risk Management, Program Development, and Incident Management.

Regulatory Compliance

Adherence to laws, regulations, and guidelines relevant to information security (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).

Summary Takeaway

These concepts define the structured approach and external mandates that shape an information security manager's role and responsibilities.
