The Four CISM Job Practice Domains

Strategic Areas for an InfoSec Manager

1. InfoSec Governance

Establishes and maintains a framework to ensure information security strategies align with business objectives.

Focus: Strategic direction, oversight.

2. InfoSec Risk Management

Identifies, assesses, and mitigates information security risks to an acceptable level for the organization.

Focus: Threat identification, mitigation.

3. Program Development & Mgmt.

Designs, implements, and manages an organization's information security program and controls.

Focus: Implementation, operations.

4. InfoSec Incident Management

Develops and manages the organization's capability to detect, respond to, and recover from security incidents.

Focus: Response, recovery.
Key Takeaway

The four CISM domains provide a structured framework for managing all facets of an organization's information security program.
