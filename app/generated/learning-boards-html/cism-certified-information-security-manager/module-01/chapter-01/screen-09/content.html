InfoSec, Cybersecurity, and Information Assurance

Understanding the Nuances

Information Security

The broadest term, covering all aspects of protecting information (digital and physical) throughout its lifecycle.

Scope: All information assets, all threats.

Cybersecurity

A subset of information security, focused specifically on protecting digital systems, networks, and data from cyber threats.

Scope: Digital realm, cyber threats.

Information Assurance (IA)

A subset often used in government, focusing on CIA plus authenticity, non-repudiation, and reliability of information.

Scope: CIA + authenticity, non-repudiation, reliability.
Key Takeaway

While related, Information Security is the overarching discipline, with Cybersecurity and Information Assurance being more specialized areas of focus.
