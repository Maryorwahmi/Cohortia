Communicating Security to Executives

Translating Technical Risks into Business Impacts

Common Mistake: Technical Focus

Overwhelming executives with intricate technical details, jargon, and low-level security configurations.

Example: Explaining the specifics of AES-256 encryption algorithms to the CEO.

Best Practice: Business Impact Focus

Translating technical risks into tangible business impacts and presenting solutions in terms of risk reduction and value creation.

Example: Explaining how encryption reduces regulatory fines and protects customer trust.
Key Takeaway

Effective communication with executives requires translating technical security concepts into clear business risks, benefits, and value propositions.
