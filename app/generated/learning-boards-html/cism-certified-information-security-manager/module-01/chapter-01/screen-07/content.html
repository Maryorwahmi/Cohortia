Key Drivers: Business Continuity & Resilience

Ensuring Uninterrupted Operations

Minimizing Operational Disruption

Security incidents can halt critical business functions, leading to significant financial losses and operational chaos.

Example: A ransomware attack encrypting core business servers, preventing access to essential data.

Ensuring Rapid Recovery

A resilient security program includes plans and technologies to quickly restore services and data after an incident.

Example: Implementing robust backup and disaster recovery solutions to restore systems quickly.
Key Takeaway

Information security is fundamental to maintaining business continuity and resilience, safeguarding operations from disruptive security incidents.
