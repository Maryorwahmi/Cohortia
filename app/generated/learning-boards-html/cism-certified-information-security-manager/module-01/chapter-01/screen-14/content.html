Key Concepts: Core Definitions

Essential Terms to Remember

InfoSec Management

Systematic approach to protecting an organization's information assets from various threats.

CIA Triad

Fundamental principles: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability of information.

Cybersecurity

Subset of InfoSec focused on protecting digital systems, networks, and data from cyber threats.

Summary Takeaway

These core concepts form the essential vocabulary and foundational understanding for effective information security management.
