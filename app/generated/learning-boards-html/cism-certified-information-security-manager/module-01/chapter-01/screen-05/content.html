The CIA Triad: Pillars of Information Security

Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability

Confidentiality

Ensures information is accessible only to authorized individuals or systems. Prevents unauthorized disclosure.

Example: Encrypting sensitive customer data to prevent unauthorized viewing.

Integrity

Maintains the accuracy, completeness, and consistency of data. Prevents unauthorized modification or destruction.

Example: Using checksums to verify that a downloaded file hasn't been tampered with.

Availability

Ensures that authorized users can access information and systems when needed. Prevents service disruptions.

Example: Implementing redundant servers to ensure a website remains online during peak traffic.
Key Takeaway

The CIA triad forms the bedrock of information security, guiding all efforts to protect data from unauthorized access, modification, and disruption.
