Key Drivers: Regulatory & Legal Obligations

Mandates for Data Protection

Legal & Compliance Mandates

Organizations must adhere to a complex web of laws, regulations, and industry standards to avoid penalties and legal repercussions.

Example: GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) for personal data protection.

Key Regulations & Standards

Specific frameworks dictate how sensitive information, such as health records or payment data, must be handled and secured.

Examples: HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOX, CCPA.
Key Takeaway

Adherence to regulatory and legal obligations is a non-negotiable driver for information security, protecting organizations from significant fines and legal repercussions.
