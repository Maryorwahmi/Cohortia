Diverse Strategic Lenses

Applying Balanced Scorecard and NIST CSF to Security

Balanced Scorecard

Translates strategy into measurable objectives across four perspectives.

  • Financial: Impact of breaches, ROI of security investments.
  • Customer: Trust in data protection, privacy satisfaction.
  • Internal Processes: Efficiency of security operations, incident response.
  • Learning & Growth: Security skill development, continuous improvement.

NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

Helps manage and reduce cybersecurity risk through five core functions.

  • Identify: Understand assets, risks, and governance.
  • Protect: Implement safeguards to ensure delivery of critical services.
  • Detect: Identify cybersecurity events.
  • Respond: Take action on detected cybersecurity incidents.
  • Recover: Restore capabilities and services impaired by incidents.
Key Takeaway

Leveraging frameworks like Balanced Scorecard and NIST CSF provides a structured, comprehensive approach to security strategy and risk management.
