Process Flow

Steps to a Robust Security Strategy

A Structured Approach to Information Security Planning

1

Business Context & Objectives

Understand mission, vision, values, and strategic priorities. Identify critical assets and threats.

2

Current State Assessment

Evaluate existing security posture, capabilities, and weaknesses through audits and assessments.

3

Gap Identification

Identify differences between current and desired future states, considering risk appetite.

4

Strategic Objectives Definition

Define specific, measurable goals for the security program (SMART objectives).

5

Roadmap Development

Detail initiatives, timelines, resources, and performance metrics to achieve objectives.

Key Takeaway

A structured, iterative process ensures the information security strategy remains relevant, adaptable, and aligned with business goals.
