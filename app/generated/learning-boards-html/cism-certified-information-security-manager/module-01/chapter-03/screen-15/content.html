Key Concepts (Continued)

Reinforcing Your Strategic Security Knowledge

Maturity Models

Frameworks used to assess the sophistication and effectiveness of an organization's security processes and capabilities.

Strategic Objectives

Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals that the information security strategy aims to accomplish.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Quantifiable metrics used to track and evaluate the performance and effectiveness of the information security program against its objectives.

Summary Takeaway

By leveraging maturity models, defining SMART objectives, and tracking with relevant KPIs, CISMs can effectively manage and communicate the value of their security programs.
