Assessment Question 1

What is the *most critical* initial step a CISM should ensure to prevent incidents from risky third-party vendors?

Correct Answer: Conducting thorough pre-contractual security assessment and due diligence.

Key Takeaway

Proactive due diligence is the cornerstone of preventing third-party risks.

Assessment Question 2

A software development vendor uses an open-source library with a known vulnerability. This is an example of:

Correct Answer: Nth-party risk.

Nth-Party Risk Explanation

This risk arises from a vendor's own dependencies, highlighting the interconnected nature of modern supply chains and the need to manage risks beyond direct relationships.
