Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › CISM's Role in TPRM Governance
Key Responsibilities
  • Develop and enforce TPRM policies.
  • Define and manage the TPRM process lifecycle.
  • Ensure adequate resource allocation (budget, staff).
  • Foster a culture of shared security responsibility.
  • Integrate security into procurement and business processes.
Key Takeaway

The CISM's leadership is essential for establishing a comprehensive and effective TPRM governance framework.

Strategic Leadership

Your role extends beyond technical oversight to strategic leadership. This involves championing TPRM initiatives, educating stakeholders, and ensuring security requirements are embedded in business decisions from the outset. For example, ensuring security is a key criterion in selecting a new SaaS platform, not an afterthought.

Shared Responsibility

Effective TPRM requires collaboration across departments. The CISM must cultivate an environment where procurement, legal, and business units understand their roles in managing third-party risks, making security a collective effort.
