Key Concepts in TPRM

Essential takeaways for managing third-party risks.

TPRM

Systematic process to identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor risks from external entities.

Third Party

Any external entity interacting with your organization's information assets.

Nth-Party Risk

Risk introduced by a third party's own vendors or subcontractors.

Due Diligence

Investigating a third party's security posture before contract signing.

SLA

Contractual agreement defining service levels, including security metrics.

Summary Takeaway

Effective TPRM requires a comprehensive understanding of risks from all external partners, managed through diligent assessment, clear contracts, and continuous oversight.
