Learning Objectives

What you will be able to do after this chapter.

01

Define TPRM and its Strategic Importance

Understand what Third-Party Risk Management is and why it's a critical strategic component for information security, especially for a CISM.

02

Identify Third-Party Relationships

Recognize the various types of external entities that require security oversight, from vendors to cloud providers.

03

Articulate the CISM's Role in TPRM

Understand the CISM's responsibilities in establishing, governing, and maintaining an effective TPRM program.

04

Understand Consequences of Inadequate TPRM

Grasp the potential negative outcomes, including data breaches and regulatory penalties, from failing to manage third-party risks.
