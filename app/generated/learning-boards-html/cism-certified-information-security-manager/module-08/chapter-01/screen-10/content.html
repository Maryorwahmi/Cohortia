Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › TPRM Scenario: Cloud HR Platform
Scenario Overview

Considering a new cloud HR platform that will store sensitive employee PII, payroll, and performance data. This is a high-risk third-party scenario.

Key Takeaway

Thorough due diligence across multiple security domains is essential when onboarding high-risk third-party vendors.

Critical Assessment Areas
  • Data Protection & Privacy
  • Access Management
  • Security Architecture & Controls
  • Incident Response & Business Continuity
  • Compliance & Certifications
  • Sub-processor Management (Nth-Party Risk)
CISM's Action

Initiate communication with HR and Procurement, clearly outlining the security requirements and the need for comprehensive due diligence before finalizing the contract.
