Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Consequences of Inadequate TPRM
Major Consequences
  • Data Breaches: Compromise of sensitive customer, financial, or proprietary data.
  • Regulatory Penalties: Significant fines from non-compliance with laws like GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA.
  • Financial Losses: Costs associated with incident response, remediation, legal fees, and lost business.
  • Reputational Damage: Erosion of customer trust, negative brand perception, and loss of market share.
  • Service Disruptions: Interruption of critical business operations due to third-party failures.
Key Takeaway

Ignoring TPRM can lead to catastrophic outcomes, impacting finances, reputation, and operational stability.

Accountability

Organizations are often held directly accountable for the security practices of their third-party processors, especially concerning sensitive data. Regulatory frameworks like GDPR and CCPA explicitly place this responsibility on the primary organization, making robust TPRM essential for compliance.

Example Scenario

If a SaaS provider hosting your CRM system experiences a data breach, your organization is ultimately responsible for the compromised customer data, leading to potential customer loss and brand damage.
