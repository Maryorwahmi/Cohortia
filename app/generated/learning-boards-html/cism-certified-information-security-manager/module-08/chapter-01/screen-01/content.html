Introduction to Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)

Securing Your Organization in an Interconnected World

In today's business environment, organizations are rarely islands. We rely on a complex web of external partners – vendors, service providers, cloud platforms, and more – to deliver essential services and support our operations. While these relationships bring efficiency and innovation, they also introduce significant information security risks. A weakness in a third party's security can directly impact your organization, leading to data breaches, service disruptions, and severe penalties. As a CISM, understanding and managing these risks is not just a technical requirement, but a strategic imperative.
