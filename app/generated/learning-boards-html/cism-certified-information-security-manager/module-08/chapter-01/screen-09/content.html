Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › TPRM Lifecycle
Lifecycle Stages
  • Selection & Due Diligence: Pre-contractual assessment of vendor security.
  • Onboarding: Establishing secure access and contractual terms.
  • Ongoing Monitoring: Continuous assessment of vendor security posture and compliance.
  • Offboarding: Securely revoking access and managing data upon contract termination.
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive TPRM lifecycle ensures risks are managed proactively from initial engagement through to the end of the relationship.

Due Diligence

This initial phase is critical for identifying potential risks before they become problems. It involves evaluating a vendor's security policies, certifications (like ISO 27001 or SOC 2), incident response capabilities, and data handling practices to ensure they align with your organization's security requirements.

Ongoing Monitoring

Security is not static. Continuous monitoring ensures that vendors maintain their security posture over time. This can involve reviewing updated audit reports, monitoring for public security advisories related to the vendor, and ensuring compliance with Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
