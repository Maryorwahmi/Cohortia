Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Assessing Third-Party Risk
Due Diligence Areas
  • Data Protection & Privacy: Encryption, retention, location.
  • Access Management: Authentication, authorization, user controls.
  • Security Architecture & Controls: Network, host, application security, vulnerability management.
  • Incident Response & BCP: Breach detection, response, disaster recovery.
  • Compliance & Certifications: ISO 27001, SOC 2, audit reports.
Key Takeaway

Thorough due diligence is the foundation of TPRM, ensuring you understand and can manage the risks posed by third parties.

Why Ask These Questions?

Asking specific questions about encryption, access controls, incident response, and compliance helps you verify a vendor's security posture. This information is crucial for identifying potential vulnerabilities, ensuring regulatory compliance (like GDPR or HIPAA), and negotiating appropriate contractual safeguards.

Example Question

"What is your process for managing vulnerabilities discovered in third-party components or open-source libraries used in your service?" This probes their Nth-party risk management capabilities.
