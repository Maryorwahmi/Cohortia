Types of Third Parties

Organizations interact with a wide range of external entities, including:

  • Vendors & Suppliers
  • Cloud Service Providers (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS)
  • Software Developers & Integrators
  • Consultants & Contractors
  • Business Partners
  • Outsourced Service Providers
Key Takeaway

Effectively managing third-party risk requires understanding the diverse nature of these relationships and tailoring oversight accordingly.

Risk Classification

It's crucial to classify third parties based on their criticality and the sensitivity of data or systems they access. This classification dictates the level of due diligence and ongoing monitoring required. For example, a cloud provider hosting sensitive customer PII requires a much more rigorous assessment than a local office supply vendor.

Misconception

Assuming all third parties pose the same level of risk. A one-size-fits-all approach is often ineffective and can lead to overlooking critical vulnerabilities or over-burdening low-risk vendors.
