Module 8›Chapter 8.2›Building a Comprehensive Inventory

Systematic Identification of Third Parties

Beyond Just Asking: A Proactive Approach

01

Collaborate with Departments

Engage procurement, legal, HR, IT, and business units to identify all existing relationships.

02

Review Contracts & POs

Scrutinize formal agreements and purchase orders for explicit vendor details and service scopes.

03

Analyze System Logs

Examine system logs and network traffic to uncover undocumented or technical integrations.

04

Document Relationships

Maintain a centralized, accurate, and up-to-date repository of all identified third-party engagements.

Key Takeaway

A proactive, multi-departmental approach to inventorying third parties ensures no critical relationship or potential risk is overlooked.
