Core Concept

Why TPRM Starts Here

Effective Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) is impossible without a clear understanding of all external entities interacting with your organization's information assets. This forms the bedrock of your security posture.

CISM's Role

Establishing a Systematic Process

The CISM must lead the charge in creating a systematic process for identifying and documenting every external entity. This requires collaboration across various departments.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive and accurate third-party inventory is the non-negotiable first step in managing external security risks effectively.

Key Component

The Third-Party Inventory

This living document lists all external entities that interact with your organization's:

  • •Information Assets (e.g., databases, documents)
  • •Systems (e.g., cloud platforms, applications)
  • •Processes (e.g., payroll, customer support)
Why it matters

Without this inventory, you cannot identify potential vulnerabilities, assess the scope of data exposure, or implement appropriate security controls for your extended enterprise.
