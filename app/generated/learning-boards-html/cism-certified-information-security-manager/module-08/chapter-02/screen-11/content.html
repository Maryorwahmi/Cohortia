Automating Third-Party Inventory and Assessment

Leveraging VRM Platforms for Efficiency

VRM Platform Benefits

  • Centralized Repository

    Stores all vendor information, assessment results, and contractual agreements in one place.

  • Automated Workflow

    Streamlines assessment processes, questionnaire distribution, and response tracking.

Key VRM Platforms

  • Archer

    A comprehensive GRC platform with robust VRM capabilities.

  • OneTrust

    Specializes in privacy, security, and third-party risk management.

  • ServiceNow GRC

    Integrates risk management with broader IT and business processes.

Key Takeaway

VRM platforms enhance efficiency in TPRM, but human judgment remains indispensable for nuanced risk interpretation and strategic decision-making.
