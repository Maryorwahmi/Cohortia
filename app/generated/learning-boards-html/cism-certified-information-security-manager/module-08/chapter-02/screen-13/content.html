Essential Takeaways

Reinforcing Core Principles of TPRM

Third-Party Inventory

A comprehensive, up-to-date list of all external entities interacting with an organization's information assets.

Vendor Categorization/Tiering

Classifying third parties based on criticality of service and access to sensitive data to determine security scrutiny.

Data Classification

The process of categorizing data based on its sensitivity, value, and impact if compromised.

Summary Takeaway

A robust TPRM program is built upon a clear understanding of external relationships, their categorization by risk, and the precise classification of data they handle.
