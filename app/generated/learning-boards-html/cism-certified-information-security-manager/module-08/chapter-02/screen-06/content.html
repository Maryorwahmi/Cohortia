Grouping Vendors by Criticality and Data Access

Tailoring Security Scrutiny to Each Relationship

Categorization Factors

  • Type of Service Provided

    What specific functions or services does the third party deliver to your organization?

  • Criticality of Service

    How essential is this service to your organization's core operations and business continuity?

  • Data Sensitivity & Access

    What type and sensitivity level of data will the third party access, store, or process?

Risk Tiers & Examples

  • Low Risk Vendor

    e.g., Office supply vendor. Minimal data access, easily replaceable service. Requires basic security questionnaire.

  • High Risk Vendor

    e.g., Cloud infrastructure provider. Extensive data access, critical service. Requires extensive due diligence and continuous monitoring.

Key Takeaway

Vendor categorization is crucial for allocating security resources effectively, ensuring that the level of scrutiny matches the potential risk.
