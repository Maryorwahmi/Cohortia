Identifying Third-Party Relationships

Safeguarding Your Data Beyond Organizational Borders

Key Idea

Effective security extends beyond your direct control, requiring diligent management of all external entities accessing your data.

In this chapter, you will learn to systematically identify and categorize all third-party relationships, apply data classification principles to shared information, and understand how these directly influence the rigor of security assessments. This foundational knowledge is critical for building a robust Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) program.

Topic 1

Build a complete inventory of all external relationships.

Topic 2

Classify data shared with third parties by sensitivity.

Topic 3

Tailor security assessments based on identified risks.
