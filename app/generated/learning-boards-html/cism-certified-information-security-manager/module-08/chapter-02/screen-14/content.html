Key Concepts Continued

Deepening Your Understanding of Data Security

Data Sensitivity

A measure of the potential harm or impact if specific data were to be disclosed, altered, or destroyed without authorization.

VRM Platform

Software solutions designed to help organizations manage the lifecycle of third-party risks, including inventory, assessment, monitoring, and reporting.

Risk-Based Assessment

Tailoring the depth and frequency of security assessments based on the identified risk level of a third party.

Summary Takeaway

By understanding data sensitivity, leveraging VRM platforms, and conducting risk-based assessments, organizations can build a resilient and adaptive security posture.
