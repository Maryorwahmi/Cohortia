RISK CATEGORY

Tampering and Counterfeiting in Hardware

Physical and firmware-level threats.

Key Hardware Risk Areas

  • Tampering: Unauthorized modification of hardware components or firmware.
  • Counterfeiting: Fake or unauthorized components passed off as genuine.
  • Malicious Insertion: Deliberate introduction of backdoors or surveillance features.
  • Manufacturing Phase: Vulnerabilities during chip fabrication or device assembly.
  • Distribution Phase: Compromise during shipping or storage before deployment.
Key Takeaway

Hardware supply chain risks can introduce persistent, difficult-to-detect vulnerabilities, necessitating rigorous verification and trusted supplier relationships.
