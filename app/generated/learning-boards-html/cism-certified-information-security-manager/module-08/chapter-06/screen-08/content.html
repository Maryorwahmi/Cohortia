CONSEQUENCES

Devastating Effects of Compromise

Beyond immediate data breaches, impacting trust and operations.

Key Impacts

  • Widespread Data Breaches: Exposure of sensitive information across many affected entities.
  • Operational Disruption: Halting of critical business processes and services.
  • Intellectual Property Theft: Loss of valuable innovations and competitive advantage.
  • Erosion of Trust: Damage to reputation and stakeholder confidence.
  • Difficult Detection: Attacks often leverage trusted channels, making them hard to spot.
Key Takeaway

Supply chain attacks have severe, cascading impacts that extend beyond technical compromise to fundamental business operations and stakeholder trust.

Impact Hierarchy

1. Initial Compromise (e.g., Vendor)

2. Propagation (e.g., Software Update)

3. Operational Disruption / Data Breach

4. Erosion of Trust / Reputational Damage

Scenario Insight

A compromised software component can lead to a chain reaction, affecting multiple customers and causing widespread outages.
