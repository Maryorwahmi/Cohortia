CORE CONCEPT

Supply Chain Security vs. TPRM

A broader, more holistic perspective on risk.

Key Differences

  • TPRM: Focuses on direct contractual relationships with vendors and service providers.
  • Supply Chain Security: Encompasses the entire ecosystem, including Nth-parties, components, software, and hardware.
  • Scope: TPRM is narrower, while Supply Chain Security is holistic and end-to-end.
  • Risk Propagation: Supply Chain Security acknowledges risks propagating through indirect dependencies.
Key Takeaway

Supply Chain Security provides a comprehensive view of risks across the entire product/service delivery ecosystem, extending beyond direct vendor relationships.
