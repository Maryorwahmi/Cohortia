Key Concepts

Reinforcing your understanding.

Hardware Supply Chain

The process of designing, manufacturing, assembling, and distributing hardware components and devices.

Software Bill of Materials (SBoM)

A formal, machine-readable list of all ingredients that make up a piece of software.

Supply Chain Attack

A cyberattack targeting an organization by compromising less secure elements in its supply chain.

Summary Takeaway

From physical hardware to software components, understanding the tools and attack vectors within the supply chain is essential for robust security management.
