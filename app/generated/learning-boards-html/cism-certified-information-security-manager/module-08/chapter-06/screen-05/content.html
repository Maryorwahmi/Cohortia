The Nth-Party Challenge

Uncovering risks beyond your direct vendors.

Understanding Nth-Party Risks

  • Definition: Risks introduced by subcontractors or components used by a direct third-party vendor.
  • Propagation: Vulnerabilities can cascade through multiple layers of indirect dependencies.
  • Visibility Gap: Often difficult to gain insight into these deeper layers of the supply chain.
  • Impact: A compromise at any Nth-party level can directly affect your organization's security.
Key Takeaway

Nth-party risks highlight the interconnectedness of modern systems, requiring vigilance over your entire extended enterprise, not just direct partners.
