Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember.

Supply Chain Security

Protecting products and services from threats arising at any point in the supply chain, from design to maintenance.

Nth-Party Risk

Risks introduced by subcontractors or components used by a direct third-party vendor, extending the supply chain.

Software Supply Chain

The entire process of developing, building, and delivering software, including all its components and tools.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the full scope of your supply chain, including indirect dependencies and software components, is crucial for effective risk management.
