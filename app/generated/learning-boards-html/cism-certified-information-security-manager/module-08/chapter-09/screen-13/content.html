Leadership & Collaboration

Collaborative Security and the CISM's Strategic Role

Fostering Adaptation and Vigilance Across the Extended Enterprise

Cross-Organizational Collaboration

Participating in industry-specific ISACs to share threat intelligence and best practices related to supply chain risks.

CISM as Strategic Leader

Anticipating future risks, advocating for necessary investments, and fostering a culture of continuous adaptation and vigilance.

Example

A CISM regularly presents to the board on emerging geopolitical risks, translating potential supply chain disruptions into financial and reputational impacts, securing budget for diversifying cloud providers and enhancing threat intelligence subscriptions.

Key Takeaway

CISMs are strategic leaders who must drive cross-organizational collaboration and foster continuous adaptation to effectively manage evolving supply chain risks and build future resilience.
