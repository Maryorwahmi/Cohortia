Geopolitical Factors and Supply Chain Integrity

How Global Politics Influence Component and Service Availability

Trade Wars & Sanctions

Economic conflicts and restrictions can disrupt access to critical components and services, impacting availability.

State-Sponsored Cyber Espionage

Nation-states targeting supply chains to gain intelligence or disrupt critical infrastructure, often through mandated backdoors.

Real-World Example

A company relying on a specific microchip manufacturer located in a region experiencing political instability might face sudden supply disruptions or concerns about product integrity due to state influence.

Key Takeaway

Geopolitical factors introduce significant risks to supply chain integrity, necessitating scrutiny of supplier national origin and potential state-backed interference.
