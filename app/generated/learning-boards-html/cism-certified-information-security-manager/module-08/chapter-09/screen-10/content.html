New Regulations Shaping TPRM and Supply Chain Security

Understanding NIS2, DORA, and Executive Order 14028

NIS2 Directive (EU)

Strengthens cybersecurity for critical entities, explicitly extending accountability to their supply chains.

DORA (EU)

Enhances digital operational resilience for the financial sector, with extensive ICT third-party risk management requirements.

EO 14028 (US)

Focuses on improving US cybersecurity, emphasizing software supply chain security and the use of SBoMs.

Impact on CISMs

These regulations carry significant penalties for non-compliance, forcing CISMs to adopt more rigorous and auditable TPRM and supply chain security programs, extending their oversight beyond internal controls.

Key Takeaway

New regulations like NIS2 and DORA explicitly extend cybersecurity accountability to an organization's supply chain, demanding more rigorous and auditable TPRM programs.
