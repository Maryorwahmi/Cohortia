Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Supply Chain Resilience

The ability of an organization and its supply chain to anticipate, withstand, and recover from disruptions, including cyberattacks and geopolitical events.

NIS2 Directive (EU)

An EU directive strengthening cybersecurity across critical entities and sectors, with significant emphasis on supply chain security.

DORA (EU)

An EU regulation enhancing the digital operational resilience of the financial sector, including extensive ICT third-party risk management requirements.

Summary Takeaway

Building supply chain resilience and complying with evolving regulations like NIS2 and DORA are critical for extending cybersecurity accountability and ensuring operational continuity.
