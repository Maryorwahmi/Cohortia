Building Supply Chain Resilience

Diversifying and Distributing for Enhanced Stability

01. Diversifying Suppliers

Reduce reliance on single vendors by establishing relationships with multiple, geographically diverse suppliers for critical components and services.

02. Geographically Distributed Operations

Avoid concentrating all critical assets or production capabilities in one region to mitigate localized disruptions.

03. Pivot to Alternative Sources

Pre-plan and establish contingency contracts with backup vendors to ensure rapid access to alternatives during crises.

CISM Collaboration

CISMs must engage with legal and risk management teams to integrate geopolitical implications into risk assessments and business continuity plans.

Key Takeaway

Supply chain resilience involves proactive strategies like diversifying suppliers, distributing operations, and planning for alternative sources to withstand disruptions.
