Key Challenge

Visibility into Nth-party risks is incredibly challenging, as organizations often lack direct insight into their vendors' sub-processors and underlying infrastructure.

Key Takeaway

The proliferation of cloud services creates an opaque and highly interconnected supply chain, making traditional risk management insufficient for Nth-party dependencies.
