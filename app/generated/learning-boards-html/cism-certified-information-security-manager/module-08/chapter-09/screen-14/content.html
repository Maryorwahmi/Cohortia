Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Cloud Supply Chain

The complex network of cloud service providers, their sub-processors, and underlying infrastructure supporting an organization's cloud operations.

AI/ML Security

Security considerations and risks associated with AI/ML systems, including data poisoning and adversarial attacks.

Geopolitical Risk

Risks from international political and economic factors impacting the availability, integrity, or confidentiality of supply chain components.

Summary Takeaway

The modern supply chain is increasingly complex, influenced by cloud dependencies, novel AI/ML vulnerabilities, and global political dynamics, all demanding a comprehensive security approach.
