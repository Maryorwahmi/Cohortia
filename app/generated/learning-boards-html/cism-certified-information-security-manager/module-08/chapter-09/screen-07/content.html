Securing Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Protecting Against Data Poisoning and Adversarial Attacks

Data Poisoning Attacks

Malicious data is introduced into an AI model's training set to corrupt its learning and alter its behavior.

Adversarial Examples

Subtly manipulated inputs that cause an AI model to misclassify or misinterpret data, often imperceptible to humans.

Example

A data poisoning attack could involve feeding an image recognition AI with mislabeled images, causing it to incorrectly identify objects in the future, potentially impacting critical systems like autonomous vehicles or medical diagnostics.

Key Takeaway

AI/ML introduces new attack surfaces like data poisoning and adversarial examples, requiring CISMs to assess the security of the entire AI development lifecycle.
