Integrating Threat Intelligence and Cyber Resilience

Designing Systems for Rapid Recovery from Disruptions

Threat Intelligence Integration

Incorporating real-time, supply chain-focused threat intelligence feeds to anticipate and mitigate emerging attack vectors.

Cyber Resilience Engineering

Designing systems and processes with inherent fault tolerance, redundancy, and graceful degradation to enable rapid recovery from disruptions.

Example

A company implements a multi-cloud architecture for critical applications (resilience engineering) and subscribes to a specialized threat intelligence feed that alerts them to vulnerabilities in cloud provider APIs, allowing them to proactively adjust configurations.

Key Takeaway

Combining real-time threat intelligence with cyber resilience engineering ensures systems are designed to withstand and rapidly recover from supply chain disruptions.
