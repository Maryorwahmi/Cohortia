Remediation Step

Step 1: Notification and Remediation Plan

Formally communicating issues and requesting a corrective strategy.

Formal Notification

Inform the vendor of the identified issue, citing specific contractual clauses or policy violations.

Detailed Remediation Plan

Require a plan with specific actions, responsible parties, and target completion dates for each deficiency.

Clarity and Accountability

Ensures both parties understand the issue and the proposed solution, fostering accountability.

Roadmap to Resolution

A detailed plan provides a clear roadmap for addressing and resolving identified security deficiencies.

Key Takeaway

Formal notification and a detailed remediation plan are the foundational steps to initiate a transparent and accountable process for addressing non-compliance.
