Core Concept

The Imperative of Continuous Monitoring

Why 'set it and forget it' is a dangerous mistake in Third-Party Risk Management.

Dynamic Security Posture

A vendor's security is not static; it can degrade over time due to new vulnerabilities, personnel changes, or evolving threats.

The 'Set It and Forget It' Trap

Relying solely on initial assessments leaves organizations exposed to dynamic risks and potential non-compliance.

Lifecycle Vigilance

Monitoring must span the entire vendor relationship, ensuring consistent adherence to security obligations and acceptable risk levels.

Scenario Example

The Evolving SaaS Vendor

Imagine a critical SaaS vendor that initially passed all security audits. Six months later, they acquire a smaller company, integrating its infrastructure without a full security review. This introduces new, unpatched systems and potential vulnerabilities into your supply chain, undetected without continuous monitoring.

Key Takeaway

Continuous monitoring is not optional; it's a fundamental requirement for adapting to the ever-changing security landscape of third-party relationships.
