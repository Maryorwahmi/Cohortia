Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential terms for managing non-compliance and performance.

Remediation Plan

A documented strategy outlining the steps, resources, and timeline required to correct identified security deficiencies or non-compliance issues.

Performance Management

The systematic process of setting expectations, monitoring performance, and providing feedback to third parties to ensure they meet security objectives.

Proactive Engagement

Consistent communication and collaboration with vendors, coupled with a clear escalation path, are vital for maintaining a strong third-party security posture.

Summary Takeaway

Effective TPRM requires structured remediation plans, systematic performance management, and proactive engagement to ensure continuous security and compliance.
