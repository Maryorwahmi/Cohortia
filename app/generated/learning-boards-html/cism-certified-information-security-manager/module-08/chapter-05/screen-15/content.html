CISM's Role

The CISM's Pivotal Role in Remediation

Driving proactive engagement and ensuring risk mitigation throughout the process.

Driving the Process

The CISM must actively lead and manage the remediation process, ensuring identified risks are addressed.

Avoiding Inaction

A critical mistake is identifying issues but failing to follow through on remediation, rendering monitoring useless.

Proactive Engagement

Consistent communication and collaboration with vendors are vital for successful remediation.

Clear Escalation Path

Define when and how to escalate issues to internal stakeholders or vendor leadership to ensure resolution.

Key Takeaway

The CISM's active leadership, proactive engagement, and clear escalation path are crucial for effective remediation and maintaining a strong third-party security posture.
