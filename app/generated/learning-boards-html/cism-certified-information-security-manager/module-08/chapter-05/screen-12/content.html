Managing Non-Compliance: A Structured Process

Responding effectively to identified security deficiencies and contractual breaches.

Structured Remediation

Implement a clear, step-by-step process to address security deficiencies and non-adherence to contractual terms.

Avoid 'Identify and Forget'

A common mistake is failing to follow through on remediation, rendering monitoring efforts useless. Ensure follow-up.

Accountability & Risk Mitigation

The process ensures vendors are accountable and identified risks are effectively mitigated, protecting the organization.

Proactive Engagement

Transforms reactive problems into proactive management opportunities, strengthening overall security posture.

Key Takeaway

A structured remediation process is vital for effectively addressing non-compliance, ensuring accountability, and preventing identified risks from escalating.
