Monitoring Method

Regular Reviews and Reassessments

Periodic deep dives into vendor security controls and compliance.

Updated Questionnaires

Requesting new security questionnaires to capture changes in policies, procedures, and controls since the last assessment.

Fresh Audit Reports

Obtaining recent audit reports (e.g., SOC 2 Type 2) to verify control effectiveness and independent assurance.

Follow-up Interviews

Conducting interviews with vendor personnel to gain qualitative insights into their security practices and culture.

Risk Level Alignment

Ensuring the frequency and depth of reviews align with the vendor's assessed risk level, with high-risk vendors reviewed annually.

Key Takeaway

Regular reviews provide essential periodic validation, ensuring vendor security practices keep pace with evolving threats and internal changes.
