Sustaining Security: Ongoing Monitoring and Performance Management

Ensuring continuous third-party adherence to security standards throughout the contract lifecycle.

As a CISM, your role in Third-Party Risk Management extends far beyond initial onboarding. This chapter explores how to implement robust, continuous oversight to protect your organization from dynamic vendor risks. We'll cover essential monitoring techniques, key performance indicators, and effective strategies for addressing non-compliance.

Key Takeaway

Proactive and continuous monitoring is vital to adapt to evolving threats and ensure third parties consistently meet security obligations, safeguarding your organization from dynamic risks.
