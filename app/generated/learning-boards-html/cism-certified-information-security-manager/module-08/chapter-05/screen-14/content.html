Remediation Steps

Steps 2 & 3: Verification and Escalation

Confirming resolution and addressing persistent non-compliance.

Verification of Remediation

Confirm that remediation actions are effectively implemented by requesting evidence, re-running scans, or conducting follow-up assessments.

Escalation Path

If remediation fails, escalate through defined channels, which may include contractual penalties or executive engagement.

Contractual Enforcement

In severe cases, escalation may lead to contract termination, emphasizing the importance of compliance.

Protecting Security Posture

The ultimate goal is to ensure identified risks are addressed and the organization's security posture is maintained.

Key Takeaway

Verification confirms remediation effectiveness, while a clear escalation path ensures persistent non-compliance is addressed, protecting organizational security.
