Assessment Method

Verifying Hardware Integrity

Ensuring the authenticity and security of physical components.

Physical Inspections

Conduct visual and functional checks upon receipt to detect signs of tampering or unauthorized components.

Trusted Suppliers

Work with vendors who can demonstrate secure manufacturing processes and a strong security posture.

DevSecOps Pipeline Assessment

Evaluate the security of the vendor's entire development and deployment pipeline, not just the final product.

Key Takeaway

Hardware security requires rigorous physical and procedural verification, extending to the vendor's entire production and delivery lifecycle.

Hardware Integrity Checklist

Verify component authenticity
Inspect for tamper-evident packaging
Review vendor's security certifications
Conduct periodic audits of critical suppliers

Common Mistake

Over-reliance on brand reputation without independent verification of hardware security controls and supply chain processes.
