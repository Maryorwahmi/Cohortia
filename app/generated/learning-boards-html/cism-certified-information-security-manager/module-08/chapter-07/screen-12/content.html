IR Phase 3

Restoring Operations and Learning from Incidents

Ensuring resilience and continuous improvement.

Recovery

Restore affected systems and services, ensuring the integrity and authenticity of recovered data and software.

Lessons Learned

Conduct a post-incident review to identify weaknesses in the supply chain security program and implement improvements for future resilience.

Key Takeaway

The final stages of incident response focus on secure recovery and leveraging insights from the incident to continuously improve supply chain security posture.

Recovery & Improvement Cycle

1.Restore from trusted backups
2.Verify system integrity post-recovery
3.Update security policies and controls
4.Enhance monitoring for similar threats

Pro-Tip

Regularly test your recovery procedures and incident response plan with simulated supply chain compromise scenarios to identify gaps before a real incident occurs.
