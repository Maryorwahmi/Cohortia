Incident Management

Tailoring Incident Response for Supply Chains

Understanding the unique demands of external compromises.

Unique Challenges

Incidents originate externally, requiring different approaches to assessment, mitigation, and response compared to internal breaches.

Reliance on External Coordination

Effective response heavily depends on established communication channels and collaboration with affected vendors and third parties.

Proactive Planning is Key

A specialized plan is indispensable to ensure swift, coordinated action and minimize impact during a supply chain compromise.

Key Takeaway

A supply chain incident response plan must prioritize external coordination and communication protocols to effectively manage third-party compromises.

Internal vs. Supply Chain IR

Internal Incident Response

  • Direct control over assets
  • Clear internal communication
  • Faster containment

Supply Chain Incident Response

  • Limited direct control
  • Complex external coordination
  • Reliance on vendor cooperation

Common Mistake

Applying a generic internal incident response plan to a supply chain compromise without adapting for external dependencies and coordination needs.
