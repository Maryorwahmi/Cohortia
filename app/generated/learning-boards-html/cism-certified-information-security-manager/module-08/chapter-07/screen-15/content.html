Critical Incident Response Terminology

Key terms for managing supply chain compromises.

Supply Chain Incident Response Plan

A specialized plan outlining procedures for detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents originating from a third-party or Nth-party compromise.

Tamper-Evident Packaging

Packaging designed to show visible signs of interference if it has been opened or altered, used to detect hardware tampering.

Vendor Security Requirements

Explicit controls and expectations an organization places on its suppliers regarding their security posture, practices, and incident response capabilities.

Summary Takeaway

A robust supply chain security program integrates specialized incident response, physical integrity controls, and clear vendor security expectations to protect the extended enterprise.
