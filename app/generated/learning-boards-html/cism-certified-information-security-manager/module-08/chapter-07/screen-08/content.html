Mitigation Strategy

Securing Hardware Sourcing and Handling

Protecting against tampering and unauthorized components.

Diversify Suppliers

Source critical hardware components from multiple trusted vendors to reduce reliance on a single point of failure.

Tamper-Evident Packaging

Utilize packaging designed to show visible signs of interference if opened or altered, indicating potential compromise.

Integrity Checks Upon Receipt

Perform physical and logical checks on hardware upon arrival to verify authenticity and detect any unauthorized modifications.

Key Takeaway

Hardware supply chain security requires a combination of strategic sourcing, physical controls, and rigorous verification processes.

Hardware Risk Mitigation Controls

1.Establish clear vendor security requirements
2.Require vendors to provide hardware SBoMs
3.Implement secure storage and handling procedures
4.Conduct regular audits of hardware suppliers

Misconception

Believing that tamper-evident packaging alone guarantees security; it's a detection mechanism, not a preventative one, and must be combined with other controls.
