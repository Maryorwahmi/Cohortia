IR Phase 1

Detecting and Scoping Supply Chain Incidents

Rapidly identifying and understanding the compromise.

Identification & Verification

Quickly determine if an incident is a supply chain compromise via vendor advisories, threat intelligence, or internal detection systems.

Impact Assessment

Determine the scope and impact of the compromise on the organization's systems, data, and operations to prioritize response.

Key Takeaway

Accurate and rapid identification, followed by a comprehensive impact assessment, forms the critical foundation for effective supply chain incident response.

Initial Response Steps

1.Verify authenticity of vendor advisories
2.Scan internal systems for Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
3.Identify all systems running affected software/hardware
4.Estimate potential data exposure and operational disruption

Common Mistake

Delaying internal action while waiting for a vendor to provide a complete solution, potentially increasing the window of exposure.
