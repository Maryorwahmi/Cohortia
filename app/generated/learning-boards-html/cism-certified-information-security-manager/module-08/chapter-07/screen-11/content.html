IR Phase 2

Coordinating Response and Containing Threats

Establishing clear protocols and limiting damage.

Communication Protocol

Establish clear communication channels and protocols with affected vendors, regulatory bodies, legal counsel, and internal stakeholders.

Containment & Eradication

Work with the vendor to contain the spread of the compromise and eradicate the threat, potentially involving system isolation or emergency patches.

Key Takeaway

Effective containment and eradication in a supply chain incident hinge on robust communication and close collaboration with the compromised vendor.

Containment & Communication Flow

1.Isolate affected systems immediately
2.Establish secure communication with vendor
3.Deploy verified patches or rollbacks
4.Communicate status to internal stakeholders & legal

Safety Note

Always verify the integrity of patches or updates from a compromised vendor before deployment to prevent further infection or re-infection.
