Core Concept

Understanding Supply Chain Vulnerabilities

Recognizing the unique challenges of external dependencies.

Beyond Internal Control

Supply chain vulnerabilities often originate from third-party vendors, manufacturers, and distributors, making direct control challenging.

Pervasive Risk Points

Vulnerabilities can exist at every stage: design, manufacturing, distribution, and maintenance of components and software.

Proactive Strategy Essential

Focus on gaining visibility into external dependencies and building resilience to withstand potential compromises.

Key Takeaway

Supply chain security demands extending your risk management beyond your organizational perimeter to encompass all external dependencies.

Supply Chain Vulnerability Lifecycle

Design & Development

Flaws introduced early

Manufacturing & Assembly

Hardware tampering, counterfeit parts

Distribution & Delivery

Tampering during transit

Deployment & Maintenance

Vulnerable updates, misconfigurations

Common Misconception

Assuming internal security measures are sufficient to protect against all supply chain risks. External dependencies require distinct assessment and mitigation strategies.
