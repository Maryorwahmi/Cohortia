Best Practices

Avoiding Common IR Mistakes

Preventing pitfalls in communication and verification.

Lack of Clear Communication

Delaying information to vendors or failing to receive timely, accurate updates from compromised suppliers can impede response.

Insufficient Coordination

Failure to establish clear roles, responsibilities, and protocols for managing external communications during an incident.

Blind Trust in Patches

Applying patches or updates from a compromised vendor without independent verification of their integrity can lead to re-infection.

Key Takeaway

Proactive planning for communication, coordination, and rigorous verification are essential to avoid common pitfalls during supply chain incidents.

Best Practices for Incident Communication

Pre-define communication templates
Maintain up-to-date vendor contact lists
Assign clear roles for external communications
Establish secure communication channels

Real-world Connection

The SolarWinds supply chain attack highlighted the critical need for organizations to verify all software updates, even from trusted vendors, before deployment.
