Essential Clauses

Data Protection & Privacy Clauses

Mandating secure handling of sensitive information.

Data Handling & Storage

Specify how data will be handled, stored, and encrypted.

Regulatory Compliance

Adherence to GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, and other relevant laws.

Encryption Standards

Mandate specific industry-standard encryption for data at rest and in transit.

Data Residency

Define where data can be stored and processed geographically.

Key Takeaway

Explicit data protection clauses are non-negotiable for compliance and mitigating legal and reputational risks.

CISM's Role in Data Clauses

  • Translate technical controls into legal language.
  • Ensure clauses are specific, measurable, and enforceable.
  • Collaborate with legal to embed robust provisions.

Example: GDPR Compliance

A contract might require a vendor to process EU citizen data only within the EU and implement specific pseudonymization techniques.
