Core Concept

Contracts as Foundational Security Controls

Formalizing security expectations and responsibilities with third parties.

  • Contracts translate due diligence into legally binding terms.
  • They formalize security expectations and allocate responsibilities.
  • Define recourse in case of security failures, protecting your organization.
  • Collaboration with legal and procurement is essential for robust clauses.
Key Takeaway

A well-defined contract is a critical control mechanism, extending your organization's security governance to third parties and mitigating risk.
