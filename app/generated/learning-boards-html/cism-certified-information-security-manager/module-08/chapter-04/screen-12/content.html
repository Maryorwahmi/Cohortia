CISM's Mandate

CISM's Role in Contractual Enforcement

Translating technical controls into legal obligations.

  • Bridge the gap between technical security and legal enforceability.
  • Provide specific language for robust security clauses.
  • Collaborate with legal and procurement for effective risk management.
Key Takeaway

Active CISM involvement ensures contracts align with security policies and regulatory mandates, making them effective security controls.

CISM's Impact on Contracts

Technical to Legal Translation

Example: Translating 'strong encryption' into 'AES-256 encryption for data at rest and in transit.'

Ensuring Accountability

By defining clear metrics and recourse, the CISM helps establish vendor accountability for security performance.

Consequence of Absence

Without CISM expertise, contracts may contain generic language, leaving the organization inadequately protected against third-party risks.
