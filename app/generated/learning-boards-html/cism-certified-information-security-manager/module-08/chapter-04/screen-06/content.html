Contractual Safeguards

Incident Response & Audit Rights

Ensuring timely action and verification of vendor security.

  • Mandate timely notification of security incidents.
  • Require cooperation in investigations and adherence to timelines.
  • Include clauses allowing independent security audits of the vendor.
Key Takeaway

Incident response and audit clauses provide transparency and verification, crucial for managing third-party security risks.

Key Clause Examples

Incident Notification Clause

"Vendor must notify Customer within 24 hours of detecting a security incident affecting Customer Data, including initial details and remediation steps."

Right to Audit Clause

"Customer reserves the right to conduct or commission annual security audits of Vendor's systems and processes relevant to the services provided, with reasonable notice."

Why it Matters

Without these, your organization could be unaware of breaches or unable to verify vendor security, increasing risk exposure.
