Negotiation Pitfalls

Pitfall 1: The Danger of Vague Language

Avoiding generic 'best effort' security terms in contracts.

  • Generic terms like "best efforts" or "appropriate security" are subjective and weak.
  • They provide limited concrete assurance or enforceability.
  • CISM must ensure terms are SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
Key Takeaway

Always advocate for specific, measurable security clauses to ensure enforceability and clear accountability.

Vague vs. SMART Clauses

Vague Clause Example

"The Vendor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Data."

SMART Clause Example

"The Vendor shall implement and maintain technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Data in accordance with ISO 27001 standards and provide annual SOC 2 Type 2 reports."

CISM's Role

Translate technical security controls into clear, enforceable contractual obligations for the legal team.
