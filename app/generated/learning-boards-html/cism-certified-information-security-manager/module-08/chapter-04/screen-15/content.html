Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

What to remember from this chapter

Right to Audit

A contractual provision allowing the organization to conduct security audits of its third-party vendors.

Incident Notification Clause

A contractual requirement for a third party to promptly notify the organization of security incidents.

Avoiding Vague Language

Insisting on SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) security terms in contracts.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive contract negotiation, clear audit rights, and specific incident notification clauses are essential for effective third-party security governance.
