Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

What to remember from this chapter

Contractual Agreements

Legally binding documents defining terms and information security responsibilities with third parties.

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A contractual agreement defining measurable security performance metrics and penalties for non-compliance.

Security Clauses

Specific provisions within a contract mandating security requirements, data protection, and incident response.

Summary Takeaway

Effective third-party risk management relies on robust contractual agreements, detailed security clauses, and measurable Service Level Agreements.
