Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Foundational Terms in Vendor Assessment

Due Diligence

Conducting investigations and assessments of a third party's security posture *before* entering a contract.

Vendor Assessment

Systematic evaluation of a third party's security controls, policies, and practices.

SIG Questionnaire

A widely used, comprehensive framework for assessing third-party information security risk.

Summary Takeaway

Effective third-party risk management begins with thorough pre-contractual due diligence and systematic vendor assessments using standardized tools.
