Gaining Assurance: Third-Party Audits and Certifications

Independent verification for a higher level of security confidence.

Why Audits & Certifications?

  • Provide independent auditor's opinion on controls.
  • Offer higher level of assurance than self-attestation.
  • Demonstrate adherence to international security standards.
  • Crucial for understanding control design and operating effectiveness.
Key Takeaway

Always request and thoroughly review the full audit report, not just the certificate, to understand the scope, limitations, and any exceptions.

Key Certifications & Reports

SOC 2 Report

Independent auditor's opinion on controls related to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.

  • Type 1: Design of controls at a specific point in time.
  • Type 2: Operating effectiveness of controls over a period.
ISO 27001 Certification

Indicates implementation of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) meeting international standards.
