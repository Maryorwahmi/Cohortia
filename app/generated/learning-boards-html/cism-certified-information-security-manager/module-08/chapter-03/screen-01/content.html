Third-Party Risk Management

Due Diligence and Vendor Assessment Frameworks

Establishing Trust and Security in External Partnerships

In today's interconnected business environment, relying on third-party vendors introduces significant risks. This chapter will equip you with the knowledge to establish trust and security in all your external partnerships, identifying potential security weaknesses before sensitive data is shared or systems are integrated.

Key Idea

Proactive risk mitigation involves identifying and addressing security weaknesses before sensitive data is shared or systems are integrated, making informed risk decisions.

In this lesson, you will explore:
Topic 1

Understand why assessing vendors *before* signing contracts is critical for risk mitigation.

Topic 2

Overview of various assessment methodologies: questionnaires, audits, and certifications.

Topic 3

Learn to tailor assessment efforts to vendor criticality using a risk-based tiering approach.
