Proactive Risk Mitigation: The Power of Due Diligence

Identifying and addressing security weaknesses before engagement.

What is Due Diligence?

Due diligence is the process of conducting reasonable investigations and assessments of a third party's security posture *before* entering into a contract or sharing sensitive information. It's the most impactful stage for mitigating risks.

  • Identifies potential security weaknesses early.
  • Allows for negotiation of stronger controls.
  • Provides opportunity to seek alternative vendors.
  • Enables informed risk acceptance with mitigation.
Key Takeaway

Pre-contractual due diligence is essential for gaining assurance and making informed risk decisions, protecting organizational assets and meeting regulatory obligations.
