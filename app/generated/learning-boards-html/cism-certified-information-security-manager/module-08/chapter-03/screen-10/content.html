Scenario Application

Case Study: Assessing a Smaller Niche Provider (Vendor B)

Tailoring assessment for less documented, specialized partners.

Vendor B Profile

  • Smaller, niche cloud provider specializing in your industry.
  • Less public security documentation.
  • Will process "Confidential" customer data.
Key Takeaway

For smaller providers, direct engagement through interviews and review of internal documentation becomes more critical to gain sufficient assurance.

Recommended Assessment Activities for Vendor B

01Comprehensive SIG Core Questionnaire

Essential for gathering specific, detailed control information across various domains.

02Request Internal Security Policies

Understand their operational security framework and procedures.

03Security Call/Interview

Direct engagement with their security team to clarify responses and assess security culture.

04Potential On-site Audit/Pen Test Review

If business impact is very high, targeted audit or review of penetration test results may be necessary.
