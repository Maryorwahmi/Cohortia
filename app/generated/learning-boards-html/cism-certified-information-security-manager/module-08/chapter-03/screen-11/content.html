Avoiding Pitfalls: Common Mistakes in Vendor Security Assessments

Ensuring thoroughness and independent verification in your evaluations.

Why Mistakes Happen

  • Pressure to onboard vendors quickly.
  • Lack of resources for thorough verification.
  • Over-reliance on vendor-provided information.
  • Misunderstanding the scope of audit reports.
Key Takeaway

Proactive verification, critical review, and a deep understanding of report limitations are essential to avoid significant residual risk in vendor engagements.

Key Mistakes to Avoid

Solely Relying on Self-Attestation

Accepting vendor claims without independent verification (e.g., audit reports, interviews) for high-risk engagements.

Accepting Questionnaires at Face Value

Not critically reviewing responses, asking follow-up questions, or requesting evidence for verification.

Ignoring Scope Limitations of Reports

Failing to understand what a report (e.g., SOC 2 Type 1) actually covers and its inherent limitations.
