Advanced Assessment

Deep Dive: On-site Audits and Penetration Testing

Uncovering real-world vulnerabilities for the most critical vendors.

When to Use Advanced Methods

  • For vendors handling highly sensitive or restricted data.
  • For critical services that could severely impact business operations.
  • When standard questionnaires and certifications provide insufficient assurance.
  • As part of a comprehensive, risk-based assessment strategy.
Key Takeaway

These methods are resource-intensive and should be reserved for the highest-risk vendors where maximum assurance is required.

Advanced Assessment Techniques

On-site Audits

Physical inspection of vendor facilities, interviews with personnel, and direct review of control implementation evidence.

  • Verify environmental and physical controls.
  • Assess security culture and operational practices.
Penetration Testing

Simulated attacks to identify real-world exploitable vulnerabilities in a vendor's systems, applications, or networks.

  • Provides real-world insights into security posture.
  • Requires careful scoping and contractual agreements.
