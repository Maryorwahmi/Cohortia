Technical Deep Dive

SOC 2 Reports: Understanding Type 1 vs. Type 2 Assurance

Distinguishing between control design and operational effectiveness.

Why This Distinction Matters

  • Impacts the level of assurance gained from a vendor.
  • Crucial for assessing ongoing security posture, not just a snapshot.
  • Directly influences risk decisions for sensitive data.
  • A Type 2 report is generally preferred for high-risk engagements.
Key Takeaway

For critical data, a SOC 2 Type 2 report is preferred as it provides stronger assurance of controls operating effectively over an extended period.

SOC 2 Report Comparison

SOC 2 Type 1

Attests to the suitability of the design of controls at a specific point in time.

  • Snapshot of control design.
  • No assurance of operating effectiveness.
SOC 2 Type 2

Attests to both design and operating effectiveness of controls over a period of time (e.g., 6-12 months).

  • Provides stronger assurance.
  • Verifies consistent control operation.
