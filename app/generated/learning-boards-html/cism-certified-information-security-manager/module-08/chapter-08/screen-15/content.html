Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms to solidify your understanding.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

The necessity for different departments (e.g., security, legal, procurement, business units) to work together effectively to manage third-party and supply chain risks.

Security Awareness Training

Educational programs designed to inform employees about information security risks, policies, and best practices, including those related to third parties.

Nth-Party Risk

Risks introduced by sub-contractors or sub-processors of your direct third-party vendors, extending the supply chain vulnerability.

Summary Takeaway

Effective TPRM and supply chain security rely on strong cross-functional collaboration and continuous security awareness training for all personnel, extending security vigilance throughout the entire ecosystem.
