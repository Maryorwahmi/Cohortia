Key Stakeholders: Business Units and IT Operations

Ensuring operational and technical alignment in third-party risk management.

Business Unit Owners' Role

  • Responsible for the business relationship with the vendor.
  • Understands the data being shared and its sensitivity.
  • Owns the residual business risk associated with the vendor.

IT Operations' Role

  • Involved in technical integration of third-party systems.
  • Responsible for ongoing monitoring and incident response.
  • Ensures secure and efficient operation of third-party systems.
Key Takeaway

Business Unit Owners manage vendor relationships and residual risk, while IT Operations handles technical integration, monitoring, and incident response for third-party systems.
