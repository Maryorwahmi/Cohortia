Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Information Security Program

The overall framework of policies, procedures, controls, and practices an organization uses to protect its information assets.

Risk Management Framework

A structured approach for identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring risks across an organization.

Governance Structure

The framework of rules, practices, and processes by which an organization is directed and controlled, including decision-making authority and accountability for security.

Summary Takeaway

Integrating TPRM and supply chain security into the Information Security Program, guided by a robust Risk Management Framework and clear Governance Structure, is paramount for holistic organizational defense.
