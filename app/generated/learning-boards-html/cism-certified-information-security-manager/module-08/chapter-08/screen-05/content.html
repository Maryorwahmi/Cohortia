Risk Management

Aligning with the Risk Management Framework

Integrating third-party risks into the enterprise risk register.

Key Steps for Alignment

  • Feed TPRM/supply chain risks directly into the enterprise risk register.
  • Prioritize third-party risks alongside internal risks based on impact and likelihood.
  • Ensure the organization's risk appetite applies consistently to third-party risks.
  • Allocate appropriate resources based on the holistic risk assessment.

Key Takeaway

Integrating third-party risks into the enterprise risk register ensures consistent prioritization and resource allocation aligned with the organization's risk appetite.
