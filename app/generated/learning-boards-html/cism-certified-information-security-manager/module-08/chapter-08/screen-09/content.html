Establishing a TPRM Governance Structure

Formalizing decision-making and accountability for third-party risks.

Benefits of Formal Governance

  • Provides strategic direction for the TPRM program.
  • Establishes clear decision-making processes.
  • Ensures accountability for managing third-party risks.
  • Facilitates cross-functional collaboration and executive buy-in.
