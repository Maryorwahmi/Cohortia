Human Factor

The Role of Security Awareness Training

Empowering all personnel to manage third-party risks effectively.

Why Training is Crucial

  • Ensures all employees understand their role in managing risks.
  • Covers topics like identifying suspicious communications and data classification.
  • Reinforces adherence to established TPRM processes.
  • Transforms employees into active participants in organizational defense.

Tailored Training for Key Roles

Procurement: Contractual Security
Business Units: Data Sharing Implications
IT Operations: Third-Party System Risks
Key Takeaway

Security awareness training is vital for all personnel, empowering them to understand their specific roles in managing third-party risks and fostering a collective security posture.
