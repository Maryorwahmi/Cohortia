Supply Chain Specific Policies

Addressing Nth-party risks and software integrity.

Key Elements of Supply Chain Security Policy

  • Addresses Nth-party risks (risks from sub-vendors).
  • Requires Software Bill of Materials (SBoM) for transparency.
  • Mandates secure development practices and software integrity.
  • Ensures visibility and verification of components throughout the supply chain.
