Building a Comprehensive Asset Inventory

A systematic approach to uncover all critical assets

1

Identify Assets

Locate all physical, logical, and intangible assets.

2

Document Details

Record type, location, owner, and associated information.

3

Collaborate Cross-Dept

Engage IT, Legal, Finance, HR to ensure completeness.

4

Review & Update

Regularly verify and update the inventory to reflect changes.

Why a Comprehensive Inventory Matters

  • Foundation of Security: You cannot protect what you don't know you have.
  • Risk Assessment Input: Essential for identifying potential vulnerabilities and threats.
  • Compliance Requirement: Many regulations mandate maintaining an accurate asset inventory.
  • Resource Allocation: Helps prioritize security investments based on asset criticality.
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive and regularly updated asset inventory provides the necessary visibility to protect what matters most, forming the bedrock of your security program.
