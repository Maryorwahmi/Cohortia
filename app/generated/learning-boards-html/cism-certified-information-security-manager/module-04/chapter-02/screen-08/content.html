Quantitative Asset Valuation

Calculating the tangible costs of compromise

Key Factors for Monetary Value

  • Replacement Cost: Expense to acquire or rebuild the asset.
  • Development Cost: Cost to create custom software or intellectual property.
  • Lost Revenue: Income loss due to downtime or service disruption.
  • Regulatory Fines: Penalties for non-compliance or data breaches.
  • Legal Liabilities: Costs associated with lawsuits or legal actions.
  • Recovery Costs: Expenses for data recovery, incident response, and remediation.

Example: Valuing a Customer Database

Data Re-collection Cost:

Effort and expense to gather customer data again.

Breach Fines & Penalties:

Regulatory fines (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) for data exposure.

Loss of Customer Lifetime Value:

Estimated revenue lost from customers who churn after a breach.

Key Takeaway

Quantitative valuation provides a clear financial justification for security investments by quantifying the tangible costs associated with asset compromise.
