Core Concept

What is an Information Asset?

An information asset is anything that has value to the organization and supports its operations. This includes physical, logical, and intangible elements.

Physical Assets

Servers, laptops, networking equipment, physical documents.

Logical Assets

Databases, applications, operating systems, intellectual property.

Intangible Assets

Reputation, brand image, customer trust, trade secrets.

Personnel Assets

Employees with unique knowledge and access.

Why a Broad Definition Matters

  • Comprehensive Protection: Ensures all valuable organizational elements are considered for security.
  • Risk Prioritization: Helps identify where security efforts will have the greatest impact.
  • Business Continuity: Protects not just IT infrastructure, but the core operations and future of the business.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Many regulations extend to all forms of data and intellectual property.
Key Takeaway

A holistic view of assets, including physical, logical, intangible, and personnel, is fundamental for effective information security risk management.
