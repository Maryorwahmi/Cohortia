Key Concepts (Continued)

Further essential takeaways from this chapter

Qualitative Valuation

Assessing an asset's value based on subjective criteria such as reputational damage, competitive advantage, and strategic importance.

Asset Owner

The individual or department formally accountable for the protection, use, and management of a specific information asset.

Data Sensitivity & Criticality

The degree to which information must be protected from unauthorized access, and its importance to ongoing operations.

Summary Takeaway

A complete asset management strategy integrates both subjective and objective valuation methods, clearly defines ownership, and considers both the sensitivity and criticality of all information.
