Qualitative Asset Valuation

Assessing intangible asset value beyond monetary figures

Key Factors for Intangible Value

  • Competitive Advantage: How an asset contributes to market differentiation.
  • Reputational Damage: Impact on public image and brand trust after an incident.
  • Customer Trust: Erosion of confidence leading to customer churn.
  • Strategic Importance: Vitality of the asset to long-term business goals and mission.
  • Legal/Ethical Obligations: Non-monetary costs of failing to meet responsibilities.

Example: Valuing Trade Secrets

Innovation Leadership:

Loss of unique market position and future product development.

Market Share Erosion:

Competitors gaining an unfair advantage, reducing revenue.

Partner Trust:

Damage to relationships with collaborators and investors.

Key Takeaway

Qualitative valuation captures the strategic and reputational importance of assets, providing a holistic view that complements monetary assessments.
