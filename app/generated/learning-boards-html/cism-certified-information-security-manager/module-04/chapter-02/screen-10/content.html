Applying Valuation: A Scenario

Combining quantitative and qualitative perspectives for informed decisions

Scenario: Stolen Proprietary Design Blueprints

A manufacturing company's unique product design blueprints are stolen, impacting its competitive edge and market position.

Key Takeaway

A holistic view of asset value, integrating both quantitative and qualitative factors, is essential for robust security strategies and effective communication with leadership.

Quantitative Impact

  • R&D Costs: Financial investment in developing the blueprints.
  • Lost Market Share: Estimated revenue loss due to competitor advantage.
  • Legal Fees: Costs for intellectual property litigation and defense.
  • Recovery & Remediation: Expenses to secure systems and recover from the breach.

Qualitative Impact

  • Reputational Damage: Erosion of public trust and brand image as an innovator.
  • Competitive Disadvantage: Loss of unique market position and future innovation.
  • Partner Trust: Damage to relationships with collaborators and suppliers.
  • Employee Morale: Negative impact on internal confidence and productivity.
