Understanding Asset Ownership

Ensuring clear responsibility for asset protection

What is an Asset Owner?

A clearly designated individual or department formally accountable for the protection, use, and management of a specific information asset.

Risk of No Ownership

Assets can become 'orphaned,' leading to neglected security controls and increased risk exposure.

Key Responsibilities of an Asset Owner

Classification Decisions:

Determining the appropriate sensitivity and criticality level.

Access Control:

Approving and reviewing who can access the asset.

Disposal Decisions:

Ensuring secure and compliant disposal when no longer needed.

Risk Management:

Acting as the primary point of contact for risk-related discussions.

Key Takeaway

Establishing clear asset ownership is fundamental for accountability, ensuring that security responsibilities are well-defined and managed effectively.
