Definition

What is Information Classification?

The process of categorizing information based on its sensitivity, criticality, and the potential impact of its unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Sensitivity

Degree of protection needed from unauthorized access.

Criticality

Importance to ongoing operations and mission.

Potential Impact

Consequences of compromise (disclosure, alteration, destruction).

Security Controls

Measures required based on classification level.

Why Classification is Essential

  • Resource Optimization: Directs security resources to where they are most needed.
  • Compliance: Helps meet regulatory and legal requirements for data protection.
  • Risk Mitigation: Ensures appropriate controls are in place to reduce potential impact.
  • Operational Efficiency: Prevents over-securing less critical data, avoiding unnecessary hurdles.
Key Takeaway

Information classification is the foundation for applying proportionate security controls, ensuring that sensitive and critical data receives the protection it truly requires.
