Core Concept

The Risk Register: A Central Repository

This dynamic document or database is the single source of truth for all identified risks, their characteristics, assessment results, and planned treatment actions.

Why it matters:
  • Provides a consolidated view of the organization's risk posture.
  • Enables informed decision-making and strategic planning.
  • Facilitates clear communication with stakeholders.
  • Ensures consistency, traceability, and accountability.
