Key Components

Essential Risk Register Attributes

Capturing specific details for each risk is crucial for effective management and communication.

Risk ID

Unique identifier for tracking.

Description

Clear statement of the risk.

Assets Affected

Information assets at risk.

Threats & Vulnerabilities

Exploitable weaknesses.

Likelihood & Impact

Probability and consequence.

Risk Level (Inherent/Residual)

Risk before and after controls.

Risk Owner

Accountable individual/dept.

Treatment Strategy & Plan

Actionable steps and approach.

Key Takeaway

A well-structured risk register requires specific, detailed attributes for each risk to ensure clarity, accountability, and effective management.
