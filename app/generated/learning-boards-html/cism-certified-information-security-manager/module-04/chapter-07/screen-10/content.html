Common Mistake

The 'Set It and Forget It' Fallacy

An outdated risk register provides a false sense of security and leads to misinformed decisions.

Consequences of Stagnation:
  • Inaccurate understanding of current risk posture.
  • Critical risks may go unaddressed.
  • Misallocation of security resources.
  • Increased vulnerability to evolving threats.
  • Failure to meet compliance requirements.
Key Takeaway

Regular review and continuous monitoring are essential to ensure the risk register remains a relevant and effective tool for managing the organization's dynamic risk landscape.
