Risk Reporting

Communicating Risk to Stakeholders

Translate complex risk data into clear, actionable insights for diverse audiences.

Executives/Board

Focus: Strategic impact, financial implications (ALE), overall risk trends. Use heat maps and dashboards.

High-Level Summary
Business Unit Managers

Focus: Operational impact, alignment with objectives, mitigation roles. Actionable insights for their domain.

Operational Relevance
Technical Teams

Focus: Detailed vulnerabilities, control specifics, actionable mitigation steps. Technical jargon is acceptable.

Detailed & Actionable
Common Mistake

Using the same generic report for all audiences. This leads to confusion, disengagement, and missed opportunities for action.

Avoid Generic Reports
Key Takeaway

Tailor your risk reports to the audience's needs, focusing on strategic impact for executives and technical details for operational teams.
