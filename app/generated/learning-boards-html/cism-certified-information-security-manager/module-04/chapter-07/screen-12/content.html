Assessment Idea

Scenario-Based Risk Reporting

Understanding how to present risk information effectively to different audiences.

Question:

A CISM is preparing a risk report for the Board of Directors. Which of the following elements would be most appropriate to include?

Options:
  • A) Detailed technical configurations of firewall rules.
  • B) A comprehensive list of all minor vulnerabilities.
  • C) A heat map of top 5 strategic risks, ALEs, and high-level mitigation strategies.
  • D) Raw output from the latest vulnerability scan.
Correct Answer & Explanation

C) The Board needs strategic, high-level information. A heat map showing top risks, their financial impact (ALE), and proposed strategies is ideal. Options A, B, and D are too technical for this audience.
