Assessment Idea

Consequences of an Outdated Risk Register

Understanding the primary danger of neglecting your risk register.

Question:

An organization's risk register hasn't been updated in over a year, with new systems and incidents occurring since. What is the primary risk?

Options:
  • A) Over-investing in security controls.
  • B) The register becoming too large and difficult to manage.
  • C) Inaccurate understanding of current risk posture, leading to misinformed decisions and unaddressed critical risks.
  • D) Difficulty finding historical data for future audits.
Correct Answer & Explanation

C) An outdated register fails to reflect current threats and controls, leading to a flawed understanding of risk and potentially leaving critical vulnerabilities unaddressed.
