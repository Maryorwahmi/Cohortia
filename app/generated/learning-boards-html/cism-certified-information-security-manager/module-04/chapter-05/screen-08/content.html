ISO/IEC 27005: Risk Management Guidelines

A systematic and adaptable international standard.

Core Aspects

  • •Part of ISO 27000 Family: Supports ISO 27001 for ISMS.
  • •Systematic Approach: Covers risk identification, analysis, evaluation, and treatment.
  • •Continuous Improvement: Emphasizes ongoing review and enhancement of risk management.
  • •Global Recognition: Widely adopted international standard.

Key Benefits

  • •Flexibility: Adaptable to various organizational contexts, sizes, and industries.
  • •Alignment: Helps align risk management with international best practices.
  • •Comprehensive Coverage: Addresses all stages of information security risk management.

Practical Application

Organizations use ISO 27005 to establish, implement, and maintain a robust information security risk management process that integrates seamlessly with their overall ISMS, ensuring consistent and effective risk handling.

Key Takeaway

ISO 27005 provides flexible, systematic guidelines for managing information security risk, supporting global best practices and continuous improvement.
