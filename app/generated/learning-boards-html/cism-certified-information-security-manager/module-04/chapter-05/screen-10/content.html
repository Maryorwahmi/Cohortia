FAIR: Factor Analysis of Information Risk

Quantifying information risk in financial terms.

Core Components of FAIR

  • •Loss Event Frequency (LEF): How often a loss event is expected to occur.
  • •Probable Loss Magnitude (PLM): The financial impact of a single loss event.
  • •Threat Event Frequency (TEF): How often a threat agent acts.
  • •Vulnerability (Vuln): The probability of a threat agent successfully impacting an asset.

Why FAIR is Powerful

  • •Financial Justification: Translates risk into clear ROI for security investments.
  • •Rigorous Analysis: Provides a defensible and transparent risk analysis.
  • •Data-Driven Decisions: Empowers executives with objective data for resource allocation.

Ideal Use Case

FAIR is particularly useful for organizations that need to justify significant security budgets to executive leadership by demonstrating the financial impact of risks and the ROI of proposed controls.

Key Takeaway

FAIR provides a rigorous, quantitative approach to measuring information risk in financial terms, essential for justifying security investments and strategic decision-making.
