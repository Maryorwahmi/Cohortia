Key Frameworks

OCTAVE: Operational Threat, Asset, and Vulnerability Evaluation

A self-directed, collaborative approach to critical assets.

Defining Characteristics

  • •Self-Directed: Organizations conduct their own assessments.
  • •Operational Focus: Emphasizes critical information assets and security practices.
  • •Collaborative: Involves workshops with operational staff.
  • •Tailored: Highly specific to the organization's context.

Benefits of OCTAVE

  • •Ownership: Fosters a sense of responsibility among staff.
  • •Relevance: Identifies risks from an operational perspective.
  • •Actionable: Leads to practical and relevant mitigation strategies.

Who Benefits Most?

Organizations that want a deep, internal understanding of their operational risks, leveraging the knowledge of their own staff, find OCTAVE particularly effective. It's ideal for tailoring security to specific business processes.

Key Takeaway

OCTAVE is a collaborative, self-directed methodology that provides a tailored, operational view of risks by engaging frontline staff.
