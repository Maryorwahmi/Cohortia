Core Concept

Assessing Risk: The Foundation of Security

Understanding how to evaluate threats and vulnerabilities.

The Risk Assessment Process

  • •Identify Assets: What needs protection (data, systems, people)?
  • •Identify Threats: What could cause harm (phishing, malware, natural disaster)?
  • •Identify Vulnerabilities: Weaknesses that threats can exploit (unpatched software, weak passwords).
  • •Evaluate Likelihood: How probable is a threat exploiting a vulnerability?
  • •Determine Impact: What would be the consequences if the risk materializes?

Why Methodology Matters

The chosen risk assessment methodology dictates how risks are analyzed, measured, and ultimately managed. It's a critical strategic decision that impacts resource allocation and security posture.
