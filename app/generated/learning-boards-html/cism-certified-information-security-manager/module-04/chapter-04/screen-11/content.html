CISM Action

Leveraging CVE and NVD for Remediation

Translating Vulnerability Data into Actionable Security Plans

1

Monitor & Identify

Continuously monitor CVE and NVD for new vulnerability announcements relevant to your organization's technology stack.

2

Assess & Prioritize

Utilize NVD's CVSS scores and impact ratings to assess the severity and potential risk of identified vulnerabilities.

3

Verify & Scope

Confirm if the vulnerability affects your organization's specific assets and determine the scope of affected systems.

4

Initiate Remediation

Based on prioritization, initiate patching, configuration changes, or other mitigation strategies to address the vulnerability.

Concrete Example

A new critical CVE for Apache HTTP Server is announced. A CISM checks NVD for its CVSS score (e.g., 9.8 Critical), confirms their organization uses an affected version, and immediately tasks the IT team to upgrade to the patched version.

Key Takeaway

Proactive monitoring and intelligent use of CVE/NVD data enable CISMs to make timely, risk-informed decisions for vulnerability remediation.
