Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Vulnerability Management

Penetration Testing

A simulated cyberattack to find exploitable vulnerabilities and demonstrate their impact.

CVE

A list of publicly known information security vulnerabilities, each with a unique identifier.

NVD

A U.S. government repository enriching CVE entries with additional information like CVSS scores.

Summary Takeaway

Penetration testing, CVE, and NVD are critical for actively validating security, referencing known flaws, and prioritizing remediation efforts effectively.
