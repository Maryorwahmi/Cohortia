Ongoing Security

Vulnerability Management as a Continuous Process

Adapting to an Ever-Evolving Threat Landscape

1. Identify

Discover new vulnerabilities through scans, reviews, and intelligence feeds.

2. Assess

Evaluate severity, likelihood, and business impact using CVSS and organizational context.

3. Remediate

Apply patches, reconfigure systems, or implement compensating controls.

4. Re-assess

Verify that remediation efforts were successful and no new vulnerabilities were introduced.

Key Takeaway

Vulnerability management is a continuous cycle of identification, assessment, remediation, and re-assessment, essential for adapting to evolving threats.

Why it Matters

New vulnerabilities are discovered daily, and systems constantly change. A static approach to security will inevitably lead to compromise.
