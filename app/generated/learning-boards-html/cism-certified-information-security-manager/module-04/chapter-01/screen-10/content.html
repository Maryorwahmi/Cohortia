Holistic Approach

Embracing a Holistic Risk Management Approach

Considering all facets of risk, beyond just technical vulnerabilities.

People-Related Risks

Includes social engineering, human error, and insider threats that exploit human trust or negligence.

Process-Related Risks

Weak or undefined security policies, lack of procedures, or poor change management practices.

Technology-Related Risks

Software vulnerabilities, hardware failures, network misconfigurations, and outdated systems.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive security requires addressing risks across people, processes, and technology, not just technical vulnerabilities.
