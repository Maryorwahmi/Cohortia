Risk Components

Understanding Impact and Likelihood

The two critical factors in assessing the severity of a risk.

Likelihood

The probability or frequency of a specific threat exploiting a particular vulnerability.

Impact

The magnitude of harm that could be caused by a risk event, including financial, reputational, or operational damage.

Key Takeaway

Assessing both likelihood and impact is essential for prioritizing risks and developing effective treatment strategies.
