Process Flow

The Information Security Risk Management Lifecycle

A continuous, iterative cycle for managing organizational risk.

1

Establish Context

Understand mission, objectives, and environment.

2

Identify Risks

Discover potential threats and vulnerabilities.

3

Analyze Risks

Determine likelihood and potential impact.

4

Evaluate Risks

Compare against risk criteria and prioritize.

5

Treat Risks

Implement controls to mitigate or transfer.

6

Monitor & Review

Continuously track and reassess risk posture.

Key Takeaway

Risk management is a continuous, adaptive cycle, not a one-time event, requiring constant monitoring and review.
