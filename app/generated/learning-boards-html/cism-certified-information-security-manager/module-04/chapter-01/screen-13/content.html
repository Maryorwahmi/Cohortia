Key Concepts: Risk Components & Lifecycle

Understanding the elements that define risk and its continuous management.

Vulnerability

A weakness in an asset or its protective controls that can be exploited by a threat.

Impact

The magnitude of harm that could be caused by a risk event, such as financial losses or reputational damage.

Likelihood

The probability or frequency of a specific threat exploiting a particular vulnerability.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management relies on understanding vulnerabilities, assessing potential impact and likelihood, and engaging in a continuous, adaptive lifecycle.
