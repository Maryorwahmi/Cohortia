Core Concept

Understanding Information Security Risk

Risk is the potential for loss or harm from a threat exploiting a vulnerability.

Asset

Anything of value to the organization, such as data, systems, or intellectual property.

Threat

Any potential cause of an unwanted incident that may result in harm, like a cyberattack or natural disaster.

Vulnerability

A weakness in an asset or its protective controls that can be exploited by a threat.

Key Takeaway

Risk is a function of likelihood and impact, stemming from threats exploiting vulnerabilities in valuable assets.
