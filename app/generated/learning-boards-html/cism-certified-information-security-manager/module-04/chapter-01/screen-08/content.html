Risk Terminology

Differentiating Inherent and Residual Risk

Understanding risk levels before and after controls are applied.

Inherent Risk

The level of risk before any security controls or mitigations are put in place. It's the raw risk.

Residual Risk

The level of risk that remains after security controls have been implemented. Some residual risk always exists.

Key Takeaway

The goal is to reduce inherent risk to an acceptable level of residual risk through effective controls.
