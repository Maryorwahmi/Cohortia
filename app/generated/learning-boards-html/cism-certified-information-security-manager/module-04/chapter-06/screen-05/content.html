Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)
Core Concept
Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)
Estimating the frequency of a threat event within a one-year period.
Expression

Expressed as a probability (e.g., 0.2 for once every 5 years, 1.0 for once per year).

Example Scenario
Threat Event
Database Corruption
Estimated Frequency
Once every 5 years
Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)
0.2
