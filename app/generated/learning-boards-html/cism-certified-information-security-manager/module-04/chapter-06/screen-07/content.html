Risk Prioritization
Prioritizing Risks Beyond ALE
Considering qualitative factors alongside quantitative metrics.
Key Factor

Regulatory & Legal Requirements: Non-compliance can lead to severe penalties, overriding ALE.

Key Factor

Business Impact Analysis (BIA): Risks affecting critical functions (low RTO/RPO) demand priority.

Example Scenario
Risk A
High ALE, low regulatory impact
Risk B
Moderate ALE, high GDPR violation potential
Prioritization Insight
Risk B might be prioritized higher due to severe legal and financial penalties, despite a lower ALE.
