The Pitfall of Relying Solely on Technical Scores
Why quantitative data needs qualitative context.
The Problem

Technical scores (e.g., CVSS) don't reflect business criticality or impact on core functions.

The Solution

Integrate technical assessments with BIA, regulatory needs, and risk appetite for holistic prioritization.

Scenario Comparison
Scenario A
Critical CVSS score on a non-critical dev server.
Scenario B
Medium CVSS score on a critical customer database.
Prioritization Insight
Scenario B is likely higher priority due to business impact, despite a lower technical score.
