Learning Objectives
What you will be able to do after this chapter
01
Calculate Risk Metrics

Estimate monetary losses per event (SLE), frequency of events (ARO), and total annual expected loss (ALE) using clear formulas.

02
Prioritize Risks

Apply techniques to rank risks by severity and business impact, ensuring focus on critical threats.

03
Integrate BIA Findings

Incorporate Business Impact Analysis data to understand how risks affect critical functions and recovery objectives.

04
Understand Limitations

Recognize the assumptions and constraints of quantitative risk calculations for informed application.
