Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Threat

A potential cause of an unwanted incident that may result in harm to a system or organization.

Threat Actor

An individual, group, or entity that is responsible for a threat, with specific motivations and capabilities.

Threat Intelligence

Information about current and emerging threats and threat actors, including their motivations, capabilities, and TTPs.

Summary Takeaway

A solid understanding of these foundational terms is crucial for any CISM to effectively identify, analyze, and respond to the dynamic threat landscape.
