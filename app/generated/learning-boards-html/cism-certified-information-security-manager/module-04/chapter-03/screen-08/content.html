Threat Intelligence

Collaborative and Internal Threat Insights

Harnessing Industry Sharing and Security Monitoring

ISACs/ISAOs

  • •Industry-specific sharing organizations.
  • •Facilitate sharing of threat information among members.
  • •Examples: FS-ISAC (financial services), E-ISAC (electricity).

Internal Security Monitoring

  • •Logs from SIEMs, IDS/IPS, firewalls, EDR systems.
  • •Reveals attack attempts or suspicious internal activities.
  • •Provides real-time visibility into your own environment.

Example Scenario

An ISAC alerts members about a new ransomware variant. Simultaneously, your SIEM detects unusual login attempts from an internal IP, indicating a potential insider threat or compromised account.

Key Takeaway

Industry collaboration through ISACs/ISAOs and internal security monitoring provide critical, tailored insights for a comprehensive and highly relevant threat landscape.
