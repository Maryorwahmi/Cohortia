Threat Analysis

Understanding Threat Actor Motivations and TTPs

Translating Intelligence into Actionable Defense Strategies

Key Elements of Analysis

  • •Motivations: Financial gain, espionage, hacktivism.
  • •Capabilities: Skill level, resources, tools.
  • •TTPs: Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures.

Example Scenario

A nation-state actor targets your industry using spear-phishing and custom malware. Knowing their TTPs allows you to strengthen email filters and deploy specific endpoint detection rules.

Key Takeaway

Analyzing threat actor motivations, capabilities, and TTPs enables the development of tailored and highly effective security defenses, moving beyond generic protection.
