Threat Classification

Categorizing Threats by Origin

Distinguishing Between Internal and External Sources

Internal Threats

  • •Originate from within the organization's trusted boundaries.
  • •Examples: Disgruntled employees, accidental insider errors (misconfigurations, data deletion).
  • •Often leverage legitimate access, making detection challenging.

Common Mistake

Underestimating the impact of internal, accidental threats, which often account for a significant percentage of security incidents.

External Threats

  • •Originate from outside the organization's control.
  • •Examples: Cybercriminals, nation-state actors, competitors, natural disasters.
  • •Require robust perimeter defenses and external intelligence.

Example Scenario

A phishing campaign targeting employees from an unknown external group. This is an external, human-induced threat requiring user training and email filtering.

Key Takeaway

Understanding whether a threat is internal or external helps tailor defense strategies and resource allocation, as internal threats often leverage legitimate access.
