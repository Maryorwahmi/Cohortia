Threat Intelligence

Leveraging Diverse Threat Intelligence Sources

Gathering Information on Current and Emerging Threats

Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)

  • •Publicly available information.
  • •Sources: News, security blogs, social media, dark web forums, government reports.
  • •Provides broad view of general threat trends.

Commercial Threat Intelligence Feeds

  • •Subscription-based, curated data from vendors.
  • •Content: Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), TTPs, emerging vulnerabilities.
  • •Provides real-time, actionable, and specific insights.

Example Scenario

Monitoring security blogs (OSINT) reveals a new phishing technique. A commercial feed then provides specific IOCs related to that technique, allowing for immediate firewall rule updates.

Key Takeaway

Proactive defense relies on timely and relevant threat intelligence, gathered from diverse sources like OSINT and commercial feeds, to anticipate and mitigate risks.
