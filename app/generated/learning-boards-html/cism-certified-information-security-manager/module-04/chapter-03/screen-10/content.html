Proactive Security

Introduction to Threat Modeling

A Structured Approach to Identifying System Vulnerabilities

What is Threat Modeling?

A structured approach to identify potential threats and vulnerabilities by analyzing the design of a system or application. It's a proactive exercise.

  • •Performed early in the development lifecycle.
  • •Helps design security controls in, not bolt on later.
Key Takeaway

Threat modeling is a proactive exercise that embeds security into system design by systematically identifying potential threats and vulnerabilities early in the development lifecycle.

Popular Methodologies

  • •STRIDE: Categorizes threats (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege).
  • •DREAD: Quantifies risk (Damage, Reproducibility, Exploitability, Affected users, Discoverability).
  • •PASTA: Seven-step, risk-centric methodology.
  • •OWASP Top 10: Good starting point for web application threat vectors.

Pro-Tip

Threat modeling is most effective when integrated early in the SDLC, shifting security 'left' to prevent costly fixes later.
