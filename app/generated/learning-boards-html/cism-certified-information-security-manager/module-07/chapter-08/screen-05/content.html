The PDCA Cycle: Plan and Do

Designing and Implementing Security Enhancements

1. Plan: Set Objectives & Design

  • Define clear improvement goals.
  • Identify areas for enhancement from audits, incidents, risk assessments.
  • Design new controls or processes to address gaps.

2. Do: Implement Changes

  • Execute the designed changes.
  • Prefer small-scale or controlled environments for initial rollout.
  • Gather initial feedback and observe immediate impacts.
Key Takeaway

The 'Plan' phase defines the 'what' and 'how,' while the 'Do' phase puts those plans into action, ideally with careful, controlled execution.

PDCA Cycle: Plan & Do

The initial steps of the continuous improvement cycle focus on strategic design and careful implementation of security enhancements.

Example: Patch Management

Plan: Identify critical systems needing patches, research updates, schedule downtime. Do: Apply patches to a test environment, then roll out to production during scheduled windows.
