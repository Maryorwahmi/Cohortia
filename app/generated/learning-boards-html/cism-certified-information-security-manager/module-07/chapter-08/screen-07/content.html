Leveraging Security Maturity Models for Assessment

Understanding Your Program's Current State and Potential

What are Maturity Models?

Structured frameworks to evaluate security capabilities across various domains (e.g., governance, risk management, incident response).

Assigning Maturity Levels

Programs are assigned levels (e.g., initial, repeatable, defined, managed, optimized) to indicate their current state of development and effectiveness.

CISM's Strategic Role

Use models to benchmark capabilities, identify gaps, develop roadmaps, and communicate progress to executive leadership effectively.

Key Takeaway

Maturity models provide a clear, objective way to assess security program strengths and weaknesses, guiding incremental improvements.

Maturity Model Benefits

Provides a roadmap for advancement, helps prioritize investments, and fosters a common understanding of security capabilities.

Common Mistake

Attempting to jump directly to the highest maturity level without building foundational capabilities, leading to unsustainable processes.
