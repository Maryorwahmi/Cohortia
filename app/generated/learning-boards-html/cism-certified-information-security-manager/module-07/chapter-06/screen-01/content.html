Security Audits, Reviews, and Testing

Validating Security Effectiveness and Compliance

This chapter equips you with the knowledge to establish and oversee critical assessment activities, providing objective evidence of control performance, identifying weaknesses, and driving continuous improvement in your security program. You'll learn to differentiate assessment types and implement proactive validation strategies.

Key Insight

Effective security isn't a one-time setup; it requires continuous validation through audits, reviews, and testing to adapt to new threats and ensure ongoing compliance.
