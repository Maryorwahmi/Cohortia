Implementing Continuous Monitoring

Beyond Annual Assessments for Proactive Security

What is Continuous Monitoring?

The ongoing process of collecting, analyzing, and reporting on security data to maintain an up-to-date security posture and identify issues proactively.

  • •Shift: From episodic to always-on vigilance.
  • •Goal: Faster identification and remediation of issues.
  • •Benefit: Reduced attack surface and improved resilience.
Key Takeaway

Continuous monitoring is the cornerstone of an adaptive security program, enabling rapid response to threats and maintaining compliance.

Key Activities

Automated Configuration Compliance

Tools continuously check systems against security baselines, flagging deviations in real-time.

Real-time Log Analysis

SIEM systems analyze logs from various sources to detect suspicious activities or threats as they occur.

Regular, Smaller-Scale Reviews

Integrating frequent, focused reviews into daily operations to catch issues early.

CISM's Advocacy Role

The CISM advocates for continuous monitoring, secures resources, and ensures findings lead to tangible improvements in the security posture, aligning with organizational risk management.
