Core Concept

The Extended Enterprise

Understanding the Scope of Third-Party Relationships

Organizations today rarely operate in isolation. They rely on a vast ecosystem of external entities for critical functions, extending their operational reach and introducing new security considerations.

  • •Cloud Service Providers: Hosting, SaaS applications, data storage.
  • •Vendors & Suppliers: Software, hardware, raw materials, office supplies.
  • •Contractors & Consultants: Specialized IT support, project work, temporary staff.
  • •Business Partners: Joint ventures, payment processors, marketing agencies.
Key Takeaway

Your organization's security perimeter effectively extends to every third party you engage with, making their security posture directly relevant to your own.
