Phase 4: Relationship Termination

Securely Ending Vendor Relationships

Mitigating Residual Risk Post-Termination Through Clear Exit Strategies

1. Define Exit Strategy

Clearly outline termination procedures in the contract, including data handling.

2. Secure Data Destruction

Mandate secure wiping of all organizational data from vendor systems.

3. Verification & Certification

Obtain a certificate of destruction as proof of secure data handling.

Key Takeaway

A robust exit strategy is as important as initial due diligence, ensuring that sensitive data does not become a lingering risk after a vendor relationship concludes.
