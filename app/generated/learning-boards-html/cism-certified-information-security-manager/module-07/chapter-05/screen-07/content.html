Phase 1: Due Diligence

Vendor Security Assessment

Thoroughly Vetting Potential Partners Before Engagement

Due diligence is the initial, critical step to evaluate a potential vendor's security posture, controls, and compliance certifications. This proactive assessment helps identify and mitigate risks early.

  • •Request Security Questionnaires: Detailed inquiries into their security practices.
  • •Review Audit Reports: Obtain SOC 2, ISO 27001, or other independent certifications.
  • •Evidence of Controls: Ask for proof of encryption, access management, incident response plans.
  • •Risk-Based Scrutiny: Adjust assessment depth based on data criticality and service impact.
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive due diligence process is the foundation for secure third-party relationships, ensuring that potential partners meet your organization's security standards.

Security Questionnaires

Detailed forms covering security policies, controls, and incident response.

Audit Reports & Certs

Independent verification like SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, or HIPAA attestations.

Data Classification

Understanding how the vendor classifies and protects the data they will handle.

Incident Response Plans

Reviewing their procedures for detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents.
