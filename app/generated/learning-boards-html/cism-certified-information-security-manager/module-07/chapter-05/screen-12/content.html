Proactive Measures

Advanced Monitoring Techniques

Leveraging Testing and Intelligence for Proactive Defense

Vulnerability Assessments & Pen Testing

Require vendors to provide results of their security testing or conduct your own (with permission) to identify weaknesses.

Threat Intelligence Sharing

Stay informed about security vulnerabilities or breaches affecting the vendor's industry or specific services to anticipate risks.

Key Takeaway

Proactive measures like security testing and threat intelligence are crucial for identifying and mitigating emerging risks within your third-party ecosystem, moving beyond reactive responses.
