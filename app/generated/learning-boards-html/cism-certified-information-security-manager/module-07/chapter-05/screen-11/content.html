Phase 3: Ongoing Monitoring

Continuous Vendor Oversight

Maintaining Security Throughout the Relationship Lifecycle

01

Regular Security Reviews

Conduct annual or semi-annual reviews of the vendor's security posture, including updated audit reports and control attestations to ensure continued compliance.

02

Performance Monitoring

Track security-related Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), such as uptime, incident response times, and security patch deployment.

03

On-site Audits

For high-risk vendors, consider conducting physical audits of their facilities and security operations, with contractual permission, to verify controls directly.

Key Takeaway

Ongoing monitoring is not optional; it's a continuous process of verification and adaptation, ensuring that third parties consistently meet your evolving security standards.
