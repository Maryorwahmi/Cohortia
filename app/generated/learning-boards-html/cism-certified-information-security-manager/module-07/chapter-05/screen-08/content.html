Beyond Self-Assessment

Verifying Vendor Claims and Tailoring Scrutiny to Actual Risk

Relying solely on a vendor's self-assessment is a common mistake. Independent verification and a risk-based approach are crucial for accurate security posture evaluation.

Common Mistake:

Over-reliance on vendor self-assessments without independent audits or evidence.

Best Practice:

Request independent audit reports (SOC 2, ISO 27001) and evidence of controls.

Key Takeaway

Always verify vendor security claims with objective evidence and tailor your scrutiny level based on the criticality of the service and sensitivity of the data involved.
