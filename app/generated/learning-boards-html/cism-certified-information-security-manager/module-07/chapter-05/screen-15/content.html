Key Concepts

Reinforcing Your Knowledge for Comprehensive Security

Contractual Security Clauses

Specific terms and conditions in a contract that define security requirements, responsibilities, and liabilities for third parties.

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A contract between a service provider and a customer that defines the level of service expected, often including security metrics and penalties.

Exit Strategy

A plan for securely terminating a third-party relationship, including data retrieval, secure destruction, and verification methods.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts are vital for managing the ongoing security and eventual termination of third-party relationships, ensuring continuous protection and compliance.
