Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember for Third-Party Security

Third-Party Security Management

The process of identifying, assessing, and mitigating security risks associated with external vendors, suppliers, and partners.

Due Diligence

The process of conducting a thorough investigation and evaluation of a potential vendor's security posture before entering into a contract.

Vendor Security Assessment

Evaluating a third-party's security controls, policies, and compliance against organizational standards and requirements.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts highlight the critical initial steps in establishing a secure foundation for any third-party relationship, emphasizing proactive risk identification and evaluation.
