Practical Application and Continuous Reinforcement

Testing Vigilance and Sustaining Awareness Through Diverse Methods

Simulated Phishing Campaigns

  • Crucial for testing employee vigilance in a controlled environment.
  • Provides immediate, targeted feedback and learning opportunities.
  • Highly effective in reinforcing training and changing behavior.

Continuous Reinforcement

  • Posters, newsletters, intranet articles for ongoing messaging.
  • Security Champions Programs for local advocacy.
  • 'Lunch and Learn' sessions for informal engagement.

Example: Phishing Simulation Feedback

After a simulated phishing click, an employee receives immediate, personalized micro-training on identifying red flags. This direct feedback loop is far more impactful than generic annual training, leading to faster behavioral correction.

Key Takeaway

Active testing through simulations and consistent, varied reinforcement are essential for embedding secure behaviors and maintaining a high level of security awareness over time.
