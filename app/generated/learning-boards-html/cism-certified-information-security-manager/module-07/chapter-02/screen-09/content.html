Policy and Portable Media Risks

Understanding Acceptable Use and Removable Media Dangers

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

  • Understanding permissible and prohibited use of company resources.
  • Ensuring actions align with security standards and legal requirements.
  • Preventing misuse and protecting company assets.

Removable Media Risks

  • Risks associated with USB drives and other portable storage.
  • Potential for malware introduction and unauthorized data exfiltration.
  • Importance of proper management and security for all portable devices.

Example: Unsanctioned USB Drive

An employee uses a personal USB drive found in the parking lot. This drive could contain malware that, once connected, compromises the company network, leading to a significant breach.

Key Takeaway

Clear AUPs and strict controls over removable media are vital to prevent data loss, malware infections, and unauthorized access, reinforcing a disciplined security posture.
