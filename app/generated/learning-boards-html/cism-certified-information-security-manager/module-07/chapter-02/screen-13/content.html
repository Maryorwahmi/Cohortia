Sustaining Engagement and Building Culture

Combating Training Fatigue and Championing Security as an Enabler

Challenge: Training Fatigue

Employees can become disengaged with repetitive or overly long training. This leads to reduced retention and a less effective security posture.

Solution: Varied, Engaging Content

Implement shorter, more frequent modules, micro-learning, and interactive elements. Make security personal and relevant to daily work and home life.

Security as an Enabler

Shift perception from security being a hindrance to an enabler. Emphasize that secure practices facilitate business operations and protect organizational value.

Example: Ethical Phishing Campaigns

Conducting simulated phishing with clear communication and educational follow-up, rather than shaming, fosters a learning environment and builds trust, reinforcing positive security behaviors.

Key Takeaway

Sustaining engagement requires varied, relevant content and a culture where security is seen as a shared responsibility and an enabler, not a burden, fostering proactive defense.
