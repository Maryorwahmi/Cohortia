Choosing Effective Training Approaches

Scalable vs. Interactive Learning: Computer-Based and Live Sessions

Computer-Based Training (CBT)

  • Interactive, often gamified, self-paced learning.
  • Highly scalable for foundational knowledge.
  • Good for broad reach and consistent messaging.

Live Instructor-Led Sessions

  • Effective for complex topics, discussions, and role-specific training.
  • Allows for direct Q&A and real-time clarification.
  • Fosters deeper engagement and tailored learning.

Example: Blended Approach

New hires complete a CBT module on general security policies. Later, their department attends a live session focused on secure handling of sensitive client data specific to their team's workflow, combining broad knowledge with targeted application.

Key Takeaway

Selecting the right delivery method, often a blend of CBT and live sessions, is crucial for optimizing learning outcomes and program effectiveness based on content and audience.
