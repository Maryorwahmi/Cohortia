Advanced Program Concepts

Key Elements for Effective Security Programs

Phishing Simulation

A controlled exercise to test employees' ability to identify and report phishing attempts, providing practical reinforcement.

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

A document outlining the rules and guidelines for using an organization's IT systems and resources, setting clear expectations.

Role-Based Training

Tailoring security training content and depth to the specific responsibilities and risk exposure of different employee roles.

Summary Takeaway

Implementing practical simulations, clear policies, and role-specific training are crucial for a dynamic and effective security awareness program.
