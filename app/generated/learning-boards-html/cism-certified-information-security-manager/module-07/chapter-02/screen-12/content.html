Assessing the Impact of Security Training

Beyond Completion Rates: Measuring Behavioral Change and Risk Reduction

Key Metrics for Behavior Change

  • **Phishing Click-Through Rates:** Reduction indicates improved vigilance.
  • **Incident Reporting Rates:** Increase can signify better awareness.
  • **Compliance with Policies:** Auditing adherence to clean desk or password policies.

Knowledge & Perception Metrics

  • **Quiz Scores:** Assessing knowledge retention after training modules.
  • **Employee Surveys:** Gauging perception and confidence in security practices.
  • **Qualitative Feedback:** Understanding cultural shifts and engagement levels.

Example: Phishing Rate Reduction

After implementing targeted phishing simulations and follow-up training, an organization observes a 50% reduction in click-through rates over six months. This directly demonstrates the program's effectiveness in changing employee behavior and reducing risk.

Key Takeaway

Effective measurement goes beyond completion rates, focusing on behavioral changes and risk reduction through a combination of quantitative and qualitative metrics to prove program value.
