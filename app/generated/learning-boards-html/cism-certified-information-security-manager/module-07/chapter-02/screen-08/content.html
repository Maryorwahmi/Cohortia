Extending Security to Mobile and Incidents

Securing Devices and Knowing When and How to Act

Mobile Device Security

  • Securing company data on personal and corporate mobile devices.
  • Best practices for strong passcodes, remote wipe, and app permissions.
  • Avoiding public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks.

Incident Reporting

  • Knowing who to contact and how to report a potential security incident.
  • Understanding that prompt reporting minimizes damage.
  • Recognizing even minor anomalies as potential indicators of a larger attack.

Example: Lost Company Laptop

An employee immediately reports a lost company laptop. This allows the IT team to remotely wipe the device, preventing unauthorized access to sensitive company data before it can be compromised.

Key Takeaway

Securing mobile devices and establishing clear incident reporting protocols are crucial for maintaining security posture in dynamic environments and enabling rapid response to threats.
