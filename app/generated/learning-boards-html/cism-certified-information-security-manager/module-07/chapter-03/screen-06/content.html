Phase 1: Proactive Preparation

Building Your Incident Response Foundation

  • 1

    Develop the IRP: Create a comprehensive Incident Response Plan, outlining procedures and policies.

  • 2

    Form & Train IRT: Establish a dedicated Incident Response Team and provide continuous training.

  • 3

    Acquire Tools: Procure necessary security tools like SIEM systems and forensic workstations.

  • 4

    Create Playbooks: Develop detailed, step-by-step guides for responding to specific incident types.

  • 5

    Define Communication: Establish clear channels and protocols for internal and external communication.

Common Mistake: Untested Plans

An IRP is only as good as its last test. Regular drills are non-negotiable.

Lack of Readiness

Without testing, teams may not know their roles or how to use tools effectively.

Hidden Flaws

Untested plans often contain procedural gaps or outdated information that only emerge during a real incident.

Key Takeaway

Preparation is the proactive phase of developing the IRP, forming and training the IRT, acquiring tools, and creating playbooks, with regular testing being crucial to avoid an untested plan.
