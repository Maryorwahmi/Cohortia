Critical Incident Response Terminology

Key Actions and Processes

Containment

Actions taken to limit the scope and impact of a security incident.

Eradication

The process of removing the root cause of a security incident.

Post-Incident Review

A formal process to analyze an incident, identify areas for improvement, and update plans and controls.

Playbook

A detailed, step-by-step guide for responding to specific types of security incidents.

Summary Takeaway

Containment, Eradication, Post-Incident Review, and Playbooks are key action-oriented terms defining critical steps in incident response.
