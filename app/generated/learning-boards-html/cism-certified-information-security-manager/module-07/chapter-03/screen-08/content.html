Phase 3: Containing the Threat

Limiting the Scope and Preventing Further Damage

  • 1

    Isolate Systems: Disconnect affected devices or segments from the network to halt spread.

  • 2

    Block Malicious IPs: Update firewalls or network access controls to prevent communication with threat actors.

  • 3

    Disable Accounts: Suspend or reset credentials for compromised user or service accounts.

  • 4

    Take Systems Offline: As a last resort, power down critical systems to prevent further compromise.

Example: Ransomware Containment

A practical scenario for limiting the spread of a critical threat.

Infected Server Detected

A critical server is found to be infected with ransomware, encrypting files.

Network Isolation

The server is immediately isolated from the network to prevent the ransomware from spreading to other systems.

Key Takeaway

Containment focuses on limiting the scope and preventing further damage from a confirmed incident by isolating systems, blocking malicious traffic, or disabling compromised accounts.
