Phase 6: Post-Incident Review and Lessons Learned

Driving Continuous Improvement

  • 1

    Thorough Review: Analyze the entire incident, from detection to recovery, to understand what occurred.

  • 2

    Document Incident: Create detailed records of the incident, actions taken, and outcomes.

  • 3

    Update Policies & Procedures: Revise IRP, security policies, and operational procedures based on new insights.

  • 4

    Enhance Security Controls: Implement new or improved technical and administrative controls to prevent recurrence.

  • 5

    Refine Training: Update IRT training and awareness programs to address identified weaknesses.

Example: Firewall Rule Gap

How a review leads to tangible security improvements.

Incident Reveals Weakness

An incident successfully exploited a previously unknown gap in the organization's firewall rules.

Policy & Control Update

The post-incident review leads to immediate updates to firewall policies and a review of network segmentation.

Key Takeaway

Post-Incident Activity involves a thorough review of the incident to identify lessons learned, update policies, enhance controls, and refine the IRP and training, driving continuous improvement in security posture.
