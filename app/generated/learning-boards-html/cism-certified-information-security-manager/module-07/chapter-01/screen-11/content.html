The Balanced Scorecard for Security Reporting

A Holistic View of Security Performance Across Business Perspectives

Financial Perspective

Measures the economic impact of security, such as cost of incidents or ROI of security investments.

Example: Annual cost savings from prevented cyberattacks.

Customer Perspective

Assesses user satisfaction with security controls and security's impact on business operations and customer trust.

Example: User feedback on security control usability.

Internal Business Processes

Evaluates the efficiency and effectiveness of internal security processes, such as incident response and vulnerability management.

Example: Mean time to detect and respond to security incidents.

Learning & Growth Perspective

Focuses on the human capital and innovation aspects of security, including training, awareness, and staff retention.

Example: Employee security awareness training completion rates.

Key Takeaway

The Balanced Scorecard provides a comprehensive framework to present security performance, aligning it with business value across financial, customer, internal, and learning perspectives.
