KPIs vs. KRIs

Measuring Success and Warning of Impending Risk

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Metrics indicating how well an organization is achieving its key business and security objectives. Focus on past performance.

  • •Example: Percentage of critical vulnerabilities patched within 30 days.
  • •Purpose: Measure success against goals.

Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)

Metrics providing an early warning signal of increasing risk exposure, allowing for proactive mitigation. Focus on future risk.

  • •Example: Number of suspicious data access attempts by privileged users.
  • •Purpose: Predict potential problems.
Key Takeaway

KPIs measure past performance against objectives, while KRIs provide early warnings of increasing risk, enabling proactive mitigation.

KPIs: Performance Measurement

Goal: 95% Patching Compliance

Actual: 92% (Last Quarter)

Measures how well you achieved a security objective.

KRIs: Risk Warning

Threshold: 5 Suspicious Logins

Current: 8 (This Week)

Provides an early signal of increasing risk exposure.
