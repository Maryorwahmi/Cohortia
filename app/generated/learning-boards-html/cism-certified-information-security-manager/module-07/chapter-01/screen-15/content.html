Key Concepts

Understanding Performance Monitoring Frameworks

Balanced Scorecard

A strategic performance management framework providing a holistic view of security performance across multiple perspectives.

Operational Metrics

Day-to-day measures of security control efficiency and activity, vital for internal team management.

Strategic Metrics

High-level measures linking security performance to business objectives and risk tolerance, typically for executive reporting.

Summary Takeaway

The Balanced Scorecard provides a holistic reporting framework, while Operational and Strategic Metrics offer different levels of insight into security performance and business alignment.
