Metric Type

Strategic Metrics: Business Alignment

Linking Security Performance to Business Objectives and Risk Tolerance

Definition

High-level measures designed for executive consumption, linking security performance directly to business objectives and risk appetite.

Cost of security incidents per quarter.

Percentage of compliance with regulatory requirements.

Overall risk posture of the organization.

Key Takeaway

Strategic metrics translate technical security performance into business language, aligning with organizational objectives and risk tolerance for executive decision-making.

Security's Business Impact

Business Objectives

Revenue growth, market share, customer trust.

Strategic Metrics

Cost of incidents, compliance rate, risk posture.

Executive Decisions

Resource allocation, risk acceptance, strategic planning.

Risk Tolerance

Acceptable level of risk for the organization.
