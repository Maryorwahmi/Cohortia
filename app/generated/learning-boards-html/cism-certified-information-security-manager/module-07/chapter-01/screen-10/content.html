Program Implementation

Implementing and Refining Your Metrics Program

Baselines, Data Collection, and Continuous Improvement

Step 3

Establish Baselines & Targets

Set a starting point (baseline) to measure against and define desired future states (targets) for improvement.

Example: Baseline patching rate: 60%; Target: 90% within 12 months.

Step 4

Data Collection & Reporting

Determine how data will be gathered, analyzed, and presented. Leverage automation for consistency and accuracy.

Example: Use SIEM systems, vulnerability scanners, and automated dashboards for reporting.

Step 5

Review & Refine

Regularly assess the metrics program's effectiveness and adapt it as the threat landscape and business priorities evolve.

Example: Quarterly review of metrics relevance and reporting frequency with stakeholders.

Key Takeaway

Effective metrics program implementation requires establishing benchmarks, automating data collection, and continuously refining metrics to adapt to evolving threats and business needs.
