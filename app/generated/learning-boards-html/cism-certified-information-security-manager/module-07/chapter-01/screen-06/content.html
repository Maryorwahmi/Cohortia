Tactical Metrics: Trends Over Time

Aggregating Data to Reveal Short-Term Trends and Performance

Definition

Metrics that aggregate operational data to show trends over weeks or months, providing a broader view of security performance.

Trend of successful phishing attacks over a quarter.

Reduction in critical vulnerabilities over six months.

Effectiveness of a new security tool's detection rate.

Key Takeaway

Tactical metrics aggregate operational data to reveal short-to-medium term trends, helping security managers adjust strategies and demonstrate progress.
