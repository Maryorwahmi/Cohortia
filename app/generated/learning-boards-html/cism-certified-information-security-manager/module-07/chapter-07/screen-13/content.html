More Key Concepts to Remember

Essential definitions from this chapter

HIPAA

U.S. law protecting the privacy and security of patient health information (PHI).

PCI DSS

A set of security standards for organizations that handle credit card information.

Data Breach Notification Laws

Legal requirements to inform affected individuals and/or regulatory bodies about security breaches.

Chapter Summary

These industry-specific regulations and breach notification requirements are crucial for protecting sensitive data and managing the legal aftermath of security incidents effectively.
