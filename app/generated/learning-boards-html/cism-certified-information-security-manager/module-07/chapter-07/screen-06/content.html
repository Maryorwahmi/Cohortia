Industry-Specific Mandates

HIPAA: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act

A U.S. law protecting the privacy and security of Protected Health Information (PHI).

  • Applies to healthcare providers, plans, and clearinghouses.
  • Mandates administrative, physical, and technical safeguards.
  • Ensures patient data confidentiality and integrity.
  • Example: Encrypting electronic health records to prevent unauthorized access.
Real-World Connection

A hospital's IT department must ensure all systems handling patient data are configured to meet HIPAA's stringent security requirements, including audit trails and access controls.

Industry-Specific Mandates

PCI DSS: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard

A set of security standards for organizations that handle branded credit cards.

  • Mandatory for all entities processing, storing, or transmitting cardholder data.
  • Includes requirements for network security, vulnerability management, and access control.
  • Aims to reduce credit card fraud.
  • Example: An e-commerce site must secure its payment gateway and customer databases.
Key Takeaway

Ignoring industry-specific regulations like HIPAA and PCI DSS can lead to severe legal penalties, loss of operational capabilities, and significant reputational damage.
