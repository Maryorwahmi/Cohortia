Governance & Frameworks

SOX: Sarbanes-Oxley Act

U.S. federal law mandating practices in financial record keeping and reporting for public companies.

  • Impacts IT controls over financial data.
  • Requires internal controls for financial reporting accuracy.
  • Aims to prevent corporate accounting scandals.
  • Example: Ensuring segregation of duties in financial systems.
Pro-Tip

While SOX is not a direct security law, its requirements for internal controls heavily influence IT security practices, especially for data integrity and access management.

Governance & Frameworks

NIST CSF & ISO/IEC 27001

Voluntary frameworks providing guidance on managing cybersecurity risk (NIST CSF) and a systematic approach to ISMS (ISO/IEC 27001).

  • NIST CSF: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
  • ISO 27001: International standard for Information Security Management Systems.
  • Both promote a risk-based approach to security.
  • Example: Using NIST CSF to assess current cybersecurity posture and ISO 27001 for formal certification.
Key Takeaway

Leveraging frameworks like NIST CSF and ISO/IEC 27001 provides a structured approach to managing cybersecurity risk and demonstrating commitment to information security.
