Key Concepts to Remember

Essential definitions from this chapter

Regulatory Compliance

Adherence to laws, regulations, and industry standards related to information security and data privacy.

GDPR

EU law on data protection and privacy for individuals within the European Union, with global impact.

CCPA

California state statute granting consumers new rights regarding their personal information.

Chapter Summary

Understanding these foundational regulations is crucial for establishing a compliant and secure information environment, mitigating legal risks, and protecting stakeholder trust.
