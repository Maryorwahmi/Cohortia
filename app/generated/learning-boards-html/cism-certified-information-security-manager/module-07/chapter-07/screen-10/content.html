Data Protection

Key Privacy Management Controls

Implementing specific controls ensures ethical and legal handling of personal data throughout its lifecycle.

  • Data Classification: Categorizing data by sensitivity.
  • Data Minimization: Collecting only essential data.
  • Data Retention: Defining how long data is kept.
  • Secure Data Disposal: Irreversibly removing data.
  • Individual Rights: Processes for access, correction, deletion requests.
Collaboration Point

CISMs often work closely with a Data Protection Officer (DPO) or privacy officer to ensure these requirements are met.
