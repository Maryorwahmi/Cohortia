Data Privacy Focus

GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation

A comprehensive EU law protecting personal data of EU residents, regardless of where the data processing occurs.

  • Mandates strict data protection principles.
  • Grants individuals rights (access, erasure).
  • Requires breach notification within 72 hours.
  • Example: A US company selling products to EU customers must comply.
Common Misconception

Compliance with one data privacy law (e.g., GDPR) does not automatically guarantee compliance with others (e.g., CCPA). Each requires specific attention.

Data Privacy Focus

CCPA/CPRA: California Consumer Privacy Act

California law granting consumers specific rights over their personal information, similar to GDPR but for California residents.

  • Grants rights to know, delete, and opt-out of data sale.
  • Applies to businesses meeting certain thresholds.
  • CPRA expanded these rights and established a dedicated enforcement agency.
  • Example: A social media company with California users must provide data access.
Key Takeaway

Organizations must meticulously identify and address the specific requirements of all applicable data privacy regulations to avoid severe penalties and maintain consumer trust.
