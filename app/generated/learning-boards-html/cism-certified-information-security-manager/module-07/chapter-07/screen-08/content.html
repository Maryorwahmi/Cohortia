Developing a Robust Compliance Framework

A systematic approach to meeting legal and regulatory obligations

01Identify Applicable Mandates

Collaborate with legal and business units to list all relevant laws, regulations, and standards.

02Conduct Gap Analysis

Compare current controls against requirements to identify areas of non-compliance.

03Develop Remediation Plan

Outline actions to address gaps, prioritizing based on risk and criticality.

04Implement Controls & Processes

Update policies, deploy new tech, and train staff to address identified gaps.

05Monitor & Report Continuously

Establish mechanisms for ongoing compliance demonstration and adaptation to changes.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined and continuously managed compliance framework is essential for proactive risk management and demonstrating due diligence to regulators and stakeholders.
