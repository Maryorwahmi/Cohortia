Core Concept

The CISM's Role in Regulatory Compliance

The CISM acts as a crucial bridge, ensuring information security practices align with legal and regulatory requirements.

  • Translates legal mandates into actionable security controls.
  • Collaborates with legal and business units to identify applicable regulations.
  • Manages risks associated with non-compliance.
  • Communicates compliance status and risks to executive leadership.
Key Responsibility

Ensure the organization operates within legal boundaries while protecting assets and stakeholders.

Key Takeaway

Compliance is not just a legal issue; it's a fundamental aspect of information security management, directly impacting an organization's resilience and trustworthiness.
