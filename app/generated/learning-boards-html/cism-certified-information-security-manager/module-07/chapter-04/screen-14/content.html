Why Test?

Validate Viability

An untested plan is a theoretical plan. Regular testing ensures the plan will function as expected during a real disaster.

  • Identifies weaknesses and gaps in procedures.
  • Ensures personnel are trained and familiar with their roles.
  • Validates recovery objectives (RTO/RPO) are achievable.
Key Takeaway

Continuous testing and maintenance are vital for transforming theoretical plans into actionable, reliable strategies for organizational resilience.

Testing Methods & Pitfalls

From Tabletop to Full-Scale

Tabletop Exercises

Team members walk through the plan mentally, discussing steps and roles.

Full-Scale Simulations

Actual failovers and recoveries are performed in a controlled environment.

Common Mistakes & Safety Notes

  • Not involving key personnel or testing end-to-end.
  • Failing to update plans after changes.
  • Inadvertently disrupting live production systems (always test in isolated environments).
