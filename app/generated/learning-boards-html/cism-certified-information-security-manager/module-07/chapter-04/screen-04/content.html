Core Concept

Large-Scale Disruptions

DR and BCP address events that impact entire operations, unlike incident response which focuses on specific security events.

  • Natural disasters (e.g., floods, earthquakes)
  • Widespread power outages affecting regions
  • Major cyberattacks crippling critical infrastructure
  • Pandemics impacting workforce availability
Key Takeaway

DR and BCP are strategic plans for organizational resilience, ensuring critical functions continue or are rapidly restored during catastrophic events.

Impact & Objective

Minimizing Damage, Maximizing Continuity

Financial Damage

Loss of revenue, recovery costs, regulatory fines.

Reputational Harm

Loss of customer trust, brand erosion.

Legal & Compliance

Breach of contracts, regulatory non-compliance.

Operational Disruption

Inability to deliver products/services.

Example: Online Banking Platform

A major regional power outage occurs. The BCP ensures customer transactions can still be processed, perhaps through a secondary data center or manual workarounds, minimizing service interruption and maintaining customer trust.
