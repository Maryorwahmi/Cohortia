Module 7›Chapter 7.4›Disaster Recovery Sites: Hot & Warm
Hot Site

Immediate Failover

A fully equipped, mirrored data center that can take over operations almost immediately after a disaster.

  • Very low RTO/RPO (minutes to seconds).
  • High cost due to active redundancy.
  • Ideal for mission-critical systems.

Example: Financial Trading Platform

A trading platform uses a hot site to ensure continuous operation with virtually no downtime or data loss, crucial for real-time market transactions.

Warm Site

Partial Readiness

A partially equipped data center with hardware, but requiring some configuration and data restoration to become operational.

  • Moderate RTO/RPO (hours to a day).
  • Moderate cost, less expensive than a hot site.
  • Suitable for less critical systems that can tolerate some downtime.

Example: Internal HR System

An internal HR system might use a warm site, where servers are ready but the latest employee data backup needs to be loaded and applications configured, taking a few hours.

Key Takeaway

Hot and warm sites offer different levels of readiness and cost, allowing organizations to tailor their DR strategy to the specific RTO/RPO needs of each system.
