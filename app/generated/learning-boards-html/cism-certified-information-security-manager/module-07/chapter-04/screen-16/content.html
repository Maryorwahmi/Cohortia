Key Concepts: Recovery Objectives & Sites

Revisiting the metrics and infrastructure for effective disaster recovery.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum acceptable duration of time a system can be down after a disaster.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum acceptable amount of data that can be lost from an IT service.

Hot Site

A fully equipped, mirrored data center for immediate failover with very low RTO/RPO.

Summary Takeaway

RTO and RPO are critical metrics that define recovery targets, guiding the selection of DR strategies and infrastructure like hot sites to meet business needs.
