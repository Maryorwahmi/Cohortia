Step 4

Strategy Development

Determine recovery strategies for people (e.g., alternative work sites), processes (e.g., manual workarounds), and technology (e.g., DR sites).

Example: Implementing a remote work policy for all employees during a facility outage.

Step 5

Plan Development

Document detailed procedures for plan activation, incident response, and recovery. These must be clear, concise, and actionable.

Example: Step-by-step guide for IT team to failover to a secondary data center.

Step 6

Testing and Maintenance

Regularly validate and update the plan through exercises (tabletop, simulations) to ensure its viability and effectiveness over time.

Example: Annual full-scale simulation of a data center outage to test recovery procedures.

Key Takeaway

Effective BCP requires not just planning, but also detailed execution procedures and a commitment to continuous testing and improvement.
