Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

Maintaining Business Functions

The overarching strategy to maintain or quickly resume critical business functions after any disruption.

  • Encompasses people, processes, technology, facilities, suppliers.
  • Asks: "How do we keep the business running, no matter what?"
  • Focuses on the entire organization's operational resilience.

Example: Workforce Dispersal

A BCP might detail how employees can work from home or an alternate office if the primary building is inaccessible due to a fire.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Restoring IT Infrastructure

A subset of BCP specifically focused on the recovery of IT infrastructure and systems after a technical disaster.

  • Addresses data, applications, hardware, network connectivity.
  • Asks: "How do we restore our IT systems and data?"
  • Focuses on technical recovery and data integrity.

Example: Data Center Failover

A DR plan would detail the steps to failover critical applications and data to a secondary data center after a primary data center failure.

Key Takeaway

BCP provides the strategic framework for overall business resilience, while DR provides the tactical steps for IT system recovery, making them interdependent for comprehensive protection.
