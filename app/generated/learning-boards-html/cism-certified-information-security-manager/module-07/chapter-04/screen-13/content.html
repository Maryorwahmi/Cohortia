Cold Site

Minimal Readiness

A basic facility with power and connectivity, requiring hardware and data to be brought in and set up.

  • High RTO/RPO (days to weeks).
  • Low cost, but significant recovery effort.
  • Suitable for non-critical systems or long-term archives.

Example: Archival Data Storage

A company stores infrequently accessed archival data in a cold site, accepting that recovery could take several days to procure and install hardware.

Virtualization & Replication

Efficient Recovery

Using virtual machines and real-time data replication to quickly recover services on alternate hardware.

  • Very low RTO/RPO (minutes to hours).
  • High flexibility and scalability.
  • Often integrated with cloud-based DR solutions.

Example: Critical Application Servers

Virtual servers running a critical application are continuously replicated to a secondary host, allowing for instant failover if the primary host fails.

Key Takeaway

From basic cold sites to advanced virtualization, DR strategies offer a spectrum of cost and recovery capabilities, requiring careful selection based on business criticality.
