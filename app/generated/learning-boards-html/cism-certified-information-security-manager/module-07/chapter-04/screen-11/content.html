Strategy 1

Backup and Restore

The simplest method, relying on regular backups of data and systems. Data is restored to new hardware after a disruption.

  • Suitable for higher RTO/RPO requirements.
  • Lower cost, but longer recovery times.
  • Requires robust backup management and storage.

Example: Archival Database

A non-critical archival database with a 24-hour RPO and RPO can be recovered using daily backups, accepting a full day's data loss and recovery time.

Strategy 2

Cloud-Based DR

Leveraging public cloud providers for replication and recovery, offering flexibility and scalability.

  • Flexible RTO/RPO depending on cloud services used.
  • Reduces capital expenditure on physical sites.
  • Scalable and geographically diverse recovery options.

Example: E-commerce Platform

An e-commerce platform replicates its production environment to a different cloud region, enabling failover within minutes to meet low RTO/RPO.

Key Takeaway

Choosing the right DR strategy involves balancing RTO/RPO requirements with budget and complexity, with cloud solutions offering a modern, scalable alternative to traditional backups.
