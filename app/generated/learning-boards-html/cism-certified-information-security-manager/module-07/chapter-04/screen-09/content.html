Module 7›Chapter 7.4›Components of a Comprehensive BCP: Part 1
Step 1

Scope and Objectives

Clearly define what the plan covers (e.g., specific departments, systems) and its overarching goals (e.g., minimize financial loss, ensure regulatory compliance).

Example: Plan covers all customer-facing services with a goal to maintain 99% availability.

Step 2

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identify critical functions, assess the impact of their unavailability, and determine Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD), RTO, and RPO.

Example: BIA reveals order processing has an MTD of 4 hours, requiring a 4-hour RTO.

Step 3

Risk Assessment

Identify potential threats (e.g., cyberattacks, natural disasters) and vulnerabilities (e.g., single points of failure) to business continuity.

Example: Identifying a regional power grid as a single point of failure for primary data center.

Key Takeaway

A robust BCP begins with clearly defined goals, a data-driven understanding of business impacts, and a proactive assessment of potential risks.
