Definition

What is BIA?

The process of identifying critical business functions, assessing the impact of their unavailability, and determining Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD).

  • Identifies core processes essential for operations.
  • Quantifies potential losses (financial, reputational, legal).
  • Establishes MTD as a key metric for recovery goals.
Key Takeaway

The BIA is the essential first step, providing data-driven insights to prioritize and justify investments in BCP and DR strategies.

BIA Process & Outputs

From Impact to Objectives

1. Identify Critical Functions

What operations are vital for the business?

2. Assess Impact of Unavailability

Financial, reputational, legal, operational consequences.

3. Determine Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)

Longest acceptable outage before unacceptable harm.

4. Derive RTO & RPO

Specific IT recovery targets based on MTD.

Example: E-commerce Platform

A BIA for an e-commerce site might reveal that 1 hour of downtime costs $50,000 in lost sales and customer trust. This directly informs the MTD and subsequent recovery objectives.
