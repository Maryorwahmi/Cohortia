Assurance Frameworks and Standards

Guiding Principles for Robust Information Security

In this chapter, you'll learn how established frameworks like ISO 27001, NIST CSF, and SOC 2 provide a structured approach to managing information security risks, demonstrating due diligence, and building trust. As a CISM, you'll be instrumental in selecting, implementing, and maintaining these frameworks to achieve organizational security and compliance goals.
