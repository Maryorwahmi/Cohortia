Third-Party Assurance

SOC 2 Reports: Trust and Transparency for Service Providers

Independent Verification of Controls for Customer Confidence

What is SOC 2?

Assurance reports issued by a CPA firm, focusing on a service organization's controls relevant to the Trust Services Criteria (TSC).

Trust Services Criteria (TSC)
  • Security
  • Availability
  • Processing Integrity
  • Confidentiality
  • Privacy
SOC 2 Report Types

Type 1 Report

  • Describes system and suitability of control design.
  • At a specific point in time (snapshot).
  • Focuses on design effectiveness.

Type 2 Report

  • Describes system and effectiveness of controls.
  • Over a period of time (e.g., 6-12 months).
  • Focuses on operational effectiveness.
Key Takeaway

SOC 2 reports, especially Type 2, are vital for third-party risk management, providing independent assurance of a service organization's security controls to build customer trust.
