Key Concepts to Remember

Core Ideas from Assurance Frameworks and Standards

Assurance Frameworks/Standards

Structured approaches to managing information security risks and demonstrating compliance.

ISO/IEC 27001

International standard specifying requirements for an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

ISMS (Information Security Management System)

A systematic approach to managing sensitive company information to ensure its security.

Annex A (ISO 27001)

A list of 114 information security controls that organizations can consider for implementation based on risk assessment.

Summary Takeaway

These foundational concepts underscore the importance of structured, risk-based approaches to information security, with ISO 27001 and its ISMS as a global benchmark.
