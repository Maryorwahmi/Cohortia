Core Functions

NIST CSF: Understanding Identify and Protect

Building a Foundation for Cybersecurity Risk Management

Identify

Develop an organizational understanding to manage cybersecurity risk to systems, assets, data, and capabilities.

Examples
  • Asset management (hardware, software, data).
  • Risk assessment and management strategies.
  • Governance structures and policies.

Protect

Develop and implement appropriate safeguards to ensure delivery of critical infrastructure services.

Examples
  • Access control (MFA, RBAC).
  • Data security (encryption at rest/in transit).
  • Protective technology (firewalls, IPS).
  • Awareness and training programs.
Key Takeaway

The 'Identify' function is foundational for understanding risks, while 'Protect' implements the necessary safeguards to defend critical assets and services.
