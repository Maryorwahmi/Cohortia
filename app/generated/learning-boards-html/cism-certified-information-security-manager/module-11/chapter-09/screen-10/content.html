Core Functions

NIST CSF: Detect, Respond, and Recover

Proactive and Reactive Measures for Cybersecurity Incidents

Detect

Identify the occurrence of a cybersecurity event.

Examples
  • Continuous monitoring for anomalies.
  • Implementing detection processes.
  • Security Information and Event Management (SIEM).

Respond

Take action regarding a detected cybersecurity incident.

Examples
  • Incident response planning.
  • Internal/external communications.
  • Analysis and mitigation of incidents.

Recover

Maintain resilience and restore capabilities impaired by incidents.

Examples
  • Recovery planning and testing.
  • Post-incident improvements.
  • Restoration communications.
Key Takeaway

The Detect, Respond, and Recover functions of NIST CSF ensure comprehensive incident management, from identification to restoration, supported by maturity-based Implementation Tiers.
