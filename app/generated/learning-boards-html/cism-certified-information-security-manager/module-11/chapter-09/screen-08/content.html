NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF): Managing Risk

A Voluntary, Flexible Approach to Cybersecurity Posture

NIST CSF Overview

A voluntary framework, primarily used in the US, designed to help organizations manage and reduce cybersecurity risk through a structured approach.

Core Structure

Organized around five core functions that provide a high-level strategic view of cybersecurity risk management.

NIST CSF vs. ISO 27001

ISO 27001

  • Certifiable international standard.
  • Focuses on ISMS implementation.
  • More prescriptive requirements.

NIST CSF

  • Voluntary framework.
  • Focuses on cybersecurity risk management.
  • More flexible and adaptable.
Key Takeaway

NIST CSF is a voluntary, risk-focused framework offering flexibility for organizations to manage cybersecurity, contrasting with ISO 27001's certifiable ISMS approach.
