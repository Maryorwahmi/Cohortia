Key Concepts: Evidence & Techniques

Essential terms for effective audit execution.

Audit Evidence

Information used by the auditor to support the audit findings and conclusions, requiring sufficiency, appropriateness, and reliability.

Interviews

Direct discussions with personnel to gather information about processes and controls, requiring corroboration.

Observation

Directly watching processes or activities to verify control implementation, mindful of the Hawthorne effect.

Summary Takeaway

Effective audit execution relies on a solid understanding of what constitutes reliable evidence and the skillful application of various gathering techniques to validate controls.
