Evidence Gathering Technique

Observation: Seeing Controls in Action

Directly witnessing processes to verify actual implementation.

Benefits of Direct Observation

  • •Real-time Insight: See controls as they are actually performed.
  • •Verify Practice: Confirm if documented procedures are followed.
  • •Identify Gaps: Uncover discrepancies between policy and reality.
Key Takeaway

Observation provides invaluable real-world evidence, but auditors must be mindful of its potential impact on observed behavior.

Example: Data Center Access

Observing a data center entry procedure to verify physical access controls, such as badge authentication, visitor logging, and escort requirements, provides direct evidence of control effectiveness. This helps confirm if security policies are truly enforced.

Common Challenge:The Hawthorne effect, where observed individuals alter behavior.
Pro-Tip:Conduct observations during normal operations to minimize behavioral changes.

Observation Scenarios

Physical Security:Entry/exit procedures, surveillance monitoring.
Incident Response:SOC analyst alert handling, team coordination.
Data Handling:Secure disposal, sensitive document processing.
