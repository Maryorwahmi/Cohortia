Audit Execution: Data Gathering and Analysis

Transforming Audit Plans into Actionable Insights

This phase is where the audit team actively collects and analyzes evidence to determine whether audit objectives are being met and if criteria are fulfilled. The goal is to obtain sufficient, appropriate, and reliable audit evidence to support all findings and conclusions.

Key Idea

Audit execution is the phase where planned objectives meet real-world data, focusing on collecting and analyzing evidence to validate security controls and compliance.
