Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms and ideas to remember.

Continuous Monitoring

Ongoing, systematic assessment of security controls and compliance status.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Metrics that measure the effectiveness of compliance activities.

Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)

Metrics that provide early warning signals of increasing risk or potential non-compliance.

Compliance Reporting

Communicating compliance status to various stakeholders, tailored to their needs.

Summary Takeaway

Effective compliance relies on continuous monitoring, strategic use of KPIs and KRIs, and clear, audience-specific reporting to maintain security posture and meet regulatory demands.
