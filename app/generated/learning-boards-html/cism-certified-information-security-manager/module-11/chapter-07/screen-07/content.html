Understanding Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Measuring the effectiveness of your compliance activities.

Definition of KPIs

Metrics that quantify the effectiveness and efficiency of compliance activities and controls.

Purpose

To demonstrate that compliance efforts are achieving desired outcomes and meeting objectives.

Key Takeaway

KPIs are essential for measuring the success and efficiency of your compliance program, providing clear evidence of control effectiveness.

KPI Examples for Compliance
  • Percentage of employees completing mandatory security awareness training.
  • Average time to remediate high-priority vulnerabilities identified by scans.
  • Number of systems configured according to security baselines.
  • Percentage of critical data encrypted at rest and in transit.
Why KPIs Matter

KPIs provide tangible evidence of your compliance program's performance. They help justify resource allocation, demonstrate due diligence to auditors, and highlight areas where security controls are strong or need reinforcement.

Practical Application

By tracking the 'percentage of critical systems patched within 48 hours,' an organization can quickly identify if its patch management process is meeting its internal SLA, directly impacting its vulnerability management compliance.
