Mechanisms for Continuous Monitoring: Audits & Reviews

Human-centric approaches to validate compliance posture.

01

Internal Audits & Reviews

Conduct mini-audits or targeted reviews of specific controls or departments more frequently than full external audits.

Example

A quarterly review of user access privileges to critical financial systems ensures adherence to the principle of least privilege.

02

Policy & Procedure Reviews

Ensure that documentation remains current and accurately reflects actual practices. This prevents 'policy drift' and ensures operational alignment.

Example

An annual review of the data retention policy confirms it aligns with current legal requirements and actual data lifecycle management practices.

Key Takeaway

Internal audits and regular policy reviews are crucial for validating human processes and ensuring documentation accurately reflects operational reality, complementing automated monitoring.
