Management Audit

Reviewing the overall information security governance framework.

Governance Framework

Reviews policies, risk management processes, and strategic alignment.

Leadership Engagement

Assesses CISO reporting to the board and alignment of security investments.

Scenario

A management audit evaluates if the organization's security strategy effectively supports its business objectives and risk appetite.

Why it matters

Ensures security is a strategic priority, effectively managed, and aligned with overall business goals.

Key Takeaway

Management audits provide assurance that security governance is robust, strategic, and supported by organizational leadership.
