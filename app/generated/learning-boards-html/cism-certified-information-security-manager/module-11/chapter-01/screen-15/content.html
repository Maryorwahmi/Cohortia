Key Concepts to Remember

Reinforcing core information security principles.

Compliance Audit

Verifies adherence to specific laws, regulations, or industry standards (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).

Operational Audit

Assesses the efficiency and effectiveness of security processes and procedures.

Management Audit

Reviews the overall information security governance framework and strategic alignment.

Summary Takeaway

Different audit types serve distinct purposes, all guided by specific audit criteria to ensure comprehensive security evaluation.
