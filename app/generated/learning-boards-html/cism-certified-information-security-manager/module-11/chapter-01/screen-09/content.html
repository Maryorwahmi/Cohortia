Operational Audit

Assessing the efficiency and effectiveness of security processes.

Incident Response

Evaluates if plans are documented, tested, and staff are trained.

Change Management

Assesses if security is integrated into changes to prevent new vulnerabilities.

Scenario

An organization conducts an operational audit to verify that their patch management process is consistently applied across all critical systems.

Why it matters

Ensures security processes are effective, efficient, and contribute to a strong overall security posture.

Key Takeaway

Operational audits validate that security procedures are not just present, but are actively and effectively implemented in daily operations.
