Information Security Audit

A systematic, independent process to evaluate security controls.

Systematic Process

Follows a defined methodology to ensure thoroughness and consistency.

Independent Evaluation

Conducted by unbiased parties to ensure objectivity and credibility.

Documented Evidence

Relies on verifiable records and findings to support conclusions.

Key Takeaway
