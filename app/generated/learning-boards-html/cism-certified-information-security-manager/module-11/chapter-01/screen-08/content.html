Compliance Audit

Ensuring adherence to laws, regulations, and industry standards.

Regulatory Frameworks

Focuses on laws like GDPR, HIPAA, or industry standards like PCI DSS.

Policy Adherence

Examines whether data handling practices meet privacy regulations.

Scenario

A healthcare provider undergoes a HIPAA compliance audit to verify patient data encryption and access controls.

Why it matters

Avoids legal penalties, maintains reputation, and builds trust with customers and partners.

Key Takeaway

Compliance audits are essential for validating legal and industry standard adherence, protecting against fines and reputational damage.
