Sector-Specific Rules

Industry Standards and Contractual Obligations

Adhering to sector-specific and agreed-upon security clauses

Key Takeaway

Even without direct government oversight, industry and contractual requirements are critical for maintaining business operations and trust.

PCI DSS

Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard: A global standard for organizations that store, process, or transmit cardholder data. Non-compliance can lead to severe fines and inability to process credit cards.

Example: Implementing network segmentation for cardholder data environments.

Contractual Obligations

Specific security clauses included in business contracts, requiring adherence to certain standards (e.g., ISO 27001 certification, SOC 2 compliance) to protect client data.

Example: A cloud provider maintaining SOC 2 compliance for customer data.
Scenario Card

Impact of Non-Compliance

Imagine a small online retailer that processes credit card payments. If they fail to meet PCI DSS requirements, they could face substantial fines from payment brands and, more critically, lose the ability to process credit card transactions. This would effectively shut down their primary revenue stream, demonstrating how industry standards directly impact business continuity.
