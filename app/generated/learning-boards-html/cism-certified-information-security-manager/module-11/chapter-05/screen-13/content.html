Risk Management

The High Cost of Non-Compliance

Understanding legal, financial, and reputational impacts

Key Takeaway

The costs of non-compliance are multifaceted and can be catastrophic, far outweighing the investment in proactive compliance efforts.

Legal & Regulatory Fines

Significant penalties imposed by government bodies (e.g., GDPR fines up to €20M or 4% global turnover).

Reputational Damage

Erosion of customer trust, damaged brand image, and loss of market share due to public failures.

Financial Loss

Beyond fines, includes legal costs, remediation expenses, loss of business, and increased insurance premiums.

Operational Disruption

Regulatory actions might force an organization to halt certain operations until compliance is achieved.
