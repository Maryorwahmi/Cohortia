Mandated Standards

Navigating Legal and Regulatory Compliance

Government-mandated laws protecting data and systems

Key Takeaway

These laws are non-negotiable and demand rigorous adherence to avoid severe legal and financial repercussions.

GDPR (EU)

General Data Protection Regulation: Governs privacy and security of personal data for EU individuals. Mandates data subject rights, breach notification, and data protection by design.

Example: Consent management for EU customer data.

HIPAA (US)

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act: Protects privacy and security of Protected Health Information (PHI) in the US healthcare sector. Mandates administrative, physical, and technical safeguards.

Example: Encrypting electronic patient health records.

SOX (US)

Sarbanes-Oxley Act: Impacts publicly traded US companies, focusing on financial reporting accuracy. Mandates internal controls over financial reporting, including stringent IT general controls.

Example: Robust access controls for financial systems.
