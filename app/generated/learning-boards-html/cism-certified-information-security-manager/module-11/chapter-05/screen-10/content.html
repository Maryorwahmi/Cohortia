Framework Spotlight

HIPAA: Safeguarding US Health Information

Ensuring privacy and security of Protected Health Information (PHI)

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is a US law protecting the privacy and security of Protected Health Information (PHI) in the healthcare sector.

  • •PHI Protection: Covers medical records, billing, and other identifiable health data.
  • •Administrative Safeguards: Policies and procedures for managing PHI.
  • •Physical Safeguards: Controls for facility access and workstation security.
  • •Technical Safeguards: Encryption, access control, audit trails for ePHI.
Key Takeaway

HIPAA demands stringent, multi-layered security for health information, with significant penalties for non-compliance.

CISM's Role

Ensuring HIPAA Compliance

  • •ePHI Encryption: Ensure electronic PHI is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • •Access Control: Implement strict, role-based access to PHI.
  • •Audit Trails: Maintain logs to track all access and modifications to ePHI.
  • •Risk Assessments: Regularly assess and manage risks to ePHI.
Example: Implementing multi-factor authentication for all access to electronic health records.
Common Pitfall

Overlooking Business Associates

A frequent mistake is to focus only on the 'covered entity' (e.g., hospital) and overlook 'business associates' (e.g., cloud providers, billing services) who also handle PHI. HIPAA's requirements extend to these third-party vendors, necessitating Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and due diligence to ensure their compliance.
