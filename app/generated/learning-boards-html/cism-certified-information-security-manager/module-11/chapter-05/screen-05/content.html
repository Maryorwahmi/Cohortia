Understanding the Drivers of Compliance

Why organizations must comply with security standards

Key Takeaway

Compliance is a multi-faceted challenge, driven by a complex interplay of external pressures and internal commitments.

Legal & Regulatory

Government-mandated laws dictating data protection and system security (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, SOX).

Industry Standards

Rules and best practices set by specific industries (e.g., PCI DSS for payment card data).

Contractual Obligations

Security clauses and requirements specified in business agreements (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2).

Internal Policies

Organizational rules and best practices for consistent security posture (e.g., AUP, Data Retention).
