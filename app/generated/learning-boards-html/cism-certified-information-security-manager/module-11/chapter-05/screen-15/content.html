Key Concepts to Remember

Core definitions in information security compliance

Compliance

Adherence to laws, regulations, standards, or internal policies.

GDPR

EU law on data protection and privacy for individuals.

HIPAA

US law protecting patient health information (PHI).

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these core compliance definitions is crucial for any CISM, as they form the bedrock of effective information security management and a robust compliance posture.
