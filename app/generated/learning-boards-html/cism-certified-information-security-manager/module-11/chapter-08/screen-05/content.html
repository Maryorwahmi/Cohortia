Process Flow

Initiating Regulatory Inquiry Response

The critical first steps when your organization receives a formal regulatory request.

01

Validate Legitimacy

Confirm the inquiry is genuine to prevent phishing or social engineering attempts.

02

Engage Legal & Stakeholders

Immediately involve legal counsel and relevant internal teams for coordinated guidance.

03

Establish Response Team

Designate a dedicated team or point person to centralize communications and track requests.

Key Takeaway

A validated, legally-informed, and coordinated initial response is paramount for effectively managing regulatory inquiries.
