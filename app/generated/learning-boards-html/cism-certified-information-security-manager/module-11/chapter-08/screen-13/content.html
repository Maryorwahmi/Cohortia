Post-Audit Activities: Remediation & Relationships

Implementing corrective actions and fostering ongoing trust with regulators and auditors.

Implement Remediation Actions

Execute the planned remediation actions to address identified security weaknesses and improve controls.

Follow-up and Verification

Track remediation progress, conduct internal verification, and prepare for potential external follow-up audits.

Maintain Relationships

Foster professional and respectful relationships with regulators and auditors to build trust and facilitate future interactions.

Safety Notes for Technical Testing

Ensuring security during auditor-led technical assessments.

Rules of Engagement

All technical testing (e.g., penetration testing) must adhere to strict, pre-approved rules of engagement.

Incident Response Ready

Always have an incident response plan active, even during an audit, for unexpected events.

Key Takeaway

Effective post-audit activities involve diligent remediation, continuous verification, and strategic relationship management to enhance security and build trust.
