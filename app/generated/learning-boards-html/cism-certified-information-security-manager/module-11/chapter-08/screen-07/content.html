Preparation Strategy

Proactive Audit Preparation: Scope & Assessment

Laying the groundwork for a successful external information security audit.

Understand Audit Scope

Review the engagement letter to know what will be audited, against which standards (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS), and for what period.

Conduct Internal Readiness Assessment

Perform a 'mock audit' or self-assessment against expected criteria to identify and remediate potential findings proactively.

Example: PCI DSS Mock Audit

Preparing for a PCI DSS audit requires specific internal checks.

Internal Scan & Review

Conduct internal vulnerability scans and review controls within the Cardholder Data Environment (CDE).

Identify & Remediate Gaps

Use findings from the mock audit to fix non-compliance issues before the external auditors arrive.

Key Takeaway

Proactive understanding of audit scope and internal readiness assessments are crucial for minimizing audit findings and ensuring a smooth process.
