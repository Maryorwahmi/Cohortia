Best Practices

Crafting Effective Inquiry Responses

Guidelines for providing accurate, concise, and legally sound information to regulators.

Be Factual and Concise

Provide only the information explicitly requested, avoiding speculation or extraneous details.

Ensure Accuracy and Consistency

All submitted information must be accurate and consistent across all documents to prevent red flags.

Legal Review is Essential

All documents should be reviewed by legal counsel to protect privileged information and ensure compliance.

Example: Responding to a Data Breach Inquiry

A regulator asks for 'all data breach notifications filed in the last 12 months.'

Correct Action

Provide ONLY the official data breach notifications. Do not include internal incident reports or remediation plans unless specifically requested.

Common Mistake

Volunteering internal details or unrequested documents can complicate the inquiry and expose additional liabilities.

Key Takeaway

Precision, factual accuracy, and legal review are essential to manage regulatory inquiries effectively and minimize organizational risk.
