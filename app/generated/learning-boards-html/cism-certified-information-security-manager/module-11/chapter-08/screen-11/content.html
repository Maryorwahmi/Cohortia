Engagement Strategy

Effective Communication & Collaboration in Audits

Fostering a transparent and productive relationship with external auditors.

Daily Debriefs

Schedule brief daily meetings with the audit team to discuss progress, clarify requests, and address issues promptly.

Documentation of Requests

Maintain a detailed log of all information requested, who provided it, and when, creating an invaluable audit trail.

Collaborative Approach

Adopt a transparent and collaborative stance, but be prepared to challenge factual inaccuracies respectfully.

Scenario: Challenging an Audit Finding

An auditor claims a critical security policy is missing.

Appropriate Response

Politely provide evidence of the policy's existence and location, demonstrating its availability and adherence.

Ineffective Response

Becoming defensive or simply accepting an incorrect finding without providing counter-evidence.

Key Takeaway

Proactive communication, meticulous documentation, and a collaborative yet assertive approach are crucial for successful audit engagement.
