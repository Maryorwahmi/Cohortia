Managing Information Flow During Audits

Implementing strict controls over data and evidence shared with external auditors.

Audit Liaison as Gatekeeper

The CISM or designated liaison controls all information requests and access to systems.

No Unfettered Access

Never grant auditors direct, unsupervised access to production systems or sensitive data.

Redaction and Logging

Ensure information is relevant, properly redacted (e.g., PII), and meticulously logged for an audit trail.

Key Takeaway

A designated audit liaison and strict control over information flow are essential to protect sensitive data during external audits.
