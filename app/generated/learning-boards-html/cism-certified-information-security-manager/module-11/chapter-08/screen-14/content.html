Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terminology for managing regulatory inquiries and external audits.

Regulatory Inquiry

A formal request for information or explanation from a regulatory body, requiring a structured and factual response.

External Audit

An independent assessment of an organization's security controls and compliance by a third party, validating security posture.

Audit Liaison

The designated primary point of contact for external auditors, controlling information flow and communications.

Key Takeaway

Understanding these core terms is crucial for effectively navigating and managing external regulatory and audit engagements.
