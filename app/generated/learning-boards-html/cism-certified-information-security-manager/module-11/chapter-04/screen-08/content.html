Crafting Findings: The 5 C's Framework

Ensuring precision, objectivity, and actionability in every finding.

01

Condition

What was found; the factual observation of the current state.

02

Criteria

What should be; the policy, standard, or best practice that was violated.

03

Cause

Why it happened; the underlying reason for the condition.

04

Effect/Risk

The impact; the actual or potential consequences of the condition.

05

Recommendation

What to do; clear, practical steps to correct the condition and mitigate the risk.

Key Takeaway

The 5 C's framework ensures audit findings are comprehensive, objective, and actionable, providing a clear path for remediation and improvement.
