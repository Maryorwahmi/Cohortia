Post-Audit Phase

Management Response: From Findings to Action Plans

Transforming audit insights into concrete remediation commitments.

Key Elements of a Management Response

  • •
    Agreement or disagreement with findings.
  • •
    Planned actions to address recommendations.
  • •
    Assigned responsibilities for remediation.
  • •
    Target completion dates for each action.
CISM's Role

Facilitate the process, ensuring responses are timely, realistic, and adequately address identified risks.

Why it's Critical

Without a robust management response and subsequent follow-up, audits become mere academic exercises, failing to deliver their intended value and leaving the organization exposed to risks.

Key Takeaway

The management response is the formal commitment to address audit findings, transforming identified risks into actionable plans with clear ownership and timelines.
