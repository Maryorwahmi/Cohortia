Core Section

Unpacking Findings and Recommendations

The heart of the report, detailing issues and proposed solutions.

Audit Findings

  • •
    Detailed presentation of each identified issue or control weakness.
  • •
    Must be precise, objective, and supported by evidence.
  • •
    Prioritized based on risk level (High, Medium, Low) to guide remediation.
Purpose

To clearly articulate what was found, enabling management to understand the problem.

Recommendations

  • •
    Clear, practical, and actionable steps to address each finding.
  • •
    Should directly mitigate the identified risks.
  • •
    Aimed at strengthening controls and improving security posture.
Purpose

To provide management with a clear path forward for remediation and improvement.

Key Takeaway

The Findings and Recommendations section is where the audit's value is most evident, providing both problem identification and practical solutions for security enhancement.
