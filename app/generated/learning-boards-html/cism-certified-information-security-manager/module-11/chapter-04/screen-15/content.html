Chapter Summary

Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential ideas to remember

Management Response

Management's documented plan to address audit findings, including actions, responsibilities, and timelines.

Follow-up Process

Verification that management's agreed-upon remediation actions have been implemented and are effective.

Stakeholder Communication

Tailoring audit results and messages to different audiences (technical, managerial, executive).

Professional Standards

Ethical principles guiding auditors, such as independence, objectivity, and confidentiality.

Summary Takeaway

Effective audit reporting extends beyond the report itself, encompassing management's response, follow-up, tailored communication, and strict adherence to professional ethics.
