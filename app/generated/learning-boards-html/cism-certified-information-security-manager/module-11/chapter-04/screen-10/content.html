Tailoring Your Message: Audience-Specific Communication

Adapting audit results for technical teams, managers, and senior leadership.

Technical Teams

  • •
    Specific technical details.
  • •
    Root causes and practical remediation steps.
  • •
    Benefit from direct, collaborative discussions.

Department Managers

  • •
    Impact on operations.
  • •
    Responsibilities in remediation.
  • •
    Resources required for resolution.

Senior Management/Board

  • •
    Strategic risks and financial implications.
  • •
    Overall security posture.
  • •
    Focus on 'so what?' and 'what next?'.
Key Takeaway

Tailoring your communication to different stakeholders ensures that audit results are understood, relevant, and lead to appropriate action across all levels of the organization.
