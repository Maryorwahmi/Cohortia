Professional Conduct

Upholding Integrity: Ethics in Audit Reporting

Maintaining independence, objectivity, and confidentiality in all audit communications.

Key Professional Standards

  • •
    Independence: Freedom from influences that compromise judgment.
  • •
    Objectivity: Reporting findings fairly and accurately, without bias.
  • •
    Confidentiality: Protecting sensitive information from unauthorized disclosure.
  • •
    Professional Skepticism: A questioning mind and critical assessment of evidence.
Ethical Imperative

These standards ensure the credibility and integrity of the audit function, fostering trust in audit results.

Why it Matters

Upholding ethical considerations and professional standards is paramount for maintaining the credibility and integrity of the audit function, ensuring that audit findings are trusted and acted upon by the organization.

Key Takeaway

Ethical conduct and adherence to professional standards are the bedrock of effective audit reporting, ensuring trust, credibility, and the ultimate impact of audit efforts.
