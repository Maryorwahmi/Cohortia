Chapter 11.4

Audit Reporting and Communication

Transforming Evidence into Actionable Security Improvements

The culmination of any audit engagement is the audit report and the subsequent communication of its findings. As a CISM, your ability to articulate complex technical and process-related issues into clear, concise, and actionable reports is paramount. A well-crafted audit report is not just a document; it's a powerful tool for driving security improvements and informing strategic decisions.

Key Idea

Audit reports are strategic tools that translate evidence into actionable insights, driving security improvements and informing decisions.
