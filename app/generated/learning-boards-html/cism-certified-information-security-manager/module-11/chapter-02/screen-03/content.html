Expanding Your Audit Planning Expertise

Bloom's Taxonomy:
LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
03

Develop Comprehensive Audit Scope

Precisely define the boundaries of an audit, delineating included and excluded systems, processes, and timeframes. This prevents scope creep and ensures focused, manageable audit engagements.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Identify & Select Audit Criteria

Choose appropriate benchmarks, including internal policies, industry standards, and regulatory requirements, against which audit evidence will be evaluated to ensure objective and credible findings.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
05

Understand Stakeholder Engagement

Recognize the importance of involving and communicating with all relevant parties throughout the audit planning phase to ensure collaboration, manage expectations, and reduce resistance.
