Avoiding Common Pitfalls in Audit Planning

Proactive awareness of common mistakes ensures a more effective and valuable audit.

Key Pitfalls to Avoid

Unclear Scope/Objectives

Scope too broad or too narrow, or objectives that are not SMART, lead to unfocused and ineffective audits.

Missing Criteria

Lack of defined benchmarks makes audit findings subjective and reduces credibility.

Ignoring Politics

Failing to manage perceptions can lead to resistance and hinder collaboration.

Operational Risks

Not planning for potential disruptions or risks introduced by audit activities.

Key Takeaway

A CISM must proactively identify and mitigate common planning pitfalls to ensure the audit is focused, credible, and non-disruptive.

Why it Matters

  • Prevents wasted resources and time.
  • Ensures audit findings are relevant and actionable.
  • Maintains the credibility and value of the audit function.
  • Reduces friction and improves stakeholder relations.
  • Protects the organization from audit-induced risks.
