Chapter Summary

Key Concepts in Audit Planning

Essential terms and principles for effective information security audits.

Audit Objectives

Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound goals that an audit aims to accomplish.

Audit Scope

The defined boundaries of an audit, specifying what systems, processes, departments, and timeframes are included or excluded.

Audit Criteria

The policies, standards, regulations, or best practices against which audit evidence is evaluated.

Summary Takeaway

Meticulous planning, clear objectives, precise scoping, and defined criteria are the cornerstones of an effective information security audit.
