Evaluation Standard

Choosing Appropriate Audit Criteria

Benchmarking Against Policies, Standards, and Regulations

Internal Policies

Organization's Information Security Policy, Acceptable Use Policy.

Industry Best Practices

NIST Cybersecurity Framework, CIS Benchmarks, OWASP Top 10.

Regulatory Requirements

GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOX, CCPA, state-specific privacy laws.

International Standards

ISO 27001, ISO 27002, NIST SP 800 series.

Example Criteria for Patch Management

Organization's Patch Management Policy, vendor security advisories, and industry best practices for patch deployment timelines.

Supporting Explanation
  • Criteria provide objective benchmarks for evaluation.
  • They ensure consistency and fairness in audit findings.
  • Clear communication of criteria avoids disputes.
  • Without criteria, findings can appear subjective.
Common Mistake

Failing to clearly document and communicate the audit criteria, leading to subjective findings and undermining audit credibility.

Key Takeaway

Selecting and communicating clear audit criteria is fundamental for objective evidence evaluation and ensuring the credibility of audit findings.
