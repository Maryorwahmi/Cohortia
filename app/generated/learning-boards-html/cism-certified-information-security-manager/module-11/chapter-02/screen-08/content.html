Practical Planning

Allocating Resources and Scheduling

Ensuring Your Audit Has the Right Team and Timeline

Identify Audit Team

  • Select members with diverse skills (network, app security, compliance).
  • Ensure necessary expertise for the audit scope.
  • Consider internal vs. external audit team composition.

Estimate Time & Budget

  • Allocate sufficient time for each audit phase.
  • Secure necessary financial resources.
  • Balance thoroughness with practical constraints.

Ensure Independence & Objectivity

  • Avoid conflicts of interest, especially for internal audits.
  • Maintain an unbiased assessment and reporting stance.
  • Credibility hinges on perceived impartiality.

Proactive Communication

  • Engage stakeholders early to manage expectations.
  • Inform department heads about audit schedules.
  • Establish clear channels for inquiries and requests.
Supporting Explanation
  • Resource planning ensures audit feasibility.
  • Team skills must match audit requirements.
  • Independence is vital for unbiased reporting.
  • Proactive communication reduces resistance.
Concrete Example

Providing the IT department with a detailed audit plan and schedule well in advance allows them to prepare and allocate their own resources for supporting the audit.

Key Takeaway

Effective resource allocation, scheduling, and continuous communication are critical for transforming an audit plan into a successful and executable strategy.
