Risk Management

Mitigating Audit-Related Risks and Disruptions

Ensure audit activities do not introduce new risks or disrupt business operations.

Safe Audit Practices

Careful Scheduling

Schedule intrusive tests (e.g., pen testing) during maintenance windows or on non-production environments.

Explicit Authorization

Always obtain written authorization for any intrusive testing activities to prevent liability and misunderstandings.

Key Takeaway

Thoroughly plan and authorize all audit activities, especially intrusive ones, to prevent business disruption and new security risks.

Why it Matters

  • Prevents system outages and data loss.
  • Maintains business continuity during the audit.
  • Protects the audit team from liability.
  • Ensures compliance with internal change management policies.
  • Builds trust by demonstrating responsible auditing.

Example Scenario

A CISM plans a network vulnerability scan. They schedule it for a weekend maintenance window, notify all affected teams, and get explicit sign-off from IT operations and senior management before execution.

Pro Tip

Always have a rollback plan for any intrusive testing. Document the exact steps to revert systems to their pre-audit state if unexpected issues arise.
