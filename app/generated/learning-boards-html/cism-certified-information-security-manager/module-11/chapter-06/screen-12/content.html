Ongoing Process

Continuous Monitoring and Review

Ensuring Sustained Compliance and Effectiveness

Key Activities

  • Control Effectiveness: Regularly assess through automated scans and log reviews.
  • Compliance Status: Conduct internal audits and assessments.
  • Policy Review: Periodically update policies, standards, and procedures.
  • Threat Landscape: Adapt to new threats and emerging vulnerabilities.
Why it Matters

The regulatory landscape is dynamic; continuous monitoring ensures your program remains relevant and effective against evolving threats and requirements.

The Continuous Compliance Cycle

Monitor Controls

Automated scans, log reviews.

Review Policies

Update for new regulations/threats.

Adapt & Improve

Adjust to business changes and new risks.

Continuous adaptation is key to long-term compliance success.
