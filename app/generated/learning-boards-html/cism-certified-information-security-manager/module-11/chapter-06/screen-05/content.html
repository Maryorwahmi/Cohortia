Identifying Applicable Compliance Requirements

Mapping Your Regulatory Landscape

Key Factors for Identification

  • Industry: Specific regulations for sectors like finance, healthcare, or retail.
  • Geography: Laws based on operational locations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).
  • Data Types: Rules for handling sensitive, personal, or financial data (e.g., PCI DSS, HIPAA).
  • Business Activities: Contractual obligations and internal policies.
Common Mistake

Overlooking or misinterpreting regulations can lead to significant compliance gaps and legal liabilities.
