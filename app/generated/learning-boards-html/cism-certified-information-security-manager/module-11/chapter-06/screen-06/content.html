Conducting a Compliance Gap Analysis

Identifying Discrepancies in Your Security Controls

The Gap Analysis Process
1. Review Requirements

Understand all applicable laws and standards.

2. Assess Current Controls

Evaluate existence, implementation, and effectiveness.

3. Identify Gaps

Document discrepancies and areas of non-compliance.

This systematic process reveals where your organization falls short of compliance.
Key Output

A detailed list of deficiencies that need to be addressed, enabling prioritized remediation.

Common Pitfall

A superficial gap analysis can lead to a false sense of security and unaddressed risks.
