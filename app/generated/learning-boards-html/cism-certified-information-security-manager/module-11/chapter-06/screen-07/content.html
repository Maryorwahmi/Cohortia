Developing Security Policies

Setting the High-Level Direction for Compliance

Key Characteristics of Policies

  • High-Level: Focus on 'what' and 'why', not 'how'.
  • Authoritative: Issued by management, reflecting organizational intent.
  • Strategic: Provides the overarching framework for security posture.
  • Mandatory: All employees and systems must adhere to them.
Example Policy Statement

"All sensitive data must be encrypted both at rest and in transit to protect confidentiality and integrity."

The Role of Policies in the Compliance Hierarchy

Policies

High-level statements of intent

Standards

Mandatory requirements for implementation

Procedures

Detailed step-by-step instructions

This hierarchy translates strategic intent into actionable steps.
