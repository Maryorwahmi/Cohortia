Detailing Security Procedures

Providing Step-by-Step Instructions for Tasks

Purpose of Procedures

  • Guidance: Offer explicit, step-by-step instructions for specific tasks.
  • Consistency: Ensure security tasks are performed uniformly and correctly.
  • Efficiency: Streamline operations by providing clear, repeatable processes.
  • Compliance: Directly support adherence to policies and standards.
Example Procedure Step

"To reset your password, navigate to the HR portal, click 'Forgot Password', enter your employee ID, and follow the prompts to set a new password meeting complexity requirements."

Procedure Flow for Password Reset
1

Access HR Portal

2

Click 'Forgot Password'

3

Enter Employee ID & Set New Password

Clear procedures ensure consistent and compliant task execution.
