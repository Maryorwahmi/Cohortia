Strategic Integration

Integrating Compliance with Risk Management

Embedding Compliance into the Risk Framework

Benefits of Integration

  • Efficiency: Avoids redundant efforts between compliance and risk teams.
  • Holistic View: Ensures security investments address both regulatory and business risks.
  • Prioritization: Non-compliance becomes a clear risk factor in assessments.
  • Strategic Alignment: Links security controls directly to organizational objectives.
Example Integration

A GDPR-mandated Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) becomes a required step in the risk management process for new projects involving personal data.
