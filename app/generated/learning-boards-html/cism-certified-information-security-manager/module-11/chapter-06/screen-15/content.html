Key Concepts: Implementation & Oversight

Critical Terms for Program Execution and Maintenance

Standards

Mandatory requirements for implementing policies, defining 'how well' controls must be applied.

Procedures

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for carrying out specific tasks to ensure compliance.

Control Implementation

Deploying technical, administrative, and operational safeguards to meet compliance obligations.

Summary Takeaway

Effective compliance relies on translating policies into actionable standards and procedures, implementing controls, and continuously monitoring for sustained adherence.
