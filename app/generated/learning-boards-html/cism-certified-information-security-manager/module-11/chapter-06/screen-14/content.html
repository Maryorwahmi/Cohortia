Key Concepts: Foundational Elements

Essential Terms for Compliance Program Establishment

Compliance Program

A structured, ongoing initiative to ensure adherence to applicable laws, regulations, standards, and policies.

Compliance Gap Analysis

A process to identify discrepancies between current security controls and specified compliance requirements.

Policies

High-level statements of management's intent and direction regarding information security.

Summary Takeaway

Establishing a compliance program begins with understanding its strategic importance, identifying gaps, and setting clear, high-level policy directions.
