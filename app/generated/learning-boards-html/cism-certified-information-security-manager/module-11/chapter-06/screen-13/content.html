Adaptation and Key Pitfalls

Ensuring Long-Term Program Success

Critical Adaptation Strategies

  • Incident Management: Assess compliance impact of security incidents.
  • Change Management: Evaluate compliance before system/process changes.
  • Training & Awareness: Keep employees informed of evolving responsibilities.
Key Pitfall

Failing to secure adequate budget and executive support can cripple compliance initiatives, leading to lack of traction and unachieved objectives.
