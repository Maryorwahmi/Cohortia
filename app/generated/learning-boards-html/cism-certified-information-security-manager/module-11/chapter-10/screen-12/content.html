Common Pitfalls in GRC Integration

Recognizing and avoiding mistakes that lead to fragmented security posture and inefficiencies.

Mistakes to Avoid
  • • Disconnected Functions: Audit, compliance, and risk operating independently.
  • • Compliance-Only Focus: Prioritizing fines over genuine risk reduction.
  • • Fragmented View: Lack of a consolidated picture of security posture.
  • • Inefficient Resource Use: Redundant efforts and wasted budget.
Key Takeaway

Avoid siloed functions and a narrow compliance-only focus to ensure GRC integration effectively reduces risk and optimizes resources.

Pitfall 1: Siloed Functions

Audit, Compliance, Risk operate independently.

Pitfall 2: Compliance-Only

Focus on fines, not asset protection.

Best Practice: Regular Review

Update GRC strategy for business, threat, and regulatory changes.
