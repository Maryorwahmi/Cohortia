Foundational Principle

Risk Management: Guiding Security Strategy

How risk management sets the strategic direction for security controls and overall security posture.

The Role of Each Discipline
  • • Risk Management: The overarching process that identifies, assesses, and treats risks.
  • • Compliance: Defines the minimum required controls, often externally mandated.
  • • Audit: Provides independent verification of control effectiveness.
  • • Assurance: Offers continuous validation and confidence in the security system.
Key Takeaway

Risk management is the strategic driver for security, with compliance, audit, and assurance serving as essential inputs and validation mechanisms.
