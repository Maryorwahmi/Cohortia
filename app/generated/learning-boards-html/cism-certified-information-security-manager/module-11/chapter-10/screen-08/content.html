Continuous Improvement

Assurance for Continuous Validation

Leveraging ongoing activities to provide continuous feedback and validate the effectiveness of risk mitigation strategies.

Assurance Activities
  • • Internal Reviews: Periodic checks by internal teams.
  • • Automated Checks: Tools for continuous configuration monitoring.
  • • Continuous Monitoring: Real-time oversight of control performance.
  • • Effectiveness Verification: Confirming controls reduce identified risks.
Key Takeaway

Assurance activities create a vital feedback loop, continuously validating control effectiveness and enabling proactive adjustments to risk mitigation strategies.
