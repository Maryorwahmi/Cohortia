Core Concept

The GRC Ecosystem: A Unified Approach

Understanding the symbiotic relationship between Audit, Assurance, Compliance, and Risk Management.

Key Components
  • • Risk Management: Identifies, assesses, and treats risks, setting strategic direction.
  • • Compliance: Defines minimum baseline controls required by external mandates.
  • • Audit: Independently verifies control implementation and operating effectiveness.
  • • Assurance: Provides confidence to stakeholders that the system works as intended.
Key Takeaway

A mature security program views Audit, Assurance, and Compliance as powerful tools that feed into and strengthen the overall Risk Management process, forming a unified GRC ecosystem.
