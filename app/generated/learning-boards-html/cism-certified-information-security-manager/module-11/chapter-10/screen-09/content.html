Strategic Perspective

Compliance as a Baseline, Not a Substitute

Understanding why regulatory adherence is the minimum acceptable security posture, not the entirety of comprehensive security.

Key Differences
  • • Compliance: Focuses on meeting external mandates and minimum requirements.
  • • Risk Management: Addresses all relevant threats and vulnerabilities, unique to the organization.
  • • Scope: Compliance is often narrow; risk management is broad and holistic.
  • • Goal: Compliance avoids penalties; risk management protects assets and achieves business objectives.
Key Takeaway

Being compliant does not automatically mean being secure; comprehensive risk management extends beyond baseline compliance to address all unique organizational risks.

Compliant

Meets PCI DSS requirements

Not Secure

Vulnerable to IP theft via phishing

The Gap

PCI DSS doesn't cover all unique organizational risks like sophisticated nation-state attacks targeting IP.
