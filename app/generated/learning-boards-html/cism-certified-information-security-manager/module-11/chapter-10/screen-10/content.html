CISM Leadership

Championing a Unified GRC Strategy

The CISM's pivotal role in establishing an integrated governance, risk, and compliance framework.

Key Responsibilities
  • • Establish GRC Framework: Implement integrated platforms for centralized management.
  • • Foster Collaboration: Bridge communication between legal, audit, IT, security, and business units.
  • • Eliminate Silos: Ensure consistency and a single source of truth across functions.
  • • Promote Shared Responsibility: Encourage collective ownership of security posture.
Key Takeaway

The CISM is crucial for establishing a unified GRC framework and fostering cross-functional collaboration to ensure consistent and integrated security management.

GRC Framework

Centralized policies, risks, controls

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Legal, Audit, IT, Security, Business

CISM as Bridge

Ensuring each function understands its role in the GRC ecosystem.
