Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance)

An integrated approach to managing an organization's overall governance, enterprise risk management, and compliance with regulations.

Symbiotic Relationship

The mutually beneficial and interdependent relationship between audit, assurance, compliance, and risk management.

Risk Register Integration

Incorporating audit findings and compliance gaps as formal entries in the organization's risk register.

Summary Takeaway

A unified GRC approach, built on symbiotic relationships and integrated risk management, is essential for a mature and effective information security program.
