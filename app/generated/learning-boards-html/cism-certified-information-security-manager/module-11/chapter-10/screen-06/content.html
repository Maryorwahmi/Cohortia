Critical Integration

From Audit Finding to Risk Register Entry

Transforming identified control weaknesses into actionable risks for systematic prioritization and treatment.

Integration Steps
  • • Identify Weakness: Audit uncovers a control gap (e.g., unpatched servers).
  • • Formal Entry: Document finding in the risk register.
  • • Assess Risk: Evaluate likelihood and impact of the weakness.
  • • Assign Rating: Determine the risk level (e.g., High, Medium).
  • • Prioritize Treatment: Trigger remediation plans based on risk rating.
Key Takeaway

Audit findings must be formally integrated into the risk register to ensure weaknesses are systematically prioritized and addressed as organizational risks.
