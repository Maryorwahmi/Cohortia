Chapter 5.2

Risk Acceptance and Escalation

Navigating Deliberate Decisions in Information Security

Key Idea

Formal risk acceptance and structured escalation are vital for managing risks that cannot be eliminated, aligning security with business objectives.

In this chapter, you will learn how organizations make informed decisions about risks that fall within their tolerance, and how to effectively communicate critical risks to the right stakeholders. This ensures a proactive and responsible approach to information security governance.
