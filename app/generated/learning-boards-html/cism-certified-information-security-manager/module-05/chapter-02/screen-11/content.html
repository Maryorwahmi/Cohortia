Formal Acceptance vs. Informal Tolerance

Differentiating deliberate strategy from accidental exposure.

Formal Risk Acceptance

A deliberate, informed, and documented decision by management to acknowledge a risk and take no further action, based on analysis.

Informal Risk Tolerance (Ignorance)

Risks are known but no deliberate action or documentation occurs, leading to unmanaged exposures and governance failure.

Key Takeaway

Always formalize risk decisions. Informal tolerance is a dangerous oversight that can lead to significant, unmanaged vulnerabilities.
