Key Distinction

Risk Appetite and Risk Tolerance

Guiding principles for an organization's willingness to take on risk.

Risk Appetite

The overall level of risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its strategic objectives. A high-level, qualitative statement.

Risk Tolerance

Specific, measurable thresholds for acceptable deviation from the risk appetite, often tied to specific risk categories or business units.

Key Takeaway

Appetite is the 'what we're willing to risk' philosophy; tolerance is the 'how much deviation is acceptable' measurable limit.
