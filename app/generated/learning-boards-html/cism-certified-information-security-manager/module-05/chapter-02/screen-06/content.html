Process Flow

The Formal Risk Acceptance Process

A structured approach to managing acknowledged risks.

1

Identification & Assessment

Fully identify the risk, analyze its likelihood and impact, and determine its residual risk level.

2

Treatment Options Analysis

Consider all viable treatment strategies (avoid, transfer, mitigate) and evaluate their costs and benefits.

3

Recommendation for Acceptance

If other strategies are impractical or uneconomical, recommend acceptance as the preferred course of action.

4

Documentation

Create a formal risk acceptance document detailing the risk, rationale, existing controls, and approving authority.

5

Approval

Obtain formal approval from the appropriate level of management, based on the risk's severity and nature.

6

Monitoring & Review

Add accepted risks to a register and periodically review them to ensure conditions for acceptance remain valid.

Key Takeaway

A formal process ensures risk acceptance is a deliberate, transparent, and accountable decision, not an oversight.
