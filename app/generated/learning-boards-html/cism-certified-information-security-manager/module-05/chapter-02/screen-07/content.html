Process Detail

Step 1: Identification and Assessment

Thoroughly understanding the risk's nature, likelihood, and impact.

Identify the Risk

Clearly define the risk, affected assets, and potential vulnerabilities. What is the threat?

Analyze Likelihood & Impact

Assess the probability of the risk occurring and the severity of its consequences (financial, reputational, operational).

Determine Residual Risk

Calculate the risk level remaining after all existing controls have been applied. This is the 'real' risk.

Key Takeaway

A thorough risk assessment, including residual risk, is the essential foundation for any responsible risk acceptance decision.
