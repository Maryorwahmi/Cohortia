Core Concept

Understanding Risk Escalation

Raising critical risks to higher management for informed decision-making.

Definition

The process of reporting risks that exceed predefined thresholds or require higher-level authority for decision-making to senior management or the board.

When to Escalate

When a risk's impact, likelihood, or complexity exceeds a team's authority or the organization's risk tolerance.

Key Takeaway

Escalation ensures critical risks are addressed by those with the authority and perspective to make enterprise-level decisions.

Escalation Triggers

Risk Score Above Threshold

(e.g., High Impact, High Likelihood)

Exceeds Financial Loss Limit

(e.g., > $100,000)

Regulatory Non-Compliance

(Potential Legal Implications)

Requires Higher Authority

Decision beyond current management level.
