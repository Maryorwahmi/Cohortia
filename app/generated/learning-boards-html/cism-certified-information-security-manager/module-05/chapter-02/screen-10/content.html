Process Detail

Step 6: Monitoring and Review

Ensuring accepted risks remain within acceptable parameters over time.

Add to Risk Register

Formally document accepted risks in a central repository for visibility and accountability.

Continuous Monitoring

Track changes in threat landscape, system criticality, or mitigation costs that might alter the risk profile.

Periodic Review

Regularly re-evaluate the risk and the validity of its acceptance, especially after significant organizational changes.

Key Takeaway

Accepted risks require ongoing vigilance; monitoring and review ensure they remain within acceptable bounds and decisions are still valid.
