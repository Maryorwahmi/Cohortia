Process Detail

Steps 2 & 3: Treatment Analysis and Recommendation

Evaluating alternatives and proposing acceptance when appropriate.

Step 2: Treatment Options Analysis

Evaluate all viable strategies: avoid, transfer, or mitigate. Conduct a cost-benefit analysis for each option.

Step 3: Recommendation for Acceptance

Propose acceptance if other strategies are impractical, uneconomical, or if the risk is within tolerance.

Key Takeaway

Risk acceptance is a last resort, recommended only after thorough analysis proves other treatments are less viable or cost-effective.
