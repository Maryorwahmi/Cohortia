Key Concepts

Essential terms for Risk Acceptance and Escalation

Risk Escalation

The process of reporting risks that exceed predefined thresholds or require higher-level authority for decision-making to senior management or the board.

Residual Risk

The risk that remains after all risk treatment strategies (mitigation, transfer, avoidance) have been implemented.

Risk Register

A central repository for documenting all identified risks, their assessments, treatment strategies, ownership, and current status.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management requires a clear escalation path for critical risks, diligent tracking of residual risks, and a comprehensive risk register for governance.
