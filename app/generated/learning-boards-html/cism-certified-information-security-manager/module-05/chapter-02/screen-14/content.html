Key Concepts

Essential terms for Risk Acceptance and Escalation

Risk Acceptance (Formal)

A documented, deliberate decision by authorized management to take no further action to reduce a risk, based on analysis.

Risk Appetite

The overall level of risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives.

Risk Tolerance

Specific, measurable thresholds for acceptable deviation from the risk appetite, often tied to specific risk categories.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management hinges on clearly defining and consistently applying formal risk acceptance, appetite, and tolerance to guide strategic decisions.
