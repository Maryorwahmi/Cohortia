Core Concept

What is Formal Risk Acceptance?

A deliberate, informed management decision to acknowledge and not act on a risk.

Definition

A documented, deliberate decision by authorized management to take no further action to reduce a risk, based on analysis.

When to Accept

When mitigation cost outweighs potential impact, or risk falls within defined appetite/tolerance levels.

Key Takeaway

Formal acceptance is a strategic, documented choice, not an oversight, ensuring risks are managed responsibly even without mitigation.

Common Mistake

Confusing formal acceptance with simply ignoring a risk. Informal tolerance is a governance failure.
