The Risk Escalation Process

Defining triggers, paths, and required information for effective reporting.

Trigger Points

Conditions that warrant escalation (e.g., risk score above X, financial loss exceeding Y, regulatory non-compliance).

Escalation Paths

Clearly defined routes for risk information to reach higher management levels (e.g., CISO, Executive Committee, Board).

Information Required

The format and content of the escalation report (e.g., risk description, impact, likelihood, proposed options, recommendations).

Key Takeaway

A well-defined escalation process ensures critical risks are communicated effectively, enabling timely and informed strategic decisions.
