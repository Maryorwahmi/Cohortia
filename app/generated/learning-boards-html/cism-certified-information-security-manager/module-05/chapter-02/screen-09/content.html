Process Detail

Steps 4 & 5: Documentation and Approval

Formalizing the decision with clear records and appropriate management sign-off.

Step 4: Documentation

Create a formal document detailing the risk, its impact, rationale for acceptance, existing controls, and approving authority.

Step 5: Approval

Obtain formal approval from the appropriate management level (department head, CISO, executive committee, or board).

Key Takeaway

Clear documentation and tiered approval are non-negotiable for legitimate risk acceptance, ensuring accountability and preventing governance failures.

Risk Acceptance Form Example

Risk Acceptance Document

Risk ID: R-007

Description: Unpatched vulnerability in non-critical legacy HR system (version X.Y).

Impact: Potential data exposure of non-sensitive employee directory information.

Likelihood: Low (system isolated).

Rationale for Acceptance: Cost of upgrade/patching ($50,000) significantly exceeds potential impact ($5,000 estimated).

Existing Controls: Network segmentation, restricted access, regular monitoring.

Approval: [Department Head/CISO Name]

Date: [Date]

Approval Levels
  • Low Impact: Department Head
  • Medium Impact: CISO / Risk Committee
  • High Impact: Executive Leadership / Board
