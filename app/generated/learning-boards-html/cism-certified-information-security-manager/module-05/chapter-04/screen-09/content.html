Scenario Example

A healthcare provider needs to demonstrate strong data protection for patient records and achieve formal certification. ISO 27002 (with 27001) would be the most suitable choice due to its prescriptive nature and international recognition for ISMS certification.

Key Takeaway

Selecting a framework requires evaluating an organization's specific needs: NIST CSF for flexible risk management, ISO 27002 for certifiable controls, and COBIT for IT governance alignment.
