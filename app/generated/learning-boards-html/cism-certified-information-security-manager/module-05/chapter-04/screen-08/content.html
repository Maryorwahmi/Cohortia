COBIT: Governance and Management of Enterprise IT

Integrating information security into broader IT governance

Core Principles

  • **Business-Focused:** Aligns IT with overall business strategy and objectives.
  • **Holistic Approach:** Integrates information security into broader IT governance.
  • **Comprehensive Processes:** Provides processes, control objectives, and management guidelines.
  • **Value Delivery:** Ensures IT investments deliver tangible value to the organization.
  • **Risk Optimization:** Manages IT-related risks effectively across the enterprise.
Key Takeaway

COBIT is a business-focused framework for IT governance and management, integrating security into broader IT processes to align with business strategy and ensure value delivery.

COBIT 5 Domains

Evaluate, Direct and Monitor (EDM)

Align, Plan and Organize (APO)

Build, Acquire and Implement (BAI)

Deliver, Service and Support (DSS)

Monitor, Evaluate and Assess (MEA)

COBIT provides a comprehensive set of processes and control objectives across these domains.
