NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

A flexible, risk-based approach for managing cybersecurity risk

Core Characteristics

  • **Voluntary Framework:** Not a regulation, but widely adopted for best practices.
  • **Five Core Functions:** Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
  • **Adaptable & Technology-Neutral:** Suitable for organizations of all sizes and sectors.
  • **Outcome-Focused:** Emphasizes desired security outcomes rather than specific controls.
  • **Continuous Improvement:** Designed to help organizations mature their cybersecurity posture over time.
Key Takeaway

NIST CSF provides a flexible, risk-based approach to cybersecurity, guiding organizations through five core functions to manage and improve their security posture.
