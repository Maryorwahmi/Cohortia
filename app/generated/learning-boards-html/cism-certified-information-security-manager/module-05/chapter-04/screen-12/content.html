Implementation Steps

Documentation, Monitoring, and Review

Ensuring sustainability and continuous improvement of the security program

Step 5: Documentation

  • **Record All Controls:** Document details of each implemented control, its purpose, and configuration.
  • **Assign Responsibilities:** Clearly define roles and responsibilities for operating and maintaining controls.
  • **Evidence of Effectiveness:** Maintain records and evidence demonstrating that controls are operating as intended.
  • **Audit Readiness:** Essential for internal management, external audits, and demonstrating compliance.

Example Scenario

An organization documents its data backup policy, including backup schedules, storage locations, and recovery procedures, to align with ISO 27002 A.12.3.1 (Information backup).

Step 6: Monitoring and Review

  • **Continuous Monitoring:** Establish processes for ongoing assessment of control effectiveness and performance.
  • **Performance Metrics:** Track key indicators to measure the success and efficiency of security controls.
  • **Regular Reviews:** Conduct periodic reviews to ensure the framework remains relevant to evolving threats and business needs.
  • **Adaptation:** Adjust controls and processes as necessary based on monitoring results and new risks.

Example Scenario

Monthly reviews of vulnerability scan reports and quarterly penetration tests are conducted to monitor the effectiveness of protective controls and identify new weaknesses.

Key Takeaway

Thorough documentation and continuous monitoring with regular reviews are vital for maintaining the effectiveness, relevance, and audit-readiness of a security program built on a framework.
