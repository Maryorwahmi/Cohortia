Risk Assessment and Control Selection

Prioritizing and implementing the right safeguards

Step 3: Risk Assessment

  • **Identify & Prioritize Risks:** Determine potential threats, vulnerabilities, and their impact on organizational assets.
  • **Quantify & Qualify:** Assess risks based on likelihood and consequence to prioritize mitigation efforts.
  • **Inform Control Decisions:** Use risk assessment results to guide the selection of appropriate security controls.

Example Scenario

A risk assessment identifies unpatched legacy systems as a high vulnerability, leading to a high risk of exploitation and data compromise.

Step 4: Control Selection and Implementation

  • **Select Controls:** Choose specific controls from the framework that address identified risks and gaps.
  • **Develop & Deploy:** Create new policies, deploy technologies, or enhance existing processes.
  • **Proportionate Measures:** Ensure controls are appropriate for the risk level, avoiding unnecessary complexity or cost.

Example Scenario

To mitigate the risk of unpatched systems, the organization implements a robust patch management policy and automated patching tools, aligning with ISO 27002 A.12.6.1 (Management of technical vulnerabilities).

Key Takeaway

A thorough risk assessment is fundamental to selecting and implementing effective security controls that are proportionate to identified threats and vulnerabilities, ensuring targeted mitigation.
