Key Concepts

Reinforcing Your Understanding

Risk Reassessment

Periodically reviewing and updating the identification, analysis, and evaluation of risks to reflect changes in the environment.

Control Optimization

Adjusting or enhancing existing security controls to improve their effectiveness, efficiency, or alignment with current risks.

Security Culture

The shared attitudes, beliefs, and behaviors of an organization's employees regarding information security.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management requires continuous reassessment, optimization of controls, and a strong security culture to adapt to evolving threats and business needs.
