Module 6›Chapter 5.7›Risk Assessments
Key Activity

Periodically Re-evaluating the Risk Landscape

Comprehensive Reviews for Emerging Threats and Vulnerabilities

Scheduled Reviews

Conduct assessments annually, semi-annually, or at defined intervals to maintain currency.

Triggered by Change

Re-evaluate after significant events like new technologies, system upgrades, or business process changes.

Comprehensive Scope

Go beyond existing risks to identify and analyze emerging threats and vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaway

Risk assessments are not static; they require periodic, comprehensive re-evaluation to ensure the organization's risk posture remains current and aligned with its risk appetite.

Example Scenario

After migrating to a new cloud provider, a comprehensive risk reassessment is conducted to identify new data residency risks, access control vulnerabilities, and compliance implications specific to the new environment.

Pro-Tip

Integrate risk assessment triggers into your change management process to ensure security implications are always considered before new initiatives are fully deployed.
