Security Implications of Organizational Evolution

Evaluating Risks from New Technologies and Business Shifts

New Technologies

Assess security implications of adopting cloud services, AI, IoT, or other emerging platforms.

System Upgrades

Evaluate security impacts of major software or hardware upgrades, ensuring no new vulnerabilities are introduced.

Business Process Changes

Analyze new workflows, mergers, or acquisitions for potential security risks and compliance challenges.

Key Takeaway

Every significant organizational change, from new technology adoption to business mergers, can introduce new risks or alter existing risk profiles, necessitating proactive security assessment.

Example Scenario

A company acquires a smaller startup. A security assessment during due diligence reveals the startup uses outdated software with known vulnerabilities, requiring a remediation plan before full integration.

Pro-Tip

Embed security reviews into your organization's project management and change control processes to ensure risks are identified and addressed early in the lifecycle.
