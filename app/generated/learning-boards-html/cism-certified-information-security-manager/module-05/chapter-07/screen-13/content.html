Strategic Approach

Doing Smarter Security, Not Just More

Prioritizing Resources and Managing Change Effectively

Optimize Resources

Focus on making existing security efforts more effective and efficient, rather than just adding more.

Prioritize by Risk

Allocate security investments and efforts based on current, prioritized risks and their potential impact.

Formal Change Management

Ensure improvement initiatives are properly funded and managed through formal change processes to avoid new vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaway

Continuous improvement is about optimizing resources and strategic prioritization, ensuring security efforts are effective and efficient, rather than simply increasing security activities.

Example Scenario

Instead of buying a new security tool, an organization invests in advanced training for its existing security team, optimizing their skills to better leverage current technologies and improve threat detection.

Common Misconception

Believing that more security tools or controls automatically equate to better security. True improvement comes from strategic optimization and effective management of existing and new resources.
