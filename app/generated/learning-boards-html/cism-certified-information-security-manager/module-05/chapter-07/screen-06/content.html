Module 6›Chapter 5.7›Control Reviews
Key Activity

Ensuring Controls Remain Effective

Auditing and Testing Security Measures

Technical Testing

Includes penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, and security configuration audits to find weaknesses.

Administrative Reviews

Focuses on policy compliance, access reviews, and security awareness program effectiveness.

Continuous Validation

Ensures controls are not just present but are functioning as intended and providing the expected protection.

Key Takeaway

Implemented controls must be continuously verified through auditing and testing to ensure they remain effective and provide the intended level of protection against current threats.

Example Scenario

A quarterly vulnerability scan identifies a critical patch missing on several production servers, indicating a control failure in the patch management process that needs immediate remediation.

Common Mistake

Assuming controls are effective simply because they are implemented. Without regular testing, their true effectiveness can degrade unnoticed, creating blind spots in your defense.
