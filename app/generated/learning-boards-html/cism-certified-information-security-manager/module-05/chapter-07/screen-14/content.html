Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Continuous Improvement

Continuous Improvement

The ongoing effort to enhance products, services, or processes; in risk management, it means continually refining the risk program.

Lessons Learned

The process of analyzing past incidents, audits, or control failures to identify root causes and implement corrective actions for future improvement.

Evolving Threat Landscape

The dynamic nature of cyber threats, including new attack vectors, malware, and adversary tactics, requiring constant adaptation of defenses.

Summary Takeaway

Continuous improvement is driven by a commitment to learning from experience and adapting proactively to the ever-changing world of cyber threats.
