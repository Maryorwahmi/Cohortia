Understanding Risk Treatment Strategies

Navigating the Four Pillars of Information Security Risk Management

Effective information security management is not just about identifying risks; it's fundamentally about deciding how to deal with them. This chapter explores the four primary strategies for treating risks: avoid, transfer, mitigate, and accept, each with distinct implications for an organization's resources, operations, and overall risk posture.

Key Idea

Proactive risk management is essential for business resilience, requiring informed decisions on how to manage identified threats.
