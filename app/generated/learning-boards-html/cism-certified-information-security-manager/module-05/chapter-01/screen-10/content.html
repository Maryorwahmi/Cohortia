MITIGATION CONSIDERATIONS

Effective Control Selection

Balancing effectiveness, feasibility, and cost-efficiency in mitigation efforts.

Effectiveness

How well the control reduces the likelihood or impact of the identified risk. Must provide tangible protection.

Risk Reduction

Feasibility

The practicality of implementing the control within the organization's existing infrastructure, resources, and capabilities.

Implementation Reality

Cost-Efficiency

Ensuring the cost of implementing and maintaining the control is justified by the reduction in risk and potential impact.

ROI for Security

Example: Cost-Benefit Analysis

Implementing a costly, complex data loss prevention (DLP) solution might be highly effective, but if the data it protects has minimal business impact, a simpler, more cost-efficient control might be preferred.

Strategic Investment
Key Takeaway

CISM professionals must perform a thorough cost-benefit analysis to select controls that are effective, feasible, and financially justifiable.
