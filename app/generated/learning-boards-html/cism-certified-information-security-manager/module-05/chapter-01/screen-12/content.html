ACCEPTANCE CONSIDERATIONS

Formalizing Risk Acceptance

The importance of documented, approved, and regularly reviewed risk acceptance.

Formal Documentation

Acceptance must be a deliberate, informed decision, formally documented and approved by appropriate management levels (e.g., senior leadership, board).

Transparency & Accountability

Example: Board Approval

A decision to accept the risk of a non-critical system's vulnerability due to high patching costs should be formally approved by the board, not just IT.

Governance in Action

Safety Note: Informal Acceptance

Informal acceptance (simply doing nothing) is a dangerous practice that can lead to significant unmanaged exposure and potential liability.

Avoid Negligence
Key Takeaway

Accepted risks must be regularly reviewed to ensure they remain within acceptable parameters as business conditions and threats evolve.
