AVOIDANCE CONSIDERATIONS

Weighing Avoidance: Opportunities and Oversight

Understanding the trade-offs and common mistakes in risk avoidance.

Common Mistake

Avoiding a risk without fully understanding the business impact of that avoidance. This can lead to unforeseen negative consequences.

Lack of BIA

Example: Lost Opportunity

Avoiding a new market entry due to perceived high security risks might mean losing out on significant revenue growth, if those risks could have been managed differently.

Strategic Impact

Safety Notes

Ensure the alternative path chosen does not introduce new, unforeseen risks or significantly impair business operations.

  • Conduct a thorough BIA.
  • Prevent new risks from emerging.
Key Takeaway

Avoidance decisions must be supported by a comprehensive BIA to prevent unintended negative consequences and ensure business continuity.
