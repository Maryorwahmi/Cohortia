TRANSFER CONSIDERATIONS

The Unoutsourcable Responsibility

Why ultimate accountability remains with the organization, even when risk is transferred.

CISM Principle

You can outsource the function, but you cannot outsource the responsibility. Ultimate accountability for risk remains with the original organization.

Core Accountability

Example: Data Breach

Even with cyber insurance, an organization is still responsible for customer trust, regulatory compliance, and reputational damage after a data breach.

Reputational Risk

Common Mistake

Assuming that transferring a service means transferring all associated risk, neglecting the need for vendor risk management and oversight.

Vendor Oversight Gap
Key Takeaway

CISM professionals must meticulously review contracts and SLAs to ensure clear responsibilities and adequate coverage when transferring risk.
