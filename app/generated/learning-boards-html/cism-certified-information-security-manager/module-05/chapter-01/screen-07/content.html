Risk Transfer: Sharing the Burden

Shifting the financial or operational impact of a risk to a third party.

What is Transfer?

Risk transfer involves shifting the financial or operational impact of a risk to a third party, also known as risk sharing.

  • Shift financial burden.
  • Shift operational impact.

Example: Cyber Insurance

Purchasing cyber insurance transfers the financial burden of a security incident, like a data breach, to an insurer, covering potential losses.

Financial Protection

Methods of Transfer

Common methods include insurance policies for financial impact and outsourcing IT functions to vendors for operational responsibilities.

  • Cyber insurance.
  • Third-party outsourcing.
Key Takeaway

Risk transfer shifts impact but not ultimate accountability; organizations must carefully manage vendor contracts and insurance policies.
