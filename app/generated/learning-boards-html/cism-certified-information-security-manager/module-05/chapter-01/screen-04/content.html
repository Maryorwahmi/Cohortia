CORE CONCEPT

The Four Pillars of Risk Treatment

Deciding how to manage identified information security risks.

Avoid

Eliminate the risk entirely by ceasing or refraining from the activity that gives rise to it. This is the most definitive strategy.

Eliminate Source

Transfer

Shift the financial or operational impact of a risk to a third party, typically through insurance or contractual agreements.

Share Impact

Mitigate

Implement controls to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk to an acceptable level. This is often the most common strategy.

Reduce Risk

Accept

Consciously decide to take no action to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk, typically when costs outweigh benefits.

Conscious Decision
Key Takeaway

Each risk treatment strategy offers a distinct approach to managing identified risks, requiring careful consideration of organizational context and objectives.
