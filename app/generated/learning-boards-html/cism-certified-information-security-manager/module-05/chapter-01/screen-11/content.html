Risk Acceptance: A Deliberate Choice

Consciously deciding to take no action against a risk.

What is Acceptance?

Risk acceptance is a conscious decision to take no action to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk.

  • Deliberate decision.
  • Formally documented.

Example: Legacy Application

A small, non-critical legacy application with a known, expensive-to-patch vulnerability and minimal business impact might be formally accepted.

Cost-Benefit Driven

When to Accept

Typically adopted when mitigation/transfer costs outweigh potential impact, or when risk is within the organization's defined risk appetite.

  • Cost vs. impact.
  • Within risk appetite.
Key Takeaway

Risk acceptance is a formal, informed decision, not an act of negligence, and must be documented and approved by management.
