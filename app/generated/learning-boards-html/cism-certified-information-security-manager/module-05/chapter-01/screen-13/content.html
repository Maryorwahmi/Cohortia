CISM DECISION FRAMEWORK

Selecting the Optimal Risk Treatment Strategy

A holistic approach considering cost, impact, appetite, and compliance.

Key Decision Factors

The choice involves a complex cost-benefit analysis, considering multiple organizational and risk-specific factors.

  • Risk likelihood and impact.
  • Cost of controls.
  • Organizational risk appetite.
  • Regulatory compliance.

Example: High-Impact Risk

A high-likelihood, very high-impact data breach risk might necessitate avoidance or robust mitigation, even if costly, to protect critical assets and reputation.

Prioritizing Criticality

CISM's Role

Facilitate decision-making, provide clear information to stakeholders, and ensure strategies are documented in a risk register and communicated effectively.

  • Inform stakeholders.
  • Document decisions.
  • Communicate widely.
Key Takeaway

Risk treatment is an integral part of information security governance, requiring a holistic approach aligned with business objectives.
