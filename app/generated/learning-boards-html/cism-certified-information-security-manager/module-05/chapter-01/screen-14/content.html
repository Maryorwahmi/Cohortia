CHAPTER RECAP

Key Concepts

Core definitions from this chapter.

Risk Avoidance

Eliminating a specific risk by ceasing or refraining from the activity that gives rise to it.

Risk Transfer

Shifting the financial or operational impact of a risk to a third party, often through insurance or contractual agreements.

Risk Mitigation

Implementing controls and safeguards to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk to an acceptable level.

Summary Takeaway

These three strategies provide distinct approaches to managing risks, from complete elimination to reducing their impact or shifting their burden.
