Key Concepts

Essential terms for CISM professionals.

Risk Acceptance

A deliberate, informed decision to take no action to reduce a risk, typically because treatment costs outweigh potential impact or the risk is within acceptable tolerance.

Risk Appetite

The amount and type of risk that an organization is willing to pursue or retain in the pursuit of its objectives.

Risk Tolerance

The acceptable deviation from the risk appetite, typically expressed in specific metrics or thresholds.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding risk acceptance, appetite, and tolerance is crucial for aligning risk management with strategic business objectives.
