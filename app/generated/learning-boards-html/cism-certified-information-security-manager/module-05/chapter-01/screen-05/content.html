Risk Avoidance: Eliminating the Threat

Completely eliminating the source of a specific risk.

What is Avoidance?

Risk avoidance is the most definitive strategy, involving the elimination of the risk entirely by removing its source.

  • Cease the risky activity.
  • Change processes to remove risk.
  • Refrain from risky ventures.

Example: Cloud Service Risk

If a new cloud service introduces unacceptable data residency risks that cannot be mitigated, an organization might choose to avoid using that specific service altogether.

Compliance Focus

Implications

While effective at eliminating specific risks, avoidance can sometimes mean foregoing potential business opportunities or operational efficiencies.

  • Eliminates specific risks.
  • May sacrifice opportunities.
Key Takeaway

Risk avoidance is a powerful strategy for eliminating threats, but requires a thorough business impact analysis to weigh costs against benefits.
