Risk Mitigation: Reducing Likelihood and Impact

Implementing controls to reduce risk to an acceptable level.

What is Mitigation?

Risk mitigation involves implementing controls to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk to an acceptable level, bringing it within risk tolerance.

  • Reduce likelihood.
  • Reduce impact.

Example: Multi-Factor Authentication

Implementing MFA for all remote access significantly mitigates the risk of unauthorized access due to compromised credentials, reducing impact.

Access Control

Types of Controls

Mitigation involves a blend of technical (firewalls), administrative (policies), and physical (access badges) controls.

  • Technical controls.
  • Administrative controls.
  • Physical controls.
Key Takeaway

Mitigation is a continuous process of implementing and optimizing controls to keep risks within the organization's acceptable tolerance levels.
