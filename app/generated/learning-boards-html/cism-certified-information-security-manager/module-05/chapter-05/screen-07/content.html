Early Warning System
Identifying Potential Threats with Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
Forward-looking metrics that signal increasing risk exposure

KRIs are metrics that provide an early warning of increasing risk exposure. They are forward-looking and help predict potential future problems, enabling proactive intervention.

Why it Matters

Proactive monitoring of KRIs allows security teams to address vulnerabilities and emerging threats before they can be exploited, reducing potential impact.

Example Scenario

A sudden increase in failed login attempts (KRI) triggers an alert, prompting the SOC to investigate for potential brute-force attacks.

Key Takeaway

KRIs are essential for anticipating and mitigating risks, shifting security from reactive to proactive.

Common Key Risk Indicators
Unpatched Critical Vulnerabilities (over 30 days)
Increase in Failed Login Attempts
Employees Failing Phishing Simulations
Average Time to Detect Security Incident
Systems Not Meeting Baseline Security Configs
