Chapter Review

Key Concepts: Reporting & Automation

Summarizing the tools and processes for effective risk communication.

Risk Reporting

Communicating an organization's risk posture, control effectiveness, and security incidents to stakeholders.

SIEM

System that aggregates and analyzes security logs and events for real-time threat detection and incident management.

GRC Platform

Software that helps organizations manage governance, enterprise risk management, and corporate compliance.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk reporting, powered by SIEM and GRC tools, ensures transparent communication and robust compliance management.
