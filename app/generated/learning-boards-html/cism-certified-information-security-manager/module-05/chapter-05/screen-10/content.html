Reporting Strategies

Crafting Effective Reporting Formats and Frequencies

Matching report style and cadence to stakeholder requirements for maximum impact.

Formats for Leadership
  • Dashboards & Scorecards: Visual, high-level trends.
  • Executive Summaries: Concise narratives, business impact.
Formats for Technical Teams
  • Detailed Logs: Raw event data for analysis.
  • Vulnerability Reports: Specific findings for remediation.
  • Incident Tickets: Actionable items for response.
Key Takeaway

Tailored formats and appropriate frequencies ensure risk information is consumed effectively by its intended audience.

Frequency for Technical & Operational
  • Real-time Alerts: For immediate incident response.
  • Daily/Weekly Reports: For ongoing monitoring and operational adjustments.
Frequency for Senior Leadership & Board
  • Monthly/Quarterly Reports: For strategic reviews and executive oversight.
  • Annual Reports: For long-term governance and compliance.
Common Mistake

Using a single, generic report for all audiences leads to information overload for some and insufficient detail for others.
