Core Concept
The Continuous Cycle of Risk Management
Beyond implementation: why ongoing vigilance is essential

Risk management is not a one-time event; it's a continuous cycle. Once risks are treated and controls implemented, ongoing monitoring and reporting are critical to ensure effectiveness and adapt to changes.

Why it Matters

Controls can degrade, new threats emerge, and the risk landscape shifts. Continuous monitoring ensures security remains effective and relevant.

Real-world Connection

Just as a car needs regular maintenance and checks, an organization's security posture requires continuous assessment to prevent failures.

Key Takeaway

Ongoing monitoring and reporting are indispensable for maintaining a resilient and adaptive security program.

The Risk Management Lifecycle
1. Identify & Assess Risks
2. Treat Risks & Implement Controls
3. Monitor & Report
4. Review & Adapt
