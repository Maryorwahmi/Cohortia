Chapter Review

Key Concepts: Monitoring Fundamentals

Reinforcing the core ideas behind continuous risk oversight.

Continuous Monitoring

Ongoing observation and assessment of security controls and the risk environment to ensure effectiveness.

Key Risk Indicator (KRI)

A metric providing an early warning of increasing risk exposure, typically forward-looking.

Key Performance Indicator (KPI)

A metric measuring the effectiveness and efficiency of security controls and processes, typically backward-looking.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management relies on continuous monitoring, using KRIs to predict future risks and KPIs to assess past performance.
