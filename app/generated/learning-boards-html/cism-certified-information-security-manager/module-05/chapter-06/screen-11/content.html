BCP Component

BCP: Strategy, Plan Development, and Testing

From Objectives to Actionable Recovery

2. Strategy Development

Determine recovery strategies (e.g., hot sites, warm sites, cloud failover, manual workarounds) based on BIA.

3. Plan Development

Document detailed procedures for activating the plan, recovering systems, and resuming operations.

4. Testing and Exercise

Regularly test the BCP through tabletop exercises, simulations, and full-scale drills to identify gaps and train personnel.

BCP Lifecycle: Plan, Test, Maintain

Document Procedures

Effective BCP requires continuous strategy development, detailed planning, and rigorous testing.
Key Takeaway

BCP's success hinges on well-defined strategies, meticulously documented plans, and a commitment to continuous testing and refinement to ensure operational readiness.
