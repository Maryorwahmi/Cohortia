IRP: Recovery and Post-Incident Activity

Restoring Operations and Learning from Experience

5. Recovery

Restore affected systems and data to normal operation, often involving backups and system rebuilds.

6. Post-Incident Activity (Lessons Learned)

Document the incident, analyze what went wrong, identify improvements, and update risk assessments.

The Cycle of Improvement
Restore from Backups
Verify System Integrity
Document Incident
Identify Improvements
Recovery restores operations, while post-incident review drives continuous security improvement.
Key Takeaway

The recovery phase restores normalcy, but the post-incident 'lessons learned' phase is crucial for strengthening defenses and preventing future incidents through continuous improvement.
