BCP: Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identifying Critical Functions and Recovery Objectives

1. Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identifies critical business functions, their dependencies, and recovery objectives (MTD, RTO, RPO).

Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)

The maximum period a business process can be inoperative before unacceptable consequences occur.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The target time within which a business process must be restored after a disaster to avoid unacceptable consequences.

BIA: Prioritizing Recovery
Identify Critical Functions
Determine MTD & RTO
Prioritize Recovery Efforts
The BIA provides the data-driven foundation for all BCP strategies.
Key Takeaway

The BIA is the critical first step in BCP, providing the data-driven insights needed to prioritize recovery efforts and align them with the organization's most vital functions.
