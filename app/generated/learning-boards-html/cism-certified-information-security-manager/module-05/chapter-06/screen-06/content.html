IRP: Preparation and Detection

Building the Foundation for a Swift Response

1. Preparation

Establish IR team, define roles, develop policies, acquire tools (forensic workstations, SIEM), and conduct training.

2. Detection & Analysis

Monitor systems for anomalies and IoCs, then analyze incidents to determine scope, nature, and impact.

Key Takeaway

Robust preparation and continuous, intelligent detection are the cornerstones of an effective IRP, enabling rapid identification and understanding of security incidents.
