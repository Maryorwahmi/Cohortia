Core Concept

The Foundation of Resilience

Risk Management, IR, and BCP as a Unified Strategy

Risk Management: The Starting Point

Identifies and assesses potential threats and vulnerabilities, setting the stage for proactive planning.

Incident Response (IR): Immediate Reaction

Focuses on preparing for, detecting, and recovering from security incidents.

Business Continuity Planning (BCP): Sustained Operations

Ensures critical business functions continue during and after disruptive events.

The Resilience Cycle
Risk management informs IR and BCP, which in turn provide feedback for continuous improvement.
Key Takeaway

Integrating risk management, IR, and BCP creates a holistic defense, ensuring proactive preparation and effective recovery from any disruptive event.
