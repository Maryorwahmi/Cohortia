Integration

The Symbiotic Relationship

How Risk Management, IR, and BCP Work Together

Risk Management's Role

Identifies threats, assesses impact, and guides mitigation controls.

IR's Role

Manages immediate crisis when controls fail, containing damage and initiating recovery.

BCP's Role

Ensures critical operations continue if an incident causes significant disruption.

Feedback Loop

'Lessons learned' from IR feed back into risk assessment, improving controls and plans.

The Integrated Resilience Framework
A continuous cycle of assessment, response, and recovery ensures robust organizational resilience.
Key Takeaway

The symbiotic integration of risk management, IR, and BCP is crucial for creating a cohesive, continuously improving framework that ensures organizational resilience against all threats.
