Understanding Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

Maintaining Operations During Disruptive Events

What is BCP?

The process of creating systems of prevention and recovery to deal with potential threats to a company, ensuring critical functions continue.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A core component of BCP that identifies critical business processes, dependencies, Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD), and Recovery Time Objectives (RTO).

Why it Matters

Prioritizes recovery efforts and allocates resources effectively, ensuring essential services are maintained or quickly resumed.

BCP: From Disruption to Resumption
Disruptive Event
BIA Conducted
BCP Activated
Operations Resumed
BCP ensures that critical business functions can continue or quickly resume after any disruption.
Key Takeaway

BCP is essential for organizational survival, ensuring that critical business functions can be maintained or rapidly restored following any significant disruptive event.
