Key Concepts: Recovery Objectives

Metrics for Business Resilience

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable duration a system can be down after a disruption.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable amount of data that can be lost from an IT service due to an incident.

Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

A documented process to recover and protect a business's IT infrastructure in the event of a disaster.

Summary Takeaway

RTO, RPO, and DRP are critical metrics and plans that define recovery targets and procedures, ensuring effective restoration of IT services and data.
