IRP Component

IRP: Containment and Eradication

Stopping the Spread and Removing the Threat

3. Containment

Limit the spread and impact of an incident by isolating affected systems or blocking malicious IPs.

4. Eradication

Remove the root cause of the incident, such as patching vulnerabilities, removing malware, or disabling compromised accounts.

Containment to Eradication Flow
Isolate Affected Systems
Block Malicious IPs
Remove Malware
Patch Vulnerabilities
Containment and eradication are critical steps to stop an incident and remove its cause.
Key Takeaway

Effective containment prevents incident escalation, while thorough eradication ensures the complete removal of threats and their underlying causes, preventing recurrence.
