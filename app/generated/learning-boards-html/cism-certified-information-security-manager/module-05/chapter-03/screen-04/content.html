Core Concept

The Foundation of Information Security

Risk mitigation is the cornerstone of information security management, involving the implementation of various controls and techniques to reduce the likelihood or impact of identified risks to an acceptable level.

Key Principle

Unlike avoidance or transfer, mitigation directly addresses the risk by putting safeguards in place.

CISM's Role

A CISM must understand control types, how they function, and how to select and implement them effectively.

Goal

Achieve a balance between security, functionality, and cost, ensuring controls are proportionate to the risk.

Risk Mitigation Process
1. Identify Risks
2. Assess Likelihood & Impact
3. Implement Controls (Mitigation)
4. Monitor & Review
Common Misconception

Mitigation is not avoidance or transfer; it's actively reducing risk through safeguards.
