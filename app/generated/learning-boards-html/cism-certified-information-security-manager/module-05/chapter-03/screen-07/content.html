Security Control Type

Protecting the Environment: Physical Controls

Physical controls are tangible measures designed to protect physical assets, facilities, and the environment where information systems reside, preventing unauthorized access and environmental hazards.

Core Purpose

Prevent unauthorized physical access, theft, damage, and environmental hazards that could impact information.

Holistic Protection

They safeguard the very foundation of your digital operations, complementing administrative and technical controls.

Examples of Physical Controls
Security Guards
Surveillance Cameras (CCTV)
Locked Doors & Access Badges
Biometric Scanners
Key Takeaway

Physical controls are essential for securing the tangible environment, preventing physical access and protecting against environmental threats.
