Choosing the Right Controls

When selecting and implementing mitigation controls, a CISM must consider several factors to ensure they are effective, practical, and aligned with organizational goals.

Strategic Alignment

Controls must align with business objectives, resources, and regulatory requirements.

Cost-Benefit Analysis

A thorough analysis is essential to justify investment and ensure controls are proportionate to risk.

Feasibility

Key Takeaway

Strategic control selection balances effectiveness, practicality, cost, compliance, and seamless integration to optimize security posture.
