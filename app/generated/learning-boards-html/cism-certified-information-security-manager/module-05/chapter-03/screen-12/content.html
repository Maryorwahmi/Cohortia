Avoiding Common Pitfalls in Control Implementation

Security controls are not static; they require continuous monitoring, maintenance, and periodic review to remain effective against evolving threats.

Pitfall: 'Set It and Forget It'

Controls need regular updates, log reviews, and performance tuning to stay effective.

Pitfall: Isolated Implementation

Implementing controls without considering their impact on other systems can lead to operational friction or new vulnerabilities.
