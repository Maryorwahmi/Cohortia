Establishing the Rules: Administrative Controls

Administrative controls are the policies, procedures, standards, and guidelines that define how people should act and how security should be managed within an organization.

Foundational Role

They establish the framework for secure operations and dictate expected behavior.

Why it Matters

Even advanced technical controls can be undermined by a lack of clear administrative guidance or poor user practices.

Examples of Administrative Controls
Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
Password Policy
Security Awareness Training
Incident Response Procedures
Key Takeaway

Administrative controls are foundational, establishing the 'rules of the road' for secure operations and human behavior.
