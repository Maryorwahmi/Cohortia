Mastering Risk Management

Strategic Principles to Remember

Defense-in-Depth

A layered security strategy employing multiple, overlapping controls for redundant protection.

Risk Mitigation

The process of reducing the likelihood or impact of a risk through control implementation.

Control Effectiveness

The degree to which a control achieves its intended purpose in reducing risk.

Summary Takeaway

These interconnected principles guide CISMs in building and maintaining a robust, adaptive, and resilient information security program.
