Key Concepts

Strategic responses to the quantum era

Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)

New cryptographic algorithms designed to be secure against both classical and quantum attacks.

Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computers (CRQC)

Hypothetical quantum computers powerful enough to break current public-key cryptography.

NIST PQC Standardization

The ongoing global effort to solicit, evaluate, and standardize quantum-resistant cryptographic algorithms.

Summary Takeaway

PQC development and NIST's standardization efforts are critical for building a resilient cryptographic infrastructure against future quantum threats.
