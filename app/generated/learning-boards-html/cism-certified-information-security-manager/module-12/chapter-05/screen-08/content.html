Scenario

Malicious actors collect currently encrypted sensitive data (e.g., intellectual property, medical records) with long confidentiality requirements.

Risk

This data, secure today, could be compromised years or decades later when powerful quantum computers become available.

Key Takeaway

Encrypted data harvested today can be decrypted later by future quantum computers, threatening long-term confidentiality.
