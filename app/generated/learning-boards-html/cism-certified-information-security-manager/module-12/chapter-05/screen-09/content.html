Quantum Risk Assessment: Cryptographic Inventory

Identifying and cataloging cryptographic assets

01

Identify Systems

Locate all systems, applications, and protocols using encryption, digital signatures, or hashing.

02

Document Algorithms

Record specific algorithms (e.g., RSA-2048, ECC P-256, AES-256) and their key lengths.

03

Assess Data Sensitivity

Determine the sensitivity and required confidentiality lifespan of the data protected by each algorithm.

04

Prioritize Assets

Use the inventory to prioritize which systems and data require the earliest migration to PQC solutions.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive cryptographic inventory is essential to identify quantum-vulnerable assets and prioritize protection efforts.
