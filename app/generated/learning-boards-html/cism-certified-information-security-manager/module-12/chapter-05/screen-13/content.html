PQC Transition: Challenges and CISM Role

Navigating complexities and advocating for readiness

Common Challenges
  • Underestimating Complexity: Cryptography is deeply embedded, making changes non-trivial.
  • Delaying Planning: Waiting until CRQCs are imminent leaves organizations exposed.
  • Vendor Engagement: Failure to engage with vendors leads to supply chain risks and compatibility issues.
CISM Responsibilities
  • Advocate Early Planning: Secure budget and resources for the long-term endeavor.
  • Educate Management: Articulate the existential threat and strategic imperative of PQC.
  • Lead Proactive Measures: Ensure the organization builds a resilient, quantum-resistant security posture.
Key Takeaway

CISMs must lead PQC transition, addressing complexities, engaging vendors, and educating management to ensure future security.
