Understand Quantum Principles

Grasp the fundamental concepts of quantum computing, including qubits, superposition, and entanglement. This knowledge is crucial for comprehending its disruptive potential.

Identify Vulnerable Algorithms

Pinpoint specific cryptographic algorithms, like RSA and ECC, that are susceptible to quantum attacks from algorithms such as Shor's and Grover's.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Explain PQC & Standardization

Articulate the concept of Post-Quantum Cryptography and the critical role of standardization efforts by bodies like NIST in developing new secure algorithms.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Assess Quantum Risk

Formulate a CISM-level strategy for assessing quantum risk, including cryptographic inventory and identifying data with long confidentiality requirements.
