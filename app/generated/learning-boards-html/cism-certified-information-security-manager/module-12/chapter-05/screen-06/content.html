Shor's Algorithm: The RSA/ECC Threat

How quantum factoring undermines public-key encryption

Shor's Algorithm

Efficiently Factors Large Numbers

A quantum algorithm that can quickly find the prime factors of very large composite numbers.

Breaks RSA

Relies on factoring large numbers

Breaks ECC

Relies on elliptic curve discrete logarithm problem

Impact: Compromises secure web browsing (TLS), digital certificates, and cryptocurrency transactions.

Key Takeaway

Shor's algorithm efficiently factors large numbers, directly compromising RSA and ECC public-key cryptography.
