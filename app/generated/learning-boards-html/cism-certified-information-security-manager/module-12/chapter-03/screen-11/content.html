How a 51% Attack Unfolds

Step 1: Attacker Gains Majority Control

Acquires >50% of network's hash rate (PoW) or stake (PoS).

Step 2: Private Chain Mining

Attacker mines a private version of the blockchain, excluding or reversing transactions.

Step 3: Public Chain Overwrite

Attacker releases their longer private chain, which the network accepts as legitimate.

Step 4: Malicious Actions Executed

Double-spending, transaction censorship, or reversal of confirmed transactions occur.

Double-Spending

Funds are spent, then the transaction is reversed, allowing the funds to be spent again.

Transaction Censorship

Attacker can prevent specific transactions from being confirmed and added to the ledger.

Key Takeaway

A 51% attack allows an attacker to rewrite history, leading to double-spending and undermining trust in the DLT.
