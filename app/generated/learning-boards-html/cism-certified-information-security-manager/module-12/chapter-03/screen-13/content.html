Key CISM Responsibilities for DLT Security

Comprehensive Risk Assessments

Evaluate DLT platforms, consensus mechanisms, and smart contract security against organizational risk appetite.

Identity & Access Management (IAM)

Implement robust controls for participant identity and access, especially in private/permissioned DLTs.

Regulatory Compliance

Ensure DLT solutions comply with data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR) and industry-specific standards.

Incident Response Planning

Develop specific plans for DLT-related threats, including smart contract exploits and 51% attacks.

Key Takeaway

CISMs are pivotal in establishing a holistic security framework for DLT adoption, covering technical, governance, and compliance aspects.
