What is a 51% Attack?

Definition

A hypothetical attack where a single entity or group controls more than 50% of a blockchain network's computing power (PoW) or stake (PoS).

Risk

Allows the attacker to potentially reverse transactions, double-spend, or prevent new transactions from being confirmed.

Key Takeaway

A 51% attack undermines the integrity of the ledger by allowing a majority controller to manipulate transaction history.

Consensus Mechanisms Overview

PoW

Proof of Work: Nodes compete to solve complex puzzles to validate blocks (e.g., Bitcoin).

PoS

Proof of Stake: Validators are chosen based on the amount of cryptocurrency they 'stake' (e.g., Ethereum 2.0).

Other

Delegated PoS, Proof of Authority: Variations with different decentralization and security trade-offs.

Common Misconception

That 51% attacks are only theoretical. While rare on large chains, they have occurred on smaller DLTs.
