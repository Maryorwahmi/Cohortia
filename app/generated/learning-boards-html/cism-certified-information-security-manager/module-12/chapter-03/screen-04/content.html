Immutability: The Unchanging Record

Definition

Once data is recorded on the ledger, it cannot be altered or deleted, ensuring data integrity.

Benefit

Provides a high degree of data integrity, auditability, and a reliable audit trail for critical transactions.

Key Takeaway

Immutability is foundational for trust, ensuring records are permanent and verifiable.

Decentralization: Distributed Resilience

Definition

Control and decision-making are distributed across a network of independent nodes.

Benefit

Eliminates single points of failure, making the system highly resistant to censorship and tampering.

Key Takeaway

Decentralization enhances resilience, making DLT robust against attacks and control by a single entity.
