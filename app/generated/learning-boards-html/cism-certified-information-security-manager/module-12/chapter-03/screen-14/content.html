Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Immutability

The property where once data is recorded on a blockchain, it cannot be altered or deleted, ensuring strong data integrity.

Decentralization

The distribution of control and decision-making across a network, eliminating single points of failure and increasing resilience.

Smart Contracts

Self-executing agreements with terms written directly into code on a blockchain, vulnerable to coding errors.

Summary Takeaway

DLT's core strengths of immutability and decentralization are powerful, but smart contracts introduce new, critical security considerations.
