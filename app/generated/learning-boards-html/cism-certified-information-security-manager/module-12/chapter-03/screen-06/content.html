Decentralized Network Architecture

No Single Point of Failure

Network continues operation even if individual nodes fail or are attacked.

Censorship Resistance

No central authority can easily block transactions or shut down the network.

Tampering Resistance

Requires control over a majority of the network, making manipulation difficult.

Key Takeaway

Decentralization provides robust resilience against attacks and ensures continuous operation.
