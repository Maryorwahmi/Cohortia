Key Concepts

Expanding your AI/ML security vocabulary

Explainable AI (XAI)

A set of tools and techniques that allow humans to understand the output of AI models, crucial for auditing, debugging, and regulatory compliance.

AI Governance

The framework of policies, processes, and responsibilities established to ensure the ethical, secure, and compliant development and deployment of AI systems.

Prompt Injection

A specific type of attack against large language models (LLMs) where malicious input is crafted to manipulate the model's behavior or extract sensitive information.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts are vital for establishing robust governance, ensuring transparency, and addressing emerging threats in AI/ML deployments.
