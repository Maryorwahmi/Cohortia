Model Inversion: Revealing Training Data

Reconstructing sensitive information from deployed AI models

The Inversion Process

An attacker repeatedly sends queries to a deployed AI model and analyzes its outputs. By observing the model's responses, they can infer characteristics of the data it was trained on.

Significant Privacy Risks

This attack poses a severe threat to data privacy, especially in domains like healthcare or finance where models are trained on highly sensitive personal information.

Example: Patient Data

A medical diagnostic AI, if subjected to model inversion, could potentially reveal specific patient attributes or even reconstruct parts of individual medical records from its training set.

Key Takeaway

Model inversion allows attackers to infer sensitive training data from deployed models, creating significant privacy and confidentiality risks.
