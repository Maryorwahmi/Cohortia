What is XAI?

Explainable AI (XAI) refers to methods and techniques that allow humans to understand, interpret, and trust the outputs and decisions made by machine learning models.

Crucial for Auditing & Debugging

By providing insights into a model's reasoning, XAI is essential for identifying biases, debugging errors, and conducting thorough security and performance audits.

Regulatory & Trust Imperative

XAI is increasingly required for compliance with regulations (e.g., GDPR's 'right to explanation') and is vital for building user trust in AI-driven systems, especially in critical applications.
