AI/ML: A New Security Paradigm

Understanding the fundamental shift in vulnerability and risk

Beyond Traditional Software

AI/ML systems are fundamentally different from traditional, rule-based software. Their behavior is learned from data, making them adaptive but also susceptible to data-centric attacks.

Data-Driven Vulnerabilities

The integrity of training data is paramount. Malicious data can introduce biases, inaccuracies, or backdoors into the model, leading to unpredictable and harmful outcomes.

Opaque Decision-Making

Many advanced AI models operate as 'black boxes,' making it difficult to understand their internal logic. This opacity complicates auditing, debugging, and detecting subtle manipulations.
