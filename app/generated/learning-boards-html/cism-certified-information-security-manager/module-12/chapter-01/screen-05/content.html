Data Poisoning: Corrupting the Source

How malicious data can undermine AI model integrity

The Attack Mechanism

Malicious actors inject corrupted, biased, or intentionally misleading data into the dataset used to train an AI model. This can happen during data collection or preprocessing.

Impact on Model Behavior

The poisoned data causes the model to learn incorrect patterns, leading to biased outputs, inaccurate predictions, or even designed failures in specific scenarios.

Real-World Example: Credit Risk

An AI system trained on poisoned data for credit risk assessment could unfairly deny loans to certain demographics or approve high-risk applicants, causing financial and reputational damage.

Key Takeaway

Data poisoning compromises AI models at their core, leading to biased or incorrect decisions with significant real-world consequences.
