Securing the Intelligent Frontier

Navigating the Security Implications of AI and Machine Learning

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are integral to modern business, but they introduce a novel set of security risks. This chapter will equip you with the knowledge to identify, understand, and mitigate these unique challenges, ensuring robust protection for your organization's intelligent systems.

Key Idea

AI/ML systems bring transformative power but also introduce complex, data-driven security risks that demand a specialized approach to protection and governance.

AI/ML Systems

Data-driven, opaque, and powerful.

Novel Security Risks

Data poisoning, adversarial attacks, model theft.
