Integrating Security into AI/ML Lifecycles

From design to continuous monitoring for resilient AI systems

Security by Design

Embed security considerations from the initial design phase of AI/ML systems, influencing data collection, model architecture, and deployment strategies.

AI-Specific Risk Assessments

Conduct regular, tailored risk assessments that account for unique AI/ML threats like data poisoning, adversarial examples, and model inversion.

Continuous Monitoring & Adaptation

Implement ongoing monitoring for performance drift, security anomalies, and emerging threats to ensure the long-term resilience and integrity of AI systems.

Key Takeaway

Proactive security integration throughout the AI/ML lifecycle, coupled with AI-specific risk assessments and continuous monitoring, is vital for resilient systems.
