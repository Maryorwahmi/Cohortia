Model Stealing: Undermining Innovation

Creating functional copies of proprietary AI models

The Replication Process

Attackers interact with a target AI model by sending numerous queries and observing its responses. This allows them to infer the model's logic and create a functionally similar copy.

Intellectual Property Theft

This attack directly undermines the intellectual property of organizations that invest heavily in developing unique AI models, potentially leading to competitive disadvantages.

Offline Vulnerability Probing

Once a model is stolen, attackers can analyze their copy offline to discover vulnerabilities without alerting the original system, making subsequent attacks more potent.
