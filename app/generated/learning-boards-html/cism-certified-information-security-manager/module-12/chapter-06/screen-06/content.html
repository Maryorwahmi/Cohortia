Key Takeaway

Automating security checks throughout the CI/CD pipeline is the technical foundation of DevSecOps, enabling continuous security validation and rapid feedback.

Why it Matters

Without automated security in CI/CD, security becomes a manual bottleneck, undermining the agility benefits of DevOps and increasing the risk of vulnerabilities reaching production.
