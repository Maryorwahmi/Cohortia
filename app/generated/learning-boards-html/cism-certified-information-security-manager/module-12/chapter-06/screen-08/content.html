Software Composition Analysis (SCA)

Managing Third-Party Risk in Modern Applications

What SCA Does

Identifies open-source components and their known vulnerabilities by scanning dependency files.

Why It's Essential

Manages third-party risk, prevents supply chain attacks, and ensures licensing compliance for open-source libraries.

Integration Point

Typically integrated into the build stage of the CI/CD pipeline.
