Core Concept

The CISM's Role in Incident Management

Leading the organization's capability to detect, respond, and recover.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and manage incident response policies and procedures.
  • Establish and empower the Incident Response Team (IRT).
  • Ensure adequate resources, training, and tools for incident handling.
  • Regularly review, update, and test the incident response plan.
Key Takeaway

The CISM provides strategic oversight and governance for incident management, empowering technical teams while focusing on business resilience and continuous improvement.

CISM's Focus vs. IRT's Focus

CISM: Managerial

Governance, Policy, Resources, Coordination, Strategy

IRT: Technical

Detection, Analysis, Containment, Eradication, Recovery

Pro-Tip

Regularly test your incident response plan through simulations and drills to identify gaps and ensure team readiness, a key CISM responsibility.
