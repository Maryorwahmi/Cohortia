Strategic Integration

Incident Management, BC, and DR

Ensuring seamless integration for comprehensive business resilience.

Interconnected Plans

  • Incident Response: Handles immediate cyberattacks.
  • Business Continuity (BC): Ensures critical functions during disruption.
  • Disaster Recovery (DR): Recovers IT infrastructure after severe events.
Key Takeaway

CISM ensures incident response plans are fully integrated with BC and DR strategies, creating a unified approach to organizational resilience.

Integration Points

Shared Risk Assessments

Identify common threats and impacts.

Coordinated Testing

Joint drills and simulations.

Unified Communication

Consistent messaging during crisis.

Common Mistake

Treating IR, BC, and DR as isolated plans, leading to disjointed responses and potential gaps during complex incidents.
