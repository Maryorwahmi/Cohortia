Process Flow

The Incident Management Lifecycle

A structured approach to handling security incidents.

1

Planning & Preparation

Develop policies, procedures, and build the IRT.

2

Detection & Analysis

Identify and assess security events.

3

Containment

Limit the scope and impact of the incident.

4

Eradication

Remove the root cause and traces of the attacker.

5

Recovery

Restore systems to normal, secure operation.

6

Post-Incident Review

Lessons learned and continuous improvement.

Key Takeaway

Each phase of the incident management lifecycle is interconnected, requiring CISM oversight to ensure a comprehensive and adaptive response strategy.
