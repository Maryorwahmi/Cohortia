Response Phase

Containment: Halting the Spread

Limiting the scope and impact of an active security incident.

Containment Strategies

  • Isolate compromised systems from the network.
  • Block malicious IP addresses or domains at network perimeter.
  • Disable compromised user accounts or services.
Key Takeaway

Containment is the immediate action to stop an incident's spread, requiring swift, coordinated efforts to minimize business disruption and impact.

Containment Actions

Network Isolation

Segmenting affected systems

Firewall Rules

Blocking malicious traffic

Account Disablement

Preventing further access

Evidence Collection

Forensic preservation

Example Scenario

Upon detecting ransomware, the IRT immediately isolates affected servers and endpoints from the network to prevent encryption spread, under CISM's direction.
