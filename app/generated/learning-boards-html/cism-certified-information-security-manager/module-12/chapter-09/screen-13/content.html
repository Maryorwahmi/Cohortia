CISM in Action

Managerial Decisions & Coordination

The CISM's overarching role in guiding incident response.

Key Decision Areas

  • Incident severity classification and escalation.
  • Resource allocation for response and recovery.
  • Communication strategy for internal and external stakeholders.
  • Integration with BC/DR plans for severe incidents.
Key Takeaway

The CISM's strategic decisions and cross-functional coordination are vital for an effective, compliant, and business-aligned incident response.

Pro-Tip

Develop clear decision matrices and escalation procedures to empower teams while maintaining CISM oversight during a crisis.
