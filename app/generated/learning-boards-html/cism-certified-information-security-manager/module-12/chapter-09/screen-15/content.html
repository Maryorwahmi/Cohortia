Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Incident Response Actions

Critical steps for managing and recovering from security incidents.

Containment

Actions taken to limit the scope and impact of an ongoing security incident.

Eradication

The process of removing the root cause of an incident and eliminating all traces of the attacker.

Recovery

Restoring affected systems and data to normal, secure operation.

Summary Takeaway

Effective incident response relies on swift containment, thorough eradication, secure recovery, and continuous learning through post-incident reviews.
