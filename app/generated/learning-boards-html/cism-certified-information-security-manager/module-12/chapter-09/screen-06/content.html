Phase Focus

Planning and Preparation: The Foundation

CISM's governance and management skills are paramount.

Key Activities

  • Develop incident response policy aligned with business objectives.
  • Define clear procedures for various incident types.
  • Establish communication protocols for all stakeholders.
  • Define incident severity, escalation paths, and decision authority.
Key Takeaway

Proactive planning, clear policies, and defined communication channels are critical for a CISM to establish a resilient incident response framework.

CISM's Planning Checklist

  • 1

    Incident Response Policy: Aligned with business goals.

  • 2

    Procedures: Detailed steps for different incident types.

  • 3

    Communication Plan: Internal, external, legal, PR, regulators.

  • 4

    Severity & Escalation: Clear criteria and paths.

  • 5

    IRT Roles: Defined responsibilities and authorities.

Common Mistake

Failing to regularly review and update the incident response plan, leading to outdated procedures and ineffective responses.
