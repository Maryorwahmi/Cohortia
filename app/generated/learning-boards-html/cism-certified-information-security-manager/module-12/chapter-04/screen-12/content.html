Data Protection & Classification Policy

Ensuring uniform data security, classification, and encryption across all cloud and on-premises environments.

Policy Statement

"All data stored or processed in cloud environments (AWS, Azure) or on-premises must be classified according to organizational data classification standards, and appropriate encryption (at rest and in transit) must be applied based on its classification and regulatory requirements."

CISM Rationale

Ensures uniform application of data classification, encryption standards, and data residency, preventing data leakage or non-compliance across the hybrid landscape.

Key Takeaway

A consistent data protection and classification policy is vital for maintaining data integrity, confidentiality, and regulatory compliance across all environments.

Data Protection Policy Elements

Data Classification Standards

Define clear categories for data sensitivity (e.g., public, internal, confidential) and associated handling requirements.

Encryption at Rest and In Transit

Mandate appropriate encryption mechanisms for data stored and data moving across networks.

Regulatory Compliance

Ensure data handling practices meet specific regulatory requirements like HIPAA, PCI DSS, or GDPR.
