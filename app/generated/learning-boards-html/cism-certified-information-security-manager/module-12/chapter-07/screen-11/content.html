BYOD Policy: Governance Considerations

Ensuring secure use of personal devices for work

Key Governance Areas for BYOD

  • •Define scope and eligibility for BYOD program.
  • •Align BYOD with organizational risk appetite.
  • •Manage the population of devices to be secured.
  • •Ensure consistency across the organization.
Example: Scope and Eligibility

Setting BYOD Boundaries

A BYOD policy must clearly define who is eligible (e.g., specific roles) and what devices are permitted (e.g., OS versions). This governance step sets program boundaries, manages the security scope, and aligns with the organization's risk tolerance and resource capacity.

Key Takeaway

Effective BYOD governance establishes clear policy boundaries and eligibility criteria, ensuring the program aligns with organizational risk appetite and resource management, rather than just technical implementation.

Common Pitfall

Implementing BYOD without a clear, governance-driven policy can lead to uncontrolled risk exposure and compliance issues.
