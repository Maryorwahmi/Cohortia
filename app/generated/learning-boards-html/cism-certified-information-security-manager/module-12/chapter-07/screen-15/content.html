Key Concepts: Information Security Governance

Essential definitions for CISM Domain 1

InfoSec Governance

The system by which an organization directs and controls information security activities to achieve its objectives.

Strategic Alignment

Ensuring information security objectives support and integrate with overall business objectives.

Policy Framework

A hierarchical structure of policies, standards, guidelines, and procedures defining security posture.

Summary Takeaway

Information Security Governance is the strategic backbone, ensuring security is aligned with business goals through a robust policy framework.
