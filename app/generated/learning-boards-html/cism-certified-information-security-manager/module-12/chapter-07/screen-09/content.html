Third-Party Risk Management Governance

Securing the supply chain through strategic oversight

CISM's Role in Third-Party Governance

  • •Establish a third-party risk management program.
  • •Develop policies for vendor due diligence.
  • •Mandate contractual security requirements.
  • •Implement ongoing monitoring of vendor security.
  • •Coordinate incident response with third parties.
Scenario: Outsourcing Customer Support

Extending Security to Vendors

When outsourcing customer support, the CISM ensures the security strategy addresses supply chain risk by mandating security assessments, contractual clauses, and continuous monitoring for the vendor. This protects organizational data even when handled externally.

Key Takeaway

Effective third-party risk management is a critical governance function, ensuring that security controls and oversight extend to external partners to protect organizational assets.
