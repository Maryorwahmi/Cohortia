Exam Pitfalls

Avoiding Common CISM Exam Mistakes

Recognizing and correcting misaligned responses

Mistakes to Avoid

  • •Choosing technical solutions over managerial/governance.
  • •Failing to consider the broader business context.
  • •Overlooking stakeholder engagement and communication.
  • •Focusing on individual controls instead of program management.
Example Scenario

Responding to a New Threat

If a new threat emerges, a CISM's initial response should involve assessing business risk, communicating with leadership, and updating policies, rather than immediately suggesting a technical patch. This demonstrates a governance-first approach.

Key Takeaway

Successfully navigating CISM exam questions requires consciously avoiding common pitfalls by consistently applying a strategic, business-focused, and governance-oriented mindset.

Pro-Tip

When in doubt, ask yourself: 'What would a senior manager do first?' This often points to a governance or strategic action.
