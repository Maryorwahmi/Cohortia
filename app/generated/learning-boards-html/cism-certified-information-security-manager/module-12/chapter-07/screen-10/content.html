Governance in Action

Connecting Business Decisions to Security Governance

Translating strategic choices into security requirements

The Connection Framework

  • •Identify business decisions (e.g., outsourcing).
  • •Assess security implications and new risks.
  • •Determine necessary governance mechanisms.
  • •Develop or update supporting policies.
  • •Align security with organizational mission and values.
Example: Outsourcing Impact

Governance for External Services

A business decision to outsource customer support necessitates the CISM to establish governance mechanisms, such as data classification policies for outsourced data, incident reporting procedures for third parties, and audit rights, to manage associated risks and ensure alignment with business objectives.

Key Takeaway

The CISM's ability to link business decisions to security governance ensures that security is a proactive enabler, safeguarding the organization's mission and values through strategic alignment.

Pro-Tip

Always ask: 'How does this business decision change our risk posture, and what governance adjustments are needed to maintain acceptable risk levels?'
