CISM Role

Key Topics in Information Security Governance

Defining the CISM's strategic responsibilities

Core Governance Areas

  • •Defining and implementing information security strategy.
  • •Establishing clear roles and responsibilities.
  • •Developing comprehensive security policies, standards, and procedures.
  • •Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
Practical Scenario

New Data Processing Activity

When an organization introduces a new data processing activity, the CISM ensures that this activity complies with both internal information security policies and external data privacy regulations like GDPR, demonstrating strategic alignment and compliance oversight.

Key Takeaway

The CISM's role in governance is to ensure a holistic, strategic approach to security, integrating it into all aspects of the business through clear strategy, defined roles, robust policies, and continuous compliance.

Pro-Tip

Always consider the 'why' behind each security activity. How does it support business objectives? This strategic thinking is central to CISM success.
