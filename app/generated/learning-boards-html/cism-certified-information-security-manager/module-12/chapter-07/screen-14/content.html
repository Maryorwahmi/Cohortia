Value Proposition

Translating Security Metrics to Business Value

Demonstrating security's contribution to organizational success

Effective Communication Strategies

  • •Avoid purely technical reports for senior leadership.
  • •Translate security metrics into business-relevant terms.
  • •Highlight reduced financial loss, improved trust, competitive advantage.
  • •Connect security directly to organizational objectives and value.
Example: Phishing Prevention

Quantifying Security's Impact

Instead of reporting '10,000 phishing emails blocked,' a CISM would communicate 'our anti-phishing program prevented an estimated $500,000 in potential fraud and maintained customer confidence.' This directly links security to financial and reputational value.

Key Takeaway

Demonstrating security's business value through clear, business-centric metrics is a critical governance responsibility, ensuring leadership understands security's contribution to organizational success.

Common Mistake

Presenting only technical security metrics to business leaders, which fails to address their concerns about business value and strategic alignment.
