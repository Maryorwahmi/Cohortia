Adopting the 'CISM Mindset'

Prioritizing business objectives and governance over technical details

Key Principles of the CISM Mindset

  • •Always prioritize business objectives.
  • •Understand strategic implications of security decisions.
  • •Focus on management and governance, not technical details.
  • •Look for the 'BEST' or 'FIRST' managerial action.
Scenario Example

New Regulatory Requirement

When a new regulatory requirement emerges, the CISM's first action is to assess its organizational impact, update policies, and communicate with stakeholders, rather than immediately deploying a technical control. This reflects a governance-first approach.

Key Takeaway

The CISM mindset requires a strategic, top-down view, focusing on governance and business alignment to determine the most appropriate managerial response in any security scenario.

Common Mistake

Choosing a technical solution when a managerial or governance response is required. Always consider the broader business context and stakeholder engagement.
