CISM Exam Strategy

Question Analysis: Decoding ISACA's Intent

Carefully reading and dissecting exam questions for the right answer

1

Read Carefully: Don't skim. Understand the full context and scenario presented in the question.

2

Identify Keywords: Look for 'FIRST,' 'BEST,' 'MOST appropriate,' 'LEAST likely,' or 'EXCEPT.' These are crucial.

3

Filter Distractors: Multiple answers may seem correct, but only one aligns with the CISM's managerial role.

4

Focus on Managerial: Prioritize answers that reflect governance, risk management, or program oversight.

Key Takeaway

Thorough question analysis, especially identifying keywords, is essential to uncover ISACA's intended managerial answer.

Keyword Spotlight

Understanding ISACA's Language

FIRST

Indicates the initial, foundational, or most immediate action.

BEST / MOST Appropriate

Requires weighing options for the most strategic CISM response.

LEAST Likely

Identifies the option that is least aligned with CISM principles.

EXCEPT

Asks for the one option that does NOT fit the given criteria.

Common Mistake

Overlooking the 'EXCEPT'

Many candidates miss the 'EXCEPT' keyword, leading them to choose a correct statement rather than the incorrect one. Always double-check these negative qualifiers.

Pro-Tip: Circle or highlight keywords like 'EXCEPT' to ensure you're answering the precise question asked.
