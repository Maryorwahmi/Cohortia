Time Management: Your Exam Ally

Pacing yourself for optimal performance on the CISM exam

1

Understand the Clock: 150 questions in 4 hours means ~1 minute 36 seconds per question. This is your baseline.

2

Practice Pacing: Simulate exam conditions during mock tests to build a natural rhythm. Don't rush, but don't get stuck.

3

Educated Guess & Flag: If a question is difficult, make your best guess, flag it for review, and move on. Avoid dwelling.

4

Review Strategically: Only revisit flagged questions if you have sufficient time remaining after completing the first pass.

Key Takeaway

Treat time as a critical resource; efficient allocation ensures you attempt all questions and maximize your score.

The 'Stuck' Question Dilemma

Imagine you're 30 minutes into the exam and encounter a question you simply can't decipher. You've spent 3 minutes on it already. What's the best approach?

Solution: Make an educated guess, mark the question for review, and immediately move on. Spending more time risks not completing other questions you might know. Return only if you have time at the end.
