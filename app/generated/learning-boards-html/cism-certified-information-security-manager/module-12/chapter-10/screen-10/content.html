CPE Requirements

ISACA's CPE Requirements and Reporting

Understanding the rules for maintaining your CISM certification

1

Total Hours: 120 CPE hours required over a three-year reporting period.

2

Annual Minimum: A minimum of 20 CPE hours must be earned each year.

3

Reporting: Accurately document and submit earned CPE hours through ISACA's online portal.

4

Compliance: Failure to meet requirements or report correctly can lead to certification revocation.

Key Takeaway

Proactive tracking and accurate reporting of CPE hours are essential responsibilities for every CISM to maintain their certification.
