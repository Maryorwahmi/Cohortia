Key Concepts: Professional Development

Sustaining your CISM certification and career growth

Continuous Professional Education (CPE)

Mandatory ongoing learning activities required by ISACA to maintain CISM certification, ensuring professionals stay current.

CPE Reporting

The process of documenting and submitting earned CPE hours to ISACA to maintain certification.

Professional Development Plan

A structured approach to ongoing learning, skill enhancement, and career growth.

Summary Takeaway

Maintaining CISM certification requires active participation in CPE, diligent reporting, and a strategic personal development plan for continuous growth.
