CPE Opportunities

Avenues for Earning CPE Credits

Diverse ways to fulfill your continuous professional education requirements

1

Conferences & Webinars: Attend industry events, workshops, and online sessions for current insights.

2

Formal Education: Enroll in relevant university courses, online certifications, or professional training programs.

3

Writing & Research: Author articles, books, or whitepapers on cybersecurity topics.

4

Volunteering: Contribute to ISACA chapters or other professional organizations.

5

Internal Training: Participate in structured, relevant security training provided by your employer.

Key Takeaway

Leverage a variety of learning opportunities to meet CPE requirements, ensuring continuous skill enhancement and career growth.

CPE Examples

Credits in Action

Conference

2-day cybersecurity conference = ~16 CPEs

Online Course

Relevant online course = significant CPEs (e.g., 20-40)

ISACA Volunteering

Committee work = 1 CPE per hour

Writing

Published article = up to 15 CPEs

Pro-Tip

Align with Career Goals

Choose CPE activities that not only meet the requirements but also align with your personal career development plan and areas where you want to deepen your expertise.

Benefit: Maximizes both compliance and personal growth.
