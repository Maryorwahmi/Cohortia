Operational Excellence

Overseeing Key Security Services

Ensuring effectiveness and alignment of vulnerability, patch, and SOC activities.

Vulnerability Management

Continuous process of identifying, assessing, and remediating security weaknesses in systems and applications.

CISM Role

Ensures a systematic approach to vulnerability scanning, prioritization, and remediation, aligning with risk appetite.

Patch Management

Systematic process of acquiring, testing, and installing code changes to address vulnerabilities or improve functionality.

CISM Role

Oversees patch deployment schedules, ensures testing, and minimizes operational disruption while maximizing security.

SOC Activities

Management of the Security Operations Center, responsible for monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cyber threats.

CISM Role

Ensures the SOC is adequately staffed, equipped, and follows defined incident response procedures, aligning with business continuity.

Key Takeaway

A CISM provides strategic oversight for critical security services, ensuring they are integrated, efficient, and continuously support the organization's overall risk management and security program objectives.
