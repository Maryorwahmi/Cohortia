Essential Risk Management Concepts

Recapping the core principles of identifying, assessing, and responding to risks.

Risk Identification

The process of finding, recognizing, and describing risks to information assets.

Risk Assessment

Evaluating the likelihood and impact of identified risks, either qualitatively or quantitatively.

Risk Response

Strategies for dealing with identified risks: mitigate, accept, transfer, or avoid.

Summary Takeaway

Effective risk management is a continuous, systematic process that forms the bedrock of a strong information security posture, guiding all subsequent security actions.
