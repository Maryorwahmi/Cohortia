Holistic View

Third-Party Risk and Domain Interplay

Integrating vendor security with overall risk management and program activities.

Third-Party Risk Management

  • •**Vendor Assessment:** Evaluate security posture of third parties before engagement.
  • •**Contractual Clauses:** Include robust security requirements in agreements.
  • •**Ongoing Monitoring:** Continuously verify compliance and performance.
Example

A CISM ensures a new cloud service provider undergoes a thorough security audit and signs a contract with strict data protection clauses before integration.

Domain Interplay: Risk & Program

  • •**Risk Drives Program:** Risk assessments identify needs, informing program development.
  • •**Program Manages Risk:** A robust program implements controls and monitors new risks.
  • •**Continuous Feedback:** Both domains constantly feed into and refine each other.
Why it Matters

Mastering this interplay ensures security investments are strategic, effective, and continuously aligned with evolving business and threat landscapes.

Key Takeaway

A CISM must manage third-party risks as an integral part of the overall security program, understanding that effective risk management constantly informs and is supported by robust program development.
