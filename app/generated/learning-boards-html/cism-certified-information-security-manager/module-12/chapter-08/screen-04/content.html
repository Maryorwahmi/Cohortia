Core Concept

Information Security Risk Management

Systematically identifying, assessing, responding to, and monitoring risks.

1. Identify Risks
2. Assess Risks
3. Respond to Risks
4. Monitor Risks
Supporting Explanation

The risk management lifecycle is iterative, meaning it's a continuous process, not a one-time event. New risks emerge, and existing risks change, requiring constant re-evaluation.

Example Scenario

A CISM identifies a new zero-day vulnerability (Identify), assesses its high impact on customer data (Assess), decides to implement a temporary WAF (Respond), and then continuously scans for exploitation attempts (Monitor).

Key Takeaway

The CISM's role is to ensure the entire risk management lifecycle is systematically applied and continuously refined to protect organizational assets and achieve business objectives.
