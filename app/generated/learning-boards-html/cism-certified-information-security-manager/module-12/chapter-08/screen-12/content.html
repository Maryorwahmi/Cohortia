Applying CISM Managerial Judgment

Prioritizing actions and communicating risk in complex scenarios.

Scenario: Critical System Vulnerability

An information security risk assessment has identified a critical vulnerability in a legacy system that cannot be patched due to vendor discontinuation. The cost of replacing the system is prohibitively high.

Common Mistake

Immediately recommending a technical patch or decommissioning without considering business impact or alternatives.

CISM Best Response

Accept the risk, but implement compensating controls and continuous monitoring. This involves alternative security measures (e.g., network segmentation, strict access controls, WAF) to reduce likelihood/impact, combined with continuous monitoring.

  • •Focus on managerial judgment and business alignment.
  • •Prioritize based on business impact, not just technical severity.
  • •Communicate risks and proposed solutions in business terms to senior management.
Key Takeaway

The CISM's role is to make strategic, business-aligned decisions, translating technical risks into actionable plans and communicating them effectively to leadership, even when resources are constrained.
