Core Competencies for CISM Success

Building Foundational Knowledge in Risk and Program Management

LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Map the Risk Management Lifecycle

Consolidate understanding of the entire information security risk management lifecycle, including identification, assessment, response, and monitoring. This skill ensures you can systematically manage risks from inception to resolution.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Build Security Programs

Reinforce knowledge of establishing, operating, and maintaining an information security program. This involves developing a roadmap, allocating resources, and integrating security into the organizational culture.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Navigate Exam Pitfalls

Identify common pitfalls and effective strategies for answering CISM exam questions related to Domains 2 and 3. This objective helps you develop a strategic approach to exam scenarios, focusing on managerial judgment.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Apply Risk Treatment

Practice applying CISM principles to scenarios involving risk treatment, including mitigation, acceptance, transfer, and avoidance. This ensures you can recommend appropriate strategies for various risk profiles.
