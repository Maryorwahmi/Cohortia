Program Management

Allocating Resources and Building Awareness

Optimizing budget, personnel, and training for program effectiveness.

Resource Allocation

  • •Managing budget for security tools, services, and initiatives.
  • •Allocating skilled personnel to critical security functions.
  • •Ensuring investments are cost-effective and risk-aligned.
Example

A CISM justifies a budget increase for a new SIEM system by demonstrating its ROI in reducing incident response time and potential financial losses.

Security Awareness & Training

  • •Educating employees on security policies and best practices.
  • •Conducting regular training and simulated attacks (e.g., phishing).
  • •Fostering a security-conscious organizational culture.
Example

After a rise in phishing incidents, a CISM implements mandatory, interactive training modules and regular simulated phishing campaigns for all employees.

Key Takeaway

A CISM must strategically manage both financial and human resources, ensuring that security awareness and training programs are as robust as technical controls to build a comprehensive defense.
