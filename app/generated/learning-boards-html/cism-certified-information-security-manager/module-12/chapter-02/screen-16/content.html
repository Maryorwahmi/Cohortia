Managing the security of an IoT device from its manufacturing, through deployment and operation, to its eventual decommissioning and disposal.

Chapter Summary

Effective IoT security relies on understanding and implementing secure communication protocols, managing the entire device lifecycle, and ensuring strict data privacy and regulatory compliance.
