Common IoT Security Mistakes

Pitfalls to avoid in IoT deployments

Frequent Errors in IoT Security

Many organizations inadvertently introduce vulnerabilities into their IoT ecosystems by failing to adhere to fundamental security practices.

  • •Default Credentials: Devices deployed with factory-set or weak passwords.
  • •Flat Networks: IoT devices on the same network as critical business systems.
  • •Outdated Firmware: Neglecting regular updates for known vulnerabilities.
  • •Poor Physical Security: Ignoring the physical accessibility of devices.
Key Takeaway

Proactive identification and remediation of common IoT security mistakes are essential for preventing breaches and maintaining a robust security posture.

Impact of Negligence

Increased Attack Surface

Each unaddressed mistake creates an exploitable entry point for attackers.

Lateral Movement Risk

A compromised IoT device can become a pivot point to critical corporate assets.

Example Scenario

An industrial IoT sensor deployed with its default password on the corporate network is compromised, allowing an attacker to gain a foothold and move to critical manufacturing control systems.
