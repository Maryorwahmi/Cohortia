Lifecycle Management

IoT Device Lifecycle: Manufacturing & Provisioning

Securing devices from their inception

Secure Manufacturing Practices

Security must be embedded during the manufacturing process to ensure devices are secure from the moment they are produced.

  • •Secure Defaults: Ship devices with strong, unique default configurations, not generic passwords.
  • •Unique Identities: Embed unique, cryptographically strong identities (e.g., certificates) in each device.
  • •Tamper Resistance: Design hardware to resist physical tampering and unauthorized modification.
Key Takeaway

Foundational security measures during manufacturing and provisioning are critical to prevent vulnerabilities from being introduced at the earliest stage of an IoT device's life.

Secure Device Provisioning

Zero-Touch Provisioning (ZTP)

Automated, secure registration and configuration of devices upon initial connection, minimizing human error.

Secure Credential Management

Ensure that initial credentials and cryptographic keys are securely generated, stored, and managed.

Common Mistake

Allowing devices to be deployed with default, easily guessable passwords or without unique identities, creating immediate vulnerabilities.
