The CISM's Role in IoT Security

Leading comprehensive IoT security programs

Strategic Leadership in IoT Security

As a CISM, you are pivotal in integrating IoT security into the organization's overall information security program, ensuring a holistic approach.

  • •Policy Development: Create and enforce policies covering the entire IoT lifecycle.
  • •Risk Assessments: Conduct regular, specialized IoT risk assessments.
  • •Incident Response: Adapt and tailor plans for IoT-specific incidents.
Key Takeaway

The CISM's leadership is crucial for developing, implementing, and overseeing a comprehensive IoT security program that addresses unique challenges and ensures organizational resilience.

Key Areas of Focus

Secure Architecture Oversight

Ensure network segmentation and secure communication protocols are implemented from design.

Vulnerability Management

Integrate IoT devices into vulnerability scanning and patch management programs.

Pro-Tip

Collaborate closely with engineering and product teams to embed security requirements early in the IoT development lifecycle, rather than trying to add them later.
