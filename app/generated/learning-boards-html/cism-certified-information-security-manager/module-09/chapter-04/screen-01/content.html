Chapter 9.4

Building an Incident Response Team (IRT)

The Human Element of Cybersecurity Resilience

A capable and well-trained Incident Response Team is the human element that executes the plan, makes critical decisions under pressure, and ultimately determines the success of an incident response effort. As a CISM, building, nurturing, and maintaining this team is one of your most vital responsibilities.
