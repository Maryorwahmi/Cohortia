Human Resources (HR)

Personnel & Compliance Management

  • Handles personnel-related issues (e.g., insider threats, employee data compromise).
  • Advises on labor laws and internal policies.
  • Supports affected employees.
  • Ensures ethical and legal handling of personnel data.
Example Responsibility

If an employee is suspected of data exfiltration, HR collaborates with the IRT to conduct an investigation and manage any disciplinary actions.

Business Unit Representative

Business Impact & Recovery Prioritization

  • Provides context on business impact of the incident.
  • Helps prioritize recovery efforts based on business criticality.
  • Ensures alignment between technical response and business objectives.
  • Acts as a liaison for affected business operations.
Key Takeaway

Incident response is a multidisciplinary effort; integrating HR and business perspectives ensures that the human and operational impacts are managed effectively alongside technical remediation.
