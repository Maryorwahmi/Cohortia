Module 9›Chapter 9.4›IRT Support Roles
Security Analyst(s)

Monitoring, Triage & Execution

  • Monitors security alerts (SIEM, EDR).
  • Performs initial triage and data collection.
  • Executes containment and eradication steps.
  • Acts as the hands-on responder.
Example Responsibility

A Security Analyst detects suspicious network activity and immediately isolates the affected workstation to prevent further compromise.

Communication Specialist & Legal Counsel

Messaging & Compliance

  • **Communication Specialist:** Manages internal/external messaging, drafts statements, coordinates with PR.
  • **Legal Counsel:** Advises on legal obligations, regulatory reporting, and evidence handling.
  • Ensures consistent public image and legal adherence.
Key Takeaway

Beyond technical response, managing communication and legal compliance is crucial to mitigate reputational damage and regulatory penalties during an incident.
