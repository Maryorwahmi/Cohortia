Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways for Building an Incident Response Team

Incident Response Team (IRT)

A dedicated group responsible for responding to and managing security incidents.

IRT Organizational Models

Centralized, Distributed, Coordinated, Hybrid – chosen based on organizational structure and needs.

Key IRT Roles

Incident Response Lead, Technical Lead, Security Analyst, Communication Specialist, Legal Counsel, HR, Business Rep.

Summary Takeaway

A well-defined IRT, structured appropriately for the organization, and staffed with clear roles, forms the bedrock of effective incident response and organizational resilience.
