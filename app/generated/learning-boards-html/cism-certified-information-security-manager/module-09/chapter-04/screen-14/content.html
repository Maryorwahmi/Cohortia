Incident Management

Clear Communication & Chain of Command

Well-defined communication channels and a clear chain of command are paramount for efficient information flow and rapid decision-making during an incident.

  • Defined reporting structures and decision-makers.
  • Secure internal communication methods.
  • Coordinated external communication (CISM, Legal, PR).
  • Psychological well-being support for IRT members.
Key Takeaway

Establishing clear communication protocols and a robust chain of command prevents chaos, ensures timely decisions, and protects both the organization and its IRT members during a crisis.
