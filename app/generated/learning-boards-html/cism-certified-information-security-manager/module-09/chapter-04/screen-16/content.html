Key Concepts

Advanced Insights for IRT Development and Management

IRT Skill Sets

Technical expertise, analytical thinking, problem-solving, communication, teamwork, decision-making under pressure.

Training Program

Comprehensive approach including technical, procedural, soft skills, and regular exercises/simulations.

Chain of Command

Clearly defined reporting structure and decision-making authority within the IRT.

Summary Takeaway

Building an exceptional IRT requires a holistic approach: developing diverse skills, implementing robust training, and establishing clear communication and command structures for optimal incident response.
