Soft Skills Training

Crisis Communication & Decision-Making

  • Crisis communication techniques.
  • Decision-making under stress.
  • Teamwork and collaboration enhancement.
  • Ethical considerations in incident response.
Example Application

IRT members participate in a workshop to practice communicating sensitive incident details to non-technical executives and external media representatives.

Tabletop Exercises & Simulations

Realistic Practice & Procedure Refinement

  • Regular drills simulating various incident scenarios.
  • Opportunity to practice roles and identify gaps.
  • Refine procedures in a safe environment.
  • Builds 'muscle memory' and improves coordination.
Key Takeaway

Combining soft skills development with realistic simulations ensures an IRT is not only technically proficient but also resilient, communicative, and coordinated under pressure.
