Centralized IRT

Single Team, Broad Scope

A single team responsible for all incidents across the entire organization. Common in smaller to medium-sized organizations or highly integrated IT environments.

  • Offers consistency in procedures.
  • Develops deep, concentrated expertise.
  • Can be overwhelmed during widespread incidents.
Example Scenario

A small tech startup with a single office and unified IT infrastructure benefits from a centralized IRT, ensuring all incidents are handled with consistent expertise.

Distributed IRT

Multiple Teams, Localized Focus

Multiple teams aligned with specific business units, geographic regions, or technical domains. Provides localized expertise and faster initial response.

  • Provides localized expertise.
  • Enables faster initial response.
  • Requires strong central coordination and oversight.
Key Takeaway

The optimal IRT model balances the need for consistent, deep expertise with the benefits of localized, rapid response, tailored to the organization's unique context.
