Technical Training

Mastering Tools & Attack Vectors

  • Deep dives into SIEM, EDR, forensic suites.
  • Understanding operating systems and network protocols.
  • Familiarity with common attack vectors.
  • Hands-on labs and practical exercises.
Example Application

An IRT member practices using a forensic toolkit to analyze a compromised hard drive image, identifying malicious files and attacker persistence mechanisms.

Procedural Training

Familiarity with IRP & Runbooks

  • Regular walkthroughs of the Incident Response Plan (IRP).
  • Review of runbooks and checklists.
  • Ensuring familiarity with organization-specific processes.
  • Building muscle memory for rapid response.
Key Takeaway

Effective IRT training combines technical skill development with rigorous procedural practice, ensuring team members are both proficient with tools and adept at following established response protocols.
