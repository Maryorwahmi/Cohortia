Coordinated IRT

Central Guidance, Distributed Execution

Combines elements of both centralized and distributed models. A central core team provides guidance and support to distributed teams or acts as an escalation point.

  • Leverages local expertise with central oversight.
  • Ensures consistency while allowing flexibility.
  • Ideal for large, complex organizations.
Consideration

Requires robust communication and clear escalation paths between central and distributed teams to be effective.

Hybrid Model

Internal & External Expertise

Includes reliance on third-party incident response services for specialized expertise or surge capacity, complementing internal teams.

  • Access to specialized skills (e.g., advanced forensics).
  • Provides surge capacity during major incidents.
  • Requires careful vendor management and integration.
Key Takeaway

The optimal IRT model is a strategic choice, balancing internal capabilities with external support, tailored to the organization's unique risk profile and resource availability.
