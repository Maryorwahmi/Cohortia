Incident Response Lead/Manager

Strategic Oversight & Executive Communication

  • Oversees the entire response effort.
  • Makes strategic decisions and ensures IRP adherence.
  • Communicates with executive leadership.
  • Often held by the CISM or a direct report.
Example Responsibility

During a major data breach, the IR Lead decides on the overall strategy for containment and recovery, and provides regular updates to the CEO and board.

Technical Lead/Forensic Analyst

Deep Technical Expertise & Analysis

  • Specializes in forensics, malware, and network analysis.
  • Identifies root causes and attacker methods.
  • Guides technical containment and eradication.
  • Requires strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Key Takeaway

Effective incident response hinges on clear strategic leadership combined with deep technical analysis to accurately understand and mitigate threats.
