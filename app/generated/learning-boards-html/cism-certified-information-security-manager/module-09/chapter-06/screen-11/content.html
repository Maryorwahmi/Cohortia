Phase 3: Recovery

Recovery: Restoring Normal Operations

Bringing Systems Back Online Securely and Efficiently

Key Recovery Actions

1

Restoration from Backups: Recover data and systems from clean, verified backups.

2

System Validation: Thoroughly test restored systems for full functionality and integrity.

3

Enhanced Monitoring: Implement increased monitoring post-recovery to detect re-infection.

4

Phased Rollout: Bring systems back online in a controlled, prioritized manner.

Supporting Explanation

  • BCP/DRP Integration: Recovery is a key component of broader business continuity and disaster recovery plans.
  • Data Integrity: Crucial to verify checksums or digital signatures of backup data.
  • Pre-Online Hardening: Systems must be patched and hardened *before* being brought back online.
  • CISM's Role: Coordinate with business leaders to prioritize recovery based on business impact.
Example

After a server rebuild, the database is restored from a verified clean backup. The server then undergoes rigorous testing and enhanced monitoring for two weeks before full production reintegration.

Key Takeaway

Recovery is about restoring secure operations, tightly integrated with BCP/DRP, emphasizing data integrity, validation, and phased rollouts.
