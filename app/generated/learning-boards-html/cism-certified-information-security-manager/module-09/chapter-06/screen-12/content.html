Recovery Best Practices

Recovery Challenges and Safety Measures

Ensuring Data Integrity and Preventing Re-infection

Key Safety Measures for Recovery

Verify Backup Integrity: Always ensure backups are clean and uncompromised (e.g., verify checksums).

Patch & Harden Systems: Ensure all systems are fully updated and secured *before* bringing them back online.

Enhanced Monitoring: Maintain increased vigilance for signs of re-infection post-recovery.

Supporting Explanation

  • Avoid Rushing: Premature recovery without validation can lead to re-infection.
  • Backup Trust: Never assume backups are clean; always verify their integrity.
  • Proactive Hardening: Security updates and configuration changes are essential before systems go live.
  • CISM's Oversight: Ensure these safety protocols are strictly followed by the IRT.
Common Mistake

Rushing recovery without proper validation, leading to re-infection or further instability of systems.

Key Takeaway

Rigorous safety measures, including backup verification and pre-online hardening, are crucial in recovery to prevent re-infection and ensure lasting security.
