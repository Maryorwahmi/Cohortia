Short-Term Containment: Rapid Response Actions

Immediate Steps to Halt Incident Progression

Key Short-Term Actions

1

Disconnect Systems: Immediately remove affected systems from the network to prevent further spread.

2

Isolate Network Segments: Use VLANs or firewall rules to quarantine compromised network areas.

3

Disable Accounts: Suspend compromised user accounts to prevent unauthorized access.

4

Block Malicious IPs: Configure firewalls to block known malicious IP addresses or domains.

5

Quarantine Malware: Isolate infected files or hosts to prevent malware execution or spread.

Supporting Explanation

  • Rapid Action: These steps prioritize speed to immediately halt the incident's progression.
  • Temporary Fixes: Often, these are temporary measures to buy time for more permanent solutions.
  • Damage Control: The primary objective is to limit the immediate damage and prevent escalation.
  • Evidence Preservation: While rapid, care must be taken not to destroy critical forensic data.
Example Scenario

Upon detecting a ransomware infection on a critical server, the IRT immediately disconnects the server from the network to prevent encryption of shared drives and other systems.

Key Takeaway

Short-term containment focuses on swift, decisive actions to prevent immediate harm and buy time for a more comprehensive incident response.
