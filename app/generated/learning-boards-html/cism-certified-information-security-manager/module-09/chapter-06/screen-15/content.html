Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Strategic Incident Planning

Supporting Elements for Comprehensive Response

Root Cause Analysis

Identifying the underlying reason for an incident to prevent recurrence.

Business Continuity Plan (BCP)

A plan for maintaining essential business functions during and after a disaster.

Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

A plan for recovering IT infrastructure and systems after a disaster.

Summary Takeaway

RCA, BCP, and DRP are crucial for a holistic incident response strategy, ensuring both operational resilience and technical recovery.
