Containment Best Practices

Balancing Speed and Evidence Preservation

Navigating the Trade-offs in Containment

The Containment Dilemma

The core challenge in containment is the inherent tension between the need for rapid action to stop an incident and the imperative to preserve forensic evidence.

Speed

Minimize damage and prevent escalation.

Evidence Preservation

Enable root cause analysis and potential legal action.

Common Mistake

Destroying critical forensic evidence by acting too quickly (e.g., disconnecting a server without capturing a memory dump).

CISM's Role & Guidelines

  • Clear Decision Trees: Provide IRT with structured guidance for various incident types.
  • Forensic Readiness: Ensure tools and procedures are in place to capture evidence before destructive actions.
  • Documentation: Emphasize thorough logging of all containment actions and observations.
  • Executive Approval: For high-risk strategies like strategic containment, ensure proper authorization.
Example Action

Before isolating a compromised server, the IRT captures a full disk image and memory dump for later forensic analysis, as mandated by the IRP.

Key Takeaway

A CISM must equip the IRT with clear guidelines to balance rapid containment with the critical need to preserve forensic evidence, preventing costly mistakes.
