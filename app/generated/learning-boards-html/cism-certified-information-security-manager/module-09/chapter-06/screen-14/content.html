Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Incident Response Foundations

Essential Terms for Effective Incident Management

Containment

Actions taken to limit the scope and impact of an incident, preventing further spread.

Eradication

The process of removing the threat and its root cause from affected systems and the environment.

Recovery

Restoring affected systems and services to normal, secure operation.

Summary Takeaway

Containment, Eradication, and Recovery are the three pillars of effective incident response, each playing a vital role in minimizing impact and restoring security.
