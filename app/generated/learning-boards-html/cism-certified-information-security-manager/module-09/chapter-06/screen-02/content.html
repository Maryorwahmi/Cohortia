Chapter Goals

Learning Objectives

Skills for Incident Response Leadership

Bloom's Taxonomy:LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Formulate Containment Strategies

Learn to craft effective containment plans for various incident types, balancing the need for rapid action with the preservation of critical forensic evidence. This skill ensures you can stop an incident's spread without compromising future analysis.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Describe Eradication Methods

Understand the techniques for completely removing threats and, crucially, identifying the root cause of security incidents. This knowledge is vital for preventing re-infection and strengthening your organization's defenses long-term.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Develop Recovery Plans

Master the creation of comprehensive recovery plans that seamlessly integrate with existing business continuity and disaster recovery objectives. This ensures a smooth and secure return to normal operations after an incident.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
