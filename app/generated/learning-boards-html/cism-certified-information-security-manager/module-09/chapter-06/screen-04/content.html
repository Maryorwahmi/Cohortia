Core Concept

The CER Phases: A Systematic Approach

Limiting Damage and Restoring Operations Post-Incident

Incident Response Flow

Containment
Eradication
Recovery

These phases are often iterative, requiring careful coordination to minimize impact while preserving forensic evidence.

CISM's Role

Ensure IRP provides clear, actionable strategies for each phase.

Key Challenge

Balancing speed of response with preservation of evidence.

Supporting Explanation

  • Limit Damage: The immediate goal is to stop the incident from spreading and causing further harm.
  • Remove Threat: This involves thoroughly eradicating the malicious elements and identifying the root cause.
  • Restore Operations: Bringing affected systems and services back to normal, secure functionality.
  • Iterative Process: Phases may overlap or require revisiting based on incident evolution.
Key Takeaway

The CER phases are a structured, iterative approach to incident response, crucial for minimizing impact and ensuring business resilience.
