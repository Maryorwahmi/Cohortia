Unpacking the Incident: Core Review Goals

A comprehensive post-incident review aims to understand the incident fully and identify actionable improvements.

01

Understand What Happened

Gather all facts to reconstruct the incident's progression, from initial detection to full recovery. This forms the factual basis for analysis.

02

Determine Why It Happened

Identify the root causes and underlying vulnerabilities, moving beyond symptoms to address systemic issues that allowed the incident to occur.

03

Assess Response Effectiveness

Evaluate the performance of the Incident Response Plan, the IRT, tools, and communication strategies. What worked well, and what didn't?

04

Prevent Future Incidents

Develop actionable recommendations to prevent similar incidents or significantly improve future response capabilities, ensuring continuous security enhancement.
