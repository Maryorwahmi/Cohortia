Driving Change: Tracking and Implementing Corrective Actions

The CISM is responsible for ensuring recommendations are actively tracked and implemented for continuous improvement.

01

Assign Ownership & Dates

Assign clear ownership for each recommendation and set realistic completion dates to ensure accountability and a timeline for action.

Why it matters

Without clear ownership and deadlines, recommendations often remain unimplemented, negating the value of the review.

02

Monitor Progress & Report

Regularly monitor progress, integrate actions into risk management, and report status to executive leadership to maintain momentum and secure support.

Pro-Tip

Utilize GRC platforms to streamline tracking and reporting, ensuring visibility and accountability across the organization.

Key Takeaway

The CISM's active role in tracking, implementing, and reporting corrective actions is vital for translating lessons learned into tangible and lasting security improvements.
