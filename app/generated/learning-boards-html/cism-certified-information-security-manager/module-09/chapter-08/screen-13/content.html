Key Concepts

Reinforce your understanding of the core ideas from this chapter.

Post-Incident Review

A formal process to analyze an incident, evaluate the response, and identify areas for improvement.

Lessons Learned

The insights gained from an incident that inform future prevention and response strategies.

Post-Incident Report

A comprehensive document detailing the incident, its impact, the response, root causes, and recommendations.

Summary Takeaway

Formal reviews, derived lessons, and detailed reports are foundational for continuous security improvement.
