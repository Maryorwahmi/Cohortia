Gathering the Evidence: Data and Timeline

Meticulous data collection and timeline reconstruction form the bedrock of any effective post-incident review.

01

Data Collection

  • •Gather incident reports, logs, and forensic data.
  • •Collect communication records and timestamps.
  • •Ensure all relevant information is captured for analysis.
Example

Collecting firewall logs and user activity records helps pinpoint the initial point of compromise and subsequent actions.

02

Timeline Reconstruction

  • •Create a detailed chronological sequence of events.
  • •Visualize the incident's progression from detection to recovery.
  • •Identify critical decision points and response actions.
Benefit

A clear timeline helps identify delays, missed opportunities, and areas for improving response efficiency.

Key Takeaway

Thorough data collection and precise timeline reconstruction are indispensable for accurate incident analysis and effective improvement planning.
