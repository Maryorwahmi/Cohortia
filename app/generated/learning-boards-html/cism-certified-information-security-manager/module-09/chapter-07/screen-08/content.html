Key Elements of an Incident Communication Plan

A comprehensive plan outlines the critical aspects of information sharing to ensure control and consistency during an incident.

01Who Communicates What

Assign specific individuals or roles for different types of communication to prevent conflicting messages.

02When to Communicate

Define clear triggers for communication at different incident stages (e.g., detection, containment, resolution).

03How to Communicate

Specify channels (e.g., secure email, dedicated website, press release, internal portal, social media).

04What to Communicate

Provide templates and guidelines for messaging, ensuring consistency and accuracy of information.

05Approval Process

Establish a clear review and approval process for all external communications, especially with legal and PR.

Key Takeaway

A predefined communication plan ensures structured, timely, and approved information flow, preventing chaos and maintaining control.
