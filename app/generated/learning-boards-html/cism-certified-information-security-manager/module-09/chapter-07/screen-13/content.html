The Imperative of Legal Review

Legal review is a non-negotiable step for all external incident communications, safeguarding compliance and protecting the organization's legal standing.

Regulatory Compliance

Ensures statements comply with data breach notification laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA).

Liability Mitigation

Prevents inadvertent admissions of liability and protects the organization's legal position.

Strategic Advice

Advises on timing and content of notifications to affected individuals and regulators.

Integration & Safeguards

Embedding Legal Review in Your Workflow

Workflow Integration: Legal review must be a mandatory step in the communication plan's approval process for all external statements.

Safety Note: Ensure internal and external communications do not inadvertently disclose sensitive information that could aid attackers or jeopardize legal proceedings. Use secure, out-of-band channels for sensitive IRT discussions.

Key Action

Always involve legal counsel early in the incident response process, not just at the final review stage, to guide communication strategy from the outset.

Key Takeaway

Legal review is essential to ensure compliance, mitigate liability, and protect the organization's legal standing during incident communications.
