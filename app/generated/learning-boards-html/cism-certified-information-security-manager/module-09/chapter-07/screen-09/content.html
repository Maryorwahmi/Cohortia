Principles for Internal Messaging

Effective internal communication during an incident requires a balance of transparency, clarity, and actionable guidance to maintain employee trust and cooperation.

Clarity & Conciseness

Use plain language, avoid jargon, and get straight to the point.

Factual & Honest

Be transparent about the situation, but cautious with unconfirmed details.

Reassurance & Actionable Advice

Provide comfort and clear instructions on what employees should do.

Example Scenario

Guiding Employees During a System Outage

Scenario: Your internal email system is experiencing an outage due to a potential security incident. Employees are unable to communicate.

Internal Communication Goal: Inform employees of the issue, provide temporary workarounds, and instruct them on reporting unusual activity.

Pro-Tip

Always include a clear 'Actions Required from You' section. This empowers employees and helps contain the incident by preventing further risky behavior.

Key Takeaway

Internal communications build trust and guide employee actions during disruption, making them a critical component of incident response.
