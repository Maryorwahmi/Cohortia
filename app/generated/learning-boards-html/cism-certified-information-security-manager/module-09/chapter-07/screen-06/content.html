External Stakeholders: Beyond Your Walls

External parties have distinct information needs and expectations that must be carefully managed to protect reputation and ensure compliance.

Affected Customers/Users

Need transparent, empathetic communication about what happened, data affected, and mitigation steps.

Regulatory Bodies

Require specific information for compliance with data breach notification laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

Law Enforcement

Needs details for investigation, especially in cases of criminal activity or cybercrime.

Third-Party Vendors/Partners

May be directly involved or affected, requiring coordinated communication and action.

Cyber Insurance Providers

Need timely and accurate details to process claims and provide necessary support.

Media

Will seek information, requiring a carefully managed public relations strategy to control the narrative.

Key Takeaway

External stakeholders require tailored communication to manage reputation, ensure compliance, and support investigations.
