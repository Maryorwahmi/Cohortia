Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Communication Plan

A documented strategy outlining who, what, when, and how information will be shared during an incident.

Stakeholder Management

Identifying and engaging with all relevant internal and external parties during an incident.

Internal Communication

Messaging directed at employees, executives, and internal departments to maintain morale and coordination.

Summary Takeaway

A well-defined communication plan, effective stakeholder management, and clear internal messaging are foundational for successful incident response.
