Key Concepts

Advanced concepts for managing incident communications.

External Communication

Messaging directed at customers, regulators, law enforcement, media, and partners.

Media Strategy

A plan for handling press inquiries and public relations during a high-profile incident.

Legal Review

Essential step to ensure all communications comply with laws, regulations, and protect the organization's legal standing.

Summary Takeaway

Effective external communication, a robust media strategy, and mandatory legal review are critical for managing incident impact and protecting organizational integrity.
