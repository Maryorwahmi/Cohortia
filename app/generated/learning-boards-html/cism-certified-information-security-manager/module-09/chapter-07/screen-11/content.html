Principles for External Messaging

External communications require a delicate balance of transparency, empathy, and actionable advice to manage public perception and rebuild trust.

Clear & Concise

Avoid technical jargon; use plain language for broad understanding.

Factual & Transparent

Be honest about the situation, but cautious about unconfirmed details.

Empathetic & Actionable

Show concern and provide concrete steps for affected parties.

Example Scenario

Customer Notification for a Data Breach

Scenario: Customer names, email addresses, and encrypted passwords were compromised in a data breach.

External Communication Goal: Inform affected customers, apologize, explain impact, and provide immediate protective actions.

Pro-Tip

Always include contact information (FAQ page, support email/phone) for further inquiries. This demonstrates support and reduces customer frustration.

Key Takeaway

External messaging must balance transparency with caution, prioritizing empathy and actionable guidance for affected parties to rebuild trust.
