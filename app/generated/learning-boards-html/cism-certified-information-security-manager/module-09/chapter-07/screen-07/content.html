Diverse Needs, Tailored Messages

Different stakeholder groups require distinct types of information and communication styles during an incident.

Executive Leadership
  • •Business impact & financial implications.
  • •Recovery timelines & strategic decisions.
Employees
  • •Clear instructions (do/don't do).
  • •Reassurance & service availability updates.
Customers
  • •Transparent, empathetic communication.
  • •Data affected, mitigation steps, actionable advice.
Regulators & Law Enforcement
  • •Specific compliance information for regulators.
  • •Detailed investigation data for law enforcement.
Key Takeaway

Effective communication is audience-centric, delivering relevant information to each group to maintain trust and control.
