Internal Stakeholders: Who Needs to Know?

Identifying key internal groups is the foundational step for a coordinated incident response. These groups are directly impacted or play a critical role in managing the incident internally.

Executive Leadership

CEO, Board of Directors. Need high-level business impact, financial implications, recovery timelines for strategic decisions.

Legal Counsel

Essential for compliance, liability, and protecting the organization's legal position.

Human Resources

Manages employee concerns, internal morale, and any personnel-related impacts.

PR/Marketing

Develops internal messaging and prepares for potential external communications.

Business Unit Leaders

Need updates on how the incident affects their specific operations and services.

IT Operations & Employees

IT Ops leads technical response; all employees need instructions and reassurance.

Key Takeaway

A clear internal stakeholder map ensures no critical internal voice is missed, facilitating coordinated response and decision-making.
