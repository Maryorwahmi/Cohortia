Strategic Alignment

Aligning with Business Objectives and Risk Appetite

Integrate your IR policy with overall information security governance and risk management strategy to ensure proportional security investments.

Low Risk Appetite

  • More stringent policies & procedures
  • Aggressive prevention & rapid detection
  • Comprehensive recovery efforts
  • Potentially significant resource allocation

Higher Risk Appetite

  • May tolerate certain minor incidents
  • Less aggressive response measures
  • Potentially lower resource investment
  • Accepts longer recovery for non-critical systems
Key Takeaway

Aligning IR with risk appetite ensures security efforts are proportional to business tolerance, avoiding over- or under-investment.
