Organizational Structure

Roles and Responsibilities in IR

Clearly define who is responsible for the overall IR program and outline the roles of all key stakeholders for a coordinated response.

CISO / Designated CISM

Overall responsibility for the Incident Response Program.

Incident Response Team (IRT) Lead

Executes the Incident Response Plan.

Legal Counsel

Advises on legal and regulatory obligations.

Human Resources

Manages personnel-related incident issues.

Public Relations

Manages external communications.

Executive Management

Provides strategic direction and resource approval.

All Employees

Responsible for reporting suspected security events.

Key Takeaway

Incident response is a collective effort; clearly defined roles for all stakeholders are crucial for effective crisis management.
