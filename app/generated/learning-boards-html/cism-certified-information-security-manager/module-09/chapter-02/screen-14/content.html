Chapter Recap

Key Concepts

Review the most important ideas and definitions from this chapter.

Incident Response Policy

A high-level document outlining an organization's strategic approach and commitment to managing security incidents.

Incident Response Strategy

The overall plan and principles guiding the organization's incident response efforts, aligning with business objectives and risk appetite.

Severity Categorization

Classifying incidents based on their potential impact (e.g., Critical, High, Medium, Low) to prioritize response efforts.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined policy, strategic alignment, and clear incident categorization are foundational for effective incident response.
