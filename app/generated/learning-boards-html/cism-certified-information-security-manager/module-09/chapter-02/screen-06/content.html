Incident Classification

Defining and Categorizing Incidents

Establish a clear definition of an incident and categorize it by severity to prioritize response efforts effectively.

What is an Incident?

Any actual or suspected event compromising Confidentiality, Integrity, or Availability (CIA) of information assets, or violating security policies.

Severity Categorization

  • Critical: Immediate, severe business impact
  • High: Significant business impact
  • Medium: Moderate impact on non-critical operations
  • Low: Minimal, isolated impact

Example: Ransomware Attack

A ransomware attack encrypting core business systems would be a Critical incident, demanding immediate, high-priority resources due to severe impact on availability and potential data compromise.

Key Takeaway

Clear incident definitions and severity categories are essential for consistent, appropriate, and timely incident response.
