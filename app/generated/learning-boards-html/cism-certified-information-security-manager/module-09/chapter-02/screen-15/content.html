Chapter Recap

Key Concepts

Reinforce your understanding of the remaining essential ideas from this chapter.

Policy Review Cycle

Regular, scheduled review and update process for the incident response policy, involving key stakeholders.

Security by Design

Integrating security considerations and controls into the initial design and development phases of systems and processes to prevent incidents.

Proactive Resilience

The combined effect of dynamic policy management and security by design to build a resilient and adaptive security posture.

Key Takeaway

An effective IR program is dynamic, proactively integrating security from design to ongoing policy maintenance.
