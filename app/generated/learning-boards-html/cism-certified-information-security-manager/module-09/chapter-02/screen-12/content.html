Policy Lifecycle

Policy Review, Approval, and Communication

Establish clear procedures for policy review, approval, and communication to ensure organizational buy-in and compliance.

1. Annual Review

Review policy at least annually or upon significant changes (threats, tech, regulations).

2. Stakeholder Involvement

Involve legal, HR, and business unit leaders to ensure broad alignment and buy-in.

3. Executive Approval

Obtain formal approval from executive leadership to grant the policy authority.

4. Effective Communication

Communicate policy to all employees via training, documentation, and reminders.

Key Takeaway

A policy is only effective if it's regularly reviewed, formally approved, and actively communicated throughout the organization.
