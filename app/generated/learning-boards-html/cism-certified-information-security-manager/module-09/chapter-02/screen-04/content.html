Core Concept

The Cornerstone of Incident Response

A robust Incident Response Policy is a strategic declaration from leadership, defining the organization's commitment to managing security incidents.

What is an IR Policy?

It's a high-level document outlining the organization's strategic approach to preparing for, detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents.

CISM's Role

As a CISM, you champion this policy, ensuring it reflects the organization's risk profile, legal obligations, and business priorities.

Why it Matters

It provides the necessary authority and framework for all subsequent incident response procedures, ensuring a coordinated and effective response.

Key Takeaway

An Incident Response Policy is more than a document; it's a strategic commitment from leadership that underpins all security incident management.
