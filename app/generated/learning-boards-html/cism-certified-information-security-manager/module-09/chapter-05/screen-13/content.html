Incident Response Process

Structured Incident Analysis: Scoping, Timeline, and Root Cause

Deepening the Investigation to Understand the Attack

3.

Scope the Incident

  • Determine affected systems, users, and data.
  • Assess the attacker's penetration depth.
  • Understand potential data exfiltration or impact.
4.

Reconstruct Timeline

  • Reconstruct the sequence of events.
  • Understand the attack methodology (initial access, lateral movement).
  • Crucial for understanding attacker actions.
5.

Initial Root Cause Analysis

  • Identify the initial entry point or vulnerability exploited.
  • Inform future preventative measures.

Concrete Example: Timeline Reconstruction

Logs show a user clicked a phishing link at 10:00 AM, a malicious executable ran at 10:05 AM, a new admin account was created at 10:15 AM, and unusual outbound traffic started at 10:30 AM. This timeline clearly outlines the attack progression, from initial compromise to data exfiltration.

Key Takeaway

Scoping, timeline, and root cause analysis are vital for a complete understanding of an incident, enabling effective containment, eradication, and recovery.
