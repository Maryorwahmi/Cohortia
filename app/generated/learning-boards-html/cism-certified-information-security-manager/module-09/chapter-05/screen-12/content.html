Structured Incident Analysis: Prioritization and Correlation

Making Sense of Alerts and Initial Scoping

1.

Prioritize the Alert

  • Based on incident severity defined in IRP.
  • Consider potential impact to CIA (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) and business operations.
  • Focus resources on the most critical threats first.
2.

Correlate Events

  • Gather additional context from other log sources.
  • Confirm the incident and understand its initial scope.
  • Determine if it's isolated or part of a larger attack.

Concrete Example: Correlating a Suspicious Login

An EDR alert flags a suspicious login from an unusual IP. The IRT correlates this with firewall logs (to check for blocked attempts from that IP), network flow data (to see traffic patterns), and email logs (to check for recent phishing attempts targeting the user). This confirms a potential compromise and reveals the attack vector.

Key Takeaway

Prioritization ensures critical incidents are addressed first, while correlation provides the necessary context to understand the true nature and scope of a security event.
