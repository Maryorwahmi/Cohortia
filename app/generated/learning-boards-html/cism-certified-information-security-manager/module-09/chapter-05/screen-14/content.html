Baseline Establishment and Anomaly Detection

Identifying Subtle Threats by Knowing What's Normal

Establishing a Baseline

  • Profile normal network traffic, user behavior, and system processes.
  • Define what 'normal' looks like for your environment.
  • Crucial for identifying deviations.

Anomaly Detection

  • Identify significant deviations from the established baseline.
  • Flag anomalies for further investigation.
  • Effective for catching zero-day attacks and novel threats.

Concrete Example: Outbound Traffic Anomaly

A workstation typically has minimal outbound network traffic. Suddenly, a SIEM alert indicates a massive spike in outbound data from this workstation to an external IP address. This anomaly, deviating from the established baseline, strongly suggests data exfiltration or a compromised system.

Key Takeaway

Baselines and anomaly detection are powerful proactive measures, enabling the identification of subtle and unknown threats by highlighting deviations from expected behavior.
