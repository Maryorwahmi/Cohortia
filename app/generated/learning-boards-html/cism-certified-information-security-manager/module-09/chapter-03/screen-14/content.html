Core IRP Principles

Essential Takeaways

Incident Response Plan (IRP)

An operational document detailing procedures, roles, and resources for handling security incidents, translating policy into action.

NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2

A widely recognized framework outlining the six phases of the incident response lifecycle, serving as an industry standard.

Six Phases of IR

Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, and Post-Incident Activity – the structured approach to incident handling.

Summary Takeaway

A robust IRP, guided by the NIST framework's six phases, is essential for proactive and effective management of security incidents, ensuring organizational resilience.
