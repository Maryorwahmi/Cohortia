Core Concept

The Incident Response Plan (IRP)

Translating Strategy into Action

What is an IRP?

The Incident Response Plan is an operational document that provides detailed, step-by-step procedures for the Incident Response Team (IRT) to follow during a security incident. It's the 'how-to' guide for incident management.

IRP vs. Policy

Unlike a high-level Incident Response Policy (which sets strategic intent), the IRP contains granular procedures, checklists, and contact information for execution. The policy is 'what to do,' the IRP is 'how to do it.'

Key Takeaway

The IRP is the operational blueprint, translating strategic policy into actionable steps for effective incident handling, ensuring preparedness and minimizing impact.

Why it Matters for CISMs

CISMs are responsible for ensuring the IRP is comprehensive, regularly updated, effectively communicated, and trained upon. It's a critical component of an organization's overall security posture.

Common Misconception

Believing a high-level policy is sufficient. Without the detailed procedures of an IRP, incident response can become chaotic and ineffective, leading to greater damage and recovery time.
