NIST IR Lifecycle

Phase 6: Post-Incident Activity

Learning and Continuous Improvement

01

Conduct Thorough Review

Analyze what happened, how the response performed, and what could be improved in a structured post-incident review meeting.

02

Document Lessons Learned

Document findings, including successes, challenges, and recommendations for improving the IRP, procedures, and technology.

03

Implement Improvements

Use the feedback loop to update the IRP, refine procedures, enhance training, and strengthen overall security posture.

Example: Communication Gap

A review reveals delays in notifying legal counsel. The IRP is updated with a clear communication matrix and automated alerts for legal stakeholders.

Risk: Skipping This Phase

Skipping post-incident review means missing critical learning opportunities, leading to repeated mistakes and a stagnant incident response capability.

Key Takeaway

Post-Incident Activity is vital for continuous improvement, transforming incidents into learning opportunities to strengthen the IR program and organizational resilience.
