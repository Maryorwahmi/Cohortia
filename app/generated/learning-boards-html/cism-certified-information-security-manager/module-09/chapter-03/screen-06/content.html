NIST IR Lifecycle

Phase 1: Preparation

Building a Proactive Defense

01

Establish Policy & Plan

Develop the Incident Response Policy and the detailed IRP, defining roles, responsibilities, and procedures.

02

Form & Train IRT

Assemble and train the Incident Response Team, ensuring they have the necessary skills and knowledge to act effectively.

03

Acquire Tools & Systems

Obtain forensic workstations, secure communication channels, logging systems, and SIEM solutions.

04

Harden & Monitor Systems

Implement security hardening measures and establish continuous monitoring to detect anomalies early.

Consequence of Neglect

Without adequate preparation, incident response becomes reactive and chaotic, leading to increased damage and recovery time.

Key Takeaway

The Preparation phase is crucial for establishing a proactive defense, ensuring the IRT, tools, and systems are ready *before* an incident occurs.
