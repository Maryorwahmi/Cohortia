IRP Best Practices

Actionable, Adaptable, and Integrated IRP

Ensuring Organizational Resilience

Actionable & Adaptable

The IRP must contain clear, executable procedures and be flexible enough to adapt to new threats and technologies. It's a living document, not static.

Regular Review & Testing

Conduct tabletop exercises and simulated incidents to identify gaps, refine procedures, and ensure the IRP remains effective and current.

Key Takeaway

An effective IRP is a living, actionable, and adaptable document, regularly tested and integrated with BCP/DRP for holistic organizational resilience.

Integration with BCP/DRP
IRP Focus

Immediate technical response to security incidents.

BCP/DRP Focus

Broader organizational resilience and recovery of business functions.

The CISM must ensure seamless handoffs and coordinated efforts between these plans to achieve holistic organizational resilience. They are complementary, not isolated.

Safety Note: Unintended Disruption

Ensure containment actions do not inadvertently cause further disruption or data loss. Forensic evidence must be handled meticulously to maintain chain of custody.
