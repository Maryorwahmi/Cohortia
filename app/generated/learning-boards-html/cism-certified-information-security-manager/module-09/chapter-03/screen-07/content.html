NIST IR Lifecycle

Phase 2: Identification

Detecting and Confirming Security Events

01

Continuous Monitoring

Actively monitor logs, IDS/IPS, SIEM systems, and user reports for suspicious activities or anomalies.

02

Event Analysis & Confirmation

Analyze detected events to confirm if they are actual security incidents, distinguishing them from false positives.

03

Scope & Severity Assessment

Determine the extent of the incident's impact and prioritize its severity based on organizational risk criteria.

Example: Phishing Alert

A user reports a suspicious email. The IRT investigates email headers, links, and attachments to confirm if it's a phishing attempt and assess potential compromise.

Common Pitfall

Dismissing alerts too quickly or overreacting to false positives without thorough analysis, which can lead to missed incidents or wasted resources.

Key Takeaway

Accurate and timely Identification through continuous monitoring and analysis is crucial for confirming security events and prioritizing an effective response.
