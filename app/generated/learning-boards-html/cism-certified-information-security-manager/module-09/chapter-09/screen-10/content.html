Maintaining the Chain of Custody

A Detailed Record of Evidence Possession and Handling

Key Steps in Chain of Custody

  • Meticulous Documentation: Record who, what, when, where, and how.
  • Cryptographic Hashing: Generate hashes (MD5, SHA256) before and after analysis.
  • Write Protection: Use hardware/software write-blockers during acquisition.
  • Secure Storage: Store evidence in physically and logically secure locations.
  • Chain of Custody Form: Document every transfer of possession.
Example Scenario

An analyst acquires a disk image, hashes it, transports it in a sealed bag, and logs its transfer to a secure lab. Each step is documented and signed by involved parties.

Key Takeaway

A unbroken, documented chain of custody is the bedrock of forensic evidence, ensuring its integrity and legal standing in any proceeding.
