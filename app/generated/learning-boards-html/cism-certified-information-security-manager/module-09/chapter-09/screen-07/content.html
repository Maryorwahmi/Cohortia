CCPA/CPRA and HIPAA

Key US Data Privacy and Healthcare Breach Notification Laws

Key Requirements Comparison

CCPA/CPRA (California)

  • Protects personal information of California residents.
  • Requires notification to affected individuals in case of a breach.

HIPAA (US Healthcare)

  • Applies to Protected Health Information (PHI).
  • Mandates notification to individuals, HHS, and sometimes media.
Important Distinction

The definition of 'personal information' under CCPA/CPRA and 'Protected Health Information (PHI)' under HIPAA are specific and must be carefully reviewed to determine applicability.

Key Takeaway

Organizations handling data of California residents or PHI must have specific incident response plans tailored to CCPA/CPRA and HIPAA requirements.
