Global Regulation

GDPR: Protecting EU Personal Data

Mandatory Breach Notification for European Residents

Key GDPR Notification Requirements

  • Scope: Applies to personal data of EU residents, globally.
  • Supervisory Authority: Notify within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach.
  • Affected Individuals: Notify without undue delay if high risk to rights/freedoms.
  • Content: Must include nature of breach, categories of data, likely consequences, and mitigation measures.
Example Scenario

A cloud service provider based in the US suffers a breach impacting customer names, emails, and payment details. If 30% of these customers are EU residents, GDPR notification rules apply to that subset of data.

Key Takeaway

The 72-hour notification window under GDPR is critical; rapid incident assessment and legal consultation are paramount for compliance.
