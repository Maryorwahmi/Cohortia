Ethical Responsibilities of a CISM

Upholding Trust and Integrity During a Crisis

Core Ethical Duties

  • Transparency: Be honest with stakeholders, balancing with legal advice.
  • Privacy: Protect affected individuals' data, disclose only what's necessary.
  • Accountability: Take responsibility for security posture and response.
  • Due Diligence: Ensure thorough and competent incident response.
  • Avoiding Conflict of Interest: Prevent personal interests from influencing decisions.
Ethical Pitfall

Prioritizing short-term reputational protection over long-term transparency can lead to greater damage and loss of trust in the long run.

Key Takeaway

A CISM's ethical conduct during an incident is paramount for maintaining organizational integrity, stakeholder trust, and long-term reputation.
