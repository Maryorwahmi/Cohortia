Cross-Border Data Flows and Jurisdictions

Navigating International Legal Complexities in Incident Response

Challenges of International Incidents

  • Multiple Laws: A single incident can trigger laws in several countries.
  • Conflicting Requirements: Different jurisdictions may have contradictory mandates.
  • Data Residency: Location of data storage impacts applicable laws.
  • Varied Notification: Different procedures for different groups of affected individuals.
  • Increased Fines: Non-compliance can lead to significant international penalties.
Common Mistake

Ignoring the complexities of cross-border data flows, assuming that local laws are sufficient, leading to non-compliance and severe international fines.

Key Takeaway

A global incident response plan must proactively address cross-border data flows and international legal jurisdictions to ensure comprehensive compliance.
