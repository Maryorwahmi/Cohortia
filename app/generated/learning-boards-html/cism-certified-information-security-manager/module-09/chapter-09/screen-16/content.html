Chapter Summary

Key Concepts to Remember

Advanced Coordination and Global Awareness

Legal Counsel Coordination

Essential partnership with legal advisors to ensure compliance, protect legal interests, and guide communication strategies.

Law Enforcement Coordination

Engagement with police or cybercrime units for criminal incidents, balancing their objectives with organizational recovery and reputation.

Cross-border Data Flows

Implications of international data movement on legal and regulatory compliance, requiring a global perspective for incident response.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive planning for legal, law enforcement, and international data complexities is vital for a CISM to ensure robust and compliant incident management.
