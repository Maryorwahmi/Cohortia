Coordinating with Legal Counsel

Ensuring Compliance and Protecting Organizational Interests

Key Roles of Legal Counsel

  • Notification Obligations: Advise on specific breach notification requirements.
  • Potential Liabilities: Assess and mitigate legal risks and financial exposure.
  • Evidence Admissibility: Ensure forensic evidence meets legal standards.
  • Communication Strategy: Guide public and internal messaging.
  • External Liaison: Act as primary contact for external legal inquiries.
Common Mistake

Delaying the involvement of legal counsel. Early engagement ensures that all incident response actions are legally sound from the outset.

Key Takeaway

Legal counsel is an indispensable partner in incident response, providing critical guidance to ensure compliance and protect the organization's legal standing.
