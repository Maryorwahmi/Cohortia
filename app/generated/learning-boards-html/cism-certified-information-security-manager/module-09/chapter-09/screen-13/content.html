Engaging Law Enforcement

When and How to Involve Police and Cybercrime Units

Considerations for Law Enforcement Engagement

  • Criminal Acts: Essential for incidents involving illegal activity.
  • Legal Counsel: Decision made in conjunction with legal advisors.
  • Differing Objectives: Law enforcement focuses on prosecution; organizations on recovery.
  • Evidence Sharing: May require providing access to systems or data.
  • Pre-existing Relationships: Can streamline cooperation during a crisis.
Common Mistake

Engaging law enforcement without prior consultation with legal counsel, which can inadvertently compromise evidence or organizational interests.

Key Takeaway

Strategic engagement with law enforcement, guided by legal counsel, is crucial for criminal incidents, balancing justice with organizational resilience.

Balancing Objectives with Law Enforcement
Law Enforcement Goals: Prosecution
Organization Goals: Recovery
Law Enforcement Goals: Justice
Organization Goals: Reputation
Aligning these differing objectives is key to successful collaboration.
