Chapter Summary

Key Concepts to Remember

Reinforcing Incident Response Principles

Data Breach Notification Laws

Legal mandates requiring organizations to inform affected individuals and/or regulatory bodies of security breaches (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA).

Evidence Handling

Procedures for collecting, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence to maintain its integrity and admissibility in legal proceedings.

Chain of Custody

A documented chronological record of the possession, transfer, analysis, and disposition of physical or electronic evidence.

Ethical Responsibilities

Moral obligations of a CISM during an incident, including transparency, privacy, and accountability to maintain trust and integrity.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering the legal, regulatory, and ethical dimensions of incident response is as crucial as technical proficiency for comprehensive organizational protection.
