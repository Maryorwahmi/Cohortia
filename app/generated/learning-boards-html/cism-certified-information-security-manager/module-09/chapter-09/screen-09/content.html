Evidence Handling Fundamentals

Preserving Integrity for Legal Admissibility

Core Principles of Evidence Handling

  • Integrity: Evidence must not be altered or corrupted.
  • Authenticity: Proof that the evidence is what it purports to be.
  • Reliability: Consistent and accurate results from analysis.
  • Admissibility: Meets legal standards for use in court.
  • Documentation: Meticulous records of all actions taken.
Common Mistake

Failing to document every step of evidence collection and handling. Any gap in documentation can compromise the evidence's legal standing.

Key Takeaway

The foundation of effective incident response for potential legal action lies in rigorous, documented evidence handling to ensure its integrity and admissibility.
