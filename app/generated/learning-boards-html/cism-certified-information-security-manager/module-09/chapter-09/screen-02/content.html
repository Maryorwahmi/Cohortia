Identify Legal Frameworks

Learn to recognize key data breach notification laws like GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA. This skill ensures your organization complies with diverse jurisdictional requirements, preventing legal repercussions.

02

Describe Evidence Handling

Understand the requirements for proper evidence collection, preservation, and chain of custody. This ensures forensic evidence is admissible in court and maintains its integrity throughout an investigation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Explain Ethical Responsibilities

Grasp the moral obligations of an Information Security Manager during an incident, focusing on privacy, transparency, and accountability. This ensures decisions align with organizational values and stakeholder trust.
