Integrating IR with Business Resilience

Ensuring Continuous Improvement and Executive Alignment

CISM's Integration & Improvement Focus
  • Integrate IR with Business Continuity (BCP) and Disaster Recovery (DRP).
  • Oversee continuous improvement of the IR program.
  • Ensure alignment with risk appetite and regulatory obligations.
  • Champion IR program to secure executive buy-in and budget.
Concrete Example

During a major ransomware attack, the IR team contains the breach. The CISM ensures a smooth handover to the DRP team to restore systems from secure backups, minimizing business disruption and data loss.

The Resilience Cycle
1

IR: Immediate Response - Contain, eradicate, recover from security incidents.

2

BCP: Business Continuity - Maintain critical business functions during disruptions.

3

DRP: Disaster Recovery - Restore IT infrastructure after major outages.

4

Continuous Improvement - Learn from incidents to enhance all plans.

Common Misconception

Incident response is a standalone technical function. In reality, it must be tightly integrated with BCP and DRP for holistic organizational resilience.

Key Takeaway

The CISM ensures IR is a cohesive part of overall business resilience, driving continuous improvement and executive support.
