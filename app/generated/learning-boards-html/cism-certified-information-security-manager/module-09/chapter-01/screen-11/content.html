Common Pitfalls in Incident Response

Overcoming Challenges in IR Program Establishment

Key Pitfalls to Avoid
  • Lack of executive support and buy-in.
  • Insufficient funding and resource allocation.
  • Undefined roles and responsibilities.
  • Inadequate training for the IR team.
  • Failure to regularly test the IR plan.
Concrete Example

A company has a detailed IR plan but no budget for specialized forensic tools or external expertise. When a sophisticated breach occurs, their internal team is overwhelmed and unable to perform a thorough investigation.

Impact of Common Mistakes
Delayed Response

Lack of clarity or resources slows down critical actions.

Increased Damage

Uncontrolled incidents lead to greater financial and reputational harm.

Compliance Failures

Inability to meet regulatory reporting and response requirements.

Erosion of Trust

Stakeholders lose confidence in the organization's security posture.

Key Takeaway

Addressing foundational weaknesses like executive support, funding, roles, and training is crucial for a resilient IR program.
