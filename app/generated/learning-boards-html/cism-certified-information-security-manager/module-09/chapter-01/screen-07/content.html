Strategic Oversight: Policy and Resources

Guiding the Incident Response Framework

CISM's Core Responsibilities
  • Develop overarching IR policy and strategy.
  • Ensure adequate resources (staff, tools, budget).
  • Establish clear roles and responsibilities.
  • Integrate IR with broader business continuity.
  • Oversee continuous improvement of the program.
Common Pitfall

Assuming technical teams will spontaneously coordinate during an incident. Without clear roles and policy, chaos can ensue, prolonging recovery.

Key Areas of CISM Influence
Policy Development

Crafting the IR blueprint.

Resource Allocation

Securing budget, staff, and tools.

Roles & Responsibilities

Defining who does what during a crisis.

Executive Communication

Reporting status and impact to leadership.

Key Takeaway

The CISM provides strategic leadership, policy, and resources, transforming IR from reactive chaos to a controlled, systematic function.
