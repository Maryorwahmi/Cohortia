Building a Practical and Resilient IR Plan

Strategies to Avoid Common Mistakes

Strategies for Success
  • Regularly test the plan through simulations and drills.
  • Adopt a holistic approach: people, process, and technology.
  • Ensure the plan is practical, concise, and actionable.
  • Secure top management endorsement for the IR policy.
  • Continuously refine based on lessons learned and evolving threats.
Concrete Example

A company conducts a quarterly tabletop exercise simulating a data breach. This reveals gaps in their communication protocol and data recovery procedures, allowing them to update the plan proactively before a real incident occurs.

Avoiding Pitfalls: Key Actions
Regular Testing

Simulations and drills validate plan effectiveness.

Holistic View

Balance technology with people and processes.

Practicality

Keep plans simple and actionable, not overly complex.

Safety & Forensics

Ensure responder safety and preserve critical evidence.

Key Takeaway

A living, tested, and holistic IR plan, continuously refined, is essential for effective and resilient incident management.
