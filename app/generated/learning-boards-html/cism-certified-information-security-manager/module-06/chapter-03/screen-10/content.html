Enforcement and Exceptions

Ensuring compliance while allowing justified deviations

Policies must have clear consequences for non-compliance, alongside a formal process for handling justified exceptions to maintain control and accountability.

  • •**Enforcement:** Clear consequences for non-compliance (disciplinary, legal).
  • •**Consistency:** Demonstrates management commitment and reinforces adherence.
  • •**Exceptions:** Formal process for requests, risk assessment, and documented approval.
  • •**Safety Note:** Uncontrolled exceptions can quickly erode policy effectiveness.
Key Takeaway

Consistent enforcement coupled with a structured exception process is vital for maintaining policy integrity and preventing security gaps.
