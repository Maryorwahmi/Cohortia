Policy Lifecycle Phase 3

Review: Collaborative Feedback and Refinement

Engaging stakeholders to identify conflicts and ambiguities

The draft policy is circulated to various stakeholders for feedback, identifying potential conflicts, ambiguities, and unintended consequences.

  • •**Purpose:** Identify conflicts, ambiguities, and unintended consequences.
  • •**Key Actions:** Gather feedback, incorporate revisions, ensure alignment.
  • •**Common Pitfall:** Limiting review to too few stakeholders, leading to overlooked issues.
Key Takeaway

Comprehensive stakeholder review is essential to ensure policies are legally sound, operationally feasible, and strategically aligned before final approval.

Key Stakeholders in Policy Review

Ensuring comprehensive assessment from all angles

Legal Counsel

Ensures compliance with laws and regulations.

Human Resources

Assesses employee impact and disciplinary actions.

Business Unit Owners

Evaluates operational impact and feasibility.

IT Operations

Assesses technical feasibility and implementation.
