Approval: Granting Official Authority

Formal sign-off from senior management or governance body

Formal sign-off from senior management or a designated governance body grants the policy official authority and mandate.

  • •**Key Action:** Formal sign-off by senior management or governance committee.
  • •**Purpose:** Grants official authority and organizational backing.
  • •**Consequence of Omission:** Policy lacks enforceability and organizational support.
Key Takeaway

Without formal approval, a policy remains a suggestion, lacking the necessary authority and organizational commitment for effective implementation and enforcement.

Why official endorsement is non-negotiable
