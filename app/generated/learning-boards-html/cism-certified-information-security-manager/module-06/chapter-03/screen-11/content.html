Policy Lifecycle Phase 7

Regular Review and Updates

Ensuring policies remain relevant and effective

Policies require regular review (at least annually) to assess their effectiveness, relevance, and accuracy against evolving threats, technology, and regulations.

  • •**Frequency:** At least annually, or more frequently with significant changes.
  • •**Purpose:** Assess effectiveness, relevance, and accuracy.
  • •**Inputs:** Feedback from audits, incidents, and employee suggestions.
  • •**Common Pitfall:** Treating policies as 'set it and forget it' documents.
Key Takeaway

Continuous review and timely updates are critical for maintaining an agile and resilient information security program that adapts to dynamic internal and external environments.

Drivers for Policy Review

What necessitates policy updates

Evolving Threat Landscape

New attack vectors and vulnerabilities.

Technological Advancements

New systems, software, and infrastructure.

Business Operations Changes

Mergers, acquisitions, new services.

Regulatory Environment Shifts

New laws, compliance requirements.
