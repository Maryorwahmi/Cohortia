Key Concepts: Policy Foundations

Essential terms and ideas to remember

Policy Lifecycle

The complete process of developing, implementing, and maintaining an information security policy, from initiation to review.

Stakeholder Engagement

Involving various internal and external parties (Legal, HR, IT, Senior Management) in policy development and review.

Policy Dissemination

The process of effectively communicating policies to all relevant employees, ensuring their awareness and understanding.

These concepts form the bedrock of effective information security governance.
