Policy Lifecycle Phase 1

Initiation: The Starting Point

Defining scope, objectives, and the problem to solve

This phase identifies the need for a new or updated policy, defining its scope, objectives, and the problem it aims to address.

  • •**Triggers:** New business requirements, regulatory changes, security incidents, or risk assessment findings.
  • •**Key Actions:** Define policy scope, objectives, and the specific problem to be solved.
  • •**Common Pitfall:** Rushing this phase can lead to ill-defined policies that miss the mark.
Key Takeaway

A clear and thorough initiation phase is crucial for building a policy that effectively addresses specific security needs and aligns with organizational goals.
