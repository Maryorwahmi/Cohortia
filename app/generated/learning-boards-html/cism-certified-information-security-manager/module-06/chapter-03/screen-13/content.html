Scenario: Remote Work Policy Dissemination

Developing a communication plan for a new security policy

As the InfoSec Manager at SecureBank, outline a communication and dissemination plan for a new 'Remote Work Security Policy' to ensure maximum understanding and compliance.

  • •**New Policy:** Remote Work Security Policy.
  • •**Target Audience:** All employees, especially those with remote work privileges.
  • •**Goal:** Ensure maximum understanding and compliance across diverse employee groups.
Key Takeaway

A well-structured communication plan is critical for successful policy implementation, ensuring employees are informed, understand their roles, and comply with new security requirements.

Communication & Dissemination Plan Template

Key steps for rolling out a new policy

1

Initial Announcement (Week 1)

Company-wide email from CISO, brief overview, link to policy, call to action for training.

2

Detailed Policy Access & Repository

Intranet portal with searchable, version-controlled policy, FAQs.

3

Mandatory Training (Week 2-4)

Online modules or live webinars focusing on secure home network, data handling, device security.

4

Ongoing Reinforcement & Reminders

Monthly newsletters, login banners, team meeting talking points.

5

Acknowledgement of Understanding

Digital signature via LMS or HR portal, annually.

6

Feedback & Q&A Mechanism

Dedicated email alias or Q&A sessions for employee queries.
