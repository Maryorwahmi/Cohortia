Drafting: Crafting the Policy Document

Balancing clarity, conciseness, and enforceability

Subject matter experts and policy writers create the document, focusing on clarity, conciseness, and legal enforceability.

  • •**Key Participants:** Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) and policy writers.
  • •**Core Principles:** Clarity, conciseness, and legal enforceability.
  • •**Language:** Use plain language, avoid technical jargon for broad understanding.
  • •**Common Pitfalls:** Being too prescriptive (should be standards) or too vague (hinders enforcement).
Key Takeaway

Effective policy drafting requires a careful balance between providing clear, actionable guidance and maintaining flexibility, ensuring the policy is both understandable and enforceable.

Drafting Best Practices

Ensuring policies are effective and understood

Plain Language

Avoid jargon; make it accessible to all employees.

Concise & Clear

Get straight to the point; avoid ambiguity.

Legally Enforceable

Consult legal counsel to ensure compliance.

Balance Flexibility & Specificity

Avoid being too rigid or too vague.
