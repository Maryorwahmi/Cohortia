Key Concepts: Policy Operations

Ensuring adherence and continuous improvement

Policy Enforcement

The act of ensuring compliance with policies, including clear consequences for non-adherence.

Exception Process

A formal procedure for requesting, assessing, and approving (or denying) deviations from established policies.

Policy Review

The periodic assessment of policies to ensure their continued relevance, effectiveness, and alignment with organizational goals.

These concepts ensure policies are actively managed and adapted to maintain a strong security posture.
