Measuring Program Success: Beyond Compliance

Assessing True Behavioral Change and Strategic Impact

Behavioral Changes

Observe changes in employee practices, such as locking workstations, proper document disposal, and challenging unknown individuals.

  • •Direct evidence of improved security habits.
  • •Reflects real-world risk reduction.
  • •Goes beyond theoretical knowledge.
Example

A noticeable increase in employees reporting suspicious emails rather than opening them.

Strategic Reporting

CISM reports metrics to senior management, translating technical findings into business impact and risk reduction.

  • •Justifies ongoing investment in security.
  • •Aligns security with business objectives.
  • •Informs strategic decision-making.
Example

Presenting a trend of reduced data loss incidents directly linked to improved employee practices.

Key Takeaway

Focus on behavioral metrics and strategic reporting to demonstrate the true value and risk reduction achieved by your security programs.
