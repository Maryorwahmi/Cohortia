Diverse Delivery Methods: Online & Live Approaches

Combining Scalable Online Modules with Interactive Live Sessions

Online Modules / E-learning

Self-paced, scalable, and ideal for foundational knowledge, often including interactive quizzes and simulations.

  • •High scalability for large workforces.
  • •Consistent content delivery.
  • •Flexible scheduling for employees.
Example

An interactive module on identifying common phishing email characteristics.

Live Training Sessions / Webinars

Allows for Q&A, deeper dives, and tailored content for specific teams, fostering direct interaction.

  • •Real-time interaction and Q&A.
  • •Customizable for specific team needs.
  • •Builds a sense of community and shared responsibility.
Example

A workshop on new data classification policies for the R&D department.

Key Takeaway

A blended approach maximizes reach and engagement, ensuring both broad foundational knowledge and targeted, interactive learning.
