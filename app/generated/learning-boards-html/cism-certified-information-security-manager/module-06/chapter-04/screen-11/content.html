Measuring Program Success: Core Metrics

Quantifying Program Effectiveness and Demonstrating ROI

Key Metrics for Evaluation
Phishing Click Rates

Decreasing trend indicates improved vigilance.

Incident Reporting

Increase suggests heightened awareness.

Audit Findings

Reduction in human error findings.

Quiz Scores

Assesses knowledge retention and understanding.

Demonstrate ROI

Quantifiable metrics provide evidence of program value and risk reduction.

Identify Improvement Areas

Data helps pinpoint weaknesses and refine future training content.

Key Takeaway

Effective measurement moves beyond simple completion rates to assess actual behavioral change and risk reduction, proving program value.
