Strategic Program Design: Content & Regulatory Alignment

Addressing Key Threats and Regulatory Mandates

Risk Assessment Driven

Prioritize topics based on the most significant threats to the organization.

Common Threat Topics

Regulatory Compliance

Align content with mandates like HIPAA, GDPR, and PCI DSS to avoid penalties.

Key Takeaway
