Key Concepts (Part 1)

Essential Takeaways from This Chapter

Security Awareness

Educating employees about risks, impact, and their responsibilities to foster a security-conscious culture.

Security Training

Providing specific skills and knowledge for employees to perform job functions securely.

Target Audience Analysis

Identifying employee groups to tailor awareness and training content to their specific roles and risks.

Summary Takeaway

A strong security program starts with understanding the distinct roles of awareness and training, and then strategically tailoring content to meet diverse organizational needs.
