Strategic Program Design: Identifying Target Audiences

Tailoring Education to Specific Roles and Responsibilities

General Employees

Need foundational awareness on phishing, password hygiene, and data handling.

Developers

Require secure coding training to prevent application vulnerabilities.

IT Administrators

Need training on secure system configuration and incident response.

Senior Management

Require awareness of strategic risks and governance responsibilities.

Audience-Specific Needs

Tailored Content

Key Takeaway

Effective programs are not generic; they are strategically tailored to the unique risks and responsibilities of each target audience.
