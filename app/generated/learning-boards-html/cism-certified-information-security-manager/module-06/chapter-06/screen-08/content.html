Design Principles

Minimization of Attack Surface

Reducing Potential Entry Points

Core Definition

Reduce the number of potential entry points for attackers by removing unnecessary services, ports, and functionalities.

Practical Application

Disable unused network ports, uninstall unnecessary software, and remove default credentials to reduce exposure.

Simplicity in Design

Reducing Complexity for Better Security

Core Definition

Complex systems are harder to secure and verify. Simpler designs reduce the likelihood of hidden vulnerabilities.

Benefit

Easier to understand, audit, and maintain, leading to fewer security flaws and quicker identification of issues.

Key Takeaway

Minimizing attack surface and embracing simplicity are fundamental for creating systems that are both secure and manageable.
