Design Principle

Defense in Depth

Layering Multiple Security Controls

Core Idea

Employ multiple, independent security controls to protect assets. If one control fails, another is ready to take its place, preventing a single point of failure.

Why it's Effective

  • Increases resilience against sophisticated attacks.
  • Reduces the impact of a single control's failure.
  • Provides multiple opportunities to detect and prevent intrusions.
