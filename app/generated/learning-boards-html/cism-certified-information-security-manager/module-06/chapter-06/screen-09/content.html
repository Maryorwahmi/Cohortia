Why Integrate Security?

  • Far more effective and cost-efficient to fix issues early.
  • Builds inherent resilience into the system.
  • Minimizes vulnerabilities and reduces overall risk.
Common Mistake

Treating security as an optional add-on or a compliance checkbox at the end of the SDLC leads to costly rework and significant risks.

Key Takeaway

Embedding security into every SDLC phase is crucial for building robust systems and avoiding expensive, reactive fixes.
