Defining Confidential Data

This classification applies to information whose unauthorized disclosure could cause moderate to severe harm to the organization, including financial, reputational, or legal impacts.

Key Characteristic

Disclosure causes moderate to severe harm, requiring robust security controls like access controls and encryption.

Control Focus

Requires stringent access controls, encryption (in transit and at rest), and careful handling procedures.

EXAMPLES & IMPLICATIONS
Employee PII

Personal Identifiable Information (e.g., addresses, non-sensitive medical).

Financial Reports

Unreleased quarterly earnings, budget details.

Proprietary Business Plans

Strategic documents, product roadmaps.

Key Takeaway

Confidential/Sensitive data requires robust security measures to prevent moderate to severe harm from unauthorized disclosure, including strict access controls and encryption.
