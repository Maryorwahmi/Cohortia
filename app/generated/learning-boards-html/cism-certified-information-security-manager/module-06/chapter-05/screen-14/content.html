Tailoring Security to Data Sensitivity

Access Control

Public data: Open access. Restricted data: Strict RBAC, MFA, least privilege.

Encryption

Confidential/Restricted data: Encryption in transit and at rest. Public data: Not typically required.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Configured to monitor and prevent unauthorized exfiltration of Confidential and Restricted data.

Retention & Disposal

Policies vary significantly by classification; highly sensitive data requires strict shredding/wiping.

Backup & Recovery

Critical, highly classified data: More frequent backups, stricter RTOs, secure offsite storage.

Safety Note

Misclassifying data can lead to either unnecessary operational burdens or, critically, inadequate protection, leaving the organization vulnerable.

Key Takeaway

Data classification is the blueprint for implementing security controls, ensuring that protection measures are proportionate to the data's sensitivity and risk level.
