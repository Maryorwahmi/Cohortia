Defining Internal Use Only

This classification applies to information not intended for public release, but whose disclosure would not cause significant harm to the organization.

Key Characteristic

Information for internal operations; disclosure causes no significant harm, but is not for public consumption.

Example Scenario

An internal employee directory: not a trade secret, but not meant for public access either.

EXAMPLES & IMPLICATIONS
Internal Memos

Communications within the organization.

Organizational Charts

Structure and reporting lines of the company.

Company Policies

Internal guidelines and procedures.

Key Takeaway

Internal Use Only data requires controlled access within the organization, as its unauthorized disclosure could cause minor, but not significant, harm.
