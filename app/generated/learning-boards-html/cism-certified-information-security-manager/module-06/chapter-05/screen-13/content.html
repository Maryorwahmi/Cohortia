Who is the Data User?

The Data User is any individual who accesses or processes data in the course of their job duties, responsible for handling it according to its classification and the organization's policies.

Core Responsibility

Adhering to data handling policies, understanding access rights, and reporting suspicious activities or incidents.

Example

A marketing specialist accessing customer demographics must follow policies on data usage and sharing.

KEY DUTIES OF A DATA USER
Handle Data per Classification

Ensures data is used and stored according to its sensitivity level.

Report Suspicious Activity

Acts as a frontline defense by identifying and reporting potential incidents.

Adhere to Policies

Follows all organizational security policies and guidelines.

Key Takeaway

Data Users are crucial for day-to-day data protection, responsible for handling information according to its classification and reporting any security concerns.
