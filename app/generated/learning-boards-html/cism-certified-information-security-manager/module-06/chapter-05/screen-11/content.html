Who is the Data Owner?

The Data Owner is the individual (often a business unit manager or executive) who has ultimate responsibility for the data, understanding its business value and regulatory requirements.

Core Responsibility

Ultimate accountability for the data's protection, determining its classification, and approving access requests.

Example

The Head of HR is typically the Data Owner for all employee Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

KEY DUTIES OF A DATA OWNER
Determine Data Classification

Assigns the appropriate sensitivity level to data assets.

Approve Access Requests

Grants or denies user access based on business need and classification.

Accountable for Protection

Ensures data is protected according to its classification and policies.

Key Takeaway

The Data Owner is the ultimate decision-maker and accountable party for data classification and protection, aligning security with business value.
