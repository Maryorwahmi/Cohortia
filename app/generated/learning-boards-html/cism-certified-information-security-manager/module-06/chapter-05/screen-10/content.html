Defining Restricted Data

This classification is for information whose unauthorized disclosure could cause grave or catastrophic harm, leading to severe legal, financial, or reputational damage.

Key Characteristic

Disclosure causes grave or catastrophic harm, demanding the most stringent security controls.

Control Focus

Requires multi-factor authentication, strict RBAC, least privilege, advanced encryption, and robust DLP solutions.

EXAMPLES & IMPLICATIONS
Customer Bank Account Numbers

Highly sensitive financial data.

Trade Secrets

Proprietary formulas, algorithms, unreleased designs.

National Security Information

Government classified data.

Key Takeaway

Restricted/Secret data demands the highest level of protection due to its potential for grave or catastrophic harm, requiring the most stringent security controls.
