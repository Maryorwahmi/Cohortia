Defining Public Data

This classification level applies to information explicitly intended for public consumption, where its disclosure would cause no harm to the organization.

Key Characteristic

Information intended for public release, where disclosure would cause no harm to the organization.

Misconception

'Public' doesn't mean 'unprotected.' While confidentiality isn't a concern, integrity and availability are still important for public trust.

EXAMPLES & IMPLICATIONS
Marketing Materials

Brochures, website content, public advertisements.

Press Releases

Official announcements to media and public.

Public Financial Reports

Annual reports after official release.

Key Takeaway

Public/Unclassified data is meant for broad distribution; its security focus is on maintaining integrity and availability, not confidentiality.
