What is Data Classification?

Data classification is the process of categorizing information assets based on their sensitivity, value, and criticality to the organization, as well as the potential impact of their unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Why it Matters

It's a foundational element for any robust security program, enabling organizations to apply appropriate security controls commensurate with the risk level of the data.

Analogy

Think of it like organizing a library: rare manuscripts get locked vaults, while common books are openly accessible. Digital data needs similar tiered protection.

CLASSIFICATION DRIVERS
Sensitivity

How much harm if exposed?

Value

Its worth to the organization.

Criticality

Impact on business operations.

Potential Impact

Consequences of compromise.

Key Takeaway

Data classification is the essential first step in building a robust security program, ensuring resources are focused on protecting the most critical assets effectively.
