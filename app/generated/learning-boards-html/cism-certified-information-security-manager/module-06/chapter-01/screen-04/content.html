Core Concept

The Strategic Imperative: Aligning Security with Business

Ensuring security efforts directly support organizational goals and risk appetite.

Why it Matters
  • Security investments must directly contribute to business resilience and success.
  • Avoid fragmented, reactive, and ineffective security efforts.
  • Enable business functions like innovation, market expansion, and customer trust.
Foundation Questions
  • What is the organization's mission, vision, and strategic goals?
  • What are the core business functions?
  • What are the critical assets supporting these functions?
  • What are the regulatory and legal obligations?
Key Takeaway

An effective information security strategy is intrinsically linked to business objectives and risk appetite, serving as a critical enabler of organizational success.
