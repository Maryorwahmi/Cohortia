Stakeholder Engagement

The Role of Key Stakeholders

Ensuring buy-in and effective implementation through collaboration.

Key Roles
  • CISO: Primary architect and advocate for the security strategy.
  • Senior Management (CEO, CFO, Legal): Provide executive sponsorship, funding, and organizational priority.
  • Business Unit Leaders: Articulate specific needs, understand operational impact, and champion the strategy within their domains.
  • IT Operations & Development: Provide insights into technical feasibility and implementation challenges.
Common Pitfalls & Solutions
  • Pitfall: Strategy developed in isolation. Solution: Foster continuous communication.
  • Pitfall: Focus solely on technical controls. Solution: Consider business impact and translate jargon.
  • Pitfall: Failure to secure executive commitment. Solution: Demonstrate tangible value and benefits.
Key Takeaway

Broad stakeholder engagement and executive sponsorship are essential for a realistic, well-supported, and successfully implemented information security strategy.
