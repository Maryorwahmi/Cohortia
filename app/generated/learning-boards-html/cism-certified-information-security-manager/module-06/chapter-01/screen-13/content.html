Continuous Improvement

The Strategy as a Living Document

Regular review and updates for evolving threats and business needs.

Why Updates Are Necessary
  • Evolving Threat Landscape: New threats and attack vectors emerge constantly.
  • Technological Advancements: New security tools and capabilities become available.
  • Shifting Business Objectives: Organizational goals and priorities can change over time.
  • Regulatory Changes: Compliance requirements may be updated or introduced.
Review Process

Establish a cadence for review (e.g., annually) that includes:

  • Reassessing the current state.
  • Evaluating the effectiveness of implemented initiatives.
  • Confirming alignment with current business goals and risk appetite.
  • Incorporating lessons learned from incidents and feedback.
Key Takeaway

Treating the information security strategy as a living document ensures it remains adaptive, resilient, and effective in protecting the organization against evolving risks and supporting changing business needs.
