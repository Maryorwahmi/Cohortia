Check Your Understanding

Reinforcing the primary drivers of an information security strategy.

Question

An organization's information security strategy should primarily be driven by which of the following?

A

The latest cybersecurity threats identified by security vendors.

B

The budget allocated by the IT department for security tools.

C

The organization's overall business objectives and risk appetite.

D

The recommendations from the external security auditor.

Key Takeaway

The organization's overall business objectives and risk appetite are the primary drivers for its information security strategy, ensuring security enables and protects the business.
