Bridging the Gap

Initiatives: Bridging Current and Future States

Identifying and prioritizing actions to achieve the target security posture.

Types of Initiatives
  • Technology Implementation (e.g., SIEM, EDR).
  • Policy Development & Updates (e.g., data privacy, access control).
  • Process Improvements (e.g., incident response, vulnerability management).
  • Training & Awareness Programs (e.g., phishing simulations, mandatory training).
  • Organizational Changes (e.g., team restructuring).
Prioritization Criteria

Initiatives should be prioritized based on:

  • Contribution to future state objectives.
  • Impact on reducing key risks.
  • Alignment with business goals.
  • Resource requirements (budget, personnel, time).
Key Takeaway

Translating strategy into action requires identifying concrete initiatives and prioritizing them based on their impact, alignment, and resource feasibility to effectively bridge the gap between current and future states.
