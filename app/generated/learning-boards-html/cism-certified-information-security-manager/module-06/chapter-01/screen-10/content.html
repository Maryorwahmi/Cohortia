Current State Assessment: A Deeper Dive

Evaluating existing controls, vulnerabilities, and the threat landscape.

Assessment Components
  • Existing Security Controls: Inventory and evaluate effectiveness of technologies, configurations, and access controls.
  • Vulnerabilities: Identify weaknesses through scanning, penetration testing, and incident reviews.
  • Threat Landscape: Analyze relevant threats (e.g., nation-state, crime, insider) specific to the organization and industry.
Risk Management Principles

The assessment should leverage risk management principles by considering:

  • Potential Impact: What would be the consequences if a risk materialized?
  • Likelihood: How probable is it that a specific risk will occur?
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive current state assessment, grounded in risk management principles, provides the essential baseline for developing an informed and effective information security strategy.
