Phases of Strategy Development

From assessment to defining the future state and bridging the gap.

Key Phases
  • Current State Assessment: Evaluate existing controls, vulnerabilities, and threat landscape using risk management principles.
  • Define Desired Future State: Outline the target security posture aligned with business objectives and risk tolerance, focusing on measurable outcomes.
  • Bridge the Gap: Identify and prioritize specific initiatives, projects, and changes needed to move from the current to the future state.
Initiative Examples
  • Implementing new security technologies.
  • Updating security policies and procedures.
  • Enhancing employee security awareness training.
  • Restructuring security teams for better efficiency.
Key Takeaway

Strategy development is an iterative cycle of assessing the present, envisioning the future, and planning concrete steps to close the gap.
