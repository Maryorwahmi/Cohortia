Avoiding Pitfalls in Strategy Development

Common errors and how to prevent them.

Common Pitfalls
  • Developing the strategy in isolation without cross-departmental input.
  • Focusing solely on technical controls without considering business impact or human factors.
  • Failing to translate technical jargon into clear business terms.
  • Lack of genuine executive commitment and sponsorship.
Prevention Strategies
  • Foster continuous communication and collaboration across all organizational levels.
  • Engage business unit leaders to understand their unique challenges and requirements.
  • Explain technical concepts in terms of business risk and value.
  • Actively seek and maintain executive sponsorship by demonstrating strategic importance.
Key Takeaway

A successful information security strategy requires broad collaboration, clear communication, and strong executive backing to avoid common pitfalls and ensure effectiveness.
