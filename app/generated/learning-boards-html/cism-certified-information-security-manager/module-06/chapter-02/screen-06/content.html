Policies: Purpose and Common Mistakes

Articulating 'What' and 'Why' Effectively

Core Purpose
  • Provide authority and justification for security controls.
  • Reflect the organization's risk appetite.
  • Address legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Set the strategic direction for information security.
Key Idea

Policies provide authority and justification for security controls, but must be clear, concise, and understandable by all employees.

Common Mistakes
  • Too Technical: Uses jargon, alienates non-IT staff.
  • Too Vague: Lacks clear guidance, open to interpretation.
  • Infrequent Review: Becomes outdated, loses relevance.
  • Lack of Management Support: Not enforced, seen as optional.
Pro-Tip: Policy Clarity

Ensure policies are written in plain language, avoiding technical jargon, so they are accessible and understandable to all employees, fostering a security-aware culture.
