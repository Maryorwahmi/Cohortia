Policies: The Strategic Foundation

Defining the Organization's Security Stance

What are Policies?

High-level, mandatory statements from management that define the organization's overall commitment to information security. They articulate what must be done and why it's important.

Key Idea

Policies are broad, mandatory statements from management defining the organization's overall security commitment and stance.

Key Characteristics
  • High-level: Focus on principles, not specifics.
  • Mandatory: Compliance is required for all.
  • Broad Scope: Apply across the entire organization.
  • Technology-Agnostic: Independent of specific technologies.
  • Stable: Rarely change, reflecting enduring commitment.
Examples
  • Acceptable Use Policy: Employees must use IT resources responsibly.
  • Data Classification Policy: All organizational data must be classified.
  • Information Security Policy: Overall commitment to protecting confidentiality, integrity, availability.
