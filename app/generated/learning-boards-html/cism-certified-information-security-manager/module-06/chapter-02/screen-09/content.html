Definition

Guidelines: Flexible Recommendations for Compliance

Offering Advice on 'How' to Achieve Security Objectives

What are Guidelines?

Recommended actions or best practices that help employees comply with policies and standards. They offer flexibility and advice on how to achieve compliance, but are not mandatory.

Key Idea

Guidelines offer recommended actions and best practices, providing flexibility and advice on how to comply with policies and standards.

Key Characteristics
  • Recommended: Not mandatory, offers flexibility.
  • Advisory: Provides advice and best practices.
  • Supports Compliance: Helps achieve policy and standard objectives.
  • Adaptable: Useful in evolving tech or judgment-based scenarios.
Examples
  • Secure Email Handling Guideline: Recommends checking sender identity before opening attachments.
  • Secure Configuration Guideline: Suggests optimal settings for web servers to enhance security.
  • Data Transfer Guideline: Advises on secure methods for sharing encrypted files.
