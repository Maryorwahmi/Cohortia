Core Concept

Standards: Specific Mandatory Requirements

Translating High-Level Intent into Specific Mandates

What are Standards?

Specific, mandatory requirements that support and elaborate on policies. They dictate how a policy is to be implemented consistently, often technology- or process-specific.

Key Idea

Standards provide specific, mandatory requirements, often technology-specific, to ensure consistent implementation of policies.

Key Characteristics
  • Specific: Detailed requirements for implementation.
  • Mandatory: Compliance is strictly enforced.
  • Technology/Process-Specific: Often tied to particular systems or workflows.
  • Ensures Consistency: Guarantees uniform application of controls.
Examples
  • Encryption Standard: Specifies AES-256 for sensitive data.
  • Password Standard: Defines minimum length, complexity, and rotation.
  • Server Hardening Standard: Mandates specific security configurations for operating systems.
