Metrics Strategy

Building a Metrics Strategy: Stakeholders & Definition

The First Steps to a Data-Driven Security Program

Why These Steps are Crucial

  • Ensures reporting is relevant to decision-makers.
  • Prevents collection of irrelevant or overwhelming data.
  • Aligns security efforts with business priorities.
  • Builds trust and credibility in the security program.
Key Takeaway

A strong metrics strategy begins with understanding who needs the information and defining metrics that are truly meaningful to them.

Strategic Steps for Metrics

01

Identify Key Stakeholders

Understand their information needs and appropriate level of detail (e.g., Board, CEO, CISO, IT Managers).

02

Define the Metrics

Based on security strategy, risk register, and stakeholder needs, ensuring they meet SMART criteria.

Scenario Example

A CISO consults with the CFO to understand financial risk concerns before defining a metric for 'Cost of Security Incidents'.
