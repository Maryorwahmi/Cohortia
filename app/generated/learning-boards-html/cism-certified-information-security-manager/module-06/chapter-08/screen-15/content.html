Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Security Metrics and Reporting

Technical Metrics

Measures related to the performance and effectiveness of specific security controls or technologies.

Management/Strategic Metrics (KPIs)

High-level measures that provide insight into the overall health of the security program and its alignment with business objectives.

Reporting

The process of presenting security metrics and insights to various stakeholders in an understandable and actionable format.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts enable CISMs to effectively measure technical control performance, communicate strategic impact to leadership, and ensure transparent, actionable reporting across the organization.
