Metrics Strategy

Effective Security Reporting: Communicating Impact

Presenting Actionable Insights to Diverse Audiences

Reporting Best Practices

  • Use dashboards/scorecards for visualization.
  • Focus on business impact for leadership.
  • Avoid technical jargon for non-technical audiences.
  • Ensure regular reporting for visibility and accountability.
Safety Note

Misleading metrics or reporting without context can lead to poor decisions or a false sense of security.

Key Takeaway

Effective reporting translates security data into business value, enabling informed decisions and continuous program improvement.

Step 05: Report the Findings

05

Report Findings Clearly & Actionably

Use dashboards and scorecards. Tailor reports to stakeholders, focusing on business impact, risk reduction, and strategic alignment.

GlobalTech KPI Example: Critical Risk Reduction

Relevance: Shows impact on highest-priority business risks.
Measurement: (Initial Risk Score - Current Risk Score) / Initial Risk Score * 100%.
Ideal Trend: Consistent downward trend in critical risk scores.

GlobalTech KPI Example: Regulatory Compliance Posture

Relevance: Demonstrates adherence to legal/industry regulations.
Measurement: (Compliant Controls / Total Required Controls) * 100%.
Ideal Trend: Stable high score (e.g., 90%+) or increasing trend.
