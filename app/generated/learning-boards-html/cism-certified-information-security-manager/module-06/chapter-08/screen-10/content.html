Metric Categories

Management & Strategic Metrics (KPIs): For Leadership

High-Level Insights into Program Health and Business Alignment

Purpose of Management Metrics

  • Provide high-level view of program health.
  • Demonstrate alignment with business objectives.
  • Justify strategic security investments.
  • Communicate risk posture to senior leadership.
Key Takeaway

KPIs are essential for leadership to understand the strategic value of security and make informed decisions about risk and resources.

Examples of Management/Strategic Metrics (KPIs)

Incident Cost

Financial impact of security incidents.

Risk Reduction

% reduction in critical business risks.

Compliance Status

Status against key regulations (e.g., GDPR).

Awareness Training

Security awareness training completion rates.

ROSI

Return on Security Investment.

Scenario Example

The Board reviews ROSI for a new security awareness program, seeing a positive return by reducing phishing-related incident costs.
