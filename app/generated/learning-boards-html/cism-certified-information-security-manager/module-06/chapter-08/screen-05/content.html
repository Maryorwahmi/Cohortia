Defining Effective Metrics: The SMART Criteria

Ensuring Your Security Metrics Provide Actionable Insights

Beyond SMART: Additional Qualities

  • Consistent: Measured the same way over time.
  • Comparable: Allows for benchmarking.
  • Actionable: Provides insights leading to improvements.
Common Mistakes

Collecting too many or irrelevant metrics, or failing to define success criteria for each metric.

Key Takeaway

Effective metrics are not just numbers; they are carefully defined to be useful, relevant, and drive positive change.

The SMART Criteria for Metrics

Specific

Clearly defined and unambiguous.

Measurable

Quantifiable and verifiable.

Achievable

Realistic and attainable.

Relevant

Tied to objectives and strategy.

Time-bound

Defined with a specific timeframe.
