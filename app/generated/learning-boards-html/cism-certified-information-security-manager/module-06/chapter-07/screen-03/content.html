Understanding the Landscape

Differentiating IR, DR, and BCP

Incident Response (IR)

Focuses on detecting, analyzing, containing, eradicating, and recovering from specific security breaches or attacks.

SPECIFIC FOCUS

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Focuses on restoring IT infrastructure and systems after a major disruptive event, such as a natural disaster or large-scale cyberattack.

IT INFRASTRUCTURE FOCUS

Business Continuity (BCP)

The overarching strategy ensuring critical business functions continue operating during and after any disruption, encompassing IR and DR.

OVERALL BUSINESS FOCUS
