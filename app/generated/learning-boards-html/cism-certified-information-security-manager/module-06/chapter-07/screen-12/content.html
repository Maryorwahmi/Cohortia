Key Concept

Tabletop Exercises

A discussion-based approach to testing plans

What it is

A discussion-based session where participants talk through their roles and responsibilities during a simulated incident or disaster.

How it works

Facilitator presents a scenario; participants discuss responses, identify gaps, and test decision-making.

Benefits

Low cost, minimal disruption, familiarizes team with plans, identifies procedural weaknesses.

Example Scenario

Ransomware Attack

Facilitator: 'A ransomware attack encrypts critical servers.'

Participants discuss: How to confirm? Who to notify? Containment steps? Communication plan?

Outcome: Identify need for clearer escalation procedures and updated contact lists.
