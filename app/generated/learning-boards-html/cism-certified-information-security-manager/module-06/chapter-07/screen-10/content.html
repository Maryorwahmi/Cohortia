Scenario-Based Assessment

Testing your understanding of IR, DR, and BCP

Question 1

An organization has a plan to restore its critical IT systems and data after a regional power grid failure causes a complete shutdown of its primary data center. This plan is best described as a:

  • a) Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
  • b) Incident Response Plan (IRP)
  • c) Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
  • d) Crisis Management Plan (CMP)
Correct Answer: c) Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
Question 2

The 'Lessons Learned' phase is a critical component of the Incident Response Plan lifecycle. What is its primary purpose?

  • a) To assign blame to individuals responsible for the incident.
  • b) To document the immediate technical steps taken during containment.
  • c) To identify areas for improvement in the IR process, controls, and overall security posture.
  • d) To inform legal counsel about potential litigation arising from the incident.
Correct Answer: c) To identify areas for improvement...

Key Takeaways

Understanding plan types and phase purposes

DRP focuses on IT system restoration after major events.

The 'Lessons Learned' phase is vital for continuous improvement.

Accurate identification of plan types is crucial for preparedness.
