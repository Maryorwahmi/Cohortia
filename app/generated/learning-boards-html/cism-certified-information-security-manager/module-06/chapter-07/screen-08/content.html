Key Concept

The Importance of Testing and Review

Ensuring plans are effective when needed

Why Test?

Plans fail if not tested. Testing identifies gaps, flaws, and areas for improvement in procedures, resources, and training.

Types of Tests

Tabletop Exercises: Verbal walkthroughs of scenarios.
Functional Exercises: Simulate specific plan components.
Full-Scale Simulations: Comprehensive real-world event simulation.

Post-Test Analysis

Crucial review to identify what worked, what didn't, and necessary plan updates.

Testing a DRP

Simulating a data center outage

Tabletop: Team discusses response steps for a power failure.

Functional: Activate recovery site servers and restore critical databases.

Full-Scale: Failover all critical systems to recovery site, simulate user access.

Review: Analyze test results, update plan, retrain team.
