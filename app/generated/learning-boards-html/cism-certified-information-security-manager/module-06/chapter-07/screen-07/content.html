IRP vs. DRP vs. BCP: A Deeper Dive

Understanding their interconnectedness and distinct roles

Incident Response (IR)

Focus: Specific security breaches (malware, data theft). Goal: Contain, eradicate, recover from the *attack*.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Focus: IT infrastructure failure (disasters, major outages). Goal: Restore IT systems and data to operational status.

Business Continuity (BCP)

Focus: Overall business operations (any disruption). Goal: Ensure critical business functions continue operating.

Interconnectedness

A layered approach to resilience

BCP relies on IR and DR to achieve its goals.

IR addresses the security event; DR addresses the IT system failure.

BCP ensures overall business function continuity.

A cyberattack might trigger both IR and DR, supporting BCP.
