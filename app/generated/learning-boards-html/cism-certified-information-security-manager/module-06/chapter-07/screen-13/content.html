Key Concepts

Remember these essential takeaways

IRP Lifecycle

Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, Lessons Learned.

DRP Essentials

Scope, Objectives (RTO/RPO), Team, Procedures, Site Strategy, Testing.

Testing & Review

Regular testing (tabletop, functional, full-scale) and post-test analysis are vital for plan effectiveness.

IR vs. DR vs. BCP

IR: Security breach. DR: IT system recovery. BCP: Overall business function continuity.

Summary

Effective IR, DR, and BCP planning, coupled with rigorous testing, forms the bedrock of organizational resilience against diverse threats and disruptions.
