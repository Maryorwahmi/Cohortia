Core Concept

Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) Essentials

Restoring IT infrastructure after major disruptions

Scope & Objectives

Define covered systems, RTO (Recovery Time Objective), and RPO (Recovery Point Objective).

Recovery Team

Assign roles and responsibilities for personnel involved in the recovery effort.

Emergency Procedures

Outline initial steps to take immediately after a disaster is declared.

Backup & Restoration

Detail procedures for restoring data and systems from backups.

Recovery Site Strategy

Plan for alternative processing sites (hot, warm, cold, cloud).

Testing & Maintenance

Schedule regular validation and updates to ensure plan effectiveness.

Key DRP Components

Checklist for a robust plan

Clear RTO and RPO defined.

Defined recovery team with clear roles.

Detailed backup and restoration procedures.

Strategy for recovery site (e.g., cloud, hot site).

Regular testing and maintenance schedule.
