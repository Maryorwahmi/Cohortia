Incident Response and Disaster Recovery Planning

Ensuring Resilience in the Face of Disruption

Welcome to our module on Information Security Program Management. In this chapter, we'll delve into a critical aspect of any robust security program: Incident Response and Disaster Recovery Planning. Even with the best preventative measures, security incidents and major disruptions are an unfortunate reality in today's digital landscape. Our ability to effectively manage these events, minimize their impact, and restore operations swiftly is a true measure of an organization's maturity. We'll explore the distinct yet interconnected roles of Incident Response, Disaster Recovery, and Business Continuity Planning, and understand the essential steps involved in preparing for, responding to, and recovering from various types of disruptive events. This knowledge is vital for any security manager aiming to protect their organization's assets and maintain operational integrity.
