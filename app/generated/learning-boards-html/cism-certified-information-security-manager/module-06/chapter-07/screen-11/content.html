Key Concept

Recovery Time Objective (RTO) & Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Defining acceptable downtime and data loss

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Maximum acceptable downtime for a system after a failure.
Example: E-commerce site RTO = 4 hours.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Maximum tolerable data loss from an IT service.
Example: Customer database RPO = 15 minutes.

Business Decision

Balancing cost vs. impact

Shorter RTO/RPO usually means higher costs.

Align RTO/RPO with business impact tolerance.

Frequent backups support lower RPO.

Redundant systems/failover support lower RTO.
