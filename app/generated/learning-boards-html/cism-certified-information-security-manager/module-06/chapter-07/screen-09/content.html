DRP Outline for E-Commerce Global

Applying DRP principles to a critical system

1. Scope & Objectives

Systems: E-commerce Website, Customer Database.
RTO (Website): 4 hours.
RPO (Database): 15 minutes.
Goal: Minimize downtime & data loss.

2. Recovery Team

IT Ops Mgr (Lead), DBAs, Network Eng, App Support, Comms Lead.

3. Emergency Procedures

Declare disaster, notify team, assess damage, activate DRP.

4. Backup & Restoration

Website: Daily full, hourly incremental. Database: Continuous replication/near real-time backups. Offsite storage.

5. Recovery Site

Warm site w/ pre-configured servers or DRaaS. Define activation & failback.

6. Testing & Maintenance

Annual full failover tests, quarterly component tests. Update plan annually or after major changes.

Example Scenario

E-Commerce Global

Critical systems identified: Website & Customer DB.

RTO/RPO targets set to minimize business impact.

Recovery site strategy chosen based on uptime needs.

Regular testing ensures the plan remains viable.
