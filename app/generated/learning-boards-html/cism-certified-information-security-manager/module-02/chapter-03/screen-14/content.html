Governance Roadmap
Sprint 0: Scope & Gap
Define scope, stakeholders, and perform a gap assessment against frameworks.
Sprint 1: Priority Standards
Draft mandatory standards for top risks and the first procedures to operationalize them.
Pilot & Validate
Test controls in a representative environment and adjust for operational impact.
Publish & Train
Communicate policies, train operators, and publish procedures and metrics.
Monitor & Review
Measure compliance, log changes, and schedule periodic reviews.
Quick checklist
  • Is each standard traceable to a policy?
  • Is each procedure executable and evidence-producing?
  • Are owners and review dates assigned?
  • Are metrics in place for monitoring?
