Core Concept
What is a Governance Framework?
A set of principles and practices to manage and measure security consistently.
• Establishes common language for leadership and technical teams.
• Suggests controls and processes to meet business objectives and regulatory needs.
• Supports monitoring, reporting, and continual improvement.
• Serves as a scaffold, not a prescriptive configuration checklist.
Key Takeaway

Use frameworks to create traceable decisions and consistent measurement across people and processes.

How to apply a framework
  • Map framework controls to your business processes and risks.
  • Prioritize controls that reduce your highest business impact risks.
  • Document required implementations as standards and procedures.
  • Use the framework terms in reports to align with auditors and executives.
