Process Flow
Policy Lifecycle
1. Initiation / Development
Draft from business drivers and risk assessment with stakeholder input.
2. Review & Approval
Stakeholder review and formal approval by the authorized body.
3. Publication & Communication
Announce policy, train affected personnel, and publish where staff can access it.
4. Implementation
Translate policy into standards, procedures, and controls and deploy them.
5. Monitoring & Enforcement
Measure compliance and apply corrective actions when necessary.
6. Review & Update
Periodic review or triggered updates when regulations, tech, or processes change.
Practical artifacts
  • Owner registry with review dates and approval authority.
  • Change log recording rationale and versioning for each update.
  • Communication record showing who was trained and when.
  • Monitoring dashboard with compliance metrics tied to standards.
Warning
Failing to review policies regularly leads to outdated controls and audit findings. A formal cadence prevents drift.
