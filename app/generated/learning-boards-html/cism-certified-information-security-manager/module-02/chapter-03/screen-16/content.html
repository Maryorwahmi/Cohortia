Policy Hierarchy
Policy → Standard → Procedure → Guideline, each with a distinct role and enforceability.
Policy Lifecycle Management
Initiate, review, approve, publish, implement, monitor, and update to maintain relevance.
Control Mapping
Map controls to business processes, assign owners, and define measurable indicators for compliance.
Final Takeaway
Governance succeeds when documentation is traceable, measurable, owned, and regularly reviewed.
