Assessment Idea
Framework Selection & Artifact Review
Task 1: Framework recommendation
Provide a short justification for the framework(s) you would adopt, including drivers, maturity, and required tailoring decisions. Show how the selection maps to business risk and audit needs.
Task 2: Artifact submission
Submit the PHI Encryption Standard and the opening of the related procedure. Make sure each requirement is measurable and traceable to the policy.
Evaluation criteria
  • Defensible selection rationale tied to drivers.
  • Standards with explicit, testable requirements.
  • Procedures with clear, executable steps and evidence points.
  • Traceability matrix or explanation linking artifacts to the policy.
