Process Flow
Selecting and Adapting a Framework
1. Scope & Identify Drivers
List regulatory, contractual, and business requirements. Identify affected assets and stakeholders.
2. Maturity & Gap Assessment
Assess current controls and identify gaps against candidate frameworks.
3. Map Controls to Risks
Match framework controls to real organizational risks and processes to prioritize effort.
4. Tailor & Document
Record tailoring decisions, scope limits, and compensating controls in a governance charter.
Example: cloud-first provider
A company with cloud-native services may map ISO Annex A controls to cloud responsibilities and use NIST functions to prioritize detection and response maturity. It might adopt COBIT metrics to show executives how cloud investments reduce identified business risk.
Common Mistake
Implementing a framework rigidly without tailoring creates unnecessary work and gaps. Document your tailoring rationale so assessors understand your risk-based choices.
Check your understanding
Draft a one-paragraph justification for selecting a framework for a mid-sized financial firm, listing the top three drivers that influenced your choice.
