Process
Roadmap & Governance
Sequence prioritized projects with clear owners, dependencies, resource estimates, and KPIs so leadership can manage trade-offs and funding.
Short-term wins
Quick controls that reduce immediate risk and build momentum.
Mid-term
Initiatives that increase capability—monitoring, CI/CD security, data classification.
Long-term
Architecture changes and culture programs that mature security over years.
Governance
  • Assign sponsors and owners for each initiative.
  • Establish a steering committee for prioritization.
  • Hold regular KPI reviews to adjust course and resources.
Key Takeaway

Balance quick risk reduction with longer investments and use governance checkpoints to keep the roadmap realistic and funded.
