Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Information Security Strategy
A long-term plan to protect information assets and support business objectives, compliance, and risk management.
Strategic Alignment
Ensure security supports the organization's mission and key business initiatives so investments are effective.
Current State Assessment
An evidence-based baseline of assets, threats, vulnerabilities, controls, and compliance that informs priorities.
Takeaway
Build strategy from facts, align it to business priorities, and measure progress with meaningful KPIs and KRIs.
