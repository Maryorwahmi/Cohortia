Desired Future State
Target security capabilities and maturity defined over a 3–5 year horizon and tied to business goals.
Roadmap
A sequenced plan of initiatives, owners, timelines, resources, and KPIs that delivers the desired state.
KPIs & KRIs
KPIs measure progress toward objectives; KRIs provide early warning of increasing risk exposure.
Summary
Use these definitions as clarity tools when presenting a strategy: they focus discussion and make decisions evidence-driven.
