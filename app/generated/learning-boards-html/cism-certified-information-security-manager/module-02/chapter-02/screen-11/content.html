Practical Activity
Draft Strategic Security Objectives
You are the CISO for FinTech Solutions. Use the template to create three SMART objectives with KPIs and KRIs.
Template
Objective: [Specific, target, timeframe]. KPI: [measure]. KRI: [early warning].
Example 1
Objective: Achieve 95% MFA coverage for mobile transactions within 12 months. KPI: % MFA coverage. KRI: Number of unprotected transactions per week.
Example 2
Objective: Reduce MTTC for critical incidents to under 2 hours within 9 months. KPI: Mean time to contain. KRI: Number of critical incidents untriaged after 24 hours.
Common Mistake

Don't pick KPIs that are easy but unrelated to business risk. Link metrics to critical assets and customer trust.
