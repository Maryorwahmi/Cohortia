Core Concept
Strategic Alignment
Ensure security initiatives map directly to business objectives so controls protect critical assets and enable business outcomes.
Traceability Matrix
Link business objectives to assets, controls, and KPIs to show how security reduces business risk.
Example Mapping
Business: Rapid mobile expansion → Security: Mobile app protection, API gateway, MFA for customers.
Common Misconception
Alignment is not lowering standards; it focuses effort and can increase controls for critical services.
Practical steps
  1. Identify top business objectives and timelines.
  2. Inventory critical assets and business processes that support those objectives.
  3. Map controls and initiatives that directly reduce risk to those assets.
  4. Prioritize initiatives by business impact and feasibility.
Key Takeaway

Alignment makes security spend defensible, measurable, and directly tied to business outcomes.
