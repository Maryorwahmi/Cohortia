Definition
Current State Assessment
An evidence-based snapshot of assets, vulnerabilities, controls, and compliance obligations that forms the baseline for strategy work.
Inputs
  • Asset inventory and data flows
  • Risk assessments, scans, pentests
  • Compliance gaps and policy reviews
Example finding
Unclassified production data in a shared cloud bucket → triggers data classification and access control initiative.
Prioritization
Prioritize findings by business impact, required effort, and regulatory urgency rather than by technical severity alone.
Deliverables
A current state report, prioritized risk list, and evidence appendix suitable for stakeholders and auditors.
Key Takeaway

Assessments must be evidence-based, repeated after major changes, and prioritized by business impact.
