Definition
Strategy Document Components
A concise, board-ready document that justifies initiatives and shows how security enables business goals.
Executive summary
High-level rationale, expected benefits, and key asks from leadership.
Vision & mission
A short statement that connects security to enabling business outcomes.
SMART objectives
Specific goals with timelines and measurable KPIs and KRIs.
Scope & principles
What is in scope, what is excluded, and the guiding security principles.
Initiatives & resources
Program descriptions, resource needs, and estimated costs.
Governance
Oversight roles, decision points, and KPI review cadence.
Example excerpts
Vision example: "Be a trusted enabler of secure digital innovation for our customers and employees." Objective example: "Reduce critical data breach incidents by 25% within two years."
Key Takeaway

A clear, evidence-backed document with SMART goals and governance is essential for leadership approval and execution.
