Core Concept
KPIs and KRIs
Select metrics that show measurable progress on strategic objectives and provide early warning of rising risk.
Good KPI examples
  • Average time to detect critical incidents (MTTD)
  • Average time to contain critical incidents (MTTC)
  • % of business-critical apps with MFA
KRI examples
  • Number of unpatched critical vulnerabilities on production systems
  • Increase in high-risk configuration deviations
Metric checklist
  • Is the metric tied to a strategic objective?
  • Can it be collected reliably and consistently?
  • Does it indicate reduced business risk or control coverage?
Key Takeaway

Define ownership, targets, and collection methods for each KPI and KRI so leadership can trust the reported progress.
