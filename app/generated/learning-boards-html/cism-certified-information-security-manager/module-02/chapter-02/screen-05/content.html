Process Flow
Four planning phases
A predictable sequence that converts assessment findings into a prioritized program of work.
1. Current State Assessment
Inventory assets, controls, threats; perform risk assessments and audits to establish a factual baseline.
2. Desired Future State
Define target capabilities and maturity over a 3–5 year horizon, aligned to business strategy.
3. Gap Analysis
Compare baseline to target, identify shortfalls, and prioritize by business impact and feasibility.
4. Roadmap
Sequence initiatives with owners, resources, timelines, and KPIs; make the plan iterative and reviewable.
Example timeline
Year 1: Remediate high gaps and secure cloud foundations. Year 2–3: Scale mature controls for production services. Year 4–5: Optimize and embed controls in development lifecycle.
Key Takeaway

Treat the roadmap as a living plan that prioritizes risk reduction and adjusts as business goals evolve.
