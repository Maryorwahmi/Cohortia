Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › KRIs
Definition

Key Risk Indicators

Leading metrics that indicate where risk is building and demand proactive attention.

Examples
Unpatched critical systems, rising phishing targeting executives, privileged accounts with excessive permissions.
Misconception
KRIs are not the same as incident counts; they forecast exposure and should prompt prevention measures.
KRI design checklist
Link KRI to a specific risk scenario and the organisation's risk appetite.
Define thresholds that trigger action and the escalation path.
Choose data sources that provide timely signals, not delayed indicators.
Concrete example
KRI: Number of critical business applications without a tested disaster recovery plan. Source: DRP inventory and test logs. Display: Donut chart with red slice for untested apps and a list of the top three missing tests.
