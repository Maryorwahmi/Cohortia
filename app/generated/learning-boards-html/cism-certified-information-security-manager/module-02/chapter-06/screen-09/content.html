Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Analysis
Steps
  1. Clean and normalize data, align identifiers and timestamps.
  2. Identify trends, anomalies, and correlations.
  3. Contextualize findings with business events and risk appetite.
  4. Validate with owners and recommend actions.
Example insight
A 20% rise in successful phishing targeting finance correlates with a 15% drop in training completion. Action: targeted training and email gateway rule update for finance.
Analysis tools & visuals
Trend lines with targets and moving averages.
Heat maps for concentration of risk across business units.
Correlation tables and short notes explaining causal assumptions.
Check your interpretation
Always ask: what is the recommended action, who owns it, and how will success be measured? If you cannot answer, refine the analysis.
