Performance Measurement and Reporting

Understand which metrics matter, where to collect them, and how to present findings so stakeholders can act.

In this lesson

Why measurement drives governance, how KPIs and KRIs differ, data collection sources, analysis techniques, and stakeholder reporting patterns.

Why it matters
Good metrics let the CISO show progress and risk in business terms — necessary to secure funding and focus.
Key Takeaway

Metrics must be business-relevant, consistent, and actionable. Pair KPIs with KRIs to avoid blind spots.
