KPI Example: Mean Time To Recover (MTTR)
Metric: MTTR for Critical Incidents
Definition: Average time from incident detected to full service restoration for critical incidents
Source: Incident Response Platform timestamps; manual validation for non-automated events
Formula: sum(recovery_time_minutes) / count(critical_incidents)
Target: <= 4 hours
Visualization: Line chart over 12 months with target band and incident count overlay
Business insight: Indicates resilience and operational capacity; decreasing MTTR reduces downtime cost
KRI Example: % critical apps without tested DRP
Metric: % critical apps without a tested DRP
Definition: Proportion of critical business applications lacking a documented and tested disaster recovery plan
Source: DRP management system and critical asset inventory
Formula: (critical_apps_without_test / total_critical_apps) * 100
Threshold: > 15% requires escalation
Visualization: Donut chart with list of top 3 untested apps
Business insight: Early warning of recovery risk and priority for resilience investment
Checklist before publishing a metric
  • Owner identified and data lineage documented.
  • Collection cadence and validation method defined.
  • Visualization and narrative prepared for the target audience.
