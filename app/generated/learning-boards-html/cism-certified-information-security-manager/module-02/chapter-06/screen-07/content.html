Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › KPIs vs KRIs
KPI

Performance

Examples: patching compliance %, MTTD, training completion.

Show trend vs target and impact on operations.
Use to judge process efficiency and improvement needs.
KRI

Risk

Examples: unpatched critical systems, increased phishing volume, privileged accounts with excess permissions.

Show thresholds, escalation steps, and the risk appetite they affect.
Use to trigger proactive mitigation and strategic decisions.
Combined display pattern
Present a KPI trend with an adjacent KRI threshold view; include a short narrative linking the two and a recommended action. This avoids misleading conclusions when one signal moves without the other.
