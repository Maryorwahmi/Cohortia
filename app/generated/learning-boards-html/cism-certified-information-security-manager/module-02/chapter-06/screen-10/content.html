Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Reporting
Board
Strategic view
High-level KPIs, KRIs, trend lines, and financial exposure. Short narrative linking to enterprise risk.
Action: approves strategy and major investments.
Executive Management
Tactical/strategic mix
Project progress, resource needs, significant incidents, and program effectiveness with business context.
Action: allocates resources and sponsors initiatives.
Operational Teams
Tactical detail
Real-time alerts, prioritized vulnerabilities, incident tickets, and remediation steps.
Action: execute fixes and operations tasks.
Reporting best practices
  • Clarity and conciseness: avoid jargon.
  • Contextualize numbers with business impact.
  • Highlight actions and owners.
  • Be transparent about challenges and non-compliance.
