How you will demonstrate skill

Each objective requires a concise explanation, a sample dashboard metric or KRI, and a short justification that ties the number to business impact. Use plain language and consistent metrics over time.

Mnemonic: MAP
Measure, Analyze, Present — repeatable steps for every indicator.
Common pitfall
Reporting raw counts without business context leads to confusion and inaction.
Assessment tip
Show trend lines and a target to make progress or risk obvious to leaders.
