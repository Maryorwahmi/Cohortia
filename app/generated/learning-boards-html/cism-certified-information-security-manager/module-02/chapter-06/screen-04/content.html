Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Why measurement is essential
Definition

Measurement as the bridge to business decisions

Measurement evaluates effectiveness and links technical activity to business outcomes so leaders can act.

Concrete example
Reporting a rise in successful phishing to finance with trend and remediation options guides targeted training and gateway rule changes.
Misconception
More raw data does not equal better insight; context and decision focus matter more than volume.
Key takeaway
Every metric should support a specific action or decision for its intended audience.
Analogy

Business flight dashboard

Just as a pilot uses instruments to maintain course and avoid hazards, leaders use metrics to steer strategy and reduce exposure.

Why it matters
Measurement enables prioritization, funding, and visibility across organizational levels.
Check your understanding
Name a metric you report and ask: "What decision does this drive?" If no clear action exists, refine the metric or add context.
