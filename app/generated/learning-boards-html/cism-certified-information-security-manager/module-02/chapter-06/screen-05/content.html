Definition

Key Performance Indicators

KPIs show how well your security program meets its objectives and targets.

Examples
  • Percentage of critical vulnerabilities remediated within SLA
  • Average time to detect and contain incidents (MTTD, MTTC)
  • Training completion percentage
Misconception
Counting activity does not equal measuring outcome. Tie KPIs to business objectives and targets.
KPI design checklist
Define the metric name, formula, and units.
Identify authoritative data source and collection method.
Set a target and review cadence.
Present as trend vs target with business implication.
Concrete example
Metric: % critical vulns remediated within 30 days
Data: Vulnerability management system
Formula: (Remediated_critical_vulns_<=30d / Identified_critical_vulns) * 100
Target: 90% monthly
Display: Line chart with target band and incident reduction note
