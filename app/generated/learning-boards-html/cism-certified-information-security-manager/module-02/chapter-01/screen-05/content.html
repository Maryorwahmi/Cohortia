Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Governance vs Management
Governance
  • Sets direction, risk appetite, and decision rights.
  • Aligns security to business objectives and priorities.
  • Provides oversight and accountability to the board and executives.
Management
  • Implements controls, processes, and operational security measures.
  • Handles incident response, monitoring, and technical compliance.
  • Reports performance and control effectiveness to governance bodies.
Concrete contrast
Board approves risk appetite for a new market (governance); security team implements controls and monitoring for the launch (management).
Key Takeaway

Both are required: governance provides direction and oversight; management delivers operational security and measurable control performance.
