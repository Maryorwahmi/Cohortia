Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Governance Components & Roles
Key Components
Board oversight: ultimate accountability and approval of risk appetite.
Executive sponsors: secure funding and cross-functional alignment.
CISO: designs program, owns reporting, and coordinates risk assessments.
Business owners: accept and manage risks within their areas.
Decision Rights Matrix
A simple matrix clarifies who accepts, who mitigates, and who funds different categories of risks. This prevents delays during incidents and ensures accountability.
Example: Market expansion
Board decides risk appetite; CISO coordinates assessment; product teams implement controls; legal ensures compliance controls are in the contract.
Common mistake
Not documenting decision rights, which leads to fragmented responses and insufficient funding for critical controls.
Key Takeaway

Assign explicit decision rights and reporting lines so governance translates into timely, accountable action.
