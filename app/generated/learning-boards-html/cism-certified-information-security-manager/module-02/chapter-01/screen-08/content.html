Risk Appetite
Define the level and type of risk the organization will accept to pursue objectives.
Business translation
Express appetite in business terms: acceptable downtime, data exposure limits, or compliance tolerance.
Concrete example
Board chooses low appetite for data residency risk; governance requires regional data controls before product launch.
Common pitfall
Not turning appetite into measurable thresholds and reporting; decisions become subjective and inconsistent.
Practical step
Document appetite statements and link them to specific metrics used in board reports.
Key Takeaway

Risk appetite turns strategy into actionable thresholds that guide governance decisions and funding.
