Understanding Information Security Governance
A strategic framework that aligns security decisions with business goals and accountability.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson

Define governance, contrast it with management, and learn program objectives that map to business outcomes.

Why it matters

Governance prevents reactive, fragmented security spending and ensures accountability from board level to operations.

Orientation
Think of governance as the steering wheel and navigation: it sets direction and ensures that management, the engine, drives toward the organization’s strategic goals.
