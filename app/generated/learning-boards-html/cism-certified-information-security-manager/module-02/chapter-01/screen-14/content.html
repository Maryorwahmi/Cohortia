From Framework to Decisions
Actionable governance requires appetite, decision rights, metrics, sponsorship, and process gates.
Step 1: Define appetite
Translate strategy into acceptable exposure and thresholds.
Step 2: Assign decision rights
Document who accepts, mitigates, and funds risks.
Step 3: Measure outcomes
Use a concise dashboard tied to business impact.
Step 4: Integrate processes
Embed mandatory security gates and vendor requirements into workflows.
Key Takeaway

Start small with a few governance actions that immediately influence decisions and funding, then expand as maturity grows.
