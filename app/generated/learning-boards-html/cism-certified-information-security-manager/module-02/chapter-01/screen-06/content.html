Governance Objectives
A process view: six steps to ensure governance delivers business value.
Strategic Alignment
Connect security initiatives to business goals and product strategy.
Value Delivery
Prioritize investments to yield measurable risk reduction or efficiency.
Risk Management
Identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor information risks systematically.
Resource Management
Allocate people, tools, and budget efficiently and where they add most value.
Performance Measurement
Define metrics and reporting to show progress and outcomes.
Process Integration
Embed security into development, procurement, and vendor processes.
Key Takeaway

Treat these objectives as an integrated program — gaps in one area weaken the whole governance approach.
