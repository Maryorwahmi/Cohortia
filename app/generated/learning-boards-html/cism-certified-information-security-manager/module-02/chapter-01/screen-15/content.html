Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Information Security Governance
System for directing and controlling security to align with business objectives and manage risk.
Corporate Governance
The organization’s overall system of direction and control; security governance is a subset needing board oversight.
Strategic Alignment
Ensure security initiatives directly support the organization’s strategic objectives and value delivery.
Summary
These concepts anchor governance conversations in business terms and help you explain why board-level accountability and clear decision rights matter.
