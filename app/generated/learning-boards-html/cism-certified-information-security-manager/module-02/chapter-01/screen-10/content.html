Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Measuring Governance: Metrics and Reporting
Performance Measurement
Design a board-ready dashboard that links activities to reduced business risk.
Suggested metrics
  • Control coverage for critical assets
  • Mean time to detect and remediate
  • Percentage of projects with security sign-off
  • Top residual risks and trend direction
Reporting tips
Use concise trend visuals, tie metrics to business impact, and explain action plans for gaps.
Key Takeaway

Focus on a small set of outcome metrics that show reduced exposure and decision-ready intelligence for the board.
