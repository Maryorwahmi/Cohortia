GlobalTech Activity
Use this template to align governance to a business expansion.
Business Objective
Rapid market entry in Europe for a new SaaS product.
Governance Objective
Ensure compliance with regional data protection rules and low customer-impact incidents.
Specific Initiative
Implement data residency policies, regional key management, and contract clauses for data handling by vendors.
How to use
  1. List 3–5 business objectives for the expansion.
  2. For each, write 2 governance objectives that support it.
  3. Propose one initiative or control to achieve each governance objective.
Key Takeaway

Use a simple mapping template to make governance decisions concrete and actionable for stakeholders.
