Reporting and Escalation
Define who gets what information, how often, and what triggers an escalation.
Board
Quarterly dashboard: top residual risks, strategic issues, funding asks.
Executive sponsors
Monthly program updates and resource requests tied to business initiatives.
Security leadership
Operational metrics and control performance for weekly review and planning.
Escalation rule
Any emerging risk that exceeds appetite or threatens availability triggers immediate board-level notification and an action plan.
