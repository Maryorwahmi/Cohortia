Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Pitfalls: IT-only Security
Symptoms
Security projects launched only after incidents or audit findings.
Funding and priorities set by short-term tactical needs.
Lack of executive oversight and cross-functional input.
Consequences & Fix
Missed legal or reputational impacts during major projects.
Fix: Reintroduce governance with clear appetite, decision rights, and executive sponsorship.
Make security a cross-functional requirement in project planning and vendor selection.
Key Takeaway

Move governance decisions to the enterprise level so security is a business responsibility, not an IT silo.
