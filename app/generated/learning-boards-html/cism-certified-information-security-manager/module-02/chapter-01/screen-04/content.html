Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What is Information Security Governance?
Definition
The system that directs and controls information security across the organization.
Core claim
Governance sets what to protect, the acceptable level of risk, who owns decisions, and how success is measured.
Concrete example
A board approves a risk appetite and requires quarterly reporting on top risks for an international SaaS launch; that direction prioritizes compliance and availability controls before release.
Common misconception
Governance is not simply writing policies or being an IT task; it is enterprise decision-making and accountability for information risk.
Analogy
Think of governance as the steering wheel and navigation system: it chooses direction and priorities. Management is the engine and driver that follow those directions day-to-day.
Key Takeaway

Good governance makes security decisions predictable, aligned to strategy, and auditable.
