Case Study

DBA with Audit Log Access

A single administrator can change records, manage backups, and delete logs — a harmful concentration of power.

Impact
  • Undetected data tampering or theft
  • Inability to conduct reliable forensics
  • Regulatory breaches due to altered records
Warning Signals

Unusual access times, gaps in audit logs, or administrative actions lacking independent approvals are red flags.

Key Takeaway

Ensure log access and review are independent from operational privileges.

Contrast

With SoD vs Without SoD

With SoD
  • Separate admins for DB and backups
  • Independent log review by security or audit
  • Automated tamper-evident logging
Without SoD
  • Single admin can alter data and logs
  • High risk of undetected insider fraud
  • Weak forensic capability
Mitigations

Apply strong role separation, enable read-only audit access, use immutable logs, and conduct independent periodic reviews.

Check

If your team can both perform and hide a critical activity, create an independent check and log all approval steps.
