Definition

Board vs Executive Management

Understand the difference between high-level oversight and hands-on implementation to align governance and operations.

Board of Directors
Ultimate fiduciary responsibility, approves strategy, monitors high-level risk.
Focus: risk appetite, program funding, and receiving concise metrics to hold executives accountable.
Example
Board approves a multi-year security investment and requires quarterly risk metrics. They do not define daily operational tasks.
Executive Management
Allocates resources, integrates security into operations, and enforces Board directives.
Focus: translate strategy into programs, assign accountable leaders, and ensure business processes incorporate security controls.
Misconception check
Executives should not micromanage technical controls. Instead, require measurable outcomes and clear accountable owners to avoid governance overload.
Reflective prompt: What one metric would you present to the Board to show security program progress?
