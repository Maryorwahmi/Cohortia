Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Operational Checks
Metric

Privileged Accounts

Count and trend privileged accounts; investigate unexpected growth quickly.

Metric

Temporary Elevations

Track active elevations, reason codes, and whether sessions are recorded.

Practice

Exceptions

Require time limits, documented approvals, and independent review before renewal.

Final Reflection

Sustained segregation relies on a small set of measurable controls, governance over exceptions, and regular testing. Make measurement and review part of operations, not an annual afterthought.
