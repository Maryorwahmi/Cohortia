Core Concept

Why clear roles matter

Define who is responsible, who is accountable, who must be consulted, and who needs to be informed to avoid gaps and overlaps.

Definition
Clear roles mean named people or teams for each security activity so ownership, metrics, and escalation paths exist.
Common consequences of unclear roles
  • Missing controls or tasks left undone.
  • Overlapping work that wastes resources and hides accountability.
  • Audit findings and slow remediation due to unclear owners.
Key Takeaway

Name a single accountable person for each critical control; that reduces ambiguity and speeds response.

Concrete example
Consider access log monitoring: list the security manager as Accountable for ensuring monitoring policy, IT Operations as Responsible for deploying collectors, and executive management as Informed of monthly summaries. This assignment makes measurement and escalation straightforward.
Misconception check
Assigning responsibility to the CISO does not mean others can ignore security. Responsibility clarifies roles; security is still a shared duty across the organization.
Check your understanding
Pick a current control and name the Responsible and Accountable roles. If you cannot name both, the control likely needs clearer assignment.
