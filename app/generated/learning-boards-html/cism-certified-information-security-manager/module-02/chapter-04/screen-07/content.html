Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Data Owner vs Custodian
Comparison

Data Owner, Data Custodian, Users

Clear separation between decision authority, technical implementation, and day-to-day use reduces error and insider risk.

Data Owner
Business lead who classifies data and approves access.
Responsible for the data’s integrity, confidentiality, and availability from a business perspective.
Data Custodian
Technical team implementing owner policies—DBAs, cloud ops.
Implements encryption, access control, backups, and operational monitoring to meet owner requirements.
Users
Employees who consume and handle data under policy.
Must follow classification rules, protect credentials, and report suspicious activity.
Concrete example
Owner classifies customer data as confidential; custodian enables encryption and access controls; users access via approved apps only.
Misconception
Custodians should not unilaterally change classification—classification is a business decision.
Check
For a dataset you use today, name the Data Owner and the technical custodian. If you cannot, document them now.
