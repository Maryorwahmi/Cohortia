Process & Metrics

Reviews, Audits, and Exception Handling

Periodic Reviews

Quarterly attestations from role owners and automated reconciliations between role assignments and actual access.

Monitoring

Alert on privilege elevation, log tampering attempts, and long-running exceptions to ensure timely detection.

Exception Policy

Documented, time-bound exceptions with independent approvals and automatic expiration are essential.

Operational Dashboard
  • Privileged accounts: current count and trend
  • Active temporary elevations: who, why, duration
  • Open SoD exceptions: age and justification
  • Time-to-remediate exception: mean and max
Audit Practice

Give Internal Audit immutable access to logs and require them to perform independent periodic SoD testing with documented findings.

Key Takeaway

Measure the health of SoD with a few actionable metrics and enforce time-limited, reviewed exceptions.
