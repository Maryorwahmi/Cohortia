CISO, Security Managers, and Operational Roles

How leadership, domain owners, and operators form a program that turns policy into action.

CISO
Owns strategy, policies, and program performance.
Accountable for risk posture, reporting to executive management and the Board as required.
Example
CISO signs off on incident response objectives and reporting cadence.
Security Managers
Translate strategy into domain programs and supervise operations.
Coordinate SOC, risk, compliance, and vendor management to deliver the CISO’s roadmap.
Misconception check
Security managers do not replace operational teams; they enable and measure them.
Operational Teams
SOC analysts, engineers, and custodians who implement controls and report metrics.
They carry out detection, containment, patching, and system hardening according to policy.
Concrete scenario
During an incident the CISO sets objectives, the Security Ops Manager coordinates responders, and SOC analysts execute containment and evidence collection.
