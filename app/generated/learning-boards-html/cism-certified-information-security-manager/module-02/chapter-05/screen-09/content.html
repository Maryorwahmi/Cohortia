Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Identify & Map

Step 1: Identify

Work with legal and business units to list laws, regulations, and contract clauses that apply to your processing activities.

Record: source, scope, effective date, deadlines, and owner.
Include jurisdictional considerations and cross-border transfer constraints.
Tag systems and data flows that are in scope.

Step 2: Map

Create a traceability matrix linking each requirement to controls, owners, evidence, and monitoring frequency.

Example: GDPR right → subject request process → ticketing workflow → logs retention = evidence.
Deliverable: a register that auditors can review to see how requirements are addressed.
Key Takeaway

Identification and mapping create the foundation for enforceable, auditable compliance programs.
