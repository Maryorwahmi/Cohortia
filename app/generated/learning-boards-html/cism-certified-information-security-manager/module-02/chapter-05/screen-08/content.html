Common Contract Types

SLAs
Define availability and response metrics that affect monitoring and incident management.
NDAs
Require confidentiality controls, access restrictions, and handling rules for shared information.
BAAs / DPAs
Obligate processors and sub-processors to follow specific protections and often include audit clauses.

Operationalization checklist

  1. Extract clause and owner from contract.
  2. Map clause to a specific control or process.
  3. Document evidence and retention period.
  4. Include clause in onboarding and monitoring workflows.
Example: a BAA requires audit access. You must configure logging retention, provide an audit report, and keep the DPA signed and available.
Key Takeaway

Maintain a contract register mapping each clause to controls, owners, and evidence so compliance is demonstrable.
