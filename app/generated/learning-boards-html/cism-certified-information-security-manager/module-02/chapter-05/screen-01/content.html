Legal, Regulatory, and Contractual Requirements

Understand the obligations that determine how data must be protected, and why compliance is central to a secure program.

We will focus on identifying applicable laws and regulations, mapping them to security controls, and designing audit-ready processes. Examples include GDPR, HIPAA, and contractual BAAs or SLAs.

Visual Concept
Obligations Map
Legal
Statutory laws requiring action, for example breach notification statutes that create deadlines and penalties.
Regulatory
Agency or industry rules such as HIPAA or PCI DSS that mandate safeguards and reporting.
Contractual
Agreed obligations like SLAs, NDAs, BAAs, and data processing agreements that impose controls and audit rights.
Notice how these overlap: the same system can be subject to multiple obligations that must be reconciled in policy and technical controls.
