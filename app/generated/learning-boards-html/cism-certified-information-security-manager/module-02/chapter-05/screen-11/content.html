Internal Audit Process

Scope definition and risk focus.
Document review and owner interviews.
Control testing and evidence collection.
Example: An internal audit of data subject request handling inspects policy, tickets showing request fulfillment, identity verification logs, and retention evidence. The auditor reports findings, recommendations, and priority for remediation. The audit team tracks closure with evidence to ensure sustained improvement.
Remember: use internal audits to find gaps early and make remediation part of normal operations.
