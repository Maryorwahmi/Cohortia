Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › GDPR vs HIPAA vs CCPA
Comparison

Core Regulatory Effects

GDPR
Personal data rights, DPIAs, breach notification, DPOs; strict documentation and potential fines.
HIPAA
Safeguards across administrative, physical, and technical domains; focus on protected health information.
CCPA/CPRA
Consumer rights to access, deletion, and disclosures; requires processes for subject requests and transparency.

Practical implications

  • Encryption and access controls are commonly required, but settings and scope depend on the regulation.
  • DPIAs and privacy-by-design are explicit under GDPR and useful for high-risk processing across laws.
  • Incident response must meet specific timelines; GDPR has a 72-hour breach notification expectation.
  • Recordkeeping and proof of due diligence are critical for demonstrating compliance during audits.
Key Takeaway

Map each regulation to concrete controls and evidence; don't assume one control is sufficient for all obligations.
