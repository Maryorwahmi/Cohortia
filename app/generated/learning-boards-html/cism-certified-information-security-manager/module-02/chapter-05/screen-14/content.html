Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Key Concepts
Types of Requirements
Legal, regulatory, and contractual sources each create enforceable obligations that must be managed.
Major Regulations
GDPR, HIPAA, and CCPA drive specific technical and organizational requirements like DPIAs, safeguards, and subject-request processes.
Audits & Evidence
Internal and external audits test existence and operating effectiveness; evidence and remediation tracking are critical.
Summary

A documented, monitored, and auditable compliance framework reduces legal risk and strengthens security posture.
