Key GDPR Requirements

Data Protection Impact Assessment
A DPIA documents the processing, risks, and mitigations for high-risk activities and guides control selection.
Data Protection Officer
A DPO centralizes expertise and helps demonstrate accountability, especially when processing is large-scale.
Breach Notification
Certain breaches must be reported to authorities, usually within 72 hours; incident response must support rapid assessment and reporting.

Concrete example

If a cloud service stores identifiers and health status for EU patients, a DPIA will evaluate the risk of linking identities to health records. The DPIA may recommend pseudonymization, stricter access controls, and changes to retention policies. Those choices will then be documented as evidence for auditors. In addition, the presence of high-risk processing could require appointing a DPO to oversee privacy compliance.
Misconception check: DPIAs are not a one-off checkbox. They should influence engineering and operational decisions and be revisited when processing or risk changes.
Key Takeaway

GDPR obligations embed privacy into design and operations; documentation is evidence and a planning tool.
