Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › HIPAA Safeguards

Safeguard Categories

Administrative
Policies, training, risk assessment, workforce management and governance activities.
Physical
Facility controls, device protections, and physical access restrictions to systems containing health information.
Technical
Access controls, encryption, audit logs, and transmission security to protect integrity and confidentiality.

Concrete example

A hosted health records service will adopt role-based access controls, require encryption at rest and in transit, keep immutable audit logs for record access, maintain formal policies and training for staff, and perform regular risk assessments. Auditors check both the technical controls and the administrative evidence such as training records and documented risk assessments.
Misconception check: Technical controls without documented policies and governance will fail an audit because proof of process is required.
Key Takeaway

HIPAA compliance demands aligned policies, technical controls, and operational evidence; treat them as a single program.
