Definition

Understanding Incident Classification

Categorizing incidents by nature, scope, and characteristics

What it is

Categorizing an incident based on its nature, scope, and specific characteristics to inform response.

Why it matters

Ensures the right resources and expertise are quickly assigned, leading to a more targeted and efficient response.

Example

Classifying an incident as 'Ransomware' immediately triggers specific isolation and recovery protocols, unlike a 'Phishing' incident which requires user awareness campaigns.

Key Classification Criteria
Incident Typee.g., Malware, DoS
Systems Affectede.g., Endpoint, Network
Data Involvede.g., PII, IP, Public
Attack Vectore.g., Phishing, Exploit
Key Takeaway

Effective incident classification provides immediate clarity, enabling a precise and efficient response tailored to the incident's specific characteristics.
