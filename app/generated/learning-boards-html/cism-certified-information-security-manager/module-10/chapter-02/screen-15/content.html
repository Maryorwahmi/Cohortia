Key Concepts: Classification & Prioritization

Essential terms for effective incident response

Incident Classification

Categorizing security incidents based on their type, scope, and characteristics (e.g., malware, data breach, DoS).

Incident Prioritization

Ranking incidents based on their potential impact on business operations, data confidentiality, regulatory obligations, and other factors to determine response urgency.

Severity/Impact

Measures of how critical an incident is, often based on the potential damage to assets, business functions, or reputation.

Summary Takeaway

Effective incident classification and prioritization are the foundational steps that dictate the success of your entire incident response strategy, ensuring resources are optimally deployed to protect critical assets.
