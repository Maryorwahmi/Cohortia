Prioritization Criteria: Business Impact & Scope

Assessing the immediate and widespread effects of an incident

Business Operations Impact

Evaluates how severely critical business functions are affected (e.g., revenue-generating systems, core services).

Severity/Scope

Assesses how widespread the incident is and the number of systems or users affected (e.g., single workstation vs. entire network).

Example

An outage of a critical payment processing system (high business impact) affecting all customers globally (widespread scope) would be a top priority.

Impact & Scope Matrix
High Business ImpactCore systems down, revenue loss
Widespread ScopeMultiple systems/users, network-wide
Low Business ImpactMinor disruption, non-critical system
Limited ScopeSingle endpoint/user
Key Takeaway

A high business impact combined with a widespread scope typically indicates a critical incident requiring immediate, top-priority response.
