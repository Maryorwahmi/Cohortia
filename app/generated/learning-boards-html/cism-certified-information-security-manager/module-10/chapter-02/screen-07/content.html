Incident Types: Unauthorized Access & Data Breaches

Distinguishing between intrusion and data exfiltration

Unauthorized Access/Intrusion

Attempts or successful breaches of systems or networks by unauthorized individuals. Focus is on illicit entry.

Data Breach/Exfiltration

Unauthorized access to or removal of sensitive data. Focus is on data compromise and loss.

Example

An attacker gains remote access to an internal server (Unauthorized Access). If they then copy customer records from that server, it becomes a Data Breach.

Key Characteristics & Initial Response
Unauthorized Access: Impact & Response
  • **Impact:** System compromise, potential for further attacks, resource misuse.
  • **Response:** Identify entry point, contain intruder, patch vulnerabilities, forensic analysis.
Data Breach: Impact & Response
  • **Impact:** Regulatory fines, reputational damage, legal action, identity theft.
  • **Response:** Legal counsel, notification to affected parties, extensive forensic investigation, data recovery.
Key Takeaway

Distinguishing between unauthorized access and data breaches is crucial for tailoring response efforts to either contain an intruder or protect compromised sensitive data.
