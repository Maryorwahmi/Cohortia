Core Concept

The Urgency Factor: Incident Prioritization

Ranking incidents based on potential business impact

Resource Optimization

Ensures finite resources are directed to incidents with the highest potential business impact, preventing waste.

CISM's Responsibility

Define, communicate, and regularly review prioritization criteria with business stakeholders to reflect risk appetite.

Example

A critical incident like a core system outage receives immediate, round-the-clock attention, while a low-priority malware infection on a single workstation is handled during business hours.

Prioritization Principles
Impact-Driven

Prioritize based on the potential damage to critical business functions, data, and reputation.

Timeliness

Critical incidents demand immediate, often 24/7, response to minimize ongoing damage.

Resource Alignment

Match the incident's priority level with the appropriate level of human and technical resources.

Key Takeaway

Prioritization is a strategic imperative, ensuring that incident response efforts are always focused on protecting the most critical business assets and functions.
