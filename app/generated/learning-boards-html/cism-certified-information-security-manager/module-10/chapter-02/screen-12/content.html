Prioritization Criteria: Reputation & Detection Confidence

Considering public perception and reliability of detection

Reputational Damage

Evaluates how the incident might affect customer trust, brand image, or stakeholder confidence.

Detection Source/Confidence

Assesses the reliability of the detection (e.g., confirmed breach vs. suspicious anomaly).

Example

A public data breach confirmed by multiple security tools (high confidence) would have significant reputational impact, demanding top priority and a carefully managed public response.

Reputation & Confidence Levels
Widespread Public AwarenessSignificant reputational damage
Confirmed BreachHigh confidence detection
Minimal Public AwarenessMinor reputational risk
Suspicious AnomalyLow confidence detection
Key Takeaway

Reputational impact and detection confidence are crucial for fine-tuning incident prioritization, often formalized through a tiered scoring system.
