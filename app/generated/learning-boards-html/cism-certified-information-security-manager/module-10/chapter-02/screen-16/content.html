Key Concepts: Response Tools & Analysis

Understanding the mechanisms for standardized and informed response

Incident Playbook

A detailed, step-by-step guide for responding to a specific type of security incident, outlining roles, procedures, and communication.

Incident Runbook

A more granular, often technical, guide for performing specific tasks or procedures within an incident playbook.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A process to determine and evaluate the potential effects of an interruption to critical business operations, crucial for defining incident prioritization criteria.

Summary Takeaway

Playbooks, runbooks, and Business Impact Analysis are vital tools that standardize and inform incident response, ensuring strategic alignment and operational efficiency.
