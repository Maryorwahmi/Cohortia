Incident Types: Malware & DoS Attacks

Understanding common threats and their characteristics

Malware Incidents

Viruses, worms, ransomware, trojans affecting endpoints or servers. Often leads to data corruption or system unavailability.

Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS)

Attacks aimed at making services unavailable to legitimate users by overwhelming systems with traffic.

Example

A ransomware attack encrypts a company's financial servers, demanding payment. This is a malware incident requiring immediate isolation and forensic analysis.

Key Characteristics & Initial Response
Malware: Impact & Response
  • **Impact:** Data corruption, system unavailability, unauthorized access.
  • **Response:** Immediate isolation of affected systems, forensic analysis, data restoration.
DoS/DDoS: Impact & Response
  • **Impact:** Service unavailability, operational disruption, reputational damage.
  • **Response:** Traffic filtering, rate limiting, DDoS mitigation services, ISP coordination.
Key Takeaway

Recognizing the specific characteristics of malware and DoS attacks is crucial for triggering the correct, specialized initial response actions.
