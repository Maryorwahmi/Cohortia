Standardizing Response: Playbooks & Runbooks

Ensuring consistent and accelerated incident handling

Playbook Definition

A detailed, step-by-step guide for responding to specific incident types, outlining roles, communication, and technical steps.

Runbook Definition

A more granular, technical guide for performing specific tasks or procedures within a playbook (e.g., network segmentation steps).

Example

A 'Ransomware Playbook' guides the overall response, while a 'Forensic Imaging Runbook' provides precise steps for a specific technical task within that playbook.

Playbook vs. Runbook
Playbook: StrategicOverall incident response strategy
Runbook: TacticalSpecific technical tasks
Playbook: Broad ScopeCovers entire incident lifecycle
Runbook: Narrow FocusSpecific procedures
Key Takeaway

Playbooks and runbooks are essential for standardizing incident response, ensuring consistency, reducing errors, and accelerating critical response times.
