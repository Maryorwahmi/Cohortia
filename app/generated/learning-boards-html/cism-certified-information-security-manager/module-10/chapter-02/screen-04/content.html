Core Concept

The Foundation: Why Classify & Prioritize?

Ensuring strategic and efficient incident response

Directs Response

Classification and prioritization immediately determine the speed, resources, and specific actions for incident resolution.

CISM's Role

CISMs establish frameworks and policies for rapid, consistent decision-making, aligning response with business objectives.

Mitigates Risks

Prevents wasting resources on minor events and ensures critical breaches are addressed, avoiding financial, reputational, or legal damage.

Process Flow
1. Event Detected
2. Incident Confirmed
3. Classification & Prioritization
4. Incident Response
5. Post-Incident Activities
Key Takeaway

Strategic classification and prioritization are the bedrock of effective incident response, ensuring resources are optimally deployed and business impact is minimized.
