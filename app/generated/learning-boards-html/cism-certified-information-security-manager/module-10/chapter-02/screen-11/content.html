Prioritization Criteria: Data CIA & Regulatory Needs

Evaluating data sensitivity and legal obligations

Data Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability (CIA)

Assesses the type of data compromised (PII, financial, IP) and its state (corrupted, unavailable).

Regulatory and Legal Obligations

Determines if the incident triggers specific reporting requirements (GDPR, HIPAA) or potential legal ramifications.

Example

A breach involving customer credit card data (high CIA impact) would trigger PCI DSS reporting requirements (high regulatory impact), demanding immediate, high-priority action.

Data & Compliance Impact Levels
Highly Sensitive DataCustomer PII, financial, IP
Immediate ReportingGDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS triggered
Limited Sensitive DataInternal sensitive data, limited PII
No Immediate ReportingMinor fines possible
Key Takeaway

Understanding data sensitivity and regulatory obligations is crucial for prioritizing incidents and ensuring legal compliance and data protection.
