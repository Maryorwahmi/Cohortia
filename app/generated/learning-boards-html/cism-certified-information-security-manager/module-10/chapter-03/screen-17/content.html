Expanding Your Incident Response Vocabulary

Essential roles and strategic approaches.

Incident Commander

The individual responsible for overall strategic management and coordination of an incident response.

Security Analyst Tiers

Categorization of analysts based on skill level and responsibilities (Tier 1 for triage, Tier 2 for investigation, Tier 3 for advanced analysis).

Cross-functional Collaboration

The necessity of involving various departments (Legal, HR, PR, Business Units) in incident response for comprehensive management.

Summary Takeaway

Effective incident response relies on strong leadership, specialized technical roles, seamless cross-departmental teamwork, and proactive strategies to address the evolving skills gap.
