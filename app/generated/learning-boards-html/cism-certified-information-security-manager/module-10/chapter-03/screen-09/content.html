Module 10›Chapter 3›Communication and Legal Guidance

Communication Specialist / PR

Manages internal and external communications during an incident, ensuring consistent messaging and protecting the organization's reputation. Acts as a liaison with the PR department.

Legal Counsel

Provides guidance on legal and regulatory obligations, data breach notification laws, and potential litigation risks. Critical for ensuring compliance and protecting organizational interests.

Key Takeaway

Effective communication and sound legal advice are paramount during an incident to manage public perception, ensure regulatory compliance, and mitigate legal exposure.

Managing Broader Impact

These roles address the non-technical, yet equally critical, aspects of incident response.

Communication Example

Data Breach Notification

A communication specialist drafts and disseminates public statements and direct notifications to affected customers, ensuring clarity and adherence to brand guidelines.

Legal Example

Regulatory Compliance

Legal counsel advises the IRT on GDPR or HIPAA notification requirements, ensuring all actions taken during containment and recovery align with legal mandates.
