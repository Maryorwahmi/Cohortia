Module 10›Chapter 3›HR and Business Unit Representatives

Human Resources (HR)

Involved in insider threat investigations, managing employee-related issues during an incident, and ensuring proper disciplinary actions if needed.

Business Unit Representatives

Provide critical business context, help assess impact on operations, and assist in recovery efforts specific to their domain.

Key Takeaway

Integrating HR and business unit representatives ensures that incident response addresses both human factors and critical operational impacts, aligning security with business continuity.

Bridging Security and Business

These roles ensure that incident response considers the full organizational spectrum, from personnel to core operations.

HR Example

Insider Threat Investigation

HR collaborates with the IRT to investigate an employee suspected of data exfiltration, ensuring legal compliance and proper handling of personnel issues.

Business Unit Example

Manufacturing System Impact

A manufacturing representative helps the IRT prioritize recovery of critical production systems after a ransomware attack, minimizing operational downtime and financial losses.
