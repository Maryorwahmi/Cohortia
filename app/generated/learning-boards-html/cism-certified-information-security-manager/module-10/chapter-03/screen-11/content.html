Module 10›Chapter 3›Beyond Security: Cross-Functional Collaboration

Why Collaboration is Essential

  • Incidents impact business operations, legal, PR, and morale.
  • Requires established communication channels and escalation paths.
  • Ensures a holistic response, not just technical containment.
  • Minimizes overall organizational impact and accelerates recovery.

The CISM's Role

Foster a culture of collaboration, ensuring stakeholders understand their roles. Implement regular tabletop exercises and simulations involving all relevant parties to test processes.

Key Takeaway

Cross-functional collaboration transforms incident response from an IT problem into a unified organizational effort, crucial for comprehensive management and recovery.

Interconnected Response

An incident's ripple effect necessitates a coordinated effort across the entire organization.

Example Scenario

Ransomware Attack

A ransomware attack encrypts critical business data. The IRT collaborates with Legal for ransom negotiation advice, PR for public statements, and affected Business Units for recovery prioritization.

Common Misconception

IT's Problem Alone

Believing incident response is solely an IT security department's responsibility overlooks the critical non-technical impacts and the need for broader organizational support and expertise.
