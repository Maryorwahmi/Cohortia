Tier 3

Threat Hunters / Forensic Specialists

Handle complex, advanced incidents, perform in-depth forensic analysis, reverse-engineer malware, and proactively hunt for threats. They possess highly specialized skills.

Key Responsibilities

  • In-depth forensic analysis of compromised systems.
  • Malware reverse engineering to understand threats.
  • Proactive threat hunting for undetected adversaries.
  • Developing advanced detection and prevention strategies.
Key Takeaway

Tier 3 analysts are crucial for uncovering sophisticated, persistent threats and enhancing an organization's long-term security posture through deep technical expertise.

The Deep Dive

These analysts go beyond reactive responses to proactively seek out and analyze advanced threats.

Example Scenario

Advanced Persistent Threat (APT)

A Tier 3 analyst identifies a subtle, long-term compromise by an APT group through memory forensics and custom tool analysis, which evaded standard detection.

Required Skills

Specialized Expertise

Requires deep knowledge of operating systems, network internals, reverse engineering tools, scripting (Python), and advanced forensic methodologies.
