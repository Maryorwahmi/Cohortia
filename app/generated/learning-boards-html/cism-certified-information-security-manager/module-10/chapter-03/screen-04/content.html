Incident Response Team (IRT)

A dedicated group of individuals responsible for responding to and managing information security incidents. They are the operational core of your defense.

CSIRT & SOC

CSIRT (Computer Security Incident Response Team): A specialized IRT with a broader mandate, including proactive security measures and threat intelligence. SOC (Security Operations Center): A centralized unit housing the IRT and monitoring functions, dealing with security issues organizationally and technically.

Key Takeaway

An effective IRT is a critical investment in organizational resilience, preventing manageable events from escalating into catastrophic breaches.

The CISM's Role: Building Resilience

As a CISM, you are responsible for designing, establishing, and maintaining a robust IRT. This involves more than just technology; it's about empowering people and refining processes.

Why it matters

Preventing Catastrophe

A poorly structured or under-resourced team can turn a manageable security event into a catastrophic breach. The IRT acts as the first line of defense and recovery.

Example Scenario

Untriaged Alerts

Imagine a SIEM generates critical alerts, but without a clear Tier 1 analyst role, these alerts go unaddressed, allowing an attacker to persist undetected.
