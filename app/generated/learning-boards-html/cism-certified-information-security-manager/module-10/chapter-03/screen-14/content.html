Module 10›Chapter 3›Navigating Staffing and Resource Challenges

Skills Gap

Severe shortage of skilled cybersecurity professionals, leading to recruitment and retention difficulties.

Alert Fatigue & Burnout

High volume of alerts and constant pressure leading to stress, decreased effectiveness, and high turnover.

Under-resourcing

Insufficient personnel and tools, often due to a lack of executive understanding of IRT value.

Evolving Threats

The constant emergence of new attack vectors and sophisticated adversaries requires continuous adaptation.

Key Takeaway

Addressing the skills gap, managing team well-being, and securing adequate resources are critical for maintaining a sustainable and effective incident response capability.

Impact on IRT Effectiveness

These challenges directly hinder an IRT's ability to detect, respond to, and recover from incidents.

Example Scenario

Missed Critical Alert

Due to alert fatigue and understaffing, a critical alert indicating a persistent threat is overlooked, allowing an attacker to maintain access for an extended period.

CISM's Role

Advocacy & Strategy

The CISM must advocate for adequate funding, resources, and executive support, and implement strategies for workload management and mental health support.
