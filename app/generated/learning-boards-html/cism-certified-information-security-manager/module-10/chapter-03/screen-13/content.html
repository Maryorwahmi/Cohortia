Clear verbal and written communication for technical and non-technical audiences, and documentation.

Soft skills are crucial for effective teamwork and decision-making, while continuous training ensures the IRT remains adaptable and proficient against evolving threats.

Cultivating a Resilient Team

Beyond technical prowess, an IRT thrives on strong interpersonal and adaptive capabilities.
