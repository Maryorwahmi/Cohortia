Incident Commander

The individual responsible for overall incident management, making strategic decisions, coordinating resources, and ensuring the incident response plan is followed.

Key Responsibilities

  • Overall incident management and strategic decision-making.
  • Coordination of all response resources.
  • Single point of contact for executive management.
  • Management of external communications (legal, PR).
  • Ensuring adherence to the incident response plan.
Key Takeaway

The Incident Commander is the strategic leader, not necessarily the technical expert, ensuring coordinated and decisive action during an incident.

Leadership in Crisis

The Incident Commander's ability to lead and coordinate under pressure is vital for minimizing damage and ensuring effective recovery.

Example Scenario

Major Data Breach

During a data breach, the IC decides on system isolation, engages external forensics, and dictates communication strategy to stakeholders and affected parties.

Common Misconception

Technical Lead

The IC is often mistakenly seen as the primary technical lead. While technical understanding is helpful, their core function is strategic management and coordination, not hands-on analysis.
