Module 10›Chapter 3›Forensic Investigators and Threat Intelligence

Forensic Investigator

Specializes in collecting, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence in a legally sound manner, ensuring chain of custody and supporting legal proceedings.

Threat Intelligence Analyst

Provides context on threat actors, their Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs), and Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), helping the team understand the adversary and anticipate future moves.

Key Takeaway

These specialized roles provide critical evidence and intelligence, transforming reactive incident response into a more informed and proactive defense strategy.

Complementary Expertise

While distinct, these roles work hand-in-hand to provide a holistic view of an incident and the threat landscape.

Forensic Example

Evidence Preservation

After a server compromise, a forensic investigator ensures all digital evidence (logs, disk images) is collected and preserved without alteration, crucial for post-incident analysis and legal action.

Threat Intel Example

Ransomware TTPs

A threat intelligence analyst provides the IRT with the latest TTPs of a specific ransomware group, including their common initial access vectors and preferred data exfiltration methods, aiding proactive defense.
