Key Concepts to Remember

Core definitions for incident response.

Incident Response Team (IRT)

A dedicated group responsible for responding to and managing information security incidents.

CSIRT

A specialized IRT with a broader mandate, including proactive security measures and threat intelligence.

Security Operations Center (SOC)

A centralized unit dealing with security issues organizationally and technically, often housing the IRT.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the distinct definitions of IRT, CSIRT, and SOC is fundamental for establishing a clear and effective incident response framework within any organization.
