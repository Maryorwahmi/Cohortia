Module 10›Chapter 3›Building a Resilient IRT: Mitigation and Advocacy

Skill Development

Invest in specialized training and certifications for staff; consider managed security services for advanced threats.

Workload Management

Implement alert prioritization, automation, duty rotation, and mental health support to prevent burnout.

Executive Advocacy

Clearly articulate the IRT's value to secure adequate funding, resources, and executive support.

Ethical Guidelines

Ensure IRT members operate within strict ethical guidelines and are subject to rigorous background checks.

Key Takeaway

Proactive mitigation strategies, strong executive advocacy, and rigorous internal controls are essential for building and sustaining a resilient and trustworthy Incident Response Team.

Strategies for Sustainability

A CISM's leadership is crucial in transforming challenges into opportunities for strengthening the IRT.

Example Scenario

Global Team Rotation

A CISM implements a 'follow-the-sun' model for a global IRT, distributing workload across time zones to prevent alert fatigue and burnout among individual analysts.

Safety Note

Internal Controls

Ensure IRT members have appropriate access controls to sensitive systems and data, and are subject to rigorous background checks to prevent potential insider threats.
