Advanced Detection Terminology

Key Elements of Modern Security

Log Analysis

Examining system-generated records of events to identify security incidents or understand behavior.

Behavioral Analytics (UEBA)

Establishes baselines of normal activity and identifies deviations that may indicate a security threat.

Threat Intelligence

Information about current and emerging threats, including IOCs and TTPs of adversaries.

Summary Takeaway

Log analysis, behavioral analytics, and threat intelligence are advanced techniques that provide deeper insights and proactive defense against evolving security threats.
