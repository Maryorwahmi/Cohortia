Proactive Security

Leveraging Threat Intelligence

Enhancing Proactive Detection and Response

Contextual Awareness

Provides insights into current and emerging threats, including IOCs and TTPs.

Automated Defense

Feeds into SIEMs, firewalls, and EDR to automatically block or alert on known malicious entities.

CISM Role

Establish processes for consuming, curating, and acting upon relevant threat intelligence feeds.

Key Takeaway

Threat intelligence is crucial for proactive defense, enabling organizations to anticipate and block threats by integrating external IOCs and TTPs into security systems.

Threat Intelligence Integration Process

1. Consume Feeds

Gather IOCs, TTPs, and vulnerability data from trusted sources.

2. Curate & Analyze

Filter and prioritize intelligence relevant to the organization's risk profile.

3. Integrate & Act

Feed intelligence into SIEM, firewalls, EDR for automated blocking and alerting.
