Pitfalls to Avoid

Navigating Detection Challenges

Recognizing and Preventing Common Errors

Alert Fatigue

Overwhelmed security teams miss legitimate threats due to excessive false positives.

Insufficient Log Coverage

Lack of logging or poor retention creates blind spots, hindering incident investigation.

Poor Baselines

Failure to establish normal behavior makes anomaly detection ineffective and prone to errors.

Unsecured Monitoring Systems

Monitoring systems themselves can be targets, compromising security posture if not protected.

Impact of Detection Mistakes

Missed Incidents

Legitimate threats go undetected, leading to potential breaches and data loss.

Delayed Response

Slow detection leads to longer dwell times for attackers, increasing damage.

Compliance Failures

Inadequate logging and monitoring can lead to regulatory non-compliance.
