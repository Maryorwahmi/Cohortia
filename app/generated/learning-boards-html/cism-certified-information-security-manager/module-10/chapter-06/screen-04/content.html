Eradication: Eliminating the Threat

Removing the Root Cause and Traces of Attackers

Definition

The phase of incident response focused on removing the root cause of an incident and eliminating all traces of the attacker's presence.

Why it Matters

Goes beyond containment to prevent re-infection and ensure long-term security. A rushed eradication leaves systems vulnerable.

CISM's Role

Oversee thorough and effective procedures, ensuring all traces are removed and underlying vulnerabilities are addressed.

Key Takeaway

Eradication is about curing the disease, not just treating the symptoms, by addressing the root cause and removing all attacker remnants.
