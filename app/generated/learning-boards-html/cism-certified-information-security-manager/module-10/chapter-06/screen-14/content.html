Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Incident Response

Vulnerability Remediation

The process of addressing and fixing security weaknesses that could be exploited.

System Rebuild

Completely reinstalling an OS and applications from trusted sources, often for heavily compromised systems.

Restoration from Backup

Recovering data and systems using previously created, verified copies to revert to a known good state.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable duration a system can be down after a disaster or disruption.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable period in which data might be lost from an IT service due to a major incident.

Summary Takeaway

These terms are crucial for defining recovery strategies, setting expectations, and ensuring business continuity post-incident.
