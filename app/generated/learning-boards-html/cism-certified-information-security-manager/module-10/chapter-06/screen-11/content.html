CISM's Role: RTO, RPO & Communication

Guiding Recovery Decisions and Stakeholder Expectations

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Maximum tolerable duration of time a system can be down. CISM ensures recovery plans meet this.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Maximum tolerable period in which data might be lost. CISM ensures backup strategies align.

Communication

Clear and consistent updates to business stakeholders to manage expectations and provide service availability status.

Key Takeaway

The CISM translates technical recovery into business impact, ensuring alignment with organizational objectives and transparent stakeholder engagement.
