Recovery: Restoring Secure Operations

Bringing Systems Back Online with Integrity and Security

Definition

The process of bringing affected systems and services back online in a secure and functional state.

Importance

Ensures business continuity, data integrity, and continued security post-incident. Requires meticulous planning.

CISM's Role

Ensure procedures are documented, tested, and aligned with RTOs and RPOs, guiding critical risk assessments.

Key Takeaway

Recovery is a strategic process focused on secure, functional restoration, balancing speed with thoroughness to minimize risk.

Recovery Objectives

Data Integrity

System Availability

Continued Security
