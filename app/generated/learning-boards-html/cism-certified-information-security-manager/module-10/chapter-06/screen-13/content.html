Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Incident Response

Eradication

The phase focused on removing the root cause of an incident and eliminating all traces of the attacker's presence.

Recovery

The phase focused on restoring affected systems and services to a secure, operational state.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

The process of identifying the fundamental reason for an incident, rather than just its symptoms.

Summary Takeaway

Eradication, Recovery, and Root Cause Analysis are interdependent processes crucial for effective incident response and preventing future compromises.
