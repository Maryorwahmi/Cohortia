Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Key Concepts: Advanced Containment & Considerations

Containment Phases

Short-term (immediate), Medium-term (limited functionality), and Long-term (prevention/eradication).

DNS Sinkholing

Rerouting malicious DNS requests to a controlled server to disrupt attacker command-and-control communications.

Trade-off

The balance between minimizing business disruption and maximizing security during containment efforts.

Summary Takeaway

A CISM must navigate the complexities of containment across different timeframes, leveraging advanced techniques while always balancing security with business continuity.
