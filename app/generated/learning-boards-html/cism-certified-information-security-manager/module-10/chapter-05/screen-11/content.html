Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › The Containment Trade-Off

The Core Dilemma

Disconnecting a critical server might stop an attack, but it also halts business operations. This creates a direct conflict between security and continuity.

Security vs. Business Continuity.
Potential financial and reputational impact.

Role of the CISM

The CISM must work with business stakeholders to understand the impact of various containment options and make informed decisions.

  • Balance: Risk reduction with operational needs.
  • Tool: Business Impact Analysis (BIA) is invaluable.
  • Outcome: Prevent containment from causing more harm.

Comparison: Containment Options

Aggressive Containment

Immediate system shutdown, network disconnection.

  • Pros: Stops spread quickly.
  • Cons: High business disruption.

Measured Containment

Targeted firewall rules, account disabling, sandboxing.

  • Pros: Minimizes disruption, preserves evidence.
  • Cons: Slower, requires more analysis.
Key Takeaway

Effective containment is a strategic decision, not purely technical, requiring a deep understanding of business impact and risk tolerance.
