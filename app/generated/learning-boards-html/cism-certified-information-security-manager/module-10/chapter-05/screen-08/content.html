Medium-Term Containment

Restoring Limited Functionality

This phase aims to restore some limited business functionality while still actively managing and mitigating the security threat. It requires careful planning to minimize disruption.

Key Actions
  • •Re-routing traffic to clean systems.
  • •Implementing temporary workarounds.
  • •Deploying emergency patches and hardening configurations.

Example: Taking a compromised web app offline for a quick patch, then bringing it back up in a hardened configuration behind a WAF.

Long-Term Containment

Preventing Recurrence and Strengthening Defenses

This phase focuses on comprehensive measures to prevent future incidents and ensure the complete eradication of the threat, often overlapping with recovery efforts.

Key Actions
  • •System rebuilds and re-imaging.
  • •Implementing architectural changes for enhanced security.
  • •Deploying enhanced monitoring and detection tools.

Goal: Ensure the threat is completely eradicated and the environment is resilient against similar future attacks.

Key Takeaway

Containment is a multi-phased process, moving from immediate disruption to strategic restoration and long-term prevention, requiring adaptability and foresight.
