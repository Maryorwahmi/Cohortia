Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Common Mistakes in Containment

Acting Too Slowly

Delay in implementing containment allows the incident to spread further, escalating impact.

Increases damage and recovery costs.

Acting Too Aggressively

Without proper planning, aggressive actions can cause unnecessary business disruption or destroy forensic evidence.

Impacts business continuity and legal standing.

Neglecting Evidence Preservation

Forgetting to take forensic images of compromised systems before making significant changes can destroy crucial evidence.

  • Safety Note: Preserve evidence *before* changes.
  • Importance: Maintains chain of custody for forensics.

Failing to Verify Effectiveness

Not confirming that containment measures are truly effective can allow attackers to find alternative routes or re-establish access.

  • Solution: Regular testing through drills and simulations.
  • Goal: Ensure IRT can execute efficiently under pressure.
Key Takeaway

Effective containment requires a balance of speed and precision, with careful attention to planning, evidence preservation, and verification.
