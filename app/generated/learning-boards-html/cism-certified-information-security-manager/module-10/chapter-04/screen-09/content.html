NIST Phase: Containment

Containment: Halting the Spread

Strategies to Limit Incident Scope and Impact

Primary Goal

Stop the spread of the attack and prevent further damage without destroying critical evidence.

CISM Role

Ensure containment procedures are well-defined, balancing security with business disruption.

Key Takeaway

The Containment phase aims to stop the spread of an attack and prevent further damage, balancing security needs with business continuity.

Containment Strategies

Isolate Compromised Systems

Disconnect from network to prevent lateral movement (e.g., ransomware).

Block Malicious IPs/Domains

At firewall or DNS level to prevent C2 communication.

Disable Compromised Accounts

Prevent further unauthorized access and privilege escalation.
