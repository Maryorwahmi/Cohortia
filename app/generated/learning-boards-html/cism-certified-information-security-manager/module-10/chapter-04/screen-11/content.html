NIST Phase: Eradication & Recovery

Recovery: Restoring Operations

Bringing Systems Back Online and Verifying Security

Safety Note

Always restore from clean, verified backups to avoid re-introducing threats.

Best Practice

Implement stronger security controls post-recovery to prevent recurrence.

Key Takeaway

Recovery involves restoring systems from trusted sources, implementing stronger controls, and rigorous testing to ensure full functionality and security.

Recovery Timeline
1

Restore Data from Backups

Use clean, verified backups to recover corrupted or lost data.

2

Rebuild Systems

From trusted images to ensure no lingering malicious code.

3

Change Compromised Credentials

Update all affected user and service passwords.

4

Extensive Testing & Verification

Confirm system integrity, functionality, and new security controls.
