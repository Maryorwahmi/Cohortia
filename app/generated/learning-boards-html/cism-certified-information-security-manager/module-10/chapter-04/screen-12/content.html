NIST Phase: Post-Incident Activity

Post-Incident Activity: Learning and Adapting

Reviewing, Analyzing, and Improving for Future Resilience

Core Principle

Transform incidents into valuable learning opportunities for organizational growth.

CISM Responsibility

Champion this phase to ensure lessons learned are translated into actionable improvements.

Key Takeaway

The Post-Incident Activity phase is vital for continuous improvement, transforming incidents into learning opportunities to strengthen future security posture.

Key Activities in Post-Incident Review

Detailed Timeline

Reconstruct the sequence of events during the incident.

Root Cause Analysis

Identify the underlying reasons for the incident's occurrence.

Recommendations for Improvement

Suggest enhancements to controls, policies, and procedures.

Evidence Retention

Archive forensic data for potential legal action or future analysis.
