NIST Phase: Eradication & Recovery

Eradication: Removing the Threat

Identifying and Eliminating the Root Cause of Compromise

Critical Step

Ensure the root cause is identified and remediated before recovery to prevent re-infection.

Common Mistake

Restoring systems without patching the exploited vulnerability leaves them vulnerable.

Key Takeaway

Eradication focuses on completely removing the threat and addressing the underlying vulnerabilities to prevent recurrence.

Eradication Steps
1

Remove Malware

Delete malicious code from all affected systems.

2

Patch Vulnerabilities

Apply security patches to exploited weaknesses.

3

Remove Backdoors

Eliminate any persistent access mechanisms left by attackers.

4

Identify Root Cause

Thoroughly investigate to understand the initial compromise vector.
