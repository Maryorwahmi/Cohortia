NIST Phase: Preparation

Preparation: Training, Assets, and Testing

Ensuring Readiness Through Education and Validation

Pro Tip

Regular tabletop exercises are crucial for identifying gaps and improving team coordination before a real incident.

Risk Alert

Untested plans can lead to confusion and delays during a critical incident, increasing potential damage.

Key Takeaway

Effective preparation includes continuous training, comprehensive asset management, and rigorous testing to validate the incident response plan.

Pillars of Preparedness

Employee Training

Security awareness for all, technical skills for IRT.

Asset Identification

Maintain up-to-date inventories of critical IT assets.

Communication Channels

Establish clear lines with internal and external stakeholders.

Plan Testing & Simulations

Conduct tabletop exercises to validate procedures and readiness.
