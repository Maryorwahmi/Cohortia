Preparation: Building Your Foundation

Developing Policies, Team Structure, and Essential Tools

Why it matters

Proactive preparation minimizes chaos and enables rapid, effective response when an incident occurs.

Common Mistake

Neglecting to develop clear policies or acquire necessary tools leaves critical gaps in readiness.

Key Takeaway

The Preparation phase establishes the foundational policies, team, and tools necessary for an effective and rapid incident response.
