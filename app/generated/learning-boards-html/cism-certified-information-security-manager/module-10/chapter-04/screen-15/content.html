Key Concepts

Phases of Incident Response in Action

Detection & Analysis Phase

Identifying security events, confirming them as incidents, gathering information, and prioritizing.

Containment Phase

Limiting the scope and impact of an incident, preventing further damage or spread of the attack.

Eradication & Recovery Phase

Removing the root cause of the incident and restoring affected systems and data to normal, secure operation.

Summary Takeaway

These active phases ensure effective mitigation, threat removal, and secure restoration of services after a security incident.
