The Integrated Resilience Framework

Unifying Incident Response, BCP, and DRP for Holistic Protection

  • •

    IR: Immediate Action. Focuses on containing and recovering from specific security incidents.

  • •

    BCP: Business Continuity. Maintains critical business functions during and after disruption.

  • •

    DRP: IT Recovery. Recovers IT infrastructure and systems post-disaster.

  • •

    Integration: The Key. Prevents siloed efforts, ensuring a cohesive and effective response to major disruptions.

Key Takeaway

Without tight integration, organizations risk having excellent incident response capabilities that cannot fully recover business operations, or robust BCP/DRP plans that are never triggered or informed by security incident data.
