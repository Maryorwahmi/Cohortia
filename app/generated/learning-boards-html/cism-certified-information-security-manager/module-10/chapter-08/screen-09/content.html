Guiding Recovery with RTOs and RPOs

Aligning Incident Response with Business Recovery Objectives

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Maximum tolerable downtime for a system or business function after an incident.

Example: 4-Hour RTO

A critical customer-facing system must be restored and operational within 4 hours to avoid significant business impact.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Maximum tolerable data loss for a system or business function after an incident.

Example: 1-Hour RPO

No more than 1 hour of data can be lost for a critical transaction database, requiring frequent backups or replication.

Key Takeaway

RTOs and RPOs are business-driven mandates that directly inform and prioritize the technical recovery actions of the Incident Response team, ensuring alignment with organizational resilience goals.
