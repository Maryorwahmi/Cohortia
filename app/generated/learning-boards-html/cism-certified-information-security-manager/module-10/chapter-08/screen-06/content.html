Understanding Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)

Recovering IT Infrastructure and Systems After a Disaster

What is DRP?

A subset of BCP, specifically focused on the recovery of IT infrastructure and systems after a disaster. It's about getting the technology back online.

  • •

    Restore Data: Procedures for recovering from backups.

  • •

    Rebuild Systems: Steps for server and network reconfiguration.

  • •

    Bring Applications Online: Detailed processes for application recovery.

Key Takeaway

DRP is the technical implementation plan for restoring IT services, forming the backbone of an organization's ability to resume operations after a significant IT disruption.

Example Scenario

Data Center Outage

A major power grid failure or cyberattack renders the primary data center completely inoperable, affecting all hosted IT systems and applications.

DRP Activation

The DRP is activated, detailing steps to failover to a secondary data center, restore critical databases from offsite backups, and re-establish network connectivity to resume IT services.

DRP focuses on the *technology* side of recovery.
