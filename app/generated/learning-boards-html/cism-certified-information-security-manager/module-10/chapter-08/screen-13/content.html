The CISM's Role in Fostering Integration

Ensuring Cohesion Across Incident Response, BCP, and DRP

Key Responsibilities

  • •

    Participate in Steering Committees: Ensure security is central to BCP/DRP.

  • •

    Advocate for Integrated Testing: Promote joint exercises and simulations.

  • •

    Bridge Communication Gaps: Foster common terminology and shared understanding.

Critical Safety Notes

  • •

    Secure Backup Systems: Protect DRP-critical backups from cyber threats.

  • •

    Regular Plan Reviews: Update BCP/DRP based on lessons learned from incidents.

  • •

    Promote Holistic View: Ensure IR considers business impact, and BCP/DRP understand cyber risks.

Key Takeaway

The CISM is instrumental in driving the integration of IR, BCP, and DRP, ensuring that security is embedded in all resilience planning and that plans are continuously improved and secured against evolving threats.
