Understanding Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

Maintaining Critical Business Functions During Disruption

What is BCP?

A comprehensive plan to ensure an organization can continue to operate its critical business functions during and after a disruption. It's about keeping the business running.

  • •

    Identify Critical Processes: Pinpoint essential business operations.

  • •

    Business Impact Analysis (BIA): Assess impact if processes are unavailable.

  • •

    Develop Strategies: Create plans for continued operation, even at reduced capacity.

Key Takeaway

BCP is the strategic framework that ensures an organization's core operations can persist through any significant disruption, minimizing financial and reputational damage.

Example Scenario

Cyberattack on Customer Service System

A ransomware attack takes down the primary customer service system, preventing agents from accessing customer data and processing inquiries.

BCP Activation

The BCP outlines activating a temporary, manual inquiry process using backup communication channels and a reduced team, ensuring critical customer support continues.

BCP focuses on the *business* side of continuity.
