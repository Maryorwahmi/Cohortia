Incident Escalation: Triggering BCP and DRP

How Security Incidents Activate Broader Resilience Plans

1. Major Security Incident

Widespread ransomware, sustained DDoS, significant data breach causing system downtime.

2. IR Classification & Prioritization

IR team assesses scope, impact, and determines if BCP/DRP engagement is needed based on predefined criteria.

3. BCP/DRP Activation

BCP or DRP plans are formally activated to manage business continuity and IT recovery efforts.

Key Takeaway

A clear, predefined escalation path from incident response to BCP/DRP activation is crucial to prevent delays and ensure a coordinated, effective organizational response to major disruptions.
