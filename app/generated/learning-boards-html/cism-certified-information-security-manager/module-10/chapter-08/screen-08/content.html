Information Exchange for Coordinated Recovery

IR's Role in Informing BCP and DRP Strategies

Critical Information from IR

  • •

    Scope of Compromise: Extent of the security breach.

  • •

    Affected Systems: Specific IT assets impacted.

  • •

    Data Integrity Status: Reliability and trustworthiness of data.

  • •

    Estimated Recovery Timelines: Realistic projections for restoration.

Impact on BCP/DRP Decisions

  • •

    BCP: Informs which business functions can be restored and alternative process needs.

  • •

    DRP: Guides prioritization of IT system recovery and data restoration strategies.

  • •

    Overall: Prevents misinformed decisions, optimizes resource allocation, and shortens recovery time.

Key Takeaway

Continuous, accurate information flow from Incident Response is indispensable for BCP and DRP teams to make effective, data-driven decisions during a crisis, ensuring a streamlined recovery.
