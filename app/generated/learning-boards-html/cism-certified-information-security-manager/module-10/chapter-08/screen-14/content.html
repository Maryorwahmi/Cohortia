Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Integrated Resilience

Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

A plan to ensure an organization can continue to operate critical business functions during and after a disruption.

Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)

A subset of BCP, specifically focused on the recovery of IT infrastructure and systems after a disaster.

Integration

The process of ensuring that Incident Response, BCP, and DRP are aligned, mutually supportive, and operate cohesively.

Summary Takeaway

BCP, DRP, and their seamless integration are foundational pillars for ensuring an organization's continuous operation and effective recovery from disruptions.
