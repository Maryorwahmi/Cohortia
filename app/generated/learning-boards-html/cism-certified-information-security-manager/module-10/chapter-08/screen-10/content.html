Leveraging Resources and Crisis Leadership

BCP/DRP's Role in Providing Strategic Support During Incidents

Resource Allocation

  • •

    Alternative Facilities: Backup offices, command centers.

  • •

    Personnel: Designated backup teams or roles.

  • •

    Communication Methods: Backup systems for internal/external comms.

IR Leverage

IR teams utilize these BCP-defined resources during widespread incidents affecting primary infrastructure.

Crisis Management

  • •

    Strategic Decisions: Senior management-led, based on IR input.

  • •

    Stakeholder Communications: Managing media, investors, regulators.

  • •

    Overall Organizational Response: Coordinated, holistic approach to the disruption.

CISM Role

Ensure IR provides critical data to the crisis management team for informed decisions.

Key Takeaway

BCP and DRP establish the essential framework for resource allocation and crisis management, providing strategic leadership and support that is critical for navigating major organizational disruptions effectively.
