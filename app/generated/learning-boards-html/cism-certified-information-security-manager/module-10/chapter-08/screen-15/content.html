Key Concepts (Continued)

Critical Metrics and Management Functions

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable downtime for a system or business function after an incident.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable data loss for a system or business function after an incident.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A process to identify and evaluate the potential effects of an interruption to critical business operations.

Summary Takeaway

RTO, RPO, and BIA define recovery targets, while Crisis Management provides the overarching strategic oversight for integrated resilience efforts.
