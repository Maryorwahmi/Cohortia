Evidence Handling

Chain of Custody: Proving Evidence Has Not Been Tampered With

A meticulous, unbroken record of evidence possession is crucial for its legal validity.

Key Steps in Maintaining Custody

  • •**Systematic Collection:** Gather all relevant data completely.
  • •**Meticulous Documentation:** Record every action, person, time, and location.
  • •**Secure Storage:** Protect evidence from unauthorized access or environmental damage.
Key Takeaway

An unbroken chain of custody log is essential to demonstrate the authenticity and integrity of digital evidence, making it legally defensible.

Chain of Custody Log Example

Evidence Item: Server Disk Image (SHA256: abc...123)

Date/Time: 2023-10-26 10:00 UTC

Collected By: Jane Doe (Forensic Analyst)

Location: Data Center, Server Rack 5

Action: Created forensic image of /dev/sda

---

Date/Time: 2023-10-26 14:00 UTC

Transferred To: John Smith (Lead Investigator)

Action: Handed over encrypted drive containing image

---

Date/Time: 2023-10-27 09:00 UTC

Accessed By: John Smith

Action: Mounted image for initial analysis

Common Mistake: Incomplete Documentation

Any missing entry or inconsistency in the chain of custody log can create doubt about the evidence's integrity, potentially leading to its exclusion from legal proceedings.
