Evidence Handling

Forensic Integrity: The Foundation of Admissible Evidence

Ensuring digital evidence is accurate, complete, and protected for legal proceedings.

Principles of Forensic Integrity

  • •**Accuracy:** Evidence must reflect the original state without distortion.
  • •**Completeness:** All relevant data must be captured, not just selected portions.
  • •**Protection:** Safeguarding evidence from alteration, damage, or destruction.
Key Takeaway

Forensic integrity is non-negotiable for legal admissibility; always prioritize preservation over hasty recovery to ensure evidence remains untainted.

Why it Matters: Legal Admissibility

Without forensic integrity, digital evidence can be challenged and deemed inadmissible in court, undermining legal action.

Example: Forensic Imaging

Instead of directly analyzing a compromised hard drive, a forensic analyst creates a bit-for-bit image of the drive. This image is then analyzed, leaving the original drive untouched and preserving its integrity as evidence.
