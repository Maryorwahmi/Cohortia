Industry Specifics

HIPAA, PCI DSS, and Sector-Specific Regulations

Exploring specialized compliance requirements for healthcare, payment card data, and other industries.

HIPAA: Healthcare in the US

  • •**Focus:** Protected Health Information (PHI).
  • •**Notification:** To affected individuals, HHS Secretary, and sometimes media.
  • •**Impact:** Strict rules for healthcare providers and their business associates.
Key Takeaway

CISMs must tailor incident response plans to incorporate specific industry regulations like HIPAA and PCI DSS, collaborating closely with legal and privacy teams.

PCI DSS: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard

  • •**Focus:** Credit card data handling.
  • •**Requirement:** Mandates specific IR procedures and notification to payment brands.
  • •**Status:** A standard, not a law, but contractually binding.

Example: Retailer Breach

A retail company experiences a breach of its point-of-sale systems, exposing credit card numbers. The CISM must immediately activate PCI DSS incident response procedures, including notifying payment card brands and forensic investigators, alongside any applicable state breach notification laws.

Sector-Specific Regulations

Beyond these, industries like financial services, energy, and critical infrastructure often have their own unique, stringent incident reporting and notification requirements due to the sensitive nature of their operations.
