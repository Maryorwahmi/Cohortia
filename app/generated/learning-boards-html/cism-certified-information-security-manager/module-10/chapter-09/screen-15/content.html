Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Roles & Ethical Considerations

Understanding the human and moral dimensions of incident management.

Legal Counsel

Attorneys providing legal advice and guidance during an incident, especially concerning regulatory compliance and liability.

Privacy Officer

An individual responsible for ensuring an organization's compliance with privacy laws and regulations.

Ethical Dilemmas

Situations during incident response where difficult choices must be made, balancing competing moral or legal obligations.

Summary Takeaway

Effective incident response requires close collaboration with legal and privacy experts, alongside a strong ethical framework to navigate complex moral choices.
