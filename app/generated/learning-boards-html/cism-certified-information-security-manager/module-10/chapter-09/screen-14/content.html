Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Legal & Forensic Foundations

Essential terms for understanding legal and evidence-based incident response.

Data Breach Notification Laws

Legal requirements mandating organizations to inform affected individuals and authorities about security breaches involving personal data.

Forensic Integrity

The principle that digital evidence must be accurate, complete, and protected from modification to be admissible in legal proceedings.

Chain of Custody

A documented, unbroken record of the possession, handling, and analysis of physical and electronic evidence.

Summary Takeaway

Effective incident response hinges on understanding and meticulously applying data breach laws, forensic integrity, and chain of custody to ensure legal compliance and evidence admissibility.
