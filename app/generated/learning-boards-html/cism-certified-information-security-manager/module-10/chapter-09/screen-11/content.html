Ethical Considerations

Navigating Insider Threats and Ethical Hacking

Addressing the ethical nuances of internal incidents and authorized security testing.

Insider Threat Response

  • •**Accidental Error:** Focus on retraining, process improvement, and support.
  • •**Malicious Intent:** Requires disciplinary action, legal involvement, and robust forensic investigation.
  • •**Balance:** Protect security needs while respecting employee rights and due process.
Key Takeaway

CISMs must establish clear policies for handling insider threats based on intent and ensure ethical hacking is always authorized and scoped to prevent unintended harm.

Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing

  • •**Authorization:** Always obtain explicit, written permission.
  • •**Scope Definition:** Clearly define targets, methods, and boundaries.
  • •**Communication:** Maintain transparent communication with stakeholders.
  • •**Avoid Harm:** Ensure testing does not cause unintended disruption or data loss.

Example: Accidental Data Leak

An employee accidentally uploads a spreadsheet with customer PII to a public cloud repository. The CISM's response focuses on immediate containment, data deletion, and then a review of training and access controls, rather than immediate punitive action, recognizing the non-malicious nature of the error.
