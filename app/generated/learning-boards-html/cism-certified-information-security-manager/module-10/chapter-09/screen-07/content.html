IR Planning

Integrating Legal & Regulatory Requirements into IR Plans

Proactively embedding compliance into your incident response framework is essential for effective management.

Key Elements for Integration

  • •**Clear Notification Triggers:** Define when specific laws require action.
  • •**Designated Reporting Contacts:** Identify who to notify (authorities, individuals).
  • •**Breach Notification Templates:** Pre-approved communication for various scenarios.
Key Takeaway

Early and continuous collaboration with legal counsel and privacy officers is paramount to build a legally sound and compliant incident response plan.

Common Mistake: Delayed Legal Engagement

Waiting to involve legal counsel until an incident is severe can compromise legal privilege, lead to incorrect breach notifications, and increase organizational liability. Engage them during planning and early in any incident.
