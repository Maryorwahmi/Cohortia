CISM's Role

The CISM's Pivotal Role in Ethical Incident Management

Guiding the organization through complexities and fostering a culture of compliance and integrity.

Key Responsibilities

  • •**Policy Establishment:** Create clear guidelines for evidence handling and ethical conduct.
  • •**Team Training:** Ensure IR teams understand legal and ethical obligations.
  • •**Relationship Management:** Foster strong ties with legal, privacy, and regulatory bodies.
Key Takeaway

The CISM is the ethical compass, ensuring all incident response actions are compliant, justifiable, and meticulously documented to protect the organization's integrity.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • •**Prioritizing Speed Over Preservation:** Can destroy critical evidence for legal action.
  • •**Delaying Legal Counsel:** Risks compromising legal privilege and incorrect notifications.

Example: Policy Development

A CISM leads the creation of an 'Evidence Handling and Preservation Policy' that clearly defines roles, responsibilities, and procedures for collecting, storing, and documenting digital evidence, ensuring it meets legal admissibility standards.
