Ethical Dilemmas

Balancing Transparency, Confidentiality, and Privacy

Navigating the complex ethical choices in incident response, especially regarding disclosure and individual rights.

The Ethical Trilemma

Transparency

Confidentiality

Privacy

Key Takeaway

Ethical incident management requires careful consideration of disclosure, protecting sensitive information, and respecting individual privacy rights.

Example: Disclosure Dilemma

During an investigation, revealing specific attack vectors could help other organizations, but it might also give the attacker insight into your defenses. The CISM must weigh the benefits of public disclosure against the risks to ongoing security and investigation integrity.

Common Mistake: Prioritizing Reputation Over Privacy

Delaying or downplaying breach notifications to protect organizational image can lead to further legal and ethical repercussions, eroding trust and increasing liability.

Pro-Tip: Data Minimization

When collecting data for an investigation, always adhere to the principle of data minimization: collect only what is strictly necessary and retain it only for as long as required.
