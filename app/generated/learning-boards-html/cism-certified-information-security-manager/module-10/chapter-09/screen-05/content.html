Regulatory Landscape

Key Data Breach Regulations: GDPR and CCPA/CPRA

Understanding the global and regional laws that mandate specific actions during a data breach.

GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation

  • •**Scope:** Applies to personal data of EU citizens, globally.
  • •**Notification:** 72 hours to supervisory authorities; high-risk cases to individuals.
  • •**Penalties:** Up to 4% of global annual turnover or €20 million.
Key Takeaway

GDPR and CCPA/CPRA impose strict notification timelines and significant penalties, requiring organizations to have robust, jurisdiction-specific incident response plans.

CCPA/CPRA: California Privacy Acts

  • •**Scope:** California consumers' privacy rights.
  • •**Notification:** Required for breaches of unencrypted/unredacted personal information.
  • •**Impact:** Grants consumers rights like access, deletion, and opt-out.

Example: Multi-Jurisdictional Breach

A SaaS company with users in both the EU and California experiences a breach. The CISM must ensure the incident response plan addresses both GDPR's 72-hour notification to EU authorities and CCPA/CPRA's specific requirements for California residents, potentially involving different communication channels and content.

Common Misconception

Many believe these regulations only apply to large, multinational corporations. In reality, any organization processing data from individuals in these jurisdictions, regardless of its size or location, may be subject to these laws.
