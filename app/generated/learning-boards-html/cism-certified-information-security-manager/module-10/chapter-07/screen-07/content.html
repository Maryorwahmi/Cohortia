Evaluating Response Effectiveness

A critical self-assessment of the entire incident response process to identify areas of success and opportunities for improvement.

  • What Worked Well? Identify successful strategies, tools, and team actions.

  • Where Were the Gaps? Pinpoint communication breakdowns, resource shortages, or process inefficiencies.

  • Tool & Training Adequacy: Assess if tools and team skills were sufficient for the incident.

Comprehensive Evaluation

This phase requires an honest, objective assessment to ensure all aspects of the response are considered for improvement.

Pro-Tip

Use a structured questionnaire or checklist during the review to ensure all critical areas, from communication to technical execution, are consistently evaluated.

Key Takeaway

A balanced assessment of strengths and weaknesses is crucial for refining incident response plans and building a more resilient security posture.
