Creating Concrete Recommendations

The most critical output: a set of concrete, actionable recommendations with owners and target dates.

  • Policy Updates: Revise security policies or operational procedures.

  • Control Implementation: Deploy new security controls (e.g., MFA, EDR).

  • Training & Awareness: Conduct additional training for IRT or general employees.

SMART Recommendations

Each action item should be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound to ensure effective implementation.

Common Pitfall

Generating recommendations without assigning clear ownership or target completion dates, leading to inaction and missed opportunities for improvement.

Key Takeaway

Action item generation is the bridge between analysis and tangible security enhancement, requiring clear ownership and deadlines for success.
