Why Conduct a Post-Incident Review?

The post-incident review is a critical phase for organizational learning and continuous improvement, ensuring incidents lead to stronger security.

  • Understand What Happened: Gain a clear, objective view of the incident's progression and impact.

  • Identify Strengths & Weaknesses: Evaluate the effectiveness of the incident response plan and team.

  • Drive Continuous Improvement: Generate actionable recommendations to prevent recurrence and enhance future responses.

Beyond Recovery

The review ensures that the organization learns from every incident, transforming challenges into opportunities for strengthening its overall security posture.

Scenario Example

After a successful phishing attack, a review revealed that while the technical response was swift, employee awareness training was outdated. The review led to a new, mandatory training program, preventing similar future incidents.

Key Takeaway

A post-incident review is a proactive measure, not a reactive formality, essential for building a resilient and continuously improving security program.
