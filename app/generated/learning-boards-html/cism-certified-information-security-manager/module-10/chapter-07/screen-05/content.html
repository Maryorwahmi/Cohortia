Building the Incident Narrative

A detailed, chronological account of the incident from initial detection to full recovery.

  • Chronological Order: Sequence events precisely from start to finish.

  • Data Sources: Consolidate information from logs, alerts, team notes, and interviews.

  • Identify Critical Points: Pinpoint key decisions and their impact on the incident's progression.

Timeline Visualization

Visualizing the sequence of events helps in understanding the attack's progression and the effectiveness of response actions.

Example Scenario

A timeline showed that a critical alert was generated at 10:00 AM, but the security team didn't investigate until 11:30 AM due to a shift change. This highlighted a gap in handover procedures.

Key Takeaway

Accurate timeline reconstruction is the bedrock for all subsequent analysis, providing the factual basis for understanding and improving incident response.
