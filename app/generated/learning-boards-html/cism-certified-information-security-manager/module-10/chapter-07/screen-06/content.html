Uncovering the 'Why'

A thorough investigation to uncover the fundamental reasons behind an incident, beyond immediate symptoms.

  • Systemic Issues: Look beyond technical vulnerabilities to process or policy gaps.

  • Process Failures: Identify breakdowns in operational procedures or workflows.

  • Human Factors: Consider training gaps, awareness issues, or human error.

The '5 Whys' Technique

Repeatedly asking 'Why?' helps drill down from symptoms to the ultimate root cause, ensuring comprehensive problem-solving.

Common Mistake

Stopping at the immediate technical vulnerability (e.g., 'unpatched server') without investigating the underlying process failure (e.g., 'broken patch management policy').

Key Takeaway

Effective RCA is crucial for implementing sustainable security improvements, preventing recurrence, and building long-term resilience.
