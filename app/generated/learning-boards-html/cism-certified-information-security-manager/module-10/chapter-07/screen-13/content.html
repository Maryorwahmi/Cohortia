Scenario: Phishing Data Breach

An organization experienced a phishing attack leading to a limited data breach. Review findings:

  • Detection: Employee reported, not automated systems.

  • Containment: 4-hour delay to disable accounts due to manual approvals.

  • Root Cause: Lack of employee awareness; outdated email gateway configuration.

Drafting Recommendations

Translate findings into specific, actionable recommendations with clear ownership and target dates.

Example Recommendation

Recommendation: Conduct mandatory, interactive phishing awareness training for all employees quarterly.
Owner: HR & Security Awareness Lead
Target Date: Q1 next year

Key Takeaway

Effective recommendations are SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) and directly address identified root causes and weaknesses.
