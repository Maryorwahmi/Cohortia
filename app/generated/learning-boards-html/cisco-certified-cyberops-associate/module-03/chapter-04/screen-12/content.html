False Positives and False Negatives

Navigating the Challenges of Alert Analysis

False Positive

Alert for legitimate activity

False Negative

Real attack goes undetected

Impact of False Positives

Leads to 'alert fatigue,' where analysts become desensitized and may miss genuine threats amidst excessive noise.

Risk of False Negatives

Allows actual attacks to succeed without detection, posing a significant security breach risk.

Key Takeaway

Effective alert analysis requires careful tuning of IDS/IPS rules and thorough investigation to minimize both false positives and false negatives.
