Essential IDS/IPS Terminology

Reinforcing Core Definitions

IDS (Intrusion Detection System)

Monitors network or system activities for malicious behavior and policy violations, producing alerts without blocking traffic.

IPS (Intrusion Prevention System)

Monitors network activities for malicious behavior and automatically takes action to prevent or block detected threats.

Signature-based Detection

Detects threats by comparing network traffic against a database of known attack patterns (signatures).

Summary Takeaway

IDS detects and alerts, IPS detects and prevents, and signature-based detection relies on known patterns to identify threats.
