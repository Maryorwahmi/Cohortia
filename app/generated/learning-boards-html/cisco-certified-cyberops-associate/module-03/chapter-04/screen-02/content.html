Differentiate IDS & IPS

Explain the fundamental differences between Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) and Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS). This objective focuses on their core functions: detection and alerting versus detection and active blocking, which is critical for understanding their roles in network defense.

Compare Detection Methods
