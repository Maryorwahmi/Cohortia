How it Works

Compares network traffic against a database of predefined attack patterns or signatures.

Strengths

Highly effective against known threats with established signatures, providing reliable detection.

Limitations

Ineffective against zero-day attacks or novel threats for which no signature yet exists.

Key Takeaway

Signature-based detection is robust for known threats but requires constant updates and cannot detect entirely new attack vectors.
