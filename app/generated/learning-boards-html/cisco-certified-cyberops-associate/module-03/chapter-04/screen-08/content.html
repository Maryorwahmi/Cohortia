Signature vs. Anomaly: Strengths & Weaknesses

Choosing the Right Detection Approach

Signature-Based Detection

  • **Strengths:** Highly accurate for known threats, low false positives for established attacks, efficient processing.
  • **Weaknesses:** Cannot detect zero-day attacks or novel variants, requires constant signature updates, reactive to new threats.
  • **Best For:** Identifying common malware, specific exploit attempts, and known attack patterns.

Anomaly-Based Detection

  • **Strengths:** Effective against unknown and zero-day attacks, identifies unusual behavior, proactive in detecting new threats.
  • **Weaknesses:** Higher rate of false positives, requires extensive training period to build baseline, 'normal' behavior can be dynamic.
  • **Best For:** Detecting insider threats, data exfiltration, and novel attack techniques.
Key Takeaway

Combining signature and anomaly detection provides a more robust defense, covering both known and unknown threats, but requires careful tuning to manage alerts.
