NIDS/NIPS Deployment Strategies

Strategic Placement for Network-Wide Protection

Perimeter Defense
Internal Monitoring

NIPS at Perimeter

Placed in-line to block incoming attacks before they reach internal systems (e.g., after firewall).

NIDS for Internal Segments

Deployed out-of-band to monitor traffic within internal networks for lateral movement or post-compromise activity.

Key Takeaway

Strategic placement of NIDS/NIPS at key network points, such as the perimeter or internal choke points, is crucial for comprehensive threat detection and prevention.
