Baseline

Monitoring network activity for deviations from a learned baseline of 'normal' behavior.

How it Works

Establishes a baseline of normal network behavior, then flags any significant deviations as suspicious.

Strengths

Effective at detecting unknown or novel attacks, including zero-day exploits, without prior knowledge.

Limitations

Prone to generating more false positives due to the difficulty of accurately defining and maintaining 'normal' behavior.

Key Takeaway

Anomaly-based detection is powerful for new threats but requires careful tuning to minimize false positives from legitimate unusual activity.
