IDS & IPS: Your Network's Silent Guardians

Detecting and Preventing Cyber Intrusions

As a CyberOps Associate, you'll learn how Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) and Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS) serve as critical security tools, automating the initial detection and response to malicious network activity. This chapter will equip you with the knowledge to differentiate these systems, understand their detection methods, and interpret their alerts.

Key Takeaway

IDS and IPS are foundational network security tools that automate the monitoring and protection of network traffic against cyber threats.

IDS: Detect & Alert

Monitors passively for threats.

IPS: Detect & Prevent

Actively blocks detected threats.
