Detection Focus

SMB/RDP Anomalies

Malware can leverage common protocols like SMB and RDP for lateral movement and C2. Monitoring for unusual activity on these protocols is key.

  • •**Failed Login Attempts:** Brute-force or credential stuffing attempts over SMB/RDP.
  • •**Unusual File Access:** Accessing shares or files not typically used by a host.
  • •**Connections from Unexpected Hosts:** RDP/SMB sessions initiated from unusual source IPs.
Attacker Mistake

Attackers often use hardcoded C2 IPs or easily detectable protocols, or fail to encrypt/obfuscate traffic, making detection simpler.

Safety & Best Practices

Secure Malware Analysis Environment

Isolated Environment

Always analyze potential malware C2 traffic or suspicious files within a sandboxed or isolated environment.

Never Execute on Workstation

Never execute suspicious files on your primary analysis workstation to prevent accidental infection or compromise.

Continuous Learning

Attackers constantly evolve; continuous learning and adaptation are crucial for effective defense and detection.

Key Takeaway

Proactive monitoring of all network protocols and secure analysis practices are essential for combating evolving malware threats.
