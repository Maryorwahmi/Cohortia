Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Reconnaissance

The initial phase of an attack where the adversary gathers information about the target network, often through port scanning and ping sweeps.

DoS/DDoS

Attacks aiming to make a service or resource unavailable to legitimate users by overwhelming it with traffic, such as a SYN flood.

MiTM

An attack where the adversary secretly relays and possibly alters communication between two parties, often using ARP poisoning to redirect traffic.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the core mechanisms of these attack vectors is the first step in developing effective detection and defense strategies.
