Key Concepts

Advanced attack components and detection terms

Malware Propagation

The process by which malicious software spreads across a network or to other systems, often exploiting vulnerabilities or social engineering.

C2 (Command & Control)

The server and infrastructure used by an attacker to maintain communication with compromised systems (bots or agents) for instructions or data exfiltration.

DNS Tunneling

A technique used to encapsulate data within DNS queries and responses to bypass firewalls or exfiltrate data covertly.

Summary Takeaway

Advanced attack techniques like C2 and DNS tunneling require sophisticated detection methods and continuous vigilance from defenders.
