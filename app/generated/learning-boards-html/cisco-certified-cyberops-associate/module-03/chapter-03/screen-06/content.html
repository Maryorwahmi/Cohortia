Attack Objective

Denial-of-Service (DoS/DDoS)

These attacks aim to make a service or resource unavailable to its legitimate users by overwhelming it with traffic or requests. DoS is from a single source, while DDoS uses multiple compromised systems (botnet).

  • •**DoS:** Single source, targets specific vulnerability or resource.
  • •**DDoS:** Multiple sources (botnet), distributed and harder to block.
  • •**Impact:** Service outages, financial loss, reputational damage.
Analogy

Imagine a popular restaurant suddenly flooded with fake reservation calls, preventing real customers from booking tables. That's a DoS attack.

Common Attack Vector

SYN Flood: Exhausting Connections

Mechanism

Attacker sends a flood of TCP SYN packets but never completes the three-way handshake (no ACK). This exhausts the target's connection table.

Traffic Manifestation

Many SYN packets from various source IPs (DDoS) or a single IP (DoS), with no corresponding ACK packets from the attacker.

Detection Tip

Look for a high ratio of SYN packets to SYN-ACK/ACK packets, indicating incomplete handshakes.
