Reconstructing Network Sessions

Reassembling data streams to view complete conversations between two endpoints.

Beyond Individual Packets

Session reconstruction allows you to view the entire conversation between two hosts, providing the full context of communication rather than isolated packet details.

The 'Follow Stream' Feature

In Wireshark, the 'Follow TCP Stream' (or UDP/HTTP) feature reassembles all related packets into a single, coherent data stream, revealing the raw data exchanged.

Key Takeaway

Session reconstruction is invaluable for forensic analysis, revealing sensitive data, malicious payloads, and complete communication narratives.

Steps to Follow a Stream

1

Select a packet within the desired session.

2

Right-click the packet.

3

Choose 'Follow' then 'TCP Stream' (or relevant protocol).

4

Analyze the reconstructed data in the new window.

Example: Uncovering Credentials

Following an unencrypted HTTP stream can reveal plaintext usernames, passwords, or other sensitive form data submitted by a user, providing direct evidence of compromise.
