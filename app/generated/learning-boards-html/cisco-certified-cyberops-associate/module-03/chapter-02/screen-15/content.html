Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential terms from this chapter to reinforce your learning.

BPF (Berkeley Packet Filter)

The syntax used for tcpdump and Wireshark capture filters to specify traffic.

TCP Flags

Control bits in the TCP header (SYN, ACK, FIN, RST) indicating segment purpose.

Session Reconstruction

Reassembling data from multiple packets to view complete communication flows.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding protocol details and the ability to reconstruct sessions are advanced skills for comprehensive network security analysis.
