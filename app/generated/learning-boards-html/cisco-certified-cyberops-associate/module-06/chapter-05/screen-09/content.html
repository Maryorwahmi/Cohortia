Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

Aligning security with business objectives and regulatory requirements.

Governance

Provides strategic direction, oversight, and accountability for security initiatives, ensuring alignment with organizational goals.

Risk Management

Identifies, assesses, and mitigates potential threats and vulnerabilities to protect organizational assets.

Compliance

Ensures adherence to internal policies, industry standards, and external legal/regulatory requirements.

CyberOps Role

Technical work feeds into GRC by providing data on control effectiveness, threats, and compliance posture.

GRC: An Integrated Approach

GRC is not just a set of individual functions; it's a unified strategy to align IT and business objectives, manage risk effectively, and meet compliance requirements efficiently.

Key Takeaway

GRC provides a holistic framework for organizations to manage security risks, ensure compliance, and align cybersecurity efforts with overall business strategy.
