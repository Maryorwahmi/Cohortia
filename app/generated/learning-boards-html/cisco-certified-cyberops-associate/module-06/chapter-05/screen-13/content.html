Risk-Based Prioritization in the SOC

Applying a risk lens to daily security monitoring and incident response.

Risk Lens

Every alert and vulnerability is evaluated based on its potential impact to critical assets and likelihood of exploitation.

Contextual Prioritization

Prioritizing alerts considers the asset's criticality, not just the technical severity of the threat or vulnerability.

Supporting Tools

Threat intelligence platforms and vulnerability management systems provide context for risk-based decisions.

Continuous Contribution

CyberOps associates continuously strengthen security governance and resilience through risk-aware actions.

Example: Prioritizing Vulnerabilities

Consider two identical critical vulnerabilities:

  • Vulnerability A: In a non-critical internal development server.
  • Vulnerability B: In a public-facing e-commerce server handling customer payments.

A risk-based approach would prioritize fixing Vulnerability B first due to its higher potential impact on critical assets and reputation.

Key Takeaway

Effective SOC operations require a risk-based approach to prioritize threats and vulnerabilities, focusing resources where they can have the greatest impact.
