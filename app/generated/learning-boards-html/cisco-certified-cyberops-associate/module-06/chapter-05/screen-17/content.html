Key Concepts: Management & Resilience

Core processes and plans for organizational security.

Risk Management

The process of identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks to an acceptable level.

GRC

Integrated approach to managing governance, enterprise risk, and compliance with regulations.

BCP & DRP

Plans to maintain critical business functions (BCP) and recover IT systems (DRP) during disruptions.

Summary Takeaway

These frameworks provide the structure for organizations to proactively manage risks and ensure operational continuity and recovery.
