Key Concepts: Understanding Risk

Essential definitions for managing cybersecurity threats.

Risk

The potential for loss or harm resulting from a threat exploiting a vulnerability to an asset.

Asset

Anything of value to an organization that requires protection, such as data, systems, or reputation.

Threat

A potential cause of an undesirable incident that may result in harm to a system or organization.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the interplay between assets, threats, and vulnerabilities is crucial for effective risk identification and management.
