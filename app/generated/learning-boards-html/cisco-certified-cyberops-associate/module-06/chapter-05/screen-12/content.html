BCP and DRP: A CyberOps Perspective

How CyberOps associates contribute to organizational resilience.

Testing DRPs

Involvement in verifying backup systems, recovery procedures, and ensuring data integrity post-restoration.

Incident Simulation

Participating in drills that simulate major outages or cyberattacks to refine response procedures.

Recovery Guidance

Following DRP guidelines during actual incidents to restore systems, isolate threats, and maintain data integrity.

Proactive Role

Ensuring backup systems are secure and operational, directly contributing to the organization's ability to recover.

Scenario: Ransomware Recovery

A CyberOps team faces a widespread ransomware attack. Their DRP guides them to:

  • Execute isolation procedures for affected segments.
  • Initiate restoration from immutable backups.
  • Monitor restored systems for any lingering malicious activity.
  • Collaborate with other teams to verify full service restoration.
Key Takeaway

CyberOps associates are integral to BCP and DRP, actively participating in testing, simulations, and the technical recovery process to build organizational resilience.
