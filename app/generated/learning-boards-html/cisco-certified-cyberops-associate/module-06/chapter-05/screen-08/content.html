RISK STRATEGIES

Mitigating and Managing Risks

Reducing Exposure to Acceptable Levels

Mitigation Strategies

Approaches to reduce risk to an acceptable level:

  • Implement Security Controls (firewalls, encryption, policies)
  • Transfer Risk (e.g., cyber insurance)
  • Avoid Risk (discontinue risky activities)
  • Accept Risk (for low-impact, low-likelihood risks)
Key Takeaway

Risk mitigation is an ongoing process that requires a combination of technical, administrative, and strategic approaches.

CyberOps Role in Mitigation

Daily activities directly contribute to reducing risk exposure:

  • Monitoring for threats and anomalies
  • Patching vulnerabilities and system hardening
  • Responding to security incidents
Common Mistakes

Failing to regularly update risk assessments, ignoring human/process risks, or implementing controls without understanding their true effectiveness.
