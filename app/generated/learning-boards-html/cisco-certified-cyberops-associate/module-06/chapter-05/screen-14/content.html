Continuous Risk Management in the SOC

Evolving strategies for ongoing threat detection and response.

Dynamic Threat Landscape

The SOC must constantly adapt to new vulnerabilities, attack techniques, and evolving threat actors.

Feedback Loop

Detection and response activities inform and refine risk mitigation strategies, creating a continuous cycle.

CyberOps Role

Staying informed, participating in training, and contributing to process improvements for enhanced defense.

Proactive Stance

Anticipating potential attacks, strengthening defenses, and updating security controls before exploitation occurs.

Example: Adapting to New Threats

When a new zero-day vulnerability is announced, the SOC would:

  • Immediately update threat intelligence feeds.
  • Scan for the vulnerability across all assets.
  • Deploy temporary compensating controls (e.g., WAF rules) if a patch isn't available.
  • Monitor for exploitation attempts and refine detection rules.
Key Takeaway

Continuous risk management is essential for the SOC to maintain an adaptive and resilient security posture against evolving cyber threats.
