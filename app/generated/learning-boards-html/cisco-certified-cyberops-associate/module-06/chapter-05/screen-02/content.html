Define Cybersecurity Risk

Clearly articulate what cybersecurity risk means and identify its four core components: asset, threat, vulnerability, and impact. This skill is foundational for effective security analysis.

Identify Threats & Vulnerabilities

Recognize and categorize common threats and vulnerabilities that pose risks to an organization's digital assets. This enables proactive defense and targeted security measures.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Explain Risk Management Stages

Describe the systematic stages of the risk management process, including identification, assessment, and mitigation. This provides a structured approach to security.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Describe Mitigation Strategies

Outline various strategies for reducing identified cybersecurity risks to an acceptable level. This includes implementing controls, transferring, avoiding, or accepting risk.
