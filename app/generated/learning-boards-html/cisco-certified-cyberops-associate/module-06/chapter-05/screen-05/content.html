RISK COMPONENTS

Identifying Assets and Threats

What We Protect and What We Protect Against

What is an Asset?

An asset is anything of value to the organization that requires protection. This includes tangible and intangible items.

  • Data (customer records, intellectual property)
  • Systems (servers, networks, applications)
  • Reputation and Employees
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive asset inventory is the foundation for understanding what needs protection from various threats.

UNDERSTANDING THREATS

What is a Threat?

A threat is a potential cause of an undesirable incident that may result in harm to a system or organization.

  • Malware (viruses, ransomware, spyware)
  • Insider Threats (negligent or malicious employees)
  • Natural Disasters (floods, fires, earthquakes)
  • External Attackers (hacktivists, nation-states)
Pro-Tip

Threat intelligence feeds are invaluable for staying updated on emerging threats and attacker tactics.
