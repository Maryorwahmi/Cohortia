Understanding Cybersecurity Risk

The Potential for Loss or Harm in the Digital Realm

What is Cybersecurity Risk?

Cybersecurity risk is defined as the potential for loss or harm resulting from a threat exploiting a vulnerability to an asset. It's the combination of the likelihood of an event occurring and its potential negative impact.

Key Takeaway

Effective cybersecurity begins with a clear understanding of risk, enabling proactive defense and informed decision-making.

Common Misconception

Risk is often confused with just 'threats'. Remember, risk requires a threat, a vulnerability, and an asset, not just a threat alone.
