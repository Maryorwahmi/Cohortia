RISK COMPONENTS

Vulnerabilities and Their Impact

Weaknesses Attackers Exploit and the Consequences

What is a Vulnerability?

A weakness in an asset or its protective controls that can be exploited by a threat.

  • Unpatched software (known security flaws)
  • Weak passwords or authentication mechanisms
  • Misconfigured firewalls or network devices
Key Takeaway

Vulnerability management is crucial for reducing the attack surface and preventing successful exploitation.

UNDERSTANDING IMPACT

What is Impact?

The magnitude of harm that could result from a successful exploitation of a vulnerability.

  • Financial loss (fines, recovery costs)
  • Reputational damage and loss of trust
  • Operational disruption or data loss
Scenario Example

A critical vulnerability in a public-facing web server (asset) exploited by an attacker (threat) to steal customer data (impact) represents a high risk.
