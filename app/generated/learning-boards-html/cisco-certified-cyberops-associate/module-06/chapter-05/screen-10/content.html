Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

Ensuring critical business functions continue during disruptions.

Core Objective

Maintain essential business operations during and after significant disruptions, ensuring organizational survival.

Scope

Covers all aspects of the business, including IT, personnel, facilities, and supply chains, not just technology.

Key Elements

Defines roles, responsibilities, communication strategies, and recovery objectives to guide actions during a crisis.

Benefits

Minimizes downtime, reduces financial losses, protects reputation, and ensures continued service delivery.

BCP in Action: A Retail Example

Imagine a major power outage affecting a retail chain's headquarters. A robust BCP would:

  • Activate a remote operations center for essential staff.
  • Shift online order processing to a backup cloud provider.
  • Implement manual payment processing for physical stores.
  • Establish alternative communication channels for employees and customers.
Key Takeaway

BCP is a holistic strategy to ensure an organization's core business functions can withstand and recover from significant disruptions.
