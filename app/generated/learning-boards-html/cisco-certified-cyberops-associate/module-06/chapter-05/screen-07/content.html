The Risk Management Process

From Discovery to Evaluation

01

Risk Identification

The process of discovering, recognizing, and describing potential risks. This involves:

  • Asset inventories
  • Threat intelligence analysis
  • Vulnerability assessments
02

Risk Assessment

Analyzing the likelihood of a threat exploiting a vulnerability and the potential impact if it occurs.

  • Likelihood evaluation (e.g., high, medium, low)
  • Impact analysis (e.g., financial, reputational)
  • Prioritization of risks
Key Takeaway

Risk identification and assessment are crucial for understanding an organization's security posture and guiding subsequent mitigation efforts.
