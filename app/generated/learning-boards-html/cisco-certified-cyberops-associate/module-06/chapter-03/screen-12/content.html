Policies in Action for CyberOps

Guiding Daily Tasks and Decision-Making

Defining Your Role

Scope and Expectations

Policies define the boundaries of a CyberOps associate's actions, responsibilities, and performance expectations.

  • •What data can be accessed and how.
  • •How security tools should be used.
  • •Reporting structures for incidents.
Scenario Example

Data Handling Violation

A data handling policy prohibits copying sensitive customer data to unencrypted removable media. A CyberOps analyst detects a violation.

Guided Response

The incident response policy and associated SOPs would guide the analyst's actions: detection, containment, reporting, and evidence collection.

Key Takeaway

Policies and procedures are not optional; they are the essential framework that guides a CyberOps associate's every action, ensuring effective and compliant security operations.
