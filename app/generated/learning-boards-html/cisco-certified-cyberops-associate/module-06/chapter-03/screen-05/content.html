Definition

What is an AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) outlines the proper use of company resources, including computers, networks, and internet access, specifying what employees can and cannot do.

  • •Protects company assets from misuse and abuse.
  • •Reduces legal liabilities for the organization.
  • •Maintains network performance and security.
Key Takeaway

The AUP is crucial for setting clear boundaries on resource usage, preventing misuse, and forming a basis for identifying security policy violations.

Key Aspects

Common AUP Provisions

Prohibited Activities

Unauthorized software installation, illegal content access, security bypass attempts.

Personal Use Limits

Guidelines on non-business related internet browsing or email usage.

Intellectual Property

Rules regarding the creation and sharing of company-owned content.

Reporting Obligations

Requirement to report suspicious activities or security incidents.

Scenario Example

An employee installing unapproved peer-to-peer software violates the AUP, risking malware introduction and data leakage. A CyberOps associate would investigate this as a policy breach.
