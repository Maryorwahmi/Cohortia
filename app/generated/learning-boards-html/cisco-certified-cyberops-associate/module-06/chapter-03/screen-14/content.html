Key Concepts

Reinforcing Core Understandings

Security Policy

A high-level statement defining rules and guidelines for protecting information assets.

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

A policy outlining the proper and permitted use of an organization's IT resources.

Incident Response Policy

A high-level policy outlining the organization's approach to managing security incidents.

Summary Takeaway

Security policies, including AUPs and Incident Response Policies, are the foundational rules that guide an organization's cybersecurity posture and operational responses.
