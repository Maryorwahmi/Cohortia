Policies vs. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

The 'What' vs. The 'How' of Security

Security Policies

High-Level Directives ('What')

Statements approved by management defining rules, principles, and guidelines for protecting information assets.

  • •Strategic and broad in scope.
  • •Focus on objectives and desired outcomes.
  • •Example: "All critical security incidents must be reported within 15 minutes."
Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Detailed Instructions ('How')

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for carrying out specific tasks or processes, ensuring consistency and efficiency.

  • •Tactical and specific in nature.
  • •Focus on actions and execution.
  • •Example: "Log into SIEM, identify ID, open ticket, escalate to Tier 2 via chat, email team."
Key Takeaway

Policies set the strategic direction, while SOPs provide the practical, step-by-step guidance needed to execute security tasks consistently and effectively.
