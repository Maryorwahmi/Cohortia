Protecting Credentials and Data Lifecycles

Understanding Password and Data Retention Policies

Policy Focus

Password Policy

Dictates requirements for password complexity, length, expiration, and reuse to prevent unauthorized access through weak credentials.

  • •Minimum length (e.g., 12 characters).
  • •Character complexity (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols).
  • •Regular expiration and prohibition of reuse.
Why it matters

Prevents brute-force attacks and credential stuffing, safeguarding user accounts and system access.

Policy Focus

Data Retention Policy

Defines how long different types of data must be stored and how they should be securely disposed of, crucial for legal compliance and efficient storage management.

  • •Specifies retention periods based on data type and regulatory needs.
  • •Outlines secure disposal methods (e.g., shredding, wiping, degaussing).
  • •Ensures compliance with privacy laws (GDPR, HIPAA).
Why it matters

Minimizes legal risk, reduces storage costs, and prevents sensitive data from being retained unnecessarily.

Key Takeaway

Both password and data retention policies are vital for maintaining strong security controls and ensuring an organization meets its legal and operational obligations.
