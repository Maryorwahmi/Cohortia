The Policy Lifecycle: Phase 1

From Conception to Formal Endorsement

Step 1

Policy Creation

Policies are drafted based on business needs, risk assessments, and regulatory requirements. This involves input from IT, legal, HR, and management.

  • •Identify specific security gaps or compliance needs.
  • •Gather input from relevant departments.
  • •Draft clear, concise, and actionable policy statements.
Step 2

Review and Approval

Drafted policies undergo a thorough review by stakeholders and formal approval by management, ensuring they are comprehensive, enforceable, and aligned with goals.

  • •Legal review for compliance and enforceability.
  • •Technical review for practicality and impact.
  • •Executive approval for official endorsement.
Key Takeaway

The initial phases of policy creation and approval are critical for establishing authoritative, relevant, and enforceable security guidelines.
