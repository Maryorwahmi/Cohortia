The Policy Lifecycle: Phase 3

Adapting to Evolving Threats and Technologies

Step 5

Review and Update

Policies require regular review and updates to remain relevant in the face of evolving threats, technologies, and business changes.

  • •Scheduled annual or bi-annual reviews.
  • •Triggered reviews after major incidents or technology changes.
  • •Incorporating feedback from enforcement and audits.
Consequences of Neglect

The Danger of Outdated Policies

Outdated policies can be as detrimental as no policies at all, creating significant gaps in an organization's security posture.

  • •Failure to address new attack vectors.
  • •Non-compliance with new regulations.
  • •Confusion among employees regarding current expectations.
Key Takeaway

Continuous review and updating ensure that security policies remain relevant, effective, and capable of protecting against current and emerging threats.
