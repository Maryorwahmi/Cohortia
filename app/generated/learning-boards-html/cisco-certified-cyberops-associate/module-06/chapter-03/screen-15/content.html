Key Concepts

Operationalizing Security Principles

Policy Lifecycle

The ongoing process of creating, reviewing, approving, communicating, enforcing, and updating security policies.

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for performing specific tasks or processes.

Compliance

Adherence to established rules, regulations, laws, and policies.

Summary Takeaway

The Policy Lifecycle, SOPs, and Compliance are interconnected concepts that ensure security policies are effectively managed, executed, and adhered to for robust organizational defense.
