The Policy Lifecycle: Phase 2

Ensuring Awareness and Adherence

Step 3

Communication

Policies must be effectively communicated to all relevant employees through training, awareness programs, and accessible documentation.

  • •Mandatory security awareness training.
  • •Regular reminders and updates.
  • •Clear, understandable language.
Step 4

Enforcement

Policies must be consistently applied, and violations must be addressed promptly and fairly to maintain credibility and effectiveness.

  • •Consistent application across all departments.
  • •Clear disciplinary actions for non-compliance.
  • •Regular audits to check adherence.
Key Takeaway

Effective communication ensures awareness, while consistent enforcement ensures adherence, both vital for policies to achieve their security objectives.
