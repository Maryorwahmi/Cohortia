Chapter Overview

Cryptographic Implementations and Tools

Securing Digital Communications and Data

This chapter explores the practical application of cryptographic principles in real-world systems, focusing on protocols like TLS/SSL, VPNs, and various disk and file encryption methods. It also introduces essential cryptographic tools such as OpenSSL and GnuPG, highlighting their usage and implications within a Security Operations Center (SOC) environment.

Key Idea

Bridging Theory to Practice: Understand how cryptographic principles are applied in essential security technologies and tools to protect data in transit and at rest.
