Core Concept

TLS/SSL Protocols

The Foundation of Secure Web Communications

What is TLS?

Transport Layer Security (TLS) is the protocol securing communication over computer networks, succeeding SSL. It's crucial for HTTPS, encrypting web traffic.

Hybrid Encryption Model

TLS uses asymmetric encryption for secure key exchange, then switches to faster symmetric encryption for bulk data transfer, combining efficiency with strong security.

Visual Indicator: HTTPS

The 'https://' prefix and padlock icon in your browser signify that TLS is actively encrypting your connection, protecting your data from eavesdropping.

Key Takeaway

TLS/SSL is the backbone of web security, using a clever hybrid encryption strategy to keep your online data confidential and integral.
