Core Concept

Virtual Private Networks (VPNs)

Creating Secure Tunnels Over Untrusted Networks

What is a VPN?

A VPN creates a secure, encrypted tunnel over an untrusted network, like the public internet, to protect data in transit.

Secure Remote Access

Allows remote users to securely access internal corporate resources as if they were physically on the internal network.

Encryption Process

Traffic is encrypted at the client, sent through the public internet, and then decrypted at the VPN server, typically at the corporate perimeter.

Key Takeaway

VPNs are essential for secure remote access, creating encrypted tunnels that protect sensitive data as it travels over public networks.
