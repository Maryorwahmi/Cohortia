Data Protection

Encrypting Data at Rest

Securing Information on Devices and Storage

Disk Encryption

Entire Drive Protection

  • Encrypts entire hard drives or partitions.
  • Prevents unauthorized access if device is lost or stolen.
  • Examples: Microsoft BitLocker (Windows), LUKS (Linux).
  • Data is unreadable without the correct key.
File-Level Encryption

Granular File Protection

  • Encrypts individual files or folders.
  • Offers more granular control over specific data.
  • Often integrated into operating systems or third-party tools.
  • Useful for protecting specific sensitive documents.
Key Derivation

Encryption keys are typically derived from a user password or a hardware-based Trusted Platform Module (TPM) for enhanced security.

Why it Matters

Essential for protecting sensitive data from physical theft or unauthorized access to storage devices, ensuring confidentiality and compliance.

Key Takeaway

Data at rest encryption, through disk or file-level methods, is vital for protecting stored information, especially on devices prone to loss or theft, using passwords or TPMs for key security.
