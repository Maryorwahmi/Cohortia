Analyzing TLS Encrypted Traffic

Insights from Metadata and Certificate Inspection

TLS Certificate Inspection

Inspecting TLS Certificates

Verify validity, check for malicious patterns, and understand metadata for threat detection.

Metadata Analysis

Source/destination IPs, port numbers, and connection times are visible even in encrypted traffic.

Certificate Validity

Check for expiration, revocation, or untrusted issuers to identify potential risks.

Malicious Patterns

Identify known indicators of compromise within certificate details, such as suspicious common names.

TLS Decryption (Controlled)

Advanced systems may decrypt TLS traffic for deeper inspection in controlled environments, raising privacy concerns.

Key Takeaway

SOC analysts use TLS metadata and certificate details for threat detection, carefully balancing security inspection with privacy, especially when considering TLS decryption.
