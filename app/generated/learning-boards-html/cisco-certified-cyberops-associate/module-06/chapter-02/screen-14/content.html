Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways: Part 2

Disk Encryption

The process of encrypting an entire hard drive or storage volume (e.g., BitLocker, LUKS) to protect data at rest.

OpenSSL

A robust, commercial-grade, and full-featured toolkit for TLS/SSL protocols and a general-purpose cryptography library.

GnuPG (GNU Privacy Guard)

A free implementation of the OpenPGP standard, used for encrypting and signing data and communications.

Summary Takeaway

Disk encryption secures data at rest, while OpenSSL and GnuPG are powerful tools for cryptographic operations and secure communication.
