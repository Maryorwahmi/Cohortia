Key Concepts: Frameworks & Impact

Essential terms for structured security and compliance

PCI DSS

Global standard for organizations handling branded credit cards, mandating controls for cardholder data security.

Cybersecurity Framework

Set of guidelines and best practices to manage and reduce cybersecurity risks (e.g., NIST CSF, ISO 27001).

Non-compliance

Failure to adhere to applicable laws, regulations, or standards, often resulting in severe penalties and reputational harm.

Summary Takeaway

Cybersecurity frameworks provide structured guidance for risk management, while understanding the severe consequences of non-compliance drives diligent CyberOps practices.
