ISO/IEC 27001: Information Security Management

Establishing a robust Information Security Management System (ISMS)

What is ISO 27001?

An international standard specifying requirements for an Information Security Management System (ISMS), a systematic approach to managing sensitive company information.

ISMS Components
  • People: Roles and responsibilities.
  • Processes: Security policies and procedures.
  • IT Systems: Technical controls and infrastructure.
  • Risk Management: Identification and mitigation.
CyberOps Role
  • Adhere to security policies.
  • Participate in risk assessments.
  • Contribute to ISMS continuous improvement.
Practical Example

Vulnerability Management in ISMS

In an ISO 27001 certified organization, when a new critical vulnerability is identified, the CyberOps team follows a documented procedure within the ISMS. This includes assessing the risk, planning the patch deployment, implementing the fix, and verifying its effectiveness. All steps are recorded, demonstrating adherence to the ISMS and contributing to its continuous improvement cycle (Plan-Do-Check-Act).

Key Takeaway

ISO 27001 provides a comprehensive, management-driven framework for information security, requiring CyberOps to integrate security practices into a systematic ISMS.
