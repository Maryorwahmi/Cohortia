Key Concepts: Regulations

Essential terms to remember for data protection

Regulatory Compliance

Adherence to laws, regulations, and guidelines for data protection and security practices.

GDPR

EU law protecting personal data of EU citizens, imposing strict obligations on data processing and privacy.

HIPAA

U.S. law protecting sensitive patient health information (PHI) through administrative, physical, and technical safeguards.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding major regulations like GDPR and HIPAA is fundamental for CyberOps professionals to ensure data protection and avoid severe penalties.
