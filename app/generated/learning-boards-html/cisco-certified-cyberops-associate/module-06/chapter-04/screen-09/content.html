NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

A flexible approach to managing cybersecurity risk

What is NIST CSF?

A voluntary framework developed by NIST to help organizations manage and reduce cybersecurity risk, structured around five core functions.

Five Core Functions
  • Identify: Understand assets, systems, data.
  • Protect: Implement safeguards.
  • Detect: Identify cybersecurity events.
  • Respond: Take action on incidents.
  • Recover: Restore impaired capabilities.
CyberOps Focus
  • Implementing security monitoring (Detect).
  • Continuous monitoring activities (Detect).
  • Incident response procedures (Respond).
Practical Example

Enhancing Detection Capabilities

Following the NIST CSF 'Detect' function, a CyberOps team might deploy new SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) tools to aggregate and analyze logs from various systems. They would then configure alerts for suspicious activities, ensuring that potential cyberattacks are identified promptly. This proactive approach helps reduce the time to detect and respond to threats, aligning with the framework's goals.

Key Takeaway

NIST CSF provides a clear, adaptable roadmap for organizations to systematically improve their cybersecurity posture, with CyberOps playing a central role in detection and response.
