Digital Certificates and Certificate Authorities

Establishing trust in digital identities and public keys.

Certificate Authority (CA)
Trusted Issuer
Digital Certificate
Binds Public Key to Identity
Purpose

Verifies ownership of a public key, establishing trust in digital identities.

Standard

Often based on the X.509 standard for public key infrastructure.

Use Cases

Securing web traffic (HTTPS), authenticating software, secure email.

Key Takeaway

Digital certificates and CAs form the backbone of trust in digital communications, ensuring that public keys are genuinely associated with their claimed identities.
