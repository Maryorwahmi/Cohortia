The Fundamentals of Cryptography

Cryptography is the science of secure communication. It involves transforming information to protect its confidentiality and integrity.

Plaintext

Original, readable information.

Encryption

Process of converting plaintext to ciphertext using a key.

Ciphertext

Scrambled, unreadable information.

Key Takeaway

Encryption and decryption, controlled by cryptographic keys, are the core processes that secure digital communication.
