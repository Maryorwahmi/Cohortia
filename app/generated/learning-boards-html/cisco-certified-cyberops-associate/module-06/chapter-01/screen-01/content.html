Introduction to Cryptography

Securing Communication in a Hostile World

Explore the foundational principles of cryptography, from basic encryption to advanced digital signatures, and understand their critical role in protecting digital information and communications.

Key Takeaway

Cryptography is the art of protecting information by transforming it into an unreadable format, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity against adversaries.

Confidentiality

Keep data secret.

Integrity

Prevent tampering.

Authenticity

Verify identity.
