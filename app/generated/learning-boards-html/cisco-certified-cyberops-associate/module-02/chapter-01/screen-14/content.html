Core SIEM Terminology

Essential concepts to remember from this chapter.

SIEM

A centralized system that collects, aggregates, analyzes, and presents security-related data from various sources to provide a holistic view of an organization's security posture.

Log Management

The process of collecting, storing, and managing log data from various systems for security, operational, and compliance purposes.

Event Correlation

The process of analyzing multiple security events from different sources to identify patterns or sequences that indicate a potential security incident.

Summary Takeaway

SIEM systems are crucial for modern cybersecurity, integrating log management and advanced event correlation to detect and respond to complex threats effectively.
