01

Explain SIEM's Purpose & Functions

Understand why SIEM systems are essential in a modern SOC and differentiate between their Security Information Management (SIM) and Security Event Management (SEM) capabilities. This knowledge forms the foundation for grasping SIEM's strategic value.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Identify Key Data Sources

Recognize the diverse log and event data sources that feed into a SIEM, such as firewalls, IDS/IPS, servers, and applications. Understanding these inputs is crucial for comprehensive security analysis and threat detection.
