Connecting the Dots: Unveiling Threats with Event Correlation

Analyzing disparate events to identify complex attack patterns.

How Correlation Works

The SIEM's correlation engine processes events from various sources, applying predefined rules, machine learning, and behavioral analytics to detect patterns that signify a security incident.

  • •Analyzes events across different devices and timeframes.
  • •Identifies sequences or combinations of events.
  • •Detects anomalies and known attack signatures.

Correlation Process Flow

1

Collect Events: Gather logs from firewalls, servers, IDS, etc.

2

Aggregate & Normalize: Consolidate and standardize event data.

3

Apply Correlation Rules: Match patterns against known threats or anomalies.

4

Generate Alert: Notify analysts of detected security incidents.

Key Takeaway

Event correlation is the SIEM's ability to turn seemingly isolated events into clear indicators of complex, coordinated attacks.
