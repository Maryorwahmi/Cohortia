Evolution of Endpoint Protection

From Reactive Signatures to Proactive Behavior

Traditional Antivirus (AV)
  • Signature-Based: Detects known malware by matching digital signatures.
  • Reactive: Effective against previously identified threats.
  • Limitations: Struggles with zero-day attacks, polymorphic, and fileless malware.
  • Role Today: Still provides a baseline defense against common, known threats.
Modern Threats & EDR
  • Behavioral Analysis: Detects suspicious activities and anomalies, not just signatures.
  • Proactive: Identifies unknown threats, zero-days, and fileless malware.
  • Capabilities: Continuous monitoring, telemetry collection, machine learning.
  • Role Today: Essential for advanced threat detection and rapid response.
Key Takeaway

While traditional AV detects known threats, modern cyberattacks require advanced solutions like EDR that focus on behavioral analysis to counter evolving and unknown threats.
