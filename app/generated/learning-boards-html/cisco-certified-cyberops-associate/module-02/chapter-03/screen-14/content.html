Key Concepts

Essential Terms in Endpoint Security

Endpoint Security Monitoring

The practice of securing and monitoring individual devices (laptops, desktops, servers) for malicious activity.

EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response)

Advanced security solutions that continuously monitor endpoint activity, collect telemetry, and use analytics to detect and respond to threats.

Antivirus (AV)

Software designed to detect, prevent, and remove malicious software, primarily based on known signatures.

Summary Takeaway

Endpoint security is a multi-faceted defense, combining continuous monitoring, advanced behavioral detection (EDR), and foundational signature-based protection (AV) to safeguard individual devices.
