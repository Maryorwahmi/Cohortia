Core Concept

The Endpoint as a Target

Endpoints are individual devices like laptops, desktops, servers, and mobile devices. They are often the primary target for attackers due to user interaction and direct access to data and applications.

Key Vulnerabilities
  • User Interaction: Humans are often the weakest link, susceptible to phishing and social engineering.
  • Software Flaws: Operating systems and applications can have vulnerabilities exploited by attackers.
  • Direct Data Access: Endpoints hold sensitive data, making them high-value targets.
  • Internal Access: A compromised endpoint can provide a foothold for lateral movement within the network.
Why Endpoint Monitoring is Crucial

Endpoint monitoring provides essential visibility into activities that network-level defenses might miss, such as internal lateral movement or malware execution.

Early Threat Detection

Identify malicious activity before it escalates into a major breach.

Internal Visibility

Monitor activities not visible at the network perimeter, like lateral movement.

Data Protection

Safeguard sensitive information stored or processed on individual devices.

Compliance & Audit

Meet regulatory requirements by tracking and logging endpoint activities.

Key Takeaway

Endpoints are critical attack surfaces; comprehensive security requires dedicated monitoring to detect and respond to threats that bypass perimeter defenses.
