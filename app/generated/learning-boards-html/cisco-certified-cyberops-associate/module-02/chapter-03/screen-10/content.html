What is Host-Based Forensics?

Investigating Compromised Systems

Host-based forensics involves systematically collecting and analyzing data from a compromised system to understand the nature and scope of a security incident.

Key Objectives
  • Understand What Happened: Reconstruct the timeline of events.
  • Identify Attack Methods: Determine how the attacker gained access and operated.
  • Assess Impact: Identify affected data, systems, and potential exfiltration.
  • Gather Evidence: Collect artifacts for remediation and legal purposes.
Why It's Critical for Incident Response

Host-based forensics provides granular details that network monitoring alone cannot, offering deep insights into internal activities post-compromise.

Granular Detail

Reveals specific processes, files, and user actions on the compromised host.

Root Cause Analysis

Helps pinpoint the initial vulnerability or entry point.

Containment & Eradication

Informs precise actions needed to stop the attack and remove threats.

Evidence Preservation

Ensures data integrity for legal or compliance requirements.

Key Takeaway

Host-based forensics is essential for deep incident investigation, providing detailed insights into attacker actions and enabling effective remediation.
