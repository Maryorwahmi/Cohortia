Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
Passive Monitoring for Suspicious Activity

An IDS passively monitors network traffic for malicious activity or policy violations, generating alerts when threats are detected.

It operates using signature-based detection (known threats) or anomaly-based detection (deviations from normal behavior).

  • Passive Operation: Detects threats but does not block or alter traffic.
  • Alerting Mechanism: Notifies administrators or SIEM systems of detected incidents.
  • Detection Methods: Signature-based (known patterns) and anomaly-based (unusual behavior).
  • Types: Network-based (NIDS) and Host-based (HIDS).
Silent Alarms

Like a silent alarm, an IDS detects and reports without direct intervention.

Key Takeaway

IDS provides crucial visibility by detecting threats passively, enabling timely alerts for further investigation.
