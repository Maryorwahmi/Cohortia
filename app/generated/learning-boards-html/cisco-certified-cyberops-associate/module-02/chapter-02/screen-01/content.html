Understanding Network Security Monitoring (NSM)
Gaining Visibility into Your Network's Communications
In today's complex digital landscape, understanding what's happening on your network is paramount to defending it. NSM specifically focuses on the lifeblood of your infrastructure: the network traffic itself, providing an unfiltered, ground-truth perspective that can reveal threats that might otherwise go unnoticed.
