Common NSM Mistakes
Avoiding Blind Spots and Tuning Issues

Effective NSM requires avoiding common errors such as incorrect sensor placement and inadequate rule tuning.

Proper integration with SIEM and regular updates are also critical for maintaining detection capabilities.

  • Blind Spots: Sensors placed incorrectly miss critical network traffic.
  • Tuning Issues: Poorly tuned IDS/IPS rules lead to excessive false positives or missed threats.
  • Lack of Integration: NSM data not correlated with SIEM hinders comprehensive incident analysis.
  • Outdated Tools: Failure to update signatures and baselines leaves defenses vulnerable to new threats.
Proactive Management

Regularly review sensor placement, tune rules, and integrate NSM data for optimal effectiveness.

Key Takeaway

Avoiding common NSM pitfalls through proper deployment, tuning, integration, and maintenance is essential for effective threat detection.
