Scenario: Choosing NSM Data
Efficiently Investigating High-Volume Traffic

A security analyst needs to investigate suspected data exfiltration, focusing on identifying high-volume outbound connections efficiently.

Which NSM data type is best suited for quickly identifying traffic volume and involved hosts without capturing full payloads?

  • PCAP: Provides full content, but is resource-intensive for broad analysis.
  • NetFlow/IPFIX: Summarizes traffic volume, participants, and duration efficiently.
  • Firewall Logs: Show allowed/denied connections, not traffic volume details.
  • DNS Logs: Track name resolutions, not traffic volume or content.
Efficient Analysis

NetFlow/IPFIX provides the necessary summary data for efficient identification of high-volume traffic.

Key Takeaway

For efficiently investigating high-volume traffic and identifying involved hosts, NetFlow/IPFIX data is the preferred choice over PCAP.
