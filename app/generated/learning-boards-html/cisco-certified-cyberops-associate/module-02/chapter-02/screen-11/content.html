NSM in Incident Response
Reconstructing Events and Understanding Scope

NSM data is indispensable during incident response, providing the evidence needed to understand and manage security breaches.

It allows analysts to reconstruct attack timelines, identify affected systems, and determine the scope of a compromise.

  • Scope Determination: Flow data helps identify all systems communicating with a compromised host.
  • Event Reconstruction: PCAP reveals exact commands, file transfers, and communication details.
  • Timeline Analysis: Establishes when the incident began, how it progressed, and its impact.
  • Evidence Gathering: Provides crucial data for remediation and post-incident reporting.
Evidence-Based Response

NSM data provides the factual basis for effective containment, eradication, and recovery.

Key Takeaway

NSM data is critical for incident response, enabling accurate assessment of scope, detailed reconstruction of events, and effective remediation.
