PCAP vs. Flow Data
Choosing the Right Data for Your NSM Needs

PCAP captures every packet, including headers and payload, offering maximum detail but requiring significant resources.

Flow data summarizes conversations (IPs, ports, bytes, packets), providing efficient, high-level visibility with lower resource demands.

  • PCAP Use Case: Deep forensic analysis, detailed incident reconstruction, capturing specific payloads.
  • Flow Data Use Case: Continuous network-wide monitoring, anomaly detection, traffic trend analysis, capacity planning.
  • Resource Needs: PCAP is high (storage, CPU); Flow Data is low to moderate.
  • Detail Level: PCAP is exhaustive; Flow Data is summarized.
Context-Driven Choice

Select data type based on investigation needs: detail vs. efficiency.

Key Takeaway

The choice between PCAP and flow data hinges on the need for granular detail versus efficient, broad network visibility.
