NSM in Forensic Investigations
Uncovering Digital Evidence from Network Traffic

NSM data provides critical evidence for forensic investigations, helping to reconstruct the 'who, what, when, where, and how' of security incidents.

Full packet capture (PCAP) is particularly valuable for revealing the exact content and methods used during an attack.

  • Direct Evidence: PCAP reveals exact files transferred, communication details, and payload content.
  • Pattern Identification: Flow data helps identify unusual communication patterns and potential data exfiltration.
  • Reconstruction: Enables detailed understanding of attack vectors and attacker actions.
  • Corroboration: NSM data can be correlated with other forensic artifacts for a complete picture.
Digital Evidence Trail

Network traffic provides a detailed trail of digital activities for forensic examination.

Key Takeaway

NSM data is crucial for forensic investigations, offering detailed evidence of network activities and attack methodologies.
