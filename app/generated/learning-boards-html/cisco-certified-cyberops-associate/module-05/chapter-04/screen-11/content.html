Key Metrics for Incident Response: Time-Based

Quantifying Efficiency in Detection, Response, and Containment

Why Measure?
  • **Demonstrate Value:** Show the effectiveness of the IR program.
  • **Justify Resources:** Provide data for budget and staffing requests.
  • **Identify Bottlenecks:** Pinpoint areas needing process or technology improvement.
Time-Based Metrics
MTTD (Mean Time To Detect): Average time to identify an incident from its occurrence.
MTTR (Mean Time To Respond): Average time from detection to full resolution.
MTTC (Mean Time To Contain): Average time to contain an incident after detection.
MTTR (Mean Time To Recover): Average time to restore systems to normal operation.
Key Takeaway

Lower time-based metrics (MTTD, MTTR, MTTC) generally indicate a more effective and efficient incident response program.
