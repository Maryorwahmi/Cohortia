Incident Reporting to Stakeholders

Tailoring Information for Internal and External Audiences

Internal Reports
  • **Audience:** Management, executive leadership, technical teams.
  • **Content:** Incident summary, impact, response actions, key recommendations.
  • **Purpose:** Inform decision-making, drive internal improvements.
External Reports
  • **Audience:** Regulatory bodies, law enforcement, affected customers, partners.
  • **Content:** Accurate, factual incident details, often legally reviewed.
  • **Purpose:** Meet legal/contractual obligations, manage reputation.
Key Takeaway

Always consider your audience when preparing incident reports to ensure clarity, compliance, and appropriate communication.
