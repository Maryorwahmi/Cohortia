Key Concepts: Reporting & Metrics

Essential terms for incident documentation and program evaluation.

Fishbone Diagram

A visual tool for categorizing potential causes of a problem.

Documentation

The process of recording all details of an incident, actions taken, and findings.

MTTD

Mean Time To Detect: Average time to detect an incident.

Summary Takeaway

Effective incident response relies on comprehensive documentation and clear metrics to measure performance and ensure accountability.
