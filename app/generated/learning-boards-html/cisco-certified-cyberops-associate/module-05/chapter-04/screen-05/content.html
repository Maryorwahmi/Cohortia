The Post-Incident Review Meeting

A Structured Approach to Learning from Incidents

Key Participants
  • **Incident Handlers:** Those directly involved in the response.
  • **Forensic Analysts:** Experts in data investigation.
  • **Network/System Admins:** Those managing affected infrastructure.
  • **Management/Legal:** For strategic oversight and compliance.
Meeting Agenda Highlights
1What happened? (Timeline)
2What went well? (Successes)
3What went wrong? (Failures)
4What could be improved? (Recommendations)
5Impact Assessment (Business, Financial, Reputational)
Key Takeaway

A well-structured post-incident review meeting is essential for a holistic understanding of the incident and for generating actionable improvements.
