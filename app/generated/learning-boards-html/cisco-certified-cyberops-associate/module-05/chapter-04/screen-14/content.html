Key Concepts: Analysis & Documentation

Essential terms for post-incident processes.

Post-Incident Review

A meeting to analyze an incident, identify successes and failures, and plan improvements.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

A systematic process for identifying the fundamental reasons for an incident.

5 Whys

An iterative questioning technique used in RCA to uncover deeper causes.

Summary Takeaway

Effective post-incident analysis relies on structured reviews, deep root cause investigation, and systematic questioning to drive continuous security improvement.
