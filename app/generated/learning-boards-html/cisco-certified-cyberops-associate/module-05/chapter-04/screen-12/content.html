Key Metrics for Incident Response: Volume & Cost

Understanding Incident Impact and Program Efficiency

Why Track These?
  • **Resource Allocation:** Justify security investments and staffing.
  • **Trend Analysis:** Identify patterns in attack types and severity.
  • **Risk Assessment:** Understand the true financial and operational impact of incidents.
Additional Metrics
Number of Incidents: Volume of incidents, categorized by type and severity.
Cost Per Incident: Estimated financial impact of each incident.
False Positive Rate: Percentage of alerts that are not true incidents.
Security Awareness Score: Measure of employee understanding and adherence to security policies.
Key Takeaway

Beyond speed, understanding incident volume, cost, and alert accuracy provides a holistic view of IR program performance.
