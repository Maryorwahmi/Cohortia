Navigating the Flood: Triage and Prioritization

Efficiently reviewing alerts and ranking incidents by impact and urgency.

Alert Triage

Quickly reviewing alerts to determine their legitimacy, severity, and potential impact. Essential to combat 'alert fatigue'.

  • **False Positives:** Alerts not indicative of a real threat.
  • **True Positives:** Alerts correctly identifying a security incident.
  • Tuning rules reduces noise.

Incident Prioritization

Ranking incidents based on their potential impact on critical assets (confidentiality, integrity, availability) and urgency.

  • High priority: Production database compromise.
  • Lower priority: Malware on non-critical workstation.
  • Guided by a clear prioritization matrix.
Key Takeaway

Effective triage and prioritization are crucial for managing alert volume, focusing resources on true threats, and ensuring timely response to critical incidents.
