Two Approaches to Threat Identification

Differentiating between known patterns and unusual behaviors.

Signature-Based Detection

Systems look for known patterns (signatures) of malicious activity, such as specific malware hashes or attack strings.

  • Effective against known threats.
  • Struggles with zero-day attacks.
  • Relies on updated threat databases.

Anomaly-Based Detection

Identifies deviations from normal baseline behavior, often leveraging machine learning or statistical analysis.

  • Can detect novel or zero-day threats.
  • May generate more false positives.
  • Requires establishing a 'normal' baseline.
Key Takeaway

Signature-based detection is strong against known threats, while anomaly-based detection excels at finding new threats but requires careful tuning.
