The Preparation Phase: Building Resilience

Laying the groundwork for effective incident response before an attack.

Core Definition

The preparation phase involves establishing a comprehensive ecosystem of people, processes, and technology *before* any security incident occurs. It's about proactive readiness.

People

Trained IR team, clear roles, ongoing skill development.

Processes

Policies, plans, playbooks, and runbooks for systematic response.

Technology

SIEM, EDR, vulnerability scanners, backup systems.

Goal

Minimize chaos, maximize positive outcomes, ensure business continuity.

Key Takeaway

A robust preparation phase is the cornerstone of effective incident response, enabling systematic, efficient, and impactful reactions to security incidents.
