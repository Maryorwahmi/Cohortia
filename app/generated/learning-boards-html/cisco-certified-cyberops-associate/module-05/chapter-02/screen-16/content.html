Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Incident Response Policy

High-level document outlining the organization's overall approach to IR.

Incident Response Plan

Detailed document describing the overall process for handling incidents.

Playbook

Step-by-step guide for specific incident types.

Summary Takeaway

Effective incident response relies on a clear hierarchy of documentation, from high-level policies to granular, actionable playbooks and runbooks.
