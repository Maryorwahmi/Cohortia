Learning from Pitfalls: Common IR Mistakes

Identifying and mitigating frequent errors in preparation and detection.

Untested IR Plans

Creating an IR plan that is too theoretical or not regularly tested through exercises. An untested plan is a broken plan.

  • Leads to chaos during real incidents.
  • Gaps and weaknesses remain undiscovered.
  • Regular tabletop exercises are vital.

Alert Fatigue

Failing to tune SIEM rules and allowing too many false positives, desensitizing analysts to real threats.

  • Critical warnings get missed.
  • Requires continuous tuning of detection tools.
  • Integrate tools for a holistic view.

Over-reliance on Automation

Believing automated systems are foolproof and neglecting the indispensable human element in detection and analysis.

  • Automated systems can be bypassed.
  • Human judgment and intuition are critical.
  • Balance technology with skilled analysts.
Key Takeaway

Regularly test IR plans, tune detection systems to minimize alert fatigue, and always maintain a human element in security analysis to avoid common pitfalls.
