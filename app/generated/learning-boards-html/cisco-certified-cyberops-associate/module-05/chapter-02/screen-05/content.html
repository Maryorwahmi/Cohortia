Strategic Directives: Policy and Plan

Understanding the high-level 'what' and 'how' of incident response.

Incident Response Policy

A high-level, strategic document outlining the organization's overall stance on IR. Defines roles, responsibilities, legal obligations, and program scope.

  • Approved by management.
  • Answers: "What" and "Who."
  • Sets the strategic direction.

Incident Response Plan

A detailed, tactical document describing the overall process for handling incidents, typically following IR lifecycle phases.

  • Provides general procedures.
  • Outlines communication, escalation, resource allocation.
  • Answers: "How" at a broader level.
Key Takeaway

The IR Policy establishes the strategic 'why' and 'who,' while the IR Plan details the tactical 'how' for managing incidents across the organization.
