Tactical Guides: Playbooks and Runbooks

Granular, step-by-step instructions for specific incident scenarios.

Playbooks

Highly specific, step-by-step guides for responding to particular types of incidents (e.g., Ransomware, Phishing).

  • Detail exact actions, commands, data to collect.
  • Ensure consistency and speed during active incidents.
  • Scenario-specific.

Runbooks

Even more granular procedures, often automated or semi-automated, for routine, repetitive tasks within an incident.

  • Integrated into playbooks.
  • Example: `iptables` commands to block an IP, Python scripts.
  • Focus on automation and efficiency.
Key Takeaway

Playbooks guide the overall response to specific incident types, while runbooks provide the precise, often automated, technical steps for individual actions.
