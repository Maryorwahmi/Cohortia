Resilience & Containment: Backups and Segmentation

Ensuring data recovery and limiting attacker lateral movement.

Backup and Recovery Systems

Ensuring reliable, tested backups of critical data and systems is non-negotiable for recovery after destructive incidents.

  • Regularly test backups for restorability.
  • Essential for ransomware recovery.
  • Prevents data loss and ensures business continuity.

Network Segmentation

Implementing VLANs, ACLs, and firewalls to segment your network limits the lateral movement of attackers.

  • Reduces the impact of a breach.
  • Isolates critical assets.
  • Prevents widespread compromise.
Key Takeaway

Robust backups are crucial for data recovery, while network segmentation is vital for containing breaches and limiting attacker lateral movement.
