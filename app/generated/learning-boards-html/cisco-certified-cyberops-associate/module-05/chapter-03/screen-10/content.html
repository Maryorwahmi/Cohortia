Operational Restoration

Bringing Systems Back Online Safely

The final phase of restoring business functions.

01. Restore from Backups

Use clean, verified backups.

02. System Hardening

Re-enforce security controls.

03. Monitor for Recurrence

Closely watch for new activity.

04. Phased Rollout

Bring systems online gradually.

05. Communication

Keep stakeholders informed.

Supporting Explanation
  • Verified Backups: Crucial to prevent re-infection.
  • Enhanced Security: Often beyond pre-incident state.
  • Gradual Return: Minimizes risk during restoration.
  • Transparency: Builds trust with stakeholders.
Concrete Example

After a database compromise, the team restores from a backup verified to be clean, then applies all pending security updates and enables multi-factor authentication for all database access.

Key Takeaway

Recovery is a meticulous process of restoring functionality while simultaneously enhancing security, ensuring resilience and confidence in resumed operations.
