Expanding Your Incident Response Lexicon

Key terms for advanced understanding.

Evidence Preservation

Protecting and maintaining the integrity of digital evidence for forensic analysis.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

Identifying the fundamental reason for an incident to prevent recurrence.

Chain of Custody

Documented history of evidence handling to ensure its integrity and admissibility.

Summary Takeaway

These terms highlight the importance of forensic integrity, root cause identification, and meticulous documentation in comprehensive incident response.
