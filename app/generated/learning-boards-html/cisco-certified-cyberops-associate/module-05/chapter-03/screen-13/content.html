Essential Terms to Remember

Reinforcing core incident response vocabulary.

Containment

Limiting the scope and impact of an incident to prevent further damage.

Eradication

Removing the root cause and all traces of a threat from compromised systems.

Recovery

Restoring affected systems and data to normal, secure operations.

Summary Takeaway

The CER phases are the backbone of effective incident response, guiding actions from initial threat identification to full operational restoration.
