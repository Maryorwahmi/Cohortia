Core Concept

The CER Phases: Stop, Clean, Restore

Understanding the flow of incident response.

01. Containment

Limit the scope and prevent further damage.

02. Eradication

Remove the root cause and all traces of the threat.

03. Recovery

Restore systems to normal, secure operations.

Supporting Explanation
  • Containment: Immediate actions to stop the incident's spread.
  • Eradication: Removing the root cause and all attacker presence.
  • Recovery: Restoring systems to a secure, operational state.
  • Evidence Preservation: Critical throughout all phases for analysis.
Concrete Example

Imagine a ransomware attack: Containment isolates infected machines, Eradication removes the ransomware and patches the vulnerability, and Recovery restores data from clean backups.

Key Takeaway

The CER phases are a structured, systematic approach to incident response, ensuring that an organization can effectively mitigate, clean, and restore operations after a security breach.
