Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Security Event

Any observable occurrence in a system or network, many of which are benign.

Security Incident

A security event that compromises the Confidentiality, Integrity, or Availability (CIA triad) of an information asset.

Incident Response (IR)

An organized approach to addressing and managing the aftermath of a security breach or cyberattack.

Summary: Understanding the difference between events and incidents, the purpose of IR, and its structured lifecycle are foundational for effective cybersecurity defense.
