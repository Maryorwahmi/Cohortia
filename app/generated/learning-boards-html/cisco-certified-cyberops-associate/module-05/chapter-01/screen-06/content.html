IR Frameworks: NIST & SANS

Leveraging established methodologies for consistent response.

Why Frameworks Matter

  • Consistency: Ensures a uniform approach to all incidents.
  • Effectiveness: Proven steps to minimize damage and recover.
  • Compliance: Helps meet regulatory and legal obligations.
NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2

Computer Security Incident Handling Guide

A comprehensive, government-backed framework with a four-phase approach, widely adopted across various sectors for detailed incident management.

SANS Institute

Incident Handler's Handbook

A practical, hands-on guide favored by incident responders, offering a six-step process with a strong emphasis on real-world application.

Key Takeaway

Both NIST and SANS frameworks provide structured, iterative processes that are crucial for effective, auditable, and continuously improving incident response capabilities.
