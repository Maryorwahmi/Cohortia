Key Roles in an IR Team (Part 1)

Understanding the specialists who drive incident response.

Incident Handler/Coordinator

Manages the incident, coordinates the team, and ensures adherence to procedures.

Forensic Analyst

Collects and analyzes digital evidence, ensuring proper chain of custody.

Threat Hunter

Proactively searches for undiscovered threats within the network using advanced techniques.

Key Takeaway

A well-structured IR team with specialized roles like Incident Handlers, Forensic Analysts, and Threat Hunters is crucial for both reactive and proactive incident management.
