Key Concepts Continued

Further essential terms for incident response mastery.

NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2

A widely adopted framework for computer security incident handling, outlining four main phases.

SANS Incident Handler's Handbook

Another popular framework providing practical, often six-step, guidance for incident handlers.

Chain of Custody

The chronological documentation showing the handling and integrity of physical or electronic evidence.

Summary: Leveraging established frameworks like NIST and SANS, alongside meticulous evidence handling through Chain of Custody, ensures a robust and legally defensible incident response.
