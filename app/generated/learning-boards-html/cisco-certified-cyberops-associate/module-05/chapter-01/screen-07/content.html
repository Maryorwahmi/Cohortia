NIST IR Lifecycle Phase 1

Preparation: Building Your Defense

Establishing the foundation before an incident strikes.

Policies & Procedures

Develop clear guidelines, roles, and responsibilities for incident handling.

Team & Training

Form an IR team, assign roles, and conduct regular training and simulations.

Tools & Resilience

Acquire necessary tools (SIEM, forensics) and ensure systems are secure and recoverable.

Common Misconception

IR Starts at Breach

Many believe IR begins only when an incident occurs. In reality, the Preparation phase, conducted beforehand, is crucial for effective and swift response.

Key Takeaway

Effective incident response is built on a strong foundation of proactive preparation, including policies, trained teams, and resilient systems, long before any attack.
