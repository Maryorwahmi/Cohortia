Team Structure & Best Practices

Key Roles in an IR Team (Part 2)

Completing the team and avoiding common pitfalls.

Additional Team Members

  • Network Security Engineer: Network-level containment and traffic analysis.
  • System Administrator/Engineer: Host-level remediation, rebuilds, patching.
  • Communications Lead: Manages internal and external stakeholder communications.
  • Legal Counsel: Provides guidance on legal and regulatory obligations.
Common Mistakes

Panic & Poor Documentation

Acting impulsively without procedures can destroy evidence or escalate incidents. Lack of detailed records hinders analysis and legal compliance.

Safety Note

Evidence Preservation

Always prioritize preserving evidence and maintaining a clear 'chain of custody' for any collected data. This is crucial for forensic analysis and potential legal action.

Key Takeaway

A diverse IR team, coupled with disciplined adherence to procedures, meticulous documentation, and clear communication, is essential for successful incident resolution.
