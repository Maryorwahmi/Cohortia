NIST IR Lifecycle Phase 2

Detection & Analysis

Identifying security events and confirming incidents.

Key Activities

  • Monitor Sources: Logs, network traffic, IDS/IPS, EDR.
  • Correlate Data: Connect disparate alerts to form a complete picture.
  • Assess Impact: Determine scope, nature, and effect on CIA triad.
  • Prioritize: Rank incidents based on severity and potential damage.
Common Pitfall

Alert Fatigue

Overwhelmed by too many non-critical alerts, analysts may miss or dismiss genuine threats. Effective tuning of monitoring systems is crucial.

Example Scenario

Unauthorized Admin Login

A SIEM alert for multiple failed logins followed by a successful login from an unknown IP to an 'admin_prod' account on a web server. This triggers immediate investigation.

Key Takeaway

The Detection & Analysis phase is about vigilant monitoring, thorough investigation, and accurate prioritization to confirm and understand security incidents.
