SANS IR Lifecycle: A Closer Look

Comparing SANS's practical approach with NIST's framework.

SANS 6-Step Process

  • Preparation
  • Identification (NIST: Detection & Analysis)
  • Containment
  • Eradication
  • Recovery
  • Lessons Learned (NIST: Post-Incident Activity)

Key Similarities & Differences

  • Both emphasize a structured, iterative process.
  • Core principles of minimizing damage and learning are shared.
  • Slight differences in terminology and phase breakdown.
  • IR is non-linear; phases can overlap and loop back.
Key Takeaway

Both NIST and SANS provide essential, structured frameworks for incident response, emphasizing flexibility and continuous adaptation to effectively manage evolving cyber threats.
