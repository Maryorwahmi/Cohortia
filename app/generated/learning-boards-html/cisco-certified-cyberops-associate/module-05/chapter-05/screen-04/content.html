Core Concept

SIEM for Incident Response

The Central Nervous System for Security Data

Key Functions in IR

  • Historical Data: Trace attacker movement and initial compromise points.
  • Correlation & Context: Connect disparate events for richer incident understanding.
  • Search & Forensics: Rapidly query vast datasets for Indicators of Compromise (IoCs).
  • Reporting: Generate compliance and incident reports.
Example Scenario

A SIEM correlates a failed login attempt from an unusual geographic location with a subsequent attempt to access sensitive files, providing a clear picture of a potential insider threat or compromised account.

Key Takeaway

SIEMs are critical for incident response by centralizing logs, enabling historical analysis, correlating events, and supporting forensic investigations and reporting.
