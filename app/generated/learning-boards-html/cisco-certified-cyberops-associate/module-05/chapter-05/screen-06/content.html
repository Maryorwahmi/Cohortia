SOAR Platforms: Automation & Response

Streamlining Incident Handling with Playbooks

Key Automation Benefits

  • Reduced MTTR/MTTC: Significantly decreases Mean Time To Respond and Contain.
  • Consistent Execution: Ensures procedures are followed uniformly every time.
  • Reduced Workload: Frees analysts from repetitive tasks, combating alert fatigue.
  • Focus on Complex Tasks: Allows human experts to concentrate on strategic analysis.
Playbook Example: Phishing Alert

1. Ingest email into sandbox. 2. Extract IoCs. 3. Query TIP for reputation. 4. Block sender/URL on firewall. 5. Scan endpoints with EDR. 6. Create IR ticket. 7. Notify handler.

Key Takeaway

SOAR automates routine IR tasks via playbooks, drastically improving response speed, consistency, and analyst efficiency by handling the initial, repetitive steps of an incident.
