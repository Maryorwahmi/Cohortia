Threat Intelligence Platforms (TIPs)

Making External Threat Data Actionable

How TIPs Enhance IR

  • Enrichment: Provides context for alerts (e.g., "known C2 server").
  • Proactive Blocking: Feeds IoCs to security tools to block known threats.
  • Contextual Awareness: Helps understand TTPs of threat actors for better hunting.
  • Centralized Data: Aggregates intelligence from diverse sources.
Example Use Case

A TIP identifies a new phishing campaign targeting your industry. It automatically pushes the malicious sender domains and attachment hashes to your email gateway and EDR, preventing the attack before it reaches users.

Key Takeaway

TIPs are essential for incident response by providing actionable threat context, enabling proactive defense, and improving overall understanding of adversary TTPs.
