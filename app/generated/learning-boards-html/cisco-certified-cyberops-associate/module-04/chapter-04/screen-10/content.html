Patch Management Process

Identification and Acquisition

The First Steps to Secure Systems

1. Identification

  • •Regularly monitor vendor security advisories.
  • •Check vulnerability databases (e.g., NVD).
  • •Stay informed about newly discovered flaws.

2. Acquisition

  • •Download necessary patches from trusted sources.
  • •Only use official vendor sites or approved channels.
  • •Avoid third-party sites to prevent malware.
Security Warning

Downloading patches from untrusted sources can introduce malicious software, compromising your systems further.

Key Takeaway

Effective patch management starts with diligent identification of vulnerabilities and secure acquisition of patches from trusted, official sources.
