Introduction to Patch Management

The Lifecycle of Software Updates

What is Patch Management?

  • •Process of identifying, acquiring, testing, and applying software updates.
  • •Updates (patches) fix bugs, improve performance, and close security holes.
  • •Crucial for maintaining endpoint security against known vulnerabilities.

Why it's Critical

Common Mistake

Neglecting patch management leaves systems vulnerable to easily preventable attacks, as attackers quickly exploit known flaws.

Analogy: Car Maintenance

Just as you perform regular maintenance on a car to prevent breakdowns, patch management is preventative care for your software to prevent security failures.

Key Takeaway

Patch management is a continuous, systematic process essential for protecting systems from constantly discovered software vulnerabilities and preventing easily exploitable attacks.
