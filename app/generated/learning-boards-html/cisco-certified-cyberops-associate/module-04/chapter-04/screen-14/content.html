Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

OS Hardening

Securing an OS by reducing its attack surface and strengthening defenses through configuration changes.

Least Privilege

Users, programs, or processes are granted only the minimum necessary access rights to perform their function.

Attack Surface Reduction

Minimizing the number of potential entry points or vulnerabilities an attacker could exploit.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive measures like OS hardening, least privilege, and attack surface reduction are foundational for building resilient and secure systems.
