Core Concept

Understanding OS Hardening

Reducing the Attack Surface and Strengthening Defenses

What is OS Hardening?

  • •Securing a system by reducing its attack surface.
  • •Strengthening defenses against potential threats.
  • •Configuring the OS to be as secure as possible.
  • •Removing unnecessary components and enforcing strict access controls.

Why it Matters: Attack Surface Reduction

Vulnerabilities

Every service, port, and app is a potential entry point.

Hardening Goal

Minimize these entry points to reduce risk.

Key Takeaway

OS hardening is a proactive defense strategy that systematically reduces vulnerabilities and strengthens system security by minimizing potential attack vectors.
