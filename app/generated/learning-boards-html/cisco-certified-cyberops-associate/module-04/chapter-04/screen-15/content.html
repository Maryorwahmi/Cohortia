Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Secure Configuration

Setting up an OS or application with security best practices, often following industry benchmarks.

CIS Benchmarks

Configuration guidelines by the Center for Internet Security to help secure systems and applications.

Patch Management

Process of identifying, acquiring, testing, and applying software updates to fix vulnerabilities.

Summary Takeaway

Secure configurations, guided by CIS Benchmarks, and continuous patch management are vital for maintaining system integrity and protecting against known vulnerabilities.
