From Antivirus to EPP: A Necessary Evolution
Addressing the Limitations of Traditional Signature-Based AV
Traditional Antivirus (Pre-2010s)

Relied on signature-based detection to identify known malware. Effective against common, static threats.

Limitations Emerge
  • Polymorphic malware: Changes signatures.
  • Obfuscation: Hides malicious code.
  • Fileless malware: Executes in memory, no disk signature.
  • Zero-day exploits: Unknown threats, no existing signatures.
The Challenge

Attackers evolved faster than signature databases could update, creating a critical security gap.

Traditional AV Limitations

Signature-based detection was no longer enough.
