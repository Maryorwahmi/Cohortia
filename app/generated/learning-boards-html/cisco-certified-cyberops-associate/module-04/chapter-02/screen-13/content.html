EPP and EDR: A Complementary Defense-in-Depth Strategy
Combining Prevention with Detection and Response
Common Mistake

Viewing EPP and EDR as mutually exclusive or believing one replaces the other. They are not.

EPP's Role: Foundational Prevention

Stops the majority of common threats (known malware, basic attacks) at the initial entry point.

EDR's Role: Advanced Detection & Response

Combats sophisticated, evasive attacks that bypass initial defenses through continuous monitoring and rapid response.

Defense-in-Depth

Layered security for comprehensive protection.

EPP: Perimeter Defense

Blocks known threats.

EDR: Internal Monitoring

Detects and responds to breaches.

Key Takeaway

EPP and EDR work together to provide a resilient, multi-layered defense against the full spectrum of cyber threats.
