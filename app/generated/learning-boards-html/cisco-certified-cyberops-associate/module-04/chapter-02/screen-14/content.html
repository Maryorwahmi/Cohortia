Key Concepts: Foundational Terms
Essential Definitions for Endpoint Security

Endpoint Protection Platform (EPP)

A suite of security tools integrated into a single agent, primarily focused on preventing known and unknown threats from executing on endpoints.

Next-Generation Antivirus (NGAV)

Advanced antivirus technology that uses machine learning, behavioral analysis, and AI to detect and prevent threats, including zero-day exploits, without relying solely on signatures.

Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)

A cybersecurity solution that continuously monitors endpoints for suspicious activity, collects telemetry data, and provides capabilities for detection, investigation, and automated response to threats.

Summary Takeaway

EPP, NGAV, and EDR represent the evolution of endpoint security, moving from basic prevention to advanced, integrated detection and response capabilities.
