Key Concepts

Essential ideas to remember from this chapter.

IR Lifecycle

A structured approach to managing security breaches, from preparation to lessons learned.

Host-Based Forensics

Collecting and analyzing digital evidence from a specific computer system to understand an incident.

Order of Volatility

Principle dictating that the most volatile data should be collected first to prevent loss.

Summary Takeaway

A structured incident response, guided by the order of volatility, is fundamental for effective host-based forensics and evidence preservation.
