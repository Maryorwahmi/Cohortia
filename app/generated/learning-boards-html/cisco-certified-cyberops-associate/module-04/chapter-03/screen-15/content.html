Key Concepts

Further essential ideas to remember.

Volatile & Non-Volatile Data

Distinguishing data that is easily lost (RAM) from data that persists (disk).

Log Analysis

Examining system-generated records to identify suspicious activities or errors.

Chain of Custody

Meticulous documentation of evidence handling to ensure integrity and admissibility.

Summary Takeaway

Effective incident response requires understanding data volatility, mastering log analysis, and rigorously maintaining the chain of custody for all evidence.
