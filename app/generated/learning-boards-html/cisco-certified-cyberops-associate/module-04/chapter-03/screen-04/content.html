Core Concept

The Incident Response Lifecycle

A structured approach to managing security breaches on endpoints.

1.Preparation
2.Identification
3.Containment
4.Eradication
5.Recovery
6.Lessons Learned
Example Scenario

A company's IR team, having prepared with playbooks and training, identifies a malware infection. They contain it by isolating the host, eradicate the malware, recover data from backups, and update their antivirus definitions based on lessons learned.

Phases Explained

  • •Preparation: Proactive measures like policy creation and team training.
  • •Identification: Detecting and confirming a security event as an incident.
  • •Containment: Limiting the scope and impact of the incident.
  • •Eradication: Removing the root cause of the compromise.
  • •Recovery: Restoring affected systems to normal operations.
  • •Lessons Learned: Reviewing the incident to improve future responses.
Key Takeaway

The IR lifecycle is a continuous process, not a one-time event, emphasizing readiness and continuous improvement.
