Chain of Custody & IR Best Practices

Ensuring evidence integrity and safe incident handling.

What is Chain of Custody?

A chronological paper trail documenting who collected what, when, where, how, and who has had access to evidence.

  • •Vital for maintaining evidence integrity.
  • •Ensures admissibility in legal/compliance contexts.
Example Scenario

An analyst documents collecting a memory dump at 10:00 AM, sealing it, and transferring it to a forensic lab, with each handover signed and dated.

IR Safety Notes & Best Practices

  • •Never work directly on a compromised system; use forensic copies.
  • •Isolate the system to prevent further compromise.
  • •Do not delete or make changes unless part of a controlled plan.
  • •Always have a pre-defined IR plan and trained personnel.
Key Takeaway

Meticulous chain of custody and adherence to safety best practices are non-negotiable for valid and effective incident response.
