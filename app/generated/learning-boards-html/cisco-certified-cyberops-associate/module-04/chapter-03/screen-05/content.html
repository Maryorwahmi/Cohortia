Foundational Principle

Host-Based Forensics and Order of Volatility

Collecting digital evidence systematically to understand a compromise.

What is Host-Based Forensics?

The process of collecting and analyzing digital evidence from a compromised system to understand what happened, how, and its impact.

Why Order of Volatility?

  • •Volatile Data: Easily lost (RAM, running processes, network connections).
  • •Non-Volatile Data: Persists (disk images, log files, registry).
  • •Collect most volatile first to prevent destruction of evidence.
Analogy

Think of it like a melting ice sculpture: you photograph the most intricate details first before they disappear, then focus on the more stable parts.

Data Volatility Spectrum

CPU Registers, Cache (Most Volatile)
System Memory (RAM)
Running Processes, Network Connections
Temporary Files, Swap Space
Hard Drives, SSDs (Least Volatile)
Key Takeaway

Always collect volatile data first to preserve critical evidence that could be lost upon system shutdown or reboot.
