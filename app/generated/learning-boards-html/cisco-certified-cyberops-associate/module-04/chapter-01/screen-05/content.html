Antivirus and Anti-Malware: First Line of Defense

Detecting, preventing, and removing malicious software from endpoints.

Purpose of AV/AM

Antivirus (AV) and Anti-Malware (AM) software are designed to detect, prevent, and remove malicious software from endpoints. They act as a crucial layer of defense against various cyber threats.

  • Protects against viruses, worms, Trojans
  • Combats spyware, adware, ransomware
  • Safeguards against rootkits and botnets

Traditional Approach: Signature-Based Detection

This method relies on matching known malware patterns (signatures) against files on the system. It's effective against known threats but struggles with new or polymorphic malware.

Limitations & Evolution

Signature-based detection is limited against zero-day exploits or polymorphic malware that constantly changes its signature. This led to the development of more advanced detection techniques.

Evolving Threats
Polymorphic Malware
Key Takeaway

AV/AM software is a foundational control, but its effectiveness against new threats depends on advanced detection methods.
