Strategic Defense

The Power of Layered Endpoint Security

Combining AV, HIPS, and host-based firewalls for robust, multi-layered protection.

Antivirus / Anti-Malware

First line of defense, preventing known malicious software from gaining a foothold.

Host-based IPS (HIPS)

Internal monitoring system, detecting and blocking suspicious behaviors and activities.

Host-based Firewall

Granular control over network traffic, closing unnecessary entry and exit points.

Key Takeaway

A multi-layered defense strategy combining AV, HIPS, and firewalls is essential for resilient endpoint security.
