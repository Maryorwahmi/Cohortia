Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Endpoint

Any device connected to a network that can be a source or destination of information.

Attack Surface

The sum of all possible points where an unauthorized user can try to enter or extract data.

Antivirus (AV) / Anti-Malware (AM)

Software designed to detect, prevent, and remove malicious software from endpoints.

Summary Takeaway

Endpoints are the primary targets, forming the attack surface, and AV/AM provides foundational protection against malware.
