Core Concept

Understanding Endpoints: The Foundation of Host Security

Every connected device is an endpoint, a critical component of an organization's attack surface.

What is an Endpoint?

An endpoint is any device connected to a network that can be a source or destination of information. This includes a wide array of devices, from traditional computers to modern IoT sensors.

  • Servers and workstations
  • Laptops and mobile devices
  • Printers and IoT sensors

Why Endpoints Matter

Endpoints are often the primary targets for attackers as they represent direct entry points into a network and hold valuable data or access credentials. Their compromise can lead to widespread network disruption.

The Attack Surface

The attack surface is the sum of all possible points where an unauthorized user can try to enter or extract data from an environment. Endpoints contribute significantly to this surface.

Key Takeaway

Securing individual endpoints is crucial for protecting the entire network from data breaches and disruptions.
