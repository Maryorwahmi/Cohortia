Key Concepts

Review the essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Cybersecurity

The practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.

CIA Triad

Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability: fundamental principles of information security.

Security Operations Center (SOC)

A centralized facility for monitoring, assessing, and defending information systems.

Summary Takeaway

Cybersecurity is the essential practice of protecting digital assets, guided by the CIA Triad, with the SOC serving as the central hub for operational defense against evolving threats.
