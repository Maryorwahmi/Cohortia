Core Concept

What is Cybersecurity?

Protecting digital systems, networks, and data from attacks, damage, or unauthorized access.

Key Principles: The CIA Triad
  • Confidentiality: Ensuring data is accessible only to authorized users.
  • Integrity: Maintaining the accuracy and completeness of data.
  • Availability: Guaranteeing systems and data are accessible when needed.
Concrete Example

Encrypting customer data (Confidentiality), using checksums to verify file transfers (Integrity), and implementing redundant servers to prevent downtime (Availability).

The CIA Triad: Pillars of Information Security

Confidentiality
Integrity
Availability

These three principles are interdependent and equally crucial for a robust security posture.

Key Takeaway

Cybersecurity is a holistic approach to protecting digital assets, fundamentally guided by the CIA triad to ensure data privacy, accuracy, and accessibility.
