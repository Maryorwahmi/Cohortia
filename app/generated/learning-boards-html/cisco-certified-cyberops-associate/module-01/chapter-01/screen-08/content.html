NIST Functions: Detect & Respond

Identifying cybersecurity events and taking swift action to mitigate their impact.

Detect: Spotting the Signs
  • Anomaly Detection: Identifying unusual system or network behavior.
  • Security Continuous Monitoring: Real-time oversight of security events.
  • Detection Processes: Implementing and maintaining detection capabilities.
Respond: Taking Action
  • Incident Response Planning: Developing and executing response plans.
  • Mitigation: Containing and eradicating the impact of an incident.
  • Communications: Coordinating internal and external information sharing.

Detect & Respond Cycle

Detect: Is something wrong?

Monitor logs for suspicious activity, analyze network traffic for anomalies.

Respond: What do we do?

Isolate affected systems, remove malware, communicate incident status.

These functions are crucial for minimizing damage and ensuring rapid recovery from cyber incidents.

Key Takeaway

'Detect' focuses on identifying security events through monitoring and analysis, while 'Respond' involves executing plans to contain, eradicate, and recover from incidents.
