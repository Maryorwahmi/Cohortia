Key Concepts (Continued)

Reinforce your understanding of advanced terms and distinctions.

NIST Cybersecurity Framework

A framework for managing and reducing cybersecurity risk (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover).

Threat & Vulnerability

A threat is a potential danger; a vulnerability is a weakness that can be exploited by a threat.

Malware, Phishing, Zero-day

Key threat types: malicious software, deceptive social engineering, and unknown software flaws.

Summary Takeaway

The NIST Framework provides a structured approach to cybersecurity, while understanding the distinction between threats and vulnerabilities, along with specific threat types like malware, phishing, and zero-day exploits, is crucial for effective defense.
