NIST Function: Recover

Maintaining resilience and restoring capabilities impaired by cybersecurity incidents.

Key Activities in Recovery
  • Recovery Planning: Developing and implementing restoration plans.
  • Improvements: Incorporating lessons learned from incidents.
  • Communications: Coordinating with internal and external parties during recovery.
Concrete Example

After a data breach, an organization uses its recovery plan to restore affected databases from backups, rebuild compromised servers, and update security protocols based on the incident's root cause analysis.

The Recovery Process

Restore Operations

Implement recovery plans to bring systems and services back online.

Post-Incident Review

Analyze the incident to identify root causes and areas for improvement.

Implement Improvements

Update security policies, procedures, and technologies to enhance resilience.

Effective recovery ensures business continuity and strengthens future security posture.

Key Takeaway

The 'Recover' function is vital for restoring operations, learning from incidents, and improving overall organizational resilience against future cyberattacks.
