NIST Functions: Identify & Protect

Understanding assets and implementing safeguards are the first steps in managing cybersecurity risk.

Identify: Know Your Assets
  • Asset Management: Inventorying hardware, software, and data.
  • Risk Assessment: Understanding potential threats and vulnerabilities.
  • Business Environment: Understanding organizational mission and critical functions.
Protect: Implement Safeguards
  • Access Control: Managing user permissions and authentication.
  • Data Security: Encryption, data loss prevention, backups.
  • Protective Technology: Firewalls, antivirus, intrusion prevention systems.

Identify & Protect in Action

Identify: What do we need to protect?

Inventory critical servers, sensitive databases, and user accounts.

Protect: How do we secure it?

Implement multi-factor authentication, encrypt data at rest and in transit, deploy firewalls.

These functions establish the baseline for an organization's cybersecurity posture.

Key Takeaway

'Identify' helps organizations understand their digital landscape and risks, while 'Protect' involves deploying safeguards to secure critical assets and services.
