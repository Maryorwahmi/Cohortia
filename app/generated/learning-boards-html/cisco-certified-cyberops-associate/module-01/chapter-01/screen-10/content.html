Common Cyber Threats: Malware & Phishing

Understanding malicious software and deceptive social engineering attacks.

Malware: Malicious Software
  • Viruses: Attach to programs, spread upon execution.
  • Ransomware: Encrypts data, demands payment for decryption.
  • Trojans: Disguise as legitimate software to gain access.
  • Spyware: Secretly monitors user activity.
Phishing: Deceptive Attacks
  • Email Phishing: Fake emails to steal credentials.
  • Spear Phishing: Targeted attacks on specific individuals.
  • Whaling: Phishing attacks targeting high-profile individuals.

Threat Examples

Malware

A user downloads a 'free' software that secretly installs a keylogger.

Phishing

An email from a fake bank website asks for your login credentials.

These threats exploit both technical vulnerabilities and human trust.

Key Takeaway

Malware encompasses various harmful software, while phishing relies on social engineering to trick users into revealing sensitive information, highlighting the importance of user awareness.
