Advanced Threats

Common Cyber Threats: DoS/DDoS & Zero-day

Understanding attacks that disrupt services and exploit unknown vulnerabilities.

DoS/DDoS: Service Disruption
  • Denial-of-Service (DoS): Overwhelming a target from a single source.
  • Distributed DoS (DDoS): Coordinated attack from multiple compromised systems (botnet).
  • Impact: Makes services unavailable to legitimate users.
Zero-day Exploit: Unknown Vulnerabilities
  • Definition: Vulnerability unknown to the software vendor.
  • Danger: No patch or immediate fix available, leaving systems exposed.
  • Exploitation: Attackers can exploit before defenses are developed.

Threat Scenarios

DDoS Attack

A website is flooded with traffic from thousands of compromised computers, making it inaccessible.

Zero-day Exploit

Attackers exploit a newly discovered flaw in a popular browser before a patch is released.

These threats require advanced detection and proactive defense strategies.

Key Takeaway

DoS/DDoS attacks disrupt services by overwhelming them with traffic, while zero-day exploits leverage unknown software vulnerabilities, making them particularly dangerous due to the lack of immediate defenses.
