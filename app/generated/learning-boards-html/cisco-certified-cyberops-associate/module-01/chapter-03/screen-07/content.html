Endpoint Protection Platforms (EPP)

The First Line of Defense Against Known Threats

What is EPP?

Traditional security suites that primarily focus on preventing known threats based on signatures and heuristics. They are the baseline defense for endpoints.

Key Functionalities

  • Antivirus/Anti-malware protection
  • Host-based firewalls
  • Data encryption
  • Signature-based and heuristic detection
Example in Action

Detecting a Known Virus

An EPP solution scans a newly downloaded file. It identifies a signature match with a known virus in its database. The EPP immediately quarantines the file, preventing it from executing and infecting the system. This is a classic example of EPP's preventative capability.

Limitations

Evasion by Modern Threats

EPPs often struggle against advanced persistent threats (APTs) and fileless malware. These sophisticated attacks can evade signature-based detection by using novel techniques or operating entirely in memory, highlighting the need for more advanced solutions.

Key Takeaway

EPPs are essential for preventing known threats but require augmentation with advanced solutions to combat sophisticated, evolving cyberattacks.
