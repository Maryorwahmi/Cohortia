Routing vs. Switching

Connecting Networks and Local Devices

Routers (Layer 3)

Connect different networks and forward packets based on IP addresses. Essential for inter-network communication.

Switches (Layer 2)

Connect devices within the same local network (LAN) and forward frames based on MAC addresses. Enable efficient local communication.

Key Takeaway

Routers manage traffic between networks, while switches manage traffic within a local network. Both generate critical logs for security monitoring.

Router vs. Switch

Router
  • Connects different networks
  • Operates at Network Layer (L3)
  • Forwards packets by IP address
  • Example: Connects your home network to the internet
Switch
  • Connects devices in same LAN
  • Operates at Data Link Layer (L2)
  • Forwards frames by MAC address
  • Example: Connects computers, printers in an office

CyberOps Relevance: Log Analysis

Router logs reveal inter-network traffic anomalies, while switch logs expose suspicious internal communication or port violations, both crucial for incident detection.
