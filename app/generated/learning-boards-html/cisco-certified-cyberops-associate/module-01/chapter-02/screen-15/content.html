Key Concepts

More Essential Terms to Remember

IP Addressing (IPv4/IPv6)

Internet Protocol versions for uniquely addressing devices on a network, crucial for traffic identification.

Subnetting

Dividing a larger network into smaller logical subnetworks, a critical security practice for segmentation and limiting attack blast radius.

Firewall & IDS/IPS

Firewalls control traffic; IDS detects threats passively; IPS actively prevents them, forming a layered defense strategy.

Summary Takeaway

Effective network security relies on understanding IP addressing for identification, subnetting for segmentation, and deploying firewalls, IDS, and IPS for comprehensive threat detection and prevention.
