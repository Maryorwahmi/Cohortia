Diagnose OSPF Adjacency Issues

Identify and resolve common problems preventing OSPF neighbors from forming a full adjacency, using `show` commands and `traceoptions`. This ensures stable routing within an OSPF domain.

02

Resolve OSPF Redistribution & Area Errors

Identify and fix issues with OSPF route redistribution and incorrect area configurations, ensuring proper route propagation. This prevents routing loops and unreachable networks.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Troubleshoot BGP Peering Issues

Identify and resolve problems preventing BGP sessions from reaching the Established state, including state transitions and authentication failures. This ensures inter-domain routing stability.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Analyze BGP Route Advertisement

Analyze BGP route advertisement and reception using `show` commands and policy debugging, ensuring routes are correctly exchanged. This prevents traffic blackholing or suboptimal routing.
