Understand Route Types (E1/E2)

Policy Inspection Commands

show policy-options policy-statement <policy-name>
show protocols ospf export

Inspect the policy's terms and actions, then confirm it's applied to OSPF for redistribution.

Key Takeaway

When redistributing routes into OSPF, verify the export policy applied to OSPF and confirm the routes exist in the routing table. Understand the impact of E1 versus E2 external route types on cost calculation.

Route Existence & Type

show route protocol <source-protocol>

Confirm routes exist in the routing table. OSPF defaults to E2 external routes; configure E1 explicitly if needed for cost incrementation.

Common Mistake

Forgetting to apply the export policy to the OSPF protocol, or not confirming the routes actually exist in the routing table before redistribution.
