Essential BGP Concepts

Critical Elements for BGP Stability

BGP Peering States

The sequence of states (Idle, Connect, Active, OpenSent, OpenConfirm, Established) a BGP speaker goes through to form a peer session.

AS Number

A unique identifier for an Autonomous System, used by BGP to identify routing domains and prevent loops.

BGP Import/Export Policies

Rules applied to BGP neighbors to control which routes are accepted (import) or advertised (export).

Summary Takeaway

Mastering BGP peering states, AS numbers, and the impact of import/export policies is crucial for effective BGP troubleshooting and ensuring robust inter-domain connectivity.
