Methodology Steps 1-3

Structured Troubleshooting: Defining and Gathering

Apply a systematic approach to diagnose complex network issues efficiently and effectively.

01

Define the Problem

Clearly articulate what is not working, who is affected, and when the issue started. Focus on symptoms and scope.

02

Gather Information

Collect relevant data using show commands, monitor commands, syslog, and traceoptions.

03

Analyze Information

Look for patterns, anomalies, and error messages in the collected data to identify potential causes.

Key Takeaway

A structured approach starts with precise problem definition, comprehensive data gathering, and careful analysis to build a clear understanding of the issue.
