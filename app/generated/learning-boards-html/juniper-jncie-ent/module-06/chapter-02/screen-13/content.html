Methodology Steps 4-7

Structured Troubleshooting: Hypothesize, Test, Verify, Document

Systematically resolve issues and learn from the process to improve future diagnostics.

04

Formulate Hypothesis

Based on analysis, propose a potential cause for the problem.

05

Test Hypothesis

Make a small, controlled change (using commit confirm) to see if it resolves the issue.

06

Verify Solution

Confirm the problem is resolved and no new issues have been introduced.

07

Document

Record the problem, solution, and lessons learned for future reference.

Key Takeaway

A systematic approach ensures thoughtful changes, safe deployments, and continuous learning from every troubleshooting scenario.
