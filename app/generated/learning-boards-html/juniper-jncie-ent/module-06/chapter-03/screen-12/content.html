JTI Best Practices and Considerations

Balancing data granularity with device resource utilization.

Compatible Collector Required

JTI streams data in a specific format (Protocol Buffers). An external collector capable of receiving, parsing, and storing this data is essential.

Resource Consumption Impact

Very high-frequency streaming or collecting too many resources can consume significant CPU and bandwidth on the Juniper device, especially older hardware.

Key Takeaway

Proper JTI deployment requires a compatible collector and careful management of streaming frequency and resources to avoid performance impacts.

Start Moderately, Adjust Gradually

Begin with moderate intervals and specific resources. Monitor device performance closely and adjust frequency or resource paths as needed.

Balance Granularity & Overhead

Always consider the trade-off between the level of detail you need and the operational overhead on your network devices. More data isn't always better.

Real-world Connection

In a data center, JTI can stream queue depth at sub-second intervals to detect microbursts, enabling proactive traffic shaping before congestion impacts applications.
