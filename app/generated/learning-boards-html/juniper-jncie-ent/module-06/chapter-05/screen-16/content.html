Module 6›Chapter 6.5›Key Concepts: Security Policy Troubleshooting

Firewall Filter (Stateless)

Rules applied to an interface to permit or deny traffic based on packet headers, without tracking connection state.

Security Policy (Stateful)

Rules applied between security zones to permit or deny traffic based on packet headers and connection state.

Term Order

The sequential processing of rules within a filter or policy, where the first match determines the action.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the difference between stateless and stateful policies, and the critical impact of term order, is fundamental to effective security troubleshooting.
