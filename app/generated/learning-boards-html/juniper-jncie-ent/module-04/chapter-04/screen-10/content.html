COMMUNITY Attribute

Tagging routes for flexible policy filtering and modification

Definition

An optional transitive attribute that allows BGP speakers to tag routes with values for policy filtering or modification by other BGP speakers.

Transitive Nature

Communities are passed along to other ASes, enabling policies to be enforced across multiple domains, offering scalable control.

Key Takeaway

COMMUNITY attributes provide a flexible, scalable way to implement complex BGP routing policies and traffic engineering.

Policy Filtering

Tag routes to identify specific customers or types of traffic, allowing other BGP speakers to apply filtering rules.

Example: `65000:100` (Customer A routes)
Traffic Engineering

Mark routes for specific actions, such as `no-export` (don't advertise outside local AS) or `no-advertise` (don't advertise to any peer).

Example: `no-export` community
