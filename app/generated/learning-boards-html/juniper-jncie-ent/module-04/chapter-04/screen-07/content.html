Key Attribute

ORIGIN Attribute

Understanding how a route entered BGP

Definition

Indicates how a route was injected into BGP, providing insight into its source and influencing its preference.

Preference Order

IGP (i) is most preferred, followed by EGP (e), and then Incomplete (?) as the least preferred. This order reflects confidence in the route's origin.

Key Takeaway

The ORIGIN attribute is a critical tie-breaker; ensure routes are injected with the correct origin for optimal path selection.

IGP (i) - Most Preferred

Route originated from an IGP within the local AS (e.g., `network` statement or redistribution from OSPF/EIGRP).

EGP (e) - Historical

Route learned from an EGP. Rarely seen in modern BGP deployments.

Incomplete (?) - Least Preferred

Route learned via other means, such as redistribution from a non-BGP source without an explicit origin (e.g., static routes).
