BGP Best Path Selection: Criteria 1-4

The initial and most influential decision points

Strict Sequential Order

The algorithm evaluates criteria one by one; the first criterion that differentiates paths determines the best path, and further criteria are ignored.

Traffic Engineering Impact

Understanding this order is crucial for effective traffic engineering, as it dictates which attributes are most effective for specific control.

Key Takeaway

LOCAL_PREF, AS_PATH, ORIGIN, and MED are the first four criteria, evaluated in strict order, with LOCAL_PREF being the most dominant for outbound traffic.

01Highest LOCAL_PREF

Prefer the path with the highest Local Preference. Primary for outbound traffic within an AS.

02Shortest AS_PATH

Prefer the path that has traversed the fewest Autonomous Systems.

03Lowest ORIGIN Type

Prefer IGP (i) over EGP (e), and EGP over Incomplete (?).

04Lowest MED

Prefer the path with the lowest Multi-Exit Discriminator. Influences inbound traffic from adjacent ASes.
