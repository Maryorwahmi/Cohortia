Key Concepts: Route Reflectors & Configuration

Consolidate your knowledge on scaling iBGP and essential commands.

Route Reflector (RR)

A designated iBGP router that reflects iBGP-learned routes to clients, overriding the split-horizon rule for scalability.

Route Reflector Client

An iBGP router that peers with a Route Reflector, receiving reflected routes and reducing the need for a full mesh.

next-hop-self

A BGP configuration option that forces the advertising iBGP router to change the next-hop attribute to its own IP, ensuring internal reachability.

Summary: Route Reflectors are key to scaling iBGP by centralizing route distribution. Proper configuration, including local-address and next-hop-self, is vital for functional and stable iBGP deployments.
