Mechanism

How Route Reflectors Work

Route Reflectors follow specific rules for reflecting iBGP-learned routes.

Route Reflector Reflection Rules

RRs modify the iBGP split-horizon rule based on these principles:

  • eBGP to All: Routes from an eBGP peer are reflected to all iBGP clients and non-clients.
  • Client to Others: Routes from an iBGP client are reflected to all other iBGP clients and non-clients.
  • Non-Client to Clients: Routes from an iBGP non-client are reflected only to iBGP clients.
  • No Self-Reflection: Routes learned from an iBGP client are NOT reflected back to the same client.
Key Takeaway

Route Reflectors selectively reflect routes based on their origin (eBGP, client, non-client) to ensure full route distribution without creating internal loops.
