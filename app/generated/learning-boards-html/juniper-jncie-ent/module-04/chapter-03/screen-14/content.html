Key Concepts: iBGP Fundamentals

Reinforce your understanding of core iBGP principles.

Internal BGP (iBGP)

BGP peering between routers within the same Autonomous System, crucial for distributing external routes internally.

Full Mesh

A topology where every iBGP router has a direct peering session with every other iBGP router in the same AS.

iBGP Split-Horizon Rule

A BGP loop prevention mechanism: an iBGP router will not advertise an iBGP-learned route to another iBGP peer.

Summary: iBGP is crucial for internal route distribution, but its full mesh requirement, driven by the split-horizon rule, presents significant scalability challenges in larger networks.
