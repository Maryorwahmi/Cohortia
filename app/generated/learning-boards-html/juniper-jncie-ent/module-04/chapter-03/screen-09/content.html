Introducing BGP Route Reflectors

Route Reflectors overcome full mesh limitations by overriding the split-horizon rule.

What is a Route Reflector (RR)?

A Route Reflector is a designated iBGP router that can override the iBGP split-horizon rule, allowing it to advertise iBGP-learned routes to other iBGP peers.

  • Overrides Split-Horizon: Breaks the rule that prevents iBGP-learned routes from being advertised to other iBGP peers.
  • Reduces Peering: Clients only need to peer with the RR, not with each other, drastically reducing the number of sessions.
  • Centralized Distribution: Acts as a central point for distributing BGP routes within an iBGP cluster.
Key Takeaway

Route Reflectors are a scalable alternative to iBGP full mesh, simplifying network design and reducing overhead by allowing a designated router to reflect routes to its clients.

Benefits of Route Reflectors

Simplified Topology

Replaces the complex full mesh with a hub-and-spoke-like model, making design and management easier.

Reduced Configuration

Fewer neighbor statements are needed on each router, minimizing configuration errors.

Lower Resource Usage

Less CPU and memory are consumed by maintaining fewer BGP sessions.
