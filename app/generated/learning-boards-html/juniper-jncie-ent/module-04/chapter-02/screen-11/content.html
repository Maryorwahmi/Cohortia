Why Use Policies?

Controlling Route Exchange

  • •Prevent accidental route leaks to external networks.
  • •Filter unwanted or incorrect routes from peers.
  • •Implement traffic engineering strategies.
  • •Enhance network security and stability.
Policy Types

Import vs. Export Policies

Export Policy

Controls routes advertised from your router to the neighbor.

Import Policy

Controls routes accepted from the neighbor into your router's routing table.

Key Takeaway

BGP policies are vital for granular control over route exchange, ensuring security and preventing routing disruptions.
