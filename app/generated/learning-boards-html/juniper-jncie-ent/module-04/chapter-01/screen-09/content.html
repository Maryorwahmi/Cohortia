BGP Peering

Internal BGP (iBGP) Peering

Ensuring consistent routing within your Autonomous System.

Definition

BGP peering between routers *within* the same Autonomous System.

Purpose

To ensure all BGP routers within an AS have a consistent view of the BGP routing table, propagating external routes internally.

Connectivity

iBGP neighbors do not need to be directly connected but require an underlying IGP for reachability.

Key Takeaway

iBGP is crucial for internal route distribution, ensuring all routers within an AS can reach external destinations learned via eBGP.
