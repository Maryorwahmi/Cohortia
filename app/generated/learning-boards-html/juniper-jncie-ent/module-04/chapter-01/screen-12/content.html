BGP vs. IGP: A Clear Distinction

Revisiting the core differences to avoid common pitfalls.

BGP's Focus

Inter-AS routing, policy enforcement, scalability, and loop prevention across independent domains.

IGP's Focus

Intra-AS routing, rapid convergence, and optimal path calculation within a single domain.

Why it Matters

Confusing them leads to incorrect network design and troubleshooting. They solve different problems.

Key Takeaway

Always remember: BGP connects the internet, while IGPs manage traffic within your network.
