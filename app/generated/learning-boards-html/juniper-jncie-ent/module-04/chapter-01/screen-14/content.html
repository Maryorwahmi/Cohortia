Key Concepts (Continued)

Deepening your understanding of BGP peering and policy

External BGP (eBGP)

BGP peering established between routers in *different* Autonomous Systems, typically for internet connectivity.

Internal BGP (iBGP)

BGP peering established between routers *within* the same Autonomous System, used to propagate external BGP routes internally.

Policy-Based Routing

The ability of BGP to influence routing decisions based on administrative policies rather than solely on path metrics.

Summary Takeaway

eBGP, iBGP, and policy-based routing are critical for managing external connectivity, internal route distribution, and traffic engineering on the internet.
