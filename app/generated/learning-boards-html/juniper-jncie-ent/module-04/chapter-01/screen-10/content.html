eBGP vs. iBGP: Key Differences and Interaction

Understanding how external and internal BGP work together.

Scope of Operation

eBGP: Between different ASes. iBGP: Within the same AS.

Connectivity Requirement

eBGP: Typically direct connection. iBGP: No direct connection needed, relies on IGP for reachability.

Route Advertisement Rule

iBGP-learned routes are generally not re-advertised to other eBGP peers to prevent loops.

Key Takeaway

eBGP and iBGP are complementary, providing external connectivity and internal route distribution, respectively, for a complete BGP solution.
