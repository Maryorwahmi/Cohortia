BGP Mechanism

BGP as a Path-Vector Protocol

How BGP builds routes and prevents loops across the internet.

Path-Vector Principle

BGP advertises routes with the full sequence of ASNs (AS_PATH) traversed, not just the next hop.

Loop Prevention

Routers discard routes if their own ASN is present in the AS_PATH, effectively preventing inter-AS routing loops.

Policy Enforcement

The AS_PATH provides rich information for network administrators to implement granular routing policies.

Key Takeaway

The AS_PATH attribute is central to BGP's ability to prevent loops and enable sophisticated policy-based routing decisions.
