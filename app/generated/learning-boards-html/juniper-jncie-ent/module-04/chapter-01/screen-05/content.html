Definition

Autonomous Systems (AS) and ASNs

The building blocks of the internet's routing architecture.

What is an AS?

A collection of IP networks and routers under a single administrative entity's control (e.g., ISP, large corporation).

What is an ASN?

A globally unique identifier assigned to each AS by regional internet registries, essential for BGP identification.

Role in BGP

ASNs allow BGP to distinguish between different networks on the internet, enabling inter-AS routing and policy enforcement.

Key Takeaway

ASes and their unique ASNs provide the hierarchical structure BGP needs to manage global internet routing effectively.
