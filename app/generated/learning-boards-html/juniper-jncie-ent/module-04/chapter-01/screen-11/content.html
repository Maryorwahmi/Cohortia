Policy-Driven Routing with BGP

Leveraging BGP attributes for granular traffic control.

Beyond Shortest Path

BGP prioritizes administrative policies over simple metric-based path selection, unlike IGPs.

Granular Control

Administrators can influence inbound and outbound traffic based on business rules, cost, or performance.

Key Attributes

Attributes like AS_PATH, Local Preference, and MED are used to implement and enforce routing policies.

Key Takeaway

BGP's policy-driven nature is its core strength, enabling network engineers to strategically manage global internet traffic flow.
