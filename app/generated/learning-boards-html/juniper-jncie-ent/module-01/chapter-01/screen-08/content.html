System Flow

How RE and PFE Work Together

From Routing Decisions to Packet Forwarding

  • •RE gathers network intelligence and builds the FIB.
  • •FIB is a concise set of forwarding instructions.
  • •RE pushes the FIB to the PFE.
  • •PFE uses the FIB for hardware-accelerated packet lookups and forwarding.
Key Takeaway

The RE and PFE collaborate by separating control and data functions, ensuring that complex routing decisions don't impede high-speed packet forwarding.
