Core Component

The Workhorse: Packet Forwarding Engine (PFE)

High-Speed Packet Forwarding with ASICs

  • •Operates as the data plane, dedicated to traffic forwarding.
  • •Composed of specialized ASICs or network processors.
  • •Performs line-rate packet processing for high throughput.
  • •Uses the FIB from the RE to make forwarding decisions.
Key Takeaway

The PFE is the data plane, optimized with specialized hardware to forward packets at maximum speed, ensuring efficient network traffic flow.

High-Speed Forwarding

Moves traffic at line rate with minimal latency.

Specialized ASICs

Hardware optimized for rapid packet processing.

FIB Utilization

Relies on the Forwarding Information Base for decisions.

Independent Operation

Can continue forwarding even if the RE is busy or experiences issues.
