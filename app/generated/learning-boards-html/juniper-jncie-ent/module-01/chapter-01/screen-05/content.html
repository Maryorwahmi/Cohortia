Architectural Principle

The Power of Separation

Control Plane and Data Plane for Resilience

  • •Ensures network stability by isolating management and routing functions.
  • •Maintains high-speed packet forwarding even during control plane issues.
  • •Critical for achieving high network uptime and performance.
Key Takeaway

Separating control and data planes is fundamental to Junos OS's resilience, ensuring continuous traffic flow even if the control plane experiences issues.

Control Plane

Handles routing protocols, management tasks (CLI, SNMP, SSH), and system processes. It's the 'brain' that makes decisions.

Data Plane

Responsible for high-speed forwarding of traffic based on decisions from the control plane. It's the 'muscle' that moves packets.
