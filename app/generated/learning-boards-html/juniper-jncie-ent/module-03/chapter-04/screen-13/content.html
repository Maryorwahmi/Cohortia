Common Summarization Mistakes & Verification

Ensuring Correct Route Aggregation and Reachability

Potential Issues with Summarization

  • •Incorrect Summary Address: Includes networks outside the area, leading to blackholing.
  • •Excluding Legitimate Networks: Makes internal networks unreachable from other areas.
  • •Blackholing Specific Routes: If a specific route within the summary goes down, traffic might still be sent to the ABR.
Key Takeaway

Always meticulously calculate summary ranges and thoroughly verify OSPF routing tables after implementing summarization to prevent blackholing or unreachable networks.
