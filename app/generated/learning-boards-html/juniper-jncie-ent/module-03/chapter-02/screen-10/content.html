Summary LSAs (Type 3)

Propagating Inter-Area Reachability

Type 3 Summary LSA Characteristics

  • •Generated by: Area Border Routers (ABRs).
  • •Describes: Routes to networks in other OSPF areas.
  • •Purpose: Summarizes Type 1/2 LSAs from one area for other areas.
  • •Flooding Scope: Flooded between areas (e.g., from Area 1 to Area 0).
Key Takeaway

Type 3 LSAs are vital for inter-area routing, allowing ABRs to efficiently share summarized network reachability information across OSPF areas, reducing LSDB size.
