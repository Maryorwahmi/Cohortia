Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

OSPF Network Types

Classifications (Broadcast, NBMA, P2P, P2MP) dictating OSPF behavior and adjacency formation.

Designated Router (DR)

Elected on multi-access segments to reduce adjacencies and manage LSA flooding.

Backup Designated Router (BDR)

Elected on multi-access segments to take over the DR role if the primary DR fails, providing redundancy.

Summary Takeaway

OSPF network types and the DR/BDR election mechanism are fundamental for efficient and stable OSPF routing, especially in multi-access environments.
