Key Concepts

Reinforcing Core OSPF Principles

DRother

Any router on a multi-access segment that is neither the DR nor the BDR, forming adjacencies only with them.

OSPF Priority

A configurable value (0-255) on an OSPF interface used to influence DR/BDR election; higher is preferred.

Non-Preemptive Election

DR/BDR roles are stable; a new router with higher priority won't take over unless the current DR/BDR fails.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding DRothers, OSPF priority, and the non-preemptive nature of election is crucial for effective OSPF design and troubleshooting.
