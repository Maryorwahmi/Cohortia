Why DR/BDR Election Matters

Scaling OSPF on Multi-Access Networks

Without DR/BDR: N-Squared Problem
With DR/BDR: Optimized Adjacencies
DR Role

Central point of contact, generates Type 2 Network LSAs, ensures synchronization.

BDR Role

Monitors DR, takes over if DR fails, provides redundancy.

DRothers

Form full adjacencies only with DR and BDR, not with each other.

Key Takeaway

DR/BDR election is a critical optimization for multi-access networks, reducing LSA flooding and CPU load by centralizing adjacency management.
