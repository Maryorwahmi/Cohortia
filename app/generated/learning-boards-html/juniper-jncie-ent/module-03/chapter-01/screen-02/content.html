01

Explain Link-State Principles

Articulate the core principles of OSPF as a link-state routing protocol, including its use of a topology database and Dijkstra's algorithm. This skill is vital for understanding how OSPF achieves rapid convergence and loop-free routing in complex networks.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Identify OSPF Packet Types

Identify and describe the purpose of the five OSPF packet types: Hello, DBD, LSR, LSU, and LSAck. This knowledge is fundamental for troubleshooting OSPF neighbor issues and understanding how routing information is exchanged.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
