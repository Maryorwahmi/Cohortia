Module 3›Chapter 3.1›Hello Packets
Key Functions
  • Neighbor Discovery: Announce router presence.
  • Adjacency Maintenance: Ensure neighbors are still active.
  • DR/BDR Election: On multi-access networks, elect a Designated Router and Backup Designated Router.
Hello Packet Contents

Includes Router ID, Area ID, Network Mask, Hello/Dead Intervals, Router Priority, DR/BDR IP addresses, and a list of 'Neighbors Seen'.

Key Takeaway

Hello packets are the foundation of OSPF neighbor relationships, ensuring discovery, ongoing health, and efficient LSA flooding on shared segments.
