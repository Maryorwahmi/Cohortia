Key Concepts: Adjacency & Configuration

Reinforcing OSPF neighbor states and practical configuration.

OSPF States

A sequence of states (Down to Full) that routers progress through to form a full neighbor adjacency.

Designated Router (DR) & BDR

Elected on multi-access networks to reduce adjacencies and LSA flooding, simplifying topology.

Troubleshooting

Identifying and resolving common issues like mismatched timers, area IDs, or IP addresses preventing adjacency.

Summary Takeaway

A solid grasp of OSPF neighbor states, DR/BDR roles, and troubleshooting techniques is essential for effective OSPF network management and stability.
