Synergistic Approach

Stateful Policies & Stateless Policing

Junos allows you to combine the robust security of stateful policies with the granular traffic management of stateless filters and policers for comprehensive network control.

Stateful Security: Zone-based policies for connection tracking and robust protection.
Stateless Policing: Granular rate-limiting for specific traffic flows or subnets.
Key Takeaway

A layered approach using both stateful security policies and stateless filters with policing provides comprehensive control over network security, performance, and resource utilization.

Benefits

Enhanced Network Management

Robust Security

Protection against various attacks and control over inter-zone traffic.

Optimized Performance

Preventing bandwidth saturation and ensuring critical traffic priority.

Simplified Management

Clear separation of security and performance concerns.
