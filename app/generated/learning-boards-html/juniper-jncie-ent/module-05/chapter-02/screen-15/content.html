Practical Applications

When to Deploy Traffic Policing

Traffic policing is a versatile tool for various network management and security scenarios, offering precise control over bandwidth usage.

Guest Bandwidth Management: Limit guest Wi-Fi traffic to protect core network resources.
DoS Attack Mitigation: Rate-limit suspicious traffic flows to prevent service disruption.
SLA Enforcement: Ensure specific traffic types adhere to agreed-upon bandwidth limits.
Key Takeaway

Policing is a flexible tool for managing network resources, protecting against attacks, and enforcing service agreements by precisely controlling bandwidth for various traffic types.

Targeted Control

Granular Traffic Management

Policers can be applied based on various match conditions within a firewall filter, allowing for highly specific control over traffic flows.

Source Address

Limit traffic originating from specific subnets or hosts.

Destination Address

Control traffic destined for particular servers or networks.

Application Type

Manage bandwidth for specific applications (e.g., HTTP, FTP, custom apps).
