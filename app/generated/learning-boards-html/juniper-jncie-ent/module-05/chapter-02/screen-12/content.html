Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Policing vs. Shaping
Policing

Hard Limit Enforcement

Policing strictly enforces bandwidth limits by immediately discarding packets that exceed the configured rate. This is a reactive measure to control traffic flow.

Packet Discard: Excess packets are dropped.
Retransmissions: Can lead to higher retransmission rates and perceived latency.
Key Takeaway

Policing is a strict bandwidth enforcement method that discards excess traffic, suitable when immediate rate limiting is paramount, even at the cost of potential packet loss.

Traffic Shaping

Smoothing Traffic Bursts

Traffic shaping buffers and delays excess traffic to smooth out bursts, ensuring that the traffic conforms to a desired rate without discarding packets.

Packet Buffering: Excess packets are temporarily held.
Increased Latency: Buffering can introduce delays, but avoids packet loss.
When to Use Which

Choosing the Right Mechanism

Use policing for strict bandwidth enforcement where some packet loss is acceptable. Opt for shaping when maintaining quality of service and minimizing packet loss are priorities, even with increased latency.
