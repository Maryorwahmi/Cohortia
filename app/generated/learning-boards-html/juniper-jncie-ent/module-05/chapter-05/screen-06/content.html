Inter-Zone Policy Scenarios

Practical Examples of Traffic Control

Common Zone Interactions

Internal to Untrust

Permit all outbound user traffic (HTTP, HTTPS, DNS).

Untrust to DMZ

Permit HTTP/HTTPS to specific web servers in the DMZ.

DMZ to Internal

Deny all traffic by default (unless specific services are required).

Management Access

Internal to DMZ

Permit management access (e.g., SSH, SNMP) to DMZ servers.

Key Takeaway

Careful planning of inter-zone policies is essential to balance security and necessary network functionality, promoting a least-privilege approach.
