Essential Routing Policy Concepts

Reinforcing core definitions

Routing Policy

A set of rules controlling the advertisement, acceptance, and modification of routing information.

Policy Statement

The top-level container for a routing policy, holding one or more terms.

Term

A logical block within a policy statement with `from` (match conditions) and `then` (actions) statements.

Chapter Summary

Junos routing policies are critical for network control, built from policy statements containing ordered terms with match conditions and actions. They enable precise route filtering and attribute manipulation.
