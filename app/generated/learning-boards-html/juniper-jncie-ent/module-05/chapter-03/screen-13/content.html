Key Policy Components and Directions

Understanding match conditions, actions, and application

Match Conditions (`from`)

Criteria to identify routes, such as source protocol, route prefix, or BGP attributes.

Actions (`then`)

What happens to a matched route, e.g., `accept`, `reject`, or modify attributes.

Policy Direction

`Import` policies affect routes entering the RIB; `Export` policies affect routes leaving the RIB for advertisement.

Chapter Summary

Mastering routing policies involves understanding how `from` conditions identify routes, `then` actions dictate their fate, and applying policies correctly in either the `import` or `export` direction.
