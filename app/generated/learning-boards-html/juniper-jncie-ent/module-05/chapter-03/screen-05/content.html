Policy Anatomy

Building Blocks: Policy Statements and Terms

Defining `policy-statement` and `term` components

Policy Statement

The top-level container for a routing policy, identified by a unique name. It encapsulates all policy logic.

Term Definition

A logical block within a policy statement, containing specific match conditions (`from`) and actions (`then`).

Sequential Evaluation

Terms are processed in order. The first matching term's actions are executed, and processing for that route stops.

Pro Tip

Always plan your term order carefully. A broad 'accept' term placed too early can inadvertently permit unwanted routes.
