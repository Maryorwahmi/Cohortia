Policy Outcomes

Executing Actions with `then` Statements

Accept, reject, and attribute modification

Accept Action

Permits the matched route to be processed further (e.g., installed in RIB, advertised to peers).

Reject Action

Discards the matched route, preventing it from being processed or advertised.

Attribute Modification

Modify route attributes like `metric`, `local-preference`, `community`, or `as-path-prepend` to influence path selection.

Key Takeaway

Always explicitly define a final `then accept` or `then reject` to avoid relying on potentially unexpected default behaviors.

Default Policy Actions

Export Policy

Default: Accept

Routes are advertised unless rejected.

Import Policy

Default: Reject

Routes are discarded unless accepted.
