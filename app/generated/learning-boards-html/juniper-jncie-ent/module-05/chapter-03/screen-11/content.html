Avoiding Policy Pitfalls and Ensuring Safety

Understanding default behaviors and testing changes

Misinterpreting Defaults

Remember: `export` policies default to `accept`, `import` policies default to `reject`. Always be explicit.

Wrong Direction

Applying an `import` policy as `export` (or vice-versa) will lead to unexpected routing behavior.

Safety First

Always test changes in a lab environment and have a rollback plan. Incorrect policies can cause black holes or loops.

Verification Commands

Use `show route advertising-protocol bgp ` and `show route receiving-protocol bgp ` to verify policy effects.
