Beyond Accept and Discard

Exploring additional actions for sophisticated traffic management and troubleshooting.

Reject

Drops the packet and sends an ICMP unreachable message back to the source, informing them of the block.

Log

Records the packet header information to the system log, useful for auditing and debugging.

Count

Increments a counter for matched packets, providing statistics without affecting traffic flow.

Policer

Rate-limits traffic that matches the term, controlling bandwidth usage (covered in a later chapter).

Why Use Other Actions?

These actions provide valuable tools for troubleshooting, auditing, and fine-tuning traffic management beyond simple blocking or permitting.

Key Takeaway

Beyond 'accept' and 'discard', actions like 'reject', 'log', and 'count' offer enhanced control and visibility for robust network security and troubleshooting.
