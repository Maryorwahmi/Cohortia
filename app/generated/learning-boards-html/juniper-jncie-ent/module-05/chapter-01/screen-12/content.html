Safe Deployment and Verification

Strategies for testing filters and monitoring their impact in production environments.

1. Lab Test Thoroughly

Always test new filters in a non-production lab environment to predict behavior.

2. Start Permissive

Begin with a more permissive filter and gradually tighten rules, or use 'count' action first.

3. Use 'Count' Action

Employ the count action to monitor matched traffic without immediately dropping it.

4. Verify with 'Show'

Use show firewall filter <filter-name> to check term hit counts and verify impact.

Why It Matters

Misconfigured filters can lead to critical network outages. These practices minimize risk and ensure stability.

Key Takeaway

Always test filters in a lab, deploy cautiously with 'count' actions, and verify behavior with 'show firewall' commands to ensure network stability.
