Avoiding Common Configuration Pitfalls

Understanding term order and the implicit deny rule to prevent unintended network behavior.

Mistake 1: Incorrect Term Order

Problematic Order

  • Term 1: then accept (broad)
  • Term 2: then discard (specific)

Specific traffic is NOT blocked.

Correct Order

  • Term 1: then discard (specific)
  • Term 2: then accept (broad)

Specific traffic IS blocked.

Mistake 2: Forgetting Implicit Deny

Missing Final Term

  • Only then discard terms.
  • No general then accept.

All other traffic is dropped.

Best Practice

  • Specific discard terms.
  • Final then accept or reject.

Desired traffic is permitted.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize specific 'deny' terms over general 'accept' terms, and explicitly handle all non-matching traffic with a final 'accept' or 'reject' term to avoid implicit denies.
