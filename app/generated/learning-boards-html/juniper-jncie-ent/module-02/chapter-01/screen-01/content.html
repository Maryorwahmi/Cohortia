Mastering VLANs and Trunking

Segmenting Networks for Enhanced Security and Performance

In this chapter, you will learn how Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs) and trunking are fundamental to modern enterprise networking. We'll explore how to logically segment your network, configure Juniper EX Series switches, and enable secure, efficient communication across different network segments. This knowledge is crucial for building scalable and resilient network infrastructures.

Key Idea

VLANs logically divide a physical network into multiple broadcast domains, enhancing security and performance by isolating traffic and reducing broadcast overhead.
