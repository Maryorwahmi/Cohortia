CORE CONCEPT

The Problem: Switching Loops

Redundancy's Hidden Danger in Layer 2 Networks

What is a Switching Loop?

A switching loop occurs when there are redundant physical paths between switches in an Ethernet network, causing broadcast frames to circulate indefinitely.

  • •Redundant paths are essential for fault tolerance but create potential loops.
  • •Broadcast frames, like ARP requests, are forwarded out all ports except the ingress port.
  • •Without control, these frames multiply and circulate endlessly.
Common Mistake

Assuming redundancy alone is sufficient. Without STP, redundant links are a liability, not an asset, at Layer 2.

Impact of a Broadcast Storm

Network Congestion

Infinite circulation of frames consumes all available bandwidth.

CPU Overload

Switches spend all resources processing duplicate frames.

Network Meltdown

Complete loss of connectivity and service disruption.

Key Takeaway

Switching loops lead to broadcast storms, consuming bandwidth and CPU, causing network collapse. STP protocols are vital to prevent this.
