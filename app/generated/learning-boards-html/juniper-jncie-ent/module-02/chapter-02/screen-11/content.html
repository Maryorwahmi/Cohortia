MSTP STRUCTURE

MSTP Architecture: Regions and Instances

Building Flexible Spanning Tree Topologies (IEEE 802.1s)

MST Regions: The Common Domain

  • •A group of switches sharing the same MST configuration.
  • •Must have identical region name, revision number, and VLAN-to-instance mappings.
  • •Allows switches to interoperate and form a single MST domain.
Analogy

Think of an MST Region as a country, and MST Instances as different states within that country, each with its own internal rules but adhering to national laws.

MST Instances (MSTIs) and CIST

MST Instances (MSTIs)

Independent spanning trees within a region, each managing a subset of VLANs for load balancing.

VLAN-to-Instance Mapping

Assign specific VLANs to an MSTI to control their forwarding paths.

CIST (MSTI 0)

Common and Internal Spanning Tree; handles unmapped VLANs and inter-region connectivity.

Key Takeaway

MSTP uses MST Regions for common configuration, MST Instances (MSTIs) for per-VLAN spanning trees, and the CIST (MSTI 0) for unmapped VLANs and inter-region connectivity.
