Dynamic ARP Inspection (DAI): Mitigating ARP Spoofing

Validating ARP Packets for Network Integrity

What is DAI?

A security feature that inspects ARP packets and validates them against a trusted IP-to-MAC address binding database to prevent ARP spoofing attacks.

ARP Spoofing (ARP Poisoning)

An attacker sends forged ARP replies, associating their MAC address with another device's IP (e.g., default gateway). This redirects traffic to the attacker, enabling man-in-the-middle attacks.

Key Takeaway

DAI is crucial for preventing traffic interception and ensuring devices communicate with their intended targets by validating ARP information.

Man-in-the-Middle Attack

By spoofing ARP, an attacker positions themselves between two communicating devices, intercepting, modifying, or dropping their traffic without detection.

How DAI Works

  • Inspects incoming ARP packets.
  • Compares source IP and MAC against a trusted binding database.
  • Drops packets that do not match valid entries.

Analogy: Verified Mail Delivery

DAI is like a system that verifies every piece of mail (ARP packet) to ensure the sender's address (MAC) truly belongs to the stated origin (IP), preventing fraudulent redirection.
