Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Safety & Best Practices
Safety Checklist
Start Small
Limit percentage or target non-critical instances first.
Stop Conditions
Use CloudWatch alarms for automated halting of experiments.
Communication
Notify teams and have on-call responders ready.
  • • Monitor closely with CloudWatch and traces to capture behavior during experiments.
  • • Keep a tested rollback plan and confirm that auto-scaling policies behave as expected.
  • • Grant FIS only the privileges it needs via an IAM role.
Common Mistake
Running experiments without conservative stop conditions or monitoring increases outage risk.
Key Takeaway

Design safety into experiments with limited blast radius and automated stop conditions before running them.
