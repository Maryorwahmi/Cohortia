Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Synthetics & RUM
Definition
Synthetics and RUM provide proactive and real-user monitoring
  • Synthetics: scripted canaries that validate endpoints and user flows on a schedule.
  • RUM: collects client-side metrics from real user sessions to measure actual experience.
  • Use both to detect and quantify customer impact during migrations or incidents.
Scenario
Create a canary that exercises login and checkout. Enable RUM for the web app to capture page load and script errors from real browsers.
When to use
Use synthetics to validate availability and RUM to measure user-perceived performance. Together they give a fuller observability picture.
Key takeaway
Simulate critical journeys and capture real-user signals to detect and quantify impact early in the lifecycle.
Common pitfall
Don't over-script canaries; script the minimal customer-impact steps so false positives are meaningful and actionable.
