Practical best practices
Design metrics for action
Ask: will this metric trigger a measurable action? If not, reconsider publishing it.
Manage cardinality
High-cardinality dimensions increase cost and complexity; roll up or sample when possible.
Prefer derived signals
Use metric math and composite alarms to reduce noisy, low-value alerts.
Test anomaly models
Train on representative data and validate false-positive rates before relying on alerts.
Operational checklist
  • Confirm metric unit and aggregation consistency.
  • Limit alarms to actionable conditions and test notification paths.
  • Design dashboards that map to runbook steps for responders.
Key takeaway
Operational discipline — clear metrics, sensible alarms, and focused dashboards — makes observability scalable and actionable.
