Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Signal Comparison
Match signals to questions
Metrics
Best for trends, alerting, and SLAs.
Logs
Best for context, stack traces, and detailed forensics.
Synthetics
Best for scripted end-to-end checks and availability.
RUM
Best for real-user performance and client-side errors.
Guideline
Use a layered approach: metrics for detection, logs for diagnosis, synthetics for proactive checks, and RUM for user-impact measurement.
Misconception check
Don't expect a single signal to reveal everything. Composite approaches yield faster, more accurate troubleshooting.
