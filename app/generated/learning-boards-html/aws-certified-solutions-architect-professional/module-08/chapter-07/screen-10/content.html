Compare
Tool matrix
CloudFormation: AWS-native, declarative, deep AWS integration.
CDK: Code-first, synthesizes to CloudFormation, reusable constructs.
Terraform: Multi-cloud, unified state, provider ecosystem.
Practical contrasts
Best for AWS-only
CloudFormation or CDK when you want native service coverage.
Best for multi-cloud
Terraform for consistent provisioning across providers.
Team fit
Choose CDK for developer-friendly languages; choose Terraform for operational portability.
Key Takeaway

There is no one-size-fits-all: pick the tool that aligns with your cloud footprint and team skills while enforcing testing and state management.
