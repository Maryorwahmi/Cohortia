Practice questions
Use these scenarios to test your design choices and to create a short lab checklist.
Question 1: Which service routes AWS Config events to a remediation Lambda? Answer: EventBridge. Consider rule matching and tagging.
Question 2: Which service buffers high volumes of operational events to avoid downstream overload? Answer: SQS. Configure visibility timeout and DLQ.
Review prompt
Map one operational workflow in your environment to the pattern mix you would use and articulate why.
Key Takeaway

Good exam-style assessments ask for the pattern and the reason: routing, buffering, notification, or automation.
