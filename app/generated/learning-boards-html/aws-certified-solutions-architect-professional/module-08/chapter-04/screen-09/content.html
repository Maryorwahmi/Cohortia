Fan-out, Buffering, Scheduling
These patterns address notification, durability, and timing respectively and are often combined for production-grade automations.
Fan-out
Use SNS or EventBridge to broadcast an event to multiple subscribers for simultaneous notification or actions.
Buffering
Insert SQS to absorb bursts and give consumers time to process messages reliably with retries and DLQs.
Scheduling
Use EventBridge scheduled rules to trigger maintenance tasks or periodic checks without external cron jobs.
Scenario
A scheduled rule publishes maintenance tasks to SQS. Lambda consumers process the queue at their own rate. An SNS notification is sent to stakeholders summarizing the run. This provides timing control, durability, and visibility.
