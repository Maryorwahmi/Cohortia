Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › SNS vs SQS
Amazon SNS
Publish/subscribe for notifications and simple fan-out to multiple subscribers including email, SMS, Lambda, and SQS.
When to use SNS:
  • Immediate notifications to teams or systems.
  • Fan-out to multiple independent consumers.
  • Low-latency broadcast of operational alerts.
Analogy
A megaphone that broadcasts a single message to many listeners.
Amazon SQS
Durable queue for buffering, retry control, and decoupling producers from consumers with visibility timeouts and DLQs.
When to use SQS:
  • Smoothing bursts to prevent consumer overload.
  • Guaranteed-at-least-once delivery with redrive to DLQ.
  • Processing workflows where order and retry behavior matter.
Analogy
A workbench where tasks wait until a worker can process them.
Pattern: combine both
Use EventBridge to route to SNS for immediate alerts and to SQS for durable background processing. This separates urgent notifications from work that can be processed with retries and backpressure.
