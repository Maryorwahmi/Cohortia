Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Dead-Letter Queues
What is a DLQ?
A queue or destination that receives messages which could not be processed after configured retries, to enable inspection and reprocessing.
Operational practices:
  • Configure redrive policies and retry counts thoughtfully to balance transient failures vs poison messages.
  • Alert on DLQ growth so teams can respond quickly.
  • Provide tooling or scripts to safely reprocess or discard DLQ messages after investigation.
Example
An SQS queue with a redrive policy moves messages to a DLQ after five failed receives. Operators inspect the DLQ to find malformed payloads or permission issues.
Key Takeaway

Always route unprocessed messages to a DLQ and monitor it. DLQs turn silent failures into actionable telemetry.
