Sampling strategies
  • Baseline sampling for general traffic to limit data volume.
  • Increased sampling for high-risk or critical routes.
  • Conditional sampling on error rates or latency thresholds.
  • Manual subsegments for critical internal operations to ensure visibility.
Common mistakes & checks
Failing to enable tracing across all relevant services creates gaps.
Not checking daemon or Lambda IAM permissions can prevent trace submission.
Overlooking firewall or network rules blocking daemon egress produces missing traces.
Key Takeaway

Instrument intentionally and sample thoughtfully so traces remain actionable and cost-effective.
