Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › What is Operational Excellence?
Definition

Operational Excellence

Running and monitoring systems to deliver business value, with processes that are continuously improved. This includes automation, testing, runbooks, and learning from incidents.

Why it matters
Reduces downtime, supports predictable migrations, and decreases mean time to recovery.
Common misconception
Monitoring alone is not enough; you must also automate, version, and refine procedures to make monitoring actionable.
Key Takeaway

Treat both infrastructure and operational practices as repeatable, testable artifacts and evolve them after incidents.

Concrete Example
Deploying a multi-tier environment from a template
Use a CloudFormation template to provision web, app, and database tiers identically across regions. This reduces configuration drift and simplifies troubleshooting after migration.
Process
Automate provisioning, test templates, and store them in version control.
Pitfall
Skipping tests before applying template updates can introduce hidden failures.
