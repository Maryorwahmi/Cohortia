Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Anticipating Failure
Architectural Patterns
Multi-AZ deployments
Distribute tiers across Availability Zones to tolerate AZ failures.
Auto-scaling and health checks
Allow capacity to scale automatically and recover unhealthy instances.
Fault-tolerant services
Use managed databases and services designed for high availability.
Exercises & Validation
Game days and chaos tests
Intentionally inject failures, such as a simulated AZ outage or forced instance termination, to validate how autoscaling and load balancing react and how the team executes runbooks.
Common pitfall
Assuming redundancy without testing can leave hidden single points of failure.
Key takeaway
Design, then validate under failure conditions to ensure resilience actually works.
