Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Learning Objectives
01
Understand the Operational Excellence pillar

Explain how running and monitoring systems delivers business value and enables continuous process improvement. This skill helps you align architecture decisions to measurable operational outcomes.

Verified Competency Milestone
02
Identify operations as code

Recognize how declarative templates, version control, and automated provisioning reduce manual errors and speed migrations. Practically, you can review CloudFormation or Terraform templates during design reviews.

Verified Competency Milestone
03
Explain small, reversible changes

Describe why frequent, incremental deployments lower risk and simplify rollbacks. Use a CI/CD pipeline to demonstrate how a single microservice update is safer than a full-stack release.

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Anticipate failures and design resilience

Articulate strategies such as multi-AZ availability, auto-scaling, and fault-tolerant databases to reduce outage impact and enable graceful recovery.

Verified Competency Milestone
