Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Refine operations procedures
Practice
  1. Create a minimal runbook for a common operational task.
  2. Run a drill or simulate the task to validate steps.
  3. Update the runbook and automate portions that are manual and error-prone.
  4. Store runbooks with templates and version them for traceability.
Example
After a migration the team found a manual certificate rotation step. They automated certificate renewal in a script, updated the runbook, and reduced rotation incidents to near zero.
Supporting Cards
Runbook checklist
Objective, prerequisites, steps, verification, rollback, contacts.
Drill cadence
Schedule game days or simulated incidents quarterly to validate runbooks and automation.
Key takeaway
Frequent refinement turns operating procedures into reliable, testable artifacts.
