Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Configuration Drift
Causes
  • • Manual hotfixes applied in production and not recorded.
  • • Emergency changes bypassing CI/CD.
  • • Environment-specific configuration added ad-hoc.
Pitfall
Drift hides root causes and increases troubleshooting time; do not accept it as normal.
Remediation
Detect drift
Use built-in drift detection or scripts to compare deployed resources against templates.
Reconcile
Redeploy known-good templates or apply documented fixes and then update the template.
Record and automate
Encode any legitimate manual change into templates and CI to prevent future drift.
