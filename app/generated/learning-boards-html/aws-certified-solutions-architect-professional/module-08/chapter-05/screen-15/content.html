Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › What to Remember
Test first
Always validate scripts and documents on staging instances before broad execution.
Stage patching
Use baselines, groups, and phased rollouts to minimize operational impact.
Attach runbooks
Link Automation documents or Run Command steps to OpsItems for fast, consistent remediation.
Final summary
Treat automation as code, validate agents and permissions, and use SSM capabilities together to achieve consistent, auditable operations at scale.
