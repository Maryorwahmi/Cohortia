Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Explorer & OpsCenter
Explorer: aggregated view
Explorer aggregates inventory, patch compliance, and other operational indicators across accounts and regions so teams can spot trends and prioritize remediation.
Use case
Spot that a migration cohort is missing a security agent by reviewing aggregated inventory and compliance metrics.
OpsCenter: manage OpsItems
OpsCenter collects issues into OpsItems and can attach runbooks or Automation documents so responders have steps to investigate and remediate.
Operational flow
Alert triggers OpsItem → OpsItem contains context and runbook → responder executes runbook → OpsItem resolved and documented.
Key Takeaway

Explorer tells you where the problems are. OpsCenter helps you manage what to do about them with attached runbooks for consistent remediation.
