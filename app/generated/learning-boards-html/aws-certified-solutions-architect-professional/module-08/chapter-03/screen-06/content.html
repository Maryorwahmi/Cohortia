Architecture
Kinesis Firehose: Ingestion and Delivery
Managed streaming delivery that buffers, compresses, and routes logs to destinations like S3 and OpenSearch.
Buffering
Tune buffer size and interval to balance latency and cost. Larger buffers mean better compression, longer wait times.
Compression & encryption
Enable GZIP or Snappy for S3 to reduce storage costs and enable KMS if you need encrypted-at-rest delivery.
Multi-destination delivery
Deliver the same stream to S3 for archive and OpenSearch for real-time search, keeping both recent and historical needs satisfied.
Operational checklist
  • Verify the Firehose IAM role has s3:PutObject and OpenSearch permissions.
  • Set S3 prefixes and error prefix for failed records.
  • Consider Lambda transform for lightweight parsing before delivery.
Example
Use Fluentd on EC2 to PUT to a Firehose named MyAppLogsToS3 with GZIP compression and S3 prefix raw_logs/.
Key Takeaway

Firehose simplifies delivery and scaling; tune buffers and compression for your latency and cost targets.
