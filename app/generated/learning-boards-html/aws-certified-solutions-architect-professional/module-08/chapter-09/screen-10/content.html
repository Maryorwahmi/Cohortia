Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Post-Mortem Analysis
Definition

Blameless Post-Mortem

A structured review focusing on systemic causes, lessons learned, and SMART corrective actions rather than individual fault.
Post-mortem steps
  • Timeline reconstruction
  • Impact assessment
  • Root cause analysis
  • Lessons learned
  • SMART action items
Example template snippet
Timeline: 12:02 error spike; 12:05 alert; 12:08 remediation started; 12:15 service restored.
Action item: Add an autoscaling policy for service-A. Owner: DevOps lead. Due: 14 days.
Follow-up is essential: track and verify action items to ensure the post-mortem leads to real improvement.
