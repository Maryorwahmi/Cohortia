Incident Management and Post-Mortem Analysis

Practical practices and AWS tools to detect, respond, and learn from incidents in multi-tier applications.

We focus on systems and processes that restore service quickly and then prevent recurrence. This includes detection with CloudWatch, alerting through AWS Chatbot, automated orchestration with Systems Manager Incident Manager, and blameless post-mortems to capture lessons and action items.

Real improvements come from combining tools with process: notification routing, escalation plans, runbooks, and a culture of psychological safety for post-incident learning.
