Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Objectives — Skills
Measureable Outcomes
  • Alert delivered to Slack channel within one minute of alarm.
  • Runbook executes minimal remediation steps automatically.
  • Post-mortem produces at least two SMART action items with owners and deadlines.
Why it matters

Without measurable outcomes, incidents repeat. Documented runbooks, escalation plans, and action items turn reactive work into lasting improvements.
