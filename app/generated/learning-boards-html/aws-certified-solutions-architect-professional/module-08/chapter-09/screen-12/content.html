Timeline

Close the loop

Assign owners and timelines for action items and verify completion as part of the post-mortem sign-off. Track metrics to show progress.
SMART Example
Add CloudWatch alarm for service-B error-rate >= 5% for 5 minutes. Owner: Platform Team. Due: 7 days.
Prioritize a small set of verifiable items so change actually happens and you can measure reduced recurrence or MTTR improvements.
