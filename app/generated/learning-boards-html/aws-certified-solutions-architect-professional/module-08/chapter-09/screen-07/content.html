Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Triage & Escalation
Process

Rapid assessment and response

  1. Confirm alert validity and collect immediate logs/metrics.
  2. Estimate business impact: affected users, revenue, SLAs.
  3. Assign severity and follow the escalation plan.
  4. Attach runbooks or automated diagnostics to the incident.
Escalation Plan Card
Severity P1: Notify on-call via SMS and Slack, engage team lead after 5 minutes, and page cross-functional responders after 15 minutes.
Severity P2: Notify on-call via Slack; escalate to on-call shadow after 20 minutes if unresolved.
Common misconception: Escalation is about alert volume. In truth, it’s about ensuring the correct domain experts engage within predictable timeframes.
