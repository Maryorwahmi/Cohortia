Core Concept

Incident Management

A structured process to restore service and reduce impact, then learn to prevent recurrence.

Key Components
  • Detection: monitoring and tracing.
  • Alerting: notify the right people or channel.
  • Triage & Escalation: assess severity and responders.
  • Diagnosis & Remediation: runbooks and fixes.
  • Communication & Post-mortem: impact and learning.
Key Takeaway

Design incident processes that restore service quickly and produce actionable learning to prevent repeats.

Lifecycle Example — High CPU Alarm
Detect
CloudWatch raises a CPU Utilization alarm on an EC2 instance.
Alert
SNS publishes to Chatbot; Slack channel receives alert.
Triage
On-call confirms severity and scope.
Remediate
Runbook restarts the service or scales the pool.
Post-mortem
Document root cause and create SMART actions.
Common misconception: Faster fixes alone are sufficient. In practice, without post-incident learning you will repeat the same outages.
