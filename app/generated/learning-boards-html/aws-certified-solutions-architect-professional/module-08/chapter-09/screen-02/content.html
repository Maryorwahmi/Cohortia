Learning Objectives

Learn the skills and measurable outcomes that show competency in incident response and post-mortem practice.

01
Establish incident response

Define detection, triage, escalation, and communication steps so teams restore service quickly and predictably.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Integrate AWS Chatbot

Route CloudWatch alarms into chat, enable safe commands, and centralize operational communication in Slack or Chime.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Automate with Incident Manager

Create incidents, notify on-call responders, and attach runbooks and escalation plans to reduce MTTR.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Conduct blameless post-mortems

Reconstruct timeline, quantify impact, find root causes, and create SMART action items to prevent recurrence.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
