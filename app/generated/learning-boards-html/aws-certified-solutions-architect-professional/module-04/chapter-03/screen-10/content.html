Comparison
Decision factors
Business drivers
Urgency favors replatforming; long-term innovation favors refactoring.
App characteristics
Tightly coupled monoliths are harder to refactor; modular apps are lower-risk candidates.
Team readiness
Refactor requires cloud-native expertise and observability tooling.
Concrete scenario comparison
Replatform
Move Oracle -> Amazon RDS for SQL Server; use Elastic Beanstalk for app servers to cut ops overhead fast.
Refactor
Extract payment processing to Lambda + SQS for asynchronous handling and independent scaling.
Key Takeaway

There is no single right answer. Create a staged plan that balances immediate operational relief with a roadmap for architectural improvement.
