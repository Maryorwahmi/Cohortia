What this term means in practice

Post-migration optimization combines cost, operations, security, and governance into a continuous improvement loop that is driven by data and automation.

Measure
Collect cost and performance metrics with Cost Explorer and CloudWatch to identify opportunities.
Decide
Prioritize actions—right-size, change storage tiers, or purchase committed discounts—based on business impact.
Automate
Implement scheduled scripts, Systems Manager runbooks, or instance schedulers to reduce manual toil.
Govern
Enforce tagging and SCPs so decisions scale across accounts and teams.
Concrete example
A team runs Cost Explorer weekly, flags EC2 instances under 10% CPU for a month, drives a rightsizing review, and automates stopping dev instances at night. Over three months, the team reduces monthly compute spend noticeably and maintains performance for production.
Key takeaway
Optimization is cyclical: measure regularly, take small actions, automate them, and enforce guardrails.
Common mistake
Treating rightsizing or a single savings purchase as a permanent fix when resource needs evolve over time.
