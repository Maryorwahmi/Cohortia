Governance stack

Tagging → Policies → Audits → Reviews

Tags enable cost allocation and automation. SCPs provide account-wide guardrails. AWS Config enforces and alerts on drift. Well-Architected reviews identify architectural improvements across pillars.

Tagging
Require Environment, Owner, and CostCenter tags; use automated checks to reject untagged resources.
SCPs & Config
Use SCPs to prevent risky API calls and Config rules to detect deviations from baseline configurations.
Well-Architected Reviews
Schedule regular reviews against the five pillars and prioritize remediation efforts based on operational and business impact.
Action
Draft and test an SCP that prevents resource creation without required tags in a sandbox OU, then expand once processes and exceptions are defined.
