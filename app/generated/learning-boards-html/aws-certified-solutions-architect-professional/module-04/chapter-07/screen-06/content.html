Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Right-Sizing vs Purchases
Right-Sizing

Match capacity to usage

Use CloudWatch and Cost Explorer to detect consistently low utilization and resize instances or database classes accordingly.

Concrete example
An EC2 instance runs under 10% CPU for weeks. Resize to a smaller family or share the workload to a container or serverless option.
Pitfall
Resizing without load testing can impact performance spikes. Validate under peak conditions.
Purchasing Options

Save on predictable usage

Savings Plans and Reserved Instances lower unit cost for steady workloads; Spot Instances lower cost for interruptible tasks.

Concrete example
After right-sizing a production fleet and observing stable load, the team purchases a Savings Plan to capture multi-month savings.
Key takeaway
Commitments are powerful but should follow evidence of stable usage; otherwise they lock in wasted capacity.
