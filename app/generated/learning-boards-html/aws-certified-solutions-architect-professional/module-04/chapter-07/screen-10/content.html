Logging & Patch Management

Centralize logs to simplify troubleshooting and auditing. Use Systems Manager to automate patching with safe maintenance windows.

Action
Define retention policies and automated archival for logs; schedule patch windows and monitor success rates.
Tip
Correlate patch failures with recent deployments to detect integration issues early.
Backups & DR

Automate snapshots and replication aligned to RTO/RPO. Test restores frequently and document steps required for recovery.

Example
Nightly RDS snapshots with weekly cross-Region copy and quarterly restore tests to validate recovery time objectives.
Common mistake
Skipping restore tests because backups appear successful. Without tests, recoverability is unknown.
