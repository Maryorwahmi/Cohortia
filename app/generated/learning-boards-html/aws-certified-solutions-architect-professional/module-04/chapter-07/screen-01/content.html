Post-Migration Optimization and Governance

How you manage cost, run operations, secure resources, and enforce governance determines whether cloud migration delivers lasting value. This lesson assembles practical patterns and tools to keep your workloads performant, compliant, and economical.

In this lesson
FinOps, operations, security, governance

You will examine continuous cost optimization techniques, operational excellence practices like monitoring and patching, security hardening methods, and governance controls anchored to the Well-Architected Framework. Each topic includes practical examples and an automation snippet you can reuse.

Why it matters
Optimizing after migration delivers measurable savings, lowers risk, and institutionalizes repeatable operations so teams can scale confidently.
