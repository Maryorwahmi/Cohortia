Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Key Concepts
FinOps
Financial accountability for variable cloud spend through measurement and action.
Operational Excellence
Monitoring, logging, runbooks, and automation to maintain reliability and speed recovery.
Security
Continuous identity, network, data protection, detection, and remediation practices.
Governance
Tagging, SCPs, and Well-Architected reviews to align cloud use with policy and business goals.
Automation
Automate routine tasks and remediation to reduce manual effort and enforce standards.
Review
Run regular Well-Architected reviews to discover and prioritize improvements across pillars.
Summary
Continuous measurement, automation, and governance convert migration effort into sustained business value and reduced risk.
