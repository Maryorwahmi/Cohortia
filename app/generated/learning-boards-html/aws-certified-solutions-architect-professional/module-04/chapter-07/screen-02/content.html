Learning Objectives

Build repeatable practices to manage cost, operations, security, and governance post-migration.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Continuous Cost Optimization

Learn to use Cost Explorer, CloudWatch, and automated controls to find waste, right-size resources, and apply Savings Plans or Spot Instances where appropriate to reduce variable spend.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Operational Excellence

Establish monitoring, centralized logging, automated patching, and tested incident runbooks so teams run workloads reliably and recover predictably.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Security Posture Enhancement

Apply least privilege IAM, enable GuardDuty and Inspector, encrypt data, and run regular vulnerability scans to reduce attack surface and exposure.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Governance & Well-Architected Reviews

Design tagging, SCPs, and Well-Architected reviews to align cloud usage with business objectives and compliance requirements on an ongoing cadence.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
