Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › ECS vs Fargate vs EKS
Amazon ECS
Managed orchestration
Simple cluster management, deep AWS integration. Choose for straightforward task-based workloads and teams that prefer AWS-native APIs.
AWS Fargate
Serverless containers
No instance management. Ideal when you want container portability without maintaining nodes and when workloads have variable scale.
Amazon EKS
Managed Kubernetes
Choose when Kubernetes APIs, portability, and advanced scheduling are required. Requires Kubernetes operational know-how.
Trade-off example
Fargate reduces ops but may cost more for very large, predictable fleets. EKS provides portability but increases operational complexity.
Key Takeaway
Match orchestration to your team's skills, runtime needs, and portability requirements rather than adopting a tool by default.
