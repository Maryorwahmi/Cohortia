Application Modernization with Serverless and Containers

Learn how to choose and design serverless and containerized architectures on AWS, integrate CI/CD, and optimize for cost and performance.

In this lesson
Compare serverless and containers, design event-driven APIs, build container images, and set up CI/CD pipelines.
Why it matters
Modern architectures reduce toil, improve scalability, and enable faster delivery cycles for critical applications.
Real outcome
You will be able to recommend serverless or container approaches and outline the CI/CD and monitoring practices that ensure safe production deployment.
