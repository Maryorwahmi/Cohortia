Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Learning Objectives — Practical Skills

Objective → Practical Action

Readiness
Create an inventory, assign complexity and risk scores, and recommend an initial R strategy.
TCO
Build a cost model that includes OpEx, CapEx, labor, licensing, and sensitivity to migration timing.
6 Rs
Draft one-line rationales and list the AWS approach that supports each chosen R for auditability.
Discovery
Collect automated metrics and dependency maps to form waves and define rollback points.
Practical tip
Always capture assumptions: licensing model, expected growth, and team capacity so the TCO is auditable and revisitable.
Verification
For each task, produce artifacts: inventory spreadsheet, TCO workbook, R-strategy matrix, and discovery report. These are the deliverables organizations use to approve waves and budgets.
