Migration Readiness Assessment

  • Business alignment and sponsorship
  • People skills and training plans
  • Process maturity for deployment and incident response
  • Platform and security posture
Action
Score each pillar and produce a prioritized remediation list that feeds the migration plan.

Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE)

The CCoE sets patterns for security, cost governance, and operations. It runs pilot projects, updates runbooks, and builds training so teams adopt cloud best practices consistently.

Practical role
Provide templates for monitoring, backups, and rollback so each migration wave follows repeatable steps.
Misconception
Assuming a single training session is enough; continuous enablement is required as teams adopt new tools and processes.
