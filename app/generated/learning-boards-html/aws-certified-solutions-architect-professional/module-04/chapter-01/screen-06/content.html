Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Total Cost of Ownership
Definition

What to include in a migration TCO

Direct costs: hardware, leases, and capital replacement cycles.

Operational costs: staff labor for patching, backups, monitoring, and incident response.

Software licensing: vendor-specific rules may persist in cloud or require alternative approaches.

Facility costs: power, cooling, rack space, and real-estate overheads that cloud removes.

Practical example
Moving a self-managed database to a managed service reduces patching labor but may change licensing and monthly fees.
Common error
Forgetting the cost of new cloud skills and migration project labor inflates expected savings.
TCO worksheet snapshot
  • List baseline on-prem costs by category.
  • Estimate cloud alternatives, include ongoing costs and one-time migration effort.
  • Run sensitivity for labor and licensing assumptions.
Key Takeaway

A robust TCO is transparent about assumptions and tests the impact of licensing and labor changes.
