Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Replatform & Refactor

Replatform

Small, targeted changes that adapt systems to managed services or platform features without full redesign.
Example
Move a self-managed MySQL to a managed relational service to reduce operational tasks.
When to choose
When you want faster wins with moderate change risk.

Refactor

Re-architect the application to use cloud primitives like serverless, managed queues, and microservices for scale and velocity.
Example
Break a monolith into services that can scale independently and be deployed with CI/CD pipelines.
Misconception
Refactor is not always cheaper; measure expected velocity and operational savings against rework cost.
Key Takeaway

Choose replatform for quick wins; refactor when roadmap and value justify higher effort and change.
