Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Testing & Rollback

Validation steps

  • Verify backups and rehearse full restores in staging.
  • Conduct load and integration tests that mimic production traffic.
  • Define and document rollback triggers and exact steps to revert.
Best practice
Use blue-green or canary patterns when possible to reduce risk and enable fast rollback with minimal impact.
Sample acceptance tests
# Acceptance checks (conceptual)
- All integration tests pass against migrated systems
- Error rate < 0.5% over 10 minutes
- Latency remains within defined SLA
- Backups completed and a point-in-time restore verified
Without rehearsed restores and clear rollback criteria, teams face long outages during unexpected failures. Build checklists and rehearse until the team can execute confidently under time pressure.
