Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › The 6 Rs

Strategy cards

Rehost (Lift-and-Shift)
Fastest path; move as-is to cloud compute. Good for urgent or hard-to-change systems.
Replatform
Make small cloud optimizations such as managed databases or platform services.
Refactor
Re-architect to leverage cloud-native services; highest benefit and cost.
Repurchase
Replace with SaaS where appropriate to reduce customization and operational overhead.
Retain
Keep on-premises for regulatory, hardware, or timing reasons; revisit regularly.
Retire
Decommission unused systems to shrink migration scope and cost.
Decision factors
  • Technical complexity and interdependencies
  • Business criticality and acceptable downtime
  • Compliance and hardware requirements
  • Available budget and team capacity
Example: Legacy HR System
Critical, tightly coupled to directory and Oracle, no dev team — often Rehost initially, then plan for future refactor or repurchase.
Note: document the reason for each R decision so stakeholders can revisit strategy as cloud skills and budgets change.
