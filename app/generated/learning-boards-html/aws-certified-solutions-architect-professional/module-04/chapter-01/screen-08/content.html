What Rehost is

Move applications as-is to cloud compute to reduce immediate hardware and datacenter risk.

Typical use cases
Legacy apps without active development teams, urgent hardware refreshes, or tight schedules.
Risks and trade-offs
You may preserve inefficient architecture and miss cloud-native cost savings unless a follow-up plan exists.
Scenario Example
A mission-critical payroll system running on a decade-old server is rehosted using block-level replication to minimize downtime. The team plans a later phase to replatform to a managed database and containerize services for better agility.
Key Takeaway

Rehost is a pragmatic first step when speed and operational continuity outweigh immediate modernization benefits.
