Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › AWS Snow Family
Snowcone
Small, rugged device for edge and smaller migrations (up to about 8 TB usable). Useful where space is limited or environments are harsh.
Snowball Edge
Larger capacity and optional compute. Storage-optimized appliances can hold around 80 TB usable and support temporary edge processing while migrating data.
Snowmobile
A 45-foot shipping container for exabyte-scale transfers, designed for moving tens to hundreds of petabytes when networks are impractical.
Security & process
Devices are encrypted before they leave your site, use tamper-evident enclosures, and are tracked through chain-of-custody processes. The workflow is: order device, connect locally, copy data, ship back to AWS, and have data ingested into S3.
Key Takeaway

Use Snow devices to bypass bandwidth limits. Choose capacity and compute characteristics that match your dataset size and any required edge processing.
