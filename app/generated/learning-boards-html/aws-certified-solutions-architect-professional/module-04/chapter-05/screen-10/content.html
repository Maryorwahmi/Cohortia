Optimization

Techniques to improve throughput and reduce cost

Parallelization
Use concurrent streams and tune concurrency after testing to reach stable high throughput.
Direct Connect
Prefer dedicated links for predictable throughput and potentially lower egress costs when moving large volumes.
Compression & deduplication
Reduce bytes moved by preprocessing data where feasible, especially for archives and redundant content.
Scheduling
Run bulk transfers during off-peak hours to reduce contention and increase effective throughput.
Cost calculator checklist
  • Per-GB transfer charges or service fees.
  • Snow Family per-job and per-day device costs.
  • Shipping and ingestion delays.
Key Takeaway

Measure and test concurrency levels; then apply Direct Connect, compression, and scheduling to balance throughput and cost effectively.
