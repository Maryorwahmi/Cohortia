Data Migration at Scale

Understand tools and designs for moving very large datasets securely and cost-effectively into AWS.

Why this chapter matters
Large migrations affect project timelines, budgets, and compliance. Choosing wisely prevents costly reruns and keeps data secure.
How to use this lesson
Use the scenarios and the hands-on activity to map service choice to your constraints: bandwidth, urgency, and data size.
Explore Key Topics
Snow Family
Offline devices for hundreds of TB to PB movement.
DataSync
Fast online sync between on-prem and S3/EFS/FSx.
Transfer Family
Protocol-compatible file ingress using SFTP/FTPS/FTP.
Visual Concept
Decision band
Bandwidth & volume guide: small-to-medium online, large-to-massive offline.
Security strip
Encryption at rest and in transit, plus chain-of-custody for physical devices.
Pilot & validate
Always run a small pilot to validate throughput, integrity, and process assumptions.
