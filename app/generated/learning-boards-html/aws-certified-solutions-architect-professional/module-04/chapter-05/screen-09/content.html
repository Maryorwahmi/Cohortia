Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Online vs Offline
Online: DataSync & Transfer Family
  • Good when reliable network bandwidth exists.
  • DataSync for system sync and accelerated bulk transfer.
  • Transfer Family when clients require SFTP/FTPS/FTP compatibility.
Design for IAM, VPC endpoints, and CloudWatch logging to ensure security and observability.
Offline: Snow Family
  • Choose when bandwidth is insufficient or too costly for timeframe.
  • Snowcone for tiny edge, Snowball Edge for large storage/compute, Snowmobile for PB+ moves.
  • Plan logistics, encryption keys, and checksum validation.
Parallelize devices to shorten wall-clock time for very large migrations.
Decision checklist
Measure throughput, estimate time and cost for both network and device options, account for security and compliance, and run a pilot load before committing.
