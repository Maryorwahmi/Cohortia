Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Activity
Instructions
For each scenario below, select the most appropriate AWS migration service and write a one-to-two sentence justification focused on the decisive constraint.
Scenarios
  1. Large Archive Data: 500 TB, 100 Mbps upload, low urgency.
  2. Ongoing File Share Sync: 10 TB, 1 Gbps Direct Connect, high urgency.
  3. Legacy SFTP Workflows: small-to-medium files daily by partners over internet.
  4. Exabyte-Scale Evacuation: 80 PB, no feasible network, six months deadline.
Example answers
Scenario 1: Recommended: Snowball Edge. Justification: Limited bandwidth makes a 500 TB transfer impractical over the network, so a physical device shipped to AWS is faster and likely more cost-effective.
Scenario 2: Recommended: DataSync. Justification: Dedicated Direct Connect and the need for continuous sync make DataSync ideal for accelerated, ongoing synchronization to S3.
Scenario 3: Recommended: Transfer Family. Justification: Maintain existing SFTP workflows for partners while moving files into S3 without changing client software.
Scenario 4: Recommended: Snowmobile. Justification: No feasible network option and extreme volume requires an exabyte-scale physical transfer service.
Key Takeaway

Map one dominant constraint—bandwidth, urgency, or protocol—to the service choice and validate via a pilot and cost comparison.
