Security, compliance, and integrity

What it means to migrate data safely and verifiably.

Bloom: Apply → Understand → Master
01
Encrypt during transfer
Use TLS and device-level encryption to protect data in motion and at rest.
Mnemonic: Transit + rest encryption.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Access controls & audits
Define IAM roles and enable CloudTrail to audit all transfer service activity.
Mnemonic: Roles + trails.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Checksum validation
Verify data integrity with checksums before and after transfer; DataSync automates integrity checks.
Mnemonic: Verify → Compare hashes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Pilot and plan
Run a small pilot to validate bandwidth, time estimates, and integrity procedures before full migration.
Mnemonic: Pilot → Adjust → Execute.
