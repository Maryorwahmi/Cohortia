Pilot strategy and pitfalls

  • Measure effective throughput, not theoretical link speed.
  • Verify checksum workflows during the pilot to ensure integrity at scale.
  • Test security configuration: IAM roles, VPC endpoints, and CloudTrail logging.
Pilot acceptance criteria
  1. Measured throughput meets expected minimum.
  2. Checksums validate across source and destination.
  3. Monitoring and audit logs capture required events.
Example pitfall
Assuming 1 Gbps equals 125 MB/s effective transfer. In practice, overhead and contention reduce this number; pilots reveal the true throughput.
Key Takeaway

A short, well-designed pilot prevents long, costly surprises during the full migration. Validate throughput, security, and integrity early.
