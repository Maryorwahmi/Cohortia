Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Checksums & Integrity
What to do
  • Compute checksums (e.g., SHA-256) before transfer and record them in a manifest.
  • Let DataSync perform integrity checks during transfer, but still validate after ingestion.
  • For Snow devices, validate local checksums against S3 after ingestion.
Example workflow
Create manifest, compute SHA-256 for each archive, run transfer, and then run an automated comparator to flag mismatches and queue retransfer for failures.
Key Takeaway

Checksums are essential. Automate their generation and verification to ensure data fidelity at scale and make failures actionable.
