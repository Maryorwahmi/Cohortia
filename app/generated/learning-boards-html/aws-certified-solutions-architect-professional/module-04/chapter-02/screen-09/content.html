Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Cutover Best Practices
Cutover checklist
  • Run multiple test launches under realistic conditions and validate logs and metrics.
  • Reduce DNS TTLs ahead of cutover and prepare DNS change playbook.
  • Define rollback steps with a single decision owner and rehearsed actions.
  • Communicate windows and expectations with stakeholders and downstream teams.
Common mistake

Failing to test third-party integrations and session-state behavior during test launches can cause production outages despite successful instance boots.

Practice scenario
Lower TTLs 24 hours before, perform two test launches with synthetic load, validate database connections and external APIs, then execute a dry-run rollback to confirm procedures.
Key Takeaway

Thorough testing and stakeholder coordination are the most effective levers for reducing cutover risk and downtime.
