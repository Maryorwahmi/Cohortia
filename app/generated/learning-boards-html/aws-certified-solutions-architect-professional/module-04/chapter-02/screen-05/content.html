Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Candidate Selection
Selection Criteria
  • Legacy, stable applications with complex dependencies.
  • COTS packages where source is not available for refactor.
  • Applications with urgent migration deadlines.
  • Parts of a larger phased migration where quick relocation helps later modernization.
Concrete example
A finance back-office system with vendor-supplied binaries and intricate on-prem database links is rehosted to EC2 to meet a data center exit while preserving functionality.
Common mistake

Don’t rehost services already designed for autoscaling and managed services. Those are better replatformed to capture cloud-native benefits.

Quick decision guide
If: Complex dependencies
Choose rehost to avoid risky refactors right now.
If: Vendor code
Rehost to preserve support and behavior.
If: Contract deadline
Rehost to meet timelines, then optimize later.
If: Cloud-native ready
Prefer replatform/refactor instead of rehosting.
Key Takeaway

Match candidate selection to business urgency, technical constraints, and long-term modernization goals to prioritize rehosting work effectively.
