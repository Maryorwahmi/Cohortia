Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Quick Reference
Tighten IAM
Narrow actions and resources for each role and validate regularly.
Layer defenses
Network, compute, data, identity, and monitoring must all contribute to protection.
Adopt zero‑trust
Treat every request as untrusted and verify with identity and telemetry.
Final takeaway
Apply one focused improvement now: tighten a role, restrict an outbound rule, or enable IMDSv2. Small actions compound into stronger security when combined with layered controls and monitoring.
