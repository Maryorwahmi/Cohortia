Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Key Concepts
Least Privilege
Grant only needed permissions to users, roles, and services to reduce blast radius.
Defense-in-Depth
Layer controls across network, compute, data, identity, and logging for resilient protection.
Zero‑Trust
Assume no implicit trust. Authenticate and authorize every request and validate continuously.
Summary: Build repeatable, auditable blueprints that combine these concepts and feed telemetry to centralized detection.
