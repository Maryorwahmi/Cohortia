Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Third‑Party Integration
Evaluation criteria
  • Deployability into an inspection VPC and Transit Gateway support.
  • Ability to use IAM roles for automation and access control.
  • Log forwarding compatibility and retention settings.
  • Performance, scale, and cost impact.
Pilot checklist
  1. Deploy in inspection VPC and configure Transit Gateway routing.
  2. Enable log forwarding to central collector and validate schema.
  3. Simulate attack traffic and confirm detection and alerting.
Key Takeaway
Integration matters more than vendor name. Prioritize tools that fit your logging and IAM model and that reduce blind spots.
