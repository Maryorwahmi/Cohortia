Least Privilege
Grant minimal permissions to reduce blast radius. Example: role limited to s3:GetObject on a single bucket.
Zero‑Trust & Micro‑segmentation
Use security groups, NACLs, and VPCs to isolate tiers and limit lateral movement.
Inspection VPC
Centralize third‑party firewalls behind Transit Gateway to inspect traffic between VPCs and the internet.
Why start here
Patterns reduce risk faster than ad hoc controls. Reference blueprints speed secure, repeatable deployments.
Quick Facts
  • Least privilege applies at IAM, network, and resource policies.
  • Defense-in-depth uses network, compute, data, identity, and monitoring layers.
  • Zero-trust enforces continuous authentication and authorization.
Starter Checklist
  1. Identify critical assets and sensitive data locations.
  2. Map communications between tiers for micro-segmentation.
  3. Enforce least privilege on roles and resource policies.
