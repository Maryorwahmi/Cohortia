Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › AWS Organizations
Control plane responsibilities
  • Manage account creation and organization membership.
  • Group accounts into OUs for policy application and inheritance.
  • Attach Service Control Policies to define maximum permissions.
Remember: Organizations does not grant permissions; it constrains them via SCPs applied at organizational boundaries.
Architecture snapshot
Management Account
Pays bills and manages organization membership and SCP lifecycle.
Security OU / Account
Central logging, CloudTrail, and monitoring services are collected here.
Environment OUs
Dev, Test, Prod OUs allow environment-specific guardrails and workflows.
Misconception

Organizations alone do not enforce resource-level access; SCPs restrict actions but IAM still grants explicit permissions within those bounds.
