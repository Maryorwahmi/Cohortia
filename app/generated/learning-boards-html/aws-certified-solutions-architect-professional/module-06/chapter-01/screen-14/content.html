Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
AWS Organizations
Central service to manage accounts, OUs, and policies.
Organizational Unit (OU)
Logical grouping of accounts for hierarchical policy application.
Service Control Policy (SCP)
Organization-level policy that restricts the maximum permissions available to accounts.
Quick takeaway

Use these building blocks to map your governance model: structure accounts, group them, and attach guardrails.
