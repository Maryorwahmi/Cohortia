Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Practice & Verification
Turn objectives into tasks
Verify account separation
Confirm billing reports and IAM cross-account access are restricted unless explicitly configured.
Test SCPs in sandbox
Attach deny SCP to a test OU and attempt the blocked action to see an Access Denied result.
Confirm OU inheritance
Apply a guardrail at a parent OU and verify it applies to all nested OUs and accounts.
Find overbroad denies
Scan SCP statements for wildcards, region-wide denies, or actions that could impact normal ops.
Key Takeaway

Verification in sandbox accounts is essential before applying SCPs broadly. Always test inheritance and region conditions.
