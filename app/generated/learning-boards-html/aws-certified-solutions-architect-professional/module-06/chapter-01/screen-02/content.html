Learning Objectives
Each objective includes a competency milestone to verify your setup.
01
Design multi-account strategy

Define account boundaries for isolation, billing, and compliance. Example: separate Prod, Dev, and Security accounts to limit blast radius.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement SCP guardrails

Apply SCPs to prevent critical actions such as disabling CloudTrail or leaving the organization. SCPs constrain maximum permissions available to accounts.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Structure Organizational Units

Group accounts logically so policies inherit predictably. Use OUs for Security, Infrastructure, and environment-based groupings.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Mitigate multi-account risks

Avoid overly broad denies and missing centralized security accounts. Test SCPs in sandbox accounts before wide deployment.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
