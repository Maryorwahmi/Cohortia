Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Common mistakes
Flat structure
No OUs means policy scaling problems and manual configuration drift.
Overbroad SCPs
Root-level denies can accidentally block needed actions. Narrow where possible.
Missing security account
Without a centralized security account, logs and alerts are fragmented and response slows.
Practical steps
  • Define OU boundaries early and keep them manageable.
  • Test SCPs in sandbox OUs and automate account baselines.
  • Establish a Security OU/account for centralized logging and detection.
