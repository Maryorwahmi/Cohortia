Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Provisioning & Migration
Provisioning steps
  1. Define baseline templates: IAM roles, SCP attachments, logging endpoints, tags.
  2. Automate account creation and OU attachment through scripts or account factory.
  3. Test SCPs in sandbox accounts before wide attachment.
  4. Migrate workloads in stages with rollback plans and verification checks.
Verification checklist
  • CloudTrail logging is enabled and centralized.
  • Required IAM roles and cross-account trust are present.
  • Billing tags and cost allocation are configured.
Pitfall
Delaying SCP attachment until after migration leaves a period of weaker controls. Apply and test early in the pipeline.
