Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Operational terms
Root Account
Primary account with ultimate control; secure with MFA and limited access.
Management Account
Payer account that manages Organizations and attaches SCPs.
Member Account
Accounts that host workloads and inherit policies from OUs.
Blast Radius
The scope of impact from an incident; multi-account design aims to minimize this by isolation and guardrails.
Final reflection

Check your high-privilege accounts for MFA and ensure you have a central security account to collect and retain audit logs.
