Hierarchy patterns
  1. 1. Security OU for logging and central detection tools.
  2. 2. Infrastructure OU for shared networking and directory services.
  3. 3. Environment OUs for Dev, Test, and Prod separation.
Keep OU structure predictable and documented so team onboarding and policy audits are straightforward.
Practical checklist
Automation
Provision accounts with a baseline template and attach required SCPs automatically.
Sandbox OU
Test new SCPs in an isolated OU before wide attachment.
Document attachments
Record which SCPs are attached to each OU and why to avoid surprises.
Common mistake

Applying an overly broad SCP at a high-level OU can unintentionally deny needed actions in many accounts. Prefer narrow attachments when possible.
