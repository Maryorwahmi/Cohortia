Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › SCP vs IAM
Service Control Policies
  • Applied at OU or account level
  • Define maximum available actions
  • Inherited by child OUs
Example: Deny stopping CloudTrail everywhere by attaching an SCP to the Production OU.
IAM Policies
  • Attached to principals inside accounts
  • Grant permissions within the SCP bounds
  • Used for fine-grained role access
Example: Grant a role permission to manage EC2 instances only in approved regions, while SCP restricts regions organization-wide.
Key Takeaway

Use SCPs for broad guardrails and IAM for precise access. Audit both to ensure intended behavior.
