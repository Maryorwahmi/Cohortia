Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Assessment & Review
Sample question 1
High-traffic e-commerce site on CloudFront + ALB needs 24/7 expert DDoS support. Which combination? Answer: Shield Advanced + AWS WAF. Shield Advanced provides advanced DDoS mitigation and DRT access; WAF protects Layer 7 exploits.
Sample question 2
You must enforce a baseline WAF policy across all ALBs in production accounts. Which service? Answer: Firewall Manager — it applies policies across an Organization and auto-associates Web ACLs for existing and new resources.
Exam strategy
  • Identify the threat layer (L3/L4 vs L7).
  • Determine operational needs: 24/7 support, cost protection, or centralized enforcement.
  • Select services that align with those needs and briefly justify why.
