Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Shield: Standard vs Advanced
Shield Standard
  • Enabled automatically for all AWS customers at no extra cost.
  • Protects against common Layer 3 and 4 attacks like SYN and UDP floods.
  • Good baseline for general public endpoints.
Use Standard for non-critical public services where baseline protection is sufficient.
Shield Advanced
  • Enhanced detection and mitigation capabilities for EC2, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, and Route 53.
  • Near real-time visibility, DDoS Response Team access, and billing protections for scaling costs caused by attacks.
  • Recommended for mission-critical applications that require 24/7 support.
Use Advanced when availability and cost predictability under attack are essential.
Quick decision guide
Ask whether the service supports core business revenue or customer experience, whether you need DDoS response team access, and whether you need cost protection. If yes, consider Shield Advanced.
