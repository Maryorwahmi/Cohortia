Order matters: first decode percent-encoded characters, then convert to lowercase for case-insensitive matches. Sample real requests to confirm behavior.
Practical checklist
  • Identify which request field contains the payload you want to inspect.
  • Choose text transformations that normalize likely attacker encodings.
  • Use sampling and CloudWatch logs to view transformed content before blocking.
  • Test in staging to detect legitimate encodings that might match your pattern.
Common mistake
Blocking immediately without sampling increases the risk of false positives affecting real users.
