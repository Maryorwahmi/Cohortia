Common pitfalls and fixes
A short catalog of operational mistakes and practical countermeasures.
Scattered logs
Fix: centralize CloudTrail and Config delivery to a logging account for faster incident correlation.
No integrity validation
Fix: enable log file integrity validation on CloudTrail to prove logs weren’t tampered with.
No automated remediation
Fix: attach Systems Manager automation to Config Rules for immediate, safe remediation of common violations.
Destructive playbooks
Fix: capture forensic artifacts before containment and design non-destructive steps where possible.
Practical rule

Build small, testable playbooks, validate cross-account permissions, and always prove your logging and validation are working before trusting automation.
