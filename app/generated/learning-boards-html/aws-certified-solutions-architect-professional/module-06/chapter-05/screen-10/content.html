Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Centralized Logging Architecture
Design goals
Immutability, centralized access, searchable analytics, and controlled retention.
Secure S3 storage
Use a logging account bucket with KMS encryption, tight bucket policies, and optional MFA delete.
Ingest & index
Stream logs to OpenSearch or a SIEM using Firehose or direct ingestion for search and analytics.
Query
Use Athena for ad hoc forensic queries or dashboards in OpenSearch for trending and alerts.
Access control
Limit read and write roles, separate analysts from production permissions, and log access to the bucket.
Example pipeline
  1. CloudTrail writes objects to central S3.
  2. S3 triggers Firehose or Lambda to index into OpenSearch.
  3. Security Hub and SIEM correlate and feed dashboards.
Key Takeaway

Central stores with secure delivery and analytic pipelines let you search, alert, and investigate efficiently without losing audit integrity.
