Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Data Residency
Definitions

Residency: the physical region where data is stored. Sovereignty: the laws that govern that data based on location.

Architectural response
  • Deploy separate stacks in required regions
  • Restrict cross-region replication unless approved
  • Ensure logs and backups also meet residency rules
Example

For a national healthcare workload, keep data, backups, and audit logs within the approved national region and document any approved transfers.

Common Pitfall

Centralized log stores in a different region can unintentionally violate residency requirements; validate locations for all artifacts.
