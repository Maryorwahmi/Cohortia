Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › AWS Artifact
Definition

AWS Artifact

A central resource for on-demand access to AWS compliance reports and select agreements.

Typical documents
  • ISO certifications
  • PCI DSS attestations
  • SOC reports and HIPAA BAA references
Example

Attach a SOC report from Artifact to your audit packet to show infrastructure control coverage.

Practices
  • Download relevant reports before an audit and map them to your controls.
  • Keep copies with your compliance evidence and note applicable date ranges.
  • Never treat Artifact documents as proof of your configuration or application controls.
Common Mistake

Assuming Artifact reports make your solution compliant without your own evidence.
