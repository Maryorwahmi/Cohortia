Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Industry Compliance
HIPAA

Emphasizes encryption, access controls, audit logging, and incident response.

Common services: KMS, S3 SSE-KMS, CloudTrail, GuardDuty.
PCI DSS

Focuses on protecting payment data via segmentation, strong controls, and testing.

Common services: VPC segmentation, WAF, Shield, KMS, Config Rules.
GDPR

Focuses on privacy, data subject rights, and data residency constraints.

Common services: Regional boundaries, Macie for discovery, encryption and deletion processes.
Design note

Map each framework requirement to specific services and evidence sources, and document who is responsible for each mapped control.
