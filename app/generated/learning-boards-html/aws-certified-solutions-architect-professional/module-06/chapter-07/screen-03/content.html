Objectives — Applied Milestones

Concrete tasks to show competency to auditors and stakeholders.

01 — Ownership

Create a one-page diagram listing which responsibilities AWS accepts and which your team must own for your application.

02 — Evidence

Download Artifact reports and store them with your compliance package for audit review.

03 — Assessment

Run an Audit Manager assessment against a framework and inspect collected evidence for gaps.

04 — Enforcement

Enable or author a Config Rule and document the remediation or alerting workflow.
