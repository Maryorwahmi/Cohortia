Post-Incident Activity
Lessons learned and remediation
Collect a timeline, list technical gaps, and identify process failures. Create actionable items with owners and due dates.
Update runbooks to reflect actual steps taken during the incident.
Automate manual tasks that repeatedly slowed response.
Improve logging, alerts, and tagging to shorten future analysis.
Governance tasks
  • Assign owners for each remediation item and track completion.
  • Re-validate automated containment in a staging environment.
  • Review IR role privileges; add MFA and IP restrictions where needed.
Key Takeaway

Lessons learned close the loop: they turn incidents into measurable improvements and safer operations.
