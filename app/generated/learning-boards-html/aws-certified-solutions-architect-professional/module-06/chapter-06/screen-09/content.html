Eradication vs Recovery
Eradication
Remove malware, rotate keys, delete malicious roles. Confirm root cause is eliminated before rebuild.
Recovery
Restore services from validated AMIs, backups, or CloudFormation stacks to ensure a clean environment.
Mistake to avoid: restoring directly from suspect snapshots without forensic validation.
Concrete recovery plan example
  1. Preserve forensic snapshots captured during containment; do not mount them for recovery.
  2. Eradicate malware and revoke compromised credentials.
  3. Rebuild using CloudFormation or validated AMIs and redeploy stateful data from clean backups.
  4. Validate monitoring and perform smoke tests before returning to production traffic.
Key Takeaway

Use preserved snapshots as evidence, not as the default recovery source; rebuild from validated artifacts after eradication.
