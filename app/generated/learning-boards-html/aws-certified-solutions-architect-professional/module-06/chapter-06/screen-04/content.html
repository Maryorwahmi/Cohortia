Core Concept
The Incident Response Lifecycle
A structured series of phases ensures you can react consistently and preserve evidence for review and compliance.
Preparation — runbooks, logging, pre-approved roles.
Detection & Analysis — GuardDuty, alarms, Amazon Detective.
Containment — isolate resources and preserve state.
Eradication & Recovery — remove cause, restore clean systems.
Post-Incident — lessons learned and runbook updates.
Key Takeaway

Treat IR as a lifecycle: prepare and instrument first so detection triggers proven containment and evidence collection.

Example: Automated containment flow
When GuardDuty reports a high-severity EC2 compromise, an EventBridge rule can invoke a Lambda that isolates the instance and snapshots volumes for forensics.
Detection
GuardDuty finding or CloudWatch alarm triggers a rule.
Action
EventBridge triggers a Lambda to isolate and snapshot.
Analysis
Use Amazon Detective and centralized logs to scope impact.
Recovery
Restore clean instances from AMIs or IaC after eradication.
