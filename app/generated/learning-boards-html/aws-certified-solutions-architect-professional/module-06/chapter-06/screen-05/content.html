Preparation
Forensic-ready foundations
  • Enable CloudTrail to centralized, encrypted S3 buckets with strict bucket policies.
  • Collect VPC Flow Logs and GuardDuty findings centrally for correlation.
  • Create runbooks with exact commands and communication steps for investigators.
  • Pre-create CloudFormation templates for analysis environments and quarantine SGs.
Key Takeaway

Preparation means predictable, testable processes and an infrastructure that preserves evidence by design.
