Learning Objectives

Skills to design secure cross-account and federated access, and to scale permissions with ABAC.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Cross-account roles
Implement assume-role trust for secure resource sharing across AWS accounts.
Mnemonic: trust policy = who can assume; permissions policy = what they can do.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identity federation (SAML/OIDC)
Connect corporate IdPs or OIDC providers to AWS to avoid separate IAM users.
Mnemonic: SAML for humans, OIDC for machines and apps.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Attribute-Based Access Control
Use tags on principals and resources to grant permissions dynamically.
Mnemonic: tag-match = permission granted.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Risk identification & mitigation
Detect overly permissive policies, wildcard principals, and tagging gaps.
Mnemonic: least privilege, precise trust, consistent tags.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
