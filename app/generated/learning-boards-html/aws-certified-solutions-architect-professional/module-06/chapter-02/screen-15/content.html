Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts — Part 2
ABAC
Tag-driven policies reduce the need for many role permutations when tagging is enforced.
AWS STS
Issues temporary credentials for assumed roles or federated sessions; they expire and reduce credential risk.
Permissions Boundary
Set the maximum permissions a role or user can receive, limiting the blast radius of delegated policies.
Final takeaway

Design narrow trust policies, validate federation assertions, govern tagging, and rely on STS sessions to keep access temporary and auditable.
