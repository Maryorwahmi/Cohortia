Architecture › Cross-account encryption
Required permissions
  • KMS key policy allows the external role kms:Decrypt (and kms:GenerateDataKey if needed).
  • S3 bucket policy allows s3:GetObject for the external role.
  • Both must be present; one without the other fails at runtime.
Concrete sequence
1) Update KMS policy to add the remote role. 2) Update S3 policy to allow GetObject from that role. 3) Test read and observe Decrypt entries in CloudTrail.
Example troubleshooting
If reads fail with Access Denied, check CloudTrail for kms:Decrypt denial and then inspect both the key policy and bucket policy for missing principals or conditions.
Common mistake
Only updating the S3 policy and forgetting the KMS key policy creates the most frequent cross-account failure mode.
