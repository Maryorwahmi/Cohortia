Definition › In-transit protections
Client-to-service
Use ACM for certificate management and front your app with ELB or CloudFront to enforce HTTPS. Verify certificate renewal and install health checks into your pipeline.
Service-to-service
Prefer VPC endpoints or PrivateLink to keep traffic on AWS backbone. Still validate TLS and mutual TLS where possible for higher assurance.
Concrete example
Serve an API over HTTPS with an ACM certificate on an ALB; route internal batch jobs to S3 via a VPC endpoint so they never traverse the public internet.
Key Takeaway

Combine TLS and private networking to minimize exposure and simplify evidence for auditors.

Misconception
Assuming traffic inside a VPC is automatically secure without TLS can leave you vulnerable to lateral movement or misrouted traffic.
