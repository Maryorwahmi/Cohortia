What to Remember
Encryption: rest + transit
Both protections reduce different risks; classify data then choose modes that meet that level.
KMS: central key control
Use customer-managed CMKs for auditability and fine-grained key policies; coordinate with service policies.
Enforce and verify
Deny-first bucket policies, negative tests, and automated audit rules keep protections effective at scale.
Summary: classify data, choose KMS-backed control when you need audit and rotation, enforce via policies, and automate validation so encryption remains reliable as your environment changes.
