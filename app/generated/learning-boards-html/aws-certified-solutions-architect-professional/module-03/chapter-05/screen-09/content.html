Comparison
SRR vs CRR
SRR keeps copies in the same region for separation and account-level replication. CRR copies across regions for resilience and lower latency for remote users. Both require versioning and an IAM replication role.
Use case — SRR
Log aggregation across accounts or replicating to a centralized audit bucket in the same region.
Use case — CRR
Disaster recovery and regional locality for global applications.
Key Takeaway

Choose SRR for intra-region needs and CRR for DR or cross-region locality. Always enable versioning and monitor replication status.

Operational checklist
  • Enable versioning on source and destination buckets.
  • Create an IAM role that allows s3:GetObjectVersion and s3:ReplicateObject among other permissions.
  • Filter by prefix or tags if you only need to replicate a subset.
  • Use S3 Batch Replication to copy existing objects.
Common misconception
Replication guarantees identical timing across regions. It is asynchronous and may lag; design for eventual consistency and check replication metrics.
