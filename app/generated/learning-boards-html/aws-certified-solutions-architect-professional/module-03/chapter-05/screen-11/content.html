Organization metrics and dashboards
What Storage Lens shows
  • Total storage and growth trends
  • Objects by size and count
  • Missing protection settings and public access reports
How to act
  • Identify cold buckets and add lifecycle transitions.
  • Enable Intelligent‑Tiering for unpredictable buckets.
  • Fix missing versioning or Object Lock for compliance buckets.
Key Takeaway

Use Storage Lens metrics to drive lifecycle and replication decisions; analytics identify candidates, automation enforces them.
