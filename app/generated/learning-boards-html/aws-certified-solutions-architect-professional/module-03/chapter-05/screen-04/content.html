Comparison
Storage classes at a glance
S3 Standard
High throughput, low latency, frequent access.
Intelligent‑Tiering
Automates moves between frequent and infrequent tiers for unknown patterns.
Standard‑IA / One Zone‑IA
Lower storage cost, higher retrieval fee; one‑zone reduces durability for cheaper storage.
Glacier tiers
Instant Retrieval, Flexible Retrieval, Deep Archive — choose by acceptable retrieval time and cost.
Key Takeaway

Decide storage class from frequency and retrieval time, then enforce it with lifecycle rules to lower cost reliably.

Concrete example & common mistake
Example scenario: logs are hot for 30 days and cold thereafter. Use Standard for 0–30 days, then transition to Standard‑IA after 30 days, and Glacier Flexible after 90 days. This saves ongoing storage cost while retaining the ability to restore quickly when needed.
# Lifecycle transitions (summary)
# After 30 days → STANDARD_IA
# After 90 days → GLACIER
# Expire after 365 days
Common mistake
Keeping all data in S3 Standard for simplicity leads to high monthly cost for rarely used or archival objects.
