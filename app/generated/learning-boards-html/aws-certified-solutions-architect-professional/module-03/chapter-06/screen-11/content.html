Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Archiving Strategies
Definition
S3 Glacier tiers and Object Lock
Use lifecycle rules to move objects from frequent access tiers to Glacier Flexible Retrieval or Glacier Deep Archive when access becomes rare. For regulatory immutability, enable Object Lock with a retention period.
Glacier Flexible Retrieval
Lower cost than standard storage; retrieval times vary from minutes to hours.
Glacier Deep Archive
Lowest cost for multi-year retention; retrieval may take hours.
Example
Move audit logs to Glacier Deep Archive after 30 days and enable S3 Object Lock for a 7-year retention period to meet legal requirements.
Common mistake
Assuming lifecycle transition alone provides immutability; Object Lock must be enabled to prevent deletions.
Key Takeaway

Combine lifecycle transitions with Object Lock and KMS encryption to meet cost, retrieval, and compliance requirements.
