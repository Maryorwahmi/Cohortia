Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Warm Standby / Active/Active
Warm Standby
Scaled-down running environment
Keep a fully functional but smaller footprint running in the DR region. Scale up during failover to meet demand.
When to choose
For business-critical apps needing minutes of RTO without full active cost.
Multi-Site Active/Active
Fully active in multiple regions
Deploy full capacity in two or more regions and distribute traffic globally. Requires careful data design and orchestration.
When to choose
For ultra-critical services where downtime or even short service interruptions are unacceptable.
Operational note
Active/Active benefits must be weighed against complexity: replication conflict resolution, global deployment pipelines, and cost. Always test failover and reconcile cross-region consistency behavior.
