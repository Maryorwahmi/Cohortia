Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › DR Patterns
Comparison
Backup & Restore · Pilot Light · Warm Standby · Multi-Site
Backup & Restore
Lowest cost, highest recovery time. Use for non-critical workloads or data that can tolerate longer outages.
RTO: hours-days · RPO: minutes-hours
Pilot Light
Minimal running footprint in DR region: database replica and copied AMIs. Scale app servers when needed.
RTO: tens of minutes-hours · RPO: minutes
Warm Standby
Scaled-down full stack in DR region that can be scaled up quickly. Good balance for critical apps with cost controls.
RTO: minutes · RPO: seconds
Multi-Site Active/Active
Full, active deployment across regions with near-zero recovery time and data loss, suitable for very high-criticality systems.
RTO/RPO: near-zero
Guidance
How to pick a pattern
  • Classify application criticality and business impact per hour of downtime.
  • Translate business needs into RTO and RPO targets.
  • Map services that achieve those targets while optimizing cost.
  • Document failover steps and test them regularly.
Misconception
Assuming that a single DR pattern fits all apps. Different services and costs make mixed strategies common.
