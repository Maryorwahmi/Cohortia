Automate backups
Policy-driven backups and cross-region copies reduce human error and meet audit needs.
Match RTO/RPO
Choose Pilot Light, Warm Standby, or Active/Active based on criticality and cost.
Archive securely
Use Glacier tiers and Object Lock with KMS encryption for long-term, immutable retention.
Ask during reviews: Are backups centralized and tested? Are cross-region copies and permissions validated? Does archiving meet retention and immutability requirements?
