Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › RTO & RPO Mapping
Architecture
Service mapping to recovery objectives
Multi-AZ / Failover
Synchronous standby in same region. Good for sub-minute failover with minimal data loss for single-region resilience.
Aurora Global DB
Multi-region replication for low RTO and low RPO across regions for global applications.
AMI copies
Fast compute rebuilds when AMIs are current and copied to a DR region.
S3 Cross-Region Replication
Asynchronous object copying for durable, regional separation of object data.
AWS Elastic Disaster Recovery
Continuous block-level replication to a staging area in AWS for near-continuous replication and fast recoveries.
Considerations
Align database, object, and compute replication windows. Test end-to-end restores to detect consistency problems between components.
Misconception
Assuming S3 CRR guarantees read consistency at the same instant; replicas are asynchronous and may lag during bursts.
Key Takeaway

Choose services based on measurable RTO/RPO and test them under realistic conditions to find hidden gaps.
