Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › DR Testing & Mistakes
Process
Plan, test, refine
Document runbooks with explicit steps, roles, and recovery checkpoints. Measure timings during live drills and update playbooks to remove friction.
Table-top exercise
Walk through failure scenarios with stakeholders and validate assumptions.
Live drill
Execute a partial or full failover to record real RTO and identify hidden dependencies.
Common mistakes
  • Not testing end-to-end
  • Underestimating RTO/RPO
  • Forgetting IAM, secrets, or DNS steps
  • Expecting backups to be a full DR solution without automation
Warning

A plan untested in production-like conditions will likely fail when required. Testing reveals true recovery times.
