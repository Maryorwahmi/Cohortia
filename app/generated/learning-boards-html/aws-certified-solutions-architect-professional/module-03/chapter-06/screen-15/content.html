AWS Backup
Centralized, policy-driven backups across AWS services, with lifecycle and cross-region copy for DR and compliance.
DR Patterns
Backup & Restore, Pilot Light, Warm Standby, Multi-Site Active/Active — pick based on RTO/RPO and cost.
Archiving & Compliance
Use S3 Lifecycle to Glacier and S3 Object Lock for WORM retention to meet long-term compliance needs.
Summary takeaway

Combine centralized backups, appropriate DR patterns, and archived immutability to satisfy availability, durability, and compliance goals. Test to verify.
