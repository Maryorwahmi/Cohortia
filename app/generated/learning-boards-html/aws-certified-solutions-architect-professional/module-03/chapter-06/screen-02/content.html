01
Design centralized backup plans
Create policy-driven backups across RDS, EBS, EFS, and DynamoDB and automate lifecycle transitions. This ensures consistent recovery points and auditability across teams.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement cross-region DR
Choose patterns like Pilot Light or Warm Standby and configure cross-region copies and permissions to meet availability targets. A pilot light uses read replicas and copied AMIs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Define and meet RTO & RPO
Map services to recovery objectives: RDS Multi-AZ and Aurora Global Database for low RTO/RPO; AMIs and S3 CRR for data portability.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Choose archiving for compliance
Use S3 Lifecycle to move objects to Glacier tiers and S3 Object Lock to enforce WORM policies for regulatory retention.
