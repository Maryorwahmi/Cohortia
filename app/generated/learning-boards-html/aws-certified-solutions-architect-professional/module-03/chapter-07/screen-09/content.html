Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Snow workflow
Pre-shipment steps
  • Calculate devices needed and schedule deliveries.
  • Prepare secure local loading stations and power/network isolation.
  • Record checksums and device serials before shipping.
Post-shipment steps
  • Confirm AWS ingestion to S3 and verify object counts and checksums.
  • Rotate or retire any local copies per your data retention policy.
  • Maintain chain-of-custody logs for compliance.
Security note
Devices encrypt data with 256-bit keys. Manage these keys securely and ensure target S3 buckets enforce least-privilege IAM roles for ingestion.
Common mistake

Loading devices without checksum verification or missing tamper checks can force repeat work and risk data integrity.
