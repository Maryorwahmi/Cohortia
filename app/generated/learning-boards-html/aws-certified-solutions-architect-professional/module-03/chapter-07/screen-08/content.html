Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Snow Family
Snowcone
Small, rugged, and portable. Good for remote sites or edge processing where space and power are limited.
Snowball Edge
Storage-optimized and compute-enabled variants. Use when you need local processing or tens of terabytes per device.
Snowmobile
Truck-mounted, exabyte-scale transport for multi-petabyte datasets. Use for very large archives or data center migrations.
Operational checklist
  • Estimate parallel device count by dividing dataset size by device usable capacity.
  • Order devices early and plan for local loading and encryption key management.
  • Validate tamper-evident seals and confirm chain-of-custody when returning devices.
Key Takeaway

Choose Snow devices when bandwidth or time constrain online transfers; plan logistics, encryption, and parallel loading carefully.
