Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Warehouse vs Lake
Data Warehouse (Redshift)
Columnar MPP for structured, curated analytics
  • Purpose: Fast BI, reports, dashboards, complex SQL joins.
  • Data Shape: Modeled tables with consistent schemas.
  • Optimization: Distribution and sort keys, RA3 for managed storage.
Example
Sales and customer dimension tables loaded nightly into Redshift for dashboards and slice-and-dice queries.
Data Lake (S3)
Raw and semi-structured data at scale; flexible for many analytics engines
  • Purpose: Store raw events, logs, backups, and ML training data.
  • Data Shape: Files in Parquet/ORC/JSON; often partitioned by date or region.
  • Optimization: Partitioning and Glue Data Catalog to avoid full scans.
Example
Clickstream events stored as partitioned Parquet files, queried directly with Athena or Spectrum for exploration.
Common Mistake

Moving all raw S3 data into Redshift without partitioning or curation leads to high storage and compute costs and slow loads. Prefer Spectrum to query in place when possible.

Key Takeaway

Choose the lake for scale and flexibility; choose the warehouse for curated performance.
