Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Concurrency Scaling
How Concurrency Scaling helps
Temporary capacity for read-heavy bursts
When to use
  • High concurrency of read queries during business hours.
  • Ad-hoc analyst workloads causing queueing.
What it does
Automatically adds transient compute to serve read queries and returns to baseline when the burst subsides.
Limits & monitoring
You receive daily free credits; monitor credit consumption and latency to avoid unexpected costs.
Misconception
It is not intended to speed long-running writes or ETL; it targets concurrent read queries only.
Key Takeaway

Use Concurrency Scaling to absorb read spikes and maintain consistent performance; track credit use and workload fit.
