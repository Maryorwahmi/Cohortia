Govern with Lake Formation
Centralized, fine-grained access control for data lakes
Capabilities
  • Table, column, and row-level permissions.
  • Central enforcement across Athena, EMR, and Redshift Spectrum.
  • Register S3 locations to manage access centrally.
Example
Grant marketing role SELECT on demographic columns but deny access to financial columns. Remove direct S3 access to force queries through Lake Formation policies.
Pitfall

Relying solely on IAM and S3 policies for fine-grained analytics access becomes unmanageable at scale. Centralize permissions in Lake Formation instead.

Key Takeaway

Use Lake Formation to reduce policy sprawl and enforce least-privilege access across analytics services.
