Aurora Global Database for Multi-Region DR
Cross-region replication with fast promotion and global read capabilities.
How it works
  • One primary region replicates to up to five secondary regions using dedicated replication infrastructure.
  • Typical replication latency is under a second, supporting low RPO.
  • Secondary can be promoted to primary in less than a minute, aiding RTO targets.
Design and operational considerations
Plan for cross-region costs, application failover logic, DNS updates or connection rerouting, and regular failover rehearsals to validate your recovery runbook.
Example
A financial app that requires sub-minute RTO promotes a secondary region to restore writes while clients update connection targets.
