Design Decisions: Compare RDS, Read Replicas, and Aurora
Make choices by mapping requirements to service characteristics.
Option: Standard RDS + Multi-AZ
Provides automatic failover within a region but no read scaling. Good for transactional durability without heavy read traffic.
Option: RDS with Read Replicas
Adds read scaling but replication is asynchronous. Use when eventual consistency is acceptable and you need more read throughput.
Option: Aurora (cluster or global)
Shared storage and fast replica sync make it ideal for near-real-time reads and multi-region disaster recovery when low RTO/RPO matter.
Practical question
Given 90% read traffic and 10% writes, which combination minimizes cost while meeting near-real-time consistency? Consider Aurora replicas with custom endpoints to separate reporting and application reads.
