Aurora Read Scaling and Custom Endpoints
Route different workloads to targeted replica groups.
Setup flow
  1. Create replicas sized for their workload.
  2. Define a custom endpoint and assign specific replicas as members.
  3. Point reporting or analytical clients to the custom endpoint to isolate load.
Example
A reporting endpoint routes heavy analytical queries to two large replicas, preventing impact on application-facing replicas.
Operational checks
Monitor CPU, connections, and lag for each endpoint group and scale replica instances when necessary.
