Common Mistakes and Mitigations
Practical checks to avoid common operational failures.
Top mistakes
  • Reactive scaling without capacity forecasts.
  • Assuming Multi-AZ provides read scaling.
  • Not monitoring replication lag or slow queries.
Mitigations
  • Plan growth and test scale events with realistic load.
  • Separate read and write endpoints and use custom endpoints for work isolation.
  • Enforce security best practices and encryption in transit and at rest.
