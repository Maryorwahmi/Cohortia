Comparison
Provisioned vs On-demand
Provisioned Capacity
Specify RCUs/WCUs. Use Auto Scaling to adjust in response to metrics.
On-Demand Capacity
Pay per request; ideal for unpredictable or spiky traffic; no upfront planning.
Operational guidance
  • Start with on-demand for new or variable apps to avoid throttling risk.
  • Switch to provisioned with Auto Scaling when traffic stabilizes to reduce costs.
  • Set Auto Scaling target utilization to balance performance and cost.
Common mistake
Not enabling Auto Scaling on provisioned mode, which can lead to unexpected throttling during traffic increases.
Key Takeaway

Match capacity mode to workload predictability; use Auto Scaling to automate safe adjustments for provisioned workloads.
