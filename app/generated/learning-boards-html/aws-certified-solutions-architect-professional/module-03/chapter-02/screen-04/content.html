Core Concept
Why use DynamoDB
DynamoDB excels at predictable, single-item access at massive scale. It is fully managed and serverless from the architect's perspective.
Concrete examples
  • Gaming leaderboards with high write and read throughput
  • IoT telemetry ingest with millions of writes per second
  • User profiles where single-key lookups are common
Key Takeaway

DynamoDB is chosen for scale and low-latency single-item access; model your data for the queries you need.

Denormalization & Access Patterns
DynamoDB encourages denormalized designs. Plan queries first, then structure tables and items to serve those queries efficiently.
Pattern: Single-item lookup
Primary key: userId. Retrieves a user's profile in one read.
Pattern: Time-ordered items
Partition by deviceId, sort by timestamp to query recent telemetry.
Misconception: DynamoDB cannot be consistent. Reality: strong consistency is available for reads when required, but GSIs are eventually consistent by default and Global Tables use last-writer-wins for conflicts.
Analogy
Think of DynamoDB as a tailored index for the queries you expect. Instead of normalizing into many tables and joining, you prepare optimized materialized views per query.
