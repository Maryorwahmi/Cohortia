Global Tables
Multi-region replication with last-writer-wins; plan per-replica capacity and conflict handling.
Capacity & Auto Scaling
On-demand for spikes, provisioned + Auto Scaling for predictable workloads to control costs.
Streams & TTL
Use Streams for event-driven processing and TTL for automatic expiry of transient data.
Final takeaway
Combine correct key design, appropriate indexes, capacity strategy, caching, streams, TTL, and Global Tables to build resilient, low-latency, globally available services with DynamoDB.
