Definition
Time To Live (TTL)
TTL removes items after the epoch time in a designated attribute passes, helping manage lifecycle and storage cost for temporary data.
Example
Set lastActive to current epoch + 86400 to expire inactive sessions after 24 hours.
Operational notes
  • Enable TTL on the table specifying the attribute name holding the expiry epoch.
  • Deletions are asynchronous—do not assume immediate removal for business rules.
  • Persist items elsewhere if you require audit or legal retention beyond TTL window.
Key Takeaway

Use TTL for ephemeral data cleanup to reduce cost, but do not rely on it for immediate enforcement or long-term archives.
