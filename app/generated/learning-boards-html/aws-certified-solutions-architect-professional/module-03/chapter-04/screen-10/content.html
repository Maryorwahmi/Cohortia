Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Kinesis Shards
Shard capacity
Each shard supplies a fixed amount of write and read throughput. Aggregate shard capacity defines total stream throughput.
Scaling patterns
  • Monitor PutRecord throttles and iterator age to detect saturation.
  • Scale shards before peaks when possible, or automate with alarms and a resharding process.
  • Avoid hot shards by distributing partition keys across shards.
Concrete example
Two shards provisioned; a spike of writes exceeds per-shard limit causing throttles. Solution: add shards ahead of expected load or buffer producers.
Misconception
Auto-scaling removes the need for monitoring. In reality, proactive metrics and hot key detection remain essential.
Key Takeaway
Design for peak shard throughput, automate scaling, and avoid partition-key hot spots to maintain steady ingestion.
