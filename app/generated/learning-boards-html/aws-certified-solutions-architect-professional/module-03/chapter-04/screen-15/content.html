Operational Best Practices
Key actions to keep pipelines stable, secure, and cost-effective.
Cost control
Combine instance fleets, Spot, and managed scaling. Validate workloads for Spot suitability before wide adoption.
Monitoring
Track shard metrics, Glue crawler runs, job durations, and EMR scaling events to detect hot spots and bottlenecks early.
Security
Use IAM roles for service access, enable encryption for S3 and streams, and integrate with Lake Formation for fine-grained access.
Final thought
Balance automation and monitoring: managed services reduce ops, but reliable pipelines require instrumentation, secure roles, and planned cost strategies.
