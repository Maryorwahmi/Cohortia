Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Key Concepts
Structured Troubleshooting
Follow the identify-isolate-diagnose-resolve-verify loop to avoid ad hoc changes and preserve evidence.
CloudWatch Logs Insights
Write parse and filter queries to reduce millions of log lines into target request IDs and metrics.
AWS X-Ray
Service maps and trace segments reveal which dependency adds latency or errors to a request.
Keep these three tools in your initial triage flow: Logs Insights to find candidates, X-Ray to confirm timing, Flow Logs for network evidence. Use EventBridge and AWS Config for automation and historical context, and store procedural knowledge in runbooks.
