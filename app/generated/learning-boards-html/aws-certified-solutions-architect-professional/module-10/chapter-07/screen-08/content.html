Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › VPC Flow Logs
What flow logs record
  • Source and destination IPs and ports
  • Protocol, action (ACCEPT or REJECT), bytes and packets
  • Network interface IDs and timestamps for correlation
Common error: not enabling REJECT logging; without REJECT entries you cannot confirm whether traffic was blocked.
CLI example to create flow logs
aws ec2 create-flow-logs \ --resource-ids vpc-0abcdef1234567890 \ --resource-type VPC \ --traffic-type ALL \ --log-destination-type cloud-watch-logs \ --log-group-name /aws/vpc/flowlogs/my-vpc-flow-log-group \ --iam-role-arn arn:aws:iam::123456789012:role/FlowLogRole
Key Takeaway

Enable flow logs and capture REJECT to obtain definitive evidence of network-level blocking during troubleshooting.
