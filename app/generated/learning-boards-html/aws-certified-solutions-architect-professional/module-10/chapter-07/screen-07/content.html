Service map and trace anatomy
Service map shows nodes and edges with latency and error rate badges. Traces break down request time into segments and subsegments for each operation or SDK call.
Segments
Represent a service or resource; include annotations and metadata for searchability.
Subsegments
Represent individual calls inside a segment — instrument those to reveal internal latency.
Practical guidance
  • Enable active tracing for services in the critical path during incidents to capture enough traces.
  • Instrument external calls with custom subsegments to see their exact contribution to latency.
  • Use service map heat to prioritize which dependencies to inspect first.
Key Takeaway

Traces show time distribution across services; combine with Logs Insights results to form a complete diagnosis.
