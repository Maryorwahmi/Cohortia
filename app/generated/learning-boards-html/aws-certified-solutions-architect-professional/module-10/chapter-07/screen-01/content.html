Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Overview
Troubleshooting Methodologies & Tools
Systematic approaches and AWS diagnostics to find and fix complex operational issues.
In this lesson
You will map each phase of the troubleshooting process to specific AWS tools and learn practical queries and runbook steps to accelerate diagnosis.
Why it matters
A reproducible process prevents firefighting, reduces MTTR, and makes post-incident analysis actionable.
Coming up
Methodology
Identify, isolate, diagnose, resolve, verify — repeatable triage steps for complex systems.
Logs & Traces
CloudWatch Logs Insights and X-Ray help move from symptoms to a specific component or span.
Network & Automation
VPC Flow Logs reveal network accepts/rejects; EventBridge and Config enable automation and change history.
Visual concept
Tools linked to phases
Identify: CloudWatch alarms — Isolate: Logs Insights filters — Diagnose: X-Ray traces — Verify: Flow Logs & Config
Use correlation IDs and structured JSON logs to trace requests across services without guesswork.
