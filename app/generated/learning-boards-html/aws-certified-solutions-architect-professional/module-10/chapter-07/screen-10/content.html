Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Runbooks & Playbooks
What to include in a runbook
  • A concise symptom description and how to reproduce it.
  • Exact Logs Insights and Flow Logs queries to gather evidence.
  • Safe remediation steps with rollback instructions and who to notify.
  • Verification queries and post-incident cleanup instructions.
Pro tip: keep runbooks versioned and test them during incident drills so they remain reliable under pressure.
Example checklist (SSH connectivity)
  1. Confirm ALB or instance security group allows source IP.
  2. Check NACLs for subnet-level denies.
  3. Query VPC Flow Logs for REJECT entries on port 22.
  4. Fix security group rule and verify successful SSH connection.
Key Takeaway

Runbooks reduce cognitive load during incidents and ensure safe, repeatable remediation steps with verifiable outcomes.
