Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Quick Reference
Capture Evidence
Collect logs, traces, and flow logs before changing any configuration.
Apply Minimal Fixes
Prefer targeted changes with rollback steps to reduce blast radius.
Record & Improve
Update runbooks and playbooks with the exact queries and steps used during the incident.
These three habits — evidence, minimal fixes, and documentation — make troubleshooting efficient and improve operational maturity over time.
