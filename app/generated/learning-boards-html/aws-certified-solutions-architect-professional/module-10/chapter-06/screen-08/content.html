Definition
Virtual Interfaces & BGP
Public VIF vs Private VIF; BGP for route exchange.
Public VIF
Used to access AWS public services over the Direct Connect link.
Private VIF
Connects your on-premises network privately to your VPCs via BGP route exchange.
Misconfiguration
Attaching a VIF without correct BGP prefixes or to a mismatched region can cause routing failures.
Operational Check
Validate BGP Sessions
Confirm adjacency, route learning, and absence of flaps before moving production traffic.
Example
Create a Private VIF for a replication pipeline so on-premises servers learn VPC prefixes via BGP and send traffic directly over Direct Connect.
Check Your Understanding
If routes are missing after VIF creation, verify BGP advertisements and the Direct Connect gateway's regional reachability.
