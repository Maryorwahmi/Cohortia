Architecture
Transit Gateway Basics
Central hub to connect VPCs, VPNs, and Direct Connect.
Attachments & Route Tables
Create attachments for each VPC or VPN and associate/propagate them to route tables to control traffic flow and isolation.
Inter-Region Peering
Peer Transit Gateways across regions to enable global connectivity while preserving regional routing controls.
Pro Tip
Plan for throughput limits per attachment and attach Direct Connect to multiple gateways for redundancy when needed.
Key Takeaway

Transit Gateway simplifies multi-VPC networks but requires explicit routing and capacity planning to meet performance goals.

Design Snapshot
Hub-and-Spoke
Attach each VPC once; use route tables to control reachability between spokes.
Attach Direct Connect
Connect Direct Connect to Transit Gateway to propagate on-premises routes to VPCs efficiently.
Test & Validate
After attachment, propagate routes and use ping and iperf3 to verify connectivity and throughput.
