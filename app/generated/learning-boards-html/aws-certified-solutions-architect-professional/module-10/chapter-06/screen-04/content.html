Core Concept
VPC Network Design
Subnets, routing, and AZ placement affect latency and resilience.
Principles
  • Place tightly coupled components in same AZ to reduce latency and avoid inter-AZ hops.
  • Keep routing paths simple to avoid asymmetric routes and packet loss.
  • Balance performance with fault tolerance; use multi-AZ where needed.
Example Scenario
A caching layer and application server that exchange many small requests should be in the same AZ private subnet with direct routes to minimize latency.
Common Mistake
Assuming inter-AZ latency is zero and not measuring after a topology change.
Key Takeaway

Design subnets and AZ placement to match application coupling and validate with pings and throughput tests.

Topology Snapshot
Private Subnet
Application servers and caches with private routing and no internet exposure.
Public Subnet
Load balancers and public endpoints that front the application.
Check Your Understanding
After a topology change, measure latency between service pairs and confirm routing tables do not introduce extra hops or asymmetric paths.
