Design
Match topology and AZ placement to application coupling and availability needs.
Connectivity
Choose Direct Connect for high-bandwidth predictable links; use VPN for backups or temporary needs.
Measure
Validate changes with iperf3 and VPC Flow Logs; iterate until targets are met.
Final Thought

Architecture is trade-offs: aim for predictable performance, design for scale with Transit Gateway, and always validate with real tests.
