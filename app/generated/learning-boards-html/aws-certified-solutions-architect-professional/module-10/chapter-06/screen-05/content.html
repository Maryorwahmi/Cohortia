Comparison
Direct Connect
Private dedicated link with Virtual Interfaces and BGP.
  • Consistent, low-latency connectivity for large transfers and real-time apps.
  • Supports dedicated and hosted connections; use Private VIF for VPC access.
  • Requires BGP route exchange with AWS and planning for throughput limits.
Concrete Example
A migration of petabytes and real-time replication should use a dedicated 10 Gbps Direct Connect and a Private VIF to the VPC.
Comparison
Site-to-Site VPN
Encrypted connection over the public internet, flexible and quick to set up.
  • Good for short-term or backup connectivity; subject to internet variability.
  • Quick deployment, useful while Direct Connect is provisioned or as redundancy.
  • Not ideal for sustained petabyte transfers or strict low-latency needs.
Concrete Example
Use VPN tunnels as a backup path for important workloads while primary traffic uses Direct Connect.
Check Your Understanding
If you need consistent low-latency for production data streams, prefer Direct Connect and reserve VPN for redundancy. Validate with throughput tests after configuration.
