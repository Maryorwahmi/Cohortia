Definition
Enhanced Networking
ENA and SR-IOV improve PPS and latency by reducing hypervisor overhead.
Enable on supported instance types and validate using packet-rate measurements and iperf3 throughput tests.
Definition
Jumbo Frames
MTU of 9001 to reduce protocol overhead and improve throughput for large transfers.
Operational Note
All devices along the path, including on-premises routers and Direct Connect circuits, must support the MTU for success.
Key Takeaway

Use enhanced networking and Jumbo Frames to raise throughput, only after verifying end-to-end support and measuring improvement with iperf3.
