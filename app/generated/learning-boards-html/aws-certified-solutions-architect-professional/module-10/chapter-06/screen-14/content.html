Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Key Concepts
VPC Network Design
Subnetting, routing, and AZ placement shape latency and cost; match them to application coupling.
Direct Connect & VIFs
Use Private VIFs and BGP for private VPC access; prefer Direct Connect for predictable high-throughput links.
Transit Gateway
Central hub for multi-VPC and hybrid connectivity; design route propagation and capacity carefully.
Summary Takeaway

Measure with iperf3 and Flow Logs, validate each change, and design networks to match application coupling, throughput needs, and fault domains.
