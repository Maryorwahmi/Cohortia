Architecture
Cross-account observability
Centralize metrics and logs for compliance, correlation, and simpler dashboards.
Patterns
  • Metric sharing or PutMetricData into a monitoring account
  • Stream logs with Kinesis Data Firehose into central S3 or CloudWatch Logs
  • Build central dashboards and alert routing in the monitoring account
Design considerations
  • Storage costs vs. diagnostic completeness when aggregating raw logs
  • Region-specific compliance or latency constraints
  • Retain local alarms for fast response; use the monitoring account for correlation
Scenario
Security team needs failed login counts across Prod and Staging. Use cross-account metric sharing to a Security monitoring account and build a single dashboard.
