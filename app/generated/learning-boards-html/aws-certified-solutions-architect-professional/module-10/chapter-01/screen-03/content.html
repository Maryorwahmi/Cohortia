Practical Milestones
Concrete verification steps to prove competency
Publish & verify
Publish a custom metric and observe it in CloudWatch.
Confirm namespace, dimensions, and recent datapoints appear.
Metric Math
Create an expression to calculate error rate or combined latency.
Visualize the expression on a graph for 1-5 minutes.
Cross-account
Share metrics/logs to a monitoring account and build a central dashboard.
Confirm the monitoring account shows source account identifiers.
Logs Insights
Run a query to find requests with latency > 500 ms and view the latest entries.
Use parsed fields to reduce search time.
