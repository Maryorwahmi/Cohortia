Composite Alarms
Group simple alarms into rules that represent meaningful failures.
Rule example
ALARM("HighProcessingTimeAlarm") OR ALARM("HighErrorRateAlarm") -> triggers CriticalOrderProcessorIssues.
Test composite alarms by forcing the underlying simple alarms into ALARM state to validate notification behavior and escalation paths.
Benefits
  • Reduce noisy alerts by requiring multiple signals
  • Encode operational judgment in alarm rules
  • Simplify on-call response with higher-confidence alerts
Common mistake
Relying on a single composite rule for every situation. Instead, create targeted composites for critical workflows.
