Learning Objectives
Master observability practices in CloudWatch for advanced architectures
01
Articulate observability importance

Explain why metrics, logs, and traces are required to find bottlenecks and prioritize work. You will link telemetry types to troubleshooting workflows so your team focuses on high-impact signals.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Configure advanced CloudWatch features

Publish custom metrics, use metric math to derive rates and ratios, and create composite alarms to reduce noise and trigger on meaningful system states.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement cross-account observability

Design a monitoring account pattern to aggregate metrics and logs from multiple source accounts, simplifying compliance and centralized alerting while controlling costs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Use Logs Insights and dashboards

Write Logs Insights queries against structured logs to find high-latency requests and design dashboards that convey trends, alarms, and context to different audiences.
