Core Concept
Why comprehensive monitoring matters
Monitoring turns unknowns into measurable signals you can act on.
What to track
  • Business indicators (throughput, processed items)
  • Application metrics (latency, error counts)
  • Infrastructure metrics (CPU, memory) for resource issues
Misconception
Relying only on built-in metrics misses application-level failures. Default alarms often produce false positives.
Key Takeaway

Design telemetry to answer real incident questions; prefer derived signals to raw counts when traffic varies.

Analogy: medical diagnostics
Just as doctors use vitals, blood tests, and imaging, architects combine metrics, logs, and traces to diagnose system health.
Scenario card
Unexpected latency during peak
Use a custom latency metric and Logs Insights to determine whether a downstream database or an internal API caused the spike.
Common mistake
Alerting on raw error counts without context leads to alert storms during traffic spikes.
Pro tip
Choose dimensions that slice meaningful operational groups like service or endpoint, not high-cardinality identifiers.
