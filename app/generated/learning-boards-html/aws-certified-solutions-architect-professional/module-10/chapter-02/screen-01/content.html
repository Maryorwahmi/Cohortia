Advanced EC2 Performance Tuning
Understand how instance types, EBS, networking, placement, and scaling combine to meet demanding workloads.
In this lesson
Choose the right instance family, provision EBS for IOPS and throughput, enable ENA/SR-IOV, and tune Auto Scaling for performance.
Why it matters
Small configuration mistakes can limit I/O, network throughput, or scaling responsiveness, causing expensive performance incidents.
Explore Key Topics
Instance Families
C, R, M, I, D, and accelerated families map to compute, memory, general, I/O, storage, and GPU needs.
EBS Choices
gp3 separates IOPS from size; io2 and Block Express deliver dedicated high IOPS and low latency.
Networking & Scaling
ENA and SR-IOV for high packet rates; placement groups and target tracking or predictive scaling for performance.
