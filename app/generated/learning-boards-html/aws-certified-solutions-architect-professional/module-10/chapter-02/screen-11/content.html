Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › EBS Diagnostics
Key metrics
Understand the signals that point to storage limits.
avgqu-sz
Average queue depth. Persistent growth means the backend cannot keep up with requests.
await / svctm
Average service time per I/O. Rising values indicate increasing latency on the storage path.
Throughput vs latency
High throughput with high latency still harms user experience; prioritize reducing latency for interactive workloads.
Key Takeaway

Correlate fio-generated load with iostat to identify whether to increase IOPS, move to io2 Block Express, or upgrade instance EBS bandwidth.

Concrete example
Running fio with a high random write mix produced avgqu-sz values near 20 and await above acceptable thresholds. Moving to gp3 with higher provisioned IOPS reduced queue depth and restored service times to acceptable levels.
Misconception
Seeing high absolute throughput doesn't guarantee low latency. Interactive applications often require low service time more than raw MB/s.
Check your understanding
If iostat shows high avgqu-sz and rising await, what is the immediate implication? (Answer: the storage path is saturated and latency will impact the application.)
