Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › ENA & SR-IOV
Enable and verify
Driver checks and simple commands give confidence that networking will perform as expected.
ENA
High bandwidth adapter used by modern instance types. Verify with ethtool reporting the ena driver.
SR-IOV
Provides virtual functions to bypass the hypervisor. Useful for the lowest latency and highest packet rates when supported.
Verification steps
Run ethtool -i eth0 and lspci -vv to confirm driver and SR-IOV capabilities; include this in AMI migration checklists.
Common mistake

Assuming security group rules are the only network limiter. Missing or wrong drivers can silently cap packet rates and increase latency.

Concrete example
After migrating to a custom AMI, ethtool reported a generic driver rather than ena. Packet rates were low until the ENA driver was reinstalled, restoring expected throughput.
Pro tip
Automate driver verification in your build pipeline so each AMI includes tests that confirm ENA and SR-IOV where required.
Check your understanding
If an instance supports ENA but your network benchmarks are poor after migration, what is a likely first check? (Answer: verify ENA driver and kernel modules on the instance.)
