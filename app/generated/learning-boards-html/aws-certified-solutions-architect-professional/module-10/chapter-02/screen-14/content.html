Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › What to Remember
EC2 Instance Types
Select family and processor to match compute, memory, and bandwidth needs. Validate published bandwidth limits.
EBS Volumes
Use gp3 for flexible provisioning; escalate to io1/io2 or io2 Block Express for guaranteed low-latency IOPS.
ENA & SR-IOV
Enable and verify drivers to achieve high packet rates and low latency; include driver checks in AMI validation.
Takeaway
Performance depends on the entire stack. Match resources, provision appropriately, validate with benchmarks, and monitor with agent-level metrics.
