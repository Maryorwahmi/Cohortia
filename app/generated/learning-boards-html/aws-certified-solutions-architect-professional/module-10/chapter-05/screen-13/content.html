Signed URLs & Cookies
Signed URLs grant time-limited access to single resources; signed cookies are better for multiple resources or directories.
Best practice: keep the private key secure, rotate keys, and test expiry behavior in a safe environment.
AWS WAF
Attach WAF to CloudFront distributions to block SQL injection, cross-site scripting, or high-volume bot traffic before it reaches your origin.
Layered defense: OAC + Signed URLs/Cookies + WAF reduces attack surface and prevents unauthorized origin access.
