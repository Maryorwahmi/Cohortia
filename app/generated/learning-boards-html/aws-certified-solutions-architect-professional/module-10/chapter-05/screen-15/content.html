Cache smart
Minimal cache keys, appropriate TTLs, versioned URLs over invalidation.
Edge compute
Use Lambda@Edge for small, latency-sensitive transformations and auth logic.
Right layer
CloudFront = caching & app-layer logic. Global Accelerator = network path & static IPs.
Final takeaway
Combine the tools thoughtfully: caching reduces load and latency, and optimized networks reduce jitter for dynamic traffic.
