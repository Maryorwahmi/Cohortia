Module 10 › Chapter 10.3 › Multi‑AZ vs Read Replicas
Multi‑AZ
Synchronous standby for high availability and automatic failover.
When to use
Critical production databases that must remain available and durable during infrastructure failures.
What it provides
Automatic failover to standby instance; not designed for read scaling.
Read Replicas
Asynchronous copies to scale read throughput across connections.
When to use
Read-heavy workloads: reporting, dashboards, or user-facing reads with relaxed consistency requirements.
What to monitor
Replication lag and read query consistency; route critical reads to primary.
Example decision
Enable Multi‑AZ on transactional master databases for HA; add read replicas for scaling read-heavy endpoints like product listings. Combine both when you need both availability and read scaling, and plan routing and failover behavior carefully.
