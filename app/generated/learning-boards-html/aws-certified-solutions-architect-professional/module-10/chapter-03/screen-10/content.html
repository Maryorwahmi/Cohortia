Module 10 › Chapter 10.3 › DynamoDB Capacity
Provisioned Capacity
Set RCUs and WCUs; use Auto Scaling for predictable workloads to control cost.
When to choose
Predictable traffic patterns with sustained high throughput where reserved capacity is cheaper.
On‑Demand Capacity
Automatic scaling per request, ideal for unpredictable workloads and early-stage apps.
Caveat
More expensive for sustained, high throughput. Use for bursty or unknown traffic.
Practical checklist
  • Analyze historical traffic patterns to choose mode.
  • When using provisioned, set Auto Scaling min/max targets.
  • Monitor Consumed RCUs/WCUs and throttling metrics to adjust configuration.
