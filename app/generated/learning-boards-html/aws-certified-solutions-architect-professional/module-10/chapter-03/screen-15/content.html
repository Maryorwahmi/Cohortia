GSI vs LSI
GSI for cross‑partition queries; LSI for same‑partition alternative sort keys.
Capacity Modes
Provisioned for predictability; on‑demand for unpredictable, bursty traffic.
DAX & Caching
Cache hot reads to reduce table load and improve latency.
Final Takeaway

Design for your access patterns, measure with observability tools, and choose the right combination of indexes, capacity mode, caching, and pooling to meet latency and cost goals.
