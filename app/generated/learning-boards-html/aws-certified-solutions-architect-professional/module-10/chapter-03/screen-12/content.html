Module 10 › Chapter 10.3 › Hot Partitions
What causes hotspots?
Low-cardinality partition keys or monotonically increasing keys concentrate traffic on few partitions.
Detection
Look for throttling spikes on specific partition key values and uneven Consumed RCU/WCU metrics across keys.
Mitigation
  • Choose high-cardinality partition keys.
  • Add random suffixes or hashed prefixes.
  • Implement sharding in the application layer for writes.
  • Cache hot items with DAX or ElastiCache for reads.
Key Takeaway

Design partition keys for even distribution first. Use caching or sharding when access patterns concentrate traffic on a few keys.
