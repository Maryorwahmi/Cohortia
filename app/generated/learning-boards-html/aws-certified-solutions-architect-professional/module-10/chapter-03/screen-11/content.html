Module 10 › Chapter 10.3 › DAX
What DAX does
An in-memory cache that serves reads with microsecond latency and reduces consumed RCUs on your table.
Java conceptual snippet
AmazonDynamoDB daxClient = AmazonDaxClientBuilder.standard()
    .withRegion(Regions.US_EAST_1)
    .withEndpointConfiguration("dax-cluster-endpoint:8111")
    .build();

GetItemRequest request = new GetItemRequest()
    .withTableName("MyTable")
    .withKey(Collections.singletonMap("id", new AttributeValue().withS("123")));

GetItemResult result = daxClient.getItem(request);
Operational notes
DAX is best for read-heavy patterns with high cache hit rates. Consider warm-up and consistency needs.
Key Takeaway

DAX reduces read latency and consumed RCUs for hot items, but maintain proper key design and caching warm-up plans.
