Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Managing backpressure and retries
Key mechanisms
Queues provide buffering, throttles limit ingress, and exponential backoff with jitter prevents synchronized retry storms. Combine them for robust backpressure control.
Queues (SQS): decouple producers and consumers so upstream continues producing while downstream lags.
Throttling: limit request rates at gateways or the app to protect service capacity.
Exponential backoff + jitter: increase retry wait and add randomness to spread retry load.
Example: SQS buffer
If InventoryUpdater is slower than OrderProcessor, SQS buffers messages so orders continue to be accepted while inventory lags; this is a form of graceful degradation for inventory freshness.
Common mistake

Retries without backoff synchronize clients and can amplify overload; always add jitter to spreads retries over time.
