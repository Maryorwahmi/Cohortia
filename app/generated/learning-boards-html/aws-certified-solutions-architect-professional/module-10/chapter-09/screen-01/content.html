Resilience & Performance under Load

Design systems that survive spikes and recover safely. Validate with load testing and chaos engineering, then apply patterns to avoid cascading failures.

In this lesson

How to test for load, inject controlled faults, and implement resilience patterns like circuit breakers, bulkheads, and graceful degradation.

Why it matters

Traffic spikes and cascading failures cause outages. Proactive testing and patterns preserve user experience and limit downtime.

Approach
  • Validate capacity with distributed load testing solutions.
  • Run small chaos experiments and automate them for continuous assurance.
  • Use backpressure, queues, and isolation patterns to protect critical paths.
Ready to explore
