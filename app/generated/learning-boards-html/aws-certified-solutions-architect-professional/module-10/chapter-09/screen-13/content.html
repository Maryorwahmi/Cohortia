Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Operationalizing Resilience
Timeline
Dev
Unit tests, integration tests, quick performance checks.
Staging
Non-destructive chaos experiments and sustained load runs.
Pre-Prod
Full-scale load tests, integration with monitoring and alarms.
Prod
Scheduled low-blast experiments with stop conditions and observability.
Store experiment templates in version control, automate runs in CI/CD, and collect metrics to build a resilience baseline you can compare after each change.
Key Takeaway

Turn testing and fault injection into routine checks so you catch regressions early and keep reliability measurable.
