Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Bulkheads & resource isolation
Architecture

Compartmentalize resources to limit blast radius

Use separate scaling groups, thread pools, or VPCs to ensure a noisy neighbor cannot exhaust shared resources. Prioritize isolation of critical services.

Separate Auto Scaling Groups for payment and analytics reduce cross-impact during spikes.
Use quotas and admission control to prevent a service from consuming unbounded resources.
Example
Place critical microservices on separate instance groups and assign dedicated thread pools to avoid thread starvation when other services queue work aggressively.
Design note

Bulkheads require capacity planning; isolate what matters and accept shared resources for less-critical components to reduce cost.
