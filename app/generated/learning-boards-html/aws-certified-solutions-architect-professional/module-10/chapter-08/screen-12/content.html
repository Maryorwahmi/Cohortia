Operational Practice
Idle Resources & FinOps
Common idle resources
  • Unattached EBS volumes and AMIs kept without owners.
  • Development instances running 24/7 instead of scheduled times.
  • Idle RDS or read replicas kept for testing but not used.
FinOps checks
Apply budgets and alerts, enforce cost allocation tags, schedule non-production shutdowns, and assign cost owners to resource groups. Run monthly reviews and act on orphaned resources.
Key Takeaway

Governance and automation convert recommendations into sustained savings; make cost accountability part of operations.
