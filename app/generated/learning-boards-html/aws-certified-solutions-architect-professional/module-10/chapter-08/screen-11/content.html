Problem Area
Data Transfer Costs
Common sources
  • Cross-AZ traffic for NAT Gateways, ALBs, or inter-AZ replication.
  • Cross-region replication for S3 or database replication.
  • Egress to the internet for large datasets or APIs.
Mitigations include using VPC endpoints to keep traffic inside AWS backbone, co-locating services to reduce AZ crossings, and batching or scheduling replication to avoid continuous transfer costs.
Checklist
  1. Audit transfer line items in Cost Explorer.
  2. Identify cross-AZ and cross-region flows.
  3. Apply VPC endpoints where applicable.
Key Takeaway

Design networks and replication for locality; small design choices can eliminate recurring transfer charges.
