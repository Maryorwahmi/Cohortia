Purchasing Options at a Glance
On-Demand
Flexibility
Pay as you go; highest per-hour cost; ideal for unpredictable needs.
Reserved Instances
Commitment
Discounts up to 72% for committing to instance types and terms; best for stable workloads.
Savings Plans
Flexible commitment
Commit to $/hour across compute; applies to EC2, Fargate, Lambda and multiple families.
Spot
Interruptible
Deep discounts up to 90% for workloads tolerant of interruptions.
Strategy card
Combine models: use Savings Plans or RIs for predictable baseline, On-Demand for unpredictable spikes, and Spot for fault-tolerant tasks. Monitor utilization of commitments to avoid wasted spend.
Key Takeaway

Match purchase model to workload predictability and risk tolerance; combine types to maximize efficiency.
