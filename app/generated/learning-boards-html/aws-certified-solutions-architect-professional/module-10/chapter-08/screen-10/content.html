EBS: gp3 vs gp2
When to choose gp3
  • When you need predictable IOPS or throughput independent of storage size.
  • When database I/O drives cost more than raw capacity.
  • When resizing to gp3 can avoid paying for unused capacity while delivering required performance.
Example
A DB needs 5,000 IOPS but only 500 GiB storage. Choosing gp3 with provisioned IOPS is often more cost-effective than enlarging a gp2 volume to meet IOPS by size.
Key Takeaway

Select EBS types based on I/O characteristics, not just storage capacity, and measure before changing volumes.
