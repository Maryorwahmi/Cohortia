Layered Purchasing Pattern
Pattern example
Baseline covered by Savings Plans, On-Demand for spikes, Spot for batch. Monitor and rebalance as utilization changes.
Baseline
Savings Plans or RIs for steady usage.
Bursts
On-Demand autoscaling for unpredictable spikes.
Batch
Spot Instances for cheap, interruptible compute.
Practical checks
  • Measure baseline hours to size Savings Plans correctly.
  • Set alerts for under-utilized commitments.
  • Design Spot-aware jobs to checkpoint and retry gracefully.
Key Takeaway

Mix purchase options to match workload characteristics and to maximize cost efficiency while maintaining performance.
