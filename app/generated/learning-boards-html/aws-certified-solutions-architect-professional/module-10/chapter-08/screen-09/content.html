S3 Storage Class Selection
Storage class matrix
S3 Standard
Frequent access, low latency, higher cost.
S3 Intelligent-Tiering
Automatic moves between tiers for unknown patterns; small monitoring fee.
Standard-IA / One Zone-IA
Lower storage cost for infrequent access; higher retrieval cost and potential availability trade-offs for One Zone-IA.
Glacier / Deep Archive
Lowest storage cost, high retrieval latency and cost; best for long-term compliance archives.
Example
Compliance logs rarely accessed after 30 days and retained for years should be moved to Glacier Deep Archive for lowest storage cost, accepting retrieval-time trade-offs.
Key Takeaway

Use lifecycle policies to transition objects automatically based on access and retention requirements to lower costs without manual intervention.
