How you'll measure success

Concrete metrics and a simple validation workflow that prevents guesswork.

Metric
Duration & ColdStart

Track percentiles before and after changes, and check logs for explicit cold start messages.

Trace
X-Ray Service Map

Use subsegments to find whether time comes from initialization, external calls, or business logic.

Gateway
Cache hits & Throttles

Confirm caching reduces backend calls and throttling protects resources without harming UX.

Validation workflow

Take a baseline, change one variable, re-run controlled invocations, then compare Duration and traces to determine impact.
