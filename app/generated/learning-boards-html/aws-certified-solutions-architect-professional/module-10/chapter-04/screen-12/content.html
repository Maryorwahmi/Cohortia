Optimize Cold Start and Trace with X-Ray

A procedural checklist you can follow in the console to measure and validate tuning changes.

Step 1: Deploy
Create ColdStartTester, enable Active tracing, and give the role S3 and X-Ray permissions.
Step 2: Baseline
Invoke multiple times, note cold-start logs and Duration percentiles.
Step 3: Tune
Increase memory in steps and compare metrics; optionally add provisioned concurrency and compare again.
Step 4: Trace
Open X-Ray, inspect service map and traces, and look for initialization segments and subsegments.
Key Takeaway

Follow a single-variable test approach, validate with traces, and weigh cost versus latency gains before committing changes.
