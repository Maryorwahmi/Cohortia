Comparison

Reserved concurrency vs unreserved

Reserved
Guarantees capacity for a function; reduces pool for others; good for critical endpoints.
Unreserved
Functions use remaining concurrency; flexible but can be affected by noisy neighbors.
Concrete scenario
Reserve concurrency for a payment processor and set a maximum concurrency on a low-priority ETL Lambda to protect the database.
Monitoring
Watch ConcurrentExecutions, Throttles, and error counts to ensure settings behave as expected.
Common mistake

Over-reserving capacity can cause excessive throttling for the rest of your functions. Reserve only what you need for critical services.
