Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › StackSets
What StackSets deliver
  • Single template deployed to many accounts and regions.
  • Auto-deployment to ensure new accounts receive templates.
  • Consistent baseline enforcement without manual per-account steps.
Use cases
Standard IAM roles, centralized CloudTrail configuration, VPC Flow Logs to a central bucket.
Cautions
A misconfigured template or wrong deployment target can affect many accounts. Always test in a sandbox OU first and validate permission and retain settings.
Deployment options
Choose correct permission model and consider RetainStacksOnAccountRemoval settings to avoid accidental deletions.
