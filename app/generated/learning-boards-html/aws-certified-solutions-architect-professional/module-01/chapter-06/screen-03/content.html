Verification Steps
  1. Provision a sandbox via Account Factory and confirm enrollment in Control Tower.
  2. In the member account, verify CloudTrail and VPC Flow Logs are present and point to a central bucket.
  3. Create a CloudFormation template for a baseline object, for example an auditor IAM role.
  4. Deploy the template as a StackSet with auto-deployment to the target OU and validate automatic delivery to new accounts.
Safety
Test in a non-production OU and verify permission models before wide deployment.
Common mistake
Deploying templates without retention settings or testing can cause mass deletion or drift.
Quick Checklist
  • Account Factory enrolls accounts into Control Tower.
  • Guardrails are preventive or detective (SCPs or Config rules).
  • StackSets use auto-deployment for new OU accounts.
