Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Control Tower vs StackSets
Control Tower
Automates landing zone creation, core accounts, centralized logging, guardrails, and Account Factory for self-service provisioning.
Best for
Foundational governance and standardized account setup.
StackSets
Deploy CloudFormation templates to multiple accounts and regions for consistent, template-defined resources like roles and trails.
Best for
Template-driven baseline resources and fine-grained configurations.
Team guidance
Start with Control Tower to get a secure foundation. Then plan StackSets for any baseline elements Control Tower does not cover or for custom templates your teams require.
