Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Account Lifecycle
Lifecycle steps
Create
Automate with Account Factory or CreateAccount + StackSets.
Customize
Teams add resources within SCP boundaries and guardrails.
Govern
Continuous monitoring via Config, GuardDuty, Security Hub.
Decommission
Terminate resources, archive logs, and close accounts safely.
Design OUs to reflect lifecycle stages. For example, a 'Sandbox' OU for testing StackSets, a 'Workloads' OU for production workloads, and an 'Archive' OU for suspended accounts whose logs and data are retained.
Practical checklist
  • Enable auto-deployment for onboarding OUs.
  • Set RetainStacksOnAccountRemoval to avoid accidental deletions.
  • Archive logs to the central logging account before account closure.
