Account Factory Flow
1. Request
Team requests an account with parameters.
2. Provision
Control Tower creates the account and core resources.
3. Enroll
Account is enrolled into Organizations and OUs.
4. Enforce
Guardrails, logging, and baseline policies apply.
Example: A product team requests a dev account. Account Factory provisions it and it automatically receives a role for security auditing and is enrolled into the 'Workloads' OU so centralized logging applies immediately.
Notes
Account Factory increases speed but not every customization is automatic. Use StackSets for additional baseline resources.
Check your understanding
When would you choose StackSets over Account Factory? Answer: when you need to deploy specific resource templates across multiple accounts and regions that are not covered by Control Tower guardrails.
