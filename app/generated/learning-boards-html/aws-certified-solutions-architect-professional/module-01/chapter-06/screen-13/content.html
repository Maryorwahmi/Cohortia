Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Safety with StackSets
Top mistakes
  • Deploying untested templates to production OUs.
  • Forgetting to set CAPABILITY_NAMED_IAM when IAM resources exist.
  • Enabling auto-deployment without sandbox validation.
  • Not setting RetainStacksOnAccountRemoval and accidentally deleting resources.
Safety steps
Test in sandbox, pilot to a few accounts, verify cross-account buckets and policies, and confirm permission models.
Quick mitigation
If a deployment shows unexpected changes, halt further instances and investigate template logic. Use small scopes for troubleshooting.
Warning

A misconfigured StackSet can cause widespread unintended changes. Always test and verify before broad rollout.
