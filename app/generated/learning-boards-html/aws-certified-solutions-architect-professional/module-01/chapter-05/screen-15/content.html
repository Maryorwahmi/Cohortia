Centralized immutable logs
Send CloudTrail and Flow Logs to a dedicated logging account with immutability to preserve evidence for audits.
Enforceable baselines
Use SCPs to prevent risky APIs, Config rules for detection, and StackSets to deploy controls consistently.
Practiced incident response
Define runbooks, delegate admin roles, integrate findings into ticketing, and test through exercises.
Final takeaway
Combine detection, enforcement, and operational processes to build a secure, auditable environment that scales with your organization.
