Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Incident Response
Runbooks & delegation
Prepare playbooks and assign ownership
Create clear playbooks for common findings and delegate a GuardDuty admin account to centralize management and avoid cross-account confusion.
Example playbook for compromised EC2
  1. Isolate the instance from the network.
  2. Capture EBS snapshots and collect forensic artifacts.
  3. Rotate IAM credentials and keys associated with the instance.
  4. Rebuild from a known-good image and restore data.
Testing & process
Test incident response regularly
Run tabletop exercises and simulated incidents to verify that logs, alerts, runbooks, and delegation work under pressure.
Pro tip
Automate low-risk remediations through Lambda or SSM to reduce human work for repetitive fixes, while keeping analysts focused on high-severity incidents.
Common mistake
Not exercising runbooks and assuming teams will know what to do during a real incident; practice reveals gaps and permission issues.
