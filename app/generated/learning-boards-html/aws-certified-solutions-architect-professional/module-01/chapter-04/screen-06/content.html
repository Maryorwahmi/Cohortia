Explorer workflow
Activate Explorer in the management account, then use filters and groupings to generate reports that attribute costs to accounts, tags, and services.
Step example
  1. Select date range for last month.
  2. Filter by linked accounts in the Development OU.
  3. Group by "Service" to list top spenders.
Forecasting
Use Explorer's forecast to project next month's spend and feed that number into Budget creation to trigger alerts earlier.
Misconception
Explorer reports trends but does not enforce policies; pair it with budgets and tag enforcement for real control.
Example: top services
To identify top three services for the Development OU last month, set the date range, filter to Development-linked accounts, then group by service and sort by cost descending.
Result
Explorer will display services and costs; export CSV if you need to share with finance for chargeback.
Key Takeaway

Explorer turns aggregated billing data into actionable, tag-filtered reports that inform budgets and chargeback.
