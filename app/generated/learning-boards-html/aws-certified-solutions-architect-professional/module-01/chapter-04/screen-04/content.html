Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Consolidated Billing
Definition
Consolidated Billing links member accounts to a management account so usage is billed together, enabling volume pricing tiers to apply across the whole organization.
Analogy
Think of it like a family grocery account: items from everyone are combined and discounts for bulk purchases apply to the whole bill.
Example
Multiple small accounts together reach a lower S3 pricing tier — each account benefits without changing resource ownership.
Key Takeaway

Use consolidated billing to capture volume discounts; combine it with tagging to preserve visibility for chargeback.

Why volume discounts matter
Aggregated usage moves the organization into higher pricing tiers for services that scale with consumption. This reduces per-unit prices for storage, data transfer, and compute hours. It is a direct lever you can control by managing organizational adoption and consolidating billing.
Common mistake
Assuming consolidation equals visibility — without tags and Cost Explorer reports, you won't know which teams used the extra capacity that triggered the discount.
Check your understanding
If a small account purchases an RI, can another account receive the discount? Yes — RI/SP benefits are shared across eligible usage in the organization.
