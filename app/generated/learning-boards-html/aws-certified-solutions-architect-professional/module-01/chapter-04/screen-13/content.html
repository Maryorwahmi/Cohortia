Monthly Governance Checklist
Account & OU mapping
Map accounts to OUs that reflect ownership and billing scopes.
Tag enforcement
Apply mandatory tags and use Config rules or SCPs to enforce compliance.
Explorer reports
Schedule cost reports grouped by account and tag for finance and engineering.
Budgets & alerts
Create budgets with clear owners and response steps.
Anomaly detection
Enable monitors for key services and tune sensitivity.
Commitment planning
Plan RI/SP purchases centrally and track utilization.
Key Takeaway

Operationalize these checklist items monthly: governance only works if it is repeated, measured, and automated where possible.
