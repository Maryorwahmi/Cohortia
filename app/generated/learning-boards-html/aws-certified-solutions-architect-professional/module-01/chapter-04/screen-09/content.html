Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Cost Anomaly Detection
How it works
Monitors learn historical patterns for cost and usage, then score incoming data and alert on statistically significant deviations.
Use cases
Detect sudden spikes in data transfer, unexpected service consumption after deployments, or anomalous regional cost increases.
Tuning
Scope monitors to accounts, services, or tags and adjust sensitivity to reduce false positives while still catching real issues.
Investigation steps
  1. Open the anomaly alert and view the affected accounts and services.
  2. Use Cost Explorer to drill into the time window and resources.
  3. Apply immediate remediation: stop runaway jobs or remove non-critical resources.
Key Takeaway

Anomaly detection catches unexpected patterns early; tune monitors and pair alerts with a quick investigative playbook.
