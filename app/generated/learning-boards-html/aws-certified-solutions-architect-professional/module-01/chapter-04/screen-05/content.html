Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Billing Comparison
Single-Account
Simple billing and reporting by default, but poor isolation and harder quota/security boundaries.
Pros
  • Single invoice, easier initial setup
  • Immediate consolidated visibility
Cons
  • Weak team isolation and quota control
  • Harder to assign ownership for chargeback
Multi-Account with Consolidated Billing
Strong account isolation combined with a single invoice and aggregated discounts — requires deliberate controls for visibility.
Pros
  • Security and quota boundaries per account
  • Aggregate billing unlocks tiered pricing
Requires
  • Enforced tagging and Cost Explorer reports for chargeback
  • Budgets and alerts per account or tag to prevent surprises
Scenario
If Finance needs chargeback plus Engineering needs strong isolation, choose multi-account with consolidated billing, then enforce tags and configure Cost Explorer to attribute costs to business units.
