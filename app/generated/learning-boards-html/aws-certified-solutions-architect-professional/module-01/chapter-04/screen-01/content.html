Cost Management and Billing in a Multi-Account Setup
Practical controls and habits to maintain visibility, accountability, and savings across an AWS Organization.
In this lesson
Learn consolidated billing, Cost Explorer workflows, tagging strategies, budgets and anomaly detection, and how RIs and Savings Plans are shared.
Why it matters
Without organization-wide cost controls you risk wasted spend, poor accountability, and missed volume discounts; these tools give you measurable levers to manage spending.
Explore Key Topics
Consolidated Billing
Aggregate usage for volume discounts and single invoice oversight.
Cost Explorer
Visualize trends, group by tags or accounts, and forecast spend.
Budgets & Alerts
Set thresholds and receive alerts before costs escalate.
Concept map
Consolidated billing connects accounts → Cost Explorer analyzes → Tags allocate → Budgets alert → RIs/SPs share savings.
Example
If several small accounts together reach a pricing tier, consolidated billing applies that lower price to all eligible usage, producing savings without moving workloads.
Common mistake
Neglecting enforced tagging — inconsistent tags break cost allocation and make Cost Explorer reports unreliable.
Coming up
We will define consolidated billing, walk Cost Explorer filters and reports, design tagging rules, set budgets and alerts, and review RI/SP sharing behaviors.
