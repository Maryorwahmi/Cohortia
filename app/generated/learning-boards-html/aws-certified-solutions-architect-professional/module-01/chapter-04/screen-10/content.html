Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › RIs vs Savings Plans
Reserved Instances
Discounts for specific instance attributes over a term. Best when you have steady, predictable instance usage.
Characteristics
  • Instance-type specific
  • Term commitments (1 or 3 years)
Savings Plans
Flexible pricing model committing to a spend rate that covers eligible compute across instance types and regions.
Characteristics
  • Flexible across instance families
  • Commit to $/hour for 1 or 3 years
Organizational sharing
Whether purchased by the management account or a member account, RI and Savings Plan benefits automatically apply to eligible usage across all accounts in the organization, maximizing utilization and reducing wasted commitments.
