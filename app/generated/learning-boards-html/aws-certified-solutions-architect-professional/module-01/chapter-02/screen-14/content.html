Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Service Control Policy (SCP)
An org-level policy that sets the maximum permissions available to member accounts.
IAM Policy
Attached to identities and grants specific permissions; effective only if not blocked by SCPs.
Explicit Deny & Inheritance
An explicit Deny anywhere in the org hierarchy overrides Allows, and policies inherit downward from root to accounts.
Summary Takeaway

Use Deny-focused, condition-scoped SCPs applied at OU level, validate in sandboxes, and keep a tested break-glass plan to maintain manageability.
