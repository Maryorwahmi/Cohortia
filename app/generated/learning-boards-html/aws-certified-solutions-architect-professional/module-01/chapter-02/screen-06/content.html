Effective permissions are the intersection of what IAM allows and what SCPs permit after all denies are applied.
Deny precedence: any Deny removes the action regardless of other Allows.
Use a mental model of transparent overlays. Each SCP adds darker blocked regions. If the same action is blocked on any overlay, the action is unreachable for identities in the account. Design your organization policy topology to place organization-wide safety rules at the top and narrower operational denies lower in the tree.
Check your understanding
If a root-level SCP denies access to a service, can a child OU attach an SCP to allow it again? No — a deny at root cannot be overridden by a child allow. Test this in a sandbox OU to see the effect.
Key Takeaway

Think in overlays: Deny at any level removes permission from the final effective set.
