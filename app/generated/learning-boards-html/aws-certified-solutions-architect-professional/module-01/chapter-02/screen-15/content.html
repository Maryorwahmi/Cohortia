Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › What to Remember
SCP = ceiling
SCPs restrict maximum permissions and cannot grant new rights.
Trace inheritance
Effective permissions are IAM allows intersected with SCP restrictions, with Deny taking precedence.
Test & prepare
Validate in sandbox OUs, use focused denies, and maintain a tested break-glass plan.
Final takeaway

Combine focused SCPs with good testing and recovery practices to govern at scale safely.
