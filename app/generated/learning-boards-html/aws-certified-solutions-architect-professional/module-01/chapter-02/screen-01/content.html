Service Control Policies: Organizational Guardrails

SCPs define the maximum permissions available to accounts beneath where they are attached. They are a filter not a grant. Learning to author and apply SCPs prevents risky actions at scale and supports compliance requirements.

In this lesson
Distinguish SCP vs IAM, author Deny rules, and test safely in a sandbox OU.
Coming up
Examples: region restriction and protecting logging buckets.
Why it matters
Prevent accidental deletions and enforce compliance across accounts.
