Why conditions matter
Conditions prevent broad denies by restricting statements to specific regions, principals, or resource patterns. They reduce false positives and preserve needed operations.
  • aws:RequestedRegion: allowlist or denylist operations by region.
  • Resource ARNs: match buckets and objects separately for S3 protection.
  • Principal ARNs: target specific service accounts or roles.
Test condition logic by attempting both matching and non-matching operations in a sandbox account.
Example: deny outside allowed regions
{
  "Version": "2012-10-17",
  "Statement": [{
    "Sid":"DenySpecificRegions",
    "Effect":"Deny",
    "Action":"*",
    "Resource":"*",
    "Condition":{
      "StringNotEquals":{ "aws:RequestedRegion":["us-east-1","us-west-2","eu-west-1"] }
    }
  }]
}
This denies any action in regions not explicitly listed. Use this when your organization restricts operations to a small set of regions.
Key Takeaway

Conditions make SCPs precise. Write and test them carefully to avoid unintended matches.
