Designing a break-glass process
Decide on an emergency administrative identity or role that is not subject to the restrictive SCPs. Store credentials securely and ensure only a small, authorized team can use them. Test the recovery steps periodically in a sandbox so you can detach a harmful SCP quickly. Maintain a runbook with contact steps, required console actions, and command sequences if you use infrastructure-as-code to manage SCP attachments.
  • Document who can execute break-glass and how to authenticate.
  • Keep CloudTrail and Config enabled to find the source policy quickly.
  • Practice removal in a sandbox to validate timelines and permissions.
Warning
Applying a deny that blocks organizations:* at root can prevent you from managing SCPs. Only do this with an assured, tested recovery plan.
Key Takeaway

Always have a tested break-glass workflow for the management account to recover from accidental organization-wide denials.
