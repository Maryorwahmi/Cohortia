Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Learning Objectives
01
Differentiate SCPs and IAM policies
Explain the roles of each policy type and how they interact.
You will learn to reason why an SCP never grants an action and why an explicit Deny in an SCP blocks IAM allows. A quick mnemonic: SCP = ceiling, IAM = key that fits under the ceiling.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain inheritance and effective permissions
Understand how policies at root, OU, and account combine.
You will map how Deny anywhere in the hierarchy cancels the action and how the account’s effective permission set is the intersection of allowed actions with denies removed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Write SCPs in JSON
Author Deny/Allow statements and use Condition keys safely.
You will practice writing Deny-focused SCPs and using aws:RequestedRegion and resource ARNs to scope effects. A short timeline mnemonic helps: Draft → Test → Attach → Monitor.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply and test SCPs safely
Attach at OU level, use sandbox OUs, and keep a break-glass plan.
You will perform a guided validation exercise: attach a deny policy to a sandbox OU, attempt the blocked action, verify an access denied response, and then detach or adjust the SCP as needed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
