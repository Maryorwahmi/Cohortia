Recognize multi-account IAM challenges
Identify credential sprawl and inconsistent policies and why they increase risk. This helps prioritize centralization efforts.

Explain IAM Identity Center benefits
Describe centralized user/group management, SSO portal, permission sets, and automated role provisioning with examples like DevAccount-001.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Configure users, groups, and permission sets
Create a user and Developers group, author a ReadOnlyAccess permission set, and assign it to DevAccount-001 for testing.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply best practices and audits
Enforce least privilege, enable MFA, integrate with external IdP, and use CloudTrail for periodic audits to maintain secure access.

SINGLE — One portal, one place to manage permissions and audit across accounts.
