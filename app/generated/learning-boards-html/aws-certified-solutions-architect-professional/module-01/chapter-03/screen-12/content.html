Governance

Audit assignments and protect root

Use CloudTrail to capture sign-ins, assignment, and permission set changes. Review these logs on a regular cadence to catch drift.
Step
Export recent CloudTrail events for Identity Center and inspect assignment and sign-in records for sample users.
Warning
Do not use the root account for daily admin tasks; use centrally managed permission sets instead.
Concrete check
Verify an assignment event
Confirm that when you assigned ReadOnlyAccess, a corresponding role appeared in DevAccount-001 and its trust policy references Identity Center.
Key Takeaway

Audit regularly and preserve root user security; Identity Center centralizes policy but governance remains essential.
