Comparison

Human SSO vs programmatic roles

Permission sets
Best for human users, single portal, automated role provisioning, centralized policy management.
Manual cross-account roles
Best for non-human entities, explicit trust relationships, and programmatic assume-role flows.
Concrete scenarios
  • Use permission sets when a developer needs console access across accounts.
  • Use manual cross-account role when an EC2 instance in Account A needs access to resources in Account B.
Question
Is this console access for a person, or programmatic access for a service?
