Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › What is IAM Identity Center
Definition

Central SSO + permission management

IAM Identity Center offers a portal for users to authenticate once and access multiple AWS accounts and supported business applications using assigned permission sets.

Control Plane
Define users, groups, and permission sets in one place and assign them to accounts.
Integration
Connect to an external IdP for corporate credentials to avoid duplicate identities.
Remember: it's more than a login portal. It creates and manages the cross-account roles that enforce permission sets, turning a portal click into an authorized console session.
Concrete example
Developer flow
dev_user logs into the portal, selects DevAccount-001 with the ReadOnlyAccess permission set, and is automatically granted a session with those permissions.
Key Takeaway

Treat Identity Center as your central identity plane for humans; it saves time and standardizes access.
