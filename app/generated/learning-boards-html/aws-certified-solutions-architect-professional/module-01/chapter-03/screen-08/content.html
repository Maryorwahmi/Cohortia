Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Automated Role Creation
Process

Assignment → Role provisioning

Assign a permission set to a user/group for a member account and Identity Center provisions the IAM role and trust policy in that account automatically.
Example
ReadOnlyAccess assigned to Developers for DevAccount-001 results in a read-only role appearing in DevAccount-001 that trusts Identity Center.
Check
Verify the trust relationship and attached policies in the created role within the member account.
Misconception
Do not assume roles are only logical pointers. They are actual IAM roles created in the member account and enforce permissions at resource time.
Key Takeaway

Automated provisioning simplifies setup and makes role enforcement consistent across accounts.
