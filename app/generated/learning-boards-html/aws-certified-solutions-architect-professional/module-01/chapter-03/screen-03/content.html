Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Applied Objectives
01 — Inventory
List accounts, users, and duplicate credentials
Check where identities are duplicated and where manual cross-account roles are used; these are prime candidates for centralization.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Explain
Describe SSO portal, permission sets, and automated role provisioning
Relate each concept to a user story such as a developer expecting access to DevAccount-001 after logging in once.
03 — Configure
Create user, group, and assign ReadOnlyAccess
Follow the lab steps to enable Identity Center, create a Developers group, and assign permission sets to a member account.
04 — Govern
Enable MFA, integrate IdP, and schedule audits
Use CloudTrail sign-in events and periodic reviews to ensure least privilege is maintained.
