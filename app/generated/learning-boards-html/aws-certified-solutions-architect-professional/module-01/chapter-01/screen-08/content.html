Definition

Service Control Policies (SCPs)

SCPs are organization-level policies that set the upper bound for allowed actions in member accounts. They help enforce compliance and prevent risky actions across many accounts.
Concrete example
Apply an SCP that denies unencrypted storage creation to an OU handling regulated workloads, ensuring consistent enforcement even if someone creates permissive IAM roles.
Practical notes
  • Use SCPs as ceilings, not to grant day-to-day access.
  • Test SCPs in a non-production OU to avoid accidental lockouts.
  • Combine SCPs with OU structure and IAM for full governance.
Common mistake

Attaching overly broad SCPs without testing can block essential workflows; always validate in a staged OU.
