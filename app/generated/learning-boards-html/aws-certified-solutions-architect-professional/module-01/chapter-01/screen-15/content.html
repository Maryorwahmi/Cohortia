Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
AWS Organizations
Centrally manage multiple AWS accounts, apply governance, and consolidate billing.
Management Account
Primary account for billing and organization control; secure it and avoid running production services there.
Organizational Unit (OU)
Logical grouping of accounts for applying SCPs and organizing governance hierarchies.
Member Account
An individual AWS account that belongs to the organization; isolate workloads here.
Consolidated Billing
Aggregate billing under the management account while preserving per-account cost details for attribution.
Blast Radius
The scope of impact from incidents; multi-account strategies reduce blast radius by isolating environments.
Quick takeaway

Design accounts to isolate risk, use OUs and SCPs for governance, and create accounts from the management account when possible.
