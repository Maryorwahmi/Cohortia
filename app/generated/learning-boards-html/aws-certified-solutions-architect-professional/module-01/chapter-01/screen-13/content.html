Best Practices

Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them

Pitfall
Flat or overly complex OU trees
Mitigation
Start simple and grow OUs as governance needs emerge.
Pitfall
Untested SCPs and late governance
Mitigation
Validate SCPs in staging OUs and use automation for rollouts.
Pitfall
Inviting legacy accounts without review
Mitigation
Audit invited accounts and remediate before relying on them for governance.
Key Takeaway

Iterate your OU design, automate account provisioning, and always validate SCPs in staging before production rollout.
