Core Concept

Risks of a Single Account

Define the monolithic account problem and why it increases risk at enterprise scale.

Blast radius
A single compromise can expose multiple environments and teams.
Permission sprawl
Hard to enforce least privilege when many teams share an account.
Auditing difficulty
Tracking access and cost ownership becomes noisy.
Key Takeaway

Account-level isolation limits damage and makes auditing and least privilege practical.

Concrete example (scenario)
Scenario:
Dev team stores CI secrets and deploys to a shared account. A leaked credential lets an attacker modify production pipelines and exfiltrate data.
Contrast / misconception:
Some teams keep a single account to reduce logins. This lowers administrative overhead but increases overall risk; the trade-off usually favors separate accounts at scale.
