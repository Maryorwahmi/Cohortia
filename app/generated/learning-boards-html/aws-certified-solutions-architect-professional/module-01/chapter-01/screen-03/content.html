Learning Objectives — Practice & Governance

These objectives prepare you to design OU hierarchies and apply governance at scale.

01
Design OU structure patterns
Choose environment, function, or business unit groupings and start simple to avoid over-complication.
Verified competency milestone
02
Apply Service Control Policies
Use SCPs at OU level to enforce compliance and prevent risky account actions.
Verified competency milestone
03
Prefer creating accounts via Organizations
Provisioning accounts from the management account gives immediate administrative control and a clean baseline.
Verified competency milestone
04
Avoid flat or overly complex OU trees
Start with a few top-level OUs and expand as governance needs emerge to keep administration simple.
Verified competency milestone
