Process Flow

OU Design Steps

Step 1
Identify top-level concerns: Security, Shared Services, Workloads.
Step 2
Map environments or teams into nested OUs only when necessary.
Step 3
Apply SCPs and test inheritance before scaling the pattern.
Example structure
Root
├─ Security OU
└─ Security-Logging Account
├─ SharedServices OU
└─ Directory Account
└─ Workloads OU
├─ Workloads/Prod
└─ ProdAppAccount
└─ Workloads/Dev
Misconceptions & tips
  • Avoid over-nesting—each level increases policy complexity.
  • Prefer functional or environment splits that match operational ownership.
  • Document OU intent so teams understand guardrails and responsibilities.
