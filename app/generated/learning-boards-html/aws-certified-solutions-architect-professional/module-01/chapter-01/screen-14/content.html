Assessment Idea

Scenario prompt

You have a single account with dev, test, and prod. Identify two risks and propose Organization-based mitigations.
Suggested answer points
  • Risk: blast radius — Mitigation: separate accounts, apply SCPs.
  • Risk: permission sprawl — Mitigation: per-account IAM and centralized identity.
  • OU design: Root → Security, Workloads → Workloads/Prod, Workloads/Dev.
Reflection prompts
  • Would you create accounts via Organizations or invite them? Why?
  • Which SCP would you attach to the Security OU?
  • How would you automate account provisioning?
Assessment takeaway

Translate risk identification into OU design, account provisioning decisions, and SCPs to show practical mastery.
