Architecture

Centralized services

Security or Shared Services OU hosts logging, directories, and network components. These services provide telemetry and baseline services for member accounts.
Example: Security account receives logs from member accounts and enforces retention and analysis centrally.

Workload autonomy

Workload teams own deployment, CI, and IAM inside their accounts. They operate independently but within SCP-imposed guardrails and shared tooling.
Best practice: deliver account bootstrap templates so teams get standardized logging, tags, and security posture from day one.
Why it matters
This model balances speed and governance: teams move quickly in their accounts while platform teams maintain security and cost controls centrally.
