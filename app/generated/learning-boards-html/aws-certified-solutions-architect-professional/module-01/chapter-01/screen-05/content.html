Comparison

Single Account

Lower account overhead, but larger blast radius and complex permissions.
Harder auditing and cost attribution across teams.
Risk of over-permissioning to keep operations simple.
Misconception check: fewer accounts reduce complexity. They reduce logins but usually increase governance complexity.
Comparison

Multi-Account

Stronger isolation: limits blast radius by separating environments and teams.
Consolidated billing under the management account for aggregated discounts and clearer cost attribution.
Enables central governance via OUs and SCPs, while allowing delegated autonomy.
Practical note: automate account provisioning to manage lifecycle and scale safely.
