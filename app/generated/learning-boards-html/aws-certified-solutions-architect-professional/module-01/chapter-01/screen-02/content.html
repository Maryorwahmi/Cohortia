Learning Objectives

These objectives guide practice and assessment in the hands-on activity.

01
Explain multi-account benefits
Describe why security isolation, cost management, and governance require more than a single account.
Verified competency milestone
02
Describe AWS Organizations components
Identify management account, OUs, and member accounts and their responsibilities.
Verified competency milestone
03
Create an Organization and OUs
Perform the steps to enable all features and create OUs for workloads and security functions.
Verified competency milestone
04
Invite or create member accounts
Understand the trade-offs between inviting existing accounts and creating new accounts under management control.
Verified competency milestone
