Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Continuous Feedback Loop
Five-step loop
  1. Regular Review: pull Trusted Advisor and Compute Optimizer reports.
  2. Prioritization: rank by savings, confidence, effort.
  3. Action: staged implementation with roll-back plan.
  4. Monitoring: CloudWatch and cost reports to verify results.
  5. Iteration: refine and extend successful changes.
Example: Resize & Verify
Resize a sample instance, monitor CPU, memory, and latency, and confirm billing impact in the following billing cycle before scaling the change account-wide.
Why this matters
Verification keeps performance stable while delivering measurable cost savings.
