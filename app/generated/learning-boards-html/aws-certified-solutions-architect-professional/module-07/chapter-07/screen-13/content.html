Sample Question
Trusted Advisor lists several m5.xlarge instances with average CPU below 10% for the past month. What immediate and follow-up actions would you take?
Immediate Action
Consider right-sizing or stopping non-production instances to capture savings.
Validation
Check memory, disk I/O, network, scheduled tasks, and Auto Scaling settings.
Operational Controls
Use staging tests, maintenance windows, and rollback plans before production changes.
