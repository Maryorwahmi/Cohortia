Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Confidence & Prioritization
Three-factor prioritization
  1. Estimated monthly savings
  2. Compute Optimizer confidence score
  3. Implementation effort and compatibility risk
Example: High-savings + high-confidence + low effort = immediate action candidate.
Practical steps
Create a backlog, tag items as quick-win, pilot, or project, and assign owners and success metrics for each item.
Key Takeaway
Use confidence to reduce risk exposure and to sequence testing and production changes safely.
