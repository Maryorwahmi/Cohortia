Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Activity Walkthrough
Steps
  1. Launch or identify an underutilized EC2 instance and attach a low-I/O EBS volume.
  2. Wait 24–48 hours for Compute Optimizer to gather data.
  3. Review recommendations for EC2 and EBS, noting savings and confidence.
  4. Simulate a resize in a maintenance window and restart the instance.
  5. Monitor CloudWatch and cost reports; validate or roll back based on metrics.
Outcome & Reflection
The expected outcome is that you learn to interpret a recommendation, test it safely, and verify the real impact. Reflect on confidence score and any compatibility issues you encountered.
Expected Result
A successful simulation provides measurable cost reduction without performance regressions and a repeatable process for other resources.
