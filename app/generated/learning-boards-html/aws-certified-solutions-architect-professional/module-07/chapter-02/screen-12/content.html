Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
Reserved Instances (RIs)
Billing discount for committing to consistent capacity for 1 or 3 years.
Savings Plans
Flexible $/hour commitment that reduces prices across compute services.
Spot Instances
Unused capacity at deep discounts, interruptible with short warning.
Summary

These options are complementary. Choose the right combination based on predictability, interruption tolerance, and how your compute footprint changes over time.
