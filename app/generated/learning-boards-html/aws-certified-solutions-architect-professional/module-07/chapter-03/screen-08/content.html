Volume types and when to use them
  • gp3: general purpose, independent IOPS/throughput scaling — often most cost-effective.
  • io2 / io2 Block Express: high performance and durability for databases.
  • st1: throughput-optimized for large sequential workloads.
Right-sizing checklist
Monitor usage, snapshot before change, perform a test resize or migration, and confirm application behavior and costs post-change.
Example
A database was provisioned with a 1TB gp3 but shows steady 200GB usage. After validating IOPS and throughput needs, the architect snapshots the volume, provisions a 250GB gp3 with matching IOPS, switches the instance to the new volume, and observes no performance loss while saving monthly storage costs.
Key Takeaway

Measure before you change. Choose gp3 for many use cases and reserve io2 or st1 for specialized performance needs.
