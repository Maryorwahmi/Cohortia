Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Governance
Governance checklist
  • Define retrieval SLAs and make them visible to stakeholders.
  • Tag resources with owner, purpose, and retention expiry.
  • Require approval for bulk retrievals from Glacier or Deep Archive.
  • Schedule periodic restore tests to validate recovery process.
Example policy snippet (summary)
All archives must include owner tags and documented retrieval SLA. Bulk retrievals require manager approval and a cost estimate before execution. Lifecycle rules must be reviewed annually.
Key Takeaway

Treat cost optimization and governance as a paired practice: savings with accountability and tested recovery ensures safety.
