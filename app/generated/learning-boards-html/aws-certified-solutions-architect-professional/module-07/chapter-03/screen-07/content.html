Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Glacier & Deep Archive
When to choose each
If data is accessed once a quarter or less, Glacier can be appropriate; if access is once a year or less, Deep Archive yields further savings. Always align retrieval time expectations with business needs.
Glacier retrievals
Multiple retrieval options: expedited for urgent needs, standard and bulk for cost savings. Test each and understand cost differences.
Deep Archive use cases
Long-term compliance, raw research data, or media that will not be accessed frequently. Ensure retrieval time is acceptable before committing.
Examples and safeguards
Archive of completed projects that rarely need restores; assign retrieval approval and monitor request volumes.
Avoid placing active datasets into Deep Archive; retrieval can be slow and expensive if mistaken.
Use retrieval testing and small restore trials to estimate both time and cost before large-scale retention decisions.
Key Takeaway

Archive aggressively only when retrievals are rare and well understood. Test retrieval types and estimate restore cost and latency.
