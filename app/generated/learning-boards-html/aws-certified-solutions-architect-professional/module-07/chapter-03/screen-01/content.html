Storage Cost Optimization Strategies
Understand and apply class, lifecycle, snapshot, and filesystem techniques to lower costs without surprising retrieval charges.
Why this matters
Storage is persistent cost. Small per-GB savings scale to large monthly reductions across fleets and archives.
Who benefits
Architects, SREs, and finance teams reviewing recurring cloud spend and retention policies.
In this lesson
  • Match S3 classes to access patterns and automate transitions.
  • Design Glacier and Deep Archive strategies while avoiding retrieval surprises.
  • Right-size EBS, manage snapshots, and leverage EFS IA.
