Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Key Concepts
S3 Intelligent-Tiering
Auto-moves objects between frequent and infrequent tiers based on access patterns.
S3 Lifecycle Policies
Rules to transition objects across classes or expire them after defined periods.
Glacier / Deep Archive
Lowest cost long-term storage with longer retrieval windows and retrieval fees.
Summary
Automate tiering and lifecycle for predictable savings, but always model retrieval times and costs and build governance around archives.
