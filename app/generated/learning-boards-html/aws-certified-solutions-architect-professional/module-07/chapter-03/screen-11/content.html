Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Continuous Audit
Audit checklist
  • Identify unattached EBS volumes and verify owners before deletion.
  • Find large or old S3 objects and apply lifecycle rules or deletion.
  • List snapshots older than retention and archive or remove.
  • Delete incomplete multipart uploads after 7 days.
Practical example
Run a monthly script that queries resources, groups by owner tag, and produces a report. Owners confirm or reject cleanup suggestions. Approved deletions are executed and logged for finance and audit purposes.
Key Takeaway

Auditing is a regular process: detect, verify with owners, and remove. This reduces recurring costs and prevents surprises.
