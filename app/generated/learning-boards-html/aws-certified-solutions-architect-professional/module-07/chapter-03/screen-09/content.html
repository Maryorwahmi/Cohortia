Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Snapshot Lifecycle
Automate and tag
Use DLM or AWS Backup to schedule snapshots. Tag snapshots with owner, retention policy, and purpose so automated deletions are safe and auditable.
Retention tiers
  • Daily snapshots: keep 7–14 days.
  • Weekly snapshots: keep several weeks to months.
  • Monthly snapshots: keep according to compliance.
Example: cleanup run
Daily automation creates snapshots; a weekend job deletes snapshots older than retention. Orchestration records deletions and reports monthly storage saved to the finance team.
Key Takeaway

Snapshots are incremental but still cost money. Automate lifecycle and tag everything so you can remove expired backups confidently.
