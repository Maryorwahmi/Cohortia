Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Lifecycle Policies
Typical lifecycle timeline
Day 0: S3 Standard
Active data with immediate access.
Day 30: Standard-IA
Move infrequently accessed items to reduce cost.
Day 90: Glacier Flexible Retrieval
Archive for longer-term retention where slower retrieval is acceptable.
Day 365: Expire
Remove objects that no longer serve business needs.
Implementation notes
  • Scope rules by prefix or tag to avoid accidental bucket-wide changes.
  • Test with a sample prefix and monitor transitions in metrics.
  • Include a rule to delete incomplete multipart uploads after a short window.
  • Estimate retrieval behavior to avoid negating storage savings.
Key Takeaway

Lifecycle rules automate predictable transitions and deletions; scope rules narrowly and test transitions to avoid surprises.
