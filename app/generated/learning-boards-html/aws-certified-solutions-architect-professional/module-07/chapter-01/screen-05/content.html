Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Cost Explorer
Overview

Find cost drivers and forecast

Use time ranges, grouping, filters, and forecasts to locate spending anomalies and predict future costs.

Common groupings

Service, Region, Linked Account, Tag, Usage Type

Action

Drill into spikes to identify whether they come from hours, data transfer, or new resources.

Pitfall

Avoid relying on high-level charts without drilling to group-level or tag-level detail.

Walkthrough
Step 1: Select range

Choose the period you want to analyze; use rolling windows to see trends.

Step 2: Group

Group by Usage Type or Service to identify dominant cost types.

Step 3: Filter

Filter by tag or account to attribute costs to teams or projects.

Step 4: Forecast

Use forecast to estimate future spend and trigger budgets early.

Example

A spike in EC2 in one region traced to unoptimized development instances after grouping by region and usage type.
