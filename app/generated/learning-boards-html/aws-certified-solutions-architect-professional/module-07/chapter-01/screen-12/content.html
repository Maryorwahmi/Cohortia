Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Lab: Project Budget
Step 1
Create Tag Policy

Define Project as required, enforce values Phoenix and Hydra, and attach to the development OU. Verify compliance reports.

Expected behavior
Resources without the tag will appear non-compliant or be prevented based on service integration; resources with Project=Phoenix will be compliant.
Step 2
Create Budget for Project:Phoenix

Set a monthly cost budget of $50, filter by Tag → Project → Phoenix, add a forecast alert at 80% to email.

Expected outcome
Costs from resources tagged Project=Phoenix will be tracked by the budget and trigger alerts per configuration.
Checkpoint
Verify tag compliance in Organizations and the budget scope in Budgets console.
Reflection
Consider how strict enforcement affects developer workflows and how to phase adoption.
Next step
Use CUR and Athena to verify that Phoenix costs appear in line-item exports for chargeback.
