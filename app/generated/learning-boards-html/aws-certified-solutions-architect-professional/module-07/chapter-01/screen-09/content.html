Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Alerts Comparison
Forecast Alert

Early warning

Predicts likely exceedance before period end based on historical trends. Use for proactive mitigation.

Example: Alert at 80% forecast for Project:Phoenix triggers investigation before the month ends.
Actual Alert

Definitive signal

Notifies when the budgeted amount is exceeded. Use for final confirmation and post-incident reporting.

Example: 100% actual alert for Production environment triggers billing review and remediation steps.
Operational mapping
  • Forecast 80–90%: Run a quick audit and pause noncritical jobs.
  • Actual 100%: Escalate to finance and owners; implement cost reduction actions.
Key Takeaway

Use both alert types in tandem and assign clear operational responses to avoid alert fatigue and ensure corrective action.
