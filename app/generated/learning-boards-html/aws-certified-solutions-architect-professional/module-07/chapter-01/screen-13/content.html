Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Governance Loop
Hierarchy

Visibility → Control → Reporting → Optimization

Each stage enables the next; create KPIs and repeat the loop to demonstrate value.

Visibility
Cost Explorer and CUR provide trends and line items.
Control
Budgets and tag policies prevent drift and enable scoped alerts.
Reporting
CUR + Athena produce chargeback-ready reports.
Optimization
Right-sizing, automation, and architecture choices reduce cost or increase efficiency.
Key Takeaway

Treat cost governance as an iterative loop; instrument, control, report, and then optimize with measurable outcomes.
