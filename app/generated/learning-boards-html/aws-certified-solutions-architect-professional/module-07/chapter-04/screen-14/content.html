Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Key Concepts
RDS Reserved Instances
Discounted pricing for RDS instances with a 1- or 3-year commitment.
Aurora Serverless
On-demand, auto-scaling Aurora configuration that bills for capacity used.
DynamoDB Provisioned
You set RCUs and WCUs for predictable workloads, often paired with Auto Scaling.
Quick takeaway

Match commitment and capacity models to workload predictability to reduce cost and operational effort.
