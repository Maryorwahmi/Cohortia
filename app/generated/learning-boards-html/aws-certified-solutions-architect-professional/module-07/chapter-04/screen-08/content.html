Process

Configure Auto Scaling with realistic targets

Define utilization targets and bounds, then test and monitor to tune behavior.

Step 1: Analyze traffic
Collect hourly and percentile metrics to understand normal and peak patterns.
Step 2: Set targets
Choose a utilization target (70–80%) and min/max capacity to constrain costs and performance.
Step 3: Test under load
Simulate spikes to confirm scaling responsiveness and absence of throttling.
Step 4: Monitor & tune
Use alarms on throttling, read/write capacity usage, and adjust as traffic evolves.
Tip: Pair Auto Scaling with adaptive capacity and consider DAX for read-heavy workloads to reduce overall RCU usage.
