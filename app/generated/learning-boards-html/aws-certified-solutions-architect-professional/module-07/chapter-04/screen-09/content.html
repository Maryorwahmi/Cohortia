On-Demand Capacity

A pay-per-request model that removes capacity planning and scales automatically with traffic.

When to use
  • New applications with unknown patterns.
  • Applications with infrequent or spiky traffic.
  • When reducing operational overhead is a priority.
Example
A product launch that may create sudden traffic spikes — On-Demand prevents throttling without manual capacity changes.
Misconception check
On-Demand reduces planning effort but is not always the lowest cost for steady, high-throughput tables. Model expected monthly requests before selecting.
Key Takeaway

Start with On-Demand for uncertainty; switch to Provisioned with Auto Scaling when traffic stabilizes and unit costs matter.
