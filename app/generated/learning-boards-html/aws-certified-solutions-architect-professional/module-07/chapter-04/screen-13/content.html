Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Idle Resources
Process

Find, evaluate, and remove or pause idle resources

Monthly audit
Run Trusted Advisor to list idle RDS, Redshift, and DynamoDB resources and prioritize remediation.
Automation
Implement scheduled stop/start for non-production databases and tag resources for lifecycle policies.
Redshift options
Pause clusters during off hours when acceptable, and use concurrency scaling for rare spikes rather than over-provisioning.
Storage cleanup
Archive or remove old datasets in S3 that drive unnecessary scan costs; consider lifecycle policies.
Key Takeaway

Make discovery and cleanup a recurring operational rhythm and automate non-production lifecycle to avoid repeated waste.
