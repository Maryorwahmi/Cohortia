Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › DynamoDB Capacity Modes
Provisioned Capacity

Set RCUs & WCUs, use Auto Scaling

Good for predictable workloads. Configure Auto Scaling to adjust provisioned capacity and avoid over-provisioning.

  • Lower unit cost at scale.
  • Requires forecasting and monitoring.
  • Auto Scaling mitigates bursts but needs tuning.
On-Demand Capacity

Pay-per-request

Ideal for unpredictable or spiky workloads and early-stage products. Eliminates capacity planning.

Example
A new mobile app launch where traffic may spike unpredictably — On-Demand avoids throttling or manual scaling.
Misconception check
On-Demand adds operational simplicity but may be more expensive for stable, high-throughput applications.
