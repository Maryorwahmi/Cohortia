Process Flow › Third-party FinOps Integration

What external tools add

Advanced visualization, forecasting, optimization recommendations, and chargeback automation.

Required inputs
Clean CUR exports, consistent tags, and documented account mappings yield better results from FinOps platforms.
Evaluation criteria
  • CUR ingestion and refresh cadence.
  • Support for your tag taxonomy and Cost Categories.
  • Automation for showback or chargeback processes.

Pilot guidance

  1. Fix tagging and category gaps first.
  2. Pilot with a subset of projects and validate outputs against Cost Explorer.
  3. Define governance and data ownership before automating chargebacks.
Key Takeaway

Integrate FinOps tools when native data is clean and stakeholders need advanced forecasting or automated chargeback workflows.
