Design considerations

Align accounts to business units or environments to balance isolation, security, and cost clarity.

Shared Services Account
Use for central logging, transit, and networking. Group in Cost Categories as SharedServices.
Project or BU Accounts
Accounts per project or business unit make ownership and chargeback straightforward.
Key Takeaway

Use Organizations for governance and consolidated billing for a single financial view; design accounts to reflect ownership boundaries.

Benefits of consolidation

  • Single aggregated bill for finance and forecasting.
  • Share Reserved Instances and Savings Plans across accounts.
  • Apply Tag Policies and SCPs at OU granularity for governance.
Example: SharedServices Category
Create a Cost Category that includes the logging account and transit gateway costs to present them separately in Cost Explorer.
Misconception
Consolidated billing reduces billing complexity but does not replace tagging or Cost Categories for business-facing reports.
