Process Flow › Remediation & Auditing

Audit and prioritize

Identify untagged resources, then focus on those that drive the highest costs first.

Audit sources
Use CUR exports, resource inventories, and Cost Explorer to find gaps.
Prioritize by spend
Fix the top spenders first to get rapid reporting improvements.
Key Takeaway

Automate remediation and focus on high-impact resources to recover accurate attribution fastest.

Automation strategies

  • Use IaC to enforce tags at provisioning time.
  • Run scripts to tag existing resources based on naming conventions or other metadata.
  • Schedule periodic audits and remediations for drift.
Misconception
Remediation is not purely manual; combine automation and targeted manual cleanup for efficiency.
