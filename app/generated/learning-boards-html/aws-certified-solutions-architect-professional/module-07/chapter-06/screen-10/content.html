Comparison › Showback vs Chargeback

Showback

Provide visibility by delivering cost reports to teams without financial transfers.

  • Encourages cost awareness and training.
  • Lower friction to adopt; useful early in FinOps maturity.
  • Requires accurate categories for meaningful reports.

Chargeback

Allocate or bill costs to consuming departments or projects to enforce financial responsibility.

  • Drives accountability but raises disputes if data is inaccurate.
  • Requires enforced tagging and validated Cost Categories.
  • Often used when teams operate with internal budgets.
Practical Guidance
Start with showback to build tagging and category reliability. Move to chargeback once rules are enforced and stakeholders agree on mappings to avoid disputes.
Key Takeaway

Choose showback to teach and chargeback to enforce, but only after tags and categories are dependable.
