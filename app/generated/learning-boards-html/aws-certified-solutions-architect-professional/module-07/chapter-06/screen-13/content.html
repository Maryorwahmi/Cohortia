Checklist › Rollout Steps

Operational checklist

1. Taxonomy & Policy
Document keys, allowed values, and Tag Policies to enforce them.
2. Remediation
Audit untagged resources and remediate the highest cost items first.
3. Cost Categories
Create categories and validate in Cost Explorer with sample data.
4. Anomaly Detection
Configure alerts and tie them to runbooks and ops channels.
5. Pilot FinOps tools
Only after data quality is good, pilot third-party integrations for automation.
6. Communication
Publish reports, define dispute resolution, and schedule governance reviews.
Key Takeaway

Roll out iteratively: enforce policy, fix high-impact gaps, validate categories, and then scale with automation and stakeholder training.
