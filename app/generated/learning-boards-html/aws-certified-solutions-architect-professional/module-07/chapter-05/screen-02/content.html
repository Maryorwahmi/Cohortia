Learning Objectives

Learn to spot egress, apply private routing and edge caching, and choose hybrid connectivity with cost in mind.

01
Identify egress sources

Use Cost Explorer and VPC Flow Logs to find where data leaves your VPC or region, and quantify monthly impact. This lets you prioritize optimizations where they pay back fastest.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement VPC Endpoints & PrivateLink

Configure Gateway VPC Endpoints for services like S3 and use PrivateLink for private service exposure. These routes avoid public internet paths and reduce egress and exposure.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use CloudFront for global delivery

Front static and cacheable content with CloudFront to reduce origin egress rates and improve user latency. This often reduces costs for global audiences.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Choose Direct Connect vs VPN

Match connectivity to volume: Direct Connect reduces per-GB rates for sustained, large transfers; VPN is cost-effective for lower-volume or temporary links.
