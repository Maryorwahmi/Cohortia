Comparison Direct Connect vs VPN

Direct Connect vs Site-to-Site VPN

Match connectivity to expected traffic patterns to minimize blended cost.

Site-to-Site VPN
  • Lower setup time and cost for low volume.
  • Traffic still subject to standard egress charges.
  • Hourly connection cost applies.
When to use
Temporary migration, low-volume hybrid connectivity, or proof-of-concept links.
AWS Direct Connect
  • Dedicated port with lower per-GB rates for large volumes.
  • Port-hour charges apply.
  • Best for steady, high-volume transfers.
Example
Regular nightly backups moving terabytes may justify Direct Connect due to lower per-GB pricing.
