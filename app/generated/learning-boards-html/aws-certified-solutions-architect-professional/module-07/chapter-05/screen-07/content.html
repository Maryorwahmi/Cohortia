Cross-AZ vs Cross-Region tradeoffs

Make locality a first-class cost decision and replicate strategically.

Design checklist
  1. Map service-to-service traffic volume and payload sizes.
  2. Prefer local reads to keep cross-AZ traffic low.
  3. Use asynchronous replication across regions when possible.
Scenario
A database replica in another AZ should be used mainly for failover and local reads; avoid constant cross-AZ writes that generate large egress.
Estimate cost impact
Calculate average payload size × transactions per second × hours per month to estimate GB/month. Compare that number to the cost of replication or regional storage alternatives.
Common misconception
Assuming multi-AZ equals no cost. Cross-AZ data transfer is billed and can be significant for chatty systems.
Why it matters: planned replication reduces surprise egress and keeps recovery objectives realistic.
