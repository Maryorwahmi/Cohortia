Estimating savings and cost tradeoffs

Model traffic and costs conservatively to choose the most cost-effective change.

Estimation steps
  1. Measure GB/month for the flow.
  2. Estimate post-change GB (cache hit ratio, endpoint routing, replication overhead).
  3. Calculate monthly cost under each option including fixed fees.
  4. Compute payback period for fixed-cost options.
Worked example
Origin: 10 TB/month. CloudFront hit ratio estimated at 80% → origin egress 2 TB. Compare origin egress cost at current per-GB versus CloudFront + remaining origin transfer to estimate savings.
Misconception
Assuming 100% cache hit rate will overestimate savings. Use conservative hit rates for planning.
