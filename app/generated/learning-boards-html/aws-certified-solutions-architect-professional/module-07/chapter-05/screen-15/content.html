Key Concepts

Keep these compact reminders for design and audits.

Data Egress
Data leaving services or regions is typically charged and should be minimized.
VPC Endpoints
Private connections to AWS services that avoid internet egress and reduce exposure.
CloudFront
Edge caching reduces origin egress and improves latency for global users.
Big takeaway

Prioritize actions that convert high-volume egress into private or cached paths and verify with billing and flow logs.
