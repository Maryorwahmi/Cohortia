Monitoring: Cost Explorer & VPC Flow Logs

Detect expensive traffic patterns and validate that changes reduce egress.

Cost Explorer workflow
  1. Filter by usage type: Data Transfer Out.
  2. Group by service and region to find hotspots.
  3. Save reports and set alerts for spikes.
Concrete example
If EC2 in us-east-1 shows high Data Transfer Out, dig into VPC Flow Logs to locate destination IPs and volumes.
VPC Flow Logs tips
  • Look for large byte counts between source and destination pairs.
  • Detect traffic leaving to internet or to other AZs unnecessarily.
  • Use aggregated queries to find top talkers and services.
Why it matters: measurement reveals the true targets for optimization and prevents blind architectural changes.
