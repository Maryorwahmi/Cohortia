Scenario Architecture checklist

Putting it together: architecture checklist

A repeatable list to evaluate egress risk and remediation options for multi-tier systems.

Step 1: Map flows
Inventory high-volume flows and estimate GB/month for each.
Step 2: Replace internet paths
Use VPC Endpoints / PrivateLink where supported to avoid NAT/internet routes.
Step 3: Cache & replicate
Front public content with CloudFront and replicate data for regional locality where needed.
Step 4: Connectivity decision
Estimate volume and choose Direct Connect for sustained high bandwidth, otherwise VPN.
Step 5: Instrument & validate
Use Cost Explorer and VPC Flow Logs to validate savings and detect regressions.
Step 6: Pilot
Run a small-scale pilot and measure origin request and Data Transfer Out before full rollout.
