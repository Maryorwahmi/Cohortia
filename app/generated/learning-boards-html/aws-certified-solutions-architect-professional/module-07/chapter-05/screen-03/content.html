Module 07 › Chapter 7.5 › Learning Objectives — continued

Verification & Design Skills

Learn how to validate cost impact and choose deployment patterns that reduce cross-AZ and cross-region egress.

Audit with Cost Explorer

Break down Data Transfer Out by service and region to identify the highest-cost flows that deserve optimization.

Inspect VPC Flow Logs

Use flow logs to detect unexpected cross-AZ or external traffic patterns that may indicate misconfiguration or inefficient architecture.

Design for locality

Place application tiers and replicas to reduce inter-AZ and cross-region reads, preferring local replicas or replication where cost-effective.

Hands-on activity
Create a Gateway VPC Endpoint for S3 and verify route table changes to confirm traffic uses the private endpoint rather than the NAT or internet gateway.
