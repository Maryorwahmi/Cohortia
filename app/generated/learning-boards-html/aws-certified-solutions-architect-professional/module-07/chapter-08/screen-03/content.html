Operational tools to sustain cost reductions
  • Tagging & Governance: enforce tags with policies to allocate costs and automate lifecycle rules based on owner and environment.
  • Monitoring & Budgets: set budgets and cost anomaly detection to notify teams when usage drifts from expectations.
  • Automated Recommendations: pipeline Trusted Advisor and Compute Optimizer findings into CI/CD or runbooks to act quickly.
  • Review Cadence: schedule architecture and cost reviews so right-sizing and arch changes are regular rather than ad hoc.
Key Takeaway

Operational practices convert architectural choices into lasting savings by making cost visible and actionable.
