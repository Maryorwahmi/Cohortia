Designing Cost-Effective Multi-Tier Architectures

Architectural choices at each tier change both daily costs and long-term TCO. This lesson shows practical service pairings and operational practices that keep performance strong while reducing spend.

In this lesson
Compute, storage, network, and governance choices that reduce cost without sacrificing availability.
Coming up
Serverless first, container options, lifecycle policies, and egress minimization techniques.
Why it matters
Small architectural changes often yield large, continuous savings when automated and governed.
Concept Map
Web Tier
S3 + CloudFront, or Lambda for dynamic API front-ends.
Application Tier
Fargate, Lambda, or EC2 with Graviton plus Savings Plans.
Data & Analytics
S3 lifecycle, Athena on Parquet, Aurora Serverless where appropriate.
Key Takeaway

Optimize by matching consumption model to workload profile, then automate and govern to keep costs under control.
