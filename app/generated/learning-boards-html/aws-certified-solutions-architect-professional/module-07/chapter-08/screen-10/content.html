Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Automated Optimization
Action pipeline
  1. Ingest recommendations from optimization services into a ticketing or CI system.
  2. Automate safe, reversible actions like lifecycle transitions or instance stop/start on schedule.
  3. Alert teams with budgets and tag-linked reports when anomalies or threshold breaches occur.
  4. Perform regular architecture review meetings to reassess long-lived design choices.
Example runbook entry
A monthly task: apply low-risk Compute Optimizer rightsizing, create tickets for DB instance resizing proposals, and update a dashboard showing savings by tag.
Key Takeaway

Treat optimization as normal operations: measure, automate safe fixes, and review remaining items with stakeholders.
