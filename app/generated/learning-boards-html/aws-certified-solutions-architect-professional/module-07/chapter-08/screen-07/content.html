Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Relational vs NoSQL
Relational (RDS / Aurora)
Best for transactional workloads and complex queries. Use RIs or Aurora Serverless for cost savings: RIs for steady-state, Serverless for spiky demand.
Example
Product catalog with transactions and joins: Aurora Serverless avoids running a large instance during low traffic periods.
Pitfall
Always evaluate cost of continuous small-scale usage on serverless; it can exceed reserved instances for steady, high-volume queries.
NoSQL (DynamoDB)
Great for key-value access, flexible schema, and extreme scale. Choose On-Demand for unpredictable traffic and Provisioned with Auto Scaling for predictable, high-volume workloads.
Example
User profile writes that spike unpredictably are a good match for On-Demand capacity to avoid provisioning errors.
Pitfall
Provisioned capacity without proper autoscaling leads to throttling or unnecessary cost; monitor and tune throughput settings.
