Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › FinOps Culture
Tagging & Governance
Define required tags such as owner, project, and environment and enforce them with policies. Use these tags to create chargeback reports and set budget alerts per owner.
Example tag
owner:team-ecommerce — enables showback and links recommendations to a team that can act.
Process
Include a cost review item in sprint retros and runbooks for responding to cost alerts.
Cultural practices
  • Make cost visible with dashboards by tag.
  • Assign budget owners and publish monthly showback reports.
  • Automate safe changes and document approval gates for larger ones.
