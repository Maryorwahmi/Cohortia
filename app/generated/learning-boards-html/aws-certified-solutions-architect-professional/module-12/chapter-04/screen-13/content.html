Assessment Idea
Governance Decisions and Trade-offs
Evaluate scenarios and justify control selections with validation steps.
Question 1
Which service meets FIPS 140-2 Level 3 HSM custody requirements? Explain why and how you'd validate it.
Question 2
How would you use Lake Formation to prevent analysts from seeing a sensitive column and validate the restriction with Athena?
Question 3
Design lifecycle rules for a dataset retained seven years and describe the audit evidence you'd collect.
Scoring guidance
Look for correct service choices, clear validation steps (run crawler, grant Lake Formation permissions, run Athena queries as different roles, inspect S3 lifecycle transitions), and reasoning about trade-offs such as operational overhead versus compliance need.
Key Takeaway

Good answers combine correct tooling with practical validation and governance rationale.
