S3 Lifecycle Policies and Retention
Automate object transitions and expirations to meet retention and cost goals.
Design rule
Tag or prefix data sets and apply lifecycle rules per dataset to meet specific retention policies.
Example rule
First 365 days: S3 Standard. Years 1–7: transition to Glacier Deep Archive. After seven years: expire and delete.
Validation
Check lifecycle transitions in the S3 console or via API and capture lifecycle logs for audits.
Key Takeaway

Automate retention rules to avoid manual cleanup and to ensure compliance and cost predictability.

Practical tips
  • Use object tagging to apply dataset-specific lifecycle rules.
  • Coordinate with legal to define retention durations.
  • Test rules on a small dataset before wide rollout.
Pitfall
Applying a blanket rule can inadvertently delete data needed for audits or litigation holds.
