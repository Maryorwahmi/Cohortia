Centralize storage
Dedicate a Data Lake account for S3 and cataloging to simplify governance.
Protect keys
Choose KMS for most cases; use CloudHSM for exclusive HSM custody in regulated contexts.
Catalog and restrict
Use Glue and Lake Formation so analysts can find data while sensitive columns remain excluded.
Keep these notes with your design checklist and validate them during deployments and audits.
