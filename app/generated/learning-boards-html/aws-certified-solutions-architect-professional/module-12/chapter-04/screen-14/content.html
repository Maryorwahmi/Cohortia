Data Lake & Warehouse
Lake for raw flexibility, warehouse for curated, performant analytics.
Encryption & Key Management
Use KMS for managed keys; CloudHSM when exclusive HSM custody is required.
Catalog & Fine-Grained Access
Glue Data Catalog plus Lake Formation permissions prevent data swamps and protect columns.
Summary takeaway
When planning a multi-account data platform, prioritize account separation, coherent governance pillars, and a maintained catalog with enforceable permissions.
