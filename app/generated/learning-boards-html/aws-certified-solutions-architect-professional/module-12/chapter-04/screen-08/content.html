KMS vs CloudHSM
A practical comparison for encryption strategies.
AWS KMS
Managed keys, integrates with AWS services, supports rotation and IAM-based grants. Best for typical encryption needs.
AWS CloudHSM
Customer-controlled HSM with FIPS 140-2 Level 3 validation. Needed when exclusive key custody is required by regulation.
Operational note
CloudHSM requires cluster management and integration work; KMS reduces operational overhead with managed features.
Decision checklist
  • Is exclusive HSM custody required by regulation? If yes, CloudHSM.
  • Do you need deep AWS integration with low ops overhead? KMS is usually sufficient.
  • Are you prepared to manage HSM clusters and backups? CloudHSM adds operational tasks.
Key Takeaway

Choose KMS for standard encryption and CloudHSM only when exclusive HSM custody and FIPS 140-2 Level 3 are required.
