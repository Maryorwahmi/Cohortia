Core Concept
Multi-account Data Lake Architecture
A central Data Lake account with controlled ingestion and separate analytics accounts.
Pattern
  • Central S3 buckets and Glue Data Catalog in a Data Lake account.
  • App accounts push raw data via cross-account roles or controlled S3 PUT.
  • Analytics accounts query cataloged data with Lake Formation permissions enforced.
Example scenario
An e-commerce app uploads sales CSVs into s3://company-data-lake-raw/sales_data/. Glue crawler runs nightly and populates the sales table for analysts.
Common mistake
Putting all data and consumers in one account increases risk and complicates policy scoping.
Key Takeaway

Separate accounts for storage, ingestion, and analytics make governance and auditing tractable.
