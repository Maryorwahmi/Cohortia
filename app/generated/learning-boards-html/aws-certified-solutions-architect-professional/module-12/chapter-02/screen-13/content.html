Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Monitoring & Recovery
Key signals to monitor
  • Error rates and latencies of critical calls.
  • SQS queue depth and age of oldest message to detect backlogs.
  • Health checks for endpoints used by Route 53 failover.
Use CloudWatch alarms and dashboards to centralize observability and drive automated or manual remediation workflows.
Isolation and recovery tactics
- Use circuit breakers and timeouts to avoid resource exhaustion.
- Route slow or failing operations to dead-letter queues for offline processing.
- Implement compensating transactions for partially completed sagas.
Operator guidance
Provide runbooks for common alarms and ensure alerts include correlated traces and the relevant message IDs for quick diagnosis.
Key Takeaway

Combine monitoring, isolation, and compensations to contain failures and recover with minimal customer impact.
