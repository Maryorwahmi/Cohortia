Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Compare Messaging Options
Amazon EventBridge
Serverless event bus with routing rules and integrations. Good for central event domains and SaaS events.
Use when you need flexible rules and cross-account or SaaS integration.
Amazon SNS
Pub/sub for fan-out to multiple subscribers. Low latency and multiple endpoint types supported.
Use when a single message must reach many consumers concurrently.
Amazon SQS
Durable queuing with at-least-once delivery, visibility timeouts, and batching.
Use when you need reliability, buffering, and retry semantics for consumers.
Combined pattern example
Publish OrderPlaced to EventBridge. EventBridge rule forwards to an SNS topic for immediate fan-out and to SQS for slow, durable consumers that process asynchronously and require retries.
