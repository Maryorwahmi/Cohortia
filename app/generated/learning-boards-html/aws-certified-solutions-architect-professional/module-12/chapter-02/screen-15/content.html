Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Quick Concepts
Saga Pattern
Sequence of local transactions with compensations to undo prior steps on failure.
Circuit Breaker
Detect failures and open to stop calls, then probe and recover with a half-open state.
Idempotency & Consistency
Make operations repeatable safely and accept eventual consistency where needed for scale.
Final takeaway

Combine the right messaging primitives, transactional patterns, and operational controls to build scalable, resilient applications that fail gracefully and recover reliably.
