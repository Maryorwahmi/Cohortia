Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Key Concepts
Event-Driven Architecture
Decoupled services communicate by publishing and consuming business events.
EventBridge
A serverless event bus for routing and integrating events across services and SaaS sources.
SNS & SQS
SNS for fan-out, SQS for durable point-to-point queues and retry semantics.
Summary Takeaway

Use event-driven patterns with appropriate AWS services, design compensations for sagas, and protect your system with circuit breakers and robust routing for global availability.
