Advanced Design Patterns for Scalability and Resilience
Event-driven architectures, sagas for distributed transactions, circuit breakers, and global routing strategies on AWS.
In this lesson
Learn to decouple services with events, coordinate multi-step transactions with sagas, and protect your system with circuit breaking and global routing techniques.
Why it matters
These patterns prevent cascading failures and let components scale independently, which is essential for microservices and serverless systems operating at global scale.
Getting started

We’ll use an order processing example throughout: publishing an order event, independent consumers, compensations on failure, and routing users globally to healthy endpoints.
