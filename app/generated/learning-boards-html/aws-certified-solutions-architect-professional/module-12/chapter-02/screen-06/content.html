Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Saga Pattern
Sequential steps
  1. Inventory service decrements stock and emits InventoryReserved.
  2. Payment service charges payment and emits PaymentCompleted.
  3. Shipping service reserves shipment and emits ShippingScheduled.
Compensation example
If payment fails after inventory was decremented, the system invokes a compensating InventoryRevert action that increments inventory and publishes an InventoryReverted event.
Implement orchestration using Step Functions for visibility and to execute compensations, or use choreography if you prefer decentralized handling.
Design considerations
  • Define explicit compensating actions for every state-changing step.
  • Ensure idempotency for both forward and compensating operations to handle retries.
  • Provide monitoring and manual reconciliation for long-running failures.
Misconception
Sagas do not offer ACID across services; they provide eventual consistency plus explicit recovery paths.
Key Takeaway

Design compensations and idempotency early. Choose orchestration when visibility or strict sequencing is required.
