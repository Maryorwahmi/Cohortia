Activity: GuardDuty & Inspector
Follow these validated steps to observe findings in a safe way.
1. Enable GuardDuty
Use the console to enable for the account or organization and allow sample findings for testing.
2. Launch a test EC2
Ensure SSM agent and a proper instance profile are present so Inspector can scan it.
3. Enable Inspector
Turn on EC2 scanning and wait for findings to populate; use sample findings for quick learning.
Sample finding (illustrative)
FindingType: UnauthorizedAccess:IAMUser/ConsoleLogin Resource: i-0abc123example Severity: Medium RecommendedAction: Review user activity, rotate keys, and restrict IAM policies
Advice
Map this finding to a runbook: identify owner, isolate affected resources if needed, collect CloudTrail logs, and apply containment or remediation steps.
