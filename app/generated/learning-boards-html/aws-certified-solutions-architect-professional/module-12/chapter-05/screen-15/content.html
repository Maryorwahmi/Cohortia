Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Remember
Shared responsibility
Providers secure the infrastructure; you secure configuration and data.
Automate safely
Use automation for repeatable low-risk fixes and always log actions.
Preserve evidence
Enable CloudTrail, VPC Flow Logs and protect logs with versioning and MFA Delete.
One-line takeaway

Proactive testing, layered controls and prepared response processes make architectures resilient and auditable.
