Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › IAM vs Network
Identity & Access (IAM)
Problem
Overly permissive roles and wildcard policies allow privilege escalation and data access.
Remediation
Apply least privilege, resource ARNs, policy conditions and role separation for services and users.
Example
An IAM role used by many services had broad 's3:*'; restrict to bucket ARNs and narrow actions to only what is needed.
Network Exposure
Problem
Security groups or NACLs that allow wide access expose internal resources to the internet.
Remediation
Place services in private subnets, restrict ports and sources, and use bastion hosts or VPNs for management access.
Check
Attempt a controlled probe from a test host without the app role; monitor CloudTrail and VPC Flow Logs for unexpected access.
