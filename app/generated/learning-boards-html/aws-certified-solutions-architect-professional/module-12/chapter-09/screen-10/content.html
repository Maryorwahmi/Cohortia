Architecture

Cloud Center of Excellence (CCOE)

A cross-functional hub that creates patterns, governance, tools, and training to scale cloud maturity.

Responsibilities
  • Define strategy, governance, and security baselines.
  • Create reference architectures and IaC templates.
  • Run enablement, training, and a peer network.
  • Measure adoption and optimize cost and operations.
Structure
Core team of architects, security, and operations plus ambassadors embedded in product teams for two-way feedback.
Architect’s Contribution
Design and maintain reference templates, mentor project teams, and incorporate implementation feedback into standards.
Metrics of Success
  • Reference architecture adoption rate.
  • Reduction in duplicated templates and effort.
  • Faster secure onboarding of new projects.
Key Takeaway

A CCOE enables scale by providing approved patterns, training, and automation while keeping room for team-level innovation.
