Comparison

Tailoring Communication

Match message format and level of detail to stakeholder priorities.

Developers
APIs, patterns, runbooks, and deployment checklists.
Business / Product
User impact, ROI, and time-to-market trade-offs.
Executives
Strategic alignment, risk, and financial impact in one slide.
Layered Deliverable
One-paragraph exec summary • One-page impact/risk • Technical appendix
This structure preserves clarity and permits deeper discussion for each audience.
Concrete Example
Propose serverless: executives see cost and time-to-market, product owners see feature velocity, developers receive operational checklists and error-handling patterns.
Key Takeaway

Adapt language and artifacts to the audience: factual detail for implementers, and clear impacts for decision-makers.
