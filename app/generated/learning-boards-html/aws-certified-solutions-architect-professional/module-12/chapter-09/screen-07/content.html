Definition

Active Listening and Avoiding Jargon

Turn stakeholder input into measurable requirements and shared understanding.

Technique
  • Ask clarifying questions.
  • Repeat intent in plain language.
  • Turn goals into measurable targets.
Example
When asked for 'lower latency', ask for a metric and a user story, then restate as 'reduce p95 from 800ms to 200ms for checkout flows'.
Quick exercise
One-paragraph summary
Write a plain-language summary of a current technical decision and validate it with a non-technical colleague.
Key Takeaway

Listening and translation reduce rework and ensure solutions meet stakeholder needs.
