Common Pitfalls

Measuring Influence

Select metrics that reflect both technical health and organizational enablement.

Suggested Metrics
  • Reference template adoption rate.
  • Incident reduction related to standards.
  • Time-to-onboard new projects.
  • Number of teams trained or supported.
Pitfalls
Avoid measuring only technical metrics; avoid creating standards without enforcement or support for adoption.
Dashboard Example
One-page summary: top-line metric, short example of a team using a template, trend chart of incidents, and a training attendance number. Use visuals to link metrics to outcomes.
Key Takeaway

Measure adoption and outcomes, not just activity. Tie metrics to business improvements to show real influence.
