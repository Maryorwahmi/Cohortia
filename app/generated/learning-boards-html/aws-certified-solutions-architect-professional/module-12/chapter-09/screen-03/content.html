Practical Skills to Practice

Small habits and verified milestones build credibility and influence quickly.

Quick Win
2-minute Executive Brief
State the problem, recommended architecture, and business impact in under two minutes.
Governance
One-page Standard
Draft a standard for a common resource with enforcement notes.
Learning
Weekly Micro-POC
Deploy a small example to learn a new service or pattern.
Key Takeaway

Break strategic work into small, verifiable milestones that demonstrate value and gain support.

Mnemonic
Brief, Build, Bake: brief stakeholders, build a POC, bake governance into CI/CD.
