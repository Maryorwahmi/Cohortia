Process

Continuous Learning Roadmap

Make learning predictable with a timeline of small, verifiable activities.

Weekly
Read an AWS blog or whitepaper and summarize one operational insight you can test quickly.
Monthly
Build a small proof-of-concept to validate service behavior and chasedown operational assumptions.
Quarterly
Present findings to peers or the CCOE and update your roadmap based on organizational priorities.
Key Takeaway

A predictable cadence of learning activities turns curiosity into organizational capability.

Learning Log
Keep a simple table: date, topic, artifact (link or repo), next step. Use it to show impact in reviews.
Alignment Tip
Prioritize learning that aligns with your organization's roadmap to deliver immediate value.
