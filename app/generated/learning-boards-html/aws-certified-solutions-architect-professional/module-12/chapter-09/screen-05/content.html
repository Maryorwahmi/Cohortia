Process Flow

Technical Leadership and Mentorship

A repeatable approach turns good ideas into adopted practices.

Step 1 — Observe
Collect patterns, pain points, and inconsistencies across teams.
Step 2 — Define
Draft a standard, template, and clear acceptance criteria.
Step 3 — Pilot
Test with one team, get feedback, refine operational assumptions.
Step 4 — Scale
Automate checks, publish templates, and run training to accelerate adoption.
Example
Convert an ad-hoc VPC setup into an approved VPC template, pilot it with a single application, then enforce it through CI/CD validations.
Pro-tip
Low-friction pilots win adoption
Start with minimal changes that deliver immediate operational benefit and build on that trust.
Check your understanding
List one pattern you could pilot and the measurable outcome that will show success.
Key Takeaway

Translate observation into repeatable patterns, validated by pilots, and scaled with automation and training.
