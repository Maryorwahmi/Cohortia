Ethics

Ethical Considerations and Responsible Innovation

Make privacy, fairness, and sustainability explicit constraints in your designs.

Privacy & Data Protection
Minimize data collection, enforce encryption and retention policies, and require audit logs for access to sensitive data.
Fairness in AI/ML
Assess training data sources, measure potential bias, and document mitigations alongside model deployment plans.
Sustainability
Right-size resources, prefer efficient architectures, and include environmental considerations in trade-off analysis when appropriate.
Ethics Checklist
  • What data is collected and retained?
  • Who can access this data and why?
  • Has bias been evaluated?
  • Is there an operational audit trail?
Key Takeaway

Embed ethics into design reviews so responsible choices are visible, measurable, and prioritized alongside cost and risk.
