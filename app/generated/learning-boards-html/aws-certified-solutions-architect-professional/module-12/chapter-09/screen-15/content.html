What to Remember

Three practical reminders you can apply right away.

State Trade-offs
Explain the business impact of technical choices for decision-makers.
Automate Enforcement
Use Config rules, SCPs, or CI/CD checks to make standards effective.
Publish Artifacts
Share templates, POCs, and summaries so the organization learns faster.
Final Takeaway

Small, verifiable actions and shared artifacts are the most effective path to becoming a strategic architect.
