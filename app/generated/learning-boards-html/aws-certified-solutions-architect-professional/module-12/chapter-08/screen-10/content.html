Embed sustainability

  • • Add sustainability checks to architecture reviews.
  • • Schedule regular right-sizing and snapshot clean-up tasks.
  • • Share Carbon Footprint Tool reports with stakeholders.
Why it matters

Repeatable governance turns one-off savings into organization-wide impact and links technical work to ESG commitments.

Practical checklist

Architecture review prompts

Is this workload right-sized? Can it use serverless or managed services? Have we minimized cross-region transfer?

Operational tasks

Automate schedules for non-prod, enforce lifecycle rules, and run Compute Optimizer reviews quarterly.
