Architecting for Sustainability and Environmental Impact

Learn pragmatic design patterns and monitoring practices that reduce energy use and carbon emissions for cloud workloads. These ideas help you build systems that are efficient, cost-effective, and aligned with ESG goals.

Right-sizing
Identify over-provisioned compute and database resources; match capacity to real usage to cut waste and cost.
Power-efficient services
Prefer serverless and Graviton-based instance types to increase performance per watt.
Measure & report
Use the Customer Carbon Footprint Tool and usage metrics to make design trade-offs visible to stakeholders.
