Process Flow › Data Transfer & Storage

Practical steps

  1. Analyze traffic: Identify cross-region and internet egress hotspots.
  2. Cache: Use CloudFront to serve content closer to users and reduce origin load.
  3. Tier storage: Apply lifecycle rules to move cold data to Glacier classes.
  4. Clean up: Delete snapshots and unused objects regularly.
Key Takeaway

Architect to keep hot data nearby and move cold data to efficient tiers to lower storage energy and transfer costs.

Concrete example

Move log archives older than 90 days to Glacier Deep Archive using lifecycle rules and delete unattached snapshots to shrink storage footprint and reduce maintenance cost.

Common mistake

Assuming cold data has no impact. Aggregated cold storage adds ongoing energy and storage overhead that lifecycle rules can address.
