Lab Walkthrough › Compute Optimizer Review

Steps to follow

  1. Launch or identify an over-provisioned instance and run with a representative workload to collect metrics.
  2. Enable Compute Optimizer and wait for analysis to complete.
  3. Review recommendations, savings opportunity, and finding reason in the console.
  4. Test a recommended type in non-production and validate performance under load.
  5. Apply change in production during a maintenance window and monitor closely.
Key Takeaway

Compute Optimizer supports evidence-based right-sizing, but validation is essential to avoid performance risk.

Scenario notes

Simulate minimal workload on a t3.xlarge, let metrics accumulate, opt into Compute Optimizer, then compare suggested t3.medium or similar types as candidates.

Safety note

Do not change production instance types without backups and validation; stopping and changing types can affect disks and attached resources.
