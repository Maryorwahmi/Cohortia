Summary › Key Concepts
Sustainable Architecture
Design workloads to minimize energy use and data movement while meeting performance and cost targets.
Right-sizing
Adjust instance and database sizes to match workload needs and avoid over-provisioning.
Power Efficiency
Prefer serverless and Graviton-based instances when appropriate to improve performance per watt.
Takeaway

Small operational changes and careful service choices add up. Measure with the Carbon Footprint Tool and include sustainability in design governance.
