Monitoring workflow

  1. Baseline: Capture current usage and projected emissions from the Carbon Footprint Tool.
  2. Optimize: Apply right-sizing, schedules, lifecycle transitions, or serverless migrations.
  3. Measure: Revisit the tool to compare projected emissions and historical trends.
  4. Report: Use visualized trends to inform stakeholders and prioritize further work.
Key Takeaway

Measurement closes the loop: optimization without reporting cannot demonstrate ESG progress to stakeholders.

Concrete example

After enabling schedules and moving cold archives, check the Carbon Footprint Tool for a reduction in projected emissions and use that graph in your sustainability report.

Common mistake

Relying only on cost reduction as proof of sustainability. Use the footprint tool to show emissions impact explicitly.
