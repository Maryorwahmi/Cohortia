Learning Objectives

Master practical sustainability patterns and measurement so your architecture choices reduce environmental impact and report clearly to stakeholders.

01
Understand sustainable architecture

Define what makes a cloud design environmentally responsible and why it matters for cost and compliance.

Mnemonic: Venn of performance, cost, and energy
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify efficient services

Recognize when serverless or Graviton-based instances reduce power per unit of work and prefer managed services where suitable.

Mnemonic: Three pillars — serverless, Graviton, managed
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Optimize resources

Apply right-sizing, shutdown schedules, and lifecycle policies to reduce idle and unnecessary storage.

Mnemonic: Use, Trim, Archive
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Measure and report

Use the Customer Carbon Footprint Tool to monitor emissions and align architecture choices with ESG metrics.

Mnemonic: Measure, Act, Report
