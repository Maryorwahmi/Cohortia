Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Why connectivity is the foundation
Definition

Connectivity is the network fabric linking on‑premises systems and AWS. It determines latency, bandwidth, and the predictability of traffic between locations.

Comparison
Direct Connect
Dedicated, private connection. Predictable performance and improved throughput.
Site-to-Site VPN
IPSec tunnel over the internet. Fast setup and cost-effective for many use cases.
Common mistake
Relying on a single VPN tunnel or a single customer gateway device creates a single point of failure for connectivity.
Key takeaway
Match connection type to workload: prioritize Direct Connect for predictable, high‑throughput needs and use VPN for rapid or backup connectivity.
Scenario cards
Production database replication
Needs consistent latency and high bandwidth for replication and queries. Direct Connect is preferable because it avoids internet variability.
Branch office access
Branch offices or development teams need quick access with limited budget. A Site-to-Site VPN provides encrypted connectivity quickly and cost-effectively.
Mixed deployment
Combine Direct Connect for critical traffic and a VPN as fallback. Use routing and Transit Gateway to prefer the private path while keeping a resilient tunnel available.
