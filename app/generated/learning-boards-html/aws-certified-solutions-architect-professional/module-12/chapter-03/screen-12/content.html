Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Direct Connect vs. VPN
Side-by-side
Direct Connect
Predictable, private, high-throughput. Longer provisioning lead time and higher cost.
Site-to-Site VPN
Encrypted over internet, fast to deploy, cost-effective. Performance can vary with internet conditions.
Decision guide
  • Need deterministic latency and high bandwidth → Direct Connect.
  • Need rapid deployment or lower cost → Site-to-Site VPN.
  • Hybrid approach → Direct Connect primary, VPN fallback.
Key Takeaway
Frame the recommendation around performance needs, time to provision, and cost, and include redundancy in either choice.
