Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Key Concepts
Hybrid Cloud
Combines on‑premises infrastructure with AWS so systems can interoperate.
Direct Connect
A dedicated private connection that bypasses the internet for predictable performance.
Site-to-Site VPN
Encrypted tunnel over the internet, quick to deploy and good for backups or branch connectivity.
Summary takeaway

Match connectivity and transfer choices to workload needs, centralize routing with Transit Gateway as you scale, and integrate identity in a way that preserves a single source of truth.
