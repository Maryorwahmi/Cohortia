Learning Objectives
Learn the skills to design, enforce, and operate governance controls across accounts, and to monitor and remediate compliance issues automatically.
01
Design Multi-account Strategies
Group accounts into OUs that mirror teams and compliance boundaries so policies can be targeted and maintained at scale.
Mnemonic: OU = Organization Unit
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Apply Service Control Policies
Use SCPs to set maximum permissions across OUs. SCPs prevent actions; they do not grant permissions.
Mnemonic: SCP = Safety Cap
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Monitor with AWS Config
Record configurations, evaluate resource compliance continuously, and trigger actions when rules are violated.
Mnemonic: Config = Continuous Check
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Automate Remediation
Use Config Rules and Lambda to repair non-compliant resources, reducing manual toil and shortening detection-to-fix time.
Mnemonic: Remediate = Repair Fast
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
