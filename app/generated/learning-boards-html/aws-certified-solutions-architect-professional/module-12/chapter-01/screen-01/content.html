Architecting for Governance and Compliance
Create organizational guardrails, continuous monitoring, and automated remediation to keep a multi-account AWS footprint secure and auditable.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson
Multi-account strategy
Group accounts into OUs and apply SCPs to enforce guardrails at scale.
Continuous monitoring
Use AWS Config and CloudTrail to detect drift and record actions for audits.
Automated remediation
Link Config Rules to Lambda to fix issues such as missing bucket encryption.
Why this matters
Governance keeps a large AWS footprint manageable and auditable. Preventive policies reduce risk; continuous monitoring shortens incident response time.
