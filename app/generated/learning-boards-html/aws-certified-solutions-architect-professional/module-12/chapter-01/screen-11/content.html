Definition
Shared Responsibility Model
Distinguish AWS responsibilities from customer responsibilities and map them to compliance controls.
AWS: Security of the cloud
Physical infrastructure, networking, and the foundational managed services are AWS responsibility.
You: Security in the cloud
Configure services securely, manage identities, encrypt data, and collect evidence required by compliance frameworks.
Audit readiness
Prepare evidence demonstrating customer-side controls: IAM configurations, Config rule results, and CloudTrail logs.
Key Takeaway

When designing for compliance, map each control to the responsible party and ensure you collect and protect the required evidence.

Contrast & examples
Example: Managed DB
AWS secures the underlying hardware. You configure encryption, user access, network isolation, and backups to satisfy compliance controls.
Misconception
A service being 'managed' does not remove customer obligations to configure and operate controls required by regulatory frameworks.
Check
For a given control, ask: who provides it, who configures it, and how will we show evidence to an auditor?
