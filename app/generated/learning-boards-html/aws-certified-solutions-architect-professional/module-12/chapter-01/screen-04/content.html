Core Concept
The architect’s governance responsibilities
Governance includes account design, guardrails, monitoring, and automated remediation to enforce policy at scale.
Definition
Governance is the set of policies and operational patterns that ensure security, compliance, and consistency across multiple AWS accounts.
Concrete example
Group finance accounts into an OU, attach stricter SCPs to deny unapproved regions and services, and collect logs centrally for auditors.
Common misconception
Governance is not only blocking: you also need detective controls and remediation to operate safely and show evidence to auditors.
Key Takeaway

Design governance as prevention, detection, and remediation. Use OUs to mirror risk boundaries and ensure evidence collection for audits.

Analogy & scenario
Analogy
Think of governance as traffic rules: guardrails (SCPs) set where you cannot drive, signs (Config + CloudTrail) record infractions, and patrols (Lambda) correct dangerous behavior automatically.
Scenario
A developer accidentally creates an unencrypted storage bucket in a dev account. With governance in place, Config flags the bucket and a remediation Lambda applies default encryption, preventing data exposure.
Check your understanding
When would you choose a preventive SCP versus a detective Config rule? Consider the impact on developer productivity and audit evidence needs.
