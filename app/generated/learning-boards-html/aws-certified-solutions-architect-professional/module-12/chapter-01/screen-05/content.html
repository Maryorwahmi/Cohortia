Process Flow
AWS Organizations & OUs
Group accounts into OUs that map to business units or risk profiles, and apply policies at the OU level.
Step 1: Create Organization
Establish a management account and enable AWS Organizations to centrally manage accounts and billing.
Step 2: Create OUs
Group accounts into logical units such as dev, prod, and security for targeted policy application.
Step 3: Plan Logging and Security Accounts
Designate accounts for centralized CloudTrail logs and security tooling to protect logs from tampering.
Pro tip

Model OUs to minimize exceptions. Use a sandbox OU to validate SCPs before applying them broadly.

Reference architecture
Example OU layout
  • Root
  • ├─ Security (logging, SIEM)
  • ├─ Production (strict SCPs)
  • └─ Development (fewer restrictions)
Example policy use
Attach an SCP to Production OU that denies creating resources in disallowed regions and deleting centralized logs.
Check your understanding
Why is it safer to test SCPs in a sandbox OU before applying to production? Think about accidental denial of critical actions.
