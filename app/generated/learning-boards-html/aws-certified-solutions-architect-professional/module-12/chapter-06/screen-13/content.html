Designing Showback and Chargeback
Use CUR, tags and cost categories to produce reliable internal reports.
Showback
Provide teams with consumption reports to encourage cost-conscious behavior without billing.
Start here to build trust in data before moving to chargeback.
Chargeback
Allocate costs to teams and feed amounts into internal charges or finance systems once allocations are validated.
Requires rigorous tag enforcement and reconciled CUR data to avoid disputes.
Key Takeaway

Implement showback first for transparency, then graduate to chargeback when you have enforced tags and reconciled CUR outputs.
