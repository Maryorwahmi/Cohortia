Compare pricing models
Understand trade-offs between discount, flexibility and interruption risk.
Reserved Instances
Discounts for committed usage; best for stable, unchanging workloads.
Savings Plans
Flexible compute commitment across families/regions; preferred for evolving fleets.
Spot Instances
Deep discounts for interruptible workloads like batch and CI/CD.
Serverless
Pay per use, removes idle capacity costs for spiky or event-driven workloads.
Decision pattern
  1. Analyze historical usage with Cost Explorer or CUR for baselines.
  2. Buy commitments only for stable baselines; leave variable load uncapped.
  3. Use Spot for appropriate fault-tolerant workloads and autoscaling strategies for availability.
Key Takeaway

Match discounts to predictability: commitments for steady load, Spot and serverless for flexibility and cost efficiency.
