Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Right-Sizing & Optimizer
Right-sizing
Match resource capacity to real demand and remove idle or orphaned resources.
  • Monitor multi-week metrics via CloudWatch.
  • Use Compute Optimizer recommendations to find downsizing candidates.
  • Schedule recurring reviews and approvals before changes.
Storage and Orphans
Audit EBS volumes, snapshots and AMIs regularly. Delete outdated backups and orphaned volumes to avoid persistent costs.
Common Mistake
Right-sizing from a single-day spike; instead use historical baselines and consider peak needs.
Key Takeaway

Right-sizing is continuous: combine monitoring, optimizer suggestions, and a review workflow to reduce waste safely.
