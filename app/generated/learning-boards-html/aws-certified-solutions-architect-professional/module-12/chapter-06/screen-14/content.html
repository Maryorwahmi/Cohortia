Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Key Concepts
Cost Attribution
Assign costs to teams, projects or environments so spend is traceable.
Cost Allocation Tags
User-defined labels that enable per-resource cost grouping and filtering.
Cost Categories
Rules-based grouping of costs to match finance structures.
Summary
Combine enforced tags, cost categories and CUR-backed reconciliation to power budgets, anomaly detection, and internal billing with confidence.
