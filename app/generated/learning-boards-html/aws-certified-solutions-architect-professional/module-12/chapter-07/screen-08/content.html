Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › CFN vs CDK
CloudFormation (Declarative)
  • Explicit resource definitions ideal for audits.
  • Works well for simple, static infrastructure blueprints.
  • Templates are consumed directly by deployment stages and policy tools.
Example

A VPC template used as a StackSet baseline for all accounts.

CDK (Code-first)
  • Leverage loops, classes, and parameters for reusable constructs.
  • Improves developer productivity and reduces boilerplate.
  • Synthesizes to CloudFormation for deployment and auditing.
Example

A reusable secure-file-processor construct that creates bucket, role, and Lambda in one call.

Key takeaway

Use CDK for productivity, CloudFormation for explicit auditability, and always synthesize and validate.

Pitfall

Relying only on CDK abstractions without reviewing synthesized templates reduces audit transparency.

Question

How would you include synthesized templates in your CI artifacts for review and scanning?
