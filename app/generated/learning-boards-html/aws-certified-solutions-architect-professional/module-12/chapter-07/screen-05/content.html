Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › CloudFormation
What CloudFormation does

Declare resources and let the service create, update, or delete them as a stack. Templates can include parameters, mappings, and outputs for reuse and integration.

Example

A VPC template creates subnets, route tables, and security groups consistently across accounts.

StackSets

Deploy the same template to multiple accounts and regions from a central admin account for consistent baselines.

Supporting notes
  • Use change sets to preview modifications before applying them.
  • Capture outputs for cross-stack or cross-account references where appropriate.
  • Maintain templates in version control and tag them for releases.
Key Takeaway

CloudFormation encodes design as code; combine with StackSets and change sets for safe multi-account rollouts.

Misconception

Templates are not a substitute for architecture decisions. They execute design, but you still must design for security and resilience.
