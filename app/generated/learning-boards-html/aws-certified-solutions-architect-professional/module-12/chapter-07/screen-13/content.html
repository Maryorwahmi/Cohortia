Production readiness checklist
Roles & permissions

Least-privilege build and CloudFormation execution roles with audit logs enabled.

Artifact validation

Synthesize templates and run static scanners before deployment; keep artifacts in a versioned bucket.

Approvals & gating

Use manual approvals for critical stages and automated checks for routine validations.

Monitoring

Collect pipeline metrics and logs and alert on failed deployments or policy violations.

Key Takeaway

Operationalize IaC with hardened roles, validated artifacts, and tested rollback paths to reduce production risk.
