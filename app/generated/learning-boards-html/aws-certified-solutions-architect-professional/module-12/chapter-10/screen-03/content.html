Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Deliverables & Assessment
Key Deliverables
Items to include in your capstone packet.
Executive Summary
Concise business benefits, migration timeline, and expected ROI in plain language for executives.
High-Level Design (HLD)
Component map, interactions, and key patterns (edge, app, data, messaging, analytics).
Migration Plan & Risks
Phased tasks, rollback points, tools to use, and mitigations for each risk.
Evaluation Criteria
  • Clear mapping from business requirement to architectural decision and pillar.
  • Practical migration phases with rollback and data strategy.
  • Operational plan: monitoring, deployment automation, and incident response.
  • Cost and sustainability considerations with sensible trade-offs.
Key Takeaway

A justified architecture explains what was chosen, why it was chosen, and how you will validate it meets business goals.
