Designing a Comprehensive Enterprise Solution
Synthesize Well-Architected principles, plan a phased migration, and justify your architecture choices for an enterprise e-commerce scenario.
In this lesson
You will develop a high-level design, migration phases, service selection rationale, and risk mitigations anchored to business needs like high availability and secure hybrid connectivity.
Why it matters
Enterprises require balanced designs that meet operational, security, performance, and cost goals simultaneously. This capstone proves you can do that.
