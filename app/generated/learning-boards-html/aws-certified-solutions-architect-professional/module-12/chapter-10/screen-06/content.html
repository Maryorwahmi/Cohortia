Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Well-Architected Pillars
Pillar Comparison
Operational Excellence
Automation with CloudFormation/CDK, CI/CD pipelines, CloudWatch, runbooks and post-incident reviews.
Security
IAM, KMS, VPC controls, WAF, GuardDuty, Security Hub and clear encryption-in-transit and at-rest policies.
Reliability
Multi-AZ, Multi-Region options, asynchronous queues, health checks, and documented RTO/RPOs.
Performance, Cost & Sustainability
Performance Efficiency: Choose right-sized compute, caches, CloudFront, Graviton instances, and database types tuned to access patterns.
Cost Optimization: Savings Plans, Spot, S3 lifecycle, tagging, Cost Explorer, and budgeting to track and reduce spend.
Sustainability: Prefer serverless and right-sizing, schedule non-prod shutdowns, and select efficient instance types to reduce energy consumption.
Pro-tip: For each major component include a one-line pillar mapping in your HLD so reviewers can see the reasoning at a glance.
