Business Outcomes

State expected availability, performance, and cost improvements in measurable terms the business cares about.

Migration Plan

Show phases, duration, critical dependencies, and a go/no-go checklist for each milestone.

Risks & Mitigations

List top risks with impact levels and specific mitigations so stakeholders understand residual exposure.

Closing Prompt
Conclude with the ask and a single question for decision makers: what is the primary constraint we should optimize for — speed, cost, or risk?
