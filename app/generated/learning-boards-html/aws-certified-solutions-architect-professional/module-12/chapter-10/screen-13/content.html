Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Key Concepts
Requirements-First

Every technical decision must map to a stated business or technical requirement and a Well-Architected pillar.

Phased Migration

Use rehost, re-platform, then re-architect phases with tests, replication, and rollback gates.

Ops & DR

Include automated deployments, monitoring, and practiced DR plans so the design runs reliably at scale.

Summary
A compelling proposal ties technical choices to measurable business outcomes, reduces migration risk through phases, and proves operational readiness with monitoring and rehearsed recovery.
