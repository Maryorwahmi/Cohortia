Cost Strategies
Tactical levers for immediate savings
  • Use Savings Plans / Reserved Instances for steady compute.
  • Adopt Spot Instances for batch and fault-tolerant processing.
  • Right-size and prefer Graviton where supported.
  • Leverage serverless (Lambda, Fargate, DynamoDB on-demand) for unpredictable traffic.
Operational Controls
Tagging, budgets, automated shutdowns for dev/stage, and automated scaling policies. Monitor cost with alerts tied to budgets.
Sustainability Actions
Prefer managed and serverless services, implement shutdown schedules, and choose efficient instance families. Measure utilization to reduce wasted capacity.
Key Takeaway

Combine cost instruments with operations and sustainability goals; show projected savings and environmental impact in your proposal.
