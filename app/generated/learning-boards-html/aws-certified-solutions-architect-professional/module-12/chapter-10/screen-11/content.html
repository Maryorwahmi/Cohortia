Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Reliability & DR
Strategy
Pattern selection by RTO/RPO
Pilot Light
Minimal resources in DR region; scale up on failover. Cost-effective but longer RTO.
Warm Standby
Small live footprint that scales up quickly; medium RTO and cost.
Active-Active
Full active deployment across regions; lowest RTO at higher cost and complexity.
Data strategies
  • DynamoDB Global Tables for active-active low RPO
  • Aurora Global Database or cross-region replicas for relational DR
  • S3 cross-region replication for assets and backups
Checklist
Define RTO/RPO, choose pattern per workload, automate failover, and rehearse the playbook regularly.
Key Takeaway

Test DR plans under realistic load to validate RTO/RPO and operational readiness.
