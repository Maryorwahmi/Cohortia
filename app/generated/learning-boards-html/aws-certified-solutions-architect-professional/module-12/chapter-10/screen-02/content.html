Learning Objectives
Master the capstone deliverables and the reasoning that supports them.
01
Holistic Design
Integrate multiple AWS services into a cohesive multi-tier architecture. Explain how each component meets a business requirement and which pillar it supports.
02
Well-Architected Application
Apply the six pillars to evaluate security, reliability, and cost. Produce explicit trade-offs and mitigation strategies tied to each pillar.
03
Phased Migration
Design a sequence: rehost, re-platform, re-architect. Include data migration, rollback, and dependency mitigation plans for each phase.
04
Presentation & Justification
Produce an executive summary, HLD, detailed component choices, risk register, and cost estimate that collectively justify the architecture.
Verified Competency Milestone
Complete the capstone deliverable and justify each major decision using a Well-Architected pillar reference.

Key Takeaway

This capstone measures your ability to choose services for clear business reasons, not service memorization. Focus on trade-offs and measurable outcomes.
