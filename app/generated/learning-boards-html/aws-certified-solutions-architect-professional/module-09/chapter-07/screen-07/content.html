Canary steps
  1. Deploy new version to a separate subset of tasks or target group.
  2. Route a small percentage of traffic (for example 5%) to canary.
  3. Monitor CloudWatch alarms and business metrics during a short validation window.
  4. If healthy, increase traffic in steps until 100%.
  5. If alarms trigger, automatically rollback and stop further shifts.
Why choose Canary
  • Limits blast radius of unknown regressions.
  • Tests real user scenarios that synthetic tests may miss.
  • Requires automated metrics and rollback configuration.
Pro tip: define both technical and business metrics for canary validation.
