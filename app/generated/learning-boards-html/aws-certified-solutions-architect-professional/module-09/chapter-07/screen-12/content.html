Definition › EKS Deployment Patterns
Kubernetes-native flow
  • Build image in CodeBuild and push to ECR.
  • Use kubectl or Helm to update deployments from CI.
  • Rolling updates are handled by the Kubernetes controller.
Progressive delivery on EKS

Use a service mesh or ingress controller to split traffic for Canary or Blue/Green. Helm charts capture configuration and make upgrades repeatable.

Common misconception: Kubernetes rolling updates provide the same rollback guarantees as Blue/Green without a separate environment — they do not.
Key Takeaway

To apply Canary or Blue/Green semantics in EKS, integrate a service mesh or create separate environments and orchestrate traffic splits from your pipeline.
