Definition › ECS + CodeDeploy
How CodeDeploy manages ECS
  • Creates new tasks for Green, registers them to a secondary target group, and shifts traffic via ALB.
  • Uses AutoRollback on CloudWatch alarm events to revert traffic to Blue.
  • Supports Canary and linear traffic-shift options in configuration.
Concrete scenario

When releasing to Fargate, CodeDeploy will provision Green tasks, route a small Canary percentage for N minutes, and then complete the shift if alarms remain healthy.

Common mistake

Not configuring alarms for relevant metrics. Without proper alarms, automatic rollback cannot protect against silent regressions.

Key Takeaway

Combine ALB-controlled traffic shifting with CloudWatch alarms to enable automated rollback for ECS deployments.
