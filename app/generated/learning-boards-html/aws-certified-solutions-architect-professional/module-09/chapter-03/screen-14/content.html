Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Asynchronous Communication

Producers send messages without waiting for immediate responses, enabling independent scaling and retryable processing.

Message Queue

Stores messages until consumed; SQS provides durable buffering with visibility timeout and DLQ controls.

Event Stream

A continuous sequence of records, consumed by multiple independent applications for analytics and archival; ordering is per-shard.

Summary takeaway

Pick primitives that match ordering and durability needs, and design idempotent consumers to tolerate retries and duplicates.
