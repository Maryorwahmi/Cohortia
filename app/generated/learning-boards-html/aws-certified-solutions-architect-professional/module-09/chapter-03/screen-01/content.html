Decoupling with Message Queues and Event Streams

Learn how SQS, SNS, Kinesis and EventBridge let systems communicate without blocking, preserve delivery properties you need, and integrate reliably across services.

In this lesson

You will compare push vs pull models, examine SQS Standard and FIFO semantics, review SNS fan-out, explore Kinesis streaming, and see EventBridge for app-level events.

Why it matters

Decoupling prevents a single slow service from stalling the entire system, enables retries and DLQs, and helps split responsibilities between ingestion, processing, and notification.
