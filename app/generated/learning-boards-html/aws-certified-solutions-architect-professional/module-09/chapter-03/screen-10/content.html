Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Decoupled Trading Pipeline
Architecture summary
Ingest orders to Kinesis for ordered, durable streaming. Use a Kinesis consumer to validate and publish processed events to SNS. SNS fans out to SQS FIFO, Lambda, and EventBridge for independent consumers.
Why each service
  • Kinesis: ordered ingestion and multiple independent consumers.
  • SNS: fast fan-out to many targets.
  • SQS FIFO: ordered, exactly-once delivery for core fulfillment.
  • EventBridge: application-level routing for other microservices.
Key Takeaway

Compose services to match the semantics you need: ordering, fan-out, durability, and routing each have their native solution.

Scenario callout

A trade arrives in Kinesis. A validator Lambda reads the shard, enriches the trade, and publishes an "OrderProcessed" message to SNS. SNS fans out to a FIFO SQS queue for fulfillment, a Lambda to notify traders, and an EventBridge bus for routing to risk and compliance services.

Operational checklist
  • Plan Kinesis shards for peak throughput.
  • Use FIFO where order matters and partition MessageGroupId to avoid bottlenecks.
  • Attach DLQs to critical queues and monitor consumer lag.
