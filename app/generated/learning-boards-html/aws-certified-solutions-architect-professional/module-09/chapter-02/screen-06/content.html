Process Flow
Auto Scaling Groups — Fundamentals
Maintain capacity, replace fails, and distribute across AZs
Step 1
Define group and launch template
Provide a launch template, VPC subnets, and the target group for the ALB.
Step 2
Set Min/Desired/Max
Choose Min for baseline redundancy, Max to cap cost, Desired for steady state.
Step 3
Attach health checks and target groups
Use ELB health checks plus a grace period for instance warm-up.
Example
An ASG with Min=2, Desired=2, Max=10 across 2 AZs keeps baseline availability and allows fast scale during traffic bursts.
Common mistake
Setting MinSize to 1 creates a single-point-of-failure in multi-AZ setups; also keep MaxSize high enough to meet peak demand.
Key Takeaway

ASGs provide controlled, resilient horizontal scaling. Tune sizes, health checks, and warm-up to align capacity with real readiness.
