Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › Practical Skills
01 Verify scaling axis
Describe a workload and justify horizontal or vertical scaling with a short checklist: stateful? single point of failure? predictable peaks?
02 Validate ASG
Create an ASG, attach to an ALB, generate load, and record CloudWatch metrics during scale actions.
03 Test load balancing
Prove ALB path-based routing and NLB static IP behavior with a short test script or curl calls.
Key Takeaway

Practical verification through configuration and monitoring is the final proof that a scaling design works as intended under real load.
