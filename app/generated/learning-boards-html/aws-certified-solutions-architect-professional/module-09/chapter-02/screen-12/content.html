Definition
AWS Lambda and Serverless Elasticity
Event-driven scaling with trade-offs in latency and concurrency
Auto scale by concurrency
Lambda spins up environments to match concurrent invocations, scaling to meet demand without you provisioning hosts.
Cold start and provisioned concurrency
Cold starts add latency; provisioned concurrency keeps function instances warm at an extra cost to guarantee low latency.
Concurrency limits
Account-level concurrency and reserved concurrency are important for reliability at scale.
Concrete example
Use Lambda consumers for SQS-based backend processing; concurrency follows queue depth, decoupling producers from consumers.
Misconception
Lambda always saves money — not necessarily for long-running CPU tasks. Analyze costs against duration and concurrency.
