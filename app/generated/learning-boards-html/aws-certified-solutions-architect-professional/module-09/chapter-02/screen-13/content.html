Warm-up, Metrics, Cost, and Avoiding Flapping
Operational tuning that prevents instability and waste
Warm-up and Grace
Allow new instances time to initialize before routing traffic. Configure health check grace periods and instance warm-up to match initialization time.
Metric selection
Choose CPU for CPU-bound, network/disk I/O for I/O-bound, and queue depth for asynchronous systems.
Cost tactics
Use Spot for fault-tolerant workloads, and serverless/Fargate for variable demand to reduce idle costs.
Avoid flapping
Set reasonable cooldowns and thresholds. Combine scheduled pre-scaling with reactive target tracking to reduce repeated scale events.
Concrete test
Run a controlled load test while watching CloudWatch metrics and scaling events. Adjust targets and warm-up until behavior matches expectations.
Key Takeaway

Tune both the responsiveness and the safety of your scaling: ready instances, appropriate metrics, and conservative cooldowns keep systems stable and costs reasonable.
