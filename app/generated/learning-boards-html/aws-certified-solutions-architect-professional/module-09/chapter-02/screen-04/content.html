Core Concept
Scalability and Elasticity Defined
Understand the difference and practical implications
Definition
Scalability
  • Ability to handle increased load by adding capacity.
  • Can be vertical (bigger instance) or horizontal (more instances).
Example: adding read replicas and larger instances during growth.
Elasticity
  • Automatic acquire/release of resources to match demand.
  • Reduces over-provisioning and cost while preserving performance.
Example: ASG scales out during a flash sale and scales in afterwards.
Misconception
Scaling manually is the same as being elastic — false. Automation is required for elasticity.
Why it matters
Prevents downtime, reduces cost, and supports unpredictable growth patterns.
