ASG Sizing and AZ Distribution
Rules of thumb for safe capacity settings
MinSize
Baseline capacity. Set at least to the number of AZs you require for redundancy.
DesiredCapacity
Target steady-state count. Use this for predictable steady load and update with scheduled scaling when needed.
MaxSize
Upper bound to control cost and avoid runaway scaling in response to spikes or misconfiguration.
Concrete setup
For a two-AZ web tier: Min=2, Desired=2, Max=8. Attach to an ALB and enable ELB health checks with 300s grace if instances have initialization tasks.
Common mistake
Ignoring warm-up: traffic routed to not-yet-ready instances leads to errors and false health failovers.
