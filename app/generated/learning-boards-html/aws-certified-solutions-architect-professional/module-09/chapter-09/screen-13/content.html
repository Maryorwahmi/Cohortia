Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Pitfalls & Troubleshooting
Common mistakes
  • Tight coupling between functions instead of using events.
  • Overly permissive IAM roles during quick deploys.
  • Neglecting cold starts for latency-critical paths.
  • Insufficient logging and missing alarms for throttles and errors.
Troubleshooting checklist
Reproduce with realistic load and inspect CloudWatch logs and X-Ray traces.
Check throttling and concurrency limits; adjust reserved concurrency if needed.
Validate VPC and ENI behavior if functions have long initialization times in a VPC.
Key Takeaway

Instrument and reproduce issues; address coupling, IAM, and cold starts first to reduce common failures.
