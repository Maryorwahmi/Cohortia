Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Distributed Transactions
Challenges
  • ACID guarantees don't span independent service datastores.
  • Two-phase commit increases blocking and reduces availability.
  • Retries without idempotency cause duplicates or inconsistent state.
Key Takeaway

Design compensating transactions, idempotency, and tracing instead of attempting 2PC across services.

Mitigations
Compensating Transactions
Define actions that undo a previous step when later steps fail, like releasing reservations or issuing refunds.
Idempotency
Design operations to be safe to repeat; use unique request identifiers and conditional writes where available.
Correlation IDs & Observability
Pass a unique ID through all services and use logs/tracing to reconstruct Saga state for troubleshooting.
