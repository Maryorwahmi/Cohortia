Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Key Concepts
Data Ownership
Each service owns its datastore and exposes data through APIs.
Polyglot Persistence
Choose Aurora, DynamoDB, ElastiCache, Neptune, DocumentDB, or Timestream per need.
Sagas & Consistency
Use eventual consistency and Sagas with compensations and idempotency to coordinate multi-service flows.
Summary

Design for ownership, pick datastores by workload, and coordinate cross-service transactions using Sagas, idempotency, and strong observability.
