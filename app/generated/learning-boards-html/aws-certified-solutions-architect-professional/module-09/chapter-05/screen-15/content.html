Ownership
Service owns its datastore and API contract.
Datastore fit
Match access pattern to Aurora, DynamoDB, ElastiCache, Neptune, DocumentDB, or Timestream.
Consistency ops
Use Sagas, compensations, idempotency, correlation IDs, and observability to manage distributed transactions.
One-line takeaway

Design services to own data, choose the best datastore for the job, and coordinate multi-step workflows with Sagas and strong observability.
