Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Saga Pattern
Two implementations
Choreography
Services publish and consume events to drive next steps. Simpler for small flows, harder to trace for larger ones.
Orchestration
A central orchestrator manages the sequence and compensating actions. Use Step Functions when you need control and visibility.
Key Takeaway

Design your Saga style based on complexity and the need for traceability; always plan compensating transactions and idempotency.

Enrollment Saga — Scenario Card
1) Enrollment service writes PENDING to DynamoDB and emits Enrollment Initiated. 2) Payment service processes payment and emits Payment Succeeded or Payment Failed. 3) On success, Enrollment service sets CONFIRMED and optionally calls Course service API to increment counts. 4) On failure, publish failure event and run compensations like refund or mark FAILED.
Anti-pattern
Two-phase commit across services and heterogeneous datastores — avoid due to blocking and availability problems.
Pro tip
Use correlation IDs to trace Saga progress across services and to troubleshoot compensating actions when they run.
