Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Replication & Caching
Patterns and AWS features
RDS / Aurora Read Replicas
Offload read traffic; use replicas for reporting and analytics to protect primary throughput.
DynamoDB Global Tables
Multi-region replication for low-latency reads and multi-active writes.
ElastiCache
Use cache-aside for predictable semantics; choose TTLs and invalidation strategies to avoid stale reads.
Global Databases
Aurora Global Database supports cross-region DB replication for disaster recovery and local reads.
Example: Product Pages
Cache product details in ElastiCache with a cache-aside pattern. Use a read replica for analytical queries like "most viewed" dashboards. When product updates occur, invalidate or update the cache and accept brief replica lag for analytics.
Pitfall
Ignoring TTLs and invalidation leads to stale data in cache and user confusion.
Key Takeaway

Design caching and replication together. Plan for invalidation, TTLs, and replica lag to maintain correctness and performance.
