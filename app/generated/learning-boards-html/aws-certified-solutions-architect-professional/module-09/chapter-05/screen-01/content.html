Data Management in Multi-Service Architectures
Why data ownership, polyglot persistence, and Saga-based consistency matter for reliable distributed systems.
In this lesson

Understand database-per-service, pick AWS services for patterns, design consistency using eventual consistency and Saga, and apply caching and replication.

Why it matters

Loose coupling of services, independent scaling, and lower blast radius are only possible if data boundaries are clearly owned and the right datastore is chosen per workload.
