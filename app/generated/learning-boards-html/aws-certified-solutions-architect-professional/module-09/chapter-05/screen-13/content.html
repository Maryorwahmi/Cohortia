Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Common Mistakes
Top mistakes
  • Shared database across services for convenience.
  • Attempting two-phase commit across heterogeneous stores.
  • Insufficient observability for Sagas and compensations.
Key Takeaway

Replace convenience with explicit contracts, event-driven sharing, and tooling for tracing and compensations.

Practical checklist
Make operations idempotent and test retry scenarios.
Attach correlation IDs to every cross-service call and event.
Design and test compensating transactions for each Saga step.
Secure datastores and limit privileges to the owning service.
