Quick Reference
What to Remember
Three practical reminders for architects
Presentation Tier
CloudFront + S3 for static, ALB for dynamic routing and TLS termination.
Application Tier
EC2, ECS/EKS, or Lambda depending on control and scaling needs.
Data Tier
Use RDS/Aurora for transactions, DynamoDB for NoSQL scale, ElastiCache for hot reads.
Final takeaway

Use tier responsibilities to guide service selection, prioritize decoupling and redundancy, and verify your foundation with a small CloudFormation deployment.
