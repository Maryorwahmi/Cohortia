Process Flow
Decoupling Tiers with Queues and Events
Buffer work, isolate failures, and enable independent scaling
Three-step pattern
  1. Accept request and enqueue work (SQS or EventBridge).
  2. Process queue with scalable workers (ECS, Lambda).
  3. Persist results to the data tier and emit events for downstream systems.
Why it matters
Queues absorb spikes, reduce end‑user latency, and allow retry strategies without blocking primary requests.
Concrete example
A checkout endpoint responds quickly with an acknowledgement and enqueues an order processing job. Background workers handle payment capture and inventory updates, retrying on transient failures and writing final state to the database.
Common Pitfall
Not planning for idempotency or dead‑letter handling can lead to duplicate processing or lost messages.
