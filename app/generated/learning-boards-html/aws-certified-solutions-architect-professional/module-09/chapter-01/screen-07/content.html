Choosing Databases and Caching
Match storage technology to workload needs
Persistence strategy
Service overview
  • Amazon RDS and Aurora for relational, transactional data.
  • Amazon DynamoDB for serverless, high‑throughput NoSQL.
  • Amazon ElastiCache for caching hot reads and sessions.
Key Takeaway
Use polyglot persistence: pick the right store for each access pattern rather than forcing a single database to do everything.
Concrete example
Use Aurora for transactional orders, DynamoDB for user session data and product metadata with predictable keys, and ElastiCache to speed frequently read product details.
Common Mistake
Ignoring backup and failover: RDS Multi‑AZ or Aurora replicas are necessary to meet availability goals.
