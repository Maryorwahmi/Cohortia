Monolithic versus Multi‑Tier Designs
Balance speed of delivery against operational needs
Monolith
Faster initial delivery and simpler deployment, but can become harder to scale and secure as the codebase grows.
When it fits
Small teams, short‑lived prototypes, or low traffic apps.
Multi‑Tier
More operational overhead but enables independent scaling, clearer security boundaries, and better fault isolation.
When it fits
High availability production systems, regulated environments, or services with distinct scaling profiles per component.
Check
Identify one indicator—such as a sustained CPU bottleneck, frequent deployment conflicts, or a security requirement—that would justify splitting components into separate tiers for your workload.
