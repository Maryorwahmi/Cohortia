What to Remember
Key Concepts
Multi‑Tier Architecture
Separate presentation, application, and data responsibilities for modularity and scale.
Decoupling
Use queues and event buses to buffer work and enable independent scaling.
High Availability
Distribute resources across AZs and use managed failover for continuity.
Summary Takeaway

Plan tiers by responsibility, decouple heavy processing, and design for AZ redundancy to build resilient AWS applications.
