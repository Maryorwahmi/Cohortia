Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Why Observability Is Hard

Definition

In microservices, telemetry is distributed across many services and platforms. Observability means collecting and correlating these disparate signals so you can answer operational questions about behavior, latency, and errors.

Challenge
Scattered logs, many metrics, and multi-hop requests complicate root cause analysis.
Misconception
Logs alone are sufficient. They are necessary but not sufficient without metrics and traces for correlation.
Practical
Use correlation IDs, consistent timestamps, and centralized storage to make cross-service queries possible.

Example scenario

An API shows intermittent 500 errors. A metric alarm fires. CloudWatch metrics show increased 5xx rate. CloudWatch Logs reveal an external timeout. X-Ray trace shows the external call takes most of the time, pinpointing the failing dependency. This end-to-end path is only visible when metrics, logs, and traces are correlated.
