01

Centralize logs to one location

Collect logs from containers, Lambdas and EC2 into CloudWatch Logs and apply retention to balance cost and forensics.

02

Use Log Insights and OpenSearch

Search and analyze logs with CloudWatch Logs Insights and stream selected logs to OpenSearch for dashboards and full-text search.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Create alarms and dashboards

Configure metric alarms, dashboards, and anomaly detection to detect and route incidents to responders automatically.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Instrument X-Ray traces

Add X-Ray SDK instrumentation so traces and service maps reveal where latency and errors occur across services.
