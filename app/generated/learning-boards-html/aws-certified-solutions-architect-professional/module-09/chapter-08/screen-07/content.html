Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Logs Insights vs OpenSearch

CloudWatch Logs Insights

  • Integrated with CloudWatch Logs for instant queries
  • Great for short-term troubleshooting and ad-hoc aggregation
  • Fast parsing and basic aggregation functions
Use when
You need quick interaction and do not require custom visual dashboards.

OpenSearch Service

  • Full-text search and complex visualizations via OpenSearch Dashboards
  • Persistent indices and rich dashboarding for long-term trends
  • Better for correlated searches across many fields and long histories
Use when
You need sophisticated dashboards or advanced search across large volumes of logs.
Pattern
Query in Insights, stream important logs to OpenSearch for dashboards.
Pitfall
Streaming everything to OpenSearch without filters increases cost and complexity.
Takeaway
Match tool to use case: fast queries vs rich dashboards.
