Module 09 › Chapter 9.4 › What are Microservices?
Definition

Microservices explained

Each service focuses on a single business capability and can be developed, deployed, and scaled independently.

Benefits
  • Independent development and deployment
  • Targeted scaling and resilience
  • Technology heterogeneity
Trade-offs
  • Operational complexity
  • Data consistency challenges
  • Inter-service communication overhead
Key Takeaway

Microservices increase agility and resilience when services are cohesive and boundaries are clear; they fail to deliver when coupling or shared data recreate a monolith.

Analogy & Scenario

Kitchen vs food trucks

A monolith is like a single large kitchen that handles all menu items; microservices are specialized trucks, each optimizing a single dish and able to scale independently.

Concrete example
An Order service owns order logic and data; a Customer service owns profile data. They evolve independently, reducing coordination overhead for feature delivery.
Common misconception
Splitting everything into tiny services guarantees agility — false. Over-fragmentation leads to excessive inter-service calls and operational overhead.
