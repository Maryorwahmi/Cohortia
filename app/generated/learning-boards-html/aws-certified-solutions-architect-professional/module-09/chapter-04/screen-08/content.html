Module 09 › Chapter 9.4 › Asynchronous Communication
Pattern

Queues and event buses

Asynchronous messaging decouples sender and receiver and improves availability during partial failures.

SQS (queues)
Reliable, point-to-point messaging for deferred processing.
SNS, EventBridge (pub/sub)
Fan-out to multiple subscribers and application-level routing for events.
Example
Ride flow
Ride Service publishes a ride-matched event to EventBridge. Notification and Analytics services subscribe and act independently, so notifications do not block ride processing.
Caveat
Overuse of async patterns can make end-to-end flows harder to reason about and delay visibility into state changes; instrument events for tracing.
