Module 09 › Chapter 9.4 › Quick Reference
Discovery
Cloud Map, ALB, or EKS DNS keep services reachable during scale events.
Communication
Choose sync for immediate answers, async for decoupling and resilience.
Compute
Lambda for serverless, ECS for containers, EKS when Kubernetes is required.
Summary Takeaway

Use discovery, communication mode, and compute choice as your primary checklist when designing microservices on AWS.
