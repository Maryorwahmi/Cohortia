Pre-deploy resilience checklist
Quick checks to reduce risk when rolling out resilience patterns.
Timeouts and metrics present for all external calls.
Retries bounded, exponential backoff and jitter applied, and idempotency ensured.
Resource pools isolated and rate limits configured for heavy flows.
Key Takeaway

Resilience requires thoughtful defaults, limits, and observability. Test and tune continuously after deployment.
