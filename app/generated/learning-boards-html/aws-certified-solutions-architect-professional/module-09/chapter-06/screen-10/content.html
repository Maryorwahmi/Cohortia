Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Fault Isolation: AZs, Regions, and Accounts
Deployment boundaries
Use AZs for availability, regions for major outages, and accounts/VPCs for strong isolation.
• Multi-AZ reduces single AZ impacts.
• Multi-region protects against regional failures and allows cross-region failover.
• Separate accounts protect blast radius and permissions scope.
Operational note
Practice failover and measure RTO and RPO to ensure cross-region strategies meet requirements.
Decoupling & buffering
Use queues and caches to smooth bursts and allow slow recovery without immediate user impact.
Queues buffer requests during downstream outages.
Read replicas and caches provide fallback reads if a primary is overloaded.
Key Takeaway

Use layered boundaries and decoupling so failures are contained and recovery can be managed without systemic collapse.
