Retry best practices
Exponential backoff, jitter, and sensible limits prevent retries from worsening outages.
Exponential Backoff
Increase delay between retries to reduce immediate pressure on a failing service.
Jitter
Add a random component so retries do not synchronize across clients.
Max Retries & Timeouts
Limit attempts and overall wait time to avoid indefinite resource use.
Concrete example
S3 read operations may transiently fail. Retry with initial delay 0.5s, double each attempt, add jitter, and stop after 4 retries.
Common mistake
Retrying non-idempotent operations without deduplication leads to duplicate effects.
Key Takeaway

Retries must be bounded and randomized; pair them with idempotency to avoid side effects.
