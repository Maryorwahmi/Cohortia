Show competency through tests
Short assessments map to implementation and validation steps.
Circuit Breaker Test
Trip the circuit by forcing errors and verify subsequent calls are blocked for the recovery timeout.
Mnemonic: monitor → trip → half-open → recover.
Retry & Backoff Test
Introduce transient failures and observe exponential delays and jitter distribution across retries.
Mnemonic: delay grows, randomness avoids collision.
Idempotency Validation
Send duplicate messages and confirm the idempotency key avoids repeated side effects.
Mnemonic: key → store → return.
Bulkhead & Rate Limiting
Saturate one pool and confirm other pools remain responsive; verify throttling prevents overload.
Mnemonic: isolate resources, limit flows.
