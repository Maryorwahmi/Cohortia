Route 53: Routing policy checklist
Select latency, geolocation, weighted, or failover routing based on your needs.
Latency
Direct users to the region with the lowest latency.
Geolocation
Serve localized content or meet residency requirements.
Weighted
Split traffic for rollouts and experiments.
Failover
Switch traffic automatically when a primary endpoint is unhealthy.
Example mapping
Latency routing for global users, geolocation override for regulatory regions, weighted routing for canary releases, and health‑checked failover for critical APIs.
