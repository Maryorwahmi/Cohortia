Key metrics to watch
  • CacheHitRatio — high is good; low suggests fragmentation or short TTLs.
  • Requests and BytesDownloaded — track traffic and bandwidth.
  • OriginErrorRate — indicates origin problems creating retries and failures.
Use CloudWatch alarms on sudden drops in CacheHitRatio or rises in OriginErrorRate so you can investigate promptly.
Access logs
Delivered to S3, logs include viewer IP, URI, status, and hit/miss flags. They are the definitive source for diagnosing why requests bypass the cache.
# Example log fields
# date time x-edge-location sc-bytes c-ip cs-method cs-uri-stem sc-status x-edge-result-type
Key Takeaway

Use metrics to detect issues, then analyze access logs to find the cache-key or TTL configuration that explains low hit ratios or origin strain.
