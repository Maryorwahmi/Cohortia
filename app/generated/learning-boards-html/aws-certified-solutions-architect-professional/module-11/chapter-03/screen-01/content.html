Module 11 › Chapter 11.3 › Overview
Advanced CloudFront: Caching, Compression, and Security
Fine-tune cache behavior, enable edge compression, and protect your origin with WAF and origin controls.
In this lesson

Learn cache and origin policies, TTLs and cache keys, Brotli/Gzip compression, WAF at the edge, origin headers and timeouts, Origin Shield, and monitoring with CloudWatch and logs.

Why it matters

Proper configuration means more cache hits, less origin load, faster page loads, and threats blocked before they reach your application.
