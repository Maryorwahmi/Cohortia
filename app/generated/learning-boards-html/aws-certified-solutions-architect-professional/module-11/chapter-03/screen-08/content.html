Comparison › Gzip vs Brotli
Gzip
Widely supported, lower CPU cost than some Brotli modes, still effective for many assets.
Brotli
Often better compression ratios for text assets, recommended when supported by viewers for static files.
Concrete guidance
Enable both Gzip and Brotli for static assets in cache behaviors. Validate viewer Accept-Encoding to ensure compatibility and check origin compression to avoid duplication.
Key Takeaway

Brotli typically gives smaller files for text assets; enable it at the edge but confirm your origin isn’t already performing the same compression.
