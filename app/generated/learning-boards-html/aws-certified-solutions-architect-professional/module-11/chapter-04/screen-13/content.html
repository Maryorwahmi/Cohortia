Module 11 › Chapter 11.4 › Mistakes & Practices
Avoid over-engineering
If a task is a simple header change or URI rewrite, prefer CloudFront Functions over Lambda@Edge to reduce cost and latency.
Respect limits
Design for 1 ms/2 MB for Functions and 5–30s for Lambda@Edge. Validate under expected traffic to avoid unexpected failures.
State management
Edge functions are stateless. Use cookies or an external datastore (via Lambda@Edge when needed) to manage session-like data.
Checklist
  • Confirm event type meets requirement.
  • Check for network calls—Functions can’t make them.
  • Publish and monitor invocation metrics and logs.
Reflection
Which item on this checklist will reduce your deployment risk the most? Plan one immediate change before your next release.
