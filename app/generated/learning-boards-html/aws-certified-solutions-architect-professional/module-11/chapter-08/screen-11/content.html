Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Shield Overview
Shield Standard
Automatic, always-on protection for common network and transport layer attacks at no extra cost.
What it covers
  • Basic mitigation for SYN/UDP floods and reflection attacks.
  • No customer configuration required.
Shield Advanced
Paid tier with DDoS Response Team access, cost protection, and advanced diagnostics for business-critical workloads.
Key features
  • DRT access for operational support during attacks.
  • Cost protection for scaling charges caused by attacks.
  • Advanced CloudWatch metrics and attack diagnostics.
