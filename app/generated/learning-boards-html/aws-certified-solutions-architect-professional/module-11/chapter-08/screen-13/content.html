Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Layered Strategy
Design pattern
Filter at the edge, apply behavior controls, and use DDoS services for volumetric attacks.
Edge
CloudFront + WAF for global filtering and managed rule groups.
Behavioral
Rate-based rules and IP sets to stop abusive clients and credential stuffing.
DDoS
Shield Standard for common attacks; Shield Advanced for business-critical cost protection and DRT support.
Operational checklist: COUNT mode for new rules, enable logging, validate metrics, and consider Shield Advanced if attacks could cause significant downtime or costs.
