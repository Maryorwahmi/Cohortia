Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Shield Advanced
DRT and incident support
Access to AWS DDoS Response Team helps analyze attacks and apply mitigations when automated protections are insufficient.
Operational value
  • Expert guidance during active incidents.
  • Custom mitigations and diagnostics to reduce impact.
Cost protection & metrics
Shield Advanced can offset scaling costs caused by attacks and provides advanced CloudWatch metrics for forensic analysis.
Integration note: Shield Advanced covers CloudFront, ALBs, EC2, Route 53, and Global Accelerator for enhanced protections and cost guarantees.
