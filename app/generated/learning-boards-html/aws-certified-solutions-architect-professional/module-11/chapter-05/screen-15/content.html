Key Concepts — Practical Pointers
Three more compact cards with operational guidance.
DNS Health Check
Monitors endpoints and drives failover decisions; tune intervals and thresholds carefully.
Failover Routing
Active-passive pattern: primary receives traffic while healthy, secondary used when primary fails.
Alias Record
Preferred for AWS resources: auto-track changes and reduce extra DNS charges.
Final Takeaway

Design DNS systems by combining routing policies, health checks, TTL strategy and private zones to meet availability, performance, and compliance goals.
