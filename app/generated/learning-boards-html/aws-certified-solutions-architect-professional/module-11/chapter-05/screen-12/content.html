Common Mistakes and Safety Notes
A checklist to prevent false failovers, slow updates, and DNS surprises.
Health checks
• Avoid overly aggressive intervals and thresholds to reduce false failovers.
• Use calculated health checks for composite system health decisions.
TTL management
Use short TTLs during active deployments but lengthen once stable to lower DNS load.
Records and zones
• Prefer Alias records for AWS resources to auto-track changes and reduce costs.
• Ensure private hosted zones are associated only with intended VPCs.
• Be careful with split-horizon setups to avoid conflicting answers.
Pro Tip

Validate DNS behavior from multiple public resolvers and inside VPCs to catch caching effects and policy interactions.
