Multivalue Answer Routing and Simple Routing
Return multiple healthy records or single answers depending on needs.
Simple routing
Used for single resources; returns one or more values in a simple configuration without health-driven filtering unless combined with health checks.
Multivalue answer
Returns up to eight healthy records. Clients choose one of the returned records, which offers redundancy but not precise control over distribution.
Practical notes
  • • Good for simple redundancy where you want clients to have multiple healthy endpoints.
  • • Not suitable for controlled percentage traffic shifts or session-aware load balancing.
  • • Combine with health checks to ensure only healthy records are returned.
Key Takeaway

Multivalue gives client-side redundancy with health-awareness; choose weighted or ALB solutions for precise distribution or connection balancing.
