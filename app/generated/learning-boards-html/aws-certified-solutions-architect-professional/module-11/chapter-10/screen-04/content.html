Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › CloudFront Metrics
Definition
Core CloudFront metrics
  • Requests: total viewer requests, surface traffic spikes.
  • BytesDownloaded: bandwidth used by viewers.
  • CacheHitRatio: percent served from cache; low ratio often signals cache-key or TTL issues.
  • OriginLatency: time to fetch from origin; high values suggest origin or network bottlenecks.
  • OriginErrorRate / 4xx / 5xx: indicate failed origin responses or client errors.
Takeaway
Key Takeaway

Track cache and origin metrics together. A cache drop plus normal origin latency suggests cache-key changes; a cache drop plus high origin latency suggests origin problems.

Example & common mistake
Example scenario
After a deployment you see CacheHitRatio drop from 98% to 40% while OriginLatency remains low. Investigation shows the cache policy was changed to forward all query strings. Fix: restore optimized cache policy and monitor recovery.
Pro-tip
Set an alarm for CacheHitRatio with a short evaluation period, but require multiple data points to avoid false positives.
Check your understanding
If OriginErrorRate spikes and cache ratio is stable, is the origin or the cache likelier the problem? (Answer: origin.)
