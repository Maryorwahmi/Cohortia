Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Troubleshooting Workflow
Flow
Detect — alarms on CacheHitRatio, OriginErrorRate, HealthyEndpointCount
Investigate — CloudTrail, access logs, query logs, origin metrics
Confirm — reproduce with curl, dig, or curl with query strings
Remediate — policy change, invalidation, fix bucket policy, adjust health checks
Validate — monitor metrics and logs to ensure recovery
Concrete example
An alarm for CacheHitRatio triggers. Investigate access logs and find many Miss entries with unique query params. Confirm by requesting the resource with and without query params and observing cache behavior. Remediate by reverting cache policy to not forward unnecessary query strings, then validate metric recovery.
Common mistake
Avoid broad changes without reproduction. Replacing endpoints or raising capacity before confirming the fault often misdirects effort and extends downtime.
