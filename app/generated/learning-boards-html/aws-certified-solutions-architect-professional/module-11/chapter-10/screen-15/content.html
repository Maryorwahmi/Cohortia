Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › What to Remember
Monitor
Alarms on CacheHitRatio, OriginErrorRate, and health counts provide fast detection.
Log
Access logs and CloudTrail give the evidence to confirm root cause.
Validate
Reproduce the issue with targeted tests and verify metric/log recovery after remediation.
Final takeaway

Adopt a metrics-first mindset, use logs to prove the cause, and apply controlled fixes so your global edge services remain reliable and performant.
