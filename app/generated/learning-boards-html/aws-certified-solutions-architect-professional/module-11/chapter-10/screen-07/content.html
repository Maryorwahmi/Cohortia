Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Origin Errors vs Permissions
Comparison
5xx Origin Failures
Cause: application errors, resource exhaustion, bad origin config. Check origin logs and CPU/memory metrics on the origin.
403 from S3
Cause: permission or OAC misconfiguration. Verify bucket policy and the CloudFront origin access identity or OAC association.
Concrete checks
  • Inspect CloudFront OriginErrorRate and 5xx metrics in CloudWatch.
  • Tail origin logs (ALB, EC2, ECS) to find application stack traces or permission denials.
  • For S3, confirm the CloudFront OAC identity is allowed in the bucket policy; avoid making the bucket public.
Example
After enabling OAC, CloudFront returns 403 even though objects exist. The bucket policy lacks the new OAC principal. Add the OAC identity to the bucket policy to restore access.
