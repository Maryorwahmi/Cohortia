Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Key Concepts
CloudFront Metrics
CacheHitRatio, OriginLatency and error rates are core indicators of cache and origin health.
Route 53 Health Checks
Health check status and query logs are essential for DNS failover and resolution troubleshooting.
Global Accelerator
HealthyEndpointCount and ActiveFlowCount reveal endpoint reachability and traffic volume through the accelerator.
Summary takeaway

Use a balanced combination of metrics, logs, health checks, and CloudTrail to detect and confirm incidents, and tune alarms to keep signal high and noise low.
