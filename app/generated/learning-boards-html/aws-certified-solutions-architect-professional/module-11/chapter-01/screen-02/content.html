Why these objectives matter

Global users expect fast, available services. Knowing which component impacts latency, cost, and resilience helps you pick the right trade-offs for a given workload.

Concrete example: Host static images in S3 and distribute via CloudFront PoPs to lower latency globally and reduce origin load.
Misconception check: Edge Locations are not full regions. They do not replace Regions or AZs for compute and durable storage.
Practice tip: Map your users first, then identify which assets benefit most from edge caching.
