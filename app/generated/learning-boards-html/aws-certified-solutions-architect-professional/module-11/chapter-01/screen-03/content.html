VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Common pitfalls
Avoid caching highly dynamic or personalized responses at the PoP without proper headers, expiration, or invalidation. Edge caching is powerful, but must match content semantics.
Example: Cache product thumbnails at the PoP but keep checkout sessions in regional servers across AZs.
Tip: Use CloudFront behaviors and header controls to separate cacheable assets from dynamic endpoints.
