Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › PoPs vs Regional Edge Caches
CloudFront Points of Presence
  • Many, widely distributed, optimized for lowest latency.
  • Smaller cache capacity; store hottest objects.
  • Best for frequently requested static assets.
Example: Homepage images that get millions of hits are likely present at most PoPs.
Regional Edge Caches
  • Fewer, larger caches between PoPs and origin.
  • Hold larger, less-frequently accessed objects.
  • Reduce origin requests for cache misses across multiple PoPs.
Key Takeaway: Use PoPs for speed and Regional Edge Caches for capacity to minimize origin load.
Pro tip
Tune TTLs: high request rate objects get longer TTLs; low-rate items rely on Regional Edge Caches.
Misconception
Do not expect every PoP to have every object; cache strategies matter.
Check
Measure cache-hit ratios and origin request rates to validate your policy.
