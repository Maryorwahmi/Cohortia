Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Three Quick Reminders
Match traffic
Use Global Accelerator for Layer 4 network optimization; use CloudFront for caching.
Verify health & security
Ensure health checks succeed and security groups permit accelerator probes for reliable failover.
Use traffic dials
Control traffic distribution for staged rollouts and instant DR without DNS delays.
Final thought
A brief lab validating static IP routing and failover will answer more than long design debates. Try it and observe behavior directly.
