Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Active/Passive DR
Process
Keep the passive region's traffic dial set to 0% in normal operation. When you need to fail over, set passive to 100% and primary to 0%. Global Accelerator performs the shift without DNS changes.
Instant failover: no waiting for DNS TTLs.
Test often: execute simulated failures to validate runbooks and health checks.
Operational checklist
  • Ensure passive endpoints are healthy and ready to serve traffic.
  • Verify security groups allow accelerator health probes and client traffic.
  • Document the exact dial change steps and monitoring indicators.
Scenario card
Example: during an outage in the primary region, flip the traffic dial to route all traffic to the passive region and validate connectivity within minutes.
Key Takeaway

Traffic dials give you safe, immediate control over regional traffic distribution for DR and staged rollouts.
