VPC Route 53 Resolver — what it answers
Clear boundaries reduce surprises during hybrid integration.
Automatically resolved
  • Public DNS names that go to the internet.
  • AWS‑provided internal names for resources in the VPC.
  • Records in private hosted zones associated with the VPC.
Not automatic
  • Forwarding queries to on‑premises DNS servers.
  • Making AWS private hosted zone names available to on‑premises systems.
Example
An EC2 instance can resolve test.aws.internal.example.com if that private hosted zone is associated with the VPC, but it cannot reach legacy.corp.local on your data center unless you create an outbound endpoint and forwarding rule.
Misconception

Do not assume the VPC resolver will automatically reach your on‑premises DNS. You must create the appropriate endpoints and forwarding rules so queries traverse Direct Connect or VPN.

Check your understanding
If an EC2 instance cannot resolve an on‑prem domain, confirm whether a forwarding rule exists and whether the outbound endpoint has healthy ENIs in multiple subnets.
Key Takeaway

The VPC resolver handles local and associated private zones by default; endpoints are required to bridge DNS across network boundaries.
