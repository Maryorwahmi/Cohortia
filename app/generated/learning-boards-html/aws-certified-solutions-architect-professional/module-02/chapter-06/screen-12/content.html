Centralized topology
Visualize TGWs, attachments, VPNs and Direct Connect links from one place to understand global connectivity at a glance.
Monitoring and alerts
Network Manager surfaces metrics and alerts so you can detect and scope performance regressions before they escalate.
Investigation workflow
  • Identify affected TGW and attachment in Network Manager.
  • Check attachment health and metrics.
  • Cross-check TGW route table associations and propagation for routing issues.
  • Correlate with Reachability Analyzer and Flow Logs for root cause.
Key Takeaway

Network Manager helps you correlate topology and performance across global networks so you can prioritize and scope fixes quickly.
