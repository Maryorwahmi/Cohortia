Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › BGP Attributes
AS_PATH
Count of AS hops; shorter paths preferred. Useful to avoid detours through multiple transit ASes.
LOCAL_PREF
Higher value wins for outbound traffic within your AS. Set this to prefer Direct Connect over VPN for your on-prem site.
MED
Offers a hint to external neighbors about preferred inbound entry; lower is preferred but scope is limited.
Concrete example
You set LOCAL_PREF higher on routes learned over the primary Direct Connect link so your on-premises network prefers that path for outbound traffic instead of the VPN.
Misconception
Do not expect MED to override LOCAL_PREF; MED is a weaker attribute and has limited scope between AS peers.
Key Takeaway

Tune BGP attributes deliberately to control traffic engineering; always validate results and document changes to avoid asymmetric routing surprises.
