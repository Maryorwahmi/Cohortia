Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Monitoring and cost controls
Practical signals to watch
  • VPC flow logs showing cross-AZ or cross-region transfers.
  • CloudWatch metrics for NAT Gateway processed bytes.
  • Cost Explorer reports filtered by data transfer categories and tags.
Key takeaway
Detect routing regressions quickly by correlating flow logs with billing trends.
Tagging strategy
Tag resources by team and purpose so Cost Explorer can attribute egress to owners and drive corrective action.
Misconception
Alerts that trigger on single hours can miss slow but costly accumulations. Use multiple signals and aggregation windows.
