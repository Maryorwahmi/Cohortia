Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › NAT Gateway vs NAT Instance
NAT Gateway (Managed)
  • Fully managed, AZ-level high availability when deployed per-AZ.
  • Auto-scaling for bandwidth; no patching or OS maintenance required.
  • Charges: hourly + per-GB processed.
Preferred for production and variable traffic.
NAT Instance (Self-managed)
  • EC2 instance running NAT software; offers full administrative control.
  • Potentially lower cost at very low throughput, but needs patching and HA design.
  • Requires Auto Scaling or scripts for failover, adding operational risk.
Suitable for constrained, low-traffic, or experimental setups where you accept the overhead.
Decision checklist
Estimate GB/month
Assess operational capacity
Plan HA per AZ
