Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Egress-Only Internet Gateway for IPv6
Concept
Egress-Only Internet Gateway enables outbound IPv6 from private subnets while blocking inbound connections initiated from the internet. It fills the IPv6 gap where NAT is not applicable.
Quick validation
After adding the gateway to your VPC and route table, verify outbound connectivity and ensure no open inbound ports appear in security scans.
Why it matters
It gives IPv6-only workloads safe outbound access without exposing them to inbound internet connections, maintaining the principle of least privilege.
Misconception
Do not assume NAT applies to IPv6. Use the egress-only gateway for IPv6 outbound-only needs.
