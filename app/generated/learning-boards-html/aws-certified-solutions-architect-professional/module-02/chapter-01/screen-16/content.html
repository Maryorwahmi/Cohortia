Avoid mesh peering
Use Transit Gateway for many VPCs to reduce peering complexity.
Attachments ≠ Routes
Always update TGW and VPC route tables after attaching a VPC.
Validate services
Confirm DNS and NTP reachability after firewall or route changes.
Summary

Follow the checklist: avoid mesh peering, configure TGW routes deliberately, and validate critical services after changes.
