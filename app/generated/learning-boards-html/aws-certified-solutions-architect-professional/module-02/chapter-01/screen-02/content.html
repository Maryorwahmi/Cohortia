Apply Security Groups and NACLs

Mitigate common networking pitfalls

Why these skills matter

Enterprise networks must balance isolation, shared services, and operational simplicity. These objectives give you a repeatable way to plan, implement, and verify networks that scale without becoming insecure or unmanageable.
