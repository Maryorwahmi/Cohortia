Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Peering vs TGW
VPC Peering
Good for a small number of pairwise connections that do not require transitive routing. Easy to configure but becomes hard to manage at scale.
Pros
  • Low operational overhead for few links.
  • No central gateway cost.
Cons
Non-transitive and scales poorly; route proliferation and management overhead grow quickly.
AWS Transit Gateway
A central transit hub that attaches VPCs, VPNs, and Direct Connect. Simplifies routing and centralizes policy enforcement using TGW route tables.
Pros
  • Scales to many VPCs with clear route management.
  • Supports central route tables to control transitive flows.
Note
Transit Gateway adds a central control plane; you still must configure route tables correctly to enforce isolation.
Key Takeaway

Choose Transit Gateway when you need scale and central policy. Reserve peering for small, static relationships.
