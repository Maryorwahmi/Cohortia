Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Hands-on Scenario
Tasks
  1. Create three VPCs with CIDRs 10.1.0.0/16, 10.2.0.0/16, 10.3.0.0/16.
  2. Create a Transit Gateway and attachments for each VPC.
  3. Create SharedServices-TGW-RT and Isolation-TGW-RT.
  4. Associate and propagate according to the isolation plan.
  5. Update VPC route tables to target the TGW for inter-VPC CIDRs.
Validation
Confirm prod cannot reach dev, but both can reach shared-services. Verify DNS and NTP remain reachable as needed.
Example mapping
SharedServices-TGW-RT: propagate shared-services; add static routes to prod and dev CIDRs. Isolation-TGW-RT: associate prod and dev; add static route to shared-services CIDR pointing to shared-services attachment.
Operational tip
Keep a runbook of associations and propagations. Mistakes in route table association are the most common cause of accidental cross-VPC access.
Key Takeaway

Plan, document, and validate your TGW associations and propagations. Test isolation with controlled probes before declaring success.
