Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Design Decisions
Rules of thumb
  • Assign clear roles to VPCs and document them.
  • Reserve CIDR ranges with headroom and on-prem overlap in mind.
  • Centralize shared services in one VPC and expose them via TGW route tables.
Governance
Maintain a change control process for routing and firewall updates, keep runbooks, and require staging validation for network changes to protect production services.
Documentation
Document TGW route table intent and associations so future teams can understand why traffic flows are restricted or allowed.
Key Takeaway

Make deliberate architecture choices that balance isolation, shared services, and operational simplicity; document intent and validate changes.
