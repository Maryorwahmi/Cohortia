Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Anycast
Route 53 Anycast endpoints answer queries from the closest network location for faster resolution.
Private Hosted Zone
Use private zones for internal names and associate them with VPCs to prevent public exposure.
DNSSEC
Enable DNSSEC for public zones to cryptographically sign records and help resolvers verify authenticity.
Quick Action

When designing DNS, document routing goals, plan private zone associations, prepare Resolver rules for hybrid needs, and consider DNSSEC and health checks before going live.
