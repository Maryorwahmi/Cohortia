Dedicated vs Hosted

Dedicated connections are physical ports ordered from AWS with large bandwidth options. Hosted connections come through partners and offer smaller increments suitable for organizations that do not need a full port.

When to choose Direct Connect
When predictable performance, lower egress for high volume, and private connectivity are required.
Design tip
Plan redundancy across Direct Connect locations or provide a VPN backup for connectivity resilience.
Key Takeaway

Select Dedicated for sustained high throughput and Hosted for incremental capacity through partners; always plan path diversity.

Visual: speed and use cases
Speeds
Common Dedicated: 1 Gbps, 10 Gbps, 100 Gbps. Hosted: 50 Mbps to 10 Gbps through partners.
Use cases
Large data migration, database replication, video ingest, and latency-sensitive transactions.
