Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Designing redundancy: best practices
Redundancy building blocks
Two VPN tunnels per connection for intra-connection redundancy.
Multiple Direct Connect connections at diverse locations for path diversity.
Design Direct Connect primary with Site-to-Site VPN fallback and use BGP for automatic route failover.
Test plan
Simulate primary link loss and confirm BGP route withdrawal triggers VPN path advertisement and traffic shift.
Key Takeaway

Diversity and automation (BGP) are the foundations of robust hybrid connectivity; verify with realistic tests.

Failure scenarios
Single tunnel loss
Traffic remains on the second tunnel without manual intervention.
Direct Connect outage
BGP withdraws routes; VPN advertises alternate routes and traffic migrates to the internet-based tunnel.
