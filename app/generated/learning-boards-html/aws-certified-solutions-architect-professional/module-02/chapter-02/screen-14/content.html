Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Hybrid Cloud
Combines on-premises infrastructure with cloud services for shared data and apps.
Site-to-Site VPN
Encrypted IPsec tunnels over the internet; two tunnels for redundancy, static or BGP routing.
Direct Connect
Dedicated private connection that bypasses the internet for predictable bandwidth and lower variability.
Summary takeaway
Choose connectivity based on bandwidth, latency, and cost. Use BGP for dynamic routing and design redundancy with diverse paths and VPN fallback.
