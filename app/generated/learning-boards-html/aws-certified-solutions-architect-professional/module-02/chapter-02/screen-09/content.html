Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › VPN vs Direct Connect: trade-offs
Side-by-side comparison
VPN
Encrypted over internet, fast to set up, two tunnels for redundancy, up to ~1.25 Gbps per tunnel.
Direct Connect
Private dedicated link with fixed bandwidth options and lower variability; available as Dedicated or Hosted.
Latency
VPN: variable. Direct Connect: consistent and lower jitter.
Cost
VPN: lower setup cost. Direct Connect: port fees but potential egress savings at scale.
Misconception
Direct Connect removes security responsibility — you still must control access with IAM, security groups, and network rules.
Scenario guidance
Low-cost, moderate bandwidth
Use Site-to-Site VPN for development, proof-of-concept, and modest production traffic.
High-performance, private
Use Direct Connect with Private VIF and Direct Connect Gateway to centralize multi-VPC access.
Key Takeaway

Match the choice to workload needs and combine both for redundancy when necessary.
