Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Operational Best Practices
Checklist
  • Test rule changes in non-production environments first.
  • Use count mode to observe potential blocks before enforcing.
  • Enable sampling and CloudWatch metrics for WAF and Network Firewall.
  • Retain Flow Logs per compliance needs and integrate with SIEM.
  • Document rollback procedures and automate deployments for consistency.
Avoid
Do not rely on a single control. Do not change rules in production without validation. Do not forget to allow required infrastructure traffic like DNS and health checks.
Key Takeaway

Operational safeguards and telemetry are essential to keep protections effective without harming availability.
