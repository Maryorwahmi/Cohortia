Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › What is Warm Standby?
Definition
Warm Standby keeps a reduced-capacity, fully functioning stack in the DR region. It receives replicated data and serves limited traffic until scaled up.
Why choose it
When your RTO must be very short and you are willing to pay for continuous, partial capacity in the DR region.
Key Takeaway

Warm Standby shortens recovery because the stack is already running; scale-up is the primary activation step.

Typical components
  • Scaled-down Auto Scaling Group with small instances.
  • Replicated database (Aurora Global Database or RDS read replica).
  • ALB and a small set of supporting services running.
Scaling example
aws autoscaling update-auto-scaling-group \
  --auto-scaling-group-name MyWarmStandbyASG \
  --desired-capacity 10 \
  --min-size 10 \
  --max-size 20 \
  --region us-east-1
Misconception
Warm Standby is not the same as active-active. It may run real traffic but typically at reduced capacity until you scale it to production levels.
