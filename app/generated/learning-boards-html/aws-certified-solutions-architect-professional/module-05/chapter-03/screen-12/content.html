Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Pilot Light
Core data replicated and artifacts stored; compute launched on demand.
Warm Standby
A minimal, running stack in DR that can be scaled up quickly when needed.
Automation & Tests
Automate AMI/template sync and run full failover drills to validate RTO/RPO and prevent drift.
Bottom line
Match your DR strategy to business RTO/RPO targets and budget, and ensure continuous replication plus automated activation and testing.
