Concept Map
Core idea
Pilot Light: data + templates ready; compute off. Warm Standby: minimal production-capable stack running.
RTO: Pilot Light tens of minutes; Warm Standby minutes. RPO depends on replication.
Common pitfalls: outdated CloudFormation, untested failovers, configuration drift.
Quick example
A Pilot Light keeps an RDS read replica and copies AMIs to the DR region; on failure you create the stack from your CloudFormation template and update Route 53 to point at the new ALB.
