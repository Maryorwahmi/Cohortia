Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › RTO, RPO & Cost
Pilot Light
RTO: tens of minutes. RPO: minutes depending on replication. Cost: low ongoing due to minimal compute.
When to choose
Suitable when cost control matters and slightly longer recovery is acceptable, such as internal tools or non-critical workloads.
Warm Standby
RTO: minutes. RPO: seconds to minutes with fast replication features. Cost: higher because resources run continuously.
When to choose
Choose for public-facing apps or revenue-impacting systems where downtime must be minimal.
Example decision
Given an RTO of 30 minutes and RPO of 5 minutes: Pilot Light with RDS read replica and CloudFormation is suitable. Given RTO under 5 minutes and RPO under 1 minute: Warm Standby with Aurora Global Database and running ASGs is appropriate.
