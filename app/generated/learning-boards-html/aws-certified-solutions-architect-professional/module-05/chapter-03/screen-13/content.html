Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › What to Remember
Replication = RPO
Choose replication tech that meets your data-loss tolerance.
Readiness = RTO
Decide whether compute runs in DR (Warm Standby) or is launched on demand (Pilot Light).
Rehearsal prevents surprises
Automate artifact sync and run isolated failover drills often.
Action
Before you finalize a DR plan, run a timed drill and measure whether your chosen strategy meets business RTO/RPO goals.
