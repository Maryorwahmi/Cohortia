Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Cross-Account Backups
Architecture

Backup account for security and cost control

Use a minimal, tightly controlled account to store and protect backups and logs.

Architecture Checklist
  • Separate account for backups and immutable audit logs.
  • Minimal compute permissions in backup account to avoid accidental hosting.
  • KMS key policies that permit cross-account decryption only to approved principals.
Operational Notes

Enable CloudTrail in all accounts and centralize logs in an immutable S3 bucket in the backup account for auditability.

Concrete Example

Share RDS snapshots cross-account into the backup account. Apply lifecycle rules to move older snapshots into Glacier Deep Archive for cost savings.

Key Takeaway

A minimal backup account reduces risk and accidental spend while simplifying compliance evidence collection.
