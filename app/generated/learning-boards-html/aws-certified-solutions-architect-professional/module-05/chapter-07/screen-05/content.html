Process Flow

Classify, assign, and optimize

A repeatable scoring process ensures DR spend aligns with impact.

Steps
  1. Score application impact on revenue, compliance, and operations.
  2. Map score to RTO/RPO targets and pick a DR pattern.
  3. Estimate cost and identify cost-saving options.
  4. Review with finance and compliance and finalize tier.
Storage Tiering

Use S3 Intelligent-Tiering for variable access patterns and lifecycle rules to move backups to Glacier or Deep Archive for long-term retention.

Example: Two apps
Payments
High revenue impact, legal risk
DR: Warm Standby / Active
Analytics ETL
Low immediate impact
DR: Backup & Restore
Pro Tip

Keep a living spreadsheet or CMDB field with RTO/RPO and review quarterly to avoid drift between business needs and DR spend.
