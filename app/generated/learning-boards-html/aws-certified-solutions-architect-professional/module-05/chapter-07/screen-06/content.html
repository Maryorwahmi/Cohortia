Comparison

RIs vs Savings Plans vs On-Demand

Choose committed pricing for predictable baseline DR capacity to save significantly over On-Demand.

When to use
  • Warm Standby always-on baseline: RIs or Savings Plans.
  • Variable compute across families: Savings Plans prefered.
  • On-Demand for infrequent or test instances.
Concrete Example

If your DR region runs a 30% scaled replica of production 24/7, commit to cover that 30% with RIs or Savings Plans to reduce cost up to published discounts.

Decision Card

Consider RIs
Flexible compute families

Intermittent or test

Key Takeaway

Commit only for the baseline you need. Monitor utilization to ensure committed discounts are realized.
