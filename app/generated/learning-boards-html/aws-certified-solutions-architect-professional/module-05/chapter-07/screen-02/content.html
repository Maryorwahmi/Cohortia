Learning Objectives

Learn, understand, apply, and verify cost and compliance controls for DR.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Align DR to criticality

Classify apps and set RTO/RPO targets so expensive DR strategies are reserved for mission-critical workloads.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Reduce DR costs

Use RIs, Savings Plans, Spot where safe, and storage tiering to lower ongoing DR expenses without breaking recovery targets.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design for compliance

Apply data residency, KMS encryption, and IAM least-privilege to meet GDPR, HIPAA, or PCI DSS constraints in DR plans.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Enable auditability

Turn on CloudTrail and AWS Config, centralize logs in an immutable account, and verify events for legal and operational audits.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
