Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Encryption & Access Control
Comparison

KMS + Least Privilege

Combine encryption with narrowly scoped IAM roles to meet compliance without over-privileging operators.

Encryption Requirements
  • Encrypt EBS, RDS, and S3 backups using KMS keys.
  • Record KmsKeyId usage in audit logs for proof.
Access Control

Design distinct roles for backup creation and for restoration, and apply IAM conditions for additional constraints like MFA or source network.

Example

To prove encryption to an auditor, show a CloudTrail CreateDBSnapshot event that includes the KmsKeyId parameter, and the IAM policy that restricted who could call the API.

Key Takeaway

Encryption alone is not enough—combine it with least-privilege access control and logging for full compliance assurance.
