Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Cost vs Recovery Targets
Core Concept

RTO & RPO drive cost

Define what 'fast' means and choose a DR strategy that meets those targets without unnecessary expense.

Explanation
  • Lower RTO/RPO requires active replicas or standby capacity, increasing cost.
  • Backup and Restore trades recovery speed for lower storage and compute cost.
  • Tiering apps avoids spending the same cost for every system.
Concrete Example

An e-commerce checkout system may require warm standby or active/active for sub-minute recovery, while a daily reporting job can use backup and restore.

Common Mistake

Treating all applications as mission-critical leads to wasted budget and complex operations. Classify and then design.

Tiering Table
Mission-critical
Active/Active — highest cost
Business-critical
Pilot Light / Warm Standby — moderate cost
Non-critical
Backup & Restore — lowest cost
Key Takeaway

Set RTO and RPO by impact, then choose the DR model that meets those targets at minimal cost.
