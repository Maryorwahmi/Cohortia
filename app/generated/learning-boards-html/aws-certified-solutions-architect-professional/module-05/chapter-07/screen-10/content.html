Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Data Residency
Process

Map data to legal boundaries

Residency rules dictate region choices and replication patterns for backups and DR copies.

Checklist
  • Classify datasets by residency constraints.
  • Select DR regions that satisfy legal boundaries.
  • Document replication flows for audits.
Example

If data must remain in the US, choose primary and DR regions that are both located within US-based regions, avoiding cross-border replication.

Operational Tip

Maintain a residency map in your CMDB and require sign-off from compliance before enabling cross-region replication for sensitive datasets.

Key Takeaway

Residency constraints can limit cost-saving options; record them early and design DR within legal boundaries.
