Best Practice

Common Mistakes & Cadence

Avoid single tests, include business stakeholders, and stage experiments to build confidence safely.

  • Run drills regularly—quarterly or semi-annual—to capture architectural drift and personnel changes.
  • Involve business stakeholders to align objectives and success criteria with operational impact.
  • Stage FIS experiments: staging → low-impact production → higher-impact tests.
Automation opportunities
Automate AMI copying, CloudFormation validation, and scripted DNS updates to shorten recovery steps and reduce manual error.
Key takeaway
Make testing repeatable, measurable, and progressively challenging so resilience strengthens over time.
