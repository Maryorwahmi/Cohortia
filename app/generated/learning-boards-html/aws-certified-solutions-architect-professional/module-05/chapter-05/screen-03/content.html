Skills and Outcomes

These skills ensure your organisation can measure resilience, perform safe tests, and act on the results.

Measurable Targets
Set RTO/RPO in Resilience Hub and use assessments to prioritize fixes that affect those targets.
Safe Execution
Design rollbacks and communications so drills run without surprise impacts to business stakeholders.
Targeted Experiments
Use FIS templates to test specific mechanisms like ASG replacement or RDS promotion under controlled conditions.
Continuous Learning
Capture action items from post-mortems and track improvements to resilience scores over time.
