Interpreting Scores & Recommendations

Not all recommendations are equal—prioritize by policy impact, testability, and implementation cost.

Database risk
Often affects RPO; cross-region backups or replication are typical fixes.
Compute risk
May indicate single AZ dependencies or ASG misconfiguration affecting RTO.
Key Takeaway
Implement the highest-impact, testable recommendations first, then verify with a drill that includes restores.
Prioritization checklist
  • Which recommendation reduces the largest gap to your RTO/RPO?
  • Can the change be tested in a drill or restore within a reasonable window?
  • Does the fix require additional automation or IAM changes to be effective?
Verification
A recommendation is only trusted after you run a drill or perform a restore test that demonstrates the desired RTO/RPO.
Pitfall
Treating recommendations as finished work without scheduling verification leads to false confidence.
