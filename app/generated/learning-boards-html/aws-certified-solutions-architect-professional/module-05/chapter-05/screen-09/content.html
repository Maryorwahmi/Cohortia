Planning a DR Drill

A good plan makes execution safe and the results actionable.

• Define scope: which app and what failure mode.
• Set objectives: RTO and RPO targets to validate.
• Identify participants: ops, DBAs, app owners, business stakeholders.
• Establish communication and rollback procedures.
• Set success criteria with measurable checkpoints.
Example: Pilot Light Drill
Scope: Internal analytics app. Objectives: RTO 4 hours, RPO 1 hour. Participants: DevOps, DBAs, App owners. Rollback: revert DNS and terminate DR resources if rollback needed.
Why planning matters
Detailed planning avoids ambiguity during execution, keeps stakeholders informed, and reduces risk of accidental production impact.
