Definition

Resilience Policies: RTO & RPO

Translate business needs into recoverability targets and let assessments report compliance.

RTO
Maximum acceptable downtime; measured in time to restore functionality.
RPO
Maximum acceptable data loss; measured in time since last recoverable snapshot or replica.
Example
Pilot-light analytics app with RTO 4 hours and RPO 1 hour: requires automated backup or replication and tested promotion steps to meet targets.
Decision guidance
  • Point-in-time backups are acceptable for higher RPO windows and simpler restores.
  • Near real-time replication suits low RPOs but increases complexity and testing needs.
  • Always validate chosen approach with a restore test during a drill to ensure it meets the policy.
Misconception check
Tighter RTO/RPO is not solved by faster hardware alone; it often requires automation, replication, and runbook readiness.
