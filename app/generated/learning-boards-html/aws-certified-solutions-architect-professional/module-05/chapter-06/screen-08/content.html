Definition
DynamoDB Global Tables
Fully managed active-active tables that replicate across regions with eventual consistency.
Benefits
  • Low-latency writes in each region
  • Automatic multi-region replication
  • Operational simplicity for global availability
Design patterns
Use idempotency, retries, and conflict resolution for non-commutative operations to handle eventual consistency safely.
Concrete Example
A social post stored in Global Tables becomes available in the local region immediately, while replication to other regions completes in seconds. Clients should handle short discrepancies and reconcile when necessary.
Misconception
Do not assume strong consistency across regions; Global Tables provide eventual consistency and can briefly diverge.
