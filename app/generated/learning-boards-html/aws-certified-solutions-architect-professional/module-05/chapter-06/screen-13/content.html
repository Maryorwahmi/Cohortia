Aurora Global DB
Near-synchronous replication with fast promotion, suitable for tight RPO/RTO across regions.
DynamoDB Global Tables
Active-active replication with eventual consistency; design for short divergence windows.
S3 CRR
Asynchronous object replication; check delete marker replication, IAM role scope, and encryption.
Final Takeaway

Select services that match your recovery objectives, instrument replication metrics, and rehearse failover to ensure predictable DR behavior.
