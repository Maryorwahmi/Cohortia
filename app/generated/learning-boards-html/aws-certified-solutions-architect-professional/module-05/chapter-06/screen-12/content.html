Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Concepts
Synchronous Replication
Writes are acknowledged only after the secondary persists the data, giving RPO=0.
Asynchronous Replication
Primary acknowledges immediately; replication to the secondary occurs later, introducing potential lag.
ReplicaLag
The metric measuring delay in seconds; use it to estimate effective RPO and trigger alarms.
Summary

Balance replication choices against your RPO/RTO goals and instrument replication to make recovery predictable.
