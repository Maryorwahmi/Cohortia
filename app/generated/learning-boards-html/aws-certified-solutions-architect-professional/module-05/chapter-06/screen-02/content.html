Learning Objectives
These objectives focus on decision-making and hands-on actions you will perform to meet DR goals.
01
Distinguish replication models
Learn how synchronous and asynchronous replication affect RPO and application latency, and when to choose each.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement cross-region replication
Create RDS read replicas and understand promotion steps, plus S3 and DynamoDB replication patterns for DR.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Understand consistency models
Identify eventual versus strong consistency, and design applications to tolerate propagation delays.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Monitor and mitigate data loss
Set up CloudWatch alarms for replication lag, and prepare failover playbooks to reduce RTO and RPO impact.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
