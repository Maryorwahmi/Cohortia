Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Compare Strategies
Backup & Restore
RTO: hours | RPO: minutes-hours | Cost: low
Use for non-critical applications where some downtime and data loss are acceptable.
Pilot Light
RTO: tens of minutes | RPO: minutes | Cost: moderate
Keep core data warm and scale app capacity on demand.
Warm Standby
RTO: minutes | RPO: seconds-minutes | Cost: higher
Run a scaled-down live environment that can be scaled to production.
Multi-Site Active/Active
RTO: seconds | RPO: seconds | Cost: highest
Full deployment across regions for mission-critical services.
Decision guidance
Tier services by business impact and apply the least complex strategy that meets the declared RTO and RPO. Include testing schedules and stakeholder sign-off in your plan.
