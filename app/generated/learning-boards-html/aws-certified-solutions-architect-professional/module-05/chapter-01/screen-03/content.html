Applied skills
Estimate targets and justify strategy choices with stakeholders.
Scenario exercise
Product Catalog
Read-heavy; browsing disruption affects sales opportunities. Typical recommendation: longer RTO, relaxed RPO compared to transaction systems.
Order Processing
Write-heavy; downtime means lost orders and revenue. Recommend tight RTO and RPO targets and more resilient strategies.
You will practice proposing targets and a short test plan to validate recovery within those targets without risking production traffic.
Milestones
  • Define RTO and RPO for two services.
  • Select a DR strategy that meets those targets.
  • Draft a simple test plan that isolates production.
