Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Business Continuity
Maintaining operations across people, facilities, and supply chains.
Disaster Recovery
IT-focused plans to restore systems and data after outages.
RTO & RPO
RTO = acceptable downtime. RPO = acceptable data loss. Both drive strategy choice.
Use this checklist: define RTO/RPO with stakeholders, map workloads to the appropriate DR strategy, automate recovery steps, and run isolated recovery drills to measure real-world performance.
