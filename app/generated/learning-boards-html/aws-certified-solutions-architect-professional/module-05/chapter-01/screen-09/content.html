Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Pilot Light
Core idea
Keep essential data and configs warm; scale application capacity on demand.
Elements always running
Replicated databases, AMIs, CloudFormation templates, and minimal orchestration components.
Scale-up steps
Launch app instances, promote replicas, adjust DNS and scaling policies, verify health checks.
Key Takeaway

Pilot Light balances cost and speed: lower ongoing cost than warm standby but faster recovery than backups, if automation is tested.

Concrete example
Maintain a read replica of RDS and store AMIs and CloudFormation in the DR region. On failure, promote the replica and launch app servers from AMIs. Expect recovery in tens of minutes given tested automation.
Common misconception: pilot light is safe without automation. In practice, manual steps negate recovery speed; script the entire scale-up process and rehearse it regularly.
