Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › RTO/RPO → Architecture
Five-step decision flow
1
Impact analysis
Quantify business effects of downtime and data loss.
2
Derive RTO/RPO
Translate business impact into measurable targets.
3
Map to DR strategy
Select Backup, Pilot Light, Warm Standby or Active/Active.
4
Design automation
Define replication, templates, and runbooks to meet targets.
5
Test and validate
Run drills, verify timelines, and isolate tests from production.
Applied walkthrough
Take an order service: impact analysis shows revenue loss per hour. You set RTO to 15 minutes and RPO to 5 minutes. This maps to Warm Standby if cost is a concern, or Multi-Site if near-zero downtime is mandatory. Design automation includes pre-built AMIs, CloudFormation templates, and database replication configuration. Your test plan includes simulated failover with DNS switch and verification of transaction integrity.
Common pitfall
Skipping stakeholder alignment. If business owners haven't approved the RTO/RPO, the chosen architecture may be over- or under-provisioned.
