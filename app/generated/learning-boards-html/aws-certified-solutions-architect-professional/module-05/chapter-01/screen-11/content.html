Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Multi-Site Active/Active
Core idea
Deploy full production capacity in multiple regions and distribute traffic between them.
Benefits
Minimal downtime and data loss; automatic failover in most cases.
Challenges
Higher cost, complex replication, global routing, and consistency concerns.
Key Takeaway

Active/Active is for mission-critical systems where every minute of downtime is unacceptable and the business can bear the ongoing cost.

Concrete example
A financial trading platform runs active services in two regions with near-synchronous replication and global routing. If one region becomes unhealthy, traffic shifts with minimal disruption and data loss measured in seconds.
Common misconception: active-active eliminates testing. It does not. Test cross-region failover, data consistency, and routing behavior to ensure SLA compliance.
