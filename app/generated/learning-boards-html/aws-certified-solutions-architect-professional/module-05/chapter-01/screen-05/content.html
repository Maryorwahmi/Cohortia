Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Recovery Time Objective
What is RTO?
Maximum tolerable downtime before service must be restored.
Business source
Defined by impact on revenue, reputation, and compliance.
Technical implication
Determines how quickly you must provision and validate infrastructure during recovery.
Key Takeaway

Base recovery design on measured business impact, then select strategies that can meet the RTO in tests.

Concrete example
An order processing service with a 15-minute RTO requires pre-provisioned templates, minimal manual steps during failover, and automation to scale application servers quickly. Contrastingly, a non-critical analytics job with an eight-hour RTO can be restored from recent snapshots after new infrastructure is provisioned.
Common misconception: RTO determines only the servers you run in a DR region. In practice it also drives automation level, monitoring, runbooks, and test cadence. If your recovery includes manual database fixes, that will extend measured RTO.
