Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Recovery Point Objective
What is RPO?
Maximum tolerable data loss measured in time since last good backup or replication point.
Business implication
Data loss can cause incorrect transactions, compliance issues, and customer harm.
Technical implication
Dictates replication frequency and whether asynchronous or synchronous replication is required.
Key Takeaway

Choose replication cadence to meet RPO and validate restores to prove the data can be recovered within that window.

Concrete example
If an order service has an RPO of five minutes, you must use replication or persistent queues that ensure transaction state is available within that window. Simple nightly backups would not meet this requirement and would risk lost orders.
Common misconception: RPO equals backup frequency. Backups are one way to meet RPO, but replication, streams, and database features may be necessary for very low RPOs.
