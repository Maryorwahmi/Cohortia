Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Backup and Restore
How it works
Regular backups stored off-site; restore infrastructure on demand.
When to use
Non-critical apps with acceptable downtime measured in hours.
Operational steps
  • Schedule snapshots and backups to off-region storage.
  • Automate restore scripts and CloudFormation templates.
  • Run timed restore drills and validate integrity.
Key Takeaway

Backup and Restore minimizes cost but increases RTO. Always include restoration tests in your schedule.

Example scenario
Product Catalog: use EBS and RDS snapshots stored in another region. Restore from latest snapshot and re-create application servers from AMIs. Expect recovery in hours depending on dataset size and provisioning speed.
Common mistake: not validating time to restore. Include a timed restore drill and compare measured recovery to your declared RTO.
