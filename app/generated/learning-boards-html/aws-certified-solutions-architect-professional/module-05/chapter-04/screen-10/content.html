Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › S3 & Other Stores
S3 Cross-Region Replication
Asynchronously copies objects between buckets in different regions. Reliable for static assets but not instantaneous.
Example: Deploy front-end assets to S3 in us-east-1, enable CRR to eu-west-1, and use CloudFront with origin groups to serve nearest cached content and fail over to the secondary origin if needed.
Other managed services
Services like ElastiCache or OpenSearch may lack native active-active replication; plan for rehydration or custom sync mechanisms if they are critical.
Key Takeaway

Inventory each data service and decide whether CRR, custom replication, or rebuild is the right approach based on criticality and recovery objectives.
