Multi-Site Active/Active (Multi-Region) DR

Design for continuous availability by serving traffic from multiple regions at once, accepting the trade-offs required for global consistency and resilience.

In this lesson
Principles, traffic routing, cross-region replication, app design, and operational tasks for active/active.
Why it matters
Near-zero downtime for revenue-critical services and global user bases.
Trade-offs
Higher cost, operational complexity, and consistency challenges.
Explore Key Topics
Global Traffic
Route 53 policies and Global Accelerator control how users reach healthy regions.
Data Replication
Aurora Global DB, DynamoDB Global Tables, and S3 CRR each behave differently for active writes.
Operational Practices
Automation, consistent IAM, monitoring, and regular DR drills keep active/active viable.
Visual Concept
Active Regions
Two or more regions serving production traffic.
Replication
Databases and storage replicated across regions.
Coming up
We'll step through traffic routing options, replication strategies for Aurora and DynamoDB, and a hands-on design for a multi-region analytics dashboard.
