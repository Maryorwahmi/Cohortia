Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Aurora Global Database
Low-latency cross-region replication for relational workloads; plan write topology and promotions.
DynamoDB Global Tables
Active-active NoSQL replication with eventual consistency; design conflict resolution and idempotency.
S3 Cross-Region Replication
Asynchronous replication for objects; reliable but not immediate for reads in the destination region.
Final Takeaway

Select replication strategies per data store, enforce identical automation and IAM, monitor replication, and run regular DR drills to keep active/active viable and dependable.
