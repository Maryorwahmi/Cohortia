Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Operational Pitfalls
Common mistakes
  • DNS caching: client resolvers can hold stale records; lower TTLs and consider Global Accelerator.
  • IAM drift: inconsistent roles across regions block automation—include IAM in IaC.
  • Insufficient monitoring: replication lag and health checks need cross-region visibility.
  • Underestimating cost: active/active runs redundant capacity; budget accordingly.
DR drills: validate routing, replication, and recovery actions under realistic load. Repeat drills at different times to uncover unforeseen issues.
Mitigations
Lower TTLs
Temporarily reduce TTLs during incidents to speed DNS propagation, balancing query volume cost.
Automate IaC
Deploy identical stacks and IAM across regions to avoid manual discrepancies.
Regular drills
Schedule full DR drills that include traffic shifting and data validation to reveal gaps.
