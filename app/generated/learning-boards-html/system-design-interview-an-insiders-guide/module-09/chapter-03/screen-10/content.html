Module 09 › Chapter 3 › Compensation logic: principles
What compensation must do
  • Express an inverse business operation, not a DB rollback.
  • Be idempotent so repeated attempts are safe.
  • Be retryable with backoff and timeout handling.
Pitfalls
Failing to plan idempotency, ignoring external side-effects, and assuming all actions are reversible are common errors when writing compensations.
Concrete examples
Payment: compensation is a refund request. Reservation: compensation is cancel-and-release. Email: cannot be unsent, so compensate by recording a retraction notice or notifying support.
Key Takeaway

Plan compensations deliberately, design for retries and observability, and accept human remediation when needed.
