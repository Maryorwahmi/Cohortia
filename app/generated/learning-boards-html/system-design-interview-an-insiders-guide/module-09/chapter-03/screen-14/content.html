Module 09 › Chapter 3 › Assessment ideas and common mistakes
Sample questions
  1. Why is 2PC unsuitable for microservices? Give a specific failure mode.
  2. Design an orchestration-based Saga for order fulfillment that recovers from shipment creation failure.
  3. List compensations and explain idempotency strategies.
Common mistakes
  • Treating compensation as a raw DB rollback instead of a business action.
  • Neglecting idempotency and retry semantics.
  • Missing observability for event chains or orchestrator state.
Grading tip
Require a failure-injection plan and verification that the final state converges to an acceptable business outcome.
