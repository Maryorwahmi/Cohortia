Event-driven flow
Each service publishes an event after its local transaction, and interested services react. No central orchestrator issues commands; the behavior emerges from event subscriptions.
OrderCreatedEvent → Payment
OrderService publishes, PaymentService processes payment and publishes PaymentProcessedEvent.
PaymentProcessedEvent → Inventory
InventoryService reserves stock and publishes success or failure events.
Failure events drive compensation
InventoryReservationFailedEvent triggers refunds and order cancellation via subscriptions.
Advantages and trade-offs
  • Low coupling between services.
  • Scales with event-driven architectures.
  • Harder to visualize end-to-end flow; debugging requires good observability.
Best practices
Document event contracts, build tracing for event chains, and design compensations as explicit events with idempotent handlers.
