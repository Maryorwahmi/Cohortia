Central orchestrator

The orchestrator controls the flow by sending commands and receiving confirmations or failure events. It records state so the flow can be resumed or compensated reliably.

Orchestrator sends CreateOrderCommand
Orchestrator sends ProcessPaymentCommand
On failure, orchestrator issues compensating commands
Advantages and trade-offs
  • Clear control flow and easier debugging.
  • Centralized visibility into the Saga state.
  • Orchestrator must be durable and highly available to avoid becoming a single point of failure.
When to use
Choose orchestration when you need clear sequencing, observability, and coordinated error handling. Consider workflow engines to manage durability and retries.
