Service Mesh and Sidecars

Explore how a dedicated infrastructure layer and the sidecar proxy pattern simplify cross-cutting network concerns so teams can focus on business logic.

In this lesson
Why meshes exist and how sidecars intercept traffic to provide traffic control, security, and telemetry.
Coming up
Traffic management patterns like canary releases and how to monitor and roll back safely.
Why it matters
Centralized policies reduce repeated code, provide consistent security, and simplify observability at scale.
Explore Key Topics
Sidecar Pattern

Traffic Management
Weight-based routing enables canary and A/B strategies centrally.
Observability & Security
Automatic metrics, traces, and mTLS for service-to-service encryption.
Concept Snapshot
Sidecar
A proxy deployed with each app instance that intercepts and manages network traffic.
Control & Data Plane
Control plane manages policies; data plane (sidecars) enforces them in the cluster.
Canary
Split traffic to test a new version with a small percentage of real users.
