Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › Istio vs Linkerd
Istio
  • Feature-rich control plane and advanced traffic management.
  • Uses Envoy as the data plane proxy.
  • Good for complex routing, policy, and observability needs.
Consider if
You need advanced routing features and policy customization and can manage higher operational complexity.
Linkerd
  • Lightweight, performance-focused, and simple to operate.
  • Uses its own efficient proxy implementation.
  • Emphasizes reliability and ease-of-use over feature breadth.
Consider if
You prefer low operational overhead and fast time-to-value with solid observability and reliability.
