Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Key Concepts
Rate Limiting
Control requests per time window to protect throughput and fairness.
Throttling
Broader limits including concurrency and resource consumption controls.
Token & Leaky Buckets
Token allows bursts; Leaky smooths to a constant rate. Choose based on burst tolerance.
Summary Takeaway

Pick an algorithm based on burst tolerance and accuracy needs, enforce limits at the edge when possible, and use a shared atomic store for distributed consistency.
