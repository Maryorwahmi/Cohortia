Process Flow

Managing eventual consistency

  • Show explicit processing states in the UI to set expectations.
  • Use sagas or compensating transactions for multi-service workflows.
  • Consider selective synchronous reads for critical confirmation paths.
Example
After OrderCreated is published, the OrderSummaryView may lag. Show 'Order processing' and update the view when the projection catches up.
Common mistake

Expecting immediate consistency and failing to provide compensations leads to poor UX and confusing errors.
