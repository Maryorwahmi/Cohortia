Process Flow

Broker responsibilities

  • Persist events with retention and compaction policies.
  • Route events to subscribers and manage partitions.
  • Expose delivery guarantees and ordering semantics.
Delivery guarantees and design implications
At-most-once
No duplicates, but some messages may be lost. Use when occasional loss is acceptable.
At-least-once
Messages retried until acknowledged. Handlers must be idempotent to tolerate duplicates.
Exactly-once
Complex guarantees requiring transactional handling or broker support; costly but avoids duplicates.
Pro tip
Favor at-least-once with idempotent consumers and clear partition keys for ordering where needed.
