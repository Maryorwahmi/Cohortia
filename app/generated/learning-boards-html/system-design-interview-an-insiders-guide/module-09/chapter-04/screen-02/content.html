Learning Objectives

What you'll be able to do after this chapter.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define feature flags

Explain how flags decouple deployment from visibility and why that reduces release risk.

Mnemonic: Deploy hidden, reveal controlled.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use progressive rollouts

Plan canary releases and dark launches to validate performance and stability before full exposure.

Mnemonic: Small seed, watch growth.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design A/B tests

Assign consistent cohorts, capture conversion metrics, and interpret results to choose the better variant.

Mnemonic: Split, measure, decide.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Manage flag lifecycle

Create, roll out, decide, and remove flags to avoid flag debt and keep the codebase clean.

Mnemonic: Create, test, decide, clean.
