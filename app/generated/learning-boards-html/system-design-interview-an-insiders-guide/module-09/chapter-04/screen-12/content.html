Module 9 › Chapter 9.4 › Control Plane & Read Path Design
Recommended patterns
Local cache

Fast reads with periodic refresh or background streaming updates.

CDN-distributed configs

Serve signed config blobs to reduce latency and central load.

Priority invalidation

Push immediate invalidation messages for critical disables to minimize propagation lag.

Operational checklist
  • Avoid synchronous reads on user requests.
  • Ensure audit logs and role-based access for safety.
  • Design invalidation for fast rollbacks when needed.
