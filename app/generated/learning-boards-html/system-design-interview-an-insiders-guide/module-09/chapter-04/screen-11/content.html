Module 9 › Chapter 9.4 › Flag Debt and Governance
Governance checklist
  • Assign a flag owner and purpose.
  • Set an expiry or review date on creation.
  • Automate audits to detect stale flags.
  • Require cleanup tickets tied to rollout decisions.
When to remove

Remove flags and dead branches after the feature is adopted or discarded to reduce complexity.

Audit example

Run a quarterly report listing flags older than X months with no recent toggles and require owners to close or extend them.
