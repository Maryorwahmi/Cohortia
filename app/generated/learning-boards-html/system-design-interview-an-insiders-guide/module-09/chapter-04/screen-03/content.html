Practical Outcomes & Signals

Define metrics, permissions, and lifecycle gates for safe rollouts.

Controls & metrics
Instrumentation
Track error rate, latency, and conversion to judge stability and impact.
Governance
Restrict who can toggle flags and keep an audit log of changes.
Rollback
Plan immediate disabling paths and automated stop conditions tied to metrics.
Performance

Use caching and low-latency storage to read flags; avoid per-request remote calls that add latency.

Cleanup

Remove flags and conditional code once decisions are made to prevent flag debt.
