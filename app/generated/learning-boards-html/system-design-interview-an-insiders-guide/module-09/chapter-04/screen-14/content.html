Module 9 › Chapter 9.4 › Key Concepts
Feature Flag
A runtime toggle that enables or disables behavior without re-deploying code.
Progressive Delivery
Stage exposure and monitor metrics to limit risk and validate under real traffic.
A/B Testing
Assign consistent cohorts and measure a primary metric to decide which variant wins.
Takeaway
Feature flags are operational tools: use them to iterate safely, but govern their lifecycle so they remain maintainable and secure.
