Distributed Consensus: Paxos and Raft

Understand why agreeing in a failing network is hard, how Paxos established the theory, and why Raft makes the same guarantees more approachable for engineers.

In this lesson
Consensus problem, CAP trade-offs, Paxos theory, Raft leader election and log replication.
Why it matters
Enables safe leader election and replicated state machines powering etcd and coordination services.
Outcome
You can explain trade-offs and trace Raft's election and commit flow end-to-end.
Concept Map
Roles, election, replication
Problem
Many nodes must agree on values despite failures and message loss.
Algorithms
Paxos: theory-first. Raft: understandable and leader-based.
Practical
etcd uses Raft; ZooKeeper is Paxos-like for coordination.
Explore key topics
Consensus problem
Why agreeing is hard when nodes fail or networks partition.
Paxos
Elegant, role-based protocol with proposers, acceptors, and learners.
Raft
Leader election and log replication designed for clarity and ease of implementation.
