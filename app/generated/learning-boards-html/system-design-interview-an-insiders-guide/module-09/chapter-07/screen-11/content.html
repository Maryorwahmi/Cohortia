Where consensus is used
  • Distributed key-value stores for cluster configuration (etcd).
  • Service discovery and metadata (ZooKeeper).
  • Leader election for sharded services and broker coordination (Kafka uses ZooKeeper for metadata management).
Decision checklist
  • Does your application require strict global ordering and safety? Choose consensus (CP).
  • Do you prefer simpler implementation and easier operational reasoning? Raft is a good fit.
  • Are you comfortable integrating theoretical variants for specific performance needs? Study Paxos variants and optimizations.
Operational note
Expect decreased availability in minority partitions when prioritizing consistency. Plan monitoring and operator runbooks accordingly.
