Process Flow › Raft: Log Replication and Commitment
Replication steps
  1. Client sends request to leader.
  2. Leader appends entry to its log and sends AppendEntries RPCs.
  3. Followers append and reply success.
  4. Leader marks entry committed after majority acknowledges.
  5. Committed entries are applied to each node's state machine.
Concrete commit example
// Leader L receives write W
L: append(W) at index 42
L -> followers: AppendEntries(index=42, entries=[W])
Followers -> L: success
Once majority respond, L marks index 42 committed and applies W
Misconception
Believing all followers must have the entry. In reality, majority replication is sufficient for safety and liveness under normal conditions.
Why it matters
Majority commits ensure any future leader will see committed entries because majorities overlap, preventing loss of committed state.
