Process Flow › Tracing a Raft Election
Initial state
N1 was leader and crashes. N2–N5 are followers. Election timers start counting down.
N3 becomes candidate
N3 increments term to 6, votes for itself, sends RequestVote(term=6, candidateId=N3, lastLogIndex, lastLogTerm) to peers.
Vote handling
Each follower checks term and log recency. If conditions pass, they grant vote and reset timers. Once N3 gets a majority it becomes leader and sends heartbeats (AppendEntries) to stabilize the cluster.
Split vote scenario
If votes split, no leader is elected and randomized timeouts lead to a subsequent election where one candidate will likely win.
Example checkpoint
After election, leader sends empty AppendEntries as heartbeats to prevent new elections and to begin log replication duties.
