Distributed Consensus
Agreement plus durability across non-faulty nodes despite failures.
Paxos
Proposer/Acceptor/Learner roles and a two-phase prepare/accept flow that preserves safety.
Raft
Leader election, AppendEntries replication, and majority commit rules designed for understandability.
Summary takeaway
Know the safety guarantees and real-world trade-offs. Match algorithm choice to your application's need for strict consistency versus availability during partitions.
