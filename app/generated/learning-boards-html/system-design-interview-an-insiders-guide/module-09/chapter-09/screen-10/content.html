Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Rebalancing
Operational process

Controlled migration steps

Detect imbalance
Use metrics to find overloaded shards by CPU, IOPS, and latency.
Plan target placement
Select destination shards and migration order to minimize cross-shard traffic.
Migrate with sync
Use dual writes or CDC-based replication, validate, then cut over reads and writes.
Example mitigation
Throttle migration rate to avoid I/O saturation and maintain tail latency SLAs.
Rollback plan
Keep source shard authoritative until validation completes; have a tested rollback if integrity checks fail.
Key Takeaway
Key Takeaway

Rebalancing is an operational exercise: plan migrations, test, monitor, and have rollback procedures to protect availability and consistency.
