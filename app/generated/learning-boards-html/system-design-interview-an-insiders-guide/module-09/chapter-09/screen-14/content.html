Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Activity: Social Media

Proposed strategy

Users: Shard by user_id. Co-locate profile and metadata with the same key.
Posts: Shard by author user_id to keep a user's posts on the same shard as their profile.
Followers: Option A — shard by followed user_id for efficient "who follows me" queries. Option B — duplicate and maintain a second table sharded by follower_id to also support "who I follow" queries.
Example rationale
Most reads are user-scoped. Co-locating user profile and posts minimizes cross-shard traffic for the common case of viewing a single user's content.

Operational considerations

  • Maintain denormalized follower counts to avoid scanning follower lists.
  • For users with millions of followers, store follower lists in a scalable store and use pagination and caching.
  • Test rebalancing and migration steps for very active accounts before production rollouts.
