Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Range-Based Sharding
Pattern

Shard by contiguous ranges

Example ranges
Shard A: user_id 1–1,000,000; Shard B: 1,000,001–2,000,000; Shard C: 2,000,001–3,000,000.
Benefit: Fast range queries — analytics or date-based reads target a small set of shards.
Risk: Hot spots if traffic concentrates in current ranges, e.g., recent users or recent orders.
Operational note: Splitting and migrating ranges requires careful coordination to avoid inconsistency and downtime.
Mitigations
  • Pre-split ranges for expected growth to reduce live migrations.
  • Use tiered storage or cold archives for old ranges to reduce load on older shards.
  • Combine range sharding with a directory when you need to move hot ranges dynamically.
Key Takeaway
Key Takeaway

Range sharding favors range queries but requires planning for splits and hotspots; use it when ordered access dominates your workload.
