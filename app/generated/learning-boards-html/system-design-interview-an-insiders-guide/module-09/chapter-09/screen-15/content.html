Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Key Concepts
Data Sharding
Horizontal partitioning of rows across database instances to scale.
Shard Key
Select immutable, high-cardinality keys aligned with queries to avoid skew.
Sharding Strategies
Hash, range, and directory strategies trade balance, query efficiency, and flexibility.
Rebalancing
Moving data safely requires migration strategies and validation to preserve availability.
Hot Spots
Mitigate with targeted moves, caching, and denormalization rather than only adding shards.
Joins & Transactions
Avoid cross-shard joins or accept denormalization; use sagas or careful coordination for multi-shard transactions.
Final takeaway

Match the sharding pattern to your workload and build operational tooling to manage migrations, hotspots, and consistency trade-offs.
