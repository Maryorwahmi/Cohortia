Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Learning Objectives
Learning Objectives
Build practical skills to prevent cascading failures and isolate faults.
01
Explain cascading failure
You will describe how one slow or failing service consumes threads and connections, causing dependent services to degrade. Recognizing this chain reaction helps you choose isolation and fast-fail strategies.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe the Circuit Breaker
You will explain the Closed, Open, and Half‑Open states, and why failing fast gives the whole system breathing room to recover.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement a circuit wrapper
You will be able to conceptually implement a circuit breaker, choose thresholds and timeouts, and interpret state transitions during faults.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply the Bulkhead
You will learn to allocate separate pools for critical dependencies so a slow external system cannot exhaust resources used by other interactions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Bloom bar: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
