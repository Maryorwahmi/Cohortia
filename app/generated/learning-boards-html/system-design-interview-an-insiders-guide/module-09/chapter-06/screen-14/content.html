Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Key Concepts
Cascading Failure
A chain reaction of failures caused by resource contention and retries.
Circuit Breaker
Stops calls to failing services, enabling fail‑fast behavior and safe probing.
Bulkhead
Isolates resources into compartments to contain failure impact.
Summary: Use circuit breakers to avoid wasted calls and bulkheads to prevent resource exhaustion. Instrument both and tune using production metrics.
