Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Hands-on Activity
Instructions
Run the simulation, then experiment with failure_threshold, reset_timeout_seconds, and success_threshold. Observe timestamps, printed state transitions, and counts.
Steps
  1. Start with defaults and note trip timing and recovery.
  2. Increase failure_threshold; observe delayed trips.
  3. Reduce reset_timeout; observe frequent probes.
  4. Raise success_threshold; observe slower recovery to Closed.
Reflection
Document which configuration best balanced protection and availability for your simulated failure rate. That becomes a hypothesis for production tuning where real traffic patterns can confirm or refute it.
Check your understanding

What change made the circuit most sensitive to transient errors? What change delayed recovery the most?
