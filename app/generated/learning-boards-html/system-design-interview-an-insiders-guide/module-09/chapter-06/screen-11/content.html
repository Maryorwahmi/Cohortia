Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Circuit Breaker vs Bulkhead
Circuit Breaker (behavior)
Stops calls to a failing service to fail fast and free up local processing capacity. Best for protecting callers from repeated waits.
  • Prevents repeated attempts to known-failing services.
  • Requires monitoring and tuning of thresholds.
  • Enables fallbacks and degraded experiences.
Bulkhead (structure)
Separates resources so failures remain local, preventing one slow dependency from consuming all threads or connections.
  • Protects unrelated flows from resource exhaustion.
  • Increases resource overhead and management complexity.
  • Apply selectively to critical dependencies.
Combined approach
Allocate bulkheads per dependency and wrap each outbound call with a circuit breaker. This ensures that failures are both contained and fail fast, preserving system responsiveness and limiting the blast radius.
