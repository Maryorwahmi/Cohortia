Comparison

Active-Passive Pattern

One active node handles traffic; passive standby receives updates and is promoted on failure.

Pros
Simpler consistency; single writer avoids multi-master conflicts.
Cons
Standby underutilized and failover may introduce a brief outage during promotion.
Concrete Example
A primary PostgreSQL streams WAL to a replica. On primary failure, a watchdog promotes the replica and updates DNS or connection strings to point clients to the new primary.
Common-mistake

Failing to test promotion and connection reconfiguration. Without rehearsed procedures, failover can take much longer than expected.

Key Takeaway

Use active-passive for strong consistency needs, but automate and test promotion to reduce RTO.
