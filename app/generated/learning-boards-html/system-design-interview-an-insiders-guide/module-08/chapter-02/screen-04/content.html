Core Concept

High Availability

An operational goal: systems remain available for the required period despite component failures.

Key properties
  • Eliminate single points of failure by adding redundant components.
  • Detect failures with reliable health checks and switch automatically.
  • Accept that failures occur and design graceful recovery paths.
Common misconception
More instances alone are not sufficient. Without replication consistency and failover automation, redundancy may not improve availability.
Key Takeaway

Treat HA as a design process: identify SPOFs, pick redundancy strategies, implement detection and failover, then test repeatedly.

Example Scenario
A single primary database is promoted to a replica on failure. The system must ensure replication keeps the standby up-to-date and a watchdog promotes the replica quickly to minimize downtime.
