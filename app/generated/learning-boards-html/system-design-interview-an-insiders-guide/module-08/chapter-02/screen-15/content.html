Key Concepts — Mechanisms

Load Balancer
Routes traffic and enforces health checks to prevent overloaded or unhealthy servers from receiving requests.
Failover
Automated switching to healthy components when failures are detected, minimizing manual steps.
Health Check
Use application-level checks that validate readiness, not just network reachability.
Data Replication
Protects durability across nodes and regions; choose sync vs async per RPO needs.
Geographic Redundancy
Deploy across AZs or regions and use global routing to reduce blast radius and latency.
Testing
Rehearse failover and perform controlled drills. Validation is the last mile of any HA design.
Final takeaway
Mechanisms only work when combined with clear policies and repeated testing. Design, automate, then rehearse.
