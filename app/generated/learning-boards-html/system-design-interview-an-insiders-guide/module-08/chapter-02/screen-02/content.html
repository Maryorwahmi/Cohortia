Learning Objectives

Four practical milestones that show you can design and evaluate HA strategies.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01 Identify SPOFs
Locate single points of failure and justify why they need redundancy.

You will map critical components and produce a short plan showing how each SPOF will be eliminated or mitigated.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Choose Redundancy
Select active-passive, active-active, or N+1 and explain the trade-offs.

You will justify a strategy for a component with respect to RTO, RPO, and operational complexity.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Implement Mechanisms
Design health checks, replication, and load balancing rules for critical services.

You will specify concrete health checks and a failover process for both stateless and stateful components.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Design HA Architecture
Apply redundancy and failover patterns to critical system components.

You will produce a short architecture sketch for an order processing service showing redundancy and data replication choices.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
