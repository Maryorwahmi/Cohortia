Tuning checklist
  • Measure real startup and dependency latencies.
  • Set initialDelaySeconds to cover warm-up plus buffer.
  • Choose periodSeconds to balance detection speed versus noise.
  • Set timeoutSeconds to slightly above expected max response time.
  • Adjust failureThreshold to tolerate transient failures but act on sustained problems.
Example tuning
If a service needs up to 120 seconds to warm caches, set readiness initialDelaySeconds to 180 seconds, periodSeconds to 10 seconds, timeoutSeconds to 5 seconds, and failureThreshold to 3 to allow brief blips without blackholing traffic.
Key Takeaway

Tuning is iterative: measure, adjust, and validate under load. Default values rarely suit slow-start or flaky networks without tuning.
