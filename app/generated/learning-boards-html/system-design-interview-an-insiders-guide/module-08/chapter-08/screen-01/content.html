Health Checks: Liveness & Readiness
Understand how probes enable self-healing and safe traffic routing in orchestrated systems.
16:9 HD Classroom
In this lesson

Learn why a running process may be unhealthy, how to choose liveness versus readiness, and how to implement probes that prevent crash loops and traffic loss.

Why it matters

Proper probes enable automated recovery, reduce downtime, and ensure only ready instances receive traffic. Misconfigured probes cause thrashing or silent outages.

Stop receiving traffic until dependencies are available.
Example: /readyz checks DB connectivity and cache warm-up before reporting ready.
