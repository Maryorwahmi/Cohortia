Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Common Mistakes
Failure modes
Crash loop
Cause: heavy liveness checks against external dependencies. Fix: move such checks to readiness and make liveness internal-only.
Readiness never passes
Cause: too-short initial delay or overly strict checks. Fix: increase initialDelaySeconds and relax non-critical dependency checks.
Flapping
Cause: transient network or tight thresholds. Fix: increase failureThreshold and timeoutSeconds, or reduce periodSeconds frequency.
Troubleshooting process
  1. Inspect pod events and probe logs to find failure reasons.
  2. Reproduce the failure locally with simulated dependency outages.
  3. Simplify liveness, tune readiness delays, and increase thresholds progressively.
  4. Observe behavior under load and iterate until stable.
Pro Tip

When in doubt, make liveness cheaper and readiness stricter. It prevents unnecessary restarts while preserving traffic safety.
