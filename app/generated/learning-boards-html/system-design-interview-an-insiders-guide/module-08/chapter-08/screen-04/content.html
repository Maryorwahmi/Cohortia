Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › What are Health Checks?
Health checks turn state into action
Use them to enable automated restarts and traffic management.
What a good check does
  • Responds quickly and deterministically.
  • Reflects recoverable operational state, not full correctness.
  • Maps directly to orchestrator action: restart or remove from service.
Common misconception
Treating health checks as deep validation leads to slow or flaky probes and instability. Keep probes focused and fast.
Key Takeaway

Health checks are lightweight operational indicators that let an orchestrator recover or reroute traffic automatically.

Concrete example
A web server process is alive but the DB pool is exhausted. Liveness: reports alive. Readiness: reports not ready until pool recovers. Orchestrator removes instance from rotation until ready again.
Analogy
Heartbeat vs. Ready-to-drive
Heartbeat means the engine runs; ready-to-drive means the driver has fastened the seatbelt and the car has fuel.
