Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Key Concepts
Correlation ID
Propagate a request id across services to link metrics, logs, and traces during diagnostics.
Sampling
Sample traces to balance cost and coverage; increase sampling when investigating incidents.
Redaction
Avoid logging sensitive fields; redact or omit secrets and personal data in logs and spans.
One-line summary
Design signals to answer the questions you expect to ask, and make sure the three pillars can be correlated for efficient triage.
