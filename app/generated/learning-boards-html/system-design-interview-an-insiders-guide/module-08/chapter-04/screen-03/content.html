Objective 05 — Metrics and cardinality

Understand counters, gauges and aggregation windows. Learn the trade-off between helpful labels and high-cardinality cost.

Why it matters
Well-chosen metrics reduce time-to-detect without exploding storage costs.
Mnemonic
Think: "labels help filters; too many labels hurt performance."

Objective 06 — Logs and correlation

Adopt structured logging and correlation ids so logs can be filtered and linked to traces for a single request.

Why it matters
Structured logs make parsing and searching efficient under load.
Mnemonic
Use a persistent correlation id across services to tie events together.
