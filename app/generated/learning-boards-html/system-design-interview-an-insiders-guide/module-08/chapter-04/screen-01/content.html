Introduction to Monitoring: Metrics, Logs, and Traces

Understand observability and how the three pillars work together to make distributed systems debuggable and reliable.

Explore Key Topics
Metrics
Numeric signals over time for trends and alerts.
Logs
Timestamped events for detailed debugging and context.
Traces
End-to-end request journeys to locate bottlenecks.
Use these topics together: metrics detect, traces locate, and logs explain. That triad is the practical essence of observability.
