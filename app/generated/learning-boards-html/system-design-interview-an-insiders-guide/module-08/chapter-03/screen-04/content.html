Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › RTO & RPO
Definitions
RTO: maximum time to restore operations. RPO: maximum allowable data loss measured backward in time.
Concrete example
If RTO=4 hours and RPO=1 hour, you must restore service within four hours and can lose at most one hour of recent writes.
Common misconception
RTO is not the same as RPO. You can have a short RTO but long RPO, and vice versa; each demands different mechanisms.
Key takeaway
Treat RTO and RPO as contractual requirements that drive technology selection and costs.
