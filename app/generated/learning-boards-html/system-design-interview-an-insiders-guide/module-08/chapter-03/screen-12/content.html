Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Common Mistakes
Frequent pitfalls
  • Not testing backups with real restores.
  • Ignoring cross-region consistency and conflict resolution.
  • Overlooking dependencies like DNS or external APIs.
  • Keeping runbooks stale or ambiguous.
  • Setting unrealistic RTO/RPO without cost assessment.
Safety checklist
Test restores annually or more often, map dependencies, keep runbooks short, and negotiate realistic RTO/RPO with stakeholders.
