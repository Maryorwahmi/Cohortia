Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › DR Drills
Designing a drill
Define objectives: restore data to meet RPO, and restore service to meet RTO. Include verification and stakeholder communication steps.
Tabletop exercise
Walk through the runbook with engineers and stakeholders to discover assumptions.
Live restore
Perform a full restoration on a sandbox or during a maintenance window to validate timings.
Postmortem
Capture time measurements and actions to improve the runbook and scripts.
Pitfalls
  • Testing only on small datasets that do not reflect production size.
  • Not validating external dependencies like DNS or third-party APIs.
  • Failing to rehearse communication and role handovers.
