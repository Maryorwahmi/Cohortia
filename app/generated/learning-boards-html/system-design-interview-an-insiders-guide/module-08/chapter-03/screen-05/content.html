Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Backup Strategies
Strategy cards
Full Backup
Complete copy at a point in time. Simple restores but slow and heavy.
Incremental
Only changed data since last backup; efficient but requires ordered restores.
Differential
Changes since last full backup; faster restore than incremental chains.
Snapshot
Fast point-in-time copy of a volume or DB, dependent on storage support.
Continuous Data Protection
Capture every write for point-in-time recovery; needed for near-zero RPO.
Restoration considerations
  • Estimate restore time for each strategy and compare to the RTO.
  • Document the ordered steps for applying backups, especially for incremental chains.
  • Test restores regularly to validate integrity and timing.
  • Automate restore orchestration where possible to meet short RTOs.
Scenario
Nightly full backup plus hourly incremental backups can meet a one-hour RPO only if incremental transfer is reliable and restores are orchestrated to finish within RTO.
