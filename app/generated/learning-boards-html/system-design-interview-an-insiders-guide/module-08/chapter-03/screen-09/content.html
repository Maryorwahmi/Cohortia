Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › User Profile DR
Starter template
Service: User Profile Microservice (NoSQL Database)
Primary Region: us-east-1
DR Region: us-west-2
RTO: 4 hours
RPO: 1 hour

1. DR Site Strategy: Warm Standby
   * Justification: Pre-provision core infrastructure in DR region to reduce setup time while avoiding full active-active cost.

2. Data Replication Strategy: Asynchronous cross-region replication (e.g., global tables or log shipping)
   * Details: Ensure replication lag is consistently under one hour; monitor and alert on lag spikes.

3. Regional Failover Steps:
   * Detection: Monitor health checks and alerts.
   * Initiation: Follow runbook to confirm and start failover.
   * Activation: Scale pre-provisioned services, verify data, update DNS, validate functionality.
Example justification
A warm standby meets the RTO by avoiding full provisioning after disaster. Asynchronous replication meets the RPO if lag is kept below one hour. Automate promotion and DNS changes to hit the four-hour window.
Misconception
Believing that replication alone ensures fast recovery. Without automated scaling and runbook steps, the DR region may still fail to meet RTO.
