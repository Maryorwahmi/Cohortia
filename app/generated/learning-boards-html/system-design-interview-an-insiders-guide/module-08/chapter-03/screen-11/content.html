Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Cost vs Recovery
Tiering services
Classify services by business impact and set RTO/RPO per tier. Apply hot standby only where justified.
Tier 1: Mission-critical
Low RTO/RPO, often hot standby with continuous replication.
Tier 2: Important
Moderate RTO/RPO, warm standby with periodic replication.
Tier 3: Non-critical
High RTO/RPO acceptable, cold standby or archival backups.
Decision steps
  1. Quantify downtime cost for each service.
  2. Estimate replication and duplicate capacity costs.
  3. Select the minimal DR posture to meet business targets.
