Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity
Design recoverable systems and organizational practices so essential services can resume after a site or regional failure, balancing downtime, data loss, and cost.
In this lesson
RTO & RPO definitions, backup strategies, DR site choices, and regional failover steps.
Why it matters
A tested DR plan prevents costly data loss and keeps business operations functioning during extreme outages.

How downtime and data loss targets determine replication and cost.
Backup Patterns

DR Site Types
Choose cold, warm, or hot standby according to RTO/RPO and budget.
Visual Brief
How choices connect
RTO
How long the system can be down before business impact becomes unacceptable.
RPO
How much recent data you can afford to lose, measured in time.
DR Site
Cold, warm, or hot — trade-offs of cost and recovery speed.
Quick start
Begin by asking business owners to specify acceptable RTO and RPO per service, then map those requirements to backup and replication strategies. Treat the runbook and drills as first-class deliverables.
