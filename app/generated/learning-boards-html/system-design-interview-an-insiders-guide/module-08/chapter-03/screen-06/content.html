Cold vs Warm vs Hot
Cold Standby
Empty site; long recovery, low cost; best for non-critical services.
Warm Standby
Pre-provisioned resources, periodic replication; moderate cost and RTO.
Hot Standby
Fully operational replica or active-active; low RTO/RPO, high cost and complexity.
Example: for a payment system you might choose hot standby. For analytics you might use cold standby. The right choice depends on business impact and the true cost of outages.
Decision checklist
  • What is the business cost of X hours of downtime?
  • Can we afford continuous replication and duplicate capacity?
  • Are there data consistency requirements that preclude async replication?
