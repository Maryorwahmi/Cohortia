Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Operational Objectives
Test and Document
Build repeatable drills and a runbook that captures detection, escalation, and recovery steps including DNS, data promotion, and verification checks.
DR Drill
Plan simulated failovers regularly to validate RTO/RPO and train teams.
Runbook
A concise, ordered checklist for detection, confirmation, rerouting, and post-failover validation.
Dependency Map
Identify third-party services, DNS, and human approvals needed during recovery.
Postmortem
Capture lessons and update RTO/RPO or runbooks after each drill or incident.
Why practice
Documentation and drills reduce hidden risks, accelerate recovery, and align expectations between engineers and business owners.
