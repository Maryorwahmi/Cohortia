Histogram
Bucketed observations plus sum and count for distribution analysis.
Why use it

Aggregates reliably across instances so you can compute percentiles like p90 or p99 for SLOs.

Design tip

Choose buckets that capture expected latency ranges and SLO boundaries to reduce blind spots and avoid unnecessary cardinality.

Concrete example

http_request_duration_seconds_bucket with buckets at 0.01, 0.05, 0.2, 1.0 will show how many requests fall under each threshold. From buckets plus sum and count you can derive approximate percentiles and see tail behavior.
