Alert Design
Static thresholds, burn rate, and severity planning.
Static thresholds

Simple rules like CPU > 80% are direct but may trigger during expected load changes.

SLO / Burn-rate alerts

Tie alerts to user impact: alert when error budget is being consumed too fast, which prioritizes business impact over raw resource signals.

Key takeaway

Align alerts with SLOs and business impact, and use severity to route incidents. Less noisy, more actionable alerts improve responder effectiveness.
