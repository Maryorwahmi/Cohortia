Alerting Workflow
Metric → Store → Rule evaluation → Notification → Deduping → Escalation
Step-by-step
  1. Metric collection from services.
  2. Storage in a time-series DB with appropriate retention.
  3. Rule evaluation with windows and durations.
  4. Notification via Alertmanager or equivalent.
  5. Grouping/deduping and escalation policies.
Concrete flow

Prometheus scrapes metrics, evaluates a rule, sends an alert to Alertmanager, which deduplicates similar signals and routes them to the proper channel with annotations and runbook links.

Check your understanding

Why include a for: duration on an alert rule? What problems does grouping solve?

Common mistake

Alerting on symptoms without a runbook causes wasted time. Link to a triage plan and prioritize SLO-aligned alerts.
