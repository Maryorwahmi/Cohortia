Avoid Alert Fatigue
Group, dedupe, annotate, and document responses.
Operational practices
  • Group similar alerts by service and incident ID.
  • Deduplicate repeated signals to avoid multiple notifications for one root cause.
  • Use escalation policies and silences for maintenance windows.
Runbooks

Attach a short runbook: one-line summary, three likely causes, and the first diagnostic command. This reduces time spent deciding what to do next.

Common mistake

Turning down alert sensitivity without improving context reduces visibility into real incidents. Tune rules, not just thresholds.
