Tools and Architecture
Core components and a typical deployment pattern.
Prometheus

Scrapes metrics, stores recent time-series, and evaluates rules using PromQL.

Alertmanager

Groups, deduplicates, and routes alerts; implements silences and escalation policies.

Grafana

Dashboards and visualization; optionally also used for alerting and annotations during incidents.

Architectural note

Scale concerns include retention and cardinality. Use federation or long-term storage adapters for aggregated, durable metrics.
