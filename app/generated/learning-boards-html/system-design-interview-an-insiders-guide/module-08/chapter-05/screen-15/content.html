Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Key Concepts
Counter & Gauge

Counter = totals; Gauge = current state.

Histogram & Summary

Histogram aggregates for fleet percentiles; summary gives per-instance quantiles.

Pull vs Push

Pull for long-lived services; push for ephemeral or constrained networks.

Takeaway

Instrument deliberately, alert for action, and attach runbooks to reduce incident friction.
