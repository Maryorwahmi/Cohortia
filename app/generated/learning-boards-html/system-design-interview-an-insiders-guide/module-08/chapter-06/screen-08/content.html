Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Shippers & Buffering
Shipper responsibilities
  • Collect logs from files, stdout, or systemd.
  • Optionally add minimal metadata and forward securely.
  • Implement local buffering and retry logic to prevent loss.
Concrete setup
Filebeat on hosts → Kafka topic as durable buffer → Logstash for parsing and redaction → Elasticsearch for storage.
Operational checklist
  1. Enable local disk buffering on agents.
  2. Monitor agent health and queue lag.
  3. Set retention and overflow policies for buffers.
Misconception: "Cloud logging agents always store everything safely." Even cloud agents benefit from buffering settings and pipeline filters to avoid transient loss or accidental PII capture.
