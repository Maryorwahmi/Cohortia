Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Key Concepts
Distributed Tracing
Observe the full journey of a request across services to diagnose latency and errors.
Trace & Span
Trace is the end-to-end container; spans are timed operations with attributes and parent links.
Context Propagation
Passing trace and span IDs across boundaries ensures spans link into a complete trace.
Takeaway
Instrument consistently, preserve context, and analyze child span durations to find the real bottleneck.
