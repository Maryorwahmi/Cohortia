Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Auth Service: SLIs & SLOs
Starter template
Service: User Authentication Service

1. SLI: Login Request Latency
   SLO: 99% of login requests complete within 300 ms over a 7-day rolling window.
   Measurement Strategy: Instrument login API at gateway, emit latency histogram, aggregate via Prometheus.

2. SLI: Successful Login Fraction
   SLO: 99.5% of first-attempt logins succeed over a 30-day window.
   Measurement Strategy: Count first-attempt successes and attempts at gateway; treat retries separately.

3. SLI: Password Reset Completion
   SLO: 98% of password reset flows complete within 1 hour over 30 days.
   Measurement Strategy: Track flow completion events and timestamps; compute success and time-to-complete.
Instrumentation note
Emit both counters and histograms. Use the histograms to compute percentiles for latency SLOs and counters to compute error rates and success fractions.
Key Takeaway

Be explicit about what counts as success, where you measure it, and how retries or external failures are handled.

Common omission
Don’t forget to include MFA and third-party identity provider failures in your definitions or explicitly document their exclusion.
