Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Pitfalls & Misconceptions
Pitfall: Internal metrics as SLIs
Remedy: Choose metrics that reflect user success and instrument at user-facing boundaries.
Pitfall: One-size-fits-all SLOs
Remedy: Tier components by criticality and set different SLOs for core journeys vs. helper services.
Pitfall: Changing metric definitions
Remedy: Version metrics, document computation details, and keep historical continuity for trend analysis.
Check
When reviewing your SLIs, ask whether each one maps to a user outcome and what action you will take when it breaches its SLO.
Key Takeaway

Clear definitions, tiering, and documented measurement rules are essential to trustworthy reliability practice.
