Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Choosing SLIs & Setting SLOs
Step 1 — Map journeys
List the critical user paths whose health matters most to the business.
Step 2 — Pick SLIs
Choose 1–2 SLIs per journey that reflect success and responsiveness.
Step 3 — Choose windows
Decide rolling windows for targets and alerting cadence.
Key Takeaway
Prioritize SLIs tied to user journeys and keep SLOs actionable so teams respond to real user impact.
Example: Checkout flow
SLI A: fraction of checkout requests that complete with an order confirmation. SLO A: 99% successful checkouts over a 30-day rolling window. SLIs B: p95 checkout latency & page load success.
Common mistake
Selecting too many SLIs dilutes focus. Limit to those you will act on and that represent critical user outcomes.
