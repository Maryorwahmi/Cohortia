Service Termination experiments
Stop instances to validate HA, draining, and auto-recovery.
Typical steps
  1. Choose a non-critical subset of instances (e.g., 1 pod).
  2. Kill the process or delete the pod for a short duration.
  3. Observe error rate, retries, load balancer behavior, and readiness transitions.
  4. Stop immediately if steady-state metrics exceed thresholds.
Example metrics to watch
  • Error rate for affected endpoints.
  • Number of requests that get retried or dropped.
  • Pod restart and scheduling time.
  • Latency spikes and connection closures.
Common mistake
Assuming restarts mean graceful recovery; check draining and in-flight requests.
Analogy
Like removing one train car to ensure the rest still reaches the station on time.
Key Takeaway

Target a tiny scope, validate draining and readiness, then expand tests once safe.
