Applied Objectives
Measure impact, keep experiments reversible, and coordinate across teams.
01
Select experiment types sensibly
Pick the fault injection that aligns with your hypothesis — pod kill for HA, network delay for timeout handling, and so on.
Mnemonic: pick what fails in production
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Measure the right signals
Track primary metrics like error rate and P99 latency and secondary metrics like connection pools and restarts.
Mnemonic: primary plus secondary
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Keep experiments reversible
Automate rollback and define a kill switch so any hypothesis violation immediately halts the test and restores state.
Mnemonic: rollback ready
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Coordinate with stakeholders
Notify ops, product, and on-call engineers. Observability and human readiness are as important as tooling.
Mnemonic: communicate first
