Why traditional testing is not enough
Understand the gap between correctness and operational resilience.
Traditional testing
  • Unit tests validate small, deterministic behavior.
  • Integration tests check defined interfaces and mocks.
  • Load tests measure throughput under controlled patterns.
  • These tests assume predictable infrastructure and limited variance.
Misconception: Many passing tests mean production is safe.
Chaos experiments
  • Inject real faults to reveal emergent behaviors.
  • Validate failover sequences, circuit breakers, and timeouts.
  • Expose undocumented dependencies and recovery gaps.
  • Designed with a hypothesis and measurable steady-state metrics.
Example: Kill a pod while traffic spikes and confirm load balancer routing and retries keep error rate below your threshold.
Example
Service maintains P99 below target when a cache becomes unavailable.
Common mistake
Only running experiments in staging gives a false sense of security.
Key Takeaway

Combine testing types: unit/integration for correctness, chaos for operational confidence.
