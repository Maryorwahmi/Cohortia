Time skew experiments
Test clock drift to reveal timestamp and auth fragility.
Why it matters
Clocks affect token expiry, event ordering, leader leases and cache TTLs. Small drift can lead to large functional regressions in production.
Design notes
  • Run on a single host first and keep duration short.
  • Monitor auth errors, TTL expirations, and scheduled job executions.
  • Ensure rollback paths restore correct time quickly.
Pitfall
Applying large clock jumps broadly may corrupt logs and trace timestamps; be cautious.
Analogy
Like changing the time on one station’s schedule and watching trains miss connections.
Key Takeaway

Test small clock variances to ensure protocols tolerate reasonable drift and authentication remains robust.
