Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Security is integral
Core claim

Security must be part of the architecture from day one. Observability ensures you can detect when protections fail and provides the data needed for incident response and audits.

Concrete example
A microservice that encrypts data at rest but logs full payloads will accidentally leak secrets. Designing which fields to mask, and where to store raw audit logs separately, prevents that risk.
Common misconception
Thinking security is a one-time task. Real security is continuous: threat modeling, scanning, rotating keys, and reviewing alerts are ongoing.
Key Takeaway

Plan for protection and visibility together, and make decisions about what to log versus what to protect.

Layered cards: design checklist
Authentication
Who is calling the service? Use JWT, MFA, or SSO where appropriate.
Authorization
What can they do? Apply RBAC or ABAC to limit actions.
Data protection
Encrypt in transit and at rest; mask extremely sensitive fields.
Observability
Structured logs, RED metrics for services, traces for distributed flows.
