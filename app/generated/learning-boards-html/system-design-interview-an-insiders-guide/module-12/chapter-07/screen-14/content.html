Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Key Concepts
Authentication
Verify identity for users and services using JWT, MFA, or certificates.
Authorization
Decide what an authenticated identity can do: RBAC or ABAC.
Encryption
Protect data in transit (TLS) and at rest; tokenize extremely sensitive fields.
Summary: security and observability are designed together to prevent, detect, and respond to incidents while balancing visibility and privacy.
