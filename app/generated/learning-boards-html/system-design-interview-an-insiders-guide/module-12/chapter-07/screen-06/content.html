Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Authorization Models
RBAC
Roles map to permissions. Easy to audit and understand. Best for stable organizations and simple permission sets.
Example: Admin=full access, CustomerService=view transactions for assigned customers, Viewer=read-only dashboards.
Pros: Simplicity, clear audit trails. Cons: coarse-grained, can explode with many roles.
ABAC
Decisions based on attributes of user, resource, and environment. Offers fine-grained and contextual policies.
Example: Allow access if user.department == "payments" and request.ip in trusted_range and transaction.amount < 10000.
Pros: Flexibility and context. Cons: More complex to manage and reason about.
Design tip
Start with RBAC for baseline permissions and add attribute rules for exceptions or dynamic constraints. Maintain clear policy documentation to avoid hidden privileges.
