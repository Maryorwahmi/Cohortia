Prompt Deconstruction: Ride-Sharing Case

A structured 10-minute routine to practice clarifying before designing.

Initial ambiguities
  • Scale and geographic distribution
  • Real-time tracking requirements
  • Billing and consistency constraints
Five clarifying questions
  1. Functional: Which core features are required at launch?
  2. Functional: Is pooled or shared rides supported?
  3. Non-functional: What is the expected peak rides per minute?
  4. Non-functional: What latency is acceptable for driver locations?
  5. Non-functional: Are there specific regulatory or payment compliance needs?
Execution ritual
Spend ten minutes listing ambiguities and questions, then summarize agreed assumptions before drawing anything.
Key Takeaway

A disciplined clarification ritual focuses your design and demonstrates control to interviewers.

Example summary
Assume single-region launch, peak 5k rides per minute, 1-second location updates. Design with these limits and mention how you'd change the design later.
Common pitfall
Designing for hypothetical extreme scale before confirming needs. Validate first, optimize later.
