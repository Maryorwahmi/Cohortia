Learning Objectives — Practice Focus

Four behavioral objectives that interviewers observe during your design conversation.

01
Ask precise clarifying questions
List missing constraints up front to avoid wrong assumptions. This shows planning and focus.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Sketch boundaries
Define ownership and data flow so the interviewer understands your system scope.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Justify trade-offs
Explain pros and cons, and when alternatives might be better. This shows maturity.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Collaborate and adapt
Invite feedback, acknowledge assumptions, and adjust design as new constraints appear.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
