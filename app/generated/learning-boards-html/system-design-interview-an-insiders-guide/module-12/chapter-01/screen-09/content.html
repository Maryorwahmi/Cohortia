Choosing where to focus for the deep dive

A decision checklist to pick the highest-value component for detailed discussion.

Pick by risk
Choose the component with the most unknowns, highest load, or greatest impact on correctness or user experience.
Deep dive plan
Restate assumptions, define target metrics, outline the data model and API, and list failure modes and mitigations.
Common mistake
Deep diving into a simple component avoids risk but doesn’t demonstrate how you handle complexity.
Key Takeaway

Choose the hardest, highest-impact area for your deep dive and show you can reason about it thoroughly.

Decision rubric
  • Uncertainty or ambiguity
  • Performance or correctness impact
  • Ease of testing and demonstrating improvements
Example
If rider wait time determines retention, deep dive into matching and routing, showing how caching and partitioning reduce latency.
