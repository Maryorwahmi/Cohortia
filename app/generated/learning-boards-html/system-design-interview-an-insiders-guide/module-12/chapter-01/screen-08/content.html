Trade-offs: Consistency, Availability, and Latency

A practical checklist to explain trade-offs rather than just naming them.

Axes to consider
Correctness, timeliness, user experience, operational cost, and complexity of implementation.
Example decision
Synchronous replication for billing ensures no lost transactions but increases latency; pick it when correctness is non-negotiable.
Common mistake
Choosing the same trade-off for all subsystems instead of tailoring choices to each component’s needs.
Key Takeaway

Explain trade-offs in terms of the customer impact and operational consequences, not in abstract technical terms only.

Decision example
If user-facing timelines must appear instant, accept eventual consistency for analytics while serving cached views to users.
Impact summary
State the SLA change, cost implications, and how the system behaves under failure for each trade-off you propose.
