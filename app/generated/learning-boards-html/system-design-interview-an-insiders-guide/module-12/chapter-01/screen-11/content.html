Definition

Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

A checklist of frequent mistakes and short recovery scripts to use during an interview.

Premature details
Pause and ask clarifying questions; restate assumptions before diving in.
Missing trade-offs
Name alternatives and explain why you prefer one based on constraints.
Ignoring failure modes
List realistic failures and simple mitigations to show operational thinking.
Key Takeaway

If you slip, stop, restate assumptions, and invite a short check-in. Recovery is a scored skill too.

Recovery scripts
"I'll list my assumptions so far and correct any that you want changed." — concise and signals control.
Red flags
  • No explicit assumptions
  • No consideration of failure modes
  • No options or trade-offs discussed
