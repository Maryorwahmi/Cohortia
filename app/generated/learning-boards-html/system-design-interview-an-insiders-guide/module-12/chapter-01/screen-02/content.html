Learning Objectives

Skills you will practice and demonstrate in a system design interview.

01
Clarify requirements
Ask functional and non-functional questions to scope the problem. This prevents building the wrong thing and focuses your design work.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
High-level architecture
Sketch major components and data flow so interviewers understand your system boundaries and scaling approach.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Deep-dive & trade-offs
Explain component choices, failure modes, and trade-offs such as consistency versus latency, with reasoned justification.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Communicate & adapt
Present your thought process clearly, invite feedback, and adapt the design as new constraints appear during discussion.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
