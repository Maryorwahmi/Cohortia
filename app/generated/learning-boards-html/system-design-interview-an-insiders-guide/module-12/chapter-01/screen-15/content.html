Key Concepts — Quick Reference

Clarify
Ask 5 targeted questions and summarize the answers.
Sketch
Show components and data flow; estimate one or two metrics.
Justify
List alternatives and clearly explain why you chose one over the others.
Final Tip

Run this three-step checklist in your head before answering an interviewer: clarify, sketch, justify. It keeps your design focused and defensible.
