Datastore and Caching Considerations

Explain the trade-offs between relational and NoSQL stores and how cache layers fit into the architecture.

Decision factors
  • Consistency needs vs. availability and latency
  • Schema flexibility and query complexity
  • Read/write ratio and hot keys
Concrete example
Store trips and billing in a relational DB for transactional integrity; use in-memory caches for location lookups and hot candidate lists.
Misconception
Caching everything without invalidation strategy creates correctness issues under partition or failover.
Key Takeaway

Match datastore guarantees to the feature's correctness needs and use caching judiciously with a clear invalidation plan.

Cache patterns
Discuss TTLs, cache warming, write-through vs write-back, and when to accept eventual consistency for low-risk reads.
Failure mode
Cache-stale reads during network partition: mitigate with versioning, short TTLs, or fallbacks that validate against the primary store for critical operations.
