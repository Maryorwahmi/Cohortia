Clarification Phase — Asking the Right Questions

A short checklist of question categories and examples to scope the design correctly.

Functional Questions
  • What are the core user flows and must-have features?
  • Is real-time tracking required or can it be eventual?
Non-Functional Questions
  • Expected scale: users, requests per second, regional distribution.
  • Latency, availability, consistency, retention, and compliance needs.
Example Clarification
"Should the driver location update be sub-second, or is one-second accuracy acceptable?" — this single question shapes architecture choices.
Key Takeaway

Spend time clarifying: it prevents wasted design work and signals control to the interviewer.
