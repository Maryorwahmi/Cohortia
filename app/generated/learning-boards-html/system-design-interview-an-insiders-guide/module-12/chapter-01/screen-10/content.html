Definition

Communication Skills that Matter

Turn internal reasoning into clear signals for interviewers by stating assumptions, summarizing, and checking for alignment.

Narration pattern
State assumption, propose option, explain trade-off, summarize impact in one sentence.
Concrete script
"I'll assume three regions and target 200ms read latency. To meet this, I propose cached reads with eventual consistency for analytics; this reduces cost but may delay global metrics."
Check for alignment
Ask a single clarifying question like "Does that focus align with your expectations?" and adapt your next steps accordingly.
Key Takeaway

Practice short, clear explanations and regular alignment checks to make your thought process visible and collaborative.

Common pitfall
Talking at length without summaries loses the interviewer. Keep each explanation under 30 seconds and end with a one-sentence summary.
Pro tip
Label your assumptions explicitly and number them; that makes it easy to refer back when the interviewer proposes a change.
