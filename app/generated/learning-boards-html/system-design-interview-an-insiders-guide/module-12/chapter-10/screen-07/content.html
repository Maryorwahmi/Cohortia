Timeline

A practical weekly plan

Example schedule to make progress measurable and repeatable.

Day 1 — Study

Read notes for the weak topic and watch one worked example.

Day 2 — Drill

Run two timed whiteboard drills focused on the specific skill.

Day 3 — Mock

Run a short mock concentrating on that weakness; record it.

Day 4 — Review

Watch the recording, capture one improvement to test next day.

Example milestone

After two weeks, aim to raise your 'communication' rubric score by one point for the practiced topic and confirm via recorded comparison.

Check

Make goals measurable, time‑boxed, and reviewable. Vague tasks do not produce reliable improvement.
