Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Common Weaknesses
Core Concept

Typical patterns and targeted fixes

Recognizing repeating mistakes lets you choose the right drill instead of random practice.

Jumping to solutions

Practice a clarifying checklist and refuse to design until constraints are explicit.

Poor whiteboard skills

Do diagram drills: draw the same architecture three times with consistent labels and interfaces.

Shallow technical depth

Schedule weekly topic sprints: read one example and solve one related drill to build depth.

Concrete scenario card
Scenario

In a mock for a real‑time chat, you repeatedly fail to explain message ordering guarantees. Action: schedule two 30‑minute drills on ordering guarantees, then run a focused mock and ask the partner to evaluate clarity on this point.

Key Takeaway

Patterns repeat. Pinpoint the pattern, prescribe one drill, and verify progress with the rubric next session.
