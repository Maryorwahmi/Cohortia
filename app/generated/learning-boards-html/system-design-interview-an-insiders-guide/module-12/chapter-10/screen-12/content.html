Sketching conventions
Shapes
  • Rectangle = service / process
  • Cylinder = persistent storage
  • Arrow = data flow; label direction and key payload fields
Annotations
  • Write brief labels, e.g., "RPS=10k" or "latency <100ms"
  • Circle failure boundaries and note retries/backoff
Rapid sketch drill

1) 2 minutes: draw components. 2) 90 seconds: label & annotate. 3) 60 seconds: summarize trade-offs.

Common mistake

Drawing overly detailed internals too early. Start coarse, then zoom into one component for the deep dive.

Example exercise

Task: draw a social feed system. 2 minutes to sketch high-level components, 90 seconds to label data stores and cache, 3 minutes to deep dive on feed generation and delivery to followers.

Checklist for legibility
  • Is every arrow labeled with the primary payload?
  • Are component names short and consistent?
  • Are failure modes marked and explained?
  • Can you explain the diagram in 60 seconds?
Key Takeaway

Consistent shapes and labels let you communicate quickly and leave time for technical depth.
