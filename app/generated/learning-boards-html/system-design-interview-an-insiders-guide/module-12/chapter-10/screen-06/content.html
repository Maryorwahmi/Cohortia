Comparison

Self assessment vs partner feedback

Your internal intent and a listener’s reception often differ. Use partner feedback to calibrate what actually landed.

Ask your partner to deliver:
  • Two strengths: what was clear and why.
  • Two weaknesses: exact moments that confused them.
  • One specific fix: a practice task to address a weakness.
Example feedback

"When you said 'we'll shard by user', I wasn't sure what field you meant — label the key and explain partitioning impact next time."

Convert feedback into a task
Task example

If partner found your diagrams unclear: practice drawing a feed architecture with labeled queues and services three times this week, and record one run to compare before/after clarity.

Common mistake

Taking vague praise as evidence of mastery. Instead, ask for a concrete example so feedback becomes testable.
