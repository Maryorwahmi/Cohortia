Simulation checklist
Environment, Role Fidelity, Timing
  • Use a whiteboard or online drawing tool and practice clear diagrams.
  • Assign a peer to act as a strict interviewer with real pushback.
  • Set a 45–60 minute timer and enforce it without interruption.
Pro Tip

Ask your mock interviewer to role-play common interviewer prompts: "Why that component?", "How will this scale?", and "What if a partition happens?"

Common mistake

Running an untimed design discussion. You must practice finishing a coherent design within a fixed window to learn pacing and prioritization.

Key Takeaway

Simulate environment, role, and timer to turn knowledge into interview-ready skills.

Structured Mock Process
01 • Prepare

Pick a prompt, invite an interviewer, and prepare your whiteboard or tool. Confirm rules: clarifying questions allowed, strict timer.

02 • Clarify

Ask focused clarification questions, confirm constraints, and list core metrics like RPS and latency targets.

03 • High-level design

Sketch the major components and data flow. Keep the diagram legible and labelled for the deep dive.

04 • Deep dive & trade-offs

Zoom into critical components, explain trade-offs, and justify choices with respect to constraints.

Example prompt
Design a real-time chat application for 1M concurrent users.

Simulate: 50-minute timer, partner asks for scaling details and message ordering guarantees, and demands a fallback for offline devices.

Check
  • Did you ask clarifying questions?
  • Is the diagram legible and labelled?
  • Was there time for trade-offs?
