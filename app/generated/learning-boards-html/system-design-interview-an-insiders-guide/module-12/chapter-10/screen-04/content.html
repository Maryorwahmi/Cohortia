Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Why Mock Interviews Matter
Definition

What is a mock interview?

A timeboxed simulation of a real interview to practice architecture, trade‑offs, communication, and timing under low stakes.

Benefit: Muscle memory

Repetition makes the structured interview steps automatic so cognitive load is lower during the real interview.

Benefit: Time management

Practice pacing ensures you cover clarification, high‑level design, and a meaningful deep dive inside the allotted time.

Benefit: Communication

You learn to explain choices succinctly and to draw legible diagrams under pressure.

Key Takeaway

Mimic the real interview closely so that performance improvements transfer directly to the actual event.

Concrete example
Run: Design a distributed rate limiter

Set a 45‑minute timer. Clarify the scope, estimate peak QPS, sketch a high‑level design, and deep dive into a token bucket or sliding window implementation. Record the session.

Misconception

Practice that lacks recording or a rubric often feels productive but leaves habits unexamined. Objective review is what converts practice into improvement.
