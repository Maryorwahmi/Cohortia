Feedback → Drill workflow
  1. Collect: partner notes, self-review timestamps, rubric scores.
  2. Group: cluster recurring issues into 3–5 themes.
  3. Design: create a micro-drill with a timebox and success metric.
  4. Schedule: repeat the drill several times per week.
  5. Measure: review recordings to verify improvement and iterate.
Theme

Clarification

Drill

90s clarification drill on 30 prompts

Success

Ask 3 targeted clarifying Qs in 8/10 prompts

Weekly plan (example)

Mon: 50-minute mock. Tue: 20-minute diagram drills. Thu: 3×90s clarification prompts. Sat: playback review and update drills.

Pitfall

Trying to fix everything at once. Prioritize the 1–2 highest-leverage habits first.

Tracking template

Drill: 90s clarification

Frequency: 3× per week

Metric: 3 clarifying Qs in 8/10 prompts

Review weekly and adjust frequency or change drill if no measurable progress.

Key Takeaway

Habits change with focused drills and measurable tracking, not with vague intentions.
