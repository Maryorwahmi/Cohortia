Process

Sample reflection framework

Score each category 1–5 and write two observations per category to make feedback precise and actionable.

Problem Understanding & Clarification
  • Asked clarifying questions?
  • Captured functional and non‑functional requirements?
High‑Level Design
  • Was architecture logical and complete?
  • Were estimations reasonable?
Deep Dive
  • Detail in chosen components?
  • Considered edge cases and pitfalls?
Trade‑offs & Justification
  • Discussed alternatives and pros/cons?
  • Linked choices back to requirements?
Communication & Engagement
  • Clear, structured explanations and thinking out loud?
  • Engaged the interviewer; accepted and incorporated feedback?
Example entry

Problem Understanding: 4/5 — Asked good clarifying questions but missed a constraint about budget. Action: Practice identifying budget constraints by reviewing 10 past prompts this week.

Action

Capture two strengths and two weaknesses, then pick one weakness to convert into three measurable practice tasks for the coming week.
