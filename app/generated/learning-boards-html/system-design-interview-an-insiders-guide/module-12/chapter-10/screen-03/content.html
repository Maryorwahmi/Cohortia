Eyebrow: Core Concept

Simulate realistic interview conditions

A realistic simulation creates the timing, cognitive load, and communication constraints of the real interview so you practice relevant skills.

Step 1 — Recruit an interviewer
Prefer someone with system design experience. If unavailable, brief the partner on the expected role and desired probing style.
Step 2 — Tools and timer
Use a whiteboard or online drawing tool and set a 45–60 minute timer. Enforce timeboxes for clarifying, high‑level design, and deep dive.
Step 3 — Record the session
Record audio or video to review pacing, filler words, and whiteboard clarity. Playback reveals hidden habits.
Step 4 — Use the rubric
Have the interviewer score each rubric category and provide two concrete examples of strengths and two of improvements.
Example

Run a 45‑minute mock of designing a distributed rate limiter, record, then spend 15 minutes rating yourself against the rubric and capturing three action items.

Supporting cards
Checklist for interviewer
  • Ask clarifying questions early.
  • Push for a deep dive on one component.
  • Note time management and pacing.
Common-mistake callout

Avoid letting the mock become a lecture. The goal is to practice dialogue: ask, propose, and confirm.

Key takeaway

Replicate the interview constraints and collect objective data to make practice productive.
