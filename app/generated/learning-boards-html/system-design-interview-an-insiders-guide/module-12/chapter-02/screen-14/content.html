Functional vs NFR
State what the system does and how well it must perform, with numbers.
Structured sequence
Ask Actions → Scale → Latency → Availability → Consistency → Scope.
Quantify constraints
Numbers like QPS, ms, and uptime directly drive architecture choices.
Summary takeaway

Keep the three mental questions: What? How well? How many? Use them to guide every clarification and avoid costly assumptions.
