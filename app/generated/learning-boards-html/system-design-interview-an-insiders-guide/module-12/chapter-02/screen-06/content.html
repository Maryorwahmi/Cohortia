Comparison
Low scale vs High scale
Low scale (e.g., 100 QPS)

Single-region deployment, simple sharding, basic cache layer, less operational complexity and lower cost.

High scale (e.g., 100k QPS)

Sharded writes, distributed storage, multi-region CDN, asynchronous processing, and substantial automation for ops.

Questions to ask
  • What is expected DAU and peak QPS for uploads and reads?
  • What is the expected average and max object size (e.g., image size)?
  • What growth rate should we plan for in the first year?
Key takeaway

Numbers guide architecture. Always convert "at scale" into explicit metrics before choosing storage and partitioning strategies.
