Translate objectives into interview lines
01 — Differentiate
One functional + one NFR

Say: "Core actions are upload and feed retrieval. Target feed latency: 200 ms at peak."

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Structure
Sequence your questions

Mnemonic: Actions → Scale → Latency → Availability → Scope.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — NFRs
List the key NFRs

Say each NFR with a number: scale (QPS), latency (ms), availability (%), consistency model.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Ask open
Avoid yes/no

Prefer: "What are the availability expectations and business impact of downtime?"

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Quick reference
  • Quantify targets whenever possible.
  • Confirm scope and integrations explicitly.
  • Avoid assumptions about scale or consistency.
