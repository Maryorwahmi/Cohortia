Core Concept
Functional vs Non-functional

Break the problem into behavior (features) and quality attributes (performance, reliability, cost). Both guide architecture.

Functional (what)
Upload, view, like, comment, search.
Non-Functional (how)
Scale (QPS), latency (ms), availability (%), consistency model.
Illustrative example
Scenario A

Feature: photo upload. NFR: 100 QPS, eventual consistency acceptable, 99.5% availability. Simple design with modest caching and single region replication.

Scenario B

Feature: photo upload. NFR: 100k QPS, strong consistency for counts, 99.99% availability. Requires sharded storage, aggressive caching, multi-region replicas and careful consistency strategy.

Key takeaway

Always attach a measurable NFR to each functional requirement before designing. Numbers change architecture.
