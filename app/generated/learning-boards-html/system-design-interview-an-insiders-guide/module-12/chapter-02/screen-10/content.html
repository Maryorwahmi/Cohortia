Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Security, Cost & Timeline
Core Concept
Security and constraints

Ask for authentication, encryption, access control, and regulatory constraints. Also confirm budget and delivery expectations.

Security

Encryption at rest, authentication flows, and authorization scopes shape design choices.

Cost & Timeline

Budget and launch deadlines determine whether you choose managed services or bespoke infrastructure.

Scenario guidance

If time-to-market is short, propose a minimal viable architecture that meets essential NFRs and leaves room to add redundancy, multi-region replication, or stricter security later. If budget is constrained, select cost-effective managed components and avoid premature over-provisioning.

Common pitfall

Designing for ideal NFRs without checking cost or timeline, leading to impractical proposals.

Key Takeaway

Always surface budget and schedule constraints early so your design balances practicality and requirements.
