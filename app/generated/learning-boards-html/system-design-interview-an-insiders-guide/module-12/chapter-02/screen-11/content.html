Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Scope & Integrations
Architecture
Define boundaries and dependencies

State which components you will design and list external services that must be integrated. Confirm whether clients are part of scope.

In-Scope checklist
  • Backend APIs
  • Storage and replication
  • Basic auth flows
Out-of-Scope examples
  • Mobile deployment specifics
  • Third-party vendor implementation
Integration prompts
  • Are any third-party services required and what are their SLAs?
  • Which clients must be supported at launch?
  • Do we own the UI, or only the backend?
Key Takeaway

State your scope assumptions aloud and confirm them to avoid designing unnecessary components.
