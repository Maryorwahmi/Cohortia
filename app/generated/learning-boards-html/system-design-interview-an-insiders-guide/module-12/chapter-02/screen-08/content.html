Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Availability & Durability
Comparison
Availability vs Durability
Availability

Measures uptime and access; ask acceptable downtime or SLA percentage.

Durability

Ensures data survives failures; ask about tolerated data loss and retention requirements.

Design implications

High availability requires redundancy and failover. High durability requires replication, backups, and careful storage choices. Both affect cost and complexity.

Clarifying prompts
  • What is the acceptable downtime per month or year?
  • What is the maximum allowed data loss in case of catastrophic failure?
  • Are there regulatory retention requirements for data?
Key Takeaway

Availability and durability are separate constraints and each leads to distinct redundancy and backup decisions.
