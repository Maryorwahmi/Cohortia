Clarifying questions for Photo Sharing
Functional
  1. What are the primary ways users interact with photos (upload, view, like, comment, share)?
  2. What user roles exist and what admin functions are required?
  3. Are there limits on photo size or formats at upload?
Non-Functional
  1. What is the expected peak number of photo uploads per second?
  2. What is the acceptable feed load latency in milliseconds?
  3. What availability SLA and data-loss tolerance are required?
  4. Are there security or compliance requirements for stored media?
Good vs Bad

Bad: "Do we need high availability?" Good: "What is the acceptable downtime per month and the recovery time objective?" Quantify, don't guess.

Example deliverable

Create a 10–12 question checklist before the interview and adapt it to the prompt. Use it to produce a concise problem statement with quantified NFRs.

Key Takeaway

Practice makes these questions second nature so you can use them quickly under time pressure.
