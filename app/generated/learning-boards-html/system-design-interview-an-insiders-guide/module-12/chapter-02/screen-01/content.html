The Clarification Phase

Learn how to ask structured questions that reveal scope, constraints, and priorities in any system design prompt.

Estimated time: 15 minutes
In this lesson

Differentiate functional and non-functional requirements and learn a checklist for probing them.

Coming up

Practice clarifying questions for a photo-sharing service and common pitfalls to avoid.

Why this matters

Design decisions hinge on constraints. Clarifying requirements steers you to the right database, caching, replication, and operational trade-offs — saving cost and avoiding catastrophic rework.

Concept map
From prompt to constraints
Functional
What the system does — user actions, APIs, workflows (e.g., upload, search, follow).
Non-functional
How it performs — scale, latency, availability, consistency, durability, security, cost.
Checklist
Ask open, specific questions; quantify targets; clarify scope and integrations.
Outcome
A concrete problem statement that drives architecture and trade-offs.
