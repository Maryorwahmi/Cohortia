Common Bottlenecks
Identify likely points of failure and concrete mitigations.
  • Single-point DB: add leader-follower replication and automated failover.
  • Hot partition: shard keys or use consistent hashing and request rerouting.
  • Cache storm: use request coalescing, early refresh, and backpressure.
  • External API slowness: circuit breakers, retries with backoff, and graceful degradation.
Prioritized Roadmap
Immediate (1–2 sprints)
Monitoring, circuit breakers, and basic replication for critical services.
Medium (quarter)
Partitioning hot keys, automated failover testing, and capacity planning.
Long-term
Architectural changes like multi-region replication and advanced consistency models.
Key Takeaway

Anticipating failure and proposing prioritized, incremental fixes demonstrates operational judgment.
