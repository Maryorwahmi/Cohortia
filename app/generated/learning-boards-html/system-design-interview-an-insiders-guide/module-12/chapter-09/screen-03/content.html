Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › How to measure progress
Milestones and practice checks
Small, repeatable checks turn communication goals into reliable interview performance.
01 — Five-minute walkthrough
Start with clarifying Qs, estimate scale, present high-level design, and finish with a 30-second summary.
02 — Whiteboard legibility checklist
Boxes labeled, arrows directional, colors purposeful, handwriting legible from a distance.
03 — Engagement score
Track how often you ask for feedback and respond to hints; aim to keep the discussion two-way.
04 — What-if drills
Practice spikes, failures, and slow dependencies; mention monitoring and mitigation steps in responses.
Key Takeaway

Assessable, repeatable drills convert communication habits into dependable interview skills.
