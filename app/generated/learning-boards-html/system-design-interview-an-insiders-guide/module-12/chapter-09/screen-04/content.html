Process Steps
A time-boxed sequence to keep the interview focused.
  1. 1. Clarify Requirements: Ask functional and non-functional questions, confirm assumptions.
  2. 2. Estimate Scale: Quick QPS, storage, bandwidth estimates to ground choices.
  3. 3. High-Level Design: Major components and responsibilities, purpose of each.
  4. 4. Deep Dive: Choose 1–2 critical components for detailed design.
  5. 5. Trade-offs: Discuss alternatives and justify choices.
  6. 6. Bottlenecks & Future Work: Anticipate problems and propose evolution steps.
  7. 7. Summary: Recap decisions and their rationale succinctly.
Tip: practice timing each step. A short, clear summary is often the most memorable part of your answer.

Common mistake
