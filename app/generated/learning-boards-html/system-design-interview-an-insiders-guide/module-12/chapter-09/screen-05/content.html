Clarifying Questions
Examples of focused questions to reveal constraints.
  • Functional:
  • Who are the users and what operations matter most?
  • Non-functional:
  • What are latency targets, consistency needs, and retention policies?
Narrate your assumptions so the interviewer can correct you if a constraint differs from their intent.
Estimating Scale
Quick arithmetic to make design choices defensible.
Example estimation
Assume: 1,000 req/s, 100 KB payload
Daily traffic ≈ 1,000 * 86,400 = 86.4M requests
Daily data ≈ 86.4M * 100 KB ≈ 8.64 TB/day
Use: caching and sharding to reduce DB load
Concrete example
If retention is short, cache eviction reduces long-term storage cost; if retention is long, consider tiered storage.
Misconception
Don't treat rough estimates as precise specs; present them as assumptions you can refine.
