Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Engagement
Engage and adapt
Simple conversational patterns that invite feedback and keep you aligned.
Ask alignment questions
Example: “Is latency more important than consistency for this feature?”
Listen and rephrase
If interrupted, rephrase the interviewer’s point to confirm understanding before answering.
Adapt depth
Offer to zoom into areas they care about and time-box the deep dive.
Concrete example
Ask “Would you like me to expand the data model or the caching strategy?” to let the interviewer steer the depth.
Misconception
Thinking out loud reveals process; it does not weaken your credibility when done clearly and concisely.
