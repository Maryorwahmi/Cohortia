Learning Objectives
Learn the communication patterns that make your design legible and defensible.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Structure your response
Organize answers from clarifying questions to summary so decisions are traceable to requirements.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Master whiteboard technique
Use clear shapes, labels, arrows, and legible handwriting to communicate architecture quickly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Engage with the interviewer
Ask for feedback, listen, and adapt so the interview becomes a collaborative evaluation.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Handle challenging questions
Address edge cases, admit unknowns with a plan, and prioritize time for key areas.
