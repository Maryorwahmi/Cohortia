Responding to curveballs
A short structure to keep your answer calm and actionable.
  1. 1. Restate the scenario: Confirm you understood the question.
  2. 2. State assumptions: Declare what you will hold fixed for the answer.
  3. 3. Propose mitigations: Concrete steps like replication, retries, or throttling.
  4. 4. Offer follow-up: Testing steps, monitoring, and timeline for stronger fixes.
Concrete example
If asked “What if DB goes down?”, describe replication and promotion, explain read-only windows during failover, and suggest monitoring and graceful degradation during recovery.
Common Mistake
Responding defensively or guessing specifics weakens your credibility; prefer structured mitigation and follow-up tests.
