Whiteboard Best Practices
Simple rules to make your diagram readable and persuasive.
  • Clean slate: Organize space and reserve a zoom area.
  • Consistent shapes: Boxes, cylinders, clouds with short labels.
  • Directional arrows: Annotate with read/write or frequency when useful.
  • Intentional color: Differentiate components, flow, and failures.
  • Minimal erasing: Redraw in a separate zone to avoid distracting the interviewer.
Practice checklist
Legibility
Could someone read the board from three feet away? If not, rewrite larger and simpler.
Labels
Every box and arrow should have a clear one- or two-word label.
Pro tip

Use a dedicated area to redraw deep-dive details so you keep the high-level picture intact.
