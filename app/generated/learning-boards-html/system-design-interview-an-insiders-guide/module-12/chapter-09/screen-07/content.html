How to pick components
Select components that directly address the stated constraints or carry operational risk.
  • Risk or complexity: data model, partitioning, caching, or external integration.
  • Impact on SLOs: anything that affects latency or availability should be prioritized.
  • Time-box the dive: announce scope and time so you stay on schedule.
Explicit bounds help the interviewer follow and ensure you cover what matters within the time limit.
