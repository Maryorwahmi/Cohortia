RTO and RPO
  • RTO: how long the system can be down and still meet business requirements.
  • RPO: how much data loss in time is acceptable after recovery.
To meet aggressive RTO/RPO, combine multi-region deployments, synchronous or near-synchronous replication for critical data, and automated failover scripts. For less aggressive targets, asynchronous replication and periodic backups may be sufficient. Always validate backups by restoring them during scheduled drills.
Multi-region
Deploy replicas in separate regions and test cross-region failover regularly.
Backups
Store backups in geographically separate locations and validate them frequently.
Planning checklist
  • Define RTO and RPO per service and document recovery steps.
  • Automate failover where possible and test with drills.
  • Balance cost vs. business impact when choosing replication strategy.
Key Takeaway

DR is both design and practice: set measurable targets and verify them through routine testing.
