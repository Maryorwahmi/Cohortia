Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Payment processing case study
Design summary
  • CAP choice: CP to protect transaction correctness during partitions.
  • Replication: leader-follower with N+1 replicas and automated promotion.
  • Downstream protection: retries with backoff + circuit breaker for bank APIs.
  • Isolation: bulkheads for payment threads and connection pools.
  • DR: multi-region replication with RPO near zero and tested failover runbooks.
Hands-on task
Complete the starter template by selecting CP or AP for the payment flow, describing a replication strategy, and naming two patterns to protect against slow bank APIs.
Concrete choices
Promote synchronous replication for leader if you require near-zero RPO. Use circuit breaker plus backoff for external calls; bulkhead payment processing threads from background work.
Key Takeaway

For critical workflows choose correctness first, then design redundancy and defensive patterns so availability is maintained without compromising data integrity.
