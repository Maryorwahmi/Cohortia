Redundancy strategies
N+1
Keep one extra component to take over load when one fails.
Active-Passive
A standby remains ready to be promoted; brief downtime during switchover.
Active-Active
All nodes serve traffic; failover is seamless but coordination is complex.
Practical checklist
  • Ensure promoted nodes have up-to-date state and capacity.
  • Automate health checks and promotions to reduce mean-time-to-recovery.
  • Design election to avoid split-brain and data divergence.
Example: leader-follower
Followers replicate a leader. If the leader fails, a follower is promoted to preserve availability and durability.
Common pitfall: failing to coordinate promotion can lead to two leaders and conflicting writes.
Key Takeaway

Redundancy reduces downtime but requires coordination, capacity planning, and automated failover procedures.
