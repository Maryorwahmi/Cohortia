Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Promoting a replica
Promotion steps
  1. Confirm failure: ensure the leader is truly unreachable, not just slow.
  2. Check replication lag: ensure the replica has all recent commits.
  3. Elect/promote: use deterministic election or operator command.
  4. Reconfigure cluster: point followers to the new leader and resume replication.
Conceptual command sequence
# On the old primary (if accessible, to prevent split-brain)
SELECT pg_failover_trigger();

# On the chosen replica
SELECT pg_promote();
Note: these are conceptual operator steps; automate and test them in your environment.
Example pitfall
Promoting a lagging replica can drop committed transactions. Never promote without confirming replication completeness.
Key Takeaway

Promotion must be deliberate, tested, and observable to avoid split-brain and data loss.
