Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Consistency Models
Strong consistency
Guarantee: once a write completes, all subsequent reads return that value.
  • Example: bank balances, payment transactions.
  • Trade-off: higher latency and coordination overhead to ensure correctness.
  • When to choose: critical state that cannot tolerate conflicts or duplicates.
Eventual consistency
Guarantee: replicas converge over time; reads may be stale during convergence.
  • Example: social feed updates, cache layers.
  • Trade-off: improved availability and lower latency at the cost of temporary staleness.
  • When to choose: high-read, less-critical flows where user experience prefers availability.
Read-your-writes
A middle-ground guaranteeing a client sees its recent updates while others may not yet.
Misconception
Eventual consistency is not the same as data loss if you design conflict resolution properly.
Key Takeaway

Map each business flow to a consistency guarantee and test the user-visible effects of stale reads.
