Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › CP vs AP systems
CP systems
Prioritize consistency under partition; may reject or delay requests to guarantee a single correct view.
When to use
Financial transactions, inventory updates, any flow requiring immediate correctness.
Behavior during partition
Block or error operations that can't be guaranteed consistent.
AP systems
Prioritize availability under partition; continue serving requests and reconcile state later.
When to use
Feeds, caches, and UX where temporary staleness is acceptable for better availability.
Behavior during partition
Accept writes and reads locally; resolve conflicts and converge after partition heals.
Example contrast
An e-commerce checkout picks CP to avoid overselling. A social feed picks AP to deliver a fast, available experience even when some nodes are unreachable. Both choices are correct depending on which loss—correctness or availability—your users cannot tolerate.
