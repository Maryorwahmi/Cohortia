Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Circuit Breaker
Circuit breaker states
Closed
Normal operation; failures are counted.
Open
Calls fail fast to protect resources.
Half-open
A few test calls allowed to check recovery.
Practical notes
  • Tune thresholds to balance sensitivity and stability.
  • Log and monitor opens so you can act on recurring failures.
  • Pair with fallbacks to continue degraded operation when possible.
Key Takeaway

Circuit breakers stop wasteful retries and preserve capacity for healthy traffic; pair them with monitoring and fallbacks.
