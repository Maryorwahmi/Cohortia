Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Learning Objectives

Applied Skills

Concrete tasks to practice and demonstrate mastery.

Cache design exercise
Propose where to place caches, what to cache, and how to invalidate after profile updates.
DB scaling exercise
Decide between replication, sharding, or both. Pick a sharding key and explain hot-spot avoidance.
Async pipeline exercise
Sketch a message queue flow for background processing such as image handling and feed generation.

Performance indicators

  • Read latency under 100ms for 10k QPS reads.
  • Write durability with replication and acceptable replication lag.
  • Queue backlog kept under control with autoscaling workers.
  • Cache hit ratio and invalidation correctness.
