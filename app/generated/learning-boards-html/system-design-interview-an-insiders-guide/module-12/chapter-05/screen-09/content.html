Leader-Follower

Read scaling and redundancy

Writes to leader; followers serve reads. Monitor replication lag and plan leader promotion for failover.

Benefits
Distribute read traffic and provide redundancy for availability.
Trade-offs
Replication lag and complexity around failover and consistency.
Operational notes
  • Monitor replication lag and set alerts on follower staleness.
  • Use read routing to send non-critical reads to followers.
  • Plan leader promotion procedures and automatic or manual failover strategies.
Key Takeaway

Replication scales reads and adds redundancy, but expect eventual consistency trade-offs and manage replication lag.
