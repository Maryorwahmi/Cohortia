Deep Dive: Scalability and Performance

Design choices determine whether a system handles growth and keeps latency low. We'll combine theory with practical patterns you can justify in an interview or design review.

In this lesson
Scaling trade-offs, caching layers, DB replication & sharding, async queues.
Coming up
A worked example: scaling a user profile service to 10k QPS reads.
Why it matters
Maintain responsiveness under surge traffic and reduce single points of failure.
Concept Map

Layered view: requests to storage

Client & CDN
Browser caches and CDNs reduce latency and offload origin servers for static and cacheable dynamic content.
App & Distributed Cache
In-memory app caches and shared caches like Redis lower read pressure on databases and provide fast access to hot data.
Database
Use replication for read scaling and sharding for write and data distribution when a single node cannot handle load.
Async Queue
Message queues decouple producers from workers, improving responsiveness and resilience to spikes.
Key takeaway
Combine horizontal scaling, caches, replication, sharding and queues to meet latency and availability targets. Justify choices by constraints, load patterns and data characteristics.
