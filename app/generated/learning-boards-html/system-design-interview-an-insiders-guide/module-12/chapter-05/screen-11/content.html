Sharding key criteria
  • Even distribution across shards to avoid hot spots.
  • Aligns with common query patterns for efficient lookups.
  • Supportable resharding strategy for growth and rebalancing.
Good example
user_id hashed across N shards gives even spread and single-user lookups map to one shard.
Bad example
created_at or last_login_date creates write concentration and hot shards.
Operational note
Plan for rebalancing: migrate ranges incrementally or use a directory service to map keys and update routing with minimal downtime.
Key Takeaway

Justify your sharding key with distribution data and a resharding plan; avoid time-based keys for hot data.
