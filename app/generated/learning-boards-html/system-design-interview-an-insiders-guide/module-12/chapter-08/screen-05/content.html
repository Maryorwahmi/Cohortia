Comparison › Consistency vs. Availability
Consistency (CP)
Guarantees a single up-to-date view across replicas at the cost of potential unavailability during partitions.
  • When to choose: financial transactions, inventory systems where correctness is essential.
  • Example: relational database with synchronous replication for transaction commits.
  • Mitigation: plan maintenance windows and graceful degradation for non-essential features.
Availability (AP)
Prioritizes serving requests even during partitions, accepting temporary divergence between replicas.
  • When to choose: social feeds, user timelines, caching layers where stale reads are acceptable.
  • Example: wide-column stores with eventual consistency and asynchronous replication.
  • Mitigation: reconciliation strategies and conflict resolution to repair divergence over time.
Concrete Scenario
Payments → pick consistency. Social feed → pick availability.
Common Mistake
Saying both are achievable without acknowledging costs. You must discuss trade-offs and mitigations.
Key Takeaway

Pick the property aligned with the system's correctness and availability needs, then plan mitigations for the accepted downside.
