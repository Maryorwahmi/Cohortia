Trade-offs and Justification
Make and explain design choices that balance requirements, constraints, and real-world trade-offs.

In this lesson you will see common trade-offs, a repeatable decision flow, and concrete examples you can present in interviews or architecture reviews. The focus is on argument quality: show alternatives, weigh pros and cons with respect to requirements, pick an option, and acknowledge mitigations.

Why it matters: clear justification shows you think beyond code — you reason about business needs, reliability, cost, and team capabilities.
