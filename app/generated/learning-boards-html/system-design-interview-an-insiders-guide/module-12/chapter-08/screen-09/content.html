Comparison › Managed vs. Self-Hosted
Managed Service
Offloads backups, patching, and scaling. Good for small teams and fast launches.
Example: AWS RDS reduces DBA tasks, at the expense of higher per-unit cost and potential vendor lock-in.
Self-hosted
Gives full control and tuning, but requires platform expertise and operational effort.
Example: self-hosting PostgreSQL for custom extensions or tight cost control at very large scale.
Decision factors
Team expertise, budget, time-to-market, and required customizations.
Common Mistake
Choosing self-hosted solely to save money without accounting for operational costs and incident risk.
Key Takeaway

Pick managed for speed and lower ops overhead unless you need custom control; quantify the trade-off in engineering hours.
