Step-by-step evaluation
  1. State alternatives: list realistic options and assumptions.
  2. Define criteria and weights: latency, cost, scale, operability.
  3. Score and compute totals: use measured estimates where possible.
  4. Select and justify: link choice to requirements and expected metrics.
  5. Mitigate downsides: monitoring, fallbacks, staged rollouts.
Concrete Example
Comparing PostgreSQL and Cassandra by weighting consistency, write throughput, and global replication requirements.
Common Mistake
Using subjective scoring without measurable estimates or forgetting to factor operational costs into weights.
Key Takeaway

A repeatable scoring process makes decisions explainable and defensible under scrutiny.
