Practice: State, Score, Select
A concise interview-ready evaluation pattern
01 — State alternatives
Name the reasonable options and assumptions you made when narrowing them.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Score options
Assign weights to criteria like latency, cost, and team ability, then score each alternative.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Select and justify
Choose the best-scoring option and explain mitigations for its trade-offs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Communicate concisely
Use one or two sentences to summarize your choice and the main trade-off you accepted.
