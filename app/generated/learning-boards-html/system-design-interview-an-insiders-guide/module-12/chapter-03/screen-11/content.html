Estimation

QPS and storage basics

Keep assumptions explicit and show the arithmetic. Round to sensible units and include peak factors when justifying capacity.

Sample calculation
// Assumption: 10,000,000 DAU, 10 req/day each
10000000 * 10 = 100000000 requests/day
100000000 / 86400 ≈ 1157 QPS

// Storage: 10 photos/user * 1 MB/photo
10 * 1 MB = 10 MB/user
10 MB * 10,000,000 users = 100,000,000 MB ≈ 100 TB (note: 1,000 MB = 1 GB)
Interpretation
Use QPS to size load balancers and service instances, and use storage estimates to justify object storage tiers and sharding. Add a peak multiplier to account for bursts and growth.
Common mistake
Presenting numbers without assumptions or peak factors makes them unusable for design decisions.
Pro tip
Annotate your sketch with the numbers used in these estimates so reviewers can follow your rationale.
Key Takeaway

Simple arithmetic and clear assumptions are often more persuasive than perfect precision during high-level design.
