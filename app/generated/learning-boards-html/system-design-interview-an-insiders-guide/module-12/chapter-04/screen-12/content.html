Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Authentication and Authorization
Authentication
Verify identity using tokens or OAuth flows. Document token type, lifetime, and refresh behavior for clients.
Example
Require a bearer token for writes and include token scope checks at the gateway.
Authorization
Apply role-based checks and ownership validation. For deletes, verify the requester is the owner or an admin.
Common mistake
Confusing authentication with authorization.
Key Takeaway

Enforce authentication at the edge and authorization checks per operation. Document ownership rules and role scopes to keep access controlled and auditable.
