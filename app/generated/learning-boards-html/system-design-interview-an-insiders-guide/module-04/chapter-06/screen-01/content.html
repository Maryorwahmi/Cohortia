Caching Strategies: Design and Implementation

Understand where caches live, the three main write/read strategies, and how to balance latency, consistency, and cost in a distributed system.

Purpose
Reduce latency and offload the database.
Strategies
Cache-Aside, Write-Through, Write-Back.
Safety
Invalidation, TTL, and eviction prevent stale data.
Pitfalls
Stampedes and over-caching are costly.
Explore Key Topics
Cache-Aside
A lazy loading pattern where the app checks cache first and populates on miss, ideal for read-heavy, low-change data.
Write Strategies
Write-Through keeps cache consistent with DB; Write-Back favors write speed but risks data loss on failure.
Eviction & Safety
TTL, LRU/LFU, and stampede mitigations are essential for correctness and stability.
