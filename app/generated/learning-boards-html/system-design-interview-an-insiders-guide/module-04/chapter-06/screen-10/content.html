Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Eviction Policies
LRU (Least Recently Used)

Evicts the least recently accessed item. Works well for workloads where recent access predicts near-future access, such as trending content.

LFU (Least Frequently Used)

Evicts items with the fewest accesses. Useful when long-term popularity matters.

FIFO & Random

FIFO evicts oldest items by insertion time. Random eviction is simple and avoids maintaining heavy metadata but is less optimal for hit rate.

Measure hit rate and access patterns before choosing. Many caches allow you to instrument and switch policies based on observed performance.
