Skills and Milestones

Concrete ways to prove you understand caching in design interviews.

Implement
Cache-Aside

Write functions that show cache hits, misses, and explicit invalidation after database updates.

Choose
Eviction Policy

Explain why LRU suits trending content or why LFU helps skewed access patterns.

Design
Stampede Mitigation

Plan locking, pre-fetching, or stale-while-revalidate techniques to avoid thundering herds.

Assessment hint
When asked to pick a strategy, justify it with read/write ratios, data volatility, and tolerance for stale reads. That reasoning is as important as the choice itself.
