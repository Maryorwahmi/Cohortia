Redis & Memcached

Shared, in-memory key-value stores used to cache hot data across many app servers. Redis provides richer features; Memcached is minimal and fast.

Operational considerations
  • Replication and clustering for availability
  • Consistent hashing or sharding for distribution
  • Warm-up strategies to avoid cold cache spikes
Use cases
  • Session storage for horizontally scaled app servers
  • Caching computed or aggregated results for fast reads
  • Coordinating invalidation via pub/sub channels
Remember: a distributed cache is part of your availability design. Treat it as a critical dependency and add observability and failover planning.
