Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Key Concepts
Caching
Store copies in fast storage to reduce latency and offload the primary datastore.
Strategies
Cache-Aside, Write-Through, Write-Back — each has distinct latency and consistency trade-offs.
Invalidation & Eviction
Use TTL, explicit invalidation, pub/sub, LRU/LFU to maintain freshness and make space safely.
Summary

Good caching choices are measured choices: align strategy with workload, enforce invalidation, and instrument to verify behavior.
