Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Choosing a Strategy
Step 1: Measure
Estimate read/write ratio and data volatility.
Step 2: Decide consistency
If immediate consistency is required, favor Write-Through or explicit invalidation strategies.
Step 3: Consider cost
Balance memory costs, DB load, and complexity of failure modes.
Example decision
For a news site with high reads and infrequent updates: choose Cache-Aside + LRU. For a logging stream that needs high write throughput and acceptable occasional loss: consider Write-Back with durability safeguards.
