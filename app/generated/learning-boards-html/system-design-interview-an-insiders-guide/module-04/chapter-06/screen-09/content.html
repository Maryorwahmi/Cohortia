Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Invalidation Techniques

TTL (Time-to-Live)

Entries expire automatically after a duration. Simple to implement but can cause stale reads until expiry.

Explicit invalidation

Application removes or updates cache entries after writes to ensure freshness immediately.

Pub/Sub & Stale-While-Revalidate

Publish database changes so cache listeners can invalidate affected keys. Stale-while-revalidate serves stale data while asynchronously refreshing the cache.

Design tip: combine techniques. Use TTL for simple items, explicit invalidation for transactional updates, and pub/sub for wide-distribution systems to keep caches in sync.
Why it matters

Invalidation policy determines how fresh your reads are and how much load you place on the database; choose policies that match consistency and scale needs.
