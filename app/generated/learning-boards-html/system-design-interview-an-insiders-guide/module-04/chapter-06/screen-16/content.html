Measure
Hit rate, miss latency, and eviction counts inform caching choices.
Strategy

Design invalidation, eviction, and stampede mitigations before roll-out.

When you propose caching in a design interview, state the workload assumptions, the chosen strategy, and the exact invalidation and eviction plan.
