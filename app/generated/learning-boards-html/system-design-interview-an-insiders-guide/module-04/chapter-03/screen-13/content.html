Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Fraud Detection
Modeling pattern
Nodes: Account, Transaction, Device. Edges: TRANSFERRED, USED_BY. Edge properties include amount and timestamp so you can filter during traversal.
Example: find paths of transfers from Account X to Account Y within 48 hours where amounts exceed a threshold.
Operational constraints
  • Limit traversal depth and apply time-window filters to bound work.
  • Precompute suspicious subgraphs or score edges to prune searches.
  • Balance storage of transaction properties with traversal performance.
Misconception: deep graph queries are always cheap — they can be expensive unless pruned and constrained.
Key Takeaway

Use graph traversal with time and property filters for fraud detection and explain pruning or precomputation strategies to control cost.
