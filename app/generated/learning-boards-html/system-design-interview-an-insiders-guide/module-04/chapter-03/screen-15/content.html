Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Quick Reminders
Column‑Family Advantage
Sparse rows and column-oriented storage make analytical scans and high-volume writes efficient.
Graph Definition
Nodes, edges, and properties model relationships so traversals and pattern matching are fast and natural.
Query‑Driven Design
Design tables and graphs for the queries you must run; denormalize where it reduces read cost.
Last Thought

In interviews, state the access patterns and operational trade-offs that led you to a column-family, graph, or hybrid design.
