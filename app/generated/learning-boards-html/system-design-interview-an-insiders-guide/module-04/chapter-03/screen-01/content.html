NoSQL: Column‑Family and Graph Databases

Understand when to adopt a wide-column store for scale and a graph database for connected data, and how to model both effectively.

In this lesson

Walk through column-family data models, Cassandra examples for time-series data, then switch to graph concepts and Cypher examples. Finish by mapping a recommendation system to both paradigms.

Coming up

Practical design steps, common mistakes to avoid, and two hands-on schema sketches you can adapt during interviews.
