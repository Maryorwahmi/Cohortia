Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Strengths & Weaknesses
Column‑Family — Strengths
  • High write throughput and horizontal scalability.
  • Efficient queries on subsets of columns across many rows.
  • Flexible, sparse schema reduces migration overhead.
Weaknesses
Not optimized for multi-hop traversals and complex joins; requires query-driven denormalization.
Graph — Strengths
  • Direct modeling of relationships with efficient traversals.
  • Expressive pattern matching for recommendations and fraud detection.
Weaknesses
Not primarily designed for extremely high-volume append-only workloads or massive single-node property blobs; consider hybrid approaches when necessary.
Key Takeaway

State the access pattern and justify your choice by describing how the chosen system serves that pattern efficiently.
