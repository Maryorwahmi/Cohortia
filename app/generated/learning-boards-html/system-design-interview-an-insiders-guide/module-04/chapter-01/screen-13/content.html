Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts — Scaling
Vertical Scaling
Increase server resources; simple but limited and becomes costly at scale.
Read Replicas
Copies for read scale; monitor lag and retain primary for writes.
Sharding
Horizontal partitioning for distributed scale; increases operational complexity and cross-shard concerns.
Final thought
Always connect the scaling pattern you propose to monitoring, failover, and a migration strategy so the interviewer trusts your operational plan.
