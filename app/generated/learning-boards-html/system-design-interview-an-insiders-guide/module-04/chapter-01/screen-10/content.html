Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Sharding
Why shard and how
Shard to distribute both reads and writes when a single primary cannot keep up. Choose a sharding key aligned with access patterns.
Design rules
Pick a key that spreads load and minimizes cross-shard operations, and plan rebalancing strategies ahead of time.
Risks
Cross-shard joins and transactions are expensive; migration and rebalancing are operationally heavy.
Example approach
If most activity is per-user, shard by user_id. This keeps user-scoped operations local to one shard and avoids many cross-shard queries.
Key Takeaway

Sharding scales horizontally but requires careful key choice, rebalancing plans, and acceptance of increased operational overhead.
