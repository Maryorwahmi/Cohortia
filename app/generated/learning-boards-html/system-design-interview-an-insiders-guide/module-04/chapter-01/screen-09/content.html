Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Read Replicas
Replica architecture
Primary accepts writes; replicas receive propagated changes and serve reads to reduce load on the primary.
Benefits
Higher read throughput, safer reporting, and read isolation from transactional load.
Risks
Replication lag and eventual consistency can surface stale reads; writes still bottleneck on primary.
Operational notes
  • Monitor replication lag and promote replicas when necessary.
  • Route read-heavy queries and reporting to replicas to reduce primary load.
Example scenario
A product catalog with heavy browsing traffic can read from replicas, while checkout remains single-writer on primary to preserve transaction safety.
Key Takeaway

Read replicas scale reads and reduce primary load, but you must handle replication lag and preserve write correctness on a primary node.
