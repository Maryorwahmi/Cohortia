Rebalancing steps
Identify imbalance, copy data in batches, verify, and switch routing while throttling to preserve performance.
  1. Detect imbalanced shards via metrics.
  2. Plan migration and reserve capacity on destinations.
  3. Copy in small batches, replay concurrent writes, and verify checksums.
  4. Switch routing and decommission or repurpose original data.
Operational advice

Always monitor destination load and add throttles. Keep a rollback path and test the whole process before production runs.

Risks & mitigations
Risk: destination overload
Mitigate with throttles and capacity planning; do not move too many shards at once.
Risk: write conflicts
Use changelogs or per-row versioning to replay concurrent writes during migration.
Verification
Verify with checksums and sample queries before updating routing metadata.
