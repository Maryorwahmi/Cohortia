Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Cross-Shard Queries
What makes them expensive
Fan-out to multiple shards increases latency and coordination cost for aggregation or joins.
  • Parallel requests increase resource usage and network traffic.
  • Aggregation and merging add CPU cost on the coordinator.
  • Ordering and pagination across shards are non-trivial.
Design principle

Prefer queries that hit a single shard. When cross-shard is necessary, design efficient aggregation strategies or maintain precomputed summaries.

Handling patterns
Global summary tables
Pre-aggregate across shards and store summaries for fast reads.
Parallel fan-out with streaming
Send parallel queries and stream partial results, merging as they arrive to reduce tail latency.
Global index or directory
Maintain an index that points to shard locations for frequently accessed cross-shard lookups.
Misconception
Assuming cross-shard queries are always rare. Measure and design the common case for efficiency.
