Scenario recap
Multi-tenant SaaS: tenant_id, user, projects, documents. Most queries are tenant-scoped; some tenants are huge; occasional global scans required.
Suggested key
tenant_id — keeps tenant-scoped queries local to a shard and simplifies access control and backups.
Suggested strategy
Directory-based sharding for flexibility: big tenants can be assigned dedicated shards and moved with minimal app changes.
Deliverable

Prepare a two-paragraph justification: key choice and strategy with two mitigations for large tenants and global scans.
