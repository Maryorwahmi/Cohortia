Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Sharding
Horizontal partitioning of rows across servers for scale and isolation.
Sharding key
Key that maps rows to shards; must be stable and high-cardinality.
Hash sharding
Even distribution but rebalancing and range scans are challenging.
Summary
Match strategy to workload, plan for rebalancing and cross-shard needs, and treat the sharding key as a long-term commitment.
