Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Practice Skills
Skills to demonstrate
Pick an effective sharding key
High cardinality and immutability reduce rebalancing and hotspots.
Defend a sharding strategy
Explain why hash, range, or directory is chosen for the workload pattern.
Propose operational mitigations
Describe caching, online rebalancing windows, and compensating actions for cross-shard work.
Interview rehearsal
Use two-minute explanations: state the constraint, propose a key and strategy, list top two trade-offs, and finish with mitigations.
Example prompt
Design sharding for a multi-tenant SaaS. Propose a key and strategy; explain cross-tenant admin scans.
Mnemonic
Key → Strategy → Trade-offs → Ops — follow this order to be clear and structured.
