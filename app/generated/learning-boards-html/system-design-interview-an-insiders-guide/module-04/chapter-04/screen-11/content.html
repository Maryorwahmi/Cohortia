Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Distributed Transactions
Why 2PC is costly
Two-phase commit blocks participants and adds multiple network rounds, increasing latency and risk of blocking on failures.
Problems
Latency, blocking, and operational complexity under network partitions or slow nodes.
When it's unavoidable
Critical financial operations may require strict coordination; plan for high availability and careful timeout policies.
Alternatives
Saga pattern
Break a workflow into local transactions with compensating actions to undo partial progress on failure.
Eventual consistency
Accept temporary divergence and converge asynchronously; suitable for social feeds and analytics.
Idempotency
Design operations to be safe on retries to simplify recovery and reduce risk of duplication.
Interview prompt
When asked about cross-shard transactions, name trade-offs and propose one alternative that keeps the business guarantees you need.
