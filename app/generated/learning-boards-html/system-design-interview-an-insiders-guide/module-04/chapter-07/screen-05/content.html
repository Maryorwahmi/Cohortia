Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Data Warehouse
Definition and Properties
  • Subject-Oriented: Grouped around business domains like sales or customers.
  • Integrated: Data from multiple sources transformed to consistent formats.
  • Time-Variant: Preserves history for trend and cohort analysis.
  • Non-Volatile: Data is appended, not overwritten, enabling reproducible reports.
Concrete Example
Load daily order snapshots into a fact table and maintain product and region dimensions for reporting monthly sales trends.
Modern Warehouse Features
Columnar Storage: Efficient for aggregates and selective column scans.
MPP: Distributes queries across nodes for parallel execution.
Compute/Storage Separation: Scale compute independently of stored data for cost control.
Key Takeaway
Warehouses provide curated data with predictable schemas and performance for business intelligence.
