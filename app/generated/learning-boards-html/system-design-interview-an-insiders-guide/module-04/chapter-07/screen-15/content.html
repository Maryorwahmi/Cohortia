Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Key Concepts
OLTP
Systems optimized for many short, consistent transactions.
OLAP
Analytical systems optimized for complex queries and aggregations.
Data Warehouse
Centralized, curated repository for reliable reporting.
Tip: When asked to design analytics, first classify your workload — real-time, ad-hoc exploration, or scheduled reporting — then choose lake, warehouse, or both accordingly.
