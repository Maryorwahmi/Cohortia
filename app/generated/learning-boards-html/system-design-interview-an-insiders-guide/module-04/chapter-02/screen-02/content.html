Learning Objectives

Progress from conceptual trade-offs to practical modeling in both key-value and document systems.

01
Differentiate SQL and NoSQL
Explain how schema rigidity and ACID behaviors affect scaling and evolution. You will practice mapping an application to a suitable model.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand the CAP theorem
Define Consistency, Availability, Partition tolerance and explain why partitions force a trade-off with practical examples.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify Key-Value characteristics
Describe when a key-value store is appropriate and what mistakes to avoid, such as attempting complex queries on opaque values.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Explain Document DB structure
Show how semi-structured documents model nested data, when to embed versus reference, and the cost of denormalization.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
