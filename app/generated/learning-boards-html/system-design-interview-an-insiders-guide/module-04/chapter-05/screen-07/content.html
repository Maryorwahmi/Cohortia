Strong Consistency

Reads always return the most recent committed write.

How it's achieved
  • Consensus protocols (Raft, Paxos) or synchronous replication.
  • Blocking writes/reads until replicas acknowledge can be required.
When to use
Financial transactions or inventory updates that must not oversell.
Trade‑offs
Higher latency and potential unavailability under partitions are the cost of immediate correctness. Document the system’s behavior so consumers understand availability expectations.
Takeaway
Choose strong consistency when correctness outranks latency and you can accept availability impacts under partition.
