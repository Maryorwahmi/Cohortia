Eventual Consistency

Replicas will converge over time; reads may be temporarily stale.

Use cases
  • Social feeds, recommendations, sensor telemetry.
  • High throughput systems where availability is key.
Design note
Plan for stale reads: user interfaces and business logic should tolerate or reconcile temporary differences.
Operational considerations
Measure and set expectations for convergence windows. Use conflict resolution strategies and make them explicit in APIs when necessary.
Takeaway
Eventual consistency enables high availability but requires application awareness of transient staleness.
