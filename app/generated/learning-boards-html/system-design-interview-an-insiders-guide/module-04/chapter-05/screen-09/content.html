Read‑Your‑Writes & Monotonic Reads

Guarantees focused on the experience of a single client session.

Read‑Your‑Writes
Guarantees a client sees its own writes immediately. Implement with sticky routing to the last‑write replica or include version tokens in reads.
Example
After updating your profile, route your subsequent reads to the replica that handled your write so you see the change immediately.
Monotonic Reads
Once a client observes a newer value, later reads will not return older versions, preventing confusing regressions during a session.
Design tip
Combine these guarantees selectively to improve UX for critical flows without imposing global consistency costs.
