How we'll apply these objectives

Examples and exercises ground theory in practical trade‑offs and observable user behavior.

User Profile Updates

Expect immediate read‑your‑writes for the user who edited their profile; other users can tolerate small delays.

News Feed

Prioritize availability and read scale; eventual convergence and slight ordering variance are acceptable.

Hands‑On
Design replication and consistency for both features and justify your choices, including failover behavior.
Common Mistakes
Confusing eventual with never consistent, or over‑using synchronous replication where it isn’t needed.
