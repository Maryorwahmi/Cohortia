Quorum Consistency

Control consistency by choosing read and write quorums (R and W) against N replicas.

Mechanics
Select R and W. If R + W > N, read operations will intersect write acknowledgements, increasing consistency.
Example
With N=3, R=2 and W=2 produce overlap, giving stronger read guarantees than R=1,W=1.
Trade‑offs
  • Lower W: faster writes, weaker consistency.
  • Higher W: stronger consistency, higher latency.
Takeaway
Quorum tuning gives a precise way to balance performance and correctness for specific operations.
