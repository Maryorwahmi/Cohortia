Failover and Operational Safety

How systems recover write capability and the safety choices that must be made.

Key procedures
  • Detect leader failure and elect a replacement.
  • Reconcile unreplicated writes or accept documented loss windows.
  • Monitor replication lag and surface it to clients or dashboards.
Testing and documentation
Run fault injection tests under load to validate failover behavior. Document the worst‑case data loss window and client expectations during failover for consumers and SRE runbooks.
Pro tip
Make idempotency and retries first‑class in APIs so transient failures and failover are less destructive.
