Scaling Data Ingestion (Kafka)

Steps to scale ingestion while keeping data durable and ordered where required.

1. Partition for throughput
Choose a partition key that balances load. Increase partition count to allow more consumers to parallelize processing.
2. Add brokers
Add broker nodes to host partitions and spread I/O. Ensure replication is configured so leaders have followers on other brokers.
3. Configure replication and acks
Set a replication factor to tolerate broker failures and use producer acknowledgement settings that prioritize durability.
4. Monitor for hot partitions
Detect uneven key distribution and mitigate with smarter partitioning or rekeying strategies to avoid a single bottleneck.
5. Plan rebalancing and failover
Test broker failover, replica catch-up, and consumer group rebalancing to avoid long pauses during maintenance or failures.
Key Takeaway

Partitioning plus replication is the foundation of scalable, durable ingestion. Monitor and plan for rebalances to protect ordering guarantees.
