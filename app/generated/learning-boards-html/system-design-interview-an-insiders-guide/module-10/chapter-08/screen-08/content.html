Scaling Storage and Sharding

Distribute data across nodes, choose shard keys that match queries, and replicate to tolerate node failures.

Sharding patterns
  • Time-based: partition by date for efficient range queries and retention.
  • Key-based: partition by user_id or other hot keys for per-entity access.
  • Hybrid: combine time and key for high-cardinality workloads.
Replication & isolation
Replicate shards across availability zones to protect against regional failures. Isolation prevents one shard’s failure from impacting others and simplifies maintenance windows.
Key Takeaway

Align shard keys with dominant queries and replicate shards across zones to balance performance, durability, and availability.
