Reliability: HA, Durability, and DR

Plan redundancy, automatic failover, and cross-region backups so services remain available and data remains safe.

High Availability
Replicate critical components and automate leader election and failover so services continue during node failures.
Durability
Ensure committed data is written to durable, replicated storage. Use replication and persistent logs for message systems.
Disaster Recovery
Replicate data across regions, define RPO and RTO, and practice restores so you can recover from regional outages.
Practice & Monitoring
Regularly test failovers, validate replica health, and monitor replication lag and restore processes to ensure reliability promises hold.
