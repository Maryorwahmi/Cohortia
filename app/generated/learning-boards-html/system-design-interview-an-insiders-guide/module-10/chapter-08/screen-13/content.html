Designing for HA and Fault Tolerance — Guided Task

Translate principles into specific mechanisms per component and define testable validation steps.

Component checklist
  • Kafka: partitions + replication + producer acks; test broker failover and replica catch-up.
  • Flink: checkpointing to durable storage; test job restart and state restore timing.
  • ClickHouse: shard replication and cross-zone placement; test read failover and consistency after recovery.
  • API: load balancer, circuit breakers, bulkheads; test graceful degradation and priority routing.
Durability & CAP
Ensure Kafka writes to durable log and Flink checkpoints are stored off-cluster. For ClickHouse, choose AP for dashboards or CP for critical tables and document the operational impact.
Key Takeaway

A design is complete only when it includes a testable validation plan: automated failover tests and restore drills make reliability real.
