CAP Theorem for Real-Time Analytics

Understand which guarantees you can and cannot provide when network partitions occur in a distributed analytics system.

AP: Availability + Partition Tolerance
Prefer availability for dashboards and monitoring where slightly stale data is acceptable. Design for bounded staleness and document expectations.
CP: Consistency + Partition Tolerance
Choose CP for critical counters or transactional work where stale reads are unacceptable, accepting potential unavailability during partitions.
Interview framing
Explain the user impact of your choice, and propose mitigations like quorum reads, read repair, or hybrid design for mixed requirements.
Concrete example
A live analytics dashboard usually accepts AP. A billing ledger requires CP; if partitioned, the ledger will block writes until consistency restores.
