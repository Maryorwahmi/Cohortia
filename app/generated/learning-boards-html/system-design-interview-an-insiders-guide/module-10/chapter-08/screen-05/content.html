Horizontal vs. Vertical Scaling

Understand the operational trade-offs and choose the right tool for each pipeline component.

Horizontal (Scale Out)
  • Add nodes to distribute load across machines and network.
  • Improves fault isolation and capacity without a single big-host cost.
  • Requires partitioning and coordination to balance load and state.
Example
Add Kafka partitions and consumer instances to increase ingestion throughput and processing parallelism.
Vertical (Scale Up)
  • Increase CPU, RAM, or disk on a single machine.
  • Simpler to implement for non-distributable services but limited in headroom.
  • Raises cost per additional unit and increases single-point risk.
Example
Upgrade a coordination service or a single-threaded API to lower latency without sharding logic.
