Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Hands-on Activity
Task summary

Design a storage plan for page views, clicks, and sessions to support the listed interactive queries while controlling cost and latency.

Example choice

Primary DB: ClickHouse for aggregations and sub-second group-bys. Use denormalized events table with ORDER BY (timestamp, page_url). Maintain hyperUnique or approximate distinct counters for unique user counts.

Key Takeaway

Design from queries: choose primary engine, set partitioning and indexes accordingly, and define retention and downsampling policies.

Activity steps
  1. Pick the primary storage engine and justify it based on queries and ingestion.
  2. Sketch a simple denormalized schema for events and any aggregate tables.
  3. Choose indexing/partitioning strategies for the common queries.
  4. Decide retention: raw events for recent hot window, aggregates for longer horizons.
Reflection

Which query forced the most restrictive choice and why? That answer reveals your primary constraint: latency, cost, or ingestion rate.
