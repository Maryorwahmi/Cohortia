Five-step design process
  1. List the most frequent queries and SLAs for latency.
  2. Identify filter, group-by, and metric fields for each query.
  3. Choose primary key and partition strategy (time-first for TSDBs).
  4. Denormalize or pre-aggregate where joins would be costly.
  5. Define retention and downsampling to manage storage and performance.
Concrete scenario

For "top 10 pages in last hour": index page_url and time, partition recent data by minute or hour, and consider maintaining pre-aggregated counters for the last few minutes to serve interactive dashboards.

Common mistakes
  • Optimizing for writes alone and forgetting query patterns.
  • Relying on joins at query time for highly aggregated dashboards.
  • Not planning retention and letting storage costs grow unchecked.
Key Takeaway

Design from queries outward: choosing the right partitions and denormalization will often determine whether your dashboards are interactive.
