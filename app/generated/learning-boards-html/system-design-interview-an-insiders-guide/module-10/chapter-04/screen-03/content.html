Design query-driven schemas

Translate the most common queries into schema decisions: denormalize where joins slow aggregations and use compound keys where range scans are frequent.

Mnemonic: queries → schema

Indexing and partition strategies

Learn when to index user IDs or timestamps, when to partition by time, and how these choices affect ingestion and query latency.

Mnemonic: index the frequent filter

07
Retention and backups

Decide how long to keep raw events versus aggregates, and ensure replication and backup strategies to prevent data loss in operational analytics.

Mnemonic: hot recent, warm aggregated, cold archived

08
Choose a primary storage technology

Be able to justify a primary choice such as Druid, ClickHouse, InfluxDB, TimescaleDB, or Elasticsearch based on ingestion, query types, and operational needs.
