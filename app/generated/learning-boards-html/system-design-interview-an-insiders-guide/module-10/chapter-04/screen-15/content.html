Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › What to Remember
Index by filter
Prioritize indexes for fields used in WHERE clauses and group-bys.
Partition by time
Use time-first partitions for windowed queries and efficient retention.
Denormalize & Aggregate
Avoid joins in OLAP paths and pre-aggregate for interactive dashboards.
Actionable checklist

When asked to design storage, state your primary queries, propose a matching engine, outline schema and indexing, and include retention and monitoring plans.
