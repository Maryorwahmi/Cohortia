Key ConceptsPart 2
Exactly-once

Guarantee that each event affects results exactly once through checkpoints and transactional sinks.

Watermarks

Signals that event-time has progressed and windows can be closed, allowing controlled handling of late data.

Monitoring signals

Watch latency, checkpoint durations, and backpressure as primary indicators of pipeline health.

Final takeaway

Choose the framework that matches your correctness and latency goals, and pair it with checkpointing, watermarks, and good observability practices.
