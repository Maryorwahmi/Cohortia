Best PracticesOperations & Monitoring
Avoid late-data blindness
Configure watermarks and allowed lateness; route very-late events to a late-data sink for reconciliation.
Bound your state
Use TTLs, compaction, or approximate structures to limit memory usage for long-running aggregations.
Design idempotent sinks
Ensure external writes tolerate retries to avoid duplicate side effects on recovery.
Monitor key signals
Watch processing latency, checkpoint duration, and backpressure to detect emerging problems early.
Respond to backpressure
Identify slow sinks or overloaded operators and scale or tune batching to relieve pressure.
Key takeaway
Operational design must be part of the job spec: correctness, bounded resources, and observability are inseparable.
