Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Materialized views & connection pooling
Materialized Views
Serve heavy aggregates from precomputed tables refreshed periodically or updated by streaming jobs to minimize scan costs.
  • Daily, hourly or minute-level views trade freshness for query speed.
  • Choose refresh cadence based on dashboard needs and pipeline capacity.
Connection Pooling
Reuse a pool of persistent DB connections to avoid the overhead of connection setup and protect the DB from bursting connection counts.
Operational note
Tune pool size by observing latency and DB concurrency; too many connections can hurt performance more than too few.
