Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Authentication & Authorization
Authentication
Validate identity with API keys, OAuth tokens, or JWTs and require HTTPS to protect tokens in transit.
Implementation snippet
Check token validity at the gateway and reject unauthorized requests with informative but non-sensitive errors.
Authorization & Rate Limits
Map authenticated identities to roles and permissions. Apply per-role limits and per-client rate limiting to protect the system.
  • RBAC ensures teams see only permitted metrics.
  • Rate limits prevent noisy clients from degrading the service.
  • Audit logs help detect misuse or credential leaks.
