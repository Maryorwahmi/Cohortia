Aggregation and Materialized Views

Why we summarize events, when to pre-compute results, and how to keep pre-computed views fresh in a real-time pipeline.

In this lesson: necessity, strategies, updates, challenges
Raw events
High volume, fine-grained, slow to query directly.
Aggregation
Summaries like counts and uniques that reduce volume.
Materialized views
Pre-computed tables for fast, repeatable queries.

Concept funnel
Raw events
Individual clicks, logins, or sales messages every second.
Aggregation
Counts, sums, and distinct approximations that shrink data volume.
Materialized view
A persisted table holding pre-computed results for fast reads.
Key Takeaway

Choose where aggregation happens to balance freshness, cost, and latency. Materialized views make repeated reads cheap.
