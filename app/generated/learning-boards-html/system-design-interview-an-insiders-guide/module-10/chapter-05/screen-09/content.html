Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Late-arriving data
Concepts to know
Late-arriving events must be considered when maintaining aggregates. Use watermarks to bound the expected lateness and configure allowed lateness to accept late events for a window.
Watermarks
Estimate a boundary in event time beyond which events are expected to be rare.
Allowed lateness
A configured delay window during which late events still update a closed window's aggregate.
Operational trade-off
Longer allowed lateness improves accuracy but increases reprocessing and update churn on the materialized view.
Key Takeaway

Plan for late data: pick watermarks and allowed lateness consistent with your acceptable historical accuracy and update cost.

Scenario

If a login event for 10:01 arrives at 10:10 due to upstream delay and your allowed lateness is 15 minutes, the processor will accept the event, update the (date, country) state, and emit an upsert to correct the materialized view. If allowed lateness had been 5 minutes, the event would be treated as late beyond the window and might be stored separately for audit or ignored for that window's aggregate.
