Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Key Concepts
Aggregation
Summarize events into counts, sums, or distinct estimates for fast queries.
Materialized View
A stored table of pre-computed results for low-latency reads.
Upsert & idempotency
Write computed aggregates atomically so retries do not corrupt state.
Summary takeaway

Balance freshness, cost, and accuracy: pre-aggregate for fast dashboards, use on-the-fly for ad-hoc analysis, and choose approximation when exactness is infeasible.
