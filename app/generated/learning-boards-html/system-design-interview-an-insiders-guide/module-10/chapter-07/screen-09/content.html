Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Performance Optimization Patterns
Patterns
  • Minimize data transfer: fetch only required fields and pages.
  • Client-side aggregation for small results to reduce queries.
  • Efficient rendering via well-tested chart libraries.
  • Pre-compute aggregates or use materialized views for heavy queries.
Quick profiling checklist
Measure three things: API latency, payload size, and client render time. Prioritize the largest contributor to overall delay.
Concrete example
A slow table can often be fixed by replacing full downloads with server-side pagination and virtual scrolling, reducing payloads by orders of magnitude.
Key Takeaway

Profile first. Then reduce transfer and compute with pre-aggregation, caching, and targeted client-side work.
