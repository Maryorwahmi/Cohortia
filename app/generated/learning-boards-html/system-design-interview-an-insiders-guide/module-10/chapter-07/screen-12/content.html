Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Pre-computation & Materialized Views
What to pre-compute
  • Fixed-window counts (1s, 10s, 1m) for high-frequency metrics.
  • Top-N aggregations for categorical rollups (top error codes).
  • Per-host or per-broker aggregates for quick status checks.
Lifecycle
Recompute frequently for recent windows, downsample for history, and expose freshness metadata to users.
Handling late-arriving data
Expect eventual consistency. Materialized views should be updated incrementally and dashboards should indicate the freshness window so users understand potential small re-computations.
Key Takeaway

Use pre-computed aggregates for frequent queries and clearly communicate freshness; pre-computation reduces query cost and improves responsiveness.
