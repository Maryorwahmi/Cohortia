Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Popular Tools
Grafana
Time-series monitoring, templating, and alerting; great for operational dashboards tied to metrics stores.
Apache Superset
User-friendly data exploration for SQL databases; strong for BI and ad-hoc analysis without coding.
Kibana
Visualization for Elasticsearch; ideal for log analysis and operational search of semi-structured data.
Custom UI
Maximum flexibility for branding and custom workflows, at the cost of engineering effort and maintenance.
Decision factors
Consider data sources, alerting requirements, self-service needs, and maintenance burden. Grafana for ops, Superset for BI, Kibana for logs, custom for special UX.
