Why UX and safety matter
A well-performing dashboard is only useful if users trust it and can act. Time zone handling, clear labels, and access control prevent mistakes and data exposure.
Quick checklist
  • Store data in UTC; convert for display.
  • Apply role-based access control for sensitive panels.
  • Review usage and tune slow panels regularly.
