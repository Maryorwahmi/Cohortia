Monitoring, Alerting, and Optimization
Operational practices to keep real-time analytics fast, reliable, and cost-effective
In this lesson

Learn the essential metrics for each pipeline layer, how to collect logs and traces, design alert rules and severity, and apply performance and cost optimizations to a live Kafka→Flink→ClickHouse workflow.

Why it matters

A real-time pipeline without observability will degrade silently. Strong monitoring and tuned alerts let you catch root causes quickly and avoid expensive over-provisioning.
