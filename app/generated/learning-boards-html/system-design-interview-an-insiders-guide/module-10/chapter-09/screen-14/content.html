Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Key Concepts
Monitoring
Continuous collection of metrics, logs and traces to observe system health.
Alerting
Actionable notifications with severity, routing, and runbooks to resolve incidents quickly.
Optimization
Continuous tuning of parallelism, queries, retention and scaling for performance and cost.
Final takeaway

Good observability is a combination of metrics, structured logs and traces, backed by actionable alerts and continuous optimization.
