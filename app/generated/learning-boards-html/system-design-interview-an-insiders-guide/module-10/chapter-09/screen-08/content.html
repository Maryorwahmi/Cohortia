Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Alerting
Design steps
  1. Choose the right metric and aggregation for the condition.
  2. Set a threshold and a sustained window to avoid flapping.
  3. Assign severity and notification channels.
  4. Attach a runbook and an owner for escalation.
Example alert
Metric: kafka_consumer_lag_user_activity — Threshold: > 10000 messages for 5 minutes — Severity: Critical — Notification: PagerDuty, Slack #on-call. Runbook: scale consumer parallelism, check downstream checkpoints.
Avoid
Do not alert on transient spikes or on metrics with no clear remediation steps; that creates noise and fatigue.
Key Takeaway

Make alerts specific, actionable, and verifiable. Test the notification path and document runbooks to reduce mean time to resolution.
