Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Monitoring
Definition

Monitoring is continuous collection and visualization of metrics, logs and traces across all system components so you can see behavior and detect anomalies.

Common misconception

Metrics alone don't give root cause — they expose symptoms that must be correlated with logs and traces.

Why it matters

Early detection protects SLAs and avoids costly firefighting. Observability also guides optimization and capacity planning.

Practical example

Imagine Kafka producer throughput drops while consumer lag increases and Flink latency climbs. Monitoring those three signals together points to an ingestion bottleneck that needs debugging.

Metric
kafka_consumer_lag_user_activity
Log
JSON structured logs with request_id and job_id
Key Takeaway
Instrument components with labeled metrics, centralize logs, and enable traces so symptoms point to causes.
