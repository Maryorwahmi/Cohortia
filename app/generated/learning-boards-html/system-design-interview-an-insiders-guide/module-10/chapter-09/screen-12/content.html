Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Common Mistakes
Top mistakes
  • Alerting on symptoms (e.g., CPU) instead of root causes.
  • No runbooks or unclear on-call rotations.
  • Single point of failure in monitoring infrastructure.
  • Indefinite raw data retention increasing cost.
Safety notes

Make monitoring highly available, distribute alert delivery, and test your notification channels regularly. Keep a compact runbook for each critical alert.

Common misconception

Assuming alerts alone fix problems. Alerts are the start of the response; runbooks and on-call ownership do the rest.

Key Takeaway

Define cause-based alerts, document runbooks, make observability redundant, and manage retention to avoid cost and complexity drift.
