Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Cost Management
Autoscaling
Autoscale stateless services to match demand; monitor scaling events to avoid thrashing and scale gracefully for stateful services.
Retention policies
Purge raw events after required retention. Keep aggregated materialized views for long-term analytics to save space.
Storage tiers
Use cheaper tiers for cold data and faster tiers for hot query paths. Move older partitions to cheaper tiers where possible.
Audit & procurement

Regular audits help find idle resources and opportunities for reserved or spot instances. Measure trade-offs between performance and cost before making long-term commitments.

Key Takeaway

Control costs by combining retention, tiering, autoscaling, and periodic architecture reviews; avoid indefinite raw data retention unless required.
