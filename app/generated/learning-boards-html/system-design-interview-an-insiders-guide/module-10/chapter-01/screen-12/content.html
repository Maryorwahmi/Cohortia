Trade-off

Cost vs Latency

Explain how reducing latency increases compute, storage, and operational costs, and propose staged upgrades as a pragmatic path.

Example
A low-latency fraud pipeline needs dedicated stream processors and low-latency stores; the operational overhead grows compared to a micro-batch pipeline.
Staged plan
Start with near real-time micro-batches, validate correctness and cost, then migrate to true streaming if metrics require lower latency.
Misconception
Assuming the lowest latency is always the best choice. Often it’s better to meet requirements at lower complexity and iterate.
Key Takeaway

Recommend pragmatic solutions tied to metrics and outline clear upgrade paths rather than promising extremes up front.
