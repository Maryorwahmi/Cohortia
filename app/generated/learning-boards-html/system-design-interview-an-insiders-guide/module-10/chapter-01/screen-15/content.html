Real-time Analytics
Processing events as they arrive to enable immediate actions, defined by freshness targets.
Functional Requirements
Events, transformations, windows, and queries define the system’s capabilities.
Non-functional Requirements
Latency, throughput, retention, consistency, availability, and durability shape architecture and cost.
Summary
