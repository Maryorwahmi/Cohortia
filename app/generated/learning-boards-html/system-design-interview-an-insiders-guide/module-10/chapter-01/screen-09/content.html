Definition

Data Volume and Retention

Decide how long to keep raw events versus aggregates. This determines storage costs, reprocessing ability, and query flexibility.

Raw retention
Supports reprocessing and audit, but increases storage cost and ingest complexity.
Aggregates
Keep long-term summaries for reporting and cheap queries while evicting raw events after a shorter window.
Tiering
Plan hot, warm, and cold storage based on query needs and retention policy.
Example
Keep raw events for 7–30 days for troubleshooting; store 1–3 year aggregates in columnar storage for reporting.
Misconception

Assuming long raw retention is always necessary. Often short raw retention plus long aggregated retention offers the best balance of cost and usefulness.
