Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Consistency & SLA
Definition

Consistency, Availability, Durability

Decide where eventual consistency suffices and where you need stronger guarantees. Describe acceptable windows for inconsistency.

Example
Dashboards can tolerate small inconsistencies; fraud alerts cannot. State your tolerance explicitly in interviews.
Design impact
Stronger consistency may require synchronous writes or coordination and can increase latency; eventual consistency favors higher availability and lower coordination cost.
Misconception
Treating consistency as a purely technical choice rather than a requirement. Always tie consistency needs to user-impact scenarios.
