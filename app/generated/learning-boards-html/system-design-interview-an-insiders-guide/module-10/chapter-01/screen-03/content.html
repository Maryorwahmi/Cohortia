Practical Interview Moves

Apply → Explain → Trade-off
Ask
Clarify freshness, expected event rates, retention, and query types. Convert answers into metric targets you can design to.
Map
Map requirements to patterns: streaming with micro-batches, streaming with windowed aggregations, or specialized low-latency engines.
Explain
Describe costs and operational complexity for each choice, and offer a pragmatic fallback if traffic grows or latency needs relax.
Pro tip

When in doubt, pick the simplest design that satisfies the tightest metric the stakeholder gives you, then describe how to scale to stricter targets if needed.
