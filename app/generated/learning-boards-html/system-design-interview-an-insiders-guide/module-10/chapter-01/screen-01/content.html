Understanding Real-time Analytics Requirements

Learn to translate vague interview prompts into concrete, testable requirements that guide architecture choices and trade-offs.

In this lesson
Define real-time, distinguish batch, and prioritize non-functional needs.
Coming up
Elicit requirements with focused questions and examples.
Why it matters
Right requirements avoid over-engineering and costly mistakes in interviews and production.
Explore Key Topics
Real-time vs Batch
Contrast latency and processing cadence to pick the right approach.
Non-functional Needs
Prioritize latency, throughput, and retention with concrete metrics.
Trade-offs
Understand cost vs performance to propose realistic architectures.
Visual Summary
Cards show scope, constraints, and outcomes for a design interview.
Outcome
You can elicit precise requirements and justify trade-offs effectively.
Common pitfall
Assuming "real-time" without asking for freshness and throughput metrics.
Quick prompt
When asked to design a system, ask: "What is acceptable freshness? How many events per second? How long keep raw events?"
