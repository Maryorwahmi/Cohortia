Process

Ask focused questions

Use the following checklist to convert vague statements into concrete requirements you can design to.

1. Use Case
What decisions are made from this data and who uses them?
2. Freshness
Acceptable delay: sub-second, seconds, or longer?
3. Throughput
Typical and peak events per second; growth expectations?
4. Retention
How long to keep raw events vs aggregated summaries?
5. Queries
Point lookups, windowed counts, joins, or ad-hoc analysis?
6. Consistency & SLA
Is eventual consistency acceptable? What availability target matters?
Example

If a product team says "real-time recommendations", ask: is a 1–2 second delay acceptable? How many users at peak? Their answers change whether you pick a stream processor with low-latency state or a micro-batch job.
