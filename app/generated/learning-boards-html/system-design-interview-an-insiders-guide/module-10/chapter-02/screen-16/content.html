Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Key Concepts — Quick Reference
Producer
Sends events; keys influence partitioning and ordering.
Consumer
Reads records and commits offsets so processing progress is recoverable.
Topic
Named feed of records; partition into logs for parallelism and order.
Schema Registry
Stores schemas to validate messages and manage evolution safely.
Dead-Letter Queue
Quarantines messages that repeatedly fail validation or processing.
Backpressure
When producers outpace consumers; solve with buffering, rate limits, and scaling.
Final Note

Keep these definitions ready and use them to justify design trade-offs during interviews or architecture reviews.
