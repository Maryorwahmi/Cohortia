Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Managed vs Self-Hosted
Managed Streaming
Pros: less ops, faster time to market, built-in scaling; Cons: cost, less control, potential vendor lock-in.
Reduce ops
Cloud provider handles provisioning and basic durability.
Vendor constraints
Limited control over fine-grained tuning and placement.
Self-Hosted Platform
Pros: tuning, cost optimization at scale; Cons: requires ops expertise and staffing for reliable operations.
Full control
Customize replication, storage, and network placement for performance.
Operational cost
Ongoing maintenance, upgrades, and incident response require staff time.
Reflection

Choose based on your team's operational capacity, latency needs, and long-term cost profile.
