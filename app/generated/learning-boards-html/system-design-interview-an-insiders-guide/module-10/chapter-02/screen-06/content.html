Why use a streaming platform?
  • Durable buffer to absorb producer-consumer rate differences.
  • Parallelism via partitions for high throughput processing.
  • Multiple independent consumers can read the same stream.
  • Retention for reprocessing historical data without replaying producers.
Design checks

Decide partition count, replication, and retention to meet your throughput, durability, and replay needs.

Example: partitioning for scale
A topic partitioned by user_id lets consumers process different user-event subsets in parallel. If ordering is required per user, choose partition keys carefully to preserve order without sacrificing parallelism.
Misconception
More partitions always equal better throughput. In reality, partitions improve parallelism but add management complexity and can affect ordering and maintainability.
Key Takeaway

Streaming platforms decouple systems and provide configurable durability and parallelism; choose partitioning and replication to satisfy your guarantees.
