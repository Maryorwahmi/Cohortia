Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Backpressure, Buffering, and Dead-Letter Queues
Backpressure strategies
Buffering, rate limiting, consumer autoscaling, and bounded retries with backoff are primary techniques to handle load mismatch.
Buffer in queue
Let the streaming platform hold messages durably until consumers catch up.
Bounded retries
Retry with exponential backoff and a maximum retry count to avoid blocking resources.
Dead-letter queue
Quarantine failed messages for offline inspection and reprocessing.
Scenario
An enrichment consumer falls behind during peak load. The topic retains messages while new consumer instances scale up. Messages that fail schema checks three times move to a DLQ for a developer to inspect and re-publish after fixes.
Key Takeaway

Avoid infinite in-place retries. Use bounded retries and DLQs to maintain throughput while preserving failed records for diagnosis.
