Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Designing for Peak Traffic and Scaling
Practical steps
  1. Estimate peak events per second, include safety margin.
  2. Measure per-partition or per-shard throughput and compute needed count.
  3. Enable batching and compression to cut network overhead.
  4. Set replication for durability and retention for replayability.
  5. Monitor metrics and scale shards or consumers proactively.
Example calculation
If one partition sustains 5,000 events per second with batching, plan at least 20 partitions for 100,000 events per second and add headroom for spikes and failures.
Monitoring focus

Monitor partition write latency, broker queue sizes, producer retry rates, and consumer lag to detect capacity issues early.
