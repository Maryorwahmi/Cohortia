Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Security and Data Protection
Transport & Storage
Use TLS for transport encryption and enable encryption at rest for broker storage to protect data throughout the ingestion path.
TLS in transit
Protects messages between producers, brokers, and consumers.
Encryption at rest
Guards stored messages from unauthorized disk access.
Access & Auditing
Restrict who can publish or consume topics and audit access to detect misuse. Secure DLQs and registries so only authorized operators can inspect quarantined data.
Key Takeaway

Secure both the channel and the stored data, and protect tooling around schema and quarantined records to meet privacy and compliance needs.
