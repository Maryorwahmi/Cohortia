Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Key Concepts
Data Ingestion Layer
Entry point that buffers and validates events before processing.
Message Queue
Durable buffer with topics and partitions for scalability and replay.
Schema Enforcement
Use registries to validate shapes and manage evolution safely.
Backpressure & DLQ
Bounded retries and quarantine keep the pipeline moving and preserve failures for inspection.
Durability
Persist to disk and replicate to survive broker failures and support replay.
Security
Encrypt in transit and at rest, and secure registries and DLQs to protect sensitive data.
Summary Takeaway

Design ingestion to absorb bursts, validate early, and quarantine bad data so downstream systems remain reliable and auditable.
