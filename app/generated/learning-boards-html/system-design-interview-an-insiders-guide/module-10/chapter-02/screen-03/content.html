Translate objectives into design actions
Partition Estimation
Estimate required partitions by matching expected producer throughput with per-partition write capacity and required consumer parallelism.
Mnemonic: Throughput ÷ Partition Capacity = count
Managed vs Self-hosted
Choose managed services to reduce ops overhead, or self-host for fine-grained control and cost optimization at scale.
Mnemonic: Ops cost vs control
Validation & DLQ Policy
Define rules for required fields, types, and quarantining invalid messages to a dead-letter queue for later inspection.
Mnemonic: Validate → Quarantine → Inspect
