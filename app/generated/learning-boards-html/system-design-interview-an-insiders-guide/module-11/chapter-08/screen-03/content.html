Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Learning Objectives

Operational goals

Security, moderation, and cost control for media at scale.

Access Control

Apply least privilege, bucket policies, and short-lived credentials for uploads.

Encryption

Encrypt objects at rest and enforce HTTPS for transfers to protect user content.

Moderation & Metadata

Extract EXIF, detect unsafe content, and store metadata to power UI choices.

Cost Management

Track CDN egress, storage tiers, and frequency of re-processing to optimize bills.
