Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Pitfalls & Anti-patterns
Anti-patterns
  • Serving raw assets without CDN or resizing.
  • Proxing large uploads through the app tier.
  • Synchronous processing during upload requests.
  • No retries, no dead-letter queue for failed jobs.
Corrections
  • Use CDN and processed sizes to reduce latency and cost.
  • Issue pre-signed URLs rather than proxying uploads.
  • Decouple transforms via events and queues; implement retries.
  • Use strict access policies and short-lived credentials.
