Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Key Concepts
Time-to-Live (TTL)
Automatically expires cached entries after a set duration to limit staleness.
Cache Invalidation
Remove or update cached data on edits, deletes, or relationship changes to avoid showing stale content.
Circuit Breaker
Detect downstream failures and serve cached or simpler responses to preserve latency and availability.
Final takeaway

Balance freshness and performance: use two-layer caching, tuned TTLs, selective invalidation, and operational protections to deliver fast and reliable feeds.
