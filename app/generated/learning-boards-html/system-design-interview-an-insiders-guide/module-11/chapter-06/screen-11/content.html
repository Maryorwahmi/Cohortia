Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Cold Start & Stale Feeds
Cold start tactics
  • Pre-generate feed for new users from follows and trending posts.
  • Warm cache for VIP or highly active accounts during peak times.
  • Provide a fallback default feed to avoid empty screens.
Staleness mitigation
Use short TTLs for feed IDs, explicit invalidation on relationship or deletion events, and selective updates for high-impact interactions.
Tune TTLs by observing how stale data affects engagement and backend load.
