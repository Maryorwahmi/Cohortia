Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Learning Objectives
Practical skills
  • Use ZADD and ZREVRANGE to maintain and read a per-user feed sorted set.
  • Store full post objects in a Redis hash and set an appropriate expire time to limit staleness.
  • Apply ZREM selectively to remove posts when an unfollow occurs, avoiding full regeneration.
  • Monitor hit rate and set alerts for high feed generation latency.
Safety & trade-offs
Shorter TTLs increase freshness but lower hit rates. Longer TTLs reduce DB load but risk staleness. Circuit breakers and graceful degradation keep the feed service responsive when downstream systems fail.
