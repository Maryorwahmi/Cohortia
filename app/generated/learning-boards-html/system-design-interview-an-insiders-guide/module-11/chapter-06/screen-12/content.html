Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Robustness & Degradation
Circuit Breaker
Trip on repeated downstream failures then fallback to cached feed or simpler logic to preserve latency.
Graceful Degradation
Return cached or chronologically ordered items when ranking models or databases are slow.
Cache Warming
Pre-generate feeds for critical users or times to minimize first-read latency.
Monitoring
Track cache hit/miss rate, feed generation latency, and eviction frequency. These metrics guide TTL and trimming policy decisions.
