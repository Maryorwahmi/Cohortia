Architecture Feed DB & storage
Feed store options
  • Redis sorted sets for fast reads and trimming by score.
  • Cassandra or wide-row NoSQL for high write throughput and range queries.
Best practice
Store compact post references and snippets, and keep per-feed lists capped to a practical length to bound cost.
Operational controls
  • Retention / TTLs to prevent unbounded growth.
  • Sharding by user ID to distribute writes.
  • Compaction jobs to remove stale or duplicate entries.
Key Takeaway

Choose a feed store that supports your chosen fan-out model, cap retention to bound storage, and prefer compact references over full payload duplication.
