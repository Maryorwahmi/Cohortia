Comparison Push vs Pull trade-offs
Push (Fan-out on Write)
  • Read latency: very low
  • Write amplification: high
  • Storage: high (duplicated references)
  • Best when reads dominate and follower lists are modest
Mitigate with batching, idempotency, and threshold checks.
Pull (Fan-out on Read)
  • Read latency: higher without caching
  • Write amplification: low
  • Storage: lower
  • Best when write cost must be minimized or many users are dormant
Mitigate with caches for hot authors and precomputation for active users.
Practical rule
Use a hybrid that pushes for most authors and pulls for super-followed authors to limit write storms while keeping reads fast for typical users.
