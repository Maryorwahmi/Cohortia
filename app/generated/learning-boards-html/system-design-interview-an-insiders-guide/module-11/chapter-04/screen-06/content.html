Comparison Push model pros & cons
Advantages
  • Low read latency — feeds are pre-generated.
  • Simpler retrieval logic — reads are lookups rather than joins.
  • Near real-time updates for followers.
Good for
Platforms where reads outnumber writes and follower lists are small to moderate.
Disadvantages
  • High write amplification for popular authors.
  • Storage overhead from duplicated post references.
  • Complexity when updating or deleting posts across many feeds.
Risk
Unbounded push to celebrity follower graphs leads to bottlenecks and large costs.
Design note: use follower-count checks and route large accounts to a pull store to avoid write storms.
