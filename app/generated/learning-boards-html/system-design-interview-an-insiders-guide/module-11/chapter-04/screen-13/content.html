Process Flow Operational pitfalls & monitoring
Common mistakes
  • Not thresholding super-followed accounts and causing write storms.
  • Not making fan-out idempotent, yielding duplicates on retries.
  • No caching for celebrity posts, increasing Post DB read load.
  • Unbounded retention leading to runaway storage costs.
Warning
Operational surprises are often due to rare but heavy-tailed users; plan for extremes not just medians.
Monitoring signals
  • Write amplification ratio over time.
  • Feed DB latency and error rate trends.
  • Celebrity cache hit rate and Post DB read latency.
  • Queue backlog and retry counts.
Operational action

Correlate alerts across queue backlog, DB write rates, and storage growth to identify whether push traffic or retention policies are the root cause.
