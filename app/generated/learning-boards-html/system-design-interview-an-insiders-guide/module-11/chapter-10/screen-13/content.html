Comparison

Blue/Green

Deploy entire new version to Green, test, and switch traffic. Rollback by switching back to Blue instantly.
Use when
You need quick rollback and can maintain two environments.
Comparison

Canary

Roll out to a small percentage of users and expand if metrics are healthy. Allows fine-grained validation against real traffic.
Use when
You want to detect regressions at small scale before full release.
Example rollout plan
  1. Deploy to Green, run smoke tests and synthetic transactions.
  2. Route 1% of traffic to canary users and monitor latency and error rates for 10 minutes.
  3. If metrics are stable, increase to 5% and continue. If anomalies occur, rollback automatically to the previous version.
