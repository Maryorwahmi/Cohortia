Definition

CI/CD & Immutable Infra

Pipeline artifacts and immutable images make deployments repeatable and rollbacks simple.

IaC
Manage servers and networks with version-controlled templates so environments are reproducible.
Pipeline example
  1. CI runs unit and integration tests on merge.
  2. Build produces an immutable image and artifacts.
  3. CD deploys to Green, runs smoke tests, then cuts traffic to Green.
Pro tip
Automate rollbacks based on health-check failures detected after switching traffic.
