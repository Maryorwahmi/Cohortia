Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Applied Milestones
Operational artifacts to prepare
  • Redundancy plan: replicas and zone placement for critical components.
  • Three dashboards: infra, application, and business metrics.
  • Deployment playbook: CI/CD steps and automated rollback criteria.
  • Incident template and post-mortem checklist with owners.
Interview checklist
Be ready to show:
  1. An alert rule with threshold and recipient.
  2. A rollback example and how you tested it in staging.
  3. A short post-mortem summarizing root cause and action items.
