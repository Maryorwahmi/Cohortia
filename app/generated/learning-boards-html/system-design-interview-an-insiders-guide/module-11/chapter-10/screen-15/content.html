Monitoring
Collect metrics, logs, and traces to detect and diagnose issues quickly.
Deployment Strategies
Blue/Green and Canary reduce risk and enable fast rollback when needed.
Incident Response
Practiced playbooks, blameless post-mortems, backups, and continuous improvement close the loop.
Final takeaway

Prioritize observable, reversible changes: instrument first, automate deployments second, and practice your incident response regularly.
