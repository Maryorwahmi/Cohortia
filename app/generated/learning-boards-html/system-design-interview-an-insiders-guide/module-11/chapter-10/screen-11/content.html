Alert design

  • Define thresholds based on SLOs (e.g., p99 > 500ms for 5 minutes).
  • Route to the right on-call team and include a runbook link.
  • Reduce noise with composite and suppression rules.
Dashboard essentials
Include SLOs, error rates, queue depths, and business KPIs so responders can triage impact quickly.
Activity example
Set an alert for Kafka consumer lag and route it to the fan-out on-call channel; include steps to restart consumer groups or scale them up.
