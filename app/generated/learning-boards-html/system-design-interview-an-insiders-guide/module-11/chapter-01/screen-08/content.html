Requirement Template
Requirement: Feed read latency
Priority: Critical
Target: 95% of feed reads < 200ms
Estimated Peak QPS: 200,000 reads/sec
Justification: Assume 10M DAU, 20% active concurrently, averaging 10 feed reads/minute per active user.
Use this format to justify component choices in interviews and design docs.
How to estimate
  1. Start with DAU, pick a concurrency percentage.
  2. Multiply by average feed interactions per minute to get reads/sec.
  3. Apply peak multipliers for spikes and buffer for growth.
