Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Mistakes & Trade-offs
Frequent Mistakes
  • Picking specific infrastructure without QPS/TPS estimates.
  • Designing for average load and ignoring spikes.
  • Assuming uniform consistency needs across entities.
  • Underestimating storage and bandwidth for media.
Guiding Trade-offs
Assume and Record:
Make explicit estimates for DAU, concurrency, read/write ratios, and peak multipliers. Use them as design constraints.
Pro Tip
Prefer simple, observable designs for MVPs, and reserve complex, costly patterns for proven needs.
