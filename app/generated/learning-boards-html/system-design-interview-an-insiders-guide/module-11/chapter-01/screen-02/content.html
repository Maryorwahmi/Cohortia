Bloom steps
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify Functional Req.
List user behaviors the system must support and why they matter for the feed.
Mnemonic: checklist of flows (login, post, follow, view, interact)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Define Non-functional Targets
Translate availability, latency, and durability goals into numbers for design decisions.
Mnemonic: S.A.L.D. — Scalability, Availability, Latency, Durability
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Establish Scope & Entities
Identify User, Post, Follow, Like, Comment and key relationships for the feed model.
Mnemonic: Entity map + primary flows
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Prioritize Trade-offs
Make pragmatic choices about consistency, cost, and time-to-market given measured targets.
Mnemonic: Cost vs Consistency vs Speed
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
How to use these objectives
  • Turn each objective into interview questions and concrete metrics.
  • Practice sizing with hypothetical QPS/TPS numbers and see how choices change.
  • Apply the prioritization template to rank new requirements by impact and cost.
Complete these milestones by drafting a one-page scope and numeric targets for a feed MVP.
