Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Key Concepts
WebSockets
Full-duplex communication over one TCP connection for real-time browser updates.
Push Notifications
Messages sent from servers through platform gateways to mobile devices, even when apps are inactive.
APNS / FCM
Platform gateways used to deliver push to iOS (APNS) and Android / cross-platform (FCM).
Summary: choose the channel appropriate to client state, persist notifications for history, and use Redis for unread counts.
