Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Key Concepts
Device Token
Unique token issued by APNS/FCM for delivering pushes to a specific device.
Notification DB
A persisted store of notifications partitioned by user for history and paging.
Unread Count
A fast Redis counter tracking unseen notifications; reset when users view their notifications.
Final takeaway: combine durable event ingestion, a central Notification Service, appropriate delivery channels, and operational practices for tokens and monitoring to create a reliable real-time notification system.
