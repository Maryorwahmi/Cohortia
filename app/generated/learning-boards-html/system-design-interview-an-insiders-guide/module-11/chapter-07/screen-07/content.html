Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › WebSocket vs Push
WebSockets (browser)
  • Persistent bi-directional connection.
  • Low-latency, immediate in-app updates.
  • Requires connection management and server-side mapping.
Example: a live comment stream updates instantly for participants with open sockets.
Push (mobile: APNS / FCM)
  • Delivered by platform services even when app is backgrounded.
  • Requires device tokens and platform-specific payloads.
  • Best-effort delivery; handle token expiration and invalidation.
Example: FCM delivers a notification to a user's phone while the app is closed, enabling re-engagement.
Common Mistake

Assuming APNS/FCM guarantee delivery like a persistent socket. They are platform gateways with best-effort semantics and token lifecycle issues.
