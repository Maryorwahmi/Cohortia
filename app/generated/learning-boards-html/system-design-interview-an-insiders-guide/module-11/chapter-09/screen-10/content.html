Noise reduction techniques
Filters
  • Stop-word removal and normalization
  • Minimum count thresholds to exclude rare tokens
  • Spam heuristics and rate limiting per source
Geographic partitioning
  • Maintain windowed counts per region key
  • Route events with geo metadata to regional processors
  • Provide both global and local top-K lists
Quality check
Measure engagement ratios for trending topics to avoid surfacing low-quality or bot-driven trends.
Common Mistake
Not partitioning by geography or language creates irrelevant trends for many users; localizing counts yields more meaningful results.
