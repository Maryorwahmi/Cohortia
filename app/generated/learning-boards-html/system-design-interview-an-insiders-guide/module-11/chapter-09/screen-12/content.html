Module 11 › Chapter 11.9 › Batch vs Real-time
When to choose which
Batch (Spark / EMR)
  • Best for heavy joins and historical reports
  • Cheaper for periodic large computations
  • Good for model training and backfills
Stream (Flink / Kafka Streams)
  • Low-latency dashboards and live metrics
  • Requires continuous state management
  • Good for sliding-window trending and alerts
Key Takeaway
Combine both modes: streams for immediacy and batch for canonical, auditable aggregates. Monitor both to ensure trust in metrics.
