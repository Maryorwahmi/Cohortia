Don't use operational DB for search
Cassandra and similar stores are optimized for key-based access, not full-text tokenization.
Use sliding windows for trends
Windowed counts highlight recent activity; cumulative metrics do not capture emergent spikes.
Durable ingestion for analytics
Stream events to a queue and archive raw events to a data lake before transformation.
Final thought: every advanced feature adds operational complexity. Call out monitoring, data quality, and privacy as part of your design to make systems that scale and remain trustworthy.
