Module 11 › Chapter 11.9 › Relevance Ranking
Ranking signals
Text relevance
Base score from search engine analyzers and inverted index matches.
Recency
Boost recent content for time-sensitive queries; apply decay functions to older items.
Popularity & signals
Likes, comments, shares can alter ordering when relevance ties exist.
Misconception: greater popularity always means better quality. Systems should prevent historical popularity from permanently dominating results and consider decay and personalization to keep results fresh and relevant to the user.
Key Takeaway

Design ranking as a combination of signals and tune weights with experiments and metrics to avoid biased or stale results.

Pro tip
Log feature values sent to ranker to enable offline analysis and debugging of ranking decisions.
