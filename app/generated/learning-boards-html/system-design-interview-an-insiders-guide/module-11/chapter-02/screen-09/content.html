Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Datastores
Relational (SQL)
Good for structured data and strong consistency: user profiles and transactional operations.
When to use
When relationships require joins and ACID properties, or when you need strict data consistency.
NoSQL & Graph
NoSQL is preferred for posts and feeds with high write/read volume and flexible schemas. Graph databases are for deep relationship queries like recommendations.
When to use
Pick NoSQL for throughput and scale; pick graph when traversals across relationships are core to functionality.
Example
Profiles in relational DB; posts in NoSQL; consider a graph for follower recommendations.
Pitfall
Mixing stores requires careful cross-service consistency design for deletes and updates.
Key Takeaway
Choose the right store per data characteristic and design coordination for cross-store operations.
