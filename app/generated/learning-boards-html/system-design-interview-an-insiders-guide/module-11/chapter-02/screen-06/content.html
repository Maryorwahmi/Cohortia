Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Microservices
User Service
Profiles, authentication, authorization, follower relationships.
Post Service
Create and store posts, extract metadata, moderate, and publish "New Post" events.
Feed Service
Aggregate posts, generate personalized feeds, maintain feed stores and caches.
Notification Service
Emit notifications triggered by events like new posts, likes, or comments.
Media Service
Handle uploads, interact with object storage, and hand off CDN URLs for delivery.
Concrete scenario
When Post Service emits a "New Post" event, Feed Service may fan-out updates to followers and Notification Service may enqueue alerts. These are independent consumers of the same event.
Coordination note
Splitting services requires designing contracts and handling consistency across stores when operations span services.
