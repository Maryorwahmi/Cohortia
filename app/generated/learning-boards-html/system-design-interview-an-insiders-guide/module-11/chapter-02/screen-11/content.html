Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Message Queues
Why use queues
  • Decoupling: producers need not know consumer details.
  • Asynchrony: producers return quickly and consumers process later.
  • Buffering: queues absorb spikes and improve resilience.
Example
Post Service publishes New Post events; Feed and Notification Services consume independently and scale as needed.
Design concerns
Plan for ordering, delivery guarantees, retries, and idempotency in consumers to avoid duplicate effects.
Key Takeaway
Queues give you decoupling and resilience; design consumers to be idempotent and plan for ordering when it matters.
