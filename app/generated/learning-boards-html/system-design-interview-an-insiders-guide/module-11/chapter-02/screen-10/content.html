Common cache patterns
  • Feed Cache: store pre-generated feeds for fast reads.
  • Post Cache: store hot post content to avoid DB lookups.
  • Lazy vs eager updates: choose between rebuild-on-read or update-on-write strategies.
Example
On a new post, Feed Service can push updates to hot followers’ caches (eager) or let caches expire and rebuild on-demand (lazy).
Pitfall
Assuming caches are always fresh; this causes correctness bugs around deletes and edits.
Key Takeaway
Design TTLs and invalidation carefully; choose eager updates for hot users and lazy rebuilds for cold users.
