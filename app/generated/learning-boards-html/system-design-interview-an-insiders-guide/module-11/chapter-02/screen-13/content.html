Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Pitfalls & Safety
Top mistakes
  • Starting with a monolith and hitting scaling limits.
  • Neglecting cross-service consistency for deletes and updates.
  • Ignoring idempotency and retry semantics for queued work.
Security & Observability
Secure inter-service traffic, encrypt sensitive data, and design metrics and logs into every service. Track queue lag, cache hit rates, and request latencies so you can spot issues early.
Action
Plan a reconciliation job for cross-service deletes and include retry and dead-letter strategies for queues.
