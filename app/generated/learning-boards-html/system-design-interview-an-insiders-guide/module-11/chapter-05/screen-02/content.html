Learning Objectives
Abilities you will demonstrate by chapter end
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Design a scalable ingestion pipeline
You will specify components and flow that support sustained millions of writes per second and explain why each stage exists.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand message queue buffering
You will explain how queues decouple producers and consumers and act as durable buffers during spikes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement sharding strategies
You will choose partition keys and explain time-bucket or composite keys to distribute write load across nodes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Ensure durability and eventual consistency
You will describe consistency levels, idempotency, DLQs, and backpressure measures that keep the system correct during spikes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
