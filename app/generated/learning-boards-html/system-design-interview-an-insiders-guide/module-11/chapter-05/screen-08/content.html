Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Hot Partition Mitigations
Comparison
Rate limit vs Time-bucket vs Dedicated nodes
Rate limiting: immediate protection, reduces per-user throughput, simplest to implement.
Time-bucketing: spreads writes, increases query complexity, good for sustained high volume.
Dedicated resources: isolates load completely, operational overhead and cost are high.
Scenario
Example: 50k messages/sec storm
Start with gateway rate limiting, ensure Kafka partitions and retention can absorb the burst, scale Post Storage Service instances and use batching to reduce DB pressure.
Pro-tip

Combine defenses: small rate limits prevent immediate overload while time-buckets and batching handle sustained high volume.
