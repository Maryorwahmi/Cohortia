Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Durability & Consistency
Concept
Durability and Eventual Consistency
Persist early and choose consistency levels thoughtfully to avoid data loss while keeping availability high.
Durability: queues and replicated database writes prevent message loss during transient failures.
Eventual consistency: accept a short visibility lag in exchange for higher availability and throughput.
Consistency tuning: choose replica acknowledgment levels that match your durability goals.
Practical Example
Enqueue first, then write with QUORUM
Enqueue for quick acknowledgement and write to Cassandra with a QUORUM consistency to ensure multiple replicas persist the data before confirming to consumers.
