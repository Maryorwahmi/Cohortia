Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Key Concepts
Durable enqueue
Persist events early so acknowledgements are reliable even when storage lags.
Queue buffering
Use queues to smooth bursts and decouple producer and consumer lifecycles.
Sharding & partitioning
Choose partition keys that distribute writes and plan for hot-partition mitigation.
Summary

Design for availability: acknowledge after enqueue, process asynchronously, and distribute writes — then monitor and adapt under load.
