Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Idempotency & DLQ
Process
Idempotency, retries, and DLQ
Design consumers to be repeatable, then fail safely to a dead letter queue with context for triage.
Idempotency
Detect duplicates by post_id or idempotency key and avoid double writes or side effects.
Dead Letter Queue
After repeated failures, move messages to DLQ with metadata for manual inspection and debugging.
Backpressure management
Use monitoring to detect lag and apply rate limiting, auto-scaling, or graceful degradation when necessary.
Example flow
Consumer receives message, attempts processing up to N times, on final failure annotate and move to DLQ for manual handling.
Key Takeaway

Retries without idempotency and DLQs can multiply failures; plan for safe retries and graceful triage paths.
