Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Key Concepts — Operations
Idempotency
Design to tolerate repeated deliveries and retries without duplicate effects.
Dead Letter Queue
Route persistent failures to a DLQ for inspection instead of blocking processing.
Monitoring
Alert on queue lag, write latency and DLQ rates and map them to playbook actions.
Final takeaway

Operational practices convert a good design into a resilient production system: instrument, automate, and triage deliberately.
