Availability vs Reliability
A direct comparison to clear common confusion.
Availability
Measured as uptime percent. Techniques include multi-zone redundancy, automated failover, and global load balancing. Think in "nines" for SLA commitments.
Example: Achieving 99.99% availability may require cross-region replicas and traffic rerouting to avoid single-region outages.
Reliability
Measures correct operation over time. Achieved through redundancy, transactional guarantees, and robust error handling. It focuses on data integrity and correctness under failure.
Example: Synchronous replication can improve reliability but may increase latency and make failover more complex, impacting availability.
Metric
Availability: uptime %. Reliability: failure probability over time.
Trade-off
Stricter consistency improves reliability but can raise latency or reduce availability during partitions.
Question
Which would you prioritize for a billing system: availability or reliability? Why?
