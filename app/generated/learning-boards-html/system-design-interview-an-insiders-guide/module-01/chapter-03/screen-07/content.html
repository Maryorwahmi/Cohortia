Reliability: Redundancy and Fault Tolerance
How to ensure correct operation over time.
Definition and practices
Reliability is achieved through redundancy, automated failover, robust error handling, and frequent recovery testing. Observability helps detect problems before they cascade.
Example
Primary database with replicas and automated promotion reduces the probability of data loss when a node fails.
Common mistake
Assuming redundancy without automated recovery and monitoring delivers reliability. It often does not.
Key Takeaway

Redundancy must be operationalized and tested regularly to become meaningful reliability.

Teaching visual — reliability elements
Replication
Multiple copies of data to prevent loss.
Failover
Automatic switching to healthy instances to maintain operation.
Error handling
Graceful degradation and retries that avoid cascading failures.
Observability
Logging, monitoring, and tracing to detect and diagnose issues quickly.
