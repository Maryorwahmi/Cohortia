Trade-offs and Interdependencies
Make trade-offs explicit and measurable.
Decision process
1) State priority pillar. 2) Choose technique(s). 3) State measurable expectation. 4) Acknowledge downside and mitigation. Apply this pattern in interviews and design documents.
Example: Financial app
Prioritize reliability and security; use synchronous replication and strict auth; accept higher latency and higher cost.
Example: Streaming feed
Prioritize availability and performance; use caching and eventual consistency; accept slightly stale reads.
Key Takeaway

Explicitly name the priority and measurable outcomes; trade-offs become defensible and testable.

Common misconceptions
Don’t assume one technique solves multiple pillars without cost. Adding replication improves reliability but complicates writes and consistency.
Interview prompt
Describe a trade-off between availability and consistency for a service you know and how you would mitigate the downside.
