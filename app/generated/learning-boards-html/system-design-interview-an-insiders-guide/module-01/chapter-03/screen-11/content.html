Security: Protecting Data and Services
Controls and practices to keep systems safe.
Core controls
Authentication, authorization, encryption, rate limits, and secret management are the baseline. Each must be paired with monitoring and rotation policies.
Example: Token-based auth plus TLS reduces the risk of session hijacking and eavesdropping on data in transit.
Operational practices
Conduct security audits, rotate keys, apply least privilege, and monitor for suspicious activity. Regular drills reduce response time to breaches.
Key Takeaway

Security is a continuous engineering discipline; retrofits are fragile and often incomplete.
