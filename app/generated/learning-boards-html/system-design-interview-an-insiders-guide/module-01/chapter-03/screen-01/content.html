Understanding the Core Pillars of System Design
A focused guide to the non-functional requirements that shape robust distributed systems and the trade-offs they demand.
In this lesson
Understand pillars, techniques, and trade-offs that drive production readiness.
Overview
Non-functional requirements define the observable qualities of a system — how it scales, how often it stays online, how fast it responds, and how easy it is to evolve and protect. They matter more as systems grow.
Consider these pillars as design constraints you review at every architecture decision.
Scalability
Grow capacity by adding resources; horizontal scaling avoids single points of failure.
Reliability
Design for correctness over time using redundancy and fault tolerance.
Availability
Keep the service reachable; measure in uptime percentages or "nines".
