Learning Objectives
Four measurable skills you will demonstrate after completing this chapter.
01
Identify and define NFRs
You will name the core pillars and explain why each is a system-level quality. Verified competency milestone.
Mnemonic: Group pillars into operational, performance, and security clusters to remember purpose and techniques.
02
Explain scalability, reliability, availability
You will describe practical effects and common techniques, like load balancers, sharding, replicas and failover. Verified competency milestone.
Mnemonic: Think "grow, survive, stay online" to keep the three distinct.
03
Understand performance and maintainability
You will measure latency and throughput, and propose approaches like caching and modular design to improve them. Verified competency milestone.
Mnemonic: Performance is speed; maintainability is the long-term cost to change.
04
Recognize trade-offs and interdependencies
You will explain how prioritizing one pillar affects others and present a reasoned trade-off for a given scenario. Verified competency milestone.
Mnemonic: Picture a balance scale — tuning one side tilts others.
