Skills & Evidence

These cards outline the observable behaviors interviewers expect when you clarify requirements and link them to design decisions.

Checklist
  • List essential features and confirm scope.
  • Capture target users and traffic metrics (DAU/MAU, QPS).
  • Record latency and uptime targets.
Linked reasoning
  • Explain how scale choices influence caches and DBs.
  • State trade-offs: consistency vs availability.
  • Show which assumptions would force re-design.
Time management
Use roughly 10–15 minutes to clarify requirements in a 45–60 minute interview. Confirm priorities, defer low-impact details, and move to architecture once core constraints are validated.
