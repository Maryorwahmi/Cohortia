Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › URL Shortener Walkthrough
Functional list
  • Generate a unique short URL for a given long URL.
  • Redirect short URL to original long URL on access.
  • Optional: custom aliases, click analytics, expiration.
NFR questions
  • How many short URLs/month? What is expected QPS for redirection?
  • What latency is acceptable for redirects? Is high availability required?
  • How important is durability and uniqueness guarantees?
Concrete implication
If redirections peak at 10,000 QPS, use CDNs and edge caches to serve redirects, and a distributed key-value store for mapping with replication for durability.
Common mistake
Assuming scale without confirmation can lead to over-engineering or an underpowered design. Always state assumptions and ask the interviewer to validate them.
Key Takeaway

Use feature and NFR answers to select caches, storage, and distribution strategies. Confirm assumptions before designing components.
