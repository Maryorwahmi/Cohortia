Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Questioning Process
Step 1 — Intent
Why build this? Who are the users?
Drive prioritization and scope by understanding the purpose and primary user journeys.
Step 2 — Functional
List required features
Record concrete actions: login, post, search, follow, comment, etc., and confirm any out-of-scope items.
Step 3 — Non-functional
Ask for metrics and targets
Seek DAU/MAU, QPS, latency bounds, and availability targets to guide architecture.
Step 4 — State assumptions
Make implicit details explicit
Say aloud any guesses, then ask the interviewer to confirm. This avoids diverging designs.
Step 5 — Summarize
Confirm priorities and next focus
Finish by summarizing agreed constraints and asking which area to design first.
Key Takeaway

A short, visible five-step checklist turns vague prompts into actionable constraints that drive architecture.
