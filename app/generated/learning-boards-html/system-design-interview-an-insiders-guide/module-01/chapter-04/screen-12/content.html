Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Time Allocation
Suggested breakdown
  • 2 min — clarify purpose and primary users.
  • 5–7 min — enumerate and prioritize functional requirements.
  • 3–5 min — probe NFRs and get numeric targets.
  • 1–2 min — summarize and confirm focus for design.
When to pivot
If the interviewer asks you to focus narrowly, ask additional targeted questions for that area and adjust your time allocation to dive deeper there.
Common pitfall
Spending too much time on peripheral features leaves insufficient time to propose and justify architecture and trade-offs. Prioritize high-impact constraints.
Key Takeaway

Timebox clarification and confirm the few constraints that will change your design decisions.
