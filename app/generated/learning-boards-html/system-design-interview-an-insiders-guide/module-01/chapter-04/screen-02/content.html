Learning Objectives

These objectives guide your questioning and scoping during a design interview.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Ask effective clarifying questions
You will learn to open with purposeful, high-level questions that reveal the system's intent and users.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Separate functional vs non-functional
You will practice listing features, then probing scale, latency, and availability to drive architecture choices.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Structure requirement capture
You will use a brief checklist to ensure features and constraints are recorded and confirmed before design.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify and validate assumptions
You will learn to state assumptions explicitly and request confirmation to avoid design drift.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
