Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Non-Functional Requirements
NFR Categories
Scale — DAU/MAU, peak QPS
Performance — latency targets
Availability — uptime percentage
Durability — data persistence guarantees
Consistency — strong vs eventual
Geo — global distribution or regional
Example — URL Shortener (NFR)
Ask how many short URLs are generated per month and redirections per second. If redirections reach 10,000 QPS, design choices include distributed key-value stores, aggressive caching, and a CDN for redirect endpoints.
Misconception
Assuming NFRs are optional leads to choosing inappropriate storage, caching, or messaging systems. Always ask for numeric targets or acceptable ranges.
Key Takeaway

Translate NFRs into measurable metrics. Those metrics are the primary inputs for architectural decisions.
