Why state assumptions?
They convert hidden choices into explicit inputs the interviewer can confirm, preventing major rework later in the design.
Good pattern
Say 'Assuming X, then Y...' and then ask for confirmation. If X is rejected, show how Y changes.
Concrete examples
  • 'I'm assuming 10 million DAU; with that we'll need partitioned databases.'
  • 'If peak QPS is only 100, we can avoid an in-memory cache and simplify the design.'
Common mistake

Never bury assumptions. Unstated assumptions are a common reason candidates design the wrong system.
