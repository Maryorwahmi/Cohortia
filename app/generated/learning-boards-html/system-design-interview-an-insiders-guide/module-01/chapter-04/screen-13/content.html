Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Common Mistakes
Missing metrics
Ask for DAU, peak QPS, latency targets, and availability goals. Without them, component choices are guesses.
Premature design
Avoid proposing detailed components before requirements are locked. Keep initial discussion at behavior and metric level.
Hidden assumptions
State assumptions explicitly and show how different values change design choices.
Final Tip

Use a short visible checklist and ask prioritized, measurable questions to avoid these common failures.
