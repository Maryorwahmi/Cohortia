Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › NFR Trade-offs
Scenario A — Prioritize low latency & availability
Choose distributed caching, asynchronous replication, and CDNs. Accept eventual consistency for faster reads.
Scenario B — Prioritize strong consistency
Use stronger coordination: single leader writes, consensus protocols, or transactional databases; accept higher latency or lower availability windows during leader failover.
Quick mapping
  • High read QPS → caches and CDNs.
  • Low write latency + strong consistency → leader-based replication.
  • Global users → geo-distribution and data partitioning.
Interview prompt technique
Offer the interviewer two realistic trade-offs and ask which they'd prefer. Then show how that selection changes key components in your design sketch.
Key Takeaway

Link explicit trade-off choices to concrete component decisions instead of guessing default requirements.
