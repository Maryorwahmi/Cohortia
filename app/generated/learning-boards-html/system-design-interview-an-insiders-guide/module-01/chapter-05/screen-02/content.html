Quantify scale from requirements
Explain how rough estimates determine whether a design needs object stores or a relational DB.
Mnemonic: small checklist — users, activity, item size, retention.

02
Compute QPS from DAU and behavior
Turn user counts into average and peak requests per second to inform ingress capacity.
Mnemonic: DAU × actions/day ÷ 86,400 → then scale for peak window.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Estimate storage at scale
Multiply item size by count and retention to determine TB or PB needs and storage choices.
Mnemonic: item size × daily count × years × 365.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Use latency and unit landmarks
Apply power-of-two conversions and latency numbers to justify caching and data locality.
Mnemonic: memorize L1, memory, SSD, disk, and cross-continent magnitudes.
