What to Remember
Compact Definitions
Back-of-the-Envelope
Quick approximations for scale.
Order of Magnitude
The power-of-ten scale of a number.
QPS
Requests per second; compute average and peak.
Storage Estimation
Item size × count × retention → TB/PB.
Bandwidth Estimation
Peak QPS × payload → egress and CDN needs.
Latency Numbers
Memorize L1, RAM, SSD, disk, and cross-continent magnitudes.
Final tip: show assumptions, keep units consistent, compute both averages and peaks, then round and explain trade-offs. That sequence communicates both competence and practical judgment.
