Estimation Checklist
Clarify & Assume
  • Operation and time window
  • Users, DAU%, actions/DAU
  • Payload size, retention
Compute
  • Daily totals → divide by 86,400 → average QPS
  • Scale for peak window → peak QPS
  • Daily storage → years → PB/TB
Round & Recommend
  • Round to powers, state basis
  • Decide CDN, cache, object storage, sharding
  • State safety margins and growth assumptions
Key Takeaway

A short, repeatable checklist keeps your estimates consistent and defensible under time pressure.
