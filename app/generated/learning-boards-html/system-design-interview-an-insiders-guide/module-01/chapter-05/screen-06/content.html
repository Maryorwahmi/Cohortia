Process Flow
Storage Estimation
Item size × daily count → daily TB → multi-year PB
Step-by-step
  1. Pick average item size (include thumbnails/metadata).
  2. Multiply by daily item count to get bytes/day.
  3. Convert to TB/day, then multiply by 365 × retention years.
  4. Round to nearest power of ten or two and state assumptions.
Example
100M photos/day × 1 MB/photo = 100,000,000 MB = 100 TB/day. Over 10 years: 100 TB/day × 365 × 10 ≈ 365 PB. This scale indicates object storage and lifecycle policies.
Implications
  • Hundreds of TB or PB justify object storage and distributed systems.
  • Consider replication, cold storage, and deletion policies to control cost.
  • Metadata and derived assets (thumbnails) can multiply storage — include them in estimates.
Key Takeaway

Long-term retention turns modest daily growth into PB-scale storage; plan storage class and lifecycle from the estimate.
