Checklist for every estimate
  • State units clearly (KB, MB, GB, QPS, ms).
  • Compute daily totals before rates.
  • Apply a peak multiplier and a peak window.
  • Round to powers of two or ten for order-of-magnitude.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Common mistake & fix
Mixing bits and bytes or forgetting peak traffic leads to wrong capacity choices. Fix by writing unit conversions explicitly and computing both average and peak QPS in your sketch.
Mnemonic visual: write DAU → actions → daily total → seconds/day → QPS → peak multiplier.
Use latency landmarks
When you cite latency numbers, explain the impact. For example, if cross-datacenter is ~150 ms, you cannot use it for tight synchronous operations; cache or local store is preferable.
Explain your rounding
State whether you round to powers of two or ten. Interviewers value the trade-off reasoning more than precise digits.
