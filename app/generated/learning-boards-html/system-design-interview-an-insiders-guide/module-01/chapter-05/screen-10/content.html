Common Mistake
Estimation Pitfalls & Fixes
Frequent mistakes
  • Skipping the calculation entirely.
  • Overstating precision instead of rounding.
  • Mixing bits and bytes or 1000 vs 1024 without saying so.
  • Using averages without computing peaks.
Practical fixes
  1. Write unit conversions explicitly.
  2. Show intermediate numbers so errors are visible and fixable.
  3. Round to powers of two or ten and state the rounding.
  4. Always compute peak QPS using a peak window and fraction.
These techniques make your reasoning transparent and let the interviewer focus on trade-offs.
