Powers of Two & Latency Landmarks
Quick unit conversions and timing anchors for design decisions
Powers of two (approx)
  • 2^10 ≈ 1 thousand (KB)
  • 2^20 ≈ 1 million (MB)
  • 2^30 ≈ 1 billion (GB)
  • 2^40 ≈ 1 trillion (TB)
Use these to round quickly and keep orders of magnitude clear.
Latency landmarks (approx)
  • L1 cache reference: 0.5 ns
  • Main memory reference: ~100 ns
  • Read 4K from SSD: ~150 µs
  • Disk seek: ~10 ms
  • Cross-continent round trip: ~150 ms
These magnitudes explain why in-memory caches reduce latency dramatically compared to remote storage.
