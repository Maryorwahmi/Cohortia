Messaging App Estimation
Step-by-step arithmetic you can show on a whiteboard
Assumptions
  • Total users: 2,000,000,000
  • DAU: 50% → 1,000,000,000
  • Messages per DAU per day: 20
  • Avg message size (with metadata): 1 KB
Calculated results
Daily messages = 1,000,000,000 × 20 = 20,000,000,000. Average QPS = 20,000,000,000 / 86,400 ≈ 231,481 QPS. If peak = 3× average concentrated over 4 hours, expect peak QPS in the hundreds of thousands. Daily storage = 20B messages × 1 KB ≈ 20 TB/day. Over 5 years ≈ 20 TB/day × 365 × 5 ≈ 36.5 PB.
Why this matters
Hundreds of thousands of QPS on the sending path implies you need horizontally scalable ingestion, partitioned queues, and shards. Multi-petabyte storage requires object storage with lifecycle policies. Always state assumptions and add safety margins for growth.
Check Your Understanding

Try re-running the numbers if messages average 2 KB instead of 1 KB and note how storage doubles while QPS stays the same.
