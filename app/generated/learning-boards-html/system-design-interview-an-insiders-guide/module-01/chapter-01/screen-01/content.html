What is System Design and Why It Matters
Learn how architects of software translate requirements into resilient, scalable blueprints and why that skill is essential for senior engineers and system design interviews.
In this lesson
Define system design, map functional and non-functional needs, and see how components like caches and queues support scale.
Why it matters
A solid design reduces outages, lowers cost, and enables teams to evolve a product without crippling refactors.
