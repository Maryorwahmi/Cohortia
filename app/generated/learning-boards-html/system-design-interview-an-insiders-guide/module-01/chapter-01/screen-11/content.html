What is maintainability?
The ease of understanding, modifying, and repairing a system over time.
  • Modular code and clear interfaces reduce coupling.
  • Automated tests and CI reduce risk of regressions.
  • Operational docs and monitoring speed incident recovery.
Technical debt
Shortcuts that accelerate delivery now but increase future cost. Manage by tracking debt and scheduling remediation.
Example
Tightly coupled logic that requires changes across services when business rules evolve.
