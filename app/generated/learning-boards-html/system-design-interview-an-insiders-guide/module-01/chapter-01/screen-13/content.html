Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Deconstruction Activity
Step 1
Identify functional requirements
List the essential actions users perform, e.g., stream video, search, manage profiles.
Step 2
Brainstorm NFRs
Name the performance, reliability, and scale needs tied to user expectations.
Step 3
Hypothesize components
Sketch candidate services like CDNs, caches, metadata stores, recommendation engines, and queues.
Step 4
Consider challenges
Identify likely bottlenecks: global distribution, encoding costs, personalization at scale.
Try this
Spend 15 minutes on your chosen app, follow the four steps, and note one design trade-off you found hardest to resolve.
