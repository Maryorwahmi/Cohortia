Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Message Queues
Queue pattern
  1. Producer enqueues a task and responds quickly to the client.
  2. Queue buffers tasks during bursts or when workers lag.
  3. Consumer processes tasks asynchronously and stores results.
Concrete example
When a user uploads a file, place a message with metadata on the queue; a separate worker handles resizing and storage.
Key Takeaway

Queues improve responsiveness and resilience, but require monitoring and idempotent consumers to avoid duplication.

Operational notes
Monitor
Track queue depth and processing lag to detect bottlenecks early.
Design for retries
Make consumers idempotent and plan retry and dead-letter handling for failed tasks.
