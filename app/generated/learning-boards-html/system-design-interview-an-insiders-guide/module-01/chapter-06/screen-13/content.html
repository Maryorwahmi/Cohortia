Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Common Mistakes
Pitfall: Premature microservices

Splitting into many services before you understand load and ownership increases coordination costs and makes reliability harder to achieve.

Countermeasure
Start coarse, measure coupling and load, and split when performance or team ownership requires it.
Pitfall: Single database assumption

A single relational database can become a bottleneck. Plan for replication, sharding, or using specialized stores for different needs.

Countermeasure
Document how you'd partition data or introduce a second store and why that addresses the bottleneck.
Interview advice
When asked, present a simple system first, then explain one or two targeted changes you'd make for higher load, and the trade-offs each change introduces.
