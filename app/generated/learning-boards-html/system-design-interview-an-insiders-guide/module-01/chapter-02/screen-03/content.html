Mnemonic
Ask · Outline · Dive · Justify
Say the phrase to center your approach. It reminds you to gather constraints first, then sketch a system, then drill into one component, and finally explain why your choices match the requirements.

Sample Script
"To scope this, can you confirm expected scale and latency?"
A short, focused question that gets you the non-functional constraints you need to choose components and trade-offs.

Pitfall Guardrail
Avoid premature detail
If you catch yourself drawing a schema within the first three minutes, stop and recap the scope. That pause often avoids misaligned assumptions.

Communication Drill
Two-sentence trade-off
State the choice and the impact, for example: "Using a CDN reduces latency for reads but increases operational complexity and cost for writes."
