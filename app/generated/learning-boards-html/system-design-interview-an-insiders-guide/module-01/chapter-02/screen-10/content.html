Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Common Pitfalls
Top Mistakes
  • Designing details before clarifying scope
  • Not stating assumptions aloud
  • Presenting a single solution without trade-offs
  • Poor time allocation during the interview
Quick Fixes
Use a short verbal plan, call out assumptions, and ask if the interviewer wants a deep dive into a specific component.
Concrete Example
Interviewee starts drawing tables immediately
Better approach: ask two quick scale questions, sketch a high-level flow, then say "If it's okay, I'll deep dive into the metadata store and show how it scales." That shift prevents wasted work and invites interviewer alignment.
Try this

Before drawing anything, say a 10-second plan that outlines the steps you'll take.
