Collaboration Habits
Invite feedback and state your plan clearly.
  • Signpost phases: "I'll clarify, outline, then dive."
  • Ask short, focused questions to gain constraints
  • Summarize after each major decision
Handling Pushback
Repeat the interviewer's point, explain the impact, and propose either a small adjustment or a reasoned defense.
Concrete Example
Interviewer asks about cost constraints
Respond: "If cost is a major constraint, I would prioritize single-region deployment with autoscaling and caching to reduce egress. That reduces availability but meets budget; does that trade-off align with your priorities?"
Key Takeaway

Clear, collaborative language turns a one-way test into a productive design conversation.
