Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Mock Interview
Problem Statement
Design a system to store and retrieve user-uploaded images.
Interviewer: target 100M daily users, 1–5 MB images, 99.99% availability, focus on upload and retrieval for now.
Interviewee Steps
  1. Ask targeted clarifying questions for 2–3 minutes
  2. Present a two-minute high-level design
  3. Deep dive into one component for 5–7 minutes
  4. Summarize trade-offs in one minute
Feedback Prompts
Use these to coach after the mock interview.
  • Did they clarify non-functional requirements?
  • Was the high-level diagram coherent and justified?
  • Were trade-offs stated and linked to requirements?
Try it now

Run a 15–20 minute session with a partner and iterate on the feedback.
