Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Leaderboard Activity
Activity template
Design cache placement, invalidation, eviction policy, and list common mistakes to avoid.
1. Cache placement: Distributed cache (Redis) for the top-100 list.
2. Invalidation: Background recompute every 30 seconds and atomic replace.
3. Eviction: Fixed size for the leaderboard; LRU/TTL for per-player caches.
Safety notes

Avoid letting the first read after expiry trigger recompute. Use scheduled jobs and atomic updates to keep reads stable.

Concrete example steps
  1. Schedule a 30-second job to recompute the top 100 from a streaming aggregator.
  2. Write the new list atomically to the distributed cache.
  3. Serve reads from the cache; measure refresh duration and hit ratio.
Check your understanding
