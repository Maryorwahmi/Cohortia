Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Eviction Policies
Eviction definitions
LRU: evict least recently used — favors temporal locality.
LFU: evict least frequently used — favors long-term popularity.
FIFO: evict oldest entry — simple but often suboptimal.
Concrete example
A news site with trending articles may benefit from LFU for longer-lived popularity, while search suggestions with recent spikes may be better with LRU.
Key Takeaway

Let access traces guide your eviction choice; monitor hit ratio and eviction churn after deployment.

Pro tip
If uncertain, start with LRU and measure. If a small set of keys dominate across long windows, consider LFU or adaptive hybrid policies.
Common mistake
Assuming FIFO is adequate. For real workloads with non-uniform access, FIFO often reduces hit ratio significantly.
