Designing a Caching Layer — Practical Scenarios
Practical guidance for placement, TTL, invalidation, eviction and monitoring so caches help rather than hurt.
Start by identifying whether a component is read-heavy or write-heavy, then match cache type and policy to its volatility.
We prioritize simple, safe caching first; introduce distributed invalidation and stampede protection as complexity grows.

Why this matters

Wrong caching choices cause stale reads, inconsistent views, or wasted memory. Thoughtful design yields big latency and cost wins without sacrificing correctness.
