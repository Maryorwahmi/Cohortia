Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › What to Remember
Start Simple
Begin with one cache layer for the highest-value endpoint and measure impact before adding complexity.
Protect the Origin
Use background refresh and single-flight protections to avoid stampedes and origin overload.
Measure & Iterate
Tune TTLs, eviction, and capacity based on hit ratio, evictions, and latency per endpoint.
Final takeaway
Caching is iterative. Use simple designs, protect against overload, and let metrics decide where to invest additional complexity.
