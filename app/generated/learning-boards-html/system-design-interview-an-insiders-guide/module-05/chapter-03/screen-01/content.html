Cache Invalidation Strategies

Understand why cached data goes stale, which invalidation techniques exist, and how to choose a trade-off for consistency and performance.

Why this matters
Freshness prevents incorrect reads; invalidation choices shape latency and complexity.
Practical outcome
Choose strategies per data type: TTL where staleness is okay; explicit invalidation where it is not.
Explore Key Topics
TTL
Simple expiration windows; easy to implement, may serve stale data until expiry.
Write Strategies
Write-through vs write-back affects latency and durability trade-offs.
Explicit Invalidation
Precise deletes or updates on change; reliable delivery and multi-cache coordination matter.
Visual Concept
Primary Store
Authoritative source of truth.
Cache Layer
Fast reads; may become stale.
Example
A user updates their profile. Without invalidation, other services may read the old profile from cache until policy or delete runs.
Coming up
TTL, write strategies, explicit invalidation, and stampede protection.
