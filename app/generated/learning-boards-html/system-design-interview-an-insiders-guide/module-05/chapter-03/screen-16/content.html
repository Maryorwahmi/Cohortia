TTL
Use when bounded staleness is acceptable. Add jitter and monitor hit rates.
Write policies
Write-through for freshness; write-back for throughput; write-around to avoid cache pollution.
Explicit invalidation
Use for immediate consistency; ensure durable delivery and idempotency across layers.
Final takeaway
Match strategy to data needs, plan reliable invalidation delivery, and protect your backend from stampedes.
