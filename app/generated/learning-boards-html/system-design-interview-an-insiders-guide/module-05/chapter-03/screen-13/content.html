Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Distributed invalidation
Reliable delivery pattern
  1. Write to DB and commit.
  2. Publish invalidation to a durable queue (transactional outbox recommended).
  3. Caches subscribe and apply invalidation with retries and acknowledgments.
Ordering note
Ensure invalidation is published after commit to avoid caches fetching pre-update values during races.
Reconciliation
Use periodic full-key scans or checksums to detect stale entries and repair them. Track invalidation delivery metrics and alert on failures.
Misconception
Assuming fire-and-forget invalidations will always succeed. For critical correctness, build durable publishing and retries.
