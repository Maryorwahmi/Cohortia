Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Cache Invalidation
Removing or updating stale cached data when the source of truth changes to avoid incorrect reads.
Invalidation Strategies
TTL, write-through, write-back, write-around, and explicit invalidation each trade freshness, latency, and complexity.
Operational Risks
Stampedes, dropped invalidations, and partial cache state require coalescing, durable delivery, and reconciliation.
Summary takeaway
Design per data class: match freshness needs to an invalidation strategy, and build reliable delivery and stampede protection into the system.
