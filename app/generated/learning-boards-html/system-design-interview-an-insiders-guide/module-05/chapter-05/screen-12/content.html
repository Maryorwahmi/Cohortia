Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Pitfalls & Fixes
Problems
  • Stale content: caused by long caching without versioning.
  • Misconfigured headers: disabling caching or setting wrong directives.
  • Purge storms: mass invalidations that overload the origin.
Fixes
Use versioned URLs to avoid purges for static assets.
Purge selectively and stagger invalidations to prevent origin spikes.
Monitor hit ratio and origin egress to detect unexpected cache behavior quickly.
Quick Checklist
Verify Cache-Control headers, test purge procedures in staging, and monitor edge metrics after deployments.
