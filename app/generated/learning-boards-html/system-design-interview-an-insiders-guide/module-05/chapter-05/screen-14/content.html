Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts
CDN

A distributed network of caches that serves content from locations near users to reduce latency and improve reliability.

PoP & Edge

Points of Presence house edge servers that cache content for regional users and apply routing and health checks.

Caching rules

Use Cache-Control, TTLs, versioned URLs, and purges to balance freshness and performance for different asset types.

Summary Takeaway
Use CDNs to move shared content close to users for speed and resilience, and pair caching policies with monitoring and invalidation plans to prevent stale data.
