Content Delivery Networks
How distributed edge caches bring content closer to users and why that matters for performance, scale, and security.
Points of Presence (PoPs)

Strategic data centers with edge servers that cache and deliver content to local users.

Edge Servers

Cache servers inside PoPs store content copies and respond to nearby requests for fast delivery.

Benefits

Reduced latency, lower origin load, higher availability, and edge security like DDoS mitigation.

Use Cases

Static assets, media streaming, versioned files, and semi-static pages that tolerate short caching windows.
