Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › CDN vs Origin-only
Side-by-side comparison
Performance
CDN: Lower latency for remote users. Origin-only: Higher latency for distant clients.
Cost
CDN: Additional service cost but reduced origin egress. Origin-only: More origin bandwidth cost.
Availability
CDN: Regional failover and distributed serving. Origin-only: Single point of failure risk.
Security
CDN: Edge filtering and DDoS mitigation. Origin-only: Origin exposed to traffic surges.
Practical considerations
  • Measure whether latency improvement materially affects your key metrics before adopting an expensive CDN plan.
  • Consider traffic shape: globally distributed traffic benefits more than region-concentrated traffic.
  • Plan for cache misses and origin capacity; a sudden invalidation storm can increase origin load temporarily.
Key Takeaway
CDNs trade additional service cost for performance, scalability, and security benefits; align that trade with product needs.
