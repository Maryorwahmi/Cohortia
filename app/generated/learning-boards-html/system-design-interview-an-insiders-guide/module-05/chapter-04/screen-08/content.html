First-In, First-Out (FIFO)

Queue-based eviction

Evict the item that has been in the cache the longest. Implementation is trivial with a queue.

  • Pros: simple, low overhead.
  • Cons: ignores access frequency and recency; may evict hot items.
Use case: data that ages predictably and where recency doesn't indicate future use.
Most Recently Used (MRU)

Evict the newest access

Remove the most recently accessed item. Useful when recent access means you won’t need it again soon, such as forward-only scans.

  • Pros: effective for negative temporal locality.
  • Cons: counterintuitive for general web access; rarely optimal by default.
Misconception: MRU is simply a flipped LRU — its effectiveness depends entirely on negative locality in the workload.
