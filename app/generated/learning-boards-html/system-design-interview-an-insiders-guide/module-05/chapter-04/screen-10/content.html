Adaptive strategies

ARC and hybrids

ARC dynamically balances recency and frequency to respond to changing workload signals, improving robustness across mixed patterns.

Cost-aware eviction
Account for re-fetch cost and item size when deciding what to evict.
When to use
When traces show mixed recency and frequency signals or varying costs by key.
Concrete example

A distributed cache might keep small configuration keys with LFU-like behavior, while large video thumbnails follow recency or cost-aware rules. ARC can adjust automatically as patterns shift.

Misconception: "Adaptive always beats simple." Adaptive policies can outperform when workload mixes patterns, but they add complexity and require measurement to justify their use.
