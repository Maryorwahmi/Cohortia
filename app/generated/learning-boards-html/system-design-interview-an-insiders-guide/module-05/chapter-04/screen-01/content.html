Why this chapter matters

Caches improve latency and throughput, but poor eviction rules can collapse those gains. This chapter gives you the vocabulary, comparisons, and a starter LRU implementation to test on real workloads.

Concept map
Eviction choices & outcomes
LRU
Favors recent items. Good for temporal locality.
LFU
Favors frequently accessed items across time horizons.
FIFO
Simple queue — evicts by insertion time only.
MRU
Evicts very recent items. Useful for negative locality scans.
Tip: measure hit ratio and simulate access patterns before choosing a policy.
