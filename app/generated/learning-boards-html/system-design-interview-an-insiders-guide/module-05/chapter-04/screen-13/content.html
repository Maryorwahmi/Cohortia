Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Pitfalls & Pro-tips
Common mistakes
  • Choosing a policy without trace data.
  • Ignoring miss cost and item size.
  • Using implementations with hidden O(n) operations at scale.
Pro-tips
  • Instrument and replay traces before deploying a change.
  • Consider size- and cost-aware eviction for heterogeneous items.
  • Start simple, then adapt if traces demand it.
Example bad choice: applying FIFO to a user-profile cache causes frequent misses for active users who were cached early.
