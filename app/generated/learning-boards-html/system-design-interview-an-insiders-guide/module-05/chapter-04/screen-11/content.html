Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Choosing an eviction policy
Practical workflow
Instrument
Collect hits, misses, timestamps, and miss costs.
Classify
Identify recency, frequency, and scan patterns.
Test
Replay traces for candidate policies and compare hit ratio.
Iterate
Deploy with an evaluation window and rollback criteria.
Example decision: for a streaming service with re-watches, start with LRU and measure. If long-term title popularity dominates and LRU loses hot items to scans, evaluate LFU or an adaptive hybrid.
