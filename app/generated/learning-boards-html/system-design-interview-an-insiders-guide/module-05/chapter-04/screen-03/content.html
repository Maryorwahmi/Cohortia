Evidence-based selection

A checklist for proving your policy choice

Instrument
Record hits, misses, and access timestamps to compute hit ratio and identify hot keys.
Replay
Replay real traces to see how policies behave under production sequences.
Validate
Compare hit ratio and miss cost before and after policy changes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE • Provide a short report: chosen policy, test traces, hit ratio change, and fallback plan.
