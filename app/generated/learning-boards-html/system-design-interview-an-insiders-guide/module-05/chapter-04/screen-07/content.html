Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Least Frequently Used (LFU)
Concept

Least Frequently Used

Evict the item with the smallest access count, favoring long-term popularity over recency.

When it shines
  • Workloads with stable popularity across long windows.
  • When occasional recency spikes are less important than overall frequency.
Misconception: "High historical count always means current importance." Without aging, old popularity can unfairly protect stale items.
Concrete example

A configuration store where settings stay popular for weeks. LFU preserves those frequently used keys even if they are not accessed in the last few minutes.

Implementation notes
Use frequency buckets or a min-heap; consider count decay to adapt to pattern shifts.
Trade-off
Better for long-term popularity but more complex and slower to adapt than LRU.
