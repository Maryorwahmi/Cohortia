LRU vs LFU

Use recency when recent access predicts future requests. Use frequency when long-term popularity matters.
Evaluation checklist
  • Is popularity short-lived or stable?
  • Are scan patterns present?
  • What is the cost to re-fetch on a miss?
Scenario examples
Short bursts
Favor LRU: session pages and recently accessed items.
Long-term hot keys
Favor LFU: configuration keys or metrics queried consistently over time.
Misconception: "LFU is always better for popularity." Without decay, LFU can lock out new relevant items.
