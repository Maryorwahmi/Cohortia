Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Cache Eviction
Removal of items when capacity is reached; core to cache behavior.
LRU & LFU
LRU uses recency; LFU uses frequency. Choose based on access stability.
Policy Selection
Instrument, replay traces, compare hit ratios, and iterate with rollback criteria.
Summary Takeaway

Eviction policy is the key lever of cache effectiveness — pick, test, and iterate based on real access patterns and measured hit ratios.
