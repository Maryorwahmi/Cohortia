Definition & Pattern

Micro-caching

Short TTLs for hot endpoints smooth origin traffic while keeping responses acceptably fresh.

Example
Cache a public stats endpoint for 3 seconds to reduce origin burst during high read traffic while keeping data near-real-time.
Warnings
Do not micro-cache personalized or authenticated responses unless fragmentation isolates private data.
Partial caching

Edge Side Includes (ESI)

Cache reusable fragments at the edge and assemble responses to avoid repeating expensive origin work for common parts of a page.

Analogy
Think of ESI as a template with cached header and footer fragments, and a small dynamic body fetched per user, reducing total origin work.
Key Takeaway

Micro-cache short-lived public endpoints and use fragment caching for pages where only parts change per user.
