Operational Checklist

Staged rollouts and metric gates

Promote changes gradually, use per-path metrics as gates, and keep shields and rollbacks ready for purges.

Stage
Apply rules in staging and simulate traffic to validate behavior.
Canary
Roll out to a region or shard and watch hit ratio and origin requests.
Promote
Widen only when key metrics show stability across cycles.
Key Takeaway

Adopt staged rollouts and metric gates to prevent accidental origin overload and ensure predictable user experience.
