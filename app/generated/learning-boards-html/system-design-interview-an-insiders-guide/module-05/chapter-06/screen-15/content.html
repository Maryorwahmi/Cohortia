Versioning over purges
Avoid wide invalidations by changing asset URLs on deploy.
Micro-cache dynamic endpoints
Use short TTLs for high-volume, near-real-time endpoints to smooth traffic.
Tight forwarding rules
Ignore irrelevant query params and limit cookies/headers forwarded to preserve hit ratio.
Final Tip

Apply each change in small steps, verify per-path metrics, and use Origin Shield during risky purges to protect your origin.
