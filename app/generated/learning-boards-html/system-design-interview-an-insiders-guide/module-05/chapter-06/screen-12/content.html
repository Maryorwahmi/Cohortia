Comparison

Bad vs Good

Bad: Forward all query params
Leads to many cache keys and low hit ratio.
Good: Ignore irrelevant params
Keep cache keys stable for identical content.
Bad: Purge entire site frequently
Causes origin stampedes and higher costs.
Good: Use versioning and targeted purges
Avoid large-scale invalidation and reduce origin load.
Operational Guidance
  • Test rules in staging, measure hit ratios, then promote changes.
  • Combine large purges with Origin Shield to limit origin concurrency.
  • Keep origin transport over HTTPS and lock access to CDN sources.
Key Takeaway

Antipatterns are often simple to fix; pair configuration discipline with monitoring to avoid repeated regressions.
