Security

Edge TLS and Origin Transport

Terminate TLS at the CDN edge for latency benefits, and always use HTTPS to the origin to keep transport encrypted end-to-end.

Checklist
  • Enable HTTPS at edge and origin.
  • Restrict origin access to CDN IP ranges or signed requests.
  • Enable WAF rules for common exploits.
  • Use edge DDoS protection for volumetric events.
Architecture

Origin Protection Pattern

The CDN is the first contact point. It filters and authenticates, then forwards traffic to your origin over a secured channel that only the CDN can use.
Analogy
Think of the CDN as a guarded gate that inspects traffic at the perimeter so the inner server farm only sees vetted, necessary requests.
Key Takeaway

Secure at the edge and preserve encryption to origin; do not expose your origin to direct public traffic.
