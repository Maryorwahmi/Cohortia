Core Concept

Cache Hit Ratio

The percent of requests satisfied by edge caches instead of your origin.

Why it matters
  • High hit ratio reduces origin load and bandwidth costs.
  • Improves user-perceived latency by serving from nearby PoPs.
  • Helps prioritize caching rules for costly endpoints.
Common mistakes

Relying on global hit ratio alone can hide per-path problems, and forwarding irrelevant query parameters can collapse cache effectiveness.

Key Takeaway

Break down hit ratio by path, validate query and cookie rules, and track origin requests alongside hits.

Example investigation

  2. Verify asset URLs changed during a deploy causing new cache keys.
  3. Inspect origin logs for 5xx errors that could force revalidation.
