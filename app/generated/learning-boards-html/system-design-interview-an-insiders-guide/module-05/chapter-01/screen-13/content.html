Caching Anti-Patterns

Identifying Poor Caching Candidates

Recognizing data that is unsuitable for caching

Comments for an Article (`/comments/{article_id}`)

  • Access Frequency: Frequent
  • Change Frequency: Constantly
  • Candidate: No
  • Reasoning: High volatility leads to constant invalidation, negating benefits.
  • Suggested TTL: N/A

Real-time Stock Ticker (`/live-stock-ticker`)

  • Access Frequency: Extremely Frequent
  • Change Frequency: Every second
  • Candidate: No
  • Reasoning: Extreme volatility makes caching impractical and inefficient.
  • Suggested TTL: N/A

Admin Analytics Data (`/admin/dashboard/analytics`)

  • Access Frequency: Rarely
  • Change Frequency: Real-time computed
  • Candidate: No
  • Reasoning: Infrequent access means no benefit, real-time nature means immediate staleness.
  • Suggested TTL: N/A
Key Takeaway

Avoid caching data that is highly volatile or rarely accessed, as it can introduce more overhead and complexity than performance gains.
