Deployment Models & Durability
Standalone vs clustered caches, sharding, and the durability risks of deferred writes.
Standalone cache
Simple to operate but a single point of failure; clients must handle misses and rebuilds.
Clustered/replicated cache
Improves availability but requires coordination, replication configuration, and consistent hashing for sharding.
Write-back durability risk
If a cache acknowledges writes before persistence, a crash can lose updates. Mitigate with persistence or synchronous replication, or choose write-through for critical data.
Key Takeaway

Match your cache deployment to your availability and durability requirements, and plan rehydration and protection for node failures.
