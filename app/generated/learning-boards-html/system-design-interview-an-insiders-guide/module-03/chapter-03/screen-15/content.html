Select layer
Pick the fastest layer that supports required invalidation and freshness.
Pick invalidation
Write-through for correctness; write-back for speed; write-around to avoid cold-write pollution.
Choose eviction
LRU for recency, LFU for long-term popularity, FIFO for simplicity.
Final Tip
