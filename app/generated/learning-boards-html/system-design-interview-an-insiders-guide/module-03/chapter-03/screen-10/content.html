TTL and Miss Handling
Bound staleness with TTLs and cache misses with negative caching and robust fallbacks.
Time-To-Live (TTL)
Automatically expire items after a set duration. Short TTLs mean fresher data and more origin traffic; long TTLs reduce origin load but risk staleness.
Negative caching
Cache not-found responses for a short TTL to avoid repeated origin lookups for missing keys.
Example choices
Product metadata might have moderate TTLs; session tokens may need short TTLs or immediate invalidation on logout for security.
Key Takeaway

Tune TTLs and negative caching to balance freshness and origin protection; monitor miss rate and origin latency to guide tuning.
