Latency & Percentiles
Use percentiles to capture user-facing slow requests.
P50, P95, P99 explained
P50 is median; P95 and P99 show the long tail. These values help you prioritize fixes that benefit affected users.
Concrete example
Checkout API: P50=80ms, P95=200ms, P99=900ms. The business impact comes from that P99 tail.
Misconception
Optimizing only the mean leaves slow tails untouched. Track percentiles and correlate with root causes.
Pro Tip

Alert on P99 when user impact is critical, and investigate P95 as a leading indicator of tail growth.
