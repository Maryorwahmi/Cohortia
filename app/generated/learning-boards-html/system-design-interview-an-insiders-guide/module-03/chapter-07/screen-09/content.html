Designing Alerts
Use composite rules, maintenance windows, and severity levels to manage noise.
Alert example
Trigger if P99 latency > 500ms and 5xx error rate > 1% for 10 minutes. Route to on-call and include runbook link in alert payload.
Avoid alert fatigue
Tune thresholds and add suppression for known maintenance windows. Use priority routing to prevent low-severity noise from waking on-call engineers.
Dashboard best practices
Place high-level system health at the top, with drill-down panels for services and dependencies.
Top panels
Overall P99 latency, total throughput, and aggregate error rate with deploy annotations and time-range selectors.
Service panes
Per-service latency percentiles, CPU/memory, and recent error logs linked to traces or log queries.
Check your understanding
How would you avoid paging someone for a planned load test that increases throughput tenfold? Consider suppression or scheduled maintenance flags.
