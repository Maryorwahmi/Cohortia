Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Learning Objectives
01
Diagnose database bottlenecks

Identify read/write contention, large dataset effects, and single-point failures; explain why each symptom points to specific scaling needs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain replication models

Describe master-slave and multi-master replication, read routing, failover, and the consistency and conflict trade-offs each model introduces.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design shard strategy

Choose and justify a sharding key and strategy (range, hash, directory), and assess how it affects distribution, cross-shard queries, and rebalancing.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply denormalization tactically

Know when duplicating data reduces joins and improves latency, and how to manage the consistency and write-side complexity it creates.
