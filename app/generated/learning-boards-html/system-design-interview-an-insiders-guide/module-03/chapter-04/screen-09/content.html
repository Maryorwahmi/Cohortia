Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Sharding Strategies
Range-based
Pros
  • Simple to implement; good for range queries.
Cons
  • Prone to hot spots when ranges receive uneven traffic.
Hash-based
Pros
  • Even data distribution; fewer natural hot spots.
Cons
  • Poor support for range queries; harder to reason about ordering.
Directory-based
Pros
  • Maximum flexibility and explicit control over placement.
Cons
  • Requires a lookup component to be scalable and consistent.
Scenario guidance
If your queries need ordered ranges, prefer range sharding but add rebalancing plans. If unpredictable traffic creates hot users, prefer hash sharding and consider consistent hashing for easier expansion. If your application needs fine-grained control, use a directory but budget the operational cost.
