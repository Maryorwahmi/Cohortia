Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Database Bottleneck
A limiting component that reduces throughput or increases latency.
Replication
Maintaining copies of data to scale reads and provide high availability.
Master-Slave Replication
One master handles writes; replicas handle reads and can be promoted on failure.
Summary
Keep these short definitions ready to explain why you chose a pattern and what operational costs you considered.
