Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Final Concepts
Sharding
Horizontal partitioning of data into independent shards to scale writes and storage.
Sharding Key
Column(s) used to route rows to shards; aim for even distribution and single-shard queries.
Denormalization
Intentionally duplicating data to reduce joins for hot read paths, accepting redundancy and write complexity.
Parting thought
When you recommend a pattern, tie it to a measurable symptom and describe one concrete mitigation for the associated cost.
