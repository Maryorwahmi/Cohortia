Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Key Concepts
Concurrency vs Parallelism
Interleaving vs simultaneous execution; name the mode before designing coordination.
Race, Deadlock, Livelock
Recognize lost updates, waiting cycles, and futile retries; design ordering and backoff.
Distributed Locks & Fencing
Require timeouts, ownership checks, and fencing tokens to avoid stale-holder writes.
Final takeaway

Design for failure and pick the simplest model that preserves correctness while enabling the availability and performance you need.
