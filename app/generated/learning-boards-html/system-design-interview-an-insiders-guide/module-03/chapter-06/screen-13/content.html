Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Common Mistakes & Safety Notes
Frequent mistakes
  • Treating remote writes as atomic across replicas.
  • Not planning for stale lock holders or crashed clients.
  • Assuming message ordering across partitions is preserved.
Reviewer checklist
Ask about ownership, failure recovery, timeouts, idempotency, and monitoring for divergence.
Defensive patterns
Fencing & timeouts
Prevent stale holders from making late writes and recover from crashed clients.
Idempotency
Make consumers tolerant of duplicate messages to simplify retry strategies.
Key Takeaway
Design with partial failures in mind: assume that anything distributed can and will fail in ways you must detect and recover from.
