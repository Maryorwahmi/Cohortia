Learning Objectives
Learn the vocabulary, the hazards, and the fixes used in distributed concurrency.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate concurrency and parallelism
Explain the conceptual difference and why it affects coordination and throughput decisions. Use the juggling analogy to reason about scheduling versus multi-node execution.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Identify race conditions, deadlocks, and livelocks
Recognize common failure patterns and reason about ordering, resource acquisition, and progress guarantees to diagnose issues.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Explain synchronization mechanisms
Describe mutexes, semaphores, atomic ops, and why in-memory locks don't suffice across machines; name distributed lock services.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Describe distributed patterns
Choose between distributed locks, eventual consistency, CRDTs, and patterns like the transactional outbox depending on availability and correctness needs.
Verified Competency Milestone
