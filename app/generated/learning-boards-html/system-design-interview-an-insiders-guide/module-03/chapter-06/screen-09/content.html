Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Eventual vs Strong Consistency
Strong consistency
After a successful write, all subsequent reads return that write. Use when correctness cannot be compromised.
Use case
Banking and inventory where immediate correctness is required.
Eventual consistency
Replicas converge over time if no new updates occur. Use when temporary divergence is acceptable for higher availability.
Use case
Social feeds and counters where immediate sync across replicas is not critical.
Design guidance
  • Choose strong consistency for operations that must be correct immediately.
  • Choose eventual consistency when availability under partition is prioritized and application semantics tolerate divergence.
  • Combine eventual models with merge strategies or CRDTs to automate conflict resolution where needed.
