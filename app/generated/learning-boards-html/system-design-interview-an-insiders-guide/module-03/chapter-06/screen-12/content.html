Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Analyzing Concurrency
Design analysis checklist
  1. Detect shared mutable state and list its writers and readers.
  2. Assess correctness: is immediate consistency required?
  3. Estimate contention and scale: will locks become a bottleneck?
  4. Plan for failures: timeouts, fencing, and recovery steps.
  5. Propose a solution: local locks, distributed lock, CRDT, partitioning, or outbox pattern.
Scenario
Two microservices updating product stock. Answer the checklist to choose lock-based serialization or a partitioned owner model.
