From diagnosis to a design decision
Interview scenario practice
  • Spot shared mutable state such as a central counter or stock value. Ask: who writes, who reads, and how often?
  • Decide whether a distributed lock is suitable or if redesign to remove the shared write is better for scalability.
  • When availability is primary, consider eventual consistency or CRDTs; when correctness is primary, choose stronger coordination.
Mnemonic
D-A-R-T: Detect, Assess, Recommend, Test. Detect shared state. Assess trade-offs. Recommend lock or model. Test failure modes.
