Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Simulate & Lock
Step 1: Illustrate the race
Two nodes both read the counter at 10 and intend to increment. Without coordination both may write 11, losing one increment.
Step 2: Lock-based solution
  • Attempt to acquire a lock key for the counter using a safe algorithm with expiry.
  • If lock acquired, read counter, increment, write, then release the lock after verifying ownership.
  • If lock not acquired, wait with randomized backoff and retry to avoid contention spikes.
Operational checklist
Use lock timeouts, ownership checks, fencing tokens, and jittered retries to be robust under failures.
Reflection
Consider CRDTs or owner-routing of writes if the lock causes a throughput bottleneck or single-point contention.
