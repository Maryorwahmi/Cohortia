Scale Up

Simple and fast, but limited and a single point of failure.

Scale Out

Resilient and high capacity, requires handling distributed complexity.

Plan & Measure

Define metrics and migration triggers; test failover and rebalancing plans.

Parting thought

Answer interviews with the bottleneck, the short-term fix, and the long-term migration plan to show both judgement and execution detail.
