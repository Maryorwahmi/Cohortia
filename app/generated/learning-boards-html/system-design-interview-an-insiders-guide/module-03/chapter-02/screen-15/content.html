What to Remember — Part 2
Three closing points to keep sharp for interviews and design work.
Health Checks
Probe both process and dependencies to avoid routing to broken servers.
Session Persistence
Useful for legacy stateful servers but plan to externalize state for scale.
Interview Framing
State your choice, defend it with trade-offs, and offer mitigations or migration paths.
Final takeaway

Balance immediate operational needs with long-term scalability: choose algorithms and features that solve current problems while keeping a path to stateless, resilient architecture.
