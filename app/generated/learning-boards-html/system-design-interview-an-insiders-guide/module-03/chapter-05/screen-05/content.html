Message Queue Components and Flow
Understand responsibilities and the channel choice that fits your workflow.
Step-by-step
1. Producer publishes a small message: order_id or image_id and minimal metadata.
2. Broker persists the message to a queue or topic and acknowledges the producer.
3. One or more consumers pull or are pushed messages and process them.
4. On success consumer acknowledges; on failure message is retried or moved to DLQ after retries.
Key Takeaway

Design lightweight messages and choose queue vs topic based on whether you need one consumer or many.

Channel Comparison
Queue
Point-to-point
Each message delivered to a single consumer. Good for load balancing work across workers.
Topic
Publish-subscribe
Same message can be consumed by multiple subscribers. Good for fan-out notifications.
Monitoring pointers
  • Queue depth: messages waiting to be processed.
  • Publish rate vs consume rate: detect mismatches early.
  • Consumer lag and DLQ counts: surface failing flows quickly.
