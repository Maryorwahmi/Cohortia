Decoupling Services with Brokers
Persistence, routing, and partitioning are your control points.
Broker responsibilities
  • Persist messages to disk or stable storage to ensure durability across crashes.
  • Route messages to queues or topics and manage acknowledgments and redelivery semantics.
  • Support partitioning to improve throughput and optionally preserve order per partition key.
  • Provide configuration for retention, retries, and DLQ integration.
Design trade-off

Durability improves reliability but can increase write latency; partitioning improves throughput but complicates global ordering.

Concrete configuration choices
Persistence: enable durable writes to disk when losing messages is unacceptable.
Partitioning key: choose a key that groups related messages when per-key ordering matters.
Acknowledgement mode: require explicit consumer ack to ensure at-least-once delivery.
Retention & DLQ: configure when messages expire or move to a dead letter queue for manual inspection.
