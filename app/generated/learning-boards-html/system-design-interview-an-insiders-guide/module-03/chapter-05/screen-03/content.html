Skills and Assessment Focus
What you must demonstrate during exercises and interview prompts.
Explain synchronous failure modes
Describe blocking, high latency, and cascading failures when downstream services are slow or unavailable.
Design consumer safety
Include idempotency, retry limits, and dead letter handling in your design to prevent duplicate side effects and blocked queues.
Choose guarantees
State when to insist on durable messages and when ordering constraints require partition control.
Monitoring & metrics
Prepare metrics: queue depth, message rates, consumer lag, and DLQ counts to identify bottlenecks early.
