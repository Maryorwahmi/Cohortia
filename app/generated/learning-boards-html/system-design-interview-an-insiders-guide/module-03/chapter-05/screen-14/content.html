Key Concepts
Three interview-ready reminders.
Why async?
Offload slow work to keep frontend responsive and avoid cascading failures.
Choose channels
Use queues for work distribution, topics for broadcast; pick based on one-to-one vs many-to-many needs.
Operational safety
Idempotency, DLQs, and monitoring are essential for reliable processing.
Keep this checklist when designing or explaining asynchronous systems: reason about guarantees, design safe consumers, and instrument thoroughly.
