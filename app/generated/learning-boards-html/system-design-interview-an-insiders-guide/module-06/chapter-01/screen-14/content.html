Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Synchronous Processing
Blocks the caller until completion; simple flow but limits concurrency.
Asynchronous Processing
Returns control immediately and completes work later; improves responsiveness and throughput for I/O-bound tasks.
Trade-offs & Patterns
Expect harder debugging and eventual consistency; mitigate with Outbox, Saga, retries, and observability.
One-line takeaway

Adopt async where responsiveness and scale matter; always pair it with patterns and observability to maintain correctness and reliability.
