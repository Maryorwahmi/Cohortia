Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Eventual Consistency
Meaning & Example

When state updates are processed asynchronously, different components may observe temporary differences. Example: inventory updated via a background job can lag, temporarily showing out-of-date availability to users.

Mitigation: Provide clear UI messaging like 'processing' and support read-your-writes where needed.
Compensation: Use compensating transactions when partial failures must be rolled back.
Design decision: Reserve synchronous flows for operations where atomicity is non-negotiable.
Interview angle

Be prepared to explain where eventual consistency is acceptable and which patterns you'd use to ensure correctness despite it. Mention compensating actions or stronger coordination when necessary.
