Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Scalability & Fan-out
Architecture Sketch
Origin service publishes N requests concurrently
Downstream services consume from queues and process independently
Aggregator collects results as they arrive or proceeds with partial data
Practical notes
  • Choose queue durability and visibility timeouts based on acceptable data loss and retry semantics.
  • Design aggregation logic that tolerates late results or applies timeouts for best-effort responses.
  • Monitor consumer lag so spikes don’t lead to unbounded queues and degraded performance.
Interview tip: draw a fan-out with queues and show how you would scale consumers independently to handle bursts.
