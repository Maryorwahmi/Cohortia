Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Reliability Patterns
Outbox Pattern

Record the domain change and the message in the same transactional write to avoid losing events when publishing to a queue.

  • Relay process reads outbox and publishes messages to a queue.
  • Ensure idempotent publishing and track published markers to avoid duplicates.
  • Monitor outbox drain rate to detect issues early.
Saga & Compensations

Coordinate multiple local transactions using a sequence of forward actions and compensating actions if parts fail. This avoids 2PC’s global locks and scales better at the cost of added complexity.

Design note: identify clear compensating steps and ensure they can be triggered reliably to restore correctness.
Key takeaway

Combine Outbox for reliable emission, relays for delivery, and Saga/compensations to reconcile partial failures when atomic global commits are impractical.
