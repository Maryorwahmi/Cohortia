Interview Strategy

Problem → Solution → Properties → Trade-offs

Problem: synchronous coupling causes latency and brittle dependencies.
Solution: insert a queue to decouple and buffer.
Properties: durability, ordering, delivery guarantee, monitoring.
Trade-offs: complexity vs reliability; idempotency costs vs duplicate risk.
Quick script
Say: "I'll use a durable queue partitioned by user ID to preserve per-user ordering, choose at-least-once with idempotent consumers, configure a DLQ, and monitor consumer lag and DLQ growth. Does payment processing in this system require synchronous confirmation at checkout?"
Key Takeaway

Structure interview answers so each technical choice is justified by a business requirement and operational plan.
