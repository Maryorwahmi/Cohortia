Process Flow

Order placed → Queue → Consumers

  1. 1. Producer publishes OrderPlaced event into the queue.
  2. 2. Message persists and waits in the queue until a consumer is available.
  3. 3. Consumers (inventory, payment, shipping, notification) retrieve and process messages independently.
  4. 4. Consumers acknowledge on success; failures may be retried or moved to a DLQ.
Concrete scenario
During a flash sale the payment service slows. Messages backlog in the queue instead of causing user-visible failures. You can add consumers to increase throughput until the backlog clears.
Misconception: Queues remove the need to think about failures — false. You still must handle retries and poison messages.
Key Takeaway

Use queues to isolate latency and failures, but combine with retries, backoff, and DLQs to maintain robustness.
