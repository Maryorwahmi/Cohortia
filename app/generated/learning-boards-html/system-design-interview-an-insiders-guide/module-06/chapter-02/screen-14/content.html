Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
Message Queue
Asynchronous buffer that decouples producers and consumers and smooths load spikes.
Delivery Guarantees
At-most-once, at-least-once, exactly-once — choose by trade-offs and design consumers accordingly.
Durability & Ordering
Persist for safety; partition by key to preserve per-entity ordering while enabling parallelism.
Keep these concepts ready when describing a queue-based design and tie each choice to a business requirement and operational plan.
