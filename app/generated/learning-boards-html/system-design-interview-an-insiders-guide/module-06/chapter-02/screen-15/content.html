Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › What to Remember
Name the primitives
Producer, Consumer, Queue/Topic, Message.
Pick guarantees wisely
Map at-most, at-least, exactly-once to business needs.
Design idempotent consumers
Use transaction IDs and atomic writes to avoid duplicates.
Use DLQs
Quarantine poison messages and surface them for manual handling.
Align partitions & scaling
Increase partitions for parallelism; scale consumers to match.
Monitor key signals
Backlog, consumer lag, DLQ growth, and processing latency.
Keep these six reminders front of mind when proposing a queue: primitives, guarantees, idempotency, DLQs, partitions, and monitoring.
