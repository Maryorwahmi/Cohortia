Offset
A sequential identifier for messages within a partition; consumers commit offsets to track progress.
Consumer Group
A named collection of consumers that divide partitions among members for parallel processing.
Replication & Zero-Copy
Replication makes copies of partitions for durability. Zero-copy moves data efficiently from disk to network to reduce CPU overhead.
Final takeaway
Keep these operational concepts in mind when designing pipelines and discussing trade-offs in interviews or architecture reviews.
