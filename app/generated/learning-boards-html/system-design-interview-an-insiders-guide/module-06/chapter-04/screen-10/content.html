Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › Scaling Kafka and Operational Considerations
Scale strategy
Add brokers to increase cluster capacity and add partitions to increase per-topic parallelism. Balance partition count with metadata overhead.
Replication & SLAs
Choose replication factor and min-insync-replicas to meet durability SLAs during maintenance and failures.
Monitoring
Watch consumer lag, under-replicated partitions, broker resource usage, and controller health for safe scaling.
Common operational mistakes
  • Creating too many partitions per topic without need.
  • Poor key design producing hot partitions.
  • Not setting appropriate replication and in-sync replica settings before maintenance.
Operational example
For a large IoT pipeline, partition by region or sensor bucket, set replication to three, and run multiple consumer groups so analytics jobs can process independently.
