Why this matters

Systems that need durable, replayable streams and parallel processing use Kafka to separate ingestion from many downstream consumers. This reduces coupling and enables independent scaling and recovery.

Example
A billing pipeline uses Kafka to record every payment event; analytics and fraud services each consume the same stream at their own pace.
