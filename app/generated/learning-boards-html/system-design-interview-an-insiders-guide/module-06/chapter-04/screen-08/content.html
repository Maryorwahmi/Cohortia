Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › Replication, Leaders, and KRaft vs Zookeeper
How replication works
Each partition has replicas across brokers. The leader serves clients and followers replicate. A new leader is elected if the current leader fails.
Replication factor
Choose 2 or 3 depending on durability needs and cluster size; more replicas increase availability but cost disk and network.
Controller and metadata
Controllers manage cluster metadata and leader election. Older Kafka used Zookeeper; newer versions adopt KRaft, simplifying deployment by internalizing metadata management.
Operational advice
Monitor under-replicated partitions, broker availability, and controller health. During maintenance, ensure your replication factor and min-insync-replicas settings keep data protected.
Common mistake
Too many partitions with low replication can increase metadata overhead and recovery time; choose a balanced partition and replication strategy for your SLA.
