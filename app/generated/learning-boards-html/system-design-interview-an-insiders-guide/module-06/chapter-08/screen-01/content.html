Interactive 16:9 Masterclass

Error Handling, Monitoring, and Reliability

Build asynchronous pipelines that tolerate transient faults, surface persistent problems, and recover without human triage for every failure.

In this lesson
You will practice retries with exponential backoff, moving bad messages to dead-letter queues, applying circuit breakers to slow or failing dependencies, and instrumenting queues and workers for real observability.
Why it matters
Asynchronous systems run out of sight. Without clear error handling and monitoring you’ll discover problems only after customers are affected. These patterns make failures diagnosable and limit blast radius.
