Chapter 6.8 › Key Concepts
Retry Mechanism
Re-attempt a failed operation to recover from transient errors.
Exponential Backoff
Increase delays between retries exponentially to reduce load on failing services.
Dead-Letter Queue (DLQ)
A quarantine for messages that repeatedly fail so they can be inspected and replayed safely.
Summary

These three ideas help you recover from temporary faults, isolate permanent failures, and avoid making recoverable problems worse.
