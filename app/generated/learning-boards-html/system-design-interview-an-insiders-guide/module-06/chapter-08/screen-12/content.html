Chapter 6.8 › Operational Pitfalls
Common mistakes
  • Blind retries that do not classify errors
  • DLQs that are unmonitored and unprocessed
  • Missing correlation IDs making tracing impossible
  • Premature over-engineering of complex protections
Safety notes
Protect brokers with authentication and authorization. Back up metadata and test recovery procedures. Make DLQ handling part of your runbook and monitor it like any other alarm.
Warning

Treat a rapidly growing DLQ as a production incident. It often signals data quality or downstream outages requiring immediate attention.
