Chapter 6.8 › Circuit Breaker
States
  • Closed: calls pass through normally while failures are tracked.
  • Open: calls fail fast or return fallback responses when failure rate is high.
  • Half-open: a limited number of test calls probe for recovery before closing.
Misconception check
A circuit breaker is not a replacement for retries. Use both: retries for transient single-call issues, circuit breakers to prevent repeated waste when the downstream service remains unhealthy.
Example flow
An order processor calls a payment service. After a burst of payment failures, the circuit opens. Subsequent payment attempts immediately receive a retryable response and the order is queued for later processing, preserving worker availability and reducing traffic to the payment service.
Key Takeaway

Circuit breakers protect your system by failing fast and enabling graceful degraded behavior while a dependency recovers.
