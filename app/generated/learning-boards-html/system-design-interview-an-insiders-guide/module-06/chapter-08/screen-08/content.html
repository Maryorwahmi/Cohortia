Chapter 6.8 › Observability
Key signals
  • Queue Depth / Consumer Lag
  • Message Production & Consumption Rates
  • Consumer Processing Latency and Error Rates
  • Worker and Broker Health (CPU, memory, disk I/O)
Tools & practices
Use a metrics backend and dashboards for alerting, a log aggregator for contextual exploration, and distributed tracing to reconstruct cross-service flows. Ensure correlation IDs are propagated in message headers and logs to connect metrics, logs, and traces.
Key Takeaway

Combine metrics, logs, and traces; each tells a different part of the story. Correlate them with IDs to make debugging efficient.
