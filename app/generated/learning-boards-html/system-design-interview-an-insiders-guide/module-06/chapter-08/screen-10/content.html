Chapter 6.8 › Broker HA & DR
Strategies
  • Cluster brokers for redundancy and load distribution.
  • Replicate messages across nodes for durability.
  • Deploy across zones or regions for disaster resilience.
Trade-offs
Synchronous replication increases safety but adds latency. Asynchronous replication improves throughput but may risk data loss in a crash.
Operational checklist
  • Automate leader election and failover where possible.
  • Back up broker metadata and configuration regularly.
  • Test failover and recovery runbooks periodically.
Key Takeaway

Design brokers for redundancy and rehearsed recovery. Know the consistency and latency trade-offs for your application’s needs.
