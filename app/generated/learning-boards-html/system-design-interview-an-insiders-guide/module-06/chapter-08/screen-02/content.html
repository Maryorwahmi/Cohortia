Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Learning Objectives
01
Robust error handling
Retries with exponential backoff and dead-letter queues
Design retry policies that distinguish transient from permanent failures. Use exponential backoff to avoid thundering retries and DLQs to isolate poison messages for manual inspection.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Circuit breaker pattern
Prevent cascading failures and enable graceful degradation
Implement open, closed, and half-open logic and plan fallbacks so a failing downstream service doesn’t consume your resources.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Monitoring & observability
Collect metrics, logs, and traces for background work
Capture queue depth, processing latency, and error rates. Use metrics and traces to infer system health and to troubleshoot invisible flows.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
High availability & DR
Clustering, replication, and zone redundancy for brokers
Plan for broker replication and multi-zone deployments and ensure metadata backup so you can recover after catastrophic failures.
