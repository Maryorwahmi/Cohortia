Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Operations
Avoid leaks
Use auto-delete or TTL for temporary queues and exchanges created by tests or ephemeral consumers.
Acknowledgements
Prefer manual ack and idempotent consumers to ensure at-least-once semantics without silent loss.
Durability
Set queues durable and publish persistent messages when you need recovery across broker restarts.
Final thought
Explain both theory and runbook
In interviews, pair your design with operational safeguards: acknowledgments, TTL, monitoring, and naming conventions for routing keys.
