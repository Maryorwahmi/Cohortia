RabbitMQ & ActiveMQ
AMQP and JMS brokers for routing, integration, and reliable delivery

These brokers insert an explicit routing layer between producers and queues. That layer enables exact-match, broadcast, and pattern-based delivery. They are optimized for per-message delivery guarantees, flexible bindings, and enterprise protocol support.
