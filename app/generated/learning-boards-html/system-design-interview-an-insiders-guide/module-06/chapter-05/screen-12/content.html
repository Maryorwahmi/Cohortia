Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Queues & Acks
Queue flags
Durable: Survives broker restart when combined with persistent messages.
Exclusive: Tied to single connection; deleted on disconnect.
Auto-delete: Removed when last consumer disconnects; avoid orphan queues.
Acknowledgements
auto_ack=True: Simpler but unsafe for critical processing.
manual ack: Call basic_ack after success for at-least-once delivery.
Idempotency: Required when consumers may receive duplicates.
Operational tip: use TTL and lifecycle rules to prevent queue/exchange buildup from dynamic tests.
