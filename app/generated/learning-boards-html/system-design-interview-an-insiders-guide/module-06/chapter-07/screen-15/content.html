Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Practical Checklist
Assume duplicates
Design for at-least-once and never rely on a single delivery attempt.
Unique id & atomicity
Make the id the authoritative record and write it atomically with the side effect.
Retention & storage
Balance TTL and cost; choose durable storage aligned with retry windows.
Final thought
Communicate boundaries clearly: exactly-once inside a system can be real, but end-to-end you will rely on idempotency and atomic writes for correctness.
