Feasible boundary
Broker-internal transactions
Systems like Kafka can offer transactional guarantees between producers and consumers inside their ecosystem. That helps but does not remove the need for idempotent consumers when external systems are involved.
Why end-to-end is hard
  • External services are often not transactional with your broker.
  • Distributed transactions are heavy and reduce availability.
  • Network partitions make global consensus impractical for many workflows.
Practical approach

Prefer at-least-once plus idempotent consumers; state and atomic writes give effective single processing in most cases.
