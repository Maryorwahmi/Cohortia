Idempotency and Exactly-Once Processing
How to prevent duplicate side effects when messages or retries reappear, and why true exactly-once is a heavier guarantee.
In this lesson
You will learn idempotent patterns, safe check-then-process flows, and practical limits of exactly-once semantics.
Why it matters
A duplicate side effect can mean double charges, inconsistent inventory, or duplicate emails. Idempotency prevents these visible errors.
Explore Key Topics
Idempotency Keys
Persistent unique IDs used to detect duplicates.
Atomic Check+Write
Combine the check and the write in one transaction to avoid partial commits.
Exactly-Once Limits
Exactly-once claims are usually bounded to a specific system boundary.
Concept Map
Idempotency keys → atomic store → idempotent consumer
Visualize each message carrying a unique id. The consumer uses that id to decide whether to act. The action and the recording of the id must happen together to avoid duplicates.
