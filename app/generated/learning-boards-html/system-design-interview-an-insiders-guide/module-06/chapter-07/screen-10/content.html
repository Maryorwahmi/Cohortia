Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Retention & Storage
Timeline: retention window
Map storage lifetime to business retry windows. Example buckets: 24 hours for UI retries, 7–30 days for asynchronous systems, months for financial records that require auditability.
Store options
  • Relational DB table with unique key and TTL or archival.
  • Durable Redis with persistence for faster lookup.
  • Append-only log or object store for audit trails.
Key scope
Decide if keys are global, per-producer, or per-user. Narrower scopes reduce storage but complicate uniqueness across producers.
Key Takeaway

Document your retention policy and choose storage that meets durability and cost needs for your retry model.
