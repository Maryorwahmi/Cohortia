Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › When idempotency fails
Problem
Irreversible or unique external effects
If an operation creates something globally unique or irreversible, naive retries cannot be made idempotent without redesigning the flow or getting idempotency support from the external system.
Solutions
  • Decouple bookkeeping and irreversible action with a reliable command table.
  • Use external APIs that accept idempotency keys for the irreversible step.
  • Design compensating transactions and auditing for manual reconciliation.
