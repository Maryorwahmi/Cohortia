Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Retries & Exponential Backoff
Retries — safe patterns
Use retries for transient errors and use exponential backoff to avoid creating retry storms that overwhelm dependencies.
Strategy
Start small, increase delays exponentially, and cap total retries.
Implementation
Use broker delay queues, visibility timeouts, or separate retry queues with delay semantics.
When to stop
Move messages to a DLQ after configured attempts for investigation and manual remediation.
Example timeline
Attempt 1
Process immediately; if it fails, schedule first retry after 1s.
Attempt 2
If failure persists, schedule next retry after 5s.
Attempt N
After configured attempts, move to DLQ for operator investigation.
Key Takeaway

Retries help transient failures recover; use exponential backoff and DLQs to avoid thrashing and to surface persistent faults.
