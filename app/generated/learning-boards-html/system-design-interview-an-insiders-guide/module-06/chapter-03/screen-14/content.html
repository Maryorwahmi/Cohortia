Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Leaderboard Design
Scenario: leaderboard under spike
Enqueue score events and let the leaderboard service pull at a sustainable rate. Add autoscaling driven by queue depth.
Why queues help
They prevent floods of write traffic from overloading the leaderboard store.
Idempotency
Ensure updates are idempotent so retries or replays do not double-count scores.
Operational plan
Scaling
Scale consumers horizontally based on queue depth and processing lag.
Fault handling
Retries with backoff; after limit, move to DLQ for investigation.
Reprocessing
Re-enqueue DLQ items after fixes with dedup safeguards to avoid double updates.
Key Takeaway

Combine load leveling, autoscaling, idempotency, and DLQs to keep leaderboards responsive and recoverable under bursts.
