Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Retries
Retry transient failures with backoff to avoid creating more load on a failing service.
Dead-Letter Queue
Store repeatedly failing messages for investigation and safe reprocessing.
Poison Pill
A message that repeatedly causes failures; isolate it to prevent blocking the queue.
Summary: combine retry strategies, DLQs, idempotency, and observability to maintain throughput and enable safe recovery when things go wrong.
