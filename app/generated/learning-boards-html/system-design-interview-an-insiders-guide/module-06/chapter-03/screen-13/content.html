Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Monitoring & Recovery
Monitoring essentials
Key metrics: queue depth, processing rate, processing latency, and DLQ counts. Alerts should be actionable and lead to scaling or investigation steps.
Alerting
Trigger on sustained depth increases or DLQ spikes, not single transient blips.
Tracing
Attach correlation IDs so messages can be followed end-to-end across services.
Poison pills and recovery
Use retries with backoff, then DLQ for persistent failures. Inspect DLQ entries, fix root causes, and reprocess when safe.
Investigation
Review payload, error traces, and environment differences to diagnose why a message fails repeatedly.
Recovery options
Fix code and re-enqueue, or archive and document if unrecoverable. Keep an audit trail for compliance and debugging.
Key Takeaway

Observability and DLQs make queues operationally safe: you can detect stuck messages and recover without losing throughput.
