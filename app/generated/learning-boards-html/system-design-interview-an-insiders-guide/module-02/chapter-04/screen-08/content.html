Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Eventual Consistency
Core claim

If updates stop, replicas will converge to the same value. Systems remain available during partitions and reconcile later.

Pros
High availability and low latency across geography.
Cons
Temporary stale reads and conflict resolution responsibilities for the app.
Key takeaway

Use eventual consistency when uptime and latency matter more than immediate global visibility.

Concrete example
Client A writes 'Hello' to a message board.
Client A reads immediately and sees 'Hello'.
Client B may read old state until replication completes, but eventually sees 'Hello'.
This pattern keeps writers responsive and converges when updates stop.
Misconception
Eventual does not mean unacceptable delays for user-critical data; choose submodels or stronger guarantees where needed.
