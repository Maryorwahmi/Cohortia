Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Remember
Partition Tolerance
Design assuming partitions will occur and choose behavior for them.
Weakest sufficient consistency
Pick the minimal guarantee that preserves correctness to improve latency and availability.
Reconciliation plan
State how the system converges: CRDTs, dedupe, or conflict resolution.
Final takeaway

Explain your design choices by referencing user-visible behavior during partitions and the concrete reconciliation technique you will use.
