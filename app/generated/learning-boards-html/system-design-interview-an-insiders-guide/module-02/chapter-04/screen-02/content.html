Learning Objectives
Four competencies you will verify during practice.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain data consistency
Define what it means for clients to see the same data across replicas and why inconsistencies create wrong behavior. This matters because correctness often depends on these guarantees.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Articulate the CAP theorem
Explain why partition tolerance is inevitable and how to reason about choosing consistency or availability during partitions using real examples.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Differentiate strong & eventual
State the semantics of linearizability and sequential consistency, and list eventual consistency variants and when they are sufficient.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Analyze trade-offs
Evaluate trade-offs between latency, availability, and correctness for features like counters, feeds, and transactional systems, and pick reconciliation strategies.
