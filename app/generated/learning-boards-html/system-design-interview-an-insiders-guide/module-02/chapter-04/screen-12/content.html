Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Like Feature Decision
Step 1 — Choose model for Like count
High availability suggests eventual consistency. Use a grow-only counter CRDT or locally applied increments with deterministic reconciliation. Ensure the user's own interface shows the increment immediately by applying read-your-writes.
Potential issue
During partition two replicas may both count the like, requiring deduplication on merge.
Step 2 — Choose model for Liked-posts list
Per-user correctness matters: provide read-your-writes so the liker sees their update, and monotonic reads so subsequent views do not regress. Eventual global convergence is acceptable for others.
Reconciliation
Use idempotent writes, dedupe identifiers, and merge order-insensitive data to avoid duplicate likes after healing.
Interview tip
When answering, name the model, justify it with CAP reasoning and user expectations, and explain the reconciliation plan for partitions.
