Common mistakes and quick fixes
A short timeline for design decisions and checks.
Mistake
Enforcing strong consistency across all data.
Fix: classify data by invariants first.
Mistake
Ignoring per-client guarantees and routing strategies.
Fix: add read-your-writes or sticky session routing where UX requires it.
Mistake
No reconciliation plan after partitions.
Fix: design deterministic merges or require reconciliation steps.
Design checklist: 1) Identify invariants. 2) Choose model per invariant. 3) Add per-client guarantees as needed. 4) Plan reconciliation and tests for partitions.
