Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › CP vs AP
CP systems (Consistency + Partition Tolerance)
  • Refuse operations when quorum is not reachable to preserve global correctness.
  • Users may see errors or unavailability in minority partitions.
  • Appropriate for financial or inventory systems with strict invariants.
Example
A bank refusing a transfer rather than risking inconsistent balances.
AP systems (Availability + Partition Tolerance)
  • Keep serving requests even with lost communication to some replicas.
  • May return stale data; conflicts are reconciled after partition heals.
  • Suited for social feeds, catalogs, and services prioritizing uptime.
Example
A news feed that accepts posts on both sides of a partition and merges them later.
Check your reasoning
When proposing CP or AP in an interview, explain the user-visible outcome during partitions and the reconciliation path after recovery.
