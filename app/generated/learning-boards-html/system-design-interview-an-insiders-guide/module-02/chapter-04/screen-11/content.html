What CRDTs buy you
CRDTs allow concurrent updates to different replicas without coordination and still ensure a deterministic merged state. They are useful when availability and local responsiveness are priorities and when merge rules can represent the domain semantics.
Counters
Merge by summing per-replica increments.
Sets
Use deterministic union and additional metadata for removals.
Registers
Choose a merge policy that reflects application intent.
Design note
Pick CRDTs only when you can express the correct merged outcome deterministically; otherwise choose reconciliation or stronger consistency.
Key takeaway

CRDTs remove conflicts by design when suitable merge rules reflect desired semantics.
