Active-Active: trade-offs
High availability and capacity for stateless, high coordination cost for stateful.
Benefits
No single active node, seamless capacity growth, fast failover since other nodes continue handling traffic.
Challenges for stateful systems
Concurrent writes require reconciliation, risk of conflicts, and often need consensus protocols like Paxos or Raft, which add complexity and latency.
Practical guidance
Prefer stateless service design, partition state by key, or accept the complexity and latency of distributed consensus when active-active is required for stateful workloads.
