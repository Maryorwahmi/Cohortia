State target
Always quantify availability or reliability targets before proposing a design.
Pattern selection
Match active-active to stateless services and active-passive to stateful writes unless you explain conflict resolution.
Operational proof
Show how MTBF and MTTR or replication choices map to the downtime budget and describe failover tests.
Final thought

When you design for uptime, be explicit about trade-offs: cost, latency, data loss risk, and the operational practices that keep your design reliable in production.
