Module X › Chapter Y › Key Concepts
Availability
Proportion of time a system is functional and accessible to users.
Fault Tolerance
Ability to continue operating despite component failures, typically via redundancy and graceful degradation.
Nines & MTBF/MTTR
Availability targets translated into downtime budgets; MTBF and MTTR are operational levers to meet them.
Summary Takeaway

Design with redundancy and recovery in mind, quantify targets with nines and MTBF/MTTR, and always test failover to avoid surprises.
