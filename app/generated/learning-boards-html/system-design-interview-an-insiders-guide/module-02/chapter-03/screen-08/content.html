Data Replication Modes
Synchronous vs asynchronous, and hybrid approaches.
Synchronous Replication
Primary waits for at least one follower to acknowledge the write before replying to the client. Stronger durability, higher write latency.
Asynchronous Replication
Primary acknowledges immediately and followers catch up later. Lower latency, possible small data-loss window on primary failure.
Hybrid example
Synchronous replication to one follower for critical durability, asynchronous to additional followers for speed and geographic redundancy.
Interview tip
Always state the expected data-loss window for asynchronous replication and how promotion of a follower will be handled during failover.
