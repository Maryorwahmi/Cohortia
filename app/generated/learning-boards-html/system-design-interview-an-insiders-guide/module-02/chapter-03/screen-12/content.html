Nines, MTBF, and MTTR
Convert targets into downtime budgets and operational actions.
Nines reference
99% (two nines) ≈ 3.65 days/year; 99.9% (three nines) ≈ 8.76 hours/year; 99.99% (four nines) ≈ 52.56 minutes/year; 99.999% (five nines) ≈ 5.26 minutes/year.
MTBF and MTTR
MTBF is average uptime between failures; MTTR is average recovery time. Availability ≈ MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR).
Practice calculation
To reach three nines, allowable downtime is 0.1% of a year, or 525.6 minutes. Propose lowering MTTR via automation and failover testing as an efficient path to higher availability when failures are frequent.
