Step-by-step

Design a sharding plan

1. Choose a key
user_id for point lookups; salt or hash if monotonic to avoid clustering.
2. Strategy
Hash-based sharding with consistent hashing or virtual nodes to ease rebalancing.
3. Range queries
Route analytics through a separate index or aggregation pipeline to avoid scatter-gather for frequent ranges.
4. Migration plan
Online copy-and-switch with routing updates, throttled copy, and monitoring to ensure consistency and minimal impact.
