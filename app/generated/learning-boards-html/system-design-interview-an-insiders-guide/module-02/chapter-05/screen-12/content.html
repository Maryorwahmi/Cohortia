Operational trade-offs

Recognize common mistakes early and design mitigations into your sharding plan.

Bad sharding key
Low-cardinality or sequential keys concentrate writes and reads. Mitigate with hashing, salts, or alternate keys.
Over-sharding
Sharding before it's needed increases complexity; start simple and shard when bottlenecks appear.
Cross-shard queries
Joins across shards are expensive. Consider denormalization or redesigning queries to be single-shard.
Practical mitigations
  • Use consistent hashing or virtual nodes.
  • Monitor shard size and latency to trigger splits.
  • Cache or denormalize to reduce cross-shard loads.
Interview tip
When proposing a sharding plan, explain the trade-offs, how you'll detect hot spots, and your migration path for rebalancing.
Key Takeaway

Design choices that ease operations are as important as raw distribution. Plan for growth and monitoring from day one.
