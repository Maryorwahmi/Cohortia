Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Remember
Pick a stable key
Align the key with common lookups and ensure even cardinality.
Plan rebalancing
Use consistent hashing or controlled online migrations to reduce movement and downtime.
Operational readiness
Monitor shard sizes and latency, and automate migration and testing before spikes occur.
Final takeaway

Sharding is powerful but operationally demanding. Balance distribution goals with practical migration and monitoring plans to keep systems healthy as they scale.
