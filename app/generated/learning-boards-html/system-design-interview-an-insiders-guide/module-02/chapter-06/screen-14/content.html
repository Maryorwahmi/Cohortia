Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Key Concepts
ACID Properties
Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability—guarantees within a single system that require coordination across services.
Distributed Transaction
A transaction spanning multiple independent services or databases requiring global coordination to commit.
Two‑Phase Commit (2PC)
Coordinator asks participants to prepare and then decides commit or abort; ensures atomicity but can block and bottleneck.
Summary
Use these concepts to explain how different protocols preserve or trade away guarantees, and to justify pattern choices in interviews or designs.
