Learning Objectives
A competency-driven checklist for interview-ready explanations and designs.
Bloom
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain ACID and distributed challenges
Define atomicity, consistency, isolation, durability and why networked services complicate them.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe Two‑Phase Commit
Walk through prepare and commit phases, voting semantics, and the coordinator/participant roles.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Illustrate the Saga pattern
Sequence local transactions and compensating steps to reach eventual consistency across services.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply concurrency controls
Choose between pessimistic locks and optimistic control using version numbers, and anticipate deadlocks and race conditions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Why these objectives
Interviewers expect you to explain trade‑offs and propose concrete patterns. These objectives give you the vocabulary and the ability to map design choices to availability and consistency outcomes.
Mnemonic
Vote → Decide (2PC)
Step → Compensate (Saga)
Lock → Block (Pessimistic)
Version → Retry (Optimistic)
