Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Limitations of 2PC
Side-by-side: 2PC vs Requirements
Guarantee
Strong atomicity across participants
Cost
Blocking, coordinator dependency, higher latency
Availability
Low when coordinator or participants fail
Scalability
Poor as participant count or transaction rate grows
Recovery complexity
Handling coordinator crashes requires persistent logs, leader election, or manual intervention to unstick participants—adding operational burden.
Concrete example
If the coordinator crashes after sending PREPARE but before GLOBAL_COMMIT, participants that voted commit remain locked. Inventory operations may be blocked, preventing other customers from purchasing the same items.
When 2PC still fits
Use 2PC when strict cross-service atomicity is required and the transaction scope is small with low concurrency, and when you can tolerate operational complexity.
Key Takeaway

2PC is correct for atomicity but expensive in availability and operations; prefer Saga when you need scale and resilience.
