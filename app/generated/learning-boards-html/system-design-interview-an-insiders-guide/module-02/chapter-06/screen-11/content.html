Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Concurrency Risks
Race conditions
When operations interleave unpredictably, final state depends on timing. Example: two buyers both see stock = 1 and both decrement, causing oversell. Detection requires coordination or conflict checks.
Deadlocks
Circular waits where each transaction waits for resources held by another. In distributed systems, detection and resolution require timeouts, lock ordering, or deadlock detection services.
Practical advice
  • Use lock ordering to avoid circular waits when using pessimistic locks.
  • Use timeouts and retries to recover from potential deadlocks in distributed locks.
  • Prefer optimistic control when conflicts are rare; revert and retry on conflict.
Key Takeaway

Map resource dependencies and choose concurrency controls and recovery strategies appropriate to conflict frequency and criticality.
