Key challenges
Four operational areas demand special attention.
Complexity
Multiple services require orchestration, deployments, and coordination policies.
Consistency
Maintaining a single correct state across replicas often forces trade-offs.
Coordination
Leader election, consensus, and transactions introduce algorithmic complexity.
Monitoring & Debugging
Tracing across services, aggregated logs, and service-level metrics are essential, and must be built in.
Concrete example: decomposed order pipeline
If you split order creation, inventory, and billing into separate services without distributed tracing and retries, diagnosing failures will require manual log correlation and lengthy incident response.
Mitigation checklist
  • Introduce tracing, centralized logging, and dashboards before large-scale decomposition.
  • Adopt explicit retry and idempotency patterns to handle transient network failures.
  • Use consensus services only where necessary; prefer simpler coordination when possible.
Common mistake
Refactoring for distribution without operational practices leads to fragile systems and slower incident recovery.
