Scalability: Vertical vs Horizontal
Understand when to scale a machine and when to scale a fleet, and how load balancing and auto-scaling keep services resilient.

Scalability means adding resources without harming performance. Vertical scaling upgrades a single node's CPU, memory, or storage. Horizontal scaling adds more identical instances and distributes traffic.

Horizontal scaling is preferred for large, cloud-native systems because it avoids single points of failure and enables zero-downtime deployments.

In this lesson: load balancers, L4 vs L7 differences, auto-scaling, health checks, and designing stateless services.
