Centralize policies
Simplify clients and enforce consistent auth and rate limits.
Design for HA
Avoid SPOF with multiple instances, load balancers, and health checks.
Choose patterns wisely
Select shared aggregation, BFFs, or managed solutions based on client needs and team capabilities.
Closing thought

When you recommend an API Gateway, pair the benefits with a realistic mitigation plan for availability and latency. That balance is what interviewers want to hear.
