Learning Objectives

Four verified milestones to demonstrate competency with API Gateways.

01
Explain necessity & core functions

Describe why a gateway is used and show examples like an e-commerce mobile app that benefits from a single entry point. Understand routing and policy centralization.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify gateway architecture components

Name and explain the request router, policy and transformation engines, service discovery integration, and logging agents that make a gateway complete.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Discuss centralized policies and benefits

Explain centralized authentication, rate limiting, caching, and aggregation and how they simplify microservice design and improve observability.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Analyze risks & mitigations

Recognize SPOF, latency, complexity, and team bottlenecks; propose redundancy, load balancers, health checks, and self-service configs as mitigations.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
