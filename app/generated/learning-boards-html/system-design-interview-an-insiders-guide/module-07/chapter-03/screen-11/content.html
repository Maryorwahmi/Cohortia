Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Risks & Mitigations
Definition

Primary risks

  • Single Point of Failure (SPOF)
  • Increased latency
  • Operational complexity & dev bottleneck
  • Cost and maintenance burden
Mitigation patterns
High availability
Multiple instances across zones, load balancer fronting them, automated health checks and auto-scaling.
Keep gateway light
Avoid heavy CPU work in the gateway; delegate heavy composition to backend or BFFs when appropriate.
Operational practices
Policy-as-code, self-service routing, and thorough monitoring prevent configuration bottlenecks.
Key Takeaway

Mitigate gateway risk through redundancy and disciplined operational practices; treat the gateway as critical infrastructure that must be tested and monitored.
