Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Authorization Service
Purpose

Central policy management and decisions

Services call the authorization service with attributes and receive allow/deny decisions. Centralization aids consistency and auditing.

Design for availability: consider local caches, sidecars, and request timeouts to avoid cascading failures.

Example flow
  1. Client -> Gateway authenticates and forwards user context.
  2. Service gathers attributes, queries authorization service.
  3. Authorization service evaluates policy and responds allow/deny.
  4. Service enforces decision and logs outcome for audit.
Design tip
Cache positive decisions for short periods and always log authorization failures for forensics.
