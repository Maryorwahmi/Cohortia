Edge (Gateway)
Authenticate, validate tokens, and perform broad role or scope checks.
Service (Owner)
Make final, resource-specific authorization decisions using local data.
Authorization Service
Centralized policy evaluation for complex or shared rules; design for caching and resilience.
Final takeaway
Combine layers for defense in depth, keep policies configurable, and enforce least privilege everywhere.
