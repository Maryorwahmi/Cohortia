Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Least Privilege
Least Privilege
Grant only the permissions necessary for tasks. Example: a reporting service that only reads data should never hold write permissions.
Key Takeaway
Minimize blast radius by restricting rights and using role templates or attribute constraints instead of global powers.
Separation of Concerns
Keep authentication, authorization, and business logic separate to simplify updates and audits. Use dedicated modules or services for policy evaluation.
Pro Tip
Design clear interfaces: services call authorization APIs or libraries and treat responses as authoritative for enforcement.
