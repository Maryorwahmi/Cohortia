Comparison
Offset-based
Query with offset and limit. Simple to understand but can be slow with large offsets and inconsistent under concurrent modifications.
GET /products?offset=20&limit=10
Use for admin pages or small collections where deep paging is uncommon.
Comparison
Cursor-based
Use a cursor referencing a stable sort order. Efficient for large datasets and resilient to inserts and deletes.
GET /products?after=SKU123&limit=5
Requires a consistent ordering key such as createdAt or a unique composite key.
Key Takeaway

Pick offset for simplicity and cursor for scale and correctness; always validate and document the chosen scheme.
