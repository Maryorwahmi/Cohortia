Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Resilience Patterns
Retries with backoff & jitter
Exponential backoff increases delay between retries to reduce load on recovering services.
Jitter randomizes delays to avoid synchronized retries from many clients.
Bound retries and retry only idempotent operations or use idempotency keys.
Circuit breaker lifecycle
Closed: normal operation until failure threshold exceeded.
Open: calls fail fast for a timeout period to protect resources.
Half‑Open: allow limited test requests to determine recovery.
Practical tip
Combine retries and circuit breakers: continue retries for transient errors while the circuit is closed, but let the breaker open if the dependency shows persistent failures.
