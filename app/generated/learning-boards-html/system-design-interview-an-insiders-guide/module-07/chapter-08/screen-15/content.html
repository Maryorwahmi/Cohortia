Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Actionable Reminders
1. Contract-first
Keep an OpenAPI spec as your authoritative contract and use it for docs and codegen.
2. Observe early
Instrument traces, logs, and metrics from day one to make incidents diagnosable.
3. Fail safely
Use bounded retries with backoff and circuit breakers to prevent cascading failures.
Use these reminders as a concise checklist when designing APIs for production or explaining your choices during interviews.
