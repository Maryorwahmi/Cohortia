Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Learning Objectives
Hands-on activity
Design a webhook registration endpoint, sketch the delivery flow, and produce a sample JSON payload. Practice explaining how you sign payloads and handle failures.
Checklist
  • Define endpoints and request/response shapes.
  • Describe security: secrets, signing, verification.
  • Design retries, backoff, and dead-letter handling.
  • Instrument for observability with correlation IDs.
Interview checkpoints
Explain fan-out choices and where to use caching versus precomputed feeds.
Justify retry policies and identify idempotency requirements.
Describe observability signals to prove system health and latency.
