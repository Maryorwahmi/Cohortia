Event Delivery
Choose polling, webhooks, or SSE by client capability and flow direction.
OpenAPI
Use an OpenAPI spec as the single source of truth for endpoints and payloads.
Observability & Resilience
Instrument traces, logs, and metrics; apply retries, backoff, jitter, and circuit breakers.
Summary
These concepts form a practical checklist you can use to design, document, observe, and harden APIs for production and interview discussions.
