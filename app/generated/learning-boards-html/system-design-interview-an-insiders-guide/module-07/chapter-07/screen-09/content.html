HTTPS/TLS Everywhere
Encrypt external and internal API traffic and automate certificate management.

Encrypting traffic prevents eavesdropping, tampering, and replay attacks. Even internal networks must use TLS because compromise can occur anywhere.

  • Requirements: TLS 1.2 or 1.3 and strong cipher suites.
  • Operational practice: automate certificate issuance and renewal and alert on failures.
  • Consider mutual TLS for high-assurance service-to-service authentication.
Pro Tip
Automate certificate renewal and include health checks that alert before expiry to avoid outages.
Common mistake
Assuming internal traffic can be plaintext because the network is trusted; adopt assume-breach thinking.
Certificate strategy
Use short-lived certificates when possible and automated provisioning systems to rotate them without downtime.
Log certificate issuance and renewal events and alert on unexpected issuer changes or approaching expiry.
Enforce hostname verification and reject self-signed or untrusted certificates from clients.
Key Takeaway

Encryption and certificate hygiene are operational responsibilities; treat them as code and monitor them closely.
