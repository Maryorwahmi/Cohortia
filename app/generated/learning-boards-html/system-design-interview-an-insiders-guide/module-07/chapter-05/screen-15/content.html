Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Key Concepts — Part 2
No sensitive payloads
JWT payloads are visible; do not store secrets there.
Enforce HTTPS
Prevent credentials and tokens from being intercepted in transit.
Validate everything
Check signature, issuer, audience, and expiration before accepting a token.
Final takeaway
In interviews, pair protocol knowledge with concrete security patterns: token validation, secure storage, and revocation. That demonstrates both conceptual and practical mastery.
