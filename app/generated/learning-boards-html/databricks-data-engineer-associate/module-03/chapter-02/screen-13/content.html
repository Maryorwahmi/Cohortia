Safety Notes
Common Mistakes to Avoid
Simple rules to keep pipelines robust and scalable.
Don't use collect
Use show or write; collect can OOM the driver when the DataFrame is large.
Careful with joins
Consider broadcasting small tables and validate join results to avoid cartesian surprises.
Nullability matters
Declare nullable correctly in explicit schemas to prevent runtime errors and unexpected nulls.
Pro tip
Call explain to inspect physical and logical plans before triggering expensive actions.
Checklist
Explicit schema, filter invalid rows, alias aggregates, avoid collect, and validate join cardinality.
