Architecture Overview

From query to optimized execution

Catalyst rewrites and optimizes your logical plan. Tungsten executes the resulting physical plan with CPU and memory-focused optimizations. Understanding both helps you interpret explain output and choose effective hints.
# Inspect plans
df.explain(True)
What to look for
  • Is a BroadcastHashJoin present for small-table joins?
  • Are filters pushed down to data sources?
  • Is whole-stage codegen enabled for CPU-efficient execution?
Learner prompt

After making a change, run explain(True) and ask: did the plan change in the way I expected? If not, reconsider hints or transformations.
