Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Caching & Persistence
Store intermediate results to avoid recomputation across multiple uses; unpersist when done.
Repartition vs Broadcast
Repartition reshuffles data; broadcast sends a small table to all executors to avoid shuffling the large one.
Catalyst & Tungsten
Catalyst optimizes plans; Tungsten executes them efficiently. Use explain to observe both layers.
Final takeaway

Combine precise analytic transforms with targeted optimization tactics and always verify with explain and small performance tests before applying changes at scale.
