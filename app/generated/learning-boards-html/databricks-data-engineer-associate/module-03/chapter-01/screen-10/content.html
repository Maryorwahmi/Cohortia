Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Databricks Integration
Managed cluster benefits
  • Provisioning and autoscaling reduce operational overhead.
  • Preconfigured SparkSession in notebooks accelerates exploration.
  • Integrated storage connectors simplify reading and writing data formats.
What you still control
  • Executor cores and memory sizing to match parallelism needs.
  • Partitioning strategies to avoid skew and stragglers.
  • Use of caching and persistence to reduce repeated work.
Scenario
Autoscaling can help with burst loads but does not correct poorly balanced partitions. Tune partitions and use joins or repartitioning strategies to reduce skew.
