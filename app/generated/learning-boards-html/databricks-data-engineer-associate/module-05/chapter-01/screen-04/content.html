Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › What is a Databricks Workflow
Definition

Databricks Workflows

A managed service to run and schedule non-interactive jobs such as notebooks, scripts, and jars with observable runs and logs.

Why it matters
Automation reduces manual steps, enforces consistency, and enables cost control through ephemeral job clusters.
Misconception
Jobs are more than scheduled notebooks; they include compute selection, logging, and retry behavior that affect reliability.
Key Takeaway

Treat workflows as production artifacts: define tasks, pick appropriate compute, schedule, and monitor their runs.

Analogy & Example
Orchestra analogy
Each task is an instrument; the workflow schedules and runs them on the right compute at the right time.
Concrete scenario
A notebook that cleans customer CSVs, standardizes fields, and overwrites a Delta table once per day.
Check your understanding
How would you prevent duplicate writes if a job retries after a transient failure?
