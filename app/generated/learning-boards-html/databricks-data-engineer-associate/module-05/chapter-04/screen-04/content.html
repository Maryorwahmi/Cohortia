Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What is an Expectation?
Definition

Expectation

A named rule attached to a table that asserts a condition over rows. Expectations are evaluated during pipeline updates, and DLT records outcomes for each rule.

Example
Require that customer_id is not null to protect joins and counts.
Misconception
Expectations are not a substitute for fixing source systems; they detect and contain issues while remediation occurs.
Why it matters
Checks run earlier reduce downstream debugging time and improve trust in analytic outputs.
Visual
Analogy
Expectations are gatekeepers — they check passports before travellers board the plane.
Gatekeepers may deny boarding, escort a traveller to quarantine, or let them pass with a warning. Expectations behave similarly for rows.
Key takeaway

Use expectations to ensure critical fields remain valid and to make quality issues visible and actionable.
