Fail for invariants
Protect core identifiers and correctness-critical fields with fail.
Warn then escalate
Observe with warn before switching to drop or fail to avoid surprises.
Monitor & remediate
Track metrics and use event logs to find and fix sources of bad data.
Final takeaway
Design expectations deliberately, measure outcomes, and iterate to improve data integrity without sacrificing availability.
