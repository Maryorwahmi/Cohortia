What is Databricks?

A managed, unified platform built on Apache Spark that brings compute, storage, collaboration, and ML tools together.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Workspace
Collaborative UI for notebooks, jobs, and data browsing.
Clusters
Managed Spark compute with autoscaling and termination.
Delta Lake
ACID storage layer for reliable data pipelines.

Why this matters

Teams often waste time wiring different systems for compute, storage, and ML. Databricks reduces that overhead, enabling reproducible pipelines and quicker experimentation.

Faster
Prototype to production quicker.
Safer
Centralized governance and secrets help protect data.
Unified
One platform for engineering, analytics, and ML.
