Scenario

Streaming IoT plus historical joins

Ingest telemetry into Delta, join with customer records, and serve both dashboards and model training from the same curated tables.

Ingest with validation and partitions for performance.
Use Delta transactions to support concurrent streaming and reads.
Provide time travel for audits and reproducible experiments.
Design checklist
  • Validate critical keys on write.
  • Partition and compact for dashboard latency.
  • Enable time travel for reproducibility.
Concrete benefit

Eliminates redundant ETL jobs and reduces latency between raw ingestion and production dashboards or model retraining.

Key Takeaway

A Lakehouse lets you operate one curated layer for many workloads while preserving freshness and trust.
