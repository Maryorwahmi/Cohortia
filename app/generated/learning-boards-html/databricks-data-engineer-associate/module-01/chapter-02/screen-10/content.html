Principle

Open formats and storage/compute separation

Choose open, columnar formats for portability, then scale compute independently for elasticity and cost control.

Open formats: query-friendly and portable
Separated compute: scale clusters independently
Operational tip

Monitor long-term storage and apply lifecycle rules for raw data while keeping curated Delta tables for production queries.

Example

Keep raw Parquet files on Azure Blob Storage and attach ephemeral compute clusters for nightly ETL or ad-hoc ML training to save costs when idle.

Common mistake

Choosing proprietary storage formats or tightly coupling compute and storage reduces portability and inflates costs.
