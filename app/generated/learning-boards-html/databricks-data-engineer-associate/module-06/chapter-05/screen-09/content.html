Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Principle of Least Privilege
Definition
Minimum necessary permissions for every identity and service.

Why it matters: least privilege limits what an attacker or misconfigured job can access, making incidents smaller and easier to remediate. It also streamlines compliance checks by narrowing the scope of privilege audits.

  • Map roles to tasks; avoid one-size-fits-all roles.
  • Use group-based assignments from your identity provider.
  • Automate periodic reviews using audit log queries.
Access Review Walkthrough
A short operational pattern to enforce least privilege.
Process
1. Inventory
List roles, groups, and service principals with their permissions and last use time.
2. Review
Have resource owners confirm or revoke access based on current needs.
3. Enforce
Apply changes through IdP group updates and Databricks ACLs; record change events in audit logs.
4. Monitor
Detect anomalous access or stale accounts and trigger a re-review.
Example scenario
A BI analyst had broad write access. After review, write privileges were removed and a managed ingestion service principal received a narrowly scoped role. This reduced writable tables exposed in the analyst's workspace and improved audit clarity.
Key Takeaway

Least privilege is an operational habit: define roles, automate reviews, and remove unnecessary access quickly.
