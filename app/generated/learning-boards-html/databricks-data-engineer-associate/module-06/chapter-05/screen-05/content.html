Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Log Delivery (Azure)
One-line process

Enable, route, and protect

  1. Enable diagnostic settings in your Databricks workspace.
  2. Select categories: audit logs, cluster events, workspace logs.
  3. Set destinations: Log Analytics Workspace, Storage Account, or Event Hub.
  4. Forward to SIEM and set retention and access controls.
Quick conceptual example (Azure)
# Conceptual steps (done via Azure Portal/ARM, not in notebook)
# 1. Go to Databricks Workspace → Diagnostic settings → Add diagnostic setting
# 2. Select categories: audit logs, clusters, dbfs
# 3. Choose Log Analytics Workspace or Storage Account as destination
# 4. Configure retention and access controls in the destination
Why it matters

Delivering logs to centralized, access-controlled locations creates durable evidence for audits and enables real-time security monitoring. Without proper routing and retention, you risk losing the ability to prove control operation during an audit or responding to incidents effectively.

Common Mistake

Relying on default retention. Short retention periods can break compliance requirements. Align retention with your regulatory needs and ensure logs are immutable where required.

Pro tip

Use SIEM correlation rules to detect anomalies from combined audit and diagnostic streams instead of chasing individual log entries.
