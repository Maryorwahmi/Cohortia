Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Databricks Secrets
Secure credential storage with runtime injection to avoid hardcoding.
Secret Scopes
Logical containers for secrets with ACLs for access control.
Customer-Managed Keys (CMK)
Keys you manage in your cloud key vault for stronger control of data-at-rest encryption.
Single Sign-On (SSO)
Authenticate via your corporate identity provider for centralized authentication.
SCIM
Automated provisioning and deprovisioning of users and groups from your IdP.
Principle of Least Privilege
Grant minimal permissions and perform regular reviews to prevent privilege creep.
Summary

These concepts work together: secure secrets, control keys, centralize identity, automate provisioning, and enforce least privilege for a compliant Databricks environment.
