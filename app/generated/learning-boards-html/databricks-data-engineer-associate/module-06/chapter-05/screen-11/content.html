Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Customer-Managed Keys
What CMK gives you
Control over key lifecycle, rotation, and access auditing within your cloud provider.
Benefits
  • Customer control of key rotation and revocation.
  • Stronger evidence for compliance reviews.
  • Integration with organization-wide key policies.
Operational Requirements
Enforce least privilege on the key vault, enable key usage logs, and have a documented rotation policy.
Misconception
Having CMKs is not a silver bullet — losing key control or failing to monitor usage undermines protection.
Example: rotation and audit
When rotating keys, coordinate with platform admins to schedule the rotation window. Verify key usage logs in the cloud provider to ensure only expected principals accessed the key during and after rotation. Keep an audit trail for compliance evidence.
Key Takeaway

CMK provides stronger tenant control for encryption, but you must secure, monitor, and rotate keys under an organizational policy.
