Enforcement
Grants are only effective when clusters and network settings are configured to enforce them.
Auditability
Credential passthrough enables user-level auditing for storage accesses, simplifying compliance reporting.
Operational hygiene
Regular audits, tests, and coordination with network teams turn features into effective controls.
Final action: pair every permission or network change with a short verification step so you can prove security controls actually work.
