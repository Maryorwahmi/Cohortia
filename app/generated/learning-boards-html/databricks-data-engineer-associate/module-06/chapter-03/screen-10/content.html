Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › VNet Injection / VPC Peering
What it does
VNet injection places Databricks compute inside your network so traffic to other private services does not traverse the public internet and is subject to your routing and firewall rules.
When to use
  • Access to private databases or on-prem systems that require private connectivity.
  • Organizational policies mandating private networking for sensitive workloads.
  • When you need to apply your network monitoring and IDS rules to Databricks traffic.
Operational checklist
  • Plan subnets for control and data planes.
  • Adjust route tables and network security groups to allow expected traffic.
  • Coordinate with network teams to ensure on-prem connectivity and firewall rules are correct.
Common misconception

VNet injection is not only for compliance; it also enables better network control and reduces exposure to public networks.
