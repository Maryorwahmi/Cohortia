Practical checklist
  • Catalog strategy: Organize by environment or domain (raw/bronze/silver/gold or by business unit).
  • Groups: Grant to groups to simplify management and auditing.
  • Least privilege: Only give necessary rights, review monthly or quarterly.
  • Column ACLs: Protect PII and sensitive fields with column-level permissions.
  • Education: Teach users to use fully qualified names to avoid mistaken grants.
Common mistake
Granting too broadly at catalog level without understanding inheritance. Always test with SHOW GRANTS and use specific grants where necessary.
Why it matters
Good governance reduces risk, simplifies compliance, and builds trust in your data assets.
