Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Unity Catalog architecture
Definition and roles
Metastore: the top-level container for metadata. Catalog: group of schemas, often by environment or business domain. Schema: holds tables, views, and functions. Table/View/Function: actual data assets.
Example
production.sales_db.customer_data is an explicit path that ensures grants target the intended object.
Misconception
Catalogs are logical, not direct storage mounts. Structure your catalogs for governance, not storage layout.
Key Takeaway

Use a consistent naming policy and fully qualified names to avoid accidental permission assignments across environments.
