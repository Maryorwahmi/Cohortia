Why these objectives matter
Consistent governance reduces accidental data exposure, simplifies audits, and scales across teams and workspaces. Using groups and least privilege reduces administrative overhead and improves security posture. Lineage and audit features accelerate troubleshooting and compliance reporting when your analytics show unexpected results.
Example
Grant SELECT on a production table to analysts but restrict PII fields with column-level ACLs to a smaller group.
Common mistake
Forgetting to use the full catalog.schema.table path can lead to granting rights on the wrong object.
