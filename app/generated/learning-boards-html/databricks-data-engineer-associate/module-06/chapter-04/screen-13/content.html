GRANT / REVOKE
Use SQL GRANT and REVOKE to assign and remove permissions at multiple scopes and verify them with SHOW GRANTS.
Lineage
Automatically captured for Spark operations to help trace data transformations and perform impact analysis.
Audit Log
Records access and administrative actions to support security monitoring and compliance reporting.
Final takeaway
Operationalize Unity Catalog by combining clear naming, group-based least privilege, regular reviews, and lineage-based troubleshooting.
