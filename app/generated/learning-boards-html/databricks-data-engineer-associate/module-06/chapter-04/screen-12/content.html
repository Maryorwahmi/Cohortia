Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
Unity Catalog
A unified governance solution for data and AI on Databricks providing access control, lineage, auditing, and discovery.
Metastore
The top-level container holding all metadata for catalogs, schemas, and permissions.
Catalog
A first-level grouping, often used for environment or business domain separation.
Summary
Use these terms to plan your governance strategy and to correctly scope GRANT and REVOKE commands.
