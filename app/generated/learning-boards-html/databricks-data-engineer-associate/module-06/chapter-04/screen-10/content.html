What inheritance means
Granting permissions at a higher scope applies to contained objects. For example, GRANT SELECT on a catalog applies to its schemas and tables unless an explicit override exists.
Risk
A broad catalog grant can unintentionally expose sensitive tables inside the catalog.
Mitigations
  • Always use catalog.schema.table in GRANT and REVOKE commands.
  • Grant to groups, not to many individual users.
  • Verify with SHOW GRANTS after changes.
Key Takeaway

Explicit object paths and verification prevent accidental over-exposure due to inheritance.
