Comparison

Spot vs On-demand

Spot
Lower cost, subject to preemption, best for fault-tolerant batch jobs.
On-demand
Higher cost but stable; use for critical interactive or stateful workloads.
Practical Tips
  • Start with spot on non-critical batch jobs and measure preemption rates.
  • Design idempotent writes and checkpointing to handle restarts.
  • Consider mixed clusters where critical tasks run on on-demand nodes.
Key Takeaway

Spot instances save cost for restartable jobs, but require fault-tolerant design and monitoring for preemptions.
