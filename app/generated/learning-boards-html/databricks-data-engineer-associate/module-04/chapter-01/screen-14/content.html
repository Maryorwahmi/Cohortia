Pro Tip
Design patterns for safe Delta pipelines
Concrete, repeatable practices to reduce risk in production.
Idempotent writes
Use MERGE or replace-partition patterns so retries do not duplicate data.
Partition for concurrency
Choose partition keys that reduce write collisions and shard hot keys when needed.
Retention planning
Set VACUUM retention above longest-running queries and audit windows.
Maintenance process
Require DRY RUNs and approvals before destructive VACUUM operations.
Operational controls
Add observability for rejected commits, track history metadata, and automate retries with exponential backoff to reduce operational load.
Key Takeaway
Process + patterns = safety. Combine technical patterns with governance to protect history and ensure reliable writes.
