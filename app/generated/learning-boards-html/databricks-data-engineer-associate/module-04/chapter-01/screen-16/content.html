What to Remember
Inspect history
Use DESCRIBE HISTORY before time travel or restore to choose the correct version or timestamp.
Design for concurrency
Make writes idempotent and partition-aware to reduce conflicts under optimistic concurrency.
VACUUM with care
Always run DRY RUN, inspect file lists, and keep retention longer than your longest queries and audit window.
One-line summary
Delta Lake's transaction log plus careful operational practices give you auditable, recoverable, and reliable data pipelines.
