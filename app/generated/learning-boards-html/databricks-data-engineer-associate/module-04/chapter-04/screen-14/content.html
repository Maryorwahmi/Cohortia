Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Unity Catalog
A unified governance solution providing centralized access control, metadata, lineage, and auditing across the Databricks Lakehouse.
Fine-grained Access
Grant permissions at metastore, catalog, schema, table, or column level to protect sensitive fields while enabling broad analytics.
Data Lineage
Runtime lineage traces how data moves and transforms across jobs and notebooks, enabling impact analysis and provenance checks.
Summary
Use Unity Catalog to centralize governance, apply least privilege, automate reviews and tests, and rely on lineage and audits to keep data safe and traceable.
