Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › What to Remember
Use Groups
Grant permissions to groups and service principals to simplify lifecycle management of access.
Least Privilege
Grant only what is necessary. Use column-level selects for sensitive fields like PII.
Automate Reviews
Schedule permission reviews, automate tests that assert denied access, and export audits for monitoring.
Action prompt
Pick one governance habit to adopt this sprint: enforce group grants, add a column-level test, or schedule the first permission review.
