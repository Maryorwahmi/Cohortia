Comparison

Over-Permissioned vs. Least Privilege

Over-Permissioned
  • Grants given widely at catalog/metastore level.
  • Individual user grants scattered across assets.
  • Hard to audit and revoke when roles change.
Risk: accidental exposure
Least Privilege
  • Roles modeled as groups with explicit permissions.
  • Grants scoped to schema, table, or columns as needed.
  • Regular reviews to remove stale access.
Benefit: safer and easier to manage
Concrete example

Instead of granting SELECT across a whole catalog to a contractor, create a group with only the schema-level SELECTs needed for their task, and remove the group membership when the contract ends.

Practical checklist
  1. Model roles as groups, not individual user grants.
  2. Prefer schema-level grants over catalog-level where possible.
  3. Use column-level grants for PII and sensitive fields.
  4. Schedule quarterly permission reviews and automation tests.
Common mistake

Granting broad rights to expedite access requests. The short-term convenience often creates long-term security debt.

Key Takeaway

Design permissions around roles and tasks. Use groups and scoped grants to reduce risk and administrative load.
