Comparison
User grants vs Group grants vs Service principals
User grants
Individual privileges; easy to mismanage and hard to audit at scale.
Group grants
Preferred: assign roles to groups, then add or remove users from groups to control access consistently.
Service principals
Used for automation and jobs; treat them like service accounts and audit their privileges regularly.
Practical mapping
  1. Create groups aligned to organizational roles such as data_analysts, data_engineers, and etl_team.
  2. Grant catalog-level SELECT to analyst groups and schema-level CREATE to engineer groups.
  3. Use service principals for scheduled jobs and grant only necessary permissions for their tasks.
Audit note
Lineage and audit logs will reference the identity that performed actions. Group use improves traceability because membership changes are auditable separately from grants.
Key Takeaway

Manage permissions through groups and service principals to scale administration and support clear audit trails.
