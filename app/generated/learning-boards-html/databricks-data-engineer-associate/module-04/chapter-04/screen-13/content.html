Automated checks and verification

Use simple commands and tests to validate permissions and column protections as part of your pipeline CI or operational checks.

Verify grants (example)
SHOW GRANTS ON TABLE my_project_catalog.raw_data.customer_pii;
Test denied access (concept)
-- As a non-privileged role, expect a permission error
SELECT ssn FROM my_project_catalog.raw_data.customer_pii;  -- should fail if column-level control is active
Automate this test and fail your pipeline if the query unexpectedly succeeds.
Operational checklist
  • Daily or weekly exports of audit logs to SIEM for anomaly detection.
  • Quarterly permission reviews with owners of catalogs and schemas.
  • CI tests that assert restricted columns remain inaccessible to non-privileged groups.
  • Use groups and service principals as canonical identities for automation.
Scenario

Integrate a nightly job that exports grants and recent access events, and surface any new broad grants to your security team for review.

Pro tip

Keep a lightweight runbook describing permission owners and review cadence; this reduces ambiguity during audits and handoffs.

Key Takeaway

Convert governance into scheduled reviews, automated tests, and alerting so compliance scales with your data platform.
