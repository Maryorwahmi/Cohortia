Process

Runtime lineage capture

Unity Catalog records the flow of data across jobs and notebooks so you can trace sources, transformations, and consumers for auditing and impact analysis.

What lineage captures
  • Source tables and files read at runtime.
  • Transformations applied (joins, filters, writes).
  • Destination tables and their schema changes.
  • Job or notebook identity that performed the operation.
Example Scenario

A daily ETL reads sales_raw, enriches with customers, and writes sales_curated. Lineage shows that sales_curated depends on sales_raw and customers and identifies which job produced the latest run.

Misconception
Lineage is not just table metadata. It records executed operations and who ran them, which is essential for accurate impact analysis.
Why it matters

Faster debugging and safer changes

  • Locate dependent assets before schema changes to avoid service disruption.
  • Prove data provenance during audits or compliance checks.
  • Prioritize fixes by seeing which reports or pipelines are impacted.
Key Takeaway

Treat lineage as an operational tool: it connects metadata to actual runtime behavior and helps you make safe, data-driven decisions.
