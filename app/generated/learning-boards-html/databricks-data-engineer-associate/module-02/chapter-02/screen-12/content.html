Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Performance considerations
Key factors
  • Lazy evaluation lets Spark optimize across steps
  • Partitioning reduces shuffles for joins and aggregations
  • Avoid collect to prevent driver OOMs
  • Cache only when you reuse intermediate results
Practical guidance
Repartition on join keys when necessary, prefer broadcast joins for small tables, and write large outputs rather than collecting them. Use explain to find optimizations.
Key Takeaway

Plan shape and data partitioning matter more than micro-optimizations. Inspect plans and avoid moving large data to the driver.
