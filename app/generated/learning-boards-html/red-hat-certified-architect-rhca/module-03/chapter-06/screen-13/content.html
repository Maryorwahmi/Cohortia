Network troubleshooting checklist
Systematic checks to find the source of networking issues.
1. DNS
Confirm service FQDN resolves; check CoreDNS pods and config if resolution fails.
2. NetworkPolicy
Look for deny rules that impact the namespaces or pod labels in question.
3. Service
Verify the Service selector and endpoints; ensure ports match container ports.
4. Route/Ingress
Check hostnames, TLS settings, and router health checks on the external LB.
Pod connectivity check
oc exec <pod> -- ping <target>
oc exec <pod> -- curl -v <service-hostname>
