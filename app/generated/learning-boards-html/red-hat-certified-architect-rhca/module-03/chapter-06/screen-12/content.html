Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Egress IP Lab
Simulate Egress IP assignment
Note: cluster-admin privileges and network support are often required.
Example steps
# namespace annotation
oc patch namespace network-test-project -p '{"metadata": {"annotations": {"network.openshift.io/assign-egress-ips": "true"}}}' --type=merge

# cluster-admin assign (simulated)
oc adm egress-ip add-ip 192.168.1.100 --selector=app=frontend -n network-test-project
Verification
oc get egressip -n network-test-project
oc get pod -l app=frontend -o wide -n network-test-project
# curl external endpoint that echoes your source IP from a pod
Operational note: ensure the chosen IP is routable from your nodes and does not conflict with other addresses.
