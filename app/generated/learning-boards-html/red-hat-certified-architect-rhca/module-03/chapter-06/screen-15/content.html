Avoid ovs-subnet for multi-tenant clusters

Test NetworkPolicies

Egress IP vs Router
Egress IP for simplicity; Router for advanced outbound processing.

Make SDN mode, exposure method, policy design, and egress choice part of your deployment checklist to prevent outages and meet security requirements.
