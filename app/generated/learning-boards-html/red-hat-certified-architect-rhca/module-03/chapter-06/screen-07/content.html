Routes and Ingress — a practical comparison
Routes (OpenShift)
  • OpenShift-native; integrates with OpenShift Router.
  • Supports TLS termination modes directly: edge, passthrough, re-encrypt.
  • Simpler for OpenShift-focused workflows.
Ingress (Kubernetes)
  • Standard Kubernetes resource; portable across clusters.
  • Use when external tooling expects Ingress resources or a custom controller is required.
  • Managed by the Ingress Operator on OpenShift when installed.
Example decision
If your CI/CD pipelines create Ingress resources and you rely on that ecosystem, prefer Ingress. If you want router-managed TLS and simpler OpenShift UX, prefer Routes.
