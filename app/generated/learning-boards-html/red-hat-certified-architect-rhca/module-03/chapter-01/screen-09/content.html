Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Image Registry
What it does
A cluster-local, Docker-compatible registry integrated with ImageStreams
Example: CI builds an image and pushes to the internal registry; ImageStream updates and triggers a DeploymentConfig rollout.
Operational check: list pods and services in the openshift-image-registry namespace to verify registry health.
Common mistake

Assuming external registries are the only source of images. Internal registry outages cause image pull errors even when images are externally available.

Quick commands
oc get pods -n openshift-image-registry
oc get svc -n openshift-image-registry
Key Takeaway

Monitor the internal registry and ImageStreams to avoid image pull issues that stop deployments.
