Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Router & Routes
How it works
Routes expose Services; Router forwards external traffic
Example: Define a Route that references a Service; the Router updates HAProxy to route requests to the service endpoints.
Checks: list router pods in openshift-ingress and inspect routes via oc get routes and oc get svc in the ingress namespace.
Operational commands
oc get pods -n openshift-ingress
oc get svc -n openshift-ingress
oc get routes -n
Misconception

Healthy Service objects do not guarantee external accessibility; Routes and Router must be present and configured correctly.
