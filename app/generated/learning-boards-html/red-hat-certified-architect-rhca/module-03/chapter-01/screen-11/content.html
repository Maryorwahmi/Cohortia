What an Operator is
An Operator is a controller that implements application-specific operational logic and exposes custom resources to represent instances of that application.
Example: Database Operator watches a Database custom resource and ensures provisioning, backups, and scaling policies are applied.
Misconception: Operators are not magic; they still require correct configuration and observability to operate reliably.
Inspecting Operators
Use oc to list operator-managed namespaces and custom resources, and check the operator controller logs to understand automated actions.
Key Takeaway

Operators encode repeatable operational work. Use them to reduce manual steps but monitor their custom resources and controllers for failures.
