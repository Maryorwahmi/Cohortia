What to Remember › Key Concepts
Prometheus
A time-series metrics system used for collection and alerting in OpenShift.
Grafana
Visualization platform that queries Prometheus to create dashboards and panels.
EFK Stack
Fluentd collects logs, Elasticsearch indexes them, and Kibana provides search and visualization.
Summary

Use metrics to detect, logs to diagnose, and targeted diagnostics to resolve issues while managing retention and alerts.
