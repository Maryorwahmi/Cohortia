Comparison › Node vs Network
Node troubleshooting
  • oc describe node <node-name> to inspect conditions and events.
  • Use journalctl on the host for kubelet and crio service logs.
  • oc adm top nodes to spot resource exhaustion causing evictions.
Network troubleshooting
  • oc get netpol -n <ns> to list policies affecting pods.
  • oc exec into a pod and curl or ping the target service to test connectivity.
  • Check Routes and Services for external access and the OpenShift router state.
