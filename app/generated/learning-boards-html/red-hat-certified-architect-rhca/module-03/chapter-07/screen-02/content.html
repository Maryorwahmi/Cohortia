Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Learning Objectives
01 — Monitor: Prometheus
Explain Prometheus components and how they collect metrics from nodes, containers, and applications. You will learn to interpret time-series data and why metrics matter for capacity planning.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Visualize: Grafana
Use Grafana dashboards to surface cluster health and application trends. You will learn to use pre-built dashboards and explore metrics for incident context.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Log: EFK stack
Describe Fluentd, Elasticsearch, and Kibana, and why collecting pod and node logs centrally helps troubleshooting and audits. Learn how the ClusterLogging operator enables the stack.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Troubleshoot: oc toolkit
Perform targeted diagnostics with oc describe, oc logs, oc exec, oc debug, and oc adm must-gather. Understand when to escalate versus when to fix in-cluster.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
