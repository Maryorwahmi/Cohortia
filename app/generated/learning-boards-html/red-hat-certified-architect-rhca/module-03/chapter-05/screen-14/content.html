Key Concepts
Persistent Volume (PV)
Cluster-scoped storage resource representing provisioned storage.
Persistent Volume Claim (PVC)
Namespace-scoped request that binds to a matching PV or triggers provisioning.
StorageClass
Blueprint for dynamic provisioning; defines provisioner and reclaimPolicy.
Final takeaway

Treat storage as an independent lifecycle: provision correctly, verify binding, and protect data with policies and backups.
