Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Projects and multi-tenancy
Understand OpenShift projects as enhanced namespaces that isolate resources and support resource quotas. A clear boundary reduces accidental cross-team interference.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Create, manage, delete projects
Run oc new-project, oc get projects, oc describe project, and oc delete project safely. Verify ownership and resource cleanup before deleting.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
RBAC principles and scope
Explain Roles, ClusterRoles, RoleBindings, and ClusterRoleBindings, and decide when to grant namespace-scoped versus cluster-scoped permissions.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
User, group, and service account administration
Assign roles to users, groups, and service accounts and follow the principle of least privilege. Use external identity providers for enterprise setups.
Verified Competency Milestone
