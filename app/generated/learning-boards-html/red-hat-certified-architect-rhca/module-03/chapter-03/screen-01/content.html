Managing Projects, Users, and RBAC
Understand projects as isolation boundaries and use RBAC to grant precise permissions for users and service accounts.

In this module we focus on commands you will run as an administrator and the policies you will design. Pay attention to the difference between namespace-scoped Roles and cluster-scoped ClusterRoles, and the safety considerations when binding roles.

In this lesson
  • How OpenShift projects map to namespaces and enable multi-tenancy.
  • Commands: creating, switching, and deleting projects with the oc CLI.
  • RBAC primitives: Roles, ClusterRoles, RoleBindings, ClusterRoleBindings, and service accounts.
Coming up
  • Hands-on activity: create a project, assign a user, and configure a service account.
  • Best practices: least privilege, audits, and using external identity providers.
