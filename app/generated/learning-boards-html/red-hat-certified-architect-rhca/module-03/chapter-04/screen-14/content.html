Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Deployment
Kubernetes standard for managing stateless app replicas and rollouts.
DeploymentConfig
OpenShift-specific resource with imageChange triggers and CI/CD integration benefits.
ImageStream & BuildConfig
BuildConfig produces images; ImageStream tracks tags and can trigger deployments when tags change.
Takeaway: map your CI pipeline to ImageStreams and decide between Deployment and DeploymentConfig based on trigger needs and portability goals.
