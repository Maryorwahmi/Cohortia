Deployment strategies
Rolling, Recreate, Blue/Green, and Canary suit different compatibility and availability needs.
Autoscaling
HPA scales pods based on metrics; set realistic resource requests so targets reflect load.
Health checks
Liveness and readiness probes ensure only healthy, ready pods serve traffic and that stuck containers are restarted.
Final takeaway: choose your strategy based on compatibility, set probes and resources correctly, and maintain tested rollback procedures to protect data and availability.
