Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Blue/Green & Canary
Blue/Green

Deploys a full parallel environment. Once the green version is validated, switch traffic and decommission the blue. Quick rollback by switching back.

Resource cost: double capacity during validation.
Rollback: immediate by switching Service/Route selector.
OpenShift implementation: two DeploymentConfigs and a Service/Route selector swap.
Canary

Route a small fraction of traffic to a new version and monitor results before broader rollout. Minimizes impact and gathers production telemetry.

Implementation: small replica set for new version and weighted routing via gateway or mesh.
Progression: increase traffic slowly as metrics validate the change.
Misconception: you don't need a full service mesh to start—small-scale canaries are possible with manual routing.
Practical tip
Combine canary or blue/green with health checks and monitoring to make rollout decisions based on real metrics, not just success/failure signals.
