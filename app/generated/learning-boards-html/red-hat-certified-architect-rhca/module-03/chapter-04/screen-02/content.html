Learning Objectives

Progress from reading manifests to running safe rollouts and autoscaling.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate Deployment vs DeploymentConfig

Identify when image-change triggers and integrated build links justify using DeploymentConfig over a standard Kubernetes Deployment. Practice by inspecting a manifest and listing triggers.

Verified competency milestone
02
Master deployment strategies

Explain rolling, recreate, blue/green, and canary rollouts and choose the best approach for availability and compatibility needs. Use a short scenario to justify your choice.

Verified competency milestone
03
Understand ImageStreams & BuildConfigs

Trace how a BuildConfig produces an ImageStream tag and how ImageChange triggers can redeploy an application automatically. Try oc new-app to create these resources.

Verified competency milestone
04
Configure scaling, probes, and limits

Set requests and limits correctly, add liveness and readiness probes, and configure an HPA to maintain responsiveness under load. Validate with oc autoscale and load testing.

Verified competency milestone
