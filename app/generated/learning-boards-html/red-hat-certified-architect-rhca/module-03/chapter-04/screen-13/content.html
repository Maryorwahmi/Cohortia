Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Best Practices & Rollbacks
Do
  • Set requests and limits.
  • Implement liveness and readiness probes.
  • Test deployment strategies in staging.
Don't
  • Rely on triggers without testing.
  • Leave requests unset and expect stable scheduling.
  • Assume rollbacks restore data automatically.
Rollback planning
Document migration steps and backups. Choose blue/green for instant switchbacks, recreate when data must not be co-located, and canary for gradual validation.
Takeaway
Operational safety comes from testing, observability, and clear rollback procedures tied to your deployment strategy.
