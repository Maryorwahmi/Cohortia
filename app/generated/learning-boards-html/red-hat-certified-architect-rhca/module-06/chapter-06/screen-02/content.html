Learning Objectives

Learn, understand, apply, and master Gluster cluster deployment tasks.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Plan and prepare nodes

Prepare NICs and dedicated bricks; avoid using OS disks to prevent instability and poor performance.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Establish a trusted storage pool

Use peer probe and peer status to form a trusted pool so nodes can participate in volumes and replicate data safely.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Create and configure volumes

Define replicated or distributed replicated volumes depending on your availability and capacity needs; follow the brick grouping rules.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Manage, expand, and monitor volumes

Use start/stop, add-brick, rebalance, heal, and status commands to operate volumes and resolve common problems like split-brain.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
