Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Split-Brain Resolution
Troubleshooting

Resolve split-brain manually

  1. List split-brain files: sudo gluster volume heal <volume> info split-brain
  2. Inspect each file across bricks to choose the authoritative copy
  3. Resolve with sudo gluster volume heal <volume> split-brain resolve <file> <brick_path>
  4. Verify with heal info until no split-brain remains
Prevention

Use quorum settings and network redundancy to reduce the chance of partitions that cause split-brain.

sudo gluster volume heal my-volume info split-brain
# Decide authoritative copy after inspection
sudo gluster volume heal my-volume split-brain resolve /path/to/file gluster-node01:/data/brick1/path/to/file
sudo gluster volume heal my-volume info
Key Takeaway

Split-brain requires manual resolution: identify conflicted files, choose the correct copy deliberately, resolve, and verify healing.
