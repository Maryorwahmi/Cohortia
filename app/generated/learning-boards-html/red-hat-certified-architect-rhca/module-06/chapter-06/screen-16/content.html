Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Quick Reminders
Dedicated storage & NICs

Avoid OS disks for bricks and use a dedicated network path for Gluster traffic to protect performance and stability.

Verify trusted pool

Use sudo gluster peer probe and sudo gluster peer status to ensure nodes are members before creating volumes.

Add bricks correctly

When adding bricks to distributed replicated volumes, add them in multiples of the replica count, then rebalance and check heal status.

Final Takeaway

Apply these rules as a short checklist before creating or modifying volumes to avoid common operational mistakes and ensure consistent behavior.
