Trusted Storage Pool

Group of Gluster servers that trust each other; use gluster peer probe and peer status to manage membership.

Volume Types

Replicated for redundancy; distributed replicated for scale with replica sets.

Add-Brick Rule

When expanding distributed replicated volumes, add bricks in multiples of the replica count so new replica sets form correctly.

Final Takeaway

Prepare nodes carefully, form a trusted pool, choose the right volume type, expand correctly, and monitor/repair with gluster commands to keep data consistent and available.
