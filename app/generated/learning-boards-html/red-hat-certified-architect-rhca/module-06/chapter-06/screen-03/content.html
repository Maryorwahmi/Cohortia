Applied Objectives

Operational tasks, client access, and troubleshooting skills.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Expand volumes correctly

Add bricks in multiples of the replica count for distributed replicated volumes, then rebalance to redistribute data.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Monitor cluster health

Use volume status, peer list, heal info, and logs to detect bricks offline, performance issues, or healing progress.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Client access methods

Mount volumes with the native FUSE client for the best feature support; NFS or SMB are alternatives when required.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Resolve split-brain

Identify conflicted files with heal info, determine the authoritative copy, and use split-brain resolve to heal safely.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
