Design for throughput and reliability
Prioritize high-bandwidth, low-latency network links and enterprise disks. Avoid shared, oversubscribed networks for Gluster traffic.
Network: dedicated 10GbE or faster for cluster traffic and client I/O.
Storage: enterprise disks and controllers reduce failures and speed healing.
Placement: spread replica members across different physical hosts and failure domains.
Practical checklist
  • Use a dedicated network for Gluster cluster traffic.
  • Choose enterprise-grade disks and adequate RAM for caching.
  • Place replicas across separate servers and racks when possible.
  • Monitor and schedule heavy rebuilds during low-traffic windows.
