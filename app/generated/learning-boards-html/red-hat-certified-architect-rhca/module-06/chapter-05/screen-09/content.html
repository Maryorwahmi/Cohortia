Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Distributed + Replica/Stripe
Combine to scale
Distribution places files across groups. Inside each group, replication or striping provides redundancy or performance.
Example: distribute 2 replica 3 on six bricks = two groups of three replicated bricks.
Failure domain: losing two bricks in the same replica group may make those files inaccessible.
Design tip: place replica members on different servers to avoid correlated failures.
Scenario card
Media studio with 6 bricks wants both capacity and redundancy. Use distribute 2 replica 3. Place replica members across different servers to survive single-node failures for each file.
Key Takeaway

Composition lets you tune capacity, redundancy, and throughput, but always reason about where each file's fragments and replicas live.
