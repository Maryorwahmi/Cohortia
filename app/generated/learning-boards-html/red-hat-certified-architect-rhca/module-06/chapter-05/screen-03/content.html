Competency checklist
  • Explain bricks, volumes, and translators in a short design note.
  • Sketch data placement for distributed, replicated, and distributed-replicated volumes on a 3-server example.
  • Describe self-heal activation and how consistency is restored after a brick returns.
  • Choose network bandwidth and disk class appropriate for the workload and explain trade-offs.
Common misconception
Treating striping as a redundancy mechanism. Striping increases throughput but does not protect data on brick failure.
Pro tip
When designing replicated sets, try to place replicas on different servers to avoid correlated failures.
