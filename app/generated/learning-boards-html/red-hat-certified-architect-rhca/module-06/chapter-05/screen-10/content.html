Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Self-Healing
Healing process
When bricks return, Gluster compares state and synchronizes missing or inconsistent data from healthy replicas or parity fragments.
Trigger: brick rejoins or manual heal request.
Actions: identify inconsistencies, copy missing files, recompute parity when needed.
Cost: network and CPU use during rebuilds.
Example workflow
Node S2 was offline. After S2 returns, Gluster compares files in S2's bricks with replicas on S1 and S3 and streams missing or updated files until parity and replicas match.
Check your understanding
Which resource will be most strained during healing: network bandwidth or disk IOPS? (Answer: both, but network bandwidth is often the limiting factor.)
