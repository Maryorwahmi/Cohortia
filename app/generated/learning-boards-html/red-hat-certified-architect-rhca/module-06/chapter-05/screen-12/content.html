Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Advantages & Limits
Advantages
  • Scales capacity and performance by adding commodity servers and disks.
  • Flexible volume types to match redundancy and throughput needs.
  • POSIX compliance makes application integration straightforward.
  • Open source with broad community support.
Limitations
  • Metadata-heavy workloads (many tiny files) can suffer performance bottlenecks.
  • Not a native block or object store; prefer other systems when direct block access is required.
  • Healing and rebuilds consume resources and may be slow on overloaded networks.
Key Takeaway

Match Gluster to file-centric POSIX workloads, and provision network and disk hardware to support healing and expected throughput.

Common mistake

Using consumer disks or oversubscribed networks; both increase failure rates and slow healing.
