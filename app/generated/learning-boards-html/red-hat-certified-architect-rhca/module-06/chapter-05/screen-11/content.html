Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Sketch Activity
Scenario
Three servers, two bricks each: S1:B1,B2; S2:B3,B4; S3:B5,B6. Sketch where fileA would be stored under different volume types and note failure implications.
Distributed: file parts across all 6 bricks. Single brick failure may make parts unavailable.
Replicated (replica 3): file fully on B1,B3,B5. Single brick failure still leaves two copies.
Distribute 2 replica 3: two groups of three bricks; file parts assigned to one group and replicated within it.
Reflection prompt
Write one sentence per sketch describing the result of a single brick failure. Place replicas across nodes to avoid correlated outages.
Why it matters

Sketching helps you see failure boundaries and choose replica counts and placement appropriately.
