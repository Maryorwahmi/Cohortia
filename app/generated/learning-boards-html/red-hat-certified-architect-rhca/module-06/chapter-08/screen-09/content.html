Hot vs Cold: design trade-offs
Choose protection and media based on access pattern and recovery needs.
Hot data
Low latency, replicated pools, fast rebuilds, typically on flash.
Cold data
Capacity-optimized, erasure-coded pools, slower rebuilds acceptable for archival use.
Operational checklist
  • Define workload heat metrics to classify objects.
  • Create distinct pools for hot and cold data with appropriate protections.
  • Monitor movement, and reassess policies on growth or changes in access patterns.
Misconception
Cache tiering is not always the best choice. Prefer clear pool separation and hardware tiering where possible.
