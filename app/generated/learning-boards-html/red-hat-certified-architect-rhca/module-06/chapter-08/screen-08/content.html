Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › OSD Failures & Recovery
Diagnose and recover OSDs
Stepwise approach to identify cause and restore redundancy safely.
Diagnostic steps
  • Run ceph -s and ceph osd df to identify unhealthy OSDs.
  • Inspect logs: journalctl -u ceph-osd@<id> for error details.
  • Check disk SMART data to detect hardware issues.
Remediation pattern
Mark out, stop, repair or replace, then bring back in while monitoring rebalance progress.
Commands & monitoring
# Identify OSDs and utilization
ceph osd tree
ceph osd df

# View logs for an OSD
journalctl -u ceph-osd@

# Mark an OSD out before repair
ceph osd out
Monitor ceph -s during every step and avoid making bulk changes that can overload the cluster's recovery capacity.
