Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Split-Brain Detection & Resolution
Detect and resolve split-brain
Treat any replication divergence as an operational priority and follow controlled reconciliation.
Warning
Ignoring split-brain leads to data corruption and inconsistent application state across replicas.
Resolution checklist
  1. Identify conflicting files or directories.
  2. Decide which copy to preserve based on application semantics.
  3. Resolve using gluster tools or manual reconciliation under change control.
Monitoring commands
# Check volume and peer status
sudo gluster volume status
sudo gluster peer status

# Inspect brick logs and glusterd logs for errors and conflicts
Document your split-brain policy and rehearse resolution steps in a lab so the team can act quickly and consistently when it occurs.
