Command-line checks and logs
Quick, reliable commands that should be part of your regular checks.
Quick commands
  • ceph -s: cluster summary and health.
  • ceph health detail: detailed warnings and suggestions.
  • ceph pg dump: show PG states and stuck PGs.
  • ceph osd perf: per-OSD perf metrics.
If you see degraded or stuck PGs, collect logs with journalctl and inspect OSD and MON processes before taking corrective steps.
Integrate metrics and logging
# Example troubleshooting steps
ceph -s
ceph health detail
ceph pg dump | grep 
journalctl -u ceph-

# For long-term metrics: configure Prometheus scrapes and build Grafana dashboards
Design alert rules for actionable conditions like OSD down, high disk utilization, or monitor quorum loss to avoid alert fatigue and ensure rapid response.
