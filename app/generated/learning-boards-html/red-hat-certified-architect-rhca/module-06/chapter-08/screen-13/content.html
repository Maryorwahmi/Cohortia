Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Snapshots, Backups & DR
Snapshots and backups
Snapshots for fast recovery; backups for site loss protection.
Snapshot uses
Point-in-time recovery for accidental deletes and quick testing. Snapshots are not substitutes for off-cluster backups.
Backup & replication
Export images, sync RGW to external object stores, and configure geo-replication for cross-site DR where required.
Operational checklist
  • Schedule and validate snapshot exports and imports.
  • Keep backups off-cluster and test restores regularly.
  • Document promotion steps for geo-replication and DR failover.
Remember: replication eases availability but does not replace the need for independent backups and tested restores.
