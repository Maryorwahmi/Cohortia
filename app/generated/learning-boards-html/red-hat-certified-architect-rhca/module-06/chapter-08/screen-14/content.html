Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Erasure Coding
Data redundancy with parity chunks that improves storage efficiency compared to full replication for large datasets.
CRUSH Map Manipulation
Customize failure domains and placement rules to match your physical topology and recovery goals.
Placement Groups (PGs)
Logical distribution units that must be sized correctly to avoid hotspots and imbalance across OSDs.
Summary Takeaway

Small configuration decisions around coding, CRUSH, and PGs have outsized operational impact. Plan, backup, and monitor every change.
