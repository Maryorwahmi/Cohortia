Advanced Storage Management, Monitoring, and Troubleshooting
Ceph and Gluster operational tasks for reliability, performance, and recovery.
This chapter arms you with commands and procedures to manage pools and CRUSH maps in Ceph, perform rebalances and quota enforcement in Gluster, set up observability with Prometheus and Grafana, and follow troubleshooting flows for OSD/brick failures and split-brain. Practical command snippets and diagnostic steps are included so you can reproduce and adapt them in your environment.
In this lesson
Ceph Operations
Pool management, PG tuning, CRUSH edits, and RBD snapshot practices.
Gluster Tasks
Volume rebalance, quota controls, split-brain detection and resolution.
Monitoring & DR
Prometheus/Grafana, centralized logs, snapshots, and geo-replication planning.
Operational Map
Pools → PGs → OSDs
Understand how pools and placement groups route objects to OSDs. Small changes can cause large data movement; plan and monitor rebalances carefully.
Monitoring Stack
ceph CLI, dashboards, Prometheus
Alerts for OSD failures, split-brain, or quota breaches reduce mean time to detection. Collect metrics and logs centrally.
