Metrics, logs, and alerts
Design the stack to generate actionable signals and reduce time to remediation.
Essential metrics
  • IOPS, throughput, latency per pool and per OSD.
  • PG states and long-running backfills.
  • Disk SMART and utilization metrics.
Alerting examples
OSD down, monitor quorum loss, high disk usage, and split-brain detection with clear runbooks for each alert.
Operational checklist
  1. Integrate Ceph and Gluster metrics into Prometheus.
  2. Create Grafana dashboards for baseline visualization.
  3. Aggregate logs centrally for cross-node correlation.
  4. Define alert thresholds and associated runbooks.
Regularly test alerts and recovery steps so the team can execute under pressure and your SLAs remain achievable.
