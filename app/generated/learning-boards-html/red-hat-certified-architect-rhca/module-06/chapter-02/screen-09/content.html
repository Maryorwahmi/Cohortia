Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Client Interaction
Two-step flow
1) Client requests the cluster map from a monitor. 2) Using the map, the client opens direct connections to the correct OSDs and performs data I/O.
Why this matters

Direct client-to-OSD I/O avoids bottlenecks and allows Ceph to scale. Ensure low-latency access to monitors and high-bandwidth paths to OSDs.

Misconception check

Monitors are not on the fast path for data; they provide control-plane information only.

Scenario
A VM requesting a disk image will fetch the map, determine the OSDs storing the image stripes, then stream data directly from those OSDs. This keeps data throughput high and avoids central bottlenecks.
Key Takeaway

Ensure both control-plane reachability to monitors and high-performance networks for direct OSD I/O to achieve scale and low latency.
