05 — Inspect CRUSH Map
Extract and decompile a CRUSH map to view devices, hosts, buckets, and rules. This confirms placement policies before production use.
Mnemonic: extract, decompile, inspect — a three-step check to validate placement domains.

06 — Hardware & Network Design
Choose SSDs for WAL/DB and robust NICs for OSD traffic; separate public and cluster networks to avoid recovery congestion.
Mnemonic: isolate control, data, and public paths to keep traffic predictable under load.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07 — Component Separation Strategies
Plan dedicated MONs, OSD servers, and RGW gateways to reduce resource contention and the blast radius of failures.
Mnemonic: one role, one primary concern — stability for monitors, I/O for OSDs, network for gateways.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08 — Recovery & Validation
Simulate or inspect OSD failures and validate the cluster rebalances and restores replicas according to CRUSH rules and PG configuration.
Mnemonic: fail, observe, verify — ensure replicas are rebuilt and clients retain access.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
