Red Hat Ceph Storage Architecture Fundamentals
Learn the roles of MON, OSD, MDS, RGW, and how CRUSH places and heals data at scale.
Orientation

This lesson explains each daemon's responsibility, the client connection model, and the CRUSH algorithm's role in replication and rebalancing. Expect practical design guidance and a command-line activity to inspect a CRUSH map.

Who this matters to

Architects, SREs, and storage engineers who must size clusters, isolate traffic, and recover from OSD failures without data loss or long outages.

Quick takeaway

Ceph separates control, data, and metadata services and uses CRUSH to distribute data without a centralized index. Design networks and hardware with that separation in mind.
