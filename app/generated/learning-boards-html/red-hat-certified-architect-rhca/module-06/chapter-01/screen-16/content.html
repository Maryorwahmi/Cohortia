What to Remember

Three quick architecture reminders to use in reviews and designs.

Map interfaces
Block, object, or file — choose based on application semantics and SLAs.
Validate infrastructure
Networks and disks must meet performance and redundancy requirements for SDS to succeed.
Test integrations
Verify CSI drivers, storage classes, and provisioning flows in your orchestration platform.
Final Takeaway

Use requirement-driven design, validate infrastructure, and test integrations to turn SDS capabilities into reliable production storage.
