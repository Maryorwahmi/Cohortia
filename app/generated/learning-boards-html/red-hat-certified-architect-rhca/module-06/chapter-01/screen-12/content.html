Common mistakes and planning for production

A practical checklist to avoid pitfalls around network, hardware, scaling, and recovery.

Top pitfalls
  • Poor network design turning replication into a bottleneck.
  • Using inappropriate disk classes for high I/O workloads.
  • No plan for rebalancing, capacity growth, or disaster recovery testing.
Planning checklist
  1. Validate enterprise drives and NICs for expected I/O.
  2. Design redundant, high bandwidth networks with separation for replication traffic.
  3. Define placement and replication policies and test failures and recovery.
Concrete example
If rebalancing runs over the same network used by clients, performance will degrade. Plan a separate path or QoS to protect client I/O during rebalancing.
Common Mistake

Treating SDS as zero‑touch hardware; instead, validate components and test failure scenarios.
