Cluster and public networks: design and rationale
Segmentation, redundancy, and capacity considerations.
  • Public network: client-facing, serve I/O requests.
  • Cluster network: inter-OSD replication, recovery, and internal traffic.
  • Separate interfaces or bonded pairs reduce contention and provide redundancy.
  • Consider higher bandwidth on the cluster network for faster rebalancing and recovery.
Design checklist
  1. Plan separate NICs or bond for public and cluster networks.
  2. Document IP ranges for public and cluster networks and firewall rules.
  3. Implement redundancy via bonding or multiple switch paths.
  4. Prefer physical separation for large clusters; consider VLANs for logical separation only when physical separation is not possible.
This rationale explains why network planning should precede OSD provisioning and bootstrap actions.
