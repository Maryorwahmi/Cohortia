Learning Objectives — Practical checks
Essential verifications before and after bootstrap.
Disk readiness
Use lsblk -f and fdisk -l to confirm devices are unpartitioned. If partitions exist, wipe them safely before provisioning.
Mnemonic
Check, Wipe, Confirm
SSH & permissions
Ensure cephadm’s SSH key can reach hosts and that sudo privileges are available for provisioning steps.
Mnemonic
Key, Sudo, Access
Firewall & ports
Open necessary Ceph ports such as 6789 for monitors and 6800–7300 for OSDs between nodes in the cluster.
Mnemonic
Ports, Rules, Verify
Config & backups
Immediately back up ceph.conf and the admin keyring after bootstrap and protect file permissions to avoid credential exposure.
Mnemonic
Backup, Secure, Rotate
