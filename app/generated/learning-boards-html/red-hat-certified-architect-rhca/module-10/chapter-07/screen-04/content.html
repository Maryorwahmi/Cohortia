Core Concept

The Pillars of Observability

Monitoring, Logging, and Alerting are essential for cloud-native applications.

Monitoring

Collecting quantitative data (metrics) about system performance and behavior.

Logging

Aggregating and analyzing event records generated by applications and infrastructure.

Alerting

Proactively notifying teams when predefined conditions or thresholds are breached.

Why it matters

Observability provides the visibility needed to understand system behavior, troubleshoot issues, and ensure reliability in complex, distributed environments like OpenShift.

Key Takeaway

Observability is not optional; it's the foundation for managing and maintaining healthy cloud-native applications on OpenShift.
