Proactive Alerting with Alertmanager

Alertmanager handles, groups, and routes alerts from Prometheus to various channels.

Alertmanager Capabilities

  • •**Deduplication:** Prevents repeated notifications for the same alert.
  • •**Grouping:** Combines similar alerts into a single notification.
  • •**Routing:** Sends alerts to specific teams or channels (email, Slack, PagerDuty).
  • •**Silence & Inhibition:** Manages alert suppression during maintenance or related incidents.
Example Scenario

Multiple pods failing for one application trigger a single, grouped alert to the responsible team via Slack.

Alerting Workflow

→→

Prometheus detects an issue, sends an alert to Alertmanager, which then routes it to the appropriate notification channel.

Key Takeaway

Alertmanager is critical for managing and routing alerts, ensuring timely and effective incident response in OpenShift.
