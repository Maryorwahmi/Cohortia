Key Concepts: Monitoring & Logging

Essential terms for understanding OpenShift observability.

Observability

Understanding a system's internal state from its external outputs (metrics, logs, traces).

Prometheus

Open-source monitoring system and time-series database for metric collection.

Grafana

Platform for data visualization, dashboarding, and alerting, commonly used with Prometheus.

ServiceMonitor

Kubernetes Custom Resource for Prometheus Operator to discover and scrape application metrics.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts form the foundation for collecting, visualizing, and understanding metrics in OpenShift environments.
