Key Concepts: Logging & Alerting

Essential terms for managing logs and proactive issue detection.

EFK Stack

Elasticsearch, Fluentd, Kibana: OpenShift's centralized logging solution.

Structured Logging

Logging in a machine-readable format (e.g., JSON) for easier parsing and querying.

Alertmanager

Handles, groups, and routes alerts from Prometheus to various notification channels.

PromQL

Prometheus Query Language, used for querying metrics and defining alerting rules.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts are crucial for managing logs effectively and implementing proactive alerting strategies in OpenShift.
