Common Observability Mistakes & Best Practices

Avoid pitfalls and ensure your observability strategy is effective and secure.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • •**Observability Noise:** Collecting too many irrelevant metrics or logs.
  • •**Untested Alerts:** Alerts that don't fire or fire too often, leading to fatigue.
  • •**Neglecting Security:** Logging sensitive data or inadequate access control.
Impact

These mistakes can lead to increased costs, delayed incident response, and security vulnerabilities.

Best Practices for Success

  • •**Purpose-Driven Data:** Focus on metrics and logs that provide actionable insights.
  • •**Test Alerting Rules:** Regularly validate that alerts fire correctly.
  • •**Structured & Sanitized Logs:** Implement structured logging and redact sensitive information.
  • •**RBAC for Dashboards:** Control access to monitoring and logging tools.
Key Takeaway

A well-designed observability strategy is purpose-driven, thoroughly tested, and secure, providing true value to operations.
