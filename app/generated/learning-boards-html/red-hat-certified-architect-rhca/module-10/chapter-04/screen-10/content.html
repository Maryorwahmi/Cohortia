Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Practices & Pitfalls
Do: Small, focused Tasks
Design Tasks around a single responsibility to improve reuse and observability.
Clone source
Build image
Run tests and scans
Deploy to OpenShift
Don't: Monolithic Tasks or over-privileged SAs
Avoid bundling unrelated steps or granting excessive cluster permissions to Pipeline ServiceAccounts.
Security checklist
  • Assign least privilege to the ServiceAccount used by PipelineRuns.
  • Scan images as an early Task to block vulnerable images.
  • Protect EventListener endpoints from unauthorized callers.
Key takeaway: Small Tasks, explicit permissions, and early scanning reduce risk and make pipelines easier to operate.
