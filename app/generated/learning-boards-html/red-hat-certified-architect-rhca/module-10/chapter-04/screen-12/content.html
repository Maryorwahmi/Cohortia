Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Key Concepts
Task
A small reusable unit of containerized steps with params and workspaces.
Pipeline
Orchestrates Tasks, declares Workspaces for artifacts and Results for metadata.
PipelineRun
A concrete execution that binds params and workspaces to run a Pipeline.
Summary takeaway
Use small Tasks, explicit Workspaces and Results, secure ServiceAccounts, and Triggers to automate reproducible pipeline executions in OpenShift.
