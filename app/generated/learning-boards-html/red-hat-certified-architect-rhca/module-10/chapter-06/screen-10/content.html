DestinationRule: Applying Post-Routing Policies

Configuring Load Balancing, Connection Pools, and Service Subsets

What it Does
  • •Defines policies applied to traffic after routing by a VirtualService.
  • •Configures load balancing algorithms and connection pool settings.
  • •Crucially, defines named subsets (versions) of a service.
Example: Defining Subsets

You can define subsets like v1 and v2 for your my-app-service based on pod labels. A VirtualService can then direct traffic to these specific subsets, enabling precise version control.

apiVersion: networking.istio.io/v1beta1
kind: DestinationRule
metadata:
  name: my-app-destination
spec:
  host: my-app-service
  subsets:
    - name: v1
      labels:
        version: v1
    - name: v2
      labels:
        version: v2
Key Takeaway

DestinationRule complements VirtualService by defining the specific policies and service subsets that govern traffic behavior once it reaches its destination.
