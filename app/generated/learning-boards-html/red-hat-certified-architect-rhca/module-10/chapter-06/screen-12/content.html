Resilience Engineering

Building Resilient Microservices

Testing and Preventing Failures with Fault Injection and Circuit Breaking

Fault Injection

Intentionally introduces delays or aborts HTTP requests to test the resilience of your application. This helps identify how services handle unexpected latency or errors.

Example

Inject a 5-second delay for 10% of requests to a specific service to see how upstream services cope with the added latency.

Circuit Breaking

Automatically stops requests to a failing service, preventing cascading failures. Configured in DestinationRule via connectionPool and outlierDetection parameters.

Mechanism

Monitors service health; if error thresholds are met, the circuit 'breaks,' isolating the unhealthy service until it recovers.

Key Takeaway

Istio's fault injection and circuit breaking are essential tools for proactively testing and building resilient microservices that can withstand failures.
