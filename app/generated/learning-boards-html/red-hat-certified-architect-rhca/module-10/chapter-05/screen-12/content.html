Route to create
apiVersion: route.openshift.io/v1
kind: Route
metadata:
  name: my-app-canary-route
spec:
  to:
    kind: Service
    name: my-app-v1-service
    weight: 90
  alternateBackends:
    - kind: Service
      name: my-app-v2-service
      weight: 10
  port:
    targetPort: 8080
  tls:
    termination: edge
    insecureEdgeTerminationPolicy: Redirect
Apply this manifest, then obtain the route host and run a curl sampling loop. Increase or decrease weights with oc patch as you gain confidence.
Sampling example
for i in $(seq 1 20); do
  curl -s http://$(oc get route my-app-canary-route -o jsonpath='{.spec.host}') | grep "Version"
done
If you see a roughly 10% share of v2 responses, the Route weighting is behaving as configured. Continue to increase weight in measured increments, monitoring key metrics after each step.
Safety reminder
Adjust weights slowly and use production metrics and logs to confirm that increases do not introduce regressions.
