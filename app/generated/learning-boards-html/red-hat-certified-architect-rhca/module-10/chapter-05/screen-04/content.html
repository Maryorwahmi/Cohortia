Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Blue/Green Deployments
Definition & purpose

Two identical environments let you validate the new version fully and flip traffic instantly if problems appear; this minimizes downtime and simplifies rollback.

Analogy
Think of Blue/Green as swapping which machine powers a stage performance: you rehearse on the spare machine and flip the power when ready.
Common mistakes
  • Assuming zero-cost duplicate resources — plan capacity for parallel environments.
  • Forgetting session or database compatibility — migrations must be safe to roll back.
  • Not testing the Service selector switch in a staging environment first.
Key Takeaway

Blue/Green gives instant traffic switch and rollback but requires planning for stateful concerns and capacity during parallel runs.

Example
This command updates a Service selector to point to the green version's pods. Use it after you validate Green is healthy.
oc patch svc my-app-service -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"my-app","version":"green"}}}'
Scenario
You have my-app-blue and my-app-green; after tests pass on green, patch the Service selector to route real traffic to green, and keep blue available to revert instantly if needed.
