Traffic control model
Service selectors define which pods receive internal traffic. Routes expose a Service and can split external traffic across services with weights. DeploymentConfig stores deployment history for rollbacks.
Service = stable internal endpoint
Route = external entry + weighted backends
Quick example
Switching a Service selector is an instant Blue/Green cutover; adjusting Route weights is a gradual canary.
Key Takeaway

Choose Blue/Green for instant cutover and rapid rollback, Canary for gradual validation, and A/B when you need business-metric experiments; always plan rollback for data changes separately.
