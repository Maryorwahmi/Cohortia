Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Canary vs A/B
Canary (Technical)
  • Goal: validate stability and performance of a new release with minimal blast radius.
  • Routing: weighted Routes are suitable for percentage-based splits.
  • Metrics: error rate, latency, resource use; watch for regressions.
A/B Testing (Business)
  • Goal: compare versions to measure business outcomes like conversion.
  • Routing: often requires header/cookie-based routing or a gateway/service mesh.
  • Metrics: conversion, engagement, retention; needs careful statistical design.
Decision guide
If you need to detect regressions and avoid impact, choose Canary. If you want to know which UI or feature improves a business metric, choose A/B testing and use an appropriate routing tool for advanced targeting.
