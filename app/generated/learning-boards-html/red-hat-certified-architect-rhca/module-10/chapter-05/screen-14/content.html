Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Key Concepts
Blue/Green Deployment
Two identical environments with an atomic traffic switch for immediate rollback.
Canary Deployment
Gradual rollout using weighted Route backends to reduce blast radius and validate stability.
A/B Testing
Traffic split for business experiments; often requires gateway or service mesh for advanced rules.
Summary Takeaway
Match strategy to objective, ensure metrics and alerts are ready, and always plan rollback steps for both code and data to recover safely.
