Implementing a NetworkPolicy

A real-world example of restricting pod-to-pod communication.

apiVersion: networking.k8s.io/v1
kind: NetworkPolicy
metadata:
  name: allow-frontend-to-backend
spec:
  podSelector:
    matchLabels:
      app: backend
  policyTypes:
    - Ingress
  ingress:
    - from:
        - podSelector:
            matchLabels:
              app: frontend
      ports:
        - protocol: TCP
          port: 8080
Key Takeaway

This NetworkPolicy ensures that only pods labeled 'app: frontend' can initiate TCP connections to 'app: backend' pods on port 8080.

Policy Breakdown

  • podSelector: Targets pods with app: backend label.
  • policyTypes: Ingress: Applies to incoming traffic.
  • from: Specifies source pods with app: frontend label.
  • ports: Limits traffic to TCP on port 8080.
Pro-Tip

Always test your NetworkPolicies thoroughly after implementation to ensure they enforce the intended communication rules without disrupting legitimate traffic.
