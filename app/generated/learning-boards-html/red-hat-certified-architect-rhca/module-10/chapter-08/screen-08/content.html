PRACTICAL APPLICATION

Crafting a Custom SCC

Tailoring security policies for specific application requirements.

apiVersion: security.openshift.io/v1
kind: SecurityContextConstraints
metadata:
  name: my-custom-scc
allowPrivilegedContainer: false
runAsUser:
  type: MustRunAsRange
  uidRange: "1000000000/10000"
seLinuxContext:
  type: MustRunAs
  seLinuxOptions:
    level: "s0:c123,c456"
# ... other configurations
Key Takeaway

Custom SCCs allow precise control over pod permissions, enabling specific application needs while adhering to the principle of least privilege.

Binding the Custom SCC

After defining your custom SCC, you must grant it to the specific ServiceAccount that your application's pods will use. This ensures only authorized pods can leverage these custom permissions.

oc adm policy add-scc-to-user my-custom-scc -z <serviceaccount-name> -n <namespace>

Replace <serviceaccount-name> and <namespace> with your specific values.

Common Mistake

Binding a custom SCC to the 'default' ServiceAccount cluster-wide, which can inadvertently grant elevated privileges to all pods in new projects.
