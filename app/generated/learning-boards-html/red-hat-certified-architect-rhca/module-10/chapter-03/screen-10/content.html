Comparison

S2I vs Dockerfile builds

S2I
Automates source injection and assembly using builder images. Best for rapid iteration and standard language stacks.
Dockerfile Build
Executes a Containerfile from your repo. Use when you need fine-grained control, multi-stage builds, or custom build steps.
Command examples
S2I quick app: oc new-app --name=my-nodejs-app --image-stream=nodejs:16~https://github.com/openshift/nodejs-ex.git
Dockerfile build: oc new-build --name=my-custom-app --dockerfile=./Containerfile --from-dir=.
Common Mistake
Choosing S2I when you actually require explicit multi-stage control, which later forces a migration to Dockerfile builds.
