Hands-on and judgement
What to practice
  • Using the oc tool to explore projects, nodes, and platform services.
  • Designing declarative Deployments and choosing persistent volumes when needed.
  • Applying resource requests, limits, readiness and liveness probes, and image scanning before production deploys.
Why these matter

Operational familiarity reduces surprises during rollout. Declarative objects enable reproducible system state, and resource discipline prevents cluster instability — all key responsibilities for architects.
