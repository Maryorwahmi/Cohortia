Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Key Concepts
Cloud-Native
Containers, microservices, immutability, declarative APIs for agility and resilience.
12-Factor App
Guidelines to keep builds portable, reproducible, and configurable at runtime.
OpenShift Platform
Enterprise Kubernetes with registry, routing, S2I, and integrated pipelines to support cloud-native patterns.
Takeaway
Apply these concepts together: design for disposability, automate builds and promotions, and map OpenShift capabilities to your architecture goals for predictable delivery and operations.
