Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › What does Cloud-Native mean?
Core definition

Cloud-native is an approach that combines containers, microservices, immutable infrastructure, and declarative APIs. These elements together enable fast delivery and resilient systems.

Containers
Consistent runtime packaging for faster deploys and reproducible environments.
Microservices
Loosely coupled services aligned to business capabilities to enable independent releases.
Immutable infrastructure
Replace resources on change rather than mutating in place; reduces drift and unexpected state.
Why architects care
  • Predictable deployments and rollbacks from declarative APIs.
  • Scalability by scaling services independently based on demand.
  • Fault isolation, which limits blast radius of failures.
Example
An e-commerce app where product, orders, and auth are separate services; each scales and updates independently.
Key Takeaway

Cloud-native is an architecture and operational model that drives faster, safer delivery at scale.
