Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Stateless vs Stateful
Stateless processes

Design services so they can be terminated and recreated without losing data. Store session and persistent data in external stores or object storage.

When state is required
  • Databases or caches requiring local disks — use persistent volumes and replication.
  • Plan for backups and failover to maintain availability and integrity.
Immutable infrastructure

Replace infrastructure rather than mutating it. Bake configuration into new artifacts and swap them in to reduce drift and simplify rollbacks.

Common mistake
Treating containers like VMs and relying on container-local storage for critical data leads to data loss on replacement.
Key Takeaway
Externalize state when possible and apply immutability to infrastructure to reduce drift and simplify operations.
