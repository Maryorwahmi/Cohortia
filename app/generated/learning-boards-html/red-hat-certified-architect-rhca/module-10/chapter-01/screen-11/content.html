Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › oc walkthrough
Read-only commands
oc login --token=<YOUR_TOKEN> --server=<YOUR_OPENSHIFT_API_URL>
oc get projects
oc get nodes
oc describe node <NODE_NAME>
oc get all -n openshift-infra
oc get all -n openshift-monitoring
oc new-project my-cloud-native-app --display-name="My Cloud Native Application"
Replace placeholders with your token and server. These commands inspect projects, nodes, and infrastructure services without changing state.
What to look for
  • Projects show multi-tenancy and resource separation.
  • Node descriptions reveal capacity, labels, taints, and allocated resources.
  • Infra namespace listings identify registry, router, and monitoring components to inform architecture choices.
Key Takeaway
Use these outputs to map cluster capacity, tenancy, and platform services to your architecture decisions.
