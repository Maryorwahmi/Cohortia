Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › E-commerce architecture
Service hierarchy
Product Catalog
Read-heavy service with its own datastore optimized for queries.
Order Processing
Handles transactions, uses queues for eventual consistency.
Authentication
Centralized identity, externalized from business logic.
Operational guidance
  • Make services stateless where possible; externalize state.
  • Design APIs for clear boundaries and versioning strategies.
  • Plan for observability, logging, and failure isolation from the start.
Key Takeaway
Start with service boundaries that reflect business capability and iterate; avoid premature micro-splitting.
