Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Traditional vs Cloud-Native
Traditional
  • Monolithic apps on VMs with manual provisioning.
  • Infrequent, large releases and manual rollbacks.
  • Operational knowledge concentrated in specialized ops teams.
Cloud-native on OpenShift
  • Containers and microservices with declarative deployments.
  • Frequent automated releases, immutable artifacts, and easier rollbacks.
  • Requires distributed-system operational skills and automation design.
Architect reflection
Decide which services to decompose first and where automation yields the highest reduction in risk and cycle time.
