Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Resource & Security
Resource controls
  • Requests and limits: inform scheduling and protect cluster stability.
  • Labels: group nodes and workloads for affinity and selection.
  • Taints and tolerations: prevent scheduling on specific nodes without explicit approval.
Security and supply chain
  • SCCs and identity management control runtime privileges and access.
  • Image scanning in pipelines prevents vulnerable artifacts from reaching production.
  • Network policies limit inter-service communication to reduce attack surface.
Key Takeaway
Standardize resource sizing, scheduling rules, and security baselines to ensure stable, secure operations on OpenShift.
