Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Resilience & Observability
Pattern
Circuit Breaker
Detect failing downstream services and stop requests temporarily to avoid cascading failures. Provide fallbacks where appropriate.
Observability
Logs, Metrics, Traces
Instrument for structured logs, Prometheus metrics, and distributed tracing so you can follow requests across services and identify bottlenecks.
Security
mTLS & Data Protection
Ensure authenticated and authorized service-to-service calls, encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest, and use service mesh features when appropriate.
Key Takeaway

Combine resilience patterns like circuit breakers with instrumentation for logs, metrics, and traces, and include authentication and encryption from design through deployment.
