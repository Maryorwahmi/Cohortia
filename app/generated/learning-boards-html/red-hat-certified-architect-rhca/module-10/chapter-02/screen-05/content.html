Process

Steps to Decompose

  1. Identify business capabilities — find cohesive areas like Product Catalog and Order Processing.
  2. Define bounded contexts — clarify the model and language used within each context.
  3. Assign team ownership — each context becomes a service owned by a team.
  4. Design contracts and events — APIs and domain events that services publish and consume.
  5. Migrate data incrementally — adopt database-per-service with careful migration and compatibility adapters.
Pilot example
Extract Product Catalog first. Provide read APIs and publish ProductUpdated events for Inventory and Frontend services to consume.
Example Card
E-commerce extraction
Product Catalog service: owns product data store, exposes product read and search APIs, publishes ProductUpdated events to Kafka for Inventory and Search services.
Key Takeaway

Split by domain responsibility, migrate data carefully, and validate boundaries with a pilot service before broader decomposition.

Pitfall
Do not separate services without giving them ownership of their data. Shared databases lock services together.
