Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › What to Remember
1. Bounded Contexts
Map services to business domains and give them data ownership to enable independent evolution.
2. Communication Choices
Use synchronous calls for immediate needs and asynchronous events for resilience and decoupling.
3. Operational Readiness
Implement service discovery, gateway routing, circuit breakers, storage persistence, observability, and security early.
Final takeaway
Microservice design on OpenShift succeeds when business boundaries are respected, communication and data strategies are chosen intentionally, and operational patterns are in place for resilience and observability.
