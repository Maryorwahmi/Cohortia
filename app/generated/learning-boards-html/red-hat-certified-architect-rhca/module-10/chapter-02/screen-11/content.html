Definition

Service Discovery

Services are discoverable by DNS names and cluster IPs created by Service objects. Clients call the DNS name rather than pod IP addresses.

Example address
product-catalog.my-app.svc.cluster.local resolves to the Service that routes to the product catalog pods.
Operational guidance
Even with DNS-based discovery, implement retries, timeouts, and circuit breakers to handle slow or failing services. Consider a service mesh for mTLS and richer telemetry across services.
Key Takeaway

Service objects provide stable discovery on OpenShift; combine this with resilience patterns to avoid failure propagation.
