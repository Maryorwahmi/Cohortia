Architecture

Event Streams on OpenShift

Design guidelines
  • Define clear event schemas and versioning strategy.
  • Design consumers to be idempotent and tolerant to out-of-order delivery.
  • Monitor consumer lag and retention settings in the broker.
Concrete example
Order service publishes OrderCreated to Kafka; Inventory and Shipping consume that topic and update their own databases independently.
Operational Note
AMQ Streams (Kafka) can be deployed on OpenShift to provide durable topics. Ensure brokers, topics, and authorization are managed as part of your platform operations.
Key Takeaway

Use events to decouple services and improve resilience, but plan for idempotency, schema evolution, and eventual consistency.

Pitfall
Treating event streams as a simple replacement for synchronous calls without addressing schema, idempotency, and operational monitoring.
