Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
perf
Function-level profiling to find CPU hotspots in user and kernel paths.
strace & lsof
Trace system calls and list open files/sockets to diagnose failures and leaks.
sar, vmstat, iostat
Historical and device-level metrics to detect trends and identify saturated devices.
Summary Takeaway

Treat tools as complementary lenses: profile, trace, inspect descriptors, and analyze history to reach evidence-driven fixes.
