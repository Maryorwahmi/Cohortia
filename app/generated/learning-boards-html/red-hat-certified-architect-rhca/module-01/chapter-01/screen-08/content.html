Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › vmstat & iostat
vmstat quick check
# Watch virtual memory and CPU activity
vmstat 1
# Look at columns: si, so, us, sy, id, wa
High si/so indicates swapping. High wa shows processes waiting for I/O; verify with iostat next.
iostat device focus
# Extended device stats with utilization and await
iostat -xz 1
# Check %util and await columns for hotspots
High %util (>80–90%) and increasing await indicate the device cannot keep up with requests and is a bottleneck.
When to use vmstat
First-pass system pressure checks, swapping, CPU wait states.
When to use iostat
Device-level throughput, latency, and utilization analysis.
Key Takeaway

Combine vmstat and iostat: vmstat signals pressure, iostat locates the device offender.
