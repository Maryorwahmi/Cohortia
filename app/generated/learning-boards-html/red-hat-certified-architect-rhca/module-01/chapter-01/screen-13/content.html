Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Pitfalls & Safety
Frequent mistakes
  • Insufficient privileges lead to incomplete data from perf or strace.
  • Long strace sessions on production skew timing and hide true behavior.
  • Assuming interactive shell limits apply to services launched by systemd or container runtimes.
Safe practice checklist
Always record baseline metrics with timestamps before making changes.
Test changes in staging and apply one change at a time for clear attribution.
Prepare a rollback plan and document results.
Key Takeaway

Good measurement plus staged, reversible changes keeps production safe while improving performance.
