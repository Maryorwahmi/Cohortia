Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Troubleshooting Workflow
Step-by-step
  1. Observe: top, vmstat, sar to classify the resource affected.
  2. Narrow: pidstat, top, ss to find candidate processes and sockets.
  3. Evidence: perf for CPU, iostat for devices, strace for syscalls, lsof for descriptors.
  4. Test: validate hypothesis in staging and measure before/after.
  5. Document & rollback: record changes and have a rollback plan.
Scenario example
A web application shows high response latency. Observe: vmstat shows rising swap and iowait. Narrow: pidstat shows process PID 234 with heavy IO. Evidence: iostat shows disk util at 99% and high await. Action: test in staging by increasing disk throughput or resizing instance. Document results and roll back if user-facing errors increase.
Key Takeaway

Follow a repeatable workflow: observe, narrow, gather evidence, test in staging, document and rollback.
