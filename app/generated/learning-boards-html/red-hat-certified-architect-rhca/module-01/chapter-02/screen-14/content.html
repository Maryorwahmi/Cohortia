Avoid these mistakes
A checklist to keep tuning safe and reversible.
  • Test in a VM snapshot before applying to production
  • Change one parameter at a time and monitor impact
  • Persist only validated values in /etc/sysctl.d with descriptive filenames
  • Rebuild initramfs after blacklisting modules used early in boot
  • Backup GRUB config before editing and have rescue media ready
Audit and documentation
Record why each sysctl, blacklist entry, or tuned profile was changed and who approved it, so future admins can understand the intent.
Key Takeaway

A disciplined, documented workflow prevents accidental downtime and creates predictable, auditable tuning at scale.
