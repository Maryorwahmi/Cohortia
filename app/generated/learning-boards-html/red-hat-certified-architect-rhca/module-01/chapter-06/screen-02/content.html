Collect sosreport Securely

03
Configure and Test kdump
Ensure kdump reserves memory, saves vmcore reliably, and is tested in VMs so kernel panics produce analyzable dumps.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Advanced journalctl & systemd Debugging
Use unit and field filters, time ranges, and priority filters with journalctl, and inspect unit properties to find common systemd issues like ExecStart errors and dependency problems.
