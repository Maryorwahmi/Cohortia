Scenario: Intermittent Reboots
Check kdump and /var/crash for vmcore. Collect sosreport for a system snapshot. Use journalctl -k and monitoring metrics to correlate crashes with prior resource spikes or driver messages. If kdump was not enabled, document that and enable it for future data capture.
Reflection Prompt
What evidence would convince you a kernel bug caused crashes versus a hardware or configuration issue? List the commands and artifacts you would gather to support your conclusion.
