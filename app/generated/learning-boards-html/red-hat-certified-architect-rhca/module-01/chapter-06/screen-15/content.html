Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Commands & Artifacts
sosreport
Collect a full system snapshot with sudo sosreport and secure the generated archive for support or baseline comparisons.
kdump / vmcore
Ensure kdump is enabled and test in a VM so vmcore files appear in /var/crash when kernel panics occur.
journalctl & systemctl
Use filters for unit, priority and time; inspect units with systemctl show and systemctl cat to diagnose service failures.
Final Takeaway

Adopt a methodical workflow, collect appropriate artifacts before changing state, and document everything so incidents become learning opportunities.
