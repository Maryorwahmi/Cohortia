Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Document & Communicate
Minimal Incident Record
  1. Time and timezone of observed symptom.
  2. Commands executed and the exact outputs saved to files.
  3. Locations of artifacts: sosreport path and /var/crash vmcore files.
  4. Actions taken, rollbacks attempted, and final status.
Shareable Summary Template
Observed: brief symptom line. Hypothesis: short statement. Tests: commands and outcomes. Artifacts: sosreport at /var/tmp/..., vmcore at /var/crash/..., timestamps attached. Next steps: recommended actions or blockers.
Security
Redact secrets and use secure channels when sharing archives; avoid public attachments with sensitive data.
