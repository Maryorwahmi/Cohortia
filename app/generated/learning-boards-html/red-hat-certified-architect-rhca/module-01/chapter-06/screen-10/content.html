Forensic Timeline (Example)
T0: Network packets dropped (tcpdump)
T1: Application logs show increased retries (journalctl)
T2: I/O wait rises (iostat)
T3: Kernel panic and reboot; vmcore created by kdump
Assemble evidence with consistent timestamps and include recovered artifacts: tcpdump pcap, journalctl excerpts, iostat samples, sosreport archive, and vmcore if present.
Practical Advice
Keep a running incident log with exact commands you ran and the outputs you captured. This makes post-incident analysis and handoffs much more effective.
Misconception Check
Don't assume the first visible error is the root cause. Build the timeline to test causality and look for upstream triggers.
Key Takeaway
A careful timeline turns separate data points into an incident story you can act on and defend during a postmortem.
