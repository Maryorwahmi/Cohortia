Mode comparison
Know the behavior and use-cases for each mode and the safe workflow to implement policy changes.
Enforcing
Blocks violations and logs AVC denials. Use this in production to enforce confinement.
Permissive
Logs denials without blocking. Use briefly to identify missing policy rules.
Disabled
No policy checks. Avoid in production; it removes important protections.
Safe workflow: reproduce the denial in permissive, gather AVCs, use audit2allow to create a .te and .pp, review the .te to keep grants minimal, then semodule -i to load and return to enforcing.
Common mistake
Never use permissive as a permanent fix. It hides policy gaps and increases risk by allowing actions that would be blocked under enforcement.
Pro Tip

Target permissive at a specific domain when possible, not the entire system, to limit exposure while collecting AVC information.
