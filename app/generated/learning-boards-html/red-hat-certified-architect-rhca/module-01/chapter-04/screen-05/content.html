mtr workflow
Run and observe
mtr -c 50 to collect fifty probes and watch packet loss and latency per hop. Repeat runs to see persistent patterns.
Interpretation
  • Loss begins at a hop and continues: suspect that hop or downstream link.
  • Loss only at final hop: problem likely at destination or its host firewall.
  • High latency spikes at a hop with no end-to-end loss require correlation with application behavior.
Misconception
Some routers deprioritize ICMP. Apparent loss in mtr may not equal TCP failure; always correlate with tcpdump or application tests.
Key Takeaway

Use mtr as a continuous diagnostic to locate the first hop with sustained loss or latency rise, then gather packet captures for confirmation.

Action
If loss appears at hop 3 and persists, collect tcpdump at an endpoint and open a support ticket with the provider including mtr output.
Pro tip
Run mtr toward multiple destinations to verify whether the problem is path-specific or widespread.
