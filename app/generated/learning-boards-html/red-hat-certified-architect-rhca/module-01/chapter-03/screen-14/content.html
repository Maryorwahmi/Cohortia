Summary › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
LVM Snapshots
Copy-on-write volumes that need sizing and monitoring.
VDO
Deduplication and compression at the block layer; verify with vdostats.
LUKS
Full-disk encryption; protect passphrases and headers to avoid data loss.
Takeaway

Use snapshots for fast recovery, VDO for storage savings when appropriate, and LUKS for data protection — always verify and protect critical metadata and keys.
