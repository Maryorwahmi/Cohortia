Advanced Storage Management and Troubleshooting
Snapshots, VDO, Stratis, iSCSI/NFS, LUKS, and filesystem repair strategies for RHEL
This chapter shows practical commands and recovery workflows you can apply in cloud or on-premise RHEL servers. You will practice creating LVM snapshots safely, deploying VDO for deduplication and compression, using Stratis for simplified local pools, diagnosing iSCSI and NFS client issues, configuring LUKS for full-disk encryption, and running filesystem repairs with the correct precautions. Each lesson highlights performance trade-offs and common mistakes to avoid.

LVM
Flexible logical volumes, snapshots via copy-on-write.
Use snapshots for consistent backups without downtime. Monitor snapshot size to avoid overflow.
VDO
Block-level dedupe and compression.
Verify savings with vdostats and watch underlying device health.
Stratis
Simplified pool and filesystem management.
Good for developer-focused ephemeral workloads; use stratis CLI and check stratisd logs when troubleshooting.
LUKS & Repair
Full-disk encryption and filesystem recovery tools.
Keep passphrases and keys secure. Never run xfs_repair on a mounted filesystem.
