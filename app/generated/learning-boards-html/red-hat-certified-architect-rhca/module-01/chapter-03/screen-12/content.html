Repeatable triage
Use this ordered checklist to isolate connectivity and performance causes quickly.
1 Network
Ping, traceroute, firewall rules, MTU mismatches.
2 Services
iscsid, nfs-client.target — ensure active.
3 Discovery
iscsiadm discovery or showmount -e for NFS.
4 Mounts
lsblk, mount, df -h to inspect state and usage.
5 Performance
iostat, dd, and sar to measure latency and throughput.
6 Logs
journalctl, server logs, and dmesg for kernel hints.
Key Takeaway

A methodical checklist prevents chasing symptoms and helps you escalate to server or network teams with concrete evidence.
