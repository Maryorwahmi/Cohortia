Comparison Runner vs Controller: Decision Guide

Run on CI runner

  • Pros: low setup complexity for quick tasks, direct control from pipeline.
  • Cons: credentials often stored in CI variables, fragmented logs, and weaker RBAC.

Trigger Ansible Controller

  • Pros: centralized credentials, RBAC, inventories, and auditing.
  • Cons: initial integration work and slight orchestration overhead.
Assessment example from the chapter: for a GitLab CI pipeline deploying to AWS while requiring secure credential management, centralized logging, and RBAC, the recommended integration is to have GitLab trigger Controller Job Templates. This approach leverages Controller's credential types and audit trails and keeps secret material out of CI runner storage.
