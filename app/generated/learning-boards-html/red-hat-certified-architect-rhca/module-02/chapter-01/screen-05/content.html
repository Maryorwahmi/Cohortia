Definition

Private Automation Hub

A private repository for certified and custom Ansible content and Execution Environment images that enforces governance and versioning.

Governance & certification
Maintain a curated set of collections and prevent unvetted changes from reaching production.
Air-gapped support
Host content internally for environments without external network access.
Example & risks
Concrete example
A regulated organization publishes vetted collections and EE images to a private hub, ensuring teams use approved versions and simplifying audits.
Why it matters
Without a private hub, inconsistent dependencies and unapproved public content increase maintenance and compliance risk.
Common misconception
Thinking the hub is optional; in many enterprise and air-gapped contexts content governance is a must-have for predictable automation.
Key Takeaway

Use Automation Hub to enforce versioned, certified content and make automation repeatable and auditable across teams.
