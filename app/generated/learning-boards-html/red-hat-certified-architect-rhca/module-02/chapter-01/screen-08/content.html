Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Deployment Topologies
Single Node

Simple & quick

Controller, DB, and runtime on one server. Good for PoC and small environments.

Limitations: no HA, constrained concurrency, and single point of failure.
Clustered Controller

Production-ready

Multiple Controllers with an external PostgreSQL database for high availability and scale.

Requires careful DB sizing, load balancing, and session design.
Distributed Automation Mesh

Scale & locality

Central control plane with execution nodes placed near managed infrastructure for latency and throughput.

Best for global deployments and air-gapped segments.
Decision guidance
  • Start with expected concurrency and growth expectations, not just current budget.
  • Design database and backup strategy early for clustered deployments.
  • Use mesh when you need local execution or operate in multiple disconnected segments.
Key Takeaway

Match topology to target concurrency, availability, and proximity needs rather than convenience alone.
