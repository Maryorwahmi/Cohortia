Question

Design for global HA, locality, and governance

Which topology meets high availability, localized execution, and strict content governance?

Correct reasoning
Clustered Controller for HA, Private Automation Hub for governance, and distributed Automation Mesh for locality.
Why alternatives fail
  • Single-node lacks HA and cannot scale to global concurrency.
  • Multiple standalone Controllers fragment governance and complicate management.
  • Using public repositories alone fails governance and auditability.
Key Takeaway

When evaluating architectures, explicitly connect each requirement to the component that satisfies it and note the operational trade-offs.
