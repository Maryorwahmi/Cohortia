Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Disaster Recovery Strategy
DR components
  • PostgreSQL streaming replication or managed DB replicas to a secondary region for low RPO.
  • Automation Hub content synchronized to replicated storage or object stores.
  • Standby Controllers provisioned and connected to the standby DB with kept configuration bundles.
Common mistake

Treating backups as a substitute for replication. Backups restore data but do not meet low RTOs without automation and warm standby infrastructure.

Failover checklist (example)
  1. Verify DB replication status and failover the replica.
  2. Point Controllers or standby Controllers to the promoted DB.
  3. Switch load balancer or DNS to the standby site and validate access.
  4. Run smoke playbooks to confirm automation functionality.
RTO vs RPO
RTO defines maximum acceptable downtime; RPO defines maximum acceptable data loss. Use replication method to meet these targets.
Concrete scenario
If RTO is 4 hours and RPO is 1 hour, implement streaming replication and a warm standby so you can promote replicas and restore services within the required window.
