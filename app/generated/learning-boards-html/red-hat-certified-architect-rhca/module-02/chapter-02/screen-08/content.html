Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › PostgreSQL Deployment Options
Managed Database Services
Benefits: built-in HA, automated backups, less DBA overhead. Drawbacks: limited low-level control, cloud dependency, potential cost considerations.
When to choose
Cloud deployments with limited DBA resources or when reducing operational complexity is a priority.
On-premises HA PostgreSQL
Benefits: full control, compliance with data residency, deep tuning. Drawbacks: requires skilled DBAs, more operational overhead for failover and backups.
When to choose
Strict compliance requirements, or when cloud-managed services are not permitted by policy.
Performance tuning notes
Tune work_mem, shared_buffers, and max_connections according to workload. Monitor queries and connections under load and adjust accordingly. Whether managed or on-prem, monitor DB health closely as it is the platform’s single source of truth.
