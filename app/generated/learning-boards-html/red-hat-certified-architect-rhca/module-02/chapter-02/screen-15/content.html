Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
High Availability

Redundancy of Controllers and external DB prevent single points of failure and allow failover without data loss.

Automation Mesh

Offload execution to Execution Nodes near managed hosts for better performance and independent scaling.

Disaster Recovery

Replication, warm standby, and documented failover playbooks are required to meet RTO/RPO objectives.

Takeaway

Combine HA, Automation Mesh placement, DR replication, and monitoring to build a resilient automation platform that supports business continuity.
