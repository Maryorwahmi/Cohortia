Role directory layout
Consistent role structure makes content discoverable and portable.
roles/
  webserver/
    tasks/main.yml
    handlers/main.yml
    templates/nginx.conf.j2
    files/
    vars/main.yml
    defaults/main.yml
    meta/main.yml
Concrete example
A webserver role installs nginx, applies a template, creates a secrets file from a vault variable, and notifies a restart handler. That same role can be used across environments by supplying different variables via inventory.
Common mistake
Duplicating tasks in playbooks instead of extracting them to roles creates maintenance debt and inconsistent behavior across runs.
Teaching visual — role contents
Tasks
Idempotent operations that ensure desired state.
Handlers
Triggered on change to restart or reload services safely.
Templates
Configuration files rendered with variables like inventory_hostname.
Vars & Vault
Role vars may come from encrypted files to avoid exposing secrets.
Key Takeaway

Keep roles focused and environment-agnostic. Supply environment-specific settings through inventory or group vars, not role internals.
