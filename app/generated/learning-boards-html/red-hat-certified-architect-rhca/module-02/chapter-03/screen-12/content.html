Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Modules & Plugins
Reuse existing collections
Search Galaxy and your private hub for modules and plugins that match your needs. Certified content reduces risk.
  • Prefer community or vendor modules to reduce maintenance.
  • Document any selection and the supported variable interface.
When to write a custom module
Only after a thorough search. Keep custom modules focused and include tests and documentation. Package them in a collection for distribution.
Key Takeaway

Curate and reuse vetted collections. Custom modules are a last resort and must be packaged and documented for team use.
