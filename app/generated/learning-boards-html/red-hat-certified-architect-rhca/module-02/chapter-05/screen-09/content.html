Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Auditing & Logging
Core Controls
Centralize, protect, and monitor logs
Forward Controller logs to a centralized SIEM, secure them against tampering, and retain per policy for audits and forensic work.
Centralize
Forward /var/log/tower/ to external aggregation.
Protect
Restrict access and ensure tamper detection.
Retain
Keep logs according to regulatory requirements.
Monitor
Alert on suspicious behaviors and changes.
Concrete Example
Configure Controller to forward syslog entries from /var/log/tower/ to your SIEM. Build alerts for repeated failed logins, RBAC changes, credential grants, and unexpected job runs. Ensure only the security team can modify the log forwarding configuration.
Misconception
Relying on local logs only is fragile. Local files can be corrupted or removed during an incident making investigations difficult.
Key Takeaway

Treat logs as immutable evidence: centralize, secure, retain, and monitor to support audits and incident response.
