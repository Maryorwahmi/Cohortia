Intelligent Data Placement and Disaster Recovery

Mastering CRUSH Maps and Multi-site Replication for Resilience

Ceph's CRUSH maps define how data is distributed across OSDs, optimizing for fault tolerance and locality. Multi-site replication ensures business continuity across geographies.

Key Takeaway

CRUSH maps are fundamental for fine-grained data placement and fault domain awareness, while multi-site replication is essential for robust disaster recovery strategies in Ceph.

Pro Tip

Regularly review and test your CRUSH map configurations and multi-site replication failover procedures to ensure they meet your RPO/RTO objectives.

Advanced Ceph Resilience Features

CRUSH Maps

Ceph's algorithm for intelligent data distribution across OSDs, respecting administrator-defined rules for fault domains (e.g., racks, rows).

Multi-site Replication

Asynchronous replication of object and block data between geographically separate Ceph clusters for disaster recovery.

Fault Domain Awareness

CRUSH maps allow you to define physical locations (e.g., hosts, racks) to ensure data redundancy across potential failure points, enhancing overall system resilience.
