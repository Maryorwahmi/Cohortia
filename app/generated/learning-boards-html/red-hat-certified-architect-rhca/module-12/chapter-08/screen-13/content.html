Deploying Nginx with ODF Persistent Storage

Verifying Data Persistence Across Pod Restarts

We'll deploy an Nginx server, mount our PVC, write data, and then verify that the data persists even after the Nginx pod is restarted.

Key Takeaway

Persistent Volumes ensure that application data remains intact and available, even if the pods accessing them are restarted, rescheduled, or deleted.

Pro Tip

Always test data persistence in your development and staging environments to confirm your storage configuration behaves as expected under various conditions.

# 1. Deploy Nginx with the PVC (nginx-deployment.yaml)
apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: nginx-with-pvc
spec:
  replicas: 1
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: nginx-pvc
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: nginx-pvc
    spec:
      containers:
      - name: nginx
        image: nginx:latest
        ports:
        - containerPort: 80
        volumeMounts:
        - name: html-volume
          mountPath: /usr/share/nginx/html
      volumes:
      - name: html-volume
        persistentVolumeClaim:
          claimName: my-nginx-pvc
oc apply -f nginx-deployment.yaml
deployment.apps/nginx-with-pvc created
