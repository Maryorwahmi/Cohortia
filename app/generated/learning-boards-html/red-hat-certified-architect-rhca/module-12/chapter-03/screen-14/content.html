Key Concepts

Reinforcing Core Principles for Red Hat Architectures

Red Hat Identity Management (IdM)

Centralized identity, authentication, authorization, DNS, NTP, and CA services for Red Hat environments.

SELinux (Security-Enhanced Linux)

A mandatory access control (MAC) system that enforces fine-grained security policies on Linux processes and files.

OpenSCAP

An open-source framework for auditing and enforcing security policies and compliance on RHEL systems.

Chapter Summary

Red Hat Identity Management, SELinux, and OpenSCAP are foundational for centralized identity, OS hardening, and compliance auditing in enterprise Red Hat environments.
