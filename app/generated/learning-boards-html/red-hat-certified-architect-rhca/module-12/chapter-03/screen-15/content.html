Key Concepts

Key Takeaways for Robust Red Hat Security

Security Context Constraints (SCCs)

OpenShift resources that control permissions and capabilities for pods, enhancing container security.

Network Policies (OpenShift)

Kubernetes resources that define how groups of pods are allowed to communicate with each other and external network endpoints.

Principle of Least Privilege

Granting users, processes, or applications only the minimum necessary permissions to perform their tasks.

Chapter Summary

OpenShift SCCs and Network Policies secure containers, while the Principle of Least Privilege and Cryptographic Services are vital for overall data protection and access control.
