Satellite and Ansible

Satellite provides lifecycle management for RHEL systems; Ansible automates tasks and ensures configuration consistency across clusters and nodes.

Automate provisioning
Playbooks create consistent clusters and reduce configuration drift that can break HA.
Scale monitoring
Collect resource, storage, and application metrics so failover delays and performance regressions are visible.
Operational checklist
  • Automate configuration and validation with Ansible.
  • Use Satellite for patching and inventory at scale.
  • Estimate monitoring volume and plan retention and alerting policies.
Why this matters
Operational overhead grows with instances; automation keeps HA designs repeatable and auditable.
