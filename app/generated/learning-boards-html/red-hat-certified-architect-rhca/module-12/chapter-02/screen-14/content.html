Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Key Concepts
Scalability
Ability to handle more work by adding resources; prefer horizontal scaling for resilience.
High Availability
Keep services running despite failures; choose active-passive for stateful and active-active for stateless services.
Platform tools
Use Pacemaker/Corosync for node-level HA and OpenShift replicas for app-level scaling; pair with Ceph for persistent storage.
Summary takeaway

Choose the right HA and scaling patterns for the service layer, automate management, and validate with realistic failover tests.
