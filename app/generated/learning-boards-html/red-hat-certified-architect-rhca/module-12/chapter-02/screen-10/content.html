Definition

Distributed storage for HA

Distributed storage replicates data across nodes so persistent volumes remain available when individual nodes fail. It must be designed with performance and recovery time in mind.

Why Ceph
Ceph offers redundancy and scalable storage for block and object needs, making it suitable for OpenShift persistent volumes in HA designs.
Practical checks
  • Confirm access modes for PVs: single-writer versus multi-writer behavior.
  • Plan replication factor and estimate rebuild times during failure.
  • Test PV detachment and reattach during pod rescheduling to validate application continuity.
Misconception
Assuming storage is instantly available after failover; rebuilds and network latencies can affect performance and availability.
