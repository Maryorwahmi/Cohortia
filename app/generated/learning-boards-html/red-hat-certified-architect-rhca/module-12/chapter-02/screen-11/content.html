Process

Failover test checklist

  1. Generate representative load to approximate peak traffic.
  2. Simulate service stop and observe detection and failover timing.
  3. Reboot nodes and observe resource relocation and rescheduling.
  4. Inject network partition and validate quorum/fencing behavior.
Practical guidance
Monitor failover with cluster status commands and platform observability. Capture metrics for failover time and application response. Validate data integrity after storage failover and rebuilds.
Common pitfalls
  • Ignoring monitor intervals that delay detection.
  • Failing to test under load.
  • Assuming shared storage will mount instantly on another node.
Key Takeaway

Testing failover with realistic load and clear metrics is the only reliable way to know whether your HA design meets production requirements.
