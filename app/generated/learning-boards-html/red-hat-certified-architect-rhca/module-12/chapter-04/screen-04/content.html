Disaster Recovery vs. Business Continuity

Understanding the Distinct Focus Areas for Enterprise Resilience

Disaster Recovery (DR)

DR is specifically about the technological aspects: recovering IT infrastructure and data after a catastrophic event. It's a subset of BC.

  • •Focuses on IT systems, applications, and data.
  • •Aims to restore technical operations.
  • •Examples: Server restoration, data backups, network failover.
Concrete Example

Restoring a database from a replicated copy at a secondary data center after a primary site outage.

Business Continuity (BC)

BC is about maintaining essential business functions during and after a disaster, encompassing processes, people, and technology. It's the overarching plan.

  • •Covers all aspects of business operations.
  • •Aims to keep the business running.
  • •Examples: Emergency communication, alternate work sites, supply chain resilience.
Why it Matters

A comprehensive BC plan ensures that an organization can survive and recover from any major disruption, not just IT failures.
