Testing Your DR Plan and Avoiding Common Mistakes

Ensuring Readiness and Reliability Through Regular Drills

The Importance of DR Testing

An untested DR plan is merely a guess. Regular, full DR drills are essential to validate procedures and ensure readiness.

  • •Simulate disaster scenarios.
  • •Perform failover and recovery steps.
  • •Document every step, including network and DNS changes.
Key Takeaway

Regular testing builds confidence in your DR plan and identifies gaps before a real disaster occurs.

Common DR Planning Mistakes

  • •Neglecting to replicate critical configuration files.
  • •Over-reliance on manual steps prone to error.
  • •Failing to account for network dependencies.
  • •Inadequate capacity or network connectivity at the DR site.
  • •Overlooking security considerations at the DR site.
Pro-Tip

Automate as many DR steps as possible to reduce human error and accelerate recovery times.
