Key Concepts: Advanced DR Strategies

Technologies and Metrics for Robust Recovery

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable amount of data loss, measured in time, from the point of failure.

Data Replication

Copying data to a remote location to ensure availability and prevent data loss.

OpenShift Multi-Cluster DR

Strategies for recovering OpenShift applications across multiple clusters (e.g., active-passive, active-active).

Chapter Summary

RPO dictates data loss tolerance, driving replication strategies. OpenShift DR leverages multi-cluster patterns and ODF for stateful application resilience.
