Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Defining Your Resilience Goals and Architectural Decisions

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable duration of downtime after a disaster. It dictates how quickly systems must be restored.

  • •Time-based metric for system availability.
  • •Influences recovery speed and infrastructure design.
Concrete Example

A critical e-commerce site might have an RTO of 1 hour, meaning it must be fully operational within 60 minutes of an outage.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable amount of data loss, measured in time, from the point of failure. It dictates how current recovered data must be.

  • •Data-based metric for acceptable loss.
  • •Influences backup frequency and replication methods.
Common Misconception

Confusing RTO and RPO. RTO is about time to recover, RPO is about how much data is lost. Both are crucial but distinct.
