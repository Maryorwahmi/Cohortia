OpenShift Data Foundation for Stateful Application DR

Simplifying Persistent Volume Replication Across OpenShift Clusters

ODF for Persistent Volume DR

OpenShift Data Foundation (ODF), built on Ceph, provides multi-cluster data replication for persistent volumes, simplifying DR for stateful applications.

  • •Enables asynchronous replication of PVs.
  • •Uses DRPolicy to define replication between clusters.
  • •Crucial for stateful applications in multi-cluster OpenShift.
Key Takeaway

ODF streamlines the complex task of replicating persistent data for OpenShift applications, making multi-cluster DR more achievable.
