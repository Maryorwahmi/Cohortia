RHEL DR Strategies

Application Failover with RHEL High Availability

Extending HA for Cross-Site Resilience with Careful Design

RHEL HA Add-On (Pacemaker/Corosync)

Provides robust high availability within a single data center. Can be extended for cross-site DR, but with significant challenges.

  • •Manages application resources and failover.
  • •Challenges: network latency, quorum management, split-brain.
Common Mistake

Underestimating the complexity of stretching a Pacemaker/Corosync cluster across geographically distant data centers due to network issues.

Primary/Standby Site Approach

A more common and manageable DR strategy for dispersed sites, involving a primary and a warm or cold standby site.

  • •**Warm Standby:** Infrastructure and applications partially running, faster recovery.
  • •**Cold Standby:** Infrastructure available but requires more setup, higher RTO, lower cost.
Key Takeaway

For cross-site DR, a primary/standby model often provides a better balance of resilience, cost, and operational simplicity than stretched HA clusters.
