OpenShift DR Strategies

Multi-Cluster Disaster Recovery for OpenShift

Active-Passive and Active-Active Approaches for Containerized Workloads

Active-Passive Multi-Cluster

Primary cluster runs applications; standby cluster in DR site is activated only during a disaster.

  • •Applications deployed on primary.
  • •Failover requires re-deployment and data restoration.
  • •Automation with OpenShift GitOps (Argo CD).
Benefit

Simpler to manage and lower cost than Active-Active, suitable for moderate RTO/RPO.

Active-Active Multi-Cluster

Applications run simultaneously on two or more OpenShift clusters in different geographical locations.

  • •Lowest RTO and RPO.
  • •Requires global load balancing.
  • •Complex data synchronization for stateful apps.
Challenge

High complexity and cost due to continuous synchronization and global traffic management.
