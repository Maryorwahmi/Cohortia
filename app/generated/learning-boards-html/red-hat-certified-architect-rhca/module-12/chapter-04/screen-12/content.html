Key Concepts: Foundational DR/BC

Essential Terms for Enterprise Resilience

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The process of recovering and restoring IT infrastructure and data after a catastrophic event.

Business Continuity (BC)

The ability of an organization to maintain essential business functions during and after a disaster.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable duration of downtime after a disaster.

Chapter Summary

DR and BC are distinct but complementary. RTO defines how quickly systems must be restored, guiding recovery strategies.
