Ansible Automation Platform — the glue
Use automation to make provisioning, configuration, and policy enforcement repeatable and auditable.
  • Automate provisioning of RHEL nodes and registration with Satellite and IdM.
  • Deploy applications and infrastructure configuration to OpenShift and VMs.
  • Use automation artifacts as versioned architecture contracts.
Concrete example
A playbook sequence creates RHEL nodes, registers them with Satellite, applies security hardening, integrates them into IdM groups, and then deploys container runtime settings required by OpenShift, all in a repeatable pipeline.
Misconception
Automation is optional — it is not. Without it, scaling and compliance become manual, error-prone tasks.
Pro tip
Keep automation declarative, test playbooks in staging, and treat playbooks as part of your architecture repository.
