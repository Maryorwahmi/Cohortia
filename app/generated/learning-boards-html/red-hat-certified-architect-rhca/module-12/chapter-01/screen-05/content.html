Process: Capture → Rank → Map → Integrate
Follow these steps to avoid mismatched product selection.
1. Capture
Interview stakeholders to gather availability, compliance, performance, and operational constraints.
2. Rank
Prioritize NFRs so trade-offs are explicit before product selection.
3. Map
Assign responsibilities to RHEL, OpenShift, Ceph, Satellite, IdM, and Ansible based on NFRs.
4. Integrate
Design identity, storage, network, and automation interactions and define lifecycle tasks.
Example scenario
A regulated finance app needs 99.99% availability and encrypted storage. Rank availability and encryption highest, then map to product choices: OpenShift for HA and automatic scaling, Ceph for encrypted persistent volumes, Satellite for consistent patching, IdM for centralized access control, and Ansible to automate deployment and compliance checks.
Common mistake
Selecting products before ranking NFRs, which can lead to expensive refactors.
