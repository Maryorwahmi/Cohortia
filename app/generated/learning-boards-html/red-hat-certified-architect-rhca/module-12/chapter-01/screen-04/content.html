Holistic Architecture — definition and scope
Understand scope, responsibilities, and lifecycle obligations across the Red Hat portfolio.
Definition
A system-level design approach that maps NFRs to product roles and plans for the entire lifecycle, from deployment to retirement.
Concrete example
Modernize a legacy Java app: decide between containerizing for OpenShift or running on RHEL VMs based on scalability, compatibility, and team skill set.
Key points
  • Map each NFR to a product responsibility and a lifecycle action.
  • Plan automation and identity integration early to avoid operational gaps.
  • Design for typical loads and scale patterns, not only rare peaks.
Misconception
Point solutions equal architecture — false. Integration and lifecycle are required.
Pro tip
Capture NFRs in a short table and map them to product choices before diving into diagrams.
