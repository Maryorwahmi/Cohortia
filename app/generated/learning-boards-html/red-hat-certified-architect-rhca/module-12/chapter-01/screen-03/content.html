Learning Objectives — Applied Skills
Turn architecture theory into a hybrid cloud platform sketch you can defend.
01 Select core products
Pick RHEL, OpenShift, Ansible, Ceph, Satellite, and IdM for specific roles and write concise justifications linked to NFRs.
02 Sketch interoperability
Describe identity flow, storage attachments, and how automation moves artifacts between systems.
03 Lifecycle planning
Show patch delivery, content lifecycle, and decommissioning strategies using Satellite and Ansible.
04 Risk and trade-offs
Assess cost, skills, and operational risk; propose mitigation such as staged rollouts and automation runbooks.
