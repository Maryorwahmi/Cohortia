Modernize or Migrate?
Use a checklist to decide between containerizing for OpenShift or moving to RHEL VMs.
Containerize (OpenShift)
Best for stateless, modular services needing autoscaling and rapid deployments.
Keep on RHEL VM
Best for monoliths with OS-level dependencies or strict compatibility constraints.
Scenario and guidance
A monolithic app with custom kernel modules remains on RHEL VM while front-end services are containerized. Plan automation for both paths: Ansible for VM provisioning and CI/CD for container pipelines. Include rollback strategies and test environments for either path.
Common mistake
Choosing containerization because it is trendy, without assessing lifecycle or team readiness.
Pro tip
Prototype one component on OpenShift to validate assumptions before committing to a full migration.
