Identity and Access: Red Hat IdM
Centralize authentication and authorization to enforce least privilege and consistent auditing.
  • Use IdM groups to map operator and developer roles across systems.
  • Plan service accounts and machine identities as part of design.
  • Automate IdM updates within Ansible lifecycles.
Practical note
Integrate IdM with OpenShift project roles and Satellite permissions so group-based policies control both deployments and lifecycle actions. Automate group management to keep identity current as teams change.
Common mistake
Letting products silo identity leads to inconsistent privileges and audit gaps.
Check
Verify that IdM groups map to OpenShift RBAC and Satellite roles for unified control.
