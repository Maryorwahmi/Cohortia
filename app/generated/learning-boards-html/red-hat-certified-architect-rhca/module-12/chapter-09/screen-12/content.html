Common GRC Mistakes and Best Practices

Avoiding pitfalls and ensuring continuous compliance

Mistake 1: One-Time Compliance

Treating compliance as a periodic check rather than a continuous process.

Mistake 2: Lack of Integration

Failing to integrate GRC tools and processes into daily IT operations.

Mistake 3: Poor Documentation

Neglecting to document policies, procedures, and remediation efforts.

Best Practices for GRC

  • Continuous Compliance: Monitor and enforce compliance constantly.
  • Integrate GRC Tools: Embed tools like OpenSCAP and Ansible into daily workflows.
  • Document Everything: Maintain clear, up-to-date policies and procedures.
  • Rigorous Testing: Always test automated remediation in non-production environments.
  • Immutable Audit Trails: Protect logs from tampering for forensic integrity.
  • Regular Review: Update GRC policies as regulations and technologies evolve.
Key Takeaway

Avoid common GRC pitfalls by adopting a continuous, integrated, and well-documented approach with rigorous testing of automated solutions.
