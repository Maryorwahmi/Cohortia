Why Multi-Factor Authentication?

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds a crucial layer of security by requiring users to provide two or more verification factors to gain access. This significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access even if one factor, like a password, is compromised.

Example: SIEM Alert for Failed Logins

A SIEM system detects 10 failed SSH login attempts to a CDE server from an unknown IP address within 5 minutes. This triggers an immediate high-priority alert to the security operations center, allowing for rapid investigation and response to a potential brute-force attack.

Key Takeaway

Centralized access control with Red Hat IdM and comprehensive, SIEM-integrated logging are fundamental for PCI DSS compliance and incident detection.
