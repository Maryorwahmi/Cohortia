Proactive Risk Management

Identifying and mitigating threats in Red Hat environments

Identify Risks

Discover potential security, operational, and compliance threats.

Assess Vulnerabilities

Evaluate the likelihood and impact of identified risks.

Mitigate Threats

Implement controls and strategies to reduce risk exposure.

Red Hat Tools for Risk Mitigation

  • SELinux: Mandatory Access Control for RHEL.
  • Red Hat IdM: Centralized identity and access management.
  • OpenShift SCCs: Control container privileges.
  • OpenShift Network Policies: Regulate network traffic between pods.
  • OpenSCAP: Vulnerability scanning and security auditing.

Continuous Process

Risk management is not a one-time event. It requires continuous monitoring, reassessment, and adaptation to new threats and changes in the IT landscape.

Key Takeaway

Proactive risk management, supported by Red Hat's security features, is crucial for protecting IT assets and ensuring operational resilience.
