Why SIEM Integration is Crucial

A SIEM system aggregates logs from various sources, normalizes them, and applies correlation rules to identify potential threats or policy violations that individual logs might miss. This centralized view is vital for detecting complex attack patterns and ensuring rapid incident response.

Example: Event to be Logged

Failed login attempts to RHEL servers via SSH. A SIEM can detect multiple failed attempts from an unusual IP address within a short period, indicating a brute-force attack, and trigger an alert for immediate investigation.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive logging and SIEM integration are fundamental for detecting security incidents, proving compliance, and ensuring accountability across Red Hat environments.
