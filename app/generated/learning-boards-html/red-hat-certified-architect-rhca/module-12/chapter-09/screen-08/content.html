Ansible for Compliance Remediation

Automating fixes for non-compliant findings

Identify Non-Compliance

OpenSCAP reports highlight deviations from compliance benchmarks.

Automate Fixes

Ansible playbooks apply necessary configuration changes to restore compliance.

Continuous Compliance

Ensures systems quickly return to a compliant state after any drift.

Example: Disabling a Non-Compliant Service

- name: Ensure telnet service is disabled and masked
  systemd:
    name: telnet.socket
    state: stopped
    enabled: no
    masked: yes

This Ansible task ensures the telnet service, often a compliance risk, is stopped, disabled, and masked across target systems.

Safety Note: Test Remediation

Always rigorously test Ansible remediation playbooks in non-production environments. Incorrectly applied fixes can lead to critical service disruptions.

Key Takeaway

Ansible Automation Platform is critical for automating the remediation of compliance findings, ensuring systems remain secure and compliant.
