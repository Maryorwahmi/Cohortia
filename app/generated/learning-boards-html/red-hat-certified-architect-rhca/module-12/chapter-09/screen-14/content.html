Key Concepts: GRC Fundamentals

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Governance

The framework of rules, policies, and processes that guide an organization's IT operations and decision-making.

Risk Management

The process of identifying, assessing, and mitigating threats and vulnerabilities to IT assets.

Compliance

Adherence to external laws, regulations, industry standards, and internal policies.

Summary Takeaway

GRC provides the essential framework for secure, accountable, and legally compliant IT operations in any enterprise environment.
