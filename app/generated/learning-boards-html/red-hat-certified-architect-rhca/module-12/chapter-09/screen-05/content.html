Establishing Effective Governance

Defining IT policies and accountability in Red Hat environments

Policy Definition

Clear policies for architectural standards, security, and operational procedures.

Accountability

Assigning roles and responsibilities for IT decisions and operations.

Operational Guidance

Directives for RHEL provisioning, OpenShift configuration, and automation management.

Red Hat Governance Examples

  • All RHEL servers must use a specific tuned profile.
  • OpenShift clusters configured with defined Security Context Constraints (SCCs).
  • Automation playbooks managed and approved via Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform.
  • RHEL systems enrolled in Red Hat Satellite for patching and content.

Common Misconception

Governance is often seen as purely bureaucratic. However, effective governance provides clear guidelines that streamline operations, reduce ambiguity, and foster accountability, ultimately enhancing efficiency and security.

Key Takeaway

Governance provides the essential structure and policies that ensure IT operations align with organizational goals and regulatory requirements.
