Core Concept

Understanding GRC in Enterprise IT

Defining Governance, Risk, and Compliance for Red Hat environments

Governance

Establishes the framework for IT decision-making, accountability, and guiding policies.

Risk Management

Identifies, assesses, and mitigates potential threats and vulnerabilities to IT assets.

Compliance

Ensures adherence to applicable laws, regulations, industry standards, and internal policies.

Why GRC Matters

  • Avoids legal penalties and fines.
  • Protects sensitive data and intellectual property.
  • Maintains customer trust and brand reputation.
  • Ensures operational stability and business continuity.
  • Streamlines audits and reporting processes.

Red Hat's Role in GRC

Red Hat technologies provide robust features and automation capabilities that directly support GRC initiatives. From security-enhanced Linux (SELinux) to Ansible Automation Platform and OpenSCAP, Red Hat offers tools to define policies, mitigate risks, and enforce compliance across your infrastructure.

Key Takeaway

GRC is a holistic approach to managing an organization's overall governance, enterprise risk management, and compliance with regulations.
