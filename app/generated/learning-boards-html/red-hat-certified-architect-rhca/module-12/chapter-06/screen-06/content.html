Ansible Collections: Packaging Automation

Standardizing Content for Sharing and Consumption

What's in a Collection?

  • •Roles: Reusable automation units.
  • •Modules: Core Ansible functionality.
  • •Plugins: Extend Ansible's capabilities (e.g., inventory, lookup).
  • •Documentation: Essential for understanding and using the content.
Key Benefit

Collections simplify sharing and consuming automation content across teams or organizations, ensuring consistency and reducing dependency issues.
