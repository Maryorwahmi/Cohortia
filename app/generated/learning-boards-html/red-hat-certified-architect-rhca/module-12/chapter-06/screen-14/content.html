Key Concepts to Remember

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Ansible Automation Platform

Red Hat's enterprise solution for automation, including Ansible Engine, Tower/AWX, and Collections.

Ansible Roles

Reusable, modular units of automation that encapsulate tasks, variables, templates, and handlers.

Ansible Collections

A standardized format for packaging and distributing Ansible content (roles, modules, plugins).

Chapter Summary

This chapter emphasized designing scalable, maintainable, and secure automation strategies using Ansible, focusing on content structure, enterprise management, and integration with cloud-native platforms.
