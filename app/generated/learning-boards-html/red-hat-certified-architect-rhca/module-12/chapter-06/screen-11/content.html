Automating Patching & Hardening

Ensuring a Secure and Up-to-Date Infrastructure

Key Automation Areas

  • •Patch Management: Keep systems updated using Red Hat Satellite or dnf.
  • •Security Hardening: Apply SELinux policies, configure firewalls, enforce password policies.
  • •Configuration Drift: Automatically correct unauthorized changes to maintain desired state.
Why it Matters

Automated security tasks reduce human error, ensure consistent application of policies, and significantly lower the risk of security breaches.
