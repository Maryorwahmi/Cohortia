Why Hybrid? Flexibility & Consistency

Red Hat OpenShift as the Universal Application Platform

Primary Drivers

  • Flexibility: Choose the best environment for each workload based on cost, performance, or compliance.
  • Avoid Vendor Lock-in: Distribute workloads across multiple providers to maintain control and leverage diverse services.
  • Compliance & Data Sovereignty: Keep sensitive data on-premise while utilizing public cloud for less sensitive workloads.
Key Takeaway

OpenShift provides a consistent Kubernetes platform across on-premise and public clouds, simplifying development and operations for hybrid strategies.

OpenShift: The Hybrid Cloud Bridge

Consistent Platform: Kubernetes-based, runs identically anywhere.

Simplified Operations: Reduces complexity in development and deployment.

Application Portability: Applications behave identically across environments.
