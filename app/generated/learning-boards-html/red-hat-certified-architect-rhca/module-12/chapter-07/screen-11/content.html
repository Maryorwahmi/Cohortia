Centralized Hybrid Cloud Management

Introducing Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes

What is Red Hat ACM?

Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes (ACM) is a management platform designed to help organizations deploy, manage, and observe multiple Kubernetes clusters across hybrid and multi-cloud environments. It provides a 'single pane of glass' for consistent operations.

Challenge

Managing disparate OpenShift clusters across on-premise and public clouds.

Solution

ACM provides unified visibility, control, and automation for all managed clusters.

Key Takeaway

ACM simplifies the complexity of multi-cluster management in hybrid cloud, offering a consistent operational experience from a single control plane.

ACM: The Single Pane of Glass
ACM provides a unified view and control plane for all your OpenShift clusters, regardless of their location.
