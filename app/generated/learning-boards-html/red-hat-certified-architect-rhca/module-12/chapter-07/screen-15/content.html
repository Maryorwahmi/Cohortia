CHAPTER SUMMARY

Advanced Hybrid Cloud Concepts

Key Tools and Principles

Red Hat ACM

A management platform for deploying, managing, and observing multiple Kubernetes clusters across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

Data Gravity

The concept that data tends to attract applications and services to its location due to cost and latency of movement.

Network Connectivity

Secure and high-bandwidth connections (VPNs, Direct Connect) between on-premise and public cloud environments.

Workload Placement

Strategic decision-making about where to run specific applications or services (on-premise vs. public cloud) based on requirements.

Summary Takeaway

Effective hybrid cloud design requires leveraging tools like ACM, understanding data gravity, ensuring robust network connectivity, and making strategic workload placement decisions.
