Embracing Multi-Cloud

Deploying Across Multiple Public Cloud Providers

Multi-Cloud Benefits & Challenges
Benefits
  • Increased Resilience
  • Avoid Vendor Lock-in
  • Leverage Best-of-Breed Services
Challenges
  • Increased Management Complexity
  • Consistent Networking & Security
  • Data Transfer Costs & Latency
Example Scenario

A global media company uses AWS for its primary content delivery network, Azure for its enterprise applications, and GCP for specialized machine learning workloads, all managed through a unified OpenShift layer.

Key Takeaway

Multi-cloud enhances resilience and flexibility but requires careful planning for consistent management, networking, and security across diverse providers.
