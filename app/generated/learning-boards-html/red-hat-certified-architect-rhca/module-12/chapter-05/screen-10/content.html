Capacity Planning for OpenShift

Ensuring scalable and efficient containerized environments

Key Factors for OpenShift

Capacity planning in OpenShift requires evaluating resources for pods and underlying worker nodes.

  • Pod Density: How many pods per worker node?
  • Resource Requests/Limits: Proper CPU/memory allocation.
  • Storage Growth: Scaling persistent volumes (e.g., Ceph).
Benefit: Prevents resource starvation and ensures fair sharing among applications.
Common Mistake

Omitting resource requests and limits for pods, leading to unpredictable performance and resource contention.
