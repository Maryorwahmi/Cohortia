Introduction to Capacity Planning

Predicting future resource needs for sustainable growth

What is Capacity Planning?

The process of determining the resources required to meet future demand, based on historical data and growth projections.

  • Proactive, not reactive.
  • Ensures infrastructure can handle future workloads.
  • Prevents performance degradation and outages.
Benefit: Avoids costly over-provisioning and ensures resources are available when needed.
Misconception

Capacity planning is only for large enterprises. All organizations benefit from understanding and planning for growth.

Key Considerations

  • Historical Trends: Analyze past resource utilization.
  • Anticipated Growth: Factor in business expansion, new projects.
  • Workload Characteristics: Understand resource demands of applications.
  • Resource Types: CPU, memory, storage, network.
Example: An e-commerce site planning for increased traffic during holiday sales, ensuring enough server capacity.
Key Takeaway

Capacity planning is a proactive, data-driven process to predict future resource needs, ensuring your Red Hat infrastructure scales efficiently and cost-effectively.
