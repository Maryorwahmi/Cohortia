Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

Capacity Planning

The process of determining the resources required to meet future demand, based on historical data and growth projections.

Resource Requests & Limits

Kubernetes mechanisms to define the minimum (request) and maximum (limit) CPU and memory resources for a container.

Horizontal Pod Autoscaler (HPA)

An OpenShift/Kubernetes feature that automatically scales the number of pod replicas based on observed CPU utilization or other metrics.

Chapter Insight

Proactive capacity planning, coupled with intelligent resource management and autoscaling, is fundamental for building resilient and high-performing Red Hat cloud-native environments.
