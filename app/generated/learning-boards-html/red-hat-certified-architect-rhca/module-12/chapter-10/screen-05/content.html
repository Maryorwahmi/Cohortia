Key Components of Architectural Documentation

Providing the right level of detail for every audience

01

Executive Summary

High-level overview for non-technical stakeholders, focusing on business problems, proposed solutions, and key benefits. Avoids jargon.

02

Architectural Overview

More technical summary, describing major components, their interactions, and the overall design philosophy. Provides a conceptual understanding.

03

Detailed Design

In-depth descriptions of each component, including technology choices (e.g., RHEL 9, OpenShift 4.14), configuration specifics, and integration points.

04

Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

Explicitly state how the design addresses scalability, availability, performance, security, and compliance. Crucial for quality assurance.

05

Trade-offs and Decisions

Document alternatives considered and the rationale behind the chosen solution, including any compromises made. Provides transparency.

06

Risks and Mitigation

Identify potential risks (technical, operational, security) and outline strategies to mitigate them. Proactive risk management is key.
