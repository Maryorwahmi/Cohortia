Common Mistakes and Safety Notes

Ensuring accuracy, security, and stakeholder sign-off

Common Documentation Mistakes

  • •Stale Documentation: Not updating documents as the system evolves, leading to inaccuracies.
  • •Overly Generic Diagrams: Using vague symbols or terms instead of specific Red Hat technologies.
  • •Lack of Detail: Insufficient information for implementation or troubleshooting.
  • •Information Overload: Too much irrelevant detail, making key information hard to find.
Pro Tip

Regularly review and validate documentation against the actual deployed system.

Important Safety Notes

  • •Sensitive Information: Never include actual production IP addresses, credentials, or other sensitive data in publicly accessible documentation. Use placeholders.
  • •Stakeholder Sign-off: Always obtain formal approval from relevant stakeholders for critical architectural decisions, especially those impacting security, compliance, or major investments.
  • •Compliance Adherence: Ensure documentation practices align with organizational and regulatory compliance requirements.
  • •Version Control Security: Protect your Git repositories with appropriate access controls.
Key Takeaway

Security and formal approval are non-negotiable aspects of architectural documentation and communication.
