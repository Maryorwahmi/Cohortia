Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms for architectural communication

Architectural Documentation

Written artifacts describing a system's design, structure, behavior, and characteristics.

Architectural Diagrams

Visual representations of system architecture using standard notation to convey structure and relationships.

Stakeholder Communication

Effectively conveying architectural designs and decisions to various audiences (technical, business, executive).

Summary Takeaway

Clear documentation and visual communication are vital for aligning diverse stakeholders with your architectural vision.
