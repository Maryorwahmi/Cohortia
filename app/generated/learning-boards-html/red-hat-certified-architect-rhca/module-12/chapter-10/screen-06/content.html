Executive Summary vs. Architectural Overview

Tailoring your message for different leadership audiences

Executive Summary

  • •Audience: Non-technical stakeholders (e.g., C-suite, business leaders).
  • •Purpose: High-level overview of business problem, proposed solution, and key benefits.
  • •Content: Focus on ROI, strategic alignment, and risk mitigation. Avoids technical jargon.
  • •Example: "This hybrid cloud solution will reduce operational costs by 20% and accelerate time-to-market for new applications."
Key Takeaway

Focus on business value and strategic impact for executive summaries.

Architectural Overview

  • •Audience: Technical stakeholders (e.g., architects, technical leads).
  • •Purpose: Technical summary of major components, interactions, and design philosophy.
  • •Content: Describes system structure, data flows, and technology choices at a conceptual level.
  • •Example: "The solution leverages OpenShift for container orchestration, with Ceph Storage providing persistent volumes for stateful applications."
Pro Tip

Use diagrams extensively in the Architectural Overview to convey complex relationships visually.
