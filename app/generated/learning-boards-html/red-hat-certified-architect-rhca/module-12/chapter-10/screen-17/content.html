Key Concepts: Part 2

Advanced considerations for architectural practice

Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

Criteria defining a system's quality attributes (e.g., performance, security, scalability).

Trade-offs

Evaluating and choosing between competing design options, acknowledging benefits and drawbacks.

Living Documentation

Keeping documentation updated and synchronized with the current state of the system.

Summary Takeaway

A successful architect balances technical design with transparent decision-making and continuous documentation updates.
