Detailed Design & Non-Functional Requirements

Ensuring technical precision and quality attributes

Detailed Design

  • •Component Descriptions: In-depth details for each part of the system.
  • •Technology Choices: Specify versions (e.g., RHEL 9, OpenShift 4.14, Ceph 6).
  • •Configuration Specifics: Granular settings and parameters.
  • •Network Topology: Diagrams and descriptions of network flows and zones.
  • •Security Controls: Authentication, authorization, encryption mechanisms.
  • •Integration Points: How components interact with internal and external systems.
Key Takeaway

The detailed design is the blueprint for implementation, requiring precise technical specifications.

Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

  • •Scalability: How the system handles increased load (e.g., users, data).
  • •Availability: Uptime guarantees (e.g., 99.99% uptime).
  • •Performance: Response times, throughput, latency.
  • •Security: Data protection, access control, vulnerability management.
  • •Compliance: Adherence to regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
  • •Maintainability: Ease of updates, troubleshooting, and evolution.
Common Mistake

Failing to define NFRs clearly can lead to solutions that are technically functional but fail to meet business quality expectations.
