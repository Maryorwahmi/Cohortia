Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Bloom's Progression
Learn
Name and identify core components

Recognize Products as groupings, Repositories as storage, and Content Views as curated snapshots used for promotion.

Understand
Why sync, bandwidth, and storage matter

Initial synchronization pulls RPMs and other content into /var/lib/pulp and can consume significant resources; plan accordingly.

Apply
Create custom products and repositories

Host internal RPMs by creating a product, adding a yum repository, uploading RPMs, and synchronizing the repository.

Master
Design, filter, publish, and promote Content Views

Use filters to include only security and bugfix errata or specific packages, publish immutable versions, and promote through lifecycle environments.
