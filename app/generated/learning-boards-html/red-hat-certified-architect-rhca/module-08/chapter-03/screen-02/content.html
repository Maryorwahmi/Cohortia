Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Differentiate Products, Repositories, and Content Views

Recognize how Products group repositories, how repositories store packages and errata, and how Content Views curate and version content for promotion. This matters because the right abstraction ensures consistent software delivery.

Verified Competency Milestone
02
Perform initial Red Hat content synchronization

Pull entitled products from the CDN into Satellite, planning for bandwidth and storage. This enables offline or localized package hosting and faster host updates.

Verified Competency Milestone
03
Create custom Products and Repositories

Host internal or third-party RPMs by creating a product container and adding a yum repository. This secures distribution and makes internal packages discoverable by hosts.

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Design and publish Content Views

Select repositories, add filters for errata or packages, publish a version, and promote that version through lifecycle environments for controlled rollouts and compliance.

Verified Competency Milestone
