Definitions and relationships
Product
A logical container for related repositories, usually representing a Red Hat offering or a custom collection.
Repository
Storage location for RPMs, errata, or other content. Each repository belongs to a product and has a content type.
Content View
A curated, filtered, and versioned snapshot of content composed from repositories and products for promotion.
Example
RHEL 8 (Product) contains BaseOS and AppStream (Repositories). A Content View bundles those repositories, filters errata to security and bugfix, and publishes an immutable version for promotion.
Supporting points
  • Products are organizational: choose meaningful names aligning to offerings or internal app groups.
  • Repositories hold the actual files; they are synchronized from CDN or uploaded for custom content.
  • Content Views are the delivery contract: publish a version and promote it through lifecycle environments.
Key Takeaway

Think: products organize, repositories store, Content Views deliver.

Common mistake
Assuming editing a published Content View version is possible. Versions are immutable; publish a new version instead.
