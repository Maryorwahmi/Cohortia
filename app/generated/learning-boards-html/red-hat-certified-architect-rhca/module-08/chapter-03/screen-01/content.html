Products, Repositories, and Content Views

Learn how Satellite stores and curates content, from syncing Red Hat products to publishing immutable Content View versions for lifecycle promotion.

Orientation

We will sync Red Hat products, create a custom product and repository for an internal RPM, and publish a Content View that can be promoted across Development, QA, and Production.

Outcome

By the end you'll be able to curate content sets, manage errata inclusion, and construct safe promotion workflows for consistent system updates.

Explore Key Topics
Sync Products

Pull entitled Red Hat product repositories from the CDN into Satellite for offline hosting and distribution.

Custom Products

Create products and yum repositories to host internal RPMs or third-party content securely inside Satellite.

Content Views

Curate, filter, version, and promote content sets so only vetted packages reach Production hosts.
