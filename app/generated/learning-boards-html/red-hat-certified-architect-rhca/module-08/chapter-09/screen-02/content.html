Learn

01 — Routine maintenance

Run and schedule cleanup tasks such as db session clear, content pruning, and Pulp housekeeping to prevent disk exhaustion and slow queries. Why it matters: prevents outages and keeps performance predictable.

Mnemonic: tidy before you scale
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Understand

02 — Backup and restore

Capture full backups with foreman-maintain, store them separately, and test restores regularly to validate recovery plans. Why it matters: avoids data loss and enables controlled recovery after failures.

Mnemonic: capture, copy, confirm
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Apply

03 — Troubleshoot common failures

Use logs and simple checks to resolve content-sync, host registration, and remote-execution problems. Why it matters: quick remediation reduces downtime and user impact.

Mnemonic: logs, connectivity, credentials
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Master

04 — Security hardening & auditing

Implement RBAC, integrate with external IdM, manage certificates, and review audit logs regularly. Why it matters: ensures least privilege, traceability, and compliance.

Mnemonic: authenticate, authorize, audit
