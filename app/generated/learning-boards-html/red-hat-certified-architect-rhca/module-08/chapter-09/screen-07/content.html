Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Log Rotation & Updates
Log Rotation

Configure rotation for all major components: httpd, pulp, foreman, and system journal. Compress and remove old logs per retention policy to avoid disk exhaustion.

Checks
  • Verify rotated files are compressed and deleted after retention period.
  • Alert when /var/log or /var/lib/pulp crosses thresholds.
System Updates

Patch the RHEL OS and apply Satellite minor updates on a controlled schedule. Test in staging and combine updates with validated backups.

Key takeaway

Rotate logs to prevent failures and patch systems with validated procedures to reduce security risk.
