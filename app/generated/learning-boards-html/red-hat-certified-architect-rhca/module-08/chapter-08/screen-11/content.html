Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Operational Safety
Top mistakes
  • Insufficient storage for /var/lib/pulp causing sync failures.
  • Incorrect forward/reverse DNS for Capsule FQDN causing registration issues.
  • Time skew leading to certificate verification failures.
  • Forgetting to synchronize Content Views to the Capsule.
Safety checklist
  1. Provision disk with headroom for pulp plus temporary sync usage.
  2. Validate forward and reverse DNS before registration.
  3. Ensure NTP configured and synchronized across client, Capsule and Satellite.
  4. Enable SSL and restrict admin access; keep backups of config and content where feasible.
Warning

Skipping these checks often leads to registration and sync failures. Address DNS, time, and disk early in the deployment process.
