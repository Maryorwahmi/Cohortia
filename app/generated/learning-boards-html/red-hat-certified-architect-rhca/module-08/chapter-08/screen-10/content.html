Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › HA & Disconnected
High Availability

HA uses a load balancer in front of multiple Capsules and shared or replicated storage for /var/lib/pulp so clients continue to receive content during failures.

Considerations
  • Manage certificate consistency and load balancer health checks.
  • Ensure storage consistency for pulp content to avoid partial states after failover.
Disconnected Capsules

For air-gapped sites, export content from an internet-connected Satellite onto transportable media and import it into the disconnected Capsule. This is a manual, repeatable process that needs strict procedures.

Pitfall

Treat disconnected Capsules as requiring scheduled manual imports. They do not auto-sync and thus need lifecycle planning to ensure hosts receive updates on time.

Key Takeaway

Choose HA for critical, production sites and plan manual import workflows for disconnected Capsules. Test failover and import procedures to ensure operational readiness.
