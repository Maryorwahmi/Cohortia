Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Troubleshooting
Quick diagnostic steps
  1. Verify forward and reverse DNS for the Capsule FQDN from client and Satellite.
  2. Check NTP/chrony status on client, Capsule and Satellite to avoid certificate issues.
  3. Confirm the Capsule can reach the Satellite and that repositories are available.
  4. Check /var/lib/pulp for free space during sync operations.
Example error path

If a client shows certificate verification failed, first check system time. If time is correct, export the Capsule certificate chain and validate the client trusts the CA used by the Satellite.

After remediation

Rerun a small content sync and register a test host to confirm the issue is resolved before resuming full operations.

Key Takeaway

Follow DNS → Time → Certificate → Connectivity → Disk as your diagnostic order, and validate fixes with a test sync and registration.
