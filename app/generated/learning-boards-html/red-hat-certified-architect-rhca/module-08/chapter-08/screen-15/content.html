Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › What to Remember
Capsule ≠ Satellite
Capsules extend and cache; Satellite remains authoritative for lifecycle management.
Prerequisites first
DNS, NTP and disk space are the top causes of failures; validate them early.
Verify with tests
Run small syncs and register a test host to confirm expected behavior before wide rollout.
Concise takeaway

Design Capsules to reduce WAN impact, size storage correctly, and validate DNS/time/certificates to ensure reliable operation.
