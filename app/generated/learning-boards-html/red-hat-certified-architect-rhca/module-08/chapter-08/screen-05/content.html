Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Planning & Sizing
Step-by-step planning
  1. Location: Identify remote sites or segments with many hosts or slow WAN links.
  2. Storage: Estimate /var/lib/pulp needs based on Content Views and retention.
  3. Network: Confirm DNS, reverse DNS, firewall rules, and reliable connectivity to the Satellite.
  4. Services: Decide whether DHCP, DNS, TFTP, Puppet or Ansible proxies are required.
Concrete scenario

For a branch of 150 hosts that receives monthly updates and occasional PXE provisioning, size storage for at least the full monthly deltas plus retention of older versions used for rollbacks. Allocate CPU and RAM to handle concurrent yum/dnf traffic during scheduled patches and at least one sync job running at a time.

Common mistakes
  • Underestimating disk for pulp content, leading to sync failures.
  • Missing reverse DNS for the Capsule FQDN, causing registration errors.
  • Not opening necessary firewall ports for puppet, ansible, or pulp sync traffic.
Key Takeaway

Plan storage first. Validate DNS, time sync, and network paths before installing to avoid common provisioning and registration failures.
