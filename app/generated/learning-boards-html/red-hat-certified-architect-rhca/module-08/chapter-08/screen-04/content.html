What a Capsule does

A Capsule caches content and proxies services so hosts in a local network consume fewer WAN resources and provision faster.

Reduced WAN bandwidth
Clients pull from a local cache instead of hitting the central Satellite across the WAN.
Localized network services
DHCP, DNS and TFTP for PXE provisioning improve reliability and speed at the remote site.
Proxying configuration
Capsules can proxy Puppet or Ansible, enabling orchestration in segmented networks.
Concrete example

If a branch with 200 hosts must install a 1GB update, a Capsule sync transfers that 1GB once over the WAN. Without a Capsule, that same update traverses the WAN 200 times, raising cost and time to completion.

Common misconception

A Capsule is not a second Satellite. The central Satellite remains authoritative; Capsules synchronize selected Content Views and act as managed proxies.

Key Takeaway

Use Capsules to localize content and services. They reduce WAN load and enable reliable provisioning in remote segments while the Satellite remains the single source of truth.
