Comparison › Certificates: internal vs external
Self-signed / generated (lab)
  • Quick to deploy with --certs-external-ca false.
  • Requires manual trust on clients or browser warnings.
  • Suitable for isolated test environments.
External CA (production)
  • Trusted by enterprise PKI and scales to many clients.
  • Requires CA integration steps before or during install.
  • Better operational security and fewer manual trust steps.
Key Takeaway

Pick external CA for production; generated certs are fine for labs but add trust overhead at scale.
