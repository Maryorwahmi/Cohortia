Planning and Installation of Red Hat Satellite Server

Assess capacity, prepare the host, run satellite-installer, and complete post-install configuration to make Satellite ready for management and content distribution.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Focus: Reliable lifecycle management
Sizing
Plan CPU, RAM, and fast storage. Storage under /var/lib/pulp is commonly underestimated and needs I/O headroom for synchronizations.
Preparation
Subscribe and enable repositories, set a FQDN, ensure DNS and NTP, and pre-open firewall ports needed by Satellite and provisioning services.
Installer & Post-work
The satellite-installer automates components. After install import your subscription manifest, sync content, and validate services and logs.
