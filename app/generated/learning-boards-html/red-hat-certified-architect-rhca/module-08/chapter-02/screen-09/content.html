Process Flow › Post-installation tasks

First checklist

  1. Open https://<your_satellite_fqdn> and log in with admin credentials.
  2. Import the Red Hat subscription manifest under Content › Subscriptions.
  3. Create Products and Repositories for the desired RHEL versions.
  4. Initiate the first repository synchronization and monitor /var/lib/pulp.
Tip: the initial sync often grows to terabytes depending on selected products; monitor available disk space and I/O closely.

Example action

Navigate to Content › Subscriptions › Manage Manifest to upload the manifest you downloaded from your Red Hat account. Without this manifest, product synchronization will be blocked.
Key Takeaway

Import the manifest immediately after install so you can synchronize content and begin managing hosts.
