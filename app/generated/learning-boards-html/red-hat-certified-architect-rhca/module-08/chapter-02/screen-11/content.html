Architecture › Separate data volumes

Why separate volumes?

Separation prevents Pulp sync I/O from impacting PostgreSQL performance and allows independent scaling and backups.

/var/lib/pulp — stores RPMs, metadata, and artifacts. Requires high throughput and capacity.
/var/lib/pgsql — stores the database. Requires low latency and reliable IOPS.

Deployment pattern

Use SSD or high-IOPS SAN for pulp. Use RAID or dedicated database storage for PostgreSQL, and plan backups and retention independently. Scaling one does not force changing the other when separated.
Misconception

A single large disk is easier but creates performance contention and complicates growth planning and disaster recovery.
