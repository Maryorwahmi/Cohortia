Architecture › Satellite, Capsule, Host
Satellite
Central control plane for Content Views, lifecycle environments, and subscription management.
Capsule
Optional regional proxy that mirrors content and serves hosts locally to scale distribution.
Hosts
Register using activation keys, receive assigned Content Views and environments, and pull packages from Satellite or Capsule.
Operational check
Confirm Capsule sync status and ensure hosts in each region point to their local Capsule or Satellite server as appropriate.
