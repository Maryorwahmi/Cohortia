Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Integration with Red Hat stack
Integration

Platform readiness and inventory

Satellite ensures the operating systems that underpin OpenShift, OpenStack, or virtual environments are consistently patched, configured, and provisioned to expected baselines.

Automation

Ansible integration

Satellite can serve as an inventory source for Ansible and trigger playbooks to configure hosts or enforce post-provisioning state.

Key takeaway

Treat Satellite as the foundational OS lifecycle manager that feeds consistent state into higher-level platform automation and orchestration.
