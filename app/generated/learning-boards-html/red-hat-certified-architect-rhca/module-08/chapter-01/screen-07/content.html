Component Role

What a Capsule does

A Capsule caches Satellite content and offers local services used during provisioning and configuration. Typical services include DNS, DHCP, TFTP for PXE, and acting as a proxy for configuration tools. Capsules are essential for geographically distributed or network-segmented environments where direct connections to a central Satellite would cause latency or bandwidth issues.
Example scenario
If your organization has three data centers, place a Capsule in each so hosts in London, Tokyo, and New York pull content locally. This reduces cross-region traffic and speeds up provisioning.
Misconception

Capsules are not just caches; they provide local provisioning services and reduce operational complexity in remote sites.
