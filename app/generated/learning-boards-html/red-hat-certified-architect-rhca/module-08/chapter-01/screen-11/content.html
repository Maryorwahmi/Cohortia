Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Common mistakes & safeguards
Mistakes
  • Underprovisioning storage and disk I/O for Satellite content.
  • Neglecting DNS, firewall ports, or network latency for Capsules.
  • Granting overly broad administrative privileges in Satellite.
Safeguards
  • Monitor disk usage and performance during content syncs.
  • Verify DNS and firewall settings, and test Capsule connectivity early.
  • Enforce RBAC and automate backups with restore testing.
Operational checklist

Before full sync: test in staging, monitor I/O, validate network and access controls. After sync: verify host reporting and subscription status across managed hosts.
