Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Content sync & promotion
Process Flow

From CDN to host

  1. Satellite Server initiates synchronization with the Red Hat CDN and stores content in Pulp.
  2. Administrator tests content in a development content view and promotes approved content to higher lifecycle environments.
  3. Capsule Servers synchronize from Satellite and cache content locally for managed hosts.
  4. Managed hosts pull updates from their assigned Capsule or Satellite and apply the promoted content.
Practical notes
  • Schedule synchronizations to minimize overlap between tests and production pushes.
  • Use content views to stage and validate updates before broad promotion.
  • Capsules reduce WAN traffic and speed up host provisioning.
Key Takeaway

Design sync cadence and content views to protect production while keeping systems up to date and storage manageable.
