Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Key Concepts
Red Hat Satellite
A lifecycle platform for content, provisioning, and subscription management.
Satellite Server
Hosts Pulp, Foreman, Candlepin, and Katello — the authoritative control plane.
Capsule Server
Caches content and offers local DNS, DHCP, TFTP, and proxy services for remote sites.
Summary takeaway

Design Satellite with a central authoritative server, deploy Capsules where locality matters, and register hosts to maintain consistent, auditable lifecycle state across your RHEL fleet.
