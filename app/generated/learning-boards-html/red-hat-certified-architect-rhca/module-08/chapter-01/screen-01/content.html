Introduction to Red Hat Satellite

Understand why Satellite is the backbone of scalable RHEL lifecycle management and what architectural pieces you must design for.

In this lesson
Core purpose • Architecture • Planning
Why Satellite matters

Centralized content and lifecycle management reduces update errors, enforces compliance, and supports air-gapped or bandwidth-constrained sites.

Core components

Satellite Server, Capsule Servers, and managed hosts form the core topology you'll design and size as an architect.

Architectural focus

Plan for storage, network latency, DNS, and RBAC to avoid common deployment issues.
