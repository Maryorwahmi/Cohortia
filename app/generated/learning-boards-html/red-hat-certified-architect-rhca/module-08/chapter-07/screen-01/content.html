Advanced Content Management

Control what packages and errata are published, distribute custom content, and promote versions safely through lifecycle environments.

In this lesson
Filter content by package, errata type, and module stream. Manage errata with intent. Add custom RPMs and images. Version and promote safely.
Why it matters
Uncontrolled enhancements or wrong package versions can introduce instability. Filtering and staged promotion protect production systems while still allowing fast security response.
