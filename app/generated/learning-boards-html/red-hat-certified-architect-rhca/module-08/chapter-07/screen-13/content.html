Best Practice › Versioning & Promotion
Step 1 — Publish
Capture the curated Content View into an immutable version with a clear description.
Step 2 — Test in Development
Validate packages, errata, and custom RPMs on registered clients in Development.
Step 3 — Promote sequentially
After verification promote to QA then Production. Automate where possible for consistency.
Naming guideline
Include version, platform, and filter summary, e.g., "v1.2.3 - RHEL8 - security+bugfix + my-app" for clear audit trails.
Key Takeaway
Immutability ensures reproducibility. Publish intentionally, test thoroughly, and use sequential promotion to reduce risk.
