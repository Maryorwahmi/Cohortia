Comparison › Mistakes vs Corrective actions
Common mistakes
  • Over-privileging with ALL or wildcards.
  • Editing sudoers directly and creating syntax errors.
  • Omitting full command paths, exposing PATH risks.
  • Failing to enable or centralize logging.
Corrective actions
  • Use explicit paths and exact commands; avoid wildcards.
  • Always use visudo and consider sudoers.d for modular rules.
  • Enable Defaults log_input, log_output when I/O capture is required.
  • Centralize policy with FreeIPA where scale requires it.
Key Takeaway

A quick audit for ALL, wildcards, NOPASSWD, and missing full paths often finds and fixes the riskiest rules.
