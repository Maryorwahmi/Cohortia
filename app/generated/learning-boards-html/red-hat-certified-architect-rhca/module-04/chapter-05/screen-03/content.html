01
Create and use Aliases

Group users and commands with User_Alias and Cmnd_Alias to make sudoers readable and less error-prone.

02
Enable I/O logging

Add Defaults log_input, log_output and verify files under /var/log/sudo-io for replayable sessions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Narrow service control

Grant only two systemctl commands for a service to a junior operator and verify denied commands are blocked.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Avoid common pitfalls

Practice full command paths, avoid wildcards, and always check for earlier deny rules in sudoers order.
