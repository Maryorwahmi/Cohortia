What to Remember › Key Concepts
Least Privilege
Grant only the exact commands needed, using full paths and avoiding wildcards.
visudo Safety
Always use visudo to edit to prevent syntax errors and potential sudo lockout.
Logging & I/O
Enable and verify sudo logging; use log_input/log_output when I/O capture is required.
Summary

Precise rules, safe edits, and auditable logs form the foundation of secure sudo and RBAC.
