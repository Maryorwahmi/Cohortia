Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Compliance
Control examples
  • Log retention: keep logs for required time windows
  • Log integrity: protect logs from modification
  • Log review: schedule regular reviews and summaries
Architect's checklist
Ensure time sync, secure transport, persistent storage, access controls, and documented review processes for auditors.
Misconception
Collecting logs is not sufficient; you must demonstrate retention, protection, and review to meet compliance obligations.
Real-world fit
Map each regulatory requirement to a specific logging control and include operational tests that produce audit evidence.
