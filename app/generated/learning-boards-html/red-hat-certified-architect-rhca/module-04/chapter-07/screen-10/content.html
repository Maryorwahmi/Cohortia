Client forwarding
# Send all messages to remote syslog server
*.* @logserver.example.com:514
UDP is simple but insecure; for production use TLS on a dedicated TCP port.
Design considerations
  • What facilities to forward (authpriv, cron, etc.)
  • Retention policies on the collector
  • TLS and mutual auth to prevent spoofing
Real-world scenario
Forward authpriv to a centralized SIEM for authentication monitoring and correlation with auditd events.
