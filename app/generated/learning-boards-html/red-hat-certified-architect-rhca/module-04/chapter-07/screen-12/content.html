Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Rotation & Storage
Logrotate example
/var/log/your_app/*.log {
  weekly
  rotate 4
}
Set rotation frequency and count to meet retention requirements; coordinate with remote archival policies.
Integrity options
  • Write-once or append-only storage for critical logs
  • Signed archives or checksums to detect tampering
  • Centralized backups with restricted access
Why time sync matters
NTP-synchronized clocks ensure logs from multiple hosts can be correlated reliably for investigations.
