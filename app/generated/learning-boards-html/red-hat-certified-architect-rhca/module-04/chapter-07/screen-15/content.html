Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Key Concepts
auditd
A kernel-level auditing framework that records security-relevant system events to the audit log.
ausearch / aureport
ausearch finds raw audit records by key or fields; aureport turns logs into readable summaries for review.
rsyslog + TLS
Centralize logs and use TLS with certificate auth to ensure confidentiality and integrity in transit.
Takeaway
Combine targeted auditing, reliable analysis, and secure transport to build a compliant logging pipeline you can prove to auditors.
