Module 04 › Chapter 4.3 › RestrictAddressFamilies
What it does
RestrictAddressFamilies=AF_UNIX AF_INET limits the socket families the service may use so it cannot open IPv6 or other sockets.
AF_UNIX — local Unix domain sockets for inter-process communication
AF_INET — IPv4 sockets for network communication
Practical test
Restrict to AF_INET and AF_UNIX then try to open an IPv6 socket; it should fail or be blocked by the service context.
Key Takeaway

Use address family restrictions as a simple process-level check that complements firewall rules.
