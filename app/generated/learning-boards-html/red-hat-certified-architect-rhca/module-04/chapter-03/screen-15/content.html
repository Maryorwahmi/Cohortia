Module 04 › Chapter 4.3 › Remember
Audit first
Identify required files, sockets, and capabilities before applying restrictions.
Incremental changes
Apply one restriction at a time and verify with systemctl and journalctl.
Set safety nets
Use MemoryLimit and TasksMax to prevent resource exhaustion even if a service is compromised.
Bottom line

These practices make hardening repeatable: audit, restrict carefully, and protect system resources.
