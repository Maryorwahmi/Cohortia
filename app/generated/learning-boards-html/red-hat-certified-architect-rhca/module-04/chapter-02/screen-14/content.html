Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts

What to Remember

Zones
Policy containers that map trust to interfaces and sources.
Rich Rules & IP Sets
Granular matches and efficient address grouping for scalable policies.
NAT (DNAT / SNAT)
Forward ports for incoming services and masquerade for outbound translation.
Summary Takeaway

Design zones for trust, prefer rich rules with ipsets for clarity and scale, and use NAT and direct rules only when topology or legacy constraints require them.
