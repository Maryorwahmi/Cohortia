Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Comparison
Rich Rules
  • High-level, integrates with zones and ipsets.
  • Readable and maintainable in most operational contexts.
  • Supports logging and actions like accept/reject/drop/masquerade.
Key Takeaway

Favor rich rules for clarity; they work with ipsets and zones for scalable policies.

Direct Rules
  • Insert raw iptables/nftables for advanced or legacy needs.
  • Higher risk of ordering conflicts and maintenance burden.
  • Use only when high-level abstraction cannot express the required behavior.
Common Misconception

Direct rules are not the default path to better performance; they increase operational complexity and should be minimized.
