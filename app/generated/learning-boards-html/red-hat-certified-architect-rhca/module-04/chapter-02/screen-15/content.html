Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Cheat Sheet
Verify zones
Confirm interfaces and sources are assigned correctly before adding rules.
Use ipsets
Group changing addresses to adjust policy without editing lots of rules.
Test and recover
Reload, run connectivity tests, inspect logs, and keep a rollback path to avoid lockouts.
Final Takeaway

Combine zones, rich rules and ipsets for scalable, auditable policies; use NAT carefully and reserve direct rules for clear, documented exceptions.
