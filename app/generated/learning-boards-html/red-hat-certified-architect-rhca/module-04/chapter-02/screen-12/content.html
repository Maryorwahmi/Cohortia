Pattern

Zones + IPSets + Rich Rules

Use zones as containers of default behaviour, ipsets as address registries, and rich rules to authorize or block specific flows. This combination balances clarity and scale.

Ownership
Assign zone responsibility to network teams and rule/content responsibility to security teams.
Automation
Update ipsets via orchestration to reflect changing cloud IPs without rule churn.
Why it matters

A modular policy reduces errors, improves audit trails and makes testing and rollbacks simpler.

Quick checklist
  • Map interfaces to zones intentionally.
  • Use ipsets for dynamic groups.
  • Prefer rich rules and logging for auditability.
  • Limit direct rules and schedule conversions.
Key Takeaway

Design policies for modularity and automation: zones for scope, ipsets for scale, rich rules for clarity.
