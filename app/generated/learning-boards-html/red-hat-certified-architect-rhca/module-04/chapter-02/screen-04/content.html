Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Zones
Definition

Zones group rules by trust

Zones are named profiles applied to interfaces or sources to enforce a consistent default policy per network segment.

public
Untrusted external networks
internal
Trusted LANs and management
dmz
Internet-facing servers with limited access
trusted
Accept all connections (use sparingly)
Checks
sudo firewall-cmd --get-active-zones
sudo firewall-cmd --list-all --zone=public
Common Mistake

Assigning an external interface to an internal or trusted zone can inadvertently expose services. Always verify assignments and test connectivity from expected networks.

Example: bind eth0 to internal
sudo firewall-cmd --zone=internal --change-interface=eth0 --permanent
sudo firewall-cmd --reload
After reloading, inspect the internal zone to confirm the allowed services and ports. Use source-based mapping when interfaces carry multiple networks or VPN clients.
Key Takeaway

Zones are the first line of defense: design them to reflect trust and verify bindings before adding rules.
