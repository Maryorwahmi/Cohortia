Host-Based Access Control (HBAC)
Example HBAC sequence
ipa hbacrule-add allow_dev_ssh
ipa hbacrule-add-user allow_dev_ssh --groups=developers
ipa hbacrule-add-host allow_dev_ssh --hosts=dev-server.example.com
ipa hbacrule-add-service allow_dev_ssh --services=ssh
ipa hbacrule-enable allow_dev_ssh
This sequence binds a subject group to a target host and service, then enables the rule so SSSD enforces it during login.
Notes
HBAC denies by default unless a rule explicitly allows access. Use hostgroups to scale rules across many servers. HBAC is evaluated on the client by SSSD.
Key Takeaway

HBAC rules let you centralize who can access what; design rules to follow least privilege and prefer hostgroups for scale.
