What to Remember
Key Concepts
FreeIPA Stack
Kerberos, LDAP, DNS, NTP, and CA operate together to provide identity, auth, discovery, and trust.
Preflight Checks
FQDN, DNS, static IP, and time synchronization are prerequisites for reliable installs and enrollment.
HBAC
Host-Based Access Control centralizes who may access which services on which hosts and is enforced by SSSD.
Summary

FreeIPA centralizes identity and policy. Ensure DNS and time are correct before install, enroll clients with SSSD, and use HBAC to enforce least privilege across your infrastructure.
