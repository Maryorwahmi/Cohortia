Comparison
DNS vs NTP vs CA
DNS (Discovery)
Publishes SRV and A records so clients can find IPA services automatically. Missing SRV records break enrollment and authentication.
NTP (Time sync)
Keeps clocks aligned. Kerberos tickets are time-sensitive; clock skew leads to authentication failures.
CA (Trust)
Issues internal certificates for TLS and client authentication, simplifying secure communication inside the domain.
Key Takeaway

Treat DNS, NTP, and CA as required infrastructure for FreeIPA; misconfigurations here are the root cause of many authentication issues.
