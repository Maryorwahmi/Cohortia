Troubleshooting
Common failures and checks
DNS
Verify /etc/resolv.conf and SRV records for _ldap._tcp and _kerberos._tcp.
Time
Ensure NTP/chrony is running and clocks are closely aligned; Kerberos depends on time accuracy.
Network & CA
Check firewall and that the client trusts the server CA certificate for TLS connections.
Logs and commands
Use journalctl -u sssd and klist/kinit to inspect Kerberos tickets. Run ipa-client-install with verbose output in a lab to capture failure details for remediation.
