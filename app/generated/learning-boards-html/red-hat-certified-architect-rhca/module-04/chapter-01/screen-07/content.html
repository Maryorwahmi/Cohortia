Process Flow

Declare and apply persistent file contexts

Step 1 — Declare mapping
semanage fcontext -a -t httpd_log_t "/opt/myapp/logs(/.*)?"
This stores the mapping in the SELinux policy store.
Step 2 — Apply mapping
restorecon -Rv /opt/myapp/logs
This applies stored mappings to existing files and directories.
Verify
Confirm with ls -Z /opt/myapp/logs and ensure the type matches what your service expects.
