Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › What to Remember
Audit-driven fixes
Use logs to build policies rather than guesswork.
Least privilege
Grant only the operations required for the service.
Test and document
Keep .te files in source control and verify changes before returning to enforcing.
Final note

SELinux protects at the kernel level. Use its audit trail to make minimal, reviewed changes so your systems stay both functional and secure.
