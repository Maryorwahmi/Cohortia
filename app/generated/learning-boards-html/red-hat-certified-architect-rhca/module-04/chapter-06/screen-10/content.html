Process Flow › Backup & recovery
What to back up
  • LUKS header (exported securely) and its storage location.
  • Key files and passphrase inventories, stored under strict access control.
  • Decide: encrypted block image vs decrypted file-level backups.
Restore practice
Regularly rehearse restores: restore header, ensure key slot access, and verify filesystem integrity. Treat restores as part of change control.
Reminder

If keys or header are lost and not backed up, the data is permanently inaccessible.
