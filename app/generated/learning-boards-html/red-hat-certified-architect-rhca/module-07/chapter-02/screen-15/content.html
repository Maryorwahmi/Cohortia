Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Network Bridge
Virtual device that connects VMs to the physical LAN and allows direct IP assignment.
NAT Network
Host translates guest traffic; guests use a private range, limiting direct inbound access without forwarding.
qcow2
Copy-on-write format supporting snapshots, thin provisioning, and optional encryption.
Summary

These concepts drive decisions about how guests connect to networks, where their disks live, and what operational features are available.
