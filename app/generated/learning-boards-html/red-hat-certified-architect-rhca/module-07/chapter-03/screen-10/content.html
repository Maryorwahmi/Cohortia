Naming & image layout
A consistent layout makes scripts and audits simple.
Naming example
Use env-role-hostname, e.g., staging-app-02 or prod-db-01.
Image directory
Organize images under /var/lib/libvirt/images/<env>/<role>/ for easier cleanup and automation.
Resource planning tips
  • Sum vCPUs and RAM allocations and compare to host capacity before provisioning.
  • Use monitoring to detect overcommit and adjust allocations accordingly.
  • Prefer LVM or storage pools for snapshot-friendly images and easier backups.
Key Takeaway
Good naming and storage policies reduce human error, orphaned images, and simplify automation at scale.
