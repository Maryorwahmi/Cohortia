Core Concept
Attach disk example
Use attach-disk with live and persistent flags to both hot-plug and record changes.
sudo virsh attach-disk --domain rhel9-webserver \
  --source /var/lib/libvirt/images/data_disk.qcow2 \
  --target vdb \
  --persistent --live
Checks after attach
  • Verify device presence in the guest and create filesystems or mount points.
  • Check dmesg or system logs for errors related to hot-plug.
  • If detaching, unmount and sync to avoid data loss.
Network hot-plug
Use virsh attach-interface and verify in the guest that the new NIC is present and configured by udev or NetworkManager.
Key Takeaway
Hot-plugging increases flexibility but requires guest support and careful verification to avoid service disruption.
