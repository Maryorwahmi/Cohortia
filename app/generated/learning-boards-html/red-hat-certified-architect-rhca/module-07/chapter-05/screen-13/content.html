Best Practice › Safe Tuning Methodology
Step-by-step
  1. Collect baseline metrics with virt-top, iostat, vmstat and top under representative load.
  2. Plan a single, targeted change and document expected outcome and rollback steps.
  3. Apply the change in a controlled window and monitor the same metrics as baseline.
  4. Compare results and either accept, adjust, or roll back depending on validated impact.
Pro Tip

Keep automated scripts to re-apply and roll back configurations so responses are fast and consistent during maintenance.

Key Takeaway

A disciplined, single-change workflow reduces risk and makes performance gains verifiable.
