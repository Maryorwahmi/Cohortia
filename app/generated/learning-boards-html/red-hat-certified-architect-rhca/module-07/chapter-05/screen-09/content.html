XML disk example
<disk type='file' device='disk'>
  <driver name='qemu' type='qcow2' cache='none' io='native'/>
  <source file='/var/lib/libvirt/images/myvm.qcow2'/>
  <target dev='vda' bus='virtio'/>
</disk>
Use cache='none' with caution and ensure guest-level fsyncs and backups are configured to protect data on host failures.
Storage and Scheduler Tips
  • Prefer fast local SSDs or high-performance shared storage for IO-bound VMs.
  • On SSDs, consider mq-deadline or noop scheduler for low latency.
  • Test cache settings under real workloads for throughput and durability trade-offs.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize storage backend and use driver options to trade off predictability versus durability; always validate with realistic I/O patterns.
