What to Remember
CPU Pinning
Dedicate physical cores to vCPUs to reduce scheduling jitter and improve cache locality. Verify with lscpu and mpstat.
Huge Pages
Use larger page sizes to reduce TLB misses for memory-intensive VMs; reserve pages on the host and configure memoryBacking in XML.
Disk & I/O
Prefer virtio-scsi and consider cache='none' io='native' for predictable I/O, but balance against durability and backup strategy.
Summary

Measure consistently, apply a single focused change, and re-measure. Use the appropriate host and VM-level levers while keeping rollback and persistence in mind.
