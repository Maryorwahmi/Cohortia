Networking options
From Linux bridges to SR‑IOV — choose based on throughput, isolation, and migration needs.
Bridged networking
Simple, flexible, migratable.
VLANs
Traffic segmentation and security boundaries.
SR‑IOV
High performance, reduced flexibility for migration.
MACVTAP
Direct host NIC attachment for certain topologies.
Storage choices
Local, LVM, NFS, iSCSI — each offers trade‑offs between performance, manageability, and migration support.
Misconception
High performance options always yield the best outcome. In reality they may hinder migration or complicate HA design; plan for operational needs beyond raw throughput.
Key Takeaway

Choose networking and storage combinations that meet performance needs and operational constraints like migration and high availability.
