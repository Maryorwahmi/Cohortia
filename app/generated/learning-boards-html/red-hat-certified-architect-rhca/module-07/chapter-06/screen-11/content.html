Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › RHV vs KVM
Standalone KVM
Powerful hypervisor with libvirt, but requires manual orchestration, shared storage setup, and scripting for HA and migration at scale.
  • Direct host management via virsh.
  • Manual shared storage and failover plans.
  • High operational complexity at scale.
RHV
Adds Manager, storage domains, logical networks, and policy-driven automation to manage many hosts and VMs centrally with HA and live migration.
  • Centralized orchestration and state tracking.
  • Built-in HA, migration, templates and pools.
  • Reduced manual configuration across hosts.
Key Takeaway

RHV is the managed orchestration layer that makes KVM practical for enterprise-scale environments by providing shared storage, centralized state, and automated policies for HA and migration.
