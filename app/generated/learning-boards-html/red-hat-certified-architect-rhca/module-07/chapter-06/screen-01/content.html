Introduction to RHV Architecture

Understand how RHV centralizes KVM management and transforms hosts, networks, and storage into an orchestrated data center platform.

In this lesson
You'll map the Manager, Hosts, Storage Domains, and the logical layers — Data Centers, Clusters, and Logical Networks — to practical architecture choices for production deployments.
Why it matters
Centralized orchestration reduces manual configuration, enables HA and live migration, and scales KVM management from single hosts to hundreds of nodes.
