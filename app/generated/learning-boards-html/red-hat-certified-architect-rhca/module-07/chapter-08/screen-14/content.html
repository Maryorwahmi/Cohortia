Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Key Concepts
High Availability (HA)

Automatic restart of highly available VMs on healthy hosts to reduce downtime.

Fencing (STONITH)

Power or isolate failed hosts to prevent split-brain and protect storage integrity.

Live Migration

Move running VMs between hosts with minimal service interruption by transferring memory and CPU state.

Summary

These features form the operational backbone for resilient RHV clusters. Configure them together and validate with tests.
