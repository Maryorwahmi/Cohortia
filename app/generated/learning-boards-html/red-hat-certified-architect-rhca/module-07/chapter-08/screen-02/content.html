Learning Objectives

Practice skills and verify competency for HA, migration, snapshots, templates, and pools.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Configure HA Policies

Set Highly Available, prioritize VMs and ensure fencing. This ensures critical VMs restart first during failures.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Perform Live Migration

Initiate and validate migrations, choose migration policies, and avoid common network pitfalls.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Manage Snapshots & Checkpoints

Create, revert, and delete snapshots; understand delta disks and app-consistent snapshots using QGA.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Use Templates & Pools

Create sealed templates and pools from golden images to scale consistent deployments for VDI or transient workloads.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
