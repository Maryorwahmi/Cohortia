Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Templates & Pools
Templates

Create a master image and seal it

Generalize the VM using virt-sysprep or sysprep, then make and seal the template so new VMs can be provisioned consistently.

Pool lifecycle
  • Pools allocate VMs from a template
  • Return or revert policies keep environments clean
  • Pools are ideal for VDI and transient workloads
Scenario

To support 10 developers with staggered access, consider a pool size that covers peak concurrent users and allows a buffer for maintenance or reprovisioning delays.

Key Takeaway

Templates speed and standardize provisioning; pools provide operational controls for reuse and freshness of VMs.
