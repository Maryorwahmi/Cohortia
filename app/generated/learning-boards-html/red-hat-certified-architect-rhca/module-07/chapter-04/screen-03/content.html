Troubleshooting & Design
Practical checks and common pitfalls to avoid when you deploy these features in production.
Troubleshoot VLAN & bridge issues
Check tag propagation, bridge membership, and physical switch configuration. Mis-tagged frames are a frequent source of isolation failures.
Enable and verify SR-IOV
Confirm BIOS support, kernel VF count, and proper assignment in guest XML. VFs offer performance but constrain migration options.
Storage network best practices
Use dedicated links or VLANs for storage traffic and verify multipathing where appropriate to avoid congestion.
SELinux & permissions
Ensure libvirtd contexts and mount options allow access to NFS and iSCSI targets. SELinux denials commonly block pool activation.
