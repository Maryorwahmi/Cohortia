TripleO (OpenStack on OpenStack)
Use an Undercloud to deploy and manage the Overcloud.
Undercloud services
Ironic, Heat, Glance and Neutron enable provisioning and orchestration.
Provisioning network
Dedicated network segment for PXE and introspection to avoid conflicts.
Final takeaway
Keep the Undercloud healthy, validate introspection, and isolate provisioning traffic to ensure reliable Overcloud deployments.
