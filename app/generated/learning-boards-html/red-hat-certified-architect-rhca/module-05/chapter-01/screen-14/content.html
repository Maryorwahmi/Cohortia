Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Service Endpoints
Activity: Inspect Endpoints
Use the OpenStack client to list services and endpoints and validate deployment registrations.
# source ~/overcloudrc
# openstack service list
# openstack endpoint list
# openstack endpoint list --service compute
Expected output shows service ID, name, type and a table of endpoints with region and URL. Compare public/internal/admin URLs against your inventory.
Key Takeaway

Endpoint inspection is your fastest initial check for API accessibility and Keystone catalog correctness during validation and troubleshooting.

Troubleshooting tip
If endpoints are missing or incorrect, verify service registration jobs in TripleO or your deployment tooling and confirm Keystone has the right catalog entries before checking downstream service logs.
