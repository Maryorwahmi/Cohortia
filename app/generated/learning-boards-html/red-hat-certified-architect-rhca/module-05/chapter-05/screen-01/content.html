Networking and Storage for RHOSP
In this lesson you'll understand Neutron, Cinder, and Ceph and why they matter for resilient cloud services.
In this lesson

Build tenant networks, connect routers to provider networks, create persistent volumes, and see how Ceph provides the underlying block storage backend.

Why it matters

Correct topology and appropriate storage tiers prevent outages and performance bottlenecks. These services are the foundation for every instance's connectivity and data durability.
