Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts
Neutron primitives
Networks = L2, Subnets = IP ranges, Routers = connect networks and need external gateways.
Cinder volumes
Persistent block devices, snapshots for backups/cloning, and volume types for tiering.
Ceph (RBD)
Scalable, replicated backend for Cinder and Glance; requires proper sizing and monitoring.
Summary
Verify router gateways, choose correct volume types, and monitor Ceph health to keep your cloud reliable and performant.
