Maintenance checklist
  • • Patch RHEL hosts and verify package consistency before upgrades.
  • • Rotate logs and clean old snapshots to avoid disk pressure.
  • • Keep certificates current and verify TLS chains used by services.
Upgrade practice

Perform upgrades in staging, document rollback steps, and snapshot or back up databases and /etc before starting production upgrades. Validate post-upgrade service health with automated checks.

Pro Tip

Automate pre- and post-upgrade checks so you can detect regressions and speed rollback decisions when needed.
