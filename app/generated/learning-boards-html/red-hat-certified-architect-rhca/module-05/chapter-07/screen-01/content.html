Advanced OpenStack Operations, Monitoring, and Troubleshooting
Operational practices to keep Red Hat OpenStack Platform resilient, observable, and recoverable.
In this lesson

You will master monitoring stacks, centralized logging, and a stepwise troubleshooting workflow. We will simulate a Nova API failure and walk through commands and configuration checks. Then we review backups, Ceph considerations, and upgrade practices.

Why it matters

Availability and fast recovery reduce customer impact and mean fewer fire drills. Observability and backups let you diagnose issues and restore service with confidence.

Quick Start

Begin by ensuring Prometheus scrapes service exporters and logs are centralized. Then verify database and message queue health before troubleshooting service failures.
