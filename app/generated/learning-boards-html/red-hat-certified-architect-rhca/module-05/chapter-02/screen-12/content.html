Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › VLAN vs VXLAN
VLAN
Low overhead layer 2 segmentation with a 4096 segment limit. Best for smaller deployments or where physical VLAN control is desired.
Pros: Low overhead, simple mapping to physical switches. Cons: limited segment count, physical VLAN management.
VXLAN
Overlay encapsulation with 24-bit segment IDs allowing millions of segments. Good for large multi-tenant clouds but requires MTU and underlay capacity planning.
Pros: Massive scale, tenant isolation. Cons: encapsulation overhead, underlay requirements.
Decision guidance
Use VLAN when segment count is small and physical mapping is acceptable. Use VXLAN when you need large-scale segmentation for tenants. Always validate MTU and bandwidth in the underlay when using VXLAN.
