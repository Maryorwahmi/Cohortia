Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Ceph Design
Design pillars
OSDs • PGs • Network
OSD hardware
Choose SSD for WAL/journal or data depending on IOPS needs; HDD for capacity pools.
Placement groups
Size PGs to OSD count to ensure even distribution and performant recovery.
Network bandwidth
Provide dedicated public and cluster networks to avoid contention with tenant traffic.
Replication vs Erasure
Use replication for small-block, IOPS-heavy pools; consider erasure for large capacity with less small-write pressure.
Always test with representative IO patterns to validate that OSD choices, placement group counts, and network capacity meet the performance targets for your VM classes.
Scenario card
For a high-IOPS VM pool: 1) Use SSD OSDs or SSD journals, 2) replication factor 3, 3) allocate separate Ceph public and cluster networks at 10GbE or higher, 4) size PGs proportional to OSDs.
Pro tip
Monitor Ceph recovery rates and network utilization during heavy operations; these are early indicators of bottlenecks.
