Core Concept › Identity Security
Practical Controls
  • Least privilege: role assignments limited to project scope.
  • Strong password policies and account rotation.
  • MFA for admin and sensitive accounts where possible.
  • Enable and review Keystone audit logs regularly.
  • Enforce TLS for service and dashboard communication.
Concrete Scenario
Simulate a role-scoped token attempt to delete another project's resources. The operation should be denied and recorded in audit logs.
Key Takeaway

Combine UI controls with API-level enforcement and monitoring to maintain a secure identity environment.
