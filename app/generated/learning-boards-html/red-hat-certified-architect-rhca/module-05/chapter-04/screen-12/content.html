openstack image list
Confirm the image is listed before using it to boot instances.
Snapshotting your own image
Configure a clean instance, verify cloud-init runs correctly, remove secrets, then snapshot and upload to Glance. Add metadata tags describing purpose and build date.
Checklist before upload
  • Remove local SSH keys and user accounts you do not want shared.
  • Install cloud-init for first-boot configuration.
  • Patch and minimize installed packages to reduce attack surface.
Why it matters

A clean, well-documented image reduces operational overhead and security risk when used across tenants and projects.
