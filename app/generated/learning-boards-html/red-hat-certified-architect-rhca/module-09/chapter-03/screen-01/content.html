Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Overview
Configuring Pacemaker for Resource Management
Define, constrain, and monitor resources so services fail over safely and predictably.
In this lesson

You will learn Pacemaker's role alongside Corosync, use pcs to write to the CIB, create IPaddr2 and Filesystem primitives, and apply location, order, and colocation constraints. Hands-on steps and verification are included.

Why this matters
Automated failover reduces downtime and aligns recovery with RTO goals.
What you'll practice
Placing cluster into maintenance, creating VirtualIP and SharedFS resources, adding constraints, and verifying failover.
Explore key topics
Pacemaker basics • Defining resources • Constraints & failover
These three topics give you the practical sequence: define what to manage, teach the cluster how to manage it, and control where it runs during failures.
