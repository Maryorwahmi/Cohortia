Process Flow › Preferences & Negative Constraints
Preferences
Assign scores to prefer nodes without preventing failover to alternatives when a preferred node is down.
Use preference constraints to balance load or keep services closer to data sources.
Negative constraints
Use negative scores to prevent risky colocations, including -INFINITY to forbid placement together or on a node.
Warning

Avoid overly broad negative constraints that leave a resource with no viable node during failures.

Check

After creating constraints, run sudo pcs constraint and simulate node failure to ensure the resource still has valid placements.
