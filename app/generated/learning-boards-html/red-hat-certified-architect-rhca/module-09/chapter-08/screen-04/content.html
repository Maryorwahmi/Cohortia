Definition

VRRP election mechanics

An election protocol where the node with the highest priority becomes master and owns the VIP.

Key signals
  • VRRP advertisements sent periodically
  • Priority values determine election winner
  • Health checks can add weight or reduce effective priority
Concrete example
Two nodes, virtual_router_id 51, priorities 101 and 100. Master is 101; if it fails, 100 becomes master and configures the VIP.
Common misconception
Thinking VRRP alone solves application availability; you still need backend health and proper HAProxy config.
Annotated flow
1
Start VRRP
Nodes send advertisements at advert_int intervals.
2
Election
Highest priority becomes MASTER and assigns the VIP to its interface.
3
Health change
vrrp_script failures reduce effective priority and can trigger failover.
4
Failover
A backup becomes master and configures the VIP; clients keep using the same IP.
