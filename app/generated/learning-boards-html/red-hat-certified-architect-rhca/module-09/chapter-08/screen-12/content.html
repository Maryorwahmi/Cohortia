Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Failover Tests

Three test scenarios

Service failure
Stop HAProxy on master and observe vrrp_script failure and VIP move to backup.
Keepalived stop
Stop Keepalived on master; the backup should take over immediately since advertisements stop.
Node reboot
Reboot master and confirm VIP moves; verify preemption behavior after recovery.
Why test

Testing these scenarios reveals timing characteristics and informs tuning of advert_int and health check intervals to achieve desired failover responsiveness without flapping.
