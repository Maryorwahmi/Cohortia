Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Firewall & Networking

Commands to apply

sudo firewall-cmd --add-protocol=vrrp --permanent
sudo firewall-cmd --add-port=80/tcp --permanent
sudo firewall-cmd --add-port=443/tcp --permanent
sudo firewall-cmd --reload
Apply these on both HAProxy nodes so VRRP advertisements and VIP ports are reachable.

Checklist

  • Open protocol 112 on both nodes.
  • Open VIP application ports on both nodes.
  • Verify VIP subnet and interface alignment using ip addr show.
Mistake to avoid
Blocking VRRP or mismatching interfaces causes failed elections or VIP not appearing.
