Keepalived + VRRP for VIP failover

Float a single Virtual IP between HAProxy nodes so clients keep a stable entry point even during a load balancer failure.

Estimated lab time: 15 minutes
Why this matters
A VIP prevents your app from losing reachability when a single load balancer fails.
What you will configure
Keepalived with vrrp_instance, vrrp_script checks, and firewall rules for VRRP.
Visual concept
HAProxy
Configures backend servers and listens on the VIP.
Keepalived
Runs VRRP elections and assigns the VIP to the master node.
Clients
Always connect to the VIP, unaware which node is master.
