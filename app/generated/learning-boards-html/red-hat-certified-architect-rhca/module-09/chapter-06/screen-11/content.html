Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Keepalived & VRRP
What Keepalived does
Keepalived provides a floating Virtual IP and implements VRRP to move that IP from a failed primary to a standby. This ensures the client-facing address remains available even when the active load balancer fails.
Keepalived can also coordinate basic Layer 4 services with LVS, but its most critical role in LB stacks is VIP failover for HAProxy or similar balancers.
Failover sequence
Primary holds VIP; Keepalived monitors. If the primary fails or signals an issue, VRRP elects a standby to take the VIP, and clients reconnect to the standby without changing the IP they use.
Operational note
Test VIP movement and connection draining policies so in-flight sessions behave acceptably during failover. Treat Keepalived as the VIP manager, not the application health authority.
