Introduction to Load Balancing
Distribute requests, improve scalability, and keep services responsive under load.

Load balancing prevents any single backend from being overwhelmed. It is a fundamental design pattern for horizontally scalable systems and pairs naturally with high-availability techniques to reduce downtime.

HAProxy & Keepalived roles

Visual concept
Load balancer directs traffic to a backend pool; VIP protects the entry point
HAProxy
Handles TCP/HTTP distribution, health checks, SSL offload, and content routing. Optimized for many concurrent connections.
Operates at Layer 4 and Layer 7
Keepalived
Manages a floating VIP via VRRP so the load balancer itself stays available during failover.
Think of HAProxy as the traffic cop and Keepalived as the backup radio that hands the badge to the next officer when one goes offline.
