Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › HAProxy Overview
Capabilities
  • Layer 4 and Layer 7 load balancing for TCP and HTTP applications.
  • Health checks that remove unhealthy servers from rotation automatically.
  • SSL offloading and content-based routing for flexible traffic policies.
  • High concurrency with tunable connection limits and timeouts.
Typical use cases
Fronting web servers, application servers, or databases where efficient, configurable traffic distribution is required.
Misconception
HAProxy is not limited to HTTP. It also handles TCP workloads effectively and is chosen for its performance across protocols.
