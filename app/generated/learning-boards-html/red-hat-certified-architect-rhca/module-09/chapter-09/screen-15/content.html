Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Key Concepts
Comprehensive HA/LB Architecture
Combine Keepalived, HAProxy, and Pacemaker to cover failures across layers.
Single Point of Failure
Any component whose failure would stop the system must be made redundant.
Layered Resilience
Address network, application, and data layers with appropriate redundancy patterns.
Summary
A successful HA/LB solution combines correct tooling, rigorous testing, and operational discipline to maintain service availability and protect data.
