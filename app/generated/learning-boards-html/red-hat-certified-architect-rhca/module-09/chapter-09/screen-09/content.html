Layered SPOF checklist
Use this checklist to identify and mitigate single points of failure.
  • Network: NIC bonding, redundant switches, and multiple uplinks.
  • Load balancer: two or more HAProxy instances with Keepalived-managed VIP.
  • Application: multiple stateless app servers behind HAProxy.
  • Data: redundant storage with Pacemaker-managed mounts and fencing.
  • Monitoring: redundant collectors and alert channels to avoid blindspots.
Common pitfalls
Focusing on compute redundancy while neglecting fencing or shared storage redundancy can lead to split-brain or data loss. Another mistake is relying on manual, untested failover procedures that lengthen downtime under stress.
Key Takeaway

A SPOF audit across layers plus planned redundancy and testing reduces surprises when failures happen.
