Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Concurrent Failure
Sequence of events
  1. Primary HAProxy fails; Keepalived on secondary assumes VIP.
  2. Clients continue to connect to the VIP now served by the secondary HAProxy.
  3. Pacemaker detects the powered-off node and invokes STONITH to fence it.
  4. Pacemaker migrates shared storage mounts and MariaDB to the remaining node.
  5. System returns to full service once resources are active and health checks pass.
Impact and considerations
Expect brief interruptions during VIP rebind and resource migration. Ensure your failover windows meet RTO expectations and monitor for any delayed service starts or storage replays.
Key Takeaway

Concurrent failures are handled by coordinating Keepalived VIP failover and Pacemaker resource migration, with STONITH preventing data corruption.
