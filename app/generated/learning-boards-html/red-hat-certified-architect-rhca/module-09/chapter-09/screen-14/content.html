Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Maintenance & DR
Maintenance best practices
  • Staged updates across redundant nodes to avoid simultaneous downtime.
  • Configuration management with Ansible to ensure identical node states.
  • Periodic validation of backups and automated restore tests.
Disaster recovery planning
Extend HA across datacenters using replication and DNS failover. Document runbooks and rehearse full recovery scenarios regularly.
Key Takeaway

Recoverability requires automation, documented procedures, and repeated rehearsal to be reliable during a real disaster.
