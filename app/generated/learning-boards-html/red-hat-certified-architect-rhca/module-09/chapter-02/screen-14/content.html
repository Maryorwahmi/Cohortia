Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › What to Remember — Corosync Essentials
corosync.conf
Defines totem, interface, quorum, and logging — must be identical on all nodes.
Quorum
Majority quorum prevents split-brain; two_node: 1 is for two-node clusters only.
Verification
Use pcs status and corosync-cmapctl; check firewall, network, config, and SELinux when nodes fail to join.
Summary

Consistent network config, clear quorum rules, and a short troubleshooting checklist allow a reliable Corosync ring that Pacemaker can use to orchestrate HA resources.
