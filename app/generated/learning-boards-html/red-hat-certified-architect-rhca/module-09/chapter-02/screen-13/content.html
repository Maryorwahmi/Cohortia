Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › Operational recommendations
Practical advice
Dedicated interface for cluster traffic
Use NTP to synchronize time
Version control corosync.conf
Quorum discipline

Record whether your cluster is two-node and only enable two_node: 1 for that case to avoid undermining majority quorum in other topologies.

Service monitoring

Ensure corosync service is enabled at boot and aggregate service health in your monitoring to detect membership drift early.

Key Takeaway

Small operational controls — dedicated network, time sync, and managed configuration — deliver outsized improvements in Corosync stability.
