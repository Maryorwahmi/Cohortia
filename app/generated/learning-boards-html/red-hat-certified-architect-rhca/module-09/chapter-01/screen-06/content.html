Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Failure Types
Hardware Failures
Disk or power failures; mitigate with redundancy, hot spares, and replication.
Software Failures
Application bugs and kernel issues; mitigate with monitoring, automated restarts, and tested upgrades.
Network Failures
Router outages and link loss; mitigate with redundant paths, bonding, and heartbeat checks.
Environmental Failures
Power outages or disasters; plan site redundancy and disaster recovery exercises.
Scenario
A router and switch failure can partition a cluster. Corosync membership detection and quorum rules dictate whether resources fail over; ensuring redundant networking avoids unnecessary split-brain events.
Misconception

Not all restarts fix problems originating in data corruption or replication lag; diagnose root causes, not just symptoms.
