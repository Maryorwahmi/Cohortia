Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › What is HA?
Definition
Systems designed to operate continuously or recover quickly to minimize downtime and data loss.
Example
An e-commerce site with RTO 30 minutes and RPO 15 minutes requires short replication intervals and tested failover so orders are preserved and service returns quickly.
Key Takeaway

HA is measurable: define RTO and RPO, then design to them with detection and automated recovery.

Common mistakes
  • Assuming more hardware alone guarantees availability without orchestration.
  • Setting RTO/RPO without testing recovery procedures.
  • Mixing active-active expectations with stateful systems that require consistency.
