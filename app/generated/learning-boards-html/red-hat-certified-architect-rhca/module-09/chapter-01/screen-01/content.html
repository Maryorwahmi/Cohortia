Introduction to High Availability (HA)

Understand the goals of HA, the recovery metrics that guide design, and the Red Hat stack components that implement failover.

In this lesson
Core HA terminology, RTO/RPO, Corosync and Pacemaker roles, and configuration patterns.
Why it matters
Downtime and data loss carry clear business costs; HA helps you architect acceptable risk and response time.
Who uses it
Database architects, platform engineers, and operations teams managing mission-critical services on RHEL.
Cluster heartbeat
Corosync: reliable messaging, membership, quorum.
Resource orchestrator
Pacemaker: monitors and fails over services using resource agents.
