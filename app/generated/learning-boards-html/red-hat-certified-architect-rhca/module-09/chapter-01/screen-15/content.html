Translate SLAs
Turn business targets into RTO/RPO and design accordingly.
Corosync vs Pacemaker
Corosync detects membership and quorum; Pacemaker acts on resources using agents.
Choose pattern
Active-passive for stateful integrity; active-active for stateless scalability with extra coordination.
Final takeaway

Design HA pragmatically: meet SLAs, pick the right tools, build agents, and rehearse failovers regularly.
