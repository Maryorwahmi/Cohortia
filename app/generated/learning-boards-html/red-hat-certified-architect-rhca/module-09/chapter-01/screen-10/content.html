Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Active-Passive
How it works
One active node handles writes while one or more passives replicate state and await promotion.
Use case
Stateful databases where a single primary ensures data integrity and avoids split-write scenarios.
Risks & validation
  • Standby must be sufficiently up-to-date to meet RPOs.
  • Failover procedures must be tested to meet RTOs.
  • Idle resources increase cost; justify with business requirements.
Check this

Plan a failover test that verifies standby freshness, application start time, and end-to-end transaction integrity.
