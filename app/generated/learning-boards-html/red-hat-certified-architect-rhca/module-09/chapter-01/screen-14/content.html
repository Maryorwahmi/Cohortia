Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Key Concepts
High Availability (HA)
Systems designed to operate continuously or recover quickly to minimize downtime and data loss.
RTO & RPO
RTO limits downtime; RPO limits acceptable data loss. Both drive replication and automation choices.
Corosync
Provides group communication, membership, and quorum services — the cluster heartbeat.
Pacemaker
Manages resources and orchestrates failover using resource agents.
Active-Passive vs Active-Active
Active-passive simplifies consistency; active-active improves utilization but increases complexity.
Resource Agents
Scripts that start, stop, and monitor services; their correctness is essential for safe failover.
Summary Takeaway

Design HA by mapping SLAs to replication, orchestration, and tests. Use Corosync for detection and Pacemaker for action.
