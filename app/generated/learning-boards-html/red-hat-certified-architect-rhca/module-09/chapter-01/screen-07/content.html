Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Corosync
Role of Corosync
Reliable group messaging, membership, and quorum services that let nodes agree on who is alive and whether the cluster can safely make decisions.
Why quorum matters
Quorum prevents split-brain by requiring a majority or a configured rule before resources are managed. Without quorum, unsafe concurrent actions can occur.
Operational tip
Place Corosync traffic on redundant low-latency links and monitor for message loss to prevent false failure detections.
Scenario
A three-node cluster loses one node. Corosync updates membership and the remaining nodes keep quorum and continue managing resources.
Misconception

Corosync does not move services. It informs Pacemaker, which performs the actual failover and recovery actions.
