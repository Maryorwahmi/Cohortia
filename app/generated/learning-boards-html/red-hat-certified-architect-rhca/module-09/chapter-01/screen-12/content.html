Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Designing HA
Design process
  1. Capture RTO and RPO from stakeholders.
  2. Classify the service: stateful or stateless.
  3. Select replication and consistency strategy to meet RPO.
  4. Define automation, agents, and constraints for Pacemaker.
  5. Test failover and rehearse runbooks regularly.
Example checklist item

For RPO 5 minutes: enable near-synchronous replication and verify replication lag under peak load during rehearsals.

Tradeoffs
Lower RPO/RTO increases complexity and cost; document tradeoffs clearly for stakeholders.
Misconception

Assuming that a tested failover in isolation proves readiness; you must test under production-like load and state.
