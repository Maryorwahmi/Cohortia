RTO — Recovery Time Objective
Maximum tolerable downtime. Drives automation and failover speed requirements.
RPO — Recovery Point Objective
Maximum tolerable data loss measured in time. Drives replication and backup design.
Practical example

If RPO is 15 minutes, replication intervals and checkpoints must keep data exposure below 15 minutes.

Implications
  • Low RPO often requires synchronous or near-synchronous replication and may increase latency.
  • Low RTO favors automated failover and validated runbooks; manual recovery rarely meets tight RTOs.
  • Both targets require regular testing under realistic load to validate assumptions.
