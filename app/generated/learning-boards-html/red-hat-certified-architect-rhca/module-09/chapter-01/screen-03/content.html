Applied Outcomes

How to translate targets into architecture and operations.

Design
Map RTO/RPO to replication and backup frequency. Example: a 15-minute RPO means replication intervals and checkpoints must limit data exposure to under 15 minutes.
Operation
Plan monitoring, failover drills, and resource agent validation so failovers behave predictably in production.
Justify
Explain tradeoffs: active-passive simplifies consistency at the cost of idle resources, active-active improves utilization but complicates state management.
