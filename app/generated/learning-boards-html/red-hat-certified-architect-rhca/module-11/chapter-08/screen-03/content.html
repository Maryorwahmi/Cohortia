Lab Focus
Verify NFS exports
  • showmount -e server to confirm visibility
  • rpcinfo -p to ensure nfs and rpcbind are registered
  • exportfs -rv after changes to apply exports
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Check Kerberos health
  • klist -kt /etc/krb5.keytab to inspect keytabs
  • Ensure rpc-gssd is enabled and running
  • Confirm time sync to avoid authentication failures
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Samba checks
  • testparm to validate smb.conf syntax
  • smbstatus to view active sessions
  • use audit logs to find SELinux denials and relabel
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Security & safety

Avoid no_root_squash unless required. Restrict exports to subnets and use strong Samba passwords and group restrictions.
