Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Key Concepts
DNAT (Port Forward)
Rewrite destination addresses in prerouting so external services can reach internal hosts.
NPTv6
Translate one IPv6 prefix to another for renumbering or multi-homing without host renumbering.
Verify & Monitor
Use ip route get, ip rule show, nft list ruleset, and tcpdump to confirm the kernel decisions and the actual packet translations.
Closing takeaway

Combine clear design choices with methodical verification to safely manage advanced routing and NAT on gateways and multi-homed hosts.
