What to Remember Commands & workflow
ping / traceroute / mtr
Quick reachability and path analysis; use mtr for continuous sampling.
ss / netstat
Confirm listening sockets and active connections to validate service bindings.
iperf3 / tcpdump
Use iperf3 for throughput and tcpdump for packet-level proof; capture narrowly and timebox tests.
Practice these commands in a lab and always attach short, focused outputs when documenting incidents. That exact evidence is what turns a guess into a fix.
