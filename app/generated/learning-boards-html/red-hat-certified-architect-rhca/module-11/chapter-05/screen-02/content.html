Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Learning Objectives
01
Identify and use RHEL network tools
Locate and run perf, netstat, ss, ip, mtr, and iperf3 to collect connectivity and performance data. Knowing which tool answers which question saves time in incidents.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Analyze traffic with tcpdump
Capture and filter packets for protocol-level diagnosis and save PCAPs for deeper analysis. Filtering avoids performance impact and overly large captures.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Interpret metrics: latency, bandwidth, packet loss
Understand what high latency, low throughput, and packet loss look like in tool outputs and what they imply for the service quality.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply a systematic troubleshooting workflow
Isolate scope, check connectivity, verify services and firewalls, analyze logs and packet flow, then measure throughput with iperf3 to confirm network limits.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
