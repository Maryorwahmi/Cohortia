Common Mistakes Pitfalls & pro tips
Mistake: Blame the network
Always verify application, host, and firewall before concluding the network is at fault.
Mistake: Over-capturing
Limit tcpdump filters and durations to avoid huge files and host impact.
Pro tip: Pack the evidence
Include short mtr reports, iperf3 summary lines, and a targeted pcap when escalating to network teams.
Pro tip: Correlate
Combine mtr loss with tcpdump retransmissions and sar peaks to build a convincing narrative of the fault.
Checklist
  • Confirm listening sockets
  • Check firewall rules on both sides
  • Run iperf3 under controlled conditions
Key Takeaway

Avoid premature conclusions; collect minimal, clear evidence and correlate across layers before making remediation decisions.
