In this lesson

You will follow a terminal-first path: start with connectivity signals, confirm socket state, measure throughput, and capture packets when needed. Each step reduces the unknowns and narrows whether the cause is network, application, or host resources.

We’ll move from fast hypothesis tests to evidence collection you can attach to an incident report.
Why this matters
Knowing the right command and what output to expect shortens incident resolution time and prevents misdiagnosis. These tools help you separate network faults from slow applications or overloaded hosts.
Safety note
Packet captures can expose sensitive payloads. Only capture traffic on networks you are authorized to monitor and protect saved capture files.
Starter commands
ping -c 5 192.168.1.20
mtr 192.168.1.20
iperf3 -c 192.168.1.20 -t 30 -P 5
sudo tcpdump -i eth0 port 80 -w capture.pcap
