Core Concept Connectivity checks
Commands to run
ping -c 5 192.168.1.20
traceroute 192.168.1.20
tracepath 192.168.1.20
Note that some devices may not answer ICMP or TTL probes; combine these checks with later tests when in doubt.
Interpreting ping
  • Look for consistent RTT numbers and low packet loss.
  • High variance suggests jitter or intermittent congestion.
  • 100+ ms on a LAN indicates a local problem; similar increases beyond the gateway point to core or remote issues.
Interpreting traceroute
  • Identify the hop where RTT jumps or loss appears.
  • Missing replies can be caused by ICMP filtering, not always a broken hop.
  • Use traceroute as a directional guide, then validate with mtr for continuous sampling.
Key Takeaway

Ping and traceroute are low-cost hypothesis tests that quickly narrow where to collect deeper evidence.
