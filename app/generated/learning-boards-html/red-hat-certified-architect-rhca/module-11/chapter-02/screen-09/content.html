DNSSEC Process

Implementing DNSSEC: Zone Signing and Chain of Trust

Securing Your DNS Zone with Cryptographic Signatures

1. Include Keys & Sign Zone

Add KSK and ZSK to your zone file, then use dnssec-signzone to create a cryptographically signed zone.

2. Reference Signed Zone

Update named.conf to point to the newly generated .signed zone file, making it active for BIND.

3. Register DS Record

Submit the DS record (derived from your KSK) to your parent zone's registrar to establish the chain of trust.

Critical Mistake

Failing to register the DS record with the parent zone breaks the chain of trust, making DNSSEC validation impossible for external users.

Key Takeaway

DNSSEC requires careful key management, zone signing, and DS record registration to establish a verifiable chain of trust and secure DNS resolution.
