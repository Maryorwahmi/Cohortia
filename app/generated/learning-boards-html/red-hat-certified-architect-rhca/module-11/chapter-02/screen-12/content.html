Troubleshooting DNS Resolution Issues

Diagnosing and Resolving Common BIND Problems

  • Check BIND Logs: Examine /var/log/messages or journalctl -u named for errors.
  • Validate Configuration: Use named-checkzone for zone files and named-checkconf for named.conf syntax.
  • Verify Zone Transfers: Ensure serial numbers are incremented on master and allow-transfer is correct.
  • Firewall Rules: Confirm TCP/UDP port 53 is open between DNS servers and clients.
  • Test Resolution: Use dig and nslookup from various clients to pinpoint issues.
  • Restart Service: Always restart BIND (systemctl restart named) after configuration changes.
Common Pitfalls
  • Forgetting to increment the serial number on the master.
  • Incorrect allow-transfer or masters directives.
  • Firewall blocking TCP port 53 for zone transfers.
  • Syntax errors in zone or configuration files.
Essential Tools

dig, nslookup, named-checkzone, named-checkconf, journalctl, and tcpdump are your go-to utilities.

Key Takeaway

Systematic log analysis, configuration validation, and network connectivity checks are crucial for effective DNS troubleshooting.
