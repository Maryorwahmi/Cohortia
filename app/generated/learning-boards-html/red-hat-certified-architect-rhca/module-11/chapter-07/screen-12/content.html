Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › OpenVPN vs IPsec
OpenVPN (client-to-site)
  • Transport: UDP or TCP, can use common ports for traversal.
  • Auth: TLS/PKI with Easy-RSA; single-file client profiles simplify distribution.
  • Use case: remote users and BYOD access.
IPsec (site-to-site)
  • Transport: IP layer, requires IKE and ESP; UDP ports 500 and 4500 and ESP protocol.
  • Auth: PSK or certificates; both endpoints must match proposals.
  • Use case: persistent network-to-network connectivity and transparent routing.
Pro tip

For troubleshooting, always compare negotiated algorithms in logs to find mismatches, and test with minimal proposals before tightening policies.
