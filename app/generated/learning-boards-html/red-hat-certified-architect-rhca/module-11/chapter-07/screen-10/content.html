Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Firewall & Verification
Essential commands
sudo sysctl -w net.ipv4.ip_forward=1
sudo firewall-cmd --permanent --add-service=ipsec
sudo firewall-cmd --permanent --add-port=1194/udp
sudo firewall-cmd --permanent --add-masquerade
sudo firewall-cmd --reload
Verification checklist
  • Confirm service status: openvpn-server or strongswan is active.
  • Inspect logs: journalctl -u openvpn-server or journalctl -u strongswan for handshake messages.
  • Use strongSwan status or OpenVPN status logs to see connections.
  • Ping an internal host across the tunnel to validate routing.
