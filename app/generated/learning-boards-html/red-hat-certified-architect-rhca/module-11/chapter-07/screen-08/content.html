Key terms
  • IKE: Protocol to negotiate SAs and keys.
  • ESP: Payload encryption and integrity protection.
  • SA: Security Association describing chosen crypto parameters.
Operational note
Ensure that both gateways have matching left and right definitions and that the IKE and ESP proposals are identical. If NAT is present, enable NAT-T and open UDP ports 500 and 4500 plus allow ESP protocol where necessary.
