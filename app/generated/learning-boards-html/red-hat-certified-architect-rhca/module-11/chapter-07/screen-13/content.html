Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Troubleshooting
Step-by-step checks
  1. Confirm service is active and enabled.
  2. Inspect journal logs for TLS or IKE errors.
  3. Compare crypto proposals and secrets/keys.
  4. Verify IP forwarding and relevant firewall rules.
  5. Perform end-to-end ping tests between internal hosts.
Concrete fixes
  • Correct certificate file paths and permissions for OpenVPN.
  • Align IKE and ESP proposals in ipsec.conf on both peers.
  • Add masquerade rules if NATing client traffic to the internet.
