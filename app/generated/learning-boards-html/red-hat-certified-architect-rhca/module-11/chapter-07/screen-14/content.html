Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Key Concepts
VPN
A secure, encrypted connection that protects traffic across untrusted networks.
OpenVPN
SSL/TLS-based VPN ideal for client-to-site access and adaptable transport options.
IPsec / strongSwan
Network-layer, site-to-site tunnels using IKE and ESP; requires symmetric policies.
Takeaway
Operational readiness requires correct PKI or secrets, forwarding and firewall configuration, and verification with logs and traffic tests.
