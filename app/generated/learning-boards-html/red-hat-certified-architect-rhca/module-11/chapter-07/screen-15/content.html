Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Final Reminders
Protect CA & keys
Store CA private keys securely and avoid distributing private keys without strong transport protection.
Enable forwarding & firewall
Confirm net.ipv4.ip_forward and open ports/protocols needed for OpenVPN or IPsec.
Verify & test
Use logs, status commands, and end-to-end pings to ensure the VPN passes real traffic.
Closing takeaway
Apply a repeatable checklist for installs and verifications to reduce risk and accelerate troubleshooting.
