Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Integrity Controls
Regular updates
Schedule sudo dnf update and use testing windows. Automation helps, but validate critical services post-update.
SELinux
Keep SELinux enforcing. Use semanage and restorecon to correct contexts and add custom ports when needed.
Time & DNS security
Use authenticated NTP sources, restrict chrony allow directives, secure DNS dynamic updates with TSIG, and restrict zone transfers.
Comparison
Patching
Reduces known vulnerabilities; requires testing and scheduling.
SELinux
Contains damage from unknown or unpatched exploits by restricting behavior.
Key Takeaway

Use updates and SELinux together, and secure clock and DNS services to prevent spoofing and time-based attacks.
