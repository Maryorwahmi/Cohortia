Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Key Concepts
Network Hardening
Reduce attack surface by minimizing services and enforcing least privilege.
fail2ban
Automatically ban repeated failed logins by parsing logs and updating firewall rules.
IDS / IPS
IDS alerts on suspicious activity; IPS can block inline but requires careful tuning.
Summary: apply layering—hardening, automated blocking, and centralized monitoring—to detect, contain, and recover from network threats.
