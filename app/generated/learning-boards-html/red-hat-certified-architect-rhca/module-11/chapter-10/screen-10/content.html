Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › IDS vs IPS
IDS (passive)
Detects and alerts without blocking; useful where availability is paramount.
IPS (inline)
Detects and blocks traffic in real time; prevents threats but can impact traffic if misconfigured.
Detection approaches
Signature-based
Matches known patterns; precise for catalogued threats.
Anomaly-based
Flags deviations from baseline; detects new attacks but needs tuning to reduce false positives.
Key Takeaway

Choose IDS for low-impact monitoring and IPS where rapid automated blocking of known threats is required, balancing false positives and availability risk.
