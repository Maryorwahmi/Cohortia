Network Security Best Practices & IDS
Practical hardening, fail2ban automation, and IDS/IPS concepts for RHEL
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
This chapter focuses on tangible steps you can take on RHEL servers to reduce risk: minimize services, secure SSH, apply a default-deny firewall policy, deploy fail2ban, and integrate with IDS/IPS or SIEM for detection. It explains what each control does, why it matters, and common errors to avoid.
In this lesson
Hardening basics, fail2ban demo, SSH config, and IDS vs IPS roles.
Coming up
Hands-on commands and verification steps you can run on a RHEL webserver.
Outcomes
You will configure automated bans, secure SSH, and prepare logs for an IDS.
Topology snapshot
SSH, web, and management VLANs with centralized SIEM
Example
A public webserver with SSH on port 2222, fail2ban protecting SSH, system logs forwarded to a SIEM for NIDS correlation.
