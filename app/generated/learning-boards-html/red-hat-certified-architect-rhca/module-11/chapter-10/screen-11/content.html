Module 12 › Chapter 11.10 › Attacks & Mitigations
Common vectors
Port scanning
Mitigate with default-deny firewalls and fail2ban detection.
Brute force
Use key auth, fail2ban, and account lockout policies.
MITM
Enforce TLS, use VPNs and strong cipher suites to prevent interception.
DDoS
Upstream DDoS protection, rate limiting, and firewall rules at the edge.
Exploits
Regular patching and SELinux to reduce and contain successful exploits.
Monitoring
Use journalctl, audit logs, and netstat/ss for visibility; forward logs to SIEM.
Scenario card
If a webserver shows repeated SSH attempts, fail2ban should ban the scanner, firewall rules should block further probes, and logs forwarded to SIEM will allow correlation with other alerts.
Key Takeaway

Layered mitigations and monitoring provide both prevention and forensic evidence for incident response.
