Why time matters

Timestamps are required for log correlation, Kerberos authentication, certificate validity, and forensic timelines.

Why Chrony

Chrony synchronizes faster and handles virtualized environments better than older implementations.

Secure sync

Use symmetric keys to authenticate NTP packets and prevent time spoofing in your network.

Verification

chronyc commands report sources, offsets, and stratum so you can confirm accuracy and health.

Chrony roles and flow
Upstream sources

Public pools or dedicated reference clocks provide the reference time chronyd queries.

chronyd

The daemon measures offset, slews or steps the clock, and can serve time to clients.

chronyc

The command-line tool shows sources, offsets, stratum, and activity for verification and troubleshooting.

Clients

Servers or workstations configure chronyd to use upstream servers or an internal NTP server on the local network.

Key Takeaway

Plan time sources, secure them with keys and firewall rules, and verify with chronyc to ensure a reliable and auditable time service.
