Module 11 › Chapter 11.3 › Chrony vs ntpd
Comparison

Behavior differences

  • Faster convergence: chronyd can reach accurate time quickly using iburst and aggressive adjustments.
  • VM resilience: handles suspensions, migrations, and intermittent networking better than ntpd.
  • Large offsets: chronyd can step the clock when necessary instead of only slewing slowly.
Concrete scenario
A host resumed from a snapshot is several minutes ahead. With ntpd you may wait many minutes for slewing to correct time; chronyd can step or apply faster adjustments so authentication and logging resume correctly.
Common misconception
Assuming ntpd and chrony behave the same. Their algorithms and default behaviors differ, so prefer chrony on RHEL 8+ for cloud and VM workloads.
Key Takeaway

Standardize on chrony for dynamic environments. Its faster adjustments improve reliability and security.
