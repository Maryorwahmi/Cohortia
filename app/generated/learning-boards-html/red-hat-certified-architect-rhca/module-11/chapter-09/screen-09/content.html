Process Flow › Policy design
Step-by-step
  1. Identify critical services and their latency or throughput needs.
  2. Define guaranteed rate and a reasonable ceil for bursts.
  3. Choose HTB for multi-class hierarchies or TBF for single-stream caps.
  4. Create parent and child classes, then add u32 filters to classify traffic.
  5. Verify with tc -s and iperf3 and iterate until metrics meet requirements.
Scenario snippet
For a 100 Mbps link: reserve 5 Mbps for SSH, 10 Mbps for web, and cap others at 30 Mbps, with ceilings that allow bursts when spare capacity exists.
