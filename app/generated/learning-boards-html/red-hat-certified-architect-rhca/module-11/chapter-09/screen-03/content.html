Module 11 › Chapter 11.9 › Practical Skills
Design a policy
  • Identify critical services and their latency/bandwidth needs.
  • Set guaranteed rate and a ceil for burstability.
  • Choose HTB for hierarchies, TBF for single-stream rate limits.
Safe deployment checklist
  • Clear existing qdisc before applying new HTB rules.
  • Keep out-of-band access when changing management ports.
  • Verify with tc -s and iperf3 in a controlled test.
Troubleshooting focus
Check handles, classid values, filter matches, and counters for misclassifications that can cause unexpected behavior.
Verification tools
tc qdisc show, tc -s class show, tc filter show, and iperf3 for active tests of shaping and prioritization.
