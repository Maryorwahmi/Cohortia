Key Concepts

Further Principles for Effective Automation

Logging

The systematic recording of events and messages by an automation script, providing an audit trail and aid for troubleshooting.

Use Case Identification

The initial process of defining the specific problem, task, or workflow that an automation solution aims to address.

Operational Efficiency

The goal of automation to streamline processes, reduce manual effort, and improve consistency in network operations.

Summary Takeaway

Effective automation requires clear use case definition, comprehensive logging for visibility, and a focus on improving overall operational efficiency and stability.
