Implementing Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Streamlining Permissions for Automation Platforms and Devices

RBAC Defined

A method of restricting network access based on the roles of individual users within an organization, rather than granting permissions directly to users.

Benefits of RBAC

Simplifies permission management, ensures consistency across users, improves auditability, and scales easily with organizational changes.

Example Scenario

An 'Automation Read-Only' role is created, allowing only 'show' commands. All inventory scripts are assigned this role, ensuring they cannot make configuration changes.

RBAC on Automation Platforms

Define who can create, modify, or execute playbooks and on which inventory groups within platforms like Ansible Tower/AWX or Cisco NSO.

RBAC on Network Devices

Implemented via TACACS+/RADIUS, mapping user groups to specific privilege levels or command sets, providing fine-grained control over device access.

Key Takeaway

RBAC assigns permissions to roles, not individuals, simplifying management, ensuring consistency, and improving auditability.
