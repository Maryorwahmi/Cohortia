The Principle of Least Privilege

Minimizing Access to Limit Potential Damage

Core Definition

Granting only the minimum necessary permissions to a user, program, or process to perform its intended function, and no more.

Why It's Critical for Automation

Limits the attack surface and potential damage if an automation account or script is compromised, preventing widespread unauthorized changes.

Example Application

An inventory script only needs 'show' commands, not 'configure terminal' access. Create a user with privilege level 1, not 15.

Key Takeaway

Limit automation accounts to the minimum necessary permissions to perform their tasks, reducing the impact of a compromise.
