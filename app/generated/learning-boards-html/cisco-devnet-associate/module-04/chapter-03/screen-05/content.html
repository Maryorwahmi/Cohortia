Authentication Basics

Quick Start: Personal Access Tokens

Your Temporary Key to Webex APIs for Development

What is a Personal Access Token?

  • User-Specific: Generated directly from your Webex Developer Portal account.
  • Bearer Token: Included in the Authorization: Bearer <TOKEN> HTTP header.
  • Temporary: Typically expires after 12 hours, requiring regeneration.
  • Convenient: Ideal for quick scripts, personal use, and initial development.
Common Mistake

Using a Personal Access Token for production applications. Its limited lifespan and lack of refresh mechanism make it unsuitable for continuous, robust integrations.

How to Use a Personal Access Token

GET /v1/rooms
Host: api.webex.com
Authorization: Bearer <YOUR_WEBEX_PERSONAL_ACCESS_TOKEN>
Content-Type: application/json

The token grants access to your Webex resources. Replace <YOUR_WEBEX_PERSONAL_ACCESS_TOKEN> with your actual token.

Key Takeaway

Personal Access Tokens provide a simple way to authenticate with Webex APIs for development and testing, but their short lifespan makes them unsuitable for production environments.
