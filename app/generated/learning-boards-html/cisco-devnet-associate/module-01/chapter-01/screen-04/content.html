The Era of Manual Network Configuration

For decades, network management relied on manual configuration via Command Line Interfaces (CLIs). While offering precise control, this method became increasingly unsustainable as networks grew.

Key Challenges

  • Human Error: Meticulous typing led to frequent configuration mistakes.
  • Inconsistencies: Difficult to ensure uniform configurations across many devices.
  • Significant Delays: Deploying new services or troubleshooting took considerable time.
  • Operational Overhead: High labor costs and time consumption for repetitive tasks.

Scenario: Configuring 50 Switches

Imagine configuring 50 new access switches, each needing identical VLANs, security settings, and NTP. Manually, this could take over 12 hours, with high risk of error.

Key Takeaway

Manual CLI, while precise, became a bottleneck for scaling and agility, leading to errors and delays.
