Why Networks Needed to Evolve

The limitations of manual configuration created significant bottlenecks, hindering business agility and demanding a new approach.

Slow Deployment
Human Error
Agility Bottleneck
Manual methods created a chasm between business demands and network capabilities.

Growing Complexity

Networks expanded exponentially, making individual device management impractical and error-prone.

Demand for Agility

Businesses required faster deployment of services and dynamic adaptation to market changes.

Key Takeaway

The demand for agility and scale forced a fundamental shift in network management, moving beyond static, manual configurations.
