Drivers for Network Automation (Part 1)

Meeting the demands of modern network growth and speed.

Scalability

  • Manual methods cannot keep pace with exponential network growth.
  • Automation allows consistent configuration and management across thousands of devices.
  • Ensures infrastructure can expand without introducing chaos or inefficiency.

Agility and Speed

  • Businesses demand faster deployment of applications and services.
  • Automation slashes deployment times from days or weeks to minutes.
  • Enables rapid response to market changes and competitive demands.
Key Takeaway

Automation is essential for managing exponential network growth (scalability) and accelerating service delivery (agility and speed).
