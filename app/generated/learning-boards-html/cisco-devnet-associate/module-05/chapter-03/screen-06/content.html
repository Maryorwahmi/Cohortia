Continuous Delivery (CD) vs. Continuous Deployment (CD)

Distinguishing readiness from automatic production rollout.

Continuous Delivery

  • •Ensures all validated changes are always in a deployable state.
  • •Ready for production deployment at any time.
  • •Typically includes a manual approval step before going live.
  • •Commonly preferred in networking for risk mitigation.
Key Benefit

Provides control and a final human safety net before critical network changes are applied.

Continuous Deployment

  • •Automatically deploys every change that passes all tests.
  • •No human intervention required for production rollout.
  • •Faster release cycles, but higher risk for critical systems.
  • •Less common in networking due to high impact of errors.
Consideration

Requires extremely high confidence in automated testing to mitigate the risk of direct-to-production errors.

Key Takeaway

Continuous Delivery ensures changes are deployable with a human gate, while Continuous Deployment automates direct production rollout, a choice often influenced by risk tolerance in networking.
