Anchor the correct table
Choose left or right based on which dataset must be kept intact for your analysis.
Filter NULLs to find gaps
LEFT JOIN + WHERE joined_key IS NULL isolates items that lack matches.
NULLs are signals
Treat NULLs as evidence of missing relationships and investigate accordingly.
Final Reflection

When you choose which join to use, ask which dataset you must retain. That choice determines whether LEFT, RIGHT, or FULL JOIN is the right tool.
