Assessment idea

Employee to Department join

Write an INNER JOIN that lists employee_name and department_name by joining Employees to Departments on department_id.

SELECT
  e.employee_name,
  d.department_name
FROM
  Employees AS e
INNER JOIN
  Departments AS d ON e.department_id = d.department_id;
INNER JOIN vs CROSS JOIN
INNER JOIN filters rows by a condition in ON. CROSS JOIN combines every row from the first table with every row from the second; use it only when you intentionally want a Cartesian product.
Reflection

When you read a query, ask: is the author trying to filter to meaningful relationships or generate all combinations? The answer tells you the intended join type.
