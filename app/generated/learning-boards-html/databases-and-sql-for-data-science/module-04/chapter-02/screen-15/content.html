Referential Integrity
FK constraints keep relationships consistent, making INNER JOIN reliable.
Composite Key Joins
Match on multiple columns when the relationship spans several attributes.
Performance
Index join columns and filter early to keep joins fast on large tables.
Final takeaway

Before you run a join-heavy query, check referential integrity, confirm ON conditions including composite keys, and ensure join columns are indexed to maintain correctness and performance.
