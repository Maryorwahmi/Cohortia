Why it matters

Intermediate query logic often needs a result of one computation as input to the next. Subqueries and CTEs give you that intermediate step without creating persistent tables.

When to use

Use scalar subqueries for single-value lookups, derived tables for temporary aggregations, and CTEs for clarity and multi-step pipelines.

In this lesson

We will examine subquery placements, rewrite derived tables as CTEs, chain CTEs for complex analysis, and review common mistakes and performance notes for data science use cases.
