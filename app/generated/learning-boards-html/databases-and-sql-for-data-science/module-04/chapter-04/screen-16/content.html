Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › What to Remember
Placement by shape
SELECT for single values, FROM for temporary tables, WHERE for filters.
Beware correlated queries
They can execute per outer row; consider aggregation + join or a CTE instead.
Name steps with CTEs
Chained CTEs are easier to read, test, and debug than nested subqueries.
One-line summary

Use the structure that matches your intermediate data shape, prioritize clarity with CTEs for multi-step work, and verify performance when moving from prototype to production.
