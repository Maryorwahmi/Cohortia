Process Flow Junction Tables
Design steps
  1. Identify the two entities with many-to-many links.
  2. Create a linking table with foreign keys to both entities.
  3. Choose a primary key: surrogate or composite of the FKs.
  4. Add attributes specific to the pairing, like quantity or enrollment_date.
  5. Enforce foreign key constraints and add indexes for joins.
Examples
Enrollments(student_id FK, course_id FK, enrollment_date)
Order_Items(order_item_id PK, order_id FK, product_id FK, quantity)
Avoid: storing a list of product IDs in Orders. Use a junction table instead.
