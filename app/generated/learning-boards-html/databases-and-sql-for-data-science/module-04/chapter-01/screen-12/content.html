Comparison Normalization vs Denormalization
Normalize (1NF–3NF)
  • Protects write and update integrity.
  • Reduces duplication and anomalies.
  • Simplifies enforcement of constraints.
Denormalize (selectively)
  • Speeds read-heavy analytical queries by reducing joins.
  • Common in star schemas and reporting tables.
  • Introduce controlled redundancy and use ETL to keep data consistent.
Key Takeaway

Normalize for correctness first. Then measure and denormalize only when read performance requires it.
