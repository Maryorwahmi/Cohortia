Definition Third Normal Form (3NF)
Rule
Table is in 2NF and no non-key attribute depends on another non-key attribute.
Violation example
Customers(customer_id, customer_name, zip_code, city) where city is functionally determined by zip_code.
Normalized fix
Create Zip_Codes(zip_code PK, city) and reference zip_code from Customers.
Key Takeaway

Remove attributes that are determined by other non-key attributes to avoid transitive redundancy.
