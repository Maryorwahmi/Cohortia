Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Quick Incident Checklist
01 — Record & Scope
Timestamp the event and list affected users or queries.
02 — Connectivity
Ping, telnet the port, and check service status before diving deeper.
03 — Preserve Logs
Copy relevant rotated logs to a secure location for analysis.
04 — Inspect Activity
Use pg_stat_activity to find blocking or long-running queries.
05 — Explain & Plan
Capture EXPLAIN ANALYZE before changing queries or schema.
06 — Backup & Validate
Back up before blocking ops and run smoke tests after maintenance.
Final Thought

A concise checklist and preserved artifacts make troubleshooting faster and safer. Keep this list where your team can access it during incidents.
