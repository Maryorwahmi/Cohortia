Backup checklist
  • Take a full backup and verify its integrity.
  • Document expected downtime and notify stakeholders.
  • Have a tested restore procedure and target restore time.
Disk-full emergency

Free quick space by removing old rotated logs or temporary files before running heavy operations. Avoid running VACUUM FULL until you have a backup and an outage window.
