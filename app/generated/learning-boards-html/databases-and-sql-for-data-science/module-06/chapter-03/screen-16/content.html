What to Remember
Three concise operational rules for monitoring and diagnosing slow queries.
1. Correlate metrics
Look for patterns across CPU, I/O, memory and latency.
2. Inspect sessions
Use pg_stat_activity to find suspect queries before deep analysis.
3. Validate changes
Use baselines and before/after EXPLAIN ANALYZE comparisons to confirm improvements.
Final Thought
A small, repeatable monitoring routine prevents surprises and makes performance work predictable and effective.
