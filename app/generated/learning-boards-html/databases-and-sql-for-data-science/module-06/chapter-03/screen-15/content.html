Key Concepts
Three compact reminders for applied monitoring.
Monitor key metrics
CPU, I/O, memory, network, connections, latency.
Inspect activity
Use pg_stat_activity or process lists to find active queries.
Analyze plans
Read EXPLAIN and EXPLAIN ANALYZE to locate slow nodes and wasted reads.
Takeaway
Combine measurement, inspection, and plan analysis with baselines to make targeted, low-cost performance improvements.
