Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Disaster recovery and replication
Continuity patterns
Replication
Streams changes to a replica for quick failover. Useful for availability but can mirror accidental deletions.
Backups
Point-in-time copies retained offline to recover prior states and audit history. Protects against replication-related mistakes.
Combined approach
Use replication for fast failover and backups for historical recovery and audits.
Operational note
Define failover runbooks and test them regularly, including DNS, clients, and any dependent services.
Key Takeaway

Replication improves availability; backups provide recoverability. Use both and test failover and restore procedures.
