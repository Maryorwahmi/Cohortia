Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What to Remember
Secure Off-site
Keep backups isolated from the primary system and encrypt sensitive files when storing off-site.
Right Cadence
Select full, differential, or incremental schedules to meet RPO and storage constraints.
Test Restores
Regularly restore backups into a safe environment and verify data integrity with queries.
Reflection prompt
Identify one critical dataset you use and write down your desired RPO, RTO, and where you'd keep backups for safe, verified recovery.
