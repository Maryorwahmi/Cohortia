What is a physical backup?

A file-level copy of the database's storage files and logs. Often faster for very large databases than logical exports.

Point-in-time recovery
Restore base files, then replay transaction logs up to a specific timestamp to reach the desired state.
Considerations
  • Usually tied to specific DB system and version; less portable than logical dumps.
  • May require offline or special backup mode to ensure consistency.
  • Combine with transaction logs for point-in-time recovery.
Key Takeaway

Use physical backups for large-scale, fast restores and point-in-time recovery, while keeping logical exports for portability.
