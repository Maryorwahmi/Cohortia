Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Backup
A copy of data used to restore a previous state after loss or corruption.
Logical vs Physical
Logical exports are portable SQL; physical copies are file-level and faster for very large stores.
Recovery
Restore procedure, roles, and validation to meet defined RPO and RTO goals.
Takeaway
Backups are effective only when they are regular, isolated, secured, and validated through test restores. Match your strategy to business needs.
