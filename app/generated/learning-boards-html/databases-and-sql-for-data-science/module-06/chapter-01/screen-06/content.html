Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Differential backups
How it works

Differential backups contain all changes since the most recent full backup. Restore requires the full backup and the most recent differential file.

Scenario
Weekly full on Sunday, differential each day. To restore Thursday, use Sunday's full plus Thursday's differential.
Pros and considerations
  • Restores are simpler than incremental-only strategies.
  • Each differential grows until the next full, so monitor storage growth.
  • Good when you want a middle ground between full and incremental backups.
Key Takeaway

Use differential backups to reduce daily backup time while keeping restore steps minimal: full plus latest differential.
