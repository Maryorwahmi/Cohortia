Database Backup and Recovery Strategies
Protecting data, restoring service, and designing repeatable recovery plans
In this lesson

You'll compare full, differential, and incremental backups, see logical versus physical backups, and follow command-line examples to back up and restore a PostgreSQL database. Emphasis on tested restores and safe storage.

Why this matters to data science

Data loss halts analysis and can invalidate experimental results. Backups and verified restores protect months of work and ensure reproducible environments for model training and testing.
