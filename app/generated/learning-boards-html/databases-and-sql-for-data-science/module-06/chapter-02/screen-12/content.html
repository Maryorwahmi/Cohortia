Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Auditing and Monitoring
Why auditing matters
Audits provide a timeline of who accessed or modified data and what actions were taken, enabling incident response and compliance reporting.
Contents of logs
User identity, query executed, objects accessed, timestamps, and error messages where relevant.
Practical tip
Protect audit logs themselves and define retention policies that meet your compliance needs.
Use cases for data scientists
  • Investigate unexpected dataset changes by tracing update and delete statements.
  • Attest to who accessed sensitive patient or financial records during an audit.
  • Detect anomalous queries that may indicate credential compromise.
Key Takeaway

Auditing helps you detect and analyze incidents. Treat logs as security data and protect them accordingly.
