Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Principle of Least Privilege
Apply least privilege
Grant only the permissions required for the user’s tasks. Use roles to encapsulate and manage these permissions centrally.
Example
An analyst role has SELECT on analytics tables; a data engineer role includes INSERT and UPDATE on staging tables only.
Common mistake
Granting ALL PRIVILEGES or temporarily elevating privileges and forgetting to revoke them.
Operational tips
  • Use role templates for common functions and assign users to those roles.
  • Automate periodic permission reviews and remove unused grants.
  • Make offboarding part of your access-revocation process.
Key Takeaway

Least privilege reduces damage from mistakes and compromises. Treat access as a lifecycle task that requires periodic review.
