Core Concept
MIN and MAX
Return the smallest and largest non-NULL values in a column. Works for numbers, dates, and strings.
  • MIN(order_date) gives the earliest date.
  • MAX(price) gives the largest price; investigate unexpected extremes.
  • For strings MIN and MAX use lexical ordering, which may not equal semantic order.
Example
SELECT
  MIN(order_date) AS earliest_order,
  MAX(order_date) AS latest_order,
  MIN(total_amount) AS lowest_order_value,
  MAX(total_amount) AS highest_order_value
FROM Orders;
If you see an unexpected MAX value, check for incorrect units, misplaced decimal points, or outliers due to bad input.
Key Takeaway

MIN and MAX quickly show the range of a column and help detect outliers or data errors.
