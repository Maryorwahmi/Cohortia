Starter queries and expected outcomes
Starter SQL
-- 1. Total sales records
SELECT COUNT(*) AS total_sales FROM Sales;

-- 2. Unique products sold
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT product_id) AS unique_products FROM Sales;

-- 3. Total revenue
SELECT SUM(quantity * unit_price) AS total_revenue FROM Sales;

-- 4. Average quantity per sale
SELECT AVG(quantity) AS avg_quantity FROM Sales;

-- 5. Earliest and latest sale dates
SELECT MIN(sale_date) AS earliest_sale, MAX(sale_date) AS latest_sale FROM Sales;
Expected results (example data)
Total sales: 7 · Unique products: 4 · Total revenue: 960.00 · Average quantity: ~2.14 · Earliest: '2023-01-05' · Latest: '2023-01-09'
Tip: If results differ, check for NULLs or unexpected duplicates before changing your query logic.
