Definition
WHERE and aggregates
Any WHERE clause limits the rows that aggregate functions consider. The aggregate result reflects only the filtered rows.
Checklist:
  • Confirm your WHERE matches the intended time range, status, or subset.
  • Use COUNT(*) to measure how many rows the filter returns.
  • Run the aggregate with and without the WHERE to compare results.
Example
Filtered revenue comparison
-- Total revenue overall
SELECT SUM(total_amount) AS total_revenue, COUNT(*) AS total_orders
FROM Orders;

-- Total revenue for 2023
SELECT SUM(total_amount) AS revenue_2023, COUNT(*) AS orders_2023
FROM Orders
WHERE order_date >= '2023-01-01' AND order_date < '2024-01-01';
Comparing these outputs helps you understand the portion of revenue coming from a specific period or filter.
