Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › WHERE vs HAVING
WHERE (row-level filter)
Applies before grouping. Use WHERE to exclude rows that should not participate in aggregates.
-- Example: exclude old orders first
SELECT customer_id, SUM(total_amount) AS revenue
FROM Orders
WHERE order_date >= '2023-01-01'
GROUP BY customer_id;
Analogy
WHERE: pick ingredients. HAVING: taste the finished dish and decide which to serve.
HAVING (group-level filter)
Applies after grouping and aggregation. Use HAVING to filter by SUM, AVG, COUNT values.
-- Example: keep customers with revenue > 1000
SELECT customer_id, SUM(total_amount) AS revenue
FROM Orders
GROUP BY customer_id
HAVING SUM(total_amount) > 1000;
Misconception

You cannot use aggregate functions like SUM inside WHERE, because those values do not exist until after grouping.
