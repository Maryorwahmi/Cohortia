Assessment: customers with high activity
SELECT customer_id,
COUNT(transaction_id) AS num_transactions,
SUM(amount) AS total_amount_spent
FROM Transactions
GROUP BY customer_id
HAVING COUNT(transaction_id) > 5
AND SUM(amount) > 1000
ORDER BY total_amount_spent DESC;
Explanation: GROUP BY makes per-customer aggregates. HAVING applies both numeric conditions to the groups, and ORDER BY sorts by total spent. Check results for expected counts and sums to validate correctness.
Common checks
  • Did you accidentally put aggregates in WHERE?
  • Are non-aggregated SELECT columns included in GROUP BY?
  • Does the ORDER BY reference a returned column or aggregate?
Learner prompt

Try changing the HAVING thresholds and observe how results include or exclude customers.
