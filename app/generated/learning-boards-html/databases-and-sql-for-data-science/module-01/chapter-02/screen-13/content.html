Maintaining Data Integrity and Safety in SQLite

Learn essential tips for protecting your file-based database from accidental data loss.

File-Based Vulnerability

Since the database is a single file, accidental deletion or corruption can lead to complete data loss.

Regular Backups

Always make copies of your `.db` file before major changes or complex DML operations.

Key Takeaway

Proactive backup strategies and careful execution of DML commands are essential for safeguarding your SQLite database against accidental data loss.

Data Safety Checklist

Simple steps to protect your database.

Know Your File Location

Always be aware of where your `.db` file is stored on your system.

Backup Before Changes

Copy your `.db` file before running any `CREATE`, `ALTER`, `DELETE`, or `UPDATE` statements.

Use `WHERE` Clauses Carefully

Always specify a `WHERE` clause with `DELETE` and `UPDATE` to target specific rows.

Common Pitfall

Forgetting that SQLite is file-based can lead to a false sense of security; it lacks the advanced recovery features of server-based systems.
