SQL: DDL & DML
Use DDL to define structures and DML to manipulate records for analysis-ready data.
Prepare data at the source
Aggregate and clean in SQL to save time in notebooks and keep transformations reproducible.
Good design habits
Choose stable primary keys, use foreign keys, and avoid SELECT * in production queries.
