Process

Join Customers, Orders, Products

Use foreign keys to match rows and pull attributes from multiple tables into a unified result.

Example query
SELECT c.customer_id, c.first_name, SUM(o.amount) AS total_spent
FROM Customers c
JOIN Orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.first_name;
  • Always include the ON condition to avoid Cartesian joins.
  • Check small samples before aggregating to confirm correct linkages.
Concrete scenario
You want the most active customers last quarter. Join Customers to Orders, filter by order_date range, aggregate by customer, and then sort by total_spent.
Common mistake

Omitting the join condition or grouping by non-unique fields can produce incorrect aggregates.
