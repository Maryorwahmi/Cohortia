Module 02 › Chapter 2.1 › Schema Awareness
What to check
  • Exact table and column names and their casing.
  • Data types to know if a field is numeric, date, or text.
  • Primary keys and join keys for multi-table queries.
Practical step

Use your client’s browser to copy identifiers into queries. This reduces spelling errors and reveals case sensitivity rules used by your database system.

Example

Before joining Customers and Orders, confirm both tables share CustomerID spelled the same way and with compatible types. That prevents runtime join errors and returned empty sets.
