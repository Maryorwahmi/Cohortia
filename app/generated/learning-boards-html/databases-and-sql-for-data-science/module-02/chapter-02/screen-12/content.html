Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
WHERE clause
Filters rows based on conditions and always follows FROM in a SELECT statement.
Comparisons
Use =, !=, >, <, >=, <= to compare numbers and dates with intentional boundary choices.
Logical operators
Combine conditions with AND, OR, NOT; use parentheses to make evaluation explicit.
Takeaway

Mastering these core filters helps you prepare accurate datasets and prevents logic errors in downstream analyses.
