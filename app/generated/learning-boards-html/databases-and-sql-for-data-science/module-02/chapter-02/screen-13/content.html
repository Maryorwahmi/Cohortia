BETWEEN
A concise inclusive range check; verify boundaries to avoid surprises.
IN
Shorthand for multiple OR comparisons; remember exact matching and NULL behavior.
LIKE
Pattern matching using % and _; be aware of case sensitivity and index implications for leading %.
Final takeaway

Use these operators to express common filters, but validate results and edge cases so your analyses remain accurate.
