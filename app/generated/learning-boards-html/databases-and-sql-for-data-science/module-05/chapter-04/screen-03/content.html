Lab steps summary
A checklist you will follow in the interactive lab: confirm table, load data, analyze in Pandas, write the summary back, and verify it was written correctly.
Step 1: Connect
Open sqlite3 connection to data_science_db.db and inspect sensor_readings.
Step 2: Load
Use read_sql_table and read_sql_query to view full and filtered data.
Step 3: Aggregate
Group by device_id to compute averages and maxima in Pandas.
Step 4: Write
Write device_summary with df.to_sql('device_summary', conn, if_exists='replace', index=False).
What you will demonstrate
Prove that you can choose the right read function, aggregate reliably, and persist results without accidentally keeping index columns or wiping production data.
Checkpoint
After writing device_summary, read it back and show the same aggregated rows and column names you expect.
