Best Practice › Workflow patterns
Recommended sequence
  1. Setup and seed database for reproducibility.
  2. Filter at source with read_sql_query when possible.
  3. Aggregate and transform in Pandas as needed.
  4. Write summaries with to_sql and verify.
Notes on automation
Automate the sequence in scripts or scheduled jobs, but add checks to prevent accidental replace operations on production tables.
Why it matters
A clear workflow reduces errors, saves compute resources, and makes analysis results reproducible for reporting or downstream use.
