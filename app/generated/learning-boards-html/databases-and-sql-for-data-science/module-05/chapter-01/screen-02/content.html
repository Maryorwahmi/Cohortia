Why these objectives?

They turn environment setup into a repeatable step you can rely on in experiments, pipelines, and shared projects. Small errors in installation or connection details are the most common source of wasted time.

Mnemonic
Think: Install, Isolate, Connect, Close. This sequence helps you remember the safe order of operations.
Quick check
If your script raises ModuleNotFoundError, confirm the virtual environment is active and the package was installed there.
