Probing Connectionless UDP Ports

Challenges and Ambiguities in UDP Scanning

UDP Scan Mechanism (`-sU`)

  • **Connectionless:** Sends raw UDP packets to target ports.
  • **Closed Port:** Receives an ICMP port unreachable error.
  • **Open|Filtered Port:** No response received.
  • **Ambiguity:** Lack of response can mean open or filtered.

Challenges & Limitations

  • **Slower:** Often takes significantly longer than TCP scans due to retransmissions and timeouts.
  • **Less Conclusive:** 'Open|filtered' state requires additional techniques to confirm service presence.
  • **Firewall Impact:** Firewalls can easily drop UDP packets, leading to 'filtered' results.
Key Takeaway

UDP scanning (`-sU`) is essential for connectionless services but is slower and often less conclusive than TCP scans, requiring careful interpretation of 'open|filtered' results.
