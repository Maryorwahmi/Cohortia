Avoiding Pitfalls in Network Scanning

Learning from Common Errors and Misinterpretations

Mistake 1: Unspecified Scans

  • **Problem:** Running Nmap without specifying target or scan type leads to default, potentially noisy or unintended behavior.
  • **Solution:** Always explicitly define your target(s) and desired scan type (e.g., `nmap -sS `).

Mistake 2: Ignoring Filtered Ports

  • **Problem:** Misinterpreting 'filtered' as 'closed' or ignoring them entirely.
  • **Solution:** Filtered ports indicate firewalls; investigate further with different scan types or firewall analysis.

Mistake 3: Not Saving Output

  • **Problem:** Forgetting to save scan results for later analysis and reporting.
  • **Solution:** Always use output flags (`-oN`, `-oX`, `-oG`) to preserve your findings.
Key Takeaway

Effective network scanning requires precision in commands, thorough analysis of all port states (especially 'filtered'), and diligent saving of all scan outputs.
