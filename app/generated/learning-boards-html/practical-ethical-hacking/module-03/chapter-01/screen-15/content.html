Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from Network Scanning

SYN Scan (`-sS`)

A 'half-open' TCP scan that sends a SYN packet and looks for SYN/ACK, then sends RST, avoiding a full connection for stealth.

TCP Connect Scan (`-sT`)

A full TCP three-way handshake scan, more reliable but noisier and more easily logged by target systems.

UDP Scan (`-sU`)

Scans for open UDP ports; often slower and less conclusive due to the connectionless nature of UDP, leading to 'open|filtered' states.

Summary Takeaway

Different Nmap scan types like SYN, TCP Connect, and UDP scans offer varied approaches to port detection, while host discovery (`-sn`) is key for identifying live targets.
