The Stealthy SYN Scan

Leveraging the TCP Three-Way Handshake for Efficiency

How SYN Scan Works (`-sS`)

  • **Half-Open:** Sends a SYN packet, but doesn't complete the TCP handshake.
  • **Open Port:** Target responds with SYN/ACK, Nmap sends RST.
  • **Closed Port:** Target responds with RST.
  • **Filtered Port:** No response or ICMP unreachable error.

Advantages

  • **Speed:** Faster than full TCP connect scans.
  • **Stealth:** Less likely to be logged by target systems or trigger simpler firewalls, as no full connection is established.
  • **Efficiency:** Quickly identifies open ports with minimal network overhead.
Key Takeaway

The SYN scan (`-sS`) is a fast and relatively stealthy method for identifying open TCP ports by initiating, but not completing, the TCP three-way handshake.
