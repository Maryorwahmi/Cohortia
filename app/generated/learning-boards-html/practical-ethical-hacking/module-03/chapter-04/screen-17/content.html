Key Concepts: Prioritization and Ethical Practice

Understanding vulnerability severity and responsible disclosure.

CVSS

The Common Vulnerability Scoring System, an open industry standard for assessing the severity of computer system security vulnerabilities (0.0-10.0).

CVE

Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures, a list of standardized identifiers for publicly known cybersecurity vulnerabilities.

Responsible Disclosure

The ethical practice of privately notifying a vendor about a vulnerability before publicly disclosing it, allowing them time to develop a patch.

Summary Takeaway

Prioritizing vulnerabilities with CVSS, identifying them with CVEs, and practicing responsible disclosure are crucial for effective and ethical cybersecurity work.
