Interpreting Scan Reports & Prioritization

Using CVSS to rank vulnerabilities and focus your efforts.

Understanding CVSS Scores

1

Exploitability: Assesses how easy it is to exploit (attack vector, complexity, privileges).

2

Impact: Measures potential harm to Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA).

3

Scope: Indicates if the vulnerability affects components beyond the vulnerable system.

CVSS Score Ranges & Severity

Critical (9.0-10.0)

Immediate attention required. E.g., Remote Code Execution on internet-facing server.

High (7.0-8.9)

Significant risk. E.g., Privilege Escalation on a critical system.

Medium (4.0-6.9)

Moderate risk. E.g., Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) in a web application.

Low (0.1-3.9)

Minor risk. E.g., Information Disclosure on an internal development server.

Key Takeaway

CVSS scores provide a standardized way to prioritize vulnerabilities, allowing ethical hackers to focus on the most severe and impactful findings first.
