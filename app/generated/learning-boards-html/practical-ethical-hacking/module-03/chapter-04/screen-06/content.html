Limitations of Vulnerability Scanners

Understanding what automated tools can and cannot detect.

What Scanners Miss

  • •Zero-day exploits: Unknown vulnerabilities without existing signatures.
  • •Complex logical flaws: Unique business logic errors in custom applications.
  • •Contextual nuances: Specific environmental factors affecting exploitability.

False Positives vs. False Negatives

False Positive

A vulnerability reported by the scanner that is not actually present on the target system. Often due to misinterpretation or incomplete data.

False Negative

A vulnerability that is present on the target system but is not detected or reported by the scanner. Can be due to newness or complexity.

Key Takeaway

Vulnerability scanners are limited to known flaws and can produce inaccurate results (false positives/negatives), making manual verification essential for critical findings.
