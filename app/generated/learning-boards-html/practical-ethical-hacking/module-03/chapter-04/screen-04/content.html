Core Concept

What is Vulnerability Analysis?

The bridge between discovery and exploitation in a penetration test.

The Analysis Process

1

Information Gathering: Collect OS, service versions, configurations, and user accounts.

2

Comparison: Match gathered data against known vulnerabilities and weaknesses.

3

Identification: Pinpoint potential attack vectors and specific exploits.

Why it Matters

  • •Transforms raw data into actionable intelligence.
  • •Determines specific exploits that might work.
  • •Focuses efforts on high-impact weaknesses.
  • •Essential bridge between discovery and exploitation phases.
Key Takeaway

Vulnerability analysis is the critical step where discovered information is evaluated to identify specific, exploitable weaknesses, guiding the next phase of a penetration test.
