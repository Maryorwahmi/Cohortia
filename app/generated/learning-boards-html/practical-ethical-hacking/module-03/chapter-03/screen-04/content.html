Module 3›Chapter 3.3›Scanning vs. Enumeration
Scanning

Identify Open Ports & Services

  • •Goal: Discover network presence and basic service availability.
  • •Method: Send probes to a range of ports to see which respond.
  • •Output: List of open ports, sometimes service versions (e.g., Port 80: HTTP, Port 445: SMB).
  • •Analogy: Seeing a house has a front door and windows.
Key Takeaway

Scanning provides a surface-level view, while enumeration dives deep to extract critical details for targeted attacks.

Enumeration

Extract Detailed Service Information

  • •Goal: Gather specific data like users, shares, configs, and vulnerabilities.
  • •Method: Actively query identified open services.
  • •Output: User lists, share names, DNS records, system details (e.g., SMB users, DNS subdomains).
  • •Analogy: Knowing who lives in the house, their habits, and where valuables are kept.
Pro Tip

Always follow up scanning with targeted enumeration to maximize your intelligence gathering.
