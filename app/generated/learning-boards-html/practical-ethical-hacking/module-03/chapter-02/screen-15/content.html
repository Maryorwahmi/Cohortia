Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

NSE Categories

Collections of Nmap scripts grouped by function (e.g., `auth`, `vuln`, `discovery`).

Aggressive Scan (-A)

A comprehensive Nmap scan that is noisy and easily detected, enabling OS, version, and default script scanning.

CVE (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures)

A list of publicly disclosed cybersecurity vulnerabilities and exposures, used to link scan results to known weaknesses.

Chapter Summary

Understanding NSE script categories, the risks of aggressive scans, and how to leverage CVEs are vital for conducting targeted, informed, and responsible vulnerability assessments.
