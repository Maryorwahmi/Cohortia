Key Concepts

Foundational terms for your ethical hacking journey

Virtualization

Technology allowing multiple operating systems (VMs) on a single physical computer.

Virtual Machine (VM)

A software-based emulation of a physical computer system, running its own OS.

VirtualBox

A free and open-source virtualization software from Oracle, used as our hypervisor.

Summary Takeaway

Virtualization, VMs, and VirtualBox form the essential technological backbone for creating a safe and flexible ethical hacking lab environment.
