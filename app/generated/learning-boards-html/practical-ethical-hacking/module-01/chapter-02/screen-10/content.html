VirtualBox Network Modes: NAT vs. Host-Only

NAT (Network Address Translation)

  • VM can access the internet.
  • Host and other VMs cannot easily initiate connections to the VM.
  • Good for downloading updates.
  • Default setting, but not ideal for lab communication.

Host-Only Adapter

  • Private network between host and VMs.
  • VMs can communicate with each other and the host.
  • Cannot access the internet.
  • Excellent for isolated labs where targets are not exposed.
Analogy Card

NAT is like a one-way street out to the internet. Host-Only is like a private cul-de-sac for your VMs and host.

Key Takeaway

NAT provides internet access, while Host-Only offers isolation between VMs and the host, but no internet.
