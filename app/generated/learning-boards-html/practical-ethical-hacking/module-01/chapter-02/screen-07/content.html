Introducing Kali Linux

  • Debian-based: Built on a stable and widely used Linux foundation.
  • Penetration Testing Focus: Specifically engineered for security auditing and digital forensics.
  • Pre-installed Tools: Comes with hundreds of tools for various hacking phases, from reconnaissance to exploitation.
  • Industry Standard: The go-to distribution for ethical hackers and security professionals worldwide.
Why it Matters

Kali Linux streamlines your workflow by providing a ready-to-use environment, letting you focus on learning techniques rather than tool setup.
