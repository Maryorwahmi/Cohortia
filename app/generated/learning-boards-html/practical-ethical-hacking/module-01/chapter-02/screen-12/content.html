Avoiding Common Lab Setup Mistakes

Not Isolating Your Lab

Always use Host-Only or Internal Network for targets to prevent exposure to your main network.

Using Default Credentials

Be aware of well-known default passwords on vulnerable VMs; never use them on real systems.

Insufficient Resources

Allocate enough RAM and CPU to VMs to avoid slow, unresponsive performance.

Forgetting Snapshots

Take snapshots before destructive actions to easily revert to a clean VM state.

Untrusted Downloads

Only download ISOs and VM images from official, verified vendor websites.

Key Takeaway

Proactive awareness of common mistakes and adherence to safety protocols are crucial for a secure and effective lab environment.
