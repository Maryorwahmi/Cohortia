The Structured Phases of a Pentest

A systematic approach to identifying and reporting vulnerabilities.

1

Reconnaissance

Gathering information passively.

2

Scanning

Actively discovering targets.

3

Enumeration

Extracting detailed information.

4

Gaining Access

Exploiting vulnerabilities.

5

Maintaining Access

Ensuring persistent control.

6

Covering Tracks

Removing evidence of activity.

Key Takeaway

A structured methodology, encompassing reconnaissance to covering tracks, is essential for comprehensive and effective penetration testing.
