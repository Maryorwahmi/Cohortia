Covering Tracks: Evading Detection

Removing evidence of the penetration test while ensuring full documentation.

What is Covering Tracks?

The final technical phase where the ethical hacker removes any traces of their activities to simulate how a malicious attacker would evade detection. This demonstrates the challenges of forensic analysis.

Common Techniques

  • Clearing system and application logs.
  • Deleting temporary files and command history.
  • Removing installed tools and scripts.
  • Modifying timestamps of files.
Example Scenario

After completing their assessment, an ethical hacker uses a tool to clear specific entries from the Windows Event Log, demonstrating how an attacker could remove evidence of their login attempts or command execution.

Crucial Distinction

While simulating track covering, ethical hackers MUST meticulously document every action for the client. The goal is to demonstrate, not to truly hide.

Key Takeaway

Covering Tracks simulates attacker evasion by removing evidence, highlighting the importance of robust logging and SIEM systems for effective detection and forensic analysis.
