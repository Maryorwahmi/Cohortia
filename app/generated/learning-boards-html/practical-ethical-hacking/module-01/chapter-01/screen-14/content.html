Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Ethical Hacking

Authorized testing of systems to find and fix security vulnerabilities.

Malicious Hacking

Unauthorized and illegal access to systems for personal gain or harm.

Reconnaissance

Passive information gathering about a target without direct interaction.

Scanning

Actively interacting with a target to discover live hosts and open ports.

Enumeration

Intrusive gathering of detailed information about services, users, and shares.

Gaining Access

Exploiting vulnerabilities to obtain unauthorized access to a system.

Summary Takeaway

Ethical hacking is a structured, authorized process to proactively identify and mitigate security risks, fundamentally distinct from illegal malicious activities.
