Reconnaissance: Gathering Intelligence

Collecting information about the target without direct interaction.

What is Reconnaissance?

The initial phase of a pentest focused on passive information gathering about the target. It's like scouting the terrain before planning an approach.

Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)

Utilizing publicly available information sources like search engines, social media, company websites, and public databases to gather data.

Information Sought
  • IP addresses & Domain names
  • Employee names & Email addresses
  • Technologies used (web servers, CMS)
  • Publicly available documents & policies
Example Scenario

An ethical hacker searches LinkedIn for employees of 'TechCorp' to identify common email formats and potential organizational structures, all without direct interaction with TechCorp's systems.

Key Takeaway

Reconnaissance is the passive collection of OSINT to build a target profile, crucial for planning subsequent, more active testing phases.
