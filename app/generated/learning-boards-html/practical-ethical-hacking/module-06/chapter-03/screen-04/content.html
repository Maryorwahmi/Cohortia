The Pillars of Web Security

Differentiating Authentication and Authorization

Authentication: Who Are You?

The process of verifying a user's identity. It confirms that a user is who they claim to be, typically through credentials like usernames and passwords.

  • •**Identity Verification:** Confirming user claims.
  • •**Credential Check:** Passwords, biometrics, tokens.
  • •**Example:** Logging into an email account.
Analogy

Showing your ID at a building entrance to prove you are a registered visitor.

Authorization: What Can You Do?

The process of determining what an authenticated user is permitted to do. It defines access rights and privileges to resources and functions.

  • •**Permission Granting:** Defining user capabilities.
  • •**Access Control:** Limiting resource interaction.
  • •**Example:** An admin accessing a user management panel.
Analogy

Your access card only allowing you into specific departments within the building.

Key Takeaway

Authentication confirms identity, while authorization grants permissions. Both are essential and distinct layers of security.
