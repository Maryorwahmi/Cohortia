Chapter Review

Key Concepts: Identity & Access

Essential terms from Authentication and Authorization

Authentication

The process of verifying a user's identity, confirming who they claim to be.

Authorization

The process of determining what an authenticated user is permitted to do.

Brute-Force Attack

Systematically trying all possible combinations to guess a password or key.

Summary Takeaway

Authentication and authorization are distinct but interdependent security layers, both vulnerable to attacks like brute-forcing if not properly implemented.
