Introducing the OWASP Top 10

Your Roadmap to Critical Web Security Risks

What is OWASP?

The Open Web Application Security Project is a non-profit foundation focused on improving software security. They provide unbiased, practical information about application security.

Purpose of the Top 10

It's a standard awareness document for developers and security professionals, highlighting the most critical security risks to web applications. It's a consensus-driven list, not exhaustive.

Key Takeaway

The OWASP Top 10 provides a crucial roadmap for ethical hackers to prioritize and focus their security efforts on the most impactful risks.

Why it Matters to You

As an ethical hacker, understanding these top risks allows you to efficiently identify and test for the most common and severe vulnerabilities, making your penetration testing more effective.

Example Category: Injection

'Injection' is a common category, encompassing vulnerabilities like SQL Injection, NoSQL Injection, OS Command Injection, and LDAP Injection. These occur when untrusted data is sent to an interpreter as part of a command or query.

Common Misconception

The OWASP Top 10 is not an exhaustive list of all vulnerabilities. It's a prioritized list of the most critical risks, meaning other vulnerabilities exist but may be less prevalent or impactful.
