The Ethical Hacker's Code

Ethical hacking operates under strict legal and moral guidelines. Adhering to these principles is paramount for responsible security professionals.

  • Legality: Always obtain explicit, written permission before conducting any security assessment.
  • Scope: Clearly define the scope of your engagement and stick to it. Do not test systems outside the agreed-upon boundaries.
  • Vulnerability Disclosure: Report all discovered vulnerabilities responsibly to the asset owner.
  • Data Handling: Protect sensitive information discovered during testing and do not exploit it.
  • Non-Malicious Intent: The primary goal is to improve security, not to cause harm or gain unauthorized access for personal benefit.
Legal Consequences

Unauthorized access or damage to computer systems can lead to severe legal penalties, including fines and imprisonment.
