Core Concept

Introduction to Active Directory Exploitation

Transitioning from enumeration to active compromise within an AD environment.

Leveraging Information: Exploitation begins by using the detailed insights gathered during reconnaissance and enumeration phases.

Goal-Oriented Attacks: The primary objectives are initial access, privilege escalation, and ultimately, domain dominance.

Common Vulnerabilities: AD's complexity and common misconfigurations often create exploitable weaknesses.

Key Takeaway

Active Directory exploitation is the strategic application of gathered intelligence to achieve unauthorized access and control within a domain.

Scenario Card

The Unlocked Backdoor

Imagine you've spent days mapping a large corporate building (reconnaissance). You've identified several entry points, but one particular service entrance seems less guarded (enumeration). Exploitation is the act of using this knowledge to pick the lock on that service entrance and gain access to the building, then finding ways to move deeper inside.

Common Misconception

Many believe AD is inherently secure due to its complexity. However, this complexity often leads to misconfigurations, making it a rich target for attackers who understand its inner workings.
