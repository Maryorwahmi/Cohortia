Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Kerberoasting

An attack that extracts service account NTLM hashes from Kerberos service tickets for offline cracking.

Service Principal Name (SPN)

A unique identifier for a service instance in Active Directory, used for Kerberos authentication.

AS-REP Roasting

An attack targeting user accounts with 'Do not require Kerberos preauthentication' enabled to extract NTLM hashes.
