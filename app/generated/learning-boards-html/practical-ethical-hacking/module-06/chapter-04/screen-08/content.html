Identifying Domain Controllers

Pinpointing the Heart of the AD Network

Key Indicator

A server with multiple core AD services (DNS, Kerberos, LDAP, SMB) running simultaneously is highly likely a Domain Controller.

Why it Matters

DCs are central for authentication and hold the entire AD database, making them the most valuable targets for enumeration.

Common Mistake

Assuming a server is not a DC if only one or two AD-related ports are open; look for the cluster of services.
