Analyzing enum4linux Output

Uncovering Valuable User and Policy Details

User Lists

Identifies usernames and RIDs, crucial for targeted password attacks like spraying or brute-forcing.

Group Memberships

Reveals privileged groups (e.g., Domain Admins), helping identify high-value targets for privilege escalation.

Password Policy

Details like minimum length and complexity inform password guessing strategies and identify weak policies.
