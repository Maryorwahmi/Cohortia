Reconnaissance and Enumeration: The First Steps

Gathering Information Without Raising Alarms

Reconnaissance

The initial phase of penetration testing, focused on gathering information about the target network and its components.

Enumeration

The process of extracting specific details about a system, such as users, groups, shares, and services, often through active queries.

The Goal

To build a comprehensive map of the target's Active Directory environment before attempting any direct exploitation.

The Recon & Enum Process

1. Passive Reconnaissance: Gather public info (DNS records, domain names).

2. Semi-Passive Scanning: Use tools like Nmap to identify open ports and services.

3. Active Enumeration: Query services like SMB/LDAP for users, groups, and shares.

Key Takeaway

Thorough reconnaissance and enumeration are foundational for effective and stealthy AD penetration testing.
