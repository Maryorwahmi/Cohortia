Human Element

The Role of Social Engineering

Exploiting human psychology to facilitate client-side attacks.

Psychological Manipulation: Tricking individuals into actions or divulging information.

Critical for Delivery: Even advanced payloads fail without successful social engineering to deliver and execute them.

Example: A fake 'invoice' email with a malicious attachment, leveraging urgency and trust.

Key Takeaway

Social engineering is often the weakest link in the security chain; human vigilance is paramount.

Social Engineering Tactics
Urgency & Fear

Creating a sense of immediate action or threat.

Authority & Trust

Impersonating trusted entities (e.g., IT, CEO, bank).

Curiosity & Greed

Enticing users with promises or intriguing content.
