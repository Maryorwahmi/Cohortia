Common Mistakes

Neglecting Payload Encoding and Obfuscation

Why basic payloads often fail against modern antivirus solutions.

AV Detection: Many antivirus solutions detect standard `msfvenom` payloads via signature-based detection.

Basic Encoding Limits: Options like `-e x86/shikata_ga_nai` are often insufficient against modern AVs.

Advanced Techniques: Custom crypters or polymorphic engines are often needed for true stealth.

Key Takeaway

Payload encoding is a continuous cat-and-mouse game; basic methods are often not enough for evasion.

Encoding Effectiveness
Raw Payload

Highly detectable by most AVs.

Basic Encoding (`shikata_ga_nai`)

May bypass older AVs, easily detected by modern ones.

Advanced Obfuscation (Crypters)

Higher chance of evasion, more complex to implement.
