Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember.

Privilege Escalation

The process of gaining higher-level access rights on a system than initially obtained (e.g., from regular user to administrator/root).

Kernel Exploit

A type of exploit that targets vulnerabilities in the operating system's kernel to gain elevated privileges.

Misconfiguration

A security weakness arising from incorrect or insecure settings in software, services, or operating systems.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these foundational concepts is crucial for identifying and exploiting privilege escalation opportunities effectively.
