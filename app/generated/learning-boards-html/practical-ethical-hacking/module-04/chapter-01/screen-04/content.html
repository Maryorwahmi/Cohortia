Core Concept

What is Exploitation?

Exploitation is the process of taking advantage of a system's vulnerability to gain unauthorized access or control. It's the active phase where a theoretical weakness becomes a demonstrated security breach.

  • •Validation: Proves a vulnerability is real and impactful.
  • •Access: Aims to achieve unauthorized entry or elevate privileges.
  • •Control: Often leads to command execution or data manipulation.
Key Takeaway

Exploitation transforms a theoretical weakness into a tangible security risk, providing concrete evidence of a system's susceptibility to attack.

The Exploitation Process

1. Identify Vulnerability
2. Develop/Select Exploit
3. Gain Access/Control

Analogy: The Unlocked Door

Imagine a vulnerability as an unlocked door. Exploitation is the act of opening that door and stepping inside. It's the action that turns a potential entry point into actual access.
