Key Concepts

Here are the essential terms and ideas from this chapter:

Exploitation

The act of leveraging a vulnerability to gain unauthorized access or control over a system.

Vulnerability

A weakness or flaw in a system's design, implementation, or operation that could be exploited.

Exploit

A piece of software, data, or sequence of commands that takes advantage of a specific vulnerability.

Summary Takeaway

Exploitation is the active process of using an exploit to leverage a vulnerability, moving from a theoretical weakness to demonstrated unauthorized access or control.
