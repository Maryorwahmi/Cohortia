Key Concepts

Here are the essential terms and ideas from this chapter:

Payload

The malicious code or action that is executed on the target system after a successful exploit.

Metasploit Framework

A powerful open-source penetration testing platform providing exploits, payloads, and auxiliary tools.

`msfconsole` & Parameters

The command-line interface for Metasploit, where `RHOSTS` specifies the target IP and `LHOST` your listening IP.

Summary Takeaway

The Metasploit Framework, accessed via `msfconsole`, uses payloads to achieve objectives after an exploit, with `RHOSTS` and `LHOST` being critical for target and listener configuration.
