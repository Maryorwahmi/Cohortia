Definition

Passive Reconnaissance & OSINT

Gathering intelligence without direct interaction

Key Characteristics

  • •No Direct Interaction: Avoids sending packets to the target.
  • •Undetectable: Minimizes the risk of alerting the target.
  • •Leverages OSINT: Relies on publicly available information.
  • •Legal & Ethical: Operates within public data access boundaries.
Key Takeaway

Passive reconnaissance is about silent, undetectable information gathering from public sources, forming the basis of OSINT.

OSINT Sources

Public Databases

WHOIS, DNS records

Search Engines

Google, Shodan

Social Media

LinkedIn, Twitter

Corporate Websites

About Us, Job Postings

Misconception

Passive reconnaissance is not about illegal data collection; it's about leveraging publicly available information.
