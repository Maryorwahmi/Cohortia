Core Concept

The Art of Reconnaissance

Gathering intelligence before the engagement

Why Reconnaissance Matters

  • •Understand Target: Gain insights into infrastructure, technologies, and personnel.
  • •Identify Vulnerabilities: Pinpoint potential weaknesses before active testing.
  • •Plan Strategy: Develop effective and targeted attack plans.
  • •Minimize Detection: Reduce the risk of alerting the target during later phases.
Key Takeaway

Reconnaissance is the critical first step, providing the intelligence needed to conduct an ethical hack effectively and safely.

Reconnaissance Cycle

Define Target
Gather Info
Analyze Data
Plan Attack
Common Mistake

Skipping or rushing reconnaissance leads to inefficient and potentially detectable attacks.
