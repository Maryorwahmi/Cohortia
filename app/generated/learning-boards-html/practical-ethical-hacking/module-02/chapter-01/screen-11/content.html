Social Engineering Prep

Social Media & Corporate Websites

Goldmines for OSINT and social engineering insights

Information to Extract

  • •Employee Names & Roles: Identify key personnel and organizational structure.
  • •Technologies Used: Often mentioned in job postings or employee profiles.
  • •Company Events & Travel: Inadvertent leaks from employee posts.
  • •Internal Project Names: Sometimes found in press releases or 'About Us' sections.
Key Takeaway

Social media and corporate sites provide rich human-centric intelligence for targeted social engineering attacks.

OSINT from Human Sources

LinkedIn

Employee roles, tech stack

Job Postings

Required skills, tech used

About Us Pages

Company history, partners

News & Press

Announcements, project names

Warning

Always cross-reference information from social media to avoid misinformation and ensure accuracy.
