Risks and Mitigation of Active Reconnaissance

Balancing Thoroughness with Stealth and Authorization

Key Risks

  • •Detection by IDS/IPS or firewalls.
  • •IP address blacklisting.
  • •Alerting the target's security team.
  • •Legal consequences if unauthorized.
Critical Warning

Unauthorized active reconnaissance is illegal and can lead to severe penalties.

Mitigation Strategies

  • •**Authorization:** Always have explicit, written permission.
  • •**Stealth:** Use slower scan rates (`--scan-delay`, `-T1`).
  • •**Targeted Scans:** Scan specific, limited ports instead of all.
  • •**Packet Fragmentation:** Use `-f` to evade simple packet filters.
Best Practice

Balance thoroughness with stealth to achieve objectives without causing disruption or detection.
