Core Concept

Active vs. Passive Reconnaissance

Understanding the Shift from Observation to Interaction

Passive Reconnaissance

  • •No direct interaction with the target system.
  • •Gathers information from publicly available sources (OSINT).
  • •Low risk of detection.
  • •Examples: Google searches, WHOIS lookups, social media analysis.
Analogy

Observing a house from across the street without approaching it.

Active Reconnaissance

  • •Direct interaction with the target's systems.
  • •Sends probes and packets to elicit responses.
  • •Higher risk of detection by IDS/IPS.
  • •Examples: Port scanning, service version detection, OS fingerprinting.
Critical Warning

Requires explicit, written authorization to avoid illegal activity.
