Maltego: Data Visualization and Relationship Mapping

Connecting Disparate Information into an Intuitive Graph

Core Function

A graphical link analysis tool used for gathering and connecting information, visualizing relationships between various pieces of intelligence.

Key Benefit

Transforms raw, isolated data points into a coherent, visual network, making complex relationships easy to understand and analyze.

Key Takeaway

Maltego's strength lies in its ability to reveal hidden patterns and connections, turning raw data into meaningful intelligence for investigative tasks.
