Combining and Correlating Findings

Building a Comprehensive Target Profile from Diverse Data

1. Gather Diverse Data

Use tools like theHarvester, Nmap, and FOCA to collect various data points.

2. Identify Connections

Look for relationships between email patterns, open ports, employee names, and internal paths.

3. Construct Target Profile

Synthesize all intelligence into a comprehensive view of the target's attack surface.

Supporting Explanation

Data correlation is the process of linking disparate pieces of information to reveal a more complete and accurate picture of the target. This holistic view is essential for identifying complex attack vectors.

Concrete Example

If theHarvester finds an email pattern and FOCA reveals an employee's name, you can craft a highly targeted phishing email. If Nmap shows an open port on a server, and Maltego links that server to a specific department, you know where to focus your efforts.

Key Takeaway

Effective reconnaissance is about synthesizing information from multiple sources, not just collecting it. This correlation builds a robust target profile for subsequent penetration testing phases.

Pro Tip

Maintain meticulous documentation of all findings. A well-organized log serves as a critical roadmap for the entire penetration test, ensuring no valuable intelligence is overlooked.
