Maltego: Revealing Hidden Connections

From Raw Data to Organizational Structure and Weak Links

1. Domain to Emails

Input a domain, Maltego finds associated email addresses.

2. Emails to Social Media

Links emails to social profiles, identifying individuals.

3. Individuals to Other Assets

Discovers other domains or IPs linked to identified individuals.

Supporting Explanation

By visualizing these connections, Maltego helps ethical hackers understand the target's ecosystem, identify key personnel, and uncover potential weak links or overlooked attack surfaces that might not be visible from isolated data points.

Concrete Example

Mapping a company's domain to an employee's email, then to their public social media profile, could reveal their personal interests, which can be used to craft highly effective spear-phishing emails.

Key Takeaway

Maltego's ability to visualize complex relationships is crucial for transforming raw data into actionable intelligence, enabling more targeted and effective penetration testing strategies.

Pro Tip

Combine Maltego's findings with information from tools like theHarvester to enrich your graph and uncover even deeper, more nuanced connections.
