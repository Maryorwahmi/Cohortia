Key Concepts

Strategic Principles for Intelligence Gathering

Data Correlation

The process of connecting disparate pieces of information gathered from various reconnaissance techniques to build a complete picture.

Metadata

Data that describes other data, often embedded within files (e.g., author, creation date, software used).

Target Profile

A comprehensive summary of all collected intelligence about a target, including network infrastructure, personnel, technologies, and potential vulnerabilities.

Chapter Takeaway

These strategic principles guide the transformation of raw data into actionable intelligence, forming the foundation for effective and ethical penetration testing.
