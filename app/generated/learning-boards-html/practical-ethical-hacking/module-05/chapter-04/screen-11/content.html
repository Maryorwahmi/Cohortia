Ethical Hacking

Responsible Persistence & Cleanup

Documenting, removing, and detecting persistence mechanisms.

Ethical Considerations

  • Always operate within the defined scope of the penetration test.
  • Prioritize 'do no harm' and minimize impact on target systems.
  • Document every change made for easy and complete cleanup.
  • Never leave backdoors on client systems post-engagement.

Detection Methods

  • Monitor unusual startup entries (Registry, Cron, Systemd).
  • Check for new or modified scheduled tasks.
  • Perform file integrity monitoring on system binaries.
  • Analyze network connections for unexpected outbound traffic.
Key Takeaway

Responsible persistence involves meticulous documentation, complete cleanup, and understanding detection techniques to ensure ethical and professional engagements.

Common Mistake: No Documentation

Failing to document changes makes cleanup nearly impossible, leading to potential client harm and ethical breaches.

Pro-Tip: Stealth & Obfuscation

Use non-obvious filenames, hidden directories, and legitimate-sounding task names to increase stealth and reduce detection risk.
