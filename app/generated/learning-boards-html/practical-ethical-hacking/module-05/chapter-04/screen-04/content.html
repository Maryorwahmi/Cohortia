Core Concept

What is Persistence?

Ensuring continuous access after initial compromise.

Definition

Persistence refers to the techniques used to maintain access to a target system across reboots, loss of initial shells, or patching of original vulnerabilities. It's about establishing a reliable backdoor.

Why It Matters

  • Avoids re-exploiting the system from scratch, saving time and reducing noise.
  • Allows for long-term assessment and data exfiltration.
  • Simulates real-world attacker behavior to demonstrate impact.
Key Takeaway

Persistence is fundamental for effective post-exploitation, ensuring your access is resilient and sustainable.

Initial Compromise

Gaining a shell on the target system.

System Reboot / Session Loss

Potential loss of access without persistence.

Persistent Access

Re-establishing connection automatically.
