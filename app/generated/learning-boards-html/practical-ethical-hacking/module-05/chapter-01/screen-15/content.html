Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

Post-Exploitation

The phase after initial access, focusing on maintaining control, escalating privileges, and achieving objectives.

Initial Access Verification

Confirming the compromised user context and privilege level immediately after gaining a shell.

System Information Gathering

Collecting details about the operating system, kernel, hostname, and installed software.

Summary Takeaway

The initial post-exploitation phase is critical for understanding your environment and laying the groundwork for successful, stealthy operations.
