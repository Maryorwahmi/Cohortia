User & Process Enumeration

Discovering Windows Users and Running Processes

Identifying local user accounts, group memberships, and active processes is crucial for understanding privilege levels and potential attack vectors on Windows.

  • •
    net user: Lists all local user accounts on the system.
  • •
    net localgroup administrators: Shows members of the local 'Administrators' group.
  • •
    tasklist: Displays all running processes, including their image name, PID, and memory usage.
  • •
    Privilege Escalation: Look for processes running with elevated privileges or vulnerable applications.
Key Takeaway

Enumerating users and processes on Windows helps identify high-privilege accounts and vulnerable services, paving the way for further exploitation.
