Privilege Escalation Techniques (Linux)
Elevating Access to Gain Full Control

After gaining initial access to a Linux system, you often find yourself with the privileges of a low-level user. This chapter will guide you through the critical process of elevating your access to a more powerful level, ideally root, to achieve full control and accomplish your objectives.

Key Idea

Gaining root access is crucial for full system control, enabling advanced actions like installing backdoors or accessing sensitive data.
