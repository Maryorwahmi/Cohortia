Expanding Your Reach: Lateral Movement & Pivoting

Navigate Compromised Networks to Reach High-Value Targets

After gaining initial access, ethical hackers often need to move deeper into a network. This chapter teaches you how to traverse complex network environments, expanding your access from an initial foothold to high-value targets using advanced post-exploitation techniques.

Key Idea

Lateral movement and pivoting are advanced post-exploitation techniques used to expand access within a compromised network, moving from an initial foothold to high-value targets.
