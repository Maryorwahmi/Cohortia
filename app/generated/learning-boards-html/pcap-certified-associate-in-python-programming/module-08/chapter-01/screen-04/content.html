Core Concept

Exam format, duration, and pacing

Understand how the 65-minute limit and question count influence how you answer and practice.

  • 40 questions total: practice mixed sets that sum to forty to simulate fatigue and pacing.
  • 65 minutes allotted: aim for an average of ~1.5 minutes per question during timed drills.
  • NDA/tutorial: an extra 10 minutes is included before the timed section — use it to settle your focus.
Example drill
Ten-question timed blocks at 15 minutes, review mistakes immediately, repeat twice.
Common mistake
Rushing through all questions to 'finish' rather than balancing speed with accuracy.
