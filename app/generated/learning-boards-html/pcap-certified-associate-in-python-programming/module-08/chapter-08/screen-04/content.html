What is active review?
A deliberate process of retrieving information from memory using flashcards, self-quizzing, and teaching aloud.
Technique
Flashcards: question on front, concise answer on back. Focus on concept, not verbatim text.
Example
Convert "generators" notes into five retrieval prompts and write a short example from memory.
Common misconception
Familiarity from re-reading is not mastery. If you can’t produce an example without notes, you haven’t learned it deeply enough for the exam.
Key Takeaway

Turn three weak topics into flashcard sets and schedule two timed recall sessions for each before your next mock.

Concrete activity
Create five flashcards for a single topic, self-quiz until you can answer all without notes, then explain the topic aloud for two minutes and write a sample snippet from memory.
Why this helps
  • Forces retrieval and clarifies gaps.
  • Builds ability to produce examples under pressure.
  • Creates durable connections used when tracing exam code.
Check your understanding
Can you explain why a type is mutable or immutable and give a two-line example demonstrating mutation or immutability?
