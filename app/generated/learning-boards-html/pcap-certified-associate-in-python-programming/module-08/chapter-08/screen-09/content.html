Process

Step-by-step registration

Follow these validated steps and checklists to confirm your exam booking without surprises.

  1. 1 Create account and verify email; match your government ID details.
  2. 2 Select Exam 300-101 (PCAP) and preferred delivery: center or online proctor.
  3. 3 Read check-in rules, prepare required IDs, and note banned items.
  4. 4 Schedule date/time that matches your practice routine; confirm cancellation policy.
Example
If your ID uses a middle initial, use that exact format when registering. For online proctoring, test your webcam and microphone at least one day before the exam to reduce technical risk.

Common Mistakes
  • Using a nickname on your account that doesn't match your photo ID.
  • Scheduling a time without checking time-zone differences for online proctoring.
  • Not reading cancellation or rescheduling fees; these vary by provider.
Key Takeaway

Confirm identity details, test your environment, and pick a slot that supports your best performance.
