Timeline

Exam day minute-by-minute plan

Before the exam
  • Arrive or log in 15–30 minutes early and verify your ID.
  • Run system checks for webcam and microphone if online.
  • Prepare scratch paper, pen, and a quiet room.
During the exam
  • Use an initial pass to answer quick questions, flag difficult ones.
  • Keep an eye on time; aim for even pacing and review flagged items last.
  • If a technical problem appears, follow proctor instructions calmly.
After the exam
Save a short note about difficult items and consider a cooldown routine before reviewing results.
Quick Tips
  • Wear comfortable clothes and bring water.
  • Avoid last-minute cram; brief review is fine.
  • Remember equal weighting; don’t over-invest time in a single question.
Key Takeaway

Controlled pacing and calm handling of procedural steps reduce errors and performance loss under stress.
