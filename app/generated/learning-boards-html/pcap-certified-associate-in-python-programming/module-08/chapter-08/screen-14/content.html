What to Remember
Active Recall

Practice retrieval through flashcards and self-quizzing to transform familiarity into usable knowledge.

Mock Exams

Use full-length, timed mocks under exam-like conditions and analyze every incorrect answer.

Exam Logistics

Register early, confirm ID and proctor rules, and pick a time that matches your practice routine to avoid surprises.

Final Takeaway

Combine disciplined study, realistic practice, and careful logistics to maximize your readiness and confidence on test day.
