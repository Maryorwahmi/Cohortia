Process Flow › Mock Exam Strategy
Before the mock
  • Set a 65-minute timer and remove notes or internet access.
  • Prepare scratch paper, a pen, and a quiet space.
  • Decide a pacing rule: answer easy questions first, flag hard ones.
During the mock
  • Keep an eye on time; move on after 1–2 minutes per question if stuck.
  • Make an educated guess then flag for review.
  • Reserve final minutes for flagged questions and sanity checks.
After the mock
Analyze every question. For each wrong answer, record: topic, mistake type, how you will fix it. Aggregate mistakes by topic to find patterns.
Concrete example
If you repeatedly miss inheritance questions, schedule focused drills on subclass method resolution and super() usage until you can trace example outputs reliably.
Key Takeaway

Use mock exams as a feedback loop: time yourself, record mistakes by topic, and then use your checklist to correct the largest gaps.
