A Diagnostic Process
Run timed mocks to reveal weak points, then target them intentionally.
1. Take a timed practice test under exam conditions.
2. Log each wrong answer and identify root cause.
3. Design targeted drills and re-test that topic after practice.
Example
If you miss questions about exceptions, write small programs that raise and handle exceptions and explain the flow of execution. Then retake a short quiz on exceptions to confirm improvement.
Common Mistake
Reviewing only correct answers. Focus on incorrect ones first; they reveal what you don't yet know.
Metric
Track topic-level accuracy over time instead of only overall score.
