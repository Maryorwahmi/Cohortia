Module 08 › Chapter 8.2 › Common Pitfalls
Cramming
Short-term gains but poor retention. Replace with spaced sessions and regular low-stakes review.
Passive Re-Reading
Creates familiarity but not recall. Replace with active tasks like teaching or coding small examples.
Action Swap
For each passive hour, schedule 30 minutes of active practice and 15 minutes of review. Track progress and iterate weekly.
