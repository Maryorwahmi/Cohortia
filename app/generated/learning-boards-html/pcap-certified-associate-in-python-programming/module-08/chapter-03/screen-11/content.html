Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Weekly Practice Timeline
Sample weekly routine
Short daily drills plus weekly simulations to build exam-ready habits.
Daily — 5 × 2-minute traces
Improve raw tracing speed and variable tracking.
Midweek — 20-question timed block
Practice two-pass method and elimination strategies.
Weekend — 40-question simulation
Record metrics, review errors, and plan targeted drills.
Why this works
Distributed practice with reflection targets specific weaknesses and prevents burned-out long sessions that have low yield.
Common pitfall
Skipping review after a simulation. Without analysis, practice does not translate into improvement.
Key Takeaway

A weekly cycle of short drills, midweek practice, and weekly simulation builds reliable exam performance.
