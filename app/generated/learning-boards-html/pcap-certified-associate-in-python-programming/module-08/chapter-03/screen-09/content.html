Micro-practices to stay steady
Small rituals you can use during the exam to keep cognition sharp.
Breathing reset
Inhale 4, hold 2, exhale 4. Repeat once. This quickly lowers stress and restores focus.
Micro-reset
If stuck, pause, re-read, and break the question into smaller pieces before tracing again.
Confidence anchor
Briefly recall a recent successful practice run to remind yourself that preparation works.
Key Takeaway

Use a short reset routine to clear stress and restore clear problem-solving in the middle of the exam.

Practical cues
When to use the reset
After a long pause, before a difficult marked question, or when you notice repeated small mistakes.
Common misconception
Thinking you must power through without stopping. Brief resets save time by preventing spirals.
