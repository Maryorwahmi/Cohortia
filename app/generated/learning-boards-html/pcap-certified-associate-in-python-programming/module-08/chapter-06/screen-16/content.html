What to Remember
Closures
Capture names from the enclosing scope; bind values with defaults when needed.
Generators
Lazy and memory-efficient, but single-use; materialize to reuse.
Exam tip
Annotate creation, binding, and truthiness steps before choosing an answer.
One final reminder: small language details are high leverage. Spend practice time tracing code rather than guessing. Your speed and accuracy will improve.
