Tuple: Ordered & Immutable
Tuples preserve order and cannot be changed after creation, making them suitable for fixed records and hashable keys.
Packing & Unpacking
Pack values into a tuple and unpack them into variables for concise and readable assignments like swapping.
Set: Unique & Unordered
Sets automatically remove duplicates and support efficient membership and set algebra operations.
Summary
Ask: does my data need order, mutability, or uniqueness? That question points to list, tuple, or set respectively.
