Why loops matter

Loops let you scale actions from single steps to thousands of items and keep logic compact and clear.

Estimated lesson time: ~10 minutes
Use case

Process lists of customers, readings, or files without repeating code.

Patterns

Count, filter, search, aggregate, and interact until conditions change.

In this lesson

What you will learn

Choose between for and while, use range for counting, control loops with break/continue, and avoid common mistakes.

for
Iterate over items or a generated sequence.
while
Repeat until a condition becomes false.
Control
Break, continue, and the loop else clause.
