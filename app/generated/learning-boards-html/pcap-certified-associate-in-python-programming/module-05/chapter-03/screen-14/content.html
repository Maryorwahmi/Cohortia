Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Encapsulation
Bundle data and methods, and expose a controlled public surface to protect invariants and ease refactoring.
Name Mangling
Double underscores change __name to _ClassName__name to avoid accidental collisions in subclasses.
@property
Create attribute-like access while running getter, setter, and deleter logic for validation or computation.
Summary Takeaway

Design APIs that enforce invariants inside classes. Use conventions and properties to communicate intent and to protect state during maintenance and inheritance.
