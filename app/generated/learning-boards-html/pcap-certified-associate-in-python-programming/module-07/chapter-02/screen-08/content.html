Formatting directives
Use directives to compose the exact output you need for logs or users.
%Y %m %d
Year-month-day (numeric)
%H %M %S
24-hour time: hour, minute, second
%I %p
12-hour hour and AM/PM
%A %B
Full weekday and month names (locale-dependent)
Examples
formatted = future_moment.strftime("Date: %A, %B %d, %Y Time: %I:%M %p")
print(formatted)
# Date: Sunday, November 01, 2023 Time: 05:30 PM
Gotcha
Using %H with %p or confusing %m (month) with %M (minute) are frequent sources of incorrect output.
