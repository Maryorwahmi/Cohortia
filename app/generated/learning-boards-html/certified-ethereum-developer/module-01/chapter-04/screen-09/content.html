How categories differ

Each category uses blockchain properties in distinct ways. Below are compact cards that highlight priorities and typical architectural choices.

DeFi
On-chain liquidity, composable smart contracts, high need for security auditing and precise economic design.
NFTs
Unique token ownership recorded on-chain; media often stored off-chain with content hashes on-chain.
DAOs
Governance rules encoded in contracts enable collective decision-making; token-weighted voting is common.
Gaming
Player-owned assets as tokens; latency-sensitive logic often kept off-chain with on-chain settlement for ownership.
Concrete contrast
A DeFi lending pool must keep all balances and interest rules on-chain to ensure composability, while a game might only store item ownership on-chain and keep movement and collision logic off-chain for performance.
