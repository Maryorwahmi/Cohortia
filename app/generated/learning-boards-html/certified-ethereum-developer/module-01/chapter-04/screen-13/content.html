Common Mistakes

Assume public ledger visibility

Transactions are public. Designers must not assume anonymity; instead, design for privacy where required and make users aware of public traces.

Mistake: Relying on frontend validation only — critical checks must live in contracts.
Mistake: Skipping tests and audits before deployment.
Mistake: Putting large files on-chain instead of storing references.
Concrete example
If a developer assumes wallets are anonymous, they may expose sensitive flows that can be traced. Plan privacy needs and communicate them to users.
Mitigations
  • Write contract-level validation for all critical logic.
  • Use testing frameworks and audits before mainnet deployment.
  • Design UX that explains signing and pending states clearly.
Key Takeaway
Treat blockchain data as public and design systems and user flows around that reality to avoid privacy surprises and security mistakes.
