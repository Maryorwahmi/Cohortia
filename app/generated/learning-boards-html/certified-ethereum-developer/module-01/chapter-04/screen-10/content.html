Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Benefits & Challenges
Benefits

Transparency and user ownership

Transparent rules and auditable transactions
True user ownership of tokens and assets
Censorship resistance and availability with the network
Challenges
Scalability and gas costs limit how frequently and cheaply you can run on-chain interactions. UX must help users manage wallets and wait for confirmations. Immutable contracts complicate upgrades and fixes.
Concrete example
A DeFi bug can cause loss of funds; because state is on-chain, exploits are often irreversible without coordinated governance and compensating actions.
Pro tip
Design UX to show pending transaction states and estimated gas. Consider batching or off-chain aggregation to reduce on-chain costs when appropriate.
