Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Smart Contracts
What they are
On-chain programs that execute deterministically and alter state based on coded rules.
Use cases
Tokens, escrows, automated payouts, DAOs, and many DApps rely on smart contracts.
Properties
Immutable by default, transparent to observers, and deterministic in execution.
Common misconception
Immutability does not equal perfection — bugs become permanent unless an upgrade pattern was implemented.
Concrete example
A freelancer escrow contract that releases funds when a deliverable is confirmed.
Scenario
Client deposits ETH into a contract; upon receipt confirmation by both parties or an oracle, the contract transfers funds to the freelancer automatically.
Key Takeaway
Smart contracts automate trust, but you must plan for upgrades and audits to manage risks from immutability.
