Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Gas Units, Price, Limit
Definitions
Clear definitions to avoid costly mistakes.
Gas Units
Measured work consumed by operations. Example: 21,000 for a simple transfer.
Gas Price
Amount of ETH you pay per Gas Unit, often in Gwei. Higher price increases priority.
Gas Limit
Maximum gas you're willing to allow. If too low, transaction fails but consumed gas is charged.
Worked example
Calculate the fee for a 21,000 gas transfer at 20 Gwei.
Gas Units * Gas Price = 21,000 * 20 Gwei = 420,000 Gwei
Convert to ETH: 420,000 * 1e-9 = 0.00042 ETH
Total fee = 0.00042 ETH
Key Takeaway
Always convert gas estimates into ETH so users understand the real cost and avoid failed transactions from low limits.
