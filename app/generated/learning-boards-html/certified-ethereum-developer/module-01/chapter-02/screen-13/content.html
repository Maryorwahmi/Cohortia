Worked examples
Step-by-step arithmetic from the chapter's scenarios.
// Example 1: Simple transfer
Gas Units = 21,000
Gas Price = 20 Gwei
Total (Gwei) = 21,000 * 20 = 420,000 Gwei
Convert to ETH: 420,000 * 1e-9 = 0.00042 ETH

// Example 2: Complex contract call
Gas Units = 150,000
Gas Price = 30 Gwei
Total (Gwei) = 150,000 * 30 = 4,500,000 Gwei
Convert to ETH: 4,500,000 * 1e-9 = 0.0045 ETH
Checks and tips
Small mistakes in conversion lead to incorrect fee displays — double-check units.
Common error
Forgetting to convert Gwei to ETH results in fees off by a factor of one billion.
Key Takeaway
Always show both gas units and the converted ETH cost to help users understand fees.
