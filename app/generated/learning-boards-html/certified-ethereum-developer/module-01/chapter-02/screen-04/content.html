Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Bitcoin vs Ethereum
Core comparison
Understand different design goals and use cases.
Bitcoin
Peer-to-peer electronic cash focused on secure, immutable transfers.
Ethereum
A programmable blockchain able to deploy and execute arbitrary logic (smart contracts).
Expressiveness
Ethereum supports complex state and interactions; Bitcoin emphasizes simplicity and security.
When to choose
Pick Bitcoin for simple transfers; pick Ethereum when you need verifiable automation and programmability.
Concrete example
Simple transfer vs deployed contract managing recurring payments.
Example: Simple ETH transfer
A transfer moves value from A to B and consumes a fixed amount of gas, with little logic required.
Example: Subscription via smart contract
A contract holds funds, checks dates or signals, and releases payments automatically when conditions are met, requiring more gas but enabling automation without an intermediary.
Key Takeaway
Design choice is trade-off between expressiveness and minimal attack surface.
