Learning Objectives
A practical list of the skills you'll build in this chapter.
01
Differentiate Bitcoin vs Ethereum
Identify primary purposes and functional differences; useful when choosing platforms.
02
Grasp the 'world computer'
Understand decentralized, deterministic execution across nodes and its implications for trust.
03
Explain Ether (ETH)
Describe ETH as a currency and as the fuel that pays for network computation.
04
Compute Gas costs
Use Gas units and Gas Price to estimate ETH fees for transfers and contract calls.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Each objective is paired with a visual mnemonic to speed recall: a two-column contrast for Bitcoin vs Ethereum, a globe-to-cpu metaphor for the world computer, a fuel gauge for Ether and Gas, and a simple arithmetic template for gas calculations. Practicing the hands-on calculation in a later screen will verify your understanding and prepare you to reason about transaction costs when designing smart contracts or DApps.
