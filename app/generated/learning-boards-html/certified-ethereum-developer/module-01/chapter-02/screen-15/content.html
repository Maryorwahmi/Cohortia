EVM
Deterministic runtime that all nodes execute to reach consensus on contract outcomes.
Gas Mechanics
Gas Units × Gas Price (in Gwei) converted to ETH determines fees; Gas Limit caps usage.
Smart Contracts
Self-executing code that automates agreements; design for immutability and upgrade decisions.
Final thought

Use these core concepts as a checklist when you design or audit DApps: correctness, determinism, cost, and upgradeability.
