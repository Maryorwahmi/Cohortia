Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Ethereum
A programmable, decentralized platform for smart contracts and DApps.
Ether (ETH)
Native currency used both to transfer value and to pay for computational work.
Gas
A unit measuring work on the EVM; priced in ETH using the Gas Price.
Takeaway

Together, deterministic execution (EVM), economic incentives (ETH & Gas), and careful contract design enable decentralized applications to operate without trusted intermediaries.
