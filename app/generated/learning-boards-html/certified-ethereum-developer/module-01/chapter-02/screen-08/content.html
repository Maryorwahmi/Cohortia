Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Gas explained
What Gas measures
An abstract unit assigned to EVM operations to quantify computational effort.
Purpose
Prevents spam, incentivizes processing, and allocates scarce compute resources.
Typical values
Simple transfer = 21,000 gas; complex contract calls can be hundreds of thousands or more.
Common mistake
Confusing Gas with ETH — Gas is a unit; you pay for it in ETH using the Gas Price.
Concrete scenario
Estimate fee for a simple transfer or a complex contract interaction using gas units and gas price.
Tip
If a transaction runs out of Gas, it fails but consumed gas remains charged — set gas limits conservatively.
Key Takeaway
Always translate gas estimates into ETH costs so users understand fees and you can design efficient contracts.
