Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Hashing Experiment

Guided steps

1) Choose a SHA-256 or Keccak-256 hash tool you trust.
2) Enter: "Hello Cohortia!" and compute the digest.
3) Add a trailing space or change case and compute again.
4) Compare the two digests and notice they differ completely.
Why this matters
You see how hashes enable compact integrity checks that detect even tiny changes.
Example outputs
Input: "Hello Cohortia!"
SHA-256: d2f... (digest example)

Input: "Hello Cohortia! " (added space)
SHA-256: 7a9... (completely different)

Input: "hello cohortia!" (lowercase)
SHA-256: 3b1... (again different)
These example digests are illustrative; run the experiment so you see exact outputs. Try two inputs that look similar to see how unrelated the digests are.
