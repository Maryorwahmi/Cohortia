Quick checks to verify understanding

These short tasks convert passive reading into confirmable skills.

Sketch the chain
Draw three blocks, add hashes and previous-hash fields to see how a tamper would break links.
Compare systems
Write one centralized, one distributed, and one decentralized example and note failure modes.
Hash experiment
Type a phrase, alter one character, and observe the hash change to internalize sensitivity.
Summarize history
Write two sentences linking early hash chains to Bitcoin and then to Ethereum's programmability.

Mnemonic aids

Simple visuals help these distinctions stick.

Venn: control vs copies
Center overlap shows systems that are both distributed and decentralized; not all distributed systems are decentralized.
Timeline: ideas → platforms
Hash chains and signatures → Bitcoin solves double-spend → Ethereum adds programmable state.
Quick tip
When in doubt, ask: who controls the rules? That question clarifies centralization vs decentralization.
