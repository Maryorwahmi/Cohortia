Quick recall

Three one-line reminders
Blockchain
Linked blocks create tamper-evident history.
Decentralization
Shared control reduces single-point risks but needs governance.
Hashing
Fixed-size fingerprints enable fast integrity checks.
Use these three ideas as a quick lens when evaluating whether a blockchain is the right tool for a problem: integrity, participation model, and governance complexity.
