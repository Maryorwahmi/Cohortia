Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Blockchain
A distributed, immutable ledger linking blocks cryptographically.
Decentralization
Distribution of control and decision-making across participants.
Cryptographic Hashing
Fixed-size, one-way fingerprints that detect tiny changes.
Summary
These three pillars—ledger structure, distributed control, and hashing—form the foundation for secure, verifiable, and trust-minimized systems. Keep them in mind as you design and evaluate decentralized applications.
