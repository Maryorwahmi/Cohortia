Common mistakes

Blockchain ≠ Bitcoin — blockchain is the technology; Bitcoin is one application.
Not all blockchains are public — private and permissioned ledgers exist for specific needs.
Immutability depends on consensus — social coordination and costs underpin how permanent history is.
Decentralization has trade-offs — more resilience can mean harder governance and lower throughput.

Decision checklist

Who must control participation?
What privacy and compliance rules apply?
Is censorship resistance essential?
Key Takeaway

Use blockchain when decentralization and tamper-evident history provide clear value; otherwise central or distributed models may be better suited.
