Timeline: key milestones

A concise sequence showing how primitives assembled into working platforms.

1990s
Hash chains & signatures
Cryptographic building blocks for integrity and authentication.
2008
Bitcoin
First successful public, permissionless blockchain solving double-spend without a central authority.
2015
Ethereum
Extends the ledger with programmable state, enabling decentralized applications beyond currency.
Today
Many forms of DLT
Public, private, and permissioned ledgers exist to meet different trust and governance needs.
Misconception
Blockchain did not appear overnight. It integrates prior cryptographic and distributed systems work into a practical ledger with social and economic mechanisms.
