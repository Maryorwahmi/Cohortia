Learning Objectives

Skills you will master and why each matters.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain blockchain fundamentals

Describe a blockchain as a distributed, immutable ledger and why linked blocks produce tamper-evidence. This matters for auditability and trust in open systems.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate system architectures

Contrast centralized, distributed, and decentralized systems with examples highlighting single points of failure and resilience.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain cryptographic hashing

Define a hash function, show sensitivity to input changes, and explain why hashes secure block links.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Contextualize blockchain history

Identify how ideas like hash chains and signatures led to Bitcoin and then to programmable platforms such as Ethereum.
