Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Block Explorers
What explorers show
Transactions, blocks, addresses, contract code, events and internal calls. Use them to verify state and investigate issues after transactions execute.
Verify Deployments
Confirm contract bytecode, source verification, and transaction receipt on Sepolia or Mainnet.
Debug Transactions
Inspect gas used, revert reasons, and any emitted events to trace failures.
Example
After sending a test transaction, paste the transaction hash into Sepolia Etherscan to confirm inclusion in a block and to view logs and status codes.
Key Takeaway

Block explorers are the primary audit surface for verifying on‑chain actions and debugging smart contract behavior.
