Checklist
  1. Install MetaMask extension for your browser.
  2. Create a new wallet and write the seed phrase on paper; store offline.
  3. Enable test networks in settings so Sepolia is visible.
  4. Switch MetaMask to Sepolia Test Network.
  5. Copy your Sepolia address and request test ETH from a faucet.
  6. Verify the balance in MetaMask before interacting with DApps.
Safety Reminder
Never enter your seed phrase into a website. Only give out your public address if you want to receive test ETH or collect feedback.
Key Takeaway

The lab gives you a safe, recoverable account for development. Proper seed backups and network checks prevent irreversible mistakes.
