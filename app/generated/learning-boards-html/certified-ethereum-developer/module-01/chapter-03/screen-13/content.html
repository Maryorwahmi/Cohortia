Best Practices
  • Write your seed phrase on paper and store offline.
  • Use hardware wallets for long‑term or high‑value storage.
  • Use different keys for development and production.
  • Always confirm the active network before signing transactions.
Common Mistake

Storing seed phrases digitally or reusing the same account across testnet and Mainnet increases the chance of permanent loss.

Scenario
A developer who saves a seed phrase to cloud storage risks that phrase being stolen. Use offline backups and hardware devices to mitigate this risk.
Pro Tip

Keep a small ‘hot’ account for everyday tests and a separate cold storage account for high value assets.
