Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Custodial vs Non‑Custodial
Custodial

Service‑held keys

A third party stores and manages private keys. Useful for new users and on‑ramps but requires trust in the provider's security and policies.

• Easier recovery options. • Centralized policies and limits. • Higher counterparty risk.
When to use
Small, frequent trades or onboarding users who value easy recovery over absolute control.
Non‑Custodial

You control the keys

Keys are stored locally or on hardware you control. Gives full autonomy and responsibility for backups and security.

• Full control and no third‑party restores. • Stronger security with hardware wallets. • Must manage backups securely.
When to use
Holding significant assets or when you require sovereignty over keys and privacy.
Key Takeaway

Select custodial when convenience and recovery matter; select non‑custodial for maximum control and security. Balance your project's needs.
