Activity steps
1. Reproduce exploit
Deploy Bank and Attacker, fund Attacker, then call the attack to observe repeated withdrawals.
2. Refactor Bank
Apply CEI: decrement balances before external call. Add ReentrancyGuard and mark withdraw nonReentrant.
3. Verify
Redeploy SafeBank and re-run Attacker. Confirm funds are not drained and that Attacker's calls revert or fail safely.
Test tip
Assert contract balances after failed attacks and include cases where receiver fallback consumes gas legitimately.
