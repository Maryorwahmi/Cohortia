Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › transfer vs send vs call
Choosing a transfer method
transfer()
Forwards 2300 gas, reentrancy-resistant in many cases, but can break receivers that need more gas.
send()
Similar to transfer, returns a boolean on success or failure. Same gas limitation applies.
call()
Forwards all available gas by default. Flexible but requires CEI and guards to be safe.
Practical rule
Prefer call with CEI and a reentrancy guard for maximum compatibility and safety.
Mistake to avoid
Relying solely on transfer for security can cause legitimate transactions to fail and ignores other attack vectors.
