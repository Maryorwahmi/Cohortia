Practice roadmap
A step sequence to make the vulnerability visible and then remove it by refactor and guard.
1. Reproduce exploit
Deploy Bank and Attacker, then observe repeated withdrawals that drain funds.
2. Apply CEI
Refactor withdraw to update balances before making external calls.
3. Add ReentrancyGuard
Inherit OpenZeppelin guard and mark withdraw nonReentrant to add a mutex.
Each step is small but crucial: seeing the exploit makes the defense concrete, and testing ensures you didn't break legitimate flows that require more gas on receive/fallback handlers.
