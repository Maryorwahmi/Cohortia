Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › DAO & History
Timeline: From bug to system change
Exploit occurs
Attacker re-enters withdraw before balance update and drains funds in repeated calls.
Community response
Large loss leads to emergency measures and a network-level fork decision in response to the incident.
Security practices evolve
CEI and reentrancy guards become widely recommended, and audits focus on external-call ordering.
Common misconception
Thinking reentrancy only matters for large contracts. Small contracts with fund flows can be exploited just as easily.
Why it matters
The exploit demonstrates systemic risk; a single vulnerability can cascade into large financial and governance consequences.
