Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Denial of Service (DoS)
An attack or failure that prevents users from accessing contract behavior.
Block Gas Limit
The maximum gas that all transactions in a block can consume; operations must fit beneath it.
Unbounded Loop
A loop over state that can grow arbitrarily, risking gas exhaustion and DoS.
Summary takeaway

Use these concepts as a rapid triage: if a function iterates over state or relies on a stoppable dependency, treat it as high risk and apply pull, batch, or redesign patterns.
