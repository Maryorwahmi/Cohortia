Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › External contract dependencies
Risk

DoS by proxy

If an external contract can be paused, upgraded, or self-destructed, your contract may lose critical functionality.

Examples
  • An ERC-20 with a pause capability stops token transfers for your DApp.
  • An oracle contract that reverts or returns invalid data breaks dependent logic.
Mitigations
Use trusted providers, multiple oracle sources, fallback mechanisms, and decentralized governance for critical dependencies.
Concrete scenario
A paused token makes payment flows revert, which can prevent refunds or trades in your DApp and thus block users.
Checklist
Map external calls, document fallback behavior, and avoid critical flows that rely on a single dependency without redundancy.
