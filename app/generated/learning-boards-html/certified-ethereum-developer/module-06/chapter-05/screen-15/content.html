Pull-based Payments
Record claimable balances and let users withdraw to avoid global loops.
Bounded Iteration
Process a fixed batch size per call so gas remains predictable.
External Dependencies
Treat third-party contracts as potential failure points and add fallbacks.
Final takeaway

Combine pull payments, batching, careful data design, and stress testing to make contracts resilient to DoS and gas attacks.
