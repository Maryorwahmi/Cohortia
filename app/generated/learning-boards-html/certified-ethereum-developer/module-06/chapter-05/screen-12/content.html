Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Defensive checklist
Audit flags
  • Unbounded loops over on-chain arrays.
  • External calls inside iteration.
  • Single-owner pause or kill controls.
Design rules
  • Prefer pull-based payments and mappings.
  • Use batching with conservative caps if iteration needed.
  • Consider Merkle proofs for large, verifiable lists.
Operational steps
  • Stress test with large datasets and adversarial inputs.
  • Document batch progress and provide relayers or incentives.
  • Use multi-sig and time-locks for critical controls.
Key takeaway
View availability as an engineering constraint. Design, test, and document for scale and adversarial behavior.
