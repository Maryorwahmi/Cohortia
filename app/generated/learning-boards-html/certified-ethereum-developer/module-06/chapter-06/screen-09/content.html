Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Time-Weighted Average Price
Timeline
TWAP sampling window
Samples taken at regular intervals are averaged to compute a stable price.
Short window (fast)
More responsive but less robust to atomic manipulation.
Moderate window
Balances timeliness and resistance to flash spikes.
Long window (safe)
Very robust to single-block manipulation but slow to react to genuine changes.
Practical note
TWAP vs spot
TWAPs protect against temporary price spikes; spot prices are susceptible to flash loans. Choose based on the application's tolerance for stale data.
Key Takeaway

TWAPs raise the cost of manipulation by requiring sustained price distortion, trading immediacy for security.
