Oracle Manipulation & Front-Running
Understand why oracles and public mempools create attack surfaces, and learn layered mitigations used across DeFi.
In this lesson
Map the oracle problem, study flash-loan price manipulation and sandwich front-running, and learn practical countermeasures used in live protocols.
Why it matters
A single bad price or a visible pending transaction can cost a protocol millions. Defenses reduce attack surface and protect user funds.
Explore Key Topics
Three focused areas you'll see in this chapter
Oracle Problem
Contracts can't fetch off-chain data directly; oracles bridge that gap but add trust assumptions.
Price Manipulation
Flash loans and low-liquidity markets let attackers distort prices used by single-source oracles.
Front-Running
Mempool visibility lets attackers reorder or sandwich transactions for profit.
