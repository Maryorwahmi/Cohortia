Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Sandwich Attack Walkthrough
Sequence
Observe → Front-run → Back-run
1
Observe pending trade
The attacker sees a large swap in the mempool.
2
Submit buy first
Attacker buys to move the price up before the victim's swap executes.
3
Victim's trade executes
The victim suffers worse slippage and pays the inflated price.
4
Attacker sells
Attacker sells into the victim's order and captures the spread.
Mitigations
Split orders, use private relays or Flashbots, set slippage tolerances, or batch transactions to reduce exposure.
User tip
When transacting large amounts, consider private relays or segmented trades to lower sandwich risk.
