Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Front-Running & Mempool
Definition
What front-running is
Observing pending transactions and submitting your own to execute earlier or later for profit.
Common vectors
  • Higher gas to get mined first
  • Miner-level ordering and censorship
  • Public mempool monitoring services
Example
A victim's large buy is sandwiched
Attacker buys first, victim's large buy executes at higher price, then attacker sells into the victim's order to profit.
Mitigation checklist
Commit-reveal, batching, private relays, and randomness can reduce exposure to mempool-based attacks.
