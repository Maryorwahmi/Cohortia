Process Flow
How a stale-data exploit happens
1
Oracle snapshot taken
The oracle records a price at t0 and publishes it on-chain.
2
Price moves off-chain
Market shifts quickly; real price diverges from the snapshot.
3
Attacker exploits window
An attacker performs actions that rely on the stale snapshot before the oracle updates.
Mitigations
Use TWAP
Average prices over a window so single-block spikes have limited effect.
Increase redundancy
Aggregate across DEXs and oracle nodes to avoid single-source drift.
Circuit breakers
Pause or revert on extreme deviations to protect funds while teams investigate.
Practical check: pick a safe TWAP window for collateral valuation and explain why it reduces flash-spike risk compared with spot prices.
