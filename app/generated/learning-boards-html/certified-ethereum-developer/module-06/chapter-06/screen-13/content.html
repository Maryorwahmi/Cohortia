Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Front-Running Mitigations
Comparison
Mitigation trade-offs
Commit-Reveal
Hides user intent until reveal, preventing mempool-based front-running; requires two-phase interaction.
Batching
Groups orders to reduce exploit granularity; may delay execution and require coordination.
Private relays
Submit transactions off-mempool to trusted relays or miner-direct channels to avoid exposure.
Advanced
Sequencers & encryption
Decentralized sequencers or threshold encryption prevent early visibility of transaction payloads, but they add protocol complexity and trust trade-offs.
Key Takeaway

Combine protections: hide critical data, batch where possible, and use private submission channels for high-value actions.
