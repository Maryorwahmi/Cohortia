Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Flash Loan Price Manipulation
Scenario
Atomic flash loan manipulation
1
Flash loan borrowed
Attacker takes an uncollateralized loan that must be repaid in the same transaction.
2
Manipulate DEX price
Large buy or sell on a low-liquidity pair temporarily skews the on-chain spot price.
3
Exploit vulnerable oracle
The protocol reads the manipulated price and grants favorable loans or liquidations.
4
Repay flash loan and pocket profit
After repaying, the attacker retains net gains made possible by the temporary price distortion.
Concrete example
WETH → Token X → Borrow DAI
Attacker borrows WETH, buys Token X to inflate price on a DEX, uses inflated Token X as collateral to borrow DAI, then sells Token X and repays the loan.
Misconception
Believing low-liquidity markets are irrelevant. In reality, low liquidity makes manipulation cheaper and more effective.
