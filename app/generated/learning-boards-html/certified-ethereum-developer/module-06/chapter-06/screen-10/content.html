Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Redundancy & Circuit Breakers
Comparison
Redundancy vs single-source
Multiple sources reduce reliance on any one feed; single-source is brittle.
Multi-source
Aggregates across nodes and exchanges to smooth outliers.
Single-source
Simple but vulnerable to targeted manipulation and insider compromise.
Circuit breakers
Deviation thresholds and pause logic
If reported price deviates too far from expected bounds, halt sensitive operations and require manual review or fallback logic.
Design tips
  • Choose thresholds that avoid frequent false positives during normal volatility.
  • Provide fallback feeds or administrative paths for safe recovery.
