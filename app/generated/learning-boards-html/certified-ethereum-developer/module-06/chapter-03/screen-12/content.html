Migrating vulnerable code to safe arithmetic
A step-by-step checklist to refactor and verify arithmetic safety.
Steps
  1. Identify arithmetic hotspots and operations on balances or totals.
  2. Decide SafeMath (if staying on <0.8.0) or upgrade to 0.8.x to rely on checks.
  3. Add unit tests for boundary cases and assert reverts.
  4. Document any unchecked blocks and justify proofs or tests.
  5. Deploy to testnet and monitor for edge-case reports.
Quick sanity checks
  • Are all transfers guarded?
  • Is totalSupply protected from overflow?
  • Do tests include max and zero boundaries?
Key Takeaway

A small, repeatable migration process reduces risk and makes the change auditable and testable.
