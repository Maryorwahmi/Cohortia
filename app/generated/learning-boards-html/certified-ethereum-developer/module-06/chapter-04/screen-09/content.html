Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Role Management
Role lifecycle steps
  1. Define role constants using keccak256.
  2. Grant DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE to a trusted governance address or multi-sig in the constructor.
  3. Grant specific roles to service accounts as needed.
  4. Revoke roles promptly when accounts leave or keys rotate.
Protect admin keys and document the process for grants and revocations. Consider off-chain approval workflows to coordinate multi-sig operations for critical changes.
Example
During deployment, call _setupRole(DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE, msg.sender) to make the deployer the initial admin. Replace that admin with a multi-sig after deployment to minimize single-key risk.
Warning

If DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE is compromised an attacker can grant themselves powerful roles—operate with caution.
