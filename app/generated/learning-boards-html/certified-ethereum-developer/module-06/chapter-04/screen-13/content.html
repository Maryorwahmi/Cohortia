Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Hands-on Activity
Starter checklist
  1. Create MyRBACControlledToken and import AccessControl.
  2. Define MINTER_ROLE and PAUSER_ROLE and grant roles in constructor.
  3. Implement mint, pause, unpause with role checks and pausedState.
  4. Deploy locally and run the test scenarios listed below.
Test scenarios
  • Unauthorized mint attempt should revert.
  • Grant MINTER_ROLE and verify mint succeeds.
  • Pause contract and verify minting is blocked until unpaused.
  • Revoke role and confirm revoked account loses permissions.
Why this matters

Testing both positive and negative cases proves that your access control enforces intended policy and prevents accidental exposures.
