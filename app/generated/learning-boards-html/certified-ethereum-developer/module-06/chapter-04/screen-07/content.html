Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Role-Based Access Control
RBAC fundamentals
  • Roles are named permissions, often bytes32 constants.
  • Granting and revoking changes which addresses hold a role.
  • DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE can manage other roles and should be guarded.
Concrete example

Grant MINTER_ROLE to a minting service and PAUSER_ROLE to a small emergency committee to limit damage from a compromised account.

Design guidance
Define narrow roles. Avoid mixing unrelated powers into a single role. Review and rotate role holders periodically as part of operations.
Misconception
RBAC is not automatically secure; poorly scoped roles can recreate the same risks as a single owner.
Check
Map each role to a real operational responsibility and verify role tests fail for non-members.
