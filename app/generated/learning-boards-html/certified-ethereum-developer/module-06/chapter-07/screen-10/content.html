Activity: test MyERC20Token
Choose Hardhat or Foundry and implement the required suite.
Required tests
  • Verify totalSupply is set and deployer receives it.
  • Transfer success between accounts and balance updates.
  • Transfer fails with insufficient balance and to zero address.
  • Approve and transferFrom success and failure cases.
Challenge
Add a fuzz test for transfer with randomized addresses and amounts, using vm.assume in Foundry or property-based libraries where available.
