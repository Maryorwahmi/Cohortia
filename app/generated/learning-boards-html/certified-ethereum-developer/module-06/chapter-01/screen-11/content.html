Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Denial of Service
What DoS looks like on-chain
An attacker causes required operations to fail or consume all gas, preventing normal users from interacting with the contract.
Unbounded loops over arrays that grow with user input.
Relying on callbacks from external contracts that may revert.
Gas costs that grow and exceed block limits.
Practical defenses
  • Prefer pull payments so users withdraw on-demand instead of forcing pushes.
  • Avoid iterating over unbounded lists inside a single transaction.
  • Fail gracefully when callbacks revert and provide alternative flows.
Why it matters

DoS can make a contract unusable even without direct theft. Resilience preserves availability for honest users.
