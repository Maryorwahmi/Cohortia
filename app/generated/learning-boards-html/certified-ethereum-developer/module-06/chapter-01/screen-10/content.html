Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Access Control
What to protect
Functions that move funds, change configuration, or destroy the contract must be protected by explicit checks.
Owner-only functions must check the caller's identity.
Public setters for policy are risky unless access is limited.
Checklist for reviewers
  • Are any state-changing functions public without checks?
  • Does the contract assume msg.sender means a user, not a contract?
  • Are there tests that call privileged functions from unprivileged addresses?
Common mistake

Assuming only trusted users will call a public function. On-chain, any address or contract can call it unless you explicitly restrict access.
