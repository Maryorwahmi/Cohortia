Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Reentrancy
Definition & mechanics
When execution yields to an external contract before internal state is final, that external actor can re-enter and repeat logic.
Key sign: external call (transfer/call) occurs before balance or state update.
Effect: attacker repeats the action, draining assets or corrupting state.
Defense: Checks-Effects-Interactions ordering and reentrancy guards.
Example scenario
A withdraw that sends Ether and only later sets balances to zero allows a calling contract to recursively invoke withdraw while the balance still appears available.
Analogy: lending someone cash before you record the ledger entry; they can repeatedly ask for cash until you update the ledger.
Common mistake

Trusting that external calls are harmless. Any external interaction can invoke untrusted code; treat it as an attacker vector.
