Introduction to Smart Contract Security
Why security is the foundation of trust on-chain

Smart contracts run in a public, immutable environment. When vulnerabilities are exploited, the consequences are often irreversible. This lesson establishes the mindset and core problems you will defend against: immutability, reentrancy, arithmetic bugs, and access control failures.
