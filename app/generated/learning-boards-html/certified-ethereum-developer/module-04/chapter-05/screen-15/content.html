Authorize minting
Use onlyOwner or role-based control to prevent uncontrolled creation of NFTs.
Match filenames
Ensure 0.json, 1.json map to tokenIds and include proper image ipfs:// references.
Verify end-to-end
After deployment, call tokenURI and ownerOf, and open the returned URI in an IPFS gateway to confirm the metadata and image.
Key Takeaway

Small, testable steps—secure minting, immutable metadata, and repeatable verification—are the most effective path to a trustworthy NFT collection.
