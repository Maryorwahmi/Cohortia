What to Remember
A short pre-deploy checklist for token safety.
Unit conversion
Confirm initialSupply uses decimals correctly.
Access control
Ensure mint is restricted to trusted accounts.
Secrets management
Use .env and ignore files; never commit private keys.
Final takeaway

Run a local deployment, test access control and unit conversion, and only then deploy to a public testnet. These steps preserve correctness and trust in your token.
