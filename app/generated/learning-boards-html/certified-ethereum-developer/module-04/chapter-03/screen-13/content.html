Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Hands-on Activity: Deploy Your Custom ERC-20
Hands-on Activity
Checklist to build, test, and deploy MyToken.
Quick checklist
  1. Initialize project and install dependencies (Hardhat, @openzeppelin/contracts, dotenv).
  2. Create contracts/MyToken.sol using ERC20 and Ownable and add mint/burn.
  3. Create scripts/deploy.js that deploys and prints balances in human units.
  4. Configure hardhat.config.js and .env for Sepolia; never commit private keys.
  5. Deploy locally, verify, then deploy to Sepolia and verify on the explorer.
Extension challenge
Add OpenZeppelin's Pausable contract to MyToken and provide an owner-only pause/unpause API. Test that transfers revert while paused and succeed when unpaused.
Why it matters
A pause mechanism gives an emergency tool to halt activity during incidents, but it must be constrained to trusted governance to avoid centralization risks.
Verification step
After Sepolia deploy, verify contract on the explorer and confirm it's recognized as ERC-20.
Hands-on check
Call balanceOf and decimals via ethers.js and compare formatted results to expected values.
Takeaway
Completing this flow gives you a deployable ERC-20 with verifiable behavior and a clear extension for emergency control.
