Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Why new ERCs emerged
Definition

Standards were created to consolidate repeated patterns: mixed asset inventories and standardized vault interactions that appeared across games and DeFi.

Analogy
Think of ERC-1155 as a warehouse with labeled bins (token ids) where some bins hold identical units and others hold unique items. ERC-4626 is a bank-safe that issues share certificates proportional to assets stored.
Benefit: fewer contracts to audit and maintain
Benefit: predictable integration points for wallets and aggregators
Common mistake: assuming standards remove all risk — you still must manage permissions and reentrancy.
Concrete example: game shop
A shop sells 100 coins, 5 potions, and a single legendary sword. With separate standards you need three contracts and multiple calls. With ERC-1155 a single batch transfer can move coins, potions, and the sword atomically.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Standards reduce repeated engineering work and make integrations predictable; design patterns that repeat are strong candidates for a standard.
