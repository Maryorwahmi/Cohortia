Process: safeBatchTransferFrom
Call safeBatchTransferFrom(from, to, ids[], amounts[], data). The EVM executes all transfers inside one transaction, and receivers implementing ERC1155TokenReceiver are validated.
Step 1: Gather ids and amounts for the bundle
Step 2: Ensure operator approvals if necessary
Step 3: Execute safeBatchTransferFrom atomically
Step 4: Verify receiver contract supports ERC1155TokenReceiver
Example: bundle purchase
A player buys 100 gold, 3 potions, and a skin. Prepare ids [1,2,1001] and amounts [100,3,1], then call safeBatchTransferFrom. All items arrive in one transaction, or none do.

Watch out
