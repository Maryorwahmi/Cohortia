Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Real-world Use Cases
Gaming

Inventories with currencies, consumables, and unique items managed by token ids and batch transfers.

Supply chain

Batch-tracked goods where batches are fungible but serialized units are unique within the same contract.

Ticketing

Issue fungible tickets, then convert some into non-fungible collectibles after the event.

DeFi vaults

Standardized deposits and conversions let wallets and aggregators interact reliably with multiple vaults.

Design tips

Plan id semantics, metadata lifecycle, and conversion rounding to keep user expectations consistent.

Key takeaway

Match standards to domains: ERC-1155 for mixed assets, ERC-4626 for composable vaults; design semantics intentionally.
