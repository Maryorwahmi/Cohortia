Transfer flow
  1. Caller constructs transfer(recipient, amount) with amount in base units.
  2. Contract checks caller balance >= amount.
  3. Contract updates state: decrease sender, increase recipient.
  4. Contract emits Transfer(from, to, value) event.
Off-chain systems subscribe to Transfer events to reflect token movements in UIs and analytics without scanning full state every block.
Example
Alice calls transfer(Bob, 2000000000000000000) on a token with 18 decimals to send 2 tokens. The contract reduces Alice's balance by 2 * 10^18 and increases Bob's by the same amount, then emits Transfer(Alice, Bob, 2000000000000000000).
Misconception
Events are helpful signals but not authoritative. For a security-sensitive check, always read contract state with balanceOf.
