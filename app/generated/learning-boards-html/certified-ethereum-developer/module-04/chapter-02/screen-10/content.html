Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › How a DEX Uses Approve & transferFrom
Components
User (Owner)
Calls approve to grant allowance and expects tokens moved on their behalf.
DEX (Spender)
Receives allowance and calls transferFrom to move tokens for trades or settlement.
Token Contract
Enforces balances and allowances and emits Transfer/Approval events.
Design note: present allowance values and recent spending activity in the UI. Encourage users to revoke or minimize allowances when not needed, and design the DEX to request minimal required approvals rather than blanket maximums when possible.
