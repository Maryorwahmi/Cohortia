Tokenization steps
A clear process helps you pick the right model and avoid integration traps.
1. Define asset
Decide unit of value and whether uniqueness matters.
2. Choose standard
ERC-20, ERC-721, or ERC-1155 based on required behavior.
3. Design metadata
Decide immutability, tokenURI patterns, decimals, and supply rules.
4. Implement
Deploy a contract that implements the standard and emits events.
5. Integrate
Ensure wallets and marketplaces can read functions and events for smooth UX.
Concrete scenario
Tokenizing a property may require fractional fungible tokens for shares and a registry NFT capturing the deed — plan standards accordingly.
Common mistake
Deploying custom functions in place of required ones, breaking tooling expectations.
