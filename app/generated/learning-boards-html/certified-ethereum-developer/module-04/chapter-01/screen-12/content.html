Why tokenization matters
Three transformative benefits that change how assets behave.
Liquidity
Fractional tokens let more people invest in high-value assets by owning shares rather than full assets.
Transparency
Immutable on-chain records provide verifiable ownership and transfer history for provenance.
Programmability
Smart contracts automate distributions, vesting, and governance tied to token behavior.
Concrete example
A property issues share tokens that distribute rent automatically. Ownership is provable on-chain and transfers are programmable.
Common misconception
Tokenization doesn't automatically create markets. Liquidity depends on demand and market structure, not tokenization alone.
