Visual concept
ERC as blueprint
An ERC specifies functions and events so different implementations behave the same. This guarantees wallets and DApps can interoperate without custom code.
Tokenization benefits
  • Fractional ownership increases liquidity.
  • Immutable ownership records enable provenance.
  • Programmable rules automate transfers and governance.
