Evolution of token standards
Adoption of one standard surfaces new use cases and inspires new standards.
Early
A minimal fungible token interface proves utility and fosters wallet support.
Middle
Specialized needs appear: asset uniqueness and metadata handling become priorities.
Later
Standards like multi-token interfaces address efficiency and mixed-use models.
Concrete takeaway
Standards grow from solving practical needs. Choose what fits your users, not what’s most popular.
