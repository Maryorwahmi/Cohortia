ERC-721 and uniqueness
Each token carries a unique identifier and usually metadata describing the asset.
Required concepts
  • ownerOf(tokenId) — who owns a specific token
  • tokenURI(tokenId) — pointer to metadata
  • safeTransferFrom — safe ownership transfer with checks
Concrete example
Digital artwork where each token ID maps to a unique image and provenance history visible on-chain.
Key Takeaway

Choose ERC-721 for assets that require unique identity and per-token metadata.

Design and pitfalls
Think about metadata immutability and how tokenURI is served. Mutable metadata can break provenance trust if not handled properly.
Analogy
A unique painting with a certificate of authenticity and a recorded ownership history.
Common mistake
Relying on an off-chain URL without a backup plan; if the URI disappears, the token may lose its referenced asset.
Check your understanding
What function would you call to confirm who owns token 42? Why does that matter for transfers?
