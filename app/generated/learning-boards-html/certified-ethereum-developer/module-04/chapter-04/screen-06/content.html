Approval functions
Understand the distinction between single-token approval and approval for all tokens for an owner.
approve(address to, uint256 tokenId)
Grants permission for a specific tokenId to one address. Useful for single-item marketplace listings.
setApprovalForAll(address operator, bool approved)
Grants or revokes an operator permission to manage all of the owner's NFTs. Commonly used by marketplaces.
Scenario: marketplace operator
A user can call setApprovalForAll to let a marketplace operator move any of their NFTs. This simplifies trading but concentrates power in the operator contract.
Warning
Because setApprovalForAll authorizes all tokens, granting it to an untrusted contract risks losing every NFT owned by the user.
Check your understanding
Differentiate when to use approve for a single token and when setApprovalForAll is appropriate. Prefer limited approvals unless you trust the operator fully.
