Activity steps
Follow these steps to inspect an ERC-721 contract and its metadata.
1. Contract reads
Open name(), symbol(), totalSupply() in the contract read tab to confirm identity.
2. Ownership checks
Call ownerOf(tokenId) and balanceOf(address) to verify the current owner and counts.
3. tokenURI and metadata
Call tokenURI(tokenId), fetch the JSON, and inspect name, description, image, and attributes.
Practical tip
If tokenURI returns an IPFS URI, fetch it through an IPFS gateway to view the JSON and confirm the image CID is immutable.
Reflection
After the inspection, ask whether metadata and image links are immutable and whether approvals or transfer behaviors expose risk.
