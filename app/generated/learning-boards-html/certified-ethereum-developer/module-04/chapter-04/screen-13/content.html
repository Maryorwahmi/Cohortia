ownerOf / balanceOf
ownerOf gives current owner for a tokenId; balanceOf gives a count of distinct tokens owned.
Transfer & Approval events
Events let external services observe ownership changes and approvals for UI and indexing.
Immutable metadata
Use IPFS or similar for metadata and image assets to prevent mutability and broken links.
Final takeaway
Combine proper transfer methods, cautious approvals, and immutable metadata to preserve NFT ownership, provenance, and value.
