Contract Security Best Practices
Design patterns and checks that prevent fund loss and logic exploits on Ethereum.
In this lesson
Core vulnerabilities, defensive patterns, and practical testing/auditing steps to protect contracts.
Practical demo
A guided refactor of a vulnerable withdraw into a secured pattern with a reentrancy guard.
Explore Key Topics
Reentrancy
External calls can re-enter before state updates; follow Checks-Effects-Interactions and consider a mutex.
Access Control
Use onlyOwner for simple cases and RBAC for multi-role systems; prefer tested libraries.
Testing & Audits
Unit tests, fuzzing, audits, and bug bounties are essential to catch real-world attack vectors.
