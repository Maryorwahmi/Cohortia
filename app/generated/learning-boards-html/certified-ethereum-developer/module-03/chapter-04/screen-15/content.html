Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Integer safety
Prefer Solidity 0.8+ which reverts on overflow; audit legacy arithmetic and assembly paths.
Front-running & DoS
Use commit-reveal and pull patterns; avoid unbounded iterations and fragile external dependencies.
Testing & audits
Make exploit tests part of CI, perform audits, and run bug bounties to catch post-deployment issues.
Final takeaway: layered defenses and rigorous verification reduce the probability and impact of security incidents. Make the practices routine.
