You will read and analyze canonical Solidity examples showing conditionals and loops. We include a small contract demo and a lab starter for a whitelist manager. Pay special attention to examples that iterate over storage arrays — these are the common source of gas issues. You will also learn concrete alternatives to on-chain iteration.
