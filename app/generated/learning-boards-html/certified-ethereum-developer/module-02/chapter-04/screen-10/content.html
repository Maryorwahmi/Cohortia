Architecture
Off-chain iteration and Merkle proofs
Move heavy work off-chain; present compact proof on-chain
External processing
Relayers or off-chain scripts process large arrays and submit batched results. The contract only verifies and records small, bounded updates.
Merkle proof pattern
Store a Merkle root on-chain. A user proves membership by submitting a Merkle proof; the contract verifies the proof in constant time without scanning the list.
Design checklist
  • Define off-chain data format and verification steps.
  • Keep on-chain verification atomic and minimal.
  • Provide monitoring to ensure off-chain processors submit timely updates.
