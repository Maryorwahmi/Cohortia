Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Quick Reference
Be explicit
Always declare visibility and mutability to express intent and enable tooling.
Least privilege
Restrict visibility to the narrowest level required to reduce attack surface.
Signal intent
Mark view/pure and use clear error messages in require/revert for readable and testable contracts.
Final thought
Test both allowed and forbidden call paths and prefer explicit, narrow declarations to make your contracts easier to audit and maintain.
