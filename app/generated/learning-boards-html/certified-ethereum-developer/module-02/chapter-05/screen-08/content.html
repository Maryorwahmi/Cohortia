Gas Efficiency

Logs are cheaper than storage

Because logs don't change contract storage, emitting events generally consumes less gas than writing state. Use them for off‑chain signals.

Audit trail: emit events for operations you want recorded for explorers or backends.
Design: avoid emitting excessively large payloads; include only necessary fields to reconstruct the event off‑chain.
Scaling: for high frequency operations, consider aggregated events or off‑chain batching where appropriate.
Misconception

Contracts cannot rely on logs as source of truth. Logs are for off‑chain consumption only and cannot be read during contract execution.

Why it matters

Choosing between logs and storage influences gas costs, UX, and what your contract can verify on‑chain.
