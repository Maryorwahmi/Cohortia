Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › CID Versions
CID v0 vs v1
CID versions differ in encoding and feature support. Pinning options often let you pick a version; be deliberate about it.
v0
Widespread support and legacy tools.
v1
Supports multibase and newer features for future-proofing.
Interoperability rule
If you work with multiple services, normalize CID versions at upload or support both when resolving content.
Checklist
Confirm cidVersion when pinning, test retrieval via your intended gateways, and document the chosen convention for your team.
