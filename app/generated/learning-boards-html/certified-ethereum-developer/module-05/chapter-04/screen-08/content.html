Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Input Validation
Checks to implement
  • ethers.isAddress(recipient) for address validity.
  • parseFloat(amount) > 0 and not NaN for amounts.
  • Convert with ethers.parseUnits(amount, decimals) before sending.
  • Verify sufficient token balance and sufficient ETH for gas.
  • Sanitize display strings to avoid XSS when showing user content.
Example error messages
Invalid recipient address. Check the address and try again.
Amount must be a positive number.
Insufficient ETH for gas. Fund your wallet or reduce gas settings.
Why it matters

Front-end validation prevents needless blockchain calls, saving users gas and time, and leads to clearer UX when something goes wrong.
