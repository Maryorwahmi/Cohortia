Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Common Mistakes
Ignoring loading

Show spinners or messages during async reads to avoid blank screens.

Generic errors

Parse library errors and give actionable guidance rather than "something went wrong."

No validation

Validate addresses and amounts before sending to save gas and time.

Stale UI

Re-fetch after confirmed transactions rather than relying on manual refresh.

XSS risks

Sanitize any user content before rendering to avoid cross-site scripting.

Gas estimation

Do not auto-resubmit with higher gas without consent; explain options to the user.

Pro tip

Log detailed errors for debugging but surface concise, actionable messages for users to keep the experience clear and safe.
