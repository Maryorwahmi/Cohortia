Explanation

Centralize connect logic and event listeners at the page level. Pass provider and signer down as props or through context. Use a numeric trigger to force child re-fetches after confirmation.

Concrete scenario

After a confirmed transfer, call handleTransferSuccess to increment refreshBalanceTrigger. TokenBalanceDisplay includes that trigger in its dependency array and refetches balance.

Key Takeaway

Keep connection and refresh logic in one place so components remain simple and reactive.
