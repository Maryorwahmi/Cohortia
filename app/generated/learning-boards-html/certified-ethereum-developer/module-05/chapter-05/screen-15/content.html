Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts
Transaction Lifecycle
Broadcast → pending → mined → confirmed. Use tx.wait() and check receipt.status.
Events & contract.on()
Events are logs that frontends can subscribe to for real-time updates. Use indexed params for filtering.
Signer & Gas
Use a signer-attached contract for transactions and estimate gas to avoid failed sends.
Final takeaway

Design UIs around confirmations and events to provide reliable, real-time feedback while handling errors and listener cleanup carefully.
