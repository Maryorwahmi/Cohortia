Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › EventLogger Lab
Activity steps
  1. Deploy EventLogger.sol to local Hardhat and copy its address and ABI.
  2. Instantiate contract with signer for transactions and provider for reads.
  3. Implement form to call setLastMessage and send transaction.
  4. Await tx.wait() and check receipt.status to confirm success.
  5. Subscribe to LogMessage with contract.on and prepend logs to state.
  6. Remove listeners in cleanup useEffect to avoid leaks.
Verification checklist
  • Sign a transaction and see 'Sending' then 'Confirmed' after waiting.
  • See the new message appear in the Recent Log Messages list without page refresh.
  • Test rejection flows and estimation errors and observe appropriate messages.
Reflection
