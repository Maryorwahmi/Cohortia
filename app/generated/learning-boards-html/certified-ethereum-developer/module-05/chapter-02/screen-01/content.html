Setup essentials for local DApp development

Follow a practical path from scaffolding your frontend to connecting a local Hardhat node, installing Ethers.js, and keeping secrets out of your client bundle.

In this lesson

Scaffold a Next.js or React app, install Ethers.js, start Hardhat locally, and connect the frontend with a safe fallback provider.

Why it matters

A reproducible environment prevents lost time, secret leakage, and brittle deployments when smart contracts evolve across networks.

Who benefits

Frontend engineers, smart contract devs, and full-stack teams building DApps that need rapid local testing and clear deployment pipelines.

Outcome

A working Next.js or React DApp connected to a local Hardhat node and a set of best practices for secrets and contract addresses.
