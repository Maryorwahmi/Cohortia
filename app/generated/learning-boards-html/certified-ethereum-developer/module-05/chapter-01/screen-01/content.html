Interacting with Smart Contracts from the Frontend
Understand ABI, addresses, providers, read vs write calls, and safe UX for DApp interactions.
In this lesson
Wire your frontend using Ethers.js. Call view functions, send transactions, and handle user wallet flows safely.
Why it matters
A correct ABI and address let your app encode calls and decode results. A provider signs and submits transactions. Get these right to avoid confusing errors.
Explore Key Topics
ABI & Address
Describe and locate a contract so your frontend encodes and targets calls properly.
Providers & Signers
MetaMask injects a provider and a signer. Remote nodes provide read-only access.
Read vs Write
View functions cost no gas; state-modifying functions require signed transactions and gas.

Contract Address

Provider
The gateway to an Ethereum node. Browser wallets inject a provider and provide a signer for transactions.
