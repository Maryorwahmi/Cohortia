Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Model Optimization
Definition
Model Optimization
The process of reducing polygon count and runtime complexity so models render efficiently in real-time engines while retaining visual fidelity via baking and LODs.
Technique
Retopology and Decimate
Detail
Bake normals and AO from high to low
Example
Turn a sculpted crate into a low-poly shell, bake wood grain into a normal map, and use that texture for visual detail instead of geometry.
Common mistake
Exporting render-ready high-poly meshes directly to the engine, causing major performance drops.
Process
From high-poly to game-ready
  1. Evaluate the model’s screen importance and set a triangle budget.
  2. Create a low-poly retopology or use Decimate to preview reduction.
  3. Bake normal and ambient occlusion maps from the high-poly to the low-poly mesh.
  4. Apply clean UVs, export maps, and assemble a material that uses the maps in-engine.
Takeaway
Move detail into textures where possible; keep geometry only for silhouette and necessary forms.
