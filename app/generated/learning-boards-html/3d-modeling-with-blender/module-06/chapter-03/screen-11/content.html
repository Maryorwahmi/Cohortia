Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Baking Maps
Baking steps
  1. Unwrap low-poly target with clean UVs and no overlapping islands.
  2. Place high-poly detail in the same space and set a ray distance or cage for projection.
  3. Choose Bake type (Normal, AO, Curvature) and run the bake to an image.
  4. Inspect for projection errors, adjust cage or UVs, and rebake if necessary.
Example
Use a sculpted crate as the high-poly and a simplified retopologized crate as the low-poly. Bake normals to preserve plank detail and small dents in the normal map for engine use.
Common issues
Incorrect cage, flipped normals, or overlapping UVs cause projection artifacts—inspect and fix before export.
Verification
Preview the baked maps in Blender by plugging them into Principled BSDF. If the result matches the high-poly look under lighting, export textures and assign them in the engine to confirm parity.
