Backup strategy
Keep separate files for high-poly masters and export-ready low-poly variants. Use clear filenames and incremental saves so destructive changes are reversible.
Test early
After each major step, export a small test (single prop) and import it into your target engine to verify scale, pivot, LODs, and material mapping. Fix issues while they’re small.
Quick checklist
  • Save high-poly master before destructive edits
  • Preview decimation without applying
  • Apply transforms and set origin
  • Bake maps, export textures, and test in-engine
Testing early prevents compounded errors later and keeps your pipeline efficient.
