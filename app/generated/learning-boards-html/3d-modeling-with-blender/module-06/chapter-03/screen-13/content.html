Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Export Troubleshooting
Symptom: Wrong scale
Fix: Check Scene Units, apply transforms, and verify export scale factor. Export a small test to confirm.
Symptom: Strange pivot
Fix: Set origin to the intended pivot (Origin to Geometry or Origin to 3D Cursor) and re-export.
Symptom: Materials missing
Fix: Bake procedural nodes to images, pack or export textures, and confirm Principled BSDF maps are assigned in the engine.
Symptom: Geometry distorted
Fix: Check for unapplied scale, parent transforms, and flipped normals; recalculate normals if required.
If multiple issues appear, isolate and test one fix at a time rather than re-exporting everything at once.
