Practical skills

What to practice in Blender before export

Scene Clean-up
Remove lights, cameras, and unused meshes, or use Selected Objects at export to limit output. This prevents accidental assets from entering your build.
Visual: filter icon + list
Apply Transforms
Use Ctrl A to apply location, rotation, and scale so the exporter writes corrected world-space values rather than Blender's unset transforms.
Visual: transform axes
Materials & Textures
Prefer Principled BSDF and ensure image node paths are valid. Pack or embed images when your target expects single-file assets.
Visual: image pack
Apply Modifiers
Make modifiers like Mirror or Subdivision permanent if the engine should see the altered geometry; otherwise, export raw meshes.
Visual: wrench icon
