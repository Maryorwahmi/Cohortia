Process Flow

Match engine axes before export

Map Blender's axes to the engine to avoid rotated or flipped imports.

Unity example: Forward = -Z Forward, Up = Y Up.
Unreal example: Maintain Z Up if the engine expects Z-up.
Test by exporting a simple cube or your asset and importing to verify standing orientation and pivot.
Common mistake

Rotating the mesh in Blender without setting export axes often still results in wrong orientation in the engine because the export remaps axes unless configured.

Quick checks
  • Confirm Blender unit scale and export scale factor.
  • Use a test import to verify orientation before batch exporting many assets.
  • Adjust Forward/Up settings in the exporter rather than re-rotating models where possible.
Example scenario
If a model lies flat after import, revise Forward/Up mapping and re-export instead of manually re-rotating in the engine.
