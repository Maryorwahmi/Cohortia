Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Deformation & bone roll
Apply transforms

Before export, apply scale and rotation to armature and meshes so the engine receives consistent values and avoids scale-based deformation.

Bone roll consistency

Inspect bone roll and correct flipped axes. Inconsistent bone roll leads to rotation and skinning problems after import.

Misconception

Do not assume the engine will correct bone rolls or transforms for you; it will faithfully use the data you exported.

Quick fixes checklist
  • Apply All Transforms on objects and armature.
  • Set FBX Export > Armature > Only Deform Bones: checked.
  • Uncheck Add Leaf Bones unless engine requires them.
  • Ensure Forward/Up axes match the engine's convention (-Z Forward, Y Up for many engines).
  • Confirm vertex groups and modifiers are applied or included.
Mini scenario

You export a character and arms twist incorrectly. You apply transforms, re-export with Only Deform Bones, and the twist disappears. This shows transforms plus unwanted helper bones were the cause.

Key Takeaway

Treat bone orientation, applied transforms, and export armature filters as the first remedies for deformation problems.
