Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › What to Remember
Key Concepts
Practical reminders for efficient animation work.
Parenting
Select child then parent; test transforms to confirm behavior.
Armature Parenting
Parent meshes to bones for articulated control; not a substitute for skinning when deformation is needed.
Modifiers
Noise for jitter, Limits to clamp values; layer them after keyframes for subtlety.
Final takeaway
Practice small exercises and ask: did I change timing, value, or interpolation? That question guides precise, efficient edits.
