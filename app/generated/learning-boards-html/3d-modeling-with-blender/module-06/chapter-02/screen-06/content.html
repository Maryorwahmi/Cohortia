Process Flow
Choosing and applying handle types
Steps to control interpolation:
  1. Select the key control point(s) you want to edit.
  2. Press V to open handle type choices and pick Vector, Free, Aligned, or Automatic.
  3. Manually drag handles when using Free or Aligned for finer shaping.
  4. Play the animation and iterate until the motion matches your intent.
Example
Use Vector at the moment of impact to create a hard snap, while keeping Aligned for smooth arcs between peaks.
Vector
Sharp corners for immediate changes. Best for mechanical or impact motion.
Free
Independent handles for asymmetric transitions. Use when approach and departure need different shapes.
Aligned
Balanced smoothness with opposite handles aligned. Good for symmetric easing.
Automatic / Bezier
Computed smooth handles for organic motion. Often a useful starting point.
Common misconception
Automatic solves everything. In practice you will still tweak handles for precise timing and spacing, especially at extremes.
Pro tip

Use Vector for impacts and extend horizontal handle length at peaks to create hang time for arcs.
