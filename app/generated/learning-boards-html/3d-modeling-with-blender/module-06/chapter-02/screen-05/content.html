Definition
F-curves and motion interpretation
Slope, peaks, and flats reveal velocity and behavior between keyframes.
Key bullets:
  • Steep slope = rapid value change = fast motion.
  • Flat curve = pause or hold in value.
  • Sharp corner = sudden change; smooth handles = organic motion.
Example scenario
A bouncing ball needs a sharp curve at impact and a smooth arc at the apex. Use a Vector handle for the impact key and Bezier for the arc.
Annotated channel cards
Z-Location
Shows jump apex and landing; extend handles at the top for hang time.
Rot X
Rotation channels often need separate smoothing to avoid gimbal-like snaps.
Scale
Flat segments are common for scale; sudden spikes look unnatural unless intentional.
Common mistake
Adjusting value without checking channel axes can introduce unintended motion in other axes.
Check this
Lock channels or hide them to focus edits on the property you intend to change.
