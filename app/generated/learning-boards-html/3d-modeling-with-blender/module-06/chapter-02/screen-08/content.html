Core Concept
Parenting and Hierarchies
Use parent-child relationships to organize transforms and simplify animation work.
Why it helps:
  • Reduces duplicate animation by passing transforms from parent to children.
  • Makes complex motion predictable by structuring dependencies.
  • Enables mixed workflows: global motion on parent, local motion on children.
Example
Parent a wheel to the car body, then animate the car position for travel and the wheel rotation for spin.
Hierarchy example
Torso
Root parent for upper body movement.
Upper arm
Child of torso. Rotating it carries forearm and hand.
Forearm
Child of upper arm; allows independent elbow animation.
Workflow
Select child → Shift+Select parent → Ctrl+P and choose Keep Transform to preserve existing positions.
Common mistake
Parenting in reverse order or without Keep Transform can cause jumps or unexpected offsets.
