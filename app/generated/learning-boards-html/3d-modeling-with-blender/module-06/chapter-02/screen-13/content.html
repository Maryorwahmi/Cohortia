Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Assessment: Arm Swing
Diagnose and Fix a Stiff Arm Swing
A stepwise assessment to apply Graph Editor and parenting knowledge.
Inspect F-curves
Open rotation channels and look for linear segments or sharp corners that make motion stiff.
Adjust handles
Switch to Bezier and extend horizontal handles at peaks to add easing.
Check hierarchy
Confirm correct parent-child order for arm parts and reparent if needed with Keep Transform.
Avoid adding keys
Prefer handle edits to adding keys, which can clutter the Timeline and obscure intent.
Optional refinement
Add a tiny Noise modifier to the hand for a natural micro-movement if needed.
Key takeaway
Systematic fixes are faster and cleaner than piling on keyframes.
