Common Mistakes

Troubleshoot typical errors

No key set — snapping occurs
Confirm the playhead and insert keys to lock poses in place.
Auto Key clutter
Toggle Auto Keying off during detailed edits and clean unwanted keys in the Dope Sheet.
Robotic motion from Linear
Use Bezier for more natural acceleration and deceleration.
Fix routine
  1. Scrub the Timeline to find the unexpected motion.
  2. Open the Dope Sheet to inspect keys across objects.
  3. Move, delete, or replace keys to restore intended timing.
  4. Play back and repeat until the motion reads correctly.
Key Takeaway

Use the Dope Sheet to find and fix accidental keys; iteration is normal when polishing motion.

Pro tip

Make a habit of playing back after every bulk edit to catch issues early.
