Keyframes: the motion building blocks

Define a few crucial poses, then let Blender calculate the frames between them. This transforms your static models into motion.

In this lesson
Concept
Keyframe & Interpolation
Tool
Timeline & Dope Sheet
Why it matters
Motion conveys intent, weight, and timing. Good timing makes simple actions feel real.
Who uses it
Animators, motion designers, and 3D artists refining poses and timing.
Result
A sequence of key poses that reads as continuous action.
Starter activity

Create a bouncing ball: set successive Z-location keyframes at frames 1, 20, 40, 60 and observe how interpolation shapes the bounce.

Common mistake
Forgetting to toggle Auto Keying or misplacing the playhead can create unwanted keys.
Visual Concept
Timeline strip (conceptual)
Frame 1 ◊ keyframe (start pose) Frame 20 ◊ keyframe (contact with ground) Frame 40 ◊ keyframe (second bounce) Frame 60 ◊ keyframe (settle)
Cards show roles: pose, timing, interpolation. Think of keyframes as the punctuation of motion.

These topics form the practical steps you'll use to block, refine, and polish simple animations.
