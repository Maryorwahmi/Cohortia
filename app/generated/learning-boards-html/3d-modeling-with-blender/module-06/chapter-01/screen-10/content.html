Step-by-step activity

1. Start a new file. Add UV Sphere, rename to 'BouncingBall'. 2. Frame 1: move Z to 2 → press I → Location. 3. Frame 20: move Z to 0 → press I → Location. 4. Frame 40: move Z to 1.5 → press I → Location. 5. Frame 60: move Z to 0 → press I → Location. 6. Frame 80: move Z to 0.75 → press I → Location. 7. Play and observe.
Refinement steps
  • Open Dope Sheet, select Z-location keys, press T to change interpolation.
  • Use S to scale timing if bounces feel too slow or too fast.
  • Delete accidental keys from Auto Keying and re-block as needed.
Key Takeaway

Block first, then refine. Use the Dope Sheet to adjust rhythm after key poses are in place.
