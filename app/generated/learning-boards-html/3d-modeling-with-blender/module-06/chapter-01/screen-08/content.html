Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Auto Keying: Speed vs Risk
Definition

Auto Keying explained

When enabled, transforms you make on frames without keys automatically create new keyframes for the active channels.

Use case: Quickly block poses for a walk cycle by moving through frames and adjusting limbs with Auto Keying on.
Risk
Small unintended adjustments will be recorded, creating noisy curves and extra keys to clean up later.
Practical guardrails
  • Enable Auto Keying for rough blocking, then disable when refining.
  • Check the playhead before large edits to avoid creating keys at the wrong frame.
  • Use Dope Sheet to quickly delete accidental keys created by Auto Keying.
Key Takeaway

Auto Keying speeds blocking but requires discipline; toggle it off for cleaning and refinement.

Pro tip

Use Auto Keying with channel filters or only during dedicated blocking passes to reduce noise.
