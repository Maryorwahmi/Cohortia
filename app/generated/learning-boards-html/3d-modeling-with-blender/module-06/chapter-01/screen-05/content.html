Process Flow

Timeline workflow

Use the Timeline to position the playhead, insert or update keys, and play back your scene to evaluate timing.

Step 1 — Scrub and plan
Drag the playhead to the frame where a key pose should sit. Accurate placement prevents accidental overwrites.
Step 2 — Insert or update
Press the insert-key action and select which properties to record, or rely on Auto Keying when blocking.
Tip
If you're updating a pose, ensure the playhead is exactly on the keyframe to replace the values instead of creating a duplicate.
Visual guide
Timeline concept strip: [Frame 1] ◊ yellow diamond (key) [Frame 20] ◊ yellow diamond (key) Playhead → vertical red line indicates current frame
Example scenario
Set initial pose at frame 1, contact at frame 20, tweak spacing by dragging the keys along the timeline to change rhythm.
Key Takeaway

The Timeline is where you decide when motions happen; accurate playhead placement prevents accidental keys.

Common mistake

Leaving Auto Keying on during detailed edits creates many unwanted keyframes.
