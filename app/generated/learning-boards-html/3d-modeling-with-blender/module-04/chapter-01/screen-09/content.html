Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Rough Stone
Quick recipe
  • • Base Color: brown-grey (Hex: #665544)
  • • Metallic: 0
  • • Roughness: ~0.8; blend a noise texture into Roughness for variety
  • • Add Normal map for bumps
Example & check
Stone feels convincing when reflections are muted and surface detail is visible through normals. To simulate wetness, lower Roughness locally and increase specular response on those patches.
Key Takeaway

High roughness plus normal detail produces believable coarse materials; use localized roughness changes for wet or polished areas.
