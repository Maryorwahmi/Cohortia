Validation steps
  1. Check color profiles on textures and set normal maps to non-color.
  2. Confirm Metallic is 0 or 1 — avoid mid-values for realism.
  3. Preview under multiple lighting setups to test Roughness behavior.
  4. Bake maps where procedural nodes can be converted to textures.
  5. Reassign baked maps in a fresh material and compare results for parity.
This verification reveals issues early and keeps your assets predictable when imported into game engines.
