Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Common Mistakes
Frequent errors
  • • Metallic between 0 and 1 for non-metals.
  • • Wrong color space on normal or roughness maps.
  • • Many per-object materials instead of texture-driven variation.
  • • Overly complex node trees for realtime assets.
How to fix
• Use Metallic as a binary flag.
• Set normal map images to non-color.
• Pack grayscale maps into channels to reduce texture counts.
• Group and reuse node setups.
