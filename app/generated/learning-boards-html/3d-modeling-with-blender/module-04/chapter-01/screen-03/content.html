Practice steps and why PBR matters
Step
Open Shader Editor and create a new material on a UV sphere. This lets you see changes in real time.
Adjust
Tweak Base Color, Metallic, Roughness to match real materials. Roughness controls gloss vs matte.
Verify
Use Material Preview to check behavior under different lights and confirm PBR consistency for game export.
