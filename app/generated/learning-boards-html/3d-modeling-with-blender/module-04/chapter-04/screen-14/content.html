Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Texel Density
Pixels per unit of surface area. Match across islands for uniform sharpness.
Pack Islands
Arrange islands inside 0-1 to maximize useful texture area without overlaps.
Straighten UVs
Align edges and vertices for cleaner tiling and easier painting.
Takeaway

A disciplined sequence — normalize density, straighten where appropriate, pack deliberately, create and save images, then paint — produces consistent, high-quality textures.
