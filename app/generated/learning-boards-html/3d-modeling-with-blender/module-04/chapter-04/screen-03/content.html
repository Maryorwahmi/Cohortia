Practice & Safety Objectives

Work habits that protect your painted images and ensure consistent visual quality.

Save your image

After creating or painting an image, use Image > Save As and give it a meaningful filename tied to your project folder.

Check paint slots

When multiple Image Texture nodes exist, set the correct active paint slot in the Tools panel so your strokes affect the intended texture.

Avoid overlaps

Ensure unique UVs for unique details. Overlapping islands mirror paint and can ruin unique markings.

Verify texel density

Use Average Island Scale or manual scaling to match pixels per unit across parts of your model before packing.
