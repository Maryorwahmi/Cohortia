Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Texel Density
Definition

What texel density means

Texel density is the count of texture pixels mapped per unit of surface area. It defines visual resolution across the model.

Concrete example
If a plank receives 1024 pixels across 1 meter and another plank receives 512, the first will appear twice as sharp.
When to vary density
Give more density to focal areas like faces or player-visible surfaces and reduce density on distant or hidden parts.
Practical steps
  1. Decide on a project target (e.g., px per meter or px per cm).
  2. Use Average Island Scale to equalize island sizes by surface area.
  3. Manually scale visible islands up and less visible ones down before packing.
Key Takeaway
Consistent texel density creates uniform sharpness; prioritize visible surfaces when allocating pixels.
