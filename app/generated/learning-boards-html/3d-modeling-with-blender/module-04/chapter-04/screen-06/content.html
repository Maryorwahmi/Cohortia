Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Pack Islands
Workflow

Pack islands without breaking texel density

  1. Use Average Island Scale to normalize island sizes relative to surface area.
  2. Run UV → Pack Islands to get an automatic arrangement.
  3. Manually rotate and move islands to fill gaps and prioritize visible areas.
  4. Scale high-importance islands slightly before re-packing if needed, but keep overall density consistent.
Example
For a set of props, pack related planks together in one region of the atlas so they share similar shading and can be tiled consistently.
Common mistake
Scaling islands indiscriminately after packing creates inconsistent texel density and uneven visual sharpness.
Visual tips
  • Prioritize faces seen up close; give them more UV area.
  • Group similar materials or tiled areas to reduce wasted space.
  • Save iterations so you can compare pack outcomes and revert if necessary.
Key Takeaway
Combine average scaling with manual packing to balance density and visual importance across your model.
