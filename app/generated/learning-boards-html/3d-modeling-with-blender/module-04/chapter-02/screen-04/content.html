Texture vs Material
Define each and see an everyday analogy
Definition

Material: how light interacts with a surface. Texture: the image that supplies color, pattern, and transparency to that material.

Analogy

Material is the clothing fabric type; texture is the printed pattern on that fabric. Change the print and the look changes; change the fabric and the physical response changes.

Example: wooden crate
Material

Principled BSDF configured with diffuse color, roughness and specular settings appropriate for wood.

Texture

Image texture with wood grain providing color variation and knots; optional alpha for decals.

Key Takeaway

Use textures to add surface detail and materials to control how that detail reacts to light.
