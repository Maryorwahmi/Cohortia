Why packing matters
Efficient packing reduces memory waste and keeps important areas high‑res in-game.
Proportional islands
Scale islands so each visible surface receives pixel budget according to its importance.
Avoid overlap
Only mirror where duplicate texturing is acceptable; otherwise ensure unique placement.
Lightmap considerations
Lightmap Pack targets baking but may introduce distortion that hurts painting workflows.
Final check
Test with a grid and in-engine previews to confirm appearance and texel balance.
Practice tip
Start packing the largest, highest‑priority islands first, then fill gaps with smaller pieces. Keep consistent padding between islands to avoid bleeding.
