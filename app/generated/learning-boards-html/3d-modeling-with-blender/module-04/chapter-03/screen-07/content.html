Step-by-step seam marking
Follow a few deliberate steps to produce clean, paintable islands.
  1. Enter Edit Mode and switch to Edge Select.
  2. Select edges along natural breaks or hidden areas.
  3. Press Ctrl + E > Mark Seam; edges turn red.
  4. Select all faces (A) and press U > Unwrap to flatten using those seams.
Analogy & example
Like cutting a cardboard box: choose cuts that hide joins and allow the surface to lie flat. For a cube: mark top and bottom loops and a vertical back seam to create six rectangular islands.
Key Takeaway
Seams are intentional cuts. Place them where paintability and minimal visible joins are balanced.
