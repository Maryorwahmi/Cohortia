Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Manipulating UV islands
Editor controls and workflow
Move, rotate, scale and pack islands to make the most of the 0‑1 texture area.
Move: G
Rotate: R
Scale: S
Select all: A
Pack: use manual arrangement to maximize space
Check stretch: View > Display Stretch
Remember to keep texel density consistent: larger visible surfaces deserve proportionally larger UV area.
Example: crate
After unwrapping six face islands, scale each rectangle so they fill the 0‑1 space evenly, rotate to align a grid for testing, and pack without overlap.
Pro tip
Use a temporary grid texture to visually confirm texel density and alignment before painting final textures.
