Why clean UVs matter for baking
Baked maps sample texture space via UVs; messy UVs produce incorrect bakes and visible seams.
• Non‑overlapping islands ensure unique texel sampling for each surface area.
• Consistent texel density prevents mismatched resolution across bakes.
• Seams should align with acceptable discontinuities to hide bake seams in final art.
Checklist before baking
  • Apply transforms and unwrap with no overlaps.
  • Confirm islands are inside 0‑1 space and padded to avoid bleeding.
  • Run a test bake and inspect seams and AO results.
