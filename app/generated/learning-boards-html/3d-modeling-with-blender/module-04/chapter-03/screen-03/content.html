Success criteria for hands-on labs
Concrete checks you will use to confirm a clean unwrap on the crate and the L‑shaped pipe.
  • Apply scale and rotation in Object Mode before entering Edit Mode.
  • Mark seams to produce contiguous UV islands for paintable regions.
  • Pack islands in the 0‑1 square and maximize usable area without overlap.
  • Use the stretch overlay to identify distortion and refine seams.
Checkpoint
Crate unwrap
Expect six rectangular islands and a grid texture aligned to faces without visible stretch.
