Understanding UV Unwrapping
Flatten a 3D mesh into a 2D map to control textures, reduce distortion, and prepare assets for engines.
Why it matters
Pixel‑perfect paint, baking normal maps, and packing into texture atlases for performance.
What you'll do
Use Smart UV Project, projections, and Mark Seam to create efficient UV islands and fix stretching.
Common pitfalls
Overlapping islands, unapplied scale, or too many seams that hinder painting.
Outcome
Produce clean UVs that map textures without visible distortion and fit the 0‑1 UV space efficiently.
Visual concept
Cards show the problem, technique, and result: an irregular mesh turned into UV islands inside the 0‑1 square ready for a texture atlas.
