Quick workflow
A compact routine to ensure consistent, game‑ready UVs.
  1. Apply transforms in Object Mode.
  2. Choose unwrap method; mark seams for manual control.
  3. Unwrap and use Display Stretch to locate distortion.
  4. Pack islands into 0‑1, test with a grid, verify texel density.
Pro tip
When in doubt, make larger islands for visible areas and accept mirrored smaller parts to save atlas space.
