Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Automatic Unwraps
Method comparison
Smart UV Project
Fast automatic unwrap that minimizes overlap but can create many small islands that complicate manual painting.
Cube/Cylinder/Sphere Projection
Project from a geometric primitive; clean for matching shapes but needs seams for caps and poles.
Follow Active Quads / Lightmap Pack
Useful for quad grids and lightmap baking; each has tradeoffs in distortion and packing efficiency.
Scenario
You have a barrel. Cylinder Projection handles the sides but top and bottom caps will stretch if projected with the cylinder. Mark seams around the top and bottom and unwrap to separate cap islands for clean results.
Misconception

Automatic equals perfect. In reality, automatic unwrapping trades human intent for speed and often requires manual cleanup for painting and baking.
