Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
UV Island
A contiguous flattened piece of the mesh in UV space.
Seam
A marked edge that defines where the mesh will be cut during unwrapping.
Smart UV Project
An automatic unwrap that quickly creates non‑overlapping islands at the cost of artist control.
Final takeaway
Practice seams and packing on simple objects until placing seams and verifying stretch become second nature.
