Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Extrude — How geometry is created
Definition

Extrude explained

Extrude generates new faces or edges from selected geometry and connects them to the original perimeter so the mesh gains depth.

Face extrude
Creates a new offset face and side faces that add thickness.
Edge/vertex extrude
Edges form new faces; vertices form new edges — extrusion adapts to the selection type.
Key Takeaway

Extrude creates new topology; movement is separate from geometry creation. Verify no zero-length extrusions remain.

Process
Extrude a face — hands-on example
Step 1 — Select and press Extrude
Select a face and press the extrude key. This creates new geometry immediately.
Step 2 — Move or constrain
Move the extrusion or press X, Y, or Z to constrain. Use numeric input for precise depth.
Step 3 — Verify mesh
If you cancel without moving, you'll leave overlapping geometry. Use Merge by Distance to clean duplicates.
