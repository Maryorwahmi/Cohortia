Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Bevel — Softening Edges
Tool

Bevel edges and vertices (Ctrl+B / Ctrl+Shift+B)

Bevel adds a chamfer or rounded profile by creating new faces along edges or at vertices. Use segments to smooth the curve.

Edges vs Vertices
Ctrl+B bevels edges; Ctrl+Shift+B bevels vertices where multiple edges meet.
Segments
Use the scroll wheel while beveling to increase segments for a rounder profile; more segments equals smoother appearance at higher cost.
Practical guidance
Bevel silhouette edges and areas that will be seen up close. Leave flat, hidden, or interior edges un-beveled to save geometry.
Misconception
More segments always look better. In reality, segment choice depends on camera distance and performance constraints.
Pro tip
Combining a modest bevel with smooth shading often beats a heavy, high-segment bevel for perceived smoothness with fewer polygons.
