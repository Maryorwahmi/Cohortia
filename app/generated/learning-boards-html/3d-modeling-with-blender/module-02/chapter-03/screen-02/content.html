Learning Objectives

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Verified Competency Milestone
01
Master Extrude
Pull faces, edges, and vertices into new geometry. Learn axis constraints and numeric control for precision.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use Extrude Variations
Choose Extrude Region, Extrude Manifold, or Extrude Along Normals to preserve manifold geometry or add thickness to curved areas.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Inset Faces for Panels
Inset creates inner faces for recessed detail. Learn region vs individual inset behavior and chaining with extrude.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply Bevels Cleanly
Bevel edges and vertices to soften highlights. Control segments for smoothness and performance tradeoffs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
