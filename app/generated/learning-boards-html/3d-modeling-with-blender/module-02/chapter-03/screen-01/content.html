Extrude, Inset, and Bevel for Detail

Master geometry creation and edge control to make models that read well, shade smoothly, and export cleanly to games.

In this lesson
Tools: Extrude (E), Inset (I), Bevel (Ctrl+B)
Common fixes: Merge by Distance, Smooth Shading
Extrude

Pull faces or edges to create new geometry and add depth.

Inset

Create inner faces for panels, windows, or recessed detail.

Bevel

Chamfer or round edges to catch highlights and look realistic.

Explore Key Topics
These three skills let you generate controlled geometry and surface detail that remains efficient and clean for games.

Verify
