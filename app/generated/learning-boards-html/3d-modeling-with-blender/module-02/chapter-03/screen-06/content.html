Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Zero-length Extrusion & Non-manifold
Problem defined

Canceled extrude creates duplicates

What happens
New vertices and edges are created at the same location as originals, causing overlapping geometry and non-manifold results.
Why it matters
Non-manifold meshes fail to export cleanly and produce shading and baking artifacts in renders and game engines.
Fix
Use Merge by Distance to collapse duplicates and delete internal faces. Inspect normals and run a quick topology check.
Pro tip
Move an extrusion by a tiny amount before confirming to avoid zero-length geometry, or immediately merge duplicates if you must cancel.
Check Your Understanding

After an extrusion, what quick check will reveal overlapping geometry? Visualize normals and run Merge by Distance, then inspect shading under smooth shading.
