Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Median vs Active
Median Point (Example)
Select several vertices and rotate — the group rotates around their average center.
Use case
Centering a cluster of faces or rotating a selection symmetrically.
Active Element (Example)
Make one vertex or face active last, then rotate; the whole selection uses that active as the pivot.
Use case
Aligning multiple pieces to a chosen anchor or snapping others to one element.
Check your understanding
If a selection rotates around a surprising point, was the desired anchor selected last? If not, switch to Active Element and reselect in the correct order.
