Together they let you produce clean, deformable geometry for animation, precise in-game assets, and natural-looking organic shapes.

Use Move, Rotate, Scale in Edit Mode
Apply transforms to selected vertices, edges, or faces for precise shape control. This is the basis of all mesh editing.
