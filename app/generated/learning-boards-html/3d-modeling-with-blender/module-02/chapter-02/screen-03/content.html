Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Learning Objectives
Objective 05
Identify and avoid common pitfalls such as unintended selections, wrong pivot points, or forgetting to toggle proportional editing.
Mnemonic
Pivot → Orientation → Proportional — quick checklist before every transform.
Quick fix
If a transform behaves unexpectedly, check pivot and orientation first; toggle proportional editing second.
How you'll practice
Hands-on tasks include subdividing a plane, creating a hill with proportional editing, and demonstrating pivot point effects on grouped staves or faces.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Complete the hill sculpt and a pivot-point exercise to show you can choose orientation and pivot to produce predictable edits.
