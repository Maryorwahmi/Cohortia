Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Pivot Points
Median Point
Average center of the selection; predictable for grouped edits.
Active Element
Last selected component becomes the pivot. Great for aligning other elements to one reference.
Individual Origins
Each selection rotates or scales around its own center. Use for repeated elements or arrays.
3D Cursor
Place the 3D Cursor and use it as a custom pivot for precise control.
Bounding Box Center
Center of the smallest axis-aligned box that encloses your selection; similar to Median but computed differently.
Pro tip

If your rotation looks wrong, check the pivot. Often switching between Median and Active Element resolves unexpected motion.
