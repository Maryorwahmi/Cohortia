Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Avoiding Mistakes
Check 1
Pivot point
Verify the chosen pivot if rotations or scales do not behave as expected.
Check 2
Orientation
Confirm Global vs Local aligns with your intent for axis constraints.
Check 3
Proportional Editing
Ensure it is off when you need isolated edits, and on when you need organic influence.
Practical habit
Make a quick mental checklist before transforming: pivot, orientation, proportional. This avoids many common mistakes and makes edits reproducible.
