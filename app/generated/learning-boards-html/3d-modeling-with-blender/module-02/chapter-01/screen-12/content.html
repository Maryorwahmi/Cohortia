Why it matters

Selection impacts topology and downstream work

Good selection keeps edge flow consistent, eases UV unwrapping, and ensures predictable deformation during animation. Poor selection leads to messy topology that’s hard to fix later.

  • Keep loops continuous for joints.
  • Avoid random vertex edits that introduce n-gons.
  • Use ring/loop tools to propagate clean changes.
Concrete scenario
If you select stray vertices around a shoulder and move them individually, you may break the loop that drives the shoulder deformation. Instead, select the whole loop and adjust it to maintain even topology.
Pro tip

When in doubt, select loops or rings to make topology-preserving edits rather than isolated vertex moves.

Key Takeaway

Selection is the first act of topology control — choose it to protect deformation and texturing workflows.
