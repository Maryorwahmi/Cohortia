Introduction to Mesh Editing & Selection Modes

Understand the building blocks of a mesh and learn the selection workflows that let you target and edit geometry precisely.

In this lesson

Switch to Edit Mode, identify vertices, edges, faces, and use selection tools that speed up modeling while keeping topology clean.

Why it matters

Accurate selection lets you modify only what you intend, reducing destructive edits and improving deformation, UVs, and performance.

Explore Key Topics
Mesh components
Vertices, edges and faces define structure and visible surfaces.
Selection modes
Vertex, Edge, Face modes map to different edit intents and tools.
Selection tools
Box, Circle, Lasso and Linked Select speed up targeted edits.

Quick tip
Remember: press Tab to toggle into Edit Mode, then use 1, 2, 3 to switch selection modes. Confirm your mode before selecting.
