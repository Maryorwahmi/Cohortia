Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Smooth & Grab
Smooth brush

Blend uneven surfaces and soften harsh edges. Activated by holding Shift with any brush. Use often to keep surfaces believable.

When to smooth: after building forms, to remove lumps and to prepare for finer detail.
Tip: alternate smoothing and building passes to retain form while removing noise.
Grab brush
Reposition larger masses without re-sculpting detail. Useful for silhouette and proportion edits before committing to fine shapes.
Concrete scenario
If a head reads too narrow, use Grab to widen the cheek area, then use Draw and Clay Strips to add volume, finishing with Smooth to blend edges.
Common mistake
Over-smoothing erases form. Smooth at moderate strength and keep an eye on major planes and silhouette.
