Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Progressive Refinement
Step 1 — Primary
Large brushes, coarse Dyntopo, build silhouette and mass. Aim for correct proportion and overall readability.
Step 2 — Secondary
Smaller brushes and reduced Detail Size to carve planes, muscles, and major surface breaks.
Step 3 — Tertiary
Fine brushes for pores, small cracks, and texture. Only add this after structure is solid.
Example: sculpting a rock formation
Primary: large, irregular silhouette. Secondary: major cracks and protrusions with Clay Strips. Tertiary: small chips and grain with small Detail Size. Keep smoothing between stages to merge strokes naturally.
Avoid: tertiary detail too early.
Do: test silhouette at distance frequently.
Key Takeaway

Stage your work: blocking, structure, then detail. This keeps your sculpt readable and manageable.
