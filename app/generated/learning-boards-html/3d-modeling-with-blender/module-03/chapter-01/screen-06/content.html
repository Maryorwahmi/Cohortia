Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Draw & Clay Strips
Draw brush

Adds volume. Use large size and coarse Dyntopo for blocking. Hold Control to invert and carve inward.

When to use: primary silhouette and large forms.
Tip: multiple low-strength passes give more control than a single high-strength stroke.
Clay Strips brush
Builds forms in overlapping strips. Great for muscle groups and rock layering. Lower strength and repeat passes for natural results.
Practical example
Block a torso with Draw. Then use Clay Strips to add rib cage and shoulder volume, smoothing edges between strips to blend planes.
Common mistake
Overusing strength in Clay Strips produces chunky, unreal forms. Build up slowly and smooth between passes.
