Essential modifiers
  • Control: inverts the active brush to subtract or carve.
  • Shift: temporarily activates Smooth to blend strokes.
Strength: lower values combined with multiple passes grant control.
Radius: match size to current stage of refinement.
Key Takeaway

Modifiers let you temporarily change tool behavior without switching brushes; mastering them speeds iteration.

Practical example
Use Draw at 30% strength with several passes to build a ridge. Hold Control to carve a groove, then hold Shift to blend the groove edges.
Common mistake
Using full Strength for big strokes often requires undoing or heavy smoothing; prefer lower strength for fine control.
Quick checklist
Memorize Control and Shift behaviors. Set default Strength and Radius for each sculpting stage and adjust as you refine.
