Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Common Pitfalls
Pitfall: Over-detail early
Symptom: huge polycount before structure is set. Fix: increase Dyntopo Detail Size and re-block primary forms.
Pitfall: Neglecting Smooth
Symptom: lumpy surfaces. Fix: hold Shift often and smooth between passes.
Pitfall: Ignoring silhouette
Symptom: detailed but unreadable shapes. Fix: test at distance and adjust silhouette with Grab.
Pitfall: Performance drop
Symptom: slow viewport. Fix: raise Detail Size, simplify dense regions, or bake detail to textures later.
Pitfall: Assuming production topology
Symptom: messy topology not suited for animation. Fix: retopologize when deformation or optimization is required.
Pro tip

Make smoothing and silhouette checks part of your routine. A disciplined loop prevents most beginner mistakes.
