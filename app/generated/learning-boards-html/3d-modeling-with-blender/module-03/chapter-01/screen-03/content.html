Practice goals and common pitfalls

01 — Avoid early over-detailing

Start with a larger Dyntopo detail size so primary forms are fast to block. Reduce detail only when secondary and tertiary forms are needed.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Monitor mesh density

Use Blender’s status bar to watch polycount and judge performance; keep game-target budgets in mind as you add detail.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Use Smooth frequently

Neglecting smoothing leads to lumpy surfaces. Use Smooth (hold Shift) often to blend transitions and maintain believable forms.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Practice the rock sculpt

Follow a small project: add a sphere, enable Dyntopo, block shapes with Draw or Clay Strips, smooth, and use Grab for silhouette work.
