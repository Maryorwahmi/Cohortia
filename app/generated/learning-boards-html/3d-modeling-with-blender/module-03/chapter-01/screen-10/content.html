Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Rock Sculpt Activity
Step-by-step
1. Switch to Sculpting workspace
2. Delete default cube; add UV Sphere (Shift > A > Mesh > UV Sphere)
3. Enter Sculpt Mode; enable Dyntopo in the viewport header
4. Set Detail Size to ~10–15 for blocking
5. Use Draw or Clay Strips to shape the mound
6. Hold Shift to Smooth often
7. Use Grab to pull larger protrusions
8. Reduce Detail Size for small cracks and surface grain
9. Inspect silhouette and adjust as needed
Practical tips
  • Work large to small and avoid evenly applying microscopic detail.
  • Smooth between passes to bond strokes into coherent planes.
  • Use Grab early to test silhouette variants quickly.
Example result
A non-symmetrical rock with readable silhouette, coarse primary forms, then selective fine cracks and chips concentrated near edges.
Key Takeaway

Follow the activity steps to internalize brush use, Dyntopo control, and silhouette-first thinking.
