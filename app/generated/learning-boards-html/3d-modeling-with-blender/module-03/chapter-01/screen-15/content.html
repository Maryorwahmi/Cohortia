Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › What to Remember
Work stage
Block → refine → detail. Ask which stage you’re in before altering the mesh.
Control Dyntopo
Use larger detail for blocking, smaller for fine work; monitor polycount regularly.
Choose brushes
Draw/Clay Strips to build, Smooth to blend, Grab to change silhouette.
Final thought: keep sculpting iterative and intentional. Each stroke should serve a stage—shape, structure, or surface.
