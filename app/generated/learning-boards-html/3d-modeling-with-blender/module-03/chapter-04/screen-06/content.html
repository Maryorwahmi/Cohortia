Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Bezier vs NURBS
Side-by-side comparison
Bezier
  • Artist-friendly handles
  • Intuitive local edits
  • Best for organic shapes
NURBS
  • Weights and mathematical precision
  • Preferred for engineering/architecture
  • Better for analytic continuity
Concrete example
Use Bezier for a decorative rope and NURBS for a precise curved panel on a mechanical part that must meet engineering specs.
Misconception
NURBS are not always better; they are specialized for precision and can be slower to sculpt for organic details.
Key takeaway
Match the curve type to the task: speed and feel for Bezier, numeric control for NURBS.
