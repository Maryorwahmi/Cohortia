Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Curves
Mathematical objects (Bezier, NURBS) defined by control points for precise, flowing shapes.
Extrusion & Bevel
Give 2D curves 3D volume with Extrude, Bevel Depth, or a Bevel Object for custom cross-sections.
Geometry Nodes
A node-based system to generate, scatter, and vary geometry non-destructively using point distribution and instancing.
Summary Takeaway

Curves simplify precise shapes; use extrusion or bevel objects for volume, convert to mesh for export, and employ Geometry Nodes to automate repetitive tasks while keeping your workflow non-destructive.
