Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Bevel Modifier and Limit Methods
Definition
Chamfer edges for realism and better shading

Bevel prevents perfectly sharp edges, improving reflections and avoiding harsh highlights. Control amount, segments, and limit method to balance realism and geometry cost.

Parameters
Amount: size. Segments: smoothness. Limit Method: Angle or Weight for selective application.
Example
Use amount 0.02 and 2 segments for a subtle, game‑friendly edge on a crate corner.
Common Mistake

Applying very high segment counts across many edges leads to heavy meshes and minimal visual gain in mid‑distance renders.

Limit Methods Visual
Angle
Automatically bevels edges sharper than the angle threshold — quick but less precise.
Weight
Mark specific edges with bevel weight for exact control over which edges get beveled.
Practical tip
Use Weight for trim details and Angle for general rounding. Combine with selection and marking to avoid accidental bevels.
