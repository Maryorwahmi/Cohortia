Hard Surface Modeling with Modifiers

A focused, non‑destructive workflow for precise mechanical and architectural objects using Blender's modifiers.

In this lesson
Work non‑destructively, cut holes with Booleans, add realistic edge detail, and stack modifiers for speed.
Why it matters
Games and products require precise, tweakable assets. Non‑destructive modifiers let you iterate quickly without rebuilding from scratch.
Explore Key Topics
Boolean Cuts
Use a separate cutter object like a cylinder and Difference to make precise holes that remain editable.
Bevel & Edges
Small bevels prevent shading artifacts and add realism; control with amount, segments, and limit method.
Stacks & Order
Stack order changes results; experiment with Mirror → Array → Boolean → Bevel → Subdivision for many hard surfaces.
