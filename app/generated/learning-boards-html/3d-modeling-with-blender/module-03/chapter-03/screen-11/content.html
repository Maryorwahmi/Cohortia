Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Sci‑Fi Crate — Boolean Cutouts & Bevels
Workflow
Cutter placement → Boolean Difference → Bevel

Position cutters and keep them editable. After cutting, add a small bevel to prevent harsh highlights and to simulate real‑world edge chamfers.

Example: use a cylinder cutter for circular vents and a scaled cube for rectangular cutouts.
Misconception: applying Booleans immediately is unnecessary; keep them live until shapes are finalized.
Key Takeaway

Bevel lightly after Boolean to improve shading; adjust weight or angle limits for selective edge control.

Example Scenario
Cut a rectangular access panel with a cube cutter, add bevel to its rim, then nudge the cutter to test non‑destructive updates.
Check normals and apply scale if Booleans misbehave.
If shading artifacts persist, add supporting loops near problematic edges before applying modifiers.
