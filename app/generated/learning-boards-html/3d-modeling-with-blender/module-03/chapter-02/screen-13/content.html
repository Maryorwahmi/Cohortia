Prepare for the assessment

Use these checkpoints and tips to validate your workflow before testing or handing off to a pipeline.

Core question
Why retopologize before using in a game? Answer: performance and animation-friendly topology.
Technique question
Which protects areas and which projects detail? Masks protect; stencils (alphas) project.
Pro tip
Use multiple low-strength passes with brushes and vary stencil placement to avoid tiling artefacts.
Final Check

Bake a quick normal map and inspect it on the low-poly. If major details appear and deformation holds, your workflow succeeded.

Avoid

Rushing retopo or decimating as a shortcut — both lead to problems later in rigging and baking.
