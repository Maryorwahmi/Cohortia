Learning Objectives

Four verified milestones that connect sculpting detail to production-ready topology.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Master advanced brushes

Use Crease, Pinch, Scrape, and Fill with intent to create sharp features, tightened edges, planar surfaces, or restored volumes. Control inversion and strength to switch behavior.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Utilize masks & stencils

Paint masks to protect areas, invert or extract masked regions, and use stencils to project image detail like pores or fabric into your sculpt.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Understand retopology

Explain why a high-poly sculpt must be rebuilt as a low-poly mesh with clean quad loops for animation, UV mapping, and baking normal maps.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply Blender retopo tools

Enable face snapping with project individual elements, build vertex by vertex, and use Shrinkwrap set to Project with both axes enabled to keep the low-poly tight to the sculpt.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
