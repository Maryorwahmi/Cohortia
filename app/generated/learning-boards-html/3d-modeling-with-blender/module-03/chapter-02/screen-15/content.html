Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Final Reminders
Brush Control
Use low strength and multiple passes; hold Control to invert Crease when testing raised vs recessed forms.
Masks & Stencils
Mask to protect areas; stencil to project alphas. Extract masks when separate topology is required.
Retopo Intent
Plan loops around deformation, enable Face snapping, and use Shrinkwrap Project with both axes for final tightening.
Closing Takeaway
Practice the loop: sculpt detail carefully, protect and project where needed, then retopo with snapping and Shrinkwrap so your art becomes a practical, animatable asset.
