What is retopology and why it matters

Retopology constructs a new low-poly mesh with intentional edge flow so your sculpted detail can be baked and used in animation and games.

Benefit: Animation
Edge loops allow predictable deformation around joints and facial features.
Benefit: Texturing
Clean topology makes UV unwrapping easier and baking normals more reliable.
Benefit: Performance
A low-polygon mesh reduces real-time rendering costs and improves frame rates.
Misconception
Decimation is not a substitute for retopology — it removes detail but does not create usable edge flow for deformation.
Key Takeaway

Retopology is a bridge between sculpt detail and production readiness: rebuild the mesh with intentional loops so the detail can be baked and animated properly.
