Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Masks
Process

Paint, invert, clear, extract

Masks let you protect geometry while you sculpt nearby detail, then convert that selection into a separate mesh if needed.

Paint
Use the Mask brush or hold M to paint the region.
Invert / Clear
Invert with Control+I, clear with Alt+M to change selection quickly.
Extract
Extract a mask into a new object for separate detailing or retopo reference.
Example
Mask the cheeks and mask out the mouth. Sculpt wrinkles around the mask edge, then invert and extract a thin lip guard if you need a separate mesh for clothing or armor.
Best Practices
  • Use soft masks for organic blending, hard masks for mechanical separations.
  • Invert masks to toggle protective regions quickly during alternating passes.
  • Extract only when you need separate UVs or distinct topology for details.
Common Mistake

Relying on masks alone for final geometry. Masks limit sculpting influence, but extraction and retopology are necessary for production meshes.
