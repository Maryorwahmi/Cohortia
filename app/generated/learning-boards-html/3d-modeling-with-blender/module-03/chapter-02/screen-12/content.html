Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Topology Comparison
Good Topology
Characteristics
  • Mostly quad faces with clean edge loops.
  • Even polygon distribution across deforming areas.
  • Loops follow muscles and joint motion paths.
Example: eye and mouth loops built to support blinks and lip shapes.
Messy Topology
Problems
  • Random triangles and uneven polygons that pinch during deformation.
  • Long thin faces that distort UVs and normal bakes.
  • Unplanned edge flow that makes rigging and corrective shapes harder.
Example: a decimated cheek that loses volume and creates artifacts when animated.
Key Takeaway

Design loops with deformation and UVs in mind. Clean topology prevents downstream problems in rigging, texturing, and baking.

Check

Before proceeding, ask if eye and mouth regions have continuous loops and avoid long thin quads in high-stretch areas.
