Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
Advanced Brushes
Crease, Pinch, Scrape, Fill each serve distinct detailing roles for organic and hard-surface work.
Masks
Protect or isolate areas, invert and extract selections for targeted sculpting or geometry creation.
Stencils (Alphas)
Project image textures onto your sculpt to add pores, fabric weaves, or patterning with precise placement.
Retopology
Create an optimized low-poly mesh with good edge flow to support animation, UVs, and baking.
Snapping (Face Project)
Face snapping with project individual elements makes placing vertices that adhere to the sculpt reliable during build.
Shrinkwrap Modifier
Project mode with both axes enabled tightens the low-poly to the high-poly sculpt for accurate baking results.
Summary
Mastering brush control, selective masking, and a deliberate retopology workflow ensures your sculpts become efficient and animatable assets ready for baking and real-time use.
