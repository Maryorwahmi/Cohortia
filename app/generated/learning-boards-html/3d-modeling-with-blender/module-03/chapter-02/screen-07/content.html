Stencils and Alphas

Load an image as a brush texture, set mode to Stencil, position it, and sculpt through it to project realistic detail.

Controls: R rotate • S scale • G grab
Load Image
In the Brush Texture panel, add your alpha and switch to Stencil mode.
Position
Use rotate, scale, and move to align the stencil to anatomy or panel lines.
Sculpt through
Apply strokes at low strength, vary placement to avoid obvious tiling.
Example
Load a pore alpha, mask cheeks, and stencil across the forehead, rotating between passes for natural variation.
Pro tip
Combine multiple alphas at low intensity to avoid repetitive surface patterns and to increase realism.
Key Takeaway

Stencils are precise but need varied placement and gentle strength to produce believable surface detail suitable for baking later.
