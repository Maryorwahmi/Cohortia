How to verify each skill

Follow these concrete checks during the demo or while practicing in your own file.

Brush Milestone
Create and compare an inverted crease
  • Use Crease to carve a sharp rim near an eyelid.
  • Hold Control to invert the operation and sculpt a raised ridge.
  • Assess edge sharpness and continuity between the two results.
Mask & Stencil Milestone
Mask cheeks, stencil pores on the forehead
  • Paint a mask to protect cheeks and lips.
  • Load a pore image as a stencil and sculpt through it on the unmasked area.
  • Bake a normal map to confirm the detail transfers.
Retopo Milestone
Build clean quad loops around eyes and mouth
  • Start a plane, leave a single vertex, and extrude across the surface.
  • Form even quads and flow loops that follow deformation.
  • Check for pinches or long thin quads and correct them.
Tool Milestone
Snapping and Shrinkwrap configured
Set snapping to Face with Project Individual Elements, enable Shrinkwrap in Project mode with Negative and Positive axes to tightly bind vertices to the sculpt.
