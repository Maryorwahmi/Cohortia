Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Crease & Pinch
Core Concept

Crease and Pinch defined

Crease carves sharp channels or ridges; Pinch pulls vertices toward the center to sharpen edges and wrinkles.

Practical example
Sculpt the eyelid crease with Crease, then use Pinch to tighten the adjacent skin folds for a crisp silhouette.
Common mistake
Overusing Pinch creates artificial geometry and can collapse polygons; prefer multiple low-strength passes.
Key Takeaway

Alternate Crease and Pinch at low strength to define and then tighten edges without damaging topology.

Step Example
A short guided micro-workflow to use these brushes on a brow area.
Base sculpt
Use Draw or Clay Strips to block in the brow and lid forms.
Apply Crease
Carve the eyelid rim at low strength. Hold Control to invert and test raised ridge behavior.
Use Pinch
Pull vertices into the crease to sharpen the fold. Use multiple gentle passes.
Key Takeaway

Crease defines form; Pinch refines it. Use inversion and low strength to avoid pinches and topology collapse.
