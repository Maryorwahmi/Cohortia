Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Eevee vs. Cycles
Eevee
Fast, interactive
  • • Rasterization and screen-space effects.
  • • Requires irradiance volumes and probes for GI.
  • • Great for game previews and rapid iterations.
Cycles
Physically accurate
  • • Path tracing yields realistic GI and caustics.
  • • Higher sample counts and render time required.
  • • Preferred for product shots and cinematics.
Hybrid workflow

Iterate and preview in Eevee, then bake high-quality maps or perform final Cycles renders where physical accuracy is essential. This balances speed and final image quality efficiently.
