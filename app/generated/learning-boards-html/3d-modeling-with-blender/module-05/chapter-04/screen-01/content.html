Visual Concept
Speed vs. Accuracy
Eevee approximates lighting to favor iteration; Cycles simulates light for final quality.
Typical Uses
Game previews and fast animations for Eevee; product shots and realistic interiors for Cycles.
Quick Tip
Use Eevee to iterate and Cycles to finalize, or bake maps in Cycles for use in Eevee or game engines.
Key Takeaway

Choose Eevee when you need speed and interactivity. Choose Cycles when the final image must obey physical light and realism.
