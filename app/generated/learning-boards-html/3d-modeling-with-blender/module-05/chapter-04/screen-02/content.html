Learning Objectives

Skills to describe, configure, and compare Eevee and Cycles, and to make practical engine choices.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate engines

Name rasterization and path tracing, and explain why they produce different results and costs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand core principles

Explain real-time approximations in Eevee and light bounces in Cycles with simple diagrams or analogies.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Configure render settings

Adjust samples, enable denoising, and bake indirect lighting for Eevee to reach the expected output quality.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Choose use cases

Select Eevee for fast previews or Cycles for final photorealism and justify the choice based on project constraints.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic
Think "E for Express" and "C for Calculated" — Eevee for quick expression and Cycles for fully calculated light.
