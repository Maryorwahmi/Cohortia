How it works

Rasterization + Approximations

Eevee renders fast by rasterizing geometry and using screen-space and baked data to simulate complex effects without full physical simulation.

Screen-space reflections

Reflections sampled from what's visible on-screen; fast but limited to visible pixels.

Irradiance volumes

Baked probes that approximate indirect lighting across space; must be updated for scene changes.

Bloom & AO

Post-processing that enhances perceived realism with low cost.

Practical workflow
When to use Eevee
  • • Game asset previews and quick iterations.
  • • Motion graphics and fast animation passes.
  • • Viewport rendering during look development.
Common mistake

Expecting Cycles-level global illumination without baking irradiance volumes and setting reflection probes properly will produce artifacts.

Key Takeaway

Eevee is optimized for speed and interactive iteration. Bake probes and enable screen-space features for the best results.
