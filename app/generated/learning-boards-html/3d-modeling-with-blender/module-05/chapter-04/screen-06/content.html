Render Properties

Checklist for Eevee

Samples (Render)

Controls final render quality for screen-space effects. Use 32–128 depending on scene and noise tolerance.

Enable: AO, Bloom, SSR

Each adds realism but also render cost. Toggle during iteration to speed up work.

Indirect Lighting & Baking

Add irradiance volumes and reflection probes, then Bake Indirect Lighting to capture GI approximations.

Tips & Example
Example workflow: iterate with Samples 32, enable AO only; when satisfied, set Samples 64, enable SSR and Bloom, place probes near reflective surfaces and bake. Render and save.
Common mistake

Not rebaking probes after moving lights leads to mismatched reflections and lighting. Always rebake before final renders.
