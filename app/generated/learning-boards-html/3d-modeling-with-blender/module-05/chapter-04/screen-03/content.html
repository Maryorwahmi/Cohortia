Hands-on Activity

Render the same scene in both engines and compare visual quality, noise, and render time.

Step 1
Eevee settings

Samples 64, enable AO, Bloom, SSR, add irradiance volume and bake.

Step 2
Cycles settings

GPU Compute if available, samples 128, enable denoising like OpenImageDenoise or OptiX.

Step 3
Compare

Open both renders, note differences in GI, reflections, noise, and record render times.

Expected outcome

Two saved images demonstrating the trade-off: Eevee renders faster but may lack some physical light detail; Cycles renders slower but shows more accurate GI and reflections. Document the differences and justify your engine selection for a sample use case.
