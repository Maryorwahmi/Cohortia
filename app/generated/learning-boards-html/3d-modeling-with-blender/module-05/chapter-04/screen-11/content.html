Common mistake
Expecting instant photorealism

Eevee needs proper baking and probes; Cycles needs samples and time. Both require workflow planning.

Pro tip
Bake maps and use hybrid workflows

Bake ambient occlusion or normal maps in Cycles for highest quality, then use those maps in Eevee or the target game engine for fast previews and exports.

Quick checklist

Rebake after lighting changes, use denoising sensibly, set samples progressively, and pick the engine that meets your timeline and fidelity needs.
