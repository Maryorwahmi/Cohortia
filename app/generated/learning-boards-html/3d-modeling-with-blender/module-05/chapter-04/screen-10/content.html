Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Activity Walkthrough
Step-by-step
  1. Prepare the scene with camera, lights, a textured subject, ground plane, and some reflective objects.
  2. Set Render Engine to Eevee: Samples 64, enable AO, Bloom, SSR, add irradiance volume and reflection probe, then Bake Indirect Lighting. Render and save.
  3. Switch to Cycles: GPU Compute if available, Samples 128, enable denoising, render and save.
  4. Compare side-by-side: note GI accuracy, reflection quality, noise level, and render time. Record observations.
Checkpoints
  • • Did Eevee require rebaking after light changes?
  • • Were reflections limited to visible pixels in Eevee?
  • • Did denoising in Cycles retain important details?
Key Takeaway

This activity trains you to spot practical engine limits and make informed choices based on observed results, not assumptions.
