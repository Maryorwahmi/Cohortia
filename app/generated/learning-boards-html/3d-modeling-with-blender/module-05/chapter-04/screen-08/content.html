Settings walkthrough

Samples and Denoising

Use lower samples for framing, then increase for final renders. Enable denoising to reduce visible noise while saving time. 
Render Properties
Render Engine: Cycles
Device: GPU Compute (if available)
Samples: Preview 32 → Final 128+
Denoising: OpenImageDenoise or OptiX
Light Paths: Limit bounces for speed
Practical example
Product shot: set final samples to 128, enable denoising, use moderate glossy and transmission bounces to capture reflections and glass without exploding render time.
Misconception

Denoising will not magically recreate high-frequency detail lost by extremely low sample counts. Increase samples to preserve detail before denoising for best results.
