Path tracing

Simulating light bounces

Cycles traces rays per pixel and computes material responses across bounces to capture realistic lighting behaviors like color bleeding and caustics.

When to use

High-quality stills, promotional renders, architectural visualization, and cinematic sequences requiring subtle lighting.

Hardware note

Use GPU Compute for faster performance when available; otherwise CPU rendering is supported but slower.

Settings to watch
  • • Samples: higher reduces noise but increases time.
  • • Denoising: allows lower samples with good results.
  • • Light Paths: controlling bounces balances realism and speed.
Key Takeaway

Cycles gives the most realistic light simulation in Blender, but requires planning for render time and samples. Use denoising and sensible bounce limits to optimize.
