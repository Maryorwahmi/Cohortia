Module 05 › Chapter 5.1 › Common Mistakes
Problem → Fix
Far point + high power
Move the light closer or increase radius; reduce power rather than overcompensating.
Fill as bright as Key
Lower Fill intensity to preserve modeling contrast and shape.
Wrong Sun angle
Adjust Angle to match weather/time of day: small for crisp shadows, large for diffuse light.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • Isolate each light to see its contribution.
  • Prefer changing size/position before increasing power.
  • Use Rendered previews and low-sample test renders to confirm fixes.
Pro tip

When in doubt, mute a single light to quickly identify whether it causes the unwanted effect.
