Point Light — definition

Emits uniformly in all directions from a single point. Ideal for lamps, candles, and small localized sources.

Properties
  • Power: intensity of emission.
  • Color: tint affects mood and material highlights.
  • Radius: controls shadow softness; larger equals softer.
Example
Use a Point Light with radius 0.1m and power 50W behind a prop for a subtle rim highlight.
Common mistake
Placing it too far and increasing power produces unnatural, washed-out illumination.
Try this in Blender
  1. Add Point Light (Shift+A > Light > Point).
  2. Set Power to 50W, Radius to 0.1m and view in Rendered mode.
  3. Move closer and reduce power; observe shadow contrast and highlight behavior.
Key Takeaway

Point Lights model small, omnidirectional sources; use radius to tune shadow softness and proximity to control intensity naturally.
