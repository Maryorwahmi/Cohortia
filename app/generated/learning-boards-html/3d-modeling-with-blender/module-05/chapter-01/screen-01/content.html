Fundamentals of Lighting in Blender
How light defines form, depth, and mood — Point, Sun, Spot, and Area explained
In this lesson

Learn each Blender light type, tweak core parameters, and apply a three-point lighting setup to a game prop or Suzanne mesh.

Why it matters

Lighting transforms models into scenes. Proper choices reveal details, control contrast, and set mood for games and renders.

Light Types
Point, Sun, Spot and Area — pick the one that matches your source.

Power, color, radius/size, and angle determine intensity and shadow softness.
Three-Point Setup
A quick, reliable composition: key, fill, and rim lights to model form.
