Module 05 › Chapter 5.1 › Radius / Size and Angle
Quick comparison
Small source
Sharper shadows, higher contrast, crisp specular highlights.
Large source
Softer shadows, gentler highlight falloff, more flattering skin/rendering for portraits.
Remember: softness is controlled by Size/Radius/Angle, not by increasing power.
Example exercise
1. Add two lights with the same power but different sizes and compare shadow edges.
2. For Sun Light, reduce Angle to 0.5° for a crisp sunny look; increase to 10° for overcast softness.
Key Takeaway

Use Size/Radius/Angle to shape shadow quality; scale sources to the scene for believable softness.
