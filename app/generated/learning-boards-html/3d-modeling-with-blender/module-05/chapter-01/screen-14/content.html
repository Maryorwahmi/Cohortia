Module 05 › Chapter 5.1 › Render Safety
Common artifacts
  • Fireflies: bright isolated pixels from very bright sources or sharp speculars.
  • Noise: visible grain from insufficient samples or extreme light contrasts.
  • Clipping: highlights that lose detail when overly bright.
Fixes: lower power, increase source size, adjust material roughness, and use denoising for final renders.
Practical checklist
  1. Reduce extreme power values first.
  2. Increase source size instead of intensity to soften sampling issues.
  3. Adjust material specular and roughness to avoid concentrated highlights.
  4. Run low-sample tests before committing to a final render.
Caution

Avoid brute-force increases in power to fix exposure. This often creates render artifacts and hides real lighting problems.
