Sun Light — definition

Directional light with parallel rays. Position has no effect on shadows; rotation and angle determine direction and softness.

Properties
  • Strength: overall intensity across the scene.
  • Color: tint of sunlight.
  • Angle: angular size affecting shadow sharpness.
Example
Outdoor scene: small angle (0.5°) for hard shadows; overcast: increase angle to soften shadows.
Common mistake
Moving the Sun in space instead of rotating it, expecting different shadow behavior.
Try this in Blender
  1. Add Sun Light and set Strength to 3 and Angle to 5° for a balanced directional key.
  2. Rotate the Sun to change the shadow direction while viewing from the camera.
  3. Lower Angle toward 0.5° to test hard shadows and increase to soften them.
Key Takeaway

Use Sun for consistent directional light; rotation defines direction and angle sets shadow character.
