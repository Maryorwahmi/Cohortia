Module 05 › Chapter 5.1 › Preview & Iterate
Practical checks
  • Preview in Rendered mode while viewing through camera.
  • Adjust one parameter at a time to see its direct effect.
  • Mute or isolate lights to evaluate their individual contributions.
Use low-sample test renders to confirm decisions; viewport previews are a guide but not the final result.
Common adjustments
Soft shadows: increase Area size or Sun angle.
Reduce specular clipping: lower power or reduce material roughness.
Improve separation: add small rim Point Light behind subject.
Key Takeaway

Preview from the camera and iterate conservatively. Isolate lights to understand their roles before merging the look.
