Module 05 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Point Light
Omnidirectional source; radius controls shadow softness.
Sun Light
Directional, rotation controls direction; angle affects shadow hardness.
Spot Light
Cone-shaped emitter; Size and Blend shape the beam and its falloff.
Area Light
Surface emitter for soft, natural shadows when scaled appropriately.
Power / Strength
Controls brightness; adjust conservatively and preview results.
Radius / Size / Angle
Control the softness of shadows; increase size/angle for gentler transitions.
Summary

Match light type to your intended source, tune power and size carefully, and preview from the camera while iterating.
