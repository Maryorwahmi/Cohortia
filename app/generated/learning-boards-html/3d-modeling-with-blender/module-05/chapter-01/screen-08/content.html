Area Light — definition

Emits from a surface area rather than a point. Larger sizes produce softer shadows, useful for studio or window lighting.

Properties
  • Power: intensity across the surface.
  • Color: tint affects ambient feel.
  • Size: larger size yields softer shadows.
Example
Use an Area Light as a Fill with Power 100W and Size 2m to soften shadows opposite the key.
Common mistake
Making the Area too small and expecting soft shadows; scale must match the scene to be effective.
Try this in Blender
  1. Add an Area Light opposite your key and set Power to 100W and Size to 2m for a neutral fill.
  2. Scale the Area to match a window or softbox that would exist in the real scene.
  3. Observe how soft shadows and subtle fills preserve shape without harsh contrast.
Key Takeaway

Scale Area Lights to achieve soft, natural shadows that support form without flattening contrast.
