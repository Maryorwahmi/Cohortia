Global Illumination — What It Is and Why It Matters

GI is the simulation of light bouncing, creating soft ambient illumination and realistic color transfer between surfaces.

Direct vs Indirect
Direct: light straight from the source. Indirect: light that has bounced one or more times, softening shadows and producing color bleeding.
Cycles vs Eevee
Cycles simulates GI physically via path tracing. Eevee approximates GI with Irradiance Volumes and Reflection Probes and requires baking for stable results.
Game workflow
Bake GI into lightmaps or textures for real-time use so indirect lighting persists without runtime path tracing.
Example
Color bleeding
A red wall next to a white floor will tint the floor in shadowed areas when GI is active. This subtle tint is a strong cue of real-world interaction between surfaces.
Key Takeaway

GI is essential for realism; choose Cycles for accuracy or bake GI for Eevee and game engines to preserve indirect light affordably.
