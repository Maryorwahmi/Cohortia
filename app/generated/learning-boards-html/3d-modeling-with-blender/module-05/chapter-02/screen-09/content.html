HDRI Environments — Setup Workflow

Step-by-step: load an HDRI, orient it with a Mapping node, then tune strength and exposure.

Step-by-step
World Properties → Surface → Environment Texture → Open .hdr
Shader Editor (World): Texture Coordinate (Generated) → Mapping → Environment Texture
Adjust Mapping Z rotation to orient the HDRI
Set Environment Strength to 0.5–0.8, then fine-tune in Rendered view
Concrete example
Load a studio interior HDR, set Strength to 0.6, and rotate Z until reflections align with your subject’s highlights.
Pitfall
Low-resolution HDRIs produce pixelated reflections; always prefer higher resolution panoramas for reflective materials.
Visualization
Environment = Ambient + Reflections
The HDRI wraps the scene, providing light from multiple directions and realistic reflective cues even without modeling the full environment.
Exposure control
If highlights blow out, lower HDRI Strength or reduce Exposure in Color Management.
Key Takeaway

HDRIs are powerful ambient providers; orient and scale strength carefully to support your three-point setup.
