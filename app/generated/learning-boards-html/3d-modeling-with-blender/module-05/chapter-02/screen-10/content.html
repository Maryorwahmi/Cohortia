HDRI Tuning and Common Pitfalls

Control resolution, strength, and orientation; watch exposure and mix with artificial lights when needed.

Resolution
Use high-resolution HDRIs to avoid pixelated reflections on glossy materials.
Strength
Start around 0.5–0.8 and adjust; very bright HDRIs can blow highlights if exposure is not managed.
Orientation
Rotate the Mapping Z value to place bright regions of the HDRI where they support your Key and rim highlights.
Checkpoints
Exposure
If highlights clip, lower HDRI strength or reduce Exposure in Color Management settings.
Mixing methods
Combine HDRI ambient with three-point lights to get both natural wrap and controlled sculpting lights.
Key Takeaway

Tuning HDRI resolution, strength, and rotation is essential to avoid pixelated reflections and blown highlights.
