Key Light — The Primary Source

The Key sets the strongest shadows and the scene’s mood. Choose power, type, and size deliberately.

Practical settings
Example: Area Light, Power 500W, Size 1m for a head-and-shoulders subject. Increase size to soften shadows.
Misconception
Brighter Key is not always better. Use brightness to reveal form, not to wash out texture.
Pro tip
Try a subtle color contrast between Key and Fill to increase visual interest without changing exposure.
Experiment
Hard vs Soft Shadows
Smaller sources or Sun lights give hard shadows; larger Areas soften edges by increasing angular size.
Distance and Falloff
Move the Key closer for stronger contrast; farther away it becomes more even and less directional.
Check your render
Switch to Rendered view and evaluate whether the Key reveals the subject’s important planes and textures.
Key Takeaway

Use the Key to sculpt form. Adjust size for shadow softness and power for readable contrast.
