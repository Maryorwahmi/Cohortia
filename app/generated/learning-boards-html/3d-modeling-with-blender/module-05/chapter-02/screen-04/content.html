Three-Point Lighting — System Overview

Define form with controlled Key, Fill, and Back lights to craft mood and depth.

Definition
Three-point lighting uses a dominant Key Light, a softer Fill Light to reduce harsh shadows, and a Back Light to separate the subject from the background.
Concrete example
Key: Area Light, Power 500W, size 1m; Fill: Area Light, Power 200W, size 1.5m; Back: Point Light, Power 100W, radius 0.1m.
Common mistake
Making the Fill too strong flattens the scene. Keep Fill noticeably weaker than Key.
Key Takeaway

Use intensity and source size to control shadow hardness; preserve contrast by keeping Key stronger than Fill.

Visual plan
Step 1 — Key Light
Position above and to one side; strongest light; sets mood and major shadows.
Step 2 — Fill Light
Opposite the Key; softer and lower power to reveal shadow detail but not remove shadows.
Step 3 — Back Light
Behind and above the subject to create a rim. Moderate power to avoid haloing.
Try this
Place the lights, switch to Rendered viewport, and tweak Area size and power until the subject reads clearly without losing shadow depth.
