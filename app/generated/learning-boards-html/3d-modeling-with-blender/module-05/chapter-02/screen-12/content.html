Irradiance Volumes & Reflection Probes

Eevee uses precomputed probes to approximate indirect lighting and reflections for fast real-time rendering.

Irradiance Volume
Place a volume to cover the scene and bake indirect lighting so surfaces receive approximated GI samples.
Reflection Probe
Set probes near reflective objects to capture local environment maps and project plausible reflections.
Common mistake
Sparse or non-overlapping probes create seams or inconsistent reflection detail; overlap and rebake when necessary.
Hands-on steps
Add probes
Shift+A → Light Probe → Irradiance Volume or Reflection Probe. Position and scale to cover the needed area.
Bake
Render Properties → Indirect Lighting → Bake Indirect Lighting so the irradiance data is saved to the volume.
Key Takeaway

Probes let Eevee approximate GI and reflections quickly. Place and bake them to preserve indirect lighting in real time.
