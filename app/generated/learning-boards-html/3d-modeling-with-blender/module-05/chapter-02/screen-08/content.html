Color, Intensity, and Mood — Creative Controls

Use intensity ratios and temperature contrasts to shape emotion while keeping form legible.

Intensity patterns
Key > Fill > Back. Increase Key-to-Fill ratio for drama; reduce it for a gentle, film-like image.
Color contrast
Cool Key + Warm Fill yields cinematic separation without losing realism.
Pitfall
Uncontrolled color plus strong indirect bounces may tint skin or materials unintentionally; monitor color bleeding.
Exercise
Two-render comparison
Render one scene driven by a strong Key with subtle Fill, and another dominated by an HDRI with soft lights. Compare mood, shadow definition, and reflections.
Key Takeaway

Color and intensity together create emotional cues. Tune both while preserving readable form and correct exposure.
