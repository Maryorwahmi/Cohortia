Fill Light — Revealing Shadow Detail

A softer, lower-power companion to the Key that uncovers texture without flattening form.

Practical settings
Example: Area Light, Power 200W, Size 1.5m. Position opposite the Key at a slightly lower height.
When to rely on HDRI
Use an HDRI or indirect bounces for natural, multi-directional ambient fill that matches the environment.
Common mistake
Too strong a Fill removes the Key’s sculpting; reduce Fill power to restore shadow modeling.
Practice
Small adjustments
Change Fill by small increments and observe shadows in Rendered mode — subtle shifts often make the biggest visual improvement.
Softness via size
Increase Area size to soften shadow edges rather than only reducing power.
Check detail
Look for recovered texture and tonal separation in shadow areas without losing silhouette clarity.
Key Takeaway

Adjust Fill power and size to reveal shadow information while protecting the Key’s sculpting effect.
