Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Adding and Navigating Cameras
Process Flow

Place, Preview, Snap

Step 1 — Add a Camera
Use Shift+A → Camera. The object appears at the cursor and is selectable for transforms.
Step 2 — Preview View
Press Numpad 0 to look through the active camera and evaluate framing before rendering.
Step 3 — Snap Camera to View
Compose a view, then press Ctrl+Alt+Numpad 0 to align the camera to your current viewport instantly.
Common Mistake
Forgetting which camera is active; verify in Scene Properties or press Ctrl+Numpad 0 with the selected camera.
Interactive Walkthrough
Concrete Example
Add a camera, position the view to capture a waist-up portrait of your bust, press Ctrl+Alt+Numpad 0, then preview in Rendered shading to confirm lighting and framing.

Key Takeaway
Use viewport preview and snapping to lock a composition quickly. Confirm active camera selection to avoid rendering the wrong view.
