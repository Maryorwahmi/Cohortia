Learning Objectives

Build camera skills that lead to consistent, intentional renders.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Add and Manipulate Cameras
Place a camera, snap it to your view, and use navigation controls to refine composition. These basics let you define the viewer's perspective reliably.
Mnemonic: camera icon + crosshairs
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand Optics
Explain focal lengths, depth of field, and F-Stop to control perspective and focus. Recognize when a lens choice will exaggerate or compress space.
Mnemonic: lens focal ring
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply Composition Rules
Use rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, and negative space to direct attention and create balance. Guides help you build stronger shots quickly.
Mnemonic: thirds grid
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Animate & Troubleshoot Camera Motion
Set location and rotation keyframes, preview motion, and fix framing issues. Smooth previewing prevents jarring camera moves in your final render.
Mnemonic: keyframe diamond
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
