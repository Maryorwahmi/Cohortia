Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Navigation Controls: Walk and Fly
Navigation Modes
Fly (Shift+F) offers free-flight controls for fast placement. Walk gives a grounded movement useful in interiors. Both can be used to block camera positions quickly.
Workflow
  1. Enter Fly or Walk mode and move to a candidate viewpoint.
  2. Adjust orientation, then snap camera to view with Ctrl+Alt+Numpad 0.
  3. Preview and refine focal length and DoF.
Misconception
Fly mode output is not final until the camera is snapped and checked for active camera status and optical settings.
Concrete Example
Use Fly to move behind your character, tilt slightly upward for presence, snap the camera, press Numpad 0 and check that the focal length and DoF values match your creative goal.
Check Your Understanding
When should you prefer Walk over Fly navigation, and how do you lock the camera once you find a view?
Key Takeaway
Use interactive navigation to find expressive camera positions quickly, then snap and verify optical settings for consistent renders.
