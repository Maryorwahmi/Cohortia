Composition Techniques

Leading Lines • Framing • Negative Space

Leading Lines
Use environmental lines to direct the eye toward your main subject and build depth cues.
Framing
Place foreground elements to create a natural frame around the subject, adding context and depth.
Negative Space & Symmetry
Negative space isolates the subject; symmetry conveys stability. Choose which feeling supports the shot's intent.
Example Exercise
Place a foreground arch, position the character in the midground, and set the camera with a moderate focal length. Use DoF to keep the subject sharp while softening foreground edges just enough to suggest depth without losing the frame.
Common Misconception
A busy frame is not automatically better. Use negative space to give the viewer a resting point and emphasize your subject.
Key Takeaway
Compose using scene geometry to support storytelling. Test by toggling elements in and out and observing where the eye lands.
