Understanding Cameras and Composition

Learn how camera placement, lens settings, depth of field, and composition choices change how your 3D work reads to an audience.

In this lesson
Add and control cameras, explore focal lengths and DoF, compose using photographic rules, and animate cinematic shots.
Why it matters
Camera choices turn technical models into clear storytelling images for portfolios, cinematics, or game assets.
Visual Summary
Camera added with Shift+A, view with Numpad 0, snap with Ctrl+Alt+Numpad 0. Focal length and DoF shape perspective and focus.

Camera & Navigation

Optics: Focal Length & DoF

Composition & Output
