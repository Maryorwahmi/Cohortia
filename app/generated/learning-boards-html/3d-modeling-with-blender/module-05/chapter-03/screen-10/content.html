Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Animating the Camera with Keyframes
Step-by-Step
  1. Select the camera and open the Timeline.
  2. Move to a frame, set position/rotation, press I → Location & Rotation.
  3. Repeat for the goal frame; preview and adjust timing and easing.
Pro Tip
Parent the camera to an empty for more intuitive path-style animation and control keyframes on the empty instead of the camera directly.
Concrete Example
Create a 120-frame dolly: frame 1 at distance -10, frame 120 at distance -4. Insert Location & Rotation keyframes on both frames and preview. Refine easing in the graph editor to smooth acceleration.
Common Mistake
Using too few frames for large moves causes sudden motion and discomfort. Increase frames or reduce distance per move.
Key Takeaway
Plan camera motion, preview at playback speed, and refine easing for cinematic results that support the shot's narrative.
