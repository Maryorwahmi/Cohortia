Activity: Cinematic Camera Setup

Follow the ordered steps to produce a composed portrait render that demonstrates focal length and DoF.

Step-by-step
  1. Add a Camera (Shift+A > Camera) and press Numpad 0 to view through it.
  2. Use Fly (Shift+F) or Walk to position and then snap with Ctrl+Alt+Numpad 0.
  3. Set focal length (try 85mm or 35mm) and enable Depth of Field.
  4. Assign Focus Object to the bust's head and set F-Stop to ~2.8 for shallow DoF.
  5. Enable Thirds guide, align eyes to an intersection, set Dimensions to 1920x1080, and review in Rendered shading.
Example Result
A portrait where the eyes are sharp, the background is softly blurred, and the composition reads as intentional across final output dimensions.
Key Takeaway
Combine optics, DoF, and guides to create a clear visual hierarchy and mood in your portrait renders.
