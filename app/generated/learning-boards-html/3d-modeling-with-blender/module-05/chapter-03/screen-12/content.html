Definition

Aspect Ratio & Resolution

Define the proportional relationship between width and height and set the pixel dimensions that Blender will render.

Practical Steps
Open Render Properties → Dimensions. Choose a resolution like 1920x1080 for Full HD and lock aspect ratio for consistent composition.
Example & Warning
If you compose for 16:9 but later need 1:1, important objects at the sides may be cropped. Set the final format early or compose with protective margins.
Check
What platform or delivery format requires your render size, and how will that affect composition?
Key Takeaway
Decide on aspect ratio and resolution early and compose with guides to protect important elements from cropping.
