Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Focal Length: Wide vs Standard vs Telephoto
Comparison

Wide • Standard • Telephoto

Wide (18–35mm)
Wide field of view, strong depth exaggeration, good for interiors and landscapes. Avoid for close-up portraits to prevent distortion.
Standard (≈50mm)
Natural perspective similar to the eye; balanced and neutral for many uses.
Telephoto (85–200mm+)
Narrower view and perspective compression; useful for portraits and isolating subjects.
Concrete Example
Place the camera at a fixed point facing your character bust. Change focal length from 35mm to 85mm and notice the background 'moving' closer and the subject's features appearing more compressed.
Explanation & Misconception
Focal length affects the angle of view and perceived distance between objects, not the physical distance. A common misconception is that a telephoto "zooms" by moving closer; in fact, it narrows the field of view and compresses perspective. To change perceived subject scale use focal length together with camera position for best results.
Check
Which lens feels right for a portrait versus an environmental shot, and why?
Key Takeaway
Match focal length to shot intent: wider for space, longer for subject isolation and compression.
