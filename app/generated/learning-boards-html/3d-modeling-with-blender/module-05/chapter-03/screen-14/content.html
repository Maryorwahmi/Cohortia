Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Camera
An object defining the perspective used for rendering; confirm the active camera before rendering.
Focal Length
Determines field of view and perspective distortion; choose wide for space, telephoto for compression.
Depth of Field
Use Focus Object and F-Stop to control what is sharp. DoF guides attention and adds cinematic depth.
Summary Takeaway

Combine camera placement, optics, and composition guides, and set output early to create intentional, story-driven renders.
