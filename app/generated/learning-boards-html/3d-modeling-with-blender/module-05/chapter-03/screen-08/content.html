Comparison

Centering vs Rule of Thirds

Centering
Conveys stability and symmetry. Works well for formal portraits and monuments.
Rule of Thirds
Creates dynamism and visual flow by placing key elements off-center along thirds lines.
Practical Tip
Enable the Thirds guide in camera Viewport Display > Guides and move the camera until key features align with intersections.
Concrete Example
Frame your character so the eyes lie on a thirds intersection. Notice how the empty space then feels directional, suggesting gaze or motion.
Leading Question
Does placing the subject off-center improve the story you want the image to tell?
Key Takeaway
Use guides to test composition quickly. The rule of thirds is a helpful default but adapt it to your intent.
