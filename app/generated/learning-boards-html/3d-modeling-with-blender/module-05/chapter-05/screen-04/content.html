Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Resolution & Aspect
Definition
Resolution sets pixel dimensions; aspect ratio sets composition proportions.
Example
Render a publicity still at 1920×1080 for Full HD, use 100% for final output.
Tip
Use 50% or 75% in the Percentage slider for quick test renders to verify lighting and materials.
Common Mistake
Rendering at unnecessarily higher resolutions wastes time and storage without proportional benefits.
Key Takeaway

Set resolution and aspect to match final delivery; preview with percentage before final render.

Practical checklist
Target Resolution
1920 × 1080, 3840 × 2160, or custom as required by the project.
Aspect Ratio
Confirm camera framing fits 16:9, 1:1, or other client constraints before final render.
Percentage Slider
Use 50–75% for test renders, then set to 100% for final images.
Frame Size Consistency
Keep consistent resolution across animation frames to avoid flicker or mismatch when compiling.
