Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Denoising & Samples
Best practice
Start with moderate samples (64–128) plus denoising, test crops, then increase samples only where detail suffers.
Example Test
Render a 400×400 crop at 128 samples with OpenImageDenoise, compare to 512 samples to evaluate quality vs time tradeoff.
Practical Settings
  • Render samples: 64–128 for many shots with denoising.
  • Enable OpenImageDenoise for crisp results on CPU/GPU.
  • Adjust Light Paths conservatively to cut ray bounces.
Common Mistake
Relying on denoising to correct a badly lit scene will often hide problems but not solve creative or exposure issues.
Key Takeaway

Use denoising to reduce render samples, but validate close-up detail and correct lighting first.
