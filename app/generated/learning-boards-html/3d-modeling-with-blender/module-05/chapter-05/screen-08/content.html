Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Compositing Workflow
Step-by-step compositing
  1. Render with desired passes enabled in View Layer Properties.
  2. Open Compositing workspace and enable Use Nodes.
  3. Add a Viewer node and check individual passes (Diffuse, AO, Normal, Z).
  4. Use Mix and Color Balance nodes to blend passes and correct highlights or shadows.
  5. Connect to Composite node and export the final image or sequence.
Concrete Example
Blend AO over Diffuse at low opacity to add contact shadow detail without re-rendering the scene.
Misconception
Compositing refines output but cannot replace proper lighting or material setup during render.
Key Takeaway

Use the Compositor to adjust passes and finalize your image with minimal re-renders.
