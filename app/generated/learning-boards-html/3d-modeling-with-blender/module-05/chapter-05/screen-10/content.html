Light path tuning
  • Reduce Max Bounces for diffuse, glossy, and transmission to speed renders.
  • Lower per-light shadow samples when shadows are not focal points.
  • Simplify refractive and layered shaders where physical accuracy isn't required.
Key Takeaway

Tweak bounces and material complexity to find a balance between speed and visual fidelity.

Concrete Example
Lower diffuse bounces from 12 to 6 and enable denoising to significantly cut samples needed while keeping image quality.
Common Mistake
Leaving extremely complex transparent materials at full physical accuracy when shot framing never showcases their detail leads to wasted time.
Pro Tip
Test changes by rendering a small crop of the frame to compare visual impact before applying globally.
