Definition › Undo & Redo
Undo and Redo basics
Ctrl+Z steps backwards. Shift+Ctrl+Z redoes. Configure Undo Steps in preferences to control history depth.
Practical example
Move, rotate and scale an object. If a step is wrong, press Ctrl+Z once or multiple times to return to the desired state, then use Shift+Ctrl+Z to move forward again if needed.
Preferences
Edit > Preferences > System > Undo Steps controls how far back you can go.
Common mistake
Relying on Undo instead of saving. If the file closes or undo history is cleared, you may lose undo states.
Key Takeaway

Combine Undo/Redo with frequent saves to experiment safely and avoid losing work.
