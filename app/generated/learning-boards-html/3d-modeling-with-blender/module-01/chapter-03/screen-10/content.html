Process Flow › Collections
Why collections matter
They group objects for visibility, selection, and render control to manage complex scenes efficiently.
How to create and move
  1. Right-click in the Outliner and choose New Collection.
  2. Select objects in the viewport, press M, and choose a collection or create a new one.
  3. Use the checkbox and camera icon in the Outliner to hide or disable rendering for that collection.
Key Takeaway

Organize early with clear collection names to simplify hiding, rendering, and collaboration.

Concrete example
Create 'Ground' and 'Props' collections, move the plane to Ground and primitives to Props, then toggle Props to focus on terrain without clutter.
Common misconception
Collections are not just visual layers; they also control render and selection behavior for grouped objects.
