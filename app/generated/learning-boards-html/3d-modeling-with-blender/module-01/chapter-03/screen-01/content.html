Creating and Deleting Basic Primitives
Add cubes, spheres, cylinders and more, tweak their initial parameters, remove unwanted objects safely, and organize scenes with collections for smoother workflows.
In this lesson
Add primitives, edit their initial parameters via the Adjust Last Operation panel, delete with confirmation, and organize with Collections.
Why it matters
Controlling geometry at creation saves editing time and polygon budget, essential for game assets and efficient scenes.
Quick visual
A sphere's segment count, a cylinder's vertex count, and spawn position from the 3D Cursor are all decided at creation unless you change them immediately.
Key Takeaway

Create shapes deliberately and adjust parameters immediately using the Adjust Last Operation panel; organize early with collections to avoid later clutter.
