Shading Modes Compared
Wireframe
Shows only edges and vertices. Best for topology and polygon flow checks.
Solid
Default shaded view for blocking shapes and general modeling tasks.
Material Preview
Previews materials and textures with basic lighting—useful for asset checks.
Rendered
Full real-time render preview for final validation; resource-intensive.
Workflow example
Start in Solid to block forms, check topology in Wireframe, preview textures in Material Preview, and finalize in Rendered.
Common mistake
Keeping Rendered active while modeling slows down interaction. Switch modes to match the task.
