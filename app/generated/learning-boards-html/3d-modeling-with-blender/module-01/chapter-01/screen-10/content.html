Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Wireframe Mode Deep Dive
What Wireframe reveals
Edges, vertices, and polygon layout. Use it to evaluate mesh density, edge loops, and spot non-manifold faces that can cause export issues.
Practical checks
  • Look for dense clusters where decimation is needed.
  • Find stray vertices and overlapping faces that indicate non-manifold geometry.
  • Verify continuous edge loops for deformation areas.
Pitfall
Wireframe alone won’t show texture problems—combine it with Material Preview for a full check.
Example
A game artist inspects a character’s hand. In wireframe they reduce unnecessary polygons on the palm while preserving loops around knuckles for deformation.
Key Takeaway

Apply wireframe to validate topology and remove problem geometry before moving to textures and rendering.
