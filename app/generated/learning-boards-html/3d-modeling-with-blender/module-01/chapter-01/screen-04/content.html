Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What is the 3D Viewport?
Definition and Role

The 3D Viewport is your primary workspace and interactive camera. It displays scene objects, accepts navigation input, and presents different shading previews to guide modeling decisions.

Live feedback
Viewport shows modeling changes in real time and supports transform handles and selection tools.
Connected panels
Selections in the Viewport reflect in the Outliner and Properties and vice versa.
Misconception
The Viewport is not merely a preview; in Rendered mode it approximates final output using the chosen render engine.
Concrete scenario
You are aligning a character’s eyes. Use the orthographic front view to remove perspective distortion, then nudge vertices until edges align perfectly. This ensures symmetry when exporting for a game engine.
Key Takeaway

Treat the Viewport as your active camera: learn to control it deliberately to reduce errors and speed modeling.
