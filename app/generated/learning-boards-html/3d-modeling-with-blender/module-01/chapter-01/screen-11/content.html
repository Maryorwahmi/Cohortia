Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Material Preview & Rendered Modes
Material Preview
Shows materials, textures, and basic lighting for fast iteration. Use it to verify UV seams, texture scales, and basic shading setups without rendering.
When to use
During texturing and material setup to confirm placement and color work under simple lighting.
Rendered mode
Use for final validation with actual render engine lighting, shadows, and effects. Resource intensive; use sparingly during modeling.
Example
Test a painted texture on a sphere in Material Preview to check tiling, then switch to Rendered to confirm reflections and final lighting before export.
Common mistake
Leaving Rendered on while modeling reduces interactivity; switch modes according to task to maintain speed.
