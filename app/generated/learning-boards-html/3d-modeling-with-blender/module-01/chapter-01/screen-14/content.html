Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
3D Viewport
Primary interactive camera and workspace for modeling and inspection.
Orbiting
Rotate the camera around a pivot; frame to keep rotations stable.
Panning
Slide the view horizontally or vertically without rotating.
Zooming
Change camera distance; wheel for speed, Ctrl+MMB for smooth control.
View Gizmo
Snap to orthographic axes and click-drag to orient views visually.
N-panel
Sidebar for numeric transforms and tool options for precise edits.
Summary

Practice these essentials in the default scene: frame, orbit, pan, zoom, snap with the gizmo, and use the N-panel to measure and set exact values.
