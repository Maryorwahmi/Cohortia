What the View Gizmo does
The gizmo displays X, Y, Z axes; click an axis to snap to the corresponding orthographic view. Dragging the gizmo or clicking its faces rotates or orients the view interactively.
Orthographic vs Perspective
Orthographic removes perspective distortion and is ideal for precise alignment.
Quick practice
Click the X label to snap to the side view, then use the grid to align an edge exactly.
Misconception
Orthographic is not only for final output; it’s the go-to mode for measurements and vertex alignment.
Concrete example
When creating level geometry for a game, snap to orthographic top and side views with the gizmo to align walls exactly to the grid and avoid perspective-based errors.
Key Takeaway

Use the View Gizmo to reach orthographic views quickly; these are essential for precise modeling and alignment.
