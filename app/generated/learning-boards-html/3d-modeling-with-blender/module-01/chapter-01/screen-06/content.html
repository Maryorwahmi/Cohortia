How to Pan

Hold Shift and drag with the Middle Mouse Button to move the camera horizontally or vertically. Panning preserves orientation and is ideal for lining up elements or moving across a wide scene.

When to use pan
  • Reposition the view to another part of a large scene.
  • Align distant assets without changing angle.
  • Move across a texture or UV island while keeping orientation.
Misconception
Dragging without Shift or MMB will orbit and change angles; this is why the Shift modifier is important.
Example task
While arranging props in a scene, pan to place a table relative to a chair, then zoom in to make small placement adjustments. This keeps the camera steady while you nudge transforms numerically if needed.
Pro tip
Combine small pans with controlled zooms for accurate placement. If you drift too far, press Home to reframe.
