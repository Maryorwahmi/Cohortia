Navigating the 3D Viewport
Your virtual camera for modeling and inspection
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
What you'll do
  • Learn orbit, pan, and zoom with mouse and hotkeys.
  • Use View Gizmo and orthographic snaps for precision.
  • Switch shading modes to inspect topology and materials.
Why it matters

Smooth navigation saves time, avoids mistakes, and helps you catch mesh issues before export. It’s the difference between clumsy edits and precise modeling.

Quick orientation visual
Think of the viewport as a live camera: orbit rotates the camera around a focus, pan moves it laterally, and zoom shifts its distance.
Explore Key Topics
Navigation Basics
MMB to orbit, Shift+MMB to pan, Wheel or Ctrl+MMB to zoom.
Shading Modes
Wireframe → Solid → Material Preview → Rendered — switch as you model.
View Gizmo & N-panel
Use the gizmo to snap views and the N-panel to set exact transforms.
Visual Concept
Cards show the flow from viewing to inspecting to refining. Use the View Gizmo to snap and the N-panel for precision.
Starting Activity
Open the default scene with a cube. Try orbiting, panning, and zooming now to feel how the viewport responds.
