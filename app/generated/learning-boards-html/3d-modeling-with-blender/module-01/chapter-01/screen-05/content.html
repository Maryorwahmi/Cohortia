Orbiting: how and why
Hold the Middle Mouse Button and drag to orbit. Frame a selection first to set the pivot; this keeps rotations predictable and centered on what you are editing.
Step-by-step
  1. Select an object with left-click.
  2. Press Numpad period to frame the object (set pivot).
  3. Hold MMB and drag to orbit smoothly.
Common mistake
Orbiting without framing causes disorientation because the pivot drifts. Fix by framing or using the View Gizmo snap.
Example scenario
You are checking the back topology of a modeled helmet. Select the helmet, press period to frame, then orbit to inspect seams and internal edges. This helps identify hidden problems before export.
Check your understanding
Question: Why frame before orbiting? Answer: Framing establishes a pivot so rotation stays centered on the object, avoiding drift and confusion.
