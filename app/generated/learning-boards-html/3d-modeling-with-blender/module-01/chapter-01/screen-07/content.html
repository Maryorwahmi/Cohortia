Zoom controls and safe framing
Scroll the Mouse Wheel for quick zoom. For finer control hold Ctrl and drag with the Middle Mouse Button. Frame selections with Numpad period to establish the pivot and avoid losing objects when zooming out.
Quick zoom
Mouse Wheel up/down for fast zooming.
Precision zoom
Ctrl+MMB drag for smooth, controlled zoom.
Safe reset
Use Home or Shift+C then frame selection if you lose the object.
Example
Zoom into small sculpted detail with Ctrl+MMB, fix it, then frame the whole head to continue modeling.
Check your understanding
Why use Ctrl+MMB instead of the wheel? Because it gives smoother, continuous zooming for delicate adjustments. If you accidentally zoom out and can't find your object, framing and Home will restore context.
Key Takeaway

Control zooming and frame objects often to keep focus and avoid losing selections.
