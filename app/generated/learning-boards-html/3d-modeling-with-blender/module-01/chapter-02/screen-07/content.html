Move Tool

Gizmos and hotkeys for controlled translation

  • Activate Move with the four-headed arrow gizmo or press G.
  • After G, press X, Y, or Z to constrain to that axis. Press Shift+Axis to exclude an axis.
  • Use gizmo arrows for visual control; the center circle moves on the view plane.
Misconception

If your object moves along unexpected directions, check whether Transform Orientation is Local vs Global; local axes follow object rotation.

Example

To slide a floor tile into place without raising it, press G then X or Y to constrain movement and avoid changing height accidentally.

Key Takeaway

Use axis constraints immediately after pressing G to move accurately and prevent misaligned placements.

Pro Tip

When precision matters, type numeric offsets after constraining to an axis to move by exact amounts.
