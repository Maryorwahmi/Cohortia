Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Transform the Default Cube
Step-by-step Lab
  1. Select the default Cube; confirm the orange outline.
  2. Press G to move freely; Left-click to confirm the new location.
  3. Press G then X/Y/Z to constrain movement to a single axis.
  4. Press R to rotate; use R then Z/X/Y for axis-specific rotation.
  5. Press S to scale uniformly; press S then an axis to scale on one axis.
  6. Move the 3D Cursor with Shift+Right-click and use Object > Set Origin > Origin to 3D Cursor.
  7. Enable the magnet and test Increment and Vertex snapping while moving the cube.
Key Takeaway

Practice each step until the behaviors are predictable: selection, axis constraints, origin effects, and snapping.

Example Walkthrough

G then X moves only along X. R then Z rotates around the Z axis. S then Y scales on Y. Set origin to cursor to change pivot for rotation tests.

Checkpoint

After each change, press Alt+A to deselect and reselect the Cube to confirm the transform stuck as expected.
