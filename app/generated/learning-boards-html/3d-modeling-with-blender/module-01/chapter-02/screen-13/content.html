Avoid predictable errors

Simple checks and habits keep transforms reliable and assets ready for animation or export.

Mistake: Wrong selection

Confirm the orange outline and consider renaming or isolating the active object to prevent accidental edits.

Mistake: Wrong pivot

Check the origin before rotating. If rotation looks odd, set origin to geometry or cursor as needed.

Mistake: Unintended snapping

Verify the magnet icon when transforms jump unexpectedly. Use Ctrl for temporary snaps instead of leaving snapping on.

Pro Tips
  • Use Alt+A to clear selection before picking a new object.
  • Place the 3D Cursor at precise anchors and set origins for repeated pivot behavior.
  • Apply transforms where necessary before exporting to keep asset behavior consistent.
