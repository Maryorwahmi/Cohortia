Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Selection

Choose objects to edit; confirm orange outline before transforming.

3D Cursor

Sets where new objects appear and can act as a pivot when used as an origin target.

Transformations

Move, Rotate, Scale change position, orientation, and size; use axis constraints for accuracy.

Summary

These three concepts together allow consistent, precise control of scene assets and ensure predictable results in modeling and game workflows.
