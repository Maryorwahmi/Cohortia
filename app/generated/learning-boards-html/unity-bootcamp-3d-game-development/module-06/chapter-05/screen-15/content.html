Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › What to Remember
Plan delivery
Prepare identity, signing, and asset delivery to reduce friction during updates.
Measure responsibly
Track clear metrics, avoid PII, and respect opt-in consent where required.
Communicate openly
Publish changelogs, known issues, and invite community testing for high-impact fixes.
Final Reflection

Write three events you will track and one communication channel for patch notes. Small plans lead to better post-launch outcomes.
