Store comparison
Steam / Epic
Requires store page, builds for target OS, and often a review or publishing steps from the developer portal.
itch.io
Flexible packaging, often used for early prototypes or experimental builds; still needs clear instructions and assets.
PC Distribution Notes
Provide system requirements, recommended settings, and clear install instructions for players.
Practical tip
Run a platform dry-run: create a fresh account, upload the listing, and simulate a purchase or install to confirm the experience before your public release.
Packaging checklist
A short actionable checklist for PC store submissions.
Build targets
Windows / Mac / Linux builds where appropriate. Verify input mappings and controller support per OS.
Assets & Media
Store screenshots, a short trailer, and localized descriptions.
Testing
Run the build on clean machines and verify installer or portability expectations.
