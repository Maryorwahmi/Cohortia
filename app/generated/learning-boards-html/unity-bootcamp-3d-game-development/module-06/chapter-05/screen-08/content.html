Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Monetization: IAP vs Ads
In-App Purchases (IAP)
Sell consumables, non-consumables, or subscriptions. Best used for cosmetics or meaningful content that does not make paid players dominant.
Ethical considerations
  • Avoid pay-to-win purchases that break fair competition.
  • Be transparent about costs and what players receive.
Unity note: enable IAP in Window → General → Services to integrate with stores.
Advertisements (Ads)
Use banners, interstitials, or rewarded videos. Rewarded video is typically the least intrusive because players opt in for a benefit.
Ethical considerations
  • Favor opt-in rewarded ads over forced interstitials that interrupt gameplay.
  • Ensure ad rewards feel fair and are not exploitative.
Key Takeaway

Design monetization to match your game's values: cosmetics and opt-in ads sustain revenue without eroding trust.
