Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Practice & Community
Instrument key events
Choose a short event list: GameStart, LevelComplete, CollectiblePickup, PowerUpUse. Keep it small and actionable.
Mnemonic: MAP — Minimal, Actionable, Persistent
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Asset delivery & patches
Adopt Addressables or a content patch system to push updates without full reinstall on mobile and PC where possible.
Mnemonic: DELTA — Deliver, Ensure, Load, Test, Announce
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Fair monetization flows
Prefer cosmetic IAP and opt-in rewarded ads. Avoid pay-to-win mechanics that fracture the player base.
Mnemonic: OPT-IN — Offer, Permission, Transparent, Incentive, Non-intrusive
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Community & maintenance
Set up support channels, publish clear patch notes, and monitor feedback to prioritize fixes and features.
Mnemonic: HEAR — Host, Engage, Answer, Report
