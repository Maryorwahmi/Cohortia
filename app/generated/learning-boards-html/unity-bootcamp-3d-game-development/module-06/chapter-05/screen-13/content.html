Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Community & Support
Support channels
Help Desk / Tickets
Structured support for bug reports and account issues.
Community Forum / Discord
Open discussion, feedback, and beta testing groups.
Social & Announcements
Broad updates, release dates, and promotional content.
Changelog structure
A clear changelog helps players understand whether an update affects them and demonstrates progress.
What changed
Bug fixes, balance changes, new content with short bullet points.
Known issues
Short list of problems you are tracking and expected workarounds if available.
Community testing prompt
Invite players to test specific scenarios after an update and tell them how to report results. Example: "Please test level 3 checkpoint stability and report console logs if you see a crash."
Key Takeaway

Transparent, focused communication makes maintenance less stressful and makes players feel part of the process.
