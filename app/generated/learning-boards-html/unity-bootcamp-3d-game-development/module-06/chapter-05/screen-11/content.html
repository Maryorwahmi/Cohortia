Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Privacy & Compliance
Definition & rules
Privacy means minimizing personal data, explaining how you use it, and offering choices. Regulations like GDPR and CCPA require transparency and sometimes explicit consent. Use anonymized keys and avoid PII in event payloads.
Consent flow example
A simple opt-in toggle that, when disabled, prevents AnalyticsEvent.Custom calls from executing.
Quick checklist
  • Publish a clear privacy policy URL
  • Provide an opt-in/opt-out for analytics
  • Avoid PII in event parameters
  • Limit data retention where possible
Testing your analytics
Send controlled events and inspect values in the dashboard. Confirm no sensitive strings are sent and that opt-out prevents events.
Example parameters to send
Level ID, completion time in seconds, item type IDs. Avoid player email or username values.
Misconception check
Don't assume analytics are anonymous; event contents and identifiers determine sensitivity.
Key Takeaway

Design analytics with consent and minimal data. Privacy-first practices protect players and reduce legal risk.
