Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Post-Launch Support
Definition & lifecycle
Ongoing bug fixes, balance changes, new content, and community maintenance measured by player metrics.
Why it matters
  • Preserves player trust and reduces churn by addressing issues quickly.
  • Allows measured balancing informed by analytics rather than guesswork.
  • Extends lifespan through new content and engaged communities.
Common mistakes
Treating launch as final; shipping large untested fixes; failing to communicate with players.
Key Takeaway

Plan a cadence of updates, measure impact with analytics, and communicate changes to keep players informed.

A simple post-launch lifecycle
A repeatable mini-cycle that teams can run for each update.
1. Triage
Collect bug reports and analytics signals.
2. Dev
Fix, balance, or author new content in feature branches.
3. Test
QA, players or internal testers exercise the build.
4. Release
Publish patch with notes and monitor telemetry.
Concrete scenario
If retention drops on day 3, check level completion rates and recent changes. Ship a small balance tweak, monitor the analytics, and publish clear patch notes describing the change.
