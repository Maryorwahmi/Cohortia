Smooth Experience

Managing Your Game's Frame Rate

Setting and maintaining optimal frames per second for a consistent player experience.

Consistency Over Peaks

A steady 30 FPS is often preferred over a fluctuating 60-15 FPS for player comfort.

Application.targetFrameRate

Programmatically set your game's desired frames per second for explicit control.

Platform-Specific Targets

Aim for 30 FPS on mobile for battery, 60 FPS on PC for fluidity.

The Frame Rate Curve

A consistent frame rate, even if lower, provides a much better user experience than one that fluctuates wildly between high and low values.

Key Takeaway

Use Application.targetFrameRate to ensure a stable and predictable gameplay experience across all target platforms.
