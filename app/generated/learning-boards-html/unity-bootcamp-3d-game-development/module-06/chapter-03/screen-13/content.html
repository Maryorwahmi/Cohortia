Best Practices

The Iterative Optimization Process

A continuous cycle of profiling, identifying, implementing, and re-profiling for peak performance.

Profile & Identify

Use the Profiler to find the biggest bottleneck; focus on areas with the highest resource consumption.

Implement & Re-profile

Apply a targeted solution, then re-profile to verify its impact and ensure no new issues were introduced.

Avoid Premature Optimization

Optimize only when a bottleneck is clearly identified; focus on functionality first, then performance.

The Optimization Loop

This continuous feedback loop ensures that your optimization efforts are always effective and targeted, leading to a robust and high-performing game.

Key Takeaway

Treat optimization as an ongoing, data-driven process to consistently improve your game's performance.
