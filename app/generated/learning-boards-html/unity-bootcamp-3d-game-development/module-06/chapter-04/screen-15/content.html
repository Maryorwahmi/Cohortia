Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › What to Remember
Measure
Use the Profiler to identify hotspots before changing anything.
Reduce at source
Adjust import settings and LODs to cut memory and build size.
Reuse at runtime
Implement pooling and avoid allocations in hot loops to reduce GC pauses.
Final takeaway

Optimization is iterative: change one thing, measure, and repeat until your game runs reliably on your target devices.
