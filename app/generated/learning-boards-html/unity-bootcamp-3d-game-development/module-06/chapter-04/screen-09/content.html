Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Instantiate vs Pooling
Instantiate / Destroy
Simple, minimal upfront memory, but creates allocations each use. Best for rare or unique objects.
Problems under load
Many Instantiate/Destroy cycles cause frequent GC.Collect spikes and frame stutter.
Object Pooling
Higher baseline memory, minimal allocations during gameplay, and more consistent frame times. Best for bullets, particles, and common spawnable objects.
Design choices
Decide on pool size, expansion policy, and thorough state reset when returning objects to the pool.
Check your understanding

When would you prefer Instantiate/Destroy over pooling? Answer: for rare or unique objects where pooling overhead isn't justified.
