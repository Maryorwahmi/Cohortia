Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
Asset Optimization
Reduce size and memory of textures, meshes, and audio without losing necessary quality.
LOD & Mip Maps
Use mip maps for textures and LODs for meshes to reduce GPU work at distance.
Object Pooling
Pre-allocate and reuse objects to avoid frequent allocations and GC spikes.
Takeaway

Measure first, change one thing at a time, and verify with the Profiler to ensure real performance gains.
