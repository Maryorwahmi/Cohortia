Core Construct

Understanding the ACI Tenant

Secure and Isolated Logical Containers

Highest-Level Container

The top-most logical partition for organizing ACI policies and resources.

Multi-Tenancy Enabled

Allows multiple independent environments to share the same physical ACI fabric.

Strict Isolation

Each Tenant has its own policies and resources, completely separate from others.

Tenant Analogy: The Apartment Building

Physical ACI Fabric

The entire apartment building structure.

Each Tenant

A separate apartment unit with its own rules, privacy, and resources.

Key Takeaway

Tenants are fundamental for logical separation, enabling secure multi-tenancy and independent policy management within ACI.
