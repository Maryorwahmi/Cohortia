Core Construct

Endpoint Groups (EPGs): The Application-Centric View

Grouping Workloads by Policy Requirements

Collection of Endpoints

Groups VMs, bare-metal servers, containers, or IP devices with shared policy needs.

Application-Centric Policy

Policies are applied to EPGs, simplifying management over individual IPs/VLANs.

Foundation of Micro-segmentation

Enables granular security by controlling communication between groups.

EPG Example: Acme Corp Application Tiers

EPG_Web_Servers

All web servers share common policies.

EPG_App_Servers

All application servers share common policies.

Key Takeaway

EPGs are the core of ACI's application-centric policy model, grouping endpoints to simplify policy application and enable micro-segmentation.
