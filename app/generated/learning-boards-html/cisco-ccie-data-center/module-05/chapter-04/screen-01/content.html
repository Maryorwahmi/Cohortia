Policy Enforcement and Contracts in ACI

Securing Communication Between Endpoint Groups

In Cisco ACI, Endpoint Groups (EPGs) are isolated by default. Contracts are the explicit rules that define and permit communication between these EPGs, establishing a robust whitelist security model. This chapter will guide you through understanding, building, and applying these essential policy constructs.
