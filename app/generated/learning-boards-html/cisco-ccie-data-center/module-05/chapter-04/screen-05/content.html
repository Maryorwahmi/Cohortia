Provider and Consumer EPGs

Defining the direction of service access

Web EPGNeeds service
Consumes Contract
App EPGProvides service
Consumes Contract
DB EPGProvides service
Key Takeaway

The provider offers a service, and the consumer accesses it, establishing a clear, directional policy flow.

Concrete Example

A 'Web EPG' consumes a contract from an 'App EPG' to access application services.

Common Mistake

Applying the contract in the wrong direction (e.g., consumer provides) will lead to communication failures.
