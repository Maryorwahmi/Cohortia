Avoiding Common Contract Pitfalls

Ensuring successful policy deployment

Mistake: Forgetting to Apply

EPGs remain isolated if a contract is defined but not explicitly applied between them.

Mistake: Wrong Direction

Configuring the provider as consumer or vice-versa will block traffic flow.

Mistake: Incorrect Filters

Mismatched protocols (e.g., UDP instead of TCP) or incorrect port numbers will block traffic.

Best Practice: Test Thoroughly

Always verify contract functionality after deployment to ensure intended communication.
