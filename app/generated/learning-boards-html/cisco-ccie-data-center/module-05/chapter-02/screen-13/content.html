Key Concepts

Review the fundamental terms and ideas from this chapter.

Leaf-Spine Topology

A two-tier network architecture where every leaf switch connects to every spine switch, providing high bandwidth, low latency, and predictable paths.

Leaf Switch

An access layer switch in the ACI fabric that connects to end-point devices (servers, storage, etc.) and to all spine switches.

Spine Switch

A core layer switch in the ACI fabric that connects to all leaf switches and provides high-speed inter-leaf connectivity.

Key Takeaway

The Leaf-Spine topology, with its distinct Leaf and Spine switch roles, forms the highly scalable and resilient physical foundation of the Cisco ACI fabric.
