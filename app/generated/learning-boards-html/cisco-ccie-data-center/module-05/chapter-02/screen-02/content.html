Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Identify Core Components

Recognize and describe the fundamental hardware and software elements that constitute a Cisco ACI fabric. This skill is vital for understanding the physical and logical structure of the network.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Explain APIC Roles & Functions

Articulate the various responsibilities of the Application Policy Infrastructure Controller, from centralized management to policy enforcement. This understanding is key to grasping ACI's policy-driven automation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Understand Leaf-Spine Topology

Grasp the significance of the Leaf-Spine network design, including its benefits for scalability, redundancy, and predictable latency in data center environments.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Describe Underlay & Overlay Networks

Differentiate between the physical underlay and virtual overlay networks, explaining how they work together to provide flexible connectivity. This is fundamental to ACI's network virtualization capabilities.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
