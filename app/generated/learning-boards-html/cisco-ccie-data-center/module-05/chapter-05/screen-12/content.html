OOB Management for Fabric Switches

Ensuring a Critical Lifeline

Core Principle

Independent Management Path

A separate OOB network for leaf and spine switches provides a critical management path that is independent of the ACI fabric's data plane and control plane operations.

Configuration
  • Configure dedicated management interfaces (e.g., mgmt0) on Nexus 9000 switches.
  • Assign IP addresses on your OOB network to these interfaces.
  • Ensure network connectivity to the OOB network from your management workstation.
Key Takeaway

Robust OOB management for fabric switches is a non-negotiable best practice, providing essential access for troubleshooting and maintaining control during critical events.
