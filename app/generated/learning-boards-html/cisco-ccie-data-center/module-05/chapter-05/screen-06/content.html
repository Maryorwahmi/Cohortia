Fabric Discovery with LLDP

How APIC Finds Leaf and Spine Switches

Core Protocol

Link Layer Discovery Protocol (LLDP)

A vendor-neutral Layer 2 protocol used by network devices to advertise their identity, capabilities, and neighbors on a local area network. In ACI, it's key for APIC to discover switches.

Discovery Process
  • Leaf/spine switches power on and connect to the fabric.
  • They send LLDP messages advertising their presence.
  • APIC receives LLDP, identifies the switch, and prompts for registration.
  • Administrator assigns unique Fabric ID and Node ID.
Key Takeaway

LLDP is the fundamental mechanism for APIC to automatically discover and inventory physical leaf and spine switches, simplifying initial fabric setup.
