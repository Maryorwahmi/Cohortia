Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

LLDP

Link Layer Discovery Protocol, a Layer 2 protocol used by APIC to discover leaf and spine switches.

Fabric Access Policies

Policies defining how external devices connect to leaf switches, including physical domains, VLAN pools, and interface policies.

Physical Domain

A logical grouping of physical infrastructure components (e.g., servers, firewalls) connecting to the ACI fabric.

Summary Takeaway

LLDP facilitates automated discovery, while Fabric Access Policies and Physical Domains are crucial for integrating and managing external devices within the ACI fabric.
