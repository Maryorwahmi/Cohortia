Physical Domains and VLAN Pools

Categorizing Infrastructure and Resources

Component 1

Physical Domains

Logical groupings of physical infrastructure components (e.g., bare-metal servers, firewalls) that connect to the ACI fabric. They help categorize and apply consistent policies to similar device types.

Example: phys_domain_servers, phys_domain_firewalls
Component 2

VLAN Pools

Ranges of VLAN IDs reserved for use by specific physical or virtual domains within the ACI fabric. These pools allow ACI to dynamically assign VLANs to endpoints as needed.

Example: vlan_pool_prod_servers (VLANs 100-200)
Key Takeaway

Physical Domains and VLAN Pools establish the foundational categorization and resource allocation for external device connectivity within the ACI fabric.
