Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

OOB Management

A dedicated network path for managing devices, independent of the data plane, used for initial setup and troubleshooting.

APIC Cluster

A group of 3, 5, or 7 APIC controllers working together for high availability and redundancy in managing the ACI fabric.

Fabric Discovery

The process by which APIC controllers identify and register physical leaf and spine switches connected to the fabric, typically using LLDP.

Summary Takeaway

The initial setup of an ACI fabric relies on robust OOB management, a highly available APIC cluster, and automated fabric discovery to bring the network under centralized control.
