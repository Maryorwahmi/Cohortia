The Power of Policy-Driven Networking

Desired State, Declarative Model, and the APIC

1. Define Policy

Specify desired application behavior (declarative).

2. APIC Translates

APIC converts high-level policy to low-level configs.

3. Fabric Enforces

Network devices automatically apply configurations.

Key Takeaway

ACI's policy-driven, declarative model, orchestrated by the APIC, automates network configuration and ensures consistent enforcement, minimizing errors and accelerating deployments.
