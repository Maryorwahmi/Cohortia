Core Concept

Understanding Control and Data Planes

The Brain and Brawn of Network Devices

Control Plane (The Brain)

  • Makes forwarding decisions (e.g., routing protocols, policy enforcement).
  • Builds and maintains network topology information.
  • In SDN, this is centralized in the controller.
Traditional vs. SDN

In traditional networks, control and data planes are integrated per device. SDN separates them, centralizing control.

Data Plane (The Brawn)

  • Performs actual packet forwarding based on control plane decisions.
  • High-speed hardware for moving data.
  • In SDN, devices become simple forwarding engines.
Key Takeaway

SDN's separation of control and data planes enables centralized intelligence and simplified, efficient packet forwarding across the network.
