Core Concept

The Limitations of Traditional Data Center Networks

Manual Configuration, Complexity, and Slow Agility

Manual CLI Configuration

Each device requires individual command-line interface configuration, leading to repetitive and time-consuming tasks across the network.

Distributed Control Plane

Decision-making logic is spread across many devices, making a holistic network view and coordinated changes difficult.

Error-Prone Operations

Manual configurations increase the likelihood of human errors, leading to network misconfigurations and potential outages.

Slow to Scale & Adapt

Scaling networks or adapting to new application requirements becomes a slow and cumbersome process due to manual intervention.

Key Takeaway

Traditional networking's device-centric, manual approach creates significant bottlenecks in agility, scalability, and error reduction for modern data centers.

Real-World Connection

Imagine updating firewall rules across 500 devices manually; this is the reality traditional networks faced, highlighting the need for a new approach.
