Nexus Family Overview

Nexus 5000, 3000, and 2000 FEX Series

Explore compact, high-density solutions for top-of-rack deployments and simplified access layers.

Nexus 5000 & 3000 Series: Top-of-Rack

  • •
    Primary Use: Compact, high-density Top-of-Rack (ToR) deployments.
  • •
    Features: Nexus 5000 supports Fibre Channel over Ethernet (FCoE) for converged networks.
  • •
    Benefit: Minimizes cabling and simplifies management in server racks.

Nexus 2000 Series: Fabric Extenders (FEX)

  • •
    Role: Acts as remote line cards for parent Nexus switches (e.g., 5000, 9000).
  • •
    Function: Extends parent switch ports into server racks.
  • •
    Benefit: Simplifies cabling and provides single-point management for large access layers.
Key Takeaway

The Nexus 5000, 3000, and 2000 FEX series provide specialized, efficient solutions for top-of-rack and access layer deployments, enhancing data center flexibility and manageability.
