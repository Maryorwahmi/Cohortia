Introducing Cisco Nexus Switches

Purpose-built for data centers, Nexus switches offer unparalleled density, low latency, and advanced features.

Nexus vs. Catalyst: Key Differences

  • •
    Purpose-Built: Nexus for data centers, Catalyst for campus/access layers.
  • •
    Performance: Nexus offers higher density, lower latency, and greater bandwidth.
  • •
    OS: Nexus runs NX-OS, Catalyst runs IOS (or IOS XE).
  • •
    Advanced Features: Nexus supports VXLAN, ACI, FCoE natively.
