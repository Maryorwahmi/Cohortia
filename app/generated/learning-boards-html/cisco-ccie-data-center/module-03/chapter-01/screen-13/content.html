Key Concepts

Review the fundamental terms and ideas from this chapter.

Cisco Nexus Switches

A family of high-performance switches designed for data center environments, optimized for density, low latency, and advanced features.

Modular Switch

A chassis-based switch where components like line cards, supervisor engines, and power supplies can be added or replaced, offering flexibility and scalability.

Fixed-Form-Factor Switch

A switch with a fixed set of ports and integrated components, typically more compact and cost-effective for specific deployment roles.

Summary Takeaway

Nexus switches are purpose-built for data centers, offering specialized architectures (modular for scalability, fixed for compactness) to meet diverse networking needs.
