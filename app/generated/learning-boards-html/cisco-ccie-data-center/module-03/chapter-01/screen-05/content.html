Nexus Family Overview

Nexus 9000 and 7000 Series

Discover the flagship Nexus series for spine-and-leaf architectures and highly modular core deployments.

Nexus 9000 Series: Versatility for Spine-and-Leaf

  • •
    Primary Use: Spine-and-leaf architectures.
  • •
    Operating Modes: Supports traditional NX-OS and Cisco ACI modes.
  • •
    Example: Nexus 9500 chassis for high-density spine roles.
  • •
    Benefit: Highly adaptable for evolving data center needs.

Nexus 7000 Series: Modular Core & Aggregation

  • •
    Primary Use: Core and aggregation layers.
  • •
    Architecture: Highly modular chassis-based system.
  • •
    Scalability: Extensive, with hot-swappable line cards, supervisors, and power supplies.
  • •
    Benefit: Unmatched flexibility and redundancy for large-scale deployments.
Key Takeaway

The Nexus 9000 and 7000 series provide foundational capabilities for scalable and resilient data center networks, offering versatility and modularity respectively.
