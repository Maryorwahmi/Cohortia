Hardware Spotlight

Cisco MDS 9000 Series Switches

Purpose-built Fibre Channel switches for enterprise SANs.

Role in SANs

The Cisco MDS 9000 Series are dedicated Fibre Channel switches that form the core fabric of enterprise Storage Area Networks, connecting servers to storage arrays.

Key Features

  • High Performance & Density: Industry-leading throughput and port scalability.
  • Advanced Services: Intelligent network services for SAN management.
  • Integrated Security: Robust security features built into the hardware and software.
  • High Availability: Designed for continuous operation with redundancy.

Operating System: Cisco NX-OS

MDS switches run Cisco NX-OS, a robust operating system with Fibre Channel extensions, providing a consistent management experience with other Cisco data center products.

MDS Form Factors

Available as modular director-class chassis (e.g., MDS 9700 series) for large-scale deployments and fixed-form-factor switches (e.g., MDS 9100 series) for smaller fabrics.

Key Takeaway

Cisco MDS 9000 Series switches are purpose-built, high-performance Fibre Channel platforms essential for scalable, secure, and highly available enterprise SANs.
