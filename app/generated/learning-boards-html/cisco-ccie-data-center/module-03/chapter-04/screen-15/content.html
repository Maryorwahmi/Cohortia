Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

Storage Area Network (SAN)

A dedicated high-speed network providing block-level access to consolidated storage devices from multiple servers.

Fibre Channel (FC)

A high-performance, low-latency protocol specifically designed for block storage traffic over dedicated fiber optic cables.

Fibre Channel over Ethernet (FCoE)

Encapsulates Fibre Channel frames within Ethernet frames, allowing FC traffic to run over standard Ethernet infrastructure for converged networks.

Cisco MDS 9000 Series

Cisco's family of purpose-built Fibre Channel switches and directors, offering high performance, density, and advanced SAN features.

SAN Zoning

A security mechanism that restricts which devices (HBAs, storage ports) can communicate with each other within the SAN.

Summary Takeaway

SANs are vital for enterprise storage, leveraging Fibre Channel or FCoE, and managed by powerful switches like the Cisco MDS series, with zoning providing essential security.
