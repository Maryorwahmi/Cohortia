Common Mistakes & Best Practices in SAN Configuration

Avoiding pitfalls for a stable and secure SAN environment.

Common Mistake: Incorrect Zoning

Misconfigured zoning can lead to servers being unable to access their assigned storage, or worse, gaining unauthorized access to other storage resources, posing a significant security risk.

Impact of Zoning Errors

  • Service Outages: Applications cannot access their data.
  • Data Corruption: Accidental writes to incorrect LUNs.
  • Security Breaches: Unauthorized data access.
  • Troubleshooting Difficulty: Hard to diagnose without proper tools.

Best Practices

  • Verify Zoning: Always double-check configurations after changes.
  • Handle Fiber Optic Cables Carefully: Avoid sharp bends and keep connectors clean.
  • Strict Change Management: Follow processes for all production SAN changes.
  • Proactive Monitoring: Regularly check SAN health and performance.
Key Takeaway

Meticulous zoning verification, careful physical handling, and strict change control are essential to prevent common SAN issues and maintain stability.
