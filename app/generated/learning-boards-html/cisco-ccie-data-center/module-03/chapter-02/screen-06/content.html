Server Form Factors

UCS C-Series Rack Servers

Traditional rack-mountable servers with UCS management capabilities.

Key Advantages

  • Flexibility: Traditional 1U or 2U rack-mountable form factor.
  • Local Storage: Supports specific local storage configurations (e.g., large disk arrays).
  • GPU Acceleration: Ideal for workloads requiring dedicated GPUs.
  • Integration: Can integrate into existing rack infrastructure without a blade chassis.
Key Takeaway

UCS C-Series rack servers offer traditional form factor flexibility for specific hardware needs, while still benefiting from UCS centralized management.
