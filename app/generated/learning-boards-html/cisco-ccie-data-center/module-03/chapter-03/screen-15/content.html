Key Concepts

Further Essential Takeaways from HyperFlex Explained

Three-Tier Architecture

A traditional data center architecture with separate compute (servers), storage (SAN/NAS), and networking (switches) layers.

Linear Scalability

The ability to increase compute and storage capacity by adding more nodes to an HCI cluster, with performance scaling proportionally.

Virtual Desktop Infrastructure (VDI)

A common use case for HCI, where desktop OS and applications are hosted on central servers and accessed remotely.

Remote Office/Branch Office (ROBO)

Another common use case for HCI, providing simplified, resilient infrastructure for smaller, distributed locations.

Summary Takeaway

HyperFlex offers a compelling alternative to traditional architectures, providing linear scalability and excelling in use cases like VDI and ROBO due to its integrated and simplified design.
