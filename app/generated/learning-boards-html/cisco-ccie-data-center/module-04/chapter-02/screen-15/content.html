Ensuring a Robust ACI Deployment

Key considerations for planning, high availability, and configuration management.

Critical Best Practices

  • •Ensure APIC cluster high availability (3 or 5 controllers).
  • •Implement robust backup and restore procedures for APIC configuration.
  • •Understand and embrace the policy model and application-centric approach.
  • •Plan the underlay network meticulously for performance and reliability.
Key Takeaway

Successful ACI deployments rely on APIC redundancy, diligent backups, and a firm commitment to the application-centric policy model.

Embrace Application-Centric Design

The most critical best practice is to fully adopt the application-centric mindset. Design your EPGs and Contracts around the needs of your applications, rather than trying to map traditional network constructs. This unlocks ACI's true power.
