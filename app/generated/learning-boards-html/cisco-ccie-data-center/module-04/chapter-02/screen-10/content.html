The ACI Fabric: Spine-Leaf and APIC

Understanding the physical and control plane of Cisco ACI.

Spine-Leaf Architecture

  • •Leaf switches connect directly to servers and endpoints.
  • •Spine switches interconnect all Leaf switches.
  • •No direct Leaf-to-Leaf or Spine-to-Spine connectivity (typically).
  • •Ensures high bandwidth and low latency.
Key Takeaway

The Spine-Leaf architecture provides the physical foundation, while the APIC delivers centralized, policy-driven control for the ACI fabric.

APIC: The Central Brain

APIC (Controller)
ACI Fabric (Spine-Leaf)

Role of the APIC

The APIC is the centralized management and policy enforcement point. It translates high-level application policies into concrete network configurations, pushing them to the Leaf switches. This eliminates manual configuration and ensures consistent policy application across the entire fabric.
