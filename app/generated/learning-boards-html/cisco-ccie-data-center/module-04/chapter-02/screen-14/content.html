Common ACI Implementation Challenges

Understanding common mistakes and their impact on network virtualization.

Key Challenges to Avoid

  • •Applying a traditional network configuration mindset to ACI.
  • •Attempting to force traditional VLAN-centric designs into ACI.
  • •Neglecting proper planning and design for the underlay network.
  • •Underestimating the importance of an application-centric approach.
Key Takeaway

Avoid common pitfalls by embracing ACI's policy-driven model and ensuring a robust underlay network, rather than forcing traditional designs.

Mistake: Traditional Mindset

Trying to configure ACI like a traditional switch, port by port, or VLAN by VLAN, will lead to complexity and negate its automation benefits. ACI requires defining policies based on application needs, not physical topology.

Pitfall: Neglecting the Underlay

While ACI uses an overlay, the underlying IP network is its foundation. A poorly designed or unstable underlay will directly impact the performance and reliability of your entire ACI fabric. Ensure the underlay is robust and well-planned.
