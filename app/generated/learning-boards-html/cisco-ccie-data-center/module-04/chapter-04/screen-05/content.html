Management vs. Orchestration

From Automation to Coordinated Workflows

Management

  • Focuses on automating individual tasks or components.
  • Provides a unified control plane for specific domains (e.g., server, network).
  • Aims to simplify routine operations and configuration within a single system.
  • Example: Provisioning a single VM, configuring a specific firewall rule.
Key Characteristic

Task-centric automation within a specific domain.

Orchestration

  • Coordinates multiple automated tasks across different systems.
  • Achieves larger, end-to-end workflows or business processes.
  • Integrates disparate components (compute, network, storage) into a cohesive whole.
  • Example: Deploying a 3-tier application, including VM, network, and storage setup.
Key Characteristic

Workflow-centric automation across multiple domains.

Key Takeaway

While management automates individual tasks, orchestration coordinates these tasks across systems to achieve complex, end-to-end business processes, significantly enhancing agility.
