Intersight provides a cloud-native, AI-driven platform for managing distributed Cisco UCS and HyperFlex infrastructure.
Key Benefits

Cloud-Native Advantages

  • Global visibility for distributed deployments.
  • Intelligent analytics and proactive support (ML/AI).
  • Simplified lifecycle management.
  • SaaS model, no on-premises management servers.
Orchestration Module

Build and automate complex workflows with cloud-scale benefits and integration with other cloud services, extending capabilities through a rich API.

Key Takeaway

Cisco Intersight offers a powerful cloud-based solution for managing distributed UCS and HyperFlex, leveraging AI and a SaaS model for enhanced visibility and automation.
