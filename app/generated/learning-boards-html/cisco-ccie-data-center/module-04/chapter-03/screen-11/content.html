Virtual SANs (VSANs) for Logical Segmentation

Partitioning a physical SAN fabric into independent logical SANs.

Benefits of VSANs

  • Enhanced Security: Isolates traffic between different environments.
  • Fault Isolation: Contains issues within a single VSAN, preventing broader impact.
  • Simplified Management: Allows independent management of logical SANs.
  • Resource Optimization: Efficiently utilizes physical MDS switch hardware.
  • Multi-Tenancy Support: Ideal for service providers or large enterprises with diverse needs.
Example

A VSAN for production servers, another for development, and a third for disaster recovery, all on one physical MDS switch.

Key Takeaway

VSANs are critical for creating secure, resilient, and manageable SAN environments by logically segmenting the physical fabric.
