Fibre Channel over Ethernet (FCoE) for Convergence

Encapsulating FC traffic within Ethernet for unified network infrastructure.

Benefits of FCoE

  • Infrastructure Simplification: Reduces cabling and adapter cards.
  • Cost Reduction: Lowers power consumption and operational expenses.
  • Unified Network: Both LAN and SAN traffic share the same physical Ethernet infrastructure.
  • Performance: Leverages high-speed Ethernet (10GbE+), maintaining FC's lossless nature.
  • Integration: Natively supported in environments like Cisco UCS.
Example

In a Cisco UCS environment, Fabric Interconnects handle both Ethernet and FCoE traffic, connecting servers to both LAN and SAN.

Key Takeaway

FCoE streamlines data center networking by converging SAN and LAN traffic, offering efficiency without compromising storage reliability.
