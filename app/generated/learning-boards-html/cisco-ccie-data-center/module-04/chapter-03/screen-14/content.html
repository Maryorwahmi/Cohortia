Best Practices and Common Pitfalls in SAN Configuration

Ensuring resilience, security, and avoiding common errors in SAN deployments.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Zoning Misconfiguration: Incorrect WWN entries or inactive zone sets.
  • Lack of Redundancy: Single points of failure in paths or hardware.
  • Inadequate Planning: Not accounting for growth or disaster recovery.
  • Poor Documentation: Lack of clear records for configurations and changes.
Best Practices
  • Implement redundant paths (multi-pathing).
  • Use redundant MDS switches.
  • Ensure proper power and cooling.
  • Regularly back up MDS configurations.
  • Thoroughly verify all zoning changes.
Key Takeaway

Proactive planning, redundancy, and meticulous verification are crucial for building a robust, secure, and high-performance SAN infrastructure.
