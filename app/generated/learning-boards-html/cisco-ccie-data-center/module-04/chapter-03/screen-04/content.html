Core Concept

Understanding Storage Virtualization

Abstracting physical storage for enhanced flexibility and management.

What is Storage Virtualization?

  • Pools physical storage from multiple devices into a single, logical resource.
  • Presents storage to servers as logical volumes, independent of underlying hardware.
  • Decouples storage from physical disks (RAID, SSDs, HDDs from various vendors).
  • Enables central management and provisioning of storage resources.
Key Takeaway

Storage virtualization creates a flexible, dynamic storage fabric that improves utilization and simplifies management in virtualized data centers.

Real-World Impact

Allows non-disruptive data migration between arrays and capacity expansion without server downtime, crucial for business continuity.
