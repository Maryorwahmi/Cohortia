Deep Dive into Storage Area Networks (SAN)

Exploring the dedicated, high-speed network for block-level storage access.

Key Characteristics of SAN

  • Dedicated Network: Isolated from regular LAN traffic for optimal performance.
  • Block-Level Access: Storage appears as local disks to servers, enabling raw data access.
  • High Performance: Designed for low latency and high throughput.
  • Protocols: Primarily uses Fibre Channel (FC) or Fibre Channel over Ethernet (FCoE).
  • Scalability: Easily expandable to accommodate growing storage needs.
Example Use Case

Virtual machine boot volumes and high-transaction databases benefit immensely from SAN's direct, high-speed block access.

Key Takeaway

SANs are the backbone for performance-critical applications, offering unparalleled speed and reliability through dedicated block-level access.
