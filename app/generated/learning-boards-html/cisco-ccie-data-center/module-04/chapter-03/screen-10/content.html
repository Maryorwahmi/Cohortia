Cisco MDS Switches: The SAN Backbone

Purpose-built switches for high-performance, scalable Fibre Channel SANs.

Key Features of Cisco MDS Switches

  • Modular Design: Allows for flexible expansion and upgrades.
  • High Availability: Ensures continuous operation with redundant components.
  • Multi-Speed FC Support: Accommodates various Fibre Channel speeds (e.g., 8Gbps, 16Gbps, 32Gbps).
  • Virtual SANs (VSANs): Logical partitioning of the physical SAN fabric for isolation.
  • Advanced Features: Offers robust security, management, and diagnostic capabilities.
Example

An MDS switch hosts multiple VSANs, logically separating production, development, and disaster recovery SANs on the same hardware.

Key Takeaway

Cisco MDS switches are essential for building scalable, secure, and highly available Fibre Channel SANs, especially with VSAN capabilities.
