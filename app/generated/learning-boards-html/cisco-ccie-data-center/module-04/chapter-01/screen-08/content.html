Cisco UCS Overview

Cisco UCS: A Unified Computing Platform

Integrating compute, networking, and storage access.

Key UCS Components

  • •
    Fabric Interconnects (FIs): Central management and I/O hub.
  • •
    UCS B-Series: Blade servers housed in chassis.
  • •
    UCS C-Series: Rack-mount servers.

Converged Infrastructure

UCS converges LAN, SAN, and management traffic, simplifying cabling and providing a unified management plane for all compute resources.

Key Takeaway

Cisco UCS integrates compute, networking, and storage through Fabric Interconnects, offering a simplified, scalable, and flexible platform for data centers.
