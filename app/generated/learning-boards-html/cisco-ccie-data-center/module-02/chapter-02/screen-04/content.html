The Scalability Challenge of VLANs

Why traditional VLANs fall short in modern data centers

Limited VLAN ID Space

Only 4094 available VLAN IDs, insufficient for thousands of VMs or multi-tenant environments.

Confined Layer 2 Domains

VLANs are restricted to a single Layer 2 broadcast domain, making L2 extension across Layer 3 networks complex.

Operational Complexity

Extending VLANs across Layer 3 often requires inefficient solutions, increasing management overhead.

Key Takeaway

While foundational, VLANs' inherent limitations in ID space and Layer 2 confinement necessitate advanced virtualization for modern data centers.
