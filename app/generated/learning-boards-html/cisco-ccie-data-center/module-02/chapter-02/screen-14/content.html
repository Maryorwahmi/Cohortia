Core Virtualization Technologies

Recalling VXLAN, VTEP, VNI, and VRF

VXLAN

Encapsulates Layer 2 frames in Layer 3 UDP packets, extending L2 domains across IP networks.

VTEP & VNI

VTEPs perform encapsulation; VNIs provide 24-bit logical segmentation (16M+ segments).

VRF

Allows multiple independent routing tables on a single device for Layer 3 segmentation and multi-tenancy.

Summary Takeaway

VXLAN and VRF are critical for building scalable, secure, and flexible multi-tenant data center networks, overcoming traditional networking limitations.
