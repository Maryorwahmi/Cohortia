Streamlining VXLAN with BGP EVPN

Automating MAC and VNI learning for scalability

BGP EVPNControl Plane for VXLAN
Automated MAC Learning

VNI-to-MAC Distribution

Efficiently distributes VNI and MAC mappings across the fabric.

Improved Scalability

Supports larger numbers of endpoints and logical networks.

Reduced Flooding

Key Takeaway

BGP EVPN provides a scalable control plane for VXLAN, automating MAC and VNI learning to enhance efficiency and reduce flooding in large data center networks.
