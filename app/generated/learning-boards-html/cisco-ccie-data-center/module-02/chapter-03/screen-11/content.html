OTV vs. EVPN: Choosing the Right DCI Technology

Understanding the Differences for Your DCI Use Case

OTV Characteristics
  • Simplicity: Easier to deploy for pure Layer 2 extension.
  • Control Plane: IS-IS based, specifically for MAC reachability.
  • Use Case: Ideal for quick, robust Layer 2 DCI without internal BGP EVPN.
  • Encapsulation: Proprietary OTV encapsulation over IP.
OTV Common Mistake

Misconfiguring the join interface's IP reachability or incorrect VLAN mapping can prevent OTV from establishing the overlay.

EVPN Characteristics
  • Flexibility: Unified L2/L3 VPN services, highly scalable.
  • Control Plane: BGP based, advertises MAC, IP, VNIs.
  • Use Case: Preferred with existing VXLAN, advanced L3 VPN needs.
  • Encapsulation: Supports VXLAN, MPLS, NVGRE.
EVPN Common Mistake

Incorrect BGP peering, misconfigured Route Distinguishers (RDs) or Route Targets (RTs), or VNI mismatches will break EVPN DCI.

Key Takeaway

OTV is simpler for dedicated Layer 2 extension, while EVPN offers a more comprehensive, flexible, and scalable framework for integrated Layer 2 and Layer 3 VPN services, especially with VXLAN.
