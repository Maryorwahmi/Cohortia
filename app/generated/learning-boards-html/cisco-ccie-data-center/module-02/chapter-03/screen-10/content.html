Introducing EVPN for Data Center Interconnect

A Unified Control Plane with BGP and VXLAN

What is EVPN?

EVPN (Ethernet VPN) is a modern control plane technology, often combined with VXLAN, providing unified Layer 2 and Layer 3 VPN services for DCI.

BGP Control Plane
  • Uses BGP to advertise MAC addresses, IP addresses, and VNIs.
  • Eliminates need for multicast in underlay for BUM traffic.
  • Offers superior traffic engineering and faster convergence.
Key Takeaway

EVPN, often combined with VXLAN, provides a highly scalable and flexible DCI solution by leveraging BGP as a unified control plane for Layer 2 and Layer 3 VPN services.

EVPN uses BGP to exchange reachability information, extending VXLAN segments across the DCI underlay.
