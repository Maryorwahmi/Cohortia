DCI Deployment: Common Pitfalls and Safety Considerations

Ensuring Robust and Secure Data Center Interconnect

OTV Common Pitfalls
  • Misconfigured join-interface (IP reachability issues).
  • Incorrectly mapped extended-vlan-list.
  • Multicast routing not enabled/working in underlay.
EVPN Common Pitfalls
  • Incorrect BGP peering configurations.
  • Misconfigured Route Distinguishers (RDs) or Route Targets (RTs).
  • Inconsistent VXLAN VNI mappings across sites.
General DCI Safety Notes
  • Careful planning of IP addressing.
  • Robust security measures for DCI links (encryption, access control).
  • Thorough testing of failover scenarios.
  • Proactive monitoring and regular audits.
Key Takeaway

Careful planning, correct configuration of interfaces and control plane elements, robust security measures, and thorough failover testing are crucial for successful and safe DCI deployments.
