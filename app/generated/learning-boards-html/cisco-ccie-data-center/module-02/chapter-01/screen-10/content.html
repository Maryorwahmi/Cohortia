Avoiding Common Pitfalls in Spine-Leaf Deployment

Ensuring a Stable and High-Performing Network

Mistakes to Avoid

  • •Implementing STP: Negates ECMP benefits; STP should be disabled on Layer 3 links.
  • •Improper Routing: Failing to configure robust routing protocols (OSPF/BGP) between Leaf and Spine.
  • •Lack of Validation: Skipping lab testing before production deployment can lead to instability.
Key Takeaway

Proper routing protocol configuration and disabling STP are critical for a functional and efficient spine-leaf network.

Avoid STP

STP blocks paths, conflicting with ECMP.

Configure Routing

Ensure OSPF/BGP adjacencies are robust.

Validate in Lab

Test configurations before production deployment.
