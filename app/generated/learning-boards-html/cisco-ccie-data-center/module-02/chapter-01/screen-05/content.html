Limitations of Three-Tier for Modern Data Centers

Why Traditional Designs Struggle with New Traffic Patterns

Challenges Faced

  • •East-West Traffic Inefficiency: Poorly optimized for server-to-server communication within the data center.
  • •Oversubscription: Bottlenecks at aggregation/core layers as traffic scales.
  • •STP Limitations: Spanning Tree Protocol blocks redundant paths, wasting bandwidth and increasing convergence time.
  • •Complex Troubleshooting: Hierarchical design can lead to intricate problem identification.
Key Takeaway

The rise of East-West traffic exposed the inefficiencies of three-tier architectures, particularly due to oversubscription and STP.
