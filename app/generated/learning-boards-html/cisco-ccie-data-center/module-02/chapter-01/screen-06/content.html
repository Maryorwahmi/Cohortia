Introducing the Spine-Leaf Architecture

The Foundation for Scalable and Efficient Data Centers

Key Characteristics

  • •Two Primary Layers: Consists of Spine and Leaf switches.
  • •Leaf Switches: Connect directly to all servers and provide network access.
  • •Spine Switches: Form the backbone, interconnecting all Leaf switches.
  • •Full-Mesh Connectivity: Every Leaf connects to every Spine.
  • •Consistent Latency: Any two servers are always two hops away (Leaf-Spine-Leaf).
Key Takeaway

Spine-leaf architecture provides a flat, high-performance network fabric optimized for East-West traffic with predictable latency.
