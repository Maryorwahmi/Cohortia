Growth Strategy

Scalability and Modularity in Spine-Leaf

Designing for Future Data Center Expansion

Scaling Methods

  • •Add Leaf Switches: To increase server capacity, simply add more Leaf switches and connect them to all existing Spine switches.
  • •Add Spine Switches: To increase overall network throughput, add more Spine switches and connect them to all existing Leaf switches.
  • •Modular Growth: Simplifies network expansion and reduces complexity compared to traditional designs.
  • •Predictable Performance: Maintains consistent latency and performance even as the network scales.
Key Takeaway

Spine-leaf offers superior scalability through modular additions of Leaf or Spine switches, ensuring predictable growth.
