Cisco Nexus and NX-OS for Spine-Leaf

The Technology Powering Modern Data Center Networks

Key Aspects

  • •Cisco Nexus Series: High-performance switches designed for data center environments.
  • •NX-OS: Cisco's robust operating system optimized for data center operations, virtualization, and automation.
  • •Leaf as Layer 3 Boundary: Routing occurs at the Leaf layer, pushing intelligence closer to the edge.
  • •Efficient East-West Traffic: Direct routing across the Spine layer reduces latency for internal communication.
Key Takeaway

Cisco Nexus switches running NX-OS are foundational for spine-leaf, enabling Layer 3 routing at the Leaf for optimal East-West traffic flow.
