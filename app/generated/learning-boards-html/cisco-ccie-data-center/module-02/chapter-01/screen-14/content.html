Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Three-tier Architecture

Traditional data center design with Core, Aggregation, and Access layers, optimized for North-South traffic.

Spine-Leaf Architecture

Modern, two-layer design (Spine and Leaf switches) optimized for East-West traffic, scalability, and low latency.

North-South Traffic

Network traffic flowing between the data center and external networks, or between clients and servers.

Summary Takeaway

The evolution from three-tier to spine-leaf architectures was driven by the need to efficiently handle changing traffic patterns, particularly the rise of East-West communication.
