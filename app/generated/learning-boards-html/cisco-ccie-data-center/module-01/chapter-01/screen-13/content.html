Key Concepts

Core Definitions to Remember

Data Center

A centralized facility housing computing, storage, and networking equipment to process, store, and disseminate large amounts of data.

Traditional Data Center

Characterized by siloed physical hardware, manual management, and limited scalability, often based on a 3-tier network architecture.

Modern Data Center

Leverages virtualization, software-defined technologies, automation, and cloud principles for agility, efficiency, and scalability.

Summary Takeaway

Data centers are the foundational infrastructure for digital operations, evolving from rigid, hardware-centric models to agile, software-defined environments.
