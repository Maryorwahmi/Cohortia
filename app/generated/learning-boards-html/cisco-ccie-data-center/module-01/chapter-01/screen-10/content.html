Power Systems

Ensuring Uninterrupted Operations

Definition

Power systems provide a continuous and stable supply of electricity to all IT equipment, often including backup solutions to guard against outages.

Why it Matters

A stable power supply is the lifeblood of a data center; without it, all IT operations cease, leading to significant downtime and data loss.

Key Power Components

UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)

Short-term battery backup

Generators

Long-term power backup

Power Distribution Units (PDUs)

Distribute power to racks

Redundant Power Feeds

Multiple power sources

Key Takeaway

Robust power systems, including UPS and generators, are fundamental to data center resilience, ensuring continuous operation and protecting against costly downtime.
