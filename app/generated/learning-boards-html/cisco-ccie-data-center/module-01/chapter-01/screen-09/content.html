Networking Infrastructure

Connecting Everything Together

Definition

The networking infrastructure connects all data center components, enabling communication between servers, storage, and external networks with high speed and low latency.

Cisco's Role

Cisco offers specialized data center networking solutions, such as the Nexus series switches, designed for high-performance and reliable connectivity.

Key Network Devices

Switches

Internal device communication

Routers

Inter-network traffic direction

Firewalls

Security traffic control

Load Balancers

Distribute traffic across servers

Key Takeaway

The networking infrastructure is the critical backbone connecting all data center components, ensuring high-speed internal communication and secure external access through devices like switches, routers, firewalls, and load balancers.
