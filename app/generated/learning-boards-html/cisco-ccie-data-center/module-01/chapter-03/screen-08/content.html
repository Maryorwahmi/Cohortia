Expanding Virtualization

Beyond Servers: Storage and Network Virtualization

Abstracting and pooling storage and network resources.

Storage Virtualization

Pools physical storage from multiple devices into a single logical resource, simplifying management and improving utilization.

  • Abstracts underlying hardware
  • Simplifies storage management
  • Improves storage utilization

Network Virtualization

Creates virtual networks logically isolated from the physical network, enabling flexible segmentation and rapid deployment.

  • Flexible network segmentation
  • Rapid deployment of services
  • Simplified network management (e.g., VXLAN)
Key Takeaway

Storage and network virtualization are crucial for building agile, scalable, and easily managed data center infrastructures, often integrated into platforms like Cisco UCS.
