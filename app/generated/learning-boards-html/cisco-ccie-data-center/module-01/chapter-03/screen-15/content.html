Key Concepts: Cloud Computing

Essential terms for understanding cloud services and deployments.

Cloud Computing

On-demand delivery of computing services over the Internet.

IaaS, PaaS, SaaS

The three main cloud service models.

Public, Private, Hybrid

The three main cloud deployment models.

Summary Takeaway

Cloud computing offers diverse service and deployment models to meet varied organizational needs for IT resource consumption.
