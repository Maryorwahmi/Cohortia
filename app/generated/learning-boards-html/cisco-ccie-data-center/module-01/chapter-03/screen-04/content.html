Core Concept

What is Virtualization?

Creating software-based representations of physical resources.

Key Takeaway

Virtualization transforms physical hardware into flexible, software-defined resources, boosting efficiency and reducing costs.

Why it Matters

It enables better resource utilization, reduces hardware footprint, and simplifies IT management, moving away from the 'one application, one server' model.

Virtualization: Physical to Virtual

Transforming hardware into flexible, software-defined resources.
