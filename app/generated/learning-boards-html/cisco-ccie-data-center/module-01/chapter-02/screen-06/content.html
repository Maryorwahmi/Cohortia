Introducing Spine-Leaf Architecture

The Foundation of Modern Data Center Networks

Core Principles

  • Two-Tier Design: Consists of Spine and Leaf switches.
  • Leaf Switches: Connect directly to servers and storage (access layer).
  • Spine Switches: Interconnect all Leaf switches (backbone).
  • Full Mesh Connectivity: Every Leaf connects to every Spine.
Key Benefit

Creates a flat, non-blocking network with predictable low latency, ideal for East-West traffic.

Key Takeaway

Spine-Leaf is the de facto standard for modern data centers, offering superior performance and scalability over traditional 3-tier designs.
