Design Best Practices

Designing Resilient Data Center Networks

Avoiding Common Pitfalls and Ensuring High Availability

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Failing to plan for future growth and scalability.
  • Underestimating the volume and importance of East-West traffic.
  • Clinging to outdated L2-centric designs when L3 is more efficient.
  • Lack of redundancy at critical network layers.
Best Practices for Resilience

Ensure redundancy at every layer (e.g., dual Spines, dual uplinks). Implement proper cabling. Rigorously test failover scenarios.

Key Takeaway

A resilient data center network requires proactive planning, understanding traffic patterns, implementing comprehensive redundancy, and thorough testing to ensure continuous operation.
