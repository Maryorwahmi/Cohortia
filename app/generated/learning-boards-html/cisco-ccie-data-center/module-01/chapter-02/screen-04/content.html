Core Concept

The Traditional 3-Tier Architecture

Understanding the Foundation of Early Data Center Networks

Key Components

  • Access Layer: Connects directly to servers and storage.
  • Aggregation Layer: Provides redundancy and aggregates traffic from Access switches.
  • Core Layer: High-speed backbone, connects to external networks and other data centers.
Traffic Flow

Primarily optimized for North-South traffic (client-to-server), where communication flows into or out of the data center.

Key Takeaway

The 3-tier model, while foundational, introduced bottlenecks and wasted bandwidth due to STP, limiting scalability for modern demands.
