Traffic Patterns

The Rise of East-West Traffic

Why Traditional Architectures Struggled with Modern Applications

Understanding Traffic Flow

  • North-South Traffic: Communication between clients outside the data center and servers within.
  • East-West Traffic: Communication between servers within the data center itself (e.g., web server to app server to database).
  • Modern applications are highly distributed, leading to a surge in East-West traffic.
The Problem

Traditional 3-tier networks introduced high latency and bottlenecks for East-West traffic due to multiple layer traversals and STP blocking links.

Key Takeaway

The shift to East-West dominant traffic necessitated a new network architecture to overcome the limitations of the 3-tier model.
