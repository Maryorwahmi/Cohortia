Key Concepts

Advanced Terms in Data Center Automation

Configuration Drift

The phenomenon where the configuration of multiple systems, intended to be identical, gradually diverge over time due to manual changes or inconsistent updates.

Cisco ACI

A software-defined networking (SDN) solution for data centers that provides a policy-driven approach to network automation and management through a centralized controller (APIC).

Programmability

The capability of network and infrastructure devices to be controlled and configured via APIs and scripting, enabling dynamic and automated management beyond traditional CLI.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these terms is crucial for diagnosing issues, implementing solutions, and leveraging advanced automation capabilities in modern data centers.
