Cisco's Vision

Cisco's Strategic Approach to Automation

Unified, Policy-Driven Data Center Management

Pillars of Cisco's Automation Strategy

  • •**Unified Management:** Centralized control across diverse infrastructure.
  • •**Policy-Driven:** Define desired state, not manual configurations.
  • •**Enhanced Agility:** Rapid deployment and adaptation to changes.
  • •**Robust Security:** Consistent policy enforcement across the fabric.

From Device-Centric to Policy-Driven

Cisco's vision simplifies complex data center management by allowing policies to define infrastructure behavior, automatically applied across the entire fabric.

Key Principle

**Programmability:** Platforms designed with rich APIs for seamless automation integration.

Key Takeaway

Cisco's automation strategy focuses on unified, policy-driven control for simplified, agile, and secure operations.
