Skills for Modern Data Center Management

01

Explain Automation Concepts & Benefits

Articulate the fundamental ideas behind data center automation and its significant advantages, such as enhanced efficiency and cost savings. This skill is vital for advocating automation within your organization.

Differentiate Operational Models

Distinguish between traditional, manual data center operations and modern, automated models. Understanding this contrast helps you identify areas ripe for transformation and improvement.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Identify Drivers & Challenges

Pinpoint the motivations for adopting automation and the common hurdles encountered during its implementation. This prepares you to strategically plan and overcome obstacles.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Analyze Automation's Impact on Agility

Evaluate how automation dramatically increases operational speed and agility, allowing organizations to respond faster to business demands. This highlights the strategic value of automation.
