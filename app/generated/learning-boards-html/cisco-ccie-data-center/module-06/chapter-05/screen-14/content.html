Key Concepts: Monitoring Essentials

Core ideas for observing and understanding your data center.

Monitoring

Continuous collection, analysis, and visualization of data to assess infrastructure health and performance.

Metrics, Logs, Events

Key data types: quantitative measurements, timestamped records, and discrete state changes, respectively.

Alerting & Visualization

Notifying personnel of issues and presenting data in understandable dashboards and graphs.

Chapter Summary

Monitoring is the foundation for understanding data center health, relying on diverse data types and effective presentation to enable proactive management and rapid response to issues.
