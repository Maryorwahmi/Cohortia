Cisco DCNM: Centralized Data Center Monitoring

Gaining comprehensive visibility into your Cisco data center network infrastructure.

DCNM Capabilities

Centralized visibility of network health and performance.

Collection of metrics, logs, and events from Nexus devices.

Rich dashboards, topology views, and performance analytics.

Proactive identification of bottlenecks and compliance issues.

Key Takeaway

Cisco DCNM is essential for managing and monitoring Cisco data center network infrastructure, providing a unified platform for visibility, performance analysis, and troubleshooting across Nexus devices.

Beyond Network Monitoring

While DCNM focuses on network infrastructure, Cisco also offers AppDynamics for deep application performance monitoring. AppDynamics provides insights into how applications are performing, tracing transactions, and identifying code-level bottlenecks, complementing DCNM's network-centric view for a complete operational picture.
