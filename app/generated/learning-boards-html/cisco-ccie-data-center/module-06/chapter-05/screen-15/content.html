Key Concepts: Orchestration & Cisco Solutions

Understanding coordinated automation and Cisco's role.

Orchestration

Coordination of multiple automated tasks across systems to achieve complex business objectives.

Cisco DCNM

Centralized management system for Cisco data center network infrastructure monitoring.

Intersight & ACI

Cisco platforms for cloud-based compute/storage orchestration and network policy orchestration, respectively.

Chapter Summary

Orchestration is key to managing complex data center workflows, with Cisco's DCNM, Intersight, and ACI providing robust solutions for monitoring and coordinating compute, storage, and network resources.
