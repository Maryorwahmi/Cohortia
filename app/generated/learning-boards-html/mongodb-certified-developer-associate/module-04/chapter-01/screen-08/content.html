Cardinality categories
  • One-to-one: usually embed.
  • One-to-many (bounded): embed.
  • One-to-many (unbounded): reference or hybrid.
  • Many-to-many: reference, or use join tables/collections.
Pattern

Embed small summaries for fast reads and keep full data in referenced collections for updates and analytics.

Example: Products & Reviews

Embed three latest review summaries in the product document for quick product pages. Store complete review documents in a reviews collection and query them when users request full review history.

Key Takeaway

Hybrids let you optimize common reads while keeping data size and update costs manageable.
