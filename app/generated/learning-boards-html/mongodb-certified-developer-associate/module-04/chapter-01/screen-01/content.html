Introduction to MongoDB Data Modeling

Understand how documents, BSON, embedding, and referencing shape application design.

Orientation

You will learn BSON document structure, the embedding vs referencing decision, and how to pick a hybrid pattern based on access patterns, cardinality, and growth. Examples use simple Product and Review models to illustrate real trade-offs.

Why this matters

Good data modeling reduces reads, avoids large document updates, and keeps your app responsive. Poor modeling leads to oversized documents, many lookups, or duplicated data and consistency issues.

Explore Key Topics
BSON & Document

Binary JSON with nested documents and arrays; efficient storage for rich types.

Embedding

Co-locate related data for fast reads when the data is bounded and accessed together.

Referencing

Keep large or shared entities in separate collections and link by id when they grow or are updated independently.

Quick note

Remember the 16MB document size limit as a hard safety boundary. If an array or sub-document may grow without bounds, prefer referencing or a hybrid pattern.
