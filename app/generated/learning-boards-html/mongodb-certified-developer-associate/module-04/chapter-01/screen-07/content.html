Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Embedding vs Referencing
Embedding
  • Single-read retrieval — good for read-heavy access.
  • Smaller number of queries, simpler application logic.
  • Risk: document growth and expensive updates to large arrays.
Referencing
  • Keeps documents compact and under size limits.
  • Supports independent updates and sharing across parents.
  • Trade-off: may require multiple queries or aggregation to assemble data.
Performance

Embedding reduces reads; referencing can add lookups.

Updates

Frequent independent updates favor referencing.

Size & Sharing

Large or shared data favors referencing to avoid duplication and growth.
