Embed when…

Data is small, accessed with parent, and rarely updated independently.

Reference when…

Data is large, unbounded, frequently updated, or shared across parents.

Hybrid pattern

Embed summaries for common reads and keep full documents referenced for growth and analytics.

Final takeaway

Design data around access patterns, consider cardinality and growth, and choose the simplest model that meets your read and write requirements.
