Consistency strategies
  • Cascade deletes or update parents when child removed.
  • Use soft deletes and maintain tombstone flags for child documents.
  • Run periodic cleanup to detect and remove dangling references.
Common mistake
Assuming MongoDB enforces referential integrity like a relational database leads to surprises; plan application checks instead.
Pro Tip

Instrument deletes and updates so you can surface potential dangling references quickly in logs or observability dashboards.
