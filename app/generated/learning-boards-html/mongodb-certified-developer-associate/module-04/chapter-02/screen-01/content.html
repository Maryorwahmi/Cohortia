One-to-One Relationships in MongoDB

Choose between embedding and referencing depending on how data is accessed, its size, and lifecycle. This lesson gives practical patterns, example commands, and the safety notes you need to design correctly.

Why this matters
Your modeling choice directly affects read latency, number of queries, and long-term scalability. Picking the wrong pattern increases complexity.
Where to start
Begin by mapping access patterns: will the related data be read together or separately? That guides embedding vs referencing.
Explore Key Topics
Embedding
Pack related small fields into the parent document for single-query reads.
Referencing
Keep large or independent data in another collection and link by _id.
Consistency
Handle deletes and updates at the application layer to avoid dangling references.
Concept Map
User
Small profile fields? Embed.
Product
Large spec? Reference.
Application Rule
Always model for the most frequent access pattern.
