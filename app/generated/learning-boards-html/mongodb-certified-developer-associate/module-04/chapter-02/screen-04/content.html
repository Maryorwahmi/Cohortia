Clear definition

A one-to-one relationship maps exactly one document to exactly one other document or sub-document. Modeling uses embedding or referencing depending on coupling, size, and access patterns.

Example: User & Profile
Profile embedded when always displayed with the user.
Example: Product & Specification
Specification referenced when it is large and viewed separately.
Common misconception
Many assume one-to-one always means embedding. The correct choice depends on whether fields are small and always read with the parent. Referencing can be the right decision even for one-to-one cases.
Analogy
Think of embedding like putting profile details inside the user’s passport: quick when you always need both. Referencing is like storing a large manual in a separate binder and keeping a reference note on the passport that points to it.
Check your understanding
  • Is the related data small and almost always read with the parent?
  • Does the related data grow independently or require separate permissions?
  • Will embedding cause document growth problems over time?
Key takeaway: pick the model that aligns with the most frequent access pattern.
