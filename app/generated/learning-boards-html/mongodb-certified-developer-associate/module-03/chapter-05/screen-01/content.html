Indexing Strategies and Performance Optimization
Understand explain(), design indexes for covered queries, and use sparse / TTL indexes safely in production.
In this lesson

You will diagnose slow queries with explain(), build compound indexes for coverage, use sparse and TTL indexes for special cases, and apply ESR ordering plus monitoring to production systems.

Why it matters

Well-designed indexes reduce disk I/O, lower latency, and keep write cost acceptable. Indexes are a performance lever that must be tuned as data grows.
