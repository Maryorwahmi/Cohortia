Definition
TTL indexes
Expire documents automatically by date using expireAfterSeconds on a single date field.
Example
db.sessions.createIndex({ "createdAt": 1 }, { expireAfterSeconds: 3600 })
This expires sessions one hour after the createdAt timestamp. Expect deletions to occur on the TTL monitor cycle.
Important notes
  • The TTL background process runs periodically, so expiration timing is approximate.
  • The indexed field must hold BSON date values for TTL to work.
  • _id cannot be used for a TTL index.
Why it matters

TTL indexes simplify lifecycle management for ephemeral data and help keep collections small without manual deletion jobs.
