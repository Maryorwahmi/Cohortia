Comparison
Over-indexing vs Low-cardinality
Both mistakes increase cost without corresponding benefit. Use workload evidence to guide indexing choices.
Over-indexing
Creates many indexes with small usage. Results in high write overhead and memory pressure.
Low-cardinality indexing
Indexing fields that have few unique values rarely reduces document scans significantly.
Practical steps
  • List and justify each index in production.
  • Use explain() and metrics to confirm benefits.
  • Consider partial or compound indexes for targeted patterns.
Pro Tip

Start with indexes that support the highest traffic queries, then iteratively add and measure others as needed.
