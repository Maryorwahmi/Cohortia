Cardinality & Selectivity
High-cardinality fields narrow results more and make better index candidates. Measure selectivity to decide.
High cardinality
Fields like email or productId — many unique values, highly selective.
Low cardinality
Fields like gender or status — few unique values, often not useful to index.
Practical step: sample representative queries, measure the fraction of documents returned, and prefer indexes that reduce that fraction substantially. If an index does not improve selectivity for your workload, it is usually not worth the cost.
Quick exercise
Pick three fields from your collection and estimate how many documents a typical equality query would return. Use these estimates to prioritize index candidates.
Key Takeaway

Cardinality predicts selectivity. Index the fields that most reduce the result set for your frequent queries.
