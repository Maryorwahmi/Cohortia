Designing a compound index
Follow a simple process to ensure the compound index supports your queries and sorts.
1
List frequent query shapes and sorts that touch the collection.
2
Choose the leftmost field as the most selective or most commonly filtered field.
3
Add secondary fields to support sorts or additional filters used together with the leftmost field.
Example
Index for queries that filter by userId and sort by orderDate descending:
db.orders.createIndex({ "userId": 1, "orderDate": -1 })
Index prefix rule
The compound index supports queries that match the leftmost prefix: {userId}, or {userId, orderDate}. It does not efficiently support queries on orderDate alone.
Quick reference
  • Leftmost field = filter priority.
  • Use explain() to confirm coverage.
  • Compound indexes can cover filters and sorts together.
Key Takeaway

Order fields in compound indexes to match your leftmost filters and common sorts so the index can be used efficiently.
