Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
GeoJSON
Standard for points and polygons; coordinate order is [lon, lat].
2dsphere Index
Enables spherical geometry queries and meter-based distances.
$near & $maxDistance
Find nearest points and restrict radius in meters for 2dsphere queries.
Summary

Use GeoJSON and a 2dsphere index to power accurate proximity and containment queries. Validate geometry and index presence before relying on these queries in production.
