Use $text when
  • You want stemming and token-based matching.
  • Relevance ranking matters.
  • Searching across multiple fields together.
Use $regex when
  • You need exact substring or pattern matches.
  • Case-sensitive or anchored searches are required.
  • Dataset is small or properly indexed for regex patterns.
When to upgrade

For fuzzy matching, synonyms, or complex ranking consider Atlas Search or an external search engine built on Lucene.
