Comparison Operators
$gt, $lt, $gte, $lte, $ne for precise numeric and date filters.
Logical Operators
$and, $or, $not, $nor to combine and negate expressions.
Element Operators
$exists and $type to check presence and BSON type of fields.
Summary Takeaway

Compose comparison, logical, and element operators carefully, validate each step on sample data, and inspect explain plans to ensure performance.
