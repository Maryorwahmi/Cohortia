Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Consistency vs Availability vs Performance
Decision framework
  • Classify operations by criticality: critical, important, best-effort.
  • Map critical operations to w:majority and primary reads with j:true where necessary.
  • Map best-effort operations to w:0 or w:1 and secondary reads for throughput.
Failure modes and responses
If quorum is lost and a write with w:majority blocks, your app should decide whether to timeout and retry, queue the write, or surface an error to the user. Avoid silent retries that mask poor availability decisions.
Practical tip
Implement timeouts and clear retry semantics around writes that may block due to write concern settings.
