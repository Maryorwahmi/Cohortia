Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › What to Remember
Primary
Reads are freshest; use for confirmation pages and immediately-after-write reads.
Secondaries
Scale reads and reduce primary load but accept possible staleness.
Write concerns
w controls acknowledgements; j controls journaling persistence to disk.
Final takeaway
Match read and write settings to each operation's tolerance for stale reads and risk of data loss. Build timeouts and retry strategies for blocked or slow operations.
