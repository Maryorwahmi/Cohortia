Module 6 ›Chapter 6.6›Key Concepts
rs.status()
Returns the replica set document with member states, optimeDate, health and heartbeat info.
rs.printReplicationInfo()
Shows configured oplog size and log length (oplog window) to judge catch-up capability.
Oplog window
Time range covered by the oplog; determines how far back a secondary can replay operations.
Summary

Use these three concepts together to measure replication health and to reason about recovery options.
