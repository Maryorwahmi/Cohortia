What dashboards give you
  • Real-time replication lag times and oplog window visualized across hosts.
  • Historical trends for capacity planning and pattern recognition.
  • Alerting for thresholds like sustained high lag, oplog window shrinkage, or primary down.
Tuning alerts
Avoid reacting to single-second spikes. Set alerts for sustained conditions, such as replication lag above X seconds for Y minutes, or oplog window below Z hours. Use historical dashboards to pick X, Y, and Z so alerts match real operational behavior.
Key Takeaway

Dashboards and alerts turn reactive checks into proactive operations; tune them from historical data to avoid noise.
