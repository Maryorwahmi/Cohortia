Alert rules to consider
Replication lag > X seconds for Y minutes (sustained condition).
Oplog window < Z minutes/hours.
health:0 or member unreachable for a sustained period.
Context to include in alerts
  • Primary optimeDate and affected secondary optimeDate.
  • Recent oplog window value and trend.
  • Heartbeat gaps or election events nearby.
Key Takeaway

Design alerts that surface actionable context and avoid noise by requiring persistence before notifying on lag or other conditions.
