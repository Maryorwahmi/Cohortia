Module 6 ›Chapter 6.6›Member indicators
Healthy signals
PRIMARY / SECONDARY + health:1
Role matches expected numeric myState and health indicates connectivity.
Advancing optimeDate
Timestamps steadily move forward indicating replication progress.
Stable heartbeats
No repeated heartbeat timeouts or large gaps between lastHeartbeatRecv and lastHeartbeat.
Problem signals
health:0
Member is unreachable or mongod is not responding; investigate process and network.
RECOVERING / STARTUP2
Prolonged states suggest inability to catch up or initial sync failures.
Shrinking oplog window
High writes can reduce the oplog window and force full resyncs when secondaries fall behind.
