What is a Replica Set?
A short definition and analogy to anchor the idea.
Analogy: Ledger across offices
Imagine one central office that records transactions and several branch offices that hold replicated copies. If the central office becomes unreachable, a branch can be promoted to accept transactions.
Properties
  • One primary accepts all writes.
  • Secondaries replicate from the primary asynchronously.
  • Members elect a new primary automatically on failure.
Common misconception
Replication is not a backup. Accidental deletes or corruption replicate across members unless you have backups or point-in-time recovery.
Concrete example
Create three data directories and start three mongod processes named rs0-0, rs0-1, rs0-2. When initialized as a replica set, one becomes primary and two become secondaries, protecting against the failure of any single member.
Check your understanding
Why does a replica set typically use at least three members? Because a majority is needed to elect a primary; with three members you can lose one and still elect a leader.
Key Takeaway

Replica sets provide redundancy and automatic failover. They are essential for production availability but do not replace backups.
