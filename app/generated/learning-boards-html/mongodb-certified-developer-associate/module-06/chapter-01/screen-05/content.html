Primary–Secondary Architecture
Roles, responsibilities, and election basics.
Role breakdown
  • Primary: Accepts writes and records them to the oplog.
  • Secondary: Tails the primary oplog and applies entries to reach the same dataset.
  • Election: Members vote; a majority wins and becomes primary.
Why 3 members?
Three voting members provide a clear majority if one node fails, reducing split-brain risk during elections.
Concrete scenario
If the primary crashes, remaining secondaries detect the absence, hold an election, and one secondary becomes the new primary. Your application regains write capability without manual intervention.
Common mistake
Configuring only two members risks split-brain because neither side can form a majority if the network partitions.
Key Takeaway

A single writable primary preserves write ordering; elections with a clear majority ensure availability when failures occur.
