Deployment Guidelines and Common Mistakes
Design choices that affect availability and recovery.
Actionable guidelines
  • Run at least three voting members for stable elections.
  • Decide geographic placement to balance latency and resilience.
  • Use arbiters carefully; they vote but store no data.
  • Size oplog so secondaries can catch up within its retention window.
  • Implement regular backups in addition to replication.
Common mistakes
Deploying a single mongod and assuming it's highly available, or configuring only two members and risking split-brain during partitions.
Key Takeaway
Design for majority-based elections, monitor replication, and maintain backups for full data protection.
