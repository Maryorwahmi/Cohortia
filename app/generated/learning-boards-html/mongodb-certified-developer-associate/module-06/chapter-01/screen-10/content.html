Automatic Failover and Elections
How elections restore write capability when a primary fails.
Election basics
Members vote to elect a primary when they detect the current primary is unavailable. A majority is required. Use three or more voting members to ensure stable elections.
Example
In a 3-member set, if the primary fails, the two remaining secondaries vote and promote one to primary so writes can resume.
Operational note
Elections take non-zero time. Applications should handle brief periods where writes are unavailable and retry safely after a leader is chosen.
Key Takeaway
Automatic failover minimizes downtime, but election design and member voting matter for predictable recovery.
