Key Concepts — Quick Reference
Three short reminders and a final question.
Replica sets
Provide automatic failover and redundant copies of your data.
Primary & oplog
Primary records writes to the oplog; secondaries replay entries in order to synchronize.
Backups
Replication is not a backup; maintain backups for deletion and corruption recovery.
Final thought
Match your replica set configuration and backup policy to your availability and recovery objectives to make architecture decisions that protect your users.
