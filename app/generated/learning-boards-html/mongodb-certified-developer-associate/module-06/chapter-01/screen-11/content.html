Replica Sets vs Single mongod
Vulnerability, recovery, and admin responsibilities.
Single mongod
  • Simple to operate but single point of failure.
  • Manual recovery and greater risk of downtime.
  • Backups essential for safety.
Replica set
  • Automatic failover and redundancy.
  • Requires replica set configuration, monitoring, and planning.
  • Still needs backups to handle deletion or corruption.
Key Takeaway
For production availability choose replica sets and complement them with backups for complete data protection.
