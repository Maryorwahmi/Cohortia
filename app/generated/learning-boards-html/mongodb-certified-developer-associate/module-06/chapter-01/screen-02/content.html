Explain benefits of replica sets

Describe primary-secondary roles
Explain that the primary accepts writes and secondaries replicate those writes asynchronously. Example: write routing behavior.

03
Understand the oplog
Know that the oplog is a capped collection that records write operations and is replayed by secondaries. Example: troubleshooting replication lag using the oplog.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify states and best practices
Recognize member states, the need for at least three members, and why backups remain necessary despite replication.
