Role
Primary
Handles all writes and records operations to the oplog.
Role
Secondary
Asynchronously applies oplog entries to maintain a copy of the data set.
Why it matters
Automatic failover keeps applications running when a primary fails, and read scaling can offload reads to secondaries for higher throughput.
