Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Understanding rs.status()
What rs.status shows
  • Member list with ids and host addresses.
  • stateStr fields indicating PRIMARY, SECONDARY, or other states.
  • Heartbeat and replication lag indicators for health checks.
Use this output to confirm your topology and to identify members that cannot reach the set or have not finished initial sync.
Quick check
Run rs.status and look for a single PRIMARY and the expected number of SECONDARY nodes. If a node is missing, inspect its log for connection or permission errors.
Misconception

Do not expect instantaneous replication completion; allow secondaries time to catch up and check oplog progress instead of assuming failure immediately.
