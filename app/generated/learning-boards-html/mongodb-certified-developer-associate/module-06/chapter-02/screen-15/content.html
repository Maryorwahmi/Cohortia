Unique dbpaths
Isolate each mongod's storage to avoid corruption and simplify logs.
Matching replSet name
Ensure the _id in rs.initiate matches the --replSet flag used at startup.
Multi-host URI
Include multiple seed hosts and replicaSet so drivers can discover topology and recover from failures.
Final thought: Small configuration mistakes cause the most downtime. Verify these three items early to keep deployments reliable.
