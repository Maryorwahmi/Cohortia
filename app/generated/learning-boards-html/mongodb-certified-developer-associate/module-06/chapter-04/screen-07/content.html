Delayed Secondary (secondaryDelaySecs)
A lagged member that provides a time-window for recovery from recent errors.
Example use
Configure with priority: 0 and hidden: true. Example delay: 3600 seconds for a one-hour window.
Caveat
Not a replacement for backups; still consumes storage and must be managed.
Deployment tip
Keep delayed members hidden and non-promotable to ensure they remain reliable snapshots for recovery.
