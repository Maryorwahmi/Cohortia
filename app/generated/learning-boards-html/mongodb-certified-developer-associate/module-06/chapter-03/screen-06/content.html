Comparison
Voting members vs non-voting members
Voting member (votes:1)
Participates in elections; counts toward majority.
Non-voting member (votes:0)
Does not vote; useful for hidden or reporting nodes that shouldn’t affect elections.
Scenarios
  • 3 voting members: one failure still allows a majority (2 of 3).
  • 2 voting members: failure of one prevents a majority and blocks elections.
  • Hidden non-voting nodes do not help elect a primary despite contributing replication capacity.
Misconception
Adding more total nodes always increases availability — false if those nodes are non-voting, they do not improve election quorum.
Key Takeaway

Design voting topology intentionally: prefer odd numbers of voting members and ensure votes are set to reflect each node's role in availability.
