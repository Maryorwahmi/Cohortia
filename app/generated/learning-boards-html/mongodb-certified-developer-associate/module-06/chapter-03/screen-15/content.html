Key Concepts — Additional Terms
Three more terms to remember for safe cluster design.
Priority
A numeric setting that biases a member's chance to become primary; priority:0 prevents leadership.
Oplog Currency
How up-to-date a member's operation log is; preferred candidates have higher currency to reduce data loss.
Split-Brain
A harmful scenario where two members think they are primary; prevented by majority voting rules.
Final takeaway
Tune priority, ensure oplog currency, and preserve majority to avoid split-brain and limit downtime during failover.
