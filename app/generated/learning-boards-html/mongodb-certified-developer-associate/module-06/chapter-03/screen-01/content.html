In this lesson
Why elections matter and what you'll practice

Elections keep a replica set writable after a primary stops responding. You will learn triggers, voting rules, how priority and oplog currency influence selection, and how to design retries in your application to minimize downtime.

Trigger
Primary unreachable or manual stepdown
Decision
Majority vote chooses new primary
Recovery
New primary accepts writes, old rejoins as secondary
Explore Key Topics
Triggers, Voting rules, and Driver behavior: these three topics explain how the cluster stays available and what your app must handle during the brief failover window.
Quick facts
Majority of voting members is required to elect. Priority and oplog currency bias selection. Drivers auto-detect primary changes if configured with multiple seeds.
Common pitfall
Two voting members can stop elections; use at least three voting members to avoid total unavailability.
Activity
Simulate a primary failure and observe rs.status changes on remaining members.
