Definition
Minimizing downtime
Cluster: voting topology
Maintain at least three voting members and place voters to avoid single-point partitions.
Cluster: priority & oplog
Set priority to steer leadership only when replication health supports it.
Application: connection
Use multiple seeds and replicaSet name so drivers can find the new primary.
Application: retries
Implement retries with backoff for transient primary-change errors.
Operational: testing
Simulate primary failure in a lab and measure recovery time to tune settings and expectations.
Write concerns
Balance w:majority durability with the possibility of brief blocks during failover.
A useful practice is to run a small failure injection drill and measure how long drivers and applications take to recover with your chosen timeouts and retry strategies. Use those observations to tune priorities, timeouts, and retry backoffs so production behavior meets your SLAs.
Key Takeaway

Combine resilient voting topology, sensible priorities, and application retries to reduce the impact of automatic failover on your users.
