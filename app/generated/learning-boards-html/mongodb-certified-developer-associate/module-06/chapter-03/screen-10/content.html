Process Flow
Reachability & availability
Network reachability determines whether candidates can contact voters and whether an election can succeed.
Ensure nodes can connect on replica set ports and that inter-node latency is low enough for timely election steps.
Plan voting member placement to avoid losing majority in single-region or single-link outages.
Tune timeouts carefully: higher timeouts allow more latency tolerance but increase unavailability duration during real failures.
Example and check
If three voters are across two regions and a link between regions fails, there may be no majority in either region. Simulate link loss and observe whether any region can still elect a primary.
Misconception
Moving voters to many regions always increases resilience — not if partitions prevent any region from holding a majority.
Try this

Run controlled network partitions between nodes in a non-production lab to verify your voting topology and timeout choices.
