Definition
Voting members and quorum
Voting members (votes:1) cast ballots during elections. Quorum and majority are computed over voting members only.
votes:1
Participates in election; contributes to majority.
votes:0
Does not vote; useful for hidden or reporting members.
Example
Five members with two non-voting results in three voting members; majority is two. After a failure you need the remaining voters to form a majority.
Operational tip
Document which nodes are voting and why, and avoid changing votes in the heat of an incident without plan.
Common Mistake

Having only two voting members can prevent elections after one fails. Use at least three voting members for safe availability.
