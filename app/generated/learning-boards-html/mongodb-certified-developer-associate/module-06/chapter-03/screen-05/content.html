Process Flow
Nomination and voting
1
Nomination
An eligible secondary declares candidacy and broadcasts that intention to other members.
2
Vote requests
The candidate sends vote requests; recipients assess reachability and data currency before casting a vote.
3
Majority decision
If the candidate gets a majority of voting members' votes, it becomes primary and starts accepting writes.
Supporting notes
  • Only members with votes:1 participate in the vote tally.
  • Oplog currency is checked to prefer the most up-to-date candidate.
  • The candidate must be reachable and be able to contact a majority.
Analogy
Think of members as a jury: a candidate needs the majority of jurors who are allowed to vote to reach a verdict.
Key Takeaway

Elections are a coordinated request-and-vote process that requires both eligibility and majority agreement among voting members.
