Key Concepts
Three essential definitions to carry forward.
Election
The process members use to choose a new primary when needed.
Automatic Failover
Seamless transition of primary role without manual intervention.
Majority
A quorum requirement: more than half of voting members must agree to elect a primary.
Summary
Design voting topology and client behavior with these concepts in mind to preserve availability and consistency during failovers.
