Core Concept
Priority and election bias
Priority values indicate which members are preferred for primary selection but they are not absolute guarantees.
priority: >0
Member is eligible and its higher value increases selection probability.
priority: 0
Member will not be elected primary; useful for reporting or disaster recovery nodes.
Concrete example
If you have a fast, local data center node you prefer as primary, give it a higher priority. But ensure it also keeps its oplog current; otherwise a slower but more up-to-date node may be preferred at election time.
Misconception
Higher priority will always win — incorrect. Oplog currency and reachability are required in addition to priority.
Key Takeaway

Use priority to steer leadership, but balance it with replication health. priority:0 protects nodes from accidental leadership.
