Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Point-in-Time Recovery
Process

Restore snapshot → Replay oplog → Stop at target time

1. Capture

Run mongodump --oplog on a secondary to create a snapshot and capture oplog entries present at dump time.

2. Restore

Use mongorestore to restore the dump contents, typically with --drop to avoid conflicts.

3. Replay

Apply the oplog entries up to the desired timestamp using --oplogReplay and optionally --oplogLimit or a sliced oplog file.

Common gotchas
  • Omitting --oplog at dump time prevents safe PITR.
  • Insufficient oplog retention may mean you cannot replay far enough forward.
  • Replaying oplog without validation can reintroduce unwanted writes; always choose the correct stop time.
Key Takeaway

Plan oplog capture and retention to align with backup cadence so you can replay to precise recovery points when needed.
