Managed backups and PITR

Atlas provides automated snapshots, archived copies, and continuous point-in-time backups that include oplog captures. This reduces setup complexity for cloud-hosted clusters.

Benefits
  • Automated scheduling and retention.
  • Continuous PITR via oplog backups.
  • Offsite storage managed by Atlas.
Responsibilities
  • Verify retention and point-in-time windows.
  • Schedule and test restores via runbooks.
  • Control costs and compliance settings.
Key Takeaway

Managed backups reduce operational effort, but you must still define retention, test restores, and integrate them into your disaster recovery plan.
