Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › What to Remember
Replica sets ≠ Backup
Replication protects availability, not logical recoveries.
Include the oplog
mongodump --oplog enables point-in-time replay during restore.
Test restores
Regular restore drills prove your backups are usable when needed.
Final Note

Backups are a combination of tools, configuration, and operational discipline. Keep them automated, offsite, and tested.
