Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts
Backup Strategy
A plan for creating and storing copies to ensure recoverability from loss or corruption.
mongodump & mongorestore
Core logical backup tools; use mongodump with --oplog and restore with --drop and --oplogReplay for PITR.
Point-in-Time Recovery
Include oplog data in your dump to replay operations up to a specific moment during restore.
Summary Takeaway

Replica sets keep systems running; backups let you recover from logical errors. Configure mongodump with --oplog on secondaries, restore with mongorestore and oplog replay, and make testing and offsite storage part of the plan.
