Understand recoverability, not just redundancy

Replica sets keep data available and replicated across members. Backups let you reverse logical mistakes and restore to previous states.

Key Takeaway

Design backups for recoverability: schedule regular hot backups, include oplog data for PITR, and test restores frequently.

Visual
Backup topics
Hot backups: live backups using mongodump or snapshots; minimal downtime.
Cold backups: stop mongod and copy data files; guaranteed consistency but involves downtime.
PITR: use oplog with dumps to roll forward to a precise moment.
