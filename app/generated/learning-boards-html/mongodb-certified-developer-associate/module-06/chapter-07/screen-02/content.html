Learning Objectives

Succeed at backup and restore tasks for replica sets.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain recovery limits of replica sets

You will describe why replication alone can't restore a logically deleted document, and why a separate backup is required.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate hot vs cold backups

You will compare downtime, consistency, and operational impact to choose the right backup type for your services.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Perform mongodump against a secondary

You will run mongodump with the --oplog option and understand how targeting a secondary reduces primary load.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Restore with mongorestore and oplog replay

You will restore a dump, use --drop responsibly, and perform oplog replay for point-in-time recovery.
