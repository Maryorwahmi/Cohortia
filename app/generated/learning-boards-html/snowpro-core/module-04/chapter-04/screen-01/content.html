Starter example
SELECT query_id, query_text, user_name, start_time
FROM SNOWFLAKE.ACCOUNT_USAGE.QUERY_HISTORY
WHERE user_name = 'JOHN_DOE'
  AND start_time >= DATEADD(hour, -24, CURRENT_TIMESTAMP())
ORDER BY start_time DESC;
Why this matters

This query shows who ran what recently, which helps with incident response, cost analysis, and compliance reporting.
