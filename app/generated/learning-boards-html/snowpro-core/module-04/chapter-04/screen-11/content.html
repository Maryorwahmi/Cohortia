Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Fail-safe vs Time Travel
Time Travel
  • User-accessible for queries, cloning, and restores.
  • Configurable retention window (default and up to edition limits).
  • Used for routine recovery and point-in-time analysis.
Fail-safe
  • Snowflake-managed 7-day recovery period after Time Travel ends.
  • Not accessible to users for queries or restores.
  • Intended for catastrophic system recovery only.
Operational advice
Do not rely on Fail-safe for day-to-day recovery. Configure Time Travel retention to meet your policy, and include recovery steps in runbooks. Treat Fail-safe as a last-resort safety net managed by Snowflake.
