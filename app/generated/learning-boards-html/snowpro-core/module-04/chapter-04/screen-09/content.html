Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › ACCESS_HISTORY: track reads/writes
What it records
ACCESS_HISTORY captures which tables and columns were read or written, the query that caused the access, the user, and the timestamp — enabling column-level auditing.
Concrete usage
If a privacy audit requests who read a sensitive column last month, ACCESS_HISTORY shows the specific read events to produce evidence.
Edition note
Enterprise requirement
ACCESS_HISTORY is available in Enterprise Edition and above; confirm your edition when planning column-level audits.
Best practice
Combine ACCESS_HISTORY with QUERY_HISTORY to map statements to access events and build a complete audit record.
Key Takeaway

Use ACCESS_HISTORY for precise, column-level evidence when your compliance requirements demand it.
