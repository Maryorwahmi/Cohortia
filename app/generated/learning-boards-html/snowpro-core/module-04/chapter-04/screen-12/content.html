Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Compliance checklist and best practices
Retention
Verify Time Travel retention and ACCOUNT_USAGE view ranges; archive if needed for longer legal holds.
Access Controls
Review GRANTS_TO_ROLES and GRANTS_TO_USERS regularly to enforce least privilege.
Protection
Use dynamic data masking and row access policies to limit exposure of sensitive values while retaining auditability.
Operational checks
  • Weekly review of LOGIN_HISTORY for anomalies.
  • Monthly query of QUERY_HISTORY for long-running or high-cost statements.
Runbooks
Document steps for UNDROP and CLONE and include contact points for escalation if Fail-safe is required.
