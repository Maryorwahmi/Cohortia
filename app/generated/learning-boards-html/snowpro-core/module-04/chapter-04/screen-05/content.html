Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › ACCOUNT_USAGE vs INFORMATION_SCHEMA
Information Schema
Scope
Database-scoped metadata and recent history, useful for immediate, local queries.
Limitations
Limited retention window and no account-wide coverage; not suitable for long-term audits.
Account Usage
Scope
Views under SNOWFLAKE.ACCOUNT_USAGE provide historical, account-wide telemetry for auditing.
Strengths
Longer retention, cross-database visibility, and views like QUERY_HISTORY, LOGIN_HISTORY, and ACCESS_HISTORY (Enterprise+).
Practical rule
For audits that require historical or account-wide evidence, prefer ACCOUNT_USAGE. For quick, local metadata checks, INFORMATION_SCHEMA is fine. Don't assume INFORMATION_SCHEMA will hold months of history.
Example question
Find queries by user across account for 30 days
Use SNOWFLAKE.ACCOUNT_USAGE.QUERY_HISTORY, not INFORMATION_SCHEMA.
Common mistake
Relying on INFORMATION_SCHEMA for long-term audits
This can lead to incomplete audit evidence during investigations.
