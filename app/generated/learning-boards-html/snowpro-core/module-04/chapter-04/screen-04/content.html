Definition

Auditing and Logging

Logging captures events like queries and logins. Auditing is the structured review of those logs to prove who did what and why it matters.

Concrete example
If a row is unexpectedly changed, QUERY_HISTORY identifies the query and user that performed the update, giving you an audit trail to investigate.
Common misconception
Logs are not optional diagnostics; they are often required evidence for compliance and incident response.
Key takeaway
Design collection and retention so you can answer security and compliance questions within policy timelines.
Annotated flow
Event generation
Users run queries, authenticate, or change grants, producing log entries.
Collection & storage
Snowflake stores events in INFORMATION_SCHEMA or ACCOUNT_USAGE depending on scope and retention needs.
Audit & response
Analysts query history views to investigate anomalies and produce compliance reports.
Key Takeaway

Treat logs as first-class evidence: retain, review, and connect them to incident response and compliance workflows.
