Data Governance and Access Control

Design role hierarchies, assign privileges with SQL, manage ownership, and automate permissions with future grants so your Snowflake environment is secure and simple to administer.

In this lesson

Understand RBAC principles, create custom roles, grant privileges, transfer ownership, and configure future grants to reduce admin work.

Why it matters

Proper governance prevents data leaks, ensures compliance, and enables teams to do their jobs without risky over-privilege.

Concept map
Roles
Grant privileges to roles; assign roles to users.
Ownership
Owner role has full control; transfer preserves grants with a clause.
Future grants
Apply privileges automatically to future objects in a container.
Auditing
Use SHOW GRANTS to review role privileges and history.
RBAC Principles

Roles hold privileges; users receive roles.

Ownership

Creator role becomes owner by default.

Future Grants

New objects inherit grants automatically.

Explore Key Topics
Create and grant roles
Use CREATE ROLE and GRANT ROLE TO ROLE to build inheritance.
Ownership & transfer
Transfer ownership with COPY CURRENT GRANTS to preserve existing permissions.
Future grants
Apply SELECT on future tables to a role so new tables are accessible immediately.
