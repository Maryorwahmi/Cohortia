Definition

Role as a permission container

A role holds privileges and can be assigned to users or other roles. Design roles to match responsibilities, not individuals.

Concrete example
Create DATA_ANALYST, grant SELECT on SALES_DB, then grant that role to users who need read access.
Common misconception
Roles are not just labels. They carry privileges and can be composed into hierarchies to inherit permissions.
Key takeaway
Model permissions as reusable buckets to simplify onboarding and auditing.
Illustration
Scenario: analyst access
-- As SECURITYADMIN
CREATE ROLE DATA_ANALYST;
GRANT USAGE ON WAREHOUSE COMPUTE_WH TO ROLE DATA_ANALYST;
GRANT USAGE ON DATABASE SALES_DB TO ROLE DATA_ANALYST;
GRANT USAGE ON SCHEMA SALES_DB.PUBLIC TO ROLE DATA_ANALYST;
GRANT SELECT ON ALL TABLES IN SCHEMA SALES_DB.PUBLIC TO ROLE DATA_ANALYST;
This series of grants gives analysts the ability to run queries without granting write or ownership privileges.
Check your understanding
If you grant DATA_ANALYST to DATA_ENGINEER, what privileges does an engineer have? Think about inherited read access and their own extra rights.
