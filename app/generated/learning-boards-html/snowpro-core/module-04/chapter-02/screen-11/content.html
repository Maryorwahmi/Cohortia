Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Auditing Grants & Mistakes
Audit commands
SHOW GRANTS TO ROLE <role_name>; SHOW GRANTS OF ROLE <role_name>; SHOW FUTURE GRANTS;
Use these commands to extract the effective privileges and to verify that future grants are configured correctly.
Common checks
  • Are any users assigned ACCOUNTADMIN unnecessarily?
  • Do roles have only the privileges required for their purpose?
  • Are grants on PUBLIC limited to safe, public resources?
Action
Schedule periodic audits and update role assignments to follow the principle of least privilege.
