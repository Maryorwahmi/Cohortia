Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Future Grants
Pattern
Use GRANT ... ON FUTURE TABLES or FUTURE VIEWS to assign privileges that apply to future objects in the specified schema or database.
-- As SECURITYADMIN
GRANT SELECT ON FUTURE TABLES IN SCHEMA SALES_DB.PUBLIC TO ROLE DATA_ANALYST;
GRANT SELECT ON FUTURE VIEWS IN SCHEMA SALES_DB.PUBLIC TO ROLE DATA_ANALYST;
Verify future grants with SHOW FUTURE GRANTS and be careful to scope by schema or database.
Concrete benefit
When tables are created by pipelines or users, future grants ensure access is available immediately without manual updates.
Example scenario
A nightly ETL creates new partitioned tables. With future grants in place, analysts retain SELECT access for those tables automatically.
Misconception: future grants do not retroactively modify existing objects. Use ALL TABLES or explicit grants for current objects and FUTURE grants for new ones.
