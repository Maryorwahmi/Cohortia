Ownership
Owner role controls all actions; transfer with COPY CURRENT GRANTS to keep grants intact.
Future Grants
Automate privileges on objects created later to maintain consistent access for teams.
Common mistakes
Avoid over-privileging ACCOUNTADMIN and accidental grants to PUBLIC; audit to catch errors.
Final takeaway

Design roles for reuse, automate with future grants, preserve grants on ownership changes, and audit regularly to maintain least privilege.
