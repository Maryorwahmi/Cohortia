Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Security Best Practices
Least privilege
Grant USAGE on database and schema, and SELECT on specific objects. Avoid ALL PRIVILEGES.
Secure views & masking
Use secure views or dynamic data masking to hide sensitive columns while still allowing analysis. Plan masking in coordination with consumers; details come in later chapters.
Audit and governance
  • Use SHOW GRANTS OF SHARE and DESCRIBE SHARE to verify configurations.
  • Keep an approval workflow and periodic reviews for shares.
  • Log who requested and approved each share for compliance.
Common mistake
Accidentally granting more privileges than necessary. Use peer review and automated checks where possible to prevent this.
