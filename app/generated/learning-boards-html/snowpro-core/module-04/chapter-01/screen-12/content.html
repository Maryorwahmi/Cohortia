Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Exchanges & Marketplace
Data Exchange
A private sharing environment for groups of accounts that simplifies management when many consumers need access within an ecosystem.
Use case: partner consortium sharing common reference datasets.
Marketplace
Enables providers to list datasets for discovery by other Snowflake users. Listings can be public or private depending on provider choice.
Use case: published datasets for broader consumption or monetization.
When to choose which
Prefer direct account-to-account shares for simple bilateral relationships, exchanges for closed ecosystems, and Marketplace for discoverable datasets. Consider governance and onboarding complexity when deciding.
