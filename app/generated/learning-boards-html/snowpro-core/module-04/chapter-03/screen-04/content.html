Core Concept › Encryption in Snowflake

What encryption protects

Encryption at rest secures stored bytes; encryption in transit secures client-service communication. Both are automatic in Snowflake.

Data at rest
Stored data is encrypted using AES-256 and a hierarchical key system managed by Snowflake.
Data in transit
TLS protects connections from clients, drivers, and web interfaces to Snowflake.
Misconception
Encryption does not replace column-level controls. You still need masking or tokenization to limit visible results for certain roles.
Key Takeaway

Automatic encryption provides a strong baseline, but visibility controls require masking policies or tokenization.

Concrete example
A table with credit card numbers is stored encrypted. If an attacker gained access to raw storage blobs without keys, the AES-256 encryption prevents reading cleartext. Separately, a user with SELECT privileges could still see those numbers unless a masking policy or tokenization is applied.
Why it matters
  • Reduces operational encryption burden.
  • Satisfies many regulatory baselines.
  • Doesn't eliminate need for role-based visibility controls.
