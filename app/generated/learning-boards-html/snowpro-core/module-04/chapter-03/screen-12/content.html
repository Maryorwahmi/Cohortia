Summary › Key Concepts
Encryption at Rest
Automatic AES-256 encryption of stored data managed by Snowflake.
Encryption in Transit
TLS-secured client-to-service communication for all Snowflake connections.
Dynamic Data Masking
Query-time obfuscation based on role or session context, leaving stored data unchanged.
Summary takeaway
Use encryption for baseline protection, masking for role-based visibility, and tokenization when data must not exist in Snowflake. Combine and govern these controls according to your compliance and performance needs.
