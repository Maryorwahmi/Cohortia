Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Transform Now vs Later
Transform during COPY INTO
Pros: simpler pipeline, fewer steps, immediate usable data. Cons: less raw traceability and harder reprocessing if transformation rules change.
When to use
For stable, lightweight conversions like casting and date parsing.
Stage then transform
Pros: auditability, easier debugging, reprocessing flexibility. Cons: extra table and ETL step.
When to use
For complex transformations, joins, or when governance requires raw preserves.
Key Takeaway
Balance simplicity and traceability: use in-line transforms for light work, staging for complex or auditable flows.
