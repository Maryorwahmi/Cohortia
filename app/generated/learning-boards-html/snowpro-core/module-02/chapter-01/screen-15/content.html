Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Stage
Temporary storage location for files before they are copied into tables.
Internal vs External
Internal stages are Snowflake-managed; external stages reference cloud buckets and require cloud permissions.
PUT & LIST
PUT uploads local files from the client and compresses them; LIST shows staged files for verification.
Chapter takeaway
Design your ingestion to stage, verify, and then load. That sequence improves reliability and makes troubleshooting straightforward.
