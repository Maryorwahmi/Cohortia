Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Recovery & Cost
Time Travel
Configurable retention window for historical access; consumes storage proportional to retained versions.
Fail-safe
A non-configurable seven-day recovery period after Time Travel, managed by Snowflake for disaster scenarios.
Transient Table
No Time Travel or Fail-safe; use for intermediate data to avoid retention storage costs.
Final takeaway

Configure retention, table type, and clustering with an eye to both recovery needs and measurable storage cost, and verify outcomes with ACCOUNT_USAGE views.
