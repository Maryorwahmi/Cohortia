Exam Technique
Question Formats and Strategy
How to approach multiple-choice and multiple-select items and avoid distractors.
Multiple-choice
One best answer. Use elimination and choose the most complete and contextually correct option.
Multiple-select
Select all correct choices. Evaluate each option independently; do not stop after one correct choice.
Distractors
Plausible but wrong options often crafted from partial truths. Check assumptions and scope against the blueprint.
Practice habit
Before reading options, note the item type and any qualifiers. Use elimination, then choose or select all that apply. Time yourself to build exam pacing.
Key Takeaway

Identify item type first. For multiple-select, treat each option independently and avoid stopping after finding one correct answer.
