Key Concepts
Exam Blueprint
The authoritative list of domains, sub-topics, and approximate weightings to guide study focus.
Domain Weighting
Percentages show relative importance; allocate more time to higher-weight domains.
Multiple-Choice
Single best answer items; use elimination to find the most complete and correct option.
Multiple-Select
Select every correct option. Evaluate each choice independently before final selection.
Distractor
A plausible but incorrect option. Look for partial truths and check against domain scope.
Summary Takeaway
Keep the blueprint visible while you study, allocate time by weighting, and practice question formats to build exam-ready habits.
