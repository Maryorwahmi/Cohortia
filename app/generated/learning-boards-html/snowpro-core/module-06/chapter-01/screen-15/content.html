What to Remember
Prioritize by weight
Allocate study time to high-weight domains where your confidence is weakest first.
Practice formats
Simulate timing and treat multiple-select as independent true/false checks for each choice.
Iterate and adapt
After each mock exam, re-annotate the blueprint and shift study hours to newly exposed weak areas.
Final Study Reminder
Use the blueprint as a living document: prioritize, practice, and iterate. Focus your effort where weight and weakness intersect.
