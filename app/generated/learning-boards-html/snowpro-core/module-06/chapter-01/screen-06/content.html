Domain Deep Dive
Virtual Warehouses
Sizing, auto-suspend/resume, multi-cluster, and scaling policies.
Sizing trade-offs
Larger warehouses increase parallelism and throughput but cost more. Right-sizing depends on workload type.
Auto-suspend / Auto-resume
Auto-suspend saves credits by pausing idle compute; set appropriate timeout to avoid unnecessary resume delays.
Multi-cluster behavior
Adds clusters to handle concurrency spikes. Choose scaling policy based on tolerance for queueing and cost sensitivity.
Comparison: Workload → Warehouse
Ad-hoc analytics
Medium/Large warehouse with auto-resume for responsiveness and moderate auto-suspend.
Concurrent dashboards
Multi-cluster with Standard scaling to reduce queueing during spikes.
Batch ETL
Right-size for throughput; auto-suspend after job completion to control cost.
Key Takeaway

Choose size and scaling policy by workload pattern, not by guesswork. Test under realistic load and measure cost.
