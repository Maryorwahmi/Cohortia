Domain Deep Dive
Shared Data
Secure Data Sharing, provider and consumer roles, and marketplace ecosystems.
Provider / Consumer
Provider creates shares and controls access; consumer queries shared objects without copying data.
Data Marketplace
A place to discover and subscribe to third-party shares, enabling data monetization and collaboration.
When to share vs copy
Share for governance and near-real-time access; copy when you need a separate, transformable dataset under different ownership.
Practice prompt
Describe a scenario where sharing is superior to copying, and one where copying is preferable. Use governance and transformation needs as your criteria.
Key Takeaway

Sharing preserves a single source of truth with centralized governance; copying creates independent datasets for local processing and control.
