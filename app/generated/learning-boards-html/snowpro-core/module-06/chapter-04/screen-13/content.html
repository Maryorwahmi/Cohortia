Safety Notes

Avoiding disqualification and careless errors

Two categories: procedural errors that risk the session and cognitive errors that hurt scoring.

Procedural pitfalls
Unauthorized materials, failed system checks, mismatched ID, or ignoring proctor instructions can lead to disqualification.
Cognitive pitfalls
Rushing, misreading qualifiers, and changing answers without cause reduce accuracy.
Preventive actions
Complete the checklist, remove extra monitors, and practice pacing and resets in mock runs.
Key Takeaway

Procedural compliance and disciplined habits prevent lost time and protect your score.

Example

Removing a second monitor and placing ID in hand before check‑in are simple steps that prevent major exam disruptions.

Practice step

Run through a checklist with a partner or record a checklist video to confirm you didn’t miss items under time pressure.

Assessment idea

If your webcam fails 10 minutes before start, contact the proctor’s technical support immediately rather than rescheduling without trying to resolve it.
