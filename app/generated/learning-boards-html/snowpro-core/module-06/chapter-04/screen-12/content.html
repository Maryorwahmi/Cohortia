Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Managing Stress
Definition

Simple techniques to maintain focus

Short breathing and behavioral routines help lower anxiety while conserving time.

Three‑breath reset
Slow inhale for four counts, brief hold, exhale for four counts — repeat three times to calm nerves.
Avoid last‑minute cramming
Cramming increases anxiety and rarely improves recall right before check‑in.
Micro‑breaks
If you feel overwhelmed, take a 10–15 second pause to breathe and then re‑read the stem.
Key Takeaway

Practice quick, repeatable stress techniques during mock runs so they help rather than distract on exam day.

Example

During a timed practice, use the three‑breath reset when a question feels jammed; note the difference in clarity after the pause.

Practice step

Include the reset in every mock exam to make it automatic during the real test.
