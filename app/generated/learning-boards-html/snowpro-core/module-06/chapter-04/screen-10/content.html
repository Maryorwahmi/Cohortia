Comparison

How and when to flag questions

Compare the short‑term cost of time spent now versus the long‑term benefit of revisiting later.

Spend extra time now
May solve one hard problem but risks leaving several easier items unanswered.
Flag and move on
Preserves time to answer all questions and allows later review with broader context.
Rule
If it exceeds first‑pass budget, flag it.
Example
A multi‑step calculation that will take longer than two minutes should be flagged and revisited later.
Practice
Use the mock mini‑quiz to decide what to flag and refine your threshold.
Key Takeaway

Flagging is a planning tool; use it deliberately to prioritize later review when time allows.
