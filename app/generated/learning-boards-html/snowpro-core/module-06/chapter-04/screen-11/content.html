Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Reading Questions
Definition

Read carefully and watch for keyword traps

Small qualifiers in the stem can invert your choice — identify them before evaluating options.

Look for qualifiers
Words like 'most', 'least', 'EXCEPT', and 'all that apply' change the selection criteria.
Concrete tip
Pause longer on the stem to mark qualifiers, then eliminate options that violate them.
Misconception
Speedy reading without parsing qualifiers leads to misread questions and avoidable errors.
Key Takeaway

Small words matter. Identify and apply them to eliminate distractor options confidently.

Example

If the stem asks for the 'most efficient' warehouse setting, an answer that is correct but not efficient will be wrong. Match the qualifier to the option.

Practice step

During practice, annotate the stem with a short note like 'most' or 'except' to create the habit before the real exam.
