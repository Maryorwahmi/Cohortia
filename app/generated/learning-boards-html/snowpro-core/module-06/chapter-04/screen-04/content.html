Process

Essential system and environment checks

Follow these steps 24–48 hours before your exam to reduce risk.

1. Run vendor system check
Verify webcam, microphone, browser, and network. Vendors include specific checks for compatibility.
2. Prepare your room
Clear desks, remove notes, secure pets and household members, and ensure good lighting for a 360° room scan.
3. ID and software
Have a government photo ID and close unrelated programs; disable VPNs and pop‑up blockers that could block proctoring software.
Key Takeaway

Early verification of equipment and environment avoids last‑minute failures and stress.

Common mistake

Skipping the vendor system check or assuming your setup is fine can result in disqualification or rescheduling.

Example

If your browser is outdated, the system check will flag it. Updating your browser or switching to the recommended browser after running the check prevents issues at check‑in.

Practice Step
  1. Run the vendor system check and save the confirmation.
  2. Clear your desk and plug in your laptop.
  3. Place ID nearby and restart before logging in 30 minutes early.
