Process Flow

Check‑in and proctor communication

Log in early and follow proctor instructions exactly; communicate technical issues immediately.

Before start
Log in 15–30 minutes early to complete ID and room scans without rush.
During issues
Use chat or mic to inform the proctor; they can escalate to technical support.
Misconception
Waiting silently worsens the situation; proctors expect and document technical problems when reported.
Key Takeaway

Communicate promptly with the proctor and follow instructions to preserve exam continuity.

Example

If your webcam stops, tell the proctor immediately via chat. They will advise steps and log the incident, preventing misinterpretation of the disconnection.

Practice step

During a mock run, simulate a chat message to the proctor explaining a fake issue to practice concise, useful reporting language.
