Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Internet & Backup
Definition

Ensure a stable connection and a fallback

Test upstream and downstream speeds and plan a tested backup such as a mobile hotspot.

Why it matters
Proctoring depends on continuous audio/video and screen monitoring; disconnections interrupt the session and add friction.
Concrete example
Test a hotspot by streaming video for 15 minutes to ensure consistent upload performance before relying on it.
Misconception
Public Wi‑Fi and untested ISP links are not safe fallbacks for a timed proctored exam.
Key Takeaway

A tested backup connection avoids last‑minute interruptions and preserves exam time.

Quick checklist
  • Run speed test in exam room.
  • Have powered hotspot and test it.
  • Plug in device to avoid power loss.
Practice step

Simulate a 30‑minute monitored session using your webcam and hotspot to confirm continuity under load.
