Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Environment & ID
Definition

Create a compliant testing space and present ID correctly

Room and identification rules are enforced during proctored check‑in to ensure test integrity.

Room requirements
Quiet, clear desk, good lighting, no extra monitors, and no visible notes or devices.
360° scan
Proctors often require a camera pan to confirm the entire room is compliant.
Identification
Valid government photo ID must exactly match the name on your exam registration.
Key Takeaway

Prepare the room and ID in advance; these rules are strictly enforced at check‑in.

Common mistake

Using a nickname on your registration or having items visible on the desk can delay or prevent the session.

Example

A candidate whose registration listed a middle name mismatch was asked to resolve it before starting; matching the registration exactly avoids this delay.

Practice step

Do a dry run where you hold your ID to the camera, pan the room slowly, and confirm a household member will not enter during your window.
