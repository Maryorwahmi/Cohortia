Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Practice Habits
Habit 1: Identify question type
Anchor your reading: conceptual, command-based, or scenario-based. This directs whether to recall a definition, a parameter, or weigh tradeoffs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Habit 2: Read all options
Avoid premature selection. Comparing remaining plausible answers reveals subtleties that decide the single best answer.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Habit 3: Independent checks
For multiple-select, treat each option as true or false; keep evaluating until every option is checked.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Habit 4: Watch distractors
Look for partially-correct wording, answers that are valid but not optimal, and absolute keywords that usually indicate incorrectness.
