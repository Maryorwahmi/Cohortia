Five-step practice

  1. 1. Summarize the requirement — one short phrase capturing the core ask.
  2. 2. Classify the type — conceptual, command-based, or scenario-based.
  3. 3. Eliminate or evaluate — cross out impossible options or check true/false for multi-select.
  4. 4. Compare and decide — ensure the chosen answer satisfies every constraint.
  5. 5. Record reasoning — a one-sentence rationale to review later.
Why record reasoning?
Reviewing your elimination notes reveals patterns: which distractors you miss, which command details need study.
