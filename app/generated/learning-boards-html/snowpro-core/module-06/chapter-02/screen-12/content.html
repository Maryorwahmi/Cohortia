Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Common Pitfalls
Frequent Study Errors
  • Rereading without testing causes illusion of knowledge.
  • Trusting forum answers without checking docs may mislead.
  • Cramming produces fragile recall; spaced repetition produces durability.
  • Ignoring practice-exam diagnostics wastes study time on known areas.
Quick Fixes
Replace a reread with a 5-minute retrieval test, verify community tips in the docs, and convert practice-exam misses into flashcards and scheduled reviews.
Why it matters

Correcting these habits makes study time more effective and raises the chance of success on exam day.
