Sample Question (Diagnostic)
Question: Which two study techniques are most effective for long-term retention of complex Snowflake concepts? Choices: A. Rereading notes. B. Creating flashcards and testing yourself. C. Watching a single lecture. D. Scheduling reviews at increasing intervals. Correct: B and D.
Reflection: When you miss such a question, note whether the gap is conceptual (need a doc dive), procedural (need an example run), or recall-based (add flashcards).
Action

Convert each missed question into one or two flashcards and schedule review intervals accordingly.
