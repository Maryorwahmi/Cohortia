Pomodoro-Based Weekly Plan
  • Daily: 1 Pomodoro for review (25 minutes), 1 Pomodoro for a new topic.
  • Three times weekly: 2 Pomodoros focused on weak domains from practice exams.
  • Weekly reflection: adjust slots based on progress and remaining weak topics.
This plan keeps study sustainable and aligns with spaced repetition by dedicating regular short reviews.
Quick Prioritization Matrix
High Impact / Weak → Schedule now
High Impact / Known → Review occasionally
Low Impact → De-prioritize
