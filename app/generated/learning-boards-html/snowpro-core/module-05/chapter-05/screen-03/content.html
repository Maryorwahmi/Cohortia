Practical Skills and Tests

Concrete checks that prove your policies work and won't disrupt production.

Grants & Ownership
Apply policies from the owning role or grant APPLY privileges to the owner. Avoid using ACCOUNTADMIN for routine application to keep least privilege.
Testing with Roles
Switch roles and run SELECTs to confirm different masked outputs and row visibility. Use mapping table rows to validate row filters.
Common Mistakes
Overly complex policy logic, incomplete mapping tables, and applying policies from non-owning roles can cause unexpected denials or performance issues.
Quick checklist
  • Grant CREATE and APPLY privileges correctly.
  • Test with at least two roles.
  • Keep mapping queries efficient.
  • Standardize tags for discovery.
