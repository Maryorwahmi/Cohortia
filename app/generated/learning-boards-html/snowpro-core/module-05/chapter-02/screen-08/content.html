Definition › Credit Types
Compute credits
Consumed by virtual warehouses while executing queries or loads. Use monitors to limit these credits.
Inspect: WAREHOUSE_METERING_HISTORY
Storage credits
Accrue as data is stored, including time travel and fail-safe. Manage with retention and cleanup policies.
Inspect: STORAGE_USAGE, DATABASE_STORAGE_USAGE_HISTORY
Cloud services
Account-level operations and metadata management consume credits used for services and optimizations.
Inspect: ACCOUNT_USAGE service views
Example contrast
A long-running analytical query raises compute credits immediately, whereas adding a large dataset increases storage credits over time.
Misconception check
A monitor will not directly reduce storage costs; you must apply retention or archive policies for that.
Key Takeaway

Match your control tools to the credit type: monitors for compute, lifecycle policies for storage, governance for services.
