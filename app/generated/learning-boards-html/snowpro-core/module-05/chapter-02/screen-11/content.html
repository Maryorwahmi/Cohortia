Common Mistakes › Pitfalls & Best Practices
Frequent mistakes
  • Not creating monitors or setting quotas too high to be effective.
  • Using notifications only for critical budgets and not enforcing suspension.
  • Applying SUSPEND_IMMEDIATE in production without sandbox testing.
  • Ignoring ACCOUNT_USAGE and not tuning quotas over time.
Best practices
  • Use account-level plus warehouse-level monitors for layered protection.
  • Tier triggers: notify early, suspend when necessary.
  • Test suspend effects in a non-production environment.
  • Schedule regular reviews of ACCOUNT_USAGE to adjust quotas.
Key Takeaway

Treat monitors as part of an ongoing governance cycle: configure, test, monitor, and tune to keep costs predictable without disrupting essential work.
