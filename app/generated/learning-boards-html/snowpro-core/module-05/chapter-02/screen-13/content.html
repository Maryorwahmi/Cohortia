Best Practice › Tiered Monitor Strategy
Account-level
A high-level budget cap for the entire account, useful as a final safety net.
Example: Monthly quota to prevent runaway total spend.
Warehouse-level
Targeted quotas for ETL, dev, or reporting to balance cost and availability.
Example: ETL_WH with lower daily quota and SUSPEND_IMMEDIATE at final threshold.
Governance
Regular review cycles and communication with teams to tune quotas and avoid surprises.
Example: Monthly review of WAREHOUSE_METERING_HISTORY and quota adjustments.
Key Takeaway

Use layered monitors and review data regularly to maintain cost control without harming critical operations.
