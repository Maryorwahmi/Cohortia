Explain resource monitors

Configure and manage monitors
Create monitors with CREDIT_QUOTA and FREQUENCY, and practice assigning them to warehouses to control specific workloads.
Mnemonic: Quota + Frequency = control window.

03
Understand credit types
Differentiate compute, storage, and cloud services credits so you can allocate budget accurately and interpret usage reports.
Mnemonic: Compute, Storage, Services — the credit triangle.

Analyze and alert using ACCOUNT_USAGE
Use WAREHOUSE_METERING_HISTORY and QUERY_HISTORY to find cost drivers and configure alerts that act on real consumption patterns.
Mnemonic: Meter, Query, Alert.
