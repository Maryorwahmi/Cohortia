Comparison › Triggers
NOTIFY
Sends email alerts at configured percentages. Best for early warning and investigation without immediate action.
Use when teams need context before action.
SUSPEND
Prevents warehouses from being resumed further in the current period but may allow running queries to finish.
A softer enforcement suited to less-critical workloads.
SUSPEND_IMMEDIATE
Immediately suspends warehouses and cancels running queries, stopping credit accrual at once.
Powerful but disruptive; always test first.
Tiered trigger strategy
Notify at 50% and 75% for visibility, then suspend or suspend immediate at 100% for enforcement.
Misuse warning
Relying solely on notifications for critical budgets risks ongoing overspend; pair notifications with enforcement where needed.
Key Takeaway

Match trigger severity to business risk: notify early, suspend for control, suspend immediate for strict enforcement.
