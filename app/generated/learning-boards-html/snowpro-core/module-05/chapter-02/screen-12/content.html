Assessment example
Create a monthly monitor for ETL_WH with 100 credit quota, notify at 80% to data_ops, and suspend immediately at 100%.
CREATE RESOURCE MONITOR etl_monthly_monitor
  WITH CREDIT_QUOTA = 100
  FREQUENCY = MONTHLY
  TRIGGERS
    ON 80 PERCENT DO NOTIFY EMAIL 'data_ops@company.com'
    ON 100 PERCENT DO SUSPEND_IMMEDIATE;
ALTER WAREHOUSE ETL_WH SET RESOURCE_MONITOR = etl_monthly_monitor;
Interpretation tip
Confirm frequency matches requirement, use NOTIFY EMAIL syntax for email alerts, and prefer SUSPEND_IMMEDIATE if immediate stop is required. Use WAREHOUSE_METERING_HISTORY to validate whether 100 credits suffice.
Key Takeaway

Assessments focus on accurate SQL, correct trigger selection, and using ACCOUNT_USAGE to justify thresholds.
