Data Replication and Failover/Failback
Why it matters: keep data available across accounts and regions for DR, BC, and lower-latency reads.
In this lesson

You will learn how Snowflake copies a primary database to secondary accounts, how failover groups coordinate role changes across related objects, and the commands to create replicas and trigger failover.

Coming up

Examples include CREATE DATABASE ... AS REPLICA OF and ALTER FAILOVER GROUP ... PRIMARY, plus a simulated hands-on activity to practice failover and failback.

Key takeaway

Replication keeps synchronized copies; failover groups let you promote a replica to writable to resume operations.
