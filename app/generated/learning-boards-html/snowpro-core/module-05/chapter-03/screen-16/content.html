Module 05 › Chapter 5.3 › What to Remember
Secondary Database
A read-only, continuously synchronized copy of the primary stored in a target account.
Failover
Promoting a replica failover group to primary, making its databases writable in that account.
Failback
The controlled return of the primary role to the original account after validating data parity.
Final takeaway

Build runbooks, rehearse failover and failback in non-production, and monitor replication to keep recovery predictable and safe.
