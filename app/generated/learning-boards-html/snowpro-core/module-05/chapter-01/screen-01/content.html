Visual summary
Snowflake Account
Isolated workspace provisioned on one cloud and region. Identified in the URL by account identifier and region id.
Organization
Umbrella for multiple accounts. Use it to centralize billing and cross-account management for enterprise scale.
Parameters
Set at account, user, or session. Session overrides are temporary and useful for one-off changes.
Tip: Always map your accounts to organizational needs: compliance, environments, and billing.
