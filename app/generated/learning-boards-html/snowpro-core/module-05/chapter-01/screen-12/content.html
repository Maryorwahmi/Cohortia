Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Roles & ACCOUNTADMIN
Role hierarchy (example)
ACCOUNTADMIN
Full account control. Reserve for emergency and high-trust tasks.
SYSADMIN
Manage warehouses and databases.
USERADMIN
Manage users and roles; day-to-day user lifecycle tasks.
Key Takeaway

Apply least privilege by creating targeted admin roles and limit ACCOUNTADMIN to tasks that truly require it.

Concrete example
Create a WarehouseAdmin role with only warehouse management privileges and assign it to operations. Keep ACCOUNTADMIN for security and billing adjustments.
Common mistake
Granting ACCOUNTADMIN widely for convenience increases chance of accidental configuration changes and broad access to sensitive controls.
Reflect
Who in your organization currently holds ACCOUNTADMIN and could have duties moved to more focused roles?
