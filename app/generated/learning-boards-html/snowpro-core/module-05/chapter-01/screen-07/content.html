Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Snowflake Organization
Definition
Top-level container for multiple accounts
Provides centralized billing, visibility, and coordination for cross-account features like replication. Accounts remain independent workspaces.
Concrete scenario
An enterprise creates an Organization with production accounts in two regions, plus development and staging accounts, so billing and governance can be centralized while teams work in separate accounts.
Key Takeaway

Organizations simplify management across many accounts but do not remove account-level isolation unless you explicitly configure sharing or replication.

Benefits
  • Consolidated billing across linked accounts.
  • Centralized control plane for administrative visibility.
  • Simplifies multi-account replication and governance.
Common mistake
Expecting an Organization to automatically unify data or permissions across accounts. You still configure sharing, replication, and roles per account as needed.
Reflect
If your company has multiple lines of business or strict environment separation, consider an Organization to centralize billing and coordination while keeping accounts separate.
