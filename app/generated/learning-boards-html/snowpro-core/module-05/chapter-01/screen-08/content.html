Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › When to Use Organizations
Use cases
  • Environment separation: dev, staging, production in separate accounts.
  • Geographic or business unit isolation for compliance.
  • Consolidated billing and cross-account coordination.
Concrete example
A company uses an Organization to centralize billing for accounts in EU and US while preserving separate accounts to comply with regional laws and reduce blast radius of changes.
Key Takeaway

Choose Organizations when you need centralized finance, governance, or replication coordination across multiple accounts while preserving isolation.

Trade-offs
  • Pros: centralized billing, governance, coordinated replication.
  • Cons: more operational coordination across accounts and potential for complex access models.
Common mistake
Creating unnecessary accounts and an Organization without documented policies. Organizational structure should follow clear needs, not just perceived best practice.
Reflect
Do you need consolidated billing and centralized replication orchestration, or will per-account management suffice for now?
