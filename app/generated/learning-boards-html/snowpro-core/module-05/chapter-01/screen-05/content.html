Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Reading the Account URL
Structure
https://<account_identifier>.<region_id>.snowflakecomputing.com
The account_identifier is unique to your account. The region_id indicates the cloud and geographic site.
Concrete example
If the URL contains aws_eu_central_1, that means the account is hosted on AWS in the EU Central region. This signals EU data residency and lower latency for EU users.
Key Takeaway

Always confirm the region_id for compliance and correct client connections; the account identifier alone does not specify location.

Why this matters
  • Connectivity: clients require the correct endpoint to connect to the intended account.
  • Compliance: region determines where data is stored and processed.
  • Latency: pick regions near your users and data sources.
Common mistake
Assuming the account identifier shows location. Instead, rely on region_id when validating compliance or planning replication.
Reflect
When you inspect your account URL today, does the region align with regulatory and proximity requirements for your primary users?
