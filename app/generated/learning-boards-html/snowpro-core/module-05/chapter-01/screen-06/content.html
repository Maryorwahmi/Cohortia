Comparison: Latency vs Compliance
Latency-focused
Choose a region closest to users and data sources for best performance.
Compliance-focused
Choose a region that satisfies legal requirements even if slightly farther from users.
Concrete example
If most users and sources are in Europe and regulations mandate EU storage, provision the account in an EU region such as azure_west_europe or aws_eu_central_1 to meet residency and latency needs.
Key Takeaway

Treat region selection as a governance decision. Document constraints and plan replication for DR rather than relying on a single account to satisfy all needs.

Planning checklist
  • Regulatory requirements for each dataset.
  • Primary user locations and acceptable latency.
  • Where upstream data sources live.
  • Disaster recovery and replication strategy.
Common mistake
Choosing region purely on cost or convenience without validating residency. That can create compliance risk even if performance is good.
Reflect
Which data sets in your organization have strict residency rules and need dedicated accounts in a compliant region?
