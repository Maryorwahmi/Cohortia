Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › What is a Snowflake Account?
Definition
An isolated environment for data warehousing
  • Provisioned on a single cloud provider and region.
  • Contains warehouses, databases, users, and parameters.
  • Provides a security and billing boundary.
Concrete example
A company uses one account for production in an EU region for customer data, and a separate account in another region for development to isolate testing workloads and billing.
Key Takeaway

Design accounts around ownership, compliance, and operational boundaries. Do not assume an account spans regions.

Why isolation matters
  • Security: limits blast radius of misconfiguration or breach.
  • Performance: resource limits and warehouses are scoped per account.
  • Compliance: regional provisioning aligns to data residency rules.
Common mistake
Choosing a single account to try to cover all regions or environments. Instead, use multiple accounts and manage them via an Organization when centralization is needed.
Reflect
Which workloads in your organization need strict regional isolation? Use that as the primary driver for separate accounts.
