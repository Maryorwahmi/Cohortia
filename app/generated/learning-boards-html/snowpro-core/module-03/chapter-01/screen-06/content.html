Size Hierarchy
Sizes map to cluster counts and approximate compute power. The pattern doubles capacity as you grow one tier at a time.
X-SMALL → 1 cluster
SMALL → 2 clusters
MEDIUM → 4 clusters
LARGE → 8 clusters (and so on)
When to choose which
  • Use X-SMALL or SMALL for light reporting and small ETL jobs.
  • Use MEDIUM or LARGE for complex analytics and heavy joins.
  • Prefer larger single clusters to speed individual complex queries.
  • Prefer multi-cluster for concurrency across many simultaneous users or queries.
Common mistake
Choosing X-SMALL for heavy analytic workloads or choosing an X-LARGE for trivial queries can cause either long runtimes or unnecessary costs. Match the size to the workload profile.
