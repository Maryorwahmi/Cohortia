Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Time Travel vs Fail-safe
Side-by-side comparison
Time Travel
  • User-accessible and configurable (0–90 days).
  • Supports AT and BEFORE queries, UNDROP, and cloning from history.
  • Used for audits, ad-hoc recovery, and development snapshots.
Fail-safe
  • Internal, non-configurable 7-day period after Time Travel.
  • Accessible only to Snowflake for disaster recovery.
  • Not queryable or directly usable for user restores.
Governance recommendation
Set Time Travel retention to meet your recovery time objective and compliance needs instead of relying on Fail-safe. Longer retention increases storage cost.
