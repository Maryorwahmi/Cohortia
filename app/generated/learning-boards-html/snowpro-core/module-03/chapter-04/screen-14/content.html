What to remember
Time Travel
Access historical data for a configured retention window to audit or recover recent changes.
AT & BEFORE
Use AT for timestamp/offset and BEFORE with a Query ID to read just-before states of a table.
Fail-safe
Internal 7-day protection after Time Travel ends; not directly accessible to users for queries or restores.
Takeaway
Design retention policies to meet recovery and compliance goals, record timestamps before risky changes, and verify recoveries before resuming production use.
