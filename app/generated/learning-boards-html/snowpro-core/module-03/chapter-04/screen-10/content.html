Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Retention settings
Retention levels and cost trade-offs
Where to set retention
  • Account-wide default retention.
  • Database or schema level overrides.
  • Table-level settings for fine-grained control.
Default: standard tables 1 day; transient/temporary 0 days; max 90 days.
Policy considerations
Decide retention by data criticality: long retention for compliance-sensitive tables, short retention for ephemeral staging. Monitor storage costs as retention grows.
Pro tip

Use table-level settings for high-value tables and keep staging objects transient to minimize cost.
