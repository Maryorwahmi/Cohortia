Why concurrency matters
Multiple teams and automated jobs often run at the same time. Without isolation, one job can slow everyone else.
Noisy neighbor example
A large ETL job runs while analysts refresh dashboards and notice slow queries and timeouts.
Misconception
Faster single servers remove contention. In practice, shared compute still causes interference without isolation.
Isolation pattern
Independent virtual warehouses provide separate compute for teams while allowing all to read the same shared storage.
Concrete result
Each team can scale or pause its warehouse without impacting others, so performance becomes predictable.
Key Takeaway

Concurrency failures are operationally painful; workload isolation is a practical way to avoid them and keep analytics flowing.
