Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Traditional On-Premise
What traditional warehouses were
Server rooms with tightly coupled storage and compute that required planning and manual operations.
Capital and maintenance
Large upfront purchase of servers, licensing, and ongoing patching and tuning by specialists.
Slow scaling
Adding capacity required procurement and setup, making bursty workloads expensive to support.
Concurrency limits
Multiple users could starve resources and see degraded performance under load.
Concrete scenario
A retailer must provision extra servers months before a holiday season. If forecasts are wrong, either reports are slow or unused servers waste money.
Common Mistake
Assuming on-premise always gives better control; in reality, the overhead of maintenance and scaling often reduces agility.
Key Takeaway

Tightly coupled systems force either wasted capacity or performance pain during peaks. This is the gap cloud warehouses address.
