Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Scaling Process
How scaling worked before the cloud
Step-by-step
1
Forecast demand
Teams predict peak usage months ahead to plan purchases.
2
Procure hardware
Purchase cycles and approvals add weeks to months.
3
Install and test
Integration and tuning require specialists and downtime windows.
4
Run and adjust
Ongoing tuning responds to evolving workloads, often reactively.
Concrete example
A team orders extra capacity for a known seasonal spike. If traffic is lower than forecast, compute sits idle. If higher, users wait for new hardware and analytics slow until scaling completes.
Why it matters
Long procurement cycles and static capacity either waste money or delay insights.
Misconception
Adding raw storage always solves scaling. Often compute is the bottleneck and more disks do not improve query throughput.
