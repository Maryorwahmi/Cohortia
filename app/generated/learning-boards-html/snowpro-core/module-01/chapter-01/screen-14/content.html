Sample Questions
Question 1
Which of the following is a primary benefit of cloud warehousing over traditional on-premise solutions? a) Higher upfront capital costs. b) Reduced operational burden due to managed services. c) Manual scaling of compute and storage. d) Tightly coupled storage and compute.
Correct: b
Question 2
Snowflake's Data Cloud vision primarily focuses on: a) cheapest possible storage, b) global data discovery and sharing, c) on-premise solutions, d) restricting access to internal teams. Correct: b.
Study tip
Contrast on-premise and cloud statements side-by-side, and verify answers by running the hands-on activity to observe behavior in a trial account.
