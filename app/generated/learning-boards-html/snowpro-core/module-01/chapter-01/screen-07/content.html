Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Cloud Benefits
Core cloud advantages
Elastic scaling, pay-for-use billing, and managed services reduce friction and speed analytics.
Elasticity
Scale compute and storage independently, adding resources in minutes for temporary load.
Pay-as-you-go
Costs align with usage so you avoid large upfront capital and waste from idle hardware.
Managed operations
Providers handle patching and many routine tasks so your engineers work on product-level problems.
Concrete example
A short marketing spike can be handled by spinning up additional compute and then shutting it down, minimizing cost while meeting demand.
Misconception
Cloud is not just cheaper hardware. Its value comes from elasticity, operational simplification, and billing tied to actual usage.
Key Takeaway

Cloud warehouses let teams align cost with demand and focus on analytics, not infrastructure chores.
