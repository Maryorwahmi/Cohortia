Built-in protections and efficiency
Compression, encryption, and recovery mechanisms improve cost, security, and continuity.
Automatic compression
Columnar storage and micro-partitioning enable high compression ratios, lowering storage cost.
Encryption
Platform-level encryption protects data at rest and in transit to meet security needs.
Concrete example
Large historical datasets are stored at lower cost via compression, while encryption guards sensitive fields and backups support recovery.
Misconception
Built-in protections replace governance. Teams still define access policies and retention aligned to compliance.
Key Takeaway

Platform features reduce operational risk and cost, but governance and access controls remain essential responsibilities.
