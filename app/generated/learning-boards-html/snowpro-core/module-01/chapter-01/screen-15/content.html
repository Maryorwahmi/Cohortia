Key Concepts
Traditional Data Warehouse
On-premise systems with tightly coupled storage and compute that require manual scaling and maintenance.
Cloud Data Warehouse
Elastic, pay-as-you-go platforms that reduce operational burden and allow on-demand scaling.
Snowflake Data Cloud
A vision for secure data discovery, sharing, and collaboration across organizations with workload isolation.
Summary
Remember: separate storage and compute, use independent warehouses for isolation, and treat the platform as an enabler of secure collaboration.
