Quick Operational Reminders
Separate storage & compute
Scale each layer independently to match workload needs and control cost.
Multi-cluster access
Use independent warehouses to prevent heavy jobs from impacting others.
Automation + governance
Leverage automation but maintain models, access policies, and retention rules.
Final thought
Observe these principles in your trial account to make the architecture tangible and prepare for practical application.
