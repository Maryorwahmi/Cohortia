Comparison

Size vs cost trade-offs

Larger warehouses increase parallelism at higher cost. Autosuspend controls idle billing. Use measurement to guide choices.

Small warehouse
Lower cost, suitable for dashboards and light queries.
Large warehouse
Higher cost, better for heavy ETL and parallel workloads.
Example
Nightly ETL: large warehouse, suspend after job completes. Interactive BI: small warehouse, short auto-suspend. Monitor credits and queue metrics to optimize.
Key Takeaway

Measure first, then scale. Use auto-suspend to keep compute costs aligned with active use.

Common mistake
Scaling preemptively without metrics. Adjust based on queue length and elapsed time rather than assumption.
