Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Storage Costs
Billing components
  • Average compressed storage billed per month (per TB).
  • Time Travel retains historical data and increases storage usage.
  • Fail-safe is a mandatory 7-day retention for disaster recovery and is billed.
Example
Storing 10 TB results in charges for 10 TB that month, and increasing Time Travel will raise that average.
Key Takeaway

Control retention windows and use zero-copy clones to reduce duplicate storage while keeping recovery capabilities.
