What Business Critical adds
  • Compliance certifications (HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP)
  • Tri-Secret Secure for enhanced key management
  • Database failover and failback for continuity
This edition is appropriate when audits and contractual obligations require documented encryption and failover controls beyond basic protections.
Concrete example
A healthcare organization needs documented key separation and failover testing to meet HIPAA. Business Critical supports these needs.
Key Takeaway

Use Business Critical when audits require advanced key control and failover capabilities.
