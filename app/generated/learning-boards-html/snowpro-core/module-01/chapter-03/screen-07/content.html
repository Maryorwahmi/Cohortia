Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Virtual Private Snowflake
What VPS provides
  • Dedicated Snowflake service instance
  • Isolated metadata and compute resources
  • Designed for agencies and very large enterprises with strict isolation needs
VPS is a specialized option and not necessary for most organizations. Evaluate contractual and legal separation requirements first.
Example
A government body that requires a fully segregated metadata service and dedicated compute would consider VPS.
Key Takeaway

VPS is for extreme isolation; choose it only when policy or law demands dedicated infrastructure.
