Credit consumption by size
  • X-Small = 1 credit/hour
  • Small = 2 credits/hour
  • Medium = 4 credits/hour
  • Each size doubles the credits of the previous one
Controls to reduce compute spend
  • Enable AUTO_SUSPEND (5–10 minutes recommended)
  • Use AUTO_RESUME to start on demand
  • Right-size warehouses to match workload
Concrete example

Short ETL jobs are cheaper on an X-Small with a 5-minute auto-suspend than on a large warehouse that is left running between jobs.
