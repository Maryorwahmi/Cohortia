Understanding Snowflake Editions & Pricing Model
How edition choice and consumption-based billing affect features, compliance, and costs.
In this lesson
Compare editions, read cost drivers for storage and compute, and learn optimization controls like auto-suspend and zero-copy cloning.
Why it matters
Choosing the wrong edition or leaving warehouses running are common causes of unexpected bills and operational gaps in compliance and availability.
Quick orientation

Edition = features & compliance. Storage = TB/month. Compute = credits while warehouses run. Cloud Services usually included.

Edition & Cost Snapshot
Standard → Enterprise → Business Critical → VPS
Standard
Core features, basic security, suitable for small teams.
Enterprise
Multi-cluster, longer Time Travel, materialized views.
Business Critical
Advanced compliance, Tri-Secret Secure, failover.
