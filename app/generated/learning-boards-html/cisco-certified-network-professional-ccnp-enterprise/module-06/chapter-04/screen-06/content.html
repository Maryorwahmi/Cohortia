MTU Mismatch

Why ExStart stalls

DBD packets must fit the peer's MTU. If they don't, DBDs are dropped and adjacency cannot proceed.

show ip interface <interface_type/number>
  (compare MTU values on both ends)
Practical tip
If tunnels or encapsulation reduce effective MTU, address the encapsulation or adjust segmentation rather than arbitrarily raising MTU.
ACLs and OSPF

Blocking OSPF traffic

ACLs that block OSPF multicast (224.0.0.5 / 224.0.0.6) or relevant unicast ports will prevent neighbor formation or LSDB exchange.

Quick checks
  • Ping the neighbor IP to verify IP reachability.
  • Inspect interface ACLs and global ACLs for multicast or TCP/OSPF-related filtering.
  • Use counters and debug only in maintenance windows.
