Learning Objectives

Measureable skills and competency milestones for troubleshooting routing.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Diagnose OSPF Adjacencies

Interpret OSPF states and use show/debug commands to find mismatched timers, MTU, area IDs or ACL blockages. Verified competency milestone.

02
Troubleshoot EIGRP Neighbor Issues

Check AS, K-values, authentication and passive interfaces to restore neighbor formation and route exchange. Verified competency milestone.

03
Resolve BGP Peering Failures

Validate TCP connectivity, remote-as, MD5 keys and update-source settings to reach Established. Verified competency milestone.

04
Perform Safe Redistribution

Apply seed metrics, OSPF subnets and route tags to exchange routes without loops or suboptimal paths. Verified competency milestone.
