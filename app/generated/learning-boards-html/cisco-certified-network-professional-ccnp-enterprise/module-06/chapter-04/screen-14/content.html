Key Concepts

OSPF Adjacency States
Progression from Down to Full indicates success of neighbor discovery and LSDB sync.
EIGRP K-values
Coefficients that must match for routes to be accepted and exchanged.
BGP Peering States
Idle to Established maps TCP and BGP message progress; TCP/179 must work first.
Summary Takeaway

Use protocol states and targeted show/debug commands to localize faults quickly, then apply the minimal corrective change and verify.
