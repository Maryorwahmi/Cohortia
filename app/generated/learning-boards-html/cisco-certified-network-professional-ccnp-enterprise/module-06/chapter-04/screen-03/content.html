How you'll apply the skills

Concrete lab tasks and verification steps to demonstrate competency.

OSPF Lab
Find a neighbor stuck in INIT, compare hello timers and correct the mismatch. Verify FULL state with show ip ospf neighbor.
EIGRP Lab
Confirm AS and K-values, correct authentication, and ensure interfaces are not passive when neighbors are expected.
BGP Lab
Test TCP connectivity to port 179, verify update-source for loopbacks, and inspect show ip bgp summary for state progress.
