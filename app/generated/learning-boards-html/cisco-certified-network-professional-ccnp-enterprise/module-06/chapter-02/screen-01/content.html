Network Monitoring, Baselining, and Alerting
Establish continuous visibility and proactive alerts using device telemetry, flow data, syslog, and synthetic tests.
In this lesson
You will learn to configure SNMP, NetFlow, Syslog, and IP SLA, collect data into a central monitoring server, and use baselining to reduce false positives.
Why it matters
Early detection and context-rich alerts let teams remediate before user impact and reduce time to root cause during incidents.
