Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Alerting Architecture
Typical alert flow
Telemetry → Collector/NMS → Baseline-aware rules → Enrichment (flows, logs, SLA stats) → Notification / Automation
Enrichment example
When an interface reports high utilization, include NetFlow top talkers and recent syslog entries in the alert payload for faster triage.
Avoid alert fatigue
Prefer sustained deviations and correlated signals for high-priority alerts; route lower-confidence signals to dashboards or low-priority tickets.
Design checklist
  • Define baseline-aware thresholds and hold time to avoid transient alerts.
  • Correlate multiple telemetry sources for higher confidence alerts.
  • Map alert severity to escalation policies and automation actions.
Key Takeaway

Well-designed alerts combine signal, context, and severity to reduce noise and speed remediation; automate enrichment where useful.
