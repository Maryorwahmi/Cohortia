Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
SNMP
Protocol for device metrics and traps; rely on MIBs and prefer SNMPv3 for security.
NetFlow / IPFIX
Flow metadata for top talkers, protocol distribution, and anomaly detection; plan exporters and sampling.
Syslog
Centralized logging with severity levels and facilities for correlation and retention control.
Summary takeaway
Secure telemetry, correlate multiple sources, baseline normal behavior, and tune alerts to reduce noise and accelerate response.
