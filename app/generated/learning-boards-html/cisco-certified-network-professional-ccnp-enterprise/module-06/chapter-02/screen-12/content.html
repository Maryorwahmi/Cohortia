Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Baselining Process
Three-step baseline process
  1. Collect: SNMP counters, NetFlow summaries, and IP SLA results over representative periods.
  2. Analyze: compute medians, percentiles, and identify recurring patterns for business hours and off-hours.
  3. Define alerts: set thresholds based on percentiles and require sustained deviations before alerting.
Example
Use the 95th percentile of interface utilization as a capacity marker and trigger alerts on sustained exceedance above that point.
Avoiding pitfalls
  • Do not set thresholds from a single day; use representative samples.
  • Rebaseline after major topology or workload changes.
  • Correlate multiple signals before firing major alerts to reduce noise.
Key Takeaway

Baselining turns raw telemetry into meaningful thresholds; collect representative data and update baselines as the network changes.
