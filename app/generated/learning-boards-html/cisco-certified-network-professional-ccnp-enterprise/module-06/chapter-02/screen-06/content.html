What a flow record contains
Typical fields include source/destination IP, source/destination port, protocol, packet and byte counts, and timestamps.
Example: incident analysis
A sudden rise in bytes from one source to many destinations appears in the collector as a top talker spike, guiding investigation to that host.
Misconception
NetFlow does not capture packet payloads; it provides metadata for visibility and forensic analysis without full packet capture overhead.
Key Takeaway

NetFlow reveals who is talking on the network and how much; use it to find top talkers and traffic hotspots, but plan sampling to protect device performance.

Collector outputs (typical cards)
Top Talkers
Hosts or prefixes producing most bytes
Protocol Mix
Distribution of TCP, UDP, ICMP, etc.
Time Series
Traffic trends for capacity planning
Anomaly Alerts
Spikes or protocol changes that deviate from baseline
