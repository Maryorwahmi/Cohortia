Process
Documentation & Baselining
What to keep current
  • Network addressing plans and device roles.
  • Standard configuration snippets and interface maps.
  • Operational baselines for CPU, memory, and latency by device and interface.
  • Change logs with author, time, and description.
Practical tip
Check change records before extensive troubleshooting—many incidents stem from recent, undocumented changes.
Key takeaway
Operational hygiene—accurate docs, baselines, and change control—reduces time to identify root cause and prevents repeated incidents.
