Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Operational Checklist
Evidence First
Collect show outputs to form facts before making changes or enabling debug.
Targeted Debug
Use protocol-specific debug, filters, and a firm stop command to protect device health.
Check Ops Context
Consult baselines and change logs early; recent changes often point to the root cause.
Final takeaway

Make evidence collection, targeted debugging, and operational checks the routine start of every troubleshooting task.
