Process
Configuration and Topology Verification
Use show running-config to find
  • Interface modes, VLAN assignments, and IP addresses.
  • ACLs and service policies that might block traffic.
  • Static routes or redistribution settings affecting reachability.
Use CDP/LLDP to
  • Confirm the neighbor device on a physical port and its system name.
  • Verify expected layer-2 topology and detect unexpected connections.
  • Cross-check VLAN or native VLAN information where available.
Key takeaway
Reconcile running-config intent with neighbor discovery to spot mismatches between design and reality before making changes.
