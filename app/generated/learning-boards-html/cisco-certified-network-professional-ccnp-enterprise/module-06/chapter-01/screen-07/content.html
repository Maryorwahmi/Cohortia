Traceroute workflow
Run traceroute and interpret each hop. Identify the first hop that fails to respond or shows increased latency, and then collect show outputs from that device to find the cause. 
traceroute 192.168.10.100
  1  10.0.10.1   1.2 ms
  2  10.0.20.1  12.8 ms
  3  172.16.5.2  *  *  *
Interpretation: hop 3 drops or filters traceroute probes. Inspect device at 172.16.5.2 for interface down, ACL, or routing issues.
Limitations
Some devices deprioritize TTL-expired responses or block ICMP; traceroute should be combined with show commands and pings to confirm findings.
Key takeaway
Use traceroute to locate the problematic hop, then verify that device's state and configuration with show commands.
