Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › monitor capture on IOS‑XE
What it is
A built-in router capture facility

monitor capture lets you define named buffers, bind them to interfaces and directions, and apply match criteria so captures are device-local and exportable to PCAP.

When to prefer
When you cannot place a mirror port or need visibility into router-processed traffic such as NAT, routing, or control-plane interactions.
Resource note & safety
  • Apply ACL match statements to limit captured traffic and avoid filling buffers.
  • Stop and clear captures when finished to free memory: monitor capture NAME stop / clear.
  • Large captures can impact router performance if left running without filters.
Key Takeaway

monitor capture is a powerful option when you need device-local packet visibility; always scope captures with ACLs and manage buffers carefully.
