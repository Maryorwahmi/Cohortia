Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Choosing the Right Capture Method
Quick comparison
SPAN
Local, simple, requires analyzer on same switch.
RSPAN
Uses dedicated VLAN across switches; isolate from production traffic.
ERSPAN
GRE-encapsulated; routable across IP networks; plan GRE and MTU.
monitor capture
Device-local capture; apply ACLs to reduce buffer use and export to PCAP for Wireshark.
Decision checklist
  • Where is the analyzer? Same switch, different switch, or remote IP?
  • Does the path cross IP boundaries or WAN links?
  • Can you dedicate an isolated VLAN or GRE tunnel for mirrors?
  • Do you need router-processed visibility that only device capture provides?
Key Takeaway

Pick the least complex method that gives you the required visibility. Simpler is safer when it meets the need.
