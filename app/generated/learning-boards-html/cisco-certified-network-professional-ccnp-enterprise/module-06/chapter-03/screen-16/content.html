Plan the method
SPAN, RSPAN, ERSPAN, or device capture — pick based on analyzer location and network boundaries.
Scope captures
Use ACLs, VLAN isolation, and filters to limit captured traffic and protect device resources.
Analyze & clean up
Use display filters, Follow TCP Stream, Expert Information, then stop and clear captures when done.
Final takeaway

Packet captures are evidence. Capture deliberately, analyze carefully, and remove artifacts to keep networks safe and performant.
