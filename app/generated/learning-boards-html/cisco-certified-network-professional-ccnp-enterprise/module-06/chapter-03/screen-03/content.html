Analysis & Troubleshooting
Skills for converting captures into diagnoses
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Export PCAPs
Use IOS‑XE export to transfer captures to a server and open them in Wireshark for detailed inspection.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Apply Wireshark filters
Construct display filters to isolate endpoints, ports, and flags so you can find retransmissions and problem flows quickly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Recognize anomalies
Identify retransmissions, duplicate ACKs, zero window, and ICMP unreachable to guide remediation.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Safe capture practice
Avoid capturing excessive traffic without filters and never connect the destination monitor port back into the production network.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
