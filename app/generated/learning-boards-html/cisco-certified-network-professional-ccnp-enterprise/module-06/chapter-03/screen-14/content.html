Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Safe Capture Practices
Workflow
  1. Plan: pick capture points and method (SPAN/RSPAN/ERSPAN/monitor capture).
  2. Scope: apply ACLs or isolate VLANs to limit captured traffic.
  3. Capture: record start/stop times and simulate traffic if needed.
  4. Analyze: filter, follow streams, and consult Expert Information.
  5. Clean up: stop and clear captures, remove sessions and ACLs.
Do and don't
Do
Document configuration and tear down after troubleshooting.
Don't
Leave mirror ports, RSPAN VLANs, or active captures in production unintentionally.
