Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
SPAN
Mirror local ports or VLANs to a dedicated destination port for analysis.
ERSPAN
Encapsulate mirrored frames in GRE to route them to a remote analyzer across IP networks.
monitor capture
Device-level captures with named buffers and ACL matches; export to PCAP for Wireshark.
Takeaway

Choose the capture method that fits your topology, scope captures to conserve resources, and use Wireshark filters and Expert Information to turn packet traces into evidence-based fixes.
