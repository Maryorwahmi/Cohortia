Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › ERSPAN: Encapsulated Mirroring
Quick concept
Encapsulate mirrored frames in GRE for IP transport

ERSPAN wraps mirrored Layer 2 frames into GRE so they can be routed to a remote capture destination across IP networks or a WAN.

Example parameters
erspan-id, source/destination IP, origin IP, and ensuring GRE is allowed by firewalls and routers.
Why to use ERSPAN

Centralized analysis or cross-subnet monitoring where RSPAN cannot reach the analyzer.

Operational notes
  • Plan routes and firewall rules to permit GRE between source and destination.
  • Check MTU to avoid fragmentation of encapsulated frames.
  • ERSPAN does not encrypt traffic by default; protect sensitive data on transit paths.
Misconception check
ERSPAN fixes connectivity problems — no. It moves mirrored frames but you still need correct routing and firewall allowances for GRE.
