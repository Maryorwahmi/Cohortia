Best practices checklist
  • Prefer declarative methods for large-scale configuration tasks.
  • Use version control for playbooks and scripts.
  • Secure credentials via vaults and never hardcode secrets.
  • Test on a small set, verify convergence, and expand progressively.
Key Takeaway
Good automation combines the right approach with secure, auditable practices and staged rollouts to deliver real operational gains.
