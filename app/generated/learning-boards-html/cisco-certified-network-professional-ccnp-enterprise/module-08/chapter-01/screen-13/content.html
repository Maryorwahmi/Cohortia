Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Key Concepts — What to Remember
Network Automation
Automating configuration and operations to make changes repeatable and auditable.
Imperative vs Declarative
Imperative gives steps; declarative defines desired state and supports idempotence.
APIs & Data Models
Structured interfaces and schemas like YANG make programmatic control reliable and parsable.
Summary takeaway
Adopt automation incrementally, choose the appropriate approach, and rely on APIs and models to make your automation robust and auditable.
