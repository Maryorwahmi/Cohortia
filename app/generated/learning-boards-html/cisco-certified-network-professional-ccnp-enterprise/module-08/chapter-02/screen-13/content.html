Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Challenges & Best Practices
Namespaces & paths
Confirm module URIs and YANG paths to avoid ignored or rejected calls.
Authentication
Use tokens or certificates in production; restrict privileges to minimize risk.
Idempotency & transactions
Design calls so repeated executions converge to the same desired state; use NETCONF commits for atomicity.
Testing and rollback
Test in lab devices, verify responses, and keep a rollback plan such as discard-changes or saved configs.
Monitoring
Log responses and error details to troubleshoot failed operations quickly.
