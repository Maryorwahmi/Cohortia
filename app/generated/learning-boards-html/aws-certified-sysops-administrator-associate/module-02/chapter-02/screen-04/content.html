Structure and intent
How log groups and streams represent ownership and sequence.
Log Group
Logical container for related streams. Apply retention and access policies here.
Log Stream
A sequence of timestamped events from one source, such as an instance or function.
Log Event
A timestamp and a message. Metric filters inspect the message for patterns.
Common mistake
Using a single log group for unrelated systems, which makes retention and access control blunt instruments and risks deleting important history.
Key Takeaway
Design groups by ownership and retention, streams by source, and keep filter use in mind when grouping.

Why this matters
  • Retention policies are applied at the log group, so plan group boundaries accordingly.
  • Streams make source-level ordering and troubleshooting easier.
  • Metric filters operate on events; clear grouping helps filter scope and manage costs.
