Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Log Integrity
What it does
Creates digest and signed digest files so you can verify CloudTrail delivered logs unchanged.
Produces hash digests
Signs digests with an AWS key
Store digest files in the same S3 bucket and retain them for audit purposes.
Example
An auditor verifies the CloudTrail log bundle against the digest and signature to confirm no tampering occurred after delivery.
Practical note

Enable integrity validation on trails that serve as forensic evidence and retain both logs and digests according to your retention policy.
