Quick Reference
Three one-line reminders plus a summary.
Enable CloudTrail across regions and deliver to S3 with integrity validation.
Enable management events by default; add data events selectively for sensitive resources.
Use Config rules to evaluate compliance and act on NON_COMPLIANT findings through remediation.
Final thought
Detection without an operational plan to respond leaves you blind to the real risk. Pair records and rules with owners and remediation steps.
