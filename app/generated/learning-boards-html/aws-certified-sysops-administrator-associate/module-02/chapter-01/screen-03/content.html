Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Practice & Assessment
Hands-on activity
Follow the guided lab: launch an EC2 instance, attach an IAM role with CloudWatch permissions, observe CPU metrics, create an alarm with SNS, simulate high CPU, and publish a custom metric via CLI.
Success Criteria
  • CPU metric visible under AWS/EC2 for the instance.
  • Alarm transitions to ALARM during simulated load and email is received.
  • Custom metric appears under the chosen namespace within minutes.
Assessment focus
You will be evaluated on choosing appropriate periods and datapoints to reduce false positives, using metric dimensions to scope views, and demonstrating a working SNS subscription for alarm notifications.
Try this
After you create an alarm for testing, run a short load and then tune the alarm from quick testing settings to production-appropriate settings like 5 minute periods and 3 of 5 datapoints.
