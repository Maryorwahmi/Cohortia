Publish thoughtfully
Publish custom metrics that answer operational questions and avoid excessive cardinality.
Dashboards for responders
Organize widgets by role and annotate deploys to correlate events with metric changes.
Tune alarms
Use longer periods and datapoints-to-alarm to avoid false positives, and test in non-prod first.
Final thought

Good observability is focused and actionable. Build metrics, dashboards, and alarms that help you detect, diagnose, and respond quickly and confidently.
