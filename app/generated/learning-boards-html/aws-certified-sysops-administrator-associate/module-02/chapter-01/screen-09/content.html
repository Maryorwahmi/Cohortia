Design checklist
  • Define the viewer and their top questions.
  • Choose 4–6 critical widgets; avoid overcrowding.
  • Use consistent time ranges to compare signals.
  • Annotate deployments and incidents for correlation.
Key Takeaway
Dashboards are for rapid diagnosis—surface the few metrics that answer critical operational questions for your team.
