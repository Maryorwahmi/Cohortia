Alarm settings explained
Metric: the series to watch. Period: evaluation interval in seconds. Statistic: Average, Sum, Maximum, etc. Threshold: the numeric value to compare. Datapoints-to-alarm: number of breached periods required for state change.
Period
E.g., 300s smooths short spikes.
Statistic
Choose Average vs Maximum based on the problem you want to detect.
Concrete example
Alarm: CPUUtilization > 80%. Period: 300 seconds. Statistic: Average. Datapoints-to-alarm: 3 of 5. This means sustained high CPU for about 15 minutes triggers the alarm, reducing false positives from transient spikes.
Common mistake
Using short periods with 1 out of 1 datapoints for production alarms causes flapping and alert fatigue.
