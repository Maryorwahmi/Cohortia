Question 1 — Cache-clearing Lambda
Best setup: CloudWatch Alarm publishes to an SNS topic subscribed by both the cache-clearing Lambda and an email address so both remediation and notification occur.
Correct answer
Use SNS topic with both subscribers for direct action and notification.
Question 2 — Remediation loops
If an instance is repeatedly stopped after a restart, the most likely cause is too-sensitive alarm settings or no cooldown. Increase datapoints-to-alarm or implement suppression after remediation.
Mitigation
Tune alarm datapoints and apply cooldowns or temporary alarm disablement during recovery.
