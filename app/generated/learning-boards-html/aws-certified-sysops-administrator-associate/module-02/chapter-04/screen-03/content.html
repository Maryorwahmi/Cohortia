Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Safety Objectives
01 — Least Privilege
Give remediation roles only the permissions they need
Example: a reboot Lambda needs ec2:RebootInstances rather than wide EC2 access. This reduces blast radius if credentials are compromised.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Test First
Validate remediation in non-production
Use canary or A/B tests and confirm logging so you know what actions ran and why.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Alarm Tuning
Avoid remediation loops with datapoints and cooldowns
Increase datapoints to alarm or implement suppression after remediation to prevent immediate re-triggering.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Visibility
Log every remediation action
Send Lambda logs to CloudWatch Logs and consider storing action records for audit and post-incident analysis.
