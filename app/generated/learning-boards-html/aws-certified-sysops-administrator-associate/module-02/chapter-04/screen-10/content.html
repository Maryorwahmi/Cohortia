Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Auto Scaling
Self-healing lifecycle
  • ASG detects unhealthy instances via health checks or CloudWatch custom metrics.
  • ASG terminates and replaces unhealthy instances automatically.
  • Use lifecycle hooks to perform graceful operations during replacement.
Tuning guidance
Set appropriate cooldowns, datapoints-to-alarm, and health check grace periods so that transient spikes and bootstrapping do not trigger unnecessary replacements or scaling events.
Common mistake
Short cooldowns causing scaling thrash during short-lived spikes; increase datapoints or cooldown to stabilize behavior.
