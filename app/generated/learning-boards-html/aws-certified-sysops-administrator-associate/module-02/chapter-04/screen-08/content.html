What SQS gives you
  • Durable storage of alarm messages until processed.
  • Controlled consumer throughput to prevent overload.
  • Retries and dead-letter queues to capture failures for postmortem.
Concrete scenario
During a cascading failure multiple alarms produce hundreds of messages. SQS lets your remediation Lambda process these at a safe rate and ensures nothing is lost if the consumer is temporarily unavailable.
Design tips
  • Use visibility timeouts and appropriate retry settings.
  • Attach a dead-letter queue to capture messages that repeatedly fail processing.
  • Monitor queue depth as an indicator of processing backlogs.
Common misconception
Queues always slow responses. In practice, queues improve reliability and prevent harmful bursts, which is usually worth the small latency increase.
