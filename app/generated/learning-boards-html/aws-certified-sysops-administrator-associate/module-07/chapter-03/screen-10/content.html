When to archive
Archive data that is rarely read but must be kept for compliance, audit, or historical analysis. Use lifecycle rules to automate transitions after a defined age.
Example
Move monthly logs older than 365 days to Glacier Deep Archive to reduce recurring storage costs while preserving retention for audits.
Practical notes
  • Glacier retrieval times are minutes to hours; Deep Archive can take hours—plan ahead for restores.
  • Lifecycle rules can transition current objects or object versions; monitor costs for retrieval operations.
  • Do not transition objects you expect to read frequently; use analytics to inform lifecycle timing.
Key Takeaway
Use Glacier for compliance and long‑term retention, and automate transitions while considering retrieval SLAs and costs.
