Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Read Replicas vs Multi‑AZ
At a glance
Multi‑AZ
Synchronous standby. Automatic failover. RPO often zero. Not for read scaling.
Read Replica
Asynchronous copy. Manual promotion possible. Good for read scaling and cross‑region DR.
Example use cases
  • Multi‑AZ: transactional OLTP systems requiring minimal data loss.
  • Read Replica: analytics or reporting replicas, or a cross‑region replica for DR.
Pitfalls
Expect eventual consistency on Read Replicas. They may lag, producing non‑zero RPO. Don’t assume they will automatically take over; plan promotion and DNS or app logic to handle role changes.
Key Takeaway
Combine Multi‑AZ for HA with Read Replicas for scale and cross‑region DR, and always include backups for point‑in‑time recovery.
