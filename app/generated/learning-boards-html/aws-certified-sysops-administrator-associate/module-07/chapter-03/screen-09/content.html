Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › S3 Cross‑Region Replication
Setup checklist
  1. Enable Versioning on source and destination buckets.
  2. Create or choose a destination bucket in another region.
  3. Define replication rule for full bucket or prefix and tags.
  4. Grant replication role permissions and enable replication.
Replication is asynchronous. Test by uploading files and verifying they appear in the replicated bucket to validate behavior and latency expectations.
Use cases & costs
CRR supports compliance and regional DR by keeping separate copies. Consider storage costs in the destination region and replication request charges when estimating budget.
Key Takeaway
CRR is a simple way to create geographically separate copies for resilience, but it must be tested and sized for replication latency and cost.
