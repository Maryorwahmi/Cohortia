Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Key Concepts — Part 2
S3 Versioning
Retain object history in the same bucket to recover prior states after accidental changes.
CRR
Asynchronous replication across regions; useful for DR but expect replication lag and cost implications.
Glacier Classes
Low‑cost archival tiers for long retention with longer retrieval time—use lifecycle rules to transition objects.
Summary Takeaway

Centralize policies with AWS Backup, use appropriate replication and archival classes for cost and SLAs, and most importantly, rehearse restores to prove your recovery procedures work under pressure.
