Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Key Concepts — Part 1
EBS Snapshots
Point‑in‑time, incremental backups of block volumes stored in S3 for recovery and volume migration.
RDS Multi‑AZ
Synchronous replication to a standby in another AZ for automatic failover and low RTO with zero data loss.
RDS Read Replicas
Asynchronous copies for read scaling or cross‑region DR; may lag and require promotion for primary role.
Summary Takeaway

Match backup and replication mechanisms to business recovery objectives: use synchronous replication for minimal data loss, asynchronous replicas for scale and cross‑region DR, snapshots for point‑in‑time recovery, and test restores regularly.
