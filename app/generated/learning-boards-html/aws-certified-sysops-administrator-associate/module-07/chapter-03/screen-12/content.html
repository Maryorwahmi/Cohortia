Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Restore Verification
Restore checklist
  • EBS: create volume from snapshot, attach, mount, and verify files.
  • RDS: perform failover or promote a replica and validate application behavior.
  • S3: retrieve a previous version and confirm integrity.
  • Glacier: initiate a restore and record retrieval time and cost.
Metrics to capture
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO) measured during the test.
- Any data divergence or replication lag observed.
- Manual steps required and opportunities for automation.
Key Takeaway
Document test outcomes and iterate backup plans to align retention and automation with actual recovery capability.
Common Mistake
Assuming backups work without testing. Regular rehearsal reveals missing permissions, unexpected restore steps, or incorrect lifecycle transitions before a real incident.
