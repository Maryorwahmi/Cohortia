Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Multi‑Site Active/Active tradeoffs
Benefits
  • Minimal RTO and RPO for critical services.
  • Traffic routing can protect against single‑region outages.
  • Offers regional performance improvements for users.
Costs and complexity
  • Continuous cross‑region data replication or multi‑master setups.
  • Operational overhead for conflict resolution and testing.
  • Higher infrastructure and monitoring costs.
Design checklist
Routing
Use Route 53 latency or geoproximity policies to distribute traffic.
Data replication
Adopt replication tools that suit consistency needs and test conflict resolution.
Pitfall
Assuming Active/Active removes the need for testing failback. It still must be rehearsed and validated.
Key Takeaway

Active/Active minimizes downtime but requires rigorous replication design and operational discipline.
