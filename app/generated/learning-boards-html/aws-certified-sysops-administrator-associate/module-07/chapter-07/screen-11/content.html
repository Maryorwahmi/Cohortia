Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › DR Testing: tabletop to full drills
Testing timeline
Tabletop
Walk through the plan, clarify roles, and validate communication steps.
Simulated Failover
Run automation and restore flows in a mirrored or non-production environment.
Full Drill
Controlled cutover of production traffic to DR region and failback testing.
Success criteria
  • Meets RTO target in the test environment.
  • Data integrity checks pass.
  • Rollback works as documented.
Post‑test actions
Run a post‑mortem, record timings, and update runbooks and automation with identified fixes.
Key Takeaway

Frequent, progressively realistic tests ensure your DR plan stays valid and teams stay practiced.
