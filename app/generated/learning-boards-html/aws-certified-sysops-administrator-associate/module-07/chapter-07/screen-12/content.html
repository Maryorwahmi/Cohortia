Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Common Implementation Mistakes
Top mistakes
Outdated documentation
Review the plan after infrastructure changes and after each drill.
Lack of automation
Automate error‑prone steps to meet RTOs.
Ignoring dependencies
Inventory third‑party services and internal components; include them in tests.
Real example
IAM role missing in DR region
Automation failed during a drill because an assumed role was not replicated. Test IAM and resource mappings during simulated failovers.
Mitigation

Use automated inventories, include IAM checks in tests, and prioritize fixes found during drills.
