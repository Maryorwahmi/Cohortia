Key Concepts

Three compact reminders for quick recall.

Amazon Route 53
A scalable DNS service that supports health checks and routing policies.
Route 53 Health Checks
Monitor HTTP/HTTPS/TCP endpoints and mark them healthy or unhealthy.
Failover Routing & TTL
Primary/secondary records with health checks plus pragmatic TTLs enable automated, visible failover.
Summary

Use health checks to drive failover, choose routing policies to meet goals, and set TTLs that balance speed with DNS load.
