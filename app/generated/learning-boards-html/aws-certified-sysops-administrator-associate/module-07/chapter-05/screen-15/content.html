Quick Reference

Three short, actionable reminders for configuration and testing.

App-level checks
Probe a health endpoint that reflects service readiness.
Failover records
Attach health check to primary and set secondary as standby.
TTL planning
Lower TTL for faster failover during critical windows; monitor DNS load.
Final Note

Combine health checks, proper routing policy, and deliberate TTLs, then validate with simulated failures and resolver checks.
