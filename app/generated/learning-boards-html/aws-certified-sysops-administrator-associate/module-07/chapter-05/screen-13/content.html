Troubleshooting

Common Mistakes, Testing, and Assessment

Avoid misconfigured checks and high TTLs. Test failover by simulating outages and verifying both health checks and client resolution.

Frequent errors
Wrong health check path, high TTL, and using simple routing without health checks.
Validation steps
Simulate failure, watch health check state, use dig/nslookup to confirm the DNS answer changes after TTL.
Assessment idea
If failover takes minutes, the most likely cause is a high TTL. Lower TTL to reduce propagation time.
Check your understanding
Question: After primary becomes unhealthy, users are not redirected quickly. Which setting is most likely causing the delay? Answer: TTL is too high. This demonstrates the interaction between Route 53's internal state and resolver caching.
Key Takeaway

Validation requires both service-level failure simulation and client-side DNS checks; TTL often dictates real-world recovery time.
