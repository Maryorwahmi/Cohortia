Designing Effective Health Checks

Make health checks validate the application experience, tune intervals, and avoid overreacting to brief glitches.

Probe an app endpoint
Use /index.html or a dedicated /health that checks service dependencies.
Choose interval & failures
Fast interval for rapid detection, and 2–3 failures to avoid reacting to transient errors.
Cost vs speed
More frequent checks increase probe traffic; balance detection speed with load.
Check design checklist
  • Use application-level paths that verify critical dependencies.
  • Prefer HTTPS for secure probes where appropriate.
  • Align intervals and failure counts to your desired RTO.
  • Monitor check trends and alert before total failure.
Key Takeaway

Health checks should reflect the end-user experience; tune them, test them, and watch for degrading trends.
