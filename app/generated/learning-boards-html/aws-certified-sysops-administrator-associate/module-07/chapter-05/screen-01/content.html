Route 53 for DNS-based Failover and Routing

Master the health checks and routing policies that let Route 53 detect failures and route users to healthy regions. Learn practical trade-offs like TTL, check intervals, and realistic probes that confirm application health — not just server liveness.

In this lesson
Health checks, failover, routing policies
Primary health
Monitor ALBs or endpoints with application-level probes.
Automatic failover
Route 53 switches to the secondary when primary is unhealthy.
TTL trade-off
Lower TTL shortens failover visibility at the cost of more DNS queries.
