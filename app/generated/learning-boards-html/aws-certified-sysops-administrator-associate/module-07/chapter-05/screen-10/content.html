Routing Policies Overview

Choose the routing policy that aligns with your goals: lower latency, traffic experiments, or geographic targeting.

Latency-based routing
Routes users to the region with lowest measured latency for them.
Weighted routing
Distributes traffic by percentage; useful for A/B testing or blue/green deployments.
Geolocation & Geoproximity
Serve users based on query location or distance bias; useful for localization and residency.
When to pair with health checks
Always combine routing policies with health checks so Route 53 excludes unhealthy resources. For example, a weighted rollout should not send test traffic to an endpoint that is down. Similarly, latency routing is useful only when healthy endpoints exist in multiple regions.
Practical tip
Use weighted routing for gradual traffic shifts, and monitor health checks closely during rollouts.
Key Takeaway

Match policy to goal and always combine with health checks to ensure unhealthy endpoints are not returned.
