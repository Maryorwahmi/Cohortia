Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Final Reminders
Spread across AZs
Protect services from a single data center failure.
Use ASG as control plane
Let ASG keep desired capacity and replace failed instances automatically.
Favor app-level health checks
Have ELB check application readiness and let ASG act on those results.
Quick test: terminate one instance launched by the ASG and watch the ASG launch a replacement while the ALB keeps serving traffic through healthy targets.
