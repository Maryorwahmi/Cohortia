Multi-AZ Deployment
Distribute EC2 instances across independent Availability Zones to avoid a single point of failure.
Definition: AZs are isolated failure domains; multi-AZ means at least two AZs per tier.
Example: Two Apache servers in AZ-A and AZ-B, with an ALB routing traffic to both.
Misconception: Subnets alone do not guarantee AZ separation; verify the AZ property of each subnet.
Key Takeaway

Place instances in multiple AZs to reduce outage impact; combine this with load balancing and ASG for resilience.

Scenario
E-commerce website: deploy two EC2 instances in separate AZs so a data center failure won't drop the storefront.
Verification steps
  1. Confirm instance AZs in the EC2 console or with CLI describe-instances.
  2. Ensure there are active targets in more than one AZ for your load balancer.
  3. Simulate a failure and watch traffic shift to the healthy AZ.
