Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
Multi-AZ Deployment
Distribute resources across AZs to protect against single AZ failure.
Auto Scaling Group
Maintains desired capacity and replaces unhealthy instances automatically.
Elastic Load Balancing
Distributes traffic across healthy targets and provides application-level health checks.
Summary
Combine these elements, tune health checks and grace periods, and test your failure scenarios to build a resilient EC2-based service.
