Comparison › Common Pitfalls
HA vs DR
HA addresses component and AZ failures within a region; DR prepares for region-level disasters and requires recovery at an alternative location.
Undefined RPO/RTO
Designing without business targets leads to mismatched cost and risk. Define RPO and RTO first.
Untested plans
A plan is theory until proven. Regular drills reveal gaps and build operational muscle memory.
Remediation steps
Start with impact analysis, set RPO/RTO, select patterns to meet them, automate recovery, and test frequently.
