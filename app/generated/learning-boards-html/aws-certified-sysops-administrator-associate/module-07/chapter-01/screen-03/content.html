Scenario Exercise
Analyze stakeholder constraints, identify RTO and RPO, and propose patterns that meet them.
Guided task: Determine RPO and RTO, then choose three services or patterns and explain their contributions.
Validation: Write a brief recovery test that verifies both the recovery time and the integrity of data after failover.
Assessment snippets
