Fault tolerance vs high availability
Fault tolerance seeks to avoid interruption entirely for certain failures; HA accepts small interruptions and recovers quickly.
  • Instant takeover via redundant components is the hallmark of fault tolerance.
  • It often requires duplication and careful orchestration to prevent split-brain or data inconsistency.
  • Apply it selectively to systems where any interruption is unacceptable.
Concrete example
Storage that mirrors data across devices so reads and writes continue immediately if one device fails, keeping user experience uninterrupted.
Precise note
Services such as Amazon S3 are designed to be fault-tolerant by replicating data across devices and facilities.
Misconception
Fault tolerance does not guarantee protection against every failure type, such as human configuration errors or regional disasters.
