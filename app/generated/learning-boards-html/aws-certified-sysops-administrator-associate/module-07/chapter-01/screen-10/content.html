Architecture › Reliability Pillar
Automatic recovery
Design services to detect and recover from failures without human intervention.
Test recovery procedures
Regular drills validate that runbooks and automation actually achieve RPO and RTO.
Scale horizontally
Increase aggregate availability and capacity by adding parallel components rather than oversized single instances.
Stop guessing capacity
Measure demand and use automatic scaling to match capacity to actual load.
Manage change through automation
Use validated pipelines, versioned artifacts, and rollbacks to reduce deployment mistakes that cause outages.
Key Takeaway

The Reliability Pillar gives operational principles that should shape design, testing, and automation choices across your environment.
