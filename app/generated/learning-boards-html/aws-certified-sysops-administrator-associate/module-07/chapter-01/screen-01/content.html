Why it matters

Reliability overview
Shared responsibility: AWS for the cloud, you for in-cloud configuration and operations.
High availability reduces downtime with redundancy and failover.
Fault tolerance aims to eliminate interruption for specific failure modes.
Disaster recovery restores operations after region-scale failures, guided by RPO and RTO.
