Process Flow › Meeting RPO & RTO
Choose replication and backup modes
Synchronous replication reduces data loss but can add latency. Asynchronous replication lowers latency but increases RPO risk.
Frequent backups lower RPO but may increase storage and processing costs.
Replication across locations reduces risk of single-site loss but can complicate consistency guarantees.
Automate recovery to meet RTO
Pre-script deployments, automate DNS and routing changes, and maintain tested runbooks. Manual steps slow recovery and increase error risk.
Testing
Run realistic recovery drills to measure whether your design actually meets RPO and RTO. Adjust backup frequency and automation until tests pass.
Pro tip
Target fault tolerance only for components where uninterrupted service justifies the extra cost.
