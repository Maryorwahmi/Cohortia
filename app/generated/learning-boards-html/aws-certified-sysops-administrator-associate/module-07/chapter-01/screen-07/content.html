Disaster Recovery essentials
DR restores operations at an alternate location after a large-scale failure, guided by RPO and RTO.
  • Define RPO and RTO with stakeholders before designing DR.
  • Choose replication and backup strategies that meet those targets.
  • Automate recovery steps and test them regularly.
Concrete note
DR often involves restoring operations in another AWS Region; the exact approach depends on acceptable downtime and data loss.
Pitfalls
Confusing HA with DR or failing to test recovery procedures are common mistakes that make DR ineffective.
Action
Write a short runbook for restoring a critical service and schedule a dry run to validate it.
