Definition › High Availability
High Availability and 'nines'
Availability targets are business choices. Redundancy and quick failover help meet those targets.
Design for redundancy across components and zones to reduce single-point failures.
Use health checks and automated failover to restore service quickly.
Balance increased cost of redundancy against the business value of higher availability.
Concrete example
Deploying application instances in multiple Availability Zones so an AZ outage affects only a subset and traffic shifts without manual steps.
Common misconception
HA addresses component and AZ failures, not necessarily full regional disasters. For region-level outages you need a disaster recovery plan.
Check your understanding
If a single AZ fails, will your service remain reachable? If not, identify the single point of failure.
