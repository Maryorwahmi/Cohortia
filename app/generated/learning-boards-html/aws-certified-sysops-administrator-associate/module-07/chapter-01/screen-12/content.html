Scenario › Holiday Sale
Identified targets
RTO (maximum downtime): 30 minutes. RPO (maximum data loss): 5 minutes. These values are business-driven and determine technical choices.
Immediate design priorities
Limit data loss with frequent replication or near-continuous backups and ensure fast recovery with automation and pre-provisioned capacity.
Suggested patterns
  • Multi-AZ deployments to address AZ failures and keep the site reachable.
  • Frequent snapshots or continuous replication for order data to meet the 5-minute RPO.
  • Pre-provisioned or warm standby capacity and automated failover to meet the 30-minute RTO.
Test
Run a recovery drill that simulates the failure and measures both data loss and recovery time against the targets. Iterate until the targets are consistently met.
