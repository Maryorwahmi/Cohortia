Key Concepts — Part 2

Definitions and practical reminders for implementation.

Multi-Site Active/Active
Full active deployments in multiple regions for immediate failover and minimal data loss.
RTO
Recovery Time Objective — the maximum acceptable downtime after an incident.
RPO
Recovery Point Objective — the maximum acceptable age of recovered data, i.e., data loss tolerance.
Remember

Test DR plans often and report measured RTO/RPO values to stakeholders along with cost and operational assumptions.
