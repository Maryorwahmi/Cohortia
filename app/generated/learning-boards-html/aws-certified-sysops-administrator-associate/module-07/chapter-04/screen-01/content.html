Disaster Recovery Strategies on AWS

Compare Backup & Restore, Pilot Light, Warm Standby, and Multi-Site Active/Active to pick the right DR posture for your application and business risk.

Concept Snapshot
RTO
Recovery Time Objective
How quickly systems must be restored after a disruption.
RPO
Recovery Point Objective
How much data loss is acceptable, measured in time.
We will map these measures to four AWS-aligned strategies and a hands-on design exercise.
Explore Key Topics
Backup & Restore
Lowest cost option. Restore from snapshots and object backups when required.
Pilot Light
Minimal core services replicate data and are ready to scale in an outage.
Active/Active
Fully active regions for near-zero downtime and near-zero data loss.
