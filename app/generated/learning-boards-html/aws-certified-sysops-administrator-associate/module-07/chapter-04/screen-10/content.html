Cost vs Operational Complexity

Look beyond resource bills to include engineering and testing effort in estimates.

Cost profile
Backup & Restore: storage costs; low baseline compute cost.
Pilot Light: small running footprint; moderate storage and compute costs.
Warm Standby: larger baseline compute; higher monthly cost.
Active/Active: near-duplicate costs across regions; highest monthly bill.
Operational overhead
Maintain runbooks and test plans; schedule and perform failover rehearsals.
Monitor replication lag and automated scaling behavior.
Manage configuration drift across regions and enforce parity where necessary.
