Learning Objectives

Build skills to choose, justify, and test DR approaches that meet business limits on downtime and data loss.

01
Identify DR strategies

Recognize Backup & Restore, Pilot Light, Warm Standby, and Multi-Site Active/Active and what they deliver.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Map RTO & RPO to strategy

Translate business RTO/RPO numbers into the minimum viable DR posture to meet them.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Analyze cost & complexity

Compare ongoing and one-time costs and operational burden so trade-offs are explicit.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Select and justify strategy

Pick the right approach per workload and document why it satisfies RTO/RPO within cost constraints.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
