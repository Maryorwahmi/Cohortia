Pilot Light

Run a minimal, replicated core in the DR region and scale up when needed.

Architecture snapshot
Always-on components
Lightweight DB replica, pre-baked application AMIs, and minimal networking in the recovery region.
Scale-up actions
Launch additional instances, scale ASGs, promote replicas, and update DNS or routing to cut over.
Typical RTO/RPO
RTO: tens of minutes to hours. RPO: minutes to hours depending on replication frequency.
Example, mistake, and test
Example
Keep an RDS read replica in the recovery region and AMIs for web servers that are kept patched and ready.
Common mistake
Assuming pilot light will handle peak load without rehearsed automation to scale and promote replicas.
Test
Simulate promotion of the replica and automated scaling to confirm the time to reach production capacity.
