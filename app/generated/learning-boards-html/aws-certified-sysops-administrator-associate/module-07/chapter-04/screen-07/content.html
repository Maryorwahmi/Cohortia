Warm Standby

Keep a functional, scaled-down copy of systems ready to scale up when needed.

What it contains
Active components
Smaller ASGs, an active DB replica, networking, and monitoring—functional but at reduced capacity.
Failover action
Scale existing instances, increase ASG min sizes, promote replicas if needed, and switch routing to the DR region.
Expected RTO/RPO
RTO: minutes to tens of minutes. RPO: seconds to minutes under continuous replication.
Example, test, and caution
Example
A customer-facing application with high-value transactions that can tolerate short scaling time but not long outages.
Test
Perform scale-up rehearsals to ensure the standby configuration scales and latency stays acceptable under load.
Caution
Running active resources increases cost; balance instance sizing and automation to keep expected costs reasonable.
