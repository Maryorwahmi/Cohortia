Multi-Site Active/Active

Run full deployments in multiple regions for near-zero recovery impact.

Benefits & Requirements
Immediate failover
Traffic is routed to healthy regions with no infrastructure provisioning step required.
Replication needs
Continuous cross-region replication or globally consistent data stores are essential.
Operational demands
Configuration management, global testing, and runbooks must handle region failover scenarios.
Example & caution
Example
A globally distributed application, like a trading system, where even seconds of downtime are unacceptable.
Caution
Data consistency across regions can be challenging; design for conflict resolution or use services that support cross-region sync.
Cost note
Expect near-duplicate running costs because the full stack is active in each region.
