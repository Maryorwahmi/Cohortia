Testing and Validating Your DR Plan

Tests prove that your RTO and RPO claims are real and repeatable.

Test cadence & steps
  1. Dry run of runbooks and automation.
  2. Component failover (DB replica promotion, instance scaling).
  3. Full cutover drill to recovery region with simulated traffic.
  4. Post-test rollback and post-mortem.
What to measure
Provision time for infrastructure.
Time to restore or promote data to usable state.
Application health and performance after failover.
Replication lag observed during test.
