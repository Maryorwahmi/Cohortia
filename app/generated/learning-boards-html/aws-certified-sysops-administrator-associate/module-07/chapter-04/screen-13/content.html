Design a DR Strategy for a Multi-Tier App

Use the template below to document tiered choices for the given RTO/RPO requirements.

Scenario
Three-tier app in us-east-1.
Tier 1 (Database): RPO 5 minutes, RTO 30 minutes.
Tier 2 (Web/App): RPO 1 hour, RTO 2 hours.
Template (fill in)
**DR Strategy Design for Multi-Tier Application**

**Application Tier: Database (Tier 1)**
* **RPO:** 5 minutes
* **RTO:** 30 minutes
* **Chosen DR Strategy:** Warm Standby
* **AWS Services & Implementation:**
  1. RDS cross-region replica - continuous replication and fast promotion.
  2. Smaller DB instance in us-west-2 kept active + automated promotion scripts.
  3. Infrastructure-as-code templates for VPC and security groups to speed provisioning.

**Application Tier: Web/Application Servers (Tier 2)**
* **RPO:** 1 hour
* **RTO:** 2 hours
* **Chosen DR Strategy:** Pilot Light (or Backup & Restore with automation)
* **AWS Services & Implementation:**
  1. AMIs in us-west-2 and S3 for static assets - quick instance launches.
  2. Pre-built autoscaling templates and CloudFormation templates for faster scaling.
  3. Periodic snapshots and an RDS read replica if needed for session data.
Assessment & Validation
Test plan
Dry run of promotion, scale-up rehearsals, measure time to reach healthy state, and verify data consistency.
Common error
Choosing costly active-active for tiers that do not need it rather than mixing strategies by criticality.
Takeaway
Match each tier to the minimum strategy that meets its RTO/RPO and test the plan end-to-end.
