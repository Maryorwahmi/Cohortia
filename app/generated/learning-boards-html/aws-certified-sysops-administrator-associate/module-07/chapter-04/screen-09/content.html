Map RTO/RPO to DR Strategies

Use RTO and RPO per component to select an appropriate strategy and avoid one-size-fits-all errors.

Quick mapping guide
  • High RTO/RPO tolerance → Backup & Restore.
  • Moderate RTO/RPO → Pilot Light with replication.
  • Low RTO/RPO → Warm Standby with active replication.
  • Near-zero RTO/RPO → Multi-Site Active/Active.
Remember to treat each application tier independently and combine strategies where appropriate.
Concrete example
Tier 1: Database
RPO 5 minutes, RTO 30 minutes → Warm Standby or Active/Active (depending on cost tolerance).
Tier 2: Web/App
RPO 1 hour, RTO 2 hours → Pilot Light or Backup & Restore with automated provisioning.
