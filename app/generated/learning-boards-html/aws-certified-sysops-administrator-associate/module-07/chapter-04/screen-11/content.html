Data Replication and Consistency

Replication choices directly influence achievable RPO and system behavior under failover.

Replication modes
Asynchronous: cheaper, possible data loss during failover.
Near-synchronous: lower lag, moderate cost.
Synchronous: minimal data loss, higher latency and cost.
Practical guidance
Measure replication lag under production load and include worst-case lag in your RPO statement.
Design conflict resolution or choose a database that supports the required cross-region semantics.
Don't assume managed read replicas are drop-in for failover; promotion steps and consistency guarantees vary.
