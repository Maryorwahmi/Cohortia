Backup and Restore

Store backups in a separate location and restore infrastructure when needed.

Process Steps
1.
Schedule and store backups (EBS, RDS snapshots, S3 versioning).
2.
Keep backups in a separate region or account.
3.
Provision recovery infrastructure (VPC, subnets, compute) when needed.
4.
Restore data, reconfigure endpoints, and bring services online.
Key Takeaway

Use when low cost is priority and long RTOs are acceptable. Automate provisioning to shorten recovery time.

Example, pitfall, and checklist
Example
Internal reporting systems with daily snapshots and S3 backups that can tolerate hours or days of downtime.
Common pitfall
Backups without automated provisioning or tested restore procedures cause unexpectedly long RTOs.
Quick checklist
  • Backups exist and are cross-region.
  • Provisioning scripts or templates are available.
  • Restores are performed regularly as tests.
