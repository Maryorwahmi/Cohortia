Hypothesis template
Example hypothesis to use: If 25% of instances in the ASG are terminated, ALB 5XX rate will remain below 1% and ASG will replace terminated instances within five minutes.
Target: ASG ARN or instance tag selector.
Action: aws:ec2:terminate-instances with instanceTerminationPercentage or a fixed COUNT.
Stop condition: CloudWatch ALB 5XX threshold or CPU above a limit.
Post-run review
  1. Compare measured metrics to hypothesis criteria.
  2. Confirm alerts and runbook actions executed as expected.
  3. Update circuit breaker thresholds, bulkhead sizes, or scaling policies as needed.
Key Takeaway

Design experiments to produce clear yes/no answers against your hypothesis to enable focused remediation.
