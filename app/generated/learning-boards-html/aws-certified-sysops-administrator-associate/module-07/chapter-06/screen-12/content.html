Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Mistakes & Safety
Top mistakes
  • Assuming patterns are effective without testing them.
  • Running experiments with no stop conditions or poor monitoring.
  • Retrying too aggressively and creating a thundering herd.
  • Over-isolating with bulkheads and reducing utilization unnecessarily.
Safety checklist
Validate stop conditions and ensure CloudWatch metrics are available to FIS.
Inform on-call teams and confirm runbooks are ready.
Scope experiments narrowly and iterate from staging to production.
Key Takeaway

Safety and observability are as important as the experiment itself; design tests to fail safely and yield clear, actionable results.
