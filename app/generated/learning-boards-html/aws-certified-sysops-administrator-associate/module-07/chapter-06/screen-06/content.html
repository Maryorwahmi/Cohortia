Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Retry + Backoff
Pattern

Retry the operation on transient errors, increasing delay between attempts exponentially. Add jitter to avoid synchronized retries across clients.

Parameters
  • Base delay, multiplier, max delay cap.
  • Maximum attempts and retryable error list.
  • Jitter strategy: randomize each delay slightly.
Integration
Combine retries with circuit breakers and bulkheads to protect resources while recovering from transient faults.
Concrete example
After a network timeout, retry with delays of 100ms, 200ms, 400ms, each with ±20ms random jitter, and stop after 4 attempts. If failures continue, the circuit breaker should open to prevent further retries.
Misconception
Retrying immediately or indefinitely will likely worsen an outage rather than fix it.
Key Takeaway

Retries are useful for transient faults but must back off and include randomness; combine with breakers for safety.
