Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Design an FIS Experiment
Step-by-step
1. Hypothesis: Define expected behavior and measurable metrics.
2. Target: Narrow scope by ASG ARN or tags to reduce blast radius.
3. Action: Choose fault type and parameters (terminate, latency, throttle).
4. Stop Conditions: CloudWatch thresholds that halt the experiment automatically.
5. Review: Validate monitoring and runbooks, inform operations, then run and inspect results.
Concrete scenario

Hypothesis: Terminating 25% of ASG instances keeps ALB 5XX below 1%. Target: ASG ARN. Action: terminate count=25%. Stop condition: ALB 5XX >= 10 for 1 minute. Review alerts, logs, and recovery time after the run.

Pitfall
Skipping stop conditions or not notifying on-call teams increases the risk of unintended customer impact.
Key Takeaway

Design narrow, measurable experiments with clear stop conditions and a remediation plan.
