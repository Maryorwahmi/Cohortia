Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Key Concepts
Chaos Engineering
Experiment deliberately to uncover weaknesses before they affect users.
AWS FIS
Managed experiments with targets, actions, and stop conditions tightly scoped to avoid harm.
Stop Conditions
Automated metrics-based halting conditions prevent experiments from causing unacceptable impact.
Final reflection

Design a minimal, measurable experiment for a critical dependency you operate. Define a hypothesis and at least one stop condition before you run it.
