Why these objectives matter
  • Detection without agents reduces operational overhead and speeds detection.
  • Centralized findings avoid alert fatigue and make prioritization consistent.
  • Config rules automate compliance checks and surface drift quickly.
  • Event-driven alerts let teams act fast and optionally automate safe containment steps.
