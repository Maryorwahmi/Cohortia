Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › SysOps Role
Definition & responsibilities

SysOps administrators monitor environments, interpret security findings, perform containment steps, and ensure forensic data is preserved for investigation.

Detection
Collect logs and alerts from GuardDuty, Config, and other sources.
Triage
Assess severity and decide whether to escalate or contain.
Containment
Isolate resources, snapshot forensics, and preserve logs.
Concrete example
Compromised EC2 instance
GuardDuty finds high outbound traffic to a malicious IP. Triage marks it high. Containment steps: revoke instance access, modify security group to block outbound traffic, snapshot attached EBS volume for forensics, stop the instance.
Key Takeaway

Detection without triage and containment leaves incidents unresolved. Plan both people and pre-approved automation for fast, safe response.
