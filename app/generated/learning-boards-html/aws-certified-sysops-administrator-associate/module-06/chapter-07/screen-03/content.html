Skills & Practices

Transform tools into safe operating practices you can rely on daily.

Test-first mentality
Validate changes in non-production
Test patches and remediation steps in staging to prevent unintended production disruptions. Use snapshots and backups during tests.
Least privilege
Narrow execution roles
Give Lambda and automation documents only the permissions they need for the remediation task, limiting risk from compromise or bug.
Observability
Log and alert automation
Ensure automation emits logs and success/failure alerts so you can investigate and iterate on policies and rules.
Iterate
Refine rules from incidents
Use findings and post-incident analysis to refine patch baselines, Config rules, and automated actions over time.
