Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › VPC Flow Logs
Process

Enable flow logs and analyze records

  1. Select the VPC, subnet, or ENI you want to monitor.
  2. Create a Flow Log: choose destination CloudWatch Logs or S3, set aggregation interval, and assign an IAM role.
  3. Wait for logs and then filter for REJECT or the IP address to identify blocked flows.
Example record
1612288000 eni-abc123 198.51.100.5 10.0.1.23 22 34567 tcp 60 REJECT
Why it matters
Flow logs let you determine whether failures are due to network filters or other issues. They are invaluable for audits and incident response.
Key Takeaway
Turn on flow logs early and use REJECT filters to quickly find which control blocked traffic.
