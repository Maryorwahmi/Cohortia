Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › AWS Shield
Overview

Shield Standard

Automatically protects all AWS customers from common network and transport DDoS attacks. Always on and free.

When it's enough
Use for general protection of internet-facing endpoints without extra configuration.
Overview

Shield Advanced

Enhanced DDoS protections with near real-time visibility, DDoS Response Team access, and financial protections against scaling charges during attacks.

When to consider it
For high-value services where downtime or cost spikes from an attack would be unacceptable.
Complementary controls
Use WAF for application-layer rules and Shield for volumetric attack mitigation.
Misconception
Believing Shield replaces WAF; Shield focuses on DDoS, while WAF inspects request content.
Key Takeaway
Combine Shield and WAF for layered web protections and choose Advanced when risk justifies deeper telemetry and support.
