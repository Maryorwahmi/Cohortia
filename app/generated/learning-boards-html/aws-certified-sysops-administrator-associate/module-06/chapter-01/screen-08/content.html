Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Policy Comparison
Identity-based

Attach to users, groups, roles

Controls what an identity can do. Best for centrally managing permissions for teams and services within your account.
Use when
Permissions follow the person or service, and you want reuse across multiple resources.
Example
S3ReadOnly policy attached to a role assumed by multiple EC2 instances.
Resource-based

Attach to the resource itself

Best when resource owners need to grant access to external principals or accounts directly on the resource.
Use when
You need to grant cross-account access or limit who may access a specific bucket or queue.
Example
An S3 bucket policy granting read access to an external account's principal.
Key Takeaway

Use identity-based policies for people and services you control; use resource-based policies to delegate access to external principals or cross-account scenarios.
