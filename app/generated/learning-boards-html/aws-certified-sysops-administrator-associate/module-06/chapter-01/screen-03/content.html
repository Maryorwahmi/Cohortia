Practical verification cards
Verify Keys
Rotate access keys regularly and prefer roles for services. Confirm only required programs have keys and record last rotated dates.
Group Management
Attach policies to groups, not individual users, to ensure consistent permissions across teams and easier offboarding.
Trust Policies
Design trust policy documents that explicitly allow the correct AWS service to assume the role; keep trust narrow to the service and account.
Policy Reviews
Scan for wildcards in actions and resources. If s3:* or arn:aws:s3:::* appears, evaluate whether it can be narrowed to exact actions and ARNs.
Check your understanding
Try these quick mental checks:
  • Can the service run without long-lived credentials?
  • Does any policy include an unnecessary wildcard?
  • Is MFA enforced for privileged accounts?
