Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
IAM Policy
JSON document declaring allowed or denied actions on resources under conditions.
Least Privilege
Grant only necessary permissions; avoid wildcards and review regularly.
Multi-Factor Authentication
A second verification factor that significantly strengthens account security.
Closing takeaway
Combine focused policies, least privilege, roles for services, and MFA to establish a secure, maintainable IAM posture.
