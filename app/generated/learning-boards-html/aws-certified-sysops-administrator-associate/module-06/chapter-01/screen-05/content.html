Why groups?

Group-based permission management

Attach policies to a group once and every member inherits those permissions. This reduces repetitive tasks and human errors.

Scenario
Add a new EC2 operator: place their user in the EC2Admins group to grant consistent instance management permissions.
Pitfall
Attaching many policies directly to users leads to drift. Use groups to keep policy assignments centralized and auditable.
Best practice
Use customer-managed policies attached to groups for reuse and clearer ownership.
Group design patterns
Build small, focused groups that map to real responsibilities. Combine groups for users who span roles rather than creating many one-off policies.
Example cards
EC2Admins
Manage EC2 instances in specified accounts or regions.
S3Developers
Read and list objects in a defined bucket, not all buckets.
Key Takeaway

Groups reduce complexity: attach policies to groups for consistent permissions and easier audits.
