Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › IAM Roles
Definition

Role = assumable identity

Roles are assumed at runtime and provide temporary credentials. They include permissions and a trust policy that governs who may assume the role.

Example trust policy (concept)
A trust policy grants ec2.amazonaws.com permission to assume the role for EC2 instances.
Concrete example
An EC2 instance assumes EC2S3ReaderRole to list and read objects from a single S3 bucket. No access keys are stored on the instance.
Key Takeaway

Prefer roles for AWS services; design narrow permissions and restrict trust relationships.

Misconceptions
  • Roles are permanent accounts. (No: they are assumed and grant temporary credentials.)
  • Any service can assume any role. (No: trust policies limit which principals may assume a role.)
Design checklist
  • Limit actions to required API calls.
  • Scope resources to the minimal ARNs needed.
  • Restrict trust to the exact service principal or external provider.
