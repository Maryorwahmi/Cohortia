Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Least Privilege
Five-step process
1
Identify required actions
List exact API calls or console operations the identity needs, for example s3:GetObject and s3:ListBucket.
2
Scope resources
Use specific ARNs, target a single bucket or resource path, and avoid global wildcards.
3
Add conditions
Restrict by IP, require MFA, or limit by time where appropriate to tighten access.
4
Assign and test
Attach the policy to a role or group, test allowed and denied actions, and iterate until minimal access is achieved.
5
Review regularly
Use logs and tools to review permissions and rotate credentials to avoid drift and stale access.
Analogy
Think of permissions like keys to rooms. Grant access only to the rooms needed to do a job, not to the entire building.
Common mistake
Using broad policies such as AdministratorAccess for convenience. This creates large blast radiuses and complicates audits.
