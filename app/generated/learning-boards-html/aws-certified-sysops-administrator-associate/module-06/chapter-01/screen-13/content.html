Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › IAM Strategy
Strategy checklist
Map actors to identities
Categorize humans, services, and federated users and choose users, roles, or federation accordingly.
Centralize policies
Keep reusable policies as customer-managed and attach them to groups or roles for clarity and reuse.
Automate reviews
Schedule policy reviews, scan for wildcards, and use logs to identify unused or excessive permissions.
Include a documented break-glass process: a limited-scope administrative role protected by MFA and monitored for use. Train teams on onboarding and offboarding so permissions match responsibilities from day one.
Operational flow
  1. Define required actions per role.
  2. Create customer-managed policies.
  3. Attach to groups or roles; test access.
  4. Schedule reviews and rotate keys.
Key Takeaway

An IAM strategy combines mapping, centralized policies, automation, and governance to maintain secure and auditable permissions.
