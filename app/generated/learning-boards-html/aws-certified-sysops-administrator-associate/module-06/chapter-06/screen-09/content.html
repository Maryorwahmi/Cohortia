Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Centralized Logging Best Practice
Pattern summary
Create an organization-wide, multi-region CloudTrail trail that writes to an S3 bucket in a dedicated logging account. Restrict write permissions to CloudTrail and deny delete actions from member accounts. Enable CloudWatch Logs delivery for real-time detection.
Why this matters
Centralization reduces the blast radius of a compromised account and gives auditors a single, controlled source of truth for events across the organization.
Common misconception
Keeping logs only within each account is simpler but leaves you vulnerable to local log tampering in the event of a compromise.
Operational checklist
  • Use an organization trail with multi-region enabled.
  • Deliver logs to an encrypted S3 bucket in a logging account.
  • Apply a bucket policy allowing PutObject from CloudTrail and denying DeleteObject from member accounts.
  • Stream to CloudWatch for metric filters and alarms.
Key Takeaway
Centralized logging in a separate account raises the bar for attackers and simplifies compliance and audit review.
