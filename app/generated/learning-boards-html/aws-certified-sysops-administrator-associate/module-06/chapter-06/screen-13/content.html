Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Common Mistakes and How to Fix Them
Missing logs
Check trail enabled state, multi-region setting, S3 delivery status, and bucket policy allowing PutObject from CloudTrail.
No CloudWatch entries
Verify the CloudWatch Logs role exists and CloudTrail is configured to stream to the correct log group.
Too many alerts
Narrow metric filters, scope data events, and consider thresholding or aggregation to reduce noise.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • Confirm trail configuration and status
  • Check S3 deliverability, encryption, and bucket policies
  • Verify CloudWatch Logs role and ingestion
  • Refine metric filters to reduce false positives
