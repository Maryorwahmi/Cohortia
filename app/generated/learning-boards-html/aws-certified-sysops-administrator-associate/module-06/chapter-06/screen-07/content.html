Best practices: enforce encryption, restrict bucket access to a central logging account, and scope data events. Automate these steps in IaC for repeatability.
Verify delivery and test
Perform a non-destructive root action such as viewing the EC2 console. Then, check that an event appears in the S3 bucket and that the CloudWatch Logs group /aws/cloudtrail/MySysOpsTrail has a matching event. Confirm permissions and encryption on the bucket and that the CloudWatch log group receives events.
Common Pitfall
Skipping verification. If you don't test, logs may not be arriving or may be misconfigured, making them unusable during an audit.
