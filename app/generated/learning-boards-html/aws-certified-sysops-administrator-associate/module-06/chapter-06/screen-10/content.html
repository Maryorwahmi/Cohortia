Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › AWS Shared Responsibility Model
AWS: security of the cloud
Infrastructure, physical security, and foundational services are AWS responsibilities. Their compliance certifications cover these components.
You: security in the cloud
You are responsible for data protection, OS and application configuration, access control, and logging configuration that prove control enforcement.
Practical example
AWS may provide compliant hardware and services, but you must ensure S3 buckets are encrypted and policies prevent public access, and you must collect logs showing these configurations for auditors.
Common misconception
Assuming AWS certifications automatically cover your workload configuration. Auditors will expect evidence of how you configured and operated your resources.
