Compliance and Auditing
Master CloudTrail, CloudWatch integration, and Audit Manager so you can collect, retain, and act on audit evidence required by compliance programs.
In this lesson
Configure multi-region trails, send logs to S3 and CloudWatch Logs, create metric filters and alarms, and see how Audit Manager collects evidence automatically.
Why it matters
Logs are the primary evidence you present to auditors and the key data for security investigations. Proper delivery and protection of logs prevents tampering and proves controls were enforced.
