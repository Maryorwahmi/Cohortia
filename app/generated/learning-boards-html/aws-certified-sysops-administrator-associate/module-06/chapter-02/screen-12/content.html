Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
Policy Evaluation Logic
Explicit Deny > Explicit Allow > Implicit Deny determines effective access.
IAM Conditions
Use keys like aws:SourceIp and aws:MultiFactorAuthPresent to narrow when policies apply.
Service Control Policies
Organization-level filters that set maximum allowed actions across accounts.
Takeaway
Combine deny-first evaluation with conditions and SCPs, then use Access Analyzer and credential reports to validate your design and catch unintended exposures.
