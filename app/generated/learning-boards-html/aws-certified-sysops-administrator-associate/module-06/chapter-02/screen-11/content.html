Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › IAM Roles Anywhere
What Roles Anywhere provides
Temporary credentials for external workloads that authenticate with X.509 certificates. It avoids embedding long-term keys outside AWS.
Key controls you still need: certificate issuance, rotation, revocation, and auditing of which certificates were used to obtain credentials.
Operational checklist
  • Define least-privilege roles for external workloads.
  • Use a trusted CA and automated rotation for certificates.
  • Log and audit role assumptions and issued credentials.
