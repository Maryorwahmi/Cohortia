Storage class comparison

S3 Standard
For frequently accessed data with low latency requirements.
S3 Standard-IA
Lower storage cost for infrequent access; retrieval costs apply.
S3 Glacier / Deep Archive
Very low cost for long-term retention; slower retrieval times and fees for restores.
Operational example: keep recent logs in Standard, move to Standard-IA after 30 days, and consider Glacier for multi-year archival. Evaluate retrieval needs before choosing Glacier.
Misconception

Cold storage always saves money. Retrieval charges and latency can negate savings if data must be restored frequently.
