Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
S3 Bucket
Regional container with a globally unique name.
Bucket Policy
JSON-based, granular permissions applied to a bucket.
Lifecycle Policy
Automates transitions and expirations to manage cost and retention.
Summary

Design buckets with region and naming intent, secure them with policies, automate lifecycle transitions, and use versioning + monitoring to protect and audit your data.
