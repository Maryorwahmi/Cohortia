Amazon S3 for SysOps

Operate buckets, protect objects, and automate lifecycle for durable, cost-aware storage.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson

Create secure buckets, apply policies and lifecycle rules, enable versioning, and monitor access to support recovery and cost control.

Why it matters

Correct S3 operations prevent data exposure, reduce storage costs, and provide recovery paths after accidental deletion.

Quick orientation
  • Objects live inside buckets and have a key, metadata, and data payload.
  • Bucket policies are JSON and provide flexible, auditable permissions; ACLs are legacy and limited.
  • Lifecycle and versioning work together: use rules to move or expire versions to control cost.
