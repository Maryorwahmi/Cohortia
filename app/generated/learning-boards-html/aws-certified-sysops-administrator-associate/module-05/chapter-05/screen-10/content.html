Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Sizing Cache & Upload Buffer
Why sizing matters
Undersized cache or buffer reduces performance and may stall uploads, affecting application responsiveness and durability guarantees.
Monitoring checklist
  • Cache hit rate
  • Upload buffer utilization
  • Network throughput and latency
Adjustment guidance
Increase local disk if cache hit rate is low or buffer is frequently full. Re-test after changes to confirm improvement.
Test baseline
For exercises, allocate at least 150 GiB to cache and upload buffer to observe realistic behavior during validation.
Warning

Do not under-provision cache expecting immediate cloud reads to be acceptable; performance will degrade under load.
