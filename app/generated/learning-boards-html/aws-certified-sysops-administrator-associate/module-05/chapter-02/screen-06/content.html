Side-by-side: What CRR does vs doesn't
Does
  • Replicate new objects and new versions.
  • Keep a copy in another region for DR.
  • Respect replication rules by prefix or tag.
Doesn't
  • Replicate existing objects present before rule creation.
  • Replicate delete markers by default.
  • Automatically re-encrypt across KMS keys without configuration.
Operational example
An audit finds that older backups exist only in the source region because CRR was enabled after the backup. The ops team uses S3 Batch Operations with a manifest to copy older objects to the destination and tags them to confirm replication.
Monitoring tip
Enable replication metrics and alerts. Check for failed replication due to permissions or key issues and review replication status for objects you expect to be present.
