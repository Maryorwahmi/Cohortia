Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Storage Lens & Batch Ops
S3 Storage Lens
Collect organization-wide metrics for usage, activity, and configuration to identify cost and protection opportunities. Dashboards provide recommendations and allow drilling into accounts or regions.
Use case
Detect buckets with unexpectedly high GET requests or missing encryption and prioritize remediation.
S3 Batch Operations
Provide a manifest of object keys and run a job to copy, tag, restore, or invoke a Lambda on each object. Batch Ops manages retries and reports results when the job completes.
Example
Apply a missing compliance tag to 5 million objects by supplying a manifest and running a tag job; review the completion report for any failures.
Best practice
Use Storage Lens to find issues and Batch Operations to fix them at scale instead of ad-hoc scripts. Maintain manifests and review job reports for compliance evidence.
