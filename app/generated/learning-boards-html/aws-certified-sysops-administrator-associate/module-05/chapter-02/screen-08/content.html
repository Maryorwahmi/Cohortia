Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Glacier vs Deep Archive
Storage class comparison
S3 Glacier
Retrieval: minutes to hours. Good for archives that may need occasional access within hours.
Glacier Deep Archive
Retrieval: typically within 12 hours. Lowest cost for very infrequent access.
Lifecycle usage
Define a lifecycle rule to transition objects after a retention window. Example: move logs to Glacier after 180 days, or to Deep Archive after 365 days depending on access needs.
Concrete example
Financial audit records: keep in Standard for one year for quick review, then transition to Glacier Deep Archive for the remaining statutory retention period where access is rare.
Misconception
Thinking archival is always cheapest overall. If you retrieve archived data frequently, retrieval costs can exceed savings—model retrieval frequency first.
