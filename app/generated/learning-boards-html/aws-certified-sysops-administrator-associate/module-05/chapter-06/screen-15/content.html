AWS Backup
Managed, centralized backups across services and on-premises gateways.
Backup Plan
Rules, lifecycle and copy actions translate policy into action.
Recovery Points
Stored in vaults and can be copied to other regions or vaults for DR.
Summary takeaway: Centralize policies, map RPO/RTO to plan fields, and validate restores regularly to ensure backups are reliable and auditable.
