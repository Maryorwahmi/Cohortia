Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Common Mistakes
Top mistakes
  • Not testing restores regularly.
  • Insufficient retention for compliance needs.
  • Backups stored only in the same region/AZ.
  • Backups not encrypted or immutable when required.
  • Relying only on crash-consistent snapshots for transactional apps.
Mitigations
  • Automate restore drills and measure RTO.
  • Use cross-region copies and tag-driven assignments.
  • Enforce encryption and consider immutability for archives.
  • Monitor backup jobs with CloudWatch and alert on failures.
Common pitfall

Assuming backups will work without regular drills. Schedule and execute restores to validate your recovery path.
