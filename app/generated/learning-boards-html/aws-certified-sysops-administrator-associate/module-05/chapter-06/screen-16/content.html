Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Operational Checklist
Encryption
Ensure recovery points use KMS-managed keys and transit encryption.
Monitoring
CloudWatch alarms for failed jobs and alerts for copy failures are essential.
DR Drills
Regular restores with timing and integrity checks validate RTOs and reveal gaps.
Checklist takeaway: Automate, encrypt, copy offsite, monitor, and test. These steps are the backbone of a reliable backup and recovery program.
