Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › RPO & RTO
Define RPO

RPO is the maximum tolerable data loss. If RPO = 1 hour, backups or replication must preserve no more than one hour of data loss.

Define RTO

RTO is how quickly systems must be recovered. If RTO = 4 hours, your runbook and automation must return service within that window.

Practical mapping
  • Low RPO: increase backup frequency or use replication.
  • Low RTO: consider warm standby, pilot light, or faster restoration mechanisms.
  • Balance cost: lower RPO/RTO increases cost and complexity.
Check your assumption

RPO and RTO are negotiated with stakeholders, then validated by tests. Don’t guess these numbers; measure them in drills.
