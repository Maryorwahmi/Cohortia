Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Best Practices
Automate
Use AWS Backup rules, CloudWatch, and Lambda to automate backups, lifecycle moves and error handling.
Encrypt & control keys
Encrypt recovery points with KMS and control access for restoration activities.
Immutability & retention
For regulatory retention use immutability features and align lifecycle with compliance timelines.
Monitor jobs
Create CloudWatch alarms for backup job failures and act on alerts quickly.
Tagging & reporting
Tag resources for assignment and auditing so reports and compliance checks are easy.
DR drills
Schedule and document restore drills to prove RTOs and update runbooks with observed bottlenecks.
