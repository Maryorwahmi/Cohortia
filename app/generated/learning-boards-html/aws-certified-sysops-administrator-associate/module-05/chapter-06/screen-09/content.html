Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Cross-Region Copies
Design considerations
  • Decide which recovery points must exist offsite and for how long.
  • Ensure IAM roles permit copy actions across regions.
  • Align secondary-region retention with compliance and storage budget.
Example
Primary: 90-day retention. Copy to eu-west-1 with 180-day retention to support DR and audits.
Check your configuration
  1. Target vault exists in destination region.
  2. Cross-region copy IAM permissions configured.
  3. Retention and lifecycle do not conflict.
  4. Document cost and test restores from destination.
Common mistake

Assuming cross-region copies are automatic. They must be included as copy actions with correct targets and permissions.
