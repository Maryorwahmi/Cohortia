Monitoring & Hands-on Goals
What to measure and the practical steps to validate your changes
Monitor EBS with CloudWatch
  • Track VolumeReadBytes and VolumeWriteBytes for throughput.
  • Use VolumeQueueLength to detect I/O backlogs that indicate insufficient IOPS.
  • Watch BurstBalance for gp2 to avoid throttling when credits deplete.

Hands-on: provision and validate

  • Modify IOPS online and verify the OS recognizes the change, extending file systems as needed.
