Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Hands-on Activity
Activity steps
  1. Launch EC2 (Amazon Linux 2) in your chosen AZ.
  2. Create gp3 20 GiB with IOPS 3000 and throughput 125 in the same AZ and tag it.
  3. Attach volume to the instance and verify with lsblk.
  4. Format (xfs), mount at /appdata, and add to /etc/fstab.
  5. Modify IOPS if needed and rescan/extend FS.
  6. Create a snapshot and tag it for backups.
  7. Clean up: unmount, detach, delete volume and snapshot, terminate instance.
Validation checklist
  • lsblk shows the device and df -h shows the mount point and capacity.
  • CloudWatch shows expected throughput and no undue queueing during tests.
  • The snapshot appears in the console and has tags for easy identification.
Expected outcome
You will complete a full EBS lifecycle for a data volume and understand the operational checks at each step.
