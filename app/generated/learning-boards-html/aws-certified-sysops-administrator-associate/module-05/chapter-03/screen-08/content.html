Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › st1 & sc1 HDD types
st1 — Throughput Optimized HDD
Best for streaming, large sequential workloads where throughput is primary.
Use cases: big data processing, data warehouses, log processing.
sc1 — Cold HDD
Lowest cost HDD for infrequent access and cold storage scenarios.
Use cases: large backups and archival that do not require high IOPS or low latency.
Operational tip
Measure sequential throughput needs with representative workloads; if your job does many small random reads, prefer SSD-based volumes instead of st1 or sc1.
