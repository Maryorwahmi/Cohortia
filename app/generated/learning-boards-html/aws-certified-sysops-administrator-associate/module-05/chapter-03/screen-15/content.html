Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Amazon EBS
Persistent block storage for EC2 that survives instance stops and supports snapshots.
Volume types
gp2/gp3, io1/io2, st1, sc1 — choose by IOPS, throughput, and cost.
Snapshots
Incremental backups stored in S3; used for restore, copy, and migration.
Summary takeaway

Understand persistence, choose the right volume type for your I/O pattern, and back up with snapshots to support recovery and migration.
