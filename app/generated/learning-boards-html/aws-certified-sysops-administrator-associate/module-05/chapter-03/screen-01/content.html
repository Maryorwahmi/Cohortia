Amazon EBS Volumes and Snapshots
Persistent block storage, snapshots, Fast Snapshot Restore, and operational monitoring for EC2
In this lesson

Understand volume types, attach and format volumes, modify performance, create incremental snapshots, and use Fast Snapshot Restore for rapid recovery.

Why this matters
Storage selection and backup strategy directly affect availability, cost, and recovery time for production systems.
Target
By following the hands-on steps, provision a gp3 volume, attach it, format, modify IOPS, snapshot, and restore quickly when needed.
Volume Types
gp3, io2, st1, sc1 — pick based on IOPS versus throughput and cost.
Snapshots
Incremental backups stored in S3; create volumes from snapshots for restore or migration.
FSR
Fast Snapshot Restore pre-provisions data for immediate full performance on restore.
Monitoring
Watch VolumeQueueLength and BurstBalance in CloudWatch to detect bottlenecks.
