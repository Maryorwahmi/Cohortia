Hands-on Activity Lifecycle configuration

Steps (conceptual)

  1. Create a new S3 bucket with a unique name and default settings.
  2. Upload files into frequent-access/ and infrequent-access/ prefixes.
  3. Create lifecycle rule TransitionToIA for frequent-access/: transition to Standard-IA after 30 days, then to Glacier Flexible Retrieval after 90 days.
  4. Create lifecycle rule ExpireInfrequent for infrequent-access/: expire after 180 days.
  5. Review and monitor transitions and costs over the next billing period.

Checks and common pitfalls

  • Ensure rule scope is limited to the intended prefix or tags, not whole bucket.
  • Remember retrieval fees when estimating savings from transitions.
  • Test lifecycle timing with a small dataset before broad application.
Outcome

You’ll have working lifecycle rules and empirical metrics to decide retention timings.
