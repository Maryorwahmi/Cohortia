Definition EBS cost levers

Operational steps

  • Right-size volumes based on CloudWatch metrics.
  • Delete unattached volumes promptly.
  • Prefer gp3 for general purpose workloads.
  • Automate snapshot creation and deletion.

Concrete example

Observed a dev volume with low read/write: reduce provisioned IOPS and switch from gp2 to gp3 to lower monthly cost; schedule automated deletion when a pipeline finishes.

Misconception: detached volumes are free—incorrect. They still incur capacity charges until deleted.
