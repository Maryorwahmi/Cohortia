Definition Glacier overview
Glacier Instant Retrieval
Fast millisecond access for archives that need occasional immediate reads at lower storage cost than IA.
Glacier Flexible Retrieval
Balanced archive tier with minutes-to-hours retrieval; good for long-term archives with occasional restores.
Glacier Deep Archive
Lowest-cost storage for regulatory or historical archives accessed very rarely; retrievals take hours.
Example

For five-year retention with occasional access choose Glacier Flexible Retrieval; for infrequent multi-year retention with rare access choose Deep Archive. Monitor expected restore events to estimate retrieval costs.
