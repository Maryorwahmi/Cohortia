Key Concepts Part 2
Right-size EBS
Monitor and reduce unused capacity; delete unattached volumes.
EFS Intelligent-Tiering
Move inactive files to EFS-IA to lower shared file system costs.
Centralize Backups
Use AWS Backup or DLM to ensure consistent retention and archival to Glacier when appropriate.
Final takeaway

Combine monitoring, policies, and centralized backup to maintain predictable storage costs while meeting access and compliance needs.
