Core Concept Why optimize S3

Definition

Optimizing storage costs means selecting storage classes and lifecycle rules so the sum of storage and retrieval costs is minimized while meeting durability and access requirements.

3 common phases
  • Hot: frequent access, fast retrieval required.
  • Warm: occasional access, balance cost and retrieval fees.
  • Cold: archival, inexpensive storage, tolerant of slower retrieval.
Key Takeaway

Think of storage as a lifecycle with transitions that you can automate; don’t pay premium prices for cold data.

Concrete example: application logs

A compact scenario that maps directly to lifecycle rules.
Days 0–7
S3 Standard for real-time troubleshooting.
Days 8–97
Standard-IA for occasional audit access.
After 97 days
Glacier Flexible Retrieval for long-term archive.
Misconception check: moving data to Glacier saves storage costs but may add retrieval time and fees; align archival choices with expected retrieval frequency and speed.
