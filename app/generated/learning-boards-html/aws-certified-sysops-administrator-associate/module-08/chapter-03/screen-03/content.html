Why policies beat manual cleanup
  • Automated transitions enforce cost rules consistently across buckets.
  • Policies scale; manual interventions introduce omission risk and drift.
  • Retention rules provide auditable proof for compliance and cost control.
When Intelligent-Tiering helps
  • Unpredictable access where monitoring fees are less than retrieval cost surprises.
  • Small files with sporadic access that are costly to classify manually.
  • Avoids repeated policy churn as patterns evolve.
EBS & EFS operational levers
  • Right-size volumes and use gp3 where possible.
  • Use EFS-IA for older files to reduce shared storage costs.
  • Automate snapshot retention with DLM or AWS Backup.
Outcome
You’ll be able to design lifecycle rules and choose storage classes so that data remains accessible when needed and inexpensive when cold.
