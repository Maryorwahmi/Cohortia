Comparison S3 classes
Side-by-side
S3 Standard: Frequent access, highest storage price, lowest retrieval cost.
Intelligent-Tiering: Auto-moves between frequent and infrequent tiers; monitoring fee per object; optional archive tier.
Standard-IA: Lower storage price, higher retrieval fee; good for backups and occasional reads.
One Zone-IA: Cheaper than Standard-IA but single AZ—use for reproducible or secondary copies.
Glacier Instant Retrieval: Archive with millisecond access; cheaper than IA but costlier than other Glacier types.
Glacier Flexible Retrieval: Low storage cost, retrieval in minutes to hours.
Glacier Deep Archive: Lowest cost for rarely accessed data with hours-long retrieval times.
Use-cases and quick decisions
Frequent app data
Choose Standard for performance and low retrieval latency.
Unpredictable access
Use Intelligent-Tiering to avoid manual tuning.
Audit backups
Standard-IA balances cost and faster retrieval for audits.
Regulatory archives
Glacier Flexible or Deep Archive based on retrieval window requirements.
Misconception: cheaper storage always means lower total cost — retrieval and operational overhead may change that calculation.
