Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › What to Remember
Choose representative metrics
Metrics should reflect user impact to be actionable.
Tune alarms to baseline
Balance sensitivity and noise with periods and datapoints.
Trace and verify
Use X-Ray for per-request insight and Flow Logs for network evidence.
Final thought
Adopt a monitoring-first workflow so you catch issues early and fix the real cause, not the symptom.
