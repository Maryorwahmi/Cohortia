Capacity mode comparison
Provisioned Capacity
Specify RCUs and WCUs. Cost-effective for predictable traffic; use Auto Scaling to avoid waste or throttling.
On-Demand Capacity
Pay per request. Ideal for unknown, spiky or infrequent traffic patterns. Simpler to operate, often more costly for steady loads.
Example
New public feature: start with On-Demand to avoid throttling and switch to Provisioned with Auto Scaling once traffic is predictable.
Key Takeaway

Choose capacity mode based on traffic predictability; use Auto Scaling to reduce human maintenance and cost risk.
