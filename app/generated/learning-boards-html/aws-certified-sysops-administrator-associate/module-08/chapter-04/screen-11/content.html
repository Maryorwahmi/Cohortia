Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Monitoring & Auto Scaling
Automated detection and scaling
RDS monitoring
Use CloudWatch alarms on CPUUtilization, FreeableMemory, and IOPS. Create runbooks for right-sizing checks triggered by sustained low utilization.
DynamoDB Auto Scaling
Set target tracking to maintain consumed capacity at a safe percentage of provisioned units. Define min/max bounds to control cost exposure.
Example alarms:
- CPUUtilization < 25% for 72 hours → schedule right-size review
- ConsumedReadCapacityUnits < 50% of provisioned for 30 days → consider lowering RCUs or switching mode
Key Takeaway

Automate metric collection and scaling. Use alarms and Auto Scaling to detect and correct over-provisioning quickly.
