Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Key Concepts
DynamoDB Modes
Provisioned for steady traffic, On-Demand for spiky or unknown workloads.
DynamoDB Optimizations
Use TTL, DAX, and compact item shapes to reduce RCUs, WCUs, and storage costs.
Aurora Serverless
Auto-scales per-second; scales to zero for idle workloads but may introduce cold-start latency.
Summary Takeaway

Match database configuration to workload patterns, automate monitoring and scaling, and prefer targeted changes over blanket upgrades to reduce recurring costs.
