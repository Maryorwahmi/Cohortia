Optimizing Database Costs
RDS and DynamoDB strategies to reduce spend without sacrificing reliability

This chapter helps you diagnose cost drivers and pick the right combination of instance sizing, storage types, availability configurations, and capacity modes. Practical checks and trade-offs are emphasized so you can take action on live fleets.

Quick concept map
RDS
Instance types, storage (gp3 vs io1), Multi-AZ and Read Replicas.
Aurora Serverless
Auto-scales by ACUs, billed per-second, scales to zero for idle workloads.
DynamoDB
Provisioned vs On-Demand, DAX, TTL, item sizing, and Standard-IA.
Explore Key Topics
Right-sizing RDS
Use CloudWatch metrics to avoid over-provisioning CPU, memory, and IOPS.
Aurora Serverless
Best for unpredictable workloads that can accept brief cold starts.
DynamoDB Modes
Pick provisioned for steady loads, on-demand for spiky or unknown traffic.
