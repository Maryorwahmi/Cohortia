Learning Objectives
Practical skills to reduce RDS and DynamoDB spend while preserving service levels
01
Evaluate RDS instances & storage
Learn to inspect CPU, memory, IOPS and choose instance/storage right-sizing to cut wasted spend.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Assess Multi-AZ and Read Replicas
Understand the availability versus cost trade-off and when asynchronous replicas are worth the expense.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Choose Aurora Serverless where appropriate
Identify intermittent or unpredictable workloads that benefit from per-second billing and autoscaling to zero.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Optimize DynamoDB capacity and lifecycle
Pick provisioned or on-demand, enable TTL and DAX where it reduces RCU/WCU consumption.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
