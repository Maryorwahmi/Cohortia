Scenario-driven evaluation
Scenario: Business-hours analytics
Traffic peaks during 9–5 with bursts; idle outside business hours. Recommended: Aurora Serverless to scale to demand and pause when idle.
Scenario: Session store in DynamoDB
Data is critical for 24 hours then irrelevant. Recommended: enable TTL to auto-delete expired sessions and reduce storage.
Ask learners to produce a short write-up: observed metrics, recommended change, estimated monthly cost delta, and rollback procedure. This ensures decisions are measurable and reversible.
Key Takeaway

Design assessments that require metric-backed choices and a rollback plan to reflect operational realities.
