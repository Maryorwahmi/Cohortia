Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Managed Services vs Self‑Manage
Tradeoffs and guidance
Managed services remove patching, backups, and scaling work from your team.
They reduce operational risk and often shorten time to market for features.
Evaluate total cost of ownership: include engineering time, downtime risk, and scaling effort.
If a service is not a strategic differentiator for your product, it is a candidate for managed services. For example, using RDS instead of running a self‑managed database can reduce day‑to‑day maintenance overhead.
Key Takeaway

Focus engineering on what differentiates your business; offload common operational tasks to managed services where it reduces cost and risk.

Example
A team running a self‑managed MySQL spends time on patching and backups. Recommendation: move to RDS to reduce operational overhead and free engineers to build product features.
Misconception: managed services always cost more. Measure TCO including labor and risk before deciding.
