Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Performance Efficiency Overview
Principles that matter
Democratize advanced tech — enable teams to use serverless, managed databases, or ML services to accelerate innovation.
Go global in minutes — deploy to regions and AZs to lower latency and improve resilience.
Experiment more often — use cloud agility to test instance types and configurations.
Mechanical sympathy — pick services that align with access patterns for better performance.
Key Takeaway

Performance comes from aligning workloads to services and iterating through experiments to find the best fit as demand evolves.

Concrete example
Serve static assets from S3 and a CDN for lower latency and less management overhead than serving from EC2.
Misconception: faster means always more expensive. The right architecture can improve both cost and performance.
