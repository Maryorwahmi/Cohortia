Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Cost Optimization Principles
Principles at a glance
01 • Implement Cloud Financial Management — establish budgets, forecasting, and cost awareness across teams.
02 • Adopt a Consumption Model — prefer serverless, burstable instances, and pay‑per‑use services to avoid idle capacity.
03 • Measure Overall Effectiveness — use CloudWatch, Cost Explorer, and Compute Optimizer to find underutilized resources.
04 • Stop Undifferentiated Heavy Lifting — use managed services to focus engineering on business value.
05 • Analyze and Attribute Expenditure — tagging and cost allocation enable accountability and targeted optimization.
Key Takeaway

Combine measurement with consumption patterns and managed services to lower total cost while preserving agility.

Example scenario
A spiky web application uses a large fixed EC2. Recommendation: evaluate serverless functions or burstable instances; add CloudWatch alarms for idle periods and apply lifecycle policies for storage to cheaper tiers.
Common mistake
Cutting services that reduce operational overhead because they appear costlier per hour, without accounting for maintenance and risk reduction.
Tools to use
  • CloudWatch metrics and alarms
  • Cost Explorer for spending trends
  • Compute Optimizer for right‑sizing suggestions
