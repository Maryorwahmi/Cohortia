CFM: people, process, tools
CFM requires tagging, budgeting, and governance so teams can be accountable for spend.
Tags and ownership
Require application, environment, and owner tags. Use automated checks to enforce the policy.
Budgets and alerts
Set budgets per project and configure alerts for anomalies so teams can respond quickly.
Reporting cadence
Share monthly and ad‑hoc cost reports with owners and leadership to drive decisions.
Example
A team applies tags to compute and storage. Cost Explorer shows that one service accounts for 40% of the spend; owner investigates and applies lifecycle rules to reduce storage class costs.
Misconception
Tags alone do not produce savings. They are the input for measurement and action that must be governed and reviewed.
Key Takeaway

CFM turns cost data into ownership and action; enforce tags, budget, and reviews to make it work.
