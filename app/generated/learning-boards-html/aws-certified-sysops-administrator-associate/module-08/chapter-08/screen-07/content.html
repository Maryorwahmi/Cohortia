Comparison: Fixed vs Consumption
Fixed capacity (EC2)
  • Predictable performance for steady workloads
  • Potential waste when idle
  • Lower per‑unit cost for sustained use
Consumption (Lambda, Fargate)
  • Pay for actual usage
  • Automatic scaling and reduced ops
  • May cost more for sustained high throughput
Experiment with short trials. Measure cost and latency across expected traffic patterns to decide which model fits the workload.
Example
A reporting job runs nightly with bursts and long idle periods. Recommendation: move the job to a serverless function or Fargate task to eliminate constant instance costs.
Misconception
Consumption always saves money — not true for steady, high‑throughput systems where reserved capacity could be cheaper.
Key Takeaway

Choose consumption for variable workloads; measure and experiment to confirm the economics for your use case.
