Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Measure Overall Effectiveness
Tools and metrics
CloudWatch: capture CPU, memory, I/O, and latency to identify low utilization.
Cost Explorer: view spending trends and identify highest cost services and accounts.
Compute Optimizer: receive right‑sizing recommendations based on historical utilization.
Validate recommendations by running short experiments or controlled load tests. Use tags to map resources to teams and cost owners before making changes.
Key Takeaway

Don’t act on a single metric. Combine utilization and cost data, validate changes, and prioritize where spend and low utilization overlap.

Example diagnostic
Find: EC2 instance with 5% average CPU and high monthly cost
Action: Run brief load test, move non‑critical jobs, consider smaller instance or schedule stop/start for idle hours
Misconception: low CPU always means removable. Confirm with application metrics and I/O patterns before right‑sizing.
