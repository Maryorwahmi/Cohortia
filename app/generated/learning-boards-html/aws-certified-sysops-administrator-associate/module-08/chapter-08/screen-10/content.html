Tag schema and enforcement
Adopt consistent tags for application, environment, and owner; automate validation when possible.
app:billing
env:prod
owner:payments
With tags in place, generate cost reports that show spend by tag. Use that to identify optimization targets like storage tiering or right‑sizing compute.
Key Takeaway

Tagging supplies the mapping from cost to owner. Enforce tags and use cost reports to prioritize actions that reduce spend.

Example action
Report shows storage costs concentrated on a single app. Action: apply lifecycle policy to move archives to Glacier and notify owner to clean up unused data.
Misconception: tagging is a one‑time task. In reality, it requires governance, tooling, and recurring audits to stay useful.
