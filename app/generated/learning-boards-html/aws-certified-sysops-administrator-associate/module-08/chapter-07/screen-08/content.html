Enforce vs Audit
Compare tools to prevent or detect missing tags and choose the right combination for your accounts.
AWS Config (Audit & Rules)
  • Evaluate resources against tagging rules.
  • Report non-compliance and trigger remediation.
  • Good for continuous monitoring and reporting.
Service Control Policies (Prevent)
  • Apply at the organization or account level.
  • Can block actions that don't meet policy constraints.
  • Best for preventing non-compliant resource creation where possible.
Integration pattern
Use SCPs for prevention, Config for detection, and automation (Lambda or runbooks) for remediation of flagged resources.
Key Takeaway

Combine preventive policies with audit rules and automated remediation to maintain tag coverage at scale.
