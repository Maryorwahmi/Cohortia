Learning Objectives
Learn, understand, apply, and master practical tagging and cost-allocation tasks.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Understand Cost Allocation
Explain why allocating costs to teams and projects creates accountability and clearer optimization choices.
Venn: Finance ∩ Engineering = Actionable budgets
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Design Tagging Strategies
Create naming standards, mandatory tags, and a minimal set of keys to prevent unallocated costs.
Mnemonic: KISS — Keep It Simple & Standard
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Activate Cost Allocation Tags
Enable selected tag keys in the Billing console so Cost Explorer and reports include those fields.
Timeline: Activation may take up to 24 hours to appear in reports

Use Resource Groups
Create tag-based groups for consolidated monitoring, automation, and permissioning across related resources.
