Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Reviewing and Auditing Tags
Audit workflow
Detect, report, and remediate tag gaps on a schedule that fits your org.
Discover
Use Tag Editor queries to list resources missing mandatory keys.
Report
Export findings and notify resource owners for remediation steps.
Remediate
Use automation or runbooks to apply missing tags or shut down unapproved resources.
Operational cadence
Schedule regular audits and use Config compliance trends to measure tagging coverage over time.
Key Takeaway

Auditing is continuous. Combine discovery, reporting, and remediation to keep tag data accurate and usable for billing.
