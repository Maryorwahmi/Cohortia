Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › RIs & Savings Plans
Reserved Instances (RIs)
One- or three-year billing discount applied to matching On-Demand usage. Payment options affect effective discount and cash flow.
Standard RI: highest discount, less flexible.
Convertible RI: lower discount, can change family or OS during the term.
Savings Plans
Commit to $/hour for 1 or 3 years; discounts apply broadly across compute services depending on plan.
EC2 Instance Savings Plans: apply to specific instance families.
Compute Savings Plans: broadest coverage across EC2, Fargate and Lambda.
Key Takeaway

Choose RIs for firm instance commitments and Savings Plans when you need flexibility across instance types and services.
