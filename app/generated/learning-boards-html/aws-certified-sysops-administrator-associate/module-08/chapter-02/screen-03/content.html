Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Learning Objectives — Applied
Operational Steps
  1. Measure metrics for CPU, memory, network, and disk over representative periods.
  2. Run Compute Optimizer to get right-sizing suggestions and validate against benchmarks.
  3. Match purchasing option to workload risk and predictability, then monitor utilization of commitments.
  4. Automate non-production schedules and test Spot interruptions with safe, isolated jobs.
Mnemonic: Measure → Recommend → Choose → Automate.
Assessment cues
Can you list the trade-offs between Convertible and Standard Reserved Instances?
Can you design an Auto Scaling group that mixes On-Demand, Spot, and RIs?
Can you schedule dev instances to stop outside business hours?
