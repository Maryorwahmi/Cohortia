Why these objectives matter

EC2 often makes up a large portion of cloud spend. Measuring usage and matching buying strategy to workload profile unlocks predictable savings and reduces surprise bills.

Real-world mnemonic
Measure → Match → Commit → Automate
Common mistake
Buying RIs without measuring utilization first leads to wasted spend.
