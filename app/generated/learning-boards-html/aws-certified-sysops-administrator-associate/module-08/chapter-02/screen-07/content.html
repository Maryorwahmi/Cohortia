Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Purchasing Options
Quick comparison
On-Demand
Most flexible, pay per use, best for unpredictable workloads and testing.
Reserved Instances (RIs)
Discounted for 1 or 3 years; best for stable, always-on workloads; manage utilization to avoid waste.
Flexible options
Savings Plans
Commit to $/hour for 1 or 3 years. Apply across EC2, Fargate and Lambda depending on plan type.
Spot Instances
Deep discounts for interruptible capacity. Use for fault-tolerant, stateless, or batch workloads.
Key Takeaway

Use commitment when usage is predictable and Spot for inexpensive, interruptible work. Savings Plans give broad flexibility across services.
