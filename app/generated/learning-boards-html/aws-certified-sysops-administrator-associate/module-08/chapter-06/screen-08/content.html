Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Provisioned Concurrency
What it is
Provisioned Concurrency pre-warms execution environments to reduce cold-start latency, with a continuous per-millisecond cost.
  • Reduces cold-starts and stabilizes latency.
  • Billed even when idle; cost is predictable but continuous.
  • Best for critical, latency-sensitive functions with steady traffic.
Decision checklist
Compare user-experience gain to the steady cost. Start small and measure latency improvements and per-request cost delta.
When to enable
Critical endpoints with tight latency requirements and predictable traffic.
When not to
Sporadic, low-volume functions where cost of idle pre-warmed environments outweighs latency benefit.
