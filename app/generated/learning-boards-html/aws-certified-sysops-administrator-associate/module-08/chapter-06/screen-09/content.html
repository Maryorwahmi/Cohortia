ECS on EC2
You manage EC2 instances and can apply EC2 cost optimizations like right-sizing, Auto Scaling, Spot Instances, and Reserved Instances.
  • Best when you can leverage instance-level discounts.
  • More operational overhead for node maintenance.
ECS on Fargate
Serverless container compute billed per vCPU and memory per second. Right-sizing tasks and autoscaling are critical to avoid paying for unused capacity.
  • Remove node management burden.
  • Use Compute Savings Plans for consistent spend discounts.
Practical advice
Stateless bursty services → Fargate. Predictable, steady workloads → EC2 with reserved capacity or Spot where acceptable.
