Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Common Mistakes
Top mistakes
  • Assuming services are free or low cost without usage analysis.
  • Using broad budgets that lack actionability.
  • Inconsistent tags that break reporting and budgets.
  • Enabling actions without sandbox testing.
Mitigation checklist
Map billing metrics, enforce tags, scope budgets narrowly, and test automations in a non-production environment before rollout.
Quick action
Run an inventory of untagged resources and create a low-risk budget for one service to validate your alerting path.
Key Takeaway

Small governance steps—tags, scoped budgets, sandbox tests—prevent the majority of unexpected charges.
