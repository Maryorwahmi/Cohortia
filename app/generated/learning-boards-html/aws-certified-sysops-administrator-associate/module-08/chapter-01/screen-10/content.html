Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Organizations & Consolidated Billing
Structure
A management account holds consolidated billing, while member accounts run resources and retain operational control.
Practical uses
  • Aggregate billing and apply organization-level budgets.
  • Enable volume pricing benefits across accounts.
  • Keep account-level budgets for operational ownership.
Example
Use the management account to run Cost Explorer across linked accounts to see department-level spend and then set an org-level budget to monitor total spend.
Governance tip
Combine organization-level views with per-account budgets so teams remain accountable for their own resources.
Key Takeaway

Consolidated billing simplifies payments and analysis but must be paired with account-level controls for operational safety.
