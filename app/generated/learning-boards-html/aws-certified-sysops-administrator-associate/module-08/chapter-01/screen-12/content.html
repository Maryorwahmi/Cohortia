Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Tags & Filters
Why tags
Tags let you attribute cost to projects, teams, or environments and power filters in Cost Explorer and Budgets for actionable insights.
Best practice
Enforce a small set of required tags such as Project, Environment, and Owner, and validate coverage regularly to ensure budgets and reports are accurate.
Example
Create a budget filtered by tag Project:MyProject to track that project’s EC2 and S3 costs independently from other work.
Operational tip
Use tagging enforcement and discovery to find untagged resources and correct them before relying on budgets that use tags.
Key Takeaway

Tagging is essential for meaningful cost allocation. Start with a few enforced tags and expand as needed.
