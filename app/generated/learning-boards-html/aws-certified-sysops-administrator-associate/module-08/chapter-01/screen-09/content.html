Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Tool Comparison
Billing Dashboard
Quick estimate, invoices, and payment settings. Best for immediate triage and confirmation of spikes.
Cost Explorer
Interactive reports, groupings, and forecasting for root-cause analysis and trend projection.
AWS Budgets
Continuous monitoring and alerting with optional automated actions to contain costs.
Workflow example
A spike is confirmed in the dashboard, analyzed in Cost Explorer to identify the service and tag, and then a scoped budget is created to monitor and optionally stop non-critical resources in future.
Check your understanding
Which tool finds the driver of a cost spike and which enforces spending limits? Cost Explorer finds the driver; Budgets enforces limits.
