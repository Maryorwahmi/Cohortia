Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Public vs Private Subnets
How exposure is decided

The route table of a subnet and whether instances have public IPs determine reachability from the internet.

Public subnet
Route: 0.0.0.0/0 → Internet Gateway (IGW). Instances can have public IPs or EIPs.
Private subnet
No direct route to IGW; outbound internet requires a NAT Gateway or equivalent.
Concrete example
Place a web load balancer in a public subnet with a route to the IGW and your application servers in private subnets that route to a NAT Gateway for outbound traffic only.
Common mistake

Assuming private subnet instances can reach the internet without a NAT; they cannot initiate outbound internet directly without NAT or IGW placement.
