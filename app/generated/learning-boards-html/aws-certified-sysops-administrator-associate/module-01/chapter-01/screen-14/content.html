Backup best practices
  • Automate snapshots and object storage backups
  • Keep backups in durable, versioned storage
  • Define and document recovery objectives
Concrete procedure
Schedule automated snapshots, copy backup artifacts to durable object storage, and perform a test restore quarterly to verify procedures.
Misconception
Backups without tested restores provide false assurance. Always practice restores under controlled conditions.
Key Takeaway

Automate backups, store them durably, and practice restores to ensure your disaster recovery plan works when it’s needed.
