Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Regions & AZs
Regions
Distinct geographic areas used to place resources near users or to meet compliance requirements.
When to choose a Region
  • User proximity and latency requirements
  • Data residency and compliance
  • Cost and available services
Availability Zones
Isolated data centers within a Region that reduce correlated failure risk when you spread resources across them.
Best practice
Deploy critical services — compute, database, and load balancers — across multiple AZs to improve availability.
Example deployment
A three-tier application: web tier auto-scales across three AZs, database in multi-AZ standby mode, and S3 for shared storage.
Misconception
Treating AZs as purely logical is risky; they are physically separate to limit failures.
Pro tip
Start multi-AZ for critical services; right-size later to balance cost and resilience.
