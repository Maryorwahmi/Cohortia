Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › On‑Demand, Reserved, and Spot
On‑Demand

Flexible pay-as-you-go pricing with no long-term commitment. Best for unpredictable workloads, development, and short-term tasks.

Reserved Instances

Offer significant discounts for 1- or 3-year commitments. Use for steady, predictable services to lower costs.

Spot Instances

Deep discounts for spare capacity. Best for interruptible, fault-tolerant workloads like batch processing and analytics.

Decision checklist
  • If workload is unpredictable: choose On‑Demand.
  • If workload is steady and predictable: evaluate Reserved Instances.
  • If workload tolerates interruption and you want max savings: use Spot.
Key Takeaway

Pick purchasing options based on workload predictability and tolerance for interruption to reduce costs without sacrificing reliability.
