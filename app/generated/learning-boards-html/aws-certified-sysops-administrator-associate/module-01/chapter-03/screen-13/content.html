Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Durability & Backup
Architecture guidance
S3
Highly durable by design across multiple AZs; ideal for long-term retention and archives like compliance logs.
EBS
AZ-scoped block storage; replication within AZ protects against device failure but requires snapshots for cross-AZ DR.
Recommended pattern
Daily EBS snapshots for operational restores, copy critical snapshots to another Region, and use S3 Glacier Deep Archive for long-term compliance retention.
Validation
Regularly test restores from snapshots and lifecycle transitions to ensure recovery times and data integrity meet objectives.
