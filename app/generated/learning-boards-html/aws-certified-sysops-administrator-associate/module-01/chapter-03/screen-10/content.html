Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › EBS Volume Types
Comparison

Choose by IO profile

gp2 / gp3
Balanced price and performance for most transactional workloads and boot volumes.
io1 / io2
Provisioned IOPS SSD for high and sustained IOPS, suitable for large databases.
st1
Throughput optimized HDD for sequential workloads like big data and logs.
sc1
Cold HDD for the lowest cost per GB on infrequently accessed data.
Operational note
When migrating volume type or resizing, snapshot first and plan for reattach operations when needed.
Example
A PostgreSQL primary may use io2 for consistent low latency; analytic nodes processing logs may use st1 for throughput efficiency.
