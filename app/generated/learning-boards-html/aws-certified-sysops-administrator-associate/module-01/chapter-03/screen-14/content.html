Amazon S3
Object storage for scalable, durable storage of files and archives.
EBS Volumes
Persistent block storage attached to an EC2 instance within an Availability Zone.
Lifecycle & Snapshots
Automate S3 tiering with lifecycle policies and protect EBS with incremental snapshots stored on S3.
Takeaway

Choose the right storage type, automate lifecycle and snapshots, and validate restores to meet cost and recovery goals.
