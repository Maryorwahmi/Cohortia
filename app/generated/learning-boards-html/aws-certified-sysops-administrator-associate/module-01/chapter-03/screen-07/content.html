Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › S3 Storage Classes
Comparison

Choose by access pattern and cost

S3 Standard
Low latency for frequently accessed objects.
Intelligent-Tiering
Auto-moves between tiers based on access, reducing manual policy work.
Standard-IA
Lower storage cost for infrequently accessed but quickly retrievable objects.
Glacier
Archival storage with retrieval times in minutes to hours.
Glacier Deep Archive
Lowest-cost long-term archive where retrievals are rare and can tolerate hours to days.
Practical example
Keep daily backups in Standard for a short period, then transition to Standard-IA after 30 days and Glacier Deep Archive for 7-year compliance.
Misconception
Don’t assume lower-cost tiers remove retrieval cost; retrieval and early deletion charges can offset savings if access patterns are not understood.
