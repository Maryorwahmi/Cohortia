Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › What to Remember
S3 is object storage
Durable, region-scoped, and ideal for static content and archives.
EBS is block storage
Attach to a single EC2 in an AZ for low-latency I/O and boot volumes.
Lifecycle & Snapshots
Automate tiering and use incremental snapshots to protect volumes and enable recovery.
Closing takeaway

On every design decision, map workload needs to storage type, automate cost and retention, and verify restores to ensure recoverability.
