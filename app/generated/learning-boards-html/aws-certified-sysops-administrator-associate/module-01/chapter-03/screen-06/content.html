Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Bucket Policies vs ACLs
Comparison
ACLs
Legacy, object-level grants; limited conditions and harder to audit at scale.
Bucket Policies
JSON-based, fine-grained, supports conditions and cross-account principals; preferred for most cases.
Policy Example (snippet)
{
  "Statement": [
    {
      "Effect": "Allow",
      "Principal": {"AWS": "arn:aws:iam::123456789012:role/PartnerReadRole"},
      "Action": ["s3:GetObject"],
      "Resource": ["arn:aws:s3:::my-bucket/*"]
    }
  ]
}
Operational guidance
  • Use bucket policies for cross-account access and conditional rules.
  • Keep Block Public Access enabled unless an exception is justified and documented.
  • Audit policies and prefer principals over public access entries.
Common mistake
Making a bucket public via an ACL or an overly broad policy without recording why increases risk of data loss or exposure.
Pro tip

When you need external access, scope a single path and test with a specific IAM principal before expanding access.
