Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Parameters
Inputs that make templates configurable at create and update time.
Mappings & Conditions
Lookups for static values and expressions to create resources conditionally.
Outputs & Exports
Return values and export them for other stacks to import and consume.
Intrinsic Functions
Ref, GetAtt, Join, and Sub let templates compute and retrieve values at deploy time.
StackSets
Administrator-led deployments across many accounts and regions to enforce baselines.
Nested Stacks
Break large templates into smaller child templates for modularity and team ownership.
Summary takeaway
Design templates to accept inputs, select correct values, expose stable outputs, and scale across accounts using StackSets and nested structures. Validate inputs and keep exports stable to reduce operational risk.
