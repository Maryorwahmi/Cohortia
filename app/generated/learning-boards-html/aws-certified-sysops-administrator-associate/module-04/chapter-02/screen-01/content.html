Advanced CloudFormation Concepts

Techniques to make templates configurable, shareable, and multi-account ready.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass

We’ll connect concrete techniques—parameters, mappings, and outputs—with intrinsic functions and deployment patterns so your templates scale across teams and regions. This session mixes design patterns with a practical refactor of an EC2 template so you can follow along.

Use these features to avoid hardcoding values, enable environment-specific behavior, and share resources securely between stacks.
