Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › In-place vs Blue/Green
In-place deployments
Updates occur on the existing instances. Use deployment configurations to control parallelism.
  • Pros: simpler, cost-efficient.
  • Cons: potential brief downtime, harder rollback.
  • Use when you can tolerate short interruptions or have small fleets.
Blue/Green deployments
Provision a new environment, validate it, then shift traffic. Ideal for zero-downtime releases.
  • Pros: minimal user impact, easy rollback.
  • Cons: higher resource usage, more orchestration.
  • Use for critical production systems requiring high availability.
Decision tip

Choose blue/green when user-facing uptime and rollback speed outweigh the cost of extra instances. Use in-place for lower-cost environments with controlled update windows.
