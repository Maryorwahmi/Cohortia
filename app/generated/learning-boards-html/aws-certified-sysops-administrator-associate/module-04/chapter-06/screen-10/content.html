Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Scheduling with EventBridge
Cron example
Use cron expressions like cron(0 17 ? * MON-FRI *) to run at 5 PM Monday through Friday. Always account for UTC vs local time when scheduling.
Testing tip

During development set a rule to trigger every few minutes to confirm behavior, then change to the production schedule once validated.

Misconception
Assuming cron expressions are identical between systems. EventBridge uses its own field positions; always verify your expression.
Safety considerations
  • Design functions to be idempotent for safe repeated runs.
  • Test schedules in a sandbox or with short intervals first.
  • Adjust for timezone differences and daylight saving if necessary.
Key Takeaway

Use cron expressions carefully, test with short intervals, and ensure functions handle retries gracefully.
