Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Manual vs Serverless
Manual operations
  • Requires human scheduling or an always-on automation host.
  • Risk of missed steps and inconsistent execution.
  • Harder to maintain an audit trail unless you add logging.
Serverless automation
  • EventBridge triggers Lambda on schedule or event.
  • Automatic logging to CloudWatch; pay-per-use cost model.
  • Requires secure IAM and careful testing to avoid mistakes.
Decision guide
If a task runs on a schedule or is driven by service events and can complete in a short time, prefer Lambda.
If the task requires long-running stateful processing, an alternate approach may be needed.
Misconception
Automation eliminates the need for monitoring. In truth, automation demands better logs and alerts so failures are visible and actionable.
Key Takeaway

Choose automation for repeatable, short tasks and pair it with observability to maintain confidence.
