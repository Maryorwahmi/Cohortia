Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Automation Scenarios
Cost Optimization

Stop or start non-production EC2/RDS on schedules to reduce runtime charges.

Backup & Recovery

Take EBS or RDS snapshots on a schedule to ensure recoverable points-in-time.

Security & Compliance

Respond to events like public S3 buckets and enforce policies automatically.

Resource Management

Clean up unattached volumes, old snapshots, and unused ENIs on a routine schedule.

Alerting & Remediation

Trigger automated restarts or ticket creation in response to CloudWatch alarms.

Custom Application Events

Route your app events through EventBridge to invoke business logic without servers.

Checklist
  • Define trigger: schedule or event pattern.
  • Scope IAM permissions to required API actions.
  • Log actions to CloudWatch and test manually first.
