Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Final Reminders
1. Buildspec is a contract
A correct buildspec ensures your build environment runs the same steps every time.
2. Artifacts connect stages
Verify artifact paths and S3 contents after builds so deploy stages have valid inputs.
3. Logs are your diagnostic tool
Open CodeBuild logs for command errors and use pipeline history for stage-level context.
Closing Thought
Practice small, repeatable commits and test builds frequently. That habit makes CI/CD powerful by finding problems early and keeping deployments predictable.
