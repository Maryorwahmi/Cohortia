High Availability and SSL/TLS with ELB

Building Resilient and Secure Applications

Ensuring Continuous Service

ELB is a cornerstone for high availability and security. Proper deployment across Availability Zones and robust SSL/TLS management are critical for resilient applications.

Multi-AZ Deployment

Always deploy your load balancer across at least two Availability Zones to prevent single points of failure.

SSL/TLS Certificate Management

Ensure certificates for HTTPS listeners are properly managed and renewed to avoid service interruptions.

AWS Certificate Manager (ACM)

Recommended service for provisioning, managing, and deploying SSL/TLS certificates with ELB.
