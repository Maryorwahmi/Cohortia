Chapter 3.4 Review

Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Elastic Load Balancing (ELB)

Service that distributes incoming traffic across multiple targets for fault tolerance and scalability.

Application Load Balancer (ALB)

Layer 7 load balancer for HTTP/HTTPS traffic, offering advanced content-based routing.

Network Load Balancer (NLB)

Layer 4 load balancer for TCP/UDP/TLS traffic, providing extreme performance and ultra-low latency.

Summary Takeaway

ELB is a critical service for managing application traffic, offering specialized load balancers (ALB, NLB, GLB) to meet diverse architectural and performance requirements.
