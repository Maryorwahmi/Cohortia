Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Quick Reference
Check Security Groups first
SG misconfiguration is the most common reason instances are unreachable.
Allow ephemeral outbound in NACLs
Without ephemeral allowances, stateless NACLs will break return traffic for client-initiated connections.
Order NACL rules deliberately
First match wins; put explicit denies at low numbers when you need them to take precedence.
Final thought
Thinking in scope, state, and order helps you pick the right control and reduces the chance of accidental outages.
