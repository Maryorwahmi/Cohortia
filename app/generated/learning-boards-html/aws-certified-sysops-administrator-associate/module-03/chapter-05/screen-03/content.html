Verification steps and mnemonics

Short checks to confirm each objective.

CloudFront Deployed

Mnemonic: "Edge first" — requests should resolve to an edge PoP and deliver cached content where available.

OAC Configured

Check: raw S3 object fetch should be denied, while CloudFront origin fetch is allowed.

DNS Delegation

Copy NS records to your registrar and verify with a dig or DNS lookup until delegation stabilizes.

Routing Policy

Test weighted or latency-based routing by comparing responses from different client locations or simulated testers.
