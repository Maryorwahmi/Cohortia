Hosted zones and Name Servers

A hosted zone stores record sets for a domain. To make Route 53 authoritative, copy the assigned NS records to your registrar.

Hosted zone: container for records that route traffic for a domain and subdomains.
NS records: four name servers you copy to your registrar to delegate DNS authority.
Record sets: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, NS, and Alias records define routing behavior.
Key Takeaway

Creating records in Route 53 is necessary but not sufficient; remember to update your registrar with Route 53's NS records to delegate your domain.

Practical example
Create a public hosted zone for yourdomain.com, copy the four NS records, and paste them into your domain registrar's name-server settings. Then create an Alias A record for www that points to your CloudFront distribution.
Note
Use Alias records instead of CNAME at the zone apex; Alias behaves like a CNAME for AWS resources but can be used at the root domain.
