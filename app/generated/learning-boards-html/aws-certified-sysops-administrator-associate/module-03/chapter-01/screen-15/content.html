What to Remember
VPC
Logically isolated virtual network where you define CIDR and subnets.
Subnets & AZs
Subnets are confined to a single Availability Zone; create matching subnets for redundancy.
Internet Gateway & Routes
Attach an IGW and add 0.0.0.0/0 in the public route table for internet access.
Summary

Design your VPC and subnet layout with clear CIDR allocation, AZ-aware subnets, and explicit route table associations so your infrastructure is reliable and secure.
