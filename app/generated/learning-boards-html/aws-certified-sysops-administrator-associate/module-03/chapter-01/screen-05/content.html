Process Flow › Planning CIDR Blocks
CIDR planning steps
1.
Inventory existing IP ranges to avoid overlap with VPNs or corporate networks.
2.
Estimate address needs for current and near-term growth.
3.
Choose a VPC prefix (for example, 10.100.0.0/16) and reserve /24 blocks for subnets.
4.
Document ranges and their intended use to maintain clarity as the environment grows.
Concrete example
VPC: 10.100.0.0/16. PublicSubnet-AZ1: 10.100.1.0/24. PublicSubnet-AZ2: 10.100.2.0/24.
Common mistakes
  • Choosing a CIDR that overlaps a corporate VPN or another VPC you will peer.
  • Allocating unnecessarily large ranges without a plan.
Key Takeaway

Plan CIDR ranges with future growth and peering in mind; avoid overlaps and document your allocation strategy.
