01

Differentiate Advanced XSS

Distinguish between DOM XSS and mutation XSS, understanding their unique execution contexts and how they bypass traditional server-side filters. This skill is crucial for identifying subtle client-side vulnerabilities.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Bypass CSP & WAFs

Understand and apply techniques to circumvent Content Security Policy (CSP) and Web Application Firewalls (WAFs) to successfully achieve XSS. This involves recognizing misconfigurations and exploiting parsing differences.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Utilize BeEF Framework

Explore the capabilities of the Browser Exploitation Framework (BeEF) for post-exploitation, understanding how to hook browsers and execute client-side commands. This extends the impact of an XSS vulnerability.
