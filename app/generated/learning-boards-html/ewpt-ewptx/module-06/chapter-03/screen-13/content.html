Avoiding Common WAF Evasion Mistakes

Strategies for persistent and effective WAF bypass attempts.

Common Pitfalls

  • Not understanding the WAF's specific rules.
  • Failing to test different parts of the HTTP request (headers, body, parameters).
  • Giving up too early due to initial blocks.
  • Not analyzing WAF responses to refine payloads.

Pro-Tip: Analyze WAF Responses

Observe if the WAF blocks, redirects, or strips characters. This feedback is crucial for refining your payloads and understanding the WAF's logic.

Effective Strategies

  • Be persistent and creative in crafting payloads.
  • Systematically test all possible injection points and encoding types.
  • Research known bypasses for the identified WAF product.
  • Iterate on your payloads based on WAF feedback.

Check Your Understanding

If a WAF strips specific characters from your payload, what does that tell you about its processing? How would you adapt your next attempt?

Key Takeaway

Successful WAF evasion requires persistence, creativity, and a systematic approach to analyzing WAF responses and refining payloads, avoiding common pitfalls like insufficient testing or early surrender.
