Evasion Fundamentals

Why and How to Bypass WAFs

Identifying gaps in WAF defenses through creative testing.

Purpose of Bypassing

To thoroughly test WAF effectiveness and identify gaps that could be exploited by real adversaries, ensuring robust security.

WAF Effectiveness Factors

  • Rule set sophistication
  • Encoding handling capabilities
  • Application context awareness
Key Takeaway

WAF bypass techniques are crucial for penetration testers to thoroughly evaluate security controls and uncover exploitable vulnerabilities.

The WAF Testing Cycle
1. Identify WAF

Determine the WAF product in use.

2. Analyze Rules

Research known bypasses and rule logic.

3. Craft Payload

Design a malicious payload with evasion in mind.

4. Test & Observe

Send the payload and analyze WAF response.
