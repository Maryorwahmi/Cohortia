Executive Summary: The Strategic Overview

Communicating high-level risk and strategic recommendations to leadership

Key Elements

  • Non-technical overview of engagement and key findings.
  • Overall risk assessment and potential business impact.
  • Strategic, high-level recommendations for improvement.
  • Concise and easily digestible for executive decision-makers.
Key Takeaway

The Executive Summary translates technical vulnerabilities into business risks, enabling informed strategic decisions.

Common Mistake

Making the executive summary too technical or too vague, failing to clearly articulate business impact and strategic priorities.
