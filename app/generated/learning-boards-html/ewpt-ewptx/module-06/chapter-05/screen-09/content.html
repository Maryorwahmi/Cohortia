Risk Assessment with CVSS

Objectively rating vulnerabilities with the Common Vulnerability Scoring System

What is CVSS?

  • Widely adopted framework for vulnerability severity.
  • Provides a standardized numerical score (0-10).
  • Based on various metrics describing the vulnerability.
  • Helps organizations objectively prioritize remediation.
Key Takeaway

CVSS offers an objective, standardized method to assess vulnerability severity, crucial for prioritizing remediation efforts.

Business Context

Always supplement the CVSS score with context-specific business risk to explain its true impact on the organization.
