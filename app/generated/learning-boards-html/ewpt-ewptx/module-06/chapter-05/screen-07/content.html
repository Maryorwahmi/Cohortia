Steps to Reproduce & Proof of Concept

Providing verifiable evidence and precise instructions for exploitation

Steps to Reproduce

  • Precise, repeatable instructions.
  • Include HTTP requests, parameters, and expected responses.
  • Leverage Burp Suite logs and command-line outputs.
  • Allows developers to independently verify the finding.

Proof of Concept (PoC)

  • Screenshots or video recordings demonstrating exploitation.
  • Redacted code snippets showing successful impact.
  • Clear visual evidence of the vulnerability's consequences.
  • Crucial for convincing stakeholders of the issue's reality.
