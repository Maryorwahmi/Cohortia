Technical Findings: Documenting Vulnerabilities

Meticulously documenting each identified vulnerability with precision

Core Elements of a Finding

  • Vulnerability Title: Clear and concise name for the flaw.
  • Description: Detailed explanation of the vulnerability.
  • Impact: Potential consequences if exploited (e.g., data breach, unauthorized access).
  • Steps to Reproduce: Precise instructions to verify.
  • Proof of Concept (PoC): Evidence of successful exploitation.
  • Remediation Recommendations: Specific, actionable advice.
Key Takeaway

Each technical finding must be a standalone entry, providing all necessary details for understanding and remediation.

Why it matters

The clarity and completeness of your technical findings directly influence the speed and effectiveness of remediation efforts by development teams.
