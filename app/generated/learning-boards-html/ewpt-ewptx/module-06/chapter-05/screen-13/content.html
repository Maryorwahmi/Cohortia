Key Concepts

Essential terms from this chapter

Penetration Test Report

The primary deliverable documenting findings, risks, and remediation recommendations for various stakeholders.

Executive Summary

A high-level, non-technical overview of findings, overall risk, and strategic recommendations for management.

Technical Findings

Detailed documentation of each identified vulnerability, including description, impact, and remediation advice.

Summary Takeaway

These core components ensure that your penetration test results are effectively communicated and understood by all stakeholders.
