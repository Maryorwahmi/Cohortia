Core Concept

The Professional Penetration Test Report

Your roadmap for security improvement and primary deliverable

Purpose & Audience

The report documents vulnerabilities and guides remediation. It must cater to both technical teams (developers, admins) and non-technical executives (management) by translating complex findings into relevant business context.

Key Takeaway

A well-structured report ensures that technical findings are understood and acted upon, driving real security improvements.

Why it matters

Without a clear, professional report, even critical vulnerabilities may go unaddressed, undermining the value of the entire penetration test.
