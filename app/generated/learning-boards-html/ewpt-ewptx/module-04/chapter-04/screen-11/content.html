Foundations of Secure Access Control

Implementing Robust Authorization Frameworks

Key Principles for Defense

Robust RBAC/ABAC: Centralized, consistent authorization framework defining roles and permissions.

Least Privilege: Grant only the absolute minimum permissions required for users and roles.

Server-Side Enforcement: All authorization decisions must be made and enforced on the server, never client-side.

RBAC vs. ABAC

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Permissions are associated with roles (e.g., 'Admin', 'Editor'), and users are assigned to roles. Simpler to manage for many applications.

Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC)

Access is granted based on attributes of the user, resource, and environment. Offers highly granular and dynamic control, suitable for complex scenarios.

Key Takeaway

A strong access control foundation relies on well-defined authorization models, minimal privileges, and strict server-side enforcement.
