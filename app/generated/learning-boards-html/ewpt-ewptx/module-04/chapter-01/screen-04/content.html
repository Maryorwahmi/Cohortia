Core Concept

Authentication vs. Authorization

Understanding the fundamental differences in access control

Authentication

  • •Verifies user identity: 'Are you who you say you are?'
  • •Typically involves credentials (username/password).
  • •First step in granting access to a system.
  • •Example: Logging into an email account.
Analogy

Showing your ID at an airport checkpoint to prove your identity.

Authorization

  • •Determines access rights: 'What are you allowed to do?'
  • •Based on roles, permissions, or policies.
  • •Occurs after successful authentication.
  • •Example: An admin user can delete posts, a regular user cannot.
Analogy

Your boarding pass indicating which gates and lounges you can access.

Key Takeaway

Authentication is about identity verification, while authorization is about permission management. Both are crucial for robust web application security.
