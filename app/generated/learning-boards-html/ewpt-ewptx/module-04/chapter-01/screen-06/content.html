Weak & Default Credentials

The easiest entry points for attackers

Weak Credentials

  • •Easily guessable passwords.
  • •Common dictionary words or simple patterns.
  • •Lack of strong password policy enforcement.
Example

password123, qwerty, 123456, yourname.

Default Credentials

  • •Pre-set usernames and passwords.
  • •Often left unchanged in production.
  • •Commonly documented or easily found online.
Example

admin:admin, root:toor, guest:guest.

Key Takeaway

Always enforce strong password policies and ensure all default credentials are changed or removed before deployment to prevent easy account compromise.
