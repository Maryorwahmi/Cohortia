Countermeasures: Rate Limiting & Account Lockout

Protecting against automated credential guessing attacks

Rate Limiting

  • •Restricts number of requests per time frame.
  • •Applies to IP addresses or user accounts.
  • •Slows down automated attacks significantly.
Example

Allowing only 5 login attempts per minute from a single IP address.

Account Lockout

  • •Disables an account after too many failed attempts.
  • •Can be temporary or permanent.
  • •Prevents continuous guessing for a specific user.
Example

Locking an account for 30 minutes after 3 incorrect password entries.

Key Takeaway

Both rate limiting and account lockout are essential server-side defenses that significantly reduce the effectiveness of automated credential guessing attacks.
