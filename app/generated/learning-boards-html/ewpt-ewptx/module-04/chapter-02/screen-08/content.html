Mitigating CSRF with SameSite

Understanding the SameSite Attribute for Cross-Site Request Forgery

`SameSite` Attribute

Controls when cookies are sent with cross-site requests, primarily to defend against CSRF attacks.

CSRF Mitigation

Prevents an attacker from tricking a user's browser into sending authenticated requests to a vulnerable site.

Key Takeaway

The `SameSite` attribute controls cross-site cookie transmission, significantly mitigating CSRF attacks by preventing unauthorized requests from being sent with session cookies.

LaxBalanced Protection

Sends cookies with top-level navigations (e.g., clicking a link) but not with other cross-site requests. Good default.

Good Usability
StrictStrongest Protection

Only sends cookies with requests originating from the same site. Can impact user experience for external links.

Highest Security
NoneNo Protection

Sends cookies with all cross-site requests (requires `Secure` flag). Offers no CSRF defense from `SameSite` itself.
