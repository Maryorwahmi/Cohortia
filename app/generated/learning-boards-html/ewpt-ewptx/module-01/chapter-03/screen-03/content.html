Identify Security-Relevant Headers

Recognize and analyze HTTP headers like CSP, X-Frame-Options, and HSTS. This allows you to spot misconfigurations that could lead to XSS, clickjacking, or insecure transport issues.

06

Assess Real-World Scenarios

Apply your knowledge to practical scenarios, such as analyzing traffic with tools like Burp Suite. This hands-on experience will solidify your understanding of how these protocols behave in real-world web applications.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07

Report Findings Effectively

Formulate clear and concise reports on identified HTTP/HTTPS vulnerabilities. This includes detailing the issue, its impact, and providing actionable recommendations for remediation, a key skill for any pentester.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08

Propose Remediation Strategies

Develop and recommend effective security controls and best practices to mitigate identified HTTP/HTTPS vulnerabilities. This proactive approach ensures you can guide developers towards more secure web application architectures.
