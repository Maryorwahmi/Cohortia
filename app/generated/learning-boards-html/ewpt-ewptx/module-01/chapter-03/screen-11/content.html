HTTPS: Encrypting the Web

The Role of TLS/SSL in Secure Data Exchange

What is HTTPS?

  • •HTTP layered over TLS/SSL (Transport Layer Security/Secure Sockets Layer).
  • •Provides encryption, data integrity, and authentication.
  • •Protects sensitive data from eavesdropping and tampering.
Why it's Critical

Without HTTPS, all web traffic is sent in plain text, making it vulnerable to interception and reading by attackers.

The TLS Handshake

  • •Client and server negotiate encryption parameters.
  • •Server presents its digital certificate for identity verification.
  • •Client verifies the certificate against trusted Certificate Authorities (CAs).
  • •An encrypted tunnel is established for secure communication.
Key Takeaway

The TLS handshake ensures confidentiality and integrity, verifying the server's identity and establishing a secure encrypted channel.

Common Mistake

Not validating certificates or using outdated TLS versions can undermine the security provided by HTTPS.
