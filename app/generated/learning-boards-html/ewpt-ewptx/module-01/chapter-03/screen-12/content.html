Pentesting Encrypted Traffic

Intercepting HTTPS: A Pentester's View

Man-in-the-Middle and Common Misconfigurations

Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) for Pentesters

  • •HTTPS prevents simple eavesdropping.
  • •Use intercepting proxies (e.g., Burp Suite) for controlled MitM.
  • •Allows decryption, inspection, and modification of traffic.
Example: Burp Suite

Configure your browser to proxy traffic through Burp Suite to analyze and manipulate HTTPS requests and responses.

Common HTTPS Misconfigurations

  • •No Certificate Validation: Allows fake certificates.
  • •Outdated TLS Versions: Vulnerable to known cryptographic attacks.
  • •Mixed Content: HTTPS page loads HTTP resources, undermining security.
Key Takeaway

Always ensure all sensitive data is transmitted exclusively over HTTPS, and its implementation is robust, up-to-date, and free from mixed content issues.

Pro-Tip

When testing, look for warnings in the browser about insecure content or certificate errors, as these often point to misconfigurations.
