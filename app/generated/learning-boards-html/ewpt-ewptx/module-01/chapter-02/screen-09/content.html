Module 1›Chapter 2›Intercepting HTTPS: The CA Certificate Challenge
Why the Warning?

Your browser doesn't trust Burp Suite's self-generated certificate, triggering security alerts for HTTPS connections.

Man-in-the-Middle (MitM)

Burp acts as an intermediary, decrypting and re-encrypting traffic, which is a form of MitM attack on your own connection.

Definition: CA Certificate

A digital certificate issued by a Certificate Authority (CA) that verifies identity and enables encrypted communication. Burp generates its own for interception.

The HTTPS Interception Problem

Without a trusted CA certificate, your browser will flag Burp's interception as a security risk.

Key Takeaway

HTTPS interception requires installing Burp Suite's CA certificate in your browser to establish trust and prevent security warnings, enabling full visibility into encrypted traffic.
