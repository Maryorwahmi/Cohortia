Essential Safety Guidelines

Proxy Management

  • Disable When Done: Always turn off browser proxy settings when you finish testing or browse sensitive sites.
  • Scope Awareness: Understand that Burp Suite logs all traffic, so avoid proxying personal data.

Certificate Security

  • Personal Machines: Never install Burp's CA certificate on your primary personal device.
  • Shared Systems: Avoid installing the certificate on any shared computer to prevent potential interception by others.

Lab Isolation

  • Keep Separate: Maintain a clear distinction between your lab environment and your daily computing.
  • Secure Tools: Ensure your virtualization software and Burp Suite instances are properly secured.
Common Mistake

Forgetting to disable your browser's proxy settings after testing can lead to unintended logging of sensitive personal browsing data by Burp Suite.

Key Takeaway

Always practice responsible proxy management and certificate handling, and maintain strict isolation of your lab environment to ensure security and protect personal data.
