Key Concepts

Continuing your knowledge reinforcement.

Burp Suite

An integrated platform for performing security testing of web applications, known for its intercepting proxy.

Intercepting Proxy

A tool that sits between a client and a server, allowing traffic to be viewed, modified, or dropped.

CA Certificate

A digital certificate that verifies identity and enables encrypted communication, generated by Burp for HTTPS interception.

Summary Takeaway

Burp Suite and its intercepting proxy capabilities, along with the understanding of CA certificates, are fundamental for analyzing and manipulating web traffic in a controlled lab environment.
