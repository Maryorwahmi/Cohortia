Distributed Architectures and Safe Testing

Beyond Monoliths: Microservices and Ethical Hacking

Modern applications often use microservices and APIs, creating distributed attack surfaces. Ethical testing requires explicit permission and adherence to legal boundaries.

  • •Microservices and APIs expand the attack surface beyond a single application.
  • •Each component and its communication channels must be assessed for security.
  • •Always obtain explicit permission before conducting any security testing.
Key Takeaway

Comprehensive testing of distributed systems requires a broad scope, and all testing must be authorized and ethical to avoid legal repercussions.

Safety Note

Unauthorized testing is illegal and unethical. Use intentionally vulnerable applications for learning.
