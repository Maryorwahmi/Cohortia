Definition

What is SameSite?

The SameSite cookie attribute is a browser security feature that controls whether cookies are sent with cross-site requests, providing a defense against CSRF.

  • **Browser-Enforced:** Modern browsers implement this attribute.
  • **Cookie Restriction:** Limits when cookies are sent with requests from different origins.
  • **First-Party Context:** Primarily designed to ensure cookies are used in the context of the site that set them.
  • **Default Behavior:** Many browsers now default to SameSite=Lax for cookies without an explicit attribute.
Why it Matters

Mitigating Cross-Site Cookie Sending

By restricting cookie transmission, SameSite directly addresses the core mechanism of CSRF attacks, where an attacker relies on the browser automatically sending session cookies with forged requests.

Key Takeaway

The SameSite cookie attribute is a browser-enforced defense that restricts cookie transmission for cross-site requests, adding a layer of protection against CSRF.
