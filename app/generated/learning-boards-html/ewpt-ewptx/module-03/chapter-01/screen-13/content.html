The Severe Impact of XSS

From session hijacking to malware installation.

Session Hijacking & Data Theft

Attackers can steal session cookies, impersonating victims and gaining unauthorized access to their accounts. They can also extract sensitive user data directly from the page.

Website Defacement & Redirection

Malicious scripts can alter the content of a web page, leading to defacement, or redirect users to phishing sites or other malicious domains.

Keylogging & Malware

Advanced XSS attacks can deploy keyloggers to capture user input or exploit browser vulnerabilities to install malware on the victim's machine.

Ethical Hacking Responsibility

As a penetration tester, understanding these impacts is crucial for responsible vulnerability assessment and reporting. Always operate within legal and ethical boundaries.

Key Takeaway

The consequences of XSS are severe, ranging from data theft and account compromise to defacement and malware, underscoring the need for robust defenses.
