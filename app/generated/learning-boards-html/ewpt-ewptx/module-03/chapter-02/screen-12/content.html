XSS Impact

Beyond Data Theft: Defacement, Redirection, Phishing

Exploring other malicious uses of XSS that impact user trust and site integrity.

Website Defacement

Injecting JavaScript to alter the visual appearance, content, or layout of a web page, damaging reputation.

Malicious Redirection

Forcing the victim's browser to navigate to an attacker-controlled site, potentially hosting malware or phishing pages.

Sophisticated Phishing

Dynamically altering trusted pages with fake login prompts or deceptive messages to steal credentials.

Scenario Example

An XSS payload on a banking site could inject a fake 'session expired' popup, prompting users to re-enter credentials on the compromised page.

Key Takeaway

XSS is a versatile attack vector capable of diverse impacts, from visual defacement to sophisticated social engineering.
