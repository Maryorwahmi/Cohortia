Learning Objectives

Skills you'll gain in this chapter to master XSS exploitation.

Bloom's Taxonomy:LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Craft XSS Payloads

Design diverse XSS payloads to achieve specific attack objectives, such as data exfiltration or browser control. This skill is fundamental for practical exploitation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Bypass XSS Filters

Understand and apply techniques to circumvent common XSS filters and sanitization mechanisms. This includes encoding, obfuscation, and event handler abuse.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Explain Advanced Exploitation

Articulate advanced XSS exploitation techniques, including cookie theft, session hijacking, and keylogging. Understand their mechanisms and impact.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Utilize BeEF

Gain practical experience using the Browser Exploitation Framework (BeEF) for advanced XSS post-exploitation scenarios. This includes hooking browsers and executing modules.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
