The HttpOnly Flag: A Critical Defense

Protecting sensitive cookies from client-side script access, even with XSS.

What is HttpOnly?

A cookie attribute that prevents client-side scripts (like JavaScript) from accessing the cookie's value.

How it Works

The browser enforces this restriction, ensuring that document.cookie cannot read or modify HttpOnly cookies.

Mitigating Cookie Theft

Even if an XSS vulnerability exists, an attacker cannot steal HttpOnly cookies, significantly reducing the risk of session hijacking.

Important Note

HttpOnly does not prevent XSS itself; it only limits the impact by protecting specific cookies. It's a defense-in-depth measure.

Best Practice

Always set the HttpOnly flag on session cookies and other sensitive authentication tokens to enhance security.

Key Takeaway

The HttpOnly flag is a crucial, yet often overlooked, defense against XSS-driven session compromise.
