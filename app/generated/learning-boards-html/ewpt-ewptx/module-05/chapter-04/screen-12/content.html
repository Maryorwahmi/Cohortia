Pitfalls

Avoiding Common Logic Flaw Mistakes

Beyond Technical Vulnerabilities: The Importance of Business Process Review

Over-reliance on Client-Side Validation

Client-side validation is for user experience, not security. It can always be bypassed by an attacker manipulating HTTP requests directly.

Neglecting Business Process Review

Focusing solely on technical vulnerabilities and overlooking a thorough review of the application's core business workflows and rules.

Failing to Consider Edge Cases & Concurrency

Developers often fail to account for unusual inputs, concurrent operations, or unexpected sequences of events, which are prime targets for logic flaw exploitation.

These scenarios can expose vulnerabilities that are not apparent during typical usage.

Key Takeaway

True security against logic flaws comes from deep business process understanding and robust server-side controls, not just client-side checks.
