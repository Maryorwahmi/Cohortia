Advanced Mitigation: Authorization and Testing

Securing Workflows with Least Privilege and Comprehensive Testing

04

Least Privilege

Apply the principle of least privilege to user roles and permissions. Ensure authorization checks are performed at every step of a process, not just at the beginning.

05

Comprehensive Testing

Conduct thorough manual and automated testing, including penetration testing, specifically targeting business logic. Think like a malicious user to break intended workflows.

06

Detailed Logging

Implement detailed logging for all critical business transactions. This helps detect and investigate suspicious activities, providing an invaluable audit trail for incident response.

Key Takeaway

A layered defense combining least privilege, adversarial testing, and robust logging provides comprehensive protection against logic flaws.
