Chapter Introduction

Unmasking Business Logic Flaws

Exploiting the Unintended Paths in Application Workflows

This chapter will equip you with the knowledge to identify and mitigate vulnerabilities that arise from defects in an application's core business processes. You'll learn to think critically about how applications are designed and how their intended logic can be subverted for malicious gain.

Key Idea

Business logic flaws exploit how an application is *supposed* to work, but in ways developers didn't foresee, leading to outcomes that violate business rules.
