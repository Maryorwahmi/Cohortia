Understanding Race Conditions

The Peril of Concurrent Operations and Non-Atomic Logic

What is a Race Condition?

A vulnerability where the outcome of an operation depends on the unpredictable sequence or timing of other uncontrollable events, typically concurrent requests.

It often occurs when multiple threads or processes try to access and modify shared resources simultaneously without proper synchronization.

Banking Example

User has $150. Sends two concurrent requests to transfer $100 each.

  • •Both requests check balance: $150 > $100 (True for both).
  • •Both proceed to debit, leading to two $100 debits.
  • •Final balance: -$50 (overdraft).

This occurs because the balance check and debit operation were not performed atomically.

Key Takeaway

Race conditions exploit timing vulnerabilities in concurrent operations, often leading to financial loss or inconsistent data states.
