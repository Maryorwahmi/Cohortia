Attack Vectors

Categorizing Business Logic Vulnerabilities

Recognizing Patterns in Unintended Application Behavior

Price Manipulation

Altering product prices or shipping costs, often by tampering with client-side parameters that are not re-validated server-side.

Workflow Bypass

Skipping or manipulating steps in a multi-step process (e.g., checkout, password reset) to achieve an unauthorized outcome.

Race Conditions

Exploiting timing issues where the outcome depends on the sequence of concurrent requests, leading to inconsistent states.

State Machine Exploitation

Manipulating the sequence of states an application or object can be in, bypassing intended transitions or re-using one-time actions.

Key Takeaway

Recognizing these common categories helps you anticipate where logic flaws might exist and guides your testing efforts.
