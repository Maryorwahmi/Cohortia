Key Concepts to Remember

Essential Definitions for Business Logic Flaws

Business Logic Flaw

A vulnerability from defects in an application's core business processes, leading to unintended outcomes that violate business rules.

Price Manipulation

Exploiting logic flaws to alter the price of goods or services, often by tampering with client-side parameters not re-validated server-side.

Workflow Bypass

Skipping or manipulating steps in a multi-step process (e.g., checkout, password reset) to achieve an unauthorized outcome.

Race Condition

A vulnerability where the outcome of an operation depends on the sequence or timing of other uncontrollable events, often exploited by sending concurrent requests.

Key Takeaway

Business logic flaws exploit the intended functionality of an application in unintended ways, leading to outcomes that violate business rules.
