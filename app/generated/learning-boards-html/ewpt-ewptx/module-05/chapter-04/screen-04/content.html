Core Concept

Understanding Business Logic Flaws

Exploiting Design Defects, Not Just Coding Errors

Business Logic Flaw

Vulnerabilities stemming from defects in an application's core business processes or workflows. They exploit the *intended* functionality in an unintended way, leading to outcomes that violate business rules.

  • •Focuses on application's workflow and rules.
  • •Often unique to each application.
  • •Requires deep understanding of business model.

Technical Vulnerability

Vulnerabilities arising from coding errors or misconfigurations, such as SQL injection, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), or buffer overflows. They exploit flaws in the underlying technology.

  • •Focuses on code syntax and security mechanisms.
  • •Often has well-defined attack patterns.
  • •Can often be detected by automated scanners.
Key Takeaway

Logic flaws are about *how* an application works, not just *what* code it runs. They require a deeper understanding of business processes to uncover.
