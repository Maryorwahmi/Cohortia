Introduction to Ethernet Switching and VLANs
How switches learn device locations, why flat networks fail, and how VLANs restore control.
In this lesson
Understand MAC address learning, broadcast domains, VLAN benefits, 802.1Q tagging, access vs trunk ports, native VLANs, and Junos commands for basic VLAN configuration.
Coming up
A CLI-focused walkthrough configures VLANs, assigns access and trunk ports, and shows verification commands used on EX switches.
Key takeaway

Logical segmentation with VLANs reduces broadcast scope and lets you group devices by role for security and manageability.

Visual concept
Cards show MAC learning, VLANs, and trunks.
MAC Learning
Switch records source MAC and port, then forwards frames only to that port when known.
VLAN
Logical broadcast domain. Devices in same VLAN communicate without routing.
Trunk
Carries multiple VLANs between switches using 802.1Q tags.
Native VLAN
One VLAN may be sent untagged; mismatches cause issues.
