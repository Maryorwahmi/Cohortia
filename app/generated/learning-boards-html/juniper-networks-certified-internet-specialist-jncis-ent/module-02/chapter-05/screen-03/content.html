Lab verification checklist
  • Show secure-access-port to confirm mac-limit and static MAC entries
  • Show dhcp-snooping binding to verify IP-to-MAC bindings
  • Test ARP inspection by checking arp-inspection logs and ensuring expected ARP remains allowed
Common pitfalls
Missing trusted uplink: DHCP offers are blocked if the DHCP server port is not trusted.
Too low mac-limit: Hypervisors, phones with PCs, or stack links may require higher limits.
Enabling DAI without DHCP snooping: Legitimate ARP can be dropped if the binding database is empty.
