Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › What to Remember
Trusted uplinks
Ensure DHCP server uplinks are marked trusted or legitimate offers will be blocked.
mac-limit planning
Inventory port use before lowering mac-limit; increase limits for hypervisors and multi-device desks.
Verify bindings
Confirm show dhcp-snooping binding entries exist before enabling ARP inspection enforcement.
Final takeaway

Layer 2 protections are effective when configured thoughtfully and verified. Use a lab-first approach and clear recovery plans.
