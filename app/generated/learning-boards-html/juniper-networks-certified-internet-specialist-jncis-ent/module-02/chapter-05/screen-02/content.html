Mnemonic

Think: Limit, Trust, Verify. Limit devices on a port, mark trusted uplinks, verify IP/MAC bindings, then validate ARP.

Why this matters

Layer 2 features prevent fast, low-effort attacks that can compromise many hosts. These objectives focus on safe, verifiable steps to deploy protection without causing outages.
