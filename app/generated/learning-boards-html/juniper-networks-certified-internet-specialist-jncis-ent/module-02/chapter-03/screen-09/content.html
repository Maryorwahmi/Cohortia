Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Timers & Convergence
Timer roles
Hello Time: interval between BPDU transmissions (commonly 2s).
Max Age: how long root info is considered valid (commonly 20s).
Forward Delay: time spent in Listening and Learning (commonly 15s each).
Why they slow convergence
These timers ensure stability across devices that may miss brief BPDUs, but they also make recovery slow after an actual link failure. Design must balance stability and recovery speed.
Practical decision
If downtime from a link failure must be minimal, prefer RSTP which provides faster transitions. If transient loops from brief link flaps are a concern, carefully tuned timers and STP behavior can be safer.
