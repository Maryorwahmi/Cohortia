Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Objectives — Practical
What you will verify
  • Define VLANs and confirm trunk membership with show vlans.
  • Create irb units and assign IP addresses inside the VLAN subnet.
  • Associate l3-interface under the VLAN to link RVI and VLAN.
  • Check show route for direct routes to each VLAN subnet.
  • Test connectivity from hosts and check firewall filters on irb if traffic is blocked.
Verification Tools
Learn to use show interfaces irb, show vlans, and show route as your primary verification commands during and after configuration.
