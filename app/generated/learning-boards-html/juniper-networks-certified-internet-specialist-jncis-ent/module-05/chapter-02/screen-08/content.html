Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Configuring area-range
Configuration

Apply area-range on the origin area

Place the area-range under the protocols ospf area stanza for the area whose prefixes you want to summarize.

Example
# On the ABR, summarizing Area 1 prefixes into Area 0
set protocols ospf area 0.0.0.1 area-range 172.16.0.0/22
This instructs the ABR to advertise 172.16.0.0/22 into Area 0 instead of the multiple /24s originated in Area 1.
Verification
# Verify summary LSA on ABR
show ospf database summary
# Verify remote router sees the summary
show route protocol ospf
Placement pitfall
Do not configure area-range under the wrong area. If prefixes originate in Area 1, place area-range under Area 1 config on the ABR.
