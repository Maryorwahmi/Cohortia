Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Discard Route
Definition

Discard route for summaries

Junos creates a route to null0 for a configured area-range to prevent misforwarding of traffic for absent component prefixes.

Why it matters
Prevents accidental forwarding and potential loops when a summary would cover addresses not present in the area.
Pitfall
If a valid host prefix lies within the summary but is missing in the origin area, the discard route will drop packets instead of delivering them. Plan summaries carefully.
Inspecting discard behavior
# On ABR
show route 192.168.8.0/21   # shows summary and next-hop (discard/null0) if configured
Design guideline
Before committing a summary, ensure component prefixes cover all required destinations or accept the discard behavior for missing addresses.
