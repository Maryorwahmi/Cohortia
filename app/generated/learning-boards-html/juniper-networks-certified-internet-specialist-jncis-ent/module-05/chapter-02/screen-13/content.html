Scenario

Summarize Area 1 into Area 0

Follow the steps below on R1, R2, and R3 and use verification commands to confirm the summary is seen on R3.

Step 1
Configure R1 interfaces in Area 0 and Area 1 and enable OSPF.
Step 2
On R2, add loopbacks to simulate multiple /24 prefixes in Area 1.
Step 3
On R1, under Area 1 configure area-range with the proper summary mask.
Step 4
Verify ospf neighbors on all routers.
Step 5
Verify R3 routing table shows the summary and not individual /24s.
Reflection

If a summary hides needed detail, consider keeping per-prefix advertisements or using more-specific summaries in parts of the network.
