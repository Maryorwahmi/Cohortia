Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Common Mistakes
Mistake

Wrong area placement

area-range must be configured under the area where the prefixes originate. Placing it under a different area has no effect on those routes.

Non-contiguous summary
Selecting a summary that spans addresses not present results in discard behavior or unexpected routing holes.
Mistake

Overly-broad summary

A too-wide summary hides per-prefix detail that might be necessary for path selection or troubleshooting.

Detection
Use show route and show ospf database to see what is being advertised and whether the summary is in effect on remote routers.
Fixing mistakes

Reassign area-range to the correct area stanza, recalculate the proper summary mask to match contiguous prefixes, and verify with show route on remote routers to ensure expected behavior.
