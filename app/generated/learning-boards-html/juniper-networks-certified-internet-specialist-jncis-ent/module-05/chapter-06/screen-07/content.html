Process Flow › monitor traffic
Live capture example
monitor traffic interface ge-0/0/0.0 no-resolve layer-2-headers matching "ospf"
Look for hello source addresses, authentication info, DBD flags and sequence numbers, and LSA headers to verify correct exchanges.
When to use
  • Intermittent adjacency flaps that don't show a clear pattern in logs.
  • DBD exchange failures such as ExStart/Exchange that imply MTU or master/slave issues.
  • Confirming that hellos carry the expected parameters like area and auth.
Misconception
Seeing packets on the wire does not prove the neighbor accepted them; always cross-check with show ospf neighbor and LSDB contents.
Key Takeaway

Use monitor traffic to corroborate what is sent and what is received; correlate captures with logs for a complete timeline.
