Learning Objectives
Skills to verify competency in OSPF troubleshooting and optimization.
01
Adjacency Diagnosis
Spot and resolve mismatched parameters that prevent Full state.
Understand area ID, auth, timers, MTU and network-type mismatches. Mnemonic: MATCH — Match Area, Timers, Auth, Cost, Host MTU.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Junos Show Commands
Use show ospf neighbor, interface detail, database, and route to confirm state.
Mnemonic: NIDR — Neighbor, Interface, Database, Routes. Use these to build your diagnosis timeline.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Controlled Debugging
Configure traceoptions and use monitor traffic to capture OSPF events safely.
Remember: enable briefly, collect logs, then remove. Mnemonic: CAPTURE — Configure, Analyze, Purge traces.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Optimization Settings
Adjust cost, priority, and timers consistently across the domain.
Mnemonic: CPT — Cost, Priority, Timers. Ensure reference-bandwidth consistency.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
