Process Flow › OSPF Timers
Timer tuning example
set protocols ospf area 0.0.0.0 interface ge-0/0/0.0 hello-interval 5
set protocols ospf area 0.0.0.0 interface ge-0/0/0.0 dead-interval 20
Tuning reduces detection time but increases control traffic and potential SPF runs; monitor resource usage after change.
When to tune
  • When application-level SLAs require faster detection and failover.
  • On stable links where increased hello rates are acceptable.
  • Not recommended across unstable or lossy links where flaps could amplify problems.
Misconception
Faster timers are always better; in reality they increase control-plane load and LSA churn and must be tested carefully.
Key Takeaway

Tune hello and dead intervals selectively, monitor CPU and show ospf statistics for increased LSA or SPF activity after changes.
