Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Redistribution Pitfalls
Common pitfalls
  • Incorrect or missing policy: Too broad or not applied; verify policy and export statement.
  • Wrong metric-type: Default E2 may hide internal cost and cause non-optimal ASBR selection when multiples exist.
  • Redistribution loops: Bi-directional redistribution without tags can re-inject routes back into the source protocol.
  • ASBR placement in stub areas: Stub and totally stubby areas cannot contain ASBRs because Type 5 LSAs are blocked.
Prevention checklist
  1. Create precise routing-policy to match only intended routes.
  2. Use metric tagging and consistent seed metrics across ASBRs when required.
  3. Apply route tags and filters for multi-protocol redistribution to prevent loops.
Watch out

Simple omissions like forgetting to apply the export policy are frequent and easy to test for during verification steps.
