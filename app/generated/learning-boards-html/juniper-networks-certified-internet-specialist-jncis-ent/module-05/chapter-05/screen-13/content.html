Assessment Idea › Troubleshooting
Sample assessment questions
Q1
How do you match routes with a BGP community before redistributing into OSPF? Define the community, use from community in a policy term, then apply the policy as an export.
Q2
When is PBR preferable to OSPF path selection? When you need to steer specific flows—like guest or voice traffic—based on source, port, or protocol, not just destination metrics.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • Confirm policy terms and order; ensure an accept exists for intended routes.
  • Check filter attachment and exact match conditions for PBR.
  • Use monitor traffic and show route forwarding-table to confirm behavior.
  • Replicate in lab before production changes.
