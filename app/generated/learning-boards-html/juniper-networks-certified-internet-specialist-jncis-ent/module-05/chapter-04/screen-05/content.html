Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Requirements & Restrictions
Checklist

Validate these before you configure

  1. Two ABRs that will form the virtual link and share a transit area.
  2. Transit area is a standard area — not stub, totally stubby, or NSSA.
  3. At least one ABR has a direct physical connection to Area 0.
  4. Router IDs for both ABRs are known and unique.
  5. Transit area carries Type 3 LSAs reliably—no filtering or blocking.
Common Mistake

Attempting to create a virtual link through a stub or NSSA transit area will prevent necessary LSAs from crossing and the link will fail.

Process

Step-by-step validation

Confirm ABR roles
Verify each candidate is advertising multiple areas and has proper router-id set.
Check area type
Ensure the transit area is standard and allows Type 3 LSA propagation.
Verify connectivity
Confirm IP reachability and neighbor formation across the transit area before creating the virtual link.
Document router IDs
Use explicit router IDs in the virtual-link neighbor-id setting to avoid ambiguity.
Key Takeaway

A quick pre-check avoids broken virtual links: ABR roles, standard transit, router IDs, and reachable LSAs are essential.
