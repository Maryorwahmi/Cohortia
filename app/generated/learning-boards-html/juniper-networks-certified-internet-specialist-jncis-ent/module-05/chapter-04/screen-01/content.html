Concept map
Virtual link + Authentication
Virtual Link
Logical OSPF connection between ABRs across a transit area to extend Area 0 reachability.
Transit Area
Must be a standard area; cannot be a stub, NSSA, or totally stubby area.
Authentication
Use MD5 or SHA for integrity and origin verification; avoid plain text in production.
