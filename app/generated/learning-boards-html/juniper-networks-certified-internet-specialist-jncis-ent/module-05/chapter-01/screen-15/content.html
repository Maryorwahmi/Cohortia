What to Remember
Multi-Area OSPF
Divide an AS into areas to localize LSA flooding and SPF work for better scalability and convergence.
Backbone Area (Area 0)
All non-backbone areas must connect to Area 0 to ensure loop-free inter-area routing and consistent path selection.
Standard Area
Default area type that carries LSAs 1–5 and optionally 7, useful for cores but costlier for LSDB size.
Summary Takeaway

Design areas to contain change and assign area types according to each site's need for external and inter-area visibility.
