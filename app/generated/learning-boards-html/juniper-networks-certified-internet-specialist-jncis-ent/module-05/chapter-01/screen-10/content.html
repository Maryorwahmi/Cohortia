Process Flow ABR and ASBR roles
ABR responsibilities
  • Maintain separate LSDBs for each connected area.
  • Advertise inter-area routes as Type 3 LSAs into connected areas.
  • Translate Type 7 LSAs to Type 5 when exporting NSSA-originated externals to Area 0.
Operational tip
Review LSDBs on ABRs to confirm summarization and translation behavior after configuration changes.
ASBR responsibilities
The ASBR injects external routes into OSPF. In standard areas it originates Type 5 LSAs; in NSSAs it originates Type 7 LSAs.
Concrete example
R5 in Area 0.0.0.3 is an ASBR in the lab scenario and redistributes a static route as Type 7 LSA in the NSSA.
Misconception
Assuming ABRs simply proxy LSAs without maintaining per-area LSDBs is incorrect; ABRs maintain distinct databases and perform translation and summarization actions.
