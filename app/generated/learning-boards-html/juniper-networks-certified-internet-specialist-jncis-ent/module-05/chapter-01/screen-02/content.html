Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Learning Objectives
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain why multi-area OSPF is necessary
Describe how segmentation reduces LSDB size and limits SPF recalculation to affected areas, improving convergence and CPU usage on routers.
Mnemonic: 'Segment to Scale' — think areas as compartments
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate OSPF area types
Identify which LSAs each area permits or blocks and the practical implications for routing tables and reachability.
Mnemonic: LSA matrix — remember 3,4,5,7 behaviors
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify characteristics and restrictions
Know where ASBRs are allowed, how defaults are injected, and what happens when area types mismatch among routers.
Mnemonic: 'Rules first' — area types must match or adjacencies fail
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Configure area types in Junos OS
Use the appropriate area statements such as stub, stub no-summaries, nssa, and nssa no-summaries to enforce filtering on ABRs.
Mnemonic: 'set protocols ospf area' sequence for area type
