Match Conditions — What they control and why they matter

You will learn how each 'from' match filters routes, when to use prefix-list vs route-filter, and how protocol, AS path, and community matches give you operational control.

In this lesson
Precision filters, prefix tools, AS path regex
Concept map
prefix-list
Exact named prefixes for reuse across policies.
route-filter
Range and length-based operators like orlonger and prefix-length-range.
AS-path & community
Regex matching and tagged route attributes for granular control.
Quick note
Remember: conditions inside a single 'from' block are ANDed. Use separate terms to express OR logic.
Key Takeaway

Choose prefix-list for exact matches and route-filter for ranges. Plan your terms to avoid accidental ANDing of unrelated conditions.
