Setting metric (MED)
A lower MED is preferred by external ASes. Use for multi-homed entry selection.
set policy-options policy-statement MODIFY_MED term T1 from protocol bgp
set policy-options policy-statement MODIFY_MED term T1 then metric 100
set policy-options policy-statement MODIFY_MED term T1 then accept
Verify with show route protocol bgp detail and look for the MED or metric field in the route attributes.
Example scenario
You operate two peering links to the same provider. Set a lower MED on the preferred peering to nudge the provider to prefer that entry point into your AS.
Misconception
Believing MED always wins: peers may ignore or reweight MED, or apply route-flap damping. MED is not as decisive as AS path length or local-preference in BGP selection.
Key Takeaway

Use metric to advise remote peers about preferred entry. Confirm with route detail and coordinate with your peer for predictable results.
