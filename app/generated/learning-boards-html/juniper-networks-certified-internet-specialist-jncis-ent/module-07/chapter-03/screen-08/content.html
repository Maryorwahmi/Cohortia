Set local-preference
Raise the local-preference to steer inbound choice of exit from your AS.
set policy-options policy-statement SET_LOCAL_PREF term T1 from neighbor 203.0.113.1
set policy-options policy-statement SET_LOCAL_PREF term T1 then local-preference 300
set policy-options policy-statement SET_LOCAL_PREF term T1 then accept
Verify with show route protocol bgp detail and look for 'Local preference: 300' in the route details for routes learned from that neighbor.
Example scenario
If ISP A is a preferred exit for most traffic, set a higher local-preference for routes learned from ISP A to bias egress through that provider.
Misconception: local-preference changes how other ASes prefer your advertised routes. It does not. Local-pref is internal to your AS for selecting exit.
Key Takeaway

Use local-preference for internal egress control; it is a strong and reliable lever in the BGP decision process.
