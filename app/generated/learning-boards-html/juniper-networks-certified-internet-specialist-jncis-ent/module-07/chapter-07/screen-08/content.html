Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Control Plane Protection (CPP)
Why lo0 matters

Packets destined for router IPs are processed by the CPU. A lo0 input filter lets you control which of those packets reach the control plane regardless of ingress interface.

Best practices
  • Permit SSH from specific management prefixes only.
  • Permit BGP only from defined peer IPs and OSPF protocol as necessary.
  • Include a discard with log term to audit blocked attempts.
Safety and testing

Maintain console or out-of-band access and test filters incrementally so you never lock out management during deployment.

Key Takeaway

Use lo0 input filters to centralize control plane defense, permit only necessary sources and protocols, and keep recovery options available.
