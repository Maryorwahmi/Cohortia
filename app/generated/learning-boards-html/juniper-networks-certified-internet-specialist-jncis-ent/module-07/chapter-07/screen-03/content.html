Applied skills and safe practices

Practice creating filters that permit known management and protocol traffic and log or discard other traffic.

Skill
Compose accept terms

Define terms for SSH, BGP peers, and OSPF as needed and place a discard term last for unmatched traffic.

Skill
Chain filters safely

Understand that in chained filters the first terminating action stops further evaluation.

Skill
Verify matches and logs

Use counters and monitor traffic to confirm which terms match and whether logging shows dropped packets.

Safety note

When applying filters to lo0 or management interfaces, keep a console or out-of-band connection available in case you lock yourself out.
