Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Verifying filters and counters
Key verification commands
  • show firewall filter <filter-name> counter — term hit counts.
  • show interfaces <interface> detail — which filters are attached and direction.
  • monitor traffic interface <interface> detail — live packet capture.
  • show log messages — see entries from log actions in filter terms.
Workflow

Capture, test traffic from known hosts, then check counters and logs to identify mismatches between packets and filter terms.

Common troubleshooting discovery

If counters for accept terms remain zero while packets arrive, check source prefixes, ports, and protocol matches — often a small mismatch in term criteria is the cause.

Key Takeaway

Measure first. Use counters and captures to locate mismatches, then adjust terms and re-verify incrementally.
