Applying Firewall Filters

Understand input vs output, control plane protection on lo0, chaining, and verification commands.

Duration: ~15 minutes content
Why this chapter matters

A correctly applied filter prevents unwanted traffic from reaching internal networks or the router CPU. Misplaced filters can drop management access or miss traffic if applied on the wrong interface.

In this lesson

Apply filters to logical interfaces and choose input or output to control packet inspection timing.

Coming up

Protect lo0 for control plane protection and verify with counters and traffic monitoring.
