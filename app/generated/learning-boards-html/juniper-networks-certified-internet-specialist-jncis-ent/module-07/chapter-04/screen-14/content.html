Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Key Concepts
Import Policy
Filters or modifies routes received before installation into the routing table.
Export Policy
Controls which local routes are advertised or redistributed to peers or other protocols.
Policy Chaining
Sequence multiple policies; use next-policy to continue evaluation when desired.
Summary
Remember application point, design modular policies, and verify with receive and advertising route outputs to ensure your intent matches reality.
