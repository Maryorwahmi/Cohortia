Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Common Mistakes
Frequent errors
  • Using import where export was intended or the reverse.
  • Incorrect chaining: missing next-policy causes unexpected stops.
  • Relying on implicit deny rather than explicit accept/reject.
  • Not running soft-in/soft-out after policy changes.
  • Typographical mistakes in policy or prefix-list names.
Troubleshooting checklist
1. show policy-options to check configuration.
2. show route receive-protocol bgp <neighbor> to see what arrived.
3. show route protocol bgp <prefix> detail to inspect attributes after import.
4. show route advertising-protocol bgp <neighbor> to view outbound advertising.
5. soft-in/soft-out or clear the session if routes are not re-evaluated.
