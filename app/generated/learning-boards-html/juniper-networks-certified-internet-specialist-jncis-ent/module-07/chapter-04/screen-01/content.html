Applying Routing Policies
Understand import vs export, where to attach policies in Junos, and how to verify their effect across BGP, OSPF and static routes.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Orientation

Policies must be applied where routing information enters or leaves: import controls received routes; export controls advertised routes. Knowing the application point prevents common mistakes.

Why it matters

Incorrect application can silently block routes or advertise unwanted prefixes. Verification commands let you confirm whether changes took effect as intended.

Concept connections
Import
Filters or modifies routes received from a neighbor before installation.
Export
Controls which local routes are advertised to peers or redistributed into other protocols.
Chaining
Sequence multiple policies so each handles a focused task, using next-policy to continue evaluation.
Explore Key Topics
BGP application
Apply import/export per neighbor or group to control inbound/outbound prefixes.
OSPF / RIP export
Use export policies to control redistribution from direct, static, or BGP into OSPF or RIP.
Verify & troubleshoot
Use show route receive-protocol and show route advertising-protocol to compare before and after policy effects.
