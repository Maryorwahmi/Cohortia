Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Operational Reminders
Inspect policies
Use show policy-options to verify terms, matches, and actions before trusting behavior.
Compare routes
Compare receive-protocol, installed protocol output, and advertising-protocol to find where routes changed.
Refresh sessions
Use soft-in/soft-out to re-evaluate policies without a full session tear-down when possible.
Takeaway
A repeatable process of inspect, compare, and refresh makes policy changes predictable and reduces the risk of routing issues.
