Common mistakes and fixes
These errors explain why expected routes sometimes disappear after policy changes.
  • Term order: Put specific rejects or matches before broad accepts to ensure intended logic.
  • Missing accept: A matched term without accept will not permit the route; add explicit accept.
  • Too-broad match: Narrow from conditions to avoid unintended matches.
  • Not referenced: Verify the policy is applied to the protocol or import/export point.
Quick fixes
Reorder terms, add explicit accept, tighten from conditions, and confirm the policy reference in protocol configuration.
Verify
Use test prefixes and show commands to confirm behavior after each change rather than making multiple changes at once.
