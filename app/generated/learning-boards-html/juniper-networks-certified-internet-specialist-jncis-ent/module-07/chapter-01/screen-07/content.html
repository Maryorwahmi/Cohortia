Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › From vs Then
From — Match conditions
Use protocol, prefix-list, AS path, or community to identify routes precisely before acting on them.
  • protocol: identify OSPF, BGP, static, etc.
  • prefix-list: match specific networks you care about
  • as-path: filter routes from particular autonomous systems
  • community: group or tag routes for policy selection
Then — Actions
Accept, reject, or modify attributes. Actions determine whether the route is installed or propagated and how it appears to others.
Accept
Route is permitted and processed further.
Reject
Route is explicitly denied and dropped by the policy.
Modify
Change metric, local preference, or community before accepting or rejecting.
Practical tip
Design from conditions to be as specific as needed. Put attribute changes in then, and always include explicit accept where you want the route to pass.
