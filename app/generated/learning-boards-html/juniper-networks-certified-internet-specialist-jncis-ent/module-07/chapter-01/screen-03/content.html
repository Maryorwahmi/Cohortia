Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Learning Objectives
Practical verification & troubleshooting
Create, apply, and verify policies; avoid implicit deny surprises.
Practice
Build a prefix-list, reference it in a term, and accept those prefixes. Then check the route display to confirm acceptance.
Troubleshoot
If expected routes are missing, verify term order and look for missing explicit accept actions; implicit deny may be the cause.
Lab tip
Use a dummy static route or an import point for OSPF into BGP to see the policy effect in isolation before production changes.
Common mistake
Placing a broad accept term before a specific reject will prevent the reject from ever matching. Order terms from most specific to least specific.
Verification
Use show policy-options and show route protocol commands to confirm that routes match and are accepted where intended.
