Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Key Concepts
Routing Policy
A named set of terms that controls route acceptance, modification, and advertisement.
Term Evaluation
Terms evaluated in order; the first match applies its then actions and stops evaluation.
Implicit Deny
If no term explicitly accepts a route, the route is rejected by default at the end of the policy.
Summary takeaway

Design policies with explicit accepts, ordered terms, and reusable match lists. Verify in a lab and confirm policy references where applied.
