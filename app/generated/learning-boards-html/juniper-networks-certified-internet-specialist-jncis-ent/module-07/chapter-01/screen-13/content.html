Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Assessment Ideas
Sample assessment prompts
Use prediction questions, small config tasks, and verification checks to evaluate understanding.
  1. Given three ordered terms, predict which term handles a route learned by a specific protocol and justify your answer.
  2. Create a policy that accepts prefixes in a prefix-list and implicitly denies all others; demonstrate verification commands.
  3. Explain a troubleshooting plan when a route expected to pass is missing after applying a policy.
Scoring guidance
Look for correct reasoning about term order, explicit accept usage, and ability to verify with show commands in a lab environment.
