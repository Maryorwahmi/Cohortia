Match Conditions & Actions
Configure precise packet matches with 'from' statements and choose 'then' actions to permit, drop, log, count, or rate-limit traffic.
In this lesson
You will inspect the types of 'from' matches, learn how conditions are ANDed, and explore the operational meaning of accept, discard, and reject. We'll ground the ideas with a working filter example that combines match conditions with policer, count, and log actions.

Filter Anatomy
Term order matters. Specific deny or protect terms come before broad accept rules. Combine many match fields to narrow a hit, and then choose the right action for security and observability.
