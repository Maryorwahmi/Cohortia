Accept
Permit the packet
Permit the packet to continue processing. Use when traffic should reach its destination.
Discard
Silently drop
Drop with no response to the sender. Good for stealthy security drops to avoid revealing policy to an attacker.
Reject
Drop + ICMP feedback
Drop the packet and send an ICMP unreachable. Useful for debugging but it exposes information about your network.
Combine these actions with observability actions like log and count. For example, a term can both log and discard to record attempts without notifying the sender. Decide based on whether you prioritize stealth or diagnosability.
Key Takeaway

Choose discard to conceal drops and reject to provide feedback; combine either with log or count for visibility.
