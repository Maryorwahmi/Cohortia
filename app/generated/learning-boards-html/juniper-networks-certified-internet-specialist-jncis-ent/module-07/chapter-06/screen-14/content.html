Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Actions & Observability
Accept / Discard / Reject
Pick discard to hide blocks, reject to inform, and accept to permit continuation.
Count & Log
Measure hits and create syslog entries before you enforce stricter actions.
Policer
Use policers with sensible if-exceeding limits to rate-limit abusive flows like SSH scans.
Final takeaway

Order rules thoughtfully, observe first with count/log, then enforce with policers or discard once thresholds are validated.
