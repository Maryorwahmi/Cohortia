Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Verification & Pitfalls
Verify filters and avoid common mistakes
Learn practical checks and correct common configuration errors.
Check
Counters & logs
Use counters and syslog to confirm how often terms match. 'show firewall filter' displays named counters you incremented with 'count'.
Pitfall
Ports without protocol
A destination-port alone won't behave as intended. Always pair ports with the appropriate protocol, for example 'protocol tcp' when matching TCP ports.
Pitfall
Term ordering
Terms are evaluated in order. A broad accept placed too early will bypass later protective terms — specific protections should precede general allows.
