Operational Pattern
Log & Count, then Enforce

1) Add count and log to suspect terms to collect data.

2) Analyze counters and logs to determine normal rates and sources.

3) Tune policers with realistic thresholds and test again.

4) Convert monitoring terms to enforcement once safe.

Example: attach count SSH_MGMT_ACCESS and log to management SSH term. Observe 'show firewall filter' counters and inspect system logs before enabling a policer that discards above 1 Mbps.
Key Takeaway

Measuring before enforcing minimizes the risk of blocking legitimate traffic and gives you data-driven policer settings.
