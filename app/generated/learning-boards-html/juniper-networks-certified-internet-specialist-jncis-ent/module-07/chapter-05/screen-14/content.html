Key Concepts
Firewall Filter
A stateless set of packet-matching rules that control data-plane traffic.
Family
Specifies inet, inet6, or ethernet-switching — choose the correct domain.
Terms
Ordered blocks with from conditions and then actions; first match wins.
Actions
accept, discard, reject, count, log — choose per intent and test first.
Implicit Discard
Default behavior: anything not explicitly accepted is dropped.
Stateless
Filters do not track connection state; allow both directions when needed.
Summary: Use filters to protect devices and traffic by writing specific, ordered terms and validating behavior with counts and logs before enforcing drops.
