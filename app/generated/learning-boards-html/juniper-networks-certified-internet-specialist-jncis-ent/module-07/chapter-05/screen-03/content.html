Practice & Safety Checks
Habits that prevent outages when deploying filters.
Predict filter effects
Trace a packet through terms and confirm whether a matching term will trigger accept or discard.
Mnemonic: trace path
Order terms carefully
Place specific matches before broad ones to avoid earlier matches shadowing later rules.
Mnemonic: specific before general
Use logging and counts for testing
Temporarily log or count matches to verify behavior before converting to discard actions.
Mnemonic: observe then act
Avoid control-plane blocks
Do not apply restrictive filters to loopback or control interfaces without careful verification.
Mnemonic: protect management
