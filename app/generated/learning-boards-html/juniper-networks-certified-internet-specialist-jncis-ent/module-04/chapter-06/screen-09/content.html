Configuring Stub Router and Duration
Example and verification guidance.
Junos Stub Example
edit protocols ospf
set stub-router
commit
You may also configure duration or conditional activation depending on platform and options supported by Junos.
Verify Behavior
  • show ospf database detail — check for LSAs with maximum metric.
  • show route protocol ospf <prefix> detail — confirm alternate path selection.
  • monitor protocol ospf — watch for LSA floods and adjacency impact during the change.
Key Takeaway

Use stub-router for planned maintenance or service nodes, but always confirm alternate paths exist before inflating link metrics.
