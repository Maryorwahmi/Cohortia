Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Verification
Expected show outputs
After virtual link is configured and active:
show ospf virtual-link
Virtual Link to 3.3.3.3 is Up
   Transit Area: 1.0.0.0
   State: Full
After summarization on R3, R1's routing table:
show route protocol ospf
192.168.10.0/23   *[OSPF/30]  via 10.0.x.x, 
   (summary from Area 2)
Troubleshoot checklist
  • Is the virtual-link neighbor in Full state?
  • Are Router IDs reachable via intra-area routes?
  • Is the transit area a standard area?
  • Are authentication settings consistent if used?
Validation tip
Ping R4 loopback from R1 with source 1.1.1.1 to confirm end-to-end reachability once virtual link and summary are active.
