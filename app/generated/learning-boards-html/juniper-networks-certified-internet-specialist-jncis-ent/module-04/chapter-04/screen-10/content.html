Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › When to use virtual links
Appropriate uses
  • Temporary reconnection after topology change
  • Lab testing and staged migrations
  • Emergency fixes until restructuring is completed
Pro Tip
Plan alerts on show ospf virtual-link output to detect flapping transit paths quickly.
When to avoid
  • As a permanent design for production backbones
  • When the transit area is stubbed or unstable
  • When readdressing or adding a direct backbone link is feasible
Risk
Increased troubleshooting complexity and dependency on transit area stability.
Reflection
If you can modify addressing or attach the area directly to Area 0, prefer those solutions over virtual links.
