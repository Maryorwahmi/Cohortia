OSPF Summarization & Virtual Links
How ABRs summarize inter-area routes and how virtual links can temporarily reconnect a detached area to Area 0.
In this lesson
Configure area-range on ABRs, form virtual links using Router IDs and transit areas, and verify with show commands.
Why it matters
Smaller routing tables mean faster SPF and more stable large-scale OSPF deployments; virtual links help restore backbone adjacency when topology constraints prevent direct Area 0 attachment.
