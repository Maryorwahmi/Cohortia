Common mistakes
Why adjacencies fail
  • Mismatched Hello/Dead timers: must be identical to form adjacencies.
  • Network-type mismatch: point-to-point vs broadcast mismatches break negotiation.
  • Different area IDs: interfaces must belong to the same area.
  • Authentication errors: missing or mismatched keys prevent adjacency.
  • ACL/Firewall blocks: OSPF multicast may be blocked between routers.
Practical checklist
  1. Confirm interface IPs and masks are correct.
  2. Compare hello/dead timers on both ends.
  3. Verify network-type and area ID match.
  4. Check for ACLs blocking 224.0.0.5/6 or UDP if used.
Example: Two routers showing Init likely indicate one-way Hello reception. Check multicast reachability, timer mismatches, and whether the neighbor list in the Hello contains your Router ID.
Key takeaway
Start with parameter comparison and multicast reachability; most adjacency problems are resolved by correcting a single mismatch.
