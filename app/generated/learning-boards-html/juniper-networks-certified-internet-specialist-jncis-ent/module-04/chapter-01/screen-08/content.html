OSPF Packet Types and Roles
Each packet type plays a specific role in discovery, database exchange, or reliability.
Hello
Discover neighbors, advertise parameters and neighbor lists; required for reaching Two-Way state.
DBD / DD
Exchange summaries of LSDB contents to detect missing LSAs during adjacency formation.
LSR
Request specific LSAs that are missing after reviewing DBD summaries.
LSU
Carry the full LSA payloads in response to requests or to flood topology changes.
LSAck
Acknowledge receipt of LSU packets to ensure reliable database synchronization.
Sequence example
Hello → DBD exchange → LSR requests → LSU reply → LSAck confirmations. This sequence moves neighbors to Full and synchronizes LSDBs.
