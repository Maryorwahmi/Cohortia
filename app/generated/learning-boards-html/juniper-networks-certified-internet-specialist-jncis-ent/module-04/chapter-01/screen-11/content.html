Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Passive Interfaces
Process
Use passive to advertise without adjacencies
Passive interfaces advertise the connected subnet into OSPF while preventing Hello packets and neighbor formation on that interface. This is perfect for access networks and segments with end devices.
Junos example
set protocols ospf area 0.0.0.0 interface ge-0/0/2.0 passive
Why it matters
Reduces unnecessary OSPF traffic on LANs, avoids accidental adjacencies with end hosts, and preserves advertisement of the subnet into OSPF.
Misuse
Do not make router-facing links passive; that will prevent expected neighbor adjacencies and break routing between routers.
Key takeaway
Passive is an advertisement control, not an advertisement blocker. Use it to expose subnets without creating neighbors.
