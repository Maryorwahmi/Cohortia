Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Verification
Useful commands
show route protocol static
show ospf database external
show route protocol ospf
show route protocol ospf detail 172.16.0.0/24
Interpretation tips
  • On the ASBR, confirm the static route exists before redistribution.
  • In the LSDB, confirm the seed metric and metric-type fields on the external LSA.
  • On receiving routers, find O E1 or O E2 and use detail output to see ASBR information and metric calculation.
Troubleshoot checklist
If routes don't appear: check policy match, export placement, ASBR reachability to external next-hop, and commit history.
Example output
show route protocol ospf
172.16.0.0/24      O E1  [ 20/ 21 ] via 10.0.0.1, 00:05:23
What to look for
The O E1 label and the bracketed metric show how the external seed (20) and the internal cost (1) were combined.
