Behavior

External Type 1 (E1)

With E1, OSPF computes cost by adding the external seed metric and the internal OSPF cost to reach the ASBR. This favors closer ASBRs when deciding an exit path.

Concrete scenario
ASBR-A seed 10, internal cost 1 => total 11. ASBR-B seed 5, internal cost 10 => total 15. E1 prefers ASBR-A because total 11<15.
When to choose E1
Use E1 for shortest-path exit
E1 is preferred when you want OSPF to pick the nearest ASBR rather than relying only on seed metrics.
Misconception
Do not assume E1 makes external paths appear as internal LSAs; they remain external LSAs but cost calculation includes internal distance.
Check your understanding
If your goal is proximity-based exit selection, configure metric-type 1 in the export policy for that redistributed route.
