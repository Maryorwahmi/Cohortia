Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
What to Remember
Multi-Area OSPF
Hierarchical design that scales by reducing per-router LSDB and localizing LSA floods.
Backbone Area (Area 0)
The required transit area; all non-backbone areas must connect to it for inter-area routing.
Area Types & LSAs
Stub, Totally Stubby, and NSSA control whether Types 3, 4, 5, and 7 are allowed, affecting routing entries and LSA flow.
Takeaway

Design multi-area OSPF to match where topology churn occurs, place ABRs with Area 0 connectivity, and choose area types to balance routing detail and router resource usage.
