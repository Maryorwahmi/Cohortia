Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › What to Remember
Final Summary
A compact checklist for multi-area OSPF design and verification.
Scale with areas
Use areas to limit LSDB and LSA impact on routers where topology churn is frequent.
Keep Area 0 contiguous
Ensure ABRs have Area 0 interfaces or plan virtual links if unavoidable.
Choose area types
Pick stub, TSA, or NSSA based on routing table needs and local external injection requirements.
Quick action

After changes, run show ospf database area <id> and show route protocol ospf to confirm LSAs and routes match your design expectations.
