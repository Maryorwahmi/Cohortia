LSA Types — Overview
Different LSA types carry router, network, summary, and external route information across and inside areas.
Type 1 & 2
Router and Network LSAs describe local topology and are flooded only within their area.
Type 3 & 4
Summary and ASBR summary LSAs originate at ABRs to advertise inter-area routes and ASBR reachability.
Type 5 & 7
External LSAs are from ASBRs. Type 7 is used inside NSSAs and translated to Type 5 by the ABR.
Quick check: Which LSA will you see in the area LSDB after an ASBR injects an external route inside an NSSA? Expect Type 7 locally and Type 5 after ABR translation.
