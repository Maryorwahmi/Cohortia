Type 1 & Type 2 LSAs
Router and Network LSAs paint the detailed area topology used for SPF inside the area.
Type 1 — Router LSA
Originated by every router. Describes its links and states and is flooded only inside the area. It is the primary input for SPF calculation within that area.
Type 2 — Network LSA
Originated by the DR on multi-access segments to represent the segment and attached routers. Also contained within the area.

show ospf database area 1.0.0.0
