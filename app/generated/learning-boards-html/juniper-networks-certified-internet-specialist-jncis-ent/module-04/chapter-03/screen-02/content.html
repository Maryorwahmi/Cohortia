Learning Objectives
Learn the why, what, and how of multi-area OSPF on Junos.
01
Explain multi-area necessity
Describe scalability, LSDB size reduction, and localized LSA flooding. You will identify when a network should move from single-area to multi-area OSPF.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate area types
Recognize Backbone, Standard, Stub, Totally Stubby, and NSSA characteristics and their LSA filters. Learn why an NSSA allows internal external routes but blocks external LSAs from outside.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe ABR roles and LSDBs
Explain how ABRs maintain per-area LSDBs, inject Type 3/4 LSAs, summarize routes, and why ABR placement matters for connectivity and scale.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Configure multi-area OSPF on Junos
Assign interfaces to areas, configure stub/no-summaries for totally stubby areas, enable NSSA and default-lsa, and verify with show commands.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
