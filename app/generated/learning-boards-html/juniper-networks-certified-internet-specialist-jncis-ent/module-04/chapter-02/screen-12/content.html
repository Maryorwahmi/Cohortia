Impact

How network type affects LSA and SPF

DR/BDR reduce the number of adjacencies and concentrate Type-2 LSA origination, which reduces the volume of flood events across a multi-access segment.
Less flooding means routers perform SPF less frequently and with smaller topology changes, improving convergence times and CPU stability during events.
When you change interface-type or force a new DR election, expect temporary LSA redistributions and SPF recomputation — plan around that behavior.
Key Takeaway

Network type selection reduces unnecessary LSA traffic and SPF load. Make changes deliberately and verify after the change.

Real-world connection
In production, reducing LSA churn on busy multi-access segments helps preserve control-plane performance during failures and maintenance.
Check your understanding
If LSA flooding spikes after a change, consider whether a network-type change or DR re-election unintentionally broadened LSA origination.
