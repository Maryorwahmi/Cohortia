Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Assessment: DHCP Failure

Assessment Scenario

Clients in VLAN 20 cannot obtain DHCP leases after enabling DHCP snooping and marking access ports untrusted.

Step 1
Attempt a DHCP request from a client to reproduce the symptom.
Step 2
Inspect DHCP snooping and trusted ports: show ethernet-switching-options secure-access-port
Step 3
If the server port is untrusted, mark it trusted and re-test. Verify binding table afterwards.
Step 4
Document the fix and consider alerts to detect if a port accidentally becomes untrusted.
