Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Verify RSTP operation
Key verification commands
Use these to inspect bridge election and per-interface roles.
show spanning-tree bridge
show spanning-tree interface
Interpretation: confirm the lowest BID is the intended root and that blocked ports align with your redundancies.
What to inspect in output
  • Root Bridge BID and priority.
  • Roles: Root Port, Designated, Alternate, Backup.
  • Guard states: root-inconsistent, loop-inconsistent, or administratively disabled for BPDU Guard.
Key Takeaway

Verification confirms design intent: the selected root is in place, uplinks block appropriately, and protections trigger under test conditions.
