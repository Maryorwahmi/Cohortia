Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Classifier to Forwarding
Mapping flow
Classifier inspects DSCP/802.1p → assigns forwarding-class label
Forwarding class → queue assignment and scheduler policy
Scheduler → enforces priority, weights, and minimum bandwidth
Rewrite rules → adjust marks on egress for consistent behavior downstream
Real-world result

Voice calls mapped to expedited-forwarding remain clearer during congestion because queues and the scheduler prioritize their transmission.

Concrete check

Configure a classifier to map known VoIP code-points to expedited-forwarding, run traffic with background load, and observe reduced jitter for the classified flows versus best-effort flows.

Misconception

Classifier labeling alone does not alter transmission order. The forwarding class plus scheduler must be configured to change service behavior.
