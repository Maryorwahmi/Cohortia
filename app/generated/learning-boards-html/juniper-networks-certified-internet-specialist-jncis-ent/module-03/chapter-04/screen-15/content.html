Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Quick Reference
Term

A block with match conditions and an action. Design for precision and order matters.

Match Conditions

Fields like source-address, destination-address, ports, protocol, and VLAN ID that identify traffic.

Actions

accept, discard, reject, count, policer — use count first, then enforce with discard or policer.

Final summary

Design filters carefully, attach them at the right place and direction, test with count, verify counters, and combine filters with CoS to preserve service quality for critical traffic.
