Firewall Filters and CoS Basics
Stateless filters at the switch and essential Class of Service components
In this lesson

Understand filter anatomy, write a basic filter, apply it to VLANs or interfaces, and learn primary CoS elements for prioritization.

Why it matters

Placing essential controls on switches helps block unwanted traffic close to endpoints and ensures voice or video are prioritized in busy networks.

Explore Key Topics
Filter Anatomy
Terms combine match conditions and actions. Order matters because matching stops on the first match.
Applying Filters
Filters can be applied to VLANs or physical interfaces in input or output direction.
CoS Basics
Classifiers, forwarding classes, schedulers and rewrite rules allocate priority for sensitive traffic like VoIP.
Visual Concept
USERS VLAN → SERVERS VLAN
Apply input filter to USERS to block HTTP while allowing other traffic.
CoS: Voice Priority
Classifier marks voice traffic, forwarding class ensures expedited forwarding and scheduler gives bandwidth.
