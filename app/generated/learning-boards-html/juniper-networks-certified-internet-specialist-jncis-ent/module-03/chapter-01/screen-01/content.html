VLANs and Trunking Deep Dive

Why VLANs matter: isolation, performance, and flexible management for enterprise networks.

In this lesson

You will see VLAN creation, access vs trunk ports, 802.1Q tagging, native VLAN security, and inter‑VLAN routing with IRB on Juniper EX switches.

Why it matters

Correct VLAN and trunk configuration prevents broadcast storms, enforces security zones, and ensures devices reach their intended default gateway.
