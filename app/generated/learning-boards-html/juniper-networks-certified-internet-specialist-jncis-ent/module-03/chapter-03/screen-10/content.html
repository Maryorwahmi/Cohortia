Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › VC Roles & Election
Member roles
  • Master: active control plane handling config and management.
  • Backup: hot standby that takes over when Master fails.
  • Linecard: forwards traffic and relies on Master for control decisions.
Election factors
mastership-priority, current role, uptime, and other deterministics influence which member wins the Master role.
Best practices
Preconfigure mastership-priority values for predictable elections, use serial numbers for member assignments, and verify with show virtual-chassis after formation.
Check your understanding

If you need a specific switch to be Master during upgrades, set its mastership-priority explicitly and confirm the assignment before committing changes.
