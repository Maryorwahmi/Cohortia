Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Verify & Troubleshoot
Useful commands
show interfaces ae0
show lacp interfaces
show interfaces ge-0/0/0 extensive
show virtual-chassis
Check ae0 admin and link state, bundled member list, LACP actor/partner state, and counters for errors. If the bundle isn’t forming, inspect LACP modes, physical link parameters, and VLAN/trunk configuration on both ends.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • At least one side must be lacp active.
  • Set minimum-links appropriately to avoid total shutdown.
  • Match VLANs and trunking across members.
  • Confirm physical speeds and duplex match.
Pro Tip

When traffic appears stuck on one physical link, consider whether the hashing method is appropriate for your traffic mix before changing physical topology.
