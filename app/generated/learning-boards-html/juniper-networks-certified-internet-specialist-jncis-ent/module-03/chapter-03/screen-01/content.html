Visual Concept
Logical view
A LAG aggregates multiple physical links into ae0. A Virtual Chassis groups switches into a single control plane where ae0 may span VC members.
Physical links: ge-0/0/0, ge-0/0/1
Aggregated: ae0 (trunk VLAN 10)
Operational view
Mastership and LACP keep forwarding stable. If one link or member fails, traffic shifts to remaining active paths.
