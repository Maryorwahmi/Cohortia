Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Virtual Chassis Overview
Architecture and benefits
  • Simplified management: one CLI and one configuration for multiple switches.
  • Increased port density: combined ports appear as one logical switch.
  • High availability: Master and Backup roles protect the control plane.
Use-case
Two EX switches as an aggregation pair that presents a single logical core with redundant control and combined uplinks to the distribution layer.
Cautions
Ensure correct VCP cabling topologies for redundancy, avoid mixing incompatible hardware or software images, and assign mastership-priority for predictable role election.
Risk
Incorrect cabling or missing priorities can lead to a split-brain where two switches think they are Master, disrupting forwarding and management.
