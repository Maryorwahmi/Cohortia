Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › What to Remember
LAG
Bundle links for higher aggregate bandwidth and redundancy.
LACP
Negotiates bundles; require at least one active side to form dynamic LAGs.
Virtual Chassis
Multiple switches, one control plane. Use mastership-priority for predictable roles.
Summary

Combine LAGs and Virtual Chassis to build resilient, high-capacity fabrics. Verify configuration and test failover to ensure predictable behavior in production.
