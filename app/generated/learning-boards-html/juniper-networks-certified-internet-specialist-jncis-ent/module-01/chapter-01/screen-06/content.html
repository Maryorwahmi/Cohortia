Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › RIB to FIB Flow
Step 1
Routing protocols populate the RIB
Protocols like OSPF and BGP run on the RE and supply candidate routes to the RIB for selection.
Step 2
RE selects best routes and builds FIB
The RE chooses best paths from the RIB and converts them to forwarding entries suitable for hardware.
Step 3
FIB pushed to PFE for installation
The PFE installs forwarding entries so packets are forwarded at line rate without per-packet control-plane interaction.
Diagram (conceptual)
Routing protocols → RIB (RE) → Best path selection → FIB → PFE hardware forwarding. The flow guarantees that forwarding continues even if the RE is busy recalculating routes.
Concrete scenario
When BGP converges on new prefixes, the RE updates the FIB and the PFE forwards traffic according to the new FIB entries.
Check your understanding
If routes update but traffic is unaffected, which component handled the packet movement? (Answer: the PFE uses the last installed FIB.)
