Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
FreeBSD UNIX
The OS base that provides process isolation and system stability for Junos.
Modularity
Independent daemons limit the impact of single-component failures and simplify maintenance.
Transactional Configuration
Staging and committing changes atomically with rollback support protects running networks from partial edits.
Final thought: Combine a secure management posture, regular monitoring of the RE, and disciplined use of transactional changes to keep Junos devices stable and predictable.
