Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Key Concepts
ping
Tests reachability and latency; use count and source to scope tests.
traceroute
Maps the path to a destination to show hop-level failures or delays.
show interfaces terse / extensive
Terse for status, extensive for error counters and media details.
Final takeaway

Combine ping/traceroute, interface inspection, routing checks, and archived logs to diagnose and remediate faults safely.
