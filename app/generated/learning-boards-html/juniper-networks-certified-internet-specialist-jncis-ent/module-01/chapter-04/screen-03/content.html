Objectives — Practice and Safety

A short checklist to develop safe, repeatable troubleshooting habits.

Step
Collect Read-Only Data

Use show commands and monitor streams before any clears or changes.

Step
Verify Before Clearing

Back up logs and confirm clears are necessary; clears remove history.

Step
Treat Zeroize as Destructive

Never run request system zeroize in production; it erases configuration and data.

Safety Reminder

Always capture logs and config backups before destructive operations and confirm approvals for resets.
