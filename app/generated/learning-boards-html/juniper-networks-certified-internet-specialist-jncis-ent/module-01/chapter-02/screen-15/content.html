What to Remember
Console First
Always validate serial connection and reach the operational prompt on first boot.
Configure me0/fxp0
Assign IP, DNS, and NTP so the device is reachable on the management network and timestamps are reliable.
Secure Access
Set strong root password, create users with login classes, enable SSH, and restrict management traffic.
Final takeaway

A reliable initial setup routine and conservative service choices reduce risk and make management predictable and secure.
