Non-disruptive troubleshooting habits

  • Always begin with read-only commands (show bgp summary, show route, show policy).
  • Avoid clear bgp unless you know the impact and have authorization.
  • Limit traceoptions size and flags, and deactivate when finished.
  • Document commands and outputs before making configuration changes.
Checklist
Collect show outputs, identify the cause, propose minimal change, apply during maintenance window if needed, verify using the same show commands.
Why it matters

A careful approach reduces the chance of causing wider routing instability and makes incident handling auditable.
