Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Practical Outcomes
Apply LOCAL_PREF for outbound control
Set LOCAL_PREF higher for routes from your preferred ISP so internal routers prefer that exit. This affects all iBGP peers and provides a single point of control.
Use MED to suggest inbound entry
Advertise lower MED on the link you want the neighbor to prefer. Remember MED is a suggestion and is compared per-adjacent AS by default on Junos.
Prepend AS_PATH for remote deterrence
When you want a remote AS to avoid a path, prepend your AS to make that path appear longer and less preferred.
Verification steps
Always verify with show route protocol bgp detail and check next-hop reachability to avoid misapplied policies or unreachable next-hops.
