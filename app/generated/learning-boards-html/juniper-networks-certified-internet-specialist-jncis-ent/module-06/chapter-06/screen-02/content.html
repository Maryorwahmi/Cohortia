Learning Objectives
Levels: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify key attributes
Recognize AS_PATH, NEXT_HOP, LOCAL_PREF, MED, and ORIGIN and their defaults on Junos. Knowing the attribute meaning prevents misinterpretation when reading route detail.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain selection order
Explain Junos' ordered best-path checks and why next-hop reachability and LOCAL_PREF are evaluated early. This sequence is used in troubleshooting and design decisions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Manipulate attributes
Create import/export policies to set LOCAL_PREF, MED or prepend AS_PATH to shape inbound and outbound flows. Safe policy application is emphasized.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Verify and troubleshoot
Use show route protocol bgp and show route detail to verify attribute values and the chosen best path. Understand common pitfalls like incorrect policy direction.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
