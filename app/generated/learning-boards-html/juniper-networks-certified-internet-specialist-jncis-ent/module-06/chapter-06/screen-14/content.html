Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Key Concepts
AS_PATH
List of AS numbers; used for loop prevention and preferred when shorter.
NEXT_HOP
IP address to forward to; must be reachable for the route to be valid.
LOCAL_PREF
iBGP-only attribute. Higher values preferred for choosing outbound exit.
Summary Takeaway

Together, these attributes and their ordering let you predict and influence BGP route selection; always verify with show route commands.
