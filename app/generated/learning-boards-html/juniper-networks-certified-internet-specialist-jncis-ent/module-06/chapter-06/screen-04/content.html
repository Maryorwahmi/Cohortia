What is a path attribute?
Descriptive metadata carried in BGP updates used to decide which route is selected.
Well-known mandatory
Examples: AS_PATH and NEXT_HOP. These are always present and are fundamental for loop prevention and forwarding.
Well-known discretionary
LOCAL_PREF is exchanged inside an AS to influence outbound decisions.
Optional
MED is optional and not transitive; it advises an adjacent AS about preferred entry.
Key Takeaway

Attributes are policy controls and must be read and tested with show route commands before trusting behavior.

Concrete example
Suppose two routes to 203.0.113.0/24 arrive. Route A has LOCAL_PREF 200 and AS_PATH length 3. Route B has LOCAL_PREF 100 and AS_PATH length 1. Junos prefers the higher LOCAL_PREF even though AS_PATH is shorter on Route B.
Misconception check
Do not assume MED and LOCAL_PREF behave the same. MED suggests an external preference; LOCAL_PREF is authoritative inside your AS.
Try this verification
show route protocol bgp 203.0.113.0/24 detail
This command reveals attributes and the chosen best path for the prefix.
