Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Troubleshooting
Common mistakes
  • Mixing up LOCAL_PREF (import) and MED (export).
  • Missing IGP route to iBGP NEXT_HOP.
  • Applying policies in the wrong direction.
  • Over-prepending AS_PATH without verification.
Debug workflow
1) Confirm next-hop with show route. 2) Inspect LOCAL_PREF and AS_PATH in show route protocol bgp <prefix> detail. 3) If export changed attributes, request the neighbor's received view.
Example verification
show route protocol bgp 192.0.2.0/24 detail
show route 203.0.113.1 detail
