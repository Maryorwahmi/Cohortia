Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › MED
What MED does
An optional attribute sent to a neighbor to suggest which entry point they should prefer when sending inbound traffic to your AS.
Comparison
LOCAL_PREF influences your AS; MED influences an adjacent AS. Use MED only when you want to affect the neighbor's decision.
Configuration example
set policy-options policy-statement PREFER_LINK1_INBOUND term 1 then metric 50
set protocols bgp group TO_ISP_LINK1 export PREFER_LINK1_INBOUND
Lower metric value suggests the ISP prefer this link when choosing how to send traffic to you.
Misconception
MED is a suggestion and applies per-adjacent AS; the neighbor may ignore it or compare MED differently depending on its configuration.
