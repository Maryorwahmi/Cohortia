Origin codes and preference
IGP (i) > EGP (e) > Incomplete (?). Origin is evaluated early in the sequence and breaks ties after AS_PATH checks in the standard order.
Practical note
Redistributed routes typically become Incomplete and will lose to IGP-originated BGP routes unless other attributes override.
Example
A route defined with a network statement in Junos is IGP-origin; a static redistributed into BGP is Incomplete. If attributes are otherwise equal, IGP-origin wins.
Misconception
Do not assume redistribution preserves origin preference. Explicit network origination is preferred when you need precedence.
