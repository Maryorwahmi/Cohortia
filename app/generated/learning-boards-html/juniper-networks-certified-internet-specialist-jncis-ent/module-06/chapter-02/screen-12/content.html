OpenConfirm and Established — Confirmation and Route Exchange
What confirmation looks like and why Established matters.
OpenConfirm role
After Open reception, peers exchange Keepalives to confirm negotiated parameters. Successful exchange moves the FSM to Established.
Established behavior
Peers exchange Update messages and the network learns advertised prefixes. Policies and attributes still determine which routes are accepted into the routing table.
Key takeaway
Established confirms peer compatibility and connectivity but does not guarantee policy alignment or desired route selection.
