Key Concepts — Part 1
Three compact reminders to keep top of mind.
Open message
Negotiates version, AS, hold time, and Router ID.
Update message
Advertises and withdraws prefixes with path attributes.
Keepalive & Notification
Keepalive preserves liveness; Notification signals fatal errors and closes the session.
Summary

Use message types and neighbor states together to rapidly identify whether issues are TCP connectivity, parameter mismatch, or protocol errors.
