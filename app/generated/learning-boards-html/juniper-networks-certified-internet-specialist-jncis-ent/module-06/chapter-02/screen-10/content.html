Idle and Connect States — Entry and Failure Modes
When peering hasn't started or when TCP connection is being attempted.
Idle state checks
  • Is the BGP group or neighbor administratively active?
  • Are the local interfaces and addresses configured and up?
  • Has the routing table an entry to reach the peer?
Connect troubleshooting
If the router cannot complete the TCP handshake, verify peer IPs, check for firewall rules blocking port 179, and ensure there is no asymmetric routing that prevents TCP completion.
Misconception: Idle usually means a local configuration or administrative state, not a remote BGP protocol bug.
