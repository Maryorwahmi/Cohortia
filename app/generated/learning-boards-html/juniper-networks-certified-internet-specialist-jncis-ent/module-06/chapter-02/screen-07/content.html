Keepalive Messages and the Hold Timer
Heartbeat behavior, timing, and failure consequences.
Process steps
1. After Open exchange, peers send Keepalives to confirm the session.
2. Keepalives are typically sent every one-third of the negotiated hold time.
3. If no Keepalive or Update arrives before the hold expires, the session is declared down and routes are withdrawn.
Concrete example
If peers negotiate a 90-second hold time, they will exchange Keepalives roughly every 30 seconds. If one peer stops receiving those messages for 90 seconds, it tears down the session and withdraws learned prefixes.
Troubleshooting tip
When routes vanish unexpectedly, check for hold-time expiry, packet loss, CPU exhaustion on the peer, or asymmetric routing preventing Keepalive delivery.
