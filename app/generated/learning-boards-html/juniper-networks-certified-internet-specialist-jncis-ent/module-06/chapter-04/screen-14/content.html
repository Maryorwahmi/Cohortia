Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Operational Reminders
IGP Reachability
Confirm loopbacks are present in the routing table before expecting BGP sessions to form.
Local-address
Set local-address to the loopback so the BGP session uses a stable source address.
RR Responsibility
Only the RR marks neighbors as clients and sets the cluster ID; design RRs to avoid single points of failure.
Final thought
Small verification steps and clear RR design choices prevent most iBGP deployment failures. Keep a concise checklist nearby when automating or scaling these configurations.
