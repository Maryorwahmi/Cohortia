Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
iBGP
BGP between routers in the same AS to distribute external routes and preserve policy attributes.
Full-Mesh
Every iBGP speaker peers with every other: N*(N-1)/2 sessions due to split-horizon limitations.
Loopback Interface
Stable /32 interface used for BGP local-address; must be reachable via an IGP for sessions to form.
Summary
Use loopback-based iBGP with careful IGP planning and consider Route Reflectors to scale while preserving route visibility and policy.
