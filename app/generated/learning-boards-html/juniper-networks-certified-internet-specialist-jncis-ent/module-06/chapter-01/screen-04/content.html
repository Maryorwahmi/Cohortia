Definition

Autonomous System (AS)

A unique administrative domain identified by an AS number. AS numbers appear in BGP advertisements and the AS_PATH attribute.

Concrete example
Enterprise AS 65000 advertises 198.51.100.0/24. That AS number marks the origin in AS_PATH.
Common misconception
AS numbers are not cosmetic tags — they control loop prevention and are used in policy decisions.
Key Takeaway

Use a correct, unique AS number. The AS number matters operationally for AS_PATH-based loop prevention and policy.

Annotated cards
AS number types
Publicly assigned (RIR) or private ranges for lab use
Operational role
Identifies origin and path for advertisements, enabling loop checks and policy decisions.
Example label
AS 65000 — enterprise; AS 100 — ISP
