Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What to Remember
Confirm AS numbers
AS identity is operationally meaningful for routing and loops.
BGP for policy
Use BGP to shape inter‑AS traffic; keep IGP for internal reachability.
AS_PATH for safety
AS_PATH prevents loops and informs policy; measure performance separately.
Closing thought
Use these three checks as a quick operational sanity check whenever you design, configure, or troubleshoot BGP in an enterprise network.
