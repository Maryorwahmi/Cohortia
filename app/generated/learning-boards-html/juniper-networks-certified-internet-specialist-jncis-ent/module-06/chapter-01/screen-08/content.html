Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Multi‑homing and failover
Architecture

Enterprise multi‑homing pattern

Enterprise AS advertises prefixes to multiple ISPs and uses BGP attributes to influence which path is chosen for traffic. If one ISP fails, BGP withdraws those routes and the remaining ISP takes over, given correct internal distribution and reachability.
Control egress
Local preference or outbound policy selects which ISP carries outbound flows.
Influence ingress
AS_PATH prepending or selective advertisement can nudge remote ASes to prefer one ISP.
Scenario card
AS 65000 uses eBGP to ISP A and ISP B. It advertises 198.51.100.0/24 to both. If ISP A’s link drops, routes from ISP A are withdrawn and traffic moves to ISP B after BGP reconvergence and IGP reachability checks.
Common mistake
Assuming symmetric traffic distribution without explicit policy or coordination with ISPs.
