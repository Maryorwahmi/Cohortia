Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Autonomous System (AS)
Unique administrative domain identified by an AS number; used in AS_PATH and loop prevention.
eBGP vs iBGP
eBGP between ASes for inter‑domain routing; iBGP inside an AS to distribute external routes.
AS_PATH (Path Vector)
Records AS sequence for loop prevention and policy; not a direct performance metric.
Summary
Confirm AS numbers, verify peer types, ensure IGP reachability, and use AS_PATH for policy and safety checks when operating BGP.
