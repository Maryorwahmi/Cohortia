IGP vs BGP — intent and behavior

IGP (e.g., OSPF)
Focus on fast convergence, shortest metric paths, and intra‑AS reachability.
BGP
Focus on inter‑AS policy, prefix advertisement, and AS_PATH-based decisions.
Practical example
Your internal routers run OSPF to keep internal routes updated quickly. Your edge routers run BGP with two ISPs to manage cost and failover. You use OSPF for next‑hop reachability inside the AS and BGP to decide which ISP advertises your prefixes and how incoming traffic is influenced.
Misconception check
Don’t expect BGP to replace an IGP’s role of fast internal convergence.
