Operational checklist

1. Verify AS number uniqueness and assignment.
2. Select BGP speakers and advertised prefixes.
3. Ensure IGP next‑hop reachability.
4. Apply export/import filters to prevent leaks.
5. Plan iBGP topology: full mesh or route reflectors.
6. Test failover and monitor AS_PATH and advertised prefixes.
Key Takeaway

BGP must be planned: identity, reachability, policy, and testing are all required for safe inter‑AS operation.
