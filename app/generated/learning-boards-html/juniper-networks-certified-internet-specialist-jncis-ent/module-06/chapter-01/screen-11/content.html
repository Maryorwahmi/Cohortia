Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Misconfigurations and misconceptions

Common mistakes

Mislabeling peer types
Check AS numbers to decide eBGP or iBGP.
Ignoring IGP reachability
BGP advertisement alone does not guarantee traffic will reach the next hop inside your AS.
Leaking prefixes
Use export filters to avoid accidental public advertisement of private networks.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm AS numbers and peering types.
  • Validate IGP next‑hop reachability after learning routes.
  • Apply route filters to control exported prefixes.
  • Simulate ISP failures and verify BGP failover behavior.
