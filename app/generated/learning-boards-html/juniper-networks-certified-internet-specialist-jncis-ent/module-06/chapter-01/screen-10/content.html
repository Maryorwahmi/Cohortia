Definition

BGP speaker & prefix

BGP speaker: a router running BGP. Prefix: a network block announced in BGP, e.g., 198.51.100.0/24.
Concrete example
Border router in AS 65000 advertises 198.51.100.0/24 to ISP peers; those peers add their AS numbers to AS_PATH when relaying the route.
Operational guidance
Decide which routers are BGP speakers and restrict prefixes to those you intend to publish. Avoid leaking internal-only networks to external peers.
Common mistake
Accidentally advertising private addressing to the internet due to misconfigured export policies.
