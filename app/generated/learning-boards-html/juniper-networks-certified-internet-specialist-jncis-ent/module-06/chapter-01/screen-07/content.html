Definition

Path vector and AS_PATH

AS_PATH lists the sequence of AS numbers that a route advertisement traversed. It is used for loop detection and policy decisions.
Concrete example
Route to 203.0.113.0/24 arrives with AS_PATH [100 200]. A router in AS 200 sees the path and avoids re-accepting if AS 200 is in the list.
Policy vs performance
Don't equate shorter AS_PATH with lower latency. AS_PATH is administrative; use measurements to confirm performance before preferring a route solely on AS_PATH length.
Key Takeaway

AS_PATH prevents loops and supports policy decisions but is not a direct measure of path quality.
