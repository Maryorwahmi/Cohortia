Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Common mistakes
Missing route

Network statements require that the prefix exist in inet.0; otherwise BGP will not advertise it.

Missing family

Forgetting family inet unicast on the BGP group prevents IPv4 exchanges even if everything else is configured.

Over-broad redistribution

Accepting all direct or OSPF routes without filters can leak internal prefixes and cause routing instability.

Mitigation checklist
  • Test policies with test policy before applying.
  • Use exact route-filters or prefix lists in export policies.
  • Confirm family settings and route presence before troubleshooting BGP advertisements.
