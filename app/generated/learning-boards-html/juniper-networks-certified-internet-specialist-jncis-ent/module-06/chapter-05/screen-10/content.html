Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Verification workflow
Commands
show route advertising-protocol bgp 10.0.0.2
show route receive-protocol bgp 10.0.0.2
show route protocol bgp
show policy policy-statement REDISTRIBUTE_OSPF_TO_BGP detail
test policy REDISTRIBUTE_OSPF_TO_BGP route 10.10.10.1/32

Follow this order: 1) Confirm route exists in inet.0. 2) Validate policy behavior with test policy. 3) Check show route advertising-protocol to see what is sent.

Troubleshooting checklist
  • Is the prefix present in inet.0? show route <prefix>
  • Is family inet unicast enabled on the BGP group?
  • Does the export policy match the prefix or protocol? test policy helps.
  • Use show route advertising-protocol to confirm what is actually sent to the neighbor.
Key Takeaway

Verification commands let you observe the full path from local route to peer advertisement; use them in order to isolate failures.
