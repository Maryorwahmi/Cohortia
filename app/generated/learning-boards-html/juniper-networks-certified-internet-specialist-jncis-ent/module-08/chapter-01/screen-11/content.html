Typical failures

  • - Assuming dual REs without enabling and verifying GRES.
  • - Overlooking the synchronization path or internal fabric health.
  • - Skipping verification after configuration or software updates.
  • - Running uncontrolled manual switchovers in production.
Avoid these mistakes by adopting verification checklists, lab testing, and discipline around change control.
Recovery tip
If you find synchronization issues, do not trust the standby RE for a switchover until you fix the path and re-run the verification commands.
Checklist: enable GRES, confirm config/kernel/fabric Done, GRES enabled: Yes, then schedule tests in a lab.
