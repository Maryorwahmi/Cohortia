Chapter 8.1 › Key Concepts
High Availability (HA)
Redundancy plus synchronized state and verification minimize downtime.
Control Plane vs Data Plane
Separate roles require independent redundancy and recovery strategies.
Graceful Routing Engine Switchover (GRES)
Enables a backup RE to take over with synchronized kernel and routing state, reducing reconvergence time.
Summary Takeaway

Design HA for both planes, enable and verify GRES, and incorporate synchronization checks into your operational runbooks to reduce outage risk.
