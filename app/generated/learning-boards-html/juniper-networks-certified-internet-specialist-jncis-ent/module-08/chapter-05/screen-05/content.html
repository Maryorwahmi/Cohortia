Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Problem identification
Process: Define and scope the problem
Checklist
  • Who is affected (users, subnet, service)?
  • What is failing (application, ping, access)?
  • When did it start and is it ongoing?
  • What changed recently (configs, deployments)?
Symptom refinement

Transform vague reports into statements like: "VLAN 10 users cannot access 192.168.1.100 since 08:15; external users unaffected." This guides your next tests.

Incident log (example)
08:12 - Users report timeout to intranet
08:15 - Confirmed: VLAN 20 cannot reach 10.0.0.100
08:20 - Checked recent changes: switch upgrade at 07:50
08:25 - Began divide-and-conquer checks on VLAN gateway
Why this matters
Clear scope narrows your search space and prevents wasted effort on unrelated systems, speeding diagnosis and reducing service impact.
