Introduction to Network Troubleshooting Methodologies

Learn a disciplined process for diagnosing and resolving network problems so you fix issues efficiently and safely.

Orientation

This chapter trains you to avoid guessing, to form testable hypotheses, and to use the OSI model as a diagnostic checklist. The techniques here prepare you to use Junos OS tools later, but they are general practices valuable in any network role.

In this lesson
Define problems, choose a methodology, test hypotheses, and document the fix.
Why it matters
Systems that encourage repeatable steps reduce downtime and protect production services.
