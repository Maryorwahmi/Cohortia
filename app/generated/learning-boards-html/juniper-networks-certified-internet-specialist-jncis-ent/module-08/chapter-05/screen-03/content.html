Hypothesis & testing

You will practice framing a clear hypothesis and selecting the right diagnostic steps to confirm or refute it, reducing wasted effort.

Change control

You will learn to make single, reversible changes and to work within change management policies to protect production systems.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Avoiding common mistakes

Recognize pitfalls such as jumping to conclusions, making multiple simultaneous changes, or relying on incomplete data.

Documentation & baselines

You will identify and use the documentation most helpful during incidents: topology diagrams, IP plans, VLAN mappings, and change logs.
