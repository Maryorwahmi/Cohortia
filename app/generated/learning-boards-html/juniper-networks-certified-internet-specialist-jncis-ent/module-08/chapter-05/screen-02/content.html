Learning Objectives

Skills you will practice and why they matter in production networks.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Problem identification

Learn to craft precise problem statements and collect evidence like affected hosts, start time, and observed errors. This reduces wasted checks and speeds diagnosis.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Methodology selection

Be able to explain and pick top-down, bottom-up, or divide-and-conquer based on symptoms and scope of impact so you test high-probability causes first.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
OSI model application

Map symptoms to OSI layers and select appropriate tools and checks for each layer, improving the focus of your tests and reducing blind effort.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Document & baseline

Capture final diagnosis, steps, root cause, and a recovery point. Baselines help you spot deviations and restore known good states quickly.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
