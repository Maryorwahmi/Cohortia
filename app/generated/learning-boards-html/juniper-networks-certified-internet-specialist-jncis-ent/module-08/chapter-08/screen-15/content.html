What to Remember
Three short operational checks to run when things fail.
Native VLAN
Ensure native-vlan-id matches on trunk endpoints. A mismatch misdirects untagged frames.
MAC Table
Use MAC learning to see where hosts actually appear on the fabric; it shows miswiring or misconfiguration.
Filter Counters
Add counts to filter terms and check the session table to prove whether traffic matched policy or was dropped.
Final Takeaway

Troubleshoot with hypothesis-driven checks and objective evidence, then document fixes for future reference.
