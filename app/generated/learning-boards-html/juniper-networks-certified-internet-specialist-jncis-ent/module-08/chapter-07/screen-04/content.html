Core Concept Why capture packets?
Packet capture explained

Inspect raw packet headers and payloads to uncover missing handshakes, protocol mismatches, or firewall behavior that higher-level tools cannot show.

Concrete example
  • Client sends TCP SYN but never receives SYN-ACK — capture shows where the reply was lost.
  • Firewall returns ICMP administratively prohibited — capture shows the reject type and timing.
Common misconception

Packet capture is not always the first step. Use it after narrowing scope; otherwise you risk huge captures and wasted time.

Analogy

Think of packet capture as a microscope and flow monitoring as a telescope. The microscope shows fine detail for a small sample; the telescope shows patterns across the whole sky.

Key signs to capture
  • Intermittent failures localized to a host or server.
  • Protocol negotiation or version mismatches.
  • Suspected firewall or ACL drops where responses are missing.
Key Takeaway

Capture narrowly and deliberately. Use packet detail to confirm exact packet sequences before changing rules or services.
