Comparison Packet capture vs Flow records
Packet capture reveals
  • Handshakes, sequence and acknowledgement numbers
  • Retransmissions and TCP flags
  • ICMP errors and payloads
Use when

You need definitive proof of protocol behaviour or payload content for a specific session.

Flow records reveal
  • Top talkers by bytes or packets
  • Protocol and port distribution across the network
  • Sudden spikes and unusual host behaviour
Use when

You need scalable, continuous visibility to spot trends and targets for deeper capture.

Key Takeaway

Combine flows to identify suspects and packet captures to validate and fix protocol-level issues. Use each tool for what it does best.
