Learning Objectives — Continued
Configure J-Flow, pick safe sampling rates, and avoid capture mistakes.
01
Configure flow sampling on interfaces and export flow records to a collector using the forwarding-options sampling configuration. This lets you collect long-term traffic patterns without capturing every packet.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Choose an appropriate sampling rate, for example one in one thousand, to reduce load while preserving visibility of top talkers and anomalies.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Avoid capture mistakes: always start with restrictive filters, verify the interface, and use no-resolve when you need raw IPs quickly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Combine packet captures and flow records in a troubleshooting workflow to decide whether a problem requires deep packet inspection or flow-level investigation.
