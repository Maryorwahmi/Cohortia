Nonstop Forwarding and Aggregated Ethernet
Why the data plane must survive control-plane and link failures

This chapter focuses on two complementary mechanisms. Nonstop Forwarding keeps packet forwarding active when the Routing Engine is unavailable. Aggregated Ethernet bundles links to protect against physical link outages and increase throughput. Together they help meet uptime requirements for enterprise traffic.

Visual concept
PFE, RE, and AE relationships
Packet Forwarding Engine
Holds forwarding table entries and continues forwarding during RE switchover. It bridges the gap while NSR and the new RE restore routing adjacencies.
Aggregated Ethernet
Bundles physical links into ae0 so a single link failure does not drop the logical interface. LACP negotiates the bundle.
Key result
Combining NSF with AE produces data-plane continuity across both control-plane and physical failures.
