Verification › Verifying AE and LACP
What to check
  • ae0 operational state and counters.
  • LACP state: actor/partner, activity, timeout, and aggregation status.
  • Per-port LACP statistics such as LACP Rx/Tx and errors.
Troubleshooting

If partner info is missing or the bundle does not form, check LACP mode matching, physical connectivity, and ensure member ports have no conflicting logical units.
