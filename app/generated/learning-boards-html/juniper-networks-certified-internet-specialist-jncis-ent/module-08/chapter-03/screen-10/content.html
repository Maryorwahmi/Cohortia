Comparison › Single Link vs Aggregated Ethernet
Single physical link
  • Single point of failure: cable or port outage drops traffic.
  • Fixed bandwidth equal to the link speed.
  • Simpler debugging, but lower resilience.
Risk
An outage causes service disruption until the physical link is restored.
Aggregated Ethernet (AE)
  • Logical interface persists if one or more member links fail (depending on minimum-links).
  • Aggregate bandwidth equals sum of active member links.
  • Load balancing distributes flows across members using hashing.
Benefit
Higher availability and throughput; careful hashing and minimum-links choices ensure expected behavior.
Check your design
Measure flow distribution and simulate member failures to confirm that AE meets performance and availability goals under realistic traffic patterns.
