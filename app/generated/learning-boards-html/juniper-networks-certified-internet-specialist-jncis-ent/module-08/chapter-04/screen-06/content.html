Comparison

Master vs Backup vs Line-card

Master
Active control plane. Manages configuration and makes forwarding decisions for the entire chassis.
Backup
Hot-standby with synchronized state, ready to take over if the master fails.
Line-card
Provides additional ports and forwarding capacity; controlled by the master and backup for state coordination.
Example & Tip
For a two-switch chassis designate one switch as master and the other as backup. Pre-provision serial numbers so member IDs remain predictable after reboots. This prevents unexpected role changes during maintenance.
Key Takeaway

Designate and provision master and backup roles to ensure control-plane continuity and predictable behavior.
