Architecture

Design advantages of Virtual Chassis

Simplified STP
STP runs once for the logical chassis, reducing blocked ports and confusion.
Cross-member LACP
Create AE bundles across members for resilient server and uplink connectivity.
Single management
One IP and one config simplifies monitoring, upgrades, and troubleshooting.
Key Takeaway

Treat Virtual Chassis as an enabler for cleaner topology and operational efficiency while still designing for physical redundancy.
