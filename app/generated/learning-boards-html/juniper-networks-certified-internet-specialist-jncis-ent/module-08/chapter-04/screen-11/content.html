Common Mistakes

Frequent causes of failure

  • Omitting serial-number provisioning so IDs are assigned unpredictably.
  • Mismatched VCP configs or wrong cable/transceiver types.
  • Poor power-on sequencing leading to unexpected elections.
Quick Checklist
Before powering on and committing:
  • Document serial-to-member-ID mapping.
  • Configure matching VCPs on both switches.
  • Confirm cable and transceiver compatibility.
Pro Tip

Run show virtual-chassis immediately after reboot to detect incomplete replication before enabling production traffic.
