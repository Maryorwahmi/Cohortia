Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › GRES only vs GRES + NSR
Scenario A

GRES only

Kernel and interfaces restore quickly, but rpd restarts and adjacencies must be re-established by the new master. Expect protocol-level reconvergence.
Example: BGP sessions may drop and re-open, causing brief route loss.
Verification: show chassis redundancy will report interface sync; show bgp summary will show session re-establishment after the event.
Scenario B

GRES + NSR

Backup has full rpd state and can become master without making neighbors wait for re-convergence. Adjacencies remain Full or Established.
Example: OSPF neighbors remain Full and routing table entries persist with no noticeable traffic interruption.
Verification: show ospf neighbor and show route will show little or no churn across the switchover.
Misconception
Enabling GRES alone is not enough to guarantee routing stability. NSR is required to preserve rpd state for routing continuity.
