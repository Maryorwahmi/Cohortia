Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Troubleshooting & best practices
Checklist
  • Confirm graceful-switchover and nonstop-routing are configured and committed.
  • Check synchronization status on both REs: show chassis redundancy reports sync Done.
  • Validate rpd process health on both REs: show system processes extensive | match rpd.
  • Record show route and neighbor outputs before testing and compare after switchover.
  • Keep console access and a rollback plan when testing in non-lab environments.
Common Pitfalls
- Enabling GRES without NSR and expecting protocol continuity.
- Failing to verify synchronization Done on the backup RE before a switchover.
- Not preserving console access during lab tests.
Recovery tip
If a switchover misbehaves, use console access to review logs, check rpd, and restore configuration or restart processes as required.
