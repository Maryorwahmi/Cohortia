Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Key Concepts
Show commands
Snapshot of interfaces, routes, and protocol state to verify what the device sees.
Monitor commands
Observe events and packets in real time; use filters to limit the scope and impact.
Ping & Traceroute
Probe reachability and trace packet paths; always consider source to reproduce client behavior.
Summary

Combine snapshot, live observation, and probing into a repeatable workflow: inspect, observe, test, collect. This sequence leads to faster, more accurate troubleshooting.
