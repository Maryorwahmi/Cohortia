Key Concepts: Roles, Goals, and Pitfalls

Understanding the components, benefits, and risks of the Mediator pattern.

Colleague

An interface or abstract class for objects that communicate via the mediator, unaware of other colleagues.

Decoupling

Reducing direct dependencies between objects, a primary goal of the Mediator pattern for increased flexibility.

God Object Anti-pattern

A potential pitfall where the mediator becomes too large and complex, accumulating too much responsibility.

Summary Takeaway

The Mediator pattern effectively decouples objects through centralized control, but careful design is needed to avoid the 'God Object' anti-pattern.
