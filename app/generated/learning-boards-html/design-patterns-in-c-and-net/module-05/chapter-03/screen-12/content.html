The 'God Object' Anti-Pattern

When a mediator becomes too powerful and complex.

Definition

What is a 'God Object'?

A 'God Object' or 'God Class' is a class that knows or does too much. In the context of the Mediator pattern, this occurs when the ConcreteMediator accumulates excessive business logic.

  • Violates the Single Responsibility Principle.
  • Becomes difficult to understand, test, and maintain.
Consequence

Negates the benefits of decoupling, leading to a single point of failure and a maintenance nightmare.

Example Scenario

Overloaded ChatRoom Mediator

If our ChatRoom mediator were to handle:

User Authentication

Verifying user credentials.

Message Encryption

Encrypting/decrypting messages.

Complex Filtering

Applying advanced content filters.

Database Persistence

Saving chat history.

...in addition to message distribution, it would become a God Object, making it brittle and hard to evolve.

Pro-Tip

Keep the mediator's role focused on coordination, not performing the actual business logic of the colleagues.
