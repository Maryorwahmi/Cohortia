Avoiding Deadlocks
The circular wait for shared resources.

What is a Deadlock?

A situation where two or more competing actions are waiting for each other to finish, and thus neither ever finishes. Threads are blocked indefinitely.

Common Scenario

  • •Thread A acquires Lock X, then tries to acquire Lock Y.
  • •Thread B acquires Lock Y, then tries to acquire Lock X.
  • •Both threads are now blocked, waiting for a resource held by the other.
Key Takeaway

Deadlocks halt application progress. Preventing them requires careful resource management and consistent lock acquisition order.
