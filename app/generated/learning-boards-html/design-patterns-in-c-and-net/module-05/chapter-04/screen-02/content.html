Differentiate Concurrency & Parallelism

Explain the core differences between managing tasks over time (concurrency) and executing them simultaneously (parallelism). This skill is vital for optimizing application design for modern multi-core systems.

Identify Concurrency Challenges

Recognize and describe common problems like race conditions, deadlocks, livelocks, and starvation. This knowledge is crucial for debugging and preventing hard-to-find bugs in multi-threaded applications.
