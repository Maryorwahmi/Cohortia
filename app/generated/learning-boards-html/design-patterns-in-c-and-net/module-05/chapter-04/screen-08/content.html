Concurrency Challenges
Livelocks and Starvation
When threads are active but unproductive.

Livelocks

Threads are continuously changing their state in response to other threads, but no actual progress is made. They are 'live' but unproductive.

  • •Threads are active, consuming CPU cycles.
  • •Often results from overly cautious conflict resolution.
Analogy

Two people repeatedly stepping aside for each other in a narrow hallway, never passing.

Starvation

Occurs when a thread is repeatedly denied access to a shared resource or CPU time, even though the resource becomes available.

  • •Can be due to unfair scheduling or consistent resource contention loss.
  • •Thread is not blocked, but never gets to run.
Key Takeaway

Both livelocks and starvation lead to unproductive threads, emphasizing the need for fair and robust concurrency control.
