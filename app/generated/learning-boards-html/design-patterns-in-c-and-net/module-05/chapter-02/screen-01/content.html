The Iterator Pattern: Traversing Collections Uniformly

Accessing collection elements without exposing their internal structure, promoting uniform access and decoupling.

Key Idea

The Iterator pattern provides a standardized way to traverse elements of a collection without exposing its internal structure, promoting uniform access and decoupling.
