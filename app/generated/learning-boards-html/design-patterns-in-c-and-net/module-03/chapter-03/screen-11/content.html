Pitfalls & Safety
Common Mistakes
Interface bloat
Only include operations in Component that every element must support.
Deep recursion
Use iterative traversal for very deep trees to avoid stack overflow.
Concurrency
Protect child collections when multiple threads can modify structure concurrently.
Performance & correctness
  • Cache expensive aggregated results and update caches on structural changes.
  • Use unique identifiers or keep references to elements you will remove frequently.
  • Document whether child-management methods on Component are optional and how leaves behave.
Warning

Unclear interface contracts and concurrency bugs are the main causes of failure in Composite-based systems. Test and document your design choices.
