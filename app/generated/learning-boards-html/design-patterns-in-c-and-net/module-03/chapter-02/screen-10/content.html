Comparison
Decorator vs Inheritance
Inheritance
Compile-time variants, simple dispatch, but class explosion for many optional features.
Decorator
Runtime composition, flexible combinations, adheres to Open/Closed and Single Responsibility principles.
Heuristic
Prefer Decorator for many optional features. Prefer inheritance when variants are small, fixed, and performance-critical.
Concrete trade-offs
  • Decorator: flexible but adds indirection layers.
  • Inheritance: efficient dispatch but brittle hierarchy.
  • Consider testability, extensibility, and runtime needs.
Key Takeaway

Choose composition when flexibility matters; choose inheritance when variations are few and performance is paramount.
