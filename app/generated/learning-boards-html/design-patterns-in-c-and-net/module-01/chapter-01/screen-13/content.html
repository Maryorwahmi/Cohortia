What to Remember

Design Pattern
A reusable template that captures intent and trade-offs for recurring problems.
Gang of Four (GoF)
Authors who cataloged 23 patterns and grouped them into three categories.
Anti-Pattern
A common but ineffective response to a recurring problem, such as over-engineering.
Final Takeaway

Use patterns to communicate intent and manage recurring problems. Always weigh benefits against added complexity and avoid applying patterns without clear need.
