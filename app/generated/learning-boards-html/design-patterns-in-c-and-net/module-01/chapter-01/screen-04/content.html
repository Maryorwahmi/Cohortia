Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What is a Design Pattern?
Definition

A reusable template of intent and structure

Patterns focus on the relationship between components and the trade-offs you accept. They help you reason about design choices and communicate them with a shared vocabulary.

Blueprint metaphor
Like an architectural plan, a pattern highlights structure and intent without prescribing materials.
Common misconception
Patterns are not paste-ready code. They are design templates that must be adapted to your context.
Concrete example
Logging abstraction
If logging calls are scattered in business classes, changing the logging destination forces edits across many files. A pattern suggests introducing an abstraction to decouple concerns so the business logic remains unchanged when logging evolves.
Key Takeaway

Patterns capture intent and trade-offs. Use them to reason about design and to communicate choices clearly to your team.
