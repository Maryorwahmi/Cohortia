Anti-Pattern

Golden Hammer Syndrome

Applying a pattern by habit rather than need creates complexity and maintenance overhead. Always measure the benefit before introducing a pattern.

Symptom
Adding patterns where simple construction or direct calls would be clearer.
Consequence
Increased verbosity, harder onboarding, and needless indirection.
Avoidance checklist
  • Is there duplicated behavior or coupling that the pattern will remove?
  • Can a simpler refactor solve the problem now?
  • Will the pattern add unnecessary maintenance burden?
Warning

Patterns used as fashion reduce clarity. Prefer readable, simple code unless patterns give clear benefits.
