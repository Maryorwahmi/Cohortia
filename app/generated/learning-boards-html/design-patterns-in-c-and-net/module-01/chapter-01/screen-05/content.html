Historical Context

Gang of Four: a shared vocabulary

The GoF book grouped patterns into three categories to help developers choose solutions by the nature of the problem rather than memorizing individual structures.

Creational
Object creation and lifecycle
Structural
Composition and relationships
Behavioral
Communication and control flow
Tip
Start with the problem category
Identify whether your issue is about creation, structure, or behavior. That focus helps you choose candidate patterns efficiently and avoid irrelevant complexity.
Key Takeaway

Use pattern categories to frame the problem. That reduces search time and improves the fit of your chosen solution.
