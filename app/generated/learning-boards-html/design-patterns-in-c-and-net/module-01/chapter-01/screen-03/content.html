Practice & Application

These objectives guide the hands-on task and reflection you'll complete to turn concept into judgement.

01
Apply
Hands-on notification scenario

You will inspect starter code, run a small console app, and note where notifications are tightly coupled to business logic.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Reflect
Decide when to refactor

You will write a short justification for introducing an interface or pattern, assessing costs and benefits for your scenario.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Assess
Identify coupling points

You will point out where a change to notification channels would force edits across multiple classes, showing the need for separation of concerns.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Communicate
Explain your choice

You will practice explaining whether a simple refactor suffices or if a formal pattern is warranted, improving team discussions about design decisions.
