Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Why Patterns Matter
Benefits

Real advantages for C# and .NET development

Common vocabulary
Say a pattern name and teammates understand the intent and trade-offs.
Proven solutions
Patterns capture approaches refined by many projects, lowering risk when applied correctly.
Maintainability & reusability
Patterns encourage modular code that is easier to extend and reuse across projects.
Concrete example
Logging abstraction
If classes depend on a logging abstraction rather than direct console calls, changing the logging destination requires only one implementation swap, not edits throughout the codebase.
Watch out

Patterns do not guarantee better code; they improve design when the problem fits. Avoid adding patterns for their own sake.
