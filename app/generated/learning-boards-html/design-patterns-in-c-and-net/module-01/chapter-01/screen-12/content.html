Trade-offs

Balance benefit and cost

Benefits
Clearer responsibilities, easier testing, localized changes.
Costs
More types, indirection, slight development overhead.
Measurement
Profile hotspots
If abstraction affects performance-critical paths, measure before and after. Often the clarity gains justify the cost except in proven hotspots.
Key Takeaway

Make deliberate trade-offs: prefer simplicity until the pattern clearly reduces long-term cost or risk.
