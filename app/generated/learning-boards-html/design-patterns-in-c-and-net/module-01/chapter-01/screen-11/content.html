Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › When to Use Patterns
Checklist

Decide with purpose

  • Is the problem recurring across the codebase or projects?
  • Does the current design cause coupling that forces wide edits?
  • Will the pattern improve communication and intent clarity for the team?
  • Do the benefits outweigh the cost of added indirection?
Concrete contrast
Simple refactor vs full pattern
Prefer a small refactor when the problem is local. Favor a pattern when repetition, coupling, and extension needs are clear.
Key Takeaway

Choose the least-complex design that meets foreseeable needs; escalate to patterns as requirements justify them.
