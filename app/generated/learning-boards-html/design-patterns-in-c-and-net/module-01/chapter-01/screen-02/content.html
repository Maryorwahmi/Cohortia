Learning Objectives

These objectives focus on conceptual understanding, historical context, and cautious application of design patterns in C# and .NET projects.

01
Define
What a design pattern is

You will explain patterns as reusable templates for recurring design problems and why they are not finished code. This matters because it clarifies expectations when applying a pattern in a team context.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Context
Gang of Four history

You will summarize why the GoF book provided a shared vocabulary and how three categories—creational, structural, behavioral—help classify solutions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Benefits
Why patterns help in C# and .NET

You will identify benefits such as clearer team communication, proven approaches, improved maintainability, and reuse of architectural ideas across projects.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Pitfalls
Avoid anti-patterns

You will learn to spot over-engineering, cargo cult programming, and the trade-offs that abstraction introduces so you don't add complexity unnecessarily.
