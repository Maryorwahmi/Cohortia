Module 3 › Chapter 2.2 › Benefits & Drawbacks
Benefits
  • Loose coupling between client and concrete types.
  • Open/Closed compliance: add new creators without modifying existing ones.
  • Better testability by injecting creators that return test doubles.
Drawbacks
  • More classes and increased indirection.
  • Risk of poorly designed Product interfaces that break polymorphism.
  • Potential over-engineering for single-variant domains.
Decision rule
Apply Factory Method when you expect product variants or when creators require subclass-specific creation logic.
Avoid
Avoid when there is no foreseeable extension and simplicity matters more than flexibility.
Key Takeaway

Match the pattern to expected change; prefer extensibility when change is likely, simplicity otherwise.
