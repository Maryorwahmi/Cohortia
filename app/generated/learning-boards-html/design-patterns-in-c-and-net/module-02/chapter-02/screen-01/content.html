Factory Method Pattern
Delegate creation to subclasses so your core logic stays decoupled and extensible.
In this lesson
You will learn the four roles of the Factory Method, see a C# notification example, and practice refactoring a logger to use this pattern.
Why it matters
Reducing direct new calls prevents tight coupling, supports the Open/Closed Principle, and makes future extension safer and easier.

Concept sketch
Creator delegates — Concrete Creators produce Concrete Products
Product
Interface or abstract class that defines the contract for created objects.
Creator
Declares the factory method and may contain business logic that uses the Product.
Concrete Creator
Overrides the factory method to instantiate a Concrete Product.
