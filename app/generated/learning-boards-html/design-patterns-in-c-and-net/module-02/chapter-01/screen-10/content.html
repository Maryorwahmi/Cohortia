Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Design Trade-Offs
Comparison
Singleton
Fast global access, simple to use, but creates tight coupling and is hard to unit test or replace.
Dependency Injection (single instance)
Provides a single instance but keeps ownership explicit, easier to mock in tests, and more flexible lifecycle control.
Practical signals
  • Use Singleton when true global access is necessary and DI is not available.
  • Prefer DI when you need testability, lifecycle control, and loose coupling.
  • Refactor large Singletons that accrue extra responsibilities into focused services.
Reflection

When you see many calls to Instance scattered across a codebase, consider whether an injection-based design would make testing and maintenance easier.
