What to judge
You will practice the trade-off between adding new families and adding new product types. Adding families is low cost; adding product types requires changing many factory classes.
Good when
Families of widgets or database components
Avoid when
Only a single product type varies
Quick checklist
  • Do you need several types that must remain compatible?
  • Will new platforms be introduced often?
  • Is adding a new product type likely?
