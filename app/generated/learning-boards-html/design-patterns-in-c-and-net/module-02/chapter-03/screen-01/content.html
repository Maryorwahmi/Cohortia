Abstract Factory Pattern
Create families of related objects without specifying concrete classes.

When a system needs sets of compatible objects, the Abstract Factory lets you obtain a full suite of implementations that work together. This reduces the risk of mixing incompatible concrete types and centralizes platform-specific code.

Use Case
Cross-platform UI toolkits
Advantage
Product compatibility
Trade-off
More interfaces and classes

We will study roles, inspect C# examples for GUI and database factories, and complete a short lab to practice creating compatible product families.
