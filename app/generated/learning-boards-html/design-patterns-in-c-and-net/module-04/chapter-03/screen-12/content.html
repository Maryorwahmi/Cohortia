Best Practices
Testing and safe defaults
Make chains observable, testable, and safe to change.
Unit test wiring
Assert that requests sent to the entry handler reach expected targets and that SetNext is invoked correctly.
Default handler
Provide an end-of-chain handler that logs or raises an error for unhandled requests.
Metrics and limits
Track how many handlers fire and the time spent to detect long chains or hotspots.
Operational tips
  • Make handlers idempotent or guard side effects to prevent duplication.
  • Centralize chain wiring so configuration changes are easy and testable.
  • Use instrumentation to spot performance regressions from chain growth.
Key Takeaway

Make chains visible and testable. Defaults and metrics prevent silent failures and long-running traversals.
