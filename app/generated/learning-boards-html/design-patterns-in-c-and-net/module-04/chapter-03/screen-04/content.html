Core Concept
Why decouple senders and receivers?
Avoid making the client responsible for routing requests to specific handlers. Let handlers forward requests until one can act.
Explanation
  • The client sends to the chain entry point only; it doesn't know handler details.
  • Handlers contain focused logic and a reference to their successor to forward requests.
  • Adding, removing, or reordering handlers is a runtime operation, not a code rewrite.
Analogy
Support ticket escalations: Team A tries first, and if unresolved, passes to Team B, then to a specialist.
Key Takeaway

Decoupling via a chain reduces tight coupling and centralizes forwarding logic, making systems easier to extend.
