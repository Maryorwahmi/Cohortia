Comparison
Concrete handlers
ConsoleLogger, FileLogger, and EmailLogger illustrate different side effects for the same request.
ConsoleLogger
Quick output for Info and above; useful for development and immediate feedback.
FileLogger
Persists Warning and above to storage for auditing; typically writes to a file or persistent sink.
EmailLogger
Triggers alerts for Critical events; may be rate-limited or guarded to avoid alert storms.
Design considerations
  • Decide if requests may be handled by multiple handlers or only a single one.
  • Make side effects explicit and easily testable in each concrete handler.
  • Consider fallback or default handlers to avoid silent failures.
Example scenario
A Critical log could be written to console, appended to a file, and also trigger an email alert; keep each action isolated in its handler.
