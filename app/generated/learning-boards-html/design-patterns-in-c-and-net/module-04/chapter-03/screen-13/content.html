Architecture
Dynamic configuration & entry points
Rebuild or adjust the chain at runtime and expose alternate entry points for special requests.
Multiple entry points
Clients may start a request at different handlers to implement bypasses, for example urgent flows.
Runtime reordering
Maintain a configuration-driven list of handlers and rebuild the chain when settings change.
Safety concerns
Ensure thread safety during reconfiguration and test for consistent ordering across updates.
Concrete scenario
Insert a DatabaseLogger temporarily during maintenance to persist warnings, then remove it later without changing handler code.
Key Takeaway

Dynamic chains add power but require careful design for updates, ordering, and concurrency.
