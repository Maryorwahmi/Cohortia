Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › What to Remember
Inject Strategies
Keep the context free of concrete implementation details by receiving strategies through DI or constructors.
Test Each Strategy
Unit test algorithms directly with representative inputs for reliable behavior updates.
Avoid Over-engineering
If behavior is trivial and unlikely to change, a conditional may be simpler and clearer.
Closing Thought
When you see branching logic tied to behavior, ask whether Strategy will reduce risk and make tests simpler. That question alone will guide many good design choices.
