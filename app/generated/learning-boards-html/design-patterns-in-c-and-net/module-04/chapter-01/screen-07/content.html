Comparison: Conditionals, Strategy, Template Method
A quick decision guide to choose the right approach.
Conditionals
Best for trivial, stable choices. Low boilerplate but poor extensibility.
Strategy
Best when algorithms are independent, evolving, and need separate tests.
Template Method
Best when algorithms share a common skeleton but vary in specific steps.
Example — Use Strategy
Different shipping carriers with distinct formulas and independent logic.
Example — Use Template Method
Report generation where all reports follow a fixed pipeline but formatting steps differ.
Misconception
Strategy is not a panacea; pick the least complex pattern that satisfies maintainability and testing needs.
