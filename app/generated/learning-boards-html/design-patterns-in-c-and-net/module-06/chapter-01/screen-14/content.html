Key Concepts

Essential terms for understanding and avoiding bad design.

Anti-Pattern

A common, ineffective response to a recurring problem that is counterproductive.

God Object/Class

A class that centralizes too much responsibility, leading to high coupling and low cohesion.

Magic String/Number

Literal strings or numbers used directly in code without named constants or enums.

Summary Takeaway

Recognizing and avoiding these common anti-patterns is fundamental to building maintainable and robust software systems.
