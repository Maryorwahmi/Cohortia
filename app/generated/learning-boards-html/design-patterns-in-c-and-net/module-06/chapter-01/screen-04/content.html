Understanding Anti-Patterns

Common, yet ineffective, responses to recurring software problems.

Definition

An anti-pattern is a common response to a recurring problem that is usually ineffective and risks being highly counterproductive, leading to negative consequences.

Why They Emerge

Often arise from quick fixes, lack of experience, or pressure, without considering long-term impacts on the codebase.

Consequences

Lead to increased complexity, reduced maintainability, poor performance, and overall degradation of software quality over time.
