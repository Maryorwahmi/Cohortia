Building a Rich Domain Model

Encapsulating behavior within domain objects for integrity and clarity.

Definition

Domain objects that encapsulate both data and behavior, containing business logic relevant to their state and enforcing invariants.

Benefits

Promotes better encapsulation, data integrity, improved maintainability, and leverages polymorphism for flexible systems.

Tell, Don't Ask

A key principle: tell an object to do something rather than asking it for its data and then acting on that data externally.

Order (Rich Model)

AddItem(), Process(), CalculateTotal() (data + behavior)
