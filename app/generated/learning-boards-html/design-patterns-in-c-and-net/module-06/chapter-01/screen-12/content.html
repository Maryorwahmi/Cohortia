Avoiding Pitfalls in Refactoring

Strategies for safely addressing anti-patterns in existing codebases.

Common Mistakes
  • Dismissing anti-patterns as minor.
  • Fixing symptoms, not root causes.
  • Attempting large rewrites without tests.
Safety Note

Always ensure comprehensive tests are in place before making significant structural changes to existing code.

Refactoring Strategy

Prefer incremental refactoring, focusing on one anti-pattern at a time, rather than a complete rewrite.

Key Takeaway

Safe refactoring requires a cautious, incremental approach, backed by robust testing, to avoid introducing new issues.
