What are Code Smells?

Code smells are surface indications that usually correspond to a deeper problem in the system. They are not bugs, but rather symptoms of poor design choices that can lead to maintainability issues.

Long Method

A method that contains too many lines of code and handles multiple responsibilities. This often violates the Single Responsibility Principle (SRP).

Large Class

A class that attempts to do too much, having too many fields, methods, or responsibilities. Also a violation of SRP.

Impact & Solutions

Reduced Readability: Overly long methods or large classes are hard to read and understand quickly, increasing cognitive load for developers.

Difficult to Maintain: Changes in one part of a large method or class can have unintended side effects elsewhere, making maintenance risky and time-consuming.

Poor Reusability: Tightly coupled, large components are harder to extract and reuse in different contexts, leading to duplicate code.

Pro Tip

When you spot a 'Long Method' or 'Large Class', consider patterns like Strategy, Command, or Facade to break down responsibilities and improve modularity.
