Transaction Management

Saga Pattern

Coordinating Business Processes Across Multiple Services

Local Transactions

Each service performs its own atomic transaction and publishes an event.

Compensating Transactions

If a step fails, previous successful transactions are undone to maintain consistency.

Orchestration vs. Choreography

Sagas can be managed by a central coordinator or through event-driven reactions between services.

Key Takeaway

The Saga pattern is essential for achieving eventual consistency in distributed transactions across microservices.
