Best Practices

Avoiding Common Microservices Mistakes

Designing for Cohesion, Loose Coupling, and Observability

Distributed Monoliths

Avoid tightly coupled services that negate independent deployment benefits.

Network Overhead

Recognize and mitigate the latency and unreliability of inter-service network communication.

Lack of Observability

Implement robust monitoring, logging, and tracing to understand and diagnose distributed system behavior.

Key Takeaway

Successful microservices require careful design, robust operational practices, and a clear understanding of their inherent complexities.
