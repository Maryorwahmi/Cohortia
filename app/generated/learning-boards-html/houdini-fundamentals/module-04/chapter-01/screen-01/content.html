Introduction to Materials and Shaders
Understand PBR, the Principled Shader, texture maps, UVs, and common troubleshooting steps.
In this lesson

You will learn how materials define surface behavior, how shaders compute lighting, and how PBR parameters like metallic and roughness control appearance. You'll also apply texture maps and check UVs for correct mapping.

Why it matters

Good materials make procedural geometry readable in context, allow assets to look correct across renderers and engines, and reduce iterative guesswork when tuning appearance.
