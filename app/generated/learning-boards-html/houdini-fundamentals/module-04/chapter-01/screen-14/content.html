Assessment Questions
Question 1
You applied Base Color and Normal maps but the surface lacks metallic sheen. What are two likely causes and how would you troubleshoot?
Answer guidance: Check Metallic value or texture and Roughness value or texture. Verify texture paths and color-space for metallic maps.
Question 2
What is the purpose of UV coordinates and what happens if they are missing or incorrect?
Answer guidance: UVs map 3D points to 2D textures. Missing or bad UVs cause stretching, repeating, or incorrect sampling of texture detail.
Quick Grading Rubric
  • Correctly identifies metallic vs roughness causes: full credit.
  • Describes UV purpose and shows a checker test: full credit.
  • Explains color-space considerations for map types: bonus.
Key Takeaway

Use diagnostic swaps and simple visual tests to verify material behavior before complex edits.
