Texture Maps
Base Color = sRGB, Roughness/Metallic/Normal = linear non-color. Correct interpretation preserves shading fidelity.
UV Coordinates
Map 3D surface points to 2D. Use checker maps and UV Quick Shade to spot stretching and overlaps.
Troubleshooting
Use MAP: Material, Assign, Preview. Swap constants and inspect UVs to isolate issues quickly.
Summary Takeaway

Apply PBR practices, validate textures and UVs, and follow a clear assignment and preview loop to produce reliable materials.
