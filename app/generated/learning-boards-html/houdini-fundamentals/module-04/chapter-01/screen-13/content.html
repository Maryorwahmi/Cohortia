Common Problems & Fixes
Flat instead of Metallic
Check Metallic parameter/map and Roughness values. Metals require Metallic near 1 and low roughness for polished appearance.
Stretched or Repeated Texture
Inspect UVs with checker textures and UV Quick Shade. Fix overlaps and unequal scale with UV Layout tools.
Missing Texture
Verify material assignment at OBJ and confirm texture path in the Principled Shader.
Diagnostic Steps
  1. Plug a constant into the parameter to see the shader response.
  2. Use a checker map to reveal UV issues.
  3. Check color-space settings for the texture type.
  4. Confirm normal map orientation and type if bumps look inverted.
