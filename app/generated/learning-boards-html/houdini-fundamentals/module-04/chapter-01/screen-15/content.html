Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Material
Defines a surface's color, roughness, metallic, and normal detail.
Shader
The program that computes lighting and shading from material inputs.
PBR
A workflow that ensures realistic and consistent results under varied lighting.
Summary Takeaway

Treat materials as structured data, use shaders to compute light response, and follow PBR practice to keep results consistent and predictable.
