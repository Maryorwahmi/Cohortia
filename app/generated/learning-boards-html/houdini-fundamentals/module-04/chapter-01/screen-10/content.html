Process Flow › Chapter 4.1 › Assigning Materials to Geometry
Assign at OBJ Level
Select the geometry object, open the Render tab, and pick the material path from the Material parameter.
Material SOP Option
Use a Material SOP inside the SOP network when you need per-face assignments or group-based materials.
Path Verification
Ensure the selected path matches the MAT node name and location, e.g., /mat/my_prop_material.
Preview Loop
After assignment: add a Camera and Environment Light, switch to Render View, and iteratively adjust shader parameters while observing the result.
Quick Troubleshoot
If the material does not show, check assignment path, ensure Render View is active, and confirm UVs exist for textured maps.
Per-Face Material
Use groups or attributes with a Material SOP to apply different materials to primitives within the same geometry object.
