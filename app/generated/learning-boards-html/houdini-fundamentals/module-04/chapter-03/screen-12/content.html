Key Concepts
Three compact reminders from this chapter.
Light Types
Point, Spot, Area, Environment: pick by physical behavior and intended effect.
HDRI
Environment maps provide reflections and global illumination; combine with local lights to sculpt form.
Parameters & Decay
Tune color, intensity, exposure and keep quadratic decay for realistic falloff; test with small renders.
Summary Takeaway
Think in roles: environment for context, area/spot for sculpting, use light linking to isolate and optimize.
