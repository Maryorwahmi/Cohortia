Core Concept
Why lighting defines realism
Light sculpts highlights, midtones, and shadows that communicate material and shape.
  • Highlights show surface gloss and curvature.
  • Shadows provide depth and anchor objects to the scene.
  • HDRI offers global cues that small lights cannot reproduce.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize light roles: key for form, fill for shadow control, rim for separation, environment for context.

Illustrative examples
Small Point
Sharp specular highlight, crisp shadow edges — suggests hard, glossy surface.
Large Area
Broad highlight, soft shadow falloff — suggests softer material and gentle illumination.
Environment (HDRI)
Provides reflections and consistent ambient color that tie objects into the scene.
Common mistake
Using many high-intensity lights without role leads to flat images and wasted render time.
Pro tip: Assign each light a clear purpose and test by disabling all but one to understand its contribution.
