Definition
Environment Light & HDRI
Load an HDRI into the Environment Map parameter to provide rich global lighting and reflections.
Key controls: Map file, Rotation, Intensity/Exposure. Rotate the map to reposition bright areas and adjust intensity to balance with local lights.
Example
Use a neutral studio HDRI at low intensity for ambient fill, then add an area key for form. Verify reflections on metallic objects for realism.
Architecture of usage
HDRI Map
360° image with high dynamic range values for accurate lighting.
Environment Light Node
Wraps the HDRI around the scene and feeds lighting/reflection data to the renderer.
Rotation
Orients the HDRI to place dominant highlights where needed.
Intensity / Exposure
Controls HDRI brightness relative to local lights.
Tip: combine HDRI for ambient information with local lights for controlled sculpting of form and reflections.
