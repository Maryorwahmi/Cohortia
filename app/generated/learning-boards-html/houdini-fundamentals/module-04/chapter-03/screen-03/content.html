Learning Objectives — Practical Checks
Simple, repeatable tests to confirm your lighting choices.
01 Swap test: Point vs Area
  • Render with point light then area light and compare shadow softness.
  • Confirm area light reduces hard-edge shadows and creates softer falloff.
Milestone: softer shadows with area present
02 HDRI reflection check
Load an HDRI, place a metallic sphere, rotate environment map, and verify distinct reflected details.
Milestone: environment details visible in reflections
03 Parameter render trio
Run three renders varying intensity, exposure, and decay to see their effects.
04 Light Linker toggle
Use Light Linker to exclude a light from the background and verify only your subject receives the key light.
