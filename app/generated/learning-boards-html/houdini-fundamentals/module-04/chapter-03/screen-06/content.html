Point Light
Omnidirectional
Emits equally in all directions from a single point. Good for small, bright sources and sharp highlights.
Common use: lamp bulb, spark.
Spot Light
Conical & directional
Shaped cone with cone angle and penumbra. Use for focused beams and rim highlights.
Common use: flashlight, stage spot.
Area Light
Surface emitter
Emits from a defined surface. Larger size softens shadows and creates realistic highlights for materials.
Common use: window light, softbox.
Example Scenario
Portrait model: Area light as key for soft facial shadows, area fill at low intensity, spot as thin rim behind subject for separation. If reflections are needed, add an HDRI environment.
Common Mistake
Relying on intensity to soften shadows instead of increasing emitter size creates unrealistic highlights.
