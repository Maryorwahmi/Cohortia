Core Concept
Decay & Quadratic Decay
Quadratic decay reduces intensity proportional to distance squared, producing natural-looking falloff.
Practical check: place a neutral gray sphere at multiple distances from a light and compare brightness. With quadratic decay, brightness will fall quickly with distance; without it, brightness will stay uniform.
Example & pitfalls
Example: set an area key at moderate intensity with quadratic decay enabled. Objects near the key will have strong modeling light while distant props remain subtly lit by the environment.
Pitfall
Turning decay off and then raising intensity creates flat illumination and hides depth cues.
Try: toggle decay on and off during single-light renders to directly observe its impact.
