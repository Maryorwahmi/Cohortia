Definition
Key parameters
Understand how color, intensity, exposure and decay change rendered results and how to balance them.
Light {
  type: "area"
  color: "#ffffff"
  intensity: 1500
  exposure: 1
  decay: "quadratic"
  size: [1.2, 0.8]
}
Explanation & example
  • Color affects both diffuse tint and reflections; use warm or cool hues intentionally.
  • Intensity sets raw emitted energy; exposure shifts perceived brightness for camera matching.
  • Quadratic decay models real-world falloff; disable only for stylized effects.
Key Takeaway
Adjust intensity then exposure, and keep quadratic decay enabled for realistic distance falloff unless you need a stylized look.
Misconception check: If shadows remain equally bright as you move objects away from the light, decay is likely disabled.
