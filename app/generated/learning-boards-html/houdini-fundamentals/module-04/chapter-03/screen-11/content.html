Hands-on Activity
Three-point lighting with HDRI
Follow these steps in your scene and render after each to learn contributions.
Step 1 — Environment (Fill)
Load HDRI, set intensity low (0.3–0.6), rotate to position main highlights for reflections.
Step 2 — Key (Area)
Place above and to the side, size to soften shadows, set intensity according to scene scale.
Step 3 — Fill (Area)
Opposite side at 20–30% of key to soften shadows without flattening shape.
Step 4 — Rim (Spot)
Place behind subject, tune cone angle and penumbra to create a thin edge highlight.
Key Takeaway
Render iteratively after each step to learn how each light shapes form. Use Light Linker to isolate rim or key from background when needed.
