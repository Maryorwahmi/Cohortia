What to Remember
Three quick rules to guide every lighting pass.
Role First
Give each light a purpose and disable extras that don't serve the image.
HDRI + Local
Use HDRI for context and reflections, and local lights to model form and emphasis.
Iterate & Isolate
Render one light at a time to learn its effect and ensure decay and exposure behave as expected.
Keep these rules visible in every lighting session to maintain consistent, efficient look development.
