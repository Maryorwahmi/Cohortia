Definition › Chapter 4.2 › Ambient Occlusion
What AO does

Ambient Occlusion darkens areas where geometry occludes ambient light. It is baked or generated and applied as a grayscale multiplier to add contact shadow and dirt placement cues.

• Multiply AO with Base Color to subtly darken crevices.
• Use AO as a mask to add localized roughness or grime layers.
• Keep AO in linear space; do not treat it as a color texture.
Practical example
Import AO.exr from Substance Painter and multiply it into the Base Color input or use it to blend a dirt material in a Material Blend node. Inspect at varying camera distances to ensure it enhances but does not overpower the material.
Tip
Use a blurred AO for gentle darkening and a sharper AO for tight crevice emphasis.
