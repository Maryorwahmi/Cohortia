Comparison › Chapter 4.2 › Normal vs Displacement
Normal Map
  • • Encodes perturbed normals; cheap and effective for small detail.
  • • Does not alter silhouette or real geometry.
  • • Best for game assets and fine grain details.
Connect to shader normal input and set Normal Map Type to Tangent Space when appropriate.
Displacement Map
  • • Grayscale map that physically displaces geometry.
  • • Requires subdivided or dense meshes for correct silhouettes.
  • • Ideal for deep cracks, carved surfaces, or cinematic close-ups.
Connect in the Displacement tab and ensure geometry is subdivided or use a Displace SOP before render.
Key Takeaway
Use normals for cheap detail, displacement for silhouette and deep relief.
Pro-tip
Preview displacement at several subdivision levels to balance detail and performance.
Pitfall
Applying displacement with sparse geometry yields aliasing and incorrect silhouettes.
