Assessment › Chapter 4.2 › Stone Wall Plan
Planned steps
1) Subdivide wall geometry
2) Connect displacement map for deep cracks
3) Apply normal map for fine surface detail
4) Create moss principledshader (green, high roughness)
5) Generate mask: AO * Worley + curvature -> fit -> mask
6) MaterialBlend(stone, moss, mask)
7) Verify color-spaces and render
Design notes
Drive moss placement with AO and curvature for plausible growth; use displacement only where silhouette change is essential and avoid excessive subdivision to limit render cost.
Key Takeaway
Combine displacement, normals, and layered masks driven by AO/curvature to achieve authentic weathered surfaces.
