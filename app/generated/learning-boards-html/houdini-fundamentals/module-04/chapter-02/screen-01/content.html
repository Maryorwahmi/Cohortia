Advanced Material Creation and Texturing
Layer blends, procedural masks, VEX noise patterns, displacement and external texture pipelines.
In this lesson

You will combine multiple Principled Shaders with Material Blend and procedural masks, learn VEX noise nodes for textures, apply displacement versus normals, and import PBR maps from Substance Painter with correct color spaces.

Why it matters

Layered materials give realistic variation and control. Procedural masks avoid tiling and maintain infinite resolution. Choosing between normal and displacement balances performance and silhouette fidelity.
