Best Practice › Chapter 4.2 › Bake vs Procedural
Keep procedural when...
  • • You require infinite resolution and iterative tweaks.
  • • You need many variations without storing large image sets.
  • • The renderer or pipeline supports the shader complexity.
Bake when...
  • • Downstream tools or game engines require image textures.
  • • You need to reduce shader cost for real-time performance.
  • • You want consistent, shareable maps across software.
Key Takeaway

Choose procedural for iteration and detail; bake for performance and cross-tool compatibility. Consider hybrid approaches like baking heavy passes only.
