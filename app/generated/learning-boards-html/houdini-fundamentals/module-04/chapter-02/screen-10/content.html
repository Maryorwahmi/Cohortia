Step-by-step
1) Create Grid/Box, UV unwrap and subdivide
2) principledshader_base_metal (Metallic=1, Roughness=0.1)
3) principledshader_rust (BaseColor=orangey, Metallic=0, Roughness=0.8)
4) materialblend: Input1=base_metal, Input2=rust_material
5) noise (Type: Worley, Frequency:10) -> fit (0.3->0.7 -> 0->1)
6) materialblend.mask = fit.output
7) Assign materialblend to geo and render
Iterate by adjusting noise and fit, or add turbulence to distort edges for more organic decay patterns.
Example scenario
A factory floor panel: polished metal shows through where paint has flaked. Use a Worley noise for flake islands and fractal noise for surface grit.
Check
If rust appears uniform at all scales, increase noise layers and introduce distortion.
