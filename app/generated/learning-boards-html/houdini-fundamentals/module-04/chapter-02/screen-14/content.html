Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Layered Materials
Combine full shaders and blend properties using masks to reflect real material transitions.
Procedural Masks
Use VEX noise nodes and remaps to create non-repeating, tweakable masks for blends and parameter variation.
Normals vs Displacement
Normals fake surface lighting; displacement changes geometry and silhouette — choose based on visual need and cost.
Summary
Blend materials with purposeful masks, use procedural noise for flexible detail, and manage textures and color space correctly when importing external maps.
