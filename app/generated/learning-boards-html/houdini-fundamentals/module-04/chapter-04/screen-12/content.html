Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Troubleshooting Renders

Noise

Cause: low samples, noisy shaders or bright small lights. Fix: increase pixel samples, denoise, or optimize shaders/lights.

Black frames

Cause: missing lights, wrong camera, or unassigned materials. Fix: check ROP camera, visibility flags and material assignments.

Missing AOVs

Cause: not adding correct Type or using a viewer that hides channels. Fix: add Extra Image Planes with correct Types and verify in MPlay.

Long render times

Cause: high samples, heavy geometry, volumetrics. Fix: use proxies, reduce samples for previews, or scale to a render farm.

Quick diagnostic
Render single frame at low samples, toggle lights/materials, then increase settings where the problem is located.
