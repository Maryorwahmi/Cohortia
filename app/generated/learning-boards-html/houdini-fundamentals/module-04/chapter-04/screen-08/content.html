Sampling controls

Karma uses pixel samples as a unified quality knob. Mantra provides per-component sampling for targeted optimization.

Preview: Low samples, fast iteration.
Final: Higher samples, less noise.
Optimization: Tackle noisy shaders or lighting instead of only raising samples.
Example
Preview 16 samples shows grain; final 128 samples smooths reflections but costs time.
Workflow
  1. Render low-sample previews to check composition.
  2. Identify noisy components (glossy, small bright lights, shaders).
  3. Optimize parameters or increase samples selectively for final render.
Key Takeaway
Control noise by balancing samples and scene optimization; don’t rely on samples alone.
