Render engines and workflow

Rendering converts your 3D scene into pixels. This module focuses on choosing between Karma and Mantra, configuring a ROP in OUT, and producing multi-channel EXR files for compositing.

Why this matters
  • Correct renderer choice speeds iteration and ensures compatibility with studio pipelines.
  • AOVs let you refine final images without costly re-renders.
  • Proper ROP setup automates sequences and keeps outputs organized.
Karma
Modern USD-based renderer. CPU & XPU modes for speed.
Mantra
Legacy CPU renderer known for flexibility and deep integration.
Both can produce high-quality output; Karma XPU adds GPU acceleration for faster feedback and final renders when available.
