Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Karma vs Mantra
Comparison

Karma

Modern, USD-based renderer. Available in CPU and XPU modes. Favored for faster interactive feedback and GPU acceleration when available.

Strengths: Speed with XPU, progressive rendering, active development.
When to choose: Fast iterations, cinematics, when GPU is available.
Example scenario
Rendering marketing stills with heavy lighting where GPU acceleration cuts iteration time.
Comparison

Mantra

Legacy CPU-only renderer with mature, deep feature support and wide historical use in pipelines. Known for flexibility and stability with older shaders.

Strengths: Broad feature set, proven results, compatibility with older assets.
When to choose: Projects relying on Mantra-specific shaders or CPU-only farms.
Gotcha
Don’t assume parity; Karma may lack some Mantra-specific behavior. Test a short scene first.
