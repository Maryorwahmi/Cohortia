Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Activity: Render with Karma

Guided steps

Follow these steps to produce a single-frame, multi-channel EXR with Karma.

Scene prep
Textured sphere, three-point light, camera in place.
Create Karma ROP
Switch to OUT and add karma node.
Set Output
Output Picture: C:/temp/my_first_karma_render.$F4.exr, Resolution: 1280x720.
Sampling
Pixel Samples: 64 for a balanced preview.
Add AOVs
Images tab → Add Extra Image Plane: P, N, Z, diffuse_color.
Render
Click Render to Disk and wait for the EXR to be written.
Verification
Open the EXR in MPlay and verify Beauty, P, N, Z and diffuse_color channels are available.
