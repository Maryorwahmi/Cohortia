Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Render to Disk vs Render View

Render View

Interactive, progressive feedback ideal for lighting and material adjustments. Fast iteration but not always an exact match to ROP output.

Use when:
  • Tweaking lights, exposure, or materials.
  • Needing immediate visual feedback before committing to a disk render.

Render to Disk

Writes final frames to Output Picture. Use for batch renders and final deliverables that require consistent naming and AOVs.

Use when:
  • Producing final frames or sequences for compositing.
  • Needing reproducible outputs with frame padding and multi-channel EXRs.
Tip
After interactive adjustments, run a single-frame Render to Disk to confirm ROP settings match your expectations.
