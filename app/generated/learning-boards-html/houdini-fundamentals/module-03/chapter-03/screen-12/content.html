Key Concepts
Three compact reminders to guide your day-to-day VEX work.
Types reflect meaning
Pick the type that represents the data's role: index, measure, spatial, or text.
Control conversions
Implicit conversions are helpful but may be lossy; cast explicitly when precision or indexing matters.
Respect attribute class
Use prim, point, detail to read across classes and set*attrib for explicit writes.
