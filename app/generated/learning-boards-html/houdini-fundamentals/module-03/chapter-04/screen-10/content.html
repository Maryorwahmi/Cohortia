Concept

Attribute Interpolation

Interpolation calculates intermediate values during transfer or promotion. Choices like Blend Width and Average determine smoothness versus fidelity.

Smoothness
Large blend widths and averaging create smooth transitions but may blur details.
Fidelity
Small blend widths and per-element mapping preserve local detail but risk patchiness.
Practical guidance
  • Decide whether you need smoothness or exactness for the attribute in question.
  • For weights and lighting data, prefer smooth interpolation and validate in the renderer or game engine.
  • For masks or crisp decals, use tighter thresholds or copy by index when possible.
Key Takeaway

Interpolation changes semantics: choose methods that preserve the attribute property—sum, mean, or local detail—you need for downstream use.
