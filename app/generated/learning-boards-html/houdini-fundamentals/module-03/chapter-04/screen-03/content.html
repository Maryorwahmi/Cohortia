05
Distance & Blend Control

Practice adjusting Distance Threshold and Blend Width until the projected attribute covers destination points without bleeding into unrelated areas. Visual checks and small test values help isolate proper ranges.

Verified Competency Milestone
06
Promotion Methods

Compare Average, Sum, Min, and Max when promoting. Know that Average produces smooth per-primitive values, while Sum may be useful for aggregated counts or total weights.

Verified Competency Milestone
07
Index & ID Matching

Use Attribute Copy when meshes share ordering or a custom ID attribute. Confirm point counts and IDs before copying to avoid silent misalignments.

Verified Competency Milestone
08
Interpolation Awareness

Understand how averaging or blend falloffs affect visual results like vertex colors or weights. Anticipate smoothing when moving from finer to coarser classes and plan accordingly.
