Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Performance Metrics
Definitions & Why They Matter
  • Polygon Count: Number of triangles in the mesh; higher counts increase GPU load.
  • Draw Calls: Number of separate draw operations; many meshes or materials raise CPU overhead.
  • Texture Memory: Size of texture assets loaded to GPU; large maps increase VRAM usage.
Common Mistake
Relying on procedural detail alone and forgetting that the engine uses the final baked mesh; procedural is for authoring, not always for runtime.
Concrete Example
Imagine an HDA that generates a cluster of rocks with per-rock micro-detail. You can either keep each rock as full geometry, which increases draw calls and polygons, or bake normal/detail maps and merge meshes to lower draw calls while preserving visual fidelity.
High Detail
Many meshes • High polygon count • High draw calls
Optimized
Merged meshes • Reduced polys • Baked maps
Key Takeaway

Measure what your HDA emits, and design to match the engine’s polygon, draw-call, and texture budgets.
