Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Material & Masks
Options for Material Assignment
  • Create material groups (mat_brick, mat_mortar) for direct export.
  • Assign primitive attribute with material ID for engine mapping.
  • Bake material masks as textures (grayscale or RGBA) to blend in engine materials.
Example
Use Attribute VOP to paint zones, export a mask via Labs Maps Baker and include both mesh and mask in the export. The engine uses the mask to blend materials without manual setup.
Key Takeaway

Provide explicit material outputs (groups, attributes, or masks) so the engine import process is predictable and repeatable.
