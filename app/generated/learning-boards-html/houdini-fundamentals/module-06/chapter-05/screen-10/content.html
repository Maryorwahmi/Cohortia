HDA Structure Checklist
  • Modularity: one job per HDA.
  • Curated Parameters: expose only safe controls.
  • LODs: include PolyReduce-based LOD outputs and expose targets.
  • Collision: generate simplified collision meshes or groups.
  • Documentation: describe purpose, acceptable parameter ranges, and export steps.
Example
A prop HDA exposes a single 'detail' slider that feeds a PolyReduce target and a noise strength. The rest of the graph remains internal and optimized. The HDA outputs three files: visual mesh, collision mesh and a baked AO texture.
Key Takeaway

Design with constraints: modular HDAs with limited, documented parameters lead to predictable engine assets.
