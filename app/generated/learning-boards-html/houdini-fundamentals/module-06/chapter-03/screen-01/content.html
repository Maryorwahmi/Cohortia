HDA Versioning, Locking, and Distribution
How to manage HDA evolution, protect internal networks, and share assets across teams without breaking scenes.
In this lesson

Version your HDAs, use locking to prevent accidental edits, and distribute via HOUDINI_PATH or shared libraries so instances remain resolved.

Why it matters

Good versioning and distribution policies prevent pipeline breaks, make rollbacks possible, and reduce wasted debugging time across the team.

Quick takeaway

Treat .hda files like code: version, save, and share via a controlled path so scenes stay stable.
