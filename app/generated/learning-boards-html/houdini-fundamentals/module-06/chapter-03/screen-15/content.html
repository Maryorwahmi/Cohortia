HOUDINI_PATH
Environment variable that tells Houdini where to find .hda files.
Unresolved HDA
Occurs when the instance cannot find its definition; repair by restoring or repointing the .hda.
Best Practices
Combine version control, documentation, and a single owner to maintain asset quality.
Parting Thought

Treat HDAs as shared tools: make them discoverable, versioned, documented, and maintained by a clear owner to keep production stable.
