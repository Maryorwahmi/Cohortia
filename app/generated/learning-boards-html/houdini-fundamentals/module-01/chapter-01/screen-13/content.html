Common Mistakes and Pro Tips
Practical habits that keep your procedural assets robust and shareable.
Scope errors
Don’t attempt mesh edits at Object level. Conversely, avoid applying global transforms inside SOPs that should be handled at Object level.
Parameter exposure
Expose only the parameters necessary for iteration to reduce accidental breakage and make assets more usable.
Network hygiene
Name nodes, use subnetworks, and add notes. This makes handover to other artists smoother.
Pro Tip

Use color and comments in the Network Editor to mark important checkpoints and decision points in your setup.

Mini checklist
  • Are node names descriptive?
  • Is parameter exposure minimal and clear?
  • Are transforms applied at the correct level?
Practice prompt
Review a small network and apply naming, comments, and at least one subnetwork to tidy its structure.
