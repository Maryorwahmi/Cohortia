Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Framing Shortcuts
What framing does
Spacebar + F centers the view on a selected object and sets the tumble pivot there. Spacebar + G frames all scene objects so you can recover orientation.
Why it matters
Framing makes pivots predictable and camera motion repeatable, which is critical for precise placement and animation setups.
Practice
  1. Select a leg and press Spacebar + F; confirm the leg is centered and tumble rotates around it.
  2. Make a fine adjustment, then press Spacebar + G to view the full table and confirm relationships.
  3. Repeat for each leg to create consistent camera pivots for placement.
Key Takeaway

Use framing to set reliable pivots and regain orientation quickly instead of guessing camera positions.
