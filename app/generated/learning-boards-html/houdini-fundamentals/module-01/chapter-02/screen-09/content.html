Handle types
  • Axis arrows: constrain movement to a single axis.
  • Plane squares: move along two axes simultaneously (XY, XZ, YZ).
  • Rotation rings: rotate around the chosen axis smoothly.
Example
Use the plane square between X and Z to slide the leg along the tabletop plane without changing height.
Mode awareness
Manipulators can operate in local or world space. Local mode aligns handles to the object's orientation, which may be rotated by a parent. World mode locks to scene axes. If movement behaves unexpectedly, check the manipulator mode and parent transforms.
Practice switching between T, R, and E while observing axis alignment and plane behavior. This will build an intuitive sense of when to use plane drags versus axis locks for precise placement.
Key Takeaway

Manipulators are fast and visual, but always confirm coordinate space and parent rotation to avoid unintended offsets.
