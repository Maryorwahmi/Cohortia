Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Viewport Navigation
How it works
  1. Tumble: Alt + LMB. Rotates around the current pivot or framed selection.
  2. Pan: Alt + MMB. Slides view without rotating the camera orientation.
  3. Zoom: Alt + RMB or mouse wheel. Moves camera toward or away from the pivot.
Practice sequence: Frame the tabletop, tumble to a corner, pan to center, zoom to adjust.
Common mistake
Confusing world dolly with pivot zoom; always frame your selection to control the pivot before close inspection.
Pro tip
Use Spacebar + F to temporarily re-center the pivot on a selected object for precise tumbling.
Practice steps
Step 1 — Frame and Tumble
Select a corner of your tabletop, press Spacebar + F, then hold Alt + LMB and rotate around that corner until the angle is clear.
Step 2 — Pan and Zoom
Hold Alt + MMB to slide the view so the corner is centered, then Alt + RMB to zoom close and align the leg origin precisely.
Key Takeaway

Control your pivot with framing hotkeys and use Alt + mouse to inspect and position elements quickly.
