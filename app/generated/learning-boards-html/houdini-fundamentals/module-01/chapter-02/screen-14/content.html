Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Quick Reminders
Check object center
Set Center Y so geometry rests on the ground plane and avoids clipping.
Consistent units
Adopt a scale convention; 1 Houdini unit as 1 meter is common for game assets.
Recover view
Use Spacebar + G to frame all objects when you lose orientation.
Final takeaway

These reminders prevent common mistakes and make your scenes predictable and export-ready. Keep them in mind as you build and iterate.
