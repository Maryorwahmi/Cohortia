Forces and the POP Solver
How forces change particle velocity and how the solver integrates them.
Core forces
  • POP Wind — directional push
  • POP Drag — simulates air resistance
  • POP Attract — pulls toward a point or object
  • Gravity Force — global downward acceleration (added in DOP)
Solver tuning
Adjust solver substeps to prevent tunneling and improve collision accuracy at the cost of performance.
Example and checks
Example
Emit particles downward with gravity, add small drag to slow them, and use POP Wind to bias flow when pouring into a tilted container.
Tip: Increase substeps for high-speed impacts
Pitfall: Too much drag makes motion look unnatural
Key Takeaway

Combine gravity, wind and drag thoughtfully and tune solver substeps to balance fidelity and performance.
