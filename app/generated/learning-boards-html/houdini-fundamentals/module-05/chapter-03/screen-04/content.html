Core Concept
What are POPs?
Particle Operators for simulating many points with attributes and rules.
Quick definition
POP nodes change particle attributes like position and velocity to create motion across frames.
Why it matters
POPs let you simulate large numbers of actors efficiently, producing complex visuals from simple rules.
Common misconception
Particles are not small rigid bodies; they behave like points with attributes. Expect different collision and mass behavior than RBDs.
Illustrative cards
Emitter
POP Source provides birth, position, and initial velocity of particles.
Solver
POP Solver advances particles and applies force results each frame.
Forces
POP Wind, POP Drag and POP Attract change particle motion over time.
Grains
POP Grains add volume and repulsion, enabling piles and cohesive flows.
Key Takeaway

POPs orchestrate many points through emitters, solvers and forces; think attributes and rules rather than individual particles.
