Key solver controls
Substeps and Collision Iterations control stability; friction and bounce shape contact response.
Substeps
More substeps sample motion more finely to avoid tunneling. Increase in small increments.
Collision Iterations
More iterations yield better contact resolution at extra cost.
Friction & Bounce
Control sliding and restitution to get believable contact interactions.
Key Takeaway

Use solver controls incrementally. Start small and increase until artifacts disappear rather than applying large values immediately.
