Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
RBDs
Simulation of solid non-deforming pieces interacting by forces and collisions.
Voronoi Fracture
Procedurally breaks geometry into many pieces using a point scatter as seeds.
Assemble (Pack)
Packs each fracture piece into a single primitive for efficient simulation.
Summary
Prepare pieces, pack them, and choose collisions wisely — this pipeline yields simulations that are both believable and manageable to compute.
