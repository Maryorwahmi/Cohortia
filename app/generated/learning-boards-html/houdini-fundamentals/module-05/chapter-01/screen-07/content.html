Solvers — the engines that compute physics
RBD, POP, and FLIP solvers each handle a type of simulation and expose tuning parameters.
RBD Solver
Handles rigid body dynamics and collision resolution. Paired with RBD Packed Objects.
POP Solver
Manages particle emission, lifespan and forces. Use with particle objects in DOPs.
FLIP Solver
Designed for liquids: grid-particle coupling, pressure, and incompressibility handling.
Practical example
A box becomes an RBD Packed Object and is solved by an RBD Solver with Gravity Force. If you used a POP Solver instead you would not get rigid-body collision behavior.
Pro tip

Match solver and object types early. When experimenting, copy the scene and swap solvers to compare behaviors rather than reauthoring geometry.
