Key Concepts
What to Remember
Three production-focused reminders.
Watertight collisions
Necessary for FLIP to prevent particle leaks and maintain correct interactions.
Rasterize attributes
Volume Rasterize Attributes must run so Pyro has density and temperature volumes to evolve.
Match resolution
Align particle separation for FLIP and division size for Pyro to your visual needs and compute budget.
Final takeaway
Validate sources early with low-resolution previews, fix geometry and attribute issues first, then increase resolution for final renders.
