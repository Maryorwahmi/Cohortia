Process Flow
Preparing Pyro sources with Volume Rasterize Attributes
Convert per-primitive or per-point attributes into voxel volumes used by Pyro.
Example SOP sequence
# SOPs
Create Sphere
Attribute Create: name= density, value=1
Attribute Create: name= temperature, value=300
Volume Rasterize Attributes: attributes = density, temperature, division size = 0.1

# Feed volumes into Pyro Source and then to a Pyro Solver in DOPs
Checks and tips
  • Confirm attribute names match between SOPs and the Pyro Source.
  • Preview generated volumes to ensure coverage of the intended region.
  • Adjust division size to trade off detail and simulation cost.
Why this step matters
Volume Rasterize Attributes is the bridge between geometry-driven intent and volumetric simulation data. Without it, Pyro has no source data to evolve.
