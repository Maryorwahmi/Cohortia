Comparing visual output and use cases
Match technical choices to your artistic goal and budget simulation resolution accordingly.
FLIP • Visual cues
Clear surface, droplets, realistic splashes; meshed surface for rendering.
Pyro • Visual cues
Soft, volumetric density, rising motion, temperature-driven buoyancy; no surface mesh required.
Combined approach
Use FLIP for the liquid body and Pyro for mist or fire. Balance resolutions to control compute cost.
Closing reflection
Decide which element is primary: fluid surface or surrounding volumetrics. That decision determines solver choice, parameter focus, and resolution budgeting for production-ready results.
