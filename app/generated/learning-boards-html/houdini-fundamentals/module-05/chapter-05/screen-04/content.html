Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Forces: What They Do
Definition
Force node as a field
A force node applies a vector influence to simulation elements. Parameters control magnitude, direction, and variation.
Strength — scales the acceleration applied by the node.
Direction — the axis or vector the force pushes along.
Noise — adds organic, non-uniform perturbation to motion.
Example: POP Wind
Settings: Amplitude = 2, Direction = 1,0,0. The wind biases particles along X so they enter and slide along container walls. Observe particle velocity vectors to verify the effect.
Pro tip
When adding strong forces, increase solver substeps or reduce force spike to avoid tunneling through thin geometry.
Key Takeaway

Match the force type to the simulation context and then tune strength and noise. Particle forces differ in parameterization from rigid-body forces.
