Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Rigid-Body Forces
Comparison
Fan vs Turbulent vs Magnet
Fan Force — localized directional push; imparts linear and angular momentum.
Turbulent Force — adds noise-driven chaotic motion; good for secondary movement in debris.
Magnet Force — attracts or repels; useful for specialized attraction effects.
Example
Use a Fan Force to nudge fractured pieces out of a pile, then add low-magnitude Turbulent Force to break up mechanical-looking motion and create believable scatter.
Common Mistake
Applying RBD-level strengths as if they were POP settings can cause numerical instability. Reduce strength and increase substeps if contacts fail.
Key Takeaway

Use RBD forces designed for mass and inertia. Tune conservatively and check collisions and substeps after changes.
