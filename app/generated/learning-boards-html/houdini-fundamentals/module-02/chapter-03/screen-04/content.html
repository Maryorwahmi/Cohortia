Understanding Geometry Attributes

Data that defines your digital assets.

What are Attributes?

Attributes are pieces of data attached to various components of your geometry. They act as metadata, describing properties like position, color, normal direction, or any custom data you wish to create. They are the core mechanism for dynamic and responsive procedural rules.

Key Takeaway

Attributes are the fundamental data points that drive all procedural effects in Houdini, making geometry dynamic and controllable.

Attribute Component Types

Points

Data attached to individual vertices (e.g., position).

Primitives

Data attached to faces/polygons (e.g., material ID).

Vertices

Data at primitive corners (e.g., UV coordinates).

Detail

Data for the entire geometry (e.g., total area).
