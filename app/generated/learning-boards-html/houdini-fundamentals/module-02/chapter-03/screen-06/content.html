The Geometry Spreadsheet

Your window into geometry data.

Inspect All Attributes

The Geometry Spreadsheet provides a tabular view of all attributes on your geometry. It's essential for understanding and debugging your procedural networks.

  • View data for Points, Vertices, Primitives, and Detail.
  • See attribute names, types, and their current values.
  • Crucial for verifying VEX expressions and data flow.
Key Takeaway

Regularly checking the Geometry Spreadsheet is vital for understanding and debugging attribute data in your Houdini scenes.
