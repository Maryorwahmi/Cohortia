Deforming Geometry with VEX

Creating wave effects using point positions.

Wave Deformation on a Grid

Displace points in the Y-axis based on their X-position using a sine wave. This creates a dynamic, undulating surface.

@P.y += sin(@P.x * 5) * 0.1; // Displace points in Y
Key Takeaway

Manipulating the @P attribute with mathematical functions in VEX enables powerful and complex geometric deformations.

VEX Components Explained
  • @P.y: Y-component of the point's position.
  • @P.x: X-component of the point's position.
  • sin(): Trigonometric sine function for wave patterns.
  • * 5: Controls the frequency of the wave.
  • * 0.1: Controls the amplitude (height) of the wave.
Real-world Connection

This technique is used to create realistic water surfaces, cloth simulations, or organic terrain deformations in games and visual effects.
