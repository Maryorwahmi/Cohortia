Key Built-in Attributes in Houdini

Standard data for position, color, normals, and more.

@P (Position)

A vector (x, y, z) attached to points, defining their location in 3D space. This is fundamental for all geometry.

@Cd (Color)

A vector (red, green, blue) for points or vertices, used to assign visual color to your geometry.

@N (Normal)

A vector (x, y, z) on points or vertices, indicating the surface direction, critical for lighting and shading.

@uv (UV Coordinates)

A vector (u, v) on vertices, used for mapping textures and defining how images wrap around your geometry.

@pscale (Point Scale)

A float attribute on points, commonly used to control the individual scale of instances when copying geometry.

Key Takeaway

Houdini's built-in attributes are the standard language for describing and manipulating geometry, forming the basis of procedural workflows.
