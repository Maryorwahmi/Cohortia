Key Concepts: Attributes & Inspection

Essential terms from this chapter.

Attributes

Pieces of data attached to geometry components that define their properties.

Attribute Types

Data can be attached to Points, Primitives, Vertices, or the entire Detail level.

Geometry Spreadsheet

The primary tool for inspecting and understanding all attribute data on your geometry.

Attribute Create SOP

A node for creating or modifying attributes with constant, uniform values.

Chapter Summary

Attributes are the data backbone of Houdini's proceduralism. Understanding their types and how to inspect them with the Geometry Spreadsheet, and creating simple ones with Attribute Create, are fundamental steps towards advanced control.
