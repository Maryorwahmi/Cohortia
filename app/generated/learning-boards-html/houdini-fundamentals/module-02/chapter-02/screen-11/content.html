Targeting Geometry with Groups

Applying operations to specific components

Understanding Groups

  • •**Definition:** Named selections of geometry components (points, edges, primitives).
  • •**Purpose:** Allows operations to target only specific parts of a model.
  • •**Creation:** Can be manual (viewport selection) or procedural (using SOPs).
  • •**Benefit:** Essential for non-destructive, flexible, and precise modeling workflows.

Example: Window Frames with PolyExtrude

After creating a wall with window openings, you can use a `Group` SOP to select only the faces that form the inner edges of the window frames. Then, apply a `PolyExtrude` SOP to this group to push those faces inwards, creating a clean, recessed frame without affecting the rest of the wall geometry.

Key Takeaway

Groups are fundamental for targeting specific geometry components, enabling precise and localized operations without affecting the entire model, crucial for complex assets.

Pro Tip: Group Naming

Always give your groups descriptive names (e.g., `wall_windows`, `car_wheels`) to maintain clarity and organization in complex node graphs. This makes your procedural assets much easier to understand and modify.
