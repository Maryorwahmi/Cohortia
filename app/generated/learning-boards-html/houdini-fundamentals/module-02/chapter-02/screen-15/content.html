Key Concepts: Advanced Manipulation & Control

Refining and targeting your geometry

PolyExtrude SOP

Extrudes faces or edges to add depth and detail to existing surfaces.

Groups

Named selections of geometry components used to target operations precisely.

Topology

The arrangement of points, edges, and faces defining a 3D model's surface.

Chapter Summary

This chapter equipped you with essential SOPs like `PolyExtrude` for adding detail, `Groups` for targeted operations, and an understanding of `Topology` for creating clean, production-ready 3D models.
