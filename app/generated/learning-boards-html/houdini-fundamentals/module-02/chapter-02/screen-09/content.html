Maintaining Clean Topology with Booleans

Understanding potential issues and cleanup strategies

Boolean Topology Challenges

  • •**N-gons:** Polygons with more than four sides, often undesirable.
  • •**Thin Triangles:** Small, elongated triangles that can cause shading issues.
  • •**Performance:** Messy topology can impact game engine performance.
  • •**Subdivision Issues:** Can lead to pinching or artifacts when smoothing.

Why Clean Topology Matters

Clean topology (preferably quads) is crucial for optimal performance in game engines, predictable subdivision smoothing, easier UV unwrapping, and overall better visual quality and flexibility in your 3D assets.

Solution: Cleanup SOPs

If `Boolean` output is problematic, use `Remesh` to re-triangulate/re-quadrangulate for a cleaner surface, or `Clean` to remove redundant components. Always inspect your mesh after boolean operations.

Key Takeaway

Be mindful of the topology generated by `Boolean` operations; use cleanup SOPs like `Remesh` or `Clean` downstream to ensure high-quality, production-ready meshes.
