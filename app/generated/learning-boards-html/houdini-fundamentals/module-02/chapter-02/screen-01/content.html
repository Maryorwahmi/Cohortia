Transforming and Combining Geometry

Building Complex Shapes with Houdini SOPs

Discover how to manipulate and combine basic geometric primitives using Houdini's powerful Surface Operators (SOPs). Learn to create intricate 3D models through a flexible, non-destructive procedural workflow.

Key Takeaway

Houdini's SOPs enable the creation of complex, editable 3D forms by manipulating and combining simple geometric primitives in a procedural manner.

Transform

Move, rotate, scale

Merge

Combine streams

Boolean

Union, subtract, intersect

PolyExtrude

Add depth and detail
