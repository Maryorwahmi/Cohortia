Unifying Multiple Geometries

Bringing distinct elements together with the Merge SOP

The Merge SOP Explained

  • •**Purpose:** Combines multiple geometry inputs into one output stream.
  • •**Inputs:** Accepts any number of geometry streams.
  • •**Functionality:** Simply stacks geometries; does not perform boolean operations or combine points by default.
  • •**Use Case:** Essential for assembling complex scenes or modular assets.

Example: Building a Facade

Imagine you're creating a building facade. You might have separate SOP networks for a wall section, a window frame, and a door. You would then connect all three of these outputs to a single `Merge` SOP. The `Merge` node will combine them into one geometry stream, allowing you to view and further process the complete facade as a single object.

Key Takeaway

The `Merge` SOP is crucial for consolidating disparate geometry streams into a single output, enabling the assembly of complex models and scenes.

Pro Tip: Organization

Use `Merge` nodes strategically to keep your node graph clean and organized. Grouping related geometry streams before merging can make complex scenes much easier to manage and debug.
