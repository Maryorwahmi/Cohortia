Sculpting with Boolean Union and Subtract

Adding and removing volumes to create new forms

Boolean SOP Modes

Union (A + B)

Combines two geometries into a single mesh, adding their volumes together. Useful for creating unified shapes.

Subtract (A - B)

Removes the volume of the second input geometry (B) from the first (A). Ideal for cutting holes or carving shapes.

Practical Example: Cutting a Window

To cut a circular window into a wall, you would use a `Box` SOP for the wall (Input A) and a `Cylinder` SOP for the window opening (Input B). Connect both to a `Boolean` SOP and set its operation to "Subtract." The result is a `Box` with a perfectly cut cylindrical hole, demonstrating precise geometric removal.

Key Takeaway

The `Boolean` SOP's Union and Subtract modes are powerful for combining volumes or precisely removing one volume from another, enabling complex shape creation.

Real-world Connection

Boolean operations are widely used in industrial design and architecture for creating intricate parts, prototyping, and designing complex structures with precise cutouts and joins.
