Parameter Editor essentials

Parameters control node outputs. Learn common controls like Size, Divisions, Type, Center, and Frequency and how they change geometry fundamentally.

Best practices
  • Adjust one parameter at a time and observe results.
  • Start with low detail, refine later for specific regions.
  • Use expressions or channels to drive repetitive variation.
Quick reference
Numeric: Size, Frequency. Toggle/dropdown: Type. Integer: Divisions/Rows/Columns. Use these to control both shape and topology carefully.
Check
Change Divisions and view the Geometry Spreadsheet to confirm the point and primitive counts match your expectation.
