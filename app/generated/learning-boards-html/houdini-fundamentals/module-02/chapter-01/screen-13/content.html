Avoid these common pitfalls
  • Do not set Divisions/Frequency excessively early—use low-detail previews.
  • Confirm polygon counts and point totals in the viewport and Spreadsheet.
  • Prefer Polygon sphere when you plan to edit, UV, or export to game engines.
  • Use Merge carefully and verify attributes remain consistent across inputs.
Safety Note
Generating millions of polygons can crash or slow your system. Monitor counts and work iteratively with lower detail.
Practical tip
Use the Geometry Spreadsheet after each major change to confirm attribute integrity and topology before committing to heavy operations.
