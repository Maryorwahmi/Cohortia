Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Points, Vertices, Primitives
Geometry components
Points
Coordinates in 3D space. Attributes like position live here and moving points updates all primitives that reference them.
Vertices
References that tie points to primitives; store per-corner data such as UVs or different normals at a shared corner.
Primitives
The faces you see—defined by an ordered list of vertices, such as triangles or quads.
Concrete example
If two faces share the same point, moving that point moves both faces. If you need different normals at a corner, use separate vertices pointing to the same or duplicated points.
Misconception
Points and vertices are not identical; many SOPs will explicitly operate on one component type, so pick the correct target for your operation.
