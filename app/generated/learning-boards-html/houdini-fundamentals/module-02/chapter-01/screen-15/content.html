Start with low detail
Preserve interactivity and add detail only where needed.
Inspect with Spreadsheet
Verify points, vertices, primitives, and attributes as you change topology.
Choose types wisely
Use polygonal geometry for editing and export; primitives for lightweight math-driven shapes.
Cliff-note takeaway
SOPs give you reproducible geometry recipes. Use primitives and parameters as deliberate levers, inspect components, and manage density for efficient, predictable results.
