Introduction to SOPs and Basic Primitives
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass

This lesson frames why SOPs matter: they let you compose, reuse, and parameterize geometry creation. You will connect nodes, change parameters, and inspect the resulting points, vertices, and primitives to understand how procedural assets are built and optimized for pipelines like game development.
