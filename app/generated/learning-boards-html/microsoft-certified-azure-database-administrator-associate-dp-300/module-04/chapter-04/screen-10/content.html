Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Statistics
Why stats matter
Optimizer uses column distribution and cardinality estimates from statistics to choose join algorithms and access paths.
Maintenance commands
  • Azure SQL: UPDATE STATISTICS table_name
  • PostgreSQL: ANALYZE or VACUUM ANALYZE
  • MySQL: ANALYZE TABLE table_name
When to update
After large bulk loads, batch deletes, or when EXPLAIN ANALYZE shows large estimate/actual gaps.
Pro Tip

Refreshing statistics is often cheaper than adding an index and can immediately improve plan choices.
