Kusto Query Language
Use let, parse, summarize, and join to turn logs and metrics into correlated evidence.
Deadlock
A circular lock wait; inspect deadlock XML to identify victim and involved queries.
Resource Contention
Competition for CPU, I/O, memory, or locks; match wait types to resources.
Summary Takeaway
Combine KQL correlation, diagnostic artifacts, and wait analysis to form evidence-based remediation and capacity plans.
