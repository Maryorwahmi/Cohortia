Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Wait Statistics
Snapshot checklist
  1. Capture waits ordered by total wait time.
  2. Identify dominant wait types (IO, lock, CPU-related).
  3. Map waits to active sessions or query plans.
  4. Decide: tune query, change indexing, or scale resources.
Example
High IO waits plus scans in query plans point to missing indexes. Adding an index reduced IO and wait time in subsequent runs.
Common pitfall
Seeing high CPU waits and immediately scaling up without checking query plans can waste cost. First verify whether inefficient plans or parallelism are the root cause.
Pro tip

Capture short, targeted snapshots while adding minimal overhead: filter to busy databases and avoid full-history scans during peak load.
