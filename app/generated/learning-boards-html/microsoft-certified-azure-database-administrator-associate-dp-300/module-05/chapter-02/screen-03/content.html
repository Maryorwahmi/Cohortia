Practical verification steps
  • Check service tier and replica counts to confirm built-in HA guarantees and expected failover behavior.
  • Confirm Availability Zone support for the chosen region and enable zone redundancy during creation or update; verify distribution across zones.
  • For Active Geo-Replication, ensure secondary shows "Replication healthy" and test read-only queries against the secondary.
  • For Auto-Failover Groups, perform a planned failover and confirm the read-write listener remains the application endpoint.
