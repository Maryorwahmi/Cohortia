Core Concept
Grace Period, RPO & RTO
Choose a grace period that reflects your tolerance for data loss, then validate recovery times with tests.
Guidelines
  • Longer grace period → lower risk of data loss, slower failover. Shorter → faster failover, higher risk.
  • Business RPO/RTO drives whether you use Business Critical (synchronous/quorum replicas) or asynchronous geo-replication.
  • Plan and measure actual failover times and application reconnect behavior with tests.
Concrete example
Set the grace period during failover-group creation. A common conservative choice is one hour, but you can set zero for immediate failover with potential data loss.
Checklist
  • Define target RPO and RTO with stakeholders.
  • Choose tier and replication tech accordingly.
  • Perform planned failovers and measure the real RTO.
Key Takeaway

RPO and RTO choices determine which Azure features you should adopt; validate with tests and tune the grace period accordingly.
