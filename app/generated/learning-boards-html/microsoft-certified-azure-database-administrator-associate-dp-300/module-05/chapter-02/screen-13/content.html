Common Mistakes
Operational pitfalls and best practices
Testing, connection handling, and latency often determine whether HA works for your app.
Not testing failovers
Always schedule planned failovers to surface application problems such as session state, connection retries, and dependency readiness.
Connection strings and listeners
Use Auto-Failover Groups to preserve a single read-write endpoint so applications do not require post-failover updates.
Latency and secondaries
Cross-region latency affects replication and read performance; measure latency and consider region placement for reads and writes.
Runbook essentials
Document steps for failover, post-failover validation, and rollback. Include expected timelines and owner contacts.
Key Takeaway

Configuration alone is not enough. Operational testing and clear runbooks are what make HA reliable in production.
