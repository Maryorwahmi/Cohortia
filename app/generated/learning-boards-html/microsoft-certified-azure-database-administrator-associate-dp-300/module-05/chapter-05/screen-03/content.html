Practice & Validation
Concrete lab checks to confirm your backups and restores will work.
Confirm Backup Types
  • Check recent full, differential, and transaction log ages in the Backups pane.
  • Record timestamps to map to your PITR window.
Test Redundancy Effects
Change redundancy on a dev DB, then confirm whether geo-restore or cross-region recovery is available.
LTR & Archive Verification
Create a sample LTR policy, wait for a scheduled LTR snapshot, and find the archived backups under Available LTR backups.
