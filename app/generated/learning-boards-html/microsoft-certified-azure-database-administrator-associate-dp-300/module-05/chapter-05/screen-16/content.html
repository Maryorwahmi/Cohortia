Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › What to Remember
Three-tier backups
Full, differential, and transaction log backups together enable PITR.
Local → Zone → Geo
LRS, ZRS, then GRS/GZRS escalate protection scope from hardware to regional.
Restore → Verify → Cutover
Always restore to a new DB, validate, then plan production switchovers to avoid mistakes.
Final Takeaway
Design backups and retention to meet RPO/RTO and compliance, choose appropriate redundancy for the failure modes you must survive, and practice restores regularly.
