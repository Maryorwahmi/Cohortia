Comparison
Backup Storage Redundancy
LRS — Locally Redundant
Three copies in one physical location; protects against hardware failure but not datacenter loss.
ZRS — Zone-Redundant
Synchronous copies across availability zones in a region; protects against datacenter-level failures.
Cross-Region Options
GRS — Geo-Redundant
Synchronous copies in primary, then asynchronous replication to a paired region. Recommended for regional DR.
GZRS — Geo-Zone-Redundant
Zones in primary region plus async replication to secondary region where available.
Choose redundancy based on whether your recovery plan must survive a datacenter outage or an entire region outage, and balance costs accordingly.
