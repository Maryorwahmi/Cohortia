Backup and Restore Strategies in Azure Databases
Understand automated backup types, storage redundancy, short and long-term retention, and how to recover with Point-in-Time Restore and geo-restore.
In this lesson
Learn backup types and retention, configure redundancy, perform a PITR, and set LTR policies for compliance.
Why it matters
Backups let you recover from accidental changes, software bugs, or regional failures; settings determine your RTO and RPO.

Explore Key Topics
Automated Backups
Regular full, differential and log backups let Azure provide Point-in-Time Restore within the retention window.
Backup Redundancy
Choose LRS, ZRS, or GRS to match your risk tolerance for datacenter or regional outages.
Restore Operations
PITR restores to a new database for verification; LTR stores full backups for compliance up to 10 years.
