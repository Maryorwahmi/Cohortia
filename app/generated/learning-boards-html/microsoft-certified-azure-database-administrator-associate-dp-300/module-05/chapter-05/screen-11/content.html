Definition
Long-Term Retention (LTR)
LTR keeps full backups for long periods, typically for compliance or audit requirements, up to 10 years.
Schedule types
Weekly, monthly and yearly retention slots let you build an archival schedule that fits policies.
Practical Considerations
  • LTR backups are full backups stored in blob storage and may incur retrieval time during restore.
  • Use LTR for compliance and historical archives separate from PITR windows.
Example Policy
Weekly retention 4 weeks, monthly retention 6 months, yearly retention 5 years—set to meet your compliance horizon.
