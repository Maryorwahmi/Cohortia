What to Remember
Three concise operational reminders.
Measure first
RTO/RPO should come from business impact; measure to prove them.
Test often
Regular tests reveal gaps; frequency should match criticality.
Alert with action
Configure Azure Monitor alerts with playbooks and assigned responders.
Reflection
Which component in your environment would be hardest to recover? Use that answer to prioritize tests and runbook improvements.
