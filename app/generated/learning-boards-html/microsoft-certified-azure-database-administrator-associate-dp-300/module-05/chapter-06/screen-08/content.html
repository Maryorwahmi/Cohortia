Runbook structure
A template to make execution reliable under stress.
Title & Purpose: One-line intent and when to use the runbook.
Prerequisites & Pre-checks: Replication health, backups, stakeholder notifications.
Action steps: Exact commands or portal steps, in order, with expected outputs.
Verification: Smoke tests and queries to confirm functionality.
Rollback & Contacts: Clear rollback steps and escalation list.
Example excerpt
Pre-checks: replication health OK, backups successful within retention. Action: initiate planned failover. Verify: run three checkout transactions and confirm writes. Rollback: follow failback procedure if errors exceed threshold.
Key Takeaway
A well-structured runbook reduces cognitive load and mistakes during an incident. Keep ownership and review cycles clear.
Common mistake
Assuming a runbook is static. Update immediately after tests and incidents to reflect reality.
