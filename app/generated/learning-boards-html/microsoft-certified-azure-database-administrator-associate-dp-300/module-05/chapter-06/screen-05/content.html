Comparison: replication and backups
Match RTO/RPO to Azure features and note trade-offs.
Low RTO/RPO
Synchronous replication or Auto-Failover Groups. Very low data loss, but possibly higher write latency and cost.
Moderate RTO/RPO
Asynchronous geo-replication and read replicas with frequent backups. Trade-off: replication lag can increase RPO.
Data corruption recovery
Point-in-time restore and long-term retention help recover from logical corruption that replication won't solve.
Concrete example
RPO = 1 hour: choose asynchronous replication with hourly backups, test failover in a staging environment, and monitor replication lag to ensure it stays below threshold.
Why it matters

Picking the wrong replication mode can either overspend for low business value or leave you exposed to unacceptable data loss.

Misconception
Assuming 'geo-replication' is a single guarantee. Replication modes and lag behavior vary; verify the actual SLA and behavior for your configuration.
