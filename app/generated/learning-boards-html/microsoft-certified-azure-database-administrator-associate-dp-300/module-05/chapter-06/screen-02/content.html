Learning Objectives
Skills to plan, test, and monitor HADR solutions
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define RTO & RPO
Translate business impact into measurable recovery and data-loss targets. This drives architecture and testing.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Design and test failover
Plan and execute simulated failovers to validate RTO/RPO and discover integration gaps across services.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Perform failback safely
Ensure data synchronization and planned downtime when returning to the original primary, and validate consistency afterwards.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Monitor and alert HADR health
Use Azure Monitor to track replication lag, failover readiness, and backups, and configure alerts to trigger responses quickly.
Verified Competency Milestone
