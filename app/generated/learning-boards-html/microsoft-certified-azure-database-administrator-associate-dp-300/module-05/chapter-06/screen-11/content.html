Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Azure Monitor
Metrics to collect
Focus on signals that map to RTO/RPO and readiness.
Replication lag
Monitor lag and alert when it approaches your RPO threshold.
Failover group health
Track primary/secondary connectivity and replication status for Auto-Failover Groups.
Backup status
Alert on failed backups and monitor LTR job health for restore readiness.
Alerting practice
Pair alerts with runbooks and communication lists to make them actionable and avoid alert fatigue.
Dashboard example
A simple dashboard showing replication lag over time, recent failover events, and backup success rate gives quick operational confidence.
Key Takeaway
Monitor signals that directly relate to your RTO/RPO and have clear playbooks for alert responses.
