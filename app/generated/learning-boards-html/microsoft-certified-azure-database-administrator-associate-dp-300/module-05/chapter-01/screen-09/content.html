Definition
Point-in-time restore and backups
PITR reverts a database to a specific time. Backups store data snapshots for recovery and retention.
Concrete example
Accidental deletion of a table: restore to five minutes before the incident using PITR to recover lost rows.
Operational tip
Combine PITR with RBAC and auditing to both prevent and recover from mistakes.
Backup characteristics
  • Locally redundant backups: fast restore within region, lower resilience to region loss.
  • Geo-redundant backups: protect against regional disasters but may require longer restore time.
  • PITR: supports fine-grained recovery to minimize data loss from human error.
Key Takeaway

Use PITR for quick recovery from mistakes and backups (local or geo) for broader protection. Pair with access controls to reduce risk.
