Architecture
Availability Zones and local redundancy
Zones reduce datacenter-level risk by separating replicas within a region.
What PaaS provides
Platform-managed replicas and options for zone redundancy in supported tiers.
Example
Enable zone-redundant HA for a PostgreSQL flexible server to place standby in another zone for quick failover.
Key Takeaway

Use Availability Zones for HA within a region. For DR across regions choose geo-replication or backups with geo-redundant storage.

Decision guide
  • When downtime must be minimal for localized failures → enable zone redundancy if supported.
  • When you must tolerate a whole-region outage → implement geo-replication or cross-region backups.
  • Consider cost and latency: cross-region replication may increase cost and introduce latency for writes.
Common misconception
Mistaking zones for regions. Zones protect within a region; they are not a substitute for cross-region DR.
