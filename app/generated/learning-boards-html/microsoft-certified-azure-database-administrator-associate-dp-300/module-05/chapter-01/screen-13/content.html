RTO/RPO ↔ cost and complexity
Understand trade-offs so you can recommend a cost-effective architecture.
Higher protection
Near-zero RTO/RPO: continuous replication, active secondaries, automated failover.
Moderate protection
Short RTO/RPO: zone redundancy, more frequent backups, and tested runbooks.
Cost factors
Compute for secondaries, cross-region storage, network egress, and operational testing.
Concrete scenario
Dropping RPO from 60 minutes to 5 minutes may require moving from hourly backups to continuous replication, increasing cost but reducing data loss risk during DR.
Key Takeaway

Map business value to cost. Only choose aggressive HADR features when the reduction in downtime or data loss justifies the expense.
