Process Flow
Map RTO and RPO to architecture
A practical sequence to convert business limits into Azure choices.
1
Capture requirements
Ask stakeholders for acceptable downtime and data loss in time units.
2
Map to features
Choose zone redundancy, geo-replication, backups, or failover groups based on targets.
3
Estimate trade-offs
Evaluate cost, complexity, and operational overhead for chosen options.
4
Validate
Run failover drills and measure actual downtime and data loss against RTO/RPO.
Concrete scenario
E-commerce team requires RTO 2 hours, RPO 5 minutes. Recommended: zone-redundant HA, active geo-replication or auto-failover groups with near-continuous replication, and tested failover playbooks.
Common misconception
Assuming backups alone will meet low RPOs — continuous replication is usually required for small RPO windows.
Key Takeaway

RTO and RPO come from the business. Choose Azure features that achieve those numbers and validate with tests.
