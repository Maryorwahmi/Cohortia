Definition
Disaster Recovery (DR)
DR restores service and data after region-level catastrophes by using cross-region replication, recovery plans, and runbooks.
Cross-region replication
Replicate data to a geographically separated region for recovery.
Failover orchestration
Plans and automation to switch services to the secondary region.
Testing and runbooks
Regular drills to validate recovery procedures and assumptions.
Concrete example
Replicating an Azure SQL database to a paired region and failing over to it after a regional outage to restore service.
Key Takeaway

DR prepares for the worst: plan cross-region replication, automate failover, and test recovery to meet business continuity goals.
