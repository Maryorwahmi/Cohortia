Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
High Availability (HA)
Redundancy and automatic failover to minimize downtime for localized failures.
Disaster Recovery (DR)
Cross-region replication and recovery to restore service after catastrophic outages.
RTO & RPO
Business-driven limits on downtime and data loss that guide HADR choices.
Summary
Start from business metrics, use PaaS defaults where sufficient, and enable zones or geo options when targets demand stronger protection. Test and document every recovery path.
