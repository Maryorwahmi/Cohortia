Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Quick Reference
Availability Zones
Use for HA to survive datacenter failures inside the same region.
Geo-redundancy
Replicate to another region for DR and cross-region recovery.
Backups & PITR
Essential for restoring from human error and corruption; choose redundancy level by recovery needs.
Final takeaway
Design from RTO and RPO, use platform defaults where sufficient, and add zones or geo features to close gaps. Always test your recovery plan.
