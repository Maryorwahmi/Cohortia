Geo-redundancy and cross-region replication
Replicate data to another region to enable recovery after a regional failure.
Concrete example
Active geo-replication maintains readable secondaries in a different region that can be promoted in a failover.
Misconception
Geo-redundancy is more than storage; it requires failover orchestration, DNS updates, and testing to be effective.
Trade-offs and checks
  • Replication: faster failover, higher cost, careful consistency considerations.
  • Geo-redundant backups: cheaper but slower recovery.
  • Orchestration: DNS, app config, and runbooks are required for full DR.
Key Takeaway

Choose replication for faster recovery and backups for cost-effective protection. Always plan orchestration steps for failover.
