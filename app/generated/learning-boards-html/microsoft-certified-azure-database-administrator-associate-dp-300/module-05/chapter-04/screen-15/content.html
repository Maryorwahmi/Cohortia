Asynchronous Replication
Primary acknowledges commits first; replicas catch up later and may lag.
Geo-Restore
Restore from geo-redundant backups to another region for reliable recovery with higher RTO.
Point-in-Time Restore
Restore a server to a specific earlier time to recover from corruption or accidental deletes.
Final takeaway

Combine synchronous HA, replicas, and backups as needed to meet your availability, performance, and recovery objectives. Test regularly.
