Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Operational Pitfalls
Checklist (monthly)
  • Verify standby exists in a different AZ for HA enabled servers.
  • Check replication lag on all read replicas; set alerts.
  • Run a promotion drill on a non-production replica and update connection strings.
  • Perform a restore from backups to confirm PITR and geo-restore behavior.
Common mistakes
• Confusing asynchronous replicas with automatic HA.
• Skipping promotion drills so teams are unprepared during a real outage.
• Not monitoring replication lag or backup completion.
• Underestimating standby and replica costs.
Keep an ops playbook, schedule tests, and include stakeholders who update connection strings and DNS records in the drill.
