What is LTR?

Long-Term Retention policies

Store full backups at scheduled intervals for archival retention and compliance needs; retain for up to 10 years.

• Configure weekly, monthly, and yearly retention rules to meet audit windows.
• Useful for legal, financial, or regulatory retention where historical restores may be required.
• Complements operational PITR; it is archival, not a rapid recovery substitute.
Configuration example

Set weekly retention to P12W, monthly to P12M, and yearly to P10Y with a chosen week-of-year for yearly backups. This keeps weekly backups for 12 weeks, monthly for 12 months, and yearly backups for ten years.

Misconception

Do not rely on LTR for fast operational restores. It is optimized for long-term archival and compliance, not sub-hour recovery.

Key Takeaway

Use LTR to satisfy retention requirements and to keep historical backups available for rare recovery or auditing needs.
