Skills & Checkpoints

Each objective turned into a measurable lab artifact and why it matters to operations and compliance.

Geo-Restore Check
  • Simulate a geo-restore to a different region and confirm DB becomes available under a new name.
  • Record restore duration and note any size-dependent delays to compare to expected RTO.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
LTR Policy Check
  • Configure weekly/monthly/yearly retention and verify archived full backups are present in LTR.
  • Confirm retrieval of one historical backup for a target restore scenario.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Auto-Failover Group Check
  • Set up primary and secondary servers in different regions and validate asynchronous replication.
  • Perform a manual planned failover to observe zero-data-loss behavior when possible.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
RTO / RPO Measurement
  • Record the time from outage start to application readiness (RTO) and the maximum data gap observed (RPO).
  • Use the results to pick the right DR configuration aligned with business needs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
