Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
DR Failover
Switching operations to a secondary region during an outage.
DR Failback
Returning operations to the original primary after validation and synchronization.
PITR across regions
Point-in-time restores may be possible via geo backups but often with coarser granularity and higher RPO than same-region PITR.
Closing takeaway

Document dependencies, include networking and DNS in your DR plan, and rehearse failovers and failbacks to measure real-world RTO and RPO for stakeholders.
