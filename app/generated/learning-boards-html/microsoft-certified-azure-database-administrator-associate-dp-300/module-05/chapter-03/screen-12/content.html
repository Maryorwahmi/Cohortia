Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Geo-restore
Restore a DB to another region from geo-replicated backups for regional outage recovery.
Long-Term Retention (LTR)
Archive full backups weekly, monthly, yearly for compliance and historical restores.
Auto-Failover Groups
Cross-region replication with a persistent endpoint to reduce recovery steps.
Summary takeaway

Choose DR solutions based on measured RTO/RPO, cost constraints, and operational readiness. Test often and include dependent resources in every drill.
