Configuring Disaster Recovery for Azure SQL

Understand how geo-restore, Long-Term Retention, and Auto-Failover Groups enable recovery from regional outages and meet compliance needs.

Orientation

This lesson shows practical recovery paths: simulate a geo-restore, set LTR policies for archival backups, and design Auto-Failover Groups to reduce the operational steps during a region failover.

Who this matters to

DBAs and platform engineers responsible for business continuity, compliance officers who require long retention, and architects who must balance budget with recovery objectives.

Quick preview

You will practice a geo-restore, configure weekly/monthly/yearly LTR rules, and understand when Auto-Failover Groups are required to hit tight RTO/RPO targets.
