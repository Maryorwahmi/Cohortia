Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › SaaS and database-like services
Definition

Consume the application; minimal engine management

Choose when you need application-level services and global distribution rather than engine-level control.

When to use
  • Global distribution with low latency requirements.
  • Multi-model or schemaless data patterns.
  • Developers wanting an API-first experience.
Concrete note

Azure Cosmos DB acts closer to SaaS for many use cases, offering global distribution and limited engine management needs compared to relational engines.

Misconception

Assuming SaaS-like services replace relational databases. They are specialized and not always suitable for transactional relational workloads.

Key Takeaway

Pick SaaS-style services for global scale and specialized models; pick relational PaaS for transactional consistency and relational queries.
