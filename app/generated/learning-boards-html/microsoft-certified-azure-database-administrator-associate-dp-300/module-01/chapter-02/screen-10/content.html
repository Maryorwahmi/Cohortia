Core Concept
Service Tiers
General Purpose, Business Critical, and Hyperscale trade off latency, HA, and scale.
General Purpose
Balanced and budget-friendly; remote storage with good performance for typical OLTP workloads.
Business Critical
Local SSD storage and multiple replicas for low latency and high resilience; ideal for mission-critical OLTP.
Hyperscale
Independent compute and storage scaling for very large databases with fast restore times.
Example: Choose a tier
A high-throughput OLTP application with reporting needs will often use Business Critical for low latency and read-scale replicas. For extremely large stores or analytics workloads needing fast growth, Hyperscale fits better.
Common mistake
Picking Business Critical for non-critical environments and incurring avoidable cost.
Key Takeaway
Match tier to your IO, latency, and size needs, and scale only when monitoring shows a real requirement.
