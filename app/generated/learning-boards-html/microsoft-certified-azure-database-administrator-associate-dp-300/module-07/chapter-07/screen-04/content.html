Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › OLTP vs OLAP
Definition

What separates transactional and analytical systems

Transactional systems optimize for consistency and short operations. Analytical systems optimize for scanning, aggregation, and combining large data sets for insights.

Analogy

Think of your operational database as a retail checkout lane handling many short purchases. Synapse and data lakes are the distribution centers where inventory is consolidated and analyzed across days and regions.

Concrete example

A monthly sales trend that aggregates millions of orders should run in Synapse to avoid slowing the order processing database.

Common mistake

Running heavy analytical queries directly on the operational database without scheduling or offloading, which causes latency for users.

Decision checklist
  • Does the query scan millions of rows frequently? → Move to analytics.
  • Does the report need near real-time updates? → Consider streaming or Synapse Link.
  • Will dashboards impact SLA-sensitive transactions? → Use separate analytical stores.
Key Takeaway

Protect transactional performance by offloading large-scan and heavy-aggregation workloads to Synapse or a data lake before visualizing or training models.
