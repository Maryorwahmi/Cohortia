ETL vs ELT

ETL transforms before load; ELT loads raw data and transforms inside the analytical store for scale.

When to use which
  • Small datasets or simple transforms → ETL in ADF.
  • Large datasets and complex joins → ELT inside Synapse or Spark.

Key Takeaway

Choose ETL or ELT based on volume and compute placement; ADF orchestrates both movement and transforms.
