Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Azure ML data flow
Flow

Use ADF to copy and prepare data into ADLS Gen2 or Synapse. Train models on curated datasets and use batch or real-time scoring as required.

Example

Collect weekly customer transactions into ADLS, run feature engineering in Spark, train a churn model in Azure ML, and write predicted churn labels back to the operational database for campaign triggers.

Misconception

Do not assume your operational schema is ready for ML. Without cleansing, versioning, and feature preparation, models will underperform.

Key Takeaway

Curate and version your training data in ADLS or Synapse, and plan whether scoring will be batch or real-time depending on business needs and latency constraints.
