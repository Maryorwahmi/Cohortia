Data Governance and Compliance

Learn to discover sensitive data, record activity, and apply masking or client-side encryption so that regulatory and business requirements are met without disrupting applications.

In this lesson
Practical steps to classify data, configure auditing across SQL, PostgreSQL and MySQL, apply Dynamic Data Masking, and design Always Encrypted deployments.
Why it matters
Regulations require evidence of protection and access control. Proper governance limits exposure and simplifies audits.
Explore Key Topics
Classification, Auditing, Masking, and Client-side Encryption in Azure SQL and managed databases.
Governance Snapshot
Classification feeds masking and auditing. Encryption protects highly sensitive columns from privileged users.
Classification
Data Discovery & Classification recommends labels like Confidential and Highly Confidential.
Auditing
Stream logs to storage, Log Analytics, or Event Hub for retention and analysis.
Protection
Use DDM for simple masking and Always Encrypted for client-side protection of sensitive columns.
