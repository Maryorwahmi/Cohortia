Definition

Data governance and compliance

Governance ensures data is controlled and accounted for. Compliance ties these practices to laws and standards that require proof and evidence.

Regulatory drivers
GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI demand protection, access controls, and retained logs to show who did what and when.
Common mistakes
Treating masking as encryption, not reviewing audit logs, and failing to re-classify evolving data schemas.
Key Takeaway

Governance links classification, protection, and evidence to reduce risk and demonstrate compliance.

Practical mapping
If the data is financial
Classify as Highly Confidential, enable strict auditing, and consider Always Encrypted for columns such as card numbers.
If the data is public
Label as public, relax masking, but still monitor for anomalous access patterns.
Analogy
Think of classification as tags, auditing as security camera footage, masking as a blurred sign, and Always Encrypted as sealing a letter before mailing.
