Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Data Classification
Process

Scan, Review, Apply

  1. Run Data Discovery & Classification scan to detect candidate columns based on patterns and names.
  2. Review recommendations and adjust sensitivity labels to reflect business context.
  3. Save classifications to enable reporting and policy checks across databases.
Concrete example
A scan recommends CreditCardNumber as Highly Confidential and Email as Confidential for the Sales.Customers table.
Remember: classification is metadata. It helps you decide where to mask or encrypt, but does not alter stored data by itself.
Reporting and policy
Use case
Export classification reports when preparing for an audit to show what data is labeled and why.
Mnemonic
Think: Tag first, Protect second.
Misconception check
Classification does not enforce masking or encryption automatically; you must apply those controls separately.
