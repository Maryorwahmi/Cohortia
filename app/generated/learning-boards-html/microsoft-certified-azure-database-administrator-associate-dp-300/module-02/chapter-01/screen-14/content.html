PaaS vs IaaS
PaaS reduces operations; IaaS gives full OS and engine control for specific compatibility needs.
Tiers & Compute
General Purpose, Business Critical, Hyperscale and vCore/DTU/serverless affect performance and billing.
Networking & Privacy
Design VNets, subnets and prefer Private Endpoints to avoid public exposure for production databases.
Summary
Map requirements to model, tier, and network choices, then simulate configuration and review costs before provisioning.
