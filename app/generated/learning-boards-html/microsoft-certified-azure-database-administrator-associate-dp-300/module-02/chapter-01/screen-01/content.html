Plan Azure Database Deployments: Service Tiers & Networking
Choose the service model and network controls that match workload needs for performance, security, and budget.
In this lesson
You will compare PaaS and IaaS, map service tiers to workload types, and plan VNets, private endpoints, and cost controls.
Why it matters
A correct service and network choice reduces downtime, improves compliance, and prevents unexpected bills during scale events.
Explore Key Topics
Service Models
PaaS vs IaaS trade-offs for operations and control.
Tiers & Compute
General Purpose, Business Critical, Hyperscale, vCore and DTU choices.
Networking
VNets, Private Endpoints, and service endpoints for secure access.
Concept Map
PaaS: Managed
Managed engine, built-in HA, less operational overhead.
IaaS: Full control
Full OS and engine control, more management work.
Quick example
A new web app needing moderate throughput, budget constraints, and private VNet-only access is often best served by an Azure SQL Database in General Purpose with a Private Endpoint.
