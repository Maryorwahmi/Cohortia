Comparison
Tiers and compute models
General Purpose
Balanced storage and compute for most apps. Use vCore for predictable performance.
Business Critical
Low-latency and higher availability for I/O-intensive workloads.
Hyperscale
Independent storage scaling for very large databases and rapid growth scenarios.
Compute models: DTU legacy units, vCore for transparent compute and storage sizing, and vCore serverless for workloads with variable usage patterns.
Concrete scenario
Choose General Purpose with 4 vCores and 256GB when you need consistent performance for a medium-sized web application. If you need near on-premises compatibility for migration, Managed Instance with Business Critical may be appropriate.
Misconception
Higher tier does not automatically solve design issues. Profile and test to align tier with workload characteristics to avoid unnecessary cost.
