Classification workflow
  1. Discover sensitive columns and assign sensitivity labels (Public, Confidential, Highly Confidential).
  2. Map labels to controls: Always Encrypted for highest sensitivity, TDE with CMK for storage-level protection, TLS for transit.
  3. Implement controls selectively to minimize disruption and maximize privacy.
  4. Automate key rotation and verification; keep audit trails for compliance.
Operational tips
  • Test key rotation in staging before production.
  • Document why each column was encrypted and who controls the keys.
  • Monitor Key Vault audit logs for unexpected access patterns.
Why it matters

Classification ensures you spend effort protecting the most sensitive data and maintain a defensible security posture during audits.
