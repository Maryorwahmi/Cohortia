Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Azure Private Link and Private Endpoints
Concept

Service projected into your VNet

A Private Endpoint uses a private IP from your subnet to connect to the managed service. This removes public exposure and forces traffic through your VNet controls.

Why it matters

Private Endpoints eliminate public network paths, which is critical for compliance and preventing data exfiltration. They also centralize control through your NSGs and route tables.

Misconception
Private Endpoints alone do not remove the need for firewall rules or NSG checks. They must be paired with DNS and access controls to be effective.
Analogy
Think of Private Endpoints as creating a private mailbox for the service inside your network; mail never goes through the public post office.
