What ATP detects
Patterns and anomalies that suggest attacks or misuse of privileges; signals are starting points for investigation.
Example detections
  • SQL injection-like statements or anomalous query shapes.
  • Brute-force attempts shown by many failed logins from one IP.
  • Unusual data access patterns or large exports.
Response example
On a confirmed ATP alert, collect relevant audit events, block the offending IP or revoke credentials if necessary, and document steps taken for post-incident review.
Key Takeaway

ATP alerts are signals to investigate; correlate with audit logs and VA findings before taking disruptive action.

Misconception
Treating ATP alerts as definitive proof. Use them as starting points for investigation and corroborate with logs and scans.
Pro tip
Tune alert thresholds and baselines to your environment to lower noise, then test that real incidents still trigger notifications.
