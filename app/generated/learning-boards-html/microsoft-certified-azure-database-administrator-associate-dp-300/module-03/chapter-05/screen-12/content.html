Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Operational Practices
Avoid common mistakes
Alert tuning
Adjust thresholds and use baselines so alerts reflect true anomalies rather than expected spikes or routine maintenance.
Log protection
Store critical audit logs in immutable storage when required and limit write/delete permissions to a few administrators.
Incident playbook elements
  • Investigation checklist and required logs.
  • Containment steps such as IP blocks or credential revocation.
  • Remediation and recovery steps and owners.
  • Post-incident review and improvements.
Correlation
Correlate Defender alerts with audit events and VA findings before escalating to reduce false positives and speed accurate responses.
Checkpoint
Schedule quarterly reviews of audit configurations, VA baselines, and action group recipients to keep the system current.
