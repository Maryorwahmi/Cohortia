Protecting audit logs
Treat logs as critical evidence: restrict access, use immutability for critical archives, and maintain a clear retention policy.
Tiered retention model
Keep recent logs in Log Analytics for active investigation and alerting, archive older logs to Storage with immutability for compliance.
Practical step
Assign minimal permissions for log deletion and ensure at least one owner outside the primary admin team can access archived logs for investigations.
Misconception
Assuming log retention is only a storage cost. It is also an assurance that you can investigate incidents and meet compliance obligations.
Checklist
Define retention windows, enable immutability for critical archives, limit access, and document policies in your incident playbook.
