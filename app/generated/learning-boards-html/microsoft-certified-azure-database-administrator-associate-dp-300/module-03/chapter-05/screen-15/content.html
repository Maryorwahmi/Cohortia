Route audits
Send audit logs to the destination that best supports your analysis and retention needs.
Scan regularly
Run Vulnerability Assessment often and act on high-priority findings, then re-scan to confirm fixes.
Automate alerts
Create Monitor alerts and action groups to notify teams or run automated containment playbooks.
Final takeaway

Monitoring and auditing are operational practices: implement them, protect logs, tune alerts, and regularly review to reduce risk over time.
