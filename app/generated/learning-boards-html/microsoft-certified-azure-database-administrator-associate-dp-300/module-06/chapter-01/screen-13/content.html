Scenario-based assessments

Question 1

Update a setting while ensuring you can undo changes. What steps do you take and why?

Question 2

Explain the main benefits of using stored procedures for administrative tasks and give one practical example.

Scoring guidance
  • A full answer includes preview SELECT, transactions for testing, and commit/rollback logic.
  • Procedure answers mention reusability, encapsulation, and permission management.
