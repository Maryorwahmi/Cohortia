Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Transaction Log Management
Why logs matter
The transaction log enables point-in-time recovery and records all transactional changes. Proper management prevents full logs and failed transactions.
Practical steps
Schedule regular log backups for MI/VM in Full or Bulk-Logged recovery models, monitor log reuse wait reasons, and avoid large single transactions that bloat the log.
Caution: DBCC SHRINKFILE may cause fragmentation and is not a routine solution. Use it only after resolving the cause of growth and with planning.
Scenario
If a nightly ETL creates long-running transactions, break loads into smaller batches and ensure frequent log backups so the log can be truncated and disk stays healthy.
Key Takeaway

Protect recovery and prevent full logs by combining frequent backups with batching strategies; reserve shrink for controlled remediation, not routine maintenance.
