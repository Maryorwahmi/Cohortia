Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Index Rebuild vs Reorganize
Rebuild
Drops and recreates index, removes fragmentation, reclaims space, and updates statistics. Can be offline unless online option is supported.
When to rebuild: fragmentation > 30%, need to reclaim space, or after massive data churn.
Consideration: use WITH (ONLINE = ON) in supported Azure services to reduce downtime.
Reorganize
Physically reorders leaf-level pages to match logical order. Always online and less resource intensive, but less effective for severe fragmentation.
When to reorganize: fragmentation between about 5% and 30% when you want minimal disruption.
Trade-off: fewer resources and no blocking, but does not reclaim disk space as well as a rebuild.
Example
If sys.dm_db_index_physical_stats shows 40% fragmentation on a critical index, plan a rebuild with ONLINE = ON during off-peak hours to restore performance and update statistics.
Key Takeaway

Choose reorganize for light maintenance and rebuild for thorough correction; schedule heavy operations to limit user impact.
