Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Index Fragmentation
Physical disorganization of index pages that increases IO and slows queries.
Index Rebuild
Drops and recreates an index, removing fragmentation and updating statistics.
Index Reorganize
Online operation that reorders leaf-level pages and reduces moderate fragmentation.
Summary

Measure fragmentation, choose the correct index action, update statistics, and confirm improved plans and performance.
