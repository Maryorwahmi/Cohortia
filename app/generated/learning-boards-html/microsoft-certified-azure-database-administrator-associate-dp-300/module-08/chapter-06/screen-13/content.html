Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Quick Reference
Monitor & Test
Always validate parameter changes and backup restores before applying to production.
Distribution Choice
Pick a high-cardinality column used frequently in filters and joins to enable efficient colocation.
Reference Tables
Replicate small lookup tables across workers to avoid remote joins and keep joins efficient.
Final reflection: For large datasets prefer Hyperscale (Citus) when your query patterns align with a distribution key; otherwise managed MariaDB may be the simpler, effective choice.
