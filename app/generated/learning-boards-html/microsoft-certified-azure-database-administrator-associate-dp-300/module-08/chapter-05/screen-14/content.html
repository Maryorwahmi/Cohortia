What to remember
Global multi-model
Supports JSON, key-value, and graph models via different APIs.
Request Units
Operations consume RUs; monitor and tune throughput and queries to avoid throttling.
Containers & Partition Keys
Containers are scaling units. Partition keys determine distribution and parallelism.
Summary takeaway

Design around distribution, monitor RUs and queries, and choose indexing and consistency to meet performance and correctness goals.
