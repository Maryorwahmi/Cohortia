Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › Common Administration Tasks
Operational checklist
Monitor
Track RU consumption, throttling (429s), and per-partition metrics to detect hotspots early.
Backups
Understand account-level backup settings and restore procedures as part of your recovery plan.
Security
Use RBAC, keys and network controls such as VNet service endpoints or Private Link to limit access.
Troubleshooting
When you see throttling, inspect per-partition RU usage, query RU charges, and indexing policies before increasing RUs.
Common mistake
Scaling RUs without addressing hot partitions or inefficient queries often wastes cost and fails to solve latency issues.
Key Takeaway

Pair telemetry with targeted fixes: investigate hotspots and query costs before increasing throughput. Secure and back up your account as part of operations.
