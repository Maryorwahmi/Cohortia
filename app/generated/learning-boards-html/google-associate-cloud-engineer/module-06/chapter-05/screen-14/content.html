Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Distributed Tracing

A method for observing requests as they flow through a distributed system, visualizing their end-to-end path and latency.

Cloud Trace

Google Cloud's fully managed service for distributed tracing, providing end-to-end request visualization.

Trace

A representation of a single request or workflow through a distributed system, encompassing its entire journey.

Summary Takeaway

Distributed tracing and Cloud Trace are fundamental for gaining deep visibility into application performance across complex microservices.
