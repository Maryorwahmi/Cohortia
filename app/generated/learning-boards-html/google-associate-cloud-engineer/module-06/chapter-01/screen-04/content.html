Core Concept

Observability: Understanding System Behavior

Moving beyond simple "up/down" checks to deep insights.

What is Observability?

The ability to infer the internal state of a system by examining its external outputs (metrics, logs, traces). It allows you to ask arbitrary questions about system behavior.

  • Goes beyond traditional monitoring.
  • Enables understanding of 'unknown-unknowns'.
  • Crucial for complex, distributed cloud environments.
Key Takeaway

Observability empowers proactive issue resolution and deep understanding of system dynamics, vital for cloud reliability.

Observability vs. Monitoring

Monitoring tells you if something is broken. Observability helps you understand why it broke.

Monitoring: Known-Knowns
Observability: Unknown-Unknowns
Common Misconception

Observability is not just a fancy word for monitoring; it's a deeper capability for understanding system state.
