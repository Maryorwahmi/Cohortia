Advanced Operational Concepts

Key principles for reliability and incident management

CMEK

Customer-Managed Encryption Keys: encryption keys provided and managed by the customer for GCP services.

SLI

Service Level Indicator: a quantitative measure of some aspect of the level of service that is provided.

SLO

Service Level Objective: a target value or range for an SLI, defining the desired performance and reliability of a service.

Blameless Post-mortem

A retrospective analysis of an incident focused on systemic improvements rather than assigning blame to individuals.

IaC

Infrastructure as Code: managing and provisioning infrastructure through code instead of manual processes.

Incident Response Playbook

A documented set of procedures and steps to follow when a specific type of incident occurs.

These advanced concepts empower you to build and operate highly reliable, resilient, and continuously improving cloud systems.
