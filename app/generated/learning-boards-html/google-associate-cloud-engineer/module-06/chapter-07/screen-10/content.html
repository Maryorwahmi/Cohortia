Building Reliability with SRE: SLOs and Post-mortems

Defining service objectives and learning from incidents

Define SLOs and SLIs

Clearly define Service Level Indicators (SLIs) as quantifiable measures of performance (e.g., latency, error rate) and Service Level Objectives (SLOs) as target values for those SLIs. Track with Cloud Monitoring.

Example: An SLO of 99.9% availability, measured by an SLI of successful HTTP requests.

Blameless Post-mortems

When incidents occur, conduct blameless post-mortems to understand root causes and contributing factors, focusing on systemic improvements rather than individual blame. Logs and metrics are key for reconstruction.

Pro-Tip: Focus on 'what happened' and 'how to prevent it' rather than 'who caused it'.

Key Takeaway

Defining clear SLOs and conducting blameless post-mortems are foundational SRE practices for proactively managing service reliability and fostering continuous improvement.
