Cost Optimization

Controlling Logging Costs: Ingestion & Retention

Strategies to reduce expenditure on Cloud Logging storage

01

Filter Logs at Ingestion

Use log sinks and exclusion filters to prevent verbose or non-critical logs (e.g., DEBUG, INFO from dev environments) from ever being stored in log buckets. This is the most effective way to save on ingestion and storage costs.

Example: Exclude all 'DEBUG' level logs from a specific application's log stream.

02

Custom Log Bucket Retention

Configure custom retention periods for your log buckets. The default 30 days is often sufficient; only pay for longer-term storage for logs that are truly necessary for compliance or deep analytics.

Example: Set a 7-day retention for development logs, but 1 year for audit logs.

Key Takeaway

Proactively managing log ingestion and retention policies is crucial for preventing unchecked growth in Cloud Logging costs and ensuring you only pay for valuable data.
