Detecting Silence: Absence Alerts

Trigger alerts when a metric stops reporting data for a certain period, detecting 'silent failures'.

Definition

An alert that triggers when a metric stops sending expected data for a configured duration.

Why it's crucial

Essential for detecting crashed processes or services that have silently failed without generating errors.

Example Scenario

If your application's custom 'heartbeat' metric stops reporting for 5 minutes, an absence alert notifies you.

Key Takeaway

Absence alerts provide a critical safety net, ensuring that even quiet failures or unresponsive services are promptly detected and addressed.
