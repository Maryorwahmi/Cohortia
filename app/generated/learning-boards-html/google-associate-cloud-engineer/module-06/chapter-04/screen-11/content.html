SLOs and SLAs: Defining Reliability Targets

Align Cloud Monitoring with Service Level Objectives and Agreements to proactively manage service reliability.

Service Level Objective (SLO)

A target level of service your application aims to achieve (e.g., 99.9% availability, 500ms latency for 95% of requests).

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A formal contract specifying the expected service level and penalties for not meeting it.

Cloud Monitoring Role

Define custom metrics and alerts that directly track SLOs, providing early warnings of potential violations.

Key Takeaway

Integrating SRE principles with Cloud Monitoring shifts focus to proactive reliability management, ensuring services consistently meet targets and build user trust.

SLO Example: Availability

Target: 99.9% availability for the core application service over a month.

Internal goal for operational teams.

SLO Example: Latency

Target: 95th percentile of API requests respond within 200ms.

Directly impacts user experience.

SLA Example: Uptime Guarantee

Contractual guarantee of 99.5% uptime with financial penalties for breaches.

External commitment to customers.
