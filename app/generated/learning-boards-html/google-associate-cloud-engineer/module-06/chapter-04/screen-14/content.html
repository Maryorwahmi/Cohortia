Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential terms from this chapter on advanced alerting and incident management.

Alerting Policy

A configuration defining conditions for alert triggers and notifications.

Threshold Alert

Triggers when a metric crosses a predefined value for a specified duration.

Absence Alert

Triggers when a metric stops reporting data for a certain period.

Log-based Alert

Triggered by specific patterns or counts of log entries in Cloud Logging.

Notification Channel

A configuration specifying where and how alerts are delivered.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive alerting with diverse policies and channels is fundamental for maintaining system reliability and ensuring rapid incident response.
