Operational Health

Avoiding Alert Fatigue

Strategies for creating actionable alerts and preventing desensitization to critical warnings.

What it is

Operators becoming desensitized to alerts due to an excessive number of non-actionable or noisy notifications.

Solution: Actionable Alerts

Focus on alerts that indicate a real problem requiring human intervention, using appropriate thresholds and durations.

Best Practice

Always include clear documentation in your alerting policies, explaining purpose, potential causes, and troubleshooting steps.

Key Takeaway

Regularly review and fine-tune alerting policies to ensure they remain relevant and effective, transforming your alerting system into a valuable tool.

Pitfall: Too Many Alerts

Constant, non-critical notifications lead to operators ignoring warnings.

Desensitization to critical issues.

Pitfall: Lack of Documentation

Responders don't know what an alert means or how to troubleshoot it.

Slows down incident resolution.
