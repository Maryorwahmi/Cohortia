Log Buckets: Storing and Retaining Your Logs

Understanding regional storage and configurable retention policies.

Regional Storage

Log buckets are geographically located, allowing for data residency control.

Default Bucket

The _Default bucket stores logs for 30 days by default.

Custom Retention

Configure custom log buckets with specific retention periods (e.g., 90 days, 1 year, 5 years).
