Best Practices

Avoiding Common Log Management Mistakes

Strategies for cost-effective, compliant, and efficient logging.

Mistake: Unstructured Logs

Makes log analysis and querying significantly harder, reducing observability.

Best Practice

Implement structured logging (JSON) for enhanced queryability.

Mistake: No Log Sinks

Logs are deleted after default retention, leading to data loss for compliance or analytics.

Best Practice

Configure log sinks for long-term archival and advanced analytics.

Mistake: Unmanaged Log Volume

Can lead to significant and unnecessary cloud costs.

Best Practice

Use log filters and appropriate retention policies to control costs.

Mistake: Inadequate Security

Exposing sensitive data or granting excessive permissions to log access.

Best Practice

Apply least privilege, redact sensitive data, and use log views.
