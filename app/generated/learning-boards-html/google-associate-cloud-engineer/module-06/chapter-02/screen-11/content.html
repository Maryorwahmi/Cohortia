Log Sinks: Exporting Your Log Data

Configuring destinations for long-term archival, analytics, or real-time processing.

What is a Log Sink?

A configuration that defines a filter for logs and a destination for exporting them.

Why Use Log Sinks?

Essential for long-term archival, compliance, advanced analytics, and real-time processing.

Key Benefit

Prevents log data loss after default retention periods and integrates with other services.
