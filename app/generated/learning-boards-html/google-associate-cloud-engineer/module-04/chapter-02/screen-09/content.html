Traffic Management

What are VPC Routes?

VPC routes define the paths for network traffic within your VPC network and to external destinations.

  • •
    Traffic Direction: Dictate where network packets should be sent based on their destination IP address.
  • •
    Internal & External: Apply to traffic flowing between subnets, to the internet, or to other connected networks (e.g., via VPN).
  • •
    Next Hop: Each route specifies a 'next hop' – the next destination for the traffic, which could be a gateway, a VPN tunnel, or a VM instance.
Analogy

Think of routes like a GPS system for your network. When a packet wants to go somewhere, the routes tell it the best path to take, guiding it through various 'intersections' (next hops) until it reaches its final destination.

Key Takeaway

Routes are fundamental for controlling network flow, enabling segmentation, and ensuring all resources can communicate as intended.
