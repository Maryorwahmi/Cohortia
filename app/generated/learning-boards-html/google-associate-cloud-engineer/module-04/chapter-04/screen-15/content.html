Key Concepts

Advanced Components and Services

Backend Service

A component of a load balancer that manages backend instances, health checks, and traffic distribution policies.

Health Check

A mechanism used by load balancers to periodically monitor the health and responsiveness of backend instances.

Cloud CDN

A service that caches content at edge locations globally to reduce latency and improve content delivery speed.

URL Map

(For HTTP(S) Load Balancers) Defines routing rules based on URL paths and hostnames to direct traffic to different backend services.

Summary Takeaway

Backend Services manage instances with Health Checks, Cloud CDN accelerates content globally, and URL Maps enable intelligent HTTP(S) traffic routing.
