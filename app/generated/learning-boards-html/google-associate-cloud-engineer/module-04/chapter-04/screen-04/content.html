The Power of Load Balancing

Distributing Traffic for High Availability and Scalability

Why Load Balancing Matters

  • ›High Availability: Ensures continuous service by routing traffic away from unhealthy instances.
  • ›Scalability: Distributes traffic across many instances, allowing applications to handle increased demand.
  • ›Fault Tolerance: Automatically detects and isolates failing instances, maintaining application responsiveness.
  • ›Performance: Prevents single points of overload, ensuring consistent low latency for users.
