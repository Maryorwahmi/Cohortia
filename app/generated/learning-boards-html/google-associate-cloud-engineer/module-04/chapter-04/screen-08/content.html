Internal Networking

Internal HTTP(S) and TCP/UDP Load Balancers

Secure Traffic Distribution within Your VPC

Internal HTTP(S) Load Balancer

  • ›Protocol: HTTP and HTTPS traffic.
  • ›Layer: Layer 7 (Application Layer).
  • ›Scope: Regional, within your VPC network.
  • ›IP Address: Uses internal IP addresses.
  • ›Use Cases: Multi-tier microservice architectures, secure frontend-to-backend communication.

Internal TCP/UDP Load Balancer

  • ›Protocol: TCP and UDP traffic.
  • ›Layer: Layer 4 (Transport Layer).
  • ›Scope: Regional, within your VPC network.
  • ›IP Address: Uses internal IP addresses.
  • ›Use Cases: Database clusters, message queues, custom internal services.
Key Takeaway

Internal HTTP(S) and TCP/UDP Load Balancers provide regional, private IP-based traffic distribution within a VPC, essential for secure and scalable multi-tier microservice architectures.
