Principle of Least Privilege

Granting Minimum Necessary Access

This fundamental security principle dictates that you should only allow the minimum necessary traffic required for a task.

  • Targeted Traffic: Only allow specific protocols and ports.
  • Restricted Sources/Destinations: Limit IP ranges, network tags, or service accounts.
Key Takeaway

Adhering to the principle of least privilege significantly reduces your network's attack surface and improves security posture.

Common Mistake

Overly Permissive Rules

Creating firewall rules that are too broad can introduce significant security vulnerabilities.

  • Example: Allowing SSH from 0.0.0.0/0 exposes your VMs to the entire internet, making them vulnerable to brute-force attacks.
  • Regular Review: Periodically audit and remove any firewall rules that are no longer necessary.
Safety Note

Misconfigured firewall rules can lock you out of your VMs. Always test new rules carefully and have a rollback plan or alternative access method.
