Importance of IP Address Planning

Designing for Growth and Avoiding Conflicts

Why It Matters
  • Future Growth: Ensure sufficient IP space for new resources.
  • Resource Efficiency: Avoid wasting large blocks of IP addresses.
  • Hybrid Connectivity: Prevent conflicts with on-premises networks.
  • Network Stability: Avoid routing issues and communication failures.
Best Practices
  • Right-size Subnets: Allocate ranges that are adequate but not excessive.
  • Document Everything: Keep a clear record of your IP allocations.
  • Plan for Expansion: Reserve space for future subnets and services.
  • Avoid Overlaps: Crucial for any network integration (on-prem, other clouds).
Key Takeaway

Meticulous IP address planning is foundational for a stable, scalable, and well-integrated Google Cloud network. Prevent overlaps at all costs.
