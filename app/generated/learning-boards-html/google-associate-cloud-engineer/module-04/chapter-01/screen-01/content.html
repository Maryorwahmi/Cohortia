Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) Networks

Your Global Network Backbone in Google Cloud

A VPC network in Google Cloud is a global, software-defined network that provides essential networking functionality for your cloud resources. It simplifies cross-region communication and forms the secure foundation for your applications.

Key Idea

Google Cloud VPC networks provide a private, global network for your resources, simplifying cross-region communication and disaster recovery.
