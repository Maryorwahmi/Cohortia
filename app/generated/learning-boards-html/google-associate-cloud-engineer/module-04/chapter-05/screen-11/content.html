Cloud VPN vs. Cloud Interconnect: Making the Right Choice

Evaluating bandwidth, latency, and cost for your hybrid needs

Decision Factors

  • **Bandwidth Requirements:** How much data needs to be transferred?
  • **Latency Sensitivity:** Are your applications sensitive to network delays?
  • **Cost Considerations:** Budget for setup and ongoing operational expenses.
  • **Setup Complexity:** How quickly do you need to establish connectivity?
Consider This

For initial deployments or less critical workloads, Cloud VPN offers a quick and cost-effective entry point into hybrid cloud.

Cloud VPN

  • Up to 3 Gbps per tunnel
  • Variable latency (public internet)
  • Lower cost
  • Quicker setup

Cloud Interconnect

  • Up to 100 Gbps per connection
  • Consistent low latency (direct link)
  • Higher cost
  • Longer setup time
Key Takeaway

The choice between Cloud VPN and Cloud Interconnect depends on a careful evaluation of your bandwidth, latency, cost, and setup time requirements.
