Direct Connection

Cloud Interconnect: High-Performance Direct Links

Achieving superior bandwidth and consistent latency

Key Advantages

  • **High Bandwidth:** Up to 100 Gbps per connection for demanding workloads.
  • **Low Latency:** Direct physical connection bypasses public internet for consistent performance.
  • **Enhanced Security:** Private connection reduces exposure to internet threats.
Ideal For

Large-scale data migrations, real-time analytics, and mission-critical applications requiring guaranteed performance.

Dedicated Interconnect

Direct physical connection from your data center to a Google POP.

Partner Interconnect

Connects to Google's network through a supported service provider.

Key Takeaway

Cloud Interconnect offers superior performance for hybrid networks through direct physical connections, with Dedicated and Partner options catering to different deployment scenarios.
