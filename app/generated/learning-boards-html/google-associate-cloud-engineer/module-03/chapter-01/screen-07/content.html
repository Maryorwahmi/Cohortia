Storage Tiers

Hot vs. Cool Data: Standard and Nearline

Balancing performance and cost for frequently and less frequently accessed data.

Standard Storage (Hot Data)

  • Access Frequency: Frequently accessed (multiple times a month).
  • Latency: Very low latency, ideal for active data.
  • Cost: Higher storage cost, no retrieval fees.
  • Use Cases: Active website content, mobile app data, real-time analytics.

Nearline Storage (Cool Data)

  • Access Frequency: Less than once a month.
  • Latency: Slightly higher retrieval latency.
  • Cost: Lower storage cost than Standard, with retrieval fees and 30-day minimum duration.
  • Use Cases: Backups, disaster recovery, long-tail content.
Key Takeaway

Choose Standard for active data needing instant access, and Nearline for less frequent access where cost savings are a priority.
