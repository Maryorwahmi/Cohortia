Storage Tiers

Cold vs. Coldest Data: Coldline and Archive

Optimizing for infrequent access and long-term retention.

Coldline Storage (Cold Data)

  • Access Frequency: Less than once a quarter.
  • Latency: Higher retrieval latency than Nearline.
  • Cost: Very low storage cost, with higher retrieval fees and 90-day minimum duration.
  • Use Cases: Archiving, compliance data, infrequently accessed logs.

Archive Storage (Coldest Data)

  • Access Frequency: Less than once a year.
  • Latency: Highest retrieval latency.
  • Cost: Lowest storage cost, with highest retrieval fees and 365-day minimum duration.
  • Use Cases: Long-term archives, regulatory compliance, rarely accessed data.
Key Takeaway

Coldline and Archive are for increasingly infrequent access, offering significant storage cost reductions at the expense of higher retrieval costs and longer minimum durations.
