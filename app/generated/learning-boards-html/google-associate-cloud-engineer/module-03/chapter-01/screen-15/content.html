Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Storage Classes

Different tiers (Standard, Nearline, Coldline, Archive) optimized for access frequency and cost.

Object Lifecycle Management

Automated rules for transitioning objects between storage classes or deleting them based on criteria.

`gsutil` & IAM

`gsutil` is the CLI tool; IAM manages granular access control for buckets and objects.

Chapter Summary

Effective Cloud Storage management relies on choosing the right storage class, automating data lifecycle, and securing access with `gsutil` and IAM.
