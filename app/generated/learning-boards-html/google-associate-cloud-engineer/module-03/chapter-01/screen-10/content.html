Security & Permissions

Granular Access with IAM Roles

Controlling who can do what in Cloud Storage.

Identity and Access Management (IAM)

  • Primary method for access control in Cloud Storage.
  • Grants granular permissions to users, groups, or service accounts.
  • Ensures the principle of least privilege is applied.
  • Recommended over ACLs for bucket-level permissions.

Access Control Lists (ACLs)

  • Legacy system for fine-grained control over individual objects.
  • More complex to manage at scale compared to IAM.
  • Generally not recommended for new implementations.
Key Takeaway

IAM is the preferred and most effective way to manage access to Cloud Storage, ensuring security through granular role-based permissions.

Common Cloud Storage IAM Roles

roles/storage.objectViewer

Read-only access to objects. Ideal for serving static content.

roles/storage.objectCreator

Ability to create objects. Needed for upload services.

roles/storage.objectAdmin

Full control over objects (read, write, delete). For data managers.

roles/storage.admin

Full control over buckets and objects. For administrators.
