Retrieval Costs

Accessing Archived Data

Archive storage incurs charges when data is retrieved. This is a key differentiator from Standard storage and is designed for infrequent access patterns.

  • •Higher per-GB retrieval fees compared to Nearline/Coldline.
  • •Retrieval operations also incur charges.
Minimum Storage Duration

365-Day Policy

Objects stored in Archive have a minimum storage duration, typically 365 days. Deleting an object before this period incurs a pro-rated charge for the remaining days.

  • •Ensures commitment to long-term storage.
  • •Avoid premature deletion to prevent extra costs.
Common Mistake

Underestimating Retrieval Costs

Failing to calculate potential retrieval costs for Archive storage can lead to unexpected bills if data access patterns are more frequent than anticipated. Always model your access patterns carefully.

Key Takeaway

Always evaluate your data's expected access frequency and retention period to choose the most cost-effective storage class, considering both storage and retrieval costs.
