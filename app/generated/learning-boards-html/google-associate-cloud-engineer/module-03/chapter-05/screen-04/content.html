Definition

Archive Storage Class

The most cost-effective Cloud Storage option, designed for long-term data retention with very infrequent access. Ideal for regulatory compliance, legal hold, or historical record-keeping.

Lowest Storage Cost

Higher Retrieval Costs

Key Takeaway

Archive storage is perfect for data you need to keep but rarely touch, offering significant cost savings on storage itself.

Use Case Example

Regulatory Compliance Data

A financial institution needs to retain transaction logs for 7 years to meet regulatory requirements. These logs are rarely accessed after the first month, but must be available if an audit occurs.

Store in Archive for 7 years to minimize costs.

Accept higher retrieval costs for rare access.
