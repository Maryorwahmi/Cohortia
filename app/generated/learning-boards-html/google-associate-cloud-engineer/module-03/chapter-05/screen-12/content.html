Common Mistakes

Avoiding Pitfalls in Archiving & Transfer

Underestimating Retrieval Costs

Always calculate potential retrieval costs for Archive. Frequent access can negate storage savings.

Premature Deletion from Archive

Deleting objects before the 365-day minimum storage duration incurs pro-rated charges.

Incorrect IAM for STS

Ensure the Storage Transfer Service account has necessary permissions on both source and destination.

Network Bandwidth Limitations

For petabyte-scale or slow connections, consider Transfer Appliance over network transfers.

Key Takeaway

Proactive planning and understanding the nuances of Archive costs and STS permissions are crucial for successful data management.
