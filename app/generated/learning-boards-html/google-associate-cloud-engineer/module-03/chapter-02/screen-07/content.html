Disk Type Deep Dive

Balanced Persistent Disk

The versatile choice for general-purpose workloads.

SSD-Backed Performance

Offers higher IOPS and throughput than Standard disks, leveraging SSD technology.

General-Purpose Workloads

Ideal for web servers, small to medium databases, and application servers needing balanced performance.

Cost-Effective Performance

Provides a good performance-to-cost ratio, making it a versatile and popular choice.

Key Takeaway

Balanced Persistent Disks are a versatile, cost-effective choice for most general-purpose workloads, offering solid SSD-backed performance.
