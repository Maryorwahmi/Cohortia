Disk Type Deep Dive

SSD Persistent Disk

Unleashing maximum performance for demanding workloads.

Highest Performance

Delivers very high IOPS and throughput, with extremely low latency for critical applications.

Demanding Workloads

Perfect for high-performance databases, real-time analytics, and applications where I/O speed is paramount.

Most Expensive

The highest cost per GB, requiring careful consideration of workload needs to justify the investment.

Key Takeaway

SSD Persistent Disks are for the most demanding, latency-sensitive workloads where maximum I/O performance is non-negotiable, despite higher costs.
