Understanding Disk Performance: IOPS & Throughput

How disk size impacts your Persistent Disk's capabilities.

IOPS (Input/Output Operations Per Second)

Measures the number of read/write operations a disk can perform per second. Critical for transactional databases.

  • •Higher for SSDs
  • •Scales with disk size

Throughput (MB/s)

Measures the rate at which data can be read from or written to the disk. Important for large data transfers.

  • •Higher for larger disks
  • •Scales with disk size

Performance Scales with Size

Larger Persistent Disks, regardless of type, generally provide higher maximum IOPS and throughput limits. This means you must size disks for both capacity and required performance.

Key Takeaway

To achieve desired performance, provision Persistent Disks not just for storage capacity, but also for the IOPS and throughput requirements of your workload.
