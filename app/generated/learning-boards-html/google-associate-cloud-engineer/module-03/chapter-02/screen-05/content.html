Disk Types Overview

Choosing the Right Persistent Disk Type

Matching disk characteristics to your workload's performance and cost needs.

Standard Persistent Disk

Cost-effective HDD storage for sequential I/O and large, non-critical data processing.

  • •Good throughput
  • •Lower IOPS
  • •Magnetic storage

Balanced Persistent Disk

Offers a balance of performance and cost, suitable for general-purpose workloads like web servers.

  • •Higher IOPS than Standard
  • •Good throughput
  • •SSD-backed

SSD Persistent Disk

Highest performance with very high IOPS and throughput, ideal for demanding, low-latency applications.

  • •Very high IOPS
  • •Very high throughput
  • •SSD-backed
Key Takeaway

Aligning your Persistent Disk choice with your workload's I/O profile is crucial for optimizing both performance and cost efficiency.
