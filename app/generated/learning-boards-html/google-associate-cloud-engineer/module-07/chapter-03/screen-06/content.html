Enhanced Control

Customer-Managed Encryption Keys (CMEK)

Taking control of your encryption keys with Cloud KMS.

Customer Manages Keys

You create and manage your encryption keys using Cloud Key Management Service (KMS).

Cloud KMS Role

KMS is a fully managed service for creating, storing, and managing cryptographic keys securely.

Key Takeaway

CMEK provides superior control over encryption keys, making it ideal for stringent compliance and data governance requirements.

CMEK vs. GMEK

Understanding the key differences in control.

GMEK: Google Control

Google manages all aspects of key lifecycle, providing transparent encryption.

CMEK: Customer Control

You manage the keys in KMS; Google uses your keys for encryption/decryption. Disabling your key renders data unreadable.
