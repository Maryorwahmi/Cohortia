Advanced Control

Customer-Supplied Encryption Keys (CSEK)

Managing encryption keys entirely outside of GCP.

External Key Management

You generate and manage your encryption key entirely outside of GCP, providing it with each upload/download.

Highest Control, Highest Overhead

Offers maximum control as Google never stores your key, but places full burden of key management (rotation, storage) on you.

Key Takeaway

CSEK provides ultimate key control but demands significant operational responsibility, making it suitable for niche, highly regulated use cases.

CSEK Workflow

How customer-supplied keys are used.

Data Operation

You initiate an upload or download operation for an object in Cloud Storage.

Supply Key

You provide your encryption key to GCP as part of the request for that specific operation.

Encrypt/Decrypt

GCP uses your supplied key to perform the cryptographic operation, then immediately discards the key.
