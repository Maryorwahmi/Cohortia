Choosing the Right Role for the Job

Selecting the appropriate IAM role is critical for security. Understand the scope and granularity of each role type to apply the principle of least privilege effectively.

Primitive Roles

Broadest access: Owner, Editor, Viewer. Apply project-wide. Generally too permissive for production environments.

Predefined Roles

Service-specific: Curated by Google for common tasks (e.g., roles/compute.instanceAdmin.v1). Offer a good balance of functionality and least privilege. Always start here.

Custom Roles

Most granular: User-defined roles with precise sets of permissions. Use when predefined roles are not specific enough. Requires careful definition.

Role Type Comparison

TypeScopeGranularityUse Case
PrimitiveProject-wideLowInitial setup, non-prod
PredefinedService-specificMediumCommon tasks, most prod
CustomHighly specificHighUnique requirements
Key Takeaway

Always prioritize predefined roles, and only resort to custom roles when predefined options are insufficient for your specific security needs.
