Command-Line Policy Inspection

The gcloud CLI provides direct access to IAM policies, essential for detailed inspection and understanding how permissions are applied across your resources.

Why use gcloud?

For scripting, automation, or when a raw, programmatic view of policy bindings is required. It complements the Console's visual tools.

Inherited Policies Pitfall

get-iam-policy on a child resource only shows explicit bindings. Inherited policies from parent resources (Folders, Organization) are not directly displayed.

gcloud Commands for IAM Policy

# Get IAM policy for a project
gcloud projects get-iam-policy my-gcp-project-123

# Get IAM policy for a specific Cloud Storage bucket
gcloud storage buckets get-iam-policy gs://my-bucket-name
Key Takeaway

When troubleshooting with gcloud, remember to check parent resources for inherited policies to get a complete view of access.
