Foundational Service Account Security

Service accounts are critical non-human identities. Implementing core best practices ensures they operate securely and minimize risk.

What are Service Accounts?

Non-human identities used by applications and VMs to interact with GCP services. Essential for automated tasks.

Risk of Over-Privilege

Granting excessive permissions to a service account creates a large attack surface. A compromised account could lead to widespread unauthorized access.

Key Best Practices

1. Least Privilege

Grant only the roles and permissions absolutely necessary for the service account to perform its specific function. No more, no less.

2. Dedicated Service Accounts

Create separate service accounts for different applications or components. This limits the 'blast radius' if one account is compromised, containing the damage.

Key Takeaway

Always adhere to the principle of least privilege and use dedicated service accounts to minimize security risks and contain potential breaches.
