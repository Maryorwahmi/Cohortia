Key Concepts for SCC Mastery

Solidifying your understanding of SCC's capabilities and processes.

Event Threat Detection

An SCC integrated service that monitors logs for advanced threats like cryptomining or compromised credentials.

Asset Discovery

SCC's ability to automatically identify and inventory all GCP resources in your organization.

Remediation

The process of fixing or resolving identified security issues based on SCC's recommendations.

Summary Takeaway

SCC provides comprehensive security from asset discovery and threat detection to actionable remediation steps.
