Effective Finding Management: Prioritization and Investigation

Establishing a clear workflow to address security issues.

1

Prioritization

Focus on Critical and High severity findings first, as they represent the most immediate risks.

2

Investigation

Understand the root cause, potential impact, and context of the finding before taking action.

Example Scenario: Prioritizing a Critical Finding

A 'Critical' finding for an unencrypted Cloud Storage bucket containing sensitive customer data would be prioritized immediately. Investigation would confirm the data type, access patterns, and potential exposure before remediation.

Key Takeaway

A clear process for prioritization and investigation ensures that critical security issues are addressed effectively and thoughtfully.
