Managing Noise: Muting Findings and Avoiding Pitfalls

Optimizing your SCC workflow and understanding common mistakes.

Muting Findings

Hide findings that are false positives or intentionally configured (e.g., public static website bucket) to reduce noise.

Benefit

Allows security teams to focus on truly actionable and critical alerts without being overwhelmed.

Common Mistakes

  • Enabling SCC without a clear remediation workflow.
  • Ignoring 'Low' or 'Medium' severity findings.
  • Remediating without careful consideration of impact.

Consequence

Can lead to security clutter, missed critical issues, or unintended service disruptions.

Safety Notes

  • SCC is a detection and visibility tool; it does not automatically fix issues.
  • Always ensure remediation actions are performed carefully to avoid unintended consequences.
  • Grant appropriate IAM roles (e.g., roles/securitycenter.admin, roles/securitycenter.viewer) to maintain SCC's own security.
Key Takeaway

Optimize SCC by muting non-critical findings and establishing clear workflows, while being mindful of common operational pitfalls.
