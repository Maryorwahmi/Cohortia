Navigating the SCC Dashboard and Findings

Gaining insights from the centralized security console.

1

Overview Dashboard

Provides a high-level summary of your security posture, including total findings, severity distribution, and asset inventory.

2

Findings Tab & Filtering

Drill down into specific findings, filter by project, service, category, or severity to focus your investigation.

Detailed Finding Information

Click on any finding to view its full details, including the affected resource, the specific issue, and recommended remediation steps.

Example Scenario

If SCC reports a 'Public Storage Bucket' finding, you'll see the bucket name, project, and a direct link to remediation instructions.

Key Takeaway

The SCC dashboard is your central hub for monitoring and investigating security findings across your GCP environment.
