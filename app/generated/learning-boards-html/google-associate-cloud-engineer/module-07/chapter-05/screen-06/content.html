Scanning for Misconfigurations and Advanced Threats

Understanding Security Health Analytics and Event Threat Detection.

Security Health Analytics (SHA)

SHA automatically scans your GCP assets for misconfigurations and compliance violations, acting as a preventative measure.

  • Focus: Misconfigurations, compliance violations.
  • Examples: Open firewall ports, unencrypted storage buckets, overly permissive IAM policies.
  • Benefit: Proactive identification of weaknesses before exploitation.

Scenario

SHA detects a Cloud Storage bucket configured for public access, alerting you to a potential data exposure risk.

Event Threat Detection (ETD)

ETD monitors Cloud Logging streams for signs of advanced threats, providing real-time detection of active attacks.

  • Focus: Advanced threats, malicious activity patterns.
  • Examples: Brute-force attacks, compromised credentials, cryptomining.
  • Benefit: Reactive detection of ongoing attacks for rapid response.

Scenario

ETD identifies unusual outbound network traffic from a Compute Engine instance, indicating a potential cryptomining threat.

Key Takeaway

SHA and ETD complement each other, offering both preventative vulnerability scanning and real-time threat detection.
