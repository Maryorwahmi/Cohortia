Avoiding Compliance Pitfalls

Common misconceptions and safety considerations for Organization Policies

Active Configuration

Compliance is not automatic; you must actively configure your environment, manage IAM, encrypt data, and log activities.

Regular Review

Continuously review and update Organization Policies as requirements evolve and new constraints become available.

Test Thoroughly

Always test policies in a controlled environment before broad application to prevent unintended operational impacts.

Common Mistake: Automatic Compliance Assumption

Assuming that because GCP is compliant, your application automatically is too. You must actively configure your environment to meet specific obligations.

Safety Note: Policy Impact

Poorly configured Organization Policies can block critical operations. Always test in a controlled environment and ensure appropriate IAM roles (e.g., roles/orgpolicy.policyAdmin).

Key Takeaway

Achieving compliance requires active configuration and continuous review, not just relying on GCP's inherent compliance. Always test Organization Policies carefully to avoid unintended operational impacts.
