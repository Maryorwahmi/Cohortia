Essential Concepts for Governance

Recap of foundational ideas

Resource Hierarchy

The organizational structure of GCP resources (Organization > Folders > Projects > Resources) used for consistent policy enforcement.

Organization Policies

Constraints that programmatically restrict the configuration of GCP resources across an organization or folder, focusing on 'what resources can do'.

Compliance Frameworks

Sets of rules and guidelines (e.g., HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR) that organizations must adhere to, with GCP providing a compliant platform.

Summary Takeaway

The GCP Resource Hierarchy, Organization Policies, and adherence to Compliance Frameworks are fundamental for establishing robust cloud governance and security.
