Meeting Industry Compliance: HIPAA & PCI DSS

How GCP supports healthcare and payment data standards

Platform Support

GCP provides a certified and secure platform designed to help customers meet various industry-specific compliance requirements.

Customer Responsibility

While GCP offers the tools, customers are responsible for configuring their applications and resources in a compliant manner.

Key Features

Features like CMEK, audit logging, VPC Service Controls, and Cloud Armor contribute to compliance readiness.

HIPAA (Healthcare)

For Protected Health Information (PHI). GCP offers Business Associate Addendum (BAA) and features like CMEK, audit logging, and access controls.

PCI DSS (Payment Cards)

For handling credit card data. GCP services like VPC Service Controls, Cloud Armor, and robust logging contribute to compliance.

Key Takeaway

GCP provides a strong foundation with features and certifications to help meet HIPAA and PCI DSS, but customer configuration of applications and resources is paramount for full compliance.
