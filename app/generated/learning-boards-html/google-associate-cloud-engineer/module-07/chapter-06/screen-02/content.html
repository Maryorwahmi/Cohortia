01

Understand GCP Resource Hierarchy

Grasp how the Organization > Folders > Projects > Resources structure is fundamental for enforcing consistent organizational policies and compliance across your cloud environment. This hierarchical model ensures that policies set at higher levels are inherited by all child resources, providing a powerful mechanism for centralized control and simplified management.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Implement Organization Policies

Learn to apply Organization Policies to enforce specific constraints across your projects and folders. This includes restricting resource locations, disabling external IP addresses, or requiring Customer-Managed Encryption Keys, ensuring your cloud configurations align with security and compliance mandates.
