Organization Policies: What They Are

Programmatically restricting resource configurations

Definition

Organization Policies are a key governance tool that allows you to programmatically restrict the configuration of your GCP resources.

Focus

Unlike IAM ('who can do what'), Organization Policies focus on 'what resources can do,' enforcing constraints on resource configurations.

Purpose

They ensure that resource deployments adhere to predefined organizational standards, enhancing security and compliance across the cloud environment.

Organization Policies vs. IAM

Organization Policies

What resources can do: Enforce constraints on resource configurations.

  • Restrict locations
  • Disable external IPs
  • Require CMEK
IAM (Identity & Access Management)

Who can do what: Control user/service account permissions.

  • Grant 'Compute Admin' role
  • Allow 'Storage Object Viewer'
  • Define custom roles
Key Takeaway

Organization Policies are a powerful, programmatic way to enforce configuration standards across your GCP resources, complementing IAM by focusing on resource behavior.
