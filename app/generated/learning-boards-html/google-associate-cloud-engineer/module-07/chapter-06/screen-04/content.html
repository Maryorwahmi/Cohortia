The Foundation: GCP Resource Hierarchy

Organizing resources for consistent policy enforcement

Core Principle

The GCP Resource Hierarchy (Organization > Folders > Projects > Resources) is the backbone for applying policies consistently.

Inheritance Model

Policies defined at higher levels (Organization or Folder) are automatically inherited by all child resources, simplifying management.

Centralized Control

This structure enables powerful centralized control, ensuring compliance and security standards are met across the entire cloud environment.
