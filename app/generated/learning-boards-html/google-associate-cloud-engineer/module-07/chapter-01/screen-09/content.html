Policy Structure

Anatomy of an IAM Policy

An IAM policy is a collection of bindings, associating members with specific roles on a resource.

Components of a Policy

  • Bindings: Each binding links one or more members to a single role.
  • Policy Evaluation: IAM aggregates all policies from the resource up the hierarchy to determine effective permissions.
  • Access Decision: If any policy grants the required permission, access is allowed; otherwise, it's denied.
Key Takeaway

IAM policies are evaluated hierarchically, meaning effective permissions are a sum of all policies from the resource up to the organization level.

IAM Policy Binding Example

Member: user:alice@example.com

Role: roles/compute.viewer

Resource: my-gcp-project-123

Policy Snippet (Conceptual):

bindings:[- role: roles/compute.viewer members:[- 'user:alice@example.com']]
