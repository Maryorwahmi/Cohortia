Core Concept

Defining Identity and Access Management

IAM answers the fundamental question: "Who can do what on which resource?"

The Pillars of IAM

  • • Who: The identity requesting access (user, service account, group).
  • • What: The actions permitted (defined by roles and permissions).
  • • Which Resource: The specific GCP asset the actions apply to (project, bucket, VM).
Key Takeaway

IAM is crucial for cloud security, providing granular control to prevent unauthorized access and ensure compliance across all GCP resources.

IAM Policy Evaluation
