Key Concepts: IAM Fundamentals

Essential terms and principles for managing access on GCP.

Member

An identity (Google account, service account, group, or domain) that can be granted access.

Role

A collection of permissions that defines what actions a member can perform on a resource.

Permission

A specific authority to perform an action on a resource, e.g., compute.instances.start.

Summary Takeaway

IAM is built upon the interaction of Members, Roles, and Permissions to establish granular access control across your GCP environment.
