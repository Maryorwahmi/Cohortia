IAM Components

Defining Permissions with Roles

Roles are collections of permissions that specify 'what' actions a member can perform on GCP resources.

Types of IAM Roles

  • Primitive Roles: Broad roles (Owner, Editor, Viewer). Generally avoid for least privilege.
  • Predefined Roles: Fine-grained, service-specific (e.g., compute.viewer, storage.objectAdmin). Recommended.
  • Custom Roles: User-defined roles with specific permissions for unique use cases.
Key Takeaway

Always prioritize predefined or custom roles to adhere to the principle of least privilege, granting only the necessary permissions.
