Securing Applications with Service Accounts

Service accounts are non-human identities used by applications or VMs to interact with GCP services.

Benefits of Service Accounts

  • No Hardcoded Credentials: Eliminates the risk of embedding sensitive user credentials in code.
  • Auditable Actions: Provides a clear audit trail for automated interactions with GCP services.
  • Least Privilege: Can be granted specific, minimal permissions required for the application's function.
Key Takeaway

Service accounts are critical for secure, automated interactions between your applications and GCP, embodying the principle of least privilege.

Example:

An application on a Compute Engine VM needs to read objects from a Cloud Storage bucket. Assigning a service account with the storage.objectViewer role to the VM allows secure, credential-free access.
