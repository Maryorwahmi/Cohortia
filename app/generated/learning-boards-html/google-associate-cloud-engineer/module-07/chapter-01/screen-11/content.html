Security Best Practice

Adhering to the Principle of Least Privilege

Grant only the minimum necessary permissions for a user or service account to perform its task.

Why Least Privilege Matters

  • Reduces Attack Surface: Limits what an attacker can do if an account is compromised.
  • Minimizes Blast Radius: Contains damage from accidental misconfigurations or malicious activity.
  • Enhances Compliance: Helps meet regulatory requirements for access control.
Key Takeaway

Always use the most restrictive predefined or custom roles that satisfy a member's operational needs, avoiding broad primitive roles.

Least Privilege vs. Over-Permissioning

Good Practice

Grant roles/appengine.deployer for App Engine deployments.

Bad Practice

Grant roles/editor for App Engine deployments.

Example Scenario:

A new developer needs to deploy applications to App Engine. Granting them roles/appengine.deployer is adhering to least privilege, while granting roles/editor would give them unnecessary broad access across the entire project.
