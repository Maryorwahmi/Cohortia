Resource Scope

Navigating the GCP Resource Hierarchy

GCP resources are organized hierarchically, from the top-level Organization down to individual resources.

Levels of the Hierarchy

  • Organization: The root node for all GCP resources.
  • Folders: Group projects for easier management and policy application.
  • Projects: Core containers for resources, billing, and services.
  • Resources: Individual services like VMs, buckets, databases.
Key Takeaway

The GCP resource hierarchy is fundamental for organizing resources and forms the basis for how IAM policies are inherited and applied.

Analogy:

Think of it like a company's organizational chart: the CEO (Organization) oversees departments (Folders), which manage teams (Projects), who work on specific tasks (Resources).
