Introduction to Identity and Access Management (IAM) on GCP

Securely control who can do what on your cloud resources.

This chapter provides a comprehensive overview of GCP IAM, covering its core concepts, components, and best practices. You'll learn how to define and manage access permissions across your cloud environment, ensuring robust security and operational efficiency.

Key Takeaway

GCP IAM is fundamental for cloud security, enabling granular control over resource access by defining who can perform what actions on which resources.
