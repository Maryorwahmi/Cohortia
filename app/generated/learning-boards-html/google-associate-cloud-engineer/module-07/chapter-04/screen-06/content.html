Rule Components

Targeting Traffic: Instances, Tags, and Service Accounts

Precisely defining where your firewall rules apply.

Defining the 'Target'

  • •All instances in the network: Broadest scope, applies to every instance.
  • •Network Tags: Labels (e.g., web-server) applied to instances for logical grouping.
  • •Service Accounts: Identity associated with an instance, preferred for stronger security.
Why Service Accounts?

Ties the rule to an identity, offering stronger security and easier management compared to just tags.

Source and Destination

Ingress Source: Where incoming traffic originates (IP ranges, tags, service accounts).

Egress Destination: Where outgoing traffic is headed (IP ranges, tags, service accounts).

Example Scenario

Allow web servers (tagged web-server) to connect to database servers (tagged db-server) on port 3306.

Key Takeaway

Firewall rules can target instances using network tags for logical grouping or service accounts for identity-based, granular control.
