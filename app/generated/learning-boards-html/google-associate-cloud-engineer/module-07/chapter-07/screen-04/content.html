The Foundation of Security: Auditing, Logging, and Monitoring

Effective security isn't just about prevention; it's also about detection and response. This requires robust auditing, logging, and monitoring capabilities.

  • •Auditing: Recording activities to establish accountability and track changes.
  • •Logging: Centralizing and storing event data from various sources.
  • •Monitoring: Analyzing logs and metrics to detect anomalies and trigger alerts.
Key Takeaway

GCP's Cloud Logging and Cloud Monitoring provide deep visibility, enabling proactive security detection and rapid incident response.

Real-world Connection

Imagine a bank vault: prevention is the thick door, but auditing and monitoring are the cameras and alarm systems that detect if someone tries to tamper with it or enters without authorization.
