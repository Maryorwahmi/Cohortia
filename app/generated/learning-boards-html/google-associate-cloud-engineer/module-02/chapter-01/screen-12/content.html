Sustained & Committed Use Discounts

Maximizing savings on your Compute Engine spend.

Sustained Use Discounts (SUDs)

  • Automatic: Applied automatically to instances running for a significant portion of the month.
  • No Commitment: No upfront commitment required.
  • Savings: Up to 30% for instances running 100% of the time.
  • Ideal For: Workloads with consistent, but not necessarily predictable, uptime.

Example: SUDs in Action

If you run an e2-medium instance for 70% of the month, Google Cloud will automatically apply a sustained use discount to that portion of its runtime, reducing your overall cost without any action on your part. This is a passive but powerful saving mechanism.

Committed Use Discounts (CUDs)

  • Commitment Required: Commit to specific resource usage for 1 or 3 years.
  • Deeper Savings: Up to 57% (1-year) or 70% (3-year) compared to on-demand.
  • Predictable Workloads: Best for stable, long-running applications.
  • Planning: Requires forecasting your resource needs.
Key Takeaway

Leverage SUDs for automatic savings on consistent workloads and CUDs for significant discounts on predictable, long-term resource commitments. Analyze usage to maximize these benefits.
