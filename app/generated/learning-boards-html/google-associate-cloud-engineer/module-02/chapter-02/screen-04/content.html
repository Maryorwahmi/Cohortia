Understanding Instance Lifecycle States

Compute Engine instances move through various states, reflecting their operational status and resource allocation. Effective management requires understanding these transitions.

Key States

PROVISIONING, STAGING, RUNNING, STOPPING, TERMINATED, SUSPENDED.

Primary Interaction

You'll mostly work with RUNNING (active) and TERMINATED (stopped) instances.

Key Takeaway

Each state has unique billing and operational implications; choose actions wisely.
