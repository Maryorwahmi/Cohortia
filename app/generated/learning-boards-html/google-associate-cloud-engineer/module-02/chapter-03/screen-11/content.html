Load Balancing: Distributing Your Application Traffic

Ensuring High Availability and Scalability for Your Services

What is Load Balancing?

  • •Distributes incoming application traffic across multiple backend instances.
  • •Prevents any single instance from becoming a bottleneck.
  • •Crucial for achieving high availability and scalability.
Key Takeaway

Load balancers are essential for distributing traffic, ensuring your applications remain performant, available, and scalable.

HTTP(S) Load Balancer

  • •A global external load balancer for HTTP(S) traffic.
  • •Provides global reach, intelligent routing, and SSL offloading.
  • •Integrates with Managed Instance Groups for auto-scaling and auto-healing.
  • •Ideal for public-facing web applications and APIs.

Why Load Balancing Matters

Without load balancing, a single point of failure or traffic spike could bring down your entire application, leading to poor user experience and lost revenue.
