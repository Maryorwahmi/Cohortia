Core Concept

Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) Networks

The Foundation of Your Google Cloud Network

VPC Network Definition

  • •A global, software-defined network that connects your GCP resources.
  • •Logically isolated from other networks, providing a secure environment.
  • •Subnets within a VPC can span multiple regions, enabling flexible architectures.
Key Takeaway

VPC networks are global containers for your regional subnets, providing isolated and scalable network infrastructure.

Subnet Definition

  • •Regional resources within a VPC network, defining IP address ranges (e.g., 10.128.0.0/20).
  • •Instances are placed within a specific subnet in a chosen region and zone.
  • •Crucial for organizing and allocating IP addresses for your VMs.

Common Mistake: IP Planning

Not planning IP address ranges carefully can lead to overlaps or insufficient addresses as your infrastructure grows. Always design subnets with future expansion in mind to avoid costly reconfigurations.
