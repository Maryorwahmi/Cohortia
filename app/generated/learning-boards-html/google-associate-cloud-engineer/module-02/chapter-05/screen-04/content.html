Why Kubernetes? Orchestrating Containers at Scale

Moving beyond single containers to managed, resilient deployments.

The Challenge

Managing many containers manually for high availability, scaling, and networking is complex and error-prone.

The Solution: Kubernetes

An open-source platform automating deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications.

Managed Service: GKE

Google Cloud's managed Kubernetes, handling master nodes, auto-upgrades, and integration with GCP services.
