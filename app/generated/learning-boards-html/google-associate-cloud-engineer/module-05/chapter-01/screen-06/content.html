Blue/Green: Instant Switchover

Maintaining two identical environments for zero-downtime releases

Strategy Overview

How Blue/Green Works

Two identical production environments are maintained: 'Blue' (current live version) and 'Green' (new version). The new 'Green' environment is deployed and tested in isolation. Once validated, traffic is instantly switched from 'Blue' to 'Green'.

Benefits & Trade-offs
  • Zero Downtime: Users experience no interruption during deployment.
  • Instant Rollback: Quickly revert to the 'Blue' environment if issues arise.
  • Higher Cost: Requires maintaining two full production environments.

Key Takeaway

Blue/green deployments offer zero-downtime releases and instant rollback capabilities by switching traffic between two identical, fully provisioned environments.
