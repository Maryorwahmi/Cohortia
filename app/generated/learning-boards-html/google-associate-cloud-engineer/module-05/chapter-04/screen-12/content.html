Rollbacks and Scaling: Adapting Your Application

Reverting to stable states and adjusting capacity.

Rollback Mechanism

  • •Revert Changes: Quickly undo a problematic deployment to a previous stable version.
  • •Revision History: Kubernetes automatically tracks Deployment revisions.
  • •Zero Downtime: Performed as a rolling update, minimizing service interruption.

Terminal Command: Rollback

# Revert to the previous deployment revision
kubectl rollout undo deployment/my-nginx-deployment
Key Takeaway

Deployments enable quick rollbacks to previous stable versions and dynamic scaling to adjust application capacity based on demand.

Scaling Your Application

  • •Adjust Capacity: Increase or decrease the number of Pod replicas.
  • •Handle Load: Scale up during peak traffic, scale down during low periods.
  • •Automated: Kubernetes manages Pod creation/termination to match desired replica count.

Terminal Command: Scale

# Scale the Nginx deployment to 5 replicas
kubectl scale deployment/my-nginx-deployment --replicas=5
Pro Tip

Always test rollbacks and scaling in a staging environment to understand their behavior before applying to production.
