Ensuring Application Health and Avoiding Pitfalls

Implement health checks and learn from common operational errors.

Health Checks

Automated checks configured with load balancers or Kubernetes probes to ensure traffic is only routed to healthy instances or Pods. Unhealthy components are removed or restarted.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Alert Fatigue: Too many non-critical alerts lead to ignoring real problems.

  • Insufficient Logging: Not enough context or relevant information for debugging.

  • Lack of Version Control: Makes rollbacks difficult and prone to errors.

  • Ignoring Health Checks: Leads to traffic being sent to unhealthy instances.

Real-world Connection

Consider an e-commerce application. If a backend service becomes unhealthy, health checks ensure that the load balancer stops sending new customer requests to it, preventing failed transactions and maintaining a positive user experience while the issue is resolved.

Key Takeaway

Health checks are vital for traffic management and reliability, while avoiding common operational mistakes is key to building robust and resilient cloud applications.
