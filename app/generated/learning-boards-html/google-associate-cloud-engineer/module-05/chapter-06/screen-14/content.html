Key Concepts

Critical strategies for application resilience

Alert Policies

Configurable rules in Cloud Monitoring that trigger notifications when specific conditions are met.

Rollback Strategy

A predefined plan and mechanism to revert a deployed application to a previous, stable version.

Health Checks

Automated checks performed by load balancers or orchestration systems to determine if an instance or Pod is healthy.

Summary Takeaway

Alert policies, rollback strategies, and health checks are crucial for proactive incident management, ensuring rapid recovery and continuous reliability of your applications on Google Cloud.
