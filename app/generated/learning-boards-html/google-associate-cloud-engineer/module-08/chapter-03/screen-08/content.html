Discount Mechanism

Committed Use Discounts (CUDs)

Substantial savings for predictable, long-term resource commitments

CUDs Explained

  • Active Commitment: Requires committing to resource usage (vCPU, memory) or spend.
  • Term Lengths: Available for 1-year or 3-year terms.
  • Significant Discounts: Up to 57% for 3-year Compute Engine commitments.
Key Takeaway

CUDs offer the highest discounts for predictable, long-term Compute Engine usage, but require careful commitment planning.

CUDs vs. SUDs

SUDs (Sustained Use)
  • Automatic
  • No commitment
  • Lower discount
CUDs (Committed Use)
  • Requires active commitment
  • 1 or 3-year terms
  • Higher discount

Commitment Example

You commit to 8 vCPUs and 32 GB of memory for 3 years in `us-central1`. You receive a significant discount on these resources, regardless of actual usage. Any usage above this commitment is charged at standard rates, potentially qualifying for SUDs.

Common Mistake: Committing based on peak usage, leading to paying for unused committed resources.
