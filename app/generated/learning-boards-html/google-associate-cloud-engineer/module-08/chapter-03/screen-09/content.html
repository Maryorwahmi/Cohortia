Management Tip

Viewing Existing CUDs

Checking your current Compute Engine commitments via gcloud CLI

Why Monitor CUDs?

  • Alignment: Ensure commitments still match your current and projected usage.
  • Cost Control: Avoid paying for unused committed resources if usage decreases.
  • Renewal Planning: Plan for renewals or adjustments as commitment terms end.
Key Takeaway

Regularly review your CUDs to ensure they align with your current usage and to maximize long-term savings.

Example: Viewing Existing CUDs

gcloud compute commitments list --region=us-central1

This command lists all active Compute Engine commitments in the `us-central1` region. The output typically includes commitment name, region, type, resources, and expiration date.

Console Navigation

While `gcloud` is powerful, you can also view CUDs in the GCP Console:

  • Navigate to Billing > Commitments.
  • Filter by product (e.g., Compute Engine) and region.
