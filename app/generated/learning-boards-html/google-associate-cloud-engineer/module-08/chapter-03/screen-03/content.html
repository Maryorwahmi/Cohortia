Apply Committed & Sustained Use Discounts

You will evaluate and apply CUDs and SUDs for predictable Compute Engine usage, maximizing discounts for stable workloads. This involves understanding the commitment process for CUDs and the automatic nature of SUDs to optimize your long-term spending.

Utilize Rightsizing Recommendations
