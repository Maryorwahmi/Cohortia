Filestore Cost Management

Optimizing managed NFS service costs for shared file systems.

Filestore Cost Drivers

Filestore costs are primarily determined by:

  • Capacity: The amount of storage provisioned.
  • Service Tier: Basic, Standard, or Premium, impacting performance (IOPS, throughput) and cost.

Cost Optimization Strategies

  • Right-size Capacity: Provision only the necessary storage.
  • Right-tier: Choose the service tier that matches your application's performance needs.
  • Monitor & Adjust: Regularly review usage and performance to identify optimization opportunities.
Key Takeaway

Provision only necessary Filestore capacity and choose the service tier (Basic, Standard, Premium) that matches performance needs.

Example: Media Rendering Farm

High-Performance Needs

A media rendering farm requires high throughput and low latency. A Premium Filestore instance, despite its higher cost, is justified here to prevent bottlenecks.

Development Environment

For a development or testing environment, a Basic or Standard tier might be sufficient, saving significant costs compared to Premium.
