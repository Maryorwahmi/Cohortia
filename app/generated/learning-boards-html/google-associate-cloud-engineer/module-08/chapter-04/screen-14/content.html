Avoiding Common Storage Cost Mistakes

Proactive strategies to prevent unnecessary cloud storage expenses.

Common Cost Pitfalls

  • Over-tiering: Storing all data in Standard storage, even cold archives.
  • Neglecting OLM: Failing to automate data tiering and deletion.
  • Unmanaged Snapshots: Not deleting old or unneeded Persistent Disk snapshots.
  • Over-provisioning: Allocating more disk/Filestore capacity or a higher tier than required.

Safety & Best Practices

Proactive management is key. Always:

  • Test OLM deletion rules on non-critical data.
  • Ensure proper backup and recovery procedures.
  • Regularly review and right-size all storage resources.
Key Takeaway

Avoid common mistakes like over-tiering and neglecting OLM through proactive management and careful automation.

Example: Costly Scenarios

Unmanaged Log Files

Application logs continuously written to Standard storage, never tiered or deleted, leading to ever-growing, expensive storage.

Accidental OLM Deletion

A misconfigured OLM rule deletes critical production data after 7 days instead of 7 years, causing irreversible data loss and operational disruption.
