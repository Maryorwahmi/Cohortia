Archive Storage: Long-Term Archival

The most cost-effective option for data accessed less than once a year.

Characteristics

  • Access Frequency: Less than once a year.
  • Latency: Highest latency (hours).
  • Availability: High availability.
  • Cost: Lowest storage cost, highest data retrieval cost.
  • Use Cases: Long-term compliance, historical records, deep archives.

Cost Implications & Best Practices

Archive storage is designed for maximum storage cost savings. However, retrieval costs are substantial, and latency is high. Always verify access patterns before choosing Archive to avoid unexpected expenses.

Common Pitfall

Underestimating retrieval costs. If data is accessed more than once a year, a higher tier might be more cost-effective overall.

Key Takeaway

Archive storage offers the lowest storage costs for data accessed yearly, with the highest retrieval fees and latency.

Example: Legal Hold Data

Seven-Year Compliance Records

Data that must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons for many years, but is almost never accessed unless a specific audit or legal request arises.

Historical Research Datasets

Vast datasets collected over decades for potential future research, where immediate access is not a concern.
