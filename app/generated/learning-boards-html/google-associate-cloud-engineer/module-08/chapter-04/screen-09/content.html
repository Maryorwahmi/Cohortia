GCS Cost Drivers

Beyond Storage: Operations and Retrieval Costs

Understanding the full cost picture for Google Cloud Storage.

Three Pillars of GCS Cost

  • Storage Cost: Per-gigabyte cost for storing data, varies by class.
  • Operations Cost: Fees for actions like listing, uploading, deleting objects.
  • Data Retrieval Cost: Fees for reading data, especially from colder tiers.

Impact of Colder Tiers

While colder tiers (Nearline, Coldline, Archive) offer significantly lower storage costs, they come with:

  • Higher data retrieval costs.
  • Potentially higher operational costs for certain actions.
  • Minimum storage durations.
Key Takeaway

GCS costs include storage, operations, and data retrieval, with colder tiers incurring higher retrieval fees.

Example: Cost Calculation Scenario

Scenario: 1TB in Archive Storage

Low monthly storage cost. If you retrieve 100GB, the retrieval fee could be substantial, potentially outweighing months of storage savings.

Scenario: Frequent Object Listing

An application that lists objects in a bucket containing millions of files every hour will incur significant operational costs over time, even if the objects themselves are small.
