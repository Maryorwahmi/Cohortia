Coldline Storage: For Infrequent Access

Optimized for data accessed less than once a quarter, with even lower storage costs.

Characteristics

  • Access Frequency: Less than once a quarter.
  • Latency: Higher latency (minutes).
  • Availability: High availability.
  • Cost: Even lower storage cost, higher data retrieval cost.
  • Use Cases: Disaster recovery data, older compliance archives.

Cost Implications & Considerations

Coldline offers significant storage savings but at the expense of higher retrieval costs and latency. It also typically includes a minimum storage duration, meaning you're billed for a set period even if data is deleted sooner.

Important Note

Be aware of minimum storage durations. Deleting data before this period can still incur charges for the full duration.

Key Takeaway

Coldline storage provides very low storage costs for data accessed quarterly, with higher retrieval fees and latency.

Example: Financial Audit Logs

Annual Audit Data

Data that needs to be retained for several years for regulatory compliance but is only accessed during annual audits.

Long-term Backup Copies

Secondary or tertiary backups that are only used in extreme disaster recovery scenarios, making quarterly access acceptable.
