Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Cloud Storage Classes

Different tiers of Google Cloud Storage optimized for various access frequencies and cost profiles.

Standard Storage

High-performance, low-latency storage for frequently accessed data.

Nearline Storage

Cost-effective storage for data accessed less than once a month.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the different GCS storage classes is crucial for optimizing costs based on data access frequency and performance needs.
