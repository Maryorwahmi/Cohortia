Key Concepts

Essential terms for cost analysis

Billing Reports

Visualizations in the Cloud Billing Console showing cost trends and breakdowns by service, project, and SKU.

Resource Labeling

Attaching key-value metadata to GCP resources for categorization and granular cost attribution.

Resource Hierarchy

The organizational structure (Organization > Folders > Projects) used for grouping and managing resources and costs.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering billing reports, resource labeling, and the resource hierarchy provides the foundational understanding for effective cloud cost management and optimization.
