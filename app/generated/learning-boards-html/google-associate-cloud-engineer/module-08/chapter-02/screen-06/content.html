What are Resource Labels?

Resource labels are key-value pairs that you can attach to almost any Google Cloud resource. They act as metadata, allowing you to organize and categorize your resources in a way that makes sense for your business needs.

Key: environment
Value: production

Key: team
Value: backend

Key: cost-center
Value: marketing

Key: project-phase
Value: development

Why Use Labels?
  • Cost Attribution: Break down costs by custom categories.
  • Resource Management: Group and filter resources easily.
  • Automation: Use labels in scripts for resource actions.
  • Policy Enforcement: Apply policies based on label values.
Key Takeaway

Consistent resource labeling is crucial for organizing resources, attributing costs accurately, and enabling powerful filtering in billing reports.

Common Mistake

Neglecting to implement a consistent labeling strategy from the beginning, leading to difficulties in retroactively attributing costs and managing resources.
