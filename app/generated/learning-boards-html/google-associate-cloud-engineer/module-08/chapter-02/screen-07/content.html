Hierarchy Levels
  • Organization: Top-level container for all GCP resources.
  • Folders: Group projects for common policies and cost views.
  • Projects: Core unit for resource management and billing.
Cost Attribution Benefit

Analyze aggregated costs at the folder level, providing a structured view of spending across departments or environments, simplifying budgeting and reporting for large organizations.

Key Takeaway

The resource hierarchy complements labeling by providing a structured, top-down approach to cost attribution and management at scale.
