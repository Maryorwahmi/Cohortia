How to Drill Down
  • Use time-range filters to isolate periods of high cost.
  • Apply service filters (e.g., 'Compute Engine') to narrow focus.
  • Further filter by SKU to identify specific resource types or operations.
Common Mistake

Ignoring small, consistent cost increases, which can accumulate into significant overspending over time.

Example Scenario

You notice a sudden surge in Compute Engine costs. By filtering the report to Compute Engine and then by SKU, you discover an unneeded `n1-standard-8` VM instance was left running in a test environment.

Key Takeaway

Granular filtering in billing reports enables proactive identification and resolution of cost anomalies, preventing budget overruns.
