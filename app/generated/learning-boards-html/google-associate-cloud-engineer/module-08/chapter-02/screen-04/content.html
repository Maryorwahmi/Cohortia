What are Billing Reports?

Visualizations within the Cloud Billing Console that show your GCP costs over time, broken down by various dimensions.

Key Features
  • Visualize costs over time (daily, monthly).
  • Filter by Project, Service, SKU, and Location.
  • Identify top cost-contributing services.
  • Spot overall spending trends.
Key Takeaway

The 'Reports' page provides essential high-level visibility into your cloud spending, acting as your initial dashboard for cost monitoring.
