Network Service Tiers: Premium vs. Standard

Choosing the right tier for optimal performance and cost efficiency.

Premium Tier

  • •Uses Google's global backbone network.
  • •Offers high performance and low latency.
  • •Optimized for global traffic, often reducing egress costs.
  • •Ideal for applications with a worldwide user base.

Standard Tier

  • •Uses standard internet service providers.
  • •Lower cost option, optimized for regional workloads.
  • •Traffic routing is less optimized, potentially higher global egress.
  • •Best for applications with users primarily in one region.
Key Takeaway

Align your Network Service Tier choice with your application's geographic reach to balance performance and cost effectively.
