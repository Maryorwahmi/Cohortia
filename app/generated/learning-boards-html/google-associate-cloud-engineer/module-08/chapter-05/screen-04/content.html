Core Concept

Understanding Egress Traffic

Data leaving Google Cloud is a primary cost factor.

Egress Traffic Defined

Data transferred OUT of Google Cloud to the internet or across regions. This is typically where charges apply.

Ingress Traffic Defined

Data transferred INTO Google Cloud. This traffic is generally free of charge.

Key Takeaway

Always consider the destination of your data. Cross-region and internet-bound data transfers are the main drivers of networking costs.
