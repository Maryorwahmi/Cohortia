Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Data Flow & IP Addresses

Essential terms for understanding networking costs in Google Cloud.

Egress Traffic

Data transferred out of Google Cloud, typically incurring charges, especially to the internet or across regions.

Ingress Traffic

Data transferred into Google Cloud, generally free of charge.

Internal IP Address

An IP address used for communication within a VPC network, typically free for inter-VM traffic within the same region.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the distinction between ingress and egress, and prioritizing internal IP communication, are foundational for managing GCP networking costs.
