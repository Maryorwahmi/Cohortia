Budget Alerts: Proactive Spending Notifications

Receiving timely warnings when spending thresholds are met.

1. Budget Set

Define your spending limit.

2. Thresholds Reached

50%, 90%, 100% of budget.

3. Notifications Sent

Email or Pub/Sub topic.

Why it matters

Budget alerts are crucial for proactive cost control, preventing unexpected overspending by providing timely warnings.

Pub/Sub Advantage

Sending alerts to a Pub/Sub topic enables programmatic responses, allowing for automated actions.

Key Takeaway

Budget alerts are monitoring tools that send notifications at spending thresholds, enabling proactive responses to manage cloud costs.
