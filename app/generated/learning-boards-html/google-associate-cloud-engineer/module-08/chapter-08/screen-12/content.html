Avoiding Common Cost Control Mistakes

Ensuring secure and effective automated cost management.

Neglecting Alerts

Setting budgets without configuring alerts or sending them to unmonitored channels makes them ineffective.

Untested Automation

Implementing automated actions without thorough testing can lead to unintended consequences, like shutting down critical resources.

Least Privilege IAM

Ensure Cloud Function IAM permissions are narrowly scoped to only perform necessary actions, preventing over-privilege.

Robust Logging & Testing

Implement thorough logging and always test automation in non-production environments first.

Key Takeaway

Careful planning, thorough testing, and strict adherence to least privilege are essential for secure and effective automated cost control.
