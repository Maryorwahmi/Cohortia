Core Concept

Understanding BigQuery's Pricing Model

BigQuery charges primarily for data storage and query processing.

Key Cost Components

  • Storage: Charges for data at rest, based on the amount of data stored.
  • Query Processing: Costs based on the amount of data scanned by your queries.
  • Streaming Inserts: Charges for real-time data ingestion into BigQuery.
  • Data Transfer: Costs for moving data out of BigQuery to other regions or services.

Pricing Models: On-Demand vs. Flat-Rate

On-Demand Pricing

Pay-per-TB scanned for query processing. Flexible for unpredictable, lower-volume workloads, but can become expensive for large, frequent queries.

Flat-Rate Pricing

Purchase dedicated 'slots' (compute capacity) for a fixed cost. More cost-effective for predictable, high-volume query workloads, offering consistent budgeting.

Key Takeaway

Choose the BigQuery pricing model that best aligns with your query volume and predictability to optimize costs.
