Core Concept

The GCP billing hierarchy structures how resources are organized and costs are attributed, ensuring clear financial oversight.

Organization

The top-level container, representing your company. It centrally manages policies and resources across all billing accounts and projects.

Billing Account

A financial account linked to a payment instrument, responsible for paying for GCP usage. Multiple projects can link to one billing account.

Project

The fundamental unit where all GCP resources are created and managed. Each project is associated with a single billing account.

Key Takeaway

Correctly linking projects to billing accounts is crucial for accurate cost allocation and preventing unexpected charges.
