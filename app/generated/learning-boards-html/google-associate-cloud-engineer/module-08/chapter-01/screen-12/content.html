Purpose

Provides a high-level overview of your GCP spending, broken down by various dimensions.

Identify Cost Drivers

Quickly see which projects, services, or SKUs are contributing most to your overall bill.

Monitor Spending Patterns

Track historical trends to detect unexpected spikes or identify consistent high-cost areas.

Inform Optimization

Use insights from reports to make informed decisions about where to focus cost-saving efforts.

Key Takeaway

Regularly reviewing billing reports is crucial for effective cost management, allowing you to catch unexpected spikes and identify optimization opportunities.
