Identify Billing Hierarchy

Recognize the structural components of GCP billing, including organizations, billing accounts, and projects. This skill is vital for correctly attributing costs and managing access across your cloud resources.

Explain Pricing Models

Articulate the differences between pay-as-you-go, sustained use, and committed use discounts. This enables you to select the most cost-effective option for diverse workloads and optimize your cloud expenditure.
