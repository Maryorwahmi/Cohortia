Module 8›Chapter 8.1›Committed Use Discounts
Mechanism

Discounts offered in exchange for committing to a specific amount of resource usage or service spend for 1 or 3 years.

Requires Commitment

You commit to a minimum spend or resource usage, regardless of actual consumption, for a fixed term.

Deeper Savings

Offers significantly higher discounts compared to pay-as-you-go or sustained use, rewarding long-term predictability.

Careful Planning Needed

Requires accurate forecasting of resource needs to avoid paying for unused committed capacity.

Key Takeaway

CUDs provide the deepest discounts for predictable, long-term workloads but demand careful planning to maximize savings and avoid waste.
