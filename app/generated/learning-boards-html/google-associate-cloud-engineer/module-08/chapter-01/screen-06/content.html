Module 8›Chapter 8.1›Sustained Use Discounts
Mechanism

Automatic discounts applied to Compute Engine instances based on their uptime within a billing month.

No Action Required

GCP automatically calculates and applies SUDs; no configuration or commitment is needed from the user.

Progressive Savings

The longer your Compute Engine VMs run, the higher the discount percentage you receive, up to a maximum.

Ideal Workloads

Best for stable, long-running, and predictable Compute Engine instances that operate for a significant portion of the month.

Key Takeaway

SUDs offer a simple, automatic way to reduce costs for consistent Compute Engine usage without any upfront commitment.
