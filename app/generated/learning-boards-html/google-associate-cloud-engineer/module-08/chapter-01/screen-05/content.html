Module 8›Chapter 8.1›Pay-as-you-go Pricing
Definition

A pricing model where you are charged only for the actual resources consumed, without upfront commitments.

Flexible & Granular

Billing is often down to the second for compute or per gigabyte for storage, providing precise cost alignment with usage.

Ideal for Experimentation

Perfect for new projects, testing, or unpredictable workloads where resource needs fluctuate rapidly.

No Upfront Costs

Avoids large initial investments, allowing you to scale resources up or down without financial penalty.

Key Takeaway

Pay-as-you-go offers maximum agility and cost transparency for dynamic cloud usage, but may not be the cheapest for stable workloads.
