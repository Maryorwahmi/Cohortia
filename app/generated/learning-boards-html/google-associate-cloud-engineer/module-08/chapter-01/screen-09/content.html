Module 8›Chapter 8.1›Quotas and Limits
Definition

Restrictions on the amount of a particular GCP resource that your project can consume.

Prevent Overspending

Acts as a safety net to prevent accidental provisioning of excessive or costly resources.

Platform Stability

Protects the overall GCP platform from abuse and ensures fair resource allocation for all users.

Indirect Cost Control

By limiting resource provisioning, quotas indirectly help manage and control your cloud expenditures.

Key Takeaway

Quotas are essential safeguards that prevent accidental overspending, protect the platform, and ensure equitable resource distribution.

Quota Example
Compute Engine CPUsDefault: 8 vCPUs
Cloud Storage BucketsDefault: 100 buckets
BigQuery QueriesDefault: 100 concurrent

Attempting to exceed these limits will result in an error.
