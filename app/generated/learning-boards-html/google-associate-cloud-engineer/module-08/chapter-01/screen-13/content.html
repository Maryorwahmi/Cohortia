Common Billing Pitfalls and Security Measures

Preventing unexpected costs and securing your billing accounts.

Common Mistakes

  • •Neglecting to link projects to the correct billing account.
  • •Failing to set up a billing account, preventing resource creation.
  • •Misunderstanding SUDs vs. CUDs, or over-committing to CUDs.

Security Best Practices

  • •Ensure only authorized personnel can modify billing accounts.
  • •Implement strong authentication (e.g., 2FA) for critical accounts.
  • •Regularly audit billing account permissions to prevent unauthorized access.
Key Takeaway

Proactive awareness of common billing mistakes and robust security practices are vital for maintaining cost control and account integrity.
