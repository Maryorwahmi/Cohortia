Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential terms from this chapter to remember.

Project

The fundamental organizational unit in GCP, acting as a container for all your resources, billing, and access control.

GCP Console

The web-based user interface for managing GCP resources, providing a graphical way to interact with your cloud environment.

Billing Account

Defines who pays for GCP resource usage and is linked to one or more projects for cost management.

Summary Takeaway

Projects, the GCP Console, and billing accounts are crucial for organizing, managing, and financially controlling your cloud resources effectively.
