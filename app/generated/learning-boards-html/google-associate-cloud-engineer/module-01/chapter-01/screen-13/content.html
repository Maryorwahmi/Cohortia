Chapter Review

Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms from this chapter to remember.

Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

Google's suite of cloud computing services, offering scalable and on-demand resources over the internet.

Region

A specific geographical location where GCP resources are hosted, providing isolation for disaster recovery.

Zone

An isolated physical data center within a region, crucial for achieving high availability and fault tolerance.

Summary Takeaway

GCP's foundation relies on its global network of regions and zones, providing the infrastructure for resilient and scalable cloud services.
