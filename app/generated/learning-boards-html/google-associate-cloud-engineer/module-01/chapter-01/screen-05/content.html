GCP's Global Infrastructure

Understanding regions and zones for high availability.

The Foundation of GCP

GCP's infrastructure is designed for global reach and resilience, ensuring your applications are always available and performant. It's a network of data centers strategically placed around the world.

  • •High Availability: Applications remain operational even if components fail.
  • •Low Latency: Data centers are close to users for faster response times.
  • •Fault Tolerance: Built-in redundancy to withstand outages.

Key Infrastructure Components

Regions

Independent geographical locations containing multiple zones.

Zones

Isolated physical data centers within a region.

Key Takeaway

GCP's global infrastructure, built on regions and zones, is crucial for deploying highly available and fault-tolerant applications.
