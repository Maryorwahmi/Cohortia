The GCP Compute Spectrum

Understand the trade-offs between control and management overhead across GCP's compute services.

1
IaaSMax Control
2
PaaSBalanced
3
ServerlessMin Control
Compute Engine

Virtual machines with full OS and infrastructure control. You manage everything.

Google Kubernetes Engine

Managed Kubernetes for containers. Google manages control plane, you manage nodes.

Cloud Run

Fully managed serverless containers. Google manages all infrastructure.

Key Takeaway

Choosing the right GCP compute service means understanding the trade-off between the control you desire and the management overhead you're willing to take on.
