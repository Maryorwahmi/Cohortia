What are MIGs?

Managed Instance Groups are collections of identical VMs that can be automatically scaled and healed, simplifying management and ensuring application resilience.

Benefits
  • Automatic scaling based on load.
  • Auto-healing by replacing unhealthy VMs.
  • Rolling updates for seamless application upgrades.
  • Regional deployment for disaster recovery.
Key Takeaway

MIGs are essential for building scalable and highly available applications on Compute Engine, automating critical operational tasks like scaling and instance replacement.
