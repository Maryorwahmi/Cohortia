Security Guidelines

Implementing IAM Best Practices

Strategies for robust and secure access management.

Least Privilege

Grant only minimum necessary permissions.

Use Google Groups

Assign roles to groups, not individuals.

Dedicated Service Accounts

Separate accounts for each application/service.

Separation of Duties

No single individual has excessive control.

Audit Regularly

Periodically review IAM policies for appropriateness.

Common Mistake

Granting the "Owner" or "Editor" role to developers or service accounts for convenience. Always strive for predefined or custom roles to ensure granular control and adhere to least privilege.

Key Takeaway

Adhering to IAM best practices like least privilege, separation of duties, and regular auditing is essential for maintaining a secure and compliant GCP environment.
