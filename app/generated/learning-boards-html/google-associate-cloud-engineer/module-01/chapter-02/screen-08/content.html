IAM Component 2

IAM Roles: What Actions are Allowed?

Collections of permissions that simplify access management.

Definition: A collection of permissions that can be granted to a member.

Purpose: Simplifies access management by bundling related permissions.

Example: The `roles/compute.instanceAdmin` role allows managing Compute Engine instances.

Key Takeaway

IAM roles are predefined sets of permissions that dictate what actions a member can perform, streamlining the process of granting and managing access.
