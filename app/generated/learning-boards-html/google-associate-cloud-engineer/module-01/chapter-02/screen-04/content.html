Core Concept

The GCP Resource Hierarchy

Organizing your cloud assets from top to bottom.

Organization: Represents your company, the root node for all GCP resources.

Folders: Group projects for departmental or environmental segregation.

Projects: Contain all individual GCP resources like VMs, databases, and storage.

Key Takeaway

The GCP Resource Hierarchy provides a structured way to organize and manage all your cloud resources, enabling consistent policy application.
