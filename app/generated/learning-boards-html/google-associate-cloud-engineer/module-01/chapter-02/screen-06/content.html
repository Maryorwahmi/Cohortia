Identity and Access Management (IAM)

Controlling who can do what with your GCP resources.

Definition: A system that defines granular permissions for GCP resources.

Principle of Least Privilege: Grant only the minimum necessary permissions for a task.

Why it matters: Prevents unauthorized access and actions, enhancing security and compliance.

Key Takeaway

IAM is GCP's robust system for defining granular permissions, ensuring that only authorized identities can perform specific actions on resources, adhering to the principle of least privilege.

Member (Who)User, Group, Service Account
Role (What)Collection of permissions
Resource (Where)Project, Bucket, VM, etc.
