Reinforcing Key Principles

Critical concepts for secure and efficient GCP operations.

Primitive Role

Broad, project-level roles (Owner, Editor, Viewer).

Predefined Role

Specific roles for particular GCP services (e.g., `storage.objectViewer`).

Custom Role

User-defined roles with a precise set of permissions.

Service Account

A special Google account used by applications and services to authenticate.

Least Privilege

A security principle of granting only the minimum necessary permissions.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding different role types and the strategic use of service accounts are vital for implementing the principle of least privilege and securing automated processes in GCP.
