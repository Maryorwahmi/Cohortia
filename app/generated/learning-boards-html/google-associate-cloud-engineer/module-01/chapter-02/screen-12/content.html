Service Accounts: Identity for Your Applications

Securing automated processes and virtual machines in GCP.

Definition: A special Google account for non-human users (applications, VMs).

Purpose: Enables applications to authenticate and access GCP resources securely.

Best Practice: Fundamental for least privilege and separation of concerns in automated workflows.

Key Takeaway

Service accounts are crucial for securing applications and services in GCP, providing a dedicated, non-human identity with specific permissions, thereby isolating application access from human user credentials.
