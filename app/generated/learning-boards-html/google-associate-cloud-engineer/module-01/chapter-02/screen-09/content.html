IAM Policies: The Access Rulebook

Combining members, roles, and resources to define access.

Member (Who)
Role (What)
Resource (Where)
= IAM Policy
Key Takeaway

An IAM policy is the complete statement that binds a member to a specific role on a particular GCP resource, effectively defining who can do what, where.
