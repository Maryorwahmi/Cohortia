Role Types

Primitive Roles: Broad Project Access

Understanding Owner, Editor, and Viewer roles and their implications.

Owner

Full control, including IAM management.

Editor

Manage all resources, but no IAM management.

Viewer

Read-only access to all resources.

Common Mistake

Granting primitive roles (especially Owner/Editor) to developers or service accounts for convenience. This can lead to significant security vulnerabilities due to excessive permissions.

Key Takeaway

Primitive roles offer broad, project-level access, granting extensive permissions but often violating the principle of least privilege, making them suitable only for specific, limited use cases.
