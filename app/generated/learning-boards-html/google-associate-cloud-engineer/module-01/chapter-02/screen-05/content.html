Key Principle

Inheriting Policies Down the Hierarchy

How access and security policies flow through your GCP structure.

Organization Policy
Applies to all below
Folder Policy
Inherits from Org, applies to projects
Project Policy
Inherits from Folder/Org, applies to resources
Resource Policy
Final applied policy
Key Takeaway

Policies defined at higher levels of the resource hierarchy are automatically inherited by all child resources, ensuring consistent governance.
