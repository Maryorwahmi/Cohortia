IP Addressing in GCP

Internal vs. External communication.

Internal IP Addresses

Used for communication solely within your VPC network. Not routable on the public internet.

External IP Addresses

Used for communication with the public internet. Essential for publicly accessible services.

Real-World Connection

Think of internal IPs as your office's internal phone system, and external IPs as the public phone number for your business. Both are necessary but serve different communication needs.

Key Takeaway

GCP uses internal IPs for private VPC communication and external IPs for public internet access, each with distinct security and routing implications.
