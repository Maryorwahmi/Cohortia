Firewall Rules: Controlling Traffic Flow

Defining ingress and egress policies for your VPC network.

Global Resource

Firewall rules apply to an entire VPC network, spanning all regions and zones.

Direction: Ingress/Egress

Control inbound traffic (Ingress) to your resources or outbound traffic (Egress) from them.

Action: ALLOW/DENY

Specify whether traffic matching the rule criteria should be permitted or blocked.

Pro Tip

Lower priority numbers (e.g., 100) indicate higher priority. The rule with the highest priority (lowest number) wins if multiple rules apply.

Key Takeaway

Firewall rules are global, priority-based policies that govern all ingress and egress traffic within your GCP VPC network.
