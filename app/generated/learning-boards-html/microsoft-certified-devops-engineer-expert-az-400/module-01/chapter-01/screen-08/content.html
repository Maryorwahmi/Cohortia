CALMS Pillar

Culture: Shifting Mindsets for Shared Success

Promoting collaboration, trust, and blameless learning.

Shared Responsibility

Moving from individual blame to collective ownership of success and failure.

Blameless Postmortems

Analyzing incidents to learn and improve processes, not to assign fault.

Trust & Transparency

Fostering an environment where teams can openly communicate and collaborate without fear.

Key Takeaway

A strong DevOps culture is built on trust, shared goals, and learning from failures through blameless postmortems.

Cultural Shift in Action
Blame
Collaboration
From Individual Fault to Collective Learning

Example: A production outage occurs. Instead of finding 'who broke it,' the team focuses on 'what happened' and 'how to prevent it' through a blameless postmortem, improving the deployment process for everyone.
