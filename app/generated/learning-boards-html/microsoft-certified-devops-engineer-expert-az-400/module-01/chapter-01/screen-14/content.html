Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

DevOps

A cultural and professional movement unifying Dev and Ops for improved collaboration and automation.

CALMS

Mnemonic for the core pillars: Culture, Automation, Lean, Measurement, Sharing.

Silos

Organizational divisions where teams work independently, hindering communication and collaboration.

Chapter Summary

DevOps is a transformative movement that breaks down traditional barriers, guided by the CALMS framework to foster efficiency and innovation.
