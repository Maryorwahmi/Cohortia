Key Concepts

Practices and principles for effective DevOps

Blameless Postmortem

Analyzing failures to learn and improve processes without assigning individual blame.

Continuous Improvement

An ongoing effort to enhance products, services, or processes over time, central to Lean and Measurement.

Feedback Loops

Mechanisms for collecting and acting on data to inform and refine processes and products.

Chapter Summary

DevOps success hinges on adopting practices like blameless postmortems and fostering continuous improvement through robust feedback loops.
