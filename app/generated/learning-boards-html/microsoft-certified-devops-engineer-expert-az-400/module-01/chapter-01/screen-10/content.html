CALMS Pillar

Lean Principles: Efficient Value Delivery

Focusing on small, frequent releases and continuous improvement.

Minimize Waste

Eliminate non-value-adding activities, such as excessive documentation, unnecessary features, or waiting times.

Small, Frequent Releases

Deliver value in small increments to reduce risk, enable faster feedback, and allow for quicker adjustments.

Continuous Improvement

Constantly seek ways to enhance processes and products based on feedback and data.

Key Takeaway

Lean principles drive efficiency in DevOps by focusing on delivering maximum customer value through waste reduction and continuous, incremental releases.

Lean in Practice
Large ReleasesHigh Risk, Slow Feedback
WasteNon-Value-Adding Activities
Eliminating Waste, Maximizing Value

Example: Instead of a yearly software update with hundreds of features, a team adopts a lean approach, releasing small, tested features weekly. This reduces deployment risk and allows for rapid customer feedback and iteration.
