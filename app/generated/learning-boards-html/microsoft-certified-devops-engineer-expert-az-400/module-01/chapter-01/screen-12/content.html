CALMS Pillar

Sharing: Fostering Collective Knowledge and Ownership

Breaking down knowledge silos through shared responsibility and documentation.

Knowledge Sharing

Mutual exchange of expertise between Dev and Ops teams.

Tooling Sharing

Standardizing and sharing tools and platforms across the organization.

Shared Responsibility

Collective ownership of the software's entire lifecycle, from code to production.

Example: A new developer joins the team. Instead of struggling to find information, comprehensive, shared documentation and a standardized toolchain allow them to quickly onboard and contribute effectively, thanks to a culture of sharing.

Key Takeaway

Sharing knowledge, tools, and responsibility is crucial for breaking down silos, fostering collective learning, and building resilient systems in DevOps.
