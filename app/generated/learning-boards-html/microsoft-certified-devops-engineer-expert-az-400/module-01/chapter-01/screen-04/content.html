Core Concept

DevOps: A Cultural and Professional Movement

More than just tools, it's a mindset shift.

What is DevOps?

DevOps is a philosophy that unifies software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops) to improve collaboration and efficiency.

Beyond Tools

It's primarily about cultural change, shared responsibility, and continuous improvement, not just adopting new software.

Bridging the Divide

Aims to break down traditional silos where Dev and Ops worked independently, often leading to friction and delays.

Key Takeaway

DevOps is a fundamental shift towards integrated, collaborative software delivery, driven by culture and shared goals.
