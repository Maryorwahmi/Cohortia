Historical Context

Breaking Down the 'Throw It Over the Wall' Mentality

Understanding the friction between traditional Dev and Ops.

Conflicting Priorities

Dev teams focused on speed and new features; Ops teams prioritized stability and uptime.

Organizational Silos

Teams worked independently with limited communication, leading to misunderstandings and delays.

Blame Culture

Production issues often led to finger-pointing rather than collaborative problem-solving.

Key Takeaway

Traditional silos created friction and inefficiencies, hindering reliable and fast software delivery.
