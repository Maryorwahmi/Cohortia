From Agile to DevOps: A Collaborative Journey

Tracing the origins of DevOps from lean and agile principles.

Agile Software Development

Iterative development, customer collaboration.

Lean Manufacturing

Minimize waste, maximize value.

Patrick Debois & DevOpsDays

Coined 'DevOps' around 2009.

The Bottleneck Shift

While Agile improved development, operations often became the new bottleneck, slowing down delivery.

Need for Integration

Realization that extending agile principles to operations was crucial for end-to-end efficiency.

Shared Responsibility

The movement emphasized breaking down barriers and fostering shared ownership for software success.

Key Takeaway

DevOps evolved from a need to unify development and operations, extending agile principles across the entire software lifecycle for faster, more reliable delivery.
