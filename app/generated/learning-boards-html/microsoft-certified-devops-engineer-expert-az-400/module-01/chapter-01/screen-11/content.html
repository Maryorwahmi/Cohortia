CALMS Pillar

Measurement: The Feedback Loop for Growth

Collecting metrics to inform and improve processes.

Key Metrics

  • Deployment Frequency
  • Lead Time for Changes
  • Mean Time to Recovery
  • Change Failure Rate
  • System Uptime
  • User Satisfaction

Feedback Loops

Metrics provide continuous feedback, allowing teams to identify bottlenecks and validate improvements quickly.

Data-Driven Decisions

Move from guesswork to informed decisions based on real-time performance and process data.

Key Takeaway

Measurement is fundamental to DevOps, providing the data necessary for continuous improvement and ensuring efforts align with business value.

Example: A team monitors deployment frequency and change failure rate. If the failure rate spikes after a new deployment, automated alerts trigger an immediate investigation, allowing for quick rollback or fix, informed by the metrics.
