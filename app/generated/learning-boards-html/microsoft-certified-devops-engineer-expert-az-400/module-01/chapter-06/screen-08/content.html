Common Mistakes and Safety Notes
Ensuring Robust and Secure Pipelines

Be aware of common pitfalls to ensure your automation is robust, secure, and efficient.

  • Lack of testing: Ensure comprehensive unit, integration, and end-to-end tests are part of your CI process.
  • Long-running builds: Keep CI builds fast to encourage frequent integration.
  • Skipping artifact publishing: Build once, test, and use the same artifacts for deployment.
  • Hardcoding secrets: Never hardcode sensitive information. Use Azure Key Vault or secure pipeline variables.
  • Inadequate permissions: Grant only necessary permissions (least privilege) to pipeline identities.
Key Takeaway

Avoiding common mistakes like insufficient testing, hardcoding secrets, and inadequate permissions is crucial for building reliable and secure CI/CD pipelines.
