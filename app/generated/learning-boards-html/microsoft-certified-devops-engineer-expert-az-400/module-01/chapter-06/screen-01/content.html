Introduction to Azure Pipelines for CI/CD
Automating Your Software Delivery Lifecycle
In this chapter, we'll explore the foundational practices of Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery, collectively known as CI/CD. These automated processes are the backbone of modern software development, enabling teams to deliver value to users faster and more reliably than ever before. We'll dive into Azure Pipelines, a powerful service within Azure DevOps that allows you to build, test, and deploy your applications with unprecedented efficiency.
Key Idea
Why CI/CD Matters

CI/CD pipelines are essential for rapid, reliable software delivery, reducing manual effort and increasing confidence in releases.
