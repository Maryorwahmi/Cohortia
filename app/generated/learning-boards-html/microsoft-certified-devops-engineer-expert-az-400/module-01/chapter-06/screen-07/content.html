Understanding Agents and Tasks
The Workhorses of Your Pipeline

Agents and tasks are the core components responsible for executing your automated workflows in Azure Pipelines.

Agents: Compute infrastructure that runs pipeline jobs.

  • Microsoft-hosted agents: Managed by Microsoft, pre-configured with software, convenient for quick setup.
  • Self-hosted agents: Managed by you, offering full control over OS, software, and hardware. Ideal for specific requirements or on-premises access.

Tasks: Pre-defined scripts or actions that perform specific operations within a job. Examples include installing Node.js (NodeTool@0), running tests, deploying applications, or publishing artifacts (PublishBuildArtifacts@1).

Key Takeaway

Agents provide the execution environment, while tasks define the specific actions performed within that environment to build your CI/CD pipelines.
