Error Budgets: Balancing Reliability and Innovation

A data-driven approach to managing service unavailability and guiding development priorities.

Understanding Error Budgets

  • **Derivation:** Directly from your Service Level Objectives (SLOs).
  • **Purpose:** Represents maximum allowable downtime or errors.
  • **Guidance:** If consumed quickly, prioritize reliability work over new features.
  • **Benefit:** Provides a data-driven way to balance velocity and reliability.
Key Takeaway

Error budgets transform reliability into a measurable resource, enabling teams to make informed decisions about when to focus on stability versus new feature development.

Error Budget in Action
SLO: 99.9% Availability

This means 0.1% unavailability is allowed.

Budget Healthy

Innovate, take risks, deploy new features.

Budget Depleted

Prioritize reliability, halt new features.

Concrete Example

If a service has an SLO of 99.9% uptime, its monthly error budget is approximately 43.2 minutes of downtime. If this budget is used up by mid-month, the team must stop new feature work and focus solely on restoring reliability.
