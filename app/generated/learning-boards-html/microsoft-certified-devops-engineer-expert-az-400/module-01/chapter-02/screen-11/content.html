Learning from Incidents with Blameless Postmortems

Fostering a culture of learning and psychological safety to build resilient systems.

Principles of Blameless Postmortems

  • **No Fault:** Focus on systemic issues, not individual blame.
  • **Understanding:** Determine what happened and why.
  • **Prevention:** Identify steps to prevent recurrence.
  • **Learning:** Foster a culture of continuous improvement.
  • **Psychological Safety:** Encourage open reporting without fear of punishment.
Key Takeaway

Blameless postmortems are crucial for building trust, encouraging transparency, and driving systemic improvements that enhance overall system resilience.

Concrete Example

After a database outage, a blameless postmortem reveals a missing backup validation step. The team then implements automated daily validation, preventing future similar incidents.
