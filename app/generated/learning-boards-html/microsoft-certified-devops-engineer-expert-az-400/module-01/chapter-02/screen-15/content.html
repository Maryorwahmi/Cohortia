Key Concepts: Part 2

Further SRE principles to master

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A formal contract with customers specifying service levels, often with penalties for non-compliance.

Error Budget

The maximum allowable rate of failure or unavailability for a service, derived from the SLO.

Toil

Manual, repetitive, automatable work that scales linearly with service growth and offers no enduring value.

Summary Takeaway

SRE uses SLAs for external commitments, error budgets to manage reliability, and actively reduces toil through automation, all supported by blameless postmortems for continuous learning.
