Differentiate Toil & Engineering Work

Learn to distinguish between repetitive, manual operational tasks (toil) and strategic, value-adding engineering work, and identify strategies for reducing toil. This enables teams to focus on innovation and long-term system health.

Describe Blameless Postmortems

Understand the importance of blameless postmortems in SRE for continuous improvement, focusing on systemic issues rather than individual fault. This fosters a culture of learning and psychological safety, leading to more resilient systems.
