Setting Performance Goals with SLOs

Defining acceptable service performance targets for your chosen SLIs.

What are SLOs?

  • **Definition:** Specific targets for your Service Level Indicators.
  • **Purpose:** Answer 'What do we consider acceptable performance?'
  • **Role:** Set expectations with users and guide engineering priorities.
  • **Boundary:** Define the line between acceptable and unacceptable service.
Key Takeaway

SLOs translate abstract reliability goals into concrete, measurable targets that drive engineering decisions and user satisfaction.

SLO Examples
Latency SLO

99.9% of requests under 300ms.

Error Rate SLO

Error rate must not exceed 0.1%.

Availability SLO

99.99% uptime for the service.

Concrete Example

For a video streaming service, an SLO might be: '99.9% of video streams must start within 2 seconds.' This directly impacts user satisfaction and retention.
