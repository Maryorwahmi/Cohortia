SLOs vs. SLAs: Internal Targets vs. External Commitments

Differentiating between internal reliability goals and external customer agreements.

Key Differences

  • **SLO (Service Level Objective):** Internal target for service performance.
  • **SLA (Service Level Agreement):** External, often legal, agreement with customers.
  • **Consequence:** SLOs guide engineering; SLAs often include penalties.
  • **Purpose:** SLOs for team management; SLAs for customer commitment.
Key Takeaway

SLOs are your team's internal goals for reliability, while SLAs are the formal promises you make to your customers, often with financial implications.

Concrete Example

A cloud provider's SLA might guarantee 99.99% uptime for a virtual machine, offering service credits if the actual uptime falls below this figure in a given month.
