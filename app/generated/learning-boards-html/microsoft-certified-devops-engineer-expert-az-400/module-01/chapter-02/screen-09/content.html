Operational Efficiency

Identifying and Eliminating Toil

Understanding manual, repetitive work that scales linearly with service growth.

Characteristics of Toil

  • **Manual:** Requires human intervention.
  • **Repetitive:** Occurs frequently.
  • **Automatable:** Can be solved with code.
  • **Tactical:** Short-term fixes, not strategic improvements.
  • **Linear Scaling:** Grows with service size, not value.
Key Takeaway

Toil is a drain on engineering resources; identifying and automating it is crucial for SRE teams to focus on strategic, value-adding work.

Toil vs. Engineering
Toil

Manual server restarts, repetitive diagnostics.

Engineering Work

Building automation, system design, feature development.

Common Mistake

Allowing SREs to spend more than 50% of their time on operational tasks, which hinders strategic improvements and leads to burnout.
