Dashboards: Customizable Project Overviews

Visualizing project health and team performance with widgets.

What are Dashboards?

Dashboards in Azure Boards provide a customizable overview of your project's progress. They display various widgets that visualize query results, charts, and key metrics, offering a quick snapshot of project health.

Key Dashboard Widgets

  • Query Results: Display lists of work items from saved queries.
  • Burndown/Burnup Charts: Track sprint or release progress.
  • Velocity Charts: Measure team's capacity and consistency.
  • Team Capacity: Visualize individual and team workload.
Key Takeaway

Azure Boards Dashboards offer customizable, widget-driven visual summaries of project progress and team performance, crucial for quick insights and effective communication to stakeholders.
