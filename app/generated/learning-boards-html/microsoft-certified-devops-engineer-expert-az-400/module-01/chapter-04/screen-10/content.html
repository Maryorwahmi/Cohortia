Managing the Backlog and Sprints

Prioritizing work and planning iterations for focused development.

The Backlog: Your Prioritized List

The backlog is a dynamic, prioritized list of all work items (Epics, Features, User Stories) that a team needs to complete. Azure Boards allows easy drag-and-drop reordering to reflect priority changes.

Sprints: Time-Boxed Execution

Sprints are fixed-length iterations in Scrum where a team commits to completing a set of work items. The Sprint Board visually tracks tasks through states like 'New', 'Active', and 'Closed'.

Key Takeaway

Effective backlog management ensures work is prioritized, while sprint planning and tracking in Azure Boards provide focus and transparency for iterative development cycles.

Example: Feature Release Cycle

A team prioritizes a 'User Profile' feature in the Product Backlog. During Sprint Planning, they pull related user stories into Sprint 1. They then use the Sprint Board to track the tasks for these stories, ensuring they are completed within the sprint's timeframe.
