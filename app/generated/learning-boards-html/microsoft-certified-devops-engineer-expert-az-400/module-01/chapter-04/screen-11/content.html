Customizing Azure Boards for Your Team

Tailoring process templates, work item types, fields, and states.

Process Template Customization

Modify existing process templates or create new ones to define custom work item types, fields, and workflow states that precisely match your team's unique development process.

Custom Fields & States

Add custom fields (e.g., 'Story Points', 'Priority Level') to work items and define custom states (e.g., 'Code Review', 'Ready for QA') to reflect your team's specific workflow stages.

Key Takeaway

Azure Boards' robust customization capabilities allow teams to adapt the platform to their exact workflow, ensuring optimal efficiency and accurate tracking of unique project requirements.

Example: Adding a 'Security Review' State

A team might add a 'Security Review' state to their 'Feature' work item workflow. This ensures that every feature undergoes a mandatory security check before it can be marked as 'Done', enforcing a critical quality gate.
