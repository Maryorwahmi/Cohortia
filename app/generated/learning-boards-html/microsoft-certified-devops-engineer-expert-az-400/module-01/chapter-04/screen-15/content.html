Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms to solidify your understanding.

Kanban

An Agile method focused on visualizing workflow, limiting work-in-progress (WIP), and continuous delivery.

Work Item

The fundamental unit of work in Azure Boards, representing tasks, bugs, user stories, features, or epics.

Backlog

A prioritized list of work items that a team needs to complete.

Query

A powerful search mechanism in Azure Boards to filter and group work items based on various criteria.

Dashboard

A customizable overview in Azure Boards that displays widgets showing project progress, metrics, and team performance.

Summary Takeaway

Kanban, Work Items, Backlogs, Queries, and Dashboards are key operational components within Azure Boards that enable teams to visualize, structure, prioritize, and gain insights into their development work.
