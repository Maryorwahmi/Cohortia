Core Concept

What is Infrastructure as Code?

IaC is the practice of managing and provisioning infrastructure through machine-readable definition files, rather than physical hardware configuration or interactive configuration tools.

Declarative Definitions

Describe the desired state, not the steps to get there.

Version Control

Track changes, audit, and rollback infrastructure configurations.

Key Takeaway

IaC treats infrastructure like software, enabling automated, consistent, and version-controlled cloud deployments.

Analogy

Infrastructure as a Blueprint

Imagine building a house. Without IaC, you'd tell workers what to do step-by-step, hoping they remember every detail. With IaC, you provide a detailed blueprint (the code) that specifies every component, material, and dimension. The builders (automation tools) then construct the house exactly as described, every single time.

Common Misconception

IaC is Just Scripting

While IaC involves scripts, it's fundamentally different from traditional scripting. IaC focuses on a declarative desired state, meaning you describe 'what' you want, not 'how' to achieve it. Traditional scripts are imperative, detailing 'how' to perform actions step-by-step. IaC tools handle the 'how' to reach the 'what'.
