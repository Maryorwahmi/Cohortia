Safety and Best Practices in Chaos Engineering

Ensuring Controlled and Responsible Experimentation

Prioritize Safety

Always define a clear 'kill switch' to immediately halt an experiment. Start small, communicate with stakeholders, and ensure robust monitoring.

  • •**Kill Switch:** Essential for immediate experiment cessation.
  • •**Start Small:** Begin with non-critical services.
  • •**Communicate:** Inform all stakeholders proactively.
Continuous Improvement

Embrace Learning from Failure

  • •**Robust Monitoring:** Detect issues quickly and accurately.
  • •**Document Findings:** Record hypotheses, experiments, and results.
  • •**Continuous Process:** Not a one-time event, but an ongoing practice.
  • •**Mindset Shift:** Failure is an opportunity to learn and improve.
Key Takeaway

Responsible Chaos Engineering is a continuous, scientific process that, when practiced safely, transforms potential failures into valuable learning opportunities for building more resilient systems.
