Core Principle 5

Minimize Blast Radius & Safety

Controlling Impact and Ensuring Safe Rollback

Limit Potential Impact

Design experiments to affect the smallest possible segment of users or services, with clear 'kill switches' to immediately halt an experiment if issues arise.

  • •Target specific instances or non-critical regions.
  • •Implement robust monitoring and alerting.
  • •Always have a well-defined rollback plan.
Safety Best Practices

Key Considerations for Safe Experiments

  • •**Clear Communication:** Inform all stakeholders before running.
  • •**Robust Monitoring:** Detect issues instantly.
  • •**Start Small:** Begin with non-critical services.
  • •**Document Findings:** Learn from every experiment.
Key Takeaway

Prioritizing blast radius minimization and having immediate kill switches are non-negotiable for responsible and effective Chaos Engineering, safeguarding your system and users.
