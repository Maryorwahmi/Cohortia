Core Principle 4

Automate Experiments

Making Resilience Testing a Continuous Practice

Integrate into Lifecycle

Chaos experiments should be a regular, automated part of your development lifecycle, not a manual, one-time event.

  • •Triggered programmatically or on a schedule.
  • •Integrated into CI/CD pipelines for post-deployment validation.
  • •Ensures consistent and scalable resilience testing.
Automation Benefits

Why Automate?

  • •**Consistency:** Ensures tests are run uniformly.
  • •**Efficiency:** Reduces manual effort and speeds up feedback.
  • •**Scalability:** Easily apply chaos to many services.
  • •**Proactive:** Catches regressions early in the pipeline.
Key Takeaway

Automating chaos experiments is crucial for embedding resilience testing into the fabric of your development process, making it a continuous and evolving practice.
