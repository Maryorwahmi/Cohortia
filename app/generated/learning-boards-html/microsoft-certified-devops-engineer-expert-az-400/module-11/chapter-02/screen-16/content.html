Key Concepts

Essential Terms for System Resilience

Chaos Engineering

The disciplined practice of intentionally injecting faults into a system to identify weaknesses and build confidence in its resilience.

Steady-State Hypothesis

A measurable prediction about the normal behavior of a system (e.g., uptime, response time) used as a baseline for chaos experiments.

Blast Radius

The potential impact or scope of a chaos experiment. Experiments should be designed to minimize this to limit disruption.

Summary Takeaway

These core concepts form the bedrock of a proactive approach to system resilience, enabling teams to anticipate and mitigate failures effectively.
