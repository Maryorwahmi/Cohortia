Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms to remember from this chapter

Experiment Tracking

The process of logging and organizing all parameters, metrics, and artifacts generated during machine learning model training runs, crucial for reproducibility and comparison.

Data Drift

A phenomenon where the statistical properties of the target variable, which the model is trying to predict, change over time in unforeseen ways, leading to model degradation.

Concept Drift

A change in the relationship between the input variables and the target variable over time, also leading to model performance degradation.

Summary Takeaway

Effective MLOps requires diligent experiment tracking and proactive management of data and concept drift to ensure models remain accurate and relevant in dynamic environments.
