MLOps Best Practices & Pitfalls

Ensuring robust and responsible AI/ML deployments

Common Mistakes

  • Treating models like regular software artifacts.
  • Not having a robust model registry.
  • Neglecting continuous monitoring of model performance.

Safety Notes

  • Ensure sensitive data is handled securely and compliantly.
  • Validate model fairness and bias before deployment.
  • Implement clear rollback strategies for model deployments.
Key Takeaway

Adhering to MLOps best practices, including robust versioning, continuous monitoring, and ethical considerations, is crucial for reliable and responsible AI/ML systems.

Why these matter
Reproducibility & Auditability

Without proper tracking, it's impossible to reproduce model results or audit their behavior, leading to untrustworthy systems.

Ethical AI

Bias and fairness validation are non-negotiable for building responsible AI that doesn't perpetuate or amplify societal inequalities.

Operational Stability

Rollback strategies are critical for quickly recovering from unforeseen issues in production, minimizing downtime and negative impact.
