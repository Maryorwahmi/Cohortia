Wasm & Composable Architectures

Exploring new paradigms for application development and deployment.

WebAssembly (Wasm) in the Cloud

  • •Portable Runtime: Binary instruction format for a stack-based virtual machine.
  • •High Performance: Near-native execution speed.
  • •Secure Sandbox: Offers a secure and isolated execution environment.
  • •Impact: Potential alternative to containers for server-side applications.

Composable Architectures

  • •Modular Components: Emphasizes highly reusable and independently deployable services.
  • •Flexible Assembly: Components can be easily combined to create new applications.
  • •DevOps Need: Drives demand for robust API management and service discovery.
Key Takeaway

Wasm and composable architectures promote more efficient, flexible, and modular ways of building and deploying software, requiring continuous adaptation in DevOps.
