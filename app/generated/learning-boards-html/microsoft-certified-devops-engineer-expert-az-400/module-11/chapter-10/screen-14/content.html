Key Concepts: Intelligent Operations & Platforms

AIOps

Application of AI and ML to automate and enhance IT operations, including monitoring, anomaly detection, and incident management.

Platform Engineering

The discipline of designing, building, and maintaining an Internal Developer Platform (IDP) for self-service capabilities.

Internal Developer Platform (IDP)

A curated set of tools, services, and processes that abstract infrastructure complexity, enabling developers to focus on business value.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts represent a shift towards more intelligent, automated, and developer-centric operations, crucial for modern cloud environments.
