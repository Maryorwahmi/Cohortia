Components of an IDP

Internal Developer Platforms provide curated tools, standardized environments, and automated provisioning.

Core Elements of an IDP

  • •Curated Tools: Selected and integrated best-of-breed solutions for development.
  • •Standardized Environments: Consistent dev, test, and prod setups.
  • •Automated Provisioning: Self-service infrastructure creation.
  • •Integrated Security: Built-in guardrails and compliance.
  • •Comprehensive Observability: Centralized monitoring and insights.

Azure Ecosystem for Platform Engineering

  • •Azure DevOps/GitHub: Source control, CI/CD.
  • •Azure Policy: Enforcing guardrails.
  • •Bicep/Terraform: IaC modules.
  • •Azure Container Apps/AKS: Standardized hosting.
  • •Azure Monitor: Centralized observability.
Key Takeaway

An IDP integrates various tools and services into a cohesive platform, simplifying development and operations for product teams.
