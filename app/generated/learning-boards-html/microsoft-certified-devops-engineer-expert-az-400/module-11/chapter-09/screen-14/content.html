Best Practices

Securing and Governing Your Power Platform Environments

Implementing robust controls for enterprise-grade LCNC solutions

Environment Management

Utilize separate Power Platform environments (dev, test, prod) and automate their creation, backup, and restoration via Azure DevOps pipelines.

Security & Governance

Enforce security with Azure AD authentication, assign least-privilege Power Platform roles, and use Azure Policy for standards (e.g., DLP).

Common Mistakes

Neglecting source control, allowing manual production deployments, and skipping automated tests are common pitfalls that lead to issues.

Safety Note: Service Principals

Always use dedicated service principal accounts for Power Platform service connections in pipelines, granting only least privilege permissions.

Key Takeaway

Implementing robust environment management, security, and governance with Azure AD and service principals is crucial for enterprise-grade LCNC ALM.
