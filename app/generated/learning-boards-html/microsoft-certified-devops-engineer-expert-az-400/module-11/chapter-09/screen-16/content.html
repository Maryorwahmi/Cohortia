Chapter Recap

Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

LCNC Platforms

Development platforms minimizing manual coding through visual interfaces for rapid app creation.

Microsoft Power Platform

A suite of low-code tools including Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, and Power Virtual Agents.

Application Lifecycle Management (ALM)

The process of managing an application's entire lifecycle, from ideation through deployment and maintenance.

Power Platform Solution

A container mechanism for packaging and deploying Power Platform components as a single unit.

Unmanaged Solution

A solution under development, allowing direct customization; used for development and source control.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering LCNC ALM requires understanding core platforms, the ALM process, and the role of solutions in managing application components.
