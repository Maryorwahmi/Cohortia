Key Challenges

Navigating DevOps Hurdles in LCNC Development

Addressing source control, testing, and deployment complexities

01

Source Control

LCNC artifacts are often in proprietary formats, making traditional Git-based version control difficult and hindering collaboration.

02

Automated Testing

Visual or configuration-driven logic makes automated unit and integration testing complex compared to code-based applications.

03

Environment Management

Managing multiple Power Platform environments (dev, test, prod) and moving solutions between them requires specialized tools and processes.

04

Deployment Automation

Reliance on manual deployments is common, leading to inconsistencies, human errors, and slower release cycles.

05

Collaboration & Governance

Bridging the gap between citizen developers and professional developers to ensure best practices and governance is a continuous challenge.

Key Takeaway

LCNC platforms introduce unique challenges for traditional DevOps, requiring specialized approaches for source control, testing, and deployment to maintain enterprise standards.
