Introducing the Federated DevOps Model

Balancing Centralization with Team Autonomy

Concrete Example

A central platform team provides standardized CI/CD templates, while product teams use these templates to build and deploy their unique applications.

Key Takeaway

The federated model enables large organizations to scale DevOps by balancing the need for consistency with the agility of autonomous teams.
