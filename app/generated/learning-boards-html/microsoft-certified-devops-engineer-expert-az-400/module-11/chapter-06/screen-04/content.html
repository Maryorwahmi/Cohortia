Core Concept

The Enterprise DevOps Challenge

Beyond Tools: Culture, Process, and Technical Hurdles

Organizational & Cultural

  • •Siloed teams & resistance to change
  • •Lack of cross-functional collaboration
  • •Fear of automation & 'blame culture'

Process & Governance

  • •Inconsistent CI/CD practices
  • •Manual approvals & non-standard environments
  • •Difficulty enforcing security & compliance

Technical Complexity

  • •Managing diverse applications & tech stacks
  • •Complex dependencies & integrations
  • •Inconsistent infrastructure provisioning
Concrete Example

A global bank with hundreds of development teams struggles with each team using different CI/CD tools and manual security checks, leading to slow deployments and audit failures.

Key Takeaway

Successful enterprise DevOps requires addressing cultural, process, and technical challenges holistically, not just implementing new tools.
