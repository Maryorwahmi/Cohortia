Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from This Chapter

Shared Services

Centralized components or tools managed by a core team and consumed by multiple product teams.

Reusable YAML Templates

Parameterized pipeline definitions shared across teams to promote consistency and reduce duplication.

Communities of Practice (CoPs)

Groups fostering knowledge sharing and continuous learning across teams in a large organization.

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging shared resources and fostering a collaborative culture are critical for driving consistency, efficiency, and continuous improvement in enterprise DevOps.
