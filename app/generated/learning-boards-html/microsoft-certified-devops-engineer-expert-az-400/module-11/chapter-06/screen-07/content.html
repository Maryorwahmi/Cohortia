Team Empowerment

Empowering Product Teams

Ownership and Agility within Defined Guardrails

Autonomy Over Applications

Product teams have the freedom to make decisions regarding their specific applications and technology choices.

Build Own Pipelines

Teams create and manage their CI/CD pipelines, often leveraging shared templates provided by the central team.

Deploy & Operate Applications

Teams are responsible for deploying their applications and managing their entire lifecycle, including operations and monitoring.

Operate Within Guardrails

While autonomous, teams adhere to the common standards, security policies, and compliance requirements defined by the central team.

Concrete Example

A product team developing a new microservice can choose Python for their backend, knowing the central team provides a Python build template and secure deployment targets.

Key Takeaway

Empowering product teams with autonomy within defined guardrails fosters innovation, ownership, and faster delivery of business value.
