From Shared Services to Platform Engineering

Building an Internal Developer Platform (IDP)

Platform Engineering Defined

The discipline of designing and building internal developer platforms (IDPs) that provide self-service tools and capabilities to product teams.

Internal Developer Platform (IDP)

A curated set of tools, services, and processes that abstract away infrastructure complexity, enabling product teams to focus on business logic.

IDP Capabilities

  • •Automated environment provisioning
  • •Standardized CI/CD pipelines
  • •Centralized logging & monitoring
  • •Integrated security scanning
Key Takeaway

Platform Engineering, through IDPs, reduces cognitive load for product teams, accelerating delivery while ensuring consistency, security, and cost efficiency.
