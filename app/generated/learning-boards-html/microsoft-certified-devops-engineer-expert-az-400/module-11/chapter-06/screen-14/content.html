Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from This Chapter

Federated DevOps Model

Balances centralized governance with decentralized team autonomy for scalable enterprise DevOps.

Platform Engineering

The discipline of building internal developer platforms (IDPs) for self-service capabilities.

Internal Developer Platform (IDP)

A set of tools and services that abstract infrastructure complexity, enabling product teams to focus on business value.

Summary Takeaway

Successfully scaling DevOps in an enterprise relies on strategic models like Federated DevOps and the enabling power of Platform Engineering through IDPs.
