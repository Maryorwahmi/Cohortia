Product Teams Consuming Templates

Accelerating Development with Shared Building Blocks

product-team/azure-pipelines.yml
# Product team's azure-pipelines.yml
trigger:
- main

extends:
  template: templates/build-dotnet-app.yml@self # @self refers to the current repo
  parameters:
    projectPath: 'src/WebApp/WebApp.csproj'
    artifactName: 'webapp-artifact'

# ... followed by deployment stages specific to the product team

stages:
- stage: DeployToDev
  displayName: 'Deploy to Development'
  dependsOn: [] # No explicit dependency as 'extends' handles the build
  jobs:
  - job: DeployWebApp
    displayName: 'Deploy Web App'
    pool:
      vmImage: 'windows-latest'
    steps:
    - download: current
      artifact: 'webapp-artifact'
    - task: AzureWebApp@1
      displayName: 'Deploy Azure Web App'
      inputs:
        azureSubscription: 'MyAzureServiceConnection'
        appName: 'my-product-dev-webapp'
        package: $(Pipeline.Workspace)/webapp-artifact/**/*.zip

`extends` Keyword for Reusability

The `extends` keyword allows product teams to easily incorporate shared templates into their own pipelines, inheriting predefined steps.

Parameter Customization

Teams can pass specific parameters to the template, tailoring the generic process to their application's unique requirements.

Key Takeaway

Consuming shared templates empowers product teams to focus on application-specific logic while benefiting from standardized, centrally managed CI/CD processes.
