DORA Metrics: The Four Key Indicators

Unlocking high performance in software delivery.

Why DORA Metrics Matter

  • •Research-Backed: Identified by the DevOps Research and Assessment team (DORA) as key differentiators.
  • •Predictive Power: Directly correlate with higher profitability, market share, and customer satisfaction.
  • •Holistic View: Cover speed, stability, and quality, providing a balanced perspective on delivery performance.
  • •Actionable Insights: Guide teams to focus on improvements that yield the greatest impact.

The Four DORA Metrics

Deployment Frequency

How often releases occur.

Lead Time for Changes

Time from commit to production.

Change Failure Rate

Percentage of changes causing issues.

Mean Time to Recover

Time to restore service after incident.

Key Takeaway

The DORA metrics provide a balanced, research-backed framework for evaluating and improving your software delivery performance.
