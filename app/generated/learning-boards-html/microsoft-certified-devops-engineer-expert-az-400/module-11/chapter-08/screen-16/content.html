Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

DORA Metrics

Four key metrics (DF, LTFC, CFR, MTTR) identified as indicators of high-performing DevOps organizations.

Deployment Frequency (DF)

How often an organization successfully releases to production, measuring agility.

Lead Time for Changes (LTFC)

The time it takes for a commit to get into production, measuring efficiency and flow.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding DORA metrics like Deployment Frequency and Lead Time for Changes is fundamental to assessing and improving your DevOps delivery speed and agility.
