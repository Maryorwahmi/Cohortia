Assessing DevOps Maturity

Evaluating your current state across key dimensions.

Why Assess Maturity?

  • •Identify Strengths: Recognize areas where your organization excels.
  • •Pinpoint Weaknesses: Discover bottlenecks and areas needing improvement.
  • •Guide Investment: Make informed decisions on where to allocate resources for maximum impact.
  • •Foster Growth: Establish a baseline for continuous improvement and track progress over time.

Key Dimensions of Maturity

Culture

Collaboration, psychological safety.

Automation

CI/CD, IaC, testing, monitoring.

Lean Flow

Small batch sizes, fast feedback.

Measurement

Metrics, dashboards, data-driven decisions.

Security

Shift-left, automated governance.

Key Takeaway

DevOps maturity assessment provides a holistic view of an organization's strengths and weaknesses, guiding strategic improvement across key dimensions.
