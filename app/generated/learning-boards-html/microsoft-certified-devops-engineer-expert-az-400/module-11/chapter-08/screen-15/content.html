Strategic Planning

Crafting Your DevOps Improvement Roadmap

Prioritizing initiatives for measurable progress.

Roadmap Principles

  • •Iterative: Focus on small, measurable changes rather than big-bang transformations.
  • •Prioritized: Address the biggest bottlenecks or areas with the most significant impact on DORA metrics.
  • •Data-Driven: Use assessment results and metrics to inform and validate initiatives.
  • •Learning Culture: Foster an environment where metrics are used for learning, not blame.

Roadmap Process

1
Assess Current State
2
Prioritize Initiatives
3
Implement & Measure
Key Takeaway

An iterative, data-driven roadmap, focused on prioritized initiatives and a learning culture, is essential for continuous DevOps maturity improvement.
