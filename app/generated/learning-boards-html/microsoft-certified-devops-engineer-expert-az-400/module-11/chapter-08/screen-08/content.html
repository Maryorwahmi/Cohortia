DORA Metric: Stability

Change Failure Rate: Measuring Quality

Percentage of production changes causing degraded service.

Understanding CFR

  • •Definition: The percentage of changes to production that result in degraded service or require rollback.
  • •What it Measures: Quality, stability, and reliability of your software delivery process.
  • •High Performers: Maintain a CFR of 0-15%, indicating robust quality assurance.
  • •Low Performers: Experience CFRs of 46-60%, highlighting significant quality issues.

CFR in Practice

High Performers: 0-15%

Stable, reliable deployments.

Low Performers: 46-60%

Frequent incidents, unstable releases.

Key Takeaway

A low Change Failure Rate is achieved through robust testing, effective deployment strategies, and a strong focus on quality throughout the pipeline.
