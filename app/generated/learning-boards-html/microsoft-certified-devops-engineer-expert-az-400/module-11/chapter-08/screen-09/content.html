DORA Metric: Stability

Mean Time to Recover: Measuring Resilience

How quickly service is restored after a production incident.

Understanding MTTR

  • •Definition: The average time taken to restore service after a production incident or failure.
  • •What it Measures: Resilience, incident response effectiveness, and operational maturity.
  • •High Performers: Recover in less than one hour, minimizing downtime.
  • •Low Performers: Take days or weeks to restore service, leading to prolonged outages.

MTTR in Practice

High Performers: < 1 Hour

Swift diagnosis and resolution.

Low Performers: Days/Weeks

Prolonged outages, slow recovery.

Key Takeaway

A low MTTR is crucial for business continuity and customer trust, demonstrating effective incident response and system resilience.
