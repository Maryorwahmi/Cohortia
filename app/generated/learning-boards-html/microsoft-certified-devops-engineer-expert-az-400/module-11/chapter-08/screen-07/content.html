DORA Metric: Speed

Lead Time for Changes: Measuring Efficiency

The time it takes for a commit to get into production.

Understanding LTFC

  • •Definition: The duration from code commit to successful deployment in production.
  • •What it Measures: Efficiency of the entire software delivery pipeline, including development, testing, and deployment.
  • •High Performers: Achieve lead times of less than one hour.
  • •Low Performers: Experience lead times of weeks or months, indicating significant bottlenecks.

Key Takeaway

A short Lead Time for Changes signifies a highly efficient and automated pipeline, enabling rapid delivery of value to customers.
