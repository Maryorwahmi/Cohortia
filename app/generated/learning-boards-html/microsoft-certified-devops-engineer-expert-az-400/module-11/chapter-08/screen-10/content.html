Module 11›Chapter 11.8›Vanity Metrics
Common Pitfall

Focus on DORA, Avoid Vanity Metrics

Distinguishing meaningful indicators from misleading ones.

Why Vanity Metrics are Dangerous

  • •Misleading: They look good but don't reflect actual delivery performance or business value.
  • •Misguided Efforts: Can lead teams to focus on activities that don't improve real outcomes.
  • •False Sense of Security: May hide underlying problems in the software delivery pipeline.
  • •Lack of Business Context: Don't directly correlate with profitability, market share, or customer satisfaction.

DORA vs. Vanity Metrics

DORA Metrics

Reflects actual delivery performance and business value.

Vanity Metrics

Looks good but doesn't show real impact (e.g., lines of code).

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize DORA metrics as they provide a holistic and accurate picture of your software delivery health and directly link to business outcomes.
