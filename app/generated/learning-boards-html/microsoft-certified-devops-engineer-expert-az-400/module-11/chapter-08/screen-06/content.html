DORA Metric: Speed

Deployment Frequency: Measuring Agility

How often your organization successfully releases to production.

Understanding DF

  • •Definition: The rate at which code is successfully deployed to a production environment.
  • •What it Measures: Agility, efficiency of release processes, and the size of changes being deployed.
  • •High Performers: Deploy multiple times a day, often on demand.
  • •Low Performers: Deploy once a month or less, indicating large, risky changes.

DF in Practice

High Performers: Daily/Hourly

Small, frequent, low-risk changes.

Low Performers: Monthly/Quarterly

Large, infrequent, high-risk changes.

Key Takeaway

High Deployment Frequency is a hallmark of agile teams, enabling faster feedback and reduced risk through smaller, more manageable changes.
