Blue/Green Deployment: The Core Idea

Switching traffic between identical environments for zero downtime.

01Two Identical Environments

Maintain 'Blue' (active) and 'Green' (inactive) environments, both production-ready.

02Deploy to Inactive (Green)

New application version is deployed to the 'Green' environment, leaving 'Blue' untouched.

03Thorough Testing

Test the new version in 'Green' with real data and dependencies, without affecting live users.

04Instant Traffic Switch

Once validated, switch the traffic router to direct all requests to the 'Green' environment.

Example

An online banking application deploys a new feature to 'Green'. After testing, traffic is instantly redirected. If issues arise, traffic can be immediately reverted to 'Blue'.

Common Mistake

Not fully replicating the production environment for 'Green'. This can lead to discrepancies that only appear after the traffic switch, causing unexpected issues.

Key Takeaway

Blue/Green deployments ensure near-zero downtime and provide an immediate rollback mechanism by swapping traffic between two identical, pre-validated environments.
