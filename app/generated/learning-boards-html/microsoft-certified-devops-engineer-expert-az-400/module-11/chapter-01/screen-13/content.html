The Role of Monitoring and Observability

Ensuring visibility and health for advanced deployments.

Real-time Visibility

Gain immediate insights into application performance and health.

Azure Monitor & App Insights

Integrate Azure services to track KPIs, error rates, and custom metrics.

Automated Alerts & Rollbacks

Set up alerts for threshold breaches, triggering notifications or automated rollbacks.

Definition: Observability

The ability to understand the internal state of a system by examining its outputs (logs, metrics, traces). It's crucial for diagnosing issues in complex distributed systems.

Common Pitfall

Insufficient monitoring during a canary rollout, leading to problems affecting a larger user base before being detected. Robust observability is paramount.

Key Takeaway

Effective monitoring and observability are the bedrock of successful advanced deployments, providing the necessary insights to maintain application health and respond swiftly to issues.
