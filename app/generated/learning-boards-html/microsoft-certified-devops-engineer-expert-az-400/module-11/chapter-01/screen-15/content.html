Key Concepts to Remember

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Blue/Green Deployment

Two identical production environments (Blue/Green); new versions deployed to inactive, then traffic switched for zero downtime and instant rollback.

Canary Deployment

New version rolled out to a small subset of users first, gradually increasing if no issues are detected, limiting risk.

Azure App Service Deployment Slots

Feature allowing deployment of different app versions to separate slots and atomic swaps with minimal downtime.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering Blue/Green and Canary deployments, especially with Azure services, is crucial for achieving high availability, minimizing risk, and ensuring seamless software delivery in production environments.
