Core Concept

DevSecOps: Integrating Security from the Start

Moving beyond reactive measures to proactive, embedded security.

Key Principle

Shift Left Security: Integrate security practices, tools, and automation as early as possible in the development process to proactively identify and mitigate risks.

Why it Matters

Early detection of vulnerabilities significantly reduces the cost and effort of remediation, preventing costly rework and potential breaches in production.

Common Misconception

DevSecOps does not slow down development. By automating security, it streamlines the process and builds confidence in faster, more secure releases.
