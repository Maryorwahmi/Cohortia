Azure Policy: Assignment and Effects

Applying policies at various scopes and controlling their impact on resources.

Assignment Scopes
  • Management Group: Broadest scope, applies to all subscriptions.
  • Subscription: Applies to all resources within a subscription.
  • Resource Group: Applies to resources within a specific group.
Initiatives

Group multiple related policies into a single initiative for easier management and assignment (e.g., 'PCI DSS Compliance Initiative').

Example Scenario

Assign a 'Require Tags' policy at the subscription level and a 'Deny Public IPs' policy to a production resource group.

Azure Policy Effects

Audit

Reports non-compliance without blocking.

Deny

Prevents non-compliant resource creation/update.

DeployIfNotExists

Deploys a resource if a condition is met.

Modify

Modifies a resource to be compliant.
