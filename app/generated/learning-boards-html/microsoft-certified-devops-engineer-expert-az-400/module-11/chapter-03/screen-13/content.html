Balancing Cost Optimization with Performance

Avoiding over-optimization and ensuring service stability.

The Delicate Balance

Aggressive cost optimization can lead to performance degradation, service instability, or security vulnerabilities. It's crucial to balance cost savings with the required performance, reliability, and security of your workloads.

Common Mistake

Over-optimizing production workloads without thorough testing, leading to unexpected outages or poor user experience, ultimately costing more in lost revenue or recovery efforts.

Pro Tip

Always start aggressive optimization in dev/test environments. Monitor performance metrics closely and gradually apply proven strategies to production after rigorous validation.
