Key Concepts

More essential terms for mastering FinOps in DevOps.

Reserved Instances (RIs)

A pricing option offering significant discounts (up to 72%) for committing to a 1-year or 3-year term for certain resources.

Azure Spot Instances

Unused Azure compute capacity available at deep discounts, suitable for fault-tolerant or interruptible workloads.

Azure Policy

A service that helps enforce organizational standards and assess compliance at scale by defining rules for your Azure resources.

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging Azure's pricing models like RIs and Spot Instances, combined with governance tools like Azure Policy and IaC, provides powerful mechanisms for advanced cost control.
