Optimization Strategy

Efficient Storage Management

Reducing costs by optimizing data lifecycle and storage types.

Lifecycle Management

Implement policies to automatically tier less frequently accessed data to cooler, cheaper storage tiers (e.g., Hot to Cool to Archive for Azure Blob Storage).

Deletion Policies

Automatically delete old backups, logs, or temporary files that are no longer needed to prevent unnecessary accumulation.

Right-sizing Storage

Choose the correct storage type (Standard HDD, Standard SSD, Premium SSD) based on actual performance requirements to avoid overspending.
