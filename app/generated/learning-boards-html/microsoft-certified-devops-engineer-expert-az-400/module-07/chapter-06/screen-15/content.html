Key Concepts

Advanced terms for secure secret management

Variable Group

A collection of variables in Azure DevOps that can be shared across multiple pipelines, often linked to Azure Key Vault for secret retrieval.

Principle of Least Privilege

Granting only the minimum necessary permissions for a service or identity to perform its function, minimizing potential damage from compromise.

Secret Rotation

The regular process of changing secrets (passwords, API keys) to minimize the impact of a potential compromise and reduce the attack window.

Summary Takeaway

Variable Groups, Principle of Least Privilege, and Secret Rotation are advanced practices for enhancing secret management, reusability, and overall pipeline security.
