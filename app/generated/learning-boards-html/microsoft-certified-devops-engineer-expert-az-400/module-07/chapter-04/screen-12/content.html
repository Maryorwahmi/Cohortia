Understanding DAST Findings

Prioritizing and triaging identified vulnerabilities.

DAST reports identify vulnerabilities like SQL Injection, XSS, broken authentication, insecure deserialization, and misconfigurations. Prioritize findings based on their risk level (High, Medium, Low) and potential impact.

Key Insight

Context is crucial: interpret DAST findings in the context of the running application to differentiate real threats from false positives.

Actionable Feedback

A clear process for triaging, verifying, and feeding findings back to the development team is vital for effective remediation.

Common Vulnerability Types
  • •SQL Injection
  • •Cross-Site Scripting (XSS)
  • •Broken Authentication
  • •Insecure Deserialization
  • •Misconfigurations
Managing False Positives

DAST can generate false positives due to dynamic application behavior. Manual review and tuning are crucial to filter out non-issues.

Key Takeaway

Interpreting DAST reports requires understanding vulnerability types, prioritizing by risk, and carefully triaging findings to manage false positives and provide actionable feedback to development teams.
