Overcoming DAST Challenges

Strategies for effective and efficient DAST implementation.

Common Challenges
  • •Scan Duration: Full scans can be time-consuming, slowing pipelines.
  • •Environment Dependency: Requires a stable, deployed test environment.
  • •Authentication: Complex to configure for authenticated application parts.
  • •False Positives: Can generate non-issues requiring manual review.
  • •Coverage: Ensuring full coverage for complex SPAs/APIs can be difficult.
Best Practices for DAST
  • •Dedicated Staging: Integrate DAST into a dedicated staging or pre-production environment.
  • •Automated Deployment: Automate deployment to the DAST environment for consistency.
  • •Quality Gate: Implement a 'Quality Gate' to halt pipelines based on critical DAST findings.
  • •Combine with SAST: Use DAST alongside SAST for comprehensive, layered security coverage.
  • •Optimize Scan Frequency: Use baseline scans frequently, full scans less often.
Pro-Tip

Tune your DAST tool's configuration and scope to balance scan thoroughness with pipeline speed, focusing on critical paths and recent changes.

Key Takeaway

Overcoming DAST challenges involves strategic planning, optimizing scan frequency, leveraging dedicated environments, and combining DAST with SAST for a robust, layered security approach in CI/CD.
