Deciphering Your SCA Reports

Understanding vulnerability severity and prioritizing remediation efforts effectively.

Assess Severity Levels

Categorize vulnerabilities (Critical, High, Medium, Low) to understand immediate impact and urgency. Focus on critical issues first.

Evaluate Exploitability Context

Determine if your application actually uses the vulnerable part of the library. Not all identified flaws are exploitable in your specific context.

Address License Compliance

Identify and resolve any open-source licenses that conflict with your product's licensing model or organizational policies to avoid legal issues.

SCA Report Overview

Prioritizing Vulnerability Remediation

C

Critical: Immediate action required. High risk of exploitation.

H

High: Urgent attention. Potential for significant impact.

M

Medium: Address in upcoming sprints. Moderate impact.

L

Low: Review as time permits. Minor impact or limited exploitability.

Key Takeaway

Effective report interpretation enables focused remediation, ensuring critical risks are addressed first and resources are used efficiently.
