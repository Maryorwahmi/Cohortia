Navigating Vulnerabilities and License Issues

Understanding the common pitfalls of using open-source components.

Known Vulnerabilities (CVEs)

Critical flaws in popular libraries (e.g., Log4Shell, Heartbleed) can expose your entire system to attack. Manual identification is slow and error-prone.

License Compliance Issues

Open-source licenses vary (permissive like MIT, restrictive like GPL). Incompatible licenses can lead to significant legal and financial repercussions.

Key Takeaway

Proactive SCA is crucial to identify and mitigate both security vulnerabilities and legal risks introduced by third-party dependencies.
