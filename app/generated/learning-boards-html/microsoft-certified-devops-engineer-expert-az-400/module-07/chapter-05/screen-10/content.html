The Hidden Dangers of Transitive Dependencies

Why ignoring indirect dependencies and inaction on reports can lead to significant risks.

Ignoring Transitive Dependencies

Many vulnerabilities are found in dependencies of your dependencies. Failing to scan the entire tree leaves critical blind spots.

Not Acting on Reports

Running SCA is insufficient without a process to review, prioritize, and remediate findings. Unaddressed vulnerabilities remain a threat.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive SCA requires scanning the full dependency tree and establishing a robust remediation process for identified issues.
