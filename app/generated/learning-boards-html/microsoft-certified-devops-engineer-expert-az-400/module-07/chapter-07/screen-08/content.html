Policy Management

Defining and Assigning Azure Policies

From definitions to initiatives and scope assignments.

Key Components of Azure Policy

Policy Definitions

The specific rules that dictate allowed actions and required configurations (e.g., 'Require HTTPS on App Services').

Policy Initiatives

Collections of policy definitions grouped to achieve a broader goal (e.g., 'PCI DSS Compliance Initiative').

Policy Assignments

Applying policies or initiatives to specific scopes (management groups, subscriptions, resource groups).

Compliance as Code

Storing policy definitions and initiatives in source control (like Git) and deploying them via pipelines ensures version control, auditability, and automated management of your governance rules. This practice is key for scalable and transparent compliance.

Key Takeaway

Effective Azure Policy implementation relies on understanding definitions, initiatives, and their hierarchical assignment to enforce standards across your cloud environment.

Real-world Connection

A large enterprise might assign a 'Security Baseline Initiative' at the management group level, ensuring all subscriptions inherit a core set of security policies.
