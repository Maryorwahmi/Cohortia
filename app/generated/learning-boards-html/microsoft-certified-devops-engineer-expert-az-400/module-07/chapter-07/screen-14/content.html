Key Concepts

Essential terms for compliance and governance (Part 1)

Compliance

Adherence to internal policies, industry standards (e.g., PCI DSS, HIPAA), and governmental regulations (e.g., GDPR).

Governance

The framework of rules, practices, and processes that ensures accountability, transparency, and adherence to organizational objectives and compliance requirements.

Azure Policy

An Azure service that helps enforce organizational standards and assess compliance at scale by defining rules for Azure resources.

Summary Takeaway

Compliance and governance are foundational for secure operations, with Azure Policy providing the automated enforcement mechanism within the Azure ecosystem.
