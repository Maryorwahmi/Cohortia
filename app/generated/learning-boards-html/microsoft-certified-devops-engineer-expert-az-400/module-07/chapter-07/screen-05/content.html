Azure Policy: Your Compliance Gate

Enforcing organizational standards at scale.

How Azure Policy Works

  • Define Policies: Create rules for allowed actions and required configurations on Azure resources.
  • Integrate with Pipelines: Policy acts as a gate, evaluating resources deployed by your CI/CD.
  • Automated Evaluation: Policy checks resources against rules, either denying deployment or auditing non-compliance.

Example Policy

You can create a policy that denies the creation of Azure Storage Accounts without encryption, or audits VMs that don't have a specific anti-malware extension installed. This prevents insecure configurations from reaching production.

Key Takeaway

Azure Policy is a powerful, scalable service for enforcing security and compliance standards, acting as a critical gate in your DevOps deployment process.

Pro-Tip

Treat Azure Policy definitions as 'compliance as code' by storing them in source control and deploying them via pipelines for version control and auditability.
