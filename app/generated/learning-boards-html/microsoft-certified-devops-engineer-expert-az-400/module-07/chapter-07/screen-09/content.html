Governance Requirement

Robust Audit Trails and Logging

Capturing immutable records for compliance.

Key Logging Sources

  • Azure DevOps Pipelines: Detailed logs of who, what, and when for pipeline runs.
  • Azure Monitor & Activity Logs: Events, resource changes, and security alerts for Azure resources.
  • Log Analytics & Sentinel: Centralized collection for long-term retention, analysis, and reporting.

Importance for Audits

Centralized, immutable logs provide the necessary evidence for forensic analysis in case of a security incident and are crucial for demonstrating adherence to regulatory standards during compliance audits. They offer a clear, chronological record of all system activities.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive and centralized logging is fundamental for establishing robust audit trails, which are indispensable for security, incident response, and regulatory compliance.

Common Mistake

Collecting logs without regularly reviewing and acting on them. Logs only provide value if they are analyzed and used for continuous improvement.
