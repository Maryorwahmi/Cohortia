Agent Pools: Microsoft-hosted vs. Self-hosted

Choosing your pipeline execution environment.

Microsoft-hosted Agents

  • •Convenience: Maintained and updated automatically by Microsoft.
  • •Clean Environment: Provides a fresh, isolated VM for each job, reducing cross-contamination risks.
  • •Shared Environment: Runs in a shared infrastructure, potentially unsuitable for highly sensitive operations.
  • •Limited Network Access: May not support specific network requirements (e.g., on-premises database access).
Pro Tip

Ideal for public projects or less sensitive internal projects where ease of management is a priority.

Self-hosted Agents

  • •Greater Control: Full control over infrastructure, network, and installed software.
  • •Specific Network Access: Ideal for connecting to private networks or on-premises resources.
  • •Security Responsibility: You are fully responsible for patching, updates, and securing the agent environment.
  • •Maintenance Overhead: Requires manual or automated management of the agent's lifecycle and security.
Consideration

Best for sensitive operations or when custom environments and network access are non-negotiable.

Key Takeaway

The choice between Microsoft-hosted and self-hosted agents depends on your security, control, and network access requirements.
