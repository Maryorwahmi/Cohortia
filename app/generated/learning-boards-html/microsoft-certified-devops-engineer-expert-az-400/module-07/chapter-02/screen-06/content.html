Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP)

Granting only the minimum necessary permissions.

Core Definition: Granting only the essential permissions for a service connection to perform its specific, intended tasks.

Risk Mitigation: Minimizes the 'blast radius' of a security breach; a compromised connection can do less damage.

Example: A deployment connection should only have permissions to deploy to its target resource group, not delete other resources.

Key Takeaway

Adhering to the Principle of Least Privilege is crucial to prevent over-privileged service connections from being exploited for unauthorized actions.
