Core Concept

The Foundation of Pipeline Security

Protecting your code, artifacts, and deployed resources.

Automated Interactions: Pipelines automate steps that interact with various services and environments, requiring credentials and permissions.

Vulnerability Risk: Poorly managed credentials and permissions can lead to significant security vulnerabilities, exposing sensitive data and systems.

Integrity & Confidentiality: Securing pipelines is crucial for maintaining the integrity and confidentiality of your code, artifacts, and deployed resources.

Key Takeaway

Robust pipeline security is essential to prevent unauthorized access, data breaches, and system compromises across your entire CI/CD process.
