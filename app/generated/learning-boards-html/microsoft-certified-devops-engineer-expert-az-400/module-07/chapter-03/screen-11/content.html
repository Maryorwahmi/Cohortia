Report Analysis

Interpreting SAST Reports and Prioritization

Making sense of vulnerability findings

Categorization by Severity

SAST tools classify findings (e.g., Critical, Major, Minor, Info) to indicate the potential risk level of each vulnerability.

Beyond Severity: Exploitability & Impact

Prioritization should also consider how easy a vulnerability is to exploit and the potential damage it could cause if compromised.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize SAST findings by considering severity, exploitability, and impact to focus remediation efforts on the most critical vulnerabilities, optimizing security resources and preventing team overwhelm.

Vulnerability Prioritization Matrix

Critical

Immediate attention, high exploitability & impact.

Major

High priority, significant exploitability or impact.

Minor

Address in due course, lower exploitability & impact.

Informational

Best practice suggestions, no direct security risk.

Always consider your specific application context when prioritizing findings.
