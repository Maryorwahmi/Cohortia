Benefits: Early Detection & Cost Savings

The exponential cost reduction of early security flaw remediation.

Cost of Fixing a Vulnerability
Cost increases exponentially over time
DesignLow Cost
DevelopMedium Cost
TestHigh Cost
ProductionVery High Cost
Key Takeaway

DevSecOps's 'shift-left' approach ensures vulnerabilities are detected early, drastically reducing remediation costs and accelerating delivery.

Example

Finding a SQL injection during code review costs hours; finding it in production could cost millions in breach response.
