Core Concept

Why Traditional Security Fails in DevOps

Understanding the incompatibility of old models with modern agility.

Key Problem

Manual reviews and late-stage penetration testing create bottlenecks and significant rework, hindering rapid software delivery.

Consequence

Vulnerabilities discovered late are exponentially more expensive and time-consuming to fix, increasing project costs and delays.

Risk Amplification

Late detection means security flaws persist longer, increasing the attack surface and potential for breaches in production.

Traditional vs. DevSecOps Security
Traditional Security
DevSecOps

Traditional security is a late-stage gate, causing delays and high remediation costs. DevSecOps integrates security throughout the entire lifecycle for continuous protection.

Key Takeaway

DevSecOps is a necessary evolution to overcome the inherent friction and inefficiency of traditional security models in agile development.
