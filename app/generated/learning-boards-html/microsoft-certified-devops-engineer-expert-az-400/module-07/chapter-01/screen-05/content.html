Foundational Principles

Core Principles of DevSecOps

The pillars that define a successful DevSecOps culture.

Collaboration

Breaking down silos between Dev, Ops, and Security teams to foster shared responsibility and communication.

Shared Goals

Automation

Automating security checks, tests, and policy enforcement throughout the CI/CD pipeline to ensure speed and consistency.

Continuous Checks

Proactive Risk Management

Shifting from reactive incident response to anticipating and mitigating security risks early in the development lifecycle.

Early Mitigation
Key Takeaway

DevSecOps is a cultural and technical transformation driven by continuous collaboration, extensive automation, and a proactive approach to security risks.
