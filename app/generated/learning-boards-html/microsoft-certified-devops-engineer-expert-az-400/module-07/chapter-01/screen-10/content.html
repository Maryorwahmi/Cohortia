Implementation Challenge

Overcoming Cultural Resistance

Strategies to foster collaboration and overcome initial team hesitancy.

Clear Communication

Articulate the 'why' behind DevSecOps, focusing on shared benefits and goals.

Training & Empowerment

Provide developers with secure coding training and tools, empowering them to own security.

Demonstrate Value

Show how integrated security actually accelerates development by preventing late-stage issues.

Key Takeaway

Overcoming cultural resistance in DevSecOps requires proactive communication, comprehensive training, and clear demonstrations of how security enables faster, more reliable delivery.
