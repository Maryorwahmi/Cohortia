Introduction to DevSecOps Principles and Practices

Integrating Security into the Modern Software Delivery Lifecycle

In this chapter, we'll explore how DevSecOps embeds security and compliance directly into your development and operations pipelines, transforming security from a late-stage gatekeeper into a continuous, integrated process. You'll understand the 'shift-left' paradigm and its profound impact on software delivery.

Key Idea

DevSecOps is about embedding security from the start, not as an afterthought, making it an inherent quality of software delivery.
