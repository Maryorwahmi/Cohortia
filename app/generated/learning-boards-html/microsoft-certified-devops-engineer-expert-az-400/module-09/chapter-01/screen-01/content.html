Introduction to Dependency Management in DevOps

Building Reliable Software with External Components

In modern software development, applications are built upon layers of pre-existing code, libraries, and tools. Effectively managing these external components, known as dependencies, is crucial for the speed, reliability, security, and reproducibility of your software delivery pipeline.

Key Idea

Dependencies are the foundation of modern software, but managing them is crucial for DevOps success.
