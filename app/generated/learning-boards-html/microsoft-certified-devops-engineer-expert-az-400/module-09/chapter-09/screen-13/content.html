Key Concepts

Foundational terms for artifact security.

Software Supply Chain Security

Securing all stages of software development and delivery against malicious attacks and vulnerabilities.

Principle of Least Privilege

Granting users or services only the minimum necessary permissions to perform their required tasks.

Upstream Source Security

Measures taken to ensure packages pulled from external registries via upstream sources are safe and untampered.

Summary Takeaway

A robust artifact management strategy relies on understanding the entire software supply chain, applying the principle of least privilege, and securing all external dependencies.
