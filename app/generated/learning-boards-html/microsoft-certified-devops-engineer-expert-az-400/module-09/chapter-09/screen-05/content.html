Implementing Least Privilege for Artifact Feeds

Granting only the minimum necessary permissions.

Principle of Least Privilege

Grant users, groups, and service accounts only the minimum necessary permissions to perform their required tasks. This significantly reduces the potential impact of a compromised account.

Why it Matters

  • Minimizes the attack surface.
  • Reduces the blast radius in case of a breach.
  • Prevents accidental or malicious package tampering.
  • Enhances compliance with security regulations.
Key Takeaway

Strictly limiting permissions to the minimum necessary reduces the attack surface and prevents unauthorized modifications to artifacts.

Azure Artifacts Feed Roles

Readers

Can download and view packages. Ideal for most developers and build agents consuming artifacts.

Contributors

Can publish and delete packages. Restricted to build service accounts and trusted release managers.

Owners

Full control over the feed, including managing permissions. A very small group of administrators.
