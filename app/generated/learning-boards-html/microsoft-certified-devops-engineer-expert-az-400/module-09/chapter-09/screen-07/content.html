Mitigating Risks from External Dependencies

Controlling and auditing packages from public registries.

The Risk of Upstream Sources

While convenient, upstream sources introduce a potential attack vector. A compromised public registry could lead to malicious packages being cached and distributed through your internal feeds, impacting your entire software supply chain.

Why it Matters

  • Prevents distribution of malicious packages.
  • Ensures integrity of internal artifact feeds.
  • Maintains control over external dependencies.
  • Reduces exposure to public registry compromises.
Key Takeaway

Treat upstream sources as potential attack vectors and implement strict controls to validate and audit external packages.

Best Practices for Upstream Security

Use a Single, Trusted Upstream

Configure your Azure Artifacts feed as the sole gateway to public registries to centralize control.

Regularly Audit Sources

Review the list of configured upstream sources to ensure only approved registries are included.

Consider Allow/Block-listing

Implement policies to explicitly allow or block certain packages or versions from being cached.
