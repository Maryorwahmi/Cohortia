Ensuring Artifact Integrity and Authenticity

The role of immutability and digital signatures.

Artifact Immutability

Once an artifact version is published to Azure Artifacts, it cannot be overwritten. This ensures that the artifact remains unchanged, preventing tampering and providing a consistent, trustworthy source.

Why Signed Packages?

  • **Authenticity:** Verifies the identity of the package publisher.
  • **Integrity:** Guarantees the package hasn't been tampered with since signing.
  • Adds an extra layer of trust for consuming packages.
Key Takeaway

Immutable and digitally signed artifacts provide strong guarantees against tampering and verify the publisher's identity, enhancing trust in your software supply chain.

How Immutability & Signing Work

Immutability in Azure Artifacts

Once a package version is pushed to a feed, its content cannot be changed. This prevents malicious overwrites.

Digital Signatures for Packages

Publishers use certificates to sign packages, allowing consumers to verify the source and detect any post-signing modifications.
