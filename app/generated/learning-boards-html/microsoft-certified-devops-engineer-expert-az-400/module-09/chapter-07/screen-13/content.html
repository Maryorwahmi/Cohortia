Striking the Right Balance: Cost, Compliance, and Reproducibility

Collaborating to define effective and sustainable artifact strategies.

Cost Efficiency

Minimize storage expenses by purging unnecessary artifacts, but avoid critical data loss.

Compliance Adherence

Retain artifacts for mandated periods to meet regulatory and auditing requirements, preventing legal issues.

Guaranteed Reproducibility

Ensure the ability to rebuild or redeploy to known stable states for auditing, security, and disaster recovery.
