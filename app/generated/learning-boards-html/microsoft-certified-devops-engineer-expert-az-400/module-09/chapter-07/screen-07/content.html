The Pitfalls of Overly Aggressive Retention

Balancing cleanup with the need for historical data.

Risk: Accidental Deletion

Setting policies too short can lead to the irreversible loss of packages needed for critical operations.

Impact: Auditing & Compliance

Missing artifacts can break audit trails and lead to non-compliance with regulatory requirements, incurring penalties.

Consequence: Rollback & Debugging Issues

Inability to roll back to stable versions or debug issues in older deployments due to missing dependencies.
