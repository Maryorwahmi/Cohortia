Retention's Role in Build Performance and Reproducibility

How artifact management affects development efficiency and reliability.

Improved Build Performance

Leaner feeds mean package managers don't waste time sifting through irrelevant versions, leading to faster build times.

Enhanced Reproducibility

Retaining specific artifact versions ensures you can always rebuild or redeploy to a known, stable state for auditing or recovery.

Risk: Broken Builds

Deleting artifacts too quickly can break older builds or deployments, making rollbacks or patching impossible.

The Dual Benefits

Speed and reliability through smart retention.

Faster Builds

Guaranteed Reproducibility
