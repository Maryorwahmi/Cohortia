Optimizing Storage Costs and Meeting Compliance

The financial and regulatory implications of artifact retention.

Reduced Storage Costs

Prevents uncontrolled growth of repositories, leading to significant savings on storage infrastructure and services.

Ensured Compliance

Helps meet regulatory requirements (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA) by retaining artifacts for mandated periods, avoiding fines and legal issues.

Improved Auditability

Maintains a clear history of software components, crucial for internal and external audits and investigations.

The Dual Imperatives

Financial prudence and regulatory adherence.
