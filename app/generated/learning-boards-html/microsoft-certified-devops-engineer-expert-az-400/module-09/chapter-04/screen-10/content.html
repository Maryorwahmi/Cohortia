The .npmrc Configuration

Your .npmrc file must include a specific line to map your organization's scope to your private feed.

# Example .npmrc for consuming scoped packagesregistry=https://pkgs.dev.azure.com/YourOrg/_packaging/MyOrgNpmFeed/npm/registry/always-auth=true@your-org-name:registry=https://pkgs.dev.azure.com/YourOrg/_packaging/MyOrgNpmFeed/npm/registry/
Key Takeaway

The @scope:registry=... line is essential for npm to find your private scoped packages.

Why Scope Mapping Matters
  • Private Resolution: Directs npm to your private feed for packages under a specific scope.
  • Avoids 404s: Prevents npm from searching the public registry for private scoped packages.
  • Consistency: Ensures local development and CI/CD pipelines resolve packages identically.
  • Security: Maintains the privacy of your internal components.
