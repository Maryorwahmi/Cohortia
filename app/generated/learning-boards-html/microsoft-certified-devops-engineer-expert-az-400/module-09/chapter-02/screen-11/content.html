Package Views: Managing Quality and Promotion

Package views are filtered subsets of packages in a feed, allowing you to promote packages through different stages of quality and maturity.

Default Views

Every feed has @local (all packages) and @release (stable, production-ready) views by default.

Custom Views

Create custom views like @dev, @qa, or @beta to match your development workflow stages.

Quality Control

Consumers can configure clients to pull from specific views, ensuring they only get validated versions.

Package Views Flow
1. Publish to @local
2. Promote to @dev / @qa
3. Promote to @release
Ensures controlled package promotion through quality gates.
