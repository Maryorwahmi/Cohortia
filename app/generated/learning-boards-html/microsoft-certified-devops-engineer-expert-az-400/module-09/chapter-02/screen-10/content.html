Controlling Access to Your Package Feeds

Azure Artifacts integrates with Azure DevOps security to manage who can publish, consume, or administer packages, emphasizing the principle of least privilege.

Feed-Level Permissions

Permissions are assigned at the feed level to users, Azure DevOps groups, or Azure Active Directory groups.

Principle of Least Privilege

Grant only the necessary permissions for a user or service principal to perform their required tasks.

Automation Access

Automated pipelines typically use service connections or build identities with 'Contributor' rights to publish artifacts.

Feed Permissions
ReaderConsume packages
ContributorConsume & Publish packages
OwnerFull control, manage permissions
Assign roles to users or groups for granular control.
