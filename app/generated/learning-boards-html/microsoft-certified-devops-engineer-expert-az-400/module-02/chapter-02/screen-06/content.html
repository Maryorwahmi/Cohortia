The Mechanics of Rebasing

Step-by-step reapplication of commits.

01

Identify Common Ancestor

Git finds the point where your feature branch diverged from the target branch.

02

Unwind & Store Commits

Your feature branch's unique commits are temporarily set aside.

03

Update to New Base

Your branch is moved to the tip of the target branch, incorporating its latest changes.

04

Reapply Commits

The stored commits are reapplied one by one onto the new base, creating a linear history.

Key Takeaway

`git rebase` works by temporarily detaching your commits, updating your branch to the latest target, and then reapplying your commits on top, effectively rewriting history.
