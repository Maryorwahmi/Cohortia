Safety Warning

The Golden Rule of Rebasing

Never rebase commits that have been pushed to a shared remote repository.

Why it's Dangerous

Rebasing rewrites history, changing commit hashes. If others have pulled the original commits, their local history will diverge, leading to conflicts.

Consequences for Team

Team members would face complex `git pull --rebase` operations or forced repository resets, causing disruption and frustration.

Force Push with Caution

If absolutely necessary, use `git push --force-with-lease` (safer than `--force`) but only after clear communication and team agreement.
