Branch Policies: Your Code's Gatekeepers

Configurable rules that enforce quality and consistency on critical branches.

Pull Request
Protected Branch

Policies act as a gate, ensuring PRs meet criteria before merging.

What They Are

Configurable rules for specific branches (e.g., main, develop) to enforce quality and compliance.

How They Work

Applied to Pull Requests (PRs) targeting a protected branch, acting as gatekeepers before a merge is allowed.

Why They Matter

Crucial for maintaining code integrity, preventing unauthorized changes, and enforcing compliance standards in critical branches.

Key Takeaway

Branch policies are essential automated controls that ensure every code change meets predefined quality and security standards before impacting your stable codebase.
