Key Concepts

Reinforce your understanding of advanced ARM Template techniques.

Nested Templates

Templates defined directly within a parent, useful for tightly coupled, encapsulated components.

Linked Templates

External template files referenced by a parent, promoting reusability and separation of concerns.

Incremental Deployment

Default mode; adds new resources and updates existing ones, leaving others untouched. Safer for updates.

Summary Takeaway

Modular templates and understanding deployment modes are foundational for building maintainable and robust Azure infrastructure as code.
