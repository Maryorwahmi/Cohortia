Complete Deployment

Ensuring exact template-defined resource group state.

Behavior

Deletes any resources in the resource group that are *not* defined in the template. Ensures exact match.

Use Case

Ideal for creating clean, consistent environments from scratch or for tearing down and rebuilding environments.

Caution

Use with extreme caution in production; accidental omission of resources from your template could lead to data loss.
