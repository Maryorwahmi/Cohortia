Multi-stage Pipelines and Approval Gates

Enforcing controlled IaC releases for sensitive environments

Benefits of Approval Gates

  • **Human Review:** Ensures critical changes are manually vetted before deployment.
  • **Risk Mitigation:** Prevents unintended or unapproved changes to sensitive environments.
  • **Compliance:** Helps meet regulatory and internal compliance requirements.
  • **Automated Checks:** Integrate security scans or policy evaluations as pre-deployment gates.
Key Takeaway

Multi-stage pipelines with approval gates provide essential control over IaC deployments, enforcing human review and automated checks to ensure secure and compliant releases to sensitive environments.

Stage 2: Deploy to QA

Misconception

Approval gates are only for manual checks. They can also integrate automated checks like security scans or compliance policy evaluations, providing a comprehensive safety net.
