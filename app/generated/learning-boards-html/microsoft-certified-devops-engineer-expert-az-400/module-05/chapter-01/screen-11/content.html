Decision Point

Selecting Your IaC Tool for Azure

Factors influencing your choice: project requirements, team expertise, and strategic goals.

ARM Templates

  • Pros: Native, deep Azure integration.
  • Cons: Verbose JSON, steeper learning curve.
  • Best for: Azure-only, JSON familiarity.

Bicep

  • Pros: Concise syntax, type safety, modules.
  • Cons: Azure-specific.
  • Best for: Azure-only, improved developer experience.

Terraform

  • Pros: Multi-cloud, HCL, large community.
  • Cons: External state management.
  • Best for: Multi-cloud, existing HCL expertise.
Consider these factors when making your choice:

Project Scope

Team Expertise

Future Strategy

Key Takeaway

Evaluate ARM Templates, Bicep, and Terraform based on project needs, team skills, and multi-cloud strategy to choose the most effective IaC tool.
