Mitigating Infrastructure Drift

Strategies to detect and prevent deviations from your IaC

Proactive Prevention
  • Read-only access: Limit manual changes in production environments.
  • CI/CD integration: Embed drift detection in your deployment pipelines.
Caution with 'Complete' Mode

While ARM/Bicep's 'Complete' deployment mode can remediate drift by deleting undeclared resources, use it with extreme caution. It can lead to unintended data loss if not managed carefully.

Key Takeaway

A combination of access control, regular drift detection, and automated remediation is key to maintaining IaC integrity.
