Simulating Infrastructure Drift

Manually introduce a change to observe drift detection

The Drift Scenario
  • A common scenario is an emergency fix or a quick manual adjustment.
  • This bypasses the IaC workflow, creating a discrepancy.
  • Our goal is to detect and then remediate this deviation.
Why This Matters

Simulating drift helps you understand its real-world impact and reinforces the importance of automated detection and remediation processes in a robust DevOps pipeline.

Key Takeaway

Manually changing a resource in the Azure portal creates infrastructure drift, which Terraform can detect.

Steps to Simulate Drift

1.Go to Azure portal > Your Terraform-created Storage Account.
2.Navigate to Configuration under 'Settings'.
3.Change Min TLS version from TLS1_2 to TLS1_0.
4.Click Save to apply the manual change.
