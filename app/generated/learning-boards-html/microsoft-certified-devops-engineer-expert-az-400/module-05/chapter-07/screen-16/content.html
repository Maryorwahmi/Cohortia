Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential ideas to remember from this chapter

Azure Policy

A governance service in Azure that allows you to create, assign, and manage policies to enforce rules and effects on your resources, ensuring compliance.

Secrets Management

The practice of securely storing and managing sensitive information (passwords, keys) separate from IaC templates and version control.

GitOps

An operational framework where Git is the single source of truth for declarative infrastructure and applications, with automated reconciliation between desired and actual states.

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging Azure Policy, secure secrets management, and GitOps principles enables robust, compliant, and automated IaC workflows.
