Blue/Green Deployments: Instant Traffic Switchovers

Maintaining two identical environments for rapid, low-risk releases.

How Blue/Green Deployments Work

  • •Maintain two identical production environments: 'Blue' (current) and 'Green' (new).
  • •Deploy the new application version to the 'Green' environment, isolated from live traffic.
  • •Thoroughly test the 'Green' environment in isolation.
  • •Once validated, instantly switch all live traffic from 'Blue' to 'Green' (e.g., via load balancer).
  • •If issues arise, instantly revert traffic back to the 'Blue' environment.
Key Takeaway

Blue/Green deployments offer near-zero downtime and instant rollback capabilities, making them ideal for critical applications, though they require duplicate infrastructure.
