Core Concept

Rolling Updates: The Foundation of Zero-Downtime Deployments

Gradually replacing old Pods with new ones to ensure continuous availability.

How it Works

  • •When a Deployment is updated, Kubernetes doesn't shut down all old Pods at once.
  • •It starts new Pods with the updated configuration.
  • •Once new Pods are healthy, old Pods are gracefully terminated.
  • •This process repeats in batches until all old Pods are replaced.
Key Takeaway

Rolling updates are Kubernetes' default strategy for deploying application changes, ensuring minimal disruption and high availability by managing Pod replacement in a controlled, iterative manner.
