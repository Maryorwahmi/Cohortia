Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting Common Kubernetes Deployment Issues

A systematic approach using essential kubectl commands for rapid diagnosis.

Systematic Troubleshooting Steps

  1. •Check Pod Status: kubectl get pods for overall health.
  2. •Describe Pods: kubectl describe pod <pod-name> for detailed events and conditions.
  3. •View Logs: kubectl logs <pod-name> for application output.
  4. •Check Deployments/Services: Ensure correct configuration.
  5. •Check Events: kubectl get events for cluster-wide issues.
Key Takeaway

A methodical approach using `kubectl` commands is your first line of defense for quickly identifying and resolving Kubernetes deployment issues, ensuring application stability.

Common Deployment Mistakes

Misconfigured Probes

Incorrect paths, ports, or thresholds leading to false failures or restarts.

Insufficient Resources

Low CPU/memory requests/limits causing Pod evictions or crashes.

Incorrect Image Names

Typo or non-existent image leading to ImagePullBackOff errors.
