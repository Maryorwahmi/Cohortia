Key Concepts: Advanced Strategies & Monitoring

Further Insights for Robust AKS Management

Canary Deployment

A strategy where a new version is rolled out to a small subset of users, then gradually to more users if successful.

Blue/Green Deployment

A strategy involving two identical environments (blue for current, green for new) where traffic is switched entirely.

Azure Monitor for Containers

Azure's solution for monitoring the performance and health of AKS clusters and containerized applications.

Summary Takeaway

Advanced deployment strategies like Canary and Blue/Green, combined with comprehensive monitoring via Azure Monitor and effective `kubectl` troubleshooting, are vital for managing robust AKS environments.
