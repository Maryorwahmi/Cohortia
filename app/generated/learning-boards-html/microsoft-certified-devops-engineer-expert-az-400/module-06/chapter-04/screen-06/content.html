Kubernetes Cluster Architecture

Understanding the Control Plane and Worker Nodes.

Control Plane (Master Node)

The 'brain' of the Kubernetes cluster. It manages the worker nodes and the Pods running on them, making global decisions about the cluster.

  • •Role: Orchestrates and manages the entire cluster.
  • •Function: Ensures the desired state of the cluster is maintained.
  • •Key: Does NOT run application containers directly.
Key Takeaway

The Control Plane is responsible for cluster management and orchestration, while Worker Nodes execute the actual containerized applications.

Worker Nodes

These are the machines (VMs or physical servers) where your actual containerized applications run. They provide the resources for your workloads.

  • •Role: Hosts application containers (Pods).
  • •Function: Executes workloads as instructed by the Control Plane.
  • •Key: Provides compute, memory, and storage resources.

Analogy: A Factory and its Workers

Think of the Control Plane as the factory's management office, planning production, assigning tasks, and ensuring quality. The Worker Nodes are the factory floor machines and workers, actually building the products (running your applications) according to the management's instructions.
