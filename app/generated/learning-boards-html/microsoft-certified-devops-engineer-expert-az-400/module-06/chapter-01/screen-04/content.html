The Challenge

Environment Inconsistencies

Applications failing in new environments due to differing OS versions, libraries, or configurations.

Common Scenario

A developer's application runs perfectly on their local machine but crashes in the staging environment, leading to debugging delays.

Impact

Slow deployments, increased debugging time, and friction between development and operations teams.

The Challenge

Complex Dependency Management

Manually tracking and installing all required libraries, runtimes, and frameworks for each application.

Example

An application requires Python 3.8, specific versions of `numpy` and `pandas`, and a particular database driver, all of which must be precisely installed.

Consequence

Dependency conflicts, version mismatches, and fragile deployment processes that break with minor updates.

Key Takeaway

Traditional deployment methods struggled with environment consistency and dependency management, leading to significant operational overhead and delays.
