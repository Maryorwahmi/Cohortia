Kubernetes Manifests: Defining Deployments

Declaratively managing your application's desired state.

What is a Deployment?

A Kubernetes Deployment describes the desired state for your application, including the container image, number of replicas, and resource requirements.

  • •Manages stateless applications.
  • •Ensures a specified number of Pod replicas are running.
  • •Handles rolling updates and rollbacks.
  • •Uses selectors to manage Pods based on labels.
Key Takeaway

Deployments are the workhorse for running and scaling your containerized applications reliably in Kubernetes.
