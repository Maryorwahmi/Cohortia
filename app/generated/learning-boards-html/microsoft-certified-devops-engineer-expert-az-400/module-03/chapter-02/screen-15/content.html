Key Concepts to Remember

Reinforcing your understanding of Azure Pipelines for CI.

Microsoft-hosted Agents

Virtual machines managed by Azure DevOps, pre-configured with common tools, offering zero maintenance.

Self-hosted Agents

Machines (physical or virtual) that you set up and manage yourself, providing full control over the environment.

Stage

A logical division in a pipeline that groups related jobs, typically representing a major phase like Build, Test, or Deploy.

Summary Takeaway

Choosing between Microsoft-hosted and self-hosted agents depends on your control needs, while stages provide essential structure for complex pipeline workflows.
