Publish Build Artifacts

  • Task: PublishBuildArtifacts@1.
  • pathToPublish: $(Build.ArtifactStagingDirectory) – a predefined variable pointing to the staging area.
  • artifactName: 'drop' – a common name for the primary build output.
  • publishLocation: 'Container' – publishes to the Azure DevOps artifact store.

Why Publish Artifacts?

  • Downstream Consumption: Essential for deployment stages or other pipelines.
  • Versioned Output: Creates a clear, traceable output of your build.
  • Archiving: Provides a record of compiled code.
  • Collaboration: Easily shareable with other teams.
Key Takeaway

Publishing build artifacts is crucial for making your compiled output available for deployment and ensuring a seamless transition from CI to CD.
