Understanding "Shift Left"

Integrating security and quality early in the SDLC

What is "Shift Left"?

"Shift Left" is a DevOps principle advocating for the integration of quality, testing, and security activities into the earliest possible stages of the software development lifecycle (SDLC). Instead of waiting for later phases, checks are performed continuously from design and coding.

Cost Reduction

Fixing issues early is significantly cheaper than fixing them in later stages or production.

Improved Quality

Continuous feedback loops lead to higher quality code and fewer defects overall.

Key Takeaway

Shifting left means finding and fixing issues early in the SDLC, significantly reducing the cost and effort compared to late-stage detection.
