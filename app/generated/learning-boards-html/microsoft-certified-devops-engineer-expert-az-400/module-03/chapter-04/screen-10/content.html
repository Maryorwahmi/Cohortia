Building CI: Artifacts & Control Flow

This section focuses on preserving important outputs from your workflow and controlling step execution based on conditions.

Upload Artifacts

The actions/upload-artifact@v4 action saves files generated during the workflow run, such as test reports, making them accessible later.

Conditional Execution (if: always())

The if: always() condition ensures a step runs regardless of whether previous steps succeeded or failed, which is crucial for capturing debugging information.

Debugging & Auditing

Uploading artifacts and using conditional steps are vital for effective debugging and auditing of your CI pipeline, providing insights even when things go wrong.

Key Takeaway

Effectively managing artifacts and leveraging conditional logic enhances the robustness and diagnostic capabilities of your GitHub Actions workflows.
