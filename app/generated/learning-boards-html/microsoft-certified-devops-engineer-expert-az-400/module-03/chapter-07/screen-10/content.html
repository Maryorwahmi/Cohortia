Best Practices

Common Mistakes & Safety

Avoid pitfalls in parallelism, caching, and triggers, and implement safety measures for critical pipeline operations.

Key Takeaway

Thorough testing and careful configuration of advanced features are essential to prevent unexpected behavior and ensure pipeline reliability.

Safety Note

Production deployments should always have robust safety checks, such as manual approvals or strict conditional logic, to prevent accidental releases.

Common Issues

Debugging Challenges

  • Incorrect cache keys leading to misses or stale data.
  • Overly complex trigger conditions that fail to activate or deactivate pipelines correctly.
  • Misconfigured dependencies breaking parallelism.
  • Unintended production deployments due to lax conditional logic.
Debugging Tip

Always review detailed pipeline logs, echo variable values, and test configurations in a safe environment before applying them to production workflows.
