The Role of Observability

Understanding system behavior through its outputs.

Observability is the ability to understand a system's internal state by examining its external outputs.

Key pillars include: Metrics (numerical values over time), Logs (timestamped event records), and Traces (request paths across distributed systems).

Together, these provide a comprehensive view for deep troubleshooting and performance optimization.

Observability is crucial for effective management and continuous improvement of complex systems.

Key Takeaway

Embrace observability by leveraging metrics, logs, and traces to gain deep insights into your system's behavior and drive continuous improvement.
