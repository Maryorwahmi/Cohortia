Safety, Security, and Cost Considerations

Ensuring monitoring systems are robust, secure, and cost-effective.

Availability: Ensure monitoring systems are highly available and resilient to avoid losing visibility during critical times.

Security: Strictly control access to monitoring data and configurations. Protect sensitive information and prevent unauthorized access.

Cost Management: Optimize data retention, configure sampling, and regularly review costs to ensure efficiency and budget alignment.

Testing: Always test alerts to verify they fire correctly and trigger the intended actions.

Key Takeaway

Implement monitoring solutions with a focus on reliability, security, cost-efficiency, and thorough testing to build trust and ensure continuous value.
