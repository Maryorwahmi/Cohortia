Common Mistakes in Monitoring

Avoiding pitfalls that undermine monitoring effectiveness.

Alert Fatigue: Creating too many non-actionable alerts leads to teams ignoring them. Focus on critical, actionable alerts.

Lack of Context: Alerts without sufficient detail (deployment info, logs) hinder troubleshooting. Ensure alerts link to relevant data.

Ignoring Non-Functional Requirements: Neglecting monitoring for performance, scalability, and security.

Monitoring as an Afterthought: Not integrating monitoring into the CD process from the start.

Key Takeaway

Proactively address common monitoring mistakes to ensure your feedback loops are effective and drive meaningful improvements.
