Learning Objectives

Understand the critical role of monitoring and feedback in CD.

Bloom's Taxonomy Learn Understand Apply Master
01

Explain Role of Monitoring

Articulate why continuous monitoring and feedback loops are vital for maintaining the health and reliability of your CD pipelines and deployed applications.

02

Identify Key Metrics

Recognize the essential metrics and telemetry data needed to effectively monitor applications and infrastructure within a CD context.

03

Integrate Azure Monitor

Learn to integrate Azure Monitor and Application Insights into CD pipelines for automated health checks and performance analysis.

04

Configure Alerts & Feedback

Set up alerts for rapid feedback and design comprehensive loops to inform teams and stakeholders about deployment status and application health.
