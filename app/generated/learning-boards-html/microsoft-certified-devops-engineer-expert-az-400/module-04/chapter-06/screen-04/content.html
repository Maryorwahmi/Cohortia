The 'Why' Behind Monitoring

Continuous Delivery is an ongoing process, not a one-time event.

Continuous Delivery is not a 'fire and forget' process. Once an application is deployed, the work isn't over; in fact, a new phase of critical activity begins: **monitoring and feedback loops**.

These are absolutely essential for understanding the real-world performance, stability, and user experience of your deployed applications. Without robust monitoring, you're essentially deploying blind.

A well-designed feedback loop closes the gap between deployment and operational insight, empowering teams to learn, adapt, and continuously improve.

Key Takeaway

Deploying without monitoring means operating without insight, risking user impact and hindering continuous improvement. Monitoring transforms CD into a cycle of learning and adaptation.
