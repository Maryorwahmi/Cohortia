Reliability and Rapid Feedback Loops

Building safety nets through comprehensive testing and ensuring immediate issue detection.

Comprehensive Testing

Multiple levels of testing (unit, integration, E2E, performance, security) act as safety nets to catch defects early.

Rapid Feedback Loops

Immediate notification of build failures, test breaks, or deployment issues enables swift problem resolution.

Key Takeaway

Reliability is built through rigorous testing, while rapid feedback ensures a 'fail fast' approach, minimizing the cost of defects.
