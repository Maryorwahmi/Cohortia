Quality Gates and Manual Approvals in CD

Enforcing compliance and quality standards throughout the pipeline.

Automated Quality Gates

Checkpoints that automatically verify criteria (e.g., security scans, performance thresholds) before proceeding.

Manual Approvals

Human sign-offs at strategic points, often for high-risk deployments or regulatory compliance.

Key Takeaway

Both automated gates and manual approvals are essential for balancing deployment speed with necessary quality and compliance checks.
