Avoiding Common Pitfalls in CD Implementation

Learning from typical challenges to build robust and reliable delivery pipelines.

Incomplete Automation

Relying on manual steps for deployment introduces errors, delays, and inconsistencies.

Neglecting Comprehensive Testing

A 'green build' doesn't mean bug-free. Lack of integration, E2E, or performance tests leads to production issues.

Environment Disparity

Staging environments not mirroring production lead to 'works on my machine' issues and unexpected failures.

Key Takeaway

Proactive mitigation of these common mistakes through full automation, robust testing, and environment parity is crucial for CD success.
