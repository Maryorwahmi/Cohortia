Automation: The Heart of Continuous Delivery

Eliminating manual steps to ensure speed, consistency, and reliability in software releases.

Why Automate?

Manual steps are error-prone, slow, and introduce inconsistencies. Automation ensures repeatable, reliable processes.

What to Automate?

Everything from code compilation, unit tests, integration tests, to infrastructure and application deployments.

Key Takeaway

Automation is the bedrock of CD, enabling faster, more reliable, and consistent software delivery by eliminating human error.
