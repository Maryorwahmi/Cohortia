Evolving Deployment Strategies

From 'Stop-the-World' to Continuous Availability.

Traditional 'Big Bang' Deployment

  • •All instances updated simultaneously.
  • •Requires application downtime.
  • •High risk of complete service outage if new version fails.
  • •Rollback can be complex and time-consuming.
Impact

Unacceptable for critical applications requiring 24/7 availability.

Modern Advanced Deployment Patterns

  • •Gradual, controlled updates (e.g., Rolling, Canary).
  • •Minimizes or eliminates downtime.
  • •Reduces risk; issues affect only a subset of users.
  • •Facilitates quick and easy rollbacks.
Benefit

Ensures continuous availability and enhances release reliability.

Key Takeaway

Modern DevOps demands deployment strategies that prioritize continuous availability and risk mitigation, moving away from disruptive 'big bang' updates.
