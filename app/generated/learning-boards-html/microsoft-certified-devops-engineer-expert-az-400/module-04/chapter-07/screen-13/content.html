Avoiding Pitfalls in Advanced Deployments

Ensuring Robustness and Security.

Common Mistakes

  • •**Rolling Updates:** Lack of proper health checks for new instances.
  • •**Rolling Updates:** `maxUnavailable` set too low, causing prolonged deployments.
  • •**Immutable Infra:** Increased build time for new images.
  • •**Immutable Infra:** Insufficient robust artifact management.
Consequence

Can lead to service degradation, extended downtime, or inconsistent environments.

Safety Notes & Best Practices

  • •Design applications for graceful shutdown/startup.
  • •Ensure robust load balancer health checks.
  • •Secure image build processes; apply all patches during creation.
  • •Always have a clear rollback strategy (e.g., deploy previous stable image).
Pro-Tip

Combine rolling updates with immutable principles for the most reliable and low-risk deployments.

Key Takeaway

Proactive planning for health checks, resource management, and robust rollback strategies is essential for successful advanced deployments.
