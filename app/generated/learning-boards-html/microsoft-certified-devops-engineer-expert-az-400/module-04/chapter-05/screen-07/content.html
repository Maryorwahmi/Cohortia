Granting Access: Service Principals and Permissions

Configuring the necessary permissions for your Azure DevOps Service Connection to access Key Vault secrets.

Key Steps for Granting Access

  • Identify Service Principal: Locate the Service Principal linked to your Azure DevOps Service Connection.
  • Navigate to Key Vault: In the Azure portal, go to your Key Vault's 'Access policies'.
  • Add Access Policy: Create a new policy, selecting the identified Service Principal.
  • Grant Permissions: Assign 'Get' and 'List' permissions for secrets.
Service Principal Role

A Service Principal acts as an identity for your Azure DevOps pipeline, allowing it to authenticate and interact with Azure resources like Key Vault. It's crucial for automated access.

Principle of Least Privilege

This security best practice dictates that users, programs, and processes should be granted only the minimum necessary permissions to perform their work. For Key Vault access, this means granting only 'Get' and 'List' for secrets, avoiding broader permissions like 'Set' or 'Delete' unless absolutely required.

Common Mistake

Granting 'All' secret permissions to a Service Principal. This is an over-permissioning risk that can lead to unauthorized modification or deletion of secrets.

Key Takeaway

Always apply the principle of least privilege when granting Key Vault access to Service Principals, typically limiting permissions to 'Get' and 'List' for secrets.
