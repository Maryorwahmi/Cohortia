Powering Advanced Scenarios with Feature Flags

A/B testing, gradual rollouts, and kill switches

A/B Testing

Release a feature to a subset of users (e.g., 10%) and compare their behavior with users who don't see the feature to make data-driven decisions.

Gradual Rollouts

Enable a feature for internal users, then beta testers, then a small percentage of production users, and finally everyone, minimizing risk.

Kill Switches

Quickly disable a problematic feature in production without rolling back the entire application, providing immediate incident response.

Trunk-Based Development

Developers can commit incomplete features to the main branch, keeping them hidden behind a flag until they are fully ready for release.

Key Takeaway

Feature Flags are a powerful enabler for modern development practices, supporting experimentation, controlled releases, and rapid incident response.
