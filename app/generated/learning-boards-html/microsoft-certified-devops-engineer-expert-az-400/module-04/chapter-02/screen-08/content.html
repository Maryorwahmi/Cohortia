Canary Deployments

Mitigating risk with phased rollouts

Definition

A new version ('canary') is deployed to a small subset of users/servers, monitored, then progressively rolled out.

Gradual Approach

Unlike Blue/Green's all-or-nothing switch, Canary allows for phased exposure to real production traffic.

Risk Mitigation

If issues are detected, the canary can be rolled back, affecting only a small percentage of users.

Key Takeaway

Canary deployments are ideal for mitigating risk and gathering real-world feedback by gradually introducing new software to a controlled user group.
