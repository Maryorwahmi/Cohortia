Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Release Control

Release Gate

A condition or check that must be satisfied before a deployment can proceed to the next stage in an Azure Pipeline.

Pre-deployment Approval

A manual gate requiring specific users or groups to approve a deployment *before* a stage begins.

Post-deployment Approval

A manual gate requiring specific users or groups to approve a deployment *after* a stage completes.

Summary Takeaway

Release gates and approvals are critical checkpoints, offering both automated and manual controls to ensure quality and compliance throughout your deployment pipeline.
