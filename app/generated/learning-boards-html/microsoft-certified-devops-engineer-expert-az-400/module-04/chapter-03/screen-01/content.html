Implementing Release Gates and Approvals

Ensuring Quality and Compliance in Azure Pipelines

In this chapter, you will learn how to integrate critical checkpoints into your Azure Pipelines, enabling both manual oversight and automated validation to ensure every deployment meets your quality and compliance standards. This approach is vital for mitigating risks and building confidence in your Continuous Delivery process.

Key Takeaway

Release gates and approvals are essential for pausing deployments to verify conditions and mitigate risks before proceeding.
