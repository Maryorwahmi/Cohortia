Setting Up Pre-deployment Approvals

Guarding Your Environments with Manual Checks

Key Takeaway

Pre-deployment approvals are configured on environments to enforce human sign-off before a stage begins, crucial for sensitive deployments.

Example Scenario

A release manager must approve all deployments to the Production environment before they can start.

1

Navigate to Environments

Go to Pipelines > Environments and select your target environment (e.g., Production).

2

Add Approvals Check

Click '...' (More options) > 'Approvals and checks', then '+ Add a check' and choose 'Approvals'.

3

Configure Approvers & Policies

Add users/groups, set 'Require at least one approver' or 'Require all approvers', and define a 'Timeout'.
