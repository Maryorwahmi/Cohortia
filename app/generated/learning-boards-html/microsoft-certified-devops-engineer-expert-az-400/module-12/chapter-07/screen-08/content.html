Alerting Best Practices

Combating Alert Fatigue: The Silent Threat

Balancing Information with Actionable Notifications

Understanding Alert Fatigue

Alert fatigue is a state of desensitization caused by an overwhelming volume of non-critical or redundant alerts, leading operators to ignore important warnings.

  • •Operators become overwhelmed and start ignoring notifications.
  • •Increased risk of missing critical incidents.
  • •Reduced team morale and increased stress.
Concrete Example

A system generates 50 'low disk space' alerts daily for non-critical logs. Operators start ignoring them, then miss a critical alert for a production database running out of space.

The Cost of Noise

Too many alerts lead to missed critical events.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize actionable alerts by defining clear rules and thresholds to prevent alert fatigue and ensure timely responses to critical issues.
