Designing for Observability: The Four Golden Signals

Ensuring Comprehensive Coverage for System Health

The Four Golden Signals of Monitoring

Latency

Time to serve a request; indicates responsiveness.

Traffic

Demand on your system; shows usage patterns.

Errors

Rate of failed requests; highlights reliability issues.

Saturation

How 'full' your service is; indicates resource bottlenecks.

Concrete Example

For an e-commerce API, monitoring latency of checkout calls, traffic volume during sales, error rates for payment processing, and database connection saturation are critical.

Distributed Tracing

Follow requests across microservices to identify bottlenecks.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize the Four Golden Signals and leverage distributed tracing to build an actionable and efficient monitoring strategy, avoiding the pitfalls of over-monitoring.
