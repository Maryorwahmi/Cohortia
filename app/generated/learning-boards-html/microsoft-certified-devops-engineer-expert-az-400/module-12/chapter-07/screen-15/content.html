Key Concepts: Alerting & Feedback Loops

Driving Improvement and Resilience

Four Golden Signals

Key metrics for monitoring a service: Latency, Traffic, Errors, and Saturation.

Alert Fatigue

Excessive or non-critical alerts leading operators to ignore warnings, potentially missing critical issues.

Action Groups

Collections of notification preferences and actions (e.g., email, SMS, webhook) triggered by Azure Monitor alerts.

Summary Takeaway

Effective alerting, guided by Golden Signals and Action Groups, combined with blameless post-mortems, creates robust feedback loops essential for continuous improvement and system resilience.
