Building Your AZ-400 Self-Assessment Matrix

Systematically review skills, identify gaps, and create a targeted study plan.

Access Official Skills Measured

Start by reviewing the 'Skills measured' section on the official Microsoft Learn AZ-400 exam page.

Create & Populate Matrix

Use a spreadsheet to list skills, rate proficiency (1-5), estimate study hours, and map to resources.

Track Progress & Refine

Monitor your progress, adjust study time, and update resources as you deepen your understanding.

Example Scenario

You rate 'Implement a secure and compliant pipeline' as a 2/5. Your matrix would then prompt you to allocate 6 hours, link to Azure Key Vault documentation, and set a target date, ensuring this gap is addressed.

Key Takeaway

A self-assessment matrix provides a clear, actionable, and personalized study plan, ensuring comprehensive coverage of all AZ-400 objectives.

Pro Tip

Be brutally honest with your proficiency ratings. Overestimating your knowledge will leave critical gaps in your preparation.
