Design Principles

Cost-Conscious Design in CI/CD & IaC

Embedding cost considerations from the outset of your projects

Concrete Example

An IaC template that provisions a test environment and automatically de-provisions it after a pull request merge prevents idle resource costs.

Why it Matters

Proactive cost design prevents costly surprises and ensures financial sustainability throughout the project lifecycle.

Key Takeaway

Integrate cost-conscious design principles into CI/CD and IaC to build inherently efficient and financially sustainable solutions.

Principles for Cost-Efficient Design

Ephemeral Environments
  • Provision on demand
  • Automate teardown
  • Avoid idle resources
Shared Services
  • Centralize common resources
  • Avoid duplication
  • Reduce overhead
Efficient IaC & Pipelines
  • Appropriate SKUs
  • Lean pipeline steps
  • Cache dependencies
Continuous Review

Regularly assess and refine cost-saving measures.
