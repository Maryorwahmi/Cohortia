Optimization Strategy

Optimizing Compute Costs

Strategies for efficient Azure compute resource consumption

Concrete Example

A dev VM used only 8 hours a day can save 66% of its compute cost by being automatically shut down overnight and on weekends.

Pro-Tip

Regularly review Azure Advisor recommendations for personalized compute cost-saving opportunities.

Key Takeaway

Right-sizing, auto-scaling, reserved instances, and scheduled shutdowns are crucial for significant compute cost savings.

Compute Cost Optimization Techniques

Right-Sizing

Match VM/App Service plans to actual workload needs.

Auto-Scaling

Dynamically adjust resources based on demand.

Reserved Instances / Savings Plans

Commit to 1 or 3 years for significant discounts.

Scheduled Start/Stop

Automate shutdowns for non-production environments.
