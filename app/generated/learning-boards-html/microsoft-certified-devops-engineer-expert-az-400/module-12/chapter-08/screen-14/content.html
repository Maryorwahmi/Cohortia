Key Concepts to Remember

Essential terms for cost management in Azure DevOps

Cost Drivers

Primary services or activities that significantly contribute to cloud expenditure (e.g., compute, storage, networking).

Right-Sizing

Matching the size and performance of a resource to its actual workload requirements to avoid over-provisioning.

Auto-scaling

Dynamically adjusting the number or capacity of resources based on demand to optimize performance and cost.

Summary Takeaway:

Understanding these core concepts is fundamental to building and maintaining cost-efficient Azure DevOps solutions.
