Security & Quality

Environment Checks and Approvals

Enforcing quality and security with a robust mechanism for controlled deployments.

  • •Configurable conditions that must be met before deployment to an environment.
  • •Acts as a critical safety net, preventing unintended or unapproved changes.
  • •Essential for production environments and compliance requirements.
  • •Combines manual and automated validations for comprehensive control.
Key Takeaway

Leverage environment checks and approvals to build a multi-layered defense, ensuring only validated and authorized changes reach critical systems.

Scenario

A production deployment requires approval from a release manager AND must occur outside business hours. Both checks can be configured on the environment.

Types of Environment Checks
Manual Approvals

Designated user/group must approve.

Branch Control

Deployments only from specific branches.

Business Hours

Restrict deployments to specific time windows.

Invoke Azure Function

Trigger external validation processes.
