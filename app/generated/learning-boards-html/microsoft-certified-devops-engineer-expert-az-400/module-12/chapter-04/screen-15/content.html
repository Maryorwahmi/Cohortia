Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential takeaways from advanced Azure Pipelines.

Deployment Gates

Automated checks running before or after a stage, ensuring conditions are met (e.g., no critical alerts).

Stages

Major divisions within a pipeline, representing different phases of a CI/CD process (e.g., Build, Test, Deploy).

Jobs

A series of steps that run sequentially within a stage, executed on an agent.

Dependencies (`dependsOn`)

Explicit relationships between stages or jobs to control their execution order.

Summary Takeaway

Stages, jobs, and dependencies provide the structural framework for orchestrating complex, multi-phase deployments with fine-grained control and automated quality assurance.
