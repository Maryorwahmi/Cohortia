Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential takeaways from advanced Azure Pipelines.

YAML Templates

Reusable YAML files for pipeline definitions, promoting consistency and reducing duplication.

Environments

Logical groupings of deployment targets in Azure DevOps with associated checks and approvals.

Checks & Approvals

Configurable conditions (manual, branch control, business hours) for deployment to an environment.

Summary Takeaway

Advanced Azure Pipelines leverage templates for reusability, environments for target management, and checks/approvals for robust quality and security control.
