A Systematic Approach to Problem Solving

Applying an 8-step methodology to diagnose and resolve complex DevOps issues.

01

Define the Problem

What exactly is failing? When did it start? What changed recently?

02

Gather Information

Collect logs, metrics, configuration files, and recent changes.

03

Formulate Hypotheses

Based on information, what are possible causes?

04

Test Hypotheses

Systematically eliminate causes. Change one thing at a time.

05

Identify Root Cause

Pinpoint the exact reason for the failure.

06

Implement Solution

Apply a fix to address the identified root cause.

07

Verify Solution

Ensure the fix works and doesn't introduce new issues.

08

Document & Prevent

Update runbooks, add monitoring, or implement automated tests.

Key Takeaway

A structured methodology ensures efficient problem resolution, fosters learning, and prevents recurrence of issues.
