Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Hardcoded Secrets

Storing sensitive credentials directly within code or configuration files, a major security anti-pattern.

Monolithic Pipelines

Overly complex, single-purpose pipelines that are difficult to manage, maintain, and troubleshoot.

"Snowflake" Servers

Unique, manually configured servers that cannot be easily reproduced, leading to environment inconsistencies.

Structured Troubleshooting

A systematic, step-by-step approach to diagnosing and resolving technical problems.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

The process of identifying the fundamental reason for a problem, rather than just addressing its symptoms.

Summary Takeaway

By understanding these advanced anti-patterns and methodologies, you can proactively build more resilient, secure, and efficient DevOps systems.
