Reproducing, Verbose Logging, and Rollback

Strategic approaches to isolate, diagnose, and mitigate pipeline failures.

01

Reproduce Locally

Attempt to replicate the failing pipeline step on your local development machine. This helps isolate the problem from the CI/CD environment.

Why it matters

Isolates environment-specific issues and allows for faster debugging with familiar tools.

02

Verbose Logging

Temporarily enable verbose logging (e.g., system.debug: true) to get more diagnostic information and deeper insights.

Why it matters

Reveals subtle details, exact commands, and environment variables that are hidden in standard logs.

03

Rollback

If a deployment fails in production, prioritize rolling back to the last known good state to minimize impact.

Why it matters

Minimizes downtime and user impact, allowing time for thorough, pressure-free troubleshooting.

Key Takeaway

These techniques are crucial for isolating problems, gaining deeper insights, and mitigating the impact of failures, especially in production environments.
