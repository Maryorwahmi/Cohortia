Decoding Pipeline Failure Messages

The first step in troubleshooting is always a thorough log analysis.

Understanding Logs

Pipeline logs are the most detailed record of execution, providing insights into every step and job. They are your primary diagnostic tool.

  • Look for explicit error messages and stack traces.
  • Identify specific task failures and their exit codes.
  • Note timestamps to correlate with recent changes.
  • Use search functionality to find keywords like 'error', 'failed', 'permission denied'.
Key Takeaway

Systematic log analysis helps pinpoint the exact point of failure and often reveals the root cause directly.

Pro Tip

Always start by reviewing the logs from the failed step, then expand to preceding steps if the cause isn't immediately clear.
