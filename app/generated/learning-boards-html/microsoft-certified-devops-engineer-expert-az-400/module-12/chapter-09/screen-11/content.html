Learning from Incidents and Continuous Delivery

Establishing feedback loops and adopting small, frequent deployments.

Anti-Pattern: Ignoring Feedback Loops

Failing to learn from incidents, performance data, or user feedback, leading to recurring issues.

  • Problem: Recurring problems, stagnation of improvement, reactive culture.
  • Impact: Reduced reliability, missed opportunities for optimization.
  • Refactor: Establish blameless post-mortems, regular dashboard reviews, integrate insights into Azure Boards.
Anti-Pattern: "Big Bang" Deployments

Deploying large, infrequent changes, increasing risk and making troubleshooting difficult.

  • Problem: High risk of introducing bugs, difficult to diagnose and roll back.
  • Impact: Extended outages, fear of deployment, slow innovation.
  • Refactor: Implement continuous delivery with small, frequent, incremental deployments. Use feature flags.
Key Takeaway

A culture of continuous learning and small, frequent deployments are essential for reducing risk and fostering innovation in DevOps.
