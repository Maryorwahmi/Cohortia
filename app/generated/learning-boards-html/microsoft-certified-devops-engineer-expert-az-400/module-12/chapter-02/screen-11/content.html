Core Concept

The Time-Pressure Equation

The AZ-400 exam requires careful time management to ensure all questions are attempted within the given duration, balancing speed with the complexity of each question.

  • •**Limited Time:** Approx. 120-150 minutes.
  • •**Variable Questions:** 40-60 questions, varying in difficulty.
  • •**Average Pace:** Roughly 2-3 minutes per question, but this is an average.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize an initial scan of the exam to understand question distribution and plan your time allocation.

Time Allocation Strategy

Balancing Question Types

Case Studies: More Time

These complex scenarios require significant reading and analysis, so budget extra time.

Simple Questions: Less Time

Answer knowledge-recall or straightforward questions quickly to 'bank' time for harder ones.

Initial Exam Scan

If allowed, quickly review all questions to gauge overall difficulty and question type distribution.
