Core Strategy

Dynamic Pacing Techniques

Adopt flexible strategies during the exam to ensure you maximize your score by efficiently managing challenging questions and leveraging practice.

  • •**Don't Get Stuck:** Avoid spending too much time on a single difficult question.
  • •**Educated Guess & Mark:** Make a best guess and flag for later review if time permits.
  • •**Practice Tests:** Use them to simulate exam conditions and refine your pacing.
Key Takeaway

Consistent practice under timed conditions is the best way to build confidence and an effective pacing strategy.

Practical Steps

Your Exam Day Flow

1.

Initial Scan & Prioritize

Quickly assess question types and difficulty to plan your attack.

2.

Answer Confident Questions First

Build momentum and 'bank' time by quickly completing questions you know well.

3.

Educated Guess & Mark for Review

For uncertain questions, make a best guess and flag them to revisit if time allows.

4.

Review Marked Questions

If you have extra time, go back to the questions you marked for a second look.
