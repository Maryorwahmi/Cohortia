Core Strategy

Systematic Case Analysis

After the initial read, delve deeper into the case study by actively extracting and organizing critical information to guide your answers.

  • •**Note Key Details:** Jot down requirements, existing systems, and pain points.
  • •**Focus on Non-Functional Requirements:** Prioritize security, scalability, cost, and compliance.
  • •**Avoid External Assumptions:** Stick strictly to the information provided in the case.
Key Takeaway

Every proposed solution must be directly justifiable by the details within the case study.

Practical Steps

Your Case Study Checklist

1.

Read Thoroughly (Again)

Re-read the case, actively highlighting or noting crucial information.

2.

Map Requirements to Solutions

Mentally (or physically) connect each requirement to potential Azure DevOps services/practices.

3.

Validate Against Constraints

Ensure your proposed solutions respect all budget, security, and technical limitations.

4.

Answer with Case Context

Formulate answers that are directly supported by the case study's narrative.
