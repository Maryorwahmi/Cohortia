Core Concept

Navigating Exam Traps

Exam questions are often designed with subtle traps to test your attention to detail and critical evaluation skills. Awareness is your best defense.

  • •**Distractor Answers:** Seem plausible but are subtly incorrect or incomplete.
  • •**Extreme Keywords:** 'Always', 'never', 'all' often signal false statements.
  • •**Negative Phrasing:** Questions like 'Which is NOT a benefit?' require careful reading.
Key Takeaway

Always read the question twice and double-check your answer against all scenario details to ensure it's the 'best' fit.

Pitfalls to Avoid

Common Mistakes & How to Counter Them

Jumping to Conclusions

Counter: Read the entire question and all options before deciding.

Ignoring Context

Counter: Ensure your answer aligns with the specific scenario and its constraints.

Misinterpreting Negatives

Counter: Rephrase negative questions in your mind to understand what is being excluded.
