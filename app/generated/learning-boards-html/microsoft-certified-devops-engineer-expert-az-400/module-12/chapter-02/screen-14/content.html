Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from Exam Strategy

Multiple-Choice Questions

Exam questions requiring selection of one or more correct answers from a given list, often scenario-based, demanding contextual analysis.

Drag-and-Drop Questions

Interactive questions where items are matched, ordered, or categorized by dragging them to target locations, testing workflows.

Case Studies

Detailed business scenarios followed by multiple questions, testing application of knowledge within a specific, constrained context.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering the diverse AZ-400 question types through strategic approaches is as vital as knowing the technical content itself.
