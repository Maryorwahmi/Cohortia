Core Concept

Targeted Interaction Questions

These question types require precise interaction, either by identifying a specific area on a visual or by constructing a ordered list of components/steps.

  • •**Hotspot:** Click on a specific part of an image or diagram.
  • •**Build List:** Select and order multiple items from a list.
Key Takeaway

Both types demand a clear understanding of component functions and logical sequences within Azure architectures.
