Core Concept

The Anatomy of a Case Study

Case studies are multi-faceted problems designed to test your ability to apply Azure DevOps solutions within a realistic business context.

  • •**Company Background:** Provides context on the organization.
  • •**Existing Infrastructure:** Details current technical setup.
  • •**Business Requirements:** High-level goals and needs.
  • •**Technical Requirements:** Specific technology or implementation demands.
  • •**Pain Points:** Current challenges or inefficiencies.
Key Takeaway

Treat case studies as real-world client briefs; a thorough initial read is non-negotiable for success.

Why They're Challenging

The Case Study Demands

Time-Consuming

Requires significant time to read and analyze the detailed scenario.

Context-Dependent Answers

Solutions must strictly adhere to the information provided in the case, not general knowledge.

Risk of External Assumptions

A common trap is to propose solutions that work generally but contradict the case's specific constraints.
