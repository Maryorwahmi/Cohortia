Key Concepts

Reinforcing Your Exam Readiness

Hotspot Questions

Questions requiring selection of a specific area on an image or diagram, testing visual recognition of components.

Build List Questions

Questions requiring selection and ordering of multiple items to form a correct solution, testing sequential logic.

Time Management

The strategic allocation of time during the exam to ensure all questions are attempted and reviewed within the given duration.

Summary Takeaway

Effective time management and an understanding of all question formats are crucial for a confident and successful AZ-400 exam experience.
