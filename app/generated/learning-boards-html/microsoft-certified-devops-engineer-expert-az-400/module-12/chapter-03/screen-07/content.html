Core Principle

Adhering to Least Privilege

Granting Only Necessary Permissions

Definition

Users, programs, or processes should be granted only the minimum level of access necessary to perform their functions.

Reduced Risk

Minimizes the potential damage or impact if an account or system is compromised.

Regular Review

Periodically audit and adjust permissions to ensure they remain appropriate and do not accumulate unnecessary access.

Least Privilege vs. Over-Privilege

Specific, Limited Access

Only what's needed for the job.

Broad, Unnecessary Access

More than required, increasing risk.

Example Scenario

A deployment pipeline needs to create resources in a specific Azure resource group. Its Service Principal should only have 'Contributor' role on that *specific* resource group, not on the entire Azure subscription.
