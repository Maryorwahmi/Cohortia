Granting Key Vault Access

Applying Least Privilege for Pipeline Integration

Identify Service Principal

Retrieve the Object ID of the Service Principal used by your Azure DevOps service connection.

Set Key Vault Policy

Grant the Service Principal specific 'Get' permissions on secrets within your Key Vault.

Enforce Least Privilege

Ensure the Service Principal only has the minimum required access to retrieve secrets, not to modify or delete them.

Azure CLI: Granting Key Vault Access

# Retrieve Service Principal Object ID
# Replace <ServicePrincipalAppName> with your SP name
az ad sp show --display-name "<ServicePrincipalAppName>" --query objectId -o tsv
# Grant 'Get' permission on secrets to the Service Principal
SERVICE_PRINCIPAL_OBJECT_ID="<your_service_principal_object_id>"
az keyvault set-policy --name $KEY_VAULT_NAME --resource-group $RESOURCE_GROUP --secret-permissions get --object-id $SERVICE_PRINCIPAL_OBJECT_ID
Key Takeaway

Granting precise, least-privilege access to your Service Principal for Key Vault ensures that your pipelines can retrieve secrets securely without unnecessary exposure or capabilities.
