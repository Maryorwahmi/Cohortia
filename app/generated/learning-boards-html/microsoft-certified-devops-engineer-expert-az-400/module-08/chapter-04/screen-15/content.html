Best Practices

Common Mistakes and Safety Notes

Avoiding pitfalls and ensuring secure, cost-effective log management.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Improper Retention: Leads to high costs or data loss.
  • Irrelevant Data: Increases costs and slows queries.
  • Poorly Structured Logs: Difficult to query and analyze.
  • Underutilizing KQL: Missing out on advanced analysis.
Key Takeaway

Proactive management of data retention, ingestion, and structuring, combined with robust security practices, is vital for an effective and compliant Log Analytics solution.

Pro-Tip: Start Small, Scale Smart

When configuring logging, start with essential logs and gradually add more as needed. Monitor ingestion volumes and costs regularly. This iterative approach helps you optimize your logging strategy without overspending or collecting unnecessary data.
