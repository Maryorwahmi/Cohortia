Alert Lifecycle

Finalizing Alert Rules: Details and Management

Naming, prioritizing, and controlling the active state of your alerts.

03

Define Alert Details

Provide a clear name and description for the alert, ensuring context for troubleshooting. Assign a severity level to prioritize responses.

Example

Name: "HighCpuAppServiceAlert", Description: "App Service Plan CPU exceeds 80%", Severity: 2 (Warning).

04

Enable/Disable Alert

Control the active state of the alert rule. This is useful for planned maintenance or when an alert is no longer relevant.

Pro-Tip

Always test alert rules in non-production environments before enabling them in production to avoid unintended side effects or alert storms.

Key Takeaway

Clear alert details and thoughtful management of their active state are crucial for preventing alert fatigue and ensuring effective operational responses.
