What is Azure Monitor?

A comprehensive solution for collecting, analyzing, and acting on telemetry from your cloud and on-premises environments.

Unified Data Platform

Ingests various types of telemetry (metrics, logs) into a single platform for consistent querying and analysis.

Key Takeaway

Azure Monitor is essential for maintaining the health, performance, and availability of your Azure solutions by providing critical insights.

Why Azure Monitor Matters for DevOps

  • •Proactive Monitoring: Identify potential issues before they impact users.
  • •Performance Optimization: Pinpoint bottlenecks and areas for improvement.
  • •Troubleshooting: Quickly diagnose root causes of problems with rich telemetry.
  • •Compliance & Auditing: Maintain records of operations and changes for regulatory needs.
  • •Automated Responses: Trigger actions based on alerts to self-heal or scale resources.
Real-world Connection

Imagine a web application experiencing slow response times. Azure Monitor allows you to correlate application performance data with underlying infrastructure metrics to quickly identify if the issue is code-related or due to resource constraints.
