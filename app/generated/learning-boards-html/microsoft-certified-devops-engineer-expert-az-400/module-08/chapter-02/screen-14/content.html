Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • •Incomplete Diagnostic Settings: Missing logs from critical resources.
  • •Logs to Storage Only: Storing logs without a plan for analysis.
  • •Static Monitoring: Not updating configurations as systems evolve.
Key Takeaway

Proactive and well-configured monitoring is crucial. Avoid blind spots and ensure logs are directed to analyzable destinations like Log Analytics Workspaces.

Best Practices for Azure Monitor

Ensure Robust Observability
  • •Centralize Logs: Always send logs to a Log Analytics Workspace.
  • •Automate Configuration: Use IaC (Azure CLI, ARM templates) for diagnostic settings.
  • •Define Actionable Alerts: Create alerts with clear thresholds and response actions.
  • •Regular Review: Periodically audit and update your monitoring setup.
  • •Utilize Workbooks: Create custom dashboards for specific teams or scenarios.
Real-world Impact

A well-implemented Azure Monitor strategy can significantly reduce mean time to resolution (MTTR) for incidents, improve system reliability, and provide valuable insights for capacity planning and optimization.
