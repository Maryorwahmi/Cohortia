Azure Monitor Fundamentals

Gain comprehensive observability across your Azure environment.

Azure Monitor is your central hub for collecting, analyzing, and acting on telemetry from your cloud and on-premises environments. Mastering it is key to maintaining the health, performance, and availability of your deployed solutions.

Key Idea

Azure Monitor provides a single pane of glass for all your telemetry, enabling proactive management.
