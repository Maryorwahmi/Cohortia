Core Concept

The Challenge of Distributed Systems

In microservices, a single request traverses multiple services, databases, and APIs. This complexity makes traditional monitoring insufficient for understanding the full request lifecycle or diagnosing performance issues.

Fragmented Logs: Individual service logs don't show the full request journey.

Hidden Bottlenecks: Latency can occur anywhere in the chain, making diagnosis difficult.

Complex Dependencies: Understanding how services interact becomes opaque.

Key Takeaway

Distributed tracing is essential for gaining end-to-end visibility and diagnosing performance in complex microservices architectures.
