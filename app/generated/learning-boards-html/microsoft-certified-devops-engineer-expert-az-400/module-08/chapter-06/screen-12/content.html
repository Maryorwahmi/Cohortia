AI-Powered Anomaly Detection: Smart Detection

Smart Detection is an AI-powered feature that automatically detects performance anomalies and failures in your application, proactively alerting you to potential problems.

AI-Powered Anomaly Detection

Uses machine learning to understand normal behavior and identify deviations like performance drops or increased error rates.

Proactive Alerts

Automatically notifies you of potential problems, often before they impact users or become critical.

Example: Detecting a Performance Degradation

Smart Detection alerts you that the average response time for your /api/products endpoint has increased by 15% over the last hour, deviating significantly from its baseline. This allows you to investigate immediately.

// Smart Detection Alert (Conceptual)
Anomaly Type: Performance Degradation
Resource: WebApp-Prod
Operation: GET /api/products
Change: Average response time increased by 15%
Baseline: 200ms
Current: 230ms
Key Takeaway

Smart Detection transforms reactive monitoring into proactive problem-solving, leveraging AI to safeguard application performance.
