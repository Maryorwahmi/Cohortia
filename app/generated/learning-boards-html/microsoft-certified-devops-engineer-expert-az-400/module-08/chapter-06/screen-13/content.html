Avoiding Common Tracing Mistakes

Ensure robust and reliable end-to-end visibility by understanding and avoiding common pitfalls in distributed tracing implementation.

Inconsistent Instrumentation

Not all services in the call chain are instrumented, leading to broken and incomplete end-to-end traces.

Broken Correlation

Manual propagation errors or services not passing correlation headers correctly result in fragmented traces.

Ignoring Sampling

Aggressive sampling can lead to missing critical traces, especially for intermittent issues. Configure sampling carefully.

Lack of Context in Custom Telemetry

Custom events or traces without OperationId or other correlation properties are hard to link to transactions.

Key Takeaway

Proactive attention to instrumentation, correlation, sampling, and contextual telemetry is vital for effective distributed tracing.
