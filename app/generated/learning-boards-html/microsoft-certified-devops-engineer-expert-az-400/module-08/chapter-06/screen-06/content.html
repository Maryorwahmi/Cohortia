Pinpointing Issues with End-to-End Transaction Details

The 'Transaction search' and 'End-to-end transaction details' view in Application Insights provide a graphical timeline of a single request's journey, revealing timings and relationships across services.

Pro Tip

Filter 'Transaction search' by 'Failed' requests to quickly isolate and investigate errors in your application.

Key Takeaway

The end-to-end transaction details view is crucial for visual diagnosis of latency and errors within a specific request's flow.
