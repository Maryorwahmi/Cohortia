Advanced Telemetry & Troubleshooting

Key Terms for Deep Observability

Dependencies

Telemetry data tracking calls made by your application to external services (databases, APIs, etc.).

Exceptions

Telemetry data recording unhandled errors or exceptions within your application, including stack traces.

Custom Events

User-defined telemetry for tracking specific business actions or interactions within your application.

Summary Takeaway

Dependencies, Exceptions, and Custom Events provide granular insights into application interactions, errors, and specific business logic, crucial for deep observability.
