The Three Pillars of Observability

Metrics, Logs, and Traces: A comprehensive view

Metrics

Numerical measurements collected over time, tracking trends and anomalies. Answers 'how much' or 'how often'.

Logs

Discrete, timestamped records of events, providing contextual details for debugging. Answers 'what happened' and 'when'.

Traces

End-to-end journey of a single request through distributed services, showing flow and timing. Answers 'where' and 'how long'.

Key Takeaway

Each pillar offers a unique perspective, and their combined use is essential for comprehensive understanding and effective troubleshooting in complex systems.
