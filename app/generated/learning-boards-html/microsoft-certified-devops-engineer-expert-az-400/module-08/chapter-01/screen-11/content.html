Challenges in Comprehensive Cloud Observability

Navigating the complexities of modern cloud environments

Data Volume & Velocity

Managing terabytes of metrics, logs, and traces from distributed systems requires robust ingestion and storage.

Integration Complexity

Integrating observability across diverse services, legacy systems, and third-party components is challenging.

Cost & Alert Fatigue

High data volumes lead to increased costs and irrelevant alerts can overwhelm teams, causing fatigue.

Security & Compliance

Sensitive data in logs/traces must be redacted to comply with privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).

Key Takeaway

Addressing the challenges of data management, integration, cost, and security is crucial for building effective and sustainable observability solutions in the cloud.
