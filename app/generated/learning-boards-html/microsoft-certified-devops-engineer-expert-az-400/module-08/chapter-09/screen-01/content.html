Chapter 8.9

Closing the Loop: Feedback & Incident Management

Transforming operational insights into continuous improvement.

This chapter explores the critical role of feedback loops in DevOps and how to integrate Azure Monitor alerts with Azure DevOps work items to streamline incident management. It covers designing effective incident workflows, leveraging post-mortems for continuous improvement, and fostering a blameless culture of learning from operational incidents.

Key Idea

Feedback loops ensure that issues detected in operations are systematically addressed by development teams, leading to continuous improvement and preventing recurrence.
