The Post-Mortem: Learning from Incidents

Fostering a blameless environment for continuous improvement.

Key Questions in a Post-Mortem

  • What happened and why?
  • What was the impact?
  • What steps were taken to mitigate and resolve?
  • What could have prevented it?
  • What actions can prevent recurrence and improve response?

Blameless Retrospective

Focuses on systemic improvements rather than individual fault, fostering transparency and psychological safety.

Actionable Output

The outcome is a set of concrete improvements, captured as work items in Azure DevOps, ensuring lessons are translated into action.

Key Takeaway

Post-mortems, conducted in a blameless environment, are essential for extracting valuable lessons from incidents and driving systemic improvements through actionable work items.
