Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Feedback Loop

A continuous cycle where operational information is fed back into development to drive improvement.

Incident Management

The process of detecting, responding to, resolving, and learning from service disruptions.

Azure DevOps Work Items

Trackable units of work (e.g., Bugs, Tasks, Incidents) in Azure DevOps Boards.

Azure Logic App

A cloud service that helps automate and orchestrate tasks, business processes, and workflows.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering feedback loops and incident management integration is crucial for building resilient systems and fostering a culture of continuous improvement in DevOps.
