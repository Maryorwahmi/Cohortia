Key Concepts (Cont.)

More essential terms to remember.

Post-mortem (Post-Incident Review)

A structured process of analyzing an incident to understand its causes and prevent recurrence, without assigning blame.

Blameless Retrospective

A principle in post-mortems focusing on systemic improvements rather than individual fault.

Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR)

A key metric measuring the average time to restore a service after an outage.

Summary Takeaway

Embracing blameless post-mortems and tracking MTTR are crucial for continuous learning and improving incident response efficiency within a DevOps framework.
