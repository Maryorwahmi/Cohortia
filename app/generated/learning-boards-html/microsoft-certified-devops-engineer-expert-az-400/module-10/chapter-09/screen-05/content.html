Alert Fatigue: The Silent Threat

Why Quality Trumps Quantity in Alerting

Definition

**Alert fatigue** occurs when teams are overwhelmed by an excessive volume of non-actionable or redundant alerts, leading to desensitization.

Consequence

Critical alerts can be missed or ignored, resulting in delayed incident response, prolonged outages, and significant user impact.

Goal

The objective is to generate high-quality, actionable alerts that provide clear context and demand immediate attention, not just a high volume.

The Cycle of Fatigue

Too many alerts lead to ignored alerts, which leads to missed incidents, and ultimately, a loss of trust in the monitoring system.

Key Takeaway

Combat alert fatigue by focusing on the quality and actionability of alerts, ensuring every notification provides value and demands a clear response.
