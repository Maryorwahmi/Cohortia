Alert on Symptoms, Not Causes

Focusing on User Experience Impact for Actionable Alerts

Symptom-Based Alerts

Trigger alerts when the **user experience is directly impacted**, such as high error rates or slow latency. These indicate a problem for users.

Cause-Based Alerts

Alerting on **internal resource metrics** like high CPU or disk I/O, which might not always indicate a user-facing problem, can lead to noise.

Why it Matters

Focusing on symptoms ensures that alerts are directly tied to service reliability and user impact, making them inherently more actionable and reducing false positives.

Symptom vs. Cause

High Error Rate (Symptom)

High CPU Usage (Cause)

Alerting on high error rates directly indicates user impact, while high CPU might just be normal system behavior.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize alerts that reflect direct user impact over internal resource metrics to ensure your team responds to what truly affects service reliability.
