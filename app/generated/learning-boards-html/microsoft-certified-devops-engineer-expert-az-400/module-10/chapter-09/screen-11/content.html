Building a Resilient On-Call Team

Fairness, Documentation, and a Blameless Culture

Fairness & Predictability

Rotations should be predictable, shared equitably, and allow for adequate rest to prevent burnout.

Runbooks & Documentation

Clear, up-to-date runbooks for common incidents empower quick and consistent responses.

Tooling & Access

Provide necessary access and tools (dashboards, log queries, deployment tools) for effective diagnosis and mitigation.

Pillars of On-Call Excellence

Equitable Rotation

Clear Runbooks

Blameless Culture

Key Takeaway

An effective on-call team is built on fairness, comprehensive documentation, proper tooling, continuous training, and a blameless culture to ensure sustainable incident response.
