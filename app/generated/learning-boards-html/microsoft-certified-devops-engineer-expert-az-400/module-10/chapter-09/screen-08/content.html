Reducing Noise and Ensuring Reliability

Deduplication, Suppression, and Regular Testing of Alerts

Deduplication

Group similar alerts that fire simultaneously into a single incident to prevent overwhelming the on-call team with redundant notifications.

Suppression

Temporarily disable or mute alerts during planned maintenance windows or known outages to avoid false positives and unnecessary paging.

Regular Testing

Routinely test alert rules to ensure they fire correctly, reach the right people, and that associated action groups function as expected.
