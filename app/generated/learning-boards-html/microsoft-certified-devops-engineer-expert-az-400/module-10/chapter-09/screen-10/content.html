Connecting Alerts to On-Call Teams

Leveraging Webhooks for External Incident Management

Webhooks Defined

User-defined HTTP callbacks that send JSON payloads to a specified URL when an event (like an alert) occurs.

On-Call Platforms

Integrate with dedicated systems like PagerDuty or Opsgenie for rotations, escalation policies, and acknowledgments.

Collaboration Tools

Send notifications to Microsoft Teams or Slack channels for team-wide awareness and collaboration during incidents.

Key Takeaway

Webhooks are the backbone of integrating Azure Monitor alerts with external incident management platforms, enabling real-time, actionable notifications.
