The SRE Reliability Triangle

Connecting Detection, Response, and Resolution for System Resilience

SRE Pillars

Alerting

On-Call

Automation

Detection

Robust **alerting strategies** ensure issues are detected quickly and accurately, minimizing false positives.

Response

Effective **on-call rotations** route detected issues to the right people, enabling swift diagnosis and initial mitigation.

Resolution

Leveraging **automation** accelerates incident resolution, reducing manual toil and restoring service efficiently.

Key Takeaway

The integrated approach of alerting, on-call, and automation is fundamental for SRE teams to maintain high reliability and minimize user impact.
