Designing for Action and Clarity

Every Alert Needs an Owner, Context, and Severity

Actionable Alerts

Every alert should have a clear owner and a defined response plan, so recipients know exactly what steps to take.

Severity Levels

Include clear severity levels (e.g., P1, P2, P3) to help prioritize incidents and allocate resources effectively.

Rich Context

Provide sufficient context: service, metric, current value, and links to dashboards or runbooks for rapid diagnosis.

Key Takeaway

An effective alert is not just a notification; it's a call to action, providing all necessary information for a swift and informed response.
