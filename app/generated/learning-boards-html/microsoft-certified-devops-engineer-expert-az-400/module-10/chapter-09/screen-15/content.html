Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Alert Fatigue

A state where individuals become desensitized to alerts due to an excessive volume of non-actionable notifications.

Actionable Alert

An alert that provides clear information, context, and a defined response plan, enabling immediate action.

On-Call Rotation

A scheduled system where engineers are designated to be available to respond to production incidents during specific periods.

Summary Takeaway

Effective SRE relies on designing high-quality, actionable alerts, establishing fair and well-supported on-call rotations, and leveraging automation to ensure rapid and efficient incident response.
