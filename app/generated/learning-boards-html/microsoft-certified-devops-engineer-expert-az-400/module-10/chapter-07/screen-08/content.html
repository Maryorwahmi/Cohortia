Startup Probes: Handling Slow Initializations

Specialized Checks for Long-Starting Applications

  • Used for applications that take a long time to start up.
  • If a startup probe fails, the container is restarted.
  • Once successful, liveness and readiness probes take over.
Key Takeaway

Startup probes prevent premature restarts for slow-starting applications, ensuring they have ample time to initialize before regular health checks begin.

Example: Large Data Load

An analytics application needs to load several gigabytes of data into memory during startup, which can take 2-3 minutes. Without a startup probe, a liveness probe might fail after 30 seconds, restarting the container repeatedly. A startup probe allows the application to complete its data load before regular checks begin.

Misconception: Replacing Liveness/Readiness

Startup probes do not replace liveness or readiness probes. They complement them by handling the initial, often lengthy, boot phase. Once the application is started, liveness and readiness probes resume their normal function.

Pro-Tip

Configure `failureThreshold` and `periodSeconds` for startup probes to provide sufficient time for your application's slowest startup scenarios.
