Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Health Check

An endpoint or mechanism within an application that reports its operational status.

Liveness Probe

Used by orchestrators to determine if a container is running and should be restarted if unhealthy.

Readiness Probe

Used by orchestrators to determine if a container is ready to serve traffic and should be removed from the load balancer if not ready.

Startup Probe

A health check for applications with long startup times, allowing them to initialize before liveness and readiness probes take over.

Summary Takeaway

Health checks and probes are foundational for orchestrators to manage application lifecycles, ensuring containers are healthy, ready, and gracefully started.
