Core Concept

Synthetic Transactions: Simulating User Journeys

Proactive Detection from an External Perspective

  • Simulates real user interactions with your application from various global locations.
  • Runs continuously and predictably, unlike real user monitoring (RUM).
  • Detects performance degradation or outages even during low traffic periods.
Key Takeaway

Synthetic monitoring provides a proactive, external view of application health by simulating user journeys, ensuring issues are detected before real users are impacted.

Comparison: Synthetic vs. RUM

Synthetic Monitoring

  • Proactive
  • Predictable
  • Detects issues 24/7

Real User Monitoring (RUM)

  • Reactive
  • Depends on traffic
  • Real user experience
Why it matters

Synthetic tests provide consistent data, even during off-peak hours, ensuring critical business flows are always functional and performant, regardless of actual user load.
