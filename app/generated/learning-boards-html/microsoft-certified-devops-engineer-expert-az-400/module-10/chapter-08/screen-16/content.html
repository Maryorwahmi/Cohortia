Key Concepts

Essential terms for designing resilient systems

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The process of recovering from a major incident that affects an entire region or data center.

Availability Zone

Physically separate locations within an Azure region with independent power, cooling, and networking.

Chaos Engineering

The practice of intentionally injecting failures into a system to test its resilience and identify weaknesses.

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging Azure's foundational constructs like Availability Zones for Disaster Recovery and practicing Chaos Engineering are crucial for building truly resilient cloud architectures.
