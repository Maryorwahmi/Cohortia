Data Resilience Strategy

Azure Storage Redundancy Options

Ensuring data durability and availability with various replication strategies

Choosing the Right Option

  • LRS: Cost-effective, single datacenter protection.
  • ZRS: Regional protection against zone failures.
  • GRS: Cross-regional disaster recovery.
  • GZRS: Maximum durability, combining ZRS and GRS benefits.
  • Consider: RPO, RTO, and budget requirements.
Key Takeaway

Selecting the appropriate Azure Storage redundancy option is vital for protecting your data against various failure scenarios, from local hardware issues to regional disasters.

Storage Redundancy Levels
LRS (Locally Redundant)

3 copies in one datacenter.

ZRS (Zone-Redundant)

3 copies across AZs in a region.

GRS (Geo-Redundant)

6 copies: 3 primary, 3 paired secondary.

GZRS (Geo-Zone-Redundant)

6 copies: 3 AZs primary, 3 AZs secondary.
