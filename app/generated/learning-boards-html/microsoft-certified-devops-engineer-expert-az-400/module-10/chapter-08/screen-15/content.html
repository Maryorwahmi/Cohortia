Key Concepts

Essential terms for designing resilient systems

Reliability

The probability a system performs its intended function for a specified time under specified conditions.

Resilience

The ability of a system to recover from failures and continue to function.

High Availability (HA)

Designing systems to minimize downtime by providing redundancy and failover capabilities within a single region.

Summary Takeaway

Reliability, resilience, and high availability are foundational for building robust cloud applications that can withstand failures and ensure continuous operation.
