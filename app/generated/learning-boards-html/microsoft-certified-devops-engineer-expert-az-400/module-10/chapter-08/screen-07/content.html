Availability Sets: VM-Level Isolation

Distributing VMs across fault and update domains for high availability

Key Components

  • Fault Domains: Physical racks with independent power and network.
  • Update Domains: Groups of VMs that reboot together during maintenance.
  • VM Distribution: Spreads VMs across these domains to prevent simultaneous outages.
  • Legacy HA: Still relevant for regions without Availability Zones or specific VM scenarios.
Key Takeaway

Availability Sets provide essential high availability for Virtual Machines by isolating them from single points of failure within a datacenter.
