Active-Passive Deployments for Disaster Recovery

Implementing standby instances for failover in case of major outages

Standby Options

  • Hot Standby: Fully running, immediate failover (lowest RTO, highest cost).
  • Warm Standby: Running but scaled down, requires scale-up (balanced RTO/cost).
  • Cold Standby: Deployed but not running, requires startup (highest RTO, lowest cost).
  • Use Case: Common for cross-region disaster recovery scenarios.
Key Takeaway

Active-Passive patterns provide a cost-effective disaster recovery solution, allowing you to balance recovery time objectives with operational costs.
