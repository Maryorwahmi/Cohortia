Paired Regions: Cross-Regional Disaster Recovery

Leveraging geographically separated regions for business continuity

Benefits of Paired Regions

  • Sequential Updates: Minimizes downtime during Azure platform updates.
  • Data Residency: Data often stays within the same geography for compliance.
  • Geo-Redundancy: Facilitates cross-region data replication for disaster recovery.
  • Faster Recovery: Enables quicker failover in case of regional outages.
Key Takeaway

Paired Regions are fundamental for implementing robust disaster recovery strategies, protecting against widespread regional outages and ensuring business continuity.
