Data Resilience Strategy

Database Replication and Backup/Restore

Ensuring continuous data availability and recoverability for databases

Best Practices

  • Automate Replication: Use built-in geo-replication or failover groups.
  • Regular Backups: Schedule frequent backups of all critical databases.
  • Test Restore Procedures: Periodically verify that backups can be successfully restored.
  • Point-in-Time Restore: Understand and utilize capabilities for specific recovery needs.
Key Takeaway

Combining robust database replication with regularly tested backup and restore procedures is paramount for achieving high availability and recoverability for your critical data.

Database Resilience Flow
Primary Database

Secondary Database(s)
