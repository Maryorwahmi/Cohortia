Core Concept

The Pillars of Resilient Design

Anticipating and mitigating failures in cloud applications

Why it Matters

  • Business Continuity: Minimizes downtime and ensures critical operations continue.
  • User Trust: Maintains a positive user experience, even during unforeseen events.
  • Cost Efficiency: Reduces the financial impact of outages and recovery efforts.
  • Competitive Advantage: Differentiates your services through superior availability.
Key Takeaway

Proactive design for reliability and resilience is essential to eliminate single points of failure and ensure graceful recovery in cloud environments.

Core Idea
Anticipate Failures

Identify potential weak points and failure modes.

Withstand Disruptions

Build systems to continue operating despite component failures.

Recover Gracefully

Implement automated mechanisms for quick and smooth recovery.

Maintain Operations

Ensure minimal disruption and continuous service delivery.
