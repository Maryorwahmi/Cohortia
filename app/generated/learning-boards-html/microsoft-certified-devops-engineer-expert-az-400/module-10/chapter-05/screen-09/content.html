Feature Overview

Live Metrics Stream: Real-time Application Health

Get a minute-by-minute view of your application's operational health, crucial for immediate feedback during critical events.

  • •
    Real-time Data: Displays incoming requests, failures, server performance, and dependency calls as they happen.
  • •
    Deployment Validation: Instantly verify the impact of new deployments.
  • •
    Incident Response: Provides immediate insights during an active incident to assess impact and guide troubleshooting.
Use Case

During a new feature rollout, monitor Live Metrics to ensure no immediate spikes in errors or performance degradation occur.

Feature Overview

Performance Blade: Identifying Bottlenecks

Provides an aggregated view of performance metrics to help identify and diagnose slow operations and dependencies.

  • •
    Aggregated Metrics: Shows average request response times, dependency durations, and top slowest operations.
  • •
    Drill-down Capabilities: Allows you to investigate specific slow operations to understand their contributing factors.
  • •
    Historical Analysis: Useful for identifying trends and long-term performance degradations.
Key Takeaway

Live Metrics offers immediate operational awareness, while the Performance Blade provides the aggregated data needed for strategic performance optimization and bottleneck resolution.
