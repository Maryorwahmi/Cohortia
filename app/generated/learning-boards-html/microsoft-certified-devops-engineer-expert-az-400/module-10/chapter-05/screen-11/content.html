Visualizing Architecture

The Power of the Dependency Map

The Dependency Map automatically visualizes your application's components and their interactions, showing performance and health status.

Real-world Impact

Quickly identify if a slow external API or database is causing performance issues in your microservices architecture.

Key Benefits

Why the Dependency Map is Essential

The Dependency Map simplifies understanding complex distributed systems and accelerates troubleshooting.

  • •
    Architectural Overview: Provides an instant visual representation of your application's components and their connections.
  • •
    Performance Hotspot Identification: Highlights components with high latency or error rates.
  • •
    Troubleshooting Aid: Helps pinpoint the exact service or dependency causing an issue in a distributed transaction.
  • •
    Microservices Clarity: Especially useful for understanding complex interactions in microservices architectures.
Key Takeaway

The Dependency Map offers a dynamic, visual understanding of your application's architecture and its real-time health, making it indispensable for managing complex systems.
