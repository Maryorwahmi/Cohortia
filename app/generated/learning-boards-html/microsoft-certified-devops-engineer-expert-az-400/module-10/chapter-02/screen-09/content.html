Designing Service Level Objectives (SLOs)
Setting targets for your SLIs that align with business value
SLO Definition

A target for an SLI, typically expressed as a percentage over a specific measurement window.

Example: Availability SLO

"99.95% of HTTP requests to api.contoso.com must return 2xx/3xx over 30 days."

Avoid 100% Availability

It's practically impossible and prohibitively expensive. Define acceptable unreliability instead.
