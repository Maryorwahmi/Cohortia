Common Pitfalls in Defining SLOs and SLIs
Strategies to ensure your reliability metrics are effective

Too Many SLIs/SLOs

Overwhelm teams, dilute focus, and lead to alert fatigue. Prioritize a few critical, user-centric metrics.

Vanity Metrics

Metrics that look good but don't reflect actual user experience or business impact (e.g., 'number of logins' vs. 'successful login rate').

Ignoring the 'Tail'

Focusing only on average latency and ignoring high-percentile latencies, which affect a significant portion of users.

Unrealistic Targets

Setting 100% availability or extremely low latency targets leads to team burnout and cynicism.

Lack of Ownership/Stakeholder Buy-in

Without clear ownership and collaboration, SLOs become neglected or misaligned with business value.
