Making SLOs SMART and Understanding Error Budgets
Crafting effective objectives for sustainable reliability
SMART Criteria

Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound – essential for effective SLOs.

Error Budget Defined

The allowable amount of unreliability derived from an SLO, balancing reliability work with feature development.

Practical Example

A 99.9% availability SLO grants a 0.1% error budget for a given period.
