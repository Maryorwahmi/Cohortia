Service Level Indicators: Latency
Quantifying the responsiveness of your service
Definition

The time taken for a service to respond to a request, typically measured in milliseconds.

Why Percentiles?

Averages can hide slow responses for a subset of users. P90 or P99 capture the experience of the slowest users.

Azure Example

Track request duration percentiles in Azure Application Insights.

Latency Distribution
P50 Latency (Median)
P90 Latency (Most Users)
P99 Latency (Slowest Users)

Higher percentiles reveal critical performance bottlenecks.
