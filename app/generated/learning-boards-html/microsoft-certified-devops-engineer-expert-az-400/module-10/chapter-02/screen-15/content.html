Key Concepts (Continued)

Further essential takeaways from this chapter

Latency

The time taken for a service to respond to a request, typically measured in milliseconds, often using percentiles (P50, P90, P99).

Throughput

The volume of work a service can process per unit of time (e.g., requests per second), crucial for understanding capacity.

Error Rate

The percentage of requests that result in an error, directly indicating service quality issues and user impact.

Error Budget

The allowable amount of unreliability derived from an SLO, used to balance reliability work with feature development.

Chapter Summary

By effectively measuring performance metrics like latency, throughput, and error rate, and strategically managing your error budget, you can achieve sustainable service reliability.
