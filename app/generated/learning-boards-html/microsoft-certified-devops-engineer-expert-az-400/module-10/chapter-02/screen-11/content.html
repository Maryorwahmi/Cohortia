Ensuring accuracy and relevance in your reliability data
Latency Measurement

Prefer percentiles (P90, P99) over averages to accurately reflect the experience of all users, including the slowest.

Aggregation Periods

Choose based on need: shorter for alerts (e.g., 5 min), longer for trend analysis (e.g., 30 days).

Why it Matters

Accurate measurement ensures your SLOs are meaningful and actionable, driving real improvements.
