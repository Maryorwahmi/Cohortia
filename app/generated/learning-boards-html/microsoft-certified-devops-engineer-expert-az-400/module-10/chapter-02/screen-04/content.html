SLA, SLO, and SLI: Understanding the Hierarchy
Distinguishing agreements, objectives, and indicators in SRE
SLA (Service Level Agreement)

A formal, legally binding contract with customers, specifying service levels and penalties.

SLO (Service Level Objective)

An internal target defining the desired level of service reliability, often more stringent than an SLA.

SLI (Service Level Indicator)

A quantitative measure used to track how well a service is meeting its SLOs, representing raw data points.

Conceptual Flow
