Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A formal, legally binding contract specifying the level of service expected by a customer, often with penalties for non-compliance.

Service Level Objective (SLO)

An internal target for a Service Level Indicator (SLI), defining the desired level of service reliability. More stringent and aspirational than an SLA.

Service Level Indicator (SLI)

A quantitative measure of a service's health, such as availability, latency, throughput, or error rate.

Availability

The proportion of time a service is accessible and operational, often measured as successful requests or uptime percentage.

Chapter Summary

Mastering SLAs, SLOs, and SLIs is fundamental for a data-driven approach to service reliability, ensuring alignment between promises, targets, and actual performance.
