Key Concepts

Understanding these metrics is crucial for data-driven reliability:

Service Level Indicator (SLI)

A quantitative measure of some aspect of the level of service provided, e.g., latency, error rate, availability.

Service Level Objective (SLO)

A target value or range for an SLI, defining the desired level of service reliability that customers can expect.

Error Budget

The allowable amount of unreliability (downtime or errors) a service can incur over a period, derived from the SLO.

Summary Takeaway

SLIs, SLOs, and Error Budgets provide a robust, data-driven framework for defining, measuring, and managing service reliability effectively.
