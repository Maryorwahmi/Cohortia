Key Concepts

Here are the essential terms and ideas from this chapter:

Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)

A discipline applying software engineering principles to operations, focusing on building and running highly reliable systems.

Toil

Manual, repetitive, automatable, tactical work that scales linearly with service growth and provides no enduring value.

Blameless Post-mortem

A retrospective analysis of an incident focusing on systemic failures and learning opportunities, not individual blame.

Summary Takeaway

SRE is an engineering approach to operations that prioritizes automation, learning from failures, and proactive reliability over reactive firefighting.
