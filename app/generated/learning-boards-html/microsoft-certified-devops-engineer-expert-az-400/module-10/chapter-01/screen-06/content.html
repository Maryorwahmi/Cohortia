SRE Principle

Eliminating Toil for Strategic Impact

Automating Repetitive, Manual Tasks

What is Toil?

Toil refers to operational work that is:

  • •Manual: Requires human intervention.
  • •Repetitive: Occurs frequently and predictably.
  • •Automatable: Can be replaced by software.
  • •Tactical: Focuses on immediate problems, not long-term solutions.
  • •No Enduring Value: Doesn't improve the system's core functionality.
  • •Scales Linearly: Workload increases directly with service growth.
Key Takeaway

Minimizing toil frees up engineers for strategic work, improving both system reliability and team morale.

Examples of Toil

Manual Deployments

Clicking through a UI or running scripts by hand for every release.

Routine Alert Triage

Manually investigating every alert, even for known, simple issues.

Capacity Planning Spreadsheets

Manually updating and analyzing spreadsheets for resource allocation.
