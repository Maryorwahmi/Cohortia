Defining Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)

Applying Software Engineering to Operations

What is SRE?

SRE is a discipline that applies software engineering principles and practices to the operational challenges of running large-scale, highly reliable systems. It originated at Google, emphasizing automation, measurement, and systemic improvements.

  • •Treats operations as a software problem, not just manual tasks.
  • •Focuses on building tools and automation to manage systems.
  • •Aims for resilience and scalability through engineering design.
Key Takeaway

SRE transforms traditional operations into an engineering discipline, using code and data to ensure system reliability and efficiency.

The SRE Philosophy

Proactive Reliability

Design systems to prevent failures, rather than just reacting to them.

Automation First

Automate repetitive tasks to reduce manual effort and human error.

Data-Driven Decisions

Use metrics and data to objectively assess service health and guide improvements.
