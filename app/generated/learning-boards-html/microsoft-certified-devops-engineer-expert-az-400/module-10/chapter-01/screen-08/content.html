The Blameless Approach

A blameless post-mortem is a retrospective analysis of an incident that focuses on:

  • •Systemic Failures: Identifying underlying issues in processes, tools, or architecture.
  • •Learning Opportunities: Extracting actionable insights to prevent recurrence.
  • •Psychological Safety: Creating an environment where individuals can share without fear of punishment.
Key Takeaway

Blameless post-mortems are crucial for continuous learning and building more resilient systems by addressing root causes, not just symptoms.

Process of a Blameless Post-Mortem

1. Incident Detection & Response

Document the timeline and initial actions taken during the incident.

2. Data Collection & Analysis

Gather all relevant data (logs, metrics, alerts) to understand the sequence of events.

3. Identify Root Causes & Actions

Determine contributing factors and define concrete, preventative action items.
