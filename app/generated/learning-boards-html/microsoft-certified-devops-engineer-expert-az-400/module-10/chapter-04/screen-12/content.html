Avoiding Alert Fatigue: Smart Alerting Practices

Designing actionable and meaningful alert rules.

Why Alert Fatigue Happens
  • Too many non-actionable alerts.
  • Lack of clear severity levels.
  • Poorly tuned thresholds leading to false positives.
  • Alerts not reaching the right person.
Key Takeaway

Effective alerting focuses on user impact, clear prioritization, accurate thresholds, and proper routing to ensure every alert is meaningful and actionable.

Best Practices for Alerting
Alert on Symptoms, Not Causes

Focus on user-impacting issues (e.g., high latency, error rate).

Define Clear Severity Levels

Prioritize alerts (P1 critical, P2 major) for faster response.

Use Appropriate Thresholds

Tune thresholds to minimize false positives and false negatives.

Integrate with On-Call Rotations

Ensure alerts reach the right person at the right time.

A well-designed alerting strategy is key to sustainable operations.
