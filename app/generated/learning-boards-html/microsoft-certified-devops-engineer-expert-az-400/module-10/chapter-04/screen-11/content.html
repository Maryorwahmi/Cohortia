Action Groups: Orchestrating Notifications and Automation

Connecting alerts to a variety of response mechanisms.

Purpose of Action Groups
  • Centralized Response: Define multiple actions for a single alert.
  • Flexible Notifications: Reach the right people through various channels.
  • Automated Remediation: Trigger workflows to fix issues automatically.
  • Integration: Connect with external systems and tools.
Key Takeaway

Action Groups are essential for automating your incident response, ensuring timely notifications and initiating automated remediation steps when alerts fire.

Common Action Group Actions
Email Notification
SMS Message
Webhook Call
Azure Function/Logic App
Tailor your response to the alert's severity and nature.
