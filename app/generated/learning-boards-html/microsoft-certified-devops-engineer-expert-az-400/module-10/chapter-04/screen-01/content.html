Proactive Monitoring with Azure Monitor

Detect and Resolve Issues Before They Impact Users

This chapter will guide you through leveraging Azure Monitor to establish a robust proactive monitoring strategy, ensuring the reliability and performance of your Azure-based applications and infrastructure. You'll learn to collect telemetry, configure alerts, and visualize operational data effectively.

Key Idea

Azure Monitor is the unified solution for Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), enabling early detection and automated responses to prevent issues before they impact users.
