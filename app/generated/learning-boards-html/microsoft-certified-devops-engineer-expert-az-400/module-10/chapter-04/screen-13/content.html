Visualization & Reporting

Workbooks: Dynamic Operational Dashboards

Combining text, metrics, and logs for rich insights.

Key Features
  • Interactive Canvas: Combine various data sources and elements.
  • Dynamic & Shareable: Filter, drill down, and share reports.
  • Rich Content: Integrate text, metrics, KQL queries, and parameters.
  • Versatile Use Cases: Dashboards, troubleshooting, post-mortems.
Key Takeaway

Azure Monitor Workbooks provide a powerful, flexible way to visualize and interact with your operational data, enabling deeper insights and better decision-making.

Common Workbook Use Cases
Operational Dashboards

Real-time health & performance.

Troubleshooting Guides

Step-by-step diagnostic flows.

Post-mortem Reports

Documenting incident analysis.

SLO/SLI Tracking

Displaying error budget consumption.

Workbooks help you tell a complete story with your data.
