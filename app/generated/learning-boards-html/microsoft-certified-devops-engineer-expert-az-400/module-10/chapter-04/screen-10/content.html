Alert Rules: Detecting Anomalies

Defining conditions to trigger immediate actions.

What Triggers an Alert?
  • Metric Thresholds: e.g., CPU usage, error rates.
  • Log Queries: e.g., specific error patterns in logs.
  • Activity Log Events: e.g., critical resource changes.
  • Smart Detection: Application Insights' AI-driven anomaly detection.
Key Takeaway

Alert rules are your automated sentinels, constantly watching for predefined conditions in your telemetry to signal potential problems early.
