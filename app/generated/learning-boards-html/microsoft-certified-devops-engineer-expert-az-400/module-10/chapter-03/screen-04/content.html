Core Concept

Defining the Error Budget

Your Allowable Unreliability

What is it?

The allowable amount of unreliability a service can incur over a specific period, directly derived from its Service Level Objectives (SLOs).

Purpose

To balance the need for service reliability with the desire to innovate and deploy new features, providing a data-driven basis for prioritization.

Key Takeaway

Error budgets transform abstract reliability goals into concrete, measurable limits for service unreliability.

SLO to Error Budget Flow
SLO: 99.9% Availability
Allowed Unreliability: 0.1%
Error Budget: 43.2 minutes (over 30 days)
Example Calculation

For a 30-day month (43,200 minutes) with a 99.9% availability SLO, the 0.1% error budget translates to 43.2 minutes of allowed downtime.
