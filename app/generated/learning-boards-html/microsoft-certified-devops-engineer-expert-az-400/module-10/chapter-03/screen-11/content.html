Learning Culture

The Power of Blameless Post-Mortems

Learning from Failure, Not Assigning Blame

Blameless Post-Mortem Focus
Key Principles
  • Focus on systemic issues, not individual blame.
  • Foster psychological safety for open sharing.
  • Identify process gaps and areas for improvement.
Document Contents
  • Incident summary & timeline.
  • Root causes (technical, process, human).
  • Lessons learned & action items.
Key Takeaway

Blameless post-mortems are crucial for deep learning and preventing recurrence by focusing on systemic improvements.
