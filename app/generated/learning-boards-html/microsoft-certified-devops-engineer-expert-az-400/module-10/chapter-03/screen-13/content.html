Chapter Review

Essential SRE Terminology

Reinforcing Core Ideas

Error Budget

The allowable amount of unreliability a service can incur, derived from its SLOs, balancing innovation with stability.

Incident Management

The structured process of detecting, responding to, and resolving service disruptions to restore normal operations quickly.

Blameless Post-mortem

A retrospective analysis of an incident focused on identifying systemic causes and learning opportunities, not individual fault.

Chapter Summary

Error budgets provide a data-driven approach to reliability, while structured incident management and blameless post-mortems ensure rapid recovery and continuous learning from service disruptions.
