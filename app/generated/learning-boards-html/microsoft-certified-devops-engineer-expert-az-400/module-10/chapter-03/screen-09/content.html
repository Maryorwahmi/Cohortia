Understanding Incident Management

Structured Response to Service Disruptions

What is it?

The structured process for detecting, responding to, and resolving service disruptions to restore normal operations.

Primary Goal

To minimize the impact of service disruptions on users and business by ensuring rapid detection and resolution.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined incident management process is crucial for transforming chaotic outages into controlled, efficient recoveries.

Incident Lifecycle
Detection
Response
Resolution
Scenario

A sudden spike in 5xx errors on your API triggers an alert. Incident management guides your team through diagnosing and fixing the issue.
