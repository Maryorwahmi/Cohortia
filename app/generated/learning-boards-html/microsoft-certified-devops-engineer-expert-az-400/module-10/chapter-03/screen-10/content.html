Key Stages of Incident Response

From Detection to Post-Mortem

01

Detection

Proactive monitoring identifies issues quickly (e.g., Azure Monitor alerts).

02

Triage & Alerting

Routing alerts to the right team, assessing severity (e.g., Azure Action Groups).

03

Response & Mitigation

Investigating, diagnosing, and implementing immediate fixes or workarounds.

04

Resolution

Service restored, incident officially closed.

05

Post-mortem

Critical step for learning and preventing recurrence.

Communication is Key

Throughout the response, keeping stakeholders informed is crucial. Establish clear communication channels and cadences.

Common Pitfall

Skipping the post-mortem phase leads to repeated issues and a failure to learn from past incidents.

Key Takeaway

Each stage of incident management is vital for a comprehensive response, from rapid detection to long-term prevention.
