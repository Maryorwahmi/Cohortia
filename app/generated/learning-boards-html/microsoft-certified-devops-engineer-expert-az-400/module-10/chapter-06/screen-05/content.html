What is Distributed Tracing?

Tracking a single request across an entire distributed system.

Distributed Tracing

A technique to monitor and observe requests as they flow through multiple services in a distributed system, providing an end-to-end view of their execution path and performance.

Trace and Span Relationship
Full Request (Trace)
Span 1: Auth Service
Span 2: Product DB
Span 3: Order Service
Span 4: Payment Gateway
Key Takeaway

Distributed tracing provides an end-to-end view of requests, with traces composed of individual spans representing operations.
