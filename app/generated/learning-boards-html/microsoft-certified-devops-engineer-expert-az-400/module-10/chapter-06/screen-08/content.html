Core Concept

The Need for Unified Logs

Why centralized log management is crucial for observability.

Decentralized Logging
  • •Scattered logs across many services.
  • •Difficult to correlate events.
  • •Manual sifting is time-consuming.
  • •Inconsistent formats and retention.
Centralized Logging
  • •All logs in a single repository.
  • •Easy cross-service correlation.
  • •Efficient querying and analysis.
  • •Unified retention and management.
Key Takeaway

Centralized log management is essential for overcoming the challenges of scattered logs in distributed systems, enabling unified analysis.
