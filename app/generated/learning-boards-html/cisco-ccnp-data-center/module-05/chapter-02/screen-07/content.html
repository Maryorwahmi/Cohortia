VXLAN Tunnel Endpoint (VTEP)

The Gateway for VXLAN Tunnels

Encapsulation & Decapsulation

VTEPs are responsible for adding (encapsulating) and removing (decapsulating) the VXLAN, UDP, and outer IP headers.

Underlay IP Address

Each VTEP has a unique IP address in the underlay network, typically a loopback interface, used for tunnel endpoints.

Tunnel Endpoint

VTEPs act as the source and destination for VXLAN encapsulated traffic, forming the 'tunnel' over the IP underlay.

Key Takeaway

VTEPs are the active network devices that enable VXLAN tunnels, handling the critical encapsulation and decapsulation of Layer 2 traffic over the Layer 3 underlay.
