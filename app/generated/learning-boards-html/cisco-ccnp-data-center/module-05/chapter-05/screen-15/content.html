Key Concepts

Essential terms for network virtualization (Part 2)

vPC Peer-Link

A dedicated Port Channel between the two vPC peer switches that carries control and data traffic for vPC-managed interfaces.

vPC Peer-Keepalive Link

A Layer 3 link between vPC peer switches used to send heartbeat messages to monitor the health of the vPC peers.

Active-Active Redundancy

A state where both redundant paths or devices are actively forwarding traffic, maximizing resource utilization and providing fast failover.

Split-Brain Scenario

A dangerous condition in vPC where both peer switches believe they are the primary, leading to network instability and potential loops.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding vPC's peer-link, keepalive, active-active redundancy, and the risks of split-brain scenarios is crucial for designing and maintaining highly available data center networks.
