The vPC Domain: Peers and Links

Understanding the core components of a vPC deployment.

vPC Peer Switches

Two Cisco Nexus switches that form the vPC domain and act as a single logical device.

vPC Peer-Link

A dedicated Port Channel between peers carrying control and data traffic for vPC-managed interfaces.

vPC Peer-Keepalive Link

A Layer 3 link for heartbeat messages, preventing split-brain scenarios during peer-link failure.

Key Takeaway

The vPC domain relies on two peer switches, a dedicated peer-link for traffic and control, and a peer-keepalive link for health monitoring to ensure stability.
