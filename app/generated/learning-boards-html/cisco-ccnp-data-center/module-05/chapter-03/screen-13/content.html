Troubleshooting Common BGP EVPN and Layer 3 VXLAN Issues

Identifying and resolving common misconfigurations and operational problems.

Common Mistakes & Solutions

  • ●

    BGP Peering: Incorrect `update-source` or AS mismatch. Verify `show bgp summary`.

  • ●

    EVPN Address Family: Missing `address-family l2vpn evpn` activation. Check BGP config.

  • ●

    NVE Ingress Replication: Misconfigured `ingress-replication protocol bgp`. Verify NVE interface.

  • ●

    IRB SVI: Incorrect IP, missing `anycast-gateway`, or VRF mismatch. Check SVI config.

Key Takeaway

Thorough verification of BGP peerings, EVPN address family, NVE settings, and SVI configurations is vital to prevent and resolve common BGP EVPN and Layer 3 VXLAN issues.

Essential Verification Commands

`show bgp l2vpn evpn summary`Verify BGP EVPN peering status.
`show bgp l2vpn evpn route-type 2`Inspect learned MAC/IP routes.
`show nve peers` / `show nve vni`Check VXLAN tunnel and VNI status.
Safety Note: Incorrect EVPN configuration can lead to widespread network outages. Always plan and deploy in phases.
