The Scalability Challenge of Multicast VXLAN

Understanding why traditional VXLAN falls short in large data centers.

Key Problems with Multicast

  • ●

    Excessive Flooding: Relies on flooding for unknown unicast, broadcast, and multicast (BUM) traffic, leading to network inefficiency.

  • ●

    Increased Underlay State: Each VTEP must join a multicast group per VNI, burdening underlay routers with extensive state information.

  • ●

    Inefficient Traffic Distribution: BUM traffic can traverse unnecessary network segments, impacting performance and potentially causing congestion.

Key Takeaway

Multicast VXLAN's reliance on flooding and multicast group management creates significant scalability and efficiency issues in large-scale data center environments.
