Core Concept

The Limitations of Traditional Data Center Networks

Why Spanning Tree Protocol and static routing fall short in modern environments.

Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) Constraints

  • •Limits active forwarding paths, leading to underutilized bandwidth.
  • •Slower convergence times during network topology changes.
  • •Not designed for large-scale, dynamic data center environments.
Problem Highlight

STP's loop prevention mechanism inherently sacrifices network efficiency by blocking redundant paths.

Layer 2 & Layer 3 Scalability Issues

  • •Managing large Layer 2 domains becomes complex and error-prone.
  • •Large broadcast domains can cause performance degradation and security risks.
  • •Extending Layer 2 across dispersed data centers is challenging with traditional methods.
Impact Scenario

A single broadcast storm in a large Layer 2 domain can cripple an entire data center's performance.

Key Takeaway

Traditional network designs, particularly with STP and large Layer 2 domains, struggle to meet the scalability, agility, and multi-tenancy requirements of modern data centers.
