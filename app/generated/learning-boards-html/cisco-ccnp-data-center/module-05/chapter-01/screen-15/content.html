Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Decapsulation

The process of removing the outer (underlay) header from an encapsulated packet at the destination, revealing the original overlay packet.

Workload Mobility

The ability to move virtual machines or containers between different physical servers or data centers without changing their IP address or network configuration.

Multi-tenancy

The ability of a single physical infrastructure to host multiple isolated virtual networks, each belonging to a different customer or department.

Summary Takeaway

Overlay networks enable advanced capabilities like seamless workload mobility, secure multi-tenancy, and programmatic control through SDN, transforming data center operations.
