The Foundation: Importance of a Well-Designed Underlay

Avoiding common pitfalls for overlay network success and stability.

Common Mistakes

  • •Underestimating the importance of a robust underlay.
  • •Failing to properly design IP routing within the underlay.
  • •Assuming the overlay will compensate for underlay deficiencies.
Warning

A weak underlay will directly compromise the performance and reliability of your overlay network.

Robust Underlay Requirements

  • •**Stability:** A highly stable and resilient physical network.
  • •**Bandwidth:** Sufficient capacity to handle all overlay traffic.
  • •**Routing:** Efficient routing protocols (OSPF, EIGRP) for full IP reachability.
Pro Tip

Prioritize a well-designed underlay as the foundational step for any successful overlay deployment.

Key Takeaway

The underlay network is the critical foundation for any overlay; its stability, bandwidth, and efficient routing directly determine the success and performance of the virtual networks built upon it.
