OTV vs. VXLAN for DCI

Choosing the Right Layer 2 Extension Technology

Cisco OTV

  • Designed specifically for DCI.
  • Built-in loop prevention (MAC routing, Site ID).
  • Control Plane: ISIS (with Adjacency Server) or Multicast.
  • Simpler for pure Layer 2 extension across sites.
  • Ideal for dedicated DCI scenarios with minimal complexity.

VXLAN (with EVPN)

  • Flexible: intra-DC and inter-DC overlays.
  • Supports Layer 2 and Layer 3 VPNs.
  • Control Plane: BGP EVPN.
  • More complex for DCI, requires full EVPN deployment.
  • Chosen for unified fabric across entire network.
Key Takeaway

OTV excels in straightforward Layer 2 DCI with robust loop prevention, while VXLAN with EVPN offers greater flexibility for unified Layer 2/3 fabrics across diverse network segments.
