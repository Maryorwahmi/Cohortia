Fabric Interconnects: The Heart of UCS

Centralizing Management and Connectivity

Unified Connectivity

Provides both network (Ethernet) and storage (Fibre Channel/FCoE) access.

Central Management

Runs UCS Manager software, offering a single point of control for all UCS components.

High Availability

Typically deployed in an active-active pair to ensure redundancy and continuous operation.

Role in UCS Domain

Fabric Interconnect Functions

Unified I/O: Consolidates network and storage traffic.

Management Hub: Runs UCS Manager for configuration.

Domain Control: Central point for all UCS component communication.

Key Takeaway

Fabric Interconnects are indispensable, providing unified I/O and centralized management for the entire UCS domain.
