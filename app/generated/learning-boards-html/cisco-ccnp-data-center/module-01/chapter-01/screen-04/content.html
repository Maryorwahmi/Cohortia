DATA CENTER EVOLUTION

The Traditional Data Center Model

Understanding the 'one application, one server' era and its limitations.

Dedicated Hardware

Each application ran on its own physical server, leading to resource isolation but also inefficiency.

Underutilization

Servers often operated at low capacity, wasting power and computing resources.

Slow Deployment

Provisioning new applications required physical hardware procurement and setup, taking weeks or months.

Common Mistake

Underestimating the total cost of ownership (TCO) due to high operational expenses for power, cooling, and manual management.

Key Takeaway

The traditional model, while simple, was inherently inefficient and lacked the agility required for modern digital services.
