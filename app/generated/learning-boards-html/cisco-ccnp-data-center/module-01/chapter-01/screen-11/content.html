DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS

Scalability: Growing with Demand

Expanding resources efficiently to meet evolving workload requirements.

Vertical Scaling (Scale Up)

Increasing resources (CPU, RAM) of an existing single server. Limited by hardware maximums.

Horizontal Scaling (Scale Out)

Adding more instances of servers or components. More flexible and resilient for large-scale growth.

  • Ensures infrastructure can handle increased workload.
  • Prevents performance bottlenecks as demand grows.
  • Often involves modular designs for easy expansion.
Pro-Tip

Design applications to be 'stateless' where possible, making them easier to scale horizontally by simply adding more instances without complex data synchronization.

Concrete Example

During a holiday shopping season, an e-commerce platform automatically scales out by adding dozens of new web server instances to handle the surge in customer traffic, then scales back down afterward.

Key Takeaway

Scalability is crucial for future-proofing data centers, allowing them to efficiently adapt to evolving business needs and workload demands.
