Key Concepts: Design and Components

Core pillars and critical design principles for data centers.

Network Virtualization

Decoupling network services from underlying hardware, enabling software-defined network segmentation and policy enforcement.

High Availability (HA)

A design principle ensuring continuous operation of services through redundancy and failover mechanisms.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

A set of policies and procedures to enable the recovery or continuation of vital technology infrastructure and systems following a disaster.

Summary Takeaway

Effective data center design integrates virtualization, high availability, and disaster recovery to create resilient, scalable, and secure infrastructures capable of meeting modern business demands.
