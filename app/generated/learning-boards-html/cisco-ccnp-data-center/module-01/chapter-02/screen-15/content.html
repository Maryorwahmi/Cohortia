Key Concepts

Advanced Technologies for Data Center Resilience

Equal-Cost Multi-Path (ECMP)

Routing strategy that distributes traffic across multiple equal-cost paths simultaneously for bandwidth and redundancy.

Cisco Nexus Switches

High-performance, high-density switches designed for data center environments, supporting advanced features for fabric implementation.

Virtual Port Channel (vPC)

Cisco Nexus feature allowing two switches to act as one logical switch for active-active link aggregation and redundancy.

Summary: ECMP, Cisco Nexus switches, and vPC are critical technologies that collectively enable the high performance, scalability, and active-active redundancy required for modern data center network infrastructures.
