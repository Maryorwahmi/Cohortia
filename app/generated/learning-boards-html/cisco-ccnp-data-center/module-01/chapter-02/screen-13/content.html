Traffic Flow Analysis

East-West Traffic Flow in Spine-and-Leaf

Optimizing Server-to-Server Communication with ECMP

Server A to Leaf 1

Traffic originates from Server A, connected to Leaf Switch 1.

Leaf 1 to Any Spine (ECMP)

Leaf Switch 1 uses ECMP to forward traffic to any available Spine switch.

Spine to Leaf 2

The chosen Spine switch forwards the traffic down to Leaf Switch 2.

Leaf 2 to Server B

Leaf Switch 2 delivers the traffic to the destination, Server B.

Key Takeaway

The two-hop path with ECMP in Spine-and-Leaf ensures efficient, low-latency East-West traffic, critical for modern distributed applications.
