Limitations of Three-Tier Architectures

Why Traditional Designs Struggle in Modern Data Centers

STP Blocks Redundant Links

Spanning Tree Protocol prevents loops by blocking active paths, leading to underutilized bandwidth and inefficient resource use.

Inefficient East-West Traffic

Traditional designs are optimized for North-South traffic, struggling with the high volume of server-to-server communication within the data center.

Scalability Challenges

Adding capacity often requires significant redesigns, making it difficult to scale efficiently with growing data center demands.

Key Takeaway

STP's link blocking and poor East-West traffic handling made three-tier designs unsuitable for modern, virtualized data centers.
