Spine and Leaf Switches

Roles and Interconnections in the Fabric

Leaf Switches (Access Layer)

Connect directly to servers, storage, and end devices. Provide network access for compute resources.

Spine Switches (Backbone Layer)

Interconnect all Leaf switches. Act as a high-capacity, non-blocking core for the fabric.

Two-Hop Rule

Any Leaf switch can reach any other Leaf switch in just two hops (Leaf -> Spine -> Leaf), ensuring predictable latency.

Key Takeaway

Spine and Leaf switches have distinct roles that together create a highly efficient and scalable data center network fabric.
