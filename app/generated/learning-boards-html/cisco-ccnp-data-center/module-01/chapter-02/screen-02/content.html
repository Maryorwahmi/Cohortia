Articulate the core concepts of the Spine-and-Leaf (Clos) architecture. This understanding is fundamental to designing scalable and high-performance data center networks.

Describe Spine-and-Leaf Benefits

Detail the advantages of Spine-and-Leaf, including improved scalability, predictable latency, and enhanced East-West traffic flow. This highlights its value in modern data centers.
