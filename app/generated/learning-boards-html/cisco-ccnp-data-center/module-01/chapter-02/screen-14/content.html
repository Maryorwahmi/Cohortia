Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways for Data Center Networks

Three-Tier Architecture

Traditional hierarchical design (Core, Aggregation, Access) limited by STP and inefficient East-West traffic.

Spine-and-Leaf Architecture

Modern, flattened two-tier design (Clos network) for high-bandwidth, low-latency East-West traffic.

Leaf & Spine Switches

Leaf switches connect servers (access); Spine switches interconnect Leaf switches (backbone).

Summary: The shift from traditional three-tier to Spine-and-Leaf architectures, with their dedicated Leaf and Spine roles, is driven by the need for scalable, high-performance networks optimized for East-West traffic in modern data centers.
