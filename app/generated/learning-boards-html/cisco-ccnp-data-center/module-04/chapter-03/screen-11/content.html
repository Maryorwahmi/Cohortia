Creating a Service Profile: A Step-by-Step Guide

Navigating UCS Manager to define and configure a new Service Profile.

Key Steps in UCS Manager

  • Navigate to `Servers` > `Service Profiles`.
  • Select `Create Service Profile` to launch the wizard.
  • Specify a unique name for the Service Profile.
  • Select UUID, MAC, and WWN pools for identity.
  • Attach pre-configured policies (Boot, Firmware, Local Disk, etc.).
  • Configure vNICs and vHBAs as needed.
Pro Tip

Always ensure your policies and pools are pre-configured and well-defined before starting Service Profile creation to streamline the process.

Key Takeaway

The UCS Manager wizard simplifies Service Profile creation by guiding you through selecting identities and attaching pre-defined policies.
