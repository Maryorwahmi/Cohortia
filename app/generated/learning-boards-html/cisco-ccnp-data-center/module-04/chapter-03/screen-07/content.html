Identity Management

Unique Identifiers: UUID, MAC, and WWN Pools

Ensuring distinct identities for servers and their network/storage interfaces.

Key Identity Pools

  • **UUID Suffix Pool:** Provides unique server identifiers.
  • **MAC Address Pool:** Assigns unique MACs to vNICs for network.
  • **WWN Pool:** Assigns unique WWNs to vHBAs for Fibre Channel/FCoE storage.
  • Ensures global uniqueness and prevents conflicts.
  • Automates identity assignment, reducing manual errors.
Why it Matters

Unique identifiers are critical for network communication, storage access, and proper server identification within the data center and beyond.

Key Takeaway

Identity pools in UCS automate the assignment of unique UUIDs, MACs, and WWNs, ensuring conflict-free and consistent server identities.
