Core Concept

Blueprints for Automation

Service Profile Templates are master blueprints for creating multiple Service Profiles, ensuring consistency and accelerating deployment across your server fleet.

Single Source of Truth

Define common settings once and reuse them for many server deployments.

Ensured Consistency

All instantiated Service Profiles inherit identical configurations, preventing drift.

Accelerated Deployment

Significantly reduce the time and effort required to provision new servers.
