Updating Templates: Centralized Management

Updating Templates maintain a live link to instantiated Service Profiles, automatically propagating changes to ensure fleet-wide consistency.

How it Works

  • 1
    Create from Template: Service Profiles are created using the Updating Template.
  • 2
    Live Link Maintained: A continuous link is maintained between the template and its profiles.
  • 3
    Automatic Propagation: Changes to the Updating Template are automatically pushed to all associated Service Profiles.

Use Case: Fleet-Wide Consistency

Essential for environments where consistency and rapid, fleet-wide changes are critical, such as large-scale virtualization clusters or cloud deployments, ensuring compliance and reducing drift.

Key Takeaway

Updating Templates are crucial for centralized management, automatically applying changes to all associated Service Profiles to maintain fleet-wide consistency and compliance.
