Key Components of a Service Profile

A Service Profile is a collection of modular policies and identity pools, designed for reusability and standardization across your UCS domain.

Identity Pools

Collections of unique identifiers that Service Profiles draw from to ensure uniqueness for each server.

  • UUID Suffix Pools
  • MAC Address Pools (for vNICs)
  • WWN Pools (for vHBAs)

Configuration Policies

Reusable settings that define various operational aspects of the server, promoting standardization.

  • Boot Policies
  • Firmware Policies
  • Maintenance Policies
  • Quality of Service (QoS) Policies
Key Takeaway

Service Profiles are built from modular identity pools and configuration policies, allowing for flexible, standardized, and error-reduced server deployments.
