B-Series Blade Servers

High-Density Compute within the Chassis

Definition

What are B-Series Blades?

Cisco UCS B-Series Blade Servers are modular, high-density compute units designed to fit into the UCS 5108 chassis, sharing power, cooling, and I/O resources.

Key Characteristics
  • Modular: Easily added or removed from the chassis.
  • High Density: Maximizes compute power per rack unit.
  • Shared Infrastructure: Leverages chassis resources for efficiency.
Ideal Use Cases

B-Series blades excel in virtualized environments, large-scale database deployments, and general-purpose compute farms where space and power efficiency are critical.

Key Takeaway

B-Series blades are the core compute elements within the UCS chassis, designed for efficiency and scalability in dense environments.

Pro-Tip

Remember that B-Series blades rely heavily on the chassis and Fabric Interconnects for their network and storage connectivity, unlike standalone servers.
