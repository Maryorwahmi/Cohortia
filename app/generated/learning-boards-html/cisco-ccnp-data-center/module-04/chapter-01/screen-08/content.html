C-Series Rack Servers

Standalone Servers Managed by UCS Manager

Definition

What are C-Series Servers?

Cisco UCS C-Series Rack Servers are traditional, standalone rack-mountable servers that can be integrated into a UCS domain and managed by UCS Manager.

Key Characteristics
  • Standalone: Independent power, cooling, and local storage.
  • Rack-Mountable: Fits into standard data center racks.
  • UCS Manager Integration: Benefits from centralized management.
B-Series vs. C-Series

B-Series: High-density, modular, shared resources within a chassis.

C-Series: Flexible, standalone, often with more local storage, managed by UCS Manager.

Key Takeaway

C-Series rack servers provide flexibility for specific workloads while maintaining unified management under UCS Manager.

Use Case Example

A C-Series server might be chosen for a large-scale data analytics application requiring extensive local NVMe storage, where a blade server's internal storage capacity might be limited.
