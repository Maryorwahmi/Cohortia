Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Cisco UCS

An integrated data center platform unifying compute, networking, and storage access.

Fabric Interconnects (FIs)

Central management and data plane components, providing converged network and storage access.

UCS 5108 Chassis

A modular enclosure housing UCS B-Series blade servers and Fabric Extenders.

B-Series Blade Servers

High-density, modular servers designed to fit within a UCS chassis.

C-Series Rack Servers

Standalone rack-mountable servers that can be managed by UCS Manager.

Summary Takeaway

Cisco UCS integrates diverse components into a unified system, with Fabric Interconnects as the core, supporting both blade and rack server form factors.
