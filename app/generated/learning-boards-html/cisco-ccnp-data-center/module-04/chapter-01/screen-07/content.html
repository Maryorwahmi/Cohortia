Fabric Extenders (FEXs) / I/O Modules (IOMs)

Extending the Unified Fabric to Blade Servers

Function

Intelligent Passthrough

FEXs are not switches; they are intelligent devices that extend the unified fabric from the Fabric Interconnects directly to the blade servers within the chassis.

Role in Connectivity
  • Data Plane Extension: Provides I/O connectivity for blade servers.
  • Simplified Cabling: Reduces the number of cables needed within the chassis.
  • Unified Fabric Integration: Ensures consistent connectivity for all blades.
Analogy

Think of a FEX as a high-speed, intelligent extension cord that brings the full capabilities of the Fabric Interconnects right to the doorstep of each blade server.

Key Takeaway

FEXs are crucial for providing efficient, high-speed data plane connectivity between blade servers and the Fabric Interconnects.

Common Mistake

Do not confuse FEXs with traditional network switches; they do not perform independent switching decisions but rather extend the fabric.
