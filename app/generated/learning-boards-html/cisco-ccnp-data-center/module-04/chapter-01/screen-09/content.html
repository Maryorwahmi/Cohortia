Cisco UCS Fabric Interconnects (FIs)

Converging Network, Storage, and Management

Definition

What are Fabric Interconnects?

Fabric Interconnects are the central management and data plane components of a UCS domain, providing converged network (Ethernet) and storage (Fibre Channel/FCoE) access.

Key Functions
  • Unified Fabric: Converges Ethernet and Fibre Channel traffic.
  • Central Management: Hosts UCS Manager software.
  • Data Forwarding: Handles all data plane operations.
  • Control Plane: Manages overall UCS environment orchestration.
Cabling Reduction

By converging traffic, FIs drastically reduce the number of physical cables needed for server connectivity, simplifying infrastructure and reducing potential points of failure.

Key Takeaway

Fabric Interconnects are the heart of UCS, providing converged connectivity and hosting the management plane for the entire domain.

Misconception Alert

Never view FIs as just network switches; their role extends to storage, management, and overall orchestration of the UCS domain.
