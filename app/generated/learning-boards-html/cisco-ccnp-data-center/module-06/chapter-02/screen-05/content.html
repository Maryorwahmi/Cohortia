Introducing Cisco Firepower Threat Defense (FTD)

Unifying Firewall and Advanced Threat Protection

What is Cisco FTD?

Cisco Firepower Threat Defense (FTD) is Cisco's unified software image that combines the capabilities of the ASA firewall with the advanced threat protection features of Firepower.

Traditional Firewall Functions

  • Stateful Packet Inspection: Tracks active connections.
  • NAT (Network Address Translation): For IP address conservation.
  • VPN (Virtual Private Network): Secure remote access.
Key Takeaway

Cisco FTD unifies traditional firewall capabilities with advanced threat protection, providing a single, comprehensive security platform for data centers.

Advanced Threat Protection Features

  • IPS (Intrusion Prevention System): Detects and blocks malicious activity.
  • AVC (Application Visibility and Control): Granular application-level policy enforcement.
  • URL Filtering: Blocks access to dangerous websites.
  • AMP (Advanced Malware Protection): Detects and stops sophisticated malware.

Why it Matters

FTD simplifies security deployment and management by offering a single platform for both foundational firewall functions and advanced threat detection, crucial for defending against diverse cyber threats.

Deployment Context

FTD appliances are deployed at the data center edge, between security zones, or in front of critical application tiers to inspect and enforce policies.
