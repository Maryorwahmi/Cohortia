Seamless Integration: Firepower and ACI Service Graphs

Steering Traffic for Advanced Threat Inspection

Unified Security Posture

Cisco Firepower and ACI integrate to create a comprehensive, end-to-end security solution, combining micro-segmentation with deep threat analysis.

Service Graphs in ACI

  • ACI construct to insert network services (like Firepower NGFW) into the data path.
  • Traffic matching a contract with a service graph is automatically steered.
Key Takeaway

ACI Service Graphs enable dynamic traffic steering to Firepower, combining ACI's micro-segmentation with Firepower's advanced threat protection for unparalleled unified security.

Traffic Steering & Inspection

  • ACI steers traffic to Firepower for deep packet inspection, IPS, URL filtering, and AMP.
  • Firepower inspects, then returns traffic to ACI for delivery.
  • Combines ACI's east-west micro-segmentation with Firepower's north-south/high-risk flow protection.

Example: PCI Compliance

All traffic for a 'PCI Compliance EPG' can be mandatorily steered through a Firepower FTD device for rigorous IPS and malware scanning, even for internal flows.

Benefits of Integration

Simplifies security service deployment and management, offering unparalleled protection by leveraging the strengths of both ACI and Firepower.
