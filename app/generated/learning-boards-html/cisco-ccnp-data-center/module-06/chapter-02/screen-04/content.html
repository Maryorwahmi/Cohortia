Evolving Data Center Security

Beyond Perimeter Firewalls to a Multi-Layered Defense

Traditional Perimeter Security

  • Relied on strong firewall at the edge.
  • 'Hard shell, soft interior' approach.
  • Assumed internal network was trustworthy.
  • Vulnerable to internal lateral movement.

Modern Data Center Challenges

  • Rise of virtualized environments and cloud-native apps.
  • Sophisticated, targeted threats.
  • Lateral movement within the data center.
  • Traditional models are insufficient.
Key Takeaway

Data center security must evolve from perimeter-focused to a multi-layered, granular approach, protecting assets both at the edge and within the fabric.

Multi-Layered, Granular Approach

  • Protects assets at the edge and within the fabric.
  • Security controls closer to workloads.
  • Fine-grained control and continuous threat detection.
  • Cisco Firepower and ACI provide this solution.

Common Misconception

Relying solely on perimeter firewalls is insufficient for modern data centers. Internal threats and lateral movement require deeper, workload-level security.

Real-World Connection

A breach in a traditional data center can quickly spread. Granular security prevents lateral movement, containing threats to a single compromised workload.
