Centralized Management

Cisco Data Center Network Manager (DCNM)

DCNM is a powerful, centralized management platform specifically designed for Cisco Nexus-based data centers. It offers comprehensive visibility, automation, and operational insights.

  • •Single Pane of Glass: Unified view of the entire fabric.
  • •Telemetry Data Collection: Gathers rich data from Nexus switches.
  • •Real-time Alerts: Notifies for critical events and deviations.
Pro-Tip

Don't underutilize DCNM; leverage its dashboards and logs for continuous, proactive management beyond initial deployment.
