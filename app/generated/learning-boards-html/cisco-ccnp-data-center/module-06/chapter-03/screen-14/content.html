Key Concepts

Core ideas to remember from this chapter

Cisco DCNM

A centralized management platform for Cisco Nexus-based data centers, providing fabric visibility, performance monitoring, and automation.

SNMP

A standard protocol for network devices to exchange management information, used for monitoring device status and collecting metrics.

NetFlow/IPFIX

Protocols for collecting and exporting network flow information for traffic analysis, including source/destination IP, ports, and byte counts.

Summary Takeaway

Effective data center monitoring relies on a combination of centralized management platforms like DCNM and foundational protocols such as SNMP and NetFlow/IPFIX for comprehensive visibility.
