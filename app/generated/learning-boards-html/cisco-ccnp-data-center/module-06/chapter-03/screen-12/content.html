Embedded Packet Capture (EPC) for Deep Analysis

Embedded Packet Capture (EPC) on Cisco Nexus switches allows you to capture network traffic directly on the device, providing granular insight for diagnosing elusive or intermittent issues.

  • •Direct Capture: No external taps or SPAN ports needed.
  • •Precise Filters: Define filters by IP, port, protocol, VLAN to capture relevant traffic.
  • •PCAP Export: Export captured packets for analysis with tools like Wireshark.
Common Mistake

Applying overly broad EPC filters can lead to massive capture files that are difficult to analyze. Always be as specific as possible.
