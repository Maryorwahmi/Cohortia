DR Orchestration and Automation
Streamlining failover and failback processes
Why Automate DR?

Manual failover is slow, error-prone, and often fails to meet strict RTOs. Automation ensures rapid, reliable, and consistent recovery.

  • Reduces human error.
  • Accelerates recovery time.
  • Ensures consistent execution.
Key Automation Tasks
VM Startup Order

Orchestrating dependencies (e.g., database before application).

Network Reconfiguration

Re-IPing VMs, connecting to correct networks, updating DNS.

Application Configuration

Updating load balancers, service registrations, etc.

Key Takeaway

DR orchestration and automation tools are essential for achieving defined RTOs by streamlining complex failover and failback processes.
