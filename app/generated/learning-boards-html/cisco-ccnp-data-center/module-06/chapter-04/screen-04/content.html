Business Continuity vs. Disaster Recovery
Understanding the distinct roles in organizational resilience
Business Continuity (BC)

The overarching strategy to ensure critical business functions can continue during and after a disaster. It's about keeping the entire business operational.

  • Focuses on the entire organization.
  • Ensures ongoing operations, not just IT.
  • Proactive planning for all types of disruptions.
Disaster Recovery (DR)

A subset of BC, specifically focusing on restoring IT infrastructure and operations after a catastrophic event. It's about getting IT systems back online.

  • Focuses on IT systems and infrastructure.
  • Restores data, applications, and network.
  • Reactive response to IT-specific disasters.
Analogy

Think of BC as having a comprehensive emergency preparedness plan for your entire home, including food, shelter, and communication. DR is like having a specific plan for restoring your home's electricity and internet after a power outage.

Key Takeaway

BC is the strategic umbrella for organizational resilience, while DR is the tactical plan for IT system recovery. Both are indispensable for modern data centers.

Common Misconception

Many believe a DR plan is sufficient for business continuity. However, a DR plan only addresses IT recovery; a full BC plan considers all business functions, personnel, and processes.
