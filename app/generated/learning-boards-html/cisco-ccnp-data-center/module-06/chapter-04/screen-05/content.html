High Availability: The Bedrock of Resilience
Ensuring continuous service within a single data center
What is High Availability (HA)?

HA is the design principle that ensures systems and applications remain operational for a maximum percentage of time, minimizing downtime from component failures.

  • Eliminates single points of failure.
  • Provides redundancy at every layer.
  • Enables automatic failover for components.
Key Components of HA
Hardware Redundancy

Dual power supplies, redundant fans, multiple NICs.

Network Redundancy

Multiple uplinks, HSRP/VRRP, vPC configurations.

Storage Redundancy

RAID configurations, storage clustering, multipathing.

Server Redundancy

Clustering, virtualization platforms, load balancing.

Key Takeaway

High Availability is the essential first step in building resilient data center operations, preventing localized failures from causing service disruptions.

Pro Tip

While HA prevents component failures from causing outages, applications must also be designed for HA to fully leverage the resilient infrastructure. A highly available network won't help a single-instance application.
