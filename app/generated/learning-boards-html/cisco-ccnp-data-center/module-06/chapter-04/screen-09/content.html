RTO and RPO: Defining Recovery Goals
Critical metrics for disaster recovery solution design
Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum tolerable duration of time that a computer, system, network, or application can be down after a failure or disaster. It's about how quickly you need to be back up.

  • Measures downtime tolerance.
  • Influences recovery speed and automation.
  • Business-driven requirement.
Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum tolerable amount of data loss, measured in time, that can occur during a disaster. It's about how much data you can afford to lose.

  • Measures data loss tolerance.
  • Influences replication strategy (sync vs. async).
  • Directly impacts data protection methods.
Key Takeaway

RTO dictates how fast you recover, and RPO dictates how much data you can lose. These business-critical metrics drive DR solution architecture.

Example Impact

An RTO of minutes and RPO of zero for a critical financial application demands synchronous replication and a 'hot-hot' data center. A less critical app with RTO of hours and RPO of a few hours might use asynchronous replication and a 'hot-cold' design.
