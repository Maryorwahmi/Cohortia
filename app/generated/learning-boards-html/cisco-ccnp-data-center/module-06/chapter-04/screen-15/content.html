Key Concepts
Essential terms to remember for data center resilience
Business Continuity (BC)

The overall process of ensuring that critical business functions can continue during and after a disaster.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The subset of BC focused on restoring IT infrastructure and operations after a catastrophic event.

High Availability (HA)

Designing systems and networks with redundancy to minimize downtime from component failures.

vPC (Virtual Port Channel)

A Cisco Nexus technology providing active-active Layer 2 redundancy by making two switches appear as one logical device.

HSRP (Hot Standby Router Protocol)

A Cisco proprietary protocol providing first-hop router redundancy for IP networks.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these foundational concepts is crucial for designing and implementing resilient data center operations that can withstand various disruptions.
