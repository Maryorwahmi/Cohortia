The Importance of DR Testing
Validating your recovery plan for real-world scenarios
Why Test Your DR Plan?

A DR plan is only effective if it works. Regular testing is crucial to validate procedures, identify gaps, and ensure readiness for an actual disaster.

  • Identifies bottlenecks and misconfigurations.
  • Validates recovery procedures and RTO/RPO.
  • Trains personnel on their roles during a crisis.
Key Aspects of DR Testing
Regularity

At least annually, or whenever significant changes occur in infrastructure.

Documentation

Keep all procedures, configurations, and contact lists up-to-date.

Training

Ensure all relevant personnel are trained and understand their responsibilities.

Key Takeaway

Untested DR plans are unreliable. Regular, documented testing and trained personnel are non-negotiable for effective disaster recovery.
