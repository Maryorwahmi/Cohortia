DR Strategy
Impact of RTO/RPO on DR Solution Design
Aligning recovery objectives with technical implementation
High RTO/Low RPO (e.g., RTO: minutes, RPO: 0)

Requires maximum data protection and minimal downtime. Often leads to complex, high-cost solutions.

  • Synchronous replication.
  • Active-active ('hot-hot') data center design.
  • High-bandwidth, low-latency network links.
  • Highly automated failover.
Moderate RTO/RPO (e.g., RTO: hours, RPO: hours)

Allows for some data loss and longer recovery time. More flexible and often more cost-effective.

  • Asynchronous replication.
  • Active-passive ('hot-cold' or 'warm') data center design.
  • Standard WAN links with sufficient bandwidth.
  • Automated or semi-automated failover.
Key Takeaway

RTO and RPO are business drivers that directly determine the technical architecture, cost, and complexity of your disaster recovery solution.
