Key Concepts: Sustainable & Intent-Based Data Centers

Reinforcing critical principles for modern infrastructure

Sustainability

Designing and operating data centers to minimize environmental impact, focusing on energy efficiency, renewable energy, and waste reduction.

Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE)

A metric used to determine the energy efficiency of a data center, calculated by dividing the total facility power by the IT equipment power.

Intent-Based Networking (IBN)

A networking approach where the network is designed to understand and enforce business intent, often leveraging AI/ML for automation and optimization.

Chapter Takeaway

Modern data centers prioritize environmental responsibility through efficiency metrics like PUE and leverage intelligent, intent-based automation for optimal performance and management.
