DCB Protocol

Enhanced Transmission Selection (ETS) - IEEE 802.1Qaz

Guaranteed Bandwidth for FCoE Traffic

ETS's Core Function

  • Allows for bandwidth allocation among different traffic classes (priorities).

  • Ensures FCoE traffic receives its guaranteed share of bandwidth.

  • Prevents other traffic types from monopolizing network resources.

Why ETS Matters for FCoE

FCoE is highly sensitive to latency and requires consistent bandwidth.

Without ETS, FCoE performance could degrade during high network utilization.

Guarantees predictable performance for storage operations over Ethernet.

Key Takeaway

ETS (802.1Qaz) is crucial for FCoE, ensuring dedicated bandwidth allocation for storage traffic, which prevents performance bottlenecks in a converged network.

Example Scenario

In a converged network, ETS can guarantee that FCoE traffic always gets 40% of the link bandwidth, while other IP traffic shares the remaining 60%, ensuring storage operations remain responsive even during peak data transfers.
