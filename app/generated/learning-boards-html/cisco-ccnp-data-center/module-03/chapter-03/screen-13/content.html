Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Fibre Channel over Ethernet (FCoE)

Encapsulates Fibre Channel frames within Ethernet, allowing SAN traffic over a converged Ethernet network.

Converged Network Adapter (CNA)

A server adapter combining Ethernet NIC and Fibre Channel HBA functionality into one.

Data Center Bridging (DCB)

A suite of IEEE standards (PFC, ETS, DCBX) enhancing Ethernet for lossless FCoE transport.

Chapter Summary

FCoE provides a unified fabric for LAN and SAN traffic, enabled by CNAs and the critical lossless capabilities of Data Center Bridging protocols.
