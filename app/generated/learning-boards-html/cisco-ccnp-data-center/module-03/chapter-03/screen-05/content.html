Definition

FCoE: Encapsulation and Lossless Requirement

Bridging Fibre Channel to Ethernet

FCoE Encapsulation

FCoE wraps native Fibre Channel frames within standard Ethernet frames.

Allows FC traffic to traverse a standard Ethernet network.

The Lossless Requirement

Traditional Ethernet: Lossy, drops frames during congestion, relies on retransmission.

Fibre Channel: Lossless, frame drops are unacceptable and cause severe issues.

Solution: Data Center Bridging (DCB) standards enhance Ethernet for lossless delivery.

Key Takeaway

FCoE requires Data Center Bridging (DCB) to transform lossy Ethernet into a lossless transport, which is essential for Fibre Channel's strict reliability demands.

Misconception Alert

FCoE is not just 'FC running on Ethernet.' It's a sophisticated encapsulation with critical Ethernet enhancements (DCB) to ensure lossless delivery, which traditional Ethernet lacks.
