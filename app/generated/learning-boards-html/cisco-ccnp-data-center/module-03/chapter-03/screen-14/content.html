Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Priority Flow Control (PFC)

A DCB mechanism (802.1Qbb) for pausing specific priority queues to prevent frame loss.

Enhanced Transmission Selection (ETS)

A DCB mechanism (802.1Qaz) for bandwidth management among traffic classes.

Data Center Bridging Exchange (DCBX)

A protocol (802.1AB) for discovering and exchanging DCB capabilities between devices.

Chapter Summary

PFC, ETS, and DCBX are the core DCB protocols ensuring lossless, managed, and consistently configured FCoE traffic, while FIP handles the virtual link establishment.
