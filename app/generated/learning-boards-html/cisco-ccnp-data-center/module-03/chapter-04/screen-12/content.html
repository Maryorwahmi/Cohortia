Safety Note

Network Design for iSCSI and NAS

Ensuring performance and security for storage traffic.

Avoid Congested Networks

Never place iSCSI or NAS traffic on heavily used general-purpose networks to prevent performance bottlenecks.

Implement VLANs and QoS

Use dedicated VLANs for storage traffic and QoS policies to prioritize it, ensuring low latency and adequate bandwidth.

Access Controls for NAS

Configure robust file system and network-level permissions to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive NAS data.

Cisco Nexus Integration

High-Performance Fabric

Cisco Nexus switches provide the robust network foundation for both iSCSI and NAS.

Jumbo Frames (iSCSI)

Consider enabling Jumbo Frames for iSCSI to reduce CPU overhead and improve throughput, ensuring end-to-end support.

Key Takeaway

Effective network design for iSCSI and NAS requires traffic segregation, QoS, and strong access controls to ensure optimal performance and data security.
