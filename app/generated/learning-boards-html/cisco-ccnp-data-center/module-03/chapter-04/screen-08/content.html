Best Practices

Optimizing iSCSI Performance and Security

Ensuring a robust and reliable iSCSI SAN.

Traffic Segregation

Use dedicated VLANs, separate physical NICs, or subnets to isolate iSCSI traffic from general network congestion.

Quality of Service (QoS)

Prioritize iSCSI traffic on switches (e.g., Cisco Nexus) to minimize latency and ensure consistent performance.

Jumbo Frames

Consider enabling larger Ethernet frames to reduce CPU overhead and improve throughput, if supported end-to-end.

Authentication

Implement CHAP or other authentication methods to secure iSCSI connections and prevent unauthorized access.

Key Takeaway

Optimizing iSCSI involves network segregation, QoS, and potentially Jumbo Frames to ensure high performance and security in the data center.
