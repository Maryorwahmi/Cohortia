Comparing Data Center Storage Solutions

Fibre Channel, iSCSI, and NAS strengths and weaknesses.

Fibre Channel/FCoE

  • Highest performance, lowest latency.
  • Dedicated hardware, complex.
  • Ideal for mission-critical databases, high-density virtualization.

iSCSI

  • Good performance, cost-effective.
  • Leverages existing Ethernet.
  • Suitable for virtualized environments, departmental databases.

NAS

  • File-level access, simple.
  • Uses standard IP networks (NFS/SMB).
  • Best for unstructured data, shared documents, backups.
Key Takeaway

Selecting the optimal storage solution depends on balancing performance needs, budget constraints, and the specific type of data and access required for your applications.
