Cables and Switches: The Fabric Backbone

Physical Medium and Intelligent Routing

Roles in the FC SAN

  • •FC Cables: Typically fiber optic, providing high-speed physical links.
  • •FC Switches: (e.g., Cisco MDS) The intelligent backbone of the SAN.
  • •Enable intelligent routing, management, and scalability.
  • •Allow multiple servers to connect to multiple storage arrays.
Key Takeaway

Fiber optic cables provide high-speed links, while FC switches (like Cisco MDS) offer intelligent routing, scalability, and resilience for the SAN fabric.

Example: Path Redundancy

In a switched fabric, if a cable or port connecting a server to a switch fails, the FC switch can automatically reroute traffic through an alternate path to the storage array. This ensures continuous data access and prevents downtime, highlighting the resilience provided by intelligent switches.

Pro-Tip: Scalability with MDS

Cisco MDS switches are designed for modularity, allowing you to expand your SAN fabric by adding more line cards or interconnecting multiple switches. This provides a highly scalable solution that can grow with your data center's storage demands without requiring a complete overhaul.
