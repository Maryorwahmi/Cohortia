Virtual SANs (VSANs)

Logical Segmentation of FC Fabrics

Understanding VSANs

  • •Logical fabrics within a single physical FC switch or across multiple switches.
  • •Analogous to VLANs in an Ethernet network.
  • •Provides enhanced security, fault isolation, and simplified management.
  • •Each VSAN has its own devices, zoning, and fabric services.
Key Takeaway

VSANs logically segment a physical FC fabric into isolated SANs, enhancing security, fault isolation, and simplifying management, similar to VLANs.

Example: Production vs. Development

A data center can create one VSAN (e.g., VSAN 10) for all production servers and storage, and another VSAN (e.g., VSAN 20) for development and testing. This ensures that any experimental configurations or potential issues in the development environment are completely isolated from the critical production infrastructure.

Pro-Tip: Simplified Management

By segmenting a large SAN into smaller VSANs, administrators can manage each logical fabric independently. This reduces complexity, especially in multi-tenant environments, and allows for more targeted troubleshooting and policy application.
