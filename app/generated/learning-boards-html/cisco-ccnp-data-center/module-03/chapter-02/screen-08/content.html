FC Topologies

Simple FC Topologies

Point-to-Point and Arbitrated Loop

Point-to-Point

  • •Direct connection between HBA and storage port.
  • •High performance for single connection.
  • •Lacks scalability and redundancy.
Key Takeaway

Point-to-Point offers dedicated performance but lacks scalability, while Arbitrated Loop shares bandwidth and has single points of failure.

Arbitrated Loop (FC-AL)

  • •Multiple devices connected in a loop.
  • •Devices share loop bandwidth; one transmits at a time.
  • •Less common due to performance limitations.
  • •Single point of failure can break the loop.

Example: FC-AL Bottleneck

Imagine an Arbitrated Loop with 8 servers and 8 storage ports. If all devices try to communicate simultaneously, they must contend for the shared loop bandwidth. This quickly leads to performance degradation and increased latency, making it unsuitable for high-demand environments.
