Historical Context

The Evolution of Data Storage

Tracing the journey from local drives to sophisticated networked solutions.

DAS

Directly Attached Storage

NAS

Network Attached Storage

SAN

Storage Area Network

Initial Simplicity

Early servers used direct connections for storage, offering straightforward setup and local performance.

Scaling Challenges

As data grew, DAS limitations in management, sharing, and capacity became significant bottlenecks.

Networked Solutions

The demand for flexibility and shared resources led to the development of NAS and SAN architectures.

Key Takeaway

Data storage evolved from simple direct connections to complex networked solutions to meet the growing demands for scalability, shareability, and efficient management in data centers.
