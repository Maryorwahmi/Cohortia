Essential VDC Terminology

Reinforcing Your Understanding of Core Concepts

Virtual Device Context (VDC)

A logical partition of a physical Nexus switch into multiple independent, virtual switches, each with its own control plane and resources.

Default VDC (VDC 1)

The initial and master VDC on a Nexus switch, responsible for chassis-level management and the creation/allocation of other VDCs.

Multi-tenancy

The ability of a single physical infrastructure to serve multiple independent clients or organizations, with each tenant's resources and data isolated from others.

Summary Takeaway

VDCs provide logical isolation on Nexus switches, with VDC 1 as the master, enabling multi-tenancy for diverse network environments.
