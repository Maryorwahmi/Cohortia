VDC 1: The Master Control Plane

Understanding the Unique Role of the Default Virtual Device Context

Default VDC (VDC 1)

Automatically created upon Nexus switch boot-up, VDC 1 is the initial and master VDC. It's the only VDC with control over the entire physical chassis.

Master Control Functions

  • Creating and deleting other VDCs.
  • Allocating physical interfaces to VDCs.
  • Managing supervisor modules.
  • Initial connection point to the Nexus switch.
Key Takeaway

VDC 1 is the indispensable master VDC, controlling all chassis-level operations and resource allocation for user-defined VDCs.
