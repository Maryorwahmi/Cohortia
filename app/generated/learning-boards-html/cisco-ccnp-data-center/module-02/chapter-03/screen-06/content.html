Streamlined Management: Administrative Separation

Delegating Control Without Compromising Overall Security

Administrative Separation

Different VDCs can be managed by different administrators or teams, each with their own login and access privileges, without granting them access to the entire physical switch.

Scenario: The 'Network Operations' team manages VDC A, while the 'Security' team manages VDC B, each with distinct access rights.

Enhanced Security Posture

  • Reduces attack surface by limiting administrative scope.
  • Minimizes risk of unauthorized changes across the entire chassis.
  • Simplifies compliance by enforcing granular access control.
Key Takeaway

Administrative separation in VDCs simplifies management, enhances security, and allows for granular control over network segments by different teams.
