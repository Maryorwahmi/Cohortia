Virtual Device Contexts (VDCs): Multi-Tenancy and Isolation

Partitioning a single physical switch into multiple independent logical devices.

VDC Definition

  • •
    Logical Partition: A single physical Nexus switch is divided into multiple virtual switches.
  • •
    Independent Resources: Each VDC has its own control plane, data plane, and administrative domain.
  • •
    Supported On: Primarily Nexus 7000 and 9000 series.

Benefits of VDCs

  • •
    Multi-Tenancy: Allows multiple departments or customers to share hardware securely.
  • •
    Fault Isolation: A failure in one VDC does not impact others.
  • •
    Resource Segmentation: Dedicated resources prevent resource contention.

Physical Switch to Logical VDCs

Key Takeaway

VDCs provide logical partitioning of a physical Nexus switch, enabling multi-tenancy, fault isolation, and efficient resource segmentation.
