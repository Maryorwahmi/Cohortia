Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Cisco Nexus Switches

A family of switches specifically designed for data center environments, offering high performance, scalability, and advanced features.

NX-OS

The modular, highly available operating system developed by Cisco for its Nexus switches, distinct from traditional IOS.

Virtual Device Context (VDC)

A feature in NX-OS that allows a single physical Nexus switch to be partitioned into multiple independent logical switches.

Summary Takeaway

Cisco Nexus switches, powered by NX-OS and its VDC feature, are purpose-built for the high demands of modern data centers, offering unparalleled performance, scalability, and operational resilience.
