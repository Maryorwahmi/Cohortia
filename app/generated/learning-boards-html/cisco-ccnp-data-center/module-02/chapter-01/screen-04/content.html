Core Concept

The Need for Nexus in Modern Data Centers

Addressing the unique demands of high-performance, virtualized environments.

Traditional vs. Data Center Networks

Traditional (Catalyst):

  • General enterprise use
  • Layer 2/3 switching
  • Monolithic OS

Data Center (Nexus):

  • High-density server connectivity
  • Low-latency fabric
  • Modular OS (NX-OS)

Key Data Center Demands

  • •
    High Density: Support for thousands of virtual machines and containers.
  • •
    Low Latency: Critical for real-time applications and high-frequency trading.
  • •
    Virtualization: Seamless integration with hypervisors and cloud platforms.
  • •
    Automation: Programmable infrastructure for rapid deployment and management.

Nexus: The Data Center Backbone

Key Takeaway

Nexus switches are purpose-built to address the unique, high-performance, and scalable demands of modern data center environments.
