Nexus 7000 Series: Core and Aggregation Scalability

Modular design for immense scalability and advanced features in large data centers.

Key Characteristics

  • •
    Modular Chassis: Supports various line cards, supervisor engines, and power supplies.
  • •
    High Port Density: Accommodates numerous 10/40/100 Gigabit Ethernet ports.
  • •
    Core/Aggregation Layer: Designed for large-scale data center interconnectivity.

Advanced Features

  • •
    Virtual Device Contexts (VDCs): Logical partitioning for multi-tenancy and fault isolation.
  • •
    Investment Protection: Flexible expansion and upgrades within the same chassis.
  • •
    High Availability: Redundant components ensure continuous operation.

Nexus 7000 Modular Architecture

Key Takeaway

The Nexus 7000 Series offers modularity and high density, making it ideal for scalable core and aggregation layers in large data centers.
