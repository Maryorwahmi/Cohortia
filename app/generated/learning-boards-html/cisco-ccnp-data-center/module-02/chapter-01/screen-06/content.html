Nexus 3000 & 5000 Series: ToR and Unified Fabric

High-performance Top-of-Rack solutions with converged LAN and SAN capabilities.

Nexus 3000 Series

  • •
    High-Performance: Designed for ultra-low latency environments.
  • •
    Top-of-Rack (ToR): Ideal for direct server connectivity in racks.
  • •
    Use Cases: High-frequency trading, big data analytics, latency-sensitive applications.

Nexus 5000 Series

  • •
    Versatile ToR: Also commonly deployed as Top-of-Rack switches.
  • •
    Unified Fabric: Converges Ethernet (LAN) and Fibre Channel over Ethernet (FCoE) (SAN).
  • •
    Use Cases: Converged infrastructure, simplified cabling, reduced operational costs.

Key Takeaway

Nexus 3000 excels in low-latency ToR, while Nexus 5000 offers unified fabric for converged LAN/SAN, simplifying data center infrastructure.
