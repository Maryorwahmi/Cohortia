Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Fabric Extender (FEX)

Nexus 2000 Series devices that act as remote line cards for parent Nexus switches, simplifying cabling and extending the fabric.

Process Restartability

A key NX-OS feature where individual software processes can crash and restart without affecting the entire switch's operation, enhancing stability.

DC Network Topologies

Architectural patterns like Top-of-Rack (ToR) and Spine-and-Leaf, used for high performance and scalability in data centers.

Summary Takeaway

FEX devices streamline rack connectivity, NX-OS's process restartability ensures high availability, and modern topologies like spine-and-leaf optimize data center performance.
