Management Strategies

Choosing Your Management Path

Comparing mgmt0 (Out-of-Band) and SVI (In-Band) Approaches

mgmt0 (Out-of-Band)

  • •Dedicated physical interface for management.
  • •Management traffic isolated from data traffic.
  • •Higher security and resilience during data plane issues.
  • •Requires separate cabling and network infrastructure.
Best For

High-security, mission-critical environments where management isolation is paramount.

SVI (In-Band)

  • •Logical interface within a VLAN for management.
  • •Management traffic shares data plane interfaces.
  • •Flexible, reduces cabling, but can be affected by data issues.
  • •Less isolation, potentially lower security than dedicated mgmt0.
Best For

Environments prioritizing flexibility and reduced infrastructure, or as a backup management path.
