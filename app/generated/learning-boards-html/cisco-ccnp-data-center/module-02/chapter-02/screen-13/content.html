Configuration Management

Verifying and Protecting Your Configuration

Essential Checks and Safety Measures for Nexus Deployments

Match Configurations

Ensure running-config and startup-config are identical after saving changes.

Lab Test First

Always test significant changes in a lab environment to prevent production outages.

Pro-Tip: Common Pitfalls

Watch out for misconfigured duplex settings and incorrect VLAN assignments, as these are frequent causes of connectivity issues.

Key Takeaway
