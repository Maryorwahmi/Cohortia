Key Concepts — Quick Reference
Three short reminders
Use keys
Primary and foreign keys are the backbone of relational integrity.
Pick by workload
OLTP for transactions; OLAP/data warehouse for analytics.
Design always
Even in cloud warehouses, well-designed schemas and cost-aware queries matter.
Final take
These reminders will guide practical schema design, pipeline choices, and the safe separation of transactional and analytical workloads.
