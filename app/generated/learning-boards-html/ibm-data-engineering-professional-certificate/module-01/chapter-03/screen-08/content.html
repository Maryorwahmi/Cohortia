Star Schema and Snowflake Schema
Design trade-offs between speed and normalization
Star Schema
Central fact table with measures and foreign keys to denormalized dimension tables. Minimizes joins for common queries.
Fact: Sales(order_id, product_id, customer_id, sale_amount, sale_date)
Dimension: Product(product_id, product_name, category)
Dimension: Customer(customer_id, name, region)
Snowflake Schema
Dimensions are normalized into sub-tables. Reduces redundancy but increases joins, which can slow queries.
Dimension split: Product → ProductCategory(category_id, category_name)
Use when dimension attributes are large and change independently.
Key Takeaway

Choose star for fast analytics; choose snowflake when storage savings and normalized updates are a priority.
