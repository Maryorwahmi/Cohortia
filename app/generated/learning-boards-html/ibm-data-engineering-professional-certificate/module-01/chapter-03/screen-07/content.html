Data Warehouse Characteristics
What makes a warehouse suitable for analytics
Four defining properties
  • Subject-oriented: organized around business areas such as sales or customers.
  • Integrated: data from different sources is cleaned and unified.
  • Time-variant: supports historical analysis with dated records or snapshots.
  • Non-volatile: data is appended, not frequently updated or deleted.
Why it matters: reliable historical records enable trend analysis and forecasting.
Concrete scenario
An e-commerce warehouse stores sales with order_date, product_id, customer_id and sale_amount. Analysts can compute monthly revenue or customer lifetime value by querying this stable, time-stamped dataset.
Misconception
Because warehouses keep historical snapshots, avoid performing high-frequency transactional updates inside them. Instead append and correct via controlled processes.
Key Takeaway

A warehouse is curated for analysis: stable history, curated integration, and subject focus are the cornerstones.
