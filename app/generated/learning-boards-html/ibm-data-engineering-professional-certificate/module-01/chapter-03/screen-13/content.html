When to Move Reporting Off OLTP
Use replicas or warehouses to avoid impacting live systems
Options
  • Read replica: provide safe reads with replication lag; good for near-real-time dashboards.
  • Data warehouse: best for large-scale historical analytics and complex aggregations.
Rule of thumb: never run heavy analytic workloads on the primary OLTP instance.
Concrete scenario
A multi-hour aggregation on orders runs concurrently with customer checkout. The result: slower writes, longer user wait times, and possible timeouts. Move such work to replicas or warehouses.
Misconception
Replicas reduce risk but do not eliminate resource contention. Schedule heavy queries and consider dedicated analytic stores for sustained workloads.
Key Takeaway

Separate transactional and analytic workloads using replicas or warehouses to protect production performance.
