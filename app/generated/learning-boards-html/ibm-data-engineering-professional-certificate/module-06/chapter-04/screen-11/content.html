Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Avoiding a Data Swamp
Common Pitfalls
  • Dropping files without catalog entries or owners.
  • No consistent partitioning or file naming conventions.
  • Missing access controls; sensitive fields unprotected.
Mitigation Steps
  • Require metadata registration on ingestion with owner and schema hints.
  • Enforce naming and partition rules in automated pipelines.
  • Apply RBAC, encryption, and auditing for sensitive datasets.
Key Takeaway
Stop swamps by baking governance into ingestion and processing rather than attempting retroactive cleanup.
