Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Tools & Checklist
Ingestion Tools
Kafka, Event Hubs for streaming; batch exports for periodic loads.
Processing Engines
Spark and similar frameworks to clean, enrich, and write columnar outputs.
Governance
Catalogs, RBAC, encryption, and audit logs to maintain trust and compliance.
Final Checklist
Every dataset should have an owner, catalog entry, retention policy, and partition plan before being used by analytics or ML.
