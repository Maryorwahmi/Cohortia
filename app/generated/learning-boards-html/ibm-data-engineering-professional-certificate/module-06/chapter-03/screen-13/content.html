Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Common Mistakes
Mistake: One-size-fits-all
Choosing NoSQL for everything instead of aligning model to query patterns creates inefficiency and technical debt.
Mistake: No validation
Allowing uncontrolled schemaless writes leads to inconsistent data and makes governance difficult.
Mistake: Ignoring backups
Relying solely on replication for disaster recovery is risky; define snapshot and restore plans.
Mistake: Forcing joins
Attempting heavy relational joins in NoSQL stores often leads to brittle, slow application-side operations.
Quick check
Before selecting a store, list the top five queries and verify that the datastore supports them efficiently without complex workaround queries.
