Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Operational Notes
Data governance
Use validation rules, schemas-as-code, and CI checks to prevent schema drift and ensure analytics remain reliable.
Security
Encrypt data at rest and in transit, and apply least-privilege access controls, especially in schemaless systems where sensitive fields may appear unexpectedly.
Backups and recovery
Plan snapshot and restore procedures; replication helps availability but is not a substitute for consistent backups.
Performance tuning
Design partition keys to avoid hotspots, set TTLs to limit unbounded growth, and tune replication for latency or durability as needed.
Key Takeaway
Operational planning—governance, security, and backups—must accompany any NoSQL adoption to avoid long-term costs and risks.
