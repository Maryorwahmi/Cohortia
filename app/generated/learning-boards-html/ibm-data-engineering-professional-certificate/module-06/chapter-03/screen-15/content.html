Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
Key-Value Store
Fast single-key reads/writes and caching; ideal for sessions and leaderboards.
Document Database
Flexible JSON/BSON documents for product catalogs and content with nested fields.
Column-Family Store
High write throughput and efficient range queries; great for time-series data.
One-line takeaway

Match your dominant query shape to the datastore family: lookups, documents, ranges, or traversals.
