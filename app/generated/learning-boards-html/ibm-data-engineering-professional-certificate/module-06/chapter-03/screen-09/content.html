Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Graph Databases
Core idea
Model entities and their relationships directly so multi-hop traversals are efficient and natural.
Examples: Neo4j, Amazon Neptune. Use cases: social graphs, recommendations, fraud detection.
Scenario
Fraud detection
Represent users, accounts, and devices as nodes and their interactions as edges to quickly find connected components or suspicious rings through traversals.
Key takeaway
If your most frequent queries follow relationships deeply, a graph database is likely the most efficient choice.
