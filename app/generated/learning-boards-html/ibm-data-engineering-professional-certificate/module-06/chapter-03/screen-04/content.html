Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Why NoSQL
Claim
NoSQL offers data models and distributed designs that better handle scale, velocity, and schema variety than typical relational systems.
Why it matters
  • Horizontal scaling reduces cost and supports massive data volumes.
  • Flexible schemas let teams iterate quickly without complex migrations.
  • Specialized models optimize common queries for throughput or traversal speed.
Key Takeaway

Choose a datastore to match data shape and dominant access patterns, not convenience.

Illustrative scenario
Hybrid architecture example
A storefront uses a relational system for orders and payments to preserve ACID, and a key-value cache to serve session and product detail reads for low latency.
Common misconception
NoSQL does not mean unreliable data. Many NoSQL systems let you tune consistency and durability depending on your needs.
Check your understanding
List three dominant queries for your app and decide whether they need immediate consistency or can use eventual consistency.
