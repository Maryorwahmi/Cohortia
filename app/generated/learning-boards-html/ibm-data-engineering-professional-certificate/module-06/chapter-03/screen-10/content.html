Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › NoSQL vs SQL
Relational (SQL)
  • Rigid schema and ACID transactions
  • Complex joins and relational integrity
  • Typically vertical scaling
NoSQL (varied models)
  • Flexible or schemaless models and horizontal scaling
  • Optimized for specific workloads: key-value, document, column-family, graph
  • Often favors availability and partition tolerance, may use eventual consistency
Decision checklist
  1. What queries dominate: lookups, document searches, range reads, or traversals?
  2. How fresh must data be: immediate consistency or eventual consistency acceptable?
  3. How will the system behave during network partitions and peak loads?
