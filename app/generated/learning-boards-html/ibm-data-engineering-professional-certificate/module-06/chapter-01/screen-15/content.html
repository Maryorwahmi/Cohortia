Veracity
Data quality and trustworthiness are essential for correct insights.
RDBMS limits
Rigid schemas and vertical scaling often make them unsuitable for many Big Data workloads.
Operational readiness
Embed security, monitoring, and recovery plans into your pipeline design from day one.
Final takeaway

Use the five Vs to guide tool choice, avoid forcing Big Data into single-server databases, and make security and operations non-negotiable parts of your design.
