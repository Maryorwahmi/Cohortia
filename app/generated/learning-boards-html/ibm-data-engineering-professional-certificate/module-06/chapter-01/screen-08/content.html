Veracity: Ensuring trustworthy data

Detect bias, missing values, and noise early to protect downstream analysis.

Checks to include
  • Schema and type validation on ingest
  • Sanity ranges and null-handling rules
  • Sampling and drift detection for incoming streams
Value: The reason to collect

If data doesn't lead to measurable outcomes, collect less or instrument differently to focus on value.

Example

If the business goal is to reduce pickup wait time, prioritize metrics and data that directly influence routing and driver incentives.

Key Takeaway

Treat quality and purpose as first-class requirements: clean inputs and clear outcomes prevent wasted engineering effort.
