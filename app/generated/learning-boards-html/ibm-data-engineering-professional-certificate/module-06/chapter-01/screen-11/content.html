Vertical Scaling

Upgrade a single machine to handle more load. Simple but bounded and costly at scale.

When to use

Small to moderate workloads or systems where distribution adds unjustified complexity.

Horizontal Scaling

Add more machines and split data/work across them for capacity and resilience.

Considerations
  • Data partitioning and locality
  • Replication for fault tolerance
  • Network and coordination overhead
Key Takeaway

Match scaling strategy to growth and workload patterns. Horizontal scale is the practical choice for Big Data despite added complexity.
