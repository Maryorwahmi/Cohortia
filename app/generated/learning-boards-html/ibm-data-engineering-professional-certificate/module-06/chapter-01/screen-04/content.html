What is Big Data?
A definition that drives architectural choices.
Volume
Very large quantities of records or files that exceed single-server capacity.
Velocity
High-rate, continuous data arrival requiring low-latency processing.
Variety
Mixed formats from structured to unstructured that need integration.
Veracity

Quality and trustworthiness issues in data that must be detected and corrected to avoid misleading insights.

Value

The purpose of collecting and processing data: measurable insight or business outcomes that justify the cost.

Key Takeaway

Use the five Vs to diagnose requirements. The dominant V should guide tool choice and pipeline design.

Concrete example
GPS streams from millions of drivers exhibit high volume and velocity; chat logs add variety and veracity concerns. The final goal is route optimization — the value.
