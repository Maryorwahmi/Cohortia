Why volume changes storage design
Massive datasets force partitioning, tiering, and format choices.
Partitioning

Split datasets by time, region, or key so reads and writes touch smaller subsets.

Compression & format

Use columnar, compressed formats to reduce storage and speed analytics over wide tables.

Concrete scenario

A logging pipeline writes billions of events daily. Partition by date and store compressed columnar files to enable fast historical analysis without scanning all data.

Common mistake

Treating storage as infinite and failing to plan retention or tiering, which inflates costs and slows queries.

Pro tip

Design storage with expected query patterns in mind: optimize for reads that support your core analytics or SLAs.

Key Takeaway

Volume drives partitioning, compression, and tiered retention to keep costs and latency manageable.
