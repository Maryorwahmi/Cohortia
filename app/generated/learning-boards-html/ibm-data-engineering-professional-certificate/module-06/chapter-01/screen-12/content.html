Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Security & Ops
Security controls
  • Authentication and role-based access control
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Field-level masking for sensitive attributes
Operational practices
  • Distributed monitoring and correlated logging
  • Automated deployments and health checks
  • Backup, replication, and disaster recovery plans
Example

A production pipeline encrypts payment data at ingestion, applies tokenization, and logs access to an audit store to meet compliance and reduce exposure.

Key Takeaway

Treat security and operations as foundational: integrate controls and monitoring across the full data lifecycle.
