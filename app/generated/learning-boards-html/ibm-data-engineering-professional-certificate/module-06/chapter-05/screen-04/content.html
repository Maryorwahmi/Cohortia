Provisioning Object Storage and Buckets
What to create, why prefixes matter, and naming guidance.
Definition
Object storage is a flat namespace of objects inside buckets. Buckets are the primary container, and prefixes inside buckets act like folders used to define Data Lake zones.
Practical choices
  • Decide between multiple buckets vs prefixes based on isolation and billing needs.
  • Use environment-aware names like my-company-data-lake-prod or include region codes when needed.
  • Apply lifecycle policies to prefixes to expire or transition objects.
Example
Bucket: my-company-data-lake with prefixes raw/, curated/ and consumption/
Key Takeaway

Pick a consistent naming and prefix strategy to simplify access control, lifecycle rules, and automation.

Design checklist
  1. Choose bucket scope and security boundaries.
  2. Define zones and corresponding prefixes.
  3. Decide retention and lifecycle policies per prefix.
  4. Record naming conventions and owner/service IDs.
Common mistake
Creating dozens of buckets without a naming convention increases management overhead. Prefer prefixes grouped by purpose unless strict isolation is required.
