Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Data Lake Zones
Landing Zone
Short-lived area for incoming files. Validate and quarantine failures here before moving to Raw.
Raw Zone
Immutable, original files. Keep them unchanged so you can replay processing if needed.
Curated & Consumption
Curated: cleaned, Parquet, partitioned. Consumption: aggregated datasets for BI or ML.
Example organization
s3://my-company-data-lake/landing/sales/date=2023-10-26/filename.csv
s3://my-company-data-lake/raw/sales/year=2023/month=10/day=26/filename.csv
s3://my-company-data-lake/curated/sales/product_category=Electronics/year=2023/month=10/
Checklist
  • Keep Raw immutable. Do not run transformations in place.
  • Partition on common query filters like dates or category.
  • Document lifecycle and access controls per prefix.
Key Takeaway

Zones reduce risk and make responsibilities clear. Use partitions and formats in Curated to enable fast analytics.
