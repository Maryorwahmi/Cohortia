Key Concepts — Tools & Practices

Three practical reminders to guide your architecture and operations.

MapReduce
Map, shuffle, and reduce phases for parallel batch aggregation tasks.
Hive & Pig
Higher-level abstractions for SQL-like queries and scripted ETL over data in HDFS.
Operational cautions
HDFS suits large, write-once files; plan for replication, monitoring, and proper access controls for sensitive data.
Final takeaway

Use the right abstraction for the job and design operational practices that account for replication, failure recovery, and access controls.
