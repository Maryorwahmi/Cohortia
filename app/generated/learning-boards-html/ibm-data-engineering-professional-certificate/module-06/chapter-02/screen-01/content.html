Why Hadoop matters and what parts do

Hadoop made it practical to store and process very large files across many inexpensive servers, adding replication for resilience and a way to schedule parallel work across that cluster.

Scale
Store terabytes by splitting into blocks and distributing them.
Resilience
Replication across Datanodes keeps data available when hardware fails.
Shared compute
YARN lets multiple processing frameworks share cluster resources.
