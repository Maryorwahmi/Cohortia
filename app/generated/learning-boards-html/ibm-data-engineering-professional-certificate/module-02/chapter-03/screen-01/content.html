Joining Tables and Subqueries
Bring normalized data together and nest queries to express multi-step retrieval logic.
Scope
Joins: match rows across tables. Subqueries: nest queries for intermediate results.
Why it matters
Normalized schemas are standard. Mastering joins and subqueries lets you create accurate reports without denormalizing data.
Coming up: INNER JOIN to report matched rows, OUTER JOINs to include unmatched records, and subqueries for aggregate-driven filters and correlated tests.
