WHERE vs HAVING
WHERE filters rows; HAVING filters grouped aggregates after GROUP BY.
Parentheses
Group OR conditions with parentheses when combining with AND to avoid logic errors.
Validate stepwise
Run WHERE, then GROUP BY, then HAVING, then ORDER BY and LIMIT to debug and confirm each stage.
Final Tip
Write queries in small steps and inspect intermediate outputs; this habit prevents logical mistakes and builds confidence in results.
