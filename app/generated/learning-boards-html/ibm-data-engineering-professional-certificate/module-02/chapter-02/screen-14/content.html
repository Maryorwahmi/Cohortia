Assessment: Apply queries to real problems

Use the examples below to test your understanding. Write the SQL, run it, and inspect both rows and aggregates to confirm correctness.

Question 1 (row-level)
Using Products table, find ProductName and Price for Electronics products costing more than 500, ordered by Price descending.
Expected structure: WHERE Category = 'Electronics' AND Price > 500 ORDER BY Price DESC
Question 2 (group-level)
Count products per Category and show only categories with more than 3 products using GROUP BY and HAVING.
Expected structure: GROUP BY Category HAVING COUNT(ProductID) > 3
Mini-quiz
  • When should you use WHERE instead of HAVING?
  • How do parentheses affect AND/OR evaluation?
  • Why is ORDER BY important before LIMIT when selecting top-N rows?
Key Takeaway

Design assessments that require both row-level filters and group-level filters to confirm mastery of WHERE vs HAVING and operator precedence.
