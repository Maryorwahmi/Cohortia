Errors to watch for
  • Forgetting single quotes around strings and dates causes syntax errors.
  • Mismatching column count and values will be rejected by the engine.
  • Violating UNIQUE or NOT NULL constraints results in insertion failure.
Common Mistake

Writing INSERT INTO Employees VALUES (3, John, Doe, ...); without quotes around John causes an error. Always use single quotes for string and date literals.
