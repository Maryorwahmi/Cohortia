Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Orchestration Responsibilities
Scheduling
Decide cadence: hourly, daily, or event-driven depending on data freshness needs.
Dependency Management
Ensure transforms and quality checks complete before loading to avoid inconsistent data.
Retries & Alerts
Configure safe retry policies and alert on persistent failures or SLA breaches.
Monitoring
Collect run metrics: run time, rows processed, error counts, and last success timestamp for each job.
Notification
Notify owners on failure and provide links to logs and run identifiers for quick diagnosis.
