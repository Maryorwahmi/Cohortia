Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › On‑Premise vs Cloud
On‑Premise Warehouse
  • High upfront CapEx for hardware and licenses.
  • Scaling requires procurement and installation.
  • Full operational responsibility: patching, backups, staffing.
  • Tight control over hardware and network locality.
Analogy
Owning a factory: you buy machines and maintain them.
Cloud Data Warehouse
  • Pay-as-you-go operational pricing; scale elastically.
  • Provider manages infrastructure, backups, and patching.
  • Compute and storage often scale independently.
  • Faster time-to-value and easier experimentation.
Analogy
Renting a managed facility with on-demand machines.
Misconception
Cloud is always more expensive. Cost depends on utilization and management choices.
Pro tip
Model peak load and idle hours to estimate real costs before provisioning.
Key takeaway
If you need elasticity and lower operational burden, cloud is often the better choice.
