Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Common Mistakes
Mistake
Keeping clusters always on.
Mitigation: schedule or autoscale compute to match workloads.
Mistake
Treating the warehouse as an OLTP system.
Mitigation: use batch appends and periodic compaction.
Mistake
Ignoring query distribution and network shuffles.
Mitigation: monitor query plans and rebalance partitions.
Example
Switching to scheduled compute for nightly ETL reduced monthly cost while preserving throughput.
