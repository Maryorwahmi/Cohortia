Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › DHCP troubleshooting: APIPA and command-line checks
Windows example
C:\> ipconfig /all
   DHCP Enabled . . . . . . . : Yes
   DHCP Server . . . . . . . : 10.0.0.2
   Lease Obtained. . . . . . : 1/1/2026 09:00:00
   Lease Expires . . . . . . : 1/2/2026 09:00:00

C:\> ipconfig /release
C:\> ipconfig /renew
Use these outputs to confirm whether the DHCP server responded and to force a new lease attempt.
Linux example
sudo dhclient -r eth0    # release
sudo dhclient eth0       # request new lease
ip a                     # show assigned address
Quick troubleshooting flow
Check local IP → release/renew → capture packets to see Discover/Offer → verify server and relay reachability.
