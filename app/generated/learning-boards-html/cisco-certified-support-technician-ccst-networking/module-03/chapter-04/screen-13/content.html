DORA
Discover, Offer, Request, Acknowledge — the four-step DHCP allocation process.
Scope & Lease
Scopes define pools; exclusions and lease duration control stability and turnover.
Troubleshooting
Use ipconfig, dhclient, and packet captures to trace DORA and confirm server reachability.
Summary takeaway

DHCP automates addressing but requires good scope planning, monitoring of leases, and protections against spoofing to keep networks reliable.
