EUI-64 explained
A standard way to convert a 48-bit MAC into a 64-bit interface identifier for SLAAC.
Step A: Split MAC
Divide the 48-bit MAC into two 24-bit halves.
Step B: Insert FFFE
Insert the fixed 16-bit value FFFE in the middle of the halves.
Step C: Flip the U/L bit
Toggle the 7th bit of the first octet to finalize the IID.
Worked example
MAC: 00-1A-2B-3C-4D-5E Split: 001A2B / 3C4D5E Insert FFFE: 001A:2BFF:FE3C:4D5E Flip 7th bit: final IID: 021A:2BFF:FE3C:4D5E
Interpretation
This IID appended to FE80::/64 produces the device LLA and can combine with an RA prefix to form a global address.
