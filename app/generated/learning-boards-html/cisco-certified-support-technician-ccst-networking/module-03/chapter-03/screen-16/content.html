Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Final Reminders
Check scope
Determine if an address is FE80, FC00, or 2000:: to direct troubleshooting steps.
Shorten safely
Omit leading zeros and use '::' once for the longest zero run; expand if unsure.
Trace SLAAC
Follow LLA → RS → RA → GUA formation to find where autoconfiguration may be failing.
Final takeaway
Practice reading and forming addresses until it becomes second nature; prefix recognition and the SLAAC flow are the fastest diagnostic tools you have.
