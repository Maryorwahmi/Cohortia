Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › OS commands
Windows
C:\> ipconfig
Ethernet adapter Ethernet:
   IPv4 Address. . . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.10
   Subnet Mask . . . . . . . . . . . : 255.255.255.0
   Default Gateway . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.1
Interpret the IPv4 Address, Mask, and Gateway to compute the network and verify connectivity.
Linux / macOS
$ ip a
3: eth0:  mtu 1500
   inet 192.168.1.10/24 brd 192.168.1.255 scope global eth0
CIDR notation gives the mask length directly. Use the brd field to see the broadcast address returned by the OS.
Quick test
Ping the default gateway to test local network reachability.
APIPA
If you see 169.254.x.x, DHCP failed — check DHCP and physical links.
Disabled
If no address appears, verify interface status and cabling before reconfiguring the host.
