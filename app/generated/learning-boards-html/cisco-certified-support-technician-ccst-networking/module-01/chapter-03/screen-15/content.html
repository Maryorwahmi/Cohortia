Quick Reminders
Three concise rules to use on the job.
Map symptom to layer
This guides which protocol to test first and prevents wasted effort.
Collect evidence
Save outputs from ipconfig/ip a, nslookup/dig, and arp -a before making changes.
Prefer standards
Vendor features can override expected behavior; revert to standard settings when diagnosing interoperability issues.
Final thought

Practice collection and mapping until it becomes routine: clear evidence and a testable hypothesis are the best tools in the field.
