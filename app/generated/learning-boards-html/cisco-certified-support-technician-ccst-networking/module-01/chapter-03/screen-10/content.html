Standards bodies you will meet
Each organization focuses on different layers and types of standards.
IEEE
Maintains link-layer standards such as Ethernet and Wi‑Fi families, ensuring consistent local network behavior across devices.
IETF
Publishes RFCs defining internet protocols within the TCP/IP suite, including behavior and implementation guidance.
ISO
Develops international standards and conceptual frameworks that help structure discussions about networking layers and design.
Practical note

When a device does not behave as expected, check whether vendor features override standard behavior. Reverting to standard settings often improves interoperability.
