Bloom Progression

01
Physical vs Logical
Distinguish and explain significance

You will define physical and logical topology and explain why both matter for tracing faults and predicting traffic patterns. Visual mnemonic: two overlapping circles labelled "cables" and "data flow".

Verified competency milestone
02
Common topologies
Describe bus, star, ring, mesh

You will list advantages and disadvantages for each physical topology and give an example use case for each.

Verified competency milestone
03
Hybrid topologies
Illustrate combined designs

You will show how combining topologies addresses limitations and where hybrids are appropriate in larger networks.

Verified competency milestone
04
Architectures
Compare client-server and P2P

You will select an architecture for two scenarios and justify your choice based on control, security, and budget trade-offs.

Verified competency milestone
How to use these objectives

Work through each lesson, then complete the two scenario designs to practice choosing topologies and intermediary devices. Use the assessment questions to check understanding of redundancy and cost trade-offs.

Quick mnemonic
Think: Physical = wires and placement; Logical = the rules that make data flow. The right mix reduces downtime and simplifies troubleshooting.
