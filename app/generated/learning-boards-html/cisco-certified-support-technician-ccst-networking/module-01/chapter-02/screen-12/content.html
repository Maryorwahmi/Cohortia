Checklist

Avoid these common mistakes

Single point of failure
Protect central devices or plan redundancy for critical segments.
Mismatched logical expectations
Verify VLANs and routing so logical flow matches operational needs.
Underestimated growth
Design for future device counts to avoid costly rework.
Overcomplex redundancy
Balance redundancy with manageability; more links increase configuration overhead.
Design process
  1. Gather uptime, security, and budget requirements.
  2. Map devices and critical services.
  3. Choose topology or hybrid that isolates non-critical devices and protects core services.
  4. Plan intermediary devices and redundancy where needed.
Concrete example

For a department that needs high uptime, choose a star for desks but a partial mesh for core switches. That protects essential services while keeping edge wiring manageable.

Key Takeaway

Design from requirements. Don’t pick a topology because it’s familiar — pick it because it meets uptime, budget, and management goals.
