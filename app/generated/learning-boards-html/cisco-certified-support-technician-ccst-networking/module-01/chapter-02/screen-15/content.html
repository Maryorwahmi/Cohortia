Network Topology
The arrangement of links and nodes that defines connectivity and paths.
Physical Topology
The actual layout of cables and devices used to locate hardware faults.
Logical Topology
How data flows across the network independent of wiring, driven by protocols and addressing.
Bus
Single backbone; simple but fragile and not scalable.
Star
Central switch with dedicated links; easy to manage, central device is critical.
Ring
Closed loop; needs redundancy to avoid outage from single link failure.
Mesh
Multiple paths between devices; high redundancy with higher cost and complexity.
Summary
When designing, list requirements first, then pick the simplest topology or hybrid that meets uptime, budget, and manageability goals. Protect central devices and plan redundancy where failure is unacceptable.
