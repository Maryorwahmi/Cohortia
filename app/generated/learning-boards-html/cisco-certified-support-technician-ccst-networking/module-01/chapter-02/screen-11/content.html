What it is

Peer-to-peer architecture

Devices share resources directly, each acting as client and server. Good for small groups and low cost, but weak on centralized management and backups.

Advantages
  • Low setup cost
  • Resilient to a single device failure affecting the whole network
Disadvantages
  • No centralized control for security or backups
  • Performance and management degrade as peers increase
Concrete example

A small 5-device home office shares files between workstations and a printer without a central server. It works well initially, but as staff numbers grow, consistent backups and access control become difficult.

Common misconception

Thinking P2P removes all administrative effort. Instead, management becomes distributed and harder to audit or secure across many devices.

Key Takeaway

P2P is a practical short-term choice for small budgets, but plan to centralize when control, backups, and security become priorities.
