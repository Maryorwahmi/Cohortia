Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Final Concepts
Client-Server
Central servers provide services to clients, easing backups, security and monitoring. Protect servers with redundancy and backups.
Peer-to-Peer
Peers share directly. Good for small budgets and quick setups, but difficult to manage as the network grows.
Design advice
Start with requirements, map critical services, pick the simplest topology that meets needs, and add redundancy where failure is unacceptable.
Final takeaway
Topology and architecture choices directly affect troubleshooting, resilience, and cost. Use star for manageability, mesh or partial mesh for critical redundancy, and client-server for centralized control. Always plan a path to migrate as needs grow.
