What it is

Hybrid topology — mixed designs

Combine topologies like star segments connected by a bus backbone or partial mesh between core switches to meet scale, manageability, and redundancy needs.

Design tip

Use simpler topologies where devices are numerous and managed locally, and reserve redundant links for critical backbone or core nodes.

Scenario illustration
Star segments
Each office floor uses a star to a local switch for easy troubleshooting and device isolation.
Backbone
Floor switches connect to a backbone that links to core routers; backbone may be a fiber bus or meshed core.
Concrete example

A medium-sized office uses star wiring on each floor and connects those floor switches to a partially meshed core so critical traffic can bypass a failed core link.

Key Takeaway

Hybrid designs let you apply the best topology to each part of the network and balance cost with reliability.
