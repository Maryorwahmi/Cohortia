What it is

Bus topology

All devices share a single backbone cable. Devices inspect shared traffic and accept packets addressed to them.

Advantages
  • Low initial cost for very small setups
  • Simple wiring for linear layouts
Disadvantages
  • Backbone failure disables the whole network
  • Performance drops as devices increase due to collisions
  • Hard to troubleshoot where the failure lies

Concrete example

An older lab used a shared coax backbone linking measurement devices. It worked for a few devices but became unreliable as more instruments joined and the cable aged.

Key Takeaway

Bus is simple and cheap at tiny scales but has a fragile single point of failure and poor scalability.
