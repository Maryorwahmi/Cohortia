Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Scenario: Department
Requirements
  • 25 devices, 3 servers, 2 printers
  • High uptime and easy scalability
Recommended topology

Star segments per zone connecting to a partially meshed core for redundancy and alternate paths.

Recommended architecture

Client-server to centralize services, security, and backups for the department’s servers.

Intermediary devices
  • Managed switches for each segment with VLAN support
  • Core switches with partial mesh and redundant uplinks
  • Router for connection to main company network and WAN
Justification

This design isolates edge devices, keeps local troubleshooting simple, and protects essential services with redundant core links and centralized servers for security and backups.

Key Takeaway

Use star segments with a resilient core and client-server architecture to balance manageability, uptime, and future growth.
