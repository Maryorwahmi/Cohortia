Network Topologies and Architectures

Learn how physical layout and logical data flow shape reliability, scaling, and troubleshooting choices for LANs and small networks.

In this lesson
Differentiate physical and logical topology and apply common topology trade-offs when designing or troubleshooting.
Coming up
Examine Bus, Star, Ring, Mesh, hybrid options and client-server versus P2P.
Practice
Two scenario designs: small home office and department network with device lists and justifications.
Why this matters

Topology and architecture choices determine where faults appear, how hard they are to fix, and what equipment is required. Good choices reduce downtime and support efficient troubleshooting.

Physical

Logical

Example: token passing that makes traffic behave like a ring even if wiring is a star.
