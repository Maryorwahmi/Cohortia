Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Practice Goals
Hands-on activity
Design topologies for two scenarios, draw simple diagrams, and list intermediary devices. Focus on justifying trade-offs and identifying a likely failure mode for each design.
Scenario 1
Small home office — 5 devices

You will choose a topology and architecture that balances budget and troubleshootability, and list required devices such as a Wi‑Fi router or switch.

Scenario 2
Medium department — 25 devices

You will choose a design that prioritizes uptime and scalability, and identify managed switches, routers, and possible backbone connections to the main network.

Assessment tips
  • Describe the single point of failure for a topology.
  • Explain why a hybrid design might be chosen for a large space.
  • Compare client-server and peer-to-peer trade-offs for management and security.
