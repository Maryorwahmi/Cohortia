What it is

Ring topology — closed loop

Devices connect in a loop and data normally travels in one direction. Rings avoid collisions through controlled access methods but depend on integrity of the loop.

Advantages
  • Orderly access can reduce collisions
  • Can be made redundant with dual rings
Disadvantages
  • Single break disrupts the loop unless redundancy is present
  • Adding devices can be disruptive
Illustration
Data circulates around the loop. If the loop is broken, traffic may stop unless a secondary ring or reroute is available.
Concrete example

Token-based networks used ring logic to ensure only one device sent at a time. Modern networks reproduce ring-like behaviour logically when needed, but physical rings are uncommon without redundancy.

Common mistake

Assuming a single ring is redundant. Without dual rings or failover, a single break stops the entire loop.
