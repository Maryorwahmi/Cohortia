Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Scenario: Home Office
Requirements
  • 5 devices need file sharing and internet access
  • Budget sensitive and easy troubleshooting desired
Recommended topology

Star topology to a router or small switch — isolates faults and is easy to extend.

Recommended architecture

Peer-to-peer for immediate low cost; add a NAS for centralized backups when needed.

Intermediary devices
  • Wi‑Fi router with wired ports or an unmanaged switch
  • Ethernet cables (Cat5e or Cat6 as budget allows)
  • Optional: NAS for backups and central storage
Justification

A star makes troubleshooting simple and is inexpensive. Peer-to-peer starts quickly; the NAS adds central backups without major redesign later.

Key Takeaway

For five devices, star with a small router and P2P or NAS meets budget and troubleshooting goals.
