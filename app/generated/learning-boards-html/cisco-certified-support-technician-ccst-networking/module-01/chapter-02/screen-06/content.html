What it is

Star topology with a central switch

Every device connects independently to a central switch or hub. Modern LANs use switches to reduce collisions and isolate faults.

Advantages
  • Fault isolation per cable or device
  • Easy to add devices and troubleshoot
Disadvantages
  • Central device is a single point of failure
  • Requires more cabling than a bus
Illustration
Each device has a dedicated cable to a switch. If one cable fails, only that device is affected. If the switch fails, the entire segment loses connectivity.
Concrete example

A typical home router with four wired ports and Wi‑Fi forms a star: devices each have a link to the router or switch. Replacing a faulty cable usually restores only that device.

Key Takeaway

Star gives simple management and isolation; protect the central device if uptime is required.
