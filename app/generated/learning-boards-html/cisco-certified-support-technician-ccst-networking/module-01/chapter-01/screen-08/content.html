Media categories
Copper (Ethernet)
Common and inexpensive for short distances; good for desktop connections.
Fiber-optic
High speed and long distance; used for backbones and provider links.
Wireless (Radio)
Offers mobility; requires careful planning for coverage and security.
Decision factors
  • Distance: fiber for long runs, copper for short runs.
  • Speed: fiber typically offers higher throughput.
  • Cost and installation complexity: copper is cheaper and easier to terminate.
  • Mobility and environment: wireless is flexible but sensitive to interference.
Example
Use fiber for a campus backbone, copper for office desks, and WLAN for guests and mobile staff. This combination balances cost and performance.
