Primary intermediary devices
Switch
Connects devices within a LAN and forwards traffic to the correct port using learned addresses.
Router
Connects different networks and determines the best path between them.
Firewall
Inspects and controls traffic according to security rules to protect resources.
Wireless access point (WAP)
Allows wireless devices to connect to the wired network.
Example architecture
Office LAN: end devices connect to switches; switches connect to a router; the router connects to an ISP WAN. A firewall protects the internal services while WAPs offer wireless connectivity for laptops and phones.
Troubleshooting hint: If everyone in the office loses external internet but local file sharing still works, check the router or WAN link first.
Common error: Rebooting a switch when the problem is the router. Identify the scope of impact before power cycling devices.
Key takeaway
Each intermediary device serves a clear role: switching, routing, securing, or bridging wireless clients. Use that role to guide tests and fixes.
