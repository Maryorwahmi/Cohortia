What is a Network?
Discover how devices, media, and intermediary systems work together to enable communication and resource sharing across small spaces and across the globe.
In this lesson
Define networks, identify end and intermediary devices, choose media, and distinguish LAN, WAN, WLAN, and PAN.
Why it matters
Understanding these basics helps you diagnose faults quickly and design networks that meet performance and security needs.
