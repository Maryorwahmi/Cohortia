Guided checks
  • Run ipconfig/ifconfig to confirm addresses.
  • Look for link lights on switches and NICs.
  • Note whether devices act as bridges, routers, or endpoints.
Common gotcha
Devices like all‑in‑one routers can fulfill multiple roles; document each role separately rather than forcing a single category.
