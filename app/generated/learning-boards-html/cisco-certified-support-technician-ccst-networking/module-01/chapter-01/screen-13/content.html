Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Mistakes & Assessment
Common mistakes
  • Confusing switches and routers; they serve different scopes.
  • Assuming internet failure without checking local connectivity first.
  • Not documenting multifunction devices' roles, causing future confusion.
Assessment ideas
  • Multiple choice: Identify LAN vs WAN and which device connects to the internet.
  • Hands-on: Record ipconfig/ifconfig output and explain what the gateway indicates.
  • Practical: Map a small office and classify each device and connection type.
Study timeline
Learn definitions → identify components → practice commands and mapping → apply troubleshooting sequence on real or simulated issues.
