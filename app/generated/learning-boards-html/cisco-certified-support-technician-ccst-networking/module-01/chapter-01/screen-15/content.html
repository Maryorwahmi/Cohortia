Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
Start at the device
Check power, link lights, and IP settings before moving to network gear.
Label device roles
Document whether a device is an end device, a switch, a router, or multiple roles.
Choose media by need
Match copper, fiber, or wireless to distance, speed, and mobility requirements.
Reflection prompt
Identify one nearby device and describe its role and connection type. Say it aloud to reinforce the habit.
