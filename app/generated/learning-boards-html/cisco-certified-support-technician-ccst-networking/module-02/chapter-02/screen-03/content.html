Diagnostic sequence
A repeatable order of tests helps you find the failing layer fast.
Step 1
Check interface configuration with ipconfig or ifconfig to confirm IP, mask, gateway, and MAC.
Step 2
Ping loopback, gateway, and external IPs to separate local stack, link, and internet faults.
Step 3
Use tracert or traceroute to find the hop where packets stop and narrow the fault location.
Example
ping 127.0.0.1
ping 192.168.1.1
ping 8.8.8.8
tracert www.cisco.com
