Process Flow

Five-step checklist

A repeatable order that maps tests to OSI layers so you find root causes faster.

Step 1
Physical checks: cables and link lights.
Step 2
Data Link: MAC tables, VLANs, switch ports.
Step 3
Network: IP, mask, gateway, routing.
Step 4
Transport/Session: ping, port checks, retransmissions.
Step 5
Application: DNS, HTTP, app logs and configs.
Concrete scenario

User can't access website but can reach network printer: follow steps 1–5 and you'll likely find DNS or gateway issues at Network/Application layers.

Key Takeaway

A stepwise mapping to layers reduces wasted work and points you to the right diagnostic tools.
