Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Physical Layer
Definition

Bits and the transmission medium

Frames are turned into signals matched to the physical medium so they can travel between devices.

Examples of media
  • Copper: electrical voltages encode bit sequences.
  • Fiber: light pulses represent bits across long distances.
  • Wireless: radio waves carry modulated symbols as bits.
Practical signal concerns
  • Signal loss and noise can corrupt frames and trigger FCS failures.
  • Link negotiation and speed mismatches produce poor connectivity.
Common mistake
Assuming packet loss is always a routing or application issue; physical problems often cause dropped frames before higher layers can react.
Key Takeaway
Always confirm physical link health and frame integrity when diagnosing unexplained packet loss.
