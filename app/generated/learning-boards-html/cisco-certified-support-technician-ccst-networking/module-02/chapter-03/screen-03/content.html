Skills & Troubleshooting

Targeted tasks to practice after learning the encapsulation workflow.

Interpret packet captures

Locate the transport, network, and link headers in a trace and explain what each field means for delivery.

Verified Competency Milestone
Pinpoint layer faults

Match symptoms to layers — FCS failures, routing errors, or port mismatches — and pick the right diagnostic tool.

Verified Competency Milestone
Explain device roles

Describe why routers decapsulate to the IP level and switches operate at MAC level, and how that affects packet flow.

Verified Competency Milestone
Recreate a flow diagram

Sketch an HTTP request’s encapsulation path and show header additions and removals for each hop.

Verified Competency Milestone
Practical tip: When you practice, always label the PDU and one key header field to ensure you look at the correct layer first during troubleshooting.
