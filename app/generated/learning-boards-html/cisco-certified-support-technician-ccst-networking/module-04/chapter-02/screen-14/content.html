Key Concepts

Three concise reminders to carry forward into troubleshooting and design.

Hub
Layer 1 repeater that broadcasts to all ports and creates one collision domain.
Switch
Layer 2 device that uses a MAC table to forward frames and isolates collision domains per port.
Router
Layer 3 device that forwards by IP using a routing table and separates broadcast domains by interface.
Summary

Use switches to reduce collisions and routers to connect networks and isolate broadcasts. Verify host addressing, ARP, switch MAC tables, and router routes when troubleshooting.
