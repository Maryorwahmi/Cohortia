Core Concept

Hub: Layer 1 repeater

A hub regenerates a physical signal to all ports and does not inspect MAC or IP addresses. All attached devices share a single collision domain and bandwidth.

Behavior
Receives signal on one port, repeats to all others; no learning or forwarding intelligence.
Misconception
Regenerating signals does not equal directed delivery — hubs increase collisions rather than resolving them.
Example
Imagine ten workstations and a server plugged into a hub. When two users print or copy large files at the same time, frames collide and both hosts must retransmit. The server's transfers slow for everyone.
Key takeaway
Why it matters

Hubs are inappropriate where multiple devices transmit concurrently; modern LANs use switches to reduce collisions and improve throughput.

Check your understanding
  • Does your network suffer when many users transfer large files? A hub would worsen it.
  • If you need port-by-port isolation, a hub cannot provide it.
