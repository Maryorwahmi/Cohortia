Definition

Collision domain

A collision domain contains devices whose frames can collide if transmitted at the same time. Collisions force retransmissions and lower throughput.

Cause
Simultaneous transmissions on the same physical medium.
Resolution
Use switches with separate ports and full-duplex links to eliminate collisions between hosts.
Concrete example
A shared hub with 8 workstations will force all traffic over the same medium. If two users copy files at once, collisions occur and throughput drops. Replace the hub with a switch and each port becomes its own collision domain, allowing simultaneous transfers without collisions.
Misconception check
Collision domains are not the same as broadcast domains; they are about simultaneous access, not reachability of broadcast frames.
Key takeaway
To fix frequent retransmits, reduce collision domains with switching and full-duplex connections.
