Key Concepts

Three technical reminders for everyday troubleshooting.

MAC Address Table
Maps MAC addresses to switch ports; essential for targeted frame forwarding and diagnosing flooding.
Routing Table
Contains destinations and next hops; missing routes cause dropped packets or reliance on a default route.
Broadcast Domain
Logical area where broadcasts propagate; routers or VLANs are needed to contain broadcast traffic between groups.
Final takeaway

Use MAC and routing tables to verify device knowledge, and design with broadcast domain boundaries in mind to prevent unwanted traffic spread.
