Common mistakes

Mistake: using a hub for busy LANs
Hubs create a single collision domain causing retransmits and poor throughput; a Layer 2 switch is the correct replacement.
Mistake: confusing switch with router
Switches do not separate broadcast domains by default. Use a router or VLANs plus inter-VLAN routing to separate broadcasts between networks.
Key takeaway

Carefully identify whether a performance problem is due to collisions or improper routing and pick the device accordingly.
