Comparison

Full-duplex vs half-duplex

Full-duplex lets a port send and receive simultaneously, eliminating collisions. Half-duplex shares the medium and can produce collisions without switching mechanisms that prevent them.

Why use full-duplex
Improves throughput and removes collision-based retransmits on switched links.

VLANs and broadcast domains

VLANs let one physical switch host multiple broadcast domains logically. Devices in different VLANs cannot reach each other's broadcasts unless routed.

Example
Finance VLAN isolates broadcast announcements from a Guest VLAN, reducing unnecessary traffic and improving security.
Misconception
A switch without VLANs still has a single broadcast domain. VLANs must be configured to split broadcasts.
