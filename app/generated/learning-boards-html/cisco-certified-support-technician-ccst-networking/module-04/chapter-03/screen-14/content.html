Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Wireless Access Point
Bridges wireless and wired segments, manages association, and broadcasts SSID.
2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz
2.4 GHz = range, more interference. 5 GHz = speed, more channels, shorter range.
WPA2 / WPA3
Use AES‑based WPA2 or WPA3; prefer Enterprise 802.1X for organizations.
Summary takeaway

Match AP capabilities and bands to your environment, secure networks with current protocols, and use a controller when managing many APs.
