Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › 2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz
Band characteristics
2.4 GHz
Longer range, better penetration, fewer non‑overlapping channels, higher interference risk from consumer devices.
5 GHz
Higher throughput, more channels, less interference, shorter range and lower penetration through walls.
Practical guidance
  • Prefer 5 GHz for high‑bandwidth devices in areas with good coverage.
  • Keep 2.4 GHz available for long‑range or legacy devices that cannot use 5 GHz.
  • Manually select non‑overlapping channels on 2.4 GHz (1, 6, 11) if interference is observed.
Analogy
2.4 GHz is like a crowded long road; 5 GHz is like a faster, less crowded highway with shorter exits.
