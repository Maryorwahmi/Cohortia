Spatial streams and efficiency
MIMO provides parallel spatial streams between AP and client. MU‑MIMO lets an AP serve multiple clients concurrently. OFDMA subdivides a channel for many small allocations.
MIMO
Multiple antennas increase per‑client throughput if both AP and client support it.
MU‑MIMO
AP serves several clients at once using different spatial streams, improving aggregate performance.
OFDMA
Divides channel resources so many clients get small allocations simultaneously, reducing contention.
Concrete example
In a classroom with fifty student devices each sending small updates, OFDMA lets the AP allocate small resource units to many clients at once, lowering latency compared to one‑by‑one servicing.
Misconception
MIMO alone guarantees speed. In reality clients must also support the feature and the environment must permit independent spatial paths.
Key Takeaway

Choose APs with MU‑MIMO and OFDMA for dense client areas to improve fairness and latency.
