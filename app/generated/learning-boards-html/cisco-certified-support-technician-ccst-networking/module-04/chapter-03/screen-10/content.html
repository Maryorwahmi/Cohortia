Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Wireless LAN Controller
What a WLC does
  • Centralizes AP configuration and SSID management.
  • Pushes firmware updates and security policies to many APs.
  • Performs RF management: channel planning and power tuning.
Example
Changing the corporate SSID or RADIUS settings on the WLC updates all associated APs automatically rather than editing each AP individually.
Practical benefits
Centralized monitoring and consistent policy enforcement reduce mistakes and speed up maintenance. Automated RF tuning helps minimize co‑channel interference across an area.
Common mistake
Assuming a WLC removes the need for site surveys. Proper placement and capacity planning are still required before relying on automatic tuning.
Key Takeaway

Use a WLC to manage scale and consistency, but still plan AP placement carefully.
