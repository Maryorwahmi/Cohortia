Skills Checklist
Small mnemonics and milestones to guide practice and verification.
Bridge & Broadcast
WAPs convert radio to wired and broadcast an SSID for clients.
Band vs. Crowd
2.4 GHz goes further but is crowded; 5 GHz is faster with shorter range.
Drop WEP
Use WPA2 or WPA3; WEP is insecure and obsolete.
Centralize with WLC
Use a Wireless LAN Controller to manage many APs and push policies.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE • Perform SSID change and enable WPA2 or WPA3 on a home router; run a channel scan to identify crowded frequencies.
Mnemonic aid
Visualize two roads: a crowded long road for 2.4 GHz and a shorter fast road for 5 GHz. Match device needs to the road you choose.
Why this matters: using the right band and security keeps networks fast and safe. Centralized tools reduce human error when you scale.
