Learning Objectives
Bloom progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain WAP role
Describe how a WAP converts radio signals to wired frames and why SSIDs are broadcast. Competency: explain a basic network link from device to switch.
02
Map Wi‑Fi standards
Identify 802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax and their bands. Competency: match a device standard to an appropriate band for performance.
03
Apply wireless security
Choose WPA2 or WPA3 and know difference between Personal and Enterprise. Competency: configure a secure passphrase and mode.
04
Describe WLC benefits
Explain how a WLC centralizes configuration and RF management. Competency: list three management tasks a controller performs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE • Demonstrate SSID configuration and enable WPA2 or WPA3 on a home router.
