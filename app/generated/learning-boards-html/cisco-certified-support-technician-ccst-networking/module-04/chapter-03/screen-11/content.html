Step-by-step troubleshooting
  1. Measure signal strength (RSSI) and confirm client location.
  2. Verify SSID and WPA/WPA2/WPA3 settings are correct.
  3. Scan channels; choose non‑overlapping channels on 2.4 GHz.
  4. Check AP placement and reduce physical obstructions.
  5. Review firmware and WLC logs for errors or misconfigurations.
Common sources of interference
  • Other Wi‑Fi networks on the same channel in 2.4 GHz.
  • Non‑Wi‑Fi devices like microwaves and cordless phones.
  • Dense client environments with unmanaged channels.
Quick fix
If short on time, run a channel scan and change to a less congested 2.4 GHz channel or move high‑bandwidth devices to 5 GHz when possible.
Key Takeaway

Use a repeatable checklist: signal, security, channels, placement, firmware, logs.
