Physical-layer troubleshooting
1
Check link lights
Confirm LED activity on NIC and switch port; dark LEDs indicate no physical link.
2
Reseat and inspect
Unplug and reseat RJ45, inspect crimps and ensure all 8 wires are visible in the connector.
3
Swap the cable or port
Try a known-good cable and different switch port to rule out a faulty cable or jack.
4
Check drivers & NIC state
If link persists down, confirm NIC is enabled and drivers are present in the operating system.
Scenario
A server shows 'No network'. Both NIC and switch LEDs are dark. You reseat the cable and replace it with a known-good cable; the link lights turn green and connectivity returns. This resolves the majority of similar incidents.
Key Takeaway

Start with physical checks. They are fast, low-risk, and fix most connectivity failures.
