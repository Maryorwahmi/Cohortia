Twisted-pair & RJ45
Twisted pairs reduce interference; RJ45 is the standard connector for Ethernet runs in LANs.
Physical cues
Cable jacket markings indicate category; thicker jackets and tighter twists often signal higher categories.
Common misconception
Assuming any Ethernet cable will deliver top speeds. Category and run length limit signal quality and achievable throughput.
Example: selecting cable
If planning a 90-meter office run for Gigabit Ethernet, Cat5e is sufficient. For 10 Gigabit needs, Cat6a is required for the full 100 meters; Cat6 supports 10 Gbps only at shorter distances.
Why this matters

Choosing the wrong cable can cause permanent bottlenecks or intermittent connectivity that is hard to diagnose remotely.
