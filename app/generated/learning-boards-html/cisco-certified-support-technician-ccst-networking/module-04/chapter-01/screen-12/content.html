Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Inspection Tips
What to inspect
  • Jacket: cuts, severe bends, or kinks.
  • RJ45 plug: all 8 wires seated; jacket clamped under crimp.
  • Fiber: check connectors for chips or cracks; avoid looking into live ends.
Link-light interpretations
No light = no physical link. Amber or lower-speed lights often mean negotiation or cable limitations. Green steady indicates an active link at negotiated speed.
Practical step

Before changing drivers or configs, swap the cable and test a different port to isolate the fault to the physical layer.
