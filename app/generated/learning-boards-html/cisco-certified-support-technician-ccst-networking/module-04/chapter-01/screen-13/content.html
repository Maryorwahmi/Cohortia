Cable Identification & Verification
Step 1 — Identify
Read jacket markings for Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a and inspect connectors for LC/SC/ST on fiber.
Step 2 — Inspect termination
Confirm all 8 copper wires are fully seated and jacket is clamped under the plug; check fiber ferrules for damage.
Step 3 — Simulate troubleshooting
For 'No network', check link lights, reseat/replace cable, and then verify NIC enablement if needed.
Instructor tip

Practice the three-step flow in a lab so it becomes a fast reflex on real incidents: link lights, reseat/replace, then check drivers.
