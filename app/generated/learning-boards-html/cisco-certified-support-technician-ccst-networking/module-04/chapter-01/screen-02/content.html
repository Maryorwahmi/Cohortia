Learning Objectives
Bloom targets: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify NIC function
Name NIC roles in preparing frames and receiving data, and know why a MAC address is essential for local delivery.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Distinguish Ethernet cables
Select Cat5e, Cat6 or Cat6a based on required speed and distance and recognize physical cues and markings.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Recognize fiber types & connectors
Understand single-mode vs multi-mode tradeoffs and identify LC, SC and ST connectors by shape and latch.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Explain common issues & troubleshooting
Perform first-response checks: link lights, reseat or replace cables, inspect terminations, and verify drivers if needed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
