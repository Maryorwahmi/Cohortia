Why fiber?
Light-based transmission provides higher bandwidth, longer distances, and immunity to EMI compared with copper.
Single-mode
Very small core, single light path, minimizes loss — used for long campus or WAN links.
Multi-mode
Larger core supporting multiple modes; suitable for data centers and short building backbones.
Safety note
Never look into an active fiber. Laser light can harm your eyes even if invisible.
Connector examples
  • LC — small form factor, common in modern equipment.
  • SC — larger square connector with push-pull latch.
  • ST — bayonet-style twist locking connector used in some legacy systems.
Practical example

Use multi-mode with short data center runs and single-mode for inter-building or campus backbones that span many kilometers.
