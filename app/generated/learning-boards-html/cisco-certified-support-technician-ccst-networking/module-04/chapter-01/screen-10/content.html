Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Fiber Comparison
Single-mode
Small core, single light path, minimal dispersion; used for long-distance, high-bandwidth links.
Connector notes
Often paired with LC or SC transceivers on long-haul optics.
Multi-mode
Larger core with multiple modes; cost-effective for short runs in buildings and data centers.
Connector notes
Commonly uses LC, SC or ST depending on equipment; check connector compatibility before mating.
Connector shapes
LC
Small form-factor with latch; widely used today.
SC
Square push-pull connector common on many transceivers.
ST
Bayonet twist connector seen on older gear.
