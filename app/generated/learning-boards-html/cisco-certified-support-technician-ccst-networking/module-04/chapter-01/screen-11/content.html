Inspecting RJ45 terminations
Visual and simple electrical checks to validate a crimped cable.
  • All 8 wires visible at the plug face and fully inserted.
  • Jacket clamped under crimp, not cut into the conductor pairs.
  • Maintain pair twists as close to the termination as possible.
Common mistake

Incorrect wiring order or poor crimping causes intermittent errors or no link; replacing a suspect cable often resolves the issue.

Wiring standards
Use T568A or T568B consistently across the network. If you must cross a cable intentionally, understand it changes pair mappings.
Field example
A desktop with intermittent drops was fixed when a technician noticed a loose pair in the RJ45 plug; re-terminating the cable restored stable Gigabit connectivity.
