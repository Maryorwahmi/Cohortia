Practice Checklist
Actions you will perform and verify
NIC verification
  • Locate NIC and note MAC address label.
  • Confirm NIC enabled in OS and link light activity.
  • Relate MAC to local switch forwarding behaviour.
Copper cable checks
  • Read cable jacket for Cat markings and inspect crimps.
  • Confirm RJ45 has all 8 wires visible and jacket clamped.
  • Replace suspect cable with known-good spare before deeper troubleshooting.
Fiber safety & inspection
  • Never look into a live fiber. Handle connectors gently.
  • Identify LC, SC, ST connectors by form factor before mating.
  • Check for bends or visible damage in the jacket.
Field troubleshooting flow
  1. Check link lights.
  2. Reseat or replace cable.
  3. Confirm drivers and NIC enablement if link persists down.
