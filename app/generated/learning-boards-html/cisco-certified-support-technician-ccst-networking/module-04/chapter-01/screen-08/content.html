Cat5e · Cat6 · Cat6a
Cat5e
Supports 1 Gbps up to 100 meters. Common for general office wiring where Gigabit is required.
Cat6
Better crosstalk control; supports 1 Gbps to 100 m and 10 Gbps up to ~55 meters under typical conditions.
Cat6a
Augmented design to support 10 Gbps across the full 100-meter distance; preferred for 10 Gbps backbones.
Example
A 75-meter backbone needing 10 Gbps requires Cat6a; Cat6 would be unreliable at that length for 10 Gbps.
Common mistake
Choosing Cat6 for future-proofing without accounting for run length can limit achievable speeds.
