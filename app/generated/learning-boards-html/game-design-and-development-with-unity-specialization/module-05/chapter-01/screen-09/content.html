Frustum Culling
Built-in: objects outside camera frustum are not rendered.
This handles simple off-screen geometry automatically and requires no setup, but it cannot skip objects that are within the frustum but occluded behind other geometry.
Example
A distant mountain outside the camera frustum is ignored by frustum culling.
Occlusion Culling
Configured and baked: prevents rendering of objects hidden behind occluders even if inside the frustum.
Use when scenes have many hidden objects, like indoor levels with walls and closed doors. Bake occlusion data in the editor and test to confirm runtime savings.
Key Takeaway

Occlusion culling is powerful in occlusion-rich scenes but offers little benefit in open worlds. Always measure after baking.
