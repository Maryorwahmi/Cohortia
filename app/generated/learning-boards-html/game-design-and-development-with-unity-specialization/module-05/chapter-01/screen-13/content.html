Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Profiling Workflow and Common Mistakes
Iterative checklist
  1. Record a baseline profile under realistic gameplay conditions.
  2. Identify the dominant subsystem or repeated spike causing visible hitches.
  3. Apply a single targeted fix and collect a new profile for comparison.
  4. Repeat until the performance target is reached.
Pitfalls to avoid
  • Optimizing without profiling and fixing areas that aren’t the bottleneck.
  • Not sharing material references, which blocks batching opportunities.
  • Calling GetComponent in Update without caching results.
  • Letting pools expand at runtime instead of pre-warming to expected peak.
Key Takeaway

Use small, measurable optimizations guided by profiler data and bake verification into your workflow.
