Unity Profiler
Record frames and drill into Main Thread, GPU and Memory to find the hot path.
Draw Calls & Batching
Reduce CPU submission overhead with static batching for stationary objects and dynamic for small movers.
Object Pooling
Pre-instantiate and reuse objects to prevent allocation spikes and reduce GC hitches.
Remember
Measure first, prioritize the largest bottleneck, apply a focused fix, and re-measure to confirm improvement.
