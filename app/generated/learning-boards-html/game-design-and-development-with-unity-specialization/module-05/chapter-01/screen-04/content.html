Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Why Performance Matters
What is performance?
Consistent frame times, low load times, and predictable memory behavior that preserve control and immersion.
Example
A scene that drops from 60 to 15 FPS during explosions causes missed inputs and feels uncompetitive compared with steady 30 FPS.
Common misconception
High graphical settings are not always the cause; heavy scripting or frequent allocations can make the CPU the bottleneck.
Key Takeaway

Measure and target the dominant cause of instability; small fixes to the hot path produce the largest gains.

Why players notice it
  • Frame drops introduce input lag and break timing-critical mechanics.
  • Hitches caused by garbage collection interrupt animation and audio, creating abrupt pauses.
  • Load time delays increase churn and lower first-impression retention.
Reflection
Consider a game you played that stuttered. Was the issue sustained or episodic? That pattern suggests either a continuous bottleneck like too many draw calls, or an occasional allocation spike from repeated creation and destruction.
