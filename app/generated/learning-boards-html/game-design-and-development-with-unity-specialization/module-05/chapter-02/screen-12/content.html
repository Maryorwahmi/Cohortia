Best Practice

Profile, act, verify

Use the Unity Profiler and frame timing to determine whether you're CPU- or GPU-bound. On GPU-bound frames, inspect post-processing and expensive shaders first.
  • Profile on target hardware — desktop profiling alone can hide mobile bottlenecks.
  • Disable effects one at a time to measure real impact.
  • Consider baking or simplifying shaders rather than adding more per-pixel math.
Common pitfalls include enabling many medium-cost effects, using high-resolution shadows for distant lights, and forgetting to use LODs.
Optimization tip: reduce shader complexity for distant or numerous objects and bake lighting where appropriate.
Key Takeaway

Measure before optimizing, change one thing at a time, and validate improvements on the hardware you care about most.
