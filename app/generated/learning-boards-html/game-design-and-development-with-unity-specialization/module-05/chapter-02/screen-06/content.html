Core effects

Bloom • AO • Color Grading

  • Bloom: simulates light bleed, great for highlights but can blow out details if overdone.
  • Ambient Occlusion: enhances contact shadows, adds perceived depth in crevices.
  • Color Grading: shifts overall palette; a powerful tool for mood and narrative tone.
  • Vignette & Depth of Field: guide attention and create cinematic focus.
Implementation

In URP and HDRP, post-processing lives in Volume Profiles attached to Volume components. A Global Volume applies effects to the whole scene; local volumes can affect areas selectively.

Example
Create a Global Volume, add Bloom with low threshold and warm Color Grading to push a dusk-time palette. Measure the frame cost before and after to validate the change.
Common mistake

Turning on many effects at high quality without profiling leads to a GPU-bound scenario and can harm gameplay fluidity.
