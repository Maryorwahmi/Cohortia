Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › URP vs HDRP
URP

Performance-first

URP favors speed and compatibility across platforms. It is the common choice for multi-platform projects and titles that need predictable performance.

Good for mobile, VR, and cross-platform builds.
Less setup complexity and lower GPU cost than HDRP.
Requires converting materials to URP formats during migration.
HDRP

Fidelity-first

HDRP unlocks advanced lighting, PBR quality, volumetrics, and high-quality shadows. It targets powerful hardware where visual realism is the priority.

Great for AAA visuals and professional renderings.
Higher GPU cost and more pipeline-specific setup.
Not suitable for low-end mobile platforms.
