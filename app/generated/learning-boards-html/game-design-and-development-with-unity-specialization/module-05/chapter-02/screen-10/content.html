Core idea

Vertex & Fragment stages

Vertex calculations transform geometry; fragment calculations determine pixel colors and lighting response. The cost mostly depends on per-pixel work and texture lookups.

Shader Graph
A node-based editor that produces shader code from visually connected nodes, making it approachable for designers and artists.
Example

Rim light via Shader Graph: compute dot product between view and normal, invert and power it to accentuate edges, then add to emission. This creates a silhouette glow without complex geometry.

Performance note
Complex node graphs with many texture samples or high-frequency math increase per-pixel cost. Optimize by baking effects into textures where possible.
