Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
URP vs HDRP
Choose URP for scale, HDRP for fidelity based on platform and goals.
Post-Processing
Bloom, AO, and grading alter mood but add GPU cost; profile changes.
Baking & Probes
Bake static lights to lightmaps; use light probes and reflection probes for dynamic objects and reflections.
Takeaway
Match the pipeline, tune a few post-processing effects, and optimize lighting with baking and probes for the best balance of visual quality and performance.
