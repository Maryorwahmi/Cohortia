Light Probes

Light probes sample baked indirect lighting at discrete points so dynamic objects can receive believable indirect illumination without being baked themselves.

Place probes where dynamic objects travel, especially near occluders and corners.
Too few probes can cause sudden lighting pops; too many can slow bake times.
Reflection Probes

Reflection probes capture environment reflections to simulate glossy materials without real-time ray tracing.

Use box projection and blend distances to smooth transitions between probes.
Refresh probe captures when the environment changes significantly, or bake them for static scenes.
Practical example
Place probes along a corridor and near reflective surfaces; move a dynamic character through Play Mode to confirm lighting interpolation and reflection blending feel natural.
