Tilemaps & Level Design

Create layered 2D worlds by painting tiles onto a Grid and enabling efficient collisions and behaviors.

In this lesson
Build tile palettes, paint multi-layered levels, and add optimized collisions.
Why it matters
Speed up level creation, ensure consistent collisions, and simplify iteration.
Hands-on
Paint a platformer ground, add colliders, and test player collisions.

Grid & Tilemap

Colliders & Optimization
Use TilemapCollider2D + CompositeCollider2D for efficient physics on large maps.
