Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Per-tile vs Composite
Per-tile Colliders
Generates individual colliders for every painted tile. Highly precise for small, interactive tiles.
Pros: simple, exact. Cons: many colliders cause physics overhead on large maps.
When to use
Small maps or tiles where per-tile collision behavior is required.
CompositeCollider2D
Merges many small colliders into a single optimized shape when Used By Composite is enabled on the TilemapCollider2D.
Pros: performance. Cons: slightly less per-tile segmentation.
When to use
Large contiguous ground layers and production builds where physics performance matters.
Key Takeaway

Prototype with per-tile colliders if helpful; convert solid ground to CompositeCollider2D before shipping to reduce physics cost.
