Rigidbody rule
At least one colliding object needs a Rigidbody2D for events to fire.
Collider choice
Use box/circle for performance; polygon for necessary precision; edge for terrain lines.
Physics timing
Apply forces and movement in FixedUpdate using Rigidbody2D APIs for stability.
Final takeaway

These three rules solve most common 2D physics issues and keep your gameplay predictable and performant.
