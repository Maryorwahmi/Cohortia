2D Physics & Collisions
Rigidbody2D, Collider2D, physics materials, and collision callbacks for 2D games.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Why this matters

Physics makes movement feel natural, supports gameplay like jumping and pushing, and keeps interactions predictable across scenes and devices.

Common impact

Choosing the right collider and body type affects performance. Use simple shapes where possible to reduce CPU cost.

Starter example

A platformer player uses a Dynamic Rigidbody2D and BoxCollider2D, ground uses a BoxCollider2D, and coins use CircleCollider2D set as triggers to detect pickups without physical bounce.
