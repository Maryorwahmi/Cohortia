Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › FixedUpdate
Physics timing and stability
Make physics changes in FixedUpdate to match the solver's timestep.
Why FixedUpdate?
Physics runs at fixed intervals; applying forces there keeps behavior deterministic and reduces jitter.
Kinematic movement
Use Rigidbody2D.MovePosition in FixedUpdate to move kinematic bodies smoothly and interact with dynamic bodies predictably.
Best practice

Place all physics changes inside FixedUpdate and avoid transform-driven motion to prevent conflict with the physics solver.
