Common issues and fixes
A checklist to diagnose jitter, missing callbacks, and performance problems.
Jittery movement
Do not set transform.position every frame. Use Rigidbody2D.velocity, AddForce, or MovePosition in FixedUpdate.
Missing events
Ensure at least one object has Rigidbody2D and verify the Physics 2D layer collision matrix.
Startup overlap
Place objects without overlap to avoid solver pushing them apart violently at scene start.
Performance
Avoid many PolygonCollider2D instances; prefer simple shapes and static bodies where possible.
Key Takeaway

Use physics-aware movement, check layer rules and Rigidbody presence, and prefer simple colliders to keep your game stable and fast.
