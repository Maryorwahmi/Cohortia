Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Directional Light
What it does
Directional Light simulates sun-like lighting with parallel rays, affecting the whole scene uniformly.
• Rotation sets the light direction and shadow angle.
• Intensity adjusts brightness; color tweaks warm/cool tone.
• Unlike point lights, position does not change its influence.
Key Takeaway

Use Directional Light to establish the primary sun or moon direction and then layer local lights for detail.

Quick Experiment
Select Directional Light in the Hierarchy. Rotate its Transform to see how shadows move. Adjust intensity to brighten or darken the scene. Try a subtle orange for a late-afternoon feel.
Common Mistake
Expecting position to change lighting direction; for Directional Light, only rotation matters.
