Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Material Step-By-Step
Steps
  1. Create a material in Materials folder and name it clearly.
  2. Select material and drag texture into Albedo in the Inspector.
  3. Tweak Metallic and Smoothness to match the real-world surface.
  4. Drag material onto model or assign in Mesh Renderer.
  5. Rotate Directional Light to confirm lighting response.
Example Walkthrough
Make CrateMaterial, drop wood.png into Albedo, set Metallic 0.05 and Smoothness 0.3, then drag onto crate mesh. Observe how texture now aligns and how highlights change when you rotate the light.
Pro Tip
Keep texture files named with their role, like wood_albedo.png or brick_normal.png, so you can quickly assign the correct map.
Key Takeaway

Materials translate textures into rendered surfaces; adjust shader properties to match the intended look.
