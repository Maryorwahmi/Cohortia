Material Basics
Create a material and assign textures to control surface look.
• Albedo: base color or texture image that paints the surface.
• Metallic: controls reflective metallic quality of the surface.
• Smoothness: controls glossiness and sharpness of reflections.
• Shaders: GPU programs that compute final pixel color based on lights and material settings.
Concrete Example
Create CrateMaterial. Drag wood.png into Albedo. Set Metallic to 0.1 and Smoothness to 0.4. Apply to crate mesh to see texture and subtle shine.
Applying Materials
Method
Drag the material from the Project window onto the model in the Scene view or into the Mesh Renderer Materials slot.
Preview
Use the material preview and the model preview to confirm texture alignment and scale; adjust UVs in the 3D modeling tool if necessary.
Key Takeaway

A texture needs a material to appear on a mesh; shaders and material properties determine how that texture responds to light.
