Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Light Types
Directional Light
Global illumination with parallel rays; rotate to change shadows.
Point Light
Omnidirectional light with falloff; ideal for lamps and bulbs.
Spot Light
Emits a cone of light; great for flashlights or stage highlights.
Practical Example
Use one Directional Light for the sun, add point lights for indoor lamps, and a spot light to draw attention to interactive objects. Adjust color and intensity to unify the scene's atmosphere.
Key Takeaway

Mix light types deliberately: global Directional for broad lighting, local lights for detail, and spotlights for focus.
