Assessment Ideas
Prefab question
Which feature lets you update many identical trees at once? Correct: Convert to a Prefab and instantiate it.
Material question
A model appears gray after import — what should you do? Correct: create a Material, assign the texture to Albedo, and apply it to the model.
Lighting question
How do you change shadow direction for a sun-like light? Correct: rotate the Directional Light; position changes do not affect direction.
Key Takeaway

Good assessment mixes procedure and concept to reveal both skill and understanding.
