Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Scene vs Game View
Scene View
Interactive editing area with gizmos and handles. Use it to construct levels, align objects, and tweak lighting without runtime overlays.
When to use
Place objects, adjust transforms, and test spatial relationships while editing.
Game View
Runtime preview from the active Camera. Use it to verify framing, UI scaling, and player perspective under play mode.
When to use
Validate player view and runtime behaviors that are not obvious in Scene editing mode.
Quick Tip
After major edits in Scene, enter Play mode and review the Game view to ensure the player's experience matches your intent.
