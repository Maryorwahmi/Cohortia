Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Aesthetics
Definition

Player aesthetics are emotional outcomes

They are the feelings a player experiences as a result of interacting with your mechanics and dynamics.

Common aesthetics
Challenge, mastery, discovery, relaxation, and social satisfaction are examples designers aim for.
Example
A precise combat mechanic can lead to feelings of mastery when players learn timing and control.
Design approach

Start with the aesthetic you want, design dynamics that produce it, and then implement mechanics that afford those dynamics.

Concrete scenario

If you want 'exploration', provide mechanics for movement and discovery with dynamics that reward curiosity. If you want 'competition', tune mechanics so skillful play produces observable advantages.

Misconception

Aesthetic is not just art and sound. If systems don't support the feeling, presentation alone will not create it for many players.

Check

After a playtest, ask players what moments felt most emotional and trace those moments back to specific mechanics and dynamics.
