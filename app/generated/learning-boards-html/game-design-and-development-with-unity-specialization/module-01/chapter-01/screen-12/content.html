Comparison

Common beginner errors

Recognizing these mistakes early saves development time and improves player experience.

No feedback
Mechanics feel unresponsive and players become confused.
Overcomplexity
Too many mechanics at once hide core decisions.
Mismatched expectations
Players expect certain mechanics and pacing depending on genre.
Fixes
  • • Add clear feedback for every meaningful action.
  • • Prioritize a small set of mechanics and polish them.
  • • Align mechanics with intended aesthetics and genre cues.
Playtest prompt

Observe whether players discover the intended dynamic within three tries. If not, change the mechanic or the context.
