Deconstruct a game

Use this template while playing and write a short analysis after the session.

Step 1 — Mechanics
List 3–5 core mechanics and describe player actions and rules.
Step 2 — Dynamics
Observe and describe emergent behaviors that appear during play.
Step 3 — Aesthetics & Feedback
Name felt emotions and list immediate feedback examples for key actions.

Example submission

Game Title: Example Runner
Genre: Platformer

1. Mechanics:
- Jump, Dash, Collect coins

2. Dynamics:
- Chaining dash after jump to reach ledges
- Risking coin routes for higher score

3. Aesthetics:
- Mastery when timing is precise
- Excitement from risky routes

4. Feedback:
- Sparkle + coin sound on pickup
- Flash + damage sound on hit
Reflection

Suggest one mechanic tweak and predict how that change will alter dynamics and aesthetics.
