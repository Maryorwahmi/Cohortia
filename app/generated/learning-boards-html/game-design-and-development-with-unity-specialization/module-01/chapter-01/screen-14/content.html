Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Assessment

Assessment format

Combine multiple-choice checks with a short written analysis to evaluate both vocabulary and applied reasoning.

MCQ example
Which component describes the player's feeling of discovery after uncovering a secret area? A) Mechanics B) Dynamics C) Aesthetics D) Feedback
Written task
Play a short clip or level, then write 200–300 words listing mechanics, describing dynamics, and naming aesthetics with evidence.
Grading guide
  • • Mechanics clearly listed and concrete.
  • • Dynamics show causal links between mechanics.
  • • Aesthetics tied to observed dynamics with examples.
Feedback tip

When returning grades, point out missing causal links more than vocabulary errors—those links show design thinking.
