Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What is a Game?
Core Concept

Definition

A concise checklist helps you spot a game: are there rules, a goal, and player choices that affect outcomes? If yes, you have gameplay.

Rules
Constraints and systems that limit or enable actions, like gravity, turn order, or resource caps.
Goals
Targets that guide player decisions, from short tasks to longer campaigns.
Agency
Meaningful choices that change outcomes and create ownership of play.
Key Takeaway

Write rules and goals before adding complexity; ensure players can reliably see how their choices change the game state.

Concrete example
In a platformer: gravity and collision are rules, reaching the level exit is a goal, and timed jumps are agency.
Common misconception

More rules do not equal better design. Extra rules can obscure player agency and reduce fun unless they create meaningful new choices.
