Process Flow

Mechanics → Dynamics → Aesthetics

Treat MDA as a two-way loop. Implement, observe, and tune. Or decide on a feeling and design systems to produce it.

Mechanics
Actions and rules you directly change in the editor.
Dynamics
Emergent behavior observed during real play.
Aesthetics
Emotional effects players experience from dynamics.
Practical loop
  1. 1. Implement a minimal mechanic prototype.
  2. 2. Run 3–5 short playtests, observing player strategies.
  3. 3. Note whether dynamics align with the intended aesthetic.
  4. 4. Adjust mechanics or level context and repeat.
Example walkthrough

Want surprise? Add a mechanic that reveals secret paths, design levels that hide rewards, and expect dynamics of exploration that lead to wonder.

Check

If playtests do not produce the intended feeling, change either the mechanic or the context rather than adding unrelated features.
