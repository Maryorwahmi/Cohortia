Core Concept

Why players play

Different motivations require different design choices. Match mechanics and rewards to what players value.

Achievement & Mastery
Clear goals, escalating difficulty, and skill-based mechanics.
Social Play
Mechanics encouraging cooperation or competition and meaningful communication channels.
Exploration & Narrative
Mechanics that reveal secrets, branching choices, and story-driven incentives.
Design tip

List player motivations early and test whether core mechanics address at least one primary motivation clearly.

Quick survey prompt

After a playtest ask: What made you continue playing? Which moment felt most rewarding? Their answers reveal motivation alignment.

Misconception

Designing for 'everyone' dilutes focus. Target a primary motivation and satisfy it well before broadening scope.
