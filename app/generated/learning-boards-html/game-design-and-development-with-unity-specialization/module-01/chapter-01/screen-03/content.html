Deconstruct a game with MDA

A focused, timed activity helps you convert theory into design habits. Use the template below during play and write a short analysis.

Mechanics
List concrete player actions and rules you observe—what the player can do and what limits them.
Dynamics
Describe behaviors and strategies that emerge when mechanics interact with player input.
Aesthetics
Name the emotional responses produced, such as challenge, curiosity, or relaxation.
Milestone

Write a 200–300 word analysis listing mechanics, describing two dynamics, naming aesthetics, and noting feedback examples.

Quick checklist

  • • Record 3–5 mechanics you use during play.
  • • Note 2 emergent dynamics that surprised you.
  • • Identify the primary aesthetic the session produced.
  • • List immediate feedback examples (visual, audio, haptic).
