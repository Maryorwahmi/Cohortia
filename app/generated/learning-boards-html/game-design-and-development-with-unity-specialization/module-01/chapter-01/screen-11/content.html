Process Flow

Action → Feedback → Result

Map every player action to an immediate sensory response and a clear game-state result so players understand cause and effect.

Visual
Animations, flashes, HUD updates.
Audio
Effects and cues for success or failure.
Haptic
Controller vibration for physicality and impact.
Design checklist
  • • Is the feedback immediate (within a fraction of a second)?
  • • Does it clearly indicate success, failure, or partial effect?
  • • Does it scale with action importance and player skill?
Concrete example

When a player collects an item, play a chime, show a score increment, and animate the pickup to confirm the action immediately.

Common mistake

Lack of feedback makes mechanics feel unresponsive; players will guess and disengage. Prioritize clarity over polish.
