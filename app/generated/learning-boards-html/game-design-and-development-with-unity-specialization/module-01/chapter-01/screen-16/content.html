Player Motivation
Design to satisfy the primary reasons players engage with your game.
Feedback Loop
Immediate visual, audio, or haptic cues that communicate action consequences.
Genre Cues
Use familiar mechanics and pacing to meet player expectations for a chosen genre.
Final takeaway

When mechanics, dynamics, aesthetics, motivation, feedback, and genre align, you create clear, engaging experiences that players understand and enjoy.
