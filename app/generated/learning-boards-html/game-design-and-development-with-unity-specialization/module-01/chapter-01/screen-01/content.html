What makes a game?

Explore the system that turns rules and player choices into compelling play. Learn to separate implementation from experience and to design with intention.

In this lesson • Coming up: MDA, genres, feedback
Definition
Game = Rules + Goals + Agency

Active choice and clear objectives distinguish games from passive media. Agency creates unique outcomes.

Framework
Mechanics → Dynamics → Aesthetics

A practical lens for designing systems that produce intended player feelings.

Why this matters

Designers who separate rules from experience can iterate more effectively and produce clearer, more engaging games for intended audiences.
