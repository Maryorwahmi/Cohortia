Genres and expectations

Each genre carries typical mechanics and dynamics. Use genres to guide design choices and to meet player expectations.

Platformer
Mechanics: jumping, momentum. Dynamics: timing chains. Aesthetic: mastery.
Action-Adventure
Mechanics: combat, puzzles. Dynamics: exploration and tension. Aesthetic: discovery and thrill.
RPG
Mechanics: progression, stats. Dynamics: personalization and strategy. Aesthetic: immersion.
Strategy
Mechanics: resource management. Dynamics: planning and tactics. Aesthetic: intellectual challenge.
Puzzle
Mechanics: constrained moves and rules. Dynamics: pattern discovery. Aesthetic: satisfaction from solving.
Design note

When combining genres, ensure mechanics from each genre harmonize to produce coherent dynamics; avoid clashing expectations.

Check

Ask: Does my core mechanic match the emotional experience players expect from this genre?
