Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
CharacterController
Handles collisions and sliding; requires manual gravity and jump logic in your script.
Rigidbody
Enables physics reactions; apply forces or set velocity in FixedUpdate for consistent physics.
Camera
Follow a CameraTarget, update in LateUpdate, smooth movement, and protect from clipping with raycasts.
Summary takeaway

Match the movement approach to the gameplay need: deterministic control or physics interaction. Use the correct update loop for stability and add camera collision checks for a polished result.
