Symptoms and fixes
Floating after jump
Cause: gravity not applied or isGrounded check incorrect. Fix: accumulate gravity into verticalVelocity each Update and set a small negative value when grounded.
Jittery Rigidbody movement
Cause: physics changes in Update. Fix: apply forces and velocity changes in FixedUpdate to align with physics timestep.
Camera clipping
Cause: desired camera position intersects geometry. Fix: raycast from target to desired position and reposition camera to the hit point minus a small offset.
Troubleshooting tips
  • Log controller.isGrounded and verticalVelocity to verify grounding behavior.
  • Compare behaviour across devices to tune dead zones and sensitivity.
  • Temporarily visualize raycasts in the Scene view to debug camera collisions.
