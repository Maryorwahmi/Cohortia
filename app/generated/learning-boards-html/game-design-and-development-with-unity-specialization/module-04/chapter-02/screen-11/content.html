Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Mini lab
Lab steps
  1. Create a plane and obstacles. Add a Capsule, name it Player, and add CharacterController.
  2. Create CameraTarget as child of Player at (0,1.5,-2) or similar offset.
  3. Add PlayerMovement script; implement camera-relative movement, manual gravity, and jumping.
  4. Add ThirdPersonCamera on Main Camera, set target, implement LateUpdate with rotation, Lerp smoothing, and LookAt.
  5. Playtest. If camera clips, add a raycast from target to desired position and clamp camera to hit point.
Tuning exercise
Change smoothSpeed and jumpForce by small increments and record how each change affects perceived responsiveness. Use this to decide default numbers for your game.
Quick checks
  • Player returns to ground after jump.
  • Camera follows smoothly and respects clamp limits.
  • When a Rigidbody hits the player (if tested), does it push appropriately?
Example result
After tweaking smoothSpeed to 0.12, the camera felt smoother without lag; increase jumpForce slightly to match level geometry heights.
