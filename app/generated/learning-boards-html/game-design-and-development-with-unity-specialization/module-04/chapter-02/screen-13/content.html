Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Assessment scenario
Scenario
A character must be pushed by rolling boulders yet retain precise, non-slippery movement while under player control. Which Unity components and update loop do you choose?
Recommended approach
Use a Rigidbody on the player, control movement by setting velocity or using MovePosition, and perform these updates in FixedUpdate. Use ForceMode.VelocityChange for immediate responses and tune drag and physics materials to reduce sliding.
Explain your reasoning
State that Rigidbody enables physical pushes, FixedUpdate keeps physics stable, and direct velocity control preserves tightness while still allowing external forces to affect the player.
Success criteria
Player can be pushed by boulder and still perform responsive moves; no jitter and physics feel consistent.
