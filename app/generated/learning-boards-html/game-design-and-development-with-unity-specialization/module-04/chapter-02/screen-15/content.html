Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts — Quick Reference
Manual gravity
With CharacterController, add gravity yourself and reset vertical velocity when grounded.
FixedUpdate for physics
Apply forces or set Rigidbody velocity in FixedUpdate to avoid timing inconsistencies and jitter.
Camera protection
Use LateUpdate, smoothing, and raycast checks to keep the camera clear and stable during play.
Final prompt

When you next test movement, ask: does the player respond immediately, does physics behave consistently, and does the camera always show the action?
