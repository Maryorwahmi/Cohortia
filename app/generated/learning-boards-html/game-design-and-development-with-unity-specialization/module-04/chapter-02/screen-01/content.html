Implementing 3D Character Controllers & Input
Understand movement approaches, map input declaratively, and craft a following camera that stays readable in gameplay.
In this lesson
Compare CharacterController vs Rigidbody, implement a PlayerMovement script, and wire a ThirdPersonCamera with smoothing and collision protection.
Why it matters
Movement and camera directly affect feel and player control. Choosing the right system avoids floating characters, jitter, and unwanted physics interactions during gameplay.
Explore key topics
CharacterController
Script-driven moves with built-in collision sliding and manual gravity.
Rigidbody Movement
Physics-driven response that reacts to forces and other dynamic objects.
Third-Person Camera
Follow, smooth, and avoid clipping using LateUpdate and raycasts.
Concept Preview
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Play loop
Input → Movement logic → Collision resolution → Camera follow. Tuning any step changes the player feel.
Input
Input Actions or WASD map to Move and Jump behaviors.
Output
Character velocity and camera position produce on-screen motion.
Quick tip
Start with CharacterController for predictable movement. Move to Rigidbody when you need physical pushes, explosions, or realistic momentum.
