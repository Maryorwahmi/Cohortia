Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Camera & Player Architecture
Component roles
  • Player: handles movement, state, and CameraTarget child updates.
  • CameraTarget: a transform offset for framing that the camera looks at.
  • Camera: separate GameObject that follows target in LateUpdate and smooths position.
Why a target
It isolates camera concerns and gives a single anchor for look-at and collision checks, simplifying future adjustments like aim offsets.
Collision avoidance pattern
Raycast from CameraTarget to desired camera position. If hit, move camera to hit point minus a short buffer distance. If not hit, use smoothed desired position.
Example
Raycast detects a wall; camera pulls closer so the player remains visible without seeing inside geometry.
Takeaway
Keep camera logic decoupled and update it after player movement to avoid visual inconsistencies.
