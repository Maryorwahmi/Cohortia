Learning Objectives
Skills you will practice and verify in a short playtest scene.
Bloom
Learn • Understand • Apply • Master
01
Differentiate Movement Systems
Explain when to use CharacterController versus a Rigidbody-based approach and why that choice affects gameplay behavior.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement Walk, Run, Jump
Write and test a PlayerMovement script that reads input, moves the character, and applies gravity and jump impulses.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Build a Follow Camera
Use LateUpdate and smoothing to follow a CameraTarget, and add raycast checks to avoid clipping through walls.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Diagnose Movement Issues
Identify common issues like floating jumps, jittery rigidbody movement, and camera clipping, and choose the appropriate fix.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic
Think: Input drives Movement, Movement drives Camera, Camera informs Player decisions. Keep update order consistent: physics in FixedUpdate, camera in LateUpdate.
Quick check
  • Is gravity applied? If not, the character may float.
  • Are physics updates in FixedUpdate when using Rigidbody?
  • Does the camera use raycasts to avoid clipping?
