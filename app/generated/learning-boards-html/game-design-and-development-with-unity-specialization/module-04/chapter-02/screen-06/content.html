Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Rigidbody vs CharacterController
Quick comparison
CharacterController
Precise, script-driven movement. Manual gravity. No physical pushes from other rigidbodies.
Rigidbody
Physics-driven, reacts to forces and collisions, uses FixedUpdate for consistent behavior.
When to choose
  • Choose CharacterController for tight platforming and deterministic control.
  • Choose Rigidbody when environmental physics must affect the player.
Practical tips for Rigidbody
  • Apply forces or change velocity inside FixedUpdate to match the physics timestep.
  • Use ForceMode.VelocityChange for immediate velocity adjustments without mass scaling.
  • Tune mass, drag, and physics materials to reduce sliding while preserving responsiveness.

Example
Use Rigidbody when a falling crate should knock the player back and change their velocity.
Misconception
Rigidbody does not automatically make control better; it makes interactions realistic but requires extra tuning for player feel.
