Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Rigidbody
Component that enables physics-driven motion and responses to forces.
Collider
Defines an object's physical shape for detection; primitives are preferred for performance.
Collision Events
Callbacks with contact data used for physical responses like bounce and impact.
Summary Takeaway
Use the right component for the job: Rigidbody for physics motion, primitive colliders for moving objects, collision callbacks for physical effects, and triggers for detection-only interactions.
