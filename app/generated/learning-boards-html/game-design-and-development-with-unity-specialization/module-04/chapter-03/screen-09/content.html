Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Bouncing Ball Example
Practical steps
  1. Add Rigidbody and a primitive collider to the ball.
  2. Create a Physics Material with desired bounciness and assign it to the collider.
  3. Use OnCollisionEnter to detect strong impacts and optionally apply force or play effects.
Tip: If collisions feel jittery, check mass ratios and solver settings, and prefer primitive colliders on dynamic objects.
