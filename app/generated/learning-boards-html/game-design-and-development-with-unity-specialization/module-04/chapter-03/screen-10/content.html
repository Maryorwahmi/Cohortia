Material properties
  • Friction — resists sliding between surfaces.
  • Bounciness — fraction of energy returned after impact.
  • Combine modes — Average, Minimum, Multiply, Maximum determine how properties mix.
Example: give a platform a Bouncy material with high bounciness and set Bounce Combine to Maximum so objects keep high rebound when colliding with other bouncy surfaces.
Combine mode guide
  • Average — use when you want a balanced blend.
  • Minimum — conservative, reduces effect.
  • Multiply — can produce stronger extremes.
  • Maximum — favors the higher value for dramatic effect.
Key Takeaway

Design materials for both objects in a collision and pick a combine mode that matches gameplay intent so interactions feel consistent.
