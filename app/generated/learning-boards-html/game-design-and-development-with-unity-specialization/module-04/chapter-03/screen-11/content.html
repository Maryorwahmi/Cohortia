Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Raycasting Basics
When to use raycasts
  • Line-of-sight checks for AI or visibility tests.
  • Object selection and interaction from the camera.
  • Ground detection where precise contact is needed.
Best practices
  • Always use a LayerMask to filter out unnecessary objects.
  • Keep ray length minimal to reduce false hits and cost.
  • Consider SphereCast for broader ground checks on uneven surfaces.
Example data returned by raycast: the hit object's collider, the hit point in world space, and the normal of the surface at the hit point.
