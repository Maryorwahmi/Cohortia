Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Collision vs Trigger
Collision callbacks

OnCollisionEnter/Stay/Exit fire when colliders make physical contact and at least one has a Rigidbody. Use the Collision data for impact response and physics effects.

  • Provides contact points and relative velocity.
  • Use for bouncing, damage on impact, or adding forces.
Trigger callbacks

OnTriggerEnter/Stay/Exit fire when an object enters a trigger volume. Triggers detect presence but do not block motion.

  • No contact points or relative velocity provided.
  • Ideal for pickups, zones, and objective detection.
Check your understanding

If a collectible doesn't trigger, confirm that either the collectible or the player has a Rigidbody and that the collectible's collider has Is Trigger checked.
