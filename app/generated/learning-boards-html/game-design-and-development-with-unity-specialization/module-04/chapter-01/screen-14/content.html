Tree & detail painting

Add Tree Prototypes and set random rotation and scale ranges to reduce repetition.

Use Paint Details for grass and rocks; choose density and render mode carefully.

Vary density with brush size and strength to form natural clusters.

Example strategy
  1. Paint scattered trees near paths for visual interest but low density for performance.
  2. Create dense forest zones with higher LODs and lower detail meshes for distant areas.
  3. Break terrain into tiles and apply vegetation per tile to limit editing scope and improve streaming.
Mistake
Painting maximum density everywhere creates draw and CPU costs. Plan density by distance from player.
Pro tip
Use randomized scale and rotation combined with a variety of detail prototypes to hide repetition effectively.
