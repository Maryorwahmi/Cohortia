When to add density

Add loop cuts to support bevels and preserve crisp edges.

Use bridge edges to connect separated pieces like archways or tunnels.

Subdivide selectively in areas that require irregular detail, like ruined edges.

Example — ruined wall
  1. Subdivide the wall face to add vertex density along the top edge.
  2. Select random faces along the edge and delete them to create jagged breaks.
  3. Use bridge edges to reconnect broken sections where needed.
Mistake
Blindly subdividing the whole mesh increases cost without visual benefit. Keep subdivisions local.
Pro tip
After topology edits, re-generate UVs if texture stretching appears, and check normals for lighting artifacts.
