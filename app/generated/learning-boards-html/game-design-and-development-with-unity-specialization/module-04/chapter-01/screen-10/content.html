Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Stay vs Export
Stay in ProBuilder
  • Rapid layout iteration and collision geometry.
  • Quick prop and modular piece creation for playtests.
  • Preserve scene context and immediate testing.
Export for final art
  • High-resolution sculpting, detailed normal maps, and complex UV seams.
  • Lightmap UV packing and advanced topology control.
  • Final texture atlasing and production pipelines.
Practical workflow
  1. Prototype in ProBuilder until the design stabilizes.
  2. Export stable pieces for detailed modeling and UV work.
  3. Re-import refined assets and replace prototypes in the scene.
