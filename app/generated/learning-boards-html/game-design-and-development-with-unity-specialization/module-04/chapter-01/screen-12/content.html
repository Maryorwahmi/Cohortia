Brush parameters
Size: area affected by the brush; large for mass, small for detail.
Strength: how much height changes per stroke; build up with low strength for nuance.
Falloff: softness of the brush edge for smooth blending.
Example workflow
  1. Large brush, moderate strength: lay out primary hills and valleys.
  2. Medium brush, lower strength: define ridges and slopes.
  3. Small brush, low strength: add localized details near structures.
  4. Smooth to remove unrealistic spikes and ensure walkable slopes.
Gameplay note
Keep slopes traversable for your player controller; steep slopes can block movement or require special handling.
Pro tip
Sculpt with brush strength under 50% for more controlled and natural-looking terrain formation.
