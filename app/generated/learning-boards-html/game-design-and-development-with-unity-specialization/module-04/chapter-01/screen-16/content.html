Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Splatmap
Blends multiple terrain textures; use soft brushes for natural transitions.
Detail Objects
Use billboards or low-poly meshes for grass and rocks; balance visual richness and cost.
Terrain Tiles & LOD
Split large terrains into tiles and attach LODs for vegetation to scale performance with distance.
Final takeaway
Combine fast ProBuilder prototypes with careful terrain sculpting and measured vegetation density. Test performance often and refine where players spend most of their time.
