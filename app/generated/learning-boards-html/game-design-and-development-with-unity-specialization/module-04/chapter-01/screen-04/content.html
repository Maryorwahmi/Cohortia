Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › ProBuilder — an in-editor modeling toolkit
What ProBuilder is

An integrated modeling tool focused on speed and iteration. Use it to block out environments, make architectural elements, and export standard meshes when needed.

Why use it
  • Direct in-engine editing shortens iteration cycles.
  • Good for collision geometry, layout, and quick props.
  • Exportable if further detail is required externally.
Key Takeaway
Use ProBuilder to iterate geometry quickly; move to a dedicated modeler when you need high-detail meshes or complex UVs.
Concrete example — room blockout
Step-by-step
  1. Create ProBuilder cube and scale to floor size.
  2. Select a top face and extrude up to form a wall.
  3. Inset and extrude a face inward to make a window opening.
  4. Bevel an edge to soften the corner.
Common mistake
Beginners often stay in object mode when they need face or vertex mode, causing unintended deformation. Always confirm your selection mode before transforming geometry.
