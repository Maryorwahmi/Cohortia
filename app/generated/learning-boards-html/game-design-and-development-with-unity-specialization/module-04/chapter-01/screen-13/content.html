Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Painting textures
Layer workflow

Add texture layers in Terrain settings, select a brush, and paint. The splatmap stores per-pixel weights to blend layers.

Paint rock on steep slopes and grass on flats, using soft brushes to blend edges.

Consider an intermediate transition texture where rock meets grass for smoother visual results.

Performance & tips
Minimize the number of unique high-resolution layers across the scene.
Use tiling settings to reduce visible repetition at large scales.
Test blends with scene lighting; albedo and normal maps interact with shading.
