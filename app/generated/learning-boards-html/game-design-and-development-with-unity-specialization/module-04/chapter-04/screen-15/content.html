Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts (Detailed)
Directional Light
Sun-like, parallel rays; rotate to change time-of-day shadows.
Point & Spot
Point: omnidirectional bulbs. Spot: cone beams for flashlights and headlamps.
Area Light
Rectangular soft emitter for realistic soft lighting; typically baked.
Real-time vs Baked
Real-time updates per frame; baked stores lightmaps for static geometry.
PBR Maps
Albedo, Metallic, Smoothness, Normal, Occlusion control material response.
Post-Processing
Bloom, AO, and Color Grading refine final look; tune sparingly.
Final reminder

Use these compact concepts to make quick, consistent decisions while iterating lighting, materials, and effects in your scenes.
