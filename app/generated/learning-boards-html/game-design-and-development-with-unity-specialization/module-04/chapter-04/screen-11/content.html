Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Normal & Occlusion Maps
What they do
Normal maps perturb per-pixel normals to simulate bumps. Occlusion maps darken crevices to enhance perceived depth and contact shadows.
Normal map tips
Keep in linear space, ensure tangent-space convention matches engine, and remember normals do not alter silhouette.
Occlusion map tips
Use occlusion to darken crevices and combine with baked lighting or SSAO for richer shading.
Example
A dungeon wall: Normal map gives mortar depth; Occlusion map darkens gaps so corners read better when baked or under SSAO. Use Light Probes to carry indirect light to moving characters that interact with these shaded areas.
Key Takeaway

Normals and Occlusion increase perceived detail efficiently; match color spaces and use probes for dynamic objects.
