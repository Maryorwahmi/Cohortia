Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Optimization
Techniques
  • Occlusion Culling: do not render hidden objects.
  • Frustum Culling: skip objects outside camera view (automatic).
  • LOD: reduce mesh complexity with distance.
  • Batching: decrease draw calls by combining meshes.
Lighting-specific
Bake static lights, place Light Probes for dynamics, and limit shadow-casting real-time lights.
Example & Checklist
Example: Indoor scene where walls and props are baked, player uses Light Probes, and only the torch is real-time. Checklist: profile CPU/GPU, toggle features, and confirm which change produced measurable gains.
Key Takeaway

Optimize iteratively: make one change, profile, then accept or revert based on measured outcome.
