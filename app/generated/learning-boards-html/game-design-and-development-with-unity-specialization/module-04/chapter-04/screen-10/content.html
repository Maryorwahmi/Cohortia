Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › PBR Texture Maps
Map breakdown
Albedo
Base color; use sRGB so colors read under lighting.
Metallic
White is metal, black is non-metal. Controls reflection behavior.
Smoothness
High smoothness gives sharp highlights; low smoothness is rough and diffuse.
Practical example
Car paint: Albedo for color, Metallic to define chrome accents, Smoothness high for gloss. Character armor: boost Metallic for metal plates, use Smoothness maps to vary wear and polish. Import settings must match map roles to avoid incorrect shading.
Common mistake
Importing normal or metallic maps as sRGB color textures, which leads to wrong lighting interpretation.
Key Takeaway

Set texture color spaces correctly and iterate with lighting to tune metallic and smoothness for the intended material look.
