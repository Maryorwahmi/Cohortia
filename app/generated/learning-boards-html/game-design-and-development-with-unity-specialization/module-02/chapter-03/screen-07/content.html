Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Collider Types
BoxCollider
Good for static geometry and crates where axis-aligned bounds are acceptable.
SphereCollider
Cheap radial checks, useful for projectiles or simple pickups.
CapsuleCollider
Well suited for standing characters because it slides and prevents getting stuck on geometry.
Configuration tips
  • Align collider center with visible mesh to avoid unexpected misses.
  • Use child trigger colliders for pickup ranges or close-proximity sensors.
  • Check that ground colliders are not triggers if you expect landing and solid contact.
Pro tip

If your player falls through the ground, ensure both player and ground have colliders, and the ground is not set as a trigger.
