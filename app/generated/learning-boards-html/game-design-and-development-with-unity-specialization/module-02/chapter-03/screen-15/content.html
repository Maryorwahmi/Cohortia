Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Final Reminders
Use Rigidbody APIs
Avoid direct transform changes on physics objects.
Colliders & Triggers
Choose solid colliders for physics, triggers for detection-only zones.
FixedUpdate & Input
Sample input and apply it in FixedUpdate to keep physics steady and controls responsive.
Closing Thought

When in doubt, ask whether the interaction needs a physical block or just a detection. That choice shapes colliders, Rigidbody use, and callback selection.
