Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Collision vs Trigger
At-a-glance
Collision
OnCollisionEnter, solid, prevents overlap, needs colliders and Rigidbody on at least one side.
Trigger
OnTriggerEnter, non-physical detection, Is Trigger checked, used for pickups and zones.
Common pitfalls
  • Neither object has a Rigidbody — no callbacks will fire.
  • Making a ground collider a trigger prevents landing interactions.
  • Large collider sizes can cause unexpected overlaps on spawn.
Concrete example
Create a Collectible sphere with a SphereCollider and check Is Trigger. Tag it Collectible. In the player script use OnTriggerEnter to Detect and Destroy the collectible. This shows detection without blocking movement.
Key Takeaway

Use collisions for physical interactions and triggers for detection-only zones; ensure at least one Rigidbody exists between interactors.
