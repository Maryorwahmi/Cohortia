Rigidbody explained
Rigidbody hands motion control to Unity's physics. Use Rigidbody2D for 2D games and Rigidbody for 3D projects.
  • Mass affects how forces change velocity.
  • Drag resists motion; angular drag resists rotation.
  • Constraints freeze position or rotation axes to prevent unwanted motion.
Common mistakes
Moving physics objects by transform.position breaks collision resolution and leads to jitter. Always manipulate Rigidbody through its API in FixedUpdate.
Key Takeaway

Attach a Rigidbody to make an object subject to physics; do not mix direct transform updates with Rigidbody-controlled motion.

Concrete example
Create a Capsule named Player, add Rigidbody, enable Use Gravity. Press Play and observe it fall onto the ground collider and settle. This shows the Rigidbody receiving gravity and collision responses.
Try it: Toggle constraints to freeze rotation if the capsule tips over when you don't want it to.
Why it matters
Using Rigidbody correctly prevents unnatural motion, ensures accurate collision detection, and lets you rely on Unity's physics rather than manual hacks.
