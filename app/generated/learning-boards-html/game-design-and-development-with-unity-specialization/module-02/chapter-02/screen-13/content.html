Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Performance & Debugging
Checklist
  • Cache GetComponent results in Awake/Start.
  • Avoid Find calls inside Update; call them at setup time or cache arrays.
  • Null-check lookups and log warnings to aid debugging.
  • Use pooling for bullets and frequently spawned objects to minimize allocations.
Debug pattern
Log at key points: after find, after GetComponent, after Instantiate, and before Destroy. This reveals lifecycle timing and missing references.
Example log
Debug.Log("Found Enemy and its Health script.");
Takeaway
Design for lifecycle: creation, use, and cleanup. Defensive checks and pooling make systems scalable.
