vMX: The Virtual MX Router

A virtualized version of Juniper's MX Series universal routing platform, offering full Junos OS routing capabilities as a software instance.

  • Full Junos OS: Supports BGP, OSPF, MPLS, VPNs, and other advanced routing features.
  • Flexible Deployment: Runs on standard x86 servers in private clouds or directly in public cloud marketplaces.
  • Operational Consistency: Utilizes familiar Junos OS CLI and management tools across physical and virtual infrastructure.
Scenario Example

A company extends its on-premises network to a public cloud, deploying vMX in the cloud to maintain consistent routing policies and management practices.

Key Takeaway

vMX enables consistent, high-performance routing in virtualized and cloud environments, bridging the gap between physical and software-defined networks.
