Common JTI Configuration Mistakes

Avoiding pitfalls for reliable telemetry streaming.

Incorrect Target Address/Port

The Junos device attempts to send data to an unreachable or incorrect IP address/port on the collector.

Solution

Verify the target-address and port in the export profile match the collector's listening configuration.

Firewall Blocking Connection

Firewalls (network or host-based) prevent the gRPC connection from being established or data from flowing.

Solution

Ensure necessary ports (e.g., 50051) are open on all firewalls between the Junos device and the collector.

Missing/Incorrect YANG Models

The collector cannot parse gRPC-encoded data because it lacks the correct YANG models for interpretation.

Solution

Install the appropriate OpenConfig or Junos native YANG models on the telemetry collector.

Overly Low Reporting Interval

Sending data too frequently can overwhelm the collector or the network link, leading to data loss.

Solution

Start with a higher reporting-interval and gradually decrease it as collector performance is validated.

Key Takeaway

Proactive troubleshooting involves verifying collector connectivity, firewall rules, YANG model compatibility, and optimizing the reporting interval.
