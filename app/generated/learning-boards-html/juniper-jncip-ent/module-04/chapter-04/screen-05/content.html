Graceful Restart (GR) Process Flow

1. RE Restart Initiated

Restarting router's control plane begins to restart.

2. GR Notification

Restarting router informs helper neighbors.

3. Traffic Forwarding Continues

Helper routers use stale routes; PFE forwards traffic.

4. Normal Operation Resumes

RE re-establishes adjacencies; helper routers update.

Key Benefit

Significantly reduces traffic disruption during control plane restarts, preventing blackholes and delays.

Supported Protocols

GR is supported by various routing protocols, including OSPF, BGP, and ISIS.

Key Takeaway

Graceful Restart ensures forwarding plane continuity during a routing engine restart by having helper routers temporarily maintain routing information, minimizing service impact.
