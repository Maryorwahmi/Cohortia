Enabling Graceful Restart for OSPF

The configuration for Graceful Restart is protocol-specific. For OSPF, it's a simple command:

set protocols ospf graceful-restart
Common Mistake

Not ensuring helper capabilities are enabled on neighboring routers, or having incompatible GR timers. This can lead to traffic disruption if the restart exceeds the timer.

Key Takeaway

Successful GR implementation requires not only enabling it on the restarting router but also ensuring helper capabilities on neighbors and compatible GR timers across the network.

GR Operational Flow & Considerations

Restarting Router Role

Informs neighbors of restart, allowing its PFE to continue forwarding traffic using existing routes.

Helper Router Role

Continues to forward traffic to the restarting router based on last known information, even if adjacency is down.

GR Timers

Crucial for successful GR. If restart exceeds timer, helper routers will tear down old routes, causing disruption.
