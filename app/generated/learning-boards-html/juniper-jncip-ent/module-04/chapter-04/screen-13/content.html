Key Concepts: High Availability Fundamentals

Essential terms for resilient networks

Graceful Restart (GR)

Allows a routing engine to restart without disrupting the forwarding plane by having neighbors temporarily hold old routing information.

Restarting Router

The router whose routing engine is restarting during a Graceful Restart event, relying on helpers to maintain traffic flow.

Helper Router

A neighbor router that assists the restarting router by maintaining forwarding state during a Graceful Restart event.

Summary Takeaway

Graceful Restart and the roles of restarting and helper routers are essential for minimizing traffic disruption during control plane restarts, ensuring network continuity.
