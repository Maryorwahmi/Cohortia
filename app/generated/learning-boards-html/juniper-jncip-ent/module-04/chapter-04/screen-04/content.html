Why Advanced HA?

Modern networks require continuous operation, especially during control plane events, to prevent service disruption.

Control Plane Events: Minimize impact during routing engine restarts, software upgrades, or process crashes.

Data Plane Continuity: Ensure traffic forwarding remains uninterrupted even if the control plane is recovering.

Service Availability: Maintain critical services like financial transactions and real-time communications without interruption.

Key Takeaway

Advanced HA features are essential for building robust, carrier-grade networks that ensure continuous service availability by minimizing disruption during control plane events.

Evolution of Network Resiliency

Basic Redundancy (VRRP, BFD)

Addresses specific failure scenarios like gateway or link failures, providing local protection.

Advanced HA (GR, NSR, NSB)

Minimizes disruption during control plane events, ensuring forwarding plane continuity.

Comprehensive Redundancy (Chassis Cluster)

Provides device-level redundancy and stateful failover for entire systems, like firewalls.
