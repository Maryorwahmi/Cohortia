Dynamic RP Discovery

The Bootstrap Router (BSR) Mechanism

Automating RP discovery for larger PIM-SM domains

BSR Election

A Bootstrap Router (BSR) is dynamically elected among PIM routers. The router with the highest BSR priority (and then highest IP address) becomes the active BSR.

Candidate RPs (CRPs)

CRPs advertise their willingness to act as an RP to the BSR. They specify the multicast group ranges they are configured to serve.

RP-Set Distribution

The BSR collects CRP information, creates an RP-set (list of RPs and their group ranges), and periodically floods this set throughout the PIM domain. All routers learn the active RP dynamically.

Advantages of BSR

  • Scalability: Automates RP discovery, simplifying management in large networks.
  • Fault Tolerance: If a CRP or the BSR fails, new elections or RP-set updates ensure continuous operation.
  • Flexibility: Allows multiple RPs to serve different group ranges dynamically.
Key Takeaway

BSR provides a dynamic, scalable, and fault-tolerant mechanism for RP discovery, making it ideal for complex PIM-SM deployments.
