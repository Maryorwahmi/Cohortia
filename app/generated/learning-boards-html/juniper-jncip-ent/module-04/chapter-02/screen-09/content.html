Inter-Domain Multicast

MSDP for Inter-Domain Routing

Connecting PIM-SM domains for global multicast reachability

The Inter-Domain Challenge

PIM-SM domains are typically isolated regarding source awareness. Without a mechanism to share source information, a domain only knows about local sources or those explicitly joined.

MSDP Peering Between Domains

MSDP peers are established between border routers of different PIM-SM domains. These peers exchange Source-Active (SA) messages, informing each other about active (Source, Group) pairs.

Building Source-Specific Trees

By exchanging SA messages, PIM-SM domains can build source-specific trees (S,G) even if the source is located in a different domain. This extends multicast reachability globally.

Global Multicast Reachability

MSDP is crucial for enabling multicast applications to span across multiple administrative domains and wide area networks, facilitating large-scale, global multicast deployments.

Key Takeaway

MSDP is vital for inter-domain multicast routing, allowing different PIM-SM domains to exchange source information and achieve global multicast reachability.
