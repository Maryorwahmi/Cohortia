Troubleshooting PIM-SM

Essential Junos OS Commands

Diagnosing common multicast issues

`show pim neighbors`

Verifies PIM adjacencies. Essential for confirming PIM is operational on interfaces and neighbors are formed.

`show pim rp`

Displays the learned Rendezvous Point (RP) for different multicast groups. Crucial for validating RP discovery mechanisms (static, BSR, Anycast).

`show pim join`

Shows active (*,G) or (S,G) joins on the router, indicating receiver interest and the state of multicast group membership.

`show multicast route`

Displays the multicast forwarding table entries, showing the incoming interface, RPF neighbor, and outgoing interfaces for multicast streams.

`show msdp peer` & `show msdp source-active`

For MSDP deployments, these commands verify peering status and check learned Source-Active (SA) information, respectively.

Key Takeaway

A systematic approach with Junos OS commands like `show pim neighbors`, `show pim rp`, and `show multicast route` is essential for effective PIM-SM troubleshooting.
