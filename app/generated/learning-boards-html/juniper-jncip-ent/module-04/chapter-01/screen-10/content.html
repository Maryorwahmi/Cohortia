PIM Sparse Mode (PIM-SM)

Explicitly Joining Groups via a Rendezvous Point

Operational Model

Uses an "explicit join" mechanism, where routers only forward traffic to segments with explicit requests.

Rendezvous Point (RP)

A central router that acts as a meeting point for sources and receivers to exchange multicast traffic.

Scalability

More scalable and efficient for networks where receivers are sparsely distributed, common in modern networks.

Key Takeaway

PIM-SM avoids unnecessary flooding by requiring explicit joins, making it ideal for large, distributed networks.

PIM-SM Explicit Join Process

1

Receivers Send Joins

Routers explicitly join groups towards the RP.

2

Sources Register with RP

Sources send traffic to the Rendezvous Point.

3

RP Connects Paths

The RP bridges source and receiver paths.

4

SPT Switch (Optional)

Receivers may switch to a direct SPT to the source.
