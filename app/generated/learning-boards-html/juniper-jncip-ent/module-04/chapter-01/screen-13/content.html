Validating IGMP and PIM-DM Status

Using show Commands to Monitor Multicast Activity

Verify IGMP Membership

Use show igmp group to confirm hosts are joining multicast groups on local interfaces.

Check PIM Neighbors

Use show pim neighbors to ensure PIM adjacencies are formed between routers.

Inspect Multicast Routes

Use show multicast route to view the multicast forwarding table and verify traffic paths.

Key Takeaway

Regular verification with show commands is critical for troubleshooting and maintaining a healthy multicast network.
