Core Concept

The Necessity of QoS in Converged Networks

Ensuring Performance for Diverse Traffic Types

Why QoS is Critical

  • •Converged Traffic: Voice, video, and data share infrastructure, each with unique performance demands.
  • •Best-Effort Limitations: Without QoS, all traffic is treated equally, leading to unpredictable performance.
  • •Application Sensitivity: Time-sensitive apps (VoIP, video) are highly vulnerable to network issues.
  • •User Experience: QoS directly impacts the quality and reliability of critical business communications.

Common Traffic Characteristics

Latency

Delay a packet experiences from source to destination.

Jitter

Variation in packet delay, causing choppy audio/video.

Packet Loss

Packets dropped by network devices due to congestion.

Key Takeaway

QoS is vital for managing diverse traffic in converged networks, preventing performance degradation for time-sensitive applications like VoIP and video conferencing.
