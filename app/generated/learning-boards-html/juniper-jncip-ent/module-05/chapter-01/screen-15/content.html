Key Concepts to Remember (Continued)

Advanced Actions and Critical Behaviors

Actions

Operations performed on packets that match a term, including accept, discard, reject, count, and log.

Implicit Discard

The default behavior where any packet not explicitly matched and accepted by a term is silently dropped.

Policer

A firewall filter action used to limit the rate of traffic by defining bandwidth and burst size limits.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding filter actions, the implicit discard, and policers is crucial for implementing effective and robust stateless security policies on Juniper devices.
