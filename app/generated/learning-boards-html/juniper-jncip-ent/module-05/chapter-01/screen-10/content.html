Policer: Rate Limiting and Traffic Shaping

Controlling Bandwidth and Preventing DoS Attacks

Rate Limiting Mechanism

Limits the rate of traffic matching a term by defining a bandwidth limit and a burst size.

Actions on Excess Traffic

Excess traffic can either be discarded (dropped) or remarked with a lower priority.

Use Cases: QoS and Security

Crucial for preventing DoS attacks, ensuring fair bandwidth usage, and enforcing QoS policies.

Key Takeaway

Policers are essential for managing network congestion and protecting against traffic-based attacks by enforcing strict rate limits.
