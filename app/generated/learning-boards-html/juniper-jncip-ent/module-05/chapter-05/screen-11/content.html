Hierarchical QoS (HQoS)

Multi-tiered traffic management for complex network scenarios

What is HQoS?

Hierarchical QoS (HQoS) provides a multi-tiered approach to traffic management, allowing QoS policies to be applied at different levels of the network hierarchy, such as per-interface, per-logical interface, or per-subscriber.

Granular Control

Enables very precise control over bandwidth allocation and prioritization across various services and users within a complex network.

Multi-Level

Allows for nested QoS policies, where a higher-level policy can define overall limits, and lower-level policies manage traffic within those limits.

Key HQoS Components

HQoS relies on specific configuration elements to define and apply its multi-tiered policies.

  • •`traffic-control-profiles`: Define overall shaping rates, guaranteed rates, and associate scheduler maps.
  • •`scheduler-maps`: Link forwarding classes to specific schedulers, defining how traffic is prioritized and allocated.
  • •Applied to interfaces or logical interfaces to enforce the hierarchical policies.
Concrete Example

A service provider might use HQoS to guarantee a total bandwidth to a business customer, then within that allocation, prioritize VoIP traffic over web browsing for the customer's employees.

Key Takeaway

HQoS provides a powerful multi-tiered framework for granular traffic management, essential for complex networks requiring precise control over bandwidth allocation and prioritization.

Why it's Advanced

HQoS allows for nested QoS policies, enabling a parent policy to control the total resources available to child policies, which then further subdivide and manage traffic.
