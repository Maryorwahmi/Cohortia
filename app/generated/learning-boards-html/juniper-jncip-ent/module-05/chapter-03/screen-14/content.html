Key Concepts

Essential Terms for IPSec VPNs

IPSec

A suite of protocols providing secure communication over IP networks through encryption, authentication, and integrity services.

IKE

The protocol used to establish and manage Security Associations (SAs) for IPSec, operating in two phases.

IKE Phase 1

Establishes a secure, authenticated channel (IKE SA) between peers, negotiating cryptographic parameters and authenticating identities.

IKE Phase 2

Negotiates the IPSec SA within the secure IKE SA, defining how user data traffic will be protected.

Summary Takeaway

IPSec VPNs rely on a two-phase IKE process to establish secure control and data channels, ensuring robust protection for network traffic.
