How PAT Works

Modifies source port numbers to create unique outbound connections from a single public IP.

IPv4 Conservation

Extremely efficient, allowing hundreds of internal devices to share one public IP address.

Key Takeaway

PAT is the default mechanism that enables many internal hosts to share a single public IP for internet access.
