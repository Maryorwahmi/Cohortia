Well-Known Communities

Predefined Policy Actions

Understanding the standard communities with specific, predefined meanings.

No-Export (65535:65281)

Routes tagged with no-export must not be advertised outside the local AS. They are allowed to be advertised to IBGP peers but not to EBGP peers. Useful for keeping routes internal.

Use Case

Preventing sensitive internal routes from leaking to external networks.

No-Advertise (65535:65282)

Routes tagged with no-advertise must not be advertised to any other BGP peer, whether IBGP or EBGP. This effectively stops all propagation.

Use Case

Completely suppressing a route from being advertised anywhere beyond the receiving router.

No-Export-Subconfed (65535:65283)

Allows routes to be advertised within a BGP confederation sub-AS, but not outside the confederation itself.

Use Case

Confining routes to a specific confederation for hierarchical AS structures.

Local-AS (65535:65284)

Routes must not be advertised to EBGP peers in a different AS. If received from an EBGP peer, the community is removed before advertising to IBGP peers.

Use Case

Controlling route advertisement specifically within the local AS context, even with EBGP neighbors.

Key Takeaway

Well-known communities offer standardized, powerful controls for BGP route propagation, essential for common policy requirements.
