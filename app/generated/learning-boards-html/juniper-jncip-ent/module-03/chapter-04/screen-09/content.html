Extended Community Types

Specialized Information Carriers

Exploring common types like Route Target, Bandwidth, and Origin Validation.

Route Target (RT)

Used to control the import and export of VPN routes between VRF instances in MPLS VPNs. Identifies which VPN routes can be imported into a VRF.

Key Role

Essential for maintaining VPN separation and route distribution.

Bandwidth

Used to signal bandwidth requirements or capabilities associated with a particular route. Can influence traffic engineering decisions.

Key Role

Informing routers about path capacity for optimized traffic flow.

Origin Validation (RFC 6810)

Used in RPKI (Resource Public Key Infrastructure) to indicate the validity of a route's origin, enhancing routing security.

Key Role

Mitigating route hijacking and improving the trustworthiness of BGP routing information.

Key Takeaway

Different types of extended communities serve specialized functions, from VPN route control to bandwidth signaling and origin validation, enabling advanced BGP services.
