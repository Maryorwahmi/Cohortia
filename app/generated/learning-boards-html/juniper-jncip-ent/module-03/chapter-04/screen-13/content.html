Key Concepts

Essential Terms for BGP Communities

BGP Communities

32-bit (standard) or 64-bit (extended) tags attached to BGP routes for policy signaling and grouping.

AS:VALUE Format

Common representation for standard BGP communities, combining the Autonomous System Number and a custom value.

Optional Transitive Attribute

BGP communities are propagated to other ASes by default, making them effective for inter-AS policy signaling.

Summary Takeaway

BGP communities are fundamental for scalable policy management, using flexible tags to influence route propagation across networks.
