Describe how MED influences inbound traffic from a specific neighboring AS, allowing you to guide external networks. This knowledge helps in optimizing traffic entry points for redundancy and performance.

Configure Local Preference

Configure Junos OS policies to set and manipulate the Local Preference attribute, enabling precise control over outbound traffic paths. This practical skill is vital for implementing traffic engineering policies.

Implement MED Policies
