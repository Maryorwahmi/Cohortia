Multi-Exit Discriminator (MED): Influencing Inbound Traffic

An EBGP Attribute for Neighboring AS Path Selection

EBGP Attribute

Optional non-transitive attribute, typically not passed beyond the immediate neighbor.

Inbound Traffic

Influences how a neighboring AS sends traffic into your AS.

Lower is Preferred

A lower MED value indicates a more preferred path for inbound traffic.

Key Takeaway

MED is an optional non-transitive EBGP attribute that influences inbound traffic from a *single* neighboring AS; lower values are preferred.

MED in Action (Same ISP)
ISP-A prefers the path with the lower MED value to reach your AS.
