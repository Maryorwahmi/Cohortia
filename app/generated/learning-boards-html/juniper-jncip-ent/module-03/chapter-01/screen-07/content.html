AS_Path: Tracing the Route's Journey

Understanding the sequence of Autonomous Systems a route has traversed

AS_Path Attribute Overview

  • •Well-known mandatory attribute.
  • •Lists sequence of ASes traversed by a route.
  • •BGP speaker prepends its own AS number to the path when advertising to EBGP.
  • •Shorter AS_Paths are generally preferred.

Crucial for loop prevention and path manipulation.

Example: Path Comparison

Route A: AS_Path (65001 65002). Route B: AS_Path (65003 65004 65005). Route A will be preferred because it has a shorter AS_Path, implying fewer intermediate autonomous systems.

Traffic Engineering Use

AS-Path prepending involves adding your own AS number multiple times to the path. This makes your path appear longer to external ASes, discouraging them from sending inbound traffic via that route.

Key Takeaway

The AS_Path attribute is a fundamental mechanism for BGP to track route origins, prevent routing loops, and influence path selection based on the number of autonomous systems traversed.
