Multi-Exit Discriminator (MED): Influencing Inbound Traffic

How to guide external ASes on their entry point into your network

MED Attribute Overview

  • •Optional non-transitive attribute.
  • •Influences how other ASes send traffic into your AS.
  • •Lower MED value is preferred.

Used for inbound traffic engineering, but with a key limitation.

Example: Inbound Path Preference

Your AS has two links to ISP X. By advertising routes with a lower MED on Link 1, you encourage ISP X to send traffic into your AS via Link 1, optimizing inbound flow.

Critical Limitation

MED is ONLY compared between routes received from the *same* neighboring AS. If you peer with two different ISPs, their MED values are not compared against each other.

Key Takeaway

MED is a powerful tool for influencing inbound traffic from a specific neighboring AS, allowing you to guide their choice of entry point into your network, but its comparison scope is strictly limited to routes from the same AS.
