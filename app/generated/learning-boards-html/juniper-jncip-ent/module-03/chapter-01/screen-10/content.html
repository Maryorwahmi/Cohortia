BGP Attribute Deep Dive

Community: Tagging Routes for Policy

Using arbitrary tags for flexible BGP policy application

Community Attribute Overview

  • •Optional transitive attribute.
  • •Arbitrary 32-bit numbers used to tag routes.
  • •Allows flexible policy application across BGP speakers.

Includes well-known communities (e.g., no-export) and custom values.

Example: Policy Grouping

You can tag all routes from a specific customer with a 'customer-X' community. Any router configured to recognize 'customer-X' can then apply a specific policy, like prioritizing their traffic or restricting advertisement to certain peers.

Extended Communities

Extended communities offer even greater flexibility and are often used in advanced scenarios like VPNs (Virtual Private Networks) to carry additional routing information and policy directives.

Key Takeaway

BGP Communities provide a powerful and flexible mechanism for tagging routes with custom metadata, enabling granular policy application and coordination across diverse BGP environments.
