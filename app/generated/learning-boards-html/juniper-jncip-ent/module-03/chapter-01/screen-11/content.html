The BGP Best Path Selection Algorithm

Understanding the sequential decision-making process

Algorithm Overview

  • •Sequential process to select the optimal path.
  • •Evaluates BGP attributes in a predefined order.
  • •If a tie exists, proceeds to the next step.
  • •Higher-priority attributes override lower ones.

Critical for predicting BGP behavior and traffic engineering.

Example: Local Preference vs. AS_Path

A route with a Local Preference of 150 will always be preferred over a route with a Local Preference of 100, even if the latter has a significantly shorter AS_Path. Local Preference is evaluated earlier.

Misconception: Shortest Path Always Wins

Unlike IGPs, BGP does not simply choose the shortest path. Its policy-driven nature means attributes like Local Preference can easily override path length (AS_Path) to meet business or operational goals.

Key Takeaway

The BGP best path selection algorithm is a strict, sequential process where attributes are evaluated in a specific order, making it essential to understand this hierarchy for effective BGP policy design and troubleshooting.
