Common BGP Mistakes and Safety Notes

Avoiding pitfalls in BGP attribute configuration

Key Mistakes to Avoid

  • •Assuming MED influences all inbound traffic (only same AS).
  • •Misconfiguring Local Preference (can cause suboptimal outbound routing).
  • •Not understanding attribute transitivity (passed vs. local).

Always test BGP policy changes in a lab environment before production deployment.

Impact of Errors

Incorrect BGP configurations can have widespread internet impact, leading to traffic blackholes, suboptimal routing, or even service outages for your own and interconnected networks.

Best Practice: Lab Testing

Before implementing any BGP policy changes in a live network, always thoroughly test them in a controlled lab environment to predict their behavior and prevent unintended consequences.

Key Takeaway

Careful planning, thorough testing, and a deep understanding of BGP attribute behavior, especially MED's scope and Local Preference's impact, are essential to avoid common mistakes and ensure stable BGP operations.
