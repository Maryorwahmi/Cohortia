Local Preference: Influencing Outbound Traffic

How to steer traffic exiting your Autonomous System

Local Preference Overview

  • •Well-known discretionary attribute.
  • •Exchanged among IBGP peers within the same AS.
  • •Influences preferred exit point for outbound traffic.
  • •Higher local preference is preferred.

Ensures consistent outbound routing decisions across the entire autonomous system.

Scenario: Multi-Homed AS

Your AS connects to two ISPs. By setting a higher Local Preference for routes learned from ISP A, all internal routers will send outbound traffic through ISP A, even if ISP B offers a path that appears shorter by other metrics.

Important Note

Local Preference only affects how traffic leaves your AS. It has no impact on how other autonomous systems choose to send traffic into your AS.

Key Takeaway

Local Preference is a critical attribute for traffic engineering, allowing an AS to consistently prefer specific exit points for its outbound traffic by communicating preferences among internal BGP routers.
