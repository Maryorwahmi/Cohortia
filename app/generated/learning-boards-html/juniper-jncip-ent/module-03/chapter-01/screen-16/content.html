Key Concepts: BGP Attributes

Essential terms to remember for BGP policy and path selection

BGP Attributes

Metadata attached to BGP routes that influence path selection and enable policy-based routing.

Local Preference

Exchanged among IBGP peers to influence the preferred exit point from an AS for outbound traffic; higher is preferred.

AS_Path

Lists the sequence of AS numbers a route has traversed; shorter paths are generally preferred for directness and loop prevention.

Summary Takeaway

BGP attributes are the core mechanism for policy-based routing, with Local Preference and AS_Path being key influencers for outbound traffic control and path selection based on network topology.
