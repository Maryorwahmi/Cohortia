Origin: How a Route Enters BGP

Understanding the source of BGP route advertisements

Origin Attribute Overview

  • •Well-known mandatory attribute.
  • •Indicates how a route was learned by BGP.
  • •Values: IGP ('i'), EGP ('e'), Incomplete ('?').

Preference order: IGP > EGP > Incomplete.

Example: Origin Preference

If a router receives two routes for 10.0.0.0/8, one with Origin 'i' (from OSPF) and another with Origin '?' (from static redistribution), the route with 'i' will be preferred due to its higher origin preference.

Common Scenario for '?'

The 'Incomplete' origin often appears when routes are redistributed into BGP from other protocols (like static routes or connected interfaces) without explicitly setting the origin type in the BGP configuration.

Key Takeaway

The Origin attribute provides critical information about a route's source, influencing BGP's path selection by prioritizing routes learned from internal gateway protocols for stability and predictability.
