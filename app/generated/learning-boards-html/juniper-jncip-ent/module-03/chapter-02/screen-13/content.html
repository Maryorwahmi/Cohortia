Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

BGP Routing Policies

Configurations that control how BGP routes are processed, filtered, and advertised, crucial for traffic engineering.

`policy-options`

The Junos OS hierarchy where all BGP routing policies are defined and managed.

Term

A logical block within a policy statement, comprising `from` (match) and `then` (actions) statements, evaluated sequentially.

`from` statement

Defines the matching criteria for a route within a policy term, such as prefixes, protocols, or neighbors.

`then` statement

Defines the actions to be taken on a route that matches the `from` conditions, including `accept`, `reject`, or attribute modification.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering the structure and components of BGP policies is foundational for effective route control and traffic engineering in Junos OS.
