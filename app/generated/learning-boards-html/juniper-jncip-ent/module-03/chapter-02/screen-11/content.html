Avoiding Pitfalls: Implicit Actions and Verification

Critical Safety Considerations for BGP Policy Implementation

Common Mistake: Implicit Actions

Forgetting the implicit `reject` at the end of an import policy or implicit `accept` at the end of an export policy can lead to unintended route filtering or advertisement.

Best Practice: Explicit Defaults

Always include a final `then accept` or `then reject` term to explicitly define the default behavior for routes not matched by previous terms.

Key Takeaway

Thorough verification and explicit default actions are non-negotiable for safe and predictable BGP policy deployments. Always test in a lab first.

Verification Commands
`show route detail`Verify the presence and attributes of specific routes in the routing table.
`show bgp neighbor received-routes`Check routes received from a peer *before* import policy application.
`show bgp neighbor advertised-routes`Check routes advertised to a peer *after* export policy application.
Safety Note

BGP policy changes can have immediate and widespread impact. Always test thoroughly in a lab environment before applying to production networks.
