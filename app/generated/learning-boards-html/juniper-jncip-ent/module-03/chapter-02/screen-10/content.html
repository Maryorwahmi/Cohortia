Controlling Route Flow: Import vs. Export Policies

Understanding Inbound and Outbound Route Advertisement Control

Import Policy

Applied to routes received from a BGP peer, before they are installed into the local routing table. Controls what routes your router learns.

Export Policy

Applied to routes advertised to a BGP peer, from your local routing table. Controls what routes your router advertises to others.

Key Takeaway

Correctly applying import or export policies is fundamental for controlling route propagation, ensuring network stability, and implementing traffic engineering goals.
