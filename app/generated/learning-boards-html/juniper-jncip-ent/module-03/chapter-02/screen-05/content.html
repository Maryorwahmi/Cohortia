Building Blocks: Terms, `from`, and `then`

Sequential Evaluation and Default Actions in Junos Policies

Policy Composition

A BGP policy in Junos OS is made up of one or more terms, each defining specific match conditions and actions.

Sequential Evaluation

Terms are evaluated from top to bottom. The first matching term's actions are applied, and evaluation typically stops for that route.

Key Takeaway

Understanding term order and default actions is critical to prevent unintended route filtering or acceptance in your BGP policies.
