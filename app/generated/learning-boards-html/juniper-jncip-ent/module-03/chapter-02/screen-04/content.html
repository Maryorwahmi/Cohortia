The Foundation of BGP Routing Policies

Understanding the `policy-options` Hierarchy in Junos OS

Key Role

BGP policies are the cornerstone of traffic engineering and route control, allowing networks to move beyond BGP's default best path selection.

Configuration Hierarchy

In Junos OS, BGP routing policies are defined under the [edit policy-options] hierarchy, providing a centralized location for all policy configurations.

Key Takeaway

The `policy-options` hierarchy is where all BGP policy logic resides, enabling administrators to customize BGP behavior for specific network requirements.

Policy Capabilities
Manipulate BGP AttributesInfluence best path selection (e.g., Local Pref, MED).
Filter RoutesControl which routes are accepted or advertised.
Control Traffic FlowDirect inbound and outbound traffic paths.
