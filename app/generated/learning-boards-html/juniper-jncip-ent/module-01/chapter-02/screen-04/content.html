Core Concept

Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs)

Logically segmenting your physical network with 802.1Q

What is a VLAN?

A VLAN is a logical grouping of network devices that allows them to communicate as if they were on the same physical segment, regardless of their actual physical location. It creates separate broadcast domains.

Why Use VLANs?

  • Improved Security: Isolates sensitive traffic.
  • Enhanced Performance: Reduces broadcast traffic.
  • Simplified Management: Easier to organize users and devices.
  • Flexibility: Devices can move without re-cabling.
Key Takeaway

VLANs are essential for creating efficient, secure, and manageable network segments on a single physical switch, using 802.1Q for traffic identification.
