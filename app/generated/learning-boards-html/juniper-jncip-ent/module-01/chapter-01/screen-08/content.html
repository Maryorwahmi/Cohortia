Advanced Packet Handling

Beyond Basic Forwarding: Filters, NAT, QoS, and Punting

Specialized processing and control plane interaction.

PFE's Advanced Capabilities

The PFE offloads complex tasks from the RE, performing them directly in hardware. This maintains forwarding speed even with advanced network policies.

When Punting Occurs

  • Routing protocol updates (e.g., OSPF, BGP).
  • Management traffic destined for the device (e.g., SSH, Telnet, SNMP).
  • Packets requiring deep inspection or complex policy decisions not handled by PFE hardware.
Key Takeaway

The PFE handles advanced processing in hardware, only punting packets to the RE when control plane interaction is strictly required.
