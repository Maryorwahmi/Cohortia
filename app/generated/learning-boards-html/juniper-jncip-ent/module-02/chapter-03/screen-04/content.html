Core Concept

The OSPF Backbone: Area 0 Contiguity

Area 0, the OSPF backbone, must always be logically and physically connected. All non-backbone areas must have a direct connection to Area 0.

  • •Central Role: Area 0 acts as the central hub for all inter-area routing traffic.
  • •Direct Connection: Non-backbone areas must connect directly to Area 0.
  • •No Segmentation: Area 0 itself cannot be split into disconnected segments.
  • •Routing Integrity: Fragmentation leads to routing failures and network instability.
Scenario

Imagine a new office connecting to your network through Area 1, but Area 1 is the only path to your existing Area 0. This creates a logical break in Area 0.

Key Takeaway

A contiguous Area 0 is non-negotiable for OSPF. Fragmentation breaks inter-area communication, demanding a solution to restore connectivity.
