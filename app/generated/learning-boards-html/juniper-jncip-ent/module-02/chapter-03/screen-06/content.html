Key Requirement

Selecting Your Transit Area

The transit area is the non-backbone OSPF area that facilitates the virtual link. It must meet specific criteria for the link to function.

  • •Shared Area: ABRs forming the virtual link must share this common area.
  • •Reachability: ABRs must be able to reach each other through intra-area routing within the transit area.
  • •Critical Restriction: Cannot be a stub area or an NSSA.
  • •LSA Filtering: Stub/NSSA areas filter LSA types (Type 3, 5/7) essential for virtual link operation.
Common Mistake

Attempting to establish a virtual link across a stub or NSSA area. This will inevitably fail due to LSA filtering, preventing the necessary information exchange.

Transit Area Type Comparison
Standard Area

Allows all LSA types. Suitable for Virtual Links.

Stub/NSSA Area

Filters Type 3, 5/7 LSAs. Not suitable for Virtual Links.

Key Takeaway

Always verify that your chosen OSPF transit area is a standard area. Using a stub or NSSA will prevent the virtual link from forming due to LSA filtering.
