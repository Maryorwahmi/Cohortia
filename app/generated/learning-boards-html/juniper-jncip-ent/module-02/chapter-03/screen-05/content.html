Definition

OSPF Virtual Links: Bridging the Backbone

A virtual link is a logical connection between two Area Border Routers (ABRs) that share a common non-backbone area, known as the transit area.

  • •Purpose: Restores Area 0 contiguity when it becomes fragmented.
  • •Endpoints: Always established between two ABRs.
  • •Mechanism: Tunnels Area 0 across a non-backbone transit area.
  • •Behavior: Treated as an unnumbered point-to-point link within Area 0.
Analogy

Think of a virtual link as a secure, logical tunnel that allows two disconnected parts of a highway (Area 0) to communicate by passing through a side road (transit area).
