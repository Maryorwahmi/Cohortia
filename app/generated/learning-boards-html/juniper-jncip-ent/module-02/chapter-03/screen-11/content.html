Simple Password Authentication: Basic Security

This method uses a plain-text password sent directly in OSPF packets, offering minimal security.

  • •Mechanism: Password included unencrypted in OSPF packet header.
  • •Verification: Receiving router compares received password with its configured password.
  • •Vulnerability: Easily sniffed from the network, making it prone to interception.
  • •Recommendation: Not suitable for production environments due to low security.
Security Risk

A simple packet capture can reveal the password, allowing an attacker to impersonate a legitimate router and inject false routing information.

Key Takeaway

Simple password authentication is inherently insecure due to plain-text transmission. Avoid it in production networks to prevent easy compromise of your OSPF routing.
