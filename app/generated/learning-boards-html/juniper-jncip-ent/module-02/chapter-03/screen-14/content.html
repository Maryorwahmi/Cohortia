Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Virtual Link

A logical connection between two ABRs over a non-backbone transit area, used to restore OSPF Area 0 contiguity.

Transit Area

A standard OSPF area that is used to carry a virtual link, connecting two ABRs that are part of a fragmented Area 0.

OSPF Authentication

A security mechanism to verify the identity of OSPF routers and protect against unauthorized routing updates.

Summary Takeaway

Virtual links provide a crucial logical bridge for Area 0 contiguity, while OSPF authentication is essential for securing your routing infrastructure against malicious attacks.
