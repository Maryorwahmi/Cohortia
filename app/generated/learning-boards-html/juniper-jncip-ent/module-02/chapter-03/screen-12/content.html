MD5 Authentication: Robust OSPF Protection

MD5 authentication uses cryptographic hashing to secure OSPF packets, providing a high level of integrity and authenticity.

  • •Mechanism: Generates a message digest (hash) of the OSPF packet and a shared secret key.
  • •Key Security: The secret key is never sent over the network, only the digest.
  • •Verification: Receiving router computes its own digest and compares it to the received one.
  • •Replay Protection: Includes a sequence number to guard against replay attacks.
  • •Recommendation: The preferred method for production OSPF networks.
Security Advantage

Because the key is never transmitted, MD5 authentication is far more resistant to passive eavesdropping and active tampering compared to simple password authentication.
