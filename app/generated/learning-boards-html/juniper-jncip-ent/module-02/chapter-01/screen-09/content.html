Understanding Transit OSPF Areas

Passing Traffic Between OSPF Areas

Intermediary Role

A transit area has two or more ABRs and is used to pass traffic between other areas or to the backbone.

Traffic Flow

Traffic merely passes through it; the transit area is not the source or destination of the traffic.

Virtual Link Support

Can be used to connect virtual links, a method for connecting non-contiguous Area 0 segments.

Key Takeaway

Transit areas are specialized for forwarding traffic between other OSPF areas, often involving multiple ABRs or virtual links.
