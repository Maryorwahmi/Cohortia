The Role of Area Border Routers (ABRs)

Connecting Areas and Summarizing Routing Information

Inter-Area Connector

ABRs are routers that connect a non-backbone area to Area 0, facilitating inter-area communication.

Multiple LSDBs

Each ABR maintains a separate Link-State Database for every area it is connected to.

Route Summarization

ABRs summarize routing information between areas, preventing detailed link-state flooding and improving scalability.

Key Takeaway

ABRs are essential for inter-area routing, acting as gateways that summarize and exchange routing information between areas and the backbone.
