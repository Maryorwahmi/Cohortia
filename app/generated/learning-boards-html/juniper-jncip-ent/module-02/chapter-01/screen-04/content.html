Why OSPF Areas? The Scalability Challenge

Overcoming the Limitations of a Flat OSPF Domain

Excessive LSDB Size

All routers maintain a complete Link-State Database for the entire network, consuming significant memory.

High CPU Utilization

Every topology change triggers SPF algorithm recalculations across all routers, straining CPU resources.

Slow Convergence

Network-wide SPF runs delay the time it takes for the network to stabilize after a change, impacting service availability.

Key Takeaway

Flat OSPF domains are not scalable for large networks due to excessive resource demands and slow convergence.
