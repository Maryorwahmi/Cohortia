Module 2›Chapter 2.3›Vulnerability Scanners

What They Do

  • •Quickly identify a vast number of potential weaknesses.
  • •Automate checks against databases of known vulnerabilities.
  • •Cover broad network ranges and many systems efficiently.
  • •Generate detailed reports for analysis.
Why They Matter

Scanners act as a crucial first pass, identifying common and easily detectable flaws, freeing up human testers for more complex analysis.

Popular Tools

Nessus (Commercial)

A widely used, comprehensive vulnerability scanner known for its extensive plugin database and accurate reporting.

Qualys (Commercial)

A cloud-based solution offering vulnerability management, compliance, and web application scanning.

OpenVAS (Open Source)

A robust open-source vulnerability scanner, part of the Greenbone Vulnerability Management suite.

Key Takeaway

Vulnerability scanners are essential automated tools that efficiently identify a broad range of known weaknesses, complementing manual testing efforts.
