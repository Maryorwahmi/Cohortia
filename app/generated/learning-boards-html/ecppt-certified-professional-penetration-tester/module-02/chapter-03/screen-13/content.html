Common Mistakes to Avoid

Blindly Trusting Scanners

Failing to manually verify critical findings, leading to false positives or negatives.

Improper Prioritization

Focusing on low-impact findings while ignoring critical, high-risk vulnerabilities.

Poor Documentation

Not thoroughly documenting findings, their impact, and remediation steps.

Safety and Ethical Conduct

Explicit Permission

Never attempt to exploit a vulnerability without explicit, written permission from the asset owner.

Adhere to Scope

Strictly follow the defined scope of your engagement and legal frameworks.

Identify & Report

Your primary role is to identify vulnerabilities and report them, not to cause damage.

Key Takeaway

Ethical conduct, adherence to scope, and thorough verification are paramount in vulnerability analysis to ensure safety and effective risk management.
