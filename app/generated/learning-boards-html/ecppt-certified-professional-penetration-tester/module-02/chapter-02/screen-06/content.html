Nmap Fundamentals

Identifying Open Ports

The first step in understanding target services and potential entry points.

What are Ports?

  • •Communication endpoints on a network host.
  • •Numbered from 0 to 65535.
  • •Services 'listen' on specific ports for incoming connections.

Why Scan Ports?

  • •Reveals active services and applications.
  • •Identifies potential entry points for attacks.
  • •Helps map the target's network attack surface.
  • •Guides further, more targeted reconnaissance.
Key Takeaway

Port scanning is the initial reconnaissance step to uncover active services, providing a roadmap for deeper security analysis.
