Active vs. Passive Information Gathering

Understanding the crucial distinction and its implications for reconnaissance.

Passive Reconnaissance

  • •No direct interaction with the target system.
  • •Gathers information from publicly available sources (OSINT).
  • •Low risk of detection and legal issues.
  • •Examples: Google searches, WHOIS lookups, social media analysis.

Active Reconnaissance

  • •Direct interaction with the target system (sending packets).
  • •Gathers precise, real-time data.
  • •Higher risk of detection and legal consequences.
  • •Examples: Port scanning (Nmap), banner grabbing (Netcat).
Key Takeaway

Always obtain explicit, written permission before engaging in active reconnaissance to avoid legal repercussions and maintain ethical standards.
