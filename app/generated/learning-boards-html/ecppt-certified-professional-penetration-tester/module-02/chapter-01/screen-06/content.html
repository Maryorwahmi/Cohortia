Practical Application

Google Dorking: Uncovering Sensitive Files

Real-World Examples of Advanced Search Queries

Common Dorking Targets

  • •Configuration files with sensitive credentials.
  • •Open directories exposing file listings.
  • •Login pages and administrative interfaces.
  • •Documents containing internal information or employee data.
Key Takeaway

Effective Google Dorking can quickly identify publicly exposed sensitive information, providing critical intelligence for a penetration test.

Example Google Dorks
site:example.com filetype:pdf
# Finds PDF documents on example.com

intitle:"index of" "config"
# Finds open directories with 'config' in the title

inurl:admin login
# Finds admin login pages

site:example.com "password"
# Finds pages on example.com containing the word "password"
Ethical Reminder

Always ensure your Google Dorking activities are within legal and ethical boundaries. Only target domains you have explicit permission to test.
