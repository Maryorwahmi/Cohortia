Key Concepts

Advanced Terms and Tools

DNS Enumeration

The process of locating DNS records for a domain to gather information about its infrastructure.

Passive Reconnaissance

Gathering information about a target without sending any packets directly to the target system, minimizing detection.

Maltego

A graphical link analysis tool used for gathering and connecting information for OSINT and forensic investigations.

Summary Takeaway

DNS enumeration, passive reconnaissance, and Maltego are crucial for mapping infrastructure, maintaining stealth, and visualizing complex OSINT data for actionable intelligence.
