Data Aggregation

Aggregating OSINT: Visualizing Complex Relationships

Connecting Disparate Data Points for Deeper Insights

Why Aggregate OSINT?

  • •Overcome data overload from multiple sources.
  • •Identify hidden connections between entities (people, domains, IPs).
  • •Visualize complex relationships for easier understanding.
  • •Transform raw data into actionable intelligence for attack planning.
Key Takeaway

Aggregating and visualizing OSINT data is crucial for making sense of complex information and identifying strategic attack vectors.

Maltego: Link Analysis Tool

Collects data from various OSINT sources.

Transforms data into visual entities (domains, IPs, people).

Connects entities to show relationships and patterns.

Helps identify potential attack paths and vulnerabilities.

Example Insight

Maltego can reveal that multiple subdomains are hosted on the same IP address, indicating a single point of failure or a shared infrastructure.
