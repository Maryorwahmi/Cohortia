Core Concept

The Power of Passive Reconnaissance

Gathering Intelligence Without Direct Interaction

What is OSINT?

  • •Collection of data from publicly available sources.
  • •No direct interaction with the target's systems or network.
  • •Minimizes risk of detection and leaves no digital footprint on target.
  • •Forms the critical foundation for subsequent active testing phases.
Key Takeaway

Passive information gathering is essential for building a comprehensive target profile stealthily, enabling more focused and effective penetration tests.

Common Misconception

OSINT is not about illegal hacking. It's about leveraging publicly available information, similar to how a journalist or market researcher operates, but with a security focus.
