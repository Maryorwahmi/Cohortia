Avoiding Common Mistakes & Ethical Considerations

Ensuring Effective and Responsible Testing

Intercept Toggle: Forgetting to turn 'Intercept off' can freeze your browser. Toggle it off when not actively intercepting.

CA Certificate: Not installing Burp's CA certificate prevents HTTPS interception and causes browser errors.

Proxy Settings: Double-check that browser proxy settings match Burp's listener address and port.

Key Takeaway

Adhere to ethical guidelines and avoid common configuration errors to ensure effective and responsible web application penetration testing.

Ethical Testing Principles

Explicit Permission: Always obtain written authorization before testing any system.

Controlled Environment: Conduct tests in isolated or staging environments to prevent impact on live systems.

Scope Awareness: Understand and respect the defined boundaries of your penetration test.

Warning: Unauthorized testing or modification of live systems can lead to severe legal and professional consequences.
