Advanced Takeaways

Deepening Your Understanding of Traffic Control

CA Certificate

A digital certificate needed in the browser to allow Burp Suite to decrypt and re-encrypt HTTPS traffic.

Intercept

The act of pausing an HTTP request or response in Burp Suite for inspection and modification.

Repeater

A Burp Suite tool that allows manual manipulation and resending of individual HTTP requests.

Summary Takeaway

Effective web application penetration testing relies on understanding HTTPS interception, active traffic manipulation, and iterative testing with tools like Repeater.
