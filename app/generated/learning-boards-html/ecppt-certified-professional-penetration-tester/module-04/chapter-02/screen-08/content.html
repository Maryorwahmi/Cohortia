Key Takeaway

Subdomain enumeration is crucial for uncovering hidden applications and services, expanding the attack surface for further testing.

Understanding Subdomain Enumeration

Subdomain enumeration is the process of finding subdomains associated with a target domain. These subdomains often host different applications or services that might have varying security configurations.

  • **Expanded Attack Surface:** Each subdomain can be a potential entry point.
  • **Different Security Postures:** Subdomains may have weaker security than the main domain.
  • **Forgotten Assets:** Old or unmaintained subdomains are common sources of vulnerabilities.
  • **Tools:** `gobuster`, `sublist3r`, `Amass` are popular choices.
Common Mistake

Using too small or outdated wordlists can lead to missed subdomains. Always use comprehensive and up-to-date wordlists.
