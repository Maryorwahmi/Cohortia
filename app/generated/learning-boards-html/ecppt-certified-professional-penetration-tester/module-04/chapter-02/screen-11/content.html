Port Scanning for Web Apps

Scanning for open HTTP (80) and HTTPS (443) ports is fundamental, but also check less common web ports like 8080, 8443, etc.

  • **Standard Ports:** 80 (HTTP), 443 (HTTPS).
  • **Alternative Ports:** 8080, 8443, 8000, 4443.
  • **Purpose:** Identify active web services and potential hidden interfaces.
  • **Tools:** `nmap` (covered in network recon).
Why it matters

Uncovering non-standard web ports can reveal development or administrative interfaces that are often less secured.

Safety & Ethical Considerations

Overly aggressive scanning can trigger IDS or cause denial of service. Always respect the scope and avoid disruption.

  • **Avoid IDS Triggers:** Use rate-limited scanning.
  • **Prevent DoS:** Never intentionally disrupt services.
  • **Respect Scope:** Stay within the agreed-upon testing boundaries.
  • **Ethical Hacking:** Prioritize non-disruptive methods on live systems.
Key Takeaway

Conduct active reconnaissance ethically and cautiously, prioritizing non-disruptive methods, especially on live production systems.
