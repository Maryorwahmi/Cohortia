Lab Walkthrough
Practical Recon: DNS and Technology
Applying `dig`, `whois`, and `whatweb` against a target
Step 1: DNS Information with `dig` and `whois`
# Target: http://testphp.vulnweb.com/dig A testphp.vulnweb.comdig NS testphp.vulnweb.comwhois testphp.vulnweb.com

**Question:** What IP addresses, name servers, and registration details did you find? Note them down.

Step 2: Technology Fingerprinting with `whatweb`
whatweb http://testphp.vulnweb.com/

**Question:** What web server, programming language, and other technologies did `whatweb` identify? Note down the versions.

Expected Output for `dig A`
;; ANSWER SECTION:testphp.vulnweb.com.    300     IN      A       62.113.227.241
Expected Output for `whatweb` (Partial)
http://testphp.vulnweb.com/ [200 OK] Apache[2.2.14], PHP[5.3.2], X-Powered-By[PHP/5.3.2]
Key Takeaway

Hands-on practice with `dig`, `whois`, and `whatweb` provides direct experience in gathering crucial DNS and technology fingerprinting data.
