Recommended for Targets

Host-only Adapter

  • Isolation: Creates a private network between host and VMs.
  • Connectivity: VMs can talk to each other and host, but NOT internet.
  • Security: Safest for vulnerable targets like Metasploitable2.
For Updates

NAT (Network Address Translation)

  • Internet Access: VMs can access the internet through the host.
  • Inbound: External machines CANNOT initiate connections to VMs.
  • Use Case: Good for Kali to download updates, but not for vulnerable targets.
Avoid for Targets

Bridged Adapter

  • Direct Connection: VM appears as a separate device on your physical network.
  • Exposure: Vulnerable targets would be exposed to your local network and potentially the internet.
  • Danger: Highly risky for Metasploitable2.
Key Takeaway

Always use a Host-only Adapter for vulnerable targets to ensure complete isolation, and understand the risks of NAT and Bridged modes.
