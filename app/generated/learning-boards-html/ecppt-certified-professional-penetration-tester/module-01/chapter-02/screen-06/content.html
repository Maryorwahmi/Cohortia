Option 1

Oracle VirtualBox

  • Open-Source: Completely free and community-driven.
  • Cross-Platform: Runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, and Solaris hosts.
  • Robust Features: Offers extensive capabilities for VM management.
  • Wide Adoption: Excellent community support and documentation.
Installation Tip

Simple Setup Process

Both VirtualBox and VMware Player follow a standard software installation. Download the installer from their official websites and simply follow the on-screen prompts. No complex configurations are needed at this stage.

Option 2

VMware Workstation Player

  • Polished UI: Offers a sleek and intuitive user experience.
  • Performance: Often provides better performance, especially for Windows guests.
  • Commercial Backing: Free version of a professional-grade product.
  • Integration: Good integration with VMware's ecosystem.
Key Takeaway

Choose either VirtualBox or VMware Workstation Player based on your preference; both provide excellent, free platforms for building your lab.
