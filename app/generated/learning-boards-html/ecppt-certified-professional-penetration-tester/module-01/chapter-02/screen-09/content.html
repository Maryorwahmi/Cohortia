What is Metasploitable2?

The Deliberately Vulnerable VM

Metasploitable2 is a Linux virtual machine designed by Rapid7 with numerous security flaws, misconfigurations, and outdated services. It serves as a legal and safe environment for practicing penetration testing.

Why Use It?
  • Safe Practice: Explore vulnerabilities without harming real systems.
  • Legal: Designed for ethical hacking practice.
  • Diverse Flaws: Contains a wide range of security weaknesses to exploit.
  • Pre-configured: Saves time on manual setup and configuration.
Getting Metasploitable2

Download as OVA Appliance

Metasploitable2 is typically downloaded as a pre-built virtual appliance in OVA format from SourceForge. This format simplifies the import process into your virtualization software.

Example: Search for 'Metasploitable2 OVA' on SourceForge.net and download the `.zip` file, then extract the `.ova`.

Key Takeaway

Metasploitable2 is your essential, legal, and safe training ground for practicing a wide array of penetration testing techniques.
