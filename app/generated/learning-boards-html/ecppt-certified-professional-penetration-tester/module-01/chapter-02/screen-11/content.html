Core Principle

The Need for Isolation

It is absolutely crucial to establish a network configuration that allows your attacking machine (Kali) to communicate with your target (Metasploitable2) while completely isolating both from your host network and the internet. This prevents accidental exposure of vulnerable systems.

Why it Matters
  • Preventing Damage: Avoid accidental harm to real-world systems.
  • Legal Compliance: Ensure all testing is within legal and ethical boundaries.
  • Security: Protect your home network from vulnerable lab components.
  • Focus: Maintain a controlled environment for learning without external interference.
Solution

Host-only Adapter

The Host-only Adapter creates a private network segment that exists only between your host machine and its virtual machines. VMs on this network can communicate with each other and the host, but NOT with the external network (internet). This is the safest option for our vulnerable lab.

Analogy: Think of it as a private, walled-off room where your VMs can play safely, but can't open the door to the outside world.

Key Takeaway

Correct network configuration, especially using a Host-only Adapter, is the cornerstone of a secure and responsible penetration testing lab.
