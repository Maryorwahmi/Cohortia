PTES Phase 2

Intelligence Gathering

This phase focuses on collecting as much information about the target as possible, both passively and actively.

Key Takeaway

Thorough intelligence gathering builds a detailed target profile, essential for identifying potential vulnerabilities and attack vectors.

Example Tools

Passive: `whois`, Shodan. Active: `Nmap` for port scanning.

Types of Intelligence Gathering

Passive Reconnaissance

Collecting public information without direct interaction (e.g., search engines, social media, `whois`).

Active Reconnaissance

Directly interacting with the target to gather information (e.g., port scanning with `Nmap`, banner grabbing).

Goal: Detailed Profile

Build a comprehensive understanding of target infrastructure, applications, and potential weaknesses.

Avoid Early Stoppage

Incomplete intelligence can lead to missed attack vectors and an ineffective test.
