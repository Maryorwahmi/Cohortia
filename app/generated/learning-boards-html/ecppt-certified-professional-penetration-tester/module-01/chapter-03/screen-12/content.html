PTES Phase 5

Exploitation

Leveraging identified vulnerabilities to gain unauthorized access to the target system.

Key Takeaway

Exploitation demonstrates the real-world impact of vulnerabilities and the level of access achievable, providing concrete evidence of risk.

Common Mistake

Focusing only on gaining access without documenting the steps or understanding the full implications of the compromise.

Exploitation Techniques & Tools

Metasploit Framework

Utilizing a vast database of exploits and payloads to compromise systems.

Custom Payloads

Crafting specialized code to exploit unique or complex vulnerabilities.

Exploiting Misconfigurations

Leveraging insecure default settings or improperly configured services.

Objective: Gain Access

The primary goal is to achieve unauthorized access and demonstrate its feasibility.
