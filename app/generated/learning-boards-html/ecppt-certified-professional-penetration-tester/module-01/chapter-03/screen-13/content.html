PTES Phase 6

Post Exploitation

Actions taken after gaining initial access, focusing on understanding the full extent of a breach.

Key Takeaway

This phase reveals the true scope and potential damage of a successful breach, including access to critical assets and persistence.

Common Mistake

Stopping after initial access without exploring further compromise misses the full impact of a vulnerability.

Key Activities in Post Exploitation

Privilege Escalation

Gaining higher-level access within the compromised system (e.g., root, administrator).

Lateral Movement

Moving to other systems on the network from the initial point of compromise.

Data Exfiltration

Identifying and simulating the theft of sensitive data from the target.

Establishing Persistence

Ensuring continued access to the compromised system for future operations.
