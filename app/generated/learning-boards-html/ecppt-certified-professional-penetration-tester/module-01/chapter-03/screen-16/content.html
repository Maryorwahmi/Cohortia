PTES Phases to Master

PTES

A comprehensive, seven-phase framework covering the entire penetration testing lifecycle from pre-engagement to reporting.

Intelligence Gathering

The PTES phase dedicated to collecting information about the target, both passively and actively, to build a detailed profile.

Reporting

The final PTES phase, involving documenting findings, impacts, and actionable remediation recommendations for the client.

Summary Takeaway

PTES provides a robust framework for professional penetration testing, guiding you from initial reconnaissance to delivering actionable reports.
