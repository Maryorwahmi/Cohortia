PTES Phase 7

Reporting

The final phase involves documenting all findings, impacts, and actionable recommendations for remediation.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive, clear, and actionable report is the primary deliverable, empowering clients to effectively improve their security posture.

Common Mistake

Delivering a report that is too technical or too vague, hindering the client's ability to understand and act on findings.

Elements of a Professional Report

Documented Findings

Detailed list of all identified vulnerabilities and exploited weaknesses.

Impact Assessment

Explanation of the potential risks and business impact of each vulnerability.

Actionable Recommendations

Clear, practical steps for clients to remediate identified security flaws.

Professional & Clear

Presented in a way that is understandable for both technical and non-technical audiences.
