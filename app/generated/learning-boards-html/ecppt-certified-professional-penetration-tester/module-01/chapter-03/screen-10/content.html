Threat Modeling

Analyzing gathered intelligence to identify potential threats, attack vectors, and prioritize testing efforts.

Key Takeaway

Threat modeling helps focus testing efforts on the most critical assets and likely attack paths, increasing efficiency and impact.

Common Mistake

Skipping threat modeling leads to unfocused vulnerability analysis and potentially missed critical risks.

Steps in Threat Modeling

Identify Critical Assets

Determine which data, systems, or applications are most valuable to the client.

Identify Potential Attackers

Consider motivations, capabilities, and typical attack patterns of adversaries.

Map Attack Vectors

Visualize how attackers might exploit weaknesses to compromise assets.

Prioritize Testing Efforts

Focus resources on the most critical and probable attack scenarios.
