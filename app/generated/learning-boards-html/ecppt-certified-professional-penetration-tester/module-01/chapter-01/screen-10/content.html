Pen Test Phases

Covering Tracks and Reporting

Removing evidence and delivering actionable insights.

Covering Tracks

  • Removing evidence of intrusion (clearing logs, deleting tools).
  • Restoring the system to its pre-test state.
Example

Deleting temporary files and command history from a compromised server to hide activity.

Reporting

  • Documenting all findings, vulnerabilities, and exploitation steps.
  • Providing actionable recommendations for remediation.
Key Takeaway

Covering Tracks ensures a clean exit and forensic understanding, while Reporting translates technical findings into actionable insights for the client, making it the most valuable deliverable.
