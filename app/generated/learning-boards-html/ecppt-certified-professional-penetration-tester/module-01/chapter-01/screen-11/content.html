Ethical Conduct

The Importance of Scope and Authorization

Operating within legal and ethical boundaries is non-negotiable.

Scope of Work (SOW)

  • Defines systems, applications, and networks to be tested.
  • Outlines permitted test types and duration.
Example

A SOW might specify testing only the public-facing web server, not the internal network.

Rules of Engagement (ROE)

  • Legally binding document outlining permissions and restrictions.
  • Crucial for avoiding legal repercussions and maintaining trust.
Key Takeaway

Always obtain explicit, written authorization (SOW/ROE) before conducting any penetration testing activities to define scope, prevent legal issues, and ensure ethical conduct.
