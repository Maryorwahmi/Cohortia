Ethical Boundaries

Common Mistakes and Ethical Boundaries

Navigating real-world scenarios with integrity.

Common Mistakes

  • Testing systems outside the agreed-upon scope.
  • Failing to report out-of-scope findings to the client immediately.
  • Not adhering to data privacy laws when handling sensitive information.

Ethical Actions

  • Strictly follow the Scope of Work (SOW) and Rules of Engagement (ROE).
  • Communicate any out-of-scope discoveries to the client for guidance.
  • Prioritize client trust and legal compliance above all else.
Key Takeaway

Ethical penetration testing demands strict adherence to scope, transparent communication, and respect for legal frameworks to maintain trust and avoid severe repercussions.
