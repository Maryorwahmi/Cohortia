Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Vulnerability Scanning

Using automated tools to identify known weaknesses and security flaws in a target system.

Exploitation

The process of leveraging a vulnerability to gain unauthorized access or control over a system.

Post-Exploitation

Actions taken after gaining initial access, such as privilege escalation, lateral movement, and data exfiltration.

Reporting

The final phase where findings, vulnerabilities, and recommendations are documented and presented to the client.

Rules of Engagement (ROE)

A document outlining the scope, limitations, and legal agreements for a penetration test.

Scope of Work (SOW)

A document defining the systems, applications, and networks to be included in a penetration test.

Summary Takeaway

The penetration testing lifecycle involves systematic phases from scanning to reporting, all governed by strict rules of engagement and ethical considerations to ensure effective and lawful security assessments.
