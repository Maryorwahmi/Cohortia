Core Concept

What is Penetration Testing?

Simulating attacks to find and fix vulnerabilities proactively.

The Purpose

  • Identify exploitable weaknesses in systems, networks, or applications.
  • Provide actionable recommendations to strengthen security defenses.
  • Validate existing security controls and compliance requirements.

Analogy: The Ethical Burglar

Imagine hiring a professional burglar to test your home security. They try every entry point, but their goal is to report weaknesses, not to steal. This helps you fortify your home.

Key Takeaway

Penetration testing is a controlled, authorized simulation of a cyberattack to identify and remediate vulnerabilities, enhancing an organization's overall security posture.
