Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Penetration Testing

A simulated cyberattack to identify exploitable vulnerabilities in systems, networks, or applications.

Ethical Hacking

A broader term for security assessment techniques, including pen testing, performed with explicit permission and within legal boundaries.

Black-box Testing

A pen test where the tester has no prior knowledge of the target system's internal structure or code.

White-box Testing

A pen test where the tester has full knowledge of the target system's internal structure, source code, and configurations.

Grey-box Testing

A hybrid pen test approach where the tester has some limited knowledge of the target system, often like an authenticated user.

Reconnaissance

The initial phase of a penetration test focused on gathering information about the target, often using OSINT.

Summary Takeaway

Penetration testing and ethical hacking are proactive security measures, with various types and methodologies, all underpinned by strict ethical and legal considerations.
