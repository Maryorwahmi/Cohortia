Pen Test Phases

Reconnaissance and Scanning

The initial steps of information gathering and vulnerability identification.

Reconnaissance (Information Gathering)

  • Gathering public information about the target.
  • Utilizes Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) like social media, public records.
  • Aims to understand the target's footprint without direct interaction.

Scanning (Vulnerability Identification)

  • Using automated tools to find live hosts and open ports.
  • Identifying potential vulnerabilities and security flaws.
  • Prepares for the exploitation phase by mapping attack surface.
Key Takeaway

Reconnaissance and Scanning are foundational phases of a pen test, systematically gathering information and identifying potential weaknesses to inform subsequent exploitation efforts.
