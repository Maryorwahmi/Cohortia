Key Concepts
Essential terms from this chapter
Post-Exploitation

The phase after initial access, focusing on understanding the compromised system, escalating privileges, and achieving objectives.

Initial Foothold

The first successful access gained on a target system, typically a low-privileged shell, serving as the entry point.

System Enumeration

The process of gathering detailed information about a compromised system, including OS, network, processes, and users.

Summary Takeaway

Post-exploitation begins with securing an initial foothold and thoroughly enumerating the system to inform all subsequent actions.
