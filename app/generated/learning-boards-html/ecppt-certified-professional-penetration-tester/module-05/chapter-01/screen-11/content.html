Avoiding Common Post-Exploitation Pitfalls
Learning from typical errors to enhance your effectiveness.
Persistence Errors
  • •Incorrect Paths: Using non-existent absolute paths for payloads.
  • •Missing Executable Bit: Forgetting chmod +x on Linux scripts.
  • •High Visibility: Creating obvious user accounts or placing payloads in easily discovered locations.
Session & Command Errors
  • •Unstable Migration: Migrating Meterpreter to a short-lived or unstable process.
  • •OS-Specific Syntax: Using commands or paths meant for one OS on another.
  • •Complex `execute` Commands: Running intricate commands via execute without proper escaping.
Operational Oversight
  • •Lack of Documentation: Failing to record actions, findings, and persistence methods.
  • •Ignoring Stealth: Not considering detectability, leading to early discovery.
  • •Insufficient Recon: Proceeding without a thorough understanding of the target environment.
Best Practices
  • •Verify Paths & Permissions: Always double-check payload locations and executability.
  • •Choose Stable Processes: Migrate to long-running, legitimate system processes.
  • •Prioritize Stealth: Use subtle persistence methods and blend in with normal activity.
  • •Document Everything: Maintain detailed records of all actions and findings.
Key Takeaway

Learning from common mistakes and adhering to best practices significantly improves the success rate and stealth of post-exploitation activities.
