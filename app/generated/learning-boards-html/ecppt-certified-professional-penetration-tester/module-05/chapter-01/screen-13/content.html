Key Concepts
Expanding your post-exploitation vocabulary
Persistence

Techniques used to maintain access to a compromised system across reboots or service restarts, ensuring continued control.

Meterpreter

An advanced, dynamic, and extensible payload within the Metasploit Framework for comprehensive post-exploitation activities.

Process Migration

The act of moving a Meterpreter session from one process to another on the target system to improve stability or evade detection.

Summary Takeaway

Persistence, Meterpreter, and process migration are critical techniques for maintaining and managing control over a compromised system effectively.
