Post-Exploitation Best Practices & OS Considerations
Systematically securing and managing your foothold.
Core Post-Exploitation Principles
  • •Systematic Approach: Always confirm access, enumerate thoroughly, and establish persistence in a structured manner.
  • •Leverage Tools: Utilize powerful tools like Meterpreter to streamline complex interactions.
  • •Meticulous Documentation: Record all findings, actions, and observations for reporting and reproducibility.
  • •Prioritize Stealth: Choose methods that minimize visibility and detectability to avoid early discovery.
Operating System Adaptability
  • •Windows vs. Linux: Recognize that persistence, enumeration, and interaction techniques vary significantly between OS types.
  • •Tooling Differences: Be proficient with OS-specific commands and utilities (e.g., schtasks on Windows, cron on Linux).
  • •Contextual Awareness: Understand the underlying system logic and common configurations for each OS.
  • •Avoid Cross-OS Errors: Do not attempt to apply techniques from one OS directly to another without adaptation.
Concrete Example

After gaining access to a Linux server, you would use crontab -e for persistence, not schtasks, demonstrating OS-specific adaptation.

Key Takeaway

A systematic, documented, and OS-aware approach is fundamental for successful and stealthy post-exploitation operations.
