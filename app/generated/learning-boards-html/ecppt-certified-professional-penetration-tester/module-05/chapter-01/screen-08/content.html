meterpreter > migrate 588
Migrating to 588...
Migrate completed successfully.
Key Process Management Operations
  • •`ps`: Lists all running processes, providing PID, user, and path. Essential for reconnaissance.
  • •`kill `: Terminates a specified process on the target system.
  • •`migrate `: Moves your Meterpreter session into a different process.
  • •Stability & Stealth: Migration to stable processes (e.g., explorer.exe) reduces session loss risk and evades detection.
Common Mistake

Migrating to a process that is unstable or about to terminate, leading to immediate session loss.

Key Takeaway

Process management, especially migration, is vital for stabilizing your Meterpreter session and enhancing stealth.
