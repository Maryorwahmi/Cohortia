Chapter Summary

Mastering Cleanup and Anti-Forensics

Critical terms for maintaining stealth and professionalism post-exploitation.

Cleanup

The process of removing all traces of an attacker's presence from a compromised system, including backdoors, tools, and logs.

Anti-Forensics

Techniques used to hinder or prevent forensic analysis of a compromised system, such as modifying timestamps (MAC Times) or data wiping.

Ethical Considerations

Adhering to legal and professional standards, scope boundaries, and client agreements during a penetration test, especially during sensitive phases.

Key Takeaway

Effective cleanup and anti-forensics are crucial for stealth, but must always be balanced with strict ethical adherence to scope and non-destructive practices.
