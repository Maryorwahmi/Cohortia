Interpreting Results and Manual Verification

Understanding automated tool output and prioritizing exploitable vulnerabilities.

Common Mistakes

  • Not understanding the tool's output thoroughly.
  • Assuming every 'finding' is immediately exploitable.
  • Failing to verify findings manually.
  • Not prioritizing vulnerabilities based on impact and reliability.

Why Manual Verification Matters

  • Confirm Exploitability: Ensure conditions for exploitation are met.
  • Reduce False Positives: Distinguish true vulnerabilities from noise.
  • Understand Context: Gain deeper insight into the system's security posture.

Key Takeaway

Automated tools streamline enumeration, but human interpretation, manual verification, and prioritization are essential for successful and safe exploitation.
