Pillar 1

Credential Reuse

Leveraging extracted credentials (hashes or plaintext passwords) from a compromised host to authenticate to other systems within the network.

  • Source: Mimikatz (Windows), hash cracking (Linux).
  • Techniques: Pass-the-Hash, Pass-the-Ticket.
Pillar 2

Network Service Enumeration

Mapping the local network from a compromised host to identify connected hosts, open ports, and running services. This reveals potential new targets.

  • Tools: nmap, netstat, ipconfig (Windows), ip a (Linux).
  • Targets: SMB, WinRM, RDP, SSH.
Why it Matters

Unlocking Deeper Access

These two techniques are foundational because they provide both the 'keys' (credentials) and the 'map' (network enumeration) needed to navigate and compromise additional systems. Without them, you're essentially blind and locked out of further progress.

Key Takeaway

Effective lateral movement relies on first obtaining credentials and then thoroughly mapping the network from the compromised host's perspective.
