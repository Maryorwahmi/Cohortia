Common Mistakes

Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Incomplete Enumeration: Not fully mapping the network from the compromised host.
  • Credential Misapplication: Attempting to reuse credentials on systems where they don't apply.
  • Tunneling Misconfiguration: Failing to properly set up proxychains or tunneling tools like chisel.
Safety Note

Authorization is Key

Lateral movement significantly increases the scope of compromise. Always ensure you have explicit authorization for every system you interact with, even indirectly. Operating outside scope can have severe consequences.

  • Scope Adherence: Strictly follow the defined scope of your penetration test engagement.
  • Ethical Conduct: Always prioritize ethical hacking principles and legal boundaries.
Pro-Tip

Document Everything

Keep meticulous records of every lateral move, credential used, and pivot established. This aids in reporting, backtracking, and ensuring you stay within scope.

Key Takeaway

Successful lateral movement requires not only technical proficiency but also careful planning, thorough execution, and strict adherence to ethical guidelines and authorization scope.
