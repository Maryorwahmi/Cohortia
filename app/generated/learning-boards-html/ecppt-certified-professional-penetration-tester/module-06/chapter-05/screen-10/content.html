Understanding Risk Rating

Risk rating is an objective assessment of a vulnerability's severity, crucial for prioritizing remediation efforts.

Key Factors
  • Likelihood: Probability of exploitation.
  • Impact: Potential consequences if exploited.
Standard Scales
  • CVSS (Common Vulnerability Scoring System)
  • Custom: Critical, High, Medium, Low, Informational
Key Takeaway

An accurate risk rating, based on both likelihood and impact, empowers organizations to make informed decisions and prioritize security investments effectively.

Example: Critical SQL Injection

For an SQL Injection on a login page:

Likelihood: High (easily exploitable with common tools).

Impact: Critical (full authentication bypass, data theft, system compromise).

Resulting Risk Rating: Critical

This rating clearly signals the immediate and severe threat, demanding urgent attention.

Common Mistake

Assigning risk based solely on technical complexity or personal opinion, rather than a balanced assessment of both likelihood and business impact.
