Report Structure

The Executive Summary: Your Client's First Impression

Delivering high-level insights for non-technical decision-makers.

Key Elements of an Executive Summary

  • Engagement Overview: Scope and objectives at a glance.
  • Key Findings: Top vulnerabilities and their business impact.
  • Overall Risk Posture: A high-level assessment of the organization's security.
  • Strategic Recommendations: High-level advice for improvement.
Key Takeaway

The Executive Summary must be concise, jargon-free, and focused on business impact to effectively engage non-technical stakeholders.

Translating Technical to Business Impact

Technical Finding

"SQL Injection vulnerability found on the user login page."

Business Impact

"Critical data breach risk due to unvalidated input, potentially exposing customer records and leading to financial loss and reputational damage."
