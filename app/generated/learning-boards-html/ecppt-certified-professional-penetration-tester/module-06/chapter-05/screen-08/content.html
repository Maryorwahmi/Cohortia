Detailed Findings

Technical Findings: Impact, Risk, and Recommendations

Quantifying severity and guiding remediation efforts.

Remaining Components of a Finding

  • 4. Impact: Clear explanation of potential consequences if exploited (e.g., data theft, system compromise).
  • 5. Risk Rating: Objective severity assessment (e.g., CVSS, High/Medium/Low) based on likelihood and impact.
  • 6. Recommendation: Specific, actionable steps for remediation, prioritized by risk.
Key Takeaway

Impact, Risk Rating, and Recommendations transform a technical observation into an actionable security improvement plan for the client.

Example: Risk & Recommendation

Impact & Risk Rating

Impact: Unauthorized access to sensitive customer data, potential financial fraud, and severe reputational damage.
Risk Rating: Critical (High likelihood, Catastrophic impact).

Recommendation

Implement parameterized queries or prepared statements for all database interactions to prevent SQL injection. Conduct thorough input validation on all user-supplied data.
