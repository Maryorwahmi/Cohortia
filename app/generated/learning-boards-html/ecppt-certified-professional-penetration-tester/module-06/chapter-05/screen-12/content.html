The Importance of Debriefs

A debrief meeting is crucial for presenting findings, clarifying technical details, and ensuring the client fully understands the report's implications.

Key Activities
  • Presenting key findings
  • Explaining business impact
  • Answering client questions
  • Clarifying technical details
Benefits of Interaction
  • Ensures understanding
  • Fosters collaboration
  • Builds trust and partnership
  • Addresses misconceptions
Key Takeaway

Proactive post-engagement communication transforms a report into a shared understanding, driving effective security improvements and a stronger client relationship.

Scenario: Clarifying a Critical Finding

During a debrief, a client's developer questions a 'Critical' SQL Injection finding, arguing it's 'Medium' due to required user interaction.

Your Response: Explain that while user interaction affects likelihood, the *impact* (e.g., full data breach) remains critical. Emphasize that risk is a function of both, and even low likelihood with high impact warrants a critical rating, especially as attack methods evolve.

This direct explanation helps the client understand the holistic risk assessment.

Common Mistake

Failing to schedule a debrief or not adequately preparing to answer client questions, leading to misunderstandings and delayed remediation.
