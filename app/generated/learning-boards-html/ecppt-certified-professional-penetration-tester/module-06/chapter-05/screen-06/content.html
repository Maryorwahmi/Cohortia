Report Structure

Defining the Engagement: Scope and Methodology

Providing transparency and context for your penetration test.

Key Information in This Section

  • What was tested: Specific systems, applications, or network segments.
  • How it was tested: Methodologies (e.g., black-box, white-box), tools used.
  • Limitations/Exclusions: Any areas explicitly not covered or restricted.
Key Takeaway

A clear Scope and Methodology section builds trust and ensures all stakeholders understand the boundaries and approach of the penetration test.

Why This Section Matters

Manages Expectations

Prevents misunderstandings about what was and wasn't covered in the assessment.

Provides Audit Trail

Offers a verifiable record of the testing process for compliance and internal audits.

Builds Client Trust

Demonstrates professionalism and a structured approach to security assessment.
