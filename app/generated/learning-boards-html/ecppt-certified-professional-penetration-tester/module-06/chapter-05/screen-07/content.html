Detailed Findings

Structuring Technical Findings: Consistency is Key

Presenting each vulnerability with clarity and precision.

Core Components of a Finding

  • 1. Finding Title: A clear, concise name for the vulnerability (e.g., "SQL Injection on User Login").
  • 2. Description: Detailed explanation of the vulnerability's technical nature and discovery.
  • 3. Proof of Concept (PoC): Concrete steps to reproduce the vulnerability, including evidence.
Key Takeaway

A consistent structure for technical findings, especially a clear PoC, is vital for client understanding and efficient remediation.

Example: Proof of Concept (PoC)

1. Navigate to login page: http://example.com/login.php
2. Username field: admin' OR '1'='1
3. Password field: anypassword
4. Click 'Login'.
Expected Result: Application logs in as administrator, bypassing authentication.

Why PoC is Critical

The Proof of Concept is the 'how-to' guide for the client's technical team. It eliminates ambiguity, confirms the vulnerability's existence, and directly aids developers in replicating and understanding the issue, which is essential for effective remediation. Without it, developers might struggle to verify the finding, leading to delays or unpatched vulnerabilities.
