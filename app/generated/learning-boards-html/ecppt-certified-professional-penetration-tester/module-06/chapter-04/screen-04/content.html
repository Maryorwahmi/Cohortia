Core Concept

Active Directory: The Enterprise Backbone

Why AD is a Prime Target for Penetration Testers

What is Active Directory?

Microsoft's directory service that stores information about network objects (users, computers, services) and provides centralized management.

  • •Centralized authentication and authorization.
  • •Manages user accounts, groups, computers, and policies.
  • •Critical for enterprise network operations.
Key Takeaway

AD's pervasive role and inherent complexity make it a high-value target, where misconfigurations can lead to full network compromise.

Why Attackers Target AD
Common Misconfigurations

Complex setups often lead to security oversights, creating exploitable weaknesses.

Path to Domain Admin

Exploiting AD is often the quickest route to gaining the highest level of network control.

Pervasive Impact

Compromising AD affects all connected systems and users, leading to widespread control.
