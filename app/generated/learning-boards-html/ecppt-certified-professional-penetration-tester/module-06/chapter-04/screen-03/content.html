Execute Credential Attacks

Perform Kerberoasting and AS-REP Roasting to obtain and crack user credentials. This is a key step in gaining initial access or escalating privileges.

Implement Lateral Movement

Apply techniques like Pass-the-Hash and Pass-the-Ticket to move stealthily between compromised systems. This is crucial for expanding your reach within a network.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Utilize Impacket & Mimikatz

Leverage powerful tools like Impacket and mimikatz for various AD exploitation and privilege escalation tasks. Proficiency with these tools is essential for real-world engagements.
