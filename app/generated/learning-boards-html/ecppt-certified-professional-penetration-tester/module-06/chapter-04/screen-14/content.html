Key Concepts

Understanding Advanced Attack Techniques

Kerberoasting

An attack exploiting SPNs to obtain NTLM password hashes from service accounts for offline cracking.

AS-REP Roasting

Targets accounts with disabled Kerberos preauthentication to obtain encrypted TGTs for offline password cracking.

Pass-the-Hash (PtH) & Pass-the-Ticket (PtT)

Lateral movement techniques using NTLM hashes (PtH) or Kerberos tickets (PtT) for authentication without plaintext passwords.

Summary Takeaway

These advanced credential theft and lateral movement techniques are crucial for gaining and expanding control within Active Directory environments.
