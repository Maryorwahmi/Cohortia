Common Mistake

Proxychains Misconfiguration or Inactive Proxy

If proxychains is misconfigured or the SOCKS proxy on the pivot host isn't active, your tools will fail to connect or bypass the pivot, leading to connection failures.

Troubleshooting Step 1

Verify SOCKS Proxy Status

netstat -tulnp | grep <port>

Confirm your SOCKS proxy (e.g., Chisel server) is running and listening on the expected port.

Troubleshooting Step 2

Check Proxychains Configuration

socks5 127.0.0.1 1080

Ensure proxychains.conf correctly points to the local SOCKS proxy IP and port.

Key Takeaway

Systematic verification of proxy status and configuration, followed by connectivity tests, is crucial for troubleshooting SOCKS proxy pivoting issues.
