Key Concepts

Here are the essential terms and techniques from this chapter.

Local Port Forwarding (SSH -L)

Forwards a local port on the attacking machine to a port on a target machine through an SSH pivot.

Remote Port Forwarding (SSH -R)

Forwards a port on the pivot host to a port on the attacking machine, often used to bypass egress filtering.

Chisel

A fast TCP/UDP tunnel over HTTP, often used for robust SOCKS proxy pivoting due to its speed and ability to bypass firewalls.

What to Remember

Mastering SSH forwarding, Chisel, and Proxychains provides powerful capabilities for advanced network pivoting and bypassing security controls.
