Definition

What is Pivoting?

Pivoting is the technique of using a compromised host as an intermediary to access other hosts on an otherwise unreachable network segment. It's like creating a bridge.

Why it's Needed

Segmented Networks

Modern networks are often segmented with firewalls and VLANs, preventing direct access from external attack machines to internal systems. Pivoting overcomes these barriers.

Key Takeaway

Pivoting is a fundamental skill for lateral movement, extending your reach into internal networks from an initial compromise point.
