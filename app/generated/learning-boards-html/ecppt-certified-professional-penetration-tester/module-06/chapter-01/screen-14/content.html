Key Concepts

Here are the essential terms and techniques from this chapter.

Pivoting

Using a compromised host as an intermediary to access other hosts on an otherwise unreachable network segment.

Tunneling

Encapsulating network traffic within another protocol to bypass firewalls or secure communications.

SOCKS Proxy

A network protocol that routes packets between a client and server through a proxy, enabling general-purpose pivoting.

What to Remember

Pivoting and tunneling are indispensable for navigating segmented networks, allowing you to extend your reach and access high-value targets.
