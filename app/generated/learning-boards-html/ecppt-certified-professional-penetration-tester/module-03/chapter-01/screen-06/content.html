Core Concept

Social Engineering

Social engineering is the psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information. It exploits human trust and vulnerabilities rather than technical flaws.

Human Element Focus

Targets human decision-making, trust, and emotional responses.

Bypasses Technical Controls

Often circumvents firewalls and intrusion detection systems by tricking users directly.

Analogy: The Con Artist

Cybersecurity's Master of Deception

Think of a social engineer as a modern-day con artist. They don't break into a vault; they convince the vault manager to open it for them. They build trust, create urgency, or exploit curiosity to get what they want, making the victim an unwitting accomplice in their own compromise.

Exploiting Trust
Key Takeaway

Social engineering is a psychological attack, leveraging human nature to bypass technical defenses, making human awareness paramount for security.
