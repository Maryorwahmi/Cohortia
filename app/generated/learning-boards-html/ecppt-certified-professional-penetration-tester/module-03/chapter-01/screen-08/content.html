Social Engineering Tactics

Pretexting, Baiting, & Quid Pro Quo

These tactics involve creating believable scenarios, offering enticing lures, or proposing exchanges to manipulate individuals into compromising actions.

Pretexting

Fabricated Scenarios

Creating a false story or identity to gain trust and obtain information. E.g., attacker poses as IT support to get login details.

Fake Identity/Story
Baiting

Enticing Lures

Offering something desirable (e.g., free software, 'found' USB drive) to trick victims into installing malware or revealing info.

Irresistible Offer
Quid Pro Quo

Service for Information

Offering a service or benefit in exchange for sensitive information or access. E.g., 'tech support' offering help for login details.

Exchange of Value
Key Takeaway

Recognizing these varied social engineering tactics is crucial for developing a robust defense against human manipulation.
