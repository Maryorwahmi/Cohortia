Social Engineering Tactic

Phishing

Phishing involves sending deceptive emails or messages that impersonate trusted entities to trick recipients into revealing confidential information or installing malware.

Key Element: Impersonation

Attackers mimic banks, IT departments, or popular services to gain trust.

Common Goals

Credential theft, malware installation, financial fraud.

Example: Fake Bank Alert

'Urgent Account Verification'

You receive an email from 'Your Bank' with the subject 'Urgent: Account Verification Required'. It states unusual activity and asks you to click a link to verify your account. The link, however, leads to a fake login page designed to steal your credentials.

Deceptive Email
Key Takeaway

Always verify the sender and scrutinize links in suspicious emails, especially those demanding urgent action or personal information.
