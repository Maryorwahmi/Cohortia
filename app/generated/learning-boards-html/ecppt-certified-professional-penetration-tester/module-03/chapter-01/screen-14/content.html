Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways from Client-Side Exploitation

Client-Side Exploitation

Attacks targeting vulnerabilities in client software, often requiring user interaction to execute malicious code.

Social Engineering

The psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information.

Phishing

A social engineering technique using deceptive emails to trick recipients into revealing credentials or installing malware.

Chapter Summary

Client-side attacks leverage both technical vulnerabilities and human psychology, with social engineering tactics like phishing being key enablers.
