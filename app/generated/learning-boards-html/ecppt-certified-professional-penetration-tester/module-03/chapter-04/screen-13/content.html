Best Practices & Warnings

Common Mistakes & Safety Notes

Avoiding pitfalls and ensuring responsible penetration testing.

Ignoring Lockout Policies

Aggressive brute-forcing can lock out legitimate accounts or trigger IDS alerts.

Generic Password Lists

Using untargeted lists reduces success; tailor dictionaries to the target for better results.

Key Takeaway

Always obtain explicit permission, start with passive enumeration, and be mindful of target security measures to ensure responsible testing.

Ethical Hacking Principles

Key considerations for responsible penetration testing.

Obtain Authorization

Never test systems without explicit, written permission from the owner.

Minimize Impact

Avoid causing disruption, account lockouts, or denial-of-service conditions.

Report Findings

Document all vulnerabilities and findings clearly and concisely.
