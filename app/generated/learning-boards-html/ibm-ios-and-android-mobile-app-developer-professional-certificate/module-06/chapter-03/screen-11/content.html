Upload and Release

Create a release, upload the signed AAB, add release notes, and choose the testing track or production rollout.

Step-by-step
  1. Create app record and complete store listing
  2. Go to Release section, select track
  3. Upload .aab, add release notes, and publish to testers or staged production
Concrete Example

Upload to internal testing with notes: "Verify login, onboarding, and crash-free startup" and invite QA testers via email.

Review Checklist
  • Store assets complete and accurate
  • Content rating questionnaire filled
  • Privacy policy URL and permissions documented if required
Key Takeaway

Provide clear release notes and follow the Play checklist to speed review and avoid rejections.

Common Mistake

Uploading a build without completing store metadata or rating questionnaire, which can delay or block release.
