Store Listing Essentials

Accurate metadata and polished assets reduce rejection risk and increase installs.

Required Items
  • App name and default language
  • App icon and feature graphic
  • Screenshots for phone, 7" and 10" tablets
  • Short and full descriptions
  • Content rating questionnaire
Practical Tip

Use real app flows for screenshots and keep promotional text truthful to avoid policy violations.

Asset Examples
Phone screenshots (3) — highlight sign-in, home, and a key feature.
Tablet screenshot (7") — show layout adapted for larger screen.
Feature graphic — used in Play Store promotion.
App icon — high resolution and centered composition.
Key Takeaway

Prepare accurate and high-quality assets before upload to increase approval chances and user trust.
