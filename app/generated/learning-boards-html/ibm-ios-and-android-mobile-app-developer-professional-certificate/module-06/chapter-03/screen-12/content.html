Common Rejection Reasons

Review checks focus on privacy, requested permissions, functionality and accuracy of the store listing.

Privacy & Permissions

Provide a privacy policy when accessing personal data and request only necessary permissions.

Functionality

Fix crashes, broken features, or behavior that fails during review testing before resubmitting.

Misleading Content

Ensure descriptions and screenshots truthfully represent the app’s experience.

Concrete Fix Example

If the review flags an unnecessary contact permission, remove the permission or explain why it’s required and show how user consent is obtained.

Key Takeaway

Treat rejection feedback as a checklist: fix, rebuild a signed AAB, and resubmit with notes explaining the changes.
