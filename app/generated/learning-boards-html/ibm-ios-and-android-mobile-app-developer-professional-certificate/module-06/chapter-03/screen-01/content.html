Deploying Android Apps to the Google Play Store

Learn signing, build configuration, App Bundles, Play Console listings, testing tracks, and staged rollouts to publish safely.

In this lesson: signing → build → release
Signing
Create and protect a keystore that identifies your app author and enables updates.
Build
Generate a signed Android App Bundle (AAB) for Play to optimize APKs per device.
Release
Use internal, closed, open testing and staged rollouts to manage risk during distribution.
Explore Key Topics
Keystore Safety

Back up your keystore and store passwords securely; losing them blocks updates.

App Bundle (AAB)

AAB lets Play produce optimized APKs, reducing download size for users.

Release Tracks

Use internal, closed, open testing to validate quality before production rollout.

Visual Overview
Keystore → Signed AAB → Play Console

This flow ensures your app is identifiable, securely signed, and distributed via Play's optimized channels.

Testing Tracks

Internal for fast checks, closed for targeted beta groups, open for broad beta, and staged rollout for controlled production release.

Key Takeaway

Protect your keystore, prefer AAB for Play, and use testing tracks to catch issues before exposing real users to a change.
