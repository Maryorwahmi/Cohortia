Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts — Part 1
App Signing
Digital signature proving app authorship and update integrity.
Keystore
File storing private keys; back it up securely to keep update ability.
Keytool
Utility to generate and manage keystores and keys for signing.
Summary

These three concepts form the minimal mental model for secure, repeatable Android app publishing: generate keys, secure them, and sign releases before uploading to Play.
