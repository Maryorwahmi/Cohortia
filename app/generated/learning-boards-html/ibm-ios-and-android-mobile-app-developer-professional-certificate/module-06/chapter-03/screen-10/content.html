Core Concept

Release Tracks and Staged Rollout

Manage distribution with internal, closed, open testing and staged production rollouts to limit exposure while testing.

Internal testing

Fast distribution to a small, trusted set of testers (up to 100) for quick smoke tests.

Closed testing

Invite groups such as QA or beta users for broader coverage and targeted feedback.

Open testing

Publish a public beta on Play to gather large-scale feedback before production.

Staged Rollout

Deploy to a small percentage of your production users, monitor crashes and feedback, then increase the percentage gradually.

Concrete Example

Start with a 5% rollout, check stability metrics and reviews, then move to 25% and finally 100% when confident.

Common Mistake

Releasing directly to 100% production without staged rollouts increases the blast radius if something goes wrong.
