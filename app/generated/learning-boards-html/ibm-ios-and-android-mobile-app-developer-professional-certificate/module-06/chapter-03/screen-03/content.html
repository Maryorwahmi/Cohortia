Practical Skills & Testing Flow

A short checklist you will apply while creating and verifying a signed AAB and the Play Console workflow.

Checklist
  • Generate keystore and record path/passwords securely.
  • Configure Gradle signingConfigs for release automation.
  • Build a signed AAB and locate the .aab file for upload.
Testing Flow
  • Internal testing for up to 100 trusted testers.
  • Closed testing for invited testers with broader coverage.
  • Open testing for large beta feedback before production.
Safeguards
  • Back up keystore outside the project repository.
  • Choose long validity and strong passwords for keys.
  • Use staged rollouts to monitor real users gradually.
Activity Ready

You will generate an AAB, capture the build notification, and upload it to an internal test track as proof of competency.

Key Takeaway

Treat your keystore like the app’s identity: losing it prevents future updates.
