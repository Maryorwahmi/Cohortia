Hands-on Activity
Prepare assets & metadata for a Task Manager app
Step-by-step
  1. Create a vector icon and export a 1024x1024 PNG for iOS store listing.
  2. Export @1x/@2x/@3x device icons and add to .xcassets for Xcode.
  3. Create Android adaptive foreground/background layers and export mipmap assets.
  4. Capture 3–5 screenshots per platform and consider localized variants.
  5. Draft metadata: name, subtitle/short description, full description, keywords, and privacy URL.
Deliverables
  • Folders with icon exports for iOS and Android
  • Screenshot sets for phone and tablet
  • Full description (200+ words) and keywords file
  • Privacy policy placeholder and age rating justification
Check your understanding
Can you open .xcassets and confirm the Icon-1024.png is present? Can you place adaptive layers into Android drawable folders and preview the launcher icon?
Key Takeaway

Completing this activity produces concrete assets and a metadata draft you can reuse when you register the app in the store consoles.
