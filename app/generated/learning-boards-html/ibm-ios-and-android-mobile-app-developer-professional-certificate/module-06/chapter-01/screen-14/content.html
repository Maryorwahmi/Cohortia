Final Steps
Upload and submit your build
Validate signing, attach assets, complete listing fields, and submit for review on the appropriate console.
Xcode → Archive → Upload to App Store Connect; complete listing fields and attach screenshots.
Android Studio → Build signed bundle → Upload to Play Console; fill store listing and rating questionnaire.
What reviewers look for
  • Does the listing match the app’s functionality?
  • Are assets correctly sized and non-misleading?
  • Is the privacy policy accessible and accurate?
Pro tip
Address any upload warnings and keep a log of versions and asset changes so reviewer questions can be answered quickly.
Key Takeaway

A correct submission is signed properly, includes verified assets and metadata, and has a reachable privacy policy; be ready to respond to review feedback quickly.
