Core Concept
Why guidelines exist
Stores review assets and metadata to ensure apps are honest, safe, and usable. Review includes visuals, legal links, and content ratings.
  • Guidelines protect users and platform consistency.
  • Reviewers check visuals, descriptions, and compliance links.
  • Incorrect assets often cause rejections even when the app works technically.
Key Takeaway

Treat the submission as product readiness: honest screenshots, correct sizes, and clear metadata reduce rejections.

Definition & Analogy
App stores are curators
Example: Screenshot mismatch
If the listing shows a feature not present in the binary, reviewers will flag the inconsistency and may reject the app until corrected.
Common mistake
Assuming code changes automatically update listings. Metadata and assets must be manually reviewed and uploaded.
Check your understanding
When packaging, ask: do screenshots accurately represent the current UI? Is the privacy policy publicly accessible? Are icons correctly sized?
