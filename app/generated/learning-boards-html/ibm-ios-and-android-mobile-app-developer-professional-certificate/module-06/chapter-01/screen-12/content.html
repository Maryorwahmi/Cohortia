Process
Common rejection reasons
  • Screenshot shows features not present in the submitted build.
  • Privacy policy URL is missing or inaccessible.
  • Icon sizes are incorrect or missing adaptive layers on Android.
  • Incorrect content rating or questionnaire answers.
Pre-submission checklist
Confirm screenshots match the current build and device frames.
Verify privacy policy URL is reachable and accurate.
Ensure icons and adaptive assets are present and properly sized.
Answer store questionnaires honestly and set appropriate age ratings.
Key Takeaway

Run a final pretend-review to catch mismatches and missing legal links; this prevents most common rejections.
