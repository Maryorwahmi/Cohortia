Definition
Launch / Splash screens
They provide a short branded experience during startup but should not be used for advertising or promotional content.
iOS method
Use LaunchScreen.storyboard or XIB for a static launch layout that the OS loads quickly.
Android method
Use a drawable or a simple layout that shows briefly before the main activity initializes.
Example
A subtle brand gradient and centered logo
Use colors and a centered mark that match your app’s first screen. Avoid text-heavy calls to action. Test for layout consistency on phones and tablets.
Common mistake
Treating launch screens as a marketing splash—this can confuse users and may be flagged by reviewers.
Key Takeaway

Provide a lightweight, branded launch screen that gives a smooth visual transition into your app and does not contain promotional content.
