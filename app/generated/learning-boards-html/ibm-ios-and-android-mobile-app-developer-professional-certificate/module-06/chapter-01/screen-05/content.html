Process Flow
Developer account setup
Follow these steps to prepare for publishing and signing your builds.
  1. 1
    Enroll in Apple Developer Program and complete payment.
  2. 2
    Register a Google Play Developer account and finalize registration fee.
  3. 3
    Create app records in App Store Connect and Play Console.
  4. 4
    Assign roles, prepare signing certificates, and confirm billing settings.
Scenario
Imagine a solo developer enrolling: once the Apple Developer subscription is active, they can create the app record, upload a signed build, and invite testers via TestFlight.
Common-mistake
Delaying enrollment until the last minute
Start enrollment early because account verification and payment processing can take time. Also plan who will manage certificates and roles to avoid hold-ups when a build is ready.
Pro tip
Record the account and team roles securely and note the steps to create signing certificates and provisioning profiles or upload keystore files for Android.
