Definition
Privacy Policy essentials
Explain what you collect, how it’s used, and how users can manage their data. Host a publicly accessible URL and include it in your store listing.
Checklist
  • Public privacy policy URL
  • Clear description of data collection/use
  • Answers to store questionnaires matching your app’s behavior
Scenario
Analytics count as data collection
If your app sends usage metrics or device identifiers to analytics services, disclose that in your privacy policy and store questionnaire to avoid review problems.
Common pitfall
Submitting a placeholder privacy link or omitting data usage in questionnaires leads to reviewer requests or rejections.
Key Takeaway

Document and publish a real privacy policy and ensure your store questionnaire answers match actual app behavior to avoid rejections.
