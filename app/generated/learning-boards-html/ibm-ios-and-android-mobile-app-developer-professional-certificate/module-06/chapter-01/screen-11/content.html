Practical steps
  • Create a vector master and export required raster sizes.
  • Import iOS images into .xcassets and verify in Xcode’s asset inspector.
  • Place Android images into res/mipmap-* and drawables for adaptive layers.
Common mistake
Relying on a single low-resolution image and letting the OS scale it up at runtime, which leads to poor quality icons.
Key Takeaway

Generate platform-specific exports and store them in asset catalogs or res folders so builds include properly sized images.
