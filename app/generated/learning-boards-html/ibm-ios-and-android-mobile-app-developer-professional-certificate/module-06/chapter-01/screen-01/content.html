Preparing for Deployment: App Store Guidelines & Asset Creation
Package your app for App Store Connect and Google Play Console. Prepare icons, launch screens, screenshots, metadata, and privacy policy for a smooth release.

Why this matters: stores enforce guidelines to ensure security and clarity. Correct assets and metadata reduce rejections and improve discoverability.

Example outcome: a “Task Manager” app prepared with iOS and Android asset sets, localized screenshots, and a public privacy policy ready for submission.
