Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › TestFlight: Distributing Beta Builds
Usage
Internal testers: Team members, immediate access.
External testers: Email invites; may require Beta App Review.
Release notes: Add instructions and focus areas for testers.
Concrete scenario
Upload a build, wait for processing, then add internal testers to quickly validate critical user flows before inviting a larger external group.
Monitor
  • Track tester feedback and crashes in App Store Connect.
  • Rotate builds by uploading a new archive with a higher build number.
Misconception

TestFlight is not a replacement for proper QA; it is a distribution channel for real-device feedback and small user groups prior to public release.
