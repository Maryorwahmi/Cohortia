Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Submitting for Review and the App Store Review Guidelines
Reviewer notes
Include login credentials and exact steps to reach key features.
Explain any special hardware or setup required for testing.
Provide context for background processes or network dependencies.
Common rejection reasons
Crashes, broken links, missing privacy policy or unjustified permissions are frequent causes for rejection.
After rejection
  1. Read Apple's feedback carefully.
  2. Fix the reported issue and test thoroughly.
  3. Increment the build number, archive, and upload the new build.
  4. Resubmit with clear notes describing the fix.
