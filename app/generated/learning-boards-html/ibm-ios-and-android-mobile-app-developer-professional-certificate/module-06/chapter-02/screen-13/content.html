Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Activity Walkthrough: Archive and Prepare for TestFlight

Step-by-step timeline

1) Prerequisite: Apple Developer membership and an Xcode project (or simple Hello World app).
2) General tab: set Bundle Identifier to com.yourname.mytestapp, Version 1.0, Build 1. Take a screenshot.
3) Signing & Capabilities: enable Automatically manage signing and select your team for practice.
4) Select Generic iOS Device and run Product → Archive; wait for Organizer.
5) In Organizer, screenshot the archived build, then choose Distribute App → App Store Connect → Upload.
6) After upload, check TestFlight processing in App Store Connect and record processing status in your log.
7) Deliverables: two screenshots (General tab, Organizer) and a short log describing actions and any fixes.
Key Takeaway

Document each step and capture screenshots. Increment build numbers for subsequent uploads and note any signing issues and resolutions in your log.
