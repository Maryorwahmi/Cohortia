Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Distribution Certificate and Provisioning Profile
Roles

Certificate vs Profile

  • Distribution Certificate: proves the developer identity and signs the binary.
  • Provisioning Profile: references an App ID and ties it to the certificate and distribution channel.
  • Why together: The profile asserts which app (App ID) the certificate is permitted to sign for distribution.
Practical check
If you get a signing error, verify certificate validity and that the provisioning profile lists the exact App ID used in Xcode.
Illustration

Issued by Apple. Identifies you as the publisher.

Common mistake

Creating a profile for the wrong App ID or an expired certificate is a frequent cause of upload failures. Renew or regenerate profiles when certificates change.
