Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Creating an App Record in App Store Connect
Essential fields
App name — user-facing title.
Bundle ID — must match Xcode exactly.
Primary language — used for store listing.
SKU — internal identifier you control.
Metadata
  • Pricing & availability
  • Screenshots and app previews
  • Privacy policy and support URLs
  • Age rating and categories
Reviewer readiness

Provide clear review notes, including test accounts and any special setup steps. If reviewers cannot reach a key feature, the app can be rejected for insufficient information.

Common rejection

Not providing reviewer access or clear testing steps results in avoidable rejections. Include credentials and step-by-step notes if login is required.
