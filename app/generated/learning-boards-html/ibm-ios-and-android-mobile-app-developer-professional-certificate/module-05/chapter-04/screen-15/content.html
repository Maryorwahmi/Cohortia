Virtualize lists
Use FlatList/SectionList with a stable keyExtractor to preserve performance.
Safe persistence
AsyncStorage is unencrypted. Use it for preferences only; encrypt secrets elsewhere.
One-line takeaway

Reliable apps validate network responses, render lists efficiently, and protect users by keeping sensitive data out of unencrypted storage.
