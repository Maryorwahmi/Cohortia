Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
State Management
Manage data that changes over time and influences UI. Choose patterns by scope.
Provider + ChangeNotifier
Provide models to descendants; models call notifyListeners to update listeners.
Consumption methods
Use watch/Consumer for UI, read for handlers, and Selector to limit rebuilds.
Summary Takeaway

Centralize shared state into models, provide them at the correct scope, and consume with the most focused listening method to produce predictable, maintainable UIs.
