Route stack explained

Think of screens as cards stacked vertically. The top card is visible. Push adds a new card; pop removes it. This preserves the previous screen's state automatically.

Common misconception
Navigation is not a tree that resets siblings automatically. Unless you replace or remove routes, the previous route and its state remain in memory.
Key Takeaway

Use push for forward moves, pop to go back, and replace/remove to alter history when needed.

Analogy
Top: Settings (visible)
Middle: Home
Bottom: Onboarding
Pop removes Settings and reveals Home without rebuilding Home from scratch.
