Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › iOS Simulator vs Device
Comparison
Simulator: Fast iterations, multiple screen sizes, bundled with Xcode.
Physical device: Real hardware, sensors, cellular and battery behavior.
Best practice: Develop on simulator, validate on real devices before release.
Concrete scenario
If your app uses a device camera and AR features, the simulator will show layout and UI but not real camera behavior—test on a physical device to validate camera latency and sensor fusion.
Key takeaway
Simulators speed development; real devices reveal hardware and performance issues that simulators cannot emulate.
