Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Learning Objectives
Assessment Tasks
  • Explore the same popular app on iOS and Android and note navigation differences.
  • Run a short checklist: launch time, responsiveness, permission dialogs, and a screen reader pass.
  • Write a 100–150 word reflection comparing UX, performance, and accessibility findings.
Success Criteria
Explain why you would prioritize Android for broad reach or iOS for consistent hardware and faster testing, and list three accessibility checks performed.
Mnemonic
RAP
Reach, Accessibility, Performance — use this to compare platform trade-offs quickly.
