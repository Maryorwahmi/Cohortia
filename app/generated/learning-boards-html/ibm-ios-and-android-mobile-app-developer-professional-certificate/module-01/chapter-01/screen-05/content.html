Hierarchy by Device
Phone
Short sessions, touch gestures, single-column flows, rely on GPS, camera, and push notifications.
Tablet
Longer sessions, multi-pane UI, richer controls and content consumption.
Wearable
Glanceable info, minimal input, voice and quick gestures prioritized.
TV / Large Screen
Remote input, large targets, simple navigation and visual clarity from distance.
Concrete Example
A music streaming app should offer a compact player and quick search on phones, a richer browse and multi-column library on tablets, simplified now-playing glance on a watch, and big, easily navigable artwork and controls on a TV. Prioritize core user tasks per device: search and playback on every device, but browsing depth only where the screen and input allow it.
Common Mistake

Treating responsiveness as only fluid scaling. You must also change navigation and content prioritization for different inputs and session lengths.
