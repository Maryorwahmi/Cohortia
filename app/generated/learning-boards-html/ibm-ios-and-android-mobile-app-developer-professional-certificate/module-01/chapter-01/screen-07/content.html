Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Designing for Performance
Steps
1
Measure: capture cold start, CPU, memory, and frame rates on devices you plan to support.
2
Prioritize: make the critical path fast so users can complete core tasks immediately.
3
Optimize: lazy-load content, compress resources, and avoid main-thread blockers.
4
Validate: test on representative devices, including lower-end Android hardware if necessary.
Concrete Example
A news app defers image decoding and loads headlines first. Users see content quickly, and the app loads images progressively. On low-memory devices, the app additionally switches to lower-resolution thumbnails to avoid frame drops.
Key Takeaway

Performance strategies should be measured, prioritized, and validated on the devices your users actually use.
