Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › User Expectations
What Users Expect
  • Speed: immediate feedback and visible progress indicators.
  • Reliability: minimal crashes and preserved state across interruptions.
  • Intuitive UI: clear navigation and familiar controls for the platform.
  • Privacy and accessibility: respectful permissions and inclusive design.
Concrete Example
A shopping app shows a skeleton product grid immediately and streams images later. It saves the shopping cart state when the app is backgrounded so users return to the same place. Clear permission prompts explain why camera access is needed for barcode scanning.
Key Takeaway

Delivering polished UX and respectful privacy increases trust, retention, and the likelihood of positive reviews.
