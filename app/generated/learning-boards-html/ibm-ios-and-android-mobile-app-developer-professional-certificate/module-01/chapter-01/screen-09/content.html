Store Differences
App Store: stricter review, privacy controls, consistent device expectations.
Google Play: staged rollouts, broader distribution, varied device submissions.
Practical Tip
Use staged rollouts to monitor crashes and performance across diverse Android devices before wide release.
Concrete Example
A feature that accesses location must include clear permission rationale and privacy disclosure. On iOS, Apple’s privacy guidelines can affect approval speed, so prepare documentation and test cases ahead of submission.
Key Takeaway

Align your release and compliance plans with store policies to avoid surprises and delays in approval.
