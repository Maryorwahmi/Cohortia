Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › iOS and Android
Definition
Ecosystem: hardware, OS, stores, tools, and community that influence how apps are built and used.
iOS: Controlled and consistent
Tighter hardware-software integration leads to predictable performance and fewer device variations to test.
Android: Open and varied
Wide device variety and customization require broader compatibility testing and flexible UI design.
Common misconception
Some developers assume Android always means lower quality. In reality, both ecosystems support high-quality apps; the primary difference is the breadth of devices and the resulting testing strategy.
Concrete Example
Imagine a photo editor. On iOS you can plan for consistent memory and GPU capabilities across a few recent devices. On Android you must design graceful degradation for low-memory devices and offer settings to reduce image resolution for performance.
Key Takeaway

Match your design and QA plan to the ecosystem: narrower test matrix for iOS, broader tuning and feature toggles for Android.
