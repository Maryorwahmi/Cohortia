Validate critical flows early
Process example: a startup building a content-driven app with a web team would score high on speed-to-market and team skills, so hybrid or cross-platform prototypes make sense. For a sensor-driven AR feature, the requirements and prototype steps will surface the need for native work. Misconception: do not assume libraries will cover every device feature — always prototype the critical feature first.
Key Takeaway

Apply this checklist early to avoid costly rework and to make a defensible architecture recommendation.
