item_user.xml
<LinearLayout
    xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android"
    android:layout_width="match_parent"
    android:layout_height="wrap_content"
    android:orientation="vertical"
    android:padding="16dp"
    android:clickable="true"
    android:focusable="true"
    android:background=?android:attr/selectableItemBackground>

    <TextView
        android:id="@+id/userNameTextView"
        android:layout_width="wrap_content"
        android:layout_height="wrap_content"
        android:textSize="20sp"
        android:textStyle="bold"/>

    <TextView
        android:id="@+id/userStatusTextView"
        android:layout_width="wrap_content"
        android:layout_height="wrap_content"
        android:textSize="14sp"
        android:text="Online"/>

</LinearLayout>
Design notes
Use selectableItemBackground for ripple feedback; keep children non-clickable to allow the root to receive taps. Provide adequate padding for accessible tap targets.
Example interaction
Root item click constructs an explicit Intent and passes userName as an extra before starting UserDetailActivity.
Common-mistake
Making child views clickable can swallow clicks and prevent the item root from firing. Keep clicks at the root unless you need per-child actions.
