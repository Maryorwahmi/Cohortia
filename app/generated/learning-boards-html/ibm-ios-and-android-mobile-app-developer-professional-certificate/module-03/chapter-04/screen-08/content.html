Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › RecyclerView components
LayoutManager
Positions items and handles scroll. Example: LinearLayoutManager for vertical lists.
Adapter
Creates ViewHolders and binds your data to them. You must notify it of dataset changes.
ViewHolder
Caches item subviews to avoid repeated lookups during binding.
Concrete flow
LayoutManager determines visible positions, Adapter provides ViewHolders, and ViewHolder.bind populates text and images for display.
Key Takeaway
Keep binding operations light; inflate item layouts once in onCreateViewHolder and rebind quickly in onBindViewHolder.
