Practical checks
  • Disk space: confirm enough room before downloading SDKs and system images.
  • Virtualization: ensure BIOS/UEFI has VT-x or AMD-V enabled for hardware acceleration.
  • SDK Tools: verify Android SDK Build-Tools, Emulator, and Command-line Tools are installed.
  • System images: choose images with Google Play Services when you need Google APIs.
Quick mnemonic
Check: Disk • Virtualization • SDK • Image
Tip: Use recommended system images for easier compatibility.
Warning: Missing HAXM or Hyper-V often causes emulator errors.
