Sizing attributes
match_parent expands to fill the parent; wrap_content sizes to the content. Use match_parent on inputs to give consistent alignment and wrap_content for labels and icon buttons.
android:id
Assign a unique id so your Activity code can find the view for reading input or wiring click handlers.
Concrete example
<EditText
    android:id="@+id/editTextUsername"
    android:layout_width="match_parent"
    android:layout_height="wrap_content"
    android:hint="Username" />

<Button
    android:id="@+id/buttonLogin"
    android:layout_width="wrap_content"
    android:layout_height="wrap_content"
    android:text="Login" />
Misconception check
Do not assume ids are optional — without ids you cannot reference views from code. And don’t overuse match_parent where wrap_content is appropriate, as it may break your intended layout.
