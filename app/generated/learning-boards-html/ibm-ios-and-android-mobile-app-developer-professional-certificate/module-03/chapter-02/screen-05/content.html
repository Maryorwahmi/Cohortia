What the manifest describes
  • App components: activities, services, broadcast receivers, and providers.
  • Permissions required by the app, declared with <uses-permission> tags.
  • Hardware and software features your app expects, helping the Play Store filter installs.
  • Entry point: the Activity with the MAIN action and LAUNCHER category.
Common mistake

Forgetting to declare a required permission in the manifest causes security exceptions or silent feature failures at runtime.

Example
To request internet access you must add this line inside the manifest root:
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET"/>
Why it matters
The manifest is checked before your app runs and is part of what the platform uses to grant access and list app capabilities to the system.
