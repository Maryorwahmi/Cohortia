Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › View & ViewGroup
Core distinction
Views are individual UI controls. ViewGroups are containers that arrange those controls. Use ViewGroups to control layout and Views for content and interaction.
Views
TextView, Button, EditText — display or receive input.
ViewGroups
LinearLayout, RelativeLayout — arrange children with rules and attributes.
Performance note
Minimize unnecessary nesting of ViewGroups to keep layouts performant and easier to maintain.
Concrete example
A login form: two EditText views to capture username and password and a Button to submit, arranged by a LinearLayout that stacks them vertically.
Key Takeaway

Separate layout concerns (ViewGroups) from content (Views) and avoid deep nesting for clarity and performance.
