Common Problems
Disk space: Xcode and builds consume large storage. Free space before installing or building.
Wrong target: Confirm the simulator or device selected in the scheme menu matches your test intent.
Compatibility: Developer Disk Image errors typically mean Xcode and device iOS versions do not match.
Quick Troubleshoot Sequence
  1. Check disk space and free up storage.
  2. Verify your run target in the scheme menu.
  3. Open Issues Navigator and read the top error message.
  4. Update Xcode or device OS if compatibility is at fault.
Key Takeaway
Resolve setup issues with a short checklist before deep-diving into code changes.
