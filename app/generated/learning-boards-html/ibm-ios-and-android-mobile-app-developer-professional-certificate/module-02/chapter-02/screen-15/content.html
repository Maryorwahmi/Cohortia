Key Concepts

let vs var
Use constants to reduce accidental mutation, variables for state that must change.
Optionals
Optionals explicitly represent missing values; unwrap safely with binding or provide defaults with ??.
Structs vs Classes
Structs copy on assignment; classes share references. Pick based on whether identity or independent copies are required.
Final takeaway
Practice these patterns in a Playground to internalize safety and clarity. Small, repeatable experiments lead to confident, correct code.
