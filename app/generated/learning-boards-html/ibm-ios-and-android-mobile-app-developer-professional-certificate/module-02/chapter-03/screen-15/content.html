Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › What to Remember
Compose small views
Smaller components are easier to preview and reuse.
Choose state ownership
@State for local, @Binding for child updates, ObservableObject for shared models.
Iterate with Canvas
Use Live Preview for quick checks but validate in simulator or device for final behavior.
Closing thought
Start small, validate often, and extract patterns as you see repetition. That workflow will keep your SwiftUI projects maintainable.
