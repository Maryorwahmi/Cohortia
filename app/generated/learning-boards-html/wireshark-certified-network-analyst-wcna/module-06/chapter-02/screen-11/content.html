Forensic Safety and Chain of Custody

Protecting Evidence and Ensuring Legal Compliance

Handle Files Securely

Always save extracted files, especially executables, to an isolated environment (e.g., VM) to prevent accidental infection of your forensic workstation.

Respect Privacy & Legalities

Be mindful of privacy and legal implications. Ensure proper authorization for capture and analysis, especially with sensitive personal information.

Maintain Chain of Custody

Work with copies of PCAP files, never originals. Document every step of your analysis to maintain a clear, defensible chain of custody for evidence.

Key Takeaway

Adhering to strict safety protocols, respecting legal boundaries, and meticulously documenting the chain of custody are non-negotiable for credible network forensic investigations.
