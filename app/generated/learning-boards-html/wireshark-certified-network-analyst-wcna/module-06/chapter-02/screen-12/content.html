Building a Timeline: Go To Packet and Time Reference

Navigating Captures and Establishing Event Chronology

Go To Packet

Quickly jump to a specific packet number or a packet identified by a filter or Expert Information entry. This saves significant time in large captures.

How to Use

Right-click on a packet, or use `Go > Go to Packet...` (Ctrl+G).

Time Reference

Adjust the time display format to establish a clear, unambiguous timeline of events. 'Seconds Since Beginning of Capture' is often preferred for forensics.

How to Set

`View > Time Display Format > Seconds Since Beginning of Capture`.

Key Takeaway

Mastering Wireshark's navigation and time display features is crucial for efficiently reconstructing event timelines and correlating forensic evidence.
