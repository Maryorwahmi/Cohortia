Anomaly Detection

Detecting Suspicious Connection Attempts and Malformed Packets

Using Wireshark to Uncover Brute-Force Attacks and Exploits

Repeated Connection Attempts/Failures

  • Indicative of brute-force attacks or port scans.
  • Look for many SYN packets without corresponding ACKs.
  • `tcp.analysis.retransmissions` can highlight issues.

Malformed Packets

  • Packets that don't conform to protocol standards.
  • Used by attackers to evade detection or exploit vulnerabilities.
  • Wireshark's 'Expert Information' can highlight these.
Key Takeaway

Monitoring for unusual connection patterns and malformed packets provides crucial early warnings of active attacks or exploitation attempts.

Wireshark Detection Methods
Filter for SYN Packets
tcp.flags.syn == 1 and tcp.flags.ack == 0
Analyze Retransmissions
tcp.analysis.retransmissions
Expert Information

Navigate to `Analyze > Expert Information` to view Wireshark's insights on potential issues, including malformed packets, retransmissions, and other protocol anomalies.
