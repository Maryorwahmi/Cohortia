Anomaly Detection

Spotting Unusual Network Traffic Patterns

Recognizing Deviations from Normal Behavior

Unusual Ports or Protocols

Traffic on non-standard ports for common services, or the presence of unexpected protocols. Attackers often use these to evade detection.

High Volume Traffic

Sudden spikes in data transfer, especially to external destinations or unusual internal hosts. This can indicate data exfiltration or botnet activity.

Key Takeaway

Proactive identification of unusual traffic patterns, such as non-standard port usage or sudden data spikes, is crucial for early detection of security incidents.
