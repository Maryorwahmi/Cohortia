Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways: Part 2

Anomaly Detection

The process of identifying unusual or unexpected patterns in network traffic that may indicate malicious activity, misconfigurations, or performance issues.

Chain of Custody

The chronological documentation or paper trail showing the seizure, custody, control, transfer, analysis, and disposition of physical or electronic evidence.

Chapter Summary

Wireshark is a powerful tool for network forensics, enabling detailed analysis of traffic, extraction of evidence, and reconstruction of events to effectively investigate security incidents while adhering to legal and ethical guidelines.
