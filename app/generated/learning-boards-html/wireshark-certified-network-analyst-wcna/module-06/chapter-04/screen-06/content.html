Performance Metric

Assessing Data Transfer Efficiency

While Wireshark doesn't directly measure real-time throughput, it provides crucial data to infer transfer volume and efficiency.

Data Volume

Wireshark shows the total amount of data transferred within a capture, allowing you to quantify the volume over time.

Transfer Efficiency

Analyze protocol usage, retransmissions, and windowing to understand how efficiently data is being moved.

Key Takeaway

By analyzing data volume and transfer efficiency, Wireshark helps you identify bottlenecks that reduce effective throughput, even without direct real-time measurements.
