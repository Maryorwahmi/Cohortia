Key Concepts

Advanced Diagnostic Terms

Zero Window

Receiver advertises a window size of zero, indicating its buffer is full and halting data transmission.

Expert Information

Wireshark feature providing a summary of potential network problems detected in the capture.

Out-of-Order Packets

TCP segments arriving at the receiver in an order different from how they were sent.

Summary Takeaway

These advanced concepts provide deeper insights into network behavior, enabling precise identification of bottlenecks and effective troubleshooting strategies.
