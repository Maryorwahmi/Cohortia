Pro Tips
Optimizing Your Filtering Workflow
Strategies for efficient and effective analysis.
Why Optimize?

Enhance efficiency, ensure accuracy, and prevent misinterpretations during complex network analysis and troubleshooting.

Key Takeaway

Adopt practices like saving filters and iterative application, and always consider the unfiltered context for reliable analysis.

1

Save Filters

Build a library of frequently used complex filters for quick, consistent application across sessions.

2

Apply Iteratively

Start with broad filters and gradually narrow down to understand the impact of each condition and avoid over-filtering.

3

Context is Key

Never draw conclusions solely from filtered data. Refer back to the full capture for crucial context if anomalies persist.
