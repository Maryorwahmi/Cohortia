Traffic Characteristics
Analyzing Traffic by Length and Time
Identifying anomalies and temporal patterns.
Length Filters

Use frame.len or ip.len to identify unusually large packets, fragmentation, or jumbo frames.

Time Filters

Filter by frame.time_relative for time since capture start, or frame.time for absolute timestamps.

Key Takeaway

Length and time filters provide a unique perspective for spotting anomalies and focusing on specific incident windows.

L

Packet Length Filters

Example: frame.len > 1500 (finds packets larger than standard MTU).

ip.len > 1000
T

Time-Based Filters

Example: frame.time_relative > 60 and frame.time_relative < 120 (packets between 60-120s).

frame.time > "2023-01-01 10:00:00"
Common Mistake

Overlooking these basic filters. They can provide crucial insights into network behavior and performance.
