Common Pitfalls in Advanced Filtering
Ensuring accuracy and avoiding lost data.
The Risk of Errors

Incorrect filters can lead to misleading analysis or crucial data being overlooked, impacting troubleshooting effectiveness.

Key Takeaway

Always verify field names and apply filters iteratively to avoid over-filtering and maintain the full context of your capture.

1

Incorrect Field Names

Example: Using http.status_code instead of http.response.code. Always verify in Packet Details.

2

Over-Filtering

Creating overly complex filters that inadvertently hide relevant information. Start broad, then narrow down.

Pro Tip

Save frequently used complex filters. This ensures consistency and saves time from retyping them.
