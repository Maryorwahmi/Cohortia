Network Overview

Statistics: Protocol Hierarchy

Understand Traffic Distribution at a Glance

Protocol Breakdown

Shows all detected protocols and their percentage of total packets and bytes.

Hierarchical View

Protocols are organized by their layer, providing context on dependencies.

Anomaly Detection

Helps quickly spot unusual traffic patterns, like excessive broadcast or unknown protocols.

Concrete Example

You're troubleshooting a slow network. Checking Protocol Hierarchy reveals that 60% of traffic is 'SMB'. This immediately suggests that file sharing is dominating the network, potentially indicating a large file transfer in progress or an inefficient SMB configuration, guiding your next steps.

Key Takeaway

Protocol Hierarchy provides a crucial high-level view of network traffic composition, enabling rapid identification of dominant protocols and potential anomalies that require further investigation.

Misconception

A common mistake is to assume a high percentage for a known protocol is always normal. Always consider the context; for example, high ARP traffic in a stable network segment is often a sign of an issue, not normal behavior.
