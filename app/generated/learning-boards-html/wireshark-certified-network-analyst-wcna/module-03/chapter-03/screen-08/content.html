Combining Conditions with Logical Operators

and, or, not for Precise Filtering

Logical Operators

  • •and (or &&): Both conditions must be true.
  • •or (or ||): At least one condition must be true.
  • •not (or !): The condition must be false.
  • •Parentheses () for grouping and order of operations.
Common Mistake

Forgetting parentheses can lead to incorrect filter logic. Always group complex conditions.

Examples

  • •tcp port 80 and host 10.0.0.50 (HTTP to specific server).
  • •not port 22 (all traffic except SSH).
  • •host 192.168.1.100 and (port 80 or port 443) (HTTP/HTTPS to specific host).
  • •src net 192.168.1.0/24 and dst port 53 (DNS queries from a subnet).
Pro Tip

Start simple and gradually add complexity. Test your filters to ensure they capture the intended traffic.

Key Takeaway

Logical operators are essential for creating complex BPF filters. Always use parentheses to explicitly define the order of operations and ensure your filter behaves as expected.
