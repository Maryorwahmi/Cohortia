1

Filter by Port Numbers

Isolate traffic related to specific services by filtering on source or destination port numbers.

tcp.dstport == 80
udp.port == 53
2

Exclude Specific Ports

Use the not operator with grouped conditions to exclude multiple port numbers from your view.

not (tcp.port == 22 or tcp.port == 443)
3

Filter by Packet Length

Identify unusually large or small packets using comparison operators with frame.len.

frame.len > 1500
frame.len < 64
Key Takeaway

Numerical filters are powerful for isolating traffic based on port numbers and packet lengths, aiding in service-specific or anomaly detection.
