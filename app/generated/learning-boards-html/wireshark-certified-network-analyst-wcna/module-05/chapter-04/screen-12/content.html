Utilizing Wireshark Statistics for Security Analysis

Leverage Wireshark's 'Conversations' and 'Endpoints' statistics to quickly pinpoint suspicious network activity and gain high-level insights.

Key Takeaway

Statistical views help identify unusual communication volumes, unexpected communication partners, and highly active hosts, guiding deeper investigation.

Expert Information

Don't forget Wireshark's 'Expert Information' feature, which can automatically flag potential issues and suspicious activities detected in the capture.

Statistical Views in Wireshark
Conversations

Summarizes communication flows between two endpoints, useful for identifying high-volume or unusual interactions.

Endpoints

Lists all unique IP/MAC addresses, showing total packets and bytes, ideal for spotting unusually active hosts.

I/O Graphs

Visualizes network throughput over time, helping to identify traffic spikes or sustained abnormal activity.
