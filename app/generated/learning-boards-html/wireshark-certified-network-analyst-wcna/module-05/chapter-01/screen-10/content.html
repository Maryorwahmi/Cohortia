Assessing Bandwidth Utilization with I/O Graphs

Visualizing network traffic patterns over time

Purpose of I/O Graphs

Visualize network traffic patterns over time to identify high bandwidth utilization, congestion, or unusual activity.

Accessing I/O Graphs

Navigate to Statistics > I/O Graph in Wireshark to open the graphing interface.

Default Metrics

By default, graphs display total packets per second and bytes per second, offering an immediate network activity overview.

Interpreting Graphs
  • •
    Spikes: Indicate sudden bursts of traffic, potentially causing temporary congestion.
  • •
    Sustained High Utilization: Suggests a bottleneck if close to link capacity.
Key Takeaway

I/O Graphs provide a crucial visual overview of network traffic, helping correlate performance issues with periods of high utilization or traffic spikes.

Important Note

High utilization at your capture point doesn't always mean the entire link is saturated, but it's a strong indicator for further investigation.
