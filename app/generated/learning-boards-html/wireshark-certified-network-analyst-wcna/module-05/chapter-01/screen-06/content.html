Analyzing Round-Trip Time (RTT) and Retransmission Timeout (RTO)

Pinpointing latency with TCP analysis

Round-Trip Time (RTT)

The total time for a signal to travel to its destination and for its acknowledgment to return. A direct measure of latency.

Retransmission Timeout (RTO)

A timer used by TCP. If an acknowledgment isn't received before the RTO expires, the segment is retransmitted, often due to high latency or loss.

Key Takeaway

Frequent RTO flags and consistently high time deltas in TCP streams are definitive signs of network latency impacting performance.

Pro Tip

When investigating latency, always consider both network path delays and server processing times. Wireshark helps differentiate.
