Key Concepts: Core Network Problems

Essential terms for Wireshark analysis

Latency

The delay experienced by data packets as they travel across a network, often measured as Round-Trip Time (RTT).

Packet Loss

Occurs when data packets fail to reach their destination, leading to retransmissions and degraded performance.

Retransmission

The process of resending a TCP segment that was not acknowledged by the receiver within a specified timeout period.

Chapter Summary

Understanding latency, packet loss, and retransmissions is fundamental for diagnosing network performance issues with Wireshark.
