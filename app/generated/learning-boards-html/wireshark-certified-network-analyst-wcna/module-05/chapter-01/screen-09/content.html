Nuance in Diagnosis

Differentiating Retransmissions: Congestion vs. Loss

Understanding the context of retransmitted packets

Retransmissions due to Congestion

Packets are delayed or buffered, causing the sender's RTO to expire, even if the original packet eventually arrives. Often called 'spurious retransmissions'.

Retransmissions due to Actual Loss

The packet genuinely never reached its destination due to physical faults, dropped connections, or faulty network devices.

Impact on Performance

Both types of retransmissions lead to perceived latency and degraded application performance as data must be sent multiple times.

Wireshark Clues
  • •
    Gaps in Sequence Numbers: Strong indicator of actual packet loss.
  • •
    Overall Pattern: Widespread retransmissions suggest congestion; isolated ones point to specific issues.
Key Takeaway

Distinguishing between congestion-induced and true packet loss retransmissions is crucial for applying the correct network troubleshooting solution.
