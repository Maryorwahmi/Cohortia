Identifying Packet Loss Fundamentals

How TCP sequence and acknowledgment numbers reveal missing data

What is Packet Loss?

Data packets fail to reach their destination, arrive out of order, or are corrupted, leading to retransmissions.

TCP Sequence Numbers

Each data segment sent by the sender is assigned a unique sequence number, like page numbers in a book.

TCP Acknowledgment Numbers

The receiver sends an ACK number indicating the next expected sequence number, confirming receipt of previous data.

Key Takeaway

Packet loss is detected by analyzing TCP sequence and acknowledgment numbers, revealing when data segments are missing or out of order.

Misconception

Packet loss isn't exclusive to wireless networks; it can occur on wired links due to congestion or faulty hardware.
