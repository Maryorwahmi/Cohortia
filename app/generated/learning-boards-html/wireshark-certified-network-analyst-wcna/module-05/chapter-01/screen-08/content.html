Diagnosing Retransmissions and Duplicate ACKs

Using Wireshark filters to spot packet loss

TCP Retransmission

The sender resends a segment because the original was not acknowledged within the expected timeframe. Filter: tcp.analysis.retransmission.

Duplicate Acknowledgment (DUP ACK)

The receiver repeatedly acknowledges an earlier segment, signaling that a subsequent segment is missing. Filter: tcp.analysis.duplicate_ack.

Investigate Context

When these flags appear, examine surrounding packets and look for gaps in sequence numbers within the TCP stream.

Key Takeaway

A high number of retransmissions and duplicate ACKs in Wireshark strongly indicates packet loss or severe network congestion.

Pro Tip

Don't just filter; follow the TCP stream to see the full conversation and pinpoint exactly where packets went missing.
