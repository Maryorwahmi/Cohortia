Identifying Rogue DHCP Servers

Detecting unauthorized DHCP servers on your network

What is a Rogue DHCP Server?

  • An unauthorized DHCP server on the network.
  • Can assign incorrect IP configurations to clients.
  • Causes network disruption and connectivity issues.
  • Often a misconfigured device or malicious actor.

Common Mistake

Overlooking the source of DHCP offers, assuming all offers are legitimate. Always verify the source IP/MAC.

Detection in Wireshark

1. Filter for DHCP Offers

Use bootp.option.type == 2 to see all DHCP Offer messages.

2. Inspect Source IP/MAC

Check the source IP and MAC address of the DHCP Offer packets.

3. Identify Unexpected Sources

If offers come from an unknown or unauthorized device, it's a rogue server.

Key Takeaway

Rogue DHCP servers are detected by identifying DHCP Offers originating from unauthorized devices on the network.
