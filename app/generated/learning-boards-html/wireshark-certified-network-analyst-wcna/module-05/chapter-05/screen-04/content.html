Definition

What is Network Baselining?

Network baselining is the process of capturing and analyzing network traffic during normal operations to establish a 'normal' performance profile. This profile serves as a benchmark for future comparisons.

Why it Matters

It enables proactive anomaly detection. By knowing what normal looks like, any significant deviation immediately flags a potential problem, allowing for faster and more targeted troubleshooting.

Key Takeaway

Baselining creates a reference point, turning reactive troubleshooting into a proactive, data-driven process.
