Common Packet Capture Mistakes

Identifying and preventing errors in your workflow

Wrong Interface

Not selecting the correct network interface leads to empty or irrelevant captures. Always verify activity graphs.

No/Incorrect Capture Filter

Capturing everything creates huge files; incorrect filters block desired traffic. Always test your filters!

Insufficient Disk Space

Not accounting for large capture files can cause captures to stop prematurely, losing valuable data.

Poor Naming/Organization

Without descriptive names and organized storage, finding and understanding old captures becomes impossible.

Ignoring Security

Treating captures as innocuous can lead to severe data breaches and legal issues. Always prioritize privacy.

Forgetting Promiscuous Mode

On some systems, promiscuous mode might not be enabled by default, leading to incomplete captures on shared segments.

Key Takeaway

Proactively identifying and mitigating these common mistakes ensures your packet capture workflows are robust, reliable, and secure.
