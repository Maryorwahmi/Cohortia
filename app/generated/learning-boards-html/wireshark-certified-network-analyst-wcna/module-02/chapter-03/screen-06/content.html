Implementing a Rolling Ring Buffer

Ensuring continuous capture without exhausting disk space

How Ring Buffers Work

  • •Specify a maximum number of capture files to keep.
  • •Once the limit is reached, Wireshark overwrites the oldest file.
  • •Creates a continuous, rolling capture of recent data.
  • •Ideal for long-term monitoring without disk space issues.
Common Mistake

Setting the ring buffer too small can lead to critical historical data being overwritten before it can be analyzed.

Configuration Example

  • •Enable 'Use multiple files' (e.g., 100MB each).
  • •Enable 'Use a ring buffer with' (e.g., 10 files).
  • •Wireshark will keep the most recent 10 x 100MB = 1GB of data.
  • •Oldest files are deleted as new ones are created.
Key Takeaway

Ring buffers are invaluable for continuous, unattended monitoring, especially for troubleshooting sporadic issues, by ensuring you always have the most recent data.
