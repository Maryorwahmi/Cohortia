Security and Privacy in Packet Capture

Protecting sensitive data during network analysis

Consent

Always obtain appropriate authorization before capturing traffic on a network you don't own or manage to avoid legal repercussions.

Minimization

Use capture filters to collect only the necessary data. Avoid capturing more than you need to reduce risk exposure.

Storage

Store capture files securely, ideally on encrypted drives or network shares with restricted access. Treat them as highly confidential.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize consent, data minimization, and secure storage to protect sensitive information contained within network captures.
