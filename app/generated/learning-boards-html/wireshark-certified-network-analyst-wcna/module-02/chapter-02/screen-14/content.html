Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Primitives

Basic building blocks of BPF expressions, such as `host`, `port`, `tcp`, `udp`, `arp`, and `icmp`.

Logical Operators

Keywords like `and`, `or`, and `not` used to combine BPF primitives for complex filtering logic.

Targeted Analysis

The ultimate goal of using capture filters: to focus on specific, relevant traffic for efficient troubleshooting and monitoring.

By combining primitives with logical operators, you gain precise control over your network captures.
