`not` Operator & Parentheses

  • •`not` negates the following condition, excluding specific traffic.
  • •Parentheses `()` group expressions to control logical order.
  • •Crucial for building complex and unambiguous BPF filters.

Scenario: Targeted Service Monitoring

To monitor SSH or RDP traffic specifically for a particular server, you need to group the port conditions. Without parentheses, `host 192.168.1.10 and tcp port 22 or tcp port 3389` would be interpreted differently, potentially capturing all port 3389 traffic regardless of the host. Parentheses ensure the logic is applied correctly.

BPF Complex Filter Example
# Capture traffic to/from 192.168.1.10
# AND (TCP port 22 OR TCP port 3389)

host 192.168.1.10 and (tcp port 22 or tcp port 3389)
Key Takeaway

Use `not` for exclusions and parentheses to group logical conditions, ensuring precise and unambiguous complex BPF filters.
