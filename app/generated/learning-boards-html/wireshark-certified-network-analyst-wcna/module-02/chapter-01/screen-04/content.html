The Essence of Packet Capture

Recording the digital conversations of your network

What is a Pcap?

A 'pcap' (packet capture) or 'trace' is a digital recording of network traffic, capturing individual data packets as they pass through a network interface.

Why it Matters

It provides raw, unfiltered data, essential for deep analysis, troubleshooting, performance tuning, and security investigations.

Key Takeaway

Packet capture transforms Wireshark into an indispensable diagnostic tool by providing direct visibility into network data flow.
