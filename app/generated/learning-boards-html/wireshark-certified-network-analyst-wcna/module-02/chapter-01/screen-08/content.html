Your First Capture: Wireshark GUI

A user-friendly approach to real-time packet monitoring

Real-time Display

Wireshark's GUI immediately shows packets as they are captured, providing instant feedback.

Saving Captures

After stopping, captured packets remain in the buffer, allowing you to save them as a .pcapng file for later analysis.

Key Takeaway

The Wireshark GUI provides a visual and intuitive way to initiate, monitor, and stop packet captures for immediate analysis.

Wireshark GUI Capture Flow

Follow these steps to start your first capture.

1

Launch Wireshark: Open the application to the 'Welcome screen'.

2

Select Interface: Choose the desired network interface(s) from the list.

3

Start Capture: Click the blue 'Start capturing packets' fin icon.

4

Stop Capture: Click the red 'Stop capturing packets' square icon when finished.
