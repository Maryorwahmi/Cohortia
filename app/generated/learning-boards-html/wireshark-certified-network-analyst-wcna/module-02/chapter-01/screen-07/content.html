Promiscuous Mode in Switched vs. Hub Networks

How network devices impact what your adapter can see

Hub-Based Networks

Hubs broadcast all traffic to all ports. Promiscuous mode on a hub sees all network traffic.

Switched Ethernet Networks

Switches isolate traffic between ports. Promiscuous mode primarily sees traffic to/from your machine, plus broadcast/multicast.

Key Takeaway

On switched networks, port mirroring (SPAN) or network taps are needed to capture all traffic, even with promiscuous mode enabled.

Network Topology & Visibility

Understanding the impact of hubs vs. switches.

Hub Network

All traffic is broadcast to all devices. Promiscuous mode sees everything.

Switch Network

Traffic is isolated to specific ports. Promiscuous mode sees limited traffic.

Solution for Switches

Use Port Mirroring (SPAN) or Network Taps to direct all traffic to your monitoring port.
