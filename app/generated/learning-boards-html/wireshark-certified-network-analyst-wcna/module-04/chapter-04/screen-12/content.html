Extending Wireshark

Introduction to Custom Wireshark Dissectors

Leveraging Lua for Specialized Protocol Analysis

Lua Scripting: Custom dissectors are typically written in Lua, a lightweight and extensible scripting language.

Automated Parsing: Teaches Wireshark to automatically parse, display, and label fields of unknown protocols.

Enhanced Analysis: Assigns meaningful names, highlights errors, and makes analysis of proprietary traffic much more efficient.

Key Takeaway

Custom Wireshark dissectors, written in Lua, extend Wireshark's capabilities to automatically parse and analyze proprietary or non-standard protocols, greatly enhancing efficiency.

Lua Dissector Concept

How a Dissector Works

Simplified Flow
  1. Register Dissector: Tell Wireshark about your new protocol and its associated port.
  2. Receive Packet: Wireshark passes raw packet data to your Lua script.
  3. Parse Fields: Your script reads bytes, identifies fields (e.g., header, payload), and extracts values.
  4. Display Data: Your script populates Wireshark's packet details pane with human-readable labels and values.
  5. Error Detection: Optionally, your script can check for protocol violations and highlight them.
Pro Tip

While full dissector creation is advanced, understanding its possibility and knowing where to find Wireshark's developer guide is a valuable skill for a WCNA.
