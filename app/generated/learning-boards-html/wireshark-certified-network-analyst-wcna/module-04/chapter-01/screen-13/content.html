Wireshark Filters

Practical Filters for TCP/IP Analysis

Targeting specific traffic patterns in Wireshark.

TCP Handshake Filters

tcp.flags.syn == 1
tcp.flags.syn == 1 and tcp.flags.ack == 1
tcp.flags.ack == 1 and not tcp.flags.syn

Isolate SYN, SYN-ACK, and pure ACK packets to observe connection establishment.

Retransmission & ACK Filters

tcp.analysis.retransmission
tcp.analysis.duplicate_ack
tcp.analysis.out_of_order

Identify segments that were retransmitted, duplicate acknowledgments, and out-of-order packets.

TCP Window Filters

tcp.window_size_value < 1000
tcp.window_size_value == 0

Spot small or zero window sizes to diagnose flow control and receiver-side bottlenecks.

IP Addressing & TTL Filters

ip.addr == 192.168.1.100
ipv6.addr == 2001:db8::1
ip.ttl < 5

Filter by specific IP addresses or identify packets with low Time-to-Live values for routing diagnostics.

Key Takeaway

Proficiency with Wireshark display filters is crucial for efficient TCP/IP analysis, enabling you to quickly isolate and diagnose specific network behaviors and issues.
