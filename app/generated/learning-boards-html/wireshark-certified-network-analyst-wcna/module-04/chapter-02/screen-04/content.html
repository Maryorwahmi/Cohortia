UDP Characteristics
  • Connectionless: No handshake, no session establishment.
  • Unreliable: No guaranteed delivery or retransmissions.
  • No Ordering: Packets may arrive out of sequence.
  • Minimal Overhead: Smaller header, faster processing.
Why UDP is Preferred

Ideal for applications where speed is paramount and occasional packet loss is acceptable or handled by the application layer. Examples include VoIP, video streaming, and online gaming.

Key Takeaway

UDP's simplicity and speed make it indispensable for real-time applications, despite its lack of built-in reliability features.
