Key Mechanism

Promiscuous Mode: Seeing All Traffic

Capturing frames not directly addressed to your Network Interface Card.

Normal Mode

Processes only frames addressed to its MAC, broadcast, and multicast.

Promiscuous Mode

Captures *all* frames seen on the network segment.

Why it Matters

Essential for comprehensive network analysis, especially when observing traffic between other devices or on shared media like Wi-Fi.

Key Distinction

On switched networks, promiscuous mode alone is not enough; a SPAN port or tap is still required to see all traffic.

Enabling in Wireshark

Typically toggled in Wireshark's capture options for a selected interface, allowing you to control its behavior.

Key Takeaway

Promiscuous mode expands your NIC's visibility, but its effectiveness depends on the underlying network topology (switched vs. hub/shared media).
