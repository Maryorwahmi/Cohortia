Core Concept

Identifying Your Network Interface

The first critical step to capturing relevant network traffic.

What are Network Interfaces?

Hardware components (Ethernet, Wi-Fi) or virtual adapters that connect your computer to a network.

Types of Interfaces

Physical (Ethernet, Wi-Fi), Virtual (VMware, VirtualBox), and Loopback (internal traffic).

Identifying Active Interfaces

Look for activity graphs in Wireshark showing fluctuating traffic, indicating data flow.

Key Takeaway

Always verify the selected interface is actively carrying the specific traffic you intend to analyze to avoid capturing irrelevant data.
