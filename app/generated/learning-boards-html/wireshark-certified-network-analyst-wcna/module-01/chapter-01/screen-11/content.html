Deep Dive: Packet Details and Packet Bytes Panes

Inspecting the decoded protocol layers and raw data.

Packet Details Pane

Provides a hierarchical view of the selected packet's decoded protocols and fields, breaking down data by OSI layer.

  • •Shows Ethernet, IP, TCP/UDP, and application layer details.
  • •Expandable layers reveal specific field values.

Packet Bytes Pane

Displays the raw hexadecimal and ASCII representation of the entire packet data, crucial for low-level debugging.

  • •Shows the exact bytes transmitted over the wire.
  • •Useful for verifying decoded values or analyzing unknown protocols.

Why it matters

These panes provide the granular detail necessary to diagnose complex network issues, verify protocol implementations, and understand data flow at its most fundamental level.

Key Takeaway

The Packet Details and Packet Bytes Panes are essential for in-depth analysis, revealing the full structure and raw content of network packets.
