Key Concepts: Core Definitions

Essential terms for understanding network analysis with Wireshark.

Wireshark

A free, open-source network protocol analyzer used for troubleshooting, analysis, and development.

Packet Analyzer

A tool that intercepts and logs traffic passing over a digital network, also known as a network sniffer.

Packet

A formatted unit of data carried by a packet-switched network, typically at the network layer (e.g., IP).

Summary Takeaway

Wireshark is the leading packet analyzer, providing detailed insights into network traffic by dissecting individual packets.
