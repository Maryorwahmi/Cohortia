A Brief History: From Ethereal to Wireshark

The evolution of the de facto standard in network analysis.

1998

Ethereal is Born

Gerald Combs creates Ethereal, a free network analyzer.

Early 2000s

Community Growth

Ethereal gains popularity, vibrant open-source community emerges.

2006

Rebranded as Wireshark

Trademark issues lead to the name change; core project continues.

Why it matters

Wireshark's open-source nature and continuous community contributions have made it the most powerful and widely adopted network analysis tool globally.

Key Takeaway

Wireshark's journey from Ethereal highlights the power of open-source development in creating and maintaining a critical industry standard.
