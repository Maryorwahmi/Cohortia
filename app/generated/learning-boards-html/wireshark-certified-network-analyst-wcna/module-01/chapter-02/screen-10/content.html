Switches and SPAN Ports: Intelligent Traffic Forwarding

Optimizing network efficiency and enabling full capture.

Switches (Layer 2)

Intelligent devices that learn MAC addresses and forward traffic only to the intended destination port.

Limited Wireshark Visibility

On a regular switch port, Wireshark only sees broadcast, multicast, and traffic to/from its own machine.

SPAN Port (Port Mirror)

A switch feature that duplicates traffic from source ports to a monitoring port for full Wireshark capture.

Key Takeaway

Switches optimize traffic flow, but require SPAN port configuration to enable comprehensive Wireshark captures for troubleshooting.

Switch Traffic Flow

Targeted forwarding vs. SPAN port mirroring.

Normal: Device A to B only.

SPAN: Device A to B, PLUS copy to Wireshark port.

Wireshark sees all mirrored traffic.
