Hubs: The Simplest Network Repeater

Understanding how hubs broadcast all traffic.

Layer 1 Device

Operates at the Physical layer of the OSI model. No intelligence about data destination.

Traffic Repetition

Repeats all incoming traffic out to all other connected ports, creating a single collision domain.

Wireshark Visibility

Capturing on a hub allows you to see all traffic passing through it, as everything is broadcast.

Key Takeaway

Hubs broadcast all traffic, making them easy to capture with Wireshark but inefficient and outdated for modern networks.

Hub Traffic Flow

Data from one port is sent to all others.

Device A sends data.

Hub receives data.

Hub sends data to ALL other ports.

Device B, C, D all receive data.
