Describe Network Models

Understand the purpose and layers of the OSI and TCP/IP models. This skill is crucial for interpreting how data is encapsulated and transmitted across networks, providing a logical framework for Wireshark's dissection pane.

Differentiate Addressing

Distinguish between IPv4, IPv6, and MAC addresses. This knowledge is fundamental for identifying devices and understanding local versus routed communication, enabling precise filtering in Wireshark.
