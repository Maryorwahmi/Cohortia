Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Privacy & Best Practices
Practical rules
• Avoid sensitive content in notification bodies. Use a small data key to fetch protected content after authentication. • Provide per-category controls and respect them. • Throttle similar messages to avoid repeated alerts. • Transmit tokens securely and never expose them publicly.
Settings UX
Offer a simple settings screen for categories and include short descriptions so users understand what each toggle controls.
Key Takeaway
Protect privacy, let users control frequency and categories, and keep notification content minimal and actionable.
