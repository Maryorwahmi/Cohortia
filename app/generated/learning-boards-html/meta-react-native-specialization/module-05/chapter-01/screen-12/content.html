Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Privacy & Consent
Consent patterns

Ask explicit permission before uploading. Explain why you need the image and how it will be used.

Example

Show a confirmation that the image will be uploaded to update the profile and include a cancel option.

Storage & security

If storing images server-side, apply access controls and minimize retention. Consider encryption when appropriate.

Key Takeaway

Treat images as sensitive data: get consent, minimize uploads, and secure storage.

Operational tip

Provide a way for users to remove or replace images and document your retention policy in-app or in privacy settings.
