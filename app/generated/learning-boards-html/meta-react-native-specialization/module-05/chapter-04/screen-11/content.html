Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Performance and accessibility timeline
Timeline
1. Design intent
Decide what to animate and why; prefer subtle, purposeful motion.
2. Implement with native driver
Use useNativeDriver for transforms and opacity where possible.
3. Test on device
Measure feel and frame rate on real hardware.
4. Optimize
Reduce re-renders, debounce gesture updates, and avoid heavy JS in the animation path.
5. Accessibility
Offer reduced-motion settings and avoid disorienting effects.
6. Final verification
Ensure gestures and navigation interact correctly after wrapping with GestureHandlerRootView.
Key Takeaway

Follow a test-driven timeline: design, implement, test on device, optimize, and add accessibility to ship smooth and safe motion.
