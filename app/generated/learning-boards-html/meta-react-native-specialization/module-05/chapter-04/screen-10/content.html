Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Interactive card swipe
Design Pattern
Threshold, interpolation, and dismissal flow for a swipe card.
Inputs
translationX, velocityX from PanGestureHandler.
Transforms
Interpolate rotate from translateX and map translateX to transform translate. Animate opacity alongside translate for smooth fade-out.
Decision rule
If abs(translationX) > SWIPE_THRESHOLD or abs(velocityX) > speedThreshold then dismiss; else spring back.
Try this

Implement threshold and test on device. Tune rotation interpolation and dismissal speed until it feels natural.

Concrete Example
Use a screen-width threshold of 25% and interpolate rotate between -10deg and 10deg for full drag. On dismissal, parallel translate off-screen and fade to zero in 300ms, then reset or remove the card from the list.
Accessibility note
Provide alternative actions for users who cannot perform swipe gestures and respect reduced-motion preferences by simplifying or disabling motion where appropriate.
