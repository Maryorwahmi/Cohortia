What is geolocation?
A device-level service returns latitude, longitude, and optional metadata such as accuracy and speed.
Core claim
Geolocation is a privacy-sensitive sensor: treat its data as personal and ask for the minimum you need.
Concrete example
A delivery app fetches the driver’s latitude and longitude and displays a real-time pin for the customer to follow.
Common mistake
Assuming maximum accuracy is always necessary; this drains battery and may frustrate users.
Key Takeaway

Design your feature to request the minimum accuracy and duration of location access necessary to protect battery and user privacy.

Quick taxonomy
LocationObject fields you will see: latitude, longitude, accuracy, altitude, speed. Libraries may return these inside a coords object.
Latitude & Longitude
Core coordinates used to place a pin on a map.
Accuracy
Measured in meters; lower means more precise but often higher battery cost.
Speed & Altitude
Useful for fitness or elevation-aware features; may be null indoors.
