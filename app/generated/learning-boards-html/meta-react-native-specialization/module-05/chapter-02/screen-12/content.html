Operational timeline
  1. Decide if foreground access suffices. If not, document why background access is necessary.
  2. Ask permission with a clear pre-dialog explanation.
  3. Allow users to enable, disable, and pause tracking from the app UI.
  4. Encrypt or anonymize stored coordinates and minimize retention windows.
  5. Document data usage in your privacy policy and surface options for users to delete their data.
Pro tip
Offer an in-app privacy control that clearly shows when location is being collected and why.
Misconception
Assuming anonymization is trivial; temporal sequences of coordinates may still identify individuals.
