Question 1

Which pattern ensures readable sequential fetches and robust error handling? Answer: async/await with try...catch. It reads linearly and centralizes errors.

Question 2

Identify the mistake: forgetting to await fetch. Fix by awaiting fetch then awaiting response.json(). Always confirm whether a variable holds a Promise or the resolved value.

Final reflection

When reviewing code, trace the data timeline: from Promise to resolved value, and ensure you use await or then accordingly. That habit prevents many bugs.
