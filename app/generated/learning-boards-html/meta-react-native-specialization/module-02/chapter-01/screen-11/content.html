Modes explained
  • cover: fills box and preserves aspect ratio, may crop edges.
  • contain: fits entire image inside box, may leave empty space.
  • stretch: forces image to fill box, may distort.
Example

Use cover for avatar circles and hero images; use contain for logos that must not be cropped.

Key Takeaway
