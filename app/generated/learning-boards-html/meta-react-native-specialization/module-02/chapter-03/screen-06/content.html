Comparison of values
flex-start
Items pack toward the start of the main axis.
flex-end
Items pack toward the end of the main axis.
center
Items grouped in the middle of the main axis.
space-between
First and last items at edges, equal space between items.
space-around / space-evenly
space-around gives equal space around items but smaller edge gaps; space-evenly distributes equal gaps everywhere.
Concrete scenario
Toolbar with a left icon, centered title, and right icon: keep flexDirection: 'row' and use space-between so the title stays visually centered while icons anchor to edges.
Pro tip
When children have different sizes, combine fixed sizes with justifyContent to shape overall spacing. justifyContent works on leftover space after fixed sizing.
