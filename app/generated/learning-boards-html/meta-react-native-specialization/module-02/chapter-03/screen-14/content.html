Sample question and answer
Q:
You must display three Text components side-by-side with equal space between them and vertically centered. Which container properties do you set?
A:
flexDirection: 'row', justifyContent: 'space-between', alignItems: 'center'. This lays out children horizontally, spaces them across the main axis, and centers them on the cross axis.
Flex: 1 explanation
flex: 1 makes the child flexible to fill available space. With multiple flex children, space is divided proportionally according to flex values.
