Core Principle

Reduce Your Attack Surface

Every active service and open port is a potential vulnerability. Proactively identify and disable services that are not critical for your system's operation. This minimizes the number of entry points an attacker can exploit.

Unnecessary services are attack vectors.
Disable what isn't essential.
Key Takeaway

A smaller attack surface means fewer opportunities for compromise. Regularly audit and disable services you don't need, and always secure default configurations.

Actionable Steps

Secure Service Configurations

  • •Change Defaults: Always modify default passwords and settings.
  • •Restrict Access: Limit service access to specific users, groups, or IP addresses.
  • •Enable Encryption: Use HTTPS for web services and other encrypted protocols.
  • •Review Permissions: Ensure service files and directories have least privilege.
Example Command
# List all active services
systemctl list-units --type=service --state=running

# Disable an unnecessary service (e.g., telnetd)
sudo systemctl disable --now telnetd
Pro Tip

Regularly review your system's open ports using sudo ss -ltnp to identify any unexpected listening services.
