Identify Active Services

systemctl for Service Status

Use systemctl to list all services currently running on your system. This helps you identify any services that might be active but not essential.

# List all active services
systemctl list-units --type=service --state=running
Action

Review the list. For any service you don't recognize or don't need, consider disabling it: sudo systemctl disable <service_name>.

Identify Open Ports

ss for Listening Connections

The ss command (socket statistics) shows listening ports and the processes associated with them. This reveals what services are exposed to the network.

# List all listening TCP ports with associated processes
sudo ss -ltnp
Security Risk

An open port for an unneeded service (e.g., Telnet on port 23) is a direct security vulnerability. Always disable such services.

Key Takeaway

Regularly audit running services and open ports using systemctl and ss to minimize your system's attack surface and enhance security.
