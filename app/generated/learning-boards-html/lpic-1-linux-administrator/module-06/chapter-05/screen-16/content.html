Essential Network & Security Concepts

Network Services & Ports

Applications providing network functionality (HTTP, DNS, SSH) and their standard communication ports.

systemd Socket Activation

systemd listens on ports, starting services only when a connection arrives, improving efficiency and security.

Principle of Least Privilege

Granting users and services only the minimum permissions required for their functions.

This chapter equipped you with the knowledge to identify common network services, leverage systemd socket activation for efficiency and security, and implement the fundamental principle of least privilege to harden your Linux systems.
