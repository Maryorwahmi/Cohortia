Core Concept

iptables Tables and Chains

The hierarchical structure for organizing firewall rules.

Tables: Rule Categories

Tables group rules by their general purpose. The filter table is the most common, used for packet filtering (allowing or denying traffic).

Chains: Packet Processing Points

Chains are specific points within a table where rules are applied. Packets traverse these chains sequentially, being evaluated against each rule.

Key Takeaway

iptables organizes rules into tables and chains, providing a structured way to manage packet flow decisions.

iptables Structure

Tables contain Chains, Chains contain Rules.

Analogy: Tables are departments, and chains are specific checkpoints within those departments.
