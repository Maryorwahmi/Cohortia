System Management

From SysVinit to systemd: Evolution of Init Systems

Orchestrating Processes After Kernel Boot

  • •

    PID 1: The first process launched by the kernel, managing all others.

  • •

    SysVinit: Traditional init system, uses runlevels to define system states.

  • •

    systemd: Modern, flexible init system, uses 'units' and 'targets' for management.

  • •

    Key Advantages: Parallel startup, dependency management, unified control.

Key Takeaway

systemd is the modern standard for init systems, offering advanced capabilities for managing services and system states compared to its predecessor, SysVinit.
