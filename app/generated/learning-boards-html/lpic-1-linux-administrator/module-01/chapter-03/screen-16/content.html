Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Init Systems & Targets

Understanding the evolution and management of Linux system states.

initramfs

A temporary root filesystem loaded into memory early to provide necessary drivers.

Init System (systemd)

The first process launched by the kernel (PID 1), managing all other system processes and states.

Targets

System states in `systemd`, defined as groups of units, replacing traditional runlevels.

Summary Takeaway

Modern Linux systems rely on initramfs and systemd targets for flexible and robust management of system states after the kernel loads.
