Hardware Initialization

BIOS and UEFI: The System's First Instructions

Firmware that Awakens Your Hardware

  • •

    Initializes Hardware: Wakes up CPU, memory, and other essential components.

  • •

    Performs POST: Conducts a Power-On Self-Test to check hardware integrity.

  • •

    Locates Bootloader: Searches for a bootable device and reads MBR (BIOS) or GPT (UEFI).

Key Takeaway

Without functional BIOS/UEFI, the system cannot even begin to load an operating system, making it a critical first point of failure.
