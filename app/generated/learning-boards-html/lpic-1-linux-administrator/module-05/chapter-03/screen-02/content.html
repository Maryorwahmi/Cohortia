Learning Objectives

Skills You'll Master in This Chapter

LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Identify Running Processes

Learn to use `ps` and `top`/`htop` to view processes and their attributes. This skill is vital for system monitoring and troubleshooting performance issues.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Understand Process States

Grasp the meaning of process states (R, S, Z, T) and the roles of PIDs and PPIDs in process hierarchy. This knowledge is foundational for effective process control.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Send Signals to Processes

Learn to use `kill`, `killall`, and `pkill` to send signals like SIGTERM and SIGKILL. This enables you to manage unresponsive or unwanted processes effectively.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Differentiate Process Types

Distinguish between foreground and background processes and learn how to manage them. This is crucial for running long tasks without tying up your terminal.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
