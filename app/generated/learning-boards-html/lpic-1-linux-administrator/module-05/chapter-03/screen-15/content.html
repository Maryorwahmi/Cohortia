Key Concepts

Advanced Techniques for System Control

Signals

Software interrupts (e.g., SIGTERM, SIGKILL, SIGHUP) sent to processes to request actions.

`killall` & `pkill`

Commands to send signals to processes by name (`killall`) or pattern (`pkill`).

Foreground/Background

Foreground: Blocks terminal. Background: Runs independently (`&`).

Job Control

`Ctrl+Z` (stop), `bg` (background), `fg` (foreground), `jobs` (list jobs) for shell management.

`nohup` & `disown`

Commands to detach processes from the terminal, ensuring they continue running after logout.

Safety & Best Practices

Always verify PIDs, prioritize graceful termination, and be cautious with system processes.

Summary Takeaway

Effective process control involves understanding signals, using targeted termination commands, and mastering job management for persistent and stable system operations.
