The Six Fields of a Crontab Entry

Decoding Minute, Hour, Day, Month, and Day of Week

Syntax Order

minute hour day_of_month month day_of_week command

Field Count

Exactly six fields, separated by spaces.

Command Position

The sixth field is always the command or script to execute.

1. Minute

Range: 0-59

2. Hour

Range: 0-23 (24-hour format)

3. Day of Month

Range: 1-31

4. Month

Range: 1-12 or Jan-Dec

5. Day of Week

Range: 0-7 (0 or 7 is Sunday, 1 is Monday)

Key Takeaway

Each of the five time fields in a crontab entry must be correctly specified to ensure your automated tasks execute at the precise desired schedule.
