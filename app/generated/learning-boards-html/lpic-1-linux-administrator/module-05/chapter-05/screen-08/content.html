Scheduling Common Automated Tasks

Applying Crontab Syntax to Daily, Hourly, and Monthly Jobs

Daily Backup Script
0 2 * * * /usr/local/bin/daily_backup.sh

Runs daily_backup.sh at 2:00 AM every day.

Service Health Check
*/5 * * * * /home/user/check_service.py

Runs check_service.py every 5 minutes.

Monthly Report Generation
0 0 1 * * /opt/monthly_report.pl

Runs monthly_report.pl at midnight on the 1st of every month.

Key Considerations
  • Always use absolute paths for commands and scripts.
  • Ensure scripts have execute permissions (chmod +x).
  • Consider redirecting output to log files or /dev/null.
Key Takeaway

By combining the five time fields with special characters, you can precisely schedule a wide range of automated tasks to meet specific operational requirements.
