NUMERIC & LOGICAL TESTS

Numeric and Combined Conditions

Perform mathematical comparisons and combine multiple conditions.

advanced_conditions.sh
#!/bin/bash

NUM1=10
NUM2=5

# Numeric comparisons
if [ "$NUM1" -gt "$NUM2" ]; then
    echo "$NUM1 is greater than $NUM2."
fi

if [ "$NUM1" -eq 10 ]; then
    echo "$NUM1 is equal to 10."
fi

# Combined conditions with [[ ... ]]
FILE="/etc/passwd"
USER="root"

# Logical AND (both must be true)
if [[ -f "$FILE" && "$USER" = "root" ]]; then
    echo "File exists and user is root."
fi

# Logical OR (at least one must be true)
if [[ "$NUM1" -lt 5 || "$NUM2" -eq 5 ]]; then
    echo "Either NUM1 is less than 5 OR NUM2 is equal to 5."
fi

Numeric Comparison Operators

  • `-eq`: Equal to
  • `-ne`: Not equal to
  • `-gt`: Greater than
  • `-lt`: Less than
  • `-ge`: Greater than or equal to
  • `-le`: Less than or equal to

Advanced `[[ ... ]]` Construct

  • `[[ condition1 && condition2 ]]`: Logical AND. Both conditions must be true.
  • `[[ condition1 || condition2 ]]`: Logical OR. At least one condition must be true.
  • Offers better string matching and avoids some `[ ... ]` pitfalls.
Key Takeaway

Use `[[ ... ]]` for more robust and readable complex conditions, especially when combining multiple tests with `&&` or `||`.
