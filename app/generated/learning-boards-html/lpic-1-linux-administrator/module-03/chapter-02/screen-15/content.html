Key Concepts: Ownership & Special Permissions

Consolidate your knowledge of file ownership and advanced access controls.

User, Group, Others

The three distinct categories of entities (owner, associated group, and all other users) to which permissions apply.

`chown` & `chgrp`

Commands used to change the user owner (`chown`) and group owner (`chgrp`) of files and directories, often requiring `sudo`.

Special Permissions

SUID, SGID, and the Sticky Bit provide advanced control over execution and directory behavior, with significant security implications.

Summary: Effective Linux administration relies on a deep understanding of ownership, `chown`/`chgrp`, and the careful application of special permissions.
