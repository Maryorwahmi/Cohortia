Core Concept

The SDLC and Security Integration

Moving from 'Bolted On' to 'Security by Design'

Traditional Approach: 'Bolted On'

  • •Security considered at the end.
  • •Reactive patching of vulnerabilities.
  • •High cost of remediation for late discoveries.
  • •Security seen as a bottleneck.

Modern Approach: 'Security by Design'

  • •Security integrated from inception.
  • •Proactive vulnerability prevention.
  • •Reduced remediation costs and attack surface.
  • •Security as an enabler of resilience.

Why it Matters: The Cost of Delay

The later a security vulnerability is discovered in the SDLC, the exponentially more expensive and difficult it becomes to fix. Early detection prevents fundamental architectural flaws that can be nearly impossible to remediate post-deployment.

Key Takeaway

Embedding security into the SDLC from the start is crucial for building robust, resilient, and cost-effective software systems.
