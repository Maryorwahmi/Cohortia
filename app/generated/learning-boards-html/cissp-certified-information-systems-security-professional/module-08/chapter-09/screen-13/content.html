Safety Notes

Common Database Security Mistakes

Avoiding critical pitfalls in database protection

Key Areas of Concern

  • Default Passwords
  • Over-Privileged Accounts
  • Unencrypted Backups
  • Ignoring Logs
  • Publicly Accessible Databases
  • No Application-Level Input Validation
Key Takeaway

Proactively identify and remediate common database security mistakes to prevent easy exploitation and strengthen your defense.

Mistakes to Avoid

Default Passwords

Easily guessed or publicly known, providing immediate access to attackers.

Over-Privileged Accounts

Granting `DB_OWNER` or `root` to application accounts violates least privilege.

Unencrypted Backups

If stolen, all data is exposed without encryption.

Ignoring Logs

Missing suspicious activity that could indicate an ongoing attack.

Publicly Accessible Databases

Exposing database ports directly to the internet without firewalls/VPNs.

No Input Validation

Even with parameterized queries, lack of validation can lead to logic flaws or DoS.
