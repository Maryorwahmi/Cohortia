Security Governance

The framework of policies, standards, guidelines, and processes ensuring security objectives are met.

Policies & Standards

High-level mandatory statements (policies) and specific mandatory requirements (standards) that support them.

Guidelines & Procedures

Recommended best practices (guidelines) and detailed step-by-step instructions (procedures) for secure tasks.

Summary Takeaway

A well-defined hierarchy of security governance components—policies, standards, guidelines, and procedures—provides the essential structure for secure software development and compliance.
