The Foundation of Secure Software

Why Governance and Compliance Matter Beyond Code

What is Governance?

The framework of policies, standards, and processes that ensures security objectives are met. It provides strategic direction and oversight.

What is Compliance?

Adhering to laws, regulations, and industry standards (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS). It's about meeting external mandates.

Key Takeaway

Security governance and compliance form the essential, non-technical backbone for building and maintaining truly secure software, guiding all development efforts.

Common Mistake

Treating security as purely a technical problem, ignoring the critical role of organizational governance and legal compliance.
