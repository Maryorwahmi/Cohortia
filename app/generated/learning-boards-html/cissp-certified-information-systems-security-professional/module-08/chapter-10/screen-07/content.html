Navigating the Compliance Landscape

Integrating Legal Requirements into the SDLC

GDPR
HIPAA
PCI DSS
SOX
SDLC Integration Points
  • Requirements Phase: Translate mandates into security requirements.
  • Design Specifications: Incorporate into architectural designs.
  • Testing: Validate compliance during security testing.
  • Operations: Continuously monitor for adherence.
Common Mistake

Treating compliance as a 'check-box' exercise rather than embedding it into the secure development culture, leading to superficial security.

Key Takeaway

Embedding compliance into the SDLC from the start is crucial for building inherently secure software and avoiding costly remediation or legal penalties later.
