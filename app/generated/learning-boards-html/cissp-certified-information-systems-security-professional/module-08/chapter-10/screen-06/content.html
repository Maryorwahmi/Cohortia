Procedures and the Importance of Enforcement

What are Procedures?

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for performing specific tasks securely, ensuring consistency and compliance.

Enforcement Mechanisms
  • Regular security audits
  • Automated checks in CI/CD pipelines
  • Strong management support
  • Consequences for non-compliance
Key Takeaway

Procedures operationalize security, but their effectiveness hinges on consistent enforcement, backed by management, to ensure compliance and accountability.
