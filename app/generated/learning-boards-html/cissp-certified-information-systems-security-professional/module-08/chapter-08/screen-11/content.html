Open-Source Governance

Evaluating and Securing Open-Source Software

Establishing a structured approach for safe OSS adoption

Policy and Governance

Establish clear policies for OSS use, including approved licenses, security review requirements, and vetting criteria.

Internal Component Library

Create a curated repository of approved and pre-scanned open-source components to reduce risk from public registries.

OSS Due Diligence Process

Assess Maturity & Support
Review Security Track Record
Consider Code Review for Criticality
Key Takeaway

Securing OSS involves robust governance, internal curated libraries, and thorough due diligence to mitigate risks while leveraging its benefits.
