Understanding the Software Supply Chain

The interconnected ecosystem of modern software development

Definition: Software Supply Chain

The entire ecosystem involved in creating and delivering software, including third-party components, open-source libraries, build tools, and deployment infrastructure.

Why it Matters: Criticality

A compromise anywhere in this chain can have devastating consequences for the consuming application and organization, making security paramount.

Key Takeaway

Securing the software supply chain means protecting every component, from its origin to its deployment, against vulnerabilities and malicious interference.
