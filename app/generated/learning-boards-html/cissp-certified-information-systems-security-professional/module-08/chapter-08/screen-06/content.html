Identifying Supply Chain Risks: Part 2

Addressing license compliance, maintenance, and deceptive attacks

License Compliance Issues

Misusing open-source components with incompatible licenses can lead to legal and intellectual property challenges.

Lack of Maintenance

Abandoned or poorly maintained open-source projects may not receive security updates, leaving them perpetually vulnerable.

Typosquatting

Key Takeaway

Beyond direct vulnerabilities, legal, maintenance, and deceptive attacks like typosquatting and dependency confusion demand careful attention in supply chain security.
