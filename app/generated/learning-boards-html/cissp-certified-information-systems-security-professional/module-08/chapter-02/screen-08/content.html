Separation of Duties

Dividing critical tasks among multiple individuals or systems to prevent any single entity from performing a malicious or erroneous action without oversight.

Developer

Writes Code

Reviewer

Approves Changes

Deployer

Deploys to Production

Key Takeaway

Separating critical tasks prevents a single point of failure or malicious action, enhancing accountability and reducing the risk of fraud or error.
