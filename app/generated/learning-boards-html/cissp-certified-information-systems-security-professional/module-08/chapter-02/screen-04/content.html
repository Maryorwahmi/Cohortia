Foundations of Secure Design

Proactively shaping systems to resist attacks from their foundational elements is paramount. This involves embedding security principles from the earliest stages of development.

Core Idea

Security is not an add-on; it's an inherent quality built into the system's architecture and design from day one.

Why it Matters
  • Reduces vulnerabilities and attack surface.
  • Lowers the cost of fixing security issues.
  • Builds trust and ensures compliance.
  • Enhances system resilience and stability.
