Threat Modeling: Proactive Risk Identification

A structured approach to identifying potential threats, vulnerabilities, and countermeasures, best performed early in the SDLC.

Core Purpose

To proactively uncover security flaws and design weaknesses that could be exploited by attackers, enabling early and cost-effective mitigation.

Benefits
  • Identifies risks before implementation.
  • Reduces cost of fixing vulnerabilities.
  • Improves overall system security posture.
  • Fosters a security-aware development culture.
