Definition & Context

Protected Health Information

Health-related subset of PII that covers medical records, lab results, treatment notes, and payment information when handled by healthcare entities.

Who’s covered
Healthcare providers, health plans, and their business associates.
Why stronger controls
PHI exposure risks include discrimination, identity exposure, and regulatory penalties.
Practical
Example
An EHR record that includes a diagnosis and billing codes is PHI and needs covered safeguards and breach procedures.
Common mistake
Treating PHI as ordinary PII without updating contracts, breach response, and access policies.
Key takeaway
Identify PHI in inventory and apply the strictest applicable controls and notification rules.
