Module 2 › Chapter 2.6 › Key Concepts — Techniques
HIPAA Safeguards
Administrative, physical, and technical measures to protect PHI and meet regulatory obligations.
Anonymization
Irreversible removal of identifiers, suitable for public-release datasets when re-identification must be prevented.
Pseudonymization
Replace identifiers with pseudonyms to allow analysis; reversible only if the mapping key is protected.
Final takeaway

Document the chosen privacy technique, the rationale, and the controls that protect the mapping or original values so auditors and engineers share a single source of truth.
