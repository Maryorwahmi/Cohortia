Apply these skills

Four task-centered milestones you will perform during privacy reviews and design sessions.

01 Inventory
Label datasets as PII or PHI
Mnemonic: Tag early. Milestone: field-level classification in the data inventory.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Legal mapping
Map controls to GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA
Mnemonic: Rights first. Milestone: DPA or consent review completed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Controls
Design encryption and access rules
Mnemonic: Least privilege. Milestone: RBAC and DLP rules implemented.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Privacy techniques
Choose de-id, anonymize, or pseudonymize
Mnemonic: Utility vs risk. Milestone: Analytics dataset technique documented.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
