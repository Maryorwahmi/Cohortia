Module 2 › Chapter 2.6 › Regulatory Comparison
GDPR

Principles & Rights

  • Data minimization and privacy by design
  • Rights: access, rectification, erasure
  • Pseudonymization encouraged to reduce risk
HIPAA

Safeguards for PHI

  • Administrative, physical, technical safeguards
  • Breach notification and business associate agreements
  • Strong logging and access controls expected
CCPA

Consumer Rights

  • Right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale
  • Applies to consumer personal information processing
  • Impacts marketing, analytics, and consent flows
Example
GDPR erasure requires deletion processes; HIPAA requires breach notifications for PHI exposures.
Common misconception
Assuming a single law governs all data globally. Jurisdiction and data type determine obligations.
Key takeaway
Map each legal requirement to a design control and document how the control meets the rule.
