Module 2 › Chapter 2.6 › Administrative Controls
Privacy Policies
Document collection purposes, retention, sharing, and lawful bases for processing PII/PHI.
Training
Conduct regular training so employees follow handling procedures and know escalation paths for suspected exposure.
Contracts & DPAs
Require vendors to meet your security and notification obligations when they process PII/PHI on your behalf.
Scenario
Onboarding an analytics vendor: verify a DPA that includes encryption, retention limits, access controls, and breach notification timelines; ensure staff know the escalation path.
Key takeaway
Administrative controls operationalize technical measures and are required for legal compliance and audits.
