Task: Prepare analytics without exposing PII

PII identification
Customer name, email address, and payment card number are PII. Payment data must be tokenized or masked in analytics.
Payment cards
Technique: Tokenization (reversible via secure vault) or masking for display. Justification: preserves reconciliation while limiting exposure.
Names & emails
Technique: Pseudonymization to preserve longitudinal analysis without exposing identities, or anonymize if linkage isn't required.
Why this matters
Balancing analytics utility and regulatory risk prevents breaches and avoids fines or reputational harm.
Common mistake
Keeping raw PII in analytics for convenience increases breach impact; avoid it by design.
Deliverable
List of PII fields, chosen technique for each with justification, retention period, and access controls.
