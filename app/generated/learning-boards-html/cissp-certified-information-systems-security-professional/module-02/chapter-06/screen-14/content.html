Module 2 › Chapter 2.6 › Key Concepts
Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
Any information that can identify a person directly or indirectly.
Protected Health Information (PHI)
Health-related subset of PII requiring HIPAA-level safeguards and breach procedures.
GDPR
EU regulation emphasizing data minimization, rights, and privacy by design.
Summary

Use these concepts as a checklist: classify data, map legal obligations, and apply layered controls that preserve data utility while reducing risk.
