Module 2 › Chapter 2.6 › Privacy Techniques
De-identification
Remove direct identifiers. Useful as an initial step, but may leave indirect identifiers that enable re-identification unless further treated.
Anonymization
Irreversible removal or alteration of identifiers so individuals cannot be re-identified from released data; often required for public datasets.
Pseudonymization
Replace identifiers with pseudonyms and store the mapping separately; allows analysis while limiting exposure if the mapping is protected.
Example
Pseudonymize patient IDs for longitudinal research while keeping the mapping in a secure vault.
Pitfall
Assuming removal of a single field like name is sufficient for anonymization — linkage via indirect identifiers remains a risk.
Key takeaway
Match technique to need: anonymize for public release, pseudonymize for reversible analysis, and de-identify as an initial risk reduction step.
