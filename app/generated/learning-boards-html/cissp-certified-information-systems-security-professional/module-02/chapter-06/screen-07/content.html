Process

Privacy by Design & Data Minimization Checklist

Step 1 — Purpose specification
Define why each field is needed and the business purpose for processing.
Step 2 — Necessity test
Confirm that data is adequate and limited; remove optional fields that are not strictly required.
Step 3 — Retention & deletion
Assign retention schedules and implement deletion or erasure mechanisms where required.
Step 4 — Technical controls
Specify encryption, RBAC, logging, DLP, and anonymization needs in design docs.
Example
A signup form that only collects email for newsletters meets minimization by not requesting birth date.
Pitfall
Adding optional fields for analytics without a necessity check increases risk without justification.
Key takeaway
Document necessity and retention for each data element and enforce through architecture reviews.
