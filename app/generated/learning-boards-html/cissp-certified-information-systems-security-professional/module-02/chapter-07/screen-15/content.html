Structured Fields
ID, owner, OS/version, IP, criticality — the minimal set for actionable inventories.
Lifecycle Gates
Procurement checks, deployment baselines, ongoing maintenance, and secure disposal keep assets managed.
Validate Discovery
Discovered assets must be assigned owners and lifecycle states; otherwise they remain risky shadows.
Final quick check

If you cannot produce an owner-prioritized remediation list in minutes, focus on structuring three fields: owner, criticality, and OS/version.
