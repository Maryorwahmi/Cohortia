Module 3 › Chapter 2.7 › Shadow IT
What is shadow IT?
Systems, services, or software used without formal approval. They commonly appear as personal cloud accounts, unsanctioned SaaS, or unmanaged devices on the network.
Why it matters
These assets often lack hardening, monitoring, or patching, increasing the attack surface and data exposure risk.
Detection & response
  • Use discovery tools and cloud billing reports to find candidates.
  • Notify potential owners and require registration into the inventory.
  • Apply secure configuration or deprovision if unsupported.
Key takeaway
Treat discovery as the start of a governance workflow; validation and onboarding are required to mitigate shadow risks.
