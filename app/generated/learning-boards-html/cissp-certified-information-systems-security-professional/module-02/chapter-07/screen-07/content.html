CMDB concept
Store configuration items (CIs) and their relationships so you can trace dependencies between applications, servers, databases, and users. Use relationship queries to perform impact analysis and prioritize remediation.
Application CI
Server CI
Database CI
Practical tip
Model the most critical business services first, then expand. Validate relationships with owners frequently to prevent drift.
Example scenario
A CMDB query returns AppX → Server-5 → DB-Prod. When DB-Prod is found vulnerable, you can list AppX and its owner, enabling rapid communication and prioritization.
Pitfall
Building a large CMDB without validation. Unverified relationships lead to false impact assessments and wasted effort.
