Module 3 › Chapter 2.7 › Asset Scope
What to include
  • Hardware: servers, endpoints, printers, IoT.
  • Software: OS, apps, middleware, licenses.
  • Data: PII, backups, IP, classifications.
  • Network: IPs, DNS, certificates, domains.
  • Services: SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, third‑party APIs.
  • Facilities & personnel ties.
Example
Record: AppX — runs on VM-123, stores customer PII in Storage-45, owner: Finance, criticality: high.
