Asset Inventory and Management
Foundations, lifecycle controls, and CMDB relationships that make security measurable.
In this lesson

Learn what to include in an inventory, how lifecycle stages affect security, and how tools like CMDBs reveal service dependencies.

Why it matters

An accurate inventory lets you identify affected systems during a vulnerability notice and prioritize remediation by owner and criticality.
