Comparison › Disposal vs Destruction
Disposal

Makes data inaccessible through normal system operations. Often used when media will be reused.

  • Clearing / overwriting.
  • Secure erase commands.
  • Appropriate for many low- to medium-sensitivity cases.
Misconception: Formatting or deleting equals secure disposal.
Destruction

Renders data permanently unrecoverable, even to advanced recovery techniques. Often used for highly sensitive data or when media will be retired.

  • Degaussing (magnetic media), physical shredding, pulverizing.
  • Suitable when reuse is unnecessary or forbidden.
  • Often requires certificates of destruction for audit evidence.
Misconception: Degaussing works for SSDs. It does not, because SSDs are non-magnetic.
Example
Deleting files from a laptop often leaves recoverable data. For sensitive records use secure erase or destruction.
Check
Always require verification evidence after disposal or destruction.
Key Takeaway

Choose disposal for reuse and destruction for permanent retirement; never rely on simple delete.
