Timeline › Disposal Plan Steps
Minimal, auditable steps
  1. Inventory item with serial and sensitivity classification.
  2. Select authorized method based on media and sensitivity.
  3. Schedule disposal and assign responsible operator.
  4. Execute sanitization or destruction and capture verification artifacts.
  5. Record artifact and update inventory; retain certificate per policy.
Pre-check: confirm encryption and key status.
During: capture logs, photos, or vendor certificate.
Post: update inventory and retain artifacts for audits.
Key Takeaway

A short, auditable checklist ensures disposals are repeatable, verifiable, and defensible during audits.
