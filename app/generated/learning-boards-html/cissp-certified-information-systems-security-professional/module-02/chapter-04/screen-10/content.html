Backup tapes (magnetic) — PHI
Recommended method: degaussing or physical destruction (shredding). Both render magnetic domains unreadable. Use destruction when tapes will not be reused.
Verification: Obtain certificate of destruction and record tape IDs in disposal log.
Why not clearing? Clearing may be insufficient against laboratory recovery for magnetic backups containing PHI.
Executive laptops (SSDs)
Recommended method: use secure erase or cryptographic erase if encryption and key handling are verifiable. If uncertain, physical destruction is safest.
Why not degauss or simple overwrite? SSDs use wear leveling; degaussing is ineffective and overwriting may not reach all cells.
Employee instruction — Do NOT:
Never toss devices or drives in general waste, never assume delete or factory reset is sufficient, and never remove certification steps.
Evidence to collect
  • Destruction certificates
  • Sanitization logs
  • Operator and timestamp records
Key Takeaway

Match method to media and sensitivity; always verify and retain proof of the disposal action.
