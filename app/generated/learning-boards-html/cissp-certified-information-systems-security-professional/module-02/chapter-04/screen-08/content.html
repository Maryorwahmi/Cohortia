Definition › Physical Destruction
Methods for media
  • Shredding, pulverizing, incineration, melting for drives and optical media.
  • Tape shredding or crushing for magnetic backups.
  • Cross-cut shredding or pulping for paper documents.
Note: physical destruction often renders media unusable and should be chosen when reuse is not intended.
Mobile devices & paper
Factory resets on phones are frequently insufficient for high-sensitivity data. Use specialized wiping tools or physical destruction when needed.
Paper disposal: prefer cross-cut shredding or pulping; strip-cut is easier to reassemble and less secure.
Common mistake

Discarding media in regular trash leaves recoverable data; always follow documented disposal procedures.
