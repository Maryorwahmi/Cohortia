Process Flow › Verification & Audit
Verification architecture
Sanitization logs with command output and timestamps.
Operator identity and procedure steps recorded.
Certificates of destruction from vendors.
Photographic evidence or inventory reconciliation entries.
Create an audit cadence and keep verification artifacts for the period required by your retention policy. Cross-check incident investigations against disposal records to ensure no accidental data loss occurred during legitimate disposal events.
Checklist
  1. Method chosen and documented
  2. Operator and time recorded
  3. Verification artifact attached
  4. Certificate stored per policy
Key Takeaway

Verification makes disposal auditable: record action, operator, and proof to reduce compliance risk.
