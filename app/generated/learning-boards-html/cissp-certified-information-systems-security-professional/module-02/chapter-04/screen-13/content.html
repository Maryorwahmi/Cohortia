What to Remember › Key Concepts
Data Retention Policy
A formal document assigning retention windows by data category with legal and business rationale.
Data Disposal
Making data inaccessible through normal means, often with overwrites or secure erase for reuse.
Data Destruction
Rendering data permanently unrecoverable, for example by shredding or degaussing.
Summary

Choose the appropriate method, match it to the media, and capture verification evidence to support audits and incident response.
