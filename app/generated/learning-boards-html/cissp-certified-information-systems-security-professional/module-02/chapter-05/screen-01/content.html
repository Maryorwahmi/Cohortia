Information Handling Requirements
Translate data classification into enforceable rules for marking, storing, transmitting and processing.
This chapter focuses on the operational steps you can take to make classified data safe throughout its active lifecycle. Expect concrete examples, command-line snippets, and checklists you can adapt to policy.

Why it matters

Quick Start
Begin by ensuring a visible classification banner and a metadata tag for every sensitive document. Pair that with an encrypted, access-controlled location for storage and an approved encrypted method for transmission.
