Assessment: Choosing Safe Transmission
Use scenario questions to verify understanding of protocol suitability and control ownership.
Scenario
Send a Confidential report to a colleague in a different office. Company policy: confidential data in transit must be encrypted.
Options (evaluate)
  • Attach to company S/MIME-enabled email
  • Upload to company-approved end-to-end encrypted file service
  • Attach to a public webmail message without extra encryption
  • Transfer over SFTP via VPN
Correct reasoning
Reject public webmail because it lacks guaranteed end-to-end encryption and places data outside organizational control. Prefer company-managed encrypted channels with logging.
Key Takeaway

Design assessments that require learners to justify choices with protocol guarantees and ownership of storage and keys.
