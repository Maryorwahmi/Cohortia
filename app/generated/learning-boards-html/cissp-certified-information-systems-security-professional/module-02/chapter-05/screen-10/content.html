Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Least Privilege & Minimization
Definition
Least privilege and data minimization
Grant minimal access and collect only the data necessary for a defined purpose to reduce exposure.
Example: service account
A notification service gets a role allowing only send-email permission, not access to billing or PII fields.
Data minimization checklist
  • Define required fields for each process
  • Restrict storage to those fields
  • Schedule deletion or anonymization when retention ends
Common mistake
Giving broad roles for convenience or collecting extra fields “just in case” increases risk and compliance burden.
Operational guidance
Define roles with minimal required permissions, document them, and audit role assignments regularly. Enforce form validation on collection points to restrict fields.
Key Takeaway

Least privilege plus minimization reduce attack surface and make compliance practical.
