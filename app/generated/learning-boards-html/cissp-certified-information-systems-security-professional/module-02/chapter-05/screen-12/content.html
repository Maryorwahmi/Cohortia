Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Training, Audits & Monitoring
Process Flow
Train → Monitor → Audit → Improve
Education reduces errors; monitoring and audits detect gaps; feedback closes the loop and improves controls.
Training focus
Show examples of proper marking, explain approved transfer methods, and give steps for secure storage and processing.
Monitoring focus
Prioritize alerts on external transfers, privilege escalations, and missing metadata on classified files.
Audit focus
Sample files for labels and storage, review access logs, and confirm retention/deletion policies are followed.
Concrete scenario
After training, run a weekly DLP report for files labeled Confidential and verify they live in approved locations and transfers are logged.
Key Takeaway

Training must be paired with monitoring and audits to create reliable, long-term compliance.
