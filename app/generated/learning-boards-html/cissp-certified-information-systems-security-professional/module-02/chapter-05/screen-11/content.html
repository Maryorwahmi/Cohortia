Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Privacy by Design
Architecture
Make privacy a requirement, not an afterthought
Identify data flows and enforce minimization, separation, and access controls during design and development.
Design checklist
  • Define required attributes and their retention periods.
  • Isolate sensitive fields into encrypted stores.
  • Model threats and include privacy acceptance criteria.
Concrete example
Design a CRM so the marketing module stores only emails, while billing stores payment details in a separate encrypted service with stricter access controls.
Common misconception
Thinking privacy by design is only legal compliance. It also reduces technical risk and simplifies future audits.
Key Takeaway

Integrating privacy early reduces costly retrofits and lowers overall exposure.
