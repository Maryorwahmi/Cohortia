Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What counts as an asset
Definition

An asset is anything with value to the organization. That includes physical hardware, software, data sets, intellectual property, and reputation.

Examples
  • Server hosting customer database (physical + information asset)
  • Unreleased product designs (intellectual property)
  • Marketing materials after publication (public asset)
Common Mistake

Limiting the asset inventory to only hardware. This overlooks spreadsheets, IP, and reputation, which can cause large business impact when lost or leaked.

Key Takeaway
Record tangible and intangible assets, assign an owner, and evaluate impact so classification labels can be applied correctly.
