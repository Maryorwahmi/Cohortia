Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Data sensitivity and control mapping
Sensitivity → Controls
Public: basic integrity checks and routine availability protections.
Internal Use Only: internal ACLs, employee authentication, and logging.
Confidential: encryption at rest and in transit, strict ACLs, audit trails.
Restricted: enhanced authentication, strict need-to-know, DLP and close monitoring.
Concrete example
A customer PII dataset labeled Confidential: encrypt the database, restrict access to HR and legal teams, enable audit logging for all exports and transfers.
