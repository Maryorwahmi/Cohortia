# SecureCorp Information Classification Policy - Confidential Data

## 1. Definition of Confidential Data
Confidential data at SecureCorp is defined as: customer personally identifiable information, unreleased product designs, financial forecasts, and other information that could cause material financial, legal, or reputational harm if disclosed.

## 2. Handling Requirements for Confidential Data
* Encryption at rest and in transit
* Strict access control based on need-to-know and least privilege
* Secure disposal methods for physical and digital copies

## 3. Responsibility for Classification
The responsibility for classifying data as Confidential rests with the Data Owner, typically a Business Unit Head, in consultation with IT Security.
How to adapt
Replace examples with organization-specific assets, add regulatory-specific controls if required, and name the approval authority for exceptions.
Pro-tip
Keep policy concise and give data owners a checklist to apply during classification decisions.
