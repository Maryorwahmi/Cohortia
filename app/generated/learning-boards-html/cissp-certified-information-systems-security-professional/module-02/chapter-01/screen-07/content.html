Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Role of the Data Owner
Who is the owner?

A business leader or manager who understands the asset’s value, legal constraints, and operational use, and who can judge sensitivity.

Owner responsibilities
  • Assign classification
  • Define handling requirements
  • Coordinate reviews
Security team role
Provide policy, guidance, enforcement tools, and technical controls such as encryption and DLP.
Scenario
A product manager identifies unreleased designs as Confidential and directs IT to enable encryption and restrict sharing to the product team.
Key Takeaway

Document and train owners so classification decisions are consistent and enforceable.
