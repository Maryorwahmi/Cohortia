Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Classification lifecycle
Lifecycle steps
1
Identify
Inventory assets and gather context about format, location, and use.
2
Assign owner
Designate a data owner who understands business impact and can decide classification.
3
Assess & label
Evaluate confidentiality, integrity, and availability impact and select a label.
4
Enforce & review
Apply controls and schedule reviews when business context changes.
Example scenario
A file of salary forecasts is identified, a business manager is named owner, it is labeled Confidential after impact assessment, encryption and ACLs are applied, and a review is scheduled annually or after major organizational changes.
Key Takeaway

Make classification a repeatable process with owners, criteria, and scheduled reviews to prevent stale labels.
