Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Classifying physical assets
Physical controls mapped to labels
  • High Impact / Confidential servers: locked rooms, badge access, CCTV, redundancy.
  • Internal equipment: standard office access, device encryption.
  • Public systems: basic availability and integrity protections.
Example
A production database server labeled High Impact is placed in a locked data center, has environmental sensors, redundant power, and limited physical access by badge and escort.
Key Takeaway
Extend classification to physical assets and ensure contractual or cloud provider arrangements match the required physical protections.
