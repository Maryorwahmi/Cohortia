Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Over- vs Under-classification
Over-classification
Too many assets labeled Confidential causes friction, extra cost, and leads users to find unsafe workarounds.
Symptoms
  • User workarounds and shadow IT
  • High operational cost for routine tasks
Under-classification
Critical assets labeled too low allow exposure to unauthorized parties and regulatory violations.
Symptoms
  • Unrestricted sharing of sensitive documents
  • Missing encryption or ACLs where needed
Mitigation
Use impact-based criteria, train owners with examples, and schedule reviews to maintain correct classification levels.
