Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Conflicts & Gaps
Problem

Conflicts of interest and missing ownership

Overlaps and omissions make it unclear who decides, who implements, and who verifies controls.

Examples
  • No named owner for a dataset — nobody authorizes classification.
  • Vendor builds and audits its own controls — no independent verification.
Mitigations
  • Name a single owner per dataset and record retained responsibilities.
  • Use independent audits and separation of duties where verification is required.
  • Run periodic role reviews during procurement and change windows.
Red flag checklist
  • Is an owner explicitly named?
  • Are delegated tasks documented with retained accountability?
  • Is independent verification possible?
Key Takeaway

Resolve overlaps and omissions by naming owners, recording delegation, and enforcing independent checks.
