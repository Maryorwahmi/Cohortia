Question 1

Who is Owner and who is Custodian?

Scenario: CFO signs financial policy; IT implements encryption and access controls for the finance database. Choose roles and explain why.

Correct answer
CFO → Data Owner (accepts risk, sets classification)
IT security team → Data Custodian (implements encryption and access controls)
Question 2

Controller and Processor in cloud hosting

Scenario: Your organization collects CRM data and chooses processing purposes. A cloud vendor hosts the CRM. Who is Controller and who is Processor?

Answer

Your organization is the Controller; the cloud host is the Processor acting on instruction. Request a DPA and audit reports to verify.

Grading rubric
  • Correct label = 1 point.
  • One-line justification = 1 point.
  • Evidence to request = 1 point.
Key Takeaway

Assessments should test role naming plus the practical evidence you would request to verify each role assignment.
