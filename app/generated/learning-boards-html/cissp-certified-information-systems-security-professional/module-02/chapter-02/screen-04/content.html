Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Data Owner — Accountability
Definition

Data Owner

The individual who has ultimate responsibility for data classification, use, and protection decisions.

Concrete responsibilities
  • Set classification and retention policy based on business needs.
  • Approve access requests and exception risk acceptance.
  • Ensure controls meet regulatory requirements.
Common mistake

Delegating accountability to operational teams. Tasks can be delegated, but the owner retains responsibility.

Example scenario

HR head classifies employee records as confidential, approves access for payroll and recruiters, and accepts retention policies tied to regulation.

Why this matters
Without an owner the organization lacks a decision-maker to balance business needs and legal risk.
Key takeaway
Ownership drives policy. Document owner decisions and exceptions for audits.
