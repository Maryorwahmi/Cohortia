Data Roles and Responsibilities

Who is accountable, who implements controls, and who uses the data.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Orientation

This chapter distinguishes Data Owner, Data Custodian, Data Controller, Data Processor, and Data User. Each role helps preserve confidentiality, integrity, and availability in a different way.

Why this matters

Ambiguous roles dilute accountability, create compliance gaps, and increase breach risk. Clear assignment means decisions and controls align with business risk and regulations.
