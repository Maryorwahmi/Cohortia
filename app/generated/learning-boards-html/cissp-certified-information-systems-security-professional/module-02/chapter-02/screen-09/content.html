Architecture

Controller ↔ Processor / Custodian contracts

Contracts translate responsibilities into measurable obligations and give the Controller audit and enforcement rights.

Technical terms
Encryption, backups, patch timelines, access controls.
Operational terms
Incident notification, audit rights, SLAs.
Example clause

The Processor will notify the Controller of any breach within 72 hours and allow security assessments annually. Encryption must meet the Controller’s documented standard.

Due diligence checklist
  • Verify encryption and access controls.
  • Confirm backup and restore practices.
  • Confirm incident notification and audit rights.
Key Takeaway

Make contract obligations specific, measurable, and verifiable through reports and audits.
