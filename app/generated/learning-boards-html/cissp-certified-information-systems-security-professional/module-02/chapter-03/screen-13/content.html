Assessment idea
Sample questions and reasoning
Practice the method of mapping states to controls and explaining why alternatives fail.
Question 1
Which state does TDE primarily protect? Explain why other states are not covered.
Question 2
You observe HTTP sending credentials internally. Which state is at risk and what immediate action do you recommend?
Answer pattern
  1. Identify the state at risk.
  2. Recommend a matching control.
  3. Explain residual risks and follow-up steps.
Exam tip
Always justify why a chosen control addresses the stated risk and state, and briefly note what it does not cover.
Key Takeaway

Answer exam-style questions by mapping states to controls and calling out limitations explicitly.
