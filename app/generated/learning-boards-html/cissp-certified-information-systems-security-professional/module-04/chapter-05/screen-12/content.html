Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Vulnerabilities & Mitigations
Common vulnerabilities
  • Weak passwords and missing MFA.
  • Exposed default RDP/SSH ports and open gateways.
  • Unpatched VPN concentrators and remote services.
  • Split-tunnel traffic bypassing corporate inspection.
  • Insufficient logging and monitoring of remote sessions.
Mitigation strategies
Enforce MFA and strong password policies.
Restrict remote desktop access to jump boxes or management networks.
Patch VPN and remote desktop gateways promptly and follow vendor advisories.
Enable logging and schedule regular reviews to detect suspicious activity early.
Key Takeaway

Layer technical controls and monitoring to prevent breaches and detect attempts early when they occur.
