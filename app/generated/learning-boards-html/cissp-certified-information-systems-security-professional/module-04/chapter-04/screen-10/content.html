Process Flow
Wireless Site Surveys
Measure, map, and mitigate to align coverage with security and performance requirements.
Steps
  1. Plan areas and measurement points.
  2. Collect RSSI, SNR, channel usage, and interference data.
  3. Create heatmaps and identify leakage or dead zones.
  4. Adjust placement, power, and antennas; retest.
Schedule surveys after major layout or device changes and combine with automated WIDS for continuous assurance.
Concrete example
A finance office shows weak coverage near secure vaults. Site survey identifies channel overlap from adjacent APs. Solution: reposition APs, lower power, and use directional antennas to focus coverage and reduce signal outside secure zones.
Measurement tools
Wi‑Fi analyzers, heatmapping software, and spectrum analyzers for RF interference.
Policy outcomes
Placement and power settings that align coverage with security zones and compliance needs.
Key Takeaway

Site surveys are practical exercises that reduce exposure and improve performance by aligning radio coverage with security policy.
