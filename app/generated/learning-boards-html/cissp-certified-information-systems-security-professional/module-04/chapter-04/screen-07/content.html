Definition
EAP‑TLS vs PEAP
Certificate-based versus tunneled password authentication: trade security for manageability.
EAP‑TLS
Client and server certificates provide strong mutual authentication; ideal when you can manage certificates.
PEAP
Server-side TLS tunnel protects password authentication; lower certificate management overhead for clients.
Decision tip
Choose EAP‑TLS for highest assurance if you can operate a PKI. Choose PEAP to reduce client certificate complexity while still encrypting credentials in transit.
Concrete scenario
A university with device management can issue certificates to managed laptops and prefers EAP‑TLS. A small office with no PKI may use PEAP with strong password policies and 2FA for management portals.
Misconception
PEAP is not inherently insecure. Proper server cert validation and password hygiene are required to maintain protection.
Key Takeaway

Match the EAP method to your capacity: EAP‑TLS for strong assurance, PEAP for easier deployment with adequate protections.
