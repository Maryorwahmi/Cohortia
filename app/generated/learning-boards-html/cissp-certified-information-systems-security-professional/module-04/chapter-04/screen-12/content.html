NFC Security Considerations
Short range reduces risk but does not remove relay and tag threats.
Benefits of proximity
Limited range makes casual eavesdropping harder, suitable for payments and close interactions.
Residual risks
Relay attacks, malicious tags, and data manipulation remain concerns that require safeguards.
Operational controls
  • Require explicit user confirmation for NFC transactions.
  • Use secure elements or tokenization for payment data.
  • Validate and sanitize data read from NFC tags.
Key Takeaway

Treat NFC as lower risk due to proximity but apply the same security hygiene: confirmation, tokenization, and firmware updates.
