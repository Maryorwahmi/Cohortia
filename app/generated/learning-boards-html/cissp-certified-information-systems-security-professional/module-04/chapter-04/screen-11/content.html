Core Concept
Bluetooth Security Basics
Short-range convenience requires pairing hygiene and firmware maintenance.
Best practices
  • Disable discoverability when not pairing.
  • Use strong pairing codes and confirm device identifiers.
  • Keep firmware updated and manage devices centrally when possible.
Threat example
A keyboard with weak pairing can be impersonated and used to inject keystrokes on a paired host.
Misconception
Short range does not equal safety. Relay attacks and nearby attackers can still exploit weak pairings or outdated firmware.
Operational controls
  • Use device management to enforce pairing policies.
  • Disable Bluetooth on devices that do not need it.
  • Educate users to reject unexpected pairing prompts.
Key Takeaway

Apply pairing hygiene and firmware maintenance; treat Bluetooth as an attack surface requiring controls and user education.
