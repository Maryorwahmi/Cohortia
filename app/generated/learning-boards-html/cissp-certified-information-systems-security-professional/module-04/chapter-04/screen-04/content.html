Core Concept
Why wireless is different
Radio signals can be received beyond physical boundaries, so wireless demands stronger operational and cryptographic controls.
Main points
  • Signals propagate past walls, enabling remote eavesdropping.
  • Open networks without per‑client encryption expose passive listeners.
  • Obscurity controls like hidden SSIDs or MAC filtering are insufficient.
Key Takeaway

Treat wireless as public medium: use cryptography, authentication, and monitoring rather than relying on secrecy.

Analogy
Think of Wi‑Fi like a loudspeaker rather than a wired phone line. Sound travels; anyone nearby can listen unless you encrypt the message.
Example: Open cafe Wi‑Fi
A user connects to an open hotspot. Without Enhanced Open or VPN, nearby attackers can passively capture session data.
Common mistake
Relying on MAC filtering or hiding SSIDs; these are easily bypassed and give false confidence.
