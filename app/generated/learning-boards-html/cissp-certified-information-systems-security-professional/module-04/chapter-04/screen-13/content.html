Hands-on Activity
Design checklist
  • Identify supplicants, APs (RADIUS clients), and RADIUS servers.
  • Choose EAP method: EAP‑TLS for certificates or PEAP for tunneled passwords.
  • Plan logging, redundancy, and certificate lifecycle management.
  • Document test plan and failover verification steps.
Deliverable: a short diagram or list describing the authentication flow, chosen EAP method, and operational controls for rollout.
Example checklist entry
- APs configured as RADIUS clients with server IPs and shared secret
- RADIUS server integrated with Active Directory for credential checks
- EAP-TLS selected for managed devices; PEAP for BYOD with strong password policy
- RADIUS logging enabled and central log collection configured
- RADIUS pair configured in active/standby for redundancy
Key Takeaway

A successful WPA3‑Enterprise deployment balances technical choices with operational processes like certificate and account lifecycle management.
