Core Concept
Rogue APs and Evil Twins
Unauthorized radios can create backdoors or be used to phish credentials via fake SSIDs.
Detection
Combine WIDS with regular physical site surveys and heatmapping to locate unauthorized APs.
Mitigation
Physically secure ports, implement 802.1X, and enforce port security on switches to prevent AP connection.
Don’t assume discovery is one‑time. Make detection continuous and pair automated alerts with scheduled physical sweeps.
Scenario
An employee plugs a consumer AP into a conference room port. The AP bridges the wired network and broadcasts the company SSID, exposing internal services to attackers who locate the device.
Immediate response
Locate and physically disconnect the AP, capture MAC and signal data, and review RADIUS logs for recent authentications.
Preventive controls
Port security, network access control, and employee training to avoid unauthorized installations.
Common Mistake

Treating rogue AP discovery as a one-off event. Without continuous monitoring, new rogue devices can appear anytime.
