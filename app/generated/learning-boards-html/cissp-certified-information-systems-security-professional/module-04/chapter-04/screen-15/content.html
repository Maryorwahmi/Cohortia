Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › What to Remember
Use WPA3
SAE for handshake security; enable PMF and use Enhanced Open for public spaces.
Implement 802.1X
Per-user authentication with EAP and RADIUS prevents unauthorized network joins.
Monitor & Survey
Use WIDS and recurring site surveys to detect rogue APs and tune coverage for security.
One-line summary
Combine modern protocols, identity-based access, and active monitoring to maintain a defensible wireless posture.
