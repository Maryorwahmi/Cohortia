Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Design Flaws & Fixes
Frequent mistakes
  • Flat network with no segmentation.
  • Management on the same VLAN as users.
  • Default credentials and Telnet enabled.
  • Overly permissive ACLs and missing logs.
Preventative measures
  • Enforce segmentation and deny-by-default policies.
  • Isolate management and use SSH plus restricted access lists.
  • Patch public servers and monitor DMZ traffic.
Actionable next step

Start with an inventory and map high‑risk systems. Prioritize segmentation and management isolation for immediate risk reduction.
