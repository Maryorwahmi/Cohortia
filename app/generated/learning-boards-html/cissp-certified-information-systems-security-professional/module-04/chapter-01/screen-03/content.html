Hands-on activity overview
SecureWidgets Task
Draw the zones and assign subnets, place external and internal firewalls, and restrict traffic flows.
Configuration Checklist
  • Use SSH for management and isolate management network.
  • Disable unused protocols and close unused ports.
  • Change default credentials and enable strong authentication.
Common Pitfalls
Flat networks, open DMZ rules, management on the same VLAN, and missing device patches.
