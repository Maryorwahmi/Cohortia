Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Management Security
Principles
  • Isolate management traffic on a separate subnet or VLAN.
  • Use SSH and encrypted management protocols only.
  • Restrict access to specific management stations and IPs.
SecureWidgets example

Management network: 10.0.5.0/24. Allow SSH and SNMP only from the management workstation at a designated IP. Disable Telnet and restrict SNMP to read‑only where possible.

Key takeaway

Treat management networks as highly confidential zones; their compromise often means control of the entire network.
