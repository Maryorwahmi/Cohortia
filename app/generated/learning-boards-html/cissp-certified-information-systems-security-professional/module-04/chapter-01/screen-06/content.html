Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › VLANs vs Physical
VLANs (Logical)
  • Flexible and cost effective.
  • Requires proper switch configuration and tagging.
  • Vulnerable to VLAN hopping when misconfigured.
Use case

Guest Wi‑Fi, office workstations, and departmental separation where cost and flexibility matter.

Physical Segmentation
  • Stronger isolation; not dependent on tagging or switch config.
  • Higher cost and less flexible to change.
  • Preferred for highest sensitivity systems or regulatory needs.
Mistake to avoid

Relying on VLANs as the only protection without enforcing firewall policies and monitoring between VLANs.
