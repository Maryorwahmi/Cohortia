Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Honeypots & Threat Intel
Process Flow
Feed, validate, enforce
  1. Deploy low-interaction honeypots to capture probes and basic exploits.
  2. Analyze collected indicators to validate maliciousness and extract IPs, domains, and payloads.
  3. Feed validated indicators into firewalls, IDS/IPS, and the SIEM for automated blocking and alerts.
Operational guidance
Isolate honeypots from production to prevent attacker pivoting.
Validate indicators before automated blocking to reduce false positives.
Document findings and update playbooks based on new tactics observed.
Key Takeaway

Honeypots and threat intelligence increase detection speed and provide practical indicators to harden defenses when validated and carefully used.

Analyst tip
Capture packet samples and netflow when honeypot alerts trigger so you can create precise signatures or rules.
