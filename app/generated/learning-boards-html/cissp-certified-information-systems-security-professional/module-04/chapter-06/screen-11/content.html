Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › IP Spoofing
Definition
IP spoofing
Forged source IPs conceal origin or impersonate hosts for reflection or bypass attacks.
Mitigation: ingress filtering to block outgoing packets with invalid internal sources; egress filtering to drop incoming packets claiming internal addresses.
Operational: ask upstream providers to implement similar checks to prevent spoofing across networks.
Controls and logs
Configure router ACLs to drop packets whose source IP does not match your assigned address space. Monitor logs of filtered packets to detect internal compromise or misconfiguration.
Key Takeaway

Both ingress and egress filtering are required to prevent spoofing and stop your network from participating in reflection attacks.
