Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Packet Sniffing
Architecture
Switched vs hub networks
Switches forward per-MAC, hubs broadcast; switched networks reduce passive sniffing but not active redirection.
Active techniques: ARP spoofing or misused SPAN ports can provide attackers with traffic copies.
Defenses: port security, disable unused ports, protect management interfaces, and enforce physical security for switches.
Encryption: TLS/SSH/IPsec ensures captured packets do not reveal sensitive content.
Incident checklist
Verify switch SPAN configuration and look for unauthorized mirrors.
Check for frequent MAC changes on gateway IPs and review ARP tables for anomalies.
Confirm encryption is used for credentials and sensitive services to mitigate impact of any capture.
Key Takeaway

Switching reduces casual sniffing, but active redirection and misconfigurations still allow captures—protect both the network and data-in-flight.
