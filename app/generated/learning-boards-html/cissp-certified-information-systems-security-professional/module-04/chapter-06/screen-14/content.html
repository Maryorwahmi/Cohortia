Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Key Concepts
Classify attacks
Identify whether an incident is volumetric, protocol-based, or application-targeted to choose the right controls.
Validate identity
Use ARP inspection, static mappings, DNSSEC, and strong crypto to protect routing and data integrity.
Proactive intelligence
Honeypots and threat feeds produce indicators that strengthen firewalls, IDS/IPS, and SIEM detection.
Takeaway: Combine classification, validation, and intelligence to reduce both attack surface and impact while preserving legitimate access.
