Applied verification tasks
Practice and evidence you can show.
Packet capture analysis
Use an ARP filter, look for duplicate IP-to-MAC claims, and check for unsolicited ARP replies. Document three indicators before escalating.
Switch hardening
Enable port security and dynamic ARP inspection. Verify spoofed MAC addresses trigger port shut or alerts.
Threat feed integration
Ingest malicious IP lists into firewall rules and confirm blocked traffic is logged in the SIEM.
Verification checklist
  • Confirm encrypted channels fail graceful downgrade attempts (no TLS strip success).
  • Show ingress filtering rules dropping packets with spoofed sources.
  • Provide a packet capture snippet with ARP anomalies annotated.
