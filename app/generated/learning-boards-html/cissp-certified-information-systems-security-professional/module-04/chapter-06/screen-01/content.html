Preventing and Responding to Network Attacks
Identify attack types, apply targeted mitigations, and use proactive intelligence to reduce risk.
In this lesson
You’ll learn to distinguish DoS and DDoS attacks, detect MITM techniques like ARP and DNS spoofing, and implement defenses including rate limiting, encryption, ARP inspection, and threat intelligence integration.
Why it matters
Availability and trust failures cause service outages, data interception, and reputational harm. Layered mitigations reduce attack surface and improve detection speed.
Explore Key Topics
DoS & DDoS
Volume, protocol, and application-layer attacks that target availability; mitigation requires filtering and distribution.
MITM & Spoofing
ARP and DNS manipulation let attackers intercept or redirect traffic; authenticated encryption and network validation are primary defenses.
Honeypots & Intel
Decoys and threat feeds help detect new tactics and feed protections into firewalls, IDS/IPS, and SIEMs.
