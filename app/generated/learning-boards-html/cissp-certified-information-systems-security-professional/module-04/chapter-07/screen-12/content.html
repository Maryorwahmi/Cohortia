VPC Flow Logs

Enable Flow Logs to capture metadata for flows. Forward records to a SIEM for retention, searches, and alerting so misconfigurations surface quickly.

What to monitor
Unexpected open ports, ingresses from unknown CIDRs, spikes in outbound connections, and traffic to suspicious external IPs.
Action
Create alerts for policy violations and integrate logs into incident response playbooks.
Protect the management plane
Hygiene
Use role-based access, MFA, least privilege, and audit logs. A breached admin account can change network controls and bypass defenses.
Common mistake
Not integrating flow logs into monitoring or not alerting on management-plane changes leads to late detection of critical changes.
Key Takeaway

Logs and management-plane controls are as important as network rules; secure both and connect logs to alerts.
