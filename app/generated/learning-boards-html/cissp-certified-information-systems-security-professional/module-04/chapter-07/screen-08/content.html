Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › Hybrid Connectivity
Connectivity options
VPN (encrypted tunnel)
Cost-effective, uses internet, good for lower bandwidth and flexible connectivity.
Direct Connect / ExpressRoute
Dedicated private link with predictable performance and stronger privacy; higher cost.
Design checklist
  1. Pick transport based on latency and cost.
  2. Plan non-overlapping IP ranges.
  3. Configure routing and encryption; validate with tests.
Concrete example
Scenario
A finance team requires low-latency, private access to cloud databases; choose Direct Connect and plan BGP routing with unique CIDRs to avoid overlap.
Misconception
Assuming a VPN automatically provides predictable latency. VPN depends on internet conditions and is not a substitute for a dedicated link when performance is critical.
Key Takeaway

Match connectivity choice to the workload: encryption and routing matter for both VPN and dedicated connections.
