Module 4 › Chapter 4.7 › Common Misconfigurations
Top misconfigurations
  • Overly permissive Security Group or NACL rules (0.0.0.0/0 on sensitive ports).
  • Leaving default VPC or route table settings unchanged.
  • Flat VPC with no internal segmentation between tiers.
  • Not enabling VPC Flow Logs or integrating them with monitoring.
  • Poor protection of the cloud management plane and credentials.
Prevention patterns
Automate
Use templates and IaC to enforce baseline SG and NACL rules.
Audit
Run periodic checks for open ports and unused rules.
Check your environment
List the most permissive inbound rules and prioritize remediation by risk and exposure.
Quick action

Start by removing wildcard access to SSH or database ports and replace them with admin CIDRs or Security Group references.
