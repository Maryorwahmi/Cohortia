Question 1
Which control is stateless and applies to an entire subnet, processing rules in order?
Reasoning
Stateless and subnet-level corresponds to a NACL. Remember to require explicit outbound rules for return traffic.
Question 2
Which connectivity suits a highly secure, low-latency path bypassing the public internet?
Reasoning
Dedicated private connections such as Direct Connect or ExpressRoute provide consistent bandwidth and privacy compared to VPN over the internet.
How to answer
  1. Identify required property: scope, statefulness, or transport.
  2. Map that property to the control's characteristics from earlier lessons.
  3. Pick the control that meets the requirement and explain why alternatives fail.
