Module 5 › Chapter 4.8 › Key Concepts
Network Monitoring
Continuous observation of activity to detect anomalies and performance issues.
Logging
Systematic recording of events; centralize logs for correlation and retention.
SIEM
Aggregates, normalizes, and correlates logs to surface alerts and provide forensic storage.
Summary Takeaway
Combine continuous monitoring, centralized logging, and a tuned SIEM to detect incidents early and produce the evidence needed for response and forensics.
