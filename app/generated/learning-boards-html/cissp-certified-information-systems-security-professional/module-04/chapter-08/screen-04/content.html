Why continuous monitoring and logging matters
Logs are the primary record of network events and the starting point for investigation.

Collect logs from all critical devices: firewalls, routers, switches, IDS/IPS, VPN gateways, and servers. These logs capture connection attempts, authentication events, and policy violations.

Centralize logs to a secure collector or SIEM so you can search and correlate events across devices and over time. Local-only logs are fragile and often lost after a device reset.

Avoid log sprawl by defining what to retain, how long to keep it, and how to protect sensitive entries. Balance verbosity with storage and privacy concerns.

Example: Compromised account
Multiple failed VPN logins followed by a successful login from a new region. Centralized logs let you correlate the attempts, the successful login, and subsequent access to sensitive hosts.
Common mistake
Relying on local device logs only. If a device is replaced or logs rotate, crucial evidence can be lost.
Key Takeaway

Centralized, persistent logs give your SOC the baseline needed for detection and forensic timelines.
