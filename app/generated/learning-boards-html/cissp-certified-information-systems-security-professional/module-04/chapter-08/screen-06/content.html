NetFlow/IPFIX: flow metadata
Efficient records that summarize conversations without storing payloads.

Flow records typically include source/destination IPs, ports, protocol, byte and packet counts, and timestamps. They give a high-level view of who talked to whom and when.

Use flow data to detect anomalies like large transfers, unexpected external connections, or unusual protocols for a host.

Flows are compact and suitable for long retention, making them ideal for capacity planning and building timelines in forensic investigations.

Collector integration
Routers export NetFlow or IPFIX to a collector. The collector aggregates flows, performs enrichment, and can forward summaries to a SIEM for correlation with logs.
Example use
During an incident you might see a spike of bytes from one host to an external IP. Flow records identify the conversation and timeframe so you can target a packet capture if you need payloads.
Key Takeaway

NetFlow/IPFIX gives long-term, high-level visibility; use it to prioritize where to take costly packet captures.
