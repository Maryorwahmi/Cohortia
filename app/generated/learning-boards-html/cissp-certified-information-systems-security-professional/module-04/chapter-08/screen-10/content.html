Packet capture: when and how to capture safely
Use packet capture for high-fidelity analysis, but manage privacy and storage carefully.

Packet captures reveal payload content and protocol-level details necessary to reconstruct attacks and verify malicious payloads.

Prefer targeted captures: narrow the timeframe and hosts to reduce volume and privacy exposure. Use taps or span ports to avoid disrupting production traffic.

Protect capture files: encrypt at rest, limit access, and document chain-of-custody for investigations.

Example
Flow data shows a large transfer from host A to an external address. A short packet capture during that window can reveal file transfer protocol and payload signatures to confirm exfiltration.
Privacy warning
Packet captures may contain passwords and PII; limit access and enforce retention policies to reduce legal risk.
Key Takeaway

Use packet capture only when logs and flows point to a specific time and host; protect and document captures to preserve forensic integrity.
