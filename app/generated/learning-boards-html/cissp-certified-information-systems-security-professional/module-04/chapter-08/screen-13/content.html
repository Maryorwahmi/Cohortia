NetFlow / IPFIX
Use flow records to find anomalies and build timelines without storing full packets continuously.
Packet Capture
Capture selectively, protect files, and document handling for forensic and privacy requirements.
Network Forensics
Prepare and rehearse the IR lifecycle using real logs and flow timelines to ensure readiness under pressure.
Final Takeaway
Telemetry must be complete, accessible, and practiced. Use logs for context, flows for detection, and packets for confirmation; train your team to use them together.
