Learning Objectives
Master four practical skills that let you monitor, analyze, and investigate network events.
01 — Implement monitoring & logging
Enable device logging and centralize events so you never rely on local device storage alone. This prevents evidence loss after an incident.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Understand SIEM
Use aggregation, normalization, and correlation to turn noisy logs into actionable alerts; learn how poor tuning leads to blind spots or alert fatigue.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Utilize NetFlow/IPFIX
Collect flow metadata to identify large transfers, unusual destinations, and to build conversation timelines without storing full packets.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Perform packet capture & forensics
Use targeted packet capture for deep analysis and evidence collection while protecting sensitive payloads and managing storage.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
