Module 5 › Chapter 4.8 › Bloom Progression
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Follow a staged progression from concept to operational readiness.
Learn

Recognize logs, flow records, and packet captures and what each type reveals about network activity.

Understand

Explain SIEM normalization and correlation, and the trade-offs of continuous packet capture versus targeted capture.

Apply → Master

Configure devices to export logs and flows, perform captures safely, and integrate findings into IR processes and tabletop exercises.

Example: Verification
Verify logs arrive in the SIEM and that a NetFlow collector shows exported flow records for the test host.
Misconception
More raw packets does not always equal better detection — storage, privacy, and noise matter.
Milestone
Complete a simulated incident where you trace attacker movement using logs, flows, and one packet capture.
