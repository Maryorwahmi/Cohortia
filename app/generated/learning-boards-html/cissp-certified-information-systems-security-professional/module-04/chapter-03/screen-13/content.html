Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › S/MIME vs PGP
S/MIME
Uses X.509 certificates and an enterprise PKI. Often integrated into email clients for managed certificate issuance and validation.
When to choose
Enterprises that require centralized management and client integration.
PGP
Decentralized web-of-trust where users sign keys. Often used with separate tools and favored by those who control their own keys.
When to choose
Individuals or groups that prefer decentralized key validation and direct control over trust relationships.
Key Takeaway

Both S/MIME and PGP provide encryption and signatures for email; choose based on management model, client support, and how keys are validated and protected.
