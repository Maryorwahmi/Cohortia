Understand how TLS, SSH, IPsec, DNSSEC, and email encryption protect confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity for different traffic types.

IPsec
Transport and tunnel modes, AH/ESP
Connections
These protocols protect different layers: TLS at the transport and application edge, SSH for administrative channels, and IPsec at the network layer. DNSSEC and email standards add integrity and trust to DNS and messages.
Explore Key Topics
TLS & Certificates
Encrypts web traffic and uses X.509 certificates from CAs to verify identity.
SSH & Key Auth
Key pairs enable secure, passwordless logins and file transfer with SFTP and SCP.
IPsec & DNSSEC
IPsec secures IP packets; DNSSEC signs DNS records to prevent spoofing.
Key Takeaway

Choose the protocol that matches the layer and threat model: TLS for web, SSH for admin, IPsec for network tunnels, DNSSEC for DNS integrity, and S/MIME or PGP for email protection.
