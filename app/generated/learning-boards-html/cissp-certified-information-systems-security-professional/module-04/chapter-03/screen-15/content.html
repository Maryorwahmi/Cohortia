X.509 Certificate
Standard format for public key certificates used in TLS and S/MIME.
Certificate Authority (CA)
Entity that issues and signs certificates, creating trust anchors for validation.
DNSSEC
Cryptographically signs DNS records so resolvers can validate authenticity and integrity.
Final takeaway

These foundational terms tie protocol mechanics to operational checks you perform every time you validate certificates, configure VPNs, or sign DNS zones.
