Key Concepts

Advanced Preventative Measures

IDS/IPS

Systems for detecting (IDS) and actively preventing (IPS) malicious network activity.

EDR

Advanced endpoint solutions for continuous monitoring, detection, and response to threats.

DLP & Honeypot

DLP prevents data loss; honeypots attract attackers for intelligence gathering.

Chapter Summary

IDS and IPS provide crucial detection and prevention capabilities. EDR offers advanced endpoint protection against modern threats, while DLP safeguards sensitive data. Honeypots are strategic decoys for threat intelligence, collectively forming a comprehensive preventative security framework.
