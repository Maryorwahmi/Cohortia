Deceptive Security

Luring Attackers with Honeypots

Gaining Intelligence Through Deception

Honeypot Definition

A single system designed to look like a legitimate, vulnerable target to attract attackers.

Honeynet Definition

A network of multiple honeypots, creating a more complex and realistic decoy environment.

Purpose

Attract and trap attackers to study their methods, tools, and motivations without risking production systems.

Key Benefit

Honeypots provide invaluable threat intelligence, acting as early warning systems and helping improve defensive capabilities.

Honeypot Deployment & Benefits
1. Attract Attackers
2. Observe & Log Activity
3. Gather Threat Intelligence
4. Improve Defenses
Honeypots provide a controlled environment to learn from real attacks.
