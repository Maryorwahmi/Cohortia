Beyond Traditional Anti-Virus

Adapting to Modern Malware Threats

Traditional Anti-Virus

Primarily relies on signature-based detection to identify known malware patterns.

Modern Threats

Utilize polymorphic code, fileless attacks, and advanced evasion techniques.

Effectiveness Gap

Traditional AV struggles against new, unknown, or highly evasive malware.

Challenge

Fileless malware operates in memory, leaving no disk footprint, making it invisible to signature-based scans.
