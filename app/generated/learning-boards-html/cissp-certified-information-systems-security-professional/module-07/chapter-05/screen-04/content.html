Core Concept

The Foundation of Security Operations

Stopping Attacks Before They Start

Proactive Defense: Preventative measures are designed to halt attacks before they can even begin, minimizing potential damage.

Layered Approach: A robust security posture relies on multiple controls working together, known as 'defense in depth'.

Reduce Attack Surface: By implementing preventative controls, organizations reduce the number of potential entry points for attackers.

Common Misconception

Preventative measures are not a 'set it and forget it' solution; they require continuous monitoring, updates, and optimization to remain effective against evolving threats.
