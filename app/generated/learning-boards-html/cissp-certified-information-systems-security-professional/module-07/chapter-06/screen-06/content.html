Core Concept

RTO, RPO, MTD

Turn business impact into measurable targets that guide architecture, backups, and recovery sites.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
Maximum tolerable downtime for a system before recovery is required.
Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
Maximum acceptable age of data after recovery; defines backup frequency.
Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)
Total allowable downtime for the business process before unacceptable consequences.
Key Takeaway

Use measurable RTO/RPO/MTD values as acceptance criteria in procurement and tests.

Concrete example
Payments DB: RTO = 4 hours; RPO = 15 minutes. This requires near-continuous replication or frequent snapshots plus automated failover to meet targets.
Misconception
RTO is not the same as MTD. RTO targets system restore; MTD covers the full process and may be longer to allow non-IT dependencies to be recovered.
Quick exercise
Take one critical process and propose RTO, RPO and MTD values based on estimated hourly loss and manual workaround feasibility.
