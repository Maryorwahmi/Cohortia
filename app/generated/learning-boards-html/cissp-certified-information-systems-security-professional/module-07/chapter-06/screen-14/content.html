Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Key Concepts
BCP & DRP
BCP keeps the business running; DRP restores IT. Both are required and must be linked.
RTO, RPO, MTD
These measurable metrics translate impact into technical requirements for recovery.
Backups & Tests
Apply 3-2-1 backups and validate restores with regular testing to prove recoverability.
Summary
Align scope, set measurable targets, and continuously validate with tests. Use evidence from tests to justify investment and operational changes.
