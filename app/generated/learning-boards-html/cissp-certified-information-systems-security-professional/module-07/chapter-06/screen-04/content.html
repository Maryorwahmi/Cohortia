Core Concept

BCP and DRP: roles and risks

BCP is organization-wide and people-focused; DRP is IT-focused and technical. Neither works well alone. Linkable objectives are essential.

Definition — BCP
A plan that ensures critical business functions continue during and after a disaster, covering people, facilities, and processes.
Definition — DRP
A subset of BCP focused on restoring IT systems, data, and infrastructure required to run business functions.
Key Takeaway

Always map IT recovery tasks to business processes and staff responsibilities before finalizing plans.

Analogy: Ship's voyage vs engine manual
BCP is the voyage plan that keeps the ship moving with alternate routes and crew assignments. DRP is the engine repair steps to restore propulsion. Both are necessary to continue the journey.
Business example
An e-commerce business must pair database recovery with payment gateway credentials and staff at alternate locations to resume sales.
Misconception
Fixing servers alone guarantees recovery — false. You also need staff, access, and supplier continuity.
