Interpreting a failed DRP test

A system with RTO 4 hours and RPO 1 hour took 6 hours to restore and lost 3 hours of data. This is a failure against both metrics and requires prioritized remediation.

Immediate fixes
  • Improve backup frequency or use replication to meet RPO.
  • Automate recovery steps to reduce restore time to meet RTO.
Longer-term
  • Reevaluate recovery site capability; consider hot site or cloud failover.
  • Increase training and test cadence to prevent recurrence.
Test result
Test failed RTO and RPO. Prioritize reducing data loss and shortening restore time before further investments.
Key Takeaway

Use test outcomes to drive measurable fixes and re-test until the plan meets defined recovery metrics.
