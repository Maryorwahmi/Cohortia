Scenario Walkthrough

OLTP vs HR Portal

Use BIA results to pick measurable RTO/RPO and a recovery strategy that balances cost and business impact.

OLTP Database (High criticality)
Suggested RTO: 1–4 hours. Suggested RPO: minutes. Recommended: hot site or cloud-based continuous replication; frequent transaction log backups.
HR Portal (Lower criticality)
Suggested RTO: ~24 hours. Suggested RPO: ~4 hours. Recommended: warm site or cloud restore; nightly full + daily incremental backups.
Key Takeaway

Justify your choices with expected loss-per-hour and consider staff and supplier readiness when choosing a site.

Backup Recommendation
OLTP: continuous replication or transaction log shipping plus off-site copy. HR: nightly full with incremental snapshots. Encrypt and test both regularly.
Testing
Validate failover of OLTP under load and run a restore of an HR backup to confirm data integrity and workflows.
Misconception
Choosing the fastest recovery option without cost justification can drain resources away from higher-value controls.
