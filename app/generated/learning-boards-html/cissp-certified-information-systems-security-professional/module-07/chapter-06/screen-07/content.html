Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Recovery Site Strategies
Comparison

Cold • Warm • Hot • Cloud • Mobile

Balance cost and recovery speed. Use the system's RTO and RPO to guide the decision and include network and licensing in your cost model.

Cold Site
Basic facility; highest RTO; lowest cost. Requires hardware procurement and installation after an event.
Warm Site
Hardware present but not fully synchronized; moderate cost and recovery speed.
Hot Site
Mirrored systems and data for near-immediate failover; lowest RTO/RPO; highest cost.
Cloud-based recovery
On-demand provisioning, flexible scaling, and managed replication. Can be cost-effective for many RTO/RPO combinations but verify networking and security constraints.
Mobile sites
Self-contained units transportable to an incident area; useful for localized physical disruption where remote sites won’t suffice.
Key Takeaway

Cost and speed are trade-offs. Choose the lowest-cost solution that satisfies your measured RTO and RPO and prove it through tests.
