Definition

Business Impact Analysis

BIA identifies and quantifies the consequences of disruption to business functions and produces measurable recovery targets.

Key outputs
  • RTO: time to recover
  • RPO: data loss tolerance
  • MTD: overall downtime tolerance
  • Criticality ranking
Inputs
  • Process owners' interviews
  • Revenue and legal impact data
  • Dependency maps (vendors, staff, systems)
Key Takeaway

Quantify impact in business terms to justify the level of DR investment for each function.

Concrete example
If order processing loses $10,000 per hour, an RTO of 2 hours implies potential loss of $20,000 — this cost helps justify a warm or hot site.
Misconception
Only counting direct revenue misses reputation, regulatory fines, and cascading supplier costs that affect true impact.
Try this
Interview a process owner and estimate loss per hour. Convert that into a recommended RTO and justify it.
