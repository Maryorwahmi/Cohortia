Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Practice & Assessment
Assessment idea
Hands-on BIA and Recovery Strategy Selection
  • Set RTO, RPO, and MTD for two systems and justify each value.
  • Recommend a DR site strategy and backup cadence for each system.
  • Outline a test plan that proves recoverability and documents lessons learned.
Common pitfalls
What to avoid
  • Building DRP without linking to business processes.
  • Not testing restores or assuming backups are restorable.
  • Failing to update plans after system or personnel changes.
Milestone
Present a one-page recovery justification that maps RTO/RPO/MTD to a chosen DR strategy and a test plan.
