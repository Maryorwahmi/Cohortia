Phase 3: Containment

Stopping the Spread and Limiting Damage

01

Isolate Systems

Disconnect affected systems or network segments to prevent further spread.

02

Block Malicious IPs

Use firewalls or network devices to block traffic from known malicious sources.

iptables -A INPUT -s [IP] -j DROP
03

Disable Accounts

Deactivate compromised user accounts to prevent further unauthorized access.

Common Mistake

Rushing to disconnect systems without proper forensic imaging can destroy valuable evidence needed for investigation.

Key Principle

Balance the need for rapid containment with the preservation of evidence to support thorough post-incident analysis.

Key Takeaway

Containment is a critical decision point where swift action is taken to limit damage, but always with a careful consideration for preserving forensic evidence.
