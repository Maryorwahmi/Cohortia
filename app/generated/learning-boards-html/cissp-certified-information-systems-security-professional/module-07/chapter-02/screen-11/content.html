NIST IR Lifecycle

Phase 6: Post-Incident Activity

Learning from Incidents for Future Improvement

01

Conduct Review

Thoroughly review the incident, analyzing what went well and what could be improved.

02

Update Policies

Update IR policies, procedures, and identify new security controls or training needs.

03

Formal Meeting

Hold a 'lessons learned' meeting with stakeholders to document findings and ensure continuous improvement.

Feedback Loop

This phase creates a vital feedback loop that strengthens overall security posture and makes future incidents easier to handle.

Example: Phishing

If a phishing attack was successful, the review might recommend enhanced security awareness training and stronger email filtering rules.

Key Takeaway

The Post-Incident Activity phase is crucial for organizational learning and continuous improvement, transforming incidents into opportunities to enhance security defenses.
