Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential Phases to Remember

Containment

The phase focused on limiting the scope and impact of an incident, preventing its spread.

Eradication

The phase focused on removing the root cause of an incident and eliminating the threat entirely.

Recovery

The phase focused on restoring systems and services to normal, secure operation after an incident.

Lessons Learned

The post-incident review process to identify improvements for future incident handling and overall security posture.

Summary Takeaway

These phases form the operational core of incident response, guiding actions from containing the threat to restoring services and learning for the future.
