Phase 2: Detection & Analysis

Identifying and Understanding the Incident

Reliance on Detective Controls

This phase heavily uses SIEM systems, IDS/IPS, log analysis, and user reports to identify potential incidents.

Key Activities

  • Determine if alert is a true positive.
  • Categorize the incident (e.g., malware, unauthorized access).
  • Assess initial scope and impact.
  • Correlate events from multiple sources.

Importance of Speed & Accuracy

Misidentifying an incident or underestimating its severity can lead to severe consequences and prolonged impact.

Common Misconception

Detection isn't purely automated; human analysts are crucial for correlating diverse data points and making informed decisions.

Key Takeaway

Effective detection and analysis require a combination of robust tools and skilled human insight to quickly and accurately identify, categorize, and assess security incidents.
