NIST IR Lifecycle

Phase 1: Preparation

Building a Strong Foundation Before an Attack

01

Establish Policy & Team

Define IR policy, form a dedicated IRT with clear roles and responsibilities.

02

Develop Plans & Tools

Create communication plans, acquire forensic toolkits, and establish playbooks.

03

Proactive Security

Harden systems, implement controls, and conduct regular security awareness training.

Common Mistake

Failing to prepare adequately leads to reactive, disorganized responses and increased damage during an actual incident.

Real-World Example

An organization with up-to-date contact lists and tested playbooks can quickly mobilize its team and follow predefined steps, significantly reducing response time.

Key Takeaway

Preparation is the most critical phase, laying the groundwork for an efficient and effective incident response by establishing policies, teams, tools, and proactive security measures.
