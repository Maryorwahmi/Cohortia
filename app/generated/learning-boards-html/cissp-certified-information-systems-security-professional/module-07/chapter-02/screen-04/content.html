Why Incident Management Matters

Limiting Damage and Ensuring Business Continuity

What is Incident Management?

It's an organized approach to addressing and managing the aftermath of a security breach or cyberattack, ensuring a consistent and effective reaction.

The NIST Framework

NIST SP 800-61 provides a widely adopted, six-phase lifecycle: Preparation, Detection & Analysis, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, and Post-Incident Activity.

Primary Goals

  • Limit damage and reduce recovery time.
  • Minimize costs associated with the incident.
  • Learn from incidents to improve future security.

Risks Without a Plan

  • Chaotic, disorganized responses.
  • Prolonged downtime and increased data loss.
  • Potential legal and reputational damage.
Key Takeaway

A structured incident response process, like the NIST framework, is essential for effective cybersecurity, ensuring resilience and continuous improvement.
