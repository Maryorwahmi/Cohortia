NIST IR Lifecycle

Phase 5: Recovery

Restoring Operations and Ensuring Security

01

Restore Data

Restore data from clean, verified backups to ensure integrity and prevent re-infection.

02

Bring Systems Online

Carefully bring affected systems and services back into full operation, prioritizing critical ones.

03

Validate & Monitor

Validate functionality and security, then implement post-recovery monitoring to ensure stability.

Common Pitfall

Rushing recovery without thorough validation can lead to re-infection or operational instability.

Prioritize Criticality

Begin recovery with the most critical systems and services to minimize business disruption.

Key Takeaway

Recovery is a meticulous process of restoring systems and services securely, with careful validation and monitoring to ensure long-term stability and resilience.
