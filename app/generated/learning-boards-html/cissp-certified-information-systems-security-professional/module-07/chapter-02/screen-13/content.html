Key Concepts

Core Terms in Incident Management

Incident Response (IR)

The organized process of identifying, analyzing, containing, eradicating, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.

NIST SP 800-61

A widely recognized guide for computer security incident handling, outlining the incident response lifecycle.

Incident Response Team (IRT)

A dedicated group of individuals responsible for managing and executing the incident response process.

Summary Takeaway

These foundational concepts define the structure, guidance, and personnel essential for any organization to effectively manage cybersecurity incidents.
