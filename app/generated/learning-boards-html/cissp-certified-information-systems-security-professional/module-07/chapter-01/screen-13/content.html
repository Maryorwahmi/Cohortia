How SecOps connects to other CISSP domains

Security & Risk Management
Operations implements policy and produces incident data to refine risk decisions.
Security Architecture
Architecture provides the controls operations monitors; operations reports on control effectiveness.
Network Security
Network telemetry and segmentation are core inputs to detection and containment strategies.
IAM
Operations audits access patterns and enforces policies for privileged access incidents.
Example
Vulnerability findings from assessment teams are handed to operations and engineering for remediation and verification, closing the feedback loop.
Operational Integration Checklist
  • Do change management and architecture reviews include operational input?
  • Are detection requirements communicated during design?
  • Are assessment findings tracked to completion by operations and engineering?
Misconception
Treating SecOps as downstream only. Instead, operations must be part of design decisions to ensure observability and response capabilities are baked in.
