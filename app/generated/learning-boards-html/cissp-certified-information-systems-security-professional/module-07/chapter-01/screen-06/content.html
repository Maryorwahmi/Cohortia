Architecture

The Operational Triangle

Operations succeed when people, processes, and technology are kept in sync and updated as systems evolve.

People
Skill sets, escalation authority, and clear role ownership — e.g., Tier 1 triage, Tier 2 analysis, incident response coordination.
Process
Playbooks, runbooks, and testing cycles that capture decisions and reduce error under stress.
Technology
Data pipelines, detection rules, and tooling maintenance ensure operations have accurate signals.
Example Workflow
Detect
IDS/ SIEM alert identifies abnormal activity.
Triage
Tier 1 validates, reduces false positives, and escalates.
Respond
Incident responder coordinates containment and recovery.
Process-Proofing Tip
Regularly review playbooks after infrastructure changes so people know which tools and logs to consult; stale playbooks are a frequent cause of operational delays.
