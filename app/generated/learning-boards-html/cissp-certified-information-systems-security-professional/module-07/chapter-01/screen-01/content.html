Foundational Security Operations Concepts

Understand the purpose, roles, control types, and integration points that make SecOps the operational arm of organizational security.

Duration: ~12 minutes
In this lesson
Roles, controls, workflows, and integration

You will map alerts to SOC roles, categorize control types, and see how SecOps executes risk decisions every day.

Why it matters
SecOps turns policy into action and keeps services available during attacks.

Well-run security operations reduce dwell time, limit impact, and provide evidence for risk decisions and audits.

Explore Key Topics
SOC Roles
Tiered analysts, incident responders, threat hunters and engineers collaborate to handle alerts and incidents.
Controls
Preventative, detective, and corrective controls form a balanced defense-in-depth posture.
Integration
SecOps translates policies from risk management and technical designs into daily operational tasks.
Visual Overview
People • Process • Technology
People
Analysts, responders, hunters and engineers interpret data and act under pressure. Human judgement reduces false positives and guides complex containment.
Process
Triage, escalation, playbooks, and after-action reviews turn alerts into repeatable outcomes and continuous improvement.
Technology
SIEM, IDS, firewalls and vulnerability scanners provide the signals; they must be tuned and maintained to be effective.
