Definition

Threat hunting explained

Hunters form hypotheses and look across telemetry to reveal stealthy activity that automated rules miss.

Example
Unusual scheduled tasks prompt a hunt. Analysts query process creation logs and network flows to find persistence mechanisms and signs of lateral movement.
Misconception
Thinking hunting replaces detection. Instead, hunting augments detection and refines rules so future activity can be detected automatically.
Operational Requirements
  • Comprehensive and searchable telemetry.
  • Time for skilled analysts to investigate hypotheses.
  • Mechanisms to convert findings into detection rules.
Key Takeaway
Hunting reduces blind spots and strengthens automated detection through iterative improvement.
