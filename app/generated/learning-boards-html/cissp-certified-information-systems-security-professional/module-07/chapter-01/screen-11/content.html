What engineers do

Engineers maintain pipelines, detection rules, and automation so that operations have reliable signals and can act quickly.

Concrete Example
Translate a hunter's discovery into a SIEM rule and endpoint signature, then deploy with a tuning window to measure impact.
Operational Caution
New rules must be tested and tuned to avoid generating excessive false positives that burden Tier 1.
Engineer Checklist
  • Centralize logs with consistent formats and timestamps.
  • Document detection rules and expected telemetry patterns.
  • Provide rollback and testing steps for new rules.
  • Coordinate with operations for tuning and feedback loops.
Key Takeaway
Engineers make detection reliable and efficient; investment here increases overall SecOps effectiveness.
