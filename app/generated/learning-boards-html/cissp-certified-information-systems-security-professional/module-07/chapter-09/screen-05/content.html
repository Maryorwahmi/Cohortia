Process Flow
Employee Lifecycle
A simple three-stage process with controls at each handoff
01 Pre-employment
Define security role requirements and perform legal background checks for sensitive jobs.
  • Criminal history where permitted
  • Employment verification
  • Credit checks for financial roles
02 Employment
Onboarding training, role-based instruction, and continuous education keep skills current.
  • Mandatory security awareness
  • Role-specific training (developers, admins, HR)
  • Periodic refreshers and simulations
03 Termination
Immediate revocation of physical and logical access and asset retrieval are essential.
  • Disable accounts, VPN, cloud access immediately
  • Collect devices and tokens
  • Conduct an exit reminder about confidentiality
Key Takeaway

Map controls to lifecycle stages and enforce timelines: the handoffs create the highest risk if poorly managed.
