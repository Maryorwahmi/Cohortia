Culture & Policy
No-Blame Reporting Culture
Make reporting safe, fast, and instructive
Make it easy
Single-click reporting or a short form lowers the barrier to report.
Leadership modeling
Visible support from leaders reinforces that reporting is valued.
Timely feedback
Respond quickly to reporters with next steps and outcomes to close the loop.
Common Misconception

No-blame culture removes fear of reporting but still requires accountability processes for malicious acts.
