Core Concept
What is Personnel Security?
Processes that ensure individuals do not pose a risk to assets.
Scope
  • Includes employees, contractors, temps, and vendors
  • Applies during hiring, employment, and termination
  • Balances verification with legal and privacy obligations
Concrete example
A new hire receives incident reporting steps and a mandatory phishing simulation; their results guide focused coaching.
Key Takeaway

Personnel controls reduce human-assisted bypasses of technical defenses and are measured by behavior as well as completion.

Definition Visual
Why it matters
Human errors and social engineering bypass many technical controls. Personnel security reduces that vector.
Common mistake
Treating awareness as a once-a-year checkbox rather than a continuous program.
Measurement
Use simulations, checklists, and task-based assessments to measure true competence.
Pro tip
Leadership visibility and no-blame reporting dramatically improve participation.
