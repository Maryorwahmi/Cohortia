Definition
Continuous & Role-Based Training
Keep skills current and focused on day-to-day risk
Continuous Education
Short periodic modules, updates after new threats, and reinforcement through simulations.
Example: Monthly 5-minute micro-lessons on a current phishing technique.
Role-Based Training
Targeted training for developers, admins, HR, and executives with hands-on tasks.
Example: Developers receive secure coding workshops and pull-request checklists.
Key Takeaway

Make role-based training compulsory for high-risk functions and pair it with task-based assessment to prove competence.
