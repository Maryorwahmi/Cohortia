Definition
Background Checks
Purpose, examples, and constraints
Types: criminal history, employment verification, credit checks when appropriate.
Why: confirm identity and role suitability; reduce insider risk.
Legal note: comply with privacy and labor laws; involve HR and legal.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Role Comparison
Privileged Administrator
Comprehensive checks and strong identity verification. Higher scrutiny is justified.
Temporary Contractor
Scoped checks focused on identity and minimal role-specific verifications.
Finance Role
Include credit checks where legally appropriate due to financial access.
Common Mistake

Applying identical checks to every role wastes resources and reduces legal clarity.
