What to Remember
Key Concepts
Personnel Security
Controls that ensure individuals do not pose risk throughout their lifecycle.
Awareness Training
Mandatory, engaging, role-relevant training measured by behavior and simulations.
Offboarding
Immediate revocation of access and retrieval of company assets to close risk windows.
Summary

Focus on lifecycle-aligned controls, make training measurable and role-relevant, and ensure offboarding is rapid and verifiable to reduce human risk.
