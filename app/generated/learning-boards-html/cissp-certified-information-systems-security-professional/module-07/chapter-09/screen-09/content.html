Process
Offboarding
Make offboarding timely, coordinated, and verifiable
Checklist (example)
  • Revoke network, cloud, and email access immediately
  • Deactivate VPN and admin privileges
  • Collect devices, tokens, and badges
  • Perform exit interview and remind of NDAs
  • Run a post-exit audit to confirm completion
Common Oversight
Delaying account deactivation until HR processes finish creates security gaps.
Key Takeaway

Treat offboarding as a cross-functional, time-bound process and verify completion with an audit.
