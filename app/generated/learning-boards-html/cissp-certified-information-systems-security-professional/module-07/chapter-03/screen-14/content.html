Log Retention
Keep logs long enough for investigations and compliance, archive with integrity controls.
Log Integrity
Use hashing, WORM storage, secure transport, and strict access controls to keep logs trustworthy.
Types of Logs
System, application, network, and security logs each add critical context; combine them in investigations.
Final reminder

Before you finish: check that critical sources are centralized, retention and integrity controls are documented, and SIEM rules are tuned to your environment.
