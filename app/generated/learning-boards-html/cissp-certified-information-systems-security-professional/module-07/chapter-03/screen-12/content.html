Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Retention lifecycle
Hot (days-weeks)
High-fidelity, quickly queryable logs for active detection and triage. Fast access and short retention to reduce cost.
Warm (weeks-months)
Indexed and retained for investigations that surface after initial detection. Balance query performance with storage cost.
Cold / Archive (months-years)
WORM or hashed archives kept for compliance and legal needs. Ensure integrity and plan legal-hold procedures to suspend deletion when required.
Audit readiness checklist: ensure indexed timestamps, preserved hashes, documented retention schedules, and role-based access. Automate periodic audits to confirm policies are enforced and logs remain tamper-evident.
Key Takeaway

A tiered retention model plus integrity controls and audit procedures keeps logs useful, compliant, and cost-managed over time.
