Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Key Concepts
Logging
Recording events that form the evidence for investigations and audits.
Monitoring
Continuous observation and analysis of logs to detect anomalies and incidents.
SIEM
Centralizes, normalizes, correlates, and alerts on log data for detection and forensics.
Remember

Combine targeted log collection, strong integrity controls, and tuned SIEM rules to make logs actionable and reliable for both security operations and compliance.
