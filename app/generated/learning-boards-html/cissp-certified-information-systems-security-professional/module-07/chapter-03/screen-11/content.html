Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Operationalizing logs
Dashboards & Views
Panels for failed auths, privilege escalations, and unusual outbound flows help analysts triage quickly.
Rule tuning
Baseline normal behavior, adjust thresholds to cut false positives, and periodically review rules after incidents.
Analyst workflows
Create SOPs for triage, escalation, and evidence collection so investigations are consistent and defensible.
Threat hunting
Schedule proactive hunts for anomalies and indicators that automated rules miss; document findings and tune rules accordingly.
Governance
Enforce strict access controls on logs, audit log access, and ensure retention policies are followed to meet compliance needs.
Key Takeaway

Operational capability requires tuned dashboards, repeatable workflows, and governance to ensure logs support rapid, reliable response.
