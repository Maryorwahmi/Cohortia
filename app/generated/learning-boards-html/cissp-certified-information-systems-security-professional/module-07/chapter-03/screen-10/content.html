Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Logging: smart vs everything
Logging everything
  • Pros: maximal data available for investigations.
  • Cons: high cost, noise, performance impact, and privacy risk.
Key takeaway

Selective, well-designed logging gives better signal at lower cost and with fewer privacy risks.

Logging intelligently
  • Define questions you need to answer and collect fields that support them.
  • Mask sensitive data, sample high-volume telemetry, and use asynchronous shipping to protect performance.
Practical checklist
  1. Identify critical events to log.
  2. Redact or avoid logging secrets or PII.
  3. Set sampling rules for verbose telemetry.
  4. Centralize and tune rules in the SIEM.
