Reporting the Findings

Documenting Conclusions for Review

Purpose of the Report

To document findings, methodology, and conclusions in a clear, concise, and defensible manner suitable for legal or management review.

Key Elements

  • Executive Summary: Overview of the incident and key findings.
  • Methodology: Detailed description of steps taken and tools used.
  • Findings: Presentation of relevant evidence and analysis.
  • Conclusions: Interpretation of findings, root cause, and impact.
  • Recommendations: Actions to prevent future incidents.
Importance

A well-written report is crucial for legal admissibility and informed decision-making, translating technical findings into actionable intelligence.

Clarity

Present complex technical information in an understandable way for non-technical audiences.

Conciseness

Avoid jargon where possible and get straight to the point, while providing sufficient detail.

Defensibility

Ensure all claims are supported by evidence and the methodology is sound and repeatable.

Objectivity

Present facts without bias or personal opinion, letting the evidence speak for itself.
