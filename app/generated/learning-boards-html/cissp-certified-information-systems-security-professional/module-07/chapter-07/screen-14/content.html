Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Digital Forensics

The scientific process of collecting, examining, analyzing, and reporting on digital evidence.

Chain of Custody

A documented history of the handling and possession of evidence, crucial for admissibility.

Evidence Integrity

Ensuring that digital evidence has not been altered or corrupted, typically via cryptographic hashing.

Summary Takeaway

These foundational concepts ensure that digital investigations are scientific, documented, and verifiable, making findings reliable and legally defensible.
