The Digital Forensic Investigation Process

A Structured Approach to Incident Response

1

Identification

Recognizing an incident and potential digital evidence.

2

Preservation/Collection

Securing the scene and creating forensically sound copies.

3

Examination

Analyzing collected data for relevant information without conclusions.

4

Analysis

Interpreting data to reconstruct events and determine root cause.

5

Reporting

Documenting findings, methodology, and conclusions for review.

Why a Process?

Ensures thoroughness, maintains integrity, and supports legal admissibility.

Key Takeaway

Following a structured forensic process is essential for reliable, defensible, and comprehensive incident investigations.
