The Chain of Custody

Documenting Every Step of Evidence Handling

What it is

A meticulous, unbroken record of who has had possession of a piece of evidence, when, and for what purpose.

Why it matters

Ensures evidence admissibility in court and proves that the evidence has not been altered or compromised since collection.

Critical Impact

Any break in the chain of custody can render evidence inadmissible, undermining the entire investigation.

1. Collect

Secure and acquire evidence.

2. Document

Record details and handlers.

3. Store Securely

Place in a controlled environment.

4. Transfer

Log all movements and recipients.

5. Present

Introduce in court/review.
