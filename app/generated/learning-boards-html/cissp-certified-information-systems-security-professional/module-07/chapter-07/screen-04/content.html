What is Digital Forensics?

The Scientific Approach to Digital Evidence

Definition

Digital forensics is the scientific collection, examination, analysis, and reporting of digital evidence in a manner admissible in court or for internal investigations.

Primary Goal

To preserve evidence integrity, ensuring findings are accurate, reliable, and untampered, to understand incidents and their impact.

Common Mistake

Untrained personnel attempting direct investigation can inadvertently alter or destroy crucial evidence, rendering it inadmissible.
