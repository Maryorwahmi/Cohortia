Verification

Verify to confirm closure

Re-scan or re-test systems after remediation to confirm vulnerabilities are closed and that no new issues were introduced.

Automation
Automate verification scans after remediation to reduce human error and speed closure.
Metrics
Report open criticals, mean time to remediate, and verification success to stakeholders.
Continuous improvement
Refinement actions
Adjust scanning cadence, expand asset lists, update prioritization matrix, and refine pen test scopes based on metrics and post-mortems.
Key Takeaway

Verification and metrics create a feedback loop that improves detection, prioritization, and remediation over time.
