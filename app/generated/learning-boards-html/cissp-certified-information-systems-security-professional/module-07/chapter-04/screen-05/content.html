Process Flow

The cyclical phases

Repeat this cycle continuously
1. Asset Inventory
Identify systems, apps, cloud resources and their criticality.
2. Vulnerability Identification
Automated scans and scheduled pen tests discover possible weaknesses.
3. Assessment & Prioritization
Use CVSS, exploitability, and asset criticality to rank work.
4. Remediation & Mitigation
Patch, reconfigure, or apply temporary controls to reduce risk.
5. Verification
Re-scan or re-test to confirm fixes and check for regressions.
6. Reporting & Improvement
Report metrics and refine scanning cadence, coverage, and policies.
Key Takeaway

The lifecycle ensures vulnerabilities are not only found but closed and verified, reducing the exposure window.
