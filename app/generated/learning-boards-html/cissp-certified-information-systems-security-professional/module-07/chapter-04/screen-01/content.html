Vulnerability Management

A proactive program that finds and fixes weaknesses across assets, using scanning, testing, prioritization, and verification. It prevents incidents by shrinking the attack surface.

In this lesson
Scanning vs Pen Test
Automated breadth and manual depth contrasted with real examples.
Prioritization
CVSS plus asset criticality to order work.
Remediation
Patching, mitigations, verification, and reporting.
Visual concept
Scanning: automated, frequent, broad signatures
Pen Test: manual, targeted, exploit verification
Remediate & Verify: patch, re-scan, confirm
Why this matters

You cannot secure what you do not monitor. Continuous discovery and prioritized action reduce exploit windows and focus scarce resources where they matter most.
