Definition

Penetration Testing

A deliberate, authorized simulation of real attacks to confirm whether vulnerabilities can be exploited and to measure impact.

Techniques
Social engineering, web app attacks (SQL injection, XSS), network exploitation, and privilege escalation.
Rules of Engagement
Explicit authorization is required to avoid legal or operational impact during intrusive testing.
Concrete scenario
Scenario
A pen tester exploits an unpatched web server to gain shell access and then escalates privileges to read sensitive customer data.
Key Takeaway

Pen tests validate exploitability and business impact; they complement, not replace, frequent automated scanning.
