Comparison
Vulnerability Scanning
Automated, scheduled, broad coverage. Good for continuous monitoring and quick identification of known flaws. Low intrusion risk when configured correctly.
Use case: Weekly scans for critical servers to detect newly published vulnerabilities.
Output: Lists of potential vulnerabilities and configuration issues, with severity indicators.
Penetration Testing
Manual, targeted, and intrusive tests that simulate real attacks to prove exploitability and assess impact. Requires explicit authorization and planning.
Use case: Annual or pre-release pen test to validate high-risk systems and attack chains.
Output: Exploitability proof, attack narratives, and remediation recommendations.
Operational pattern
Use scanning for continuous detection and pen testing for targeted validation. Treat scanner results as inputs to prioritization and plan pen tests when confirmation of impact or complex attack paths is required.
