Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Learning Objectives

Operational skills

Learn to verify remediation with re-scans or targeted retests, to prepare concise management reports that translate technical findings into business risk, and to use lessons learned to adjust cadence and scope.

Verification
Re-scan and confirm that remediation closed the vulnerability without introducing regressions.
Reporting
Show trends, outstanding critical items, and mean time to remediation for stakeholders.
Continuous Improvement
Adjust scanning cadence, update asset lists, and refine rules of engagement.
Governance
Establish policies for patch windows, exceptions, and testing authorization.
Quick mnemonic
D-I-P-R-V
Discover, Identify, Prioritize, Remediate, Verify — use this to remember the lifecycle and keep actions traceable.
