Surveillance and visitor controls

Use CCTV and visitor logs to detect incidents and to produce forensic evidence after events.

CCTV

Position cameras for coverage, enable motion alerts, and retain footage per policy for investigations.

Visitor management

Log visitors, issue temporary credentials, assign escorts, and record purpose and duration.

Maintenance & audits

Regular testing of cameras, alarms, and review of access logs ensures systems remain effective.

Concrete scenario

A CCTV motion alert during off-hours is correlated with an access badge event. Review shows a visitor was issued temporary access without escort; the incident triggers retraining and tightening of visitor issuance procedures.

Misconception

CCTV alone deters all threats. Unless footage is reviewed and logs audited, it often only helps after an incident occurs.

Key Takeaway

Integrate CCTV with access logs and visitor systems, and commit to scheduled maintenance and reviews to keep detection meaningful.
