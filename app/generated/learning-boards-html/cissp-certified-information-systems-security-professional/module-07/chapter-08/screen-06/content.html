Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Perimeter Security

Perimeter controls

Barriers, lighting, guards, and detection that shape approach and access.

Fencing & gates

Physical barriers channel traffic to monitored entrances and slow intruders.

Lighting

Motion-activated lighting reduces concealed approaches and improves camera images.

CCTV & sensors

Cameras, motion, and perimeter sensors provide early alerts when integrated with response teams.

Operational example

A perimeter camera detects motion after hours. The motion event triggers lighting, alerts the monitoring center, and the on-site guard reviews live footage before dispatching a patrol. The event and footage are logged for later review.

Misconception

Installing cameras without integrating them into an alert and review process yields little protective value.

Check

When was the last time perimeter cameras and lights were tested and logs reviewed?

Pro tip

Design patrol routes informed by camera blind spots to maximize coverage.
