Physical Security Operations

Protect assets with layered perimeter, building, and internal controls tied to operational practices.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson
Layered controls, access mechanisms, environmental systems
Coming up
Server-room design scenario and operational checks
Why it matters
Physical failure can bypass logical controls and cause downtime or theft
Perimeter

Fencing, lighting, cameras, guards

Building

Hardened doors, alarms, access readers

Internal

Mantraps, rack locks, environmental systems

Key Takeaway

Layered physical controls prevent a single point of failure from exposing critical assets.

Visual concept
Concentric layers protecting a server room
Threat

An intruder bypasses an exterior gate

Control

Internal mantrap prevents access beyond entry vestibule

Example

A secure campus uses fences and guards, building readers, and a mantrap at the server-room entry so that even if one layer fails, others remain.
