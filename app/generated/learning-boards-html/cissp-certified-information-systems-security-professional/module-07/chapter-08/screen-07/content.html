Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Building Security

Harden doors, log entries, and resist tampering

Building controls balance physical strength with auditability and egress safety.

Hardened entry

Reinforced doors, secure frames, and protective glazing make forced entry harder.

Access readers

Electronic readers give audit trails, support credential revocation, and integrate with alarms.

Alarms

Door and window sensors should connect to a monitored response center and be tested regularly.

Comparison: mechanical locks vs electronic readers
  • Mechanical locks: low cost, limited audit, manual rekeying required on compromise.
  • Electronic readers: higher cost, central management, immediate credential revocation and logs.
Misconception

Electronic systems are secure only if their databases and controllers are physically protected and maintained.

Check

Can credentials be revoked immediately after an employee leaves?

Pro tip

Protect access control panels and wiring from tampering with locked enclosures and tamper sensors.
