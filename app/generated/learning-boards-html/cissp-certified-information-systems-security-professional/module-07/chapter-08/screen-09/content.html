Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Access Control Mechanisms

Compare access technologies

Understand trade-offs between cost, audit, and privacy.

Mechanical locks

Simple and cheap, no audit trail, manual rekeying required on compromise.

Electronic card systems

Centralized management, credential revocation, and access logs for forensics.

Biometrics

High assurance, privacy concerns, and higher acquisition/maintenance costs.

Security guards

Human judgment and response; require training and schedules to be effective.

Concrete example

A high-value lab combines card readers for staff, biometrics for sensitive cabinets, and a guard who verifies deliveries. Audit logs are reviewed weekly and biometric templates are stored and managed to protect privacy in policy.

Misconception

Stronger technology alone solves risk. Without policies, revocation, and maintenance, even biometrics can fail to protect assets.

Key Takeaway

Select controls to balance assurance, auditability, privacy, and cost, and define the operational practices that keep them effective.
