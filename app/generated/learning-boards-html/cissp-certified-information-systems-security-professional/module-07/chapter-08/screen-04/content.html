Physical security anchors your defenses

A short definition and operational framing you will use to justify controls.

Deterrence

Visible measures—fences, lighting, cameras—reduce likelihood of attempted breach.

Delay

Hardened doors and mantraps slow attackers to allow response.

Detection

Alarms, CCTV, and access logs expose incidents for response and forensics.

Key Takeaway

Physical controls provide deterrence, delay, and detection and must be operated and maintained to remain effective.

Concrete example

Imagine a server closet behind an unlocked door. An intruder can remove a hard drive, bypass encryption keys stored elsewhere, and cause data loss. Adding a card reader and rack locks prevents casual removal and provides an audit trail.

Misconception

Assuming logical controls alone are sufficient. Physical breaches often nullify digital protections.
