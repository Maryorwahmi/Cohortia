Environmental protections for continuity

Temperature, fire, power, and water risks can cause major outages or damage; control them proactively.

HVAC

Maintain temperature and humidity. Use redundant units for failover.

Fire suppression

Prefer inert gas systems for electronics to avoid water damage; enable early detection.

Power

UPS for short outages, generators for long outages, and redundant feeds where possible.

Water detection

Sensors under floors and near cooling equipment detect leaks early to prevent damage.

Concrete example

Use redundant HVAC units configured for automatic failover, inert gas suppression for fires, UPS batteries with monitoring, and a generator exercised monthly under load. Pair water sensors with alarm integration so staff are alerted to leaks before they reach racks.

Misconception

Water sprinklers are acceptable for all spaces. For server rooms, water-based suppression tends to cause widespread equipment loss even if it extinguishes the fire.

Key Takeaway

Design environmental redundancy and test it regularly to avoid single-point failures that cause downtime.
