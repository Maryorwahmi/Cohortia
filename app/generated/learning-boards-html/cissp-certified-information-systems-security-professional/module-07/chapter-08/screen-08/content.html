Internal security architecture

The innermost protections for the most critical equipment require both physical and procedural controls.

Mantraps

Prevent tailgating by allowing only one person per authentication cycle.

Rack locks

Prevent removal of drives and provide physical separation at the cabinet level.

Access policies

Strict issuance and revocation rules for elevated credentials and visitor escort requirements.

Key Takeaway

Apply stricter access and tamper protections to the spaces that host your highest-value assets.

Concrete scenario

Design a mantrap at the server-room entrance that requires a valid card plus biometric confirmation. Log all entries and review logs weekly. Rack locks and internal CCTV create additional barriers and forensic evidence in case of tampering.

Misconception

Internal security is not a luxury; it is essential because attackers who reach internal zones can cause the most damage.

Check

Are elevated access credentials tracked and revoked promptly when people change roles?

Pro tip

Combine physical controls with weekly log reviews to detect suspicious patterns early.
