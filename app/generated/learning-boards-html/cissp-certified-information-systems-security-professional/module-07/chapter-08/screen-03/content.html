How you'll demonstrate skill

Concrete deliverables and operational checks to prove competency.

Deliverable
Server-room security plan

Three layers, two controls per layer, and rationale based on defense-in-depth.

Operational
Testing & maintenance

Alarms, locks, CCTV, and fire systems require scheduled checks and log reviews.

Safety
Drills & egress

Clear evacuation routes, regular fire drills, and non-obstructed exits keep people safe during incidents.

Key Takeaway

Operational checks and documentation are as important as the physical hardware you choose.
