Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Access Control Lists
Definition

ACLs as object-attached rules

ACLs list principals and allowed operations for a resource. They are enforcement artifacts used by many models.

Operational guidance
  • Prefer generating ACLs from roles or policies to avoid manual drift.
  • Record ACL provenance for auditing and review.
ACL example
{
  "resource": "/reports/Q4.csv",
  "acl": [
    {"principal": "group:Finance", "permissions": ["read"]},
    {"principal": "user:auditor1", "permissions": ["read"]}
  ]
}
Common mistake

Maintaining ACLs manually across many resources without automation leads to stale permissions and audit headaches.

Key Takeaway

Treat ACLs as enforceable outputs of your higher-level policy; automate their generation and auditing.
