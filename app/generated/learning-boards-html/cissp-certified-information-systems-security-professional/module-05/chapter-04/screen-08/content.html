Declarative role mapping

Example mapping illustrates role creation, permissions, and membership without depending on a specific directory product.

{
  "role": "Financial Analyst",
  "permissions": ["reports:view","reports:export_lite","forecasts:create"],
  "members": ["user:alice@example.com","group:finance_interns"]
}
Operational notes
  • Approve role definitions through a change-control process.
  • Automate membership removal when users change departments.
Scenario
Alice joins finance. Instead of editing many ACLs, add Alice to Financial Analyst role. When she leaves, remove the role assignment to revoke access consistently.
Common mistake

Treat role creation as informal. Poor governance causes role proliferation and hidden privileges.

Key Takeaway

RBAC reduces per-user changes; pair it with governance to prevent role sprawl.
