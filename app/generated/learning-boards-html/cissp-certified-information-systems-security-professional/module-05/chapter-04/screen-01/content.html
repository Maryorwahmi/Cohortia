Authorization Models and Access Control

You will learn DAC, MAC, RBAC, and ABAC, how ACLs implement policies, and why least privilege is essential. Expect practical guidance for enterprise design and a hands-on financial system scenario.

Why this matters
Your access model shapes security posture, admin cost, and auditability. The right model prevents privilege creep and enforces segregation of duties.
What you'll practice
Designing RBAC role definitions, mapping ACL entries, and proposing an ABAC rule to tighten a sensitive export workflow.
