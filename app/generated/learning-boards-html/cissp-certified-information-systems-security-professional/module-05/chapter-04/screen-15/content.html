Model fit
Match model to sensitivity, scale, and governance capacity.
Automate
Generate ACLs from roles/attributes and automate deprovisioning to reduce drift.
Least Privilege
Grant minimal rights and review regularly to contain risk.
Final takeaway

Authorization is an operational discipline: choose the right model, automate enforcement, govern roles and attributes, and audit decisions continuously.
