Process

Least privilege as lifecycle

Design minimal roles, provision automatically, review regularly, and remove access promptly when no longer needed.

Operational checklist
  • Automate provisioning and deprovisioning from HR or directory events.
  • Schedule regular entitlement reviews and remove unused permissions.
  • Enforce segregation of duties in role definitions.
Concrete scenario
For transaction processing, separate roles: Creator (create transactions) and Approver (authorize transactions). No single user holds both roles, preventing fraud and errors.
Common mistake

Granting admin or default elevated rights to reduce friction creates long-lived excessive privileges. Fix with role-based defaults and time-limited elevation when needed.

Key Takeaway

Treat least privilege as an automated lifecycle: design, provision, review, prune, and log decisions for auditability.
