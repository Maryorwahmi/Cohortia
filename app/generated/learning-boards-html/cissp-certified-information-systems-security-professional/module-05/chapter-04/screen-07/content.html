Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Role-Based Access Control
Architecture

Permissions attached to roles

Roles represent job functions. Assign permissions once to a role; assign users to roles. This simplifies onboarding and auditing.

Benefits
  • Simpler administration and consistent policy application.
  • Faster onboarding and role-change handling.
Challenges
  • Role explosion and overlapping permissions when roles are poorly designed.
  • Need for role entitlement reviews to prevent privilege creep.
Role example and hierarchy
Roles:
  • Financial Analyst: view reports, export limited data.
  • Approver: approve transactions, no export rights.
  • Senior Analyst: inherits Financial Analyst plus additional read-write rights for forecasts.
Common mistake

Defining a role for every task leads to many narrow roles that are hard to manage. Group responsibilities into meaningful role sets instead.

Key Takeaway

Use RBAC for enterprise scale, but invest in role design, hierarchies, and regular entitlement review.
