Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Authorization
Deciding what an authenticated identity may do within a system.
Access Control Models
DAC, MAC, RBAC, ABAC each trade flexibility, control, and administrative cost.
Least Privilege
Grant the minimum necessary rights and prune regularly to reduce attack surface.
ACLs
Object-attached lists that specify principals and allowed operations; audit their provenance.
Segregation of Duties
Split critical tasks across roles to prevent single-person control over sensitive processes.
Governance
Combine policy, automation, reviews, and logging to keep authorization effective and auditable.
Summary takeaway

Select the model that balances control and manageability for your risk profile and enforce it with repeatable governance, automation, and auditing.
