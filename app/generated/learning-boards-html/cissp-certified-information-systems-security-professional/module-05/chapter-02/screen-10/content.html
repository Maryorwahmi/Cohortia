Attack patterns

Identify likely attacks and choose high-impact mitigations for each threat class.

Phishing / credential theft
Defend with phishing-resistant MFA, user training, and monitoring for anomalous login locations or devices.
SIM swap / SMS interception
Avoid SMS for high-risk systems. If SMS is used, implement carrier protections and alternative recovery methods.
Token theft / device compromise
Use device management, fast revocation, and cryptographic tokens that bind to hardware when possible.
Concrete countermeasures
Phishing-resistant MFA
Security keys and WebAuthn prevent credential replay and phishing-based interception.
Strong recovery and identity verification
Design recovery flows to require equivalent proofing to prevent social engineering takeovers.
Monitoring and incident readiness
Log authentication events, alert on suspicious patterns, and rehearse revocation and re-issue procedures.
Key Takeaway

Focus on the highest probable attacks and invest in the mitigations that give the best assurance per cost, while closing weak recovery paths.
