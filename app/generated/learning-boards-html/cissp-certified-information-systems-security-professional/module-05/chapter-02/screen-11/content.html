Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › VPN MFA Activity
Process Flow

Three-step evaluation

  1. Current state: Identify vulnerabilities of username/password, such as phishing and credential reuse.
  2. Proposed MFA: Choose two different factor types and justify using security, usability, and cost.
  3. Implementation: Plan enrollment, lost-device handling, and strong recovery verification for 500 staff.
Scenario example
Proposed solution
Use a passphrase plus authenticator app for general staff. For administrators, require a hardware security key plus PIN. This balances cost and phishing resistance.
Deployment challenge
Managing lost devices and enrollment at scale; mitigate with staged rollouts, documented recovery policies, and training.
Key Takeaway

A strong proposal combines complementary factors and operational plans so security gains are realized in practice.
