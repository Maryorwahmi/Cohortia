Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
Identity Proofing
Verify authoritative attributes before creating or elevating accounts to prevent imposters.
Authentication Factors
Something you know, have, or are — each with different security and operational implications.
Multi-Factor Authentication
Require two or more different factor types. Prefer phishing-resistant options for high-assurance systems.
Summary
Design authentication by matching proofing and factor choices to asset sensitivity and likely attacks. Operational details like recovery and enforcement determine actual security.
