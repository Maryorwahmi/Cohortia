Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Common Mistakes
Lift-and-shift
Copying on-prem permissions to the cloud without adapting increases risk due to broader resource scope and different semantics.
Unprotected service accounts
API keys and service accounts often lack MFA and are overlooked in user-focused IAM policies.
Delayed deprovisioning
Manual workflows create orphaned access; automate deprovisioning from HR triggers to avoid lingering accounts.
Mitigations
  • Audit and refactor roles to enforce least privilege.
  • Protect and rotate service keys with a secrets manager.
  • Automate deprovisioning and verify via sync logs and access reviews.
