Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › IAM Components
Identity Provider
Issues tokens and manages authentication, SSO, and federation.
Provisioning Engine
Automates account lifecycle across directories and applications.
Directory Connector
Syncs authoritative attributes from on-prem directories to the cloud.
Governance
Access reviews, role management, and policy enforcement.
Monitoring
Collect logs and detect suspicious activity in identity events.
Connectors & APIs
Integrations to SaaS and cloud provider IAM APIs for provisioning and role assignment.
Key Takeaway
Design each component to work together: authoritative directories feed connectors, IdP issues tokens, provisioning automates accounts, governance enforces policy, and monitoring validates behavior.
