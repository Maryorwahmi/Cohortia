Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Selecting Protocols
Enterprise SaaS
SAML is often the preferred choice for mature enterprise SSO integrations due to wide vendor support and attribute mapping features.
API Delegation
OAuth handles delegated authorization and scope-based access for third-party apps interacting with APIs.
Mobile & Web Auth
OIDC provides compact identity tokens suited for modern clients and social login providers.
Operational fit: factor vendor metadata management, certificate rotation, and ease of attribute mapping into your choice. For internal domain authentication, Kerberos remains the default due to Active Directory integration and ticketing efficiency.
Key Takeaway

Choose protocols by use case, vendor support, and operational manageability rather than assuming one protocol fits all needs.
