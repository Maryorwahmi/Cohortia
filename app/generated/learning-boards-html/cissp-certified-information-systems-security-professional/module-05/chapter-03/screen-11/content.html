Architecture

IdP and SP responsibilities

Identity Provider (IdP)
  • Authenticate users and enforce MFA
  • Issue signed assertions or tokens
  • Manage signing keys and certificate rotation
Service Provider (SP)
  • Validate signatures and claims
  • Map attributes to local roles
  • Enforce application-level authorization
Operational checks
  • Confirm certificate trust and RSS rotation policy.
  • Verify attribute schemas and stable user identifiers.
  • Test mapping from attributes to roles in the SP.
Key Takeaway

IdP and SP share trust responsibilities: secure signing at IdP and strict validation at SP are both required for safe federation.
