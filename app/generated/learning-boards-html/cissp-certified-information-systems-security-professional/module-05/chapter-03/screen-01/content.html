Why this matters
Central identity reduces password sprawl, improves auditability, and simplifies user access, but concentrates risk at the IdP. You will learn how to balance usability and security.
Visual concept: identity provider issues assertions or tokens that service providers trust. This central trust model reduces repeated logins while enabling centralized policy enforcement.
IdP
Authenticates users and issues assertions or tokens.
SP
Relies on IdP assertions or tokens to grant access.
