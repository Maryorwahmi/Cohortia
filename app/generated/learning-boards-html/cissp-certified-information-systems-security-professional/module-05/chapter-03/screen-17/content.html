JWT Validation
Always verify signature, issuer, audience, and expiration on JWTs used for authentication.
Kerberos Hygiene
Keep clocks synchronized and register accurate SPNs to prevent ticket failures.
IdP Hardening
Protect signing keys, require MFA at the IdP, monitor issuance, and design for high availability.
Final Takeaway
Combine protocol-appropriate choices with operational controls: validate tokens, maintain Kerberos hygiene, and harden the IdP to protect the entire federated authentication ecosystem.
