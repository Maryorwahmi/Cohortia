Module X › Chapter 5.6 › Common IGA Challenges

Four common barriers

Data quality
Incomplete or incorrect identity attributes undermine governance.
Scale & complexity
Many disparate systems and entitlement models make aggregation hard.
Resistance
Business owners may push back on frequent reviews or new processes.
Operational overhead
Manual controls are time-consuming and error-prone.
Strategies to overcome
  • Automate provisioning, deprovisioning, and access reviews.
  • Integrate IGA with HR and directory services as authoritative sources.
  • Educate owners, provide clear SLAs, and run pilots on high-risk systems.
  • Implement continuous monitoring and audit logging to detect drift.
Concrete example
Integrating the HR authoritative feed improved provisioning accuracy and cut orphaned accounts by reducing manual entries and ensuring timely deprovisioning at termination.
Key Takeaway
Practical IGA progress begins with data and integration. A focused pilot on a high-risk system yields momentum for broader adoption.
