Ongoing program
IGA requires continuous attention and periodic recertification, not a single cleanup.
Prioritize pilots
Start with critical systems and expand after demonstrating measurable results.
Automate & integrate
Link IGA to HR and directories, and automate provisioning and reviews to scale governance.
Final takeaway
Pick one small, high-impact control to implement first, measure its effect, and use that success to expand your IGA program.
