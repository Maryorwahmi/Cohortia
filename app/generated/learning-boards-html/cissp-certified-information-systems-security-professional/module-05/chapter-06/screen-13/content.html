Module X › Chapter 5.6 › Common Mistakes

Frequent pitfalls and fixes

Rubber-stamping
Require justification for approvals and surface anomalies first so reviewers focus on risky entitlements.
Unmanaged service accounts
Include service accounts in discovery and vault them to eliminate embedded secrets.
No remediation
Track remediation tasks with SLAs and escalate stale items so attestations lead to actual change.
Too broad an initial scope
Pilot on high-risk systems to demonstrate value before enterprise-wide rollout.
Practical check
For your next review, require the reviewer to comment on any confirmation of elevated access and ensure that any acceptances without comment are flagged for a secondary check.
Key Takeaway
Meaningful governance requires action: detect, certify, remediate, and measure to reduce real risk.
