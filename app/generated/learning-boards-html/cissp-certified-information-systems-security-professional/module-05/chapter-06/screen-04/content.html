Core Concept

IGA: Policies, processes, tools

IGA provides visibility and control across the identity lifecycle, answering who has access, why, and whether it should continue.

What it includes
  • Lifecycle management: provisioning, change, deprovisioning.
  • Governance processes: access reviews, certifications, attestations.
  • Policy enforcement and audit reporting for compliance.
Analogy
Think of IGA as corporate HR for digital access: it sets the rules and keeps the record of who should have which privileges.
Key Takeaway

IGA makes access decisions auditable and aligned with business policy so IAM isn’t just a technical control.

Governance vs. IAM
IAM (technical)
Authentication, authorization, tokens, enforcement points.
IGA (governance)
Policies, lifecycle rules, reviews, audit evidence.
Common misconception
Treating IGA as a one-off cleanup misses its ongoing nature. IGA requires continuous reviews and integration with authoritative data to remain effective.
Concrete example
A company uses IGA to require quarterly attestations for CRM access. Department heads receive an automated list of users and explicitly confirm or revoke access, which then drives automated deprovisioning in connected systems.
