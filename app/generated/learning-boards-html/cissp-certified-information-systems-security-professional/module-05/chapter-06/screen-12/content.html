Module X › Chapter 5.6 › Operationalizing IGA

Turn policy into action

Automate
Provisioning, deprovisioning, and review workflows reduce manual errors.
Integrate
Connect HR and directory services so identity attributes drive access decisions.
Educate
Train owners on reviews, exceptions, and remediation responsibilities.
Phased rollout
Start with critical systems to demonstrate measurable gains before wider rollout.
Metrics to track
Number of orphaned accounts, time-to-remediation after a certification, exception counts, and proportion of privileged accounts in the vault.
Practical tip
Show a simple before-and-after metric from a pilot to secure executive support: for example, orphaned accounts reduced by X% or mean remediation time reduced by Y days.
Key Takeaway
Automation and integration are the practical levers that make governance scalable and sustainable.
