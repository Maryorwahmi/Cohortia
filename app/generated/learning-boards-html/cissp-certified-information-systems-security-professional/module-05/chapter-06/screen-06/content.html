Module X › Chapter 5.6 › Segregation of Duties
Definition

Prevent conflicts by splitting roles

SoD reduces the chance that a single person can initiate, approve, and complete a high-risk transaction. It is an important audit control and a core part of IGA.
Example: Finance
Invoice creation, approval, and payment are separated across three roles to avoid fraud.
Example: IT
Account provisioning separated from privilege granting; admin who creates users cannot give themselves domain admin rights.
Audit perspective
SoD violations are common audit findings. Demonstrating defined conflicting roles and automated enforcement or compensating controls reduces audit risk.
Implementation tips
  • Map critical processes and identify conflict pairs.
  • Enforce at assignment time by blocking conflicting role combinations.
  • Use compensating controls (peer review, approvals) where separation isn't feasible.
Common mistake
Defining SoD rules without operational processes for exceptions or compensating controls leads to business friction and ignored controls.
