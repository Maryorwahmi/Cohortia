Module X › Chapter 5.6 › Key Concepts
IGA
Policies and processes that manage the identity lifecycle and provide auditability for access decisions.
Access Reviews & SoD
Periodic certifications and separation of duties enforce least privilege and prevent conflicts that enable fraud.
Privileged Access Management
Secure discovery, vaulting, JIT access, rotation, and session recording to protect elevated accounts.
Summary Takeaway
Combine governance, certification, separation, and privileged controls to reduce risk, demonstrate compliance, and make access decisions operational.
