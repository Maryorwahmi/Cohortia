Module 5 › Chapter 5.7 › Quick Concepts
Trust Anchor
A root CA key in a client store anchors validation of certificate chains.
Revocation
Use OCSP for timely checks; enable stapling for better client performance and privacy.
Operational Hygiene
Protect private keys, automate renewals, and correctly configure chains to avoid outages.
Final takeaway
Apply these principles when inspecting or operating certificates: verify chain, check status, protect keys, and automate where possible.
