Module 5 › Chapter 5.7 › Certificate Lifecycle
Timeline
  1. Issuance: generate key pair, submit CSR, verify identity, CA signs certificate.
  2. Usage: certificate authenticates and enables encryption or digital signatures for its purpose.
  3. Renewal: obtain a new certificate before expiration to maintain continuous trust.
  4. Revocation: remove trust immediately when keys are compromised or authorization changes.
Concrete example
Automated renewal prevents expired certificates from causing outages; monitoring alerts teams before expiry.
Misconception
Assuming certificates auto-renew without configuration can lead to unexpected downtime.
Key Takeaway
Treat lifecycle as operational: automate issuance and renewal, and plan for immediate revocation when needed.
