Certificate inspection checklist
  • Issued To: confirm the common name or subject alternative names match the endpoint you expect.
  • Issued By: identify the CA and check whether the browser or OS trusted store contains its root.
  • Valid From / To: check expiration to avoid outages and warnings.
  • Serial Number: useful for revocation investigations and CRL lookups.
  • Public Key Algorithm and Size: ensure it meets your policy for strength.
Concrete example
Inspect a browser's server certificate to list the Issued To, Issued By, validity, serial number, and public key algorithm as part of your audit.
Why this matters
Quick certificate checks catch expired certificates and mismatched names that otherwise cause user-visible errors or allow impersonation.
