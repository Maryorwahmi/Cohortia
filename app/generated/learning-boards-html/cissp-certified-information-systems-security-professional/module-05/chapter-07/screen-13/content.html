Module 5 › Chapter 5.7 › Key Concepts
Public Key Infrastructure
A framework of policies, authorities, and certificates that enables public-key trust.
Asymmetric & Symmetric
Asymmetric keys solve distribution and authentication; symmetric keys are used for efficient session encryption.
Revocation & OCSP
CRLs publish lists of revoked certificates; OCSP gives real-time checks and stapling improves performance and privacy.
Summary takeaway
PKI combines cryptography and trusted authorities with operational policies. Inspect certificates, protect private keys, and use timely revocation to maintain trust.
