Anatomy

Essential certificate fields

  • Public key: the key bound to the identity for encryption or verification.
  • Subject identity: common name or alternative names identifying the holder.
  • Issuer: the CA that signed the certificate.
  • Validity period: start and end dates that constrain use.
  • Serial number: unique identifier used in revocation lists.
  • CA signature: cryptographic assurance of origin and integrity.
Concrete checklist
When inspecting a certificate, verify the subject name, issuer, validity period, serial number, and signature. If any element mismatches, the certificate may not be trustworthy.
Common mistake
Ignoring subject alternative names or using a certificate that does not include all required hostnames.
Key Takeaway

Knowing the fields helps you confirm identity, detect misconfiguration, and identify items to check for revocation.
