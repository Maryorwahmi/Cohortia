Core Concept

Verifying claims

Authentication confirms that the party presenting an identifier is who they say they are by checking one or more factors.

Something you know: passwords, PINs. Easy but often weakest.
Something you have: hardware token, mobile authenticator, smart card.
Something you are: biometrics — convenient, but sensitive and less replaceable.
Key Takeaway

MFA combines factor types to strengthen assurance; choose enforcement based on risk.

Authentication decision guide
Low risk
Single-factor password, periodic re-authentication.
Medium risk
Password + mobile authenticator or hardware token.
High risk
MFA with separate factor categories; consider hardware tokens for admins.
Misconception
Two passwords or two codes from the same source do not provide the same assurance as combining distinct factor categories.
