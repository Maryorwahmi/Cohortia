Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Identity & IAM
Framework of policies and tech that ensures the right users have proper access.
Authentication & MFA
Combine factor categories to improve assurance and reduce credential risk.
Authorization
RBAC or ABAC enforce what an authenticated identity may do; design for least privilege.
Accountability: logs, SIEM, and retention
Privilege management: reviews, time-bound access
Lifecycle: automated provisioning and prompt deprovisioning
Summary

Treat IAM as an integrated program: unique IDs, strong authentication, scoped authorization, and auditable logs together reduce risk and support operations.
