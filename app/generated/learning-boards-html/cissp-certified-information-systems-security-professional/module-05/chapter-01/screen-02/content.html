Learning Objectives

Skills you will gain

These objectives move from definition to practical design and review. Each objective includes a small mnemonic to help you remember the task.

01
Define IAM
Explain IAM and why identity controls are central to enterprise security.
Mnemonic: Pillars diagram (policy, provisioning, enforcement)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Distinguish the four A's
Identify and explain identification, authentication, authorization, and accountability.
Mnemonic: Four-step checkpoint
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design authentication
Choose factors and require MFA where risk warrants it.
Mnemonic: Something you know/have/are
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Ensure accountability
Define logging, retention, and review to trace actions and support forensics.
Mnemonic: Record, review, respond
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Bloom's progression
Learn
Understand
Apply
Master
We move from learning definitions to applying them in a hands-on policy task, then to mastering review and monitoring techniques that sustain security over time.
How to use these objectives
  • Refer back when building an IAM policy or runbook.
  • Use the milestones as checkpoints during design reviews.
  • Practice the hands-on task to cement the concepts.
