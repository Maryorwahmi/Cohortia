Identity and Access Management

Learn the four A's that secure digital identities: Identification, Authentication, Authorization, and Accountability.

Duration: 12 min explainer

IAM is more than a login page. It is a lifecycle and control framework that ensures every access decision is traceable, reversible, and aligned with business needs. Effective IAM reduces breach risk, enforces least privilege, and supports compliance reporting.

As you progress, you'll practice defining identities, selecting authentication factors, assigning scoped permissions, and designing logging for accountability. Expect real-world examples and a short hands-on policy design task.
