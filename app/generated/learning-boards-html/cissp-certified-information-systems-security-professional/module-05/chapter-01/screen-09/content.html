Process

Stopping rights from growing

  1. Detect: Inventory role memberships and permissions regularly.
  2. Review: Managers attest assignments on a schedule.
  3. Remediate: Revoke or reduce permissions that exceed job needs.
  4. Automate: Enforce expiry on temporary access and use policy checks for new grants.
Example: After a project ends, temporary elevated access is automatically revoked and the team lead confirms no further access is needed.
Practical controls
Access reviews
Quarterly attestations by managers.
Time-bound roles
Temporary elevation that expires automatically.
Separation of duties
Preventing single-person control over critical operations.
Audit trails
Use logs to verify who approved and when changes occurred.
Common mistake: letting temporary rights become permanent due to manual deprovisioning delays.
