Why invest

Benefits of IAM

  • Security: reduced unauthorized access and blast radius.
  • Compliance: auditable trails for regulatory reporting.
  • Operational: faster onboarding and consistent policies.
Challenges

Common obstacles

  • Integration with legacy applications lacking modern APIs.
  • Balancing strict controls with user productivity and adoption.
  • Maintaining accurate roles and avoiding privilege creep.
Measure success with provisioning time, number of excessive privileges, and speed of deprovisioning after termination.
