Clean-up responsibilities

Removing all artifacts is essential to preserve system integrity. Clean-up reduces risk of accidental misuse and supports auditability.

Actions
  • Delete temporary files and scripts.
  • Remove created accounts and scheduled tasks.
  • Uninstall payloads and tools installed for testing.
Verification

Document the removal steps and provide evidence that the environment was returned to baseline.

ROE reminder

The Rules of Engagement should specify who is responsible for clean-up, and any timing or reporting constraints for rollback activities.

Key Takeaway

Restoring systems and documenting removal is part of professional, ethical testing and must be planned in the ROE.
