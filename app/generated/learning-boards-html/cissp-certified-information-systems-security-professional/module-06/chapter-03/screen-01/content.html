Penetration Testing Techniques

Simulate attacks to validate exploitability, build an exploit chain, and deliver prioritized remediation backed by evidence.

Terminal Demo Mode
In this lesson

Understand pen-test phases, approaches, common tools, and legal guardrails so you can plan and evaluate tests responsibly.

Why it matters

A good pen test gives evidence of impact and helps prioritize fixes that reduce real attacker success, not just theoretical risk lists.
