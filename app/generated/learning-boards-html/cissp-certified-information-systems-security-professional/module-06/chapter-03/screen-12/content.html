Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Rules of Engagement
ROE must include
  • Clear scope: in-scope and out-of-scope systems and networks.
  • Objectives and allowed techniques, including social engineering or DOS allowances.
  • Duration, working hours, and emergency contact details for incidents.
  • Clean-up and artifact removal requirements and reporting format.
Common mistake

Starting work without a signed ROE or with unclear emergency contacts, exposing both testers and the client to legal risk.

Key Takeaway

A signed, detailed ROE is the legal and operational foundation of any penetration test.
