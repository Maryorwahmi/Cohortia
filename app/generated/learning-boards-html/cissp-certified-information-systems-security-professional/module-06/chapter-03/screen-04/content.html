Penetration testing defined

A simulated, authorized attack that attempts exploitation to demonstrate impact.

Why exploit rather than just scan?
  • Exploitation shows actual attacker capability and impact.
  • Remediation priorities shift when exploits are proven.
  • Scans are frequent; pen tests are deeper, evidence-focused evaluations.
Example
A scanner lists an open FTP port; a pen tester exploits an unpatched FTP daemon to change files and prove integrity risk.
Misconception
Pen tests do not replace continuous scanning; they complement it by validating what an attacker can achieve.
Key Takeaway

Penetration testing produces evidence of real exploitability and should be run under a signed, scoped agreement.

Quick comparison
Vulnerability Scan
Automated discovery of potential weaknesses, low intrusiveness, frequent.
Penetration Test
Active exploitation to prove impact, evidence-rich, performed under authorization.
