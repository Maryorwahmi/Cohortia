Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Reporting
Executive summary

High-level impact and prioritized recommendations for decision-makers.

Technical findings

Detailed evidence, reproduction steps, and tools/commands used for each issue.

Remediation

Actionable fixes prioritized by risk reduction and expected effort.

Evidence

Include raw outputs, timestamps, and command lines so engineers can reproduce and validate findings.

Limitations

Document test boundaries and any factors that may have constrained results.

Key Takeaway

A clear, evidence-backed report is the testing product that drives remediation and risk reduction.
