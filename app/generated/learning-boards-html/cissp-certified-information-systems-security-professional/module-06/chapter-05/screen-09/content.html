Centralized log architecture
Collect, parse, store, and analyze in a central system to enable correlation and retention.
Collection
Agents, syslog, or collector pipelines forward events reliably and securely.
Parsing & Indexing
Normalize fields for fast search and correlation across sources.
Storage
Retention policies and hardened storage protect evidence for audits.
Alerting
Rule-based alerts and correlation reduce manual work and speed detection.
Benefits
  • Unified view for incident correlation.
  • Automated, consistent alerting across sources.
  • Efficient forensic searches and regulatory retention.
Key Takeaway

Centralization is about speed, context, and preserving evidence. It amplifies your detection and response capabilities when combined with good detection rules.
