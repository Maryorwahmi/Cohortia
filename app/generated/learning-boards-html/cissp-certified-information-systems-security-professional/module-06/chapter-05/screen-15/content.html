Key Concepts — Part 2
Remember the trade-offs and operating guidance.
IDS
Monitors and alerts on suspicious activity without blocking.
IPS
Can actively block traffic but must be tuned to avoid interruptions.
Detection methods
Signature-based for known threats; anomaly-based for novel behavior but with more false positives.
Final summary

Collect relevant logs, centralize for correlation, tune detection, and enable prevention only after careful testing to balance security and availability.
