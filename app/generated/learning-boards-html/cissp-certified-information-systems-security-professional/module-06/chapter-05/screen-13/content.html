Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Monitoring Playbook
A simple monitoring playbook
Concrete priorities to improve detection and investigation speed.
1
Prioritize assets and ensure logging coverage.
2
Centralize logs with parsing and indexing.
3
Deploy detection rules and baseline behavior.
4
Tune IPS in detect-only mode, then pilot prevention.
5
Document playbooks and train responders.
Key Takeaway

A focused, repeatable playbook applied to critical assets yields faster detection and more reliable investigations than attempting broad coverage all at once.
