Security Assessment — definition
A holistic evaluation combining technical scans, policy review, and risk analysis.
Includes technical reviews, policy reviews, and process checks to form a consolidated risk view.
Example scenario
Review incident response plans, confirm physical controls, and run non-intrusive scans of the internal network to identify known vulnerabilities.
Common misconception
An assessment is not only a policy checklist nor only scanning; it must connect technical findings to governance and process implications.
Key Takeaway

Assessments produce consolidated evidence to prioritize remediation and inform risk decisions.

Assessment Components
Technical review
Vulnerability scans, configuration reviews, and technical control validation.
Policy and process review
Evaluate whether documented procedures exist and are followed in practice.
Risk analysis
Estimate impact and likelihood to prioritize remediation actions.
Analogy: A health check that collects lab results, a medical history, and a physical exam to produce a treatment plan.
