Legal and ethical duties
Obtain written authorization, avoid unauthorized testing, and protect sensitive evidence discovered during assessments.
Unauthorized testing can be perceived as an attack and may lead to legal charges or business disruption.
Ethical duty: do no unnecessary harm and limit disclosure of sensitive findings to authorized personnel only.
Key Takeaway

Professional testing requires authorization, harm-minimization, secure handling of findings, and clear communication with stakeholders.

Practical checklist
Confirm signatories and authorization scope before starting testing.
Ensure an emergency contact and rollback plan are available for the testing window.
Securely store and transmit vulnerability evidence; limit distribution to those who must remediate or report.
