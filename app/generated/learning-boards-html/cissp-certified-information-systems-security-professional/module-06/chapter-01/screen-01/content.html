Fundamentals of Security Assessment and Testing
Understand the purpose of assessments, hands-on testing, and formal audits, and how each feeds risk decisions.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson
Distinguish assessment, testing, and auditing; learn ROE; connect outputs to risk management.
Coming up
Types of tests, legal safeguards, and a hands-on ROE exercise for an internal scan.
Why it matters
Evidence from assessments drives prioritized remediation and demonstrates due diligence to stakeholders.
Assessment
Broad posture review
Technical checks plus policy and process reviews to identify weaknesses across domains.
Testing
Hands-on technical probes
Active scans and simulated attacks to find exploitable flaws.
Audit
Formal compliance verification
Independent review against standards or regulatory controls to confirm implementation and effectiveness.
Key Takeaway

Use the right activity for the right question: posture insight, exploitability, or compliance. Always obtain written authorization before active testing.

Visual summary
