Objective 05 — Translate findings
Learn to write remediation-ready findings with severity, impact, and steps to fix or mitigate.
Why this matters
Leadership needs concise decisions: accept, mitigate, transfer, or avoid. Clear findings enable those choices.
Verification step
Produce a one-page findings summary for a simple internal scan.
Objective 06 — Safe testing practices
Draft an ROE snippet, including scope, out-of-scope systems, permitted hours, and communication protocol.
Common mistake: vague scope that accidentally includes production databases. Explicitly list IPs and hosts to exclude.
Practice step: define permitted testing window and emergency contact for any unexpected impact.
