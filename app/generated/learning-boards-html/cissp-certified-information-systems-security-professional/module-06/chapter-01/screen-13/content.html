Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › From Findings to Risk
Turning findings into decisions
Analyze impact and likelihood, then choose a risk response and assign remediation ownership and verification steps.
Decision options
Accept, Mitigate, Transfer, or Avoid — choose based on business impact and the organization's risk appetite.
Prioritization tip: Remediate actions that most reduce likelihood of high-impact events first.
Key Takeaway

Assessment outputs are inputs to risk management; translate technical detail into business impact to prioritize action.

Example prioritization
A critical vulnerability on a payment system: high impact, high priority mitigation. A minor info disclosure on a non-production host: lower priority or scheduled remediation.
Verification
After remediation, validate fixes with re-scans, retests, or evidence collection and update the risk register.
