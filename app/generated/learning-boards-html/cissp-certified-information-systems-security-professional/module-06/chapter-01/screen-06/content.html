Security Audit — what it does
Verifies controls against standards or regulatory criteria and issues formal findings and an opinion.
Focuses on governance, documentation, control design, and operational evidence rather than exploitability alone.
Example
A compliance audit confirming that required controls in a payment environment are implemented according to the standard.
Misconception
An audit does not prove a system is free of exploitable vulnerabilities; it proves controls meet defined requirements.
Audit Evidence
Configuration records and logs demonstrating the control operation.
Policies, process documents, and training records showing governance and process alignment.
Interview notes or observation evidence confirming controls are followed in practice.
Key Takeaway

Audits provide a compliance opinion and require documented operational evidence, not just the presence of controls.
