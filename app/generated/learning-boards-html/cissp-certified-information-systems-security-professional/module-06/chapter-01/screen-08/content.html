Why ROE matters
ROE prevents legal exposure and operational harm by defining clear testing boundaries and responsibilities.
ROE checklist
  • Explicit scope: IP ranges and hosts.
  • Out-of-scope: critical systems and services to exclude.
  • Permitted hours and time zone.
  • Permitted techniques and prohibited actions like DoS.
  • Reporting timeline and emergency contacts.
Legal note: never perform intrusive testing without documented authorization and an agreed rollback plan.
Practical snippet
---
Assessment Type: Internal Vulnerability Scan
Scope: 192.168.1.0/24
Out-of-scope: Production DB server 192.168.1.10, HR app server 192.168.1.15
Permitted Hours: Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM local time
DoS Testing: Prohibited
Social Engineering: Prohibited
Reporting: Initial findings due 3 days after scan completion
Emergency Contact: IT Manager (name/phone)
---
Common mistake
Being vague about assets or permitted techniques — always list explicit IPs and hosts to include or exclude.
