Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Compare
Assessment
Purpose
Holistic posture evaluation and prioritized recommendations.
Actors: internal teams, consultants.
Testing
Purpose
Find and sometimes exploit technical vulnerabilities to demonstrate impact.
Actors: security testers, red teams.
Audit
Purpose
Independent verification of control existence and effectiveness against a criterion.
Actors: auditors, compliance teams.
Reporting differences
Label findings clearly by whether they show exploitability, policy gaps, or non-conformance. Each audience expects different evidence.
Key Takeaway

Use the right activity for the question you need answered and ensure reports match the audience’s decision needs.
