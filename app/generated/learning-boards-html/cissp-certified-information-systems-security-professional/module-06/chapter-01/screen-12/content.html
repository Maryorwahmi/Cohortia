Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Penetration Testing
Penetration test overview
Planned simulation of attacks to demonstrate exploit chains and business impact.
Use cases: validate critical systems, prove impact to stakeholders, test incident response during simulated compromise.
Risk: intrusive actions can cause outages. Always include a rollback plan and emergency contact in the ROE.
Key Takeaway

Pen tests provide strong evidence of impact but must be planned, scoped, and authorized to avoid harm.

Deliverables
Attack narrative and evidence showing how vulnerabilities were chained and what was accessed.
Reproducible steps and remediation recommendations prioritized by business impact.
Post-test debrief and validation to confirm fixes were applied correctly.
