Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Quick Reference
1. Match activity to question
Choose assessment, test, or audit depending on whether you need posture, exploitability, or compliance evidence.
2. Authorize testing
Use a Rules of Engagement to define scope, exclusions, permitted hours, and emergency contacts before testing.
3. Convert findings to risk
Estimate impact and likelihood to prioritize actions, assign owners, and verify remediation.
Summary Takeaway

Assessment, testing, and auditing together create evidence for better risk decisions — plan, authorize, validate, and verify.
