Definition
Manual Code Review
Human examination of source to find complex and business logic flaws.
  • Find logic paths and decision flaws that tools miss.
  • Check authentication, authorization, and cryptography usage.
  • Use focused checklists and prioritize high-risk modules.
Key Takeaway

Apply manual review strategically to high-risk areas and use findings to improve automated rules and training.

Scenario — business logic bypass
A reviewer traces a payment flow and discovers a missing authorization check when a particular flag is set. The fix requires adding server-side validation and regression tests. This kind of flaw is often invisible to automated scanners but critical in practice.
Benefit
Detects complex threats and improves secure design practices.
Best practice
Use checklists, pair reviews, and focus on modules with high privilege or sensitive data.
