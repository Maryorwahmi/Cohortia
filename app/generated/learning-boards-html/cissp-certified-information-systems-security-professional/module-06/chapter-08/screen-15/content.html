DAST
Tests the running app and validates exploitability for authentication, session, and runtime issues.
SCA
Inventory dependencies, detect known CVEs, and prioritize dependency upgrades.
Fuzzing
Feed malformed inputs to discover crashes and memory corruption in critical parsers.
Final Takeaway
No single tool solves application security. Build a layered approach that shifts left, validates at runtime, inventories dependencies, and uses humans to verify complex logic.
