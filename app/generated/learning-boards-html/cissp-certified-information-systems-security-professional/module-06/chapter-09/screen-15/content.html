Module 7 › Chapter 6.9 › Key Concepts
Shared Responsibility Model
A division of security duties between provider and customer that dictates assessment scope.
CSPM
Tools that continuously assess cloud configurations and detect drift or exposure.
Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
A HIPAA-required contract ensuring a vendor handling PHI follows required safeguards and reporting obligations.
Final takeaway
Operationalize these concepts through your onboarding, contracts, and continuous monitoring so vendor assurance remains measurable and current.
