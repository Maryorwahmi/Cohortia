Module 7 › Chapter 6.9 › Key Concepts
Third-Party Risk
Risk introduced by external vendors that can affect your data, operations, or reputation.
Third-Party Security Assessment
Collecting and evaluating evidence about a vendor's controls and operations.
Security Questionnaire
A self-attested set of questions used for initial vendor screening and low-risk checks.
Summary
Use this trio of concepts to classify vendors, choose evidence, and explain residual risk to stakeholders.
