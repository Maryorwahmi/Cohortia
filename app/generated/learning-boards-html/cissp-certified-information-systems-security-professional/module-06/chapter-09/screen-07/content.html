Module 7 › Chapter 6.9 › Type 1 vs Type 2
Type 1

Design suitability at a point in time. Useful for initial vendor review but limited as evidence of ongoing operations.

Strength
Confirms controls are designed to meet trust criteria.
Type 2

Design and operating effectiveness over a period. Auditors test that controls were followed consistently, providing stronger assurance.

Strength
Evidence that controls functioned during the audit window.
Key Takeaway

Prefer Type 2 when you need assurance that controls operated effectively over time, especially for sensitive or critical services.
