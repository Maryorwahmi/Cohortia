Module 7 › Chapter 6.9 › Compliance Impact
GDPR
Processors must provide sufficient guarantees of appropriate measures. Expect contractual clauses and documented technical controls.
Practical ask
Data processing agreements, evidence of encryption, and access control practices tied to personal data handling.
HIPAA & PCI DSS
HIPAA requires BAAs and assessments for business associates. PCI requires validation and controls for entities handling card data.
Practical ask
Signed BAAs, PCI attestation of compliance, and evidence of encryption, logging, and segmentation controls.
Key Takeaway

Map each vendor to applicable regulations and request evidence and contract language that satisfy those specific obligations.
