Module 7 › Chapter 6.9 › Vendor Playbook
Playbook steps
1. Classify
Data, systems, and regulation impact.
2. Evidence
Questionnaire, SOC, pen test, on-site as needed.
3. Contract
Audit rights, incident notifications, remediation SLAs.
4. Monitor
CSPM, external monitoring, and periodic reassessment.
Key Takeaway

A clear, risk-based playbook reduces ad hoc decisions and ensures vendor assurance matches potential impact.
