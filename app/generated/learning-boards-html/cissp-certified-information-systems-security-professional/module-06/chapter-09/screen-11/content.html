Module 7 › Chapter 6.9 › SOC 2 Evaluation
Given SOC 2 Type 2 summary (excerpt)
Audit period: Jan 1–Jun 30. Trust criteria: Security, Availability. Management asserts logical access and encryption controls were suitable and operating effectively. Auditor exceptions: two privileged access approvals without documented secondary approval; IR plan outdated and lacking cloud specifics.
Strengths
  • Logical access controls tested and operating.
  • Encryption at rest and in transit effective during period.
Weaknesses
  • Privileged access control violations need remediation evidence.
  • Outdated incident response plan missing cloud incident scenarios.
Follow-up actions
  • Request remediation evidence and verify with follow-up testing or controls logs.
  • Require vendor to update IR plan for cloud incidents and provide proof of tabletop exercises.
  • Decide on compensating controls or temporary restrictions until remediation is validated.
Why Type 2 matters
Type 2 is preferred because it verifies that controls operated effectively during the audit period rather than only confirming design suitability at one point in time.
