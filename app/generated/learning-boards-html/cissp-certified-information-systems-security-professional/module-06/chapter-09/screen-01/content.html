Third-Party Security Assessments and Compliance
Understand vendor assurance methods, independent evidence, cloud challenges, and compliance impact.
In this lesson

Why third-party risk matters, how to assess vendors, interpreting SOC reports, cloud-specific assessment needs, and regulatory obligations.

Why it matters

A vendor breach can expose your data and reputation. Proper assessments reduce residual risk and inform contractual protections.

Quick checklist
  • Request SOC reports or ISO certificates for critical vendors.
  • Use questionnaires for low-risk vendors with follow-up validations.
  • For cloud services, verify shared responsibility and monitor configurations.
  • Embed audit rights and remediation obligations in contracts.
Chain metaphor
A single weak vendor link can compromise the entire chain. Assess links appropriately.
Assessments range from self-attested questionnaires to on-site audits and independent reports like SOC.
Explore Key Topics
Assessment Methods
Questionnaires, audits, audit reports, and monitoring, chosen by vendor risk and criticality.
SOC Reports
SOC 2 Type 2 gives operational evidence over time and is often preferred for security assurance.
Cloud Responsibilities
Shared responsibility means you must secure what you control; CSPM helps with continuous checks.
