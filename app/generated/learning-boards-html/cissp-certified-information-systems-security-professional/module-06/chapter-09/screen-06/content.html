Module 7 › Chapter 6.9 › SOC Reports
SOC 1
Controls related to financial reporting — relevant to auditors and finance teams.
Use when
Vendor processes or influences transactions that affect your financial statements.
Example
Payroll processors or vendors that handle accounting reconciliation.
SOC 2
Focused on security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy — most relevant for cybersecurity assessments.
Type 1 vs Type 2
Type 1 describes design suitability at a point in time. Type 2 also reports operating effectiveness over a period, offering stronger assurance.
Example
A cloud storage vendor's SOC 2 Type 2 shows controls were tested and operated effectively for the audit period.
SOC 3
A public summary of SOC 2 intended for general audiences; it lacks the detail required for security assessments and does not replace a SOC 2 Type 2 report.
