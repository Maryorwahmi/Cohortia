Module 7 › Chapter 6.9 › Contracts & Audit Rights
Essential contract elements
  • Security requirements and control baselines to be maintained.
  • Evidence delivery: frequency and document types (e.g., SOC 2 Type 2).
  • Audit rights and on-site inspection conditions, with confidentiality protections.
  • Incident notification timelines and remediation SLAs.
  • Termination or compensation clauses for unresolved critical findings.
Example clauses
Delivery of SOC 2 Type 2 within 90 days of audit completion; 30-day notification for incidents affecting customer data; remediation plan with milestones and evidence of completion.
Key Takeaway

Negotiate concrete evidence and remediation rights into contracts so assessment findings can be validated and enforced.
