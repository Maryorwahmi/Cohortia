Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Operational Challenges
Top challenges
  • Cost: licensing, storage, and staffing.
  • Complexity: onboarding and normalization work.
  • Data volume: scaling ingestion and retention.
  • False positives: alert fatigue without tuning.
  • Skill gap: need for trained analysts and tuning expertise.
Mitigations
Phase onboarding and prioritize high-value logs first.
Use tiered storage and aggregation to control costs.
Establish a tuning cadence and feedback loop with analysts.
Consider co-managed or managed SIEM if staff are constrained.
Key Takeaway
Treat SIEM as an operational service: budget people, time for tuning, and technical debt — not just a one-time product purchase.
