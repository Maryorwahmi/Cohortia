Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Correlation Rule Example
Rule narrative
Detect a sequence where a user accesses a sensitive file, attempts a transfer blocked or allowed by DLP, and an outbound connection to a suspicious external destination occurs within five minutes from the same host. Exclude approved roles and known business transfers to reduce false positives.
Common tuning levers
  • Time window (e.g., 5 minutes)
  • Count thresholds for repeated actions
  • Allow-listing of business cloud domains and approved users
Pseudocode example
IF EDR.Event = File_Access AND File.Classification = Sensitive
AND DLP.Event = Sensitive_Data_Transfer_Attempt AND DLP.User = EDR.User
AND Firewall.Event = Outbound_Connection AND Firewall.SourceIP = EDR.HostIP
AND TimeWindow <= 5 minutes
AND User.Role NOT IN (approved_roles)
THEN Alert = "Potential Insider Data Exfiltration"; Severity = High; CreateIncidentTicket();
Treat this as a template: map your actual field names when implementing in your SIEM and validate against historical logs before enabling automated blocks.
