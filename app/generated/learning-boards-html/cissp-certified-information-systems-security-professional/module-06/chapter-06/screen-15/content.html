Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Tuning & Maintenance
Rule lifecycle
  1. Draft and test rules on historical data.
  2. Deploy in monitoring mode to observe noise.
  3. Tune thresholds, add context, then enable alerting.
  4. Review and retire rules that are no longer useful.
Analyst feedback loop
Use triage results to refine rules and enrich data with asset and identity context.
Retention & tiers
Hot: recent logs for fast search.
Warm: less frequent queries, medium cost.
Cold: long-term retention for compliance.
Key Takeaway

Make tuning an ongoing process and balance retention against cost and regulatory needs so the SIEM stays effective and sustainable.
