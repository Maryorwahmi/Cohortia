Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › SIEM Use Cases
Threat Detection
Correlate network, endpoint and application logs to find APTs, malware, and insider threats.
Incident Response
Centralized timelines and stored logs speed investigation and scope identification.
Compliance Reporting
Automate reports for PCI DSS, HIPAA and other controls using retained logs and dashboards.
Vulnerability Management
Correlate vulnerability scans with event evidence to prioritize exploited vulnerabilities.
SOC Enablement
Provide analysts with an organized view, playbooks, and ticketing integration for operational response.
Operational Insight
Trend analysis and dashboards reveal persistent gaps and improvements over time.
