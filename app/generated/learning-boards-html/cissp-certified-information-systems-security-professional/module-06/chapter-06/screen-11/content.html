Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › SIEM Architecture
Component roles
  • Log Collectors/Agents: Reliable ingestion and secure forwarding.
  • Event Processors: Aggregate, normalize, filter.
  • Correlation Engine: Apply rules to find incidents.
  • Database/Storage: Hot and cold tiers for retention and forensics.
  • Console/UI: Dashboards, alert triage, reporting.
Design considerations
Scale processors to ingestion rates and use storage tiers to balance cost and access speed.
Place collectors near data sources where possible to reduce network reliability issues.
Design for high availability and plan for logging continuity during outages.
Common mistake
Selecting a SIEM without sizing for your peak ingestion leads to performance bottlenecks and missed events.
