Security Information and Event Management
Centralize logs, reduce noise, and reveal multi-source attack paths.
In this lesson

You will see how collection, aggregation, normalization, and correlation turn raw logs into alerts and reports that SOC teams rely on.

Why it matters

A well-tuned SIEM reduces investigation time and helps prove compliance, while a poorly tuned one creates alert fatigue.

Connecting the pieces
Imagine event streams flowing into collectors, being standardized by normalization, then correlated into meaningful incidents. That flow is what turns volume into signal.
Quick start tip
Start by feeding authentication logs and critical asset logs first. These often reveal early indicators of compromise and give immediate value.
