Vulnerability Assessment Methodologies
Discover, prioritize, and act on security weaknesses before attackers do.

Vulnerability assessments list known security flaws and rank them by severity so teams can make informed remediation decisions. Regular scans close the window of exposure as new vulnerabilities appear daily.

We will study credentialed vs non-credentialed scans, common tools like Nessus and OpenVAS, interpreting reports, and building a remediation plan that balances CVSS with business impact.

In this lesson
Understand scan types, run practical scans, analyze reports, and create prioritized remediations.

Scan Types: credentialed for deep internal checks, non-credentialed to simulate external attackers.
Tools: Nessus for commercial workflows, OpenVAS for open-source scanning options; both support credentialed scans.
Remediation: combine CVSS scores with business impact to prioritize and verify fixes with re-scans.
