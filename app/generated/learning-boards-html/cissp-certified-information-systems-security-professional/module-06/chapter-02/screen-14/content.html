What to Remember
Vulnerability Assessment
Identify and rank known weaknesses so you can prioritize remediation.
Credentialed vs Non
Credentialed scans see internal details; non-credentialed scans simulate outsider views.
Prioritize & Verify
Use severity, exploitability, and impact; verify results before remediation and re-scan after fixes.
Summary: Regularly scan, verify high-impact findings manually, prioritize by business risk, and confirm fixes with re-scans.
