Core ConceptDefinition
What a vulnerability assessment is
A process that identifies, quantifies, and ranks known vulnerabilities so teams can plan remediation.
Discovery & Inventory: produce a list of known issues and affected assets.
Prioritization: provide severity-based guidance for remediation scheduling.
Verification loop: re-scan after fixes to confirm mitigation.
Common misconception: assessments prove security. They reveal known flaws but do not test every exploit path or business logic vulnerability.
Analogy
A vulnerability assessment is like an inventory audit for safety hazards: you list hazards, rank them by risk, and schedule fixes. Penetration testing is like a controlled stress test that demonstrates whether hazards can be exploited.
Key Takeaway
Use vulnerability assessments for regular discovery and prioritization, then verify and remediate rapidly.
