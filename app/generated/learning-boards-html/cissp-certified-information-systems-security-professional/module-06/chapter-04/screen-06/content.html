Planning & Scoping

Define objectives, scope, criteria, stakeholders, and deliverables so the audit is measurable and focused.

Step 1
Define objectives
Step 2
Set scope
Step 3
Choose criteria
Step 4
Assign resources
Example scope

Objective: verify least privilege for the financial application. Scope: production application servers, admin accounts, approval logs from the last 12 months.

Planning checklist
  • Identify stakeholders and evidence owners.
  • Document criteria and acceptance thresholds.
  • Define timeline and deliverables.
  • List expected evidence sources and formats.
Common mistake

Leaving criteria vague. Without explicit criteria, evidence evaluation becomes subjective and inconsistent.

Key Takeaway

Clear, measurable scope and criteria are the controls that make an audit reliable and repeatable.
