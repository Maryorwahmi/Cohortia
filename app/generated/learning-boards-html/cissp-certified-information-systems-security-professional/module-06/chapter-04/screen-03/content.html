Practical objectives and evidence skills

Practice the methods auditors use to collect evidence and turn findings into action.

05
Plan fieldwork and collect evidence

Timeline mnemonic: D.I.O.T (Docs, Interviews, Observation, Testing)

Analyze findings and root cause

Classify observations as strengths, control deficiencies, or non-compliance and identify underlying causes.

Mnemonic: Observe → Categorize → Root-cause
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Write clear audit reports

Separate findings from recommendations, include supporting evidence, and highlight priorities for management.

Venn: Evidence + Clarity

Follow-up and remediation tracking

Verify corrective actions are implemented and operating effectively to close the audit loop and drive continuous improvement.

Timeline: Report → Remediate → Re-audit
