Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › What is a Security Audit?
Core Concept

Security Audit

A formal, independent examination that confirms controls meet specific criteria and operate effectively.

Key points
  • Audits compare evidence to criteria: policy, regulation, or standard.
  • Purpose is assurance and improvement, not active exploitation.
  • Findings are classified as observations, control deficiencies, or non-compliance.
Example
Verify quarterly access reviews by reviewing review logs and approvals rather than attempting to circumvent controls.
Key Takeaway

Audits provide evidence-based assurance; they document what works and what needs fixing.

Definition panel
Evidence types
  • Document review (policies, diagrams)
  • Interviews with control owners
  • Observation of processes
  • Technical configuration checks
Common mistake

Treating compliance as equivalent to security. Compliance is a point-in-time check; security requires ongoing assurance and improvement.
