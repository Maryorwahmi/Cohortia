Security Audits and Reviews

Audits confirm that controls match policy, regulatory criteria, and risk appetite. They are governance tools that produce evidence-based findings and specific recommendations for remediation.

In this lesson

You will learn audit types, the lifecycle from planning to follow-up, how to collect evidence, and how to interpret findings so they lead to improvement.

Why it matters

Audits provide documented assurance and a roadmap for fixing control gaps. They support regulators and build stakeholder trust.

Explore Key Topics
Audit Types

Internal, external, compliance, technical, and operational audits serve different assurance needs.

Audit Lifecycle

Planning, fieldwork, analysis, reporting, and follow-up form the audit lifecycle that closes the improvement loop.

Reporting

A clear report separates findings from recommendations and drives remediation tracking.
