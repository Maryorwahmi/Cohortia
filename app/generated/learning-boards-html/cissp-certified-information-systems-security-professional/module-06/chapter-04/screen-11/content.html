Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Follow-up, Remediation, and Continuous Improvement
Follow-up

Track and verify remediation

Assign owners, set dates, and define acceptance criteria so recommendations become sustained controls.

Track
Log owner, status, and target completion date.
Verify
Re-check evidence and operation after remediation.
Improve
Use trends to update controls and processes.
Example

If access reviews repeatedly miss privileged accounts, remediation could include automated review tooling, role redesign, and quarterly audit verification for two cycles.

Pro tip

Define measurable acceptance criteria at the time of recommendation to avoid ambiguity during follow-up.

Key Takeaway

Follow-up is the mechanism that makes audits useful; track, verify, and use lessons learned to improve controls over time.
