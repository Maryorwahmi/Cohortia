Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Audit Reporting
Reporting

Structure and tone

Executive summary, scope, methodology, findings with evidence, recommendations, and follow-up plan.

Executive Summary
Highlight major risks and prioritized actions for leadership.
Appendices
Store raw evidence and technical details that support findings.
Example finding text
Finding: Lack of multi-factor authentication for remote admin access. Evidence: access logs and configuration screenshots. Impact: increased risk of unauthorized access. Recommendation: Implement MFA for all remote administrative accounts within 30 days; owner: IT security; acceptance: MFA verified on 100% of targeted accounts.
Key Takeaway

Make findings evidence-first and recommendations specific, measurable, and assigned so action is straightforward.
