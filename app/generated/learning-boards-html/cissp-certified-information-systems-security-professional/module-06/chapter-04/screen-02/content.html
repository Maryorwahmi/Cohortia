Learning Objectives

Master these skills so you can design, evidence, and act on security audits.

01
Define purpose & scope

Describe why an audit is performed, what it will cover, and which criteria will be used. This helps prevent scope creep and keeps evidence focused.

Venn: goals ∩ criteria

02
Differentiate audit types

Recognize internal vs external, compliance, technical, and operational audits and the assurance each provides.

Mnemonic: I E C T O
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain audit phases

Plan and scope, collect evidence, analyze results, report findings, and follow up to ensure remediation.

Timeline: Plan → Fieldwork → Report
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify standards & frameworks

Map audit criteria to ISO 27001, NIST CSF, PCI DSS, HIPAA, or GDPR and explain their audit relevance.
