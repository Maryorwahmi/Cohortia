Analysis & Evaluation

From evidence to findings

Classify observations, identify root causes, and assess impact to craft prioritized, actionable recommendations.

Finding
What was observed and which criteria it fails.
Root Cause
Why the control failed (process, people, or technology).
Recommendation
Specific corrective action, owner, and timeline.
Example finding and recommendation
Finding: Remote admin accounts lacked multi-factor authentication; evidence: config screens and access logs. Recommendation: Enable MFA on all remote admin accounts within 30 days; owner: IT security.
Common mistake

Writing vague recommendations without a responsible owner or measurable acceptance criteria.

Key Takeaway

Findings must be evidence-based and recommendations must be specific, assigned, and measurable to enable effective follow-up.

Check your understanding

When writing a recommendation, include the action, the owner, and the timeframe — how would you phrase that for a weak password policy?
