Fieldwork

Methods of evidence collection

Use a mix of document review, interviews, observation, and technical sampling to substantiate audit claims.

Document Review
Policies, diagrams, previous reports, and logs.
Interviews
Confirm roles, responsibilities, and practice from people who operate controls.
Observation
Watch processes occur, such as backups or access reviews, to confirm execution.
Technical Sampling
Check configurations, patch levels, and logs on representative systems.
Practical example

For an access review: collect the access policy, the most recent review sign-offs, current access lists, and logs of recent account changes or approvals.

Common mistake

Relying solely on policy documents without confirming that procedures are followed operationally.

Key Takeaway

Triangulate evidence from multiple sources to support reliable findings and reduce ambiguity for remediation owners.
