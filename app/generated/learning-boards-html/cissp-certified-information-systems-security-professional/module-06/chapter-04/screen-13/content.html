Compliance Audit

Focuses on adherence to specific laws, regulations, or standards with mapped evidence.

Technical Audit

Examines technical configurations and implementations, often using sampling and scans.

Audit Finding

The documented observation from analysis; should be distinct from the recommendation.

Final takeaway

Use these quick definitions when reading reports so you can prioritize remediation and track closure effectively.
