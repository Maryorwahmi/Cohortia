Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › What is Incident Response?
Core Concept

Incident Response defined

A coordinated set of actions and roles to minimize damage, collect evidence, and return services to safe operation.

Why it matters

Fast, repeatable response prevents larger outages and reduces legal and reputational exposure.

Common misconception

It's not just technical work; communications and legal are part of a successful response.

Key Takeaway

Treat incident response as an organizational capability: policy, people, and tools working together.

Analogy & Example

Think of response like emergency triage: quickly stabilize critical systems, collect evidence like vital signs, and then plan definitive treatment. Example: a compromised web server is isolated, RAM is captured, disk images are taken, and stakeholders are briefed per a runbook.
