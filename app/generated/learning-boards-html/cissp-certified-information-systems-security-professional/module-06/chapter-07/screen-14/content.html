Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts
Incident Response
A structured approach to limit damage, restore service, and learn from the event.
IR Lifecycle
Six phases guide action: preparation through post-incident activity, used iteratively as needed.
Forensics
Preserve evidence, work on copies, verify integrity with hashes, and document chain of custody.
Summary

Prepare, detect, contain, eradicate, recover, and learn — while preserving evidence and documenting every action.
