Recovery

Bring services back safely

Validate eradication, restore from trusted backups, and monitor closely in phased rollouts.

Phased approach
Prioritize critical systems and bring them online first after validation and testing.
Verification
Confirm backups are clean and vulnerability patches are applied before restoration.
Assessment prompt

During recovery, what is the crucial step before restoring a critical database? Verify root cause eradication and that the backup used for restoration is clean and patched.

Why it matters

Proper recovery prevents immediate re-infection and supports a reliable return to business operations.
