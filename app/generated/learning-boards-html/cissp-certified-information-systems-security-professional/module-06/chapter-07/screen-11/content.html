Post-Incident Activity

Review and improve

Conduct a formal review, document timelines, and update policies and runbooks based on findings.

Documentation
Complete incident timeline, evidence logs, and tool usage records including hashes.
Actions
Update detection rules, patch plans, and runbooks; schedule training and tabletop exercises.
Analogy

Think of lessons learned like a sports team reviewing footage to identify missed coverages and improve future plays. The review must translate observations into concrete drills and rule changes so the same gap cannot be exploited again.

Check your plan

After updates, run tabletop exercises to validate that runbooks, communication, and legal coordination actually work under pressure.
