Process Flow

Six phases

Use these phases as a roadmap during incidents, but expect iteration and concurrent activities.

Preparation
Runbooks, tools, training, logging, backups.
Detection & Analysis
Monitoring, alerts, triage to confirm incidents.
Containment
Isolate affected systems and stop spread.
Process cards
Eradication
Remove malware, patch vulnerabilities, rebuild compromised systems.
Recovery
Validate systems are clean, restore from trusted backups, bring services back in phases.
Post-Incident
Document findings, update runbooks, and train teams based on lessons learned.
Misconception

The lifecycle is not strictly linear; detection and containment often feed each other and require iteration.
