Hierarchy

Roles and responsibilities

Incident Manager
Coordinates response, assigns incident IDs, and communicates with leadership.
Forensic Analyst
Collects evidence, creates images, runs hashes, and analyzes artifacts.
Communication Lead
Manages internal/external messaging and coordinates with PR and customers.
Legal & HR
Advises on legal obligations and personnel actions during investigations.
Exercise

Run a tabletop where each role practices their checklists and communication templates to validate coordination under pressure.
