Module 03 › Chapter 3.5 › MDM vs MAM
MDM
Device-level control: remote wipe, enforced encryption, OS updates, and password policy.
Typical use
Corporate-owned devices or situations requiring full administrative control.
MAM
App-level management: containerization, selective wipe, app policies without controlling the whole device.
Typical use
BYOD where employee privacy must be preserved while protecting corporate data.
Key Takeaway

Choose MDM for corporate control and MAM for app-level protection; combine when you need both device controls and app isolation.
