Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Architecting a Secure Medical Records System
Architecture Sketch

High-level design

Segment data domains, enforce least privilege, use models selective to policy goals, and keep controls usable for clinicians.
Data Segregation
Clinical records, billing, and research are isolated; anonymization transforms data for research access.
Access Controls
RBAC with least privilege and just-in-time elevation for emergency scenarios; automated audits for entitlement changes.
Transactional Integrity
Use Clark-Wilson-style transformation procedures for treatment plan updates and dual approval for critical changes.
Implementation Notes
Monitoring & Revocation
Automate logging and immediate revocation of tokens or roles when assignments change.
Usability with clinicians
Design workflows so security steps do not delay care; use MFA that minimizes friction and fast recovery paths.
Key Takeaway
Compose principles and models for each domain: confidentiality, integrity, and conflict separation, then test for usability and failure behaviors.
