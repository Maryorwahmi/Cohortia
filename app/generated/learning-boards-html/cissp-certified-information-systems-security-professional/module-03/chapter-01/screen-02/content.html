Learning Objectives

Progress from concept to applied architecture decisions using formal models and principles.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate core principles
Name and explain principles like least privilege and economy of mechanism, and why each reduces specific risks.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain formal models
Describe Bell-LaPadula, Biba, Clark-Wilson, and Brewer-Nash and their practical applications.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply to architecture
Map principles and models to roles, data flows, and controls in a conceptual architecture sketch.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify and mitigate mistakes
Recognize over-complication, incomplete mediation, and usability failures and propose practical mitigations.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Objective Mnemonics
Venn: Goals vs Controls
Overlap confidentiality, integrity, and availability to pick targeted controls.
Timeline: Design → Review → Iterate
Architectures need continuous review and adjustment as threats change.
Simple Icon: Lock + Check
Security design combines enforcement and verification steps.
