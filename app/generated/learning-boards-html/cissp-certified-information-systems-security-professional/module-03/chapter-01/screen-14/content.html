Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Scenario-Based Design Task
Task

Design a conceptual architecture

Follow the task steps to map principles to models and identify architecture components that enforce them.

Step 1: Identify 5 critical principles
List and justify each principle in one sentence tied to patient privacy or treatment integrity.
Step 2: Select models
Choose Bell-LaPadula, Clark-Wilson, Brewer-Nash, or Biba where they apply and justify your choices.
Deliverable
A one-page conceptual architecture describing roles, data zones, enforcement points, and failure behaviors.
Hints
Hint 1
Prioritize least privilege and automated reviews for role changes.
Hint 2
Design fail-safe behavior: outages should deny access and alert, not allow permissive fallback.
Common errors to avoid
Overly complex RBAC matrices, missing token revocation, and ignoring usability in clinician workflows.
