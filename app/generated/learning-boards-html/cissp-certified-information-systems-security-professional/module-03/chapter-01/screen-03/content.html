Applied outcomes

Translate principles and models into specific architectural recommendations and mitigations.

Analysis Skill
Read a design, state security goals, and map controls to risks. Produce at least three recommended changes with rationale.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Model Selection
Choose Bell-LaPadula when confidentiality dominates; choose Biba or Clark-Wilson when integrity is essential; choose Brewer-Nash when conflicts of interest exist.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Usability Check
Recommend mitigations that users can follow; avoid policies that force risky workarounds.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Continuous Review
Propose review cadence and metrics to ensure architecture stays aligned with evolving threats and requirements.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic
Key Takeaway

Match model to primary goal: confidentiality models for secrecy, integrity models for correctness, and conflict models for separation between competing data sets.
