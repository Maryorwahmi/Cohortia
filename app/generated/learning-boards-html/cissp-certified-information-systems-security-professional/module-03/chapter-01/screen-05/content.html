Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Applying Least Privilege and Separation of Duties
Process Flow

Design steps

1
Inventory subjects and resources
List users, services, and data domains to understand what must be constrained.
2
Define minimal roles and permissions
Assign only the permissions necessary for a role’s function, avoid broad admin rights.
3
Implement enforcement and transaction controls
Use RBAC, policy engines, or TPs to enforce separation and require approvals when needed.
4
Continuous review and deprovisioning
Automate audits and remove stale privileges to keep the model accurate over time.
Example Scenario
In a hospital, clinicians have role-based access to their patients' records. Critical actions like releasing records for research require a second approval by compliance, enforcing separation of duties.
Supporting Cards
Misconception
Least privilege is not a one-time assignment; it requires regular validation and removal of unused rights.
Pro Tip
Automate entitlement reviews and use just-in-time privilege elevation to reduce standing rights.
Key Takeaway
Combine role design, technical enforcement, and automated reviews to keep least privilege and separation of duties effective.
