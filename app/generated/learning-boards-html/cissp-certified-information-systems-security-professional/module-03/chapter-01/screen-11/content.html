Formal Models

Practical integrity and conflict controls

Clark-Wilson
Enforce integrity with well-formed transactions, transformation procedures, and separation of duties to ensure only authorized programs change critical data.
Brewer-Nash (Chinese Wall)
Dynamically restricts access to prevent conflicts of interest. Access to one firm's data can block access to competitors' data for that user.
Example
In a brokerage, once an analyst accesses private data about Company A, Brewer-Nash rules can prevent them from accessing Company B's confidential data to avoid insider trading risk.
Design Note
Compose models
Apply different models to different domains; combine Clark-Wilson transaction controls with Biba integrity checks when needed.
Misconception
No single model fits all needs; choose the right model for each data domain and combine them prudently.
Key Takeaway
Use Clark-Wilson for transactional integrity and Brewer-Nash for conflict-of-interest separation; compose with other models as required.
