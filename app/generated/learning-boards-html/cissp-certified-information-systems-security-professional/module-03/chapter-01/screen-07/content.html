Definition & Rationale

Make security verifiable

Complete Mediation
Every access must be checked at the time of request. Invalidate caches and tokens after permission changes.
Economy of Mechanism
Simplicity reduces hidden vulnerabilities and makes formal review feasible.
Open Design
Rely on secret keys, not secret designs. Peer review strengthens algorithms and protocols, avoiding security through obscurity.
Example
When a user's role is revoked, token invalidation and immediate access checks prevent continued privilege despite cached decisions.
Checkpoints
Misconception
Obscuring logic gives the illusion of security; only reviewable design leads to robust trust.
Pro Tip
Document decision points and keep core security modules small for easier review and formal testing.
Key Takeaway
Complete mediation plus simple, peer-reviewed mechanisms make enforcement reliable and auditable.
