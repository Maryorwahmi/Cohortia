Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Least Privilege
Grant minimal permissions to reduce attack surface and contain compromise impact.
Model Selection
Choose Bell-LaPadula for confidentiality, Biba or Clark-Wilson for integrity, Brewer-Nash for conflict separation.
Simplicity & Usability
Prefer small, well-reviewed designs and usable controls so policies are followed in practice.
Takeaway

Map business goals to principles and models, keep designs simple and testable, and enforce continuous review to maintain resilient systems.
