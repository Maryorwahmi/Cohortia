Common Failures & Fixes
Compromised CA Key
Fix: revoke all issued certs, publish CRL/OCSP updates, establish a new CA and reissue certificates.
Improper Validation
Fix: enforce chain checks, expiry and revocation; configure fail-closed behavior where feasible.
Insecure Key Storage
Fix: move keys into HSMs or protected keystores; audit access and remove keys from code/repos.
Misconfigured Revocation
Fix: deploy redundant OCSP responders, reduce CRL update windows, and monitor availability.
Key Takeaway

Operational discipline, separation of duties, and automation are the best mitigations against PKI failures. Test incident plans and protect backups.
