Rotation Process
Plan: define rotation frequency and triggers.
Automate: use tools to generate keys, create CSRs, and deploy certificates.
Coordinate: rotate dependent systems and update bundles to avoid failures.
Archive: protect historical keys for legal or decryption needs with strong access controls.
Scenario
A company rotates TLS keys yearly. They automate CSR creation and certificate deployment to avoid downtime. If a certificate is revoked mid-cycle, automated reissuance minimizes service interruption.
Misconception: rotation only changes keys. In reality, it requires updated certificates, distribution, and possibly revocation of the old material.
