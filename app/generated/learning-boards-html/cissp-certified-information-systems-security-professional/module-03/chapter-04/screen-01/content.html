Public Key Infrastructure and Key Management
A practical view of certificates, authorities, revocation, and secure key practices that protect system trust.

This chapter teaches how keys are created, validated, trusted, and retired. You will learn roles and policies that make PKI operable and the operational controls that protect private keys.

In this lesson: definitions, lifecycle, best practices, revocation, and common failures.
