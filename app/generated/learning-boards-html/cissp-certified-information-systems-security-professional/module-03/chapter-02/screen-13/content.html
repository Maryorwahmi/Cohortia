Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Assessment Scenarios
Scenario 1: Protect enterprise signing keys
Requirement: centralized management, tamper resistance, and FIPS-level assurances for signing certificates. Best choice: HSM for centralized high-assurance key management and throughput.
Scenario 2: VM modifies host files
Symptom: guest process affected host files. Likely cause: VM escape exploiting the hypervisor or a privileged management interface. Focus investigation on hypervisor logs and management access controls.
Scoring rubric
Full credit requires naming the component, explaining why it fits the requirement, and identifying at least one verification step you would run.
Reflection prompt
If you had to choose where to focus limited hardening resources, which TCB component or hypervisor control would you prioritize and why?
