Architecture

Hypervisor as the control plane

The hypervisor manages resources for many VMs and mediates guest access to physical hardware. This central role makes it a critical trust anchor in virtualized environments.

VM Escape Risk
A hypervisor vulnerability can allow a guest to access host resources or other guests.
Management Hardening
Use dedicated management networks, strong authentication, and least privilege for hypervisor control planes.
Scenario
A malicious or outdated VM image with a driver exploit tries to trigger a hypervisor flaw. If successful, the attacker could control the host or other VMs. Regular image audits and timely hypervisor patching reduce this risk.
Common mistake
Treating hypervisor management like a general admin portal. Management interfaces should be isolated and have separate credentials and monitoring.
