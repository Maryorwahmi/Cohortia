Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › VM Escape
Concept

VM escape: breach of isolation

When code inside a guest gains privileges outside the VM, reaching the host or other guests, usually by exploiting hypervisor bugs or virtual device drivers.

Attack vector
Exploitable hypervisor flaw, malicious device driver, or compromised management interface.
Impact
Full host compromise, access to multiple tenants, and loss of separation guarantees.
Analogy
Think of the hypervisor as an apartment building manager. A VM escape is like a tenant finding a way to access the manager's office and then all other tenants' apartments.
Prevention checklist
  • Patch hypervisor and virtual drivers promptly.
  • Isolate management networks and require strong authentication.
  • Audit and harden VM images before deployment.
