Learning Objectives

From threat identification to environmental resilience—observable skills you can demonstrate.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify Physical Threats

Recognize unauthorized entry, environmental hazards, and utility failures. Categorization helps prioritize controls based on risk and impact.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe Access Controls

Explain locks, card readers, biometrics and mantraps and where each is appropriate within a layered model.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain Environmental Controls

Select and justify fire suppression, HVAC, UPS, generators, and water detection for critical facilities to protect availability.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design a Physical Security Program

Combine layered controls, environmental resilience, and procedures to avoid single points of failure and human lapses.
