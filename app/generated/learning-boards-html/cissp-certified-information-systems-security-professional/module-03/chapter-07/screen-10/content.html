Environmental Controls

Fire Detection & Suppression

Detection

Smoke detectors (ionization, photoelectric) and air-sampling systems provide early warning tailored to data centers.

Water-based Systems

Sprinklers include wet, dry, pre-action, and deluge. Pre-action reduces accidental discharge risk and is preferred where water damage is a concern.

Clean Agent Gases

FM-200 and Novec 1230 suppress fires without residue, making them suitable for electronic equipment when human safety protocols are followed.

Decision Guidance
Example Selection

For a server hall, pair air-sampling detection with a clean agent system to minimize equipment damage and detect issues early.

Safety Note

CO2 systems are hazardous to people; use only with strict safety interlocks and evacuation procedures.

Key Takeaway

Prefer clean agent suppression and early-detection systems in data centers to protect equipment and availability.
