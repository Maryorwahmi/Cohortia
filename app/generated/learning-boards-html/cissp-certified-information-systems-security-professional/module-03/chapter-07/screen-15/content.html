Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Key Concepts
Layered Defense
Multiple complementary barriers from perimeter to interior reduce single-point exposure.
Access Controls
Locks, card readers, biometrics, mantraps, and guards limit and log entry to sensitive areas.
Environmental Controls
Fire suppression, HVAC, UPS/generators, and water detection maintain equipment integrity and availability.
Summary Takeaway

Combine layered controls with environmental resilience and strong operational processes to defend facilities and maintain uptime.
