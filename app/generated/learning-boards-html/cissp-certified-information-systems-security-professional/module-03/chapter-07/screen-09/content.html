Data Center

Server Room Controls

Restricted Zones & Cages

Physical separation reduces cross-tenant access and improves accountability for shared facilities.

Visitor Control

Require sign-in, escort, time-limited badges, and logging to maintain traceability of all access events.

Asset Tagging

Label equipment for inventory and theft detection; integrate with monitoring to spot unexpected movement.

Example & Scenario
Scenario

A server scheduled for maintenance is moved without a ticket. Asset tags trigger an alert and CCTV timestamps show who moved it. Proper visitor logs and escort records speed investigation.

Key Takeaway

Treat server rooms as high-assurance zones with strict access, tracking, and environmental controls.
