Building Interior

Access Control Technologies

Locks & Electronic Readers

Mechanical locks suit low-risk areas. Electronic readers allow logging and revocation—important for audits and rapid deprovisioning.

Biometrics

High assurance, but require privacy, liveness detection, and fallback methods to avoid lockouts or spoofing.

Mantraps & Guards

Mantraps prevent tailgating; guards provide human judgment and response capability to alarms.

Example Design
Server Room Access Stack
  • Reinforced door with electronic card reader and audit log.
  • Biometric confirmation for high-privilege technicians.
  • Mantrap vestibule to prevent tailgating and allow verification.
  • CCTV recording and security guard patrols for rapid response.
Common Mistake

Not revoking access promptly when staff leave, which creates lingering insider risk.
