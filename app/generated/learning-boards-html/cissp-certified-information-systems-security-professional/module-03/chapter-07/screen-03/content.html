Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Apply & Assess

Practical application

These objectives ensure you can produce auditable, maintainable security designs and verify controls operate as intended.

Map Threats to Controls

Translate threats like theft, fire, and power loss into specific mitigations such as CCTV, clean-agent suppression, and redundant power feeds.

Write Layered Access Plans

Specify multiple technologies across layers to defend against bypass and tailgating, and document visitor handling and badge revocation.

Operational Practices

Include maintenance, testing schedules for fire systems, HVAC, UPS, and CCTV recording retention to preserve effectiveness.

Design Template

Produce a server room plan that lists threats, layered controls, environmental systems, and redundancy measures for stakeholders to review.
