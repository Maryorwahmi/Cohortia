Water & Cabling

Water Detection

Place sensors under raised floors, around CRAC units, and near critical infrastructure. Early detection reduces repair costs and prevents equipment damage.

Example

A leak from a condensate drain triggers underfloor sensors and alerts operations to isolate the affected area before rack-level damage occurs.

Cable Management

Airflow & Security

Tidy cabling preserves airflow, reduces hotspots, and minimizes the risk of accidental disconnection or tampering.

Key Takeaway

Integrate water detection and disciplined cable practices into monitoring and change management to prevent silent failures.
