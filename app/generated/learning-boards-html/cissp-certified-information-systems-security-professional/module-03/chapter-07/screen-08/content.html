Definition

Tailgating & Mantraps

Tailgating is unauthorized following through an access point. Mantraps are physical vestibules that enforce single-person passage and reduce tailgating risk.

Practical Example

A mantrap at a server room ensures the first door locks before the second opens, preventing an unauthorized person from slipping through with a badge-holder.

Operational Note

Design for emergency egress and throughput. Provide monitored override and clear escort procedures to balance safety and security.

Check Your Understanding
Misconception

Believing biometric gates alone stop tailgating. They help, but procedures, physical vestibules, and monitoring are needed to close gaps.

Design Prompt

Describe how you would combine a mantrap, CCTV, and an escort policy to secure a visitor with limited access while ensuring safe evacuation.

Key Takeaway

Control human behavior with physical design and clear procedures; do not rely on a single device to prevent tailgating.
