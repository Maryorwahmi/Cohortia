Redundancy
Design A+B feeds, N+1 HVAC, and UPS plus generators to avoid single-point outages.
Human Controls
Training, escort policies, and timely access revocation reduce social and operational risks.
Monitoring & Testing
Regular system tests and sensor monitoring keep protections effective and reveal degradation early.
Final Takeaway

Sustained physical security depends on layered devices plus disciplined operational practices: redundancy, people, and testing.
