Best Practices

Operational Controls

Human Element

Train staff to challenge unknowns, avoid propping doors, and follow visitor escort policies to limit social engineering and convenience-driven lapses.

Redundancy

Identify and eliminate single points of failure in power, cooling, and access systems with redundant components and diverse feeds.

Maintenance & Testing

Schedule regular tests for fire suppression, HVAC failover, UPS batteries, and generator load tests to ensure reliability when needed.

Exercise & Checklist
Spot the Vulnerability

Run a walkthrough to find propped doors, poor lighting, uncovered cable trays, and outdated access lists. Prioritize fixes that reduce risk quickly.

Key Takeaway

Operational discipline, testing, and staff training close the majority of practical security gaps.
