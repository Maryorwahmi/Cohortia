Environmental Controls

HVAC & Thermal Management

Dedicated Cooling

Use CRAC/CRAH units dedicated to server rooms to maintain stable temperatures between recommended operational ranges.

Redundancy

Design for N+1 or greater so a single unit failure does not cause thermal excursions that damage equipment.

Airflow & Cable Management

Maintain clear airflow paths and tidy cabling to avoid hotspots and improve cooling efficiency.

Example & Monitoring
Practical Example

Two CRAC units with distributed load and temperature sensors at rack inlets. If one unit fails, the other increases output and alerts operations to replace the failed unit.

Key Takeaway

Ensure dedicated, redundant cooling and monitor thermal conditions continuously to protect equipment and uptime.
