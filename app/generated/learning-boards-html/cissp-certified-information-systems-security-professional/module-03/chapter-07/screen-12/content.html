Power Protection

UPS & Generators

Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS)

Provides immediate battery backup to cover transfer time and allow graceful workloads to shut down or transfer to generator power.

Generators

Provide sustained power during extended outages; require fuel planning and regular testing.

Redundancy & Feeds

A+B feeds, dual UPS paths, and surge suppression reduce single points of failure.

Example & Test
Capacity Planning Example

Determine required runtime for critical loads, size UPS batteries accordingly, and configure generator auto-start with regular load bank tests to validate endurance.

Common Mistake

Failing to test generators under load or ignoring UPS battery health leads to unexpected downtime when utility power fails.

Key Takeaway

Design power resilience to match required runtime and test regularly to ensure reliability during outages.
