Learning Objectives
From concept to command line: what you'll be able to do after this chapter.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate symmetric vs asymmetric
Explain when to use a shared secret and when to use public/private key pairs, and why each matters for performance and key exchange.
Verified competency milestone
02
Explain hashing and salting
Describe deterministic, one-way hashes, the role of salts for passwords, and how small changes alter the digest.
Verified competency milestone
03
Describe digital signatures
Understand the hash-then-sign flow, and how signatures provide authenticity, integrity, and non-repudiation.
Verified competency milestone
04
Understand secure key exchange
Explain Diffie-Hellman key agreement and why ephemeral shared secrets and randomness are critical to secure channels.
Verified competency milestone
