Process Flow
Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange
Establish a shared symmetric key without sending the key itself over the network.
Step 1: Choose parameters
Both parties agree on base values and a large prime.
Step 2: Exchange derived public values
Each side sends a derived value computed with their secret.
Step 3: Compute shared secret
Each participant computes the same secret locally using their private secret and the received public value.
Practical note
Use ephemeral Diffie-Hellman for forward secrecy and combine with authentication to prevent man-in-the-middle attacks.
Analogy
Two people each mix secret paints and exchange the mixed colors. After further mixing locally they reach the same final color without revealing their original pigment. Authentication is the identity check that ensures the person across is who they claim to be.
Misconception
Believing DH ensures authenticity. DH does not authenticate by itself; combine with signatures or certificates.
Key Takeaway

Diffie-Hellman establishes shared secrets securely but must be paired with authentication to be safe against impersonation.
