Chapter 3.3
What to Remember
Modes & IVs
Even AES can be broken by poor mode or IV choices; ensure uniqueness and randomness.
Salts & Passwords
Salt every password and use a slow KDF to resist brute-force attempts.
Key Management
Protect private keys, rotate them, and use hardware or managed stores where feasible.
Closing takeaway
Cryptography is both algorithm and practice. Correct implementation and lifecycle management make the difference between secure systems and insecure ones.
