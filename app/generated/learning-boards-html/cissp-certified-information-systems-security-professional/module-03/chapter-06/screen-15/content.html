Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Key Concepts
Vulnerabilities
Unpatched firmware, default credentials, insecure protocols, flat networks, and supply chain risk.
Mitigations
Segmentation, signed updates, strong auth, monitoring, hardening, and supply chain controls.
Standards
Use IEC 62443 and NIST SP 800-82 as frameworks for architecture, policies, and controls.
Final takeaway

Balance security and safety. Use layered, standards-informed controls that respect device constraints and protect physical processes.
