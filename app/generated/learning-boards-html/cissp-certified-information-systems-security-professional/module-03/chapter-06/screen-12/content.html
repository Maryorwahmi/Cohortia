Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Firmware & Supply Chain
Secure firmware updates
Ensure authenticity and integrity before installing any update on a device.
Required elements
  • Cryptographic signing of firmware images
  • Device-side signature verification and integrity checks
  • Secure bootloaders and rollback capability
Supply chain checks
Verify component provenance and ensure manufacturing processes preserve software integrity.
Concrete example
A remote smart sensor verifies the digital signature of an OTA image before applying it. If verification fails, the device refuses the update and raises an alert to the operator.
Key Takeaway

Transport security is not enough. Require signed firmware and device verification, and include supply chain validation to reduce pre-deployment risks.
