Standards and monitoring
Standards give structure; monitoring gives visibility; safety sets limits on what is acceptable.
IEC 62443
Framework for industrial automation security, covering zones, roles, and secure development life cycles.
NIST SP 800-82
Guidance specifically for industrial control system security, operational considerations, and recommended controls.
Anomaly detection
Behavioral monitoring for unusual commands, traffic patterns, or timing deviations useful when patches are limited.
Safety-first evaluation
Any security control must be evaluated for its potential operational impact and effect on human safety.
Concrete guidance
Use standards to build zone models and controls, tune anomaly detection for process norms, and document safety implications for each control before deployment.
Key Takeaway

Standards and monitoring are tools. Always balance them against safety and availability in ICS — adapt, test, and document to avoid unintended harm.
