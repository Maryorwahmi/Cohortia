Learning Objectives
From recognition to applied design: the skills to secure constrained devices and control systems.
01
Identify unique constraints
You will name device limits like CPU, memory, and update gaps, and explain why they affect security design.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate IT and OT security
You will explain how availability and safety change priorities and which controls must be adapted.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe common vulnerabilities
You will list and give examples of unpatched firmware, weak credentials, and legacy protocol risks.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply mitigation & standards
You will recommend segmentation, secure updates, monitoring, and adherence to IEC 62443 / NIST guidance.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Bloom's Taxonomy
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
We move from recognizing constraints to designing OT-aware controls and applying standards to real systems.
