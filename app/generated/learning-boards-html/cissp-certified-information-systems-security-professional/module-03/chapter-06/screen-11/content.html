Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Mitigation Strategies
Practical mitigation patterns
Layered controls tailored to safety-critical constraints reduce risk without breaking operations.
Network Segmentation
Zones, DMZ, and whitelists limit lateral movement and expose fewer services.
Secure Updates
Signed firmware images and in-device integrity checks prevent malicious updates.
Authentication & Configs
Unique credentials, role-based access, and hardened defaults reduce the attack surface.
Monitoring & Anomaly Detection
Behavioral monitoring for unusual commands or traffic helps detect attacks when patches are unavailable.
Supply Chain & Physical Security
Verify component provenance and protect devices from tampering or key extraction.
Concrete scenario
For a water plant, put PLCs in a control zone, add a DMZ with the historian, restrict enterprise access to read-only via the DMZ, and run IDS on conduits to detect unusual command flows.
Key Takeaway

Mitigations must be combined and adapted to maintain safety and availability while reducing attacker access and movement.
