Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Long Lifecycles & Updates
Update lifecycle challenges
Devices that remain in service for decades create update and support challenges that demand planning.
Deployment
Devices may be deployed remotely, making frequent maintenance difficult.
Vendor support ends
Patching may stop when a vendor no longer supports older models.
Operational constraints
Downtime or recertification costs prevent regular upgrades for critical controllers.
Design responses
  • Implement secure, signed firmware updates when possible
  • Provide rollback and integrity checks to avoid bricking devices
  • Plan network isolation and compensating controls for unpatchable devices
Key Takeaway

Assume some devices cannot be frequently updated. Design architectures and policies that reduce exposure and detect attacks instead.
