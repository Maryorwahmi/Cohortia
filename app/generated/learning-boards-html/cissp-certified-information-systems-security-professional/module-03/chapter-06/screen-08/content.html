IT vs OT: priorities & controls
A direct comparison clarifies why controls differ and why convergence is risky.
IT
Focus: confidentiality and integrity. Regular patching and standard protocols are common.
OT
Focus: availability and safety. Legacy protocols and strict uptime requirements dominate.
Convergence risk
Connecting IT and OT without segmentation enables attackers from enterprise networks to reach sensitive control systems.
Practical controls that respect OT
  • DMZ between IT and OT with strict rules
  • Whitelisted traffic between designated systems only
  • Gateways that translate and secure legacy protocols
Key Takeaway

Treat OT priorities as primary when designing controls. Use segmentation and monitored gateways to avoid importing IT threats into the OT domain.
