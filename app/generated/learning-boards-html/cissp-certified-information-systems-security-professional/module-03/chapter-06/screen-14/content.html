Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Key Concepts
Embedded Systems
Specialized devices with limited resources and often long service lives.
Industrial Control Systems
SCADA, DCS, and PLCs that control physical processes where safety is critical.
OT vs IT
OT prioritizes availability and safety; IT prioritizes confidentiality and integrity.
Summary

Keep these definitions handy when designing controls: match the control to the component and the safety requirement.
