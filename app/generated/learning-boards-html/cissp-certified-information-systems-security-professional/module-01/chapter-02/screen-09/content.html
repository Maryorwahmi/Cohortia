Comparison

SoD vs Least Privilege

Separation of Duties

Splits critical process steps among different people to prevent single-person control.

Least Privilege

Restricts access rights to only what is necessary. Complementary to SoD but not identical.

Example

Financial controls: initiation, authorization, and reconciliation performed by different people. IT example: code author, reviewer, and deployer are distinct roles.

Practical enforcement

Use technical approvals, role mapping, and audit logs to detect violations.

Misconception

SoD is not solved solely by reducing privileges. It requires dividing workflow responsibilities.

Key Takeaway

Map critical processes and assign non-overlapping roles; use logs and approvals to enforce separation.
