Process Flow

Designing onboarding

Welcome & policy overview

Present AUP, classification, and reporting channels.

Role-specific tasks

Tailored modules for code handling, privileged access, or customer data.

Acknowledge and measure

Quizzes, signatures, and initial phishing tests to establish baseline competence.

Why it matters

Onboarding sets norms. When employees understand rules and reporting, they are more likely to act as defenders rather than risks.

Example
R&D engineer

Receive code-handling rules and repository access expectations in week one.

Misconception
Long training equals better security

Short, relevant modules are retained better and drive behavior change more effectively.

Key Takeaway

Make onboarding brief, role-specific, measurable, and linked to ongoing awareness.
