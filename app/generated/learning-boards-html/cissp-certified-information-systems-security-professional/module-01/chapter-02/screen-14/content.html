Module 1›Chapter 1.2›Key Concepts
Pre-employment Screening

Role-based checks that verify identity and suitability while respecting legal boundaries.

Security Awareness Training

Ongoing, role-specific, and measurable programs using simulations and micro-learning.

Acceptable Use Policy

A clear, enforceable statement of permitted actions for organizational assets.

Takeaway
These controls work together: screening limits risk at hire, training builds resilience during employment, and policies enable consistent enforcement.
