Learning Objectives

Learn the controls and actions that manage human risk.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Design screening criteria

Define role-based background checks that balance risk and privacy. Select checks proportionate to access level.

02
Build training programs

Create onboarding and ongoing awareness tailored to roles, using simulations to measure effectiveness.

03
Apply organizational controls

Use separation of duties, job rotation and mandatory vacations to reduce fraud and single-point failures.

04
Secure offboarding and vendors

Implement checklists and contractual controls to revoke access, collect assets, and manage third-party personnel security.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Prepare an enhanced offboarding checklist for a sensitive employee.
