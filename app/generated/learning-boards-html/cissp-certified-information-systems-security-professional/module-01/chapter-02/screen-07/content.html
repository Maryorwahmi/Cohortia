Module 1›Chapter 1.2›Ongoing awareness
Process Flow

Sustain awareness

Simulations

Run realistic phishing exercises and track who reports vs clicks.

Micro-learning

Short modules, videos, and newsletters that focus on current threats.

Coaching and remediation

Provide respectful feedback and tailored training to those who need it.

Measurement

Use reporting rates, click-throughs, and remediation completion as metrics. Target interventions to high-risk groups.

Example
Quarterly phishing

Simulate attacks and measure who reports within 48 hours, then coach those who clicked.

Misconception
Annual training suffices

Threats change; behavior must be reinforced frequently and respectfully.

Key Takeaway

Measure and coach; make awareness routine and relevant to the job.
