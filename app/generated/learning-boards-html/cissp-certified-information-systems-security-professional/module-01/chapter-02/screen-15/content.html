Separation of Duties

Divide process steps so no single individual has end-to-end control.

Rotation & Vacations

Rotate roles and require vacations to surface anomalies and share knowledge.

Offboarding

Revoke access immediately, recover assets, and document handovers with audits for sensitive roles.

Final Reflection
Operationalize these controls in contracts, checklists, and automated deprovisioning to make personnel security reliable.
