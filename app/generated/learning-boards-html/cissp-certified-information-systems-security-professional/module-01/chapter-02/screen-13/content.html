Module 1›Chapter 1.2›Insider threat
Definition

Insider threat categories

  • Negligent insiders: mistakes or careless handling.
  • Malicious insiders: intentional misuse or data theft.
  • Compromised insiders: credentials used by external attackers.
Mitigations
Policy & training

Clear AUPs, onboarding, and ongoing awareness reduce negligent incidents.

Technical controls

Access controls, logging, and privileged session monitoring limit and detect misuse.

Operational controls

SoD, rotations, vacations and offboarding to reduce opportunity and reveal anomalies.

Key Takeaway

Layer policy, people programs and technical controls to manage insider risk effectively.
