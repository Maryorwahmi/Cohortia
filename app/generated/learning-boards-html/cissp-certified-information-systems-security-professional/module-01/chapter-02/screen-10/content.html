Process Flow

Rotation & vacation steps

Plan rotation schedule

Define cadence and overlap to maintain continuity.

Execute mandatory vacations

Ensure duties are covered to surface hidden activities.

Document handovers

Require clear documentation to avoid operational gaps.

Why it matters

These controls reveal anomalies and reduce reliance on a single person for critical tasks.

Example

An employee's vacation uncovers unexplained reconciliations handled by others, revealing fraud.

Misconception

These measures are only HR tools; they have direct security benefits and must be planned securely.

Key Takeaway

Plan rotations and vacations to preserve operations while uncovering irregularities and cross-training staff.
