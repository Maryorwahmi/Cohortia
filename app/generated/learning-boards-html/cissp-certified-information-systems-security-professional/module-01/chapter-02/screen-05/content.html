Process Flow

Screening steps

1
Identity & basic checks

Verify identity, right to work, and basic employment history.

2
Role-based deeper checks

Criminal history, education, and references for sensitive roles.

3
Legal and consent controls

Document consent, privacy notices, and lawful basis for checks.

4
Record and review

Store results securely and reassess when access changes.

Why it matters

Appropriate screening reduces risk of insider compromise and aligns checks with the sensitivity of resources the person will access.

Example
System administrator

Requires more extensive verification than a non-privileged intern due to the risk of access to critical systems.

Common mistake

Applying identical checks to all roles or failing to document legal compliance.

Key Takeaway

Make screening depth proportional to access and document consent and legal rationale.
