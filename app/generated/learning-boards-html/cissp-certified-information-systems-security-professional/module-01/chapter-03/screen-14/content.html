Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Risk definition
Likelihood × impact; requires asset, threat, vulnerability, likelihood, impact.
SLE / ARO / ALE
SLE = AV × EF; ALE = SLE × ARO. Use to compare control costs with expected loss.
Inherent vs Residual
Inherent risk is before controls; residual is what remains and must be accepted, transferred, or further mitigated.
Summary takeaway

Use component-based definitions and both qualitative and quantitative methods where appropriate. Document decisions and monitor continuously.
