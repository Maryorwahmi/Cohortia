Risk matrix (qualitative)
Plot likelihood on one axis and impact on the other to get an overall risk level.
Low
Medium
High
Tip: record brief justification for each placement to reduce assessor disagreement.
Worked example
Asset: customer payment DB (very high impact). Vulnerability: unencrypted legacy schema. Threat: public exploit exists. Likelihood: high. Overall risk: very high.
Action: urgent mitigation such as encryption and strong access control.
If mitigation is too costly, consider transfer or avoid with documented rationale.
Common mistake

Labeling everything with the same risk level because of vague definitions of likelihood or impact.
