What is a vulnerability?
A weakness that a threat can exploit to harm an asset.
  • Unpatched software
  • Weak or reused passwords
  • Misconfiguration or missing encryption
Key Takeaway

Document vulnerabilities and the controls that address them; controls convert inherent risk to residual risk.

Control types and examples
Technical
Encryption, MFA, patches
Procedural
Change control, training
Physical
Locks, environmental controls
Detective
Monitoring, IDS
Example: encryption reduces impact; access controls reduce likelihood. Both change residual risk and treatment priority.
