Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Remember
Treatments
Avoid, Transfer, Mitigate, Accept — pick and justify one per risk.
Analysis methods
Use qualitative for speed, quantitative for financial justification with validated inputs.
Continuous monitoring
Regular scans, log reviews, and incident follow-up ensure controls remain effective.
Parting thought

Small, regular monitoring actions and clear documentation turn risk management from a one-time event into an operating practice.
