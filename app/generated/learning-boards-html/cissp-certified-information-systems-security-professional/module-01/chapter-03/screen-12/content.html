Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Risk Treatment
Avoid
Eliminate the activity that creates risk.
Transfer
Shift financial or operational burden to another party.
Mitigate
Reduce likelihood or impact through controls.
Accept
Consciously retain residual risk with managerial approval.
Decision criteria
  • Control cost versus expected reduction in ALE
  • Operational impact of removing the activity
  • Alignment with risk appetite and tolerance
Example actions
Avoid: stop storing sensitive data. Transfer: buy cyber insurance or outsource storage. Mitigate: encrypt and add MFA. Accept: document and monitor small residual risk.
