Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Threats
Threat categories
Natural, environmental, accidental, and intentional — include all plausible sources.
Natural
Floods, earthquakes
Environmental
Power outages, cooling failure
Accidental
Human error, misconfiguration
Intentional
Malware, insider misuse, targeted attacks
Example: an unpatched internet-facing service invites remote exploitation; this increases the practical likelihood and changes treatment urgency.
Pro tip

Consider both capability and intent: high capability, low intent can still be high risk if the asset is attractive to many attackers.

Common mistake

Ignoring insider threats or environmental issues that carry real operational impact.
