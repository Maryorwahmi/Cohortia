Asset categories
  • Data: customer records, IP, logs
  • Hardware: servers, endpoints
  • Software: applications and OS
  • People and reputation
Tip: map each asset to owners and business processes for clearer impact analysis.
Example: legacy PII database
List the elements that make this asset valuable: regulatory exposure, customer trust, remediation cost, and operational disruption.
Regulatory impact
Potential fines and reporting costs
Operational impact
Downtime and recovery work
Reputation
Customer churn and brand harm
Remediation cost
Incident response and legal expense
Key Takeaway

Be explicit about what makes an asset valuable; that clarity determines whether a qualitative tier or a monetary estimate is most useful.
