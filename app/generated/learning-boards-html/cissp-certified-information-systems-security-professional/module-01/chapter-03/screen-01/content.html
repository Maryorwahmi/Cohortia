Risk Management Concepts
Why risk is more than a score — it’s a decision framework.
In this lesson

Define assets, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact. Compare qualitative and quantitative analysis. Learn treatment strategies and continuous monitoring.

Why this matters
Clear risk thinking helps prioritize controls and justify spending to management. It reduces surprise incidents and directs limited resources.
Key Takeaway

Risk management links technical details to business decisions; it balances cost, likelihood, and impact to protect what matters.

Concept map
Asset
Data, hardware, reputation
Threat
Malicious or accidental actor
Vulnerability
Weakness or misconfiguration
Likelihood
Probability of exploitation
Impact
Effect on confidentiality, integrity, availability
