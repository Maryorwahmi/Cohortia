Inherent vs Residual
Inherent risk exists before controls; residual risk remains after controls.
Inherent: raw exposure requiring assessment.
Residual: acceptable remaining risk, often documented.
Appetite vs Tolerance
Appetite is strategic; tolerance is operational and measurable.
Appetite: leadership declares acceptable overall posture.
Tolerance: e.g., thresholds or allowable incident counts.
Document these so treatment decisions align with leadership expectations.
