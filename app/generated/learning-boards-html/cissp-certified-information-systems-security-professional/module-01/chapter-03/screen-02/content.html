Learning Objectives
Build practical skills for assessing and treating information risk.
01
Define risk components
Explain asset, threat, vulnerability, likelihood, and impact in information security and why each part is essential to measurement.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate analysis methods
Describe qualitative and quantitative approaches, when to use each, and the trade-offs in speed and precision.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain risk vocabulary
Define inherent and residual risk, and distinguish risk appetite from risk tolerance in managerial contexts.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply treatment strategies
Propose an avoid, transfer, mitigate, and accept action for a specific risk and justify each choice.
