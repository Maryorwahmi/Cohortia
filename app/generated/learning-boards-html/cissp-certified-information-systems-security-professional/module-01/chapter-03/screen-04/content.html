Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › What is risk?
Definition
Risk is the likelihood a threat exploits a vulnerability and causes impact to an asset.
  • Asset: something valuable to the organization.
  • Threat: potential danger, from natural events to attackers.
  • Vulnerability: weakness in an asset or its controls.
  • Likelihood and impact combine to form risk.
Key Takeaway

Split concerns into asset, threat, vulnerability, likelihood, and impact to prioritize effective controls.

Annotated example
Asset
Customer PII in legacy DB
Vulnerability
Unencrypted, unpatched, weak access controls
Threat
External attacker or compromised credential
Impact
Financial loss, regulatory fines, reputational damage
Misconception: don't call something “high risk” without specifying which component drives that label. Is it high impact, high likelihood, or both?
