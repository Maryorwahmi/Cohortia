Risk appetite and tolerance
Strategy versus operational thresholds: appetite is broad, tolerance is specific.
Appetite: senior decision on how much overall risk to accept.
Tolerance: measurable limits, such as acceptable number of minor incidents.
Misconception: appetite is not the same as tolerance — appetite is a posture, tolerance is applied policy.
Continuous monitoring
Use scans, logs, and incident reports to reassess risks frequently and catch changes in the threat landscape.
Routine vulnerability scans and patch windows.
Periodic risk review meetings with business owners.
Why it matters: controls age and threats evolve, so assessments must be updated and documented.
