Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › PII Risk Treatment
Risk
Unauthorized access to customer PII stored in an unencrypted legacy database.
Why it matters: regulatory fines, remediation cost, customer trust, and operational disruption.
Ownership: assign a business owner and security lead to the treatment plan.
Treatment actions
Avoid: stop storing PII directly; use a third-party payment processor to eliminate the exposure.
Transfer: purchase cyber insurance or outsource storage to a vendor under contractual liability.
Mitigate: encrypt data at rest, enforce MFA, patch the database, and restrict administrative access.
Accept: after mitigation, if residual risk is within tolerance, document the acceptance with managerial sign-off and monitoring requirements.
Key Takeaway

A good treatment plan maps options to specific actions, explains how each reduces likelihood or impact, and records approval and monitoring steps.
