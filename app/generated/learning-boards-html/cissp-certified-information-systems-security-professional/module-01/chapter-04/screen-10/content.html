Tabletop Exercise
A discussion‑based walk through of a scenario to validate roles, decision points, and communications.
Use when you want quick validation and minimal technical risk.
Full‑Scale Simulation
A technical failover where systems are actually recovered at an alternate site or in the cloud to validate orchestration and data integrity.
Use when you need high confidence that recovery steps work under load and timing constraints.
Testing workflow
  1. Plan the exercise with objectives and success criteria.
  2. Assign roles and gather required artifacts and scripts.
  3. Execute the test under controlled conditions.
  4. Capture findings, update plans, and schedule follow-up tests.
Common mistake
Assuming a plan works without testing or failing to document lessons learned for plan updates.
Pro tip
Start with tabletop exercises and graduate to technical failovers once roles and assumptions are validated.
Key Takeaway

Regular, structured testing with defined success criteria is the only reliable way to validate that a BCP/DRP will work when needed.
