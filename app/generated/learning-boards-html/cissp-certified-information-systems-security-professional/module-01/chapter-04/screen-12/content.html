Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Cohortia Scenario
Scenario
E‑commerce platform: RTO = 4 hours, RPO = 1 hour. Decide recovery site and BCP measures, and design a test.
Recommended DR site: hot site or cloud recovery capable of near‑real‑time replication. Justification: meets short RTO and tight RPO.
Non‑IT BCP Measure
Implement manual order capture and communication templates so customers receive confirmations and orders are reconciled later. This preserves customer trust during outages.
DRP Testing Method
Use a mix of tabletop exercises to validate roles and a scheduled partial or full failover simulation to verify technical recovery and data consistency. Define success criteria such as transaction verification and recovery within four hours.
Example test checklist
  • Initiate failover to recovery environment
  • Restore database to within the one hour RPO
  • Run transaction consistency checks and sample orders
  • Confirm customer communication protocols operate
Key Takeaway

Map objectives to site capability, prepare non‑IT continuity measures for customer service, and validate all assumptions through progressive testing.
