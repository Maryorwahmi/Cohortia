Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Recovery Site Options
Hot Site
Fully equipped, mirrored data center with current data for near-immediate failover.
Readiness: very high • Cost: high • Typical use: mission-critical systems with low RTO/RPO.
When to choose
When RTO is hours or less and RPO must be minimal.
Warm Site
Partially equipped site requiring setup and data restoration before full operation.
Readiness: medium • Cost: moderate • Typical use: systems tolerating moderate downtime.
When to choose
When some downtime is acceptable and cost must be controlled.
Cold Site
Facility with power and network but no pre-installed hardware or data; long setup time.
Readiness: low • Cost: low • Typical use: non-critical systems with long acceptable downtime.
When to choose
When downtime and data loss tolerance are high and cost must be minimized.
Decision guidance
Match the RTO and RPO from the BIA to a site type that can deliver the required readiness. For Cohortia’s e-commerce example with RTO 4 hours and RPO 1 hour, a hot site or a cloud recovery solution capable of near‑real‑time replication is appropriate. A warm or cold site would likely not meet those objectives without additional investments.
Common mistake
Selecting a lower-cost site without verifying it can meet the RTO/RPO in the BIA.
Pro tip
Consider cloud recovery or mobile sites for flexibility, but validate that replication and recovery orchestration meet objectives.
