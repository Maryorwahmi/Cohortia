Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Business Impact Analysis
Process
1. Identify critical functions
List business activities such as order processing or billing.
2. Map dependencies
Connect functions to applications, servers and third parties.
3. Quantify impact
Estimate financial and operational impact by downtime intervals.
4. Derive RTO/RPO
Set measurable recovery targets to prioritize recovery actions.
Example
Order processing is identified as top priority. Dependencies include payment gateway, order database, and fulfillment API. The BIA assigns an RTO of 4 hours and an RPO of 1 hour, driving a near‑real-time replication design for the database.
Why this matters
The BIA ensures recovery resources are focused where they prevent the greatest business impact. It reduces ambiguity for implementers and avoids ad hoc decisions under pressure.
Output card
Prioritized function list with RTO/RPO and mapped dependencies to guide DRP and site selection.
Common mistake
Stopping at high-level priorities without technical dependency mapping leaves recovery teams guessing order of restores.
Key Takeaway

A BIA produces the measurable recovery objectives that DRP must meet; map dependencies and document assumptions.
