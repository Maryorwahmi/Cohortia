Learning Objectives
Learn, Understand, Apply, Master
Bloom
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate BCP and DRP
Explain scope and roles of BCP and DRP and why both are needed to sustain operations after an incident.
Mnemonic: two concentric rings — BCP is the outer ring, DRP the inner server-focused ring.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Conduct a Business Impact Analysis
Identify critical functions, dependencies and quantify impact over time so recovery can be prioritized.
Mnemonic: dependency map — functions linked to apps and servers.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Define RTO and RPO
Set measurable targets for downtime and data loss that inform backup frequency and site choices.
Mnemonic: timeline — RPO marks the restore point, RTO marks the deadline to resume service.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Select and test recovery options
Compare hot, warm, cold, mobile and cloud recovery and plan tests to validate readiness.
Mnemonic: cost vs readiness grid.
