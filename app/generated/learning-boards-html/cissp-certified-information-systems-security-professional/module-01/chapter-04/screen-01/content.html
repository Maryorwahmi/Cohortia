Business Continuity Planning and Disaster Recovery
Practical strategies to keep operations running and restore IT after a disruption.
In this lesson

You will learn definitions, how to run a Business Impact Analysis, set Recovery Time and Point Objectives, and select recovery sites that meet business needs.

Why it matters

Downtime and data loss become measurable risks. Matching objectives to recovery options prevents unacceptable losses and excessive costs.

Getting started

Start by identifying critical functions, then set RTO and RPO in a BIA to prioritize recovery strategies.
