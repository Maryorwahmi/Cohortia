Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Cloud & Mobile Recovery
Cloud Recovery
Use cloud infrastructure to host replicated systems and data, offering scalability and fast provisioning when designed to meet RTO/RPO targets.
Pros
Elastic capacity, no physical data center lease.
Cons
Requires orchestration and verification; may have cost spikes.
Mobile Sites
Trailers or modular units with preinstalled equipment transported to a site. Useful where proximity or specific infrastructure is needed quickly.
Pros
Portable and preconfigured for predictable recovery.
Cons
Logistics and secure transport add complexity.
Design checklist
  • Map RTO and RPO to cloud replication or mobile preconfiguration.
  • Validate identity, network, and data consistency during failover tests.
  • Confirm regulatory and data residency constraints before selecting cloud regions.
Misconception
Cloud does not automatically meet objectives — you must design replication cadence and recovery orchestration to match the BIA.
Key Takeaway

Treat cloud and mobile as options, not defaults; validate that they meet defined RTO and RPO constraints before relying on them.
