Key Concepts
Three compact reminders to guide decisions
BCP & DRP
BCP covers business continuity; DRP restores IT that supports it.
RTO & RPO
RTO = downtime tolerance; RPO = acceptable data loss; both drive design.
Site Selection & Testing
Choose hot, warm, cold, cloud or mobile based on objectives and test to validate readiness.
Summary Takeaway

Translate business priorities into measurable objectives, pick recovery strategies that meet them, and prove readiness through testing and regular plan maintenance.
