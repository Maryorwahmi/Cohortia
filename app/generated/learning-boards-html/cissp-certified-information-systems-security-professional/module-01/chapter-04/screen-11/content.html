Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Review & Update
Maintenance Checklist
  • Review schedule and owners.
  • Trigger review after new systems, vendor changes, or tests.
  • Record versioned updates and approvals.
Training
When the plan changes, update training so staff can execute new procedures without hesitation during an incident.
Post-test follow-up
Every test must produce a findings report, action items, and a scheduled update. Use this feedback loop to remove assumptions that failed and to strengthen recovery steps.
Common mistake
Failing to version and approve changes so different teams reference different plan copies.
Pro tip
Maintain a single source of truth and a short public change log so stakeholders know the plan's current state.
Key Takeaway

A living plan requires scheduled reviews, version control, and training so it stays effective as systems and people change.
