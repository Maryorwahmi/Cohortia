Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)
Predicts expected time between failures. Use this to prioritize redundancy where failures are frequent.
Mean Time To Repair (MTTR)
Average time to repair a failed component and restore function. Use this to decide on spare parts, staff, or alternate sites.
How to use in planning
  • Compare MTTR to required RTO. If MTTR exceeds RTO, implement redundancy or faster repair strategies.
  • Use MTBF to prioritize preventive maintenance and where to invest in higher‑reliability hardware.
  • Document MTBF/MTTR assumptions in the BIA so recovery costs are grounded in expected behavior.
Common mistake
Ignoring repair time when setting RTO — you may pick recovery targets that engineering cannot meet without extra investment.
Key Takeaway

Include MTBF and MTTR in recovery planning to ensure chosen strategies and investments realistically meet business objectives.
