Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Learning Objectives

Application & Assessment

Concrete demonstrations that show governance is working.

Task
Map policy to artifacts

Draft a high-level policy, then derive one standard, one guideline, and three procedure steps.

Audit evidence
Show enforcement

Patch records, MFA logs, vendor security assessments, and audit trails demonstrate ongoing diligence.

Pitfalls
Common mistakes

No executive buy-in, overly generic policies, or no periodic review weaken governance.

Use the exercises and the verification milestones to practice drafting enforceable documentation and to gather evidence that your organization is exercising both due care and due diligence.
