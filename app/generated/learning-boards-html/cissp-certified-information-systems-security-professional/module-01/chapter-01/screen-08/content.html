Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Due Care and Due Diligence
Core Concept

Due care & due diligence explained

Both are required to reduce legal exposure: take reasonable precautions and evidence ongoing assessment and enforcement.

Due care example

Installing firewalls, antivirus, and backups demonstrates reasonable protective actions.

Due diligence example

Regularly scanning for vulnerabilities, performing pen tests, and auditing patch compliance show ongoing effort to manage risk.

Scenario

An organisation has antivirus installed (due care). If it fails to update signatures or to run vulnerability scans and audits, it lacks due diligence and remains at risk despite the installed controls.

Key Takeaway

To demonstrate governance you must both implement reasonable protections and show continuous assessment and enforcement.
