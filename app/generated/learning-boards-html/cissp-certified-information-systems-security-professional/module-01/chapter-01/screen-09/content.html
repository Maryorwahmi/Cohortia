Hierarchy

From policy to procedure

Each layer translates leadership intent into operational reality with increasing detail and enforceability.

Policy
High-level intent and mandatory statements from management.
Standards & Baselines
Mandatory requirements and minimum configuration settings that implement policy.
Guidelines & Procedures
Recommended practices and the step-by-step instructions for consistent execution.
Mapping checklist
  • Map each policy to one or more standards that specify mandatory controls.
  • Define baselines that implement standards for each system type.
  • Publish guidelines for user behavior and procedures for operational tasks.
Key Takeaway

Consistency across all layers is essential: policies give authority, while standards, baselines, guidelines, and procedures make that authority operational and auditable.
