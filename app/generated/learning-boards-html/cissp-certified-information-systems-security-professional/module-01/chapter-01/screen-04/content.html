Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Security governance as a strategic process
Core Concept

Leadership, direction, and oversight

Governance creates a continuous cycle that turns security decisions into measurable actions and changes.

Process steps
  1. Set strategy and policy through board and senior management decisions.
  2. Translate strategy into standards, baselines, and procedures.
  3. Implement controls and collect monitoring data.
  4. Report outcomes and adjust strategy or resourcing.
Example

When leadership approves remote work goals, governance ensures policies, technical controls like VPN and MFA, and monitoring are in place and measured.

Common misconception

Governance is not just paperwork; it is active oversight, measurable controls, and decision-making backed by evidence.

Management loop
1
Set strategic direction
Board and senior management define outcomes.
2
Define controls
Policies, standards, baselines, procedures translate strategy into action.
3
Execute & monitor
Operational teams implement and generate performance data.
4
Review & adjust

Key Takeaway

Governance connects strategic objectives to technical controls and makes security measurable and accountable.
