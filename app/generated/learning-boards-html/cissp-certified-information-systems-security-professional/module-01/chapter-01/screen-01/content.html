Security Governance Principles

A strategic framework that ensures security supports business objectives through policy, roles, and ongoing oversight.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson

  • Differentiate policy, standard, baseline, guideline, procedure.

Why it matters

Good governance turns security from an ad-hoc cost into an aligned investment that reduces risk, ensures compliance, and protects reputation.

Quick orientation

You will explore the documentation hierarchy, role responsibilities, and two legal concepts — due care and due diligence — and then practice mapping a remote access policy into standards, guidelines, and procedure steps.
