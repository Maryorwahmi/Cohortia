Architecture

Who does what in governance

Define clear ownership from oversight to operations so policies become enforceable requirements with accountable teams.

Board / Senior leadership
Set strategy, provide authority and funding, and review high-level performance.
CISO / Security leadership
Translate strategy into policies and standards; report to leadership and steward security programs.
Security & IT teams
Implement controls, apply baselines, and provide monitoring data and remediation.
End users
Follow policies and guidelines; report incidents and comply with procedures.
Evidence of ownership
  • Documented approvals and role descriptions.
  • Assigned remediation tasks with SLA tracking.
  • Audit trails showing who performed enforcement actions.
Key Takeaway

Clear, documented ownership and evidentiary trails close gaps and make governance auditable.
