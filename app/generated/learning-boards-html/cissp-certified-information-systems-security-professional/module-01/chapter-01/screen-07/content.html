Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Guidelines and Procedures
Definition

Recommendations and step-by-step actions

Guidelines encourage secure behavior; procedures document exact steps to ensure consistency and repeatability.

Guideline example

Encourage strong unique passwords and advise against using public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks.

Procedure example

Step-by-step instructions for provisioning a new user's remote access, including approvals, account creation, MFA setup, and verification steps.

Practical guidance
  • Use guidelines for user-facing advice that supports policy goals.
  • Write procedures with precise, auditable steps and checkpoints.
  • Keep procedures versioned and subject to change control.
Key Takeaway

Guidelines steer behavior; procedures guarantee repeatable execution that can be audited.
