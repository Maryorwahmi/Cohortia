Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Policies: high-level mandates
Definition

Policies are mandatory, outcome-focused statements

They declare what is required and why — providing authority and direction for the rest of the documentation hierarchy.

Concrete example

An Acceptable Use Policy states company assets are for business use and lists prohibited activities, creating a clear obligation for employees.

Why it matters

Policies provide the authority to enforce standards and procedures, and they demonstrate leadership commitment to security.

Common mistake

Making policies too detailed turns them into operational manuals that are hard to change and enforce.

Policy checklist
  • Is the statement mandatory and outcome-focused?
  • Does it reflect leadership intent and authority?
  • Can compliance to the policy be demonstrated with evidence?
Key Takeaway

Write policies as concise, auditable mandates that enable enforceable standards and procedures.
