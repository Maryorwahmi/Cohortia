Common Mistakes

Where governance often fails

Recognize recurring problems and how to prevent them so governance remains effective and sustainable.

No executive buy-in
Without leadership support, security initiatives lack funding and authority.
Overly generic or complex policies
Make policies clear, mandatory, and auditable rather than vague or unwieldy.
Treating security as only technical
Integrate security with business processes to ensure relevance and adoption.
Failure to review
Schedule periodic reviews to adapt to technology and threat changes.
Prevention checklist
  • Obtain executive sponsorship and defined budgets.
  • Write clear, mandatory, and auditable policies.
  • Assign ownership and proof points for enforcement.
  • Review documentation on a regular schedule.
Key Takeaway

Avoid these pitfalls by embedding governance in business processes, documenting ownership, and providing evidence of enforcement.
