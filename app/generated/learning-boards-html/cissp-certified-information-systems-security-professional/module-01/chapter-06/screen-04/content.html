Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Security Policy Lifecycle
Process Flow
1
Initiation
Triggers: regulation, audit, risk, business need
2
Development
Draft with legal, HR, tech, and business inputs
3
Approval
Senior management endorsement and resource allocation
4
Dissemination
Portal, training, acknowledgements
5
Implementation
Translate policy into standards and controls
6
Enforcement
Monitoring, audits, and disciplinary steps
7
Review & Update
Periodic and event-driven reviews
Explanation
  • Initiation secures authority and defines the driver for the policy.
  • Development must balance technical accuracy with operational feasibility.
  • Approval gives enforceability; without it employees may ignore the policy.
  • Dissemination aims for understanding, not mere distribution.
Example
After adopting new cloud services, the organization triggered initiation and moved to an out-of-cycle review to update scope and controls.
Key Takeaway
A single-stage policy project is insufficient; continuous governance is required.
