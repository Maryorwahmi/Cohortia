Code-style Template
Below is a short draft example you can adapt. It separates the policy statement from enforcement language and keeps technical steps in procedures.
Cohortia Mobile Device Security Policy

Policy Statement:
  - Company-issued mobile devices must use full-disk encryption and require a passcode with a minimum length of 8 characters.
  - Remote wipe capability must be enabled and tested annually.

Enforcement Clause:
  - Compliance will be monitored via device management reports and periodic audits.
  - Non-compliant devices will be quarantined, required to remediate, and incidents escalated to IT and HR for further action.
Explanation
  • Policy statement: mandatory language and measurable criteria allow objective checks.
  • Enforcement clause: defines monitoring approach and predictable consequences.
  • Procedures: where to keep remediation steps, testing schedules, and technical configuration guides.
Example
Device with disabled encryption → automated alert → quarantine → remediation and re-enrollment.
Common Mistake
Writing vague, unenforceable statements like 'devices should be secure'.
