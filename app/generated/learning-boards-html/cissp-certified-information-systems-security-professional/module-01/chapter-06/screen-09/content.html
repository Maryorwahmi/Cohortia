Monitoring & Audits
Automated monitoring: logs, configuration scans, device reports.
Periodic audits: scheduled checks, compliance reports, and spot audits after incidents.
Verification: remediation evidence and re-test before closing a finding.
Consequences & Remediation
Define a graduated response: remediation and education for inadvertent non-compliance; access controls and HR involvement for repeated or willful breaches. Document remediation owners, deadlines, and verification steps.
Example
Scan finds unpatched server → temporary isolation + patch within SLA → audit record and closure when re-scanned.
Key Takeaway
Consistency in monitoring and consequences sustains policy compliance and reduces repeat incidents.
