Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Assessment Idea
Scenario
An employee requires unencrypted access for performance reasons. Choose the correct process to decide on an exception.
  1. Conduct a risk assessment and propose compensating controls.
  2. Require documented approval with an expiration date.
  3. Implement monitoring and remediation responsibilities.
Scoring
Evaluate answers on whether they include a risk assessment, compensating controls, documentation, expiration, and monitoring metrics. Partial credit for reasonable compensating controls; full credit when enforcement and review are specified.
Reflection
Which metric will indicate the compensating control is effective: reduced exposure, successful audits, or no related incidents during the exception period?
