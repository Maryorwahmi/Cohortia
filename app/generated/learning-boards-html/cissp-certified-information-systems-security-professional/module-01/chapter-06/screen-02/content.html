Objective details

Each objective combines knowledge and practice: you will both explain policy parts and draft short enforceable clauses. Assessment will include a short drafting task and a scenario where you must recommend compensating controls for an exception.

Mnemonic: Lifecycle loop (Initiate • Draft • Approve • Disseminate • Enforce • Review)
Timeline: Attach review and effective dates to every policy to avoid drift.
