Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Essential Components
Definition
Purpose
Explains why the policy exists and the protection goals, e.g., confidentiality, integrity, availability.
Scope
Specifies who and what the policy covers: employees, contractors, systems, and locations.
Policy Statements
High-level mandatory rules phrased as requirements, measurable where possible.
Roles & Responsibilities
Assigns accountability for oversight, enforcement, and adherence.
Supporting items
Compliance / Enforcement
Describes monitoring, audits, and consequences for violations.
Definitions
Clarifies technical or legal terms used in the policy to avoid ambiguity.
References & Dates
Links to standards, procedures, and includes effective and review dates for auditability.
Key Takeaway
Structure policies for clarity and enforcement; put detail into standards and procedures.
Common mistake
Using vague language that makes enforcement subjective and inconsistent.
