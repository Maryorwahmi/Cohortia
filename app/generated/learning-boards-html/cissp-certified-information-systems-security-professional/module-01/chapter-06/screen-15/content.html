Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Key Concepts
Security Policy Lifecycle
A continuous loop from initiation to review that keeps policies current.
Policy Types
Organizational, issue-specific, and system-specific policies have different audiences and detail levels.
Exceptions & Controls
Exceptions must be documented, risk-assessed, and paired with compensating controls and an expiry.
One-line Takeaway
Effective policy programs combine clear language, ownership, training, measurable enforcement, and continuous review.
