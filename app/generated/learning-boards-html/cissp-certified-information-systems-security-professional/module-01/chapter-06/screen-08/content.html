Dissemination Methods
  • Authoritative storage on an internal policy portal with version control.
  • Targeted announcements for affected teams and stakeholders.
  • Mandatory role-based training modules with short quizzes.
  • Signed acknowledgements tied to HR records and onboarding.
Awareness Tactics
Use scenario-based exercises to show consequences of non-compliance and correct behavior.
Provide quick reference cards for common operations like remote access and mobile device handling.
Measure understanding with short quizzes and track incident trends to validate effectiveness.
Example
When rolling out a Mobile Device Security Policy, require re-enrollment in device management, an online training module showing proper configuration, and a short quiz to verify understanding. Track completion and correlate with device compliance reports.
