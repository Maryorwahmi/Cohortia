Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Drafting Activity
Task
Create three short items: a policy statement, an enforcement clause, and an exception scenario with compensating control.
Helpful tip: Use active, mandatory verbs like "must" and include measurable criteria such as minimum length, required features, or time windows.
Remember to keep procedures and technical steps in a procedure document rather than the policy text.
Starter Example
Policy Statement:
  - Company-issued mobile devices must have full-disk encryption enabled and require a passcode with a minimum length of 8 characters.

Enforcement Clause:
  - Device compliance will be verified through mobile device management reports and quarterly audits. Non-compliant devices will be quarantined until remediated.

Exception Scenario:
  - Scenario: Contractor requires local data storage for a time-limited assignment and cannot install the management agent.
  - Compensating Control: Provide a segmented network environment, mandatory strong VPN, strict logging, and a 90-day expiration on the exception.
Key Takeaway
Draft statements that are measurable, pair exceptions with compensating controls, and set expiration dates.
