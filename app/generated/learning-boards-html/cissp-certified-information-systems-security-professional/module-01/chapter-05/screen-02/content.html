Learning Objectives

Bloom levels: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master

Bloom progress
01
Map legal frameworks

Identify applicable laws and regulations for an organization based on industry and geography, and explain why they require specific security measures.

Verified Competency Milestone
02
Differentiate IP types

Explain copyright, patent, trademark, and trade secret with concrete product and software examples and the protections they afford.

Verified Competency Milestone
03
Apply privacy law principles

Describe GDPR and CCPA basics, rights granted to individuals, and how these shape data lifecycle practices like retention and access control.

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Integrate ethics and frameworks

Recognize professional ethics, responsible disclosure, and how frameworks like NIST or ISO/IEC 27001 inform governance and controls.

Verified Competency Milestone
