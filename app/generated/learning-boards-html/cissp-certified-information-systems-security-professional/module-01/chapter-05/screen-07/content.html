Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Trademark vs Trade Secret
Comparison

Public brand protection versus confidential advantage

Trademark
Protects brand identifiers; registration and public use strengthen rights. Example: Cohortia name and logo.
Trade secret
Protects confidential know‑how through secrecy and controls. Example: a proprietary process or recipe.
Operational controls
Trademarks need policing and registration. Trade secrets need NDAs, access limits, and monitoring for leaks.
Practical decisions
Decision question
Do you need public recognition or do you need secrecy to retain value? The answer points to trademark or trade secret.
Common pitfall
Accidentally publishing a trade secret destroys protection; consider whether patenting is better if disclosure is unavoidable and the invention is patentable.
Key takeaway
Classify assets, choose public or secret protection, and apply matching legal and operational measures.
