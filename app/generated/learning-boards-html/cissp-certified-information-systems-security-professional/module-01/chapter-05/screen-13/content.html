Hands-on Activity

Assess GDPR and CCPA requirements for a new service

GDPR: Two key requirements
  1. Lawful basis and documentation: record whether processing uses consent or legitimate interest and maintain a processing register.
  2. Data minimization and purpose limitation: collect only required fields and enforce retention schedules and deletion workflows.
CCPA: Two consumer rights
  1. Right to know: implement an interface and verification process reporting what personal information you collect.
  2. Right to delete: provide a verified deletion workflow that removes personal information and documents the action.
Owners
Assign a consent steward, a data protection officer for GDPR tasks, and a compliance owner for CCPA workflows.
Artifacts
Produce an impact summary that maps requirements to controls, owners, and timelines for remediation or implementation.
Misconception
Assuming rights apply only in origin country. Rights apply to residents; a global service must implement multiple jurisdictional flows.
