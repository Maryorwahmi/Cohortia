From Objectives to Work

Each objective maps to a concrete artifact you can produce and measure.

Verified milestones
01 Mapping
Applicable Law Matrix

Inventory data categories, locations, and relevant laws to show where obligations arise.

02 IP Register
Catalog IP and chosen protections

Record whether assets are copyrighted, patented, trademarked, or protected as trade secrets.

03 Privacy Impact
Privacy Impact Assessment

Document GDPR/CCPA requirements and controls for data collection and retention.

04 Framework Plan
Framework alignment

Map selected frameworks to controls, assign owners, and plan audits or certification steps.

Quick tip
Start with data classification: it's the key to selecting applicable laws and appropriate controls.
Common mistake
Treating compliance as a one-time project rather than an ongoing program leads to gaps when laws or processes change.
Why this matters
Auditors expect artifacts; regulators expect demonstrable, repeatable controls. Produce evidence, not just claims.
