Process Flow

Operational governance steps

Step 1: Inventory and classify
Identify assets and data types to determine applicable laws and frameworks.
Step 2: Map to controls
For each obligation, define the technical and administrative controls that satisfy it.
Step 3: Evidence and ownership
Assign control owners and collect artifacts auditors require: policies, logs, scan reports.
Step 4: Monitor and adapt
Implement continuous monitoring and update controls when laws or systems change.
Governance artifacts
Obligation register
List of laws, regulations, and framework requirements mapped to assets and controls.
Control mapping
Spreadsheet or tool showing where each control is implemented and its evidence.
Key takeaway
Governance makes compliance auditable and repeatable by tying obligations to owners and artifacts.
