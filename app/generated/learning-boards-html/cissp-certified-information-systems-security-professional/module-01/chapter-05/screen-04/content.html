Core Concept

Definitions and practical mappings

Turn abstract legal text into controls and artifacts that auditors can verify.

Law
Binding statutory requirements from legislatures; they set duties and penalties.
Regulation
Agency rules that implement laws, often with reporting and technical specifications.
Compliance
Operational practices: policies, controls, monitoring, documentation and audits that demonstrate adherence.
Process mapping
Example: Privacy law to control
  • Law: right to privacy and data protection.
  • Regulation: breach reporting windows, data handling rules.
  • Compliance: implement consent flows, encryption, retention policies, and incident plans.
Common misconception
Equating compliance with security. Compliance sets minimum standards; security must address real risk beyond tick-boxes.
Key takeaway
Document the mapping from legal text to operational control so auditors and regulators can verify your decisions.
