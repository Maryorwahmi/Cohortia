Frameworks

Architectural view of common frameworks

NIST Cybersecurity Framework
Voluntary risk-management functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. Use for program design and risk mapping.
ISO/IEC 27001
Formal ISMS standard; certification demonstrates systematic controls and continual improvement.
PCI DSS
Mandatory standard for cardholder data security with prescriptive technical controls.
Industry specifics
HIPAA
Requires administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for protected health information.
SOX
Impacts IT controls related to financial reporting for public companies.
Layering frameworks
Combine programmatic standards like ISO with prescriptive standards like PCI to cover both governance and technical details.
Key takeaway
Map chosen frameworks to your legal obligations, then collect evidence and assign owners for each mapped control.
