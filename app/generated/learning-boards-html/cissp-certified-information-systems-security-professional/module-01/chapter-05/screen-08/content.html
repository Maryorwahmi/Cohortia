Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › GDPR vs CCPA
Comparison

GDPR — core principles

  • Lawful, fair, and transparent processing.
  • Data minimization and purpose limitation.
  • Accountability and rights: access, rectification, erasure.
Practical example
A global personalization service must implement consent where required, maintain records of processing activities, and support data subject requests under GDPR.
Comparison

CCPA — core rights

  • Right to know what is collected.
  • Right to delete personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale of personal information.
Practical example
For California users, provide mechanisms to respond to access and deletion requests and a clear opt-out for selling data.
Shared controls
Data classification, access controls, retention policies, and incident response are foundational for both laws.
Misconception
Assuming compliance with one law covers all jurisdictions. You may need layered controls for multiple laws.
Key takeaway
Build strong data governance first, then implement jurisdiction-specific rights and notices.
