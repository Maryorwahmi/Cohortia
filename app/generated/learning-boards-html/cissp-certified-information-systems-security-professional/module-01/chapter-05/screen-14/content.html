Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Key Concepts
Intellectual Property
Protect creations with copyright, patents, trademarks, or trade secret measures based on asset type.
Privacy Laws
GDPR and CCPA grant rights and impose obligations; design data governance accordingly.
Compliance Frameworks
NIST, ISO, PCI, HIPAA and SOX guide controls and audits; map them to your obligations.
Summary takeaway

Legal obligations and professional ethics should directly shape your security architecture and governance artifacts. Document decisions, assign owners, and maintain evidence to show compliance and protect the organization.
