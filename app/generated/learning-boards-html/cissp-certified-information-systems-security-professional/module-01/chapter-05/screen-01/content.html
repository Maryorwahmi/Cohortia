Legal, Regulatory, and Compliance Issues

Understand how obligations from law, regulation, and standards influence data protection, intellectual property, privacy, and professional ethics. This chapter connects legal duties to technical controls and governance practices.

Duration: short module
In this lesson

Distinguish law, regulation, compliance; IP types; privacy laws; ethical duties; compliance frameworks and governance integration.

Why it matters

Non-compliance risks fines, legal action, and reputation damage. Security controls must align to these obligations.

Explore Key Topics
Intellectual Property
Copyright, patents, trademarks, trade secrets and practical examples for software and processes.
Privacy Laws
GDPR and CCPA principles that change how you collect and retain personal data.
Compliance Frameworks
NIST, ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS, HIPAA and SOX guide security controls and governance.
Quick example
A US healthcare provider must apply HIPAA safeguards and may also need ISO 27001 practices to demonstrate systematic control. A payment processor must follow PCI DSS for card data.
Visual Map
Law
Statutory obligations passed by legislatures.
Regulation
Agency rules that implement laws with specific requirements.
Compliance
Ongoing programs and controls to meet laws and standards.
Ethics
Professional conduct that goes beyond legal minimums.
Starting question
Which obligations affect the systems you operate? Consider industry, location, and the kinds of data handled. That mapping determines required controls and audits.
