What to Remember

Reinforcing your understanding

Isolation (Blockchain)

Smart contracts operate in a sandboxed environment, unable to directly initiate network requests to external systems.

Off-chain

Refers to data or processes that exist outside of a blockchain's native environment, requiring oracles to bring them on-chain.

On-chain

Refers to data or processes that exist within and are recorded by a blockchain, benefiting from its security and immutability.

Single Point of Failure (SPOF)

A component of a system that, if it fails, causes the entire system to stop working. Centralized oracles are a classic SPOF.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding blockchain's isolated nature, the distinction between on-chain and off-chain data, and the risks of a single point of failure are crucial for designing robust decentralized applications.
