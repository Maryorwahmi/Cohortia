Problem Statement

The Need for Off-Chain Data

Unlocking the full potential of smart contracts

DeFi Lending

Protocols need real-time cryptocurrency prices to calculate collateral ratios, trigger liquidations, or determine interest rates accurately.

Decentralized Insurance

Smart contracts require external data like flight delays, weather conditions, or natural disaster reports to automatically process claims and payouts.

Prediction Markets

These platforms rely on external event outcomes, such as election results or sports scores, to settle bets and distribute winnings fairly.

Key Takeaway

Without access to real-world data, smart contracts are severely limited. Oracles are essential for expanding their utility and enabling complex, real-world decentralized applications.
