Immutability: Permanent Records

How data on a blockchain becomes unchangeable and verifiable.

Unchangeable Data

Once recorded, data or transactions cannot be altered or deleted from the blockchain.

Cryptographic Security

Achieved through hashing and consensus, linking blocks in an unbreakable chain.

Real-world Impact

Ensures integrity for financial records, digital identity, and supply chain tracking, where trust in data is paramount.
