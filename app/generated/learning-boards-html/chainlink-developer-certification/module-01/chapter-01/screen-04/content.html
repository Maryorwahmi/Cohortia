Web2 vs. Web3: A Paradigm Shift

Understanding the fundamental differences in architecture and control.

Web2: Centralized

  • •Data stored on central servers.
  • •Single points of control and failure.
  • •User data controlled by corporations.
  • •Censorship and data breaches are risks.
Example

Social media platforms like Facebook or X, where a company manages all user data and content.

Web3: Decentralized

  • •Data distributed across a network (blockchain).
  • •No single point of control or failure.
  • •User ownership and control over data.
  • •Resilience against censorship and attacks.
Example

Decentralized exchanges like Uniswap, where users trade directly without a central intermediary.

Key Takeaway

Web3 shifts power from centralized entities to users, fostering a more open, transparent, and resilient internet.
