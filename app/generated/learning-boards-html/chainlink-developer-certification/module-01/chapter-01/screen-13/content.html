Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 1

Revisiting the foundational terms of Web3.

Web2

The current internet, characterized by centralized applications and data controlled by corporations.

Web3

The next generation internet, built on decentralized networks, emphasizing user ownership and transparency.

Decentralization

Distribution of control and data across a network, rather than concentrating it in a single entity.

Summary Takeaway

Web3 represents a fundamental shift from centralized control to a decentralized, user-empowered internet, built on principles of distributed power and data integrity.
