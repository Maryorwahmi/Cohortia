Web3 Principle

Transparency: The Open Ledger

How public visibility builds trust in decentralized systems.

Publicly Verifiable

All transactions and data on a public blockchain are visible to anyone, fostering accountability.

Trustless Verification

Users can verify the integrity of data themselves, eliminating the need to trust intermediaries.

Misconception

Transparency does not equal anonymity. While identities are often pseudonymous, transactions are traceable on public blockchains.

Key Takeaway

Transparency on public blockchains allows anyone to view and verify transactions, fostering accountability and trust without relying on central authorities.
