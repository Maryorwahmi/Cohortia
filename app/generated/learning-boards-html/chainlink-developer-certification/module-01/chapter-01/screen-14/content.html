Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 2

Consolidating your understanding of Web3 technologies and applications.

Immutability

The property of data on a blockchain that, once recorded, cannot be altered or deleted.

Transparency

The ability for all participants to view and verify transactions and data on the blockchain.

Smart Contract

A self-executing agreement with terms written into code, deployed and run on a blockchain.

Summary Takeaway

Immutability, transparency, smart contracts, and dApps are the core technological components that enable the trustless and automated nature of Web3.
