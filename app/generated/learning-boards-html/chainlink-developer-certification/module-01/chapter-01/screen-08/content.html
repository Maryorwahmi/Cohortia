Smart Contracts: Self-Executing Agreements

The code that automates trust on the blockchain.

Code-Based Agreements

Terms are written directly into code, which automatically executes when conditions are met.

Tamper-Proof & Immutable

Deployed on a blockchain, they inherit security, ensuring their logic cannot be changed.

Analogy

Think of a smart contract like a vending machine: you put in the correct input (money), and it automatically dispenses the output (snack) based on its programmed logic, without human intervention.

Key Takeaway

Smart contracts automate agreements on the blockchain, providing a trustless and efficient way to execute logic without intermediaries.
