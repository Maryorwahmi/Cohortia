Abstraction and Future-Proofing

Designing for maintainability and upgradeability in your dApps.

Benefits of Abstraction

Wrapping the AggregatorV3Interface in your own contract creates a clean abstraction layer.

  • Decouples your dApp logic from direct Chainlink interface details.
  • Simplifies future upgrades or changes to the underlying data feed.
  • Enhances code readability and maintainability.
Scenario

If Chainlink updates its `AggregatorV3Interface`, only your `PriceConsumerV3` needs modification, not every contract that uses it.
