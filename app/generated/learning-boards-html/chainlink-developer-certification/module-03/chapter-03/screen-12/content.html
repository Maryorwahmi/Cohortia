Common Mistakes and Safety Notes

Not Checking `answeredInRound`

Can lead to using an old price if the network experiences a temporary hiccup or a round is skipped.

Not Checking `updatedAt`

The most common vulnerability; dApps might use prices that are hours or days old, leading to exploits.

Hardcoding `STALE_DATA_THRESHOLD`

This value might need to be configurable in production to adapt to changing market conditions or feed configs.

Assuming `price` is always positive

For most asset prices, this is true, but adding `price > 0` as a sanity check is good defensive practice.

Pro-Tip: Gas Costs

These `require` checks are cheap in terms of gas, as they are `view` calls and revert early if conditions are not met, preventing further execution and saving gas for invalid states.
