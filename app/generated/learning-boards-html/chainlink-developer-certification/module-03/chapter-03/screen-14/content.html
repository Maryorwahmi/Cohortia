Key Concepts (Continued)

Advanced parameters and thresholds for data feed management.

`roundId`

The current round identifier. Used to verify data validity by comparing with `answeredInRound`.

`STALE_DATA_THRESHOLD`

A configurable maximum time difference (in seconds) for acceptable data freshness in a dApp.

`heartbeat` & `deviationThreshold`

Chainlink parameters governing update frequency: `heartbeat` for time, `deviationThreshold` for price change.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding `roundId`, setting a `STALE_DATA_THRESHOLD`, and knowing `heartbeat` and `deviationThreshold` are crucial for robust data feed integration.
