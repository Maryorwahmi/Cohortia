Why Data Freshness Matters

Vulnerability Risk

Outdated prices can lead to exploits, incorrect liquidations, or unfair trades, compromising dApp security.

Market Volatility

Prices change rapidly. Stale data fails to reflect current market conditions, leading to inaccurate calculations.

Time-Sensitive Operations

Operations like liquidations or margin calls demand real-time data. Delays can have severe consequences.
