Practical Uses of Historical Data

Leveraging getRoundData() for advanced dApp features

Auditing Past Transactions

Verify past transactions against historical prices, ensuring operations were executed correctly based on the price at that exact moment.

Example: Confirming a liquidation was fair by checking the price at the time it occurred.

Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP)

Calculate an average price over a period by querying multiple historical rounds. More robust than a single spot price for large trades.

Example: Determine a fairer liquidation price over an hour using multiple historical data points.

On-Chain Analytics

Build on-chain analytics tools that track price movements over time, visualize trends, or backtest strategies directly within your smart contract logic.

Example: Track the average daily price of an asset over the last month for a dashboard.
Key Takeaway

Historical data retrieval is a powerful feature for building transparent, fair, and analytically rich dApps.
