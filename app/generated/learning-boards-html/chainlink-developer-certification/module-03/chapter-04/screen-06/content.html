Choosing the Right Data Feed: Core Criteria

Ensuring accuracy and relevance for your dApp

Network Compatibility

Ensure the data feed is on the correct blockchain where your dApp is deployed (e.g., Ethereum Mainnet, Polygon, Arbitrum).

Example: Polygon dApp needs a Polygon-native feed.

Exact Asset Pair

Double-check that it's the precise asset pair you need (e.g., ETH/USD vs. WETH/USD). Small differences matter.

Example: Using WETH/USD for an ETH-denominated loan can cause errors.

Correct Decimals Interpretation

Always be aware of the decimals() value for correct interpretation of the price. Misinterpreting this can lead to significant calculation errors.

Example: A price of 180000000000 with 8 decimals is 1800.00.
Key Takeaway

Thoroughly vetting the network, asset pair, and decimals of your chosen data feed is non-negotiable for dApp reliability.
