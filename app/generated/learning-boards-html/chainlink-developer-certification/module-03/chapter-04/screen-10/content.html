Implementing Robust Error Handling: Manual Overrides & Graceful Degradation

Alternative and complementary strategies for dApp resilience

Manual Override

A trusted multisig or admin key can manually set a price as a last resort during emergencies, ensuring critical operations can continue.

Example: DAO votes to approve a manual price update during extreme market volatility.

Graceful Degradation

Design the dApp to operate in a limited capacity without real-time price data, disabling non-critical features rather than halting entirely.

Example: A game pauses competitive play but allows inventory viewing if price data is down.
Key Takeaway

These strategies provide multi-layered resilience, ensuring dApps can adapt and maintain functionality even during severe data disruptions.
