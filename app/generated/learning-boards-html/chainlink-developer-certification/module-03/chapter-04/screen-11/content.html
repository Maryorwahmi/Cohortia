Security Best Practices: Access Control & Reentrancy Guards

Protecting your smart contracts from common vulnerabilities

Access Control

Ensure only authorized functions or roles can call price-sensitive operations. Prevents unauthorized manipulation.

Example: Only a designated liquidator role can call executeLiquidation().

Reentrancy Guards

If your contract makes external calls after fetching a price, use reentrancyGuard or Checks-Effects-Interactions pattern.

Example: Update internal balances *before* sending ETH in a withdrawal function.
Key Takeaway

These two practices are fundamental for preventing common smart contract exploits and ensuring the integrity of your dApp's logic.
