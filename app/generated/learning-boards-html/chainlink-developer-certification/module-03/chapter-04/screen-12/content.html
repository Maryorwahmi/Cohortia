Security Best Practices: Upgradability

Ensuring long-term adaptability for your dApp

Why Upgradability?

Allows for updates to the Chainlink AggregatorV3Interface address or your STALE_DATA_THRESHOLD without redeploying the entire dApp.

Proxy Patterns

Typically implemented via proxy contracts, which point to an upgradable implementation contract, enabling seamless logic updates.

Example Scenario

Chainlink releases a new version of its aggregator contract. With upgradability, you can update your dApp to use the new address without a costly migration.

Benefit

Enhances security by allowing quick vulnerability patches and provides flexibility to adapt to ecosystem changes, ensuring long-term viability.

Key Takeaway

Upgradability is a crucial investment for long-lived dApps, enabling secure and flexible adaptation to evolving blockchain environments.
