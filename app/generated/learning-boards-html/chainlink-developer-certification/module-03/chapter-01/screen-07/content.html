Decentralized Oracle Networks (DONs)

Collaborating for Data Integrity

Group of Chainlink Nodes

A DON is a collection of independent Chainlink nodes working together.

Provide Specific Data Feeds

Each DON is dedicated to providing a particular data feed, e.g., ETH/USD price.

Consensus Mechanism

Nodes within a DON collaborate to reach a consensus on the correct data value, ensuring integrity.

Network of Trust

DONs ensure data resilience and decentralization by aggregating reports from multiple nodes.

Example: A DON for weather data might have 15 nodes, each reporting from different weather stations.
Key Takeaway

DONs are crucial for ensuring data integrity and resisting manipulation through collective reporting and consensus.
