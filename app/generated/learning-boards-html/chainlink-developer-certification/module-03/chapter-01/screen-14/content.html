Key Concepts

Advanced Components and Data Flow

Aggregator Contracts

On-chain smart contracts that aggregate and store data from DONs.

Off-chain Reporting (OCR)

Protocol for off-chain data aggregation and single, gas-efficient on-chain submission.

Data Feed

A continuous stream of aggregated, real-world data provided by a Chainlink DON.

Summary Takeaway

Chainlink's advanced mechanisms ensure efficient, secure, and continuous delivery of real-world data to smart contracts.
