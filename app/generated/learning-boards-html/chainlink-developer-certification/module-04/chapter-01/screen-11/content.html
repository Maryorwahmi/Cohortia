Advanced Features

Granular Control in VRF v2

Optimizing gas and flexibility for your dApps.

Callback Gas Limit

  • Explicit Control: Set a specific gas limit for your fulfillRandomWords function.
  • Prevents Failures: Ensures complex callback logic has enough gas to execute successfully.
  • Improved Reliability: Reduces the risk of transactions reverting due to out-of-gas errors.
Key Takeaway

VRF v2's enhanced control features lead to more reliable, gas-efficient, and flexible dApp development.

Request Multiple Random Words

  • Single Request: Obtain multiple random numbers in one VRF request.
  • Gas Optimization: Reduces transaction costs compared to multiple individual requests.
  • Streamlined Logic: Simplifies dApp development for scenarios needing several random values.
Developer Benefit

These features make VRF v2 a more robust, cost-effective, and user-friendly solution for integrating secure randomness.
