Key Concepts

Essential ideas from this chapter

On-chain Randomness

The inherent challenge of generating truly unpredictable and secure random numbers directly within a deterministic blockchain environment.

Pseudo-randomness

Random number generation methods that appear random but are based on deterministic algorithms and can be predicted or manipulated on-chain.

Verifiable Random Function (VRF)

A cryptographic primitive that generates a random number along with a proof that the number was generated correctly and without bias.

Chapter Summary

Understanding the inherent challenges of on-chain randomness and the vulnerabilities of pseudo-random methods is crucial for appreciating the secure, verifiable solution offered by VRF.
