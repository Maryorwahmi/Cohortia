Verifiable Random Functions (VRF)

A cryptographically secure approach to on-chain randomness.

What is a VRF?

  • Off-Chain Generation: Random numbers are generated outside the blockchain by an oracle.
  • Cryptographic Proof: A mathematical proof accompanies the random number, verifying its integrity.
  • On-Chain Verification: Smart contracts can verify the proof to confirm the randomness is unbiased and untampered.
  • Provably Fair: Ensures that the random number was generated correctly and cannot be manipulated.
Key Takeaway

VRF provides a robust, cryptographically secure source of randomness that is provably fair and tamper-proof for smart contracts.

Real-World Connection

Imagine a digital lottery where a trusted third party rolls a dice and provides a signed, verifiable receipt of the roll to everyone.
