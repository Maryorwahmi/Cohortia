Troubleshooting

Common VRF v2 Integration Issues

Identifying and resolving frequent problems during VRF implementation.

Insufficient LINK Funding

Your subscription might not have enough LINK to cover the gas costs of requests and callbacks, causing transactions to revert.

Consumer Not Authorized

You must explicitly add your deployed consumer contract's address to the VRF v2 subscription's allowed consumers list.

Low CALLBACK_GAS_LIMIT

If the callback gas limit is too low, fulfillRandomWords will run out of gas and fail, preventing random numbers from being received.

Troubleshooting Tip

Always check the Chainlink VRF v2 Coordinator UI for your subscription's LINK balance and authorized consumers. For gas issues, estimate callback gas on a local network.

Key Takeaway

Proactive checks on funding, authorization, and careful estimation of CALLBACK_GAS_LIMIT are essential to prevent and troubleshoot common VRF v2 integration failures.
