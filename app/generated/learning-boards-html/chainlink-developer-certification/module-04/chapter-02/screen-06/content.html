Contract Foundation

Building Your VRF Consumer Contract

Inheriting from VRFConsumerBaseV2 and understanding constructor parameters.

Inherit from Base Contract

Your Solidity contract must inherit from VRFConsumerBaseV2.sol to access necessary interfaces and helper functions for VRF interaction.

VRF Coordinator Address

The address of the Chainlink VRF Coordinator contract on your chosen testnet. This is how your contract finds the VRF service.

Subscription ID

Your unique VRF v2 subscription ID, linking your contract to its funded LINK balance for requests.

Key Hash (Gas Lane)

A bytes32 identifier for the VRF network and the maximum gas price the oracle node will pay for fulfillment.

Concrete Example

When deploying your contract to Sepolia, you'd pass the specific Sepolia VRF Coordinator address, your Sepolia subscription ID, and the corresponding Sepolia key hash into the constructor.

Key Takeaway

The constructor parameters are critical for linking your consumer contract to the correct VRF service and subscription, enabling it to make valid requests.
