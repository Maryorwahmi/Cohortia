Request Parameters

Understanding requestRandomWords Parameters

Configuring your VRF request for security, cost, and desired output.

i_keyHash (Gas Lane)

Identifies the VRF network and sets the maximum gas price the oracle will pay for fulfillment.

i_subscriptionId

Your unique VRF v2 subscription ID, linking the request to your funded LINK balance.

requestConfirmations

Number of blocks the VRF service waits for transaction finality, enhancing security against reorgs.

CALLBACK_GAS_LIMIT

Maximum gas the VRF Coordinator can spend to execute your fulfillRandomWords callback function.

Consequence of Low Gas Limit

If CALLBACK_GAS_LIMIT is too low, fulfillRandomWords will fail, and your contract won't receive the random numbers, leading to lost LINK.

Key Takeaway

Each parameter in requestRandomWords is vital for the security, cost-efficiency, and successful asynchronous delivery of verifiable randomness to your dApp.
