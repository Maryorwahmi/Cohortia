Setup Guide

Setting Up Your VRF v2 Subscription

Configure and fund your subscription using the Chainlink VRF Coordinator UI.

Step 1

Connect Wallet & Fund

Connect MetaMask to a testnet (e.g., Sepolia) and ensure it's funded with testnet ETH and LINK from Chainlink Faucets.

Step 2

Create Subscription

Navigate to the Chainlink VRF v2 Coordinator UI for your testnet and create a new subscription, noting down the unique subscriptionId.

Step 3

Fund with LINK

Fund your newly created subscription with sufficient testnet LINK tokens to cover future VRF request and callback gas costs.

Step 4

Add Consumer Address

Once deployed, add your consumer contract's address to the subscription's allowed consumers list in the Coordinator UI.

Common Mistake

Forgetting to fund the subscription or not adding your consumer contract's address will cause VRF requests to revert.

Pro-Tip

Always over-estimate your LINK funding slightly to account for fluctuating gas prices and unexpected callback complexity.

Key Takeaway

A properly configured and funded VRF v2 subscription, with your consumer contract authorized, is the essential first step for any on-chain randomness requests.
