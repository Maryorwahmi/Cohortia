Gas Management

Understanding CALLBACK_GAS_LIMIT

Ensuring your VRF fulfillment callback has enough gas to succeed.

Purpose

Specifies the maximum gas the VRF Coordinator can spend to execute your fulfillRandomWords function.

Consequence of Too Low

If set too low, fulfillRandomWords will run out of gas, revert, and your contract will not receive the random numbers, leading to lost LINK.

Good Practice

Estimate gas usage on a local network, then add a significant buffer (20-50%) to your CALLBACK_GAS_LIMIT.

Misconception

A higher CALLBACK_GAS_LIMIT doesn't necessarily mean higher costs; it's a maximum. You only pay for the gas actually consumed.

Key Takeaway

Accurately setting CALLBACK_GAS_LIMIT is paramount for reliable VRF fulfillment, preventing silent failures and ensuring your dApp receives its random numbers.
