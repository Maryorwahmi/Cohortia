Chapter Review

Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

requestRandomWords

The function called by a consumer contract to initiate a request for cryptographically secure random numbers.

fulfillRandomWords

The callback function invoked by the VRF Coordinator to deliver the generated random numbers and their proof.

callbackGasLimit

A crucial parameter specifying the maximum gas the VRF Coordinator can spend to execute the fulfillRandomWords function.

Summary Takeaway

The interplay between requestRandomWords, fulfillRandomWords, and the carefully set callbackGasLimit forms the core of secure and efficient VRF v2 integration.
