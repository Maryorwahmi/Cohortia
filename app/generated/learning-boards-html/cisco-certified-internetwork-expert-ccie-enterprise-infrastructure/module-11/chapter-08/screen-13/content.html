Why honesty matters
Mock lab scores only mean something if they reflect unaided performance. Hints and lookups should be recorded separately so you can trust the trends your data shows.
Create a 'learning log' for any hint: record the hint, why you needed it, and the follow-up exercises you will do so the next mock measures real improvement.
Make a rule
Decide one integrity rule for your next mock, such as 'no internet lookup' or 'record every hint in the learning log.'
