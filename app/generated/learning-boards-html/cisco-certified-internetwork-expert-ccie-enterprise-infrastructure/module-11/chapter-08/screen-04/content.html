8-hour mock timeline (example)
Design • Deploy • Operate & Optimize
Design: 2 hours — architecture, IP plan, high-level verification.
Deploy: 3 hours — configure routing, switching, services, and verify reachability.
Operate & Optimize: 3 hours — fine-tune policies, troubleshooting, final verifications.
Concrete check
Cross-reference your lab tasks with the blueprint. Ensure every major topic appears across the full run, not just in isolation.
Why short or partial mocks mislead
  • They mask stamina issues because you never simulate full attention drift.
  • They hide time-management failures that only appear when tasks accumulate.
  • They encourage score-chasing on isolated tasks instead of diagnostic learning.
Example action
Combine two targeted labs that cover routing and SD-WAN into a single 8-hour script. Add verification checkpoints and timers to enforce realistic pacing.
