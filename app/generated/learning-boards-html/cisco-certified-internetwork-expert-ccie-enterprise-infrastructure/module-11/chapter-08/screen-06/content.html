Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Snapshots & Logs
Snapshot schedule (example)
After initial IP plan and addressing
After core routing is up and adjacency verified
Before policy or redistribution changes
Command-log review steps
  1. Export or view the terminal session log for the task.
  2. Highlight the first incorrect command or missed verification.
  3. Tag the root cause and note time spent before the fix.
Example snippet
Log shows repeated attempts to match a route-map with incorrect sequence numbers. The fix was a reorder, not a new policy. This reveals a configuration habit, not a theory gap.
Why this matters
Snapshots and logs turn time-consuming rebuilds into focused replays and produce precise evidence for remediation tasks.
