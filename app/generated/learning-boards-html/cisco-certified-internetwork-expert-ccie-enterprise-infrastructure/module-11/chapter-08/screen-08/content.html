Remediation mapping
  • Knowledge: short theory review + 2 targeted labs.
  • Configuration: typed command drills and checklist verification.

Concrete example
If BGP path selection costs points, build a 2-hour lab where every exercise isolates path attributes, then re-run a quick verification checklist to confirm understanding.
Misconception to avoid
Don't assume low scores mean you simply need more hours. Often a short focused plan resolves repeated errors faster than more full mocks.
