Learning Objectives

Four verifiable milestones to guide your study and lab practice.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Articulate exam sections

Explain the goals of Design, Deploy, and Operate & Optimize and why each requires a different approach.

Mnemonic: D-D-O (Design, Deploy, Operate)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe scoring methodology

Understand points per task, partial credit, and the implication of section thresholds on overall pass/fail.

Visual mnemonic: weighted bar showing partial credit
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify lab components

Recognize typical devices, baseline topologies, available tools and the constraints of the workstation environment.

Timeline mnemonic: book, schedule, workstation
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Outline booking & preparation

Plan slot booking, know exam-day rules, and prepare physically and mentally to avoid preventable mistakes.

Mnemonic: Reserve, Prepare, Verify
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
