Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Common Pitfalls
Pitfall: Time Allocation

Overfocusing on early low-value tasks. Fix: skim tasks and prioritize high-point items early.

Pitfall: Missing Verification

Not confirming operational state after changes. Fix: run immediate show commands and end-to-end pings.

Pitfall: Design Mismatch

Choosing solutions that contradict requirements. Fix: reference constraints explicitly in your design rationale.

Actionable Exercise

After a practice run, note one time allocation change and one verification habit to fix. Implement both in the next mock lab.
