Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Time Allocation
Suggested allocation
Design: 1.5–2 hours

Read requirements and justify a coherent design before any configuration.

Deploy: 4–4.5 hours

Hands-on configuration and verification; prioritize high-point tasks.

Operate & Optimize: 1.5–2 hours

Troubleshoot and tune the network; verify that previous changes hold under load.

Practical routine
  • Skim all tasks in the first 10–15 minutes to plan priorities.
  • Reserve short verification windows after each major change.
  • Keep 30–45 minutes at the end for cross-module checks and final fixes.
Practice drill

Run a timed mock lab and note where you over or under-shot your planned blocks. Adjust allocations based on task weight and personal speed.

Key Takeaway

Timeboxing and immediate verification reduce cascading errors and keep you on track for high-value tasks.
