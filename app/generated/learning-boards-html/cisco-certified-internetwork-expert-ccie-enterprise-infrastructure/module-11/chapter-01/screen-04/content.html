Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Design Section
Definition

Design: extract constraints and justify choices

The section evaluates your architectural understanding and your ability to align technology choices with business goals and constraints.

Extract explicit constraints from the scenario before proposing solutions.

Provide trade-offs and a short justification for each recommended technology.

Avoid assuming implementation details that contradict constraints in the brief.

Example Scenario
Retail branch with segmentation and limited WAN

Choose VLAN segmentation, local gateway redundancy, and bandwidth-conserving WAN options. Justify the trade-offs between cost and recovery time.

Key Takeaway

Design answers why a solution fits requirements. A clear rationale prevents misaligned deploy work later.

Practice Callout
Read the brief. List three constraints. Propose two options. Write a one-paragraph justification for your top choice. Example: Constraints: limited WAN bandwidth, need for segmentation, fast failover. Option A: Centralized firewall + MPLS. Option B: Local firewall with VPN and redundant local gateway. Justification: Option B balances cost while meeting segmentation and recovery targets by keeping failover local and reducing WAN traffic.
Common Mistake
Watch out

Proposing complex solutions that ignore stated constraints, such as cost or maintenance limits.
