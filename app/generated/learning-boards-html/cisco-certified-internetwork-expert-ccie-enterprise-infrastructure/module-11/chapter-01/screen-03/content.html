Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Practice Milestones
Design Milestone
Extract constraints and justify a design in 90 minutes

Practice: read a scenario, list constraints, pick technologies, and write a short rationale. Verify choices against requirements.

Scoring Milestone
Self-mark tasks and log partial credit cases

Practice awarding points for partial solutions so you know what qualifies for partial credit in real lab tasks.

Booking & Preparation
Rehearse arrival and workstation setup

Simulate the exam start: open tools, load topology, and run quick verification commands to warm up your workflow.

Lab Components Milestone
Know device families and CLI patterns

Practice core commands such as show ip interface brief and show running-config to become fluent before the exam.

Verification Habit
Verify immediately after each change

Example: after configuring OSPF, run show ip ospf neighbor. This reduces rework during the operate module.
