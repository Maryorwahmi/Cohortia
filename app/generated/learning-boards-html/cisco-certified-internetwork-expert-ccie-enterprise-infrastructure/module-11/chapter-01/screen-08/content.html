Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Scoring Methodology
Concept

Task-based scoring with partial credit

Points are assigned per task or subtask. Accumulated points determine the overall result rather than a strict per-module pass/fail.

Practical implication

Work through as many tasks as possible and secure partial credit where full completion is unlikely. Small tasks contribute meaningfully to the total score.

Common Misconception

Assuming a single module can be sacrificed entirely. Low performance in Design can undermine an otherwise strong Deploy performance.

Practice Drill

Run a mock lab, divide tasks into points, and self-mark. Note partial-credit opportunities you might achieve quickly under time pressure.

Key Takeaway

Aim to collect points broadly across tasks and use partial-credit opportunities to improve overall score.
