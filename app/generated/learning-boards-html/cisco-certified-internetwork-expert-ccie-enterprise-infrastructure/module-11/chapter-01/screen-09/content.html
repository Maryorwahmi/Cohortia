Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Booking & Logistics
Booking

Plan and book a lab slot with enough preparation time. Lab locations vary; schedule travel and rest accordingly.

Exam-day expectations

You will use a provided workstation with baseline topology and limited references. Proctors enforce rules and do not provide technical help.

First-15-minute checklist
  • Open topology and confirm baseline is reachable
  • Run show ip interface brief and show running-config
  • Confirm available tools and note any restrictions
Practical tips

Arrive well-rested, bring required ID and permitted items only, and use the initial minutes to orient to the workstation to avoid start-of-exam surprises.

Key Takeaway

Administrative readiness reduces stress. Use early minutes to stabilize the environment and check key show outputs.
