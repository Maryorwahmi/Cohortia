Core Concept Treat the CCIE Blueprint as the Syllabus
Spreadsheet structure

Create columns for blueprint section, sub-topic, confidence (1–5), primary resource, lab idea, and last-reviewed date. Use this as your master tracker.

"Blueprint Section","Sub-topic","Confidence Level (1-5)","Primary Resource","Lab Scenario Idea","Date Last Reviewed"
"1.0 Network Infrastructure","1.2 Implement and Troubleshoot Layer 3 Technologies","4","Cisco OSPF Command Ref","Design OSPF multi-area with summarization","2023-10-28"
"Routing Protocols","BGP: multi-homing & local-pref","2","Cisco BGP Config Guide","Implement multi-homed BGP with local preference policies",""
Example
BGP multi-homing lab idea
Design AS edge routers, set local preference to prefer one path, verify route selection and failover behavior.
Common mistake
Assuming topic covered
Believing you 'covered' a topic without a recorded lab or confidence score leads to hidden gaps.
Key Takeaway
Make the blueprint actionable: every item gets a resource, a lab, and a confidence rating you update after practice.
