Active Learning Spaced Repetition and Active Recall
How to use flashcards
Create atomic cards: each card should test a single fact or short command sequence. Keep questions compact and answers exact.
Sample card
Q: How to show OSPF neighbors and their states?
A: Use the command: show ip ospf neighbor
Practice
Try to configure a VLAN or route from memory, then check your work to identify gaps.
Misconception
Flashcards are a replacement for labs — they are not. Use them to support recall only.
Key Takeaway
Pair spaced repetition for facts with active recall exercises that force synthesis and troubleshooting in the lab.
