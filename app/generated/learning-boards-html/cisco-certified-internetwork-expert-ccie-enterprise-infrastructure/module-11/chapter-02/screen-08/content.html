Lab Mastery Designing Effective Lab Sessions
Session progression
  1. Guided build to learn the expected steps.
  2. Recall build from memory to force retrieval.
  3. Fault injection and troubleshooting for diagnostic skill.
  4. Timed task to practice prioritization and pacing.
Example
OSPF multi-area: build, reconfigure from memory, inject route summarization errors, then recover within a time limit.
Post-mortem
Categorize failures as concept, syntax, or time issues and record corrective actions in your spreadsheet.
Key Takeaway
Progressive challenge and structured post-mortems turn errors into a precise study roadmap.
