Iterative Planning Make Your Study Plan Dynamic
Weekly review ritual
At the end of each week, review mock lab results, update confidence scores, and set three priorities for next week: one high-priority remediation, one maintenance area, and one new challenge.
High-priority
Topics with low confidence and recurring errors.
Maintenance
Topics with stable confidence that need periodic review.
New challenge
Introduce a tougher lab or a design problem to expand capability.
Example
If BGP policy tests remain weak, schedule two focused BGP labs and create three flashcards for policy command syntax.
Misconception
Believing a plan is fixed. Instead, use weekly data to reassign effort dynamically.
Key Takeaway
Treat your plan as feedback-driven: change it when results say something different than your intention.
