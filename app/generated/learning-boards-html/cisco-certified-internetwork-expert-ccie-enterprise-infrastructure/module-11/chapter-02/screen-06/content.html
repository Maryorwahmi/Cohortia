Practice Strategy Adopt a 70/30 Lab-to-Theory Split
Weekly schedule template
Lab Blocks
3×2 hour sessions for topology builds and timed troubleshooting.
Theory Reviews
1×1 hour session for targeted reading and clarifying concepts.
Active Recall
Daily 20-minute flashcard or free-recall exercise.
Example
Topology from memory
Attempt to design and configure a multi-area OSPF network without notes, then verify with documentation.
Pitfall
Passive repetition
Repeating the same lab without increasing challenge or focusing on errors will stall progress.
Key Takeaway
Make lab sessions deliberately harder and use theory to close gaps uncovered during practice.
