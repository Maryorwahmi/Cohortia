Module 11 › Chapter 11.9 › Pre-Exam Timeline
Evening Before
  • Finish heavy study 1 hour before bed; light review only.
  • Pack bag: snacks, documents, clothes, quick-reference notes.
  • Early dinner and relaxing activity; aim for 7–9 hours sleep.
Morning Of
  • Wake early enough to stretch and eat a balanced breakfast.
  • Quick review: five verification commands or checks for 10 minutes.
  • Box-breathe for two minutes; leave with extra travel time and arrive early.
Practice plan
Run this timeline three times before test day. Each rehearsal reduces surprise and frees cognitive capacity on the exam morning.
