Box breathing: a reliable pattern
Inhale 4 • Hold 4 • Exhale 4 • Hold 4. Repeat 2–3 cycles.
Inhale  4 counts
Hold    4 counts
Exhale  4 counts
Hold    4 counts
Repeat  2–3 cycles
Practice during study sessions until the sequence is automatic. Combine with a short micro-break to interrupt rumination and let your mind reprioritize.
Micro-break mechanics
  • Duration: 30–60 seconds. Strictly enforced.
  • Action: stand, stretch, gaze at a distant object, take three slow breaths.
  • Goal: interrupt panic loops and restore working memory capacity.
Example: after repeated errors during route redistribution, box-breathe twice, stand for 45 seconds, then return and re-check basic connectivity.
Common mistake: letting breaks become avoidance. Use a fixed internal rule: break = 60 seconds max, then resume with a defined next task.
