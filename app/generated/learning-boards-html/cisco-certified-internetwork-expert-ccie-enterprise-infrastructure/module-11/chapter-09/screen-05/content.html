Four-step reset
Acknowledge → Breathe → Micro-break → Act
1. Acknowledge: Name the feeling. Saying "I'm stressed" reduces its power and stops suppression.
2. Box breathing: inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Repeat 2–3 cycles to lower arousal.
3. Micro-break: 30–60 seconds. Stretch, close eyes, look away—no problem solving.
4. Act: Save config if needed, pick a quick-win, and commit to a small, timed step.
Supporting notes
  • Practice the breathing until it reliably reduces rapid heartbeat; the technique must be automatic.
  • Keep micro-breaks strict and short—use a mental timer or count to twenty slowly.
  • Always end a reset with a specific action to avoid avoidance cycles.
Example: after 25 minutes stuck on OSPF redistribution, say "I'm stuck," box-breathe twice, stretch for 45 seconds, then save and switch to a known verification task.
Common mistake: excessive debugging during stress. Instead, use a structured troubleshooting plan after the reset.
