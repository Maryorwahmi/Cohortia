Learning Objectives
Four measurable skills to rehearse and verify before exam day.
01
Manage stress and anxiety
Learn quick interventions—acknowledge stress, use box breathing, and take micro-breaks to restore focus.
Mnemonic: Breathe → Break → Back
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Cultivate positive self-talk
Practice reframing negative thoughts into constructive steps and short affirmations to regain momentum.
Mnemonic: Notice → Name → Reframe
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement exam day routines
Build repeatable evening and morning rituals that optimize sleep, nutrition, and readiness without surprises.
Mnemonic: Plan → Pack → Practice
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Understand proctor etiquette
Know what to report, how to report it clearly, and what questions are inappropriate during the exam.
Mnemonic: Report → Respect → Repeat
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Bloom's taxonomy
Learn
Understand
Apply
Master
Practice each objective during mock labs, then verify with short checklists: can you reset focus in 60 seconds? Can you reframe a negative thought into an actionable step? Can you follow your pre-exam checklist under time pressure? Can you report equipment issues concisely?
