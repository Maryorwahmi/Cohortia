Module 11 › Chapter 11.9 › Practice & Verification
Rehearse under pressure
Short, repeatable simulations build automatic responses that survive anxiety and fatigue.
Timed mock lab: run a 90-minute session and apply a breathing/reset when you feel stuck. Record whether focus returned within 60 seconds.
Self-talk drill: when an error occurs, vocalize a reframe and an immediate next action. Repeat until it’s natural.
Checklist test: run through your evening and morning routine three times and note deviations.
Verification cards
Breathing verified: Calm returned within 60s × 2
Self-talk verified: Reframe spoken and followed by action × 2
Routine verified: No missing items × 3 mornings
Proctor script verified: Clear report phrased and timed × 2
Key Takeaway

Measurement turns practice into trust. Use simple pass/fail checks during mock labs to know when a technique is reliable.
