Module 11 › Chapter 11.9 › Preparation Routines
Routine architecture
Sleep → Nutrition → Exercise → Ritual → Arrival
Sleep: maintain consistent schedule for two weeks prior; avoid last-minute cramming.
Nutrition: balanced meal before start; bring protein and complex carbs for steady energy.
Exercise: short walk or mobility routine to reduce tension and sharpen focus.
Pre-exam ritual example
  1. Evening: light review, pack bag, prepare snacks, early dinner, relax.
  2. Morning: light stretching, healthy breakfast, quick review of five key commands, box-breathe for two minutes.
  3. Arrival: arrive 45–90 minutes early, settle, and perform one final breathing cycle.
Example: bring a small notebook with quick verification commands and a list of high-value tasks to preserve cognitive memory.
Key Takeaway

Make routines predictable and repeat them before mock labs so they reduce surprises on test day.
