Why this matters
Stress narrows attention and slows problem-solving. Practiced rituals and short resets protect your working memory so troubleshooting stays efficient across long sessions.

Breathing, mini-breaks, positive self-talk, quick decision rules, and proctor communication—each practiced until instinctive.
