Appropriate vs Inappropriate
Appropriate: "My keyboard is not responding; I need a replacement." Report condition, impact, and request.
Inappropriate: Asking for hints, detailed clarifications about tasks, or technical advice on solutions.
Scripted report example
"My console session disconnected and devices are not reachable. I can’t continue this task. Please advise on environmental support or equipment replacement." Keep it factual and brief.
Practice: rehearse a five-sentence report for common issues during mock labs so you can communicate quickly under stress.
Pro tip: avoid casual conversation to relieve stress; it wastes time and can be misinterpreted.
