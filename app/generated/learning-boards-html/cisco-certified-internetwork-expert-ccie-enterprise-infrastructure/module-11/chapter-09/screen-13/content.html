Template: Triggers & Coping
List triggers and assign a specific coping action to each.
Trigger: stuck >15 minutes → Action: save config, box-breathe 2 cycles, switch to quick-win task.
Trigger: clock anxiety → Action: take a 30s micro-break and then review remaining tasks by value.
Trigger: cascading service failures → Action: verify core connectivity, document state, escalate to a different task.
Routine & Affirmations
Short scripts you can run automatically.
Evening before: light review (≤60 min), pack bag, early sleep.
Morning: light exercise, healthy breakfast, review 5 commands, two-minute box-breathe.
Affirmations: "I am prepared." "I will take one step at a time." "I will prioritize completing tasks."
Practice check

Rehearse the plan in three mock labs. Verify breathing calms you within 60 seconds and that you can switch tasks without losing time.
