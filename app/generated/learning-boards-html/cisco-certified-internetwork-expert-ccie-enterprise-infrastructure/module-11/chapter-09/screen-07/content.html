Negative vs Positive
Negative: "I can't do this; I'm failing." Outcome: narrow attention, increased panic, poor choices.
Positive reframe: "This is hard; verify basics for five minutes." Outcome: actionable focus, improved recovery.
Practice the reframe aloud during mock labs to build a default habit. The script should be short: observe, single step, and timed commitment.
Supporting practice
Script example: "I'm stuck on this policy. Step one: check connectivity to controller. I'll try for 5 minutes."
Drill: when frustrated, verbalize negative then immediately reframe and act. Repeat during mock labs until reframe is automatic.
Pro tip

Constructive self-talk focuses on the next verifiable action, not on judged outcomes.
