Module 11 › Chapter 11.9 › Caffeine & Energy
Moderation is the rule
Avoid energy spikes and crashes. Test any stimulant plan during practice, not on exam day for the first time.
Prefer protein and complex carbs for steady glucose. Bring small snacks like nuts, fruit, or energy bars.
Hydrate consistently. Dehydration worsens concentration and fatigue.
Practice plan
Run a mock exam with your intended caffeine and snack strategy. Record whether jitteriness, clarity, or crashes occur and adjust accordingly.
Warning: excessive stimulants increase anxiety and can cause performance drops. Trust preparation, not stimulants.
