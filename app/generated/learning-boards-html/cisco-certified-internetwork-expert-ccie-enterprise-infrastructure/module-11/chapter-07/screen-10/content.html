Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Connecting Virtual Labs to Physical Networks
Safety checklist
  • Use NAT or a single bridged interface, not full bridging for all lab traffic.
  • Run lab services on local lab servers instead of public internet where possible.
  • Apply firewall rules to prevent accidental exposure of management ports.
Concrete example
Create a small NATed DMZ for your lab and host a local DNS/NTP service. Point virtual devices to that service for realistic behavior without exposing devices to your home network.
Key Takeaway

External connectivity is valuable but should be scoped tightly to avoid security and licensing issues.
