Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Resource Management Strategies
Hardware & images
Suggested host guidance: prioritize RAM and NVMe responsiveness. Use lean IOS XE variants when they meet your features needs and reserve heavy images for when fidelity is required.
Image optimization
Import smaller images or reduced feature builds to shrink RAM footprint and increase density per host.
Selective power‑on
Power only the nodes required for a task to free CPU and RAM for active devices.
Snapshotting
Save known good states and revert quickly after errors to avoid long reconfiguration time.
Practical checks
When troubleshooting a single router in a 20‑node lab, power off unrelated nodes, open a snapshot of the last working state for the router set, and avoid re-importing images unless necessary.
