Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Activity: Build and Measure
Follow the activity steps, snapshot a working state, then vary the number of active nodes and record CPU/RAM changes. Use the results to tune image choices and selective power‑on habits.
Activity steps
  1. Create lab and add devices.
  2. Configure IPs and OSPF, verify pings.
  3. Snapshot working state.
  4. Power additional nodes and record host CPU/RAM.
Measurement log
Note baseline CPU/RAM after three nodes, then after 10 nodes, and after 20 nodes. This informs your scale strategy and when to use lean images or cloud/off‑host emulation.
Key Takeaway

Measuring resource usage under realistic conditions helps you choose images and plan selective power‑on strategies for reliable CCIE practice.
