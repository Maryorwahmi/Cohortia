Module 11 › Chapter 11.3 › Mental Resilience
Practical techniques
  • 30-second breathing pause to reset focus.
  • Positive self-talk to reframe setbacks as solvable challenges.
  • Micro-goals and immediate verifications for frequent wins.
Physical prep
Rest well before exam, stay hydrated, and use accepted snacks to maintain energy during long sections.
Guided micro-break
Close your eyes for 30 seconds, inhale for four counts, hold two, exhale for five. Repeat twice. Return to the console with the aim to verify a small item quickly.
Key Takeaway
Small mental resets and micro-goals maintain decision quality across long exams.
