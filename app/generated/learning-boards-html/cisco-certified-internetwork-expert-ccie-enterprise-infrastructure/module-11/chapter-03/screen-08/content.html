Five-step recovery routine

  1. Stop: acknowledge the timebox end.
  2. Save: write the configuration to memory or the running file.
  3. Document: short note of commands tried and observed symptoms.
  4. Switch: move to a prioritized quick task to secure points.
  5. Return: come back later with fresh perspective and the notes you left.
Practical tip
Keep your notes brief: commands run, interface checked, and the minute mark. Long logs slow you down later.
Scenario
You spent 18 minutes on an OSPF adjacency that won't form. Use the routine: save, note commands run, switch to an ACL task you can complete in 6 minutes, then return to OSPF with fresh eyes and preserved config.
Key Takeaway
Small structured moves protect your momentum and maximize earned points across the exam.
