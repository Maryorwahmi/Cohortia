Module 11 › Chapter 11.3 › Prioritization
Priority Hierarchy
Level 1 — Quick Wins
Small configurations you can verify immediately and that award certain points.
Level 2 — Foundations
Core reachability, adjacency, and trunking that other tasks rely upon.
Level 3 — Advanced Services
QoS, redistribution, complex security—perform after baseline is stable.
Common mistake
Tackling advanced redistribution before OSPF/EIGRP adjacency exists leads to wasted time and low points.
Scenario Card
You have tasks: basic addressing (5 pts), OSPF adjacency (10 pts), ACL to deny Telnet (8 pts), and BGP confed (20 pts). Apply the heuristic: do addressing and ACL first for points and verification, complete OSPF adjacency to unblock dependent routing, then attempt BGP if time remains.
Example decision
Complete addressing and ACL quickly, then ensure OSPF adjacency before BGP tasks.
Key Takeaway
Prioritize to earn reliable points and remove dependencies that block later tasks.
