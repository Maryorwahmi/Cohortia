Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Interpreting the Score Report
Process Flow

Inspect → Detect → Prioritize → Translate

A repeatable method to make the report actionable and measurable.

Inspect Sectional Scores
Identify which major sections and technology domains are below expected levels.
Detect Patterns
If multiple sections show similar weaknesses, you likely have a methodological or troubleshooting gap.
Prioritize by Impact
Focus on the topics that carry the largest weight or those you need in your day job.
Translate to Labs
Write one measurable lab task per weakness, including success criteria and verification steps.
Key Takeaway

Treat the report as a diagnostic checklist and convert each weakness into a verifiable lab task.

Example Translation
Reported Weakness: QoS
  1. Design a lab that places voice and video traffic on separate classes.
  2. Inject congestion and verify that voice packets maintain lower latency.
  3. Document commands and verification outputs; set a time goal to complete the task under simulated pressure.
Common Mistake

Rereading theory without hands‑on verification rarely improves lab performance; active fault injection is more effective.

End question: Which three lab tasks will move your score the most?
