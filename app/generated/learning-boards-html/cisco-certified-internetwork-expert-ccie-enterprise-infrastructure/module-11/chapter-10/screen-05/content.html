Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Decompression
Definition

What decompression is — and is not

Decompression is purposeful rest that protects memory accuracy and reduces emotional responses that lead to poor study choices.

Short Routine
Walk 20–30 minutes, hydrate, and perform one enjoyable, non-technical activity to shift focus.
High‑Level Note
Write a single paragraph describing major impressions: time pressure, unexpected topology, or confidence zones.
Misconception
Replaying every command soon after the exam usually harms, not helps, accurate diagnosis.
Key Takeaway

Delay deep analysis until you have objective data; use decompression to preserve the quality of that analysis.

Concrete Example

Scenario: A candidate feels they failed because a troubleshooting task took longer than expected. After decompression they note time pressure and a gap in SD‑WAN troubleshooting confidence. Using the official score report later, they confirm low scores in the troubleshooting domain and schedule five targeted fault‑injection labs focusing on SD‑WAN data plane issues.

Reflection

Plan your 48‑hour buffer now: what will you do to ensure accurate later analysis?
