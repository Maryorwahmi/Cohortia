Goals → Sprints → Tasks

Structure your remediation into measurable units so progress is objective and repeatable.

Goal
Improve SD‑WAN troubleshooting.
Sprint: 6 weeks
Sprint
Week 1: Fault injection labs. Week 2–3: Policy debugging. Week 4–6: Timed drills.
Outcome: Debug within 20 minutes
Task
Configure an SD‑WAN policy, induce data plane failure, and restore services while documenting verification commands.
Verification: show control connections, ping tests, and packet counters.
Weekly Review
Record time to resolve, missed steps, and new automations to add. Adjust the next sprint accordingly.

Objective: Confirm voice priority under congestion. Steps include configuring class maps, creating policy maps, applying to interfaces, and using traffic generators to create load.

Success Criteria: voice jitter < 30ms and packet loss < 1% during test.
