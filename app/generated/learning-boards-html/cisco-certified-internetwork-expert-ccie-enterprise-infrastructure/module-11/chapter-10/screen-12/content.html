Activity Template

Fillable Continuous Learning Plan

Use this template to document focus areas, resources, recert strategy, and milestones.

Instructions
  • Identify three learning areas based on your score report or career path.
  • Pick 2–3 verifiable resources per area and note expected CE credits where applicable.
  • Choose your recert strategy and map activities with target dates.
Example Filled Plan (short)
Advanced Python for Network Automation — DevNet Sandbox, online course, automation project (target Q1–Q3).
Cloud Networking with AWS — hands-on labs, certification guide (Q2–Q4).
Recert method — One professional exam + 40 CE credits (exam in Year 2, CE across Year 1 and 3).
