Process

Choose the right recert path

Recertification can be exam-driven, credit-driven, or a blend. Match the route to your role, travel budget, and learning cadence.

Expert exam
Pass any expert-level exam to renew for three years. Direct and definitive.
Two professional exams
Pass any two professional-level exams as an alternative path.
One professional exam + 40 CE
Combine testing with structured learning to balance effort and validation.
120 CE credits
A fully credit-based path that rewards continuous contribution and learning.
Key Takeaway

Recertification is not one-size-fits-all. Choose exams when you need validation, and CE credits when you want a continuous learning path.

Comparison

How the options compare in practice

Resource impact
Exams cost time and focus; CE credits spread effort across many activities.
Timing flexibility
CE credits let you recertify gradually; exams may require concentrated study blocks.
Professional signaling
An expert exam signals mastery; CE credits show ongoing engagement and contribution.
Risk of last-minute rush
Waiting reduces options and often forces exam-only recertification under pressure.
Common misconception
Many assume CE credits are easy to collect. In reality, credits must come from approved activities and be tracked — treat them like planned study deliverables, not incidental events.
