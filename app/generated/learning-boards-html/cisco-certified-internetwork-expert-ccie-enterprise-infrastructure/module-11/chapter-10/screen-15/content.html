Three final reminders

Plan early
Map exams and CE activities across three years.
Document evidence
Save certificates, slides, and completion records as you go.
Respect confidentiality
Share methods, not exam specifics — protect your credential and others'.
Closing thought
Recertification is a professional rhythm. Plan, act, and reflect — that sequence keeps your skills current and your career resilient.
