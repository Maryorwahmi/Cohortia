Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Immediately After the Exam
Core Concept

Decompress and Delay Analysis

Recovering your mental bandwidth first improves the accuracy and usefulness of later score-driven decisions.

Step 1 — Decompress
Short, physical activities reduce stress and memory distortion. Avoid immediately re-living the exam.
Step 2 — Record High‑Level Notes
Write brief impressions, not command transcripts. Capture emotions and major events only.
Step 3 — Wait for the Report
Cisco usually releases results within 48 hours. Use the official report for objective feedback.
Common Pitfall
Over-analyzing immediately can lock you into inaccurate beliefs about your performance.
Key Takeaway

Short-term recovery preserves clear judgment; schedule analysis after you have official data.

Concrete Example
Example plan: take 4 hours off, sleep, then on day two: collect high‑level notes, wait for the 48‑hour score, and schedule a 90‑minute diagnostic lab focused on the lowest section.
Analyst's Checklist
  • Breath and rest
  • Limit immediate discussion
  • Note major events only
  • Await official report
Reflection Prompt

What one specific lab task will I schedule first based on my strongest memory of the exam? This helps start a constructive cycle.

Remember: emotional processing is valid; plan it then move to diagnostic work.
