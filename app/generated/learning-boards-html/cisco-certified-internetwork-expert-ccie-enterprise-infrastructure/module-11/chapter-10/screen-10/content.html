Timeline

Three-year roadmap

Split the window into yearly milestones, then map exams and CE activities to firm dates.

Year 1 — Foundations
  • Collect low-friction CE: webinars, vendor labs, approved short courses.
  • Start automation and documentation projects that can earn credits.
  • Choose target professional exam if using exam-based path.
Year 2 — Focus
  • Schedule and prepare for a professional or expert exam.
  • Deliver a technical session or complete a specialization for CE credit.
  • Track and submit credits promptly after each activity.
Year 3 — Consolidate
  • Finish remaining CE credits; submit evidence and documentation.
  • Decide whether to take a final exam or rely on credits.
  • Confirm recertification well before expiry to avoid gaps.
Checklist
  1. List target activities and map to CE credits.
  2. Set approximate dates and backup slots for exams.
  3. Log evidence immediately after each activity.
Avoid this pitfall
Waiting until the final year reduces options. Plan credit-earning activities into your normal professional development routine.
