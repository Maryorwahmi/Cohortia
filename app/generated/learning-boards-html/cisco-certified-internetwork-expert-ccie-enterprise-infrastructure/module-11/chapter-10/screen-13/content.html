Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Exam Confidentiality
Definition

Protect exam content and respect the NDA

Confidentiality preserves the CCIE's value. Learn how to share lessons responsibly.

What not to share
  • Specific exam questions, step sequences, or topology screenshots.
  • Exact commands tied to exam scenarios or direct lab content.
What to share instead
  • High-level lessons, study strategies, and types of skills to practice.
  • Anonymized practice labs and troubleshooting steps you created yourself.
Consequences
Sharing exam content can lead to formal action, including revocation of certification. Ethical conduct protects your reputation and the community.
Key Takeaway

When in doubt, anonymize and generalize. Focus discussions on methodology and learning, not on exam specifics.
