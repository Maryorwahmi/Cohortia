Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Continuing Education Credits
Definition

What counts as CE credit

Event attendance
Cisco Live and approved conferences provide predictable credit values. Keep your attendance certificates.
Authorized courses
Complete vendor or partner training and track the completion proofs for submission.
Authoring and teaching
Writing technical articles, producing labs, or presenting counts — save slides and links.
Practical checklist
  • Before: confirm activity is eligible.
  • During: capture attendance or completion evidence.
  • After: submit documentation to the recert portal promptly.
Mapping to credit goals
If you need 40 CE credits, plan a mix: one conference (20–30 credits depending on vendor), a specialization (10–15), and authored material or workshops to fill the remainder.
Key Takeaway

CE credits reward consistent, documented learning and contribution. Treat them as scheduled professional development, not incidental events.
