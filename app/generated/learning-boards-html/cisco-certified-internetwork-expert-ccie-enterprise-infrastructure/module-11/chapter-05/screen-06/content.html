Annotated example checklist
  • Placeholders: interface names, IPs, ACL names — replace them consistently.
  • Identifiers: AS numbers, process IDs, policy names — set to match your topology.
  • Implied defaults: preconditions or omitted lines shown earlier in the document.
  • Verification: note verification commands in the surrounding text and run them after applying changes.
Analogy

Think of an example as a recipe template: you must pick the specific ingredients for your kitchen before you begin.

Concrete scenario

A BGP example may show neighbor address 10.0.0.2 and AS numbers 65001; map those to your neighbor IP and the AS you will actually use. If an ACL name is referenced but not shown, find or create the ACL before applying the neighbor statement.

Key Takeaway

Never paste a snippet without checking placeholders, implied prerequisites, and verification steps in the surrounding text.
