Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Common Pitfalls
Pitfall Summary
Typos
Use tab and ? to reduce mistakes and double-check addresses and masks.
Missing no shutdown
Always verify interface state with show ip interface brief after configuration.
Switchport mode errors
Confirm access vs trunk settings to prevent VLAN mismatches.
More pitfalls & checks
ACL placement
Test ACLs in a lab and validate direction and interface before applying widely.
Redistribution mistakes
Use explicit metrics and route-maps to avoid loops; verify routes after changes.
Not saving configs
Archive and copy running-config to flash or startup-config to prevent loss after reloads.
