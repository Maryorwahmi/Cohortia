Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Data Sources
Sources
Devices and metric categories
  • Routers & switches: interface counters, errors, queue depths, routing events.
  • Firewalls & security appliances: session counts, deny/permit logs, detailed syslog.
  • Wireless APs: client counts, airtime, retransmissions, association events.
  • Load balancers and servers: connection metrics and application-level counters.
Prioritization
Map metrics to SLAs and common incident types. Collect high-fidelity metrics where failure impact is highest and use sampling elsewhere to balance cost.
Example: Voice SLA
Collect jitter, packet loss, latency, and queue depth on voice VLAN interfaces and edge devices.
Pitfall
Collecting all metrics with no retention plan creates noise and storage pressure.
