Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Data Storage
Definition
Storage types and retention strategies
Choose a time-series database for high-frequency metrics, an indexed search engine for logs, and flow-specific storage for traffic records. Define retention windows: recent data at full resolution for troubleshooting, older data aggregated for trend analysis and capacity planning.
Retention Pattern
Keep weeks of full-resolution metrics and months of downsampled summaries for trends and capacity planning.
Cost Control
Aggregate and downsample older data to limit storage while preserving analytical value.
Concrete example
Store 30 days of per-10-second metrics at full resolution, then downsample to one-minute aggregates for 6 months and hourly aggregates for multi-year trend analysis.
