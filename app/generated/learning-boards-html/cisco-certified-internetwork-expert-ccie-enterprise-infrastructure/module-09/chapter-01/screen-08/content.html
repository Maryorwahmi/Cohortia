Architecture
Reliable and secure movement of telemetry
Select transport by considering reliability, performance, and security. Use gRPC over TLS for model-driven telemetry where fidelity matters. Use UDP for sampled flow exports where occasional loss is acceptable. Ensure collectors are reachable over robust paths and consider regional collectors or aggregation points to limit long-haul telemetry volume.
Design Checklist
  • Transport protocol choice
  • TLS and authentication
  • Collector scaling and placement
  • Aggregation and filtering points
Security
Encrypt telemetry in transit and authenticate devices to prevent spoofing and information leakage.
Concrete example
Use gRPC over TLS for per-interface streaming at 10-second intervals to a regional aggregator, which pre-filters and forwards enriched events to the central analytics cluster for long-term storage.
