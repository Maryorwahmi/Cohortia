Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Dashboards
Visualization
Designing dashboards for SLA, operations, and RCA
Executive
High-level SLA health tiles and trend spark lines.
Operational
Per-device health, active alerts, and recent anomalies for on-call teams.
Investigative
Aligned time-range panels to correlate metrics and logs during RCA.
Concrete rule
Always allow time alignment across panels so engineers can view multiple metrics over the same interval for correlation.
Pro-tip
Use focused panels and actionable links to runbooks so engineers have immediate steps after an alert is investigated.
