Introduction to Network Assurance and Telemetry
From snapshots to continuous insight — why telemetry powers modern network assurance.
This chapter explains how streaming telemetry provides continuous, high-fidelity operational data so engineers can detect anomalies early, reduce MTTR, and automate remediation. You'll study data sources, transport, storage, analysis, and practical CLI exploration.
In this lesson
  • Why traditional monitoring falls short for modern enterprise networks.
  • How telemetry streams support proactive assurance and RCA.
  • Key components of a telemetry-based assurance pipeline.
