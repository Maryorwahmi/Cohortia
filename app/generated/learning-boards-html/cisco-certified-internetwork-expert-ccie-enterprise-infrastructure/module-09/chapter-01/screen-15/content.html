Quick Reference
Network Assurance
Proactive validation that the network meets business outcomes and SLAs through data and automation.
Network Telemetry
Automated, structured transmission of device operational data to collectors for analysis.
Streaming Telemetry
Push-based, high-granularity data delivery that enables near real-time analytics and proactive detection.
Final thought

Design telemetry pipelines around operational outcomes and ensure each component supports the fidelity, security, and retention your assurances require.
