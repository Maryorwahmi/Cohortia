Learning Objectives
Learn the essential capabilities and outcomes of telemetry-driven assurance.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate Monitoring vs Assurance
Describe limits of periodic polling and contrast with continuous telemetry-driven assurance for proactive outcomes.
Verified competency milestone
02
Explain Assurance Benefits
Articulate how telemetry enables proactive detection, faster RCA, capacity planning, and improved security posture.
Verified competency milestone
03
Identify Telemetry Components
List data sources, collection methods, transport, storage, processing, visualization, alerting, and automation components.
Verified competency milestone
04
Shift to Proactive Operations
Explain how telemetry enables automated remediation and reduces MTTR, turning teams from reactive to proactive operators.
Verified competency milestone
