Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Processing & Analysis
Processing Pipeline
Enrich, correlate, and detect
Parse & Normalize
Convert streams into structured metrics with consistent units and timestamps.
Enrich
Add context like device role, location, and application tags to support correlation.
Detect & Predict
Statistical baselines and ML identify anomalies and predict failures when quality data exists.
Concrete example
Correlate a rolling median deviation in interface error rate with simultaneous route flaps and CPU spikes to find the root cause faster than manual inspection.
Misconception
Anomaly detection needs labeled events and good baselines; without them, alerts will be noisy and untrustworthy.
Key Takeaway
High-quality processing pipelines are essential to transform telemetry into dependable, actionable insights.
