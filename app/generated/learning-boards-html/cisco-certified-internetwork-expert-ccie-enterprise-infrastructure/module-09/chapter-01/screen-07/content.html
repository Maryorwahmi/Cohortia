Process Flow
How data leaves devices
  1. SNMP Polling: periodic reads of counters; low complexity but snapshot-based.
  2. NetFlow/IPFIX: flow exports for traffic analysis; often UDP-based and sampled.
  3. Streaming Telemetry: push-based model-driven streams (gRPC/YANG) delivering structured, near real-time data.
  4. Syslog: event messages for discrete occurrences; useful for event correlation.
Design point
Define sampling and filtering at the source to reduce unnecessary telemetry volume while preserving critical signals.
Transport Considerations
UDP for low-latency flow exports; TCP or gRPC for reliable, structured telemetry. Security and network impact must be assessed in design.
Misconception
Streaming telemetry isn't free—tune intervals, apply filters, and choose transports to control bandwidth and collector load.
