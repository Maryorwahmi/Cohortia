Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Learning Objectives
Skills & Outcomes
Practical tasks tied to telemetry: choose metrics, design retention, perform RCA, and craft automation triggers.
Metric Selection
Identify interface counters, CPU, memory, routing events, logs, and flow data needed for troubleshooting and SLA verification.
Storage & Retention
Choose time-series databases for high-frequency metrics and indexed systems for logs to balance cost and fidelity.
Root Cause Analysis
Correlate metrics across devices and time to move from symptom to root cause more quickly than manual show commands.
Automation Triggers
Define safe remediation playbooks that act on telemetry signals, reducing repeated manual steps during incidents.
