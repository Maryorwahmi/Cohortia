Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Monitoring vs Assurance
Traditional Monitoring
Polling & Snapshots
  • Periodic SNMP polling and scheduled checks provide intermittent snapshots of state.
  • Misses short-lived anomalies and often requires manual 'show' commands to gather context.
  • Reactive: alerts are typically generated after a clear threshold breach or outage.
Common mistake
Assuming higher-frequency polling equals telemetry; it may still lack structured models and push-based delivery.
Modern Assurance
Streaming Telemetry & Analysis
  • Devices push structured, high-granularity data in near real-time to collectors.
  • Enables correlation across metrics, logs, and flows for faster RCA and anomaly detection.
  • Supports automation and closed-loop remediation based on data-driven triggers.
Key takeaway
Choose telemetry when you need continuous observability, early detection, and actionable context.
Concrete example
A one-minute spike in packet drops during a backup window would be invisible to five-minute polling but visible in streaming telemetry. Correlating that spike with increased CPU and a routing flap helps identify whether the cause is congestion, a buggy process, or route instability.
