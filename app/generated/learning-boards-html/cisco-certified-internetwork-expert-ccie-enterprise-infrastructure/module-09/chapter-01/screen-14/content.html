What to Remember
Continuous Telemetry
Streaming, model-driven data captures transient issues and enables correlation.
Assurance Pipeline
Sources, collection, transport, storage, analysis, visualization, alerting, and automation form a complete solution.
Prioritization
Collect what maps to SLAs, downsample older data, and automate only safe, tested actions.
Summary Takeaway

A telemetry-driven assurance design reduces surprises, shortens MTTR, and gives engineers the data needed to operate networks proactively and strategically.
