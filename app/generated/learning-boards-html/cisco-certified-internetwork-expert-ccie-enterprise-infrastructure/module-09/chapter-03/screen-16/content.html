Chapter Review

Essential Concepts: NetFlow & IPFIX

Recap of key terms for traffic analysis

NetFlow / IPFIX

Cisco-developed technology (NetFlow) and IETF standard (IPFIX) for collecting and exporting IP traffic flow information.

Flow

A unidirectional sequence of packets between a source and destination sharing common characteristics (e.g., IPs, ports, protocol).

Flexible NetFlow

A customizable version of NetFlow on Cisco IOS XE, allowing definition of custom match and collect fields.

Summary Takeaway

NetFlow and IPFIX provide detailed traffic visibility through the concept of 'flows', with Flexible NetFlow offering advanced customization for granular data collection and analysis.
