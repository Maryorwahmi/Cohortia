Avoiding Common NetFlow Mistakes

Ensuring accurate and efficient traffic data collection

Not Applying to Interfaces

Forgetting to apply `ip flow monitor` to the desired interfaces (input/output) means no traffic data will be collected.

Incorrect Exporter Destination/Port

Mismatch between device configuration and collector listening settings will prevent flow records from being received.

Over-collecting Data

Collecting too many fields or applying NetFlow to high-traffic interfaces can consume significant CPU/memory resources.

No Functional Collector

Configuring NetFlow without a collector to receive and analyze the data makes the entire setup useless.

Key Takeaway

Successful NetFlow deployment hinges on correctly applying monitors to interfaces, verifying exporter settings, optimizing data collection to avoid resource strain, and ensuring a functional NetFlow collector is in place.
