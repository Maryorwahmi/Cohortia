Avoiding Common Syslog Mistakes

Ensuring effective and efficient log collection for network assurance

Not Sending to a Remote Server

Relying only on local buffers makes logs volatile and difficult to analyze centrally, especially after reboots. This prevents correlation across devices.

Too High/Low Logging Level

`logging trap debug` can overwhelm the server. `logging trap critical` might miss important warnings. Find the right balance for your operational needs.

No Timestamps

Without accurate timestamps (`service timestamps`), correlating events across different devices is nearly impossible, hindering troubleshooting.

No NTP Synchronization

If device clocks are not synchronized via NTP, even with timestamps enabled, the timestamps will be inaccurate, making event correlation unreliable.

Key Takeaway

Effective syslog implementation requires careful attention to remote logging, appropriate severity levels, and accurate, synchronized timestamps to ensure logs are actionable and reliable for network assurance.
