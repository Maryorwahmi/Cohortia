Core Concept

Understanding Syslog Severity Levels

Prioritizing events from Emergencies to Debugging for focused analysis

Cisco IOS XE Severity Levels

0 - Emergencies:System unusable.
1 - Alerts:Immediate action needed.
2 - Critical:Critical conditions.
3 - Errors:Error conditions.
4 - Warnings:Warning conditions.
5 - Notifications:Normal but significant conditions.
6 - Informational:Informational messages.
7 - Debugging:Debugging messages.

Importance in Filtering & Analysis

  • •Prioritization: Lower numbers indicate higher severity, demanding quicker response.
  • •Filtering: Configure devices to send only messages at or below a certain severity level to avoid overwhelming the collector.
  • •Troubleshooting: Temporarily increase logging levels (e.g., to 'debugging') for detailed diagnostics.
  • •Resource Management: Prevents excessive log generation and network traffic.
Example

Setting `logging trap informational` means messages with severity 0 through 6 will be sent, capturing all critical and significant events.

Key Takeaway

Mastering syslog severity levels allows you to intelligently filter and prioritize log messages, ensuring that critical events are addressed promptly while managing logging volume effectively.
