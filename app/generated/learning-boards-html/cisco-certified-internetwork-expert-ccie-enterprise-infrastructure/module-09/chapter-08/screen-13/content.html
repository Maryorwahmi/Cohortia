Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Safety Best Practices
Checklist
  • Test automation extensively in lab environments.
  • Use Ansible Vault or secrets manager instead of hardcoded credentials.
  • Design idempotent playbooks and explicit rollbacks.
  • Apply rate limiting and suppression to prevent flapping.
  • Provide human override and escalation mechanisms.
Concrete Example & Pitfall
If an automation repeatedly tries to clear counters every minute, it may mask deeper issues and create flapping. Add suppression windows and exponential backoff to avoid this pitfall.
Common Mistake

Hardcoding credentials into scripts for convenience during a lab demo without transitioning to vaulted secrets for production.
