Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Telemetry Collection & Ingestion
Telemetry Architecture
Device Streaming (gNMI) or Polling (SNMP/RESTCONF)
Collector / Broker: normalize, buffer, and forward
Storage: Time-series DB or log store for analytics
Analytics & Alerting: baseline, detect anomalies, emit events
Practical Notes
  • Normalize units and timestamps before analysis.
  • Derive rates and baselines rather than relying on raw counters alone.
  • Use short-term buffers to handle bursts and prevent data loss.
Concrete Example
Stream interface counters into a TSDB and create a Grafana alert for sustained errors per minute. That alert becomes the event for remediation.
Misconception: more telemetry without normalization increases noise and false positives. Design filters and baselines first.
