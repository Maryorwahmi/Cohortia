Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › Key Concepts — Part 2
SNMPv3 authPriv
Authentication plus encryption for messages.
Protect communities
Bind community strings to ACLs; avoid defaults.
Traps vs Informs
Traps are best-effort; informs require acknowledgment and retries.
MIB / OID
MIBs define objects; OIDs are numeric addresses used by tools.
64-bit counters
Use where supported to avoid counter wrap and improve accuracy.
Audit and rotate
Regularly review SNMP configs and rotate credentials to reduce risk.
Final takeaway
Prioritize secure configuration and selective use of resources. SNMP remains useful when deployed with strong controls and regular operational checks.
