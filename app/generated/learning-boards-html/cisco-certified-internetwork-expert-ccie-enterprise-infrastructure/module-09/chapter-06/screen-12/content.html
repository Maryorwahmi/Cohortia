Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Key Concepts — Part 1
DNA Center (DNAC)
Centralized network management and automation platform that houses Assurance features.
Assurance
Analytics and ML that correlate telemetry into health scores and actionable insights.
Health Scores
Dynamic color-coded indicators for network, client, and application health to guide prioritization.
Summary
Use these concepts together: scores to prioritize, Client 360 to investigate, Path Trace to confirm, and sensors to detect regressions early.
