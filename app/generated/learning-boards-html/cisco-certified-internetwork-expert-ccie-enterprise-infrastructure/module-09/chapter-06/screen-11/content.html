Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Common Mistakes & Best Practices
Top mistakes
  • Overloading DNA Center with unplanned telemetry volume.
  • Failing to onboard devices correctly so telemetry is missing.
  • Trusting anomalies before baselines are established.
  • Reacting to alerts without validating correlated evidence.
Best practices
Size the platform for telemetry ingestion and retention.
Onboard and manage devices consistently with correct credentials and reachability.
Allow baseline periods before relying on anomaly detection.
Use health scores to prioritize, then Client 360 and Path Trace to investigate.
Key Takeaway

Operational readiness and disciplined workflows turn statistics into reliable operations. Validate alerts and use evidence-driven remediation.
