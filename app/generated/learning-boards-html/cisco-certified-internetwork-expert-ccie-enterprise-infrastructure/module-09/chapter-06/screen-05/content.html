Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Health Scores Explained
Score types
Network / Device Health: control-plane, interfaces, CPU/memory
Client Health: association success, RSSI, retries, authentication
Application Health: flow-level latency, jitter, packet loss, DPI classification
Interpretation matrix
Example
Low app health + good network health suggests application server or service degradation. Low client health + low device health suggests access-layer or wired infrastructure problem.
Misconception
A single low score rarely proves the root cause. Use the score to prioritize, not to conclude without correlating evidence.
Key Takeaway

Read scores together and follow a triage flow: prioritize by score, correlate telemetry, then dig deeper with Client 360 or Path Trace.
