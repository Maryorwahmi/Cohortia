Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Client 360 Walkthrough
What Client 360 shows
  • Connectivity history: associations, disassociations, roam events, signal strength.
  • Authentication and onboarding results.
  • Application experience metrics and flow activity.
  • Events timeline and suggested troubleshooting steps driven by analytics.
Concrete example
Search for MAC 00:11:22:33:44:55, check health trend, view association history, and launch Path Trace if client transit looks suspect.
Investigation steps
Step 1: Verify client health trend and recent disconnect timestamps.
Step 2: Check association, RSSI, and roaming events to detect radio or AP issues.
Step 3: Review application metrics; if app latency appears, compare to device health.
Step 4: Launch Path Trace from the Client 360 view to get hop-level diagnostics.
Common Mistake

Relying on a single timestamp or counter without cross-referencing the client timeline with device counters can mislead diagnosis.
