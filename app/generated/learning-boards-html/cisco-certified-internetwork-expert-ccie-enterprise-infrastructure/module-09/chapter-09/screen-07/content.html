Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Centralized Data Platform & Scale
Platform Components
  • Collectors and pipeline: reliable ingestion from devices and probes.
  • Storage: TSDB for metrics, indexed log store for events, object/store for raw flow samples.
  • Analytics: correlation engine and dashboards for rapid investigation.
  • Redundancy: failover collectors and analytics nodes.
Retention & Aggregation
Keep high-resolution data for short windows and aggregated series for long-term baselines. Align retention with compliance and analysis needs.
Security & Compliance
Control access to sensitive logs, verify integrity, and generate compliance reports from stored telemetry as needed.
Example Architecture
Ingest streaming telemetry to a TSDB, send syslog to an indexed log store, export NetFlow to a flow processor, and run correlation in an analytics layer that queries across these stores.
Key Takeaway

Design centralized ingestion with redundancy and storage plans that balance resolution, retention, and query performance to support fast, reliable RCA and compliance.
