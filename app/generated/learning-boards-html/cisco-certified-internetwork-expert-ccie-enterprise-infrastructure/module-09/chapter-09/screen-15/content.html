Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Key Concepts
Comprehensive Strategy
Align assets to KPIs/SLOs, prioritize monitoring based on business impact.
Layered Telemetry
Combine device, event, flow, probe, and UX data for complete visibility.
Centralized Correlation
Ingest and join telemetry in a scalable platform for fast RCA and reporting.
Summary takeaway
A deliberate combination of prioritized monitoring, layered telemetry, centralized correlation, structured incident response, and an improvement loop backed by automation is the foundation of robust network assurance.
