Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Escalation Paths & Post-Incident Review
Escalation Criteria
  • Impact to critical SLOs or multiple users.
  • Duration above defined thresholds without mitigation.
  • Unable to isolate or remediate with available resources.
Handoff Packets
Provide time-aligned telemetry extracts, key events, actions taken, and current hypothesis to the next engineer to avoid duplicated effort.
Blameless PIR
Capture timeline, root cause, corrective actions, and preventive actions. Assign owners and deadlines for follow-through.
Example Actions from PIR
Add interface-flap alerts, plan physical layer checks, increase redundancy, and automate diagnostic collection on similar future incidents.
Key Takeaway

Escalation rules and blameless PIRs institutionalize learning so incidents lead to fewer repeats and better automation choices.
