Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Define KPIs and SLOs
What to measure
Choose metrics tied to user impact: latency, availability, error rates, and utilization on critical paths.
Writing SLOs
Express SLOs as measurable targets, for example: application response <200ms during business hours or 99.999% uptime for a critical API.
Operational Use
SLOs determine probe placement, alert severity, and escalation policies. Keep them focused and actionable.
Example
For a payment gateway: SLO = 200ms p99 latency during peak hours. Instrument IP SLA, application metrics, and flow sampling across relevant WAN links.
Common Mistake
Defining too many SLOs or choosing low-value metrics. Focus on a small set tied to user experience and business continuity.
Key Takeaway

KPIs and SLOs make assurance measurable. Prioritize high-impact services and instrument them with the right telemetry to act when thresholds are breached.
