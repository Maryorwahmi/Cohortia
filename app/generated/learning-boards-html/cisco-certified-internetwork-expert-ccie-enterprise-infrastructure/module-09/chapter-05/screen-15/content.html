Streaming advantage
Push-based delivery captures transient events and reduces request overhead compared to polling.
YANG validation
Always verify XPath filters against the loaded YANG modules to avoid empty telemetry streams.
Transport choice
Use NETCONF for transactional config tasks and gRPC/Protobuf for efficient telemetry streaming.
Final Thought

Design telemetry with schema-first thinking, secure the transport, and validate collector reachability to build a dependable data pipeline for automation and analytics.
