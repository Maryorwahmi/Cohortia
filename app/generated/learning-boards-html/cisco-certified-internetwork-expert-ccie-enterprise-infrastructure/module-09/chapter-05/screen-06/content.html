YANG roles
  • Define configuration schema (what can be set).
  • Define operational state (what is reported).
  • Define RPCs and notification structures.
Concrete example
A subscription with XPath /interfaces-state/interface/statistics targets per-interface counters described by the interfaces YANG model. If the path is incorrect, no data will be returned.
Why YANG matters

YANG gives a consistent, machine-readable blueprint. Collectors parse structured data rather than vendor-specific messages.

Key Takeaway

Use YANG paths to limit telemetry payloads to only the fields your analytics require.
