Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Polling vs Streaming
SNMP Polling (Pull)
  • Periodic requests from the NMS.
  • May miss transient, sub-poll events.
  • Scale issues at high poll rates.
Example: 5-minute polling interval misses a 10-second outage.
Streaming Telemetry (Push)
  • Device pushes updates by event or frequency.
  • Near real-time visibility for transient events.
  • Use YANG filters to limit data to what matters.
Example: streaming interface counters at 1s intervals captures bursts and short spikes.
Concrete Scenario

Imagine troubleshooting packet drops that last 200 milliseconds. With a five-minute polling window, you will not see them. Streaming telemetry configured for sub-second or one-second updates records those bursts and lets analytics correlate them with events.

Key Takeaway

Use streaming when you need accurate, high-granularity observability. Use filters to manage resource use.
