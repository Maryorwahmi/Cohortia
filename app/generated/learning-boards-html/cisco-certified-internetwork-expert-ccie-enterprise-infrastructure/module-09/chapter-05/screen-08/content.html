Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Enable gRPC
Command snippet
! Enable gRPC on the device
grpc
 port 57000
 no shutdown
Ensure the chosen port is open on any intermediate firewalls and not used by other services.
Verification
show grpc
Confirm the service is enabled. In push models, the router often acts as the gRPC client and connects to the collector at the configured receiver address.
Safety note
Production use should secure gRPC with TLS and authentication. Lab setups may use insecure channels for testing, but never do that in production.
