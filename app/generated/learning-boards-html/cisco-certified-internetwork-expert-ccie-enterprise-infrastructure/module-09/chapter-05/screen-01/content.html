Streaming Telemetry with gRPC and NETCONF/YANG
From push-based streams to YANG-defined schemas and transport options for IOS XE.
In this lesson

Understand why polling can miss transient events and how push-based telemetry delivers higher granularity and lower latency, how YANG structures telemetry, and how NETCONF and gRPC differ as transports.

Coming up

IOS XE configuration snippets, a conceptual Python collector discussion, verification commands, and common mistakes to avoid during deployment.

Key Takeaway

Streaming telemetry uses YANG schemas and efficient transports like gRPC to provide scalable, near real-time operational data that polling cannot reliably deliver at high granularity.
