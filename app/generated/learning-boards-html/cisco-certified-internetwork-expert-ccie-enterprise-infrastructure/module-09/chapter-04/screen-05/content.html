Definition
Key IP SLA Metrics
Understand what each measurement means for the services you run.
Latency (RTT)
Time for a probe to travel to the target and back. Affects transaction responsiveness.
Jitter
Variation of delay between packets. Crucial for voice/video; high jitter requires buffering or leads to quality loss.
Packet loss
Percentage of probes that never return. Even small loss can impact real-time streams and transactions.
Concrete example
A UDP Jitter probe reports average RTT 30 ms, jitter 50 ms, and 2% packet loss. Although RTT looks good, jitter and loss will degrade voice calls and may require rerouting or QoS adjustments. Use thresholds aligned to the application's tolerance, not arbitrary low numbers.
Interpretation
Combine metrics; trend data is more valuable than a single spike.
Misconception
Low RTT does not guarantee good voice quality if jitter and loss are high.
Key Takeaway

Evaluate RTT, jitter, and loss together and set thresholds based on the application's tolerance and baseline measurements.
