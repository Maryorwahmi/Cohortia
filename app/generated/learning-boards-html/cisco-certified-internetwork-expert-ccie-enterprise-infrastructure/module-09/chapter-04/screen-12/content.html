Common Mistakes and Safety Notes
Compare risks and safe practices to reduce false alarms and resource issues.
Risk: Over-configuration
Too many high-frequency probes increase CPU and memory use. Restrict probes to critical paths and use reasonable frequencies.
Risk: Missing responder
UDP Jitter requires ip sla responder on Cisco targets. Without it, jitter and loss calculations are invalid.
Risk: Bad thresholds
Setting thresholds without a baseline leads to noisy alerts or missed issues. Measure normal behavior first.
Risk: Asymmetric routing
RTT is round-trip. If forward and return paths differ, RTT may not represent single-direction performance.
Pro tip

Test configurations in a lab and add small track delays to avoid rapid flapping in production.
