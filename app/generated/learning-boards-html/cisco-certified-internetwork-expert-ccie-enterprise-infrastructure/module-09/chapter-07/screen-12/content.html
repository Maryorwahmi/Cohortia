Module 9 › Chapter 9.7 › Data Correlation
Process

Pivot from metrics to logs to flows

Use timestamps and device identifiers as the glue between datasets to find root causes faster.

Step 1: Detect anomaly in Grafana (e.g., bandwidth drop or error spike).
Step 2: Search Kibana for logs matching the device, interface, and time window to find error messages or flaps.
Step 3: Inspect NetFlow for traffic shifts and confirm whether traffic volume or application mix changed.
Correlation Example
Observed: Interface error spike + BGP neighbor flaps + NetFlow drop → Likely physical/link issue.
Misconception: Correlation equals causation. Correlation narrows hypotheses; you must still validate with device checks or tests.
Operational tip: Ensure consistent device naming and timezone handling across sources to reduce investigation friction.
