Module 9 › Chapter 9.7 › Grafana vs Kibana
Grafana

Time-series visualization & alerts

Connects to TSDBs and many datasources to build performance dashboards and alerts.

  • Best for numeric, timestamped metrics
  • Supports many panel types and alerting backends
  • Query languages depend on datasource (InfluxQL, PromQL, SQL)
Kibana

Log exploration & full-text search

Built for indexed documents in Elasticsearch, with strong search and drilldown features.

  • Best for text-heavy logs and event searching
  • Powerful query DSL for full-text and structured fields
  • Works seamlessly with Elasticsearch indices and mappings
Practical guideline
Use Kibana for searching and investigating log messages and Grafana for time-series dashboards and alerts; correlate across both to reduce time to root cause.
