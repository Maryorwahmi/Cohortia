Module 9 › Chapter 9.7 › Anomaly Detection
Definition

Baselines, deviations, and alerts

Establish what normal looks like, then detect meaningful deviations and prioritize them with context.

Collect representative data and compute percentiles and moving averages to form baselines.
Define anomaly rules that consider duration and magnitude to avoid short-lived spikes triggering alerts.
Correlate anomalies with logs and flow data to reduce guesswork during incidents.
Example & Pitfalls
Example: Flag sustained interface error rate above baseline for more than five minutes for investigation.
Pitfall: Over-alerting. Tune thresholds and use correlation to cut false positives.
Tip: Include correlation context in alert payloads so responders see likely root causes immediately.
