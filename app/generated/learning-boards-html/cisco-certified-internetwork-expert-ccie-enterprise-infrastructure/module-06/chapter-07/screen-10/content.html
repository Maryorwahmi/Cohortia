URL List Example
url-list MY_INTERNAL_WEB_APPS
 url-list-item "Internal Wiki" "https://wiki.cohortia.local"
 url-list-item "HR Portal" "https://hr.cohortia.local"
After publishing, clicking "Internal Wiki" from the portal opens the proxied HTTPS session through the headend to the internal server.
Troubleshooting
  • Verify the VPN device can resolve wiki.cohortia.local and reach its IP.
  • Check for application redirects that change hostnames; adjust URL entries accordingly.
  • Use debug webvpn to see proxy errors when a click fails.
Key Takeaway

URL lists are secure and simple for web apps but depend on the headend's DNS and routing to internal hosts.
