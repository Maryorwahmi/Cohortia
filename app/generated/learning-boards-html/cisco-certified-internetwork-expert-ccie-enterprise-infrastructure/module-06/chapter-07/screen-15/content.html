Key Concepts
Three concise cards to remember.
SSL/TLS Protocol
Handshake authenticates the gateway; symmetric keys protect the session.
Clientless WebVPN
Browser portal for URL, file, and limited port access without client installs.
IOS‑XE WebVPN Components
Gateway + trustpoint, webvpn context, and policy groups form the portal structure.
Summary Takeaway

Select clientless WebVPN for simple browser-based access and client-based VPNs for full network needs; always validate both TLS trust and backend reachability.
