Phase 1 — Hub-and-Spoke

Characteristics

  • No direct spoke-to-spoke tunnels.
  • Hub handles all forwarding and IPsec processing.
  • Simple multicast and routing handling via the hub.
Example flow

R2 → R1 (hub) decapsulate/re-encapsulate → R3. Hub processes both encryption and forwarding steps.

When to choose

Choose Phase 1 for small deployments, strict inspection requirements, or when hub capacity meets expected traffic loads.
