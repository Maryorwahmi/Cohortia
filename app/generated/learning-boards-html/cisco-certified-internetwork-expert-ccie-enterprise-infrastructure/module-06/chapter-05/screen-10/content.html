Process

Spoke-to-spoke shortcut lifecycle

  1. Spoke initiates traffic to another spoke.
  2. Spoke queries hub via NHRP for destination's public IP.
  3. Spoke builds direct GRE-over-IPsec tunnel and traffic flows directly.
Routing note

EIGRP works well over DMVPN without special network-type commands. OSPF may require explicit network type commands on the hub to properly form neighbors across the NBMA cloud.

Common mistake

Expecting immediate direct traffic: initial packets may still traverse the hub while the NHRP query and shortcut setup complete.
