Site-to-Site IPsec VPN with IKEv2
A practical blueprint to configure, verify, and troubleshoot a hub-and-spoke VPN between two IOS‑XE routers using pre-shared keys.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
What you'll configure
  • IKEv2 proposal, policy, keyring, and profile
  • IPsec transform-set and profile with PFS
  • ACL defining interesting traffic and interface binding
Why it matters

Correct parameter alignment ensures secure key exchange and persistent tunnels. The ACL triggers Phase 2; without it, data plane SAs never form.

Verification

Use show crypto ikev2 sa and show crypto ipsec sa to confirm control and data SAs, and check packet counters to confirm encrypted traffic.

Common mistake

Mismatched DH group or an ACL that doesn't match remote/local networks prevents Phase 2 from being triggered despite Phase 1 being ready.
