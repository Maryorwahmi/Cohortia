Assessment › Common scenarios
Scenario A

GRE and IPsec SAs are up, but large file transfers experience drops and retransmits. Likely cause: MTU/MSS mismatch leading to fragmentation.

Scenario B

IPsec is up, tunnel shows up/up, but OSPF adjacency is not established. Likely cause: GRE misconfiguration or crypto map ACLs not matching GRE outer IPs.

Key Takeaway

Design assessment items that require layered diagnosis: connectivity, tunnel, routing, security, and MTU adjustments.
