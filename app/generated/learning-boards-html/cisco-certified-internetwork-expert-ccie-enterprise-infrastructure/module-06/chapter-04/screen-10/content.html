Definition › MTU & MSS Tuning

Why it matters

GRE plus IPsec increases packet size. If the encapsulated packet exceeds the underlying path MTU, it will be fragmented, causing retransmits and poor throughput.

• Recommended starting values: ip mtu 1400 and ip tcp adjust-mss 1360 on the tunnel interface.
• Measure and adjust upward if your path supports larger sizes without fragmentation.
Example scenario
A 1500-byte TCP payload becomes larger after GRE and IPsec headers. Lowering the tunnel MTU ensures routers fragment or segment at the right layer so encapsulated packets do not exceed the path MTU.
Common mistake

Only changing the physical interface MTU is insufficient. Tune the tunnel MTU and the TCP MSS negotiation on the tunnel interface to prevent end-to-end fragmentation.
