Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Lab Walkthrough
Step 1: Configure DPD in the IKEv2 profile
crypto ikev2 profile COHORTIA_IKEV2_PROFILE
 dpd 10 3 periodic
Step 2: Configure PFS in the IPsec profile
crypto ipsec profile COHORTIA_IPSEC_PROFILE
 set pfs group 14
Step 3: Initiate traffic and verify
show crypto ikev2 sa detail
show crypto ipsec sa detail
Interpretation tips:
  • Confirm DPD probe timestamps and absence of probe responses to detect a teardown.
  • Confirm Phase 2 shows a PFS DH group exchange during rekey.
  • If Phase 2 fails, check that both peers use the same set pfs group value.
Quick test

After configuring, power‑cycle the remote peer or temporarily remove its route to observe DPD teardown and confirm alternate routing or redundancy takes over.

Key Takeaway

Follow the configure→verify→test cycle. Verify DPD probes and PFS DH exchanges during rekeys to ensure both liveness detection and key separation are working.
