Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Dead Peer Detection

What is DPD?

A liveness probe mechanism that tears down SAs when a peer stops responding, preventing wasted encrypted traffic.

• Sends encrypted "hello" probes and expects a reply; if retries fail, the SA is removed.
• Configure per crypto ikev2 profile with retry interval and count for operational control.
• Important for high availability and for systems that may fail silently without sending a graceful notification.
Example

Configure DPD every 10 seconds with 3 retries so a dead peer is detected within roughly 30 seconds plus processing delays.

Common mistake

Relying on default DPD or not configuring it per profile can leave detection intervals too long for critical services.

Key Takeaway

DPD is about reachability — enable it with sensible intervals to reduce blackout windows and speed failover.

Operational view

Use show crypto ikev2 sa detail to confirm DPD probes and look for messages indicating DPD timeouts in debug output.

crypto ikev2 profile COHORTIA_IKEV2_PROFILE
 dpd 10 3 periodic
