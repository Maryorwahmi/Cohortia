Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Configuring DPD
DPD configuration steps
1
Decide interval and retries

Measure normal latency and packet loss. Choose an interval larger than transient spikes; three retries is common for a balance of sensitivity and stability.

2
Apply to IKEv2 profile
crypto ikev2 profile COHORTIA_IKEV2_PROFILE
 dpd 10 3 periodic
3
Verify with show and debug

Use show crypto ikev2 sa detail to confirm probes and debug crypto ikev2 for messages indicating DPD failures during tests.

Example tradeoff

With high-latency WAN links, increasing the interval reduces false teardowns; on low-latency links, shorter intervals speed recovery.

Misconception

DPD does not re-route traffic by itself; it simply clears an SA so routing or redundancy can take effect afterward.

Key Takeaway

Pick DPD values that align with link characteristics and verify during controlled failover tests to avoid surprises in production.
