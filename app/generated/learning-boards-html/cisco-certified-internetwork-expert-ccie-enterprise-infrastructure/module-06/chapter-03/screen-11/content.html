Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Common Failure Modes

If the SA does not form

  • Authentication errors: check pre‑shared keys or certificates; debug shows auth failures.
  • Parameter mismatches: check IKE/IPsec proposals and PFS group alignment on both peers.
  • NAT‑T blocked: verify UDP 4500 is permitted and that the peer address reflects NAT when present.

If the SA forms but traffic fails

  • Routing issues: confirm remote networks are advertised or static routes point into the tunnel.
  • Interesting traffic ACLs: ensure proxy ACLs match the traffic you expect to encrypt.
  • Firewall/policy placement: ensure rules do not block UDP 500/4500 or ESP and are applied in the correct direction relative to crypto processing.
Operational heuristic

Ask whether the SA is present. If not, focus on auth and parameter negotiation. If yes, focus on routing, ACLs, and policy ordering. This keeps troubleshooting efficient.

Key Takeaway

Classify the failure early and use targeted commands—this avoids chasing the wrong symptoms and shortens incident time.
