Learning Objectives

Practice configuring and verifying critical IPsec features for production environments.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Configure and verify DPD & PFS

Apply DPD to detect dead peers and enable PFS so each Phase 2 SA uses a fresh DH exchange. This prevents stale SAs and limits key exposure.

Verified Competency Milestone
02
Explain NAT‑Traversal

Understand how UDP 4500 encapsulation preserves IPsec integrity across NAT devices and why ACLs can break NAT‑T.

Verified Competency Milestone
03
Use advanced show & debug

Run targeted show and debug commands to reduce noise and identify negotiation or crypto engine errors quickly.

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Identify and resolve complex issues

Diagnose routing and security policy interactions that let tunnels form but prevent traffic from flowing, and apply fixes.

Verified Competency Milestone
