Process›IKE Phase 1
Phase 1 goals
Authenticate peers, negotiate encryption/hash/DH group, and establish the ISAKMP SA for a secure messaging channel.
Typical parameters
  • Encryption (e.g., AES-256)
  • Hash (e.g., SHA-384)
  • Authentication method (pre-shared key or certificates)
  • Diffie-Hellman group and lifetime
Troubleshooting

Mismatched Phase 1 policies often manifest as failures to establish any IPsec SAs. Verify encryption, hash, auth, and DH group first.

Concrete policy
Policy example: AES-256, SHA-384, pre-shared auth, group 14, lifetime 86400 seconds.
Key Takeaway
Phase 1 builds the secure channel for IKE. If it fails, Phase 2 cannot proceed.
