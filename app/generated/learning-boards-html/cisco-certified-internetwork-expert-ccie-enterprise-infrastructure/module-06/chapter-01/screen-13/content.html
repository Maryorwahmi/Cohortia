Real-world›ESP + Tunnel Mode
Why ESP + Tunnel
Encrypts entire original packet so internal IP addresses remain private and multiple subnets can be tunneled through a single IPsec session.
Operational note

Encapsulation increases packet size. Verify MTU/MSS to avoid fragmentation across the tunnel path.

Concrete example
Branch office tunnels two internal subnets to HQ using ESP tunnel mode; HQ routes the received original packets to internal services without exposing addresses to the internet.
Key Takeaway
For site-to-site privacy and multi-subnet support, ESP in tunnel mode is the recommended approach; manage MTU impacts proactively.
