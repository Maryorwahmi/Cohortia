SA definition
A simplex contract listing encryption, integrity, keys, and lifetime that two peers agree on to protect traffic in one direction.
Common parameters
  • Encryption algorithm and key (e.g., AES-256).
  • Hashing algorithm for integrity (e.g., SHA-256 or SHA-384).
  • Authentication method and Diffie-Hellman group for key exchange.
  • SA lifetime and replay window settings.
Misconception

An SA is not bi-directional by default. Expect two SAs for bidirectional traffic unless a combined mechanism is used.

Concrete example
ESP with AES-256 and SHA-256-HMAC, tunnel mode, SA lifetime 3600 seconds, PFS enabled with DH group 14.
Check your understanding
When you inspect a peer's SA list, verify algorithms, lifetimes, and whether the SA is simplex or part of a pair.
