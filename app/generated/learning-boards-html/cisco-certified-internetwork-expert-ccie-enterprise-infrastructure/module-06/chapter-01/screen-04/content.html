Chapter 6.1›What is a VPN?
Definition
A VPN is an encrypted tunnel over a public network that makes remote resources appear as part of a private network.
Benefits
  • Confidentiality and authentication for traffic over untrusted networks.
  • Cost savings by using the internet instead of leased lines.
  • Flexible remote work and distributed operations.
Key Takeaway

Treat the VPN as transport security; combine it with access control and segmentation inside the tunnel.

Primary types
Site-to-site: Connects entire networks, commonly used for branch-to-headquarters links.
Remote access: Individual users connect into the corporate network from anywhere.
Analogy
Think of a VPN as a private, locked hallway built through a public building; it prevents eavesdroppers from seeing who passes through.
