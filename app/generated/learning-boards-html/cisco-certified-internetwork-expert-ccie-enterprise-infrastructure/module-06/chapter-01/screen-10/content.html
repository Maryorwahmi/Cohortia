Comparison›IKEv1 vs IKEv2
IKEv1
Uses main and aggressive mode; main mode hides identities while aggressive mode is faster but less secure. Widely supported but older and more complex.
IKEv2
Streamlined, fewer messages, better NAT traversal, supports MOBIKE for mobility, and generally more reliable and efficient.
Operational note

IKE versions are incompatible. When migrating, verify both peers support IKEv2 and test re-key behavior and NAT traversal settings.

Concrete benefit
IKEv2 reduces negotiation steps, simplifying recovery when an endpoint's IP changes and improving overall reliability.
Key Takeaway

Choose IKEv2 for new deployments, but confirm support and compatibility before migrating.
