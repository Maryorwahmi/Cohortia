Chapter 6.1›Key Concepts
Virtual Private Network
An encrypted connection over a public network that extends private network reach securely.
IPsec
A suite providing authentication, integrity, confidentiality, and anti-replay for IP traffic.
IKE & SAs
IKE Phase 1 creates the secure channel; Phase 2 negotiates IPsec SAs that protect data traffic.
Final takeaway

When diagnosing tunnels, follow the negotiation sequence and verify exact parameter matches at each phase.
