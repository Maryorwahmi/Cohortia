VPN Scalability
Plan for throughput, concurrent sessions, algorithm cost, and licensing to avoid surprises.
Cryptographic Performance
Stronger crypto increases CPU work; hardware offload changes capacity planning significantly.
Monitoring & Logging
Centralize syslog and SNMP for alerting and auditing; correlate logs across devices for incident analysis.
Final takeaway

Combine redundancy, security, and telemetry into a tested, measurable design so VPNs remain resilient and auditable at scale.
