Validate failover

Use traceroute, show commands, and OSPF metrics to prove traffic shifts to the backup tunnel. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Focus

These objectives emphasize reliable behavior and observable metrics so that HA choices meet operational SLAs and security requirements.

Mnemonic
Think "Device, Tunnel, Route" — ensure redundancy at all three layers.
Why this matters
You must be able to prove failover works before declaring an HA deployment ready for production.
