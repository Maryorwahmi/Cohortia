Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Monitoring & Logging
What to collect
Syslog
Send authentication, DPD, and failover messages to a central SIEM.
SNMP
Poll tunnel status, throughput, and concurrent session counters for dashboards and alerts.
Configuration audits
Periodic checks of crypto params, PFS, and ACLs to detect drift and vulnerabilities.
Common mistake
Keeping logs only locally; you need a centralized, durable store to retain evidence after device failures.
Alerting tip
Alert on tunnel flaps, sudden drops in throughput, excessive rekeys, and session count thresholds so you act before users notice.
