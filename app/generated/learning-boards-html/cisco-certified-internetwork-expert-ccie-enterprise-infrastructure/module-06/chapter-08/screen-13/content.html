Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Security Best Practices
Cryptography
  • Prefer AES‑GCM or strong AES modes; use SHA‑384/512 for integrity where available.
  • Enable PFS to protect past sessions if keys are compromised.
  • Choose appropriate Diffie‑Hellman groups based on device capability and policy.
Authentication & Access
  • Prefer certificates or centralized AAA over PSKs for scalability and revocation.
  • Enforce MFA for remote access clients.
  • Apply ACLs to restrict internal access for VPN users following least privilege.
Audit
Regularly audit crypto policies, AAA integrations, and access lists. Replace legacy algorithms and rotate keys as part of your maintenance cycle.
