Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Key Concepts
High Availability (HA)
Design redundancy across links, devices, and tunnels so services continue after failures.
Redundant VPN Tunnels
Independent IPsec tunnels between sites or headends for path diversity and failover.
HSRP / VRRP
First-hop redundancy protocols that maintain a virtual gateway IP for continuity.
Summary takeaway

HA is a systems problem: duplicate components, synchronize state if needed, and ensure routing or clients can fail over cleanly.
