Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › VPN Scalability
Scaling factors
Throughput
Depends on crypto offload — test with real encryption algorithms and traffic patterns.
Concurrent sessions
Memory and SA limits determine how many simultaneous tunnels or users a device can support.
Crypto performance
Stronger algorithms increase CPU work; balance security needs with device capability.
Licensing
Vendor licensing can impose throughput or user limits that affect scaling plans.
Key Takeaway
Plan capacity for peak concurrent sessions, crypto cost, and growth; test under realistic load before production.
Common mistake
Sizing only for current traffic and ignoring rekey churn or session spikes leads to unexpected failures.
