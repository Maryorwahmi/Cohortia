Security Best Practices

User Authentication Methods for AnyConnect

Securing access with local, RADIUS, TACACS+, and certificates.

Available Authentication Methods

Local Database

Simple for small deployments, credentials on VPN headend.

RADIUS

Centralized AAA, scalable, supports MFA, common for remote access.

TACACS+

Separates AAA, often preferred for device administration.

Digital Certificates

Strong, password-less authentication, often combined with username/password.

Key Takeaway

Choosing the right authentication method for AnyConnect VPNs balances ease of management, scalability, and security requirements, with RADIUS being the most common enterprise solution.
