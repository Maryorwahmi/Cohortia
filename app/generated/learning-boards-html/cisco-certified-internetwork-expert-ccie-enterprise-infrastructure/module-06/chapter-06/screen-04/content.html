Core Concept

Remote Access vs. Site-to-Site VPNs

Understanding the fundamental differences and use cases.

Remote Access VPN

  • Connects individual remote users.
  • Client-to-network tunnel.
  • Ideal for mobile workforce.

Site-to-Site VPN

  • Connects entire networks.
  • Network-to-network tunnel.
  • Ideal for branch office connectivity.
Example Scenario

A sales representative working from a hotel needs to access the company's CRM system. A Remote Access VPN is the perfect solution, establishing a secure tunnel from their laptop to the corporate network. In contrast, connecting a new branch office to the main headquarters would require a Site-to-Site VPN.

Key Takeaway

Remote Access VPNs enable secure, flexible connectivity for individual users, while Site-to-Site VPNs link entire networks.
