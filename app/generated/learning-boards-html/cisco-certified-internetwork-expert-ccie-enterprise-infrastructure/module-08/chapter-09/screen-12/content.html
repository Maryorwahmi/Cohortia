Essential show commands

show macsec interface GigabitEthernet1/0/1
show mka sessions interface GigabitEthernet1/0/1
show interface GigabitEthernet1/0/1 capabilities

Use these commands to verify MACsec status, MKA session state, and hardware capability respectively.

Troubleshooting checklist

  1. Confirm Layer 1 link is up and interfaces are matching.
  2. Confirm key chain name and key-string match on both sides.
  3. Confirm MKA policy names and parameters match.
  4. Check for MKA session establishment and SAK derivation.
  5. Inspect encryption or replay counters for errors.
Common failure

Mismatched pre-shared keys or MKA policy names are the most frequent causes of MACsec sessions failing to form.
