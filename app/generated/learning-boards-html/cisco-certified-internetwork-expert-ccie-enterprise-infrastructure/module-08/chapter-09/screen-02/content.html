Learning Objectives

Bloom progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master

Bloom: Apply-focused
01
Explain TrustSec

Describe identity-based enforcement and why it scales better than IP ACLs. Verified competency milestone: explain SGT vs IP ACL tradeoffs.

02
Use SGTs & SGACLs

Classify users/devices and apply SGACL policies delivered from ISE. Verified competency milestone: map SGTs to enforcement points and SGACLs.

03
Configure MACsec

Apply MKA, CAK and SAK concepts to enable MACsec on trunk links. Verified competency milestone: build MACsec on a point-to-point trunk and verify connectivity.

04
Deploy safely

Identify common pitfalls and verification steps for production rollouts. Verified competency milestone: produce a checklist for TrustSec and MACsec deployment.
