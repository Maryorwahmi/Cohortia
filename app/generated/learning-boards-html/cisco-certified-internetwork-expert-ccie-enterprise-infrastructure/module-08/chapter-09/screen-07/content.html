Components

ISE, Enforcement Points, and Transport

Cisco ISE

Centralized authentication, SGT assignment, and SGACL management. Acts as the PCCP for TrustSec policy distribution.

Enforcement Points

Switches, routers, and controllers that apply SGACLs. They must be configured as TrustSec peers and receive SGT mappings.

Transport: SXP / Inline

Inline tagging where supported; SXP propagation for devices that cannot tag traffic directly.

Design checklist
  • Inventory enforcement-capable devices
  • Define SGT matrix and naming
  • Test SGACLs in lab
  • Plan certificate and time sync for ISE
