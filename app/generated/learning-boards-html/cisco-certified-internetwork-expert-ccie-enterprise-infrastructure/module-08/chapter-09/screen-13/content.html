Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › TrustSec vs MACsec

TrustSec

Identity-based segmentation using SGTs and SGACLs. Scales with user and device mobility because policies are independent of IP addressing.

Use case

Enforce who-can-talk-to-whom across a campus fabric or wireless network.

MACsec

Link encryption and integrity for Ethernet frames. Secures physical links against interception or tampering.

Use case

Protect trunk or uplink links between switches or to routers where cabling could be exposed.

Design guidance

Apply TrustSec for broad policy intent; add MACsec where link confidentiality or integrity is required. Validate device support and roll out in phases.
