Term

Security Group Tags (SGTs)

SGTs are 16-bit labels representing class membership. They travel with traffic and are used in policy decisions at enforcement points.

Example SGTs
Employees (100), Contractors (200), Servers (10), Printers (50)
Policy

Security Group ACLs (SGACLs)

SGACLs describe allowed traffic between source and destination SGTs. They are authored in ISE and applied at enforcement devices.

Concrete example

SGACL: Employees → Servers : allow TCP 443, TCP 80; deny others.

Common pitfall

No SGT governance leads to overlapping or conflicting SGACLs. Maintain a clear matrix and test changes incrementally.

Key Takeaway

SGTs label identity; SGACLs express intent. Keep both small, clear, and testable.
