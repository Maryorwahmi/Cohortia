Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › TrustSec Ops

Common mistakes

  • Missing SGT governance and naming conventions.
  • Applying SGACLs without staged testing.
  • Ignoring device compatibility for inline tagging.
  • Inadequate ISE integration planning (certs, HA, time).

Best practices

  • Create and maintain an SGT matrix and version control policies.
  • Test SGACLs in lab and roll out incrementally.
  • Confirm inline tagging support or plan SXP fallback.
  • Keep SGACL rules minimal and explicit for easier audits.
Key Takeaway

Operational rigor — governance, testing, and device planning — is as important as the TrustSec technology itself.
