Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Operational Best Practices
Strong Keys
Use RSA 2048+ and prefer AES for encryption where available.
Trusted Time & Logs
Authenticate NTP and secure syslog transport; use a loopback as source-interface.
Recovery Paths
Keep local admin accounts or console access available before enforcing centralized controls.
Final Takeaway

Security of network devices depends on encrypted transports, verified authentication, and operational practices that ensure recoverability and auditability.
