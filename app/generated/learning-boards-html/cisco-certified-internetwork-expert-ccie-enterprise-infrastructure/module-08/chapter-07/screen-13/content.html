Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Pitfalls & Recovery
Common Pitfalls
  • Removing local admin before AAA is validated.
  • Generating keys without hostname/domain set.
  • Changing VTY transports without keeping a test session open.
  • Enabling NTP auth or syslog TLS without server-side configuration.
Recovery Strategies
  1. Maintain a local privileged account or out-of-band console access.
  2. Apply changes in small steps and verify immediately.
  3. Use a maintenance window and have a rollback plan.
A small verification after each step can save hours in recovery and reduce service disruption.
