Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Hardening Checklist
Essential Items
  • Disable unused services (Telnet, HTTP, CDP/LLDP if not required).
  • Enforce strong passwords and centralized AAA (TACACS+/RADIUS).
  • Keep a local admin account or out-of-band access for recovery.
Operational Controls
  • Use login block-for and login delay to inhibit brute force.
  • Secure management protocols: SSH, SNMPv3, NTP auth, secure syslog.
  • Perform regular audits and configuration backups.
Key Takeaway

Hardening is a set of prioritized controls that together reduce surface area, limit impact of compromise, and improve recoverability.
