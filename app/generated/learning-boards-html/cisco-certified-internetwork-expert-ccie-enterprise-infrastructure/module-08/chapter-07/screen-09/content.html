Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › NTP Authentication
Why authenticate NTP?
  • Prevents spoofed time updates that corrupt logs.
  • Maintains consistent timestamps for forensic correlation.
  • Ensures services relying on time, such as certificates, behave predictably.
Example Configuration
ntp authentication-key 1 md5 CohortiaNTPKey
ntp authenticate
ntp trusted-key 1
ntp server 10.1.1.1 key 1
Verify with show ntp associations detail to confirm the association is authenticated and stable.
