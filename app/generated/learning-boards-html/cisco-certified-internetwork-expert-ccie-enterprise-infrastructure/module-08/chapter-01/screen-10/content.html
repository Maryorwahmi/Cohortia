Operational hardening checklist
  • Use unique, strong shared secrets and rotate periodically.
  • Deploy redundant AAA servers and configure failover.
  • Enable accounting and centralize logs for review.
  • Use command authorization to enforce least privilege.
  • Test fallbacks and disaster scenarios in a lab.
Real-world note
A mismatched shared secret often appears as a server rejection; make secret synchronization part of your change checklist to reduce incidents.
Key Takeaway

Treat AAA servers as critical infrastructure: secure them, monitor them, and make sure your devices can survive their temporary loss.
