Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Key Concepts
AAA
Authentication, Authorization, Accounting: who you are, what you may do, and an audit trail of actions.
TACACS+
Best for device administration: full-packet encryption and separate AAA functions for granular command control.
RADIUS
Used for 802.1X and network access; sends AVPs that can dynamically assign VLANs, QoS, and ACLs.
Summary takeaway

Deploy TACACS+ for admin tasks and RADIUS for network access, secure shared secrets, and always include tested fallback and redundancy.
