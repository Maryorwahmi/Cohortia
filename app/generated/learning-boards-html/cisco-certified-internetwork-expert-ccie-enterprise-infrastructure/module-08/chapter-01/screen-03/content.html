Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Objectives — Expanded
Method lists & fallback

Create ordered authentication and authorization lists that attempt TACACS+ first and then local if the server is unreachable. Test the fallback on a non-production device to confirm you won't be locked out during rollout.

Mnemonic: "Try server first, keep console local"
Verified competency milestone
RADIUS attributes & ISE

Map AD groups to ISE policies and ensure your RADIUS server sends the correct Vendor-Specific Attributes for VLAN or ACL assignment. A simple test user can confirm if VLAN assignment arrives and is acted on by the switch.

Mnemonic: "Identity drives network policy"
Verified competency milestone
Supplicant & certificates

Validate that client supplicants are configured for the chosen EAP method and that server and client certificates chain to a trusted CA. Certificate trust failures commonly block EAP-TLS and PEAP authentication.

Mnemonic: "Trust the chain"
Verified competency milestone
Redundancy & auditing

Deploy at least two AAA servers and configure failover; collect and review AAA logs regularly to spot suspicious authentication events. Redundancy reduces the chance of widespread lockouts.

Mnemonic: "Two is one, one is none"
Verified competency milestone
