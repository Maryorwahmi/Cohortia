Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Quick Reference
Fallback VLAN
Configure guest VLANs and fail actions to avoid service disruption when authentication fails.
EAP Selection
Match EAP to PKI readiness and client support. EAP-TLS for PKI environments; PEAP or EAP-FAST when avoiding client certs.
Verify Sessions
Use show dot1x all and show authentication sessions plus RADIUS logs to confirm authentication outcomes.
Final takeaway

Treat 802.1X as identity-driven access. Pair clear host-mode and EAP choices with robust RADIUS design and verification practices for reliable security.
