Advanced Data Plane Security with ACLs and uRPF

Practical techniques to filter traffic by time, state, and source reachability so your border and internal routers forward only legitimate packets.

In this lesson

Create time-based ACLs, reflexive session filters, and apply uRPF in strict and loose modes to stop spoofing and control connections.

Target lab

Configure a border router to block social media during business hours, permit return sessions, and enable uRPF on the LAN interface.
