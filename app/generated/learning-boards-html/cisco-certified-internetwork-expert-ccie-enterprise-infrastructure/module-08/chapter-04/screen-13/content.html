Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Assessment Scenario

Question

Why can internal users browse external sites while external users cannot initiate connections to internal web servers after reflexive ACL implementation?

Correct interpretation

Reflexive ACLs create temporary inbound permits only after an internal host initiates a session, blocking unsolicited inbound connections.

Common misconception

Assuming reflexive ACLs are equivalent to full stateful firewalling or that they permit inbound connections without an outbound initiation.

Remediation advice

If legitimate inbound traffic is needed, explicitly permit that traffic via targeted rules; otherwise rely on reflexive entries to minimize attack surface.
