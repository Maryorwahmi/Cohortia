Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › Learning Objectives — Safety

Practical checklist

Estimate safe rates

Observe baseline control-plane traffic patterns before choosing CIR and burst values; account for small frequent messages and occasional large updates.

Test in lab first

Apply policies to non-production or with console access to prevent being locked out if a rule is too restrictive.

Monitor and tune

Use show policy-map control-plane to check drops and adjust policies until legitimate traffic flows smoothly.

Plan rollback

Have an out-of-band management path or local console so you can revert settings if necessary.
