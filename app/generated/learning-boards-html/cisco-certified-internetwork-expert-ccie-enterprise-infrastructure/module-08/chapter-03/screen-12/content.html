Module 08 › Chapter 8.3 › Common Mistakes
Top mistakes
  • Setting CIR or BC too low for routing protocols.
  • Using overly broad ACLs for management traffic.
  • Applying MPP remotely without out-of-band console access.
  • Failing to monitor show policy-map control-plane after changes.
How to avoid them
Start conservative
Deploy low-impact policing in lab, then raise CIR/BC after observing legitimate traffic behavior.
Document and rollback
Write explicit rollback steps and maintain console access before applying MPP to production devices.
