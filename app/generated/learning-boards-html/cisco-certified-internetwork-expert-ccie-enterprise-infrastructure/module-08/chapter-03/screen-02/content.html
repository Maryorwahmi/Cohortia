Learning Objectives

Master classification and access control for the control and management planes, with safe verification practices.

Bloom path
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain CoPP and MPP
Describe why policing the control plane and restricting management interfaces prevents CPU exhaustion and reduces attack surface. Relate to routing and management traffic types.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Configure CoPP
Create ACLs and class-maps to identify SSH, OSPF, BGP, ICMP, and a default class, then apply policing actions in a policy-map and attach it to the control plane.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement MPP
Restrict management traffic to specific interfaces, permitting only approved protocols from trusted interfaces to the control plane.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Verify and tune
Use show policy-map control-plane and real traffic observations to adjust policing rates and burst sizes, avoiding unintended drops of legitimate control traffic.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
