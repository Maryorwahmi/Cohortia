Common Mistakes and Operational Safeguards
Practical checks to avoid overloads and false positives.
Too many FNF fields
Collecting every field increases flow entry size and device overhead. Start minimal and expand later.
High-frequency probes
Probing too often can create false positives and consume CPU. Choose a balanced frequency.
Unrepresentative targets
Ensure the IP SLA destination reflects the real service endpoint, not just a reachable network device.
Assuming flows prove intent
NetFlow patterns indicate behavior; correlate with additional telemetry before acting.
Export source issues
Use a routable loopback or ensure routing to the collector so export packets arrive reliably.
Staged rollout
Validate show outputs and collector ingestion, simulate failures, then expand coverage.
