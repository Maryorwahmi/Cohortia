Detecting Threats with NetFlow Patterns
Map flow characteristics to likely security incidents.
DoS / DDoS
Sudden increase in bytes/packets to one destination or destination port; many source IPs or one source with high packet rate. Use counters to spot volume and connection-rate anomalies.
Worms / Scanning
Many short-lived flows to many destinations or many destination ports from the same source indicate scanning or propagation activity.
Insider Data Exfiltration
Unusual large transfers to external hosts or east-west transfers to storage segments warrant investigation.
Policy Violations
Flows on restricted segments or to blocked services should not exist; their presence indicates misconfiguration or violation.
Concrete detection example
Observed: destination 10.1.1.5 port 445, packets 1,200,000 in 60s
Interpretation: potential SMB flood or worm activity — escalate to packet capture and containment.
Cross-check flows with DNS logs, IDS alerts, or packet captures before taking automated containment actions.
