What to Remember
Key Concepts — IP SLA & Tracking
Three compact concept cards for IP SLA integration and operational use.
Flow Monitor
Binds record and exporter, manages cache, and is applied to interfaces for collection.
IP SLA
Active operations that measure RTT, jitter, packet loss, and application responsiveness.
Tracking Object
Translates IP SLA success/failure into an up/down state used to automate routing and responses.
Final Takeaway
Use focused telemetry and representative probes, validate behaviour with show commands and tests, and automate failover with tracking to preserve service availability while responding to detected threats.
