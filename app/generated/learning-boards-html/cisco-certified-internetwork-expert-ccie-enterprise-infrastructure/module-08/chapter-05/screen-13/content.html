Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › What to Remember
Mark trusted ports
Only uplinks to DHCP servers and trusted infrastructure should be trusted for DHCP Snooping and DAI.
Verify bindings first
Check show ip dhcp snooping binding before enabling DAI or IPSG; add static bindings as needed.
Use conservative modes
Prefer restrict over shutdown for port-security in production and monitor counters to detect issues.
Final thought
A documented trust model and binding inventory make secure deployments repeatable and reduce the chance of accidental outages when you enforce Layer 2 protections.
