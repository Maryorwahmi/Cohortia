Onboarding process
A simple checklist you can follow and validate in a lab or production pre‑stage.
1. Add WLC to DNA Center inventory
Provide management IP, credentials, and confirm secure management connectivity.
2. Enable fabric mode
Provision the WLC as a fabric edge so it understands VN and SGT concepts and can tag traffic.
3. Map SSIDs to VNs in DNA Center
Define which VN and VNI each SSID should use so the WLC applies correct encapsulation.
4. Validate with test clients
Authenticate a client, confirm SGT from ISE, and verify VNI and policy enforcement in the fabric.
Key Takeaway

Validation after provisioning prevents silent failures where clients appear connected but lack proper policy.

Pro tip
Automate baseline checks in DNA Center to confirm VN and SGT mappings are present after provisioning.
