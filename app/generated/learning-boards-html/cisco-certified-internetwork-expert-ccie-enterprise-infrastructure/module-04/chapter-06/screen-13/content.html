Three quick reminders
Use these checks to validate your lab or production changes.
WLC fabric mode
DNA Center must manage the WLC and fabric mode must be enabled for VN mapping to function.
Verify SGT & VNI
After mapping, authenticate a client and confirm the SGT and VNI are present in DNA Center and on the WLC.
Deny‑all for IoT/Guest
Start with restrictive SGACLs and only open specific ports and hosts required by the devices or guests.
Final thought
These checks reduce common deployment failures and make troubleshooting straightforward when wireless clients do not receive expected access.
