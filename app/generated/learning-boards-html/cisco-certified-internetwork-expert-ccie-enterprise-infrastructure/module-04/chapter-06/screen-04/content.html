Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Fabric‑enabled WLC
What is a fabric‑enabled WLC?
It acts like a Fabric Edge Node, performing authentication, tagging, and VXLAN encapsulation for wireless traffic.
Authentication and SGT assignment
WLC integrates with ISE to determine the SGT based on AAA decisions.
VXLAN encapsulation
Traffic is tagged with a VNI and SGT before entering the fabric overlay.
Consistent policy
Wireless clients get the same policies as wired clients via SGT and VN mapping.
Concrete example
Example mapping: 'Employee-WiFi' → Employee_VN (VNI 100). A user authenticates via ISE, gets SGT 10, the WLC encapsulates traffic with VNI 100 and tags it with SGT 10, and the fabric enforces employee policies across the network.
Key Takeaway

Make sure WLCs are onboarded and provisioned by DNA Center to gain fabric edge capabilities and consistent policy enforcement.

Common mistake
If ISE integration is missing or misconfigured, SGTs will not be applied and policies may fail silently.
