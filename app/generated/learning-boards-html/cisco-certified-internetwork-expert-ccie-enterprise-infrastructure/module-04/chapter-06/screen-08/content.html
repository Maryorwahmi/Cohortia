Multicast in the fabric
DNA Center automates multicast provisioning inside the overlay while leveraging underlay multicast capabilities.
Why automation helps
Manual multicast with PIM across many devices is fragile; SD‑Access reduces configuration surface and centralizes control through DNA Center.
Operational checklist
  • Ensure underlay supports IP multicast routing between fabric nodes.
  • Confirm DNA Center has provisioned multicast for the relevant VNs.
  • Test with representative sources and receivers across segments.
Example
A video server in one VN delivers to meeting rooms in other VNs without manual PIM per-edge device; DNA Center configures the overlay distribution and the underlay forwards packets efficiently.
Misconception
Automation does not remove the need to validate underlay multicast; both overlay and underlay must be healthy for multicast to work end‑to‑end.
