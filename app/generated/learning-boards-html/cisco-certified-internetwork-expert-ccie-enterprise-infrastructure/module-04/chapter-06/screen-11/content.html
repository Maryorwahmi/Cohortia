Fabric in a Box (FiaB)
A compact fabric option where a single device assumes multiple fabric roles for smaller sites.
Benefits
  • Simplified hardware footprint and reduced CAPEX for small sites.
  • Consistent SD‑Access policy and segmentation even at branches.
Tradeoffs
Less redundancy and single device capacity limits; plan for QoS and peak wireless traffic.
Operational checklist
Capacity plan the switch for expected client density and traffic types, verify QoS end‑to‑end, ensure ISE integration, and validate SSID→VN mappings with test clients before production.
Pro tip
For voice and video, reserve appropriate wireless airtime and prioritize VLANs or service classes in the fabric to maintain quality.
