Guest access workflow
Provide internet access while keeping guest traffic isolated from internal resources.
Design rules
  • Map guest SSID to a dedicated Guest_VN.
  • Enforce internet-only SGACLs; deny internal VN access.
  • Use ISE captive portal or sponsored accounts for onboarding.
Operational checks
Verify guests receive the Guest SGT and cannot reach corporate servers. Test portal flows and timed expirations for temporary credentials.
Example
'Guest-WiFi' → Guest_VN (VNI 200), web auth via ISE assigns SGT 20; SGACL restricts traffic to permitted outbound ports only.
