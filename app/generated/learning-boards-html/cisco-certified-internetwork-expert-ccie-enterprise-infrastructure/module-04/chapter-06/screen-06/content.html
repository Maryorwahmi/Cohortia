Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Auth & Tagging Flow
Step-by-step flow
A clear sequence to follow when diagnosing client connectivity or policy issues.
1
Client associates to AP
The AP forwards frames to the WLC for handling control and data plane operations.
2
WLC forwards AAA to ISE
ISE performs authentication and returns an SGT and any authorization attributes.
3
WLC maps SSID to VN
DNA Center mapping tells the WLC which VNI to use for this SSID.
4
VXLAN encapsulation and SGT tagging
Traffic is encapsulated and tagged so the fabric enforces SGACLs and routing.
Concrete diagnostic checklist
  • Verify WLC appears as fabric edge in DNA Center and shows SSID→VN mappings.
  • Check that ISE returns an SGT for the client and that the WLC logs the authorization result.
  • Confirm the VNI is present in the fabric and that VXLAN encapsulation is observed on the edge.
  • If traffic is denied, verify SGACLs are permitting the expected intra‑VN communication.
Common Mistake

Assuming successful association equals correct SGT. Always verify ISE assigned the expected SGT and not a default or fallback tag.
