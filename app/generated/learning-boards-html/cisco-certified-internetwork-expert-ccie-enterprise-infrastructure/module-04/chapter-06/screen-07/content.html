Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › SSID → VN Mapping
Common mapping examples
Translate access needs into Virtual Networks and SGTs.
Employee WiFi
Example mapping: 'Employee-WiFi' → Employee_VN (VNI 100), SGT 10. Full access to corporate resources with appropriate SGACLs.
Guest WiFi
Example mapping: 'Guest-WiFi' → Guest_VN (VNI 200), SGT 20. Internet-only and isolated from internal resources.
IoT Devices
Example mapping: 'IoT-Sensors' → VN_IoT_Devices (VNI 300), SGT 30. Strict SGACLs only permitting allowed server ports.
BYOD
Create a BYOD_VN with limited reach to critical servers and apply posture-based policies before granting internal access.
Example activity

Complete the SSID→VN mapping table for an office using provided VNs and propose a new BYOD VN and SGT.

Check
After mapping, authenticate clients and verify both VNI and SGT appear in DNA Center and on the WLC.
