Operational best practices

Simple operational habits prevent most production incidents.
Proactive monitoring
Set alerts in DNA Center for device, client, and app health to detect regressions early.
Regular backups
Back up DNA Center and ISE configurations regularly to reduce recovery time after issues.
Consistent naming
Use clear naming for VNs, SGTs, and pools so troubleshooting traces are obvious.
Phased rollout
Start with a small pilot to validate design and procedures before wide deployment.
MTU verification
Ensure underlay supports ~1550 bytes for VXLAN; inconsistent MTU causes dropped encapsulated traffic.
Lab testing & docs
Test policies and changes in lab; keep design and policy documentation current for incident response.
Key Takeaway

Operational discipline — monitoring, backups, testing, and documentation — multiplies the value of DNA Center automation.
