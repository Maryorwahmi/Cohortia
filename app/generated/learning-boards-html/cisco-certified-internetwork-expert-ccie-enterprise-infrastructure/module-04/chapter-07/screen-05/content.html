Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Structured troubleshooting
Process Flow

Underlay → LISP → VXLAN → Policy

Step 1 — Underlay
Ping RLOCs, `show ip ospf neighbor`, `show ip route ospf`, and traceroute to prove basic reachability.
Step 2 — LISP control plane
Check EID registration with `show lisp site` and `show lisp eid-table` and confirm map-cache entries with `show lisp map-cache`.
Step 3 — VXLAN data plane
Validate VTEPs, VNI bindings, and MTU to ensure encapsulated traffic is forwarded properly.
Step 4 — Policy
Use Path Trace to see SGTs and SGACL actions; check ISE Live Logs and `show cts sgt-map` on edge nodes.
Concrete checks
Underlay example
ping 10.255.255.2
traceroute 10.255.255.2
show ip ospf neighbor
If these fail, stop and fix underlay first.
LISP example
show lisp eid-table vlan 100 summary
show lisp map-cache 192.168.2.20
No mapping means the destination endpoint isn’t registered or the control plane is unreachable.
Common Mistake

Jumping to policy changes before confirming underlay and mapping often wastes time and can mask the real fault.
