Practical design checklist

Categorize traffic
Voice, signaling, critical apps, interactive, bulk.
Assign DSCP
EF for voice, CSx/AFx for others. Keep assignments conservative.
Map to PHBs
Document queue mappings on edge and core devices for consistent enforcement.
Verification
Include show commands and packet capture steps in the design to validate behavior regularly.
Operational maintenance
Update NBAR packs, review ACLs, and audit DSCP mappings periodically to avoid policy drift.
