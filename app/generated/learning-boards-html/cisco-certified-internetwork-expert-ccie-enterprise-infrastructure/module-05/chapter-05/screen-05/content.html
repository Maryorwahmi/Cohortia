Comparison

Three models, three trade-offs

Understand guarantees vs. scalability and choose the model that fits operational constraints.

Best-Effort
No guarantees. Works until congestion appears.
IntServ (RSVP)
Per-flow reservation, strict guarantees, poor scalability.
DiffServ
Edge marking and core PHBs for scalable, class-based treatment.
Misconception

DiffServ provides absolute per-flow guarantees like IntServ — not true. DiffServ gives class-based behavior and relies on provisioning and consistent marking.

Operational note
Enterprises generally adopt DiffServ because it limits state in core devices and scales to many flows. Use IntServ selectively where strict guarantees are required and the network supports per-flow reservations.
Quick decision guide
  1. Need per-flow hard guarantees? Consider IntServ in small domains.
  2. Need scalable class-based control for many flows? Choose DiffServ.
  3. Do you have unmanaged links and no QoS policy? Then behavior will default to Best-Effort.
