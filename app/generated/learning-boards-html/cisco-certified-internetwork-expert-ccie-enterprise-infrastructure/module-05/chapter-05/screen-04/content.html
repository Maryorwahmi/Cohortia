Core Concept

QoS — manage packet treatment

Guarantee performance for critical apps by prioritizing and shaping traffic. QoS focuses on latency, jitter, loss, and bandwidth allocation when links are congested.

High-level steps
  • Identify traffic that needs special treatment.
  • Mark packets to indicate their service class.
  • Enforce policies at edge and core devices via queuing or policing.
Analogy
General traffic
Acts like cars in regular lanes; no guarantees under congestion.
Priority lanes
Reserved for voice or video to reduce delay and jitter.
Control traffic
Network control and signaling that must be preserved.
Misconception
QoS does not increase link capacity; it reallocates existing capacity to meet service objectives when contention occurs.
