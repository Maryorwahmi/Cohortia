Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts
QoS
Differentiate treatment to protect latency-sensitive apps during congestion.
DiffServ
Edge classification and DSCP marking with scalable per-hop behaviors in the core.
DSCP
Six-bit field for marking packets — EF and AF values are common in enterprise QoS.
Summary takeaway

Use a documented, edge-based marking strategy with consistent DSCP mappings and verification steps so QoS delivers predictable application performance.
