Module 6 › Chapter 5.8 › Multicast vs Unicast
Concept
Multicast: 224.0.0.0/4 group addresses identify receivers who join. Routers forward traffic only to segments with active members. Unlike unicast or broadcast, multicast scales for many receivers without duplicating traffic on shared links.
Concrete example
One IPTV stream to 50 viewers consumes one stream on backbone links instead of 50 separate unicast streams.
Misconception
Multicast does not auto-propagate without IGMP and multicast routing. Routers must be configured to forward it.
Why it matters
Saves expensive bandwidth on aggregation links and is essential for scalable streaming and replicated data distribution.
Quick reference
Address range: 224.0.0.0/4
Analogy
Multicast is like a live TV broadcast delivered only to subscribers who tuned in; routers duplicate only where paths split.
Key Takeaway

Design networks to support multicast where many receivers share the same content; confirm IGMP and multicast routing are enabled to avoid silent failures.
