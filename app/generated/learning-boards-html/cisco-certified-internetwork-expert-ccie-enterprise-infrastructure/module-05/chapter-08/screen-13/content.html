Module 6 › Chapter 5.8 › Troubleshooting
Prioritized checklist
  1. Confirm ip pim sparse-mode on all interfaces.
  2. Verify RP is reachable via unicast routing.
  3. Ensure RP mappings are consistent across routers.
  4. Check ACLs and filters for multicast ranges and PIM control messages.
  5. Confirm show ip igmp groups shows listeners.
  6. Validate RPF with show ip mroute versus unicast route.
Common mistakes
  • Missing ip pim sparse-mode on a critical interface.
  • RP not reachable by some routers due to unicast routing gaps.
  • Inconsistent RP configuration across the PIM domain.
  • ACLs accidentally blocking multicast or PIM control traffic.
Reflection

When in doubt, check unicast reachability to the RP and the presence of ip pim sparse-mode before more exotic debugging.
