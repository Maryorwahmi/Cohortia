RP mechanics

The RP is the meeting point for sources and receivers in PIM-SM. Receivers join toward the RP, and sources register to it so traffic is delivered down the shared tree. Consistent RP mapping across routers is required or multicast delivery fails.

Static RP
Explicit mapping via ip pim rp-address. Common in labs for predictable behavior.
Auto-RP
Cisco proprietary mapping using RP-Announce and RP-Discovery messages.
BSR
Open standard election and distribution of RP information across domains.
Operational checklist
  • Ensure RP IP is reachable via unicast from all PIM routers.
  • Confirm consistent RP mapping with show ip pim rp mapping.
  • Provide redundancy for RP placement in production.
