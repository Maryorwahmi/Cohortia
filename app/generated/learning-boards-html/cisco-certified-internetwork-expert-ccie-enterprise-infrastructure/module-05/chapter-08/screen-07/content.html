Module 6 › Chapter 5.8 › PIM Modes
PIM Dense Mode
Floods multicast traffic to all PIM-enabled interfaces when a source appears, then prunes where no listeners exist. Works only where most segments are expected to have receivers.
When not to use
Avoid in large enterprise networks due to flooding overhead and scaling issues.
PIM Sparse Mode
Uses an RP for join-and-prune operation. Receivers join up to the RP; sources register with the RP. More scalable and widely deployed for enterprise WANs.
Why use PIM-SM
Avoids initial flooding and limits multicast forwarding only to interested segments, improving bandwidth efficiency.
Concrete contrast

PIM-DM floods first and prunes later; PIM-SM waits for explicit joins toward an RP. Choose based on receiver density and topology scale.

Takeaway

PIM-SM is the standard for enterprise multicast due to predictable scaling and control via RPs.
