Membership
show ip igmp groups — confirm listeners.
Control
show ip pim neighbor / show ip pim rp mapping — verify adjacencies and RP.
Forwarding
show ip mroute — inspect (*,G) and (S,G) and check RPF incoming interfaces.
Final takeaway

Use the Membership → Control → Forwarding sequence to isolate multicast faults quickly and ensure your RP is reachable and consistently mapped.
