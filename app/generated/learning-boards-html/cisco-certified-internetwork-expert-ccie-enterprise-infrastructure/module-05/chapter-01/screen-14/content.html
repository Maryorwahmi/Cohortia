Monitoring Maintenance
Operational maintenance
Keep NTP healthy with monitoring, key rotation, and runbooks.
Monitoring checklist
  • Alert on loss of synchronization and large offsets.
  • Track reachability and polling statistics from show ntp associations.
  • Audit upstream server health and changes.
Key rotation: plan coordinated changes, test in staging, and maintain fallback windows to avoid invalid states.
Runbook excerpt
1) Ping server; 2) Verify UDP 123; 3) Check keys; 4) Validate upstream sync; 5) Escalate if hardware clock suspected.
