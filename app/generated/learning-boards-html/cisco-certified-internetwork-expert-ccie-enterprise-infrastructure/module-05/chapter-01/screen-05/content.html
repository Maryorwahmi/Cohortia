Definition NTP hierarchy and strata
NTP strata explained
How stratum numbers convey distance from a reference clock and guide architecture.
Stratum levels
  • Stratum 0: Reference clocks (atomic, GPS). Not networked directly.
  • Stratum 1: Servers directly attached to stratum 0 devices.
  • Stratum 2+: Servers that sync to lower strata; higher numbers mean further from reference.
Example: an enterprise core router syncing to two public stratum 1 servers will present itself as stratum 2 to internal clients.
Design recommendations
  • Use internal stratum 2–3 servers for clients to reduce public traffic.
  • Always have multiple upstream sources across providers/locations.
  • Avoid exposing every client to public NTP servers directly.
Key Takeaway

Stratum numbers guide trust and architecture — lower is better, but redundancy matters more than a single low stratum.
