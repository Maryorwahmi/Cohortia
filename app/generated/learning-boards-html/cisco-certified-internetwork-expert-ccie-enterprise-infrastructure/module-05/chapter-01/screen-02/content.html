Learning Objectives
Skills you will practice and verify during the lab.
01
Explain time importance
Explain why synchronized clocks matter for logging, security, and forensic correlation. You will relate time errors to real operational impacts.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Describe NTP strata
Describe stratum levels from 0 to higher strata and how they affect accuracy and design of internal servers.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Configure NTP on IOS
Practice ntp server, ntp master, source interface, and authentication-key commands for clients and servers on Cisco IOS.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Troubleshoot NTP
Use show ntp status and show ntp associations, verify UDP 123, and check authentication keys to resolve synchronization problems.
Verified Competency Milestone
