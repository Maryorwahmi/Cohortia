Troubleshooting FHRP failures

A structured checklist reduces mean time to repair for FHRP issues.

  1. Validate interface IPs and matching FHRP group numbers.
  2. Check show standby/vrrp/glbp brief for roles, virtual IP, and MACs.
  3. Confirm preempt and priority values are as intended.
  4. Ensure hellos are not blocked by ACLs or firewall rules.
  5. Verify authentication strings if used, and inspect tracking settings.
Quick commands
show standby brief
show vrrp brief
show glbp brief
show glbp 3 detail
Interpretation: Look for expected active/master/AVG roles, virtual MACs, and whether preemption or tracking has modified priorities.
Common mistake

Forgetting to enable preempt makes a recovered higher priority router remain standby until manual action.
