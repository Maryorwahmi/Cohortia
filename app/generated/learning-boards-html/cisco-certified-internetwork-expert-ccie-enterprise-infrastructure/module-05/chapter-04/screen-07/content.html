Comparison

VRRP vs HSRP

VRRP is the open standard alternative to HSRP; choose it for multi‑vendor interoperability and slightly different operational semantics.

Master vs Active
VRRP master can retain use of its physical IP; HSRP active primarily forwards for virtual IP.
Virtual MAC format
VRRP uses 0000.5E00.01xx; HSRP uses 0000.0C07.ACxx where xx is group number.
Use case
Prefer VRRP when multiple vendors are present; prefer HSRP for Cisco‑only features and behaviors.
Practical checks
Priority and preempt: Configure to control master election and recovery.
Timers and hellos: Verify that hello intervals and hold times meet your availability requirements.
Interoperability: Test ARP behavior and ensure master reachability to upstream networks.
Takeaway

VRRP and HSRP deliver gateway redundancy; choose VRRP for open standards and HSRP when Cisco‑specific behavior is required.
