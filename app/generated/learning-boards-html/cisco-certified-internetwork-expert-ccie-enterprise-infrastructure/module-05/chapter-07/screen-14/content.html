What to Remember
Traffic Policing
Enforces max rate by dropping or re-marking packets that exceed tokens.
Traffic Shaping
Buffers excess and paces transmission to meet the average CIR; adds delay but avoids loss.
Burst Parameters
CIR, Bc and Be determine token refill and burst allowances; tune them to real traffic.
Summary
Pick policing for strict, low-latency enforcement and shaping when you can tolerate delay to prevent drops. Always verify with show policy-map interface under test traffic.
