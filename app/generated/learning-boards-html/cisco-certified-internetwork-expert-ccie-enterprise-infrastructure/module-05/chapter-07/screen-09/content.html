Parameters CIR, Bc, Be
Definitions
  • CIR: Committed Information Rate — tokens added per second (average allowed rate).
  • Bc: Committed Burst Size — how much can be sent at CIR before excess.
  • Be: Excess Burst Size — additional burst allowance beyond Bc that is less guaranteed.
Tuning guidance
Estimate expected maximum short-term burst (e.g., MTU bursts from aggregation) and set Bc large enough to avoid marking single bursts. If you observe drops, increase Bc/Be or add buffer, keeping in mind shaping increases latency. Use realistic traffic measurements rather than guesswork.
Key Takeaway

Correct burst sizing prevents unnecessary exceed counts and keeps short transfers from being penalized.
