Process Flow Policing decision steps
Policing step-by-step
Follow tokens → conform/exceed → configured action to understand outcome under load.
Step 1
Tokens accumulate at CIR.
Step 2
Packet arrives and requests tokens based on size.
Step 3
If tokens exist → conform; else → exceed and apply drop or remark.
Example
Policing to 1 Mbps: short bursts beyond tokens will immediately be marked exceed and dropped if drop is configured.
Misconception
Policing does not queue. Expect loss, not delay, for packets over the limit.
