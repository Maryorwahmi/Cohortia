Comparison Policing vs Shaping
Policing (Valve)
  • Immediate action on exceed: drop or remark.
  • Stateless, no buffering, low latency.
  • Best applied inbound to protect resources or enforce strict limits.
Example: Drop excess that would otherwise overwhelm a data center uplink.
Shaping (Reservoir)
  • Buffers excess packets and transmits at CIR.
  • Stateful, adds delay, reduces packet loss for bursts.
  • Best applied outbound at customer edge to meet ISP SLA.
Example: Prevent ISP drops by pacing outbound bursts to a 2 Mbps average.
Decision checklist
If immediate protection and low delay are primary, choose policing. If preserving packets and smoothing bursts is primary and some delay is acceptable, choose shaping. Consider per-class treatments for latency-sensitive traffic.
