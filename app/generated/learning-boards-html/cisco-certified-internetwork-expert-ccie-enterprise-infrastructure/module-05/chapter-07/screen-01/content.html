Policing vs Shaping — quick orientation

Understand the enforcement model, effects on packet loss and latency, and where to apply each tool in your network.

In this lesson
Distinguish policing and shaping and apply Cisco policy-map commands to enforce SLAs.
Key params
CIR, Bc, Be and token-bucket behavior for both mechanisms.
Verification
Use show policy-map interface to read conform, exceed, queue, and drop counters.

Explore Key Topics
Policing
Immediate enforcement, stateless, drop or remark when tokens are lacking.
Shaping
Buffers bursts, smooths transmission to match CIR, stateful queueing.
Verify
show policy-map interface reveals conform/exceed and queue statistics to verify behavior.
