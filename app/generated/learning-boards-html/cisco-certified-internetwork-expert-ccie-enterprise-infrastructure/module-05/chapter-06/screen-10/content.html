What WRED does
Weighted Random Early Detection
Monitors queue depth and performs random early drops; configured to drop lower-priority DSCPs more aggressively.
  • • Signals TCP senders to slow before queues overflow.
  • • Reduces tail-drop-induced global synchronization.
  • • Best applied to class-default or best-effort traffic.
Configuration hint
policy-map CONGESTION_POLICY
 class class-default
  random-detect dscp-based
Enable DSCP-sensitive WRED to drop lower-priority packets sooner and preserve higher-priority traffic during congestion.
