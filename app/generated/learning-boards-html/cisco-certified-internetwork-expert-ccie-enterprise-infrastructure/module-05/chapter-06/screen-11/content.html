TCP + WRED
WRED drops early; TCP responds by reducing its window, easing congestion. This cooperative feedback loop stabilizes queues.
Result: fewer large synchronized drops and better overall throughput during overload.
UDP behavior
UDP-based flows usually do not reduce send rate on packet loss, so WRED's drops do not throttle them; drops only reduce media quality.
Design implication: protect UDP with LLQ or guarantees, and use WRED on TCP-heavy best-effort traffic.
