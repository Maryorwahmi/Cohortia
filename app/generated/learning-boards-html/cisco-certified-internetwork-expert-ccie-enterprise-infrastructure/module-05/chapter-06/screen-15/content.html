Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Concepts
Congestion
When traffic exceeds link capacity, causing queues, loss, and delay.
Queuing
Buffering and scheduling packets; choice of algorithm matters for mixed traffic.
WRED
Early, weighted drops to signal TCP senders and avoid full queues; not a replacement for priority protection.
Summary takeaway

Combine CBWFQ guarantees, LLQ priority with limits, and WRED for best-effort traffic to build stable QoS that protects real-time flows while fairly sharing remaining capacity.
