Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › What to Remember
CBWFQ
Define classes and guarantee minimum bandwidth to ensure predictable service for critical flows.
LLQ
Use strict priority for voice and video but always set a rate limit to prevent starvation.
WRED
Apply to best-effort traffic to drop early and signal TCP to reduce rate, preventing full queues.
One-line takeaway

Plan allocations, set priority limits, and verify — those three steps make QoS predictable and effective.
