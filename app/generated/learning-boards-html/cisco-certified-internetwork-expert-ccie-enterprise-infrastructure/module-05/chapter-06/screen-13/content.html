Key commands
show policy-map interface GigabitEthernet0/0 output
show policy-map interface GigabitEthernet0/0
show running-config | section policy-map
Use these to inspect queue depths, drops, and to confirm the active policy and its counters during test traffic.
Troubleshooting checklist
  1. Confirm DSCP markings at the packet source and on the router.
  2. Check class-map matches and policy-map configuration.
  3. Verify interface bandwidth matches physical link speed.
  4. Observe counters while running combined traffic to reproduce the issue.
