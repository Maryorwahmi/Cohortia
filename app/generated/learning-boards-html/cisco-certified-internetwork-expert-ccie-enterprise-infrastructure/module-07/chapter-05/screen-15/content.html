Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › What to Remember
Inspect raw replies
Always print or log the XML/JSON structure before parsing or extracting fields.
Use namespaces & keys
Provide the correct XML namespace and list keys so the device can map your payload to the right YANG resource.
Verify and be idempotent
Retrieve, diff, apply only on changes, and re-read to confirm the intended state was achieved.
Reflection
When would you prefer NETCONF's candidate/commit over a RESTCONF PATCH? Consider transaction needs, validation, and orchestration complexity.
