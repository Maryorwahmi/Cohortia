Header fields to check
  • module name, namespace, and prefix — used by tooling and for unique identification.
  • organization and contact — helpful for vendor models.
  • revision statements — always confirm the model revision against your device.
Practical tip

Use the namespace and revision to pick the right YANG file for your device software version.

Top-level nodes

Modules expose top-level containers or lists that map to logical groupings, such as interfaces or VLANs. Finding these top-level nodes gives you the starting path for building payloads.

Quick checklist
  • Confirm module name and revision.
  • Locate the top-level container or list for your target configuration.
  • Identify any imports or typedefs referenced by the module.
