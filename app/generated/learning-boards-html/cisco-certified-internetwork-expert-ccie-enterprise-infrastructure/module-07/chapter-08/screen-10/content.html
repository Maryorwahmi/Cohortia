Collector responsibilities and flow

Collectors are more than gateways; they validate, normalize, and optionally aggregate telemetry before forwarding.
Accept streams
Bind to a gNMI port, authenticate devices, and accept subscriptions.
Parse & normalize
Decode GPB/JSON, normalize timestamps, and map fields for storage.
Enrich & aggregate
Optional aggregation reduces retention volume; enrichment adds metadata for queries.
Forward & persist
Forward to a broker or a time-series database like InfluxDB for dashboards.
Telegraf example (conceptual)
[[inputs.gnmi]] bind_address = ":57500" username = "admin" password = "cisco" [[outputs.influxdb]] urls = ["http://localhost:8086"] database = "telemetry_data"
Common mistake
Treating the collector as stateless. In production, handle reconnections, malformed payloads, and scaling issues.
Key Takeaway

Design collectors to validate, normalize, and buffer data; plan for authentication, TLS, and high availability.
