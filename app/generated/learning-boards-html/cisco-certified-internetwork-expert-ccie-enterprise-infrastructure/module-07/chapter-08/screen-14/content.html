Key Concepts

Streaming Telemetry
Devices push continuous, YANG-path-based operational data to a collector in near real time.
SNMP Polling
Pull-based polling yields snapshots that can miss transient events and scales differently than push models.
gNMI & NETCONF
gNMI uses gRPC and often GPB; NETCONF subscriptions use XML/JSON and leverage existing NETCONF infrastructure.
Summary Takeaway

Design telemetry pipelines to match fidelity, scale, and security requirements: choose protocols and encodings to balance performance and operational simplicity.
