Architecture

End-to-End Telemetry Pipeline

Network Devices
Publish YANG-path data per sensor groups and subscriptions.
Telemetry Collector
Receives and parses gNMI/NETCONF streams; forwards to storage or broker.
Message Broker (optional)
Buffers and distributes streams for scale and decoupling.
Time-Series DB
Optimized for high ingest, retention, and time-based queries.
Processors / Consumers
Anomaly detectors, analytics, and enrichment pipelines.
Visualization
Grafana dashboards and alerts surface trends and thresholds.
Concrete example
IOS XE device streams interface statistics to Telegraf (gNMI input), Telegraf writes to InfluxDB, Grafana visualizes in-octets and out-octets.
Operational caution
Plan retention policies and aggregation to avoid unbounded growth in storage when sampling at high frequency.
Key Takeaway

Build pipelines that balance fidelity and cost: where needed, add brokers and aggregation to maintain performance and reliability.
