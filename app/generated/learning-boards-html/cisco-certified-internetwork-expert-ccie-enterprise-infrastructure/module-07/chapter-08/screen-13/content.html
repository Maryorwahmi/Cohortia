Assessment Idea

Design trade-offs under constraints

Present a scenario requiring sub-second visibility but with constrained device CPU and limited collector capacity, and ask learners to propose a telemetry plan.
Scenario
Intermittent microbursts impact application performance. SNMP polling at five-minute intervals missed the events. You have limited collector resources and must avoid overloading devices.
What to propose
Protocol choice, YANG paths, sample interval, encoding, retention and aggregation strategy, and security measures.
Scoring criteria
Correct protocol rationale, defensible cadence, encoding trade-offs, and realistic storage/retention plan.
Key Takeaway

Good solutions balance visibility and resource constraints rather than simply maximizing sample frequency.
