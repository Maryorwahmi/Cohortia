Operational Considerations

Pitfalls and how to avoid them

  • Over-subscribing: Requesting too many metrics or too high a cadence can overload devices and collectors.
  • Security: Always use TLS and authenticate collectors to prevent unauthorized access to telemetry streams.
  • Data volume: Plan retention, aggregation, and summarization to limit storage growth.
  • YANG path mismatch: Verify paths against device software version and supported models.
  • Collector reliability: Ensure high availability and consider brokers for buffering.
Concrete mitigation
Pilot with a narrow set of YANG paths at moderate cadence, measure device and network load, then iterate toward production settings.
Common mistake to avoid
Assuming telemetry does not need the same operational security posture as other management interfaces—secure it the same way you secure config channels.
Key Takeaway

Design telemetry with controls for cadence, security, storage, and collector availability to avoid common operational failures.
