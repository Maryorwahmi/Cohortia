Intent-Based Networking
What it is
Declare the desired state of the network and let the system translate and enforce that state across devices.
Concrete example
Declare that branch traffic must reach central services with a latency SLA. The system applies routes, QoS, and verification checks to meet the intent.
Common pitfall
Relying on intent without telemetry or validation can hide mismatches between desired and actual state.
Why it matters
Focus on outcomes, reduce device-specific errors, and enable continuous compliance monitoring.
Check your understanding
Name one policy in your network that could be expressed as an intent and monitored automatically.
