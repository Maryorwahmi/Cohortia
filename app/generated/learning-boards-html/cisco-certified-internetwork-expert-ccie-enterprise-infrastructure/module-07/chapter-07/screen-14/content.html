Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Key Concepts
Network Orchestration

Coordinate automated tasks across domains to deliver end-to-end services with resource and transaction management.

Cisco NSO

Model-driven orchestration using YANG and transactional commits to maintain desired-state across devices.

CI/CD for Networks

Version control, automated tests, staging, approval gates and orchestrated deployment with verification and rollback.

Summary takeaway

Combine orchestration and CI/CD to achieve faster, safer and repeatable network change delivery while maintaining auditable rollback paths.
