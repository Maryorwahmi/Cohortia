Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Verification & Closed-Loop
Post-deploy checks
Ping and basic reachability tests to confirm endpoints respond.
Routing and adjacency checks: OSPF adjacency state, route table entries.
Service tests: application-level health or service availability where applicable.
Telemetry validation: compare metrics and intent-state from the orchestration database.
Closed-loop actions
  • Automatic remediation: apply corrective playbooks when safe.
  • Rollback trigger: revert changes if remediation fails or risk is high.
  • Alerting: create tickets with CI/CD logs and test output for human operators.
Misconception

Assuming telemetry alone prevents outages. Telemetry enables remediation but you must design safe automated actions and fallback rollbacks to avoid cascading failures.

Key Takeaway

Verification plus telemetry-driven closed-loop automation reduces time-to-detect and time-to-remediate while preserving operational safety.
