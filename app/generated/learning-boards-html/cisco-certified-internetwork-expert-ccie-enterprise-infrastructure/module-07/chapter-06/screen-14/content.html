Module 07 › Chapter 7.6 › Roles & Project Layout
Typical layout
  • inventory.ini
  • playbooks/ (top-level playbooks)
  • roles//{tasks,templates,vars,defaults,handlers}
  • group_vars/ and host_vars/ for targeted variables
Use a requirements.yml to pin collection versions and recreate environments consistently across team members and CI.
Role design guidance
  • Keep roles focused and single-purpose: a role per function such as configure_interfaces.
  • Provide sensible defaults and document required variables in the role README.
  • Include idempotent tasks and verification steps inside the role where appropriate.
Key Takeaway

Structuring automation as roles and tracking everything in version control makes collaboration safer and rollbacks straightforward.
