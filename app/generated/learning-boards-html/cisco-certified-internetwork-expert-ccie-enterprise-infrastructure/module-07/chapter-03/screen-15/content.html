Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › What to Remember
Check capabilities
Confirm available YANG modules and operations before sending model-specific payloads.
Use correct namespaces & keys
Namespaces bind elements to YANG modules; list keys uniquely identify entries in URIs or payloads.
Validate and be idempotent
Follow updates with reads and design scripts to be repeatable without side effects.
Final thought

NETCONF and RESTCONF are complementary. Master both to design robust, safe, and automated network operations.
