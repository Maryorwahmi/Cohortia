Why separate collection and parsing

Separating these steps makes debugging and parser development easier. You can tweak parsing rules on stored files without reconnecting to devices or re-running collection jobs.

Practical workflow
  1. Collect raw output files named predictably.
  2. Develop parser(s) against sample files in a separate script.
  3. Validate parser outputs against expected keys and types.
  4. Integrate parsing into reporting pipelines once stable.
Practical note
TextFSM and genie will be addressed in later chapters; for now, store clean raw outputs and design file names that include device and command.
Example filename
R1_show_version.txt or 192.168.1.101_show_ip_interface_brief.txt
Key Takeaway

Collect raw CLI text first. Parse later with dedicated libraries to make your automation maintainable and testable.
