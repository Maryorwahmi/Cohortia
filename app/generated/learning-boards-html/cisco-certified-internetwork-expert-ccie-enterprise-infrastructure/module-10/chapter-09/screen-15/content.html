Connectivity First
Ping and SSH checks find most UNREACHABLE or timeout errors quickly.
Payload Validation
Validate YANG models for NETCONF/RESTCONF and structure JSON precisely for controller APIs.
Structured Logging
Include host, action, timestamp, and exception details to speed post-mortems.
Summary Takeaway
Keep a short checklist and reproduce failing operations manually before changing automation. Use dry-run options and log everything so fixes are safe and traceable.
