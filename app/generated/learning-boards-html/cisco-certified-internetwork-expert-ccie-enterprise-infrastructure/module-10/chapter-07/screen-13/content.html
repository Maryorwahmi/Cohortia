Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Safety and rollback planning

Change safely

Always gather pre-change evidence, apply minimal edits, and verify with objective counters.

Before change
  • Collect show access-lists, show crypto isakmp sa, and show crypto ipsec sa.
  • Record NAT translations and interface ACL attachments.
During change
  • Make minimal, reversible edits and test with limited hosts.
  • Use short debug windows and monitor device load.
After change
  • Re-run show commands and confirm counters increment with test traffic.
  • Revert quickly using the saved pre-change configuration if needed.
Key Takeaway

Discipline and short feedback loops keep production stable while you troubleshoot.
