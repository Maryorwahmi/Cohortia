Learning Objectives

Master targeted diagnostics and safe corrective actions for VPN and security issues.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Diagnose IPsec Phase failures

Identify whether failures occur during IKE Phase 1 negotiation or during Phase 2 transform negotiation. This matters because fixes differ: pre-shared key or DH group vs transform-set or ACLs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Troubleshoot DMVPN NHRP

Validate NHRP mappings, network IDs, and redirect/shortcut flags so spokes can build direct tunnels and avoid hub hairpinning.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Correct firewall and ACL logic

Read ACLs top-down, check hit counts, and verify direction so a deny doesn’t accidentally block VPN control traffic or interesting traffic definitions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Debug NAT and translation timing

Inspect translations and ordering to ensure NAT does not alter packets before encryption or that no nat rule prevents VPN traffic from matching the crypto ACL.
