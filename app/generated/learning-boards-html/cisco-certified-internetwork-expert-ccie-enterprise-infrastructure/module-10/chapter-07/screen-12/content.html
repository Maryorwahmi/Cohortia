CLI checklist for Router A

First commands
show crypto isakmp sa
show crypto ipsec sa
show crypto map
show access-lists
show ip nat translations
Concrete checks
  • Look for peer IP and Status QM_IDLE or UP.
  • Confirm pkts encaps/decaps increment after test traffic.
  • Verify crypto ACL lists 10.1.1.0/24 and 10.2.2.0/24.
Example debug usage

If show crypto isakmp sa shows no entry, enable debug crypto isakmp on the side that is rejecting negotiations, watch for authentication failures, then immediately stop the debug after you capture the error.

! Example outputs to interpret
show crypto isakmp sa  -> expect QM_IDLE or UP with peer 198.51.100.1
show crypto ipsec sa   -> look for pkts encaps = X, pkts decaps = Y

! If counters are zero, inspect crypto ACL and NAT exemptions
Key Takeaway

Collect the same show outputs before and after a fix to confirm the problem is resolved by observing state changes and packet counters.
