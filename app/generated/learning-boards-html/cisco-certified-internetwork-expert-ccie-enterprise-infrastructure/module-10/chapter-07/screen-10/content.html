Core Concept

ACL processing and policy checks

ACLs are order-sensitive and applied per-interface direction. Hit counts and session tables reveal what was matched.

Quick checks
  • Identify which device filters the traffic path.
  • Use show access-lists to inspect rules and hit counts.
  • Confirm ACL applied direction with show ip interface.
Concrete example

If a crypto ACL shows the correct networks but traffic still fails, check upstream firewall ACLs for an early deny. After permitting test traffic, verify that the access-list hit counter increments to confirm the rule matched.

Danger note

Changing ACLs or firewall policies can immediately block users; always test with a rollback plan and minimal change scope.

Key Takeaway

Use hit counts and session tables as evidence that a rule matched or failed to match expected traffic.
