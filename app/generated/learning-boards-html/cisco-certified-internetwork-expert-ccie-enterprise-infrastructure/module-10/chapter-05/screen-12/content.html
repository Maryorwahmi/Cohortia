Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › LISP & VXLAN
Concept
EID → LISP map → RLOC → VXLAN forward
LISP maps host IPs (EIDs) to VTEP addresses (RLOCs).
VXLAN tunnels between VTEPs carry encapsulated frames whose inner header uses the EID.
Both mapping and tunnel adjacency must be healthy for forwarding to succeed.
Analogy
Think of LISP as a directory service that tells you which post office (RLOC) holds a street address (EID); VXLAN is the truck that moves the package between post offices.
Check your understanding
If VXLAN tunnels are up but the map‑cache lacks an EID entry, what happens to packets destined for that host? (Answer: dropped or unreachable until mapping is registered.)
