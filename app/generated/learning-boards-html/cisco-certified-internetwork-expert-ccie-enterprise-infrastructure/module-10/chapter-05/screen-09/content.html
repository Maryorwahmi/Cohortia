Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Common Mistakes
Mistake
Changing policies before collecting evidence
Remedy: gather command outputs, compare to a known-good baseline, and reproduce the issue in a lab prior to wide changes.
Mistake
Assuming underlay is healthy from partial tests
Remedy: run traceroute, check routing tables, and inspect MTU and queueing to exclude transient underlay causes.
Operational tip
When escalating, attach show control connections, show ipsec sa, and show app-route statistics to provide context for the WAN engineering team.
Mistake
Tuning SLA thresholds in production to hide problems
Remedy: use a lab to validate threshold changes. Treat threshold changes as a last resort and document the reason clearly.
