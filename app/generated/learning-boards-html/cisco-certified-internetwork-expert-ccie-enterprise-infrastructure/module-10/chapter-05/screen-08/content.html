Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Hands‑on AAR Debug
Step sequence
1) show control connections — confirm vSmart is up and policies can reach vEdge‑BR1.
2) show ipsec sa & show bfd sessions — confirm MPLS and Internet tunnels and BFD health.
3) show app-route statistics — verify classification for 1.2.3.4:443 and chosen path.
4) show policy service-vpn 1 — verify the AAR policy is applied to VPN 1 and correct direction.
Simulated CLI outputs
> show app-route statistics
1.2.3.4:443 matched, path-id 1->internet, path-id 2->mpls
Chosen path: internet
Metrics: internet latency 45ms, mpls latency 120ms (threshold 100ms)

> show policy service-vpn 1
AAR-policy applied: AAR-priority, direction: from-site
Example conclusion
The MPLS link exceeded the SLA threshold, so AAR selected the internet link. Fix could be link improvement or threshold tuning after lab validation.
