Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › VXLAN, VTEP & LISP
Checklist
Underlay → VTEP → LISP → SGT
Confirm underlay routes with show ip route and traceroute between fabric nodes.
Confirm VTEP and VXLAN tunnel state with show vxlan tunnel on edge/border nodes.
Confirm LISP mappings and map‑cache entries with show lisp outputs.
Confirm SGT propagation and CTS role permissions for segmentation enforcement.
Example outputs
> show vxlan tunnel
VTEP to 172.16.1.5: state up, encapsulation vxlan, learned endpoints: 125

> show lisp eid-table 0 map-cache
10.10.1.23 -> RLOC 172.16.1.5 via VTEP-12

> show cts sxp connections
SXP peers: 2 connected, SGTs propagated
Key takeaway
Missing LISP map‑cache entries or down VTEPs are primary causes of in‑fabric reachability failures. Repair mapping or VTEP adjacency first.
