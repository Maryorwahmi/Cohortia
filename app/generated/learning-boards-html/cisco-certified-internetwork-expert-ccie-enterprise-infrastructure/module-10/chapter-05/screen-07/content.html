Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Policy & AAR
Comparison
Policy misconfiguration vs SLA-driven steering
Misconfiguration: Policy not applied to the right VPN, wrong from/to direction, or incorrect sequence numbers.
SLA-driven: Preferred path fails SLA thresholds so AAR chooses the next-best path automatically.
Classification failures: Incorrect application ID, port, or destination causes no match.
Terminal checks
> show policy service-vpn 1
Policy: AAR-priority applied to VPN 1, direction: from-site

> show app-route statistics

Example scenario
If the MPLS latency exceeds the policy threshold, AAR will shift traffic to the internet link even when MPLS SAs and BFD are up.
