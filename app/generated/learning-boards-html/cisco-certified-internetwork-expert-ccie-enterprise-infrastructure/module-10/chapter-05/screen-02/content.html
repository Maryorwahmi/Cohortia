Learning Objectives
Master checks, commands, and interpretation for SD‑WAN and SD‑Access troubleshooting.
01
Control plane diagnosis
Verify vSmart/vBond connectivity and certificate trust. This skill prevents mistaking certificate or orchestration failures for routing problems.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Data plane & tunnels
Check IPsec SAs, BFD sessions and overlay routes so you know whether traffic can be forwarded across intended links.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Policy & AAR
Validate that application‑aware routing policies are applied and that SLA thresholds or color preferences allow the chosen path.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
SD‑Access fabric checks
Onboard and control plane validation, VTEP/VXLAN checks, LISP map‑cache, and SGT propagation ensure segmentation and reachability.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
