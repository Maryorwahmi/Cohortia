Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Troubleshooting Playbook
Checklist
Capture → Underlay → Control → Overlay → Policy → Correlate → Plan
1. Capture: list affected users, VN/app, timestamps, and initial symptoms.
2. Underlay: ping/traceroute and show ip route between peers and controllers.
3. Control: show control connections, show orchestrator connections, show pnp profile.
4. Overlay: show ipsec sa, show bfd sessions, show vxlan tunnel, show lisp map-cache.
5. Policy: show app-route statistics, show policy service-vpn, and verify application matching.
6. Correlate: use DNA Center logs and fabric health; save outputs with timestamps.
7. Plan: test remediations in lab; schedule controlled changes and have rollbacks ready.
Check your work
Before closing a ticket, confirm that traffic follows the expected path and that monitoring shows steady state for a defined observation period.
