Architecture
Onboarding, DNA Center, and control plane roles
DNA Center performs discovery and PnP provisioning for fabric devices.
Control plane nodes run LISP map‑server functions to map EIDs to RLOCs.
Ensure certificates and management-plane reachability exist prior to role assignment.
