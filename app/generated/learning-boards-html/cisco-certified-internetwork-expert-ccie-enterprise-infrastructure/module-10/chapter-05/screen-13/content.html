Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › DNA Center Diagnostics
Workflow
Inventory → Onboarding logs → Policy deployment → Device CLI
Use DNA Center inventory to find the affected device and view onboarding logs for discovery errors.
Check policy deployment status in DNA Center before assuming a push succeeded.
Retrieve device CLI outputs for definitive mapping and forwarding evidence.
Terminal checks
> show fabric control-plane summary
Control nodes: 3 online, map-servers healthy

> show fabric edge summary
Edges: 12 registered, 11 active
Practical tip
When escalating to platform teams, include DNA Center logs, device show outputs, and timestamps. This speeds root cause isolation across management and device planes.
