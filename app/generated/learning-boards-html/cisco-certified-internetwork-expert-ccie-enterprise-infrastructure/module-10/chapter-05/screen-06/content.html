Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Data Plane & Tunnels
Key commands
show ipsec sa · show bfd sessions · show ip route vpn · show omp routes
Look for SA state, packet counters, and correct peers.
Confirm BFD session is up and reporting acceptable loss/latency.
Ensure overlay routes exist for the destination and point to expected TLOCs.
Example outputs
> show ipsec sa
IPSec-SA to 203.0.113.2: name tloc-mpls, state: up, in-packets: 124512, out-packets: 123998

> show bfd sessions
Peer 203.0.113.2: state UP, tx: 50ms, rx: 48ms, loss: 0%

> show ip route vpn 0
10.2.0.0/24 via 10.0.0.5 (tloc-mpls)

> show omp routes
1.2.3.0/24 learned via vSmart, best next-hop 203.0.113.2
Misconception
An IPsec SA in the table does not prove application reachability. Correlate with packet counters, BFD, and overlay routes to confirm forwarding.
