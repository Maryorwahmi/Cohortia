Troubleshooting SD‑WAN and SD‑Access Fabric Issues
Diagnose control plane, data plane, and policy failures across Viptela overlays and SD‑Access fabrics.
In this lesson
Run focused show and debug commands, interpret their outputs, and apply a safe troubleshooting playbook for SD‑WAN and SD‑Access.
Why it matters
Control or policy failures can silently alter traffic paths, causing outages or compliance breaches. Fast, accurate diagnosis reduces downtime.
