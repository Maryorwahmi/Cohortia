DHCP, DNS, NTP, and AAA are foundational. This lesson gives you inspection steps, IOS show/debug commands, and safe practices to resolve failures quickly and correctly.

Addressing, name resolution, time sync, and access control are prerequisites for reliable services and secure operations.
