Module 10 › Chapter 10.3 › BGP route advertisement and filtering

Root cause sequence

  • Confirm prefix exists locally (show ip route).
  • Check BGP origin and presence in RIB (show ip bgp [network]).
  • Inspect redistribute statements and subnets keyword.
  • Evaluate route-maps / prefix-lists / ACLs applied in or out of BGP neighbors.
CLI example
show ip bgp 203.0.113.0
show ip bgp neighbors 10.1.1.2 advertised-routes
show run | section router bgp
show ip prefix-list
Misconception

Assuming missing route means inbound filtering. Often the route was never originated into BGP or was removed by redistribution rules.

Takeaway

Work from the origin outward: local routing, redistribution, then neighbor advertisement and filtering.
