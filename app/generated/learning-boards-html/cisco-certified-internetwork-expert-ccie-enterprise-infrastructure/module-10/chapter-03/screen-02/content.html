Learning Objectives

Master diagnosis and remediation for OSPF, EIGRP and BGP.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Diagnose OSPF adjacency issues

Verify hello/dead timers, network type, area ID and authentication to restore LSA exchange and adjacencies.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Resolve EIGRP neighbor and SIA states

Use neighbor, topology and interface detail commands to detect passive interfaces, filters, or unreachable replies causing SIA.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Troubleshoot BGP peering and advertisements

Confirm TCP reachability, neighbor state, and advertised/received routes; inspect attributes affecting path selection.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply safe diagnostic practices

Use scoped debug, plan rollbacks, and avoid disruptive clears in production to maintain stability while troubleshooting.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
