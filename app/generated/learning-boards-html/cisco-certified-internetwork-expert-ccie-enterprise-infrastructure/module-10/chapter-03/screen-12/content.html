Check authentication

Verify key-chain names, key-id, and lifetime match on both peers before assuming a network fault.

Inspect ACLs/Firewalls

Confirm multicast hellos and TCP 179 are not blocked along the path between neighbors.

Timers and types

Align OSPF hello/dead timers and ensure network types match; mismatches prevent adjacency.

Summarization

Confirm auto-summary is disabled if not desired and that manual summaries are not hiding routes.

Prevention tip

Maintain a standard checklist and automate configuration validation where possible to surface mismatches early.

Takeaway

Most protocol outages stem from a small set of human errors; a checklist accelerates diagnosis and prevents repeat incidents.
