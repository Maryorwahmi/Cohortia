Module 10 › Chapter 10.3 › EIGRP neighbor formation fundamentals
What to check first
  • show ip eigrp neighbors — check adjacency table and uptime.
  • show ip interface brief — confirm the interfaces are up and IPs present.
  • show ip eigrp interfaces detail — verify participation and no passive flags.
  • Verify AS number and key-chains for authentication.
Example
R2# show ip eigrp neighbors
H   Address        Interface     Hold Uptime  SRTT   RTO  Q  Seq
0   10.0.0.3       Gi0/1         12  00:05:12  50   100  0  3
Misconceptions & tips

EIGRP does not use area IDs; beginners often look for OSPF-like area misconfigurations when AS or passive-interface is the real issue. Also avoid global clears; reset only affected neighborships after confirming cause.

Key Takeaway

Focus on AS, reachability, authentication, and passive-interface flags first. Then inspect topology for SIA symptoms if routes are missing.
