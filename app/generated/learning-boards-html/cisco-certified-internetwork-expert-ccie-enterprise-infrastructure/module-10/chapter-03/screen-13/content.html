Module 10 › Chapter 10.3 › Hands-on scenario: three-router troubleshooting lab
Scenario steps
  1. Reproduce the issue and capture show outputs that confirm the symptom.
  2. Choose the smallest, safest debug to reveal the cause.
  3. Apply the minimal corrective change and immediately verify results.
  4. Document commands and rollback steps.
Sample CLI snippets
R1# show ip ospf interface Gig0/0
R2# show ip eigrp neighbors
R1# show ip bgp summary
R1# telnet 10.0.0.4 179
Operational notes

Avoid broad clears in production. Use no debug all immediately after capturing output. Notify operations before running debug on critical routers.

Validation

After fixes, confirm adjacency and route tables with show ip ospf neighbor, show ip eigrp topology, and show ip bgp summary/route.
