Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Key Concepts
Systematic Troubleshooting
A methodical, evidence-driven process that replaces trial-and-error.
Methodologies
Top-Down, Bottom-Up, Divide and Conquer, OSI Model — match method to symptom.
Verification
Confirm fixes eliminate the symptom and produce no collateral impact.
Final takeaway

Combining the right methodology with a disciplined process and safe tool use gives you repeatable, verifiable troubleshooting results.
