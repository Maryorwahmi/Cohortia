Hypothesis

Predict and test

A good hypothesis predicts what you will observe if it is true and suggests a focused diagnostic to confirm it.

Example
If show ip route lacks 192.168.100.10, hypothesis: route advertisement failed. Test: check routing neighbors and route redistribution settings.
Mistake to avoid

Applying multiple changes at once prevents knowing which change fixed the issue.

Hypothesis template
  • Observation: what fails and when.
  • Hypothesis: proposed root cause.
  • Prediction: what you will see if hypothesis is true.
  • Test: the exact command or probe to confirm.
Key Takeaway

A single, testable hypothesis reduces wasted effort and produces clear evidence to act on or discard.
