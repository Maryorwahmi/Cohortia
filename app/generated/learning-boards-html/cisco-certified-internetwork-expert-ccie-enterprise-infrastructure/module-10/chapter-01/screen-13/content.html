Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Common Pitfalls & Documentation
Best practices
Pitfall

Changing multiple settings at once makes it impossible to identify which change fixed or broke service.

Change management

Schedule risky changes, notify stakeholders, and have a tested rollback procedure ready.

Debug caution

Limit debug scope, monitor CPU, and avoid prolonged debug on production devices.

Documentation template (short)
  1. Problem statement and scope
  2. Collected evidence (commands and outputs)
  3. Hypotheses and tests
  4. Implemented fix and verification steps
  5. Rollback plan and follow-ups
Key Takeaway

Discipline in execution and documentation prevents repeated outages and shortens future troubleshooting time.
