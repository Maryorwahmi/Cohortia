Module 11 › Chapter 10.10 › Identify & Contain
Identification Checklist
  • Validate the alert and reproduce the symptom if possible.
  • Collect logs, monitoring graphs, and timestamps for initial timeline entries.
  • Estimate impact: services, users, regions, and business priorities.
Example Evidence
Routing table flaps and increasing CPU across multiple routers suggest a loop rather than a single link failure.
Containment Patterns
Isolate
Remove the faulty element from the topology, e.g., shut the problematic interface.
Block or Rate Limit
Use ACLs or policing to stop malicious or runaway traffic temporarily.
Fallback
Switch to a known-good configuration or route while investigating.
Common Mistake

Broadly disabling services without a rollback plan can increase outage impact. Prefer reversible, scoped containment.
