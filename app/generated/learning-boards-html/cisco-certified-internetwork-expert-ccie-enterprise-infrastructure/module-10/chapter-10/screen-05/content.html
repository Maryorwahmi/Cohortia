Module 11 › Chapter 10.10 › Six-Phase Process
Process Steps
Preparation
Playbooks, monitoring, roles, contact lists, diagrams.
Identification
Confirm incident, scope, and initial evidence collection.
Containment
Limit spread while minimizing business impact.
Eradication
Eliminate root cause, deploy fixes, patch or revert.
Recovery
Validate services, monitor closely, and finalize restoration.
Post-Incident
Timeline, RCA, lessons learned, and SMART actions.
Key Takeaway

Use reversible containment steps and contemporaneous notes to make RCA reliable and safe.

Supporting Details
  • Preparation reduces mean time to acknowledge by having ready contacts and playbooks.
  • Containment should balance risk: isolate only what is necessary and document impacts.
  • Post-incident activity turns temporary fixes into permanent system changes.
Common Mistake
Making irreversible changes during containment that complicate recovery.
