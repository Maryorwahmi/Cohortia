Incident Management and Post-Mortem Analysis
Build predictable responses and learnings that reduce future outages.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass

This chapter shows how disciplined incident response and blameless post-mortems move you from firefighting to continuous improvement. Expect clear playbooks, communication patterns, RCA methods and example action items that apply directly to complex routing, switching, and automation environments.

Practical outcome: after an incident you will be able to restore service safely, determine why it happened, and create concrete changes to prevent recurrence.

In this lesson
You will learn a six-phase incident response process, how to perform RCA, and how to run blameless post-mortems with SMART action items.
