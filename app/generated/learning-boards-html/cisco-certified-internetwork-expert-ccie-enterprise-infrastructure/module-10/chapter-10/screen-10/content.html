Post-Mortem Template
Incident Summary
What happened, when, impact and duration in plain language for stakeholders.
Timeline of Events
Chronological sequence with timestamps, actions, and observed effects.
Root Cause & Contributing Factors
RCA output and fishbone findings presented clearly and without blame.
Action Items
List SMART tasks, owners, verification steps, and due dates. Track to closure.
Example SMART Item
Create route-churn alert with threshold 50 updates/min, owner: routing team, verify by generating controlled churn in lab and confirming alert within 2 weeks.
Key Takeaway

Post-mortems must produce tracked, measurable changes. Blameless framing produces honest analysis and better fixes.
