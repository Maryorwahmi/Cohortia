5 Whys — Example Walkthrough

Start with the incident: "A routing loop caused a four-hour outage." Then ask why repeatedly until you reach a systemic cause:

  1. Why did the loop occur? — Incorrect route redistribution.
  2. Why was redistribution incorrect? — No filtering in the change.
  3. Why was filtering missing? — No peer review or template was used.
  4. Why was peer review absent? — Process didn't mandate it for this change type.
  5. Why was the process incomplete? — Change management lacked specific templates and training.
Fishbone Categories
People, Process, Tools, Environment, Equipment — use these to brainstorm contributing factors without stopping at blame.
Actionable Outcome
RCA should produce clear action items: mandate peer review, create templates, add monitoring for route churn, and schedule training.
Key Takeaway

Push analysis beyond the immediate human error to find process or tooling gaps that enabled it.
