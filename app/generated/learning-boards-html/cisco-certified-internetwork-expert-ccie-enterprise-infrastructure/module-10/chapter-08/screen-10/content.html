Multicast failure modes

Check adjacency, RP, mroute, and IGMP snooping in sequence. Many problems are unicast route or RPF related rather than L2 flooding.

PIM adjacency
show ip pim neighbor
RP mapping
show ip pim rp-mapping
Example outputs
show ip pim neighbor
Neighbor 10.0.0.2  upstream -  00:01:12

show ip pim rp-mapping
Group 239.1.1.1 -> RP 10.0.0.1

show ip mroute 239.1.1.1
239.1.1.1/32, flags S, rp 10.0.0.1
  Incoming interface: Null, RPF Check failed
Key Takeaway

Follow the adjacency → RP → mroute sequence. RPF failures usually point to unicast routing problems or asymmetry.
