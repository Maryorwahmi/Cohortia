QoS Evidence

Read match counts and counters before changing queues or policers.

Policing vs Shaping

Policing drops; shaping smooths and delays. Choose based on loss vs latency tolerance.

Multicast Control

PIM adjacency, RP mapping, and mroute IIF/OIL determine forwarding. RPF uses unicast routing for validation.

Summary Takeaway

Work from evidence to action: confirm class matches and DSCP, inspect policing/shaping counters, and align L2 snooping and L3 mroute state to restore service safely.
