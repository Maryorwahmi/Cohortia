WRED and CBWFQ counters

Random-drops indicate WRED activity. Queue-depths and drops per class indicate which flows are impacted during peak load.

Pro Tip

Collect counters during busy windows to make tuning changes based on real conditions rather than instantaneous snapshots.

Example Tuning Options
  • Lower WRED min threshold for less aggressive drops on small queues.
  • Increase CBWFQ bandwidth for a congested critical class instead of enlarging queues.
  • Exclude interactive classes from WRED if their packets should not be dropped.
Key Takeaway

Tune WRED and bandwidth allocations using measured queue behavior; avoid increasing queue depth to hide congestion from higher layers.
