Where QoS problems appear

Classification, marking, policing, shaping, and queuing each produce distinct symptoms. Confirm which stage is acting on traffic before making changes.

Classification
0 matches mean your match statement is wrong.
Policing
Drops if exceed-action is drop; check units and burst.
Shaping
Buffers and queue-limit indicate smoothing and added latency.
Queuing
CBWFQ shows per-class queue depths and priority treatment.
