Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Shaping vs Policing

Comparison

Policing drops or re-marks at the enforcement point. Shaping buffers and smooths outbound traffic but increases delay.

Policing (Hard limit)
Immediate drops on exceed; good for protecting links but can harm media if too aggressive.
Shaping (Buffering)
Buffers bursts and enforces average rate; avoids drops but introduces delay and possible queueing.
Concrete example
Class-map: Video
 police: 5000000 bps, burst 62500
 shape average 5000000
 queue-limit 5000
Key Takeaway

Decide based on tolerance: policing for strict caps, shaping when avoiding loss matters more than minimal added latency.

Common Pitfall

Applying shaping in the input direction; shaping should be applied on egress where the device controls output pacing.
