Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Remember
1. Evidence First

Use show policy-map, show ip mroute, and packet captures to prove the fault.

2. Small, Safe Changes

Avoid open-ended debug and large queue increases; use short debug windows and reversible config steps.

3. L2 ↔ L3 Correlation

Match switch snooping state with router mroute and unicast routing to eliminate RPF and forwarding mismatches.

Final Reflection

Adopt the habit of measured evidence collection and minimal intervention; it reduces downtime and helps you explain the fix to others.
