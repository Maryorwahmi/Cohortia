Troubleshooting QoS and Multicast Issues

This lesson focuses on practical verification steps: how to read policy-map outputs, identify policing and shaping symptoms, and inspect multicast mroute and PIM state. Expect CLI examples, packet-capture checks, and a lab scenario simulating RPF failures.

In this lesson
  • Interpret show policy-map interface outputs and counters.
  • Diagnose policing, shaping, queuing, and WRED behavior.
  • Troubleshoot multicast: PIM, RP, mroute, RPF, and IGMP snooping.
Why it matters

Voice and video are sensitive to marking and queuing. Multicast saves bandwidth but can fail silently when routing or snooping is wrong. Fast, correct diagnostics prevent outages and misconfiguration cascades.
