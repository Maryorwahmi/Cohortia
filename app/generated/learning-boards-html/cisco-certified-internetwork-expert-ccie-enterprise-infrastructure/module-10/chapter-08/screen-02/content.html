Learning Objectives

Skills, checks, and milestones you will practice in the lab.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Verify QoS Classification

Use policy-map counters and packet captures to confirm traffic matches and DSCP marking. This ensures critical flows are identified and treated correctly.

Mnemonic: Match → Mark
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Diagnose Policing and Shaping

Interpret police and shape counters to find misconfigured rates, burst sizes, or direction errors that cause drops or latency.

Mnemonic: Drop vs Delay
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Manage Queuing and WRED

Read queue depths and random-drop counters to tune thresholds and prevent TCP global synchronization.

Mnemonic: Queue → Tune
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Use Show and Debug Safely

Collect needed evidence with targeted debug filters and terminal monitor without overwhelming the device or the NOC console.

Mnemonic: Targeted, Timed
Verified Competency Milestone
