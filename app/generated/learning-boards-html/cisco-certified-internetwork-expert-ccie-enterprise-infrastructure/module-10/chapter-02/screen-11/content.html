Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Operational Safety
Safety checklist
  • Ensure console or out-of-band access before enabling debug.
  • Use most-specific debug commands rather than global debug.
  • Monitor CPU and stop debugging immediately if load spikes.
  • Document timestamps and commands for reproducibility.
Capture and privacy
Run count-limited and host/port-limited captures to reduce exposure of sensitive data and to speed analysis.
Privacy reminder
Packet captures may contain user payloads. Ensure authorization and retention rules are followed before capturing in production.
Common mistake
Running `debug ip packet` to 'see everything' — it often creates noise and risks device performance.
