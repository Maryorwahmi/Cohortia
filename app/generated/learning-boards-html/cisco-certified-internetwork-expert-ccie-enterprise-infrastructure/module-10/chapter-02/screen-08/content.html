Capture example
# tcpdump -i GigabitEthernet0/1 host 172.16.1.100 and port 80 -c 5
listening on GigabitEthernet0/1, link-type EN10MB
10:30:01.123456 IP 192.168.10.5.54321 > 172.16.1.100.80: Flags [S]
10:30:01.123500 IP 172.16.1.100.80 > 192.168.10.5.54321: Flags [S.]
10:30:01.123600 IP 192.168.10.5.54321 > 172.16.1.100.80: Flags [.]
This capture shows the TCP handshake and subsequent pushed data frames, confirming that HTTP traffic reached the server interface.
Wireshark filters
Apply display filters like `ip.addr == 172.16.1.100 and tcp.port == 80` or `http.request` to isolate the conversation of interest.
Analysis checklist
  • Confirm three-way handshake (SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK).
  • Look for retransmissions and out-of-order packets.
  • Inspect TCP flags, sequence numbers, and payload lengths.
  • Correlate timestamps with debug and syslog events.
Common mistake
Capturing without filters produces huge pcaps that slow analysis and often mask the problem signal.
