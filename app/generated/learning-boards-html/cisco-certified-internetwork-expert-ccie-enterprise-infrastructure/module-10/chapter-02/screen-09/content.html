Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Syslog & SNMP
Syslog basics
Syslog messages include a facility and severity; configure a logging host to centralize messages.
logging host 10.10.10.5
logging trap warnings
Central logs let you search for correlated messages across multiple devices during the incident window.
SNMP traps
Traps are unsolicited alerts sent to the SNMP manager when important events occur.
snmp-server host 10.10.10.6 traps public
Combine traps and syslog to build a timeline that reveals when a problem began and which devices were affected.
Tip
Prioritize alerts by severity and configure retention so critical events remain searchable.
Warning
Unsynchronized clocks lead to incorrect correlations; ensure NTP is configured everywhere.
Mistake
Treating all syslog messages equally instead of focusing on critical and error severities during outages.
