Diagnostic sequence
  1. show etherchannel summary — verify bundle state and protocol.
  2. show interfaces Port-channelX switchport — check trunk/native/allowed VLANs.
  3. show interfaces trunk — confirm per-link native and allowed lists.
  4. show vlan brief — ensure VLAN exists on both switches.
  5. show spanning-tree vlan [id] — inspect port roles and BPDU behavior.
Example pattern: bundle shows up, but VLAN missing from Port-channel allowed list — symptom points to trunk allowed VLAN mismatch, not physical link failure.
Quick reference
First compare: show interfaces Port-channelX switchport (both sides)
Then compare: show interfaces trunk (both sides)
Finally: show vlan brief and show spanning-tree vlan [id]
