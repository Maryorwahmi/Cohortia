Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Safe Change Workflow
Steps
  1. Collect: save outputs from both switches for later comparison.
  2. Hypothesize: correlate outputs to identify the likely misconfiguration.
  3. Plan: prepare the minimal, reversible CLI commands and rollback steps.
  4. Apply: make changes during a maintenance window if needed.
  5. Verify: re-run the same show commands and monitor STP and traffic.
Verification checklist
  • show interfaces Port-channelX switchport
  • show interfaces trunk
  • show vlan brief
  • show spanning-tree vlan [id]
  • Confirm host connectivity and monitor for flaps
