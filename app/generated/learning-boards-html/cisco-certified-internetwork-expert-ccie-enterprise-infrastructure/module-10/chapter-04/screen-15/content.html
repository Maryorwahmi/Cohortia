Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Final Reminder
Collect Evidence

Start with show etherchannel summary, show interfaces switchport, show interfaces trunk, show vlan brief, and show spanning-tree vlan X.

Align Configs

Ensure native VLANs, trunk allowed VLANs, and member interface parameters match across both ends.

Verify After Fix

Re-run the original show commands and check spanning-tree state to confirm the network is stable.

Closing Takeaway

Consistent configuration and evidence-led changes are the fastest way to a stable Layer 2 network.
