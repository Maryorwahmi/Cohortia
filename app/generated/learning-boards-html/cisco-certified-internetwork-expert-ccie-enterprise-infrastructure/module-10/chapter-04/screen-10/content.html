Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › EtherChannel Lab
Given

Two switches, Gi0/1 and Gi0/2 bundled to Port-channel1 with LACP. Port-channel shows (SU) but VLAN 10 traffic is failing.

SW1 configuration (provided)
interface range GigabitEthernet0/1 - 2
 channel-group 1 mode active
!
interface Port-channel1
 switchport mode trunk
 switchport trunk allowed vlan 10
Diagnostic commands to run
  • show etherchannel summary — verify bundle and protocol.
  • show interfaces Port-channel1 switchport — confirm trunk and allowed VLANs.
  • show interfaces trunk — compare native VLAN and allowed lists on both switches.
  • show vlan brief — ensure VLAN 10 exists on both switches.
  • show interfaces Gi0/1 etherchannel — inspect member link state and errors.
Key Takeaway

If the Port-channel is up but VLAN traffic fails, look first for allowed VLAN or native VLAN mismatches across the Port-channel on both switches.
