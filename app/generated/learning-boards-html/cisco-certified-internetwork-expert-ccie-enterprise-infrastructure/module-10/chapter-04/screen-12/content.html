Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Common Mistakes
Top mistakes
  • PortFast enabled on trunk ports — leads to loops.
  • Mismatched EtherChannel modes or member parameters — prevents bundle formation.
  • Relying on DTP in production — surprises from negotiation mismatches.
  • Using BPDU Filter improperly — stops STP participation and allows loops.
Mitigation steps
  1. Use configuration templates for port groups and Port-channel members.
  2. Prefer explicit switchport mode trunk in production.
  3. Enable BPDU Guard on PortFast access ports.
  4. Document STP priorities and plan maintenance windows for changes.
