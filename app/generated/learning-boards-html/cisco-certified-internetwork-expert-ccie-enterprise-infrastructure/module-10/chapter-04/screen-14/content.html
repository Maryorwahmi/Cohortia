Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Key Concepts
VLAN & Trunking

Confirm VLAN presence, native VLAN, and allowed lists on both ends to prevent dropped or mis-tagged frames.

Spanning Tree

Root election and port roles control forwarding; mis-elections or BPDU loss cause suboptimal or unstable topology.

EtherChannel

Protocol modes and member interface parameters must match; use show etherchannel outputs to confirm bundle health.

Summary Takeaway

Diagnose with show commands, apply minimal fixes, and always verify STP and channel state to avoid creating larger outages.
