Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › EtherChannel Troubleshooting
Core checks
  • show etherchannel summary — bundle state codes (S, D, U, etc.).
  • show etherchannel port-channel — logical Port-channel details.
  • show interfaces Gi0/1 etherchannel — member link status and flags.
  • Confirm mode compatibility: LACP active/passive or PAgP desirable/auto.
Example configuration (SW1)
interface range GigabitEthernet0/1 - 2
 channel-group 1 mode active
!
interface Port-channel1
 switchport mode trunk
 switchport trunk allowed vlan 10
If SW2’s Port-channel lacks VLAN 10 in its allowed list, VLAN 10 traffic will not pass even though the bundle shows up as (SU).
