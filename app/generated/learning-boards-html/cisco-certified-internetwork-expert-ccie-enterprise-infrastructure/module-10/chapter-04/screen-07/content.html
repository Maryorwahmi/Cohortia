Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › PortFast & BPDU Guards
Feature comparison
PortFast — puts access ports straight to forwarding for end devices; do not use on trunks.
BPDU Guard — disables port when BPDU received; pairs with PortFast to block accidental switches.
BPDU Filter — suppresses BPDUs; dangerous if used on ports that must participate in STP.
Scenario

A helpdesk technician plugs a new switch into a PortFast-enabled port. If BPDU Guard is enabled, the port will be shut down and prevent a loop. If BPDU Guard is not enabled, the new switch may introduce a loop or change STP topology unexpectedly.

Key Takeaway

Use PortFast only for end-hosts and enable BPDU Guard on those ports to prevent accidental topology changes.
