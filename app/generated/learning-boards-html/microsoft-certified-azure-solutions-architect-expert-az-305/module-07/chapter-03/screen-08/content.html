Data Warehouse

Azure Synapse Analytics Dedicated SQL Pools

High-performance data warehousing for structured analytics.

A massively parallel processing (MPP) system for querying petabytes of structured data.

Ideal for modern data warehouses, supporting star and snowflake schemas.

Key architectural decision: data distribution (Hash, Round-Robin, Replicated) impacts query performance.

Ensures efficient processing for business intelligence and reporting workloads.

Distribution Strategies

Hash Distribution

Even spread for large tables on join keys.

Round-Robin

For staging or no join key.

Replicated

For small dimension tables.
