Cost Optimization

Data Lifecycle Management

Optimizing storage costs by managing data over time.

Automate data movement and deletion based on age or access patterns.

Transition data from 'hot' to 'cool' or 'archive' tiers to reduce costs.

Set policies for automatic deletion after retention periods expire.

Essential for managing costs of large analytical datasets.

Storage Tiers

Hot

Frequent access, higher cost

Cool

Infrequent access, lower cost

Archive

Rare access, lowest cost
