Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Power BI

Microsoft's cloud-based business intelligence service for data visualization, reporting, and dashboarding.

Power BI Desktop

The authoring tool for creating Power BI reports and data models on your local machine.

Power BI Service

The online SaaS platform for publishing, sharing, and collaborating on Power BI content.

Summary Takeaway

Power BI provides a comprehensive, integrated ecosystem for transforming raw data into shareable, interactive business insights.
