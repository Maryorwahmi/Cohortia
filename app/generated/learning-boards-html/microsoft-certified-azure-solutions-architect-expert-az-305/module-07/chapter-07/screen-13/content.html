Report Delivery Mechanisms

Choosing how to distribute and consume your Power BI insights

Power BI Service & Embedded

  • •**Power BI Service:** Web-based access for publishing, sharing, and collaboration.
  • •**Power BI Embedded:** Integrate reports directly into custom applications for seamless user experience.

Automated & Real-time Delivery

  • •**Email Subscriptions:** Schedule reports or dashboards to be sent via email.
  • •**Real-time Dashboards:** Connect to Azure Stream Analytics for near-instantaneous updates.
Key Takeaway

Select the appropriate delivery mechanism based on user needs, desired interactivity, and data freshness requirements to maximize the impact of your Power BI solutions.
