Sharding for Extreme Scalability

Partitioning data across independent physical databases

What is Sharding?

Sharding involves partitioning a single logical database into multiple smaller, independent physical databases called 'shards.' Each shard holds a subset of the data.

Purpose & Benefits

It's used for extreme horizontal scalability, especially with write-heavy workloads or massive datasets, distributing data and workload for near-linear growth.

Sharding vs. Other Scaling

Unlike vertical scaling's limits or read replicas' read-only focus, sharding scales both reads and writes by distributing the entire dataset and operations.
