What is a Sharding Key?

The column or set of columns used to determine which shard a row of data belongs to, crucial for data distribution.

Avoiding Hot Spots

An inappropriate sharding key can lead to 'hot spots' where one shard becomes disproportionately busy, negating the benefits of sharding.

Key Takeaway

Careful selection of the sharding key is paramount to ensure even data distribution and prevent performance bottlenecks caused by hot spots.
