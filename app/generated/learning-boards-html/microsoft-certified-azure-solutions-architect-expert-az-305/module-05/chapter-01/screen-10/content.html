Performance, Scalability, and Cost Considerations

Balancing technical needs with economic realities is crucial for long-term success.

Scalability & Performance

Will the database handle massive transactional loads or petabytes of data? Different tiers offer varying capabilities.

Cost & TCO

PaaS often has higher per-hour cost but lower TCO due to reduced operational expenses compared to IaaS.

Key Takeaway

Evaluate performance and scalability needs against the total cost of ownership, recognizing that PaaS often provides better long-term value despite potentially higher hourly rates.

Example Scenario

High-Growth SaaS Application

A Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) application anticipates rapid user growth and unpredictable spikes in demand. Azure SQL Database with the Hyperscale tier is chosen for its ability to scale compute and storage independently and rapidly, ensuring performance under extreme loads, even if the per-hour cost is higher than a fixed IaaS solution.
