Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 2

Understanding recovery metrics and data protection mechanisms.

Read Replicas

Secondary, read-only copies for open-source databases, used for read scaling and potential DR.

Automated Backups

Azure's built-in service for regular backups, stored in geo-redundant storage.

Point-in-Time Restore

Ability to restore a database to any specific moment within the backup retention period.

RTO (Recovery Time Objective)

The maximum acceptable downtime after a disaster.

RPO (Recovery Point Objective)

The maximum acceptable data loss after a disaster.

Summary Takeaway

Automated backups and point-in-time restore are crucial for data integrity, while RTO and RPO define business recovery targets.
