Azure SQL HA

Business Critical Tier & Availability Zones

Achieving superior fault tolerance with replicas and AZs.

Business Critical HA Features

  • •Multiple Isolated Replicas: Uses an Always On Availability Group-like architecture.
  • •Instant Failover: Provides rapid recovery with minimal downtime in case of primary replica failure.
  • •Superior Fault Tolerance: Designed for mission-critical workloads requiring highest availability.
  • •Availability Zone Support: Replicas can be deployed across different physical data centers.
Architectural Decision

Deploying replicas across Availability Zones is crucial for protecting against zone-level failures within a region.
