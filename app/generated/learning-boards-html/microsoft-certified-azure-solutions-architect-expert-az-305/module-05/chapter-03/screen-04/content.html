Core Concept

High Availability vs. Disaster Recovery

Understanding the fundamental differences and their scope.

High Availability (HA)

  • •Focuses on keeping systems operational within a single Azure region.
  • •Protects against localized failures like hardware issues or zone outages.
  • •Aims for minimal downtime and continuous service.
  • •Example: Automatic failover to a standby replica in the same region.
Common Misconception

HA does not protect against an entire region becoming unavailable. It's for intra-region resilience.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

  • •Focuses on recovering from a catastrophic regional outage.
  • •Protects against region-wide failures, ensuring business continuity.
  • •Aims to restore service in a secondary, geographically distant region.
  • •Example: Geo-replication to a database in another Azure region.
Key Takeaway

HA is about resilience within a region; DR is about recovery across regions.
