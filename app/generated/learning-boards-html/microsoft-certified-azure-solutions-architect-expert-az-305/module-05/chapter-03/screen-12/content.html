DR Metrics

Defining RTO and RPO

Aligning HA/DR strategies with recovery objectives.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

  • •Definition: Maximum acceptable downtime after a disaster.
  • •Focus: How quickly systems must be operational again.
  • •Impact: Influences choice of failover mechanisms (e.g., automated vs. manual).
  • •Example: An RTO of 30 minutes means the business can tolerate 30 minutes of downtime.
Key Driver

A lower RTO often requires more complex and costly HA/DR solutions.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

  • •Definition: Maximum acceptable data loss after a disaster.
  • •Focus: How much data (in time) can be lost.
  • •Impact: Influences choice of replication (e.g., synchronous vs. asynchronous).
  • •Example: An RPO of 5 minutes means up to 5 minutes of data loss is acceptable.
Key Driver

A lower RPO often requires synchronous replication or very frequent backups.
