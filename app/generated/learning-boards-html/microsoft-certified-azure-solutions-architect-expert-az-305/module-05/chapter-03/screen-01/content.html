Chapter 5.3

Designing for High Availability and Disaster Recovery in Azure Relational Databases

Ensuring Business Continuity for Mission-Critical Applications

Orientation

This chapter explores how to build resilient database solutions in Azure, differentiating between High Availability (HA) for localized failures and Disaster Recovery (DR) for regional outages. You'll learn to implement strategies that keep your applications running and data protected.

High Availability

Keeps systems running despite localized failures.

Disaster Recovery

Recovers from catastrophic regional outages.
