Avoiding Common HA/DR Pitfalls

Key considerations for robust and tested strategies.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • •Confusing HA with DR: Assuming built-in HA protects against regional outages.
  • •Untested DR Plans: Not regularly validating RTO/RPO objectives through drills.
  • •Static Connection Strings: Hardcoding application connections instead of using failover group listeners.
  • •Ignoring Replication Lag: Not monitoring asynchronous replication lag for RPO impact.
Pro-Tip

Schedule regular DR drills to ensure operational readiness and validate your recovery objectives.
