Service Tier

Hyperscale

A specialized tier for extremely large databases (up to 100 TB) offering highly scalable storage and independent, non-linear scaling of compute and storage resources.

Key Takeaway

The premier choice for handling massive datasets and unpredictable growth, enabling independent scaling of compute and storage.

Consideration

While offering immense scale, it's important to understand its distributed architecture and potential differences in performance tuning compared to traditional tiers.

Characteristics
  • Supports up to 100 TB of data
  • Independent scaling of compute and storage
  • Distributed architecture (compute, page servers, log service)
  • Rapid backup and restore
  • Ideal for large-scale data warehousing and analytics
Example Use Case

A large online gaming platform storing extensive player data, game state, and historical logs, requiring massive storage and flexible scaling.
