Service Tier

General Purpose

A balanced compute and storage service tier suitable for most business workloads, offering a good mix of performance, availability, and cost with configurable storage redundancy.

Key Takeaway

The default choice for many applications, providing reliable performance and cost-effectiveness for typical transactional workloads.

Consideration

While balanced, it may not offer the ultra-low latency or highest transaction rates required by extremely demanding, mission-critical applications.

Characteristics
  • Balanced compute and storage
  • Configurable storage redundancy (LRS/GRS)
  • Suitable for most business applications
  • Good performance for typical I/O and transaction rates
  • Cost-effective
Example Use Case

A customer relationship management (CRM) system or an inventory management application with moderate transaction volumes.
