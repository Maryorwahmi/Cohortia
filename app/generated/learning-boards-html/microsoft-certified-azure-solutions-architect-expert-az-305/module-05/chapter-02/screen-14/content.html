RTO, RPO, and Service Tiers

Aligning your application's Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) with the appropriate Azure SQL Database service tier for optimal availability and durability.

Key Takeaway

Strict RTO/RPO requirements are a primary driver for selecting the Business Critical tier, while less stringent needs may be met by General Purpose or Hyperscale.

Consideration

Always document your RTO/RPO requirements clearly. This documentation will guide your service tier selection and justify the associated costs.

Definitions
  • RTO (Recovery Time Objective): Max acceptable downtime after a failure.
  • RPO (Recovery Point Objective): Max acceptable data loss after a failure.
Service Tier Alignment
  • Business Critical: Lowest RTO/RPO, highest availability, fastest failover.
  • General Purpose: Balanced RTO/RPO, good availability, standard failover.
  • Hyperscale: High availability, focus on massive scale, RTO/RPO considerations for very large databases.
