DTU vs. vCore

Understand the two purchasing models for Azure SQL Database: DTU (Database Transaction Unit) offers a blended performance metric, while vCore provides granular control over CPU, memory, and storage.

Key Takeaway

vCore offers greater transparency and control for architects, while DTU provides a simpler abstraction for basic needs.

Recommendation

For most architectural designs, the vCore model is preferred due to its clarity and alignment with on-premises resource management.

DTU Model
  • Blended measure of CPU, memory, I/O
  • Simplified performance levels (Basic, Standard, Premium)
  • Less transparent, harder to map to on-premises
  • Good for simpler workloads or quick start
vCore Model
  • Explicitly choose vCores, memory, storage
  • More transparent and predictable
  • Aligns with on-premises resource sizing
  • Preferred for architects and complex workloads
