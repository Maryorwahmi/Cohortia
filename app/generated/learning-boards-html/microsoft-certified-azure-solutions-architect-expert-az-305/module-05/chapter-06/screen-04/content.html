Core Concept

Why Migrate to Azure?

Migrating relational databases to Azure offers significant benefits like scalability, cost-efficiency, and enhanced security. However, it introduces complexities that demand careful planning.

  • •Minimize Downtime: Crucial for business continuity.
  • •Ensure Data Integrity: Prevent loss or corruption during transfer.
  • •Optimize Performance: Leverage Azure's infrastructure for better speed.
  • •Reduce Operational Costs: Shift from CapEx to OpEx.
Common Mistake

Underestimating the complexity of application changes post-migration, leading to unexpected issues.

Key Takeaway

A well-designed migration plan is essential for a successful, low-risk transition to Azure, focusing on business continuity and data integrity.
