Process Flow

Steps in an Online Migration

1. Source Online

Applications continue writes.

2. Replicate Data

Continuous sync to Azure target.

3. Cutover

Switch application traffic.

Benefits
  • •Minimal to near-zero downtime.
  • •Ensures business continuity for critical apps.
  • •Users experience no interruption.
Drawbacks
  • •Higher complexity in setup and management.
  • •Requires robust network connectivity.
  • •Potential for synchronization issues if not monitored.
Use Case Example

Migrating a global financial trading platform where even seconds of downtime can lead to millions in losses, requiring continuous operation.

Key Takeaway

Online migration is crucial for high-availability applications, leveraging continuous data synchronization to achieve near-zero downtime, despite its increased complexity.
