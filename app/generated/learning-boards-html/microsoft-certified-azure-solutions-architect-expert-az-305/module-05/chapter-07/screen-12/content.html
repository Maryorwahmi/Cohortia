Leveraging Azure Advisor for Optimization

Personalized, actionable recommendations across five pillars.

Azure Advisor Pillars

  • •Cost: Recommendations to reduce your overall Azure spend.
  • •Security: Suggestions to improve the security posture of your resources.
  • •Reliability: Recommendations to ensure business continuity and high availability.
  • •Operational Excellence: Guidance for process and workflow efficiency.
  • •Performance: Advice to improve the speed and responsiveness of your applications.

Example Recommendation

Azure Advisor identifies an Azure SQL Database that has been consistently underutilized for the past 30 days. It recommends scaling down the database to a lower service tier or considering a serverless option, projecting significant monthly cost savings.

Pro-Tip: Regular Review

Integrate regular review of Azure Advisor recommendations into your operational processes. Prioritize and act on high-impact suggestions to continuously optimize your environment.

Key Takeaway

Azure Advisor is a powerful, personalized tool that provides actionable recommendations across five pillars to continuously optimize your Azure relational database solutions.
