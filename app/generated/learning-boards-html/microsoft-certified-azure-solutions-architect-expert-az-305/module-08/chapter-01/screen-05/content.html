High Availability: Preventing Downtime

Minimizing interruptions within a single operational environment.

HA Definition

High Availability focuses on preventing downtime within a single operational environment, minimizing service interruptions by eliminating single points of failure.

Key Goal

Achieve continuous operation, often measured in 'nines' of availability (e.g., 99.99% uptime).

HA Implementation Examples

Availability Zones

Deploying VMs across physically separate locations within an Azure region for fault isolation.

Azure Load Balancer

Distributing incoming traffic across multiple healthy instances of an application.

Database Replicas

Configuring secondary copies of databases for automatic failover within a region.

Key Takeaway

HA is about preventing localized outages and ensuring continuous service within a single operational footprint.
