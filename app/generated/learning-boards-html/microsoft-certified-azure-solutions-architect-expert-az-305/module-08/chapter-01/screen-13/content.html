Architecting for Resilience: Key Safety Notes

Implementing isolation, documentation, and regular testing.

Design with Blast Radius in Mind

Limit the impact of a failure by isolating critical components. Deploying services into separate resource groups or subscriptions can contain failures.

Document BCDR Plans Thoroughly

Comprehensive documentation, including recovery procedures, contact lists, and runbooks, is essential for effective response during a crisis.

Regularly Review and Test Plans

A BCDR plan is only effective if it's validated. Conduct realistic tests regularly to identify and address any gaps or issues before a real disaster occurs.

Key Takeaway

Proactive design for isolation, meticulous documentation, and continuous testing are non-negotiable for building truly resilient Azure architectures.
