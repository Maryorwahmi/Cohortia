Applying RTO and RPO to Business Services

Aligning technical solutions with business tolerance for downtime and data loss.

Shopping Cart Service

  • •RTO: 15 minutes
  • •RPO: 5 minutes
  • •Solution: Active-passive deployment with near real-time data replication (e.g., Azure Site Recovery, database failover groups).

Product Catalog Service

  • •RTO: 2 hours
  • •RPO: 30 minutes
  • •Solution: Regular backups with geo-redundant storage, potentially warm standby VMs (e.g., Azure Backup, Geo-redundant storage).

Order Processing Backend

  • •RTO: 30 minutes
  • •RPO: 10 minutes
  • •Solution: Active-passive with frequent replication, potentially using Availability Zones for HA within the primary region (e.g., Azure SQL Database Geo-replication).
Key Takeaway

Tailoring BCDR solutions to specific service RTO/RPO requirements optimizes resilience and resource allocation, avoiding over-engineering or under-protecting.
