Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Specifying the maximum acceptable data loss during a disaster.

Definition

RPO: The maximum acceptable amount of data that can be lost from an application or service due to a major incident.

Data Freshness

Determines how frequently data must be replicated or backed up to meet the business's tolerance for data loss.

RPO Examples by Criticality

Zero Data Loss (0 minutes)

Requires synchronous replication, often for critical financial transactions or real-time data.

Low Data Loss (Minutes)

Asynchronous replication, suitable for e-commerce orders or frequently updated user profiles.

Moderate Data Loss (Hours/Days)

Regular backups, acceptable for archival data or less frequently updated content.

Key Takeaway

RPO defines the maximum tolerable data loss, driving the choice of data replication and backup strategies to ensure data integrity post-disaster.
