Key Concepts: Foundational BCDR Terms

Revisiting the essential definitions for resilience.

High Availability (HA)

Ensures continuous operation and minimizes downtime within a single environment by eliminating single points of failure.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Focuses on restoring operations after a catastrophic event affecting an entire region, typically involving failover to a geographically separate location.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum acceptable duration of time that an application or service can be down after a disaster.

Summary Takeaway

HA, DR, and RTO are fundamental pillars of BCDR, defining strategies for continuous operation, catastrophic recovery, and acceptable downtime.
