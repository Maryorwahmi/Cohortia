Key Concepts: Advanced BCDR Elements

Consolidating your understanding of critical BCDR metrics and services.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum acceptable amount of data that can be lost from an application or service due to a major incident.

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A commitment from a service provider (like Azure) regarding the uptime, performance, and responsiveness of its services.

Availability Zone (AZ)

Physically separate locations within an Azure region, each with independent power, cooling, and networking, designed for fault isolation.

Summary Takeaway

RPO, SLA, and Availability Zones are crucial for quantifying data loss tolerance, understanding service commitments, and building fault-isolated, highly available systems.
