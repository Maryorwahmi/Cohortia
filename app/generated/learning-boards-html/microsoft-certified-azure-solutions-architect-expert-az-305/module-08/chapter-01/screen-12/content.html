Common BCDR Planning Mistakes

Recognizing and mitigating common errors in resilience design.

Underestimating Failover Complexity

Failover is not a simple switch; it involves intricate orchestration of services, network changes, and application dependencies. Neglecting this complexity can lead to failed recovery attempts.

Neglecting Regular DR Testing

A BCDR plan is only as good as its last test. Untested plans often fail in real-world scenarios due to overlooked configurations, dependencies, or procedural gaps.

Failing to Account for Data Consistency

Simply replicating infrastructure without ensuring application-level data synchronization can lead to corrupted or inconsistent data upon failover, making recovery ineffective.

Ignoring Application Dependencies

Focusing only on infrastructure recovery without a clear understanding of application dependencies and startup order can result in a recovered environment that cannot run the business application.

Key Takeaway

Proactive planning, thorough testing, and a holistic view of data and application dependencies are crucial to avoid common BCDR pitfalls.
