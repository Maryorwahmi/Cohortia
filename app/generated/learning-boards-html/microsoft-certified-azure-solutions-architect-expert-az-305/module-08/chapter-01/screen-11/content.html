Important Note

Azure's SLA applies to the underlying infrastructure. Your application's effective SLA depends on your architectural choices for HA and DR.

Key Takeaway

Azure SLAs are foundational for BCDR, providing a baseline for infrastructure availability that must be augmented by application-level resilience strategies.
