The Essence of Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

Ensuring operational resilience in the face of disruption.

Definition

Business Continuity (BC): The capability of an organization to continue delivery of products or services at acceptable predefined levels following a disruptive incident.

Definition

Disaster Recovery (DR): A set of policies, tools, and procedures to enable the recovery or continuation of vital technology infrastructure and systems following a natural or human-induced disaster.

Why BCDR Matters

Minimize Downtime

Reduce service interruptions and maintain accessibility for users.

Protect Data Integrity

Safeguard against data loss and corruption during incidents.

Ensure Compliance

Meet regulatory and industry standards for data protection and service availability.

Key Takeaway

BCDR is a strategic imperative, not just a technical task, ensuring business resilience and protecting critical assets.
