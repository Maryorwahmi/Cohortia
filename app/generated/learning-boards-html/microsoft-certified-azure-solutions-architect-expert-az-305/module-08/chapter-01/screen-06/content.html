Disaster Recovery: Recovering from Catastrophe

Restoring operations after large-scale, catastrophic events.

DR Definition

Disaster Recovery deals with recovering from large-scale, catastrophic events that impact an entire region or significant infrastructure, involving failover to a geographically separate location.

Key Goal

Restore business operations within acceptable timeframes, even if it means temporary data loss.

DR Scenario Examples

Natural Disasters

Floods, earthquakes, or severe weather impacting an entire Azure region.

Widespread Outages

Major power grid failures or network disruptions affecting a large geographical area.

Major Cyberattacks

Security breaches that compromise an entire data center or region's infrastructure.

Key Takeaway

DR is about cross-regional recovery from catastrophic events, ensuring business continuity even with some data loss.
