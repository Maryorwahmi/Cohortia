Essential Takeaways

Core Concepts for Resilient Storage

Locally Redundant Storage (LRS)

Data replicated three times within a single data center in the primary region, offering lowest cost protection against local hardware failures.

Zone-Redundant Storage (ZRS)

Data replicated synchronously across three Availability Zones within the primary region, providing zero data loss and high availability.

Geo-Redundant Storage (GRS)

Data replicated locally and then asynchronously to a paired secondary region, protecting against regional disasters with a non-zero RPO.

Read-Access GRS (RA-GRS)

GRS with read-only access to the secondary region, even when the primary is active, enhancing read availability and disaster recovery.

Summary Takeaway

Choosing the right Azure Storage redundancy option is a strategic decision that directly impacts your application's resilience, cost, and ability to meet RPO/RTO objectives.
