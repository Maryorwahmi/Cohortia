The Foundation of Data Resilience

Understanding Azure Storage Redundancy Options

Azure Storage provides various redundancy options to ensure data durability and availability, crucial for any robust BCDR strategy. These options allow you to choose the right level of protection based on:

  • •Data criticality and importance
  • •Recovery Point Objective (RPO) requirements
  • •Recovery Time Objective (RTO) requirements
  • •Overall cost considerations
Key Takeaway

Selecting the appropriate redundancy option is fundamental to designing resilient storage solutions that meet specific business needs and compliance requirements.

Azure Storage Redundancy Spectrum

LRS

Local protection

ZRS

Zone protection

GRS

RA-GRS

Each option offers a different balance of durability, availability, and cost.
