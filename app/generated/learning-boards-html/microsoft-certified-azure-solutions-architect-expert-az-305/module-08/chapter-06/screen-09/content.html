Selecting the Optimal Redundancy

Aligning with RPO, RTO, and Cost Requirements

Key Decision Factors

  • •Data Criticality: How vital is the data to business operations?
  • •RPO (Recovery Point Objective): Maximum acceptable data loss (e.g., minutes, zero).
  • •RTO (Recovery Time Objective): Maximum tolerable downtime (e.g., minutes, hours).
  • •Cost: Budget constraints and cost-effectiveness of the solution.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • •Using LRS for critical data requiring regional DR, leading to significant data loss.
  • •Not understanding the difference between GRS (entire account) and Object Replication (granular blobs).
  • •Over-provisioning redundancy for non-critical data, incurring unnecessary costs.
  • •Neglecting strong access controls (RBAC) on storage accounts.
Key Takeaway

Aligning your storage redundancy choice with RPO, RTO, and cost is paramount. A mismatch can lead to either insufficient protection or unnecessary expenses.
