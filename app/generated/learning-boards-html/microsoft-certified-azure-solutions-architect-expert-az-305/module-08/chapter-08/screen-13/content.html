Post-Recovery Steps and DR Best Practices

Ensuring application accessibility and data security.

Effective AKS DR extends to post-recovery actions, continuous testing, and robust security measures to ensure business continuity.

Key Takeaway

Regularly test your DR plan, secure your backups, and understand RPO/RTO to minimize impact during a real disaster.

Pro Tip

Implement immutable storage policies on your Velero backup storage account to prevent accidental or malicious deletion of backups.

Critical Considerations
DNS Updates

Update DNS records (Azure DNS, Traffic Manager, Front Door) to point to new application endpoints in the DR region.

Common Mistakes

Not regularly testing DR procedures, assuming Velero handles everything, or neglecting RPO/RTO considerations.

Safety & Security

Ensure encrypted backups, strong RBAC on storage accounts, and immutable storage policies to prevent data tampering.
