Key Concepts

Advanced ASR terminology

Recovery Plan

An orchestrated sequence of steps that automates the failover process for a group of VMs, including startup order and custom scripts.

Test Failover

A non-disruptive operation in ASR that creates copies of replicated VMs in an isolated network in the target region for DR drills.

Failover Operations

Includes Planned Failover (controlled migration) and Unplanned Failover (immediate disaster recovery), followed by a commit operation.

Summary Takeaway

Recovery Plans, Test Failovers, and understanding different Failover Operations are crucial for executing and validating your DR strategy effectively.
