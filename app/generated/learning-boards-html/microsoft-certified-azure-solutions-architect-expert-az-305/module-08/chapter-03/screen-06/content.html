ASR Components

On-Premises Integration with ASR

Bridging on-premises workloads to Azure for robust disaster recovery.

Key On-Premises Components

  • •Configuration Server: Central management for VMware/physical servers.
  • •Process Server: Handles data caching, compression, and encryption.
  • •Hyper-V Host: Coordinates replication for Hyper-V VMs directly.
  • •Enables replication of on-premises VMs and physical servers to Azure.
Key Takeaway

ASR extends robust DR capabilities to on-premises environments through specialized servers that manage and optimize data replication to Azure.
