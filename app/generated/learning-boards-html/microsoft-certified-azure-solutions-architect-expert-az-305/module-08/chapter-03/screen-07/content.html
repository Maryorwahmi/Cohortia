Design Steps

Setting Up Your ASR Environment

Creating the Recovery Services Vault and defining your target DR region.

Step-by-Step: Initial ASR Configuration

1

Choose Target DR Region

Select a secondary Azure region geographically distant from your primary region to host your recovered workloads.

2

Create Recovery Services Vault

Provision a new Recovery Services Vault in the chosen target DR region. This vault will manage all replication and recovery operations.

3

Prepare Network Configuration

Ensure target virtual networks, subnets, and network security groups are ready in the DR region for recovered VMs.

Key Takeaway

The Recovery Services Vault must be created in the target DR region to ensure its availability during a primary region outage.

Why Target Region Placement?

Placing the Recovery Services Vault in the target DR region is a critical design decision. If the primary region goes down, the vault in the secondary region remains accessible, allowing you to initiate failovers and manage your recovery process without interruption. This ensures the control plane for your DR strategy is always available.

Common Mistake: Vault Location

A frequent error is creating the Recovery Services Vault in the same region as the primary workloads. In the event of a region-wide disaster, this would make the vault, and thus your ASR configuration, inaccessible, preventing you from initiating recovery operations. Always verify the vault's location.
