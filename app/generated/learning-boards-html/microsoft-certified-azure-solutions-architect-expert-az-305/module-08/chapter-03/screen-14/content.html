Key Concepts

Essential terms for Azure Site Recovery

Azure Site Recovery (ASR)

An Azure service that orchestrates replication, failover, and recovery of workloads to another Azure region or on-premises site.

Recovery Services Vault

A storage entity in Azure that houses data for Azure Backup and Azure Site Recovery, including replication policies and recovery points.

Replication Policy

Defines the frequency of recovery point creation, retention duration, and multi-VM consistency settings for ASR.

Summary Takeaway

ASR, its central vault, and carefully defined replication policies form the foundational pillars of a robust disaster recovery strategy.
