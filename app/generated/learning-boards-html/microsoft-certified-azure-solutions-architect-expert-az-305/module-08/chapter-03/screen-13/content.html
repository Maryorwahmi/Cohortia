Avoiding Common ASR Pitfalls

Ensuring a robust and secure disaster recovery strategy for your Azure workloads.

Key Challenges & Solutions

!

Network Configuration

Ensure target VNETs, NSGs, and DNS are correctly set up in the DR region.

!

Untested Recovery Plans

Regularly perform test failovers to validate functionality and measure RTO.

!

Capacity & Licensing

Verify sufficient capacity in the DR region and address licensing implications for on-premises servers.

Key Takeaway

A robust ASR strategy requires meticulous planning, continuous validation through testing, and careful consideration of network, capacity, and security aspects.

Pro-Tip: Automate Network Updates

Integrate Azure Automation runbooks into your Recovery Plans to automatically update DNS records or reconfigure load balancers post-failover. This significantly reduces manual intervention and speeds up the RTO for network-dependent applications.

Security Best Practice: Vault Protection

The Recovery Services Vault is a critical asset. Ensure it's protected with strong Azure RBAC policies, granting only necessary permissions. Consider applying resource locks, especially 'CanNotDelete' locks, to prevent accidental deletion of the vault, which would cripple your DR capabilities.
