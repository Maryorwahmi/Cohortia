ASR Components

The Recovery Services Vault

Centralizing replication metadata and policies for disaster recovery.

Key Functions of the Vault

  • •Stores replication metadata for all protected items.
  • •Houses recovery points (snapshots) for point-in-time recovery.
  • •Defines and manages replication policies (RPO, retention).
  • •Orchestrates replication for Azure VMs natively.
  • •Central management point for recovery plans.
Key Takeaway

The Recovery Services Vault is the indispensable control plane for ASR, managing all aspects of data replication and recovery orchestration.

Vault in Action: An Example

When you enable replication for an Azure VM, the Recovery Services Vault records the VM's configuration, tracks its replication health, and stores the recovery points generated every few minutes. If a disaster strikes, you'd use this vault to initiate a failover, selecting a specific recovery point to restore your VM to.

Safety Note: Protecting Your Vault

Given its critical role, securing your Recovery Services Vault is paramount. Implement Azure Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to restrict who can manage the vault. Consider applying immutable locks to prevent accidental deletion or modification, as losing the vault could severely impact your DR capabilities.
