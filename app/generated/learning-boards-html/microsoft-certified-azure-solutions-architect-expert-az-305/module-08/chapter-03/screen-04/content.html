Core Concept

Azure Site Recovery: Your DR Cornerstone

Protecting critical workloads against region-wide outages.

What is Azure Site Recovery (ASR)?

  • •Azure's native disaster recovery service for region-wide protection.
  • •Enables replication, failover, and recovery of diverse workloads.
  • •Supports Azure VMs, on-premises VMware/Hyper-V VMs, and physical servers.
  • •Crucial for achieving low Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO).
Key Takeaway

ASR provides comprehensive, orchestrated disaster recovery, ensuring business continuity and minimal disruption for critical applications.

Why ASR Matters: Real-World Scenario

Imagine a critical e-commerce platform hosted in a single Azure region. A major power outage affects the entire region, rendering all services unavailable. Without ASR, the business faces prolonged downtime, significant financial losses, and reputational damage.

With ASR, the platform's VMs are continuously replicated to a secondary region. In a disaster, a pre-configured recovery plan automatically brings up the application in the healthy region, minimizing downtime and ensuring customers can continue shopping.

Common Misconception

Some believe Availability Zones (AZs) are sufficient for disaster recovery. While AZs provide high availability within a region by protecting against datacenter failures, they do not protect against region-wide outages.

ASR is essential for cross-region disaster recovery, offering a higher level of resilience against broader catastrophic events.
