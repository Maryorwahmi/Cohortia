Validation

Performing Disaster Recovery Drills

Validating your recovery strategy with test failovers.

The Test Failover Process

  • •Non-Disruptive: Creates copies of VMs in an isolated network.
  • •Validation: Allows testing of recovery plans and application functionality.
  • •RTO Measurement: Helps accurately measure the actual Recovery Time Objective.
  • •Cleanup: Test resources are removed without affecting production.
Key Takeaway

Regular test failovers are indispensable for verifying your DR strategy, identifying issues, and building confidence in your ability to recover.

Why an Isolated Network?

The use of an isolated network during a test failover is crucial. It prevents any network conflicts or interference with your production environment. This means you can bring up your test VMs, configure them, and run full application tests without any risk of impacting your live services or causing data inconsistencies.

Untested Plan: A Gamble

Relying on an untested recovery plan is a significant risk. Without regular drills, you won't know if your VMs will start correctly, if your scripts will run, or if your applications will function post-failover. An untested plan can lead to extended downtime and failed recovery during an actual disaster.
