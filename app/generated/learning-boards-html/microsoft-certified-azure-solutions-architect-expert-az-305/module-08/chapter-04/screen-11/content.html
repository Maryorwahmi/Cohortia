Restore & Management

Executing Restore Operations and Centralized Management

Ensuring Data Integrity with Backup Center

Flexible Restore Options

  • VMs: Restore entire VMs, specific disks, or individual files/folders.
  • SQL DBs: Point-in-time restore to new or existing databases.
  • Ensures granular control over recovery.
Critical Practice

Regularly test restore procedures to validate RTO and data integrity.

Backup Center

  • Unified management experience in Azure.
  • Monitor, operate, and govern backups across your entire estate.
  • Consolidated view of backup health, compliance, and alerts.
Benefit

Simplifies enterprise-wide backup management and governance.

Key Takeaway

Successful restore operations are paramount, and Backup Center provides the centralized visibility and control needed for effective backup governance.
