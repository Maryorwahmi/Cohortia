Crafting Effective Backup Policies: Frequency & Retention

Meeting RPO and Compliance Requirements

Backup Frequency

  • Defines how often backups are taken (e.g., daily, weekly, hourly).
  • Directly impacts your Recovery Point Objective (RPO).
  • More frequent backups mean lower RPO (less data loss).
Example

For a 24-hour RPO, a daily backup is sufficient. For a 1-hour RPO, more frequent backups are needed.

Retention Range

  • Specifies how long backups are kept (e.g., 30 days, 7 years).
  • Crucial for meeting compliance and regulatory requirements.
  • Often involves tiering for cost optimization.
Compliance Note

Regulatory mandates often dictate long-term retention periods, requiring careful policy design.

Key Takeaway

Effective backup policies balance backup frequency with retention needs to meet RPO and compliance, forming the foundation of a resilient data strategy.
