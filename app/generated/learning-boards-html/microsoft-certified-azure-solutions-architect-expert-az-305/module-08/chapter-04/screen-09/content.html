Optimizing Retention with Backup Tiers

Balancing Cost and Restore Speed

Operational Tier

Stores recent backups for faster restore. Ideal for immediate recovery needs, offering quick access at a higher cost.

Benefit

Rapid recovery from recent data loss events.

Vault-Archive Tier

Stores older backups for cost-effective long-term retention. Suitable for compliance, with longer restore times.

Consideration

Lower cost, but data rehydration is required before access, increasing RTO.

Key Takeaway

Strategic use of retention tiers allows architects to balance cost efficiency with recovery speed, aligning with both operational and compliance needs.
