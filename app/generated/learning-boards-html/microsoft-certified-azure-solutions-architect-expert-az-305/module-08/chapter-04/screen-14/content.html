Key Concepts: Part 2

Critical Definitions for Architects

RPO (Recovery Point Objective)

The maximum acceptable amount of data loss, directly influenced by backup frequency.

Retention Tiers

Different storage tiers (operational, vault-standard, vault-archive) for backups, offering trade-offs between cost and restore speed.

Soft Delete

A feature for Recovery Services Vaults that retains deleted backup items for a specified period, allowing recovery from accidental deletion.

Backup Center

A unified management console in Azure for monitoring and managing backup operations across the enterprise.

RTO (Recovery Time Objective)

The maximum acceptable duration of time within which a business process must be restored after a disaster.

Test Restore

Regularly performing restore operations to verify data integrity and validate RTO, ensuring recovery readiness.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts are vital for designing resilient, cost-effective, and secure data protection solutions in Azure.
