Azure Backup Best Practices: Cost, Security, and Testing

Avoiding Common Pitfalls and Ensuring Resilience

Cost Optimization

  • Evaluate retention requirements carefully.
  • Leverage archive tiers for long-term, cost-effective storage.
  • Avoid over-retaining data in expensive tiers.
Pro-Tip

Regularly review your backup reports to identify opportunities for tiering and cost savings.

Enhanced Security

  • Ensure VMs use disk encryption for an extra layer of protection.
  • Protect Recovery Services Vault with strong RBAC.
  • Enable soft delete for Recovery Services Vaults to prevent accidental/malicious deletion.
Safety Note

Soft delete provides a grace period to recover deleted backups, a critical safety net.

Regular Restore Testing

  • Crucial for verifying data integrity and validating RTO.
  • Untested backups cannot guarantee recovery.
  • Integrate testing into your operational procedures.
Why it Matters

Ensures your recovery strategy works when a real disaster strikes, building confidence in your BCDR plan.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive Azure Backup strategy integrates cost optimization, robust security measures, and diligent restore testing to ensure maximum data resilience.
