Choosing Between Traffic Manager and Front Door

Aligning service capabilities with application requirements.

Traffic Manager Strengths

Simpler, DNS-based, ideal for non-HTTP/S workloads or external endpoints. Higher failover latency due to DNS propagation.

Front Door Strengths

Layer 7, advanced routing, WAF, CDN, SSL offloading, faster failover, global network acceleration. Ideal for modern web apps and APIs.

Decision Factors

Key Considerations

  • Workload Type: HTTP/S vs. non-HTTP/S.
  • Failover Latency (RTO): Seconds vs. minutes.
  • Security Needs: WAF, DDoS protection.
  • Performance: Global acceleration, CDN.
  • Complexity & Cost: Front Door is more advanced and potentially pricier.
Key Takeaway

The choice between Traffic Manager and Front Door hinges on your application's specific requirements for protocol, RTO, security, performance, and budget.
