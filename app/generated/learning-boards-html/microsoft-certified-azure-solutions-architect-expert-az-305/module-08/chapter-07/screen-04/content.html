Core Concept

Global Traffic Management for Application Resiliency

Beyond infrastructure: ensuring application accessibility and performance.

Holistic Resiliency: Infrastructure HA/DR is foundational, but application-level traffic management ensures user access and performance.

Azure Services: Azure Traffic Manager and Azure Front Door are key for global load balancing and failover.

Layer Differences: These services operate at different OSI layers, impacting their capabilities and failover characteristics.

Key Takeaway

Effective global traffic management is essential for ensuring continuous application accessibility and optimal performance, complementing traditional HA/DR strategies.

Scenario Card

Global E-commerce Platform

An e-commerce site serves customers worldwide. If its primary region experiences an outage, global traffic management ensures users are seamlessly redirected to a healthy secondary region, preventing lost sales and maintaining customer trust. Without it, even a perfectly replicated backend would be unreachable.

Common Misconception

HA/DR is Enough

Many believe that simply having highly available infrastructure or data replication is sufficient for application resiliency. However, without a global traffic management strategy, users might still be directed to an unhealthy region or experience high latency, negating the benefits of backend HA/DR.

Pro-Tip Card

Start with Requirements

Before choosing between Traffic Manager and Front Door, clearly define your application's requirements for latency, failover speed (RTO), security (WAF), and content delivery. This will guide you to the most appropriate service.
