Avoiding Common HA Design Pitfalls

Ensuring Robustness and Security in Database Solutions

1. Neglecting Network Latency

High latency between regions for synchronous replication can severely impact performance. Design with asynchronous replication for cross-region scenarios where latency is a concern.

2. Not Testing Failover Scenarios

Untested failover can lead to unexpected behavior or extended downtime during a real disaster. Regularly practice failovers and validate application connectivity.

3. Inadequate Access Controls

Databases hold sensitive data. Implement strong RBAC, Network Security Groups, and Private Endpoints to secure your database servers from unauthorized access.

4. Relying Solely on HA for Data Protection

HA protects against outages, but not logical corruption or accidental deletion. Always configure independent database backups to protect against these scenarios.

Key Takeaway

Proactive planning, rigorous testing, and comprehensive security measures are crucial to avoid common pitfalls and ensure the long-term robustness of your highly available database solutions.
