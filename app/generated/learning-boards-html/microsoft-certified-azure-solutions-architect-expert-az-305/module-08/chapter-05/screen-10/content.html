Consistency Trade-offs

Cosmos DB Consistency Models

Balancing Consistency, Availability, and Latency

Strong Consistency

Reads always return the most recent committed data. Highest consistency, but higher latency for writes.

High Consistency | Low Availability/Latency

Bounded Staleness

Reads lag behind writes by a maximum time or number of versions. Good balance for many apps.

Balanced Consistency | Balanced Availability/Latency

Session Consistency

Guarantees monotonic reads, monotonic writes, read-your-own-writes, and write-follows-reads within a session. Common for user-centric apps.

Good Consistency | High Availability/Latency

Consistent Prefix

Reads return a prefix of the writes, meaning you won't see out-of-order writes. Weaker than Session, stronger than Eventual.

Moderate Consistency | High Availability/Latency

Eventual Consistency

No order guarantee for reads. Highest availability and lowest latency, but data can be inconsistent temporarily.

Low Consistency | Highest Availability/Latency
Key Takeaway

Selecting the appropriate consistency model is crucial for optimizing Cosmos DB's HA and performance, balancing strict data integrity with global responsiveness.
