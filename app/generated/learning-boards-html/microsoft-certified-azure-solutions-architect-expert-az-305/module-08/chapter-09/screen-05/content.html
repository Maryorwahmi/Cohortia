BCDR Plan: Business Impact & Risk

Understanding critical functions and potential threats

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identifies critical business functions, their dependencies, and the financial/reputational impact of their unavailability. This drives RTO and RPO targets.

  • •Maps critical processes to IT systems.
  • •Determines maximum tolerable downtime (RTO).
  • •Determines maximum tolerable data loss (RPO).

Risk Assessment

Identifies potential threats (e.g., natural disasters, cyberattacks, human error) and evaluates their likelihood and potential impact on business operations.

  • •Categorizes threats by type and severity.
  • •Assesses probability of each threat occurring.
  • •Informs mitigation strategies and recovery priorities.
Key Takeaway

BIA and Risk Assessment are foundational, providing the data-driven insights needed to prioritize and design effective BCDR strategies that meet specific business needs.
