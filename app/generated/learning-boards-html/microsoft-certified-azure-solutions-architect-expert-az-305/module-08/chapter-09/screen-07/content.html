BCDR Plan: Communication & Runbooks

Informing stakeholders and operationalizing recovery steps

Communication Plan

Defines how stakeholders (employees, customers, media) will be informed during an incident, ensuring transparency and managing expectations.

  • •Pre-approved messages and channels.
  • •Internal and external communication strategies.
  • •Maintains trust and reduces panic.

BCDR Runbooks

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for executing the recovery strategy, guiding the recovery team through failover and failback processes.

  • •Operational backbone of the BCDR plan.
  • •Ensures systematic and efficient recovery.
  • •Reduces human error under pressure.
Key Takeaway

A robust BCDR plan combines a clear communication strategy with detailed runbooks to ensure both informed stakeholders and effective operational recovery during a crisis.
