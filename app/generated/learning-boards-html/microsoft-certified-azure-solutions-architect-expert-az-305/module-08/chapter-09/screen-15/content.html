Key Concepts: Validation & Improvement

Ensuring and evolving BCDR effectiveness

Disaster Recovery Drill

A simulated disaster event to test and validate the effectiveness of the BCDR plan and runbooks, crucial for readiness.

Actual RTO/RPO

The measured time to recover and data loss during a real or simulated disaster, used to assess plan performance against targets.

Continuous Improvement

The ongoing process of reviewing, updating, and enhancing the BCDR strategy based on testing, lessons learned, and evolving requirements.

Summary Takeaway

Effective BCDR relies on rigorous testing, objective measurement of recovery performance, and a commitment to continuous adaptation and enhancement.
