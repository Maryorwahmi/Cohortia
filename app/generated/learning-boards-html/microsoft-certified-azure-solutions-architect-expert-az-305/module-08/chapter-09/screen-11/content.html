BCDR Metrics and Reporting

Demonstrating effectiveness and identifying improvements

Key BCDR Metrics

  • •Actual RTO/RPO: Time taken and data lost during recovery.
  • •Successful DR Drills: Number of tests completed without major issues.
  • •Time to Detect: How quickly an incident is identified.
  • •Cost of BCDR: Investment in resilient infrastructure and services.
  • •Compliance Adherence: Meeting regulatory requirements.
Why Reporting Matters

Reporting provides objective evidence of BCDR effectiveness to business leadership, justifies investments, and highlights areas needing improvement. It transforms BCDR from a technical exercise into a business-critical function.

Example

If your actual RTO is consistently 4 hours but the business requires 1 hour, this metric clearly indicates a need to optimize recovery procedures or re-evaluate the strategy.

Key Takeaway

Robust BCDR metrics and reporting are vital for demonstrating value, ensuring accountability, and driving continuous improvement in your organization's resilience posture.
