Continuous Improvement in BCDR

BCDR is not a one-time project, but an evolving process

Key Aspects of Continuous Improvement

  • •Regularly review RTO/RPO targets with business stakeholders.
  • •Keep runbooks current with infrastructure and application changes.
  • •Monitor new Azure BCDR features and capabilities.
  • •Learn from real-world incidents (even minor ones) and incorporate lessons.
Common Mistake

Treating the BCDR plan as a static document that sits on a shelf. Without regular review, testing, and updates, it quickly becomes obsolete and ineffective.

Why it Matters

An adaptive BCDR strategy ensures your organization remains resilient against evolving threats and changing business needs, providing sustained protection and peace of mind.

Key Takeaway

BCDR is an iterative journey; continuous review and adaptation are paramount to maintaining an effective and relevant resilience strategy.
