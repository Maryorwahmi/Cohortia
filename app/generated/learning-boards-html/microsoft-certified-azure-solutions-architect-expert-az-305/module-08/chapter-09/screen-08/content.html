Anatomy of a BCDR Runbook

The step-by-step guide for Azure recovery

01

Prerequisites

VPN connectivity, Azure CLI/PowerShell access, valid credentials.

02

Order of Operations

Which services to fail over first (e.g., databases, then application tiers).

03

Azure Commands/Steps

Specific Azure CLI/PowerShell commands or portal steps for failover.

04

Validation Steps

How to confirm services are running correctly in the DR region.

05

Rollback Procedures

Instructions for reverting to the primary region (failback) once restored.

06

Contact Information

Internal teams and Azure support contacts for escalation.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive BCDR runbook is a highly detailed, prescriptive document that guides the recovery team through every step of failover and failback, minimizing ambiguity and maximizing efficiency during a disaster.
