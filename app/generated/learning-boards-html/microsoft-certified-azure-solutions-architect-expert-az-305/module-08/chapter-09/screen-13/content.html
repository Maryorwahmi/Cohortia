Common BCDR Pitfalls and Safety Notes

Avoiding mistakes and ensuring plan accessibility

Common Pitfalls

  • •Static Plan: Treating the BCDR plan as a one-time, unchanging document.
  • •Underestimating Failback: Overlooking the complexity of returning to the primary region.
  • •Lack of Testing: Not regularly validating the plan's effectiveness.

Safety Notes

  • •Secure Storage: Store BCDR plans and runbooks securely.
  • •Easy Accessibility: Ensure recovery team can access plans even if primary systems are down (e.g., physical copies, offline storage).
  • •Data Synchronization: Plan carefully for data consistency during failback.
Key Takeaway

Proactively addressing common pitfalls and ensuring secure, accessible plan storage are vital steps to building a truly resilient and actionable BCDR strategy.
