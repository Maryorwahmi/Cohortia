BCDR Plan: Recovery Strategy & Team

Defining how to recover and who is responsible

Recovery Strategy

Outlines the chosen HA/DR patterns (e.g., active-passive, active-active) and the specific Azure services used for recovery.

  • •Specifies Azure services like ASR, SQL Failover Groups.
  • •Details how applications and data will be restored.
  • •Ensures alignment with RTO/RPO targets.

Recovery Team and Roles

Defines who is responsible for what during a disaster, including contact information and escalation paths for efficient coordination.

  • •Assigns specific tasks to individuals/teams.
  • •Establishes clear communication channels.
  • •Prevents confusion and delays during an incident.
Key Takeaway

A well-defined recovery strategy and clearly assigned team roles are crucial for a coordinated and effective response during a disaster, ensuring smooth execution of the BCDR plan.
