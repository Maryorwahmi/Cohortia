Key Concepts: Foundational Elements

Essential terms for BCDR strategy

BCDR Plan

A comprehensive document outlining strategies, procedures, and responsibilities for maintaining business operations during and after disruptive events.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Identifies critical business functions, their dependencies, and the financial/reputational impact of their unavailability, driving RTO and RPO.

BCDR Runbook

Detailed, step-by-step instructions for executing the recovery strategy, including failover and failback procedures, serving as the operational guide.

Summary Takeaway

A robust BCDR strategy is built upon a clear plan, a thorough understanding of business impact, and detailed operational runbooks to guide recovery.
