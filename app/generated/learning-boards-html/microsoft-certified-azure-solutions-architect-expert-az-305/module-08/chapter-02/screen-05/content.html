Availability Sets: Protecting Against Local Failures

Ensuring VM redundancy within a single data center.

How it Works
  • • Logical Grouping: VMs are grouped to signal redundancy needs to Azure.
  • • Fault Domains: Distributes VMs across different physical racks, each with isolated power/network.
  • • Update Domains: Distributes VMs into groups that can be rebooted independently during maintenance.
  • • Protection: Guards against single hardware failures and planned maintenance within a data center.
Key Takeaway

Availability Sets distribute VMs across fault and update domains to protect against single hardware failures and planned maintenance within a data center.

Common Misconception

Availability Sets do NOT protect against data center-wide outages. They are for intra-data center resilience only.
