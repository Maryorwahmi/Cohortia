Service Capabilities

Zonal vs. Zone-Redundant Services

Understanding how Azure services interact with Availability Zones.

Zonal Services

  • • Definition: Services that can be explicitly deployed or 'pinned' to a specific Availability Zone.
  • • Control: You choose which zone each instance of the service resides in.
  • • Examples: Virtual Machines, Standard Public IP addresses.
  • • HA Strategy: Requires manual distribution of instances across zones to achieve high availability.

Zone-Redundant Services

  • • Definition: Services that automatically replicate data or instances across Availability Zones.
  • • Control: Azure manages the distribution and replication across zones for you.
  • • Examples: Zone-redundant storage (ZRS), Azure SQL Database (with zone redundancy enabled).
  • • HA Strategy: Provides built-in high availability and resilience against zone failures.
Key Takeaway

Zonal services are pinned to a specific AZ, while zone-redundant services automatically replicate across AZs for built-in HA, requiring different architectural considerations.

Architect's Note

Always verify the zonal capabilities of each Azure service in the official documentation, as this impacts your HA design and resilience.
