Choosing Your Regional Strategy for BCDR

Balancing availability, disaster recovery, and complexity across Azure regions.

Single Region

Relies on Availability Zones for HA within one region. Suitable for less stringent RTO/RPO.

Region Pairs

Two regions within the same geography, facilitating efficient cross-region replication and data residency.

Multi-Region

Deploying across any two or more regions for maximum resilience, but with increased complexity.

Key Takeaway

The choice of regional strategy directly impacts your application's resilience and disaster recovery capabilities, requiring careful consideration of RTO/RPO and operational overhead.
