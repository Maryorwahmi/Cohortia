Service Tiers

Azure Cache for Redis Tiers: Basic, Standard, Premium

Understand the different service tiers and their features to choose the right fit for your workload.

Basic Tier

  • Single-node configuration.
  • No SLA (Service Level Agreement).
  • Best for development, testing, and non-critical workloads.

Standard Tier

  • Two-node primary/replica configuration.
  • Includes an SLA for reliability.
  • Offers automatic failover for basic high availability.
  • Suitable for production workloads requiring reliability.

Premium Tier

  • Builds on Standard with enhanced features.
  • Includes Persistence, VNet Integration, Clustering, Geo-replication.
  • Essential for mission-critical applications.
Key Takeaway

Choosing the right Redis tier depends on your application's specific needs for availability, security, and advanced features, with Premium offering the most robust capabilities.
