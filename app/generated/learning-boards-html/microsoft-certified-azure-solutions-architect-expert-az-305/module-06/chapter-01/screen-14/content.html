Key Concepts: Non-Relational Data Storage

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Non-Relational (NoSQL) Databases

Database systems offering flexible schemas and horizontal scalability, diverging from traditional relational models.

Horizontal Scalability (Scale-out)

Increasing capacity by adding more servers or nodes to a distributed system, rather than upgrading existing ones.

Schema-on-Read

A data modeling approach where the data structure is interpreted at read time, not strictly enforced at write time.

Summary Takeaway

Non-relational databases are crucial for modern applications, offering flexibility, horizontal scalability, and diverse data models to meet evolving data demands.
