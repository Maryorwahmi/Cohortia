Learning Objectives

What you'll achieve in this chapter.

01

Differentiate Database Paradigms

Clearly distinguish between relational and non-relational database models, understanding their fundamental architectural differences and when each is appropriate. This skill is vital for making informed design decisions in complex cloud environments.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Identify NoSQL Characteristics

Recognize the core characteristics and benefits of non-relational (NoSQL) data stores, such as flexible schemas, horizontal scalability, and diverse data models. This understanding will enable you to leverage their strengths for modern application needs.
