Azure RBAC for Blob Storage Access
Fine-Grained Control within Your Organization
What is Azure RBAC?

Azure Role-Based Access Control is a system for managing who has access to Azure resources, what they can do with those resources, and what areas they can access. It's the primary method for internal access management.

Key Benefits for Blob Storage
  • Fine-grained control: Assign permissions at storage account, container, or blob level.
  • Integration: Works seamlessly with Azure Active Directory identities (users, groups, service principals).
  • Principle of Least Privilege: Grant only necessary permissions.
  • Auditable: Provides clear records of access assignments.
Key Takeaway

Azure RBAC is the recommended, secure, and scalable method for managing internal access to Blob Storage, ensuring precise control over data permissions.

Example: Data Science Team Access

A data science team needs to read data from a 'raw-data' container. You assign the 'Storage Blob Data Reader' role to their Azure AD group at the 'raw-data' container scope.
