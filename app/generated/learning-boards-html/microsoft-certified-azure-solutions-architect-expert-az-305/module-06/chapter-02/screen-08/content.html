Cool and Archive Tiers: Balancing Access and Retention
Optimizing for Infrequent and Long-Term Data Storage
Cool Access Tier
  • Cost: Lower storage costs than Hot, higher access costs.
  • Latency: Immediate access.
  • Use Cases: Short-term backups, disaster recovery, older datasets.
  • Minimum Retention: 30 days.
Archive Access Tier
  • Cost: Lowest storage costs, highest access costs and retrieval latency.
  • Latency: Hours (flexible).
  • Use Cases: Long-term backups, historical archives, compliance data.
  • Minimum Retention: 180 days.
Key Takeaway

Cool and Archive tiers offer significant cost savings for less active data, but require careful consideration of access frequency, retrieval costs, and minimum retention periods.

Scenario: Long-Term Compliance

A financial institution needs to retain transaction logs for 7 years for regulatory compliance. The Archive tier is the most cost-effective solution, accepting the hours-long retrieval time for rarely accessed historical data.
