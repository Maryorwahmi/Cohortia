Block Blobs: The General-Purpose Choice
Optimized for Efficient Uploads and Large Data Storage
Key Characteristics
  • Composed of blocks of data, up to 50,000 blocks per blob.
  • Each block can be up to 100 MB (or 4000 MB with larger block sizes).
  • Optimized for parallel and resumable uploads.
  • Efficient for large files and general-purpose data.
Ideal Use Cases
  • Storing documents, images, and videos.
  • Backup files and disaster recovery data.
  • Data for data lakes and large analytics datasets.
  • Most common type for general unstructured data.
Key Takeaway

Block blobs are the most flexible and widely used blob type, perfect for large, general-purpose files due to their efficient upload and management capabilities.

Analogy: Building with LEGO Bricks

Imagine a block blob as a structure built from many individual LEGO bricks. You can add, remove, or replace specific bricks without rebuilding the entire structure, making it very flexible for large files.
