Strategies for Cost Optimization

Maximizing Value Across Azure Non-Relational Services

Tiered Storage

Utilize Hot, Cool, and Archive tiers in Blob Storage and Data Lake Gen2 based on access patterns to reduce costs.

Cosmos DB RUs

Carefully provision RUs, use auto-scale, and optimize queries to minimize consumption and reduce costs.

Right-sizing Cache

Choose the appropriate Redis tier and size for your workload, avoiding over-provisioning to save costs.

Data Lifecycle Management

Implement policies to automatically move data between tiers or delete old data to manage storage costs.

Serverless Options

Consider Cosmos DB Serverless for unpredictable or bursty workloads to pay per operation, avoiding provisioned throughput costs.

Key Takeaway

Proactive and continuous cost optimization through tiered storage, efficient RU management, right-sizing, lifecycle policies, and serverless options is fundamental to sustainable Azure data architectures.
