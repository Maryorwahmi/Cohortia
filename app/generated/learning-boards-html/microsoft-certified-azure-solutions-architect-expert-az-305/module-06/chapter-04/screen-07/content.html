Consistency Deep Dive

Strong and Bounded Staleness Consistency

Highest consistency with controlled lag

Strong Consistency

Reads are guaranteed to return the most recent committed version of the data. All replicas are synchronized.

  • Highest latency
  • Lowest availability during partitions

Bounded Staleness

Reads are guaranteed to be within a 'staleness window' (time or operations) from the most recent writes.

  • Good balance of consistency and performance
  • Configurable staleness window
Key Takeaway

Strong consistency ensures immediate global data reflection, while Bounded Staleness offers high consistency with a predictable, configurable delay, balancing integrity and performance.

Example: Global Inventory System

For a global inventory system where stock levels must be absolutely accurate across all regions at all times, Strong consistency is essential to prevent overselling.

Pro-Tip: When to use Bounded Staleness

Bounded Staleness is excellent for applications like real-time analytics dashboards or personalized recommendation engines where data needs to be fresh but can tolerate a few seconds or operations of lag.
