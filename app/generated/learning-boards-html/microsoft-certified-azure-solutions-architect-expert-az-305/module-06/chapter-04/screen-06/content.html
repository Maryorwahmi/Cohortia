Data Consistency

Cosmos DB Consistency Models: An Overview

Balancing consistency, availability, and latency

The CAP Theorem

Cosmos DB, as a distributed system, inherently provides Partition Tolerance. You choose between Consistency and Availability.

Five Models

Strong, Bounded Staleness, Session, Consistent Prefix, and Eventual. Each offers a different balance of guarantees.

Architectural Decision

Choosing the right model is critical for application behavior, impacting data integrity, user experience, and system resilience.

Key Takeaway

Cosmos DB's five consistency models provide granular control over the trade-offs between data consistency, availability, and latency, guided by the CAP theorem.

Example: Financial Transactions

For financial applications, Strong consistency is often required to ensure all transactions are processed and reflected globally before any read, preventing discrepancies.

Why it Matters: User Experience

A social media feed might use Eventual consistency to prioritize fast loading times, as a slight delay in seeing a new post is generally acceptable to users.
