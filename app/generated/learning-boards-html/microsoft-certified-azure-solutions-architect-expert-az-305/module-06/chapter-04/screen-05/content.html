Performance & Cost

Understanding Request Units (RUs)

The performance currency of Cosmos DB

What are RUs?

A normalized measure of system resources (CPU, IOPS, memory) consumed by database operations like reads, writes, and queries.

Pricing & Provisioning

You provision throughput in RU/s. Pay for provisioned capacity or use serverless (pay-per-operation) for unpredictable workloads.

Optimization is Key

Minimizing RU consumption through efficient queries, data modeling, and indexing is vital for cost management and performance SLAs.

Key Takeaway

RUs are the fundamental unit for measuring and provisioning throughput in Cosmos DB, directly impacting both performance and cost.

Example: RU Consumption

A 1KB point read might cost 1 RU. A complex query on a large dataset could cost hundreds of RUs. Understanding these costs helps in designing efficient queries and data structures.

Common Misconception: RUs are just CPU

RUs are not just CPU cycles. They abstract a combination of CPU, memory, IOPS, and network resources. It's a holistic measure of database operations, simplifying capacity planning.
