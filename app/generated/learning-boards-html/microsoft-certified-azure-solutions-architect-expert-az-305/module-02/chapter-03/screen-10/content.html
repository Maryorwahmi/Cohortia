Introducing Cross-Tenant Access Settings

Enhancing B2B collaboration with granular control

What are Cross-Tenant Access Settings?

These settings allow you to define inbound and outbound trust relationships between your Azure AD tenant and specific partner tenants, providing more granular control over B2B collaboration.

Problem Solved

  • Limited Control: Moves beyond individual guest invitations to policy-driven relationships.

  • User Experience: Improves collaboration by trusting partner's security claims.

Core Concept: Trust Relationships

Instead of just inviting users, you establish explicit trust relationships with entire partner tenants. This allows for centralized management of inbound and outbound B2B collaboration policies.

Your Tenant ↔ Partner Tenant

Key Takeaway

Cross-tenant access settings enable granular, policy-driven B2B collaboration by establishing explicit trust relationships between Azure AD tenants.
