Advanced B2B

Optimizing Cross-Tenant Collaboration

Leveraging trusted claims and fine-grained access policies

Benefits of Trusted Claims

  • MFA Trust: Trust multi-factor authentication claims from partner tenants, reducing redundant prompts.

  • Device Compliance: Trust device compliance claims, allowing seamless access for compliant devices from partner organizations.

  • Improved User Experience: Streamlines the sign-in process for partner users, enhancing collaboration efficiency.

Use Case Scenario

Your organization has a long-term partnership with 'Contoso Corp'. You configure cross-tenant access to trust Contoso's MFA, so their users don't re-authenticate when accessing your shared project management tool.

Granular Control Options

  • Block Collaboration: Prevent B2B collaboration with specific tenants entirely.

  • Restrict to Applications: Limit B2B collaboration to specific applications within your tenant.

  • Inbound/Outbound Policies: Define separate policies for users coming into your tenant and your users accessing partner tenants.

Key Takeaway

Cross-tenant access settings allow you to trust partner security claims and apply fine-grained access policies, optimizing B2B collaboration for long-term partnerships.
