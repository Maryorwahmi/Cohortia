B2B vs. B2C: Understanding the Core Differences

Distinguishing solutions for partners and customers

Azure AD B2B Collaboration

  • •

    Purpose: Business-to-Business (B2B) scenarios.

  • •

    User Type: External users (guest users) from partner organizations, vendors, contractors.

  • •

    Identity Source: Maintain original identities (Azure AD, Microsoft accounts, social IdPs).

  • •

    Use Cases: Access to Microsoft 365, Azure subscriptions, internal LOB apps.

Example Scenario

A consulting firm needs access to your SharePoint project site. You invite their existing Azure AD accounts.

Azure AD B2C (Business-to-Consumer)

  • •

    Purpose: Customer-facing applications.

  • •

    User Type: Millions of customers, general public.

  • •

    Identity Source: Local accounts (email/password) or social IdPs (Google, Facebook).

  • •

    Use Cases: E-commerce sites, mobile apps, public portals.

Example Scenario

Your e-commerce website allows customers to sign up with their email or Google account.

Key Takeaway

B2B is for trusted organizational partners accessing internal resources, while B2C is for public customers accessing your applications.
