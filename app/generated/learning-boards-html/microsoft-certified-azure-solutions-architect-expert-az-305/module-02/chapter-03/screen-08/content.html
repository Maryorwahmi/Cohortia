Definition

Azure AD B2C: For Your Customers

A dedicated identity service for consumer applications

What is Azure AD B2C?

Azure AD B2C is a cloud-based identity management service that enables your customers to sign up and sign in to your applications using local accounts or social identity providers.

Key Features

  • Scalability: Supports millions of customer identities.

  • Customization: White-label experience to match your brand.

  • Flexible Identity: Supports local accounts and social identity providers.

Architectural Separation

B2C operates as a separate Azure AD tenant, ensuring customer identities are isolated from your corporate employee identities. This separation is crucial for security and compliance.

B2C Tenant (Customers) vs. Corporate Azure AD (Employees)

Key Takeaway

Azure AD B2C provides a scalable, customizable, and separate identity solution for customer-facing applications, isolating consumer identities from corporate ones.
