Module 2 › Chapter 6 › Analyzing Results and Continuous Compliance
Refine Provisioning

Analyze patterns in review outcomes (e.g., high denial rates) to identify and correct inefficiencies or over-provisioning in initial access granting processes.

Example: Adjust group membership criteria based on review data.
Improve Reviewer Engagement

Assess reviewer performance and feedback to enhance training, clarify responsibilities, and ensure timely, accurate decisions, especially for critical access.

Example: Provide targeted guidance on justifying denials.
Integrate with SIEM

Feed access review data into SIEM solutions like Azure Sentinel to enable comprehensive monitoring, alerting, and auditing for a unified view of access governance.

Example: Trigger alerts for suspicious access review patterns.
Key Takeaway

Continuously analyzing and acting upon access review results is fundamental to maintaining a strong, adaptive security posture.
