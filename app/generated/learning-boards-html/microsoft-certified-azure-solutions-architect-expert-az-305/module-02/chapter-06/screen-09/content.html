Module 2 › Chapter 6 › Best Practices for Managing Access Reviews
Align Frequency

Set review frequency based on the sensitivity and risk of the access. Critical resources require higher frequency (e.g., quarterly), while less sensitive ones can be reviewed annually.

Avoid review fatigue by not over-scheduling.
Communicate Clearly

Ensure reviewers understand the purpose, their responsibilities, and the importance of timely and accurate decisions. Provide context for the access being reviewed.

Example: Send clear instructions with review notifications.
Test Automation

Always test 'Auto-apply results' settings carefully. Start with 'No change' or 'Take recommendations' before enabling automatic removal to prevent accidental access revocation.

Crucial for preventing unintended access removal.
Key Takeaway

Implementing best practices ensures that access reviews are effective, efficient, and contribute significantly to a robust security posture.
