Module 2 › Chapter 6 › Configuring Access Review Settings
Frequency

Determine how often access should be reviewed. Options include weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually. Sensitive access requires higher frequency.

Example: Quarterly review for finance groups.
Duration

Set the length of time reviewers have to complete the review once it's active. Common durations are 7, 14, or 30 days.

Example: 7-day review period for urgent access.
Reviewers

Select who will perform the review: group owners, application owners, managers, or specific individuals. Choose the most informed approver.

Example: Manager reviews their direct reports' access.
Key Takeaway

Carefully configuring frequency, duration, and reviewer assignments ensures that access reviews are both thorough and manageable.
