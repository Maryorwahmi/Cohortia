Module 2 › Chapter 6 › Simulating the Reviewer's Task
Notification & Access

Reviewers receive notifications or can access reviews via the Azure portal under 'Reviews of me.' They see a list of users requiring evaluation for a specific group or application.

Example: Reviewing access to 'Project Phoenix Resources'.
Decision Making

Reviewers decide to 'Approve' or 'Deny' access for each user. Recommendations (e.g., inactivity) can assist in making informed decisions.

Provide justification when denying access.
Submission & Automation

After submitting decisions, if 'Auto-apply results' is enabled, Azure AD processes the outcomes, automatically revoking access for denied users or non-responders.

Understanding this workflow ensures effective review design.
Key Takeaway

The reviewer's experience is central to access reviews; understanding their workflow helps in designing efficient and effective review processes.
