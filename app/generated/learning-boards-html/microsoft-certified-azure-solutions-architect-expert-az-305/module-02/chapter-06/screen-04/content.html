Module 2 › Chapter 6 › The Purpose of Access Reviews
Core Principle

The Principle of Least Privilege dictates that users should only be granted the minimum permissions necessary to perform their job functions. This minimizes the potential impact of compromised accounts or insider threats.

The Risk

Over time, access permissions can become outdated due to role changes, project completions, or employee departures. This stale access significantly increases an organization's attack surface and poses a compliance risk.

The Solution

Azure AD Access Reviews provide a systematic process to regularly verify and manage user access, ensuring permissions remain appropriate and compliant. This proactive approach is key to a mature identity and access management strategy.

Compliance Driver

Many regulatory frameworks and internal policies mandate periodic audits of access rights. Access reviews help demonstrate adherence to these requirements by providing auditable evidence of access verification.

Key Takeaway

Regularly reviewing and revoking unnecessary access is fundamental to maintaining the principle of least privilege, reducing security risks, and ensuring ongoing compliance.
