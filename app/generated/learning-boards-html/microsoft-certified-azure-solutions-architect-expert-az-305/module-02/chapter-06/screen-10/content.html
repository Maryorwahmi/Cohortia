Module 2 › Chapter 6 › Common Pitfalls in Access Review Design
Incorrect Frequency

Setting the review frequency too low for critical resources or too high for low-risk ones can lead to security gaps or reviewer fatigue, respectively.

Align frequency with risk and sensitivity.
Poor Communication

Failing to clearly communicate the purpose, reviewer responsibilities, and decision-making context can lead to inaccurate or incomplete reviews.

Provide clear instructions and context to reviewers.
Inadequate Automation

Not enabling or properly configuring automated actions ('Auto-apply results,' 'If reviewers don't respond') leads to manual remediation delays and potential security gaps.

Test automation settings thoroughly before deployment.
Key Takeaway

Awareness of common pitfalls is crucial for designing robust and effective Azure AD access review solutions that truly enhance security and compliance.
