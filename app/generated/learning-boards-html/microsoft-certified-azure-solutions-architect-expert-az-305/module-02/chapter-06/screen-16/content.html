Key Concepts

Reinforcing critical takeaways.

Access Review

Systematic process to verify user access to groups, apps, or roles.

Compliance

Adherence to policies and regulations via access controls.

Least Privilege

Granting only necessary permissions.

Scope

The target of the review: groups, apps, or PIM roles.

Reviewer

The individual responsible for evaluating access.

Stale Access

Unnecessary permissions that pose a security risk.

Effective access reviews are a cornerstone of modern identity and access management.
