Delegated Administration

Granular Control with Administrative Units

Administrative Units (AUs) provide a way to logically group users and groups in Azure AD, allowing for delegated administration over specific subsets of your directory.

  • Scope Delegation: Restrict admin permissions to specific AUs.
  • Enhanced Security: Reduces the blast radius of compromised accounts.
  • Organizational Alignment: Reflects departmental or regional structures.
Example Scenario

A global company creates AUs for each country. Local IT administrators are assigned the 'User Administrator' role, scoped only to their country's AU, preventing them from managing users in other regions.

Key Takeaway

Administrative Units enable granular delegation of administrative tasks over specific user and group subsets, crucial for large organizations to maintain security and efficiency.
