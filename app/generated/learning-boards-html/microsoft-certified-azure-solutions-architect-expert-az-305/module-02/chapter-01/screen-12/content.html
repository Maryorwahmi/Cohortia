Decision Framework

Matching Editions to Organizational Needs

Choosing the right Azure AD edition involves a strategic assessment of your organization's unique security, compliance, and operational requirements.

  • Security Posture: Advanced threat detection, risk-based access.
  • Compliance Requirements: Regulatory mandates for access control.
  • Budget Constraints: Balancing features with cost-effectiveness.
Common Pitfall

Failing to select the right edition can lead to a lack of critical security features, leaving your organization vulnerable, or incurring unnecessary costs for unused advanced features.

Decision Flow

Step-by-Step Edition Selection

1. Identify Core Needs

Basic identity, SSO, cloud app access.

2. Evaluate Advanced Needs

Hybrid identity, Conditional Access, SSPR for hybrid users.

3. Consider Premium Security

Identity Protection, PIM, risk-based Conditional Access.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize security and compliance requirements, then map them to Azure AD edition features, and finally consider budget to make an informed decision.
