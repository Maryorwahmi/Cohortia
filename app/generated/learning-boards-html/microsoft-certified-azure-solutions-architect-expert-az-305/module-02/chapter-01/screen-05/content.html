Design Strategy

Single vs. Multi-Tenant Architectures

The choice between a single or multi-tenant Azure AD architecture is fundamental to your identity solution design. It dictates how identities are managed and isolated.

  • Single Tenant: Centralized management, simplified synchronization.
  • Multi-Tenant: Required for complex structures or M&A scenarios.
  • Isolation: Each tenant is a distinct, isolated instance of Azure AD.
Common Pitfall

Underestimating the complexity of multi-tenant management. While necessary for some, it introduces challenges in cross-tenant collaboration and identity synchronization.

Decision Factors

When to Choose Each Model

Single Tenant (Default)

  • Most organizations with unified IT.
  • Centralized management and governance.
  • Simplified identity synchronization.

Multi-Tenant (Specific Cases)

  • Large enterprises with distinct subsidiaries.
  • Mergers, acquisitions, or divestitures.
  • Strict legal or administrative autonomy requirements.
Key Takeaway

A single Azure AD tenant is standard for most, but multi-tenant architectures are crucial for complex organizational structures or M&A scenarios requiring distinct identity isolation.
