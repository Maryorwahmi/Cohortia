Activation Controls

Maximum Activation Duration and MFA

Setting Time Limits and Strengthening Authentication

Why These Settings Are Crucial

  • Limit Exposure: Reduces the window of opportunity for attackers to exploit elevated privileges.
  • Prevent Stale Access: Ensures permissions are automatically revoked after a set period.
  • Stronger Authentication: MFA adds a critical layer of defense against credential compromise.

Maximum Activation Duration

Defines the maximum time a user can be active in an elevated role after activation. This enforces the JIT principle.

Typical: 1-8 hours
Automatic Revocation

MFA Requirement on Activation

Requires users to complete a Multi-Factor Authentication challenge before a privileged role can be activated.

Non-negotiable Best Practice
Protects against Credential Theft
Key Takeaway

Configure a sensible maximum activation duration and always require MFA for privileged role activations to enforce JIT and enhance security.
