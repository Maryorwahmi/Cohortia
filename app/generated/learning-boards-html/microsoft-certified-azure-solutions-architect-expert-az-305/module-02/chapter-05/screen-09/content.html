Justification and Approval Requirements

Adding Human Oversight and Accountability to Role Activation

Benefits of Justification & Approval

  • Enhanced Accountability: Users must articulate the need for elevated access.
  • Human Oversight: Designated approvers validate the legitimacy of requests.
  • Improved Audit Trail: Provides clear records of the 'why' behind each activation.

Justification Requirement

Users must provide a business justification when activating a privileged role. This context is logged for auditing.

Example: "Resetting user password"

Approval Requirement

Activation requests must be approved by one or more designated approvers before access is granted.

Approvers: Security Managers, Team Leads
Common Mistake

Improperly assigning approvers (too many leading to delays, too few creating single points of failure).

Key Takeaway

Justification and approval requirements add critical layers of human oversight and accountability, ensuring privileged access is both necessary and validated.
