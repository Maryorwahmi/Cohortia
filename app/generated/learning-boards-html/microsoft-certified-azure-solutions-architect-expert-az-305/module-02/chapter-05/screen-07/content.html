Configuring PIM Activation Settings

Defining Controls for Privileged Role Elevation

Why Configure Activation Settings?

  • Enforce JIT: Ensures access is temporary and time-bound.
  • Strengthen Security: Adds layers like MFA and approval to prevent unauthorized elevation.
  • Improve Auditability: Provides clear records of who, when, and why a role was activated.

Key Activation Settings

  • Maximum Activation Duration: Sets the time limit for active role assignment.
  • MFA Requirement: Demands Multi-Factor Authentication for activation.
  • Justification Requirement: Requires a business reason for activation.
  • Approval Requirement: Mandates approval from designated approvers.
  • Ticket Information Requirement: Requires a ticket number for auditing.
Pro-Tip

Always require MFA for privileged role activations. This is a fundamental security control that significantly reduces the risk of credential compromise.

Key Takeaway

PIM activation settings provide granular control over how and when privileged roles are elevated, enforcing JIT principles and enhancing security through MFA, justification, and approval.
