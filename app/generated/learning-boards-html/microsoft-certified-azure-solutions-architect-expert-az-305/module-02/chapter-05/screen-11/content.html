Access Governance

Time-Bound Access and Access Reviews

Ensuring Permissions Remain Current and Justified

Benefits of Ongoing Governance

  • Eliminate Stale Access: Automatically revokes permissions when no longer needed.
  • Enforce Least Privilege: Ensures users always have the minimum necessary permissions.
  • Compliance & Auditability: Provides documented proof of regular access validation.

Time-Bound Access

When a user activates a role, permissions are granted for a specific duration and automatically revoked afterwards.

Automatic Revocation
Enforces JIT

Access Reviews

Scheduled reviews where users or managers confirm the continued need for privileged role assignments.

Periodic Verification
Automated Revocation
Key Takeaway

Time-bound access and recurring access reviews are essential PIM features for maintaining the principle of least privilege and ensuring continuous access governance.
