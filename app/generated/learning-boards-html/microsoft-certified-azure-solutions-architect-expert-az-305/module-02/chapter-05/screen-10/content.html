Activation Controls

Ticket Information and Approver Design

Integrating Service Management and Optimizing Approval Workflows

Why These Settings Matter

  • Enhanced Auditability: Links privileged actions to documented service requests.
  • Operational Efficiency: Well-designed workflows prevent delays while maintaining security.
  • Stronger Governance: Ensures approvals come from appropriate, knowledgeable individuals.

Ticket Information Requirement

Users must provide a ticket number (e.g., from a service desk system) for auditing purposes when activating a role.

Links to ITIL/ServiceNow

Designing Approval Workflows

Carefully select approvers with sufficient authority and understanding of the business need for role activation.

Balance Security & Efficiency
Pro-Tip

Consider a tiered approval model: single approver for low-risk roles, multiple or specific security team for high-risk roles.

Key Takeaway

Leverage ticket information for robust auditing and meticulously design approval workflows to ensure both security and operational agility.
