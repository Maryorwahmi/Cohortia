Implementing Access Reviews

Automating the Verification and Revocation of Privileged Access

Access Review Process

  • Schedule Review: Define frequency (e.g., quarterly) and scope (specific roles/users).
  • Notify Reviewers: Users or managers receive notifications to review access.
  • Review & Justify: Reviewers confirm or deny continued access, providing justification.
  • Automated Action: PIM automatically removes access if not justified or denied.

Benefits of Access Reviews

  • Continuous Least Privilege: Ensures permissions are always aligned with current needs.
  • Reduced Attack Surface: Proactively removes unneeded, high-risk access.
  • Compliance & Audit: Provides evidence of regular access governance.
Scenario

A contractor's project ends. An access review prompts their manager to confirm access. Manager denies, PIM automatically revokes 'Contributor' role.

Key Takeaway

Access reviews are crucial for ongoing governance, ensuring privileged access is regularly verified and automatically revoked if no longer justified.
