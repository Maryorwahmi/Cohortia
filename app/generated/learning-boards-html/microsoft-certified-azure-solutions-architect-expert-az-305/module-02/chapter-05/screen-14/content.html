Key Concepts: PIM Fundamentals

Reinforcing Core Privileged Identity Management Principles

Privileged Identity Management (PIM)

An Azure AD service that manages, controls, and monitors access to important resources, enforcing JIT and JEA principles.

Just-In-Time (JIT) Access

Granting elevated permissions only when needed and for a limited time, rather than as permanent assignments.

Just-Enough-Access (JEA)

Granting only the minimum necessary permissions required to perform a specific task, nothing more.

Eligible Role Assignment

A PIM assignment where a user is eligible to activate a role but does not have permanent access; they must activate it when needed.

Summary Takeaway

PIM is a powerful service that leverages JIT and JEA principles to transform permanent privileged access into temporary, controlled, and auditable assignments, significantly enhancing security.
