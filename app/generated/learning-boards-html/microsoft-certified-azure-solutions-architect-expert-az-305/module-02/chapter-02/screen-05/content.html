Policy Enforcement

Conditional Access: The Policy Engine

Automating responses to detected risks.

Policy Structure
  • If Condition Met: Based on Identity Protection risk levels (High, Medium), user location, device state, or application.
  • Then Enforce Action: Block access, require MFA, require password change, limit session, or grant access with conditions.
  • Report-only Mode: Evaluate policy impact without enforcement, crucial for testing and tuning.
Key Takeaway

Conditional Access policies dynamically enforce security controls based on Identity Protection risk detections, automating responses to threats.
