Core Concept

Understanding Azure AD Identity Protection

Detecting and responding to identity-based risks.

Key Detections
  • User Risk: Indicates a user account may be compromised (e.g., leaked credentials, unusual activity).
  • Sign-in Risk: Indicates a specific sign-in attempt may be compromised (e.g., impossible travel, anonymous IP, infected device).
  • Machine Learning & Heuristics: Powers the detection of anomalous patterns and known threat indicators.
Key Takeaway

Azure AD Identity Protection proactively identifies potential identity compromises through user and sign-in risk detection, enabling timely remediation.
