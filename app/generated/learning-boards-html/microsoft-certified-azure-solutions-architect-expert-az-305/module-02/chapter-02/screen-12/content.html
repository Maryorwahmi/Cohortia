Addressing Lockout Scenarios

The role of SSPR and break-glass accounts.

Scenario

Administrator locked out after forgetting password; cannot use SSPR without MFA.

Common Mistake & Mitigation
  • Mistake: Not enforcing SSPR registration before MFA policies.
  • Mitigation: Require users to register for SSPR (and MFA) as a prerequisite for accessing sensitive apps or during onboarding.
  • Note: Break-glass accounts are for tenant-level emergencies, not routine user recovery.
Key Takeaway

Ensuring users register for SSPR and MFA is crucial to prevent lockouts when passwords are forgotten.
