Common Misconfigurations and Best Practices

Ensuring a robust identity protection posture.

Key Considerations
  • Break Glass Accounts: Secure, cloud-only, rarely used, heavily audited emergency access accounts.
  • User Education: Crucial for safe sign-in practices and understanding MFA importance.
  • Licensing: Ensure Azure AD Premium P2 licenses are assigned for Identity Protection features.
  • Continuous Improvement: Regularly review reports, refine policies, and adapt to evolving threats.
Key Takeaway

Avoiding common misconfigurations and adhering to best practices, like securing emergency accounts and user education, is vital for robust identity protection.
