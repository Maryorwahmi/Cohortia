Designing an Azure AD Identity Protection Solution

Proactively Detect, Investigate, and Remediate Identity Risks

In today's digital landscape, simply managing identities isn't enough; safeguarding them from compromise is paramount. Azure Active Directory Identity Protection is your advanced defense system, leveraging sophisticated machine learning and heuristic analysis to uncover potential vulnerabilities affecting your organization's identities. It meticulously identifies risks like leaked credentials, unusual sign-in patterns, and compromised devices, categorizing them into user risks and sign-in risks. As an architect, your role is to design a proactive, automated strategy that uses these powerful detections to shield your users and valuable resources. This chapter will guide you through understanding these capabilities, integrating them with policy enforcement, and ensuring continuous monitoring for an unbreachable identity posture.
