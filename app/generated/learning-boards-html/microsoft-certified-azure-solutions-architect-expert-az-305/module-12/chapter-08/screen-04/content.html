Understanding Rehost (Lift and Shift)

A Quick Path to Cloud Adoption with Minimal Changes

What is Rehost?

Rehost, or Lift and Shift, involves moving on-premises applications and their underlying infrastructure (VMs, databases on VMs) to Azure IaaS with minimal or no changes to the application code or architecture.

Why Choose Rehost?

  • Quick path to cloud adoption.
  • Exit data centers and reduce hardware refresh cycles.
  • Gain immediate cloud benefits like elasticity and global reach.
  • Avoid upfront investment in refactoring applications.
Key Takeaway

Rehost offers a fast, low-risk entry to Azure IaaS, providing immediate infrastructure benefits without extensive application changes.

Analogy: Moving House

Imagine moving your entire household, furniture and all, from one apartment to another without redecorating or buying new items. You simply 'lift' your existing setup and 'shift' it to a new location. This is similar to how Rehost works for applications.

Immediate Benefits

  • Reduced operational costs for hardware.
  • Improved business continuity and disaster recovery.
  • Enhanced scalability and performance on demand.
  • Foundation for future cloud-native modernization.
Common Misconception

Rehost is not a full modernization. It moves the 'as-is' state, and further optimization is needed to fully leverage cloud-native services.
