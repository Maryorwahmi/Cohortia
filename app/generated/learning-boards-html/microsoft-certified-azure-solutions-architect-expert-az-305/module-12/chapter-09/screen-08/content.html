Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Monolith to Microservices
Concept

Breaking down a large, monolithic application into smaller, independently deployable services, each focused on a specific business capability.

Key Characteristics
  • Independent Development & Deployment
  • Focused on Single Business Capability
  • Communication via APIs or Messaging
  • Technology Diversity per Service
Key Takeaway

Microservices enable greater agility, scalability, and resilience by decomposing applications into manageable, independent units.

Benefits
  • Faster Innovation & Deployment Cycles
  • Improved Scalability (scale individual services)
  • Enhanced Resilience (fault isolation)
  • Technology Flexibility
Challenges
  • Increased Complexity (distributed systems)
  • Inter-service Communication Management
  • Distributed Transaction Management
  • Requires Mature DevOps Practices
