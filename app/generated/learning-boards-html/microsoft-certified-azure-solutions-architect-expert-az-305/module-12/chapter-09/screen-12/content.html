Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Choosing the Right Strategy
Key Decision Factors
  • Business Drivers: Cost reduction vs. faster innovation.
  • Application Complexity & Age: Monoliths vs. modular apps.
  • Budget & Timeline: Rehost (fastest/cheapest) to Rearchitect (slowest/most expensive).
  • Team Skills: Expertise in cloud-native patterns and distributed systems.
  • Future Vision: Application evolution needs in the cloud.
Strategic Alignment

The choice between Refactor and Rearchitect (or Rehost) depends on a holistic assessment of these factors. Azure Migrate provides initial insights, but architectural expertise is key to selecting the optimal target Azure services and migration path.

Example Guidance
  • Refactor: Suitable for applications needing modernization with moderate changes, focusing on PaaS benefits and reduced ops.
  • Rearchitect: Ideal for applications requiring maximum agility, scalability, and cloud-native capabilities, accepting higher complexity and investment.
Key Takeaway

Carefully evaluate business drivers, application characteristics, resources, and future goals to select the most effective migration strategy: Refactor for optimization or Rearchitect for transformation.
