Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Containerization with AKS and Container Apps
Concept

Packaging applications and dependencies into containers (e.g., Docker) for consistent deployment, managed by orchestration services like AKS or Container Apps.

Azure Services
  • Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS): Fully managed Kubernetes for complex orchestration.
  • Azure Container Apps: Serverless platform for microservices and containerized apps, simpler than AKS.
Key Takeaway

Containerization ensures portability and consistency, while AKS and Container Apps provide scalable platforms for managing containerized applications in Azure.

Benefits
  • Portability and Consistency across environments.
  • Isolation of applications and dependencies.
  • Simplified Scaling and Management (via orchestrators).
  • Efficient resource utilization.
Choosing Between AKS and Container Apps
  • AKS: For complex microservices, full Kubernetes control, advanced networking.
  • Container Apps: For simpler microservices, web apps, serverless container needs, easier management.
