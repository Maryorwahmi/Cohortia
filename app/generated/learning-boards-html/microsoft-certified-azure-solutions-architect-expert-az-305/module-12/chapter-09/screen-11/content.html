Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Refactor vs. Rearchitect: Key Differences
Refactor (Replatform)

Incremental changes to leverage PaaS services. Focuses on reducing operational overhead and gaining some cloud benefits.

Characteristics
  • Minor code/config changes
  • Faster migration, lower initial cost
  • Leverages managed services (PaaS)
  • Less complex, lower risk
Rearchitect

Significant redesign and rewrites to fully embrace cloud-native patterns. Aims for maximum agility, scalability, and resilience.

Characteristics
  • Major architectural changes
  • Longer migration, higher initial cost
  • Embraces cloud-native (microservices, serverless)
  • More complex, higher potential value
Key Takeaway

Choose Refactor for incremental optimization and reduced operational burden; choose Rearchitect for maximum cloud benefits, agility, and long-term value, accepting higher complexity and cost.
