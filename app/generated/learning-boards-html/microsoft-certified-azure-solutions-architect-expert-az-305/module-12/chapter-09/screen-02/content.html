Learning Objectives
Understand the nuances of Refactor and Rearchitect migration strategies.
01
Differentiate Strategies

Clearly distinguish between Refactor (Replatform) and Rearchitect migration approaches based on their goals and impact.

02
Design PaaS Solutions

Design solutions for migrating applications to Azure PaaS services like App Service and Azure SQL Database.

03
Design Cloud-Native Solutions

Design solutions for rearchitecting applications using microservices, containers (AKS), and serverless functions.

04
Evaluate Modernization

Understand the benefits and challenges of modernizing applications during migration and select appropriate Azure services.
