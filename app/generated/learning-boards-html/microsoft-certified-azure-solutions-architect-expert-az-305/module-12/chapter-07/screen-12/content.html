The Foundation of Success: Thorough Assessment

Understanding Your Landscape Before You Move

Why is Assessment Critical?

A thorough assessment is the critical first step in any migration project. It provides the necessary insights to make informed decisions, choose appropriate strategies, and mitigate potential risks, preventing costly surprises down the line.

Benefits of a Detailed Assessment

  • Accurate cost estimation and right-sizing.
  • Identification of dependencies and blockers.
  • Informed strategy selection for each workload.
  • Reduced risk of post-migration issues.
Key Takeaway

A comprehensive assessment is crucial for informed decision-making and successful migration outcomes, covering technical, business, and dependency aspects.

Key Assessment Aspects

**Application Inventory:** Documenting all applications, components, and dependencies.

**Technical Assessment:** Analyzing architecture, tech stack, performance, and compatibility.

**Business Assessment:** Understanding criticality, cost implications, and compliance needs.

**Dependency Mapping:** Identifying interconnections between applications and systems.
