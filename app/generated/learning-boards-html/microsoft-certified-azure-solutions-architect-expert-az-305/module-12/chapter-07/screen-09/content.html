Retire: Simplifying Your IT Landscape

Eliminating Unused Applications to Reduce Scope and Cost

What is Retire?

Identifying applications that are no longer needed or used and decommissioning them. This reduces the migration scope, saves costs, and simplifies the overall IT landscape.

Benefits of Retirement

  • Reduces migration scope and complexity.
  • Saves operational and licensing costs.
  • Frees up resources for strategic initiatives.
  • Simplifies IT management and security.
Key Takeaway

Retiring unused applications is a crucial step to streamline IT, reduce migration scope, and optimize costs, requiring active assessment and planning.

Identifying Candidates for Retirement

**Low Usage:** Applications with minimal or no active users.

**Obsolete Technology:** Running on unsupported or outdated platforms.

**Redundant Functionality:** Duplicated by newer systems or SaaS solutions.

**High Maintenance Cost:** Expensive to maintain relative to business value.
