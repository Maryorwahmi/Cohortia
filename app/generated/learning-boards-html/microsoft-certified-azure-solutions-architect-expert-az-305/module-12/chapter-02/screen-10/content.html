Handling Failures with the Dead-Letter Queue

Preventing Poison Messages from Blocking Processing

What is the DLQ?

A sub-queue where messages are automatically moved if they cannot be processed successfully after multiple attempts or if they expire.

Purpose
  • Prevents 'poison messages' from blocking the main queue.
  • Allows inspection and debugging of failed messages.
  • Enables reprocessing or discarding of problematic messages.
Architectural Approach

Implement a separate process (e.g., Azure Function) to monitor the DLQ, investigate failures, and decide on reprocessing or manual intervention.

Key Takeaway

A robust DLQ strategy is critical for fault tolerance, ensuring no data is lost and system issues are promptly addressed without impacting overall message flow.
