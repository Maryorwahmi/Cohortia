Backup and Disaster Recovery

Ensuring Business Continuity

Key Takeaway

Azure Backup and Azure Site Recovery are essential for data protection and business continuity, ensuring applications can recover from failures and outages.

Azure Backup
  • Provides reliable data protection for Azure and on-premises resources.
  • Configure backup policies for frequency and retention (RPO).
  • Supports various workloads including VMs, SQL databases, and file shares.
Azure Site Recovery (ASR)

Enables disaster recovery by replicating workloads to a secondary Azure region. Facilitates failover to meet Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and ensure business continuity.
