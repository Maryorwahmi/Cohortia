Auto-Scaling and Storage Optimization

Matching Resources to Demand and Tiering Data

Key Takeaway

Auto-scaling dynamically adjusts resources to meet demand, while tiered storage and data cleanup significantly reduce storage costs.

Auto-Scaling
  • Dynamically adjust compute resources (App Services, VMSS, AKS) based on metrics like CPU or queue length.
  • Scale out during peak demand, scale in during low demand.
  • Ensures performance while optimizing costs by avoiding over-provisioning.
Storage Optimization

Utilize Azure Blob Storage tiers (Hot, Cool, Archive) based on access frequency. Regularly delete unneeded snapshots, backups, and orphaned disks to reduce storage costs.
