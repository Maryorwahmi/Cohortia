Resource Tagging and Azure Policy

Enforcing Standards and Ensuring Compliance

Key Takeaway

Consistent resource tagging and Azure Policy are essential governance tools for enforcing standards, managing costs, and ensuring compliance across Azure resources.

Resource Tagging
  • Apply metadata to resources for organization and management.
  • Use tags for cost allocation, environment identification, and ownership tracking.
  • Essential for automation and policy enforcement.
Azure Policy

Define and enforce organizational standards and compliance rules. Prevent non-compliant deployments and ensure resources meet security and governance requirements.
