What is Workflow Orchestration?

Automating complex business processes or tasks that involve multiple systems, ensuring a coordinated sequence of actions and data flow.

Key Principle

To react to events, transform data, and coordinate actions across a diverse set of endpoints without managing underlying infrastructure.

Concrete Example

An automated workflow that monitors a shared folder for new files, triggers a data validation service, and then updates a database with the processed information.

Benefits of Orchestration

Process Automation

Automates multi-step processes, reducing manual effort and errors.

System Coordination

Ensures actions across different systems are executed in the correct order.

Visibility & Control

Provides monitoring and management of complex workflows.

Adaptability

Easily modify or extend workflows as business requirements change.

Key Takeaway

Workflow orchestration is vital for automating and coordinating complex business processes across multiple systems, ensuring reliable and efficient execution.
