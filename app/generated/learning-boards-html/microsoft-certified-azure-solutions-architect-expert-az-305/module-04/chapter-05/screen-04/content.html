Core Concept

Foundations of Security Monitoring and Auditing

Establishing comprehensive visibility for compliance and threat detection.

Why it Matters

  • •**Compliance:** Meet regulatory and internal governance requirements.
  • •**Threat Detection:** Identify suspicious activities and potential breaches early.
  • •**Incident Response:** Provide crucial data for investigating and resolving security incidents.
  • •**Accountability:** Track 'who did what, when, and where' for all resource changes.

Key Pillars of Cloud Security

Visibility

See all security-related events.

Detection

Identify suspicious activities.

Compliance

Meet regulatory standards.

Accountability

Trace all actions to sources.

Key Takeaway

A robust security monitoring and auditing strategy is non-negotiable for cloud compliance, threat detection, and effective incident response.
