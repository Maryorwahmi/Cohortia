Proactive Monitoring

Triggering Notifications with Alert Rules

Defining conditions to detect critical issues in your Azure environment.

What are Alert Rules?

Conditions defined in Azure Monitor that proactively trigger notifications or automated actions when specific thresholds or patterns are met in your monitoring data.

Key Considerations for Design

  • Thresholds: Set appropriate values to avoid alert fatigue or delayed detection.
  • Aggregation Period: Define the time window over which data is evaluated.
  • Severity: Align with business impact (e.g., Critical, Warning, Informational).
Key Takeaway

Well-designed alert rules are essential for proactive incident management, ensuring timely detection and response to critical conditions in your Azure environment.

Types of Alert Rules

  • 1Metric Alerts: Based on numerical data from platform or custom metrics. Ideal for real-time performance thresholds (e.g., CPU usage > 80%).
  • 2Log Alerts: Based on Kusto Query Language (KQL) queries against data in a Log Analytics Workspace. Powerful for complex patterns (e.g., >5 failed logins in 10 mins).
Misconception

Setting too many alerts or overly sensitive thresholds can lead to 'alert fatigue,' causing teams to ignore legitimate warnings. Focus on actionable alerts.
