Common Monitoring Pitfalls

Recognizing and mitigating typical challenges in monitoring design.

Over-monitoring

Collecting excessive, irrelevant data leads to increased costs and 'alert fatigue'.

Under-monitoring

Missing critical data points results in undetected issues and potential outages.

Reactive Systems

Only alerting after a problem has occurred, preventing proactive intervention.

Key Takeaway

Effective monitoring avoids the extremes of over- and under-monitoring, and prioritizes proactive detection over reactive responses to ensure system stability and cost efficiency.
