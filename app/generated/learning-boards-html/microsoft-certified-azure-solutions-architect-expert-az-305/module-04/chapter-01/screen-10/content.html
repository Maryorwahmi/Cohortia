Defining KPIs for Business Outcomes

Translating business goals into measurable monitoring objectives.

1

Identify Business Goals

Start with clear business objectives (e.g., 99.9% uptime, 5-second checkout).

2

Define Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Translate goals into measurable KPIs (e.g., successful transactions, login success rate).

3

Map KPIs to Metrics & Logs

Connect KPIs to specific technical metrics (CPU, latency) and logs (errors).

Key Takeaway

Effective monitoring is KPI-driven, ensuring that technical data collection directly supports and measures the achievement of critical business objectives.
