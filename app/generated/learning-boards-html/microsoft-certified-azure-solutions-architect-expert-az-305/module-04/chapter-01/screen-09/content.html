Bridging Technical and Business Requirements

Aligning monitoring with both operational health and organizational goals.

Technical Requirements

  • •Focus: Resource health and performance.
  • •Examples: VM running, CPU usage < 80%, API latency < 200ms, error rates.
  • •Goal: Operational stability and efficiency.
  • •Data Sources: Raw metrics and detailed logs.

Business Requirements

  • •Focus: Tangible business outcomes.
  • •Examples: E-commerce checkout < 5s, minimal login failures, 99.9% uptime.
  • •Goal: Achieve business objectives and customer satisfaction.
  • •Data Sources: KPIs derived from technical metrics/logs.
Key Takeaway

A successful monitoring strategy translates technical health into business impact by defining KPIs and mapping them to specific metrics and logs, ensuring alignment with organizational goals.
