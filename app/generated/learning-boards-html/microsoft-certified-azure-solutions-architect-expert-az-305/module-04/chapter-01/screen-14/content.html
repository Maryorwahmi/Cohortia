Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Monitoring Strategy

A planned approach to observe, collect, and analyze data to ensure the health, performance, and security of applications and infrastructure.

Operational Excellence

A pillar of the Azure Well-Architected Framework focused on monitoring, reliability, and continuous improvement.

Proactive Monitoring

Identifying and addressing potential issues before they impact users or services.

Summary Takeaway

A robust monitoring strategy, guided by Operational Excellence, prioritizes proactive approaches to ensure the continuous health, performance, and security of cloud solutions.
