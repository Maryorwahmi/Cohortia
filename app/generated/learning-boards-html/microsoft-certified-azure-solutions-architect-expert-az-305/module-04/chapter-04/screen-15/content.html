Key Concepts to Remember - Part 2

Advanced features and best practices

Application Map

A visual representation of the components of your application and their dependencies, showing performance metrics and failures.

Live Metrics Stream

A real-time, unfiltered view of application performance, including requests, failures, and process metrics, for immediate operational awareness.

Custom Events/Metrics

User-defined telemetry points that capture specific business logic or application-specific performance indicators, tailoring monitoring to business needs.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering these advanced features allows for deep diagnostics, real-time insights, and tailored business intelligence, ensuring optimal application health and performance.
