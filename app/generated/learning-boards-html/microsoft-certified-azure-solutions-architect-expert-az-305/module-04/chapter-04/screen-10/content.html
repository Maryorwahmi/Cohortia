Designing Custom Events and Metrics

Capturing business-specific application insights

What are Custom Events/Metrics?

User-defined telemetry points that capture specific business logic or application-specific performance indicators, going beyond standard out-of-the-box data.

Business Process Monitoring

Track key conversion rates, user journeys, and critical business operations within your application.

Correlate Technical to Business

Link application performance directly to business outcomes, such as sales figures or user engagement metrics.

Concrete Example

In a streaming service, a custom event 'SubscriptionUpgraded' with properties like 'OldPlan' and 'NewPlan' provides valuable data on user behavior and revenue impact, far beyond what standard metrics would show.

Key Takeaway

Custom events and metrics are crucial for capturing business-specific insights, enabling correlation between technical performance and business outcomes.
