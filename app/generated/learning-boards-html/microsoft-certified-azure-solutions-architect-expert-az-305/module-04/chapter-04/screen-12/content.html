Common Mistakes in Application Insights - Part 1

Ensuring comprehensive and efficient instrumentation

Missing Instrumentation

  • •Not instrumenting all critical components of a distributed application.
  • •Leads to gaps in distributed tracing and incomplete end-to-end visibility.
  • •Creates blind spots that severely impact troubleshooting and root cause analysis.

Over-instrumentation & Sampling

  • •Collecting too much verbose data (e.g., debug logs in production) without proper filtering.
  • •Leads to increased costs and noise, making it hard to find relevant information.
  • •Understand and adjust adaptive sampling to ensure critical data is not missed while managing volume.

Concrete Example

If a critical payment gateway microservice isn't instrumented, the Application Map will show a broken link, making it impossible to diagnose payment failures. Conversely, sending every debug log entry from production can quickly exhaust your Application Insights budget and obscure real issues.

Key Takeaway

Ensure all critical components are instrumented, and manage data volume effectively through proper filtering and understanding of sampling to avoid blind spots and excessive costs.
