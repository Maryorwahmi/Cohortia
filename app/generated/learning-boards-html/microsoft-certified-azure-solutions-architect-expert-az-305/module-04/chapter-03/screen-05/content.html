Architectural Design

Designing for Scale and Compliance

Common Log Analytics Workspace Architectures and Their Trade-offs

Centralized Workspace

A single workspace for the entire organization. Simplifies management but can lead to data noise and RBAC complexity.

  • Easier initial setup
  • Consolidated billing
  • Potential for RBAC challenges

Multiple Workspaces

Workspaces per department or application. Offers better isolation and cost attribution but higher management overhead.

  • Strong data isolation
  • Clear cost attribution
  • Increased management complexity

Hybrid Approach

Central workspace for security/governance, application-specific for operational logs. Balances isolation and management.

  • Balanced control
  • Optimized for specific needs
  • Requires careful planning
Key Takeaway

The choice of Log Analytics Workspace architecture depends on organizational size, regulatory needs, and data sovereignty, balancing management simplicity with isolation and cost attribution.
