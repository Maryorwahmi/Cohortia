Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Azure Event Hubs

A highly scalable data streaming platform for ingesting large volumes of events from various sources.

Azure Automation Runbooks

Scripts that can be triggered by Azure Monitor alerts to perform automated operational tasks.

Azure Logic Apps

A cloud service that helps schedule, automate, and orchestrate tasks, business processes, and workflows.

Power BI

A business intelligence service providing interactive visualizations and capabilities for custom reports and dashboards.

Summary Takeaway

These services form the backbone of advanced, integrated, and automated monitoring and reporting solutions in Azure.
