Avoiding Common Monitoring Mistakes

Ensuring effective and secure custom monitoring solutions

  • •

    Dashboard Complexity: Avoid clutter; design for specific roles/use cases.

  • •

    Alert Fatigue: Ensure alerts have clear owners and defined response plans.

  • •

    Security Oversight: Apply RBAC and least privilege to all custom solutions accessing monitoring data.

  • •

    Lack of Version Control: Manage workbooks, KQL, and automation scripts in source control.

Key Takeaway

Proactive avoidance of these mistakes ensures your custom monitoring solutions are robust, secure, and truly valuable.
