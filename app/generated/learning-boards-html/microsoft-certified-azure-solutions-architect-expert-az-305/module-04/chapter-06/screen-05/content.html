Primary Cost Drivers in Azure Monitor

Understanding where your monitoring budget is spent is the first step towards effective cost optimization.

1. Data Ingestion

The volume of data (GB/month) sent to a Log Analytics Workspace is the most significant cost factor. More verbose logs and higher frequency metric collection directly increase costs.

2. Data Retention

Storing data beyond the initial free retention period (typically 31 days) incurs additional costs, which increase with longer retention periods.

3. Data Export

Exporting data to other services like Azure Storage or Event Hubs can have associated costs, though often less significant than ingestion or retention.

4. Queries

Extremely complex or long-running Kusto Query Language (KQL) queries can incur costs, especially in high-volume scenarios or with advanced features.

Key Takeaway

Understanding these four cost drivers is fundamental to designing a cost-optimized monitoring strategy in Azure.
