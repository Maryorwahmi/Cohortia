Understanding Monitoring Trade-offs

Designing an optimal monitoring solution requires a conscious balance between monitoring granularity, data retention, and overall cost.

Granularity vs. Cost

Higher frequency data collection provides more detail but significantly increases ingestion costs.

Retention vs. Storage

Longer data retention enables deeper historical analysis but incurs higher storage expenses.

Factors Influencing Monitoring Design
Workload Criticality
RTO/RPO Objectives
Compliance Requirements
Budget Constraints

Start with reasonable defaults and iterate based on observed performance and cost to find the optimal balance.

Key Takeaway

Monitoring design is a strategic decision requiring careful consideration of trade-offs to align with business needs and budget.
