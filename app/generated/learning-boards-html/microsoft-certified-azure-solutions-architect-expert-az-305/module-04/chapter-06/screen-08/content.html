Optimizing Data Retention Strategies

Reviewing compliance and operational requirements is key to setting appropriate data retention periods and leveraging cost-effective storage solutions.

Assess Retention Needs

Differentiate between compliance-mandated long-term retention and shorter operational retention needs.

Export to Azure Storage

For long-term archival, export logs from Log Analytics to cheaper Azure Blob Storage.

Lifecycle Management for Cost Savings
Log Analytics (Hot Data) - 30/90 Days
Blob Storage (Cool/Archive) - 1+ Year

Use lifecycle policies to automatically move older data to cheaper storage tiers, like Archive.

Key Takeaway

Leverage Azure Storage and lifecycle management for cost-effective long-term log retention, reserving Log Analytics for active data.
