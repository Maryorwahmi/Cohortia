Strategic Perspective

DLP as Part of a Broader Security Strategy

DLP is a critical component, not a standalone solution, requiring integration with other security measures.

Why Integration is Key
  • Layered Defense: Multiple controls protect against diverse threats.
  • Holistic Protection: Covers data from various angles (access, encryption, exfiltration).
  • Resilience: Prevents single points of failure in security.
  • Comprehensive Compliance: Addresses broader regulatory requirements.

DLP

Prevents data exfiltration

Encryption

Protects data at rest and in transit

Access Controls

Restricts unauthorized access

Threat Protection

Defends against cyberattacks

Key Takeaway

DLP is most effective when integrated into a broader, defense-in-depth security strategy that includes access controls, encryption, and threat protection.
