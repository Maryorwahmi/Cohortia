The DLP Lifecycle: Identify, Monitor, Protect

A continuous cycle for data security, ensuring sensitive information is always under organizational control.

01

Identify

Discover and classify sensitive data across all repositories.

02

Monitor

Continuously track data movement and usage for policy violations.

03

Protect

Apply actions like blocking, encrypting, or auditing data movement.

Concrete Example

A DLP solution identifies a document with credit card numbers, monitors an attempt to email it externally, and then blocks the email, alerting security.

Key Takeaway

The DLP lifecycle is a continuous, iterative process of identifying, monitoring, and protecting sensitive data to ensure ongoing security and compliance.
