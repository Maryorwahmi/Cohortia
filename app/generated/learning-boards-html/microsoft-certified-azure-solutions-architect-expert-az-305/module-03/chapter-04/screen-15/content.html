Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms to remember from this chapter

Microsoft Purview Information Protection (MPIP)

The evolution of Azure Information Protection, providing data classification, labeling, and encryption capabilities.

Labels

Metadata applied to documents and emails to indicate their sensitivity and trigger protective actions like encryption or usage restrictions.

Endpoint DLP

DLP capabilities extended to user endpoints (workstations, laptops) to monitor and restrict sensitive data activities directly on devices.

Audit-only Mode

A DLP policy deployment mode where violations are logged but no enforcement actions are taken, used for testing and refinement.

Shadow IT

Unauthorized or unsanctioned use of IT systems, devices, software, applications, and services within an organization.

Summary Takeaway

MPIP and Labels provide crucial data classification and encryption, while Endpoint DLP extends protection to devices, and Audit-only Mode ensures safe policy deployment, all contributing to a robust security posture.
