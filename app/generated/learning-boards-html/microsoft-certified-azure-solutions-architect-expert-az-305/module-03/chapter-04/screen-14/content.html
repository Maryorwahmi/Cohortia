Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Strategies and tools to prevent sensitive information from being exfiltrated or shared inappropriately.

Microsoft Purview

A unified data governance solution that helps organizations manage and govern their data across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

Sensitive Information Types (SITs)

Pre-defined or custom patterns used by DLP solutions to identify specific types of sensitive data (e.g., credit card numbers, national ID numbers).

Summary Takeaway

DLP, powered by Microsoft Purview and its Sensitive Information Types, forms the foundation for identifying and protecting critical data assets across your entire data estate.
