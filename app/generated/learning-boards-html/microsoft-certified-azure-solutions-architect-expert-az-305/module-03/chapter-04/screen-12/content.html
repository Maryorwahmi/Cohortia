Operational Considerations

Common Challenges in DLP Deployment

Navigating complexities for effective and sustainable data protection in Azure.

Mitigation Strategies
  • Balance Security & Usability: Provide clear guidance, not just blocks.
  • Continuous Review: Regularly update policies for evolving data/regulations.
  • Extend to Endpoints: Use Microsoft Defender for Endpoint to cover unmanaged devices.
  • Holistic Security: Integrate DLP with access controls, encryption, and threat protection.

User Experience

Overly restrictive policies hinder productivity.

Policy Maintenance

Policies need regular review and updates.

Shadow IT & Unmanaged Endpoints

Bypass cloud DLP, creating blind spots.

Key Takeaway

Successful DLP deployment requires addressing user experience, maintaining policies, and extending protection to all endpoints to counter shadow IT and evolving threats.
