Understanding Rehydration Costs and TCO

Factoring Retrieval Expenses into Archiving Solutions

What is Rehydration?

  • Process: Moving data from Archive tier back to Hot or Cool for access.
  • Incurs Costs: Charges per gigabyte for data retrieval.
  • Latency: Can take hours to retrieve data, depending on priority.

Impact on Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

Unexpected Expenses
Budget Overruns
Operational Delays

Always model potential retrieval scenarios and their associated costs for a realistic TCO.

Key Takeaway

Rehydration costs are a critical component of TCO for archiving solutions; neglecting them can lead to significant unexpected expenses.
