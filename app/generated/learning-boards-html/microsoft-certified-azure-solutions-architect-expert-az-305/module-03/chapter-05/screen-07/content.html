The Archive Tier: Cost-Effective Cold Storage

Minimizing Expenses for Rarely Accessed, Long-Term Data

Key Characteristics of Archive Tier

  • Lowest Storage Costs: Ideal for massive volumes of cold data.
  • Highest Access Costs & Latency: Retrieval can take hours and incurs 'rehydration' fees.
  • Minimum Retention: Data should be stored for at least 180 days.

When to Use the Archive Tier

Historical Backups
Compliance Archives
Long-Term Audit Logs

Only move data to Archive if it is truly cold and rarely accessed to avoid rehydration costs.

Key Takeaway

The Archive tier is the most cost-effective for long-term, rarely accessed data, but requires careful consideration of rehydration costs and latency.
