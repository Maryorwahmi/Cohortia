Automating Data Tiering with Lifecycle Policies

Efficiently Managing Data Across Storage Tiers

How Lifecycle Management Works

  • Define Rules: Create policies to automatically move or delete blobs.
  • Based on Age/Access: Rules trigger after a certain number of days since last modification or access.
  • Tiering Actions: Move from Hot to Cool, Cool to Archive, or delete.

Example Lifecycle Flow

Data Created (Hot)
After 30 Days: Move to Cool
After 90 More Days: Move to Archive
After 7 Years: Delete

Automated policies reduce manual overhead and ensure compliance with retention schedules.

Key Takeaway

Lifecycle management policies are essential for automating data tiering and deletion, optimizing costs and ensuring compliance without manual intervention.
