The Imperative of Data Retention

Meeting Legal, Regulatory, and Business Demands

Why Data Retention Matters

  • Regulatory Compliance: Adhere to industry-specific laws like HIPAA, GDPR, and PCI DSS.
  • Legal Obligations: Preserve data for potential legal discovery and litigation.
  • Business Needs: Support internal audits, historical analysis, and operational continuity.

Consequences of Non-Compliance

Key Takeaway

Understanding the 'why' behind data retention is fundamental to designing compliant and cost-effective Azure storage solutions.
