Retention Policies: Time-Based and Legal Hold

Flexible Options for Data Immutability

Time-Based Retention

  • Fixed Duration: Data remains immutable for a set period (e.g., 5 years).
  • Non-Reducible: Once set, the period can only be extended, not shortened.
  • Use Case: Regulatory compliance with fixed retention periods.

Legal Hold

  • Indefinite Retention: Data remains immutable until the hold is explicitly cleared.
  • Flexible Management: Can be set and cleared as needed, requiring careful oversight.
  • Use Case: e-Discovery and litigation support.
Key Takeaway

Choose time-based retention for predictable compliance and legal hold for flexible, indefinite preservation during e-discovery.
