Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember (Part 2)

Legal Hold

An immutable storage policy that retains data indefinitely until explicitly released, typically for legal discovery purposes.

Lifecycle Management Policies

Automated rules in Azure Storage to transition blobs between tiers or delete them based on age or access patterns.

Rehydration

The process of moving data from the Archive tier back to a Hot or Cool tier for access, incurring costs and latency.

e-Discovery

The process of identifying, collecting, and producing electronically stored information (ESI) in response to a legal case.
