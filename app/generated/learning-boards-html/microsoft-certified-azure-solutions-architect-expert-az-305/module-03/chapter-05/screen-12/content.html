Managing Legal Holds and e-Discovery

Preserving Data for Litigation and Audits

Legal Holds: The Override

  • Indefinite Preservation: Overrides existing retention policies to preserve data indefinitely.
  • Critical for e-Discovery: Ensures data is available for legal requests.
  • Requires Management: Must be explicitly released when no longer needed.

e-Discovery Challenges with Archived Data

High Rehydration Costs
Increased Retrieval Time
Complex Data Indexing

Always factor rehydration costs into your TCO for archiving solutions, especially for e-discovery.

Key Takeaway

Legal holds are paramount for e-discovery, but architects must plan for the significant costs and time associated with rehydrating archived data.
