Deep Packet Inspection with Azure Firewall TLS Inspection

Decrypting, inspecting, and re-encrypting outbound TLS traffic.

What is TLS Inspection?

TLS inspection (available with Azure Firewall Premium) is a feature that decrypts outbound TLS traffic, inspects its content for threats, and then re-encrypts it before forwarding. This allows for deep packet inspection of encrypted communications.

Benefits & Considerations

  • Threat Detection: Identifies hidden malware, data exfiltration.
  • Enhanced Security: Adds a layer of defense against sophisticated attacks.
  • Certificate Management: Requires careful design and management of certificates.
Key Takeaway

TLS inspection is powerful for threat detection in encrypted traffic but demands meticulous certificate management.
