Data in Transit: Why Encryption Matters

Protecting information as it moves across networks.

What is Data in Transit?

Data in transit, or data in motion, refers to any data actively moving from one location to another. This includes data traversing public networks, flowing between applications, or moving between on-premises environments and cloud services like Azure.

Key Vulnerabilities

  • Eavesdropping: Unauthorized interception of data.
  • Tampering: Malicious alteration or corruption of data.
  • Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) Attacks: Intercepting and relaying communications between two parties.
Key Takeaway

Securing data in transit is as critical as securing data at rest to maintain confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity.
