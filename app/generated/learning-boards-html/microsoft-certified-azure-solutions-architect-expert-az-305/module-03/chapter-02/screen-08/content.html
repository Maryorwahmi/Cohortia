Azure ExpressRoute: Dedicated Private Connections

High-bandwidth, low-latency connectivity bypassing the public internet.

What is Azure ExpressRoute?

ExpressRoute provides a private, dedicated connection between your on-premises infrastructure and Azure over a connectivity provider. It bypasses the public internet, offering superior reliability, lower latency, and higher bandwidth.

Key Advantages

  • Higher Bandwidth: Up to 100 Gbps.
  • Lower Latency: Predictable and consistent network performance.
  • Enhanced Reliability: Dedicated private connection, not subject to internet conditions.
  • Bypasses Public Internet: Reduces exposure to internet-based threats.
Key Takeaway

Even with ExpressRoute, consider additional encryption (e.g., IPsec tunnels) for highly sensitive data.
