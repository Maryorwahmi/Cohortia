Granular Data Access Control with Azure RBAC

Managing who can do what, where, across your Azure resources.

What is Azure RBAC?

Azure Role-Based Access Control is Azure's authorization system for managing access to resources. It defines who can access resources, what actions they can perform, and at what scope.

Principle of Least Privilege

A core security best practice: grant only the minimum necessary permissions for users or services to perform their tasks. This reduces the potential impact of a security breach.

Scope of Application

RBAC permissions can be applied at various levels: subscription, resource group, or individual resource. This allows for highly flexible and precise control over access.
