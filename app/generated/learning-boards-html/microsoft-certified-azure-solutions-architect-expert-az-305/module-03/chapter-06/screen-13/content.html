Balancing Data Utility and Privacy

Navigating the critical trade-offs in data protection solutions.

The Balancing Act

  • •More aggressive anonymization = stronger privacy, but lower data utility.
  • •Less stringent masking = higher data utility, but increased privacy risks.
  • •Requires collaboration with data owners, developers, and compliance teams.
  • •Different environments may require different levels of protection.
Key Takeaway

Achieving the right balance between data utility and privacy is crucial; it requires careful assessment of use cases and regulatory requirements.
