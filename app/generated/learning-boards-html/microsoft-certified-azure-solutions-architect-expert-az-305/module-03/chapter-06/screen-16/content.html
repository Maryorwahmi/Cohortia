Key Concepts: Advanced Protection & Strategic Considerations

Understanding advanced techniques, trade-offs, and risks in data privacy.

Pseudonymization

A form of anonymization where direct identifiers are replaced with artificial identifiers, potentially reversible with additional information.

Data Utility

The usefulness of data for its intended purpose (e.g., analysis, testing) after masking or anonymization.

Re-identification Risk

The risk that anonymized data can be combined with other information to identify individuals.

Summary Takeaway

Architects must strategically balance data utility with privacy, carefully considering pseudonymization and mitigating re-identification risks to build resilient solutions.
