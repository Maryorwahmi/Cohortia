Anonymization Technique

Pseudonymization: A Reversible Anonymization Approach

Replacing direct identifiers with artificial ones, with potential for re-identification.

Key Characteristics

  • •Direct identifiers replaced with artificial identifiers (pseudonyms).
  • •Potential for re-identification by combining with other information (e.g., a key).
  • •Offers a balance between strong privacy and data utility for specific analyses.
  • •Requires careful management of the re-identification key.
Key Takeaway

Pseudonymization provides enhanced privacy while allowing controlled re-identification for specific, authorized purposes, balancing utility and protection.
