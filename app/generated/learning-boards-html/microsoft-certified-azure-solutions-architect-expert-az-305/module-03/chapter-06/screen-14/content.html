Common Pitfalls

Avoiding Re-identification Risk and Other Masking Mistakes

Identifying and mitigating common challenges in data protection solutions.

Key Pitfalls to Avoid

  • •**One-size-fits-all approach:** Assuming a single technique works for all environments.
  • •**Ignoring re-identification risk:** Failing to consider combining anonymized data with external datasets.
  • •**Inadequate testing:** Not verifying masking effectiveness for all user roles and scenarios.
  • •**Lack of collaboration:** Not involving data owners, developers, and compliance teams.
Key Takeaway

Proactive risk assessment, tailored solutions, and continuous vigilance are essential to prevent re-identification and ensure robust data privacy.
