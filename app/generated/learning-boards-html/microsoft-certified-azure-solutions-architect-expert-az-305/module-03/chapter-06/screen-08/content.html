Core Concept

Data Anonymization: Irreversible Privacy Protection

Removing or obscuring PII to prevent individual identification.

What is Anonymization?

  • •Comprehensive process to remove or obscure PII.
  • •Goal: Prevent direct or indirect identification of individuals.
  • •Typically involves irreversible transformations of data.
  • •Crucial for compliance with strict privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR).
Key Takeaway

Anonymization provides the strongest form of privacy protection by making re-identification impossible, essential for high-risk data sharing and analytics.
