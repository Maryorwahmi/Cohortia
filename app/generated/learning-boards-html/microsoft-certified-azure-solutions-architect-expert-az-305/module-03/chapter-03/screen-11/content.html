Safeguarding Against Accidental Deletion

Understanding Soft-Delete and Purge Protection

Soft-Delete: Your Safety Net

  • Retention Period: Retains deleted items for a configurable period (default 90 days).
  • Recovery: Allows recovery of accidentally deleted keys, secrets, or certificates.
  • Prevents Data Loss: Acts as a crucial safeguard against human error.
  • Default Behavior: Enabled by default on new Key Vaults.
Key Takeaway

Soft-delete and purge protection are vital for preventing accidental or malicious permanent deletion of Key Vault items.

Purge Protection: Enhanced Immutability

Prevents Permanent Deletion

Even privileged users cannot permanently delete items during soft-delete period.

Protection Against Insider Threats

Adds a critical layer of defense against malicious actions.

Compliance Mandates

Helps meet regulatory requirements for data retention and protection.

Enable both for maximum protection of your Key Vault contents.
