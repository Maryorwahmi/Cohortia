Alternative Solution

Azure Virtual WAN

Azure Virtual WAN is a networking service that provides optimized, automated, and global connectivity for large-scale branch-to-branch, branch-to-Azure, and VNet-to-VNet connectivity.

  • •Managed Hub-and-Spoke: Simplifies complex network designs with a managed global transit network.
  • •Built-in Transitive Routing: Eliminates the need for manual UDRs for spoke-to-spoke or spoke-to-on-premises communication.
  • •Centralized Connectivity: Integrates VPN, ExpressRoute, and point-to-site VPN into a single operational pane.
Key Takeaway

Virtual WAN is ideal for enterprise-scale, global networks, offering a simplified and scalable approach to advanced connectivity compared to manual VNet peering configurations.

Virtual WAN vs. VNet Peering

VNet Peering

Best for simpler, localized VNet-to-VNet connectivity. Requires manual UDRs for transitive routing. Cost-effective for fewer connections.

Azure Virtual WAN

Ideal for complex, global, and large-scale networks. Offers managed transitive routing and centralized connectivity for various endpoints. Higher initial cost but simplifies management at scale.

When to Choose Virtual WAN

Consider Virtual WAN when you have many spoke VNets, multiple regions, extensive hybrid connectivity requirements, or need simplified global transit routing.
