Front Door: Anycast, Edge WAF, and Caching

Deep dive into global load balancing, SSL offloading at the edge, and caching

  • Global Load Balancing: Routes traffic to the fastest and most available backend based on latency and health probes.

  • SSL Offloading at the Edge: Terminates SSL connections at the closest edge location, reducing latency and offloading backend servers.

  • Web Application Firewall (WAF): Provides centralized WAF protection at the edge, before traffic reaches your Azure region.

Key Takeaway

Front Door's combination of global load balancing, edge WAF, and caching makes it ideal for accelerating and securing web applications worldwide.
