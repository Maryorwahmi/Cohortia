Continuous Auditing and Improvement

Establishing a framework for ongoing network governance

Key Areas for Continuous Review

  • Azure Policy Compliance: Monitor reports and remediate non-compliant resources.
  • Security Posture: Conduct regular audits of NSG rules, firewall policies, and access controls.
  • Cost Reports: Analyze network-related costs to identify areas for optimization.
  • Performance Metrics: Review trends and adjust resource sizing or architecture as needed.
Key Takeaway

Continuous auditing and improvement are vital for maintaining a secure, compliant, cost-effective, and high-performing Azure network architecture over time.

The Cycle of Improvement

Monitor & Audit

Regularly check compliance, security, cost, and performance.

Analyze & Identify

Pinpoint areas for optimization, remediation, or architectural changes.

Implement & Document

Apply changes, update documentation, and restart the monitoring cycle.

Example Scenario

A quarterly review of Azure Security Center recommendations leads to the refinement of NSG rules and the implementation of a new Azure Policy to enforce stricter access controls.
