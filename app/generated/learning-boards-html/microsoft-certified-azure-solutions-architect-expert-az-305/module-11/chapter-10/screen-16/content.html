Key Concepts (Continued)

Advanced principles for resilient and optimized networks

Zone Redundancy

Deploying resources across multiple Azure Availability Zones to protect against single zone failures.

Regional Paired Regions

Azure regions paired for disaster recovery planning and data residency, enabling multi-region architectures.

Redundant Connectivity

Implementing multiple paths or services for network connectivity to ensure high availability and no single point of failure.

Chapter Summary

Designing for resilience involves zone redundancy, leveraging regional paired regions for disaster recovery, and implementing redundant connectivity for hybrid environments.
