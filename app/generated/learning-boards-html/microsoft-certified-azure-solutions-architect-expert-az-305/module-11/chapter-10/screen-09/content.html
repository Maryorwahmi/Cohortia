Regional Paired Regions & Redundant Connectivity

Building multi-region architectures for business continuity

Regional Paired Regions for Disaster Recovery

  • Design multi-region architectures using Azure's paired regions.
  • Utilize Azure Traffic Manager or Front Door for global traffic failover between regions.
  • Ensure data replication and application synchronization across regions.
Key Takeaway

Multi-region designs with paired regions and redundant connectivity are essential for robust disaster recovery and maintaining business continuity.

Redundant Hybrid Connectivity

Multiple ExpressRoute Circuits

Implement diverse paths for dedicated connectivity to Azure.

ExpressRoute + VPN Gateway

Combine dedicated and encrypted internet-based connectivity for failover.

Example Scenario

An application deployed in East US and West US, using Azure Front Door to direct users to the healthy region, with two ExpressRoute circuits connecting on-premises to each region.
