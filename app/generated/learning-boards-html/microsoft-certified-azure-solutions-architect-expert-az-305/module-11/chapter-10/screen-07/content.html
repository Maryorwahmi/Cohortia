Naming Conventions and Tagging

Organizing and managing your Azure network resources effectively

Importance of Naming Conventions

  • Easy Identification: Quickly understand resource purpose, environment, and location.
  • Simplified Management: Easier to filter, search, and manage resources in the portal or CLI.
  • Improved Troubleshooting: Faster diagnosis of issues by quickly locating relevant components.
Key Takeaway

Consistent naming and comprehensive tagging are foundational for managing complex Azure environments, enabling better organization, cost control, and policy enforcement.

Benefits of Resource Tagging

Cost Management

Categorize resources by cost center or department for accurate billing and chargeback.

Resource Organization

Group resources by environment (prod, dev), owner, or application for better visibility.

Policy Scoping

Apply Azure Policies to resources based on their tags, enabling granular governance.

Example Tagging

Tagging all resources with Environment:Production and Owner:Finance allows for easy filtering and cost tracking for the Finance department's production resources.
