Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from network governance and best practices

Azure Policy

A service to create, assign, and manage policies that enforce rules and effects over Azure resources, crucial for network governance.

Naming Conventions

Standardized rules for naming resources to improve identification, organization, and management.

Resource Tagging

Applying metadata (key-value pairs) to Azure resources for categorization, cost management, and policy enforcement.

Chapter Summary

Designing for network governance involves leveraging Azure Policy for compliance, implementing clear naming and tagging strategies for organization, and continuously auditing for improvement.
