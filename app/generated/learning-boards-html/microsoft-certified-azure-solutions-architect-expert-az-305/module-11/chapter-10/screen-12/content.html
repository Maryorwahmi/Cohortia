Right-sizing & Resource Clean-up

Further strategies for optimizing network service costs

Right-sizing Network Services

  • Provision network services (gateways, firewalls) with appropriate SKUs.
  • Scale down during off-peak hours if workload patterns allow.
  • Regularly review usage metrics to avoid over-provisioning.
Key Takeaway

Right-sizing and diligent clean-up of unused resources are essential for minimizing unnecessary network costs and maximizing your Azure investment.

Resource Clean-up & Reserved Instances

Clean-up Unused Resources

Identify and delete unused public IPs, load balancers, or VPN gateways to stop incurring charges.

Azure Reserved Instances

Consider for long-running VMs (e.g., NVAs) to gain significant cost savings with commitment.

Example Scenario

After a proof-of-concept, an architect reviews Azure Monitor cost reports and identifies several unused public IP addresses and a VPN Gateway, which are then deleted to reduce monthly expenses.
