AZURE FIREWALL FEATURES

Built-in Availability and Centralized Policy

Simplifying security management at scale.

Built-in High Availability & Scalability

Azure Firewall automatically scales to meet demand without manual intervention. This ensures continuous protection and performance, even during traffic spikes, eliminating the need for complex HA configurations.

Benefit: Reduced operational overhead, consistent performance.

Centralized Policy Management

Manage all firewall rules from a single pane of glass, simplifying security administration across multiple VNets and subscriptions. This ensures consistent policy enforcement.

Benefit: Simplified administration, consistent security posture.
Key Takeaway

Azure Firewall's built-in HA and centralized policy management streamline security operations and ensure robust protection without manual scaling or complex rule distribution.
