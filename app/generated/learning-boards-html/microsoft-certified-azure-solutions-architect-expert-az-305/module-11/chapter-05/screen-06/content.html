AZURE FIREWALL FEATURES

Advanced Filtering and Threat Protection

Granular control and intelligence-driven defense.

FQDN Filtering

Filter outbound HTTP/S traffic based on Fully Qualified Domain Names (FQDNs), not just IP addresses. This is crucial for controlling access to specific SaaS services or external websites with precision.

Use Case: Restricting access to approved external services.

Threat Intelligence-based Filtering

Automatically blocks traffic from known malicious IP addresses and domains, sourced from Microsoft's threat intelligence feed. This provides proactive defense against emerging threats.

Use Case: Automated protection against botnets and malware.
Key Takeaway

Azure Firewall's FQDN filtering and threat intelligence capabilities provide granular control over outbound traffic and automated protection against known malicious sources.
