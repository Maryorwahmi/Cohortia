Azure Firewall vs. Third-Party NVAs

Choosing the right security solution for your needs.

Azure Firewall

  • Pros:
  • Fully managed, highly scalable, built-in HA.
  • Integrates with Azure Monitor/Security Center.
  • FQDN filtering, threat intelligence.
  • Cons:
  • May lack highly specialized features (e.g., deep packet inspection for specific application protocols).
  • Less control over underlying OS.

Third-Party NVAs

  • Pros:
  • Rich feature sets, deep packet inspection.
  • Existing enterprise familiarity, greater control over configuration.
  • Cons:
  • Requires VM management (patching, scaling, HA configuration).
  • Potential licensing costs, complex deployment and routing.
  • Potential performance bottlenecks if not sized correctly.
Key Takeaway

Choose Azure Firewall for a managed, scalable solution with low overhead. Opt for NVAs when specialized features, deep control, or integration with existing vendor ecosystems are paramount, accepting higher management complexity.
