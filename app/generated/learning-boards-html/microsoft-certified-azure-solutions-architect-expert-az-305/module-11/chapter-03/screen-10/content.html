Core Concept

Introducing Azure ExpressRoute

Private, dedicated, high-performance connectivity to Azure.

Key Benefits

  • **Private Connection:** Traffic bypasses the public internet.
  • **High Bandwidth:** Up to 100 Gbps.
  • **Low Latency:** Predictable and consistent network performance.
  • **Enhanced Security:** Dedicated connection reduces exposure.
  • **Compliance:** Meets strict regulatory requirements.
Key Takeaway

ExpressRoute is the premium choice for mission-critical workloads requiring high bandwidth, low latency, and private connectivity.
