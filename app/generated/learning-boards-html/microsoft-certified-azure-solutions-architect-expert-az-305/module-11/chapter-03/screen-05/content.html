VPN Gateway SKUs and High Availability

Choosing the right gateway for performance and resilience.

Key Considerations

  • **Performance:** SKUs (Basic, VpnGw1-5) offer varying aggregate throughput.
  • **Features:** Different SKUs support BGP, ExpressRoute/VPN coexistence, etc.
  • **High Availability (HA):** Zone-redundant (AZ) SKUs for resilience against zonal outages.
  • **Cost:** Higher SKUs and HA options incur higher costs.
Key Takeaway

Always select a zone-redundant (AZ) SKU for production VPN Gateways to ensure maximum resilience and continuous connectivity.

VPN Gateway SKU Comparison
Basic SKU

Dev/Test, lower throughput, no BGP.

VpnGw1-5 SKUs

Production, higher throughput, BGP support.

VpnGw1-5AZ SKUs

Zone-redundant, highest HA for production.

Misconception

Basic SKU is not suitable for production HA needs.

Example Scenario

A global enterprise migrating critical applications to Azure would choose VpnGw3AZ or higher for guaranteed performance and zonal resilience.
