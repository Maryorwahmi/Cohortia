ExpressRoute Redundancy and Safety

Ensuring high availability and secure operations.

Key Design Principles

  • **Multi-Circuit Redundancy:** Provision multiple ExpressRoute circuits for high availability.
  • **VPN Failover:** Use Site-to-Site VPN as a backup for ExpressRoute.
  • **Robust Security:** Implement strong firewall rules and intrusion detection.
  • **Microsoft Peering Safety:** Carefully manage NAT rules and advertised IP prefixes.
  • **BGP Best Practices:** Meticulous planning and testing to prevent routing issues.
Key Takeaway

Combine ExpressRoute with VPN failover and rigorous security measures to build a highly resilient and secure hybrid network.
