Preventing IP Overlap

Strategic Address Space Selection

The Problem: Overlapping IP Ranges

Using common or small IP ranges (e.g., 192.168.1.0/24) can lead to severe routing conflicts when connecting to other networks (on-premises, other VNets).

Consequence: Connectivity issues, complex NAT configurations, and costly re-architecture.

The Solution: Plan for Growth

  • Unique Ranges: Always choose a sufficiently large and unique private IP range.
  • Future-Proof: Account for potential future expansions, mergers, or hybrid connectivity needs.
  • Documentation: Thoroughly document your VNet and subnet IP allocations.
Key Takeaway

Proactive planning with unique, large IP address spaces prevents costly IP conflicts and ensures network scalability.

Good vs. Bad Address Space Choices

Common & Small

192.168.1.0/24

High risk of overlap

Unique & Large

10.0.0.0/16

Low risk, scalable

Why it matters:

Choosing a unique and sufficiently large address space from the start prevents complex network reconfigurations and ensures seamless hybrid connectivity as your cloud footprint grows.
