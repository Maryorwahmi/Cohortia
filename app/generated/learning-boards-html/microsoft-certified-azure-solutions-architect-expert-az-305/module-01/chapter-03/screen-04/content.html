Core Concept

What is Azure Policy?

Establishing and enforcing organizational standards at scale.

Azure Policy

A service to create, assign, and manage policies that enforce organizational standards and assess compliance.

Compliance
Security
Purpose

Azure Policy establishes and enforces organizational standards at scale, ensuring resources comply with defined rules and best practices.

How it Works

It evaluates your Azure resources against policy definitions, describing conditions for evaluation and the effect if conditions are met.

Key Benefits

Ensures compliance with corporate requirements, regulatory standards, and security best practices across your entire Azure estate.

Beyond Prevention

Can audit existing resources, automatically deploy required configurations, or modify resources to bring them into compliance.

Key Takeaway

Azure Policy is essential for automated, large-scale governance, ensuring your cloud environment is consistently compliant and secure.
