Key Concepts

More essential terms to remember.

Policy Assignment

The act of applying a policy definition or initiative definition to a specific scope (management group, subscription, resource group).

Policy Effect

The action taken when a resource meets the conditions of a policy definition (e.g., Audit, Deny, DeployIfNotExists, Modify).

Remediation Task

An action to bring existing non-compliant resources into compliance with a DeployIfNotExists or Modify policy.

Summary Takeaway

Policy assignments activate rules, effects define the outcome, and remediation ensures continuous compliance for existing resources.
