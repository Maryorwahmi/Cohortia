Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Azure Policy

A service that helps enforce organizational standards and assess compliance at scale across your Azure environment.

Policy Definition

A JSON-based rule that specifies the conditions for evaluation and the effect to take if conditions are met.

Initiative Definition

A collection of related policy definitions grouped together for simplified assignment and management.

Summary Takeaway

Azure Policy provides the framework for defining, grouping, and enforcing governance rules across your cloud environment.
