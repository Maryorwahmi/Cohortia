Management Groups: Governing at Scale

Applying policies and RBAC across multiple subscriptions simultaneously.

Definition

Management Groups are containers that provide a level of scope above subscriptions, allowing for centralized management and policy application across multiple subscriptions.

Key Benefits
  • Centralized Governance: Apply policies and RBAC roles to many subscriptions at once.
  • Hierarchy: Organize subscriptions into a logical tree structure for easier management.
  • Inheritance: Policies and roles applied at the management group level are inherited by all child subscriptions.
Example Scenario

Create 'Production' and 'Development' management groups. Apply a strict security policy to the 'Production' group, ensuring all production subscriptions automatically adhere to it.

Key Takeaway

Management Groups are essential for applying consistent governance, policies, and access controls across large, multi-subscription Azure environments.
