Key Concepts Revisited

Reinforcing the foundational ideas of Azure resource organization.

Naming Conventions

Standardized rules for naming Azure resources to ensure consistency, clarity, and ease of identification.

Tagging Strategy

A plan for applying metadata (key-value pairs) to Azure resources for cost allocation, reporting, and policy enforcement.

Resource Lock

A feature in Azure that prevents accidental deletion or modification of critical resources, with types CanNotDelete and ReadOnly.

Summary Takeaway

Implementing consistent naming, strategic tagging, and robust resource locks are fundamental practices for maintaining a well-governed and secure Azure environment.
