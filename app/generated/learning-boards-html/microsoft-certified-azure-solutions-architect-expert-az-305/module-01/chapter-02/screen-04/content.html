Foundations of Azure Resource Hierarchy

Understanding the layered structure of Management Groups, Subscriptions, and Resource Groups.

Management Groups
Subscriptions
Resource Groups
Key Components
  • Management Groups: Top-level containers for subscriptions, enabling policy and RBAC inheritance.
  • Subscriptions: Billing units and logical boundaries for resources, often used for environment segregation.
  • Resource Groups: Granular containers for resources sharing a common lifecycle within a subscription.
Why it Matters

A well-designed hierarchy simplifies governance, streamlines cost management, enhances security, and ensures compliance across your entire Azure estate.

Key Takeaway

The Azure resource hierarchy provides a foundational structure for applying consistent governance and management practices at scale.
