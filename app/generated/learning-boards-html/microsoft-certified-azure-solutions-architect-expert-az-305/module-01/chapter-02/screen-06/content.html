Subscriptions: Billing and Logical Boundaries

Segregating environments and managing different cost centers.

Definition

Azure Subscriptions are billing units and logical containers for resources, serving as a boundary for management, billing, and policy application.

Common Use Cases
  • Environment Segregation: Separate subscriptions for Production, Development, and Testing.
  • Cost Management: Allocate costs to specific departments or projects.
  • Resource Quotas: Manage resource limits and scale for large deployments.
Example Scenario

A company uses one subscription for its 'Marketing' department to track their cloud spend, and another for 'Engineering' to isolate development workloads.

Key Takeaway

Subscriptions are critical for managing billing, isolating environments, and enforcing logical boundaries within your Azure cloud footprint.
