Deep Dive: Management Groups

Centralizing governance above individual subscriptions

Key Characteristics

  • Containers for Azure subscriptions.
  • Enable policy and access control application at a level above subscriptions.
  • Facilitate centralized management for large organizations.
  • Policies inherit down to all contained subscriptions and resources.

Benefits

  • Simplified policy enforcement.
  • Consistent compliance across the entire Azure estate.
  • Streamlined cost management through aggregated views.
  • Improved organizational structure for cloud resources.
Concrete Example

A 'Production' Management Group applies a stringent security policy requiring all VMs to use managed disks and be encrypted, ensuring all production subscriptions adhere to this standard.
