Common Governance Challenges & Solutions

Avoiding pitfalls and building resilient frameworks

Common Challenges

  • Lack of Stakeholder Involvement: Policies don't meet business needs.
  • Over-engineering: Too many restrictive policies stifle innovation.
  • Under-governance: Leads to cloud sprawl, security risks, and cost overruns.
  • Static Frameworks: Governance not evolving with cloud environment.
Common Mistake

Implementing a 'deny all' policy without proper exceptions can block critical operations and halt development, leading to frustration and workarounds.

Mitigation Strategies

  • Early Stakeholder Engagement: Involve finance, security, and operations.
  • Iterative Refinement: Start foundational, then evolve policies.
  • Balanced Approach: Strike a balance between control and innovation.
  • Continuous Review: Regularly update policies as the environment changes.
Key Takeaway

Governance is an ongoing, iterative process that requires balancing control with innovation and continuous adaptation to be effective.

Pro-Tip

Always test new governance policies thoroughly in non-production environments to prevent unintended operational disruptions.
