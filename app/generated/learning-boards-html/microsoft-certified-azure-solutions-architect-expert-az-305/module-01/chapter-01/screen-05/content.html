Defining Azure Governance

What it means to manage and control your cloud estate

Key Aspects of Governance

  • Who can do what: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and identity management.
  • Where resources can be deployed: Geographic restrictions and resource location policies.
  • How resources are configured: Enforcing security settings, naming conventions, and tagging.
  • How costs are managed: Budgeting, cost allocation, and resource optimization.
Concrete Example

A policy preventing the deployment of virtual machines in regions outside the EU to comply with data residency regulations.
