Introducing the Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF)

Microsoft's blueprint for successful cloud adoption

What is CAF?

  • A comprehensive set of guidance, tools, and best practices.
  • Helps organizations adopt and manage cloud environments effectively.
  • Covers the entire cloud journey from strategy to operations.

Governance within CAF

  • A distinct discipline focused on managing and monitoring cloud adherence.
  • Ensures alignment with corporate policies and regulatory standards.
  • Emphasizes governance as an integral, not an afterthought, part of cloud strategy.
Concrete Example

A company uses CAF to structure its migration, ensuring governance policies are defined before any production workloads are moved to Azure.
