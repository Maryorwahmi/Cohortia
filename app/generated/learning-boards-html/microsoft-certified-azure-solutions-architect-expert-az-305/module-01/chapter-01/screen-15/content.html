Key Concepts

Reinforcing your Azure governance knowledge

Subscription

A logical container for Azure resources that serves as a billing unit and a boundary for resource deployment and management.

Resource Group

A logical container for related Azure resources that share the same lifecycle, simplifying deployment, management, and deletion.

Policy Initiative

A collection of Azure Policy definitions grouped together to achieve a single overarching goal, simplifying assignment and management.

Summary Takeaway

These constructs are fundamental for organizing, managing, and enforcing consistent governance across your Azure environment.
