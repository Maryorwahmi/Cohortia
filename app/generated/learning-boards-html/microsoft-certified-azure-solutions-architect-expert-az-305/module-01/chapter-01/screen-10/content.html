Azure Resource Hierarchy Overview

Organizing your cloud resources for effective governance

Why Hierarchy Matters

  • Policy Inheritance: Policies applied at higher levels flow down.
  • Centralized Management: Easier to manage large-scale environments.
  • Access Control: Granular control over who can access what.
  • Cost Allocation: Better tracking and management of cloud spending.
Concrete Example

Applying a 'deny public IP' policy at a Management Group level ensures no contained subscription or resource group can deploy public IPs, enforcing a security standard.

Azure Hierarchy Levels
Tenant Root GroupEntire Azure AD tenant
Management GroupsContainers for subscriptions
SubscriptionsBilling boundary, resource container
Resource GroupsLogical container for related resources
Key Takeaway

A well-designed Azure resource hierarchy is crucial for effective policy inheritance, access management, and cost control across your cloud estate.
