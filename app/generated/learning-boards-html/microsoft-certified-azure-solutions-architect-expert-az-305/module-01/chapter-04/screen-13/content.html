Avoiding Pitfalls in RBAC Design

Key considerations for robust access control.

Common RBAC Mistakes

  • Over-privileging: Granting 'Owner' or 'Contributor' roles too broadly.
  • Individual Assignments: Using individual users instead of Azure AD groups.
  • No Regular Reviews: Permissions accumulate, leading to 'privilege creep'.
  • Confusing Roles: Mixing Azure AD roles with Azure RBAC roles.

Safety Notes for RBAC Design

  • Test in Non-Prod: Always test new role assignments in a non-production environment.
  • Break-Glass Account: Maintain a highly secured emergency account with high privileges.
  • Document & Review: Document all role assignments and review them periodically.
Key Takeaway

Proactive management, adherence to least privilege, and regular auditing are essential to prevent common RBAC misconfigurations and maintain strong security.

Impact of Mistakes

Increased Security Risk

Over-privileging can lead to unauthorized access or data breaches.

Management Overhead

Individual assignments and lack of reviews create administrative burden.

Compliance Issues

Failure to adhere to least privilege can lead to audit failures.
