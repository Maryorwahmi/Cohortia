Key Concepts: Advanced RBAC & Integration

Expanding your RBAC knowledge for complex scenarios.

Custom Role

A user-defined role definition with specific, granular permissions.

Scope

The level at which a role assignment applies (Management Group, Subscription, Resource Group, Resource).

Azure Active Directory (Azure AD)

Microsoft's cloud-based identity and access management service that manages security principals.

Just-In-Time (JIT) Access

Temporary, time-bound access to privileged roles, often managed by Azure AD Privileged Identity Management (PIM).

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging custom roles, precise scope, Azure AD integration, and JIT access empowers architects to build highly secure and compliant Azure environments.
