Azure Policy Compliance Dashboard

The Azure Policy compliance dashboard offers a centralized view of your environment's adherence to assigned policies.

  • •Centralized View: See overall compliance status, assigned policies, and compliant vs. non-compliant resources.
  • •Drill-down Capabilities: Identify specific non-compliant resources and the exact reasons for their non-compliance.
  • •Remediation Tracking: Monitor the status of 'DeployIfNotExists' or 'Modify' policy remediation tasks.
  • •Proactive Governance: Crucial for identifying trends, addressing systemic issues, and ensuring policy effectiveness.
Practical Application

Integrating into Operations

Architects should integrate regular dashboard reviews into operational procedures and configure automated alerts for critical policy violations.

Scenario Example

An organization has a policy requiring all storage accounts to use HTTPS. The compliance dashboard shows 10 non-compliant storage accounts. An architect can drill down to identify these accounts and initiate remediation, ensuring secure data transfer.

Key Takeaway

The Azure Policy compliance dashboard is essential for proactive governance, providing actionable insights into resource adherence to defined standards.
