Strategy Design

Designing a Comprehensive Auditing and Monitoring Strategy: Part 3

Focus on robust reporting and continuous reviews for ongoing compliance and security.

  • •5. Reporting: Utilize Defender for Cloud for regulatory compliance reports and Sentinel for security incident reporting.
  • •6. Regular Reviews: Periodically review compliance dashboards, audit logs, and security recommendations.
  • •Continuous Improvement: Adapt strategy to evolving threats and compliance requirements based on review findings.
Ensuring Effectiveness

Adaptation and Validation

Reporting provides demonstrable proof of compliance, while regular reviews ensure the strategy remains effective and responsive to new challenges.

Concrete Example

A quarterly review of Defender for Cloud's regulatory compliance dashboard reveals a new recommendation for a specific resource type. The team then updates Azure Policy to address this, ensuring ongoing compliance with the latest benchmarks.

Key Takeaway

Reporting and regular reviews close the loop on your monitoring strategy, ensuring continuous adaptation and validation against evolving security and compliance needs.
