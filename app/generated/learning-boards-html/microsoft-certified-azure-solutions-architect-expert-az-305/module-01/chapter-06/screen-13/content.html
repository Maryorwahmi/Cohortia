Best Practices & Pitfalls

Common Mistakes and Safety Notes in Auditing

Avoid common errors and ensure the security and cost-effectiveness of your audit data.

  • •Data Graveyards: Collecting logs without a plan for analysis or alerting makes them useless.
  • •Retention Policy Failure: Incorrectly defined retention leads to excessive costs or non-compliance.
  • •Access Control: Strictly control access to audit logs and monitoring tools via RBAC due to sensitive information.
  • •Cost Implications: Be mindful of logging costs; choose appropriate log levels and retention periods.
Ensuring Integrity

Balancing Security and Efficiency

Effective auditing requires not just collecting data, but also securing it, analyzing it, and managing its lifecycle efficiently.

Concrete Example

A company initially logs everything at a verbose level and retains it indefinitely, leading to huge storage bills. By implementing a tiered retention policy and filtering non-critical logs, they reduce costs while maintaining compliance for essential data.

Key Takeaway

Proactive planning for log analysis, retention, access control, and cost management is crucial for an effective and secure auditing strategy.
