Blueprints vs. ARM Templates

Understanding their distinct roles in Azure deployment.

ARM Templates: Declarative language to define and deploy individual Azure resources or groups of resources.

Azure Blueprints: Higher-level orchestration service that composes entire environments, including resource groups, ARM templates, policy assignments, and RBAC.

Blueprints leverage ARM templates but provide a more comprehensive solution for environment standardization and governance.

Key Takeaway

ARM templates are for deploying resources, while Azure Blueprints orchestrate the deployment of entire standardized environments, incorporating ARM templates, policies, and RBAC.

Analogy

An ARM template is like a blueprint for a single room; an Azure Blueprint is the architectural plan for an entire house, including its structure, codes, and responsibilities.
