Blueprint vs. ARM vs. Policy

Clarifying their roles in Azure governance.

ARM Templates: Define and deploy individual Azure resources.

Azure Policy: Enforce governance rules and compliance standards.

Azure Blueprints: Orchestrate entire environments, composing ARM templates, policy assignments, and RBAC.

Blueprints integrate these tools for repeatable, compliant deployments.

Key Takeaway

Azure Blueprints provide a higher-level orchestration layer that integrates ARM templates for deployment and Azure Policy for governance, ensuring standardized and compliant environments.

Analogy

ARM templates are ingredients, Policy enforces cooking rules, and Blueprints are the recipe book for creating entire meals consistently.
