Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

High-Performance Computing (HPC)

The use of supercomputers and computer clusters to solve advanced computation problems, typically involving massive parallel processing.

N-series VMs

Azure Virtual Machines optimized for GPU-intensive workloads, featuring NVIDIA GPUs, ideal for deep learning, graphics rendering, and scientific simulations.

H-series VMs

Azure Virtual Machines optimized for high-end CPU-intensive workloads, featuring fast CPUs, large memory, and often InfiniBand networking, ideal for traditional HPC applications.

Summary Takeaway

Azure provides specialized compute resources like N-series and H-series VMs to meet the diverse demands of High-Performance Computing workloads.
