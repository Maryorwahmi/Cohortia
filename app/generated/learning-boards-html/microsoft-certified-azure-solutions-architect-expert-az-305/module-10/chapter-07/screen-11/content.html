The Role of InfiniBand in Tightly Coupled HPC

Enabling ultra-low latency communication between compute nodes for demanding applications.

What is InfiniBand?

InfiniBand is a high-bandwidth, low-latency communication technology crucial for tightly coupled HPC applications that require frequent and fast communication between nodes.

  • •Provides significantly higher throughput and lower latency than standard Ethernet.
  • •Essential for applications where compute nodes must exchange data rapidly and continuously.
  • •Supported by many H-series and some N-series Azure VMs.
Key Takeaway

InfiniBand is vital for tightly coupled HPC workloads, ensuring high-speed, low-latency communication between nodes for optimal performance.

Why InfiniBand is Critical

Avoids Bottlenecks

Prevents network communication from becoming the limiting factor in tightly coupled simulations.

Enables Scalability

Allows HPC applications to scale across hundreds or thousands of cores effectively.

Optimizes Performance

Ensures applications achieve their maximum potential speed and efficiency.

Common Mistake

Overlooking InfiniBand requirements for tightly coupled HPC applications, leading to severe performance degradation.
