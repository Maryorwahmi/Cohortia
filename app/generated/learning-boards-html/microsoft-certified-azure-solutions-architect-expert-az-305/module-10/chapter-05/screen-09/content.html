Choosing the Right Hosting Plan

Balance cost, performance, and features for your workload.

Consumption Plan

  • Cost: Lowest, pay-per-execution.
  • Performance: Can experience cold starts.
  • Features: Basic, automatic scaling to zero.

Best for: Spiky, unpredictable workloads where cold starts are acceptable.

Premium Plan

  • Cost: Higher minimum cost due to always-on instances.
  • Performance: No cold starts, consistent low latency.
  • Features: VNet integration, longer execution, custom containers.

Best for: Production-grade, latency-sensitive applications requiring VNet integration.

App Service Plan

  • Cost: Predictable, pay for VMs 24/7.
  • Performance: Consistent, dedicated resources.
  • Features: Consolidate with other App Service apps, specific App Service features.

Best for: Existing App Service infrastructure or specific App Service feature requirements.

Key Takeaway

The choice of hosting plan is a strategic decision. Evaluate your workload's cost sensitivity, performance demands, and integration needs to select the most appropriate plan.
