Durable Functions: Practical Use Cases

Solve complex business problems with stateful serverless workflows.

Common Scenarios

  • Order Fulfillment: Orchestrate multi-step processes like payment, inventory, and shipping.
  • Multi-Step Approvals: Manage workflows that require human interaction or external approvals.
  • Complex Data Processing: Coordinate long-running data transformations and aggregations.
Key Takeaway

Durable Functions are essential for building resilient, stateful serverless solutions for complex, long-running business processes that require reliable orchestration.

Example: Image Processing Pipeline

Step 1: Download Image (Activity Function).

Step 2: Resize/Watermark (Activity Function).

Step 3: Upload Processed Image (Activity Function).

Challenge

Without Durable Functions, managing state and retries across these steps would require significant custom code and complexity.
