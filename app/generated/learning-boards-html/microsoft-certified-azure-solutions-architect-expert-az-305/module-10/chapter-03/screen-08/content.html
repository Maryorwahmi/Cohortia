Deployment Slots: Traffic Routing & A/B Testing

Gradual rollouts and experimentation with production traffic.

Traffic Routing

Direct a percentage of production traffic to a staging slot for testing new features.

A/B Testing

Compare different versions of your app with real users to gather feedback and data.

Key Takeaway

Traffic routing with deployment slots enables A/B testing and gradual rollouts, reducing risk for new features.

Traffic Routing Example

1

Deploy New Feature to Staging

e.g., mywebapp-staging.azurewebsites.net

2

Route 10% Traffic to Staging

az webapp traffic-routing set --name mywebapp --resource-group myrg --slot staging --percent 10

3

Monitor & Gather Feedback

Observe performance and user behavior.

4

Full Rollout or Revert

Based on results, swap or remove routing.
