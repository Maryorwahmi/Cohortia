Custom Domains & SSL Certificates

Establishing a professional and secure online identity.

Custom Domains

Map your own domain name (e.g., www.yourcompany.com) to your App Service app for branding.

SSL Certificates

Encrypt communication between clients and your application, protecting sensitive data. Azure provides free managed certificates.

Key Takeaway

Custom domains provide branding, while SSL certificates encrypt traffic, ensuring a professional and secure web presence.

Secure Your Domain

Steps to a professional and encrypted web presence.

1. Acquire Custom Domain

e.g., yourcompany.com

2. Map to App Service

Configure DNS records (CNAME/A) to point to your App Service.

3. Bind SSL Certificate

Use Azure-managed free certificate or upload your own.
