VNet Integration: App Service Environment (ASE)

Dedicated and isolated deployments for highly secure workloads.

What is ASE?

A single-tenant deployment of Azure App Service that runs directly within your VNet, providing full network isolation.

Use Cases

Highly secure, large-scale enterprise workloads with stringent compliance requirements.

Common Mistake

Exposing backend services publicly when VNet integration (Regional or ASE) could provide a secure, private channel.
