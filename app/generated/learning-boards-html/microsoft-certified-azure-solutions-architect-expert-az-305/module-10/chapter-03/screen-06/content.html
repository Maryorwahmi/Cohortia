Advanced Tiers

Premium & Isolated App Service Plans

Enhanced performance, scaling, and network isolation for demanding workloads.

Premium Tier

Offers enhanced performance, more scaling options, and features like VNet integration for high-traffic apps.

Isolated Tier

Provides network isolation and dedicated resources within your VNet for highly secure and compliant applications.

Common Mistake

Over-consolidating too many diverse applications onto a single App Service Plan can lead to resource contention and performance degradation.

Tier Comparison: Standard vs. Premium vs. Isolated

S

Standard

Auto-scaling, custom domains, SSL. Good for typical production.

P

Premium

Enhanced performance, more scaling, VNet integration. For high-demand apps.

I

Isolated

Network isolation, dedicated resources. For highly secure/compliant apps.
