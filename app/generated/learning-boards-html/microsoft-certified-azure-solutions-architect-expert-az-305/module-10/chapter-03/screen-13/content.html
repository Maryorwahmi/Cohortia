Managed Identities: The Zero-Secret Approach

Securely access Azure services without managing credentials.

What are Managed Identities?

Azure Active Directory identities automatically managed by Azure, allowing your App Service app to authenticate to other Azure services.

Zero-Secret Principle

Eliminates the need to store connection strings or secrets in your application code or configuration, enhancing security.

Key Takeaway

Managed Identities provide Azure AD identities for App Service apps to securely access other Azure services without storing credentials.

Managed Identity Flow

App Service authenticates to Azure AD, then to other services.
