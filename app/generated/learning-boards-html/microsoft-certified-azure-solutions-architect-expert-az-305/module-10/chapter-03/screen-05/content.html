App Service Plan: Compute Resources

Defining the underlying infrastructure for your applications.

What it is

The App Service Plan defines the underlying compute resources (VMs) that host your applications. It dictates CPU, memory, and storage.

Pricing Tiers

Choose a tier (Free, Shared, Basic, Standard, Premium, Isolated) based on required features and performance.

Key Takeaway

App Service Plans define the compute resources and features available to your applications based on the chosen pricing tier.

Common App Service Tiers

F

Free/Shared

Dev/Test, limited resources, no custom domains.

B

Basic

Dedicated VMs, custom domains, no auto-scaling.

S

Standard

Production workloads, auto-scaling, custom domains, SSL.
