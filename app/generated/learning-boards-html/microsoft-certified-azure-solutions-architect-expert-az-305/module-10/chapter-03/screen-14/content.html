Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

App Service Plan

Defines the compute resources (VMs) and pricing tier for your App Service applications.

Deployment Slots

Live apps for staging, testing, and performing zero-downtime swaps with production.

Custom Domains

Your own domain name mapped to your Azure App Service application for branding.

Summary Takeaway

These core components provide the foundation for scalable, reliable, and professionally branded Azure App Service solutions.
