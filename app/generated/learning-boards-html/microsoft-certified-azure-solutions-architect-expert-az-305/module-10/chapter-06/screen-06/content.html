Container Apps and Sidecars

Deploying your workloads as single or multiple containers.

  • Container App: A deployment unit for your application, can be a single container.
  • Sidecar Container: An auxiliary container running alongside the main app container.
  • Shared Lifecycle: Main and sidecar containers start, stop, and scale together.
  • Shared Network: Containers communicate over localhost, simplifying inter-process communication.
Key Takeaway

Sidecars enable decoupling of concerns, allowing the main application to focus on business logic while auxiliary services handle cross-cutting tasks.
