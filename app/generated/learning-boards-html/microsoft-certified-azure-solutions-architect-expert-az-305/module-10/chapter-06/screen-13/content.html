Monitoring & Design

Observability and Microservice Design Principles

Monitoring with Azure Monitor and Log Analytics, architectural best practices.

  • Native Integration: Azure Monitor and Log Analytics for comprehensive insights.
  • Stateless Design: Prefer stateless microservices for easier scaling and recovery.
  • Leverage Dapr: Use Dapr for state management, messaging, and other patterns.
  • Managed Identities: Ensure secure, credential-free service-to-service communication.
Key Takeaway

Design for observability and resilience by embracing statelessness, Dapr, and Managed Identities within ACA.
