Integrating with Azure Services: Managed Identities & VNet

Secure authentication and private communication for your microservices.

  • Managed Identities: Eliminate credential management for secure authentication.
  • VNet Integration: Access private endpoints for secure, private communication.
  • Key Vault: Securely retrieve secrets without hardcoding them.
  • Azure SQL/Storage: Authenticate and connect to data services privately.
Key Takeaway

Leverage Managed Identities for secure authentication and VNet integration for private, compliant communication with Azure services.
