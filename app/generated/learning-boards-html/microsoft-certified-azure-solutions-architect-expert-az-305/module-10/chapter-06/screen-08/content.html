Traffic Splitting for Controlled Rollouts

Enabling blue/green deployments and A/B testing with ease.

  • Gradual Rollouts: Shift traffic incrementally to new revisions.
  • Blue/Green Deployments: Deploy new versions alongside old, then switch traffic.
  • A/B Testing: Route a subset of users to test new features.
  • Instant Rollback: Quickly revert all traffic to a stable revision if issues occur.
Key Takeaway

Traffic splitting minimizes deployment risk and enables data-driven decisions for feature rollouts.
