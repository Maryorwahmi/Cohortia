Cloud Management

Embracing FinOps

FinOps is an operational framework that brings financial accountability to cloud spending, fostering collaboration between engineering, finance, and business teams to make cost-aware decisions.

Key Takeaway

Empower teams with data to make informed, cost-effective cloud decisions collaboratively.

Core Principles

Inform, Optimize, Operate. Foster collaboration, data-driven decisions, and financial accountability.

Business

Goal
