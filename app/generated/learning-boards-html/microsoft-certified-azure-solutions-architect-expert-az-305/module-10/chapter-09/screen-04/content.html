Core Concept

Azure Reserved Instances (RIs)

Commit to a 1-year or 3-year term for specific Azure compute resources and receive significant discounts compared to pay-as-you-go pricing. This is ideal for workloads with predictable, steady-state resource consumption.

Key Takeaway

By committing to a term, you can achieve savings of up to 72% on your compute costs for stable workloads.

Common Mistake

Paying full price for always-on resources that could benefit from a Reserved Instance commitment.
