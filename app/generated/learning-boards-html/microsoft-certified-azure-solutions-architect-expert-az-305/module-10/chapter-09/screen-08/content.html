Right-Sizing Compute Resources

Continuously match compute resources (VMs, App Services, etc.) to actual workload requirements by monitoring utilization and adjusting SKUs to avoid over- or under-provisioning.

Key Takeaway

Avoid paying for unused capacity and ensure applications perform optimally by aligning resources with demand.

Common Mistake

Initial over-provisioning due to 'just in case' scenarios, leading to ongoing wasted costs.
