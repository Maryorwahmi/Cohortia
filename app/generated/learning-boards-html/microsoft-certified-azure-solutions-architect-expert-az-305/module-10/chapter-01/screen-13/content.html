Key Selection Criteria: Scalability and Management

Understanding how growth requirements and operational burden impact compute service decisions.

Scalability Requirements

  • **Rapid/Elastic:** PaaS (App Service, AKS) and Serverless (Functions) offer built-in auto-scaling.
  • **Stable/Predictable:** IaaS (VMs) can scale, but often requires more manual configuration or VM Scale Sets.
  • **Impact:** Choose services that match your application's traffic patterns and growth needs.
Scalability Example

An application needs to process large batches of image files uploaded by users, with tasks queued and workers scaling as needed for peak loads.

Azure Functions or Azure Container Apps are suitable for their elastic scaling capabilities to handle burstable processing.

Management Overhead

  • **IaaS:** Highest overhead (OS patching, security, software updates).
  • **PaaS:** Reduced overhead (Microsoft manages OS, runtime).
  • **Serverless:** Lowest overhead (Azure manages all infrastructure).
  • **Impact:** Less control often means less management effort for your team.
Management Overhead Example

A development team wants to focus purely on API logic without worrying about VM provisioning or OS updates.

Azure App Service or Azure Container Apps reduce management overhead significantly, allowing developer focus.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize PaaS and Serverless for applications requiring elastic scalability and minimal management overhead, reserving IaaS for specific control needs.
