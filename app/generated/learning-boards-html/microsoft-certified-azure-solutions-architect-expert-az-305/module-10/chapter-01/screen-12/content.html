Key Selection Criteria: Cost and Control

Evaluating how financial models and infrastructure access influence compute service choices.

Cost Considerations

  • **IaaS (VMs):** Predictable costs, often with reserved instances. Pay for provisioned capacity.
  • **PaaS (App Service, AKS):** Consumption-based, pay for resources used by your application.
  • **Serverless (Functions):** Pay-per-execution, highly optimized for burstable workloads.
  • **TCO:** Includes resource costs, operational costs, and potential downtime.
Cost Example

A startup with unpredictable traffic patterns needs to minimize costs during idle periods.

Serverless (Azure Functions) is ideal due to its pay-per-execution model, avoiding costs for idle capacity.

Control Level

  • **IaaS:** Highest control over OS, middleware, and applications.
  • **PaaS:** Less control over OS/runtime, more focus on application code.
  • **Serverless:** Least control over infrastructure, Azure manages everything.
  • **Impact:** Higher control often means higher management overhead.
Control Example

An organization requires custom kernel modules and specific OS configurations for a critical application.

IaaS (Azure VMs) is the most appropriate choice to allow for deep OS customization.

Key Takeaway

Balance cost optimization with the necessary level of infrastructure control to align compute choices with business and technical requirements.
