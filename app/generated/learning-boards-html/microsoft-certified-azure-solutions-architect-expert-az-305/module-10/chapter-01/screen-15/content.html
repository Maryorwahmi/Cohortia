Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Selection Criteria

Revisiting critical factors for choosing Azure compute services.

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

A financial estimate including direct and indirect costs of a product or system, encompassing resource, operational, and potential downtime costs.

Scalability

The ability of a system to handle a growing amount of work by adding resources, either vertically (scaling up) or horizontally (scaling out).

Management Overhead

The effort and resources required to maintain and operate a system or service, including patching, monitoring, and troubleshooting.

Summary Takeaway

Effective compute service selection requires a holistic view of TCO, scalability needs, and the acceptable level of management overhead.
