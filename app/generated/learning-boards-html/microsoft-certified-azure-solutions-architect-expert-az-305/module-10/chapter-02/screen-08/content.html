Selecting VM Series for Workloads

Matching Azure VM types to specific application requirements.

Why VM Series Matter

Each VM series is optimized for different workloads, impacting performance, cost, and suitability for your application. Correct selection prevents over-provisioning or under-provisioning.

Key Considerations

  • CPU-to-memory ratio
  • Disk throughput and IOPS
  • Specialized hardware (e.g., GPU)
  • Cost-effectiveness for burstable workloads
Key Takeaway

Understanding your application's specific resource demands is paramount to selecting the most appropriate and cost-effective VM series.

Azure VM Series Overview

General Purpose (D, B-series)

Balanced CPU/memory; dev/test, web servers.

Compute Optimized (F-series)

High CPU; web servers, batch processes.

Memory Optimized (E, M-series)

High memory; in-memory DBs, analytics.

Storage Optimized (Ls-series)

High disk IOPS/throughput; NoSQL DBs.

Common Mistake

Choosing a VM series without thoroughly analyzing the application's specific CPU, memory, and I/O requirements can lead to either wasted costs or severe performance bottlenecks.
