High Availability

Availability Sets for In-Datacenter Resilience

Protecting your VMs from planned and unplanned failures within a single data center.

What is an Availability Set?

A logical grouping of VMs that ensures distribution across different underlying hardware (fault domains) and updated at different times (update domains) within a single data center.

Key Benefits

  • Protects against planned maintenance events.
  • Mitigates unplanned hardware failures (e.g., power, network, disk).
  • Ensures at least one VM remains operational.
Key Takeaway

Availability Sets are crucial for high availability within a single Azure data center, safeguarding against common hardware and maintenance disruptions.

How Availability Sets Work

Fault Domains

Separate physical racks, power, network.

Update Domains

VMs updated at different times.

Concrete Example: Two Web Servers

Placing two web servers in an Availability Set ensures they are on different physical racks and won't reboot simultaneously during Azure maintenance, preventing application downtime.

Common Misconception

Availability Sets do NOT protect against entire data center outages. Their scope is limited to a single data center.
