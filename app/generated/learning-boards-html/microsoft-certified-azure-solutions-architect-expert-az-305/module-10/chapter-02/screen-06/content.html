High Availability

Availability Zones for Regional Resilience

Achieving the highest level of resilience against data center-wide failures.

What is an Availability Zone?

Physically separate locations within an Azure region, each with independent power, cooling, and networking, tolerant to local failures.

Key Benefits

  • Protects against data center-wide failures.
  • Offers the highest level of resilience within a region.
  • Ensures business continuity for mission-critical applications.
Key Takeaway

Availability Zones are crucial for maximum uptime and protection against regional data center outages, offering superior fault isolation.

Availability Zones in Action

Zone 1

Zone 2

Separate from Zone 1

Zone 3

Concrete Example: Mission-Critical Database

Deploying database replicas across three Availability Zones ensures that even if one entire data center fails, your database remains operational and accessible, providing superior resilience.

Best Practice

For mission-critical applications, deploy at least two VMs across two or three Availability Zones to maximize resilience.
