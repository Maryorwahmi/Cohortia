Azure VPN Gateway: Secure Internet-Based Connections

Connecting on-premises networks to Azure over the public internet.

Key Characteristics

  • Encrypted over Internet: Establishes secure tunnels over the public internet.
  • Cost-Effective: Generally more affordable for non-critical workloads or smaller bandwidth needs.
  • Connection Types: Supports Site-to-Site (network-to-network) and Point-to-Site (client-to-network) connections.
Misconception

VPN Gateway is not just for individual users; Site-to-Site VPNs are robust for connecting entire on-premises networks to Azure VNets.

Key Takeaway

VPN Gateway offers flexible and secure hybrid connectivity over the public internet, ideal for non-critical or budget-conscious scenarios.
