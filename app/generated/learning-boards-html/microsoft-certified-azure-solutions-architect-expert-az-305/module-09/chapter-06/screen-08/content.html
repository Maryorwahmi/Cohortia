VPN Gateway vs. ExpressRoute

Choosing the right hybrid connectivity solution for your needs.

VPN Gateway Overview

  • Connectivity: Over public internet (IPsec VPN).
  • Performance: Variable, depends on internet traffic.
  • Cost: Generally lower.
  • Use Cases: Dev/test, non-critical workloads, smaller branches.
Key Takeaway

Align your hybrid connectivity choice with workload criticality, performance needs, and budget constraints.

ExpressRoute Overview

  • Connectivity: Private, dedicated connection.
  • Performance: High bandwidth, low latency, predictable.
  • Cost: Generally higher.
  • Use Cases: Mission-critical apps, large data transfers, strict compliance.
Scaling Tip

For ExpressRoute, monitor usage and plan for scaling circuit bandwidth. For VPN Gateway, consider multiple tunnels for redundancy.
