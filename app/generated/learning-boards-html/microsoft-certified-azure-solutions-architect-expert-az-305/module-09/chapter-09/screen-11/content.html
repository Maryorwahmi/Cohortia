Leveraging Azure Advisor for Cost Insights

Your personalized cloud consultant for optimization

Azure Advisor's Role in Cost Optimization

Azure Advisor continuously analyzes your resource configuration and usage, providing personalized, actionable recommendations to reduce costs across your Azure environment.

Pillars: Cost, Security, Reliability, Operational Excellence, Performance.

Benefit: Proactive identification of cost-saving opportunities.

Actionable Cost Recommendations

Advisor provides specific recommendations such as right-sizing underutilized VMs, purchasing Reserved Instances, or deleting unprovisioned resources. Regularly reviewing these is crucial.

Example: Downsize a VM with consistently low CPU utilization.

Pitfall: Ignoring recommendations can lead to significant missed savings.

Key Takeaway

Integrate Azure Advisor into your operational workflow to continuously identify and act on cost optimization opportunities.
