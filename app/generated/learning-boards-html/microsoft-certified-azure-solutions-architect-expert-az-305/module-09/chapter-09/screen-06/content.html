Auto-scaling & Right-sizing

Dynamic resource management for fluctuating demands

Auto-scaling for Elasticity

Automatically adjusts compute resources (VMSS, AKS) based on demand. Scales out during peak loads and scales in during off-peak periods, ensuring you only pay for what's needed.

Benefit: Prevents over-provisioning and reduces costs from idle resources.

Example: Web application scaling for holiday traffic spikes.

Right-sizing for Efficiency

Regularly review performance metrics to ensure VMs and other compute resources are appropriately sized for their workload. Downsizing oversized resources saves costs without impacting performance.

Tool: Azure Advisor provides recommendations for right-sizing.

Pitfall: Over-provisioning 'just in case' leads to unnecessary expenses.

Key Takeaway

Combine auto-scaling with regular right-sizing to dynamically match compute resources to demand, ensuring optimal cost efficiency.
