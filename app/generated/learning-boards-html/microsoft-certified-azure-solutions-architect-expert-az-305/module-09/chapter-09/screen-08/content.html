Redundancy & Storage Tiers

Matching data needs with cost-effective durability

Choosing the Right Redundancy

Select the least expensive redundancy option (LRS, ZRS, GRS, GZRS) that meets your RPO/RTO requirements. Higher durability options come with increased costs.

LRS: Lowest cost, least durability (single data center).

GZRS: Highest durability, highest cost (geo-redundant with zones).

Common Mistake

Defaulting to the highest redundancy for all data, even non-critical, leading to unnecessary costs.

Leveraging Storage Tiers

For Blob Storage, utilize Hot, Cool, and Archive access tiers based on data access frequency. Each tier has distinct pricing for storage and access operations.

Hot: Frequent access, higher storage cost, lower access cost.

Archive: Rarely accessed, lowest storage cost, highest access cost.

Key Takeaway

Carefully match your data's criticality and access patterns to the appropriate storage redundancy and access tiers to optimize costs.
