Cost Management Principles

Implementing FinOps Principles

Bringing financial accountability to cloud spending

What is FinOps?

FinOps is a cultural practice that brings financial accountability to the variable spend model of cloud. It fosters collaboration between finance, operations, and development teams to make business decisions based on cloud costs.

Goal: Maximize business value from cloud spend.

Collaboration: Finance, Operations, Development teams.

The Three Phases of FinOps

FinOps operates in a continuous cycle of three phases: Visibility, Optimization, and Governance. Each phase builds upon the others to ensure ongoing cost efficiency.

Visibility: Understanding where cloud costs are being spent (e.g., tagging).

Optimization: Implementing strategies to reduce costs (e.g., RIs, right-sizing).

Governance: Setting policies and budgets to control spending.

Key Takeaway

FinOps is a holistic approach that embeds financial accountability into cloud operations, driving continuous cost efficiency through cross-functional collaboration.
