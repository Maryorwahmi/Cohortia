Core Concept

Balancing HA, Scalability, and Cost

The inherent trade-offs in cloud solution design

The Optimization Challenge

Designing for high availability (HA) and scalability often requires redundant resources and increased compute power, directly impacting costs. The challenge is to meet these architectural demands without overspending.

High Availability: Redundancy across zones/regions, increased resource duplication.

Scalability: Dynamic resource provisioning, often leading to higher peak capacity.

Common Misconception

Cost optimization means always choosing the cheapest option. Instead, it's about the most cost-effective solution that meets business SLAs.

Intelligent Resource Allocation

Effective cost optimization involves understanding Azure's pricing models and making strategic decisions about resource types, quantities, and deployment patterns. It's a continuous process of evaluation and adjustment.

Align with Business Needs: Match resource choices to RTO/RPO and performance SLAs.

Leverage Azure Features: Utilize pricing models and services designed for efficiency.

Continuous Review: Cloud environments are dynamic; regularly reassess and adjust.

Key Takeaway

Cost optimization is an integral part of architectural design, ensuring solutions are both technically robust and financially sustainable.
