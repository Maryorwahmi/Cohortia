Ensuring Continuous Operation: High Availability

Minimizing downtime and maximizing uptime for critical systems.

What is High Availability?

The ability of a system to operate continuously without failure for a long period, minimizing downtime and ensuring accessibility.

  • Redundancy: Duplicating critical components to prevent single points of failure.

  • Fault Tolerance: System's ability to continue operating despite component failures.

  • Rapid Failover: Quick transition to a healthy component upon detection of a failure.

Key Takeaway

High Availability is about designing systems to operate continuously, minimizing downtime through redundancy and fault tolerance.
