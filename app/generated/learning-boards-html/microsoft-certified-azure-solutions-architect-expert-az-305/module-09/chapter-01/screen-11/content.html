Strategic Design: Business Drivers and Avoiding Pitfalls

Aligning technical solutions with business needs and recognizing common design errors.

Business Drivers

  • Cost per minute of downtime for critical applications.

  • Acceptable latency for user experience.

  • Regulatory compliance and data integrity requirements.

Example Scenario

A critical financial application demands near-zero downtime and data loss, driving high RTO/RPO targets.

Common Pitfalls

  • Over-engineering for availability when not strictly required.

  • Under-provisioning for scalability, leading to performance bottlenecks.

  • Confusing High Availability with Disaster Recovery, leaving gaps in resilience.

Pro Tip

Always start by understanding the business requirements before translating them into technical objectives.

Key Takeaway

Effective architectural design balances business drivers with technical solutions, proactively avoiding common pitfalls to build truly resilient and cost-effective systems.
