Essential Concepts for Resilient Architectures

Reinforcing the foundational ideas from this chapter.

High Availability (HA)

The ability of a system to remain operational and accessible for a high percentage of the time, minimizing downtime.

Scalability

The ability of a system to handle increasing workloads or to be easily expanded to accommodate growth.

Vertical Scaling

Increasing the capacity of a single resource (e.g., more CPU, RAM for a VM).

Summary Takeaway

High Availability, Scalability, and Vertical Scaling are fundamental concepts for building robust systems that minimize downtime and adapt to growing demands.
