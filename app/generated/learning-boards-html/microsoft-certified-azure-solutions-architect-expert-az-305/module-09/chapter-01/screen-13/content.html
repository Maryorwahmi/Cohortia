Critical Metrics and Strategies for Resilience

Key terms and approaches for designing robust cloud solutions.

Horizontal Scaling

Adding more instances of a resource (e.g., adding more VMs or containers).

Elasticity

The ability of a system to automatically scale resources up or down based on demand.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The process of recovering data and restoring business operations after a catastrophic event.

Summary Takeaway

Horizontal Scaling, Elasticity, and Disaster Recovery (including RTO and RPO) are crucial for building adaptable, cost-efficient, and resilient cloud solutions that can recover from major disruptions.
