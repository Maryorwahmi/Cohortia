Recovering from Catastrophe: Understanding Disaster Recovery

Planning for and restoring services after widespread, regional disruptions.

What is Disaster Recovery?

The process of recovering data and restoring business operations after a catastrophic event that affects an entire region or data center.

  • Catastrophic Events: Focuses on regional outages, natural disasters, or widespread power failures.

  • Recovery Plan: Involves a predefined strategy to restore services.

  • Geographic Separation: Often involves a secondary region for restoration.

Key Takeaway

Disaster Recovery is about restoring operations after major regional outages, requiring a separate recovery plan and often geo-redundancy.
