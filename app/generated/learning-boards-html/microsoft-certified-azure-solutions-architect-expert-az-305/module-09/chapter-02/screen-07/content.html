Important Considerations

Regional Support and Service Awareness

Not all Azure regions support Availability Zones, and not all services are inherently zone-redundant or zone-aware. Verification is crucial for successful HA deployments.

Regional Availability

Availability Zones are not available in all Azure regions. Always check the latest documentation.

Service Compatibility

Some services are zone-redundant (automatic replication), others are zone-aware (manual zone selection).

Planning Impact

Lack of support means your HA strategy needs to adapt, potentially relying on Availability Sets or cross-region DR.

Verification Flow

Ensuring AZ Compatibility

Start: Design HA Architecture
Check Azure Documentation

Verify AZ support for chosen region and services.

Supported

Proceed with AZ deployment.

Not Supported

Re-evaluate HA strategy (e.g., AS, cross-region DR).

Key Takeaway

Always confirm Availability Zone support for your chosen Azure region and services to ensure your high availability design is viable.
