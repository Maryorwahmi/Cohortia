Advanced HA Concepts

Understanding Maintenance and Service Behavior

Update Domain (UD)

A logical group of VMs that can be rebooted together during planned maintenance within an Availability Set, ensuring minimal downtime.

Zone-redundant services

Azure services that automatically replicate data or instances across multiple Availability Zones for built-in resilience.

Zone-aware services

Azure services that allow you to specify the Availability Zone for deployment of specific instances, offering granular control.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding Update Domains and the distinction between zone-redundant and zone-aware services is key for advanced HA design.
