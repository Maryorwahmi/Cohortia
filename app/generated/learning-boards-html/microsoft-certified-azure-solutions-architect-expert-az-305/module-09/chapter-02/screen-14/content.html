Decision Point

Availability Zones vs. Availability Sets

Choosing between Availability Zones and Availability Sets depends on the level of resilience required and the scope of failure you need to protect against.

Availability Zones (AZs)

Highest availability, protects against data center-wide failures (power, network, cooling).

Availability Sets (ASs)

Fault tolerance within a single data center, protects against localized hardware failures or planned maintenance.

Key Question

What is the acceptable downtime and the most critical failure scenario for your application?

Key Takeaway

Availability Zones offer broader protection against data center failures, while Availability Sets provide granular fault tolerance within a single data center.
