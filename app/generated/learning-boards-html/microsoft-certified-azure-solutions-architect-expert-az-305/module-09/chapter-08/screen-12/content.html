Common Mistakes in Microservices Design
Avoiding Pitfalls for Successful Implementation

Many projects stumble due to common microservices design mistakes. Recognizing these pitfalls is the first step toward avoiding them and ensuring a successful implementation.

Key mistakes include creating distributed monoliths, over-engineering solutions, neglecting data consistency, lacking observability, and overlooking security at service boundaries.

Key Takeaway

Awareness of common microservices pitfalls is crucial for designing robust, scalable, and maintainable systems.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid
  • Distributed Monolith: Tight coupling, negating microservice benefits.
  • Over-engineering: Applying microservices when not needed.
  • Data Consistency Issues: Ignoring distributed transaction patterns (e.g., Saga).
  • Lack of Observability: Insufficient logging, metrics, and tracing.
  • Inadequate Security: Not securing service-to-service communication.
  • Network Latency: Excessive synchronous calls between services.
