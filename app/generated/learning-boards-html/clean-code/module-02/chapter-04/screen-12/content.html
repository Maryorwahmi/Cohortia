Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › When to Rename or Split
Refactor Decision Process
  1. One-sentence responsibility test: can you state the responsibility clearly?
  2. Caller clarity: do importers know what to expect?
  3. Method cohesion: do methods belong together conceptually?
  4. Test coverage and safe renaming practices.
Practical tip: small, frequent renames are better than delayed big refactors. They keep intent visible and reduce long-term technical debt.
Key Takeaway

Use a short checklist to decide when names or files should change. Clear names prevent future complexity.
