Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Common naming anti-patterns
Anti-patterns
  • Single letters: Used outside tight scopes, they force inference.
  • Generic tokens: data, info, manager — too broad to be useful.
  • Cryptic abbreviations: team-only short forms that new contributors won't understand.
  • Type prefixes: strName or iCount add clutter and can mislead after refactors.
Replacement patterns
Prefer domain-specific names: positive_numbers, active_user_count, csv_parser. Consistency matters more than sloganized brevity.
Review advice
When suggesting a rename, include a concrete alternative and a short justification. That makes reviews constructive and speeds adoption of better names.
Common mistake

Labeling something as a 'manager' or 'utility' usually reveals an opportunity to extract a clearer responsibility name.
