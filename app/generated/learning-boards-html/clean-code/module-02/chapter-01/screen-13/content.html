Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Names as documentation
Team benefits
  • Faster code reviews thanks to a shared vocabulary.
  • Easier onboarding when domain terms are clear and consistent.
  • Reduced need for external documentation because names reveal intent.
Tip: keep a short glossary of common domain terms in your repo to align names across modules.
Practical step
During a sprint, identify five frequently used domain concepts and standardize their naming. Add examples to contribution guidelines and encourage naming comments in PRs when intent is not obvious.
Key Takeaway

Shared naming conventions encode team knowledge and shrink the cost of collaboration.
