Orchestrator role
Coordinate focused helpers, describe the process, and manage errors and sequencing.
Readability
Orchestrators act like a short script: validate, transform, persist, notify.
Test strategy
Test helpers in isolation and the orchestrator with mocked helpers to verify flow and error handling.
Anti-pattern
Do not let the orchestrator implement business rules; delegate those to helpers.
Example flow — register_user
validate_user_data()
Pure checks, raise errors if invalid.
hash_password()
Deterministic transformation, easy to test.
store_user_in_database()
Command with side effects; mock during tests.
The orchestrator calls these helpers and handles the overall success or failure, keeping each helper focused.
