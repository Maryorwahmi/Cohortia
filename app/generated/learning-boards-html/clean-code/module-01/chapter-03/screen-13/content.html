Sample Question
Why does process_data_and_save(raw, path) violate SRP?
Correct reasoning
Transforming data and saving it are separate reasons to change. Refactor into transform_data and save_data_to_file with an orchestrator.
Counter Side Effect Question
Identify side effects in increment_and_get_status and propose mitigation.
Correct Answer
Mutating a global counter and printing are side effects. Mitigate by returning new counter values and separate logging outside the pure calculation.
