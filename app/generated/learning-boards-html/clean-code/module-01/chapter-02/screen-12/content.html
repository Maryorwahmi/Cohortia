Question 1
get_val(arr, idx) — what are two better names?
Suggested answers: get_element_at_index, retrieve_list_item; items or numbers_list for the collection.
Question 2
if status == 1 — how to make intent explicit?
Suggested answer: Define STATUS_ACTIVE = 1 and use if status == STATUS_ACTIVE for clarity.
Question 3
cust_info — what name fits best?
Suggested answer: customer_info or customer_profile to match business vocabulary.
