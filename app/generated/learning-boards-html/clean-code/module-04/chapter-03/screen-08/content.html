Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Composition vs Inheritance
Inheritance example
Vehicle with drive and refuel. ElectricCar may not refuel in the same way, leading to awkward overrides or empty methods.
Pitfall: subclasses inherit responsibilities they don't need, increasing coupling and violating SRP.
Composition example
Vehicle has an IPowerSource. Implement GasolineEngine or ElectricMotor and inject into Vehicle. Each power source has a single responsibility.
Benefit: swap behaviors at runtime, simpler tests, clearer SRP alignment.
