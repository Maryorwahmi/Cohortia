Comparison
Strategy
Interchangeable algorithms encapsulated as objects.
  • Best when algorithms vary independently.
  • Clients choose or are injected with a strategy.
  • Good for runtime swapping and testing.
Comparison
Template Method
Stable control flow with variable steps implemented by subclasses.
  • Best when the sequence of steps must be preserved.
  • Subclasses override specific steps.
  • Good for reuse of shared steps across variants.
Quick decision checklist
Is the variation a whole algorithm? → Strategy
Is the flow fixed but steps vary? → Template
Will runtime swapping or injection be needed? → Strategy
