What to Remember
Three practical rules to keep your classes clean.
Name the mission
If you cannot name it in one line, refactor.
Encapsulate
Expose actions, not internal storage.
Extract
Move unrelated work into collaborators with clear interfaces.
Final prompt
Before your next commit, pick one class and ask: what is its single reason to change? If unclear, plan one small extraction and test it.
