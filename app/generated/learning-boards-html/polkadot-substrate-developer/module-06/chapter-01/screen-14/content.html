Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Backup & Recovery
Archive backups

Archive nodes require large snapshot strategies. Use incremental backups and off-site storage. Test restores to verify time and integrity.

Validator recovery

Securely backup key material and test restore procedures with your chosen HSM or remote signer. Have an emergency rotation and failover plan.

Restore rehearsals

Regularly rehearse full restores from backups to reduce surprises and ensure version compatibility and data integrity.

Key Takeaway
Backups are valuable only when restore plans are documented and tested under realistic conditions.
