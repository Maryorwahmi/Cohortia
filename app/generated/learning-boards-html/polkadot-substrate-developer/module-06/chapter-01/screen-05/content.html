Network Bootstrapping

Stable bootnodes enable reliable discovery

Embed bootnodes in chain_spec.rs or pass them at startup. Prefer stable, operator-run peers that remain reachable.

Example: placeholder bootnode
boot_nodes: Some(vec!["/ip4/127.0.0.1/tcp/30333/p2p/Qm...".into()]),
Misconception: using random or unreliable peers for bootstrapping can cause long sync times or network partition problems. Validate reachability from multiple locations and document your bootnode endpoints.
Practical checks
  • 1. Test bootnode address connectivity from outside your hosting network.
  • 2. Ensure P2P port 30333 is allowed for peer discovery.
  • 3. Use --bootnodes on a local launch to validate addresses before committing to chain_spec.
Pro tip

Keep a small set of bootnodes and maintain a rotation plan to replace failed peers without disrupting new node bootstrapping.
