Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Deployment Checklist
Timeline

Plan, Harden, Rehearse, Launch

  1. Plan: Design node roles and stable bootnodes.
  2. Harden: Remove sudo, set realistic balances, secure keys.
  3. Infrastructure: Provision full, archive, and validator hosts.
  4. Network: Configure ufw and reverse proxies for RPC/WS.
  5. Visibility: Enable telemetry and log aggregation with alerts.
  6. Rehearse: Run mirror testnet for upgrades and restores.
  7. Launch: Coordinate bootnodes, monitor closely, and be ready to rollback.
Pro tip

Document every step and keep a recovery runbook so that routine maintenance can be performed safely under pressure.
