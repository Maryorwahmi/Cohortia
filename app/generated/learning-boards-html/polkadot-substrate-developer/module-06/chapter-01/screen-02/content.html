Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Learning Objectives
01
Configure a Substrate node
Refine chain_spec.rs and flags for stable production behavior.
You will understand how genesis state and bootnodes are expressed and why removing dev shortcuts is critical. Visual mnemonic: a small chain of three linked cards representing genesis, bootnodes, and args.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand node types and roles
Differentiate full, archive, and validator nodes and their resource needs.
Knowing the role guides your hardware, storage, and key-management decisions. Mnemonic: three stacked servers labeled Full, Archive, Validator.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Secure production nodes
Protect RPC/WS, use firewalls and HSMs for validator keys.
Security includes limiting RPC and WebSocket exposure, using a reverse proxy, and strong OS hardening. Mnemonic: a shield over a server stack.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify and mitigate pitfalls
Avoid development defaults, enable monitoring, and rehearse upgrades on a testnet.
You will learn common mistakes and how to plan logging, telemetry, and testnets to catch issues early. Mnemonic: warning triangle with checklist.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
