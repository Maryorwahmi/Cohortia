Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Archive Node
Definition & Impact

Why archives matter and their costs

Archive nodes retain all historical state, enabling queries at any past block. This is essential for explorers, analytics, and forensic reconstruction.

Storage
Much larger disk requirements and planning for growth over time.
Sync & Backups
Longer initial sync, complex snapshot strategies, and careful restore planning.
Concrete guidance
  • Allocate scalable storage and plan incremental snapshots.
  • Run archive nodes separately from validators to reduce risk.
  • Use an indexer layer to serve heavy query loads instead of exposing the archive node directly.
Key Takeaway

Treat archive nodes as specialized infrastructure with distinct backup and scaling requirements.
