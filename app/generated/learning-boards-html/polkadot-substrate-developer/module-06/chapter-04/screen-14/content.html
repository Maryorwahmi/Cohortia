Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Polkadot Relay Chain

The central chain of Polkadot, providing shared security, consensus, and interoperability for parachains.

Parachain

An independent, application-specific blockchain connected to the Relay Chain, benefiting from its security and interoperability.

Parachain Slot

A limited resource on the Relay Chain that a parachain must acquire to connect and benefit from shared security.

Candle Auction

A unique auction mechanism for allocating parachain slots, designed to prevent last-minute bidding and encourage true price discovery.

Crowdloan

A mechanism where a project's community members lock up DOT tokens to help fund a parachain auction bid, receiving rewards in return.

Summary

Polkadot's architecture, driven by the Relay Chain and parachains, leverages unique mechanisms like candle auctions and crowdloans to foster a secure, scalable, and community-driven multi-chain ecosystem.
