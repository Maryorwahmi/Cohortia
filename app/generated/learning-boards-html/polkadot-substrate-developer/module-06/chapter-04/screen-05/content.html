Process Flow

Acquiring a Parachain Slot

The competitive candle auction mechanism

1

Identify Need

Substrate chain needs shared security & interoperability.

2

Enter Candle Auction

Bid for a limited parachain slot with a randomized end time.

3

Secure & Lease Slot

Winning bid leases slot for a fixed duration (e.g., 6 months - 2 years).

Why Candle Auction?

Prevents last-minute 'sniping' by randomizing the exact end time, encouraging participants to bid their true valuation earlier.

Challenge

Slots are limited and competitive, requiring significant capital and strategic bidding to secure and renew leases.

Key Takeaway

Parachain slots are a scarce resource acquired through a unique candle auction designed for fair price discovery, with leases requiring periodic renewal.
