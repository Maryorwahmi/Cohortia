Governance in Action

Polkadot's On-Chain Governance: Shaping the Network's Future

Understand how community participation and on-chain mechanisms drive Polkadot's evolution.

Token Holders

Propose and vote on referenda, directly influencing network changes.

Council

Elected representatives proposing important changes and vetoing malicious proposals.

Technical Committee

Fast-track urgent proposals and ensure technical soundness of upgrades.

Key Aspects of Polkadot Governance

  • **Referenda:** Direct voting by token holders on proposals.
  • **Council:** Elected body representing passive stakeholders, proposing and vetoing.
  • **Technical Committee:** Ensures technical soundness and can fast-track urgent proposals.
  • **Treasury:** Funds community initiatives and network development.

Real-World Impact

Polkadot's governance has successfully enacted numerous runtime upgrades and treasury spending proposals, demonstrating its effectiveness in adapting the network to new challenges and opportunities. This ensures continuous innovation and security without disruptive hard forks.

Key Takeaway

Active participation in Polkadot's on-chain governance is crucial for developers and token holders to collectively steer the network's evolution and ensure its long-term health and decentralization.
