Engaging with the Polkadot Community

Connect, contribute, and grow through official chat channels, forums, and dedicated builder programs.

Chat & Forums

Official Discord/Element channels for real-time discussions and support, alongside the Polkadot Forum for structured proposals and knowledge sharing.

  • •Real-time support & discussions
  • •Structured proposals
  • •Knowledge sharing

Builder Programs & Grants

Programs like the Substrate Builders Program and Web3 Foundation Grants offer funding and support for promising projects and developers.

  • •Project funding
  • •Technical support
  • •Ecosystem integration

Hackathons & Workshops

Regular events organized globally to foster innovation, skill development, and networking among Polkadot and Substrate developers.

  • •Innovation challenges
  • •Skill-building sessions
  • •Networking opportunities
Key Takeaway

Active engagement with Polkadot's diverse community channels is crucial for support, collaboration, and staying updated on the network's rapid evolution.

Concrete Example

Joining the Polkadot Discord allows you to get real-time answers to your development questions from core developers and community members.

Why it Matters

Community participation is vital for Polkadot's on-chain governance, empowering token holders and elected bodies to collectively shape the network's future.
