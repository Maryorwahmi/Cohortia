Chapter Review

Key Concepts: Growth & Future

Review the programs supporting development and key future advancements in Polkadot.

Substrate Builders Program

A program by Parity Technologies to support teams building Substrate-based projects.

Asynchronous Backing

A future upgrade aimed at increasing parachain block processing speed and throughput.

Parathreads

A flexible parachain model where chains pay for block execution on a pay-as-you-go basis.

Summary Takeaway

These initiatives and technical advancements are crucial for supporting developers and scaling Polkadot's capabilities for a robust and flexible Web3 future.
