Common Challenges & Best Practices

Ensuring Smooth and Secure Runtime Upgrades

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Insufficient Testing: Leads to bugs, unexpected behavior, or chain halts.
  • Poor Communication: Causes community confusion, distrust, and failed proposals.
  • Governance Mismanagement: Leads to delays or rejection of critical upgrades.

Best Practices for Success

  • Extensive Testing: Utilize local dev chains and dedicated testnets (e.g., Rococo).
  • Clear Communication: Document changes and engage proactively with the community.
  • Multi-Stage Governance: Implement layered approval for critical upgrades.
  • Storage Migrations: Crucial for `spec_version` changes to ensure state compatibility.
Key Takeaway

Successful runtime upgrades demand rigorous testing, transparent communication, effective governance, and careful management of storage migrations to prevent chain instability.
