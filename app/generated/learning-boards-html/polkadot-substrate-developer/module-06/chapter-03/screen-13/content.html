Governance Roles

Council vs. Technical Committee

Balancing Representation and Expertise in Governance

Council: The Representative Body

  • Role: Represents passive stakeholders, proposes referenda, manages treasury.
  • Focus: Broader policy, strategic direction, community consensus.
  • Pace: More deliberative, often slower for wider input.

Technical Committee: The Expert Responders

  • Role: Identifies/implements urgent bug fixes, critical upgrades.
  • Focus: Technical emergencies, security patches, rapid deployment.
  • Pace: Agile, can fast-track proposals for urgency.
Key Takeaway

The Council ensures democratic representation and strategic direction, while the Technical Committee provides expert, agile responses to technical emergencies, creating a balanced and resilient governance system.
