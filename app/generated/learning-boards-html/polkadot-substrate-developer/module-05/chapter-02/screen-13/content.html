Prepare for transactions and events

Before you submit extrinsics, verify preconditions like balances and constants. Use the same conversion and Option handling when parsing results and events.
Future lessons will show api.tx construction, signing, signAndSend patterns, and event decoding after blocks are included.
Checklist
