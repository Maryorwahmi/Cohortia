Secure signing patterns
Prefer external signers or key management so that application processes never hold raw private keys. Build a signer adapter that implements the signing interface and forwards requests to hardware or a secure microservice.
Hardware signer
Leverage a device that performs signatures without exporting private keys.
KMS / secure store
Use a managed secrets store and perform signing via a protected interface.
Connection hygiene
Always call unsub() after finalization to stop streaming updates. Call api.disconnect() when your script completes to close the WebSocket cleanly.
Key Takeaway

Operationally separate signing from business logic and manage connections to avoid leaks and reduce attack surface.
