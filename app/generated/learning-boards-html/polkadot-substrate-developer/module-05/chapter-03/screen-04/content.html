Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Transaction lifecycle
Concept

A signed extrinsic moves through statuses exposed by the API. Understanding these ensures correct UX and backend behavior.

Ready / Broadcast
Local acceptance and propagation to peers.
InBlock
Included in a block — visible on-chain but not yet irreversible.
Finalized
Block finalized by the consensus; considered irreversible.
Key Takeaway

Decide whether your app needs quick inclusion feedback or full finality and react to the appropriate status.

Status timeline
1
submit()
Client sends signed extrinsic to peers
2
inclusion
Extrinsic included in a block, events emitted
3
finality
Block finalized; consider action confirmed
Example: A payment UI can show 'processing' on inclusion and only mark the transfer complete on finalization when balances are immutable. Misconception: treating inclusion as final can lead to inconsistent business state when chain reorganizations occur.
