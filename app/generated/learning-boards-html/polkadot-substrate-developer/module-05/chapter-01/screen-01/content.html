Connecting to Your Substrate Chain

Interacting with Polkadot-JS Apps UI

This chapter guides you through establishing a connection to your Substrate node using the Polkadot-JS Apps UI. You'll learn to navigate its powerful features for account management, chain state exploration, and transaction execution, gaining hands-on experience with your blockchain.

Key Takeaway

Polkadot-JS Apps UI is your essential tool for interacting with and debugging any Substrate-based blockchain, providing a visual interface for on-chain operations.
