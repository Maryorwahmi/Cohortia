Your On-Chain Identity

Creating and Securing Accounts

Account Creation & Use

  • •Create new accounts or restore existing ones from seed phrases.
  • •Connect hardware wallets for enhanced security.
  • •Utilize pre-funded 'Alice', 'Bob' accounts for development testing.

Security Best Practices

  • •Securely store your seed phrase and JSON backup file.
  • •Never share your seed phrase or store it insecurely.
  • •Avoid reusing seed phrases for multiple accounts.
Key Takeaway

Account management is crucial for interacting with your chain, and robust security practices for seed phrases are non-negotiable.
