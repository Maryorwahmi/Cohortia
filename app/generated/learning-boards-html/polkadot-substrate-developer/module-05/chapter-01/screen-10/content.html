Sending Transactions

Interacting with Dispatchable Functions

01

Select Sending Account

Choose the account (e.g., 'Alice') that will sign and submit the transaction.

02

Choose Pallet & Extrinsic

Select the target pallet (e.g., balances) and the specific extrinsic call (e.g., transfer).

03

Input Parameters

Provide any required values, such as recipient address and amount for a transfer.

04

Sign & Submit

Review transaction details, including fees, then sign and submit to the chain.

Common Mistake

Sending funds to the wrong address. Blockchain transactions are irreversible, so always double-check all details before signing.

Key Takeaway

The Extrinsics tab is your primary tool for modifying chain state, but requires careful verification of all transaction details.
