Declare events
Use decl_event! and emit with Self::deposit_event(RawEvent::...).
Return specific errors
Use decl_error!, then ensure! or ok_or to return the right variant.
Prefer checked ops
Map checked_add/checked_sub failures to Overflow rather than panicking.
Developer checklist
Before merging: events declared and emitted? errors specific and tested? no panics or unwraps?
