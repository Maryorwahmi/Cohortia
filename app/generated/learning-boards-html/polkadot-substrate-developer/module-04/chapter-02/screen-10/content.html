Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Checked Arithmetic
Example
let auction_id = <NextAuctionId<T>>::get();
let new_auction_id = auction_id.checked_add(&T::AuctionId::from(1u32))
    .ok_or(Error::Overflow)?;
<NextAuctionId<T>>::put(new_auction_id);
Why this matters
  • Prevents unexpected wrap-around and panics in the runtime.
  • Maps arithmetic failure to a clear error variant developers and users can act upon.
  • Allows tests to assert overflow behavior on boundary inputs.
