Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Events vs println!
Why println! is unsuitable
  • No on-chain record: node logs are ephemeral and not part of block receipts.
  • Potential non-determinism tied to the host environment.
  • No easy programmatic access for indexers and dApps.
  • Risk of leaking sensitive data to operator logs.
Why events are superior
  • Persisted with the block, creating an auditable trail.
  • Deterministic emission inside the runtime.
  • Indexed and filterable by off-chain services for real-time UX updates.
