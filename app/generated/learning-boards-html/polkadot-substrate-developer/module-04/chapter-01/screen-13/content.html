Assessment and design questions

Question 1

Why is StorageMap<AccountId, Vec<NftId>> often a poor choice? Explain alternatives.

Answer hint: state bloat, concurrency, inefficient queries; prefer StorageMap<NftId, AccountId> or bounded vectors or off-chain indexing.
Question 2

Give one DEX example for StorageMap and one for StorageDoubleMap.

Answer hint: StorageMap for AccountId to Balance; StorageDoubleMap for (AccountId, AssetId) to Balance.
Verification

After implementing, mint and read storage, approve and check contains_key, transfer and confirm approvals removed. These direct checks validate correctness beyond events alone.
