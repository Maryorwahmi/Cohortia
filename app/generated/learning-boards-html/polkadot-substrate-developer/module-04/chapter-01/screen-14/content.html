Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
StorageMap
Single-key mapping such as AccountId to Balance for direct lookups.
StorageDoubleMap
Two-key mapping for relationships like (Owner, Spender) to approved amount.
Hashers & Safety
Prefer blake2_128_concat, validate inputs, and use checked arithmetic to avoid runtime faults.
Takeaway
Design storage by access pattern: choose the simplest keyed lookup that supports your common queries and keep heavy aggregation off-chain.
