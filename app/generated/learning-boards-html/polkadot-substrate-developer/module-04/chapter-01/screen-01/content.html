Efficient Data Management with Storage Maps

Learn how on-chain key-value storage in Substrate reduces expensive lookups, and how to select StorageMap or StorageDoubleMap to model relationships cleanly and efficiently.

In this lesson

You will see declaration patterns, safe read/write idioms, hasher trade-offs, and a hands-on NFT activity that applies these patterns.

Why this matters
Efficient storage reduces gas, storage bloat, and complexity while enabling direct lookups for common on-chain operations.
Explore Key Topics
StorageMap
Single-key map for direct account or asset lookups.
StorageDoubleMap
Two-key lookup for relationships like owner and spender.
Hashers & Safety
Pick hashers and use checked arithmetic to avoid underflow and bloat.
Concept Map
Balances: StorageMap<AccountId, Balance>
Direct per-account balance lookup
Allowance: StorageDoubleMap<Owner, Spender, Balance>
Direct owner-spender approvals
Best practices
Checked arithmetic, validate inputs, avoid unbounded vectors
