Learning Objectives

Four clear, verified skills to practice as you work through examples and the hands-on NFT activity.

01
Recognize when to use StorageMap

You'll identify single-key access needs such as per-account balances and learn why direct lookups are cheaper than scanning stored lists.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement StorageDoubleMap for relations

You will model owner-spender approvals or user-asset pairs so lookups use both keys and avoid iteration.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Interact safely from dispatchables

Use generated getters, insert/remove, existence checks, and checked arithmetic to prevent underflow and invalid state.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design for performance and scalability

Avoid unbounded vecs on-chain, choose appropriate hashers, and prefer direct keyed access to reduce fees and state bloat.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
