Web3 Ecosystem

Beyond Crypto: The Broad Vision of Web3

Exploring DeFi, NFTs, DAOs, and New Paradigms

Key Pillars of Web3

  • •Decentralized Finance (DeFi): Financial services without intermediaries.
  • •Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs): Unique digital ownership for assets.
  • •Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs): Community-led governance.
  • •Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI): User-controlled digital identity.
Key Takeaway

Web3 is a comprehensive ecosystem powered by blockchain, extending beyond cryptocurrencies to revolutionize finance, ownership, governance, and digital interaction.

New Paradigms Emerging

  • •Social Networking: User-owned profiles and content.
  • •Gaming: True ownership of in-game assets and economies.
  • •Content Creation: Direct monetization and ownership for creators.
  • •Supply Chain: Transparent and verifiable tracking of goods.

Common Misconception

Web3 is often narrowly defined as 'just crypto.' While crypto is a part, Web3's scope is much broader, aiming to decentralize the entire internet.
