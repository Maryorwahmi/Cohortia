Web Era 3

Introducing Web3: The Decentralized Web

Shifting Power to Users and Embracing Transparency

Core Principles of Web3

  • •Decentralization: Control and data distributed across a network.
  • •Transparency: Open and verifiable transactions and data on public ledgers.
  • •User Ownership: Individuals control their data and digital assets.
  • •Censorship Resistance: No single entity can easily block content or users.
Key Takeaway

Web3 aims to create a more equitable internet by decentralizing power, enhancing transparency, and empowering users with true ownership over their digital presence.

Addressing Web2 Shortcomings

  • •Centralization: Replaced by distributed network architecture.
  • •Data Privacy: Users control their data, not corporations.
  • •Censorship: Network resilience makes arbitrary blocking difficult.
  • •Single Points of Failure: Distributed nature enhances system robustness.

Analogy

If Web2 is a social club run by a single owner, Web3 is a self-governed community where members collectively make decisions and own their contributions.
