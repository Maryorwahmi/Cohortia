True Data Ownership

Controlling Your Digital Assets and Identity

What Data Ownership Means in Web3

  • •Control: You decide who accesses your data and how it's used.
  • •Cryptographic Keys: Your digital signature secures and proves ownership.
  • •Digital Assets: Ownership of NFTs, tokens, and other digital items.
Key Takeaway

Web3 shifts data control from corporations to individuals, enabling true ownership of digital assets and self-sovereign identity through cryptographic security.

Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI)

  • •User-Controlled: Individuals manage their own digital identity.
  • •Decentralized: Not tied to a central provider like Google or Facebook.
  • •Portable: Your identity can be used across different dApps and platforms.
  • •Privacy-Preserving: Share only necessary information, not your entire profile.

Example

Using a crypto wallet to log into a dApp, where your identity and assets are linked to your keys, not a centralized username/password system.
