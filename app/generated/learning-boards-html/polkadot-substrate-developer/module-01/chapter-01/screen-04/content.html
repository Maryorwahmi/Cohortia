Why it matters

Understanding the historical context of the web's evolution is crucial for appreciating the innovations and necessity of Web3, especially in addressing the limitations of previous eras.

Key Takeaway

Each web era represents a distinct paradigm shift in technology, user interaction, and underlying philosophy, leading to the current push for decentralization.

Key Shifts Across Eras

  • •Architecture: From centralized servers to distributed networks.
  • •User Interaction: From passive consumption to active participation and ownership.
  • •Data Control: From corporate ownership to individual sovereignty.
  • •Trust Model: From intermediaries to cryptographic verification.

Common Misconception

Many believe the internet has always been interactive. In reality, early web was largely static, a stark contrast to today's dynamic experience.
