Web3 Mechanics

Decentralized Applications (dApps)

Running on Distributed Networks

What are dApps?

  • •Decentralized Backend: Code and data run on a blockchain network.
  • •Peer-to-Peer: Interactions occur directly between users and the network.
  • •Open Source: Often, the underlying code is publicly verifiable.
  • •Censorship Resistant: No central authority can shut them down.
Key Takeaway

dApps are the core applications of Web3, leveraging blockchain to provide decentralized, transparent, and censorship-resistant services, shifting power from corporations to users.

How dApps Differ from Web2 Apps

  • •Data Storage: On blockchain vs. central server.
  • •Control: Distributed vs. centralized.
  • •Trust: Cryptographic proof vs. corporate policy.
  • •Intermediaries: Eliminated vs. essential.

Example

A decentralized social media platform like 'Lens Protocol' allows users to own their social graph and content, unlike traditional platforms.
