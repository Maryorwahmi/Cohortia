01

Distinguish Web1, Web2, and Web3

Learn to differentiate these eras by their architecture, user interaction models, and data ownership paradigms. This skill is foundational for understanding the internet's evolution and future direction.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Articulate Web2's Problems

Identify and explain the core limitations of the Web2 paradigm, focusing on centralization, data privacy concerns, and the implications for user control. This helps contextualize the need for Web3.
