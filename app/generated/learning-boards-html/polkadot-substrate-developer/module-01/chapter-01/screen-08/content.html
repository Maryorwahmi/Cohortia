Web2 Limitations

Data as a Product, Not an Asset

The Erosion of User Control and Freedom

The Web2 Data Model

  • •Data Collection: Extensive tracking of user behavior and preferences.
  • •Monetization: Data used for targeted advertising, sold to third parties.
  • •Implicit Consent: Users often agree to terms without full understanding.
Key Takeaway

Web2's model of data monetization and centralized control leads to significant privacy concerns and risks of censorship, diminishing user autonomy.

Impact on Users

  • •Privacy Erosion: Constant tracking and profiling without full transparency.
  • •Censorship: Platforms can remove content or ban users arbitrarily.
  • •Lack of Ownership: Users don't truly own or control their digital assets or identity.
  • •Single Points of Failure: Data breaches at central servers expose millions.

Analogy

Imagine a library where the librarian reads all your borrowed books, records your interests, and sells that data to advertisers. That's Web2's data model.
