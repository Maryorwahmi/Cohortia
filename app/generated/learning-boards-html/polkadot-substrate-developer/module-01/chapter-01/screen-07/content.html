Centralization: Power in Few Hands

Understanding the Risks of Intermediaries

What is Centralization?

  • •Single Point of Control: A single entity or small group manages the system.
  • •Data Silos: User data is stored and controlled by platform providers.
  • •Intermediaries: Large tech companies act as essential middlemen for interactions.
Key Takeaway

Centralization in Web2 creates dependencies on powerful intermediaries, leading to potential issues with control, censorship, and data security.

Consequences of Centralization

  • •Censorship Risk: Platforms can unilaterally remove content or ban users.
  • •Data Breaches: Centralized data stores are attractive targets for hackers.
  • •Single Points of Failure: System downtime if the central server fails.
  • •Lack of User Control: Users have limited say over platform policies or data usage.

Example Scenario

A social media platform banning a user and deleting their content without explanation highlights the power of centralized control.
