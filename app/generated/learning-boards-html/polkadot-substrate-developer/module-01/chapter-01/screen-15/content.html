Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Core Principles

Understanding Foundational Web Paradigms

Centralization

A system where control and data are managed by a single entity or a small group of entities, common in Web1 and Web2.

Decentralization

A system where control and data are distributed across a network of participants, with no single point of authority, a core principle of Web3.

Data Ownership

The concept that individuals should have control over their personal data, including who can access, use, and profit from it, central to Web3.

Summary: Web3's shift from centralized control to decentralized networks empowers users with true data ownership, primarily through decentralized applications (dApps).
