Key Concepts: Web Eras

Understanding the Internet's Evolution

Web1 (Read-Only Web)

The early internet (1990s-early 2000s) characterized by static websites and minimal user interaction, primarily for information consumption.

Web2 (Read-Write Web)

The current internet era (2004-present) defined by interactive platforms, user-generated content, and social media, but also by centralization and corporate control over user data.

Web3 (Decentralized Web)

The emerging internet paradigm focused on decentralization, user data ownership, censorship resistance, and peer-to-peer interactions, primarily powered by blockchain technology.

Summary: The internet has evolved from a static information source (Web1) to an interactive, social platform (Web2) with centralization issues, now moving towards a decentralized, user-owned future (Web3).
