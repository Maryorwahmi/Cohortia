Key Concepts

Review the essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Immutability

The property of data on a blockchain that, once recorded, cannot be altered or deleted, ensuring a permanent and tamper-proof record.

Consensus Mechanism

A protocol used by a distributed network to agree on the single, true state of the ledger, ensuring all participants have the same, valid copy.

Private Key

A secret cryptographic number used to sign transactions and prove ownership of assets on a blockchain. Must be kept absolutely confidential.

Chapter Summary

Blockchain's immutability, coupled with robust consensus mechanisms and secure private keys, forms the foundation for trustless and transparent digital interactions.
