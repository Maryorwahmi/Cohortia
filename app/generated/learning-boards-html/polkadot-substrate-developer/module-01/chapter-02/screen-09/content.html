Proof of Stake (PoS)

Efficiency Through Staked Collateral

Validators & Staking

Participants (validators) 'stake' or lock up their cryptocurrency as collateral. The amount staked influences their chance of being selected to create new blocks.

Block Creation & Rewards

Selected validators propose and validate new blocks. They earn rewards for honest participation, similar to miners in PoW, but without intense computation.

Security & Efficiency

Malicious validators risk losing their staked assets, incentivizing honest behavior. PoS is significantly more energy-efficient and generally offers higher transaction throughput.

1. Validators Stake Crypto

Lock up assets as collateral

2. Selected to Validate

Based on stake size

3. Block Added & Reward

Network verifies, validator gets crypto (or loses stake)

Key Takeaway

Proof of Stake offers an energy-efficient and scalable consensus method by incentivizing honest validation through economic collateral rather than computational power.
