Core Concept

Public-Key Cryptography

Your Digital Identity on the Blockchain

Private Key: The Secret

A secret cryptographic number used to digitally sign transactions, proving ownership and authorization. It must be kept absolutely confidential, like a password.

Public Key: The Address

Derived from your private key, this acts as your blockchain address. It can be shared freely, allowing others to send you funds or verify your digital signatures.

Asymmetric Relationship

While anyone can verify a signature with your public key, only you, with your private key, can create that signature. This ensures authenticity and non-repudiation.

Private Key

Secret, used to SIGN transactions

Public Key

Shareable, used to VERIFY signatures

Key Takeaway

Public-key cryptography uses a private key to sign transactions and a public key to verify them, ensuring secure and verifiable digital identity and asset ownership.
