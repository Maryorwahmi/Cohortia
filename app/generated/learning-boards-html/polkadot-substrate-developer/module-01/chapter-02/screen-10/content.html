PoW vs. PoS: A Comparison

Different Approaches to Blockchain Consensus

Proof of Work (PoW)

  • Mechanism: Miners solve complex computational puzzles.
  • Resource: High energy consumption (electricity).
  • Security: Very robust, requires 51% attack (control of network hash rate).
  • Scalability: Often limited transaction throughput.

Proof of Stake (PoS)

  • Mechanism: Validators 'stake' cryptocurrency as collateral.
  • Resource: Economic stake (cryptocurrency).
  • Security: Robust, relies on economic disincentives (slashing).
  • Scalability: Generally higher transaction throughput.
Key Takeaway

While both PoW and PoS secure blockchains, PoW uses computational power and energy, whereas PoS uses economic stake, leading to differences in efficiency and scalability.
