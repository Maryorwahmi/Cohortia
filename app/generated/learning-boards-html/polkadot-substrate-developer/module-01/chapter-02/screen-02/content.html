Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

01

Define Blockchain Architecture

Explain blockchain as a distributed, immutable ledger and its core components, such as blocks and the chain structure. This skill is fundamental for understanding decentralized data management.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Analyze Immutability with Hashing

Understand how cryptographic hashing ensures data integrity and prevents tampering within the blockchain. This is critical for maintaining trust in the ledger's historical record.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Compare Consensus Mechanisms

Differentiate between Proof of Work (PoW) and Proof of Stake (PoS), explaining their operational principles and trade-offs. This knowledge is key to understanding network security and efficiency.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Apply Public-Key Cryptography

Grasp the basics of public-key cryptography and its role in securing blockchain transactions and identities. This mechanism ensures authenticity and non-repudiation in a decentralized environment.
