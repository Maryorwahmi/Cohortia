Proof of Work (PoW)

Computational Effort for Network Security

Miners & Puzzles

Participants (miners) compete to solve complex mathematical puzzles requiring significant computational power. This 'work' validates transactions and secures the network.

Block Creation & Rewards

The first miner to solve the puzzle adds the next block to the chain and is rewarded with cryptocurrency and transaction fees. This incentivizes honest participation.

Security & Cost

PoW makes it economically unfeasible for malicious actors to alter the chain due to the immense computational power needed. However, it is energy-intensive and can face scalability issues.

Key Takeaway

Proof of Work relies on computational competition to secure the blockchain, making it robust against attacks but resource-intensive.
