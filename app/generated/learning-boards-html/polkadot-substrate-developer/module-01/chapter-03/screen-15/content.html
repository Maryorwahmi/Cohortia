Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts — Quick Reference
Relay Chain
Consensus, governance, and pooled security for the network.
Parachain
Specialized chain with a leased slot and steady access to shared security.
Parathread / Bridge
Pay-as-you-go access and external chain connections with distinct tradeoffs.
Final Note

Keep these three cards in mind when planning architecture: they guide choices about cost, security, and interoperability.
