Governance

Community-driven upgrades and referenda

Proposal lifecycle
Proposals move through stages to ensure review and community input before enactment.
DOT holder role
Holders vote on protocol parameters and upgrades, shaping network direction.
Concrete Example
A referendum to adjust auction timing would be proposed, discussed, and then voted on by DOT holders, with clear on‑chain records of the decision process.
Key Takeaway

On‑chain governance enables transparent, community-led evolution of the network but requires active participation and clear proposals.
