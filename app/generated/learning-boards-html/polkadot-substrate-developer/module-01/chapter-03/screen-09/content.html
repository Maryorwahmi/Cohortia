Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Bridges: Connecting to External Chains
Definition

Bridges connect Polkadot to other blockchains

Purpose
Enable transfer of assets and data across chains that are not native Polkadot participants.
Implementation
Can be Parachain-based or separate protocols that verify external chain state.
Risk
Different finality and trust models create additional security considerations.
Concrete Example
Bridging an ERC‑20 token into a Polkadot Parachain lets it be used in local DeFi primitives while maintaining a record of its origin chain through the bridge verification mechanism.
Key Takeaway

Bridges enable broader interoperability but must be evaluated for trust assumptions and how they verify external chain state.
