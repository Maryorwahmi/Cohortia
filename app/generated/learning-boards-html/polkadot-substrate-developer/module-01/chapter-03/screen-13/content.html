Activity

Explore Parachain projects

Step 1
Find two projects: one DeFi and one privacy or identity-focused project, and note whether each is a Parachain or dApp.
Step 2
For each, explain how they leverage shared security, interoperability, or Bridges in 100–150 words.
Step 3
Summarize which architecture you’d recommend for a sample project and why.
Example Summaries
A DeFi hub as a Parachain benefits from stable throughput and shared security. A privacy compute service can be a Parachain to offer consistent availability and integration for other chains via message passing.
Key Takeaway

Hands-on comparison of projects clarifies how architecture choices map to real product needs and constraints.
