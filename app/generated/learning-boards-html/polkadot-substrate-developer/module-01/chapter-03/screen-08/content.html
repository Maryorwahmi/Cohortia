Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Parachain vs Parathread
Parachain
Leased slot
Continuous block production and reliable throughput.
Best for
Projects with steady, high-volume needs like DeFi platforms.
Parathread
Pay-as-you-go
Competes for block inclusion when needed, lowering continuous cost.
Best for
Projects with intermittent activity or those testing product-market fit.
Decision prompt
Assess traffic patterns, budget, and the need for consistent latency when choosing between Parachain and Parathread.
Key Takeaway

Choose slots for steady availability; choose Parathreads for flexible and cost-sensitive approaches.
