Decision Criteria
Bandwidth & Throughput
Continuous high traffic favors a Parachain. Intermittent or bursty workloads may be suited to a Parathread.
Cost & Slot Access
Leasing a slot involves auction economics; Parathreads use a pay-as-you-go model that can be cheaper for some use cases.
Interoperability Needs
Projects needing cross-chain flows or connections to external networks may require Bridges or specific Parachain features.
Assessment Prep

Practice explaining a project's choice in 2–3 sentences: state the problem, list the tradeoffs, and recommend Parachain or Parathread with a short rationale.
