Architecture

Custom runtimes and leased slots

Customization
Design runtime logic and economics specific to your application domain.
Slot leasing
Acquire a dedicated slot, usually through auction, for continuous block production access.
Shared benefits
Gain interoperability and pooled security while operating independently.
Concrete Example
A finance Parachain sets transaction ordering and fee models to optimize settlement. Rather than building its own validator network, it leases a slot and relies on Relay Chain validators for security.
Key Takeaway

Parachains offer high customization and continuous connectivity, but teams must plan for the cost and operational responsibilities of leasing a slot.
