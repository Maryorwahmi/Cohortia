Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Query vs Tx
api.query (Read)
Snapshot reads of on-chain storage. No signing required and no state change occurs.
  • Use for balances, storage maps, and constants.
  • Instant and safe to call frequently for debugging; consider rate limits for public RPCs.
api.tx (Write)
Builds extrinsics that mutate state. Requires signing and payment of fees.
  • Use signAndSend to broadcast and monitor lifecycle events.
  • Always verify sender, amount, and parameters before signing.
Key takeaway

Choose query for reads, tx for writes, and subscriptions for real-time notifications; treat hash as broadcast and check status to confirm finalization.
