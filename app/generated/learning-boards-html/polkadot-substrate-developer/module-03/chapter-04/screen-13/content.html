Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Production Practices
Secure signing
Use hardware wallets or secure signing services instead of mnemonic URIs in production.
Trusted endpoints
Prefer nodes you control or vetted providers to avoid incorrect state or censorship.
Monitoring & logging
Log transaction hashes, statuses, and subscription callbacks so you can trace and recover from failures.
