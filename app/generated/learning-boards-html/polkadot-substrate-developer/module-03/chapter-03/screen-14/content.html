Pallet
A modular runtime module encapsulating storage, dispatchables, events, and errors.
Storage
Choose StorageValue, StorageMap, or StorageDoubleMap based on your keying and access pattern.
Dispatchables & Events
Dispatchables change state and emit events; errors provide precise failure reasons for callers and UIs.
Takeaway

Design pallets to be modular, observable, and safe. Validate origins, pick correct storage, and plan migrations for upgrades.
