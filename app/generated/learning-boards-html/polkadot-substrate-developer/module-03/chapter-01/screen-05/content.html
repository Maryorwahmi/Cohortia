Module 3›Chapter 3.1›Node vs. Runtime: A Clear Separation
Substrate Node

The Infrastructure Layer

  • •Executable program running on your computer.
  • •Handles networking, peer discovery, transaction pooling.
  • •Manages block production and finalization.
  • •Provides the 'how' of running a blockchain.
Example

A node ensures your blockchain can connect to others and relay transactions across the network.

Substrate Runtime

The Custom Logic Layer

  • •WASM blob defining unique blockchain features.
  • •Dictates how accounts work, allowed transactions, balance management.
  • •Provides the 'what' and 'why' of your blockchain's behavior.
Example

The runtime defines the specific rules for a custom token, like its supply and transfer conditions.

Key Takeaway

The node is the generic engine, while the runtime is the custom software that makes your blockchain unique and functional.
