Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Pallet

A self-contained module within FRAME that encapsulates specific blockchain logic, like managing balances or custom features.

WASM (WebAssembly)

A binary instruction format for a stack-based virtual machine. Substrate runtimes are compiled to WASM for platform-agnostic execution and forkless upgrades.

Summary Takeaway

Pallets are the modular units of blockchain logic, and WASM is the underlying technology enabling the runtime's flexibility and upgradeability.
