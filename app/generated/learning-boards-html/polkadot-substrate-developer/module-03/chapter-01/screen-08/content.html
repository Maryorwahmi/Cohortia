Pallets: Self-Contained Logic Modules

A pallet is a self-contained module within FRAME that encapsulates specific blockchain logic, such as managing accounts or balances.

  • •Each pallet has its own storage, events, errors, and dispatchables.
  • •Enables focused development for specific features.
  • •Reduces risk by isolating logic.
Concrete Example

The pallet_balances module handles all aspects of token management, from transfers to locking funds.

Key Takeaway

Pallets are the modular units that allow you to build and customize your blockchain's features with precision and maintainability.
