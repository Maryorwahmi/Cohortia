Practical Application

Implementing Custom Digital Assets

To add a new feature like unique digital assets (NFTs) to your blockchain, you would primarily add a new FRAME pallet within the Substrate runtime.

  • •The runtime is where all custom business logic resides.
  • •FRAME pallets are modular building blocks for such logic.
  • •Avoid modifying the generic Substrate node.
Scenario Breakdown

A new pallet_assets would define asset creation, ownership, transfers, and associated events/errors.

Key Takeaway

FRAME's modularity allows you to extend your blockchain with highly specific, custom features by simply adding new pallets to the runtime.
