Key Indicators

Look for 💡 New epoch and ✨ Imported #X messages, which confirm your node is actively producing and finalizing blocks.

What These Messages Mean

  • `New epoch`: Indicates the start of a new block production cycle.
  • `Imported #X`: Confirms a new block with number `X` has been successfully added to your chain.
  • Increasing Block Numbers: Shows your blockchain is progressing and active.
Troubleshooting Tip

If you don't see block production messages, check your terminal for error messages or ensure your node is running with the correct flags.

Key Takeaway

Interpreting node output, especially block production messages, is crucial for verifying your local blockchain's health and activity.
