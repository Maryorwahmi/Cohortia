Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Bitwise Operators
Operators
  • & bitwise AND
  • | bitwise OR
  • ^ bitwise XOR
  • ~ bitwise NOT
  • << left shift, >> right shift
Concrete example
5 & 3 -> 0101 & 0011 = 0001 -> 1
Use cases
Flags and masks, efficient multiplications/divisions by powers of two, and compact representation of boolean sets.
Key Takeaway

Bitwise tools operate on binary digits; understand masks and document masks with named constants for readable code.
