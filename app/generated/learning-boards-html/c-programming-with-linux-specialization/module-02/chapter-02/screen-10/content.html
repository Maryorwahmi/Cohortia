Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Precedence & Associativity
Rule examples
(5 + 3) * 2 = 16 because parentheses force addition first.
5 + 3 * 2 = 11 because multiplication has higher precedence.
Associativity example
10 / 2 * 5 = (10 / 2) * 5 = 25 because / and * are left-associative.
Practical advice
When expressions become complex, add parentheses to show intended grouping. This reduces mental load and bugs.
Key Takeaway

Precedence and associativity determine evaluation order; use parentheses to make grouping explicit and avoid surprises.
