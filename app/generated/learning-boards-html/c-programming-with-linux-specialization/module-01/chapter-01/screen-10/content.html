Comparison

Copy versus Move

Understand intent: preserve originals or reorganize your project tree.

cp

Copies files or directories. Use it to make backups or duplicate templates.

mv

Moves or renames files and directories. Use it to reorganize or rename safely after confirmation.

Overwrite caution

Both commands can overwrite; list the destination first or use interactive flags where available.

Key Takeaway

When in doubt, copy first as a reversible step, then move or remove after verification.

Examples
# Copy a file to another directory
cp chapter1_code/hello.c chapter2_code/

# Move and rename in one step
mv chapter2_code/hello.c chapter2_code/first_program.c

# Verify
ls -la chapter2_code
Notice the two-step pattern: copy for preservation, then move to reorganize or rename when you're ready.
Practice copying starter files as templates to avoid losing your original work, especially when experimenting with changes.
