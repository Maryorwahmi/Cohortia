Strategy list

Practical escapes

Random walk
Perturb the force after detecting stalled motion for N iterations; simple and local.
Wavefront propagation
Precompute a global potential that expands from the goal; avoids local minima on the grid.
Navigation functions
Mathematically guaranteed potentials without spurious minima; complex to construct.
Hybrid approach
Use a global planner to find a coarse path, then local potentials to avoid dynamic obstacles and smooth motion.
Concrete trade-offs
Random walk is cheap and easy but may fail in clutter. Wavefronts and navigation functions give stronger guarantees but require precomputation and may not adapt to moving obstacles. Hybrids provide a practical balance: global planners set the strategic route while potential fields handle tactical avoidance.
