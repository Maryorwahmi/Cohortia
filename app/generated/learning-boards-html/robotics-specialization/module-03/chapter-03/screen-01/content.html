Concept Map
Reactive view
Goal
Attractive potential
Quadratic pull, negative gradient leads the robot downhill toward the goal.
Obstacle
Repulsive potential
Short-range inverse effect that grows sharply as distance drops.
Net behavior
Net force equals attractive plus repulsive; motion follows that vector.
