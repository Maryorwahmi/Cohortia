Module 4 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Potential field
Attractive and repulsive components define a force landscape.
Local minima
Balanced forces can trap the robot; detect stalls and escape or replan.
Gradient descent
Iteratively move waypoints down the negative gradient to smooth and optimize paths.
Summary takeaway

Treat potential fields as fast local reflexes and sampling-based planners as strategic tools. Combine them and use gradient refinement to achieve both safety and smoothness.
