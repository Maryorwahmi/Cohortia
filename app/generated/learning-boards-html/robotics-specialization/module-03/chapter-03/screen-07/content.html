Module 4 › Chapter 3.3 › Total Force
Process Flow

Sense → Compute → Act

At each control step: measure distances, compute repulsive gradients, compute attractive force, sum to F_total, and command motion.
1
Sense obstacles
Find nearest obstacles and compute d(q) and ∇d(q).
2
Compute forces
F_att from goal and sum of F_rep for active obstacles.
3
Act
Convert F_total into a velocity command, clamp magnitude, and send to the controller.
Example note
Summing repulsive forces from multiple obstacles is common; consider normalization or clamping to avoid a single obstacle producing an extreme response.
Debugging tip
Plot force vectors across the workspace to reveal saddles and small valleys that indicate local minima.
Key Takeaway

The control loop must compute gradients correctly and manage multiple obstacles, clamping extremes to keep motion safe and stable.
