Sampler
Uniform vs biased sampling
Uniform sampling is simple but may miss narrow passages. Bias toward boundaries or known critical regions to increase roadmap connectivity where it matters.
Neighbor rules
k-nearest balances connectivity and cost; radius-based neighbors adapt to sampling density and dimension.
Connection tests
Straight-line checks and discretization
For articulated robots, verify the interpolated trajectory by sampling along the segment. Resolution affects both safety and cost.
Concrete tip
Increase neighbor radius or k in sparse areas, and bias sampling near narrow openings to improve connectivity without excessive samples.
Misconception
Adding samples indiscriminately doesn't guarantee finding narrow passages; targeted sampling or adaptive neighbor rules are often required.
Key Takeaway

Choose sampling and neighbor parameters deliberately: they set the roadmap's ability to discover narrow passages and balance cost against connectivity.
