Sampling scales
Sample instead of grid in high-dimensional C-spaces to avoid combinatorial explosion.
PRM vs RRT
PRM for many queries in static maps; RRT for single queries; RRT* when quality matters.
Optimize collision checks
Use lazy checks and spatial structures; collision-testing strategy shapes planner performance.
Final takeaway
Combine algorithmic understanding with measured experiments to choose and tune planners for real robots.
