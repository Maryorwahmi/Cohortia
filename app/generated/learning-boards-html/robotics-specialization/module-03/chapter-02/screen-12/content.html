Prompt
Planner choice for a 7-DOF arm
Write a short justification choosing between A-star, PRM, RRT, and RRT* for a cluttered environment. Include considerations of time, collision checks, and whether the scene is static.
Assessment checklist
  • State dimensionality and why A-star is infeasible.
  • Declare environment dynamics and query count.
  • Choose a planner and justify trade-offs.
Reflection guide
Consider how many collision checks each planner will perform, and whether computation is performed offline or online. If you need high-quality paths for many queries in a static scene, favor PRM or RRT*; for single, fast, or changing tasks, favor RRT.
Key Takeaway

Ground your planner choice in constraints: time, environment dynamics, query frequency, and desired path quality.
