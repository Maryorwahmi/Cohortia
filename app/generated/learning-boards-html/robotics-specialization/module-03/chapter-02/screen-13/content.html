Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
Probabilistic completeness
More samples increase the probability of finding a valid path when one exists.
PRM
Offline construction then fast multi-query search; best for static environments.
RRT & RRT*
RRT for fast single-query planning; RRT* adds rewiring for asymptotic optimality.
Summary takeaway
Match planner to problem: environment dynamics, number of queries, collision-check cost, and path-quality goals determine whether PRM, RRT, or RRT* is the best fit.
