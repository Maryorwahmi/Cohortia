Configuration Space and Graph Search
Transform workspace geometry into tractable search problems for robots of any dimension.
In this lesson
Understand C-space, C-obstacles, grid/graph models, and three core search algorithms used in robotics.
Why it matters
Motion planning is the foundation for safe robot behavior across arms, mobile bases, drones, and surgical systems.
Explore Key Topics
C-space vs Workspace
Obstacles become forbidden regions in a space of robot poses.
Graph Search
Turn the C-space into nodes and edges for pathfinding.
A* & Heuristics
Use admissible heuristics to guide search toward the goal efficiently.
Visual concept
Workspace transforms to C-space; robot becomes a point.
Illustration
Imagine a circular mobile robot. In workspace it is a disk moving around obstacles. In C-space, expand obstacles by the robot radius and treat the robot as a point navigating free space.
Practical note
Higher-dimensional robots produce high-dimensional C-spaces, so planners often use discretized graphs or sampling methods to remain tractable.
