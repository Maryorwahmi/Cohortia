Configuration Space
All robot poses; obstacles become forbidden regions.
Graph Representation
Discretize C-space into nodes and edges for search.
Search Algorithms
BFS, Dijkstra, and A* address different cost and performance needs.
Summary
Model your C-space carefully, represent it in a graph appropriate for your robot, and choose an algorithm and heuristic that balance optimality and practical runtime.
