Degrees of freedom matter
Higher DOFs increase state-space size and change planner feasibility.
• Low DOF (mobile base): grid-based A* is practical and fast.
• High DOF (manipulator): joint-space grids explode; prefer sampling or hierarchical approaches.
• Geometry matters: circular robots allow simple expansions; articulated robots need swept-volume or collision checking per configuration.
Key Takeaway

Plan in the simplest representation that captures necessary motion constraints; avoid unnecessarily high-resolution discretization for high-DOF systems.

Design checklist
  • List robot DOFs and decide which are essential for your task.
  • Decide representation: grid, sampling, or hybrid.
  • Choose collision modeling fidelity: conservative expansion vs exact swept volumes.
This checklist helps you balance safety, path quality, and computational cost when designing a planner for a specific robot and task.
