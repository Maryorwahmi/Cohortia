Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Nonlinear dynamics
Legged robots include trigonometry, switching, and velocity effects that produce rich behaviors.
Limit cycles & stability
Stable limit cycles represent repeatable gaits and must attract nearby trajectories.
Series Elastic Actuators
A motor, spring, load chain that gives force sensing, shock tolerance, and energy storage.
Energy & COT
COT = E / (m·g·d); recover energy with springs to improve efficiency.
Actuator trade-offs
Balance torque, speed, power, and bandwidth for each joint role.
Technology choices
DC/servos, SEAs, hydraulics, pneumatics each offer different power, compliance, and control trade-offs.
Summary takeaway
Use phase-space analysis to design controllers and choose actuators that enable stable, energy-efficient, and safe legged locomotion.
