Actuator types and practical trade-offs
Map actuator properties to joint roles and mission needs.
DC motors + gearboxes
Pros: high torque, mature technology. Cons: rigid, transmits shocks, may need wide gear ratios.
Servo motors
Pros: integrated control, easy to use. Cons: often underpowered for large legged robots.
Series Elastic Actuators (SEA)
Pros: compliance, force sensing, energy storage, safety. Cons: spring tuning and possible bandwidth trade-offs.
Hydraulic
Pros: very high power density for explosive motions. Cons: noisy, complex pumps and maintenance.
Pneumatic
Pros: light and compliant. Cons: low power density and difficult precise control.
Key Takeaway

Match actuator type to joint role: SEAs for interaction and energy recovery, hydraulics where extreme power is unavoidable, and DC/servos for simpler tasks.
