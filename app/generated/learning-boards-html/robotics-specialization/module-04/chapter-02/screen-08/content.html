Process Flow
Energy flow and specific cost of transport
Trace kinetic, potential, and elastic energy during locomotion and compute COT to evaluate efficiency.
Energy flow
Kinetic energy rises when limbs propel the body. Potential energy varies with height. Springs store elastic energy during compression and return it on extension, reducing motor work.
COT formula
COT = E / (m * g * d). Use g = 9.81 when computing. Compare to biological values to judge efficiency.
Concrete: with m=10 kg, d=100 m, E=5000 J, COT = 5000 / (10 * 9.81 * 100) ≈ 0.051.
Misconception: weight alone doesn't determine COT; actuator efficiency and elastic storage are major factors.
Key Takeaway

Design for energy recovery with compliant components and improve actuator efficiency to lower COT.

Energy cycle card
Kinetic → Potential → Elastic → Kinetic. Springs reduce motor energy when timed with the stride.
Design prompt
Consider where to place springs: series elasticity at ankle for push-off or parallel springs for limb support. Evaluate energy return vs control complexity.
