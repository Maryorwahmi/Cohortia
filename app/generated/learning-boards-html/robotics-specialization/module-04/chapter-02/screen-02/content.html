Learning Objectives
Bloom steps: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Analyze nonlinear dynamics
Plot phase portraits and spot fixed points, spirals, and open trajectories to assess stability for legged motions.
Mnemonic: phase = position × velocity
Verified competency milestone
02
Understand energy and power
Compute specific cost of transport and trace how kinetic, potential, and elastic energy move through a stride.
Mnemonic: K→P→E cycle
Verified competency milestone
03
Select actuators by requirement
Balance torque, speed, and power needs and check bandwidth requirements for balance and tracking.
Mnemonic: T × ω = P
Verified competency milestone
04
Evaluate compliance trade-offs
Decide when springs improve safety and energy recovery versus when stiffness and bandwidth are essential.
Mnemonic: spring = safety + storage
Verified competency milestone
