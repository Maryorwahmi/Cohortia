What to Remember Quick Reference
Kinematics
Compute v and ω from wheel speeds for motion prediction.
Odometry
Integrate increments; expect and correct drift.
Nonholonomic
Design controls that generate admissible velocities; plan sequences to reach poses.
Final reflection
When designing a mobile system, ask: does the environment favor wheel efficiency or leg adaptability, and how often will you rely on external sensors to correct odometry?
