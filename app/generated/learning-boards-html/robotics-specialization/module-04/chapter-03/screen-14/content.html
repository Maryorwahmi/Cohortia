What to Remember Key Concepts
Differential kinematics
v = (v_R + v_L)/2, ω = (v_R − v_L)/L.
Odometry
Integrate velocities into Δx, Δy, Δθ. Errors accumulate without external correction.
Nonholonomic
Velocity constraints that limit instantaneous motion but not reachability through sequences of motions.
Takeaway
Combine kinematic understanding with simulation and sensor fusion to build reliable wheeled robot navigation systems.
