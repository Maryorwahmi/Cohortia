Module 5 › Chapter 4.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Derive forward kinematics
Translate left/right wheel speeds and track width into linear and angular velocity. This is essential for motion prediction and control.
Verified competency milestone
02
Implement odometry
Use encoder readings to integrate motion over time and update x, y, and heading. Understand sources of drift so you can compensate later.
Verified competency milestone
03
Explain nonholonomic constraints
Recognize velocity-only constraints and their control implications. Distinguish velocity constraints from position constraints and give examples.
Verified competency milestone
04
Compare locomotion types
List trade-offs between wheeled, legged, and hybrid platforms and choose based on terrain, speed, cost, and complexity.
Verified competency milestone
