Architecture Rocker-bogie
Suspension for rough terrain
How it works
Pairs of linked rocker and bogie arms let wheels articulate independently, distributing loads and keeping the body stable as wheels climb obstacles.
Benefits
  • Handles obstacles larger than wheel radius.
  • Maintains traction by keeping multiple wheels on the ground.
  • Used on planetary rovers for proven reliability.
Practical note
Rocker-bogie adds mechanical complexity and weight, but it enables wheeled platforms to operate in environments where simple wheels would fail. It is not a leg system but a passive articulation strategy.
Key Takeaway

Good suspension design makes wheels useful off-road; rover mobility often depends as much on mechanics as on motors.
