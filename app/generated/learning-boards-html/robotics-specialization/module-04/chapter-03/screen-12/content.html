Comparison Locomotion types
Pros and cons at a glance
Wheeled
Efficient, simple control, low cost, high speed on flat ground. Poor on stairs and soft terrain.
Legged
Versatile on rough terrain, can step over obstacles and climb. Higher energy and control complexity.
Hybrid
Combine wheels and legs for adaptable locomotion. Mechanically complex but can offer the best operational range.
Real-world note
Rover designs, like planetary explorers, often mix wheel efficiency with suspension or leg-like features to handle rocks and slopes while maintaining long traverse ranges.
