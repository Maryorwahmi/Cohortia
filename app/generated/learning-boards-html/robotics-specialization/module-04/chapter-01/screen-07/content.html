Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › SLIP Dynamics
Stance equations
m * ẍ = k * (L0 - L) * sin(α)
m * z̈ = k * (L0 - L) * cos(α) - m * g
These equations show spring force split into horizontal and vertical components, driving center-of-mass accelerations during contact.
Flight equations
ẍ = 0
z̈ = -g
During flight, horizontal velocity is constant and vertical acceleration is gravity-only.
Transitions and events
  • Touchdown: when leg reaches ground at the touchdown angle and contact is made.
  • Liftoff: when spring force drops to zero or leg reaches rest length and no normal force remains.
  • Handle events precisely in simulation to avoid spurious behavior across phases.
Misconception: hybrid means unpredictability. In fact, hybrid systems can be predictable if event definitions and parameters are chosen carefully.
Key Takeaway

Treat stance and flight as separate dynamical modes with clear event rules. That separation makes analysis and simulation tractable despite hybrid behavior.
