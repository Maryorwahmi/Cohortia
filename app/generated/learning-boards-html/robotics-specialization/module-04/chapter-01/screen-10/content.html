Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Trade-offs
Trade-off table
Stability vs Speed
Walking is slower but statically stable; running is faster but dynamically balanced and relies on active control.
Energy vs Versatility
Statically stable gaits cost energy to maintain support; dynamic gaits can use elastic energy storage for better efficiency at speed.
Leg complexity
More legs increase passive stability and redundancy but also increase coordination and energy costs.
Key Takeaway

Designers must prioritize which property matters most for the mission and tune parameters and morphology accordingly.

Application example

For a collapsed-building mission, adaptability and obstacle negotiation take priority over raw energy efficiency; designers might accept more legs or complex control to ensure access to survivors.

Misconception check: optimizing for one metric in isolation often degrades others. Effective design balances mission needs with practical actuation and sensing limits.
