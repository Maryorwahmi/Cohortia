Mode-aware control pattern
Design controllers that recognize and plan across modes: swing, touchdown, stance, liftoff. Use event detection to switch gains and objectives at precise instants.
Swing phase
Plan foot trajectory and final placement. Actuation changes the leg configuration but cannot instantly change center-of-mass state.
Stance phase
Use ground reaction forces and spring-like behavior to control energy and balance. Contact forces dominate dynamics.
Practical guideline
  • Detect contact events reliably and switch control goals at events.
  • Exploit passive dynamics during stance to save energy where possible.
  • Use fast corrective actions after impact to reject disturbances not handled passively.
Misconception check: treating the system as fully actuated through events leads to unrealistic controllers. Respect underactuation during flight and exploit mechanics during stance.
Key Takeaway

Hybrid-aware controllers and event-driven switching are essential for robust, energy-aware legged locomotion.
