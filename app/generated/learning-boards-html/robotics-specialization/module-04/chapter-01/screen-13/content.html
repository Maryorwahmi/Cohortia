Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Template → Robot
Step-by-step
  1. Use template to choose k, L0, α, and nominal v for desired gait shape.
  2. Design mechanical compliance (springs or series elasticity) to approximate the ideal spring.
  3. Implement event detection and mode-aware controllers for swing, touchdown, and stance.
  4. Validate in simulation with Poincaré analysis, then iterate in hardware with robustness checks.
Key Takeaway

Templates inform system design, but successful hardware needs compliant elements, event-aware control, and robustness to nonidealities.

Engineering pitfalls
  • Neglecting actuator bandwidth leads to inability to track desired leg forces.
  • Ignoring sensor latencies can break event timing and produce unstable transitions.
  • Over-relying on templates without fallback control reduces resilience to large disturbances.
