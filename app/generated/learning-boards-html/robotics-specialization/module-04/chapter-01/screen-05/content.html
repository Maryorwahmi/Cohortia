What is a template?

A minimalist model that captures essential mechanical behavior for analysis and design.

Use case
Predict center-of-mass motion for running, then select leg stiffness and touchdown angle to produce desired hop height.
Limitations
Templates omit limb inertias and actuator limits, so they guide but do not replace full robot models for control implementation.
Analogy
Think of a template as a map that highlights the main roads; you still need the full city map to navigate every street.
How templates are used
  • Parameter selection for desired gait shape and periodicity.
  • Simplified stability analysis to find robust operating ranges.
  • Design of reduced-order controllers that enforce template behavior in hardware.
Key Takeaway

Use templates to focus on the dynamics that matter most, then extend models for actuator and sensor realities when building controllers.
