Simulation steps
  1. Set parameters: m=1 kg, k=1000 N/m, L0=1 m, α=70°, v=2 m/s.
  2. Integrate stance ODE with event detection until liftoff.
  3. Compute flight analytically until touchdown at next contact geometry.
  4. Plot x vs z and leg length over time. Vary α and k to compare results.
Pitfall: missing or incorrect event handling yields discontinuities. Use the solver's event callbacks to capture touchdown and liftoff cleanly.
