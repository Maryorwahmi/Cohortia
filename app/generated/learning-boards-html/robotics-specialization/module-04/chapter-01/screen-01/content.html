Legged Locomotion and Bioinspired Templates

Understand why legs provide adaptability across uneven terrain and how templates such as SLIP and compass gait simplify design and analysis for legged robots.

In this lesson
Why legs exceed wheels on complex surfaces, SLIP dynamics, compass gait, and practical trade-offs.
Coming up
A hands-on SLIP simulation exercise using provided parameters to explore gait changes.
Why it matters
Designing robust legged robots is key to search-and-rescue, planetary exploration, and navigating human environments.
Concept map
Templates connect dynamics to design
SLIP
Point mass on a springy leg: models running as stance and flight phases with parameters k, L0, α, and v.
Compass gait
Two rigid legs and a hip mass produce passive walking via falling and catching.
Parameters
Leg stiffness, rest length, touchdown angle and speed shape trajectories and stability.
Trade-offs
Balance stability, efficiency, and adaptability when choosing gait and morphology.
Key Takeaway

Templates simplify design by isolating essential mechanics. They let us predict behavior and choose parameters that produce stable, efficient gaits for specific tasks.
