Legged vs Wheeled
Legs step and adapt to uneven terrain, wheels are efficient on flat, prepared surfaces.
Bioinspired Templates
Simplified mechanical models that capture key dynamics for analysis and design guidance.
SLIP Model
Point mass on a springy leg; four parameters determine running and hopping dynamics and stability.
Summary

Use these concepts as quick references when choosing locomotion modes, performing simulations, and designing control architectures for legged robots.
