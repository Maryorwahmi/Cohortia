Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Hands-on Activity
Lab steps
  1. Motor control script: forward, backward, left, right for two seconds each.
  2. Sensor verification: print distance every 0.5s and compare to a ruler.
  3. Implement avoidance with 20 cm threshold and test obstacles.
  4. Log data to CSV and plot to analyze response.
Troubleshooting checklist
  • No motor response: check motor battery, driver enable pins, and wiring polarity.
  • Pi resets: verify separate motor power and common ground, check voltage under load.
  • No echo from sensor: validate TRIG/ECHO pins and add timeouts to the read loop.
Safety and best practices
Add an emergency stop switch on the motor battery line, use protected LiPo circuits if used, and test on a flat open surface to avoid falls. Label connectors and keep wiring neat to speed debugging.
