Core parts
Raspberry Pi, motor driver, motors, chassis, power, and sensors.
Motor driver necessity

Motor drivers handle current spikes and direction switching. Never connect motors directly to GPIO pins — the driver is your current bridge and protection layer.

Sensors overview
  • HC-SR04 ultrasonic: distance via sonar, medium range and cheap.
  • IR proximity: short range and edge detection.
  • MPU6050 IMU: accelerometer and gyroscope via I2C.
  • Pi Camera: optional visual feedback for line following or detection.
Parts checklist
Raspberry Pi 4 or Zero 2 W
Motor driver: L298N or TB6612FNG
Two DC motors with wheels (encoders optional)
Power: USB bank for Pi, separate battery for motors
Breadboard, jumper wires, chassis, mounting hardware
Key Takeaway

Match components by power and interface: Pi handles logic, driver handles current, sensors provide perception.
