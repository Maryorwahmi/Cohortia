Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Quick Reminders
Verify wiring
Double-check polarity, common ground, and BCM pin numbering before powering up.
Use timeouts
Guard sensor reads with timeouts to avoid blocking the control loop indefinitely.
Log and analyze
Record timestamped CSVs for diagnostics and plotting after each trial.
Final thought

Small, repeatable checks and clear logs make physical robotics development predictable and safe.
