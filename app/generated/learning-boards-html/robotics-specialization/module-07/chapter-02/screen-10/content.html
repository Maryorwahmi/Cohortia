Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Data Logging
What to record
  • Timestamp
  • Distance reading (cm)
  • Left and right motor speeds
  • Behavior state (forward, turning, stopped)
  • Optional: battery voltage, encoder ticks
CSV example
timestamp,distance_cm,left_speed,right_speed,state
1620000000.123,35.2,0.6,0.6,forward
1620000000.223,18.7,-0.5,0.5,turning
Why this helps

Plots of distance vs time and motor commands reveal reaction latency and false positives, guiding threshold and delay tuning.
