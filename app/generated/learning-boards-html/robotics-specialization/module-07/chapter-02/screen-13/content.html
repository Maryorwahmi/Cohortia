Power rules
  • Use separate motor and Pi power supplies.
  • Confirm common ground between supplies.
  • Include a power switch and, for LiPo, a protection circuit.
Emergency and testing

Install an emergency stop on the motor supply line, and always test movements with short pulses before long runs. Charge and store batteries safely and inspect wiring regularly.

Quick checklist
  1. Polarity checked
  2. Separate supplies verified
  3. Emergency stop present
