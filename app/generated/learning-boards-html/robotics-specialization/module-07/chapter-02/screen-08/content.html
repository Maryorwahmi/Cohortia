Behavior cards
Obstacle avoidance — reactive turn at a threshold distance. Pros: simple, robust. Cons: no map awareness.
Line following — uses IR or camera to stay on a marked line. Pros: precise navigation on a course. Cons: needs a prepared surface.
Wall following — maintain distance to wall for corridor navigation. Pros: steady corridor traversal. Cons: fails at openings or gaps.
Random walk + avoidance — explores without a map. Pros: simple exploration. Cons: inefficient coverage.
When to combine

A practical strategy is behavior arbitration: prefer line following when a line is detected, otherwise use obstacle avoidance. This hybrid approach leverages strengths and mitigates weaknesses of single behaviors.
