I2C wiring

SDA → GPIO 2, SCL → GPIO 3. Enable I2C on the Pi and run an I2C scan to confirm the device address.

Useful readings

Raw accelerometer axes and gyroscope axes. Detect spikes for collisions and integrate angular rates carefully for orientation estimates.

Scenario

If the rover glances a curb and the ultrasonic sensor misses it, the IMU will often detect the sudden drop or rotation. Use that signal to trigger a recovery routine and prevent damage.

Common mistake

Trusting raw IMU orientation without filtering; fuse accelerometer and gyro cautiously and expect drift over time.
