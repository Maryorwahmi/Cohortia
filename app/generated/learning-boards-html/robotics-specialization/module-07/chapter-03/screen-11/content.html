Architecture

Cascade control + EKF

Outer loop (position):
Compute position error and a desired tilt; limit tilt magnitude to safe bounds to avoid instability.
Inner loop (balance):
High-gain PD on theta and theta_dot that stabilizes the pendulum at the commanded tilt.
EKF:
Fuses IMU measurements of theta and theta_dot and encoder x_dot to produce the state estimate used by both loops.
Key Takeaway

Separate fast stabilization from slower position control and use an EKF to handle noisy sensors for stable closed-loop performance.
