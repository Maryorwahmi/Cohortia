Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Covariance Bug
The incorrect code
% Buggy covariance prediction
P = A * P + Q;
Corrected form
% Correct covariance propagation
P = A * P * A' + Q;
Validate with a test where A rotates the state. Compare elements of P step-by-step. Missing A' will break the off-diagonal terms and mislead the Kalman gain computation.
Quick test
  1. Set Q = 0 and choose A as a small rotation matrix.
  2. Initialize P diagonal and step the prediction several times analytically and numerically.
  3. Confirm that P rotates consistent with A in the correct implementation.
Key Takeaway

The A' term preserves covariance orientation. Use simple synthetic tests to reveal its absence.
