Definition

Intrinsics & Bundle Adjustment

Intrinsics include focal length, principal point, and distortion — they define how camera geometry maps to pixels. Bundle adjustment jointly optimizes camera poses and 3D points to minimize reprojection error.

Purpose of bundle adjustment

Refine all camera parameters and 3D points simultaneously to correct accumulated pose and triangulation errors.

Scale ambiguity

Monocular SfM recovers translation only up to a scale factor because the essential matrix determines direction not magnitude. Without a metric reference, absolute distances are unknown.

Practical fix

Introduce a known baseline, a calibration object, or fuse IMU data to recover metric scale.
