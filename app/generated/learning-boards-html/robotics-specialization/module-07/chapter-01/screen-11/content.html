Stability region

Linear controllers are valid near θ = 0. Beyond a threshold angle, nonlinear terms dominate and recovery becomes unlikely. Test limits in simulation by increasing initial tilt until controller fails.

Instrument plots to show when torque saturates and when θ grows rapidly, indicating a fall.
Fail-safe design
  • Threshold check on |θ|, e.g., 30°–50° depending on system.
  • Motor shutoff and brake engage when threshold exceeded.
  • Log pre-fail sensor traces for debugging and tuning.
Misconception

Assuming the controller will always recover if you increase gains. In hardware, actuator limits, delays, and sensor noise can prevent recovery regardless of gains.

Concrete example

Simulate with initial θ = 1 rad and plot torque saturation and angle growth to demonstrate failure modes.

Why it matters

Fail-safes prevent hardware damage and provide useful logs for tuning and debugging after a fall.

Key takeaway

Always pair controllers with safety monitoring and actuator limits; test fail-safe triggers in simulation under noise before hardware trials.
