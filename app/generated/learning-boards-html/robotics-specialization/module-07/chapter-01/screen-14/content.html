Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Key Concepts
MIP dynamics
Coupled nonlinear equations derived from Lagrangian mechanics.
Linearization
Approximate sin and cos near θ=0 to form A and B for controller design.
Cascade PD
Fast inner balance loop for θ and slower outer position loop for x.
EKF
Fuse IMU and encoder data with prediction-update steps to estimate full state.
MATLAB simulation
Use ode45 to integrate nonlinear dynamics and plot true and estimated states.
Safety
Monitor angle thresholds and saturate actuators; include fail-safe motor cutoffs for hardware trials.
Summary

Model accurately, design nested controllers, estimate reliably, simulate thoroughly, and protect hardware with clear safety checks.
