Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Tuning experiments
Gain scenarios
  • Too low Kp_theta: slow corrections, possible fall.
  • Too high Kp_theta: high-frequency oscillation and noise amplification.
  • Good tuning: quick, well-damped response and smooth position tracking.
EKF tuning effects
  • R too small: filter chases noise.
  • Q too large: filter slow to adapt.
  • Balanced: smooth estimates that track true state without oversensitivity.
Concrete exercise

Run three gain sets and plot angle traces. Explain why each trace behaves as it does.

Common mistake

Changing inner and outer gains together without isolating effects. Change one loop at a time to attribute effects properly.

Key takeaway

Systematic experiments and well-labeled plots make tuning decisions reproducible and justifiable in your report.
