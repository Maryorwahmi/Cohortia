Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Hands-on Activity
Activity steps
  1. Define world, body, and camera frames with rotations and translations.
  2. Form T_world_body and T_body_camera as 4×4 matrices.
  3. Compute T_world_camera = T_world_body * T_body_camera.
  4. Transform p_camera_h to p_world and then apply inverse to recover p_camera_h.
  5. Validate with R*R'≈I and det(R)=1 checks.
Starter hint
Use radians for rotation functions and keep the homogeneous 1 in the point vector so translations apply correctly.
Reflection
How does a small error in rotation affect the world coordinates of a point several meters away? Consider that when calibrating sensors.
