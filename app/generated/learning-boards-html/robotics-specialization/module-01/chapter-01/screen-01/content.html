Overview

Why coordinates matter

Every measurement and command in a robot is relative to a frame. Vectors give positions and directions. Matrices rotate, scale, or reflect. Homogeneous matrices combine rotation and translation so you can chain transforms with matrix multiplication.

Key Takeaway

Start every transform by identifying which frame each vector is expressed in. Mixing frames causes the most common mistakes.
