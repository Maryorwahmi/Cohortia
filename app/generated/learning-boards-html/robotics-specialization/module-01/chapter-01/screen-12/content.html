Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Determinant, Eigen, SVD
Determinant
For rotations det(R) = 1. If det ≠ 1 then the matrix scales or reflects space.
Eigenvalues
Solve Av = λv to find directions preserved up to scale. Useful in stability and PCA.
SVD
A = U Σ V^T; use for least-squares, robust decomposition, and vision algorithms.
Key Takeaway

Run quick numeric checks like determinant and R*R'≈I, and use SVD or eigenanalysis when you need stable decompositions or diagnostics.
