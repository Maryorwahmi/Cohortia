Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Vectors & frames
Always annotate the frame and never mix frames without a transform.
Rotations
Use R(θ) in 2D and Rx,Ry,Rz in 3D; order matters for Euler sequences.
Homogeneous transforms
T = [R p; 0 1] lets you chain rotation and translation with multiplication.
Takeaway

Label frames, use homogeneous matrices for chaining, and validate matrices with orthogonality and determinant checks before applying transforms on a real robot.
