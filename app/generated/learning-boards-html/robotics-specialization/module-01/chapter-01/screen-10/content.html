Inverse formula
% For T = [R, p; 0, 1]
T_inv = [R'   -R'*p;
         0     1]

% Where R' is the transpose of R
This avoids a full matrix inverse and is exact for rotation matrices.
Concrete example
Given R and p for a camera in the body frame, compute R' and -R'*p to obtain the camera-to-body transform and validate by applying to a world point and back.
Key Takeaway
Use the R' and -R' p pattern to invert transforms quickly and reliably.
