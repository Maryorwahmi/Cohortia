Frame representation

Represent positions and orientations with vectors and matrices so you can move measurements between frames.

Rotation matrices

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Homogeneous transforms

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Matrix checks & inverses

Compute inverses, determinants, and eigenvalues to validate rotations and to invert frame transforms efficiently.
