Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Chaining Transforms
Step-by-step chain
  1. Compute T_world_camera = T_world_body * T_body_camera.
  2. Express p_camera in homogeneous form p_cam_h = [x;y;z;1].
  3. Compute p_world = T_world_camera * p_cam_h.
  4. Validate with a known reference or inverse transform.
Concrete scenario
Given p_camera = [0.5;0.3;2.0;1], and known T_world_body and T_body_camera, compute p_world and verify with a calibration marker in the environment.
Check
Use the inverse transform to map back to camera frame and confirm you recover p_camera.
