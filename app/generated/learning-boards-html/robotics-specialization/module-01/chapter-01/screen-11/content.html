Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Dot & Cross Products
Dot product
a · b = |a||b|cos(θ). Use it to get cosine of angle or project one vector onto another.
Example
If a and b are orthogonal unit vectors, a · b = 0, indicating perpendicularity.
Cross product
a × b is a vector orthogonal to both; magnitude equals area of parallelogram spanned by a and b.
Robotics use
Compute moment arms, normals, and torques. Use cross to get a perpendicular axis for rotation calculations.
