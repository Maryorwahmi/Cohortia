Practice checklist
Unit test
Verify R * R' equals the identity within numerical tolerance to confirm orthogonality.
Determinant
Check det(R) is 1 to ensure no reflection or scaling was introduced.
Round-trip
Apply a transform and its inverse to a point and verify you recover the original coordinates.
These quick checks form the foundation for confident coordinate arithmetic.
