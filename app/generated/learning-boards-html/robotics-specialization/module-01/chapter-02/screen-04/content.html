Position, Velocity, Acceleration
p(t) → v(t) = dp/dt → a(t) = d2p/dt2. Match these to sensors and understand noise implications.
Definition
p(t) = [x(t),y(t),z(t)]^T; v(t) = dp/dt; a(t) = d2p/dt2.
Concrete example
If x(t) = sin(t): v_x = cos(t), a_x = -sin(t). Use this to validate numerical derivatives in code.
Common mistake
Differentiating noisy encoder signals without filtering causes amplified noise and unstable control loops.
Key Takeaway

Derivatives provide control signals but require filtering or estimation to avoid noisy actuations.

Sensors and quantities
  • IMU provides linear acceleration and angular velocity.
  • Encoders provide joint angles; differentiate for angular velocity.
  • GPS gives position samples that require smoothing and interpolation.
