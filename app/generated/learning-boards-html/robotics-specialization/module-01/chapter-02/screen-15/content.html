Euler: baseline
Fast and easy but sensitive to dt. Good for prototypes and learning.
RK4 & ode45
Higher accuracy per step or adaptive stepping reduces manual tuning and often yields more reliable results.
Stability & stiffness
For stiff systems, use solvers designed for stiffness; otherwise, small dt alone may not be efficient or stable.
Final thought: validate simulation choices by comparing methods and checking physical quantities like energy or expected damping before trusting results on a robot.
