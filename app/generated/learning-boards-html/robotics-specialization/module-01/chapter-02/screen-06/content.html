Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Rotating Frames
v_world = v_body + ω × r
This relation captures how frame rotation adds apparent velocity to points fixed in the body.
Physical example
A point fixed at r on a rotating platform has zero body-frame velocity but nonzero world-frame velocity equal to ω × r.
Coriolis note
When differentiating velocities in rotating frames, cross terms lead to Coriolis-like effects in dynamic equations.
Key Takeaway

Transform velocities including the ω × r term to preserve physical correctness and avoid control errors.

Scenario card
Ball on rotating platform: v_body = 0, v_world = ω × r. Observers disagree on motion if the rotation term is ignored.
