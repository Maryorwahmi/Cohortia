Quick map
Key equations: p(t), v = dp/dt, a = d2p/dt2; rotation update dR/dt = R * Omega; ODE form dx/dt = f(x,u,t); numerical methods Euler and RK4; MATLAB ode45 for adaptive integration.

Why start here
Good models let you validate control before hardware tests, find unstable behaviors early, and choose solvers that match system stiffness and accuracy needs.
