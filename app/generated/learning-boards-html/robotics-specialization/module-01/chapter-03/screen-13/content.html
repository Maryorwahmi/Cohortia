Common Mistake

Pitfalls to detect

Learn to spot when standard Gaussian-based fusion is inappropriate and choose mitigations.

Non-Gaussian noise
Outliers and multimodal errors require robust or sampling-based methods rather than a simple Kalman filter.
Ignored covariance
Dropping off-diagonal elements incorrectly assumes independence and may produce overconfident posteriors.
Normalization & linearization
Always normalize posteriors when comparing hypotheses; be cautious when applying linear assumptions to strongly non-linear systems.
Pro-tip

Validate your noise models with collected sensor data before deploying filters in the field.

Example consequence
If a sensor produces sporadic large errors and you treat them as Gaussian, the estimator may place excessive trust in bad readings and steer the robot incorrectly. Detect and handle spikes with gating or switch to robust estimators.
Misconception check: smaller reported variance does not always mean more accurate sensor — it may reflect a mis-specified model.
