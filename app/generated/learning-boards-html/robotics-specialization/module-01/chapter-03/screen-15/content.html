Key Concepts

Random variables
Model sensor outputs probabilistically to quantify uncertainty.
Gaussian distribution
Mean and sigma summarize center and spread; useful for many sensor errors.
Covariance matrix
Encodes variances and covariances; off-diagonals show correlations across variables.
Bayes' theorem
Posterior ∝ Likelihood × Prior. Use it to update beliefs with measurements.
Common pitfalls
Non-Gaussian noise, ignored covariance, and missing normalization can break estimators.
Practical impact
Validate model assumptions, then choose an appropriate estimator for robust state estimation.
Summary takeaway
Probability and Bayesian updates transform noisy sensor readings into principled state estimates. Use Gaussians and covariance where justified, and switch methods when data contradicts assumptions.
