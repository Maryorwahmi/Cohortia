Mean & Covariance
Compactly capture center and multi-dimensional spread; preserve them when transforming estimates.
Bayesian update
Posterior combines prior and likelihood to yield refined beliefs after measurements.
Validate assumptions
Test noise distributions and correlations. If Gaussians fail, use robust or sampling-based methods.
Final reflection
Which sensor in your system is most likely to violate Gaussian assumptions, and how would you detect it? Answering that will guide your estimator design.
