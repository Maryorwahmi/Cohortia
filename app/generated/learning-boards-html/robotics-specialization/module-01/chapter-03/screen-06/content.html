Definition

Multivariate Gaussian & Covariance

For vector quantities, mean is a vector and covariance is a symmetric positive definite matrix that encodes variances and covariances.

Formula
p(x) = (1 / sqrt((2π)^n * det(Σ))) * exp(-0.5 * (x-μ)ᵀ * Σ⁻¹ * (x-μ))
Matrix structure
Σ = [var_x, cov_xy; cov_xy, var_y]. Off-diagonal covariances tell you about correlation between components.
Key Takeaway

Covariance captures both spread and correlation — keep off-diagonal terms when variables are linked.

Concrete example
A 2D mean μ = [10; 5] and covariance Σ = [4, 1; 1, 9] imply variances of 4 and 9 and a positive covariance of 1 linking x and y errors. Generating samples from mvnrnd will produce an ellipse aligned according to the covariance.
% MATLAB snippet to define a 2D Gaussian
mu = [10; 5];
Sigma = [4, 1; 1, 9];
samples = mvnrnd(mu, Sigma, 1000);
scatter(samples(:,1), samples(:,2), '.');
hold on; plot(mu(1), mu(2), 'r+', 'MarkerSize', 10);
Remember: if you drop covariances, you assume independence which may be wrong and will distort fused estimates.
