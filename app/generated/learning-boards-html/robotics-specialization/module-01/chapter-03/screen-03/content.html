Skills & Evidence

Practical checks to verify understanding and readiness to apply these tools to real robots.

Interpretation
Compute confidence intervals
Translate mean and sigma into a numeric interval, and explain what that interval means for a robot's decision-making.
Visualization
Plot covariance ellipses
Generate a scatter or ellipse for a 2D Gaussian sample and explain axis orientation and spread.
Coding
Run a MATLAB fusion script
Show prior, likelihood and posterior plots and fuse a second measurement to observe reduced sigma.
Diagnosis
Identify common mistakes
Explain when Gaussian assumptions break and why keeping covariance matters when transforming estimates.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY: Complete the MATLAB script and answer a short question about why the posterior variance decreases.
