1D Gaussian fusion

Combine a prior and a measurement analytically when both are Gaussians and independent.

Posterior mean
μ_post = (σ_meas^2 * μ_prior + σ_prior^2 * μ_meas) / (σ_prior^2 + σ_meas^2)
Posterior variance
σ_post^2 = (σ_prior^2 * σ_meas^2) / (σ_prior^2 + σ_meas^2)
Key Takeaway

Weight estimates by their confidence: smaller variance means stronger influence on the posterior.

Worked numeric example
Prior: μ=10, σ=3. Measurement: μ=12, σ=2. Compute using the formulas to see the posterior mean shift toward the more precise measurement and variance decrease.
% MATLAB-style computation
mu_prior = 10;
sigma_prior = 3;
mu_meas = 12;
sigma_meas = 2;
sigma_prior2 = sigma_prior^2;
sigma_meas2 = sigma_meas^2;
mu_post = (sigma_meas2 * mu_prior + sigma_prior2 * mu_meas) / (sigma_prior2 + sigma_meas2);
sigma_post2 = (sigma_prior2 * sigma_meas2) / (sigma_prior2 + sigma_meas2);
Misconception check: don't average means without accounting for variance; equal averaging ignores differing confidences.
