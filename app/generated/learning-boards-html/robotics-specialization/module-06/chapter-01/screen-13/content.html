Hands-on Activity
EKF lab: circular trajectory
Run for 100 steps with dt = 0.1 s, v = 1 m/s, omega = 0.2 rad/s. Add Gaussian noise to GPS and odometry as specified.
Steps:
  1. Simulate true states for N steps.
  2. Synthesize noisy GPS and odometry measurements.
  3. Run EKF loop with Jacobians and store estimates.
  4. Plot true path, GPS, and EKF estimate; compute RMSE.
Evaluation & diagnostics
Compare RMSE of raw GPS and EKF estimate to quantify improvement. Plot innovation residuals and the trace of P over time to monitor health of the estimator.
Expected result
EKF should smooth noisy GPS and compensate for odometry drift, yielding smaller RMSE than GPS alone in typical conditions.
Check
If EKF shows higher RMSE than GPS, inspect Q/R, check Jacobian computations, and verify measurement alignment with predicted z_pred.
