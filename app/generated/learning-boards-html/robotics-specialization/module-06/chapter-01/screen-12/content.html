Comparison
EKF vs Alternatives
EKF
Linearize with Jacobians; efficient but may fail for strong nonlinearities or multimodal posteriors.
UKF (mentioned)
Better captures nonlinear transforms of Gaussians via deterministic sigma points; more accurate for some problems.
Particle filters (mentioned)
Represent arbitrary distributions with samples; robust to multimodality but computationally heavier.
Practical advice
Start with an EKF for many robotics problems, but validate in simulation. If you see large, consistent innovation residuals or covariance that grows unexpectedly, test an alternative filter in simulation before hardware experiments.
Warning

Do not assume EKF fixes bad models or incorrect noise specifications. It amplifies modeling errors if linearization is applied blindly.
