Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Learning Objectives
Derivation checkpoint
  • Show algebra that leads to x̂_k|k-1 and P_k|k-1.
  • Derive K_k and the form of P_k|k.
  • Explain assumptions: linearity and Gaussian noise.
MATLAB & simulation
  • Run 1D tracker, plot true path, measurements, and estimates.
  • Compute RMSE and compare sensors vs filter.
  • Test sensitivity by changing Q and R.
EKF checks
  • Compute Jacobians F and H at the current estimate.
  • Check that linearization is valid for your operating range.
  • Compare EKF estimate to GPS measurements and true path.
Diagnostic tips
  • If filter diverges, check model, Jacobians, and R/Q scaling.
  • Ensure covariance P remains positive definite.
