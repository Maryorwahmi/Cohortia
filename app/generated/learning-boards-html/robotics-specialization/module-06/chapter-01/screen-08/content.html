Kalman gain: Trust slider
K adjusts how strongly measurements move the predicted state.
When R is large
Measurements are noisy; K is small; the filter favors prediction.
When P is large
Prediction uncertainty is high; K is larger; the filter trusts measurements more.
Concrete check: double R and observe K shrink. Doubling Q tends to increase P and therefore K. Use these experiments to validate whether your filter reacts as expected.
Diagnostic cards
Example
GPS σ = 0.5 m (small R) plus uncertain odometry leads to a high K that quickly corrects position errors.
Misconception
K is not manually tuned; it is a function of P and R. If you want different behavior, adjust Q and R, not K directly.
