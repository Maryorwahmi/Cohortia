Occupancy grid map
Grid of per-cell probabilities used for planning and mapping from range sensors.
Log-odds updates
Convert probabilities to log-odds to add evidence linearly and avoid numerical underflow.
SLAM & loop closure
Simultaneously estimate poses and map; loop closures add constraints that correct drift via graph optimization.
