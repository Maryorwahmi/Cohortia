Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Mapping vs SLAM
Mapping (known poses)
Direct projection
With accurate poses, sensor readings are transformed into world coordinates and directly used to update the occupancy grid. There's no need to infer pose from the same sensors used for mapping.
Example: a robot with motion capture or a precise GPS can focus only on mapping, producing consistent maps quickly.
SLAM (unknown poses)
Coupled estimation
Pose uncertainty and map uncertainty are entangled. Errors in pose estimates distort the map, and bad maps can mislead pose estimation. Solving SLAM requires techniques that jointly reason about both.
Example: odometry drift over a long loop will warp a map unless loop closure and global optimization correct the trajectory.
Contrast
Online vs Full SLAM
Online SLAM estimates the current pose for navigation; full SLAM optimizes the whole trajectory offline for better global consistency.
Key Takeaway
Why SLAM is harder
Because pose and map must be estimated together, small errors amplify without global constraints like loop closure.
