Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Log-Odds Representation
Why use it?

Additive updates and numerical stability

Log-odds turn multiplication of small probabilities into addition of evidence terms. This prevents underflow and makes incremental fusion straightforward.

Update rule
l_t = l_{t-1} + inverse_sensor_model - l_0. With l_0 = 0 for a 0.5 prior, you simply add sensor evidence to the existing log-odds.
Concrete example
Repeated hits
If each occupied observation contributes +0.4 in log-odds, three consistent hits raise l by +1.2, and converting back to probability yields a high occupancy belief. This additive behavior is simple to reason about.
Key Takeaway

Use log-odds for safe, additive fusion of sensor evidence and to avoid numerical underflow from repeated multiplications.
