Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Pitfalls & Practices
Coordinate transforms
Verify world-to-grid indexing and rounding rules. A one-cell offset looks like consistent map shift.
Free vs occupied updates
Apply free evidence along rays as well as occupied evidence at endpoints to build accurate free-space representation.
Loop-closure verification
Use descriptor matches plus geometric checks to avoid false loop constraints that can distort the map.
Practical rule
Start with moderate log-odds increments (for example -0.1 free, +0.4 occupied), validate in simulation, and adjust for sensor noise to balance convergence and robustness.
