Module 7 › Chapter 6.3 › Degeneracy & Impoverishment
Definitions
Degeneracy: most weights near zero. Impoverishment: loss of diversity after resampling duplicates high-weight particles.
Measure
Effective particles N_eff = 1 / sum(w_i^2). Trigger resampling when N_eff < threshold.
Key Takeaway

Monitor effective sample size and balance resampling frequency with diversity-preserving steps to avoid long-term failure.

Concrete scenario
If one particle holds most probability, resampling will duplicate it. If the robot then moves unexpectedly, the filter may lose the true pose because there are too few distinct hypotheses.
Check
Compute N_eff over time and plot it. Resample only when the curve dips below your chosen threshold to avoid unnecessary impoverishment.
