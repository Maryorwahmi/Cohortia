Module 7 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
Particle filter
Non-parametric sample-based belief representation that supports multimodal distributions.
Predict → Update → Resample
The core MCL cycle: motion update, sensor likelihood weighting, and resampling to focus particles.
Degeneracy & recovery
Monitor effective sample size, use adaptive resampling, regularization, and random injection to preserve diversity and enable recovery.
Summary takeaway
Use particle filters when you need to represent multiple hypotheses or non-Gaussian noise. Balance particle count and resampling strategy to meet accuracy and compute constraints.
