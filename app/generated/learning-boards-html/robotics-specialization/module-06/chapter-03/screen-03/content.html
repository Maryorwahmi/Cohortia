How to verify each objective
Monte Carlo intuition
Mnemonic: landscape and pebbles. Visual test: particle density should match high-likelihood regions after update.
Implementation checklist
Motion update, sensor likelihood via ray-cast, normalization and weight storage, systematic resample routine passing unit tests.
Resampling policy
Compute effective particle number and resample only when it drops below threshold. Check diversity after resampling.
Comparison rule-of-thumb
Kalman for single Gaussian, particle for global or non-Gaussian. Evaluate compute budget versus robustness need.
