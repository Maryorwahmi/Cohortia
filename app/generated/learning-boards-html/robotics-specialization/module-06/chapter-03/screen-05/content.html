Module 7 › Chapter 6.3 › Particles as Hypotheses
What each particle holds
  • Pose: x, y, theta
  • Weight: likelihood given sensor measurements
  • Optional: history or importance sampling metadata
Concrete example
Initialize 1000 particles uniformly across free space for global localization. After a few steps, high-weight particles cluster near the true pose.
Key Takeaway

Particles give a flexible, sample-based view of belief. Use visualization of weight to understand which hypotheses matter.

Visual debugging
Plot particles as blue dots. Encode weight as size or opacity. Plot true pose as red and estimated mean as green. Watch cluster formation over time to validate behavior.
Misconception
Treating particles as equal votes ignores the crucial role of their weights. Resampling depends on normalized weights, not counts alone.
