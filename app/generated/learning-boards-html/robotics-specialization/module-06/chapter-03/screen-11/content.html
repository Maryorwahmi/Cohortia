Module 7 › Chapter 6.3 › Adaptive Resampling
Effective particles
Compute N_eff = 1 / sum(w_i^2) after normalizing weights. Use this scalar to assess weight concentration.
Policy
If N_eff < threshold (e.g., N/2), perform resampling; otherwise skip to save computation and preserve diversity.
Key Takeaway

Adaptive resampling reduces unnecessary resampling and helps keep particle diversity when the weight distribution is broad.

Practical adjustments
  • Add epsilon when normalizing to prevent division by zero.
  • Optionally inject a small fraction of random particles on resample to aid recovery.
  • Tune threshold empirically for your sensor and motion noise characteristics.
