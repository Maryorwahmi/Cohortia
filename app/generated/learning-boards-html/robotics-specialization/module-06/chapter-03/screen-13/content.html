Module 7 › Chapter 6.3 › Regularization & Injection
Regularization
After resampling, add small Gaussian noise to particle poses to avoid identical duplicates and maintain local exploration.
Random injection
Insert a small percentage of particles sampled uniformly across free space at resample time to allow recovery from large errors.
Key Takeaway

Use both small jitter and occasional random injection to preserve the filter’s ability to explore and recover.

Practical example
In a 1000-particle filter, injecting 10–50 random particles (1–5%) at resample time helps recover from kidnapping without disrupting steady-state performance.
Misconception
Random injection is not a full reset. Treat it as an exploratory safeguard and tune the rate conservatively.
