Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › 3D Newton–Euler dynamics
Translational dynamics
m · p̈ = R · [0; 0; u] − [0; 0; m·g]. Rotation matrix R maps body thrust into the world.
Rotational dynamics
I · ω̇ = τ − ω × (I · ω). The cross term is gyroscopic torque from angular momentum coupling.
When to include gyroscopic term
Include it for agile, fast rotations where rotor spin and body rotation interact; neglect it for gentle hover to simplify the model.
Practical checks
  • Verify R is constructed using the same Euler convention as transforms you use elsewhere.
  • Confirm units for I, τ, and ω are consistent (N·m, rad/s, kg·m²).
  • Test simple motions: pure thrust, pure torque, and combined inputs to validate coupling.
Key Takeaway

Newton–Euler captures full coupling; rotation matrices move forces between frames and gyroscopic terms capture cross-coupling from spinning rotors.
