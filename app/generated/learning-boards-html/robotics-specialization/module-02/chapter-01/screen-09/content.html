Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Euler angles vs quaternions
Comparison table
Euler angles
Intuitive, three parameters; suffer from singularities (gimbal lock) at certain attitudes.
Quaternions
Four parameters, no singularity, smooth interpolation; must normalize to avoid drift.
When to use which
Euler for derivations and small angles; quaternions for full 3D flight and production simulators.
Concrete example
If pitch approaches 90 degrees, Euler-based control can behave erratically because roll and yaw become indistinguishable. With quaternions you can represent that same orientation without losing a degree of freedom.
Misconception
Quaternions are not human-readable angles; they require conversion to visualize, but they give numerical robustness for controllers and simulators.
Key Takeaway

Use Euler angles for intuition; use quaternions for reliability when rotations approach singular configurations.
