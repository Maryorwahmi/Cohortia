Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Motor inputs → forces
Linear mapping of inputs
Define motor thrusts u1..u4. Compute collective and differential commands for thrust and torques.
Equations (concept)
Total thrust u = u1 + u2 + u3 + u4. Roll torque τx ∝ (left side thrusts) − (right side thrusts). Pitch torque τy ∝ (front) − (back). Yaw τz arises from rotor reaction signs.
Concrete example
If u1=1.0, u2=1.0, u3=1.0, u4=1.0 then u=4.0 and no torques. If u1 and u4 are reduced by 0.1 and u2 and u3 increased by 0.1, u stays 4.0 but a roll torque appears.
Misconception
Adjusting a single rotor changes thrust and torques simultaneously. To create pure torque you must offset thrust changes among rotors so the total stays constant.
Mixer visualization
Collective: u = Σ ui
Roll: difference left vs right
Pitch: difference front vs back
Yaw: rotor spin imbalance
Key Takeaway

Motor mixing turns desired thrust and torques into four motor commands; understanding the mix is essential for control and for preserving altitude while commanding rotation.
