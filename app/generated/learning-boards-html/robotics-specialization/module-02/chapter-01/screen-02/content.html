01
Model 3D pose with rotation matrices
Write rotation matrices and homogeneous transforms to map body vectors to the world frame. This matters because thrust is produced in the body frame and must be expressed in inertial coordinates to compute acceleration.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Derive 1D, 2D, and 3D equations of motion
From m·z̈ = u − m·g to Newton–Euler, derive translational and rotational equations. You will use these for simulation and controller design.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Relate motor thrusts to torques and motion
Understand how differential rotor speeds create roll, pitch, and yaw torques and how that coupling leads to underactuation and the need to tilt to move horizontally.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Implement a MATLAB dynamics simulator
Write a dynamics function for ode45, simulate hover and trajectories, and analyze how attitude affects translation. This is essential to test controllers safely.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
