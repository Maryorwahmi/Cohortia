Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › 2D simulation lab
Lab instructions
Implement a 2D dynamics function for ode45 and run two experiments: a hover at z=2 m and a horizontal motion to x=5 m while maintaining altitude.
Parameters
m = 0.5 kg, Iy = 0.01 kg·m², g = 9.81 m/s². Simulate for 10 seconds and plot x vs z.
Assessment question
Why is the quadrotor underactuated? Use your simulation results to explain how tilt generates horizontal acceleration and why direct control of x is impossible with only rotor thrusts.
Common pitfalls
  • Forgetting to include gravity in z̈.
  • Using inconsistent Euler order for rotation matrix construction.
  • Attempting to set ẍ directly instead of commanding attitude.
Key Takeaway

The lab turns theoretical equations into measurable trajectories and clarifies underactuation through practical controller design and plots.
