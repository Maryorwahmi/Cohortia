Skills & verification steps
  • Model check: rotate a body thrust into the world frame and confirm p̈ when u = m·g equals zero.
  • Dynamics check: derive m·z̈ = u − m·g and simulate step responses to thrust commands.
  • Torque decomposition: write roll, pitch, yaw torques as differences of rotor thrusts and include rotor direction signs for yaw.
Common pitfalls
Treat Euler angles as intuitive but limited. Test quaternion conversions for large rotations. Verify the inertia matrix orientation and include gyroscopic effects only when needed for agile maneuvers.
