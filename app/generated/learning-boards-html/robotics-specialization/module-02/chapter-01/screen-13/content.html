Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Underactuation
Four motors, six degrees of freedom; tilt is required for horizontal motion.
1D & 2D dynamics
m·z̈ = u − m·g; in 2D, ẍ = (u/m)·sin(θ), z̈ = (u/m)·cos(θ) − g.
3D Newton–Euler
m·p̈ = R·[0;0;u] − [0;0;m·g]; I·ω̇ = τ − ω×(I·ω).
Takeaway
These concepts connect to controller design and simulation: understand mappings, choose rotation representations carefully, and validate models with simple experiments before advanced maneuvers.
