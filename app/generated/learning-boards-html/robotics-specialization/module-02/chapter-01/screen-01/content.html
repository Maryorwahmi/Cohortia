Quadrotor Kinematics and Dynamics
Model pose, derive motion equations, and simulate flight dynamics using MATLAB.

Orientation and position combine into a 12-variable state: position, velocity, Euler angles, and angular rates. Four rotor thrusts give a total thrust and three torques, creating coupling between translation and rotation.

Four rotors → thrust & torques

Quick equation
m · p̈ = R · [0; 0; u] − [0; 0; m·g]
Rotation matrix R maps body thrust into inertial acceleration. This single line captures the translation coupling to attitude.
