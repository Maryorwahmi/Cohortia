Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Minimum-snap Trajectories
Concept
Minimum-snap minimizes the integrated fourth derivative of position, producing trajectories with smooth acceleration and reduced high-frequency actuation.
When to use
Aggressive waypoint transitions or when actuator stress from abrupt commands must be minimized.
Trade-offs
  • Improves higher-derivative smoothness and actuator behavior.
  • Requires solving an optimization problem and careful constraint setup.
  • Needs accurate modeling to realize benefits on hardware.
Key Takeaway

Use minimum-snap when smoothness of higher derivatives matters for actuator limits; prefer cubic polynomials for simpler, lower-complexity tasks.
