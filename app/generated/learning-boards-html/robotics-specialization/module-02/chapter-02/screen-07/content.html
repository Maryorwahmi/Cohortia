MATLAB pattern

function tau = attitude_controller(angles, angles_dot, angles_des, Kp, Kd)
    e = angles_des - angles;
    tau = Kp .* e - Kd .* angles_dot;
end
This uses element-wise operations to compute torques for roll, pitch and yaw simultaneously.
Concrete scenario
Suppose angles_des = [0;0;0] and measured roll = 5 degrees with roll rate nonzero. The error produces a restorative torque on roll, and derivative action opposes the roll rate. Repeat similar logic for pitch and yaw.
Misconception
Treating axes as fully decoupled ignores aerodynamic and inertial coupling. Verify independence in simulation before assuming no cross-coupling on hardware.
Key Takeaway

Vectorized PD controllers simplify implementation and provide separate tuning knobs per axis, but validate coupling effects for your vehicle.
