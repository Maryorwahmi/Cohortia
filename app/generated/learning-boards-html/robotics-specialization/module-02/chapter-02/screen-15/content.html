Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › What to Remember
Error-first design
Make the control loop reduce the error robustly given real sensors and actuators.
PID balance
Kp for speed, Kd for damping, Ki for steady-state; add anti-windup when necessary.
Smooth trajectories
Use cubic or minimum-snap polynomials so acceleration and motor commands are feasible.
Final takeaway

Test controllers in simulation with realistic disturbances and saturation, then iterate gains and trajectory constraints until behavior is dependable and safe for flight.
