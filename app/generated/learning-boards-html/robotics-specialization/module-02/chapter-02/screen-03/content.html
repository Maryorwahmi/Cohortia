Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Learning Objectives — Practical Outcomes
Simulation Goals
Run ode45 simulations to compare altitude responses under specified gain sets and visualize overshoot, settling, and steady-state error.
Example task
Simulate z(t) for Kp=1,Kd=0; Kp=5,Kd=2; Kp=10,Kd=5; Kp=20,Kd=8. Plot them together and discuss dynamics.
Controller functions
Implement altitude_controller and attitude_controller as small MATLAB functions with feedforward and vectorized PD behavior.
Design & Safety
Practice anti-windup by clamping the integral when actuators saturate. Confirm controllers are robust to a small constant disturbance before hardware trials.
Verification
  • Plot z(t) and compare settling times and overshoot.
  • Introduce a constant downward disturbance and compare PD vs PID.
  • Check acceleration continuity for trajectories.
