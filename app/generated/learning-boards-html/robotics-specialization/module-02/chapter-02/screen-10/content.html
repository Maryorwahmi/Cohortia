Comparison of methods
Ziegler–Nichols
Experimental method to find initial gains using the critical oscillation point. Good for starting values but can be aggressive.
Manual tuning
Iterative, intuitive approach: increase Kp, add Kd for damping, then Ki for steady-state. Requires patience and validation.
Optimization
Use simulation and an objective like minimum settling time or minimal overshoot to find gains, assuming an accurate model.
Practical guidance
Start conservative, validate under disturbances and actuator limits, and prefer gradual refinement. Use simulation cases like the four altitude gain sets from the chapter to compare behavior.
Misconception
A single tuning method is sufficient for all scenarios. In practice combine methods and validate for realistic disturbances and limits.
