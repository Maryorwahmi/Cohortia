Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Integrator Windup
What happens
When actuator limits are reached, the integral keeps accumulating error and leads to large stored corrections that cause overshoot when the actuator recovers.
Clamping
Limit the integral term to specified min and max values so it cannot grow unbounded during saturation.
Conditional integration
Pause integrating while the actuator is saturated and the integral would increase the saturation further.
Concrete validation
In simulation, saturate thrust at a realistic motor maximum and compare responses with no anti-windup, clamped integral, and conditional integration. Measure overshoot and recovery time to select the best approach for your quadrotor.
Misconception
Anti-windup is not only for extreme events; even moderate saturation during maneuvers can accumulate integral and produce poor behavior.
