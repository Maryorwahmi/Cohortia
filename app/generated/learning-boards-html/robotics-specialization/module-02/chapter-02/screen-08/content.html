Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › PID Controller
Definition
Proportional, integral and derivative terms combined
Formula
u(t) = Kp*e(t) + Ki*∫e(t)dt + Kd*de(t)/dt
Role of integral
Accumulating error until steady offsets are corrected; essential under constant disturbances.
Integrator Windup
When actuators saturate, continued accumulation causes large stored integral and later overshoot. Use clamping or conditional integration to prevent it.
Concrete example
Introduce a downward disturbance of 0.5 N while hovering. A PD controller will leave a steady offset. Adding Ki gradually increases thrust until the offset disappears. Watch how Ki must be moderate to avoid slow oscillatory behavior.
Key Takeaway

Use integral action to remove steady-state error, but design anti-windup and retune PD gains after adding Ki.
