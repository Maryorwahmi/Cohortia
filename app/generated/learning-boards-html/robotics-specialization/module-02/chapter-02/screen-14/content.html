Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Control loop
Reference, error, controller, plant, sensor — the error drives corrective action.
PD & PID
Kp for response, Kd for damping, Ki for steady-state correction; tune cautiously and validate.
Feedforward
Include physics terms such as m*g for hover so feedback focuses on deviations.
Anti-windup
Clamp or pause integral accumulation during actuator saturation to avoid post-saturation overshoot.
Tuning
Use heuristics, manual refinement, or optimization and always validate with realistic disturbances.
Trajectories
Cubic polynomials for simple transitions; minimum-snap for higher-derivative smoothness when needed.
Takeaway

Design controllers to reduce error under realistic constraints, include feedforward where appropriate, prevent integrator windup, tune methodically, and generate smooth trajectories that motors can follow safely.
