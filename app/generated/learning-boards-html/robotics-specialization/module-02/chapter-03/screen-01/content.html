Motion Planning & Waypoints
Why smooth timing and derivatives matter for quadrotor flight

This chapter develops a pipeline: select waypoints for a mission, convert them into a time-parameterized trajectory that is continuous up to the derivatives required by the vehicle, and use feedback control to track it. Practical choices — cubic versus quintic polynomials or minimum-snap optimization — change how easy the controller’s job will be, how aggressive the flight looks, and how safe your system is in the real world.

In this lesson
  • Define motion planning, trajectory generation, and tracking with examples.
  • Compare cubic, quintic, and minimum-snap approaches and when to use each.
  • Implement and reason about a cascade controller and safety features.
