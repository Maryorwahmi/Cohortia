Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Trajectory Comparison
Cubic
Pros: simple, low compute. Cons: acceleration discontinuities at joins.
Quintic
Pros: continuous acceleration, smoother thrust. Cons: higher algebraic complexity.
Minimum-Snap
Pros: minimizes high-order derivatives and control effort. Cons: requires solving a QP, more compute.
Exercise
Generate the same waypoint mission with all three methods, plot velocity and acceleration, and note peak values and continuity. Decide which method best balances safety and speed for your mission.
