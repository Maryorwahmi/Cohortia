Module 6 › Chapter 5.3 › Bundle Adjustment
Objective
minimize Σ ||p_ij − π(K, R_i, t_i, P_j)||^2
Here p_ij is an observed image point, π denotes projection with intrinsics K and pose (R_i, t_i), and P_j is the 3D point. Levenberg‑Marquardt iteratively updates parameters to reduce this sum. It uses a damping parameter to interpolate between Gauss‑Newton and gradient descent steps for stability.
Concrete check
Compare total reprojection error before and after optimization to quantify improvement.
Caution
Bundle adjustment is sensitive to initialization and can converge to local minima if starting poses are poor.
Practical note
Implement a simple LM loop that perturbs poses and points, computes Jacobians of projection, and updates parameters until reprojection sum no longer decreases noticeably.
Key Takeaway

Bundle adjustment is the gold standard for finalizing SfM results but depends on a reasonable initial reconstruction.
