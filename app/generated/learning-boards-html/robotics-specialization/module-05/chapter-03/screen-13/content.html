Module 6 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Epipolar geometry
Correspondences lie on epipolar lines; reduces matching search and enables E/F estimation.
Essential vs Fundamental
E for calibrated cameras (E=[t]×R); F for uncalibrated pixel coordinates, F relates to E through intrinsics.
Bundle Adjustment
Joint nonlinear refinement of camera poses and 3D points to minimize reprojection error.
Final takeaway

These building blocks let you move from matched images to a consistent geometric model; remember to validate with reprojection error and to handle scale when metric output is required.
