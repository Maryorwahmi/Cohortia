E & Decompose
Triangulate
Form 3D points from matched rays.
Incremental
Add images and points sequentially using prior poses.
Bundle Adjustment
Global refinement of all poses and points to minimize reprojection error.
Note that early mistakes amplify later. Robust feature matching and careful outlier rejection are essential before triangulation and bundle adjustment. Visualization of intermediate clouds and reprojection error plots helps diagnose failures.
Concrete example
Run ORB detection on two images, match descriptors, estimate E with RANSAC, decompose and triangulate points, then run a simple bundle adjustment to reduce reprojection residuals.
Why it matters

SfM produces both a sparse geometric model and the camera trajectory — the basis for mapping and navigation tasks.
