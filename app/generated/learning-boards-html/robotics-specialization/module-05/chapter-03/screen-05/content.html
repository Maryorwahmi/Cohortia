Module 6 › Chapter 5.3 › E vs F
Essential matrix

E = [t]× R encodes rotation and translation direction for calibrated cameras. It satisfies p2^T E p1 = 0 for normalized coordinates.

Fundamental matrix

F relates pixel coordinates directly and is used when camera intrinsics are unknown. F = K'^{-T} E K^{-1} shows the link to E.

Common pitfall

Failing to enforce the rank‑2 constraint creates degenerate matrices; always correct the estimate with SVD to ensure valid epipolar geometry.

Relationship
F = K'^{-T} · E · K^{-1}
Example: if you have intrinsics K and K', you can transform an estimated F into an E for decomposition by multiplying with the appropriate inverses.
