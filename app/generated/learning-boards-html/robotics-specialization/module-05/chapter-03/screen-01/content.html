Module 6 › Chapter 5.3 › Overview
Pose Estimation and Structure from Motion
How two images determine rotation, translation, and a 3D point cloud that we then refine.
In this lesson

You will study epipolar geometry, compute the essential and fundamental matrices, decompose E to recover rotation and translation up to scale, triangulate 3D points, and refine with bundle adjustment.

Why it matters

These methods let a robot infer motion and build a map from camera images alone, enabling navigation, mapping, and model capture without depth sensors.

Quick Roadmap

Match features → estimate E or F → decompose to R,t → triangulate points → bundle adjust to minimize reprojection error.
