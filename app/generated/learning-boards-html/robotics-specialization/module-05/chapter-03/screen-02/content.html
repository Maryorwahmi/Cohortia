Module 6 › Chapter 5.3 › Learning Objectives
01
Estimate relative pose
Compute E and extract R and t to recover camera motion up to scale.
You will set up the epipolar constraint, solve a linear system for E, and check which decomposition yields positive depth. Verified competency milestone: estimate relative pose on a pair of images.
02
Triangulate 3D points
Recover 3D positions from matched image coordinates and camera poses.
You will compute the least squares intersection of two rays for many matches and visualize the point cloud. Verified competency milestone: produce a stable 3D scatter of matched features.
03
Bundle adjustment
Formulate and minimize reprojection error over cameras and points.
You will set up the objective Σ||observed − projected||² and run a Levenberg‑Marquardt routine to reduce it. Verified competency milestone: show reprojection error before and after refinement.
04
MATLAB SfM pipeline
Implement detection, matching, E estimation, triangulation, and simple bundle adjustment.
You will run ORB or built‑in feature detectors, RANSAC for robust 8‑point estimation, and plot the reconstructed cloud. Verified competency milestone: deliver a two‑view reconstruction script with before/after error plots.
Bloom's progression
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Each objective is paired with a small practical milestone so you can verify competency and see the immediate effect of applying these methods to real images.
