Objective checks
  • Pose estimation: show that R and t yield epipolar consistency and that one decomposition produces positive depth for triangulated points.
  • Triangulation: plot the 3D cloud and camera centers; check that points lie in front of both cameras.
  • Bundle adjustment: graph reprojection error before and after optimization; expect a decreased sum of squared errors.
  • MATLAB pipeline: provide script output: matched keypoints, estimated E, triangulated cloud, and reprojection error plots.
Common checkpoints
Use RANSAC to filter outliers, enforce rank constraints on F/E, and test decompositions by checking whether reconstructed 3D points have positive depth relative to both camera poses.
Mnemonic
Remember: Match → Estimate → Triangulate → Refine. The refinement closes the loop by minimizing reprojection mismatch across all views.
