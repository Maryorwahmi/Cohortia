Feature Extraction and Matching
Why local keypoints and descriptors matter in robotics
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson
Detect keypoints, compute descriptors, perform matching, and apply RANSAC to keep only consistent matches for geometric estimation.
Why it matters
Robust features and matching underpin visual odometry and SLAM. Without them, pose estimates are noisy or catastrophically wrong.
Concrete example
Match ORB features between two images of the same room, use the ratio test, then run RANSAC to estimate an affine transform and display inliers.
