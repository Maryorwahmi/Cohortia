Scale-space steps
1. Build Gaussian pyramid
Convolve image with Gaussians of increasing sigma and downsample to create levels.
2. Compute Difference of Gaussians
Subtract adjacent blurred images to emphasize structures at particular scales.
3. Find extrema in space and scale
Local maxima or minima across the DoG volume are candidate keypoints with scale estimates.
Concrete example
A round knob at medium distance produces a DoG peak at a sigma matching its radius. If distance changes, the same structure peaks at a different scale, but scale-space detection preserves the correspondence by recording the sigma.
Common misconception
Detecting features at a single scale is sufficient if a camera stays at fixed distance. In real robotics, distance and zoom change often, so multi-scale detection is necessary.
Key Takeaway

DoG across a Gaussian pyramid produces scale-labeled keypoints, enabling reliable matching across image size changes.
