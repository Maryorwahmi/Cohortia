Workflow
  1. Match descriptors and filter with ratio test.
  2. Run RANSAC to keep inliers for a chosen model.
  3. Estimate essential or fundamental matrix for general motion.
  4. Decompose to obtain rotation and translation up to scale; refine with bundle adjustment.
Practical caution
A high inlier count concentrated in one region yields poor geometric constraints. Aim for spatially distributed inliers for stable pose estimates.
Key Takeaway

Reliable pose estimation depends on robust matching plus geometric verification and refinement to account for noise and limited overlap.
