Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › SURF vs ORB
SURF
Uses integral images and Haar wavelets for fast filtering. Produces a 64-D descriptor with good robustness and lower cost than SIFT.
ORB
Combines FAST corners with BRIEF binary descriptors. Orientation and learning-based tweaks make it rotation-invariant and extremely fast using Hamming distance for matching.
Use-case guide
  • High-speed, onboard tracking: ORB for framerate and low CPU usage.
  • Balanced accuracy and speed: SURF where SIFT is too slow.
  • Maximum matching quality: SIFT when compute resources allow.
Key Takeaway

Match your detector to hardware and application needs: ORB for real-time, SURF for speed with quality, SIFT for maximum robustness.
