Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › SIFT Pipeline
Four steps
1. Scale-space extrema
DoG detects candidate blobs and corners across scales.
2. Keypoint localization
Fit a local quadratic, remove low contrast and edge-like points.
3. Orientation assignment
Compute dominant gradient orientation for rotation invariance.
4. Descriptor computation
Sample gradients in a 16×16 patch and build a 128-dimensional vector.
Practical notes
  • SIFT descriptors are robust to illumination because they use gradient directions.
  • Localization reduces unstable detections that would cause bad matches.
  • Computational cost is higher than binary descriptors but offers strong matching quality.
Key Takeaway

SIFT's sequence of detection, refinement, orientation, and descriptor yields reliable, scale- and rotation-invariant matches at moderate computational cost.
