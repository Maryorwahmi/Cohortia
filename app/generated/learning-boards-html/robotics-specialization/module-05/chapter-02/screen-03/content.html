Practical tasks
  1. Detect SIFT, SURF, ORB on the same pair of images and compare keypoint counts.
  2. Extract descriptors and match with nearest neighbor; implement the ratio test.
  3. Use RANSAC to estimate affine or homography and compute the inlier ratio.
  4. Repeat with three viewpoint differences and plot inlier ratio vs rotation magnitude.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: produce a plot and short interpretation of results.
Common evaluation checks
- Confirm that ORB runs substantially faster than SIFT on the same images.
- Verify that ratio thresholds between 0.7 and 0.8 reduce ambiguous matches.
- Observe how RANSAC inlier ratios fall as viewpoint difference increases.
