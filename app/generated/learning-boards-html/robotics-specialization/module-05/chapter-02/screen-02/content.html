Use descriptor distances and the ratio test to reject ambiguous matches and reduce false correspondences.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply RANSAC
Estimate transforms robustly by iteratively sampling matches and keeping the model with most inliers.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Understand Scale-space
Explain Gaussian pyramids and the Difference of Gaussians as a mechanism to detect features across scales.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Why these skills matter
  • Reliable features let robots track motion even with lighting or viewpoint changes.
  • Good descriptors reduce false matches and speed up search in descriptor space.
  • RANSAC shields geometric estimation from the inevitable outliers in real scenes.
Complete the milestones by running small tests on pairs of images and measuring inlier ratios.
