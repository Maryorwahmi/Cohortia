Repeatability

Descriptor tradeoffs

Verify matches
Apply the ratio test and RANSAC before using matches for geometry or motion estimation.
Final takeaway
Feature extraction and matching are a pipeline. Tuning each stage and verifying geometrically are essential to reliable robot perception.
