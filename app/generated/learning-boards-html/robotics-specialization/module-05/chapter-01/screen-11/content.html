Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Radial Distortion
Model
x_distorted = x * (1 + k1*r^2 + k2*r^4 + k3*r^6)
where r = sqrt(x^2 + y^2)
This multiplicative factor increases with radius and bends straight lines away from the center for barrel distortion or inward for pincushion distortion depending on sign.
Example and check
Photograph a printed grid. If grid lines curve near the edge, radial distortion is present. After estimating k1..k3, undistort the image and verify lines are straight.
Common pitfall
Using too few radial terms can leave residual curvature; use as many terms as needed to fit the observed bending during calibration.
