Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Full Projection
Pipeline (algebra)
p_homogeneous = K * [R | t] * P_homogeneous
Then u = p_homogeneous(1) / p_homogeneous(3)
     v = p_homogeneous(2) / p_homogeneous(3)
This sequence—transform then project then normalize—is the canonical flow used in vision algorithms for reprojection and error computation.
Misstep to avoid
Omitting the normalization step leaves coordinates scaled by depth and will produce incorrect image locations for comparisons.
Concrete scenario
Use this pipeline to reproject known world landmarks and compute reprojection error during calibration. Small reprojection error means your estimated K, R, and t are consistent with observed corners.
Key Takeaway
The chained transform is the single mathematical statement that all camera geometry relies upon; keep the order correct and always normalize.
