Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Tangential Distortion
Model
x_distorted = x + [2*p1*x*y + p2*(r^2 + 2*x^2)]
y_distorted = y + [p1*(r^2 + 2*y^2) + 2*p2*x*y]
p1 and p2 capture asymmetric shifts due to lens decentering or tilt relative to the image plane.
Practical check
Estimate p1 and p2 during calibration and inspect whether undistorted images remove asymmetric shifts in corner positions.
Key Takeaway
Include tangential terms when mechanical alignment is imperfect; undistortion uses all estimated coefficients to restore geometric fidelity.
