Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Extrinsic Parameters
Transform
World to camera: P_cam = R * P_world + t. R rotates, t translates. Together as [R | t] they convert world homogeneous coordinates to camera homogeneous coordinates.
Example
A camera 1m forward and pitched down changes the Z coordinate of points relative to the camera, altering their projected u and v values.
Common pitfall
Mixing camera and world frames without applying R and t leads to incorrect projections. Always transform world points into camera coordinates first.
Key Takeaway
Extrinsics are the moving part of the projection pipeline. They must be estimated or known to place image measurements into the world.
