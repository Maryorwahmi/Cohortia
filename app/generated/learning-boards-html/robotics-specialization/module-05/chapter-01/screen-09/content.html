Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Calibration
Step-by-step
1
Capture 10–15 images of a checkerboard at varied angles and distances to cover the image plane.
2
Detect corner points in each image to get imagePoints and boardSize.
3
Generate worldPoints from boardSize and squareSize to create correspondences.
4
Run an optimization to estimate K and distortion coefficients and inspect reprojection error.
Validation
Look at mean reprojection error and visually compare original vs undistorted images. Straightened grid lines indicate successful undistortion.
Practical tip
Avoid shots where checkerboard fills only a corner; ensure the pattern appears at multiple scales and rotations.
Common mistake
Using near-identical, small viewpoint changes yields unstable parameters even with many images because the data lacks informative variation.
