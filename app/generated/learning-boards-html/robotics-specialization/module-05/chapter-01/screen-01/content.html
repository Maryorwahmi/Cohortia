Camera Models and Projective Geometry
Understand how 3D scenes become 2D images and why calibration is essential for precise measurement.
In this lesson
Pinhole model, projection equation, K matrix, extrinsics, calibration, and distortion.
Why it matters
Robots use calibrated cameras to measure distances, estimate pose, and build accurate 3D maps.
Practical check
Try projecting a known 3D point with given f and cx, cy to verify your calibration numerically.
Common pitfall
Using raw images without undistortion leads to curved lines and biased feature locations in geometry estimates.
