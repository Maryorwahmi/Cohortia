Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Undistortion
Visual comparison
Place the original and undistorted images side-by-side. Look for straightening of grids and alignment of edges. Small reprojection error plus clean visuals means parameters are reliable.
Checklist
  • Do straight lines appear straight after undistortion?
  • Is mean reprojection error within acceptable range for your task?
  • Were enough diverse views used during calibration?
Concrete example
Undistort a chessboard image and draw straight lines. If they line up with grid corners and edges, undistortion succeeded where radial bending was visible before.
Key Takeaway
Combine numerical reprojection metrics with visual checks to decide if a calibration is ready for use in geometric tasks.
