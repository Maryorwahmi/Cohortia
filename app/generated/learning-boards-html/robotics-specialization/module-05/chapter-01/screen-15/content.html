Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Calibration workflow
Detect corners, build correspondences, estimate K and distortion with an optimizer.
Undistortion
Apply estimated coefficients to remap pixels and restore straight geometry.
Validation
Use reprojection error and visual straightening as combined acceptance criteria before using parameters operationally.
Final takeaway

Calibration and undistortion turn images into accurate geometric measurements. Verify both numerically and by eye before trusting the camera for navigation or reconstruction.
