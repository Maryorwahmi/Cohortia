Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Pinhole Model
Equation
The central projection is u = f X / Z + cx, v = f Y / Z + cy. It tells how 3D coordinates map to pixel locations given camera internals.
u = f * X / Z + cx
v = f * Y / Z + cy
Concrete check
If f=500px, cx=320, cy=240 and P=[2,1,5], then u=520, v=340. Use this to validate estimated f and principal point.
Visual intuition
As Z grows, X/Z and Y/Z shrink. That inverse relation is the reason objects appear smaller the farther they are from the camera.
Misconception
Focal length is not always a physical millimeter distance in image processing; it is commonly expressed in pixels after combining with pixel pitch.
Key Takeaway: Use the projection equation to test and validate calibration numerically.
