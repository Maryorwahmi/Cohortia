Comparison › setState() vs provider

setState() (Local)

  • Good for widget-private data like form inputs or simple toggles.
  • Easy to implement, minimal ceremony.
  • Becomes hard to manage when siblings need the same data.
Common pitfall: Prop drilling — passing values and callbacks through many levels.

provider + ChangeNotifier (Global)

  • Provide models high in the tree for shared access.
  • ChangeNotifier plus notifyListeners triggers targeted rebuilds.
  • Consumer and Selector let you limit the rebuild scope for efficiency.
Pro tip: Use Provider.of(listen:false) in event handlers to avoid rebuilding the caller.
Example
Suppose a cart total is needed by a header, product list, and checkout screen. Keeping that total local forces many callbacks; using provider centralizes the logic and exposes add/remove methods across screens.
Check
If you find yourself passing the same prop through three or more widgets, consider elevating to provider.
