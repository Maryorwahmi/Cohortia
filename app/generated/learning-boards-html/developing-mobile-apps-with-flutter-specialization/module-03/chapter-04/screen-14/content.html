Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Separation of concerns
Keep UI and business logic independent to simplify testing and maintenance.
Cubit vs Bloc
Cubit: direct methods and emits. Bloc: add events and map to states for complex flows.
flutter_bloc widgets
BlocProvider, BlocBuilder, BlocListener, BlocConsumer each have a specific responsibility.
Takeaway

Pick Cubit for simple state, Bloc for coordinated events; always emit new instances and keep side effects separated from UI rendering.
