Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › What to Remember
Emit new instances
Avoid mutating lists or maps; emit([...state, item]) for adds.
Pick Cubit or Bloc
Cubit for direct calls, Bloc for event coordination and async flows.
Use the right widget
Provide, build, listen, or combine — each widget has a focused role.
End with: which feature will you refactor to Bloc/Cubit next?
