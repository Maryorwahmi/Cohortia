Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Navigator 1.0 vs Navigator 2.0
Navigator 1.0 (Imperative)
  • Push/pop model. Simple and direct API.
  • Great for most mobile flows and small apps.
  • Easy to reason about in a widget-driven lifecycle.
If you only need straightforward screen transitions and simple back behavior, Navigator 1.0 is usually the best choice.
Navigator 2.0 (Declarative / Router)
  • Describe a list of Page objects and synchronize pages with app state.
  • Strong for deep linking, web URLs, and complex route stacks.
  • Requires RouterDelegate and RouteInformationParser or a helper library.
If your app needs URL synchronization, nested navigation stacks, or programmatic stack reconstruction, Navigator 2.0 or a routing library is worth the investment.
Reflection
Ask yourself: does my app need deep links or programmatic stack control? If not, stay with Navigator 1.0 for simplicity.
