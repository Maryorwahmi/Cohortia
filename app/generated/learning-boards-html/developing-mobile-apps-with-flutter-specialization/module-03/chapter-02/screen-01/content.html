Implementing Complex Navigation Patterns
A practical guide to Navigator, routes, named navigation, and common UI patterns in Flutter.
In this lesson

You will learn push/pop navigation, named routes and onGenerateRoute, BottomNavigationBar and Drawer patterns, and when to consider Navigator 2.0.

Why it matters

Consistent navigation prevents lost state, enables deep links, and keeps user flows predictable across devices and platforms.
