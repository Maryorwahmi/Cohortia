Push & Pop
Push to add a route, pop to remove and optionally return data to the awaiting caller.
Named Routes & onGenerateRoute
Centralize route names and validate arguments in onGenerateRoute for safer navigation.
State & Safety
Use IndexedStack to preserve tab state and check mounted before navigating after async operations.
Final takeaway

Follow these patterns to build navigation that scales from simple mobile flows to apps that may later require deep linking or web integration.
