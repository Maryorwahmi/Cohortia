Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Common Mistakes and Assessment
Mistakes to fix
  • Missing await: awaiting push is required to receive results.
  • Unchecked casts: always verify ModalRoute arguments before use.
  • Invalid context: check mounted after async operations before navigating.
  • Drawer navigation: close then navigate to avoid animation conflicts.
Sample assessment
Question: Which BottomNavigationBar property triggers tab changes? Answer: onTap. This assessment reinforces that onTap reports the tapped index and you must update currentIndex to reflect the change.
