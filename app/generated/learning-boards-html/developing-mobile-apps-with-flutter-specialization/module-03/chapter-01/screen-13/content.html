Assessment Idea Check your understanding
Question 1
Which widget is best to change a list from single-column to two-column based on device width?
Answer: MediaQuery, because this is a global screen-level breakpoint decision based on full device width.
Question 2
If a Row has two fixed 50px children and a Flexible child, and the total width is 300px, how wide is the Flexible child?
Answer: 200px. Subtract fixed widths (100px) from total width and the Flexible child expands into the remaining space.
