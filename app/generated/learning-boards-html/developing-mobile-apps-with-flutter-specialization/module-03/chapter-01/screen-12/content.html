Performance & Best Practices
Reduce unnecessary rebuilds
Extract static parts out of builders, prefer const where possible, and limit heavy computations inside LayoutBuilder or OrientationBuilder closures.
  • Move headers and footers outside adaptive builders.
  • Use const constructors for unchanged widgets.
  • Avoid deep nested builders unless needed for separate adaptive concerns.
Quick checklist
  1. Scope builders narrowly.
  2. Extract stable children.
  3. Profile when in doubt.
Common pitfall
Embedding large trees in builders leads to frequent and expensive rebuilds that cost layout and paint time.
Key Takeaway

Keep adaptive logic local and efficient; extract stable UI to reduce rebuild scope and improve performance.
