Comparison Flexible vs Expanded
How they behave
Expanded = Flexible(fit: FlexFit.tight). Flexible default = FlexFit.loose. Use Expanded to force children to fill; Flexible to allow optional growth.
Row(
  children: [
    Expanded(
      child: TextField(),
    ),
    SizedBox(width: 8),
    Flexible(
      child: ElevatedButton(child: Text('Send')),
    ),
  ],
)
  • Expanded divides leftover space by flex values.
  • Flexible lets a child stay at its intrinsic size if it prefers.
  • Choose based on whether you want forced distribution or optional growth.
Concrete example
A chat input: TextField wrapped with Expanded fills remaining width; a small Send button wrapped with Flexible keeps its size but can grow if needed.
Common mistake
Wrapping every child in Expanded causes uniform splitting that often makes controls look stretched and unusable.
Key Takeaway

Use Expanded for forced distribution and Flexible for optional growth to preserve intrinsic sizing where appropriate.
