Process Flow Progressive approach
Layering strategy
MediaQuery for global breakpoints, LayoutBuilder for component constraint checks, Flexible/Expanded for internal space distribution.
Example flow
1) MediaQuery decides columns. 2) ProductCard uses LayoutBuilder to choose Column or Row. 3) Inside Row, Expanded is used for text and Flexible for buttons.
Why it matters
This separation reduces layout bugs and keeps each widget responsible for its own adaptivity.
Developer checklist
  • Choose MediaQuery for page-level changes.
  • Use LayoutBuilder for components used in different parent sizes.
  • Wrap inline flexible areas with Flexible or Expanded as needed.
Key Takeaway

Layer your responsiveness to keep logic local, predictable, and efficient.
