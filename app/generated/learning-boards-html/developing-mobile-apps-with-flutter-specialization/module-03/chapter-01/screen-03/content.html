Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Bloom Progression
Learn & Understand
  • Learn widget roles: MediaQuery gives device metrics; LayoutBuilder reports parent constraints.
  • Understand fit behavior: Expanded is tight, Flexible can be loose and respects intrinsic size.
  • Contrast common mistakes: reading full-screen metrics in nested widgets causes brittle layouts.
Apply & Master
  • Apply by implementing the Adaptive Product Card activity and testing different widths.
  • Master by extracting components and minimizing rebuilds triggered by LayoutBuilder or OrientationBuilder.
  • Verify across emulators and small tablets to ensure consistent behavior.
Quick mnemonic
Think "global vs local": MediaQuery = global, LayoutBuilder = local, Flexible/Expanded = inside-row sizing.
