Building Responsive UIs with Layout Widgets & Media Queries
Understand how to read device metrics and parent constraints, distribute space, and change layouts by orientation so your app looks great on phones and tablets.
In this lesson
A practical walkthrough of MediaQuery, LayoutBuilder, Flexible/Expanded, and OrientationBuilder with code patterns and common mistakes to avoid.
Why it matters
Apps that adapt keep interactions predictable and reduce layout bugs across devices.
Explore Key Topics
MediaQuery
Global device metrics like width and orientation for top-level breakpoints.
LayoutBuilder
Local constraints let a component adapt to the space its parent gives it.
Flexible & Expanded
Distribute available space inside Rows and Columns with proportional sizing.
Interactive Concept
MediaQuery gives screen size numbers for breakpoint decisions.
LayoutBuilder reacts to parent constraints for modular components.
