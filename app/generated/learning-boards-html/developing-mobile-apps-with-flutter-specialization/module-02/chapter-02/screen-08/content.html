Definition

FloatingActionButton

A circular elevated button representing the main action on a screen. Place with Scaffold.floatingActionButton and provide a tooltip for accessibility.

Design tips
  • Use one FAB per screen for a single primary action.
  • Choose a clear icon and provide a tooltip or semantics for clarity.
  • Ensure FAB doesn't obstruct important content — adjust padding or docking if necessary.
