SafeArea
Automatically adds padding to keep content visible inside safe device bounds. Use it for full-screen or scrollable content.
Example: wrapping your ListView with SafeArea ensures the top list item is not hidden behind the status bar.
Expanded vs Flexible
Expanded forces its child to fill remaining main-axis space. Flexible lets the child size itself up to the available space.
Concrete example: wrap the middle column in a Row with Expanded so text wraps instead of causing an overflow.
Analogy
Expanded is like giving a bookshelf all remaining shelf space; Flexible is like allowing a book to grow but not forcing it.
Common mistake
Putting an unbounded child in a Row without Expanded or Flexible leads to overflow errors during layout.
