Refactor steps
Follow these steps to convert your app to use sqflite-backed Todos.
1. Add dependencies
sqflite and path_provider in pubspec.yaml, then flutter pub get.
2. Create model
Implement Todo with toMap and fromMap converting booleans to integers.
3. DatabaseHelper
Singleton pattern, _initDatabase with onCreate, and CRUD methods.
4. UI wiring
Call insert/update/delete and reload the list; await operations before calling setState.
Verify
Todos persist across app restarts.
Verify
Checkbox toggles update the database.
Verify
Deletes remove the corresponding database row.
