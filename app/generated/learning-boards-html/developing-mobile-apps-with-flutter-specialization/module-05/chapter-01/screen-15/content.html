Firebase (BaaS)
Managed services for auth, database, and storage so you can focus on the frontend.
flutterfire CLI
Automates generation of firebase_options.dart and platform configuration for Flutter projects.
firebase_core init
Call ensureInitialized then await Firebase.initializeApp(options: DefaultFirebaseOptions.currentPlatform) in main.
Summary Takeaway

Use the Console and flutterfire CLI as your configuration sources of truth, add firebase_core, and initialize in main to avoid startup errors.
