Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Sign Out
Sign-out steps
Always await signOut calls and clear provider sessions when present.
1. await FirebaseAuth.instance.signOut()
2. If using Google, await _googleSignIn.signOut()
3. Rely on authStateChanges to switch to AuthScreen
Example (HomeScreen)
Call signOut from a tap handler and await it. Only after signOut completes should you assume currentUser is null or the StreamBuilder will reflect the change.
Common mistake
Not clearing provider session leaves automatic re-login behavior that confuses users who expect to choose accounts again.
