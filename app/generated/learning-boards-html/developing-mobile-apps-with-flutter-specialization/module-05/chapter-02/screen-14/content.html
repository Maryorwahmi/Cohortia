Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Best Practices
Initialization
Call Firebase.initializeApp in main and await it before runApp so services are available for auth flows.
Async discipline
Await register, sign-in, and sign-out calls to avoid race conditions when reading currentUser.
Resource hygiene
Dispose controllers in dispose(), and clear GoogleSignIn sessions on sign-out.
Testing
Exercise registration, login, wrong credentials, and sign-out paths to verify error handling and StreamBuilder transitions.
