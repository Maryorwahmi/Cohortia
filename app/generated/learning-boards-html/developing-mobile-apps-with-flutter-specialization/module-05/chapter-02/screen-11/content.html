Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Comparison
Email / Password
  • Works across platforms; no external provider required.
  • Requires password policies and recovery flows.
  • Easy to enable in Firebase Console with a toggle.
Google Sign-In
  • Simpler onboarding for users with Google accounts.
  • Needs google_sign_in, OAuth client IDs, and consent configuration.
  • Remember to sign out of GoogleSignIn to clear local sessions.
Recommendation
Offer both: provide email/password for universality and Google for convenience, and handle provider setup and sign-out explicitly.
