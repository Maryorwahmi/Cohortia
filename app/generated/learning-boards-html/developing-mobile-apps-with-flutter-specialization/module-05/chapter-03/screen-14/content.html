Documents & Collections
Documents store fields; collections group documents; use subcollections for growing related items.
Realtime vs One-time
Use snapshots() with StreamBuilder for live updates; use get() for single fetches.
CRUD essentials
add(), set(), update(), get(), snapshots(), delete(). Choose methods per needs and handle errors.
Summary takeaway

Design your Firestore model for the reads your app needs, use streams for live UIs, and secure your rules before shipping.
