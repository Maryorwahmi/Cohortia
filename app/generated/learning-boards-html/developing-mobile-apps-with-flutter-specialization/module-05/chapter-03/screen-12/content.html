Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Data Modeling
Common patterns
Posts: posts collection, each doc has userId, content, timestamp.
Chat: chatRooms collection for metadata; messages subcollection for chat items.
Todos: todos collection with userId to filter per user.
Why it matters
Modeling decisions directly affect performance, security rules complexity, and cost. Think about queries first.
Mini scenario
You need to show a feed of posts by a user. Recommendation: top-level posts collection with userId field and query posts.where('userId', isEqualTo: userId).orderBy('timestamp', descending:true). This avoids deep nesting and makes queries simple and performant.
Misconception
Don't over-normalize like a SQL model; Firestore encourages denormalization when it reduces reads and simplifies mobile UX.
