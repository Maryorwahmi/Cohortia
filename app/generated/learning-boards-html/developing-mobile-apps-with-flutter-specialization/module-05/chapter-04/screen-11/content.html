Architecture › Data Modeling
Denormalization

Duplicate read-heavy fields into documents to avoid multiple lookups. Example: include a user's displayName in posts to render a feed without reading the users collection for each post.

Subcollections

Use when data is owned by a parent and accessed in that context, like comments under a post. Subcollections let you scope rules and queries naturally.

Arrays vs. growing lists

Arrays are fine for small static lists. For growing lists or items that need individual rules or queries, prefer subcollections to avoid large array costs.

Practical guidance
  • Model for your most frequent queries to minimize reads and cost.
  • When denormalizing, plan batched writes or transactions to update duplicates reliably.
  • Prefer subcollections for per-item security rules and high-growth lists.
Key takeaway: there is no single right model—trade read cost, write complexity, and security needs to choose the best approach.
