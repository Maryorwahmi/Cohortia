Full vs Partial Mesh

Full mesh: maximum redundancy, extreme cabling. Partial mesh: redundancy for critical nodes with fewer links.

Use case: critical backbone links and inter-data-center connections where downtime is unacceptable.
Planning questions: port count, cable cost, and management overhead.
Concrete example

Two core switches with multiple redundant links to distribution switches form a partial mesh. If one uplink fails, alternate links carry the traffic with little impact. Combine with routing protocols for fast convergence.

Key Takeaway

Mesh adds resilience at cost; design partial mesh to protect the most important links.
