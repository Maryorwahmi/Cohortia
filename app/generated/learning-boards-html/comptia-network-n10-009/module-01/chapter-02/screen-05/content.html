Star: definition and use

Every node connects to a central device; ideal for offices and labs where ease of wiring and fault isolation matter.

Advantage: simple troubleshooting; single-cable faults affect one device only.
Risk: central switch is a single point of failure unless redundancy is added.
Concrete example & mitigation

A 24-port switch in a wiring closet supports a department. Single desktop failure is easy to fix. To reduce outage risk, add an uplink to a second switch or use switch stacking and UPS power.

Key Takeaway

Star fits most LANs for cost and manageability; protect the central device for high availability.
