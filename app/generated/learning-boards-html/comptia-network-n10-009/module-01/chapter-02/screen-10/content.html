Single-mode vs Multi-mode

Single-mode: small core, long distance, high bandwidth. Multi-mode: larger core, shorter distance, lower cost transceivers for short runs.

Connector types: LC, SC, ST. LC is common in modern equipment.
Safety: never look into a live fiber and keep connectors clean.
Concrete selection example

For a 300 meter link between floors carrying aggregation traffic, choose single-mode for distance headroom and lower attenuation. For 10–50 meter links in a rack, multi-mode with LC connectors is cost-effective.

Key Takeaway

Fiber solves distance and EMI problems; match fiber type and connectors to your transceivers and safety rules.
