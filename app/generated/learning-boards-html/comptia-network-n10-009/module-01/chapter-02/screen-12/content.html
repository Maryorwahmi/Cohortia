Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Cable Selection Activity
Scenario 1 — Workstation, 15m

Choose Cat6 UTP and RJ45. Cat6 supports Gigabit to 100m; cabling is flexible and low-cost for desktop runs.

Reason: distance short, EMI normal, cost-effective.
Scenario 2 — Switches, 300m

Choose single-mode fiber with LC connectors. Single-mode supports long distances and is immune to EMI, making it ideal for multi-floor uplinks.

Reason: long distance and high bandwidth needs.
Scenario 3 — Street to modem

Choose coaxial cable with F-type connector, the standard for broadband provider drops and RF delivery.

Reason: provider infrastructure and RF modulation compatibility.
Scenario 4 — Server to switch, 2m

Choose Cat6a UTP for 10G up to 100m, or multi-mode fiber with LC for rack density. For 2m, copper is often cheaper and practical if both ports support 10G over copper.

Reason: very short distance favors copper; fiber adds headroom in high-density racks.
