Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Quick Reference
LINK
Verify physical link lights and basic connectivity before deeper troubleshooting.
TEST
Use a cable tester to confirm pair order and continuity; measure length and attenuation when suspect.
CHOOSE
Select media based on distance, bandwidth, and EMI. Document your choice for future maintenance.
Final Note

A consistent on-site routine of checking link, testing cable, and choosing the right medium prevents many common network problems.
