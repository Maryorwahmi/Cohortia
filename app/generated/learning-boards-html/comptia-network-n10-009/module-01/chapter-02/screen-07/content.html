Bus topology

Single shared backbone cable. Pros: simple and inexpensive. Cons: break in backbone disrupts all devices and performance worsens with load.

Check terminations and continuity for faults.
Replacement recommendation: migrate to star for better isolation.
Ring topology

Devices form a closed loop and pass frames around the ring. Pros: orderly passing of frames. Cons: single break can disrupt the whole ring unless redundancy is added.

Use case: some legacy or industrial networks; modern networks prefer star or mesh with redundancy.
