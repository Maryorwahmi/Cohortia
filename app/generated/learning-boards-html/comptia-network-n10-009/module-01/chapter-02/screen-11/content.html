Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Coaxial Cable
Construction and uses

Coax has center conductor and shield. Common for cable TV and broadband street drops. Check connectors and shielding to avoid signal loss.

Practical checklist
Inspect BNC or F-type connectors for corrosion.
Verify impedance matches devices to avoid reflections.
Key Takeaway

Coax still serves access networks well; prefer fiber for backbone bandwidth and EMI immunity.
