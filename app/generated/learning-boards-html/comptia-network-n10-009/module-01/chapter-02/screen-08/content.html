UTP vs STP

UTP is common and flexible. STP adds shielding for high-EMI environments. Choose STP only when EMI risks justify the extra cost and grounding complexity.

Advantage: UTP is cheap and easy; STP reduces interference when installed correctly.
Installation tip: maintain twist pairs as close to the termination as possible to limit crosstalk.
Category summary
Cat5e: 1 Gbps up to 100m
Cat6: 1 Gbps to 100m; 10G up to ~55m
Cat6a: 10G up to 100m
Key Takeaway

Match Cat rating to bandwidth needs and future-proof where rewiring is costly.
