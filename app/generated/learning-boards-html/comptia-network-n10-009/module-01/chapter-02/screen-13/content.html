Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Network Topology
Arrangement of devices and data flow; both physical and logical views matter.
Twisted-Pair Categories
Cat5e/6/6a define speed and distance; follow category limits and terminate correctly.
Media Selection
Pick copper for short runs, fiber for long or EMI-prone links, coax for specific access roles.
Summary Takeaway

When designing or troubleshooting, check topology, verify termination, and confirm media fit the environment.
