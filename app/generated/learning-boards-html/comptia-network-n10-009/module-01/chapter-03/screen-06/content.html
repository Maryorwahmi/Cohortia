Comparison
Collision Domain
Where frames can collide because multiple hosts share the same physical medium.
  • Hubs: single collision domain.
  • Switch ports: separate collision domains per port.
  • Concerned with simultaneous transmissions and retransmits.
Comparison
Broadcast Domain
Which devices receive broadcast messages like ARP requests.
  • Switches forward broadcasts to all ports in the same VLAN.
  • Routers separate broadcast domains; they typically do not forward broadcasts between interfaces.
  • Use VLANs to create multiple broadcast domains on one switch.
Why it matters
Knowing which domain is causing problems directs your fix: replace a hub or segment with switches for collision issues; apply VLANs or routing when broadcasts are causing unnecessary load or security exposure.
