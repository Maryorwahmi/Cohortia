Network Devices and Their Functions
Understand how hubs, switches, routers, and other devices forward and protect traffic.
In this lesson

Identify device roles, learn collision and broadcast domains, and practice basic connectivity tools used in troubleshooting.

Why it matters

Selecting the right device affects performance, security, and manageability. Misplacing a hub where a switch is needed can cause collisions and outages.
