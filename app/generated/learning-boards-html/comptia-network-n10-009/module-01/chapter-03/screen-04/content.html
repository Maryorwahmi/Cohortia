Definition
Hub
Layer 1 multi-port repeater that broadcasts incoming signals to all ports.
Analogy
A loudspeaker in a room that repeats everything it receives to every listener, even when not intended for them.
Collision domain
  • All ports share a single collision domain.
  • Concurrent transmissions cause collisions and retransmits.
Common mistake
Assuming a hub filters traffic like a switch. Hubs do no MAC learning or intelligent forwarding.
Example scenario
Three clients connect to a hub. One uploads a 200 MB file; the hub repeats every frame to all ports, causing unnecessary traffic and raising collision count on the segment.
Check your understanding
If you replace a hub with a switch, what changes would you expect in collisions and bandwidth usage? Answer: collisions decrease dramatically because each switch port is a separate collision domain and bandwidth is used only for intended destinations.
Key Takeaway

Hubs simply repeat signals at Layer 1. Replace hubs with switches to reduce collisions and improve performance.
