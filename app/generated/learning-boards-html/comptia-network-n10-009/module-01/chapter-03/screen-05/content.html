Core Concept
Switch
Layer 2 device that forwards based on MAC and supports VLANs to control broadcast domains.
How MAC learning works
  1. Switch sees frame; records source MAC and incoming port in MAC table.
  2. Checks destination MAC in MAC table.
  3. Forwards to the specific port if found; otherwise floods to learn location.
VLAN mnemonic
Think "Virtual fence" — VLANs partition one physical switch into separate broadcast fences.
Example
PC-A on port 2 sends to PC-B on port 5. Switch learns PC-A's MAC on port 2 and later forwards frames to port 5 only, reducing unnecessary traffic and collisions.
Key Takeaway

Switches provide intelligent forwarding at Layer 2 and let you create VLANs to control broadcasts and improve security.
