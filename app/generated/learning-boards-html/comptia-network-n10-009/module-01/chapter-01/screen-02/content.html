Learning Objectives

A roadmap of practical skills and the reasoning you will acquire.

01
Network Purpose
Explain why networks exist and their benefits

You will describe how shared resources and data exchange improve productivity in homes and businesses and give a home Wi‑Fi example to ground the idea.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Network Types
Distinguish PAN, LAN, WAN, and WLAN

You will map real situations to type definitions, like Bluetooth for PAN and Wi‑Fi for WLAN, and explain difference in scope and typical technologies.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Components
Identify hosts, devices, media, and protocols

You will name examples such as laptops, switches, Ethernet cable, and the role of protocols in enabling communication.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Models
Differentiate client-server and peer-to-peer, and learn OSI/TCP‑IP stacks

You will explain when central servers are better than peer sharing and why layered models help with troubleshooting.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Bloom's progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
