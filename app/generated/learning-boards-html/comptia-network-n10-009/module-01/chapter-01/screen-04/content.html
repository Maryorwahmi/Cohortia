What is a Network?

A definition with a simple, practical home example.

Formal Definition

A network is a collection of interconnected devices that share resources and exchange data under agreed rules.

Benefit
Shared internet, centralized files, and easier communication.
Example
Phone streaming a video via home Wi‑Fi through an access point to the internet.
Common misconception
Networks aren’t only big; even two devices sharing a cable are a network.
Key Takeaway

Networks require devices, media, and rules. All three must be present for useful communication.

Illustration: Home Wi‑Fi Flow
Hosts: phone, laptop, smart TV
Network devices: access point, router, modem
Media: radio waves for Wi‑Fi, broadband link to ISP
Protocols: Wi‑Fi standards and TCP/IP on the internet
