Network Types: PAN and LAN

Compare scope, technology, and troubleshooting hints.

PAN
Personal Area Network
  • Very short range, personal devices like earbuds and smartwatches.
  • Uses short‑range wireless such as Bluetooth.
  • Simple pairing and limited scope; no routing to wider networks typically.
Example: Pairing wireless earbuds to your phone while jogging.
LAN
Local Area Network
  • Covers a home, office, or campus.
  • Uses Ethernet or Wi‑Fi; higher data rates than PAN.
  • Supports shared resources like printers, file servers, and internet access.
Example: Office Wi‑Fi and wired desktops connected to a switch and router.
Quick Tip
To classify, ask: who uses it and over what distance?
Misconception
Not all wireless networks are LANs; Bluetooth is usually PAN not LAN.
Key Takeaway
Scope and technology determine type and troubleshooting steps.
