Learning Objectives — Practical Skills

Apply classification and troubleshooting in everyday network scenarios.

Scenario Practice
  • Classify setups as PAN, LAN, WLAN or WAN using scope, technology, and examples.
  • Choose client-server or peer-to-peer models based on whether services are centralized.
  • Identify the most relevant components to check when a connection fails.
Mnemonic: Hosts, Hardware, Highways, House Rules
Micro-Checklist
  1. Identify scope (personal, local, wide)
  2. Identify dominant media (wireless, copper, fiber)
  3. Decide on a model (central server or peer sharing)
