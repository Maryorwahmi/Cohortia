Network Types: WAN and WLAN

Understand geographic scope and media-focused networks.

WAN
Wide Area Network
  • Connects networks over large geographic distances.
  • Often uses provider links, routing, and VPNs for connectivity and security.
  • The internet is the primary example of a public WAN.
Example: Headquarters in New York connected to branch offices in London and Tokyo with routing and VPNs.
WLAN
Wireless Local Area Network
  • LAN that uses wireless access points and radio waves for host connectivity.
  • Focuses troubleshooting on RF, access point placement, and security settings.
  • Common in public Wi‑Fi, offices, and homes.
Example: Coffee shop Wi‑Fi that provides customer access while connecting to the internet via an ISP link.
Classification Tip
Ask: Are we describing distance or the medium?
Misconception
WLAN describes wireless access; WAN describes distance and inter-network links.
Key Takeaway
Differentiate scope versus medium when classifying networks.
