Client-Server vs Peer-to-Peer

Choose a model based on scale, security, and manageability.

Client-Server
Dedicated servers provide services to clients.
  • Centralized management and security.
  • Better for backups, permissions, and scaling.
  • Example: Web browser requests a page from a web server.
Good for businesses with many users and shared resources.
Peer-to-Peer (P2P)
Each device can act as both client and server.
  • Simple setup and low cost for small groups.
  • Harder to secure, manage, and scale.
  • Example: Friends sharing files at a LAN party.
Avoid for enterprise-level resource sharing and compliance.
Decision Hint
Ask: Do I need centralized control, backups, and policies?
Misconception
P2P is not a drop-in replacement for server-based architectures at scale.
Key Takeaway
Choose client-server for centralized control; choose P2P only when simplicity and cost trump management needs.
