Key Concepts

Three compact concept cards to carry forward.

Network
A set of connected devices sharing resources and data under agreed rules.
Types
PAN, LAN, WLAN and WAN describe scope and common technologies like Bluetooth, Ethernet, Wi‑Fi and provider links.
Models & Models
Client‑server centralizes services; P2P shares directly. Use OSI and TCP/IP as complementary frameworks.
Final Summary
Classify networks by scope and medium, identify the four components, and choose an architecture that fits scale and security needs.
