Process Flow
Physical layer checklist
  1. 1. Confirm power to the device and switch. Look for link LEDs on the NIC and port.
  2. 2. Swap the cable with a known-good cable and test again.
  3. 3. Move to a different switch port or patch panel to rule out a bad port.
  4. 4. If available, test with another NIC or a USB network adapter.
  5. 5. If fibre or SFPs are used, confirm transceiver seating and link speed compatibility.
Key Takeaway

Most full-loss incidents are fixed by replacing a cable or using a different switch port.

Annotated scenario
Symptom
Workstation reports no network; no link LED on NIC.
Immediate check: is the patch cable fully seated?
Example fix
Swap the cable with a known-good cable. If the link returns, replace the old cable and document the port and cable ID.
Common Mistake
Jumping to DNS or firewall checks before confirming physical connectivity wastes time.
Pro tip
Keep a labeled box of known-good patch cables and a small USB NIC in your toolkit for fast isolation.
