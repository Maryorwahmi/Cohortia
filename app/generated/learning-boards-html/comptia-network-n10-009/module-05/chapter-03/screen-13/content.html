Definition
Environmental and power impacts
What to monitor
  • Temperature and humidity in server and wiring rooms.
  • UPS logs and power event records.
  • Device uptime and unexpected reboot history.
Practical fixes
  • Install proper airflow and cooling in closets and racks.
  • Use UPS for critical devices and document power events.
  • Plan redundant power or failover where outages are costly.
Example
Intermittent interface errors correlate with rising rack temperatures. Improving cooling stabilizes devices.
Common Mistake
Rushing to replace hardware without checking environmental logs wastes budget and misses root cause.
Key Takeaway

Always check environmental and power history for intermittent failures; fix conditions before replacing network gear when possible.
