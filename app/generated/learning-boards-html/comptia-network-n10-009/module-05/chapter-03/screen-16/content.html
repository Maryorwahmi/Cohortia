Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › What to Remember
Start at Layer 1
Check power, link lights, and cables before anything else.
Use the right test
Adapter details for IP, lookup tools for DNS, ping/traceroute for performance.
Document & escalate
Record outputs, timestamps, and device IDs so others can reproduce and act on your evidence.
Final takeaway

A repeatable, documented process reduces time to resolution and prevents unnecessary hardware replacement or misdiagnosis.
