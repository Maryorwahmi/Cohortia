Bandwidth Saturation
Symptom: many users experience slow transfers. Tooling: flow data, SNMP counters, network interface utilization. Fixes: identify heavy flows, apply QoS, or upgrade the link.
Example
An uplink consistently reports 95% utilization during work hours; non-critical traffic should be deprioritized and capacity planned.
Comparison
High Latency
Symptom: interactive apps lag, small transactions take long. Tooling: ping and traceroute to measure RTT and identify slow hops. Fixes: investigate routing, reduce queuing, or address overloaded devices along the path.
Example
Traceroute shows a single hop adding 200 ms; investigate that device or the ISP segment for congestion or queuing policies.
Key Takeaway

Distinguish throughput problems from latency. Use flow metrics for saturation and ping/traceroute for delays, then apply QoS or capacity planning as appropriate.
