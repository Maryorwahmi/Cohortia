Packet loss: trace, test, and correlate
Symptoms
  • Audio or video gaps during calls.
  • Retransmissions and slow TCP throughput.
  • Intermittent application timeouts.
Measure loss with sustained pings and note the first-hop where loss appears.
Common causes
  • Congestion on a saturated link.
  • Interface errors from cabling or bad transceivers.
  • Faulty network hardware or misconfiguration.
Example
Ping shows 8% loss to server; local switch interface has CRC errors. Replace cable and retest to confirm remediation.
Common Mistake
Assuming all packet loss is due to internet issues. Local hardware often causes similar symptoms.
Key Takeaway

Correlate observed packet loss with device counters to determine whether to replace hardware or escalate upstream.
