01. Physical diagnostics
Check link lights, cable integrity, NIC and switch ports. This prevents wasted time chasing higher-layer faults.
Verified Competency Milestone
02. IP & DHCP troubleshooting
Read and interpret ipconfig or ip addr output, renew DHCP leases, and resolve duplicate address scenarios.
Verified Competency Milestone
03. DNS resolution
Confirm name resolution with nslookup or dig and identify when DNS servers or firewall rules are blocking lookups.
Verified Competency Milestone
04. Performance & environment
Measure latency, packet loss, and bandwidth; understand environmental impacts like power and cooling on device reliability.
Verified Competency Milestone
Objective details
  • Learn the simple checks that eliminate many incidents within minutes.
  • Understand the signs of misconfiguration vs hardware failure.
  • Apply command-line tools to gather evidence and propose fixes.
  • Master a compact escalation plan that includes security and environment checks.
Each milestone maps to a concise hands-on task so you can prove the skill in a lab or on the job.
