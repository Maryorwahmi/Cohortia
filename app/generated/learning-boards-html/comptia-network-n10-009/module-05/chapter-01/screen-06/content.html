What NetFlow Records Contain
Source/destination, ports, protocol, byte and packet counts, timestamps.
NetFlow gives you an efficient summary of conversations. It's ideal for capacity planning, identifying heavy users, and spotting suspicious large transfers. Collectors aggregate records and present top-talkers, top-destinations, and protocol distribution over time.
Example workflow
When a link saturates, consult NetFlow to see which sources or applications are consuming bandwidth, then use SNMP for device counters and packet capture for a detailed look if needed.
Limitation
NetFlow lacks payloads and packet-level timing. It cannot show the exact content exchanged or reconstruct sessions like a packet sniffer can.
Practical tip
Export flows to a collector and keep aggregated retention for trend analysis, while storing raw capture for short windows only when you need deep inspection.
Key Takeaway

Use NetFlow to quickly identify traffic patterns and heavy flows; add packet capture when you need payload or packet timing details.
