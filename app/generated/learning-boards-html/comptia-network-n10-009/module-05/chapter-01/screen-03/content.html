Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Learning Objectives
Objective Actions
Collect
Gather CPU, latency, bandwidth for a week to define normal operating ranges.
Observe
Use SNMP to poll counters and traps for device-state insights.
Aggregate
Export flow records to analyze traffic patterns and heavy users.
Diagnose
Capture packets and centralize logs to confirm root causes and trigger alerts.
Mnemonic
Baseline → Observe → Flow → Capture
(Think: establish normal, check device, check traffic, capture packets)
