Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Packet Sniffer
Capture raw packets to inspect headers and payloads for retransmissions and errors.
Syslog
Centralize device logs to search, correlate, and trigger alerts across the network.
Alerting
Design alerts based on baselines and correlated signals to reduce noise and focus responses.
Final takeaway
Combine baselines, SNMP, NetFlow, packet capture, and centralized logs in a reproducible workflow so monitoring leads directly to fast, confident troubleshooting.
