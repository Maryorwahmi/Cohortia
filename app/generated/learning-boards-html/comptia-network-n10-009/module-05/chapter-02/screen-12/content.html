Troubleshooting Methodology
A structured approach reduces wasted effort and prevents guesswork.
Test with evidence
Use commands like ipconfig /all, ip addr show, and ping to confirm or deny hypotheses.
Document Findings
Record steps, root cause, and prevention so future incidents are faster to solve.
Summary Takeaway
Practice the method on real scenarios and keep concise documentation; speed comes from discipline, not guesswork.
