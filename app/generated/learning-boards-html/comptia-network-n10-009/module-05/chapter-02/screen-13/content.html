Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › What to Remember
1. Determine scope
Single user vs. site-wide changes your diagnostic focus and tests.
2. Test before change
Use ipconfig /all, ip addr show, and ping to verify behavior before altering configuration.
3. Verify & document
Confirm service, gather evidence, and record steps and prevention to help others later.
Final takeaway
A short, methodical sequence and good notes will make you faster and prevent repeat incidents.
