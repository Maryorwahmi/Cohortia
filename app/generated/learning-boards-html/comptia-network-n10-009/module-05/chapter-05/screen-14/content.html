Best Practice

Audits & Maintenance

Monthly

Sample critical devices and recent changes for quick verification.

Quarterly

Full inventory reconciliation and diagram review.

Automation

Use scripts to collect firmware and reachable IP checks, but verify physical labels manually.

Common pitfalls
  • Multiple unsynced spreadsheets
  • No change log or delayed documentation updates
  • Vague or inconsistent naming
Key Takeaway

A regular audit cycle plus update discipline keeps documentation trustworthy and actionable.
