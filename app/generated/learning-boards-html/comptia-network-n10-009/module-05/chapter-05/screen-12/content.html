Physical Topology — sketching tips

Document rack position, patch panel mappings, switch ports, cable types, and power so technicians can act without guesswork.

Rack elevation

List units from top to bottom, devices by U, and PDU outlet numbers for power tracing.

Cable labeling

Label both ends: source→destination with port numbers and location tags.

Example physical note

"SW1 G0/12 → PP1:12 → WALL2-OUTLET15 → DESK3". Cable type: Cat6. Label both ends with the full path and port mappings.

Why it matters

Precise physical notes prevent technicians from unplugging the wrong connection and speed on-site recovery.
