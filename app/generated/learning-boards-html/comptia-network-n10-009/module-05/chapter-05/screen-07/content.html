Process Flow

Inventory & Asset Management

Record

Device type, model, serial, purchase and warranty dates.

Identify

Management IP, MAC, firmware, and configuration notes.

Locate

Physical location, rack U, switch port, and cable endpoints.

Practical note
Use a single, authoritative inventory: a structured spreadsheet or a simple database. Define update triggers so the inventory is updated whenever equipment is added, replaced, or reconfigured. This prevents drift between records and reality.
