Why this chapter matters

A hardened network reduces the attack surface and helps ensure availability. Without these practices, attackers can exploit default credentials, unpatched firmware, or open management ports to gain access. Hardening also supports faster recovery when incidents occur.

Starter checklist
  • Change default credentials and enable strong passwords.
  • Enable SSH and disable Telnet or HTTP for device management.
  • Schedule scans and a patch window; keep config backups.
  • Lock wiring closets and document DR contact procedures.
