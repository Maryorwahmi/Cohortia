Key Concepts — Continued
ACL (Access Control List)
Rules that filter traffic to enforce least privilege for management and data flows.
Physical Security
Locks, surveillance, and environmental controls that protect hardware and cabling.
DR & BC
Plans and tests to restore services (DR) and continue critical operations (BC).
Final Takeaway

Combine technical controls, processes, and physical safeguards, and test them regularly to maintain a hardened and resilient network.
