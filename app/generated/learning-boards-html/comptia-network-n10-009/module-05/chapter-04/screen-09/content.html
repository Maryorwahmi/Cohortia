Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Physical Security
Protecting the Physical Layer
Access Controls
Keycards, biometrics, and locked doors to prevent unauthorized entry.
Locked Racks
Secure switches, routers, and patch panels inside locked cabinets.
Environment
Temperature, humidity control, and fire suppression to prevent hardware failures.
Checklist
  • Lock wiring closets and restrict keys or cards.
  • Log and review physical access events.
  • Secure cabling and label patch panels to reduce accidental impact.
Example
Locked racks prevented a contractor from connecting a rogue device in a recent audit scenario.
