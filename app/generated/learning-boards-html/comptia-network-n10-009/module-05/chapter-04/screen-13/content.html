Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Testing & Maintenance
Ongoing verification and audits
Automated scans
Daily or weekly vulnerability and compliance scans to detect issues quickly.
Audits
Periodic human review of change logs and configuration snapshots.
DR tests
Restore tests and failover drills to confirm RTO and RPO targets are met.
Key Takeaway

Combine automated detection with scheduled human reviews and regular recovery tests to maintain a hardened and resilient network.
