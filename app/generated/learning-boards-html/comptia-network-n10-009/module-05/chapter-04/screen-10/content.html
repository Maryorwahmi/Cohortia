Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity Timeline
Identify critical services and set RTO and RPO targets.
Design redundancy: redundant links, failover servers, and offsite backups.
Document recovery procedures, communication plans, and responsible personnel.
Schedule regular tests and drills to validate failover and backup restores.
Example
Failover to a secondary site during a power outage validated the recovery plan and communication timeline.
Misconception
Backups alone are not a full DR solution without tested restoration and failover procedures.
Tip
Include communications and roles in exercises so people know what to do under pressure.
