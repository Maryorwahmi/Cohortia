Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › DoS / DDoS Defenses
Mitigation process
1. Detect
Monitor traffic for abnormal spikes and patterns that indicate an attack.
2. Filter
Apply rate limits and block malicious sources at the network edge or via scrubbing services.
3. Failover
Shift traffic to redundant infrastructure or mitigations so legitimate users remain served.
Concrete example
A sudden surge from thousands of IPs calls for edge filters and routing through a scrubbing center while failover handles remaining legitimate traffic.
Common mistake

Assuming one device will stop a large DDoS. Attackers vary vectors; layered capacity and filtering are required.

Key Takeaway

Prepare detection, edge filtering, and failover so availability survives traffic-based attacks.
