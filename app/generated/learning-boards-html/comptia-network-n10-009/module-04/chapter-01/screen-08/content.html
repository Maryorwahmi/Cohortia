Comparison

Threats and targets

Compare the main threat types and which CIA property they commonly attack to guide defenses.

Malware
Includes viruses, worms, Trojans, ransomware, spyware. Targets confidentiality and integrity by stealing or altering data, or availability by encrypting systems.
Phishing
Tricks users into revealing credentials or visiting malicious sites. Often leads to compromised accounts and loss of confidentiality.
DoS / DDoS
Floods services to make them unavailable. Directly attacks availability and requires resilience and traffic filtering to mitigate.
Mitigation examples
For malware
Use antivirus, IDS/IPS, patching, and least-privilege access to reduce impact and spread.
For phishing
Combine technical filters with security awareness training and policies to reduce successful credential theft.
For DoS/DDoS
Implement rate limits, traffic filtering, and redundant capacity to maintain availability under load.
Key Takeaway

Map threats to the triad to pick the controls that directly address attacker goals and reduce risk efficiently.
