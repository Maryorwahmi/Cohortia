Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Availability
Definition

Availability: resilience and access

Keep systems and data accessible for authorized users. Plan redundancy, failover, and recovery to maintain availability during failures and attacks.

Redundancy
Duplicate critical components so single failures do not interrupt service.
Failover & backups
Ensure services can switch to backups and that data recovery processes are tested.
DDoS protection
Mitigate traffic floods to preserve access for legitimate users.
Concrete example
A public web portal uses multiple data centers and a load balancer so a single-site failure or surge does not take the site offline.
Common mistake

Securing everything behind strict controls without failover can make services unavailable when staff need access most.

Key Takeaway

Plan and test redundancy, failover, and recovery so availability survives failures and attacks.
