Phishing: technical plus human defenses
How it works
Attackers craft messages or fake sites to harvest credentials or deliver malware. Social engineering targets trust and routine behaviors.
Technical controls
Email and web filtering reduce exposure to malicious content.
Administrative controls
Policies, training, and simulated phishing exercises improve detection and reporting.
Concrete example
An email spoofing payroll requests credentials. The correct response is to verify via an established phone number rather than clicking links.
Common mistake

Assuming technical filters eliminate phishing. Attackers practice evasion; continuous training is necessary.

Key Takeaway

Pair filters with training and clear reporting so users act as part of your defense, not the weakest link.
