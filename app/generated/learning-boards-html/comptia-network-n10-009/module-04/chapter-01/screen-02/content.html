Learning Objectives

Learn, understand, apply, and begin to master the chapter outcomes.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain the CIA triad
Describe confidentiality, integrity, and availability and why they are the foundation of all security decisions.
Mnemonic: Vault (Confidentiality), Stamp (Integrity), Bridge (Availability)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify common threats
Recognize malware, phishing, and DoS/DDoS attacks and how they affect network security.
Mnemonic: Bugs (malware), Bait (phishing), Flood (DoS)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe security policies
Explain the role of policies and best practices such as acceptable use and password requirements.
Mnemonic: Rulebook
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Differentiate control types
Distinguish technical, administrative, and physical controls and when to use each.
Mnemonic: Tools, Paper, Locks
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
