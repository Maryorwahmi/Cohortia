Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Confidentiality
Only authorized users may access information; encryption and access control enforce this.
Integrity
Ensure data is accurate and unmodified; verify with hashes and signatures.
Availability
Keep services and data accessible through redundancy and recovery planning.
Summary takeaway
Map threats to confidentiality, integrity, or availability, then choose layered technical, administrative, and physical controls to reduce risk while preserving usability.
