Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Confidentiality
Protect reading access with encryption and access control.
Integrity
Detect tampering using hashes and digital signatures.
Availability
Ensure access with redundancy, failover, and tested recovery.
Practical prompt
Pick one critical asset, identify the top threat, map it to confidentiality/integrity/availability, and list one technical, one administrative, and one physical control you would apply.
