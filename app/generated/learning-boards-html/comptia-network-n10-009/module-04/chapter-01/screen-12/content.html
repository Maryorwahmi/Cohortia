Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Password Policy Analysis
Scenario

Review this draft policy

Password Policy Draft:
- Minimum length: 8 characters
- Must include at least one uppercase letter and one number.
- Passwords expire every 180 days.
- Users cannot reuse their last 3 passwords.
- Users can write down their passwords if they keep them in a locked drawer.
Guided mapping
  • Minimum length: targets confidentiality by increasing guess resistance; consider raising length or using passphrases.
  • Complexity rules: aim to defend against simple guesses; combine with length for stronger protection.
  • Expiration and reuse: reduce long-lived compromises, but frequent rotation can burden users and affect availability.
  • Writing passwords down: administrative allowance that undermines confidentiality; prefer secure password managers.
Suggested improvements
Increase minimum length
Recommend longer passphrases instead of only complexity, strengthening confidentiality.
Add multi-factor authentication
MFA greatly reduces credential compromise impact, supporting confidentiality and integrity.
Remove insecure notes allowance
Prohibit writing passwords in unsecured physical locations; recommend a vetted password manager.
Key Takeaway

Map policy items to CIA to find trade-offs: improve confidentiality with length and MFA, but avoid excessive rotation that harms availability.
