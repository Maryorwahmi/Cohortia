Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Common Mistakes and Troubleshooting
Troubleshooting checklist
Verify ACL rule order and that specific permits exist before denies.
Ensure the operator's IP is included in permit statements to avoid lockout.
Confirm management protocols are SSH/HTTPS and that Telnet/HTTP are disabled.
Recovery plan

If locked out, use console or out-of-band access to remove the ACL or restore a backed-up configuration, then reapply controls incrementally.
