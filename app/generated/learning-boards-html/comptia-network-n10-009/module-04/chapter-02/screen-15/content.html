Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › What to Remember
SSH & HTTPS
Always choose encrypted protocols for management to protect credentials and control traffic integrity.
ACLs for management
Use ACLs to allow only trusted management hosts; verify rule order and implicit deny effects.
Firmware & backups
Backup config, test firmware updates in lab, schedule maintenance, and validate device health after updates.
Short summary: protect credentials with encryption, limit access with ACLs, and keep device software current with safe procedures.
