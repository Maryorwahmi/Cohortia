Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Apply an ACL to VTY Lines
Commands (example)
Router(config)# access-list 1 permit 192.168.10.50
Router(config)# access-list 1 deny any
Router(config)# line vty 0 4
Router(config-line)# access-class 1 in
Router(config-line)# exit
Router(config)# end
Router# write memory
This example limits SSH to the single management workstation at 192.168.10.50.
Practical caution
  • Verify the permit includes your current management IP or have console access available.
  • Place permit rules before deny rules to avoid blocking needed traffic.
  • Test from allowed and denied IPs to confirm behavior.
Common Mistake

Applying an ACL without verifying your management IP can lock you out. Always test before saving permanent changes.
