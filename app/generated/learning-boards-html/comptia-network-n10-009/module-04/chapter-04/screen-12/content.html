Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › SSL/TLS VPNs
Clientless Convenience
Access internal web applications through a browser without installing a VPN client. Good for ad-hoc remote access.
Strengths
Ease of use and broad compatibility with browsers.
Considerations
Require MFA, strict authorization, and monitor sessions closely.
Risks & Mitigations
  • Vulnerability from single-factor authentication — enforce MFA.
  • Split tunneling can bypass protections — consider tunnel-all when accessing sensitive systems.
  • Session timeouts and logging reduce exposure from unattended sessions.
Key Takeaway
Use SSL/TLS VPNs for flexibility, but pair them with strong authentication, least-privilege authorization, and monitoring to keep risk low.
