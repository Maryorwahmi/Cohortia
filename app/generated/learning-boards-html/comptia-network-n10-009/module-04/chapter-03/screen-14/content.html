What to Remember
Use AES-based encryption
WPA2 (AES) or WPA3 provides practical confidentiality; decommission WEP and TKIP.
Prefer Enterprise authentication
802.1X with RADIUS gives per-user keys and easier revocation than shared PSKs.
Disable risky features
Turn off WPS, change defaults, and apply firmware updates to reduce attack surface.
Summary takeaway
Protocol strength plus operational hygiene are both required to secure wireless networks effectively.
