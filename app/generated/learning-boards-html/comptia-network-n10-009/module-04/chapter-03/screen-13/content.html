Assessment › Common scenarios
Scenario 1: Legacy WEP
Question: What is the primary risk and the recommended minimum upgrade?
Answer: WEP's cryptographic weaknesses allow key recovery; upgrade to WPA2-PSK with a strong passphrase or WPA2-Enterprise for per-user authentication.
Scenario 2: Evil Twin
Question: What is the attacker's goal?
Answer: Trick users into connecting to a malicious AP that mimics a legitimate SSID so traffic and credentials can be intercepted or manipulated.
Scenario 3: WPS enabled
Question: Why is WPS risky?
Answer: WPS PIN can be brute-forced to obtain the PSK; disable WPS and use secure onboarding methods.
