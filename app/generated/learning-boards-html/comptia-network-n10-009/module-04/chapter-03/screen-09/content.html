Core Concept › Rogue APs & Evil Twins
Rogue Access Point
An unauthorized AP connected to the network, often unintentionally introduced by staff.
Mitigation: disable unused switch ports, use NAC and 802.1X, monitor wired-to-wireless mappings.
Evil Twin
A malicious AP impersonating a legitimate SSID to capture client connections and intercept traffic.
Concrete example
An attacker sets up an AP named "CorpGuest" with strong signal near the building entrance. Employees who previously used that SSID may connect automatically and expose credentials. If the network used PSK, the attacker can also capture and replay traffic more easily.
Defensive checklist
  • • Enforce 802.1X to avoid shared keys.
  • • Monitor for duplicate SSIDs or unexpected BSSIDs.
  • • Physically secure ports and audit APs on the wired network.
