Quick Reference
Use AES
WPA2/WPA3 with AES is the baseline for confidentiality.
Choose Enterprise when needed
802.1X + RADIUS gives per-user keys and easier revocation.
Harden operationally
Disable WPS, update firmware, change defaults, and monitor for rogue APs.
Final takeaway
Strong protocols plus disciplined operations secure wireless networks effectively; treat them both as required investments.
