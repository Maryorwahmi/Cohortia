Definition › WPS & MAC Filtering
WPS risk
WPS PIN can be brute-forced to reveal the PSK; disable WPS unless a secure alternative is used.
Action
Turn off WPS in AP settings and use secure provisioning procedures for devices.
MAC filtering trade-off
MAC filtering may deter casual guests but is bypassable by address spoofing and should not be relied upon.
Example
An attacker scanning a network can learn an allowed MAC and configure a device to match, so MAC filtering only raises the bar slightly.
