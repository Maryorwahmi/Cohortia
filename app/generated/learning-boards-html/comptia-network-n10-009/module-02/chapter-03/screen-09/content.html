Definition

Single-mode and Multi-mode

Single-mode (SMF)
Very small core, single light path, supports long distances and high bandwidth for WAN links.
Multi-mode (MMF)
Larger core, multiple light paths, suitable for shorter distances within buildings.
Safety note
Never look into a live fiber or touch the ferrule; use dust caps and cleaning kits.
Connectors and example uses
Common connectors
SC, LC, ST — ensure equipment and patch cords match.
Practical example
Use single-mode fiber between buildings for a 2‑km link; use multi‑mode between floors within a campus.
Field considerations
Expect higher cost, fragility, and specialized termination for fiber compared to copper.
Key Takeaway

Use fiber when distance and bandwidth needs exceed copper; follow safety rules and use trained termination techniques.
