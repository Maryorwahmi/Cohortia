Comparison

Straight-through and crossover explained

Straight-through
Same wiring standard on both ends. Use between dissimilar devices: PC↔Switch, Router↔Switch.
Crossover
One end T568A, other end T568B; swaps transmit/receive pairs for similar-device links.
Misconception
Today, crossover cables are rarely needed because most modern devices support Auto MDI‑X.
Practical scenario
Example
Connecting two older switches directly without Auto MDI‑X requires a crossover cable so transmit pins map to receive pins across the link.
Field tip
Keep a small supply of both straight and crossover patch cables and a cable tester in your toolkit for legacy troubleshooting.
Key Takeaway

Most modern gear negates the need for crossovers, but understand the mapping and test cables when connecting older devices.
