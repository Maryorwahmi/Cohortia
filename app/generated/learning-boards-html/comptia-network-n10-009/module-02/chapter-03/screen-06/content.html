Definition

Recognize RJ‑45, SC/LC, F‑type and BNC

RJ‑45
8-position connector for twisted-pair Ethernet used with Cat cabling and patch panels.
Fiber (SC, LC, ST)
Different form factors; LC is common on modern gear, SC offers robust latching, ST uses bayonet-style coupling.
F‑type
Screwed connector often seen on coax for television and cable internet.
BNC
Bayonet connector historically used for coaxial network segments and test equipment.
Safety & care
Never touch fiber ferrules, keep connectors clean, and cap unused fiber ends.
Practical example
A modem coming from an ISP may use coax with an F‑type; internal LAN uses RJ‑45 patching to a switch.
