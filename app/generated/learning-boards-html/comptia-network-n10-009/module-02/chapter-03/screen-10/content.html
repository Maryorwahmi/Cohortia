Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Coaxial Cable
Definition

Coax: central conductor, dielectric, shield, jacket

Its shielded construction makes it appropriate for radio frequency and cable television signals.

RG‑6 vs RG‑59
RG‑6 is thicker and suited for modern cable modem and TV; RG‑59 is older and used for short video runs.
Connectors
F‑type common for consumer cable; BNC used in specific broadcast and test setups.
Use case & example
Practical example
An ISP delivers internet over coax using F‑type to a home modem; the modem provides Ethernet to the home router.
Misconception
Coax is not obsolete—it's still widely used for consumer broadband and video distribution.
Field tip
Ensure correct impedance and tight connectors to reduce reflections and signal loss on long runs.
Key Takeaway

Match coax type and connector to the service. Tight, matched fittings preserve signal quality and prevent service issues.
