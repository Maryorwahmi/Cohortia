How media and devices connect
Twisted-pair cables connect devices to switches via RJ‑45. Switches forward frames using MAC addresses.
Patch panels provide a flexible termination point; patch cords link panel ports to switch ports.
Fiber spans between buildings use SMF for long distance; connectors differ from RJ‑45.
Key Takeaway

Choose the right cable and connector for distance, speed, and environment, and follow a consistent wiring standard to avoid link problems.
