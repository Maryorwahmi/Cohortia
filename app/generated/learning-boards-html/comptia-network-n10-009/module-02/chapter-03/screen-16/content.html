What to Remember

Field Checklist
Preserve twists
Untwisting increases crosstalk. Keep twists close to the connector when terminating.
Label both ends
Consistent labeling and documentation dramatically reduce troubleshooting time.
Test every cable
A cable tester validates continuity and pair mapping; don’t skip this final step.
A short checklist—preserve twists, label runs, and test—will prevent most physical layer issues and save time on site.
