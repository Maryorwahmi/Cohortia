Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Termination Workflow
Process Flow

Prepare → Terminate → Crimp → Test

1. Choose standard
T568A or T568B—decide and document for consistency across the site.
2. Prepare cable
Strip jacket minimally, maintain twists, align wires in correct order.
3. Crimp
Insert fully into RJ‑45 and crimp; wire lengths must be even and reach contacts.
4. Test
Use a tester to verify continuity, pair mapping, and absence of opens or shorts.
Example & troubleshooting
Example
After crimping, the cable tester reports pair 1 and 2 reversed. Recut and re‑order to the chosen pinout, crimp and retest until all pairs are correct.
Common mistake
Not testing immediately and deploying many unverified cables is a frequent source of network outages.
Pro tip
Label both ends after a successful test and record the cable ID in a patching spreadsheet for future troubleshooting.
Key Takeaway

A small, repeatable workflow plus testing prevents most physical layer faults before they reach production systems.
