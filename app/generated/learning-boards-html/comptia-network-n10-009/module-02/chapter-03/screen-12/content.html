Device Spotlight

Routers, patch panels, WAPs, modems, and firewalls

Router
Forwards packets between networks using IP, often provides NAT and sometimes integrated firewall features.
Patch Panel
Passive termination point for permanent runs; use patch cords to connect to switches for flexible changes.
Wireless Access Point
Provides Wi‑Fi access, broadcasts SSIDs, and connects wirelessly-capable clients to the wired LAN.
Modem
Converts between ISP medium and your network; often the demarcation device to the internet.
Firewall
Enforces rules for inbound and outbound traffic; can be hardware or software and protects the network perimeter.
Misconception
A WAP is not a router; it bridges wireless clients to the wired network and typically does not route between networks.
Rack placement example
Permanent runs → Patch panel → Short patch cords → Switch → Router → Modem/ISP. Place firewall at or before the router for edge policy enforcement.
Document port labels and maintain service loops for neatness and airflow; this reduces troubleshooting time.
