Learning Objectives

Skill goals: identify cables, wire correctly, know devices, and practice safe installation.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify Cable Types
Recognize twisted-pair, fiber, and coaxial cables and their connectors.
Mnemonic: Think of three lanes—copper pairs, light core, and shielded coax.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain Wiring Standards
Describe T568A and T568B pinouts and when to use straight or crossover cables.
Mnemonic: A and B swap orange and green pairs—pick one and stay consistent.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe Devices
Explain switches, routers, patch panels, WAPs, modems, and firewalls and their roles.
Mnemonic: Think of a network garage—patch panels as toolboards, switches as lanes, routers as gates.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply Safety & Management
Label cables, control slack, and follow fiber safety to protect people and equipment.
Visual mnemonic: a tidy rack, labeled patch panel and secured cable ties.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
