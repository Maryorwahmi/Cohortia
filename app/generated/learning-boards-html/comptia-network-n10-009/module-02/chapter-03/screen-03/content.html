How you'll practice these skills

Concrete hands-on checks to make wiring and testing routine.

Try these tasks
Inspect physical cables
  • Identify RJ‑45, F‑type, LC/SC connectors by shape and size.
  • Feel for shield braid or metallic sheen to spot STP versus UTP.
Draw and check pinouts
Sketch T568A and T568B orders, then confirm accuracy before crimping.
Use a cable tester
Verify continuity, pair order, and split pairs to avoid intermittent links.
Plan rack placements
Map patch panels and switches, label both ends of patch cords, and leave service loops.
Start small: test one patch cable fully before deploying many.
Label both ends to save troubleshooting time later.
Misconception: a cable that looks good may still have crossed pairs; always test.
