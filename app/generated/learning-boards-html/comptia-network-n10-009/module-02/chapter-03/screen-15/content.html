Key Concepts

Quick reference
Twisted-Pair
Copper pairs twisted to reduce EMI; UTP common, STP for noisy environments.
T568A / T568B
Two RJ‑45 wiring orders. Pick one and be consistent; test crimps.
Fiber Optic
Light-based transmission; single-mode for long distance, multi-mode for shorter runs; follow safety rules.
Keep a tester, labels, and a small inventory of patch cables. Testing and clear documentation are as important as the cable choice itself.
