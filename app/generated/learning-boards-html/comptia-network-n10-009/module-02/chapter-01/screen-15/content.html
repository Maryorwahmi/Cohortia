Key Concepts
Operational terms and management concepts to retain.
NTP
Synchronizes device clocks using a stratum hierarchy for accurate timestamps.
SNMP
Monitoring and management protocol using managers, agents, and MIBs; use SNMPv3 for security.
DORA & MIB
DORA describes DHCP's exchange; MIB is SNMP's hierarchical object database used for queries and control.
Final reflection

When diagnosing a problem, collect one decisive piece of evidence first — the output of ipconfig/ifconfig, an nslookup, or a traceroute — then use the service definitions above to guide your next steps.
