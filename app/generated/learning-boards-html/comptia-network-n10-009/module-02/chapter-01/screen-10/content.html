Network Time Protocol (NTP)
Organize time sources by stratum and keep device clocks aligned for logs and authentication.
Stratum model
Stratum 0: reference clocks. Stratum 1: direct to stratum 0. Stratum 2+: synchronized to higher strata.
Why time matters
Logs must share timestamps for event correlation. Authentication and certificates depend on accurate time to prevent replay or validity errors.
Troubleshooting steps
Verify configured NTP servers, check stratum responses, and confirm network reachability. Adjust drift settings if necessary.
Example: If an authentication server reports a time offset of two minutes relative to clients, Kerberos tickets may fail. Check the server's configured NTP peers and its reported stratum to find the root cause.
