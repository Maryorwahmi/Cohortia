Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Client-Server vs P2P
Client-Server
  • Centralized management and backups.
  • Stronger security and policy enforcement.
  • Requires server hardware and redundancy planning.
Key Takeaway

Use client-server when backups, security, and centralized policies are priorities.

Peer-to-Peer
  • Low cost and easy setup for small networks.
  • No centralized control; backups and security are manual.
  • Not ideal for larger or security-sensitive environments.
Common Mistake

Choosing peer-to-peer for a business that needs centralized backups and policy enforcement leads to management and security problems.
