Network Topologies and Architectures

Understand how physical wiring and logical data flow differ, and how architecture choices affect security and scalability.

In this lesson: identify star, mesh, bus, ring, hybrid; compare client-server and peer-to-peer; design a small office network.
Explore Key Topics
Physical Topologies

How cables and devices are placed. This affects fault isolation and cabling cost.

Logical Flow

How data travels. Logical behavior can differ from the physical wiring layout.

Architectures

Client-server versus peer-to-peer and when to use each pattern.

Topology vs Architecture
Wiring diagram and service model shown as linked cards
Sample: 8 workstations, 2 printers, 1 server — choose star or hybrid for manageability and backups.
Key Takeaway
Topology choices trade cabling and central points for redundancy and cost. Architecture choices trade central control for simplicity. Use the right balance for the environment’s scale and security needs.
