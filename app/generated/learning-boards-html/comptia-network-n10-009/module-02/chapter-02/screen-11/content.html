Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Peer-to-Peer Architecture
Concept

Peer-to-Peer: distributed sharing

Each device can act as client and server. Easy and low-cost for small networks but lacks centralized management and consistent backups.

When to use

Small home or temporary networks where centralized control isn’t necessary.

When to avoid

Environments needing centralized security, backups, or policy enforcement.

Example

Three home computers sharing files and a printer directly without a dedicated server. Simple but requires manual backup of important data.

Operational Risks
Security & Backups

Without centralized servers, enforcing policies and performing regular backups is harder and often manual.

Key Takeaway

Choose peer-to-peer only when simplicity and low cost outweigh the need for centralized control and backups.
