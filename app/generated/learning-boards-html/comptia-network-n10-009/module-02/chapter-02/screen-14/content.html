Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Physical Topology

The cabling and device layout that you can touch and label.

Logical Topology

How data flows and which devices see traffic, independent of wiring.

Topologies (Star, Mesh, Bus, Ring)

Each has distinct cabling, fault tolerance, and cost characteristics to weigh against requirements.

Takeaway
Match topology and architecture to organizational needs: manageability, redundancy, and budget determine the best fit.
Reflect: what is the single point of failure in your current network?
