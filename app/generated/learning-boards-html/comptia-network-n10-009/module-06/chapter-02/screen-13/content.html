Packet sniffing — what to look for

  • Retransmissions and duplicate ACKs indicating packet loss.
  • Unexpected protocols or large flows using bandwidth.
  • Traffic patterns that correlate with performance degradation times.
Keep captures short and focused to reduce sensitive data exposure. Document why you captured and what you found so that fixes are evidence-driven.
Ethics & safety
Only capture with authorization and within policy. Avoid storing or analyzing private user content when not necessary for troubleshooting.
