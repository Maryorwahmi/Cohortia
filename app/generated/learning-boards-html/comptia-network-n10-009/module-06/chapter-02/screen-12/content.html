Slow network performance

Measure bandwidth, latency, and packet loss and compare to a known baseline to find where the bottleneck lies.

Useful indicators
  • High interface utilization: indicates saturated link.
  • High latency and packet loss: could suggest congestion or faulty links.
  • Excessive retransmissions in captures: suggests packet loss or errors on the media.
# Check active connections and listening sockets
C:\> netstat -anob

# Monitor interface counters on a switch or router for errors and utilization
show interface GigabitEthernet0/1
Real-world connection
If peak business hours show slow performance, compare utilization graphs to off-peak periods. If spikes match business processes, plan capacity or traffic shaping; if not, investigate faulty hardware or misbehaving applications.
