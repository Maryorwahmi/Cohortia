Core Concept

No connectivity triage

A stepwise checklist to eliminate physical, link, IP, and gateway causes.

Step 1: Physical checks
Check link lights, swap cables, try another switch port, and confirm NIC is enabled.
Step 2: Interface state
Use ipconfig or ifconfig to confirm an IP address and interface status.
Step 3: Layer 2 checks
Verify VLAN assignment and MAC table entries on the switch.
Step 4: Layer 3 gateway
Ping the default gateway by IP to prove routing beyond the host.
Key Takeaway

Progress from physical to higher layers and collect simple evidence at each step to avoid misguided changes.

Example terminal checks
C:\> ipconfig /all
Ethernet adapter Local Area Connection:
   Connection-specific DNS Suffix  . : example.local
   IPv4 Address. . . . . . . . . . : 169.254.10.15
   Subnet Mask . . . . . . . . . . : 255.255.0.0
   Default Gateway . . . . . . . . :

# A 169.254.x.x address indicates APIPA; the client could not reach DHCP.
Common mistake
Restarting services before verifying link lights often wastes time. Verify physical evidence first.
Pro tip
Label cables and test with a known-good cable when possible to rule out intermittent faults quickly.
