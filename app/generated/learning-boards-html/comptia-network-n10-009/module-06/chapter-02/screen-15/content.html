Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
nslookup / dig
Force queries to specific servers to isolate local DNS problems from upstream issues.
Packet sniffer
Use captures to confirm retransmissions, high latency, and bandwidth patterns when other checks point to network-level causes.
EMI
Consider electromagnetic interference for intermittent faults, and test with a different cable route or shielded cabling.
Final takeaway

Start with simple tests that reveal the layer at fault, gather evidence, then apply targeted fixes. Repeatable diagnosis reduces downtime and avoids unnecessary changes.
