Intermittent connectivity: causes

  • Physical damage: broken pair, loose terminations, or intermittent connector contact.
  • Electromagnetic Interference (EMI): motors, fluorescent lights, or nearby transmitters.
  • Wireless issues: channel overlap, signal fading, or client movement.
  • Duplex mismatch: symptoms worsen under load and cause collisions.
Tool: cable tester
Detect opens, shorts, and split pairs on Ethernet runs.
Tool: Wi‑Fi analyzer
Identify channel congestion and signal strength issues.
Example scenario
Users report short drops every few minutes. The pattern aligns with an automated device nearby switching on. A Wi‑Fi scan shows a burst of traffic on the same channel during drops, confirming interference. Changing the AP channel or moving the interfering device resolves the issue.
Common mistake

Replacing network equipment before verifying environmental interference often results in wasted hardware swaps.
