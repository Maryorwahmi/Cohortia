Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
OSI Model
Use layers to narrow where a problem originates and choose the right diagnostic command.
Approaches
Top-down, bottom-up, and divide-and-conquer guide the order and scope of checks.
Tools
ping, tracert/traceroute, ipconfig/ifconfig, and netstat provide immediate, actionable evidence.
Summary

Focus on evidence, isolate the problem, change one thing at a time, and document results to build faster, safer troubleshooting skills.
