Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › ipconfig / netstat
ipconfig / ifconfig — what to look for
  • IPv4 Address and Subnet Mask: ensure they are in expected range.
  • Default Gateway: necessary for routing beyond local subnet.
  • DNS Servers: critical for name resolution.
netstat — what to inspect

Look for listening ports for expected services and unusual established connections that could indicate misconfiguration or unrelated problems.

Key Takeaway

Capture and save interface and connection outputs before changing settings. These snapshots are vital for comparison and rollback decisions.
