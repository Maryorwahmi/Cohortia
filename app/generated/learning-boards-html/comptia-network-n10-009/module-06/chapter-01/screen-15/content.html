Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Capture evidence
Save ipconfig, ping, traceroute, and netstat outputs before changing settings.
Change one thing
Make a single change, then test and document the outcome before proceeding.
Compare to baseline
Use known-good configs and baselines to spot what changed and why.
Closing takeaway

These habits make troubleshooting faster, safer, and easier for teams to learn from. Keep them visible on your next incident.
