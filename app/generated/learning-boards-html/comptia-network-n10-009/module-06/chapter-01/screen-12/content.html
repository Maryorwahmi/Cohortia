Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Common Mistakes
Mistake vs Fix
No baseline
Capture normal state metrics and configs
Multiple changes at once
Change one item, then test and document
Assuming symptom is root cause
Form hypothesis and verify with evidence
Clarifying questions
  • What exactly isn't working, and for whom?
  • When did it start, and what changed recently?
  • Is the problem reproducible and limited to a segment?
Key Takeaway

A disciplined process avoids guesswork. Document and verify each step so your fixes are reliable and reversible.
