Audits and Scans
Combine automated tools with human review and tracked remediation.
Cadence examples
  • Vulnerability scans: weekly or monthly, depending on risk.
  • Configuration audits: after changes and quarterly for critical devices.
  • Restore tests: at least annually, or with major changes.
Remediation workflow
  1. Scan or audit and create findings.
  2. Assign owner and priority, schedule changes with backups.
  3. Apply fixes, verify, and close the ticket with evidence.
Misconception

Running tools without a remediation process wastes effort. Track and verify fixes to make audits meaningful.

Key Takeaway

An audit program with scheduled scans, remediation tracking, and restore tests sustains hardening over time.
