Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Common Misconfigurations
Frequent misconfigurations
Small oversights often enable large breaches.
Examples
  • Default administrator passwords still active.
  • Weak SNMP community strings or SNMPv1/v2 instead of SNMPv3.
  • Overly permissive firewall or ACL rules.
  • No segmentation — single flat network for users and devices.
Remediation steps
  1. Change defaults immediately and enforce password policies.
  2. Upgrade management protocols and secure SNMP with authentication and encryption.
  3. Harden firewall rules and map services to owners.
  4. Implement segmentation and baseline configurations, then audit regularly.
Misconception

Believing only exotic attacks are dangerous misses the reality: attackers rely on default credentials and simple misconfigurations more than zero-day exploits.

Key Takeaway

Fix defaults first, then tighten configurations and establish auditing to prevent regression.
