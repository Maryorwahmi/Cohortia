Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Network Segmentation
Why segment?

Segmentation reduces lateral movement and limits the impact of a compromise.

Example design

Guest Wi‑Fi VLAN, employee workstation VLAN, server VLAN, management VLAN. Use ACLs and firewalls to control permitted traffic between these zones.

Key Takeaway

Segmentation confines breaches and simplifies incident response by reducing exposure.

Compare flat vs segmented
Flat network

All devices are on the same network. An attacker who compromises one device can often reach others directly.

Segmented network

Devices are separated by VLANs or subnets with controlled flows. Breach impact is limited and containment is simpler.

Misconception

Segmentation must be planned; adding segments without rule control can create operational complexity. Balance security with manageability.
