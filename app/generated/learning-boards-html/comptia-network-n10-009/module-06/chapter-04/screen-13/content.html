Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Environmental Controls
Environmental Controls
Systems that prevent physical causes of outages and equipment damage.
HVAC

Provides cooling and ventilation to avoid overheating and thermal failure.

UPS & Generators

Maintain power during outages and prevent data corruption from abrupt shutdowns.

Fire Suppression

Protects equipment from fire; follow systems that minimize collateral damage to electronics.

Key Takeaway

Environmental systems are part of security; include testing and maintenance in your hardening schedule.

Safety note: When implementing physical controls, always leave emergency exits unobstructed and follow local fire codes. Test UPS and suppression systems on a schedule and record results.
