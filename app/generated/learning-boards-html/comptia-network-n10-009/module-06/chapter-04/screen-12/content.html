Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Disable Unnecessary Services
Close the doors you don't need
Audit services and remove anything not required for the device's role.
Checklist
  • Run a port scan to list listeners.
  • Map each port to a necessary service and owner.
  • Disable or uninstall unneeded services and update firewall rules.
Common Mistake

Assuming a Windows service is stopped is enough; verify no alternate processes are listening on that port.

Example

A switch used only for forwarding may not need a management protocol enabled on the network interface. Disable network management protocols on data-facing ports and restrict management to a dedicated VLAN.

Verification

After disabling a service, rescan the host and confirm the port no longer listens. Update documentation and inform owners of the change.

Key Takeaway

Treat each open port as an actionable item: verify necessity, disable if not needed, and document decisions.
