Network Hardening and Security Best Practices

Secure devices and infrastructure by reducing attack surface, enforcing access controls, and ensuring resilient operations.

In this lesson
Patch, Backup, Protect
Patch Management

Schedule, test, and apply updates to firmware and OS to close known vulnerabilities.

Access Control

Combine physical locks, MFA, strong passwords, and least privilege for robust access restrictions.

Segmentation

Use VLANs and subnets to contain breaches and reduce lateral movement.

Environmental Controls

Power, cooling, and fire suppression keep equipment running and safe from damage.

Key Takeaway

Hardening combines software and hardware measures plus process controls; do all four: patch, backup, restrict, and protect environment.
