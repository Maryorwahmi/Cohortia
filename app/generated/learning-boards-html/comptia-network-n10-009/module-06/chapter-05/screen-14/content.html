Core Concepts
Three concise reminders
RTO & RPO

RTO limits downtime; RPO limits acceptable data loss. They drive recovery choices.

Backup Types

Full, incremental, and differential each trade backup time, storage, and restore complexity differently.

Redundancy & HA

Redundant hardware, links, and devices plus load balancing and clustering enable high availability.

Takeaway

Design choices must align to RTO/RPO and be proven through documented, repeatable tests.
