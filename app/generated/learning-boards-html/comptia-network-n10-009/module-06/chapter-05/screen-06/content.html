Comparison

LACP vs Independent Links

LACP provides a bundled logical link with failover and bandwidth aggregation; independent links to different devices or ISPs provide path diversity for higher resilience.

LACP (link aggregation)
Bundles multiple physical Ethernet links into one logical channel for bandwidth and failover within the aggregation scope.
Independent paths
Use different switches, fiber conduits, or ISPs to avoid a single physical or provider point of failure.
Misconception
Putting all aggregated links into the same switch or conduit removes the benefit of redundancy; diversify physical routes when possible.
Concrete examples
Server with LACP
Two NICs in an LACP group to a switch. If one NIC or cable fails, traffic moves to the remaining link without session loss.
Dual ISP setup
Primary and secondary ISP uplinks with routing failover provide internet resilience when one provider has an outage.
Check your understanding

If your datacenter has a single fiber conduit, what hidden risk remains even with LACP? (Answer: the conduit is a single physical path.)
