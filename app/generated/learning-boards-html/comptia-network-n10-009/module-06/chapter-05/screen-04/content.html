Core Concept

Business Continuity vs Disaster Recovery

BCP focuses on sustaining operations across people and processes; DR focuses on restoring IT systems and infrastructure. Both are required for true resilience.

BCP example
Redirect order processing to an alternate site and use manual order entry until systems return.
DR example
Restore the e-commerce database from recent backups or replicated replicas to bring the website back online.
Key Takeaway

BCP preserves essential business function; DR restores IT. Document both and test them under realistic scenarios.

Analogy & Checklist

Analogy: Fire drill vs rebuilding

Think of BCP as the fire drill and temporary workarounds that keep people safe and productive. DR is rebuilding the damaged building so normal operations can resume.

Essential checklist
  • Identify critical business functions and dependencies.
  • Document recovery owners, steps, and communication plans.
  • Define RTO and RPO for each critical system.
  • Schedule periodic drills and test restores.
Common mistake
Relying solely on backups without addressing people, alternate facilities, or supplier dependencies.
