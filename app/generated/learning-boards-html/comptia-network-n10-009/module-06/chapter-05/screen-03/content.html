Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Objectives

What you'll practice

Translate reliability targets and business priorities into redundancy, backup cadence, and test plans. Learn how to justify the cost of higher availability with clear RTO and RPO trade-offs.

Fault Tolerance
Maintain service with multiple redundant components and automatic failover.
Load Balancing
Distribute workloads to prevent overload and enable seamless failure handling.
Clustering
Group servers to share state and take over services when a peer fails.
Assessment tip

When designing, always document assumptions: expected failure modes, RTO/RPO values, restoration steps, and who owns each task during an incident.
