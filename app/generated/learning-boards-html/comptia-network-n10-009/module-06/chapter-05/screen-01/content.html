Disaster Recovery & High Availability Concepts

Understand why planning for outages is essential, how redundancy and backups reduce risk, and how RTO/RPO shape practical recovery choices. This chapter prepares you to design resilient systems that balance cost and continuity.

A compact view of key tactics that keep services available and recoverable.
Redundancy

Backups
