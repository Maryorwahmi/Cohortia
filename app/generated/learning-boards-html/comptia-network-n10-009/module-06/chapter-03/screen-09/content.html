Process Flow

QoS Stages

Classification
Identify traffic for special treatment.
Marking
Tag packets with DSCP or other priority markers.
Queuing
Place packets into priority queues for transmission order.
Policing
Drop or remark traffic exceeding defined rates.
Shaping
Smooth bursts by buffering to conform to a rate.
Concrete Example
Prioritize VoIP by classifying voice RTP streams, marking with high DSCP, and placing them in a low-latency queue so voice quality remains steady during backups.
Misconception
QoS does not add bandwidth; it ensures available bandwidth is allocated according to policy when contention occurs.
Key Takeaway

Design classification and marking carefully; misclassification can cause poor outcomes for critical apps.
