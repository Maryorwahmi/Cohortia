Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
Network Monitoring
Continuous observation and analysis of devices and traffic to ensure availability and performance.
Baselining
Establish normal ranges for metrics so you can identify sustained deviations objectively.
Key Metrics
Latency, throughput, utilization, packet loss, and jitter — interpret them together to understand impact.
Summary: Combine counters, logs, and flow records; tune alerts based on baselines; and make sure monitoring outputs lead to action.
