Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
SNMP
Polls and traps that provide device counters for baselining and alerting.
Syslog
Centralized event messages for auditing and incident context.
NetFlow / sFlow
Flow metadata that shows who is talking to whom and how much bandwidth they consume.
Keep protocols and QoS rules consistent across the path, and always correlate multiple sources when diagnosing performance issues.
