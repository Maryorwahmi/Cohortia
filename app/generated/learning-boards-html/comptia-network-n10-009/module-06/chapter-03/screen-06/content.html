Architecture

SNMP Workflow

Agent
Runs on devices and exposes counters from MIBs.
Manager
Polls agents, ingests counters, and handles traps.
Database
Stores time-series data for baselining and trend analysis.
Example Scenario
An SNMP poll shows rising interface errors; a matching Syslog entry shows frequent link flaps. Together they point to physical problems rather than congestion.
MIBs & Traps
MIBs are structured lists of objects you can query. Traps are unsolicited alerts devices send when thresholds or events occur.
Misconception
SNMP is not a packet-level capture tool; it provides counters and state for device health and trends.
Pro Tip
Poll at intervals suitable for trend detection; avoid polling too frequently or you overload the manager and devices.
Key Takeaway

Use SNMP for device health metrics and alerts; combine with logs and flows for full visibility.
