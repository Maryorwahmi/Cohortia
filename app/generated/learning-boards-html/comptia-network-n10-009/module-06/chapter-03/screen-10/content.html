Before vs After QoS

Before
High utilization causes latency, jitter, and packet loss for voice during backups.
After
Voice maintains low latency and stable jitter while bulk transfers are deprioritized or shaped.
Checklist
  • Identify heavy flows with NetFlow/sFlow.
  • Confirm link utilization with SNMP counters.
  • Classify and mark voice traffic with DSCP.
  • Apply queuing and shaping policies across key devices.
  • Validate with test calls and metric correlation.
Key Takeaway

Use monitoring data to drive QoS choices and verify results with the same metrics you used to diagnose the problem.
