Directed forwarding vs flooding

Directed forwarding occurs when destination MAC is found in the MAC table. The frame is forwarded only out of that port.
Flooding happens when the destination MAC is unknown. The switch sends the frame to all ports except the ingress port to find the destination device.
Unknown unicast flooding is distinct from a broadcast, which intentionally addresses all devices.
Example walk
Switch starts empty. Host1 sends to Host2. Switch learns Host1's MAC and floods to find Host2. Host2 replies; switch learns Host2's MAC. Later unicast traffic goes directly to Host2's port.
Key takeaway: Flooding is temporary and enables learning so direct forwarding can follow.
