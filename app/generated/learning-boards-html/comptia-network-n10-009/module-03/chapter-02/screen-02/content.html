Learning Objectives

Skills you'll practise and verify in this chapter.

01
Explain
Purpose & benefits of VLANs

Describe how VLANs reduce broadcast scope, aid security, and improve management. You will see a company example to ground this skill.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand
How VLANs segment broadcast domains

Explain broadcast behavior on switches and why isolating departments into VLANs prevents excess broadcast traffic and collisions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply
Configure VLANs & assign ports

Follow the simulated switch commands to create VLANs, name them, and place access ports into the correct VLANs for end devices.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Master
802.1Q trunking & STP basics

Understand how trunk links tag frames with VLAN IDs and how STP blocks redundant ports to prevent broadcast storms and recover from failures.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
