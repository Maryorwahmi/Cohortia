Key Concepts

Three compact reminders from the chapter.

VLAN
Logical grouping that segments a broadcast domain and isolates traffic.
802.1Q Trunk
Tags frames with VLAN IDs so multiple VLANs can share one link between switches.
STP
Prevents Layer 2 loops by electing a root bridge and blocking redundant ports until needed.
Summary takeaway
VLANs organize, trunks carry, and STP stabilizes — together these mechanisms let you build segmented, resilient Layer 2 networks.
