What to Remember

Three concise checklist items to use in the lab and on the job.

Group logically
Design VLANs for clear operational groups so policies and broadcast domains match organizational needs.
Align trunks
Ensure trunk modes, allowed VLANs, and native VLANs match on both ends of the link.
Enable STP
Use STP to prevent loops and know how to check port roles and BPDUs when troubleshooting.
Final takeaway
A repeatable checklist and verification habit prevents common VLAN and STP misconfigurations.
