Recovery

STP convergence and redundancy

If an active link fails, STP recalculates the tree and can move a blocked port to forwarding to restore the path.

Failure handling
BPDUs detect topology change and switches recompute roles. A previously blocked port can become forwarding when needed.
Performance note
Classic STP reconverges in tens of seconds; for faster recovery consider STP enhancements where appropriate.
Design questions
  • Is the reconvergence time acceptable for your applications?
  • Do you need faster protocols for critical links?
Key Takeaway

STP provides loop-free redundancy but classic STP may require tens of seconds to reconverge after a topology change.
