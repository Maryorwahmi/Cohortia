Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Routing Protocols at a Glance
RIP
Distance-vector, hop count metric, limited to 15 hops, simple and legacy — suitable only for small networks.
OSPF
Link-state protocol, computes shortest paths using link cost, scalable and commonly used inside large enterprise networks.
EIGRP
A hybrid protocol combining distance-vector and link-state principles, known for fast convergence and efficient updates.
BGP
Exterior gateway protocol used between autonomous systems; focuses on policy, path attributes, and internet-scale routing.
Practical guidance

Select RIP only for tiny labs or legacy appliances; use OSPF or EIGRP for interior routing at scale; use BGP for inter-AS policy and internet routing.
