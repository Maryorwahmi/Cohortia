Watch out

Three frequent mistakes

  • Not subtracting network and broadcast: always do 2^(host bits) - 2 for usable hosts.
  • Borrowing wrong number of bits: test 2^n vs required subnets to avoid under- or over-splitting.
  • Wrong block size calculation: compute 256 - mask octet or 2^(host bits in octet) to find increments.
Example
For /27, last octet host bits = 5, block size = 2^5 = 32. Subnets at .0, .32, .64, .96, etc. Verify by counting hosts and broadcasts each step.
Pro tip
When in doubt, convert to binary for the critical octet. Binary makes borrowing and block size obvious and prevents overlaps.
Check your work
List the first three subnets after your calculation and ensure their ranges are contiguous and non-overlapping.
