Key Concepts
fabric-samples repository
The official sandbox of examples, scripts, and configurations for learning and testing Fabric.
--branch flag
Pin to a release branch to ensure script and binary compatibility for reproducible labs.
test-network & chaincode
Use test-network to bring up a simple local network and chaincode folders to find deployable examples in your language.
Takeaway

Follow branch pinning, read scripts, and verify binaries to make your Fabric labs predictable and repeatable.
