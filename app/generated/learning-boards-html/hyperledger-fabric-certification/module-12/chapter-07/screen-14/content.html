Key Concepts What to Remember
Suitability Criteria
Ask the five questions: multi-party, immutability, privacy, smart contracts, and governance.
Permissioned vs Permissionless
Fabric is permissioned: identity-driven privacy and predictable performance for consortia.
Total Cost of Ownership
Include infrastructure, specialist development, operations, and governance when estimating ROI.
Summary
Use these concepts as a quick filter during architecture reviews and to guide the artifacts you present to decision makers.
