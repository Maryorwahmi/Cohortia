Architecture Scalability & Performance
Key metrics
  • Transaction Throughput (TPS) — depends on endorsement and chaincode.
  • Latency — time to finality often in low seconds.
  • Storage — ledger growth and snapshots must be planned.
State DB trade-off
LevelDB: fast key-value lookups. CouchDB: rich queries but needs indexing strategy to avoid bottlenecks.
Practical steps
  • Benchmark with realistic endorsement policies and chaincode complexity.
  • Plan CouchDB indexes for any expected rich queries.
  • Design node topology and channel layout to balance isolation and replication.
Key Takeaway

Performance depends on chaincode, endorsement and DB choice; measure with representative workloads and plan indexing and storage growth accordingly.
