Comparison Fabric, Corda, Quorum, Databases
Fabric
Modular, channels and PDCs for privacy, pluggable consensus, strong for diversified consortia.
Corda
Point-to-point transaction model ideal for finance where global ledger replication is not desired.
Quorum
Ethereum-based with privacy features; useful when EVM compatibility matters.
Traditional Databases
Fast, cost-effective within a single trust domain. Avoid distributed ledger overhead unless multi-party trust and immutability are required.
When to pick a DB
Single org, high throughput, low coordination
When to pick Fabric
Consortium, privacy partitions, verifiable audit trails
