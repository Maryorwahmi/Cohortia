Compliance Legal Considerations
Data Residency
Define where peers and off-chain stores reside and ensure locations comply with local regulations.
Right to be Forgotten
Keep PII off-chain with ledger hashes. Delete off-chain PII to satisfy erasure requests and record the deletion event on chain.
Jurisdiction & Governance
Document which laws apply, how disputes are arbitrated, and the legal effect of chaincode outputs in consortium agreements.
Example mitigation
Store customer PII encrypted in region-specific storage. Ledger stores only a hash and an access log. Deletion removes the off-chain record and logs the event on the ledger.
Key Takeaway

Legal rules shape architecture choices. Capture residency, deletion, and jurisdiction in consortium agreements before launch.
