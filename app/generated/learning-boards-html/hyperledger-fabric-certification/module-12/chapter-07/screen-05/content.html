Process Flow Stakeholder Mapping
Decision Steps
  1. List all stakeholders and their legal domains.
  2. For each data item, note required visibility and control.
  3. Identify whether immutability and auditable access are required.
  4. Estimate the governance model and legal agreement needed.
Scenario examples
Scenario A: Internal asset tracking — single legal entity, database likely best. Scenario B: Cross-bank KYC — multiple banks, Fabric fits.
Common Mistake
Treating any trust issue as blockchain-ready. If parties already trust a central operator or can use federated access to a database, blockchain adds complexity without clear benefit.
Key Takeaway

If independent organizations with different incentives need shared, auditable state, Fabric’s consortium model is often preferable to a central database.
