Comparison Fabric vs Public Chains
Fabric (Permissioned)
  • Identity management with certificates.
  • Private channels and PDCs for selective data sharing.
  • Predictable performance and no native gas fees.
Public Blockchains (Permissionless)
  • Open participation and strong decentralization.
  • Higher latency, variable fees, and limited built-in privacy.
  • Resilience through broad miner/validator distribution.
Practical trade-offs
If your consortium needs controlled participation, privacy, and predictable costs, Fabric is typically better. If your system requires censorship resistance and open access, consider a public chain.
Key Takeaway

Choose Fabric for controlled, private, high-throughput enterprise use cases; choose public chains when open participation and censorship resistance are priority goals.
