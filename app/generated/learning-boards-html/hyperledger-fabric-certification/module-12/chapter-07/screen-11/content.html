Process Flow TCO Breakdown
Infrastructure
  • Nodes: peers, orderers, CAs
  • State DB: CouchDB/LevelDB
  • Orchestration, backups, network
Development
  • Chaincode and client apps
  • Integration with existing systems
  • Testing and benchmarks
Operations & Governance
  • Monitoring, upgrades, security
  • Consortium legal and admin costs
  • Support contracts or managed services
Practical estimation tip
Annualize one-time setup and ongoing operating costs. Personnel for operation and governance is often the largest recurring expense — surface this early in procurement discussions.
Key Takeaway

TCO is broader than infrastructure. Include development, operations, and governance to avoid underestimating long-term costs.
