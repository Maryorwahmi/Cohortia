HSM and KMS
Use hardware and managed key services for critical key custody and encryption keys.
Logging & SIEM
Centralize, protect, and alert on security-relevant Fabric events for rapid detection.
Routine testing
Periodically test certificate renewals, revocations, endorsement changes, and PDC access controls in staging.
Final thought: security is continuous. Make small, repeatable operational processes—automation, monitoring, and testing—to keep your Fabric network resilient.
