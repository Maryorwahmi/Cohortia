Auditing and Logging
Collect security-relevant events and centralize them for detection and response.
What to log
Identity enrollment/revocation, transaction submission and rejection, access attempts, and key operations.
Integration
Forward logs to a SIEM for correlation and real-time alerting. Include HSM/KMS audit trails where possible.
Common mistake
Keeping logs only locally makes them vulnerable to tampering during an incident and hinders forensic analysis.
Detection examples
  • Alert on repeated failed sign-in or enrollment attempts.
  • Alert on unexpected revocations or mass key operations.
  • Monitor for unusual endorsement patterns indicating policy misuse.
Key Takeaway

Protect and centralize logs; they are crucial evidence for detecting incidents and supporting response actions.
