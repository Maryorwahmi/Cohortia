Common Security Pitfalls
Identify and remediate frequent operational mistakes that cause incidents.
Expired certificates
Plan automated renewals and expiry alerts to avoid sudden outages when TLS or enrollment certs lapse.
Shared CA keys
Sharing CA private keys increases compromise impact. Keep keys per organization and use HSMs for critical keys.
Weak endorsement policies
Overly permissive policies allow unauthorized commits; align policies with governance and test changes.
Remediation checklist
  • Automate cert renewals and monitor expiry.
  • Use HSMs for CA and ordering keys.
  • Centralize and protect logs; review them regularly.
Warning

Operational neglect, not platform limits, causes most security incidents. Operational discipline is critical.
