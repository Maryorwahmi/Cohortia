Approve and Commit with a Signature Policy
Follow lifecycle steps and increment sequence when changing policies.
Key commands (example)
peer lifecycle chaincode approveformyorg -o orderer.example.com:7050 --channelID mychannel --name fabcar --version 1.1 --package-id <FABCAR_1.1_PACKAGE_ID> --sequence 2 --signaturePolicy "AND('Org1MSP.peer','Org2MSP.peer')" --tls --cafile $ORDERER_CA
peer lifecycle chaincode commitchanneldefinition -o orderer.example.com:7050 --channelID mychannel --name fabcar --version 1.1 --sequence 2 --signaturePolicy "AND('Org1MSP.peer','Org2MSP.peer')" --tls --cafile $ORDERER_CA --peerAddresses peer0.org1.example.com:7051 --tlsRootCertFiles $PEER0_ORG1_CA --peerAddresses peer0.org2.example.com:9051 --tlsRootCertFiles $PEER0_ORG2_CA
Notes
The signaturePolicy is part of the chaincode definition, not the chaincode source. Increment sequence when you change the definition.
Key Takeaway

Use lifecycle commands with an explicit signaturePolicy and validate behavior after commit.
