Endorsement Policies
Explicit policies ensure only agreed signers can endorse transactions.
Explicit (Recommended)
Use AND, OR, or OutOf constructs like AND('Org1MSP.peer','Org2MSP.peer') or OutOf(2, 'Org1MSP.peer', 'Org2MSP.peer', 'Org3MSP.peer').
Implicit (Risky)
Policies such as ANY allow any single org to endorse, which may not meet consortium governance needs.
Concrete test
After changing to AND('Org1MSP.peer','Org2MSP.peer'), try endorsing with only Org1; it should fail. Endorsing with both should succeed.
Policy checklist
  • Map policy to business process and trust model.
  • Avoid overly permissive ANY policies for sensitive ops.
  • Plan policy changes and test them in staging.
Key Takeaway

Endorsement policies are governance controls: encode who must agree and test any changes carefully.
