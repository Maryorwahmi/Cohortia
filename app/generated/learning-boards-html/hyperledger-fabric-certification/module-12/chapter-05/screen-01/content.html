Security Best Practices in Hyperledger Fabric
Design identity, transport, endorsement, and data protections that match your consortium's trust model.
In this lesson
Identity & MSPs, TLS/mTLS, endorsement policies, private data strategies, HSMs, and auditing.
Why it matters
A single compromised private key or expired certificate can halt a production network or leak sensitive ledger data.
Outcome
You will be able to recommend and implement practical controls for a secure Fabric deployment.
Explore key topics
Identity & CAs
Run org CAs, protect CA keys, and use MSP attributes for access control.
Endorsement Policies
Define explicit AND or majority policies to match your business trust model.
Private Data
Use PDCs and off-chain encryption to keep sensitive values private while keeping audit hashes on-ledger.
