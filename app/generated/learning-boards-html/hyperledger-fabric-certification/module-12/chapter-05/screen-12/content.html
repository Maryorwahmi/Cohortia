PDC vs Off-chain Encryption
Compare privacy patterns to choose the appropriate approach for your data.
Private Data Collection
Private data shared among collection members; ledger stores only the hash. Good for peer-to-peer private workflows inside Fabric.
Off-chain Encryption
Encrypted blobs stored externally; ledger holds hash and a reference. Good when deletion or stronger separation is required.
Decision factors
Consider compliance, need for deletion, recovery, and operational overhead when selecting a pattern.
Concrete guidance
  • Choose PDC for Fabric-native private sharing between known orgs.
  • Choose off-chain encryption for regulated data or when deletion may be needed.
  • Protect local private state and KMS keys regardless of approach.
Key Takeaway

Neither approach removes the need for strong key and host security; choose based on privacy, compliance, and operations.
