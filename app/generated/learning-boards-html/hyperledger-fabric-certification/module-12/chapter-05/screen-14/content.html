Lab: Strengthen the fabcar Endorsement Policy
Follow the lifecycle approval and commit steps, then validate endorsements fail or succeed as expected.
Steps (summary)
  1. Deploy fabcar with initial policy and observe single-org success.
  2. Package new version, increment sequence, approve for Org1 and Org2 with AND policy.
  3. Commit the new definition and test single-org endorsement fails, dual-org succeeds.
Validation
Capture peer logs for endorsement failures and confirm the committed signaturePolicy matches the intended AND rule.
Gotcha
Do not forget to increment the sequence when changing the chaincode definition; otherwise the approve/commit will not apply the change.
CLI hint
Use the --signaturePolicy flag in both approveformyorg and commit steps and verify peer logs for endorsement policy evaluation errors.
Key Takeaway

This lab builds muscle memory for changing trust rules safely and verifying enforcement across organizations.
