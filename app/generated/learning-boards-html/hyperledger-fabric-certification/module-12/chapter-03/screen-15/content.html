Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Remember
Use Profiles & TLS
Keep endpoints and certificate information in profiles and always enable TLS.
Protect Identities
Use wallets; escalate to HSMs when necessary to protect private keys.
Confirm Commits
Add and remove commit listeners to ensure your app only treats transactions as final after events arrive.
Final thought

Integrating these reminders into your development process will reduce defects, improve security, and make your Fabric clients operationally manageable.
