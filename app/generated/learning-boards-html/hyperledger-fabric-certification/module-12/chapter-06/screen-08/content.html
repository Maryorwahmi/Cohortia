Other industry opportunities
Government records, energy trading, IP management, and controlled gaming economies.
Government services
Land registries and identity systems gain auditability and tamper-evidence while preserving controlled access.
Energy and IP
Energy trading and intellectual property benefit from verifiable transactions and timestamped provenance.
Decision rule
If multiple organizations require a shared authoritative ledger and no single party should control writes, Fabric is a strong candidate; otherwise prefer simpler systems.
Common pitfall
Choosing Fabric for problems that do not require multi-party trust adds unnecessary governance and integration overhead.
Key Takeaway

Assess cross-organization trust needs first; when trust and auditability matter across many parties, Fabric’s model adds clear value.
