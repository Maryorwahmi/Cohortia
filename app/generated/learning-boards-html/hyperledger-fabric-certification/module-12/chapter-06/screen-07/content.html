Healthcare: privacy and traceability
Record access, enable selective sharing, and track pharmaceuticals end-to-end.
Patient record management
Log accesses and permit selective data sharing so providers can collaborate while preserving patient consent and privacy.
Drug supply chain integrity
Record manufacturing and distribution events to detect counterfeit drugs and verify provenance at dispensing points.
Design considerations
  • Define access controls and consent models up front.
  • Keep sensitive health data off-chain or in PDCs and store only verification hashes on the ledger.
  • Engage compliance teams to map regulatory obligations to ledger events.
Common mistake
Treating blockchain as the primary medical record store risks privacy and compliance. Use Fabric for audit, sharing controls, and verification, not raw data replication.
Key Takeaway

Use Fabric to create verifiable access logs and provenance while keeping sensitive patient or pharmaceutical details confined to private collections.
