Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Technical Mapping
Map requirements to Fabric features
Decide channels, PDCs, smart contracts, and identity based on data visibility and trust.
Channels
Use for group-level private ledgers where multiple participants share all details of a workflow.
Private Data Collections
Keep sensitive attributes private to subsets while storing verification hashes on the channel ledger.
Smart contracts
Encode business logic and automate multi-party agreements to reduce manual reconciliation.
Practical rule
Start with the fewest channels and PDCs needed to meet privacy. Increase only when governed requirements demand tighter segregation.
Design pitfall
Excessive channels and PDCs create operational complexity and higher maintenance costs. Optimize for clarity and governance simplicity.
Key Takeaway

Translate visibility needs to channels and PDCs, encode rules in smart contracts, and enforce identity and endorsement policies to align technical design with legal responsibilities.
