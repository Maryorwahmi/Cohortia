Activity: Design a Fabric use case
A practical project you can complete and present.
Step 1: Choose industry and problem
Pick a focused process with multiple known parties and clear pain points.
Step 2: Map participants and visibility
Who reads, writes, and needs private data? Use this to design channels and PDCs.
Step 3: Draft governance and contracts
Define onboarding, endorsement policies, and smart contract responsibilities.
Presentation guide
Five-minute brief: problem, participants, Fabric features, governance sketch, expected benefits and risks.
Student tip
Keep the pilot limited in scope to deliver tangible results quickly and validate assumptions before scaling.
Key Takeaway

A focused pilot with clear governance and integration plans provides the strongest evidence of Fabric’s value.
