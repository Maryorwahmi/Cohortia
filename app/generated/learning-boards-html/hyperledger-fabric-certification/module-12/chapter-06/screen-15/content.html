Assessment: concept checks
Two example questions and the reasoning behind correct choices.
Question 1
Which Fabric features best support traceability while keeping pricing confidential? Correct: permissioned network + multiple channels + private data collections.
Question 2
Why would a regulated exchange choose Fabric? Correct: strict control over participants and identity to meet regulatory requirements and accountability.
Study tip
Practice mapping requirements to features: privacy to PDCs/channels; identity to certificate authorities and policies; automation to smart contracts.
Key Takeaway
Successful answers explain the mapping between specific business requirements and the Fabric features that directly satisfy them.
