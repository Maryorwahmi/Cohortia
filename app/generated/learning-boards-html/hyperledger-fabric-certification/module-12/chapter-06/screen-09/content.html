Why enterprises choose Fabric
Five strategic areas where Fabric delivers business value.
Privacy
Channels and PDCs allow selective disclosure while keeping a verifiable ledger of shared facts.
Performance & scalability
The execute-order-validate flow separates execution from ordering for efficient throughput.
Identity & governance
Certificate authorities and endorsement policies enforce who can take network actions.
Regulatory and cost impact
Immutable audit trails ease compliance; smart contracts reduce manual reconciliation and lower operating costs over time.
Key Takeaway

Articulate these strategic advantages in business terms—reduced time to reconcile, improved auditability, and lower dispute costs—to justify Fabric adoption.
