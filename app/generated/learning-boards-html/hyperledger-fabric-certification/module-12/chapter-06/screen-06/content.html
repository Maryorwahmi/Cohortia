We.trade: digitize trade agreements
Banks and clients use Fabric to record trade terms, reduce errors, and shorten transaction times using private channels for confidentiality.
ASX CHESS: regulated post-trade
A permissioned rebuild of a clearing system demonstrates how Fabric meets strict regulatory and identity requirements while improving efficiency.
Design checklist
  • Define endorsement policies to reflect legal responsibilities.
  • Assign identity and KYC responsibilities across members.
  • Choose channels for private negotiations and PDCs for selective data.
Common misconception
Blockchain automatically removes the need for governance. In regulated finance, governance and identity controls are essential and often more important than the ledger itself.
Key Takeaway

Financial use cases benefit from private channels and smart contracts, but require clear governance and identity controls to meet regulation.
