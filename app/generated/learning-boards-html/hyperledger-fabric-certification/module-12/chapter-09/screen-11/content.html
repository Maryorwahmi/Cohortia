Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Invalid Transactions: MVCC & Phantom
Definitions
MVCC_READ_CONFLICT: A key read during endorsement was modified before commit, invalidating the transaction.
PHANTOM_READ_CONFLICT: A range query returned a different set of keys at validation than at endorsement.
Resolutions
  • Refactor chaincode to avoid broad range queries and hot key writes.
  • Reduce contention by sharding keys or changing the access pattern.
  • Consider slower commit batches during high contention to reduce conflicts.
Key Takeaway
Invalid transactions often point to contention or poor access patterns; redesign reads and writes to minimize conflicts.
