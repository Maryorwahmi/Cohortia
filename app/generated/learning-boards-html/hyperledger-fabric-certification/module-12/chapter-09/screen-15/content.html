Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Key Concepts
Fabric Logs
Collect peer, orderer, CA, and chaincode logs and correlate by tx ID and time to form your initial hypothesis.
Remote Debugging
Attach a debugger to chaincode in a controlled environment to inspect runtime state when logs are insufficient.
Metrics & Profiling
Use Prometheus/Grafana and pprof to locate performance hotspots and verify improvements after changes.
Final Takeaway
Combine logs, debugging, and metrics in a repeatable workflow: reproduce, collect, hypothesize, test, and confirm.
