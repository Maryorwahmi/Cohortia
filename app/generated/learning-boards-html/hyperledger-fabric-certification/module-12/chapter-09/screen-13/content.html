Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Go Profiling (pprof)
Steps
  1. Enable the operations service and pprof endpoints on the component.
  2. Capture a CPU or heap profile during the problematic period.
  3. Analyze with pprof to identify functions consuming the most resources.
  4. Form a hypothesis and retest after changes, comparing profiles.
Guidance
Profiles show where time or memory is consumed; use logs to understand why that path is hot. Restrict pprof access and use it in a controlled window.
Key Takeaway
Profiles reveal hotspots; combine them with logs and metrics to target optimizations safely.
