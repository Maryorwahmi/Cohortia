Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Metrics: Prometheus & Grafana
What to monitor
  • Transaction throughput and commit latency across channels.
  • CPU, memory, disk I/O, and network usage for peers and orderers.
  • Endorsement and ordering latency to isolate slow components.
How to use metrics
Correlate metric spikes with log events and use them to prioritize where to profile or run deeper diagnostics. Keep dashboards for baseline and anomaly detection.
Key Takeaway
Metrics reveal systemic issues and guide where to collect logs and profiles for root cause analysis.
