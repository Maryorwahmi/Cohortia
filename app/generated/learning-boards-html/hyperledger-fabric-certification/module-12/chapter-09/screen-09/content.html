Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Network Connectivity & TLS Troubleshooting
Checks
  • Use ping and telnet to confirm reachability on expected ports.
  • Verify firewall rules allow fabric ports between nodes (peers 7051, orderer 7050, CA 7054).
  • Check DNS resolution for hostnames used by peers and orderers.
TLS Errors
Look for messages like x509: certificate signed by unknown authority, bad certificate, or hostname mismatch. These indicate cafile or certificate issues, expiration, or incorrect hostname overrides.
Fix checklist
Confirm cafile paths, replace expired certificates, and set ordererTLSHostnameOverride when hostnames differ from certificate CN.
Key Takeaway
Network reachability and TLS trust are separate checks; do both when diagnosing connectivity problems.
