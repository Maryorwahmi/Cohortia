Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Diagnosing Endorsement Failures
Quick Checklist
  • Inspect CLI output for policy violation messages.
  • Query chaincode policy: peer chaincode querycommitted to view the endorsement policy.
  • Check chaincode logs on each endorsing peer for execution errors.
  • Verify chaincode versions and transient field presence.
Scenario
If your policy is AND('Org1MSP.peer','Org2MSP.peer') and you only contact Org1, the proposal cannot collect sufficient endorsements. The CLI or orderer will surface a policy violation message; peer logs will show missing endorsements.
Key Takeaway
Endorsement failures require checking both policy alignment and chaincode execution on endorsers; either can be the root cause.
