Verification steps

Collect artifacts that prove your diagnosis

  • Log excerpt with transaction ID and error string.
  • Debugger variable snapshot showing failed branch.
  • Ping or telnet output proving connectivity or a blocked port.
  • Prometheus chart or pprof snapshot showing resource contention.
Tip

Store artifacts alongside your incident notes. That makes postmortems precise and repeatable.
