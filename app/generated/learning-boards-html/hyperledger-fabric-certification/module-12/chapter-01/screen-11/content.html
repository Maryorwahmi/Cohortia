Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Peer placement patterns
High-availability checklist
Concrete steps to reduce single points of failure.
  • Deploy minimum two peers per org across failure domains.
  • Colocate endorsement peers with client-heavy regions for latency benefits.
  • Use multiple anchor peers to improve discovery resilience.
Example layout
Manufacturer: endorsement peers in Region A. Distributor: endorsement peers in Region B. Both maintain commit peers in both regions for redundancy.
Key Takeaway
Distribute peers with role awareness: reduce latency by placing endorsers near clients, and increase resilience by duplicating commit and anchor peers across domains.
