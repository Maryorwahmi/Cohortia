Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Single vs Multi-channel
Single-channel
Pros and cons for simple topologies.
  • Pros: operational simplicity, single chaincode lifecycle.
  • Cons: broad visibility, larger ledgers, potential performance bottlenecks.
  • Common misuse: using it to avoid planning for privacy boundaries.
Misconception
Simplicity justifies exposing all data; instead, evaluate privacy and ledger growth costs first.
Multi-channel
Partition ledgers to match business processes and privacy needs.
  • Pros: smaller ledgers per participant, stronger privacy boundaries.
  • Cons: more channels to manage and coordinate; chaincode promotion across channels increases work.
  • When to use: distinct audit boundaries or differing membership per process.
Key Takeaway
Choose multi-channel for privacy and ledger size control; choose single-channel for simplicity when privacy is not a concern.
