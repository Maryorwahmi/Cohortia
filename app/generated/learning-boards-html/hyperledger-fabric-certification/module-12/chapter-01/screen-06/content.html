Five-step checklist
Follow these steps to decide minimal channel membership and manageability.
1. List transaction types
Record the data elements and access needs for each transaction.
2. Identify required readers
Who must see the record for operations or audit?
3. Consider PDCs
Can a portion of the data be private on a shared channel instead of creating a new channel?
4. Estimate volume and growth
High-throughput or large-history transactions may need isolation for performance.
5. Group and finalize
Group transactions with identical membership and similar throughput into one channel.
Example result
Raw Material channel: Manufacturer + Distributor. Shipping channel: Distributor + Retailer. Compliance channel: Manufacturer + Distributor + Retailer.
