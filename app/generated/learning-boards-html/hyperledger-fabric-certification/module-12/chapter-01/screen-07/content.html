Organization & MSP
Each org manages its own CA and MSP config; document responsibilities before onboarding.
Peer types
Endorsing peers execute chaincode. Committing peers store the ledger. Anchor peers enable cross-org discovery.
Pro tip
Place at least two peers per org across failure domains and designate endorsement peers near client workloads to reduce latency.
Practical checklist
  • Document CA ownership and MSP directories for each org.
  • Plan at least two peers per organization across AZs or data centers.
  • Assign which peers will endorse high-volume transactions.
  • Configure multiple anchor peers for cross-org resilience.
Key Takeaway
Define MSP and CA responsibilities and place peers for both latency and redundancy before channel creation.
