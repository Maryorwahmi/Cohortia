Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Hands-on: Supply Chain
Scenario tasks
Design channel membership, note where PDCs help, and outline endorsement and ordering choices.
  1. Channel 1 (Raw Material): Manufacturer, Distributor.
  2. Channel 2 (Shipping & Inventory): Distributor, Retailer.
  3. Channel 3 (Regulatory): Manufacturer, Distributor, Retailer.
Instructor hint
Use a PDC for private pricing between Distributor and Retailer rather than creating an additional channel.
Operational sketch
Place at least two peers per org across AZs. Choose a Raft ordering cluster sized for your fault tolerance target and distribute nodes across failure domains.
Reflection
Which channel would grow fastest in your workload and might need a split later? Document that risk in the design.
