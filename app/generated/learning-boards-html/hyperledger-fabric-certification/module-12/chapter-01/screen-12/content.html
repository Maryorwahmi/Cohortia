Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Governance & Onboarding
Governance flow
Define who approves changes and how onboarding is executed to keep the network secure and auditable.
Onboarding checklist
Collect MSP artifacts, CA details, anchor peer recommendations, and require governance approval before channel join.
Config-as-code practices
Keep configtx.yaml and channel profiles in version control, run staged tests, and apply changes via scripted pipelines.
Key Takeaway
Governance and automation prevent drift and make network evolution predictable and auditable.
