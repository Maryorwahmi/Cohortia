What is a PDC?
A mechanism for sharing private state among a subset of channel members while keeping a hashed reference on the channel ledger.
  • Private data is exchanged peer-to-peer among collection members.
  • The channel ledger stores only a hash, preserving a tamper-evident anchor.
  • Collection policies define membership and endorsement requirements.
Misconception
PDCs are a free substitute for channels; they are best for limited private subsets within a channel, not wholesale partitioning.
Practical example
Pricing agreements between two parties on a shared shipping channel can be stored in a PDC so other channel members cannot read the details.
Key Takeaway
Use PDCs when you need private subsets on a shared channel and keep collection policies simple to avoid chaincode complexity.
