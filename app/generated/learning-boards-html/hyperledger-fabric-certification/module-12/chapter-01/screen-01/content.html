Fabric Network Design Best Practices
Architect networks for privacy, throughput, and operational resilience by matching topology to business needs.
A clear design links who needs access to which data with channel choices, peer roles, ordering configuration, and governance. This avoids costly rework and reduces privacy exposure as your consortium scales.

Key Takeaway

Good network design maps data access needs to channels, peers and ordering choices. Start with the minimum visibility required and keep governance simple.
