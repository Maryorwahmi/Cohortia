Peer & Ordering

PDCs & Governance
Use PDCs for narrow privacy and enforce governance with config-as-code and version control.
Summary Takeaway
Start from the business, choose the minimal visibility per transaction, place peers smartly, and govern changes via code so your network remains private, performant, and auditable.
