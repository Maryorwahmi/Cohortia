Policy

Design governance for trust

Network-level
Membership rules, ordering service governance, and overall trust anchors.
Channel-level
Who sees what data, channel policies, and private data collection membership.
Chaincode-level
Endorsement policies, lifecycle approvals, and business-rule enforcement.
Standards
Adopt common identity and interoperability standards to reduce friction across partners and tools.
Audit strategy
Use audit channels or hashed anchors so regulators can verify integrity without accessing raw PDC content unless requested.
Key Takeaway

Clear, layered governance plus standards and audit anchors lets enterprises innovate while keeping regulators and partners confident in network behavior.
