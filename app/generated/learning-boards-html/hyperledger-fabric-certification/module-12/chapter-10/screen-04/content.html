Core Concept
Fabric roadmap priorities
How improvements translate into design choices and operational work.
Performance & Scalability
Optimizations to ordering, peer processing and state DB for higher throughput.
Privacy Enhancements
Private data improvements and potential use of zero-knowledge proofs.
Developer Experience
Better SDKs and deployment tooling reduce errors and speed delivery.
Security Hardening
Stronger crypto, identity, and resilience practices to protect networks.
Key Takeaway

Treat roadmap items as operational projects: evaluate maturity, integration effort, and the resulting changes to your network topology and lifecycle processes.

Roadmap process — practical checklist
A short process to evaluate and adopt roadmap features.
1. Identify
Find candidate features and their goals.
2. Assess
Check maturity, security, and integration needs.
3. Prototype
Run a small test with real data flows and policies.
4. Plan roll-out
Define rollout, monitoring, and rollback plans.
Example scenario
A consortium evaluates a ZKP-based proof for supplier compliance. They prototype the proof, update endorsement policies for proof verification, and verify performance before adoption.
