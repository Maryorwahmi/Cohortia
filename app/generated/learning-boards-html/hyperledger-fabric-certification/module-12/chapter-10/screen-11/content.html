Privacy

ZKP fundamentals

A zero-knowledge proof lets a prover convince a verifier that a statement is true without revealing the data. Use cases in Fabric include proving eligibility, thresholds, or compliance without exposing sensitive records.

When to use
When the verifier needs only a yes/no or numeric assertion and the raw data must remain secret.
Complementary tools
Combine ZKPs with PDCs: use proofs for public verification and PDCs for confidential multi-party computation when needed.
Misconception
ZKPs are not a drop-in privacy bolt-on; they require careful statement design and engineering for proof generation and verification.
Concrete scenario
A supplier proves revenue > threshold to qualify for a shipment contract. The supplier generates a proof off-chain; the verifier (or chaincode verifier) checks the proof and accepts participation without seeing the underlying financial records.
Integration patterns
  • • Off-chain proof generation with on-chain verification call or trusted verifier oracle.
  • • Store proof anchors or commitments on the ledger to bind proofs to transactions.
  • • Use events to trigger off-chain verification or settlement when proofs are validated.
Key Takeaway

ZKPs allow proof without disclosure, and they complement rather than replace Fabric's private data approaches. Design statements narrowly for practicality.
