Module 12 › Chapter: Future Trends › Key Concepts
Fabric Roadmap
Planned enhancements in performance, privacy, APIs, and security that guide future Fabric releases.
Interoperability
Enables secure exchange of data and assets across heterogeneous ledgers and standards.
Decentralized Identity
User-controlled identifiers and verifiable credentials anchored or verified by Fabric systems.
Tokenization
Representation of real-world assets as on-chain tokens managed by chaincode with governance rules.
Zero-Knowledge Proofs
Cryptographic proofs that validate facts without revealing the underlying sensitive information.
Governance & Standards
Policies, approval thresholds, and standards that ensure interoperability, compliance, and trust.
Summary
These concepts together shape how Fabric evolves: technical capabilities paired with governance and standards determine real-world adoption and trust.
