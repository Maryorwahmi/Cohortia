Map visibility
Decide what is public, private, or off-chain before you design chaincode and channels.
Plan interoperability
If cross-chain flows may be needed, design events and adapters up front for smoother integration.
Governance first
Define approval, audit, and emergency procedures to make networks trustworthy and operationally safe.
Closing summary
Technical innovation paired with clear governance and interoperability planning is the practical path to production-grade Fabric solutions.
