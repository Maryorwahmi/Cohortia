Interoperability patterns
Frameworks, atomic swaps, and standards form three practical approaches.
Hyperledger Cactus
A modular bridge framework that provides adapters for different ledgers and APIs to coordinate cross-chain calls.
Atomic Swaps
Cryptographic protocols ensuring paired operations across chains succeed or fail together.
Standards
Common message formats and interoperable identity models reduce bespoke integration work.
Common mistake
Assuming chains share the same finality and semantics. Always model differences in confirmation and asset representation.

Bridge (Cactus)
Coordinates cross-chain operations, enforces atomicity, and translates asset semantics.
Ethereum Payment

Example
A supply chain confirms goods on Fabric and pays on Ethereum. The bridge enforces an atomic swap so funds are transferred only when confirmation is final.
