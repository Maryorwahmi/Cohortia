Architecture

Token model & storage

Design tokens as chaincode assets. Decide which attributes are public, which go into private data collections, and which are stored off-chain with on-chain hashes.
Token lifecycle
Mint → Assign ownership → Transfer or Fractionalize → Redeem. Enforce rules at each step via chaincode.
Compliance hooks
Integrate KYC checks and transfer restrictions into your chaincode flow; emit events for off-chain payment and custody systems.
Misconception
A token is not inherently a liquid currency — it encodes rights and must obey business and regulatory logic you define.
Example: Real estate fractionalization
Create token representing a property. Chaincode mints 1,000 fractional tokens. Private collection stores investor agreements and KYC. Transfers update world state and emit events to a custodian.
On-chain
Ownership records, provenance hash, transfer history.
Private
Investor agreements, valuation inputs, KYC data in PDCs.
Off-chain
Clearing, custody, fiat settlement systems react to chain events.
Events
Emit token transfer events for external systems to perform settlement or compliance checks.
Key Takeaway

Tokens formalize rights on-chain; plan storage, compliance, and off-chain integrations to make tokenized assets practical and auditable.
