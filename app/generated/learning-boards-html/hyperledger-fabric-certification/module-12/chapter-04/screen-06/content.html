Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Backup & Restore
What to back up
  • • Peer ledger directory (block files and chaincode data).
  • • State database: LevelDB or CouchDB. Use CouchDB native tools for consistent backups.
  • • Cryptographic material: MSP directories, CA data, and private keys for all organizations.
  • • Channel configurations and orderer snapshots where applicable.
Key Takeaway

Back up both data and keys; without the crypto you cannot restore usable identities even if ledger files are present.

Restore test checklist
  1. Restore ledger and state DB to a fresh node and bring the peer online.
  2. Restore MSP and CA material and verify identities can enroll and sign transactions.
  3. Validate ledger continuity by querying committed blocks and running sample transactions.
  4. Measure RTO and adjust procedures until targets are met.
RPO & RTO
Define recovery point and time objectives to determine backup frequency and acceptable downtime for your business.
