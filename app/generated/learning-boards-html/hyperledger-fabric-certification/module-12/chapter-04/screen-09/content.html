Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Disaster Recovery
DR runbook essentials
  1. Confirm scope: full network vs critical services only.
  2. Restore orderers and ensure Raft quorum in the recovery region.
  3. Restore peers and MSP material, then let gossip synchronize ledgers.
  4. Switch client endpoints or DNS and validate transactions commit.
  5. Document failback steps and perform a controlled return when source region returns.
DR drill checklist
• Schedule a rehearsal and record timing for each step.
• Validate ledger integrity and that archived keys restore correctly.
• Verify that client transactions are accepted and processed end-to-end.
• Update runbooks with lessons learned and measured RTOs.
Common mistake
Not rehearsing DR leads to unknown timing and missing dependencies during a real outage.
