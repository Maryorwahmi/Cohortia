Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › CouchDB & Crypto
CouchDB backup notes
Use consistent snapshots or CouchDB native tools to export databases and indexes. Preserve view index definitions and re-index as needed after restore to ensure queries behave as before.
Crypto material
  • • Back up full MSP directories and CA databases, including private keys and certificates.
  • • Store backups encrypted, off-site, and with strict role-based access control.
  • • Periodically test that restored keys allow enrollment and transaction signing.
Practical restore tip
Automate a restore into an isolated environment, run a chaincode query, and perform a test transaction to validate both ledger and crypto restoration.
Common misconception
Peer replication via gossip is not a substitute for off-site backups of crypto material and channel configurations.
