Verification tasks
Each objective maps to a hands-on check you can run in staging before production changes.
Monitoring
Enable peer metrics, build a Grafana dashboard, and create alerts for CPU, disk and transaction failures.
Backups
Perform a full restore of ledger and MSP material to a fresh node and verify ledger continuity.
HA & DR
Simulate a peer outage and confirm the network continues to process transactions via other peers and orderers.
Upgrades
Run a chaincode upgrade sequence in test-network and verify the new definition is committed and functions as expected.
Mnemonic
MAPU — Monitor, Archive, Protect, Update.
Why verify

Verification converts procedures into proven playbooks so production actions are repeatable and auditable.
