Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Upgrade Strategies
Rolling Upgrades
  • • Upgrade nodes incrementally and validate health between steps.
  • • Preserve Raft quorum and peer endorsement availability.
  • • Easier to rollback individual nodes if problems appear.
Big-Bang Upgrades
  • • All nodes updated at once, often causing downtime.
  • • Higher risk of incompatibility across components during transition.
  • • May be faster in elapsed time but risks larger outages.
Decision rule

Prefer rolling upgrades unless a coordinated maintenance window and thorough staging validation make a big-bang practical and acceptable for your risk profile.
