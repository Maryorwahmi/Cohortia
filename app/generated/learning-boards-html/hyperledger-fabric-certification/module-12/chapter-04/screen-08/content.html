Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › High Availability
Component patterns
Peers
Multiple peers per org across AZs. Gossip keeps ledgers in sync and supports failover.
Ordering (Raft)
Odd-numbered Raft nodes across failure domains to maintain quorum during node loss.
CAs
Deploy redundant CAs with a shared database to preserve enrollment availability.
Pro tip

Test client failover by rotating endpoints and confirming transactions still commit during node outages.

DR planning
For disaster recovery replicate critical components and backups to a separate geographical region and prepare runbooks for failover and failback.
Common mistake
Placing all replicas in the same region or availability zone defeats the benefits of redundancy.
