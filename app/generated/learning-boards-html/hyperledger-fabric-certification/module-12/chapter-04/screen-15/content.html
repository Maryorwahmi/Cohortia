Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Key Concepts
High Availability
Design peers and orderers across failure domains and confirm quorum behavior.
Rolling Upgrades
Update nodes incrementally, preserve quorum, and validate after each step.
Vulnerability Management
Patch Fabric and underlying layers, test in staging, and validate functionality post-patch.
Final takeaway: operational maturity comes from repeatable procedures, rehearsed runbooks, and automated checks that keep your Fabric network resilient and secure.
