Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Operational Best Practices
Why this matters
Reliability, security, and recoverability reduce business risk and protect ledger integrity.

In production, observability and backups are not optional. Logs and metrics let you diagnose faults quickly. Backups and tested restores protect your ledger and identities. Redundancy keeps services running during node failures. Planned upgrades and patching remove vulnerabilities without surprising your peers.

Monitoring
Collect Prometheus metrics and logs centrally. Alert on resource and transaction anomalies.
Backup
Regularly back up ledger data, state DB and MSP/CA material. Test restores to meet RPO and RTO.
HA & DR
Deploy multiple peers, odd Raft orderers, and cross-region DR for true resilience.
Upgrades
Use rolling upgrades and Fabric lifecycle for chaincode to minimize downtime and coordinate org approvals.
Key Takeaway

Operational readiness combines observability, backup discipline, HA design and upgrade processes. Treat it as part of your development lifecycle.

Operational map
Connect telemetry, backups, HA, and upgrades into a repeatable runbook.
Logs & Metrics
Central aggregation, retention policy, alerting thresholds and dashboards.
Backups
Ledger, CouchDB, MSP/CA keys. Secure off-site storage and periodic restore tests.
Upgrades
Staging validation, rolling upgrades, and coordinated chaincode lifecycle actions.
