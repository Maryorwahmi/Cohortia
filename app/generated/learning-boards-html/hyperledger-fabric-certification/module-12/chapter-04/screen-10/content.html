Upgrade sequence
  1. Upgrade ordering nodes in a rolling fashion, confirm quorum and block production after each node.
  2. Upgrade peers one at a time and verify ledger sync and endorsement behavior.
  3. Upgrade CAs and verify enrollment and certificate issuance from upgraded CAs.
  4. Validate client compatibility and application integration tests.
Common mistake

Upgrading all nodes at once or skipping staging tests increases the risk of prolonged outages and incompatibilities.

Practical checks
  • • Confirm backups and restore tests are available in case rollback is needed.
  • • Monitor metrics and logs closely during each upgrade step.
  • • Coordinate version compatibility across organizations and clients.
