Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Security Practices
Best Practice Checklist
  • Enable TLS and mTLS for peer and orderer communication.
  • Define endorsement policies that match your trust model without being overly permissive.
  • Use private data collections for narrowly scoped confidential data.
  • Keep MSP and CA roots organized and rotate keys as part of operations.
Insecure Defaults to Avoid
Avoid open policies that accept a single org, disabled TLS in production, or storing private data on the public channel ledger.
Exam tip
When presented with a security scenario, map the requirement to the smallest set of controls that meet confidentiality and integrity without unnecessary complexity.
