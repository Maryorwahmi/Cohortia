Typical Client Flow
1. Build & sign proposal
Prepare chaincode invocation and sign with the client's identity.
2. Send to endorsers
Gather endorsement responses and read-write sets.
3. Submit to orderer
Orderer sequences the transaction into a block for peers to validate and commit.
Example: Listen for Chaincode Events
An application subscribes to chaincode events to react when a transaction of interest commits, avoiding blind polling and improving responsiveness.
Common misconception
Evaluate calls resemble reads but do not change state. Do not expect them to produce ledger commits or events.
Key takeaway
Practice both evaluate and submit flows plus event subscription to explain when each is appropriate in exam scenarios.
