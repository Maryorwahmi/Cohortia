Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Identity & Membership
Core Concepts
Certificates, CAs, MSPs, and the chain of trust.
Certificate Authority (CA)
Issues X.509 identities used to sign proposals and transactions.
MSP
Defines trusted roots and membership for organizations and channels.
Practical task
Inspect an MSP directory to confirm the presence of tlsca and signcerts and map an identity to a known root.
Example Scenario
An admin enrolls with the CA and receives a certificate. If the channel's MSP bundles do not include that CA's root, the admin will not be recognized for channel operations even though the certificate is valid.
Common mistake
Assuming a certificate equals membership. Verify the MSP config for trusted roots and OU policies before assuming permissions.
Key takeaway
Practice reading certificate chains and MSP files so you can quickly determine why an identity is accepted or rejected during an exam scenario.
