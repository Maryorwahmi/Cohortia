Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Exam Format
Common Sections
  • Multiple-choice and true/false — test conceptual understanding.
  • Scenario and troubleshooting questions — apply reasoning to a failure.
  • Hands-on labs — perform CLI and deployment tasks in a timed environment.
Timing & Strategy
Allocate time blocks: quick pass for simple questions, a focused block for scenarios, and a reserved slot for labs. Practice timed runs in advance.
Tip
If a lab stalls, record the error and move on. You can often recover partial credit and avoid wasting the clock.
