Advanced Security: HSMs and Key Management

Understand how hardware-backed key protection and disciplined lifecycle practices keep Fabric networks trustworthy and resilient.

Private keys are the authority behind endorsements and CA signatures. If a key is stolen, an attacker can impersonate a peer or CA and corrupt the ledger or issue fraudulent identities. HSMs reduce this risk by performing cryptography inside tamper-resistant hardware and never exporting keys in plain text.

This chapter walks from principles to practice: PKCS#11, component configuration, SoftHSM hands-on steps, and operational checks such as audit logging and key rotation.
