Module 11 › Chapter 11.9 › What is an HSM?
HSM basics
An HSM holds keys in hardware and performs signing and other cryptographic operations without exposing key bytes. This reduces risk from OS or container compromises because the key material is isolated inside the module.
Deployment types
Network HSMs and PCIe HSMs offer different trade-offs for latency and placement.
Operational note
HSM availability is critical; plan redundancy and monitoring.
Analogy
Think of an HSM as a locked safe that performs signing on your behalf; you can ask the safe to sign a document but you cannot extract the key from inside the safe.
Misconception
HSMs reduce, but do not eliminate, security risk. They must be configured and monitored correctly.
