Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Key Concepts
BatchTimeout
Maximum wait time before cutting a block; shorter timeouts lower latency but may reduce throughput.
CouchDB Indexing
Define JSON indexes for fields used in selectors to avoid full table scans and speed queries.
Read‑Write Conflicts
Conflicts arise when concurrent transactions modify the same key; mitigate with sharding, composite keys, and client-side retries.
Final Takeaway

Optimization combines configuration, data modeling, and client behavior. Change one thing at a time and use metrics to confirm improvement.
