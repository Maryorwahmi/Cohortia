Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Key Concepts
Transaction Throughput
Transactions processed per second, key for capacity planning.
Latency
Time from submission to final ledger commit; important for responsiveness.
BatchSize
Controls how many transactions are grouped into a block; affects throughput and latency trade-offs.
Summary

Use these concepts together: choose batching and resource allocation based on whether you prioritize throughput or latency, and validate with metrics.
