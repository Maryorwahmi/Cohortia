Optimizing Fabric for Performance and Scalability

Tuning ordering, peers, chaincode, and clients to meet enterprise throughput and latency goals.

This chapter focuses on practical levers you can change and the monitoring you need to validate them. We'll show configuration parameters and development patterns that have measurable impact, and a short lab to demonstrate CouchDB indexing benefits.
