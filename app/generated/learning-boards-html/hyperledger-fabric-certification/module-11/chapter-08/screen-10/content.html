Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Managed vs Self-Managed
When to use self-managed Kubernetes
  • Need for custom Fabric versions, custom consensus, or tight integration with internal tooling.
  • Strong DevOps and Kubernetes expertise available in the team.
  • Requirements for specific storage, networking, or compliance controls.
When to prefer a managed blockchain service
  • Rapid proof-of-concept or limited operational staff.
  • Preference to offload provisioning, scaling, and patching to the provider.
  • Accepting some limits on customization for reduced operational burden.
Assessment scenario
