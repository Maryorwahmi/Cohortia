Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › StatefulSet vs Deployment
Comparison table
Deployment
Pods are stateless and interchangeable. No stable hostnames or persistent volume guarantees for specific replicas.
StatefulSet
Provides stable network IDs, ordered operations, and per-replica PVCs that keep ledger data attached to the correct peer.
Concrete scenario
If you run peer0 and peer1 for Org1 and replace peer0 with a fresh pod under a Deployment, its hostname and PVC mapping may change. Using StatefulSet keeps peer0's identity stable and reattaches the same PVC, preserving ledger continuity and channel membership.
Pitfall
Mistakenly using Deployment for peers leads to lost identity or detached ledger volumes after rescheduling.
Key Takeaway

Use StatefulSet and PV/PVC for any Fabric component that requires stable identity and durable ledger storage.
