Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Common Mistakes & Safety
Common mistakes
  • Inadequate PV performance: Causes slow ledger writes and endorsement delays.
  • Network configuration errors: Services or policies blocking required ports disrupt gossip and ordering.
  • Exposed sensitive endpoints: Publicly reachable peers or CA endpoints without proper auth are high risk.
  • Ignoring chaincode lifecycle: Chaincode must be able to restart and re-read ledger state.
Safety notes
Isolate production clusters from test clusters and apply strict RBAC.
Back up persistent volumes regularly and test restores.
Use secure image registries and scan images for vulnerabilities.
Recovery drill
Simulate a node failure and verify PVC reattachment and ledger continuity, then document the runbook steps required for recovery.
