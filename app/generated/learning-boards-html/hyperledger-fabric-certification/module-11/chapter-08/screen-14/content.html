Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Key Concepts
Kubernetes
Orchestrates containers, manages scaling, and automates restarts for Fabric components.
Helm
Packages charts to install and upgrade complex Fabric topologies with configurable values.
StatefulSet / PV
Ensure stable identities and durable ledger storage for peers and stateful Fabric roles.
Takeaway
Map Fabric requirements to Kubernetes primitives, automate with Helm, secure TLS and Secrets, and choose managed services or self-managed clusters based on your control and operational needs.
