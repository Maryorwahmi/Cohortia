Verification Steps
  1. Fetch the new config block from the orderer.
  2. Decode it and inspect channel_group.groups.Application.groups for Org4MSP.
  3. Check AnchorPeers under the org's values to confirm gossip connectivity.
Optional check

Onboard peer

Configure Org4 peer TLS and MSP and run peer channel join after retrieving the latest channel block. If join fails, re-check AnchorPeers and TLS certs.

Troubleshoot

If Org4 cannot join, confirm that the peer's MSP and TLS cert paths match those declared in the config and that AnchorPeers are reachable.

Key Takeaway

Verification and peer joins prove the change had operational effect; always validate after submission.
