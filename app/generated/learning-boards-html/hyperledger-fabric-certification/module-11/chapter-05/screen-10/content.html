Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Submit & Commit
Submission

Submit the fully signed envelope to the orderer. The orderer validates signatures and policies, then commits the update to the ledger as a block.

peer channel update -f config_update_in_envelope.pb -o orderer.example.com:7050 --ordererTLSHostnameOverride orderer.example.com -c mychannel --tls --cafile $ORDERER_CA
Verify commit

Fetch and decode the new config block to confirm the expected additions are present and the ledger shows the new block.

Troubleshooting

If rejected, inspect error messages: insufficient signatures, incorrect TLS CA, or malformed envelope headers are common causes.

Key Takeaway

Submission is the final audited step. Verify signatures and TLS/MSP context before attempting to submit to production orderers.
