What to Remember Quick reference
Choose event type
Chaincode events for business signals; block events for full ledger context.
Checkpoint safely
Only advance checkpoints after durable downstream writes to avoid data loss.
Design idempotent consumers
Use transactionId checks and existence queries to prevent duplicates.
