Module 11 › Chapter 11.7 › Learning Objectives

Practical outcomes

Each objective is paired with a hands-on check: emit, subscribe, recover, and scale.

Emit & Verify
Modify CreateAsset in Go to call SetEvent and include the asset JSON. Deploy and trigger the chaincode to confirm the event appears in logs.
Subscribe & Log
Create a Node.js listener that registers addContractListener and prints eventName, payload and transactionId for each chaincode event.
Recover & Checkpoint
Persist last-processed block or txn ID, restart the listener, and resume from the checkpoint to avoid dropped events.
