Summary Key Concepts
Fabric Events
Asynchronous notifications for ledger updates and business outcomes.
Checkpointing
Persist last-processed block or txn so listeners can resume reliably.
Idempotency
Ensure repeated processing does not cause duplicate or inconsistent external state.
Takeaway

Use chaincode events for targeted notifications, block events for full context, and design listeners with checkpointing, validation, and idempotency to ensure correctness.
