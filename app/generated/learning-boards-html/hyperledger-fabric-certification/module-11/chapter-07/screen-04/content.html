Core Concept Event motivation

Definition

Events are asynchronous notifications emitted by peers or chaincode to inform subscribers about ledger activity.

Why it matters
  • Reduces polling overhead and latency by delivering changes on commit.
  • Decouples producers (chaincode) from consumers (external services).
  • Enables responsive microservices, triggers, and analytics pipelines.
Key Takeaway

Events let consumers react immediately to business outcomes. Combine with safeguards for reliable processing.

Concrete example

A payment chaincode emits PaymentProcessed with payment details. The notification service receives the event and sends an email or SMS without scanning every ledger entry.
Misconception check

Events are not a substitute for validation. Treat event payloads as untrusted until validated by the consuming service, and assume duplicates are possible unless you build stronger guarantees.
