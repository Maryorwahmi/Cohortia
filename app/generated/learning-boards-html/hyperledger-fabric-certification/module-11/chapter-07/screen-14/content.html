Architecture Scaling
Pattern
  1. Listener subscribes to Fabric and validates events.
  2. Listener publishes events to a durable queue or topic.
  3. Multiple consumers process messages in parallel with idempotency checks.
Trade-offs
  • Pros: buffering, parallelism, and better resilience to spikes.
  • Cons: added operational components and potential ordering complexity.
  • Ensure secure transport and monitor consumer lag closely.
Key Takeaway

Introduce a queue between Fabric and consumers to scale processing and decouple ingestion from downstream work, but account for ordering and operational cost.
