Definition Chaincode events
How to emit

Call ctx.GetStub().SetEvent with a name and payload after writing state to the ledger.

Best practices:
  • Emit minimal, structured payloads the listener needs.
  • Keep events meaningful and aligned with business outcomes.
  • Document the payload schema for consumers to validate.
A concrete scenario
After CreateAsset completes and PutState succeeds, emit AssetCreated with the asset JSON. An external listener uses this to update a search index or send notifications.
Safety note

Treat payloads as untrusted until validated. Chaincode bugs can produce malformed data and listeners must verify structure before acting.

Key Takeaway

Chaincode events are the most efficient way to deliver business-level notifications from Fabric to external systems. Validate payloads and design for duplicates.
