Consensus Mechanism

Raft Consensus: The Leader-Follower Model

Understanding Fabric's default crash-fault-tolerant ordering service.

Crash-Fault-Tolerant (CFT): Raft ensures the ordering service remains operational even if some non-malicious nodes fail.

Leader-Follower Model: A single leader node is elected to order all transactions and replicate them to follower nodes.

Strong Consistency: All nodes agree on the exact order of transactions, crucial for ledger integrity.

Key Takeaway

Raft's leader-follower model provides a robust, fault-tolerant, and consistent ordering service, simplifying management compared to BFT protocols.

Analogy

Think of Raft like a team meeting with a designated facilitator. All agenda items go through the facilitator (leader), who ensures everyone agrees on the order of discussion points (transactions) and records them consistently.
