Custom Consensus Challenges

Deep Dive: Practical Limitations (Part 2)

Addressing the long-term maintenance burden and loss of community support for custom consensus.

Significant Maintenance Burden

  • •Requires continuous adaptation to Fabric's evolving codebase and new versions.
  • •Demands dedicated resources and specialized personnel for ongoing support.
  • •Can be a substantial drain on organizational resources and time.

Loss of Community Support

  • •Forfeits the benefit of a strong, active community for troubleshooting and improvements.
  • •Increases the time and cost of resolving issues without collective wisdom.
  • •Can hinder the adoption and scalability of your custom solution.
Key Takeaway

The ongoing maintenance burden and the absence of community support for custom consensus protocols make them an unsustainable and high-risk choice for most enterprise Fabric deployments.
