Best Practices

Common Mistakes & Safety Notes in Customization

Avoiding pitfalls and ensuring network integrity and security during customization.

Common Mistakes

  • •Underestimating complexity of core component replacement.
  • •Ignoring performance implications of customizations.
  • •Lack of thorough testing in various failure scenarios.
  • •Breaking compatibility with future Fabric versions.

Safety Notes

  • •Approach deep customization with extreme caution.
  • •Always refer to official Hyperledger Fabric documentation.
  • •Ensure custom code is rigorously reviewed and tested.
  • •Errors can compromise network integrity and security.
Key Takeaway

Careful planning, rigorous testing, and adherence to official guidelines are paramount when customizing Fabric to avoid critical errors and ensure network stability and security.
