Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Fabric Architecture & Raft Consensus

Revisiting the foundational ideas of modularity and transaction ordering.

Pluggable Architecture

A design allowing system components to be easily swapped or customized without affecting the rest of the system.

Raft Consensus

A crash-fault-tolerant (CFT) consensus algorithm used by Fabric's ordering service, operating on a leader-follower model.

Leader-Follower Model

In Raft, a leader node is elected to process all client requests and replicate them to follower nodes for strong consistency.

Summary Takeaway

Fabric's modularity, powered by Raft's leader-follower consensus, provides a robust and adaptable foundation for enterprise blockchain solutions.
