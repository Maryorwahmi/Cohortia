Other Pluggable Components: MSP & Chaincode Execution

Exploring identity management and flexible chaincode environments.

Membership Service Provider (MSP)

  • •Theoretically allows integration of other identity schemes beyond X.509.
  • •However, this is a very deep and complex customization.
  • •Often more practical to integrate off-chain identity systems.

Chaincode Execution Environment

  • •External Builders/Launchers allow chaincode in virtually any language.
  • •Highly practical and commonly used customization.
  • •Leverages existing developer skill sets, accelerating development.
Key Takeaway

While custom MSPs are highly complex, Fabric's pluggable chaincode execution environments offer significant practical benefits by supporting diverse programming languages and developer skill sets.
