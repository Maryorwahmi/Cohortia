Advanced Customization

Customizing Consensus: Theory vs. Reality

Exploring the possibilities and significant limitations of replacing Fabric's core ordering protocol.

Theoretical Possibilities

  • •Implement a new consensus plugin adhering to Fabric's ordering service interface.
  • •Develop a new consensus algorithm, such as a BFT protocol (e.g., PBFT or SBFT).
  • •Integrate with the ordering service's communication and block-cutting logic.

Practical Limitations

  • •Extreme complexity and error-proneness in implementing distributed consensus.
  • •Requires deep expertise in distributed systems and cryptography.
  • •Significant testing, formal verification, and industry certification needed.
Key Takeaway

While Fabric's design allows for custom consensus, the practical challenges of implementation, testing, and maintenance make it a rarely pursued and highly complex endeavor for most organizations.
