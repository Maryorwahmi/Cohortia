Architecture

Leader and followers

The leader accepts proposals and replicates them; followers keep replicated logs and participate in elections if the leader fails.
Replication
Leader appends to its log and sends entries to followers. Followers acknowledge; once a quorum acknowledges, entries are considered committed.
Election
Followers trigger a timed election to select a new leader if the current one fails, preserving operation continuity.
Durability note
Raft commits entries only after replication to a quorum, which ensures committed blocks survive leader changes and node restarts.
Concrete scenario
During a leader failure, remaining nodes run an election. A leader is chosen and ordering resumes; already committed blocks remain durable due to quorum replication rules.
Misconception
Raft tolerates crash faults but not arbitrary Byzantine behavior. Trust and access control remain important for orderer nodes.
