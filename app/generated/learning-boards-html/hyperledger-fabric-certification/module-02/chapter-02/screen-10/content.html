Common mistakes and checks

Insufficient nodes
Use N = 2F + 1. Choose N to tolerate your required F failures.
TLS misconfiguration
Mismatched certificates block connections and prevent log replication between orderers and peers.
Genesis and channel mismatch
Ensure the generated genesis block and channel configs reference the correct endpoints and certs for your deployed nodes.
Warning

Orderer failures can freeze block production and cause peers to lag. Treat orderers as critical infrastructure: secure, monitor, and provision them correctly.

Quick diagnostics
  1. Check orderer logs for TLS or connectivity errors.
  2. Verify configtx.yaml Orderer endpoints match deployed nodes.
  3. Confirm node count meets fault tolerance target.
