Definition

Channel policies

Policies set the rules for who can perform administrative operations such as updating the channel configuration or adding organizations. They are part of the channel configuration and are included in the genesis block.

  • Update policy: who can submit configuration updates.
  • Membership policy: who may be added or validated as organizations.
  • Endorsement policy defaults: how chaincode endorsement may be judged if not overridden.
Common mistakes
  • Policies referencing misnamed MSPs or non-existent admins, preventing signature collection.
  • Requiring signatures from too many parties, locking future updates.
  • Forgetting to set anchor peer policies, which breaks cross-org gossip discovery.
Key Takeaway

Align policies with real admin identities and test update flows; overly strict or incorrect policies can make channels unmanageable.
