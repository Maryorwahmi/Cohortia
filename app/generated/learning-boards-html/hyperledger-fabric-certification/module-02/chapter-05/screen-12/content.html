Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Mistakes & Safeguards
Common Mistakes
  • Misconfigured policies that reference wrong MSPs or unreachable admins, preventing updates.
  • Forgetting anchor peer updates after adding orgs, which breaks gossip discovery.
  • Over-segmenting into many channels, causing operational and chaincode lifecycle overhead.
Practical safeguard: maintain an onboarding template that lists policy entries to update, anchor peers to register, signatures to collect, and verification commands to run. This template reduces the chance of missing critical steps during channel changes.
Quick checklist
  1. Validate policy MSP names and admin identities.
  2. Collect and verify required signatures before submission.
  3. Update anchor peers and verify gossip connectivity.
  4. Consider private data collections to keep highly sensitive data without adding channels.
Key Takeaway

Operational templates and staged testing reduce risk when creating or modifying channels. Plan for governance, anchor updates, and verification steps.
