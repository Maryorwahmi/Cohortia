Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › LevelDB vs CouchDB
LevelDB
  • Embedded, low-overhead key/value store.
  • Best for simple key-based reads and high throughput.
  • Lower operational complexity; runs with the peer process.
CouchDB
  • External JSON document store with rich query support.
  • Enables complex selectors and indexing for structured data.
  • Higher CPU, memory, and disk requirements; separate backup procedures.
Decision guide
If your workload is simple key lookups and you want minimal operational overhead, use LevelDB. If you need ad-hoc JSON queries over complex documents, pick CouchDB but budget resources and maintenance effort accordingly.
