Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Peer Configuration
Essential settings
  • MSP ID & certificates: Identify organization membership and sign requests.
  • Listen address & ports: Must be reachable by clients and other peers.
  • State DB selection: LevelDB or CouchDB based on query need and resources.
  • Persistence and backups: Regular backups of state DB and certificates.
Operational notes
Secure private keys and TLS certificates to prevent impersonation.
Ensure firewall rules allow required ports; otherwise peers cannot join channels.
Plan capacity: CouchDB requires more CPU/disk than LevelDB; size accordingly.
Troubleshooting hint
If a peer cannot join a channel, verify MSP IDs, certificate paths, and network address bindings first.
