Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Key Concepts
Chaincode
Code that implements business rules and CRUD operations on the ledger.
Lifecycle
Package, install, approve per organization, and commit to activate chaincode on a channel.
Determinism
Chaincode must produce identical outputs across endorsers given identical inputs to preserve consensus.
Takeaway: Build deterministic, testable chaincode and follow the lifecycle to deploy it under collaborative governance.
