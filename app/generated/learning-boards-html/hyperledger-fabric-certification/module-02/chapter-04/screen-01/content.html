Chaincode: Smart Contracts and Business Logic
Implement business rules that peers execute; governed and activated through a collaborative lifecycle.
What you will learn
Why chaincode is central to Fabric, how its lifecycle enforces governance, and how to write deterministic Go chaincode.
Why it matters
Correct chaincode preserves ledger integrity, supports confidential workflows, and prevents consensus failures caused by non-determinism.
