Topology Decisions
Choose number of orgs, peers per org, and ordering nodes based on availability and policy needs.
Impact on endorsement
Your endorsement policy must reference MSPs present on the channel. If you require endorsements from an org that isn't on the channel, transactions cannot meet policy.
Orderer sizing
Batch sizes and number of orderer nodes affect throughput and fault tolerance. Consider expected load when planning nodes.
Common-mistake

Defining an endorsement policy that cannot be satisfied by the organizations on a channel.

Example topology (conceptual)
Two organizations, one peer each, and a single orderer for a minimal development setup. For production add peers per org and an etcdraft orderer cluster for resilience.
Check your understanding
If your policy needs two org signatures, what happens when one org is offline? How does topology choice affect availability?
