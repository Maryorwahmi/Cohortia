Best Practices
Protect private keys
Never commit keystore files to version control. Limit access to keystores and back them up securely.
Backup root CAs
Keep copies of root CA certificates; losing them prevents verification of existing certs.
Use CRLs and rotation
Publish CRLs and plan certificate rotation with channel config updates to change trust anchors safely.
Operational checklist: secure CA admin credentials, validate MSP contents after enrollment, and ensure TLS cert paths are correct for CA communication.
