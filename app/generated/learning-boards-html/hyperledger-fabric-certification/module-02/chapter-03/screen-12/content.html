Comparison
TLS Certificate
  • Used to secure transport (TLS handshake).
  • Presented by endpoints to encrypt communication.
  • Often short-lived and managed separately from identity certs.
Identity Certificate
  • Used to sign transactions and prove identity to peers.
  • Validated against MSP trust roots during authorization.
  • Stored in signcerts/ and paired with a private key in keystore/.
Misconception check: do not assume a TLS certificate automatically grants signing rights. Fabric checks the signing certificate against MSPs for authorization independent of TLS validation.
