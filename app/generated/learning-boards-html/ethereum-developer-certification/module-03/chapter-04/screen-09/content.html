Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Front-running
Problem

An attacker observes a pending transaction and submits a competing transaction with higher gas to be included earlier, altering outcomes.

Example: DEX trade

A trade that will move price can be front-run by submitting a buy first and a sell after to capture value.

Mitigations
Commit-Reveal

Two-step process hides details until reveal, preventing profitable reordering based on content.

Private submission / relayers

Avoid exposing sensitive txs to public mempool, but weigh trust and infrastructure costs.

Key takeaway

Design for confidentiality of sensitive inputs or accept UX trade-offs for commit-reveal flows.
