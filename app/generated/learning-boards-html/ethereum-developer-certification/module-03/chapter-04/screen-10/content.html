Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Denial of Service & Gas
Problem patterns
  • Unbounded loops over user-controlled arrays.
  • Push payment models that iterate over many recipients in one tx.
  • Conditional logic that can be forced to revert for all users.
Concrete mitigation

Switch to pull payments where each user claims their funds, and implement batching with explicit limits to bound gas per call.

Testing tips
  • Simulate large arrays and stress-test gas usage.
  • Write negative tests where an attacker grows state to try to break operations.
Key Takeaway

Design every function to remain bounded and test growth scenarios to prevent DoS through gas exhaustion.
