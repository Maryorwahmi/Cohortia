Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
Conditional Statements
if, else if, and else determine flow. Pair with require or revert for predictable failures.
Loops and Gas
for and while exist but iterate at gas cost. Prefer bounded iterations or off-chain work for large sets.
Visibility & Mutability
Choose public/private/internal/external and view/pure/payable to express who can call a function and its state effects.
Summary
Explicit checks, bounded work, and precise function contracts reduce risk and improve clarity in your Solidity code.
