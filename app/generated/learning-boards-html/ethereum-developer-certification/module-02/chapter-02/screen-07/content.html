What a Contract binds
  • ABI: the function and event signatures the contract exposes.
  • Address: where the contract lives on the chosen network.
  • Provider or Signer: determines whether calls are read-only or signed transactions.
Pattern
Keep a provider-bound contract for UI reads and a signer-bound contract for writes to avoid accidental failures and reduce risk.
Concrete example

After deploying, instantiate: const myContract = await MyContract.deploy(...); or new hre.ethers.Contract(address, abi, signer). Then call await myContract.greeting() for reads or const tx = await myContract.setGreeting('Hi'); await tx.wait(); for writes.

Key takeaway

How you instantiate a Contract controls whether you can only read or also write. Pick the correct binding for the task.
