HTTP vs WebSocket
HTTP for simple calls; WebSocket for subscriptions and live updates.
Injected Providers
MetaMask handles signing and account management securely in the browser.
Async / Promises
Await or chain .then to reliably access results from Web3 calls.
Final takeaway
Provider selection and proper handling of asynchronous calls solve many early integration problems. Verify these behaviors with a small connect script before building more complex features.
