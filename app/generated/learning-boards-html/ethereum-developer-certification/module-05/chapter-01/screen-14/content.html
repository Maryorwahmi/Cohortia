Decentralized Application
Frontend paired with smart contracts for trust and immutability.
Web3.js
A JavaScript library that talks to nodes and enables contract calls and transactions.
Ethereum Node
A client that syncs blockchain state and exposes RPC endpoints for Web3.js to call.
Summary takeaway
Master these concepts to reliably connect your frontend to contracts, choose appropriate providers, and design DApps that balance trust, cost, and performance.
