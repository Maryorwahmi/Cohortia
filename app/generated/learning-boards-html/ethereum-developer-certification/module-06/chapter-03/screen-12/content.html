Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Timestamp Dependence
Time and randomness on-chain
Block times are approximate and timestamps can be nudged by miners.
Problem
Using block.timestamp for randomness or critical checks gives miners a small manipulable lever.
Safer options
Use an external VRF oracle for randomness and design time-based logic tolerant of small timestamp variance.
Key Takeaway

Avoid using block.timestamp for security-critical randomness. Prefer verifiable oracles and tolerant time designs.
