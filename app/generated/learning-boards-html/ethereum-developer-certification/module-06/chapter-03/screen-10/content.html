Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Front-running
Mempool visibility and ordering attacks
Transactions are visible before inclusion, enabling adversarial ordering.
Real scenario
A large swap is visible; bots place a higher-fee transaction to be mined first, profiting from the new price.
Defensive measures
Use commit-reveal, batch processing, or design controls that limit profit from ordering changes.
Why it matters

Front-running can undermine fairness and lead to losses; consider it when your contract exposes high-value intent to the mempool.
