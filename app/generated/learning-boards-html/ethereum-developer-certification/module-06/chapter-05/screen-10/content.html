Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Delegated Voting
Definition
Delegated voting allows holders to delegate their voting power to another address without transferring token ownership. Delegates vote on behalf of delegators for proposals during voting periods.
  • • Delegation is reversible; owners can undelegate or change delegates.
  • • Delegates accumulate voting weight equal to delegated tokens.
  • • Often used to increase effective participation and expertise in votes.
Concrete Scenario
Alice delegates to a trusted contributor Bob. Bob votes for a treasury proposal using combined weight from many delegators. If Bob votes poorly, delegators can revoke delegation in the next cycle.
Takeaway
Design delegation with transparency and expiry to balance convenience with checks on concentrated influence.
Comparison — Direct vs Delegated Voting
Direct Voting
Every token holder votes directly. High burden on participants; low risk of concentrated proxy power.
Delegated Voting
Allows delegation to trusted voters. Improves turnout but introduces representative influence that must be monitored.
Common Mistake
Treating delegation as permanent ownership transfer. Delegation only assigns voting power and should be revocable.
