Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Challenges & Ethics
Benefits Recap
  • • Transparency: rules and votes are auditable on-chain.
  • • Alignment: members have a direct stake in outcomes.
  • • Global access: participation from anywhere with tokens.
Challenges Recap
  • • Voter apathy and low turnout reduce legitimacy.
  • • Whale dominance can centralize decision power.
  • • Legal and regulatory ambiguity across jurisdictions.
Ethical Design Prompts
  1. • Who should have a voice beyond token holders?
  2. • How will you reduce wealth-based influence in decisions about public goods?
  3. • What safeguards protect minority or non-token stakeholders?
Capstone Prompt
Design a small DAO for a community grant program. Define token distribution, voting rules, delegation options, timelock delay, and multisig treasury controls. Describe how you will encourage participation and protect against whale dominance. This exercise synthesizes governance mechanics, security, and ethical design.
