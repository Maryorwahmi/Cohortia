Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Delegated Voting
Delegation lets inactive holders assign voting to trusted parties while retaining token ownership and revocation rights.
Multi-signature Wallet
Requires multiple private keys to approve transactions and is commonly used as a DAO treasury guardian.
Off-chain Voting
Platforms using signed messages enable gas-free, accessible voting that can inform or trigger on-chain actions via multisigs.
Final Takeaway

Combine tooling and governance models thoughtfully: use timelocks and multisigs for treasury safety, pick voting mechanisms that match community goals, and integrate off-chain processes to boost participation.
