Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Decentralized Autonomous Organization
An organization governed by rules encoded as smart contracts and controlled by its members rather than a central authority.
Token-based Governance
Voting power is distributed according to governance token holdings, aligning incentives but risking concentration.
Quadratic Voting
A voting scheme that makes each additional vote cost more, limiting the influence of large holders and encouraging broad preferences.
Summary Takeaway

Choose governance models intentionally: pick mechanisms that align incentives while protecting fairness and security.
