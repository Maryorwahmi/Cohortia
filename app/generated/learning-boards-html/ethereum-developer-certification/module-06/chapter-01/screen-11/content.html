Gas refunds

Deleting a storage variable sets it to zero and produces a gas refund as an incentive to reduce state. Use it when the data is truly obsolete.

Events vs Storage
Emitting events stores data in logs which is cheaper than sstore. Use events for UI updates or indexing, not for persistent on-chain logic.
Cautions
  • Do not delete state merely to capture refunds without clear semantic reason.
  • Events are not accessible to on-chain contracts; they are off-chain signals.
  • Measure the net gas impact of deletes and new writes before refactoring.
