Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Data packing
Packing example
Poor order
uint256 c; uint8 a; uint8 b; — results in three storage slots because uint256 prevents packing.
Packed order
uint8 a; uint8 b; uint256 c; — a and b pack into one slot, c uses another slot, saving a slot versus the poor order.
Supporting notes
  • Group small types together to allow packing into 256-bit slots.
  • Switching types without reordering may not produce savings.
  • Always test gasUsed after changing types and layout.
Analogy
Think of packing like filling boxes: small items grouped together fill a single box instead of leaving wasted space next to large items.
