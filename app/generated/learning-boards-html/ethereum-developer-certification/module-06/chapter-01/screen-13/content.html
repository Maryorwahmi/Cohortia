Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Measuring gas
Recommended steps
  1. Deploy baseline contract locally (Hardhat or Remix).
  2. Run representative transactions and record gasUsed.
  3. Apply a single refactor and retest identical transactions.
  4. Compare results using a gas reporter and document changes.
Practical notes
  • Average multiple runs to avoid noise.
  • Keep functional tests to ensure no behavior regressions.
  • Share simple reports with baseline, updated gas, and percent savings.
Example
Measure addNumber and sumNumbers before and after refactors to show clear, per-function improvements.
