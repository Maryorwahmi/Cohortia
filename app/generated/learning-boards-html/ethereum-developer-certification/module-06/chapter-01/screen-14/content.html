Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Gas
Unit of computational effort used by the EVM and paid in Ether.
Gas Price
Amount of Ether paid per gas unit; set by users and network demand.
Gas Used
Total gas consumed by a transaction; it's the main lever developers can optimize.
Summary
When optimizing, reduce storage writes, prefer memory for temporary data, pack small variables, cache reads in loops, choose external for array inputs, and measure with a gas reporter.
