What is a sidechain?
An independent blockchain running parallel to Ethereum with its own validators and consensus, connected by a two-way bridge for asset movement.
Example: a DeFi app migrates high-frequency operations to a sidechain to lower gas costs and increase throughput, accepting a different security model in exchange.
Security considerations
  • Sidechain validators control finality for that chain.
  • Bridge contracts implement lock and mint mechanics; their custody model is critical.
  • Compromise of a sidechain can endanger assets until bridges and funds are reconciled.
Document threat models and inform users about withdrawal processes when relying on a sidechain.
