Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
Layer 2 (L2)
Off-chain processing anchored to L1 for scalability.
Rollups
Bundle off-chain transactions and post data or proofs to L1.
Optimistic Rollup
Assumes validity; disputes handled during a challenge period.
Summary
These concepts connect performance improvements to the settlement guarantees of Ethereum L1 and shape choices for deployment and user UX.
