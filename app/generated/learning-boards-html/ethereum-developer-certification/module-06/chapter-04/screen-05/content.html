Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Post & Settle
Step-by-step
  1. Sequencer orders transaction pool and creates a batch.
  2. Batch is compressed or processed into a proof or calldata.
  3. One L1 transaction posts the summary or proof.
  4. Settlement occurs on L1: either immediate verification or a challenge window opens.
  5. Users rely on L1 anchors for finality or dispute resolution.
Example: an L2 bundles 2,000 token transfers and posts a single calldata entry. The L1 gas cost is shared by all transfers instead of paying per transfer on-chain.
Supporting notes
  • Optimistic: posts calldata; allows fraud proofs within a challenge period.
  • ZK: posts validity proof that proves correctness; no challenge period is needed.
  • Sequencer role: collects and orders transactions; availability and censorship are operational concerns.
Common Mistake

Assuming all rollups handle data the same way. Always check whether a rollup publishes calldata or only proofs.
