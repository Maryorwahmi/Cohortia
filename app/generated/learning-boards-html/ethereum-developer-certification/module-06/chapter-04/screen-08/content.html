Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Optimistic vs ZK
Optimistic Rollups
  • Proof model: fraud proofs during challenge window.
  • Withdrawal: delayed until challenge period expires.
  • Cost: lower proof cost per batch.
  • Compatibility: many are EVM-friendly today.
ZK-Rollups
  • Proof model: cryptographic validity proofs per batch.
  • Withdrawal: near-instant finality after verification.
  • Cost: higher compute cost for proof generation.
  • Compatibility: tooling may vary; some are not fully EVM identical yet.
Decision example
If your app needs rapid user withdrawals and strong finality, favor ZK-Rollups. If you need low per-transaction cost and can tolerate delayed exits, optimistic approaches may suit you better.
