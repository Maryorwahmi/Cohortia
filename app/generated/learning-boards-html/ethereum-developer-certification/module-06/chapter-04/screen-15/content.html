ZK-Rollup
Uses validity proofs so L1 verification confirms correctness before finality.
Sidechain
Independent chain with its own validators and bridge-based connectivity.
Blockchain Bridge
Moves assets by locking and minting; security depends on the bridge model.
Final takeaway
Match technical guarantees to product requirements, validate assumptions with a local demo, and treat bridges and off-chain actors as critical security surfaces.
