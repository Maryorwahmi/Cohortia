Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › ZK-Rollups
How it works
Off-chain transactions are executed and a cryptographic validity proof is generated that attests the batch's correctness without revealing unnecessary detail.
Concrete example: a ZK rollup generates a single proof for thousands of transfers. Once L1 verifies the proof, state transitions are final and withdrawals proceed without a challenge period.
Trade-offs
  • Stronger finality: immediate verification on L1.
  • Higher compute cost: proof generation can be expensive and complex.
  • Tooling: some ZK systems require special compilers or non-EVM stacks.
Pro Tip

Check EVM compatibility and proof-generation tooling before committing to a ZK rollup for your DApp.
