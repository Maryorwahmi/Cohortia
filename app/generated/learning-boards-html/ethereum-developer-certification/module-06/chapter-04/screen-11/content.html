Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Actors & Roles
Sequencer
Orders transactions and creates batches for L2. Availability and censorship-resistance depend on sequencer design.
Validators / Provers
In ZK-rollups they generate proofs. In sidechains they participate in consensus and finality decisions.
Relayers & owners
Relayers move events between chains. Simple demo bridges use a single owner to release funds, which is not production-safe.
Always document who holds which privileges and reduce single points of trust where possible.
