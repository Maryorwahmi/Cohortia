Introduction to Blockchain Technology
Understand the ledger, its guarantees, and practical implications for systems beyond money.
In this lesson
You will learn decentralization, block structure, hashes, consensus, benefits, and see a short Python demo that links blocks with hashes.
Why it matters
Blockchain provides tamper evidence and shared verification, enabling trusted coordination without a single central authority.
Explore Key Topics
Blocks & Hashes
How transactions are grouped and linked via cryptographic fingerprints for integrity.
Consensus
Mechanisms like Proof of Work and Proof of Stake that let nodes agree on ledger state.
Applications
From supply chains to voting, blockchain's immutability and transparency enable new trust models.
