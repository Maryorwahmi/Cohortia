Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Common Mistakes & Pro-Tips
Common mistakes
  • Equating blockchain solely with cryptocurrency.
  • Accepting a blockchain label without checking decentralization.
  • Assuming hashes provide privacy for stored data.
Pro-tips
  • Model blocks and serialization deterministically to keep hashes stable.
  • Plan off-chain verification so on-chain data reflects real-world truth.
  • Choose consensus based on resource and governance trade-offs.
Key Takeaway
Be explicit about requirements. Build data and verification pipelines that make immutability and decentralization meaningful in your context.
