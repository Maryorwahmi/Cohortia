Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Benefits and Challenges
Benefits
  • Tamper-evident history for auditing
  • Shared verification across organizations
  • Improved provenance and accountability
Concrete use cases
Supply chain tracking, secure medical records, and tamper-proof voting are examples where blockchain's guarantees add value.
Challenges
  • Scalability and transaction throughput limits
  • Regulatory uncertainty across jurisdictions
  • Energy consumption for some consensus designs
  • Complex integration and governance choices
Design advice
Always assess if decentralization and immutability are actual requirements; otherwise the overhead may outweigh benefits.
