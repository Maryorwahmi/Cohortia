Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Blockchain
A distributed, immutable ledger that records transactions across a network.
Decentralization
Distribution of control and validation across many nodes to avoid single points of failure.
Immutability
Once recorded and chained, data is tamper-evident because changing earlier blocks alters hashes.
Summary Takeaway

These concepts — blockchain, decentralization, and immutability — together enable a shared, verifiable history that reduces the need for centralized trust.
