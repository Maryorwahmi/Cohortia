How PoS works
Validators lock up cryptocurrency as stake. Protocol selects validators to propose or attest to blocks. Misbehavior can result in slashing of stake.
Concrete example
A validator stakes tokens, is randomly selected to propose a block, and earns rewards for honest participation or faces penalties for misbehavior.
Misconception
Proof of Stake does not inherently equal centralization. Design choices determine whether stake concentration creates central power or not.
PoS trade-offs
  • Lower energy footprint than Proof of Work
  • Security depends on economic incentives and stake distribution
  • Faster finality can be achieved with proper protocol design
