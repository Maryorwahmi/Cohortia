Application cards
Supply chain
Track goods from origin to consumer to prove provenance and reduce fraud.
Healthcare records
Immutable audit trails for patient consent and record changes to improve data integrity.
Voting & IP
Tamper-proof evidence for auditability in elections and timestamped ownership for intellectual property.
Caveat example
Even if data is stored immutably, blockchain does not validate the original data source. For example, a correct product serial number recorded on-chain only proves the recorded value did not change; it does not prove the serial was correct at the time of recording.
Key Takeaway
Pair blockchain with reliable off-chain verification and governance to realise real-world value.
