Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
Block
Contains transactions, timestamp, previous-hash, and a computed hash.
Cryptographic Hash
A fixed-size fingerprint derived deterministically from input data.
Consensus Mechanism
The protocol nodes use to agree on which blocks are valid and the chain state.
Node
A participant computer that stores and validates ledger data.
Transparency
Transactions are visible and verifiable by network participants.
Use these terms
They form the vocabulary for design discussions and trade-off decisions.
Final takeaway

These compact definitions help you explain architecture, validate integrity, and choose the right consensus model for your application.
