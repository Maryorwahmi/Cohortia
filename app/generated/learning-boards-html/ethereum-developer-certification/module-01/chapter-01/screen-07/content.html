Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Proof of Work
Process
Miners compete by trying many values until one produces a hash meeting the network difficulty target. The winner proposes the block and earns a reward.
Concrete scenario
A miner assembles transactions, iterates nonce values, and checks the block hash. When the hash is valid under difficulty, the block is broadcast and verified by nodes.
Trade-offs
High security and decentralization potential, but energy-intensive and slower to scale compared to some alternatives.
Flow steps
  1. Miner builds candidate block with transactions and timestamp.
  2. Miner iterates a nonce until hash meets difficulty.
  3. Miner broadcasts solution; nodes verify hash and transactions.
  4. Network accepts the block; chain advances and miner earns a reward.
