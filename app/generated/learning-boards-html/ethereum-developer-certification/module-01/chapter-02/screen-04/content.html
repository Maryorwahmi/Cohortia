Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Bitcoin vs Ethereum
Core Concept
Philosophy & Trade-offs
Compare goals, security, and scripting power with practical examples and a common decision rule.
Bitcoin: Secure value transfer, limited scripting, simpler risk profile.
Ethereum: Programmable logic, arbitrary computation, higher surface for complex apps.
Decision rule: Choose Ethereum if your app needs on-chain enforcement of business logic.
Key Takeaway

Different design goals lead to different tooling: Bitcoin for settlement, Ethereum for programmable applications.

Concrete example — Automated escrow
An escrow smart contract holds funds and releases them when predefined conditions are met, with no third party needed.
Steps in escrow contract
  • Client deposits funds into contract.
  • Freelancer submits work and a verification flag is set.
  • Contract logic verifies and releases funds automatically.
Common misconception
Programmability means "free" features — in fact every on-chain operation requires gas and design trade-offs matter.
