Best Practices › Gas Efficiency
Reduce cost by design
Identify expensive patterns and use cheaper alternatives where possible.
Expensive patterns
Storing large arrays, unbounded loops, and heavy initialization increase gas dramatically.
Cheaper alternatives
Use mappings, emit events for history, split work across multiple transactions, and profile gas usage.
Key Takeaway

Plan for gas at the architecture stage and profile functions to prevent expensive surprises for users.

Concrete example
Replace full-array scans with indexed mappings and pagination to avoid O(n) costs in frequent calls.
Common mistake
Assuming off-peak costs eliminate bad patterns — poor design remains costly at any gas price.
