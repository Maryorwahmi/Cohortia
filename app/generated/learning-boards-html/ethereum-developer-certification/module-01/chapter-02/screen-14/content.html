Key Concepts
Three short cards that capture the chapter's essentials.
Smart Contract
A self-executing program stored on-chain that enforces coded rules without intermediaries.
Ethereum Virtual Machine
The replicated, deterministic runtime that executes contract bytecode on every node.
Gas
A unit of computational effort. Total fee = Gas Used × Gas Price, paid in Ether.
Summary takeaway
Use these three anchors when designing and evaluating dApps: what runs on-chain, how it executes, and how much it costs.
