What makes a dApp
An on-chain smart contract backend paired with a user-facing frontend to create a decentralized service.
DeFi: lending, exchanges, and automated market makers.
NFTs: tokenized collectibles with on-chain provenance.
Gaming: on-chain assets and marketplaces.
Supply chain: verifiable tracking and immutable logs.
Key Takeaway

dApps deliver trust-minimized services but require careful design around cost, privacy, and upgradeability.

Concrete example
A lending dApp encodes collateral rules on-chain so loans are issued and liquidated based on contract logic, not a central party.
Misconception
dApps are not automatically private or cheap. On-chain transparency and gas costs are trade-offs developers must manage.
