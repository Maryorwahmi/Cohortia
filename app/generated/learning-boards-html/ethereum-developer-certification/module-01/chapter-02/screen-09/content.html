Calculate transaction fees
Use the formula and understand variables that change cost and speed.
// Formula
// Fee = Gas Used × Gas Price
// Example values shown conceptually
Gas Used = 50000
Gas Price = 20 gwei
Fee (in gwei) = 50000 * 20 = 1,000,000 gwei
// Convert gwei to ether as needed for final fee
Practical example
If a transfer uses 21,000 gas and gas price spikes, the same transfer costs more during congestion. Estimating gas prevents surprises.
Common mistake
Ignoring variable gas price when planning operations — always consider peak and off-peak costs.
Key Takeaway

Both gas used and gas price determine cost; optimize gas use and set appropriate gas price for timeliness.

Why validators care
Validators prioritize transactions by gas price because it determines their reward; higher price increases priority.
User-facing tip
Show estimated fee ranges in your dApp and recommend gas price choices based on current demand.
