Definition › Gas
What gas is and why it matters
A unit that measures the cost of computation; paid in Ether to validators.
Purpose
Prevents infinite loops and resource spam, and rewards validators for work.
Analogy
Think of gas as mileage: operations consume units and you pay for those units with Ether as fuel.
Practical tip
Estimate gas for contract functions and avoid expensive patterns during heavy usage periods.
Key Takeaway

Gas enforces economic limits on computation and aligns incentives for validators through fees.

Common misconception
Gas is not a separate coin — it is a unit you pay for with Ether; fees equal Gas Used times Gas Price.
Why gas price matters
If you set a low gas price, validators prioritize other transactions and your transaction may be delayed or never included.
