Quick reminders
Three short prompts to use during design and review.
Ether is fuel
You pay gas in Ether; no Ether means no on-chain operations.
Contracts are immutable
Plan upgrades carefully and audit before deployment.
Gas shapes choices
Optimize for gas to protect users and keep dApps usable under load.
A short checklist: minimize on-chain state, profile gas, audit contracts, and protect private keys before release.
