Lab + Safety Objectives
Practical steps and safe habits during debugging.
Practice: fix, verify, measure
Lab: VulnerableToken
Start with a failing transfer test. Add logs, run tests, identify the incorrect require, and fix it.
Verify fixes
Re-run the test suite to confirm behavior and all revert paths are covered by tests.
Measure cost
Enable the gas reporter to ensure your fix does not increase gas unexpectedly.
