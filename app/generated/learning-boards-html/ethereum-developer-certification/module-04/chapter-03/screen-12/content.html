Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Gas reporting
Why measure gas
Gas reporting shows the real cost of functions and helps you target optimizations that reduce user fees.
Insights from the reporter
  • Which functions consume the most gas per call.
  • How refactors change gas consumption.
  • Trade-offs between storage writes and computation.
Run example
npx hardhat test --gas-reporter
Practical tip
Optimize hotspots that are called frequently; measure before and after each change to verify real gains.
Key Takeaway

Measure first, prioritize by call frequency, and re-run the reporter after refactors to validate improvements.
