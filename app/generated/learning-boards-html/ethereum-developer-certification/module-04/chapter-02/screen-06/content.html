Process Flow

Token sale integration scenario

1. Deploy
Deploy ERC20Token and TokenSale, setting price and passing token address.
2. Provision
Mint or transfer tokens to TokenSale and approve spending if needed.
3. Purchase
Buyer sends Ether to buyTokens, TokenSale transfers tokens and emits TokensPurchased.
Checks & assertions
  • Contract addresses and initial state are correct after deployment.
  • Buyer token balance increases, sale contract token balance decreases.
  • Sale contract's Ether balance increases by msg.value and a TokensPurchased event is emitted with correct arguments.
  • Edge cases: insufficient Ether, insufficient tokens, and approval failures are asserted and revert as expected.
