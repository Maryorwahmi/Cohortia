Test ideas (Wallet.js)
  • Check owner set on deployment via owner() equals deployer address.
  • Deposit 1 ether and assert contract balance increases using ethers.provider.getBalance(contract.address).
  • Owner withdraws amount: assert contract balance decreases and owner balance increases accordingly.
  • Non-owner withdraw should revert: expect(...).to.be.revertedWith("Only owner") or your message.
  • Withdraw exceeding balance should revert to prevent overdrafts.
Key Takeaway

The Wallet lab connects ownership, Ether transfers, and revert checks into a single practical exercise you can run in Hardhat.
