Chapter 1.2

Mastering the TDD Cycle

Red, Green, Refactor: The Heartbeat of Disciplined Development

This chapter introduces the fundamental Test-Driven Development (TDD) cycle, comprising the Red, Green, and Refactor phases. Learners will understand the purpose and actions within each phase, apply the cycle to develop simple functions, and recognize common pitfalls to avoid for effective TDD practice.

Key Takeaway

The Red, Green, Refactor cycle is the foundational, iterative process that drives Test-Driven Development, ensuring disciplined, test-backed code creation.
