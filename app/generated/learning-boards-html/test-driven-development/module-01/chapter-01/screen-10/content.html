TDD: Quality Over Quantity

Fostering testable, focused, and independently verifiable code.

Misconception: Just More Tests

Many believe TDD is simply about increasing test coverage. While it does, its core value is in using tests to drive *better design*, not just more tests.

Focus: Design Quality

TDD fosters small, focused, and independently testable units of code. This leads to modular, loosely coupled components that are easier to understand, maintain, and reuse.

Example: Monolithic vs. Modular

Instead of one large process_order() function, TDD encourages breaking it into validate_order(), calculate_total(), and save_order(), each with its own test.

Key Takeaway

The true power of TDD lies in its ability to guide you towards superior software design, resulting in a codebase that is inherently more testable, maintainable, and robust.

Anti-Pattern

Writing complex, tightly coupled code first, then struggling to add tests later, often indicates a lack of TDD principles.
