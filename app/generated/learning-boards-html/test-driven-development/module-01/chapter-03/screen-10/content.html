Crafting Effective Tests

Balancing Quality, Coverage, and Maintainability

Key Idea

The challenge lies in writing tests that are robust, isolated, and appropriately scoped, avoiding brittleness or excessive overhead.

Key Takeaway

Invest in learning to write clear, concise, and behavior-focused tests, and continuously refine your approach to test coverage.

Pro-Tip

Focus on testing the public API and observable behavior of your units, rather than internal implementation details.

Test Quality vs. Quantity

Writing Good Tests is Hard

Tests can be brittle, coupled to implementation, or slow, becoming a maintenance burden.

Over-Testing vs. Under-Testing

Finding the right balance of test coverage to ensure robustness without excessive overhead.

Effective testing requires a balance of quality, scope, and maintainability.
