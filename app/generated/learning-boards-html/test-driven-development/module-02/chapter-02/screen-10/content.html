Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › assertEqual vs specialized assertions
Comparison
Specific vs generic

Use assertEqual for exact value equality. Use specialized assertions when the property under test is membership, truthiness, identity, a relation, or tolerance.

Examples
  • assertIn(element, container) instead of assertTrue(element in container)
  • assertIsNone(value) instead of assertEqual(value, None)
  • assertAlmostEqual for float comparisons
Misconception

Using assertEqual exclusively does not make tests simpler; it often hides the specific property you intended to test and leads to less informative failures.

Key Takeaway

Choose the assertion that directly states the condition you care about. Readability of tests is as important as correctness.
