Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Big-bang rewrite vs incremental
Big-bang rewrite
  • High initial cost and schedule risk.
  • Likely to miss undocumented behaviors.
  • Long feedback loops and integration pain.
Incremental, test-backed approach
  • Low-risk, small changes protected by tests.
  • Deliverable improvements and early validation.
  • Supports Strangler Fig migration and partial replacement.
Key takeaway: opt for incremental, test-driven improvements wherever possible.
