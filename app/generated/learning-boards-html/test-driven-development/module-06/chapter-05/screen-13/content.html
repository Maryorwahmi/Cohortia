Chapter Review

Key Concepts

Essential ideas to remember from this chapter

Continuous Integration (CI)

Developers frequently merge code, triggering automated builds and tests for rapid feedback.

Continuous Delivery (CD)

Ensuring software can be released reliably and frequently to production at any time.

DevOps Culture

Practices combining Dev and Ops to shorten cycles and provide continuous delivery with high quality.

Summary Takeaway

TDD is a fundamental enabler for CI/CD and DevOps, fostering a culture of quality, collaboration, and rapid feedback throughout the software delivery lifecycle.
