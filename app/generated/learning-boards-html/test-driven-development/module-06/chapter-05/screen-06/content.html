Strategic Advantage

TDD Enabling Continuous Delivery

Building confidence for reliable and frequent software releases

CD Benefits with TDD

  • •High confidence in codebase for automated deployments.
  • •Reduced risk associated with frequent releases.
  • •Enables multiple deployments to production daily.
  • •Promotes modular, loosely coupled, deployable code.
Key Takeaway

TDD is the bedrock for Continuous Delivery, transforming risky deployments into routine, confident releases through comprehensive test coverage.

Real-world Connection

Companies like Amazon and Netflix deploy thousands of times a day. This scale of CD is only possible because of deep automation and a high degree of trust in their test suites, heavily influenced by TDD principles.
