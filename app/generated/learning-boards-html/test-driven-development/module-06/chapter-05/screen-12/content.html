Avoiding Common Pitfalls in CI/CD with TDD

Maintaining a healthy and trustworthy test suite and pipeline

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • •Slow Pipelines: Discourages frequent commits.
  • •Flaky Tests: Erodes trust and leads to ignored failures.
  • •Insufficient Coverage: Allows subtle bugs to slip through.
Key Takeaway

Proactively address pipeline speed, test flakiness, and coverage gaps to ensure TDD and CI/CD deliver their full value.

Safety Note

Monitor test coverage, but focus on *meaningful* coverage of critical paths rather than blindly chasing 100% coverage.
