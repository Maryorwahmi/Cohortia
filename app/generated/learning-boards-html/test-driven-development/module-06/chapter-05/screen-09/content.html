Shift-Left Quality with TDD

Proactively addressing quality concerns early in the development lifecycle

Benefits of Shifting Left

  • •Defects identified and fixed earlier.
  • •Significantly reduces cost and effort of bug resolution.
  • •Leads to more robust and resilient systems.
  • •Reduces burden on later-stage QA and operations.
Key Takeaway

TDD is a primary driver for 'shift-left' quality, embedding defect prevention and early validation into the core development process.

Analogy

Think of it like building a house: it's far cheaper to fix a blueprint error than to tear down and rebuild a wall after the foundation is laid.

Traditional vs. Shift-Left Quality
