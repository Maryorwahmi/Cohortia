Key Concepts (Continued)

Further essential ideas to remember from this chapter

Shift-Left Testing

Moving testing activities to earlier stages of the SDLC to find and fix defects sooner.

Automated Test Suite

A collection of tests (unit, integration, E2E) that run automatically, providing rapid feedback.

Meaningful Coverage

Focusing test efforts on critical paths and complex logic, not just a percentage target.

Summary Takeaway

By embracing 'shift-left' quality and building a robust automated test suite through TDD, teams can achieve higher quality software with greater efficiency and confidence.
