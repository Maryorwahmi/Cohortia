Learning Objectives (continued)
These four hands-on objectives map directly to the normalize_text activity and assessment questions.
05
Design generators that match the domain
You will choose strategies that produce empty, long, and special-character strings so tests exercise realistic and edge inputs.
Mnemonic: a small venn of example and edge-case icons.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06
Write properties for idempotence and invariants
You will assert that applying a transform twice equals applying it once, and that outputs match structural invariants like length or element membership.
Mnemonic: double-arrow idempotence icon.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Interpret shrunk counterexamples
You will analyze a minimal failing input reported by the framework and decide whether it reveals a bug or a mistaken property.
Mnemonic: magnifying glass over a small list.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Avoid common PBT mistakes
You will recognize weak properties, insufficient generators, and when side effects make PBT unsuitable so you can choose the right testing mix.
Mnemonic: caution triangle with a test icon.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
