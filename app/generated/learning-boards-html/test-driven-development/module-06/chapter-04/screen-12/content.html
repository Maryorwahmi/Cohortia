Common Mistakes
Pitfalls and Limitations of PBT
Practical guidance to keep your PBT suite useful and stable.
Weak or Overly-Strong Properties
Ensure properties express the contract clearly. Test both necessary and sufficient invariants.
Poor Generators
Include edge cases like empty and extreme values; sample generators to confirm coverage.
Side Effects & External Dependencies
Avoid applying PBT directly to non-deterministic systems without isolation and mocking.
Performance
Run long exploratory tests in scheduled CI; use targeted runs locally.
Debugging
Use the shrunk example to decide whether to fix code, adjust generators, or refine the property.
