When PBT Excels (and When Not)
Pick PBT for deterministic logic; prefer examples for side effects and integration.
Great fits for PBT
  • • Pure functions with no side effects.
  • • Data transformations: parse, normalize, serialize.
  • • Mathematical and idempotent operations.
When to avoid PBT alone
  • • Functions with complex external dependencies or nondeterminism.
  • • End-to-end UI interactions without isolating logic.
  • • Cases where modeling the domain is impractical.
Real-world connection: use PBT to harden parsing and normalization utilities inside your system, but keep integration tests to validate end-to-end behavior across services.
