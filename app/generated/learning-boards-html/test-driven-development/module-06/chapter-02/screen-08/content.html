Fast feedback

Reasoned integration

Strategic E2E
Tune E2E coverage to business-critical flows only.
Example: CI impact
A suite heavy with E2E tests turns CI into a developer bottleneck; broad unit coverage preserves quick feedback and reduces the need for many E2E checks.
Key Takeaway

Measure test costs and prioritize fast checks; use slower tests sparingly for high-value assurance.
