Questions & troubleshooting
MCQ example
Why did the commit succeed even though pytest showed failures? Correct answer: the hook script did not exit with a non‑zero status code on failure.
Short answer
Explain two benefits of CI even with local hooks: consistent environment catches integration issues, and team visibility prevents broken merges.
Troubleshooting checklist: compare dependency lists, inspect CI logs for the failing step, and reproduce the failure locally using a clean virtual environment to isolate causes.
