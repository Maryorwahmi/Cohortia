Isolation, Speed, Realism

Consider what you are willing to trade: fast feedback or realistic coverage. Different features need different balances.
Unit: high isolation, very fast, low realism.
Integration: medium realism, medium speed, tests contracts.
Acceptance: high realism, slow, high maintenance cost.
Decision heuristics
  • Protect critical business flows with acceptance tests.
  • Protect seams and integrations with focused integration tests.
  • Protect algorithmic and local logic with unit tests.
Practical rule

Use the pyramid to guide allocation, but adjust based on risk and feedback needs.
