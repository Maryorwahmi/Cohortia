Extending TDD

Behavior-Driven Development (BDD)

Collaborative, user-centric development

Definition

BDD is an agile process that extends TDD by emphasizing collaboration among developers, QA, and business participants to achieve a shared understanding of desired behavior through concrete examples.

  • Focuses on writing executable specifications, not just tests.
  • Uses a ubiquitous language for shared understanding.
  • Reduces misunderstandings and costly rework.
Key Takeaway

BDD enhances collaboration and ensures software aligns with business vision by focusing on shared, executable specifications of behavior.

BDD: Bridging the Gap

BDD Example

Instead of "test_add_item_updates_total", a BDD scenario is: "Given a user has an empty cart, When they add 'Laptop', Then the cart total should be $1200."
