Definition
Over-mocking — Why Tests Become Brittle
Identify brittle assertions and replace them with public contract checks
Symptom
Many failing tests after internal refactor despite unchanged public behavior.
Cause
Assertions tied to internal method calls or call order rather than external effects.
Fix
Refactor tests to assert public outcomes, or limit mocks to collaborators that represent clear boundaries.
Warning

Excessive internal assertions increase maintenance cost and slow refactoring. Prefer fewer, meaningful interaction checks.
