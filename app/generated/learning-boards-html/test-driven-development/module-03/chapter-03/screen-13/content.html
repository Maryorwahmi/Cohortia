Final Practice
Test Design Checklist
A short sequence to review before committing tests
1. Define behavior
What public effect must the unit guarantee?
2. Decide mocking
Mock slow, external, or side-effect collaborators only.
3. Configure mock
Use spec and set return_value when consumed by the SUT.
4. Choose assertion
Pick exact, any-call, or not-called depending on strictness.
5. Simulate failures
Use side_effect to verify error handling.
6. Avoid internals
Keep assertions focused on behavior consumers depend on.
Key Takeaway

A short checklist helps you write interaction tests that verify behavior while remaining resilient to internal refactoring.
