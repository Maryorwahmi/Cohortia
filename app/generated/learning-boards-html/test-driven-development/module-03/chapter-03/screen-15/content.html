What to Remember
Three simple, practical rules
1. Mock side effects
Email, audit logs, and external APIs belong behind mocks in unit tests.
2. Configure safely
Use spec and return_value to align mocks with real collaborator behavior.
3. Test behavior, not choreography
Verify public outcomes to keep tests resilient and meaningful.
Final takeaway

Use mocks deliberately to verify behavior, configure them for realism, and avoid coupling tests to implementation details.
