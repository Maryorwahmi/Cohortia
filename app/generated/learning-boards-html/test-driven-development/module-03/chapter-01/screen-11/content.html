Pitfall Alert
Over-mocking
Tests that assert every internal call become brittle. Focus on public contract and essential interactions.
Example
Asserting the exact order of helper calls inside a method often couples the test to implementation.
Over-complex fakes
Fakes that replicate production complexity duplicate bugs and increase maintenance cost.
Remedy
Prefer small fakes focused on the behaviors your tests require, and cover integrations with dedicated integration tests.
Checklist to avoid mistakes
  • Ask what the test is trying to prove: data, interaction, or state.
  • Use the simplest double that gives confidence.
  • Document assumptions your double encodes so maintenance is easier.
