Definition
Mock — verify interactions
Use mocks to assert that the unit called its collaborator correctly.
When to use
When sending notifications, logging important events, or triggering side effects is part of the behavior under test.
Misconception
Mocks should not be used to assert every internal call; focus on meaningful interactions that constitute the unit's contract.
Key Takeaway

Use mocks to verify side effects that are essential to the unit’s behavior, and avoid asserting incidental implementation details.

Concrete mock example
When registering a user, verify an EmailService mock received a call to send_welcome_email with expected args.
# Pseudo-code illustrating a mock assertion
email_mock = MockEmailService()
repo_stub = UserRepositoryStub()
service = UserService(repo_stub, email_mock)
service.register_user('alice', 'alice@example.com')
# Verify that send_welcome_email was called once with email and username
assert email_mock.assert_called_with('alice@example.com', 'alice')
Note: the mock both stands in for the service and records the call for verification.
