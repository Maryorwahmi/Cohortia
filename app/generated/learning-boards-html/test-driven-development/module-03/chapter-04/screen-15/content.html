Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Quick Reference
When to fake
Stateful flows and multiple dependent calls that benefit from deterministic in-memory behavior.
When to spy
When you need to observe an important internal call but keep its real effect during the test.
Daily habit
Start simple, escalate only when necessary, and document limitations of test doubles.
Final Tip
A small, well-documented fake plus a focused spy often beats a large mock-heavy suite in clarity and speed.
