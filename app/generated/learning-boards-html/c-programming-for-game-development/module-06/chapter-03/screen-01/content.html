Parallelism and Concurrency
Understand when tasks actually run at the same time versus when they are managed together.
Why it matters
Higher frame rates and smoother experience by leveraging multiple CPU cores.
What you'll do
Use std::thread, std::mutex, and std::async to parallelize work and protect shared data.
In this lesson
Distinguish concurrency vs parallelism, write threads, prevent race conditions, and prefer task-based APIs when appropriate.
