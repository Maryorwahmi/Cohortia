Polymorphism & Virtual Functions

Understand dynamic dispatch, avoid slicing, and design safe polymorphic hierarchies.

16:9 HD Classroom
In this lesson

How virtual functions enable late binding; practical examples with Animal and Shape hierarchies; override and final best practices; why destructors must be virtual.

Why it matters

Polymorphism reduces conditional logic, supports plugin-style designs, and keeps code maintainable as systems grow and types multiply.
