Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Practice & Safety
What you'll practice
  • Transform & accumulate: Map values and reduce results without manual index arithmetic.
  • Remove-erase idiom: Use std::remove_if then container.erase to actually remove elements.
  • Custom sort: Provide a comparator lambda that captures nothing or only what it needs.
  • Iterator awareness: Match algorithms with iterator categories to avoid compile-time errors or slow code.
Quick note

Prefer std::vector unless a use case requires frequent middle insertions or stable iterators; understand erase complexity for your chosen container.
