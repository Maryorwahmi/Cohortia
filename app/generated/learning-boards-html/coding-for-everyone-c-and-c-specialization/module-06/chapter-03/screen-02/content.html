Bloom's progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Concept
Iterators as generalized pointers
Describe what iterators do and why begin and end form a half-open range. Knowing this lets you reason about algorithms without knowing container internals.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Category
Iterator categories and capabilities
Differentiate input, output, forward, bidirectional, and random access iterators and match each to supported operations and example containers.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply
Use STL algorithms with lambdas
Compose std::sort, std::find, std::for_each, std::transform, and std::count_if with lambda predicates to perform transformations and queries.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Safety
Avoid iterator invalidation and use erase-remove
Recognize operations that invalidate iterators and apply the erase-remove idiom to filter containers safely.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
