Architecture
Designing IAM for Multi-Cloud
Map roles to duties, avoid broad permissions, and enforce continuous review across cloud providers.
Role mapping
Create roles aligned to job functions and attach only the permissions necessary for that role.
Policy constraints
Limit actions to resources and use conditions such as source IPs or MFA requirement when available.
Lifecycle & audits
Review policies regularly and remove stale permissions to reduce privilege creep.
Concrete example
A service role used by an application is given read-only access to a specific bucket ARN; it is not allowed to list other buckets or perform write actions.
Common mistake
Granting ‘‘Allow" for all actions on all resources with a '*' action or resource. This creates easy privilege escalation paths.
Key Takeaway

Implement least privilege and audit IAM continuously. Make role definitions part of your deployment pipeline.
