Definition
Encryption & Bucket Policies
Combine encryption with access policies to protect data and limit exposure.
Encryption at rest
Enable provider-managed or customer-managed keys to ensure data remains unreadable if storage is compromised.
Encryption in transit
Use TLS for service-to-service and client-to-service communication.
Bucket policies & IAM
Grant access to specific principals and deny public access when not needed.
Concrete example
Enable "Block all public access", set default encryption to SSE-S3 or SSE-KMS, and enable versioning to recover from accidental deletion.
Validation checks
  • Attempt to list the bucket with a read-only user; confirm listing is denied.
  • Try to upload with the same user; confirm PutObject is denied.
  • Check logs for access attempts and enable alerts for public exposure.
Key Takeaway

Encryption and strict bucket policies prevent accidental public data exposure and support recovery workflows.
