Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › IDS and IPS
Definition

Intrusion Detection System (IDS)

Passive monitoring that generates alerts for suspicious patterns without blocking traffic.

Use cases
Monitoring, forensics, baseline tuning, and early detection.
Definition

Intrusion Prevention System (IPS)

In-line device that inspects traffic and can drop or block malicious packets in real-time.

Use cases
Stop active attacks at perimeter choke points or protect critical servers inline.
Example: NIPS vs HIPS

Place NIPS at perimeter choke points to catch network scans and suspicious flows. Deploy HIPS on critical hosts to detect local exploitation or suspicious process behavior. Use IDS where you prefer visibility without risk of blocking.
