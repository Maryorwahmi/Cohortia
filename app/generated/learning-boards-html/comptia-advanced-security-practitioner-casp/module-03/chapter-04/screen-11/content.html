Feed types and uses

IP addresses: high-value for automated blocking when confidence is high.
Domains/URLs: useful for URL filtering and SIEM enrichment; often require validation.
File hashes: precise for blocking malware once confirmed.
Integration pattern
Automate ingestion, apply confidence thresholds, block high-confidence indicators, route other indicators to SIEM for correlation and analyst review.
Operational caution

Do not auto-block everything from feeds. Validate impact in monitoring mode and maintain allowlists for essential business services.
