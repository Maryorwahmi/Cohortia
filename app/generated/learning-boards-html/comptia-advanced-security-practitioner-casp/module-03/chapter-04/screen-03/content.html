Hands-on Skills

Four concrete practice objectives for the lab walkthrough.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01 Configure App & URL Policies

Allow HTTP/HTTPS but block P2P and risky categories. This keeps productive traffic flowing while reducing exposure to malware and C2 sites.

02 Place NIPS & HIPS

Put NIPS at perimeter choke points and HIPS on critical hosts to capture network and host-level indicators of compromise.

03 Tune IPS Signatures

Identify noisy rules, confirm legitimate behavior, and create exceptions or custom signatures to prevent service disruption.

04 Ingest Threat Feeds

Prioritize IPs, domains, and file hashes for automated blocking and feed them into NGFWs and IPS for proactive defense.
