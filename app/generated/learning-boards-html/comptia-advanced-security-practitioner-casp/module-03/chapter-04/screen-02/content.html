Learning Objectives

Four competencies you'll verify by chapter end.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate NGFW vs Traditional

Explain DPI, application and user awareness and why NGFWs let you enforce policies beyond ports. This matters when attackers tunnel traffic over common ports.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Design IPS/IDS Deployments

Place NIPS at choke points and HIPS on critical hosts, and create a tuning plan to minimize false positives while preserving detection coverage.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Integrate with SIEM

Aggregate firewall and IPS logs in a SIEM to correlate events from multiple sources and detect complex attack patterns that individual devices miss.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Use Threat Intelligence

Prioritize indicators for automated blocking and feed trusted threat lists into NGFW and IPS to proactively reduce risk.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
