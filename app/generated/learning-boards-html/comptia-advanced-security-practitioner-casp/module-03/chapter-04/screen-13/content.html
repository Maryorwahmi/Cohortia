Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Lab Walkthrough

Lab steps

  1. Configure NGFW app control and URL filtering in monitoring mode; log all matches.
  2. Deploy NIPS at perimeter and HIPS on a critical host; collect baseline alerts.
  3. Forward NGFW and IPS logs to SIEM for correlation and dashboarding.
  4. Identify noisy IPS signatures; verify with app owners, then create scoped exceptions or custom signatures.
  5. Integrate threat feed; auto-block high-confidence IPs, route lower-confidence indicators to SIEM.
Checkpoint: Did blocking affect business services?
Checkpoint: Have you documented exceptions and retention?
Checkpoint: Are SIEM correlations tuned to reduce noise?
