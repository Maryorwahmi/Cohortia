Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Secure Management

Use SSHv2 and SNMPv3 with scoped views and key-based auth to protect administrative access and monitoring.

Protect Services

Authenticate NTP and deploy DNSSEC while hardening the servers and transport to preserve timestamps and name integrity.

Plane Protections

Apply CoPP to rate-limit CPU-targeted traffic and MPP to restrict where management connections are accepted.

Summary

Secure protocols, hardened services, and control/management plane protections together form a repeatable baseline for resilient network operations.
