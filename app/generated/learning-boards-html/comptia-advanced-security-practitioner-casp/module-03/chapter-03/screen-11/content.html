Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Management Plane Protection (MPP)
Concept

Restrict management access

MPP ensures administrative traffic is accepted only on approved interfaces or from designated IP ranges, reducing exposure if other interfaces are compromised.

Key Takeaway

MPP reduces attack surface by limiting management paths; combine it with strong auth for defense-in-depth.

Concrete example

Configure device to accept SSH and SNMP only on management interface Gig0/0 and from the operations subnet 10.254.0.0/24. Deny management from user VLANs and enable logging of denied attempts for audit.

Misconception

Some teams assume MPP replaces authentication. It does not. MPP narrows entry points; authentication must still be strong to prevent credential misuse on allowed paths.
