Hardening Network Devices and Services

Practical controls and configuration patterns to secure routers, switches, and firewalls against compromise and DoS targeting device CPUs.

Device-first mindset
Protect management and control planes
Why this chapter matters

Compromised network devices can stop traffic, hide attacker activity, or be used as a foothold. Hardening prevents easy access and preserves logs, TLS, and routing integrity. The controls you will practice are practical, repeatable, and prioritized for enterprise deployments.

Outcomes
  • Secure administrative protocols (SSH, SNMPv3)
  • Protect NTP and DNS responses
  • Apply CoPP and MPP to preserve CPU and access
