Learning Objectives

Skills to configure, verify, and defend network devices and services.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Device Hardening
Implement advanced hardening for routers, switches and firewalls, remove defaults, and close unused ports so the device attack surface is minimal.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Secure Management Protocols
Configure SSHv2, SNMPv3 and NetFlow/IPFIX with encryption and access controls so administrative and telemetry data remain confidential and reliable.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Protect Time and DNS
Configure authenticated NTP and plan DNSSEC deployment so logs remain accurate and name resolution cannot be silently spoofed.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Control & Management Plane Defense
Design and apply CoPP and MPP to limit CPU-directed traffic and restrict management access to authorized interfaces and addresses.
Verified Competency Milestone
