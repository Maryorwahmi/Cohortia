What to Remember

WPA3 Enterprise
Stronger key establishment and forward secrecy with SAE and 802.1X.
802.1X
Port-based access control that outsources authentication to RADIUS.
EAP Methods
EAP-TLS for mutual certs; PEAP/TTLS for server-side tunnel with inner auth.
Summary Takeaway
Secure enterprise Wi‑Fi requires authentication, certificate management, and operational checks. Plan those three together rather than in isolation.
