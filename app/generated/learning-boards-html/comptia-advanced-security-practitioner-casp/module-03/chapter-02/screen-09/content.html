Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › MAM & BYOD Strategies
Comparison

MAM for BYOD

MAM protects apps and corporate data, enabling selective wipe and preventing data sharing outside approved apps.

How MAM Works
Containerizes corporate apps and data, enforces app-level policies, and restricts data transfer between managed and unmanaged apps.
Concrete BYOD Example
Corporate email runs in a managed app; administrators can wipe only the corporate container when an employee leaves, leaving personal apps intact.
Misconception
Treating MAM as a replacement for all MDM features. In some cases you may need both: MDM for corporate devices, MAM for BYOD.
Policy Card
MAM Policy Example
Require app-level authentication, encrypt corporate data at rest in managed apps, block copy/paste to unmanaged apps, and allow selective wipe of corporate containers.
Key Takeaway

Use MAM to protect corporate data on personal devices while preserving user privacy; combine with MDM for devices under full corporate control.
