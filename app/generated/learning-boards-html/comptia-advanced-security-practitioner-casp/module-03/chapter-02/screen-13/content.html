What to Remember

MDM
Device-level control for corporate-owned devices, enabling remote wipe and enforced settings.
MAM
App-level protections and containerization to protect corporate data on personal devices.
Rogue AP & Guest Network
Detect rogues with WIDS and secure guest networks with VLAN/firewall isolation and captive portals.
Final Reflection
Decide whether to require device enrollment or to rely on app controls for BYOD, and pair your choice with detection and isolation strategies to maintain security across the extended perimeter.
