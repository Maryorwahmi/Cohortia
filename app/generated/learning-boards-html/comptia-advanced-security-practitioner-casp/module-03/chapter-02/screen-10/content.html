Threat Defense

Detecting and Preventing Rogue APs

Combine RF monitoring, active scans, port security, and physical audits to find and eliminate unauthorized access points.

Detection Methods
  • WIDS: monitor for unknown SSIDs and anomalous RF activity.
  • Active scanning: administrators scan wired and wireless networks for shadow APs.
  • Physical inspection: verify wiring closets and user areas.
Prevention Techniques
  • Switch port security: limit MAC addresses and disable unused ports.
  • Strict physical controls over network jacks and wiring closets.
Rogue Response Card
Response Checklist
  1. Confirm WIDS alert and capture AP details (SSID, BSSID).
  2. Perform a directed RF and wired port scan to locate source.
  3. Physically inspect and disconnect device; disable switch port if unauthorized.
  4. Document incident and update security controls.
Key Takeaway

Treat rogue AP defense as a program: automated WIDS alerts plus manual audits and port-level prevention yield the best protection.
