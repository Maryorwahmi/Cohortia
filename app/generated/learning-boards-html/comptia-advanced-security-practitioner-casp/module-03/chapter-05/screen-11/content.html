EDR: Beyond Traditional Antivirus

Continuous monitoring and advanced threat detection

Traditional Antivirus (AV)
  • •Primarily signature-based detection.
  • •Detects known malware patterns.
  • •Limited visibility into post-infection activity.
  • •Struggles with fileless malware and zero-days.
Analogy: A security guard checking IDs against a list of known troublemakers.
Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)
  • •Continuous monitoring of endpoint activity.
  • •Uses AI/ML, behavioral analysis for detection.
  • •Detects sophisticated threats (fileless, zero-day, APTs).
  • •Provides deep visibility, investigation, and rapid response.
Analogy: A team of detectives constantly observing, analyzing behavior, and responding to any suspicious activity.
Key Takeaway

EDR provides a proactive, intelligent defense against advanced threats by continuously monitoring endpoint behavior, far surpassing traditional signature-based antivirus.
