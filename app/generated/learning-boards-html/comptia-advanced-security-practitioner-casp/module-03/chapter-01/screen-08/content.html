Definition

Microsegmentation and SDN/NFV

Microsegmentation ties policies to workloads so each app or tier has dedicated rules. SDN and NFV provide the control and enforcement mechanisms for that approach.

Example policy
Allow only application servers on port 5432 to reach database servers; block all other east-west flows between those tiers.
Risk to watch
The SDN controller is high value. If attackers manipulate policies centrally they can subvert microsegmentation across the estate.
Operational guidance
  • Harden the SDN controller and restrict administrative access.
  • Use change control and logging for policy distribution.
  • Test policies in a staging environment before wide rollout.
Pro tip

Start microsegmentation with a small application or tier, iterate on policies, and automate policy verification to avoid accidental over-permissiveness.
