Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Secure Backups
Security controls
  • Encryption at rest and in transit with managed keys and rotation.
  • Access controls using least privilege and MFA for administration.
  • Dedicated backup networks or VLANs to isolate traffic.
  • Immutable storage options and air-gapped media for ransomware protection.
Integrity verification
Automate checksum creation and retention, and schedule periodic full restores to an isolated environment to validate recovery procedures.
Common mistakes
Assuming encryption alone solves all risks. If keys or decryption procedures are unavailable during recovery, encrypted backups are unusable. Also, backups on the same network and credentials as production may be compromised simultaneously by ransomware.
Key Takeaway

Security and recoverability go together: protect keys, isolate backup access, and test restorations to ensure backups are usable when needed.
