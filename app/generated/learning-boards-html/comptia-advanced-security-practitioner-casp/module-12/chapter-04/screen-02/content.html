Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Learning Objectives
01
Evaluate backup types
Decide when to use full, incremental, or differential based on RPO and storage trade-offs.
Mnemonic: "Map RPO to Type" — pick the backup cadence that meets your point-of-loss tolerance.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Analyze storage options
Compare on-site, off-site, and cloud for speed, durability, and regulatory constraints.
Mnemonic: "3-2-1" — always plan three copies, two media types, one off-site.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design replication & snapshots
Choose replication or snapshots when RPO demands near-real-time protection.
Mnemonic: "Replicate to replicate loss" — use streaming replication or SAN replication for minimal data loss.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Implement secure backups
Apply encryption, key management, least privilege, and integrity verification to backup data.
Mnemonic: "Encrypt, Verify, Isolate" — protect backups at rest and in transit and test restores.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
