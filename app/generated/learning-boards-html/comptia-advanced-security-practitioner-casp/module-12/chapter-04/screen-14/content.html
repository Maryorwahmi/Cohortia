Full / Incremental / Differential
Different restore complexity and storage cost trade-offs.
3-2-1 Rule
Three copies, two media types, one off-site for resilience.
Replication & Snapshots
Good for low RPO but not a replacement for off-site backups.
Summary takeaway
Design backups for recoverability: test restores, secure backups, and keep isolated off-site copies to survive disasters.
