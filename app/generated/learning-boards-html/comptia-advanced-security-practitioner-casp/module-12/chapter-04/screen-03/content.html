05
Create recovery plans
Write restoration order, roles, verification and rollback procedures for critical services.
Mnemonic: "Inventory, Order, Verify" — a plan without order and verification is risky.

06
Test restores regularly
Perform checksum validation and restore tests to ensure backups remain usable.
Mnemonic: "Test to Trust" — assume backups may fail unless you test them.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Secure backup access
Implement least privilege, MFA, and separate networks for backup traffic when possible.
Mnemonic: "Separate and Limit" — restrict who and what can touch backups.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Plan for ransomware resistance
Use immutability and air-gapped copies to protect backups from compromise or encryption.
Mnemonic: "Lock and Isolate" — immutability and isolation reduce ransomware risk.
