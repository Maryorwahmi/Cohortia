Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Why backups matter
What makes a backup usable?
  • Integrity: checksums and periodic restore tests confirm data is intact.
  • Security: encryption at rest and in transit, with careful key management.
  • Versioning: clear inventory of backup points and retention policies.
  • Accessibility: stored where it can be retrieved within RTO constraints.
Concrete example
An organization with RPO 4 hours uses hourly snapshots plus daily encrypted off-site backups and weekly full backups to ensure multiple recoverable points.
Key Takeaway

Backups must be treated as critical, tested, and secured artifacts; otherwise recovery will fail when needed most.

Common misconception
"If it backed up, it is safe."
This is false unless you verify backups with integrity checks and test restores. Backups can be corrupted, incomplete, or encrypted by ransomware if protections are absent.
Pro tip
Automate checksum generation and keep a small, frequently restored sample set to validate workflows without disrupting production.
