Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Backup Schedules
Design steps
  1. Identify RPO and RTO for each service.
  2. Choose anchor full backup frequency (daily/weekly) based on retention and restore speed.
  3. Add differential or incremental runs to reduce daily windows and storage.
  4. Introduce replication or continuous protection for services with near-zero RPO.
  5. Schedule regular restore tests and adjust cadence as needed.
Example schedule
Full: weekly on Sunday 02:00. Differential: Monday–Saturday 02:00. Transaction log archive: every 15 minutes for critical DBs.
Operational tips
  • Avoid large backup jobs during peak hours to protect performance.
  • Monitor backup success and retention policies to prevent accidental deletions.
  • Validate restores under time constraints to confirm actual RTO.
Common mistake
Selecting the fastest backup type without testing restores can create hidden recovery gaps.
