Module 12 › Chapter 12.4 › Overview
Data Backup and Recovery Strategies
Design recoverable, secure, and verifiable backups that meet business RPO and RTO requirements.
In this lesson
You will evaluate backup types and schedules, choose media and locations, and learn secure practices and recovery plan essentials.
Why it matters
Without verified, secure backups, recovery fails and business continuity is at risk. Your design choices determine recovery fidelity and risk exposure.
Coming up
Practical schedules, replication patterns, snapshot uses, and a sample recovery procedure to practice with.
Explore Key Topics
Backup Types
Full, incremental, differential: trade-offs affect storage and restore complexity.
Storage & 3-2-1
On-site, off-site, cloud: keep copies on different media and one off-site for resilience.
Security & Testing
Encrypt, control access, verify integrity, and test restores regularly to ensure recoverability.
