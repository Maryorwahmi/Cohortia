Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Warm Standby Activity
Step 1

Choose Warm Standby

A scaled-down, ready environment enables quicker failover than pilot light and costs less than active-active multi-site.

Step 2: IaC Template Structure
  • Network: VPC, subnets, route tables, security groups.
  • Compute: Auto Scaling Group with a minimal instance count and AMI definitions.
  • Database: RDS read replica or cross-region replication settings with point-in-time recovery.
  • Storage: S3 with cross-region replication for static content.
  • DNS: Route 53 failover records and health checks.
Example Commands
# Trigger a DR runbook with Ansible (conceptual example)
ansible-playbook -i inventory/recovery_site.ini playbooks/dr_full_recovery.yml --extra-vars "target_region=us-east-2"

# Deploy a recovery CloudFormation stack
aws cloudformation deploy --template-file cloudformation/dr_stack.yaml --stack-name ProductionAppDR --region us-east-2 --capabilities CAPABILITY_IAM

# Azure Site Recovery conceptual failover command
az site-recovery failover-planned --resource-group myResourceGroup --name myRecoveryPlan --direction primaryToRecovery
Benefits of IaC
  • Consistency and rapid provisioning to meet RTO.
  • Version-controlled infrastructure for audit and rollback.
  • Automated dry runs reduce surprise during real incidents.
