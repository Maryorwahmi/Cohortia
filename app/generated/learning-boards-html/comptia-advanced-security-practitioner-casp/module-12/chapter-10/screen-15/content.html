Cyber Resilience

Integrate threat anticipation, detection, and secure recovery so systems are safe when returned to service.

Orchestration

Automate multi-step recovery workflows to ensure correct sequencing and reduce manual errors during DR execution.

Predictive Resilience

Use telemetry and ML to predict failures and trigger preemptive recovery actions, reducing the chance of major outages.

Final Note

Combine these levers into automated, testable runbooks to achieve continuous cyber resilience.
