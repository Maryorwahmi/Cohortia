Module 12 › Chapter 12.10 › Learning Objectives
01 — Analyze cloud impact
Examine how cloud services replace traditional hot/warm/cold sites and how patterns like cross-region replication and managed databases affect RTO and RPO decisions. This skill helps you recommend cost-effective, verifiable recovery options for critical workloads.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Evaluate AI/ML uses
Assess predictive analytics, anomaly detection, and intelligent recovery automation, including model training risks and false positive impacts. You'll learn when to rely on ML signals and how to validate their outputs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Differentiate cyber resilience
Understand how cyber resilience extends DRP by embedding security at every recovery step: anticipate, protect, detect, respond, recover, and adapt. This distinction ensures restored systems are verified and monitored for lingering threats.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Explore orchestration benefits
Learn how orchestration and IaC codify runbooks, automate recovery sequences, and enable repeatable testing to reduce human error and meet RTOs. This skill helps you design automated DR exercises and validated failovers.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
