Emerging Trends in BCP/DRP

Understand how cloud platforms, AI/ML, orchestration, and cyber resilience change the goals and mechanics of continuity planning. This orientation explains what matters and why modern designs focus on fast, verifiable, and automated recovery.

Why this matters
Organizations can move from slow, manual recoveries to fast, predictable, and verified recoveries that reduce business impact.
What you will learn
Cloud-native resilience patterns, IaC-driven recovery, AI/ML uses, orchestration, and the cyber-resilience mindset for secure recovery.
Concept Map
Key ideas and relations
Cloud-Native Resilience
Cross-region replication, managed DBs, IaC, containers
AI / ML for Resilience
Predictive analytics, anomaly detection, intelligent automation
Orchestration & Automation
Automated runbooks, testing, error reduction
Reflection: As you continue, think about which of these topics most changes your current DR assumptions.
Explore Key Topics
Cloud-Native Resilience — multi-region replication and managed services reduce manual DR effort.
AI/ML — predict failures and automate recovery decisions, but models must be validated.
Orchestration — codify runbooks to eliminate manual error and speed consistent recoveries.
