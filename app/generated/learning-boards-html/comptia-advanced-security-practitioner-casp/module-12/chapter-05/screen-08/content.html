Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Preservation vs Restoration
Preservation techniques
  • Prioritized imaging of critical hosts before restoration.
  • VM and cloud snapshots for quick state capture.
  • Offloading logs to immutable storage for later analysis.
  • Restoring from known-good golden images on new instances.
  • Dedicated forensic team to acquire evidence rapidly.
Example action
Take EBS-volume snapshots of compromised cloud instances, move snapshots to a separate secure account, then proceed to restore services from golden images on fresh instances.
Tradeoffs and misconception
The tradeoff is a small, planned delay in restoration to secure evidence versus uncontrolled loss of forensic data if you restore immediately. Misconception: 'forensics equals long delays' — with snapshots and prioritized imaging you capture evidence quickly and let DRP continue.
Key Takeaway

Plan prioritized acquisitions and snapshot procedures so evidence is preserved without derailing recovery timelines.

Pro tip
Pre-script imaging commands and snapshot procedures for common platforms so they can be executed quickly during an incident.
