Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Handoff Protocol
Escalation Threshold (example)
DRP activation is triggered if any of the following occur: more than 50% of production database servers are encrypted; a critical application remains unavailable for more than 4 hours after containment; primary and secondary backups are confirmed compromised.
Required handoff information (minimum)
  • Confirmed IOCs: malicious file hashes, command-and-control IPs, suspicious registry keys.
  • Full list of compromised systems and last-known-good state.
  • Identified attack vector and likely propagation methods.
  • Forensic preservation status: snapshots or images taken and storage locations.
  • Backup integrity confirmation: which backups are tested clean or suspected compromised.
Forensic preservation action (example)
Before any wipe or restore, capture snapshots of compromised virtual machines and copy them to an isolated, immutable account for later analysis. Document custody, timestamps, and the individual who performed the acquisition.
Key Takeaway

Embed this checklist into your incident ticket template so DRP restores never proceed without the required IR artifacts and preservation steps.
