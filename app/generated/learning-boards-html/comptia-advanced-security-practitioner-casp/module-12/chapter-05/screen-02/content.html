01
Explain interdependencies
Describe how IR, BCP, and DRP share triggers and intelligence.
You will map how an IR playbook can escalate to BCP or DRP based on measurable impact. This skill prevents siloed responses that delay recovery.

02
Design escalation & handoff
Create thresholds and a joint command structure for activation.
You will author measurable criteria and a war-room protocol so activation is timely and unambiguous, reducing executive indecision under pressure.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Establish communication protocols
Define channels, logs, and single-source reporting for crisis coordination.
You will specify secure, separate communications and reporting artifacts so teams share timely, accurate status without risking compromised networks.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Preserve forensic evidence
Balance evidence collection with rapid restoration needs.
You will plan prioritized imaging, snapshots, and immutable logging approaches so legal and recovery objectives are both met.
