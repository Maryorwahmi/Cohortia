Deliverable: Handoff Protocol
A documented sequence and required fields for IR to transfer control to DRP.
Includes incident summary, IOCs, list of compromised systems, backup status, and forensic preservation steps.
Verified Competency Milestone
Deliverable: Escalation Criteria
Measurable triggers derived from BIA to declare 'major incident' or 'disaster'.
Tie to RTO/RPO and system counts so decisions are consistent and auditable.
Verified Competency Milestone
Deliverable: Communications Plan
Secure crisis channel, briefing cadence, and single-source incident status artifact.
Predefine internal and external messaging roles, templates, and escalation paths to reduce confusion.
Verified Competency Milestone
Deliverable: Forensic Preservation Plan
Procedures for imaging, snapshots, and log offload that preserve evidence without halting recovery.
Include prioritized lists so critical hosts are captured first and teams know who owns acquisition.
Verified Competency Milestone
