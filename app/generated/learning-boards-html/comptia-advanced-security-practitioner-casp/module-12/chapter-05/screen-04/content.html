Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Interdependencies
Definition & meaning
What interdependency looks like in practice
Interdependency means IR, BCP, and DRP share triggers, intelligence, and operational steps. IR discovers cause and scope. DRP needs that discovery to choose safe recovery sources. BCP needs status to route critical functions through alternate means.
Concrete Example
Ransomware encrypts database servers and backups. IR confirms backup compromise. DRP selects older clean backups and BCP activates manual order-taking until systems are restored.
Common misconception
Assuming backups are always reliable. Without IR validation, restores can reintroduce the incident.
Key Takeaway

Make IR intelligence a required input for any DRP recovery decision to prevent re-infection and align recovery points with business impact.
