Escalation Criteria
Measurable thresholds tied to the BIA that trigger BCP/DRP activation.
Unified Command
A joint war room and single source of truth to align decisions and messaging.
Forensic Preservation
Snapshots, imaging, and immutable logging that protect evidence during recovery.
Summary
Codify thresholds, command roles, communications, and preservation steps in playbooks. Validate through integrated tabletop exercises so the plans work when a real incident occurs.
