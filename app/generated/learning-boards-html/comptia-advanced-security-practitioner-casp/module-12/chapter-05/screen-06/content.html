Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Joint Command
Expansion checklist
  • 1. Formal invite to BCP/DRP leads
  • 2. Executive management briefing cadence
  • 3. Secure crisis communications channel established
  • 4. Forensic acquisition ownership assigned
  • 5. Unified incident status artifact created
Key Takeaway

A preset expansion checklist eliminates role confusion and speeds coordinated decisions under pressure.

Role cards and responsibilities
Incident Commander
Assesses scope and triggers activation. Owns incident summary and technical decisions.
BCP Lead
Coordinates alternate business processes and continuity workarounds.
DRP Lead
Prioritizes system restores and approves recovery points based on IR intelligence.
Communications & Legal
Manage messaging and regulatory steps; advise on disclosures and evidence handling.
Example coordination: IC asks DRP to queue golden image restores, IR confirms compromised vectors and hands off IOCs, BCP opens manual channels for critical functions, and legal prepares regulatory notifications if thresholds are met.
