Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Escalation Criteria
Activation Triggers (measurable)
  • •Loss of a critical business function for > 2 hours (per BIA).
  • •> 50% of mission-critical servers compromised or unavailable.
  • •Primary and secondary backups confirmed compromised or encrypted.
  • •Regulatory notification thresholds met.
Activation Steps (concise)
  1. Incident Commander verifies impact against BIA criteria.
  2. IC notifies CISO, CIO, and executive management.
  3. BCP/DRP leads are formally activated.
  4. Joint command center and unified communications are established.
  5. IR hands over IOCs, affected systems, and forensic status.
Example scenario
A malware outbreak encrypts a cluster of database servers. IR reports 60% of DB nodes unreachable and confirms backup snapshots during the attack window. By threshold, DRP is activated, golden images are queued, and BCP redirects transaction processing to a manual mode to maintain revenue flow.
Key Takeaway

Make triggers measurable and linked to BIA so activation is fast, defensible, and repeatable.

Common mistake
Using vague language or too many discretionary steps delays actions. Keep the protocol short and measurable.
