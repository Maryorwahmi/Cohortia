Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Why testing is essential
Core Concept

Testing shows whether recovery assumptions hold

A tested plan reveals missing steps, stale contacts, and resource gaps before an incident becomes a crisis.

Concrete example
Restoring a DB to a test node exposed a missing transaction log in backups.
Common misconception
Only doing walkthroughs is enough. Walkthroughs don't validate technical restorations.
Key takeaway
Use a mix of test types and act on findings during the hotwash.
Analogy

Think of testing like fire drills

Fire drills train people and expose problems like blocked exits or missing alarms. Similarly, BCP/DRP tests train teams and expose procedural and technical barriers to recovery.

Key Takeaway

An untested plan is an assumption. Testing replaces assumptions with verified capabilities and documented corrective actions.
