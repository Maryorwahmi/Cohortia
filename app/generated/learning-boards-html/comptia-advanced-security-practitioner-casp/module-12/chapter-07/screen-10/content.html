Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Establishing KPIs
Definition

Measure outcomes, not activity

KPIs turn test events into objective evidence. Capture RTO, RPO, documentation accuracy, communications, and team performance.

RTO Achievement
Compare actual recovery time to the target RTO for critical systems.
RPO Achievement
Measure data loss against the allowable RPO.
Practical Metrics
Collect timestamps, validation checks, issue counts, and communications logs for each test. Use these to prioritize corrective actions by business impact.
Key Takeaway

KPIs provide evidence for decision making. Capture precise metrics during tests and link them to corrective actions with owners and deadlines.
