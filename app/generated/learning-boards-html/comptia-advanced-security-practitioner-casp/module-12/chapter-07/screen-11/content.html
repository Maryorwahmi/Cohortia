Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Post-Test Analysis
Hotwash

Capture observations immediately

Bring participants together to record what went well and what failed. Use structured notes and timestamps.

Document observations
Record failures, unclear steps, and communication gaps with evidence and timestamps.
Root-cause analysis
Investigate why the issue occurred so corrective actions address the cause, not just the symptom.
Corrective Actions

Assign, track, and verify

For each finding: create a specific action, name an owner, set a deadline, and plan a verification test. Without verification, the loop remains open.
Key Takeaway

A test only improves readiness if findings become tracked corrective actions and are re-tested to ensure closure.
