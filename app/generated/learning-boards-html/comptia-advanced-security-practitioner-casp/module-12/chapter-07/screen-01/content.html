Quick map
Testing types & outcomes
Walkthrough
Document review; low risk; good for initial checks and familiarization.
Simulation
Partial activation in test environments to validate technical steps.
Full interruption
Highest realism and risk; requires rollback and approvals.
Visual Concept
Risk vs. Reward: pick test types based on tolerance and impact.
KPIs: measure RTO, RPO, documentation accuracy, and communications.
Hotwash: record findings, assign owners, and update plans.
