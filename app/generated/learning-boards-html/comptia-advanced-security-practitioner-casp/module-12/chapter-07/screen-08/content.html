Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Parallel Tests
Architecture

Recovery site fully activated

Bring systems online in recovery environment with replicated data while production stays active. Validate end-to-end behavior.

Example
E-commerce checkout processed on recovery replica while production accepts live traffic.
Measurements
RTO achievement, RPO delta, service correctness, and time to bring dependent services online.
Checklist
  • • Ensure data replication is current and consistent.
  • • Segment networks to avoid traffic mix-up.
  • • Pre-stage DNS and load balancer settings for test validation only.
  • • Define rollback procedures and criteria in advance.
Key Takeaway

Parallel tests provide strong validation of recovery operations but require resources and careful isolation from production.
