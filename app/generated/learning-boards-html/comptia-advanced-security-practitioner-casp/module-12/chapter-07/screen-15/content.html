KPIs
Measure RTO, RPO, time to restore, documentation accuracy, and communications.
Hotwash
Debrief immediately, document observations, and collect objective evidence for each finding.
Corrective Actions
Create specific fixes with owners, deadlines, and a plan to verify closure in follow-up tests.
Final Takeaway

Testing is only valuable when it produces measurable findings that are owned, fixed, and re-tested. That loop creates true resilience.
