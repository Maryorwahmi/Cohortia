Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Common Pitfalls
Pitfalls

What undermines test value?

  • • Lack of realism — scenarios too simple to reveal issues.
  • • Check-the-box mentality — testing only for compliance.
  • • Insufficient scope — ignoring business processes or communications.
  • • Lack of executive buy-in — limited resources and approvals.
Mitigations

Practical strategies

Involve diverse stakeholders, use realistic scenarios, pre-stage resources, and require documented corrective actions with owners and deadlines. Combine low-risk and high-fidelity tests strategically.
Key Takeaway

Avoiding these pitfalls turns testing into a genuine capacity-building practice rather than paperwork.
