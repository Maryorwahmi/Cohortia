Differentiate test types

Establish KPIs
Define measurable outcomes like RTO, RPO, accuracy of documentation, and time to restore functions.
Mnemonic: measure outcomes, not effort.

Conduct post-test analysis
Lead hotwashes, document findings, assign corrective actions, and update plans to close gaps.
Mnemonic: observe, own, operate — then update.
