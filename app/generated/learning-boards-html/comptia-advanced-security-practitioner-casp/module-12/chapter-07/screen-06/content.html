Module 12 › Chapter 12.7 › Designing a Testing Program
Process Flow

A repeatable testing cycle

1. Establish schedule
Map frequency to criticality and regulatory requirements.
2. Define objectives
Link each test to RTO/RPO and specific plan sections.
3. Prepare scenarios, roles, and resources
Pre-stage backups, test environments, and runbooks; assign facilitator and observers.
Execution & review

Run, record, and improve

Execute with timestamps
Capture sequence of actions and time-to-complete critical steps.
Hotwash and corrective actions
Document issues, identify root causes, assign owners and deadlines.
Update plan and re-test
Close the loop by revising documentation and scheduling follow-up tests.
