Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Mistakes vs Best Practices
Common Mistakes
  • Immediate restoration without forensic imaging.
  • Backups stored only on the same site as production.
  • Tests that exclude legal, communications, or IR teams.
  • No recorded sign-offs or immutable log exports during recovery.
Best Practices
  • Forensically sound imaging and chain of custody before restores.
  • Offsite, immutable archives that meet retention needs.
  • Compliance-driven tests with legal and communications observers.
  • Automated export and retention of logs and test evidence.
Example comparison
Failed exercise: team restored a compromised server immediately and lost volatile memory and log entries. Corrected exercise: team imaged the server, exported logs to immutable storage, documented chain of custody, and then performed an authorized restore with legal sign-off.
