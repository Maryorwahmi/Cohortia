Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › PCI DSS & DRP
Definition
PCI DSS requires a formal incident response and a business continuity plan that is tested and documented.
Testing must demonstrate restoration of card data protections, including encryption and access controls.
PCI compliance checklist for DRP
  • Include cardholder environment in DRP scope and tests.
  • Demonstrate key management and encryption during restores.
  • Document test evidence and obtain annual sign-off.
Key Takeaway

Integrate PCI scope into DRP tests; auditors expect documented proof that card protections are restored.

Example test case
Simulate database corruption in the cardholder environment. Steps: verify backups, restore database to isolated test environment, reapply keys, validate transactions and logs, document results and sign-off.
Common mistake
Running DRP tests without validating key management or re‑establishing access controls for card data.
Restoration that leaves keys or access misconfigured fails to meet PCI requirements even if systems come back online.
