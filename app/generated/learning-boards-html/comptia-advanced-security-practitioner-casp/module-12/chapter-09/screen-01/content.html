Regulatory Compliance and Legal Aspects of BCP/DRP
Translate legal requirements into recoverable, testable, and auditable actions that protect operations and evidence.
In this lesson

Identify key laws and standards, map retention and notification duties to RTO/RPO, and add evidence‑preservation steps to your DRP runbook.

Why it matters

Non‑compliance can lead to fines, lawsuits, and loss of license. Compliance ensures your recovery actions are both effective and defensible under scrutiny.

Explore Key Topics
HIPAA
Requires a disaster recovery and data backup plan for ePHI availability and integrity.
GDPR
Mandates resilience of processing and timely breach notification; Article 32 and Articles 33/34 apply.
Evidence
Preserve forensic images, logs, and chain of custody to retain legal admissibility during recovery.
Compliance-Driven Recovery Map
Align legal duties with recovery steps and evidence controls
Identify
Map laws and required outcomes per system and data class.
Preserve
Forensic imaging and immutable logs before restoration.
Restore
Validated recovery that meets RTO/RPO and documentation for audits.
Key takeaway
Design DRP steps that both restore service and create an auditable record; recovery without defensible evidence may create legal exposure.
