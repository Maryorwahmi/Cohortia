Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Retention & RTO/RPO
Process
Four steps to align retention with recovery
  1. Assess legal retention and deletion obligations per data class.
  2. Map retention to backup architecture and archival tiers.
  3. Validate restores to meet RTO/RPO and integrity requirements.
  4. Implement secure deletion when retention expires and document it.
Takeaway

Retention planning affects architecture and recovery testing; treat it as a continuity requirement, not only a storage task.

Example: financial record retention
If regulations require seven years of records, use an immutable archival tier with tested restores and an access control model that preserves confidentiality. Ensure procedures exist to securely destroy expired records and log those deletion actions.
Common mistake
Assuming daily backup retention alone meets long-term legal retention without an archival strategy.
Daily backups may rotate too quickly; archive and immutable storage are necessary for many regulated retention periods.
