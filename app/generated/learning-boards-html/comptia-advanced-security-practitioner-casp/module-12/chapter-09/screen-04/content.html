Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › HIPAA & DRP
Definition
HIPAA requires a disaster recovery plan and a data backup plan to restore ePHI.
This includes encrypting backups, verifying integrity, storing copies offsite, and testing restores.
Three-point HIPAA checklist
  • Encrypted, regular backups of ePHI with verified integrity.
  • Offsite or segregated copy storage to survive site loss.
  • Documented recovery tests and legal sign-off in the DRP.
Key Takeaway

Backups alone are not enough; you must be able to recover ePHI and show documented tests to auditors.

Example runbook checkpoint
Before restoring a database containing ePHI:
1) Confirm encrypted backup integrity and checksum.
2) Verify offsite copy exists and encryption keys are accessible under approved procedure.
3) Log restore start and completion with tester sign-off and retention of logs for audit.
Common mistake
Assuming backups stored on-site satisfy HIPAA during a facility-level disaster.
Backups must be stored and managed so they remain available after the same event that disabled the primary systems.
