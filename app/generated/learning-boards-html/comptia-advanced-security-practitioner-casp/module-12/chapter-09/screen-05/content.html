Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › GDPR & DRP
Definition
Article 32: ensure ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience; Articles 33/34: notify authorities and, when required, data subjects.
This means DRP must include restoration capabilities and a decision process for notifications approved by legal.
Three-step GDPR action plan
  • Set measurable RTO/RPO for systems processing personal data.
  • Ensure immutable log export and storage for incident analysis.
  • Define notification triggers, owners, and pre-approved message templates with counsel.
Takeaway

GDPR ties technical recovery to legal timelines; your DRP must enable both fast restoration and timely, accurate notifications.

Notification timeline (visualized)
A clear internal clock for discovery to notification is required. Many regulations demand notification without undue delay and often within a specific period; DRP tests should validate the organization's ability to collect facts, seek legal input, and notify authorities promptly.

For a breach affecting EU customers, the DRP should start evidence capture, run a rapid assessment, and gather information for notification to the supervisory authority while preserving logs and images for later forensic analysis.
