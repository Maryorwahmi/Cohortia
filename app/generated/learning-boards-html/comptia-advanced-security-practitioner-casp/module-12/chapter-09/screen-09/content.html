Integration process (4 steps)
  1. Legal review and citation of applicable rules in the DRP.
  2. Cross-reference DRP steps to regulatory outcomes (retain, recover, notify).
  3. Create compliance-driven test scenarios and runbooks.
  4. Log and retain test evidence with sign‑offs for audits.
Pro tip

Automate export of logs and test results to immutable storage during exercises to reduce human error in evidence capture.

Example test-case: GDPR 72‑hour exercise
Objective: demonstrate notification readiness. Steps: detect simulated breach, capture forensic images, run quick impact assessment, prepare draft notification for counsel, and attempt to notify within simulated timeframe. Record timestamps and obtain legal sign-off.
Common mistake
Treating legal review as a checkbox rather than a recurring activity integrated into tests and updates.
