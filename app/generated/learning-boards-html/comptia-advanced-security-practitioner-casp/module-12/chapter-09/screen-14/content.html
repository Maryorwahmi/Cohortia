Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Key Concepts
Map obligations
Translate laws into RTO/RPO, retention, and notification steps.
Preserve evidence
Image systems, export logs, and document custody before restoration.
Test with legal
Run compliance-driven scenarios and retain auditable evidence of outcomes.
Summary takeaway
Make legal requirements a structural part of DRP: map, preserve, test, and document. That approach reduces fines, legal exposure, and preserves organizational trust.
