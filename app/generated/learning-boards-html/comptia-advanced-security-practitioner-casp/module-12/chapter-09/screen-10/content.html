Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Legal Implications
Definition
Legal implications arise when DRP fails to meet statutory or contractual obligations, exposing the organization to enforcement actions and liability.
Potential outcomes include regulatory fines, civil lawsuits, criminal charges in extreme cases, loss of licenses, and reputational damage affecting customers and contracts.
Example: healthcare outage
If a provider cannot restore patient records, HIPAA enforcement actions and patient lawsuits could follow, alongside loss of patient trust and potential contract losses with insurers.
Key Takeaway

Legal risk should influence recovery prioritization and investment in resilience; regulatory consequences can outsize technical costs.
