Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Learning Objectives
01
Identify frameworks
HIPAA, GDPR, PCI DSS, SOX and similar rules
You will map each framework to specific BCP/DRP obligations and know where to look for clauses that require backup, recovery, or notification.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Analyze retention & notification
Translate retention periods, RTOs, RPOs, and notification timelines
You will create DRP requirements that meet retention laws and demonstrate timely notifications in an incident scenario.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Embed compliance into testing
Design scenarios and audit trails that prove compliance
You will add test cases that simulate regulatory outcomes, such as meeting a 72‑hour notification window, and record evidence for audits.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Preserve evidence
Ensure chain of custody and forensic integrity
You will define runbook checkpoints for imaging, log export, and custody documentation before restoration proceeds.
