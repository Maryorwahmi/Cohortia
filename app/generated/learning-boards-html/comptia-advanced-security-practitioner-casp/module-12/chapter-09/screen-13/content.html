Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Compliance Checklist
Runbook checklist (numbered)
  1. Confirm whether regulated data/systems are affected.
  2. If yes, instruct IR to capture forensically sound images and export logs to immutable storage.
  3. Document chain of custody: who handled each artifact and when.
  4. Notify legal counsel and communications for potential regulatory notifications.
  5. Perform controlled restoration only after sign-off; record validation tests and sign-offs.
  6. Retain all evidence and test artifacts in an auditable archive for regulators.
Key Takeaway

A short checklist enforces preservation and coordination without sacrificing the speed of a controlled restore.

Example DRP checkpoint
"Before restoring web_server_01: IR Lead must confirm forensic snapshot exists in immutable storage and legal has been notified of potential breach. Record checkpoint with timestamps and personnel names."
Common mistake
Having long, unfocused checklists that are ignored during high-pressure incidents.
Keep the checklist short, role-specific, and required for sign-off to ensure compliance steps are followed.
