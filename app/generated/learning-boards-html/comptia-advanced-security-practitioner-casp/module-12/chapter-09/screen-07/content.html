Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › SOX & DRP
Definition
SOX requires public companies to maintain internal controls that ensure the integrity of financial reporting.
Recovery must preserve control points so financial statements remain accurate after restoration.
Control continuity checklist
  • Include reconciliation and control validation steps in DRP tests for financial systems.
  • Document who signs off on control integrity after recovery.
  • Retain records that demonstrate control effectiveness post-recovery.
Takeaway

Recovery must include control verification for financial systems; auditors expect proof that reconciliations and controls survived the outage.

Example verification procedure
After restoring the ERP: run reconciliation scripts, compare balances with immutable pre-incident records, verify user access logs, and obtain control-owner sign-off recorded in the test artifacts.
Common misconception
SOX does not mandate specific technologies; it requires demonstrable internal controls and evidence that they function after recovery.
