Below is a mock disaster recovery clause you can adapt. Use it in conversations and contract drafts.
Service Level Agreement - Disaster Recovery Clause

1. Recovery Time Objective (RTO): Vendor commits to restoring the critical service within 4 hours of a declared disaster.
2. Recovery Point Objective (RPO): Vendor commits to a maximum data loss of 1 hour for the critical service.
3. Backup & Recovery: Vendor shall maintain daily full backups and hourly incremental backups, stored encrypted and off-site. Test restores shall be performed quarterly.
4. Incident Notification: Vendor shall notify Customer within 30 minutes of detecting any incident impacting service availability or data integrity.
5. Audit Rights: Customer reserves the right to review Vendor's BCP/DRP documentation and audit its testing results annually.
Key Takeaway

Translate recovery metrics into contract language and require test evidence or audit rights to verify vendor claims.

Supporting explanation
  • Make RTO and RPO explicit and measurable; avoid vague phrasing such as "reasonable efforts."
  • Require backup encryption and off-site storage with documented restore tests.
  • Include incident notification windows and escalation points for critical incidents.
  • Negotiate audit rights or third-party attestations when possible.
Common negotiation challenge
Vendors may resist audit rights; when that happens, seek alternative compensations such as more frequent backups, stronger encryption, or staged failover commitments.
