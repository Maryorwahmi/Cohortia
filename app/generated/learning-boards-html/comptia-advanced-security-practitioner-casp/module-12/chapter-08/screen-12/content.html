Summary
Key Concepts
What to remember and why it matters
Supply Chain Resilience
Ability to anticipate and respond to vendor disruptions to preserve operations.
Third-Party Risk
Risk introduced by external vendors, suppliers, and service providers you depend on.
Fourth-Party Risk
Risk from a vendor's own suppliers; include them in assessments and SBOM reviews.
Summary Takeaway
Make vendor resilience measurable, auditable, and part of your recovery plans so external failures do not become internal crises.
