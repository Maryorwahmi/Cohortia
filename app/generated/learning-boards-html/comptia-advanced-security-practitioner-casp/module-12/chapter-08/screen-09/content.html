Mitigation Strategies
Patterns to reduce vendor-related risk
Diversification
Use multiple vendors or multi-cloud architectures to avoid a single point of failure for critical services.
Redundancy
Replicate data and services across regions or vendors and validate restore processes to meet your RPO and RTO.
Contingency plans
Create operational workarounds and manual processes to bridge the gap until a vendor recovers or you switch providers.
Key Takeaway

Combine technical redundancy, contractual guarantees, and tested operational playbooks to reduce vendor failure impact.

Practical cards
Tabletop exercises
Include critical vendors in exercises to test communication, failover, and role responsibilities.
SBOM & SSDLC
Require SBOMs and secure development practices from software suppliers to quickly identify and remediate component vulnerabilities.
Monitoring
Continuously monitor vendor performance and security posture and trigger playbooks when thresholds are crossed.
