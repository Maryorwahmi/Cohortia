Process Flow
Inventory Critical Vendors
Step-by-step checklist to build a usable inventory
1
Gather BIA outputs
Identify critical business functions and map which vendors touch them.
2
Document services and dependencies
Record vendor role, interfaces, subcontractors, geographic location, and contact points.
3
Attach recovery metrics
Assign required RTO/RPO for the business function and compare with vendor claims.
4
Prioritize and plan mitigations
Rank vendors by impact and craft contractual or technical compensations for gaps.
Key Takeaway

A usable vendor inventory is the foundation for risk assessments, SLAs, and exercises; it must include subcontractors and RTO/RPO mapping.

Supporting cards
Mapping
Link vendor services to specific applications and manual processes to see true impact.
Fourth-party check
Ask vendors for their subcontractor list and include them in your risk profile; nested dependencies can cascade failures.
Example
A payment gateway vendor relies on a regional bank for settlement; a bank outage can affect transaction clearing for your service.
Try this
Create a mini-inventory for three critical functions this week and identify any missing subcontractor information.
