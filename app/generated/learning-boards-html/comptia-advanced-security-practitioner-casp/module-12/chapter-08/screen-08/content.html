Process
Assessment Techniques
SIG Lite, CAIQ, certifications, and testing
Questionnaires
Use standardized forms to compare vendors and capture controls, incident history, and subcontractor use.
Certifications & audit reports
Request SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, or equivalent reports and review the scope and any exceptions.
Testing
Negotiate penetration testing rights or request vendor-run test results for interfaces that affect your environment.
Try this
Add a question to your vendor intake form requiring a subcontractor list and the last restore test report.
Due diligence actions
  • Check financial filings or credit indicators for vendor stability.
  • Review incident history and how the vendor communicated and recovered.
  • Confirm subcontractor lists and ask how the vendor manages downstream risk.
Common mistake
Relying solely on certification reports without checking scope, exceptions, and recent test evidence leaves gaps.
