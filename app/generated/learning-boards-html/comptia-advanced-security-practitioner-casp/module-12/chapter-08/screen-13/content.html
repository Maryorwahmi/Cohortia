What to Remember
Key Concepts & Checklist
Short terms and a practical checklist to use immediately
Vendor Due Diligence
Evaluate security, financial health, and subcontractors before onboarding.
Service Level Agreement
Contractual RTO/RPO, backup, notification, and audit rights — keep them measurable.
SBOM
Inventory of software components to trace and remediate vulnerabilities quickly.
Practical checklist
  • Maintain vendor inventory mapped to BIA outputs.
  • Require measurable SLAs for RTO/RPO and incident notification.
  • Request SBOMs and SSDLC evidence from software vendors.
  • Negotiate audit/testing rights for critical integrations and include vendors in tabletop exercises.
