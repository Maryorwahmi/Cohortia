RTO & RPO — Vendor vs. Organization
Compare metrics and plan compensations for gaps
Organizational need
  • CRM: RTO 4 hours, RPO 15 minutes
  • Payment processing: RTO 1 hour, RPO near-zero
Vendor claim
  • Vendor RTO: 24 hours
  • Backups: daily full + hourly incrementals (verify retention and restore tests)
Mitigation example
If the vendor RTO is longer than your need, implement streaming replication or an application-level continuous protection solution to meet your RPO, and negotiate SLA improvements for RTO.
Key Takeaway

Quantify gaps between vendor claims and organizational needs, then prioritize mitigation for highest-impact vendors.

Fourth-party and backup validation
Ask vendors for subcontractor lists and for test evidence: restore test reports, backup schedules, and encryption controls. Request time-stamped logs or reports where available.
Example question set
"What are your RTO and RPO for the managed database service? Provide recent restore test results and subcontractor lists."
Common misconception
Treat certifications as inputs, not proof of alignment; always validate with metrics and test results.
