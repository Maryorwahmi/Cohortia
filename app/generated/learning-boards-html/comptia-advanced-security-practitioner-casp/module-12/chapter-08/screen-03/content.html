How these objectives map to practice
  • Map each objective to at least one artifact you can request from a vendor: SLA, BCP documentation, test reports, or SBOM.
  • Create a vendor scorecard with thresholds for acceptable RTO/RPO alignment.
  • Plan tabletop exercises that include your most critical vendors and test handoffs and notifications.
