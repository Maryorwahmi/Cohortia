Supply Chain Resilience and Third-Party Risk
How vendor dependencies shape your BCP/DRP and enterprise security posture
In this lesson you will treat vendors as part of your security boundary, learn practical assessment questions, and get strategies to mitigate gaps. Real examples include a SaaS outage affecting CRM and a vulnerable open-source library found in a supplier's product.
In this lesson
  • Inventory and map critical vendors to business functions.
  • Assess vendor BCP/DRP: RTO, RPO, backups, and fourth-party risk.
  • Write contractual SLA clauses and negotiate audit rights.
Coming up
Vendor assessment checklist and mitigation strategies.
Quick orientation
Treat your ecosystem as a single continuity system. If a critical vendor fails, your internal plans must compensate immediately.
