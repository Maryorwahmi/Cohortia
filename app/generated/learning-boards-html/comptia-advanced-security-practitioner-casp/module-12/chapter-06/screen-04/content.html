Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Role of communication
Definition
Crisis communication in the continuity context

Crisis communication assigns who speaks, what is said, and how it is sent, as an operational part of BCP/DRP. Its purpose is to reduce uncertainty, coordinate actions, and preserve trust while recovery proceeds.

Common misconceptions
  • Technical transparency is always best—too much detail can hinder operations or help attackers.
  • Silence is safe—delayed updates often erode trust and invite speculation.
Concrete example
If a payment gateway outage affects checkout, an initial message like “We are aware of a checkout issue affecting some customers; teams are investigating and we will update at 10:30” helps customers decide whether to retry or postpone purchases.
Key Takeaway

Prepare brief factual structures you can reuse so messages are timely and clear under pressure.
