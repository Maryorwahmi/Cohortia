Effective vs Ineffective Communication
Effective
  • Plain language, actionable steps, and empathy.
  • Consistent facts across all spokespeople and channels.
  • Regular updates with expected timings.
Example: Website alert giving impact and next update time.
Ineffective
  • Delayed or no updates; silence breeds speculation.
  • Contradictory statements from different teams.
  • Technical jargon that confuses customers and media.
Pitfall: Legal delays without interim status updates may appear evasive.
Tool
Single Source of Truth
Use a single approved message store for all channels.
Process
Approval SLA
Define a short review window for routine statements during incidents.
Check
Q&A Script
Equip customer-facing staff with expected answers to common questions.
