Social media monitoring strategy
Design a focused listening plan for the first 48 hours and beyond.
Tools
Select social listening platforms and set alerts for rapid spikes.
Keywords
Monitor brand, product names, and incident terms; maintain a dynamic list.
Owners
Assign PR, security, legal, and a community manager to handle escalations.
Severity rules
Define thresholds for mention volume or influencer reach that trigger executive briefings.
Response templates
Prepare targeted replies that direct users to official updates and avoid amplifying speculation.
Key Takeaway

Monitor strategically: prioritize influential misinformation and route all official details to one canonical source to keep responses focused.
