Crisis Communication and Stakeholder Management
Prepare to inform the right people, with the right level of detail, at the right time.
In this lesson

Build a crisis communication plan, map stakeholders, craft clear messaging templates, and set legal-aware notification triggers that align with BCP/DRP activities.

Why it matters

Technical fixes restore services; communication restores confidence. Good messaging reduces reputational damage and helps meet regulatory deadlines.
