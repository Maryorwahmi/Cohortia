What to Remember
Three practical prompts to guide action during an incident.
Message
Short & factual
Acknowledge, impact, actions, next update, empathy.
Source
Single source of truth
Centralize approved facts to avoid contradictions.
Legal
Align with legal
Coordinate technical facts with legal for defensible disclosure.
Final takeaway

Use templates, a stakeholder map, and monitoring to respond quickly and consistently while meeting legal obligations.
