Plan Elements — Step sequence
1
Designated Spokespersons
Identify the authorized voices and ensure media training and legal alignment.
2
Channels
Select secure internal channels and official external outlets for announcements.
3
Message Templates
Prepare short, plain-language templates matching common incident types.
4
Notification Triggers
Define severity thresholds and time-based triggers for stakeholder updates.
5
Approval Process
Designate reviewers and a maximum approval turnaround to avoid delays.
6
Monitoring
Set up media and social listening to find misinformation and measure sentiment.
Operational cards
Template example
Initial notification
Acknowledge the issue, state affected service, describe next steps, provide the next update time, and express empathy.
Approval shortcut
Rapid approval chain
Identify two approvers from legal and executive teams with clear SLA for sign-off during incidents.
Monitoring play
Listening and escalation
Assign owners for channels and set alert thresholds for spikes in negative mentions.
Common mistake: vague approval processes. Fix this by naming alternates and maximum review times so messages are timely and consistent.
