Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Stakeholder identification
Stakeholder matrix
Categorize stakeholders to tailor tone, channel, and content.
Internal stakeholders
  • Employees: operational instructions, scripts, escalation paths.
  • Executive leadership: concise impact and resource needs.
  • Legal/HR: compliance steps and personnel guidance.
Mnemonic: PEOPLE = Prepare, Empathize, Outline, Plan, Lead, Escalate
External stakeholders
  • Customers: impact, protective steps, next update time.
  • Regulators: factual disclosures and timeline requirements.
  • Media & Public: transparent, empathetic messages to prevent speculation.
  • Investors/Partners: business impact and mitigation status.
Tip: provide tailored Q&A for customer-facing staff to keep external messages consistent.
Channel
Email, website alert, and verified social accounts for public notices.
Owner
Assign a single owner for each stakeholder group to avoid conflicting statements.
Cadence
Set expected update times to reduce the pressure to speculate between updates.
