Notification triggers and cadence
Map events to notifications and set predictable follow-up timings.
Trigger table (example)
EventNotifyCadence
Service outage (severe)Customers, Execs, SupportInitial, 2hr, hourly
Confirmed data exposureRegulators, Affected Customers, LegalInitial within legal window, daily updates
Minor degradationSupport onlyInitial, next update at resolution
Guidance
  • Define what constitutes a trigger clearly and measurably.
  • Assign owners to confirm the trigger and initiate messages.
  • Set realistic update windows and communicate them publicly.
Common Pitfall

Committing to unrealistic update frequency leads to missed deadlines; choose a cadence you can meet and stick to it to preserve credibility.
