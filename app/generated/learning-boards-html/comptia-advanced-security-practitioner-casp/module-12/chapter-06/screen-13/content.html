Activity: Draft an initial customer notification
Use the provided template and internal checklists to create a concise, empathetic message.
Template (Website/Email)
Subject: Important Security Notice Regarding Your Account

Dear Valued Customer,

We are writing to inform you that we detected and contained unauthorized access to a portion of our systems. Our investigation indicates that a limited set of customer account numbers and email addresses may have been accessed. We have no evidence at this time that passwords or full payment details were compromised.

Our teams have contained the incident and are working with external experts. We will provide an update by [time]. In the meantime, please review your account statements and report any suspicious activity to our security line.

Sincerely,
The SecureBank Incident Response Team
Internal checklist (front-line staff)
  • Key message: Use the approved customer template; do not speculate.
  • Escalation: Route suspected fraud reports to the incident desk immediately.
  • Support script: A short Q&A for common customer questions.
Social monitoring
Set alerts for brand and breach keywords and assign owners for 48-hour coverage.
Approval
Route the message to legal and executive approvers using the defined SLA.
Takeaway
Keep the message factual, empathetic, and actionable; avoid operational or forensic detail in public notices.
