Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Spokesperson & approvals
Designated spokesperson & approval processes
Clear authority and fast approvals are essential for timely external messaging.
Spokesperson rules
  • Keep the group small and trained.
  • Include communications and technical subject matter experts.
  • Pre-approve messages and talking points with legal.
Approval matrix
Define who approves which message type and the SLA for routine and regulator-facing communications.
Training & exercises
Run tabletop exercises focused on messaging and Q&A so spokespeople learn to translate technical updates into plain language and avoid operational detail leaks.
Key Takeaway

Name spokespeople, train them, and document a fast approval chain to ensure consistent, timely public statements.
