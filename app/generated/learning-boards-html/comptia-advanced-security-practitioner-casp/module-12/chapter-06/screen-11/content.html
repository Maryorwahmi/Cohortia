Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Legal & ethical
Legal and ethical considerations
Ensure disclosures meet requirements while remaining transparent and accurate.
Regulatory timeline
Identify applicable regulators and notification windows and assign owners to meet them without delay.
Ethical guidance
Favor factual transparency while avoiding operational details that could worsen exposure.
Role of the security professional
Provide the nature and scope of compromise, confidence levels, and evidence so legal can determine what to disclose and when.
Misconception: Holding back all information reduces liability. In many cases, delayed notification increases legal risk and damages reputation. Coordinate with legal to balance timing and content.
Key Takeaway

Document technical facts and confidence levels immediately so legal and PR can craft accurate, timely, and defensible disclosures.
