Key Concepts
Three compact reminders to carry into incidents.
What to remember
Crisis Communication Plan
A structured guide listing spokespeople, channels, templates, triggers, approvals, and monitoring.
Stakeholder Management
Identify who needs what information, via which channel, and how often.
Designated Spokesperson
An authorized, trained individual who ensures consistent, defensible public statements.
Summary

Keep plans simple, map stakeholders clearly, and train spokespeople so your organization can communicate credibly under pressure.
