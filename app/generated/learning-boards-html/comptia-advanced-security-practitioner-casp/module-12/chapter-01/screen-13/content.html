Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
Maximum acceptable downtime for a function; used to prioritize recovery sequencing and resources.
Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
Maximum tolerable amount of data loss measured in time; drives backup and replication frequency.
Critical Business Function (CBF)
An activity essential to operations whose disruption would cause significant negative impact.
Final reflection

Use RTO and RPO to prioritize and test recovery capabilities. If test results do not meet those metrics, the gaps identify where to invest and exercise next.
