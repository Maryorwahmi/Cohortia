Foundations of Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

A strategic, enterprise-level approach to keep essential operations available during and after disruptions.

In this lesson

Learn the BCP definition, its scope across people and processes, how it links to DRP, the core phases like BIA and testing, and common pitfalls to avoid.

Why it matters

Plans that only recover systems may not restore business operations. A strong BCP aligns technical recovery with real business needs, protecting revenue and reputation.
