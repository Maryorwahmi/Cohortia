Process

Phases from initiation to maintenance

01
Project Initiation & Management
Form the team, define scope, and secure executive sponsorship and resources to run the program.
02
Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
Identify critical functions, their dependencies, and measure impact to derive RTO and RPO values.
03
Strategy Development
Choose recovery options balancing cost, speed, and feasibility, and align them to business priorities.
04
Plan Development
Document procedures, communication plans, checklists, and roles for execution during disruption.
05
Testing & Exercising
Run realistic exercises to validate assumptions, test coordination, and reveal gaps to fix.
06
Maintenance & Review
Schedule periodic updates, lessons-learned reviews, and governance checks to keep the plan current.
Example timeline

A small initial program might spend 1–2 months on initiation and BIA, another month on strategy and plan drafting, then run tabletop tests quarterly and full exercises annually to refine the plan.

Misconception

One-time planning is insufficient. Regular testing and governance are required to keep continuity effective as systems and organizations change.

Key Takeaway

Treat BCP as an ongoing program: plan, test, learn, and update under clear governance.
