Testing types
Tabletop: role discussion and scenario walkthrough.
Functional: test specific tasks and systems.
Full exercise: simulate a complex incident end-to-end.
After-action review

Capture findings, assign corrective actions, update the plan, and retrain staff. Use test results to validate whether RTOs and RPOs are achievable.

Common mistake

Testing only on paper or allowing long gaps between exercises leaves teams unprepared. Regular, realistic practice is essential.

Key Takeaway

Treat tests as learning opportunities: update the plan and training promptly after each exercise.
