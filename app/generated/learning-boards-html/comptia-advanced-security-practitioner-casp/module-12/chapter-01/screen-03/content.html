Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Applied Objectives
BIA and Recovery Metrics

You will learn to list critical functions, map dependencies, and set RTO and RPO targets tied to business impact.

Scope & Stakeholder Drafting

You will produce a compact scope statement and a stakeholder list that includes business owners, IT, legal, and key vendors.

Testing & Governance

You will design simple tests and a maintenance cadence so the plan remains current and credible to executives.

Example

A four-hour RTO for order processing ties to alternate staff locations, temporary manual workarounds, and prioritized database restore steps.

Pitfall to avoid

Do not treat BCP as only IT recovery. Ensure business process continuity is planned and exercised.
