Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Governance & Roles
Team Composition
Business owners for each critical function
IT operations and DRP implementers
Legal, compliance, communications, procurement
Vendor and third-party liaisons
Governance Elements

Define sponsorship, review cadence, approval authorities, and escalation criteria so the plan stays current and actionable.

Stakeholder Example

For a critical microservice, stakeholders might include the service product owner, SRE team, legal/compliance, trading desk representatives, procurement, and the cloud provider account team. Each has a role during planning and incident response.

Ownership rule

Assign a named owner to each critical function who approves RTO/RPO and leads exercises. Ownership reduces ambiguity during incidents.
