Scope & Stakeholders

Draft a scope for a critical service and list internal and external stakeholders: business owner, IT operations, legal, compliance, communications, and vendor contacts.

Potential disruptions
  • Cloud provider outage affecting infrastructure
  • Faulty software deployment that breaks order processing
  • Targeted cyber incident that impacts transaction data
Example scope

This BCP covers the TradeExecutionEngine service, including order intake, validation, and temporary manual order capture processes. It does not cover physical facilities unrelated to trading floor operations or corporate-wide crisis communications owned by executive staff.

Key Takeaway

A focused scope plus stakeholder alignment produces a plan that can be executed, tested, and approved by leadership.
