Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Key Concepts
Business Continuity Planning (BCP)
A holistic process that prepares the organization to maintain critical functions during disruption.
Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)
A focused subset of BCP that covers IT system and data recovery after an incident.
Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
A systematic process to identify critical functions, dependencies, and to derive RTO and RPO values.
Summary

Keep these terms linked: BIA defines the metrics, BCP uses them to prioritize continuity strategies, and DRP provides the technical steps to reach the objectives.
