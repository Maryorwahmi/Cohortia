Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Common Pitfalls
Mistake
No executive buy-in: plan lacks resources and visibility.
BCP treated as compliance paperwork rather than operational readiness.
Incomplete BIA: missed dependencies and misprioritized recovery.
Remedy
Secure sponsorship early and present concise business impact evidence.
Integrate BCP into enterprise risk management with measurable milestones.
Conduct a thorough BIA mapping people, systems, and third parties.
Vendor & Testing Gaps

Excluding suppliers or testing only internally leaves external failure modes unchecked. Include vendor responsibilities and test vendor failover if possible.

Action checklist
  • Get executive sponsorship and periodic reviews.
  • Run tabletop and full exercises regularly.
  • Include third parties and maintain offsite secure copies of plans.
