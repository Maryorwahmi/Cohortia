Key Concepts

DRP Focus
IT recovery to meet RTO and RPO established by the BIA.
Site Strategies
Hot, warm, cold, and cloud trade cost vs readiness; select by service tier.
Secure Recovery
Encrypt backups, restrict access, preserve forensics, and mirror production security controls.
Summary
Design DRPs that meet business targets, choose recovery sites that match those targets, and keep security central during every recovery step.
