Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › Roles & Testing
Definition

Who does what

Recovery Lead
Authorizes failover and coordinates teams.
System Owners
Verify service-specific restore steps and sign off on readiness.
Security Lead
Ensures forensic preservation and enforces IAM during restore.
Testing and communication
  • Run tabletop exercises to validate decision logic and communications
  • Run periodic full restores to verify backups, templates, and scripts
  • Record lessons learned and update the DRP and IaC templates
Common mistake
Testing only in development or neglecting to test the full restore path creates a false sense of readiness.
Key Takeaway

Define roles, rehearse decisions, and treat tests as discovery exercises that improve the DRP continuously.
