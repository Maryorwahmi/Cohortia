Comparison

Cloud as cold, warm, or hot

Cloud offers a spectrum from snapshot-based restores to fully active multi-region deployments; choose based on RTO/RPO and budget.

Snapshot Backup (cloud cold)
Store encrypted snapshots, restore on demand. Low cost, longer RTO.
Warm Standby (cloud)
Scaled-down environment running continuously and scaled up when needed. Faster RTO, moderate cost.
Active/Active Multi-Region
Full replication across regions for near-zero RTO and RPO. Highest cost and complexity.
Pilot Light
Core services running; application servers provision on demand. Good balance for many scenarios.
Security checklist
  • Encrypt backups in transit and at rest with managed keys
  • Maintain consistent IAM and network rules across regions
  • Validate cross-region replication integrity and logging
  • Consider data sovereignty and compliance requirements
