Learning Objectives

A Bloom-style progression prepares you to design and secure DRPs end-to-end.

01
Explain DRP purpose and objectives
Describe how DRP focuses on restoring IT to meet RTO and RPO set by the business impact analysis. This matters because recovery priorities determine resource allocation and acceptable data loss.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Categorize disaster types and impact
Differentiate natural, accidental, and intentional incidents and explain their specific recovery and security implications, such as handling forensic evidence after an attack.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Compare recovery site strategies
Evaluate hot, warm, cold, and cloud options against RTO/RPO and cost trade-offs to select an appropriate recovery approach for a service.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design secure DRP components
List and explain data recovery, system restoration, network reconfiguration, communications, and role matrices and how to secure them through encryption, IAM, and verification steps.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
