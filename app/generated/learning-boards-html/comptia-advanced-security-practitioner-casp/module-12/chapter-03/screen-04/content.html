Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › DRP vs BCP
Core Concept

DRP defined

DRP focuses on IT recovery steps, roles, and verification so systems meet RTO and RPO set by the business impact analysis.

Key point: Living document with responsibilities and test plans
Misconception: DRP is not only backups; recovery automation and network reconfiguration matter
Why it matters: Reduces downtime and preserves integrity during recovery
Key Takeaway

DRP is the technology plan that implements BCP priorities with secure, testable steps to restore services.

Definition

How DRP supports business continuity

What DRP contains
  • Recovery objectives and timelines
  • Data recovery and integrity checks
  • System rebuild and automation steps
  • Network reconfiguration and communications
Concrete example
A payment cluster with 1-hour RTO will store hourly encrypted backups, use warm standby replication, and assign a named recovery lead with documented runbooks.
Check your understanding
When testing a DRP, verify that recovery steps include both integrity verification and access control checks; a green system that is insecure is not a successful recovery.
