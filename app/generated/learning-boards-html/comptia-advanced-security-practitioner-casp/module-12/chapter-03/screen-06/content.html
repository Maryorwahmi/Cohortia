Comparison

Hot vs Warm vs Cold

Use RTO and RPO as your primary selection criteria, then layer in cost and security considerations.

Hot Site
Fully mirrored, immediate activation. RTO: minutes-hours, RPO: near zero. Highest cost and continuous maintenance.
Warm Site
Partial setup, some provisioning required. RTO: hours-days. Balanced cost and readiness.
Cold Site
Basic facility only. RTO: days-weeks. Lowest cost but highest lead time to recover.
Decision checklist
  • Map RTO and RPO requirements
  • Estimate ongoing operational cost
  • Verify security posture and access controls
  • Plan test frequency and procedures
Scenario
A billing system with a 15-minute RTO and 5-minute RPO typically requires a hot site or active-active solution to meet those aggressive targets.
