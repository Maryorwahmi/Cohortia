Module 12 › Chapter 12.3 › RTO/RPO Mapping
Process Flow

Map RTO/RPO to strategy

1. Gather BIA
Collect RTO, RPO, and business impact for each service.
2. Tier services
Assign tiers such as mission-critical, business-critical, and non-critical.
3. Map to recovery option
Match tiers to hot, warm, cold, or cloud strategies based on targets and budget.
4. Validate and test
Estimate costs, run exercises, and adjust the plan based on findings.
Concrete example
A service with 4 hour RTO and 1 hour RPO maps to warm standby or pilot light in cloud, with continuous replication for data and automated scale-up scripts to meet the RTO.
Checklist
  • Do the chosen strategies achieve target RTO and RPO?
  • Are security controls replicated in the recovery site?
  • Have you scheduled periodic tests and table-top exercises?
  • Is there a rollback plan if a recovery introduces new risks?
Misconception
Choosing a strategy solely on cost without verifying RTO/RPO typically results in unmet recovery objectives during a real event.
