RPO: data loss tolerance

RPO defines the maximum age of data that can be accepted after a recovery, guiding backup and replication strategies.

Mapping RPO to technology
  • Near-zero RPO → synchronous replication or continuous data protection.
  • Short RPO (minutes) → frequent incremental backups or near-real-time replication.
  • Longer RPO (hours/days) → periodic backups or batch replication.
Questions to ask
What regulatory, contractual or consumer harms arise from lost transactions? Which datasets require near-zero loss?
Example
A portal that handles billing might need an RPO of 15 minutes to prevent billing disputes and compliance problems.
Misconception
Assuming thicker backups alone reduce RPO. Recovery architecture and operational processes determine achievable RPO.
