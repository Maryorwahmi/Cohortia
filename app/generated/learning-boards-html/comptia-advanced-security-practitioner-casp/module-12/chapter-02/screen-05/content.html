Why scope matters

A focused scope ensures data collection is targeted and that results are actionable in a short time frame.

Example scope
Assess the Secure Customer Portal and its supporting services: identity provider, customer database, payment gateway.
Objective template
"Estimate RTO and RPO and list top 5 dependencies required to restore portal functionality."
Practical checklist
  • Identify stakeholders and process owners.
  • Decide start and end dates for the BIA phase.
  • Define measurable impact thresholds (e.g., $ loss/hour).
  • Schedule interviews and workshops.
Common misconception
Wider scope equals better results. In reality, a phased approach yields higher quality, verifiable data and faster validation.
Key Takeaway

Start with a narrow, high-impact scope and measurable objectives to produce defensible recovery metrics quickly.
