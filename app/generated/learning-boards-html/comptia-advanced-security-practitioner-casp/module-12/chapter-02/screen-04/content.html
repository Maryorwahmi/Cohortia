Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › BIA Methodology
Process Flow
  1. 1. Scope & objectives: decide boundaries and goals for the BIA.
  2. 2. Plan data collection: prepare templates and identify stakeholders.
  3. 3. Collect data: interviews, questionnaires and workshops.
  4. 4. Impact analysis: quantify financial/non-financial losses over time.
  5. 5. Identify CBFs & document: produce prioritized recovery tiers and recommendations.
Example question
"What is the estimated revenue loss per hour if this function is down during peak hours?" Use a monetary figure to ground RTO decisions.
Data collection template (core fields)
Function description
Business purpose, users, frequency.
Dependencies
Systems, vendors, people, and facilities required.
Impact metrics
Revenue/hour, regulatory exposure, customer impact.
Recovery needs
Required RTO, RPO, and criticality tier.
Key Takeaway

A repeatable template makes stakeholder answers comparable and the analysis defensible.
