Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Data Gathering
Collection methods
  • Interviews: uncover undocumented dependencies and nuanced impacts.
  • Questionnaires: capture comparable numeric data across functions.
  • Workshops: reconcile conflicting inputs and build consensus.
Validation
Cross-check stakeholder claims with monitoring, billing, or incident history and record the evidence source.
Practical template snippet
Question: Estimated revenue loss per hour?
Record numeric value, source (report or estimate), and confidence level.
Question: Critical dependencies?
List required services, vendors, and recovery constraints.
Pitfall
Accepting undocumented estimates. Always ask for evidence or a confidence rating.
Key Takeaway

Use multiple methods and require documented evidence so BIA outputs are defensible and auditable.
