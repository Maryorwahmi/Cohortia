Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
RTO: time until acceptable recovery

RTO is the target maximum time to restore a function to an acceptable level of service after an incident.

Timeline example
Plot cumulative impact over time and mark the RTO where harm becomes unacceptable. Use service-level objectives to map minimal operation versus full restoration.
  • Minutes RTO — high-frequency trading or payment authorization.
  • Hours RTO — customer portals or transactional websites.
  • Days RTO — archival systems, reporting.
Practical steps
  1. Calculate loss/hour and consequences.
  2. Define minimum viable function for the RTO.
  3. Plan recovery actions to meet the RTO and verify them in exercises.
Example
An order-matching engine may require an RTO of minutes; the reporting engine can have hours or days.
Misconception
RTO equals full recovery. In reality, RTO often targets minimum viable operation; full recovery may take longer and is measured by WRT.
