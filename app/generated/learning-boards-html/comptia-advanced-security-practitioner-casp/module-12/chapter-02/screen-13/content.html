Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Challenges & Best Practices
Common challenges
  • Incomplete or optimistic stakeholder estimates.
  • All departments claiming top criticality.
  • Difficulty keeping the BIA current with changes.
Facilitation tip
Use focused workshops and neutral facilitation to reconcile conflicting priorities.
Best practices
  • Secure executive sponsorship.
  • Use templates and evidence requirements.
  • Protect BIA data as sensitive information.
  • Schedule regular reviews or change-triggered updates.
Security note
Limit distribution of critical asset lists and store BIA results with appropriate access controls.
Key Takeaway

Address human factors with governance and facilitation, and treat BIA outputs as sensitive organizational assets.
