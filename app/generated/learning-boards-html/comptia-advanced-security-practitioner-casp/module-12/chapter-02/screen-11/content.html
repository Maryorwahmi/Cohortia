Tiering approach

Use RTO, RPO and impact to assign functions to recovery tiers and map supporting assets beneath each function.

Tier 1 — Mission-critical
Minutes to 1 hour RTO, near-zero RPO. Requires high-availability and redundancy.
Tier 2 — Important
Hours RTO. Warm standby or faster restores recommended.
Tier 3 — Non-critical
Days RTO. Cold site or batch restores acceptable.
Map assets
List databases, identity providers, external APIs, and staff roles needed to restore each CBF.
Contract & SLA notes
Identify third-party dependencies and confirm SLAs match your tier expectations.
Key Takeaway

A tiered map of functions and assets focuses recovery efforts and makes investment decisions justifiable.
