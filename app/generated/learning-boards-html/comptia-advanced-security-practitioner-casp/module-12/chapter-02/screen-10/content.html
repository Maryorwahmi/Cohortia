Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › MTD & WRT
MTD (Maximum Tolerable Downtime)

The business-level hard limit on acceptable downtime. Past this point, consequences are intolerable.

Example
A non-core service with MTD of 72 hours where lost business beyond that creates unacceptable contractual or regulatory harm.
WRT (Work Recovery Time)

Time after meeting RTO needed for verification, reconfiguration, and full operational testing.

Example
If RTO is 4 hours and it takes 2 additional hours to validate and test systems, WRT is 2 hours.
Tip
Set RTO inside MTD to leave buffer for verification and unexpected delays.
Pitfall
Ignoring WRT when calculating total time to normal operations leads to unrealistic expectations.
Check
Document RTO, WRT and MTD together for each CBF so recovery sequences are clear.
