Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Financial vs Non-Financial
Financial impacts
  • Lost revenue per hour
  • Extra operational costs (overtime, hardware)
  • Regulatory fines and contractual penalties
Example calculation
If average revenue is $10,000/hour and an outage lasts 3 hours, direct loss is $30,000 before secondary costs.
Non-financial impacts
  • Reputational damage and loss of trust
  • Legal and compliance exposure
  • Customer churn and competitive disadvantage
Scoring approach
Assign qualitative scores or proxies, such as projected churn × lifetime value, to compare with monetary losses.
Scenario planning
Evaluate best, likely and worst cases to capture uncertainty in both monetary and reputational outcomes.
Communicate assumptions
Document how non-financial scores were derived so decision-makers can judge the basis for prioritization.
Pitfall
Ignoring non-financial harm because it is harder to quantify leads to skewed priorities.
