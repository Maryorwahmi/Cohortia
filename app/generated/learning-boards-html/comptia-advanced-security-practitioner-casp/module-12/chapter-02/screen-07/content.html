Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Impact Analysis
Quantify losses over time
Build a timeline of cumulative impact: immediate hourly revenue loss, escalating costs, penalties, and long-term reputational erosion. Use conservative and optimistic scenarios to account for uncertainty.
Monetary
Lost sales, overtime, recovery hardware, fines.
Non-financial
Reputation, customer trust, legal exposure, employee morale.
Identify CBFs
  • Rank functions by combined impact score.
  • Document dependencies that cause cascading failures.
  • Assign preliminary RTO/RPO recommendations for each CBF.
Example
For a portal, the CBF might be authentication plus customer data access; losing either has immediate high impact across revenue and compliance.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize based on combined impact, not technical complexity, and document rationale so decisions are repeatable.
