IRT — Core Roles
Assemble technical, legal, and communications expertise with clear decision authority.
Incident Handler
Lead technical response: contain, collect evidence, and coordinate restorations.
Forensic Analyst
Preserve artifacts, analyze root cause, and produce evidence usable by legal.
Legal & Compliance
Manage breach notification obligations and advise on regulatory risk and liability.
Communications & PR
Control external messaging to customers, regulators, and media to protect reputation and meet disclosure requirements.
Human Resources
Handle employee-facing issues, insider threat investigations, and notifications where personnel are involved.
Management / Executive
Declare incident severity, authorize public statements, and allocate resources for recovery.
Use a RACI matrix to make responsibilities explicit and update it after tabletop exercises to reflect real-world constraints.
