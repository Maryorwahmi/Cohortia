NIST Incident Response Lifecycle
A disciplined four-phase process that guides actions during incidents.
1. Preparation
Policies, team formation, tools, training, and playbooks before incidents occur.
2. Detection & Analysis
Alerts, triage, and scope determination to understand what happened and where.
3. Containment, Eradication & Recovery
Limit impact, remove threat, and restore systems safely.
4. Post-Incident Activity
Lessons learned, playbook updates, and improvements to monitoring and controls.
Key Takeaway

Define clear entry and exit criteria for each phase so teams can act without delay.

Concrete Example
A malware alert triggers detection. Analysts confirm lateral movement, containment isolates network segments, eradication removes the payload, recovery restores clean images, and post-incident reviews change patching cadence and backup verification cadence.
Common Mistake
Treating phases as strictly linear; in practice, analysis and containment iterate as new data appears.
