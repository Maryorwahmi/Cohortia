IR vs BC/DR — Roles
IR contains and removes threats; BC/DR restores services and business functions. Both must align on priorities.
Incident Response
Technical investigation, containment, evidence preservation, and root cause analysis.
Business Continuity / DR
Maintain or restore business-critical services through alternate processes or recovery environments.
Concrete Example
Following a ransomware event, IR confirms which images are clean. BC/DR uses only validated backups to restore services. If backups are compromised, BC/DR switches to alternate recovery sites or manual workarounds while IR removes the threat.
Common Mistake
Running BC/DR restores without IR validation, which can reintroduce compromised systems into production.
Key Takeaway

Align IR and BC/DR priorities and handoffs so recovery is fast but also safe from reinfection or data loss.
