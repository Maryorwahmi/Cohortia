What to Remember
Three compact, high-impact actions to improve readiness.
Action 1
Align playbooks with critical assets and expected threat vectors.
Action 2
Run tabletop and live exercises to validate tools, templates, and contacts.
Action 3
Feed IR lessons into BC/DR priorities and update recovery sequences accordingly.
Take a five-minute audit now: pick a playbook and confirm it has current contacts, tested runbooks, and a validated backup strategy.
