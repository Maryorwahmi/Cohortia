Tabletops and Simulations
Use discussion and technical drills to validate plans and tools.
Tabletop Exercise
Scenario discussion focused on decisions, communications, and escalation paths.
Live Simulation
Technical drill that validates tool readiness and recovery procedures under time pressure.
Red/Blue Exercises
Offensive tests expose detection gaps while defenders exercise containment and eradication steps.
After-Action Review

Capture findings, assign remediation, update playbooks, and schedule follow-up tests.

Concrete Example
A tabletop revealed stale PR contact info; a live drill showed backup encryption. Both findings led to quick corrective actions and improved playbook fidelity.
