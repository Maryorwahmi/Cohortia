Playbooks and Runbooks
Create clear triggers, decision points, and exact technical steps for repeatable response.
Playbook Example: Ransomware
Isolation, backup verification, legal notification, recovery order, and communication templates.
Runbook Content
Exact imaging commands, verification checksums, and steps to restore from a specific backup set.
Concrete Scenario
During a drill, a runbook encountered a changed file path. The runbook was updated immediately and the revised version re-tested, which prevented similar delays in the real incident later.
Common Mistake
Failing to tie playbooks to specific critical assets and not updating runbooks after system changes.
Key Takeaway

Make playbooks actionable, asset-specific, and keep runbooks current through regular testing.
