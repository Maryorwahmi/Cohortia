Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Preparation Phase
Preparation — Practical Components
Build policy, procedures, playbooks, identify team, choose tools, and run tests before incidents.
Policy & Procedures
Define authority, reporting thresholds, and procedural steps for common incident types.
Playbooks & Runbooks
Step-by-step guides for specific incidents like ransomware or data breach, with decision points and recovery priorities.
Training & Testing
Tabletops and simulations validate assumptions and reveal missing tools or stale contacts.
Key Takeaway

Preparation is active: documentation plus practiced, asset-focused playbooks and tested tools.

Concrete Scenario
Imagine a ransomware alert on a build server. A playbook instructs immediate isolation of the server, verification of last clean backup, notification of backups owner, and legal assessment. Because the playbook was tested, the backup verification step is automated and contact info is current, shortening downtime substantially.
Common Mistake
Creating high-level policies without actionable playbooks tied to specific assets and teams leaves responders uncertain under pressure.
Pro Tip
Keep a prioritized list of critical assets and ensure a playbook exists for the top-tier assets first.
