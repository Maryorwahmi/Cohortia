Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures
TTPs describe what actors do and how they do it, providing behavioral patterns that drive effective detection and response.
Tactic
High-level objective, e.g., credential access
Technique
Method used, e.g., credential stuffing
Procedure
Tooling and steps, e.g., botnets and specific endpoints targeted
Use in operations
Map detection rules to techniques, and map playbooks to procedures for faster containment.
Concrete example
A credential stuffing campaign shows repeated login attempts from distributed IPs with a consistent user-agent and an unusual login cadence. Analysts tag this as a technique and update detection to look for the cadence and user-agent pattern, which is resilient when IPs rotate.
Misconception check
Do not treat TTPs as immutable signatures. Actors adapt; use TTPs as behavioral insights to inform adaptable rules.
