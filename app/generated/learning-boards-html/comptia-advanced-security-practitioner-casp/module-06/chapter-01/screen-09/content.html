Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Collection
Primary sources
  • Internal logs and telemetry (auth, web, network)
  • OSINT and dark-web monitoring
  • Commercial threat feeds and vendor enrichments
  • ISAC/ISAO sharing for sector context
Quality check
Ensure timestamps, identifiers, and required fields are present before forwarding to processing.
Source mapping example
IR: Which botnet ranges target our login API? Primary sources: authentication logs and commercial botnet feeds. Secondary: web server logs and OSINT forum chatter for corroboration.
Common mistake
Collecting too many generic feeds without mapping them back to requirements results in high storage costs and low analyst value.
