Planning
Define intelligence priorities tied to assets and threats to avoid collecting everything.
Analysis
Correlate IoCs, attribute activity, and produce recommendations for detection and response.
Feedback
Stakeholder input refines requirements and ensures products remain relevant.
Why it matters
The lifecycle enforces discipline: collection stays focused, analysis produces context, and feedback closes the loop so intelligence evolves with need.
