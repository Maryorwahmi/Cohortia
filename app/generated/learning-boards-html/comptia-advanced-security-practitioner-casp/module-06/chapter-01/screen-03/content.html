Scenario Practice
You will identify assets, hypothesize threat actors, and write intelligence requirements. Each requirement must be a question whose answer reduces risk or improves detection.
Template
Organization, Recent incidents, Key assets, IRs, Sources
Example IR
What botnet ranges and user-agent strings are used in credential stuffing attempts? (Sources: logs, commercial botnet feeds)
Why this matters
Clear requirements reduce noise and speed mitigation because collection targets specific answers tied to risk.
Sources Quick Guide
  • Internal logs and telemetry for observable activity
  • OSINT and dark-web forums for actor chatter
  • Commercial feeds for curated IoCs and enrichment
  • ISACs for sector-specific context and sharing
