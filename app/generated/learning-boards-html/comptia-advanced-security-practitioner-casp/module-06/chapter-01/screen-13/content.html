Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Feedback
Feedback mechanisms
  • Surveys and product reviews
  • SOC ticketing and analyst notes
  • Operational metrics: IOC action rate, time-to-detect
Continuous improvement loop
Collect feedback, update requirements, adjust collection and processing, and measure outcomes. Repeat on a cadence that suits your threat context.
Practical step
Hold quarterly reviews with SOC and leadership to validate intelligence value and re-prioritize requirements.
