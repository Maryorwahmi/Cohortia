Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › IoC vs TI
Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)
  • Atomic observables: IPs, hashes, domains
  • Feed directly into detection tools
  • Fast to apply but limited context
Pitfall
Relying solely on IoCs creates alert fatigue and may miss the broader campaign.
Threat Intelligence (TI)
  • Enriched and analyzed outputs
  • Includes TTPs, attribution, and impact assessment
  • Guides prioritization and strategic decisions
When to use
Feed IoCs to security tools; use TI for playbooks, executive briefings, and detection tuning.
Concrete example
A feed lists IPs used in scanning. Intelligence links those IPs to a group using credential stuffing against e-commerce sites, identifies the targeted endpoints, and recommends both immediate blocking and longer-term mitigations such as rate limiting and MFA enforcement.
