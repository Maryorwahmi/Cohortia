Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Intelligence Levels
Strategic
High-level trends, geopolitical motivations, and long-term risks for leadership decisions and investment planning.
Operational
Actor TTPs, campaign context, and playbooks for SOCs and incident responders.
Tactical
IoCs and short-lived indicators for automated detection and blocking in security tools.
Guideline
Always tailor product format and detail level to the consumer; do not hand raw IoCs to executives or remove context from operational reports.
