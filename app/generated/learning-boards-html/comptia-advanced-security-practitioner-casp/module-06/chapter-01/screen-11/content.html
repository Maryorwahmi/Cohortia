Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Analysis
What analysis produces
Attribution hypotheses
Reasoned links between activity and known actors, including confidence levels.
TTP mapping
Identify techniques used and recommend detection signatures or behavior rules.
Impact assessment
Estimate potential business impact and urgency for response.
Actionable recommendations
Concrete steps for SOCs, engineering, and leadership to reduce risk.
Misconception
Analysis is more than running correlation rules; it requires context and judgment to produce useful intelligence.
