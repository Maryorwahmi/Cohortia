Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Planning
Start with Assets and Risks
Inventory 3–5 critical assets and their acceptable risk levels. Map threat scenarios that could impact each asset and convert scenarios into intelligence questions.
Example asset list
Customer database, payment gateway, web servers, authentication services
Sample intelligence requirement
What botnet IP ranges and user-agent strings are currently used against our login API?
Who should be involved
Security leadership, risk owners, SOC leads, and analysts should jointly prioritize requirements so collection supports decision-making.
Practical tip
Limit initial requirements to three focused questions to ensure rapid, high-quality collection and analysis.
