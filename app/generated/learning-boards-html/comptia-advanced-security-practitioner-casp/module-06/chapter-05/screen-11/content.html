Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Safety & Privacy
Operational Safety Checklist
  • Prefer analysis in copies or non-production environments.
  • Coordinate with change control and system owners for live investigations.
  • Minimize capture windows and limit host scope for packet or memory captures.
Privacy & Compliance
Restrict access to user data, redact sensitive fields when sharing, and record justifications for access.
Resource Impact
Large packet captures and heavy EDR queries can affect performance. Use targeted captures and scheduled forensics when possible.
Document actions to preserve chain of custody and to support follow-up response steps.
Key Takeaway

Balance investigative needs with safety and privacy: minimize impact, document actions, and use retained telemetry when possible.
