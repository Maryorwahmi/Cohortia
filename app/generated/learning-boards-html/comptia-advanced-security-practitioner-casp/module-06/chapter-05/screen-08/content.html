Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Behavioral Analytics
What is a baseline?
A statistical model of normal activity for a user or host: logon patterns, typical destinations, and usual process families.
How hunters use it:
  • Prioritize anomalies that deviate strongly from baseline.
  • Correlate anomalies with SIEM events and EDR process histories.
  • Reduce noisy alerts by tuning baselines and exclusion rules for known exceptions.
Concrete Scenario
A user who normally works 9 to 5 logs in at 3 AM from a foreign IP and reads a sensitive database. This sequence is an anomaly that deserves a hunt.
Key Takeaway

Treat anomalies as high-value leads, then validate them with telemetry and context before concluding compromise.

Misconceptions & Guidance
• Anomaly does not equal compromise; investigate context.
• Baselines must be periodically refreshed to reflect legitimate changes in behavior.
• Use anomaly scoring to stack-rank investigations by risk, not to auto-contain without review.
Pro Tip
Enrich anomaly alerts with asset criticality and recent patch status to prioritize human review.
