Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › User reports, threat intel, and hunting
Process

Hypothesis-driven hunting and human reports

Form a hypothesis, gather EDR and network data, analyze for patterns, and feed validated indicators back into SIEM and controls.

Step 1: Hypothesis
Define what you are looking for, e.g., a skimmer C2 pattern across POS hosts.
Step 2: Collect
Gather EDR logs, netflow, DNS queries, and relevant host artifacts for the time window.
Step 3: Analyze
Look for patterns, pivots, or persistent connections until you can confirm or refute the hypothesis.
Concrete scenario
An employee reports POS slowness. Threat intel shows a skimmer C2 IP. Hunt across POS servers for matching outbound patterns and suspicious processes.
Key takeaway
Human reports and hunting complement automated detection and often reveal stealthy compromises faster.
