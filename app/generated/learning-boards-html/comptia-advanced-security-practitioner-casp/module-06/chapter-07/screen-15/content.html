Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Short-term containment
Fast actions to stop spread: isolation, segmentation, service shutdown, or account disablement.
Long-term containment
Re-architect network segments and deploy enhanced detection to prevent re-infection.
Common mistakes
Destroying evidence by acting too fast, or allowing persistence by acting too slowly.
Final takeaway: Containment choices must be guided by analysis and evidence preservation, and their operational impact must be documented and communicated.
