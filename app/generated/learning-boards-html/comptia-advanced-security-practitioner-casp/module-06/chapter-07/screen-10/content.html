Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Triage: quick assessment
Checklist

Ask focused questions and capture volatile evidence

Triage must be fast but evidence-aware: isolate only after capturing memory and logs when possible.

Immediate questions
  • What alerted and why?
  • Is the activity expected maintenance?
  • Which services are affected?
Priority evidence
  • Memory dump and running process list
  • Active network connections and packet capture
  • Relevant logs (auth, application, firewall)
Decision record
Document triage conclusion, severity, evidence collected, and who performed the collection.
Common mistake
Shutting systems down without capturing volatile data destroys forensic artifacts and complicates later analysis.
Check your understanding
Given a high-severity alert, name the top three items you would capture before any shutdown.
Key Takeaway

Triage must be rapid and evidence-first: capture volatile data, document decisions, and avoid premature shutdowns.
