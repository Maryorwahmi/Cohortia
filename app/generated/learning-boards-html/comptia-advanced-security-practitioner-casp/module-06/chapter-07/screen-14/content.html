Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Detection
Identifying security events using SIEM, EDR, IDS/IPS, user reports, and hunting.
Analysis
Investigate alerts to find scope, severity, and initial root-cause leads.
Triage
Rapid assessment to separate noise from true incidents and set preliminary severity.
Scoping
Determine affected systems, accounts, and duration of presence.
Severity
Assign risk level by impact to confidentiality, integrity, and availability and to business operations.
Evidence preservation
Collect volatile and persistent artifacts and record chain-of-custody before destructive steps.
Summary: Use these terms as a decision checklist during incidents to guide evidence capture and containment.
