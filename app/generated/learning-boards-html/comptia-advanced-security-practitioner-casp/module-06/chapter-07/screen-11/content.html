Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Scoping and severity assessment
Hierarchy

Map compromise → pivots → data access → business impact

Work outward from the confirmed host to find affected accounts and systems, and then rate severity by business consequences.

Level 1
Initial host or account confirmed by logs and EDR.
Level 2
Pivot points: lateral movement paths and elevated accounts used.
Level 3
Data access: what sensitive files or databases were exposed or modified.
Concrete example
POS server contacts C2. Scope: other POS hosts, payment DB queries, merchant account access. Severity: high due to cardholder data exposure.
Key takeaway
Scoping must extend beyond the initial host to capture pivot paths and data access that determine severity.
Documentation prompt

For each scope level, note evidence sources, timestamps, and the assessor who validated the finding. This supports containment and later root-cause analysis.
