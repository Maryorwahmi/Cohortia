Learning Objectives

Understand and apply detection, analysis, containment, and evidence preservation.

Bloom
Learn Understand Apply Master
01
Identify detection sources

Recognize SIEM, IDS/IPS, EDR, AV, user reports, intelligence, and hunting as signals. Knowing who and what alerts you speeds response.

Mnemonic: "Signals" = SIEM, IDS, EDR, Staff reports
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Perform rapid analysis

Use triage and scoping to determine if an event is real, how many systems are affected, and what data is at risk. This focuses effort effectively.

Visual: quick triage flowchart (Triage → Scope → Severity)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Choose safe containment

Apply short-term actions like isolation or segmentation, and plan long-term controls. Balance risk to evidence and business continuity.

Visual: containment spectrum (isolate ↔ segment ↔ shutdown)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Preserve evidence consistently

Collect forensic images, volatile memory, and logs before disruptive containment steps whenever possible. Proper handling preserves investigative value.

Visual: evidence bag icon with memory, disk, logs
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
