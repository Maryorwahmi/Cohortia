Practical checks and anti-patterns

Short prompts and mistakes to avoid when time matters.

Quick Triage Prompts
  • Confirm the alert source and correlate logs to avoid false positives.
  • Identify impacted services and potential business impact.
  • Decide which volatile evidence to capture first and who will collect it.
Containment Anti-patterns
  • Too-fast shutdowns that destroy memory or live forensic data.
  • Over-containment that halts essential business processes unnecessarily.
  • Under-containment that leaves lateral movement paths open.
Stakeholder Communication
Document proposed containment, likely impact, and evidence steps before action to align with operations and legal teams.
Tabletop Readiness
Use drills to practice triage questions, evidence capture order, and reversible containment so real incidents are less chaotic.
