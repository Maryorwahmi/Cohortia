Example
A SIEM flags outbound connections from a point-of-sale server. An analyst triages, preserves memory, then isolates the host or segments the POS VLAN depending on risk.
Common Mistake
Shutting down systems before collecting volatile data can destroy crucial evidence and hamper analysis.
