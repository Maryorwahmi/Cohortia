Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › Internal Sources
Internal telemetry stack
SIEM
Aggregates logs and correlates events to reveal patterns across systems.
EDR
Endpoint alerts and process behavior that expose compromise methods.
Network Telemetry
NetFlow and PCAP provide communication context and anomalies.
Human Reports
Analyst observations and help-desk tickets often reveal suspicious emails or behavior.
Explanation & example
  • Internal sources show who and what are affected, enabling targeted response and forensics.
  • Example: a SIEM flags multiple failed logins from one IP, then a successful login — a high-priority lead to investigate for credential compromise.
  • Misconception: treating internal logs as complete; missing telemetry or parsing errors can hide events.
  • Validation step: normalize timestamps, enrich with asset owner, and correlate across EDR and network logs before blocking.
Key Takeaway

Internal intelligence anchors alerts to assets and users, making response more precise.
