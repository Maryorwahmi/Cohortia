Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › IoCs
Common IoC types
  • IP addresses (e.g., 192.0.2.1)
  • Domains and URLs
  • File hashes (for example, SHA256 hashes)
  • Email addresses used in phishing campaigns
Operational notes
  • IoCs are ideal for quick signatures and IOC-based blocks.
  • They expire fast; confirm persistence before long-term blocks.
  • Enrich with TTPs and actor context to make decisions more durable.
Example
An IP listed in a public blocklist is a tactical IoC. To elevate it to operational intelligence, seek associated TTPs, actor attribution, or corroborating internal evidence before taking disruptive action.
