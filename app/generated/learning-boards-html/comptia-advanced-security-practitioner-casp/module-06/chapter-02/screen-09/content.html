Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › TTPs
What TTPs tell you
They describe attacker behavior such as credential theft, lateral movement, or command-and-control techniques. TTPs focus detection on the methods attackers use, not on single-use artifacts.
Operational use
  • Map alerts to known techniques to prioritize response.
  • Design detections for sequences of behaviors rather than isolated artifacts.
  • Enrich TTP findings with IoCs and actor context for decision-making.
Example
Instead of blocking a specific hash, detect the behavior of credential dumping and alert for investigation, which thwarts variants that change their binary but use the same technique.
