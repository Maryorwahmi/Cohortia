Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › STIX / TAXII
STIX
A structured language for representing indicators, TTPs, and actor information in machine-readable format.
TAXII
A protocol to transport STIX objects between servers and clients, enabling automated synchronization of intelligence.
Integration checklist
  • Normalize timestamps and object fields on ingest.
  • Filter by relevance: industry, geography, and confidence levels.
  • Enrich STIX objects with internal asset context before acting.
