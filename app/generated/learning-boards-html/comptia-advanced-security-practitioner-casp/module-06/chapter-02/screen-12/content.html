Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › Validate & Curate
Validation steps
  1. Assess source credibility and curation level.
  2. Correlate with internal telemetry for corroboration.
  3. Enrich with asset and TTP context.
  4. Apply relevance filters and confidence scoring.
Curation practices
  • Keep provenance metadata for audit and tuning.
  • Use staged automation to limit impact from low-confidence indicators.
  • Run periodic reviews to refine thresholds and sources.
Example Mistake
Blindly ingesting a public blocklist can create many false positives. Instead, score each entry and require internal corroboration before blocking high-risk assets.
