Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › Signal → Action
4-step response process
  1. Detect: identify suspicious events in SIEM or IDS.
  2. Enrich: pull EDR, NetFlow, and authentication logs for context.
  3. Validate: cross-reference external feeds and known TTPs.
  4. Decide: block, isolate, monitor, or escalate with minimal disruption.
Supporting notes
  • Automate enrichment to add EDR evidence and asset owner before human review.
  • Misconception: immediate blocking is always best — consider business impact first.
  • Use internal context to justify temporary containment when external confirmation is pending.
  • Keep an audit trail of correlation and decisions to refine detection rules later.
Scenario
Multiple failed logins from one IP, then a success. Enrich with EDR and NetFlow to confirm lateral movement before isolating the host.
