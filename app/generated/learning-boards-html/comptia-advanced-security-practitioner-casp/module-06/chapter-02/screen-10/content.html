Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › Actor Profiles
What’s included
  • Motivation and typical targets
  • Observed TTPs and historical campaigns
  • Known infrastructure and associated IoCs
How to use profiles
  • Prioritize controls for assets likely targeted by the actor.
  • Adjust monitoring and playbooks to detect the actor’s favored techniques.
  • Update incident response plans with actor-specific considerations.
Caution
Attribution can be uncertain. Use profiles to inform mitigation and monitoring, not to assume motive without corroboration.
