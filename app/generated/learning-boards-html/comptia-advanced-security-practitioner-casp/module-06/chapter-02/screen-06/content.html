Module 06 › Chapter 6.2 › OSINT
What OSINT gives you
  • Early warnings about new vulnerabilities and campaigns.
  • Public IoCs and researcher analysis that can guide immediate checks.
  • Community-driven context that may not yet appear in commercial feeds.
Limitations & precautions
  • Can be noisy, unverified, or stale.
  • May require legal/ethical review for some sources.
  • Always validate before automated blocking to avoid false positives.
Example
A public advisory describes a new exploit being used in the wild. Use it to prioritize patching and then look for confirmatory internal logs or trusted community reports before broad network blocks.
