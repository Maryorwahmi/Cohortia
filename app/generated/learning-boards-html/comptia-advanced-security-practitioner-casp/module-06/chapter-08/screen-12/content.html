Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Speed vs Thoroughness
Pitfall comparison
Rushed recovery
Fast restoration but high risk of re‑infection when root cause remains.
Overly cautious
Extended downtime and business impact, though lower technical risk.
Mitigation strategy
Use acceptance tests, isolated staging restores, and parallel rebuilds to meet business needs while ensuring safety. Document all acceptance criteria and decision rationale.
Checklist
Acceptance tests: artifact absence, updated patches, rotated credentials, successful functional tests, and monitored baseline for a defined window.
