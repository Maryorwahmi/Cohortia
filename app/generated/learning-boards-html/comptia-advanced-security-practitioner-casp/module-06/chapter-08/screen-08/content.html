Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Patching & Credentials
Patching checklist
  • Identify vulnerable systems and similar configurations.
  • Apply tested patches in a controlled rollout with backups.
  • Record patch timestamps and versions for audit and verification.
Tip: coordinate with change control and maintain isolation until validation completes.
Credential rotation steps
  1. Prioritize high‑risk credentials: administrative and service accounts.
  2. Rotate secrets and revoke old tokens; ensure propagation across services.
  3. Log rotations and confirm successful authentication for dependent services.
Pitfall
Rotating credentials while persistence remains can capture the new credentials. Remove persistence or monitor closely during rotation.
