Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Restore & Rebuild
Recovery options
Restore from clean backup
Fast when backup integrity is verified and the snapshot predates the compromise.
Rebuild / reimage
Safer when compromise depth is unknown or backups may be tainted; takes longer but reduces risk.
Validation checklist
  • Verify backup timestamp and immutability.
  • Scan restored image with updated signatures and heuristics.
  • Test authentication and business workflows in isolation.
  • Enable enhanced logging and IDS before production rejoin.
Key Takeaway
Isolation plus verification is the guardrail that prevents a restored system from reintroducing an attacker.
