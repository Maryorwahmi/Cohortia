Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Role of Threat Intelligence in Hunting
Definition

How intelligence guides and improves hunts

Strategic
High-level advisories and threat actor targeting that prioritize where to hunt.
Operational
TTPs and ATT&CK mappings used to form strong, testable hypotheses.
Tactical
IoCs for quick sweeps and for enrichment when a hit is found.
Practical use
Use operational TTPs to craft a hypothesis, then validate quickly with tactical IoCs if available, and prioritize with strategic context.
Common mistake
Not recording intelligence provenance and confidence reduces trust and reusability. Always document source and date.
Key Takeaway

Blend strategic, operational, and tactical intelligence for prioritized, focused hunts and feed validated findings back into your intelligence platform.
