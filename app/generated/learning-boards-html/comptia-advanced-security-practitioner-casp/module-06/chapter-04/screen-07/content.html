Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Threat Hunting Methodologies
Comparison

Choose the right method for the situation

Hypothesis-driven
Start with a TTP-based claim and test it with targeted queries. Efficient when you have operational intelligence.
Known indicator
Search for specific IoCs from feeds. Fast but limited by indicator freshness.
Behavioral
Identify anomalous behaviors and deviations from baselines to detect novel threats.
Concrete example
For PowerShell Empire: hypothesis-driven hunting looks for encoded PowerShell and suspicious parent processes; indicator hunting checks recently published C2 IPs; behavioral hunting finds unexpected process chains and spikes in credential usage.
Misconception check
Relying only on indicators misses unknown variants. Combine methods to cover fast validation, focused depth, and unknown detection.
Key Takeaway

Select methodology based on intelligence and urgency, and combine methods to reduce blind spots.
