Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › What is a TIP?
Core Concept

Threat Intelligence Platform — definition

A TIP aggregates and operationalizes intelligence so teams can turn data into defensive actions.

Definition
Central hub that lifecycle-manages threat data from many sources.
Role
Normalize, enrich, correlate, prioritize, and distribute intelligence.
Concrete example

An analyst submits a suspicious phishing URL. The TIP enriches it, links it to past campaigns, assigns a high risk score, and pushes it into a SOAR playbook to block and investigate.

Misconceptions & checks
  • Not just storage: TIPs add context and workflows; they do not magically fix gaps without tuning.
  • Human oversight still required: scoring and source selection need active governance.
  • More sources don't always help—quality and relevance matter more than quantity.
Key Takeaway

A TIP is the operational hub that converts raw feeds into prioritized intelligence ready for automated and analyst-driven action.
