Firewalls, EDR, and Vulnerability Systems

Design graded responses and ensure rollback to avoid business disruption.

NGFW / IDS
Push curated blocklists with expiry and confidence thresholds to reduce false positives.
EDR
Feed IoCs and hashes for endpoint detection and drive containment playbooks when internal compromise is suspected.
Vulnerability Mgmt
Ingest intelligence on actively exploited CVEs to reprioritize patching and asset remediation lists.
Concrete example
TIP identifies a malicious domain used in phishing; it is added to the NGFW's blocklist and the SIEM watches for internal DNS queries referencing that domain.
Best practice
Implement staged actions (alert → quarantine → block) and always log and audit automated changes for rollback and tuning.
