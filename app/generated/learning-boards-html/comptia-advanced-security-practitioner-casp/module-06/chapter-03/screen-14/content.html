Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
Threat Intelligence Platform (TIP)
A centralized system for aggregating, normalizing, enriching, analyzing, and disseminating intelligence.
Ingestion Capabilities
Connectors for commercial feeds, TAXII/STIX, OSINT, SIEM/EDR exports, and manual inputs.
Normalization
Convert varied formats into a consistent schema so correlation and enrichment work reliably.
Summary

TIPs are valuable when paired with defined intelligence needs, source hygiene, and governance for automated actions.
