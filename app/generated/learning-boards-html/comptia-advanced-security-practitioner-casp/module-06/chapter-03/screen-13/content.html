Deployment challenges and mitigations

Address source quality, legacy systems, governance, and tuning as part of deployment planning.

Source vetting
Define intelligence needs and subscribe only to relevant, high-quality feeds; measure signal-to-noise.
Legacy integration
Use exports, ETL, or lightweight forwarders to map legacy fields into the TIP schema reliably.
Governance
Document who can change automated actions, scoring thresholds, and how to audit TIP-driven changes.
Tuning
Continuously review scoring, enrichment quality, and correlation outcomes; incorporate analyst feedback loops.
Key Takeaway

Deploy a TIP in phases with focused sources, integration mapping, governance, and continual tuning to turn intelligence into a reliable operational asset.
