Process Flow

Correlation engines and pattern detection

Combine normalized indicators and enrichment to identify related activity and emerging campaigns.

Correlation methods
  • Rule-based matches (shared IPs, domains, ASN)
  • Heuristic clustering (similar hosting and naming patterns)
  • Temporal linking (sequence and reuse of infrastructure)
Operational guidance
Tune thresholds, review provenance, and create feedback loops so analysts can refine correlation rules over time.
Concrete example
A phishing domain is linked to previously known C2 infrastructure through shared hosting patterns and recent historical sightings, prompting a medium-to-high priority flag.
Misconception
Correlation suggests relationships; it rarely proves definitive attribution. Analysts must validate links using provenance and supporting evidence.
