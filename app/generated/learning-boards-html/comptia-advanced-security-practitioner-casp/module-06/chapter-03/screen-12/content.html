Automated ingestion and dissemination

Enable speed but retain controls: thresholds, expiry, and human gates.

Benefits
  • Faster detection and response
  • Consistent enforcement across controls
  • Lower manual workload
Guardrails
  • Scoring thresholds and expiry
  • Human-in-the-loop for high impact changes
  • Comprehensive logging and rollback
Staged actions
Alert → Review → Quarantine → Block, based on confidence and impact.
Example
A TIP pushes high-confidence C2 IPs to NGFW blocklist automatically and creates a SOAR incident for analyst review of related internal alerts.
Common mistake
Automating actions without expiry or rollback leads to persistent blocking of legitimate services after false positives.
