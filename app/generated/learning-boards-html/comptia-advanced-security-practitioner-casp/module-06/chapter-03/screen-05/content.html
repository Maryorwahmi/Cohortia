Ingestion connectors and policies

Ingest with intent: pick sources that match your threat profile and manage freshness and volume.

Connector types
  • APIs and webhooks from commercial feeds
  • TAXII/STIX for structured sharing
  • CSV ingestion from exports and legacy tools
Operational policies
  • Polling cadence and freshness windows
  • Rate limits and error handling
  • Filter rules to reduce irrelevant data
Manual inputs
Analyst submissions and embargoed reports that require handling and verification before distribution.
Example
A TIP polls a TAXII server for STIX objects hourly, imports SIEM-observed suspicious IPs every 15 minutes, and accepts analyst-submitted IoCs for immediate enrichment and triage.
Common mistake
Enabling many feeds without vetting causes noisy ingestion and alert fatigue. Start small and tune.
