Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Scoring & Prioritization
Core Concept

Scoring indicators to reduce alert fatigue

Rank and prioritize intelligence using configurable factors tied to your threat profile.

Scoring factors
  • Source reputation and feed quality
  • Freshness and prevalence
  • Enrichment and correlation evidence
Operational use
Drive alerts, SIEM rules, SOAR playbooks, and firewall push lists using thresholds and verification gates.
Transparency
Expose scoring factors so analysts can understand and tune priorities, and for auditing decisions.
Example
An indicator with multiple corroborating sources and recent internal sightings receives a high score and is fed into automated containment flows.
Pitfall
Automating blocking solely on score can cause business disruption; implement confirmation for high-impact actions.
