Risk Scoring Elements

  • Impact: sensitivity and business value of the resource.
  • Exposure: public endpoints, network reachability, or internet-facing risks.
  • Exploitability: presence of known vulnerabilities or weak authentication.
  • Context: related identities, recent changes, and environment tags.
Example prioritization

A low-severity S3 public read rule may be deprioritized unless it appears in an attack path to a database containing sensitive PII, in which case it becomes high priority.

Attack Path Snapshot

Graph analysis shows chained steps: compromised account → privileged role assumption → access to sensitive storage. Use this to recommend targeted fixes such as restricting role assumption or revoking service permissions.
Common Mistake

Treating all findings equally leads to wasted effort. Prioritize based on path impact and exploitability.
