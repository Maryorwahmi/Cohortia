Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Operational Guidance

Integration and ownership

Assign remediation owners, feed findings into ticketing, and integrate with SIEM for context.

Key metrics
  • Time to detect and time to remediate
  • Recurring misconfiguration count
  • Percentage of automated remediations that succeeded
Runbook example
  1. Validate finding and identify owner
  2. Assess risk and attempt guided remediation
  3. If repeat offender, enforce IaC policy to prevent future deploys
Common Mistake

No designated owner leads to backlog and drift. Ensure SLAs and automation where appropriate.
