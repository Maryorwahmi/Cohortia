CSPM: Continuous posture monitoring

A discipline that focuses on cloud resource configurations and compliance over time.

Why CSPM matters
  • Detects misconfigurations like public storage, open security groups, and over-privileged roles.
  • Continuously checks resources against benchmarks such as CIS or regulatory controls.
  • Helps evidence collection for audits and compliance reporting.
Concrete example

An S3 bucket set to public-read becomes visible in CSPM, tagged as a CIS failure, and assigned a remediation playbook.

CSPM capability map
Asset Discovery

Continuous inventory across accounts and subscriptions prevents blind spots.

Policy Assessment

Evaluate resources against CIS, NIST, PCI and custom rules.

Findings & Reports

Create auditor-ready evidence and prioritize remediation.

Remediation

Options range from guided instructions to fully automated fixes.

Key Takeaway

CSPM reduces time-to-detect for misconfigurations and supports repeatable compliance checks when integrated with operations.
