Operational CSPM Outcomes

Practical capabilities that turn posture data into prioritized actions and fewer false positives.

Risk Scoring

Use impact and exploitability to rank findings so teams work the most dangerous issues first.

Attack Path Analysis

Graph-based analysis shows how combined small issues can lead to high-value targets.

CIEM

Detect excessive or unused permissions and enforce least privilege across cloud accounts.

Integration

Ship findings to SIEM, ticketing, or Security Hub to tie CSPM into incident response and change control.

Alert Hygiene

Tune rules, apply suppression for known safe configurations, and build runbooks to avoid alert fatigue.
