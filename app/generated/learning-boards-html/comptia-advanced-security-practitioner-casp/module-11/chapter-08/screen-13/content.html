CSPM Roadmap

Phase 1
Discover
Inventory and baseline scan to find urgent issues.
Phase 2
Policy
Define rules, enable audit-only monitoring.
Phase 3
Remediate
Triage, pilot automation, assign owners.
Phase 4
Shift Left
IaC scanning in CI/CD to prevent insecure code.
Phase 5
Mature
CIEM, attack-path analysis, prioritized automation.
Key Takeaway

Adopt CSPM iteratively: validate policies and owners first, then add automation and shift-left controls to maintain a secure baseline.
