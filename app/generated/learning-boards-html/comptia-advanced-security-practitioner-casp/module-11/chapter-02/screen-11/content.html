Operational best practices

Institutionalize repeatable controls to cover customer responsibilities across IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS.

Data classification and encryption
Label data sensitivity, encrypt accordingly, and manage keys with clear ownership.
IAM and least privilege
Centralize identity, enforce MFA, and periodically review roles and service accounts.
Logging and monitoring
Collect logs, retain audit trails, and alert on anomalous activity relevant to customer controls.
Incident response and vendor due diligence
Have cloud-specific runbooks and verify provider certifications and shared responsibility matrices.
Implementations
  • CSPM for continuous configuration checks
  • Automated patch pipelines and hardened images
  • Secrets vault integrated into CI/CD
Key Takeaway

Turn customer responsibilities into policies and automation so they become repeatable and auditable as your cloud footprint grows.
