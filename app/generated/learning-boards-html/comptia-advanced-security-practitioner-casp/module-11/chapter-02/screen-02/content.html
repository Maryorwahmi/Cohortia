Learning Objectives
Develop actionable skills to assign, test, and enforce cloud security responsibilities.
01
Articulate the model
Explain what CSPs secure versus what customers secure, with examples from IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify responsibilities
List which security tasks are typically managed by the CSP and which remain customer duties for each model.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Analyze shifts
Compare how the boundary moves as you choose IaaS, PaaS, or SaaS and what that means for controls and monitoring.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design customer controls
Create practical strategies — patching, IAM, logging, and audits — to cover customer-side responsibilities.

These stages guide how you'll move from recognizing responsibilities to implementing and validating customer-side controls.
Why this matters
Misunderstanding where responsibility lies is a frequent cause of breaches. These objectives are designed to turn that confusion into clear, testable controls.
