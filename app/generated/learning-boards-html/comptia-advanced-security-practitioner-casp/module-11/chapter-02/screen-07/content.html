Comparison

What stays with you in SaaS

Identity and access
User accounts, MFA, role assignments, and lifecycle management remain customer duties.
Data governance
Classification, retention policies, export controls, and compliance mapping stay with the customer.
Application configuration
Privacy settings, sharing options, and permission scopes must be configured and audited by you.
Key Takeaway

SaaS reduces platform risk but increases reliance on identity hygiene and data governance — prioritize those controls.

Concrete Example
A SaaS office suite operator manages the service's availability and patching. You must enable MFA for users, set sharing defaults to private, and implement retention rules to satisfy compliance.
Misconception
Believing SaaS removes organizational responsibility for data lifecycle or access is a risky assumption that leads to exposed data and weak audit trails.
