Common mistakes and fixes

Mistake
Overly broad IAM policies
Remedy: scope policies to specific actions and ARNs.
Mistake
Hardcoded secrets or env variables used as secrets
Remedy: migrate to Secrets Manager and enforce runtime retrieval.
Mistake
Missing input validation
Remedy: enforce schemas and parameterized DB access.
Mistake
Insufficient logging and monitoring
Remedy: log structured events and forward to SIEM with alerts.
Final takeaway

Apply the small, specific remediations above consistently across functions to reduce risk and make security measurable and auditable.
