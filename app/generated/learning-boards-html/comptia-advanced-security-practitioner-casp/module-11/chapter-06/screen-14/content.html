Module 11 › Chapter 11.6 › Key Concepts
Function-as-a-Service
Short-lived, stateless compute that requires code-level defenses.
Shared responsibility
Provider secures runtime; customer secures code, config, data, and IAM.
Observability
Structured logs, masked secrets access, and SIEM forwarding for detection and response.
Summary

Design functions with layered protections: validate at edge and in-code, scope permissions, and secure secrets while ensuring logs support rapid triage.
