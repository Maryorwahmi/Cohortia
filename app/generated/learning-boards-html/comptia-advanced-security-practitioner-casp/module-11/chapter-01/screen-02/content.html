Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Learning Objectives
01
Differentiate deployment models

You will identify public, private, hybrid, and multi-cloud models and articulate their unique security implications, including tenancy, control, and compliance effects.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain service model responsibilities

You will explain what customers must secure versus what the provider secures for IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, and FaaS, with practical examples like OS hardening and application-layer controls.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Analyze trade-offs

You will weigh security, control, and operational overhead when recommending models, explaining why an organization might accept more responsibility for better control.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design secure architectures

You will map application components to appropriate cloud models, justify decisions for security and compliance, and outline secure connectivity approaches for hybrid designs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
