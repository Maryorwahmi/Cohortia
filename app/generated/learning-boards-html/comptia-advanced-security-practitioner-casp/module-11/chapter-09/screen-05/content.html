Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › Shared Responsibility Model (IR)
Definition

The shared responsibility model separates provider-owned infrastructure from customer-managed workloads; this determines investigative access.

Provider responsibilities
Physical data center security, hypervisor maintenance, and underlying host environments.
Customer responsibilities
Application security, configurations, access controls, logs, and snapshots for forensics.
Analogy & takeaway
Analogy: imagine renting a storage unit. The facility secures the building; you lock your boxes and keep inventory. During a theft, the facility provides footage; your inventory and locked containers are your responsibility. Similarly, in cloud IR you must maintain logs, snapshots, and access controls to investigate incidents affecting your data and services.
