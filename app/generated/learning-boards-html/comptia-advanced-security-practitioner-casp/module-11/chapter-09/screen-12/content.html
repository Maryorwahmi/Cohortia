Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › Break Glass procedures and playbooks
Break-glass essentials
  • Secure storage in a vault with audit logs and time limits.
  • Multi-person approval and immediate logging of access events.
  • Limited-scope, time-bound credentials to reduce risk.
Playbook components
Alert trigger, scoped CLI commands for containment, snapshot steps, artifact tagging, and notification and escalation actions. Include required IAM privileges and expected outputs.
Practice

Run tabletop drills that exercise the playbook and break-glass access so teams can perform under pressure.
