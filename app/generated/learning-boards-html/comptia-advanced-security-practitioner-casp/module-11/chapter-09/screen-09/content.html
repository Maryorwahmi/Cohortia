Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › Snapshotting and preserving artifacts
What to snapshot and why
  • All attached volumes for a compromised VM, including root and data volumes.
  • Databases or backups that could contain exfiltrated data or altered records.
  • Configuration snapshots and IAM role metadata to track potential privilege misuse.
Tag snapshots with incident identifiers and store original API call logs that show the snapshot creation command to support chain of custody.
Storage & protection
Keep artifacts in versioned, encrypted storage with restricted access and audit logging. Record who created or accessed each artifact and when.
Key Takeaway

Snapshots plus the logs showing their creation form the defensible forensic package you will analyze offline.
