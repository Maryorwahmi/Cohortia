Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › API-driven collection & chain of custody
Collection checklist
  • Export relevant audit and flow logs for the incident timeframe.
  • Create snapshots and tag with incident metadata.
  • Record API calls that performed collection and preserve those logs.
Chain of custody items
Incident ID, collector identity, timestamps, storage location, and access events. Maintain auditable records and restrict access to artifacts.
Common Mistake

Using personal accounts for evidence collection breaks the chain of custody. Use auditable service principals or break-glass credentials with full logging.
