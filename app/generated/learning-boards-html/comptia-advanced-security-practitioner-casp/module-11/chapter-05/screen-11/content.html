Process Flow

Audit and monitor IAM

  • Enable CloudTrail / Activity Logs to capture IAM API events.
  • Use Access Analyzer and identity governance tools to find risky policies.
  • Schedule periodic reviews of roles, groups, and keys.
Alerting example

Create alerts for root account usage, policy changes, or unexpected key creation and integrate them with your incident response workflow.

Concrete checklist
  • Enable and retain audit logs for all accounts and regions.
  • Run Access Analyzer to detect external access to resources.
  • Automate periodic reviews and decommission unused credentials.
Common mistake

Assuming logs alone are enough; without review, alerts, and governance, important events will be missed.

Key Takeaway

Collect logs, analyze them with identity tools, and tie alerts to response playbooks so IAM incidents are discovered and remediated quickly.
