Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Key Concepts
Identity and Access Management
Controls who can access what resources and which actions they can perform.
Federated Identity
Authenticate with an external IdP so cloud accounts are not separately managed.
Single Sign-On (SSO)
One authentication that grants access to multiple independent services.
Summary takeaway

Centralize identity and enforce policies consistently. Federation plus IAM policies and MFA form a layered, auditable defense.
