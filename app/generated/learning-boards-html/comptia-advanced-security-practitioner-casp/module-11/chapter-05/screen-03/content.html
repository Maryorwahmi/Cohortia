Learning Objectives — Applied Readiness

01
Federation verification

Map AD groups to an IAM role via SAML metadata exchange, sign in through your IdP, and confirm temporary credentials are issued by the cloud provider.

Mnemonic: metadata handshake
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Policy scope test

Create a tag-restricted policy and attempt actions on tagged and untagged resources to confirm only allowed operations succeed.

Mnemonic: tagged gate
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
MFA enforcement check

Add aws:MultiFactorAuthPresent condition and verify that sensitive actions fail when MFA is not present and succeed when it is.

Mnemonic: two-factor gate
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Programmatic role validation

Attach a role or managed identity to a compute resource and confirm it can access storage without embedded access keys.

Mnemonic: no keys on disk
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
