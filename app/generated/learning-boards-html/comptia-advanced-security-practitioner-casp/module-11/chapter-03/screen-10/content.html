Definition • Lifecycle
Versioning • Retention • Archival
Core controls
  • Enable versioning on object stores to recover deleted or overwritten objects.
  • Apply lifecycle rules to move objects to archive tiers and to expire objects when retention ends.
  • Keep separate, immutable logs and backups with restricted access.
Avoid these mistakes
  • Leaving buckets publicly accessible by default.
  • Not enabling default encryption for new buckets or volumes.
  • Granting broad IAM permissions to storage resources.
Key Takeaway

Use versioning, lifecycle policies, and encryption together. Reduce stale data and log access to detect misuse while controlling costs and exposure.
