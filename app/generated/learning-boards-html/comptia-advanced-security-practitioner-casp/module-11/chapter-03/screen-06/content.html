Comparison
Managed identity vs hardcoded credentials
Managed Identity / Instance Profile
  • No long-lived credentials in instance files.
  • Attach scoped policies that reference specific resources.
  • Rotate and audit permissions centrally.
Example: an instance role allowing s3:GetObject and s3:PutObject for a single uploads bucket.
Hardcoded credentials
  • Credentials may be stored in files or environment variables and are harder to rotate.
  • Compromise of the instance can expose the keys to attackers.
  • Often paired with overly broad policies, creating elevated risk.
Misconception: network-only protections prevent misuse of credentials — they do not stop misuse once credentials are present.
Key Takeaway
Prefer managed identities and scoped policies; review privileges periodically to keep permissions minimal.
Check
Audit IAM policies for wildcards and unused permissions monthly.
