What to Remember
Three core areas
Instance Hardening
Remove unnecessary services, enforce key-based auth, and bake secure images.
Encryption
Enable default encryption at rest and TLS for transit across all storage types.
Network Segmentation
Use VPCs, subnets, security groups, and NATs to control flows and reduce lateral movement.
Summary
Combine hardening, encryption, least privilege, and segmentation with monitoring and lifecycle policies to build resilient IaaS environments.
