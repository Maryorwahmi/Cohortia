What to Remember
Practical audit items
Encrypt & Block Public
Ensure default encryption and block public access at account and bucket levels.
Tighten Security Groups
Remove wide CIDRs, prefer security-group references for inter-tier access.
Automate Patching & Images
Bake patches into images and automate deployments to keep instances current.
Final takeaway
Combine these practical checks into your day-to-day audits to catch the most common configuration risks quickly and reliably.
