Operational Skills
Read & Remediate CSPM Findings
Map alerts like public storage or open SSH to specific remediation steps and verify closure in the tool.
Prioritize by Context
Use asset criticality and threat intelligence to surface the highest-impact issues first.
Integration & Avoiding Mistakes
Integrate with SIEM & CI/CD
Forward critical alerts to SIEM and block unsafe images in CI/CD pipelines before deployment.
Common Mistakes
Ignoring tuning leads to alert fatigue; agents deployed without testing can cause performance issues.
