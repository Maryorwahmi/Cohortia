Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Compliance Monitoring
Capability

Automated compliance mapping and reports

CSPM maps configuration findings to CIS and regulatory frameworks and generates evidence that supports audits and controls verification.

Report Example
Show percent compliance for benchmarks and list critical non-compliant resources with remediation guidance.
Validation
Validate critical findings before action to avoid disrupting intended exceptions or test environments.
Practical Tips
  • Customize frameworks to internal standards and tag known exceptions.
  • Ensure exports are auditor-friendly and scoped per account or subscription.
  • Prioritize critical findings and verify before remediation.
Key Takeaway

Use CSPM reports to provide audit evidence and to focus remediation on the most impactful non-compliant resources.
