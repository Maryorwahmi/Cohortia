Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Scenario: CSPM & CWPP Integration
Step 1: Identify Gaps
  • Multi-cloud visibility gaps across AWS and Azure.
  • Configuration drift from manual changes.
  • Insufficient runtime detection in Kubernetes and VMs.
Use CSPM to close posture gaps and CWPP to add runtime telemetry and protection.
Step 2: Choose Features
CSPM: Multi-cloud auditing, compliance mapping, risk prioritization.
CWPP: Image scanning in CI/CD, runtime protection, micro-segmentation.
Step 3: Integration Points
Connect CSPM to accounts via read-only APIs and configure policies.
Deploy CWPP agents/sidecars with staging tests and forward telemetry to SIEM.
Develop IR playbooks and feed findings into developer workflows for remediation tracking.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize features that close visibility gaps and enable automated, auditable remediation while preserving developer velocity.
