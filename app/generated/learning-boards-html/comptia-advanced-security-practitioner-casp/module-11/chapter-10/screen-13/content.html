Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Assessing Alerts
CSPM Alert
Verify exposure, classify sensitivity, prioritize by impact, and remediate configuration or update policies. Add evidence to audit logs.
Checklist
  • Confirm the finding and its scope.
  • Assess asset criticality and data sensitivity.
  • Remediate or document approved exception.
CWPP Alert
Treat as a potential incident: collect telemetry, consider isolating the workload, and forward to SOC for containment and investigation.
Checklist
  • Capture runtime logs and traces.
  • Isolate or throttle the workload if evidence supports containment.
  • Correlate with CSPM findings in SIEM for root cause.
Key Takeaway

Correlate configuration and runtime alerts. CSPM guides fixes; CWPP guides containment and investigation. Both are needed for a confident response.
