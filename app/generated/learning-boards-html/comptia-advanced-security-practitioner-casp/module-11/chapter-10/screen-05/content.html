Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › CSPM Features & Capabilities
Process

Capabilities you will use

1. Configuration Auditing
Continuous scanning for misconfigurations like public buckets, 0.0.0.0/0 ports, and missing MFA on root accounts.
2. Compliance Monitoring
Mapping findings to CIS and regulatory frameworks and producing audit-ready reports.
3. Risk Prioritization
Combine asset criticality and threat intelligence so high-impact issues surface first.
Examples & Choices
Automated Remediation
If allowed, apply a policy to block public access or trigger a function to remediate detected misconfigurations.
Visibility
A single pane of glass across AWS and Azure helps maintain consistent posture across accounts and subscriptions.
Common Mistake
Not tuning alert rules leads to noise and alert fatigue. Integrate findings into workflows to avoid churn.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize CSPM findings with context and integrate remediation into operations; automated fixes are powerful but must be controlled.
