Operational Practice
Health Checks and Capacity Planning
Scheduled monitoring and tests keep ingestion, indexing and searches healthy.
Key metrics to monitor
  • Indexing queue length and indexing latency.
  • Search head CPU, memory, and query latency.
  • Storage consumption and retention compliance.
  • Parsing error rates and unparsed events percentage.
Maintenance cadence
Weekly: review ingestion queues and slow searches.
Monthly: capacity trending and storage tier adjustments.
Quarterly: load tests and architecture validation against growth forecasts.
Takeaway: Treat capacity planning as continuous; use metrics to guide scaling rather than reactive upgrades.
