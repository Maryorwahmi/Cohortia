Reducing False Positives with Baselining
Turn noisy thresholds into behavioral alerts by measuring normal activity.
Baselining steps
  1. Collect baseline metrics per user, asset, and service.
  2. Build statistical filters to detect deviations from baseline.
  3. Create exceptions for known automated or high-volume accounts.
  4. Measure false positives and iterate on rules.
Concrete example
Instead of alerting on >10 failed logins, alert when failed logins exceed three times a user's baseline within a time window, excluding scheduled batch accounts.
Pro tip: Automate baseline recalculation periodically to adapt to evolving patterns.
