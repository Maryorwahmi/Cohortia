Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Statistical vs Behavioral
Statistical correlation
Detects deviations from historical aggregates: spikes, rates, and counts. Works well for noisy, high-volume metrics.
Example: failed login rate > historical mean + 3 standard deviations.
Best for: servers, network flows, bulk transfers.
Behavioral correlation
Builds entity profiles and alerts on deviations from established behavior patterns, focusing on identity and intent.
Example: a user who never accessed repos now downloading source code.
Best for: high-value users, privileged accounts, critical services.
Combined strategy
Use statistical baselines to catch volume anomalies and behavioral profiles for identity-based attacks. Weight alerts by asset criticality and confidence. Track model drift and retrain when necessary.
Key Takeaway

Choose statistical methods for high-volume signal detection and behavioral profiling for entity anomalies. Use both together for higher fidelity.
