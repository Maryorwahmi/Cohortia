Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Alert Fatigue
Noise reduction workflow
  1. Filter known benign events and reduce verbosity from instrumentation.
  2. Establish baselines per user and asset to identify meaningful deviations.
  3. Apply dynamic thresholds that adapt to time-of-day and historical context.
  4. Prioritize alerts by asset criticality, role, and confidence score.
Metric examples
Track true positive rate, false positive rate, mean time to respond, and analyst time per alert to measure tuning effectiveness.
Tuning patterns
Add contextual checks rather than removing rules; for example, require sensitive data access plus suspicious login flags.
Use time-aware thresholds: different limits during business hours vs off hours.
Group related alerts into incidents to reduce analyst cognitive load.
Key Takeaway

Tune rules and thresholds; prioritize by context. Measure outcomes to ensure tuning improves detection and analyst efficiency.
