Log Management and Event Correlation at Scale

This lesson shows how to collect terabytes of logs, normalize and enrich those records, and apply correlation logic that detects attack chains while reducing analyst noise. It emphasizes practical pipelines, parsing patterns, and real investigative workflows.

In this lesson
Centralized collection and scale
Designing pipelines and storage to handle high-velocity streams.
Coming up
Parsing, normalization, enrichment
Map diverse fields to a common model and add context for correlation.
Also focus
Event correlation & alert tuning
Temporal, statistical, and behavioral patterns; reducing noise.
Concept Map
Ingestion & Storage — scalable queues and distributed indices
Parse → Normalize → Enrich — consistent fields for correlation
Correlation & Hunting — temporal windows, baselines, and threat hunting
Why this matters
Centralized, enriched logs let you connect low-severity events into high-confidence incidents and shorten time-to-detect and time-to-respond.
