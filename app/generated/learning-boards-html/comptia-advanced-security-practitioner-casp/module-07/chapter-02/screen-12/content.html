Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Hunting & Forensics
Hunt example
Search for unusual process creation on endpoints combined with outbound connections to unknown IPs in the last 24 hours. Pivot to user accounts and recent authentication events to build context.
Investigation tips
  • Save hunts as templates and annotate results for future refinement.
  • Use enrichment to prioritize results by asset criticality and user role.
  • Export immutable timelines for legal or compliance requirements.
Forensic workflows
Normalize timestamps to UTC and verify time synchronization across sources before building timelines.
Preserve raw log copies in cold immutable storage and restrict access to maintain chain of custody.
Document pivots and query steps to support reproducibility of findings.
Key Takeaway

Centralized, normalized, and enriched logs enable efficient hunting and accurate forensics; keep them durable and auditable.
