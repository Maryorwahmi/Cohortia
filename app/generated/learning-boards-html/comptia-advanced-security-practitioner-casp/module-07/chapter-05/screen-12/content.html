Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Operational Checklist
Common mistakes
  • Failing to enable data events for S3 or equivalent.
  • Not enabling logs in all necessary regions.
  • Misconfigured retention and overly wide read access to log buckets.
  • Not forwarding orchestration/audit logs from container platforms.
Onboarding timeline (example)
Day 1 — Enable CloudTrail, VPC Flow Logs, and Activity/Audit Logs for the account.
Day 2 — Enable data events for critical S3 buckets and configure encryption and access policies.
Day 3 — Verify logs arrive in the central SIEM and test metric filters.
Day 4 — Tune baselines, set retention policies, and document the configuration.
Validation
Automate checks to confirm logging is enabled and alerts are tested. Maintain change control for logging configurations and SIEM ingestion rules.
