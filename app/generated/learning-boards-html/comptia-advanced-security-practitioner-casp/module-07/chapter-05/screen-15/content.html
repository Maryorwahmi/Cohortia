Enable
Ensure CloudTrail / Activity Logs / Audit Logs and data events are enabled for critical resources.
Integrate
Send CSPM and CASB findings, plus provider logs, to your SIEM for enrichment and correlation.
Correlate
Combine API anomalies with network or posture signals for high-confidence detections.
One final thought

Treat logging and monitoring as code: automate, test, and validate so your visibility is dependable when an incident occurs.
