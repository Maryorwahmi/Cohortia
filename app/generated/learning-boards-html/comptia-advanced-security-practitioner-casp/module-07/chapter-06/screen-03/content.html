Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Learning Objectives — Applied Skills
Practical Skills

Outline and demonstrate actions for a phishing compromise and understand verification steps before returning systems to service.

Playbook drafting

Create step-by-step actions for detection, containment, and cleanup with assigned roles.

Evidence handling

Record chain of custody entries and always work on copies of original evidence.

Containment choices

Learn short-term vs long-term containment tradeoffs for ransomware and credential compromises.

Verification

Threat hunt and deep scans before recovery; avoid restoring into a compromised state.

Assessment prompts
  • Explain the primary objective of immediate isolation during a ransomware event and its operational consequence.
  • Draft the first four steps for a phishing playbook covering Preparation through Eradication.
  • List at least three pieces of chain of custody metadata you must record.
