Chain of Custody essentials
  • Record: who, when, why, and what was handled
  • Always image originals and work on copies
  • Record hashes/checksums of evidence media
  • Keep secure, access-controlled storage for originals
Practical example

Imaging a compromised server: document the imaging utility and version, record the operator, record start and end times, compute and store hash values, and store the original in a secure evidence locker.

Regulatory note

Reporting obligations and privacy controls vary by jurisdiction and sector; involve legal counsel early when required.

Common mistake

Altering original evidence or failing to log handlers invalidates chain of custody and harms legal outcomes.
