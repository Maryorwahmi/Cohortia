Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Eradication Phase
Eradication checklist
  • Remove malware and persistence mechanisms
  • Patch exploited vulnerabilities and reconfigure services
  • Rotate compromised credentials and revoke access tokens
  • Reimage hosts when integrity cannot be guaranteed
Practical example

After detecting credential theft and lateral movement, the IRT resets passwords, revokes sessions, hunts for unknown service accounts, and patches the exploited server. If unknown binaries or rootkit behaviors remain, the team chooses a reimage to ensure a clean state.

Misconception

Deleting suspect files is sometimes insufficient; persistent mechanisms may survive and allow re-entry.

Key Takeaway

Verification through threat hunting and scans is mandatory before recovery to avoid reopening an incident.
