DFIR Lifecycle
A continuous loop that converts incidents into lessons and stronger defenses.
Preparation

Team, playbooks, tools, logging, and rehearsals make response predictable and fast.

Identification

Validate alerts, correlate events, and confirm the presence and scope of compromise.

Containment

Stop spread while balancing evidence preservation and availability.

Key Takeaway

The lifecycle is cyclical: Lessons Learned must feed back into Preparation to reduce future risk.

Process flow
Prep
→
Identify
→
Contain
→
Eradicate: remove root cause and persistence.
Recover: restore services from clean backups and verify integrity.
Lessons Learned: update playbooks, detection rules, and training.
Example: Phishing that leads to stolen credentials
  • Preparation: MFA and playbook prepared.
  • Identification: unusual login alerts from SIEM and user report.
  • Containment: force password reset and terminate sessions.
  • Eradication: check endpoints for persistence, reimage if infected.
  • Recovery: verify logs and restore access with stronger controls.
  • Lessons Learned: update email filters and user training.
