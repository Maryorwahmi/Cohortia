Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Containment Strategies
Short-term containment
  • Isolate endpoints from the network
  • Block malicious IPs and URLs
  • Force password resets and terminate sessions
  • Quarantine emails or removable media
When to use it

When rapid action is needed to stop spread; document steps and preserve evidence copies before destructive actions.

Long-term containment
  • Reconfigure network segmentation and firewall rules
  • Patch vulnerabilities and rotate keys
  • Remove persistence mechanisms and backdoors
  • Harden identity and access controls
Purpose

Prevent attacker re-entry and reduce the likelihood of recurrence while preserving forensic evidence.

Common mistake

Taking broad, irreversible actions without evidence copies can destroy critical forensic data and delay legal investigations.
