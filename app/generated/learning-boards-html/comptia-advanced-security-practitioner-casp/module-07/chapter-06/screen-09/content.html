Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Recovery Phase
Recovery checklist
  • Restore systems from verified clean backups
  • Verify file and system integrity
  • Bring systems online incrementally and monitor
  • Validate application workflows and connectivity
Example

After eradication, a server is restored from a backup taken before the incident, patched, tested under load, and monitored for suspicious behavior before being returned to production.

Common mistake

Restoring into a network without verifying eradication can re-introduce an attacker into critical systems.

Pro tip

Keep multiple backup generations and test restore procedures regularly to ensure recoverability when it matters.

Why it matters

Recovery ties technical fixes to operational trust — thorough testing preserves business continuity and stakeholder confidence.
