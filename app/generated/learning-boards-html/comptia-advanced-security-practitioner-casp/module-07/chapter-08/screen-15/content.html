Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Quick Reference
Record hashes
Compute SHA256 before analysis and include it in reports so defenders can block or hunt quickly.
Isolate and snapshot
Use dedicated VMs, disable host sharing, and take clean snapshots before execution.
Document and correlate
Log timestamps, tool outputs, and IoCs so you can correlate Procmon, Wireshark, and debugger findings.
Final takeaway

A disciplined, documented approach combining static, dynamic, and reverse-engineering yields the most reliable threat intelligence.
