Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Key Concepts
NSM (Network Security Monitoring)
Collect, analyze, and escalate network data to detect and respond to incidents.
IDS / IPS
IDS alerts; IPS blocks. Tune both to reduce false positives and operational risk.
FPC & DPI
Full packet capture and deep inspection reveal payloads for forensic proof when needed.
Summary takeaway

Combine alerts, flows, and targeted packet captures, apply governance, and tune consistently to keep NSM effective and compliant.
