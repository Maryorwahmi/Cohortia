Concept Preview
Layered telemetry: alerts, flows, and packets
Alerts
IDS/IPS identify suspicious signatures or behaviors and raise alerts for analyst review.
Flows
NetFlow/IPFIX summarizes conversations for pattern analysis across the network.
Packets
Full packet capture enables payload inspection and forensic reconstruction.
Key Takeaway

Use the least data necessary for detection and the right data for investigation: flows for scale, packets for content.
