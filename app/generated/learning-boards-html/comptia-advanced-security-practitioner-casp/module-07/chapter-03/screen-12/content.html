Rules, retention, and governance

  • Tune IDS/IPS regularly and test rules in passive mode before blocking inline.
  • Retain flows longer than packets; keep packet captures scoped and time-limited.
  • Encrypt captures at rest, limit access, and log access events for chain-of-custody.
  • Document retention justifications and review them for compliance.
Common mistake
Retaining large volumes of raw packet data without governance creates storage and legal risk. Prefer targeted captures with clear retention windows.
Pro tip

Automate enrichment (threat intel, DNS resolution, asset tags) so analysts see context before opening a packet file.
