Process

Turn flow records into leads

  1. Baseline: Learn normal ports, volumes, and top talkers so deviations stand out.
  2. Detect: Use thresholds and anomaly detection to surface unusual spikes or new destinations.
  3. Prioritize: Focus on sensitive hosts and connections to suspicious geographies or ports.
  4. Enrich: Correlate with IDS alerts, DNS logs, and endpoint telemetry for context.
  5. Capture & Confirm: Run targeted packet captures when you need payload or session reconstruction.
Concrete example
A server shows frequent flows to an external IP on port 4444. Correlate with IDS alerts for unusual DNS, then run a targeted capture to look for C2 payloads.
Pro tip
Store aggregate flow metrics longer than packets; flows are small and powerful for retroactive hunting across months of history.
Pitfall

Avoid chasing isolated spikes without enrichment; they often reflect legitimate maintenance or backups.
