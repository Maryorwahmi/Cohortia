Comparison

IDS (passive) vs IPS (inline)

Know the strengths and the operational trade-offs when choosing monitoring versus prevention.

IDS — detect and alert
  • Generates alerts without affecting traffic flow.
  • Good for broad visibility, baseline creation, and forensics.
  • Lower operational risk but may require more analyst follow-up.
IPS — active prevention
  • Can block or drop malicious traffic inline.
  • Useful for immediate containment of known threats.
  • Requires careful tuning and testing to avoid service disruption.
Practical advice
Deploy IDS broadly and use IPS selectively. Test new blocking rules in passive mode first. Keep logs of blocked traffic to preserve forensic trails and review signature updates regularly.
Common mistake

Activating default IPS rules without tuning leads to false positives or, if suppressed, missed threats. Tune with real traffic baselines.
