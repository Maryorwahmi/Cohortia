Full Packet Capture

Capture when payload matters

Use FPC to reconstruct sessions, view payloads, and collect evidence for forensics and malware analysis.

  • Ideal for incident response and confirming what data was transferred.
  • Deploy selectively to critical segments or after an anomaly is detected.
  • Implement strict access control, encryption, and retention policies to protect captured content.
Example policy
Keep targeted captures for a limited investigative window, encrypt them, and log access so only authorized analysts can read packet payloads.
Common toolset
Use tcpdump, Wireshark, and Arkime (Moloch) for capture and analysis. Filter before capturing to limit scope.
Watch out

Don’t capture everything; storage and privacy risks grow quickly. Prefer targeted captures with clear retention rules.
