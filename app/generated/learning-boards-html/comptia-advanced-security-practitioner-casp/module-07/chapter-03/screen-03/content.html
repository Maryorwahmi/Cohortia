Applied Objectives

These objectives stress repeatable actions: tuning, targeted capture, retention, and hypothesis-driven hunting.

01 • Tuned Detection
Reduce noise by creating custom rules and scheduling reviews of signature updates.
02 • Sensor Placement
Place IDS/IPS where visibility meets risk: perimeter, DMZ, and sensitive internal segments.
03 • Capture Strategy
Targeted FPC with filters to avoid storing unnecessary or sensitive traffic while preserving evidence.
04 • Hunting Hypotheses
Form hypotheses such as DNS tunneling and use flow and Zeek logs to validate them efficiently.
Verification
Each objective is paired with a verified competency milestone: run a capture, tune a rule, analyze flow spikes, and correlate alerts in a SIEM.
