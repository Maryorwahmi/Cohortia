Endpoint Detection and Response

Understand how EDR agents gather telemetry, how platforms detect behavioral anomalies, and how automated responses reduce mean time to respond.

In this lesson

You will explore EDR architecture, agent deployment strategies, telemetry types, threat hunting techniques, automated response actions, and SIEM integration through concrete examples.

Why it matters

As perimeter protections are bypassed, endpoints become critical detection points; EDR turns raw telemetry into actionable detection and containment capabilities.
