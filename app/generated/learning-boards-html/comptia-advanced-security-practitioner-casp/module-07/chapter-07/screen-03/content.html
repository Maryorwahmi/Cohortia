05
Use Write-Blockers Correctly
Understand when to employ a hardware write-blocker to prevent accidental writes to evidence drives.
Mnemonic: Block writes, keep originals pristine

06
Create & Verify Hashes
Generate MD5 or SHA256 on both original media and image to prove they match exactly.
Mnemonic: Hash original, hash copy, compare
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Apply Network Forensics
Capture and analyze full packet captures to extract payloads, reconstruct sessions, and spot beaconing.
Mnemonic: Capture → Parse → Reconstruct
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Maintain Chain of Custody
Document who handled evidence, when, and what tools were used to preserve admissibility of findings.
Mnemonic: Log every touch
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Practical Reminders
  • Always operate from trusted media when acquiring live memory.
  • Hash both the original and the image immediately after acquisition.
  • Work on copies for analysis; never alter the original evidence.
