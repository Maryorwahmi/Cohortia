Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Chain of Custody
What to Record
  • Who handled the evidence, with timestamps and signatures where possible.
  • Every tool and command used, including version numbers and checksums.
  • All hash values for originals and copies, and storage locations.
Practical Tip
Use standardized custody forms or an evidence management system to ensure consistent, auditable records across the team.
Common Failure
Informal notes, missing hashes, or unsigned transfers are frequent causes of evidence challenges in court.
Key Takeaway

Documentation is the thread that ties evidence handling to your analysis outputs. Without it, technical findings may be disregarded.

Quick Checklist
Photograph, label, hash, write-block, image, hash image, secure storage, document every transfer.
