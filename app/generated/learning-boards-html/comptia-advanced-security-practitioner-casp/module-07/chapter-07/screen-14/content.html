Module 07 › Chapter 7.7 › Key Concepts
Digital Forensics
Acquiring, preserving, analyzing, and reporting on electronic data to uncover evidence.
Live Acquisition
Collect volatile RAM, processes, and network state from a running system.
Dead Acquisition
Collect data from powered-off media, typically via a write-blocker to protect the original.
Takeaway

Choose the acquisition method to match the evidence type: volatile data first, persistent media with a write-blocker next, and always verify with hashes.
