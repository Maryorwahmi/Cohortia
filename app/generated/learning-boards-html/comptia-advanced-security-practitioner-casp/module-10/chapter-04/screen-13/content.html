Remediation Prioritization and Policy Enforcement
How to rank fixes and turn findings into enforced actions.
Key ranking factors
  • Exploitability and exploit maturity
  • Whether the vulnerable code path is used
  • Dependency type: direct versus transitive
  • Business criticality of the component
Policy examples
Fail builds for critical direct CVEs; create advisory comments for low-risk transitive items.
Analogy
Think of triage like emergency response: treat immediate threats to critical systems first and schedule lower-priority maintenance later.
Common mistake
Treating every finding as equal can overwhelm teams and delay fixes for truly critical items.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize by exploitability, usage, dependency type, and business impact, and reflect those priorities in automated policies.
