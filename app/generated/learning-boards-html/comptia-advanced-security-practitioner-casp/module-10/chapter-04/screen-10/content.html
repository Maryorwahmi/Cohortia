Strategies
Managing and Mitigating OSS Risks
Practical policies and operational controls to reduce exposure.
Component governance policy
Define acceptable licenses, approval steps, and vulnerability thresholds for components.
Automate SCA scanning
Run scans in PRs and builds and generate SBOMs per artifact.
Curate internal repositories
Host vetted versions of critical components to control what enters builds.
Additional practices
  • Integrity checks: verify hashes and signatures of downloaded packages.
  • Monitor vulnerability feeds and integrate them with your SCA tooling.
  • Educate developers on dependency hygiene and interpreting SCA reports.
Operational note
Pair enforcement with remediation processes and ticketing to avoid blocking development without a path to fix.
Key Takeaway

Implement layered controls: policy, automation, curation, integrity checks, monitoring, and education work together to reduce risk.
