Integration
Integrating SCA into CI/CD
Shift detection left and tie findings to workflows.
Pull request checks
Run lightweight scans on PRs to stop risky dependencies before merging.
Build-time scans
Scan full build artifacts to include transitive dependencies and compiled modules.
Policy actions
Fail builds, open tickets, or mark as advisory depending on configured thresholds.
Practical considerations
  • Tune rules to reduce developer friction and focus on high-impact findings.
  • Scan images and artifacts in addition to source to catch automation-introduced packages.
  • Integrate SCA findings with ticketing so remediation is tracked and verified.
Example rule
Block merges when a direct dependency has a critical CVE, but only raise an advisory for transitive medium-severity findings.
Key Takeaway

Place SCA in PRs and builds, tune policies for signal, and connect findings to your remediation workflow.
