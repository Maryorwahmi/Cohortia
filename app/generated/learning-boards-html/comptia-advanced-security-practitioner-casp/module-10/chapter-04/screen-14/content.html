Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Key Concepts
Software Supply Chain Security
Protect the integrity and provenance of all components and processes in software delivery.
Software Composition Analysis (SCA)
Automated tools that detect components, transitive dependencies, CVEs, and license issues.
Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)
A machine-readable inventory of components, versions, suppliers, identifiers, licenses, hashes, and dependency graphs.
Summary takeaway
Combine SCA, SBOMs, and governance to reduce attack surface and enable fast, targeted remediation when vulnerabilities are disclosed.
