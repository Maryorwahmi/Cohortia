Core Concept
What is Software Supply Chain Security?
Protect the integrity and provenance of code, components, and build processes.
Definition
Security across development, build, and deployment steps including third-party components.
Analogy
Like an ingredient list for a product: you need transparency to react when an ingredient is unsafe.
Why it matters
  • Reduces incident scope by identifying affected artifacts.
  • Supports compliance and faster remediation when CVEs are disclosed.
Concrete scenario
A web service uses a popular logging library. That library receives a critical vulnerability disclosure. Without an inventory you must search repositories and builds manually; with an SBOM and SCA you can immediately identify which builds and containers include the vulnerable version and prioritize remediation.
Common misconception
Relying solely on runtime or perimeter defenses gives a false sense of safety. Visibility into what you ship is required to act decisively.

Key Takeaway

Supply chain security is about visibility and control of components and processes to limit exposure and accelerate response.
