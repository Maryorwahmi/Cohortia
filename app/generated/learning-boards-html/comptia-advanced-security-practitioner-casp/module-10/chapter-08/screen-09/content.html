Manufacture
Provisioning Service
Customer Cloud
Sequence: secure key injection -> device receives manufacturer certificate -> device boots and presents certificate to a provisioning service -> service validates manufacturer claims and issues operational credentials scoped to the deployment.
Concrete controls: signing keys in HSM, transport encryption during handoff, unique identities per device, and revocation capability. Keep an auditable record for traceability.
Key Takeaway

Automate provisioning with chained attestations and keep private keys isolated to prevent mass compromise at scale.
