Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Unique Challenges
Definition
What makes IoT devices different
  • Resource constraints: limited CPU, memory, storage, and power affect crypto and logging.
  • Physical access: devices may be tampered with or probed on-site.
  • Long lifespans: devices can operate for years without maintenance.
  • Supply chain risk: components or firmware can be compromised prior to deployment.
Concrete Example
An industrial temperature sensor with a tiny microcontroller sending unencrypted UDP packets may be easy to intercept and spoof. Without signed firmware and a unique identity, a single compromised unit can be cloned or used to inject false telemetry.
Common Misconceptions
  • Encryption alone fixes all problems — false for constrained devices without proper key storage.
  • Physical protections are optional — physical access enables many powerful attacks.
Key Takeaway

Treat device constraints, physical exposure, and supply chain integrity as primary design drivers for practical security.
