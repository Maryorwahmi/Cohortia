Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Logging & Response
Telemetry Requirements
  • Log failed authentications, configuration changes, and update events.
  • Keep event schemas compact for constrained devices.
  • Authenticate telemetry and transport over TLS/DTLS.
Gateway Aggregation Pattern
Use gateways to aggregate device logs, normalize events, and forward to SIEM. Gateways can buffer during connectivity loss and enforce stronger crypto on outbound connections.
Key Takeaway

Define the smallest set of high-value events devices can produce, secure their transport, and ensure they feed into incident response workflows for meaningful detection.
