Design: threat model, choose secure components, minimize privileges, plan FOTA. Manufacture: secure key injection, unique identities, supply chain vetting. Deployment: zero-touch onboarding, certificate provisioning, network segmentation. Operation: encrypted telemetry, signed updates with rollback protection, centralized logging. Decommission: secure erase of keys and data, revoke certificates.
Concrete example: provision unique X.509 device certificates at the factory, sign firmware images with an HSM-held key, and require devices to verify signatures and prevent rollbacks. Maintain an SBOM to track vulnerable components over time.
Key Takeaway

Secure provisioning and signed updates are operational controls that significantly reduce long-term compromise risk for long-lived devices.
