Embedded Systems and IoT Security
Constraints, hardware trust, lifecycle protections, and secure communications for real-world devices.

This chapter explains why embedded devices need a layered approach. We will show practical examples such as signed firmware for remote sensors, device identity at manufacture, and lightweight transport security chosen for constrained CPUs. Expect design-level recommendations and deployable mitigations you can apply in enterprise and industrial environments.
