Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Hardware Security
Architecture
Chain of trust from HRoT to application
Immutable HRoT initializes and verifies bootloader. Bootloader verifies kernel. Kernel verifies applications. Each step uses digital signatures that trace to the HRoT.
TPM vs HSM
TPM: device-level secure storage and measurements. HSM: higher-assurance key management used for signing in gateways or cloud services that provision many devices.
Concrete Workflow
  1. Manufacturing: inject unique identity and provisioning certificate.
  2. Signing: firmware signed by an HSM-held key.
  3. Runtime: HRoT verifies bootloader that verifies signed firmware.
Common Mistake

Relying on software-only checks without an immutable HRoT makes signature checks vulnerable to firmware rollback or bypass.
