Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Attack Vectors & Mitigations
Default/Weak Credentials
Mitigation: enforce unique strong passwords, disable default accounts, or use certificate-based auth.
Unpatched Vulnerabilities
Mitigation: secure FOTA, SBOM, and prioritized patching based on risk.
Physical Tampering
Mitigation: tamper-evident seals, encrypted storage, HRoT and hardware protections.
Insecure Communication
Mitigation: TLS/DTLS with mutual authentication and vetted ciphers.
Supply Chain Attacks
Mitigation: vet suppliers, verify component integrity, and sign firmware.
DDoS from Compromised Devices
Mitigation: segment networks, apply rate limits, enforce device-side hardening.
