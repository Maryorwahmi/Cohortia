Diagram (Conceptual)
Supplier manifests, manufacturing record, injected keys, signed firmware, and device deployment form a traceable chain. Maintain an SBOM to quickly identify devices using vulnerable components.
Supplier Manifest
Signed component list and origin metadata.
Manufacturing Record
Key injection events and device serials.
Signed Firmware
HSM-signed artifacts and version history.
Key Takeaway

Procure only from vendors who provide SBOMs and signed manifests, and require auditable manufacturing records for key handling.
