Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Key Concepts
Embedded System
A dedicated computer inside a larger system, often with real-time constraints.
Internet of Things
A network of physical objects that collect and exchange data over the internet.
Hardware Root of Trust (HRoT)
An immutable hardware component that anchors trust and starts the verification chain.
Summary Takeaway

These core terms define threat models and the foundational controls you will prioritize when securing devices.
