Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Scanning & Signing
Pipeline steps
  1. Build image in isolated builder agent.
  2. Run image scanner (Trivy, Clair) and evaluate results against policy.
  3. If pass, sign image (Cosign, Notary) and push to registry.
  4. Enforce signature verification on deploy and at admission time.
Automation benefits
  • Consistent checks and no manual bypasses.
  • Faster feedback to developers and earlier remediation.
  • Provenance for auditing and rollback decisions.
Concrete scenario
A PR triggers CI: a scan job runs and a signing job only runs if the scan passes defined severity thresholds. Signed images are pushed to the registry and admission controllers reject unsigned images at deploy time.
Misconception: scanning once is enough. In reality, images should be rescanned periodically and before production promotion.
Key Takeaway

Put scans and signatures into CI gates. Enforcement must be automated to prevent vulnerable or unsigned images from reaching production.
