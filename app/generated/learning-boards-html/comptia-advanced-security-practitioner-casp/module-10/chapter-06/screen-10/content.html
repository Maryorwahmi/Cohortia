Deny-by-default NetworkPolicy
apiVersion: networking.k8s.io/v1
kind: NetworkPolicy
metadata:
  name: deny-all-ingress
  namespace: default
spec:
  podSelector: {}
  policyTypes:
  - Ingress
  - Egress
  ingress: []
  egress: []
This policy denies all ingress and egress unless other policies explicitly allow traffic, forcing teams to declare required flows.
RBAC guidance
  • Assign least-privilege roles to service accounts used by workloads.
  • Audit cluster roles and bindings for unexpected broad permissions.
  • Rotate credentials and avoid reusing high-privilege service accounts for many apps.
Misconception

Network Policies limit pod network flows but are not a substitute for host hardening or admission controls. Combine controls for defense-in-depth.
