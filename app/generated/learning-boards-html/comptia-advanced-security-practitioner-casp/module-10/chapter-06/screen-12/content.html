Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Runtime Security
Detection & response patterns
  • Instrument hosts and pods with runtime detectors (Falco) to watch for suspicious syscalls and process activity.
  • Use network observability tools (Cilium, eBPF) to detect anomalous flows and enforce segmentation.
  • Forward logs and alerts to SIEM and tie them to identity and image metadata for context.
  • Create playbooks for containment, image quarantine, and credential rotation after detection.
Concrete detection rule
A Falco rule might alert on unexpected shells spawned by a web process or write to /etc, signaling potential compromise. Correlate with image and service account to decide containment steps.
Operational tip
Ensure alerts include image digest, pod name, and service account so responders can act quickly and revoke permissions if needed.
Key Takeaway

Runtime monitoring detects active threats and should be integrated with SIEM and runbooks to enable fast containment and recovery.
