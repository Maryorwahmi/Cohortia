Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › SOAR & Orchestration
What SOAR Does

Orchestrates alerts, enriches context, automates triage, and triggers remediation playbooks.

Automation Examples
Create tickets, enrich alerts with CVE data, run specific scans, or block artifacts automatically.
Design Considerations
Ensure playbooks are idempotent, auditable, and have safe rollback paths.
Scenario
An SCA scan flags a critical dependency. SOAR creates a triaged ticket, enriches it with CVE details, notifies teams, and optionally starts an automated rebuild workflow for non-blocking cases. Analysts review and validate before broader remediation steps run.
Key Takeaway

SOAR scales response and reduces manual triage, but should complement human judgment for high-risk decisions.
