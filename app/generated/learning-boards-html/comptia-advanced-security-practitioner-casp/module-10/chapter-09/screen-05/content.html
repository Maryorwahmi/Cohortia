Process Flow
Redundancy → Detect → Failover → Recover
A small chain of actions ensures continuity: duplicate, monitor, switch, and restore.
Redundancy
Active-passive or active-active replicas across zones or regions.
Failover
Automated promotion or traffic reroute when health checks fail.
Automatic Recovery
Controllers restart failed processes and reconcile declared state.
Concrete Scenario
A database primary fails. Replication ensures a warm replica is current. The replication controller promotes the replica and the application reconnects using updated DNS or a floating IP.
# Kubernetes: desired state ensures recovery
kubectl get deployment myapp
# If a Pod fails, the Deployment controller creates a replacement Pod
kubectl rollout status deployment/myapp
Key Takeaway

Redundancy must be paired with detection and automation so failover and recovery are reliable and testable.
