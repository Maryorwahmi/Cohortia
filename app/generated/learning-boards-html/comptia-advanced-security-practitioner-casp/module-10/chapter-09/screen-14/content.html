Common Pitfalls
What often goes wrong
Over-engineering
Optimizing for rare corner cases increases complexity and testing burden.
Untested recovery
Backups or failovers that are never exercised fail when needed.
Human factors
Ambiguous runbooks and lack of rehearsals slow response times.
Unsafe experiments
Large blast radius and no rollback lead to real outages.
Key Takeaway

Balance engineering effort with test coverage, automate critical recovery steps, and rehearse incident playbooks using small, controlled experiments.
