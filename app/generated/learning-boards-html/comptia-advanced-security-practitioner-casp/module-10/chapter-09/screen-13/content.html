Secure Resilience
Treat failover, replication, and recovery as security-sensitive operations requiring access controls and audit trails.
Secure backups & replicas
Encrypt replication streams and restrict who can trigger promotion or restore.
Validate playbooks with experiments
Confirm alerts, roles, and rollback procedures work under test conditions.
Operational Practices
  • Run game days and simulated outages.
  • Ensure separation of duties for failover actions.
  • Track experiment findings in change management and compliance artifacts.
Common mistake

Treating resilience as only ops work and not validating security implications of failover paths.
