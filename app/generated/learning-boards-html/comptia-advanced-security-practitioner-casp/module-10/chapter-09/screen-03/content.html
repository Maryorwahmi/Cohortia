Secure design, testing, and playbook integration
05. Secure redundancy and failover
Ensure backups, replicas and failover channels are encrypted, access-controlled and regularly tested so recovery is reliable and auditable.
Mnemonic: SRR (Secure, Repeatable, Reviewed)

06. Implement containment patterns
Apply circuit breakers and bulkheads to stop cascading failures and to isolate capacity issues to specific subsystems.

07. Design idempotent retries
Make operations safe to repeat so transient errors can be retried without data corruption or duplicate effects.
Mnemonic: Repeatable ⇒ Idempotent

08. Update incident playbooks
Convert experiment findings into concrete steps: alert thresholds, rollback procedures and communication channels for outages.
