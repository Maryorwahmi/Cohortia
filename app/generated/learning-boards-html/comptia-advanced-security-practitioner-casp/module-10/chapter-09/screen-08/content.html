Architecture
Bulkheads
Partition resources to prevent a failure in one service from draining shared capacity.
Example: Thread pools
Use separate pools per service or per operation class to avoid starvation.
Example: Namespaces
Deploy critical services in separate namespaces and enforce quotas.
Practical Steps
  • Identify shared resources and split them into isolated pools.
  • Apply quotas, rate limits and separate connection pools.
  • Instrument per-domain metrics to detect noisy neighbors.
  • Use chaos tests to stress one compartment and verify isolation.
Common mistake
Assuming isolation removes the need for observability; isolation without metrics can hide failures.
