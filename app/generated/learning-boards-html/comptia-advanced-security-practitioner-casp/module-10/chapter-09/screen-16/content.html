Key Concepts
Circuit Breaker
Stops calls to failing services and provides fallbacks until recovery.
Bulkheads
Isolate capacity so faults can't drain shared resources.
Chaos Engineering
Introduce small, controlled failures to validate resilience and incident response.
Summary

Apply these patterns together with observability and secure controls to build, test and maintain resilient enterprise systems.
