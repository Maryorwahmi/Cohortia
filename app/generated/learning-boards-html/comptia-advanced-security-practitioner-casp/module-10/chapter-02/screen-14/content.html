Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Key Concepts
Threat Modeling
A structured process to identify and prioritize threats during design.
STRIDE / DREAD / PASTA
STRIDE finds threats, DREAD helps prioritize, and PASTA ties business impact to attack simulation.
Secure Design Principles
Least privilege, defense in depth, secure defaults, compartmentalization, and usable controls reduce risk by design.
Summary takeaway
Integrate threat modeling into design, prioritize threats with risk-focused methods, and apply secure design principles with measurable verification so security is an architectural property.
