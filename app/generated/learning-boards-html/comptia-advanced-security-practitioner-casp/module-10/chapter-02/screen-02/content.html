How to use these objectives

Work through the chapter by first mapping a simple data flow, then applying STRIDE to populate threats. Use DREAD or PASTA to prioritize and propose mitigations that embody secure design principles. The hands-on activity gives a concrete microservice to practice on.

Assessment-ready

Each milestone produces artifacts you can show: a threat list, a prioritized risk table, and principle-driven mitigation notes for review.
