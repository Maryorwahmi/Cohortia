Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Key Concepts
Authentication & Tokens
Use OAuth and signed tokens, and treat keys as secrets to rotate and revoke.
Authorization & BOLA
Verify that the authenticated caller is allowed to access each requested object.
Validation & Encoding
Validate all inputs and encode outputs that may be rendered in clients.
Summary Takeaway

Layered controls — identity, authorization, input/output safety, and edge protections — keep APIs robust and reduce systemic risk.
