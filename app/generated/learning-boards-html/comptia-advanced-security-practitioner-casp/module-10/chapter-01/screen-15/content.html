Three quick reminders

Use these prompts in design reviews, sprint planning, and deployment checks.

Abuse cases
Ask what an attacker could do and make that a requirement to prevent it.
Combined testing
Use SAST, DAST, fuzzing, and pen testing together for broader coverage.
Secure defaults
Bake hardened settings into IaC and block insecure deployments automatically.
Final prompt

Before every merge or deployment, ask: which abuse case, STRIDE threat, or policy does this change address? If none, create one.
