Maintenance & Ops

Monitoring, patching, and incident response

Sustain security through continuous monitoring, disciplined patching, and practiced incident response that feeds improvements back into the SDLC.

Monitoring

Collect logs and security events, correlate in SIEM, and alert on anomalies to shorten detection time.

Patching

Maintain a regular patch cadence for OS, libraries, and components, and prioritize critical fixes to reduce exposure windows.

Incident response

Plan and practice response playbooks, and ensure lessons learned update requirements and designs.

Key Takeaway

Operational controls and continuous feedback turn security from a one-time effort into an evolving capability.

Maintenance workflow
Detect
Log aggregation and SIEM alerts.
Respond
Run incident playbooks and contain impact.
Remediate
Patch vulnerable components and update configurations.
Improve
Feed lessons back into requirements and design for continuous improvement.
Exercise: Create a prioritized remediation backlog and plan one tabletop incident exercise this quarter to validate response procedures.
