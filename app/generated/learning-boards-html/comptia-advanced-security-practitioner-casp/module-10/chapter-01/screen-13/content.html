Advocacy

Security-by-design and security-by-default

Use pilots, metrics, and close collaboration to turn security from resistance into a team habit.

Pilot change
Apply one hardening change in IaC templates and measure reduction in misconfiguration findings.
Pairing
Embed a security engineer in a delivery team for a sprint to reduce friction and transfer knowledge.
Metrics
Track time to remediate, high-severity findings at release, and percent of stories with security criteria.
Addressing objections

When stakeholders worry about pace, present pilot results showing how early fixes reduce later rework and release risk.

Key Takeaway

Small, measurable pilots and close collaboration convert security policy into everyday engineering practice.
