Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Rotation Policies
Timeline
Rotation cadence by key type
Session keys
Rotate every session; ephemeral and discarded after use.
VPN / Service keys
Rotate monthly or as policy dictates depending on exposure risk.
Master encryption keys
Rotate annually or quarterly with re-encryption or controlled archival of data.
Operational checklist
  • Automate generation and distribution through KMS where possible.
  • Coordinate re-encryption or maintain wrapped key archives for data recoverability.
  • Log rotation events and enforce rollback plans to prevent outages.
Concrete example
Database master key rotation
Rotate quarterly, rewrap backups, and only destroy the old key after verifying data accessibility and complying with archival rules.
Mistake to avoid
Rotating keys without updating or notifying dependent services can cause decryption failures and service outages.
