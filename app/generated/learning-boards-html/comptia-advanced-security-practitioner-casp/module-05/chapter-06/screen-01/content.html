Quick Tip
Treat secret key material as the most sensitive asset. Design systems so keys never leave secure boundaries unencrypted, and automate rotation where possible to reduce manual error.
Why it matters
Well-managed keys limit attacker impact, protect data confidentiality, and help meet regulatory and audit requirements.
