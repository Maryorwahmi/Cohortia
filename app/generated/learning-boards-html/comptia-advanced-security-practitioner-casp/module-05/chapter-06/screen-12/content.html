Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Escrow and Recovery
Process
Secure escrow design steps
Assess necessity
Only escrow keys with compelling business or legal reasons.
Split keys & multi-party control
Divide key material among custodians and require multiple approvals for recovery.
Audit & limited scope
Log each recovery event, and restrict escrow to minimal necessary key types.
Concrete policy example
Split-key escrow with two-of-three approval
A recovery key is divided into three parts; two custodians must present credentials and a documented justification to reconstruct it; every reconstruction is logged and reviewed.
Risk note
Escrow systems themselves must be hardened and audited since they become attractive targets for attackers seeking universal access.
