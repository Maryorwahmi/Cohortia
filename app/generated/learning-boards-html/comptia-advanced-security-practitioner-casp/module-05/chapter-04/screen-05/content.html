Process Flow › Root CA & HSMs
Operations for Root CA
Air-gap the Root CA and limit online exposure to key ceremonies only.
Key ceremony steps
  1. Prepare offline environment and authentication tokens.
  2. Authorize operators under multi-person control policy.
  3. Use HSM to sign issuing CA certificate; record audit logs.
Misconception
Software-only key protection is sufficient. In reality, host compromise can expose software keys; HSMs prevent key export.
HSM considerations
What HSMs provide
  • Tamper-resistant key storage and signing operations.
  • Prevents private key export and provides cryptographic proof of operations.
  • Supports role separation and multi-person activation for sensitive operations.
Key Takeaway
Specify HSMs for CA private keys and bake their operational controls into procurement and runbooks.
