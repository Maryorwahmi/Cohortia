Definition › Certificate Templates & AD CS
What a template defines
  • Key usage and extended key usage values.
  • Subject and SAN restrictions and format rules.
  • Private key exportability and key storage settings.
  • Validity period and renewal overlap.
Misconception
Templates are optional. In enterprise PKI they are essential for consistent, auditable issuance.
AD CS auto-enrollment
Group Policy ties templates to OUs to enable automatic request and renewal for domain objects.
Example scenario
Place web servers in an OU, assign a server template that issues SANs and enforces non-exportable keys. Servers auto-renew certificates before expiration without manual intervention.
Key Takeaway
Design templates with least privilege and automate issuance to reduce human error at scale.
