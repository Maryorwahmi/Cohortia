Bloom's pathway
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Design PKI Architecture
Choose two- or three-tier hierarchies with offline root and online issuing CAs. This limits exposure and enables recovery.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement Issuance Procedures
Use AD CS or other PKI platforms and define templates for consistent certificates and auto-enrollment.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Manage Revocation
Configure CRL distribution and OCSP responders with availability and correct AIA/CDP settings.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Evaluate HSM Use
Specify HSMs for CA private keys and critical server keys with physical and role-based controls.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Why these objectives matter
  • Clear architecture reduces compromise scope and supports disaster recovery.
  • Automation prevents expired certificates and reduces human error across thousands of assets.
  • Reliable revocation avoids accepting invalid certificates and preserves trust.
  • HSMs protect trust anchors and support compliance with strong key protection standards.
