Skill cards
Template Configuration
Define key usage, SANs, and lifetime to match server and client roles. Correct templates prevent over-privileged certificates.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Auto-Enrollment
Use Group Policy and AD CS templates to automate renewal and avoid expired certificates for domain members.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Operational checklist
  • Publish CRL to HTTP/LDAP CDP with appropriate cadence.
  • Deploy OCSP responders and enable stapling for TLS servers.
  • Specify HSMs for CA keys and document physical access procedures.
  • Enable logging and auditing for certificate requests and revocations.
