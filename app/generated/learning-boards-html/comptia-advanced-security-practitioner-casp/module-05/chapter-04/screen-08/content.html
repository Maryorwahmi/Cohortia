Process Flow › Revocation Operations
Design checklist
CDP and AIA accuracy
Ensure certificate CDP and AIA fields point to reachable, redundant endpoints and that URLs are correct and resolvable by clients.
Publication cadence
Choose CRL cadence based on revocation frequency; balance freshness with distribution capacity.
Availability and monitoring
Load-balance and monitor CDP/OCSP endpoints and run synthetic client checks to detect outages before users do.
Example architecture
Multiple CRL servers in different network segments and one or more OCSP responders behind a load balancer, with servers configured to staple responses where supported.
Key Takeaway
Redundancy and accurate certificate extensions are what make revocation effective in real systems.
