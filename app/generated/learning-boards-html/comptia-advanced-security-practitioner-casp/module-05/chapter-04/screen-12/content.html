Common Mistake › Operational Pitfalls
Top mistakes
  • Root CA kept online or not protected by HSM.
  • Templates that allow broad key usages or long validity.
  • Single-point revocation endpoints and no stapling for TLS.
  • Lack of automation for mass certificate lifecycles.
Remediation checklist
Require HSMs and multi-person activation for CA operations.
Define strict templates, short validity for high-risk certs, and auto-enrollment where possible.
Deploy redundant CDP and OCSP endpoints and enable stapling for web sites.
Implement logging, audits, and synthetic monitoring to detect failures quickly.
Key Takeaway
Operational discipline prevents most PKI failures; automate, monitor, and secure keys with HSMs.
