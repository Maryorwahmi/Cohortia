Assessment Idea › Design Exercise
Design requirements
  1. Offline Root CA in HSM with documented key ceremony and multi-person controls.
  2. Online Issuing CA(s) for web, VPN, and devices with templates and limited validity.
  3. CRL distribution and OCSP responders with redundancy and monitoring.
  4. Automation for certificate issuance and renewal where possible.
  5. Recovery and re-issuance playbooks for CA compromise scenarios.
Grading hints
  • Look for HSM requirements and multi-person procedures.
  • Templates should include SAN handling and proper key usage.
  • Revocation plan must include availability and monitoring details.
