Learning Objectives

Learn, understand, apply, and move toward mastery across modes, asymmetric choices, and key lifecycle practice.

AEAD Evaluation
Evaluate AEAD modes such as AES-GCM for confidentiality and integrity in protocol designs. You will learn to justify AES-GCM in TLS and storage solutions, and to spot when separate MACs are necessary.
Verified competency milestone
02
RSA vs ECC Comparison
Compare security, key sizes, performance and resource trade-offs to select RSA or ECC for signatures, key exchange, and constrained devices.
Verified competency milestone
03
Symmetric Key Management
Implement generation, distribution, storage, rotation, and destruction policies, and integrate KMS or HSM where appropriate to reduce operational risk.
Verified competency milestone
04
Mathematical Analysis
Analyze the mathematical assumptions behind RSA and ECC so architectural choices rest on understood hardness problems rather than convenience.
Verified competency milestone
