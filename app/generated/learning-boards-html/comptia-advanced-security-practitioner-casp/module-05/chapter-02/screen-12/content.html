What to Remember
AEAD (AES-GCM)
Combined encryption and authentication; include associated data for header integrity without encrypting it.
ECC vs RSA
ECC gives similar strength with smaller keys and faster ops; choose based on performance and compatibility needs.
Key Management (KMS/HSM)
Centralize generation, storage, rotation, and destruction to reduce human error and provide auditability.
Summary
Prefer AEAD for new designs, prefer ECC for constrained or high-performance contexts, and integrate a KMS/HSM to manage keys lifecycle and auditing effectively.
