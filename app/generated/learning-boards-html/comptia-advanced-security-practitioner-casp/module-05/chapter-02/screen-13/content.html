What to Remember
Unique IV/Nonce
Never reuse nonces or IVs under the same key; uniqueness is required for GCM/CTR security.
Verify Authentication
Always check the authentication tag; do not accept decrypted data if verification fails.
Strong Entropy
Generate keys with a CSPRNG and sufficient entropy. Weak randomness undermines all cryptography.
Final Takeaway

Operational discipline — unique nonces, authenticated verification, and strong key generation — makes cryptography effective in real systems.
