Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Registration Authority
Definition
What an RA does

An RA verifies identities and forwards validated certificate signing requests to the CA, enabling the CA to remain controlled and secure.

  • Verify identity: documents, directory records, or domain ownership checks.
  • Collect CSRs: ensure keys and request data are correctly formed before submission to CA.
  • Audit trail: logging verification steps supports later investigation and compliance.
Misconception

The RA is not merely optional in enterprise PKI; it is often required to centralize trusted identity checks and enforce policy.

Example: employee certificate issuance
An employee requests a client authentication certificate. The RA verifies HR records and a corporate email challenge, records evidence, and forwards the CSR to an intermediate CA for issuance.
Step 1
Collect CSR
User generates key pair and CSR, submits to RA.
Step 2
Verify identity
RA checks HR and email ownership and logs evidence.
