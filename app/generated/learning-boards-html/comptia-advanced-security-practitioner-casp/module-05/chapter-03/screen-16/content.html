Protect the root
Keep the root offline and use intermediates for operations to limit impact of compromise.
Align extensions
Populate SAN, set Key Usage, and EKU to match certificate purpose and avoid acceptance failures.
Plan revocation
Choose OCSP for immediacy, CRLs for offline resilience, and document responder redundancy and staleness SLAs.
Final takeaway
PKI is a system of policies and services. Design it to minimize exposure, enforce identity checks, and make revocation effective and timely.
