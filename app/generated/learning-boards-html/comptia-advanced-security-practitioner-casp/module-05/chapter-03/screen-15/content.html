Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Certificate Authority (CA)
Trusted entity that issues and manages certificates and signs intermediates to form trust chains.
Registration Authority (RA)
Performs identity verification and forwards validated CSR requests to the CA to keep the CA secure.
X.509 Certificate
Contains Subject, Issuer, public key, validity, SAN, Key Usage, and EKU which guide trust decisions.
Summary
Protect roots, separate duties, and match extensions and revocation methods to your operational and risk requirements.
