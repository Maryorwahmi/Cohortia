Public Key Infrastructure
Foundations: authorities, certificates, publication, and revocation

PKI binds keys to identities and provides the policies and services to manage those bindings at scale. In enterprise environments PKI supports TLS, VPNs, email encryption, and code signing. Learn the components and the certificate lifecycle so you can design secure, available deployments.

Why this chapter

PKI is the operational layer that makes public-key cryptography trustworthy in production systems. Good PKI design prevents outages, reduces compromise impact, and shapes organizational identity controls.
