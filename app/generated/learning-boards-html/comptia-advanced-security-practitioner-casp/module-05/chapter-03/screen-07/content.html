Process Flow
CRL publication and consumption

A CA compiles revoked certificate serial numbers into a signed CRL and publishes it to a repository. Clients download the CRL on a schedule and check certificate serials against it.

  1. CA revokes a certificate and appends its serial number to the CRL.
  2. CA publishes the signed CRL to an accessible repository (HTTP/LDAP).
  3. Clients periodically download or check cached CRLs when validating certificates.
Pro Tip

Use delta CRLs where possible to reduce download size, and set publication intervals that balance bandwidth and timeliness.

Example scenario
A web server's private key is suspected of compromise. The CA revokes the certificate and publishes a new CRL. Clients that download the updated CRL will reject the compromised certificate once their cache refreshes.
Key downside
Staleness window: until clients fetch the new CRL, the revoked certificate may still be accepted.
When to use
Environments where clients can cache lists and online responders are unavailable or undesirable.
