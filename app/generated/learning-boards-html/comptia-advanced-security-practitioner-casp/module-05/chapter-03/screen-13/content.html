Decision factors
  • Timeliness: OCSP is faster; CRLs can be stale.
  • Scale: CRLs can become large; OCSP scales better for individual checks but needs responder capacity.
  • Availability: OCSP depends on responder uptime; CRLs depend on client ability to fetch lists.
  • Privacy: OCSP reveals which certificates a client checks unless stapled by server.
Recommendation
Use OCSP for high-value, time-sensitive certificates and CRLs or delta CRLs where client reachability or responder availability is limited. Combine stapling to reduce client load and privacy leaks.
Example scenarios
If a TLS server private key is stolen, OCSP with stapling helps ensure clients reject the cert quickly. For isolated IoT devices with intermittent connectivity, delta CRLs may be more practical.
Checklist
Define staleness SLA, deploy redundant responders, log revocation events, and test client behavior under responder failure.
Misconception
Selecting OCSP alone guarantees safety. Without redundancy and monitoring, responder outages can cause validation failures or force fail-open behavior.
