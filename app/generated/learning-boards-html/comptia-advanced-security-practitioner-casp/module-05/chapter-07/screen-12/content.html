Key Concepts › Part 1
Brute-Force Attack
Systematically try all keys or passwords until the correct one is found; prevent with large keyspace and key-stretching.
Side-Channel Attack
Exploit leaked physical or timing signals from implementations to recover secrets; mitigate with constant-time code and shielding.
Chosen-Plaintext Attack (CPA)
Attacker can obtain ciphertexts for chosen plaintexts; indicates the need for randomized or authenticated schemes and avoiding encryption oracles.
Summary

These concise definitions help you classify and respond to findings quickly during assessments and remediation planning.
