Core Concept › Post-Quantum Cryptography
Why PQC matters
Quantum computers threaten certain asymmetric algorithms; plan now for long-lived keys and signatures.
• Example: certificates and code-signing keys that must remain valid for many years require early migration planning.
• Misconception: PQC is only theoretical — in practice you must identify assets with long confidentiality windows and prepare them.
Key Takeaway
Monitor PQC progress and prioritize early migration for assets that need long-term confidentiality or integrity.
Operational steps
• Inventory assets and identify keys that require long-term protection.
• Build migration plans and test quantum-safe alternatives when they mature.
• Ensure agility so switching to PQC algorithms is a configuration and deployment task, not a rewrite.
