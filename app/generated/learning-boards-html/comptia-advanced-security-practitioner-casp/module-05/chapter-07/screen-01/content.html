Cryptographic Attacks and Countermeasures
Understand attack vectors against keys, implementations, and protocols, and design practical mitigations.
In this lesson
You will learn common attacks such as brute-force, side-channel, chosen-plaintext/ciphertext, birthday, and downgrade attacks, plus defenses including key-sizing, constant-time coding, authenticated encryption, HSTS, and cryptographic agility.
Why it matters
Even strong algorithms fail when implementations leak information or when systems can be forced to use weak protocols. This chapter turns abstract cryptography into actionable security engineering.
Explore Key Topics
Brute Force
Large key space and key-stretched passwords make brute-force infeasible for modern algorithms.
Side Channels
Implementation leaks like timing and power draw require constant-time code and shielding.
Protocol Hardening
Disable weak TLS versions, prefer authenticated encryption, and enable HSTS to stop downgrade attacks.
