Cryptographic Principles and Algorithms
Understand primitives, secure choices, and why hybrid designs are the standard.
In this lesson
You will compare symmetric and asymmetric methods, explore hashing and key stretching, and learn how Perfect Forward Secrecy prevents recorded-traffic decryption even if long-term keys are exposed.
Why it matters
Picking the wrong cipher, or diminishing PFS, can turn recorded traffic into future data loss. These choices shape compliance, latency, and resilience.
