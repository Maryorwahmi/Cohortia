Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Hash Collisions
Why collisions matter
A collision allows two different files to share the same digest. If verification relies only on matching digests, an attacker who can produce a collision can bypass integrity checks.
Operational impact
Signing or trusting artifacts based on weak hashes can enable forging and distribution of malicious artifacts that appear valid.
Mitigation steps
  • Inventory where weak hashes are used and prioritize replacement where signatures or trust depend on them.
  • Migrate to SHA-2 or SHA-3 families for signatures and integrity checks.
  • Design systems for cryptographic agility to swap algorithms when needed.
Key Takeaway

Retire broken hashes from signature and verification workflows. Plan migration and treat key management as part of the solution.
