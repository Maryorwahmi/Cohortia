Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Distribution
Trust models
Direct trust: publish a public key through a trusted channel and configure clients to trust it. Delegated trust: use a certificate authority to vouch for the key.
  • Direct trust works for controlled fleets and internal tooling.
  • Delegated trust scales to broad public audiences and third-party clients.
Operational controls
  • Secure key storage and automated rotation.
  • Revocation mechanisms and incident playbooks for compromised keys.
  • Verification of public keys via trusted channels or certificate chains.
Key Takeaway

A signature’s credibility depends on how verifiers obtain and trust the signer’s public key. Design trust delivery carefully.
