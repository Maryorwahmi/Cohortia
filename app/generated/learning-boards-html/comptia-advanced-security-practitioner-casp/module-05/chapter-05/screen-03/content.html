Practice Tasks
Concrete activities to verify your competency
Explain choice
Write a short justification for using a MAC vs a signature in a closed internal API.
Sign & verify
Create a detached signature for an artifact and verify it using a public key.
Migration plan
Document where MD5 or SHA-1 are used and propose replacements and rollout steps.
These tasks mirror real operational needs: distributing updates securely, auditing legacy code, and ensuring your cryptography choices support scale and trust.
