MAC workflow
  1. Sender and receiver share a secret key.
  2. Sender computes MAC(message, key) and appends it to the message.
  3. Receiver recomputes MAC and compares; matches mean authenticated integrity.
When to use
Use MACs for internal APIs, secure channels, or where pre-shared keys are practical.
Concrete example
HMAC pairs a cryptographic hash with a secret key to produce a MAC. It is efficient and resistant to known weaknesses when the underlying hash is secure.
Key Takeaway

Use MACs when both parties can securely share a key; otherwise, prefer digital signatures for broad verification.
