Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Privacy Regulations
Regulatory implications
GDPR
Data protection by design, rights to access and erasure, DPIAs, cross-border rules.
CCPA
Consumer access, deletion rights, transparency and opt-outs for California residents.
HIPAA
Safeguards for protected health information across administrative, physical, and technical domains.
Concrete compliance steps
Maintain a data map, enable secure deletion or cryptographic erasure, provide export formats, and document DPIAs and consent frameworks.
Misconception
Compliance is not a single control; it is a collection of technical measures, processes, and documented evidence aligned to legal obligations.
Key Takeaway

Translate legal rights into measurable technical capabilities: mapping, deletion, portability, and audit trails.
