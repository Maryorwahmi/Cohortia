Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Privacy-Enhancing Technologies
PETs at a glance
Anonymization & Pseudonymization
Remove or obscure identifiers; pseudonymization preserves re-linking capability when needed under strict controls.
Homomorphic Encryption
Compute over encrypted data to avoid exposing plaintext during processing.
Differential Privacy
Add noise to outputs so individuals cannot be singled out while preserving statistical meaning.
Secure Multi-Party Computation
Allow joint computation without revealing each party’s raw inputs.
Concrete use case
A research collaboration uses differential privacy to publish aggregate health statistics while protecting patient identities.
Misconception
PETs reduce risk but do not remove the need for governance, monitoring, and legal controls; they must be integrated into processes.
Key Takeaway

Select PETs based on analytic needs and threat models; expect trade-offs in complexity and performance.
