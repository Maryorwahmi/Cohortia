Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Privacy by Design
DPIA process
1. Map Data
Where data is stored and flows it follows.
2. Identify Risks
Assess likelihood and impact to data subjects.
3. Mitigate
Apply minimization, PETs, retention limits, and encryption.
4. Document
Record decisions, residual risk, and implementation owners.
Concrete example
Analytics pipeline DPIA recommends pseudonymization, six-month retention, and differential privacy on outputs to reduce re-identification risk.
Misconception
DPIAs are not mere compliance checkboxes; they should change design choices early and produce technical mitigations.
Key Takeaway

Embed privacy into design and use DPIAs to drive measurable mitigations and owner assignments.
