Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Encryption in Transit
Core mechanisms
TLS/SSL
Application-layer encryption for web and APIs. Prefer TLS 1.3 and strong ciphers.
VPN
Secure tunnel for remote or cross-network traffic; useful for site-to-site protection.
IPsec
Network-level tunneling with strong mutual authentication and encryption.
Concrete example
Enforce TLS 1.3 for browser traffic, use mTLS for service-to-service APIs, and IPsec for cross-datacenter backend links containing sensitive data.
Misconception
A firewall does not encrypt. Only proper transport-layer encryption prevents passive eavesdropping of payloads.
Key Takeaway

Treat TLS as the minimum for application traffic and design certificate and cipher management into your operational plan.
