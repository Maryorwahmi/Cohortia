Learning Objectives
Learn the practical skills you will use when building secure applications and pipelines.
01
Differentiate AST methods
Describe SAST, DAST, IAST and RASP, how they operate, and the stage where each gives most value.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Interpret and prioritize findings
Assess severity, exploitability and business impact to guide timely remediation decisions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Explain manual tests: pen test and fuzzing
Understand when human-led pen tests or fuzzing reveal chained exploits or parser crashes automated scans miss.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
