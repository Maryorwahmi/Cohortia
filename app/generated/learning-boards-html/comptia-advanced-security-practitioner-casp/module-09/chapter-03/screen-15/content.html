Key Concepts
Quick Reference
Three practical reminders to apply immediately.
Fuzzing
Use for parsers and file handlers to reveal crashes and memory issues.
Penetration Testing
Budget manual tests to validate complex logic and measure business risk.
Prioritization
Rank findings by severity, exploitability and business impact; require repro and retest.
Final tip: integrate automated tests into CI/CD, complement them with targeted manual testing, and use runtime protections to reduce exposure while fixes are applied.
