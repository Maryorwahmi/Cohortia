Core ConceptPenetration Testing
Penetration Testing — Manual Assessment
Simulate real attacks to find complex, chained vulnerabilities and demonstrate business impact.
Characteristics
  • Manual and creative testing by experienced analysts.
  • Focus on business logic, authentication, and chained exploits.
  • Deliverable: actionable report demonstrating exploitability and impact.
When to run
Before major releases or after significant architectural changes, and to validate automated testing results.
Common misconceptions
Pen tests do not replace automated scanning; they are complementary and provide realistic exploit demonstrations.
Key Takeaway

Penetration testing supplies human creativity to reveal vulnerabilities and attack chains that automated tools miss, and it helps quantify business risk.
