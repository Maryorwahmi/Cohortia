Definition

Threat Modeling: STRIDE & DREAD

Identify threats by category, then prioritize remediation using scoring or impact assessment.

STRIDE categories
  • Spoofing
  • Tampering
  • Repudiation
  • Information Disclosure
  • Denial of Service
  • Elevation of Privilege
DREAD prioritization
Score threats on damage, reproducibility, exploitability, affected users, and discoverability to decide remediation order.
Concrete example
Photo upload flow: apply STRIDE to the upload data flow and annotate threats for each trust boundary.
Example threats
Spoofing: fake user uploads; Tampering: modified photo metadata; Disclosure: EXIF data exposed publicly.
Mitigations
Server-side auth, validate and sanitize metadata, strip EXIF data, and enforce rate limiting.
Misconception
Threat modeling is not just an academic checklist; it drives concrete design choices and test cases.
Reflective question: Which STRIDE category produces the highest impact in your system?
