Key Concepts

SSDLC
Integrate security across all SDLC phases to prevent costly fixes later.
Threat Modeling
Use STRIDE/DREAD to list and prioritize threats tied to flows and components.
Secure Coding
Combine developer training, SAST, and peer review to reduce common vulnerabilities.
Summary takeaway
Treat SSDLC as an engineering practice: measurable requirements, prioritized mitigation, and continuous feedback.
