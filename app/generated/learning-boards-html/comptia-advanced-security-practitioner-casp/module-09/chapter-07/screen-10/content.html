Security Gates & Release Criteria

Use measurable, risk-based rules that block releases until necessary remediation or mitigation plans are in place.

Example criteria
No open critical vulnerabilities; high severity allowed only with mitigation plan and owner assigned.
Automation
Integrate SAST/DAST checks into CI pipelines so gate feedback is immediate and actionable for developers.
Practical policy
Include override rules, remediation timelines, and clear owners to avoid blocked releases becoming bottlenecks.
Pitfall
Gates without remediation pathways create friction. Pair gates with clear remediation playbooks.
Key Takeaway
Gates should be objective, automated where possible, and backed by remediation processes and ownership.
Reflective question: Which gate would most reduce your production risk without blocking delivery?
