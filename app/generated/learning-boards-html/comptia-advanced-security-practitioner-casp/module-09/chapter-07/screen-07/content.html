Secure Design & Defense-in-Depth

Translate requirements and threat models into layered controls that limit impact and detect abuse.

APIs and gateways
Use API gateways for authentication, throttling, and central logging.
Layered authorization
Enforce least privilege at service and resource levels, not just at the perimeter.
Data segregation
Separate sensitive data stores and limit access through roles and tokenization.
Scenario
Design an upload service: validate and sanitize inputs, use isolated storage, scan content, and restrict access via roles.
Why not obscurity?
Hiding endpoints or relying on non-public URLs is brittle—real threats exploit weak auth and unchecked inputs.
Pro tip
Map each STRIDE finding to one or more architectural controls and verify with design reviews.
