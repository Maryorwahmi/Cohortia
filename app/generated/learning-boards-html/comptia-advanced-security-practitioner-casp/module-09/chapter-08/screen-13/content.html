RASP
Runtime protection that can block attacks but must be tuned to avoid disruption.
Pen Test
Controlled expert assessment to find complex flaws and validate impact.
Remediation
Prioritize, fix root causes, retrain teams, and re-test to ensure closure.
Summary Takeaway

Succeeding at application security requires tools and human processes that are integrated into development and operations, with clear priorities and evidence-based re-testing.
