Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Pen Test & Bug Bounty
Penetration Testing
Manual, structured testing performed by skilled testers. Best for finding complex business logic flaws and validating exploit chains.
When to use: before major release milestones and when you need controlled, documented evidence of impact.
Bug Bounty Programs
Crowdsourced testing that stimulates continuous discovery. Requires clear scope, reward tiers, and a disclosure policy.
Benefit
Broader researcher perspectives and continuous feedback.
Challenge
Program management and triage of externally reported issues.
