Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Governance
Common operational failures
  • No formal classification and ad hoc handling.
  • Weak or outdated encryption choices with poor key control.
  • Missing encryption between internal services and poor certificate management.
  • Ignoring residency constraints when replicating data globally.
Governance actions
Assign classification owners and set review cadences.
Automate enforcement: tagging, policy engines, and deployment guardrails.
Audit encryption, key management, and access logs regularly.
