Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Data States
Three states and example protections
At Rest
Encryption (FDE, TDE, storage encryption), RBAC, retention policies, secure deletion.
In Transit
TLS, SSH, VPNs, IPsec to prevent eavesdropping and tampering during transfer.
In Use
TEEs, memory protections, secure coding, minimal residence time, and advanced methods like homomorphic encryption in niche cases.
Practical checklist
  • Label where data lives and moves.
  • Apply encryption for rest and transit when required by classification.
  • Consider TEEs or minimize memory exposure for sensitive processing.
Misconception
Encrypting at rest solves all problems. It does not protect data while it’s being processed in memory.
