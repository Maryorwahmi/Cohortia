Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Data in Transit
Common protections
  1. Use TLS for all client-server traffic and APIs.
  2. Use SSH for remote administration; disable weak ciphers.
  3. Use VPNs or IPsec for secure site-to-site or admin tunnels.
  4. Implement mutual TLS for service-to-service authentication when needed.
Example
Require TLS 1.2+ with strong cipher suites and automated certificate management for public APIs and internal services handling confidential or restricted data.
Operational checklist
  • Centralize certificate management and automate renewal.
  • Audit for unencrypted endpoints and block insecure protocols.
  • Log and monitor TLS connections for anomalies.
Common mistake
Assuming internal networks are safe and skipping encryption between services. Treat internal links as potential attack surfaces.
