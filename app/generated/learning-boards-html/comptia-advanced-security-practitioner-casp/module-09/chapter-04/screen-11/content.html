Module 9 › Chapter 9.4 › Transitive Dependencies: Hidden Risk
Comparison

Direct vs. Transitive dependencies

  • Direct: You choose and can pin or replace readily.
  • Transitive: Inherited and often invisible; requires graph analysis to manage.
Mitigation
Use SBOMs and dependency graph scanners; apply overrides or upgrade direct dependencies that pull in risky transitives.
Concrete example
A service depends on framework A. Framework A depends on utility B which has a vulnerability. Unless you scan transitives, you will not see utility B listed in your direct dependency manifest and may miss the risk entirely.
Key Takeaway

Include transitive dependencies in scans and SBOMs; don’t assume direct-only checks are sufficient.
