Module 9 › Chapter 9.4 › Supply Chain Risk Assessment
Process
Automate monitoring of advisories and CVEs against your SBOM, score vendor posture, and map findings to service impact.
Assessment signals
  • Release cadence and responsiveness.
  • Presence of security policy and SBOM.
  • License compatibility and community activity.
Concrete action
Score the top 10 third-party components by criticality and require deeper vetting or contractual SLA from high-risk vendors. Automate alerts when a component with high criticality receives a new CVE.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize vendor and OSS vetting by business impact and automate advisory monitoring to reduce detection time.
