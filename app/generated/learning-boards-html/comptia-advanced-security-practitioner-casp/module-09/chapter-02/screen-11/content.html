Module 9 › Chapter 9.2 › Sessions & Access
Session hygiene
  • Use strong random IDs and mark cookies HttpOnly and Secure.
  • Regenerate IDs after login or privilege changes.
  • Expire and invalidate sessions on logout and inactivity.
Access control
  • Enforce authorization server-side for every operation.
  • Apply least privilege and audit roles regularly.
  • Test for horizontal and vertical privilege escalation.
Key Takeaway

Treat sessions and access checks as core security controls; enforce them server-side and rotate identifiers after login.
