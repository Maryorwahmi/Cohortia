Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Common Challenges in DLP Deployment
Key Frictions

Operational pain points

DLP generates alerts that must be triaged, rules that must be tuned, and integration tasks across many platforms. Expect iterative tuning and cross-team coordination.

  • False positives cause business disruption and require rule refinement.
  • False negatives occur when patterns fail or attackers obfuscate data.
  • Complexity and performance impacts increase with inline inspection and scale.
Example

A finance team’s email with a routine vendor spreadsheet is blocked because the rule flagged a column that looks like credit card format. That stops payroll tasks until the alert is triaged.

Tip: Begin in monitoring mode, collect examples, and then switch to blocking for high-confidence patterns.
False Positive
Legitimate work flagged

Occurs when rules are too broad or context is missing. Tune with user feedback and contextual rules.

False Negative
Sensitive data missed

Happens when detection methods lack coverage. Add fingerprinting or ML models for unstructured content.

Complexity
Rules across many platforms

Consistency across endpoints, network, and cloud is essential. Use centralized policy management where possible.

Performance
Latency and scale tradeoffs

Choose scanning modes carefully: inline blocking vs. passive monitoring will affect user experience and throughput.

Key Takeaway

Plan for phased rollout, involve stakeholders, and measure detection quality. DLP must be tuned over time to be effective without disrupting business.
