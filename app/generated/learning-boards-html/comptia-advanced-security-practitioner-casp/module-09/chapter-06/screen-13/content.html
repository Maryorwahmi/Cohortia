Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Key Concepts — DLP Operations
False Positives / Negatives
Aggressive rules increase false positives; lenient rules increase false negatives. Tuning and layered checks reduce both.
Policy Granularity
Policies tied to classification, user, destination, and app context are more accurate and less disruptive.
Integration with Controls
DLP is effective when integrated with IAM, encryption, and incident response workflows to close the loop on alerts.
Summary

Tune iteratively, align policies with classification, and embed DLP into operational processes to protect sensitive data without stopping business.
