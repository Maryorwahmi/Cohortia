Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Best Practices & Pilot Planning
Checklist

Practical best practices

  • Start small: pilot one department and one channel before expanding.
  • Classify data: tie rules to labels like Restricted, Confidential, or Public.
  • Stakeholder buy-in: legal, HR, and business owners must sign off on exceptions.
  • Measure: track false positives, blocked incidents, and time to triage.
  • Train users: communicate why rules exist and how to request exceptions.
30/60/90 Day Pilot Plan

30 days: detection-only on a single high-risk flow, collect alerts.

60 days: refine rules and apply targeted blocking to high-confidence patterns.

90 days: extend coverage, onboard additional teams, and automate reviews.

Integration Points
  • Data classification and labeling systems.
  • Identity and access management for contextual rules.
  • Encryption and key management to limit exposure risk.
  • Security awareness and incident response workflows.
Key Takeaway

DLP works best when aligned with classification and business processes. Start with a focused pilot, involve owners, and expand based on measurable success.

Reflection: Choose one dataset and one channel for a 30-day pilot and define success metrics.
