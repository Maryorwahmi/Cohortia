Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Technologies

Understand how DLP inspects content, applies policy, and prevents unauthorized data exfiltration across endpoints, networks, and cloud apps.

Why this matters

Sensitive data can leave through legitimate workflows. DLP acts as the last line of defense by matching content to classification and enforcing protections to reduce accidental or malicious exposure.

DLP Policy Engine
Matches content to rules, then enforces actions
Endpoint Agents
Monitor removable media, clipboard, printing
Cloud DLP
CASB integration, policy on cloud storage and apps

Endpoint DLP
