Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › Microservices & Mesh
Microservices challenges
  • Consistent authentication and authorization across many services.
  • Encrypted service-to-service communication to prevent interception.
  • Observability and distributed tracing for incident analysis.
  • Policy enforcement without changing application code.
Service mesh benefits
Automated mutual TLS, centralized policies, traffic routing, and observability.
Mutual TLS for all services
Centralized policy rules
Traffic control and canary deployments
Distributed tracing and metrics
Rollout advice
Pilot a single namespace or service group, confirm mutual TLS and policy behavior, then expand. Monitor latency and operational overhead as you scale.
