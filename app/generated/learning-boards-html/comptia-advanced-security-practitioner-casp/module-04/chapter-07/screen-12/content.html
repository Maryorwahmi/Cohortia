Module 5 › Chapter 4.7 › CSPM vs CWPP
CSPM (Configuration focus)
  • Continuous checks for misconfigurations and compliance drift.
  • Alerts for unencrypted resources and permissive IAM policies.
  • Good for large account visibility and automated remediation suggestions.
CWPP (Runtime focus)
  • Behavioral detection for VMs, containers, and workloads.
  • Host-level controls, micro-segmentation, and process monitoring.
  • Detects exploitation and anomalous runtime activity.
Key takeaway
CSPM and CWPP solve different problems and should be correlated in operations to guide both preventative and detective responses.
