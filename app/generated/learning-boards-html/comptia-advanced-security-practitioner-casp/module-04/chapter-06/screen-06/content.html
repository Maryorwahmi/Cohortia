Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Encryption for Data at Rest
Comparison
SSE-S3 vs SSE-KMS vs Client-side
Provider-managed (SSE-S3)
Simple, automatic encryption with provider-controlled keys. Good for low-sensitivity data, limited key control.
KMS-managed (SSE-KMS)
Customer-managed keys via KMS, rotation and audit logs. Balance of control and manageability for sensitive data.
Client-side encryption
Encrypt data before sending to the cloud so only you control keys and plaintext. Highest control, higher complexity.
Example & Guidance
Selecting an approach

For customer payment data or PHI, prefer client-side or SSE-KMS with strict key policies and logging. For static public content, SSE-S3 is sufficient. Always pair encryption with least privilege access and logging.

Analogy: keys control who can open the vault — who holds keys matters as much as the lock type.
Key Takeaway
Choose the level of key control that reflects data sensitivity and operational capability.
