Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Cloud Compliance Frameworks
Frameworks
GDPR • HIPAA • PCI DSS
GDPR: Data residency, subject rights, breach notification — choose regions and logging to demonstrate compliance.
HIPAA: Protect PHI with encryption, access controls, audit logs, and ensure business associate agreements are in place.
PCI DSS: Enforce segmentation, strong encryption, and continuous monitoring for cardholder data workflows.
Implementation
From control to configuration

Use provider certifications as a baseline, then configure services to meet the specific controls: region selection, encryption modes, segmentation, and audit logging. Document evidence and testing steps for audits.

Example: For PCI DSS, isolate cardholder data into dedicated VPCs, encrypt all storage and transit, and enable continuous monitoring.
Key Takeaway
Design documented mappings from regulation to service configuration, and maintain evidence through logs and tested controls.
