Learning Objectives
Four practical competencies to convert policy and law into cloud controls.
01
Classify cloud data
Determine sensitivity, legal constraints, and retention needs so controls map to business risk. Use categories such as Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted.
Mnemonic: Venn — business value ∩ legal requirements
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Encrypt across states
Apply storage encryption, TLS for networks, and consider confidential computing for processing to limit exposure of plaintext data.
Mnemonic: Timeline — at-rest → in-transit → in-use
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Manage keys securely
Use a KMS and HSM-backed keys, control access, and rotate keys to reduce exposure. Never store keys in code or unsecured config.
Mnemonic: Key hierarchy diagram
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design for compliance
Translate GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS requirements into region planning, logging, segmentation, and encryption controls that are auditable.
Mnemonic: Checklist — residency, access logs, encryption
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
