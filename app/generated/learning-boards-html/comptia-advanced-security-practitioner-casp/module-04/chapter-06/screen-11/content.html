Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › KMS in Practice
Pattern
KMS + envelope encryption + HSMs
Create short-lived data keys, wrap them with KMS-managed keys, and keep master keys under HSM protection when regulatory requirements demand it.
Grant precise decrypt rights to services rather than broad admin privileges.
Integrate key rotation into deployment pipelines and document procedures for DR.
Operational Example
Service calls KMS for decryption at runtime

An application requests a short-lived data key from KMS to encrypt a file, uses it, then discards it. The KMS master key remains in the KMS, and access is logged for audits.

Ensure the management plane (who can manage keys) has strict separation of duties and MFA enforced.
Key Takeaway
Integrate KMS into your architecture and operations so keys are both secure and auditable.
