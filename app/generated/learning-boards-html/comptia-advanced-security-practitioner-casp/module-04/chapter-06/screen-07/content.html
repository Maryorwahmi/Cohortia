Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Encrypting Databases & Storage
Architecture
Patterns for RDS, object buckets, and backups
Object storage: ensure bucket-level encryption and consider SSE-KMS for key control and auditability.
Databases: enable Transparent Data Encryption or engine-level encryption and ensure replicas inherit encryption.
Backups & snapshots: verify encryption is applied during snapshot creation and cross-region replication.
Example Scenario
E-commerce database and media bucket

For order and payment records use RDS encryption plus application-layer field encryption for card data. For product images, use bucket encryption; if images later include PII, plan for reclassification and client-side encryption option.

Operational check: include automated audits to detect unencrypted snapshots.
Key Takeaway
Encryption must be end-to-end: storage, replicas, and backups must all be covered and reviewed regularly.
