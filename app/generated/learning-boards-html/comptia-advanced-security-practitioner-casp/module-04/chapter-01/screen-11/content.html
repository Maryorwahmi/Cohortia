Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Monitoring & IR
Core steps
  1. Forward cloud provider audit and flow logs to your SIEM.
  2. Normalize timestamps, identities, and resource identifiers.
  3. Standardize alerting and incident playbooks across environments.
Key takeaway
Correlation across environments is essential for timely detection and effective response.
Concrete example
If a storage resource shows abnormal reads, trace the event in your SIEM to the initiating identity, determine whether the credential was valid or compromised, and follow the incident playbook to rotate keys and revoke sessions.
Common mistake
Keeping provider logs in isolated silos prevents cross-environment correlation and slows incident response.
