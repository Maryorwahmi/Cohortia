Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Deployment models
Public, private, hybrid and community define tenancy and control boundaries for security and compliance.
Shared responsibility
Providers secure infrastructure; customers secure data, apps and configurations within their tenancy.
Integration controls
Connectivity, identity, encryption and centralized monitoring are essential where clouds connect.
Summary

Choose the model that meets your data and compliance needs, map responsibilities clearly, and enforce integration controls through automation and monitoring.
