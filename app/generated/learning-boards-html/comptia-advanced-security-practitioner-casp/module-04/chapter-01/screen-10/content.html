Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Data Protection
Data classification
Identify sensitivity, regulatory constraints, and residency requirements, then map storage and processing locations accordingly.
Encryption & key governance
  • Encrypt data at rest and in transit by default.
  • Use customer-managed keys when separation of duties is required.
  • Log key usage and retain audit trails for compliance.
Concrete example
For GDPR-covered data, restrict storage to compliant regions, enable encryption with keys you control, and forward encryption key logs to your central SIEM for auditability.
Common pitfall
Using provider-managed keys without understanding where keys are stored can break residency or separation requirements. Pair encryption with key governance.
