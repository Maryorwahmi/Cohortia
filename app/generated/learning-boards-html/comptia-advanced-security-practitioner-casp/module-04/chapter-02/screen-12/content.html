Common mistakes and remedies
Identify and prevent frequent customer-side exposures.
IaaS
Unpatched VMs, default credentials. Remedy: automated patching and hardened images.
PaaS
Secrets in config, over-privileged platform roles. Remedy: vaults and RBAC review.
SaaS
Permissive sharing or no MFA. Remedy: enforce MFA, review sharing, apply DLP.
Practical guardrails
  • Automate patching and image pipelines.
  • Use managed identity or vaults for secrets.
  • Enforce least privilege and schedule reviews.
