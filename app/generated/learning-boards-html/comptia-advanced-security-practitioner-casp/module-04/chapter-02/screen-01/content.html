Cloud Service Models and Security
Why the division of responsibility matters for patching, identity, and data governance.

This lesson clarifies where your team must act and where the cloud service provider takes responsibility. Focus on controls you can influence: OS hardening, secure coding, identity integration, and DLP. Recognize provider-managed controls so you avoid duplicating effort or missing gaps in your incident plans.

In this lesson
  • Define IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS using practical analogies.
  • Map customer vs provider security responsibilities.
  • Design controls and spot common misconfigurations.
