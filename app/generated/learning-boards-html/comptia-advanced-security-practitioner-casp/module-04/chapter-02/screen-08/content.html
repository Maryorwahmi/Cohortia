SaaS — definition & analogy
Think: riding a public bus. Provider manages the vehicle and route; you are a passenger who controls your seat and behavior.
Provider: application stack, runtime, OS, and infrastructure.
Customer: user accounts, MFA, data access, and app configuration.
Example
Microsoft 365 or Salesforce: provider secures app and platform; you secure users, MFA, and data sharing.
SaaS configuration checklist
  • Enforce strong authentication and MFA for all accounts.
  • Define and enforce least privilege for user roles.
  • Configure data loss prevention and sharing restrictions.
  • Audit and review tenant settings periodically.
Key Takeaway

SaaS minimizes host duties but places importance on governance of users, access, and data-sharing settings.
