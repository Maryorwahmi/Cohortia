Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › RBAC vs Direct
Why RBAC?
Roles group permissions for job functions; bind identities to roles rather than attaching policies directly to users.
Role example
Database Administrator role: permission set includes database management APIs, backup access, and monitoring read access. Assign to multiple DB admins.
Direct assignment example
An individual developer is given full database console access. When they leave, revocation requires multiple manual steps and may be missed.
RBAC reduces error-prone manual changes and provides clearer audit trails when role membership and role definitions are maintained.
Common pitfalls
  • Creating too many narrowly scoped roles per person.
  • Assigning roles with excessive permissions without review.
  • Failing to remove role membership when users change teams.
Check
Does your environment have role definitions that align to job functions, or are permissions scattered across many users?
Key takeaway
Favor role definitions for consistency, and combine RBAC with least privilege and periodic role reviews to prevent drift.
