Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › User & Role Creation
Conceptual steps
  1. Create the narrowly scoped IAM policy in JSON.
  2. Choose a user for humans or a role for services and compute.
  3. Attach the policy to the identity and name it purposefully.
  4. Enable MFA for human users and enroll recovery securely.
  5. Log events to immutable storage and verify in your SIEM.
Checks and validation
Perform allowed and denied operation tests.
Verify the policy appears in audit logs when attached.
Confirm MFA enforcement and document recovery processes.
Common mistake
Delaying MFA enrollment or failing to test denied operations can leave privileges wider than intended.
