Cloud Identity and Access Management (IAM)
Design identity as the cloud perimeter: who, what, when, and how.
In this lesson

Understand identities, roles, policies, MFA, federation, and auditing. Practice a least-privilege S3 policy and learn how to review IAM events.

Why it matters

Misconfigured IAM expands the attack surface. Correct role design limits blast radius and reduces breach impact.

Explore Key Topics
Three focused topics you'll practice in this chapter.
Principle of Least Privilege
Grant only the minimum permissions required for a role.
Role-Based Access Control
Define roles once and map users or services to them for consistent control.
Audit & Monitoring
Record management events and integrate with your SIEM for alerts and investigations.
Visual concept: Roles, Identities, Policies
Cards show the three pillars that enforce access in cloud platforms.
Identities
Users, groups, service principals, and roles that act in the cloud environment.
Permissions / Policies
Policy documents express allowed actions and the resources they apply to.
Enforcement
The cloud control plane enforces policies at API, console, and service levels.
Use these pillars to reason about privilege boundaries, MFA requirements, and logging strategies when you design or audit cloud systems.
