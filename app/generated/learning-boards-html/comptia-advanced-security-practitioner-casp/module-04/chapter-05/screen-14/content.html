Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › What to Remember
Design roles
Define roles by job function and assign identities to those roles for consistent permission management.
Enforce least privilege
Scope actions and resources narrowly and validate both allows and denies during testing.
Collect logs
Send audit data to immutable storage and a SIEM for detection and rapid response.
Final reflection
Use role reviews, MFA enforcement, and log retention as routine controls to keep IAM secure as your cloud estate changes.
