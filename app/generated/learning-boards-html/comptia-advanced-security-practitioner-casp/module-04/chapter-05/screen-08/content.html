Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Federated Identity
What federation provides
Single sign-on, centralized authentication policies, and reduced credential management overhead.
Protocols
SAML and OpenID Connect are common federation protocols; they exchange assertions or tokens from the identity provider to the cloud provider.
Practical example
Use Azure AD Connect to sync on-premises AD users to Azure AD, enabling SSO to Azure services and SaaS apps without separate cloud passwords.
Operational considerations
  • Map attributes and group claims so policy evaluation works correctly in the cloud.
  • Plan for identity provider availability and secure recovery processes.
  • Limit role mappings to necessary privileges to avoid overprovisioning.
Key takeaway
Federation reduces credential duplication and centralizes policy, but requires careful claim mapping and availability planning.
