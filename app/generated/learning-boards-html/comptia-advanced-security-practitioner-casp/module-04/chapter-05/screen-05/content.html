Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Least Privilege Process
PoLP: 5-step workflow
1
Identify required actions
Map the APIs and operations an identity needs to succeed.
2
Scope resources precisely
Target specific ARNs, buckets, or resource IDs instead of wildcards.
3
Create and test policy
Validate in a sandbox and watch for legitimate denied calls before deployment.
4
Deploy and monitor
Collect logs to ensure the policy allows intended behavior without excess.
5
Review and refine
Regularly remove stale roles, keys, and unused permissions.
Key takeaway
PoLP is a repeatable workflow: identify, scope, test, monitor, and refine.
Concrete example
An app that only needs to read from secure-app-data-12345 should receive a policy granting s3:GetObject on arn:aws:s3:::secure-app-data-12345/* and nothing more.
Common misconception
Some teams think granting broad access reduces operations friction. In practice it increases risk and troubleshooting complexity when incidents occur.
Try this
Run a policy analyzer and identify any policies with action or resource wildcards, then plan a narrow replacement.
Why it matters
Narrow policies reduce the blast radius if credentials are compromised and simplify audits.
