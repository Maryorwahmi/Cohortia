Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Pitfalls & Remediation
Wildcards are dangerous
Policies using action or resource wildcards expand privileges broadly and should be replaced with explicit actions and ARNs.
Remediation steps:
  • Find policies with s3:* or arn:aws:s3:::*
  • Identify required actions and specific resources
  • Replace wildcards with narrow statements and test
Stale credentials
Unused access keys, stale roles, and orphaned service principals are silent risks if not revoked and monitored.
Action list
  • Audit key last-used dates and rotate or revoke old keys
  • Remove roles or principals with no documented purpose
  • Automate periodic checks and remediation where possible
Key takeaway
Treat wildcards and stale credentials as immediate priorities for remediation and continuous detection.
