Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Hardening the Hypervisor
Process

Patch, restrict, and minimize

  • Patching: Apply vendor patches and firmware updates promptly.
  • Management access: Enforce MFA, RBAC, and limit IP ranges for management networks.
  • Service reduction: Disable SSH and other services unless required; use jump hosts for admin tasks.
  • Firewalling: Host-based rules to limit management plane exposure.
Concrete checklist
Validate patch levels monthly and after advisories
Restrict management VLANs and use dedicated jump hosts
Enable logging to a remote collector
Misconception
Treating hypervisor hardening as optional or infrequent increases systemic risk — make it part of routine operations.
Key Takeaway

A disciplined, documented hardening routine limits potential exploit paths into the hypervisor and preserves guest isolation.
