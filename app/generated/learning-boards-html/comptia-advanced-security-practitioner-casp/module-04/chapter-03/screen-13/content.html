Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Logging, Monitoring, and Incident Detection
Process

Collect, correlate, and act

  • Forward hypervisor management logs to SIEM
  • Collect guest OS and EDR telemetry
  • Correlate events for multi-stage detection
  • Maintain retention for forensic analysis
Concrete playbook
If SIEM correlates guest scanning with management-plane config changes: isolate affected VMs' port groups, revoke management credentials, and begin forensic snapshot capture of the host and guests.
Misconception
Thinking that alert volume is the primary problem — proper tuning and correlation reduce noise and surface real incidents faster.
Key Takeaway

Centralized logs and tuned correlation are essential to detect VM escape attempts and platform-wide misuse swiftly.
