Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Managing VM Sprawl
Process Flow

Prevent, Detect, Decommission

  1. Prevent: Use IaC templates and role-based provisioning to enforce baselines.
  2. Detect: Automated inventory and asset discovery to find untracked VMs.
  3. Decommission: Scheduled reviews and automated expiry to remove dormant VMs.
Concrete example
IaC template with tags and agents
A provisioning template automatically installs the EDR agent, applies CIS baseline, and includes tags for owner and expiry. The inventory flags expired tags for review and decommissioning.
Misconception
Deleting unused VMs is the whole solution — false. Governance and automation are needed to prevent reoccurrence and ensure compliance.
Key Takeaway

Combine IaC, inventory, and lifecycle policies so VMs are provisioned consistently and retired when no longer needed.
