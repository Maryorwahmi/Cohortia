Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Hypervisor
Software or firmware that creates VMs and enforces isolation.
Type 1 vs Type 2
Type 1 runs on hardware (smaller attack surface). Type 2 runs on a host OS and inherits its risks.
VM Escape
When a guest breaches its sandbox to access the host or other guests; patching and RBAC help prevent it.
Summary: Protect the hypervisor, choose the appropriate hypervisor model for your risk profile, and prioritize patching and management-plane controls to prevent escape and platform compromise.
