Concept map
Hypervisor → Guests → Networks → Monitoring
If the hypervisor is compromised, every guest and virtual network can be at risk. Hardening and segmentation aim to stop that chain.
Threat
VM Escape
A guest breaks isolation and attacks the hypervisor or other guests.
Operational Risk
VM Sprawl
Uncontrolled VM creation that increases attack surface and drives poor patching.
Quick checklist
  • Choose Type 1 for production
  • Restrict management networks
  • Automate provisioning with IaC
Key Takeaway

A small, well-patched hypervisor plus segmented networks and lifecycle controls reduce the chance of wide-scale compromise.
