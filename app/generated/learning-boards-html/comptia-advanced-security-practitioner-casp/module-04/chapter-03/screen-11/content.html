Architecture

Port groups, VLANs, and distributed switches

Segment VMs into logical zones and apply policies at the virtual switch to control traffic. Use VLAN IDs and separate port groups for management, DMZ, and internal services to reduce lateral movement.
Management VLAN — restricted access
DMZ port group — public-facing web tiers
Internal port group — databases and sensitive apps
vSphere example
Create a vSphere Distributed Switch, add port groups: MGMT (VLAN 10), DMZ (VLAN 20), INTERNAL (VLAN 30). Apply firewall rules so DMZ services only reach INTERNAL on specific ports.
Misconception
Assuming VMs on the same host are automatically isolated — they share virtual networking and require explicit segmentation to limit discovery and access.
Key Takeaway

Implement port groups and VLANs with strict firewall rules at the virtual switch to limit the blast radius of a compromised VM.
