Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts — Part 2
VM Sprawl
Uncontrolled VMs increase attack surface; prevent with IaC, inventory, and lifecycle policies.
Segmentation
Apply VLANs and port groups to separate management, DMZ, and internal zones and enforce policies at the switch.
Monitoring
Collect hypervisor and guest logs centrally and tune correlation to detect multi-stage attacks quickly.
Final summary: layered, operational controls across provisioning, hardening, segmentation, and monitoring reduce both the chance and impact of virtualization platform compromises.
