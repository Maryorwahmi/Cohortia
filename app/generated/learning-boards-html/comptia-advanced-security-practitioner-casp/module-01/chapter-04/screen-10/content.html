Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › CSPM vs CWPP
CSPM

Continuous posture monitoring across cloud accounts. Detects misconfigurations, compliance violations, and account-level risks. Good for guardrails and policy enforcement at scale.

Finds public buckets, overly permissive IAM policies, or disabled encryption.
Provides compliance reports and automated remediation suggestions.
CWPP

Protects individual workloads with vulnerability scanning, runtime protection, and host-based intrusion detection. Suitable for VMs, containers, and serverless monitoring.

Detects anomalous process behavior and block exploit attempts at runtime.
Offers granular workload telemetry for incident response.
Key Takeaway

CSPM and CWPP are complementary: use CSPM for posture and guardrails, CWPP for runtime protection, and integrate alerts into a single remediation process.
