Key Concepts — Part 2

CSPM
Continuously monitor cloud accounts for misconfigurations and compliance violations.
CWPP
Protect workloads—VMs, containers, and functions—with runtime detection and vulnerability management.
IAM Best Practices
Prefer roles and temporary credentials, enforce MFA, scope policies narrowly, and audit regularly.
Final takeaway

Combine design, tool selection, and operational automation so cloud controls remain effective as environments grow and change.
