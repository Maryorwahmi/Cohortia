Practical architecture patterns for dividing responsibilities, protecting workloads, and controlling identity across IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, containers, and serverless platforms.

Why this matters

Cloud shifts many infrastructure responsibilities away from teams. Misunderstanding those boundaries is the top cause of configuration gaps that lead to breaches. Learn where to build controls and where the provider already protects you.
