Question 1: Overly broad Lambda role
Problem: Lambda role with s3:* and logs:* grants excessive privileges. Fix: restrict to s3:GetObject for the specific bucket ARN and explicit CloudWatch log permissions for the function's log group.
Why it matters

Scoped permissions reduce the blast radius if credentials or function are compromised.

Question 2: SaaS CRM responsibilities
Problem: Concern about data residency and protection. Customer responsibilities include IAM, data classification, DLP configuration, and tenant settings; provider handles application and infrastructure security.
Practical steps
  • Enforce SSO and MFA for the SaaS tenant.
  • Configure DLP and audit logs in the application.
  • Validate provider data residency and encryption guarantees.
Key Takeaway

Translate exam scenarios into specific, testable fixes: narrow permissions and confirm tenant-level security settings rather than assuming provider default covers your needs.
