The Power of 'Shift Left' Security

Addressing vulnerabilities early saves time and resources.

Proactive Approach

Integrate security from the very beginning of the architecture lifecycle, rather than as an afterthought. This means security is a design requirement, not an add-on.

Cost-Effectiveness

Fixing vulnerabilities in early stages (planning, design) is significantly less expensive and disruptive than addressing them during development or, worse, after deployment in production.

Key Takeaway

Embedding security early ('shift left') is crucial for building resilient systems, reducing costs, and enabling business objectives by preventing costly retrofits and breaches.

Cost of Fixing Vulnerabilities Across Lifecycle

Planning Low Cost
Design Moderate Cost
Implementation High Cost
Operations Very High Cost

The later a security flaw is discovered, the exponentially higher the cost and effort to remediate it.
