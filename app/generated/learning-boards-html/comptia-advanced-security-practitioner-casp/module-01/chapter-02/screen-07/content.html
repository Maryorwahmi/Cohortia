Security in the Planning & Strategy Phase

Defining the foundational security posture and principles.

Key Activities

  • Define overall security strategy & risk appetite.
  • Establish security principles (Zero Trust, least privilege, defense in depth).
  • Select architectural security frameworks.
  • Formulate high-level security policies.

Key Deliverables

  • High-level security strategy document.
  • Enterprise risk register.
  • Security policy framework.
  • Compliance requirements mapping.

Example Scenario

A company plans to expand into the EU market. The architect must ensure the security strategy incorporates GDPR compliance requirements from the outset, defining data residency and privacy principles.

Impact

Prevents costly legal penalties and rework by embedding regulatory compliance into the core strategy.

Key Takeaway

The planning phase is critical for establishing a robust security foundation, aligning strategy with business goals, and addressing compliance proactively.
