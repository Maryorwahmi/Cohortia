Applying Threat Modeling Frameworks

Using STRIDE and DREAD to systematically uncover weaknesses.

STRIDE Methodology

A framework for categorizing and identifying threats against a system:

  • Spoofing: Impersonating entities.
  • Tampering: Unauthorized data modification.
  • Repudiation: Denying actions.
  • Info Disclosure: Unauthorized data access.
  • DoS: Preventing legitimate access.
  • EoP: Gaining higher privileges.

DREAD Methodology

Used for rating the severity of identified threats:

  • Damage: How bad is the impact?
  • Reproducibility: How easy to repeat?
  • Exploitability: How easy to exploit?
  • Affected Users: How many users impacted?
  • Discoverability: How easy to find?
Safety Note

Inadequate threat modeling can lead to fundamental design flaws that are extremely difficult and costly to fix later, potentially leaving critical systems exposed.
