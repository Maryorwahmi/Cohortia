Key Concepts

Essential principles for architectural security.

Shift Left

Integrating security activities and considerations earlier in the software development and enterprise architecture lifecycle.

Threat Modeling

A structured process for identifying potential threats, vulnerabilities, and countermeasures within a system or application.

STRIDE

A common threat modeling methodology categorizing threats as Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, and Elevation of Privilege.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive security integration, systematic threat identification, and structured methodologies are foundational for building resilient enterprise architectures.
