Mitigating IAM Architectural Weaknesses

Identifying and addressing common vulnerabilities

Common Weaknesses

  • Overly Permissive Policies: Violating least privilege.
  • Lack of MFA: Especially for administrative accounts.
  • Weak Password Policies: Easily guessable passwords.
  • Unmanaged Service Accounts: Broad privileges, rarely audited.
  • Broken AuthN/AuthZ: Flaws leading to bypasses.
  • Shadow IT Identities: Unauthorized cloud service accounts.

Key Takeaway

Proactive identification and mitigation of IAM weaknesses are crucial. This involves continuous auditing, strong policy enforcement, and user education to maintain a robust security posture.

Mitigation Strategy

Implement automated tools (e.g., CSPM) for continuous auditing, enforce strict MFA and password policies, and establish clear processes for managing all account types, including service accounts.

Real-World Impact

A single unmanaged service account with excessive privileges can be exploited by an attacker to gain a foothold and escalate privileges, leading to a major data breach.
