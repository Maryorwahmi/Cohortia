Identity Federation Principles

Sharing identities securely across disparate security domains

What is Federation?

The process of linking identities and their associated attributes across different security domains, allowing users to authenticate once and access resources in multiple domains.

Core Components

  • Identity Provider (IdP): Authenticates the user and issues assertions.
  • Service Provider (SP): Consumes assertions to grant access.
  • Trust Relationship: Agreement between IdP and SP to trust identity information.

Key Takeaway

Identity federation is crucial for seamless and secure access across hybrid and multi-cloud environments, reducing administrative overhead and improving user experience.

Use Case Example

An employee logs into their corporate network (IdP) and then accesses a SaaS application like Office 365 (SP) without re-entering credentials, thanks to federation.

Why it's Secure

Federation doesn't share passwords. Instead, it shares cryptographically signed assertions or tokens, ensuring that the identity information is trustworthy and tamper-proof.
