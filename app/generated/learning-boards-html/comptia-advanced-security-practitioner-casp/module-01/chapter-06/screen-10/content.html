Privileged Access Management (PAM)

Protecting high-value accounts from compromise

What is PAM?

Solutions and strategies for securing, managing, and monitoring privileged accounts (e.g., root, administrator, service accounts) to minimize their attack surface.

Key PAM Features

  • Secure Credential Storage: Vaulting privileged passwords.
  • Just-in-Time (JIT) Access: Temporary, time-limited access.
  • Session Monitoring: Recording and auditing privileged sessions.
  • Password Rotation: Automating password changes.

Key Takeaway

PAM is essential for protecting the most powerful accounts in your environment, significantly reducing the risk of a complete system takeover by attackers.

Real-World Scenario

An IT administrator needs root access to a server for 30 minutes. PAM grants temporary access, monitors their session, and revokes access automatically after the time limit, then rotates the root password.

Safety Note

A compromised privileged account can lead to complete system takeover. PAM is a top priority for minimizing this severe risk.
