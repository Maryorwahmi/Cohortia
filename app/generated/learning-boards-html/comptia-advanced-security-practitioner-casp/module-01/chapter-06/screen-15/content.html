Key Concepts (Continued)

Advanced terms from Identity and Access Management Architecture

Federation

Linking identities across different security domains for seamless access.

PAM

Solutions for securing, managing, and monitoring privileged accounts.

IGA

Manages identity lifecycle, access requests, certifications, and SoD.

Summary Takeaway

Advanced IAM components like Federation protocols (SAML, OAuth, OIDC), PAM, and IGA are crucial for comprehensive security, delegated access, and regulatory compliance in complex IT environments.
