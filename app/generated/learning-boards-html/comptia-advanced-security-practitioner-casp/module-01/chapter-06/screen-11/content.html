Identity Governance and Administration (IGA)

Managing identity lifecycle and ensuring regulatory compliance

What is IGA?

Solutions that manage the identity lifecycle, access requests, access certifications, and segregation of duties to ensure compliance and maintain a clean identity posture.

Key IGA Capabilities

  • User Provisioning/De-provisioning: Automating account management.
  • Access Workflows: Streamlining requests and approvals.
  • Access Certifications: Regular reviews of user access rights.
  • Segregation of Duties (SoD): Preventing conflicts of interest.

Key Takeaway

IGA is vital for maintaining a secure and compliant identity environment by automating lifecycle management, access reviews, and enforcing critical policy controls like SoD.

Compliance Example

A financial institution uses IGA to ensure no single employee can both initiate and approve a large transaction, thus preventing fraud and meeting regulatory requirements.

Why it Matters

Without IGA, organizations risk 'access creep' where users accumulate excessive permissions over time, increasing the attack surface and making compliance audits difficult.
