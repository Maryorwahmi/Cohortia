Differential Privacy
Adds noise to results to protect individuals while retaining statistical utility. Useful in dashboards and public reports.
Secure Multi-Party Computation
Enables joint computation without sharing inputs. Well-suited for collaborative analytics across organizations.
Federated Learning
Train models on-device or locally and aggregate updates centrally. Reduces raw-data movement but requires model governance.
