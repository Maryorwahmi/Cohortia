Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Key Management
Key lifecycle steps
  1. Generate keys with sufficient entropy in a trusted boundary (HSM or KMS).
  2. Store keys securely and limit access to cryptographic operations only.
  3. Rotate keys on a policy-driven schedule and during suspected compromise.
  4. Revoke and recover keys with documented fallback procedures.
Design considerations
  • Use HSMs for high-value keys to prevent export of raw key material.
  • Use KMS for centralized management, RBAC, and audit trails across services.
  • Plan re-encryption strategies to avoid breaking data access during rotation.
Misconception
Encrypting without proper key lifecycle controls is ineffective; a stolen key defeats encryption.
