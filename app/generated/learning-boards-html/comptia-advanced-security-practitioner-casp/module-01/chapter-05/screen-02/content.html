Learning Objectives

These objectives give you actionable outcomes: from classification to privacy-preserving analytics.

01
Design classification schemes

Create categories like Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted, and map each to mandatory controls and retention rules. This ensures each dataset receives the right protection level and audit requirements.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement encryption & key management

Select at-rest, in-transit, or advanced cryptographic options and pair them with HSMs or a KMS for lifecycle management, rotation, and revocation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Architect DLP & database protections

Define DLP policies from classification rules, enable database auditing and DAM, and apply role-based controls to limit exposure and enable investigation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Integrate PETs & ensure compliance

Use differential privacy, SMC, or federated learning where analytics require privacy, and embed privacy-by-design to meet GDPR, HIPAA, and CCPA obligations.
