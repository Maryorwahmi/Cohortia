Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › HIPAA Storage
Required controls
  • Encryption at rest (server-side or client-side)
  • TLS for all transfers
  • Customer-controlled keys or HSM-backed keys
  • Access controls, logging, and monitoring
Operational proof
Maintain auditable logs of key creation, access, and rotation. Restrict key admins and document emergency revocation procedures to present to auditors.
Concrete answer
Use server-side encryption for objects, manage keys with a customer KMS backed by HSM, enforce TLS, and retain key and access logs for audit.
