Learning Objectives — Deepening Skills

These objectives focus on making controls practical, auditable, and resilient.

Policies to practice
  • Create handling workflows that map to classifications and retention rules.
  • Train users and automate classification where possible to reduce mislabeling.
  • Measure policy adherence and tune for operational fit.
Key lifecycle & KMS
  • Define generation, rotation, backup, and emergency revocation procedures.
  • Use HSMs for sensitive key operations; use KMS for distributed management and audits.
DLP rules & tuning
  • Combine pattern-based detection and contextual policies to limit false positives.
  • Define actions: alert, block, encrypt, or quarantine based on risk level.
Privacy by Design
  • Minimize collection, pseudonymize where possible, and embed deletion mechanisms to meet rights requests.
