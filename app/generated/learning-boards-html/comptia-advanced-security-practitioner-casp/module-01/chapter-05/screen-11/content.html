Module 1 › Chapter 1.5 › Compliance
Regulatory mappings
  • GDPR: minimization, pseudonymization, erasure rights
  • HIPAA: ePHI protections, encryption, access logs
  • CCPA: consumer rights and data transparency
Privacy by Design checklist
  • Collect only necessary data and document purposes
  • Enable pseudonymization and deletion paths
  • Provide auditable processing logs and consent records
Example trade-off
An analytics pipeline may require identifiers for re-aggregation; plan pseudonymous keys and a secure re-identification process under strict controls.
