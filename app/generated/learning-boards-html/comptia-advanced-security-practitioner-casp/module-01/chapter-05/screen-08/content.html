DLP policy elements
  • Detection patterns (regex, keywords, ML)
  • Contextual filters to reduce false positives
  • Actions: alert, block, encrypt, or quarantine
  • Channel coverage: email, web, cloud, endpoints, removable media
Operational integration
Connect DLP alerts to SIEM/SOC playbooks. Provide analysts with classification metadata and redacted context so they can act without exposing full content. Track metrics for false positives and policy drift.
Example rule
Block outbound email containing more than two credit card patterns; alert SOC and require justification for release.
