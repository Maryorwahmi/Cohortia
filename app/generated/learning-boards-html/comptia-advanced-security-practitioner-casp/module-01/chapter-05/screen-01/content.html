Imagine a mobile app that collects location, email, and payment details. The choices you make at collection, transit, microservice processing, and database storage determine both risk and compliance scope. Use classification to decide which fields are "Restricted" versus "Confidential", then apply tokenization and encryption at the right boundaries, add DLP to stop accidental exfiltration, and use PETs where analytics must avoid revealing individuals.
