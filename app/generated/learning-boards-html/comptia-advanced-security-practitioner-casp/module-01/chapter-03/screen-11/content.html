Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › SDN & NFV
SDN: central control plane
Program network behavior centrally to automate policies and respond to threats, but protect the controller as a high-value asset.
Hardening checklist: authenticated APIs, role-based access, dedicated management networks, secure boot for controllers, and continuous change auditing.
NFV: virtualized services
Deploy firewalls, load balancers, and VPNs as VNFs for flexibility. Verify VNF integrity, limit inter-VNF permissions, and monitor performance.
Concrete Example
Deploy a VNF-based NGFW in the hub VPC managed by an SDN controller. Ensure controller access requires strong auth and audit logs are shipped to a central SIEM.
