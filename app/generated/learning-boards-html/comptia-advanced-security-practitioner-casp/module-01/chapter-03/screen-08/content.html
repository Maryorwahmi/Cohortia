Hub-and-spoke basics
Centralize shared services, logging, and inspection in a hub VPC and connect spokes to the hub using transit gateways or peering where appropriate.
Peering: simple, but does not scale to many VPCs.
Transit Gateway: scalable hub to connect many VPCs and on-prem links.
Direct Connect: private circuit to on-prem for sensitive database traffic.
Operational note
Plan inspection capacity for NGFWs in the hub. Offloading TLS or sampling may be required to avoid bottlenecks while retaining visibility.
Concrete Example
Use a transit gateway to connect 10 VPCs to a hub that hosts a centralized NGFW cluster and a logging stack receiving flow logs from spokes.
