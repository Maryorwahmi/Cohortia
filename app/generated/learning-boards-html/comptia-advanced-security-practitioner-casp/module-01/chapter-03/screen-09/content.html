Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › VPNs & ZTNA
IPsec VPN
Layer 3 tunnels for site-to-site links; strong crypto but can grant broad network access that must be segmented further.
SSL/TLS VPN
Layer 4–7 remote access often browser-based; easier for distributed workforces and more application-aware.
ZTNA
Application-specific access with continuous trust checks, reducing exposure compared to full VPN access.
Concrete Scenario
A large distributed workforce needing access to internal web apps benefits from SSL/TLS VPN or ZTNA because deployment is simpler and access is scoped to apps rather than entire subnets.
Misconception
Assuming all VPNs provide equivalent security. Protocols differ in scope, manageability, and the breadth of access they grant.
