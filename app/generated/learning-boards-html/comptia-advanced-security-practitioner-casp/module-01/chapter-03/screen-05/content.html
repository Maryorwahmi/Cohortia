Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Micro-segmentation: Process
Four-step enforcement flow
1.
Plan
Map tiers, protocols, ports, and required peer relationships.
2.
Monitor
Enable monitor-only mode, flow logs, and packet capture to observe real behavior.
3.
Refine
Adjust rules to cover legitimate flows, remove overly broad allows, and group policies.
4.
Enforce
Move to enforcement in waves, validate, and maintain rollback plans.
Pitfall to avoid: enforcing without a rollback or without log baselines.
Concrete example
# Example flow mapping for SecurePortal

Key Takeaway

A monitor-first, phased enforcement reduces outages and yields higher-quality rules for micro-segmentation.
