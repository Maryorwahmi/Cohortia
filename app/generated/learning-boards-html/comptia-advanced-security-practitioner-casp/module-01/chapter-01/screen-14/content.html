Key Concepts: Core Definitions

Essential Terms to Remember

Enterprise Security Architecture (ESA)

The strategic discipline aligning security with business goals, technology, and regulations across an organization.

Security Engineering

The tactical implementation and configuration of security solutions based on architectural designs.

Security Operations (SecOps)

Ongoing monitoring, maintenance, and incident response activities for implemented security controls.

Chapter Summary

Understanding the distinct roles of architecture, engineering, and operations is crucial for building and maintaining a cohesive and effective enterprise security posture.
