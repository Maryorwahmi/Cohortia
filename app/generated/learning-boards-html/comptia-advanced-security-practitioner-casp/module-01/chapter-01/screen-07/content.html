Role Distinction

Security Operations: The 'Running' Phase

Frontline Defense and Continuous Effectiveness

Architecture

Strategic planning and design ('What' & 'Why').

Engineering

Tactical implementation and building solutions ('How').

Operations

Ongoing monitoring, maintenance, and incident response ('Running').

Interdependence

These three roles are distinct but interdependent. Effective ESA requires strong collaboration to ensure designs are implementable and operable, creating a continuous feedback loop for security.

Key Takeaway

SecOps is the critical last line of defense, ensuring that the security architecture and engineering efforts translate into continuous, effective protection against real-world threats.
