SABSA: Business-Driven Security

Linking Security Directly to Business Requirements

What is SABSA?

  • Sherwood Applied Business Security Architecture: A highly business-driven framework.
  • Uses a matrix-based approach, starting with business attributes.
  • Ensures every security control is directly linked to a business requirement.
Example: Deriving specific data encryption requirements from a business need for 'confidentiality of customer financial records'.

SABSA Architectural Layers

01Conceptual: Business requirements and attributes.
02Logical: Security services and policies.
03Physical: Specific security technologies and products.
04Component: Implementation details and configurations.
05Operational: Ongoing management and monitoring.
Key Takeaway

SABSA ensures that security architecture is always driven by and traceable back to the organization's core business objectives and risk appetite.
