Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Technical Controls
Key technical controls
Email authentication, MFA, EDR, conditional access, and filtering reduce exposure and impact.
SPF/DKIM/DMARC to reduce domain spoofing.
Multi-factor authentication to prevent credential misuse.
Email filtering and sandboxing for attachments and links.
Endpoint detection for post-click activity and rapid containment.
Prioritization guidance
If budget is limited, prioritize MFA and robust email authentication/filtering first to reduce attack surface quickly.
Operational tip:
Combine controls with monitoring and response so that simulated or real breaches trigger remediation playbooks.
