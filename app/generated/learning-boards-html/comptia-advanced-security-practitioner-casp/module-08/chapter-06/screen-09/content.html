Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Physical Pen Testing
Common test techniques
Tailgating, badge cloning (when in scope), lock testing, planting inert devices, and surveillance assessment.
Tailgating attempts under supervision and rules of engagement.
Documenting CCTV blind spots and nonfunctional cameras.
Testing physical barriers and locks without causing damage.
Planting inert devices that log insertion but do not execute code.
Governance and safety
Written authorization, stakeholder notification, and get-out-of-jail documentation are mandatory. Tests must have abort criteria and safety plans.
Common mistake
Running tests without notifying HR or operations, causing unnecessary alarm or legal exposure.
