Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Vishing
Phone-based manipulation
Impersonation over voice channels to extract information or actions.
Concrete example: a caller claiming to be a vendor representative asking to update payment wiring instructions.
Why it works: urgency and the pressure to resolve financial issues quickly.
Misconception: trusting caller ID; attackers can spoof corporate numbers.
Verification controls
Require written confirmations for financial requests, use known corporate numbers to call back, and limit information shared over calls.
Pro Tip
Create a short playbook employees can read and practice: call-backs, escalation, and documentation procedures for verifying callers.
