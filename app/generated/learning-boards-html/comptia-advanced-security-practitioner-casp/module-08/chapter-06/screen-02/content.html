Learning Objectives
Concrete capabilities you will demonstrate after this chapter.
01
Identify social engineering tactics

Recognize phishing, pretexting, baiting, and vishing. This matters because detection prevents credential or data loss.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Assess physical security risks

Locate weak access controls, CCTV blind spots, and unsecured assets. This matters because physical access often enables cyber compromise.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Plan ethical simulations

Define scope, obtain written authorization, and design harmless payloads. This matters because legality and trust depend on clear rules.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Recommend mitigations and training

Combine technical controls like MFA and EDR with targeted awareness to reduce successful attacks.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
