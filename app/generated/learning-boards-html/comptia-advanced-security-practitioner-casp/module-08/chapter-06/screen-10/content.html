Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Phishing Simulation
Simulation steps
Select theme, draft message, set harmless tracking, define IoCs, obtain authorizations, and plan debrief.
  1. Choose a realistic theme such as password expiry or IT maintenance.
  2. Draft an email that is convincing but non-traumatic and does not solicit credentials.
  3. Use a tracking link that logs clicks only and redirects to a safe landing page explaining the test after clicking.
  4. Obtain written approvals and notify HR, legal, and executives as required.
  5. Aggregate results, run targeted training, and measure improvement.
Conceptual phishing email (simulation)
Subject: Urgent: Your Password Expires Today - Action Required! From: IT Support <support@yourcompany.com> (spoofed) To: [Employee Name] Dear [Employee Name], Your company account password is set to expire today. To avoid service interruption and ensure continued access to company resources, please update your password immediately by clicking the link below: https://simulated-login.cohortia-test.example/login Failure to update your password within 24 hours may result in account lockout. Thank you, Cohortia IT Security Team
Simulation safety note
Never collect real credentials. Use logging-only links and immediate educational landing pages after a click.
