Social Engineering and Physical Penetration Testing
Why people and premises matter as security controls.

This chapter teaches you to see security beyond code. You will learn attacker techniques that exploit trust, emotion, and routine, and you will learn how ethical testing safely reveals practical weaknesses. Expect practical examples of phishing, pretexting, USB baiting, tailgating, and controlled physical tests that stop short of harm.

In this lesson
Identify social engineering tactics, learn safe simulation rules, and recommend mitigations that combine training and technical controls.
Phishing
Simulated emails that test susceptibility to malicious links and impersonation.
Physical Tests
Controlled attempts to access facilities, plant devices, or assess locks and surveillance.
Mitigation
Awareness training, MFA, EDR, access control, and visitor management.
