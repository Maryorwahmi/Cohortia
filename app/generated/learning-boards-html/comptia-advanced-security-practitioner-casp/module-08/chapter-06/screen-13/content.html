Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Reporting & Debrief
Reporting steps
Aggregate metrics, identify trends, recommend prioritized fixes, and preserve privacy.
Example metrics: click rate, credentials submitted (simulated), time-to-report, and repeat offenders by role.
Recommended outputs: targeted training, filtering rule changes, MFA rollout plan, and physical control improvements.
Misconception: that publicizing names increases deterrence; it often reduces trust and reporting.
Debrief checklist
  • Explain goals and scope of the simulation.
  • Show anonymized results and IoCs.
  • Provide immediate training resources for participants.
  • Outline remediation steps and timelines.
Key Takeaway: Use results to educate and improve policies; preserve employee dignity to maintain a culture of reporting.
