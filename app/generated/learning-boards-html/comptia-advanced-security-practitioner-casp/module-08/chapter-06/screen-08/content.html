How tailgating works
An attacker exploits social norms like politeness to follow people into secure areas.
  • Attackers time arrivals to coincide with busy entrances.
  • They may pose as delivery, maintenance, or visitors to reduce suspicion.
  • Weak or broken locks and uncontrolled side doors increase success rates.
Mitigations
Mantraps or security vestibules enforce single-person access.
Turnstiles and monitored entry points reduce tailgating risk.
Active security personnel and clear employee challenge policies increase deterrence.
