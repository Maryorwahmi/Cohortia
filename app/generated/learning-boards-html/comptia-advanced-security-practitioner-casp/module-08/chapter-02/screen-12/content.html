Process Flow
Prioritization & Remediation Planning
Use severity, exposure, and exploitability to build a remediation roadmap.
Prioritization steps
  1. Filter informational and validate suspicious findings.
  2. Assess exposure: public-facing first.
  3. Evaluate exploitability and available mitigations.
  4. Plan patches with testing, rollback, and monitoring.
Action plan example: Critical Apache
1) Identify impacted hosts. 2) Verify with authenticated scan. 3) Reproduce in staging and upgrade to supported version. 4) Apply to production during maintenance window. 5) Validate logs and functionality post-change.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize remediations by impact and exposure, and always include verification and rollback steps to avoid unintended outages.
