Authenticated Scan
Use credentials for deeper checks, but apply least privilege and secure storage.
Specialized Scans
Network, web app, container, and cloud scans target different attack surfaces and require different tools.
CI/CD Integration
Scan images in pipelines to catch vulnerabilities earlier and prevent insecure artifacts from deploying.
Summary Takeaway

Use layered scans, verify findings before action, prioritize by exposure and exploitability, and integrate scanning into development workflows while maintaining authorization and safety.
