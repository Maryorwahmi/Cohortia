Comparison
Nessus vs OpenVAS
Nessus
Commercial, extensive plugin database, detailed reporting, easier policy templates and credential integrations.
OpenVAS
Open-source; flexible and customizable, but may require more maintenance and manual tuning for parity.
Concrete scenario
A small security team selects Nessus to benefit from ready-made policies and support. A research lab chooses OpenVAS to tailor checks and share plugin updates.
Common mistake
Assuming a commercial tool removes the need for verification. Human triage remains essential regardless of tool choice.
Key Takeaway

Tool selection depends on operational fit, plugin quality, and integration. Expect to tune any scanner to your environment.
