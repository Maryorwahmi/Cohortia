Skills & Risks
Report triage
  • Recognize informational plugin outputs and service detection that are not vulnerabilities.
  • Verify critical findings with credentials or manual checks before remediation work orders.
  • Correlate scan output with asset inventory and compensating controls.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Operational safety
  • Use least-privilege credentials and secure storage for scanner access.
  • Schedule intrusive tests during maintenance windows and use non-destructive options where possible.
  • Obtain explicit authorization for production scans to avoid legal or operational risk.
