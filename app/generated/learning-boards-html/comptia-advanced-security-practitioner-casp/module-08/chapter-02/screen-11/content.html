Interpreting Scan Reports and False Positives
Filter info, validate findings, and assess business impact to prioritize remediation.
Report excerpt (simulated)
1. Plugin 10180: SSH Weak MACs — Medium
2. Plugin 19506: Nessus Scan Information — Info
3. Plugin 21745: SMB Signing Not Required — High
4. Plugin 10394: Service Detection — Info
5. Plugin 10400: Apache ETag Info — Low
6. Plugin 97993: Outdated Apache 2.4.6 — Critical
Triage checklist
  1. Mark clear INFO items and document for inventory.
  2. Verify detection via credentialed scan or manual verification.
  3. Assess exposure: public-facing services get higher priority.
  4. Prioritize by exploitability and business impact.
Concrete example
Critical Apache 2.4.6 on a public host: high priority to patch or isolate. SMB signing not required on a Windows host accessible internally: prioritize enabling signing and auditing SMB access.
Misconception

Not every high-severity finding is immediately exploitable; always verify context and exposure before urgent escalations.
