Match the scan
Choose unauthenticated, authenticated, web, or container scans based on asset and goal.
Validate results
Verify high findings with authenticated checks or manual validation to reduce false positives.
Automate & tune
Integrate scans into CI with thresholds so only actionable issues block builds.
Final takeaway

Use layered scanning, verify findings, and integrate scanning into development responsibly to make security decisions faster and more accurate.
