Typical role hierarchy
Who owns what in the program
Vulnerability Manager
Program scope, policy, metrics, and escalation authority.
Security Analysts
Perform scans, analyze results, and recommend priorities.
System Owners / Dev Teams
Implement remediation and coordinate testing.
Risk & Compliance
Translate technical findings into business risk and provide audit oversight.
Escalation example
If a critical vulnerability is not remediated within 24 hours: 1) Notify application owner; 2) Notify service manager after 8 hours; 3) Escalate to security leadership after 24 hours. Document each step to show due diligence.
Misconception: spreading responsibility across teams ensures coverage. Reality: it often causes delays. Assign one accountable owner and clear escalation to avoid bottlenecks.
