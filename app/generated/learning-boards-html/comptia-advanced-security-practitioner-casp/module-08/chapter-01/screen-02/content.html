Learning Objectives
Concrete skills you'll practice and verify
01
Establish lifecycle
Define discovery, identification, assessment, remediation, verification, and monitoring as a continuous cycle. This matters because a lifecycle enforces repeatability and measurement.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate assessment types
Choose between unauthenticated and authenticated scans, and when to use manual testing to reduce false positives and improve coverage.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Develop policies and SOPs
Write remediation timelines and scanning frequency for specific asset classes to ensure predictable responses and audit evidence.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Integrate with risk & compliance
Map vulnerabilities into the risk register and provide evidence for frameworks like NIST or ISO through documented scans and remediation logs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

These objectives progress from knowing the lifecycle to applying policies and mastering integration with risk registers and compliance evidence. Each objective is paired with a milestone you can prove with artifacts like scan reports and remediation tickets.
