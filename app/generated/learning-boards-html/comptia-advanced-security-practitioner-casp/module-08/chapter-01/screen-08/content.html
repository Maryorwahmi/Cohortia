Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Remediation Strategies
Remediation methods
Choose the fix that balances speed, safety, and effectiveness
Patching
Apply vendor fixes; schedule testing and change windows to avoid disruption.
Configuration changes
Disable vulnerable services, tighten defaults, or remove unnecessary exposure.
Compensating controls
Temporary measures like segmentation or extra monitoring when immediate patches are impractical.
Example remediation workflow
1) Create remediation ticket with owner and SLA. 2) Schedule change window if patching is required. 3) Apply fix in a controlled manner. 4) Verify with re-scan or manual test. 5) Document evidence and close ticket.
Pro tip: When immediate patching risks service stability, apply compensating controls and record the decision with a timeline and verification plan. This maintains security posture while avoiding accidental outages.
