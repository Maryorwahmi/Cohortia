Two-factor prioritization
Combine technical score with business context
Technical factors
  • CVSS base score
  • Exploitability and known exploits
  • Scan confidence
Business context
  • Asset criticality and data sensitivity
  • Public exposure and network reachability
  • Compensating controls like MFA and segmentation
Practical rule
Prioritize remediation where exploitability and business impact overlap, not solely on raw CVSS values.
Example decision matrix
Finding A: CVSS 9.8 on internal non-production system with MFA — assessment: medium priority due to low exposure and compensating controls; document rationale and schedule patching in next maintenance window.
Finding B: CVSS 6.8 on public production web server handling sensitive data — assessment: high priority due to exposure and potential business impact; remediate immediately or apply compensating firewall rules until patching completes.
Misconception: higher CVSS always equals higher business risk. Correct: CVSS is a technical baseline that must be combined with organizational context to make effective decisions.
