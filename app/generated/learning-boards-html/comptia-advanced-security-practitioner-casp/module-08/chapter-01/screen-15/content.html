Three Practical Reminders
Live inventory
Keep asset data and ownership current so scans and remediations are effective.
Contextual prioritization
Use CVSS plus exposure and business impact to rank work.
Mandatory verification
Require re-scans or manual tests and store evidence for audits.
Quick action

Use a weekly checklist: update inventory, review top prioritized findings, confirm verification evidence for closed tickets.
