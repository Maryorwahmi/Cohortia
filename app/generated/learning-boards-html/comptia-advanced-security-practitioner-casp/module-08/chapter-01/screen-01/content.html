Foundations of Vulnerability Management Programs
A practical orientation to continuous discovery, prioritization, and verification.

This chapter shows how inventory, automated scanning, risk-based prioritization, remediation, and verification form an ongoing cycle that reduces real enterprise risk. It explains required policies and the team roles that keep the cycle operating reliably.
