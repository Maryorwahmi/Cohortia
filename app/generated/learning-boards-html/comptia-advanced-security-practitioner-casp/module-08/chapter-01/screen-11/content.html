Module 08 › Chapter 8.1 › Integrating with Risk & Compliance
How data flows
Findings → Prioritization → Risk register → Audit evidence
Risk register entry
Include finding summary, business impact, remediation plan, timeline, and owner.
Evidence retention
Store scan reports, remediation tickets, and verification artifacts with timestamps.
Documented exceptions
If remediation is delayed, record compensating controls and approval to demonstrate due diligence.
Example mapping
A public-facing vulnerability with medium CVSS is linked to a risk register entry showing potential impact to customer data. The entry lists remediation actions, compensating controls, owner, and a planned completion date. The risk entry and remediation tickets are supplied to auditors as evidence.
Misconception: vulnerability teams only produce technical reports. Correct: the program supplies structured risk data and evidence that leadership and auditors use to make business decisions.
