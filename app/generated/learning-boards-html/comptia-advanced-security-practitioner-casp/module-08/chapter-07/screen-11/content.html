Contrast

Noisy scanning vs stealthy emulation

Noisy scanning reveals obvious alerts but offers little insight into whether defenders catch persistent, credential-based intrusions.
Noisy: mass probes and loud payloads, early detection, limited downstream insights.
Stealthy: valid accounts, minimal churn, shaped C2, reveals gaps in anomaly and behavior detection.
Example comparison
Using T1078 valid accounts is more representative of how adversaries maintain access than running a loud network-wide scan. Configure exercises to reflect the realistic threat you want to test.
Key Takeaway

Design trade-offs consciously: stealth reveals detection gaps, noise tests baseline alerting. Pick the right approach for the learning goals.
