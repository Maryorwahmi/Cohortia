Principles

Keep the exercise credible and safe

OPSEC prevents accidental detection and preserves realism so defenders face tactics an adversary would use.

Dedicated infrastructure: separate C2 servers and VMs.
Mimic legitimate traffic: Malleable C2 headers and URIs.
Avoid noisy tools: prefer stealthy discovery methods.
Stealthy payloads: fileless techniques or reflective injection to reduce artifacts.
OPSEC checklist
  • Isolated infra and test domains
  • Malleable traffic resembling normal applications
  • Low-noise discovery and scanning
  • Controlled exfiltration paths
Common Mistake

Using default tools and aggressive scans that trigger obvious alarms and invalidate the exercise realism.
