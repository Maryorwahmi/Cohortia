Module 08 › Chapter 8.7 › MITRE ATT&CK
What it is

A knowledge base of tactics and techniques

Use ATT&CK entries to choose realistic techniques to emulate and to label findings consistently for defenders.

Plan: pick ATT&CK techniques as exercise building blocks.
Design: sequence techniques into attack chains.
Measure: map detections to technique IDs to find coverage gaps.
