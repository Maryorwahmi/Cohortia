Module 08 › Chapter 8.7 › Purple Teaming
Process Flow

Execute → Review → Tune

A coordinated debrief where red and blue teams convert attack paths into prioritized detection improvements.
Red executes emulation and documents ATT&CK mapping.
Blue shows which detections fired and where gaps exist.
Teams prioritize actionable rules and telemetry changes.
Outcome

Short-term rule tuning and longer-term telemetry engineering tracked with owners and success metrics.

Practice
Bring a mapped attack and a prioritized detection-gap list to the session to make the debrief productive.
