Red Teaming and Adversary Emulation

Understand strategic objectives, ATT&CK-driven emulation, C2 frameworks, OPSEC and purple teaming.

In this lesson
Strategic emulation • ATT&CK mapping • C2 and OPSEC • Purple teaming
Why it matters

Red teaming tests the people, processes, and technology together. It reveals gaps that scoped vulnerability scans miss and shows whether defenses detect real adversary behavior.

Real world link

Using ATT&CK creates a repeatable way to plan attacks, record techniques used, and measure security improvements after purple teaming.

Card: Core idea
Goal-oriented emulation
A red team pursues high-level objectives like privilege escalation or data exfiltration, adapting TTPs rather than only exploiting individual vulnerabilities.
