Question

Which principle and feature explain the C2 configuration?

Identify the OPSEC principle and the specific C2 capability that allows traffic to resemble legitimate HTTPS to cloud services.

Correct answer (conceptual)
Principle: OPSEC — the red team is avoiding detection by blending with legitimate traffic. Feature: Malleable C2 profiles that customize headers, URIs, and output formatting.
Why it matters
Linking principle and feature shows understanding of both strategy (OPSEC) and concrete configuration steps (Malleable C2) that reduce signature-based detection.
Reflection

Also consider safeguards to prevent OPSEC choices from causing harm, such as white-listing test domains and ensuring legal approvals are in place.
