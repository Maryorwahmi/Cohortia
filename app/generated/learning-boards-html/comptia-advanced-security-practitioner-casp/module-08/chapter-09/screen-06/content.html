Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Prioritization Framework
Triage matrix rules
Immediate: CVSS high or critical, asset criticality high, or active exploitation reported.
Plan: CVSS high but asset criticality low and no active exploitation.
Monitor: CVSS medium with low criticality; add to watchlist and monitor threat feeds.
Accept or future plan: Low CVSS and low criticality; reassess periodically.
Concrete example
Vulnerability A

CVSS 9.8 on public customer portal handling PII. Threat feeds show exploit proof-of-concept. Action: immediate patch and outage plan.

Vulnerability B

CVSS 9.8 on isolated dev server with no sensitive data and no active exploit. Action: schedule patch in next maintenance window; continue monitoring.

Documentation tip

For each ticket include one justification sentence citing CVSS, asset criticality level, and any threat intel used to prioritize it.
