Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Vulnerability Disclosure
Responsible disclosure steps
  1. Privately notify vendor with reproducible technical details.
  2. Provide affected versions, impact assessment and suggested mitigations.
  3. Allow reasonable remediation time before public disclosure.
  4. Coordinate public messaging and document the timeline.
When vendors are unresponsive

Escalate through vendor security contact paths, involve legal if needed, or use coordinated disclosure with trusted third parties. Keep leadership informed of actions and risks.

Key takeaway

Responsible disclosure reduces user risk and helps vendors patch effectively; document everything for traceability.
