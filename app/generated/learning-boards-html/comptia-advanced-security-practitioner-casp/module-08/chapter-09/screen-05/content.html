CVSS Base Metrics
  • Attack Vector — network, adjacent, local, or physical access.
  • Attack Complexity — how many special conditions are required.
  • Privileges Required and User Interaction — who must act to exploit.
  • Scope — whether exploitation affects other components.
  • Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability impacts — degree of loss.

Practical guidance
  • Use CVSS to compare technical severity across findings and communicate consistent severity levels.
  • Do not use CVSS alone to schedule work—always add asset criticality and threat intel.
  • Document your prioritization rationale in the report so decisions are auditable.
  • Update scores with new information like exploit availability or changed system exposure.
Key takeaway

Treat CVSS as a consistent technical signal; translate it into business priority with asset context and threat data.
