Report anatomy
Executive Summary

High-level risks, top three business impacts, recommendations and timelines for leaders.

Technical Findings

CVE identifiers, PoC evidence, affected assets, remediation steps and verification steps.

Prioritization

Combine CVSS, asset criticality and threat intelligence to rank action items.

Tracking

POAM entries, owners, deadlines, verification and status updates for continuous visibility.

Why this matters
Actionable reporting turns findings into reduced business risk.
Clear communication ensures leaders fund priorities and engineers can act quickly with minimal clarification.
