Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Learning Objectives
04 Practical tasks
  1. Draft an executive summary identifying top three business risks and recommended next steps.
  2. Create a technical appendix with CVE, PoC, affected hosts, and remediation steps.
  3. Complete a POAM entry assigning owner, deadline, and verification criteria.
  4. Define two KPIs and a reporting cadence for leadership visibility.
Verification tips
Executive summary is under one page and lists top three business impacts.
Technical appendix includes reproducible evidence and exact remediation steps.
POAM items have owners and measurable verification tasks.
KPIs are numeric and reported regularly to stakeholders.
