Scenario summary

Vulnerability 1: Critical RCE on public customer portal (handles PII and payments) — CVSS 9.8. Vulnerability 2: Critical RCE on internal dev server with no customer data — CVSS 9.8.

Recommended decision

Prioritize the public portal remediation due to higher asset criticality and public exposure. Document the rationale in the remediation ticket.

POAM example
  • Owner: WebOps lead
  • Milestone: Patch deployment and verification within 24 hours
  • Compensating control: WAF rules and active monitoring until patch applied
  • Verification: External exploit test and log confirmation of blocked attempts
