Exploit Frameworks and Pen Testing
Why proving impact matters and how to do it safely and methodically.
You will: learn the distinction between scanning, pen testing, and red teams; practice Metasploit console commands; and apply PTES phases to organize assessments. Expect concrete examples, practical terminal steps, and clear safety rules that prevent accidental harm.
