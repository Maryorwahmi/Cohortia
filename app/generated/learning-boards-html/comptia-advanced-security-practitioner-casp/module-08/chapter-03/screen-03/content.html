Why this practical skillset?
Without hands-on practice, theory about exploits is abstract. These objectives give you the confidence to configure modules and payloads that prove real business impact while preserving safety. You’ll also be able to explain choices to non-technical stakeholders when writing reports.
Mnemonic: Configure, Confirm, Communicate
Set options, confirm impact in a controlled way, then communicate business risk.
Misconception check
Having credentials makes a test easier, but white-box testing focuses on depth while black-box focuses on detection capabilities.
