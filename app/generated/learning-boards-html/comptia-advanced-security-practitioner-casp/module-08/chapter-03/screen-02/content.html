Why these objectives matter
A technical finding without proof of impact can be deprioritized. Demonstrating exploitation informs risk-based decisions and improves defender response. Clear authorization protects you and your client from legal and operational harms.
Mnemonic: Prove, Protect, Report
Prove impact, Protect with RoE, and Report findings in business terms.
Misconception check
Finding many low-severity scans doesn't mean systems are safe; exploitation may still show high-impact paths.
