The seven PTES phases
A timeline that ensures each test is planned, executed, and reported responsibly.
1. Pre-engagement Interactions
Define scope, RoE, schedules, and legal permission to avoid misunderstandings and liability.
2. Intelligence Gathering
Collect passive and active information such as public records, open ports, and service banners.
3. Threat Modeling
Analyze attack paths to prioritize targets and expected adversary behavior.
4. Vulnerability Analysis
Use scanners and manual techniques to identify exploitable weaknesses.
5. Exploitation
Attempt controlled exploitation to demonstrate the impact of identified vulnerabilities.
6. Post-Exploitation
Maintain access, escalate privileges, and map internal impact while respecting scope limits.
7. Reporting
Translate technical findings into business impact and remediation guidance for stakeholders.
Key Takeaway

Do not skip planning or reporting; PTES ensures tests are useful, safe, and actionable.
