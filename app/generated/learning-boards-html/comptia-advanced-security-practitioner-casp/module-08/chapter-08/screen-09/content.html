Module 08 › Chapter 8.8 › GDPR & HIPAA
GDPR
Requires lawful processing, rights for data subjects, breach notification and appropriate measures. Evidence: data inventories, processing agreements, and breach records.
HIPAA
Protections for patient health information; evidence includes PHI access logs, policies, and breach response documentation.
Concrete example
Breach handling timeline and notification records show legal obligations were followed.
Common mistake
Focusing only on technical logs while neglecting contracts, consent, and process documentation.
Key Takeaway

Privacy frameworks require cross-functional evidence: technical logs + legal and procedural artifacts to demonstrate compliance.
