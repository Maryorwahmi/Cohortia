Module 08 › Chapter 8.8 › ISO/IEC 27001
What auditors expect
  • Defined ISMS scope and documented policies
  • Risk assessments and selected controls
  • Records of internal audits and corrective actions
  • Management review and continual improvement evidence
Concrete artifacts
Scope statement and asset inventory
Risk register and treatment plans
Internal audit reports and POAM entries
Key Takeaway

ISO 27001 certifies that a management system exists and is practiced; collect management reviews and internal audit evidence to show operation.
