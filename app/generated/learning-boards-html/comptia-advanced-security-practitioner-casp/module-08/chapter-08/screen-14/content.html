Module 08 › Chapter 8.8 › External Audit Prep
Checklist for external audits
  • Inventory of artifacts mapped to requirements
  • Configuration scan outputs and baseline references
  • Centralized log excerpts, alert definitions, and retention proof
  • POAM with owners and verification artifacts
  • Rehearsed control owner interviews
Label artifacts with dates, scope, and the specific control they satisfy. Provide indexes and quick-reference tables to speed the auditor’s work.
Concrete practice
Run a mock external audit, collect requests, and measure how quickly you can produce labeled artifacts and verification evidence.
Common mistake
Providing unlabeled or unsourced artifacts forces auditors to waste time matching evidence to requirements.
Key Takeaway

Index and label evidence clearly, pair technical artifacts with process documentation, and rehearse owners so audits are efficient and conclusive.
