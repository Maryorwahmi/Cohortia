Module 08 › Chapter 8.8 › Internal vs External
Internal Audit
  • Conducted by internal staff for continuous improvement.
  • Flexible schedule and scope tied to operational priorities.
  • Focuses on detecting local process gaps and driving remediation.
External Audit
  • Independent third party provides objective assessment.
  • Often required for certification or legal compliance.
  • Requires formal artifacts and time-bound evidence.
Evidence expectations
Design evidence
Policies and documented control objectives showing intent.
Operational evidence
Configuration scans, access change logs, and retained event logs showing controls operated.
Remediation evidence
POAM entries with owners, dates, and verification steps for findings.
Misconception

Passing a single scan is not equivalent to sustained compliance—auditors expect documented and verifiable operation over time.
