Five core functions
Identify
Protect
Detect
Respond
Recover
Identify evidence
Asset inventory, risk assessments, and scope documents.
Detect evidence
SIEM alerts, retained logs, and correlation rules.
Respond evidence
Incident playbooks, tickets, and after-action reports.
Concrete example
Centralized logging and a documented triage workflow support Detect and Respond: logs show events, triage shows action.
Misconception
Treating functions in isolation. Effective programs iterate across all five functions with evidence linking them together.
Key Takeaway

Use the five functions as an audit map: collect artifacts that together demonstrate lifecycle coverage.
