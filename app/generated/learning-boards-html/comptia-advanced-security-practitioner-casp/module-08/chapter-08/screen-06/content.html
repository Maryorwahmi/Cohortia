Module 08 › Chapter 8.8 › Compliance Frameworks
Framework cards
NIST CSF
Function-based model: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. Use to prioritize improvements.
ISO/IEC 27001
An ISMS standard; certification demonstrates a systematic approach to information security management.
GDPR
EU data protection regulation covering lawful processing, rights, and strong privacy obligations for personal data.
HIPAA
U.S. law protecting patient health information with privacy and security rules for covered entities.
PCI DSS
Standards for entities handling payment card data requiring specific technical and operational controls.
Mapping example

Map controls that overlap frameworks and reuse evidence. For example, centralized logging supports NIST Detect, GDPR breach detection expectations, and PCI logging requirements.

Concrete mapping
Centralized logs + retained alerts = evidence for detection controls across multiple frameworks.
Misconception
No single framework covers every requirement; choose the framework(s) that align with legal and business needs and map controls accordingly.
Key Takeaway

Identify overlapping control requirements and collect artifacts that satisfy multiple frameworks for efficient audit preparation.
