Module 08 › Chapter 8.8 › What is a Security Audit?
Definition

A systematic evaluation of controls, policies, and procedures to determine effectiveness and adherence to standards.

Why it matters
Provides assurance, reduces legal risk, and builds stakeholder trust by proving controls work over time.
Concrete example
Review password policy, enforcement logs, and a sample of user account changes to confirm policy is enforced.
Common mistake
Relying only on a single scan without documented procedures and historical evidence.
Comparison: Audit vs Compliance Test
Audit
  • Evaluates design and operation of controls
  • Uses policies, logs, and interviews
  • Focus on continuous improvement
Compliance Test
  • Checks specific requirements vs standard
  • Produces pass/fail evidence
  • Often required by regulation or contract
Key Takeaway

Treat scans as evidence, not as the entire audit. Combine policies, automation, and logs to show a control is effective over time.
