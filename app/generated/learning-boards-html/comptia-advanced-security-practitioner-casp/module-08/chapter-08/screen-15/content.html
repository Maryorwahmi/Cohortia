Module 08 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts
Security Audit
A systematic evaluation of controls, policies, and procedures to determine effectiveness.
Compliance Testing
Verifies that organization meets requirements of frameworks, laws, or standards.
Internal & External Audits
Internal: improvement focused. External: independent assurance for certification and regulators.
Summary takeaway

Use clear definitions to map artifacts and processes to audit requirements so evidence is consistent and verifiable.
