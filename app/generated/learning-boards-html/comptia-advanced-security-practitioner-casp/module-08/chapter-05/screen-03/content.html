Safe Testing Practices
Environment choices, non-destructive checks, and authorization
Environment
Emulators, test devices, and dedicated cloud accounts limit risk and allow safe experimentation.
Non-destructive Tools
Use scanners and read-only CLI queries to enumerate settings before performing active tests.
Scope & Permission
Obtain written permission and follow provider policies to avoid account suspension or legal issues.
