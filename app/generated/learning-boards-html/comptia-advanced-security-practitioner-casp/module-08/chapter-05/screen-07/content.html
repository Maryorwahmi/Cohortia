Reverse engineering & tampering
Why decompilation exposes risks and what to verify
What testers look for
  • Hard-coded keys or endpoints
  • Logic that trusts client-side checks
  • Paths that skip authentication or authorization
Mitigations
  • Remove secrets from builds and use secure storage
  • Server-side validation and token binding
  • Use obfuscation plus runtime checks, but assume determined attackers can bypass
Analogy
Think of the app as a building whose floor plans are bundled with the keys. If an attacker can read the plans and obtain keys, they can reach sensitive rooms. Hide plans and require server-side guards to reduce exposure.
