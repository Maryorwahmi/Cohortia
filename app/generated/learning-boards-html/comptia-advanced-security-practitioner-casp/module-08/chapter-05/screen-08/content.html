Shared Responsibility Model
Who secures what in cloud environments
Provider responsibility
Physical datacenters, hypervisor security, and managed infrastructure components.
Customer responsibility
Customer data, OS/app configuration, IAM policies, and network rules.
Analogy: hotel room vs house
A hotel secures the building; you lock your room. In the cloud, the provider secures the platform and you secure your deployed resources.
