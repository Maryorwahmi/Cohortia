Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Hybrid Network Connectivity
Connectivity options
Choose based on security, bandwidth, and latency needs.
VPN
IPsec or SSL tunnels provide encrypted, flexible connections over the internet.
Direct Connect / ExpressRoute
Private dedicated links for higher bandwidth and stable latency, ideal for large data transfer needs.
Operational guidance
Always apply segmentation and encryption, test failover, and monitor flows with IDS/IPS or cloud threat detection.
Example

A backup pipeline over ExpressRoute should still encrypt data in transit, restrict access by subnet and IAM, and be validated for failover.

Misconception

Private links do not remove the need for network controls or monitoring; they complement them.
