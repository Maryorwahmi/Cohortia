Process Flow
Onboarding: Agent → Validate → Scale
Follow a pilot, validation, then phased scale with capacity monitoring.
Agents
Installed on servers and endpoints for rich telemetry and reliable delivery.
Syslog Forwarders
Common for network devices and Unix hosts; watch for reliability and batching delays.
APIs
Used for cloud services and modern apps; handle rate limits and authentication carefully.
Validation steps
  • Generate known events and confirm arrival and parsed fields.
  • Check field mappings and timestamp alignment for correlation accuracy.
  • Monitor ingestion rates and pipeline queue lengths for 48–72 hours.
  • Document rollback, credential rotation, and fallback connector methods.
Common mistake
Overloading the SIEM by onboarding verbose debug logs without filtering, which leads to dropped or delayed events.
