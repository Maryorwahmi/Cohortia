SOAR Playbook Pattern
Enrichment → Containment → Remediation → Reporting, with safety gates and rollback plans.
Enrichment
Query EDR, asset inventory, and threat feeds to add context to the alert.
Containment
Block IPs, isolate endpoints, or disable accounts. Require approvals for high impact actions.
Remediation & Reporting
Run scans, patch, restore from backups, and open incident tickets with documented findings.
Safety checklist
  • Test playbooks in a sandbox environment before production.
  • Use human approval steps for blocking or isolation actions that affect business operations.
  • Design clear rollback procedures and logging for every automated action.
Example
A SIEM high-confidence alert triggers a SOAR playbook that enriches with EDR, opens a ticket, and isolates the host after analyst approval.
