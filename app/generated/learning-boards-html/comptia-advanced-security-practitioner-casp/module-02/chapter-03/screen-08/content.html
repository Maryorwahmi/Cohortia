Architecture
Threat Intelligence → SIEM Enrichment
Feed IOCs into the SIEM, map them to fields, and use reputation scoring to influence alerting and playbooks.
Sources: commercial vendors, open-source, ISACs, internal intelligence.
Mapping: ensure IOC fields map to canonical SIEM fields like threat.ip, threat.hash, threat.domain.
Validation: test feed accuracy against historical events before enabling automated actions.
Practical rules
  • Assign confidence scores to feeds and use thresholds for automated escalation.
  • Split feeds by trust level: high-confidence feeds can trigger SOAR playbooks; noisy feeds should be used for analyst context only.
  • Rotate and refresh feeds to ensure IOCs remain current.
Example
A successful login from an IP flagged by a high-confidence feed increases alert severity and triggers immediate enrichment queries to EDR and asset inventory.
