Comparison

Automated Actions vs Operational Risk

Low-impact
Alerts, forced scans, notifications — safe to automate broadly.
Medium-impact
Terminate processes or quarantine files — test in staging and limit scope.
High-impact
Network isolation and selective wipes — require approvals or restricted groups.
Operational Controls
  • Define device groups and permissible automated actions per group.
  • Require human confirmation for high-impact actions on critical systems.
  • Create rollback and recovery steps for mistaken actions.
Key Takeaway

Balance speed with safety: automate where safe, require approval where risk to operations is high, and pilot extensively.
