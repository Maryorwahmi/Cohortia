Comparison

EDR compared to Antivirus

Understand the strengths, limits, and how they complement each other in a layered defense.

Primary focus: behavior, investigation, and response.
Data: continuous process, network, file, and registry telemetry for timeline reconstruction.
Common use: threat hunting, root-cause analysis, and containment actions.
Misconception: EDR replaces AV. Reality: they operate together — AV for mass prevention, EDR for advanced detection and response.
Key Takeaway

Use AV for high-volume blocking and EDR for behavioral visibility and response; plan telemetry retention to avoid overload.

Concrete Example
Imagine a script uses PowerShell to download a loader and then runs a living-off-the-land binary. A signature-only tool may not flag it, but EDR telemetry shows the unusual parent-child chain, the network connection to an unknown IP, and a new executable in a nonstandard folder. Analysts can hunt for similar chains and isolate affected hosts.
