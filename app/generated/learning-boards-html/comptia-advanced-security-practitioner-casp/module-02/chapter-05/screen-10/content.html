Hierarchy

Policy Types and Containerization

Device Controls
Passcodes, encryption, OS updates, remote wipe.
App Controls
Whitelisting, blacklisting, app configuration, managed app data.
Network & Access
VPN, Wi‑Fi profiles, conditional access tied to device posture.
BYOD Example
Use a work profile or container on personal Android and iOS devices for corporate email and apps. When employment ends or the device is lost, perform a selective wipe of the container, leaving personal data untouched.
Key Takeaway

Design policies by device ownership and test them in pilots to avoid operational friction and privacy violations.
