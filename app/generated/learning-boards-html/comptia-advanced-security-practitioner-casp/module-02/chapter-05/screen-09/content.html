Definition

MDM vs UEM

MDM secures mobile devices; UEM unifies management across device types to simplify policy application and visibility.

Apple DEP: zero-touch enrollment for corporate-owned iOS devices.
Android Enterprise: profiles and work managed devices, supporting containerization.
Manual enrollment: user-initiated, used for BYOD where privacy concerns limit control.
Policy Impact
DEP and Android Enterprise allow stronger enforcement like required passcodes, device encryption, and remote wipe. Manual enrollment requires careful privacy controls and selective management of corporate containers only.
Concrete Example
A company uses DEP for corporate phones to enforce full device encryption and remote wipe, while BYOD users enroll a work profile to keep corporate apps and data separate from personal apps.
Common Mistake

Applying corporate-only controls to BYOD frustrates users and encourages bypass; test UX in pilot groups.
