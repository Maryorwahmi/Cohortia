Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Quick Checklist
Automate & Gate
Run SAST, dependency, and image scans in CI and fail builds for critical vulnerabilities.
Stage & Validate
Use DAST and IAST in staging to confirm exploitability and provide developer context.
Protect & Monitor
Deploy WAFs, API Gateways, and RASP and forward logs to SIEM for correlation and response.
Final Thought

Tools are powerful when paired with automation, tuning, and monitoring. Prioritize fixes, tune rules, and keep the pipeline secure.
