Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
Identity Federation
Authenticate once with a trusted IdP to access multiple SPs without re-authenticating.
Single Sign-On (SSO)
One authentication session grants access to multiple related systems while centralizing policy enforcement.
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Requires two or more verification factors to raise assurance and reduce credential theft risk.
Summary
Centralize authentication with federation and SSO, protect the central anchor with MFA, and validate mappings and metadata to maintain secure access across environments.
