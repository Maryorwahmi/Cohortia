PEP Role

The Policy Enforcement Point (PEP) acts as a gateway, authenticating users and devices and verifying their posture before granting access to specific applications.

Common Mistake

Overly broad ZTNA policies or insufficient device posture checks can undermine the "never trust" principle, creating new vulnerabilities.

Safety Note

Ensure high availability of the ZTNA gateway and robust backup authentication mechanisms to prevent service disruption, as it's a critical control point.
