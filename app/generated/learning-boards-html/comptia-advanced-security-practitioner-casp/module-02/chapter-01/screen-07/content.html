Deployment Choices

IPS Deployment: In-line vs. Out-of-band

Balancing Protection, Performance, and Availability

In-line Deployment

  • •Direct Traffic Path: Sits directly in the network flow.
  • •Active Blocking: Can immediately prevent detected threats.
  • •Risk: Single point of failure, potential latency.
Best For

High-priority segments where immediate threat prevention is paramount, and performance impact is acceptable after thorough testing.

Out-of-band Deployment

  • •Monitors via SPAN/Mirror Port: Receives a copy of traffic.
  • •Alerts Only: Cannot actively block traffic in real-time.
  • •Benefit: Less disruptive, no single point of failure.
Best For

Environments where network uptime is critical, and the ability to alert and manually intervene is sufficient for threat response.

Common Mistake

Deploying an IPS in-line without sufficient testing, leading to legitimate traffic being blocked or significant network performance degradation.
