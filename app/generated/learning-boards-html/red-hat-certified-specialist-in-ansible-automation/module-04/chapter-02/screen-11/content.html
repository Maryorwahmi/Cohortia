Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Common Mistakes
Mistake: Using shell instead of modules
Why it matters: loses idempotency and cross-distro behavior. Fix: prefer the appropriate ansible.builtin module.
Mistake: Missing owner/group/mode
Why it matters: services may not access files. Fix: declare owner, group, and mode in file, copy, or template tasks.
Mistake: Broken templates
Why it matters: rendering fails and deploys stop. Fix: test templates and run a dry render before applying widely.
Quick process to follow
  1. Pick a specific module over shell when possible.
  2. Declare filesystem ownership and modes clearly.
  3. Validate templates and variables before deployment.
  4. Run tasks twice to confirm idempotency.
