Readable when
Name complex logic, use parentheses, and guard undefined values.
Normalize loops
Use loop and dict2items so item access is predictable and testable.
Safe templating
Render on control node, use default/is defined, and avoid leaking secrets.
Closing takeaway

Small, testable patterns beat large, implicit logic: keep conditions clear, loops normalized, and templates guarded for reliable automation.
