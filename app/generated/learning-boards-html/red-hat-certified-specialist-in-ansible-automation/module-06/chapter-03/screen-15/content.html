Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Quick Reference
Subject vs Actor
inventory_hostname and ansible_host are the subject; ansible_delegated_host is the actor.
One-time tasks
run_once ensures a single execution per batch; combine with delegate_to for controlled execution location.
Controller caution
When using delegate_to: localhost, secure the controller and remove temporary artifacts after use.
Final takeaway

Delegation and run_once let you centralize actions without losing clarity about who is being managed and who performs the action.
