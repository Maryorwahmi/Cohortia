Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Troubleshooting
Symptoms and fixes
  • Connection errors when delegating — check inventory entries and SSH keys for the delegated host.
  • Wrong host recorded in external systems — validate whether you used inventory_hostname or ansible_delegated_host.
  • run_once running multiple times with serial — check batching and serial settings to understand the first host per batch.
  • Temporary files left on controller — ensure cleanup tasks with delegate_to: localhost and run_once are present and conditioned on creation.
Quick debugging checklist
1. Re-run with increased verbosity to see execution host. 2. Confirm registered values and where they were created. 3. Test delegated tasks in isolation against the delegated host.
Common mistake

Assuming delegate_to provides original-host facts. It does not; gather and pass facts explicitly when needed.
