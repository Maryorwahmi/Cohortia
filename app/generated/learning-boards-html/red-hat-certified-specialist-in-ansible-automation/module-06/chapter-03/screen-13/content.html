Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Best practices
Security guidance
Limit what runs on the control node. Clean temporary files and avoid storing long-lived secrets locally. Ensure the control node access is hardened if you rely on delegate_to: localhost for critical operations.
Design alternatives
Use a separate play targeting the management host for centralized steps or a two-play sequence: build in play one, deploy in play two. This often yields clearer auditing and fewer delegation surprises.
Document
Annotate delegated tasks so maintainers know why execution is shifted off the target.
Use facts intentionally
Gather and pass facts when a delegated task must operate on the subject host's data.
Combine carefully
If you combine run_once and delegate_to, ensure the intended host will be the executor to avoid unexpected results.
