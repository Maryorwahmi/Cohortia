Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Operational Habits
Protect password files
Use chmod 600 and keep files outside repositories or in secured secret stores.
Inject in CI
Use the pipeline secret facility to inject a temporary password file, then delete it after the run.
Avoid plaintext
Do not decrypt secrets on disk unless absolutely necessary; re-encrypt or delete plaintext immediately.
Final takeaway

Operational controls and proper password delivery make Vault practical for teams and CI/CD pipelines.
