Introduction to Ansible Roles

The foundation for modular, reusable automation

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Why this matters

When playbooks grow, locating logic, templates, and variables becomes slow and error-prone. Roles give a predictable place for each artifact and let you reuse and version components across projects. That lowers defects and speeds collaboration.

In this lesson

Learn role layout, how to init a role, and run a test playbook.

Outcome

Create a simple role and confirm it runs with ansible-playbook.
