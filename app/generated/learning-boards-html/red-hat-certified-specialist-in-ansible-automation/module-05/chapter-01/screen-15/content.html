What to remember

Ansible Role
A packaged unit containing tasks, handlers, variables, files, templates, and metadata for a single concern.
Directory Structure
Use tasks, handlers, defaults, vars, files, templates, and meta so roles are predictable and discoverable.
Modularity & Reuse
Roles promote composability, easier maintenance, and team collaboration by centralizing behavior.
Summary takeaway

Follow the role conventions and test roles early. Small, focused roles are easy to share, fix, and reuse across projects.
