Learning Objectives

Skill-focused outcomes you can verify

01
Explain challenges without roles

Describe why a single playbook grows unwieldy and identify two specific pain points in maintenance and reuse.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Define an Ansible Role

State the role as a self-contained unit of automation and relate it to a programming function or module with inputs and outputs.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify standard directories

Locate tasks, handlers, defaults, vars, files, templates, and meta in a role and explain the purpose and precedence of each.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Articulate role benefits

Explain how modularity, reusability, collaboration, and maintainability are improved by adopting roles with a short example.
