Practical checks to keep roles clean and reusable

Checklist
  • Does the role do one clear job? If not, split it.
  • Are handlers colocated with the tasks that notify them?
  • Are environment-overridable settings in defaults?
  • Are dynamic files in templates rather than files?
  • Are dependencies declared in meta/main.yml?
Quick fixes
Refactor repeated tasks into a role and replace copies with role calls.
Move sensitive or implementation-specific values into vars and document why they must not be overridden.
Add meta dependencies to avoid hidden assumptions when sharing the role.
Pro tip

Start roles small. It's easier to combine small, focused roles than to split a large, mixed one later.
