Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
Role Tasks
Orderly, idempotent steps in tasks/main.yml define the role's behavior.
Handlers
Defined in handlers/main.yml and run only when notified by a changed task.
Variables
defaults are low-precedence and overridable; vars are higher-precedence and for internals.
Takeaway
Organize role files intentionally and test behavior to ensure your role is flexible and safe to reuse.
