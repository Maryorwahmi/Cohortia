Role internals

Use vars/main.yml for variables that should remain consistent and are part of role implementation details rather than user-facing options.

When to prefer vars
  • Paths or names that the role's tasks rely on
  • Calculated values used by multiple internal tasks
Example: vars/main.yml
# my_webserver_role/vars/main.yml
---
# Internal variable, not meant to be easily overridden
nginx_config_path: "/etc/nginx/nginx.conf"
Contrast

defaults: user-friendly and overridable. vars: role-internal and higher precedence.
