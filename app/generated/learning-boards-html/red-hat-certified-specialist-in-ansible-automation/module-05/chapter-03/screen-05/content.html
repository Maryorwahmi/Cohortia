Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Declare Dependencies
Step-by-step
  1. Create meta/main.yml under your role's meta/ folder.
  2. Under dependencies:, list roles in the order they must run.
  3. Pass variables to dependent roles inline when needed.
  4. Test the role in a small playbook on a test host to verify order.
Pro tip
Keep dependencies minimal and document why a role depends on another to aid future maintainers.
Example YAML and notes
# role/meta/main.yml
---
dependencies:
  - role: common_os_config
    common_os_config_firewall_enabled: true
  - role: user_management
    user_management_users:
      - name: webadmin
        groups: sudo
        state: present
Note: Variables passed here apply when the dependent role runs. They do not magically merge into your role's tasks; follow variable precedence and testing practices.
