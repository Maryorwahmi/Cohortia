Learning Objectives
Skills you will demonstrate and why they matter
01
Pass Arguments to Roles
Send variables when invoking a role to configure behavior per environment. This lets one role serve many targets without editing its files.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Conditional Logic with when
Guard tasks, blocks, or role execution on facts or variables so playbooks adapt to each host's context and avoid unnecessary changes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
import_role vs include_role
Understand static parsing versus runtime inclusion to choose safer or more flexible patterns depending on needs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Blocks, pre_tasks, post_tasks
Group tasks, add rescue and always sections, and place setup or cleanup outside roles for predictable orchestration.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
