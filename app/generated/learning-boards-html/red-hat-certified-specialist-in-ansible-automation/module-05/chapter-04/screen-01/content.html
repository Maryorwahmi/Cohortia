Advanced Role Techniques
Make roles reusable across environments with arguments, conditionals, and controlled inclusion.

This chapter teaches patterns that let a single role adapt to many situations. You will pass role arguments to override defaults, guard tasks and roles with when conditions, and decide between import_role for static safety and include_role for runtime flexibility. Blocks and pre/post tasks give structured control over multi-step operations and cleanup.
