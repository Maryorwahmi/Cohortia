Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Final Reminders
Power sizing
Choose supplies that handle stall and start currents with headroom.
Common ground
Tie grounds between the Arduino and external supplies so signals have a reference.
Protection
Use flyback diodes, opto-isolators, and fuses appropriate to the load.
Closing takeaway

Good actuator design combines the right choice of device, proper switching hardware, and safe power wiring. These basics keep projects reliable and safe as complexity grows.
