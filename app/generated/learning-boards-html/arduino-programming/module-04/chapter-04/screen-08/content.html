What is a DC motor?
A device that converts DC electrical energy to continuous rotational motion; used for wheels and propellers.
  • Simple two-terminal device; speed increases with voltage and load affects current draw.
  • Stall conditions can draw large current spikes that damage microcontroller pins.
  • Older motor driver modules provide direction and current handling via H-bridges.
Practical example
Use a MOSFET as a low-side switch: Arduino PWM pin drives the MOSFET gate, the MOSFET switches the motor's ground side, and the external supply provides motor current. Add a flyback diode across the motor to protect the transistor from voltage spikes.
Key Takeaway
Always place a current-handling device between the Arduino and a DC motor to avoid damage and instability.
