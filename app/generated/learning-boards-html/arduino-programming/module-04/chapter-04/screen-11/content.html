What a relay does
It isolates and switches a separate high-voltage circuit using a low-voltage control signal through a coil-operated contact set.
- Opto-isolators improve safety by electrically separating the Arduino from the coil drive.
- Flyback diodes protect the driving transistor from inductive kick when the coil turns off.
Practical wiring
Control pin → module input, module Vcc and GND connected to Arduino or separate supply as module requires. Switched mains go to the relay's common and normally-open or normally-closed contacts, rated for the load.
Safety note
Avoid handling energized mains wiring. Always verify isolation and ratings, fuse the circuit appropriately, and consult training before working with mains voltages.
