Why it matters
Understanding actuators and power wiring is essential for turning sensor readings into reliable motion or switched power. It keeps your board safe and your project predictable under load.

Quick safety note
Never power motors or mains loads from the Arduino 5V pin. Always plan an external supply and common ground, and avoid working on mains wiring unless trained.
