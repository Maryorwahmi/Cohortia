Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Practice & Safety
Practice tasks
Short, verifiable demos that prove competency and surface wiring or power issues early.
Servo sweep verification
Load the sweep sketch and confirm the servo arrives at 0°, 90°, and 180° without jitter.
Motor driver stress check
Run full PWM duty cycle range and confirm the Arduino remains powered and the driver keeps temperature in range.
Relay isolation test
Switch a known low-voltage lamp first and verify the relay coil is driven by the module and the Arduino is isolated.
Power supply check
Measure voltage under motor load and verify ground continuity between Arduino and external supply.
Safety checklist
Simple rules that prevent damage and reduce risk when experimenting with motors and relays.
  • Never power a motor directly from an Arduino pin; use a driver or transistor and separate supply.
  • Always tie grounds together when using an external supply so control signals share a reference.
  • Use relay modules with opto-isolation and flyback diodes for coil protection and safer switching.
  • Avoid working on mains wiring unless trained; test with low-voltage loads first.
Instructor note
If a demo causes Arduino resets under load, immediately disconnect the motor and re-evaluate the supply current capability and wiring.
