Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Hands-on Activity
Step-by-step
  1. Wire servo Vcc to 5V external supply, tie external ground to Arduino GND, and connect signal to pin 9.
  2. Upload the sweep sketch and confirm the servo reaches 0°, 90°, and 180° reliably.
  3. Add a potentiometer: connect 5V, GND, and center to an analog pin.
  4. Read analog, map the range to 0–180, and call servo.write with the mapped value.
  5. Observe and debug: check grounds and supply if motion is erratic.
Example mapping
Read analog value 0–1023, then map to 0–180. Use smoothing or small delays to avoid jitter when the potentiometer is noisy.
Key Takeaway
This lab ties together wiring, power, sensor input, and position control — and highlights how a shared ground and adequate supply are essential for predictable behavior.
