Learning Objectives
Learn, understand, apply, and verify practical actuator skills for Arduino projects.
01
Control a servo with the Servo library
Attach a servo, send target angles, and observe accurate positioning for robotics or mechanisms.
Mnemonic: angle dial — think 0 to 180 degrees.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Drive a DC motor with a transistor or motor driver
Use a transistor or L298N/L293D module to control speed and direction without harming the Arduino.
Mnemonic: H-bridge or switchboard controls direction.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use relays to switch high-voltage devices
Control a lamp or fan using a relay module with opto-isolation and flyback protection for safety.
Mnemonic: low-voltage finger presses a safe switch for high-voltage loads.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Understand power and grounding for actuators
Choose external supplies, tie grounds, and avoid powering motors from the Arduino 5V pin to prevent resets and damage.
Mnemonic: common ground is the conversation ground between supplies.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Verification plan
For each objective, build a small demo: a servo sweep, a PWM-driven motor on a driver, a relay toggling a lamp with isolation, and an external supply powering the motor with shared ground.
Assessment idea
Explain why a DC motor should never be connected directly to an Arduino pin and name the correct component to protect the board.
Instructor tip
When verifying, measure supply voltage and confirm the motor driver or relay coil current is within the selected supply capability before connecting components to the Arduino.
