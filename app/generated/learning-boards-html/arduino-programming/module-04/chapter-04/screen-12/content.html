Sizing supplies
Select a supply that can provide the motor's continuous current and tolerate short stall peaks; add margin to avoid voltage collapse.
If a motor stalls, current spikes that are much larger than running current can appear. A well-sized supply and soft-start techniques reduce the chance of resets and component stress.
Grounding and decoupling
Always connect the external supply ground to the Arduino ground so control signals have a reference. Place electrolytic capacitors near motor supply terminals to smooth spikes.
Key Takeaway
Shared ground and adequate supply capacity keep the Arduino stable and the actuators reliable under load.
Quick checklist
  • Check motor stall current.
  • Choose supply with margin.
  • Tie grounds and add decoupling caps.
Common error
Powering motors from the Arduino 5V pin leads to resets — use a dedicated motor supply.
Real-world connection
Robots, automated doors, and motorized lids all demand careful power planning to avoid failure in the field.
