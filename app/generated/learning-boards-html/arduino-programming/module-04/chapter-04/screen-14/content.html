Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Servo motor
Closed-loop angle control; use for joints and precise position tasks.
DC motor
Continuous rotation; always use a driver or transistor and an external supply sized for current.
Relay modules
Use modules with opto-isolation and flyback protection; test with low-voltage loads first.
Summary takeaway

Match actuator type to task, provide proper switching hardware for current and direction, and plan power and ground so your Arduino remains stable and safe.
