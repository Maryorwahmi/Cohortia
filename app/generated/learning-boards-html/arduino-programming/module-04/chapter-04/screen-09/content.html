Low-side MOSFET switch
Use N-channel MOSFET for switching motor ground with Arduino PWM driving the gate.
Key elements: logic-level MOSFET, gate resistor, flyback diode, and a motor supply sized for current draw. Test at low duty first and measure temperature under sustained load.
H-bridge driver modules
Modules like the L298N and L293D provide two-direction control and separate motor supply terminals with built-in protections on some boards.
Use direction pins to select forward or reverse and a PWM input to control speed. Driver modules simplify wiring and reduce the chance of wiring mistakes when reversing motors frequently.
Checklist
  • Choose device rated above stall current.
  • Include flyback diode if discrete switch is used.
  • Use heat sinking if the driver dissipates power.
Misconception
A single transistor cannot reverse motor polarity. For reversing, you need two transistors in an H-bridge arrangement or a driver module.
Key Takeaway
Select switching hardware that safely handles the motor's maximum current and supports the control you require, whether single-direction switching or reversible drive.
