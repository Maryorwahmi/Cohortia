Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Servo Wiring
Wiring architecture
External supply, shared ground, and signal reference form the reliable wiring pattern for servos.
Power sources
Use the Arduino 5V only for light loads. For one or more standard servos, prefer a dedicated 5V supply rated for the combined stall current.
Grounding
Tie external supply ground to Arduino ground. Without a common ground the signal has no reference and the servo will behave erratically.
Noise mitigation
Add a decoupling capacitor across the servo supply to smooth spikes. Keep signal wires away from high-current motor leads.
Annotated wiring card
Follow this pattern: External 5V supply to servo Vcc, external ground tied to Arduino GND, Arduino digital PWM pin to servo signal. Confirm voltages before connecting the servo.
Checklist before power-up
  • Confirm supply voltage matches servo rating.
  • Confirm grounds are tied between supplies and Arduino.
  • Verify signal pin is PWM-capable and not driving heavy loads.
Key Takeaway
Shared ground and adequate supply current are non-negotiable; they are the difference between a working servo and an unpredictable one.
