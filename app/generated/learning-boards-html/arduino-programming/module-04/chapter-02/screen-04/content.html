Definition
Ultrasonic distance by echo timing
The sensor emits pulses and raises the echo pin for the time it takes sound to travel to an object and return. Timing is the measurement; math converts it to centimeters.
Key points
  • Send a short trigger pulse to start emission.
  • Measure echo pin HIGH duration in microseconds.
  • Distance = duration × 0.034 ÷ 2 (centimeters).
Common misconception
If you forget to divide by two you report double the distance. Weak echoes from soft materials can produce noisy or missing readings.

Example
An echo duration of 1000 microseconds becomes 1000 × 0.034 ÷ 2 ≈ 17 cm. Repeat measurements and average for stability.
Key Takeaway
Timing is the measurement. Convert microseconds into distance with the speed of sound and the round-trip correction.
