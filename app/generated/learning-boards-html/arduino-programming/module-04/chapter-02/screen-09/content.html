Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Ultrasonic vs PIR
Comparison
Ultrasonic
Provides distance in centimeters; relies on echo strength; sensitive to surface material and angle.
PIR
Indicates motion presence via infrared changes; configurable hold time and sensitivity; not a distance sensor.
Common mistake
Expecting PIR to replace an ultrasonic distance measurement. Use PIR for presence gating, not for measuring centimeters.
Concrete scenario
Parking sensor: PIR detects a person or approach, enabling the ultrasonic sensor to run distance checks only while someone is present, reducing false alarms and activity when no one is nearby.
Key Takeaway
Choose sensors by the information they provide; combine them to complement strengths and mitigate limitations.
