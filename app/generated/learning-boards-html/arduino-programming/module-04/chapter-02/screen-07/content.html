Why 0.034?
The chapter provides 0.034 as the speed of sound in centimeters per microsecond. Multiply duration by this factor, then divide by two to correct for the round trip.
Quick example
Echo duration 1000 microseconds → 1000 × 0.034 ÷ 2 ≈ 17 cm.
Limitations
The sensor can measure roughly from 2 cm to 400 cm. Soft or angled surfaces weaken echoes, and readings degrade at extremes.
Practical filtering
  • Take several readings and compute an average.
  • Discard zero or clearly out-of-range values before averaging.
  • Use brief pauses between triggers to avoid overlapping echoes.
Key Takeaway
Use the provided factor and the round-trip correction, and apply simple filtering to improve measurement reliability.
