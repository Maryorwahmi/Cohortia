Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Filter & validate
Average valid samples, discard zeros/outliers, and pause between triggers to avoid overlapping echoes.
Know limitations
Expect less accuracy at very short and very long ranges; surface material and angle affect echoes.
Combine thoughtfully
Use PIR to gate ultrasonic reads and design mappings that yield clear, actionable feedback for users.
Final takeaway
Reliable sensor projects pair correct wiring and timing with filtering and thoughtful integration to produce useful, predictable behavior in real environments.
