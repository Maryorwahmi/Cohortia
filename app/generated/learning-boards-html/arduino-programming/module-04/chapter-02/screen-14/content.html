HC-SR04 & pulseIn()
Use a precise trigger pulse and measure echo duration with a timing function to obtain distance.
Speed of sound formula
Distance = duration × 0.034 ÷ 2. The divide-by-two corrects for the round-trip path of the sound.
PIR principles
PIR reports motion via infrared change. Use sensitivity and hold-time adjustments to tune behavior.
Summary takeaway
Combine timing-based distance with PIR presence signals to build reliable and efficient motion-aware projects.
