How PIR works
PIR senses changes in infrared radiation. When the pattern changes, the module's output pin goes HIGH for a configurable period.
Wiring
VCC → 5V, GND → GND, OUT → digital input. Read the output with a simple digitalRead to detect motion.
Module adjustments
Two potentiometers adjust sensitivity and the duration the output stays HIGH. Use these to tune behavior for your environment.
Concrete example
Connect the PIR output to a digital pin and an LED to show detection. When the PIR reports HIGH, light the LED; when it reads LOW, turn the LED off. The module holds the signal HIGH for the configured time.
Key Takeaway
PIR provides a single, easy-to-read digital signal when motion occurs. Tune sensitivity and time to match your use case.
