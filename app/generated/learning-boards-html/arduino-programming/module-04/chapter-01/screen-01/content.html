Environmental Sensors

Understand how TMP36, LM35, DHT11/DHT22, LDRs, and MQ-2 produce signals and how to convert those signals into meaningful readings.

Signal Types
Analog sensors give a voltage; digital sensors send timed data. Choose the correct read method.
Conversions
Use datasheet formulas to map voltage to degrees or percentages. Libraries simplify this for digital parts.
Wiring
Many sensors need power, ground, and a signal pin. LDRs require a voltage divider to produce a readable voltage.
Practical Use
You’ll build a DHT11 reader and an LED threshold that turns on when temperature rises above a set point.
