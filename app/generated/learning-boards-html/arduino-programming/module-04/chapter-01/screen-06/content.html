Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › TMP36 vs LM35
TMP36
Scale: 10 mV per °C with a 500 mV offset. Datasheet examples show 750 mV at 25°C.
Conversion
temperatureC = (voltage - 0.5) × 100
When to use
Good for single-supply designs; remember to apply the offset in software.
LM35
Scale: 10 mV per °C with no offset. Output near 0 V at 0°C according to its datasheet.
Conversion
temperatureC = voltage × 100
Common mistake
Applying TMP36 offset to LM35 gives readings that are too low by about 0.5 V equivalent in degrees.
