Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › TMP36
Code Example
int reading = analogRead(A0);
float voltage = (reading / 1023.0) * 5.0;
float temperatureC = (voltage - 0.5) * 100.0;
Step 1 — ADC to voltage
Divide the ADC reading by 1023 and scale by Vref (5.0 V) to get voltage in volts.
Step 2 — Apply TMP36 formula
Subtract 0.5 V offset, then multiply by 100 to convert volts to degrees Celsius.
Explanation & Example
Given a measured voltage of 0.73 volts from the TMP36, subtract the 0.5 volt offset to get 0.23, then multiply by 100 to get 23°C. Forgetting the 0.5 offset creates a consistent error in the entire reading range.
Takeaway
Use the datasheet’s scale and offset. Check your math with a known temperature point to validate wiring and reference voltage.
