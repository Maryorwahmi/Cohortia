Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Formatting Output
Why formatting matters

Small screens must be concise. Limiting decimal places and reserving space for numbers keeps readouts tidy and predictable.

Use fixed precision for floats so "23.123456" becomes "23.1" or "23.12" depending on readability needs.
Build strings with padding or use dtostrf to ensure fixed-width number fields.
Refresh only on meaningful change or at a moderate interval to avoid flicker and bus congestion.
Concrete Example
If a humidity sensor returns 46.789, format to one decimal as "46.8%" and update only if value changed by 0.1 or after two seconds. Pad to three characters for alignment: " 6.8" vs "46.8".
Gotcha
Printing floats without formatting often leads to long, unreadable numbers and display clutter.
