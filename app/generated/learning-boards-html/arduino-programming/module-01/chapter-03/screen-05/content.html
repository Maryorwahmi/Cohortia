Installer steps
Follow the installer for your OS and accept system prompts, especially driver installations on Windows.
  1. Download the IDE installer for your system.
  2. Run the installer and accept required permissions.
  3. On Windows, accept the USB-to-serial driver if prompted.
  4. After install, plug in the board and confirm a serial port appears.
Misconception: macOS always requires drivers. Often it does not; confirm before installing a third-party driver.
Quick verification
  • Plug the Uno and watch for the green power LED.
  • Open your OS device list to identify a new COM or /dev entry.
  • If unsure, disconnect and reconnect to observe which device appears.
Example: On Windows the board often appears as COM3 or similar in Device Manager.
Takeaway: The installer and driver step are the bridge between the OS and your Arduino hardware.
