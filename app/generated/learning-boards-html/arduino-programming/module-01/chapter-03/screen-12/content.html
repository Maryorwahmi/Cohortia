Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Bootloader & avrdude
What is the bootloader?
A small program on the microcontroller that accepts uploads and writes them to flash.
  • Runs briefly to accept uploads, then hands off execution to your sketch.
  • Makes USB upload via avrdude possible without extra hardware.
  • Most Arduino Unos include it by default.
What is avrdude?
A host tool the IDE uses to transfer the compiled binary to the bootloader over serial.
  • Handles low-level serial protocols for writing flash memory.
  • Reports transfer and connection errors when uploads fail.
Troubleshoot: If avrdude reports connection errors, re-check port selection and that no other app holds the serial device.
