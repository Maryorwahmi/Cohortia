Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Troubleshooting Uploads
Fast checklist
A short sequence to isolate upload failures.
1. Confirm board and port selection.
2. Use a data-capable USB cable and verify the power LED.
3. Resolve compile errors shown during verify.
4. Close other apps that may hold the serial port.
5. On Windows, re-install drivers if the port never appears.
Concrete scenarios
Scenario A: Board powers but no port. Try a different USB cable or reinstall drivers.
Scenario B: avrdude reports timeout. Close other terminal apps and retry upload.
Scenario C: Compilation errors. Fix code and libraries, then verify again before upload.
