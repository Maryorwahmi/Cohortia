Quick mnemonic

Say the steps out loud before you start a session. It quickly surfaces a missing step and saves debug time.

Common verification
  • Confirm green power LED on board after connecting USB.
  • If port is unclear, unplug and replug to see which device appears.
  • Choose Arduino Uno from Boards to match the ATmega328P core.
  • Verify before upload to catch syntax or missing library errors.
