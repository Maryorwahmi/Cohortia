Learning Objectives

Four measurable skills to guide your practice

Define Arduino
Explain the platform as open-source hardware plus software and why that lowers barriers to making. Practice by naming the parts a beginner needs.

Microcontroller vs Microprocessor

Map education, prototyping, interactive art, and IoT examples to inputs and outputs you would use for each.

Identify ecosystem benefits
