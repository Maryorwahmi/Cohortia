Arduino Uno at a glance

Key parts you will use when wiring and programming.
ATmega328P
The microcontroller that runs your program.
Digital pins (14)
Used for on/off signals and simple control.
Analog inputs (6)
Measure sensor voltages with analog-to-digital conversion.
USB & power jack
Program and power via USB, or use external supply with regulator.
Concrete wiring example

Blink an LED: connect LED and resistor to a digital pin and ground, upload a program that alternates the pin state, and verify the LED blinks at the expected rate.

Common mistake
Using the wrong external voltage can damage the board; always use the recommended 7–12V for the jack or 5V regulated supply to the 5V pin.
Key Takeaway

Identify pins and power options before wiring. Start with USB power during learning to reduce risk.
