Open-source platform
Hardware and software designs are shared, enabling compatibility and community contributions.
Microcontroller
A compact chip built for repeated sensing and control, not general-purpose multitasking.
Applications
Education, prototyping, art, and IoT all benefit from accessible hardware and shared code.
Summary

Keep the ecosystem and safe wiring practices in mind as you plan your first project; start small and iterate.
