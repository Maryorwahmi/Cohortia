Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Powering Arduino

Power options

  • USB power: convenient for development and safe current limits.
  • Barrel jack: accepts recommended 7–12V to feed the regulator.
  • 5V pin: supplies regulated 5V, avoid feeding higher voltages here.
Concrete scenario
Drive a motor with a separate power supply and motor driver while sharing ground with the Arduino to complete the circuit safely.
Common mistake
Applying unregulated voltage to the 5V pin or using incorrect polarity will likely damage the board; check labels and specs before connecting power.
Battery considerations
Choose battery chemistry and capacity based on current draw and expected runtime. Account for regulator efficiency losses on the barrel jack.
Key Takeaway

Plan power early, use proper supplies for motors and high-current devices, and confirm voltages before connecting to the board.
