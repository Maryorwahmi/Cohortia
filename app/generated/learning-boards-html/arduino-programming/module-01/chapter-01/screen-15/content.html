Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
Power first
Confirm voltages and power high‑current devices separately to avoid board damage.
Test incrementally
Verify sensors and outputs one at a time to locate issues early.
Use libraries wisely
Leverage community code to speed work, but inspect usage and limits to avoid surprises.
Final takeaway

Start small, plan wiring and power, and use community resources to iterate quickly and safely.
