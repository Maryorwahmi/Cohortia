What is Arduino and Why Use It?

A short orientation: learn the hardware basics, how a microcontroller differs from your laptop processor, and practical reasons to choose Arduino for learning and prototyping.

In this lesson
Define Arduino and microcontroller, see Uno features, and explore real uses.
Why it matters
Accelerates prototyping so ideas become working devices fast.
Coming up
Hands-on activity: map sensors and actuators for three projects.
Concept sketch
Board + sensors + code
Arduino Uno
ATmega328P microcontroller, 14 digital pins, 6 analog pins, USB power.
Microcontroller
A compact, single-purpose computer optimized for sensing and control.
Ecosystem
Free IDE, libraries, and a large community that shares projects.
Key Takeaway

Arduino packages microcontroller hardware and friendly software so creators focus on ideas instead of low-level setup.

Origin story
Started in 2005 to help students and artists prototype quickly.
Core components
Board, microcontroller, IDE, libraries, and community projects.
Starter project ideas
Weather station, smart garden, simple robot examples show inputs and outputs.
