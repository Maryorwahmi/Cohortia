Basic Electronics for Arduino

Groundwork for safe breadboard wiring, Ohm's Law, LED polarity, and current-limiting resistors.

In this lesson
Concepts, wiring, and hands-on LED build
Voltage
Like water pressure — the force that pushes electrons.
Current
Like flow rate — how much charge moves per second.
Resistance
Like a narrow pipe — limits how much current can flow.
Quick wiring preview
You'll place an LED and series resistor on a breadboard, connect one end to Arduino pin 13 and the other to ground, and upload the Blink sketch to observe the LED blink. We'll show how resistor value changes brightness.
Safety highlight
Always disconnect power when rearranging wiring, use a resistor with LEDs, and avoid shorts between 5V and ground.
Visual concept
Voltage is the push; current is the flow between the battery and load; resistance reduces the flow.
