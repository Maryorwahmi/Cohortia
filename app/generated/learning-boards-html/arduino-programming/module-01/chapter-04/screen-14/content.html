Ohm's Law
V = I × R and its rearrangements let you compute current or resistance for safe designs.
LED Polarity
Long leg is anode. Always use a series resistor to limit current and protect components.
Breadboard Layout
Edge rails and groups of five in the center are the key patterns to recognize before wiring.
Takeaway

Use calculation, correct placement, and simple safety checks to prototype reliably and protect your Arduino and parts.
