Learning Objectives

Concrete skills tied to breadboard wiring and simple calculations.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Relate V, I, and R
Explain Ohm's Law and why voltage, current, and resistance determine circuit behavior. This lets you predict currents before connecting parts.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify components
Recognize resistors, LEDs, jumper wires, and breadboard features so you assemble correct circuits without confusion.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Build an LED circuit
Wire an LED with a series resistor from Arduino pin 13 to ground and confirm it blinks with the Blink sketch.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Follow safety rules
Disconnect power before changing wiring, avoid shorts, and never omit a resistor for an LED to protect the Arduino pin.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
