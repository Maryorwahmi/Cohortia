Hands-on learning targets

Move from calculation to wiring with clear checks and habits.

Computation
Use V = I × R and its rearrangements, I = V / R and R = V / I, to size resistors and estimate currents before wiring.
Breadboard practice
Verify which rows are connected and keep power rails organized with red and blue wiring conventions to avoid confusion.
Component handling
Identify LED anode and cathode by leg length and use resistors in series to limit current. Resistors are non-polarized so orientation doesn't matter.
Verification habit
Always disconnect power, double-check wiring visually, and only then reapply power to test your build.
