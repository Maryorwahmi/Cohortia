Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Lab: Blink
Procedure
  1. Power off. Place LED and 220Ω resistor in series between pin 13 and ground.
  2. Connect breadboard ground to Arduino GND, then power and upload Blink.
  3. Observe blink and perceived brightness; power down and swap to 1kΩ.
  4. Power up and observe brightness change; record observations.
Why this matters
The experiment links calculation to sensory evidence: increasing resistance lowers current and reduces brightness, confirming Ohm's Law practically.
Reflection
Which resistor produced the safest brightness while still visible? How did the measured behavior match your calculated current?
