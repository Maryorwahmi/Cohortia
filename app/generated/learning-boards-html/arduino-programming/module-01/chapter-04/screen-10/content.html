Checklist
  • Turn off power before changing wiring.
  • Place LED and resistor in series between pin 13 and ground.
  • Connect breadboard ground to Arduino GND.
  • Upload Blink sketch and observe the LED blink.
Example observation
With a 220-ohm resistor the LED will be bright but safe. Replacing it with 1kΩ will dim the LED, demonstrating how resistor value controls current and brightness.
