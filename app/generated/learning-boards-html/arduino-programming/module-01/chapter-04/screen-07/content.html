Definition

LED polarity and forward voltage

An LED only lights when current flows from anode to cathode. Forward voltage determines the voltage dropped by the LED when conducting.

Identify polarity
Long leg = anode (+), short leg = cathode (−). If uncertain, check datasheet or LED marking.
Forward voltage
Subtract forward voltage from supply to find the voltage the resistor must drop.
Scenario
If you connect a red LED backwards it simply won't light. If you omit the resistor, the LED and the Arduino pin may be exposed to excessive current. Always include a resistor in series and check LED orientation before powering the circuit.
Pro tip
If unsure which leg is which, bend the legs slightly and note the longer one for the anode; practice makes the identification quick.
