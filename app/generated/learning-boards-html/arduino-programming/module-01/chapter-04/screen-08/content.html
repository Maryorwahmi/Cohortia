How the breadboard is wired

Recognize the power rails, the grouped five-hole rows, and the center groove so you can place parts and route connections without accidental shorts.

Power rails
Long columns along the edges commonly serve as +5V and ground; all holes in a rail are connected.
Middle rows
Rows of five holes are connected. Use them for single components and to chain connections with jumper wires.
Common error
Mistaking rows across the central groove as connected leads to wiring failures. The groove intentionally isolates the halves so ICs can straddle it.
Practical check
Before powering, trace one hole in a rail and one hole in a middle row and confirm they are part of the same group to build confidence in your layout.
