Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Component placement
Step-by-step
  1. Insert LED so anode and cathode occupy separate rows of five.
  2. Place resistor so one lead connects to the LED cathode row and the other to the ground rail.
  3. Use a jumper from Arduino pin 13 to the LED anode row.
  4. Use a ground jumper to connect the breadboard ground rail to Arduino GND.
  5. Verify no direct connection exists between 5V and GND rails before powering.
Example layout
LED anode → jumper to pin 13. LED cathode → resistor → ground rail → Arduino GND. This ensures current flows through resistor then LED to ground.
Troubleshoot
If the LED doesn't light, check polarity, ensure the resistor is in series, and confirm the ground rail is connected to Arduino GND.
