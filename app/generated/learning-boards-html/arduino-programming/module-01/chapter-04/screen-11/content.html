Common mistakes
Short circuits
Directly connecting 5V to ground creates a short. Always check for accidental bridging before powering.
No resistor on LED
Omitting a resistor exposes the LED and Arduino pin to excessive current.
Incorrect breadboard rows
Assuming rows across the groove are connected leads to invisible wiring mistakes.
Safety checklist
Disconnect power before changes, use resistors with LEDs, connect breadboard ground to Arduino GND, and perform a visual rail and row check before powering.
If in doubt
Choose a larger resistor to reduce current or double-check wiring; a slightly dim LED is safer than a burned component.
