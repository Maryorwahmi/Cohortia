Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Power pins
5V and 3.3V rails exist; choose the correct rail and avoid powering motors from the board.
Analog readings
A0–A5 map 0–5V to 0–1023 via a 10-bit ADC; convert readings to volts when needed.
Pinout habit
Always check a pinout diagram before wiring to avoid swapped pins or wrong voltage connections.
Final takeaway
Put safety first: match voltages, respect current limits, and label pins so your projects are reliable and your Uno stays healthy.
