Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Current limits
Per-pin currents and recommendations
Use 20 milliamps as a safe working limit; forty milliamps is the absolute max.
LEDs: include a resistor and aim for ≤20 mA from a pin.
Motors: use driver circuits and an external power supply rather than the 5V pin.
Modules: check their current draw; many small sensors are fine on 5V but verify specification before connecting.
Practical wiring checklist
  1. Estimate the current each component draws.
  2. If any device draws more than 20 mA, use a driver or separate power supply.
  3. Never draw motor starting currents directly from an I/O pin or the onboard 5V rail.
Common mistake

Attempting to power motors from the Uno's 5V pin can overload the board and cause voltage drops or damage. Always separate control and power when current is high.
