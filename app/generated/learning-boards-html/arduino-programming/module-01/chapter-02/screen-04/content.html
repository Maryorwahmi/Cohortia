ATmega328P: definition and role
The microcontroller stores and runs your program and controls I/O at a five-volt domain.
Memory summary
32 KB flash for programs, 2 KB SRAM for variables, 1 KB EEPROM for persistent data.
Clock & Voltage
Runs at 16 MHz and operates at 5V, so digital HIGH equals five volts.
Practical example
A blink sketch is stored in flash; counters run in SRAM, and settings can be saved to EEPROM when needed.
Key Takeaway

The ATmega328P defines the Uno's capabilities: memory, clock speed, and operating voltage determine both code behavior and wiring constraints.

Annotated notes
  • Flash stores your sketch until you overwrite it.
  • SRAM holds variables while the sketch runs; limited space means be mindful of large arrays.
  • EEPROM persists small data between power cycles.
Common misconception
Believing the microcontroller can safely power high-current devices. In reality, per-pin current is limited and motors need external drivers and power.
