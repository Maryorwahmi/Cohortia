Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Reading a pinout
Step-by-step labeling
Follow a systematic routine to avoid common wiring mistakes.
1. Find the ATmega328P and use it as a reference.
2. Mark digital pins 0–13 and highlight pins 0 and 1 as serial.
3. Mark analog A0–A5 and note ADC range 0–1023.
4. Identify PWM pins (~3,5,6,9,10,11), power rails (5V, 3.3V), and ground pins.
Verification and common errors
  • Confirm onboard LED is wired to pin 13 to use it for tests.
  • Double-check voltage expectations: 3.3V parts must not be connected to 5V pins.
  • Ensure high-current loads have separate power and drivers.
Key Takeaway

A consistent pinout-check routine prevents common mistakes and protects both the board and attached components.
