Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
ATmega328P
The microcontroller: flash, SRAM, EEPROM and the 5V operating domain.
Digital pins 0–13
Binary I/O. Be cautious with pins 0 and 1 used for serial.
PWM pins (~)
Pins 3,5,6,9,10,11 produce PWM — use for dimming and analog-like outputs.
Summary
Identify the controller, map pins by purpose, and check current and voltage before wiring to keep projects safe and predictable.
