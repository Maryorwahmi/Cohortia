Hands-on activity
Label a printed Uno pinout, identify PWM and analog pins, and mark two ground pins.
Goal: label PWM pins (~3,5,6,9,10,11)
Goal: find analog A0–A5 and note ADC 0–1023
Goal: locate ATmega328P and USB power
Goal: identify onboard LED on pin 13 and reset button
Assessment checks
  • Which pins support PWM?
  • What is the maximum safe current per pin?
  • What voltage represents a HIGH signal on the Uno?
Quick reference
A short checklist to keep at your bench when wiring the Uno.
Power
5V and 3.3V rails exist; avoid powering motors from the 5V pin.
Pins
Digital 0–13, Analog A0–A5, PWM marked with a tilde (~) on the board.
