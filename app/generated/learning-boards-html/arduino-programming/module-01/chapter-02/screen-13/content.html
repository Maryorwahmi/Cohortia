Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Hands-on activity
Label & test steps
  1. Open or print a Uno pinout diagram and locate the ATmega328P as the reference.
  2. Label digital pins 0–13 and mark PWM pins (~3,5,6,9,10,11).
  3. Label analog pins A0–A5 and note ADC range 0–1023.
  4. Mark 5V, 3.3V, and at least two GND pins.
  5. Upload a blink sketch for the onboard LED on pin 13 to confirm programming.
  6. Attach an LED with a resistor to a PWM pin and vary duty cycle to observe dimming.
Safety prompts
  • Verify 3.3V vs 5V requirements before connecting modules.
  • Use resistors for LEDs and keep per-pin current near or below 20 mA.
  • Use a driver and separate power for motors.
Key Takeaway

This short activity reinforces correct pin identification, safe wiring practice, and quick verification using the onboard LED and a PWM-controlled LED.
