Power rails and connectors
5V and 3.3V rails, multiple GND pins, USB-B, and barrel jack provide power options.
5V pin: useful for small components but not high-current devices like motors.
3.3V pin: for modules that require lower voltage; do not substitute 5V for 3.3V modules.
USB-B connector: convenient for programming and low-current powering.
Barrel jack: external power via regulator for higher current or voltage sources.
Onboard features
  • Reset button to restart the program immediately.
  • ICSP headers for advanced programming of the microcontroller.
  • Power LED to show board is powered and onboard LED wired to pin 13 for testing.
Key Takeaway

Choose the right power source and rail for the current your devices require and never assume a rail can drive motors without a separate driver and power supply.
