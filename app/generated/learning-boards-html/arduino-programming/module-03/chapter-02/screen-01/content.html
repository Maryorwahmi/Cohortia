Digital Input: Reading Buttons and Switches
How to read switches reliably with pull resistors and debouncing

This lesson explains pin configuration, floating inputs, pull resistors, the Arduino internal pull-up, and software debouncing. Practical wiring and a simple millis-based debounce are included.

In this lesson: reliable input wiring, internal pull-up use, and debounce implementation.
