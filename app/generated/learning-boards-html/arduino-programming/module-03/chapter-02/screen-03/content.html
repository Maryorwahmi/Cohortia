Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Practice & Assessment
Practice tasks
  • Wire a button to pin 2 and use digitalRead() to log HIGH/LOW.
  • Build both pull-down and pull-up wiring on a breadboard to compare default states.
  • Enable internal pull-up and adapt your wiring so button connects to ground when pressed.
  • Implement debounce using millis() and toggle the onboard LED on each confirmed press.
Assessment ideas
Explain why a floating pin reads unpredictably and how a pull resistor fixes it.
Describe the difference in wiring between a pull-down and the Arduino internal pull-up.
Show a sketch that uses millis() to debounce and explain why delay() would be problematic for responsive code.
Tip
When you enable the internal pull-up, the switch ties the pin to ground when pressed. That inverts your logic: pressed becomes LOW.
