Analog Input: Reading Sensors with analogRead()

Turn continuous voltages into usable numbers, convert those numbers into volts or sensor units, and calibrate for accuracy.

In this lesson you will: read analog values, convert to volts, calibrate sensors, and map ranges for practical use.
Orientation

The Arduino does not measure analog in human terms by default. It measures voltage steps. We will use the built-in ADC reading, a simple conversion formula, and sensor conversion factors so your program understands physical quantities like volts or degrees.

Ruler Analogy
10-bit ruler: 0–1023 across 5 volts
Quick formula
float voltage = (analogRead(A0) / 1023.0) * 5.0;
Use this conversion to translate raw readings into voltage before applying sensor-specific scaling.
