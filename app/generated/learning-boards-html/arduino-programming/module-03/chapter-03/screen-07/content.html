Definition

Potentiometer as a voltage divider

Wire the outer pins to 5V and ground, and the middle pin to an analog input. The middle pin yields a variable voltage proportional to knob position.

5V
Wiper (A0)
GND
Checklist
  • Outer pins to 5V and ground, middle to A0.
  • Share common ground with the Arduino board.
  • Expect values near 0 and 1023 at the ends of travel.
Concrete example

Use a 10k potentiometer: middle pin to A0, one outer to 5V, other outer to ground. Turn the knob and observe the Serial Monitor values sweep from near 0 to near 1023. Use the same reading to control an LED blink rate or brightness in code.

Common mistake

Wiring the wiper to power or ground instead of the analog pin will not produce a usable reading. Always confirm the wiper middle pin is connected to the analog input.

Key Takeaway

A potentiometer gives a reliable, easy-to-read voltage when wired as a divider. Validate with serial prints and use it to control outputs directly.
