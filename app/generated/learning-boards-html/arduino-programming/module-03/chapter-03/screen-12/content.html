Short, actionable cards to keep top of mind while building and debugging.

ADC: 0–1023
Treat analogRead as a discrete ruler across Vref rather than a direct physical unit.
Convert to volts
Use voltage = (raw / 1023.0) * Vref, then apply sensor-specific scaling factors.
Calibrate & map
Record reference readings and map ranges carefully; verify ordering to avoid reversed outputs.
Summary takeaway

Use the ADC ruler, convert to volts, apply sensor math, and calibrate. Print values at each step to find and fix problems quickly.
