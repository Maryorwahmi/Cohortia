Two-point calibration and mapping

Capture a low and high reference, then rescale raw readings into a useful range with map or manual scaling.

Step 1
Measure darkValue and brightValue from the sensor.
Step 2
Use map(raw, darkValue, brightValue, 0, 100) to compute percent.
Step 3
Verify ordering: darkValue must be less than brightValue to avoid inversion.
Common pitfall

Reversed reference values produce reversed output. Check your captured references before using map.

Concrete example

If darkValue = 120 and brightValue = 900, mapping a raw value of 510 yields roughly middle percent. Use float math for smoother scaling when needed and record calibration values for reproducibility.

Key Takeaway

Calibration turns raw counts into meaningful ranges. Capture references carefully and double-check ordering before mapping.
