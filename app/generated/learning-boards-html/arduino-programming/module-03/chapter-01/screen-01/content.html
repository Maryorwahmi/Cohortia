Digital Output: Controlling LEDs and Other Devices
From a single blink to multi‑LED patterns and safe driver circuits.

This lesson connects code to circuits. You will configure pins as outputs, switch them HIGH and LOW, wire LEDs with resistors, and build simple patterns. Understanding current limits and when to use transistors is essential to protect your Arduino and components.
