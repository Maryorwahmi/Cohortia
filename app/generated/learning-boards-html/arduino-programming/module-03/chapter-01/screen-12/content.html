Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Troubleshooting
Quick checklist
  • Verify resistor is in series with the LED anode.
  • Confirm LED polarity: long leg → anode; short leg → cathode.
  • Ensure LED cathode is connected to Arduino GND.
  • Confirm pinMode(pin, OUTPUT) is in setup.
  • Upload a minimal blink sketch to isolate hardware issues.
When things fail
If a pin or LED becomes hot, disconnect power immediately. Heat indicates excessive current or a short circuit. Review resistor values and switch to a driver if necessary.
Simplify to isolate
Remove extra LEDs and run a single LED blink on one pin. This shows whether the issue is wiring/code or interaction between components.
Key Takeaway

A short, methodical checklist — polarity, resistor, ground, pinMode, upload — resolves most LED issues. Stop and inspect before continuing.
