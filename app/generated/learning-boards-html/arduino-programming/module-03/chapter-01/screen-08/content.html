Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Current limits
Per-pin limits vs. driver circuits
Pins can safely handle modest indicator currents; use external switching for larger loads.
Safe example
Single LED with 220Ω resistor draws under safe current and can be driven directly from a pin.
When to use a driver
Motors and large relays need a transistor, MOSFET, or dedicated driver plus an external power supply to handle current safely.
Misconception
Do not parallel pins as a solution for high current. Use a proper transistor or driver for reliability and safety.
Practical wiring pattern
Use the Arduino pin to drive the base or gate of a transistor. The transistor then switches current from a separate supply to the load. Include a diode for inductive loads like motors or relays.
Concrete example
Drive a small motor by using an N‑channel MOSFET as a low-side switch. The MOSFET gate is controlled by the Arduino pin, motor powered from a separate supply, and a diode placed across the motor to clamp inductive spikes.
Key Takeaway

Match current needs to a proper switching method: pins for small indicators, transistors/drivers for larger loads and motors.
