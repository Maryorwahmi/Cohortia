Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Driving other devices
When to use drivers
Devices that need more current or produce inductive spikes require transistors, MOSFETs, or driver modules and protection like diodes.
Buzzer
Small buzzers may be pin-driveable; for louder or continuous sound, use a transistor and external power.
Relay
Use a transistor to switch the relay coil and add a flyback diode across the coil to protect the transistor and Arduino from voltage spikes.
Concrete wiring pattern
Pin → base/gate via resistor → transistor; transistor switches external supply to load; common grounds required. Add flyback diode for inductive loads.
Misconception
Never assume the Arduino pin can deliver a device's full current. When in doubt, use a driver and separate power supply.
Key Takeaway

Treat the Arduino pin as a control signal for larger power paths; protect both board and device with proper switching components and diodes.
