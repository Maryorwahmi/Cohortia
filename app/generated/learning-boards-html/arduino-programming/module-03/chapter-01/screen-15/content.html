Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Resistor first
Include a current‑limiting resistor for each LED to protect the LED and the pin.
Use drivers for heavy loads
Transistors or MOSFETs let you switch higher currents safely while the Arduino supplies only a control signal.
Debug methodically
When things fail, simplify the setup, test one LED and one pin, and check wiring and pinMode before expanding.
Final takeaway

Small circuits and clear habits prevent damage and make your Arduino projects predictable and extendable.
