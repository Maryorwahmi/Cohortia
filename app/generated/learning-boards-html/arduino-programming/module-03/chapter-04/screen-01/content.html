Analog Output with PWM
Simulate analog voltages to control LEDs, motors, and buzzers using PWM and Arduino functions.
In this lesson
Learn PWM fundamentals, identify PWM pins on the Uno, use analogWrite for fading and motor control, and use tone for sound.
Why it matters
PWM gives you efficient, precise control of actuators without extra DAC hardware — crucial for prototyping and most hobby projects.
Explore Key Topics
PWM Basics
Duty cycle, frequency, and how rapid switching creates an average voltage.
analogWrite & Pins
Use analogWrite with PWM-capable pins 3,5,6,9,10,11 to produce 0–255 values.
tone() and Sound
Generate square-wave tones for buzzers, and stop them with noTone when needed.
