Key Concepts
Three quick reminders to carry forward.
PWM & Duty Cycle
On/off pulses averaged over time; duty cycle controls perceived output.
PWM Pins
Use pins 3,5,6,9,10,11 on the Uno; they are marked with a tilde.
analogWrite Mapping
Value ranges zero to two hundred fifty-five; value/255 × Vcc ≈ average voltage.
Final takeaway
PWM is a simple, effective way to control many actuators. Match hardware, pins, and logic to your project's needs.
