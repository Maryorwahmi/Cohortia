Safe motor wiring
The Arduino provides the PWM signal; use a transistor or driver to provide current and protect the board.
Scenario
Use a logic-level MOSFET as a low-side switch. PWM drives the gate, motor connects to supply, include a diode across the motor for flyback protection.
Driver options
Single MOSFET for one direction, H-bridge for reversible motors, or dedicated motor drivers for current sensing and braking.
Common mistake
Connecting a motor directly to an Arduino pin — it can exceed current limits and damage the microcontroller.
Key Takeaway

Always use a switch or driver for motors; the Arduino provides timing, not high current.

Quick checklist
  • Shared ground between Arduino and motor supply
  • Flyback diode across motor terminals
  • Properly rated MOSFET or driver
