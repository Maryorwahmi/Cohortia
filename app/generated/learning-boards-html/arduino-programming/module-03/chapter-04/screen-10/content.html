Generating tones
Use tone to emit a square-wave at a chosen frequency and noTone to stop it.
// Conceptual usage // call tone on a pin with a frequency in hertz // optional duration stops the tone automatically // use noTone to stop early
Concrete example
Play a one thousand hertz beep for five hundred milliseconds to signal completion of a step.
Common issue
Expecting musical quality. tone makes a square wave, which sounds richer in harmonics than a sine wave from a DAC.
Key Takeaway

tone and noTone are great for alerts and simple melodies; use proper audio hardware for music-quality sound.

Practical note
Be mindful that tone can interfere with PWM on pins that share timers; if you need both, choose pins and timers carefully.
