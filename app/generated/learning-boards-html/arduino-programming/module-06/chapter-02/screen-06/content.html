Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Switching Peripherals
Checklist
Steps to remove idle current
  1. Inventory always-on LEDs and modules.
  2. For small sensors, consider powering from a GPIO pin and set it LOW to turn off.
  3. For larger loads use a transistor or MOSFET switch for VCC control.
  4. Disable ADC, peripherals, and unused interfaces in firmware.
Example scenario
An always-on status LED may draw a few milliamps continuously. Removing or switching it reduces base load. An I2C sensor with its own regulator can draw current even when not polled; switching its VCC with a MOSFET stops that draw entirely.
Pro tip
Use a MOSFET for cleaner switching on higher current modules to avoid voltage drops.
Misconception
Toggling data lines rarely stops device quiescent current; switch the supply instead.
Key Takeaway

Power peripherals only when needed. Switching VCC is the most reliable way to remove idle draw.
