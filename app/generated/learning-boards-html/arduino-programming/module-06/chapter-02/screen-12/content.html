Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Design Checklist
1. Board & Voltage
Choose a minimal board or 3.3V option to avoid regulator losses.
2. Power Path
Prefer regulated supply to the 5V pin or native board voltage over the barrel jack.
3. Peripherals
Power down sensors and LEDs when idle; use transistors for larger loads.
4. Firmware
Use sleep modes and disable ADC/BOD during sleep for lowest idle current.
5. Measure
Measure before and after each change. Data shows what truly helps.
6. Iterate
Change one variable at a time and record currents to guide decisions.
Key Takeaway

A repeatable checklist and measurement-driven approach turn low-power goals into reliable results.
