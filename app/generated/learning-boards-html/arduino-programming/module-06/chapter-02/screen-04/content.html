Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Board Tradeoffs
Summary
Uno vs Pro Mini vs Feather
  • Uno: includes USB and regulator. Useful for prototyping. Higher baseline draw.
  • Pro Mini: minimal components. Easier to lower baseline. Good for battery projects.
  • Feather/ESP32: some offer deep-sleep and charging, choose based on your sensors' voltage.
Practical tip
Use a 3.3V board for 3.3V sensors to avoid regulators.
Common mistake
Feeding 9V through barrel jack thinking the board will be efficient.
Comparison table
Feeding via barrel jack (9V)
Regulator drops voltage from 9V to 5V and dissipates energy. Works but wastes battery capacity.
Feeding via 5V pin (regulated)
Provide regulated 5V to avoid regulator losses. Ensure correct polarity and stable regulator source.
Using a 3.3V board
Match battery voltage to board voltage to reduce conversion losses and baseline current.
Key Takeaway

Match supply voltage to board and minimize on-board always-on components to reduce idle drain.
