Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Regulator Efficiency
What is regulator efficiency?

It is the energy delivered to the board divided by the energy drawn from the battery. With linear regulators the extra voltage becomes heat.

Concrete example
Feeding an Uno from a 9V battery through barrel jack forces the regulator to drop to 5V. The regulator dissipates the difference as heat and wastes battery energy.
Practical check
Measure current with regulator connected. Then feed regulated 5V directly and compare readings.
Battery selection cards
Use matching voltage
If your board is 3.3V, prefer a 3.3V supply chain to avoid conversion losses.
Avoid high-drop regulators
Feeding much higher voltages forces the regulator to dissipate extra energy as heat.
Key Takeaway

Match supply voltage and minimize regulator voltage drop to conserve battery energy.
