Power Management and Battery Operation
Practical ways to reduce battery drain on Arduino projects.
In this lesson

Compare boards and power paths. Use sleep modes. Switch off peripherals. Measure actual current with a multimeter.

Why it matters

Battery life determines where and how long projects can run. Efficient power design saves time and components.

Quick action

Measure current now. Then remove one always-on LED and measure again to see the change.
