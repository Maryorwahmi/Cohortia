Battery & Regulator Efficiency
Match supply voltage to the board to reduce regulator dissipation.
Sleep Modes
Use library-supported sleep and disable ADC/BOD to cut idle current.
Measure with a Multimeter
Place the meter in series and compare before/after optimizations.
Summary
These three practices—match voltage, sleep properly, and measure—are the most reliable levers to extend battery life in Arduino projects.
