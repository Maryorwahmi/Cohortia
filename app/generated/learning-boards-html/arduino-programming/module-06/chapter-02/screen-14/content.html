Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
Turn off peripherals
Power sensors and modules only when needed to avoid continuous draw.
Low-power board options
Consider Pro Mini, Feather, or boards with deep-sleep for very low-power applications.
Measure and iterate
Change one thing at a time, measure current, and record results to improve design decisions.
Final takeaway
Small, measured changes compound. Use matching voltage, peripheral switching, sleep modes, and measurement to extend battery life reliably.
