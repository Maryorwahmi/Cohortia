Comparison

Focused prints vs. excessive logging

Compare the trade-offs and learn when each approach helps or hurts.

Focused prints
  • Answer a specific hypothesis
  • Minimal impact on timing
  • Easy to remove
Excessive logging
  • May hide timing bugs
  • Creates noisy output
  • Hard to prune later
Example scenario
An intermittent sensor failure that disappears when you add prints suggests a timing or initialization dependency. Reduce prints, add a small delay or initialize earlier, and reproduce without prints to confirm the fix.
Pitfall

If printing hides an intermittent bug, you must change the test strategy; printing alone is not a fix.
