Module 7 › Chapter 6.4 › Pump Control & Safety
Architecture snapshot
Controller (Arduino) sends a control signal to a relay module. The relay switches the pump's external power. Keep the pump's supply separate and document voltage and current. Use limited run times and cooldowns in software to avoid overwatering.
Checklist
  • Use a separate power supply for the pump and document its specs.
  • Switch the pump using a relay or dedicated driver, not the Arduino pin directly.
  • Limit pump activation time and add a cooldown interval in code.
  • Include wiring diagrams and safety notes in your README for assessment.
