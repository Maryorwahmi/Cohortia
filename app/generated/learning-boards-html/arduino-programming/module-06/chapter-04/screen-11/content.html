Assembly checklist
  • Prototype on a breadboard and verify each sensor with Serial output.
  • Label wires and draw a wiring diagram with parts and pins in the README.
  • Use separate pump power, secure connections, and include flyback protection for inductive loads.
  • Place sensors consistently and document the exact depth or orientation used for tests.
  • Take photos and a short overview video showing the block diagram and wiring for presentation.
Why this affects grading
Assessors check that sensors read correctly, the display is clear, alerts are appropriate, and that documentation allows another person to reproduce your build. Neat wiring and clear notes make your design easier to evaluate and reuse.
