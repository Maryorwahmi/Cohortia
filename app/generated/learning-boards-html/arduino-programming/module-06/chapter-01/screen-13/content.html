Interrupts & ISRs
Pause loop, run a short routine, then return. Use for short, urgent events.
attachInterrupt() modes
Choose FALLING, RISING, CHANGE, or LOW according to wiring and needs.
volatile & millis()
Mark ISR-shared variables volatile and use millis for non-blocking periodic tasks.
Final takeaway

Combine short ISRs, proper trigger modes, volatile for shared data, and millis-based timing for reliable, responsive Arduino projects.
