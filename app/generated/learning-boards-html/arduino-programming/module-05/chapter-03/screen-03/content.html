Objective Deep Dive
Concrete actions and verification for each learning objective.
Test 1
Bluetooth wiring & serial
Wire VCC, GND, TX, RX with safe voltage levels, pair with phone, send a command. Confirm LED toggles.
Test 2
SoftwareSerial workaround
Use alternate pins for Bluetooth; verify uploads still work and commands are received reliably.
Test 3
ESP board upload
Upload a simple sketch to an ESP8266 or ESP32 with the Arduino IDE and confirm networking capability.
Why verify
Small checks catch wiring mistakes, serial conflicts, or forgotten authentication steps before you publish a device.
Quick mnemonic
Wire, Pair, Parse, Protect — the four steps to reliable wireless projects.
