Module 6 › Chapter 5.3 › Assessing board choice and tradeoffs
Assessment Idea
Compare and justify your board choice
Use resource, wiring, and deployment criteria to support your answer.
Prompt
Explain the main advantage of choosing an ESP32 over an Arduino Uno plus ESP8266 module for a web server project. Provide one concrete scenario and rationale.
Scoring guidance
Look for mention of built‑in Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth, more memory, faster processor, simpler networking, and fewer wiring conflicts as supporting points.
Common pitfall
Avoid answers that only restate that both use the Arduino language; explain the practical differences in resources and networking.
Key Takeaway

Good answers show how resource and networking differences affect the project's success, not just surface similarities.
