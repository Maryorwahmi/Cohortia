Quick checklist
1. Baud match
Confirm Serial.begin value equals the monitor selection.
2. Wiring
Cross TX to RX and ensure a common ground between devices.
3. Line endings
Set the monitor to send newline or trim carriage returns in your parser.
4. Print rate
Avoid flooding the monitor; add short delays or sample at sensible intervals.
Why this helps

These checks reduce ambiguity. Most visible errors are resolved by matching baud and wiring; the rest come from line endings or SoftwareSerial limitations.
