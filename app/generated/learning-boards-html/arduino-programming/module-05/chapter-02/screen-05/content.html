Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Wiring I2C Devices
Step-by-step
1. Tie all SDA lines together and to Arduino SDA (A4 on Uno). Tie all SCL lines together and to Arduino SCL (A5 on Uno).
2. Ensure pull-ups on SDA and SCL, typically 4.7 kilo-ohm to 5 V or 3.3 V depending on devices.
3. Power devices and run an I2C scanner sketch to list 7-bit addresses present on the bus.
4. If two devices share an address, change configuration, use a multiplexer, or choose SPI for one device.
Example
You wire an I2C LCD and a DS3231 RTC. After powering, the scanner shows addresses 0x27 and 0x68. The display updates and the clock responds because addresses are distinct and pull-ups are present.
Misconception
Devices do not share or change addresses automatically; the designer must check and manage addresses.
