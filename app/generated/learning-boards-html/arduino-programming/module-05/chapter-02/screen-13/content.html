Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Debugging tips
I2C
  • Confirm pull-ups exist and are correct for voltage.
  • Run an I2C scanner to reveal addresses.
  • Resolve duplicate addresses before coding.
SPI
  • Ensure one SS is active at a time and release before selecting another.
  • Match clock polarity and phase (mode) to device requirements.
  • Test devices one at a time to isolate wiring errors.
Quick checklist
Power rails, pull-ups, scanner output, single-device testing, then incremental addition of peripherals.
