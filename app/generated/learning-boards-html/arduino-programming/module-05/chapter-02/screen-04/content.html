Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › I2C basics
Definition
I2C uses SDA (data) and SCL (clock). Devices use 7-bit addresses so multiple peripherals share the bus.
Signals
SDA: bidirectional data. SCL: clock generated by the master.
Key takeaway
Open-drain lines need pull-up resistors to form valid highs.
Common mistake
Wiring without checking device addresses leads to conflicts and silent devices.
Example scenario
An I2C LCD at address 0x27 and a temperature sensor at 0x48 both connect to SDA and SCL on the Uno. The master addresses each device by its 7-bit address to fetch readings or update the display.
