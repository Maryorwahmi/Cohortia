I2C and SPI
Understand shared buses, wiring, Arduino pins, and practical trade-offs for sensors and displays.
In this lesson
Pin mapping, code examples, and a practical scanner for I2C address conflicts.
Why it matters
Choosing the right bus saves pins and avoids timing problems when adding many devices.
Explore Key Topics
I2C Wiring
SDA and SCL plus pull-ups; multiple devices share the bus by address.
SPI Signals
MOSI, MISO, SCK, and separate SS lines per device for selection.
Picking a Bus
Consider pin count, speed needs, and library support for your sensors.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Concept map
I2C
SDA, SCL, addresses — great for many low-speed sensors.
SPI
MOSI, MISO, SCK, SS — fast and simple framing per device.
Practical link
Many devices support both; wiring and available pins usually decide which to use.
Quick start
Try adding two I2C sensors to SDA and SCL on your Uno and run an I2C scanner to confirm addresses before wiring a display.
