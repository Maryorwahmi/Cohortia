Shared lines and selects
MOSI, MISO, and SCK are common to all devices. Each device gets a dedicated SS pin to be driven low when selected. The master must toggle SS lines correctly to avoid collisions.
SD card: SS = D4
Display: SS = D7
Master: drives SCK and toggles SS
Common mistake
Leaving a device selected when starting a new transfer often causes bus contention. Always deassert previous SS lines before asserting another.
Key takeaway
Proper SS sequencing is as important as wiring MOSI/MISO/SCK correctly for multi-device SPI systems.
