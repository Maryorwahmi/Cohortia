Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Safe Change Timeline
1. Record and capture
Capture current configs and recent logs so you can roll back or compare results after the change.
2. Apply minimal change
Make a targeted HBA entry, open a specific firewall rule, or adjust a single setting and avoid broad open changes.
3. Reload and verify
Reload PostgreSQL when possible, then run pg_isready and attempt a psql connection to validate the fix.
4. Document
Log the change, verification steps, and rationale so future responders can follow the same path.
Quick checklist
  • Backup configs
  • Make one change
  • Reload where possible
  • Verify with tools and logs
