Scenario: Client cannot connect
You are provided: telnet fails, listen_addresses shows 0.0.0.0, pg_hba.conf includes an allow for the client. What is the likely cause and the minimal fix?
Expected diagnosis
A firewall is blocking port 5432. The minimal safe fix is to adjust firewall or cloud security group rules to allow inbound TCP 5432 from the client or test by temporarily allowing the port for that IP range and retesting. Document and revert any temporary broad rules after verification.
Why this lab helps
It forces you to isolate one variable at a time: network, HBA, or resource. Developing that habit prevents overcorrection and speeds restoration.
