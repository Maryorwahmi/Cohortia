Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Overview
Advanced Troubleshooting of Connection & Authentication
A practical checklist to isolate network, authentication, and server-side limits that block clients from connecting.
In this lesson

Learn commands, config checks, and diagnostic techniques to restore connectivity quickly and safely.

Why it matters

Connection failures make the database unreachable; a repeatable process reduces downtime and prevents misconfiguration cascading into outages.

Explore Key Topics
pg_hba.conf
Order and methods determine who can authenticate and how; reload after edits.
Network & Firewall
Test port reachability with telnet or netcat and verify listen_addresses and security groups.
Resource Limits
Monitor max_connections and idle in transaction sessions via pg_stat_activity.
