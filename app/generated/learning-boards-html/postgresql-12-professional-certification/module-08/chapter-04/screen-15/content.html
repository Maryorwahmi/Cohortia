Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Quick Concepts
listen_addresses
Controls which interfaces PostgreSQL binds to; adjust and restart as needed for remote access.
pg_isready
Quick check for server readiness without authenticating; useful for monitoring and initial diagnosis.
pg_terminate_backend(pid)
Forcefully terminate a backend by PID to free slots, but coordinate and document since it may rollback work.
Final takeaway

Build a habit: probe the network, verify HBA, check logs, inspect connections, make one change, and verify. That routine reduces downtime and confusion.
