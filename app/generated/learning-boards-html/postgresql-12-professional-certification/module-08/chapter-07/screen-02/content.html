Learning Objectives

From diagnosis to remediation: measurable skills you will gain.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Diagnose replication lag
Read sent, write, flush and replay LSNs and convert LSN diffs into actionable bottlenecks.
Mnemonic: LSN ladder (sent→write→flush→replay)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Troubleshoot network & bandwidth
Use ping, iperf, tcpdump and port checks to separate latency from throughput issues that affect WAL shipping.
Mnemonic: PING → PATH → PERF
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Fix WAL archiving failures
Detect archive_command failures, check permissions, paths and archive storage health to avoid pg_wal growth.
Mnemonic: ARCHIVE checklist (path, perms, network, disk)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Resolve logical replication conflicts
Identify conflicts, repair subscriber rows and use subscription controls to skip a problematic LSN when safe.
Mnemonic: FIND → FIX → SKIP
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
