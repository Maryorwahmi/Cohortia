Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Key Concepts
Replication Lag
Measured by LSN diffs and sent/write/flush/replay columns.
WAL Archiving
archive_command failures lead to pg_wal growth; check permissions, path, network and disk.
Replication Slot
Slots hold WAL; inactive slots can block WAL removal and must be reviewed before dropping.
Takeaway
Diagnose with facts, prioritize non-destructive checks, and document any slot or subscription changes.
