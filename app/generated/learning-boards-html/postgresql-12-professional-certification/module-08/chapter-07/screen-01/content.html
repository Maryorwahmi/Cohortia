Advanced Replication Troubleshooting

Practical diagnostics for physical and logical replication issues, WAL archiving, slots and conflict recovery.

16:9 HD Classroom
In this lesson
  • Measure replication lag with LSN diffs and pg_stat views.
  • Diagnose network, I/O, WAL archival and slot problems.
  • Resolve logical conflicts and manage replication slots safely.
Why it matters

Minimizing replication lag keeps your RPO low and prevents long recovery times. Fixing WAL and slot issues prevents disk fill and long resyncs.

Concept map

Monitoring

Actions
