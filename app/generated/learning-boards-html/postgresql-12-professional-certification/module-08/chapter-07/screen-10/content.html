Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Replication slots

Inspect replication slots

Find inactive slots and decide whether to recover or drop them.

-- Monitor replication slots
SELECT slot_name, slot_type, active, wal_status, restart_lsn, catalog_xmin,
       pg_size_pretty(pg_wal_lsn_diff(pg_current_wal_lsn(), restart_lsn)) AS wal_lag_bytes
FROM pg_replication_slots;

-- Drop an inactive replication slot (use with caution)
SELECT pg_drop_replication_slot('my_inactive_slot');
Checklist before drop
  • Confirm slot is inactive and belongs to an unreachable subscriber.
  • Ensure dropping is acceptable for downstream recovery strategy.
Consequence
Dropping a slot may require a full resync of the subscriber.
