Timeline

Collect snapshots across low, medium, and peak loads

Include iostat, vmstat, top/htop, and a snapshot of pg_stat_statements and pg_stat_activity.
Store these snapshots and visualize trends rather than relying on single measurements.
Monitoring checklist
  • Automate periodic collection of OS and PostgreSQL metrics.
  • Create alerts for sustained high iowait, swap activity, or %util near 100%.
  • Re-baseline after configuration or workload changes.
Key Takeaway

Baselines let you spot true anomalies quickly and avoid chasing expected variance as incidents.
