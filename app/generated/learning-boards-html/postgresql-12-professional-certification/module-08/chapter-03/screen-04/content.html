Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › CPU Utilization and I/O Wait
Comparison

CPU breakdown: user · system · iowait

Read these fields before deciding on scaling or tuning. They point to compute, kernel, or storage waits respectively.

Example: CPU-bound task
-- A CPU-heavy PL/pgSQL loop
SELECT cpu_intensive_task();
-- Observe htop: high %user on postgres workers, low iowait
Example: I/O-heavy task
iostat -x 1
# Watch %util and await increase during large writes or scans
Indicators to check
  • High %user: check heavy SQL, functions, or background workers.
  • High %system: kernel-level activity, often tied to I/O or context switches.
  • High iowait: storage responses are slow — investigate with iostat.
Key Takeaway

Distinguish compute vs storage waits from CPU columns; match with iostat to choose the right remedy.
