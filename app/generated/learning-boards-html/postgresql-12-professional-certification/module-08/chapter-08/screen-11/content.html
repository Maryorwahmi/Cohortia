Module 7 › Chapter 8.8 › Post-Mortem Analysis
Post-mortem structure
  1. Timeline — chronological events and timestamps.
  2. Root cause analysis — why the failure occurred.
  3. Impact assessment — users, data, business effect.
  4. What went well — effective responses or tools.
  5. Action items — specific owners and deadlines.
Example action items
  • Add WAL archiving health check and alert — owner: ops — due in 7 days.
  • Update 'service down' runbook with log paths — owner: oncall — due in 3 days.
  • Schedule quarterly failover drills — owner: team lead — due in 30 days.
Blameless culture
Encourage open reporting and focus on systems, not individuals. This increases transparency and learning.
Key Takeaway
A short, tracked list of action items is more valuable than a long narrative. Measure closure and verify fixes.
Mistake to avoid
Leaving action items vague or unassigned. That leads to repeated incidents and wasted time.
