Physical (Streaming)
Byte-for-byte standby via WAL streaming
Use when you need very low RPO and fast promotion. Requires base backup, compatible versions, and WAL shipping.
Low RPO
Fast failover
Logical Replication
Row-level logical changes across databases
Best for selective table replication, heterogeneous versions, or replicating to analytics systems. Requires publications and subscriptions.
Flexible topology
Potentially higher lag
Decision checklist
  • RTO and RPO targets — how quickly and how much data loss is acceptable?
  • Cross-version requirements — do you need replication across major versions?
  • Operational complexity — automation, monitoring, and failover testing required?
Example
E-commerce site: use physical streaming for low RPO on the primary and logical replication to push subset tables to a reporting cluster for analytics.
Key Takeaway

Choose replication by matching its guarantees to your RTO/RPO and operational constraints.

Common Mistake
Assuming logical replication alone is sufficient for PITR or low-RPO failover. It is not a substitute for base backups plus WAL archiving when you need point-in-time recovery.
