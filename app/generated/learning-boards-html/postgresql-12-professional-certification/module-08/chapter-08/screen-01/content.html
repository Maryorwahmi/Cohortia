Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Overview
Incident Response & Disaster Recovery
Operational playbook for PostgreSQL outages, replication failures, and recovery drills.
This chapter focuses on three pillars: a reproducible incident response plan, a disaster recovery strategy driven by RTO and RPO, and a culture of testing and post-mortems. Expect practical runbooks and PostgreSQL commands you can test in staging.
In this lesson
Build IR plans
Detect, triage, mitigate, and restore PostgreSQL services with clear steps and examples.
Coming up
Design DR for RTO/RPO
Choose backups, WAL archiving, and replication to meet business recovery targets.
Practice
Create & test runbooks
Simulate failovers and PITR on staging. Verify backups restore cleanly.
