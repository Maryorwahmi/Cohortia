Module 7 › Chapter 8.8 › Failover & Switchover
Failover steps (emergency)
  1. Confirm primary unreachable and check WAL availability.
  2. Check replication lag: ensure standby has required WAL.
  3. Promote standby (command or trigger file).
  4. Repoint applications and update monitoring/HA proxies.
  5. Reconfigure remaining standbys to follow the new primary.
Common promote commands (examples used in testing):
# Promote a Debian/Ubuntu cluster
pg_ctlcluster 12 main promote

# Or, if using a trigger file in older recovery.conf setups
# touch /var/lib/postgresql/12/main/trigger_file
Switchover (planned)
Coordinate a graceful step-down of the primary, wait for standbys to catch up, switch traffic, then demote and reconfigure the former primary as a standby.
Pitfall
Promoting without verifying WAL continuity risks losing transactions that never arrived at the standby.
Pro Tip
Automate promotion checks with your cluster manager but always include a human-approved failback checklist for major incidents.
