Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
Maximum acceptable data loss measured as time. RPO guides WAL archiving frequency and replication design.
WAL Archiving
Continuously save WAL segments off the primary so you can replay changes for PITR or restore to a specific time.
Physical Backup (pg_basebackup)
A full copy of the data directory used as a base for PITR and for creating new standbys quickly.
Final takeaway
Combine tested base backups, WAL archiving, and appropriate replication to meet your RPO and RTO targets reliably.
