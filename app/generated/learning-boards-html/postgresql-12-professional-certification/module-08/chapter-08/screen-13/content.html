Module 7 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts A
Incident Response (IR)
A structured approach for detecting, triaging, diagnosing, mitigating, and recovering from incidents that affect database operations.
Disaster Recovery (DR)
Plans and mechanisms to recover services and data after major failures, such as data-center loss or catastrophic corruption.
Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
Maximum acceptable downtime after a disaster. Your RTO determines how quickly your procedures and automation must restore service.
Summary
Keep IR and DR plans short, test restores regularly, and measure restore time against your RTO goals.
