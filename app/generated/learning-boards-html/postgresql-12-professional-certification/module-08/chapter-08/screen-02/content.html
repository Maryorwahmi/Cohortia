Learning Objectives
Skills to design, test, and learn from incident and disaster scenarios.
01
Build an Incident Response Plan
Document detection, triage, diagnosis, mitigation, resolution and post-mortem steps so teams respond consistently during outages.
Mnemonic: timeline checklist (detect → triage → diagnose → mitigate → recover → learn)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Define RTO & RPO
Translate business limits into technical targets and map them to backup and replication choices to meet service requirements.
Mnemonic: clock (RTO) vs. timeline (RPO)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design backups & replication
Choose pg_basebackup, WAL archiving, and streaming or logical replication and document retention and storage locations.
Mnemonic: three-legged stool (base backup + WAL + standby)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Create & Test Runbooks and Post-Mortems
Write clear runbooks for common incidents and practice restores in staging; hold blameless post-mortems to drive improvements.
Mnemonic: PLAN → DO → REVIEW
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
