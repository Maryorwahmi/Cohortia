Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Operational Checklist
Prevention

Concrete configuration and operational items

Enable data checksums
Set checksums during initdb so pg_checksums can detect block-level corruption later.
full_page_writes and fsync
Keep full_page_writes on and ensure filesystem and hardware properly support fsync to avoid partial page writes.
Backups and PITR
Maintain regular base backups, archive WAL continuously, and rehearse restores to verify your recovery plan.
Monitoring
Automate checks of age(datfrozenxid), age(relfrozenxid), and autovacuum process health; alert long before the freeze age threshold.
Key Takeaway

Proactive configuration, monitoring, and tested backups are the best defense against wraparound and corruption incidents.
