Quick map
From warning to recovery
Signs
Log warnings about databases needing vacuum; rising age(datfrozenxid) values.
Immediate action
Tune autovacuum, run VACUUM FREEZE in maintenance window, or restore from backup if corruption appears.
Long term
Enable checksums at initdb, keep full_page_writes on, test backups and monitor autovacuum effectiveness.
Activity
Hands-on lab: simulate XID ageing on a test table, observe relfrozenxid, then run VACUUM FREEZE to see the reset. Do not attempt on production.
