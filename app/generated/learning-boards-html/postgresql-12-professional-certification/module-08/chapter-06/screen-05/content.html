Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › How PostgreSQL Prevents Wraparound
Process Flow

Autovacuum → Freeze → Warnings

Normal VACUUM
Removes dead tuples and reclaims space for reuse.
Freezing
Marks very old XIDs as frozen so they no longer need comparison checks.
Aggressive autovacuum
When age approaches autovacuum_freeze_max_age, autovacuum runs more often and warns in logs.
Operational signals
  • Log warnings that a database or table must be vacuumed within N transactions.
  • High values returned by age(datfrozenxid) or age(relfrozenxid).
  • Autovacuum processes not visible in pg_stat_activity or taking too long to finish.
Misconception
Believing that autovacuum will always catch up without tuning. In heavy-change environments, autovacuum must be given enough workers and less throttle to do its freeze work.
Key Takeaway

Autovacuum prevents wraparound, but it must be properly resourced and tuned for busy databases.
