Resolving Disk Space and Storage-Related Problems

Measure usage with pg_* functions, diagnose table and index bloat, tune autovacuum, and choose safe reclaim strategies.

In this lesson

You will identify large relations, observe bloat impact, run VACUUM and VACUUM FULL, tune autovacuum, and monitor XID age to avoid wraparound.

Why it matters

Unchecked storage growth can slow queries, increase I/O, and cause outages if disks fill or transaction IDs approach wraparound thresholds.
