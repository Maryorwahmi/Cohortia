Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › VACUUM strategies
Step-by-step
  1. Measure relation sizes and dead tuple counts.
  2. Run VACUUM VERBOSE to reclaim space for reuse and update stats.
  3. If disk must be shrunk, schedule VACUUM FULL during a maintenance window.
  4. Monitor and tune autovacuum to prevent recurrence.
Commands
-- Non-blocking: reclaims space for reuse
VACUUM VERBOSE my_table;

-- Blocking: rewrites and returns space to OS
VACUUM FULL VERBOSE my_table;
Common-mistake

Running VACUUM FULL repeatedly without addressing autovacuum tuning or update patterns will cause recurring bloat and repeated blocking operations.

Concrete example

Start with VACUUM VERBOSE on an audit_logs table with many dead tuples to reduce n_dead_tup and improve reuse. If you must free disk immediately, plan VACUUM FULL in a maintenance window.

Key takeaway

VACUUM is your routine tool; VACUUM FULL is a heavy hammer used when reclaiming OS space is essential and downtime is acceptable.
