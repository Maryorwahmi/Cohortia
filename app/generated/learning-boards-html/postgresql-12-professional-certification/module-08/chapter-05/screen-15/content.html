Measure first

Queries exist to show sizes and tuple counts; always capture them before and after maintenance.

Prefer non-blocking fixes

Start with VACUUM and autovacuum tuning; use VACUUM FULL only when you must release OS space.

Monitor critical signals

Watch log_temp_files for spills and age(datfrozenxid) for XID risk; act early.

Final note

A small routine of measurement and targeted fixes prevents the most costly storage problems in PostgreSQL.
