Best Practices
Avoid noisy logging and secure your logs
Make logging diagnostic without causing performance or storage issues.
Don'ts
Avoid log_statement = 'all' in production and never ignore rotation and disk monitoring.
Dos
Use duration thresholds, short diagnostic windows, timestamped filenames, and restrict access to pg_log.
Common mistake
Turning on everything permanently and then wondering why the system is slow or storage is exhausted.
Practical checklist
  • Enable logging_collector and set a predictable log_filename.
  • Use log_min_duration_statement for targeted slow-query capture.
  • Rotate logs by age or size and monitor pg_log size.
  • Secure the log directory and restrict access to administrators.
When you need deeper access, increase verbosity only for a defined period and revert when done to avoid ongoing overhead.
