Analysis
Performance vs. Error Investigation
Use different log signals to diagnose slow queries, warnings, and fatal errors.
Performance signals
Repeated log_min_duration_statement entries, temporary file logs, and long execution times suggest tuning or indexing needs.
Error signals
ERROR and FATAL lines indicate operational failures; correlate them with connection logs and timestamps for root cause.
Key Takeaway
Use patterns across logs to prioritize fixes: recurring slow queries or warnings usually deserve higher priority than single transient errors.
Action steps
  • Filter logs by timestamp and PID to find related events.
  • Extract repeated slow SQL and run EXPLAIN ANALYZE to inspect plans.
  • Investigate WARNING lines such as autovacuum notices before they escalate.
  • Archive logs once analyzed and free disk space if needed.
Misconception
Treating log files as immutable archives; in practice you must rotate, archive, and then analyze selected windows to avoid being overwhelmed.
