What to Remember
Quick final checklist
Run as Administrator
Ensure installer has rights to register the service and write to Program Files.
Edit and Restart
Make changes in postgresql.conf or pg_hba.conf and restart the service for them to apply.
Firewall Rule
Open TCP port 5432 only for trusted networks if remote access is required.
Final takeaway: document your install choices—paths, port, and postgres password—so you can manage and troubleshoot confidently.
