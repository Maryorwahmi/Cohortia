Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Background workers
Roles explained
  • walwriter: flushes WAL records from wal_buffers to disk to ensure durability.
  • checkpointer: writes dirty pages from shared_buffers to disk and creates checkpoints to limit recovery scope.
  • autovacuum launcher: schedules autovacuum workers to reclaim space and update planner statistics.
Practical contrast: walwriter is about WAL durability and ordering of writes; checkpointer is about when full pages are synced to disk and when consistent checkpoints are recorded. Autovacuum is about cleaning up obsolete row versions and keeping planner statistics accurate so queries remain efficient.
Common mistake
Overlooking autovacuum leads to table bloat and planner statistics drift; do not disable it without a replacement maintenance strategy.
Example
Under heavy writes, walwriter frequently flushes WAL so transactions persist; checkpoints still batch page writes to avoid constant full-page writes.
Key Takeaway

Each background worker has a distinct role; inspect the right worker when diagnosing slow recovery, high I/O, or table bloat.
