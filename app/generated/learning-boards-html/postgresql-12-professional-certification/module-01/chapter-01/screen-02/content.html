Learning Objectives
Skills to verify with a terminal and inspection tasks
01
Map server processes
Identify postmaster, postgres backends, walwriter, checkpointer and autovacuum. This helps you assign responsibility when diagnosing issues.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain shared memory trade-offs
Describe shared_buffers and wal_buffers, recommended sizing and the risk of memory contention with OS and other apps.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Trace a connection lifecycle
Follow authentication through pg_hba.conf, spawning of a backend, and the backend's role in parsing and executing queries.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Inspect PGDATA and permissions
Locate base, pg_wal, global, and configuration files; verify ownership and restrictive permissions to avoid startup errors.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Bloom Progress
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Use the milestones to verify you can perform terminal checks and explain why a configuration change affects behavior.
