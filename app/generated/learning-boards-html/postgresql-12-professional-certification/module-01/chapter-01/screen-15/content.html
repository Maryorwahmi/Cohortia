Process-per-client
Each client gets a dedicated backend process forked by the postmaster.
Durability team
WAL, walwriter, and checkpointer work together to preserve and recover committed work.
PGDATA safety
PGDATA contains data and config; correct ownership and restrictive permissions are required for safe startup.
Final takeaway
Use process roles, WAL activity, and PGDATA checks as your first triage actions when diagnosing PostgreSQL behavior.
