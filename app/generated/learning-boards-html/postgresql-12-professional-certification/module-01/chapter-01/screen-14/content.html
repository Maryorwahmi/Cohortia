Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
postmaster & backends
postmaster forks a postgres backend for each client, giving process-level isolation and clear diagnostics.
Shared memory
shared_buffers and wal_buffers cache pages and WAL records; size them with system RAM in mind.
WAL & walwriter
WAL records are written before data pages and walwriter flushes WAL to pg_wal for durability and replication.
Takeaway
When diagnosing startup or performance issues, check process roles, shared memory settings, and PGDATA ownership as your primary evidence sources.
