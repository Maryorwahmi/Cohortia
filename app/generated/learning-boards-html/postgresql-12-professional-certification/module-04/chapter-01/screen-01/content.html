Why this matters

DML commands power your app's data lifecycle. Mistakes can corrupt data or cause downtime. Understanding transactional behavior and privileges helps you choose safe, efficient commands.

Visual Concept
INSERT
Add rows
UPDATE
Modify rows
DELETE / TRUNCATE
Remove rows
Key Takeaway

Be explicit: list columns for INSERTs, always scope UPDATE/DELETE with WHERE unless you truly intend to affect every row.
