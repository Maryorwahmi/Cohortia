Privileges, triggers, and identity behavior

Understand who can run destructive commands and what other changes happen beyond row removal.

Identity reset

TRUNCATE typically resets serial identity counters; DELETE does not.

Trigger behavior

Row-level triggers fire for DELETE but not for TRUNCATE, so auditing logic may be skipped on TRUNCATE.

Privileges

TRUNCATE can require elevated privileges; check with DBAs before running in production.

Practical check

Confirm foreign keys, triggers, and privileges before running TRUNCATE; prefer DELETE when triggers or cascades are required.
