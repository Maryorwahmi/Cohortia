Comparison

When to TRUNCATE and when to DELETE

TRUNCATE
  • Removes all rows quickly by deallocating storage
  • Resets identity columns
  • Does not fire row-level triggers
  • May require higher privileges
DELETE
  • Removes matching rows and fires row-level triggers
  • Writes per-row changes to WAL and supports ROLLBACK
  • Appropriate when cascades or triggers must run
Key Takeaway

TRUNCATE is for fast, full clears. DELETE is for safe, audited, and trigger-backed removals. Choose based on needs.
