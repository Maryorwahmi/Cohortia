Advanced Concurrency Patterns & Pitfalls

Design for throughput and correctness. Compare optimistic and pessimistic approaches, learn queue-processing patterns with SKIP LOCKED and NOWAIT, and understand XID wraparound and VACUUM.

In this lesson
Locking strategies, queue workers, XID wraparound, and monitoring tips.
Hands-on
Live psql demo using FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED on a tasks table.
Why it matters
Correct locking minimizes conflicts and prevents catastrophic wraparound problems.
Key Takeaway

Match locking strategy to expected contention, use SKIP LOCKED for worker pools, and keep autovacuum healthy to avoid XID wraparound.

Pessimistic Locking
Acquire locks ahead of modification to prevent conflicts; safe, but can block concurrency.
Optimistic Locking
Allow concurrent reads and detect conflicts at update time using versions or timestamps.
XID Safety
VACUUM/autovacuum freeze old XIDs to prevent wraparound and data loss.
