Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Key Concepts
Optimistic Locking
Detect conflicts at write time with version checks and retry logic in the application.
Pessimistic Locking
FOR UPDATE acquires exclusive locks to serialize updates, but be cautious of blocking and deadlocks.
SKIP LOCKED & NOWAIT
SKIP LOCKED skips locked rows for worker pools; NOWAIT raises an error for fail-fast semantics.
Summary

Pick the right locking model, monitor locks and XID age, and keep transactions short to maintain throughput and safety.
