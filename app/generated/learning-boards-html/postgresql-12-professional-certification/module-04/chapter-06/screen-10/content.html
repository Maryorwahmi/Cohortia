Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Long Transactions & Locks
Why long transactions are bad
  • They prevent VACUUM from removing old row versions and freezing XIDs.
  • They hold locks that block other transactions and reduce concurrency.
  • They contribute to table bloat and increased xid_age.
Mitigations
  • Keep transactions short and do non-DB work outside transactions.
  • Avoid table-level locks; prefer row-level locking and consistent lock ordering.
Check for offenders
Inspect pg_stat_activity for long-running or idle-in-transaction sessions and act to close or fix them.
Deadlock risk
Inconsistent lock acquisition order between transactions is a leading cause of deadlocks; standardize lock order to avoid them.
Key Takeaway

Short transactions and consistent lock ordering reduce blocking, bloat, and wraparound exposure.
