Select-then-write anti-pattern

When two clients both SELECT for existence, both may decide to INSERT, causing a duplicate key or one client to fail. That race window is the core problem UPSERT addresses.

UPSERT advantage

INSERT ... ON CONFLICT performs the conflict check and resolution in one atomic operation, eliminating the fragile time window between SELECT and INSERT.

CTE chaining advantage

A writeable CTE that inserts a parent and then uses RETURNING to insert children does both steps in one statement, preventing partial writes or the need for manual transaction management across multiple statements.

Pro tip
Simulate concurrent writers during testing to verify your DO UPDATE logic does not inadvertently overwrite legitimate concurrent changes.
