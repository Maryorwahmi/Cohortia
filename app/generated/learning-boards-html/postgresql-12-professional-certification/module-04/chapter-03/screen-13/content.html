Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Transaction
A logical unit of work that commits or rolls back as one.
ACID
Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability protect integrity and recovery.
WAL & MVCC
WAL supports atomicity/durability; MVCC supports isolation via snapshots.
Summary takeaway

Group related updates inside transactions, rely on constraints and WAL for safety, and use savepoints when you need partial rollback control.
