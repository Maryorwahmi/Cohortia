Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Quick Reference
BEGIN / COMMIT / ROLLBACK
Explicitly bound multi-statement operations to ensure atomic outcomes.
SAVEPOINT
Use for recoverable sub-steps and partial rollback without aborting the whole transaction.
WAL & MVCC
WAL supports atomicity/durability; MVCC supports isolation via snapshots.
Final thought

Design transactions around business operations, enforce invariants with constraints, and validate behavior by testing commit and rollback paths.
