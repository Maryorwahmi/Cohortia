Learning Objectives

Skills to explain, apply, and troubleshoot concurrency controls in PostgreSQL.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain concurrency necessity
Describe why multi-user databases need coordination to avoid lost updates and inconsistent reads using real examples like inventory updates.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe MVCC internals
Explain row versions, xmin/xmax visibility, and why VACUUM is necessary to remove dead tuples and control storage growth.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use locks correctly
Choose between row-level, table-level, and advisory locks and apply SELECT FOR UPDATE or FOR SHARE when you must serialize a read-modify-write.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Troubleshoot blocking
Inspect pg_locks to identify waiting and granted locks, and know how to resolve deadlocks by rolling back or changing lock order.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
