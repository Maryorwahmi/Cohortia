Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › FOR SHARE vs FOR UPDATE
Comparison
FOR UPDATE
Exclusive lock; blocks other modifiers and competing FOR UPDATEs; required when you will update the row.
FOR SHARE
Shared lock; allows multiple shared holders; blocks updates and deletes until shares are released.
Example
Use FOR SHARE to validate a condition across rows that must not change during the check. Use FOR UPDATE when preparing to write the chosen rows.
Misconceptions & tips
Don't assume share allows writes
Shared locks block writers; they are not a substitute for exclusive locks when you intend to update.
Performance tip
Acquiring only the necessary lock type reduces contention and improves throughput.
Decision rule

If you will update: FOR UPDATE. If you only need stability for checks: FOR SHARE.
