Lab Checklist

Follow these checkpoints during the guided lab to confirm expected behavior.

Task 1
Simulate FOR UPDATE blocking
Open two sessions, acquire FOR UPDATE in one, attempt same in second, observe blocking and unblock on commit.
Task 2
Inspect pg_locks
Use a third session to query pg_locks and correlate pids to sessions to find who waits and who holds locks.
Task 3
Try advisory locks
Acquire pg_advisory_lock for an identifier in one worker and show that another worker blocks or fails to acquire it with pg_try_advisory_lock.
Checkpoint
Record the pg_locks output when blocked: note granted=false rows and the relation or tuple identifiers shown.
