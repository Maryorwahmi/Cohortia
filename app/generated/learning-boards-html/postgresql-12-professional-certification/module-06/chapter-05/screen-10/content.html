Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Detecting Table Bloat
Which stats to inspect
n_live_tup, n_dead_tup, last_autovacuum, last_autoanalyze and relation size together reveal bloat symptoms.
Example query
SELECT relname, n_live_tup, n_dead_tup, last_autovacuum, last_autoanalyze
FROM pg_stat_user_tables
WHERE relname = 'audit_log';
If n_dead_tup rises significantly after DML and last_autovacuum is old, investigate autovacuum settings or blocking transactions.
When to act
Persistent high dead tuple counts or large table files that don't shrink after VACUUM indicate bloat requiring targeted remediation.
Misconception check
Large relation size alone isn't proof of bloat; correlate with dead tuple counts before disruptive operations.
Key Takeaway

Combine tuple counts, timestamps and sizes to diagnose bloat and decide if autovacuum tuning or maintenance is needed.
