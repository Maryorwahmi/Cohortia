Three practical rules
Keep autovacuum on
Disabling causes bloat and wraparound risk.
Prefer non-blocking fixes
Use VACUUM, REINDEX, or per-table tuning before VACUUM FULL.
Tune per-table
Adjust scale factors and thresholds only where necessary, after testing.
Final Takeaway

Monitor, tune selectively, and prefer low-impact maintenance to keep clusters stable and performant.
