When VACUUM isn't enough
Evaluate whether indexes or table files need rebuilding or a full rewrite is necessary.
REINDEX
Rebuild indexes to remove dead index entries. Useful when index bloat causes slow lookups and index size growth.
VACUUM FULL
Rewrites table and indexes to return space to the OS. Requires exclusive lock; plan downtime.
pg_repack (third-party)
Rewrites tables online without exclusive locks. Consider when you need to avoid downtime and VACUUM cannot reclaim space.
Avoid
Do not disable autovacuum. Using VACUUM FULL repeatedly as a substitute for proper tuning leads to recurring problems.
Key Takeaway

Prefer non-disruptive fixes and per-table tuning; use VACUUM FULL or pg_repack only when necessary and planned.
