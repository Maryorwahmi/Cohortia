What to remember
MVCC
Updates create new versions; old versions are dead tuples needing vacuuming.
Autovacuum
The daemon runs VACUUM and ANALYZE automatically based on thresholds.
Tuning
Tune naptime, workers, thresholds and cost settings; prefer per-table adjustments and testing.
Summary

Monitor n_dead_tup and last_autovacuum, tune where needed, and use disruptive operations only with planning and caution.
