Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Autovacuum Daemon
Autovacuum workflow
A background loop that wakes, inspects tables, and schedules cleanup work.
Wake interval
Controlled by autovacuum_naptime; shorter intervals increase responsiveness.
Workers
autovacuum_max_workers sets concurrent processes; large clusters may need more workers.
Tasks
Runs VACUUM and ANALYZE when thresholds are met, and performs freezing for old transactions.
Practical note
If autovacuum is too slow relative to write activity, n_dead_tup will rise. Increasing workers or lowering thresholds for busy tables often fixes backlog. Always test settings before production rollout.
Key Takeaway

Treat autovacuum as the primary cleanup mechanism; ensure it wakes frequently enough and has sufficient workers for your workload.
