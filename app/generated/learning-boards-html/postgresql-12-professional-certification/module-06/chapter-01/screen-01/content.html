Analyzing Query Performance with EXPLAIN
Learn what the planner proposes and how real runs reveal the truth
In this lesson
Interpret estimated plans, run EXPLAIN ANALYZE to collect actual times and buffers, spot sequential scans and join pain points, and validate fixes like indexes.
Why it matters
A wrong plan wastes CPU and I/O. Reading EXPLAIN and correcting statistics or adding an index gives measurable performance wins.
Explore Key Topics
EXPLAIN output
Understand estimated costs, rows, and plan nodes that form the execution tree.
EXPLAIN ANALYZE
Collect actual time, rows, loops, and Buffers to compare estimates to reality.
Diagnose & act
Spot Seq Scans, inefficient joins, stale stats, and validate index benefits.
Planner → Executor → Buffers

Executor

Buffers
