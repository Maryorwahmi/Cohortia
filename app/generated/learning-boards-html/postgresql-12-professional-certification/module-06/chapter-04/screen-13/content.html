Integrating with external monitoring

Export pg_stat_* metrics into dashboards for history, alerting, and cross-system correlation.

Examples: Prometheus, Grafana, Zabbix, Datadog
What they collect

Exporters poll pg_stat_* views and push metrics into a time-series store for graphing and alerting.

Why it helps

You gain historical context, correlation with OS metrics, and alerts that reduce mean time to detection.

Caution

Understand metric semantics and avoid noisy alerting — tune thresholds to meaningful patterns derived from your workload.

Key Takeaway

External tools provide history and alerts but rely on correct mapping to pg_stat_* metrics; use them to complement, not replace, direct inspection with pg_stat_activity and pg_stat_statements.
