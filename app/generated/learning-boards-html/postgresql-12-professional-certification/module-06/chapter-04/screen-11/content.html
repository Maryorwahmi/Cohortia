Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Logs & slow queries
Configuration tip
# Example setting in postgresql.conf
log_min_duration_statement = 1000  -- log statements taking longer than 1 second
Practical guidance

Choose a threshold that captures meaningful slow queries without creating excessive logs. Rotate and archive logs so they remain manageable.

Tools

Use log parsers to summarize slow queries and warnings. pgBadger is commonly used to convert logs into readable reports that speed diagnosis.

Key Takeaway

Logs are a persistent record. Use thresholds and parsers to turn them into actionable information without overwhelming storage.
