Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Why proactive monitoring matters
Definition

Proactive monitoring

Collect runtime and aggregated data so you can detect blocking, spikes, or cumulative cost before users notice.

Concrete example

An app reports intermittent timeouts. pg_stat_activity shows a backend waiting for a lock; pg_stat_statements shows a query pattern with high total_time caused by frequent calls. Together they reveal a hotspot to optimize.

Common misconception

Only long-running queries matter — false. Many short, frequent queries can consume more aggregate time than a single slow report.

Key Takeaway

Use live and aggregated views together: live to triage, aggregated to prioritize.

Analogy

Traffic control

Think of pg_stat_activity as the traffic camera showing who is stopped at an intersection right now, and pg_stat_statements as a monthly report of which routes carry the most cumulative traffic.

Try this
First check
SELECT pid, usename, application_name, client_addr, backend_start, state, query_start, query
FROM pg_stat_activity
WHERE state != 'idle'
ORDER BY query_start;
