Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › pg_stat_statements analysis
Example
SELECT query, calls, total_time, mean_time, rows, stddev_time
FROM pg_stat_statements
ORDER BY total_time DESC
LIMIT 5;
Interpretation guide
  • total_time: cumulative cost — target for large-impact savings.
  • mean_time: slow per-call statements — target for plan or index fixes.
  • calls: high-volume queries — consider caching, batching, or application-level changes.
Practical step

After identifying candidates in pg_stat_statements, run EXPLAIN ANALYZE on the specific statement to inspect the plan and estimate the effect of possible changes.

Key Takeaway

Choose your ordering based on what you want to improve: total cost, single-call latency, or frequency-driven load.
