Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › pg_stat_* views
pg_stat_database

Provides per-database totals like transactions, block reads/hits, and tuple activity useful for overall health checks.

pg_stat_user_tables

Shows sequential/index scans and DML counts for user tables. Use it to find hot tables or those needing vacuum/analyze.

pg_stat_user_indexes

Reports index scans and tuple fetches to tell you whether indexes are used effectively or candidates for removal.

pg_stat_bgwriter

Tracks background writer and checkpoint activity to help diagnose I/O pressure and checkpoint-induced stalls.

pg_stat_replication

For replication setups, reports sender/receiver stats and is essential for tracking replication lag and throughput.

Practical drill

Start broad with pg_stat_database, then drill to table or index level to find the root cause rather than chasing symptoms in a single view.
