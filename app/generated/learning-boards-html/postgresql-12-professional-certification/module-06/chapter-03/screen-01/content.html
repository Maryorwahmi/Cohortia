Orientation

You will learn to recognize CPU, I/O, memory, and network bottlenecks and to choose configuration changes that match your workload type—OLTP or OLAP. We focus on shared_buffers, work_mem, maintenance_work_mem, effective_cache_size, wal_buffers, and checkpoint tuning. Emphasis is on safe testing, reload vs restart behavior, and monitoring the effects.
