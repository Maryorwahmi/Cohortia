Key Concepts — Part 1
Three compact concept cards to remember.
Bottleneck
Resource limiting throughput: CPU, I/O, memory, or network.
Workload Analysis
Classify OLTP vs OLAP and read vs write patterns to guide tuning.
shared_buffers
Postgres' internal cache for data pages; allocate carefully versus OS cache.
Summary
Always diagnose the limiting resource, choose a targeted parameter to test, and validate changes with representative workloads.
