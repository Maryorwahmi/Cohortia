Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Monitoring & Testing
What to monitor
  • Replication lag, WAL throughput, and pg_stat_replication metrics.
  • CPU, memory, disk I/O, and network latency.
  • Postgres logs and HA manager events for promotion actions.
Testing cadence

Schedule regular failover drills and switchover rehearsals in staging. Verify logs, observe client reconnection behavior, and measure real RTO times.

Practical checklist
  1. Set alerts for replication lag and WAL backlog tied to service RTO.
  2. Automate promotion with Patroni or Repmgr and verify leader election behavior.
  3. Run a staged failover, update PgBouncer, and confirm app-level success.
Common pitfall

Treating a single successful drill as complete validation. Repeat after upgrades and configuration changes.
