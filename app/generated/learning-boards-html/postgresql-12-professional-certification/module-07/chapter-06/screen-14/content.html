Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Operational Rules
Monitor Everything
Metrics and alerts should map to your recovery objectives so you get timely notice of issues.
Rehearse Regularly
Scheduled failover drills catch gaps and prevent surprises during real incidents.
Document & Secure
WAL archiving, secure replication, and up-to-date runbooks enable reliable recovery and governance.
Final takeaway

Tools solve coordination problems; operational practice makes tools reliable. Combine both to meet your HA goals.
