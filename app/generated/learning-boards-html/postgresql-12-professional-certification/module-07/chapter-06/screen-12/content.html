Infrastructure redundancy
Verify multiple NICs, redundant switches, and healthy consensus store nodes.

Replication & WAL
Confirm streaming replication, WAL archiving, and recovery tests.

Failover drill
Simulate primary failure, confirm promotion, and measure RTO.

PgBouncer routing

Security & Docs
Validate TLS for replication, restrict roles, and confirm runbooks are current and rehearsed.
