Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Network & Geo Redundancy
Network redundancy checklist
  • Multiple NICs and separate switches for DB nodes.
  • Multiple routing paths and redundant DNS entries for PgBouncer endpoints.
  • Test switch and interface failures to confirm failover behavior.
Geographic redundancy patterns

Use synchronous replication within a region for consistency and async to remote regions for disaster recovery, understanding the latency and failover trade-offs.

Security & validation

Secure replication channels with SSL/TLS, restrict replication roles, and validate cross-site promotions in staging to ensure clients handle longer failover times.

Key Takeaway

Redundancy reduces single-point failures, but cross-site designs must be tested for performance and operational complexity.
