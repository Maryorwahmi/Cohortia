Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › PgBouncer Failover
Step 1 — Primary failure

HA manager detects primary unreachable via health checks and replication status.

Step 2 — Promotion

An available standby is elected and promoted to primary by the HA manager, ensuring only one node becomes primary.

Step 3 — PgBouncer update

HA manager or operator updates PgBouncer’s [databases] mapping to the new primary and reloads PgBouncer.

Observability checks
  • Confirm HA manager logs show promotion and leader election.
  • Confirm PgBouncer reloaded and established connections to the new host.
  • Verify application reconnect behavior and absence of stale writes.
Common mistake

Reloading PgBouncer before the new primary is ready causes connection failures. Coordinate the promotion sequence and validate readiness before reloading.

Practice this flow in staging and capture timestamps for each phase to understand your RTO.
