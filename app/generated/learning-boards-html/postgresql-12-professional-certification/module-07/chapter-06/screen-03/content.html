Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Learning Objectives
01 Monitor & Alert
Track CPU, memory, disk I/O, network, replication lag, and WAL throughput. Define clear alert thresholds tied to RTO targets and test that alerts arrive at the right recipients.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Network Redundancy
Design multiple network paths and redundant switches to avoid a single point of failure that would render the HA cluster unreachable.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 WAL Archiving
Combine streaming replication with continuous WAL archiving to durable storage to enable point-in-time recovery and guard against logical corruption.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Documentation & Drills
Maintain runbooks and perform regular failover drills in staging to ensure that automation and people behave as expected during outages.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Mnemonic
"MEND": Monitor, Exercise, Network, Document — a quick checklist to validate HA readiness.
Use these checks as a pre-deployment acceptance gate.
