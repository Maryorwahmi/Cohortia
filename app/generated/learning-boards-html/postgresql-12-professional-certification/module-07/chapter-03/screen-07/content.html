Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Pros and Cons
Asynchronous

Pros

Low latency
  • Minimal commit latency on primary.
  • Simple operational requirements.
  • Good for high-throughput web and analytics workloads.
Consider if your RPO can tolerate a few seconds or minutes of possible loss.
Synchronous

Pros

RPO=0
  • Transactions are durable on at least two servers before commit returns.
  • Essential for applications that cannot lose acknowledged transactions.
Be prepared for increased commit latency and operational overhead to keep standbys healthy.
Quorum pattern
Use synchronous_standby_names = 'FIRST N (...)' to require confirmations from any N of a list of standbys. This reduces single-standby failure impact while keeping synchronous guarantees.
Quick tip
Measure additional commit time under real network conditions before switching to synchronous mode.
