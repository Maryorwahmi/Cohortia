Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Configure the Standby
Standby primary_conninfo
# On standby_server_a's postgresql.auto.conf
primary_conninfo = 'host=<primary_ip> port=5432 user=replicator_user password=mysecretpassword application_name=standby_server_a'
Ensure the application_name value exactly matches a name listed in synchronous_standby_names on the primary.
Operational notes
  • Restart the standby after changing primary_conninfo so the new name is sent to the primary.
  • Verify the standby's connection and check pg_stat_replication on the primary to confirm registration.
  • Check credentials and max_wal_senders if connection fails.
Mismatch example
If synchronous_standby_names references 'my_standby' but the standby reports application_name='standby_server_a', the primary will not treat that standby as synchronous and commits may block waiting for the expected name.
