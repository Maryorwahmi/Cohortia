Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Assessment Scenario
Two quick scenarios to reason through.
Scenario A
synchronous_standby_names = 'FIRST 1 (standby_a, standby_b)'. standby_a crashes. What happens?
Answer: If standby_b is healthy and streaming, the primary still gets confirmation from standby_b and transactions continue without blocking. Only if all listed standbys fail will the primary block.
Scenario B
pg_stat_replication shows sync_state = sync but replay_lsn lags. Why worry?
Answer: A lagging replay_lsn indicates standby replay or I/O issues. Even if the standby flushes WAL, slow replay or resource saturation can still increase commit latency; monitor LSN gaps and standby health.
Practice: for each scenario, list the monitoring checks and immediate remediation steps you would take.
