Replication essentials
Keep multiple copies, reduce downtime, and scale reads without changing application writes.
High availability: standby can take over fast when primary fails.
Read scaling: distribute read queries to reduce primary load.
Disaster recovery: create geographically separate copies for promotion.
In this lesson
You will compare physical vs logical replication, understand WAL, and learn how synchronous choices affect data loss and latency.
Concept map
Primary → WAL → Standbys; physical = byte-for-byte, logical = row/statement based

Logical
