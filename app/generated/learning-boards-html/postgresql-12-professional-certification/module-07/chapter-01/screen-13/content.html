Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Common Mistakes and Pro Tips
Common mistakes
  • Not setting wal_level to replica or logical before streaming.
  • Insufficient max_wal_senders for planned standbys.
  • Relying on replicas instead of maintaining archives and backups.
  • Assuming synchronous replication eliminates operational risk.
Pro tips
  • Monitor WAL send/receive and replay lag continuously.
  • Test promotion and failover in a staging environment.
  • Reserve enough max_wal_senders for growth and backup processes.
  • Place synchronous standbys with low network latency to reduce commit delay.
Pitfall to avoid
Configuring synchronous replication without redundancy can cause service stalls during network partitions; plan quorum and failover behavior carefully.
Key Takeaway

Operational discipline—correct WAL settings, monitoring, backups, and tested promotion—keeps replication reliable.
