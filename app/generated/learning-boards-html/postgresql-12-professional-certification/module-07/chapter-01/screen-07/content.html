Selective replication
Logical replication replicates data changes, enabling table-level or schema-level subscribers.
Use case: migrations
Stream changes to a new major version while both systems are writable in a controlled cutover.
Use case: analytics feeds
Subscribe to specific tables for downstream reporting systems without copying the entire database.
Concrete example
Replicate only customer and orders tables to an analytics cluster, leaving other tables out of the replication stream.
Common pitfall
Expecting DDL to replicate automatically. Logical replication focuses on row changes; schema changes may need manual coordination.
Key Takeaway

Choose logical replication when you need selective, cross-version, or multi-system replication and plan for schema handling and overhead.
