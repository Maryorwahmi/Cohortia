Reasons to replicate
Availability, performance, and recovery are the core motivations for replication.
High availability
Standbys can be promoted to reduce downtime after failures.
Read scaling
Route read-only queries to standbys to preserve primary write performance.
Disaster recovery
Standbys provide geographically separate copies that can be promoted if needed.
Key Takeaway

Replication supports availability and scaling, but it does not replace backups or eliminate operational trade-offs.

Concrete example
An e-commerce catalog can serve product pages from standbys to handle read spikes while writes remain on the primary.
Common mistake
Assuming replicas remove the need for point-in-time backups. Replication duplicates mistakes and deletions unless WAL archives and backups are maintained.
