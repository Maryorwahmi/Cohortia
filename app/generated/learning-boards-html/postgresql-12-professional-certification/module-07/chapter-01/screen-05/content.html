Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Primary and Standby roles
Role hierarchy
Primary records WAL and serves writes; standbys replay WAL and serve reads.
Primary
Handles all writes and streams WAL to standbys. Single-write source in native streaming replication.
Standby
Replays WAL to remain synchronized and is typically used for read-only queries.
Promotion
When promoted, a standby becomes primary and can accept writes.
Concrete example
A reporting standby handles analytics queries while the primary continues to process transactional writes for orders.
Common misconception

A physical standby is not writable; attempting writes will lead to confusion and data divergence. For selective writable replicas, logical replication is used.

Key Takeaway

Identify promotion candidates and monitor WAL lag to ensure standbys are viable failover targets.
