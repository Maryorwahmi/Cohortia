Practice
Runbook essentials
  • Pre-checks: LSNs, replication slots, and standby health.
  • Step sequence: promote, verify writes, update connections, rebuild standbys.
  • Verification queries and success criteria for each step.
  • Post-incident re-integration tasks and postmortem actions.
Communications
Define who announces a promotion, who updates configs, and who confirms recovery to avoid duplicated actions.
Drill example
Simulate a primary outage by stopping its service in staging, promote a standby, update a load balancer entry, verify writes, then rebuild the old primary from a base backup. Time each step and record issues.
Key Takeaway
Testing turns theoretical procedures into reliable operational muscle memory. Rehearse end-to-end with network, auth, and monitoring active.
