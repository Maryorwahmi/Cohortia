Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Key Concepts
Failover
Unplanned promotion due to primary failure; prioritize speed but expect tradeoffs.
Switchover
Planned role swap with write pause and catch-up to avoid data loss.
pg_ctl promote
Preferred command to make a standby primary in PostgreSQL 12.
Split-Brain
Two servers accepting writes leads to divergent histories; always fence the old primary.
pg_last_wal_replay_lsn()
Shows the last WAL byte actually applied on a standby; use it to choose the freshest node.
pg_rewind
Can resynchronize an old primary to the new timeline if histories are compatible; use with caution.
Summary
Preserve availability by choosing the freshest standby, promoting safely, updating connections, and re-integrating servers using conservative, verifiable methods.
