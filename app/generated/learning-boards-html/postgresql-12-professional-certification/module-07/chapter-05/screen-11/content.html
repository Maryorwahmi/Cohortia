Common Mistakes
Split-Brain
Never allow the old primary to accept writes while a standby is active as primary; fence or shut down the old host.
Promoting outdated standbys
Always verify replay_lsn. Promotion of a lagging standby causes data loss.
Incorrect pg_rewind use
pg_rewind requires compatible timelines; misuse can leave nodes inconsistent.
Prevention and checks
  • Fencing policy: remove network access to the failed primary immediately.
  • Automated LSN monitoring to pick the freshest standby under pressure.
  • Test pg_rewind in staging to learn its constraints.
Lesson
Conservative, repeatable steps and fencing are your best defenses during failover operations.
