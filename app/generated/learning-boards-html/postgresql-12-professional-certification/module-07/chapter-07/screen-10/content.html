Designing a Disaster Recovery Strategy
A clear sequence that maps objectives to architecture, networking, and testing.
Step 1: Define RPO & RTO
Establish acceptable data loss and downtime to guide replication and automation choices.
Step 2: Architecture
Local HA (primary + local standbys) and remote DR (asynchronous standbys) plus WAL archiving.
Step 3: Backups
Regular base backups and continuous WAL archiving to durable storage for PITR.
Step 4: Networking
Redundant paths, adequate bandwidth, and secure replication channels (VPN/peering).
Step 5: Monitoring
Alerts for replication lag, WAL archive failures, and server health.
Step 6: Runbook & Tests
Document procedures and run drills to validate RPO and RTO assumptions.
Key Takeaway

Design iteratively: define objectives, build the architecture, secure and monitor it, then test and improve.
