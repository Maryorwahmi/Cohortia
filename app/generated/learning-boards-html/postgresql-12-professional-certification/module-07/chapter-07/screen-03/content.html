Operational Objectives
Secure networking, monitoring, runbooks, and testing
05
Secure replication and networking
Ensure encrypted replication links, VPNs or private network peering and redundant paths to limit outage exposure. Mnemonic: lock + network line.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06
Monitoring and alerts
Track replication lag, WAL archive status, and process health with clear alerts before failures cascade. Mnemonic: gauge icon.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Runbook and documentation
Document failover, PITR steps, contacts, and verification checks so recovery is repeatable. Mnemonic: book + checklist.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Testing and drills
Schedule full restores and failover drills to validate real RPO and RTO and fix gaps. Mnemonic: calendar + stopwatch.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
