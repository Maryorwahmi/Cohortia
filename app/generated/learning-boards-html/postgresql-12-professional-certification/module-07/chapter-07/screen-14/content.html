Activity: DR Plan for E-commerce
Apply the chapter concepts to meet RPO and RTO requirements.
Requirements
  • RPO: max 5 seconds of data loss.
  • RTO: max 15 minutes of downtime.
  • Protection against single server and whole data center failures.
  • Ability to recover from accidental DELETE via PITR.
Suggested architecture
  • Local HA: Primary + 2 local synchronous standbys with Patroni for automated failover.
  • Remote DR: 1 asynchronous standby in another data center to protect against regional outages.
  • WAL archiving and daily base backups to durable cross-region storage for PITR.
  • Application proxy (PgBouncer) pointed by Patroni updates during promotions.
Testing
Quarterly failover drills and annual full restoration from backups with WAL replay.
Monitoring
Alert on replication lag > 5s and on WAL archive failures.
Validation
Measure actual RPO and RTO during drills and iterate architecture accordingly.
