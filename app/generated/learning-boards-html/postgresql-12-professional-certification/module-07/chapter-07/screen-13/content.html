Runbook Creation and Regular Testing
Document procedures and run drills to ensure recoverability.
Runbook essentials
  • Clear failover steps for automated and manual paths.
  • PITR steps: restore base backup and apply WAL to target time.
  • Contacts, responsibilities, and verification checks to confirm successful recovery.
Testing cadence and scope
  • Test at least annually and after significant infrastructure changes.
  • Include full restores, failover drills, and logical corruption recovery scenarios.
  • Measure true RPO and RTO and update the runbook based on results.
Key Takeaway

Testing is the only way to be confident your DR design works. Run full restores and failover drills, measure results, and iterate your runbook.
