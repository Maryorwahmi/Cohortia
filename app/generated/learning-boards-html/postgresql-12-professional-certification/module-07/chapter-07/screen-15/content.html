Key Concepts
What to remember from this chapter.
Disaster Recovery (DR)
Recover data and operations after catastrophic events using replication and archives.
RPO & RTO
RPO governs acceptable data loss; RTO governs acceptable downtime—both determine replication and automation choices.
WAL Archiving & PITR
Archive WAL and keep base backups to enable point-in-time recovery from logical corruption.
Summary
Combine replication, WAL archiving, secure networking, monitoring, and runbook testing to build a resilient PostgreSQL DR capability.
