Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Key Concepts (cont.)
Operational Reminders
Three practical controls to keep your DR plan reliable.
Standby roles
Assign standbys by purpose and configure mode accordingly for HA and DR.
Monitoring
Alert on lag and archive failures so you can act before SLA violation.
Testing cadence
Regularly run failover drills and full restores and record measured RPO/RTO.
Final takeaway
Operational rigor—role-based standbys, secure monitoring, and disciplined testing—turns DR designs into reliable recovery capabilities.
