Networking, Security, and Monitoring
Protect replication links and instrument metrics that matter for RPO/RTO.
Networking & Security
  • Use VPNs, private peering, or dedicated links between regions.
  • Encrypt replication traffic and restrict access with firewall rules.
  • Use strong credentials and rotate keys for replication and archive access.
Monitoring & Alerts
  • Monitor replication lag and last archived WAL timestamp.
  • Alert when lag threatens your RPO or WAL archives fail to upload.
  • Include archive integrity and base backup freshness in your checks.
Pro tip

Instrument tests that run automatically and report metrics so you can see trends in replication lag and archive health over time, not just snapshot alerts.
