Logical Replication
Replicates DML operations so you can select tables and support cross-version replication.
Publication & Subscription
Publication lists what to share; subscription pulls initial data and applies changes continuously.
Operational requirements
Set wal_level=logical, size slots and workers, ensure compatible schemas, and manage DDL manually.
Final takeaway
Logical replication gives flexibility and selective control but shifts responsibility for schema changes and resource planning to the operator. Use it when those trade-offs match your migration, reporting, or distribution needs.
