Validation Checklist
  1. Restore a logical dump to a sandbox, verify schema and row counts.
  2. Restore a physical base backup, replay WALs to a chosen point, and confirm data integrity.
  3. Apply pg_dumpall output to restore roles and tablespaces, and check permissions.
  4. Time each step and log the total duration to validate RTO.
  5. Document issues and remediate, then rerun the drill until it succeeds reliably.
What to record
  • Start and finish times for each restore step
  • Any missing WAL segments or permission errors
  • Manual steps that could be automated
Key Takeaway

Only a successful restore proves recoverability. Schedule and document restore drills, and use their timing to validate your RTO commitments.
