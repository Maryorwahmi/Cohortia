Process

PITR sequence

1. Take a consistent physical base backup (pg_basebackup)
2. Configure archive_mode and archive_command to copy WAL files to durable storage
3. On restore, restore base backup then replay archived WALs until the desired time
4. Test the restore into a sandbox and validate application-level data

Practical notes

  • WAL archiving increases storage needs — plan retention and lifecycle.
  • Missing WAL files mean you cannot reach the intended recovery point.
  • Simulate accidental deletes and test recovery to confirm procedures work.
Key Takeaway

PITR requires both a consistent base backup and reliable WAL archiving; do not assume either alone provides full recovery capabilities.
