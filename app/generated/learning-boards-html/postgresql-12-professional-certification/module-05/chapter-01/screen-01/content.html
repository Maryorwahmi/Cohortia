Backup strategies in PostgreSQL

Understand the practical differences and how to combine approaches for reliable disaster recovery.

Estimated lesson length: 12 minutes
In this lesson

Compare logical and physical backups, learn key tools, and plan using RPO and RTO trade-offs.

Why it matters

Choosing the wrong backup can make restores impractical or impossible under time pressure.

Chapter focus

Practical guidance: when to use pg_dump, pg_dumpall, pg_basebackup, and WAL archiving. Real examples and a hands-on activity to compare backup sizes.

Most robust plans combine both: physical for fast full recovery and PITR, logical for portability and schema-level restores.
