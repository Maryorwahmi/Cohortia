Quick definitions
Logical Backup
SQL statements or archives from pg_dump; portable and selective.
Physical Backup
Binary copy of PGDATA via pg_basebackup; fast and comprehensive.
PITR
Restore base backup and replay archived WALs to a chosen transaction time.
Final thought
Decide with RPO and RTO in mind, automate archive and backups, and validate by restoring. That combination keeps your data safe and recoverable.
