Decision factors
Low RPO & RTO → physical backups + WAL archiving + restore automation
Portability & migration → logical backups with pg_dump and pg_dumpall
Large data sets → physical backups are faster to create and restore
Run a restore drill, measure time-to-restore, and incorporate those timings into your RTO commitments. Remember that storage and network speed constrain both backup and restore operations.
Example scenarios
  • Several small apps needing migration: choose logical backups for portability.
  • Multi-terabyte, high-traffic cluster needing minute-level RPO: prioritize physical backups with WAL.
Key Takeaway

Translate business RPO/RTO into backup cadence and automation. Validate assumptions with timed restore drills and monitoring of archive completeness.
