Plan elements
  1. Scheduled physical base backups (e.g., weekly)
  2. Continuous WAL archiving with monitored uploads
  3. Nightly logical dumps for portability and testing
  4. Retention policies and periodic cleanup of old backups
  5. Automated restore drills and monitoring with alerts
Operational tips
  • Store backups and WAL archives off primary storage to survive hardware failures.
  • Validate every backup with a restore into a sandbox on a regular cadence.
  • Automate alerts for archive_command failures to prevent silent data gaps.
Key Takeaway

A layered backup plan balances speed, portability, and durability. Automate, monitor, and test restores to ensure the plan meets RPO and RTO commitments.
