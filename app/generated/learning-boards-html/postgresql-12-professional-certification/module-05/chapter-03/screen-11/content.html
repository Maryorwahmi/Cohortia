Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Parallel restoration
Example command
pg_restore -U postgres -d mydb_restore --jobs=4 /tmp/mybackup.custom
When it helps
Large backups with many independent table restores; a multi-core, high-I/O server.
Risks and tuning
  • Monitor disk I/O and CPU to avoid saturation.
  • Test different job counts in staging to find optimal N.
  • Not all archive formats or operations parallelize equally.
Misconception
Higher job counts always equal faster restores. In practice, I/O or lock contention can become the bottleneck.
