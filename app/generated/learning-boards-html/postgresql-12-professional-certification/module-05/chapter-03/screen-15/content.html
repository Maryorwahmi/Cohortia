Selective restoration
--table and --schema let you restore specific objects without a full restore.
pg_restore -l
List archive contents so you can plan a selective restore or create a use-list file.
--jobs=N
Run parallel jobs to speed restores, but tune N to your server's resources to avoid contention.
Summary takeaway
Plan your restore: inspect archives, choose selective or full restore, consider --clean/--create for object control, and test on staging before production recovery.
