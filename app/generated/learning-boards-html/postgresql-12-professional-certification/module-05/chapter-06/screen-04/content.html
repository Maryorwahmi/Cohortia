Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › What is PITR
Definition

Base backup plus WAL replay

A base backup provides a consistent data snapshot, and WAL archives supply the ordered sequence of changes applied after that snapshot. Recovery replays those changes to reach the desired point.

Analogy
Photo plus video: photo is the base backup; video frames are the WALs you play forward to reach a specific frame.
Common misconception
Some assume snapshots alone are sufficient. Without continuous WAL archives, you cannot restore to times after the base backup.
Key Takeaway

Both components are required: a consistent base backup and a complete chain of WALs up to the recovery point.

Teaching visual
Visual sequence
Base backup (consistent snapshot) → WAL segments archived continuously → Recovery replays WALs until recovery target.
Example constraint
If WAL segment X is missing between the backup and your target time, recovery cannot proceed past that point and will stop with an error.
