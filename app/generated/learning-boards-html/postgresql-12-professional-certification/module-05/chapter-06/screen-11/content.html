Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Common PITR failures
Missing WAL segments
Symptoms: recovery will log a requested WAL filename and an error when it cannot find it. Fix: confirm archive_command success, re-copy missing WALs from a secondary location, or restore from an earlier base backup with available WALs.
Broken restore_command
Symptoms: repeated failures to fetch WALs. Fix: run the restore_command manually as postgres and correct paths, credentials, or CLI usage.
Insufficient disk space
Symptoms: I/O errors or aborted writes during replay. Fix: free space or move PGDATA to a larger filesystem before retrying recovery.
Pro tip

Keep a secondary copy of WAL archives and automate checks for stalled archive_command runs; this reduces the risk of irrecoverable gaps.
