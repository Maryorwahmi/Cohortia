recovery_target*
Use time, xid, or named restore points to control where replay stops.
recovery_target_action
Controls promotion behavior after reaching the recovery target, e.g., promote or shutdown.
Verify & Promote
After recovery, confirm data and promote the server if you need write access using pg_ctl promote or recovery_target_action=promote.
Final takeaway
Operationalize PITR with runbooks, automated WAL archive checks, and routine tests so recovery is fast and reliable when it matters most.
