Point-in-Time Recovery (PITR)

Use archived WAL files plus a base backup to restore your PostgreSQL 12 instance to an exact timestamp or transaction. Reliable WAL archiving makes this possible.

In this lesson

A step-by-step lab showing how to stop the server, restore a base backup, configure recovery.conf, replay WALs, and verify recovered data.

Why it matters

PITR lets you undo destructive operations, recover from corruption, and meet recovery-time objectives when you can’t rely on a simple snapshot restore.

Quick example
If an accidental delete occurred at 2023-10-27 10:25:00 UTC, set recovery_target_time to one second before that moment to restore the pre-delete state. Ensure your restore_command can fetch those WALs.
