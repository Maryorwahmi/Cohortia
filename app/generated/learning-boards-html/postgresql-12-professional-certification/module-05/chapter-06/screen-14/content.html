Point-in-Time Recovery
Restore to a precise moment using a base backup and archived WAL files.
recovery.conf
Configuration file (PostgreSQL 12) specifying restore_command and recovery targets for PITR.
restore_command
Shell command PostgreSQL runs to fetch archived WAL files during recovery.
Takeaway
Keep your archive chain intact and your restore_command tested. These concepts are the minimum you must master to perform reliable PITR.
