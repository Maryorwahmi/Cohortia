Pitfalls and prevention

Missing global objects
Prevention: run pg_dumpall and restore it before per-database restores.
Wrong format choice
Prevention: use custom or directory for large datasets and selective restore needs.
Not testing restores
Prevention: schedule regular restore drills in a separate environment.
Insecure backup storage
Prevention: store backups off-host with restricted permissions and consider encryption.
Final thought

A predictable and tested backup procedure is the best defense against operational surprises.
