Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Inspect tar backups
List tar contents
Use tar -tf and grep to spot pg_wal and base.backup inside the archive.
tar -tf /mnt/pg_backups/base_backup_tar_*.tar | grep pg_wal
# and
tar -tf /mnt/pg_backups/base_backup_tar_*.tar | grep base.backup
Inspect plain backup
For plain format, look directly at the pg_wal directory and base.backup file.
ls -l /mnt/pg_backups/base_backup_plain_*/
ls -l /mnt/pg_backups/base_backup_plain_*/pg_wal
# Look for base.backup and WAL segment files
Validation tip

Always confirm the presence of base.backup and WALs in the backup artifact immediately after the run.
