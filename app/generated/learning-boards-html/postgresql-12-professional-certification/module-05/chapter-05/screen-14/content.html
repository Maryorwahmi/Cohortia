Best practice checklist
Practical steps to keep backups usable for Point-in-Time Recovery.
Use -X stream
Include WALs during the backup to prevent gaps.
Timestamp destinations
Avoid accidental overwrites by creating unique backup names.
Archive WALs
Configure archive_mode and archive_command and monitor archival activity.
Test restores
Periodically restore a backup to verify its usability for PITR.
Operational reminder

A base backup is the anchor of PITR; combine streaming WALs, continuous archiving, and regular restore testing to maintain an unbroken recovery chain.
