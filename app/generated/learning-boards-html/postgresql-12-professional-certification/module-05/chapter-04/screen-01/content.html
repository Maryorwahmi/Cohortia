Physical Backups & WAL Archiving
Understand how base backups and continuous WAL archiving work together to enable crash recovery and Point-in-Time Recovery.
In this lesson
  • What physical backups are and why they use PGDATA
  • How WAL records changes and where segments live
  • Configuring archive_mode and archive_command safely
Quick orientation
Think of a base backup as a full photograph and WAL archives as a continuous video. The photograph alone shows one moment; the video fills in everything that happened after it.
