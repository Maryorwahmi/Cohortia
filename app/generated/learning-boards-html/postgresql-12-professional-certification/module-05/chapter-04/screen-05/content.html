Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Physical vs Logical
Physical backups
  • Binary copy of PGDATA; fast to take and restore for large datasets.
  • Requires compatible on-disk layout and often same PostgreSQL major version.
  • Must be combined with WAL archives to recover beyond the snapshot.
When to choose

Choose physical for whole-cluster, large-scale recovery and low RPO when you can manage WAL archiving.

Logical backups
  • Exported SQL or data; portable and useful for partial restores or migrations.
  • Can be slower and heavy for very large databases.
  • Not sufficient for low-RPO continuous recovery alone.
Example decision
Migrate one table between versions: logical. Recover entire multi-terabyte cluster quickly: physical plus WAL.
