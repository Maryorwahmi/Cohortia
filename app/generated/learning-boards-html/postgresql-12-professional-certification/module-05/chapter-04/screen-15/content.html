External archive
Archive to a mount outside PGDATA with postgres ownership and ample disk space.
Robust archive_command
Avoid overwrite, return zero on success, and test manually as the postgres user.
Monitor continuously
Alert on pg_wal growth and archive_command errors to detect problems early.
Final thought
These operational rules convert theory into a resilient archive pipeline that supports PITR and low RPO.
