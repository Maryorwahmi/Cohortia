What archive_mode does
Turns WAL archiving on or off. Use on for PITR setups.
Important note
Changing archive_mode requires a full server restart to take effect.
Example postgresql.conf snippet
# postgresql.conf wal_level = replica archive_mode = on # After restart, completed WAL segments will be handed to archive_command for copying
After editing, restart PostgreSQL and confirm archive pieces appear in the archive target directory.
