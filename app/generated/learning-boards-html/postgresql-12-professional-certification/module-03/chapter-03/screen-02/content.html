Learning Objectives

Learn to assign, audit, and test role attributes and inheritance for secure teams and services.

01
Identify role attributes
Recognize SUPERUSER, CREATEDB, CREATEROLE, REPLICATION, BYPASSRLS and their effects on privileges.
Mnemonic: attribute table → capability vs risk
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Apply attributes with CREATE/ALTER
Set LOGIN, VALID UNTIL, and the administrative flags safely during role creation or alteration.
Mnemonic: create, then restrict
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Manage membership with GRANT/REVOKE
Use group roles to consolidate privileges and simplify audits.
Mnemonic: grant to group, not to many users
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Control inheritance and temporary elevation
Use NOINHERIT and SET ROLE to require explicit privilege adoption for sensitive actions.
Mnemonic: "assume when needed"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
