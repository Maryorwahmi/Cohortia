Security Consideration

Minimize blast radius

  • Limit SUPERUSER and BYPASSRLS to very few accounts.
  • Prefer narrowly scoped attributes for service accounts.
  • Use VALID UNTIL to enforce temporary passwords.
  • Include membership changes in audit logs and onboarding workflows.
Practical steps
- Review who has SUPERUSER and BYPASSRLS monthly.
- Use group roles to centralize privileges and reduce individual grants.
- Remove privileges with REVOKE during offboarding and record each change.
Common mistake
Granting SUPERUSER to troubleshoot permission issues without revoking it later.
Key Takeaway

Treat elevated attributes as sensitive credentials and manage them with the same rigor as system accounts.
