Key Concepts

Three compact reminders to use when designing and auditing roles.

Role Attributes
Attributes like SUPERUSER and BYPASSRLS change capability and risk.
LOGIN vs NOLOGIN
Separate login identities from group roles that hold privileges.
INHERIT / NOINHERIT
INHERIT gives automatic privileges; NOINHERIT needs SET ROLE for elevation.
Summary Takeaway

Model privileges as group roles, grant membership to login roles, and keep high-risk attributes tightly limited and audited.
