Process Flow

GRANT and REVOKE membership

Add or remove users from group roles to manage who inherits privileges.

Add a member
GRANT analytics_team TO analyst_amy;
Remove a member
REVOKE analytics_team FROM analyst_amy;
Auditing tip
Record membership changes in your identity management logs and tie them to onboarding/offboarding events.
Example & pitfalls
Example:
GRANT read_only_access TO analyst_bob; -- now analyst_bob inherits SELECT (if INHERIT)
REVOKE read_only_access FROM analyst_bob; -- access removed immediately
Pitfall: granting CREATEROLE to a junior admin inadvertently gives them the power to create roles and expand access. Keep high-scope flags limited and review them regularly.
Key Takeaway

Membership is the primary control point for sharing privileges; manage it with care and audit trails.
