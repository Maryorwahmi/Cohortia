Quick Reference

Keep these three rules in mind when you assign roles and attributes.

Limit SUPERUSER & BYPASSRLS
Treat them as emergency or maintenance accounts only.
Use NOLOGIN group roles
Centralize grants so single changes propagate cleanly.
Require SET ROLE for sensitive access
NOINHERIT forces explicit elevation, improving control and auditability.
Final Thought

Design roles with minimal attributes, centralize privileges, and require explicit elevation for sensitive tasks to keep access predictable and auditable.
