Role hierarchy patterns

Practical patterns for teams, services, and sensitive privileges.

Group roles (NOLOGIN)
Hold grants for teams, e.g., analytics_read.
Login roles
Individual accounts or service users that receive membership.
Service roles
Give REPLICATION or limited attributes, not SUPERUSER.
Example hierarchy
analytics_read (NOLOGIN) ← grants SELECT on reporting tables analytics_team (NOLOGIN) ← aggregates multiple capabilities analyst_jane (LOGIN) ← member of analytics_read
Misconception: more nested groups equal better re-use. In practice, shallow, well-named groups are easier to audit and reason about than deep nesting.
Key Takeaway

Design groups to match organizational roles, centralize grants, and minimize per-user exceptions.
