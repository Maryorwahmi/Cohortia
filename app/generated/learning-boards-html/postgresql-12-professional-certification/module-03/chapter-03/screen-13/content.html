Check Your Understanding

Assessment ideas and scenarios

Scenario 1
An ETL account needs temporary temp tables and replication slots, no DB creation.
Appropriate attributes: temporary table creation and REPLICATION; avoid CREATEDB and CREATEROLE.
Scenario 2
A team lead with NOINHERIT needs to run a report from a team role.
Action: execute SET ROLE team_role to assume the privileges temporarily.
Use these scenarios as lab tasks: implement the roles, attempt the actions, observe failures, then grant or SET ROLE and observe success. This pattern is the most reliable way to internalize role behavior.
