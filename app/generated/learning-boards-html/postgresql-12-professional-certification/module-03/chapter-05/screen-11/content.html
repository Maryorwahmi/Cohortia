Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Best Practices
Practical checklist
  • - Principle of Least Privilege: avoid ALL or SUPERUSER grants when not required.
  • - Strong password policies and scram-sha-256 to protect role credentials.
  • - Secure connections: require SSL/TLS and use pg_hba.conf hostssl entries for remote clients.
  • - Network-level controls: permit only trusted application servers or IP ranges.
  • - Regular auditing and logging for failed logins, privilege changes, and policy reconfigurations.
  • - Keep PostgreSQL updated and maintain tested backups for recovery.
Operational tips
- Document roles with BYPASSRLS and review them periodically.
- Automate tests that verify RLS policies after schema changes or patching.
- Use role separation: administrative tasks on separate accounts from application roles.
Key Takeaway

Security is layered: combine RLS with authentication, encryption, network controls, auditing, and backups for a resilient posture.
