Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Key Concepts
USING clause
Condition that determines which rows are visible or modifiable for certain commands.
WITH CHECK clause
Ensures inserts or updates produce rows that meet the policy before writing.
BYPASSRLS
Role attribute that exempts a role from all RLS policies; restrict and audit its use.
Final Summary

Use explicit USING and WITH CHECK predicates, limit BYPASSRLS, and pair RLS with least privilege, secure connections, logging, and backups for robust security.
