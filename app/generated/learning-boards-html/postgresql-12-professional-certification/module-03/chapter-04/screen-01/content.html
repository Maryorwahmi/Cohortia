Authentication Methods in PostgreSQL 12
Control who may connect and how their identity is verified using pg_hba.conf rules.
In this lesson

Locate pg_hba.conf, read its TYPE/DATABASE/USER/ADDRESS/METHOD structure, and pick secure methods for different scenarios.

Why it matters

A single wrong rule can allow open access or block administrators; ordering and method choice are security-critical.

Concept Map
pg_hba.conf

Ordered rules control connection type, database, user, client address, and method. First match wins.

Danger

Placing a permissive trust or broad host rule above specific rules is a common and severe mistake.
