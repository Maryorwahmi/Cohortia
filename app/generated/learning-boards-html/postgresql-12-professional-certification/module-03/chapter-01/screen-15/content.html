What to Remember

Three compact reminders to apply every time you manage roles.

Create explicitly
Use CREATE ROLE with explicit attributes and strong passwords for login roles.
Contain fast
Use ALTER ROLE NOLOGIN to quickly prevent access while you investigate incidents.
Clean removal
Run REASSIGN OWNED and DROP OWNED before DROP ROLE to avoid orphaned objects.
One-line takeaway

Design roles deliberately, protect credentials, and remove roles cleanly to keep your PostgreSQL environment secure and maintainable.
