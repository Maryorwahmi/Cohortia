Comparison

CREATEDB, CREATEROLE, SUPERUSER

Grant narrow privileges when possible. Only use SUPERUSER for tasks that truly require full control.
CREATEDB: allows creating new databases.
CREATEROLE: allows creating and managing roles.
SUPERUSER: bypasses privilege checks and RLS; high risk if compromised.
Practitioner guidance
Best practice
Assign only the minimum attributes needed for a role's responsibilities; review and remove elevated rights regularly.
Common mistake
Granting SUPERUSER for convenience rather than assigning CREATEDB or CREATEROLE as required.
Key Takeaway

Use attribute-specific grants and reserve SUPERUSER for essential platform-level administrative tasks only.
