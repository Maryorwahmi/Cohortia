Key Concepts

Three compact concepts to keep in mind when managing PostgreSQL roles and users.

Role vs User
A role with LOGIN is a user; without LOGIN it's a group/privilege container.
Create and Alter
Use CREATE ROLE and ALTER ROLE to set LOGIN, PASSWORD, VALID UNTIL, and privilege attributes.
Cleanup & Privilege Hygiene
Reassign or DROP OWNED before DROP ROLE; prefer narrow attributes over SUPERUSER.
Summary

Treat roles as flexible identities: assign LOGIN only when needed, protect credentials, and remove roles cleanly to maintain a secure and tidy database.
