Process Flow

Reassign, drop, then remove

Plan ownership transfer and privilege removal before dropping the role to keep schema consistent.

Commands
-- Transfer ownership to new_owner
REASSIGN OWNED BY old_role TO new_owner;

-- Remove privileges owned by old_role
DROP OWNED BY old_role;

-- Finally remove the role
DROP ROLE old_role;
Checklist
Step 1
Decide where to move owned objects to avoid losing access to critical schema objects.
Common mistake
Dropping the role first and then discovering orphaned objects that create maintenance overhead.
Key Takeaway

Always reassign or drop owned objects before DROP ROLE and verify ownership after each command.
