Definition

LOGIN vs NOLOGIN

LOGIN gives a role connection capability and requires authentication. NOLOGIN keeps a role as a privilege container only.

Quick examples
CREATE ROLE analyst_john WITH LOGIN PASSWORD '...' → can connect.
CREATE ROLE analytics_team; → cannot connect but can hold privileges.
Misconception
NOLOGIN does not revoke privileges. A NOLOGIN role can still own objects or hold grants; it just cannot authenticate to the server.
Practical check
Action
Use a non-superuser session to attempt psql connection as the role to validate LOGIN behavior.
Key Takeaway

Choose LOGIN only for roles that must authenticate; use NOLOGIN for privilege grouping.
