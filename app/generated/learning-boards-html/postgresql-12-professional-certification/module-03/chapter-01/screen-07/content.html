Comparison

MD5 vs SCRAM-SHA-256

SCRAM-SHA-256 offers stronger protection than MD5. When available, prefer SCRAM for hashing stored passwords.

Why it matters
Stronger hashing reduces the risk that leaked hashes can be reversed or used for offline attacks.
Practical rule
Always use unique, strong passwords for login roles and rotate them when necessary.
Scenario and response
Compromise response
If a login role is suspected compromised, either ALTER ROLE to set a new password or ALTER ROLE NOLOGIN to lock the account immediately.
Key Takeaway

Hashing is important, but strong password entropy and rotation policies are equally critical.
