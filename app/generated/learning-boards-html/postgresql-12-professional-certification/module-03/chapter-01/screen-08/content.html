ALTER ROLE examples

ALTER ROLE updates an existing role's attributes without recreating it. Use it for password rotation and temporary locks.

Code examples
-- Rotate a password
ALTER ROLE app_user WITH PASSWORD 'NewSecurePassword456!';

-- Temporarily disable login
ALTER ROLE app_user NOLOGIN;

-- Re-enable login
ALTER ROLE app_user LOGIN;
Cards
Misconception
NOLOGIN is not a permission revocation; privileges remain assigned, only connection capability is removed.
Pro tip
Use NOLOGIN for faster incident containment than immediately rotating passwords in some scenarios.
Key Takeaway

ALTER ROLE is your primary tool for lifecycle and incident management on roles—use it deliberately and verify results.
