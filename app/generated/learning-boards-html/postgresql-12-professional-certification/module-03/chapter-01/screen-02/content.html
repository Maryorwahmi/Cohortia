Learning Objectives

These objectives guide hands-on practice: create roles, manage login and passwords, and cleanly remove roles without leaving orphaned objects.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
01
Differentiate roles and users

Explain how a role becomes a login-capable user and when a role should be a group container.

Mnemonic: a single object, two behaviors.
02
Create and secure login roles

Use CREATE ROLE with LOGIN and strong passwords; understand the password hashing options.

Mnemonic: create, authenticate, hash.
03
Modify and disable accounts

Use ALTER ROLE to rotate passwords or toggle LOGIN to lock accounts temporarily.

Mnemonic: alter, lock, restore.
04
Cleanup and least privilege

Reassign or drop owned objects before DROP ROLE and avoid granting SUPERUSER unless necessary.

Mnemonic: reassign, drop, minimal rights.
