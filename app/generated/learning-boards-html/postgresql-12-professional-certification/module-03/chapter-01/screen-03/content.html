Practice-Focused Objectives

Each objective becomes a task: create, verify, modify, and safely remove roles. Use these tasks as study checkpoints.

Task 1
Create a login role

Use CREATE ROLE with LOGIN and a strong password. Verify by attempting to connect with psql as that role.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Task 2
Create a group role and add members

Create a non-login role and make a login role a member using the IN ROLE clause during CREATE ROLE or GRANT membership later.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Task 3
Modify and lock accounts

Use ALTER ROLE to change passwords or NOLOGIN to lock a compromised account temporarily.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Task 4
Clean up before removal

Use REASSIGN OWNED and DROP OWNED to transfer or remove objects before DROP ROLE to avoid orphaned objects.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
