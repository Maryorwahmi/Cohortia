Definition › WITH GRANT OPTION
Behavior

WITH GRANT OPTION allows the recipient to grant the same privilege to others. It is delegation of the right, not transfer of ownership. Delegation can create grant chains that are harder to track.

  • Grantee can grant the same privilege onward.
  • Grantee cannot revoke the original grant given to them by someone else.
  • Revoking the original grant may require CASCADE to remove dependent grants.
Concrete example
GRANT SELECT ON TABLE sensitive_data TO data_steward WITH GRANT OPTION; Now data_steward can grant SELECT to other roles, increasing administrative flexibility but also risk.
Common misconception
Assuming delegation is restricted or temporary. Delegated grants persist until explicitly revoked and may require CASCADE to remove downstream grants.
