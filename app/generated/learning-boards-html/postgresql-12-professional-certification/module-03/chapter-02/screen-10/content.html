Core Concept › REVOKE Behavior
Key points
  • REVOKE removes a specific grant; other grants may still apply.
  • Dependent grants created by WITH GRANT OPTION may require CASCADE to remove.
  • Inherited privileges from role membership are unaffected by REVOKE on the member directly.
Common mistake
Assuming REVOKE ALL PRIVILEGES removes access from a user that still inherits privileges via role membership.
Example
REVOKE INSERT ON TABLE orders FROM app_user;

-- To remove a grant and its dependent grants
REVOKE SELECT ON audit_logs FROM db_admin CASCADE;
After revoking, connect as the role and try the operation to validate the effect.
