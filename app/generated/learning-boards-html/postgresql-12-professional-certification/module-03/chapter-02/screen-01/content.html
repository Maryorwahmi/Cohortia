Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Granting & Revoking
In this lesson
Object-level privileges, GRANT/REVOKE, delegation options, and verification

You will see clear examples and terminal-style code templates to apply GRANT and REVOKE safely. We emphasize practical patterns that prevent over-privileging while keeping application workflows intact.

Coming up
  • Define object-level privileges and why they’re critical
  • Use GRANT for fine-grained access and ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES
  • Delegate with caution using WITH GRANT/ADMIN OPTION
  • Revoke safely and verify effective permissions
Why it matters

Proper privileges reduce attack surface, prevent accidental data changes, and align access with roles used by applications and humans.
