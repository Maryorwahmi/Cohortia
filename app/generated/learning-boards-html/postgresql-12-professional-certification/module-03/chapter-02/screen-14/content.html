Process Flow › Best Practices
Role design
  • Map roles to job functions and services.
  • Avoid granting to individual users; use role membership.
  • Limit WITH GRANT/ADMIN OPTION to trusted accounts.
Operational controls
  • Use ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES for consistent defaults.
  • Audit grants and memberships regularly.
  • Verify changes by testing as the affected roles.
Practical takeaway
Treat privilege management as a lifecycle: design, apply, verify, audit, and adjust. That discipline keeps access tight and predictable.
