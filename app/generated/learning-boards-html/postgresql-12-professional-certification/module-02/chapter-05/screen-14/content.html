Quick Reference
REINDEX CONCURRENTLY
Non-blocking rebuild of an index; preferred for production.
VACUUM
Reclaims space and helps maintain table and index health; not always sufficient to fix fragmentation.
pg_stat_user_indexes
Provides idx_scan and tuple statistics to inform index retention and rebuild decisions.
Bottom line

Monitor, measure, and maintain: combine targeted index design with periodic checks and concurrent rebuilds to keep systems performant and available.
