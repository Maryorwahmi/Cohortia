Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
INSERT & RETURNING
List columns, omit serial keys, and use RETURNING to capture generated ids or timestamps immediately.
WHERE & Transactions
Always preview with SELECT, use WHERE to scope changes, and wrap critical operations in a transaction for rollback capability.
TRUNCATE semantics
TRUNCATE is fast, resets sequences with RESTART IDENTITY, and may need CASCADE to affect dependents; it differs from DELETE in triggers and row events.
Summary

Follow explicit columns, WHERE checks, RETURNING, and transactions to perform DML safely and predictably.
