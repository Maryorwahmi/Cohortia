How to validate your DML operations
Bloom: Apply → Master
01
Use RETURNING
Retrieve generated IDs or changed fields immediately after INSERT, UPDATE, or DELETE to avoid an extra SELECT.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Preview and transaction safety
SELECT the rows you expect to change before COMMIT, and use BEGIN / ROLLBACK when uncertain.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Handle foreign key actions
Know whether deletion is restricted or cascades to dependent tables; delete dependents first when necessary.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Restart identities when needed
Use TRUNCATE ... RESTART IDENTITY to reset sequence counters for temporary or test tables.
