Module 02 › Chapter 2.2 › CHECK Constraints
Status constraint example
status VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'pending' CHECK (status IN ('pending','processing','shipped','delivered','cancelled'))
This CHECK guarantees status is one of the allowed values, guarding against typos from application code.
Concrete example
For quantity and price, CHECK constraints prevent negative numbers that would break downstream reporting or accounting.
Misconception
CHECK constraints are not transaction-aware across tables; they validate single-row expressions. For cross-row invariants consider triggers or application-level transactions.
Key Takeaway
Use CHECK for local business rules and enumerations; keep expressions simple and test them on sample data.
