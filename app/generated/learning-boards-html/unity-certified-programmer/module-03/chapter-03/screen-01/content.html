Physics Materials and Forces
Tune friction, bounciness, and force application to shape game feel and consistent physics behavior.
This chapter shows how to create Physics Material assets, assign them to colliders, and choose ForceMode options for Rigidbody.AddForce and AddTorque. You will practice creating a bouncy ball, a slippery plane, and a rocket that uses continuous thrust and impulses. Expect hands-on steps and code patterns that avoid common pitfalls so your physics feels predictable across machines.
