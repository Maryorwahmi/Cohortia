Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Common Mistakes and Debugging Tips
Frequent issues
  • Applying forces in Update causes inconsistent physics.
  • Using default ForceMode for instant effects leads to wrong behavior.
  • Overlapping colliders at startup cause extreme solver corrections.
  • Not testing combine modes when mixing materials.
Debug Tip
Log when forces fire and inspect velocity in Play mode.
Test Tip
Simplify to a single sphere-plane interaction to validate material settings.
Placement Tip
Ensure initial positions don't overlap to avoid corrective impulses on start.
Key Takeaway

Clear separation of input and physics, intentional ForceMode selection, and careful initial placement reduce unpredictable physics and make tuning faster.
