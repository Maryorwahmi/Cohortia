Bounciness explained
Bounciness sets restitution at collisions. Use high values for trampoline-like behavior and low values for damped impacts. Combine this with Bounce Combine to define how two materials share bounce influence.
Use Case
Trampolines, bouncy pickups, and rubber balls.
Caution
Expect differences due to mass and collision solver damping; bounciness alone does not guarantee identical bounce height across objects.
Concrete example
Set 'BouncyMaterial' bounciness to 0.9 and Bounce Combine to Maximum. Assign to a sphere and drop on a low‑bounce plane to see the sphere retain high rebound.
Key Takeaway

Bounciness controls restitution but combine modes and object properties determine the observed rebound; test and iterate.
