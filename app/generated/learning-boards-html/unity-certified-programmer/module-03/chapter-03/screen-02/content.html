Learning Objectives
From materials to force modes — what you'll practice and why it matters
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Create and configure Physics Materials
You will make materials that tune dynamic and static friction and bounciness to control surface response in collisions.
Mnemonic: small Venn of Friction + Bounce
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Apply Physics Materials to colliders
Assign the material asset to a collider's Material slot so surfaces interact as configured during runtime.
Mnemonic: timeline create → assign → test
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Utilize AddForce and AddTorque correctly
Apply continuous and instant forces to produce thrust, jumps, and rotation using Rigidbody methods.
Mnemonic: push (force), burst (impulse), twist (torque)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Choose and apply ForceMode options
Understand Force, Impulse, VelocityChange, and Acceleration so each action has predictable mass behavior.
Mnemonic: continuous vs instant, mass-dependent vs independent
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
