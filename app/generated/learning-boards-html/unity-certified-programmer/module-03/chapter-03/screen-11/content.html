Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › VelocityChange vs Acceleration
ForceMode.VelocityChange
Applies an immediate change to velocity and ignores mass. Use for instant, identical boosts across different masses.
Example: a mid-air dash that must feel identical whether the player is light or heavy.
ForceMode.Acceleration
Applies continuous acceleration ignoring mass. Use for consistent acceleration effects like controlled levitation or engineless drift.
Example: environmental acceleration that should move all objects equally regardless of mass.
Comparison Summary

VelocityChange is an instant, mass-ignoring velocity edit. Acceleration is a continuous, mass-ignoring acceleration. Choose based on whether you need an instant set or ongoing acceleration and whether mass should matter.
