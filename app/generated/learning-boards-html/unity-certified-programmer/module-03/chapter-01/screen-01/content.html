Introduction to Unity Physics and Rigidbodies
How Rigidbodies make game objects behave like physical bodies

This short module defines the Rigidbody role in Unity's 3D physics system and previews the hands-on examples you'll build: a falling sphere and a launched projectile. Mastering these basics prevents common physics bugs and enables believable gameplay.

In this lesson
Add Rigidbodies, tune mass and drag, and apply impulses from C#.
Why it matters
Physics creates natural motion and reduces manual animation work for collisions and movement.
Visual concept
Static
No Rigidbody. Collidable but immovable — ideal for floors and walls.
Dynamic
Has Rigidbody. Responds to forces and gravity.
Kinematic
Moved by script, can push dynamics but not be pushed.
