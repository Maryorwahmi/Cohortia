Use Gravity toggle

When enabled, the Rigidbody is pulled by the physics engine's global gravity. Disable it when you want custom vertical control or floating behavior.

Concrete example
Uncheck Use Gravity for a hover drone and apply upward forces selectively to simulate flight instead of falling.
Misconception
Disabling gravity does not automatically make position stable; you must manage vertical forces to hold an object in place.
Practical notes
  • Use gravity for most naturally falling objects for simplicity.
  • Disable it when you need controlled motion or custom gravity behavior per object.
  • Apply manual AddForce calls for bespoke gravitational effects or zero-gravity gameplay.
