Learning Objectives
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Understand Rigidbody role
Identify why a Rigidbody is required for physics-driven motion and collisions. This matters because only objects with Rigidbodies are simulated by the physics engine.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate object types
Choose between static, kinematic and dynamic objects depending on whether you need movement, CPU efficiency, or scripted control.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Configure physics properties
Tune mass, drag, angular drag, and gravity to match desired inertia and resistance for gameplay clarity.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply forces in C#
Use Rigidbody methods like AddForce and ForceMode.Impulse inside Start or FixedUpdate to initiate movement and avoid common scripting errors.
