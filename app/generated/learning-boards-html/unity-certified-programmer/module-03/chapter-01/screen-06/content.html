Mass — how heavy an object behaves

Mass influences acceleration under force and the result of collisions. In gameplay tune mass to match relative object expectations rather than strict physics units.

Example
A boulder with high mass requires strong pushes and will impart high momentum to lighter objects it hits.
Misconception
Mass is not a speed clamp. It affects acceleration for given forces, not a direct velocity cap.
Key Takeaway

Set mass relative to other objects to create believable momentum and collision responses.

Tuning checklist
  • Adjust mass while the scene runs to observe collision outcomes.
  • Pair mass adjustments with drag changes to shape stopping behavior.
  • Use mass differences to communicate weight and solidity to players.
Quick scenario
If a crate seems too floaty, increase mass or apply less force on interaction to make it feel heavier.
