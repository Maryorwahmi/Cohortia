Raycasting and Linecasting
Non-physical queries for detecting colliders, surface normals, and distances.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Raycasts are lightweight checks executed by the physics engine. They return detailed hit data and can be filtered with LayerMask for performance and control. Use them for precise hits, selection, and line-of-sight logic without a Rigidbody-driven projectile.
