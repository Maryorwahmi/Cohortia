Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Practical Use Cases
Shooting (instant hit)
Cast a ray from muzzle or camera. If hit, apply damage using hit.transform and show impact at hit.point aligned with hit.normal.
AI Line of Sight
Cast a ray from the agent to the player's position. If the ray hits the player and not an obstacle, the agent has sight.
Placement & Grounding
Use camera rays to find a placement point on scene geometry, and short downward rays to confirm grounding for characters.
Example
Place a turret at hit.point found by a camera ray, and rotate it to face targets based on hit.normal or transform positions.
