Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Debugging Raycasts
Quick checks
  • Does the target have a Collider component?
  • Is the object's layer included in your LayerMask?
  • Is the direction normalized and pointing where you expect?
  • Are you reading RaycastHit only after a successful cast?
Visual verification
Use Debug.DrawRay(ray.origin, ray.direction * maxDistance, color) to see the exact ray in the Scene view. Toggle colors based on condition to make state obvious.
Performance tip

Limit ray frequency and scope. Use masks and reasonable maxDistance to avoid expensive checks in dense scenes.
