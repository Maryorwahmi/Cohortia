Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › What is Raycasting?
Definition and mental model
Raycasting: an instantaneous query that reports the first collider intersected along a direction. No physics interactions are produced by the ray itself.
When to use it
  • Player look-at selection and clicking on objects.
  • Instant hit detection for weapons with visual-only bullets.
  • Line-of-sight checks for AI agents.
Common misconception
Rays do not apply forces or trigger rigidbody collisions. They only report information when they intersect colliders.
Key Takeaway

Use raycasts for fast, deterministic detection when no physical projectile or force is required.

Concrete example
Cast a ray from the camera through the mouse position. If it hits an object, change its material color to highlight it. The ray provides a hit point and the transform for identification.
