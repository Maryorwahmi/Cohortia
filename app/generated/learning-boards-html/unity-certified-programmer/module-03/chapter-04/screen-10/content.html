Linecast
Linecast(start, end, out hit, layerMask) checks a segment directly between two points and is useful when you already know both endpoints.
SphereCast
SphereCast(origin, radius, direction, out hit, maxDistance, layerMask) casts a moving sphere and detects objects that intersect that volume during the sweep.
When to choose a volume
  • When objects can be missed by a thin ray due to size or offset.
  • For sweep checks to detect collisions along movement paths.
  • When you need area-like detection for attacks or proximity checks.
Performance note

Volume casts are more expensive than rays. Use them only when necessary and limit their frequency.
