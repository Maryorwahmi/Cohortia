Primitive collider types
Box Collider: Efficient for rectangular objects like crates, floors, and walls.
Sphere Collider: Best for rounds and projectiles; simple overlap checks.
Capsule Collider: Common for characters to allow smooth movement over bumps.
Example: Player uses a Capsule Collider so stepping over small obstacles does not produce snagging.
Practical patterns
  • Combine boxes to approximate a table or complex crate instead of a mesh collider.
  • Spheres simplify contact math and can reduce jitter for fast-moving objects.
  • Capsules are forgiving on slopes and are stable for continuous character movement.
Common mistake: tightly fitting a primitive to every detail. Overfitting increases checks for little gameplay benefit.
