Learning Objectives
Master the practical skills needed to optimize Unity physics
01 — Fixed Timestep & FixedUpdate
Explain the fixed timestep concept and use FixedUpdate for physics. Verified competency: adjust a project's fixed timestep and observe fixed update calls.
02 — Layer Collision Matrix
Use layer filtering to remove needless collision checks. Verified competency: disable enemy-enemy collisions and confirm profiler improvement.
03 — Common Bottlenecks
Identify expensive colliders, too many Rigidbodies, and transform abuse. Verified competency: refactor a scene to use compound colliders and measure savings.
04 — Dynamic vs Kinematic
Choose Rigidbody modes wisely to save CPU and ensure correct interactions. Verified competency: convert an obstacle to kinematic and observe behavior.
Verified Competency Milestone
You can run a profiler, identify Physics.Simulate time, make a targeted change in layers or Rigidbody type, and demonstrate a reduction in physics CPU cost.
