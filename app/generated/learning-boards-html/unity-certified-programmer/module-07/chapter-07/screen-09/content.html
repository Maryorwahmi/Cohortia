Designing for desktop
Provide Quality presets that change texture fidelity, shadows, and post-processing levels.
Use multi-threading for CPU-bound work where safe and supported.
Test across a range of hardware, including lower-spec machines.
Example
Create Low–Ultra presets and expose them to players so they can tune performance on older GPUs.
Pro tip
Prioritize reducing draw calls and efficient culling to benefit both low and high end machines.
Misconception
Assuming desktop hardware eliminates the need for optimization. Even powerful machines can suffer from poor batching or expensive scripts.
Key Takeaway

Offer scalable settings and test across hardware; desktop gives options but not unlimited resources.
