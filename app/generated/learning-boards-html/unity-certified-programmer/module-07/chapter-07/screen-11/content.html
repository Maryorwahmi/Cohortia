Pre-deploy checklist
  • Profile on target hardware with a Development Build and Autoconnect Profiler.
  • Remove or conditionally compile Debug.Log and development-only code.
  • Validate Quality Settings and provide player controls for scalability.
  • Confirm Addressables/asset bundles do not load unused content at startup.
  • Set correct publishing flags and permissions for mobile platforms.
  • Test on older or lower-spec devices that represent your audience.
Pro tip
Automate a small post-build script that extracts the Build Report and saves it alongside each build to speed comparisons.
Key Takeaway

A short, repeatable checklist reduces last-minute surprises and gives confidence that a build is production-ready.
