Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Scene Management & Active GameObjects
Concept

How load mode affects memory

Additive scenes keep assets in memory and require explicit unloads. Single load replaces the previous scene and frees references owned by it.

Additive trade-off
Good for modular levels but you must track and release resources explicitly.
Pitfall example
A persistent audio manager kept references to scene-specific clips; unloading scenes did not free those clips until references were removed.
Practice
Create two scenes, load one additively and attach persistent managers. Unload the additive scene and capture snapshots to verify assets tied to the additive scene were released after clearing references.
Key Takeaway
Make ownership rules explicit: who is allowed to hold references across scene boundaries, and when they must release them.
