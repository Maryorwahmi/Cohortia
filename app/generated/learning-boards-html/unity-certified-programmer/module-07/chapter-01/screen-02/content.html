Learning Objectives

Each objective pairs a measurable skill with why it matters in shipping a smooth, consistent experience.

01
Differentiate CPU vs GPU limits

Recognize profiler signatures for CPU-bound and GPU-bound performance so you fix the correct subsystem. This matters because the wrong optimization wastes time and can make performance worse.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain draw calls and overdraw

Describe how many small draw calls and overlapping transparent objects increase rendering cost, and why batching or reducing transparency helps.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe physics and simulation costs

Explain why many colliders, complex mesh colliders, and high solver iterations increase CPU time, and when to simplify or change simulation mode.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Manage memory and asset loading

Understand how large textures, many loaded scenes, and retained assets affect RAM and load times, and why addressables and unloading matter.
