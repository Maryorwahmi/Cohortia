Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Physics Scenario
Activity

Create many colliding bodies and profile solver cost

Steps
  1. Spawn 100–200 physics objects and let them collide.
  2. Profile physics and FixedUpdate times.
  3. Replace some with kinematic or animated props and compare.
Observations
  • Watch solver iteration cost and contact resolution.
  • See if simpler colliders lower CPU time.
Reflection

If physics time drops after simplifying colliders or reducing active bodies, optimize simulation strategy rather than rendering settings.
