Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Physics Costs
Core Idea

Colliders, joints and solver work

Every active physics object adds to CPU solver cost. Complex shapes and many contacts increase iterations and time spent in the physics step.

Use primitive colliders like boxes and spheres where possible.
Reduce solver iterations when high precision is not required.
Use kinematic bodies and trigger checks for objects that do not need full dynamic simulation.
Concrete Scenario
Explosion with debris

Simulating hundreds of fragments as rigidbodies is expensive. Use pooled animated debris or fewer physics-enabled pieces to approximate the effect with far lower CPU cost.

Misconception

Physics cost is not just collision checks; contact resolution and solver iterations also contribute heavily in crowded scenes.

Key Takeaway

Simplify collision shapes and limit active rigidbodies to reduce physics CPU time.
