Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › GPU Scenario
Activity

Measure draw-call impact with a replicated scene

Steps
  1. Scatter 500 identical meshes, each with its own material instance.
  2. Profile and record rendering and draw-call counts.
  3. Switch to shared material or GPU instancing and measure again.
What to observe
  • Does rendering time drop after batching or instancing?
  • Does the CPU time for issuing draw calls decrease?
Reflection

If instancing reduces frame time significantly, draw-call overhead was likely the limiting issue. Consider batching or LODs for similar content.
