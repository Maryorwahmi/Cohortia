Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Overdraw
Definition

What is overdraw?

Overdraw is rendering the same pixel multiple times due to overlapping transparent or layered geometry. Each pass increases pixel fill work and shader cost.

Symptoms

High pixel cost in GPU profiler, many layers of blended geometry, worst on large-screen resolutions or mobile.

Mitigations

Reduce transparent layers, use opaque geometry where possible, combine effects into single passes.

Concrete Example
Particle explosion with many translucent sprites

Hundreds of overlapping translucent particles cause high overdraw. Replace many small sprites with fewer, optimized textures or a billboard system to lower fill rate.

Common misconception

Reducing triangle count alone may not reduce GPU cost if transparent layers still stack over the same pixels.
