Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Shader & Overdraw
Shader cost

Keep shaders lean

Reduce texture samples and complex lighting math in shaders. Where possible, precompute data or use cheaper approximations to lower per-pixel execution time.

Small wins
  • Remove unnecessary branches and expensive functions.
  • Use simpler lighting models for distant or less-important objects.
Overdraw

Avoid redundant pixel shading

Overdraw happens when pixels are drawn multiple times due to overlapping geometry and transparency. Prefer alpha clipping to avoid blending where it reduces redundant rendering.

Example

Swap blended foliage to clipped cards to reduce fill rate and avoid heavy overdraw.

Measure

Use overdraw visualization and fragment profiling to find hotspots.
