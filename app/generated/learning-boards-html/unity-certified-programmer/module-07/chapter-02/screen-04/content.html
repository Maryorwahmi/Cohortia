Module 07 › Chapter 7.2 › What the Profiler records
Definition
The Profiler samples subsystems every frame and records aggregate graphs plus a detailed timeline for each frame.
Example: A frame shows a large Scripts segment; expanding the timeline reveals that EnemyAI.CalculatePath is the costly method.
Misconception: Seeing a big "Rendering" bar does not mean shaders are the cause — check the timeline for batching or script preparation costs.
Why this matters
  • Sampled data points let you correlate spikes across subsystems.
  • Expandable timelines point to exact methods or processes to optimize.
  • Accurate sampling reduces guesswork and wasted effort.
Visual guide

Key Takeaway

Always expand spikes to their timeline; high-level graphs show symptoms, not the fix.
