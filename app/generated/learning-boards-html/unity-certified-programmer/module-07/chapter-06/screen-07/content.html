Attach and Break
Attach your IDE to the running Unity Editor so breakpoints pause execution and reveal state.
1. Attach to Unity from your IDE.
2. Set breakpoints in the gutter on suspicious lines.
3. When paused: hover variables, check Locals, add Watches, inspect Call Stack.
Stepping Commands
Step Over (F10), Step Into (F11), Step Out (Shift+F11), Continue (F5).
Practical tip
Use Step Into when you suspect a method's internals; use Step Over when you trust called methods and want to move faster.
Locals
Shows local variables in the current scope.
Watch
Track specific expressions or variables across steps.
Call Stack
See the chain of method calls that led to the current line.
