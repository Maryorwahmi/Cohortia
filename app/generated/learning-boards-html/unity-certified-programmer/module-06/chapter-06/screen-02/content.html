Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Learning Objectives
01
Evaluate reactive limitations
Explain where FSMs and simple behavior trees break down when many factors must be weighed together. This matters because brittle state models lead to unconvincing or unmaintainable AI for complex scenarios.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Understand Utility AI principles
Define actions, considerations, scoring functions, and decision evaluation. Knowing these lets you model decision trade-offs explicitly and tune behavior quantitatively.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design a simple Utility AI
Compose actions and considerations for a decision problem like attack, heal, and flee so the agent can prioritize correctly in varied contexts.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Implement in Unity with C#
Create ScriptableObject considerations and actions, and write a Decision Maker that evaluates utilities on a short interval to select and execute behaviors.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
05
Analyze trade-offs
Assess advantages and disadvantages of Utility AI: flexibility and nuance vs. tuning complexity and debugging difficulty, and performance considerations for many actions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06
Tune and debug utilities
Learn to log utility scores, visualize curves, and adjust considerations so the AI behaves predictably under different game states.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Deploy incrementally
Build one action at a time, verify its considerations and curves, then add more to avoid combinatorial surprises and make tuning manageable.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Measure behavior in playtests
Observe how tuned considerations produce emergent behavior, and use recorded utility logs to refine scoring for better results.
