Decision Making & Utility AI
Score candidate actions and pick the most useful one for the current context.
In this lesson
Understand scoring functions, build Consideration ScriptableObjects, wire AIAction assets, and implement a decision loop in Unity.
Why it matters
Utility AI produces more nuanced and context-aware NPC behavior than simple reactive systems, improving player experience in complex scenarios.
Practical focus
Work through attack, heal, and flee examples and learn tuning and debugging techniques for real projects.
Key Takeaway

Treat decision-making as a scoring problem: convert state into comparable utilities and choose the best action.

Visual concept
Actions, considerations, and scoring produce a ranked choice.
Action
Attack
One behavior the agent can execute.
Consideration
MyHealth

Scoring
Curve

Decision
Select Highest
Run the action with the top utility.
