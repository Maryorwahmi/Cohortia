Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Reactive vs Utility
Reactive
Finite State Machines
  • Clear states and transitions make behavior easy to visualize.
  • Works well when behaviors are mutually exclusive and simple.
  • Risk: state explosion when many interacting variables exist.
Common mistake
Trying to encode every combination of conditions as separate states instead of abstracting decisions.
Utility AI
Score and Select
  • Compute a utility score for each action from multiple considerations.
  • Actions are prioritized by numeric utility; the highest runs.
  • Flexible: add or retune considerations without refactoring flow logic.
Practical tip
Evaluate utilities at a fixed interval and only for relevant actions to reduce overhead.
Example scenario
A squad leader weighs health, enemy proximity, and reinforcements to choose between attack, heal, or retreat.
Misconception
Utility AI is not automatically smarter; it requires correct considerations and tuned curves to behave well.
Key takeaway
When choices depend on multiple graded inputs, prefer scoring over many discrete states.
