Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Performance Pitfalls
Pitfall: Excessive carving
Carving many objects every frame causes frequent NavMesh updates. Use carve sparingly and toggle it when needed.
Pitfall: High avoidance on all agents
High-quality avoidance scales poorly. Assign quality by importance and test with realistic counts.
Pitfall: Missing CompleteOffMeshLink
Forgetting to call it after manual traversal prevents the agent from resuming normal pathfinding.
Pitfall: Bad link placement
Ensure endpoints lie on valid NavMesh areas; otherwise pathfinding will report Partial or Invalid.
Key Takeaway

Design with role-based settings and selective carving to balance fidelity and performance. Test under realistic conditions.
