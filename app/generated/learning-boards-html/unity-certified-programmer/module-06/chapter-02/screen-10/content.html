Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Raycast Avoidance
Avoidance Steps
  1. Cast a forward ray with Physics.Raycast from the agent origin.
  2. Use a LayerMask to filter only relevant colliders.
  3. On hit, try rotated forward directions or cast side rays to find a clear path.
  4. Normalize the chosen direction and move using speed times delta time.
Debugging Tip
Switch LayerMask in the Inspector to include or exclude layers and watch Debug.DrawRay change color when a hit occurs.
Concrete Example
If a forward ray hits within three units, rotate the forward vector by forty five degrees and test that rotated vector. If that rotated ray is clear, use it. If not, rotate the other way and test again. This reactive approach keeps the AI moving instead of stopping or penetrating colliders. For taller obstacles, cast from a higher origin or use multiple vertical rays to detect variety in height.
Key Takeaway

Raycasts give a simple reactive method to avoid obstacles; combine angled checks and LayerMasks for better results.
