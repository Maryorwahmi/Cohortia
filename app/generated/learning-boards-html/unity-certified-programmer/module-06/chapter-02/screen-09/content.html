Rotation Patterns
transform.LookAt
Instantly orient forward axis to target. Quick and simple, but can tilt objects and cause snaps.
Quaternion.LookRotation + Slerp
Compute desired rotation from movement direction, then smoothly interpolate for controlled turning.
Keep Upright
Zero the vertical component of direction before look rotation to avoid pitching when target is above or below.
Concrete Tip
If your agent snaps when the player passes overhead, build the rotation from a horizontal direction vector and then Slerp towards that rotation. This preserves upright posture and yields a natural turn. Tune rotationSpeed in small increments while watching tuning results in Play mode.
Key Takeaway

Use quaternion-based rotations with smoothing for stable, natural orientation behavior and prevent gimbal-like tilting by controlling the up axis.
