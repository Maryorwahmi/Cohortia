Multi-ray Strategy
Cast rays at several angles when an obstacle is detected. Choose the first clear ray or compute a score that prefers directions closest to the original path. Normalize and move along the chosen vector. Tune ray count and angles to balance CPU work and navigation quality.
Angle Examples
Check 30°, 60°, and 90° left/right and choose the most suitable clear path.
Weighted Selection
Prefer directions that minimize deviation from the original target direction to keep the agent efficient.
Performance Note
Only perform the full sweep when a forward ray hits. Keep ray count moderate — three to five side checks are often sufficient. Use LayerMask to minimize unnecessary physics checks.
Common Mistake

Casting too many rays every frame can burden performance; trigger additional checks only when necessary.
