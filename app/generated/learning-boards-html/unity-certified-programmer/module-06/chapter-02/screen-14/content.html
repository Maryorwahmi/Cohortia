Vector Mathematics
Positions and directions are Vector3 values; normalize for unit direction and control speed separately.
Seeking & Fleeing
Seek uses the direction vector; flee uses the inverse. Use conditions to switch behaviors dynamically.
Time.deltaTime
Scale per-second speeds by delta time to compute accurate per-frame movement and ensure consistent results across devices.
Summary Takeaway
Combine direction math, delta time scaling, and clear decision rules to build reliable and testable AI movement systems.
