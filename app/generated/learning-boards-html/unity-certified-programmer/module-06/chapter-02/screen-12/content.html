Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › LayerMask & Debugging
Common Issues
  • Raycast ignores obstacles because LayerMask excludes their layer.
  • Raycast origin is inside another collider or below ground, causing missed hits.
  • Movement jitter near target due to tiny residual displacements.
  • Conflicting updates between physics and direct transform changes.
Debug Sequence
1. Confirm targetTransform is assigned and not null.
2. Visualize rays with Debug.DrawRay and check colors change on hits.
3. Log RaycastHit.collider and verify the collider's layer and tag.
4. Adjust LayerMask in Inspector to include obstacle layers only.
Key Takeaway

Systematic checks with visual feedback quickly pinpoint most movement and detection issues; tune LayerMask and origins first.
