Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Hands-on: SeekAndAvoidAI
Lab Steps
  1. Create SeekAndAvoidAI script and add public fields for tuning.
  2. In Update verify targetTransform is assigned before proceeding.
  3. Compute direction, zero the y component, normalize.
  4. Cast forward ray and on hit rotate forward by avoidanceTurnAngle or cast side rays to find a clear path.
  5. Move using finalDirection times moveSpeed times Time.deltaTime and rotate with Slerp.
  6. Use Debug.DrawRay and OnDrawGizmosSelected for visualization and tuning.
Testing Notes
Place a few cube obstacles on a dedicated layer, assign that layer to obstacleLayer, and watch how tuning changes behavior in Play mode.
