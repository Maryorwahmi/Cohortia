Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Seeking & Fleeing
Behavior Cards
Seek
Apply normalized direction toward target, multiply by speed and delta time.
Flee
Invert the normalized direction to move away from the target. Use conditions to switch modes.
Condition Example
If distance to target is less than a threshold, set isFleeing true; otherwise false.
Concrete Scenario
A guard cube seeks the player until the player gets too close, then flees back to a safe distance. Implementing this requires only a distance check and toggling whether movement uses direction or its inverse. Pair this with raycast checks to maintain collision-safe fleeing.
Key Takeaway

Seek and flee share the same vector math; invert the direction to change intent and add condition checks to switch behaviors.

Check Your Understanding
How would you make the agent slowly switch from seeking to fleeing instead of toggling instantly? Consider blending directions over time.
