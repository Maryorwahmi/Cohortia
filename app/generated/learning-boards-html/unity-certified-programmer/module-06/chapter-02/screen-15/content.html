MoveTowards vs Lerp
Choose MoveTowards for constant, clamped motion; choose Lerp for smoothing where gradual slowing is desirable.
Quaternion Rotation
Slerp between current and target rotations to create smooth, natural facing behavior without abrupt snaps.
Raycasting & LayerMask
Use LayerMask to target specific colliders and cast multiple rays when needed; visualize with Debug.DrawRay for fast debugging.
Final Takeaway
Combine reliable vector math, time scaling, appropriate helper functions, and careful raycasts with layered detection to build predictable and debuggable AI movement systems.
