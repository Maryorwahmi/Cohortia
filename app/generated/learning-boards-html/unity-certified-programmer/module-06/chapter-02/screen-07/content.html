Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › MoveTowards vs Lerp
Side-by-Side
MoveTowards
Moves by a max delta per frame. Prevents overshoot and maintains predictable step sizes when scaled by delta time.
Lerp
Interpolates toward the target based on a factor. Produces exponential decay when used with delta time and smoothing factors.
Practical rule: use MoveTowards for navigation and collision-critical movement, Lerp for smoothing and visual polish where constant speed is not required.
Concrete Test
Place an agent ten units from a target. With MoveTowards and max delta two, it will march two units per frame interval until arrival. With Lerp and factor 0.1 times delta time, it will cover a large portion early and then slow, never exactly reaching the target in finite frames without special handling.
Key Takeaway

Choose MoveTowards for consistent speed and collision safety; choose Lerp for smoothing and a gentle slow-down approach.
