Key Concepts
Remember these ideas as you design AI
Modularity
Perception, movement, and decision are separate systems.
FSM Structure
One state active at a time; use Enter/Execute/Exit.
Engineering Safety
Use delta-time, cache references, and add hysteresis to thresholds.
Summary

These practices give you predictable, maintainable AI that can be tuned quickly for gameplay.
