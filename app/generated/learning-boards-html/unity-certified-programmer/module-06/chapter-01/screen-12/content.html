Testing & Debugging AI
Practical editor techniques to validate behavior
Gizmos
Draw detectionRange with a wire sphere and show patrol point markers so you can visually confirm where transitions occur.
Selective Logging
Log state transitions and important events, but avoid per-frame logs to keep the console useful.
Edge Case Testing
Test missing patrol points, destroyed player references, and rapid enter/exit scenarios to ensure graceful recovery.
Frame-rate Checks
Validate movement across frame rates to ensure Time.deltaTime is applied correctly.
Why it matters

Visual testing accelerates tuning and prevents surprises during playtests. It saves time compared to trial-and-error debugging.

Gizmo Example
void OnDrawGizmosSelected()
{
    Gizmos.color = Color.red;
    Gizmos.DrawWireSphere(transform.position, detectionRange);

}
