Introduction to AI in Games & State Machines
Why simple AI patterns create stable, testable enemy behavior

Game AI focuses on patterns and systems that create believable, consistent NPC actions. Instead of trying to mimic human thought, we define states, transitions, and perception so agents behave in ways that matter for gameplay. Expect to design decision logic, movement, and sensing as separate concerns that together form robust behaviors.
