Designing a Simple FSM
Practical checklist for states and transitions
1. Enumerate meaningful states tied to gameplay goals — avoid micro-states for tiny visual changes.
2. For each state, write Enter, Execute, Exit logic so responsibilities are separate and testable.
3. Define clear transition guards, e.g. "playerDistance < detectionRange" or "timer >= idleDuration."
4. Add hysteresis or buffers to prevent rapid toggling between states when conditions hover at thresholds.
5. Draw scenarios to validate recovery paths and ensure no dead-end states.
