What is Game AI?
A functional definition for game development
Game AI is about building rules and algorithms that turn simple inputs into meaningful, testable behavior. It groups into navigation, perception, decision-making, and utility systems. Think of these as separate tools you combine: a perception system tells the decision layer what it can sense, pathfinding tells movement how to reach a location, and decision logic chooses the next action.
Example: Guard
Patrol between points, detect player within range, then chase. Simple rules produce engaging behavior.
Misconception
More complexity does not equal better gameplay. Predictability and tuneability often matter more.
Key Takeaway

Treat AI as modular systems: perception, navigation, and decision layers make behaviors easier to build and test.

Categories
Pathfinding
Navigate around obstacles to reach targets.
Perception
Sense players using simple range and line-of-sight checks.
Decision
Rules or scoring systems that choose behavior.
