Comparison Avoidance Quality & LOD
Compare the settings for player-facing and background agents to decide where to cut cost.
Player-near Agent (High Importance)
  • ObstacleAvoidanceType: High
  • targetUpdateInterval: 0.1s
  • decisionInterval: 0.2s
  • Full perception checks and raycasts
Favors accuracy over CPU. Validate for responsiveness.
Background Patrol Agent (Low Importance)
  • ObstacleAvoidanceType: Low or None
  • targetUpdateInterval: 0.5–1s
  • decisionInterval: 0.5–1s
  • Simpler logic, fewer raycasts
Favors CPU savings while preserving broad movement patterns.
Practical Tip
Change avoidance quality and intervals dynamically based on distance or a priority value, and measure the effect in the Profiler to tune thresholds.
