What to Remember
Three compact reminders for your lab and projects.
Object Pooling
Pre-create and reuse agents to eliminate frame-time spikes from Instantiate/Destroy.
Throttling
Use update intervals and events so agents do not all recompute heavy logic every frame.
Profiler First
Locate the hot path, hypothesize a fix, and re-measure to confirm improvement.
Summary

Optimize with evidence: pool to reduce allocation, throttle expensive updates, and visualize behavior to validate correctness and player-facing quality.
