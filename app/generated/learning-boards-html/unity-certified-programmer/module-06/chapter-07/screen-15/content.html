What to Remember
Three practical reminders for your implementation steps.
Reset State
Clear health, targets, timers, and NavMesh paths when returning agents to the pool.
Stagger Timers
Randomize initial timer offsets to prevent agents from updating in lockstep and causing bursts.
Tune Avoidance
Lower obstacle avoidance for background agents and check for visual artifacts during playtests.
