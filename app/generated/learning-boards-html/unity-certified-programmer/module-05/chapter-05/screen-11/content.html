Common Mistakes Troubleshooting

Mistakes & Performance

Watch for missing listeners, wrong spatial settings, unoptimized imports, and volume imbalance.

Missing Listener
Confirm Main Camera or player has one active Audio Listener; otherwise no audio will be heard.
Wrong Spatial Blend
Use 0 for UI/music and 1 for world sounds; mixing them causes inconsistent behavior.
Import & Memory
Use Streaming for long tracks; avoid decompressing long clips in memory.
Performance tip
Limit simultaneous SFX and consider pooling or prioritizing important cues.
Balance tip
Route sources to Mixer groups to control and balance music versus SFX and dialogue centrally.
