Hands-on Lab Objectives

Follow these concrete steps to apply the concepts in a small scene and validate your audio setup.

Lab checkpoint 1
  • Import background music as Streaming, use Vorbis for compression.
  • Import short SFX with Decompress On Load or Compressed in Memory when necessary.
  • Verify Audio Listener is present on Main Camera or player.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Lab checkpoint 2
  • Create MainAudioMixer with Music and SFX groups and expose volumes.
  • Route Audio Sources to mixer groups and test volumes.
  • Implement AudioManager to loop music and play one-shot SFX.
