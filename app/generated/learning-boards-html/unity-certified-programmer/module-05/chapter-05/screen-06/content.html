Min/Max Distance & Attenuation

Tune distances to match level scale so 3D sounds feel natural and balanced.

Step-by-step tuning
  1. Start with Min Distance at a small value (e.g., 1) so nearby sources are full volume.
  2. Set Max Distance where the sound should no longer be heard (e.g., 10–30 depending on scene size).
  3. Play the scene and move the listener to validate smooth fade between min and max.
  4. Adjust volume and consider a low-pass filter on distant ambient groups for realism.
Misconception: doubling distances without testing will not necessarily translate across different camera rigs or scales.
Example
A bell in a village: Min Distance 2 for close clarity, Max Distance 40 so villagers far away hear a faint chime. For a coin pickup use Min 1 and Max 15 so it’s local to the player.
Key Takeaway

Always tune Min and Max to your scene scale; test by moving the listener and listening for smooth attenuation.
