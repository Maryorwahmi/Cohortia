What to Remember

Key Concepts

Three quick cards summarize the essentials you will use most often.

Audio Listener
The ear of the scene. Exactly one active listener outputs mixed audio to the device.
Audio Source
Plays Audio Clips. Configure Spatial Blend, volume, loop, and output to mixer groups.
Audio Mixer
Group and process audio, expose parameters, and control categories like Music and SFX.
Summary

Use these primitives together to create balanced, efficient, and immersive audio in your game.
