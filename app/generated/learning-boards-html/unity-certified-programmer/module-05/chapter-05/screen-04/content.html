Core Concept Audio Roles

Audio Listener, Source, Clip

Understand the responsibilities of each to make design decisions that scale across scenes.

Definitions & relationships
Audio Listener: the active receiver; attach once to camera or player.
Audio Source: plays a clip, can be 2D or 3D, and exposes playback properties.
Audio Clip: imported sound file with import settings like Load Type and Compression.
Chain: Clip → Source plays it → Listener outputs to speakers.
Example
A MusicPlayer GameObject has an Audio Source with a background music clip set to loop and Play On Awake; the camera’s listener hears it when the scene starts.
Key Takeaway

Separate storage, playback, and reception to control audio behavior and routing cleanly.

Concrete scenario
Scene: player walks through a forest. Place the listener on the player. Attach Audio Sources to ambient triggers and animals with Spatial Blend = 1 so sounds change as the player moves.
Misconception: multiple listeners multiply sound — only one active listener matters. Use inactive listeners only for debugging, not production.
Check your understanding
If you hear nothing, verify there is one active Audio Listener and your Audio Source has a clip assigned and output volume set.
