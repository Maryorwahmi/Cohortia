Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Profiling & Troubleshooting Audio
Checklist
  • Open the Profiler and inspect Audio.Update / Audio.ProcessEffects for spikes.
  • Use mute and isolate on mixer groups to find which group causes load.
  • Check VU meters for clipping and inconsistent levels.
Fixes
Apply pooling, adjust import settings, move heavy effects to groups, and use culling to reduce voice count.
Test Targets
Always profile on the platform you ship to; desktop may hide mobile I/O or CPU bottlenecks.
Key Takeaway

Measure before optimizing. Use the Profiler and Mixer meters to identify the actual cause, then apply targeted, reversible changes.
