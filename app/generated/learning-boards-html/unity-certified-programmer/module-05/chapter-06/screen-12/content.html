Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Dynamic Music Systems
Layering
Play multiple stems on different sources and fade their volumes to create intensity changes without restarting the main loop.
Stingers
Play short phrases for punctuated events, then return to the layered mix.
Crossfading
Smoothly transition between variations using snapshots or scripted volume ramps to avoid abrupt musical cuts.
Key Takeaway

Design music stems and snapshots so musical transitions are smooth and context-sensitive rather than abrupt resets.
