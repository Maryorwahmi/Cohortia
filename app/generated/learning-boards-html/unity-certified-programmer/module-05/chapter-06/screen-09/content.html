Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Audio Source Culling & Bypass
Culling

Set Max Distance appropriately and choose rolloff curves that match how loud you want a source to be at range. Unity will stop updating sources past audibility thresholds to save work.

Prioritization
Mark critical sounds with higher priority and allow non-critical ones to be culled when many sounds play simultaneously.
Bypass Options
Use Bypass Effects and Bypass Listener Effects to eliminate unnecessary mixer processing on known clean sources.
Example & Test
If a distant ambient insect loop is barely audible, lower its Max Distance or reduce its volume and let the mixer handle the rest. In a stress test with many gunshots, check the Profiler for Audio.Update spikes and adjust culling and prioritization accordingly.
Key Takeaway

Culling and bypass flags are small but essential levers for keeping audio CPU predictable in dense scenes.
