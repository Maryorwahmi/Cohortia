Architecture

Pooling System

Pool Lifecycle
  1. Pre-instantiate pool at startup with N Audio Source objects.
  2. On play request, take a free source and configure clip and transform.
  3. Play and monitor completion; when done, reset and return to pool.
Sizing & Overflow
Pick pool size to cover expected concurrent sounds. On overflow either expand to a cap or skip low-priority sounds to remain stable.
Common pitfalls
  • Not resetting position, volume, pitch, and routing when reusing an Audio Source.
  • Mismatched pool size causing unintended sound drops or overflow allocations.
Key Takeaway

Pooling moves allocation cost out of tight gameplay loops and reduces GC pressure, keeping audio performance smooth under load.
