Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Key Concepts
Spatial Audio
Perception of location, distance, and direction in 3D space.
Occlusion
Muffling/attenuation when geometry blocks sound.
Mixer Effects
Group-level DSP like reverb and filters for global changes.
Snapshots
Saved mixer states you transition to for synchronized changes.
Pooling
Reuse sources to avoid allocation and GC spikes.
Profiling
Measure Audio.Update and mixer meters to find real costs.
Summary
Use groups and snapshots for global control, pool sources to stabilize runtime cost, and profile to verify your optimizations on the target hardware.
