Main, Emission, Shape, Color, Size — the essentials for most effects.

Performance: Trim & Share

Trim Max Particles, reduce overdraw, share materials for batching, consider pooling.

Control: Play & Pool

Use Play/Stop for simple control, and pool prefabs for frequent spawning to avoid costs.

Final takeaway

Focus on module choices, keep an optimization mindset, and control effects via scripts or pooling. These habits lead to great-looking, efficient VFX.
