Render modes overview
Billboard
Always faces camera, good default for sprites.
Stretched Billboard
Stretches along velocity for streaks.
Mesh
Renders geometry per particle; higher quality, higher cost.
Horizontal/Vertical
Useful for effects that must sit on a plane or wall.
Example
Spark streaks

Use Stretched Billboard with a small texture and velocity-based stretch for visually clear sparks with low particle count.

Materials and overdraw

Prefer simple transparent shaders and small textures. Overdraw occurs when many transparent pixels overlap and increases GPU load dramatically.

  • Share materials across systems to enable batching and instancing.
  • Avoid multi-pass or costly fragment operations in particle materials.
  • Use atlases for sprite variation while keeping a single material.
Performance note

Test with reduced Max Particles to check visual fidelity at lower budgets and avoid overdraw hotspots.
