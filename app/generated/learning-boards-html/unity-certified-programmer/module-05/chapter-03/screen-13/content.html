Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › ExplosionTrigger
Starter pattern

On input, raycast to find a point in the world. Instantiate the ExplosionEffect prefab at that point, call Play, and Destroy the GameObject after the particle system's main.duration plus a small buffer.

Example snippet
ParticleSystem newExplosion = Instantiate(explosionPrefab, hit.point, Quaternion.identity);
newExplosion.Play();
Destroy(newExplosion.gameObject, newExplosion.main.duration + 0.1f);
Pooling note

If you spawn many explosions, pull from a pool and schedule return instead of destroying. This keeps allocations stable and reduces frame spikes.

Sequencing secondary effects

For a smoke puff after the blast, start a coroutine or use Invoke to instantiate the smoke prefab after a short delay, e.g., 0.2–0.4 seconds, and let it play its own lifecycle.

Avoid
Destroying the parent GameObject immediately if it contains trailing systems; use duration-based cleanup per system.
Key Takeaway

Match cleanup timing to each particle system's main.duration and use pooling when spawn rates are high.
