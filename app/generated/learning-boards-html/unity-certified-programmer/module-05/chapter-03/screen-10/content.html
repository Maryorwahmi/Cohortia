Common strategies
  • Max Particles: trim to the minimum needed
  • Overdraw: use small soft textures and limit overlap
  • Culling: verify off-screen systems are culled
  • Batching: share materials to enable fewer draw calls
Pooling pattern

Pooling reuses GameObjects: pre-instantiate a pool of particle systems, enable and play them on demand, then disable and return to pool. This avoids allocation spikes and repeated destruction cost.

Example sequence
  1. Prewarm pool with N particle systems.
  2. On trigger, take one, set position, call Play.
  3. After main.duration, Stop and return to pool.
Performance focus

Always profile. Reduce particle counts before increasing texture resolution or adding per-particle lights.
