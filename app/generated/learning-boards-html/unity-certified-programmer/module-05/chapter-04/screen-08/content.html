Module 05 › Chapter 5.4 › Depth of Field
Definition

Simulate lens focus to guide attention

Control what remains sharp and what is softly blurred to emphasize elements and reduce background distraction.

Focus Distance
Distance in world units where objects remain sharp.
Aperture
Controls blur amount; lower values produce shallower depth of field.
Focal Length
Affects perspective and how blur is perceived across the scene.
Common mistake
Overusing strong blur can remove important spatial information and hurt gameplay readability. Use subtle blur to accent, not to hide elements.
Concrete example
Focus on an NPC during a conversation by setting Focus Distance to the NPC and slightly lowering Aperture. Background geometry will soften, keeping the player’s eye on the speaker.
Check your understanding
Identify a camera shot where selective focus would improve clarity and describe the focus distance you would try first.
Key Takeaway

Depth of Field guides attention; keep blur moderate to preserve spatial cues and playability.
