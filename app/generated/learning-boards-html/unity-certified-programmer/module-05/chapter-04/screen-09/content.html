Color Grading

Tone, contrast, and saturation for mood

Use Temperature and Tint for warm/cool shifts, Saturation to enrich or mute colors, Contrast to emphasize midtones, and Lift/Gamma/Gain for detailed tonal control. Choose HDR or LDR mode based on your range needs.

Temperature & Tint
Shift blue to orange or green to magenta to set warm or cool moods.
Saturation & Contrast
Increase saturation for vivid scenes, reduce it for gritty or desaturated looks.
Vignette
Edge darkening
Use Intensity and Smoothness to subtly darken corners and center the composition. Avoid heavy vignettes that cut off important UI or scene elements.
Concrete example
For a survival horror scene, desaturate colors slightly and raise contrast for grittiness. Add a medium vignette to keep the player's focus centered and heighten tension.
Key Takeaway

Color grading and vignette together shape mood and focus, but they should enhance—never hide—your art and gameplay clarity.
