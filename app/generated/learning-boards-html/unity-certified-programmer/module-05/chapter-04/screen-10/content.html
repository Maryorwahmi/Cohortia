Ambient Occlusion (SSAO)

Adds soft contact shadows for depth

AO darkens crevices and junctions to increase perceived contact and realism. Use a low intensity for outdoor scenes and a slightly higher one for interiors or cluttered geometry.

Practical tip
Turn down AO on wide open landscapes, and prefer it on close-quarter scenes where it improves detail definition.
Motion Blur
Simulate shutter movement
Use small amounts to smooth fast motion or camera pans. Avoid heavy blur in gameplay-critical scenes where responsiveness is required.
Concrete example
Add subtle motion blur during cinematic camera sweeps to feel cinematic, but disable it during player-controlled combat to preserve clarity.
Key Takeaway

Both AO and Motion Blur are powerful when targeted; always weigh visual gain against performance and gameplay clarity.
