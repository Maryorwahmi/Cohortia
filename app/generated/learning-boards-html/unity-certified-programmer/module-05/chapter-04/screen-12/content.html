Optimization Strategies

A set of practical rules to reduce cost while keeping most of the visual benefit.

Use only what you need
Disable effects that add little to the scene’s mood or readability.
Lower quality settings
Reduce resolution or sample counts for Bloom and SSAO on constrained devices.
Volume management
Localize expensive effects with targeted volumes instead of one heavy global profile.
Profile blending
Blend between profiles for smooth transitions, and ensure blending weights are reasonable to avoid heavy simultaneous processing.
Profile testing
Measure frame time with effects on and off on each target device to make data-driven decisions about which effects to keep or tweak.
Key Takeaway

Balance visual fidelity and performance: prefer subtle, well-placed effects and profile-based localization over a single heavy global pipeline.
