Anchors: point vs stretch
Pick anchors based on whether the element should hold position or resize with its parent.
Point anchors
Anchors at a single point (corners or center) make Pos X/Pos Y offsets relative to that anchor. Use for elements that should remain at a fixed offset, like a corner button.
Example
Bottom-right button with Pos X -50, Pos Y 50 stays offset from edges regardless of screen size.
Stretch anchors
Anchors stretched across the parent make the child resize with the parent. Left/Right/Top/Bottom become padding values rather than simple position offsets.
Example
Full-width header: anchors stretched left to right with Top and Bottom padding to control height.
Misconception check
Changing anchors can alter offsets. Use the anchor presets with care: choose whether Unity should preserve position or update offsets when you change anchors to avoid surprises.
