Event-driven vs Polling
Subscribe to performed, started, canceled events on actions rather than checking state every Update.
Example
Move.performed delivers a Vector2 when input changes; Jump.performed fires once when the button is pressed.
Practical notes
  • Use performed for actions that trigger gameplay responses.
  • Poll only when you need continuous, frame-precise sampling.
  • Unsubscribe on disable to avoid unexpected callbacks.
Key Takeaway

Event subscriptions make intent explicit and often reduce per-frame noise in your code. Prefer them for discrete and reactive inputs.
