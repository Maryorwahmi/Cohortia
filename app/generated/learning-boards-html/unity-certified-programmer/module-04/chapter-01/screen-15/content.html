Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Action
An abstract command like Move or Jump independent of device.
Binding
A mapping from a physical control to an action, including composites.
Processors & Interactions
Processors modify values; interactions define timing and press semantics like Hold or Tap.
Final takeaway
Use actions for intent, bindings for device links, and processors/interactions to tune feel and behavior. This layered approach leads to robust, maintainable input systems.
