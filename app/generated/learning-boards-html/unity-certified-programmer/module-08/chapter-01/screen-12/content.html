Security, Disconnections, and Performance

Practical strategies to keep your multiplayer game fair and stable.

Security
Always validate critical actions on the server and treat clients as untrusted.
Disconnects
Clean up orphaned objects, restore ownership or allow reconnection flows to maintain consistency.
Performance
Send only needed data, compress where possible, and use NetworkVariables for frequent small updates.
Common mistake
Allowing clients to directly modify authoritative values or relying on frequent unbatched RPCs.
Key Takeaway

Design with the assumption of malicious clients, plan reconnect behavior, and optimize data to preserve responsiveness and fairness.
