Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Playtesting
Design a session
  1. Define objective and tasks.
  2. Pick representative players and environment.
  3. Observe, record, and collect structured feedback.
Measurement
Use time-on-task, error counts, and Likert-style responses combined with open comments to capture both behavior and sentiment.
Example task
Ask a player to craft an item from a recipe and record success rate and completion time.
Follow-up
Turn validated issues into prioritized tickets and schedule regression playtests after fixes.
Key Takeaway
Structured playtests reveal user-facing problems that automated tests cannot and guide meaningful design fixes.
