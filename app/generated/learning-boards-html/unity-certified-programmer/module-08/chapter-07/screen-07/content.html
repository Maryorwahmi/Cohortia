Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › PlayerStats Tests
Test goals
  • Confirm start at max health.
  • Confirm taking damage reduces health correctly.
  • Confirm health clamps at zero and IsDead is true.
  • Confirm healing caps at MaxHealth.
  • Confirm negative inputs do not invert behavior.

Debug flow
Failing test should state the expected value and the actual value to speed diagnosis.
Misconception
Testing internal counters rather than final outputs leads to brittle tests during refactors.
