Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Bundles vs Addressables
Asset Bundles (low level)
  • Full control over packaging and bundle layout.
  • Requires you to implement mapping and dependency tracking.
  • Useful for very custom delivery pipelines.
When to pick this
Only when your build or runtime constraints demand a bespoke pipeline.
Addressables (high level)
  • Name-based loading and AssetReference support.
  • Automatic dependency tracking and a generated catalog.
  • Simplifies remote updates and refactoring of assets.
When to pick this
For most projects: easier maintenance, safer runtime behavior, and built-in versioning support.
Common misconception
Addressables always adds unacceptable overhead. In practice, it gives you control over grouping and compression to tune performance while reducing development complexity.
