Comparison

Compression formats

ASTC
Flexible block sizes, high quality for mobile where supported.
ETC2
Broad Android support, decent quality, less flexibility than ASTC.
DXT
Common on PC, good tooling support.
PVRTC
Older mobile GPUs and some iOS hardware, lower quality at small sizes.
Practical example
Replace large uncompressed textures with platform-specific compressed versions and provide lower-resolution overrides for distant LODs and particle systems to cut memory use.
Misconception
Assuming a single compression format is ideal for all devices; instead, provide per-platform texture import settings and test on devices.
Key Takeaway

Choose compression per platform and test visual impact on device; smaller textures often give the best performance wins.
