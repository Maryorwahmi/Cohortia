Why UniTask can be faster

UniTask uses value-type awaitables and avoids heap allocations in many common patterns, which reduces GC pressure compared to frequent coroutine yields.

Measure first
Profile allocations and frame times before refactor to ensure the change benefits your case.
Watch closures
Capturing heavy closures in async lambdas can allocate; minimize captured state in tight loops.
Threading costs
Switching threads adds overhead; only switch when necessary and switch back before touching Unity APIs.
