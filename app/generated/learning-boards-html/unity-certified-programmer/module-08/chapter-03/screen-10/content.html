Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Cancellation
Token lifecycle
Create in Start/Awake, pass token to every awaited call, cancel and dispose in OnDestroy.
Checkpoints
Call ThrowIfCancellationRequested after delays and before heavy tasks to stop early.
Handle OperationCanceledException
Catch it to update UI and skip further processing; this indicates an intended stop.
Test scenario
Run a scene load, destroy the loader GameObject during progress, and confirm that progressText shows "Loading Cancelled" and no background logs persist.
