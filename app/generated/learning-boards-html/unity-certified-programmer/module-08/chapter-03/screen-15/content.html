Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Quick Reference
Cancellation
Create/Cancel/Dispose your CancellationTokenSource and pass the token into every UniTask call to stop work deterministically.
Progress
Use Progress<float> with ToUniTask for SceneManager.LoadSceneAsync to update UI percentage while awaiting completion.
Threading
After background work, await UniTask.SwitchToMainThread before touching transforms, UI, or GameObjects.
One-line summary

UniTask gives you readable async flows with return values, predictable error handling, and better allocation characteristics than many coroutine patterns.
