Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Launch Checklist
Pre-Flight
  • Verify bundle identifier and version match metadata.
  • Confirm signed Release Build with production key.
  • Ensure privacy policy and contact info are included.
Day‑One Monitoring
  • Enable analytics and crash reporting.
  • Watch for jump in crash clusters or retention drops.
  • Prepare hotfix plan and team roles.
Community & Communication
  • Publish release notes and known issues.
  • Open channels for player feedback and debugging steps.
  • Be transparent on fixes and timelines.
Final Thought
A short, practiced checklist avoids last-minute mistakes and keeps player trust high after launch.
