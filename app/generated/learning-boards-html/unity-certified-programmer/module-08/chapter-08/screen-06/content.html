Stores & Requirements

Google Play (Android)
Preferred artifact: AAB. Requires bundle id, version code, signing key, screenshots, feature graphic, content rating, and privacy policy.
Apple App Store (iOS)
Requires provisioning profiles, capabilities declaration, app icons in required sizes, screenshots, review notes, and strict privacy and permission disclosures.
PC & Consoles
PC stores vary widely; consoles require vendor certification and technical compliance testing with a checklist of requirements.
Submission Packet
  • • Build artifact signed for the target platform
  • • Store assets: icons, screenshots, trailers, feature graphic
  • • Metadata: short & long descriptions, tags, category
  • • Age rating and content questionnaire
  • • Privacy policy URL and permission declarations
Common Mistake
Skipping thorough metadata preparation causes rejections or delays — stores often reject for missing screenshots, wrong icon sizes, or insufficient privacy disclosures.
Key Takeaway

Treat submission as project work: prepare assets, documentation, and device tests before starting the upload to minimize rejection cycles.
