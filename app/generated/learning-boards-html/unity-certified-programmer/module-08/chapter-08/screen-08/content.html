Process

Post-Launch Workflow

1. Monitor
Daily crash digest, key funnel metrics, and sudden KPI drops.
2. Triage
Classify issues by severity and affected audience, then assign priority.
3. Patch & QA
Build a hotfix branch, run smoke tests, and verify on affected devices before release.
4. Communicate
Post changelogs, issue trackers, and timelines to your community to maintain trust.
Scenario
If analytics show high drop-off on level 2 and crash reporting shows frequent exceptions on Android model X, prioritize a device-specific patch and a small content tweak to level 2 that reduces friction.
Pro Tip
Automate nightly release builds and a small suite of smoke tests so you can validate fixes quickly and reduce manual QA time for hotfixes.
Key Takeaway

Combine automated monitoring with transparent community updates to respond quickly and keep players engaged after launch.
