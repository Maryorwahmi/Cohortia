Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Key Concepts
GameObject
A named container with a Transform; inert until composed.
Component
Modular functionality: renderer, collider, Rigidbody, or custom scripts.
Composition
Compose behaviors rather than building complex inheritance trees for reuse and flexibility.
Final takeaway

Organize scenes by composing focused components on GameObjects, use Transform relationships intentionally, and prefer reusable components over inheritance to build scalable projects.
