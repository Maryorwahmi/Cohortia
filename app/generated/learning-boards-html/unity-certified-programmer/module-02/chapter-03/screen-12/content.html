Best practices
  • Organize folders: Prefabs, Scenes, Materials, Scripts.
  • Use clear, consistent naming conventions for scenes and prefabs.
  • Prefer Prefab Variants over duplicate assets for similar objects.
  • Minimize overrides; apply to asset only when change is truly global.
Avoid

Avoid creating multiple slightly different assets when a variant or override would suffice — it increases maintenance cost.

Checklist
Scenes in build?

Confirm every playable scene is added to Build Settings before packaging the game.

Prefab references assigned?

Ensure scripts that Instantiate prefabs have their prefab fields assigned in the Inspector.

Are overrides intentional?

Review bold fields in the Inspector and decide whether to Apply All or Revert All.

Final tip

Make small, reviewable changes to Prefabs and use variants for family differences to keep projects maintainable.
