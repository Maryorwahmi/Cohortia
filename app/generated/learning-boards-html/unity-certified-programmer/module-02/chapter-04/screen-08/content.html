Why LateUpdate

LateUpdate runs after Update. Use it for camera follow and logic that must read final transform positions for the frame to avoid visual lag.

Example: Smoothly interpolate the camera to the player's updated position in LateUpdate for stable visuals.
Contrast: Updating the camera in Update may compute positions before character movement is complete, causing jitter.
Quick checklist
  • Does this logic need final positions? → LateUpdate
  • Is it purely animation/UI? → Update
  • Is it physics? → FixedUpdate
Key Takeaway

Use LateUpdate for camera and any logic that must read stabilized transforms after all updates are finished for the frame.
