Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Update vs FixedUpdate
Update

Runs once per frame. Use it to poll input, play animations, or update UI. Frequency varies with rendering.

Example: Input.GetAxis in Update to capture user input every rendered frame.
Mistake: applying Rigidbody.AddForce in Update can cause jitter when framerate changes.
FixedUpdate

Runs on a fixed timestep. Use it for physics interactions such as MovePosition, AddForce, or altering Rigidbody velocity.

Example: rb.MovePosition(rb.position + dir * speed * Time.fixedDeltaTime) for stable movement.
Pro-tip: store input sampled in Update to consume during FixedUpdate for consistent control.
Check: Is this visual-only or physics-driven? Choose Update for visuals and FixedUpdate for physics.
Mnemonic: Sample in Update. Apply in FixedUpdate.
Why it matters: prevents jitter and inconsistent collisions across machines.
