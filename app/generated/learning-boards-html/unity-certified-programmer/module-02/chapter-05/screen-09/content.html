Collision based ground check
Use OnCollisionEnter to set isGrounded when colliding with objects tagged Ground and OnCollisionExit to clear it.
Raycast or sphere check
Cast a short downward ray or sphere to detect ground contact under the player; this is often more reliable on slopes or thin colliders.
Tip: Tune ray length or sphere radius to the player's collider size to avoid false negatives.
Concrete scenario
In a level with steps, collision events may flicker. A short sphere check under the character center gives a stable grounded result.
Misconception
Assuming collision normals always indicate ground will fail on thin geometry or edge cases. Combine checks for robustness.
Key Takeaway
Choose the ground detection that fits your level geometry and test on slopes and jumps to validate behavior.
