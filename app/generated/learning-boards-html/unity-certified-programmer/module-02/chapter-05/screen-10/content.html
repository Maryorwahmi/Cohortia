Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Mouse Look
Pattern
Read Mouse X and Mouse Y in Update, multiply by sensitivity and Time delta, rotate player around Y for horizontal look, update camera local X rotation for pitch and clamp it to prevent inversion.

Concrete steps
  1. Expose mouseSensitivity and a camera Transform in the script.
  2. Multiply Mouse X and Mouse Y by sensitivity and Time delta in Update.
  3. Use transform Rotate for horizontal and camera localRotation for vertical pitch.
  4. Clamp pitch between -90 and 90 degrees for comfort.
Misconception
Applying vertical rotation to the player object instead of the camera causes the whole body to tilt and breaks movement alignment.
