Camera Controls
Orbit
Right mouse + move. Rotates view around current pivot so you can inspect models from all angles.
Pan
Middle mouse or Alt + left drag. Shifts the camera without rotating so alignment stays intact.
Zoom / Dolly
Scroll wheel to zoom; combine with W/S keys for dolly motion when precise depth adjustments are needed.
Frame Selection (F)
Centers the camera on a selected object. Use it when an item is off-screen or too small to locate quickly.
Practice Drill
Step 1
Select the Main Camera in Hierarchy and press F to frame it.
Step 2
Orbit with the right mouse till you see the object from multiple angles, then pan to align it with the horizon.
Step 3
Scroll to zoom in, then press F again if you lose the object. Repeat until the sequence is natural.
Key Takeaway

Short, repeated drills build the reflexes needed to inspect scenes quickly and accurately.
