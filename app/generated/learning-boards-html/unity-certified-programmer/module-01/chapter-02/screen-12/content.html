Comparison and Logical Operators
Write clear conditions to control behavior at runtime.
Comparison Examples
  • playerScore >= 100
  • health == 0
  • playerName != ""
Logical Examples
  • isAlive && (health > 0)
  • hasKey || hasPass
  • !isPaused
Practical Guidance
Group comparisons with parentheses and avoid assignment inside conditionals. Test edge cases like equality and boundary values to ensure your conditions behave as expected.
