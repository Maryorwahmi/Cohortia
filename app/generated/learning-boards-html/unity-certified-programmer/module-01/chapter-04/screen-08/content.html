Core Concept

Access modifiers

Control who can read or change your fields and methods. Encapsulation reduces bugs and future refactor pain.

  • public: visible everywhere, appears in Inspector.
  • private: visible only inside the class.
  • protected/internal: for inheritance or same-assembly access.
Practical pattern

Keep data private. Offer public methods like TakeDamage or Heal to mutate state while preserving invariants like clamping.

Pro tip
Expose only what designers or other systems need. Use [SerializeField] on private fields if you want editor visibility without public access.
