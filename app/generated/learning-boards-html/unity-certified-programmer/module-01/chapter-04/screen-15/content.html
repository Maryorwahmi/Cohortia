Key Concepts

GetComponent
Retrieve components on a GameObject; always check for null.
Access modifiers
Use private to hide internals and public methods to expose behavior safely.
Refactor Update()
Split responsibilities into named methods to improve readability and testability.
Final Takeaway

Adopt small, testable methods, use clear visibility rules, and wire components safely. These patterns will save time and reduce bugs as your game grows.
