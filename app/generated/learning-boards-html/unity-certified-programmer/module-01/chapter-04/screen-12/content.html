Comparison

GetComponent vs alternatives

GetComponent
Efficient for same GameObject; cache in Awake/Start and null-check.
FindObjectOfType
Searches scene; use sparingly for singletons or editors, not for high-frequency lookup.
new vs AddComponent
new does not create an attached component. Use AddComponent<T> to attach a component at runtime.
Practical advice
  • Cache GetComponent results when you need them repeatedly.
  • Avoid FindObjectOfType in performance-sensitive code.
  • Use AddComponent when you intentionally need a runtime-attached component.
Key Takeaway

Pick the right reference strategy: efficiency and correctness matter more than convenience when wiring components.
