Learning Objectives
Master conditionals, loops, gameplay applications, and best practices.
01
Implement Conditional Statements
Write if, else if, else and switch constructs to choose runtime behavior. This skill lets your game respond differently to health, input, and events.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Utilize Looping Constructs
Use for, foreach, while and do-while to repeat logic for enemies, inventory, and retries. Picking the right loop reduces errors and improves readability.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Apply Flow to Gameplay Systems
Connect conditionals and loops to player states and spawners so behavior is dynamic and testable in the Editor and at runtime.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify Pitfalls & Performance
Spot infinite loops, off-by-one indexing, and costly per-frame operations. Apply caching and move heavy work off Update when necessary.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
