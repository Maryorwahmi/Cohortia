Key Concepts

What to remember from this chapter

Connected Data

Data where relationships between entities are as important as, or more important than, the entities themselves.

Nodes

The fundamental entities or data points in a graph database, representing objects like people or products.

Relationships

The connections or edges between nodes in a graph database, indicating how entities are related.

Chapter Summary

Understanding connected data, nodes, and relationships is crucial for grasping how graph databases intuitively model and efficiently query complex real-world connections.
