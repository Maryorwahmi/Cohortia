The Power of Index-Free Adjacency

How direct connections enable lightning-fast queries.

What is Index-Free Adjacency?

  • •Relationships are stored as direct physical pointers between nodes.
  • •No expensive index lookups or `JOIN` calculations at query time.
  • •Cost of traversing a relationship is constant, regardless of database size.
Contrast with Relational

Relational databases infer relationships via foreign keys and calculate them with `JOIN`s, which slows down deep traversals.
