Relationships: The Connections That Matter

Understanding directed connections and their properties.

What is a Relationship?

  • •A directed connection between two nodes.
  • •Must have a Type (e.g., FRIENDS_WITH, BUYS).
  • •Can also store descriptive data as Properties.
  • •Relationships are first-class citizens, not just pointers.
Example Relationship: FRIENDS_WITH

A relationship of type :FRIENDS_WITH from Alice to Bob, with property {since: '2020-01-15'}.

Relationship Structure
:Person
Alice
:Person
Bob
:FRIENDS_WITH {since: '2020'}
Key Takeaway

Relationships are directed, typed connections with properties, providing rich context and enabling powerful graph traversals.
