A New Paradigm

Graph Databases: Built for Connections

Understanding the fundamental building blocks: Nodes, Relationships, and Properties.

Core Components

  • •Nodes: Entities or data points (e.g., Person, Product).
  • •Relationships: Connections between nodes (e.g., FRIENDS_WITH, BUYS).
  • •Properties: Key-value pairs describing nodes or relationships.
Why it Matters

This model directly mirrors how we think about data and its connections, making it highly intuitive for design and querying.
