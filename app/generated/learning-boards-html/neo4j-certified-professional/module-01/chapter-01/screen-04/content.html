Relational Databases: Strengths and Strains

Understanding where tabular models face challenges with connections.

Strengths: Structured & Transactional

  • •Excellent for structured, tabular data.
  • •Ensures data integrity with ACID properties.
  • •Ideal for transactional workloads (e.g., banking).

Strains: Highly Connected Data

  • •Complex relationships require many `JOIN` operations.
  • •`JOIN`s become computationally expensive and slow.
  • •"Impedance mismatch" between connected data and rigid tables.

Key Takeaway

Relational databases are not inherently bad, but their tabular structure becomes a performance bottleneck for deeply connected data traversals.
