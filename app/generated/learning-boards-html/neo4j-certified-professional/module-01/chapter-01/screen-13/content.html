Graph Thinking: Avoiding Relational Pitfalls

Why forcing a graph problem into a relational model is inefficient.

Common Mistake: Relational Over-Modeling

  • •Attempting to model highly connected data in rigid tables.
  • •Leads to overly complex SQL with many `JOIN`s or recursive CTEs.
  • •Results in poor performance and difficult maintenance for deep queries.

The Graph Advantage

  • •Simplifies data representation for connected data.
  • •Improves query performance for relationship traversals.
  • •Unlocks new analytical capabilities and insights.
Relational vs. Graph for Connected Data
Relational: Complex JOINs
Graph: Direct Traversal
Key Takeaway

Recognize when your data is inherently graph-like and choose a graph database to avoid the performance and complexity issues of relational models.
