Directionality: The Flow of Connections

Understanding why relationship direction matters for clarity and queries.

  • Explicit Flow: Relationships always flow from a start node to an end node, making connections unambiguous.

  • Query Impact: Directionality guides graph traversals, making queries more intuitive and efficient.

  • Clarity: A clear direction prevents ambiguity, ensuring the model accurately reflects real-world relationships.

Key Takeaway

Always define a clear direction for relationships to ensure an accurate and understandable graph model.
