Best Practices

Effective Graph Modeling: Asking the Right Questions

Guiding principles for building robust and intuitive graph schemas.

  • What are my entities? (Nodes)

  • How are they connected? (Relationships)

  • What descriptive information do they need? (Properties)

  • What is the nature and direction of those connections? (Relationship Types & Direction)

Key Takeaway

Consistently asking these questions will guide you to build clear, efficient, and powerful graph models.

Modeling Flow
Start

Identify Entities (Nodes)

Define Connections (Relationships)

Add Details (Properties)

End

Refine & Iterate

Common Mistake

Trying to force a relational database schema directly into a graph model, losing graph advantages.
