Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Advanced Graph Principles

Recap of crucial aspects for performance and modeling.

Directionality

The inherent direction of a relationship, crucial for clear modeling and intuitive queries.

Label

A named tag used to categorize nodes, allowing for flexible, multi-faceted entity representation.

Index-Free Adjacency

Neo4j's architectural principle for constant-time relationship traversal, ensuring high performance.

Summary Takeaway

These advanced principles are key to designing efficient, clear, and high-performing graph database solutions.
