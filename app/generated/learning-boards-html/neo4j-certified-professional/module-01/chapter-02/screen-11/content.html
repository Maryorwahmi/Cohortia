Graph Modeling: Defining Relationships and Properties

Connecting nodes with typed, directed relationships and enriching them with details.

Scenario: Movie Database (Cont.)

Connect Tom Hanks to "Forrest Gump" (acted in, with role) and Robert Zemeckis to "Forrest Gump" (directed).

Step 2: Relationships and Properties
  • 1

    Identify connections: Tom Hanks acted in Forrest Gump, Robert Zemeckis directed Forrest Gump.

  • 2

    Assign relationship types and directions: ACTED_IN, DIRECTED.

  • 3

    Add properties to relationships: role: 'Forrest Gump' for ACTED_IN.

  • 4

    Example: (tHanks)-[:ACTED_IN {role: 'Forrest Gump'}]->(forrestGumpMovie)

Key Takeaway

Relationships with types, directions, and properties capture the full context of connections in your graph.

Conceptual Sketch: Relationships
Relationship

(tomHanks)-[:ACTED_IN {role: 'Forrest Gump'}]->(forrestGump)

Relationship

(robertZemeckis)-[:DIRECTED]->(forrestGump)

Common Pitfall

Forgetting to add relevant properties to relationships, losing valuable contextual information about the connection.
