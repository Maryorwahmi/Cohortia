Chapter Goals

Learning Objectives: Core Graph Elements

What you'll achieve by the end of this chapter.

Bloom's Taxonomy:
LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Define Core Elements

Define and differentiate between Neo4j's core graph elements: nodes, relationships, and properties. This skill is vital for accurately translating real-world data into a graph structure.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Categorize with Labels & Types

Understand the role of labels in categorizing nodes and relationship types in defining connections. This enables precise data organization and efficient querying.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Explain Relationship Directionality

Explain the significance of directionality in Neo4j relationships and its impact on queries. This ensures clear and unambiguous graph traversals.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Grasp Index-Free Adjacency

Grasp the concept of index-free adjacency and its importance for graph traversal performance. This reveals why Neo4j excels at deep, complex queries.
