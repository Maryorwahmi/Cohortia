Core Concept

Relationships: Connecting Entities

Defining associations with relationship types and directionality.

  • Connections: Relationships represent how entities (nodes) are associated with each other.

  • Relationship Type: A named category (e.g., ACTED_IN, DIRECTED) describing the nature of the connection.

  • Direction: Indicates the flow of the connection from a starting node to an ending node.

Key Takeaway

Clear relationship types and directions are crucial for an explicit and intuitive graph model.
