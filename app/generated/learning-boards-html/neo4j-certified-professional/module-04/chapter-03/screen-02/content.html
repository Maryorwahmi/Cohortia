Learning Objectives

What you'll achieve in this chapter.

LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Understand Index Role

Grasp how indexes fundamentally accelerate Cypher query performance, transforming slow scans into rapid lookups. This skill is key to diagnosing and solving performance bottlenecks in your graph applications.

Verified Competency Milestone
02

Create & Drop B-tree Indexes

Acquire the practical skills to create and remove B-tree indexes on node properties using Cypher. This allows you to directly control and optimize your graph's lookup mechanisms for specific query patterns.

Verified Competency Milestone
03

Grasp Uniqueness Constraints

Understand the concept of uniqueness constraints, how they enforce data integrity, and their crucial role in automatically creating indexes. This ensures your graph data remains consistent and queries are optimized.
