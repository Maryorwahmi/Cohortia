Verifying Your Schema: SHOW INDEXES and SHOW CONSTRAINTS

Commands to inspect existing indexes and constraints in your graph.

`SHOW INDEXES`

Lists all active indexes, including their type (e.g., B-tree), label, properties, and current state (e.g., ONLINE).

`SHOW CONSTRAINTS`

Displays all defined constraints, such as uniqueness and existence constraints, showing what rules are enforced on your graph data.

Key Takeaway

Regularly inspect your schema using SHOW INDEXES and SHOW CONSTRAINTS to ensure indexes and constraints are correctly applied and optimized.
