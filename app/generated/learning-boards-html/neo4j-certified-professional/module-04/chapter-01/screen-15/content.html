Key Concepts: `CASE` Forms

Understanding the two types of conditional logic.

Simple `CASE`

Compares a single expression against multiple specific values (`CASE expr WHEN val THEN res END`).

Searched `CASE`

Evaluates multiple boolean conditions sequentially (`CASE WHEN cond THEN res END`).

Chapter Summary

Both simple and searched `CASE` statements are powerful for dynamic data transformation, but choosing the correct form depends on whether you're matching specific values or evaluating complex boolean conditions.
