Key Concepts: Part 2

Understanding Performance Metrics and Bottlenecks

Database Hits (db hits)

Metric indicating read/write operations against storage; high numbers suggest I/O inefficiency.

Rows

Metric showing records processed by each operation; high counts can mean inefficient filtering.

Cartesian Product

An operation that combines every row from one input with every row from another, leading to exponential growth.

Summary Takeaway

High `db hits` and `rows` often signal bottlenecks like full scans or Cartesian products, which are critical targets for query optimization.
