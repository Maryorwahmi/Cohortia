Mastering Data Deletion in Neo4j

Safely Removing Nodes and Relationships with DELETE and DETACH DELETE

This chapter will equip you with the knowledge to confidently manage data removal in Neo4j, focusing on the critical differences and safe application of the DELETE and DETACH DELETE clauses.

Key Takeaway

Understanding the nuances of DELETE and DETACH DELETE is crucial for maintaining graph integrity and preventing data loss.

DELETE Clause

Removes relationships or isolated nodes, preserving graph integrity.

DETACH DELETE Clause

Removes a node and all its connected relationships simultaneously.

Safety & Verification

Always verify targets before deletion; data loss is often irreversible.
