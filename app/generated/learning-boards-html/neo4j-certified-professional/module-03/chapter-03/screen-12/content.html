Key Concepts: Deletion Clauses & Integrity

Essential takeaways from deleting graph data

DELETE Clause

Removes relationships or nodes that have no relationships. Will error if used on a connected node.

DETACH DELETE Clause

Removes a node and all its incoming and outgoing relationships simultaneously.

Graph Integrity

The principle that relationships should always connect existing nodes, avoiding dangling pointers.

Summary Takeaway

Always verify your deletion targets and understand the implications of each Cypher clause to maintain a healthy and consistent graph database.
