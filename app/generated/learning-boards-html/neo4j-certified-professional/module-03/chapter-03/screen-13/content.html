Key Concepts: Safety & Irreversibility

Reinforcing best practices for data deletion

Irreversible Operation

Data deletion cannot be undone easily; always exercise extreme caution and plan carefully.

Verification

The practice of running a MATCH and RETURN query to confirm targets before executing deletion.

Scoped Deletion

Limit deletion queries using specific WHERE clauses to avoid unintended broad removals.

Summary Takeaway

Always verify your deletion targets and understand the implications of each Cypher clause to maintain a healthy and consistent graph database.
