Cypher Clause

Introducing DETACH DELETE

The powerful solution for removing connected nodes and their relationships

Purpose of DETACH DELETE

Designed to remove a node and all its incoming and outgoing relationships simultaneously.

Atomic Operation

Performs a 'clean sweep' of a node and its entire immediate network in a single command.

Caution Advised

Extremely powerful and convenient, but must be used with caution due to its cascading, irreversible effects.

Key Takeaway

DETACH DELETE is for comprehensive node removal, including all its connections, but demands careful targeting.
