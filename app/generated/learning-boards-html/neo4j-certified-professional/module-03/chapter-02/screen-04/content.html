Core Concept

Evolving Your Graph Database

Transitioning from data retrieval to active graph modification.

Why Modify?

Graphs are living data models. Modification allows them to reflect real-world changes, add new insights, and grow over time.

The Core Toolkit

Cypher provides specific clauses: CREATE, MERGE, SET, and REMOVE, each with a distinct role in data manipulation.

Beyond Retrieval

Moving past just reading data, you'll gain the power to build and shape the graph's structure and content.

Cypher Data Manipulation Flow
CREATEAdd new nodes & relationships
MERGEMatch or create idempotently
SETUpdate properties & labels
REMOVEDelete properties & labels
