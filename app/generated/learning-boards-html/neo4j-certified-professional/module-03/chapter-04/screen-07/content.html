Result Control

Pagination with SKIP and LIMIT

Managing large result sets efficiently

How SKIP & LIMIT Work

  • •SKIP: Number of initial results to bypass.
  • •LIMIT: Maximum number of results to return.
  • •Always applied after ORDER BY for consistent pagination.
  • •Essential for fetching data in chunks (pages).
Example: First 10 Movies after 2000
MATCH (m:Movie)WHERE m.releaseYear > 2000RETURN m.titleORDER BY m.titleSKIP 0 LIMIT 10
This fetches the first 10 movies released after 2000, sorted by title. For the next 10, change SKIP to 10.
Key Takeaway

SKIP and LIMIT are crucial for managing large result sets, improving application responsiveness and user experience by delivering data in manageable pages.

Common Mistake

Applying SKIP/LIMIT before ORDER BY, which can lead to inconsistent and unpredictable pagination results.
