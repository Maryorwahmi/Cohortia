Optimization & Pitfalls

Performance and Common Mistakes

Writing efficient and error-free advanced queries

Common Mistakes

  • •Misplacing Clauses: ORDER BY, SKIP, LIMIT must be after RETURN/WITH.
  • •Implicit Grouping: Forgetting Cypher's automatic grouping with aggregations.

Performance Considerations

  • •SKIP/LIMIT: Can be expensive on large datasets as skipped rows are still processed.
  • •DISTINCT: Adds overhead due to uniqueness checks.
  • •Profiling: Use EXPLAIN and PROFILE to understand query execution.
Key Takeaway

Always profile your queries to understand performance characteristics and avoid common pitfalls like misplacing clauses or overlooking implicit grouping.

Pro Tip

For very large datasets, consider filtering early in the query to reduce the amount of data processed by subsequent clauses like SKIP, LIMIT, or DISTINCT.
