The WITH Clause: Pipelining Results

Breaking down complex queries into manageable steps

Purpose of WITH

  • •Passes results from one query part to the next.
  • •Acts as an intermediary pipeline.
  • •Allows filtering, sorting, or aggregating intermediate data.
  • •Improves readability and can aid performance.
Conceptual Flow
MATCH (...)WITH <intermediate_results>WHERE (...)WITH <filtered_results>RETURN (...)
This illustrates how WITH creates distinct stages in a query, passing refined data along.
Key Takeaway

The WITH clause is a powerful intermediary that pipelines results, enabling you to break down complex queries into manageable, readable, and often more performant steps.

Pro Tip

Use WITH to explicitly define variables and their scope, which can prevent errors and make your queries easier to reason about.
