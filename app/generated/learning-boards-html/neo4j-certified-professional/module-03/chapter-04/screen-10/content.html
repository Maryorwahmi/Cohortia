Advanced Aggregation

Aggregation Functions: MIN, MAX, COLLECT

Finding extremes and gathering values into lists

Specialized Aggregations

  • •MIN(property): Finds the minimum value.
  • •MAX(property): Finds the maximum value.
  • •COLLECT(expression): Gathers all values into a list.
  • •COLLECT is powerful for grouping related items.
Example: Actors in 'The Matrix'
MATCH (m:Movie {title: 'The Matrix'})<-[:ACTED_IN]-(p:Person)RETURN COLLECT(p.name) AS ActorsInMatrix
This query returns a single list of all actors who acted in 'The Matrix'.
Key Takeaway

COLLECT() is particularly powerful for grouping related items into a list, transforming multiple results into a compact, structured format.

Pro Tip

COLLECT can be combined with other aggregations, for example, to get a list of unique items or to count items within the collected list.
