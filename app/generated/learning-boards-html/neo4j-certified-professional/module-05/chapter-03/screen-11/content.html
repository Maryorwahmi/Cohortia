Module 5.3›Chapter 5.3›Granular Privileges
Cypher Commands
GRANT CREATE ON NODES Person TO my_publisher_role;
DENY DELETE ON NODES Movie TO bob;
Grants create privilege on 'Person' nodes and denies delete on 'Movie' nodes.
DENY Precedence

The `DENY` command always takes precedence over `GRANT`, ensuring explicit restrictions are enforced.

Auditing Permissions

Regularly review and audit user and role permissions to ensure they remain appropriate and secure.

Security Principle

Principle of Least Privilege

Grant only the minimum permissions necessary for a user or role to perform their duties, reducing the attack surface.

Common Mistake

Over-Privileged Accounts

Granting broad administrative roles unnecessarily creates significant security risks and violates the principle of least privilege.

Key Takeaway

Implement granular privileges and strictly adhere to the Principle of Least Privilege, using `DENY` to enforce critical restrictions and regularly auditing access.
