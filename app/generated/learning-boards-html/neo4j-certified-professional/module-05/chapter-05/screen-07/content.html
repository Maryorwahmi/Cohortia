Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Strategy Comparison
Vertical

Scale up

  • Simple to implement
  • Good for memory/I/O limits
  • No built-in high availability for a single node
Read Replicas

Scale reads

  • Distribute read queries across replicas
  • Preserves write consistency on cores
  • Monitor replication lag
Fabric

Shard across clusters

  • Federates queries across shards
  • Requires partitioning strategy
  • Operationally most complex
Decision checklist
Does your dataset fit a single cluster?
Are reads dominant? Consider replicas.
Is data locality important? Consider Fabric.
