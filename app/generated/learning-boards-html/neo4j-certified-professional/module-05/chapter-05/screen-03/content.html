Design & Evaluation

Convert workload metrics into prioritized scaling steps you can implement and test.

Data Volume
Estimate nodes/relationships growth and decide if a single cluster can contain the dataset with acceptable performance.
Query Complexity
Complex multi-hop traversals have longer execution times—profiling guides whether to optimize queries or scale horizontally.
Read/Write Ratio
High read ratios favor read replicas. High write rates require careful core sizing and attention to consistency.
Concurrency
Estimate concurrent sessions and plan for replica pools or additional cores to avoid contention.
Latency
Understand acceptable response times; cross-shard or cross-region queries add network latency that affects SLAs.
Operational Cost
Balance the cost of larger machines, more replicas, or multiple clusters against business requirements.
Use this checklist in architecture reviews. Start simple, measure, then evolve the deployment incrementally rather than committing to a complex shard strategy early.
