Visual summary
Scaling choices at a glance
Vertical: add CPU, RAM, and faster disk. Simple but limited.
Horizontal: add read replicas for throughput, use Fabric to shard across clusters.
Cloud: Aura or managed VMs reduce operations work and simplify scaling.
