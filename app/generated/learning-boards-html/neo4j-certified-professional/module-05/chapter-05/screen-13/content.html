Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Read-Heavy Case Study
Scenario

90% reads, 10% writes

Prioritize read scaling by deploying a Causal Cluster with a small core quorum and multiple read replicas.

Practical steps
  • Deploy 3 cores to maintain quorum for writes.
  • Add read replicas to increase read throughput and distribute queries.
  • Route reads to replicas and monitor replication lag for freshness.
Why not Fabric yet?
Fabric is for datasets that exceed a cluster’s capacity or require strong data locality. For this scenario, replicas are simpler and more efficient.
Check your decision
If data size grows such that a single cluster cannot hold it, then evaluate Fabric and plan partitions before migrating data.
Key Takeaway

For read-dominant workloads, read replicas paired with a minimal core quorum are the most efficient initial scaling choice.
