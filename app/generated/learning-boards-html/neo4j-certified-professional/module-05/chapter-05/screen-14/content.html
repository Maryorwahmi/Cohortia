Exercise

When and how to shard

If dataset size or locality demands exceed a single cluster, plan Fabric shards by choosing a partition key that keeps most queries local.

Reflection prompts
  • What natural locality exists in your data: region, tenant, or domain?
  • Which queries would become cross-shard and how frequently would they run?
  • How will you manage backups and monitoring for multiple shards?
Example partition
Shard by region for a global customers graph. Each region holds local entities and relationships to keep most queries single-shard.
Warning
Sharding without locality increases cross-shard joins and will degrade performance rather than improve it.
Key Takeaway

Shard only when a cluster’s capacity or locality needs demand it; choose partition keys that keep frequent queries within one shard.
