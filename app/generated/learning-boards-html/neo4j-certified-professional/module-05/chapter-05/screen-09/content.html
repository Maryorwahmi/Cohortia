Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Region-Sharded Architecture
Architecture

Shard by geographic region

Partition data by region so most queries are served locally, and use Fabric to federate when you need global results.

Design steps
  1. Choose partition key aligned with locality (region or country).
  2. Place related entities together to avoid cross-shard links.
  3. Register shards in Fabric and test common queries for cross-shard cost.
Operational notes
Each regional cluster needs monitoring, backups, and capacity planning; automation helps manage multiple clusters.
Key Takeaway

Align shard boundaries with natural locality to keep most queries single-shard and fast; accept federation cost for global queries.

Concrete scenario
A global social network shards user profiles and relationships by region. A regional friend search queries only that region's cluster and returns quickly.
Check your understanding
What cross-shard queries are unavoidable in your application, and how often will they run? If they are frequent, reconsider your partition key.
