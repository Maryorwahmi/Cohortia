Definition

Managed vs Self-managed

AuraDB is fully managed: it automates provisioning, backups, patching, scaling, and HA. Self-managed deployments on cloud providers give you full control over infrastructure and operations.

When Aura helps
When you prefer to reduce operational overhead and rely on a managed service for scaling and availability.
When to self-manage
When you need custom networking, special storage, or infrastructure control that a managed service does not provide.
Benefits of managed AuraDB
  • Fully managed operations: provisioning, backups, and patching handled for you.
  • Elastic scaling: instances can be resized with minimal operational overhead.
  • Built-in high availability and disaster recovery without manual setup.
Even with managed services, plan capacity, monitor performance, and understand cost implications as you scale up or out.
Key Takeaway

Managed services reduce operational burden and accelerate scaling, but architects still must plan partitions and capacity for performance and cost.
