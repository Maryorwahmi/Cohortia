Strategic Tuning Workflow & Safety

Best practices for safe and effective optimization.

Systematic Tuning Workflow

1. Monitor & Analyze

Identify bottlenecks using metrics and query plans.

2. Change One Parameter

Make isolated adjustments to settings or indexes.

3. Measure Impact

Quantify the effect of your changes on performance.

4. Test in Staging

Validate changes in a non-production environment.

Key Takeaway

Adopt a systematic workflow: analyze, change one parameter, measure, and test in staging to ensure safe and effective performance tuning.

Important Safety Notes

  • •Avoid Simultaneous Changes: Isolating changes helps pinpoint cause and effect.
  • •Caution with Large Operations: DELETE or MERGE can lock parts of the graph, affecting concurrency.
  • •Await Indexes: Use CALL db.awaitIndexes() after creating indexes in production to ensure they are fully built.
Pro Tip

Document all changes and their observed impacts. This creates a valuable knowledge base for future tuning efforts.
