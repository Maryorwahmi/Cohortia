Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Designing DR
Backup strategies
Choose between neo4j-admin dump for full offline dumps or Enterprise online backup for scheduled, lower-RTO snapshots. Store backups off-site to survive a region failure.
Cross-region replication
Asynchronous replication to a separate cluster provides an extra layer of protection and supports DR failover with a defined RPO and RTO.
Testing and runbooks
Schedule restoration drills, document step-by-step recovery procedures, and record recovery time measurements to validate SLAs.
Common mistake
Not testing backups. Always perform restores periodically to ensure backups are usable.
Backup command (example)
neo4j-admin dump --database=neo4j --to=/backups/neo4j.dump
DR drill
Simulate a region outage, restore backup to a fresh cluster, validate queries and connectivity.
Checklist
Backups, off-site storage, DR runbook, test schedule, cross-region options.
