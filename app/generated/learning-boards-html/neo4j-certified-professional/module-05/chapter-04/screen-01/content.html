High Availability & Disaster Recovery

Understand Causal Cluster roles, quorum rules, failover behavior, and DR planning to protect your Neo4j deployment.

In this lesson
Causal Cluster roles, Raft quorum, and recovery planning.
Coming up
Configuring neo4j.conf and simulating a 3-core setup.
Why it matters
Minimize downtime and data loss during outages.
Concept Map
Cores, Raft, Read Replicas, Backups
Causal Cluster

Core members form a Raft group for consistent writes. An odd number is required to maintain quorum.

Read Replicas

Scale read capacity; receive data asynchronously and are eventually consistent.

Example config
dbms.mode=CORE
dbms.cluster.initial_discovery_members=core1:5000,core2:5000,core3:5000
# listen_address and advertised_address must be reachable
Quick checklist
  • Odd number of cores
  • Consistent discovery list
  • Backups to remote location
