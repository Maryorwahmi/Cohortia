Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Failover & Leader Election
Step-by-step
1
Leader becomes unreachable
Followers detect heartbeat timeout and prepare for election.
2
Election starts
Cores exchange votes; a candidate becomes leader when quorum is achieved.
3
Writes resume
Applications can resume writes after the new leader commits the election.
Supporting notes
  • Read replicas keep serving stale reads based on last sync.
  • If quorum is lost, writes are blocked until enough cores return.
  • Monitor election frequency; frequent elections indicate instability.
Common mistake
Expecting immediate write failover without accounting for the brief election pause or verifying quorum requirements.
Key Takeaway

Leader elections keep consistency intact but can briefly pause writes; ensure quorum to avoid extended write outages.
