From ProductViewed to recommendations
A concrete end-to-end path that keeps recommendation data fresh.
Event
ProductViewed {userId, productId, timestamp}
Consumer
Parses event, MERGE user and product, MERGE VIEWED relationship, set timestamp.
Recommendation query
Short traversal to find users who viewed similar products or identify products co-viewed by many users.
Practical note
Recommendation quality comes from relationship richness and recency. Instrument metrics for view counts, relationship freshness, and query latency.
