Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Monitoring
What to monitor
A practical list of metrics and checks that indicate health and freshness of your real-time graph pipeline.
Consumer lag
Time difference between latest event and consumer position.
DLQ size
Number and age of messages in the dead-letter queue.
Retry & failure counts
Rates of transient and persistent failures per time window.
Query latency
Response time for critical read queries that drive user experiences.
Suggested checks
  • Periodic consistency queries that verify recent events materialized in the graph.
  • Correlation ids and logs to trace failed events.
  • Alerts on lag and DLQ growth to trigger human investigation.
