Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
What to Remember
Real-time Graph Analytics
Process and analyze connected data immediately to enable instant pattern detection.
Event-Driven Architecture
Components communicate by producing and consuming events via brokers, enabling loose coupling and scale.
Message Broker
An intermediary that buffers, partitions, and routes events to consumers like Neo4j-backed services.
Summary takeaway

Design idempotent consumers, choose triggers or CDC based on scope, and instrument retry and monitoring so your real-time graph is trustworthy and responsive.
