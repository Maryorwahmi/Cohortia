Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Error Handling
Retries & Dead-letter queues
Plan bounded retries for transient failures and move persistent failures to a dead-letter queue for diagnosis.
  • Implement exponential backoff and a retry limit for transient errors.
  • Move persistent failures to a DLQ with payload and error context.
  • Keep consumers idempotent so retries are safe.
Operational checklist
  • Track processed, retried, and failed counts.
  • Monitor consumer lag and oldest unprocessed event age.
  • Alert on DLQ growth and unresolved failures.
