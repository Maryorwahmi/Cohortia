Deployment Strategies and Best Practices
Understand options from standalone to managed services, and the operational controls required for production readiness.
Standalone
Simple, for development or very small workloads. Low ops overhead but no HA.
Causal Cluster
Core servers plus read replicas for fault tolerance and horizontal read scale.
AuraDB
Managed cloud service that reduces operational overhead—backups, patches, scaling handled for you.
Why this chapter matters

Deployments determine availability, operational burden, and cost. The right choice protects data, improves performance, and reduces incidents. We’ll give you practical patterns you can apply immediately.
