Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Backup & Restore
Definition
Backup and restore options
Offline dump & load
Use neo4j-admin dump to create an offline snapshot and neo4j-admin load to restore it. Good for periodic full backups.
Hot backups (Enterprise)
Use neo4j-admin backup for online backups without downtime in supported editions.
Disk snapshots
Cloud provider snapshots can supplement backups, but verify consistency and restore process.
Key Takeaway

Backups are only useful if you rehearse restores. Automate, secure, and test restore procedures to meet your recovery objectives.

Checklist
  • Automate regular backups
  • Store copies off-site and encrypt them
  • Test the restore process regularly
