Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What to Remember
Connection Pooling
Create one driver per process and tune pool size to match concurrency and DB capacity.
Transactions
Use transaction helpers and keep transactions short to maintain ACID and avoid contention.
Parameterization
Always pass user input as parameters to prevent injection and improve plan reuse.
Final takeaway

Adopt these operational practices early: they pay off in predictable performance, security, and reliability as your Neo4j-backed application grows.
