Object-Graph Mapper
Maps nodes and relationships to language-native objects for clearer domain code.
Loading Strategies
Lazy delays fetches; eager fetches relationships up front. Choose based on access patterns.
Repository & Raw Cypher
Use repositories for routine tasks; use raw Cypher for complex, performance-sensitive traversals.
Summary Takeaway

OGMs improve productivity and maintainability, but they require profiling, indexing, and the occasional custom Cypher to achieve production performance.
