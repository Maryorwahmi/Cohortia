Best Practice
Designing Your Graph Model for Optimal Queries
Prioritize anticipated query patterns during the initial design phase.

Key Design Questions

  • What questions will you ask of your data?
  • What traversals will you perform?
  • What entities will you start from?
  • What properties will you filter on?
  • What paths will you need to discover?

Example: Social Network Queries

If you frequently need to find 'friends of friends', ensure FRIENDS_WITH relationships are well-defined and potentially indexed for efficient traversal.

Proactive design for queries prevents costly refactoring later.
Why it Matters

A query-driven design ensures your graph model is performant, scalable, and directly supports the analytical needs of your application.

Key Takeaway

Design your graph model with your most important queries in mind to ensure efficiency and avoid future performance issues.
