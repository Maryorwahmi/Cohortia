Graph Pattern
Identifying and Managing Super-nodes (Dense Nodes)
Recognize nodes with high connectivity and strategies to optimize their impact.

The Super-node Problem

A node with an extremely high number of relationships. Can lead to slow queries if not managed, as traversing its many connections becomes resource-intensive.

Example: A single 'City' node connected to millions of 'Person' nodes.

Solution: Distribute Connections

Introduce intermediate nodes to distribute relationships. Re-evaluate if the 'super-node' is truly an entity or if its attributes should be spread out.

Example: (Person)-[:LIVES_IN]->(Neighborhood)-[:IS_PART_OF]->(City).
Why it Matters

Proper management of super-nodes is crucial for maintaining query performance and ensuring the scalability of your graph database.

Key Takeaway

Identify and refactor super-nodes by distributing connections to improve graph performance and scalability.
