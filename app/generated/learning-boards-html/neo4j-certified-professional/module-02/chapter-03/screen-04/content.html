Core Concept
Representing Complex Relationships and Hierarchies
Translate intricate real-world connections into intuitive graph structures.
Why Graphs Excel
  • Directly map real-world connections.
  • Simplify recursive queries and traversals.
  • Enhance model clarity and understanding.
  • Avoid complex joins common in relational models.
Example: Organizational Chart

An Employee node connects to a Manager node via a REPORTS_TO relationship, allowing for easy traversal of reporting lines.

Key Takeaway

Graphs naturally model hierarchies and complex relationships, making data intuitive and queries efficient.
