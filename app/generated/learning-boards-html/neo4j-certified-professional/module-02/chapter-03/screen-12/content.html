Best Practice
Consistent Naming for Clarity and Maintainability
Standardize naming conventions for labels, relationship types, and properties.

Labels

Use PascalCase for node labels.

Person, Movie, Order

Relationship Types

Use SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE for relationship types.

ACTED_IN, FRIENDS_WITH, OWNS

Properties

Use camelCase for properties.

name, born, releaseDate
Why it Matters

Consistent naming makes your graph easier to understand, query, and maintain for everyone on your team, fostering collaboration.

Key Takeaway

Adhere to standard naming conventions for labels, relationships, and properties to ensure model clarity and maintainability.
