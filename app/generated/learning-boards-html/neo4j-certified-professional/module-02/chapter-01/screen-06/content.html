Best Practice

Nodes vs. Properties: What's an Entity?

Distinguishing between core entities and their attributes

Incorrect: Attribute as Node

Modeling 'age' as a separate Age node.

Results in a cluttered graph with many trivial nodes.

Makes queries more complex and less intuitive.

Visual: (Person)-[:HAS_AGE]->(Age {value: 30})

Correct: Attribute as Property

Storing 'age' as a property on the Person node.

Keeps the graph clean and focused on core entities.

Enables straightforward and efficient querying.

Visual: (Person {age: 30})

Common Mistake

Don't model simple descriptive attributes as separate nodes; they should be properties of existing nodes or relationships.
