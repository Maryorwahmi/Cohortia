What are Nodes?

Nodes are the fundamental entities or 'things' in your graph. They represent discrete items or concepts, acting as the vertices of your graph.

Flexible Containers

Unlike relational tables, nodes don't have a fixed schema. They are flexible and can hold descriptive attributes called properties, allowing for evolving data models.

Examples in Practice

In a social network, 'Alice' and 'Bob' are nodes. In e-commerce, 'Laptop X' and 'Customer Y' are nodes. They are the distinct items you want to model.

Key Takeaway

Nodes are the core entities of your graph, representing distinct items or concepts, and are highly flexible in their structure.
