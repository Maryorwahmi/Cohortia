Core Concept

Graph vs. Relational: A New Perspective

Understanding the fundamental shift in data storage

Relational View

Data organized in tables (rows & columns).

Connections via foreign keys (implicit).

Schema-rigid, predefined structure.

Example: Customer and Order tables linked by customer_id.

Graph View

Data organized as nodes and relationships.

Connections are explicit, first-class citizens.

Schema-optional, flexible structure.

Example: (Customer)-[:ORDERS]->(Product) directly.

Key Takeaway

Graph databases excel at modeling complex relationships, making connections explicit and highly performant for traversal, unlike the implicit joins of relational systems.
