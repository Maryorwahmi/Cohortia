Properties on Relationships

Relationships can hold key-value pairs (properties) that describe the connection itself, adding context and detail.

Examples

For FRIENDS_WITH, a property could be since: 2020. For ORDERS, it might be quantity: 2 or orderDate: '2023-10-26'.

Semantic Richness

This capability makes relationships highly expressive, allowing them to carry meaningful data directly, unlike simple foreign keys in relational models.

Key Takeaway

Relationships are not just links; they can store their own properties, providing rich semantic context to the connections in your graph.
