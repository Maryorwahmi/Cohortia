Introduction to Constraints

Ensuring Data Quality and Validity in Your Graph

What are Constraints?

Rules enforced by the database to ensure data integrity and validity, preventing inconsistent or erroneous data.

Node Key Constraints

Ensure a property (or combination) is unique for all nodes with a specific label, often used for primary keys.

Property Existence Constraints

Ensure a specific property must exist on all nodes with a given label or relationships of a given type.

Key Takeaway

Constraints are vital for maintaining data consistency and reliability in your graph database.

Uniqueness

No duplicate identifiers

Existence

Required properties present

Prevent Errors

Stop invalid data from entering the graph

Implicit Indexing

Node Key Constraints also create an index for fast lookups.
