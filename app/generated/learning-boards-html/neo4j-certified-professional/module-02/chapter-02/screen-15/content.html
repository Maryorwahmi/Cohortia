Key Concepts (Continued)

Reinforcing Data Integrity and Constraint Types

Constraint

A rule enforced by the database to ensure data integrity and validity, preventing inconsistent or erroneous data.

Node Key Constraint

Ensures a property is unique for all nodes with a specific label, implicitly creating an index.

Property Existence Constraint

Ensures a specific property must exist on all nodes with a given label or relationships of a given type.

Chapter Summary

Constraints are vital for enforcing data integrity, ensuring uniqueness, and guaranteeing the presence of essential properties in your graph model.
