Avoiding Common Modeling Pitfalls

Strategic Use of Labels, Indexes, and Constraints

Forgetting Indexes

Leads to slow queries, especially on large datasets. Always consider indexing properties used in MATCH or WHERE clauses for direct lookups.

Over-Indexing

Indexing too many properties can degrade write performance. Be selective and only index properties that are frequently used for lookups.

Not Using Constraints

Leads to dirty data, duplicate entities, and inconsistent property values. Always use UNIQUE constraints for natural or surrogate keys.

Confusing Labels & Properties

Labels categorize; properties describe. Don't use labels for values that change frequently or are specific to an instance (e.g., :Age30 is an anti-pattern).

Key Takeaway

Judicious application of labels, indexes, and constraints prevents common issues and leads to robust, performant graph models.
