Comparison Efficiency and Scalability Benefits
Full node vs SPV client
Full nodes download and verify full blocks and transactions. SPV clients keep only headers and request Merkle paths to verify inclusion, saving bandwidth and storage at the cost of higher trust in provided proofs.
Concrete comparison
To verify a single transaction among 1,024 leaves, an SPV client uses roughly log2(1024) ≈ 10 sibling hashes instead of 1,024 full transaction payloads.
Trade-offs
  • Bandwidth and storage savings for SPV clients.
  • Dependence on correct proofs provided by full nodes.
  • Privacy considerations: provided siblings reveal partial structure.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Merkle trees make verification scalable by reducing data to a logarithmic set of sibling hashes, enabling light clients to operate efficiently.
