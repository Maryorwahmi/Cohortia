In this lesson
You'll learn the defining properties of cryptographic hashes, how Merkle trees are built, why Merkle roots appear in block headers, and how light clients verify inclusion with minimal data.

Why this matters
These concepts ensure tamper-evidence and efficient verification for light clients, making blockchain practical on phones and embedded devices.
Example
Changing one transaction bit changes its leaf hash and thus the Merkle root, so a tampered block is immediately obvious.
