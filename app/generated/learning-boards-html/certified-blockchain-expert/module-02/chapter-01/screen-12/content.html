Comparison Common Pitfalls and Implementation Errors
Weak algorithms
Avoid MD5 and SHA-1 for integrity-critical uses. Use SHA-256 or SHA-3 families that are actively recommended.
Ordering and concatenation
Define and document whether pairs are sorted or positional and whether you concatenate bytes or hex strings. Inconsistency breaks proofs.
Duplication of odd nodes
Be explicit about duplicating the final node when a level has an odd count. Mismatched duplication yields different trees.
Pro tip
Record and compare intermediate hashes between producer and verifier in development to catch subtle encoding or ordering mismatches quickly.
