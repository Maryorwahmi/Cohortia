Core Concept Collision Resistance and Its Importance
Why collisions matter
If two different transactions produce the same hash, an attacker could substitute one for the other without changing the Merkle root, defeating inclusion proofs and enabling undetected tampering.
Misconception check
Length alone does not guarantee security. Use well-reviewed algorithms such as SHA-256 or SHA-3 rather than relying on digest size alone.
Practical guidance
  • Prefer standard, vetted hash functions.
  • Keep cryptographic libraries up to date to receive security patches.
  • Log intermediate hashes for audit trails and debugging.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Collision resistance is foundational; choose strong hash functions and monitor cryptographic research for weaknesses.
