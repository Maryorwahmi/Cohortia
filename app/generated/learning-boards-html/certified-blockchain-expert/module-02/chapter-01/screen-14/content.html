Summary Key Concepts
Cryptographic Hash
A fixed-size, one-way digest used for integrity and identity.
Merkle Tree
A hierarchical hash structure that summarizes many leaves into one root.
Merkle Proof / SPV
A compact path of sibling hashes that proves inclusion without full data.
Summary takeaway
Use strong hash functions, a consistent construction convention, and Merkle proofs for efficient verification on constrained clients.
