Learning Objectives
Four targeted competencies to explain, apply, and analyze hashing and Merkle trees.
01
Explain cryptographic hash properties
Define determinism, pre-image and collision resistance, and why they matter for tamper evidence.
Mnemonic: Venn of determinism vs one-way vs collision-resistance
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate hash algorithms
Recognize use cases for SHA-256 and why older hashes like MD5/SHA-1 are unsafe for integrity guarantees.
Mnemonic: timeline showing deprecation of weak hashes
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe Merkle tree construction
Explain leaf hashing, pairing, parent hash calculation, and the formation of the Merkle root included in a block header.
Mnemonic: small tree diagram or stacked cards
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Analyze Merkle proofs and pitfalls
Show how SPV clients verify with a small Merkle path and identify common implementation errors that break verification.
Mnemonic: checklist of proof steps
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
