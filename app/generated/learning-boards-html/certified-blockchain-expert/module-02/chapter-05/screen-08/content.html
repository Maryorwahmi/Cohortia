PoS trade-offs and failure modes

Understand where PoS requires careful governance and protocol choices.

Nothing-at-stake

Early PoS allowed validators to support multiple forks cheaply. Slashing and attestations reduce this risk.

Centralization risk

Large stakers or pooled services can concentrate voting power and block proposal influence.

Complex security model

Security now depends on the economic cost of acquiring stake, which can be dynamic and context-dependent.

Concrete scenario

Liquid staking providers may aggregate many users’ stakes, increasing single-point influence unless protocols limit concentration.

Pro tip

Design slashing to distinguish malicious equivocation from accidental downtime and provide clear restitution or governance paths for contested cases.
