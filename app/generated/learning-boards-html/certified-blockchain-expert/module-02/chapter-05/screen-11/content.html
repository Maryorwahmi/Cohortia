Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › When to choose each mechanism

Decision architecture

Frame your choice by three axes: energy cost, finality speed, and participant identity.

PoS — Public, energy-aware

Good for public chains seeking lower energy needs and faster finality than PoW.

DPoS — High throughput

Best when performance and predictable block times are essential and representative governance is acceptable.

PBFT — Permissioned finality

Ideal for consortiums and private networks where participants are known and immediate finality is required.

Concrete scenario

A public payments network prioritizing sustainability may select PoS. A gaming chain needing thousands of TPS might lean toward DPoS with strong voter accountability.

Checklist
  • - Define acceptable centralization level.
  • - Decide on finality guarantees required.
  • - Identify whether participants are permissioned and known.
Key Takeaway

Pick consensus by enforcing the assumptions you need: economic penalties, voter accountability, or known identities.
