Alternative Consensus Mechanisms

Learn why PoS, DPoS and PBFT exist, how staking and delegation work, and how to pick the right mechanism for a blockchain project.

In this lesson

You will compare energy, security, and decentralization, study staking mechanics and slashing, see DPoS elections, and review PBFT rounds for permissioned networks.

Why it matters

Consensus choice shapes who can validate, how fast transactions finalize, and how secure the chain is against economic and Byzantine attacks.
