Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Proof of Stake (PoS)

Consensus secured by locked stake rather than computational work.

Validator

A participant who stakes tokens to propose and attest to blocks, earning rewards when honest.

Staking

Locking tokens as collateral to participate in consensus and governance.

Summary takeaway

Staking, validators, and slashing create the economic incentives that replace energy as the main cost of attacking a PoS network.
