Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT)
Core Concept

PBFT rounds for agreement

A primary proposes; replicas prepare and then commit. Finality occurs once enough commits are collected.

Pre-prepare

Primary proposes an ordered request to replicas.

Prepare

Replicas broadcast they received and agree on the proposal.

Commit

After collecting enough prepares, replicas commit and the request becomes final.

Fault tolerance

PBFT tolerates up to f faulty nodes where f < N/3; with fewer faults the protocol guarantees safety and liveness.

Concrete use

Private ledgers and enterprise networks where participants are known and can be authenticated commonly use PBFT variants.

Misconception

PBFT is not well-suited to large, permissionless networks because of quadratic communication growth as nodes increase.
