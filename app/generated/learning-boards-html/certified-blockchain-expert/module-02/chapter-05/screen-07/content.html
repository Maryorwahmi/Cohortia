Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Advantages of Proof of Stake
Comparison

PoS benefits at a glance

Energy efficiency

No mining race; validators stake capital instead of consuming electricity.

Faster finality

Many PoS protocols use attestations to reach finality faster than deep block confirmations.

Lower hardware barrier

Validators need stable infrastructure and stake rather than specialized ASICs.

Concrete example

Ethereum’s transition to PoS reduced energy use and altered the security model from hardware cost to economic stake and slashing.

Misconception

Assuming PoS automatically increases decentralization. Stake concentration can still centralize influence over time without proper delegation designs.

Key Takeaway

PoS shifts security costs to economic stake, reducing energy and often improving finality, but economic design is critical to preserve decentralization.
