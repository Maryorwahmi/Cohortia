Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Hands-on activity: PoS vs DPoS scenarios
Activity

Compare two governance scenarios

Scenario 1 — Pure PoS

100 validators; protocol upgrade needs two-thirds of total stake to approve. Consider influence of a 30% staker.

Scenario 2 — DPoS

100,000 token holders elect 21 witnesses; upgrades require two-thirds of witnesses. Consider influence of a holder with 5% of tokens.

Questions
  • - Who can block or influence upgrades and how?
  • - Which scenario is more centralized in practice?
  • - For a payment network, which model better fits and why?
Hint

In PoS, stake-weighted voting gives direct economic influence; in DPoS, influence flows through elected delegates and voter turnout.

Common misconception

Believing that a large token holder always blocks upgrades. In PoS they need a threshold; in DPoS, influence is indirect and depends on delegate control.

Key Takeaway

Practice reasoning about who holds decision power: stake directly in PoS, delegates in DPoS—and design governance accordingly.
