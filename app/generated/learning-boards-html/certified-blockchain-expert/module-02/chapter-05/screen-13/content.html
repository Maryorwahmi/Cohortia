Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Trade-offs recap and real-world matches

Mechanism → Example → Why it fits

PoW — Bitcoin

High security through energy; decentralized but energy intensive and slower finality.

PoS — Ethereum

Energy reduction, stake-based security, and faster finality after protocol design choices like slashing.

DPoS — EOS/TRON

High throughput with elected delegates; chosen when performance and predictable finality are prioritized.

PBFT — Hyperledger variants

Immediate finality among known participants in enterprise or consortium settings.

Key questions
  • - Open membership or permissioned?
  • - Is energy or throughput the priority?
  • - What centralization is acceptable?
Designer note

Document the trust assumptions and governance steps you will rely on before committing to a mechanism.

Key Takeaway

Match the consensus to your application's openness, performance needs, and governance capacity — there is no one-size-fits-all.
