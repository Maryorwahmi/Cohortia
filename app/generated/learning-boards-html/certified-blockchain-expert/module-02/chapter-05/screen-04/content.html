Core Concept

Why alternatives to PoW exist

Different consensus mechanisms reassign the cost of security from computation to financial stake or to known identities to better meet energy and performance goals.

  • Energy and hardware are expensive in PoW, so PoS reduces energy by using stake as collateral.
  • Finality differs: PoW is probabilistic; PoS and PBFT can offer faster or immediate finality.
  • Membership matters: PBFT assumes known nodes and scales poorly as participants grow.
Concrete example

Ethereum moved from PoW to PoS to reduce energy consumption while keeping chain security through staking and slashing.

Misconception

Lower energy use does not guarantee better decentralization; capital concentration can still centralize power.

Trade-off cards
PoW

High energy, probabilistic finality, strong decentralization through mining competition.

PoS

Lower energy, stake-backed security, faster finality but requires economic design like slashing.

PBFT

Immediate finality among known nodes, high throughput for small networks, not suitable for open permissionless systems.

Key Takeaway

Choose a consensus mechanism by prioritizing energy, finality, and participant identity for your application.
