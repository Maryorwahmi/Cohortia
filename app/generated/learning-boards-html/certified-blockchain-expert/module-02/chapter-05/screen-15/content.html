Slashing

Protocol penalty that removes stake for provable misbehavior to enforce honest validation.

Delegated Proof of Stake

A vote-elected subset of delegates produce blocks, enabling high throughput at the cost of more centralization.

PBFT

Multi-round voting protocol for known participants that offers immediate finality when faults < N/3.

Final takeaway

Remember these concise definitions as you evaluate consensus for energy, governance, and finality requirements.
