Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Staking, validators, and slashing
Definition
Economic security
Staking

Lock tokens as collateral to participate in validation and governance.

Validator

A staker who is active in proposing and attesting to blocks and earns rewards.

Slashing

Penalties that remove stake for provable misbehavior to deter attacks.

Walkthrough

Validator signs block; other validators attest; finality emerges when enough attestation confirms the block and slashing rules discourage equivocation.

Concrete example

If a validator signs two conflicting blocks, the protocol can detect both signatures and slash the validator’s locked stake.

Common mistake

Assuming slashing eliminates all operational risk. Poorly designed slashing can harm honest validators after outages.
