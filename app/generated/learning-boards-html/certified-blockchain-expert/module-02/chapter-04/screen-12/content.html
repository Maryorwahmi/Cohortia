Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Scalability Limits
Bottlenecks
Block interval and size cap maximum throughput. During spikes, users pay higher fees to be included, and confirmation times may lengthen for low-fee transactions.
Design trade-off: decreasing block time reduces latency but raises orphan rates and network centralization pressure.
Scaling approaches
Options include increasing block parameters, optimizing propagation, and adding off-chain or layer-two solutions. Each approach affects decentralization and security differently.
Misconception: a single parameter change fixes everything. In reality, increases in throughput demand holistic changes to maintain security and decentralization.
Example: doubling block frequency without faster propagation will raise orphan rate and advantage well-connected miners.
Check: how do higher fees during congestion affect user behavior and miner revenue?
