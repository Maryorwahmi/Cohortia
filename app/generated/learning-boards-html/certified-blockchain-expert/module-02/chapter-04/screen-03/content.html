Applied Lab & Assessment Objectives
Run the Python miner
Measure time-to-find for difficulties 4, 5, and 6, and record how time grows with each increment. Consider why the growth is exponential, not linear.
Predict and test
Before each run, predict how much longer the next difficulty will take. Compare your prediction with measured results and explain any discrepancy.
Write concise answers
Explain nonce vs difficulty target and describe how the 2,016-block adjustment responds to a sudden doubling of hash rate.
Reflect on trade-offs
Summarize two negative outcomes for a chain without difficulty adjustment and how they affect decentralization and security.
