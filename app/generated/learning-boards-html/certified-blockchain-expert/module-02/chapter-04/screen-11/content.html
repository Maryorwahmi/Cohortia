Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Energy & Trade-offs
Why energy is needed
The proof of real-world cost makes attacks expensive. Continuous hashing ensures that creating a long private chain requires sustained operational expense.
Design effect: more efficient hardware reduces per-hash cost, increasing total network hash rate and prompting difficulty increases to preserve block time.
Design trade-offs
PoW trades energy for robust, time-tested security. Alternatives may reduce energy but offer different security models and failure modes, so choice depends on application priorities.
Misconception: energy use is always negative. In PoW it underwrites economic costliness that deters manipulations.
Reflection: how might miners' location choices affect network decentralization?
Pro-tip: consider both energy source and cost per kilowatt-hour when evaluating mining efficiency.
Check: why does greater efficiency not reduce total energy use automatically?
