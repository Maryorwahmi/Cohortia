Public-key cryptography
Key pairs enable verification without revealing private secrets.
Digital signature
Signs the message digest to prove origin and detect tampering.
ECDSA & secp256k1
Curve-based signatures used for compact, strong keys in Bitcoin and Ethereum.
Summary takeaway
Sign hashes, verify with public keys, and protect private keys. These three practices are the foundation of secure blockchain operations.
