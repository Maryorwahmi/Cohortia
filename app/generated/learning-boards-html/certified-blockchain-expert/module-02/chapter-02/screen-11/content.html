Protecting private keys

Key secrecy is the single most important security control for blockchain assets. Choose storage methods according to risk tolerance and convenience needs.

Hardware wallets
Sign transactions offline inside a secure chip. Best for large balances.
Cold paper backup
Write seed phrases on paper and store in a safe. Protect against online compromise but guard physical risks.
Password managers / hot wallets
Convenient but exposed to malware and phishing. Use for small, frequent transactions only.
Operational checks
Test restores, use multi-location backups, and avoid single points of failure.
Common attack vectors
Phishing, malware, weak randomness, and careless backup disclosure. Plan to defend against each.
Hardware wallet visual
A small device signs the transaction internally and only sends the signature out, keeping the private key isolated.
Decision guide
For daily small amounts, convenience may win; for significant holdings, prefer hardware wallet and cold backups despite reduced convenience.
