Public-Key Basics

A pair of keys: one secret you keep and one public you share. The private key signs; the public key verifies.

Sign & Verify

Sign a transaction hash with your private key. Others confirm with your public key that nothing changed.

Safety & Backup

If the private key is lost or stolen, funds are gone. Backups and hardware wallets reduce risk.
