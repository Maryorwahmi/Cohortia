Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Quick Reference
Protect Private Keys
Never share private keys; back them up offline and test restores.
Sign the Hash
Hash the transaction, sign the digest, and verify by recomputing the hash.
Use Secure Storage
Prefer hardware wallets and cold storage for high-value keys; restrict hot wallets to small amounts.
One-line summary
Sign carefully, verify deterministically, and guard your private keys to uphold blockchain security.
