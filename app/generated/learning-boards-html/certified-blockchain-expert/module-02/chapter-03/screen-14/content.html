Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Consensus Mechanism

Protocol that enables nodes to agree on a single, consistent state using rules, evidence, and incentives.

Byzantine Generals

Thought experiment showing the difficulty of agreement when participants or messages may be malicious.

Proof of Work

A puzzle-based method where finding a solution is costly and verifying it is cheap, providing economic security for a chain.

Takeaway

Choose consensus by matching security, decentralization, and cost to the network’s purpose; PoW secures by making history costly to rewrite.
