Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Trade-offs of Proof of Work
Pros & Cons
Pros
  • Strong, measurable security via work
  • Permissionless participation
  • Simple verification
Cons
  • High energy consumption
  • Hardware specialization and potential centralization
  • Throughput limits due to difficulty adjustments
Practical considerations

When choosing PoW, operators accept energy costs in exchange for a widely verifiable security model. For payment systems where censorship resistance and immutability are paramount, PoW remains attractive. For private or low-energy systems, alternatives may better match goals.

Designer prompt

If you run a public currency network, which trade-off would you optimize: absolute security or lower environmental cost? Explain the short-term and long-term consequences of your choice.

Key Takeaway

PoW’s value is security under resource cost; use cases decide whether that cost is acceptable.
