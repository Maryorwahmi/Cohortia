Introduction to Consensus Mechanisms

Understand how decentralized networks reach agreement, why failure would break a blockchain, and how Proof of Work secures shared state.

In this lesson

Define consensus, study the Byzantine Generals Problem, learn PoW mechanics, and compare alternatives.

Why it matters

Without consensus the ledger diverges. Consensus is the backbone of integrity, security, and trust in a permissionless network.

Key Takeaway

Consensus lets many parties maintain a single, reliable ledger; Proof of Work secures agreement by making block creation costly to find but cheap to verify.
