Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Alternative Consensus
High-level comparison
Proof of Stake (PoS)
Block creators chosen by stake. Low energy, different economic attack surfaces tied to stake distribution.
Delegated PoS (DPoS)
Holders elect delegates to produce blocks. Efficient but may concentrate power among delegates.
PBFT-style
Voting rounds with known participants. Fast and deterministic finality for permissioned setups; relies on trusted membership.
Design prompt

Match a consensus model to an application: choose which model fits a high-throughput private ledger versus a permissionless cryptocurrency, and explain why.

Misconception

No consensus model is universally best; each makes trade-offs in security assumptions and resource requirements.

Key Takeaway

Alternatives to PoW reduce energy or change trust assumptions; choose based on what the network must protect and which trade-offs are acceptable.
