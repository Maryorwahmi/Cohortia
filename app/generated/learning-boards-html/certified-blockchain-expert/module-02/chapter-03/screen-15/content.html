Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › What to Remember
Agreement without authority

Consensus lets many independent nodes behave as a single trusted system.

Handle malicious actors

Design protocols to tolerate a bounded number of faulty or malicious participants.

Economic security

Proof of Work turns security into a resource cost that defends the ledger.

Final thought

When you evaluate a consensus mechanism, ask how it defends against conflicting histories, how it tolerates faults, and what economic or performance trade-offs it requires.
