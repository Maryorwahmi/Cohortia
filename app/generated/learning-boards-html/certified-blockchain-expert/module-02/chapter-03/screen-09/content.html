Why miners mine

Economic motive

Rewards compensate miners for hardware, electricity, and maintenance. They ensure miners find it rational to secure the network rather than attack it.

Reward flow
  1. Miner finds valid proof and broadcasts block.
  2. Network verifies and accepts the block.
  3. Miner claims block reward once confirmations are sufficient.
Risks and distribution

Large rewards can encourage specialized operations, concentrating hashing power. Concentration increases risk of manipulation even if economically costly to execute.

Misconception

Rewards do not eliminate risk; they change the cost-benefit calculus for attackers but do not make attacks impossible.

Key Takeaway

Incentives are a central part of consensus; economic alignment reduces attacks but must be combined with protocol design to ensure resilience.
