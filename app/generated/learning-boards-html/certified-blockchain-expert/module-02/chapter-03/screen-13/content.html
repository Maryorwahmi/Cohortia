Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Assessment & Pitfalls
Assessment prompts
  • Explain how the Byzantine Generals Problem relates to double-spending.
  • Describe how PoW makes rewriting history economically expensive.
  • Design a short protocol for five nodes tolerating one traitor and justify the confirmation rule.
Common pitfalls
  • Equating consensus with simple majority without verification rules.
  • Ignoring the probabilistic nature of PoW finality.
  • Assuming energy is wasted rather than a costed defense mechanism.
Practice task

Write a one-paragraph answer mapping a double-spend attempt to the generals analogy, and explain why PoW forces attackers to out-resource honest miners to succeed.

Key Takeaway

Assessments should test mapping between analogy and blockchain behavior and require explicit reasoning about trade-offs and costs.
