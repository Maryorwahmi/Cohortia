Analogy

Grain of sand search

Finding a unique grain on a beach is hard; showing that grain is easy. This captures PoW’s core trade-off between searching effort and verification simplicity.

Mapping
  • Search = mining trials
  • Unique grain = valid nonce/hash
  • Show grain = quick verification by peers
Activity

Follow the thought experiment: design a protocol for five generals with one traitor. Consider message rounds, confirmations needed, and how to detect contradictions.

Starter protocol
  1. Round 1: each general broadcasts proposed action.
  2. Round 2: each general rebroadcasts what they heard from others.
  3. Decision: choose the action that appears in a majority of collected reports.
Key Takeaway

Analogies make protocol concepts intuitive; the generals exercise shows why rounds and redundancy matter to tolerate traitors.
