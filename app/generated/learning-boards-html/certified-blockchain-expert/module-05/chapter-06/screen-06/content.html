Hybrid Pattern: Off‑Chain PII, On‑Chain Hash
Follow these steps to keep data mutable while proving integrity.
1. Collect & encrypt
Collect PII with consent and store it encrypted in a controlled off‑chain database.
2. Salt & hash
Generate a nonce or salt, compute a cryptographic hash of the structured PII, and keep the salt off‑chain.
3. Commit hash on-chain
Write only the salted hash on the ledger as an immutable commitment and audit record.
4. Erasure and rectification
Delete or update off‑chain PII; publish a new hash if record changes to maintain an auditable timeline.
Key Takeaway

A salted hash on chain proves past existence without exposing PII, enabling erasure of the off‑chain data when required.

Common mistake
Not protecting salts and linking metadata, which can allow reconstruction or re‑identification.
Pro tip
Rotate access keys, limit retention, and use hardware protections for salts and encryption keys.
