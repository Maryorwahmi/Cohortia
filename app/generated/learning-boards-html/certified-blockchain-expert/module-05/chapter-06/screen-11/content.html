Hybrid Compliance Architecture
Identity, storage, oracles, and governance working together.
Identity
KYC at gateways or verifiable credentials for privacy-preserving proofs.
Off‑chain storage
Encrypted, mutable store for PII with audit logs and erasure capability.
On‑chain commitments
Salted hashes or verifiable claims used to prove state without exposing PII.
Oracles & governance
Signed oracle feeds for sanctions and attestation status, plus governance rules for updates.
Operational controls
Key management, logging, incident response, and retention policies.
Legal agreements
Define roles, liabilities, and dispute resolution between actors.
Key Takeaway

Architectures must explicitly document trust boundaries and include technical and legal controls to limit compliance risk.
