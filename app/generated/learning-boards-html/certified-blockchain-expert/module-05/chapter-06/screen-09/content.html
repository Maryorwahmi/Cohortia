Smart Contract Liability
Legal enforceability depends on facts, contracts, and governance.
Where responsibility may land
Developer errors, deployer choices, auditor oversights, and operator conduct are all potential sources of liability depending on agreements and jurisdiction.
  • Documentation and terms of service shape expectations.
  • On‑chain code plus off‑chain agreements determine remedies.
  • Dispute resolution can be built in via arbitration clauses or on‑chain governance triggers.
Mitigations
Audits, insurance, clear legal terms, bug bounties, and designated dispute processes reduce exposure.
Misconception
'Code is law' is a technical view; legal systems still evaluate contracts, intent, and statutory duties when adjudicating disputes.
