Three practical definitions
OFAC / Sanctions
Government lists define prohibited parties; intermediaries must screen and can be held liable for failures to comply.
Smart Contract Liability
Legal responsibility for code failures depends on contracts, deployment roles, and jurisdictional law.
Off‑chain Data Storage
Store PII in mutable, controlled systems and write non‑revealing commitments to chain to enable erasure and auditability.
Final takeaway

Document trust boundaries, protect linking metadata, and choose oracle and governance models that match your compliance requirements.
