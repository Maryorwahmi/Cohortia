Regulatory Compliance and Legal Aspects
How identity, privacy, sanctions, and legal enforceability shape secure blockchain design.

This module focuses on practical ways to reconcile blockchain immutability with laws that demand identity verification, data erasure, and sanctions enforcement. Expect architectures that combine off‑chain storage, cryptographic commitments on chain, and oracle feeds for external compliance lists. We highlight tradeoffs, common mistakes, and design patterns a certified blockchain expert should recognize and propose.

In this lesson

KYC/AML challenges, GDPR constraints, OFAC and sanctions handling, smart contract liability, and oracle-based compliance patterns.
