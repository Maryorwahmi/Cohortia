Sanctions Compliance
The enforcement gap between permissioned and permissionless ledgers.
Operational challenge
On public chains, transactions are permissionless and cannot be blocked by protocol rules alone; intermediaries must perform screening and decide whether to process transactions to comply with sanctions.
  • Intermediaries are the usual compliance enforcement points.
  • Oracles can supply sanctions lists to contracts but introduce trust requirements.
  • Design must weigh decentralization vs. legal risk and potential penalties for non‑compliance.
Risk note
Non‑compliance can lead to severe liabilities for regulated entities that operate on‑ or off‑ramp services.
Solution pattern
Use trusted oracle feeds to provide up‑to‑date lists, combined with policy gates in contracts or middleware that prevent disallowed actions.
Common mistake
Assuming the protocol alone is sufficient to avoid sanctions exposure. Enforcement usually depends on off‑chain actors and trusted feeds.
