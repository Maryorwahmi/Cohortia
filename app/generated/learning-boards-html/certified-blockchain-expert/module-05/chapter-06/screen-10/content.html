Oracle Patterns for Compliance
Feeding sanctions and identity attestations into smart contracts.
Oracle modes
Centralized provider: simpler but single point of failure. Decentralized network: higher integrity but more complex. Hybrid: delegating aggregation to an off‑chain service, publishing signed snapshots.
Attestation types
Boolean checks, signed lists, zero‑knowledge attestations, or hashed proofs—avoid putting raw PII on chain.
Risk & mitigation
Compromised oracle leads to false enforcement. Mitigate with multisignatures, reputation, and cross‑checking sources.
Governance
Define who can change oracle sources and how updates are authenticated to avoid unauthorized changes.
Key Takeaway

Choose an oracle design that matches your compliance risk: more sensitive enforcement favors decentralized, auditable attestation models with clear governance.
