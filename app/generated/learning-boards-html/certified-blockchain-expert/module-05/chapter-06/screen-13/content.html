Operational Controls Checklist
Procedures that enforce and evidence compliance.
Key management
Use hardware protections for salts and encryption keys; rotate keys and control access tightly.
Logging & auditing
Maintain immutable logs for off‑chain access and record deletion events to prove compliance.
Oracle monitoring
Detect and alert on feed anomalies, stale data, or signature mismatches.
Incident response
Predefine steps for compromised oracles, data breaches, and legal requests, including notification flows and mitigation.
Human review & governance
Automated flags should escalate to trained reviewers; governance defines who may act on sanctions or disputes.
