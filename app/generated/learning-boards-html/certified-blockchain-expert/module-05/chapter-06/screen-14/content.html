Key Concepts
Three concise concept cards
KYC / AML
Verify identities and monitor transactions; implemented at gateways or via verifiable credentials.
GDPR & Immutability
Rights like erasure conflict with permanent ledgers; hybrid off‑chain/on‑chain patterns resolve the tension.
Oracles & Sanctions
Oracles supply sanctions and identity attestations; their integrity and governance determine enforceability.
Summary

Combine technical and operational controls—off‑chain storage, on‑chain proofs, oracle attestation, and governance—to meet legal requirements while preserving blockchain benefits.
