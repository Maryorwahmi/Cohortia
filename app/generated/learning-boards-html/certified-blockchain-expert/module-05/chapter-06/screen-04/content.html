Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › KYC/AML Comparison
Traditional Finance
Centralized accounts, identity docs, continuous monitoring by regulated entities.
Account-based identity
Banks link identity to accounts and keep centralized logs for audits.
Continuous AML monitoring
Transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting are routine.
Misconception
Assuming decentralized services cannot comply. In practice, compliance is achieved by locating verification at regulated interfaces or using verifiable credentials.
Blockchain Ecosystem
Pseudonymous addresses, on-chain transactions, and new identity patterns like DIDs and VCs.
Pseudonymity vs. identity
Addresses are not identities; linkages to off‑chain data create real identity associations.
Decentralized identity
Verifiable Credentials allow privacy-preserving attestations while enabling compliance checks at needed points.
Key Takeaway

Place KYC at legal choke points and use verifiable credentials to limit on‑chain exposure while preserving privacy.
