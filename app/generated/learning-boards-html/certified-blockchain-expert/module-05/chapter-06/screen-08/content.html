Sanctions Event: Typical Operational Response
What intermediaries do when a sanctioned address is identified.
Detection
Service providers monitor lists and chain activity to flag addresses that match sanctions data.
Action
Freeze custodial assets, refuse withdrawals, and report transactions to authorities as required by law.
Community & governance
Decisions about protocol responses may involve governance votes, upgrades, or community-led mitigation steps.
Legal exposure
Operators risk penalties if they fail to enforce sanctions restrictions when law requires them.
Reflection

Protocols and service providers must agree on roles: who detects, who acts, and how to prove compliance without undermining necessary decentralization.
