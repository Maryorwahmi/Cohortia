Transactional PETs
CTs, ring signatures, and CoinJoin each hide different transaction signals; combine as needed.
ZKP selection
SNARKs for compact proofs, STARKs for transparent, scalable proofs; weigh setup trust and proof size.
Operational safety
Cryptographic audits, key policies, and threat models are required to realize privacy gains.
Final takeaway

Treat PET selection as a design exercise: define privacy goals, required verifiability, and acceptable trust/performance trade-offs before choosing technologies.
