Architecture

Where PETs live

Map each PET to a system boundary: transaction layer, off-chain computation, or auditing channel. Each placement carries cost and trust implications.

Prover nodes

Off-chain workers generate proofs; they require compute resources and secure environments.

Wallets & keys

Clients must manage commitments, view keys, and encrypted payloads, affecting UX and security.

Constraints
  • Proof generation cost and latency — may affect UX.
  • On-chain verification gas and data cost — larger proofs increase chain resource usage.
  • Trusted setup logistics and ceremony management for SNARKs.
Key Takeaway

Design PETs with explicit responsibility boundaries, and document performance, trust, and audit assumptions for stakeholders.
