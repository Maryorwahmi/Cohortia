Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Transparency vs. Privacy
Core Concept

Ledger visibility and deanonymization

Public transaction data enables verification but also provides signals that can be correlated with off-chain information to re-identify users or reveal business flows.

  • Addresses are pseudonymous; clustering reveals owner patterns.
  • Transaction timing and amounts create fingerprints that can be linked to external events.
  • Simple practices like new addresses reduce but do not remove linkability.
Common mistake

Assuming pseudonymity equals privacy. Without additional PETs, transactions remain linkable and subject to deanonymization.

Definition
Why transparency helps

Network participants can audit state and detect fraud, preserving trust without central authority.

Why it hurts privacy

Visible flows reveal spending, balances, and relationships unless amounts or identities are concealed.

Example
A vendor's regular payouts can reveal revenue if amounts are public.
Takeaway
Identify which data must be private and which must remain verifiable before selecting PETs.
