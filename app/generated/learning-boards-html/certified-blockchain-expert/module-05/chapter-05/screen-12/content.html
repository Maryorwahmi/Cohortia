Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Privacy vs Auditability
Analysis

Balancing competing goals

Designers must make explicit trade-offs between data privacy, public transparency, and the ability to audit or regulate effectively.

Privacy gain

Reduced linkability and concealed amounts protect users and businesses.

Transparency cost

Less public data makes wide-audience verification harder and may complicate policy enforcement.

Mitigation

Selective disclosure, aggregated proofs, and auditable channels reconcile needs when designed carefully.

Design checklist
  • Identify which data must remain private and which must be verifiable.
  • Estimate proof generation and verification costs and their UX impact.
  • Plan selective disclosure and revocation paths for auditors and regulators.
