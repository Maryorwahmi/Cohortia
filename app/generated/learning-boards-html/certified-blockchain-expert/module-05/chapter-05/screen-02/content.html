Learning Objectives

Progress from explanation to mastery: Learn, Understand, Apply, Master.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain privacy challenges

Describe how public ledgers reveal transaction flows and how deanonymization can link addresses to identities. This skill matters for risk assessments and system design.

Verified Competency Milestone
02
Describe ZKPs and variants

Explain the zero-knowledge idea and compare zk-SNARKs and zk-STARKs for trusted setup, proof size, and scalability, so you can choose appropriate proofs.

Verified Competency Milestone
03
Understand Homomorphic Encryption

Explain how computations on encrypted data produce encrypted results, and when HE is practical for private analytics or confidential contracts.

Verified Competency Milestone
04
Evaluate privacy trade-offs

Analyze trade-offs between privacy and auditability and describe selective disclosure options for compliance without full public exposure.

Verified Competency Milestone
