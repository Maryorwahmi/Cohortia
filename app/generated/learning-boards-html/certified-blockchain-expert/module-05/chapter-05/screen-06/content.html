Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › zk-SNARKs vs zk-STARKs
Comparison

zk-SNARKs

Small, succinct proofs with fast on-chain verification, commonly used in privacy coins and rollups. Often requires a trusted setup ceremony.

  • Very small proof size — low verification cost.
  • Trusted setup required for many constructions — trust assumption.
  • Good fit when verification cost dominates system constraints.
Example use
Proving correctness of off-chain rollup state before committing on-chain.
Comparison

zk-STARKs

Transparent, quantum-resistant proofs designed for scalability; proof size and verification grow logarithmically with computation, but absolute proof sizes are larger than SNARKs.

  • No trusted setup — transparency reduces trust assumptions.
  • Quantum-resistant constructions.
  • Better for very large computations where setup transparency matters.
Example use
Large private computations where avoiding a trusted setup is a priority.
Key takeaway

Choose SNARKs for compact proofs and low verification cost when a trusted setup is acceptable; choose STARKs when transparency and quantum resistance are required and larger proofs are tolerable.
