Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Selective Disclosure
Concept

Auditable privacy

Selective disclosure provides authorized viewers with specific data or proofs without opening everything to the public, enabling compliance and auditability alongside privacy.

  • View keys let users share transaction visibility with auditors.
  • Proofs of solvency reveal aggregate holdings without exposing individual accounts.
  • Design must specify revocation, minimal disclosure, and secure key handling.
Cards
Concrete scenario

An exchange shares a view key with a regulator to prove compliance while keeping full order books private.

Misconception

Sharing a view key gives access to specific data; secure sharing and strict policy are required to avoid abuse.

Takeaway

Selective disclosure enables regulated use of PETs; design disclosure rules, revocation, and minimal exposure up front.
