Privacy-Enhancing Technologies
Tools and protocols that limit what public ledgers reveal while preserving integrity.
Zero-Knowledge Proofs
Prove a statement is true without revealing the secret witness.
Homomorphic Encryption
Perform computations on ciphertexts so decrypted results match plaintext operations.
Summary takeaway

Choose PETs based on which data must remain private, the verification needs, and the acceptable performance and trust trade-offs for your system.
