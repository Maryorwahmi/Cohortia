Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Denial‑of‑Service (DoS) in contracts
DoS by gas exhaustion
Unbounded operations can make critical functions impossible to call due to block gas limits.
Example: Distributing funds by iterating over an ever‑growing array can eventually exceed gas limits and revert.
Mitigation: Use pull payments where each beneficiary claims funds individually, or paginate distributions across batches.
Design rule: Keep each transaction's gas bounded and avoid making operational correctness depend on unbounded loops.
Misconception check: Adding gas funds to an account is not a solution — the core problem is the operation's unbounded work. Redesign the flow instead of increasing gas allowances.
