Front‑running: an overview
Attackers can observe pending transactions and try to execute first to capture profit.
Example
Watching a large DEX trade, an attacker submits their own trade with higher gas to be mined first and benefit from the expected price move.
Mitigation options
Commit‑reveal, obfuscating values, off‑chain ordering, or using relays. Each has usability and trust tradeoffs.
Misconception check: You cannot fully eliminate front‑running on public blockchains, but you can make profitable exploits harder and less likely by reducing exposed intent and adding protocol-level or application-level defenses.
