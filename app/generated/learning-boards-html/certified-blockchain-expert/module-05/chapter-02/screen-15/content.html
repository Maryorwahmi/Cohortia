Key Reminders
Short mitigation and process reminders to use before deployment.
Checks‑Effects‑Interactions
Order operations to prevent reentrancy by ensuring internal state is updated before external calls.
Reentrancy Guard
Use a mutex or OpenZeppelin's nonReentrant modifier to prevent nested calls to functions that transfer value.
Testing & Audit
Run testnet deployments, static analysis, manual audits, and consider a bug bounty to catch remaining issues.
Final takeaway: Treat security as part of development velocity — a consistent checklist, tools, and manual review reduce risk more than ad hoc fixes.
