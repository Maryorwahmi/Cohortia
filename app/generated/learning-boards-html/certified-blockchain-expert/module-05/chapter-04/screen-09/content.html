DID Methods: what to compare

Methods implement resolution and update semantics. Different methods trade off cost, latency, and recovery models.

did:ethr
An example method that leverages Ethereum for resolution and key anchoring.
did:ion
A Sidetree-based method that uses anchored operations with a focus on scalability and censorship resistance.
Decision factors
  • Resolution availability and latency
  • Cost to anchor or update DID Documents
  • Supported recovery and key rotation mechanisms
  • Interoperability with your verifier ecosystem
Misconception
Not all DID methods are interchangeable; method-specific rules determine how identifiers behave on their network.
Key Takeaway

Evaluate DID methods by their resolution semantics, cost, and recovery options to match project constraints and security goals.
