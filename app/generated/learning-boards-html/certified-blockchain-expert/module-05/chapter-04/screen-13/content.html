Resolving a DID

Inspect the example DID and interpret the resulting DID Document to identify public keys, authentication methods, and services.

Example DID
did:ethr:0x2289569055848529080064239828D2209772605E
What to identify
  • The id field matching the DID.
  • Keys listed under verificationMethod and which are used for authentication or assertion.
  • Any service endpoints and their purpose.
Analyzing the DID Document

When the resolver returns JSON, locate verificationMethod entries and match referenced ids in authentication. Confirm that the public key verifies signatures made by the Issuer. Note that service endpoints are metadata, not private keys.

Misconception
Do not treat public keys or service endpoints as secret. They enable verification; secrets must remain in the holder's wallet or secure hardware.
Key Takeaway

Resolving and reading a DID Document builds confidence in distinguishing public verification material from private secrets and evaluating issuer trust.
