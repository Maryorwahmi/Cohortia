Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Decentralized Identifier (DID)
A unique, cryptographically verifiable identifier owned by the subject, resolvable to a DID Document.
Self‑Sovereign Identity (SSI)
A framework where people control their identities, enabling privacy, portability, and persistent identifiers.
DID Document
A JSON‑LD document listing public keys, service endpoints, and verification methods for a DID.
Summary Takeaway

These three concepts — DID, SSI, DID Document — together explain how decentralized identity publishes verification material while returning control to individuals.
