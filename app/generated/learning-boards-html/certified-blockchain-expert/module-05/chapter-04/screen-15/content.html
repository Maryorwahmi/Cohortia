Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Verifiable Credential (VC)
A signed digital claim a Holder can present; Verifier checks signature and issuer trust.
Issuer / Holder / Verifier
Roles that create, store, and verify credentials in the DID ecosystem.
Minimal Disclosure
Reveal only the attributes required to satisfy a verifier's request, protecting privacy.
Final Takeaway

Together, credentials, participant roles, and minimal disclosure enable verifiable and privacy‑preserving identity interactions in decentralized systems.
