Use Case
Where decentralized identity adds value

Practical scenarios show how minimal disclosure and portability reduce friction and central exposure.

Web3 Login
Authenticate with DID control rather than passwords.
Supply Chain
Attach verifiable origin claims to products.
Healthcare
Patients grant temporary access to records using selective disclosure.
KYC/AML
Streamline identity checks with issuer‑signed attributes shared across institutions.
Concrete Scenario
For KYC, a bank accepts a credential issued by a government agency. The user presents only the verified attribute needed for onboarding. The bank verifies the issuer signature and records minimal transaction metadata, avoiding repeated document uploads.
Misconception
DIDs do not eliminate regulatory checks; they change how verified attributes are exchanged while preserving auditability through cryptographic proofs.
Key Takeaway

DIDs and VCs apply across domains where verifiable, minimal disclosure of attributes reduces friction and exposure to breaches.
