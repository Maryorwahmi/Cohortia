Process Flow
Verifiable Credentials in action
Issuer
Signs a credential asserting a claim, then issues it to the Holder.
Holder
Stores credential in a wallet and decides what to disclose to a Verifier.
Verifier
Requests proof and verifies signatures against trusted Issuer keys referenced via DIDs.
Concrete Example
For age checks, a Holder can present a credential that proves "over 21" without revealing date of birth or other personal data. The Verifier validates the credential signature and issuer trust before accepting the claim.
Misconception
VCs do not remove the need for trusted issuers; they replace centralized checks with cryptographic verification tied to issuer trust policies.
Key Takeaway

Verifiable Credentials enable privacy-preserving proof of claims, but they rely on issuer signatures and verifier trust decisions.
