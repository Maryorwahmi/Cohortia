Why audits are essential
Immutable deployments and irreversible financial impact demand pre-deployment assurance.
Definition
A systematic review to find security and logical flaws before deployment.
Why it matters
  • Immutable contracts make fixes expensive and risky.
  • Small logic errors can lead to large financial losses.
  • Audits preserve user trust and project reputation.
Concrete Example
A contract with an access-control mistake can let any caller change ownership or drain funds; an audit finds the missing check before deployment.
Common misconception
Relying solely on automated tools will miss subtle business logic interactions that only a human review reveals.
Key Takeaway

Treat auditing as insurance: design your development timeline so audits complete before final deployment and include re-audits after fixes.

Audit roles and methods
Automated tools — quick scans for known patterns like reentrancy and integer issues.
Manual review — deep inspection of business logic and interactions between contracts.
Formal verification — mathematical proofs for critical properties on small, critical components.
Why combine methods?
Automated tools provide breadth, manual review provides depth, and formal verification provides the highest assurance for critical invariants. Together they reduce different classes of risk.
