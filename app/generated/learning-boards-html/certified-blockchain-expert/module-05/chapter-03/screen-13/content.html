Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts — Additional Notes
Formal Verification
Use mathematical proofs for the smallest, most critical invariants when high assurance is required.
Vulnerability Severity
Classify findings by exploitability and impact to guide remediation order.
Re-audit
Confirm fixes and check for regressions after remediation; don’t skip this final validation step.
Summary Takeaway

Audit practice combines tools and expert review, targets the highest-risk components for formal assurance, and closes the loop with re-audit after remediation.
