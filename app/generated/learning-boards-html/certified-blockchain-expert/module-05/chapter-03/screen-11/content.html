Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Developer Safety Notes
Practical design patterns
  • Use battle-tested libraries for tokens and access control.
  • Validate all external inputs and authenticate cross-contract messages.
  • Update internal state before external calls and employ reentrancy guards where needed.
  • Plan upgradeability carefully and audit any upgrade mechanism itself.
Testing and CI
Integrate automated scanners into CI, maintain exploit-focused tests, and ensure every critical change triggers a design review.
Common mistake
Applying quick fixes without re-audit. Fixes can introduce new risks and must be verified by auditors.
Key Takeaway

Reduce audit burden by adopting secure libraries and patterns, validating inputs, and making security checks part of development workflow.
