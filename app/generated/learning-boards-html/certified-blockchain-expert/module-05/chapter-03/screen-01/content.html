Auditing Blockchain Systems and Smart Contracts
A focused walkthrough of why audits are essential, what methods auditors use, and how to structure a reliable, repeatable audit lifecycle.
In this lesson
Understand methodologies, follow the audit phases, run basic static analysis, and learn from real incidents to improve future audits.
Why audits save value
Immutable ledgers make early detection critical to avoid costly migrations.
Method mix
Combine scanners, human review, and formal proofs where appropriate.
Key Takeaway

Plan audits early, insist on manual review of business logic, and reserve formal verification for the smallest critical components.
