Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Consensus Architecture
Architecture view
Components shaped by consensus
Consensus affects validator roles, reward distribution, slashing rules, and node requirements. These in turn affect decentralization and attack surface.
Validator composition
Who runs validators? Are they exchanges, institutions, or many individuals?
Incentive rules
Reward schedules, slashing conditions, and unbonding periods shape behavior and attack deterrence.
Practical examples
PoW networks watch pool sizes and geographic spread; PoS networks monitor stake distribution and delegation patterns. Both may add protocol checks and off-chain governance to react to concentration risks.
Operational mistake
Allowing exchanges to auto-delegate large stakes without decentralization controls can centralize voting power quickly.
Key Takeaway

Consensus determines the resource that secures the network. Architecture and governance must ensure that resource stays widely distributed and costly to corrupt.
