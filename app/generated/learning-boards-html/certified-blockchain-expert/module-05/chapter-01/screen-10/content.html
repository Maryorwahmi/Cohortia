Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › PoW vs PoS
Comparison table
Proof-of-Work
Security asset: computational power. Costly to acquire and run. Vulnerable to hash-power concentration and energy economics.
Proof-of-Stake
Security asset: staked tokens. Attacks risk slashing and self-defeating price impact. Vulnerable to stake concentration and governance capture.
Shared reality
Both use economic disincentives to make attacks costly. The defender’s job is to ensure the resource that secures the chain remains costly and widely distributed.
Concrete assessment checklist
  • Measure concentration: top miners or stakers share percentage.
  • Estimate cost: what does an attacker need to buy or stake to reach majority?
  • Consider incentives: would attacking reduce token value and thus attacker's stake?
Misconception
PoS is not inherently less secure because it lacks energy costs; it trades one scarce resource for another — capital — which can be equally prohibitive to acquire.
Key Takeaway

Compare the resource an attacker must control — computation or stake — and inspect its distribution to assess the true security of a consensus mechanism.
