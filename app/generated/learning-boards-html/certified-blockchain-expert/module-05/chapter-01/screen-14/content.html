Key Concepts
Three concise concept cards for quick recall.
Immutability
Hash-linked blocks create tamper evidence for recorded data.
Decentralization
Distribution of nodes reduces single-point-of-failure risk.
51% Attack
Majority control can enable reorgs and censorship; feasibility depends on resource distribution.
Summary
Use hash checks, node/stake distribution analysis, and monitoring of concentration as quick signals when assessing a blockchain's real-world security.
