Hands-on & Analysis Objectives
Concrete verification tasks and evaluation practices.
Apply → Verify → Assess
01
Compute & Verify Hashes
You will hash transaction data using SHA-256 and confirm that any change alters the digest. This check is the fastest way to detect tampering.
Mnemonic: Change breaks hash
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Verify Digital Signatures
You will sign a message and verify it with the public key, observing how tampering breaks verification and makes the signature invalid.
Mnemonic: Signature ties key to message
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Assess Consensus Risk
You will estimate attack cost for a 51 percent scenario and identify when PoA or small validators introduce centralization risk.
Mnemonic: Cost vs Control
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify Human Factor Risks
You will spot operational mistakes like poor key management, trusting unknown nodes, or assuming smart contracts are inherently safe.
Mnemonic: People are often the weak link
