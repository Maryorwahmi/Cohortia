What to Remember
Three quick cards with actionable reminders.
Hashing
Deterministic, non-reversible digests used for integrity checks and identifiers.
Signatures
Bind a private key to a message, enabling verification and non-repudiation when keys are secure.
Operational Controls
Hardware wallets, multi-sig, audits and governance are necessary to realize cryptographic guarantees.
Final takeaway
Combine cryptography, distributed design, and strong operations to build resilient systems; each layer covers gaps left by the others.
