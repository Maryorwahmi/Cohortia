Comparison

On‑Chain vs Off‑Chain

On‑Chain
Immutable, verifiable, tamper‑resistant. Good for assets and rule enforcement.
Off‑Chain
Fast, cheap, and scalable. Good for large files, UI assets, and analytics.
Concrete example
Store an NFT image on IPFS and keep only the content hash on the smart contract to prove authenticity while saving cost.
Trade‑off guide
  • On‑chain: security and transparency at the cost of gas and latency.
  • Off‑chain: performance and storage efficiency with additional trust considerations.
  • Use cryptographic hashes or oracles when off‑chain data must be trusted.
Pro Tip

Keep only the minimal verifiable reference on‑chain and fetch larger content off‑chain using that reference.
