Design Checklist for DApps

1. Need for immutability?
Store on‑chain only if public verification or censorship resistance is required.
2. Cost & frequency
Estimate gas and prefer off‑chain for frequent or large updates.
3. Privacy concerns
Avoid storing sensitive data openly on public chains.
4. Hosting strategy
Choose between rapid updates and censorship resistance for the frontend and assets.
5. Upgradeability & failure modes
Plan for upgrades or compensating controls if bugs or exploits occur.
Key Takeaway

Use this checklist to justify on‑chain choices and reduce surprise costs while protecting user experience and privacy.
