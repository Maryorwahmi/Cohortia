Key Concepts

DApp Architecture
Three layers: frontend, middleware, and on‑chain backend that enforce rules.
Tech Stack
React, ethers.js/web3.js, Solidity, Hardhat, IPFS form a practical toolchain.
On‑chain vs Off‑chain
Keep proofs on‑chain and heavy data off‑chain; use content hashes to link them.
Summary Takeaway

Architect DApps by assigning responsibilities to the layer that best matches security, cost, and performance requirements.
