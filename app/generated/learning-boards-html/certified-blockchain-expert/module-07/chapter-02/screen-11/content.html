Architecture

Hosting Frontend & Assets

Choose hosting based on needed guarantees: fast updates or censorship resistance.

Centralized Host
Simple deployment and quick updates; may be vulnerable to takedown.
Decentralized Storage (IPFS)
Content addressed and persistent; pair with on‑chain content hashes for verification.
Hybrid
Host frontend centrally for rapid iteration and store critical assets on IPFS for permanence.
Scenario
An artist might publish artwork to IPFS and store the CID in a contract while serving the UI on a centralized host to iterate on marketplace features. The content remains verifiable even if the UI changes.
Key Takeaway

Choose hosting that matches your need for permanence versus agility and keep verifiable links on‑chain when necessary.
