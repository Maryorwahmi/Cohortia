What to Remember

Map Responsibilities
Assign each function to the layer that best matches trust and performance needs.
Pick a Minimal Stack
React + ethers.js + Solidity + Hardhat covers most projects with low friction.
Justify On‑Chain
Store only verifiable proofs on‑chain and keep heavy data off‑chain with content hashes.
Summary Takeaway

Use the checklist and minimal stack to design efficient, secure, and user‑friendly DApps.
