Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Assessment

Sample assessment questions

Question 1
Which feature distinguishes a DApp from a Web2 app? Correct reasoning: DApps execute backend logic via smart contracts on-chain rather than on centralized servers.
Question 2
Why does a transaction fail with 'insufficient funds for gas'? Explain gas as the unit of computation that prevents spam and compensates validators; remedy: supply native tokens on the correct network.
Scoring guidance
Good answers define terms, provide an example, and include a short reflection on the trade-offs involved. For gas, show both the practical fix and the conceptual role gas plays in network health.
