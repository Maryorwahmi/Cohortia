Motivations for DApps

Transparency & Auditability
On-chain rules and history can be inspected by anyone, increasing accountability.
User Sovereignty
Users keep custody of assets and can participate in governance through tokens.
Censorship Resistance
No single authority can easily remove or block application functionality on-chain.
Composability
Open protocols can be combined to create new services and rapid innovation.
Example trade-off
A transparent finance app enables trustless inspection but exposes transaction history publicly. Decide whether transparency or privacy is the priority for your users.
