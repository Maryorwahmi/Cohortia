Learning Objectives

Four competencies you will achieve and verify.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define Web3
Explain how Web3 differs from Web2 and name the core tenets: decentralization, censorship resistance, and user ownership. Why it matters: it changes trust boundaries and architecture.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain core principles
Describe decentralization, censorship resistance, and ownership, and contrast resulting risks like immutability and the need for audits.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify DApp components
Map frontend, web3 library, smart contracts, blockchain network, and optional decentralized storage, and explain their roles in a transaction flow.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Evaluate motivations & trade-offs
Recognize why developers choose DApps and what costs arise: gas fees, scalability limits, and UX complexity. Assess when decentralization is the right tool.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
