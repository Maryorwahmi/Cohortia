Audit & Test
Smart contracts control assets; rigorous testing and audits reduce irreversible risk.
Clear Wallet UX
Design UIs that explain approvals and gas so users can make informed signing decisions.
Hybrid design
Use on-chain rules where trust matters and off-chain services where speed, cost, or privacy matter.
Final thought: weigh security, privacy, and accessibility in each design decision. Decentralization is a tool—apply it where its benefits outweigh its costs.
