Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Web2 vs Web3
Web2

Centralized model

  • Control: Single provider manages servers and data.
  • Custody: Platforms often hold user accounts and personal data.
  • Recoverability: Data can be modified or rolled back by operators.
  • Example: Traditional bank or social media platform.
Common misconception
Centralized systems are always safer; in fact, a single breach can expose many users at once.
Web3

Decentralized model

  • Control: Logic and state live in smart contracts and network consensus.
  • Custody: Users control keys and assets through wallets.
  • Transparency: Contracts and transactions are auditable on-chain.
  • Example: DeFi protocol replacing bank functions with smart contracts.
Why it matters
Shifting custody to users increases agency but also requires users to manage keys and understand gas costs.
Example scenario: A bank can reverse an erroneous transfer; a DeFi swap executes immutably on-chain and requires prebuilt safeguards. Think about whether reversibility or transparent immutability is more important for your use case.
