Activity: connect a wallet and observe

Step 1 — Wallet setup
Install MetaMask, create or import a wallet, and switch to a testnet like Sepolia so your observations are safe.
Step 2 — Connect
Visit a simple DApp, click connect, and approve the wallet connection. Notice what the DApp learns and what remains private.
Step 3 — Initiate
Start a transaction flow but do not confirm. Observe the wallet popup: gas fees, recipient, and approvals are shown before signing.
Step 4 — Reflect
Cancel if you do not intend to spend tokens. Reflect on what the wallet signs and what the DApp can do after connection.
Concrete observation
You will see a wallet popup listing gas fees and transaction details. The frontend cannot execute the transaction without your signature.
Safety note
Use a testnet to avoid spending real assets and always review transaction details in the wallet before signing.
