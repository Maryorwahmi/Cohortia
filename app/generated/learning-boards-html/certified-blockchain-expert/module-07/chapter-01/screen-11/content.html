Managing real-world trade-offs

Scalability & Fees
Public chains can slow and charge high fees when congested. Layer 2s are one mitigation mentioned in this chapter.
UX & Wallets
Users must manage wallets and gas; poor UX raises the chance of errors and abandoned flows.
Immutability & Security
Immutable contracts require audits and careful upgradeability plans because fixes are nontrivial after deployment.
Concrete example
A user receives an "insufficient funds for gas" error because they need native tokens for transaction fees; resolving it requires supplying the correct token to their wallet.
Common mistake
Assuming decentralization removes all operational risk; instead, design responsibility shifts to protocol design, tooling, and the user.
