Access Control
Protect privileged actions
Choose the right administrative model for your risk profile.
Owner-only modifiers
Simple to implement, but centralizes power in one key, increasing compromise risk.
Role-based access control
Assign roles to functions for principle of least privilege and clearer audits of permissions.
Multisig & delays
Require multiple approvals and optional time-locks for high-value operations to enable human review.
Key Takeaway

Design access control for least privilege, operational resilience, and auditability. Use multisig for critical decisions.
