Denial of Service
Gas limits and blocking patterns
Design to avoid scenarios where a single actor can make functions unusable.
Problem sources
  • Unbounded loops that grow with user input.
  • Assuming external calls always succeed.
  • Push payment patterns that iterate over recipients.
Mitigations
  • Use pull over push payments: let users withdraw their own funds.
  • Shard large operations off-chain or into batched on-chain steps.
  • Include pause and circuit-breaker mechanisms for emergencies.
Common mistake

Believing that occasional external-call failures are rare; they happen and must be handled to prevent full-service outages.
