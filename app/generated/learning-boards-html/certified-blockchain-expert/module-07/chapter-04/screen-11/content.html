Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Error Handling
Diagnose and Surface Errors
Use try/catch around async calls to classify errors and provide helpful messages to users. Distinguish three common cases and respond accordingly.
User rejected
Handle error.code === 4001 and show that the action was cancelled.
Transaction revert
Inspect error.data.message to extract require/revert reason and show it to the user.
Network/RPC
Check address, ABI, and provider network; provide retry or check-network guidance.
Pro Tip

Log full error objects in development, but present concise, actionable messages in production UIs.
