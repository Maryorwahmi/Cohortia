Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Software, Hardware and Human Oracles
Software Oracles
Fetch web APIs, databases, or blockchain data. Use for price feeds and public information.
Hardware Oracles
Collect sensor and device readings. Use for supply chain, environmental monitoring, and device attestation.
Human Oracles
Trusted individuals confirm complex or subjective events. Use when machines lack reliable interpretation.
Choosing the right source
Evaluate three properties: authenticity, freshness, and context. A price feed prioritizes freshness and authenticity; a sensor prioritizes physical attestation; a human oracle provides context where rules are fuzzy. When stakes are high, combine sources and aggregate results to reduce manipulation risk.
Use hardware oracles when physical truth is required and devices can be attested.
Use software oracles for frequent, low-latency price updates from trustworthy APIs.
