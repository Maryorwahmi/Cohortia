Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › The Oracle Problem
What it is
Blockchains are deterministic and cannot directly access external APIs or sensors. This intentional isolation preserves consensus but prevents direct real-world input.
  • Determinism ensures every node computes the same contract result.
  • External calls during execution can introduce nondeterminism and disagreement.
  • Oracles are needed to bring verified off-chain data on-chain in a controlled way.
Concrete example
A DeFi lending contract needs ETH/USD to value collateral. Without an oracle it cannot fetch the current price, preventing automatic margin checks or liquidations.
Misconceptions
An oracle does not magically make data trustless. It moves trust from one place to another. Centralized oracles introduce single points of failure. Decentralized networks reduce but do not eliminate trust assumptions.
Key Takeaway

Design your DApp assuming the oracle is part of the trusted boundary. Choose redundancy and freshness checks for critical flows.
