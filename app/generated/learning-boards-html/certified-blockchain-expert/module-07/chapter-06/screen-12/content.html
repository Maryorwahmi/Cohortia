Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Common Mistakes & Safety
Common mistakes
  • Using a single centralized oracle for critical financial decisions.
  • Not checking timestamps or round identifiers, leading to stale data usage.
  • Failing to validate fulfill caller identity and requestId, opening up malicious callbacks.
  • Underfunding LINK or ignoring gas costs required for node fulfillments.
Mitigations
Use decentralized feeds, add staleness checks, require multiple sources, secure fulfill functions, and monitor feed health and node diversity.
Pro tip
Record and verify the requestId on send and check it inside fulfill to ensure the callback corresponds to your request.
Warning

Relying on a single data source for high-value logic reintroduces trust and attack risk. Use aggregation for safety.
