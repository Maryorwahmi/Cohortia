Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › What to Remember
Trust Boundaries
Document who and what you must trust when using an oracle.
Decentralize Critical Feeds
Use aggregated feeds for any contract that affects user funds.
Validate Freshness & Format
Check decimals, timestamps, and round IDs before acting on data.
Final Takeaway

Treat oracle design as part of your security architecture. Choose decentralization and validation where user funds or safety are at stake.
