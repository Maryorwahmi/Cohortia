Oracles and Off-Chain Data
Why reliable external data is essential for useful DApps.
Blockchains cannot fetch off-chain information by design. Oracles act as bridges that securely deliver verified data to smart contracts. Choosing the right oracle pattern affects security, decentralization, and cost. Throughout this chapter you'll meet definitions, comparisons, Chainlink's architecture, sample Solidity code, and a hands-on deployment workflow for a price feed consumer.
