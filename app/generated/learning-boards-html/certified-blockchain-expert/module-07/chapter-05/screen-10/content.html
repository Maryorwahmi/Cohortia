Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Pinning & Persistence
What pinning does
Pinning marks content to be retained on a node and prevents it from being garbage collected during cleanup. It guarantees a node will continue to serve the content.
Example
After uploading NFT metadata, pin the CID on at least one reliable node or use a pinning service to avoid availability loss.
Common mistake
Assuming public gateways will always have your content. Gateways are convenient but not a guarantee of long-term hosting.
Persistence options
  • Run your own IPFS node and pin the content
  • Use a pinning service or multiple nodes for redundancy
  • Replicate content across trusted hosts
Key takeaway
Pinning is essential for production availability. Plan replication and pinning as part of your deployment checklist.
