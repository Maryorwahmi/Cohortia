Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Key Concepts
Decentralized Storage
Distributes files across peers for resilience and censorship resistance.
Content Addressing (CID)
Content is identified by its cryptographic hash, ensuring integrity and immutability.
Pinning
Marks content to be retained by nodes so it remains available over time.
Summary
Store compact proofs on-chain and large assets off-chain. Use IPFS CIDs for integrity, pin for persistence, and design for privacy where required.
