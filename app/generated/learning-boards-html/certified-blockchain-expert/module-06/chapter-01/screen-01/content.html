The Blockchain Scalability Trilemma

A practical framework for deciding which two properties a blockchain prioritizes and what is sacrificed as a result.

You will learn definitions with concrete examples, see how layered architectures are used to balance trade-offs, and learn an evaluation approach you can apply to real projects.

Coming up
Definitions • Trade-off patterns • Layer 1 vs Layer 2 • Case studies and hands-on analysis template
Why it matters
Trade-offs define performance, censorship-resistance, and cost. Choosing the right balance unlocks the appropriate applications, whether payments, smart contracts, or high-frequency data settlements.
