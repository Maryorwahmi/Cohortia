Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Learning Objectives
Practice template
Use the hands-on activity to analyze two blockchains and justify which two trilemma attributes are prioritized.
Checklist for analysis
  • Identify consensus and validator distribution.
  • Note throughput and latency characteristics.
  • Explain which property is compromised and why.
Tip
Use concrete technical details—block time, validator count, or L2 strategy—to justify your claim rather than marketing language.
Assessment
Prepare two short analyses (200–300 words each) as described in the hands-on activity template and be ready to explain the trade-offs you observed.
Sample prompt
Choose two blockchains and explain which two trilemma properties they prioritize and the consequences for users.
