Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Decentralization
Definition
Distribution of control and data across many independent participants, reducing single points of failure.
Benefit
Strong censorship resistance and resilience to targeted attacks.
Cost
Lower throughput and higher resource requirements for validators.
Misconception
Counting nodes alone is insufficient; consider distribution of influence and participation barriers.
Key Takeaway

Decentralization increases trust and resistance but often limits raw transaction throughput.

Concrete scenario
If validators must store large blocks and validate every transaction, small participants may drop out, concentrating validation in fewer, resource-rich operators.
Reflection
Ask whether your application requires broad public participation, or if permissioned validators are acceptable to gain throughput.
