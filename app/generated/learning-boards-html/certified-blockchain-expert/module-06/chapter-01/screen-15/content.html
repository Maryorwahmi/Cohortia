Scalability Trilemma
A blockchain can optimally achieve only two of decentralization, security, and scalability.
Decentralization
Distribution of control and data that reduces single points of failure and censorship risk.
Security
Cryptographic and economic strength that makes attacks costly and protects ledger integrity.
Takeaway
Use these concepts as a checklist when analyzing any blockchain architecture or claim.
