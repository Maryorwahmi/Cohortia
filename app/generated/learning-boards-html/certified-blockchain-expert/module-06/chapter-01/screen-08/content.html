Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Trade-off Scenarios
Scenario: Larger blocks
Increasing block payload raises throughput but increases storage and bandwidth needs and may exclude smaller nodes.
Consequence
Decentralization decreases; validator diversity shrinks.
Misconception
Throughput gains are always net positive; they can create new centralization pressures.
Scenario: Fewer, faster validators
A reduced validator set cuts latency and raises throughput, but hands more control to fewer operators and shifts trust assumptions.
Consequence
Scalability improves, decentralization drops, and attack surfaces change.
Reflection
Every change alters who can participate and what it costs to attack. Evaluate changes both technically and economically before adopting them.
