Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Misconceptions
Common overclaims
  • Claiming to maximize all three trilemma properties simultaneously.
  • Treating performance parameters as neutral rather than structural.
Skeptical questions
  • Who validates and how many independent validators exist?
  • Where does final settlement occur and what does Layer 2 assume?
  • What is the real cost to attack the network?
Practice prompt
When you read a project claim, map each technical detail back to how it affects decentralization, security, and scalability.
