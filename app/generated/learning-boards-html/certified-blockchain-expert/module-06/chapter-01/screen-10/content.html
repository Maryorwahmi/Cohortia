Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Engineering Strategies
Layered offloading
Move frequent interactions off the base chain and settle periodically to preserve security while increasing throughput.
Sharding
Partition state so that not every node processes every transaction, raising aggregate capacity while requiring cross-shard design.
Specialized validators
Use powerful or permissioned nodes to improve latency and throughput, accepting concentrated trust assumptions.
Concrete note
These strategies are often combined. For example, a base chain may shard while supporting rollups on top. Evaluate the total effect on guarantees.
