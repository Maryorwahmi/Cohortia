Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Bitcoin vs Throughput L1
Bitcoin (example)
Prioritizes decentralization and security. The trade-off is lower transactions per second, which the network accepts to maintain strong guarantees.
Assessment: excellent for censorship-resistant settlement and robust immutability.
Throughput-focused L1 (contrast)
May use fewer validators or faster blocks to raise throughput, at the cost of decentralization or changing security assumptions.
Assessment: suitable for high-throughput payment rails but requires scrutiny of validator control and attack incentives.
