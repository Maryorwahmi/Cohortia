Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › What the Trilemma Claims
Claim
A blockchain can only optimally achieve two of: decentralization, security, and scalability. This shapes every architectural decision.
Why it matters
It helps you decide which properties to accept or optimize based on the application’s needs.
Common misconception
Believing a single design can simultaneously maximize all three without meaningful compromises.
Key Takeaway

Use the trilemma as a decision lens: pick two to optimize and be explicit about sacrifices.

Concise definitions
  • Decentralization: distributed control and many independent validators.
  • Security: cryptographic and economic resistance to attacks.
  • Scalability: throughput and latency that supports growth.
Concrete example
Bitcoin prioritizes decentralization and security; as a result it processes relatively low transactions per second, which is an acceptable trade-off for its goals.
