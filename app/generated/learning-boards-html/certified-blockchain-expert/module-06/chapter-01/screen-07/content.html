Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Scalability
What scalability measures
Throughput, latency, and the network’s ability to grow without compromising performance.
Challenges
Every-node validation and storage replication limit raw throughput.
Trade-off example
Faster blocks can increase bandwidth and storage requirements, excluding smaller nodes.
Concrete question
Does your use case need finality on the base layer or can secondary layers handle fast interactions?
Key Takeaway

High throughput usually requires accepting constraints on who can validate or moving work off the base layer.
