Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Data Availability
What is data availability?
Ensuring published shard data can be retrieved and verified by other participants and light clients.
Why it matters
Missing or hidden shard data prevents verification and opens the network to invalid states or fraud.
DAC example
A Data Availability Committee attests that shard data is retrievable and may help reconstruct shards for light clients.
Trade-offs and checks
  • DACs reduce bandwidth burdens but add trust assumptions about committee honesty.
  • Designers must plan fallback paths if a DAC becomes unavailable or malicious.
  • Verify who can read and reconstruct shard data and how light clients validate commitments.
Common misconception

Treating availability solely as storage ignores accessibility and verifiability; a system must provide both for safe sharding.
