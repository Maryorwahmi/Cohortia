Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
Sharding
Dividing the network into shards that each process transactions and hold part of the state.
Parallel Processing
Multiple shards handle independent transactions concurrently to increase aggregate throughput.
Cross-Shard Communication
Protocols for message passing and atomic operations between shards to maintain consistency.
Summary

Keep these concepts ready: they form the core language for evaluating sharded designs and their security and performance trade-offs.
