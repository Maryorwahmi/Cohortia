Staged deployment steps
Sharding is phased: research, testing, coordination, and then wider rollout.
Research & Spec
Design protocols for cross-shard messaging, beacon coordination, and data availability.
Client & Consensus
Update clients and consensus rules to handle shard validation and validator rotation.
Testnets & Rollout
Run limited shards, verify cross-shard primitives, and stage broader deployment.
Why it’s long
Sharding touches consensus, client code, and application design. Each layer must be audited and tested to avoid regressions in security or finality.
