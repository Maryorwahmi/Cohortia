Cross-shard transaction flow
A three-step conceptual process ensures atomicity across shards.
Step 1 — Origin validation
Origin shard validates the transaction, locks or burns tokens, and emits a message to the destination shard.
Step 2 — Destination apply
Destination shard verifies the message and applies the state change, crediting the recipient.
Step 3 — Confirmation
Shards exchange receipts and finalize the operation so both sides reach a consistent state.
Example
Alice on Shard A locks tokens; a message arrives to Shard B and Bob is credited; receipts confirm finalization so tokens are not double-spent.
Design trade-offs
  • Atomic protocols are safer but add latency and complexity.
  • Optimistic or asynchronous routes are faster but need dispute-recovery mechanisms.
  • Careful message ordering and finality assumptions are required to prevent forks and replay attacks.
Common mistake

Assuming message arrival implies finality. Without finality guarantees you can see inconsistent intermediate states and potential double spends.
