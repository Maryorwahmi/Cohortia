Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Security & Beacon Chain
Threat model
Smaller per-shard validator sets can be easier targets. Coordination increases attacker cost.
Beacon chain role
Coordinates validators, provides randomness, and anchors finality across shards.
Random sampling
Randomly assign validators to shards to reduce predictability and targeted attacks.
Analogy
Think of the beacon chain as the traffic control center that rotates police officers across highways so no single highway is left unmonitored.
Practical checks
  • How are validators assigned and how often do they rotate?
  • What mechanism enforces shard attestations and penalties for misbehavior?
  • Does the design require a trusted committee for data availability?
Key Takeaway

Effective shard security uses coordination to make single-shard attacks costly while preserving overall decentralization through randomized, transparent validator assignments.
