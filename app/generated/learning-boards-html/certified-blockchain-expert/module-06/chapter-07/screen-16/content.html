Quick reference
What to Remember
VRF
Provable randomness with on‑chain proof verification; use for lotteries, fair trait assignment, and games.
Computation Oracle
Off‑chain compute with proofs. Ideal when on‑chain gas or complexity is prohibitive; choose ZK or optimistic verification.
Identity Oracle
Attests to attributes privately for compliance, access control, and reputation while minimizing exposed personal data.
Final takeaway
Always map requirements to verification strength, latency tolerance, and trust assumptions. That mapping guides the right oracle design for secure, reliable dApps.
