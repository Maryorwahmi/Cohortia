Supply Chain & RWA Tokenization
Attesting physical state and legal records on‑chain via oracle attestations.
Supply chain example
A shipment's temperature sensors publish signed readings. When readings remain within a contractual range, the smart contract releases payment to the carrier. Deviations trigger alerts and hold funds until dispute resolution.
Design note: aggregate multiple sensors and use signed attestation sets to reduce manipulation risk.
RWA tokenization example
Tokenized property needs verified ownership records and periodic valuation feeds. Identity oracles confirm legal ownership, while data oracles provide appraisal data that can trigger rebalancing in tokenized portfolios.
Key Takeaway

Use multi-source verification and accredited attestations to ensure on‑chain representations map reliably to off‑chain legal reality.
