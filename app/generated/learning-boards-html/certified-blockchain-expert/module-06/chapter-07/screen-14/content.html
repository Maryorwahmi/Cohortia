Security Challenges & Design Considerations
Anticipate attack vectors and choose mitigations early.
Common pitfalls
  • • Single‑node oracle dependence and centralization risks.
  • • Failure to verify proofs on‑chain or to check request IDs.
  • • Stale data assumptions for time‑sensitive payouts.
Mitigations
  • • Use aggregated sources and multi-signatures to reduce single points of failure.
  • • Choose ZK proofs for strong guarantees or optimistic schemes with challenge economics as appropriate.
  • • Define freshness, revocation, and dispute windows in contract logic.
Operational notes
Plan for key rotation, monitor oracle health, and ensure that user-facing guarantees match the actual latency and settlement model of your oracle setup.
Key Takeaway

Document trust assumptions, decentralize inputs where possible, and pick verification patterns aligned with the application's risk budget.
