Parametric Insurance: Automated Payouts
Contracts pay automatically based on oracle-verified events.
Example: crop insurance
A policy defines a region and rainfall threshold. A distributed sensor oracle publishes aggregated rainfall measurements. When aggregated data confirms a shortfall below the threshold, the contract triggers payouts automatically to holders of the policy token.
Design check: ensure aggregated sources and dispute windows to handle sensor anomalies.
Design considerations
  • • Source decentralization: aggregate multiple sensors to reduce manipulation risk.
  • • Freshness and measurement windows: define exactly how and when an event is measured.
  • • Dispute and fallback: plan for disagreements and how off‑chain arbitration may occur.
Key Takeaway

Parametric designs demand carefully chosen, verifiable data sources and mechanisms for aggregation and dispute resolution to be reliable.
