Definition
Wrapped Assets
Tokens on one chain that represent locked or reserved value on another.
Backing models

Custodial reserve or on-chain lock verified by bridge logic.

Use cases
  • Liquidity provision across chains
  • Collateral inside lending protocols
  • Cross-chain trading and composability
Evaluation tips

Verify redeemability, audit history, and who holds custody of the original asset.

Key Takeaway

Wrapped tokens enable multi-chain utility but require explicit backing and clear redemption rules to be trustworthy.
