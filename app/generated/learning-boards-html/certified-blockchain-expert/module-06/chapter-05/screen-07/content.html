Process Flow
Burn-and-Mint
Used when tokens are native on both chains or during migrations.
Typical steps
  1. Burn token on source chain.
  2. Produce and relay proof of burn.
  3. Mint equivalent token on destination chain.
Example scenario

A token issued on Chain A is burned, validators attest the burn, and Chain B mints the same amount to the user's address to preserve total supply across chains.

Risks & mitigations
  • Incorrect or replayed burn proofs may cause unauthorized mints.
  • Compromised minting authority can inflate supply.
  • Mitigation: auditable burn logs and decentralized verification.
Pitfall

Assuming burns are final without checking for replay protections and unique proof handling across chains.
