Core Concept
Cross-Chain Communication Protocols
What they do

Allow chains to exchange messages, call contracts remotely, and coordinate multi-step workflows across networks.

IBC example

IBC standardizes packet transfer, acknowledgement, and verification so sovereign chains can trade assets and data without a central intermediary.

Practical cautions
  • Messages may be delayed by finality or relayer checks
  • Design for retries, idempotency, and reconciliation
  • Understand each chain's finality model
Key Takeaway

Cross-chain messaging unlocks composability but requires careful handling of ordering, retries, and finality differences between chains.
