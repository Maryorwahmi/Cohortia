Custodial vs Non-Custodial Bridges
Contrast the security and operational trade-offs to pick an appropriate bridge.
Custodial (Centralized)
  • Operator holds assets on source chain.
  • Faster, simpler operational model.
  • Single point of failure and trust requirement.
When used

Often for convenience or when the operator's reputation and legal structure reduce perceived risk.

Non-Custodial (Decentralized)
  • Rely on smart contracts, proofs, or validator sets.
  • Stronger decentralization alignment, fewer single points of failure.
  • More complex and new attack surfaces exist.
Misconception

Decentralized designs can still fail if validators collude or private keys are compromised; decentralization reduces some risks but does not eliminate all risk.
