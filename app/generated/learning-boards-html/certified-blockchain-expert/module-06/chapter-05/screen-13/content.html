Due Diligence & Practical Safety Notes
Custody model

Is the bridge custodial? If so, who holds keys and what legal protections exist?

Audit history

Look for independent audits that cover verification and minting logic, not just surface code.

Operational controls

Timelocks, public proof logs, and clear incident response are key indicators of mature operations.

Practical Rule

Start with small transfers, verify proof publication, and gradually increase exposure as confidence grows.
