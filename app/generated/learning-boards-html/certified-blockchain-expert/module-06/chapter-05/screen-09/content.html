Security Risks & Common Vulnerabilities
Top vulnerabilities
  • Smart contract bugs in bridge logic
  • Compromised validator or operator keys
  • Replay or proof manipulation attacks
  • Economic exploits and oracle manipulation
Mitigation patterns
  • Independent audits and bug bounties
  • Multi-signature or threshold cryptography
  • Transparent proof publication and timelocks
  • Decentralized validator sets and slashing rules
Example of a risk

If validators are compromised, an attacker may mint wrapped assets without actual locks. Mitigation might include a delay for large mints, on-chain challenge windows, and diversified signers so no single key compromise yields a catastrophic mint.
