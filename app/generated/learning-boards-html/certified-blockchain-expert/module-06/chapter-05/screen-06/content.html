Process Flow
Lock-and-Mint
1
Initiation & Lock

User sends native asset to a bridge contract on the source chain. The contract locks the asset so it cannot be spent on that chain.

2
Verification

A validator set or watcher verifies the lock and produces cryptographic proof that is recognized by the destination chain's bridge logic.

3
Minting & Use

Destination contract mints a wrapped token representing the locked value, which the user can use in the destination chain's ecosystem.

Reverse flow (redeem)

To recover the original asset, the user burns the wrapped token on the destination chain and the bridge unlocks the original asset on the source chain after verification.

Concrete scenario

Send 10 ETH to the CohortiaBridge contract on Ethereum, validators verify the lock, CohortiaChain mints 10 wETH, and the user spends wETH in CohortiaChain dApps.

Common Mistake

Assuming wrapped tokens are autonomous; their redeemability depends on the bridge's verification and custody model.
