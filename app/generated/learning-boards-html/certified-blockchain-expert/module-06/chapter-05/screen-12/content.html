Label which component holds custody and which produces proofs to understand trust assumptions.
Risk & mitigation

Risk: validator compromise or stolen keys can create false proofs and unauthorized mints.

Mitigations: threshold signatures, decentralized validator selection, timelocks for large mints, public proof logs, and economic slashing.
