Validator Sets & Key Management
Validator threshold choices

A lower threshold risks collusion; a higher threshold can hurt availability. Balance depends on network size and incentives.

Key storage options
  • Single-host HSM (higher centralization)
  • Multi-party computation (MPC) or distributed keys
  • Hardware wallets with multi-sig
Governance & detection

Timelocks, on-chain reporting, and slashing mechanisms deter misbehavior. External monitors can watch for suspicious mint events and raise alarms during challenge windows.

Pro Tip

Prefer bridges with public validator lists, rotation policies, and a history of safe key management practices.
