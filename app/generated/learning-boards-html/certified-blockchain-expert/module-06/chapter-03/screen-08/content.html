Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Fraud Proofs
What it proves

Invalid state transition

A fraud proof shows that applying the posted transactions to the previous state would not produce the submitted new state root, demonstrating a fault.

  • Requires sufficient evidence: inputs, receipts, and a verifiable mismatch.
  • Submitted to L1 during the challenge window for on-chain verification.
Why the challenge period
It gives watchers time to detect, build, and submit fraud proofs. Without it, incorrect batches could finalize immediately on L1.
Common misconception: fraud proofs are not pre-emptive guarantees. They are reactive and require time to be effective.
Key Takeaway

Fraud proofs plus a challenge period create the contestable security model that allows optimistic rollups to be simpler at the cost of delayed finality.
