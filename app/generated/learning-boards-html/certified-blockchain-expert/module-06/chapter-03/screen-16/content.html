Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Practical Reminders
Data Availability
Post enough data or commitments on L1 so state can be reconstructed if operators fail.
Proof Model
Fraud proofs need watchers and time; validity proofs need proof infrastructure and give instant finality.
Operational Readiness
Ensure watchers, penalties, sequencer incentives, and recovery plans are in place before going live.
Final Thought

Translate these reminders into your rollup selection and operational plan for a safer and more reliable Layer 2 deployment.
