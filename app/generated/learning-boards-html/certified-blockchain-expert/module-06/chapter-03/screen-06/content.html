Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Optimistic vs ZK
Optimistic

Assume then challenge

Batches are assumed valid. Watchers submit fraud proofs during a challenge period to revert invalid batches. Simpler proof logic; withdrawal delay required.

EVM friendly and easier to port existing dApps.
Risk: users must wait for challenge windows before final L1 settlement.
ZK-Rollup

Validity proofs give instant finality

Each batch comes with a zero-knowledge validity proof that the state transition is correct. L1 verifies the proof and accepts the batch immediately.

Immediate withdrawals and strong cryptographic guarantees.
Trade-off: higher computational overhead and cryptographic complexity.
Key Takeaway

Choose optimistic for developer ease and EVM compatibility; choose ZK when immediate finality and cryptographic guarantees are required despite higher complexity.
