Rollups: Rollup Patterns, Proofs, and Trade-offs

This lesson explains how rollups bundle off-chain transactions and record a compact proof on Layer 1. You'll see the different guarantees optimistic and ZK rollups provide, and why data availability on L1 matters for security and reconstruction.

Concept Map
Sequencer · Batch · Proof · L1 Commitment
A sequencer orders transactions, forms a batch, posts a data commitment and either a validity proof or an optimistic batch to L1. Data availability ensures anyone can reconstruct state from the commitment.
Explore Key Topics
Optimistic Rollups
Assume validity, allow fraud proofs during a challenge period; simpler, EVM-friendly.
ZK-Rollups
Generate validity proofs for immediate finality; stronger cryptography and higher proof cost.
Data Availability
Posting batch data or commitments on L1 ensures reconstructibility and censorship resistance.
