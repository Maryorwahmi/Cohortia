Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › EVM Compatibility
Why it matters

EVM compatibility reduces friction for teams migrating smart contracts, using existing libraries, and keeping familiar tooling and debuggers.

Optimistic: closer to native EVM semantics.
ZK: requires circuits or ZK-EVM emulation, which is improving but more complex.
Practical decision
If time-to-market and developer familiarity are priorities, optimistic rollups are attractive. If instant finality is core, plan for ZK engineering work.
Key Takeaway

Map your team's skills to the rollup choice: EVM comfort points to optimistic; strict finality points to ZK with investment.
