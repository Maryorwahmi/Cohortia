Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Watchers & Pitfalls
Watcher duties
  • Monitor batches and state roots for inconsistencies.
  • Construct and submit fraud proofs within the challenge window (optimistic).
  • Verify posted calldata and alert on missing data to preserve reconstructibility.
Common mistakes
Assuming transactions are final immediately after batch submission (optimistic).
Underestimating proof generation time and cost (ZK).
Not providing sufficient data availability on L1.
Pro tip
Build monitoring and alerting around state root mismatches, missing calldata, and sequencer liveness to reduce operational risk.
