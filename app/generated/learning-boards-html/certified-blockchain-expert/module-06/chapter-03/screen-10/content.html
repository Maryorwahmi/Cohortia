Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Trade-offs
Compare core metrics
Withdrawal Time
Optimistic: delayed until challenge window expires. ZK: immediate after proof verification.
Computational Cost
Optimistic: lower verification cost on L1, relies on watchers. ZK: higher off-chain proof generation cost.
EVM Compatibility
Optimistic: typically higher. ZK: evolving via ZK-EVM projects but more complex.
Practical guidance
If your team needs quick porting of Solidity contracts, optimistic is often more practical. If instant finality and fast withdrawals are core, plan for ZK investment.
Key Takeaway

Match rollup choice to app needs: prioritize developer experience for optimistic or instant finality for ZK despite higher proof costs.
