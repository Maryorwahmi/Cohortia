Module 06 › Chapter 6.3 › Decision Checklist
Checklist
  1. Is instant finality required? If yes, prefer ZK-Rollups.
  2. Does the team prefer existing EVM tooling? If yes, optimistic is easier.
  3. Can you invest in proof-generation infrastructure? If not, optimistic may be pragmatic.
  4. How will you publish data to L1? Full calldata vs commitment influences cost and recoverability.
  5. Who will run watchers and how are penalties enforced? Operational readiness is essential.
Closing thought
No one-size-fits-all: match architecture to constraints and revisit choices as tooling and ZK ecosystems evolve.
