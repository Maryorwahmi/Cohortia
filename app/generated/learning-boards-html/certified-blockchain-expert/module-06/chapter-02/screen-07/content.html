Scenario
Alice & Bob channel sequence
Open: Alice deposits 5 ETH, Bob deposits 3 ETH. Initial state: {Alice:5, Bob:3} — both sign.
Tx1: Alice → Bob 1 ETH. New state: {Alice:4, Bob:4} — both sign.
Tx2: Bob → Alice 0.5 ETH. New state: {Alice:4.5, Bob:3.5} — both sign.
Tx3: Alice → Bob 2 ETH. New state: {Alice:2.5, Bob:5.5} — both sign.
Close: Latest signed state {Alice:2.5, Bob:5.5} broadcast; contract verifies and releases funds.
Why only final state?
Because the final signed state represents the most recent mutually acknowledged distribution; the contract enforces that the sum matches the locked deposit and that signatures prove agreement.
Common pitfall
If one participant broadcasts an old state, the other must submit a newer signed state within the dispute window, otherwise funds may be misallocated.
