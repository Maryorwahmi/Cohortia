Architecture
Sidechain Components
Validator model: small fast set vs larger decentralized set
Consensus: tuned for throughput (faster block times, larger blocks)
Bridge type: custodial multisig / federation or cryptographic proof-based
Governance: upgradeability, slashing, and emergency procedures
Evaluation checklist
  • Who can sign bridge withdrawals?
  • Are there slashing and incentives for validators?
  • Does the chain support fraud proofs or withdrawals without trust?
Key Takeaway

Assess bridge custody and validator incentives; these determine how much L1-like security a sidechain effectively provides.
