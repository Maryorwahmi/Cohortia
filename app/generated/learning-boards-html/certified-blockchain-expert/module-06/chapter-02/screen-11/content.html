Pitfalls
  • Stale-state broadcasts in channels allow fraud if not challenged.
  • Participant offline time can lead to funds being exposed or locked.
  • Sidechain validator compromise may permit theft on that chain.
  • Bridge bugs or custodial designs can lose assets in transfer.
Protections: dispute windows, signed state version numbers, monitoring services, and slashing.
Operational: backup keys, delegate watchers, transparent validator selection.
Checklist
  1. Define dispute and challenge windows.
  2. Require state versioning and signature schemes.
  3. Design validator incentives and slashing for sidechains.
  4. Plan monitoring and incident response for bridges and channels.
