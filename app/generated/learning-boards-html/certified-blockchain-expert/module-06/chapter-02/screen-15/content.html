State Channels
Off-chain signed updates; final settlement on L1. Best for repeated bilateral interactions.
Sidechains
Parallel chain with a two-way peg. Fast and flexible but validator-dependent security.
Security Trade-offs
Every L2 adds operational or trust costs; map them to your application's tolerance for risk and downtime.
Final note
Design choices should be driven by transaction patterns, required finality, and the security model you can operate and audit reliably.
