Layer 2 Scaling Solutions
State channels and sidechains that reduce Layer 1 load while enabling faster, cheaper transactions.
Why this chapter matters

When blockchains slow under load, L2s let you keep decentralization at L1 while handling volume elsewhere. Understanding these patterns helps you choose the right architecture for payments, games, and high-throughput dApps.

Who benefits

Developers building micropayment systems, marketplaces, or high-frequency dApps, and architects comparing trade-offs between throughput, security, and decentralization.

Visual overview
Illustrative cards show: L1 as the secure settlement layer, state channels as off-chain lanes between participants, and sidechains as parallel blockchains connected by a two-way peg.
