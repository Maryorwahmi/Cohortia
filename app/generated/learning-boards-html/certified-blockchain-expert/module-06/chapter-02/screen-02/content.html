Learning Objectives
Master the mechanics, examples, and security considerations for state channels and sidechains.
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Bloom progression to show skill growth across the chapter.
01
Differentiate L1 vs L2
Explain operational differences and why L2s relieve L1 bottlenecks. Verified Competency Milestone
You will compare how validation and settlement responsibilities shift between layers, and when to avoid changing L1 consensus directly.
02
Explain state channels
Describe locking, off-chain updates, and final settlement. Verified Competency Milestone
You will simulate channel flows and articulate dispute considerations and uptime trade-offs for participants.
03
Describe sidechains
Explain two-way peg and validator model. Verified Competency Milestone
You will compare security dependencies between sidechain validators and the L1 peg contract and evaluate trust assumptions.
04
Assess trade-offs
Identify pitfalls and select the suitable L2 for your application. Verified Competency Milestone
You will be able to weigh throughput, latency, setup cost, and security to recommend state channels, sidechains, or other L2s.
