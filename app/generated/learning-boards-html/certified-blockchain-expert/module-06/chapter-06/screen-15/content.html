Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Quick Reminders
State trust assumptions
Document who can update data and which providers are trusted.
Prefer aggregation
Use multiple nodes and robust aggregation rules for critical financial feeds.
Combine incentives & monitoring
Staking and slashing help, but pair them with monitoring, staleness checks, and fallback logic.
Final takeaway

Design oracle integrations with explicit trust assumptions, redundancy, and economic safeguards to keep on-chain systems reliable and secure.
