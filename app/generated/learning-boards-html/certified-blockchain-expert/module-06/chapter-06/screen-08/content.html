Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Data Aggregation
Aggregation methods
Median
Resists extreme outliers; common for price feeds.
Trimmed mean
Drop top and bottom values then average; balances robustness and smoothing.
Weighted average
Weight sources by reliability, but introduces trust in weights.
Other stats
Mode, percentile methods, or custom aggregators for special data types.
Concrete numeric example: node reports 250000, 250100, 249900, 250050, and 1000000. The mean would be pulled upward by the extreme 1000000 value; the median remains at 250000, preserving the expected price.
Risk
If many nodes read the same compromised source, aggregation cannot protect you. Diversity of providers is essential.
Key Takeaway

Aggregation reduces the impact of faulty or malicious node reports, but it must be paired with diverse, independent data sources and sound aggregation rules.
