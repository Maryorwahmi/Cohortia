Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Centralized vs Decentralized
Centralized Oracle
Single data provider or single node acting as the oracle. Easy to implement and low-latency.
  • Advantages: simple, fast, low operational overhead.
  • Disadvantages: single point of failure, trust centralization, vulnerable to compromise.
Decentralized Oracle
A network of independent nodes fetches and submits data. Aggregation produces a single on-chain value.
  • Advantages: redundancy, resistance to single-node compromise, more trust minimization.
  • Disadvantages: higher complexity, coordination for staking and penalties, potential latency.
Example
A prototype price feed may use a single trusted oracle. A production DeFi feed should use many nodes and aggregation.
Common Mistake
Assuming decentralization makes systems risk-free. It reduces some risks but adds coordination and slashing considerations.
Key Takeaway

Choose the oracle architecture that matches your application's tolerance for trust, complexity, and operational overhead.
