Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Economic Incentives
How incentives work
Nodes stake tokens to participate. Correct reporting earns rewards; misbehavior can result in slashing of stake. These mechanisms make manipulation costly and align node operators with network reliability.
Example flow
  • Node stakes collateral to register.
  • Node reports data and earns fees when reports match aggregated consensus.
  • Repeated divergence or proven fraud leads to slashing or removal.
Considerations: high staking requirements can deter small operators and reduce decentralization, while low requirements may invite sybil attacks. The best designs balance security and broad participation.
Checklist
When evaluating an oracle, check stake size, slashing rules, reward distribution, and how misbehavior is detected and audited.
Misconception
Staking alone is not a silver bullet; monitoring, provider diversity, and governance are also required to maintain integrity.
Key Takeaway

Economic incentives reduce the chance of dishonest reporting by making attacks costly, but they must be evaluated alongside technical and operational protections.
