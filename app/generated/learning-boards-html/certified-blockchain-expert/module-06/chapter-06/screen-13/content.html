Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Deployment & Simulation
Step-by-step
  1. Deploy MockPriceOracle. Note the owner address and contract address.
  2. Deploy PredictionMarket with the oracle address passed to the constructor.
  3. As the owner, call updatePrice(250000) to simulate $2,500.00.
  4. After predictionTime has passed, call resolveMarket and observe MarketResolved with the fetched price.
Validation checklist
Confirm PriceUpdated event, confirm MarketResolved event, check lastFetchedEthUsdPrice equals the updated value, and ensure predictionTime logic was respected.
Common pitfalls
  • Deploying consumer before the oracle, leaving an invalid address.
  • Forgetting to call updatePrice as the owner.
  • Not waiting until predictionTime has passed before resolving.
Key Takeaway

A controlled simulation reveals trust assumptions and helps you design monitoring, aggregation, and fallback checks before production deployment.
