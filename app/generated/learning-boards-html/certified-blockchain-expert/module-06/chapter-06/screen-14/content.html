Oracle Problem
Smart contracts are deterministic and cannot perform external reads during execution.
Blockchain Oracle
A service that fetches, verifies, and delivers off-chain data to smart contracts.
Centralized vs Decentralized
Centralized is simple but risky; decentralized uses multiple nodes and aggregation to improve robustness.
Summary

When integrating oracles, evaluate aggregation, data provider diversity, staking and slashing rules, and monitoring to maintain reliable on-chain inputs.
