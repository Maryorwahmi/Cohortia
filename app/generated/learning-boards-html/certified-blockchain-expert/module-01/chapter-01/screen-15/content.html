Quick Reference

Blockchain ≠ Bitcoin
Treat Bitcoin as an early application of blockchain technology, not the whole story.
Four core properties
Decentralization, immutability, transparency, security — explain each with a short example.
Choose the right model
Match public, private, or permissioned deployments to your trust and privacy requirements.
Final takeaway

Use the ledger metaphor and the four properties to guide architecture choices and to explain blockchain’s benefits and limits clearly.
