Hands-on Activity

Simulating Centralized vs Decentralized Ledgers

A conceptual exercise to experience tampering and group validation.
Part 1 — Centralized ledger
Create a text file called central_ledger.txt and append simple transactions. Then edit an old line to see how easily history is altered without detection.
Reflection
Who verifies this ledger? How easy is tampering to hide?
Part 2 — Decentralized ledger
Use a shared document and invite two collaborators. Agree to append-only rules and check the previous entry before adding new ones. Attempt a secret edit and observe detection by others.
Reflection
How does group agreement make tampering visible? What governance did you use to accept entries?
Example log (conceptual)
central_ledger.txt Alice sends 10 BTC to Bob Bob sends 5 BTC to Charlie Charlie sends 3 BTC to Alice In the shared document, each node appends after confirming previous entry.
Key Takeaway

The exercise demonstrates how distribution and agreement change verification responsibilities and make undetected tampering much harder.
