Introduction to Blockchain

A conceptual tour of the distributed ledger that enabled Bitcoin and a new trust model
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass

Blockchain is a shared, append-only ledger maintained across many participants. It removes the need for a central verifier by using cryptographic links between records and a distributed agreement on the correct history.

This chapter explains why Bitcoin emerged as the first successful application, the core characteristics that make blockchains useful, common misunderstandings, and practical considerations for security and governance.

In this lesson
Understand the ledger model, trace Bitcoin's origin, and compare centralized and decentralized trust models with real examples.
Why it matters
This technology changes how trust, auditability, and resilience are designed in financial and institutional systems.
