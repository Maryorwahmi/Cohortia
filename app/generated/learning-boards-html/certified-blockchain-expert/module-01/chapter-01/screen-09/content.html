Core Concept

Transparency and Pseudonymity

Why public visibility matters and how it interacts with identity.
What transparency gives you
Open audit trails, verifiable histories, and the ability to independently check records without a central auditor.
Pseudonymity
Addresses do not contain names. Tracing ownership requires off-chain linking, which means transactions are visible but identities are not guaranteed.
Design consideration
Choose public or permissioned models based on whether auditability or privacy is the priority for your application.
Visual: trace vs identity
Traceability
You can follow flows between addresses. For example, you can confirm that an address sent funds to another, which supports auditing and verification.
Key Takeaway

Transparency supports auditability, but identity linkage requires external data and privacy design choices.
