Core Concept

Security: Cryptography and Network Defense

How signatures, hashes, and distribution work together to protect the ledger.
Cryptographic building blocks
Signatures prove authorization; hashes guarantee data integrity. These primitives let anyone verify authenticity without a central party.
Network defense
Distribution makes rewriting history costly. An attacker needs to control a significant portion of validating power to succeed.
User-level caution
Losing private keys or interacting with buggy contracts can lead to irreversible loss despite strong blockchain security.
Visual: security layers
Layered defense
Cryptography secures individual entries; distributed consensus secures the ledger. Both are required for strong protection.
Key Takeaway

Combine cryptographic controls with distributed validation, and never neglect user-level security like key backup.
