Key Concepts

Blockchain
A distributed, immutable ledger that records transactions securely across a network.
Decentralization
Control and validation distributed among network participants to reduce single points of failure.
Immutability
Tamper-evidence produced by chaining and consensus; powerful but contingent on protocol and governance.
Summary takeaway

Use the ledger metaphor to reason about trust, and always pair network-level protections with strong user and application security practices.
