Genesis: Bitcoin and the Double-Spending Problem

A short timeline that shows the layered solution
Problem: Double-spending
Digital assets can be copied. Without a single trusted verifier, preventing a unit from being spent twice was unsolved.
Layered solution
  1. Cryptographic signatures prove ownership of funds.
  2. Hash-linked blocks create tamper-evident history.
  3. Network consensus establishes a canonical chain without a central authority.
Timeline
Pre-2008: Centralized ledgers
Trusted intermediaries handled verification and prevented double spends.
2008: Bitcoin paper
Satoshi combined known cryptographic tools into a decentralized cash system.
Post-2008: Adoption of the ledger model
The chained ledger and consensus idea spread into other domains beyond currency.
Key Takeaway

The invention combined existing cryptographic tools into a system that establishes consensus without a central party.

Misconception

Cryptography is not new; the novelty was the system-level combination to solve double-spending in a decentralized network.
