Types of Blockchains

Public, private, and permissioned models and when to use each.
Public blockchains
Open to anyone, provide high transparency and censorship resistance; suitable when public auditability is required.
Private blockchains
Controlled by a single organization; useful for internal workflows needing performance and privacy, but more centralized.
Permissioned/Consortium
Multiple known organizations share governance and validate transactions; balance of control and shared trust.

Key Takeaway

Select a deployment model based on trust assumptions, privacy needs, and governance requirements, not on buzzwords.
