Definition › Hashing vs Encryption
Hashing
  • One-way; used for integrity checks.
  • Fixed-size digest regardless of input size.
  • Not suitable for confidentiality.
Encryption
  • Two-way; used for confidentiality.
  • Requires keys for decryption.
  • Not appropriate when only integrity is needed.
Key Takeaway
Use the right primitive for the job: hashing for integrity, encryption for privacy, and signatures for authenticity.
