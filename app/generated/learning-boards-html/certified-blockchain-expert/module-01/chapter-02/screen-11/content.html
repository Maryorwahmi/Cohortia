Core Concept › Non-repudiation & Trade-offs
Non-repudiation
A valid signature that verifies against a public key ties an action to a key holder and serves as strong evidence in disputes.
Consequences
Errors and compromises are lasting because signatures cannot be undone; key duties must be managed accordingly.
Key custody trade-offs
  • Online keys: convenient but exposed.
  • Hardware/cold storage: safe but less convenient.
  • Backup strategies: necessary to recover lost access without exposing keys.
Key Takeaway

Non-repudiation is a double-edged sword: it provides strong evidence but demands disciplined key management and thoughtful trade-offs between usability and security.
