What to Remember › Practical reminders
SHA-256
A secure, fixed-size digest algorithm commonly used to link blocks and hash transactions.
Merkle Proofs
Use proofs to verify inclusion without full data transfer; always validate the root against the block header.
Private Key Safety
Treat private keys as the single most important secret; protect them with hardware wallets and encrypted backups.
Final takeaway
Cryptography supplies strong building blocks, but correct use, verification, and custody practice turn those blocks into secure, usable systems.
