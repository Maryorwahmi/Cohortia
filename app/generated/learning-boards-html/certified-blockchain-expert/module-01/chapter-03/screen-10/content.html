Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Nonce & Target
Nonce
A number used once that miners change to derive a different header hash. It is the primary variable miners iterate.
Target Difficulty
A threshold the hash must meet; practically expressed as required leading zeros. Networks adjust target to keep block time stable.
Key Takeaway

Changing a tiny nonce gives miners a way to spend compute for security; difficulty scales the expected work.

Concrete Illustration
If a target requires one extra leading zero, expected tries increase exponentially. This multiplicative effect is why targeting many zeros drastically increases energy use.
Misconception
The nonce alone may be insufficient to explore all hashes; miners also adjust timestamps or extra header fields to enlarge the search space.
