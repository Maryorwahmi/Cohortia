Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › PoW Security & Challenges
Security Strength
Costly work makes rewriting history expensive, requiring re-mining many blocks faster than honest miners.
51% Attack
If an entity controls >50% of hashing power they can outpace honest miners and potentially reverse transactions.
Energy Consumption
The mining race consumes substantial electricity, which raises environmental and cost concerns.
Scalability
Block times and propagation limit transactions per second compared to some permissioned systems.
Centralization Pressure
Specialized hardware and cheap power can concentrate mining in pools or regions, reducing effective decentralization.
Design Trade-off
PoW offers robust security at the cost of energy and speed; choose consensus based on the application’s priorities.
Reflective Question
Would you accept higher energy cost for stronger censorship resistance, or choose a permissioned consensus for throughput and privacy? Consider the stakeholder priorities.
