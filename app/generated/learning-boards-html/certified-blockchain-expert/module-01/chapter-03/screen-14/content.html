DLT ≠ only blockchain
Blockchain is a type of DLT; other structures exist for different goals.
Choose ledger by trust model
Open networks require stronger computational security; enterprise networks can trade decentralization for speed.
PoW trade-offs
High security for established networks but with energy, scalability, and centralization concerns.
