Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Why Consensus?
Definition
Consensus Mechanism
A protocol that ensures all honest participants agree on the order and validity of transactions so the ledger remains consistent.
  • Resolves conflicts from competing proposals.
  • Defines who can validate and how finality is reached.
  • Uses incentives and technical rules to discourage dishonest behavior.
Examples & Contrast
Public networks need consensus tolerant of anonymous participants. Permissioned networks can use faster consensus because validators are known.
Misconception
Consensus is more than fault tolerance; it decides ordering, finality timing, and the economic model that keeps validators honest.
Key Takeaway

Pick a consensus that matches the network’s trust assumptions, finality needs, and performance goals.
