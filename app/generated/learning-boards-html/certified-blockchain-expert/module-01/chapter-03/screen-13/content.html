Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Distributed Ledger Technology
A replicated, synchronized ledger across multiple nodes without central control.
DLT Types
Public, private, and permissioned ledgers trade off openness, privacy, and performance.
Proof of Work
A consensus that uses nonce iteration and target difficulty to convert compute into network security.
Summary Takeaway
Align ledger structure and consensus choices with the system’s trust model, performance goals, and tolerance for energy cost.
