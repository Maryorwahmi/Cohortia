Key Concepts

Node Types
Full, light, and mining nodes play distinct roles in validation, efficiency, and block production.
P2P Network
Peer-to-peer gossip spreads transactions and blocks without centralized servers.
Transaction Anatomy
Inputs, outputs, signatures, and implicit fees determine spend validity and priority.
Summary takeaway

These concepts together explain how decentralization, validation, and incentives enable transactions to be securely recorded without a trusted central party.
