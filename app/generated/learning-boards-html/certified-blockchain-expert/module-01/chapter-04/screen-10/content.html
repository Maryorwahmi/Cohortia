Process Flow

Broadcast and Mempool Validation

Once signed, a transaction is broadcast and validated by peers before being stored in mempools for later inclusion in a block.

Initial validation checks
  • Format and structure correctness
  • Digital signatures verify ownership of inputs
  • Inputs are unspent and values cover outputs plus fee
Mempool behavior
Transactions that pass checks are stored pending miner selection; miners often prioritize by fee rate.
Example
A wallet relays a transaction to five peers; each peer validates it and, if valid, adds it to its mempool and forwards it, causing network-wide propagation.
Pro tip
If your transaction remains unconfirmed, increasing the fee via replacement or child-pays-for-parent may help, depending on policy and network support.
