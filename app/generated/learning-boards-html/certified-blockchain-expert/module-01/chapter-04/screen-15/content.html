Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Key Concepts — Continued
Mempool
Temporary queue of validated transactions awaiting inclusion in blocks.
Forks & confirmations
Forks resolve to the longest valid chain; confirmations strengthen finality over time.
Node syncing
Full nodes require time to download and verify history; sync status affects what a node reports.
Closing takeaway

Use these concepts as a checklist when inspecting transactions or running nodes: check mempool, fees, confirmations, and node sync status before drawing conclusions.
