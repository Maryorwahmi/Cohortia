Common Mistakes, Safety Notes, and Node Syncing

Practical advice helps avoid irreversible mistakes and manage expectations about latency and validation.

Underestimating fees
Low fees may keep transactions in the mempool for long periods during congestion.
Waiting for finality
High-value transfers often wait multiple confirmations to reduce reversal risk.
Address verification
Blockchain transfers are irreversible; always double-check recipient addresses before sending.
Node syncing
A full node must download and verify history, which can take substantial time; this is normal and expected.
Activity: Trace a transaction
Use a block explorer to inspect inputs, outputs, fee, block height, and confirmation count to connect theory to a live transaction.
Reflection
Which risk matters most to you: slow confirmation, incorrect fee estimate, or address mistakes? Plan mitigation steps accordingly.
