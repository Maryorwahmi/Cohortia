Core Concept

Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Network Architecture

P2P means each node exchanges data directly with peers, using routing and gossip to propagate transactions and blocks without a central coordinator.

Definition
Nodes act as both clients and servers, relaying messages and validating what they receive.
Why it matters
Resilience to outages, censorship resistance, and distributed verification without a trusted central service.
Common misconception
P2P is not a full mesh; nodes keep limited peer lists and rely on gossip protocols to reach the whole network.
Illustration: Gossip propagation (conceptual)
Nodes forward messages to peers until many nodes have the data. This inexpensive redundancy makes the network robust.
