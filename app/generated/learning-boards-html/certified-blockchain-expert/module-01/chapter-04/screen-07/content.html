Core Concept

Mining Nodes and Validators

Miners gather transactions from the mempool, build candidate blocks, and compete to create the next block. Validators perform a similar role under Proof of Stake with different incentives.

Transaction selection
Miners prioritize transactions offering higher fees to maximize reward.
Block proposal
Upon solving the puzzle or being chosen, a miner/validator broadcasts the block for validation by peers.
Example: Fee-driven ordering
During congestion, miners will pick higher-fee transactions first, leaving low-fee transactions longer in the mempool unless fees rise.
Misconception check
Miners validate transactions before including them; they don't accept invalid transactions just to win fees.
