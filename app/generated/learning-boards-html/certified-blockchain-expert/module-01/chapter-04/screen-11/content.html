Process Flow

Block Creation and Miner Selection

Miners fill candidate blocks from the mempool, validate transactions, add the coinbase reward, and attempt to find a nonce meeting consensus conditions.

Selection policy
Fee rate is the dominant factor in many miner policies, especially during high demand.
Validation before mining
Transactions are rechecked before inclusion; miners risk block rejection if they include invalid transactions.
Concrete example
During congestion, a miner may include transactions paying 100 satoshis per byte rather than those paying 1 satoshi per byte to maximize block reward.
