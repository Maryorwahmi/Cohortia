Comparison

Human coordination and social consensus

Off-chain processes involve developers, forums, meetups, and influential miners or validators discussing and signaling support for changes.

Strength: Flexibility and depth of discussion
Strength: Fast emergency response when humans coordinate
Weakness: Potential opacity and concentration of power
Weakness: Risk of hard forks when consensus breaks
Example
Bitcoin BIP process

Proposals are developed and debated off-chain. Adoption happens when miners and node operators upgrade software, signaling support rather than casting votes inside the protocol.
