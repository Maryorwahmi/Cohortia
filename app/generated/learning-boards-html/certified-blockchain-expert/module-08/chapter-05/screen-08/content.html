Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › Centralized vs Decentralized
Comparison

Centralized governance

Fast decisions, clear accountability, but risk of insider control and reduced community trust.

When it helps

Early-stage coordination, emergency fixes, and when legal accountability matters.

Comparison

Decentralized governance

Widely distributed authority increases legitimacy but can slow changes and permit plutocratic outcomes without safeguards.

When it helps

Long-term sustainability, censorship resistance, and attracting community contributions.

Misconception check

On-chain voting does not automatically decentralize power. Without quorum and delegation safeguards, voting power can mirror token concentration and result in plutocracy.
