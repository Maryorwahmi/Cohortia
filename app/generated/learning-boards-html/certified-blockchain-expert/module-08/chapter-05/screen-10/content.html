Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › Protocol Examples
Bitcoin
Off-chain governance

BIPs and social debate guide adoption; nodes and miners choose when to run upgrades. This process emphasizes social consensus and caution.

Polkadot
Hybrid on-chain model

Referenda, a council, and a technical committee balance broad participation with expert handling of urgent changes.

Compound
On-chain token governance

Holders of the governance token vote on economic parameters and upgrades, demonstrating token-driven decision-making in DeFi.

Key takeaway

Study who proposes, who decides, and how proposals are executed — these define the practical behavior of any governance model.
