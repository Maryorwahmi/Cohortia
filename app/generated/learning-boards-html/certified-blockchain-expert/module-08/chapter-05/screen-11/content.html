Common Challenges

Where governance fails

  • Misaligned stakeholder incentives leading to short-term decisions.
  • Over-centralization when a few actors control upgrades or funds.
  • Low voter participation skewing representation.
  • Governance attacks via temporary token control or flash loans.
  • Hard forks resulting from irreconcilable disagreement.
Mitigation patterns
Quorum & thresholds to ensure representative participation.
Proposal deposits and timelocks to prevent spam and allow review.
Delegation with revocation and reputation layers to combine expertise and accountability.
Watch out

No single mitigation is a silver bullet. Combine rules, incentives, and social norms for robust outcomes.
