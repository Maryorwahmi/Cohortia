Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › Learning Objectives

Deeper Skills

Apply governance theory to real protocols, create mitigation plans, and evaluate hybrid designs that combine human discussion with automated enforcement.

Skill
Identify failure modes

Spot voter apathy, whale dominance, governance attacks, and hard-fork risks, and explain their impact on legitimacy and security.

Skill
Design mitigations

Propose quorum rules, delegation safeguards, or staggered windows to reduce capture and increase representative outcomes.

Skill
Evaluate hybrid approaches

Explain how off-chain discussion informs on-chain votes and why that combination can improve nuance while preserving enforceability.

Skill
Interpret examples

Use Bitcoin, Polkadot, and Compound examples to support judgments about governance outcomes and community dynamics.
