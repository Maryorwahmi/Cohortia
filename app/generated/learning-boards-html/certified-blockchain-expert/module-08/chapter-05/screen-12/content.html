Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › Key Concepts
Blockchain Governance
Rules and processes by which a network makes collective choices and evolves.
Off-Chain Governance
Human coordination and social consensus; flexible but can concentrate power.
On-Chain Governance
Voting encoded in protocol logic; transparent and enforceable but needs safeguards.
Summary

Align incentives, choose mechanisms to match protocol goals, and combine rules with social norms to preserve legitimacy and adaptability.
