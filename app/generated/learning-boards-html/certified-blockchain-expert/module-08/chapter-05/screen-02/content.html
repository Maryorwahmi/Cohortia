01
Identify governance models

Recognize off-chain, on-chain, and hybrid governance and explain when each is used. This matters because selecting the wrong model can stall upgrades or centralize control.

02
Analyze on-chain mechanisms

Explain direct voting, delegated voting, councils, and treasury management and assess their implementation trade-offs with examples like Compound and Polkadot.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Evaluate centralization risks

Compare centralized vs. decentralized governance, focusing on power concentration, whale dominance, and how these affect network trust and resilience.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design a governance sketch

Create a governance model for a DeFi protocol with proposal thresholds, voting periods, and mitigation for voter apathy; justify these choices logically.
