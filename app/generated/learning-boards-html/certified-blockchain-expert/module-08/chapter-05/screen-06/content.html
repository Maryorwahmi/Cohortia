Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › On-Chain Governance
Process Flow

Mechanisms

  1. Direct voting: token holders vote and the protocol enforces results.
  2. Delegated voting: holders delegate to representatives for expertise or convenience.
  3. Council voting: a smaller elected group handles proposals and urgent matters.
  4. Treasury rules: funds are allocated via voted proposals encoded in contract logic.
Diagrammatic cards
Delegation
Flexible trust: delegate to experts while retaining reclaimable power.
Treasury
Programmatic spending via approved proposals increases transparency of funds.
Attack vectors
Flash loans or token accumulation can temporarily grant voting power to attackers without long-term stake.
Key Takeaway

On-chain governance enforces results automatically but requires carefully designed quorum, thresholds, and protections against capture.
