Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Key Concepts
Securities Law
Classification triggers registration, disclosure, and trading rules for tokens that function as investments.
Howey Test
Use four prongs—investment, common enterprise, profit expectation, reliance on others—to assess securities status in the U.S.
AML / KYC & FATF
VASPs must implement identity checks and Travel Rule messaging for larger transfers, posing privacy and design challenges.
Takeaway
Use classification tests and AML rules as your operational checklist when designing tokens, architecture, and launch plans.
