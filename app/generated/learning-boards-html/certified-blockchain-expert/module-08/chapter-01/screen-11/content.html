Common mistakes and their consequences
  • Assuming uniform classification: A token classified as a utility in one country may be a security elsewhere.
  • Ignoring AML/KYC: Missing Travel Rule obligations can lead to penalties and service shutdowns.
  • Launching without counsel: Failing to secure legal opinions increases regulatory exposure.
Consequence
De-listings, fines, or forced changes to token economics can substantially harm users and project viability.
Mitigation
Design modular compliance, seek jurisdictional advice, and maintain clear documentation of decisions.
Reflective prompt
Which jurisdictions are core to your market, and do your token terms expose you to securities or AML obligations there?
Key Takeaway
Plan for uncertainty by building adaptable processes and documenting legal assumptions for every major market you target.
