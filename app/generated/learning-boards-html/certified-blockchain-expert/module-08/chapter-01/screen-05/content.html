What triggers securities law?
Look for an investment of money, shared enterprise, expectation of profit, and reliance on others’ efforts. When these elements appear together, many jurisdictions will treat the asset like a security.
Concrete example
A token sold in an initial offering that promises future revenue share or appreciation due to the team’s work will often trigger securities rules.
Common misconception
Labeling a token a "utility" in marketing does not prevent regulators from examining the economic reality behind the sale.
Supporting points
  • Registration: Securities often require registration or exemptions for public sales.
  • Disclosure: Issuers may need to provide investor disclosures and ongoing reporting.
  • Market access: Classification influences which exchanges can list the token.
Key Takeaway
Assess a token’s economic rights and fundraising context early; plan for legal review if investment characteristics exist.
