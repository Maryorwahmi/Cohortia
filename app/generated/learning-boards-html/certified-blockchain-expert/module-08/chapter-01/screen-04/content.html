Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Regulatory challenge
What causes regulatory friction?
  • • Identification: Who is the legal actor if a protocol causes harm?
  • • Jurisdiction: Which country’s laws apply to a borderless network?
  • • Privacy vs compliance: How to reconcile pseudonymity with AML/KYC duties?
Example
A DAO issues governance tokens; if the team markets the token and users expect profits, regulators may treat it like a security.
Common mistake
Assuming decentralization eliminates legal obligations is incorrect when identifiable entities remain involved.
Visual: actor mapping
Foundations, core teams, service providers: likely legal targets for enforcement.
Decentralized contributors: less clearly subject to specific obligations absent coordination roles.
Key Takeaway
Map control points of your project; legal exposure follows identifiable actions, not architecture alone.
