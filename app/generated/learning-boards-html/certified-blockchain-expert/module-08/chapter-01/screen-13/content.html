Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Hands-on: Token Analysis
Task steps
  1. Define the token: purpose, distribution, and rights.
  2. Apply Howey Test, documenting evidence for each prong.
  3. Map to FINMA categories and justify your choice.
  4. List compliance implications and an issuance strategy.
Example prompt
A token sold to fund development that offers discounts and promotion-focused appreciation may require securities analysis and AML controls.
