Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › VASPs
Common VASP roles
  • Exchanges: Matching, custody, fiat on-ramps.
  • Custodians: Hold private keys and provide safekeeping services.
  • Wallet providers: Ranges from custodial to fully non-custodial designs.
Example
A platform offering custody plus fiat rails will almost always need robust AML/KYC, while a pure open-source wallet may not, depending on additional services provided.
Compliance design tips
  • Entity separation: Place custodial functions in regulated entities where possible.
  • Data architecture: Build secure channels for identity and Travel Rule messaging.
  • Operational controls: Implement KYC workflows and suspicious activity reporting processes.
Key Takeaway
Treat architecture as policy: the more control your service exerts over funds, the more compliance obligations you must design for and staff accordingly.
