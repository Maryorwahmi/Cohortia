Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › AML & FATF
Key obligations for VASPs
  • KYC: Verify customer identities and assess risk.
  • Transaction monitoring: Detect suspicious patterns and report as required.
  • Travel Rule: Transmit originator and beneficiary data for larger transfers.
Example
When an exchange forwards a transfer above the threshold, it must include sender and receiver identifying information in the transfer metadata or accompanying message.
Design challenges
  • Privacy trade-offs: How to share identity data while preserving user privacy?
  • Decentralization gap: Which protocols have intermediaries subject to rules?
  • Technical solutions: Verifiable credentials and off-chain messaging can help meet obligations.
Pro Tip
Start AML/KYC design early and build secure off-chain identity channels to support Travel Rule messaging if your service touches custodial or transfer flows.
