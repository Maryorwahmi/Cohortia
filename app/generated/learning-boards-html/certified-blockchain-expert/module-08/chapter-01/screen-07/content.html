FINMA token types
Payment Token
Used as a means of payment or exchange; treated closer to currencies for some purposes.
Utility Token
Provides access to a specific application or service; regulatory impact depends on function and sale structure.
Asset Token
Represents assets or claims, such as shares or revenue rights; often treated like securities.
Implications for issuers
  • Draft to match function: Token terms and utility must align with the claimed category.
  • Due diligence: Prepare legal opinions and disclosure aligned to classification.
  • Market access: Asset tokens will trigger higher compliance and listing scrutiny.
Key Takeaway
Classify by function, not labels. Align token mechanics and offering structure to the chosen category to reduce regulatory friction.
