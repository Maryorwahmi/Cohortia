Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Sandboxes
What a sandbox provides
  • Limited live testing with regulatory oversight.
  • Reporting and monitoring obligations in exchange for regulatory flexibility.
  • Data collection to inform broader rule-making.
Example
A sandbox for a new stablecoin might cap issuance, require reserve audits, and mandate transparency reports while allowing experimental features.
Practical checklist
  1. Define metrics and limits for the trial.
  2. Prepare robust consumer protections and reporting plans.
  3. Engage regulators early and document governance and risk controls.
Key Takeaway
Use sandboxes as structured, supervised tests that reduce regulatory uncertainty and inform safer product design.
