Howey Test: four prongs
  1. 1. Investment of money: Did purchasers give value in exchange for tokens?
  2. 2. Common enterprise: Are returns pooled or dependent on the same venture?
  3. 3. Expectation of profit: Is there a reasonable expectation of financial gain?
  4. 4. Efforts of others: Are profits derived mainly from the promoter’s or team’s efforts?
Worked example
A token sold to fund development with marketing promising future gains likely meets each prong because funds are invested, success links to the project, buyers expect appreciation, and the team drives growth.
Notes & checklist
  • Evidence: Use whitepapers, marketing, and distribution terms to test each prong.
  • Documentation: Keep a written prong-by-prong analysis for counsel and auditors.
  • Misconception: Decentralization after a centrally managed launch may not remove security status.
Key Takeaway
Apply each Howey prong with evidence. Treat the test as an operational checklist when preparing a token offering.
