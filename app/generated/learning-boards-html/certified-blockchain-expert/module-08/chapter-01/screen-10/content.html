Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › MiCA & Consumer Protection
Why consumer protection matters
High volatility and scams increase consumer risk. Regulation aims to give buyers clear information and avenues for redress.
MiCA objective
Provide legal certainty for crypto-asset issuers and service providers while protecting consumers and financial stability.
Example
Stablecoin issuers will face governance and reserve requirements to protect holders.
Practical steps
  • Map product to MiCA definitions and obligations.
  • Draft consumer risk disclosures and operational governance documents.
  • Plan authorization timelines and compliance resources before market entry.
Key Takeaway
MiCA creates a predictable compliance path focused on consumer safety and governance; map responsibilities early to avoid operational surprises.
