Comparison › DAOs vs Traditional
DAO Advantages
  • Public transparency of rules and transactions.
  • Global, permissionless participation by token holders.
  • Automated execution reduces bureaucracy.
  • Censorship resistance when truly decentralized.
Traditional Advantages
  • Clear legal personhood and liability assignments.
  • Established banking and contracting relationships.
  • Faster centralized decision-making when needed.
  • Administrative infrastructure for compliance and accounting.
Example

A DAO approves a grant on-chain; a traditional NGO uses contracts and bank transfers. The DAO state change is immediate; the NGO uses legal paperwork to manage liability.

Key Takeaway

Choose DAO structures when open, auditable coordination is primary, and add legal wrappers when interacting with external institutions.
