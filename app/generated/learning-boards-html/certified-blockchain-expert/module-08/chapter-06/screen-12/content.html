Explanation
  • createProposal checks token balance against proposalThreshold to limit spam.
  • castVote records hasVoted and adds weighted power from govToken balance.
  • executeProposal confirms voting period end, checks quorum, and then calls the target contract.
Common mistake

Using naive vote storage inside structs with mappings increases storage complexity and requires careful gas-aware design.

Try this

Consider adding timelocks, multisig guardians, and off-chain checks before allowing executeProposal to call sensitive targets.
