Core Component › Treasury Flow
Typical treasury flow
Proposals that pass call treasury contracts or vaults which then disburse funds according to preset rules or calls to target modules.
Proposal passes
Vote meets quorum and approval threshold.
Treasury call
Contract instructs vault to send funds or start a payment stream.
Disbursement
Funds are transferred, possibly with limits or timed releases.
Example control

A timelock delays execution 48 hours, and a multisig guardian can pause new proposals under emergency conditions.

Common mistake

Relying only on automated transfers without human oversight for accounting, compliance, and tax reporting.

Scenario
A DAO votes to fund a series of monthly grants. The treasury starts a streaming contract to disburse monthly payments that can be halted by another governance vote.
Key Takeaway

Design treasury flows to combine automation with controls like timelocks and multisigs for accountability and recovery options.
