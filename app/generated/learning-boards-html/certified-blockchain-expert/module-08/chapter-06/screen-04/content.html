Core Concept › What is a DAO?
Formal definition

An organization represented by rules encoded as a transparent computer program, controlled by members, with operations recorded on a blockchain.

Metaphor
Think of a DAO as a hive where the rules are part of the hive, not just instructions from a leader.
Key Takeaway

DAOs blend code and community: rules execute automatically, but people still propose and interpret changes.

Components visualized
Smart Contracts
Enforce rules and automate execution, e.g., release funds when votes pass.
Governance Tokens
Assign voting power; often 1 token equals one vote but designs vary.
Treasury
Holds and dispenses funds based on governance decisions.
Concrete example

A DAO member proposes funding an open-source grant. If token-weighted votes meet quorum and support exceeds opposition, the smart contract automatically transfers funds to the grantee.
