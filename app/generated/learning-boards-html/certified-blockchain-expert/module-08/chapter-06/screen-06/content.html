Process Flow › Proposal Lifecycle
Step-by-step
1. Propose
Creator meets threshold and submits proposal details.
2. Vote
Token holders cast votes within the voting window; votes weighted by balance.
3. Quorum
Contract checks that required participation percentage is met.
4. Timelock
Delay before execution allows review and potential intervention.
5. Execute
Contract performs the action, such as moving treasury funds.
Example

If votes meet quorum and support, executeProposal is called to invoke a target contract that transfers funds to a grantee.

Misconception

Voting does not always equal immediate execution; contracts enforce checks and delays to reduce risk.
