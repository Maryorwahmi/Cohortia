Best Practice › Mistakes & Safety Notes
Frequent mistake

Assuming autonomy removes legal responsibilities. DAOs interact with real-world law and should plan accordingly.

Security oversight

Skipping audits or using un-reviewed code for treasury controls invites catastrophic loss.

Governance design

Poor participation structures can create plutocracy, eroding community trust.

Hardening checklist
  • Conduct security audits and staged deployments.
  • Use timelocks and multisigs for critical treasury actions.
  • Choose a legal wrapper consistent with operational needs.
  • Build off-chain forums and clear proposal processes to improve participation quality.
