Apply & Assess

Four competency milestones to practice during the hands-on activity.

Bloom: Apply → Analyze
Map governance mechanics

Sketch token roles, quorum, thresholds, and execution triggers for a sample DAO proposal.

Mnemonic: TOKEN → ROLES
Perform a security scan

Identify attack surfaces like reentrancy, upgrade paths, and treasury access; propose mitigations.

Mnemonic: SAFE → Audit
Run a legal scan

Decide between an LLC, foundation, or cooperative based on liability, contracts, and banking needs.

Mnemonic: WRAP → Wrapper
Design participation plan

Propose incentives or delegation to reduce whale dominance and increase voter turnout.

Mnemonic: VOTE → Engage
