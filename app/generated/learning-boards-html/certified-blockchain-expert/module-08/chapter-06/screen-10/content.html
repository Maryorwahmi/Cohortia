Wyoming, USA
Allows DAOs to register as LLCs, offering limited liability and clearer personhood.
Marshall Islands
Has enacted legislation recognizing DAOs as legal entities in certain forms.
Switzerland / Liechtenstein
Progressive frameworks allow foundations or associations to support decentralized protocols.
Example

Registering as an LLC gives a DAO a legal actor who can enter contracts and open bank accounts, but it brings corporate compliance and tax responsibilities.

Key Takeaway

Jurisdiction choice affects liability, banking, and regulatory obligations; legal wrappers trade decentralization for legal clarity.
