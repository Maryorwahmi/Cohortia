Legal Strategy › Wrappers & Trade-offs
Unincorporated Association

Default form without a legal wrapper; members may be jointly liable for actions.

Foundation

Non-profit entity can hold treasury and IP and reduce member liability while supporting the ecosystem.

LLC / DAO LLC

Provides legal personhood and limited liability; useful for banking and contracts but requires local compliance.

Example

A protocol that needs to sign agreements with service providers may form an LLC to appoint representatives who can legally contract on behalf of the DAO.

Key Takeaway

Pick a wrapper that fits operational needs: banking, contracting, liability protection, and be clear about the compliance burden it creates.
