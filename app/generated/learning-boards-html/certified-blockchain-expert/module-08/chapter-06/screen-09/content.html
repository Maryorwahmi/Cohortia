Key Challenge › Coordination & Participation
Problem layers
  • Voter apathy reduces effective quorum and legitimacy.
  • Token-weighted voting can concentrate power in whales.
  • Off-chain discourse shapes votes but lacks formal enforcement.
Mitigations
  • Delegation with accountable delegates.
  • Quadratic or reputation-weighted voting to reduce wealth bias.
  • Incentives for participation like staking rewards.
Scenario

When one holder controls 60% of tokens, they can unilaterally pass proposals. This is plutocracy and undermines decentralization.

Pro-tip

Combine on-chain voting with off-chain forums and clear delegation paths so expertise informs proposals and accountability remains visible.

Key Takeaway

Design participation mechanics deliberately to prevent whale dominance and make governance resilient and legitimate.
