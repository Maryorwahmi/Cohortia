Key Concepts

Three compact reminders to guide future work.
Interoperability
Standardized bridges and protocols enable seamless asset and data movement across chains.
Digital Identity
Self-sovereign identity and verifiable credentials give users control and reduce centralized data risks.
Tokenization of RWAs
Fractional ownership and liquidity for real-world assets, paired with clear legal mappings.
Summary

These concepts form a practical checklist: make systems interoperable, protect and empower identity, and design tokenization with legal clarity and user trust in mind.
