Tokenization of RWAs
Fractional ownership, liquidity, and automation through tokens and smart contracts.
Benefits
  • Increased liquidity and fractional access to high-value assets.
  • Programmable asset management and automated distributions.
  • Broader investor participation through smaller ticket sizes.
Risks & Trade-offs
  • Legal title often remains off-chain and requires binding contracts.
  • Custody and dispute resolution must be explicit.
  • Speculative liquidity can create price volatility without investor protections.
Comparison: Use Cases
Real Estate
Fractional tokens allow small investors to hold shares and receive rental income automatically.
Art & Collectibles
NFTs represent provenance; fractional shares can broaden access to ownership.
Carbon Credits
On-chain registries can improve traceability and automate retirement and trading.
Example

A property is represented by tokens; smart contracts distribute rental yields and enforce transfer restrictions based on KYC.

Key Takeaway

Tokenization enables access and automation, but legal and custody assurances are required to make tokens trustworthy.
