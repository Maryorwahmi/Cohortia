DAOs as Organizational Architecture
DAOs combine on-chain governance, token economics, and execution controls to coordinate groups.
Core components
  • Proposal & voting mechanics
  • Treasury and execution contracts
  • Timelocks, multisigs, and off-chain coordination
Common misconception

Believing DAOs are purely leaderless. Effective DAOs often rely on contributors, delegates, or core teams for coordination.

Example Architecture
Investment DAO
Token holders submit proposals to invest. A quorum and majority threshold trigger execution through a timelock and treasury contract.
Public Goods DAO
Members vote on grants. Multisig signers release funds after timelock and audit checkpoints.
Design questions
  • What voting power model prevents capture?
  • How are disputes resolved?
Key Takeaway

DAOs can create new institutional forms, but require explicit governance, legal clarity, and safeguards against concentration of power.
