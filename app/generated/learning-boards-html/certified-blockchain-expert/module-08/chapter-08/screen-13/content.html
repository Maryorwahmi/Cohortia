Capstone Activity

Visioning Exercise: Sectoral Impact Analysis

Apply course concepts to a real industry and evaluate benefits, risks, and societal impact.
Step-by-step Process
  1. Identify 2–3 current challenges in your chosen sector.
  2. Propose specific blockchain solutions and the components used (SSI, tokenization, DAOs, ZK-rollups).
  3. Predict societal impacts: transparency, cost reduction, privacy effects.
  4. Identify one major adoption risk: ethical, regulatory, or technical.
Example: Healthcare (Model)

Challenges: fragmented records, privacy, counterfeit drugs.

Solution: SSI and VCs for patient consent; hashes on-chain and encrypted off-chain storage; permissioned supply-chain ledger for pharmaceuticals.

Societal Impact: improved patient care, reduced fraud, stronger privacy controls.

Risk: interoperability with legacy systems and regulatory approvals for medical data sharing.

Measure
Define one metric: e.g., percent reduction in record retrieval time or number of counterfeit incidents prevented.
Pilot
Start with a narrow pilot involving a small set of healthcare providers and patients to validate integration points.
Stakeholders
Identify early partners: regulators, data custodians, and end users who must trust and adopt the system.
Key Takeaway

Translate problems into specific blockchain components, plan for integration, and choose a measurable pilot metric to prove impact.
