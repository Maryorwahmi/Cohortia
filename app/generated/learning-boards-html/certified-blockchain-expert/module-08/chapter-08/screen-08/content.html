Sustainability & Environmental Responsibility
Design choices determine energy use and ethical outcomes.
Technical levers
  • Consensus selection: prefer energy-efficient alternatives when suitable.
  • Layer 2s and batching to reduce per-transaction cost.
  • Off-chain storage for large datasets; store hashes on-chain.
Ethical note

Sustainability ties to fairness. Higher costs can exclude users; environmental claims require measurable evidence.

Practical checklist
  • Estimate energy per transaction for your chosen stack.
  • Prefer designs that reduce on-chain writes through compression or batching.
  • Design transparent carbon accounting if claiming offsets.
Key Takeaway

Sustainability must be measured and prioritized; choose efficient protocols and minimize on-chain data to reduce impact.

Scenario

A DePIN smart city design prefers Layer 2 settlement and local off-chain sensors with on-chain hashes to reduce energy while preserving auditability.
