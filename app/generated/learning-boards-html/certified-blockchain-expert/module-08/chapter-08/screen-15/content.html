Key Concepts

Technical safeguards and social responsibilities to carry forward.
Sustainability
Prioritize energy-efficient consensus and measure environmental impact.
Accountable Governance
Design DAOs and contracts with clear liability, dispute, and upgrade mechanisms.
Cryptographic Readiness
Plan migrations to post-quantum algorithms and protect long-lived secrets.
Final Reflection

These guardrails combine technical choices with policy and governance. Prioritize the one most likely to determine your project's success and document how you will measure it.
