Objectives — Applied
Each objective below becomes a practical habit for design and review.
01 • Scenario Mapping

Draft a short scenario that links interoperability, UX, and regulatory touchpoints. Example: cross-chain payments for remittances with on-ramp KYC.

Verified Competency Milestone
02 • Impact Scoring

Create a three-factor score: transparency, equity, and cost. Use it to compare alternative designs before prototyping.

Verified Competency Milestone
03 • Ethics Checklist

Ask: Who benefits? Who is excluded? Where could smart contract logic embed bias? Document mitigations.

Verified Competency Milestone
04 • Sustainability Estimate

Estimate energy per transaction and explore design choices that reduce carbon footprint, from consensus to Layer 2 batching.
