Forecasting Pitfalls

Common Mistakes in Future Predictions

Why many forecasts miss the mark and how to make better projections.
Frequent Errors
  • Overly optimistic timelines that ignore social and regulatory friction.
  • Underestimating incumbent resistance and institutional inertia.
  • Ignoring human factors like UX, education, and incentives.
  • Neglecting regulatory realities and compliance costs.
Misconception: A successful demo equals quick mass adoption. Reality requires standards, integrations, and trust.
Better Approach: Scenario Planning

Build scenarios across three axes: technical readiness, regulatory fit, and human adoption. For each scenario, identify the single assumption whose failure would invalidate the forecast.

Use early pilots to test the riskiest assumptions and iterate based on real user and regulator feedback.

Quick Checklist
  1. List assumptions and rank by impact.
  2. Design small pilots to falsify the highest-risk assumptions.
  3. Incorporate regulatory engagement into your timeline.
Key Takeaway

Forecasts are stronger when they test the riskiest assumptions early and account for social and regulatory friction, not just technology capability.
