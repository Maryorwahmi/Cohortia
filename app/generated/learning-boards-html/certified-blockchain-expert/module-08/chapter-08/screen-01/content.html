Understand likely directions, societal trade-offs, and what responsible progress looks like.
Orientation

We connect course concepts to future scenarios: interoperability for seamless value flows, SSI for personal data control, tokenization for liquidity, and DAOs for new governance.

Why it matters

These shifts affect privacy, markets, regulation, and the environment. Predicting responsibly helps you design systems that are equitable and sustainable.
