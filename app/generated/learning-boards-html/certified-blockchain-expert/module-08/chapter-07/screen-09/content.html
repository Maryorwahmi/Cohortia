Definition

ZK-SNARKs and ZK-STARKs

Both prove correctness without revealing private inputs, but they make different trust and performance trade-offs.

ZK-SNARKs
Succinct proofs, fast verification, may require trusted setup depending on construction.
ZK-STARKs
Transparent setup, scalable provers, larger proof sizes but different trust assumptions.
Design question

Decide whether proof size, verification cost, or trust assumptions are the primary constraint for your application before choosing a proof family.

Comparison table
Proof size
SNARKs typically produce smaller proofs; STARK proofs are larger but growing more efficient.
Trust model
SNARKs may use a setup; STARKs avoid trusted setup through transparency assumptions.
Practical note
Engineering maturity and tooling vary across projects. Consider ecosystem support for your target VM and language.
Reflection: which constraint—proof size, prover cost, or transparency—is dominant for your design? Your answer guides the proof choice.
