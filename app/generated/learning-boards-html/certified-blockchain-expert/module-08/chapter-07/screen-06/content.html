Core Concept

DePIN: tokens meet hardware

A protocol rewards contributors who deploy and operate real-world devices that provide measurable utility for a network.

How it works
Devices produce data or capacity, the network verifies contributions, and smart contracts issue tokens as rewards.
Verification
Combine cryptographic proof, service measurement, and reputation to avoid fraud and low-quality contributions.
Impact
Democratizes infrastructure ownership and opens new income models for individuals and small operators.
Reflection: what metric would you use to verify a sensor's claimed measurements without exposing exact location data?
Example Gallery
Helium
Hotspot operators earn tokens for providing LoRaWAN or wireless coverage verified by the network.
Hivemapper
Contributors capture street-level imagery and earn tokens when their data is validated and used.
Render Network
Users provide GPU compute power and are compensated with network tokens for valid work.
Common mistake

Rewarding contributions without robust verification leads to low-quality or spoofed data. Design checks before token distribution.
