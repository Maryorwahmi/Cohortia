Hands-on Activity

Smart City DePIN sketch

Step 1: Purpose
Collect validated environmental metrics for planners to make evidence-based decisions.
Step 2: Tokenomics
Token CITY; rewards for deployment, uptime, and validated readings; 50% immediate, 50% vested for longevity.
Step 3: Verification
Device signatures, hashed payloads on decentralized storage with on-chain anchors, and peer cross-validation for quality checks.
Step 4: Privacy
Publish aggregated metrics and hashes; allow verifiers selective proofs via verifiable credentials; avoid raw location on-chain.
Step 5: Attacks & Mitigations
  • Sybil nodes: require staking and reputation buildup.
  • GPS spoofing: hardware attestation and cross-node checks.
  • Low-quality data: peer validation and decreasing rewards for anomalies.
Architecture sketch
[Citizen Sensor] --sign--> [Gateway/Node]
   --hash+store--> [Decentralized Storage]
   --anchor hash--> [Smart Contract]
   --verify--> [Reward Distribution to Wallet]
