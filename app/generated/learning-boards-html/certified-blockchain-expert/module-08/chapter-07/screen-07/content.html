Process Flow

Design steps

  1. Purpose: Define the specific problem the DePIN solves for the city.
  2. Token rules: Who earns tokens, for what actions, and under what quality constraints.
  3. Verification: Cryptographic signatures, data hashes, cross-validation, and reputation.
  4. Privacy: Hash pointers, aggregation, and selective disclosure.
  5. Utility: Payment for data, staking for SLAs, governance participation.
Pro tip

Incentivize quality over quantity. Use periodic audits and vesting to discourage one-time signups and exits.

Example: Smart City DePIN
Token: CITY
Earning rules: CITY awarded for device uptime, validated data submissions, and positive peer validation. 50% immediate, 50% vested over 6 months to ensure continued operation.
Verification
  • Device signs data with its private key.
  • Data hashed and stored in decentralized storage; hash anchored on-chain.
  • Nearby nodes cross-validate readings; reputation adjusts reward scale.
Privacy
Publish only hashes and aggregated metrics on-chain; allow verifiers to request proofs via verifiable credentials that reveal minimal information.
Check your design: list three potential attacks and a mitigation for each, such as sybil nodes, spoofed GPS data, and compromised devices.
