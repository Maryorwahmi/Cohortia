Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Quantum Resistance
Definition

Post-Quantum Cryptography

Algorithms designed to resist attacks from quantum computers, protecting digital signatures and key exchange.

The threat
Quantum algorithms could derive private keys from public keys for current widely-used schemes like RSA and ECC.
PQC families
Include lattice-based, hash-based, and multivariate approaches; each has different performance and key-size trade-offs.
Practical advice

Inventory long-lived keys, follow standards for PQC, and prototype hybrid signatures that combine classical and PQC methods during migration.

Protocol implications
Transition complexity
Switching signature schemes requires coordinated upgrades, careful key rotation, and attention to backward compatibility and long-term data access.
Example mitigation
Use hybrid signatures that validate with both classical and PQC schemes during a transition window to maintain compatibility and improve security.
