Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Key Concepts (cont.)
Zero-Knowledge Proofs
Proofs that verify correctness without revealing inputs, enabling privacy-preserving validation.
Post-Quantum Cryptography
Algorithms chosen to resist potential future quantum attacks on classical cryptography.
SSI / VCs / DIDs
User-controlled identifiers and cryptographically signed credentials that support selective disclosure and revocation.
Final takeaway: combine these tools—ZK proofs for privacy, PQC for long-term security, and SSI for identity control—to design systems that scale, protect user data, and remain resilient over time.
