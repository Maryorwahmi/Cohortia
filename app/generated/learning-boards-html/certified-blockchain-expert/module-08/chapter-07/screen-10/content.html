Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › ZK vs Optimistic
Comparison

Finality and verification

ZK-rollups
Cryptographic proofs give instant finality and Layer 1 security; proof generation can be resource-intensive.
Optimistic rollups
Assume correctness and allow a challenge period; lower proof complexity but finality is delayed by the challenge window.
Mistake to avoid

Picking optimistic rollups solely for lower complexity without planning monitoring services risks long disputed states and user frustration.

Scenario choice
High-throughput payments
Prefer ZK-rollups for instant settlement and strong Layer 1 guarantees.
Complex VMs or legacy code
Optimistic rollups may be easier to port code to, with the trade-off of longer finality and a need for fraud detection services.
Developer tooling
Check available tooling and ecosystem support; this often determines practical feasibility.
