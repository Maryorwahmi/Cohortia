Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Key Concepts
Web3
A decentralized internet where users own data, identity, and digital assets anchored by blockchain.
DePIN
Token-incentivized networks that reward physical devices for providing infrastructure and data.
ZK-rollups
Batch off-chain transactions and post a single cryptographic proof to the mainnet for instant finality.
Keep these three concepts as primary design lenses: ownership and identity (Web3), physical tokenized infrastructure (DePIN), and cryptographic scaling (ZK-rollups). Use them to guide architecture and threat modeling.
