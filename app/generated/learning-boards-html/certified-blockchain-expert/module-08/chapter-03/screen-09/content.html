Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Verifiable Credentials & SSI
Concept

User-held credentials and selective disclosure

Issuers provide attestations; users store them and reveal minimal attributes to verifiers, limiting the spread of PII across services.

KYC example

Instead of sending passport images to each VASP, a user presents a verifiable credential from a trusted issuer proving identity attributes. The blockchain records issuance and revocation events, not the credential data itself.

Design focus: revocation mechanisms and issuer governance determine legal accountability.
Practical point

VCs minimize data transfer and give users control, but you must define issuers, revocation, and who is the data controller to satisfy GDPR.
