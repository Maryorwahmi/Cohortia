Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Pseudonymization & Residency
Pseudonymization

Replace identifiers with reversible tokens. It reduces exposure but still counts as personal data if re-identification is possible.

Design: store mapping keys securely off-chain and limit access to reduce re-identification risk.
Anonymization

Aim for irreversible removal of identifiability. True anonymity is difficult and must be evaluated against auxiliary data risks.

If achieved, GDPR no longer applies to anonymized data; proving irreversible anonymization is challenging in practice.
Residency concern

Distributed nodes may cross jurisdictions. Use node selection, off-chain stores in the EU, or cryptographic proofs that avoid transferring PII to meet residency requirements.
