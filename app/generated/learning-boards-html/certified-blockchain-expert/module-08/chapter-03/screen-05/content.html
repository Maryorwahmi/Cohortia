Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › GDPR articles that matter for blockchain
Article 17

Right to Erasure

Individuals can require deletion of personal data. On immutable ledgers this right cannot be met by simply wiping a record, creating a direct legal conflict.

Article 5

Data Minimization & Accountability

Collect only necessary data and demonstrate compliance through clear controller responsibilities and records of processing activities.

Article 20

Data Portability

Provide data in machine-readable form. Hybrid systems must ensure off-chain stores serve portability requests while on-chain proofs remain interpretable.

Concrete scenario: a supply chain dApp keeps supplier contact details off-chain and writes hashes on-chain. Portability requires exporting the supplier data from the off-chain store in a usable format, while the on-chain hash is only an integrity marker.
Common mistake

Encrypting PII on-chain does not equal deletion; encrypted payloads remain stored and count under GDPR if re-identification is possible.
