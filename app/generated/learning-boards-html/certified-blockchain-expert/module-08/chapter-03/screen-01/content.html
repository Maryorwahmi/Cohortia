Data Privacy, GDPR, and Blockchain

Understand the policy conflict, learn technical patterns for compliance, and design hybrid architectures that keep personal data controllable while preserving ledger integrity.

In this lesson
Why immutability clashes with the Right to Erasure, and practical mitigation patterns.
Core topics
Off-chain storage, ZKPs, Verifiable Credentials, permissioned ledgers.
Outcome
Sketch a GDPR-aware data flow and justify trade-offs.
Key Takeaway

Design privacy from the start: keep PII off-chain, store proofs on-chain, and assign clear data controller roles in your architecture.
