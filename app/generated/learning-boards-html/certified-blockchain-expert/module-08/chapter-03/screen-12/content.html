Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Pitfalls & Governance
Developer mistakes
  • Encrypting PII on-chain and assuming erasure is satisfied.
  • Storing reversible pseudonyms without protecting mapping keys.
  • Failing to identify a data controller in practice.
Governance steps
  1. Define controller and processor roles and publish processing records.
  2. Implement deletion runbooks and maintain auditable logs.
  3. Select node hosts and jurisdictions consistent with residency requirements.
Key Takeaway

Technical design must be paired with governance artifacts: policies, runbooks, published controllers, and auditable deletion evidence to satisfy regulators.
