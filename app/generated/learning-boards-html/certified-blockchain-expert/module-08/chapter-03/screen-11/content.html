PII examples
  • Patient name and contact
  • Date of birth and medical history
  • Identifiers and consent records
Hybrid architecture
Off-chain database holds PII and supports deletion APIs. Smart contract records hashes, consent receipts, and access logs as non-PII proofs. Verifiable credentials manage user consent and selective disclosure.
Erasure workflow
  1. Authenticate and validate erasure request.
  2. Delete PII from off-chain store and revoke access tokens.
  3. Record the deletion event or revocation on-chain as a proof.
  4. Ensure exported portability data was provided earlier or manage an export before deletion if requested.
Design caution

Keep proofs on-chain but avoid embedding PII. The off-chain component must be auditable, secure, and capable of fulfilling GDPR requests in a timely manner.
