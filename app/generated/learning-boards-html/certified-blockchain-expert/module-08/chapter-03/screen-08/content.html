Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Zero-Knowledge Proofs
Concept

Prove without revealing

A prover demonstrates a property is true—such as "age > 18"—without disclosing the underlying personal data used to derive that property.

Concrete example

A user proves they are over 18 using a ZKP. The verifier gets assurance without the user's birth date being transmitted or stored.

Benefits & constraints
  • Supports data minimization by replacing full disclosure with attestations.
  • Reduces off-chain storage of PII but requires proof management.
  • Does not eliminate controller obligations; architecture still matters.
Misconception check

ZKPs reduce exposure but do not by themselves assign or remove legal responsibility for data processing.
