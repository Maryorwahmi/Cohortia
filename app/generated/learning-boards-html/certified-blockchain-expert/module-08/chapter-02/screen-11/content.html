Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Common Pitfalls
Top legal mistakes
  1. Assuming a universal classification across jurisdictions.
  2. Marketing a token as an investment while calling it a utility.
  3. Launching without tailored legal advice for target markets.
  4. Failing to build compliance features into contracts and operations.
Mitigations & checklist
  • Segment token sales and implement jurisdiction-based gating.
  • Document and publish reserve management and redemption policies for stablecoins.
  • Embed transfer restrictions or accredited checks for asset-backed tokens.
  • Keep marketing focused on functional utility when that is the intended classification.
Key Takeaway

Anticipate legal pain points and build compliance into product, contract, and communications rather than retrofit after launch.
