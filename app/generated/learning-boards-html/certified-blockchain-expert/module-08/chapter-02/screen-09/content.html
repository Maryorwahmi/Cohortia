Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Non-Fungible Tokens
Definition & Examples

NFTs represent unique digital items such as art, collectibles, or in-game assets. Each token is distinct and not directly exchangeable for another token of the same type.

Example
Digital art collections and in-game unique items illustrate classic NFTs.
Risk signal
Linking NFTs to revenue streams or fractional ownership increases the chance of securities classification.
Design and disclosure checklist
  • Explicitly document rights attached to the NFT (use, royalties, revenue share).
  • Avoid marketing language promising appreciation if staying in collectible space is the goal.
  • When fractionalizing, plan for securities analysis and compliance mechanisms.
Key Takeaway

NFTs are not automatically outside securities law; the rights and distribution model determine the legal treatment.
