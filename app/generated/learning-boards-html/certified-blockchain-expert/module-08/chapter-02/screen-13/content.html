Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › From Classification to Implementation
Step 1 — Legal analysis

Identify likely classification in target markets, required registrations, disclosure obligations, and AML/KYC duties.

Step 2 — Engineering

Map legal duties to contract features: gating, redemption, reserve proofs, or burn mechanisms. Implement identity oracles and audit trails.

Step 3 — Communications

Align marketing and documentation with the economic reality. Remove investment-forward messaging if aiming for utility classification.

Implementation checklist linking classification to three contract functions and two marketing edits.
