Comparison table — conceptual
U.S. test
Focuses on investor expectations and whether profits come from others' efforts.
Switzerland (FINMA)
May treat genuine utility tokens more favorably if economic function is clear.
EU (MiCA)
Creates categories for stablecoins and e-money tokens with explicit reserve rules.
Practical engineering response
  • Prepare region-specific onboarding and exclusion rules for sales and transfers.
  • Design contract hooks that enable or disable transfer gating by jurisdiction.
  • Keep marketing statements region-aware and avoid global claims about investment returns.
Common misconception
One legal opinion does not ensure global compliance; classification is context-sensitive and requires local analysis.
