Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › GAME_XP Case Study
Task

Design GAME_XP to function primarily as an in-game utility token used for purchases and governance, while minimizing security classification risk.

Suggested contract features
  • buyInGameItem: transfers tokens to a game escrow and triggers item issuance; tokens are consumed or held by the game.
  • delegateVote: separate governance mechanics from financial payoff to reduce profit expectation signals.
  • usage metrics on-chain: record spending to show tokens are consumed for services, not hoarded for profit.
Marketing guardrails
  • Emphasize functional benefits like item access and gameplay enhancements.
  • Avoid promises about token price appreciation or speculative gains.
Assessment prompts
  1. Write a one-paragraph primary utility statement for GAME_XP.
  2. List three smart contract features that reinforce utility.
  3. List two marketing phrases you will avoid because they imply investment returns.
Key Takeaway

Concrete contract features plus disciplined marketing reduce securities risk for tokens intended to be utilities.
