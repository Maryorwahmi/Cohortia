Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Key Concepts
Payment Token
Medium of exchange or store of value; AML/KYC focus.
Utility Token
Access to services; value tied to functional use rather than profit expectation.
Security / Asset Token
Represents ownership or profit rights; triggers securities laws and investor protections.
Summary

Match product design, contract features, and communications to the intended classification; when in doubt, design conservative compliance measures and consult counsel.
