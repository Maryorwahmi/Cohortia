Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Learning Objectives
Signals to evaluate
  • Expectation of profit from others' efforts (classic securities signal).
  • Token used to access a functioning service versus a promised future utility.
  • Transferability and marketing emphasis on investment returns.
  • Presence of dividends, profit shares, or fractionalized real-world assets.
Common misconception

Calling a token a "utility" is not enough. Regulators use substance — how it's marketed, sold, and used — to classify it.

Engineering responses
Transfer restrictions and accredited checks for security tokens.
Reserve management, redemption logic, and audit trails for stablecoins.
Usage-only functions and in-app spending flows for utility tokens.
Assessment task: create a short brief that links the most likely classification to three concrete smart contract or off-chain controls and two marketing guardrails.
