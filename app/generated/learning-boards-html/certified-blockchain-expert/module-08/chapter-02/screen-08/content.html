What is a stablecoin?

A stablecoin maintains a predictable value relative to a reference asset like a fiat currency through collateralization or algorithmic mechanisms.

Types
Fiat-collateralized, crypto-collateralized, and algorithmic approaches exist, each with distinct operational risks.
Regulatory focus
Reserve management, redemption rights, disclosures, and systemic stability are primary concerns for regulators.
Example
Fiat-backed stablecoins like widely-known dollar-pegged tokens illustrate reserve and redemption obligations.
Design checklist
  • Define reserve custody and audit frequency.
  • Specify redemption mechanics and timelines for holders.
  • Design operational resilience and failover for de-pegging scenarios.
Key Takeaway

Stablecoins reduce volatility but increase issuer obligations for transparency, reserves, and consumer protections; treat them as regulated payment-like instruments.

Common mistake
Underestimating reserve reporting and redemption risk creates regulatory exposure and consumer harm in de-peg events.
