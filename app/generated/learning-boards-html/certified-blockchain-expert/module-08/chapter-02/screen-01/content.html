Understand how classification drives rules for issuance, trading, and compliance across jurisdictions.

16:9 HD Classroom
Orientation

You will learn five dominant legal classes, the regulatory consequences of each, and how to design contracts and marketing to align with the intended classification.

Why this chapter matters

Classification shapes legal duties: securities registration, AML/KYC, reserve disclosures for stablecoins, and whether smart contracts must embed compliance. It is a core design concern for any blockchain project.

Coming up
Payment Tokens
Used as medium of exchange or store of value. Example: Bitcoin.
Utility Tokens
Access to network services. Example: Filecoin-style storage utility.
Security & Stablecoins
Securities need registration; stablecoins need reserve and redemption rules.
Key Takeaway

Classify early. Legal classification affects architecture, contracts, marketing, and compliance obligations from day one.
