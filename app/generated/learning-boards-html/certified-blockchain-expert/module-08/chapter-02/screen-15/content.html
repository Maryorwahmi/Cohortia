Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Key Concepts
Stablecoin
Requires reserve policies, redemption mechanics, and transparency obligations.
NFT
Unique ownership token; may become a security if tied to revenue or fractional shares.
Form over Substance
Regulators examine marketing, economic reality, and distribution to classify an asset.
Final takeaway

Before launch, create an implementation checklist tying classification to contract features, compliance tasks, and marketing guardrails, then validate the plan with legal counsel.
