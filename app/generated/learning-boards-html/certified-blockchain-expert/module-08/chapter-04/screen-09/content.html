Comparisons

Trade vs Spend vs Gift

Crypto-to-crypto trade
Usually treated as a sale of the asset you gave up; recognize gain and set new cost basis for the asset received.
Spending
Using crypto to buy goods equals a sale at FMV of the goods; compute gain using your cost basis of the crypto.
Gifting and donation
Gifts may have donor reporting; donations to qualified charities can be deductible like appreciated stock in some cases.
Scenario
You buy a laptop using crypto. The laptop's FMV is the sale proceeds. If the crypto was acquired at a lower cost, you realize a capital gain equal to laptop FMV minus cost basis.
Common mistake
Assuming trades are only taxable when converted to fiat; many tax systems tax trades when ownership changes.
Key takeaway
Always capture FMV at the moment of trade or spending to compute proceeds for gain/loss.
