Assessment Idea

Calculate and defend your result

Task: Given a transaction export, compute ordinary income and capital gains using FIFO. Then redo dispositions using Specific Identification and compare tax outcomes. For each calculation, document FMV source and holding periods.

Example question:
Sarah bought 1 ETH @ $2,000 on Jan 1. On June 1 she used 0.5 ETH to buy a laptop when ETH = $3,500.
Q: Is this taxable? If so, compute the gain and label it as short-term or long-term.
A: Taxable. Cost basis for 0.5 ETH = $1,000. Proceeds = 0.5 * $3,500 = $1,750. Gain = $750. Holding period < 1 year → short-term.
Evaluation criteria
  • Correct numeric gains
  • Correct application of FIFO or Specific ID
  • Documented FMV sources and timestamps
Key takeaway
Assessment success = accurate math plus clear documentation of assumptions and data sources.
