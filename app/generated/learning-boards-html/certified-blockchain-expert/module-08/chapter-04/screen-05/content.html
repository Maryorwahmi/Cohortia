Process

How to treat different acquisitions

1
Buying with fiat
Not a taxable income event. Cost basis = fiat paid plus fees.
2
Mining / Staking / Airdrops / Wages
Treated as ordinary income at FMV when received; that FMV becomes cost basis for later disposals.
Common mistake
Not recording FMV at receipt for staking or airdrops leads to incorrect income reporting and fuzzy cost basis.
Concrete example
You receive 0.1 ETH as a staking reward when 1 ETH = $2,500. Ordinary income reported = 0.1 * $2,500 = $250, and your cost basis for that 0.1 ETH = $250.
Why this matters
Treating receipts consistently as income ensures correct taxable income and sets accurate cost basis for when you later sell, trade, or spend the token.
