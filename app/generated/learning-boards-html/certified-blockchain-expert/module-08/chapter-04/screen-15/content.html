Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Key Concepts
Taxable Event
Any transaction that triggers tax, such as selling, trading, or receiving crypto as income.
Cost Basis
The original value for tax purposes; necessary to compute capital gain or loss at disposal.
FMV & Income
FMV at time of receipt determines ordinary income for mining, staking, airdrops, and wages.
Summary takeaway
Identify events, record dates and FMV, pick and apply a cost-basis method, and document every assumption for defensible tax reporting.
