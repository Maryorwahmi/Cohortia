Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Learning Objectives
Application & Compliance

Turn rules into routine

Capture FMV

Use exchange or reliable market prices to document fair market value at the time you receive tokens as income.

Classify disposals

Label each disposal as sale, trade, or spending and compute capital gain relative to cost basis and holding period.

Business rules

For businesses consider corporate tax, VAT/GST issues, and ambiguity with DAOs when assigning reporting responsibility.

Record retention

Keep acquisition dates, FMV, receipts, and transaction hashes to support FIFO or specific-lot identification choices.

Common mistake
Failing to record FMV at receipt turns income into uncertain cost basis, increasing audit risk.
Pro tip
Integrate exportable transaction reports from wallets and exchanges into your record system to automate FMV capture.
Milestone
Be able to compute the income value at receipt and a capital gain at disposal, with a clear audit trail.
