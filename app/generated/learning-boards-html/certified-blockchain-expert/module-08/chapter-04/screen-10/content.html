Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › FMV & Ordinary Income
Definition

FMV at receipt sets income and basis

If you receive tokens as rewards or wages, report ordinary income equal to the token's FMV when you received it. That FMV becomes your cost basis for any later capital calculation.

Example: 0.1 ETH staking reward when 1 ETH = $2,500 → ordinary income $250 and cost basis $250 for that 0.1 ETH.
Common mistake
Using a later price instead of FMV at receipt understates or overstates income and confuses later basis calculations.
Practice
Record timestamped quotes and the data source used to determine FMV to support your reporting method.
Checklist
  • Timestamp of receipt
  • FMV and data source
  • Transaction ID or hash
Key takeaway
Capture FMV at the moment tokens enter your control to accurately report income and create a defensible cost basis.
