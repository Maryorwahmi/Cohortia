Practical tactics

Reduce risk with consistent processes

Automate exports
Keep raw transaction data with timestamps and IDs to reconstruct FMV and lot matching reliably.
Choose one basis method
Apply FIFO, LIFO, or Specific ID consistently and document your choice; inconsistency invites questions.
Separate accounts
Label business and personal wallets and track intra-entity transfers to prevent double counting.
Advisor note
Advise clients to capture FMV when they receive tokens and to provide complete exports for any tax review.
Common pitfalls
Aggregating transactions, missing FMV timestamps, and inconsistent basis methods are frequent causes of misreporting.
