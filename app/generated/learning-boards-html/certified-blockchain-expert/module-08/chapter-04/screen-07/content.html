Comparison

FIFO vs LIFO vs Specific Identification

FIFO
Sells oldest lots first. Simple, default in many jurisdictions, may produce larger gains if early buys were cheap.
LIFO
Sells newest lots first. Less common for crypto and sometimes restricted by tax rules.
Specific Identification
Choose exact lots to sell for tax planning. Requires precise records and demonstrable identifiers for each lot.
Practical note
Specific Identification can reduce immediate tax by choosing higher-cost lots, but preserve documentation for audit support.
Illustration
Buy 1 BTC @ $10k and 1 BTC @ $20k. Sell 1 BTC @ $30k: FIFO gain = $20k; Specific ID selling $20k lot gives $10k gain.
Common mistake
Switching methods mid-stream or failing to document specific-lot choices invites challenges from tax authorities.
