Checklist

Minimum items for every transaction

  • Date of acquisition and disposition
  • Cost basis and FMV at receipt if income
  • Transaction type and identifiers (hash, trade ID)
  • Supporting receipts, invoices, payroll records for businesses
Operational tip
Export raw transaction files from exchanges and wallets; retain them in immutable storage so FMV timestamps remain auditable.
Common mistake
Keeping only aggregated monthly totals prevents lot-level matching and accurate FIFO or specific-lot calculations.
Key takeaway
Itemized, timestamped records are the foundation of defensible tax reporting for digital assets.
