Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Disposal & Taxable Events
Core idea

Disposal equals realization

When you dispose of crypto you generally realize a capital gain or loss: proceeds minus your cost basis. The holding period determines whether it is short-term or long-term.

Selling for fiat
Classic taxable event: proceeds in fiat less cost basis produces gain or loss.
Crypto-to-crypto trades
Often treated as a sale of the asset you gave up. Gain recognized even without fiat conversion.
Spending or gifting
Spending equates to sale at FMV of goods; gifting may have separate gift-tax implications.
Concrete example
You bought 0.1 ETH for $200 and later use 0.1 ETH to buy a gift card when 0.1 ETH = $300. You realize a $100 capital gain on the disposal.
Check this misconception
Thinking only fiat sales are taxable leads to missed reporting on trades and spending.
Key takeaway
Treat most transfers that change ownership or exchange value as potential taxable disposals and record FMV and dates.
