Definition

Property classification and capital gains

When crypto is property, disposals are capital events and subject to capital gains rules, including holding periods that determine short-term or long-term treatment.

Example calculation (conceptual):
Bought token A for $1,000
Traded token A for token B when A = $4,000
Capital gain realized = $4,000 - $1,000 = $3,000
New cost basis for token B = $4,000
Common misconception
Crypto-to-crypto trades are not always deferred until fiat conversion; many regimes tax the disposal as a sale.
Key takeaway
Classification drives whether gains are computed on disposal and which rates apply based on holding period.
Concrete scenario
You receive crypto from an exchange trade. If crypto is property, the trade is a disposal and you must compute gain relative to cost basis of the sold lot.
