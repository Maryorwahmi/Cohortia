Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Compilation
Solidity => EVM bytecode for execution and ABI for encoding/decoding.
Deployment
Deployment transaction contains bytecode and creates a contract account at an address.
Interaction
ABI + address let you call view/pure functions for free and send transactions to modify state for gas.
Summary Takeaway

Treat compilation artifacts and environment choice as your safety net: test locally, fund and confirm on testnets, then verify sources to ensure trust and reproducibility.
