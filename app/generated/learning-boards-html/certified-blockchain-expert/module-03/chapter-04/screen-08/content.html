Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Testnet Deployment
Preparation checklist
  • Switch MetaMask to Sepolia (or chosen testnet).
  • Fund account with testnet Ether from a faucet.
  • Compile contract and confirm constructor inputs.
  • Select Injected Provider in Remix and connect wallet.
Deployment flow
  1. Click Deploy in Remix; MetaMask opens a confirmation dialog.
  2. Review gas estimate and network; approve to send the transaction.
  3. Wait for the transaction to be mined; Remix terminal shows the receipt and contract address.
Example
Deploying SimpleNumberStorage with constructor 777 on Sepolia will require confirming a MetaMask transaction and paying gas with Sepolia Ether. After mining, the contract address is available in the receipt.
Caution
If you are on the wrong network or lack funds, the transaction will fail and any gas consumed up to failure is still spent.
