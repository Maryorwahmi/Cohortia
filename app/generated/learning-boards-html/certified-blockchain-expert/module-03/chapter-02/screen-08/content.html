Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Mempool & Inclusion
Mempool to Block
Transactions wait in the mempool; miners select profitable transactions until the block gas limit is reached.
Sourcing
Nodes share transactions; miners pull from this shared pool.
Selection
Higher gasPrice transactions are prioritized. Selection continues until the block’s gas capacity is exhausted.
Propagation
Once produced, the block is broadcast and other nodes verify and add it to their chains.
Block gas limit
A per-block cap (e.g., ~30M gas) limits the total gas of included transactions. This limit shapes throughput and fees under congestion.
Example
If many complex transactions are queued, miners may select fewer high-fee ones, leaving simpler, low-fee transactions pending longer.
Pro tip
Monitor mempool conditions and present users with fee options: slow, standard, fast, tied to observed gasPrice percentiles.
