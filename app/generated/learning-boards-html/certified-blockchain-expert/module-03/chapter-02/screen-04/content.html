Gas explained
A unit measuring computational effort. Used to prevent abuse, allocate resources, and pay validators.
Analogy
Like fuel for a car: more complex trips consume more fuel. Simple Ether transfer ≈ 21,000 gas; contract calls are higher.
Key points
  • Gas itself is a unit; convert to Ether via gasPrice measured in Gwei.
  • If a transaction runs out of gas, it reverts state but paid gas is not refunded.
  • Gas creates economic cost to deter spam and align resource usage.
Example
21,000 gas × 30 Gwei = 0.00063 ETH for a basic transfer at 30 Gwei.
Misconception
Gas is not Ether; setting gasLimit high doesn't mean you pay that full amount if gasUsed is smaller.
Concrete scenario
Sending 0.01 ETH to a friend: set gasLimit at or above 21,000. Choose a gasPrice based on network estimates to avoid delays.
Key Takeaway

Gas controls computation cost; monitor estimates and never assume a transaction with low gasPrice will be mined quickly.
