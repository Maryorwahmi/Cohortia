Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Finality
Probabilistic finality
Each confirmation makes reversal less likely. Decide depth by the cost of a reversal to your application.
When to wait
High-value transfers and cross-chain settlements should wait more confirmations than low-value UI changes.
Mitigation
Design application flows that tolerate rare reorgs or that verify state after a chosen confirmation depth.
Example policy
Exchange settlement: 12 confirmations. Social tip: 1 confirmation. Adjust by value and attack incentive.
Misconception
A single confirmation is not guaranteed finality. Think in probabilities, not absolutes.
Reflection
What confirmation depth would you require for a large payment integration? Use the potential cost of reversal to decide.
