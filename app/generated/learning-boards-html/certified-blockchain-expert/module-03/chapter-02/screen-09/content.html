Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Block Anatomy
Block contents
Blocks bundle transactions and include metadata used for verification and ordering.
Block number
Also called height; shows how many blocks precede this one.
Previous hash
Links blocks cryptographically to the prior block for immutability.
Gas used / Gas limit
Sum of transaction gas and the block cap; indicates block saturation.
Base fee (EIP-1559)
A minimum fee per gas that adjusts with demand; tips still incentivize miners/validators.
Inspecting a block
Look at gasUsed vs gasLimit to see demand; pick a transaction to compare its gasUsed to an estimate.
Real-world connection
For payments, wait multiple confirmations; for low-risk reads, a single confirmation may be sufficient depending on your reorg tolerance.
