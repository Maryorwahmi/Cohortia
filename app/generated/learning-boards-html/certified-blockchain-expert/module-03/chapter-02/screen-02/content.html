Learning Objectives
Build practical skills: estimate fees, inspect transactions, analyse blocks, and set confirmation policies.
01
Define and compute gas
Explain gas, gas limit and gas price, and calculate transaction fees with real numeric examples to estimate costs for transfers and contract calls.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Read transaction fields
Describe nonce, to, value, data, gasLimit, gasPrice and signature and explain how they prevent replay and authorize state changes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain blocks and gas limits
Understand how transactions are batched, how a block gas limit constrains inclusion, and what block metadata tells you.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply finality rules
Decide confirmation depth for payments and state changes, and explain the risk of reorgs and probabilistic finality.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
