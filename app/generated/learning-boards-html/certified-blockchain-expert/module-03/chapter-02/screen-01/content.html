A practical tour of fees, transaction anatomy, block inclusion, and finality for reliable dApp design.
In this lesson
How gas sets computational cost and protects the network
Understand fuel math, transaction fields, and how blocks enforce limits that shape throughput.
Why it matters
Fees, ordering, and finality affect UX, security, and cost for every dApp user.
Mistakes in gas settings can lead to failed transactions or long delays; understanding blocks helps you design confirmation policies.

Quick Safety Tips
  • Always double-check recipient addresses — transfers are irreversible.
  • Set a sufficient gas limit to avoid failure and still refund unused gas.
  • Use recommended gas price estimates to avoid long mempool delays.
