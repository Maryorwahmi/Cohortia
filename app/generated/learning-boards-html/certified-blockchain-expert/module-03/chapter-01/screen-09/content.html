Formula

Fee calculation

Fee is gasUsed multiplied by gasPrice; gasLimit is your maximum willingness to spend.

Transaction example:
{
  from: "0xAlice",
  to: "0xBob",
  value: 1 ETH,
  gasLimit: 100000,
  gasPrice: 20 Gwei
}

Execution consumes: gasUsed = 80000
Fee = 80000 * 20 Gwei = 1,600,000 Gwei = 0.0016 ETH
Notes & pitfalls
  • Low gasLimit: causes revert and still consumes gas up to failure.
  • Unused gas: refunded automatically; you only pay gasUsed.
  • Urgency: higher gasPrice increases the likelihood of faster inclusion.
Quick calculation

Multiply gasUsed by gasPrice and convert units carefully between Gwei and Ether when comparing fees.
