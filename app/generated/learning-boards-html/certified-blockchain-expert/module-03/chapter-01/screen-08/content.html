Concept

Mempool behavior and validation checks

Transactions wait in the mempool until miners choose them. Nodes perform early validation for signature, nonce, and balance before relaying.

Example

Low gasLimit on a contract call causes execution to run out of gas during mining; the call fails but gas consumed is not returned.

Checks before inclusion

Signature validity, correct nonce sequence, and sufficient balance for value plus potential gas costs.

Misconception

Mempool acceptance does not guarantee success; execution outcome is only finalized when the block is produced and executed.

Quick checklist
  • Signature: correct signing key?
  • Nonce: does it match the sender's next expected value?
  • Gas: gasLimit sufficient for contract execution?
  • Fee: gasPrice competitive for inclusion?
Key Takeaway

When debugging, confirm mempool visibility, nonce order, and correct gas settings before looking deeper into contract logic.
