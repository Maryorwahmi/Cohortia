Process Flow

Transaction lifecycle steps

1
Sign
EOA signs transaction with private key; transaction includes gasLimit and gasPrice.
2
Broadcast to mempool
Nodes receive and relay the transaction; it waits for inclusion.
3
Validate & include
Validators check signature, nonce, and balance then include it in a block.
4
Confirmations
As new blocks are added, the transaction becomes more final and harder to revert.
Supporting notes
  • Validation checks: signature, nonce ordering, and sufficient balance for value plus gas limit.
  • Mempool: not ordered globally; miners pick by fee and local policy.
  • Failure: if gas runs out, state reverts and gas is consumed up to failure point.
Key Takeaway

To debug a transaction, inspect signature, nonce, gas, mempool presence, and block inclusion.
