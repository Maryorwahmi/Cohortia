Architecture

Internal transactions and call chains

A top-level transaction can invoke a contract which then calls other contracts. These internal invocations are part of the same execution and are traced rather than signed separately.

Example

A DEX router receives a swap request and calls multiple token contracts to perform transfers and adjust balances internally.

Misconception

Internal transfers are not separate signed transactions; they are traces produced during contract execution.

Audit tips
  1. Check internal traces for transfers and token approvals.
  2. Confirm gas usage across nested calls to estimate cost.
  3. Look for unhandled reverts in internal calls that could cause overall failure.
Key Takeaway

Internal actions reveal the full behavior of a contract call; inspect traces to see all state changes that occurred.
