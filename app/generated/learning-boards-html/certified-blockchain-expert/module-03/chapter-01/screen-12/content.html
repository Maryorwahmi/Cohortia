Activity

Exploring account pages and transactions

Follow the activity template to identify an EOA and a Contract Account, then record example transactions and observations for each.

EOA checklist
  • Look for direct ETH transfers and a transactions list without a contract tab.
  • Record a simple transfer: Txn Hash, Method, Value, Gas Used.
Contract checklist
  • Find the 'Contract' label or verified code, inspect internal transactions and traces.
  • Record incoming calls and any internal transfers triggered.
Reflection

Note visual differences: contract pages often show source and internal transactions; EOAs show straightforward transfer histories.

Key Takeaway

Real-world inspection of account pages reinforces how EOAs initiate actions and contracts execute logic visible in internal traces.
