Solidity Basics and Smart Contract Structure
How contract files, types, state, visibility and constructors form safe, gas-aware programs on the EVM.
In this lesson

Learn the pragma directive, the contract block, state variables and visibility. See a simple storage contract and a number-storage example you can compile in Remix.

Why it matters

Small mistakes in types or visibility increase gas cost or open vulnerabilities. Clear structure leads to auditable, maintainable contracts.

Contains state variables, functions, constructor and modifiers that define behaviour.
