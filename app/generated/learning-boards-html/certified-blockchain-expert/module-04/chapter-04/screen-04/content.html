Core Concept

What makes Corda different?

Corda emphasizes legal clarity and selective sharing. Below are concise definitions you can use when designing systems.

Point-to-Point Communication
Share transaction data only with involved parties, not the whole network.
Local Ledgers
Each node records a subset of transactions it participates in, producing a private view.
Role Separation
Notaries confirm uniqueness; contracts enforce business rules.
Key Takeaway

Design for 'who must know' rather than 'who could know' and separate who checks uniqueness from who checks correctness.

Contrast: Broadcast vs Point-to-Point
Broadcast model
  • All nodes see every transaction.
  • Global ledger state is visible network-wide.
  • Privacy requires extra layers or channels.
Corda model
  • Transactions shared only with relevant parties.
  • Each node keeps a local ledger subset.
  • Privacy is achieved through selective sharing.
Example: Syndicated loan
Only the lender, borrower and an appointed auditor need the loan state. Corda prevents unrelated parties from seeing sensitive terms while preserving a full audit trail for participants.
