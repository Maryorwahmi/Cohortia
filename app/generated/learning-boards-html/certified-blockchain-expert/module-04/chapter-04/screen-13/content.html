Checklist

Pre-deployment validation

Participants: least-privilege and legal roles correct.
Contracts: unit tests for verify(), including failure paths.
Flows: error handling, retries and clear failure messages.
Notary: trust and performance trade-offs checked.
Identity: certificates and network map configuration verified.
Avoid these mistakes
  • Relying on Notary to validate business rules.
  • Including unnecessary parties in participants.
  • Skipping negative and edge-case contract tests.
Key Takeaway

A short, repeatable checklist reduces legal and privacy risks and improves deploy-time confidence.
