Architecture

Nodes

Each node represents a legal entity, holds its identity and certificates, stores its local ledger subset, and runs CorDapps specific to its business role.

Identity and Network Map: unique node identity managed by network services.
Ledger storage: a node records only transactions it participates in.
CorDapp hosting: runtime for states, contracts, and flows.
Architecture

CorDapps

CorDapps implement business logic using three building blocks:

  • States: immutable facts with participants and ownership.
  • Contracts: code with verify() enforcing transaction rules.
  • Flows: orchestrated steps that exchange proposals and signatures.
Pro tip

Keep contract logic deterministic and keep flows responsible for communication and retry semantics, not business validation.
