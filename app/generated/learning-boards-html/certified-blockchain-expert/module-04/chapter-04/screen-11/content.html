Hierarchy

States → Contracts → Flows

Keep state shape clear, write contracts that deterministically verify transactions, and make flows responsible for communication and retries.

  • States: data and participants.
  • Contracts: single source of business rules via verify().
  • Flows: multi-party orchestration and error handling.
Example change
If the legal terms of a loan change, update the state model and contract.verify() to reflect the new rules; flows may only need small adjustments to collect new approvals.
Check your understanding

Ask: does this change alter the fact model, the rules for valid transitions, or just the orchestration sequence?
