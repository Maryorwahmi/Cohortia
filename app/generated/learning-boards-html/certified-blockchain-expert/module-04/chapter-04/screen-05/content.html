Comparison

Broadcast Model

  • All or many nodes receive transaction data for visibility.
  • Simplifies global auditing but increases exposure risk.
  • Often requires channels or permissioning to segment data.
Misconception
Broadcast systems are not inherently private even if encrypted; key management and access control still matter.
Comparison

Corda Point-to-Point

  • Transaction payloads are sent only to involved parties and services.
  • Each node stores a local ledger subset relevant to its role.
  • Reduces unnecessary disclosure and helps meet regulatory confidentiality needs.
Practical tip
Explicitly list auditors or regulators as participants only when legally required to avoid over-sharing.
