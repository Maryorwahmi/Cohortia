Comparison › Peer Types
Endorsing Peer

Simulates chaincode, signs proposal responses, and enforces the chaincode's endorsement policy during simulation.

Example: an endorser returns a simulated asset transfer result and a signature to the client.
Committing Peer

Validates blocks, checks endorsements and read-write sets, and updates the world state and blockchain ledger.

Example: after ordering, committers mark or append transactions and update state DB.
Anchor Peer

Acts as a contact point for cross-organization gossip to discover peers on the same channel and exchange blocks.

Example: peers in Org A use the anchor to learn about Org B’s peers for channel communication.
Common mistake

Confusing endorsement with commit: endorsement proves simulation validity, but the commit only happens after ordering and validation.
