Definition › Certificate Authority
What a CA provides
  • Issue X.509 certificates for identities and TLS.
  • Manage enrollment, issuance, and revocation for organization members.
  • Establish trust between organizations through certificate chains.
Operational note: plan revocation propagation and secure private keys to avoid network outages.
Concrete example

Org1 runs its own CA. When a new peer is added, the CA issues its certificate which peers and orderers trust via the MSP configuration.

Common mistake

Failing to rotate or revoke compromised keys leads to broken TLS or unauthorized access. Plan certificate lifecycle management.
