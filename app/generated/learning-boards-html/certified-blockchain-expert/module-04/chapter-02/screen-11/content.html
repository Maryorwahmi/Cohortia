Core Concept › Channels & Privacy
What a channel does

Provides an isolated ledger and transaction layer for a subset of organizations, controlling visibility and policies.

Example: a channel between Supplier A and Manufacturer B keeps pricing confidential from other consortium members.
Operational note

Too many channels increase operational overhead; consider private data collections when only payload secrecy is needed within a channel.

Common mistake

Creating channels for every pair of organizations leads to complexity. Design channels with governance and lifecycle in mind.
