docker-compose.yaml (excerpt)
version: '2'

services:
  ca.org1.example.com:
    image: hyperledger/fabric-ca:latest
  orderer.example.com:
    image: hyperledger/fabric-orderer:latest
  peer0.org1.example.com:
    image: hyperledger/fabric-peer:latest
Observation: services include a CA, an orderer, and a peer. The CA issues certificates used by peers and clients to authenticate.
configtx.yaml (excerpt)
Orderer:
    OrdererType: solo # Or Raft, Kafka

Organizations:
    - &Org1
        Name: Org1MSP
        Endorsement:
            Type: Signature
            Rule: "OR('Org1MSP.peer')"
Observation: OrdererType is solo in this snippet; Org1 endorsement rule requires a peer from Org1 to endorse.

Question: If an endorsement policy requires 3 organizations but only two endorse, what happens at validation? Answer: The transaction will be invalid and marked so in the ledger.
