Common Operational Mistakes
Confusing public and private state
Always query a recipient node to verify private state; non-participants won't show private updates.
PTM misconfiguration
Verify PTM public keys and network addresses; misconfigured keys prevent delivery and decryption.
Over-relying on permissioning
Permissioning controls membership but requires off-chain governance for identity and policy management.
Weak key security
Protect node and PTM keys with rotation, secure stores, and audit trails to preserve confidentiality.
Operational Checklist

Include PTM tests, private-state verification, key management, and governance approval steps in your deployment pipeline to avoid privacy regressions.
