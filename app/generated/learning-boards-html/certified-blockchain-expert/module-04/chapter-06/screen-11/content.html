Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Enterprise Ethereum Alliance
EEA Purpose
A cross-industry organization that creates open specifications and best practices to enable enterprise Ethereum interoperability and common operational patterns.
Specification guidance for permissioning and privacy
Encourages common APIs and models across clients and PTMs
Reduces fragmentation for multi-vendor deployments
Why it matters

Standards from the EEA help architects design systems that interoperate and follow community-accepted privacy and permissioning patterns.
