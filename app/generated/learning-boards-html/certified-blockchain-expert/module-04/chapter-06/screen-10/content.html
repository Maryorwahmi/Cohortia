Hyperledger Besu
An open-source Java-based Ethereum client that supports public and permissioned networks and offers private transaction capabilities through PTMs like Orion or Tessera.
  • Modular architecture and Java ecosystem
  • Supports IBFT and PoA consensus modes
  • Private transactions with PTM integration
Choosing a Client
Base your choice on language familiarity, required integrations, operational tools, and vendor support. Both clients rely on similar privacy patterns, so operational fit matters more than theoretical privacy differences.
Design Tip

Map your existing tooling and monitoring stack to the client before making a selection; language and ops familiarity reduces deployment risk.
