Consensus Choices
Istanbul BFT (IBFT)
Byzantine Fault Tolerant; immediate finality; tolerates some malicious nodes. Best when you need robustness against arbitrary faults.
Raft
Crash fault tolerant and simple. Use when nodes are trusted and you want a straightforward leader-based protocol.
Clique
Proof-of-Authority style where authorized signers rotate. Use where identity and authority are primary governance levers.
Concrete Scenario
A clearinghouse picks IBFT so finality is immediate and a small group of validators cannot fork the ledger. If every participant is a trusted regulated bank, Raft might be selected for operational simplicity.
Misconception
Choosing a faster consensus doesn't replace governance. Faster finality helps throughput but your governance model must still address validator membership and key compromise scenarios.
Pro Tip

Document trust assumptions and failure modes before picking a consensus. Match IBFT to adversarial settings and Raft to cooperative, trusted groups.
