Tessera and Orion
These PTMs handle encryption, storage, and distribution of private payloads to designated recipients' PTMs using public-key cryptography.
  • Hold private keys securely and map recipient public keys
  • Store encrypted payload blobs for retrieval
  • Deliver payloads point-to-point; the chain only receives a hash
Operational Notes
PTMs are critical infrastructure. Misconfiguration or key compromise can break privacy guarantees. Include key rotation, access controls, and monitoring in your deployment plan.
Pitfall

Using the wrong public key for a recipient prevents decryption and halts private execution, causing difficult-to-debug failures.
