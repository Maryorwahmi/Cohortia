Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Quick Reference
Validate Existence

Always GetState before PutState or DelState for operations that depend on prior existence.

Deterministic Logic

Avoid randomness and direct external calls; use oracle patterns for off-chain data.

Follow Lifecycle

Package, install, approve, and commit. Commit is the step that activates the chaincode definition on the channel.

Final takeaway

Apply these rules while implementing functions like transferAsset to maintain consistent, secure, and governed contracts in production.
