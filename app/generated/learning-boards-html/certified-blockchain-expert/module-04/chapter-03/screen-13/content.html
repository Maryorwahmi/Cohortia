Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Chaincode

Business logic running on peers; must be deterministic and well-validated.

Ledger APIs

GetState, PutState, DelState manage JSON-marshaled key-value data and must be error-checked.

Lifecycle (v2.x)

Package, install, approve per org, and commit to make chaincode active on a channel.

Summary

Design deterministic, well-tested chaincode, validate inputs and state transitions, and use the v2.x lifecycle to publish contracts under multi-org governance.
