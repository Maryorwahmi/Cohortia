Comparison

Public vs Enterprise throughput

Public chains
Optimized for open participation and censorship resistance; consensus choices limit transaction rate in many public networks.
Enterprise platforms
Use BFT-like or leader-based consensus and architecture tuning to increase throughput and achieve low-latency finality for business processes.
Considerations: required peak transactions per second, transaction finality needs, number of validators, and acceptable decentralization trade-offs.
Example trade-off
High-volume payments
An interbank settlement system must process many transfers quickly and ensure finality. Choosing a BFT protocol with a smaller set of validators can achieve this, while the governance agreement manages validator behavior and liability.
Misconception
Faster means insecure
In enterprise settings, faster consensus is achieved by changing trust assumptions and validator sets, not by ignoring security; governance and contracts provide operational controls.
Key Takeaway

Match consensus and validator topology to throughput and finality needs, understanding the trade-offs in decentralization and operational governance.
