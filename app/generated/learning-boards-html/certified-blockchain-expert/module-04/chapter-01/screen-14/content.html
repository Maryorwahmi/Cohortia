Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Permissioned Blockchain
Authorized participants and identity enable compliance and accountability.
Privacy Mechanisms
Private channels, off-chain storage, and cryptographic proofs limit visibility to authorized parties.
Performance & Governance
Consensus and validator topology shape throughput; governance defines operational rules and liability.
Summary
Enterprise blockchain succeeds where known participants need shared verification, selective visibility, and governance. Use a focused pilot to validate privacy, integration, and measurable efficiency gains.
