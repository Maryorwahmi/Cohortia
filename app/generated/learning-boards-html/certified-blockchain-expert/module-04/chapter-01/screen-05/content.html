Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Permissioning Process
Process

Onboard → Transact → Audit

1
Onboarding
Register participants, issue credentials, and formalize governance through agreements and KYC as needed.
2
Transaction controls
Use identity to authorize who can propose, endorse, or view transactions and to enforce selective visibility.
3
Audit and dispute
Attribute actions to entities so governance rules can resolve disputes and provide legal evidence.
Key Takeaway

Identity and onboarding shape who can do what on the ledger, which lets enterprises meet legal and regulatory obligations while sharing state.

Example scenario
Supplier shipment record
A supplier submits a shipment event that only the manufacturer and regulator may read. Other participants see a hashed commitment to preserve supply-chain integrity without exposing confidential terms.
