Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Learning Objectives
Objective evidence cards
01 — Explain
Write a paragraph comparing permissionless and permissioned networks and list three consequences for identity and compliance.
02 — Map
Create a use-case map linking business pain to blockchain benefits and to measurable KPIs.
03 — Sketch
Sketch a platform architecture that identifies privacy mechanisms and a consensus choice with rationale.
04 — Apply
Complete a scenario checklist and recommend whether blockchain is a fit, with trade-offs noted.
Visual mnemonic
Evidence flow
Problem → Business driver → Platform requirement → Recommendation. This flow ensures each recommendation ties to measurable needs and technical capabilities.
Quick tip
Always document the assumptions that underlie your recommendation: participant trust model, data sensitivity, and existing system constraints.
