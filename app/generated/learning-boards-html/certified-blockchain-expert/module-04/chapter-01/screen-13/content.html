Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › AutoCorp Case Study
Scenario summary
AutoCorp faces manual data entry across many suppliers, causing delays, disputes, and slow recalls. The goals are transparency, faster recalls, and fewer disputes.
Checklist applied
  • Multiple untrusted parties need shared verification: yes.
  • Immutability required for provenance and recalls: yes.
  • Intermediary friction causing cost/delay: yes.
Recommended design elements
Permissioned consortium with identity management and onboarding rules.
Privacy using private channels for suppliers and hashed anchors for public verification.
Consensus tuned for throughput and predictable finality to handle high event rates.
Proof-of-concept focusing on provenance, recall time, and dispute reduction KPIs.
Key Takeaway

AutoCorp should pilot an enterprise blockchain for provenance with clear governance and integration plans, measuring recall speed and dispute rates as success criteria.
