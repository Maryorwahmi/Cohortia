Definition & techniques

Private channels, off-chain storage, and proofs

Private channels
A subset of members shares full transaction data; the wider network may see commitments or hashes.
Off-chain data stores
Sensitive fields are stored securely off-chain with ledger references that preserve integrity without exposing values.
Cryptographic proofs
Techniques like zero-knowledge proofs let you validate claims without revealing underlying confidential data, at a cost of added complexity.
Example and trade-offs
Pharmaceutical provenance
Manufacturers and regulators may need full visibility to batch records, distributors need shipment proofs, and the public only needs aggregated authenticity markers. A mix of private channels and hashed anchors can meet these needs.
Common mistake
Assuming one technique fits all
Privacy strategies must be chosen per-use-case; some require audits by regulators while others need only limited partner visibility.
Key Takeaway

Privacy is multi-dimensional; select private channels, off-chain storage, or cryptographic proofs based on visibility, audit needs, and operational complexity.
