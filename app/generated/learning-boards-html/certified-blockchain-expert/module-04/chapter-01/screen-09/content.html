Integration patterns

Adapters, middleware, and canonical models

Use adapters to translate internal events into ledger transactions and middleware to buffer and validate messages.
Agree on a canonical data model to avoid mapping mismatches across participants.
Keep sensitive fields in local systems, anchoring integrity on-chain with hashes or commitments.
Example
An ERP system emits shipment confirmations to middleware, which validates, enriches, and posts a transaction to the ledger. The ledger holds proofs and settlement triggers; detailed invoice data stays in the ERP under the control of each participant.
Common mistake
Treating blockchain as a primary operational database causes duplication, inconsistent state, and governance confusion. Use it for shared validation and coordination instead.
Key Takeaway

Design integrations that preserve data sovereignty, map events reliably, and manage consistency between internal systems and the shared ledger.
