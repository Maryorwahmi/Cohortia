Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › When to Choose Blockchain
Checklist
1.
Do multiple untrusted parties need to share and verify data?
2.
Is an immutable audit trail required for compliance or recalls?
3.
Are intermediaries creating measurable cost or delay?
4.
Can you meet privacy and governance requirements practically?
Quick Guidance

If core questions are affirmative, produce a small proof-of-concept that validates integration, privacy controls, and governance before full roll-out.

Common challenges
  • Defining governance and legal agreements for consortium membership and liability.
  • Integrating heterogeneous enterprise systems and preserving data sovereignty.
  • Balancing privacy needs with transparency and auditability.
Key Takeaway

Use a focused checklist and a short proof-of-concept to validate fit, integration, and governance before committing to a broad deployment.
