Certificate Authorities: Fabric's Permissioned Nature

How CAs issue digital identities for authentication and authorization in the network.

What is a CA?

A Certificate Authority is a trusted entity that issues digital certificates, verifying the identity of network participants.

Role of PKI

Fabric uses Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) for identity management, where each entity has a unique digital identity.

Authentication & Authorization

Certificates enable the network to authenticate participants and control their access to resources and actions.
