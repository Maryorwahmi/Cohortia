The Ordering Service: Chronological Transaction Blocks

Understanding the critical role of the Orderer in collecting, ordering, and broadcasting transactions.

Collect Endorsed Transactions

Receives transactions that have already been simulated and signed by endorsing peers.

Order & Package into Blocks

Establishes a chronological order for transactions and groups them into immutable blocks.

Broadcast to Committing Peers

Distributes the newly formed blocks to all relevant committing peers on the channel.
