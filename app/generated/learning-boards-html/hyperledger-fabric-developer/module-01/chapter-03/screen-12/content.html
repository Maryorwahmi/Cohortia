Transaction Lifecycle

Fabric Transaction Flow: Proposal & Endorsement

Tracing the initial steps from client proposal to peer endorsement.

Step 1:Client Proposal

A client application creates a transaction proposal and sends it to a set of endorsing peers.

Step 2:Peer Endorsement

Endorsing peers simulate the transaction, execute chaincode, and sign the proposal with their endorsement.

Example Scenario

A client wants to transfer 'asset123'. It sends a 'transferAsset' proposal to two endorsing peers. Both peers simulate the transfer and, if valid, sign the proposal.
