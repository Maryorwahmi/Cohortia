Core Components of a Fabric Network

Unpacking the Architecture of Hyperledger Fabric

This chapter will guide you through the essential building blocks of a Hyperledger Fabric network. You'll learn how Peers, Orderers, and Certificate Authorities collaborate to process transactions, maintain the ledger, and ensure the integrity and privacy of your data. Understanding these components is key to mastering Fabric.

Key Takeaway

Hyperledger Fabric's modular design relies on specialized components working in concert to ensure ledger integrity, transaction processing, and data privacy.
