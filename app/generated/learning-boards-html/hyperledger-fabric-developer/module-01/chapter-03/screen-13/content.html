Transaction Lifecycle

Fabric Transaction Flow: Ordering & Commit

Following the transaction from ordering to final ledger commitment.

Step 3:Submission to Orderer

Client collects endorsements and submits the endorsed transaction to the Ordering Service.

Step 4:Ordering & Blocking

Ordering Service orders transactions into blocks and broadcasts them to committing peers.

Step 5 & 6:Validation & Commit

Committing peers validate the block, update their world state, and append it to their ledger.

Key Takeaway

The separation of endorsement, ordering, and commitment ensures a robust, secure, and private transaction lifecycle in Hyperledger Fabric.
