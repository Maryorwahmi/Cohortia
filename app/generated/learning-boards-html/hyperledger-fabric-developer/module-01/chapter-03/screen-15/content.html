Key Concepts: Structure & Data

Understanding how Fabric organizes participants and stores data

Organization

A logical grouping of network components representing a real-world entity.

Channel

A private communication pathway with an isolated ledger between a subset of organizations.

Ledger

The distributed, immutable record of all transactions, comprising world state and blockchain.

Summary Takeaway

Organizations and Channels provide the structural framework for private collaboration, while the Ledger's World State and Blockchain ensure both current data access and an immutable transaction history.
