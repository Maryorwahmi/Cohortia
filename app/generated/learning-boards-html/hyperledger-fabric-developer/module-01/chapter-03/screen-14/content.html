Key Concepts: Fabric Components

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Peer

A fundamental component that hosts ledgers and instances of chaincode.

Orderer

Collects endorsed transactions, orders them, and packages them into blocks.

Certificate Authority (CA)

Issues digital certificates for identities, enabling permissioned access.

Summary Takeaway

Peers, Orderers, and CAs are the foundational components that enable Hyperledger Fabric's distributed ledger, transaction processing, and secure identity management.
