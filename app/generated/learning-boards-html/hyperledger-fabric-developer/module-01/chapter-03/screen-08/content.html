Network Structure

Organizations & Channels: Collaborative Privacy

How entities group components and create isolated ledgers for specific collaborations.

Organizations Defined

A logical grouping of network components (peers, CAs) representing a real-world entity like a company or department.

Channels for Privacy

Private communication pathways between a subset of organizations, each with its own isolated ledger.

Example: Supply Chain

Manufacturer and supplier use one channel; distributor and retailer use another, ensuring data confidentiality.

Common Misconception

All organizations in a Fabric network share the same ledger. In reality, channels allow for multiple, isolated ledgers, ensuring privacy between specific participants.
