Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Hands-on Activity
Centralized model
One person maintains the official ledger. Draw a simple list of transactions. Ask: if a transaction is disputed, how does the central operator prove correctness? What happens if the ledger is corrupted?
Reflection prompt
What risks do you see from centralization in this scenario, and who bears them?
Decentralized model
Each friend keeps their own copy. When a transaction occurs, everyone records it. Decide an agreement rule: do all sign, or is majority enough? Consider how mismatches are detected and corrected.
Key Takeaway
This activity highlights that distribution improves resilience, but agreement rules and identity checks are necessary to prevent divergence and detect tampering.
