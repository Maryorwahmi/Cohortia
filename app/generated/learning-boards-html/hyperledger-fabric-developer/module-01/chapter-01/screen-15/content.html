Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Decentralization
Sharing control increases resilience but requires shared governance and coordination.
Consensus
Agreement protocols make distributed copies converge; choose one for your performance and finality needs.
Permission Models
Public, private, and consortium models trade openness for privacy and governance; select the model that fits your stakeholders.
Concluding takeaway
Use the lenses of trust, visibility, and consensus to decide when and how to apply DLT. These principles guide practical architecture choices when building with Hyperledger Fabric.
