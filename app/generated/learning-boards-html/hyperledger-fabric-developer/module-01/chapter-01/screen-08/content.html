Transparency explained

Visibility of ledger entries supports independent verification and audit. The degree of transparency varies between public and permissioned networks.

Public chain
Anyone can read; strong public verification but less privacy for sensitive data.
Permissioned chain
Access is limited to authorized participants, allowing privacy while keeping a shared truth among collaborators.
Scenario
Supply chain example
Partners share shipment status but keep pricing private. Permissioned controls let them verify delivery without exposing confidential fields.
Key Takeaway

Design visibility intentionally: choose the level of transparency that supports verification without exposing sensitive data to unauthorized parties.
