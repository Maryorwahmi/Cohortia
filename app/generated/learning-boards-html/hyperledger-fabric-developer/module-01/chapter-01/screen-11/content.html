Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Limits of Centralized Systems
Common limitations
  • Single point of failure can cause outages or data loss.
  • Data silos hinder interoperability across organizations.
  • Intermediaries impose cost, delay, and require trust.
Illustration
If a single operator's ledger is corrupted, participants have little recourse. Shared ledgers distribute risk and enable independent verification.
When to use DLT
Decision factors
Evaluate whether your process needs cross-organization verification, whether intermediaries add cost, and whether reducing a single point of trust produces measurable benefit.
Key Takeaway

DLT reduces single points of failure and intermediary dependence but requires governance and design to capture real business value.
