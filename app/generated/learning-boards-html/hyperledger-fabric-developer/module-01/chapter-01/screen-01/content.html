Build a practical mental model of DLT and blockchain. Understand immutability, decentralization, transparency, and consensus and why enterprises choose permissioned, consortium networks.

Explore Key Topics
Immutability
Why cryptographic links make past records hard to change.
Decentralization
Multiple copies reduce single points of failure.
Consensus
How participants agree on valid state changes.
Centralized Ledger
One operator keeps the authoritative copy. Single point of trust.
Distributed Ledger
Each participant holds a copy; updates are validated by consensus.
Immutability: blocks linked cryptographically to resist tampering.
Privacy: permissioned networks restrict who can read or write.
Consensus: a coordinated agreement process to update the ledger.
