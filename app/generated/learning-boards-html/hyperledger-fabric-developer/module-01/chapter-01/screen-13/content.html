Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Why Hyperledger Fabric
Enterprise priorities

Enterprises need privacy, identity, and predictable performance. Fabric's permissioned architecture enforces authorized participation and access control while allowing organizations to share selected data.

Consortium governance
Multiple organizations jointly manage policies, improving trust without exposing data publicly.
Key Takeaway

Fabric fits use cases where controlled sharing, identity, and governance are more important than fully public openness.

Practical example
Supply chain consortium
Manufacturers, shippers, and retailers jointly run the network. They verify events while keeping commercial details private to authorized parties.
Misconception
Permissioned does not mean centralized. A consortium distributes governance among known partners to reduce single-party control.
