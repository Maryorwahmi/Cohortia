Hyperledger Fabric is NOT Bitcoin or Ethereum

Fabric is a permissioned, modular framework for private business networks. It prioritizes identity, confidentiality, and pluggable components to fit enterprise constraints.

In this lesson
Identity, channels, consensus
How Fabric supports private collaboration with modular components.
Coming up
Chaincode overview
Smart contracts in Go, Node.js, or Java.
Why it matters
Enterprise controls
Regulatory compliance and data privacy are built-in.
Concept map
Permissioned
Known participants via Certificate Authorities
Channels
Private ledgers for subsets of members
Consensus
Pluggable ordering like Raft
Key Takeaway

Fabric gives enterprises identity, privacy through channels, and modular consensus to meet regulatory and performance needs.
