Benefits at a glance
Privacy
Private channels and collections keep sensitive data visible only to authorized members.
Performance
Pluggable consensus avoids resource-heavy mining and supports predictable throughput.
Compliance
Verifiable identities and auditable transactions aid regulatory reporting and governance.
Use case: Supply chain
Track provenance across many parties while sharing pricing only on a supplier-manufacturer channel for confidentiality.
Use case: Healthcare
Enable secure, auditable sharing of patient records between authorized providers using channels and identity controls.
