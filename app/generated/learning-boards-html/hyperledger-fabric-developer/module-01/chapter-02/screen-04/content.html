Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Fabric vs Public
Public Blockchains
Open participation, pseudonymous accounts
  • Anyone can join; identities may be pseudonymous.
  • Consensus often uses mining or staking with higher resource cost.
  • Transparency is broad, making sensitive data exposure likely.
Common misconception
Public chains are always more secure — security models differ and depend on threat assumptions and incentives.
Hyperledger Fabric
Permissioned, identity-first, modular
  • Participants have issued identities via Certificate Authorities.
  • Private channels provide ledger-level isolation for subsets of members.
  • Ordering and consensus are pluggable to suit enterprise needs.
Key benefit
Control over identity, visibility, and consensus allows compliance while preserving immutability where needed.
