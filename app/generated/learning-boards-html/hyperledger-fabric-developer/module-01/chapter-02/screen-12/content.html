Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Hyperledger Fabric
An enterprise-grade, permissioned DLT framework focused on known participants and modular components.
Permissioned Network
All participants are identified and authorized, typically via Certificate Authorities for auditability and governance.
Private Channels
Ledger-level isolation so a subset of network members can transact privately within a shared network.
Summary takeaway
Use Fabric when verifiable identity, controlled visibility, and modular consensus are required to meet enterprise constraints.
