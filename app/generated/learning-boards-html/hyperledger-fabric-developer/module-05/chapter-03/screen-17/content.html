Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Off-chain Data

Data that is stored outside the blockchain ledger, typically for performance, cost, or privacy reasons.

Cryptographic Hash

A fixed-size string generated from data, used to ensure the integrity of off-chain data by storing the hash on-chain.

IPFS

A decentralized peer-to-peer network for storing and sharing data, often used for off-chain storage with Fabric due to its content-addressing capabilities.

Chapter Summary

Off-chain data, secured by cryptographic hashes, provides a scalable and efficient way to manage large or sensitive information, complementing the on-chain ledger.
