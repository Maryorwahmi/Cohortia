Key Concepts

Essential terms from this chapter

Endorsement Policy

A rule specifying which organizations or identities must approve a transaction for it to be valid.

Raft Ordering Service

A crash-fault-tolerant (CFT) consensus mechanism in Fabric, providing high availability and resilience.

Service Discovery

A Fabric feature allowing client applications to dynamically query network topology and endorsement policies.

Chapter Summary

This chapter provided a deep dive into advanced Hyperledger Fabric operations, covering dynamic channel configuration updates, endorsement policies, the Raft ordering service, and service discovery. These concepts are vital for building and maintaining resilient, scalable, and adaptable blockchain networks.
