Consensus Mechanism

The Raft Ordering Service

Ensuring crash-fault tolerance and high availability

What is Raft?

  • Recommended consensus mechanism in Hyperledger Fabric.
  • Crash-Fault-Tolerant (CFT): Can tolerate failure of a minority of nodes.
  • Ensures high availability and resilience for the ordering service.
  • Replaced Kafka-based ordering services due to its CFT nature.

Leader-Follower Model

  • Leader Node: Receives transactions, orders them into blocks, and disseminates blocks.
  • Follower Nodes: Replicate the leader's log and participate in leader elections.
  • Leader Election: If the leader fails, remaining nodes elect a new leader to maintain service.

Raft Principles for Stability

  • Leader Election: A robust process to select a new leader if the current one fails.
  • Log Replication: Ensures all follower nodes have an identical, ordered log of transactions.
  • Commit Rule: Defines when a transaction is considered finalized and immutable across the cluster.
  • Fault Tolerance: A cluster of N nodes can tolerate (N-1)/2 failures (e.g., 2 failures in a 5-node cluster).
Key Takeaway

Raft's CFT design and leader-follower model provide a highly available and resilient ordering service, crucial for stable Fabric network operations.
