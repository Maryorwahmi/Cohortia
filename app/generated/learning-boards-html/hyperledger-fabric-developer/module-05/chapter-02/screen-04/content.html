Core Concept

Evolution of Chaincode Lifecycle

From centralized control to decentralized governance in Fabric v2.0+

Pre-Fabric v2.0

  • •Simpler `install` and `instantiate`/`upgrade`
  • •Primarily single administrator control
  • •Less flexible governance

Fabric v2.0+ (New Lifecycle)

  • •Decentralized, multi-organization governance
  • •Consensus-based approval for deployment/upgrade
  • •Enhanced security and trust
Key Takeaway

The new chaincode lifecycle in Fabric v2.0+ shifts from single-admin control to a collaborative, multi-organization approval process, significantly improving network security and trust.

Why it matters

This decentralized approach ensures that no single organization can unilaterally deploy or update chaincode, fostering greater transparency and shared responsibility across the blockchain network.

Real-world Connection

Imagine a consortium of banks. The new lifecycle ensures that all member banks must agree on the smart contract logic before it governs their shared transactions, preventing any one bank from making unauthorized changes.
