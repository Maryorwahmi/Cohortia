Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Approve for My Org

An explicit step where each organization formally approves a chaincode definition (name, version, sequence, policy) for their own use.

Commit

The final step where a chaincode definition, once approved by a sufficient number of organizations, is made active on the channel.

Sequence Number

A monotonically increasing integer that identifies successive versions of a chaincode definition, crucial for upgrades.

Chapter Summary

The approval and commitment steps, guided by the sequence number, ensure decentralized governance and enable seamless chaincode upgrades, including vital data migration processes.
