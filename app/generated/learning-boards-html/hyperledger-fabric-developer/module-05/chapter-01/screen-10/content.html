The Risk of Misconfiguration

Incorrectly defined collection policies can lead to critical transaction failures.

Endorsement Failures

Peers of unauthorized organizations will reject private data transactions.

Example Scenario

Org3 tries to write to a collection only for Org1 and Org2; transaction fails.

Best Practices for Policies

Ensure your collection policies are accurate and thoroughly tested.

Meticulous Review

Verify all required organizations are correctly specified in the policy.

Test Thoroughly

Simulate transactions from different organizations to confirm expected behavior.

Key Takeaway

Always double-check your collection policies; a misconfiguration can lead to transaction rejections and compromise data privacy.
