Key Concepts to Remember

Essential Takeaways (Part 1 of 2)

Private Data Collections (PDC)

A Fabric feature enabling a subset of organizations on a channel to share confidential data, hidden from others.

Off-chain Storage

Actual private data is stored in a private database on authorized peers, not directly on the blockchain ledger.

Hash on Ledger

Only a cryptographic hash of the private data is committed to the main ledger, proving existence without revealing content.

Summary Takeaway

Private Data Collections are a cornerstone of Hyperledger Fabric's privacy model, enabling selective data confidentiality through off-chain storage and on-chain cryptographic proofs.
