Scenario: Public Asset, Private Price

Manage asset details with varying confidentiality requirements.

Public Data

assetID and owner are visible to all channel participants.

Private Data

assetPrice is confidential between buyer and seller.

Implementing the Solution

Leverage both public and private data APIs in your chaincode.

putState / getState

For public assetID and owner details.

putPrivateData / getPrivateData

For confidential assetPrice within a dedicated collection.

Key Takeaway

PDCs enable a hybrid data model, allowing public and private information to coexist and be managed securely within the same chaincode and channel.
