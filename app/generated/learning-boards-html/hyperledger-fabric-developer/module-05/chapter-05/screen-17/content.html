MSP (Membership Service Provider)

Fabric's identity management component, defining an organization's members and their cryptographic identities.

Secrets Management

Securely storing and managing sensitive information (e.g., private keys, certificates) using dedicated services.

High Availability (HA)

Designing a system to operate continuously without failure for a long time, typically through redundancy and failover mechanisms.

Chapter Insight

A resilient Hyperledger Fabric network relies on robust identity management, secure handling of secrets, comprehensive data recovery plans, and architectural redundancy.
