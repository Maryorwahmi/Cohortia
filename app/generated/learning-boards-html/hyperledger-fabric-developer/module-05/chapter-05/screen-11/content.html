Core Strategy

Phased Recovery for Fabric

A systematic approach to restoring network operations after a catastrophic event.

1

Restore Critical Components

Prioritize CAs, orderers, and a subset of peers from secure backups.

2

Allow Peers to Sync

New or restored peers will automatically sync their ledger from healthy network components.

3

Verify Network Health

Confirm all components are operational and transactions are being processed correctly.

Common Misconception

Disaster recovery is only about data restoration. It's equally about restoring operational capability and network consensus.

Example Scenario

Data Center Outage Recovery

Imagine a complete data center failure hosting your Fabric network.

1

New Infrastructure

Provision new infrastructure in a different, unaffected data center.

2

Restore Core Services

Restore CAs and orderers from their latest backups to the new infrastructure.

3

Bring Up New Peers

Deploy new peer instances. They will connect and automatically sync their ledgers from the restored orderers and any other healthy peers.

Key Takeaway

Regularly testing your disaster recovery plan is as important as having one, ensuring your network can quickly resume operations after an unforeseen event.
