Security Best Practices

Essential Security: TLS and Access Control

Protecting Communication and Identities in Fabric

TLS (Transport Layer Security)

All communication between Fabric components (peers, orderers, CAs, clients) *must* be secured with TLS to prevent eavesdropping and tampering. Ensure certificates are properly issued and configured.

Why it matters

TLS is the first line of defense, encrypting data in transit and ensuring secure, private communication across your network.

Access Control

  • •**MSP (Membership Service Provider):** Fabric's identity management system. Configure correctly for authorized actions.
  • •**RBAC (Role-Based Access Control):** Implement for clients and admins, ensuring least privilege.
  • •**Firewalls:** Restrict network access to only necessary ports and IP ranges.
Common Mistake

Overlooking granular access controls, potentially granting excessive permissions to users or applications.
