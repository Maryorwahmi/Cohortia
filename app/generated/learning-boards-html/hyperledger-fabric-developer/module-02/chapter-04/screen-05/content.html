Network Architecture

Components of the Test Network

Understanding the Building Blocks

Ordering Service

Ensures transaction order.

Two Organizations

Org1 & Org2 for transactions.

Peer Node per Org

Maintains ledger, endorses transactions.

Certificate Authorities

Issues cryptographic identities.

Component Roles
  • Ordering Service: Guarantees consistent transaction ordering across the network.
  • Organizations (Org1, Org2): Represent distinct business entities participating in the network.
  • Peer Nodes: Store the ledger and execute chaincode, endorsing transactions.
  • Certificate Authorities: Provide digital identities for secure authentication and authorization.
Real-world Analogy

Think of the `test-network` as a small, self-contained company with two departments (Org1, Org2). The Ordering Service is like the company's internal mailroom, ensuring all documents are processed in order. Each department has a records office (Peer Node) to keep copies of all transactions, and an HR department (CA) to issue employee IDs.

Key Takeaway

Each component plays a specific, interdependent role in maintaining the integrity and functionality of the Fabric network.
