This output confirms the creation of 'mychannel' and that both Org1 and Org2 peers have successfully joined it.

What is a Channel?

A channel is a private communication layer that enables specific organizations to transact and share ledger data confidentially.

Command Actions
  • Generates the channel configuration artifact.
  • Instructs specified peer nodes (Org1, Org2) to join the new channel.
Misconception

Not all organizations on a Fabric network automatically share all data. Channels enforce necessary privacy and data segregation.

Key Takeaway

Channels are fundamental for privacy and controlled data sharing, defining who can see and interact with specific transactions.
