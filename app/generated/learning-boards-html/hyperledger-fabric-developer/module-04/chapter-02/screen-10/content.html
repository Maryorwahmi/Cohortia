Registration vs. Enrollment

Two Distinct Steps to a Fabric Identity

1

Registration

An administrator registers a new user with the Fabric CA, assigning an enrollmentID and a secret. This creates an entry in the CA's database, pre-approving the user.

Admin Action
2

Enrollment

The registered user (or application) uses their enrollmentID and secret to request and obtain their digital identity (certificate and private key) from the CA. This identity is then stored in a Wallet.

User/App Action
Key Takeaway

Registration is the administrative act of creating a user record with the CA, while enrollment is the user's action of obtaining their cryptographic identity from the CA.
