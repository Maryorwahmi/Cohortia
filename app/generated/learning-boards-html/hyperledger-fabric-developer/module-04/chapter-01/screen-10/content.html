Smart Contracts: Querying vs. Submitting Transactions

Reading State vs. Modifying the Ledger

Evaluate Transaction (Query)

  • •Used for read-only operations on the ledger.
  • •Does not modify the ledger state.
  • •Executes on a single peer; no full consensus required.
  • •Example: readAsset(assetId) to get current details.
Analogy

Like checking your bank balance – it doesn't change anything.

Submit Transaction

  • •Used for operations that modify the ledger state.
  • •Involves full transaction lifecycle (endorsement, ordering, commit).
  • •Waits for commitment to ensure immutability.
  • •Example: createAsset(id, value) or transferAsset(id, newOwner).
Analogy

Like making a deposit or withdrawal – it changes your balance.

Key Takeaway

Always differentiate between querying for data and submitting transactions to modify the ledger, as they involve distinct SDK methods and network processes.
