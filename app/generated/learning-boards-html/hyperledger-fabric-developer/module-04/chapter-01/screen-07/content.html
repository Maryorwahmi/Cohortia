Digital Identities: Who You Are on the Network

Authenticating Participants with X.509 Certificates

What is an Identity?

A digital representation (X.509 certificate and private key) of a participant in the Fabric network.

Purpose

Used to sign transactions, proving the origin and authenticity of requests, and ensuring authorization.

Issuance

Identities are issued by a Certificate Authority (CA) within the Hyperledger Fabric network.

Key Takeaway

Digital identities, based on X.509 certificates, are fundamental for authentication, authorization, and ensuring trust in a Fabric network.
