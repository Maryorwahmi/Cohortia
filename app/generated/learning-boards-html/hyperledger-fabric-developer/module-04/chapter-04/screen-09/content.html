Private Data Collections: Confidentiality on the Ledger

Managing sensitive data among a subset of organizations.

What are Private Data Collections?

  • A feature to keep certain data confidential among a subset of organizations on a channel.
  • The hash of the private data is stored on the public ledger, maintaining integrity.
  • Ensures data privacy while still leveraging the immutability of the blockchain.
Key Takeaway

PDCs allow for confidential data sharing on a channel, with client applications needing appropriate permissions to interact with them.

Client Interaction & Permissions

  • Client applications invoke chaincode functions that operate on private data, similar to public data.
  • The client identity must have the necessary permissions to access the specific private data collection.
  • Chaincode uses `ctx.Stub.GetPrivateData` and `ctx.Stub.PutPrivateData` to manage private data.
Important Note

While the data is private, the *fact* that a transaction occurred (and its hash) is still recorded on the public ledger, maintaining transparency of activity.
