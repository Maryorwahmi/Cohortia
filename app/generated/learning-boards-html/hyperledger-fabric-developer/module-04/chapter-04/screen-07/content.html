Chaincode Events: Custom Business Logic Notifications

Emitting specific events from your smart contracts.

What are Chaincode Events?

  • Custom events defined and emitted directly by your chaincode.
  • Use `ctx.Stub.SetEvent(eventName, payload)` within chaincode to emit.
  • Client applications subscribe to these specific events to react to business logic.
Key Takeaway

Chaincode events enable powerful, custom notifications from smart contracts, allowing client applications to react to specific business outcomes.

Example Use Cases

  • **Asset Transfer:** Emit "AssetTransferComplete" with asset ID and new owner when an asset changes hands.
  • **Order Processing:** Emit "OrderPlaced" or "OrderStatusUpdate" with order details for supply chain applications.
  • **Custom Notifications:** Alert users or integrate with external systems based on any specific chaincode logic.
Pro-Tip

Design your event payloads carefully to include all necessary data for the client application to react effectively, avoiding unnecessary ledger queries.
