Key Concepts: Production & Security

Essential takeaways for robust and secure client applications.

Private Data Collections (PDC)

A feature allowing a subset of organizations on a channel to keep specific data confidential.

Connection Pooling

Reusing established Gateway connections to improve performance and resource utilization.

Identity Management

Securely storing and managing client identities and private keys, avoiding hardcoding.

Chapter Summary

Implementing private data collections, connection pooling, and robust identity management are crucial for building efficient, secure, and production-ready Hyperledger Fabric client applications.
