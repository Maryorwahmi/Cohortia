The Hyperledger Fabric Chaincode Lifecycle

A governed, multi-step process for deploying smart contracts.

Why the New Lifecycle?
  • Enhanced Governance: Requires explicit agreement from multiple organizations.
  • Transparency: All channel members can see and approve chaincode definitions.
  • Robustness: Reduces the risk of unauthorized or faulty chaincode deployment.
  • Flexibility: Supports more complex deployment scenarios and upgrades.
Key Takeaway

The Fabric v2.x chaincode lifecycle ensures secure, transparent, and collaborative smart contract deployment across the network.

Key Stages of the Lifecycle
1

Package: Bundle chaincode source into a deployable archive.

2

Install: Place the packaged chaincode onto endorsing peers.

3

Approve: Each organization approves a chaincode definition.

4

Commit: The approved definition is committed to the channel, making the chaincode active.

5

Initialize: (Optional) Invoke the Init function if required by the definition.
