The Chaincode Interface: Init and Invoke

Entry Points for Your Chaincode Logic

The Chaincode Interface

All Hyperledger Fabric chaincode must implement the Chaincode interface from the shim package, which defines the contract for smart contracts.

INIT

Init Method

Called once during chaincode instantiation or upgrade. Used for initial ledger setup, default values, or configuration.

INVOKE

Invoke Method

Called for all subsequent transaction proposals. It handles the actual business logic based on the function requested by the client application.

Key Takeaway

Init and Invoke are the two fundamental entry points for your chaincode, managing its lifecycle and transaction processing.
