Core Concept

Chaincode: The Smart Contract of Fabric

Defining the Business Logic for Your Blockchain

Definition

Chaincode is Hyperledger Fabric's implementation of smart contracts, encapsulating the business logic that governs asset creation, transfer, and management on the ledger.

Role in Fabric

It defines the rules and interactions for your decentralized application, ensuring all operations adhere to predefined business logic.

Key Takeaway

Chaincode is the essential layer that brings intelligence and automation to your Hyperledger Fabric blockchain, enforcing rules and managing assets.
