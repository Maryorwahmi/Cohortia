Chapter Summary

Essential Chaincode Concepts

Reinforcing Your Understanding

Init Function

Called once for initial setup during chaincode instantiation or upgrade.

Invoke Function

Called for all subsequent transaction proposals to execute business logic.

PutState(key, value)

A ChaincodeStubInterface method to write a key-value pair to the ledger.

GetState(key)

A ChaincodeStubInterface method to retrieve the value associated with a key.

DelState(key)

A ChaincodeStubInterface method to delete a key-value pair from the ledger.

Summary Takeaway

The `Init` and `Invoke` methods manage the chaincode lifecycle, while `PutState`, `GetState`, and `DelState` provide the essential CRUD operations for ledger interaction.
