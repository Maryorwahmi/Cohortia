Advanced Risk Mitigation

Replay Attacks

Ensure transactions are unique and cannot be replayed to have different effects; leverage Fabric's TxID.

Infinite Loops / Resource Exhaustion

Design efficient, bounded logic to prevent timeouts or excessive resource consumption on peers.

External Dependencies

Avoid external network calls from chaincode to maintain determinism; use transaction proposals or oracles.

Key Takeaway

By addressing replay attacks, resource management, and external dependencies, you can build highly secure and performant Hyperledger Fabric applications.
