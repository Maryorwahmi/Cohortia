Why Rich Queries?

  • •Simple key-value lookups are often insufficient for complex business logic.
  • •Need to query data based on multiple criteria, ranges, or partial matches.
  • •Essential for reporting, analytics, and dynamic application interfaces.
Key Concept

Rich queries allow sophisticated data retrieval from the ledger, moving beyond basic key-value pairs to support complex filtering and analysis, especially with CouchDB.

Key Takeaway

Rich queries are vital for building dynamic and data-driven Hyperledger Fabric applications, enabling complex data filtering and analysis.
