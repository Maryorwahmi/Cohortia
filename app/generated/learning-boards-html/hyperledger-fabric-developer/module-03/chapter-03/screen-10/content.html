Why Finer-Grained Control?

  • •Fabric's channels and PDCs provide network-level access.
  • •Chaincode needs granular control over function execution and data access.
  • •Client Identity (CID) library provides this capability.
CID's Role

CID allows chaincode to inspect the transaction creator's identity (e.g., MSP ID, attributes) to enforce authorization logic directly within the smart contract.

Key Takeaway

Client Identity (CID) is crucial for implementing robust, fine-grained access control within your chaincode, enforcing business rules at the smart contract level.
