Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Environment Art
Modular Asset Principles
Build reusable pieces that assemble cleanly in the editor.
Consistent Scale & Pivots
Design pieces with consistent units and pivot points for snapping and tiling.
Texture Atlasing
Group similar materials into atlases to reduce draw calls while keeping texture quality where it matters most.
Targeted Optimization
Optimize the heaviest objects first: those contribute most to triangle and texture budgets.
Example Task
Given a dense modular city block, identify three assets with the highest triangle counts and create simple LOD versions to cut polygon cost at distance.
Exam Tip

When asked for the most efficient artist solution, prefer reuse, LODs, and atlasing over writing new runtime systems.
