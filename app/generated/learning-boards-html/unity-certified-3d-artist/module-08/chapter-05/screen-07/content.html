PBR Map Roles
Short definitions and how they combine to form believable surfaces.
Albedo
Base color without baked lighting. Keep it flat and neutral to respond correctly to lighting.
Metallic
Grayscale channel: white = metal, black = dielectric. Affects reflection color and intensity.
Smoothness / Roughness
Controls micro-surface reflections; smooth surfaces reflect sharply, rough surfaces scatter light.
Normal Map
Simulates surface detail without geometry by altering per-pixel normals used in lighting.
Example: Polished Metal Plate
Use a high Metallic value and high Smoothness. Provide a neutral albedo and a normal map for fine scratches. AO can add depth in crevices.
Common Mistake

Using albedo textures with baked shadows or highlights makes PBR lighting inconsistent. Replace such textures with neutral albedo maps before testing.

Key Takeaway

PBR relies on clean, purpose-specific maps. Verify each map's color space and channel packing before assigning to materials.
