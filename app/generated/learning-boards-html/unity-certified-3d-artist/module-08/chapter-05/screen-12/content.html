Two-Pass Exam Strategy
A clear pacing method to maximize points under time constraints.
Pass 1

Quickly answer all questions you know without overthinking. Flag the rest.

Pass 2

Return to flagged items, apply domain judgment, allocate time to multiple-selects, and finalize guesses if needed.

Final Review

If time permits, review flagged answers and check for misread keywords like "most efficient" or "least impact."

Practice Drill

Simulate a timed test with a 90-second per-question target and practice making quick, defensible choices.

Exam Day Checklist
  • • Quiet, stable environment
  • • Charged device and reliable connection
  • • Five-minute domain review before starting
