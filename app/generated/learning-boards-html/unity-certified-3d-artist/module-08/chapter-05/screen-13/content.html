Self-Assessment: Domain Mapping
A repeatable activity to prioritize hands-on practice.
Step 1: List Domains
Asset Management; Materials & Shaders; Lighting & Cameras; Environment Art; Animation; Optimization.
Step 2: Map Chapters
For each course chapter, write the primary domain(s) it supports and note overlaps.
Step 3: Score Confidence
Rate yourself 1–5 for each domain. Pick the lowest two to three for focused practice.
Create Practice Plan

Write two hands-on tasks for each weak domain and a measurable success metric, such as "reduce draw calls by 30%" or "set up a PBR material and verify under three lighting conditions."

Reflect

After each practice, note one insight or change in workflow. These reflections build the practical judgment the exam assesses.
