Comparison Pitfalls & Remedies
Over-animation vs Purposeful motion
Over-animation: long, decorative motion that slows the user. Purposeful motion: short, communicative, and consistent with your UI language.
Inconsistency vs Defined language
Inconsistency confuses users. Define standard timings and a small palette of easings and apply them across the project.
Performance vs Profiled effects
Unprofiled particles and shaders can drop frames. Profile, reduce particle counts, and provide lower-detail options.
Accessibility vs Defaults
Provide reduced-motion and low-effect settings to accommodate players sensitive to motion and flashing effects.
Check your work
Do animations communicate a single purpose each? Are timings consistent and under test on target hardware?
