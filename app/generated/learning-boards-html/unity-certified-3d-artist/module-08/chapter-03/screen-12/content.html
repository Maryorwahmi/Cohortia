Integration Triggering Particles
Animator-driven
Create a ClickEffect clip that enables emission at 0s and disables at the end. Transition to ClickEffect via ClickTrigger; it will play in sync with other animated properties.
Use when
You need precise timing tied to other visual changes in the clip.
Script-driven
Call clickParticles.Play() in OnPointerClick for immediate feedback. This is simple and low overhead for one-shot bursts.
Use when
You want minimal latency and simple control from code.
Pro tip: combine both methods by triggering the Animator for visual timing and calling Play for immediate particle onset. Always profile with multiple simultaneous effects to ensure frame-rate stability.
