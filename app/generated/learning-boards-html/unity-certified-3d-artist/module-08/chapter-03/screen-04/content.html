Core Concept Animation Principles for UI
Rules drawn from character animation that guide micro-interaction design.
Principles explained
  • Squash & stretch: adds tactile response and perceived weight to presses.
  • Anticipation: hints an imminent change to prepare attention.
  • Follow-through: small trailing motion that sells realism.
  • Ease curves: control acceleration for natural movement.
Concrete example
Hover to 1.1 in 100ms (ease-out). Press to 0.9 in 50ms then rebound to 1.05 and settle to 1 in 120ms.
Why this matters
Short, purposeful motion makes interfaces feel responsive and guides player attention. It can also increase perceived polish and user confidence in inputs.
Common mistake
Over-animation or long easing that slows interaction; avoid multi-second transitions in UI.
Key takeaway
Use principles to make short, communicative micro-interactions that feel deliberate and fast.
