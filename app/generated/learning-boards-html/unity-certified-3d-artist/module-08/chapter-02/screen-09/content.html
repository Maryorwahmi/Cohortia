High-poly vs Optimized for Icons

High-poly: more surface detail, heavier render cost, often unnecessary for small icons.
Optimized: remove unseen geometry, bake detail into textures, prioritize silhouette and texel density.
Concrete example
Bake high-frequency detail into normal maps and reduce polygon count for small icons; keep UVs focused on visible faces for better texel allocation.
Misconception

Higher polygon counts always improve icons — not true; at small sizes, silhouette and readable shading matter most.
