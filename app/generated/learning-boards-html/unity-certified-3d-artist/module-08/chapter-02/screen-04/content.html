Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Why use 3D assets?
Definition

3D-to-2D Icon Workflow

Use a single 3D source to render consistent 2D images for UI, preserving materials and lighting while enabling automation for many sizes and variants.

Core benefit
Consistency between in-world assets and UI, plus flexible re-rendering for states and resolutions.
Common misconception
People assume 3D rendering is slower than 2D art. With optimized models and batch scripts, it becomes faster and more consistent.
Concrete example
A weapon model is rendered to a 256x256 PNG for inventory and a 64x64 thumbnail for menus, all from the same mesh and material setup.
Key Takeaway

Choose 3D-to-2D when consistency, multiple sizes, and automation outweigh purely hand-painted stylization.
