Why Atlases

Reduce Draw Calls

Pack many small sprites into a single texture so they share a material and can be batched together. Less setPass and fewer draw calls.

Overdraw
Layering many semi-transparent textures causes the GPU to shade the same pixels multiple times. Keep large backgrounds opaque where possible and minimize stacked alpha layers.
Practical Atlas Tips
  • Group icons and small UI elements that appear together into one atlas.
  • Use padding between sprites to avoid bleeding when compressed.
  • Choose compression formats thoughtfully per platform to balance size and sharpness.
Check

Use the Frame Debugger to inspect draw calls and overdraw; iteratively move sprites into atlases until draw calls drop suitably.
