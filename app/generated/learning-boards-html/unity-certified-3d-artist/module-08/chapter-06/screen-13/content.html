Module 8 › Chapter 8.6 › Asset Prep
Sizing

Export images sized for their on-screen pixels. Avoid oversized source textures for small HUD items. Use padding for atlases to prevent bleeding.

Compression & Mipmaps

Choose compression by platform and enable mipmaps for scaled UI only. Test for artifacting and adjust atlas padding.

Readability Tips
  • Use consistent iconography and color palette to lower cognitive load.
  • Prefer higher contrast for text over subtle color differences for accessibility.
  • Apply small outline or drop shadow to text for separation from busy backgrounds.
Try This

Compare a HUD icon at 64, 128, and 256 pixels in the Game view; pick the smallest size that preserves legibility and compress it in an atlas.
