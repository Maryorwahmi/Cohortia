World Space Canvas
UI functions as a 3D object, subject to perspective, occlusion, and transforms.
Billboarding & RectTransform
Rotate toward the camera but keep upright; tune RectTransform size and transform scale for legibility.
Performance & Troubleshooting
Use shared materials, TextMeshPro, and depth offsets; handle occlusion with repositioning or selective drawing.
Chapter takeaway

Place UI into the world with intention: anchor it correctly, make it face the camera, and optimize for performance and clarity.
