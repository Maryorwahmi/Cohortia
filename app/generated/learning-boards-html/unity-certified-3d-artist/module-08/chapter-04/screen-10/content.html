Diagnose & fix z-fighting

Flicker is usually depth precision fighting between two nearby surfaces—introduce separation to fix it.

Depth offset
Move the Canvas slightly away from the model along its normal; even a tiny offset solves many cases.
Adjust camera clipping
Modify near clip plane or use a different canvas mode to avoid precision edge cases near the camera.
Render order
For Screen Space - Camera, adjust plane distance to separate UI from geometry or change render queue for minimal cases.
Scenario & test
If a health bar flickers when the player circles the enemy, nudge the Canvas 0.01 units away from the model and test across camera angles until stable.
Misconception
Assuming large mesh edits are required. Often a small positional fix or render setting change suffices.
Key Takeaway

Test depth separation and use small offsets or render order changes to eliminate flicker without harming immersion.
