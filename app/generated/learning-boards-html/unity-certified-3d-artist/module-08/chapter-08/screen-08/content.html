Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Layers & Additive
Why use layers?
Separate motion concerns: locomotion, upper-body gestures, facial poses. Layers with masks let you target bones precisely and avoid conflict.
Additive mode
In additive mode, a layer applies offsets to the base pose instead of replacing it. This is useful for gestures that should blend over movement.
Key Takeaway

Organize independent actions into layers and use Avatar Masks to scope influence, keeping base locomotion stable while overlaying gestures.

Concrete example
Base layer: locomotion with root motion and legs.
Upper layer: additive aiming/waving with an Avatar Mask selecting spine, chest and arms.
Adjust layer weight to blend gesture strength from 0 to 1 during runtime.
Misconception
Putting everything on one layer and hiding complexity with transitions makes debugging difficult. Layers add explicit structure and make behavior predictable.
