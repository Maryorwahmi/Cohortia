Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Retargeting
Humanoid
Standardized bone mapping via Avatar enables retargeting across characters that share humanoid structure. Good for mocap and reused walk cycles.
Generic
Non-standard skeletons require specific animations; Generic clips are not automatically retargetable across different bone layouts.
Key Takeaway

Pick Humanoid when reuse matters and verify Avatar mapping; choose Generic when rig specifics prevent reliable retargeting.

Practical checklist
Set Rig to Humanoid on import and configure Avatar mapping.
Preview clip on the Avatar and examine joint orientation and foot placement.
Adjust root motion or offsets if the retargeted pose shows translation or rotation errors.
Misconception
Retargeting does not magically fix differences in proportion or scale. You must still validate and sometimes tweak animations after retargeting.
