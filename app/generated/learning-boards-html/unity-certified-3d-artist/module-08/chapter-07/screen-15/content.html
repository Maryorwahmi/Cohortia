Practical Rules
Bake Static Light
Use lightmaps for scenes that don't require moving lights to gain quality and runtime efficiency.
Use Probes
Place Light and Reflection Probes so dynamic objects and PBR materials match the baked environment.
Reduce Draw Calls
Share materials, static batch, and atlas textures for repeated small assets to cut draw calls.
Final prompt
Before your next build, pick one optimization to test and measure on target hardware. Small, measured steps produce the most reliable results.
