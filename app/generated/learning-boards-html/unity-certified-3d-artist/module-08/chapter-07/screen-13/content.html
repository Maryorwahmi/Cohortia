Common Mistake Optimization Trade-offs
Plan early, measure often
Trade-offs to consider
  • Bake time vs lightmap quality: iterate with low-res bakes during work.
  • Texture size vs memory: compress and test on target devices.
  • LOD complexity vs visual popping: smooth transitions and test movement speeds.
Testing checklist
Measure draw calls and frame time, test on devices, visualize LOD and occlusion, and compare before/after metrics to validate benefits.
Mistake: optimizing without measurement may move cost to a different subsystem instead of reducing it overall.
Key Takeaway

Optimization decisions should be driven by measurement and player perception; perform small iterative changes and validate on target hardware.
