Definition Composition & Modularity
Compose purposeful levels with reusable pieces
Composition Principles
  • Rule of thirds and focal points help prioritize what the player sees first.
  • Leading lines and contrast guide movement and attention.
  • Place visual anchors to support navigation and storytelling.
Modular Asset Workflow
Create reusable pieces with consistent pivot points and UV scale. Combine pieces in different arrangements to build varied spaces without many unique materials.
Tip: design modular pieces for batching by sharing materials and avoiding unique textures per piece.
Key Takeaway

Good composition and modular design let you scale visual fidelity while keeping materials and draw calls manageable.
