Core Concept Mesh & Texture Optimization
Efficient geometry and texture use
Mesh Optimization
  • Reduce polys where silhouette is preserved.
  • Use LODs for distant objects.
  • Avoid unnecessary sub-meshes that create separate materials.
Example: background furniture can be much simpler than nearby interactive props.
Texture Optimization
  • Pick resolution by perceived size in game and target platform memory.
  • Use compression formats suitable to platform and enable mip maps.
  • Pack textures into atlases when it reduces material count without harming quality.
Mistake: high-res textures on tiny objects waste bandwidth and memory.
Key Takeaway

Optimize where players look: reduce asset complexity outside focus and test changes on target hardware to validate quality and savings.
