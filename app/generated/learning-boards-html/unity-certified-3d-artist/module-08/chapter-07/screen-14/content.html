What to Remember
Key Concepts
Lighting Strategy
Bake static, use real-time for motion, and probes for dynamic objects.
Modular Assets
Reusable pieces with shared materials support batching and scale environments efficiently.
Measure & Iterate
Use LODs, occlusion, and atlases and test on target hardware to balance quality and performance.
Summary
Apply these principles together: lighting choices affect probes and baking, modular design affects batching, and optimizations must be validated on device.
