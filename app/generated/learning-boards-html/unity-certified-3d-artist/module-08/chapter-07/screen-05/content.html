Definition Light Types
Directional, Point, Spot, Area & Probes
Directional Light
Simulates distant sun; good for global illumination and outdoor scenes.
Point Light
Omnidirectional bulb; ideal for lamps and local illumination.
Spot Light
Cone-shaped light for flashlights and stage effects; good for focused highlights.
Area Light
Rectangular soft fill used for photographic softboxes; often baked or approximated due to cost.
Reflection & Light Probes
Probes sample baked lighting and environment reflections so dynamic objects and PBR materials match the scene.
Mistake to avoid: forgetting probes for moving characters, which makes them look unlit in a baked scene.
Key Takeaway

Match light type to the artistic need: directional for sun, point for bulbs, spot for cones, area for soft fills; add probes to integrate dynamic objects.
