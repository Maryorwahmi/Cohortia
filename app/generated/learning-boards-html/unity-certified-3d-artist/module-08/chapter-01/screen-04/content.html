Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › What Is Visual Feedback?
Definition & purpose

Visual feedback is an immediate, perceivable response to action or state. It guides attention and confirms results, improving player confidence and control.

Role
Communicate effect, location, and severity without text where possible.
Example
Damage: red vignette, material tint, and small particle impact for clarity.
Misconception
More effects are not always better; they can distract or obscure gameplay.
Key Takeaway

Design feedback to be perceivable, meaningful, and consistent; use layered cues rather than a single signal.

Annotated example: Character takes damage
V
Screen-space vignette
Global severity cue that doesn’t hide the scene but signals impact.
M
Material tint on model
Ties feedback to the character, reinforcing who is affected.
P
Localized particle effect
Clarifies impact point and adds visual satisfaction.
Check your understanding
If a player misses the hit indicator, which layer would you adjust first? Try increasing particle contrast or brief material tint duration before raising vignette intensity.
