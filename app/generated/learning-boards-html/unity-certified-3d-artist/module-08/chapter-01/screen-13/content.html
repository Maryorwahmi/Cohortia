Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Key Concepts
Visual Feedback
Immediate responses that make player actions and state understandable.
Feedback Types
Screen-space, world-space, and particles; each has distinct roles and trade-offs.
Accessibility & Consistency
Provide options for intensity and avoid color-only cues so all players understand feedback.
Summary
Design predictable, layered, and safe feedback that supports gameplay and is easy to test and iterate.
