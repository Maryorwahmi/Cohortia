Hands-on tasks
  1. Create a simple collectible object and assign a renderer.
  2. Author an emissive highlight material and a short particle prefab.
  3. Write a trigger script that instantiates the effect, swaps the material, and disables the object.
  4. Tune timing and intensity; test with reduced-motion and colorblind presets.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Why these tasks matter

They link artistry with gameplay: your assets become part of the game’s communication system. The tasks ensure your effects are readable, performant, and aligned with player expectations.

Check
If a player misses a pickup, the feedback is too subtle. If the screen blinks violently, it’s too intense. Aim for clear, quick signals.
