Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Common Mistakes
Mistake: Afterthought design

Design feedback alongside mechanics so visuals answer player questions the moment they occur. Align timing and role with gameplay needs.

Fix
Prototype feedback early and test it with real interactions, not just as a decorative polish pass.
Mistake: Inconsistency and intensity

Use a consistent visual language: map colors and motion to meanings. Avoid extremes that create discomfort or confusion.

Fix
Create a short guide: colors for categories, thresholds for intensity, and allowed particle budgets.
Key Takeaway

Treat feedback as a design requirement, keep a consistent language, tune intensity, and test accessibility and performance early.
