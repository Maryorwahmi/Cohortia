Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Accessibility
Safety checklist
  • Avoid rapid flashing lights and excessive strobe effects.
  • Provide toggles for shake and particle intensity.
  • Ensure critical feedback uses shape or motion in addition to color.
Concrete example
For pickups, add a shape change such as a short scale pulse or icon overlay in addition to color. Offer a setting to reduce particle density for players who request it.
Key Takeaway
Accessibility improves clarity and makes your work usable by more players. Test with real users whenever possible.
