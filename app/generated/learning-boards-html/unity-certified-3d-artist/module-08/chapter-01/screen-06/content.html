Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Layering Feedback
Step-by-step
  1. Material tint: brief color or flash on the affected model to indicate the actor.
  2. Localized particles: short burst at impact to show exact location and add tactile weight.
  3. Screen-space vignette: subtle global cue for severity only when needed.
Tuning
Start subtle. Increase only the layer that needs emphasis. Avoid simultaneous intensity increases across all layers.
Example scenarios
Minor hit
Short material flash and tiny particle burst; no vignette.
Critical hit
Longer tint, larger particle burst, and a mild vignette for clarity.
Key Takeaway

Layer selectively: let one cue carry the focus while others support the message.
