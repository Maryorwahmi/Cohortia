Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Testing & Iteration
Test checklist
  • Single-event clarity test: does a player notice the feedback once?
  • A/B test: no feedback vs minimal vs layered.
  • Performance stress: spawn many feedbacks to confirm pooling.
  • Accessibility check: reduced-motion and colorblind palettes.
Assessment idea

Use the provided multiple choice questions to assess design judgment about damage and interactable cues. Discuss why the correct options balance clarity and restraint.

Key Takeaway
Fast, focused tests reveal whether your visual feedback communicates. Iterate on the smallest failing layer first.
