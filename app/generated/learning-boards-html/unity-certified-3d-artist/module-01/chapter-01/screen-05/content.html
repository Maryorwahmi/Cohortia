Step-by-step camera drill
  1. Pan: Hold middle mouse and drag to shift the view parallel to screen.
  2. Zoom: Use scroll wheel; for fine control, focus first, then zoom.
  3. Orbit: Hold right mouse and move to rotate around pivot.
  4. Fly: Hold right mouse and use W/A/S/D + Q/E to move through scene.
  5. Focus: Press F on a selected object to center pivot and improve orbit precision.
Key Takeaway

Sequence your movements: focus first, orbit slowly, then pan and fine-tune to avoid overshooting small details.

Example Drill

Select a flickering lamp model, press F, orbit to inspect the bulb's rim, fly in with W while holding right mouse to position, then use middle-mouse pan to align against the pole. Repeat until the silhouette is clean from multiple angles.

Common mistake
Zooming aggressively without focusing can change the pivot and make rotations feel slippery.
Pro tip
Set a comfortable mouse sensitivity and practice slow orbits for checking normals and texture seams.
