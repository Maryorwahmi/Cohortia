Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Scene View — Your 3D canvas
Definition

The Scene view is the editable, real-time 3D viewport used for arranging and manipulating GameObjects and visual elements.

Focus (F) to center selected object and reset orbit pivot.
Orbit with right-click; pan with middle mouse; zoom with mouse wheel for quick inspection.
Combine right-click with W/A/S/D and Q/E to fly through large scenes precisely.
Key Takeaway

Make a habit of focusing small objects and using fly controls to inspect for shading and alignment issues.

Practical Example

Select a prop, press F to focus, then orbit to check normals and silhouette from multiple angles. Adjust position and rotation until the composition reads clearly at the camera distance you expect the player to see.

Common mistake
Relying only on Game view for composition; you lose access to editing gizmos and fine placement control.
Pro tip
Use F frequently and orbit slowly to spot texture seams or incorrect pivot placement before you bake lighting.
