Best practices
  • Create top-level folders: Models, Textures, Materials, Scenes, Prefabs.
  • Place imports immediately in the appropriate folder to avoid clutter.
  • Use descriptive and consistent naming conventions for assets and folders.
Why it matters

Clear structure reduces duplicate imports and saves time when assembling scenes or collaborating with others.

Example organization
Models/Rocks/LowPoly/Rock_A.fbx
Textures/Rocks/Rock_A_Diffuse.png
Materials/Rocks/Rock_A_Mat
Common mistake
Dropping all imports in the project root and relying on search later; this becomes unmanageable quickly.
Pro tip
Create a simple README in top folders documenting naming conventions and LOD rules for your team.
