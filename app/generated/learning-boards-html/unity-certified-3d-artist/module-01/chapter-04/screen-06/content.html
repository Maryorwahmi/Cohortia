PBR Principles
Physical properties: Metallic and Smoothness define reflection type and clarity.
Energy conservation: surfaces do not reflect more light than they receive, keeping shading believable.
Material separation: diffuse color and reflectivity are controlled independently for realism.
Concrete Example
Compare a painted metal and bare metal. Painted metal has Metallic = 0, Smoothness high, and a visible diffuse color. Bare metal has Metallic = 1 and its color primarily appears in reflections.
