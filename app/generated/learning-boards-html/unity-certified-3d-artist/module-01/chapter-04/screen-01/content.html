Introduction to Materials and Textures
Make your 3D objects respond to light realistically using PBR and texture maps.
In this lesson
Define Materials, learn PBR basics, import texture maps, and create a Standard Shader material. You will apply it to a cube and test metallic and smoothness settings.
Why it matters
Consistent PBR materials make assets look correct under varied lighting, reduce rework, and increase realism in games and scenes.
