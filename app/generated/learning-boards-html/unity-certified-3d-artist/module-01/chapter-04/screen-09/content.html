Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Metallic
Concept
Metallic controls whether reflections come from metal-like behavior or dielectric scattering. Metals generally have no diffuse component and color their reflections.
Practical rule: prefer 0 or 1 for physical accuracy. Use a Metallic map for mixed surfaces like metal screws on a plastic body.
Example
A tool: metallic rivets map to white in a Metallic texture while the handle stays black. This per-pixel control produces correct reflections on rivets and normal diffuse shading on the handle.
Mistake to avoid: mid-range values for primarily metal or non-metal parts cause unrealistic shading. Use masks instead of blended metallic values where physical accuracy is needed.
