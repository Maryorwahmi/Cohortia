What a Normal Map Does
It changes normal vectors per-pixel to fake bumps, dents, and fine detail for lighting without adding geometry.
Always set Texture Type to Normal Map in the Inspector and click Apply to avoid incorrect lighting artifacts.
Concrete Fix Example
If T_Wall_Brick_Normal.png shows odd shading, select the texture, change Texture Type to Normal Map, Apply, and reassign to the material. That usually resolves incorrect bump lighting.
Pro tip: preview normals in your image editor or Unity preview to ensure they look like blue/purple normal maps rather than greyscale images.
