Role of AO
AO multiplies ambient light to darken occluded areas, adding soft contact shadows and depth without dynamic lights.
Import as non-color data or mask. Keep AO separate from Albedo for fine control.
Example
Apply AO to a worn crate to darken seams and corners. This subtle darkness improves material readability and perceived realism.
Misconception: AO is not a lighting solution by itself. Use it to complement direct and baked lighting, not replace them.
