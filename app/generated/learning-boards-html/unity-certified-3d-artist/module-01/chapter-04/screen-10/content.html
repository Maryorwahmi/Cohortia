Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Smoothness vs Roughness
How it works
Smoothness determines the spread of specular highlights. High smoothness produces crisp highlights and reflections; low smoothness produces broad, soft highlights.
If you only have a Roughness map, invert it to produce a Smoothness map or enable invert on import where available.
Example & Pitfall
Polished marble: Smoothness high. Weathered concrete: Smoothness low. Pitfall: plugging roughness directly into a smoothness slot gives the inverse look.
Common Mistake

Do not forget to invert Roughness maps when using a Smoothness channel; check import options or shader settings.
