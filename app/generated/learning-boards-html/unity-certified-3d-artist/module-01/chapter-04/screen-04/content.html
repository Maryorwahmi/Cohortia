Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › What Is a Material?
Definition
A Material is an asset that defines surface appearance and how light interacts with that surface. It contains shader selection and texture slots rather than geometry.
Why this matters
  • Enables reuse across multiple objects for consistent visuals.
  • Separates art iteration from geometry editing.
  • Allows shader-based control of reflections, roughness, and detail.
Key Takeaway

Materials are reusable surface definitions that control shading and texture mapping, not geometry.

Concrete Example
Create M_PBR_Cube in Assets/Art/Materials, assign an Albedo and Normal map, then drag the material onto a cube. The cube's Mesh Renderer uses the material to determine its visible surface.
Reuse: One material, many crates
Iteration: tweak smoothness centrally
Common misconception: Materials do not add vertex positions or polygons. Normal maps fake detail without changing geometry; that is a material effect, not a mesh change.
