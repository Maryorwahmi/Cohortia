What Albedo Does
Albedo defines base colors and patterns. Keep it free of baked lighting and use sRGB color space on import.
Use clean color data for tint and detail.
Avoid baked shadows unless stylized.
Concrete Example
A painted metal panel uses an Albedo with paint chips. The same Albedo on a non-metal will show diffuse color; on a metal it mostly tints reflections.
Mnemonic: Albedo = "appearance color". Store separately from AO and other masks to preserve control.
