Process

Vertex, Grid, Surface Snapping

Choose snapping mode based on the geometry and alignment precision you need. Test with a small selection first.

How to use
  • Vertex Snap: Hold V and drag a vertex to another vertex.
  • Grid Snap: Enable Snap Settings (magnet) and configure grid size.
  • Surface Snap: Hold Ctrl/Cmd + Shift while dragging to snap to surface.
Misconception
Snapping always produces perfect results — not always. Check normals, parent rotations, and mesh density before trusting a snap for final placement.
Key Takeaway

Snap thoughtfully: choose the mode that matches the geometry. Use grid for modular layouts, vertex for tight mesh fits, and surface for uneven placement.

Practice — Align a Lamp to a Rock
// Practice steps

Reflect
Which snap mode gave the cleanest result? If vertex snapping caused penetration, try surface snap or adjust geometry.
