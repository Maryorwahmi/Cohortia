Frequent Mistakes
  • Editing the mesh when you meant to change the GameObject container.
  • Duplicating without renaming or parenting, causing confusion.
  • Using Local mode incorrectly on rotated parents, producing unexpected motion.
  • Ignoring pivot issues and ending up with incorrect rotations.
Practical Habits
  • Name and parent as you create assets.
  • Use Inspector numeric fields for exact values after visual placement.
  • Create empty pivot proxies rather than re-exporting immediately when short on time.
  • Verify snaps by changing camera angles to avoid hidden intersections.
Pro Tip

Adopt naming and grouping as non-optional steps in your scene pipeline. They pay back time in debugability and iteration speed.
