Tool Walkthrough

Move (W), Rotate (E), Scale (R)

Practice keyboard shortcuts and inspector edits together for both speed and exactness.

Visual cues
  • Move: colored arrows; center cube moves on camera plane.
  • Rotate: colored rings around axes; drag to rotate.
  • Scale: cubes on axes; center cube scales uniformly.
Common Mistake
Scaling a child when parent has non-uniform scale can distort children — consider scaling the parent or re-parenting to avoid compounding scales.
Key Takeaway

Learn the gizmo shapes and shortcuts, but use numeric Inspector values when you require exact placement or consistent scaling across parts.

Code-like Steps — Editor Practice
// Practice sequence
Select Table_Group
Press W → drag center cube to place table on floor
Press E → rotate 15 degrees on Y to angle the table
Press R → uniform scale by dragging center cube or set Scale to (1.2,1.2,1.2)
Inspect numeric fields for precise values
Pro tip
Combine a quick gizmo move for visual placement with a precise Inspector edit to lock in exact coordinates or rotation values.
