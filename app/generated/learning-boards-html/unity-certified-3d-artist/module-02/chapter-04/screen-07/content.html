Comparison Tuning LODs
Aggressive vs Conservative
Aggressive: Early swaps, large poly reductions, strong performance gains but risk of popping and silhouette loss.
Conservative: Late swaps, small reductions, less risk to visuals but smaller performance improvement.
Example
A distant rock can be aggressively reduced; a statue used in cutscenes should be conservative.
Practical test
  • Record camera paths and observe swap points.
  • Measure triangle counts and GPU frame time before and after.
  • Tune thresholds where swaps are hidden by camera motion or scene elements.
Misconception
Assuming automated tools set perfect thresholds. Human tuning is usually required for the best result.
