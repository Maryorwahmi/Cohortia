Key Concepts What to Remember
LOD (Level of Detail)
Swap meshes by distance to cut GPU cost for distant objects.
LOD Group
Unity component for assigning LOD slots and screen percentage thresholds.
Occlusion Culling
Baked visibility system that prevents rendering of fully hidden static objects.
Summary
Combine LODs, occlusion, and simplification and measure gains. Prioritize assets by role and iterate on thresholds and bake settings for reliable results.
