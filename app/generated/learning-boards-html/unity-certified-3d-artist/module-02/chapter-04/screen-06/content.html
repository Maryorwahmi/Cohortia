Definition Mesh Simplification
Where to simplify
  • Background props and distant foliage — aggressive reduction is fine.
  • Hero characters and focal props — prefer retopology to preserve form.
  • When creating LODs, use simplified meshes as LOD1/LOD2.
Tools
Blender Decimate Modifier — controlled percentage reduction.
ProBuilder — quick in-Editor optimization and fixes.
Third-party reducers — good for batch processing many assets.
Example and checks
After simplification, inspect silhouette and normals under scene lighting. Also check UVs if texture fidelity matters. If details collapse, back off the reduction or retopologize key regions.
Common mistake
Over-simplifying without reviewing silhouette causes assets to look blocky even at medium distances.
Key Takeaway

Match the simplification method to the asset’s importance: automated decimate for background, retopo for hero assets.
