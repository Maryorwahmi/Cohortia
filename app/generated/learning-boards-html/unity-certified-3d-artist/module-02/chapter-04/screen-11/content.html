Definition Mesh Simplification
What to preserve
  • Silhouette and overall proportions.
  • Major edges and silhouette-defining features.
  • UV layout and shading cues where textures carry detail.
Example
A distant statue loses fine sculpted engraving, but keeps overall pose and outline so it still reads in the scene.
Workflow tips
  • Try automated decimation, then inspect and adjust.
  • Bake normal or detail textures if surface detail is lost.
  • Keep a version-controlled copy of the original high-poly mesh.
Common mistake
Assuming the same reduction percentage works for every asset. Each model needs evaluation against its viewing distance and role.
