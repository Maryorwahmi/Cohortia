Hands-on Activity Create LODs & Bake Occlusion
Steps
  1. Import a complex model and duplicate it twice for high/mid/low versions.
  2. Simplify duplicates in your DCC or with ProBuilder for mid/low meshes.
  3. Place high-poly in Scene and Add Component → LOD Group.
  4. Assign renderers to LOD slots and tune Screen Percentage sliders.
  5. Build a simple room, mark walls Occluder Static, props Occludee Static.
  6. Open Occlusion Culling window, set parameters, and Bake. Visualize results.
Example checks
  • Move camera closer and farther to observe LOD swaps.
  • Enter Play Mode and verify occluded objects are not rendered.
  • Adjust thresholds and re-bake as necessary.
Key Takeaway

This lab puts LODs and occlusion together to produce measurable frame-time improvements in a small scene; iterate until visual issues are resolved.
