Comparison DCC vs Unity
DCC Tools
  • Fine control over retopology and UVs.
  • Best for hero assets and characters.
  • Allows manual correction after decimation.
Unity & Asset Store
  • Fast in-Editor adjustments with ProBuilder.
  • Third-party tools can batch-process many assets quickly.
  • Good for level-building and tuning iteration speed.
Example guideline
Automate background prop simplification with fast tools. Reserve DCC retopology for characters and hero props where deformation, shading, and silhouette fidelity are critical.
