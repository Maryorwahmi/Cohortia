Strategy Combining Techniques
Asset prioritization
  • Hero assets: manual retopo, multiple LODs, careful thresholds.
  • Repeated props: automated simplification and 2–3 LODs.
  • Background scenery: aggressive reduction and fewer LODs.
  • Large static architecture: mark as occluders for occlusion baking.
Example plan
Statue: 3 LODs with retopo. Trees: 2 LODs, billboards at far distances. Indoor furniture: static occludees and simplified models.
Measure and iterate
  • Use the Profiler to compare draw calls and frame time before and after changes.
  • Iterate thresholds and bake parameters until performance goals are met.
  • Document decisions so team members apply the same rules consistently.
Key Takeaway

Optimization is a targeted, measurable process. Prioritize assets, apply the right tools, and confirm gains with profiling data.
