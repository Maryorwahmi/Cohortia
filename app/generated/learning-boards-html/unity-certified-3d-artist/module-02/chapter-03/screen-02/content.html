How to use these objectives

Work through the hands-on activity: import a model with Albedo and Normal textures, extract assets, and adjust import settings. Use the milestones to verify editing ability and visual correctness.

Mnemonic
Think: Create, Extract, Configure, Test — a short workflow to follow on every import.
Quick Tip
When in doubt, extract materials to make edits without re-exporting from your DCC tool.
Validation
Apply the material to a simple sphere and view lighting to check normal and sRGB settings.
Common Pitfall
Incorrect texture type or sRGB will cause wrong lighting or color shifts.
