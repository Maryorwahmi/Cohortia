Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › What are Materials and Textures?
Definition

Materials vs Textures

A material holds shader properties and links to textures. Textures are images that supply color or data to those properties.

Albedo (Color)
Represents the base color of the surface and should use sRGB.
Normal Map
Stores surface normals as vectors and must be treated as a data texture with sRGB disabled.
Concrete Example
Imagine a wooden crate: the Albedo map paints the wood grain color, the Normal map fakes deep grain and scratches, and the Metallic/smoothness maps control how shiny parts of the crate appear. No extra polygons are needed to show dents or grain when the normal map is correct.
Key Takeaway
Treat textures by role: color vs data. This preserves lighting and color accuracy.
Common Mistake
Setting a normal map as a color texture causes flat, incorrect lighting and broken surface detail.
