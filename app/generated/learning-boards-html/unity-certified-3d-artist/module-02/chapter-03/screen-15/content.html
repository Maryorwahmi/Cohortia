Mip Maps
Precomputed levels to reduce shimmering and GPU bandwidth for distant objects.
Compression
Use platform-appropriate formats (DXT, PVRTC, ETC, ASTC) and test for artifacts.
Sampling & Tiling
Wrap and Filter modes control tiling and scaling; Aniso improves angle clarity at a cost.
Final Takeaway

Adopt a repeatable import workflow: extract assets when you need to edit, set texture roles correctly, enable mips for 3D, choose compression by platform, and validate visually.
