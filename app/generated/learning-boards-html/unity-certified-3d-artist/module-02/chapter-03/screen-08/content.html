Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Normal Maps: Data Textures and sRGB
Definition

Normal Map = vector data

Normal maps encode x/y/z direction per pixel. They are not colors and must avoid sRGB conversion to remain numerically correct.

Quick Rule
Texture Type: Normal Map. sRGB: Disabled. This preserves lighting calculations.
Concrete Example
If a terrain tile looks flat after import, inspect the normal map settings. If you see sRGB enabled or Texture Type set to Default, switch to Normal Map and disable sRGB, then click Apply to restore correct surface detail.
Check
Apply the texture to a material and move lights around to confirm the normal detail responds as expected.
Misconception
Treating normal maps as colors will cause incorrect shading; they must be linear data.
