Process Flow
Nested Prefabs
Compose assemblies by nesting reusable Prefab parts to allow updates at the part level to affect all assemblies that use it.
Atomic Parts
Create small Prefabs for parts you will reuse independently.
Compose
Nest parts into larger Prefabs for final assets.
Update
Change a part and see updates propagate across assemblies.
Watch Outs
Avoid overly deep nesting which complicates hierarchy navigation.
Example
Car_Prefab
Contains multiple Wheel_Prefabs; Wheel_Prefab contains Tire_Prefab and Rim_Prefab. Update Tire_Prefab to affect all wheels and cars that use it.
Why it matters

Nested Prefabs let teams reuse and maintain parts independently, keeping projects modular and efficient.
