What to Remember
Three compact reminders to keep your Prefab workflow healthy.
Configure First
Always set up components and children in scene before creating the Prefab asset to capture the desired configuration.
Manage Overrides
Check bolded properties and choose Apply or Revert rather than assuming editor will auto-save the right choice.
Use Variants & Nesting
Prefer variants for family differences and nesting for modular composition versus duplicating assets.
Final Tip

Treat Prefab changes as deliberate, save and commit, and maintain clear naming so your project scales smoothly.
