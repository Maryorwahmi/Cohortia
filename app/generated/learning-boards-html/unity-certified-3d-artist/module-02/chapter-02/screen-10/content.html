Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Generate Lightmap UVs
Why UV2 matters
Lightmaps require a second UV channel with non-overlapping islands for baked lighting accuracy.
Author UV2 for complex models in your DCC for best control.
Use Unity's Generate Lightmap UVs when you need a quick automatic UV2 for simpler geometry.
If UV2 is missing, static objects will not bake lighting correctly and may look flat.
Practical checklist
  • Mark static objects that will receive baked lighting.
  • Confirm UV2 exists or enable Generate Lightmap UVs in the Model tab.
  • For large or complex assets, create UV2 in the DCC for predictable packing and texel density.
Misconception
Unity's generator is not a replacement for thoughtfully authored UV layouts when quality and texel density matter.
Key Takeaway

Ensure a proper UV2 exists for any static object you expect to bake; prefer authored UVs for control.
