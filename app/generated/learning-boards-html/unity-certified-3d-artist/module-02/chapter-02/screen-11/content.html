Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Preserve Hierarchy & Import Options
Top: When to preserve
Preserve Hierarchy is useful when exported parent-child relationships affect animation or transform logic.
Use Preserve Hierarchy for grouped modular props or imported rigs you will animate.
Avoid importing cameras and lights unless you need them to replicate a DCC preview setup.
Bottom: Practical cleanup
If you imported extra cameras or lights by accident, remove them and recreate Unity-native versions to maintain scene clarity and performance.
Scenario
An exported demo scene included 5 DCC cameras. Rather than importing them, recreate a clean Unity camera setup to control gameplay framing.
Misconception
Keeping everything from the DCC scene saves time — it often creates clutter and hidden dependencies.
