Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › What to Remember
Units first
Keep DCC and Unity units aligned so Scale Factor stays at 1 when possible.
Performance defaults
Disable Read/Write unless needed, prefer primitive colliders for moving objects, compress environment meshes first.
Lighting readiness
Ensure UV2 exists or enable Generate Lightmap UVs for static assets that will be baked.
Final takeaway

A small, repeatable checklist prevents most import errors: check units, choose compression and Read/Write deliberately, and verify UVs for light baking.
