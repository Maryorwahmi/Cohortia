Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Frustum vs Occlusion
Frustum culling

Automatic

Skips objects fully outside the camera volume. No bake required. Cheap per-frame test.

Good for open scenes and moving cameras.
Occlusion culling

Editor-baked

Precomputes which cells and objects can be hidden by static occluders. Requires marking and baking. Great for indoor scenes.

Good for rooms, corridors, urban blocks with many hidden props.
