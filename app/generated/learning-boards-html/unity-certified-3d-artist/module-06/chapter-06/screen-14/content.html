Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Key Concepts
Frustum Culling
Automatically skips objects outside the camera volume.
Occlusion Culling
Baked optimization that hides objects blocked by static occluders.
Visualization
Use the Occlusion Culling window and Draw Modes to check what the bake recorded.
Takeaway

Focus on static, opaque occluders for successful bakes and use visualization to iterate quickly.
