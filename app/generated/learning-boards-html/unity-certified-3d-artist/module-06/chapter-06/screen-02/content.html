Learning Objectives

Tasks you can perform after this lesson
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain frustum vs occlusion
Define the camera frustum and why frustum culling alone misses objects blocked by opaque geometry. Verified competency milestone.
02
Mark occluders and occludees
Correctly set Static flags for walls, floors and props to ensure accurate baked data. Verified competency milestone.
03
Bake and tune settings
Adjust smallest occluder, smallest hole and distances to balance precision and bake performance. Verified competency milestone.
04
Visualize and debug
Use the Occlusion Culling window and Draw Modes to validate occlusion cells and correct misconfigurations. Verified competency milestone.
