Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Tuning Strategy
Follow this iteration timeline when tuning bake settings for real projects.
Step 1
Quick representative bake with moderate thresholds.
Step 2
Visualize problem areas and identify missed culling.
Step 3
Adjust thresholds only where needed, rebake those areas.
Key Takeaway

Target your precision where it yields meaningful runtime gains and avoid global extremes that slow iteration and bloat data files.
