Key Concepts
Three compact reminders.
Profiling
Measure runtime performance to identify bottlenecks and guide artist optimizations.
Unity Profiler
The tool that surfaces CPU, GPU, Memory and Rendering timelines for live analysis.
CPU-bound
When CPU time dominates, often caused by many draw calls; fix with batching and material sharing.
Summary Takeaway

Read profiler signals, map them to artist causes, apply the targeted fix, and re-profile to confirm improvement.
