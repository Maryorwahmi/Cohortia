Profiler process
A repeatable observe-isolate-change-measure loop.
1
Start and target hardware
Profile on the device you ship to; Editor numbers can mask mobile or VR constraints.
2
Observe timeline spikes
Look for spikes in CPU or GPU graphs and open the relevant sections to see detailed entries.
3
Drill down to Camera.Render
Camera.Render entries like DrawMeshes or Render.OpaqueGeometry indicate draw-call and geometry costs.
4
Change one thing and re-profile
Make a single artist change — mark static, consolidate materials, lower particle count — then re-run to measure effect.
Quick check
If Draw Calls are high and Camera.Render dominates CPU time, prioritize material sharing and batching before changing shaders.
Profiler visual
Misstep to avoid: changing multiple art settings between profiles — you must isolate variables to know what helped.
