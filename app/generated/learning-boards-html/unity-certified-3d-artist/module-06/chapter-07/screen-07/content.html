Overdraw and pixel cost
Pixels rendered multiple times waste GPU cycles; transparency is a common cause.
How to spot it
Enable Overdraw in Scene view; bright white areas indicate heavy overdraw. Check GPU spikes in Profiler.
Example
Many transparent spheres stacked to simulate haze will produce bright white areas and GPU spikes; reduce sphere count or change shader.
Artist fixes
  • Reduce transparent layers and particle counts.
  • Use cheaper shader passes or alpha testing.
  • Order rendering: opaque first, then limited transparent elements.
Common Mistake

Treating frequent transparent effects as inexpensive. Transparency should be budgeted and optimized like other visual features.

Visual Cue
Try replacing overlapping transparent spheres with a single optimized billboard or a volumetric trick that draws fewer pixels.
