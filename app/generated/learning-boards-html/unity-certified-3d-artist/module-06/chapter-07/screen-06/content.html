Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › CPU vs GPU
CPU-bound indicators
High Main Thread time, Camera.Render dominated, many Draw Calls.
Cause
Inefficient material use, many unique materials, lack of batching.
Artist fixes
Static batching, material consolidation, texture atlases, GPU instancing.
Key Takeaway

If Camera.Render and DrawMeshes dominate CPU time, prioritize batching and material sharing.

GPU-bound indicators
High GPU time, lots of triangles, and bright Overdraw visuals.
Cause
High polygon counts, complex multi-pass shaders, and overdraw from transparent layers.
Artist fixes
LOD Groups, mesh decimation, simpler shaders, reduce overlapping transparent geometry and particle counts.
Misconception: believing texture downscaling automatically fixes GPU-bound rendering costs. It may reduce memory pressure but not triangle count or shader passes.
