Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › High Polygon Counts
When to reduce meshes
If Total Triangles drive GPU time, create LODs and simplify meshes.
Identify expensive models
Use the Profiler and Rendering stats to find which models contribute most to triangles and GPU time.
LOD Groups
Assign multiple mesh LODs to swap complexity with distance and preserve silhouette with fewer triangles.
Mesh decimation & cleanup
Use decimation tools and remove unseen geometry such as interior faces or backfaces of closed models.
Key Takeaway

Balance visual fidelity and triangle budgets with LODs and careful decimation for target hardware.

Example steps
  1. Profile and note high triangle assets.
  2. Create LODs with progressively reduced meshes.
  3. Re-profile and compare GPU time and triangle counts.
Misconception: always decimating everything. Instead focus on models that appear often or close to the camera.
