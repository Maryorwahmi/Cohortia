More Key Concepts
Three practical reminders to use during profiling.
GPU-bound
When GPU time is high, reduce triangles, shaders complexity, and overdraw.
Overdraw
Visualize with Scene view Overdraw to find pixel re-draw hotspots.
Rendering Stats
Keep Batches, Draw Calls, and Triangles in view and compare them to platform budgets.
Final Takeaway

Profiling is an artist's tool: use data, not guesswork, to preserve visual quality while meeting performance goals.
