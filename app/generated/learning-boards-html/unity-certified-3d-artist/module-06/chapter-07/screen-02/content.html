Learning Objectives
Skills to practice and why they matter.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Understand Profiling Importance
Explain why profiling is essential for artists and how it prevents visual decisions from causing crashes or poor frame rates.
Mnemonic: magnifying glass → root cause.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use Unity's Profiler Panels
Open and read CPU, GPU, and Rendering timelines to spot spikes and relevant sections like Main Thread and Render.OpaqueGeometry.
Mnemonic: timeline bars → hotspots.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify Artistic Culprits
Recognize overdraw, excessive draw calls, and high polygon counts as common artist-driven performance issues.
Mnemonic: red alert icons → hotspots.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Interpret Rendering Stats
Read Batches, Draw Calls, and Total Triangles and compare them to your target platform budget to prioritize fixes.
Mnemonic: ledger → budget.
