Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Rendering Stats
Batches • Draw Calls • Triangles
Use these numbers to choose the right artist optimizations for your target platform.
Batches
Number of grouped draw operations actually sent. Lower is generally better for CPU.
If low vs draw calls → batching works
Draw Calls
CPU-issued commands for drawing objects. High counts often mean many unique materials or unbatched objects.
If high → consolidate materials
Total Triangles
Aggregate geometry complexity. High triangle counts usually increase GPU time and fillrate cost.
If high → add LODs
Platform budgets
Compare numbers to your target: mobile budgets are far lower than high-end PC. Use that comparison to prioritize reductions.
Misconception
Focusing on triangles alone can miss CPU costs from many small unique materials causing many draw calls.
