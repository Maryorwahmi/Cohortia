Practice diagnosing profiler scenarios
Match the profiler symptom to the correct artist fix and metric to re-check.
Scenario A
Main Thread spikes, Camera.Render heavy, Draw Calls high and close to Batches. What do you do?
Answer: Consolidate materials, use atlases, enable static batching or GPU instancing; re-check Draw Calls and Main Thread time.
Scenario B
GPU spikes and Overdraw visuals bright white. What do you do?
Answer: Reduce transparent objects, simplify particle systems or shaders; re-check GPU time and Overdraw view.
Scenario C
Total Triangles skyrocket when a model appears. What do you do?
Answer: Add LODs or decimate the model; re-check Total Triangles and GPU time.
Assessment Tip

When justifying your fix, name the profiler entry you used to decide and the exact metric you will compare after the change.
