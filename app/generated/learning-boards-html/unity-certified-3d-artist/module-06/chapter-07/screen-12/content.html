Optimization workflow
A disciplined, repeatable loop to measure real impact.
1. Profile
On the actual hardware target.
2. Identify
CPU or GPU bottleneck and offending entries.
3. Prioritize
Pick the largest, most cost-effective fix first.
4. Change & Measure
Make one change, re-profile, and compare graphs.
Pro tip
Keep a short log of each optimization attempt and its measured effect so you learn what works for your content type.
Reflection: which optimization in your current project would you test first using this loop?
