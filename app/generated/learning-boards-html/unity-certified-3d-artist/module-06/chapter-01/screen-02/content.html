Learning Objectives

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master

01
Apply Rule of Thirds

Use the grid to place subjects on intersections to create balance and energy. This helps avoid static center framing and makes landscapes feel natural.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Design Leading Lines

Arrange terrain, props, or light to guide the eye toward your hero object and deepen perceived scale. Leading lines create movement and narrative flow.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Establish a Focal Point

Create contrast with light, color, or scale so one object reads as the primary subject. This directs player attention immediately.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Use Unity Camera Tools

Evaluate composition in Game view, tweak field of view, and use temporary overlays or screenshots to iterate quickly and catch tangents.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
