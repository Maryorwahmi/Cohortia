Process Flow

Designing Leading Lines

Use lines to create movement and pull focus toward the subject. Prototype with simple shapes, then refine materials and lighting.

Step 1 — Define the focal point

Decide the primary subject and where you want the viewer's eye to end.

Step 2 — Place line elements

Add paths, rows of props, or light shafts that direct flow toward the focal area.

Step 3 — Test in Game view

Check the camera, adjust spacing, and reduce any element that competes with the focal point.

Visual example
Prototype: path of rocks
Concrete example
A winding stone path that converges at a lit shrine creates a natural travel direction and builds depth.
Misstep
Avoid making lines that overshadow the focal object; reduce contrast or brightness if needed.
