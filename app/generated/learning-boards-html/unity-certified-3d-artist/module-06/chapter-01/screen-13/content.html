Assessment Idea

Sample Questions & Fixes

Recognition question
Which principles are used when an off‑center ruin sits against a wide empty sky? (Expect: Rule of Thirds + Negative Space)
Practical fix
Given an image with a tangent, explain and implement how a small camera rotation or object nudge removes the tangent and why that improves silhouette clarity.
Hierarchy task
Improve an entrance's visual priority by changing scale, lighting, or saturation and document the before/after screenshots.
Evaluation rubric
Evidence
Require screenshots demonstrating clear improvement and a short rationale for each change.
Reasoning
Good answers explain why the chosen adjustment affects readability or narrative priority.
Common error
Students sometimes change style without measuring read‑ability; require side-by-side comparison to avoid this.
