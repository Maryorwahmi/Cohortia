Definition

Tangents & Cropping Issues

These are perspective problems caused by camera alignment and framing that confuse silhouettes or cut important details.

Tangent example

A tree trunk aligned with a character's head appears as a single merged silhouette.

Cropping example

A character's hands are cut off by the frame edge, making the pose read awkwardly.

Quick fixes

Slightly rotate or move the camera, or nudge objects in depth to separate silhouettes; adjust FOV for better fit.

Practice
Try this sequence in Unity: 1) Frame your subject in Game view. 2) Look for any silhouettes that accidentally align. 3) Nudge the camera rotation by a few degrees. 4) If still problematic, move the subject slightly in depth. 5) Re-check the composition.
Concrete fix
Often a 0.5 to 2 degree camera rotation or a 0.5 to 1 meter object shift is enough to remove a tangent.
Misconception
Tangents are not usually solved by retexturing; they are solved by spatial adjustments.
