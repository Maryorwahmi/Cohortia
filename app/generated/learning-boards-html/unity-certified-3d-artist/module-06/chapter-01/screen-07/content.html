Focal Point Techniques

Contrast, scale and light each create attention in different ways; combining them is powerful.

Contrast

Color or material differences make an object pop against its environment.

Scale

Changing relative size signals importance and can define focal order across the frame.

Lighting

Spotlights, rim light or fog can isolate and emphasize the subject without altering composition.

Concrete example
Example: lit statue with color contrast and larger scale
Why combine methods
Contrast grabs attention while scale and light confirm importance and context.
Misconception
Relying only on color contrast can fail in monochrome scenes; add light or scale where necessary.
