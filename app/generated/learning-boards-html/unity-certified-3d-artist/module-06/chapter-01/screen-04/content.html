Core Concept

Rule of Thirds

Divide your frame into nine zones. Position key elements on lines and intersections for natural balance without static centering.

Why it matters

Off‑center placement tends to feel more cinematic and gives room for leading lines or environmental context.

Common misconception

The rule is a guideline, not a command. Use it to compare frames, not to limit creativity.
