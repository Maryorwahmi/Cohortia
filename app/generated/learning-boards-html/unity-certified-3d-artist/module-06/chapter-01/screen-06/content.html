Definition

Negative Space

Use empty areas to magnify your subject and set emotional tone through contrast and scale.

Example

A solitary ruin against a large, empty sky emphasizes isolation and grandeur.

Practical test

Hide background clutter in your Unity scene to judge whether the subject still reads clearly.

Pitfall

Filling every space removes breathing room and flattens narrative focus.

Visual check
Mock Game view: lone tree on horizon with large sky area
Concrete scenario
Use space to convey scale: a small character in a large empty field will feel isolated or epic depending on context.
Why it matters
Mood and readability are shaped as much by what you omit as by what you include.
