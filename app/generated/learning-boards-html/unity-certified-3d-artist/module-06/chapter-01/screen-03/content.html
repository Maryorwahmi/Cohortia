Learning Objectives

Apply and avoid mistakes while composing scenes.

01
Control Negative Space

Use empty areas to emphasize scale or mood. Negative space can isolate a subject or provide calm around busy details.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Build Visual Hierarchy

Arrange scale, color, detail and lighting so the viewer reads elements in the intended order: primary, secondary, tertiary.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Detect Tangents & Cropping

Spot when objects visually merge or important parts are awkwardly cut, and learn to fix them with camera or object adjustments.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Compose a Hero Shot

Combine grid placement, leading lines, focal contrast, and clean framing to create a single compelling image you can evaluate and iterate on.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
