What to Remember

Three quick, repeatable rules to guide your daily composition work.

Compose in Game View
The Game view shows the player's framing and reveals tangents and cropping accurately.
Enforce Focal Order
Decide primary/secondary/tertiary and adjust scale, light, and detail accordingly.
Iterate with Evidence
Capture screenshots each pass and compare to choose the clearest composition objectively.
Quick Action

Before you move anything, name the hero of the shot. That clarity will guide every composition choice you make.
