Hands-on Activity

Compose a Hero Shot

Follow this workflow to produce a single, evaluable composition in Unity.

1. Block out scene

Place a plane, hero object, and a few props to define foreground and path elements.

2. Frame with camera

Choose FOV and place the hero near a thirds intersection; test both centered and off‑center frames.

3. Add guiding elements

Create leading lines and targeted light to emphasize the hero.

4. Iterate and test

Check Game view screenshots, remove tangents, and unclutter the background until the focal order is clear.

Example starter assets
  • Plane as ground
  • Capsule or cube as hero
  • Cubes/cylinders as props for a path
  • Spotlight to model focal light
Check your understanding
After composing, ask: does the image show primary subject first, and does the eye flow naturally to supporting elements?
Common trap
Avoid adding decorative props that distract from the hero; every prop should support the read.
