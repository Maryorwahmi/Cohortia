Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Common Mistakes
What Breaks Batching
Small differences can prevent objects from batching. Diagnose by simplifying materials and shaders.
Material Differences
Even a different color tint or a cloned material instance stops batching. Use a single shared material or texture atlases to allow visual variety without separate materials.
Shader Features
Vertex displacement, procedural modifications, or shader keywords may break both static and dynamic batching. Prefer instancing-compatible shaders for large instanced sets.
Per-Instance Data & Shadows
Unique per-instance properties set on materials and inconsistent shadow settings can prevent batching. Check shadow caster/receiver flags and property-block usage.
