Key Concepts — Quick Reference
Verify First
Always measure Batches and Draw Calls before and after changes.
Share Materials
Use shared materials and atlases to avoid invisible batching blockers.
Pick the Right Tool
Static for immobile props, instancing for many identical movers, dynamic only for very small moving objects.
Final Prompt

Before you optimize your next scene, pick one small system, apply a single batching change, and confirm the effect with the Stats window.
