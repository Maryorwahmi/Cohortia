Dynamic Batching Overview
Automatic per-frame batching for small moving objects that share the same material.
When It Applies
  • Objects must share the exact same material instance.
  • Vertex count must be low—typically under a few hundred vertices.
  • Shaders must not use features that break batching such as vertex displacement.
Example: a handful of simple moving rocks or simple parts of a particle system could be dynamically batched. But if you have thousands of moving small objects, dynamic batching’s CPU cost may become the bottleneck.
Limitations & Misconceptions
  • Because vertices are transformed on the CPU each frame, dynamic batching can be costly.
  • It is less effective than static batching or GPU instancing for large numbers of objects.
  • Do not assume dynamic batching will scale well for thousands of instances.
Pro Tip

If dynamic batching gives negligible gains, prefer GPU instancing or static batching depending on object movement and sameness.
