Optimization Checklist and Workflow
A measured, stepwise approach prevents wasted effort and unintended regressions.
1. Isolate & Baseline
Remove unrelated systems and record Stats numbers before changes.
2. Change One Thing
Mark Static, or enable instancing on a material; then re-measure.
3. Diagnose Failures
Swap to a simple shared material and shader to find batching blockers.
Example: test Static on 10, 50, 100 objects to balance draw call reduction and combined mesh memory growth. Keep a short test log of counts for reproducibility.
