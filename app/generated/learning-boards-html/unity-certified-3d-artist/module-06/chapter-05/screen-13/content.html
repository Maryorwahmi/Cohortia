Quiz & Common Scenarios
Apply what you've learned with concrete scene scenarios and answers.
Scenario 1 — Static Rocks
200 identical rocks that never move: best choice is static batching. Mark as Static and share material to combine into fewer draw calls.
Answer
Static Batching (C). Consider memory effect of combined meshes.
Scenario 2 — Swaying Grass
Thousands of identical blades that sway: best choice is GPU instancing; share mesh and material with an instancing-enabled shader.
Answer
GPU Instancing (C). Dynamic batching will typically not scale for thousands of moving blades.
