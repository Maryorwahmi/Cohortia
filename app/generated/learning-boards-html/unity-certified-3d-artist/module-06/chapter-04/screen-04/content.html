Core Concept
Mesh Complexity
Triangles and vertices determine vertex processing cost on the GPU.
Explanation
  • Each triangle adds vertex transforms and rasterization work.
  • Distant objects often don’t need high triangle counts.
  • High polycount is justified only when visible and resolving detail.
Concrete example
A decorative statue with 500k triangles looks great up close but can be replaced by a 50k LOD at distance to save GPU time.
Key Takeaway

Measure triangle counts, then reduce polygons where they are not visible.

Common misconception
More polygons always equal better visuals. In reality, careful placement of polygons and normal maps often provides equivalent appearance at far lower cost.
