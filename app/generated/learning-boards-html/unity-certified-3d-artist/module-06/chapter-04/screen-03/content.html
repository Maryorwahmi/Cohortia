Practical Application
These tasks form the lab walkthrough: inspect, LOD, atlas, and validate in Unity.
Task 1 — Inspect models
Record triangle counts and identify faces hidden from view. Decide which assets need retopology or decimation.
Task 2 — Create LODs
Prepare LOD0, LOD1 and LOD2. Keep UV layouts consistent if you will share textures across LODs.
Task 3 — Atlas and share
Combine small textures into one atlas, create a SharedProps material, and apply it to multiple props.
Task 4 — Validate
Use Unity's Stats or Profiler to verify draw call and triangle count reductions after changes.
