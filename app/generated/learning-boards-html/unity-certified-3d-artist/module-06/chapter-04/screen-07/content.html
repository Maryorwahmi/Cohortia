Comparison
Threshold strategies
Conservative thresholds
Keep higher LOD active longer. Pros: better visuals. Cons: higher cost.
Aggressive thresholds
Switch to lower LOD earlier. Pros: saves GPU. Cons: risk of popping if not tuned.
Practical checklist
  • Test with expected camera movement speeds.
  • Favor 2–3 LOD levels, not many small steps.
  • Keep consistent UVs if textures are shared.
Key Takeaway

Tune thresholds in motion to avoid distracting LOD swaps while saving meaningful GPU work.

Misconception check
Tighter thresholds are not always better — they can make swapping more visible. Test to find the sweet spot.
