Definition
Shader Complexity & Texture Resolution
Higher shader complexity and oversized textures increase GPU and memory cost; match them to platform needs.
Simple shader
Unlit or single texture sampling – low cost.
PBR shader
Multiple maps and dynamic lighting – higher cost.
Practical guidelines
  • Use smaller textures for small props: 512 or 256 when appropriate.
  • Compress textures using platform formats to reduce memory usage.
  • Use simpler shaders on lower-end targets and reserve advanced shading for hero assets.
Key Takeaway

Match shader and texture cost to on-screen importance and target hardware to get best value.
