Check Your Understanding
Assessment Steps
  1. Record baseline draw calls and triangle counts in a minimal scene.
  2. Apply one optimization at a time (LOD, atlas, compression) and re-measure.
  3. If improvement is missing, isolate an asset and confirm material sharing and UVs are correct.
Quick fixes
  • Ensure materials reference the same asset to enable batching.
  • Verify UVs map into atlas regions and are not pointing to old textures.
  • Adjust LOD thresholds and test with player-speed camera motion to reduce popping.
Reflective question

Which optimization reduced CPU work most: material sharing or LODs? Your profiler data will tell the story.
