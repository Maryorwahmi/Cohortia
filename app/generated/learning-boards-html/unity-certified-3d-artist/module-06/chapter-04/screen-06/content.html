LOD Group Structure
Prepare mesh versions and assign them to LOD slots. Tune screen percentage thresholds for swapping.
LOD0
High detail — close camera
LOD1
Medium detail — mid distance
LOD2
Low detail — far away
Unity steps (summary)
  • Drag highest‑poly model into scene and add LOD Group component.
  • Place each mesh into its LOD slot and set screen percentage sliders.
  • Test by moving camera to watch transitions and adjust thresholds to avoid visible popping.
Example scenario
A distant tree uses LOD0 when the player is close, LOD1 when at medium range, and LOD2 or a billboard when far. This reduces draw cost without losing scene density.
Common Mistake

Creating too many LODs with small differences leads to popping and management overhead. Prefer fewer, well-contrasted levels.

Pro tip
Keep UVs consistent across LODs if they share textures; otherwise textures will shift visually as LODs swap.
