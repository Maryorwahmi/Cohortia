Material Instancing
Share a single material asset to reduce draw calls and enable batching.
Texture Atlasing
Combine small textures into a single atlas so many objects can use the same material with different UVs.
Shader Complexity
More textures and lighting calculations increase per-pixel cost; pick the simplest shader that works.
Final takeaway
Use a combination of LODs, shared materials, atlases, and appropriate shader choices to reach your performance goals without losing important visual detail.
