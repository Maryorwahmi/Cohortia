Comparison
Problem → Fix
Over-detailed distant objects → use LODs or lower-res textures.
Unique materials per prop → atlas textures and share a single material.
Unnecessary transparency → replace with opaque solutions when possible.
No texture compression → apply platform-appropriate compression like DXT or ETC2.
Concrete scene example
A scene of 20 props each with unique materials and 1024 textures will reduce draw calls dramatically if you atlas textures and assign one shared material.
Key Takeaway

Look for patterns of redundancy: repeated shapes, repeated textures, and hidden geometry are low-hanging fruit.

Misconception
Assuming visuals always require maximum resources. Thoughtful optimization keeps the look while lowering cost.
