Learning Objectives
Learn the tools, understand the storytelling role of props, apply compositional variation, and master Prefabs.
01
Place assets efficiently
Use vertex and grid snapping to align modular pieces quickly and maintain architectural accuracy. This reduces errors and speeds iteration.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use props for storytelling
Arrange objects to imply past actions, like overturned barrels or clustered market goods, so the environment communicates narrative without text.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Balance variety and repetition
Apply scale, rotation, and grouping to avoid tiling while keeping performance-friendly repetition patterns.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Manage props with Prefabs
Create reusable assets that update globally to maintain consistency and streamline edits across scenes.
