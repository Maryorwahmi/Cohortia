Tiling Artifacts
Repeating textures show seams and patterns at scale. Break them with decals, unique props, or varied material instances.
Fix list
  • Place unique props along large surfaces.
  • Use decals or vertex paint to vary appearance.
  • Mix material instances with subtle parameter differences.
Unnatural Distribution
Even spacing and exact alignment look artificial. Use small offsets, grouping, and varied scales to produce organic patterns.
Common Mistake
Placing many copies without storytelling leads to an 'asset dump' — props need purpose and placement intent.
Example Fix
Scatter a few crates and a barrel near a wall seam to distract from tiling, and rotate/scale them slightly so they don't repeat visually.
