Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Variety vs Repetition
Comparison: A vs B
Pure Repetition
Many identical models, often in a grid. Fast to author but looks artificial and repetitive.
Controlled Variation
Small asset set with scale, rotation, and material tweaks. Looks natural and remains performant.
Practical recipe
  1. Create 2–4 base models for similar props.
  2. Randomize scale in a tight band (0.9–1.1) and rotate on Y.
  3. Group small clusters and make cluster Prefabs for natural repetition.
Example
Use three rock meshes to build 5 cluster Prefabs; scatter clusters rather than single rocks to create organic formations.
