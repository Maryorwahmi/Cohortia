Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Hands-on Activity
Step-by-step
  1. Import modular walls, floor tiles, and small props into your project.
  2. Assemble a corner using vertex snapping for precise joins.
  3. Create Prefabs: drag each prop into the Project window.
  4. Place props with intent: clusters, overturned items, and pathway cues.
  5. Duplicate using Prefabs, vary scale (0.9–1.1) and Y rotation, and create cluster Prefabs for scatter placement.
  6. Verify from multiple camera distances and fix tiling or floating issues with decals or small props.
Concrete checks
  • Are props grounded without floating?
  • Do repeated assets show obvious tiling?
  • Do clusters read well at camera distance and close-up?
Try this
Make one intentional anomaly — a tipped barrel or footprint decal — to make the corner feel lived-in and to anchor the story.
