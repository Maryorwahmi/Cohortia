Core Concept
What is Whiteboxing?
A working, low-fidelity 3D sketch that prioritizes function over form.
Why it matters
  • Lets teams validate scale and flow without final art.
  • Enables rapid movement of large geometry during iteration.
  • Reduces costly rework later in development.
Example
Build a 10x10 plane floor, place cubes as walls and cover, add a 2-unit capsule to check doorway heights and jump reaches.
Key Takeaway

Keep geometry simple. Test scale and sightlines first; replace art later.

Blockout Anatomy
Primitives
Cube, Plane, Capsule, Cylinder used as placeholders for buildings, floors, players and obstacles.
Player Reference
A capsule set to roughly 2 units tall to test doors, platform reach, and movement collisions.
Functional Areas
Color-code zones for cover, hazard, and traversal during playtests.
Iteration
Move large elements quickly and run early playtests; adjust scale and flow before adding art.
