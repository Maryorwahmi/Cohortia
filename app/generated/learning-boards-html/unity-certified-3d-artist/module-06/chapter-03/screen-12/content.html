Definition
Common Mistakes
A short checklist to prevent wasted effort during whiteboxing.
Over-detailing
Stop adding final art until layout and gameplay are stable.
Ignoring Player Scale
Always test with a capsule to prevent navigation issues.
Poor Playtesting Habits
Make single, prioritized changes and retest quickly.
Quick Validation Checklist
  • Player-scale verification with capsule.
  • Sightline checks from at least three vantage points.
  • Cover functionality: short vs tall cover tests.
  • At least one short external playtest run after major changes.
Key Takeaway

Keep blockouts minimal, test with a player, iterate quickly, and validate each art swap to preserve gameplay intent.
