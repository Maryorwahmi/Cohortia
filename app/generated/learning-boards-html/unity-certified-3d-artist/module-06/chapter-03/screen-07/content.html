Design Focus
Sightlines, Cover & Choke Points
Tune tactical play by arranging simple geometry and color codes.
Sightlines
Test visibility from key vantage points to ensure balanced encounters and meaningful exploration.
Cover
Size cover so it functions as intended: short for crouch, tall for full protection.
Choke Points
Narrow funnels create tension but can block flow if overused; balance with open areas.
Example Scenario
Arena Entrance Test
Create a narrow doorway using two Cubes and place staggered cover in the room. Color the doorway red to mark danger and cover in green. Have a tester approach from multiple angles to see if cover actually creates tactical choices.
Common Mistake
Placing too many identical cover objects that make encounters repetitive and slow down movement.
Pro Tip
Use cylinders or angled cubes to break predictable sightlines and encourage movement around cover.
Challenge: can you reduce a choke point without losing its tactical function?
