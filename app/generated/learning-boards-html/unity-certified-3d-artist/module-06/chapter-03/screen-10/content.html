Architecture
Blockout → Art Pass
Use the whitebox as scaffolding for final art, swapping assets progressively and verifying gameplay at each step.
Step 1 — Low-poly Swap
Replace key primitives with low-poly assets that match the blockout's scale and footprint.
Step 2 — Parent and Align
Parent final assets to blockout objects at first to ensure position and rotation remain correct.
Step 3 — Playtest After Swap
Confirm collisions, sightlines, and cover behavior after each replacement prior to detailed texturing.
Practical Example
Cover Swap
Replace a cube used as cover with a low-poly crate model that has the same bounding box. Playtest to ensure it still provides expected protection and doesn't create new blind spots.
Common Mistake
Assuming final art will match primitive behavior; always test new assets immediately after placement.
Pro Tip
Keep color-coded materials until the new art has been validated in playtests to preserve clear communication of function.
