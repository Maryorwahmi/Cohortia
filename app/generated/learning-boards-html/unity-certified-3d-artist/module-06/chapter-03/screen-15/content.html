Quick Reminders
Three short prompts to guide your blockout decisions.
Keep it Simple
Whitebox is a sketch — avoid final visual detail until design is proven.
Use Player Scale
A capsule reference ensures consistent measurements across the level.
Iterate & Test
Make single, prioritized changes and retest to validate design moves.
Parting Thought

A good blockout lets you make large, meaningful design choices quickly. Keep it simple, test with the player, and iterate deliberately.
