Hierarchy
Verticality & Elevation
Design vertical layers that support movement and tactical choices.
Primary Routes
Stairs and ramps that players use to move reliably between levels.
Secondary Platforms
Optional vantage points or flanking ledges that reward exploration.
Forbidden Drops
Clear visual cues for dangerous falls or inaccessible areas to avoid player confusion.
Key Takeaway

Prototype vertical elements early and test reachability with the player capsule to keep movement intuitive.

Example Layout
Two-level Arena
Create a raised platform reachable by a 1-unit ramp. Place waist-high cover on the platform to test whether the elevated position provides meaningful control over the ground area.
Common Mistake
Adding elevated areas without clear paths makes players feel lost; always provide logical traversal routes.
Playtest Prompt
Ask testers: can you reach this platform naturally? Is the elevated view worth the effort?
