Process Flow
Using Unity Primitives
A quick step sequence to build a functional blockout.
Step 1 — Ground Plane
Add a Plane and scale to arena size, e.g., 10x10 units for a small arena.
Step 2 — Player Capsule
Place a Capsule at player start, about 2 units tall, to check clearances.
Step 3 — Walls & Cover
Use Cubes for walls and different sized cover; vary heights to create tactical choices.
Step 4 — Rounded Obstacles
Cylinders are useful for columns and for breaking straight sightlines.
Key Takeaway

Build iteratively: place primitive, test with player placeholder, adjust scale and position until gameplay feels right.

Guided Example
Arena Blockout Example
Ground: Plane scaled to X=10 Z=10. Player: Capsule at Y=1. Walls: Cubes 10x3x0.5. Cover: smaller cubes and cylinders at varying heights. Test: move the capsule through the arena, check that short cover blocks line of sight when crouched or standing.
Common Mistake
Misaligned primitives that leave collision gaps or unreachable ledges; always snap to the floor and test movement.
Pro Tip
Use simple unlit materials with bright colors to communicate function quickly during playtests.
