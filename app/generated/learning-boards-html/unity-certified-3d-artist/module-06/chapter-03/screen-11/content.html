Process
Playtesting & Iteration
How to run focused tests and translate feedback into blockout changes.
Short Test Routine
1. Watch a tester move between two points. 2. Note hesitation and stuck spots. 3. Make a single geometry change. 4. Retest immediately.
Prioritize Changes
Fix navigation and clarity first. Cosmetic tweaks come later during the art pass.
Communication
Use colored materials and labels to make test intent obvious and reduce misinterpretation during feedback sessions.
Key Takeaway

Treat playtesting as an iterative loop: test, change, and retest until the level communicates movement and choices clearly.

Example Feedback Cycle
Observed Issue
Testers consistently hug the left wall and miss the intended door. Fix: widen entry and add a small visual cue at the doorway, then retest.
Common Mistake
Making too many small changes after a test, which blurs cause and effect. Change one thing at a time.
Pro Tip
Record short clips of tester runs to see subtle navigation issues you might miss live.
