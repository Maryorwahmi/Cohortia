Player Scale & Proportion
Practical checks that prevent navigation problems.
Scenario A — No Player Reference
Design by eye; leads to inconsistent doorways and unreachable platforms that only appear during playtests.
Scenario B — With 2-unit Capsule
Use the capsule to size doors, platforms, and ceilings; movement tests catch problems early.
Key Takeaway

Make the player capsule your ruler. It prevents inconsistent scaling and saves time in iteration.

Quick Checklist
  • Door height and width meet capsule clearance.
  • Platform jump distances are reachable from capsule start height.
  • Ceiling clearance prevents clipping at head height.
  • Step-up heights are comfortable to traverse without unintended climbs.
Example
Adjust a doorway from 1.2 to 2.6 units wide after capsule testing to reduce jamming points.
Common Mistake
Designers gauging scale visually without a player reference end up with inconsistent gameplay zones.
