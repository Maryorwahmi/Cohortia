Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › Resolution Strategy
Lightmap Resolution
Defines texels per unit used to rasterize lighting into lightmaps.
  • • Low resolution: faster bakes, softer shadows, potential blockiness.
  • • High resolution: accurate shadows and detail, longer bakes and larger textures.
  • • Test with values like 10, 40, 80 to understand impact on your scene.
Per-object override
Use Mesh Renderer > Lightmap Settings > Scale In Lightmap to give critical objects more texel density without increasing the global resolution.
Example Allocation
Chair visible up close: Scale 2.0. Distant building: Scale 0.5. Global resolution: 40 texels/unit as a baseline.
Key Takeaway

Use per-object scale to concentrate detail where the player will notice it, keeping overall lightmap cost low.

Quick Check

If a small object still looks blocky after increasing scale, inspect UV2 coverage and padding before raising resolution further.
