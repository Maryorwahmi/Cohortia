Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › What is Light Baking?
Light Baking — the definition
Precompute lighting for static meshes and store the results in lightmaps applied at runtime.
  • • Performance: removes per-frame lighting cost for static objects.
  • • Quality: captures indirect bounces and soft shadows not feasible in realtime on some targets.
  • • Trade-off: baked lighting is static; moving lights or objects require re-baking.
Key Takeaway

Bake static illumination when you need high-quality indirect lighting and predictable runtime performance.

Concrete Example
Imagine an interior with a red accent wall. Baking captures the subtle red tint that bounces onto the floor and adjacent walls, producing realism without runtime cost.
Misconception
People sometimes believe baked lighting prevents all dynamic effects. In practice, you can hybridize: bake static world lighting and use realtime lights for characters or interactive elements.
