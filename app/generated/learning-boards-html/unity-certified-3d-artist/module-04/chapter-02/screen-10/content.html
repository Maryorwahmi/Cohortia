Padding, Indirect Intensity, and AO
These parameters help you avoid artifacts and craft the final lighting mood.
Lightmap Padding
Buffer in pixels/texels between islands. Increase to avoid bleeding at seams.
Indirect Intensity
Scales bounce brightness. 1 is physical; tweak for artistic intent.
Ambient Occlusion
Adds contact shadowing to enhance depth. Tune intensity and thickness to taste.
Practical tip
AO can mask small UV issues but should not be used to hide overlapping UV2s; fix UVs first.
Key Takeaway

Small padding and AO adjustments often resolve visual imperfections without increasing resolution.

Common Mistake

Relying on AO to hide UV problems leads to inconsistent results. Fix UV2s and padding first.
