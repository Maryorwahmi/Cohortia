Lightmaps explained
Textures that contain baked lighting data applied to static meshes so runtime sampling produces lit surfaces without per-frame calculation.
Color vs Directional
Non-directional lightmaps store color only. Directional lightmaps also encode normal direction information for better interaction with normal maps and reflections.
When to use
Use directional mode when you need accurate shading with normal-mapped surfaces, at the cost of extra texture memory.
UV2 and common artifacts
  • • Overlapping UV2s cause light from one island to appear on another, creating seams and blocky patterns.
  • • Unity can generate UV2s on import, but complex meshes may need manual unwrapping in your modeling tool.
  • • Increasing lightmap padding can reduce bleeding between adjacent islands in the lightmap.
Common Mistake

Assuming auto-generated UV2s are always sufficient. Inspect UV2s on imported complex geometry to prevent artifacts.

Pro Tip

If you see seams but resolution is adequate, check UV2 overlap first before raising global resolution.

Check your UVs
Open the mesh import settings and enable Generate Lightmap UVs, or export a clean UV2 from your modeling tool.
