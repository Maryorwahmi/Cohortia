Running a bake
Open Lighting Settings, verify parameters, then click Generate Lighting. Monitor the progress and preview outputs when available.
  1. • Do fast low-res test bakes to check composition.
  2. • Increase resolution and QA for final bakes.
  3. • After changes to static geometry or lights, re-bake to update results.
Practical Tip

Use small regions or isolated test scenes to reduce iteration time when tuning settings.

Inspecting results
Look for blocky light, seams, or color bleeding. If present, check UV2 overlap first, then increase padding or adjust resolution per-object.
Warning

Large final bakes can take hours; ensure you have a plan for incremental testing to avoid long wasted waits.
