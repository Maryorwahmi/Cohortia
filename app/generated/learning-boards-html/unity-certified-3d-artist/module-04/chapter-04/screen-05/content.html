Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Configure Cascades
Step-by-step
  1. Open Project Settings → Quality and set Shadow Cascades to 2 or 4.
  2. Move the camera through typical player positions and note close vs distant shadow clarity.
  3. Tweak cascade split values to place boundaries where the player spends most time.
  4. Profile draw calls and GPU cost to ensure added cascades justify their overhead.
Example split
Four cascades split example: 0.1, 0.3, 0.6
Illustration & checks
  • Watch for seams or abrupt transition popping at boundaries; refine splits to smooth them.
  • If GPU cost becomes excessive, reduce cascade count or lower shadow resolution per cascade.
Common mistake

Adding many cascades without profiling will often increase draw cost more than the quality benefit justifies.
