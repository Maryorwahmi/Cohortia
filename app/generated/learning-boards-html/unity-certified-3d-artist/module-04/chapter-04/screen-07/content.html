Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Near Plane
Concept

Near plane governs how close a shadow caster can be to the light's view frustum. If set incorrectly, thin or camera-close objects can disappear from shadow maps or clip badly.

Checks
  • Verify light range and frustum include thin geometry.
  • Use backface tolerance and "Is Transparent" when baking thin or translucent materials.
Example & Fix

If fence posts cast no shadow, increase the shadow frustum coverage or reduce the near plane value so that the posts fall inside the shadow camera. If baking, increase backface tolerance to avoid light leaking through thin geometry.

Misconception

Assuming bias alone fixes all thin-geometry shadows is incorrect; frustum and near plane may be the root cause.
