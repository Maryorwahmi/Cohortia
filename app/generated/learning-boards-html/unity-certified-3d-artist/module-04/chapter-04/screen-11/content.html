Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Apply Masks & Layers
Step-by-step checklist
  1. Group objects into meaningful layers: Player, Environment, Props.
  2. For each light, open its Culling Mask and deselect layers it should ignore.
  3. In URP/HDRP, create Light Layers and assign matching layers to lights and renderers.
  4. Profile frame cost and visually verify lighting correctness.
Example scenario

A cluster of decorative lights in a building only needs to illuminate Props. Remove Player and Environment from their culling masks so those lights don't compute lighting for unrelated objects, improving performance.

Key Takeaway

Restricting light influence reduces unnecessary per-object lighting work and can yield large runtime savings.
