Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Performance Bottlenecks
Common issues
  • Too many realtime shadow-casting lights — convert static ones to baked.
  • High shadow resolution or distance — tune quality settings and cascades.
  • Large global lightmap textures — use per-object parameters instead.
  • Overlapping lightmap UVs — fix UV2 packing to avoid artifacts and long bakes.
  • Realtime reflection probes used unnecessarily — prefer baked probes when possible.
Prioritization

Profile first, then target the single largest cost. Often converting several lights to baked or adjusting shadow distance produces larger gains than micro-optimizing many small settings.

Quick fixes
  • Use Lightmap Parameters to reduce global texture sizes.
  • Restrict lights via culling masks to reduce per-object cost.
  • Switch reflection probes to baked unless dynamic updates are required.
