Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Activity
Lab steps
  1. Create scene: Plane, Cubes, Directional Light with soft shadows.
  2. Shadow cascades: none → two → four, move camera and observe near/far quality.
  3. Tune bias: create thin cube, increase shadow bias slightly, then use normal bias to refine.
  4. Create Lightmap Parameters assets and assign them, then bake and inspect density.
  5. Use a culling mask on a decorative light to limit influence to a Prop layer.
Checkpoints
  • Can you remove acne without producing peter-panning?
  • Does the hero prop show visibly denser lightmap texels than the background?
  • Did restricting a light's culling mask reduce per-frame cost in your profiler?
