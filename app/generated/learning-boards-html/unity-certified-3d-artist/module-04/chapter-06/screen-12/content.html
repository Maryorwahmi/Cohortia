Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Hands-on Activity
Step-by-step
1. Scene: Import hero prop and ground plane.
2. Key: Spot Light, Color #FFFFDD, Intensity 1.5, Range 10, Angle 40, Soft Shadows.
3. Fill: Duplicate key, move opposite, Color #DDDDFF, Intensity 0.5, No Shadows.
4. Back: Duplicate key, place behind, Color #00AAFF or #FF8800, Intensity 0.8, Angle 30.
5. Refine: Camera checks, toggle rim, adjust grading.
Refinement checklist
  • Does the hero prop read at close, mid, and far distances?
  • Does fill reveal necessary detail without flattening shape?
  • Does rim separate silhouette without becoming the focus?
  • Does color grading unify the mood without masking gameplay cues?
Key Takeaway

Follow the block-in values, then measure outcomes at gameplay distances and refine with small, targeted changes.
