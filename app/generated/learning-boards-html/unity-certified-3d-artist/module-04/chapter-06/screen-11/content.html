Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Iterative Workflow
Quick workflow
  1. Block-in key light to set time of day and primary form.
  2. Test from close, mid, and far gameplay cameras.
  3. Add fill and rim; evaluate separation and detail.
  4. Solicit focused feedback and iterate with small, reversible changes.
Practical checkpoints
Use desaturated previews to verify shape language, toggle rim to measure separation, and compare before/after color grading to ensure mood alignment.
Try this

Set a single slider change as an experiment: increase key-to-fill ratio by 20% and note whether readability improves at gameplay distance.
