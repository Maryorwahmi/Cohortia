Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Back / Rim Light

Role

A thin edge highlight that visually separates the subject from surrounding elements.

Example
Spot Light, Color #00AAFF or #FF8800, Intensity 0.8, Spot Angle 30, Penumbra 40. Use no or hard shadows depending on desired rim crispness.
When to use rim
On hero props, characters, or architectural features that must stand out against complex or dark backgrounds.
Key Takeaway

A subtle rim often improves perceived depth more than increasing the key intensity; use it to preserve drama while improving readability.
