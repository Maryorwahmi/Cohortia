Purpose of Fill

Recover detail in shadowed areas while preserving the key light's shape and dramatic intent.

Set intensity lower than key, often disable shadows to avoid secondary shadow shapes.
Use cool or neutral fill colors to subtly complement a warm key.
For large scenes, leverage baked GI for broad ambient fill instead of many small fills.
Example setup
Duplicate the key, move opposite, set Color #DDDDFF, Intensity 0.5, Range 15, Shadow Type: No Shadows.
Common Mistake

Using too many fills or fills with similar strength flattens the scene. Maintain a clear intensity hierarchy.
