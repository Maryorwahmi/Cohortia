Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Lighting Scenarios
Daylight
Use a directional key, sky fill, and subtle color grading; keep moderate contrast unless you want dramatic sun shafts.
Tools: Directional Light, Reflection Probes, baked GI for static scenes.
Night
Low ambient, emphasize local light sources as focal points; color and falloff control mood.
Tools: Local Spot/Point Lights, emissive materials, lower global ambient.
Interior
Artificial lights and complex bounces mean probes and baked GI matter; manage color mixing carefully.
Tools: Light Probes, Baked GI, Area Lights, color grading.
Exterior
Combine directional keys with environmental fills and accents; consider time of day transitions.
Tools: Directional Light, Weather-based color shifts, reflection probes.
Key Takeaway

Scenario determines which light role dominates. Choose a dominant role, then add fills and accents to support mood and gameplay clarity.
