Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Contrast & Mood
High Contrast
Creates drama, strong silhouettes, and hides information intentionally; useful for tension and mystery.
How to achieve
Increase key intensity, reduce fill intensity, sharpen shadow penumbra, and use color separation.
Low Contrast
Conveys calm, openness, and safety; good for hub areas or tutorials where clarity matters.
How to achieve
Raise fill relative to key, soften shadows, and use gentle color grading.
Key Takeaway

Contrast is a deliberate design choice. Tune key and fill, shadow sharpness, and color grading to match the desired emotional tone and gameplay readability.
