Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Bloom — creating believable glow
Parameters explained
  • Intensity: How strong the glow appears.
  • Threshold: Brightness cutoff for pixels that will bloom.
  • Scatter: How far the glow spreads from its source.
Concrete example
Neon sign: Threshold high enough to exclude surrounding lights, Intensity moderate (0.3–0.6), Scatter small to medium for tight halos.
Common mistake
Overuse: lowering threshold to near zero makes bloom wash the scene and hide mid-tone contrast.
Tune visually
Toggle Bloom on and off while watching emissive materials. Start with threshold that isolates true highlights and increase intensity in small steps until the glow reads but doesn't dominate.
Performance tip
Lower bloom resolution, reduce iteration count, or use a lower-intensity pass for constrained hardware.
Key Takeaway

Bloom should highlight bright sources subtly. Use threshold to protect mid-tones and avoid washing your image.
