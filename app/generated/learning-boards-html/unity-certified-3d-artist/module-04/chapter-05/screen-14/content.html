Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Key Concepts
Post-Processing
Full-screen image effects applied after rendering to shape mood and clarity.
Post-Process Volume
Defines where effects apply; can be global or localized and holds a Profile reference.
Post-Process Profile
An asset storing individual effect settings you can swap or blend between volumes.
Summary takeaway

Bloom and Color Grading carry the largest artistic weight; use AO, Vignette and DoF for depth and focus. Always profile and prefer subtle adjustments to preserve both performance and readability.
