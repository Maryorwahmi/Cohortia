Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Ambient Occlusion (SSAO)
How SSAO works
SSAO inspects nearby depth and normal information in screen space to estimate where contact shadows should be stronger.
Parameters
Intensity, Radius, Quality — tune to add subtle depth without broad darkening.
Concrete example
Interior scene: Intensity 0.6, Radius 0.4 to bring out junctions and furniture contact shadows while preserving lit areas.
Limitations & misconception
SSAO is screen-space only. It cannot add occlusion from objects not visible in the current view. Rely on baking for off-screen or static AO when needed.
Key Takeaway

AO adds subtle contact shading. Use it with moderation and complement it with baked AO for complete results.
