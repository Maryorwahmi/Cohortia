Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Vignette & Depth of Field
Vignette
Adjust Intensity and Smoothness for subtle edge darkening. Use to focus the eye without cutting peripheral readability.
DoF
Set Focus Distance to the target object and Aperture to control blur amount. Useful for cinematic framing and close-ups.
Examples & pitfalls
  • Cinematic close-up: low Aperture, focus on character eyes.
  • Gameplay camera: keep DoF off or minimal to preserve clarity.
  • Vignette: subtle values 0.2–0.4 often suffice; avoid dramatic dark rings.
Key Takeaway

Use DoF and vignette to guide attention, but preserve gameplay clarity by limiting DoF during action.
