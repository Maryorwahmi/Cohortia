Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › What to Remember
Iterate from neutral
Start with low intensities and increase only as needed so each effect contributes clearly.
Prioritize Bloom & Grading
These two effects most directly shape mood and highlight emissive sources—tune them first.
Profile and optimize
Use the Profiler and reduce quality or radius before removing effects entirely to preserve artistic intent.
Final thought

Post-processing is a powerful polish. Use it deliberately to support mood and gameplay while keeping performance within your target budget.
