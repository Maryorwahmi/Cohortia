Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Balancing Visual Impact & Performance
Measure first
Use Unity Profiler and Frame Debugger to see which effect draws the most GPU cost while post-processing is enabled.
Bloom: reduce resolution or iterations for big savings.
AO: lower Radius and Quality samples to cut cost quickly.
DoF: use lower sample counts or restrict DoF to cinematics.
Practical strategies
  • Toggle expensive effects during gameplay; enable for cutscenes.
  • Lower quality steps that are less perceptible to players.
  • Document acceptable visual delta and iterate until both goals are met.
Key Takeaway

Optimize by measuring, then tune quality parameters and resolution. Perceptual changes often yield the best cost/benefit trade-offs.
