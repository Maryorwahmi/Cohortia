Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Assessment: Sci-Fi Scene
Scenario
Futuristic interior with neon accents and reflective surfaces. Goal: moody, desaturated palette with readable glowing lights.
Choose Bloom for neon glow; tune Threshold so only neon blooms.
Use Color Grading to desaturate and cool shadows while keeping specular highlights warmer.
Add AO with small Radius for contact depth; avoid large radii that darken broad areas.
Evaluation check
  • Does bloom emphasize neon without washing mid-tones?
  • Does grading preserve highlight detail while creating mood?
  • Is AO subtle and localized to contacts?
Key Takeaway

Decide effects by artistic priority and measure performance impact—choose minimal settings that achieve the mood.
