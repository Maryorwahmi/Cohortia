Post-Processing for Visual Fidelity
Use final-image effects to define mood, focus attention, and add photographic polish.
In this lesson
Set up a Post-Process Volume, tune Bloom and Color Grading, add SSAO, and choose DoF and Vignette settings while balancing performance.
Why this matters
Post-processing is often the difference between a technically correct render and a striking, playable world.
Explore Key Topics
Post-Process Volume
Global or local volumes, profile assignment and blending for scene-wide or area-based effects.
Bloom & Color Grading
Glow for highlights and color remapping to unify mood and palette.
AO, Vignette, DoF
Depth, focus, and framing tools that direct player attention and add realism.
Visual Overview
Global Volume
Applies effects across the whole scene for consistent mood.
Profile
The saved settings asset where individual effects live and can be swapped or blended.
Tune
Adjust intensity, threshold, radius and quality per effect to balance look and performance.
