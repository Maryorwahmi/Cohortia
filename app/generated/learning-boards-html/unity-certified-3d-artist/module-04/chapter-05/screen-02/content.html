Bloom, Grading, AO — at a glance
Bloom
Intensity, Threshold, Scatter — use low values first to avoid washout.
Color Grading
Use ACES or low/high definition modes and adjust shadows, midtones, and highlights for a consistent palette.
Ambient Occlusion
Tight radius and moderate intensity add depth without strong darkening of large areas.
