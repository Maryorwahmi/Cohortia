Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Color Grading — modes and wheels
Core controls
  • Mode: Low/High Definition or ACES for different precision and cinematic feel.
  • Temperature & Tint: overall warm/cool and green/magenta shifts.
  • Color Wheels: independent shifts for Shadows, Midtones and Highlights.
Concrete example
Horror mood: move Shadows slightly toward blue, lower Saturation, increase Contrast. For a warm fantasy, warm Midtones and lift Saturation modestly.
Common misconception
Don't apply grading as a single heavy wash. Grading should refine shots and support lighting decisions, not hide them.
Key Takeaway

Use color wheels to sculpt mood while preserving detail; test across scenes and devices to ensure consistent intent.
