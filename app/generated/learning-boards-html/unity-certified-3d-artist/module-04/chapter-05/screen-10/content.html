Built-in vs URP vs HDRP
  • Built-in: Post-Processing Stack package, flexible but varied support.
  • URP: Integrated, optimized for performance across many platforms.
  • HDRP: High-fidelity features and extra quality controls for desktop/console.
Practical advice
When changing pipelines, revalidate profiles and retune effects rather than assuming one-to-one parity. Use pipeline-specific quality knobs to balance cost.
Key Takeaway

The concept set is stable across pipelines; implementation and quality controls vary—test and retune when migrating.
