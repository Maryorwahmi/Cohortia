Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Placement
Cover movement paths, heights, and transitions; avoid interior geometry placement.
Bake
Always rebake after changes so probes store correct samples and reflections.
Blending
Use blend distance between overlapping probes to avoid abrupt reflection or lighting changes.
Final takeaway

Probes are the bridge between baked and dynamic. Place them thoughtfully, bake after changes, and validate with moving objects to maintain realism and efficiency.
