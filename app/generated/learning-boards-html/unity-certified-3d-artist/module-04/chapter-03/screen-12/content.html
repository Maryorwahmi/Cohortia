Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Light Probes
Sample baked indirect lighting to light dynamic objects via interpolation.
Reflection Probes
Capture a cubemap so reflective materials show localized environment reflections.
Influence Volume
The box region where a probe's cubemap or samples are applied to objects.
Takeaway

Use Light Probes for ambient interpolation and Reflection Probes for localized reflections. Localize placement and rebake after changes to keep visuals accurate and performant.
