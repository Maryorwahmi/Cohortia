Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Placement & Blending
Where to place probes
Per room center, near water, at reflective clusters, and around doorways. Localized probes reduce parallax artifacts.
Overlap strategy
Place probes with overlapping boxes and use blend distance to create smooth changes between them.
Validation
Move a reflective sphere across an overlap and look for gradual changes rather than abrupt jumps.
Blend distance tuning
Start small and increase until the reflection transition looks natural. Balance sharpness and smoothness for each overlap zone.
Practical example
Two probes in adjacent rooms: set blend distance across the doorway to soften the reflection switch as an object moves between rooms.
