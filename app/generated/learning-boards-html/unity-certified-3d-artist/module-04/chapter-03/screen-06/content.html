Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Editing & Baking Probes
Edit and duplicate probes
Select probes in edit mode, move them near surfaces, change heights, and duplicate to fill gaps along movement paths.
Tip
Use duplication to extend probe coverage quickly along a corridor or stairwell while keeping spacing consistent.
Validation
Move a capsule or player object through the area after rebaking to inspect interpolation and detect popping.
Bake lighting
After every meaningful placement change, generate lighting so probes obtain fresh baked samples. Probes only store samples after a bake.
Common mistake
Forgetting to rebake leaves probes with old data and gives the illusion that probes are broken.
Check
Confirm the Mesh Renderer on dynamics is set to Blend Probes so they use interpolated data automatically.
Key Takeaway

Edit probes, rebake, and validate with dynamic objects. Add probes where lighting transitions occur rather than uniformly increasing density.
