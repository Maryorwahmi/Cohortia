Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Mistakes & Balancing
Too few probes

Leads to abrupt lighting transitions and popping on dynamic objects. Add probes specifically at transitions rather than applying a uniform grid everywhere.

Symptom
Character lighting suddenly changes when crossing an uncovered threshold.
Too many probes

Raises memory and bake time for diminishing returns. Focus probes where they change perception, not everywhere.

Symptom
Longer bakes and larger lighting data but marginal visual improvements in uniform areas.
Reflection probe note
Avoid a single large reflection probe for a complex room. Use multiple localized probes with blend distances to correct parallax and keep reflections geometrically plausible.
