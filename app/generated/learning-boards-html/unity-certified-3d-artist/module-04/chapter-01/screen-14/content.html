Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Directional Light
Parallel rays; rotation defines illumination; ideal for sun or moon.
Point & Spot
Point = omnidirectional; Spot = cone with inner and penumbra angles for falloff control.
Area Lights
Emit from surfaces; soft shadows; commonly used in baked or HDRP workflows.
Intensity
Controls brightness; tune with exposure for natural results.
Range & Angles
Limit influence to necessary geometry to save performance.
Shadows & Optimization
Limit shadow casters, reduce distant resolution, and use culling masks to improve frame rate.
Summary takeaway
Choose light types to match physical behavior, tune properties to scale, and optimize shadows and culling to balance look and performance.
