Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Point Light
Properties

Range and Intensity

Small range for localized pools, intensity to control perceived brightness. Tune both instead of pushing extreme values.

Example
Place a point light over a lamp model with Range 5 and Intensity 0.8 to create a believable nearby glow without lighting the whole room.
Common mistake
Large range values widen influence and increase per-frame calculations. Keep ranges focused on intended receivers.
Optimization tip
Disable shadows on minor point lights or restrict their culling mask to avoid unnecessary shadow passes.
Key Takeaway

Use point lights for local sources and keep range tight; disable shadows where they aren't visually important.

Scenario

Placing a lamp in a room

Set the point light slightly above the lamp's bulb, choose a warm color, set Range to cover nearby surfaces, and enable soft shadows only if the lamp's shadows are important to composition.

Check your understanding

Pro tip
When iterating, temporarily disable shadow casting on point lights to speed up scene updates, then re-enable for final testing.
