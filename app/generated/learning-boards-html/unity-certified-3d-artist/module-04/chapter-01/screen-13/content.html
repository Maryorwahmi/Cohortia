Common questions & quick answers

Q: Flashlight & moon
Answer: Spot Light for flashlight, Directional Light for moon. Spot for cone, directional for distant parallel rays.
Q: Many point lights cause lag
Answer: Use culling masks, tighten ranges, and reduce shadow casters rather than increasing ranges or just swapping shadow type.
Debug tip
Toggle shadow casting per light and profile frame time to identify the costly lights to optimize first.
Design check
If the scene is visually washed out, reduce light intensities and rely on baked indirect light for ambient energy.
Reflection prompt

Which optimization will you try first in your project: culling masks, disabling shadows, or baking static lights? Why?
