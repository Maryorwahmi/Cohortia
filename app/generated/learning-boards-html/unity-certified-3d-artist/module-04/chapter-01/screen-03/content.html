Practice pathway

A clear sequence of tasks that map to the lesson objectives and assessment items.

Step 1
Scene prep
Add a plane and several props. This creates visible geometry for lighting and shadow observation.
Step 2
Directional Light
Rotate and tint the directional light to simulate time-of-day and observe cast shadows.
Step 3
Point & Spot Lights
Add localized and cone lights, tune range, intensity, and soft shadows for natural falloff.
Verification
You can explain which light types you used, why, and the performance impact of shadow settings.
Tip
Start with low shadow resolution when iterating, then raise it for final visuals only where it matters.
