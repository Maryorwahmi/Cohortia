Overview

Light types at a glance

Directional, Point, Spot, and Area lights each offer unique distributions and performance characteristics; choosing one shapes both look and cost.

Directional
Simulates distant sun or moon: rotation matters, position doesn't.
Point
Omnidirectional light from a point—good for localized sources like bulbs.

Quick reference

Light Types

  • Directional: parallel rays for sun/moon.
  • Point: omnidirectional bulb.
  • Spot: cone of light for flashlights.
Assessment idea

Which two lights would you use for a flashlight and a distant moon? Consider distribution and rotation behavior.

Key Takeaway

Use a few well-placed realtime lights and tune shadows and culling masks to balance fidelity and performance.
