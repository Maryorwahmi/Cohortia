Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Dynamic Scene Lighting Setup

Step-by-step

Follow these ordered tasks in Unity to practice realtime lighting fundamentals.

Scene Preparation
  • Add Plane for ground and several Cube and Sphere props.
  • Group decorative props on separate layers for culling mask tests.
Directional Light
Rotate to X:45, Y:-30, Color #FFDDAA, Intensity 1.2, Soft Shadows.
Point Light
Place near a cube, Color #AADDFF, Intensity 0.8, Range 5. Enable shadows only if needed.
Spot Light
Point at a sphere, Intensity 1.0, Range 8, Spot Angle 45, Penumbra 60, Soft Shadows on.
Experiment
Adjust Shadow Resolution and Shadow Distance under Quality and note visual vs performance changes.
Optimization
Use culling masks to limit light influence on layers, reducing unnecessary lighting work.
Reflection
Which change produced the biggest visual improvement and which cost the most frame time?
