Key Choices

Hard vs Soft

Hard shadows are crisp and less costly; soft shadows are natural but more expensive. Choose based on visual priority and budget.

Project settings
Adjust Shadow Resolution and Shadow Distance under Quality to limit cost for distant shadows.
Concrete example
Enable soft shadows for the main directional light and disable shadow casting on minor decorative lights to reduce render passes.
Common mistake
Using unnecessarily high shadow resolution for distant objects where the detail is not visible.
Key Takeaway

Optimize shadows by limiting shadow-casting lights, lowering distant resolution, and using soft shadows where value is high.

Quick checklist
  • Are shadows visible to the player? If not, disable shadow casting.
  • Is shadow distance tuned to camera range?
  • Use higher resolution only for close-up critical shadows.
Pro tip
When profiling, toggle shadow options to measure which setting produces the largest performance change and prioritize adjustments there.
