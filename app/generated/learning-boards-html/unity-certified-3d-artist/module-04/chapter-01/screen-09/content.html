Process

Tuning workflow

  1. Set base color to convey time-of-day or mood.
  2. Adjust intensity to reach target brightness without clipping exposure.
  3. Set range to cover only intended receivers.
  4. For spotlights, tune inner and penumbra angles for realistic edges.
Concrete example
For a streetlamp, pick a warm color, set wider range and moderate intensity so nearby buildings are subtly lit, and enable soft shadows for ambience.
Misconception
Turning intensity up to compensate for poor range yields unrealistic falloff. Fix range first, then fine-tune intensity.
Key Takeaway

Tune properties in order: color, range, intensity, and angles; match the light's physical scale for believable results.

Cards
Color
Sets mood and temperature. Warm colors feel inviting; cool colors feel distant or eerie.
Intensity
Brightness control; avoid overexposure by checking camera exposure settings.
Range & Angles
Define where light reaches and how focused cone lights appear.
