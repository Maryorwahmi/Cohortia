Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Lab Activity
Lab Steps
  1. Create or open an effect (Rate 500, Lifetime 5, Size 0.5)
  2. Record baseline in Profiler; note ParticleSystem.Update and Draw Calls
  3. Reduce emission and max particles; re-profile
  4. Reduce size/lifetime for overdraw; re-profile
  5. Try Culling Mode and texture atlasing where applicable
Assessment prompt

After your edits, record the percentage change in ParticleSystem.Update time and draw calls. A successful optimization shows measurable reduction in at least one key metric while keeping the effect visually acceptable.

Key Takeaway

This lab locks in the habit: profile, change one thing, and re-profile to quantify improvement.
