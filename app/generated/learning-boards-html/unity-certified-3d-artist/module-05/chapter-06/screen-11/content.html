Profiler-driven example

Follow the steps below to capture a baseline, change one parameter, and re-profile to measure impact.

Steps to capture and compare:
1. Open Profiler (Window → Analysis → Profiler) and press Record
2. Play scene with the particle effect for 3–5 seconds
3. Stop recording and inspect CPU → ParticleSystem.Update and Rendering → Draw Calls
4. Change Emission Rate over Time from 500 to 100 and lower Max Particles
5. Re-record and compare timing and draw calls
Example adjustments
  • Rate over Time: 500 → 100
  • Max Particles: 1000 → 200
  • Start Size: 0.5 → 0.2
  • Culling Mode: Always Simulate → Pause And Catch-up
Key Takeaway

Compare profiler captures before and after each single change to understand its true impact on CPU and GPU costs.
