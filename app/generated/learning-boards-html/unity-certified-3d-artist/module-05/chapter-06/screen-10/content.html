Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Post-Processing
Definition

Which post-process effects cost the most?

SSR, high-quality Depth of Field, and high-sample Ambient Occlusion are common heavy hitters. Measure each effect's cost in the Profiler.

Selective enable

Turn off all post-processing, then enable effects one at a time while profiling to measure per-effect cost.

Lower internal quality

Reduce resolution, sample count, and complexity of the heaviest effects first to gain large savings with small visual cost.

Concrete example

Disabling SSR and reducing DOF resolution in a scene dropped GPU time by over 20% while preserving overall mood and composition.

Key Takeaway

Treat post-processing as a budgeted resource. Keep effects that contribute most to your art direction and reduce or remove the rest.
