Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Post-Process Checklist
Checklist
  1. Disable all effects and profile baseline.
  2. Enable effects one at a time to measure per-effect cost.
  3. Lower effect-specific resolution or sample counts.
  4. Consider cheaper approximations or partial resolution passes.
  5. Create platform-specific profiles with reduced quality for constrained devices.
Concrete example

Disabling SSR and halving DOF resolution in a complex scene reduced PostProcessLayer.Render time by roughly 40%, with limited perceptual change from typical gameplay distances.

Key Takeaway

Measure the cost of each post-process pass and apply the least visually invasive reduction that yields a solid performance win.
