Module 6 › Chapter 5.6 › Count vs Size
Comparison

Many small vs Few large particles

Small particles increase simulation calls but may have low pixel coverage. Large particles reduce simulation overhead but can spike fill rate due to overdraw.

Scenario A: Many small sparks

High particle count, low per-particle pixel cost. Watch CPU simulation time.

Scenario B: Few large smoke sprites

Lower particle count but high overdraw and fill-rate pressure on the GPU.

Decision matrix
If CPU-bound

Lower particle count, reduce per-particle work, simplify emission rules.

If GPU-bound

Reduce particle size, shorten lifetime, use opaque or simpler blend modes to cut overdraw.

Example

Switching from 800 large smoke sprites to 300 medium sprites and faster fade reduced fill rate by 35% while keeping perceived density.

Key Takeaway

Tune both count and size based on which resource your profiler shows as the bottleneck.
