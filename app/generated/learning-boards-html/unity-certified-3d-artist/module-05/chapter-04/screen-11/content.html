Comparison Performance vs Quality

Balance visual fidelity with cost

High Quality (costly)
  • Full resolution Color Grading in HDR mode
  • High-intensity Bloom with large scatter
  • Multiple local volumes blending at run-time
Optimized (recommended)
  • Conservative Bloom Intensity and Threshold
  • Global mild Color Grading, heavy effects reserved for cutscenes
  • Profile presets and lower sample counts for bloom if available
Practical test

Profile before and after enabling each effect. If frame time rises significantly, reduce quality or scope the effect to local volumes.

Key Takeaway

Measure and prioritize. Use subtle global adjustments and restrict expensive passes to where they contribute most to perceived quality.
