Definition Vignette

Vignette: drawing the eye

Parameters to know
  • Intensity: darkness of the edges, start 0.2–0.4.
  • Smoothness: feather quality between center and edge.
  • Color: tint the vignette for mood.
Example

Use a slightly warm tint and Intensity 0.3 on a photo-real interior to gently focus the view on the center object.

Misconception

A heavy vignette does not equal cinematic; it often hides framing problems. Use it to support composition, not to fix it.

Try this

Apply a vignette, play the scene, and toggle to compare. Reduce intensity until the viewer’s attention remains but edges don’t feel artificially dark.

Key Takeaway

Vignette is a subtle tool to guide attention. Use low intensities and test across brightness ranges to ensure natural results.
