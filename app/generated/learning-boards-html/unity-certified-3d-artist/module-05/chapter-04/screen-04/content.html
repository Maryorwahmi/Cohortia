Core Concept What is Post-Processing?

Post-processing defined

Full-screen corrections applied after rendering to adjust glow, color, and lens effects across the whole image.

Analogy & Example

Imagine finishing a photo and adding a soft glow to highlights and a slight vignette to draw attention. In Unity, Bloom and Vignette do that for a camera’s image. For example, neon signs with emissive materials look more believable when Bloom is applied; the light bleeds beyond the mesh edges just like a camera sensor would capture it.

Common misconception

Don’t assume stronger values equal better visuals. Excessive Bloom or heavy grading often washes out detail. Start small, iterate, and check the scene at multiple exposures.

Key Takeaway

Post-processing is a subtle but powerful last step. Use it to unify lighting and direct attention without overpowering detail.
