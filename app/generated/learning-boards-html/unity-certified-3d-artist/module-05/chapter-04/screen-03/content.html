Apply and validate basic effects

This screen gives practice cues and mnemonics to help you reliably tune settings in the editor and avoid the most common mistakes.

Bloom — Mnemonic: Light Leak

Start with Intensity 0.5, Threshold 1.05. Increase slowly and watch for washed-out highlights.

Vignette — Mnemonic: Soft Curtain

Try Intensity 0.2–0.4 and subtle color shifts to avoid heavy framing effects.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Color Grading — Mnemonic: Color Paint

Tweak Temperature and Contrast slightly, preview in game view, and prefer small increments to retain natural skin tones.
