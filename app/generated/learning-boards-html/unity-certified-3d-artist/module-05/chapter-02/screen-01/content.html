Advanced Particle System Properties & Materials

Deepen your control of appearance and behavior: materials, blend modes, sub-emitters, collision, and lifetime forces that turn simple particles into rich VFX.

In this lesson
Why materials and modules are the visual heart of particle effects and how they chain to form multi-stage effects.
Why it matters
Good material choices and module tuning create believable VFX while keeping performance under control.
Visual concept
Texture Atlas + Sheet Animation
Pack many sprite variations into one texture and step through frames to animate a particle sprite without extra draw calls.
Blend Mode Choice
Additive brightens overlaps for glow; Alpha Blended respects soft alpha for smoke and fog.
Sub-emitter Chain
Parent burst → On Death sparks → smoke puffs. Sub-emitters let events spawn child systems to build staged effects.

Materials
Create particle materials with Additive or Alpha Blended shaders to control transparency and glow.
Sub-Emitters
Trigger child systems on birth, death, collision, or trigger to build layered effects.
Collision & Forces
Use collision, damping, and forces over lifetime to make particles interact believably with the scene.
