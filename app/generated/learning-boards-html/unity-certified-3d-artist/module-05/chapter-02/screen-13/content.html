Tuning process
  1. Test at target camera and resolution with a short loop.
  2. Compare blend modes to confirm artistic intent.
  3. Adjust emission counts and lifetime to balance visibility and cost.
  4. Profile draw calls: replace multiple materials with an atlas where possible.
  5. Prefer Planes for simple ground collisions to reduce World-check cost.
Quick checklist
  • Atlas used? ✔
  • Blend mode appropriate? ✔
  • Sub-emitters timed? ✔
  • Collision type performant? ✔
  • Particle counts reasonable? ✔
Reflective prompt
Which single parameter change most improved the visual at lowest cost: blend mode, lifetime, or emission count?
