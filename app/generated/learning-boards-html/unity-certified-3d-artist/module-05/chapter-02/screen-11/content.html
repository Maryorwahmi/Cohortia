Definition & Types
Collision Type options determine scope of detection. Planes are lightweight, Colliders are explicit, and World checks everything in the scene.
Response properties
  • Damping — how much velocity is lost on impact
  • Bounce — fraction of velocity retained after collision
  • Lifetime Loss — reduce particle life on collision
Example: raindrops
Set Type to World or Planes, Damping moderate, Bounce low, Lifetime Loss to 1 to make drops splash and disappear on terrain impact.
Performance note
World collision can be costly with many particles. Use Planes for simple ground collisions or reduce particle counts and lifetimes.
