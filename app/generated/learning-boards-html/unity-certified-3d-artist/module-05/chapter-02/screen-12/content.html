Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Lifetime Modules
Forces over Lifetime
Apply a continuous vector force (e.g., wind) so particles drift as they age. Combine with noise for turbulence.
Limit Velocity over Lifetime
Cap particle speed to avoid unrealistic acceleration and preserve readable motion.
Color & Size over Lifetime
Use curves to change color and size from birth to death. Smoke expands and fades; fire shifts color before disappearing.
Example
Add a gentle horizontal force to embers and a velocity limit to keep arcs natural, then fade color to transparent with a curve.
