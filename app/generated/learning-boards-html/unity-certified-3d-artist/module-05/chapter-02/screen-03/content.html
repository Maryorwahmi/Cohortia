Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Practice Focus
Exercise: Materials
Create a particle material using Particles/Additive and assign it to a short-lived flash emitter. Compare with an Alpha Blended material using the same sprite to judge glow versus softness.
Tip: Keep camera fixed and loop 1 second for quick judgments.
Exercise: Sub-Emitter Chain
Make a parent burst with a death-triggered child. Tune child burst count, speed, and gravity to read as sparks or smoke depending on your artist intent.
Exercise: Collision & Forces
Enable Collision Type Planes for ground contact, set Lifetime Loss to 1 for disappearing splashes, and add Forces over Lifetime to simulate wind drift or falling behavior.
Performance note: Use Planes instead of World when many particles need cheap collision checks.
Quick Checklist
  • Use atlas for sprite animation
  • Choose blending per material goal
  • Chain sub-emitters for staged effects
  • Tune collision and lifetime loss for contact behavior
