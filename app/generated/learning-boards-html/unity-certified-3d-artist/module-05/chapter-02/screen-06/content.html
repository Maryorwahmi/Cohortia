Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Blending Modes
Additive

Glow and light-emission

Overlapping pixels add brightness. Best for fire, sparks, and magical energy that should intensify where particles overlap.

Example
A burst of fire particles brightens in the center where many sprites overlap, creating a convincing glow without extra lights.
Common pitfall
Additive on smoke makes it look unnaturally bright and ethereal.
Alpha Blended

Soft opacity and occlusion

Uses alpha to composite particles over the scene. Good for smoke, fog, and soft splashes that should obscure objects behind them.

Example
Smoke that fades and blocks background details reads natural when rendered with alpha blending.
Decision aid
Ask: Is this particle emitting light? If yes, favor Additive. Is it a surface or volume occluding the scene? Choose Alpha Blended.
