Cost drivers
  • Max Particles — caps active particles; higher costs increase memory and CPU use.
  • Rate over Time — increases spawn rate and can inflate active particle count.
  • Draw size and overdraw — large transparent sprites cost more on the GPU.
Tip: shorten lifetime or lower size to reduce draw cost without changing the look drastically.
Practical checklist
  1. Prototype at low particle counts and test on target hardware.
  2. Use bursts rather than enormous steady rates for transient spikes.
  3. Profile CPU/GPU when adjusting Max Particles or Rate over Time.
Key Takeaway

Balance visual fidelity and performance: small changes in rate or lifetime can multiply costs, so iterate conservatively and profile.
