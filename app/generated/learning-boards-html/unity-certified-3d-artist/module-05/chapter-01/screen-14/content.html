Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Particle System (Shuriken)
System that spawns and controls many particles to make complex effects.
Main Module
Controls lifetime, speed, size, color, looping, and simulation space.
Emission & Shape
Emission sets spawn timing (Rate or Burst); Shape sets where particles originate.
Takeaway
Start simple: prototype with default materials and conservative Max Particles, then iterate on Main, Emission, and Shape while profiling performance.
