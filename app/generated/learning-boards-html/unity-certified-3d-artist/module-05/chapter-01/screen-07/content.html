Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Simulation Space
World Space

Particles exist in world coordinates and do not follow emitter movement after spawning. Ideal for smoke that should linger or environmental effects.

Example: A campfire placed on the ground uses World to let smoke rise and remain while the campfire is stationary.
Local Space

Particles are tied to the emitter transform and move with it. Use for effects attached to characters or vehicles.

Example: Magic dust on a character’s hand should use Local so it follows animations cleanly.
Common mistake

Selecting World for a moving emitter will create trailing particles that break the illusion of attachment. Test movement early to validate your choice.
