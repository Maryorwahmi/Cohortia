Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Troubleshooting
Fast diagnostic checklist
  • Looping & Duration — ensure continuous effects loop as intended.
  • Simulation Space — switch between World and Local to test attachment.
  • Renderer material — confirm it supports vertex color if color changes should apply.
  • Emission — check Rate over Time and Burst timing for sparse or missing particles.
  • Max Particles — ensure cap is not clipping your active particles.
Example fix
If a hand-mounted dust effect trails behind, change Simulation Space to Local. If color over lifetime is invisible, switch to a material that multiplies particle color.
Key Takeaway: Narrow the source of error by testing one setting at a time, starting with Looping and Simulation Space.
