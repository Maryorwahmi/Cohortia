Objective details
01 Renderer setup
Use a default material to confirm behavior, then add a soft sprite material to define particle appearance for the final pass.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Burst vs Continuous
Choose Burst for sudden, timed releases and Rate over Time for steady streams like rain.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Simulation Space
Local attaches particles to the emitter; World leaves particles in world coordinates—choose based on whether the emitter moves.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Safety checklist
  • Start with low Max Particles (for example 100) and low Rate over Time.
  • Test on target hardware and observe frame rate before raising counts.
  • Prefer bursts for single impacts and Rate over Time for continuous effects.
Quick mnemonic
Prototype fast, then refine visuals and performance in small increments.
