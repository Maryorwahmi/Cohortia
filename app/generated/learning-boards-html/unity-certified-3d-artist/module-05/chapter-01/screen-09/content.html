Common Shapes
  • Cone — fire, exhaust; tweak Angle and Radius.
  • Sphere — aura, radial explosions; adjust Radius.
  • Box/Edge — waterfalls, linear sources.
  • Mesh — spawn from vertex or surface when you need precise emitter geometry.
Practical notes
Match the shape to the physical source and preview density. Shapes that concentrate emission in a small area may require lower Rate over Time to avoid excessive overlap and high particle counts.
Example: Cone Angle 15°, Radius 0.1 for focused smoke column from a small source.
Tip: Switch shapes while the system is playing to compare live distributions quickly.
Key Takeaway

Choose the emitter shape that best maps to the effect’s physical source, then tune radius and angle for realistic distribution and efficient particle use.
