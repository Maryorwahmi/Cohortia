Rate over Time

Defines how many particles spawn per second. Use for ongoing effects like rain or smoke.

Tip: Start with a low value and increase until the visual density looks right while watching Max Particles.
Bursts

Scheduled, sudden releases of particles at specific times — ideal for impacts and explosions.

Examples & combos
Waterfall: Rate over Time + Box shape to cover an edge.
Impact: Burst at t=0 with a single short lifetime to simulate a splash.
Layered: combine low Rate over Time with occasional Bursts for variation without spiking steady particle counts.
Check

If you see a performance drop, inspect Rate over Time and scheduled Bursts first, then lower counts or shorten lifetimes.
