Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › What is a Particle System?
Definition & role

A particle system simulates a large number of small images or meshes and controls them collectively to create complex visuals like smoke, fire, rain, and magical effects.

Analogy: Particles are brush strokes; together they form the visual texture.
Use case: Long lifetime and gentle speed for drifting smoke.
Misconception: More particles = better look. Instead, distribution and lifetime matter more.
Concrete examples
Smoke
Long Start Lifetime, low Start Speed, negative Gravity Modifier to rise slowly, Looping on for continuous smoke.
Explosion
Short Start Lifetime, high Start Speed, use Bursts in Emission for sudden release.
Rain
Rate over Time with a Box or Edge Shape to cover a wide area.
Magic Dust
Small Start Size, moderate lifetime, local simulation space to follow a character’s hand.
Key Takeaway

Choose particles when you need many independent elements to create soft, stochastic motion that is hard to animate with single meshes.
