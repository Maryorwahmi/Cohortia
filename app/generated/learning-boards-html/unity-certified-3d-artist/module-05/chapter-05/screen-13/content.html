Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Common mistakes & Pro tips
Common mistakes
  • Using post-processing to hide poor lighting or composition.
  • Running SSR at full resolution without profiling cost.
  • Applying extreme SSAO or DoF that reduces clarity or reads as dirty.
Pro tips
  • Use conservative defaults and enable expensive effects per-camera.
  • Combine SSR for dynamic objects with reflection probes for global coverage.
  • Create presets for different hardware targets to quickly adjust quality vs performance.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize readability and performance. Use post-processing to enhance, not to mask deeper issues.
