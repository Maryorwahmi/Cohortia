Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts — Visual Effects
Depth of Field
Simulates camera focus; use Focus Distance and Aperture to control sharpness and blur.
Screen Space Reflections
Real-time reflections from screen pixels; limited to on-screen content and sensitive to resolution.
SSAO
Approximates ambient occlusion in crevices and corners to add soft contact shadows.
Summary takeaway
Combine these effects thoughtfully. Tune parameters conservatively, validate across camera movement, and profile on target hardware to balance fidelity and performance.
