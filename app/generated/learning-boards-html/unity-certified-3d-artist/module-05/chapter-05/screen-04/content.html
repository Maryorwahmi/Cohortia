Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Depth of Field (DoF)
Core Concept
Depth of Field
Simulates camera lens focus to guide attention and convey scale.
Definition
Objects at Focus Distance are sharp; others receive a blur based on Aperture and Focal Length.
Concrete example
Set Focus Distance to the character's eyes and Aperture low to softly blur the background, emphasizing facial animation.
Key Takeaway

Use DoF subtly. Strong blur can hide interactive elements or break readability.

Visual controls
Focus Distance
Sets the distance where objects remain perfectly sharp.
Aperture
Controls blur strength and the size of bokeh highlights.
Focal Length
Alters perceived compression and how foreground/background relate.
Common mistake
Applying excessive blur that hides important gameplay information or causes disorientation.
