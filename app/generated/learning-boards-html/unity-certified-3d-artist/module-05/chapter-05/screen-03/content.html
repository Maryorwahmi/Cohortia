Practical mnemonics
Quick mental models and trade-offs to speed decision-making during iteration.
Aperture = Iris
Treat aperture like a camera iris. Start with conservative values and lower aperture only when you want strong cinematic separation. Too much blur hides important detail.
Visual mnemonic: camera icon
SSR = Screen mirror
Think of SSR as a mirror that only sees what the camera sees. Reduce resolution to improve performance and avoid relying on SSR for out-of-view reflections.
Visual mnemonic: mirror fragment
SSAO = Crevice depth
Use SSAO to ground objects. Increase radius for broader contact shadows and intensity to make them visible, but avoid crushing the midtones.
Visual mnemonic: corner shadow
Blend Distance = Transition buffer
Apply a blend distance on local volumes to avoid abrupt jumps. Tune it to camera speed and the spatial scale of the transition area.
Visual mnemonic: soft edge
