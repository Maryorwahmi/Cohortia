Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › SSR: Performance trade-offs
Options and effect
Resolution
Lower to save performance; expect softer, blurrier reflections.
Max Roughness
Limit reflections to smoother surfaces to reduce sampling work.
Intensity
Lower to blend reflections more subtly and mask artifacts.
Concrete tuning example
Recommended trade-off

Set SSR Resolution to Half, Max Roughness to ~0.8, and Intensity to 0.5 on constrained hardware to keep reflections believable while lowering cost.

Misconception
Expecting SSR to cover every reflective need. In practice combine SSR with reflection probes for off-screen and distant reflections.
