Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Chromatic Aberration & Grain
Chromatic Aberration
Simulates color fringing near image edges. Use low intensity for stylistic flair only.
Grain
Adds film-like noise to unify tones and hide banding. Keep intensity low to preserve detail and UI clarity.
Lens Distortion
Simulates barrel or pincushion optics for stylized or photographic effects. Apply subtly and test with HUD elements present.
Key Takeaway

Treat these as finishing touches that complement good lighting and material work. Avoid using them to hide scene problems.
