Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Observe, profile, iterate
Validation checklist
Visual checks
  • Toggle effects on/off while playing to compare clarity and mood.
  • Move camera through typical paths to observe DoF and SSR behavior.
  • Confirm HUD and interactive elements remain readable.
Performance checks
  • Profile on target hardware after enabling each effect.
  • Measure frame-time delta when toggling SSR resolution and DoF precision.
  • Document the trade-offs and keep conservative defaults for runtime.
Key Takeaway

Make decisions with both visual checks and performance data. Iteration and profiling ensure the effects support gameplay and hardware targets.
