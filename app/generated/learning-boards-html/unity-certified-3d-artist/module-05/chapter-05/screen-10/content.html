Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Post-Process Volumes
Create local volume
Uncheck Is Global to make a volume that only affects the camera inside its bounds. Assign a dedicated profile and set Blend Distance to soften the transition.
Example profile
Increase Vignette, lower Color Grading saturation, and slightly darken exposure for cave mood.
Pitfall
A zero Blend Distance makes abrupt visual jumps; always use a blend buffer sized to camera speed.
Concrete scenario
Place a local volume at a cave entrance. Set Blend Distance so that moving into the cave the vignette and desaturation build gradually over a few meters, avoiding sudden changes that break immersion.
Key Takeaway

Blend Distance is the control that makes local volumes feel natural. Tune it against camera motion and spatial scale.
