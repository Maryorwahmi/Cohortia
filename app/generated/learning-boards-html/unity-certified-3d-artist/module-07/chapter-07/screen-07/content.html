Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Animating Virtual Camera Properties
When to Animate a VCam
Use nested animation for lens changes, noise bursts, or other timed shifts that need precise keyframes while the Brain still blends shots.
Example Workflow
// Pseudo-steps (do this in Timeline)
1. Add an Animation Track under the Cinemachine Shot.
2. Click record and set FOV keyframes at start and end.
3. Stop record and scrub to confirm the animated property changes only during the shot.
Pro Tip

Animate the virtual camera, not the main camera. The Cinemachine Brain composes the final transform and will override main camera keyframes.

Visual: Nested Animation
FOV Ramp
Animate Field of View from 35 to 50 during a two-second push to simulate camera movement.
Noise Burst
Keyframe Noise amplitude for a short impact to add subtle shake without global animation.
Common Mistake

Animating the main camera instead of the virtual camera will be overwritten by the Brain; always animate the vcam exposed properties.
