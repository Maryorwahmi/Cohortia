Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Hands-on Lab: Multi-Shot Cinematic
Checklist
Prepare Scene: Ensure character and Timeline from previous chapter are present.
Create vcams: WideShot, CloseUpShot, RevealShot — set Follow/LookAt where needed.
Timeline: Add Cinemachine Track; drag vcams as Cinemachine Shot clips and overlap for blends.
Post-Processing: Create PostProcessGlobal volume, assign CinematicProfile with Bloom and Color Grading.
Animate: Add Animation Track to PostProcessGlobal and keyframe Bloom intensity across the timeline.
Quick Example: Bloom Keyframes
// Timeline keyframes for Bloom intensity
t = 0s  -> Bloom = 0.0
t = 6s  -> Bloom = 0.5 (dramatic beat)
t = 12s -> Bloom = 0.0
Checkpoint

Play the Timeline and confirm camera cuts and bloom animation happen in sync; if not, verify bindings and PostProcessLayer settings.
