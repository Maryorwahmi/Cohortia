What is a Virtual Camera?
A virtual camera stores framing rules: Follow, Look At, lens, body, and aim. Cinemachine computes the world camera from those rules.
Definition: An asset that defines camera behavior, not a rendered camera by itself.
Example: 'CloseUpShot' with Follow and Look At targets keeps the actor framed as they move.
Misconception: Cinemachine avoids all keyframes. In reality, nest animation when precise property control is required.
