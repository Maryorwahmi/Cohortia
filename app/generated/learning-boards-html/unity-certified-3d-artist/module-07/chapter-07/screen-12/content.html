Checklist for Troubleshooting
Cinemachine: Is Cinemachine Brain on main camera? Are vcam clips bound?
Post-Processing: Is there a PostProcessLayer on the camera? Do layer masks match the volume?
Timeline: Is the Animation Track recording on the volume instance rather than a profile asset?
Performance Considerations
Costly effects: Depth of Field and heavy Bloom can be GPU intensive.
Optimize: Lower sample counts, use cheaper approximations, and limit global volumes where possible.
Blend strategy: Animate weight rather than stacking many heavy volumes at full weight.
Quick Fix

If post-processing is missing, first check PostProcessLayer and layer mask, then verify the volume's profile and Is Global flag.
