Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Control Track vs Animation Track
Animation Track
Keyframe specific effect parameters on the Post-Process Volume instance.
Best for: Bloom ramps, DOF focus pulls, targeted color shifts.
Pros: Precise control, single-instance editing.
Cons: Manual keyframing for multiple properties.
Control Track + Weight
Activate or swap entire volumes, animating their weight to blend profiles.
Best for: Switching between distinct visual states or layering complete looks.
Pros: Quick swapping, clean profile management.
Cons: Less granular control over individual parameters unless profiles are built for it.
Key Takeaway

Match technique to the creative need: keyframe properties for surgical changes, weight-based volume blends for whole-look transitions.
