Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Key Concepts
Cinemachine
Procedural virtual cameras that define framing intent via Follow and Look At.
Cinemachine Brain
Component that blends active virtual cameras into the main camera transform during Timeline playback.
Post-Processing
Volumes and Profiles define visual effects; animate the volume or its weight in Timeline for scene transitions.
Summary Takeaway

Design shots with vcams, let the Brain blend them, and animate post-processing on Timeline to match camera beats and storytelling intent.
