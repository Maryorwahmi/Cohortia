Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › Animation vs Script
Animation-driven
  • Pros: natural stride, accurate artist intent.
  • Cons: harder to integrate with physics and predictable input control.
  • Use case: cinematic locomotion, complex attack moves.
Script-driven
  • Pros: deterministic, easier to network and test.
  • Cons: may require extra work to sync foot placement visually.
  • Use case: precise platforming, physics-based movement.
Key Takeaway

Align movement ownership to your gameplay needs: fidelity favors root motion, predictability favors script-driven movement.
