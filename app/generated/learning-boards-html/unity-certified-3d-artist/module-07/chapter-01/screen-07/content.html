Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › Configure Avatar
Checklist
  • Confirm required slots are filled: Hips, Spine, Head, Arms, Legs.
  • Resolve yellow/red markers by assigning bones manually.
  • Use Pose preview to check T-pose orientation and local rotations.
Concrete example: If the left arm is flipped, the wrist bone may have an inverted local rotation. Correcting the bone assignment or fixing the exporter’s joint orientation resolves the issue.
Pro Tip

If the source T-pose differs from Unity’s, it's often faster to fix the pose in your DCC application and re-export rather than trying to compensate with avatar mapping alone.

Common Mistake

Ignoring yellow markers because the model ‘looks fine’ — small mapping errors become visible when retargeting different animations.

Key Takeaway

Configure Avatar is required for reliable humanoid retargeting — verify every required bone and the T-pose.
