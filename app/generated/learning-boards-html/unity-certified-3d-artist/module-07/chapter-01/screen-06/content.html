Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › Humanoid vs Generic
Humanoid
Retargeting enabled
  • Maps bones to a standard avatar definition.
  • Enables applying animations from other humanoid characters.
  • Requires correct T-pose and verified bone mapping.
Example: Reusing a walk cycle from one character across a cast without re-authoring the animation.
Generic
Direct bone hierarchy
  • Applies animation to the model’s existing bones directly.
  • Ideal for non-humanoid rigs, vehicles, or specialized skeletons.
  • No Mecanim retargeting; relies on authoring per model.
Misconception: Generic is weaker. Reality: It’s the correct choice for non-humanoid setups and preserves exact bone relationships.
Key Takeaway

Choose the rig type based on skeleton shape and reuse goals; always configure and verify the avatar after switching to Humanoid.
