Learning Objectives
Learn the practical skills to build and synchronize short cinematics using Timeline.
01
Create Timeline & Playable Director
Make a Timeline Asset and attach a Playable Director to an empty manager to play it. This creates a reusable sequence file.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Add and bind Tracks
Use Animation, Activation, Audio, and Control tracks by dragging target GameObjects to the track header so the Timeline knows what to control.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Record camera & animate timing
Record keyframes for camera position, rotation, and FOV or place animation clips to craft pans and reveals.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Synchronize audio and events
Align audio clips and activation events to hit beats and reveal moments in the timeline for cohesive storytelling.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master. These objectives move you from creating assets to reliably authoring simple cutscenes and syncing audio-visual beats.
