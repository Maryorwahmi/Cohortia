Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Mistakes & Practices
Common mistakes
  • Not binding GameObjects to tracks so clips have no effect.
  • Multiple Playable Directors acting on the same objects simultaneously.
  • Using Animation Tracks for root-motion clips when Animator Tracks are required.
Best practices
Modularize with small Timeline Assets, nest with Control Tracks, save and commit Timeline Assets regularly, and test playback in Play mode.
Key Takeaway

Adopt a modular, versioned workflow and resolve director ownership to produce predictable cinematic sequences.
