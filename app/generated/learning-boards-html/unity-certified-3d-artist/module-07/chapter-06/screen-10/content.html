Recording workflow
  1. Create a Camera GameObject and add an Animation Track bound to it.
  2. Click the record button and move the playhead while adjusting camera transforms.
  3. Key FOV changes for subtle zooms to emphasize storytelling beats.
Tip: use small FOV adjustments and smooth tangents to avoid jarring perspective shifts.
Example
Record a slow dolly out and slightly narrow FOV as the character walks in to heighten drama and reveal context.
Key Takeaway

Recording camera keyframes in Timeline provides fine-grained, editable motion that easily syncs with other tracks.
