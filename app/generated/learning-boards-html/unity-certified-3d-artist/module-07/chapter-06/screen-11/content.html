Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Audio & Sync
Steps to sync
  • Drag an audio clip onto an Audio Track.
  • Use snapping and the playhead to align the clip with a visual event.
  • Trim and set fades to smooth transitions between cues.
Preview in Play mode to confirm spatialization and mixer effects.
Example
Place a music swell at the exact frame of a character reveal and start the walk animation just after, creating an audio-visual cue pair.
Pro Tip

Use subtle fades to blend audio clips seamlessly when shifting between cues or when looping background music.
