Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › What is Timeline?
Core definition
Timeline stores sequences as assets and provides tracks to control different types of content over time. A Playable Director plays that asset in a scene.
Example
Create an IntroCinematic asset, add an empty IntroSequenceManager with a Playable Director, then bind tracks for activation, animation, and audio to stage a reveal.
Why use it
It centralizes timing and makes iteration simpler for artists who want to align visual and audio beats without constant scripting.
Quick reference cards
Activation Track
Enable or disable GameObjects at specific times.
Animation Track
Play animation clips or record keyframes for transforms and properties.
Audio Track
Play music, SFX, or dialogue and snap timing to beats.
Key Takeaway

Timeline makes sequence timing visible and editable. Treat Timeline Assets as versioned cinematic files and use Playable Directors to play them in-scene.

Common Mistake
Forgetting to bind the GameObject to a track — the track will have no effect if left unbound.
