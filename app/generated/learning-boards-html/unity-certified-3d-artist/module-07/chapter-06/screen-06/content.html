Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Track Types
Track quick-reference
Animation Track
Play clips or record keyframes; binds to a GameObject to control its properties.
Activation Track
Enable or disable objects at specific times — useful for reveals and lifecycle control.
Audio Track
Place and snap audio clips to hits and beats for precise sound design.
Control Track
Control nested timelines, particle systems, or custom playable assets for composition.
Binding and behavior
  • Always drag the target GameObject onto the track header to bind controls.
  • Animation Tracks override transforms directly; use Animator/Animator Track if you need root motion or state machine control.
  • Control Tracks are perfect for nesting sequences so that large scenes remain modular.
Key Takeaway

Pick the track that matches the asset you need to control: visibility, clip playback, audio timing, or nested composition.
