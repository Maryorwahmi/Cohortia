Bind Targets
Tracks control bound GameObjects; ensure binding is done after adding a track.
Choose Track Type
Animation for clips, Animator for root motion, Activation for visibility, Audio for cues.
Nest & Version
Use Control Tracks to nest and commit Timeline Assets to version control for safe iteration.
Final Note

Treat Timeline as a visual hub for timing. Bind, choose the correct track, nest modular assets, and test early to build reliable cinematics.
