Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Organizing Complex Controllers
Use sub-state machines
Group related states such as locomotion into a single sub-state machine to reduce top-level complexity and localize logic.
Naming conventions
  • Prefix subsystem: Loc_Idle, Loc_Walk, Combat_Attack.
  • Use descriptive names, not Clip1 or NewState.
Why it helps
Encapsulation reduces accidental breakage when you edit unrelated parts of the controller.
Workflow
Create a sub-state machine, open it, author inner states, and then create transitions between the parent and the sub-state machine as needed.
Key Takeaway

Sub-state machines keep controllers readable and safer to maintain. Use them early when you anticipate multiple related states.
