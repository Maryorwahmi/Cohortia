Symptoms & Causes
  • Jerky blends — thresholds too close or clip mismatch.
  • Upper body overrides legs — missing or wrong Avatar Mask.
  • Layer not applied fully — Weight < 1 or incorrect mask scope.
  • Unexpected transitions — Sync enabled or conditions misconfigured.
Practical checks
  1. Preview Blend Tree slider and watch clip weights.
  2. Play the scene and inspect Animator Parameters live.
  3. Open Avatar Mask and confirm bone selections.
  4. Check layer Weight and Sync settings.
Key Takeaway

Systematically inspect parameter values, masks, and weights to isolate animation conflicts quickly.
