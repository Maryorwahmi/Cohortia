Events
Pros: frame-accurate, easy for sound/VFX, clip-level reuse.
Cons: dependent on correct Function names and GameObject components; can be skipped if misconfigured.
Physics checks
Pros: spatially authoritative, works with colliders and networking, less fragile.
Cons: may need extra setup, less tied to precise animation frame without synchronization.
Use case: Footsteps
Events are perfect — cosmetic audio tied to frame timing.
Use case: Melee damage
Event can mark the moment, but confirm with a short raycast or collider overlap before applying damage.
Best practice
Design events for visuals and use physics for authoritative outcomes.
