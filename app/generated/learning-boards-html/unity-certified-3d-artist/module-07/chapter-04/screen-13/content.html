Activity: Retarget and Add Hand IK
A step-by-step lab you can perform in the Editor to validate both systems.
Steps
  1. Import two humanoids and a shared animation clip; set Rig → Humanoid and Apply.
  2. Validate each Avatar mapping and correct T-pose alignment where needed.
  3. Create an Animator Controller, add the clip, and assign it to both models' Animator components.
  4. Create RightHandTarget as a child of CharacterA and position it near the hand.
  5. Attach CharacterIKController, assign RightHandTarget, press Play, toggle IK, and move the target to test.
Checks
  • Both models play the same Animator state.
  • Right hand moves to the target when IK is active.
  • No extreme stretching or flipped elbows when the target moves.
Reflection
Which interactions in your game would benefit most from IK, and where would retargeting reduce art workload? Consider trade-offs when planning your animation pipeline.
