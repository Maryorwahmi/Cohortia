Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Humanoid Avatar
How the Avatar translates motion
The avatar maps role-based joint motion to the model’s specific bones and proportions.
Make sure to:
  • Set the FBX Rig type to Humanoid for both source and target.
  • Align a proper T-pose and assign required bones in the Avatar configuration.
  • Use the Avatar validation preview to confirm mapping and detect mismatches.
Common misconception
The avatar doesn’t change authored motion qualitatively; it reinterprets it to match the model’s bone lengths and orientation, not to create new motion shapes by itself.
Avatar checklist
T-pose alignment
Ensure default pose matches the source to avoid offsets in retargeted motion.
Bone mapping
Map hips, spine, arms, legs, head and confirm no missing required bones.
Validation preview
Use the avatar preview to catch flipped bones or wrong orientations.
