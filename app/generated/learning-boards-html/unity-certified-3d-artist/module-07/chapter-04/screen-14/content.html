Key Concepts
Animation Retargeting
Apply animation from one Humanoid to another by mapping through the avatar.
Humanoid Avatar
A standardized skeleton mapping used by Mecanim to translate motion for different models.
Inverse Kinematics (IK)
Define an end effector goal and let the solver compute joint rotations to reach it.
Summary
Use retargeting to reuse animation assets and use IK to refine limb contact with the environment.
