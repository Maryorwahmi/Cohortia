Practical IK scenarios
Examples where IK reduces animation overhead and increases realism.
Foot Placement
Raycast to find ground under the foot, set IK goal to that position, and adjust foot rotation to match the surface normal for stable planting.
Hand Interaction
Attach a target to an object handle or button and set the hand IK target so fingers and palm meet the object reliably during motion.
Head Tracking
Use look-at weights to smoothly rotate the head toward a moving interest point without modifying body animation significantly.
Weapon Aiming
Drive hand and upper-body IK goals so the weapon aligns to a target while base movement remains driven by clips.
Practical caution
Clamp IK targets and blend weights to avoid limbs snapping or stretching when targets jump or move outside reach.
