Core Concept

Emission and lighting interaction

How to make surfaces glow and when that glow affects scene illumination.

Visual emission
Adds a self-illuminated color to the final pixel output, useful for lit screens or magic effects.
Lighting contribution
Emission does not automatically light nearby objects unless emissive global illumination or baking is used.
Example
Make a sphere emit red at high intensity to simulate a glowing orb; note it appears bright even in shadowed parts of the scene.
Misconception
Emission alone does not replace actual lights for illuminating other objects unless baked or using realtime GI systems.
Key Takeaway

Use Emission for glowing visuals; enable emissive lighting features when you need that glow to affect the environment.

Practical note
When to bake emissive lighting
If you need emission to light nearby surfaces on consoles or mobile, bake emissive GI or use light probes; otherwise emission remains a visual effect.
