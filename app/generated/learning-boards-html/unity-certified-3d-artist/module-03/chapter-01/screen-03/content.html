Skills You Will Practice

Short, checkable tasks that reinforce the core concepts and prepare you for asset authoring.

Sphere experiment

Create a sphere, assign a material, vary Albedo, Metallic, and Smoothness. Observe reflections and color shifts under directional or HDR lighting.

Texture assignment

Plug Normal, AO, and Smoothness maps into the Standard Shader and compare the visual difference against a flat material.

Emission & special effects

Enable Emission to create a glowing object and note how emission adds apparent light without changing global illumination unless additional light components are used.

Optimization checkpoint

Practice reducing texture resolution and observe performance/visual trade-offs to prepare for platform targets.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: For each task, note one visual change and explain which Standard Shader property caused it.
