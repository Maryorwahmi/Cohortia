Definition

Normal maps, Height maps, Ambient Occlusion

Techniques for perceived surface detail and localized darkening without extra geometry.

Normal maps
Encode normal perturbation per texel to change how light interacts with the surface.
Height maps
Describe depth for parallax or displacement effects to suggest larger depth variation.
Ambient Occlusion
Multiplies ambient lighting to darken nooks and crevices, reinforcing depth perception.
Example
Barrel planks: normals for fine grain and ridges, subtle height for warped boards, AO in seams for depth.
Import note
Always import normal maps as normal map type to ensure correct interpretation by the shader.
Key Takeaway

Use normal and height maps to fake depth and AO to enhance perceived contact shadows while keeping geometry light.

Troubleshooting
Flat normal symptoms
If shading looks flat, confirm normal map import settings and that the shader reads the normal map slot.
AO misuse
AO should not be used to fake large shadow shapes; it is for local, subtle darkening of crevices.
