Comparison

Albedo · Normal · Ambient Occlusion

Roles and authoring notes for the most common texture inputs in PBR workflows.

Albedo
Base color map. Avoid baked shadows. Provides the diffuse color the shader uses.
Normal map
Encodes surface normals to fake micro-detail. Impacts highlights and shading strongly.
Ambient Occlusion
Darkens crevices to indicate limited ambient light exposure. Multiply with base lighting.
Practical tip
Keep Albedo free of lighting and hue shifts from reflections; let the shader handle lighting response.
Common mistake
Using diffuse textures authored for non-PBR shading will produce inconsistent results with the Standard Shader.
Scenario

Weathered wooden barrel

Albedo provides the grain and color variations. Normal map simulates plank ridges. AO darkens gaps between planks for depth.

Key Takeaway

Author texture maps for PBR: clean Albedo, correctly encoded normals, and AO to boost perceived depth without geometry.
