Process Flow

Practical walkthrough: Weathered barrel

A stepwise sequence to combine maps and sliders for a believable worn barrel.

  1. Albedo: assign wood grain and color variation. Avoid baked shadows.
  2. Normal: add micro-detail like plank ridges and nicks; ensure normal import is correct.
  3. Ambient Occlusion: darken seams between planks to ground crevices.
  4. Metallic map: mark metal bands as metal; these areas will reflect environment color.
  5. Smoothness map: vary for worn edges versus polished metal; controls reflection sharpness.
  6. Emission (optional): only if parts must glow visually.
Example
Set metal bands Metallic high and Smoothness medium for slightly weathered metal; worn edges show lower Smoothness and altered Albedo.
Check your work
Preview under bright directional light and in indoor, low light scenarios to confirm consistency.
Misconception
Do not bake shadows into Albedo; you will lose correct dynamic shading when lighting changes.
Key Takeaway

Combine maps deliberately, preview under varied lighting, and avoid baking lighting into Albedo for flexible, believable materials.
