Process Flow

How Standard Shader uses PBR inputs

A step-by-step conceptual pipeline of what the shader calculates from material inputs.

Step 1: Sample maps
Read Albedo, Metallic, Smoothness, Normal, AO, and Emission from the material.
Step 2: Compute diffuse & specular
Combine Albedo and Metallic to partition energy between diffuse and specular reflections.
Step 3: Apply normals & AO
Perturb normals for detail and darken crevices with Ambient Occlusion for depth.
Step 4: Add emission
Emission is added as self-illumination on top of lighting calculations.
Key Takeaway

The shader treats inputs as contributors to physically motivated lighting terms. Adjusting one parameter often changes how others appear.

Compact diagram

Example
High Smoothness narrows reflection highlights; combined with high Metallic this creates the bright, mirror-like appearance of polished metal.
