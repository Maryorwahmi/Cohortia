Learning Objectives

Progress from definition to practical PBR authoring and verification.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate material & shader

Explain the distinct roles and how data and programs combine at render time. Verified competency milestone: explain with an example of a sphere and its material settings.

02
Understand PBR principles

Describe how physical properties like metallic and roughness affect reflections and color consistency under lighting. Verified competency milestone: predict behavior of a metallic sphere under sunlight.

03
Identify Standard Shader properties

Recognize Albedo, Metallic, Smoothness, Normal, AO, and Emission and know when to use each. Verified competency milestone: configure a material to look like plastic, metal, and stone.

04
Avoid common mistakes & optimize

Detect wrong workflows like using non-PBR textures with PBR shaders and make performance-conscious choices. Verified competency milestone: explain one optimization for mobile builds.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE: Demonstrate the sphere experiment and describe how three Standard Shader properties change the appearance in scene lighting.
