Common mistakes and optimizations

Recognize common pitfalls and apply practical strategies to keep visuals and performance balanced.

Common mistakes
  • Treating material as only color.
  • Using non-PBR textures with PBR shaders.
  • Importing normals incorrectly.
  • Unnecessary Transparent mode causing overdraw.
Optimization strategies
  • Share materials across similar assets.
  • Use appropriate texture resolutions and mipmaps.
  • Atlas textures when feasible.
  • Prefer Cutout to Transparent where valid.
Scenario
Large open-world use of many unique 4K textures will increase memory pressure; target LODs and shared maps to stay within platform limits.
Misconception
Believing PBR alone eliminates the need to optimize textures and material counts is incorrect; PBR improves realism but still requires performance planning.
Key Takeaway

Check texture authoring for PBR compatibility and make pragmatic choices about resolution, atlasing, and material reuse to meet performance targets.
