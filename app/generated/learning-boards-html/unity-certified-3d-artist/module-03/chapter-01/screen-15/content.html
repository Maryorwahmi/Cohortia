What to Remember

Three concise reminders to guide your material authoring process.

Keep Albedo clean
No baked lighting in the base color.
Shaders interpret data
Switching shader changes how maps are read.
Balance quality & performance
Use appropriate resolutions and share materials when possible.
Final takeaway

Apply PBR inputs carefully, verify imports, and choose rendering modes that match visuals and performance goals.
