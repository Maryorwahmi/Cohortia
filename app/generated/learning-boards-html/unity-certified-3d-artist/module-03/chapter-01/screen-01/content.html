Materials and Shaders — the recipe and the chef

Understand how data-driven materials and GPU shaders combine under PBR to produce consistent, realistic surfaces across scenes and lighting.

Recipe + Chef
Material = recipe · Shader = chef
Visual concept
Material

Albedo color, textures, metallic, smoothness, normals, occlusion, emission — the data that describes the surface.

Stores values and texture maps
Shader

Small program running on the GPU that combines material data with lighting to compute final pixel color.

Executes on render hardware

Key Takeaway

Materials provide the descriptive data; shaders perform the rendering. PBR links physical surface properties to consistent visual responses under lighting.
