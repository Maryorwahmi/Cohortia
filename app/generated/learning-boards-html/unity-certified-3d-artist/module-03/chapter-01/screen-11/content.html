Definition

Rendering Mode and Transparency

How the Standard Shader blends pixels and the trade-offs for performance and sorting.

Opaque
Fastest path; writes depth, no alpha blending. Use when surface is solid.
Cutout
Alpha-tested silhouette. Good for leaves or grills with sharp edges.
Fade / Transparent
Smooth blending for glass or UI, but incurs sorting and overdraw costs.
Key Takeaway

Select a rendering mode that fits the silhouette and performance needs of your asset. Avoid Transparent unless necessary.

Practical notes
Sorting artifacts
Transparent surfaces can overlap incorrectly. Plan draw order or minimize overlapping transparency to prevent issues.
Performance
Alpha blending increases overdraw. Cutout is cheaper than Fade/Transparent for many masked shapes.
