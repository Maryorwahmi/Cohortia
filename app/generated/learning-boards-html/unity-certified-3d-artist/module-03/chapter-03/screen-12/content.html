Check Your Understanding

Assessment & Exercise

Quick Questions
  • Q1: Bricks look too large. Which property fixes scale? Tiling X/Y.
  • Q2: Emission glows but doesn't light nearby surfaces. Why? Emission alone doesn't cast real-time light; add lights or bake GI.
Practical Exercise
  1. Create a Plane and a Material named M_TestMaterial.
  2. Assign Albedo pattern and Normal map; set Tiling to 3–5.
  3. Make two color-tinted variants and enable Emission on one.
  4. Add a Point Light to observe emissive illumination, or bake GI for static illumination.
Key Takeaway

Do the exercise with a neutral lighting setup and compare results across variants to internalize the effect of each property.
