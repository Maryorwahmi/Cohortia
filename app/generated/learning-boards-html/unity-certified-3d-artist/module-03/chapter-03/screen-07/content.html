Process

Tiling & Offset Steps

  1. Open the Material Inspector and locate Tiling & Offset.
  2. Increase Tiling X/Y to repeat the texture more times across the surface.
  3. Use Offset X/Y to move the texture origin and align patterns or hide seams.
  4. Preview in Scene and reduce tiling if repetition becomes obvious.
Tip: Combine moderate tiling with varied detail maps or decals to break up repetition.
Concrete Example
Plane with sci-fi grid texture: set Tiling to 5,5 to make grid smaller and more detailed.
If emblem sits off-center, adjust Offset X to 0.2 until it aligns with model features.
Common Mistake

Avoid extremely high tiling values that create obvious repetition artifacts. Instead, tile moderately and add variation with decals or AO maps.
