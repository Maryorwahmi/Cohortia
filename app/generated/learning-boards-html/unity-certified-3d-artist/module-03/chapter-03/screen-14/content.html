Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Tiling
Controls repeat count across U and V; increase to make patterns appear smaller.
Offset
Shifts where the texture starts on the surface, useful for aligning motifs and hiding seams.
Emission
Creates surface glow; pair with lights or baked GI for environmental illumination.
Final Tip
Confirm import types and test materials under consistent lighting to validate appearance before applying them widely.
