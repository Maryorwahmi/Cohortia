Practice Tips

Small habits make material creation fast and robust in larger scenes.

Descriptive Names
Use prefixes and clear names like M_SciFiPanel so materials are easy to reuse and share across scenes.
Immediate Feedback
Assign materials to a test mesh and adjust sliders while viewing the Scene to judge scale and specular response.
Import Settings
Mark Normal textures correctly and confirm sRGB settings for color maps to avoid incorrect color or normal artifacts.
Emission & Lighting
Use emission for visual glow; add lights or bake GI if you need emission to illuminate other objects.
