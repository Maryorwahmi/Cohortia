Definition

Albedo · Metallic · Smoothness

Albedo: Base color or texture that defines diffuse appearance.

Metallic: 0–1 slider; metals reflect environment and tint specular highlights.

Smoothness: Controls highlight sharpness; high values yield mirror-like reflections.

Key Takeaway

Treat these three as a set: adjust them together to produce convincing materials like metal, plastic, or wood.

Examples & Contrast
Polished Metal: Albedo dark grey, Metallic 1.0, Smoothness 0.9
Varnished Wood: Albedo brown texture, Metallic 0.0, Smoothness 0.6
Rubber: Albedo colored, Metallic 0.0, Smoothness 0.2
Misconception
Don't make non-metals metallic to fake brightness. Metals and non-metals reflect light differently; mixing that expectation breaks realism.
