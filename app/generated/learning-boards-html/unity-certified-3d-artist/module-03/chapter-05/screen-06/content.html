Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Emission Map
How emission maps work
A grayscale texture controls per-pixel emission; pair it with color and intensity for artistic control.
Map rules
White = full emission, black = none, gray = partial. Use masks to isolate glow areas like screens or runes.
Color & intensity
Choose an emission color and an intensity multiplier to match scene exposure and artistic intent.
Example: sci-fi panel
Use an albedo for base detail and a separate UV-aligned emission map for buttons and indicators. Set emission to vibrant blue and tune intensity for readability in-game.
Practice
Create a simple black-and-white rune texture. Assign it to the Emission slot and adjust intensity while viewing in scene exposure.
Misconception
Emission alone does not necessarily illuminate other objects unless GI contribution is enabled and baked or realtime GI is used.
Key Takeaway

Emission maps let pixels look self-lit; use color and intensity carefully and enable GI contribution when you want surrounding surfaces to receive light from that glow.
