Core channels and meanings
Understand what each channel does so you can predict real lighting responses.
Albedo
Base color that controls diffuse reflection and visible pigmentation under neutral lighting.
Metallic
When high, reflections carry surface tint and diffuse energy is reduced; when low, reflections remain neutral.
Smoothness
Controls microfacet sharpness: high for mirror-like highlights, low for matte, diffused scattering.
Concrete example
Take a stone material: Albedo dark gray, Metallic near 0, Smoothness low. Under a warm Point light you get soft, warm illumination and subtle fuzzed highlights.
Practice
Set a sphere: vary smoothness from 0.1 to 0.9 and watch highlights narrow and intensify. Toggle metallic to see color in reflections.
Misconception
High smoothness is not always better. Excessive smoothness without environment detail looks like overpolished plastic.
Key Takeaway

Adjust Albedo, Metallic, and Smoothness together while keeping lighting constant to predict the final appearance of a material under scene lights.
