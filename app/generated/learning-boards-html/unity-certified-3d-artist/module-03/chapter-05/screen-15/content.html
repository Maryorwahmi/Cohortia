Key Concepts
Emission Map
A texture that designates glowing pixels; white = glow, black = none; pair with color and intensity.
Global Illumination (GI)
Simulates indirect light bounces; choose Baked for static or Realtime for dynamic indirect lighting.
Bake rules
Enable Emission GI on the material and mark objects Static before generating lighting to capture emission in lightmaps.
Summary

Use emission for stylistic glow, enable GI and bake for environmental tinting, and combine with low-cost lights for directional cues when necessary.
