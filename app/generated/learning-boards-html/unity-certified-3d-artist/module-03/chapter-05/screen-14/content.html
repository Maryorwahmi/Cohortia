Key Concepts
Directional Light
Simulates distant light with parallel rays and consistent shadows across the scene.
Point Light
Emits light radially from a point with falloff; ideal for localized sources like bulbs.
Spot Light
Emits within a cone for focused and directional illumination with a controllable penumbra.
Summary

Choose the light type that matches the physical behavior you want: sun-like, local bulb, or directional cone.
