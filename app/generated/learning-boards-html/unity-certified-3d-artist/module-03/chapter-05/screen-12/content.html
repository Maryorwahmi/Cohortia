Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Emission vs Emission+Point
Emission-only
Pure emission visually brightens pixels without adding directional shadows; best for decorative effects and baked GI.
Pros
Cheap when baked, strong stylistic glow, useful for screens and neon.
Cons
Lacks directional cues and shadows; can wash out details if intensity is too high.
Emission + Point Light
Combine emission for pixel brightness and a low-intensity Point Light for directional lighting and soft shadows that increase readability.
Pros
Adds spatial cues, small shadows, and depth while preserving emission aesthetics.
Cost
A single low-intensity Point Light is often cheaper than enabling realtime GI for dynamics and provides better directional readability.
Check Your Understanding

If your emissive object must move and tint surroundings, consider if realtime GI is required. Otherwise, bake emission and add a small Point Light for directional clarity.
