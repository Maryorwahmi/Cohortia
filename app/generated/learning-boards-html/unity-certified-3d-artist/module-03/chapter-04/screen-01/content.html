Quick takeaway

Small decisions in materials and textures compound across many objects. Reuse, instance, and profile to keep your scenes efficient.

Concept Map
Material Instance → Shared shader + varying properties → possible GPU instancing and batching.
Factory metaphor: identical products travel one line; small visual variations can force separate lines unless instanced correctly.
