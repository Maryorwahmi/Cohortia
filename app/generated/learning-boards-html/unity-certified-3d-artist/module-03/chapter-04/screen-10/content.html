Profile, then simplify
Use the Profiler and Frame Debugger to find hot spots and focus optimization where it counts.
Simplification tactics:
  • Remove math that doesn’t change the final pixel noticeably.
  • Share computations across nodes and reuse intermediate results.
  • Provide shader variants for quality tiers and disable expensive features on low-end targets.
Concrete example
Replace a costly procedural noise for low-end builds with a simple panner texture and a lower sample rate, keeping the high-end procedural version for stronger hardware.
Key Takeaway

Optimizing shaders often yields larger performance gains than small mesh or texture tweaks; profile to find the biggest wins.
