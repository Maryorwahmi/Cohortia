Hands-on checkpoints
Verify these outcomes in your scene to confirm competence.
Batching verification
Confirm draw calls drop when many objects share the exact same material and shader.
Shader Graph output
Build outline effect and expose OutlineWidth and OutlineColor properties.
Profile shaders
Use Unity Profiler or frame debugger to spot expensive shader calculations.
Texture checks
Set max sizes and compression appropriate to your target platform.
Practice tip
Start with a small scene of 100 repeated objects. Toggle between a shared material and duplicated materials to see draw call and batching changes.
