Definition

UV Mapping

Unwrap a 3D surface into 2D UV space so textures are sampled consistently. Each vertex maps to U and V coordinates between 0 and 1.

Common issues
  • Overlapping UVs cause repeated sampling.
  • Stretched UVs produce distorted textures.
  • Misaligned islands create visible seams.
Practical check

Inspect UV islands in your modeling tool and ensure consistent texel density for even texture detail across the model.

Key Takeaway

Fix UVs early: they determine how textures map to surfaces and directly impact final shading and detail distribution.

Example: Tiling vs Unique Detail

Overlapping UVs work for tiling bricks but fail when unique detail is required. Choose UV strategy based on asset needs.

Quick inspection

If a texture appears stretched on import, open the model in your 3D tool and check UV island scale and orientation before re-exporting.
