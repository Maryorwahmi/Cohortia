Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Metallic vs Smoothness
Comparison

Metallic Map

White = metal, black = non-metal. Metals reflect environment color and have minimal diffuse contribution.

Example: Polished pipe

Metallic = white, Smoothness = high. Expect crisp environment reflections that show color from surroundings.

Comparison

Smoothness Map

Controls reflection clarity. White = smooth (sharp), black = rough (scattered). In Unity, this is Smoothness rather than Roughness.

Example: Painted wood

Metallic = black, Smoothness = medium to low. Reflections are soft and diffuse, preserving base color.

Optimization

Pack metallic and smoothness into one texture when possible (smoothness in alpha) to reduce texture count while ensuring your shader reads the correct channels.

Misconception

Do not leave Smoothness as sRGB color data. These are numerical values and should be treated as data, not color.
