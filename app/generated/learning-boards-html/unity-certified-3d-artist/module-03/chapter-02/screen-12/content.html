Channel Packing & Texture Strategy

Combine grayscale maps into channels to reduce texture count. Maintain documentation so shaders read the correct channel layout.

Example pack

RGB: Metallic map, Alpha: Smoothness. Shader must read alpha for smoothness values.

Compression tip

Use compression that preserves single-channel precision for data maps. Avoid heavy color compression on packed data textures.

Checklist

Document channel layouts, disable sRGB on packed data, and test in-scene after optimization to confirm visual fidelity.

Reuse and Tiling

Reusing high-quality tiling textures across props is often better than many unique large maps. Adjust tiling to match scale and preserve texel density.

Pitfall

Packing color and data together without care, or leaving sRGB enabled on packed data, will corrupt numeric values used by shaders.
