Definition

Normal Map

Stores surface normal vectors to simulate fine surface detail without additional geometry. Alters how light interacts with a pixel.

Why it matters

Saves polygon budget while adding convincing micro-detail. Essential for performance-sensitive projects.

Misconception

Normal maps do not modify object silhouette. They only influence per-pixel lighting calculations.

Pro Tip

Verify the Normal Map preview looks bluish and set Texture Type to Normal Map before assignment.

Practical Example

Apply a normal map of brick mortar to a flat plane. Under directional light, the mortar will cast micro-shadows and highlights despite the plane being flat.
