Definition

Signal Bleed

Unintended Propagation

Wireless signal extends beyond the intended physical boundaries of a secure area.

Security Risk

Creates an attack surface for unauthorized users outside the building, even with strong authentication.

Example

An office Wi-Fi signal reaching a public sidewalk or an adjacent, unsecured building.

Mitigation

RF Containment

Confined Signals

Designing a WLAN so its RF signals are strictly confined within a specific physical area.

Achieving Balance

Providing necessary coverage without overshooting the secure perimeter.

Strategies

Strategic AP placement, directional antennas, adjusted transmit power settings.
