Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Rogue AP

An unauthorized Access Point connected to a network, often installed by an employee, posing a significant security risk.

Evil Twin AP

A malicious Access Point designed to mimic a legitimate one, often used in phishing attacks to trick users into connecting.

Security Site Survey

A detailed analysis of the wireless environment to identify security vulnerabilities, rogue devices, and signal leakage.

Summary Takeaway

Vigilance against rogue and evil twin APs, combined with thorough security site surveys, is crucial for maintaining WLAN integrity.
