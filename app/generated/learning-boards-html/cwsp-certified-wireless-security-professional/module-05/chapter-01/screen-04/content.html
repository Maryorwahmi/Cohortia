Core Concept

Security by Design

Integrate security into every phase of WLAN planning, not as an afterthought.

Proactive Approach: Address inherent wireless vulnerabilities from the ground up.

Layered Defense: Build security into every layer of the network architecture.

Foundational Step: Begin with threat modeling to identify potential risks.

Key Takeaway

A secure WLAN starts with a 'security by design' mindset, embedding protection from the initial planning stages.

Initial Step

Introducing Threat Modeling

Identify Threats

Systematically pinpoint potential threats, vulnerabilities, and attack vectors relevant to your WLAN environment.

Analyze Context

Understand how these threats apply to your specific network, data, and user base.

Inform Decisions

Use insights from threat modeling to guide design choices for APs, authentication, and encryption.
