Core Principle

Dual Goals of AP Placement

Coverage & Capacity

Ensure all intended areas have strong, reliable signal and sufficient bandwidth for users.

Security Considerations

Prevent signal leakage, minimize attack surface, and protect physical devices.

Common Mistake

Prioritizing signal strength alone without considering the physical boundaries of the secure area.

Scenario

The Perimeter AP Dilemma

Problem: AP near Exterior Wall

An Access Point placed too close to a building's perimeter can extend its signal beyond the intended secure zone.

Risk: Signal Bleed

Unauthorized users outside the building could potentially connect, increasing the attack surface for reconnaissance or DoS attacks.

Solution: Strategic Relocation

Move APs further inside, use directional antennas, or adjust transmit power to contain the signal.

Key Takeaway

Secure AP placement prioritizes containing the wireless signal within the secure physical boundaries, not just maximizing coverage.
