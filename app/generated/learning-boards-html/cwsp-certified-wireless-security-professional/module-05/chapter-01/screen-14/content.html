Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Threat Modeling

A structured process for identifying potential threats, vulnerabilities, and attack vectors to a system or application.

Signal Bleed

When a wireless signal extends beyond the intended physical boundaries of a secure area, potentially allowing unauthorized access.

RF Containment

The practice of designing a wireless network such that its radio frequency signals are confined within a specific physical area.

Summary Takeaway

Proactive security design, understanding signal propagation, and strict signal containment are cornerstones of a secure WLAN.
