Survey Techniques

Validating RF Containment and Perimeter Checks

Ensuring wireless signals remain within secure physical boundaries.

Method 1

Perimeter Walk-Around

  • •Physically walk the building perimeter with survey tools.
  • •Check for signal strength and bleed-through of internal SSIDs.
  • •Confirm signal drops off sharply at the secure boundary.

Example: Scanning for your office Wi-Fi from the public sidewalk.

Method 2

Unauthorized Device Detection

  • •Identify unauthorized ad-hoc networks or client devices.
  • •Look for personal hotspots or peer-to-peer connections bypassing security.
  • •Use tools like Ekahau Pro or `airodump-ng` (ethically).

Risk: These devices can create unmonitored access points or data leakage.

Key Takeaway

Physical perimeter checks and unauthorized device detection are vital for validating RF containment and maintaining network integrity.
