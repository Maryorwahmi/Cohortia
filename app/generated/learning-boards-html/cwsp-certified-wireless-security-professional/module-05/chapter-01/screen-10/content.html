The Security-Focused Wireless Site Survey

An indispensable tool for identifying vulnerabilities and validating design.

Primary Goals

Beyond Basic Coverage

  • •Identify potential vulnerabilities in the wireless environment.
  • •Validate the implemented security design and its effectiveness.
  • •Assess RF containment to ensure signals stay within boundaries.

Example: Checking for signal strength outside the building perimeter.

Key Threats Detected

Unauthorized Devices

  • •Rogue APs: Unauthorized APs connected to the corporate network.
  • •Evil Twin APs: Malicious APs mimicking legitimate ones for phishing.
  • •Unauthorized Clients: Devices operating outside of network policy.

Risk: These devices can create backdoors or facilitate credential theft.

Key Takeaway

A security-focused site survey is crucial for real-world validation of your WLAN security design and detecting hidden threats.
