Advanced Concepts

Antenna Gain & Beamwidth

Antenna Gain

Measures an antenna's ability to direct radio energy. High gain = greater range, but higher bleed risk if not aimed.

Beamwidth

Angular width of the radiation pattern. Focused patterns (narrow beamwidth) provide tighter containment.

Analogy

A high-gain antenna is like a powerful spotlight; it reaches far but must be aimed precisely.

Pitfalls

Common Antenna Mistakes

High-Gain Omnis at Perimeter

Using high-gain omnidirectional antennas near exterior walls leads to excessive signal spill.

Ignoring Physical Security

Failing to secure antennas against tampering or theft, creating a physical entry point for attackers.

Key Takeaway

Careful consideration of antenna gain, beamwidth, and physical security is essential for effective RF containment and overall WLAN security.
