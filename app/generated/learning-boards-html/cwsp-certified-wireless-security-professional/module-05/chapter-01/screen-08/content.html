Antenna Selection and Radiation Patterns

Choosing the right antenna to achieve secure coverage and containment.

Type 1

Omnidirectional Antennas

  • •Broadcast signals in all directions (360 degrees).
  • •Suitable for general open areas requiring broad coverage.
  • •Prone to Signal Bleed: Signal extends equally outwards, increasing risk.

Analogy: A light bulb illuminating an entire room.

Type 2

Directional Antennas

  • •Focus the signal in a specific direction (e.g., patch, Yagi).
  • •Ideal for covering specific zones or point-to-point links.
  • •Minimizes Signal Leakage: Reduces spill into unwanted areas.

Analogy: A flashlight directing light to a specific spot.

Key Takeaway

Selecting the correct antenna type is a critical security decision to control signal propagation and achieve RF containment.
