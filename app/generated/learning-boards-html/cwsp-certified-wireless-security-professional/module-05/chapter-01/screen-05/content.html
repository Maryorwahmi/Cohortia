Process Flow

Key Questions in Threat Modeling

1

What Data?

Identify the sensitivity and value of data traversing the wireless network.

2

Who are Attackers?

Determine potential adversaries, from casual users to sophisticated threats.

3

Motivations & Capabilities?

Understand their goals and technical prowess to anticipate attack methods.

4

What Assets?

Define the critical resources (e.g., intellectual property, uptime) to be protected.

Impact on Design

Informing Security Decisions

AP Placement: Where to position Access Points to optimize coverage and minimize risk.

Authentication: Choosing robust mechanisms like 802.1X or WPA3-Enterprise.

Encryption: Selecting appropriate standards (e.g., AES-256) for data confidentiality.

Network Segmentation: Isolating different types of traffic or user groups.

Pro Tip

Regularly revisit threat models as your network evolves or new threats emerge.
