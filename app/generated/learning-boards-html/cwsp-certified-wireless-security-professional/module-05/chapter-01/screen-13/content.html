Secure Channel Planning

WIPS Effectiveness

Proper channel separation ensures Wireless Intrusion Prevention Systems (WIPS) can clearly monitor the RF environment.

Avoiding Overlap

Overlapping channels can hinder WIPS, making it harder to detect malicious activity amidst legitimate traffic.

Benefit

Clear channels allow WIPS sensors to accurately identify and respond to threats.

Hardware Protection

Physical Security of APs

Prevent Tampering & Theft

Easily accessible APs can be tampered with, stolen, or used as physical entry points.

Secure Mounting

Mount APs securely in areas requiring physical access control (e.g., locked closets, high ceilings).

Key Takeaway

Both intelligent channel planning and robust physical security for APs are crucial for a comprehensive and resilient WLAN security posture.
