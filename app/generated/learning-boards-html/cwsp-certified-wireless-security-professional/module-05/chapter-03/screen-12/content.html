Practical Applications

Managing Guest Access and BYOD with NAC

NAC streamlines secure access for diverse users and devices, balancing convenience with robust security.

Guest Access Management

  • Automated Credential Provisioning: Simplifies temporary access for visitors.
  • Captive Portal Enforcement: Requires agreement to terms of service.
  • Isolated VLANs: Ensures guests have limited, internet-only access, separate from internal resources.

BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)

  • Streamlined Onboarding: Simplifies the process for employees to connect personal devices.
  • Posture Checks on Personal Devices: Ensures personal devices meet security standards.
  • Role-Based Segmentation: Assigns devices to appropriate network segments based on user roles and compliance.
Key Takeaway

NAC intelligently manages diverse access needs for guests and BYOD, ensuring security and compliance without sacrificing usability.
