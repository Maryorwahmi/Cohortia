Integrating NAC with 802.1X Authentication

NAC's effectiveness is significantly enhanced by its seamless integration with 802.1X authentication, providing a robust framework for initial device and user verification.

  • Port-Based Access Control: 802.1X is an IEEE standard for authenticating devices at the network port.
  • AP as Authenticator: Wireless APs act as authenticators, relaying requests from clients (supplicants).
  • RADIUS Integration: Authentication requests are sent to a RADIUS server, often the NAC policy server.
Why it's crucial

802.1X provides a standardized, secure method for initial identity verification, forming the essential foundation for NAC's advanced policy enforcement.
