Threat Landscape

WIDS: Common Threats Detected

Identifying Key Attack Patterns in the Wireless Environment

Rogue APs

Unauthorized Access Points connected to the network, bypassing security controls.

Evil Twin APs

Malicious APs mimicking legitimate networks to trick users into connecting.

Deauth/Disassoc Attacks

Flooding the airwaves with frames to disconnect legitimate clients, causing DoS.

Wireless Reconnaissance

Attackers scanning for vulnerabilities or gathering network information.

Client Misassociation

Legitimate clients attempting to connect to unauthorized or malicious APs.

Wireless DoS Attacks

Attacks aimed at disrupting or making the wireless network unavailable to users.

Key Takeaway

WIDS provides critical early warnings by detecting a broad spectrum of wireless threats, from rogue devices to active denial-of-service attempts.
