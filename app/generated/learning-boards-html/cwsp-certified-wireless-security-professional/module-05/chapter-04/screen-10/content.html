WIPS Functionality

WIPS: Client Misassociation & DoS Prevention

Protecting Clients and Network Availability

Client Misassociation Prevention

Detects and prevents legitimate clients from connecting to unauthorized or malicious APs, protecting them from 'evil twin' attacks.

Wireless DoS Attack Mitigation

Identifies and blocks traffic patterns indicative of denial-of-service attacks (e.g., excessive deauthentication frames), ensuring network availability.

Key Takeaway

WIPS actively safeguards both individual clients from malicious connections and the entire network from disruptive denial-of-service attacks.

WIPS Protection Scenarios

Client Misassociation

WIPS detects a client attempting to connect to an 'Evil Twin' AP and prevents the association, protecting the client from credential theft.

Wireless DoS Attack

WIPS identifies a flood of deauthentication frames from a malicious source and blocks the attacker, restoring network service.
