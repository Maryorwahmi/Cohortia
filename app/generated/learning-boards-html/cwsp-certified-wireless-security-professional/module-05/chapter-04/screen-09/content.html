WIPS: Rogue AP Containment

Neutralizing Unauthorized Access Points

What is a Rogue AP?

An unauthorized Access Point connected to your network, often unknowingly, creating a potential backdoor for attackers.

How WIPS Contains

  • Detects unauthorized APs.
  • Sends deauthentication frames to clients attempting to connect.
  • Disrupts client association, making the rogue AP unusable.
Key Takeaway

Rogue AP containment is a powerful WIPS feature that actively prevents clients from connecting to unauthorized access points, but requires careful legal and technical consideration.
