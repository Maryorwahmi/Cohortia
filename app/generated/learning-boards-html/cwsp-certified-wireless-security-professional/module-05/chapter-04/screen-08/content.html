WIPS: Active Threat Prevention

Moving Beyond Detection to Real-time Countermeasures

What is WIPS?

A Wireless Intrusion Prevention System (WIPS) is an active security solution that detects wireless threats and automatically takes countermeasures to prevent or mitigate them in real-time.

Key Differentiator

Unlike WIDS, WIPS doesn't just alert; it actively intervenes to stop attacks, providing a proactive defense posture.

Key Takeaway

WIPS offers real-time, active defense against wireless threats, making it essential for environments requiring immediate threat neutralization.

WIDS vs. WIPS: Action Comparison
WIDS (Detection)

Monitors RF, identifies threats, generates alerts.

WIPS (Prevention)

Monitors RF, identifies threats, generates alerts, AND takes active countermeasures.

Caution: Active Intervention

WIPS countermeasures, like rogue AP containment, must be used with extreme caution and legal review due to interference with radio transmissions.
