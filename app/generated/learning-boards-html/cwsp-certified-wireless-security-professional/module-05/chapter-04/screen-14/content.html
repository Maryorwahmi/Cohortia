Key Concepts to Remember

Essential Terms for Wireless Security

Wireless Firewall

Inspects and filters wireless network traffic based on predefined rules, specific to 802.11 protocols.

WIDS

A passive system that monitors the RF spectrum for suspicious activity and alerts administrators to potential wireless threats.

WIPS

An active system that detects wireless threats and automatically takes countermeasures to prevent or mitigate them in real-time.

Summary Takeaway

These core systems form the backbone of a robust wireless security strategy, each contributing a unique layer of defense from filtering to active prevention.
