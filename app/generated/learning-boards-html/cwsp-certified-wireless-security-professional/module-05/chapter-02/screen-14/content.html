User Governance

The Wireless Acceptable Use Policy

An Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for wireless networks defines how users are permitted to use the network, what activities are prohibited, and the consequences of violations.

Permitted Use: Clearly outlines allowed network activities.

Prohibited Activities: Forbids illegal downloads, malicious content, excessive bandwidth use.

Consequences: Specifies disciplinary actions for policy violations.

Key Takeaway

A clear and well-communicated AUP is vital for governing wireless network usage, protecting the organization from misuse, and fostering responsible user behavior.

AUP Communication & Enforcement
Onboarding

Introduce AUP during new employee setup.

Acknowledgement

Require users to formally accept terms.

Regular Review

Update AUP as technology and threats evolve.

Consequence Enforcement

Consistent application of penalties for violations.

Common Mistake

Having an AUP that is too vague or not regularly communicated, leading to user confusion and inconsistent enforcement.
