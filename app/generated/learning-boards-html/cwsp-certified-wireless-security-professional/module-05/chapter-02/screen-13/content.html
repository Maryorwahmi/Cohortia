Incident Response

The 6 Steps of Rogue AP Response

When a rogue AP is detected, a clear incident response plan is essential to identify, classify, contain, eradicate, recover, and learn from the incident.

1

Identification: Confirm it's a rogue AP.

2

Classification: Determine if benign or malicious.

3

Containment: Disconnect or jam the device.

4

Eradication: Remove device and investigate origin.

5

Recovery: Restore network to secure state.

6

Lessons Learned: Update policies, educate users.

Key Takeaway

A structured 6-step incident response plan ensures efficient and effective handling of rogue APs, minimizing their impact and preventing recurrence.

Rogue AP Response Flow

Common Mistake

Failing to have a clear, documented incident response plan for rogue APs, leading to disorganized and ineffective remediation efforts.
