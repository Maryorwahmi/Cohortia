Key Concepts: Policy Foundations

Essential terms for robust wireless security policies.

BYOD

A policy allowing employees to use personal devices for work, requiring specific security controls.

MDM

Software used to enforce policies, manage, and secure mobile devices within an organization.

MAM

Focuses on managing and securing individual applications on mobile devices, often for corporate apps.

Summary Takeaway

BYOD, MDM, and MAM are interconnected concepts forming the basis for managing and securing personal devices in an enterprise wireless environment.
