Guest Policy Details

Comprehensive Guest Network Controls

Guest policies dictate access methods, usage restrictions, and monitoring, ensuring a controlled and secure experience while strictly isolating traffic.

Captive Portals: Controlled onboarding with terms acceptance or registration.

Session Management: Timeouts and bandwidth limits for fair usage.

Content Filtering & Logging: Block malicious sites and record activity.

Key Takeaway

Effective guest policies combine controlled access via captive portals with usage restrictions and monitoring, ensuring security and accountability without compromising internal resources.

Prohibited Activity
