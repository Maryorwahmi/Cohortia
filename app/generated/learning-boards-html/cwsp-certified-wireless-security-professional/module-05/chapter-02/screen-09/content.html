Guest Network Architecture

Achieving Network Segmentation

Guest networks must operate on separate VLANs with their own dedicated internet egress, ensuring complete segmentation from sensitive internal corporate resources.

Separate VLANs: Logically partition guest traffic from internal traffic.

Dedicated Egress: Guest internet traffic exits via its own gateway.

No Internal Path: Prevents guests from accessing internal servers.

Key Takeaway

Implementing guest networks with separate VLANs and dedicated internet egress is fundamental to achieving robust isolation and protecting internal corporate resources.
