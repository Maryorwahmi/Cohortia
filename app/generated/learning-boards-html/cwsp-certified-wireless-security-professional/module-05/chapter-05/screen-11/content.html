Proactive Measures

Continuous Monitoring & Employee Training

Strengthening wireless security through vigilance and human awareness.

Continuous Monitoring

Ongoing collection and analysis of security data to detect threats in real-time.

  • •WIPS alerts for intrusion prevention.
  • •SIEM systems for log analysis and correlation.
  • •Network performance monitoring for anomalies.

Employee Training & Awareness

Educating users to reduce the attack surface and strengthen human defenses.

  • •Risks of rogue APs and Wi-Fi phishing.
  • •Secure BYOD practices and strong passwords.
  • •Importance of reporting security incidents.
Key Takeaway

Continuous monitoring detects threats in real-time, while employee training empowers users to be a strong defense layer.
