Operational Excellence

Thorough Documentation & Regular Reviews

Maintaining security, facilitating audits, and ensuring business continuity.

Key Documentation Areas

  • •Wireless network design and architecture.
  • •Detailed configurations of all wireless devices.
  • •Wireless security policies (BYOD, guest access).
  • •Incident response procedures for wireless threats.
Key Takeaway

Thorough, regularly updated documentation is vital for maintaining security, facilitating audits, and ensuring business continuity.

Documentation Lifecycle

Create: Document initial design, configurations, and policies.

Update: Reflect changes in network, threats, and regulations.

Review: Annually or after significant changes, ensure accuracy and relevance.
