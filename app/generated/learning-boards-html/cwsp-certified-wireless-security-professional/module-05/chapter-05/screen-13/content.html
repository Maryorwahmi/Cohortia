Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Compliance & Auditing

Essential terms for understanding wireless security regulations and assessments.

PCI DSS

Ensures secure handling of credit card information.

HIPAA

Protects patients' Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI).

GDPR

Regulates collection, storage, and processing of personal data in the EU.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these regulatory frameworks is fundamental for building compliant and secure wireless networks.
