WPA3-Enterprise: Transition & Safety Notes

Preparing your network for WPA3 and ensuring secure implementation.

WPA3 Transition Steps

  • Hardware Support: Ensure APs and WLCs support WPA3 and its stronger cryptographic suites.
  • RADIUS Server Update: Verify your RADIUS server supports WPA3-compatible EAP methods and algorithms.
  • Client Updates: Ensure client devices (laptops, phones) have WPA3-compatible supplicants.
  • Phased Deployment: Consider a mixed mode initially to support both WPA2 and WPA3 clients during transition.
Why Update Clients?

Client updates are crucial for them to negotiate the stronger WPA3 security protocols and benefit from enhanced protection, preventing fallback to weaker modes.

Critical Safety Notes

  • Strong Shared Secrets: Always use unique, complex shared secrets for RADIUS client communication.
  • Certificate Rotation: Implement a schedule to rotate server certificates before expiration.
  • Audit RADIUS Logs: Regularly review logs for suspicious authentication attempts or anomalies.
  • Harden RADIUS Server: Keep the server patched, minimize services, and never expose it directly to the internet.
Key Takeaway

A successful WPA3-Enterprise transition involves comprehensive updates across infrastructure and clients, coupled with continuous vigilance and hardening of the RADIUS server itself.
