Common PKI Mistakes and Mitigation

Avoiding pitfalls for a robust certificate infrastructure.

Key Mitigation Strategies

  • **Use Trusted CAs:** Always use certificates from public or enterprise CAs trusted by clients.
  • **Monitor Expiration:** Implement automated monitoring and renewal for all certificates.
  • **Verify Revocation:** Ensure CRLs are accessible and up-to-date, or OCSP is configured.
  • **Protect Private Keys:** Never reuse private keys; store them securely (e.g., HSMs).
  • **Secure CA Infrastructure:** Protect your Certificate Authority with stringent security policies.

Common PKI Mistakes

**Self-Signed Certificates:** Not trusted by clients by default, leading to warnings and security risks.

**Expired Certificates:** Lead to authentication failures and service disruptions.

Key Takeaway

Proactive management of trusted certificates, vigilant monitoring of their lifecycle, and rigorous protection of private keys are essential to prevent common PKI vulnerabilities.
