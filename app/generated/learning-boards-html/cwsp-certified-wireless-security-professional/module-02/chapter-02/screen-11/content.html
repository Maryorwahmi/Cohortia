PKI Process

The Certificate Lifecycle in PKI

From request to verification, ensuring digital trust.

01

Certificate Request

Server/client generates a key pair and a Certificate Signing Request (CSR) containing its public key.

02

Issuance

The CA verifies identity, signs the CSR, and issues a digital certificate containing the public key.

03

Deployment

The issued certificate is installed on the respective server or client device for use in authentication.

04

Verification

During authentication, parties verify the certificate's signature, validity period, and revocation status (CRLs/OCSP).

Key Takeaway

The certificate lifecycle, from request to verification, is a structured process ensuring the authenticity and validity of digital identities within a PKI.

Common Misconception

A certificate, once issued, is not permanently valid. It can expire or be revoked, requiring continuous management and checks.
