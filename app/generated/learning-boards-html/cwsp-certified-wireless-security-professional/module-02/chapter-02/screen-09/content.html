Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) Explained

The system of trust for digital identities.

Components of PKI

  • **Digital Certificates:** Electronic documents binding public keys to identities.
  • **Certificate Authorities (CAs):** Trusted entities that issue and manage certificates.
  • **Registration Authorities (RAs):** Verify identities before CA issuance.
  • **Certificate Repositories:** Databases for storing and retrieving certificates.
  • **Revocation Mechanisms:** Methods like CRLs and OCSP to invalidate compromised certificates.

Why PKI is Essential

**Establishes Trust:** Verifies identities of users, devices, and servers in digital interactions.

**Enables Secure Communication:** Underpins TLS/SSL, VPNs, and secure wireless authentication.

Key Takeaway

PKI is a comprehensive system that uses digital certificates and CAs to establish and manage trust, which is fundamental for secure digital communications.
