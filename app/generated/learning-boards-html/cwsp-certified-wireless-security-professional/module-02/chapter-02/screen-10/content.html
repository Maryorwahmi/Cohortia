PKI in Action

PKI's Role in EAP-TLS Authentication

Establishing a chain of trust for mutual authentication.

Certificate Requirements

  • **RADIUS Server:** Needs a certificate from a CA trusted by clients.
  • **Client Devices:** Each needs a unique certificate from a CA trusted by the RADIUS server.
  • **Mutual Trust:** Both parties must trust the CA that issued the other's certificate.
  • **Chain of Trust:** Root CA certificates must be present in respective trust stores.

Preventing Attacks

**Rogue AP Protection:** Client verifies server's certificate, preventing connection to malicious access points.

**Unauthorized Access:** Server verifies client's certificate, preventing unapproved devices from joining.

Key Takeaway

PKI enables EAP-TLS's mutual authentication by ensuring both server and client certificates are issued by mutually trusted Certificate Authorities.
