Learning Objectives: EAP & PKI Foundations

01

Differentiate EAP Types

Distinguish between PEAP, EAP-TLS, and EAP-TTLS, understanding their unique operational flows and security implications. This skill is vital for selecting the appropriate authentication method for various network requirements.

Explain PKI's Role
