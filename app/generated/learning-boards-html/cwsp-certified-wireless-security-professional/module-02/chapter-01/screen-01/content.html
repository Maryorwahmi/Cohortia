Understanding 802.1X and EAP

The Foundation of Enterprise Wireless Security

Securing enterprise wireless networks demands more than just a simple password. This chapter explores how 802.1X and EAP provide a robust framework for authentication, ensuring only authorized users and devices gain network access.

Key Insight

802.1X and EAP are critical for scalable, accountable, and dynamic authentication in enterprise wireless environments, overcoming the limitations of shared keys.
