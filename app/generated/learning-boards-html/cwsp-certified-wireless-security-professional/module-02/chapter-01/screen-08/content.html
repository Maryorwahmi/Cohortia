Initiating the Authentication Exchange

From Connection to Identity Request

01

Supplicant Associates with AP

The client device attempts to connect to an 802.1X-enabled Access Point. The AP initially places the client in an unauthorized state.

02

AP Sends EAP-Request/Identity

The Authenticator (AP) sends an EAP-Request/Identity message to the Supplicant, asking for its identity.

03

Supplicant Responds with Identity

The Supplicant replies with an EAP-Response/Identity, providing its username or other identifying information.

The Initial Handshake

This initial exchange establishes communication and allows the authentication server to prepare for the specific authentication method required.

Example Scenario

A new employee's laptop connects to the office Wi-Fi. The AP immediately asks for the laptop's identity before allowing any internet access.
