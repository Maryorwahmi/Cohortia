Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Authentication Flood

An attacker floods an AP with fake authentication requests, consuming its resources.

CTS/RTS Flood

An attacker floods the network with fake Clear-to-Send/Request-to-Send frames, making other devices believe the channel is busy.

Beacon Flood

An attacker floods the airwaves with a large number of fake beacon frames, causing confusion and potential client resource exhaustion.

aireplay-ng

A tool from the Aircrack-ng suite used to inject frames, including deauthentication frames, for DoS attacks.

802.11w (MFP)

An IEEE standard that adds cryptographic protection to certain 802.11 management frames, preventing spoofed deauthentication/disassociation attacks.

WIPS

A system that actively detects and prevents wireless attacks, including DoS, by taking automated countermeasures.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding various DoS attack types like authentication, CTS/RTS, and beacon floods, along with tools like aireplay-ng and crucial defenses like 802.11w and WIPS, is key to comprehensive wireless security.
