WIDS/WIPS: Your Wireless Sentinels

Wireless Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (WIDS/WIPS) are crucial for monitoring airwaves and identifying patterns indicative of DoS attacks.

High Deauth/Disassoc Frames

Detects unusually high volumes of deauthentication or disassociation frames.

Excessive Auth Requests

Identifies a surge of authentication requests from single or multiple MAC addresses.

RTS/CTS Spikes

Monitors for sudden increases in Clear-to-Send/Request-to-Send traffic.

Example: Deauth Flood Alert

Real-time Threat Identification

A WIPS detects a sudden, sustained burst of deauthentication frames targeting multiple clients on a corporate Wi-Fi network. It immediately triggers an alert to the security operations center, providing details on the suspected attacker's MAC address and the affected AP. This allows administrators to quickly investigate and initiate countermeasures, preventing a prolonged outage.

Key Takeaway

WIDS/WIPS are essential for detecting wireless DoS attacks by monitoring for specific anomalous traffic patterns and can initiate automated responses to mitigate threats.
