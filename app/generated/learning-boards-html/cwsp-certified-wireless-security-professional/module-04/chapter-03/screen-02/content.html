01

Categorize Wireless DoS Attacks

Identify and distinguish between various types of wireless Denial-of-Service attacks, such as deauthentication floods, jamming, and beacon floods. This skill is vital for accurately assessing threats and selecting appropriate defense mechanisms.

Verified Competency Milestone
02

Understand Deauth/Disassoc Floods

Grasp the underlying mechanics and disruptive impact of deauthentication and disassociation flood attacks. This includes understanding the 802.11 protocol vulnerabilities that these attacks exploit to disconnect clients.

Verified Competency Milestone
