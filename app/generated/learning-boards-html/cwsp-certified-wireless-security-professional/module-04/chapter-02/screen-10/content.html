Sophisticated Evil Twins with `hostapd`

Creating more robust and trustworthy malicious access points.

`hostapd` Capabilities

  • •

    WPA2 Encryption: Configure fake APs with robust encryption, appearing more legitimate.

  • •

    DHCP/DNS Integration: Seamlessly combine with dnsmasq for full network services.

  • •

    Fake RADIUS: Capture enterprise credentials by mimicking a RADIUS server.

Ethical Note: Legal and Ethical Boundaries

Setting up Evil Twin or Rogue APs without explicit permission is highly illegal and unethical. These techniques are for educational and defensive purposes only.

Key Takeaway

`hostapd` enables advanced Evil Twin configurations, including WPA2 and RADIUS, making them harder to distinguish from legitimate networks.

`hostapd` Configuration Example
interface=wlan0mon
driver=nl80211
ssid=MySecureNetwork
channel=6
hw_mode=g
wpa=2
wpa_passphrase=SuperSecretKey
