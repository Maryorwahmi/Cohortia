Detecting Evil Twins: Subtle Clues

Identifying impersonation attacks that mimic legitimate networks.

Key Indicators

  • •

    Client Alerts: Warnings about duplicate SSIDs or certificate mismatches.

  • •

    Signal Fluctuations: Sudden, stronger signals for a known SSID.

  • •

    Connection Issues: Intermittent connectivity or unexpected redirects to login pages.

  • •

    MAC Address Changes: Sudden alteration of a known SSID's BSSID.

  • •

    WIDS/WIPS Alerts: Detection of deauthentication floods or suspicious AP configurations.

Pro Tip: Verify Authenticity

Always verify the authenticity of a network, especially in public places. Check certificates for enterprise networks or ask staff for official network names.

Key Takeaway

Evil Twins are detected by observing anomalies in client behavior, network signals, and MAC addresses, often aided by WIDS/WIPS solutions.
