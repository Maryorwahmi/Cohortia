Key Concepts: Understanding the Threats

Essential terms for wireless security.

Rogue AP

An unauthorized access point connected to a legitimate network, providing an unmonitored entry point.

Evil Twin

A malicious access point configured to impersonate a legitimate one, designed to trick users into connecting.

MITM Attack

An attack where the attacker secretly relays and possibly alters communication between two parties.

Summary Takeaway

Rogue APs, Evil Twins, and MITM attacks are distinct but related threats that exploit network vulnerabilities and user trust through deception.
