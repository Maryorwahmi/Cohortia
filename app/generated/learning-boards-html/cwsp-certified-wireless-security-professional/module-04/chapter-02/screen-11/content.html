Detecting Rogue APs: Proactive Monitoring

Identifying unauthorized access points on your network.

Detection Methods

  • •

    Regular Wireless Surveys: Conduct physical scans of your environment using tools like Kismet.

  • •

    Wireless Intrusion Detection Systems (WIDS): Deploy systems to continuously monitor for unauthorized APs.

  • •

    Physical Audits: Periodically inspect network ports and physical locations for unauthorized devices.

Common Mistake: Incomplete Monitoring

Relying solely on network-based detection without physical verification can miss hidden or misconfigured rogue APs.

Key Takeaway

Proactive wireless surveys, WIDS, and physical audits are crucial for detecting and neutralizing Rogue APs before they compromise network security.

Detection Flow

2. Identify Unknown APs
