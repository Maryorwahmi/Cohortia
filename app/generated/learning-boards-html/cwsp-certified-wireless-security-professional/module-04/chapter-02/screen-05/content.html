Definition

Rogue Access Points: Unauthorized Entry

An unapproved AP connected to a legitimate network, bypassing security controls.

Key Characteristics

  • •

    Unauthorized Device: Not approved by network administrators.

  • •

    Internal Connection: Physically connected to the legitimate wired network.

  • •

    Bypasses Security: Creates an unmonitored entry point into the secure network.

Key Takeaway

Rogue APs are internal threats that provide unauthorized network access, often due to insider actions or physical breaches.
