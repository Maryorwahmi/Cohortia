The Myth of the 'Hidden' SSID

Obscurity is Not Security

What is a Hidden SSID?

An Access Point configured not to broadcast its SSID in beacon frames, often mistakenly believed to be invisible.

The Security Flaw

Disabling broadcast only hides the SSID from beacon frames; it's still transmitted in other management frames, making it discoverable.

Why it Matters

Relying on obscurity rather than strong encryption and authentication leaves networks vulnerable to easy discovery and potential attacks.

Analogy Card

Imagine trying to hide a house by simply turning off its porch light. While it might be less visible at night, it's still there, and anyone who knows its address or sees someone enter can easily find it. The house's security comes from its locks and alarm system, not its visibility.

Key Takeaway

Security through obscurity is a weak strategy; robust wireless security relies on strong encryption, authentication, and proper access controls.
