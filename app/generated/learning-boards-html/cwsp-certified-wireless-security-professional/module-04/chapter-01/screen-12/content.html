Kismet's Advanced Features & Use Cases

Aggregating Intelligence for Comprehensive Views

Aggregated Information

Kismet collects and aggregates data over time, building a comprehensive picture of the wireless environment, including channel usage and signal strength trends.

GPS Integration

Integrates with GPS to map out wireless networks geographically, providing visual insights into coverage and physical security.

Anomaly Detection

Detects new or unusual devices, such as rogue APs, by comparing current observations against historical data.

Site Survey Use Case

A security team uses Kismet with GPS to conduct a comprehensive site survey, identifying areas of weak signal, unauthorized APs, and potential physical security vulnerabilities in their wireless coverage.

Key Takeaway

Kismet's long-term aggregation and geographical mapping capabilities provide a powerful, holistic view for proactive wireless security and threat detection.
