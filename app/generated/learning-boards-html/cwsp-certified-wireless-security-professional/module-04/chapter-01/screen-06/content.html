Revealing Hidden SSIDs: Probe Frames

The Client's Role in Exposure

Probe Request

A management frame sent by a client device containing the SSID it wishes to connect to, used to find non-broadcast networks.

Probe Response

A management frame sent by an AP in response to a probe request, confirming the presence of the requested SSID and often containing it.

Plain Text Transmission

Both probe requests and responses transmit the SSID in plain text, making it easily capturable by passive monitoring tools.

Client Vulnerability Example

A client device configured for a hidden home network will continuously send probe requests for that SSID, even when in public places. An attacker can capture these requests, revealing the home network's SSID.

Key Takeaway

Client devices, by actively seeking hidden SSIDs, inadvertently broadcast them in probe frames, making passive discovery straightforward.
