Kismet: Passive Wireless Reconnaissance

A Network Detector and IDS

Kismet's Role

A passive wireless network detector, sniffer, and intrusion detection system that identifies networks, clients, and can map wireless environments.

Passive Operation

Kismet operates purely passively, collecting packets without sending any itself, making it stealthy and minimizing its footprint on the network.

Key Capabilities

Identifies hidden SSIDs, detects wireless clients, infers network names, and can even detect non-802.11 devices like Bluetooth.

Rogue AP Detection Example

Kismet can continuously monitor your network perimeter. If a new, unauthorized AP appears, Kismet can alert you, indicating a potential rogue device or an attacker attempting to establish a foothold.

Key Takeaway

Kismet provides a stealthy, comprehensive overview of the wireless environment, making it invaluable for both reconnaissance and intrusion detection.
