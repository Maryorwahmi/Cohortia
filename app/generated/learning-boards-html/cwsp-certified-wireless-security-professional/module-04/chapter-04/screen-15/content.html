Key Concepts

What to remember from this chapter

Client-Side Exploitation

Exploiting vulnerabilities in software or drivers on user devices (laptops, smartphones) connected to a wireless network.

Wi-Fi Protected Setup (WPS)

A feature designed to simplify connecting devices to a wireless network, but with a critical PIN-based vulnerability.

WPS PIN Brute-Forcing

An attack that exploits the WPS PIN design flaw to quickly guess the PIN and retrieve the WPA/WPA2 passphrase.

Summary Takeaway

Advanced wireless exploitation targets client-side software and AP vulnerabilities like WPS, requiring a deep understanding of attack vectors and tools like Reaver.
