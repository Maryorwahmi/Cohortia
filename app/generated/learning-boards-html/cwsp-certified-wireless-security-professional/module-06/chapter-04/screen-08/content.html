`EAPHammer` - Automated Evil Twin

Streamlining Enterprise Credential Harvesting

Why `EAPHammer`?

  • More automated framework compared to hostapd-wpe.
  • Simplifies complex setup for various EAP methods.
  • Automatically generates certificates, sets up AP, and captures hashes.
  • Reduces manual configuration and potential errors.

Key Benefits of Automation

EAPHammer handles the intricacies of certificate generation and EAP negotiation, making it significantly easier to trick client devices into connecting and authenticating, even with some forms of client-side validation.

`EAPHammer` Command Example

A single command can launch a full Evil Twin attack, automating the setup and credential capture process.

sudo eaphammer --interface wlan0mon --ssid Corporate_WiFi --channel 6 --creds
  • --interface wlan0mon: Specifies the wireless interface to use.
  • --ssid Corporate_WiFi: Sets the SSID for the rogue AP.
  • --channel 6: Designates the operating channel.
  • --creds: Activates credential capture.
Key Takeaway

EAPHammer streamlines Evil Twin attacks through automation, simplifying certificate generation and AP setup to efficiently capture credentials from enterprise networks.
