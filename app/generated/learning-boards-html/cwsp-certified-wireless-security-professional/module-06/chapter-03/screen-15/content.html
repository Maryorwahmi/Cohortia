Chapter Review

Key Concepts: WPA/WPA2 & WPS Attacks

Essential terms from WPA/WPA2-PSK and WPS exploitation.

Deauthentication Attack

Sending forged deauthentication frames to a client to disconnect it from an AP, often used to force a WPA/WPA2 handshake capture.

Dictionary Attack

Attempting to crack a passphrase by trying a list of common words and phrases from a wordlist against a captured handshake.

Brute-Force Attack

Systematically trying every possible combination of characters until the correct passphrase is found.

WPS

A security standard that simplifies the connection process but contains a design flaw allowing PIN brute-forcing.

Pixie-Dust Attack

An offline WPS attack that exploits a vulnerability in some APs to instantly crack the WPS PIN.

Summary Takeaway

Active attacks like deauthentication, dictionary, and brute-force target WPA/WPA2-PSK, while WPS is vulnerable to PIN cracking via Reaver and the rapid Pixie-Dust attack.
