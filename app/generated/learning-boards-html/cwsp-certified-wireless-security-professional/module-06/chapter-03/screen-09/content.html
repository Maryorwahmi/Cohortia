WPA/WPA2 Exploitation

Capturing the WPA/WPA2-PSK Handshake

Using airodump-ng to target and capture the 4-way handshake.

Monitor Mode & Targeting

Place your wireless adapter in monitor mode and use airodump-ng to target the specific AP by its channel and BSSID.

Handshake Detection

airodump-ng will indicate "WPA Handshake" in its output once a complete 4-way handshake has been successfully captured.

Key Takeaway

A successful WPA/WPA2-PSK handshake capture with airodump-ng is the foundational step for any offline cracking attempt against the network.

# Capture WPA/WPA2-PSK handshake
sudo airodump-ng --channel 11 --bssid 11:22:33:44:55:66 --write wpa_capture wlan0mon
Explanation:
  • --channel 11: Specifies the target AP's channel.
  • --bssid 11:22:33:44:55:66: Filters for the target AP's MAC address.
  • --write wpa_capture: Saves captured data to files prefixed with 'wpa_capture'.
  • wlan0mon: Your wireless interface in monitor mode.
Pro Tip

Monitor the airodump-ng output carefully for the "WPA Handshake" message to confirm successful capture before stopping the process.
