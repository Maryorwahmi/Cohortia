Chapter Goals

Learning Objectives

What you'll achieve in this chapter.

Bloom's Taxonomy:
LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Recall WEP Vulnerabilities

Identify the fundamental design flaws that render WEP easily exploitable, focusing on its weak Initialization Vector (IV) implementation. Understanding these weaknesses is crucial for comprehending the evolution of wireless security.

Verified Competency Milestone
02

Perform WEP Cracking

Execute a WEP cracking attack using the Aircrack-ng suite, including capturing packets and performing ARP replay attacks. This hands-on experience demonstrates the practical steps involved in exploiting WEP networks.

Verified Competency Milestone
03

Capture WPA/WPA2-PSK Handshake

Understand the process of capturing a WPA/WPA2-PSK 4-way handshake, a critical step for offline dictionary and brute-force attacks. This involves monitoring network traffic and potentially forcing client reauthentication.

Verified Competency Milestone
