Penetration Test Reporting

Recommendations and Appendices

Providing actionable steps and supporting evidence for remediation.

Recommendations

Clear, actionable steps for remediation, prioritizing critical vulnerabilities. Examples: firmware upgrades, strong passphrases, 802.1X, network segmentation, WIPS.

Appendices

Supporting evidence like screenshots, captured hashes, or log files, providing proof of concept and additional context.

Key Takeaway

Effective reporting culminates in specific, prioritized recommendations backed by evidence, enabling organizations to move from vulnerability identification to concrete security improvements.

Example Recommendations

Upgrade Firmware

Regularly update AP firmware to patch known vulnerabilities.

Strong Passphrases

Implement complex, unique passphrases for WPA2/WPA3-Personal networks.

Network Segmentation

Use VLANs to isolate different network segments (e.g., guest vs. internal).

Deploy WIPS

Implement Wireless Intrusion Prevention Systems to detect and mitigate attacks.

Misconception: One-Size-Fits-All Recommendations

Providing generic recommendations without considering the client's specific environment, budget, or existing infrastructure is a common pitfall. Effective recommendations are tailored, practical, and prioritize risks based on the client's unique context, ensuring they can actually be implemented and make a difference.
