Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas to remember from this chapter.

Karma Attack

A client-side attack exploiting how devices probe for known networks, tricking them into connecting to a rogue AP.

Wi-Fi Pineapple

A hardware device for wireless auditing, facilitating rogue AP and client-side attacks with a user-friendly interface.

Post-Exploitation

The phase after gaining initial access, involving maintaining control, escalating privileges, pivoting, and data exfiltration.

Summary Takeaway

From client-side deception to maintaining control, these concepts are central to understanding the practical execution and long-term implications of wireless network compromises.
