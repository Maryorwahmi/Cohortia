Core Concept

Wi-Fi Direct and BLE Vulnerabilities

Exploring security weaknesses in device-to-device communication protocols.

Wi-Fi Direct Weaknesses

Often uses WPS for setup, making it vulnerable to PIN cracking. Misconfigurations or outdated firmware can lead to unauthorized access or data exposure.

Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Risks

Commonly suffers from weak authentication, lack of encryption, or easily guessable PINs, especially in IoT devices.

Exploitation Tools

Tools like GATTacker and Bettercap can scan, intercept, and spoof BLE devices, leading to compromise or data exfiltration.

Key Takeaway

New attack surfaces in Wi-Fi Direct and BLE require dedicated assessment due to their unique vulnerabilities and integration with IoT ecosystems.

Vulnerability Landscape

WPS Exploitation

Wi-Fi Direct's reliance on WPS makes it vulnerable to brute-force PIN cracking, leading to unauthorized network access.

Weak BLE Authentication

Many BLE devices use simple or default PINs, or lack encryption, making them easy targets for interception and spoofing.

Firmware & Misconfigurations

Outdated firmware or incorrect settings in Wi-Fi Direct can expose devices and data to unauthorized access.

IoT Ecosystem Bridge

These protocols often connect traditional networks to IoT, creating a broader attack surface that must be secured.

Example Scenario: Smart Home Compromise

An attacker uses Bettercap to scan for BLE devices in a smart home. They discover a smart lightbulb using a default, unencrypted pairing process. By intercepting the pairing, the attacker gains control of the bulb, and potentially other connected devices if the network is poorly segmented. This demonstrates how a seemingly minor BLE vulnerability can be a gateway to broader system compromise.
