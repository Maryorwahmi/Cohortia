Interpreting Wireless Traffic: Beacons and Probes in Detail

Understanding the significance of beacon frames, probe requests, and probe responses.

Key Frame Types & Insights
Beacon Frames: AP announcements of presence, SSID, and security settings. Reveals network availability.
Probe Requests: Client queries for specific or any available networks. Can reveal hidden SSIDs.
Probe Responses: AP replies to probe requests, confirming presence and providing network details.

Common Vulnerability: Hidden SSIDs are often revealed by client probe requests, as clients broadcast the SSID when trying to reconnect, defeating the 'hidden' aspect.

Beacon Frame Example (Simplified)
Frame 1: Beacon
  Source: AP_MAC_ADDRESS
  Destination: Broadcast
  SSID: "MySecureNetwork"
  Channel: 6
  Encryption: WPA2-PSK (AES)
Probe Request Example (Simplified)
Frame 2: Probe Request
  Source: CLIENT_MAC_ADDRESS
  Destination: Broadcast
  Requested SSID: "MySecretWiFi"
Probe Response Example (Simplified)
Frame 3: Probe Response
  Source: AP_MAC_ADDRESS
  Destination: CLIENT_MAC_ADDRESS
  SSID: "MySecretWiFi"
  Channel: 6
  Encryption: WPA2-PSK (AES)
Key Takeaway

Analyzing beacon frames, probe requests, and probe responses provides deep insights into network configurations, security, and client behavior, even for hidden networks.
