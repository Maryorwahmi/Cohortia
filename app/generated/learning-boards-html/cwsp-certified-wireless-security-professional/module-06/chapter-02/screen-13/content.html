Advantages and Limitations of Passive Scanning

Weighing the benefits and drawbacks of non-intrusive wireless reconnaissance.

Key Considerations
Stealth: Undetectable by IDS, leaves no logs.
Speed: Can be slower, dependent on network activity.
Data Richness: Gathers SSIDs, BSSIDs, channels, encryption types, clients.
Completeness: May miss inactive networks or those on unmonitored channels.

Ethical Consideration: Collecting client probe requests can reveal personal network histories, raising privacy concerns. Always ensure you have proper authorization and adhere to ethical guidelines.

Passive vs. Active Scanning

Passive: Stealthy, observational, low risk of detection.

Active: Interactive, faster, higher risk of detection.

Key Takeaway

Passive scanning is crucial for stealthy initial reconnaissance, but its limitations mean it should be complemented by active methods for a complete security assessment.
