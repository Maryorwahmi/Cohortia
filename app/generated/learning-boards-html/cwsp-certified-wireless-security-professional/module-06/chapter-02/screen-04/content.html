Core Concept

What is Passive Scanning?

Passive scanning involves gathering information about a wireless network by simply listening to broadcasted traffic, without sending any active probes or requests that could be detected.

Listen Only

Observe and collect data transmitted over the airwaves without active interaction.

Avoid Detection

Prevents generating logs or alerting intrusion detection systems.

Build Comprehensive Picture

Collects data like SSIDs, BSSIDs, channels, encryption, and client devices.

Key Takeaway

Passive reconnaissance is the stealthy foundation for understanding a target's wireless infrastructure and identifying vulnerabilities.
