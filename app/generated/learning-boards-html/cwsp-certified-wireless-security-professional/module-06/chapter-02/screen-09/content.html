Practical `airodump-ng`: Identifying Network Details

Deciphering the output of `airodump-ng` to understand network characteristics.

Key Output Columns
BSSID & ESSID: Unique AP MAC and network name.
PWR & Beacons: Signal strength and AP presence confirmation.
#Data & CH: Active traffic count and operating channel.
ENC, CIPHER, AUTH: Security protocols and authentication methods.

Example Scenario: You observe an AP with high 'Beacons' but low '#Data' and no clients. This could indicate an unused or misconfigured network, or even a honeypot, which is valuable intelligence for a security assessment.

Key Takeaway

Interpreting `airodump-ng` output is fundamental for quickly assessing network presence, activity, and security, guiding further reconnaissance efforts.
