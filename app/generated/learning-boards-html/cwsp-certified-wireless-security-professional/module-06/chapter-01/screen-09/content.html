PEN TEST PHASES

Phase 3 & 4: Vulnerability Analysis & Exploitation

Identifying Weaknesses and Leveraging Them

03

Vulnerability Analysis

Analyze gathered information against known vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, or outdated security practices to identify potential weaknesses.

  • Compare configurations to known flaws.
  • Prioritize critical weaknesses.
  • Understand potential impact.
04

Exploitation

Attempt to leverage identified vulnerabilities to gain unauthorized access or achieve a specific objective, demonstrating real-world impact.

  • Crack WPA2-PSK passphrases (e.g., aircrack-ng).
  • Exploit software flaws in access points.
  • Gain access to internal network segments.
Key Takeaway

Vulnerability analysis identifies weaknesses, while exploitation demonstrates their real-world impact, providing concrete evidence for remediation.
