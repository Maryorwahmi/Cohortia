PEN TEST PHASES

Phase 5 & 6: Post-Exploitation and Reporting

Maintaining Access and Documenting Findings

05

Post-Exploitation

If exploitation is successful, this phase involves maintaining access, escalating privileges, exploring the network, or exfiltrating data.

  • Maintain access to compromised systems.
  • Escalate privileges.
  • Explore further network segments.
  • Exfiltrate data to demonstrate impact.
06

Reporting

The final and most critical phase: documenting all findings, their impact, and clear recommendations for remediation in a comprehensive report.

  • Document all exploited vulnerabilities.
  • Detail the impact of the breach.
  • Provide clear, actionable remediation recommendations.
  • Deliver a comprehensive report to the client.
Key Takeaway

Post-exploitation reveals the full impact of a breach, while comprehensive reporting translates technical findings into actionable security improvements.
