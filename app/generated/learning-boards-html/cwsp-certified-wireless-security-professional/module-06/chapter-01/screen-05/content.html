CRITICAL CONSIDERATION

Ethical and Legal Boundaries

Always Get Explicit Authorization

The Importance of Authorization

Any intrusive interaction with a wireless network without explicit, written permission is illegal and can lead to severe legal consequences.

Rules of Engagement (RoE)

  • Formal document defining test scope.
  • Specifies SSIDs, IP ranges, locations, timeframes.
  • Outlines permitted and out-of-scope attacks.
  • Crucial for legal and ethical compliance.
Common Mistake

Exceeding the defined scope or testing unauthorized systems without explicit permission can lead to serious legal issues.
