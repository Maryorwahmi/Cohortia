Prevention Mechanism

Containing Rogue APs: Deauthentication

WIPS uses deauthentication frames to disconnect clients from rogue APs and disrupt rogue AP operations.

1

Rogue AP Detected

WIPS identifies an unauthorized access point.

2

Deauthentication Frames Sent

WIPS sends deauthentication frames to clients or the rogue AP.

3

Threat Contained

Clients disconnect, rogue AP operation disrupted.

Example Scenario

A WIPS detects a rogue AP broadcasting a 'Free_Guest_WiFi' SSID. It sends deauthentication frames to corporate laptops connecting to it, forcing them to reconnect to the secure enterprise network.

Key Takeaway

Deauthentication is a swift and effective WIPS action to break connections between clients and rogue APs, protecting users from potential threats.
