Incident Handling

Human Intervention: Manual Response Procedures

Manual response procedures are vital for complex WIPS incidents, ensuring human oversight and adaptability.

Alert Triage & Verification

Security personnel review, prioritize, and verify WIPS alerts to confirm legitimacy.

Investigation & Containment

Deeper investigation into confirmed incidents, followed by manual steps to contain and eradicate the threat.

Example Scenario

A WIPS detects a 'Rogue Access Point Bridging to Wired Network' alert. Automated actions deauthenticate clients, but a security analyst must manually locate and physically unplug the rogue AP, then collect evidence for forensic analysis.

Key Takeaway

Human-driven incident response, from verification to recovery, complements automated WIPS actions, providing essential adaptability and thoroughness for complex security incidents.
