Example Scenario

A WIPS detects a rogue AP. The SIEM correlates this alert with switch logs, identifying the exact wired port the rogue AP is connected to, providing immediate physical location context.

Key Takeaway

SIEM integration transforms WIPS from a standalone tool into a powerful component of a unified security ecosystem, enabling comprehensive threat visibility and rapid response.
