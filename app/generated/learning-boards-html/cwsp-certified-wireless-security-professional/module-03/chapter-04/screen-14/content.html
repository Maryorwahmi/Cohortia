What to Remember

Building a Resilient Wireless Defense

False Positive (WIPS context)

An incorrect prevention action taken against legitimate network activity, leading to service disruption.

Manual Response Procedures

Human-driven steps for investigating, containing, and recovering from security incidents.

Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)

A system that aggregates and analyzes security logs and events from various sources for centralized monitoring and incident response.

Summary Takeaway

A robust WIPS strategy integrates automated prevention with human-led incident response and SIEM platforms to provide a holistic and adaptive defense against wireless threats, while carefully managing the risk of false positives.
