Core Concept

Beyond Detection: The Power of WIPS Prevention

WIPS actively thwarts attacks and enforces security policies, moving beyond mere detection to neutralize threats before they cause harm.

  • WIPS identifies threats using advanced detection techniques.

  • It initiates automatic prevention actions to neutralize threats.

  • Requires careful planning and configuration to avoid unintended consequences.

Key Takeaway

WIPS transforms passive monitoring into active defense, but its power demands meticulous configuration to prevent legitimate service disruption.

Common Prevention Mechanisms
Rogue AP Containment

Deauthentication of clients/APs, wired port shutdown.

Malicious Client Containment

Client deauthentication/blocking, wireless honeypots.

DoS Attack Mitigation

Attack source deauthentication, rate limiting.
