Key Concepts to Remember

Reinforcing Core WIPS Principles

Automatic Prevention Actions

Automated countermeasures taken by a WIPS upon detecting a threat.

Deauthentication Frames

Management frames used by WIPS to disconnect clients from APs or to disrupt rogue APs.

Wired Port Shutdown

An aggressive WIPS action to disable a switch port connected to a detected rogue AP.

Summary Takeaway

WIPS leverages automatic prevention actions like deauthentication frames and wired port shutdown to actively neutralize wireless threats, forming the backbone of proactive wireless security.
