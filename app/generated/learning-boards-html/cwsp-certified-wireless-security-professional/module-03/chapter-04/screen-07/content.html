Client-Side Prevention

Neutralizing Malicious Clients

WIPS identifies and contains clients performing malicious activities to protect the network.

Client Deauthentication/Blocking

WIPS sends deauthentication frames to malicious clients, disconnecting them. It can also instruct legitimate APs to block the client's MAC address, preventing reconnection.

Wireless Honeypots/Decoys

Advanced WIPS can deploy decoy SSIDs or 'honeypot' APs to lure and contain malicious clients, isolating them for analysis away from the production network.

Example Scenario

A WIPS detects a client performing a deauthentication flood. It immediately sends deauthentication frames back to the attacker's device, disrupting their attack and protecting network availability.

Key Takeaway

Targeted client deauthentication, blocking, and honeypots are effective WIPS tools for containing and analyzing malicious client behavior, safeguarding network integrity.
