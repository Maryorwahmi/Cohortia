Attack Response

Mitigating Denial-of-Service (DoS) Attacks

WIPS actively disrupts DoS attacks by targeting the source of malicious traffic, ensuring network availability.

1

DoS Attack Detected

WIPS identifies a deauthentication flood or similar DoS activity.

2

Attack Source Deauthentication

WIPS sends deauthentication frames back to the attacker's device.

3

Attack Disrupted

DoS attack is neutralized, network availability restored.

Example Scenario

An attacker initiates a deauthentication flood against a corporate AP. The WIPS identifies the attacker's MAC address and sends targeted deauthentication frames, forcing the attacker's device offline and stopping the flood.

Key Takeaway

WIPS provides a critical defense against wireless DoS attacks by actively disrupting the attacker's ability to sustain the malicious traffic, ensuring network uptime.
