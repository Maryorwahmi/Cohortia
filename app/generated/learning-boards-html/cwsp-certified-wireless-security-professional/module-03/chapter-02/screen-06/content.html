Deployment Model

Integrated AP-based Sensors (Unified Deployment)

Leveraging existing infrastructure for security functions

Cost-Effective

Utilizes existing APs, reducing hardware and deployment costs.

Simplified Deployment

No additional cabling or power infrastructure needed.

Potential Performance Impact

Scanning may affect client service or be less continuous.

Key Takeaway

Integrated APs offer a cost-effective WIDS/WIPS solution by leveraging existing hardware, but may compromise on continuous monitoring or client performance.
