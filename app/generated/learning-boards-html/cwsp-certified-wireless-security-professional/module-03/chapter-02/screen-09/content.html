Optimizing Sensor Placement: RF Line of Sight & Channel Coverage

Ensuring sensors 'see' all wireless activity

RF Line of Sight

Ensure unobstructed paths for wireless signals; signals weaken through obstacles.

Channel Coverage

Scan all relevant 802.11 channels (2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, 6 GHz) to detect threats.

Common Pitfall

Focusing only on primary channels, neglecting the broader spectrum where threats hide.

Key Takeaway

Effective WIDS/WIPS requires sensors to have clear RF line of sight and the ability to scan all relevant wireless channels to prevent blind spots.
