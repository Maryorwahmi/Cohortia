WIDS/WIPS Architectures and Deployment Models

Building a Robust Wireless Security Foundation

This chapter explores the foundational architectures and deployment models for Wireless Intrusion Detection and Prevention Systems (WIDS/WIPS). We'll compare dedicated (overlay) and integrated (unified) sensor approaches, discuss critical factors like sensor placement and RF coverage, and analyze the role of centralized management in ensuring robust wireless security.

Key Idea

The effectiveness of WIDS/WIPS hinges on its architecture and deployment, dictating how threats are detected and mitigated.
