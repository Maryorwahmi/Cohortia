Dedicated Sensors (Overlay)

High Security, High Cost

  • •Purpose-built for security, deep inspection.
  • •Continuous, uninterrupted monitoring.
  • •No impact on client AP performance.
  • •Higher hardware and deployment costs.
  • •Requires separate infrastructure.
Best For

Environments with strict security, high-value assets, and zero tolerance for performance impact.

Integrated AP-based Sensors (Unified)

Cost-Effective, Potential Trade-offs

  • •Leverages existing APs, lower cost.
  • •Simplified deployment and management.
  • •Scanning may be time-sliced, less continuous.
  • •Potential performance impact on client radios.
  • •May miss fleeting or sophisticated threats.
Best For

Organizations with budget constraints, simpler security needs, or a desire for streamlined infrastructure.

Key Takeaway

The optimal WIDS/WIPS deployment balances security requirements, budget, and operational complexity; a hybrid approach often provides the best of both worlds.
