Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential terms to solidify your understanding

RF Coverage

The area within which a WIDS/WIPS sensor can effectively monitor wireless signals.

Centralized Management

A single console or server that controls, configures, and aggregates data from all WIDS/WIPS sensors.

Time-Sliced Scanning

A method where an AP briefly pauses client service to scan other channels for security threats.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding RF coverage, the benefits of centralized management, and techniques like time-sliced scanning are crucial for optimizing WIDS/WIPS operational effectiveness.
