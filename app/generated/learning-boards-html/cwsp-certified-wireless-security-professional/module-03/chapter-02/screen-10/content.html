Optimizing Sensor Placement: Critical Areas & Interference

Strategic positioning for maximum threat detection

Proximity to Critical Areas

Higher density in areas with sensitive data, high traffic, or external exposure.

Avoid Interference

Keep sensors away from sources of RF interference that degrade monitoring.

Quality over Quantity

Intelligent placement is more effective than simply deploying many sensors randomly.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize sensor placement in critical areas and actively avoid sources of RF interference to ensure reliable and effective WIDS/WIPS operation.
