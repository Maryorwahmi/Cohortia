RF Analysis and Packet Inspection

WIDS/WIPS systems use deep RF analysis and detailed packet inspection to gain comprehensive insight into wireless transmissions for accurate threat identification.

RF Analysis: Examines signal strength, channel utilization, and interference levels.

Packet Inspection: Dissects 802.11 frames to analyze headers, payloads, and types.

Enhanced Accuracy: Multi-layered approach reduces both false positives and false negatives.

Key Takeaway

Combining RF analysis and packet inspection provides a holistic view of the wireless environment, essential for accurate and reliable threat detection.

How They Work Together

Rogue AP Detection: RF analysis identifies an unknown AP's signal, while packet inspection confirms its BSSID and whether it's bridging to the wired network.

Deauth Attack Validation: RF analysis might show unusual channel activity, and packet inspection confirms a flood of deauthentication frames from a single source.

Malicious Payload: Packet inspection can identify suspicious data within frame payloads, while RF analysis might indicate unusual transmission characteristics.

Real-World Connection

Think of it like a detective investigating a crime: RF analysis is observing the scene (signal strength, interference), while packet inspection is examining the evidence (individual packets, headers, content) for clues.
