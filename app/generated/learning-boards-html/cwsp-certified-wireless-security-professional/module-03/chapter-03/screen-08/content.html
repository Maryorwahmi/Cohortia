Anomaly Example

Detecting Anomalous Traffic Surges

WIDS/WIPS systems identify anomalous traffic by observing significant deviations from a learned baseline of normal network activity, such as sudden spikes in packet rates.

Baseline Learning: System learns typical packet rates and client behaviors.

Sudden Surges: Flags dramatic increases in data or management frames.

Client Behavior: Detects unusual client actions, like connecting to many SSIDs.

Key Takeaway

Anomaly detection is crucial for identifying novel threats that don't match known signatures by recognizing deviations from established normal behavior.

Anomaly Detection Process
1. Collect Data
2. Build Baseline
3. Detect Deviations
Common Misconception

Anomaly detection is not foolproof. A slow, subtle attack that mimics normal behavior or a gradual shift in network patterns might not trigger an anomaly alert, requiring careful tuning and monitoring.
