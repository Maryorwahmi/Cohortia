Denial of Service, Brute-Force & Exploits

Mitigating Disruptive and Exploitative Wireless Attacks

Deauthentication/Disassociation Floods

DoS attacks that disconnect legitimate clients from APs, disrupting wireless service.

Brute-force Attacks

Attempts to guess Wi-Fi passwords or pre-shared keys by systematically trying many combinations.

Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks

Intercepting and potentially altering communication between clients and APs to snoop or manipulate data.

Wireless Exploits & Misconfigurations

Attacks targeting vulnerabilities in protocols/firmware or risks from weakly configured APs (e.g., WEP).
