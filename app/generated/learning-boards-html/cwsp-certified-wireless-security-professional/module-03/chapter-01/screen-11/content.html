WIDS/WIPS Architecture: Wireless Sensors

The Eyes and Ears of Your Wireless Security System

Continuous RF Scanning

Sensors constantly scan the radio frequency spectrum across multiple channels and frequencies, acting as the primary data collectors.

Raw Data Collection

They collect raw wireless frames and RF data from all devices within range, including authorized and unauthorized signals.

Integrated or Dedicated

Sensors can be dedicated hardware devices or integrated as software functions within existing access points, offering deployment flexibility.
