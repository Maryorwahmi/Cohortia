Wireless Intrusion Prevention System (WIPS)

Beyond Detection: Actively Blocking Wireless Threats

Active Countermeasures

WIPS doesn't just alert; it takes immediate action to block or contain detected threats, providing a proactive defense.

Threat Containment

It can send deauthentication frames to rogue APs or clients, disrupting malicious operations and preventing data compromise.

Automated Response

WIPS can identify attack sources and send targeted frames to neutralize threats, such as turning a deauthentication flood back on the attacker.

WIDS: Detect & Alert

Observes and notifies

WIPS: Prevent & Block

Actively intervenes

Key Takeaway

WIPS provides active, automated defense against wireless threats, going beyond detection to prevent harm.
