Key Concepts: Detection & Prevention

Essential Terms for Wireless Security

Wireless IDS (WIDS)

Monitors wireless traffic for suspicious activity and generates alerts without active intervention.

Wireless IPS (WIPS)

Detects and actively blocks or contains wireless threats, providing proactive defense.

Rogue Access Point

An unauthorized AP connected to a legitimate network, creating a security vulnerability.

Summary Takeaway

WIDS and WIPS are distinct but complementary systems for detecting and preventing wireless intrusions, with rogue APs being a primary concern.
