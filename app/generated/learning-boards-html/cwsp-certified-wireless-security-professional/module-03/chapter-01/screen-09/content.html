Unauthorized Access: Rogue & Evil Twin APs

Identifying Malicious Access Points and Ad-hoc Networks

Rogue Access Points

Unauthorized APs installed by employees or attackers, creating backdoors into the wired network.

Evil Twin Access Points

Malicious APs masquerading as legitimate ones to capture credentials or traffic from unsuspecting users.

Ad-hoc Networks

Peer-to-peer wireless connections that can bypass corporate security controls, posing a data exfiltration risk.

Key Takeaway

Unauthorized access points like rogue and evil twin APs exploit trust and network access, requiring vigilant WIDS/WIPS detection.
