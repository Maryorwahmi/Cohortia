Key Concepts: Threats & Architecture

Understanding Wireless Attacks and System Components

Evil Twin AP

A malicious AP mimicking a legitimate one to trick users into connecting and compromising data.

Deauthentication Flood

A DoS attack where forged deauthentication frames disconnect legitimate clients from an AP.

Wireless Sensor

A device or function that continuously monitors the RF spectrum for WIDS/WIPS data collection.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding specific threats like Evil Twin APs and Deauthentication Floods, alongside the role of Wireless Sensors, is vital for comprehensive wireless security.
