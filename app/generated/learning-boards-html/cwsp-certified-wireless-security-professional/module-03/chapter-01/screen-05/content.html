Wireless Intrusion Detection System (WIDS)

Identifying Suspicious Activity on the Airwaves

Passive Monitoring

WIDS continuously listens to wireless traffic without interfering with network operations. It's like a security camera for your Wi-Fi.

Anomaly Detection

It analyzes patterns against known attack signatures and established baselines of normal behavior to spot deviations.

Alert Generation

When a threat is detected, WIDS generates alerts, notifying administrators of potential breaches or attacks in progress.

Monitor

Passive listening

Alert

Key Takeaway

WIDS provides crucial early warning by detecting suspicious wireless activity without active intervention.
