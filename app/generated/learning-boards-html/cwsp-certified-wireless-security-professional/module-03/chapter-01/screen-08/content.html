WIPS: Countering Attacks in Real-Time

How WIPS Actively Neutralizes Wireless Intrusions

1. Detect Rogue AP

WIPS identifies an unauthorized Access Point operating within the network's vicinity.

2. Disconnect Clients

Automatically sends deauthentication frames to clients connected to the rogue AP, forcing them to disconnect.

3. Disrupt Rogue AP

Sends deauthentication frames to the rogue AP itself, disrupting its operation and preventing it from serving clients.

4. Neutralize DoS Attacks

Identifies the source of deauthentication floods and sends targeted frames to the attacker's device, restoring service.
