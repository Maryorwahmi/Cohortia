Attack Vector: Reconnaissance

Active Wireless Reconnaissance

Probing networks to uncover hidden details by sending packets and analyzing responses.

Passive Reconnaissance

  • •Method: Listens to traffic, no packets sent.
  • •Detection: Very low detectability.
  • •Goal: Gather basic network info (SSIDs, BSSIDs, channels).
  • •Example: Wardriving with Airodump-ng.

Active Reconnaissance

  • •Method: Sends packets to elicit responses.
  • •Detection: Higher detectability due to traffic.
  • •Goal: Discover hidden SSIDs, detailed AP info.
  • •Example: Sending probe requests for hidden networks.
Key Takeaway

Active reconnaissance provides deeper insights but carries a higher risk of detection, making it a trade-off for attackers seeking specific network details.

Common Misconception

Hiding an SSID (network name) offers minimal security, as it can be easily discovered through active probing or even passively when clients connect.
