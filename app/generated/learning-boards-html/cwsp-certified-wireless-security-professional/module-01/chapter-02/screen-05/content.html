Attack Vector: Reconnaissance

Passive Wireless Reconnaissance

Gathering network intelligence without direct interaction, simply by listening to broadcasted wireless traffic.

Listen to Traffic

Capture raw 802.11 frames.

Identify Network Details

SSIDs, BSSIDs, Channels, Encryption.

Map Wireless Footprint

Wardriving to locate and analyze networks.

Example: Airodump-ng Output
BSSID              PWR  Beacons    #Data, #/s  CH  MB   ENC  CIPHER AUTH ESSID
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
00:11:22:33:44:55  -55      123        0    0   6  54e  WPA2 CCMP  PSK  MyHomeWiFi
AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF  -70       80        5    0   1  54e  WPA2 CCMP  PSK  OfficeNet

STATION            PWR   Rate    Lost    Frames  Probe
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11:22:33:44:55:66  -60   1e-24e     0       100  MyHomeWiFi

This output reveals AP MAC addresses (BSSID), signal strength (PWR), encryption type (ENC), and network names (ESSID), along with connected clients (STATION).

Key Takeaway

Passive reconnaissance is a stealthy method for attackers to gather extensive network intelligence without detection, highlighting the need for strong encryption and access controls.

Pro Tip

Regularly perform your own passive scans to understand your network's visible footprint and identify any unintended information leakage.
