IEEE 802.11: The Early Wi-Fi Standards

The IEEE 802.11 family of standards dictates how wireless devices communicate, evolving significantly over time.

802.11 (1997)

Original standard, 1-2 Mbps. Laid the groundwork for WLANs.

2.4 GHz

802.11b (1999)

First widely adopted, 11 Mbps. Prone to interference.

2.4 GHz

802.11a (1999)

Faster, 54 Mbps. Shorter range due to 5 GHz band.

5 GHz
Key Takeaway

Early 802.11 standards laid the foundation for Wi-Fi, balancing speed, range, and frequency band choices, setting the stage for future advancements.
