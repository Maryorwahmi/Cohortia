Learning Objectives

01

Explain RF Principles

Understand the core concepts of radio frequency communication, including frequency, wavelength, and the electromagnetic spectrum. This knowledge is foundational for comprehending how wireless signals transmit data and interact with their environment.

02

Differentiate 802.11 Standards

Distinguish between various IEEE 802.11 standards (e.g., b, a, g, n, ac, ax) and their respective impacts on network performance, speed, and security features. This helps in selecting appropriate hardware and configuring networks for optimal operation.
