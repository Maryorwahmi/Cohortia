Antenna Fundamentals

Antenna Types and Gain: Shaping Your Signal

Antennas are crucial for directing and receiving wireless signals, with different types serving distinct purposes.

Omnidirectional Antennas

  • •Radiate and receive signals equally in all horizontal directions.
  • •Ideal for general coverage within a defined area (e.g., home Wi-Fi).
Security Risk

Can extend network reach unintentionally, making it accessible to unauthorized users outside the intended area.

Directional Antennas

  • •Focus RF energy in a specific direction, creating a concentrated beam.
  • •Suitable for point-to-point links or specific sector coverage.
Key Takeaway

Antenna gain (dBi) measures efficiency in directing power; higher dBi means a more focused beam, crucial for range and security.
