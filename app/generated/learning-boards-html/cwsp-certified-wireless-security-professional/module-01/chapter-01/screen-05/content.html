Frequency Bands

2.4 GHz vs. 5 GHz: Choosing the Right Band

Wireless networks operate primarily in the unlicensed 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz ISM bands, each with distinct characteristics.

2.4 GHz Band

  • •Offers greater range and better penetration through obstacles.
  • •Prone to interference from microwaves, Bluetooth, and other devices.
  • •Lower overall throughput compared to 5 GHz.
Common Misconception

Longer range always means better performance. Interference can severely degrade 2.4 GHz performance despite its range.

5 GHz Band

  • •Provides higher throughput and less interference.
  • •Shorter range and poorer penetration through walls.
  • •More non-overlapping channels available for better channel planning.
Key Takeaway

Optimal band selection depends on balancing range, throughput, and interference considerations for your specific environment.
