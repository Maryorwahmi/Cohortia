Signal Propagation: Attenuation, Reflection, and Refraction

Radio waves do not travel in a straight line; various phenomena affect their path and strength.

Attenuation

Loss of signal strength over distance or when passing through obstacles.

Signal Weakening

Reflection

Signal bounces off smooth surfaces, creating multiple paths.

Bouncing Signals

Refraction

Bending of a signal as it passes through different mediums.

Key Takeaway

These propagation effects can lead to signal degradation, dead zones, and multipath interference, impacting network reliability and requiring careful planning.
