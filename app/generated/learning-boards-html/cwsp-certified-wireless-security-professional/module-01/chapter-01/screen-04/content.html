Core Concept

The Invisible Medium: Radio Frequency Fundamentals

Wireless communication relies on electromagnetic waves characterized by frequency and wavelength.

Frequency (Hz)

The number of wave cycles per second. Higher frequency means more data capacity.

Wavelength (λ)

The physical distance over which a wave's shape repeats. Inversely proportional to frequency.

Key Takeaway

Frequency and wavelength are fundamental properties of radio waves, directly influencing signal range and data throughput.

Wave Characteristics

High Frequency: Shorter wavelength, higher data rate, shorter range.

Low Frequency: Longer wavelength, lower data rate, greater range.
