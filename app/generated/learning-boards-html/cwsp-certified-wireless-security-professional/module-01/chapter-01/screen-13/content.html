Channel Planning

Wireless Channels: Avoiding Interference

Proper channel selection is crucial for minimizing interference and maximizing wireless network performance.

2.4 GHz Channels

  • •11-14 channels available (region dependent).
  • •Only 3 non-overlapping channels in North America: 1, 6, and 11.
  • •Overlapping channels cause significant co-channel interference.
Common Mistake

Leaving APs on default channels or using auto-channel selection without a site survey, leading to hidden interference.

5 GHz Channels

  • •Many more non-overlapping channels available.
  • •Easier channel planning due to less congestion.
  • •Reduced likelihood of co-channel interference.
Key Takeaway

Always conduct a site survey to identify existing RF interference and strategically plan channel assignments for optimal network health.
