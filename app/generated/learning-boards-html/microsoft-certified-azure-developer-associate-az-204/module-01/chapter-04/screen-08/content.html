Choosing the Right Compute Service

Understand the trade-offs in control, management, scalability, and cost for VMs, App Services, and Functions.

Key Considerations
  • Control: How much OS/environment access is needed?
  • Management: How much operational overhead can you handle?
  • Scalability: What are the expected load patterns?
  • Cost: What is the budget and pricing model preference?
VMs (IaaS)

Max Control, Max Overhead. Best for legacy/custom.

App Services (PaaS)

Balanced Control, Managed. Best for web apps.

Functions (FaaS)

Min Control, Min Overhead. Best for event-driven.

Common Mistake

Using VMs when PaaS/FaaS is more efficient.

Key Takeaway

Align your choice of Azure compute service with your application's specific requirements for control, management, scalability, and cost.
