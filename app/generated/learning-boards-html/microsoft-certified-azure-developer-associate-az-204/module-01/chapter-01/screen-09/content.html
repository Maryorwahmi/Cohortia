The Azure Portal: Your Web-Based Console

The Azure Portal is a web-based console that provides a comprehensive graphical interface for managing all your Azure resources.

  • Ideal for exploration, initial setup, and visual monitoring of resources.
  • Allows creation, configuration, and deletion of resources with a few clicks.
  • Provides a clear visual representation of your cloud environment.
Key Takeaway

The Azure Portal is your go-to for visual management and quick interactive tasks, offering a comprehensive overview of your cloud resources.
