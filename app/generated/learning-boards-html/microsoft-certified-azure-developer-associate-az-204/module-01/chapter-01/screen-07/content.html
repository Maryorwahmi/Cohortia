Management Groups: Governing Multiple Subscriptions

Management Groups provide a layer of organization above subscriptions, enabling centralized governance for multiple subscriptions.

  • Allows management of access, policies, and compliance across many subscriptions.
  • Policies defined at this level inherit down to all associated subscriptions.
  • Ideal for large enterprises needing consistent governance across their cloud estate.
Key Takeaway

Management Groups are crucial for centralized policy enforcement and streamlined governance across numerous Azure subscriptions.

Hierarchy of Control
