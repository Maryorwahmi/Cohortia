Deleting an Entity

Removing data with `DeleteEntityAsync`.

The `DeleteEntityAsync` method permanently removes an entity from a table. It requires the `PartitionKey` and `RowKey` of the entity to be deleted. Ensure you are certain before performing this irreversible operation.

Key Takeaway

`DeleteEntityAsync` provides a direct way to remove specific entities, requiring their unique `PartitionKey` and `RowKey` for accurate targeting.

Deletion Process
Method

`await _tableClient.DeleteEntityAsync(partitionKey, rowKey);`

Requirements

Requires the exact `PartitionKey` and `RowKey` of the entity to be deleted.

Caution

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Ensure accuracy before execution.
