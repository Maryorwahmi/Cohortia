Hybrid Redundancy and Strategic Choices

Combining zone and geo-replication for ultimate protection.

Geo-Zone-Redundant Storage (GZRS)

  • Replication: ZRS in primary + async to secondary region.
  • Protection: Against data center and regional outages.
  • Benefit: High availability within region, plus disaster recovery.
  • Use Case: Mission-critical applications requiring maximum resilience.
Key Takeaway

GZRS offers a powerful combination of intra-regional and cross-regional data protection.

Read-Access GZRS (RA-GZRS)

  • Replication: GZRS + read access to secondary region.
  • Protection: Highest level of availability and disaster recovery.
  • Benefit: Read-only access during primary region outages, even with zone failures.
  • Use Case: Applications demanding continuous read availability during any failure.
Pro Tip

For the most critical applications, RA-GZRS provides unparalleled data resilience.
