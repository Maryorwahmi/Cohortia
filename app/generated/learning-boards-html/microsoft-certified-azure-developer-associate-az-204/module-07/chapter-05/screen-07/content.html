Core Concept

Azure Durable Functions: Stateful Orchestration

Building long-running, resilient workflows in a serverless environment.

What are Durable Functions?

  • •An extension of Azure Functions for stateful workflows.
  • •Overcomes the stateless nature of typical serverless functions.
  • •Orchestrates long-running business processes.
  • •State is durably persisted, surviving host restarts.

Why Stateful Workflows?

Many real-world business processes are not instantaneous. They involve sequences of operations, delays, and external interactions. Durable Functions provide the necessary state persistence and orchestration capabilities to manage these complex, long-running processes reliably in a serverless context.

Concrete Example: Human Approval

Consider a document approval workflow. A Durable Function can initiate the approval, then pause and wait for a human to approve or reject the document. Once the external event (approval) occurs, the function resumes, continuing the workflow. This wait could be hours or days, yet the state is maintained.

Key Takeaway

Azure Durable Functions are ideal for orchestrating complex, long-running, and stateful workflows in a serverless environment, ensuring resilience and continuity even across extended waits.
