Rolling Updates and Rollbacks

Kubernetes rolling updates gradually replace old Pods with new ones, ensuring zero downtime, while rollbacks allow quick reversion to previous stable versions.

Key Takeaway

The rolling update strategy is fundamental for maintaining application availability during deployments, minimizing risk and user impact.

Common Mistake

Not configuring `maxUnavailable` and `maxSurge` appropriately can lead to either too much downtime or resource contention during updates.
