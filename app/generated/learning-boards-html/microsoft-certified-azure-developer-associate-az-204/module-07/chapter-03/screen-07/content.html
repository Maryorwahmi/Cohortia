Advanced Networking: Ingress Controllers

Ingress controllers route external HTTP/S traffic to internal services based on defined rules, enabling efficient multi-service exposure and SSL management.

Key Takeaway

Ingress controllers consolidate multiple services behind a single entry point, simplifying management and improving cost-efficiency compared to individual LoadBalancer services.

Why it Matters

Essential for microservices architectures where numerous applications need to be accessible externally, Ingress provides flexible and powerful traffic routing capabilities.

Ingress Controller
Acts as an HTTP/S router, directing external traffic to internal Kubernetes Services based on rules defined in Ingress resources.
