Custom Topics: Publishing Your Own Events

Decoupling publishers from subscribers in custom applications.

What are Custom Topics?

  • Topics you create to publish your application's events.
  • Managed by you, providing full control over event schema.
  • Enable complete decoupling between event publishers and subscribers.
  • Ideal for internal application events or integration with external systems.

Example: E-commerce Application

An 'OrderProcessor' service publishes 'OrderProcessed' events to a custom topic. 'InventoryUpdater' and 'EmailNotifier' services subscribe to this topic, reacting independently without direct knowledge of 'OrderProcessor'.

Key Takeaway

Custom topics are fundamental for building modular, scalable microservice architectures, allowing your applications to communicate effectively through a shared event bus.

When to use Custom Topics

Use custom topics when your application needs to generate and distribute its own events to other internal or external systems. This is ideal for microservices communication, business process automation, and integrating disparate application components.
