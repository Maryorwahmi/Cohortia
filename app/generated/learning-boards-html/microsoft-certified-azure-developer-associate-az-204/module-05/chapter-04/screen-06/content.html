Publish-Subscribe with Topics and Subscriptions

Topics allow a sender to broadcast messages, and multiple subscriptions enable different receivers to get copies, often with filters.

Topic Definition

A messaging entity where a sender publishes messages, and multiple receivers can subscribe to receive copies.

Subscription Role

Each subscription acts as a virtual queue, allowing a specific receiver to get a filtered copy of messages from the topic.

Key Takeaway

Topics enable publish-subscribe messaging, allowing multiple subscribers to receive copies of messages, with subscriptions offering filtering capabilities for targeted delivery.
