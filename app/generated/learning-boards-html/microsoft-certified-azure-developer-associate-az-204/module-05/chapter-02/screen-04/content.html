Azure Logic Apps: Your Serverless Workflow Engine

Automate and integrate across apps, data, and systems without extensive code.

What are Logic Apps?

A serverless platform for building automated workflows that integrate apps, data, services, and systems across enterprises or organizations. They act as a powerful, visual, "if this then that" engine for the cloud.

Key Benefits

  • Serverless: No infrastructure to manage.
  • Visual Designer: Build complex flows with drag-and-drop.
  • Extensive Connectors: Integrate with hundreds of services.
  • Scalable: Automatically scales to meet demand.
Key Takeaway

Logic Apps simplify complex integrations and automation, allowing developers to focus on business logic rather than infrastructure management.
