Core Definition

What is Azure Monitor?

Azure Monitor is a comprehensive platform service that collects, analyzes, and acts on telemetry data from your Azure and on-premises environments. It provides full observability across your applications, infrastructure, and network.

Key Capabilities
  • Data Collection: Gathers metrics and logs from diverse sources.
  • Analysis & Visualization: Provides tools for querying, charting, and dashboarding.
  • Alerting & Actions: Notifies on critical conditions and automates responses.
Analogy: The Health Monitor

Think of Azure Monitor as a comprehensive health monitoring system for a large hospital. It collects vital signs (metrics) from all patients and detailed incident reports (logs) from every department. It then processes this information to identify critical situations, alert staff, and even trigger automated emergency protocols, ensuring the entire hospital runs smoothly.

Key Takeaway

Azure Monitor acts as a unified 'single pane of glass' for all operational data, enabling proactive management and rapid issue resolution across your cloud estate.
