Avoiding Common Troubleshooting Mistakes

Recognize and prevent frequent errors that can hinder effective problem resolution.

Mistake 1: Insufficient Logging

Deploying without adequate diagnostic settings leaves you blind to application behavior and errors.

Mistake 2: Ignoring Service Health

Always check for platform-level issues first; your problem might be an Azure incident, not your code.

Mistake 3: Jumping to Conclusions

Avoid guessing; follow a systematic approach and let the data guide your diagnosis.

Mistake 4: Not Checking Recent Changes

Most issues are introduced by a change; this is often the quickest way to identify the culprit.

Mistake 5: Forgetting Cold Starts

For serverless functions, initial high latency might be a cold start, not an error. Understand the difference.

Mistake 6: Overlooking Networking Issues

DNS resolution, firewall rules, or VNet integration problems can manifest as application errors. Use tools like `nslookup`.

Why these are critical

These mistakes lead to wasted time, misdiagnosis, and prolonged downtime. Avoiding them streamlines your troubleshooting efforts.

Key Takeaway

Proactively addressing common troubleshooting pitfalls through comprehensive logging, systematic investigation, and awareness of platform behavior is crucial for efficient incident resolution.
