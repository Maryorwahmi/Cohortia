Incident Response

Starting Your Troubleshooting Journey

Learn the first critical steps to verify scope, check health, and review recent changes.

1

Verify Scope

Determine if the issue affects all users/regions/functionalities or just a subset. This helps narrow down the problem domain.

2

Check Azure Service Health

Always check the Azure Portal's Service Health blade for ongoing Azure service incidents that might be affecting your resources.

3

Review Recent Changes

Identify any recent deployments, configuration changes, or scaling events that might have preceded the issue. Changes are often the culprit.

Why these steps?

These initial checks provide high-level context, often revealing the problem's nature or even its direct cause without deep-diving into logs.

Common Mistake

Ignoring Azure Service Health or recent changes can lead to hours of unnecessary debugging when the problem is external or self-inflicted.

Key Takeaway

The first steps in troubleshooting are about broad context gathering: understanding the impact, checking platform health, and identifying recent modifications to quickly narrow the focus.
