Why Resilience Matters in the Cloud

Designing applications to handle failures gracefully and recover quickly.

Cloud Reality

Underlying cloud components can and will experience issues. Your application must be prepared.

User Experience

Unplanned downtime or slow performance directly impacts user satisfaction and business reputation.

Business Continuity

Resilient systems minimize financial losses and maintain critical operations during disruptions.

The Resilience Cycle
1
Detect Failure
2
Handle & Mitigate
3
Recover & Restore
Key Takeaway

Resilience is about anticipating failures and building systems that can automatically adapt and recover, ensuring continuous service.
