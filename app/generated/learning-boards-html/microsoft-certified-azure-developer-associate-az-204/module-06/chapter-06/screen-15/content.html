Designing for Graceful Degradation

Maintaining core functionality during partial system failures.

Embrace Partial Failures

Acknowledge that not all components can always be fully operational in distributed systems.

Prioritize Core Features

Identify and protect the most critical functionalities to ensure business continuity.

Maintain User Experience

Provide a functional, albeit potentially reduced, experience rather than a complete outage.

E-commerce Example
Core Functionality: Browse, Cart, Checkout
Non-Essential: Recommendation EngineDOWN
Result: Site Still Operates (No Recommendations)
Key Takeaway

Graceful degradation ensures that even with partial failures, the most critical parts of your application remain functional for users.
