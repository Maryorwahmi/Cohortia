Circuit Breaker and Bulkhead

Preventing cascading failures and isolating components.

Circuit Breaker

Prevents repeated calls to a consistently failing service, allowing it time to recover and preventing resource exhaustion.

Bulkhead

Isolates different parts of an application, so a failure in one area does not bring down the entire system.

Key Takeaway

Circuit Breakers and Bulkheads are crucial for preventing cascading failures and isolating faults in complex distributed systems.
