Azure Advisor in Action: Key Recommendations

Practical Steps to Reduce Your Azure Bill

Right-sizing VMs

Advisor identifies underutilized virtual machines and recommends shutting them down or resizing them to a more cost-effective tier.

Benefit

Avoid paying for unused compute capacity.

Deleting Unused Resources

Identifies resources that have been idle for extended periods, such as unattached disks or old public IPs, which still incur costs.

Benefit

Eliminate charges for dormant or forgotten assets.

Reserving Instances

Suggests Azure Reservations for consistent workloads to achieve significant discounts by committing to a 1-year or 3-year term.

Benefit

Substantial savings for predictable, long-term resource usage.

Leveraging Cheaper Services

Recommends alternatives or more cost-effective tiers when suitable options exist, such as moving to cooler storage.

Benefit

Optimize service choices for specific workload requirements.
