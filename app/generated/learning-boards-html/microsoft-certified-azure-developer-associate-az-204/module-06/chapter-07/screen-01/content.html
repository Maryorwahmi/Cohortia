Cost Optimization and Resource Management

Maximizing Cloud Value, Minimizing Unnecessary Spend

The cloud offers immense flexibility, but without careful planning, costs can quickly escalate. This chapter focuses on strategies and tools to build cost-aware solutions and leverage Azure's capabilities for efficient resource management, ensuring you get the most value from your cloud investment.

Key Idea

Cost optimization is about maximizing value, not just reducing expenditure, by aligning resources with actual needs.
