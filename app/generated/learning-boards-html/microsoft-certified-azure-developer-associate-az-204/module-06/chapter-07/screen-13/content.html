Developer Best Practices: Lifecycle Management

Managing Resources from Creation to Deletion for Cost Savings

Delete Unused Resources

Regularly audit and remove resources that are no longer needed (e.g., old dev environments, abandoned VMs).

Benefit

Eliminate charges for dormant or forgotten assets that still incur costs.

Optimize Storage

Choose the right storage tier (hot, cool, archive) for your data based on access patterns to reduce costs.

Benefit

Match storage costs to data access frequency, saving money on infrequently accessed data.

Schedule Shutdowns

For non-production environments, schedule VMs and other compute resources to shut down outside business hours.

Benefit

Only pay for resources when they are actively being used by your development or testing teams.

Key Takeaway

Integrating cost awareness into the entire resource lifecycle, from creation to deletion, is vital for building efficient and sustainable cloud solutions.
