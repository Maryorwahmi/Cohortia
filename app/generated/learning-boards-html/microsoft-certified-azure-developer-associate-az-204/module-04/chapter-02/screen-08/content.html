Scope: The 'Where' Access Applies

Scope defines the level at which a role assignment applies. It is critical for implementing the principle of least privilege by limiting access to specific areas.

  • •Management Group: Broadest level, organizes subscriptions.
  • •Subscription: Applies to all resources within a specific subscription.
  • •Resource Group: Applies to all resources within a specific resource group.
  • •Resource: Narrowest level, applies to a single resource.
Key Takeaway

Always assign roles at the narrowest possible scope to minimize potential security risks and adhere to least privilege.

Common Mistake

Assigning roles at too broad a scope, like subscription level, when resource group or individual resource scope would suffice. This violates the principle of least privilege.
