Key Concepts: Advanced RBAC & Best Practices

Refining your access control strategy.

Scope

The level at which a role assignment applies (management group, subscription, resource group, or resource).

Custom Roles

User-defined role definitions created to meet specific, granular permission requirements.

Least Privilege

A security best practice: grant only the minimum permissions necessary for an identity to perform its job.

Summary Takeaway

By combining granular scope, appropriate built-in or custom roles, and the principle of least privilege, you can build a robust and secure access control strategy in Azure.
