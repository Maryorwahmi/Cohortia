Key Capabilities

Deep Dive into Defender for Cloud Features

Leveraging Secure Score, Recommendations, and Threat Protection

Secure Score

A quantitative measure of your organization's security posture, providing a clear benchmark and guiding improvements.

Security Recommendations

Actionable advice to improve security across your Azure resources, such as 'Enable MFA' or 'Apply NSGs to subnets.'

Advanced Threat Protection

Detects and alerts on threats across various Azure services (VMs, SQL, Key Vault, Storage) by identifying unusual or suspicious activities.

Regulatory Compliance

Helps meet compliance requirements by continuously assessing your environment against industry standards and regulatory benchmarks.

Holistic Security View

Defender for Cloud provides a holistic view of your security posture, enabling proactive identification and remediation of vulnerabilities and threats.

Key Takeaway

Leverage Defender for Cloud's Secure Score, recommendations, and threat protection to proactively manage and enhance your Azure security posture.
