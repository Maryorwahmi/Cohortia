Security Best Practices

Hardening Your Azure Key Vault

Essential measures for robust secret protection

Access Control & Configuration

Properly configuring access and settings is paramount:

  • •Least Privilege: Grant only the minimum necessary permissions.
  • •Prefer RBAC: Use Azure Role-Based Access Control over Key Vault access policies for new deployments.
  • •Protect Key Vault URI: Store the Key Vault URI as an environment variable, not hardcoded.
  • •Network Firewall: Restrict access to specific virtual networks or IP ranges.
Key Takeaway

Implement least privilege, use RBAC, protect the Key Vault URI, enable soft-delete, and audit logs for comprehensive security.

Data Protection & Monitoring

Ensure your secrets are protected against accidental deletion and monitor for suspicious activity:

  • •Soft-Delete: Prevents immediate, irreversible deletion of vaults and their contents, providing a recovery window.
  • •Purge Protection: Adds another layer of protection, preventing permanent deletion during the soft-delete retention period.
  • •Audit Logs: Regularly review Key Vault access logs to detect and respond to suspicious activity.
Common Mistake

Failing to enable soft-delete and purge protection. Without these, accidental or malicious deletion of your Key Vault or its contents can lead to irreversible data loss and service disruption.
