The Consumption Plan: Pay-Per-Execution

Understanding automatic scaling and cost optimization.

Automatic Scaling

Azure automatically scales your function app instances based on the incoming event rate, scaling out during high demand and scaling to zero during inactivity.

Pay-Per-Execution Billing

You are billed only for the compute time (gigabyte-seconds) and executions your functions consume, not for idle resources.

Key Takeaway

The Consumption Plan is ideal for cost-effective, highly scalable solutions for intermittent and unpredictable workloads.

Cost Efficiency

Eliminates the need to provision and pay for always-on servers, significantly reducing operational costs for many scenarios.

Example: Image Processing

A function that resizes images uploaded to blob storage. It only runs when an image is uploaded, making the Consumption Plan highly efficient.

Consideration

While cost-effective, be aware of potential 'cold starts' for infrequently invoked functions.
