Learning Objectives

Master the skills to choose and implement the right Azure compute solutions.

Bloom's Taxonomy:
LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Evaluate Azure Compute Services

Assess App Service, Functions, ACI, AKS, and VMs based on application requirements. This skill enables you to understand the unique strengths and weaknesses of each service.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Develop a Decision Framework

Create a structured approach for selecting the optimal compute solution. This framework will help you systematically weigh trade-offs and justify your architectural choices.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Understand Key Considerations

Grasp the impact of cost, scalability, management overhead, and security on compute choices. This knowledge is vital for making balanced decisions that align with business goals.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Identify Best Practices

Recognize and apply best practices for deploying and managing applications. This ensures your solutions are secure, cost-effective, and maintainable in the long run.
