ACI vs. AKS: Making the Right Choice

Matching container services to your application needs

Key Decision Factors

  • Application Complexity: Single container vs. microservices.
  • Orchestration Needs: Simple execution vs. advanced management.
  • Scalability: Basic vs. sophisticated auto-scaling.
  • Management Overhead: Serverless vs. managed cluster.
Key Takeaway

Align your service choice with your application's scale, complexity, and operational requirements.

Common Mistake

Over-engineering with AKS for simple workloads that ACI could handle more efficiently.

ACI vs. AKS Comparison

ACI

  • Single containers
  • Serverless
  • Fast deployment
  • Minimal management

AKS

  • Microservices
  • Managed Kubernetes
  • Advanced orchestration
  • High availability
Choose based on complexity & scale

When to use ACI

Batch jobs, dev/test, simple APIs, burstable workloads.

When to use AKS

Microservices, complex apps, CI/CD, advanced scaling.
