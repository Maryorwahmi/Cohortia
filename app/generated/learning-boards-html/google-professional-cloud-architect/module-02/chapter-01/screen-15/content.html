Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms for cloud architects

Scalability

A system's ability to handle a growing amount of work without performance degradation.

Vertical Scaling

Increasing resources (CPU, RAM) of a single instance to handle more load.

Horizontal Scaling

Adding more instances of a resource to distribute load across multiple machines.

High Availability (HA)

Ensuring a system remains operational and accessible despite component failures.

SLA

A formal agreement defining expected service levels and uptime between provider and customer.

SLO

Internal targets for a service's performance and availability, guiding engineering efforts.

Summary Takeaway

Scalability and High Availability are core to cloud architecture, with distinct scaling methods and critical metrics (SLA, SLO) defining service expectations.
