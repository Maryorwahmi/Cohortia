Strategic Design

Balancing Requirements: Avoiding Over-Engineering

Aligning design with actual business needs

The Balancing Act

Architects must balance cost, complexity, performance, scalability, and high availability to meet business requirements effectively.

The Pitfall of Over-Engineering
  • Designing for availability levels not truly required.
  • Leads to unnecessary expenses and increased operational overhead.
  • Diverts resources from more critical areas.
Key Question

What is the true business impact of downtime and data loss for this specific application?

Example Scenario

A company's internal blog has an RTO of 4 hours and an RPO of 24 hours. Designing a multi-region active-active setup for this blog would be over-engineering. A simpler, single-region deployment with daily backups would be more appropriate and cost-effective.

Key Takeaway

Always align your architectural design choices with the actual RTO, RPO, SLA, and SLO requirements of your application to avoid over-engineering and ensure optimal resource utilization.
