RTO and RPO: Recovery Objectives

Defining acceptable downtime and data loss

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

The maximum acceptable downtime after an incident. It dictates how quickly a system must be restored to operation.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

The maximum acceptable amount of data loss, measured in time. It defines how much data can be lost from the point of failure.

Achieving Low RTO/RPO

Low RTO often requires active-passive/active-active redundancy. Low RPO typically needs continuous data replication or frequent backups.

Example Scenario

A critical banking system requires an RTO of 5 minutes and an RPO of 0 seconds. This necessitates a multi-region active-active setup with synchronous data replication to ensure immediate failover and no data loss.

Key Takeaway

RTO and RPO are fundamental for disaster recovery planning, dictating the speed of recovery and acceptable data loss, and must be aligned with business criticality.
