Availability Metrics

Quantifying High Availability

Measuring system uptime and resilience

High Availability (HA)

The ability of a system to remain operational and accessible for a specified period, even when individual components fail. It focuses on minimizing downtime.

Why Metrics are Essential
  • Provide a common language for reliability.
  • Set clear expectations for system performance.
  • Guide design decisions for redundancy and recovery.
  • Enable objective measurement of actual availability.
Misconception

HA does not mean 'never fails.' It means designing to minimize downtime and data loss within defined, measurable parameters.

Example Scenario

A healthcare application processing patient records must be highly available. Its HA design ensures that even if one database server fails, a replica can take over within seconds, maintaining continuous access to critical patient data.

Key Takeaway

High availability is a critical design goal, and its effectiveness is measured through specific metrics that define acceptable levels of uptime and resilience.
