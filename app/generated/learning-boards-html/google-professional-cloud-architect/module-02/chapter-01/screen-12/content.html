Trade-offs: Cost and Complexity

Balancing resources for extreme scalability and HA

Increased Cost

Higher levels of scalability and HA require more compute, storage, and networking resources, plus data transfer costs for replication.

Higher Complexity

Managing distributed systems, ensuring data consistency, and orchestrating failover processes add significant operational overhead.

Visual Concept: The Trade-off Scale
Low Cost/ComplexityHigh Cost/Complexity
Lower HA/ScalabilityHigher HA/Scalability
Example Scenario

A startup building a new internal tool might opt for a single-region Cloud SQL instance for simplicity and cost-effectiveness. A global enterprise, however, might invest in a multi-region Cloud Spanner deployment for its mission-critical financial application, accepting higher cost and complexity for extreme HA.

Key Takeaway

Architects must balance the desire for extreme scalability and HA against the realities of increased cost and complexity, aligning design choices with actual business requirements.
