Best Practices

Setting Minimum and Maximum Instance Counts

Balance resilience, performance, and cost control.

Minimum Instances (`min-num-replicas`)

  • •Ensures baseline capacity, even during low traffic.
  • •Provides immediate resilience against instance failures.
  • •Guarantees a minimum level of service availability.

Maximum Instances (`max-num-replicas`)

  • •Acts as a cost control mechanism, preventing over-provisioning.
  • •Prevents runaway scaling due to misconfigurations or attacks.
  • •Defines the upper limit of your application's capacity.
Common Mistake

Setting `min-num-replicas` too low can lead to insufficient capacity during unexpected load, causing performance degradation and poor user experience.

Key Takeaway

Carefully configured min/max instance counts are crucial for balancing application resilience, performance, and cost efficiency in MIG deployments.
