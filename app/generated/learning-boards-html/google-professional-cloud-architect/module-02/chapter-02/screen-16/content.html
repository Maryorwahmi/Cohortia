More Key Concepts

Reinforcing your understanding of MIGs.

Health Check

A mechanism that monitors the health of individual instances within a MIG, enabling automatic replacement of unhealthy instances.

Regional MIG

A MIG that distributes instances across multiple zones within a specific region, enhancing availability against zone failures.

Rolling Update

A method of updating instances in a MIG gradually, replacing old instances with new ones to minimize downtime.

Summary Takeaway

Health checks, regional MIGs, and rolling updates are advanced features that collectively ensure high availability, resilience, and seamless deployments for your cloud applications.
