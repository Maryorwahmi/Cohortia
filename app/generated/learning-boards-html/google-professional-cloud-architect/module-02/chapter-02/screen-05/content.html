Key Capability

Dynamic Capacity: Auto-scaling with MIGs

Automatically adjust instance count to match changing load.

Demand Increases

MIGs detect higher load (e.g., CPU, requests) and provision new instances.

Dynamic Adjustment

Capacity scales out to meet demand, maintaining performance and responsiveness.

Demand Decreases

MIGs gracefully shut down excess instances, optimizing resource usage and saving costs.

Concrete Example

An e-commerce site experiences a holiday traffic surge. MIGs automatically add web servers, ensuring smooth customer experience without manual intervention.

Key Takeaway

Auto-scaling in MIGs provides elasticity, ensuring applications perform optimally during peak loads and reduce costs during low demand periods.
