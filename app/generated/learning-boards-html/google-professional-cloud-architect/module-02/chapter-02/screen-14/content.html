Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Ensuring stable and cost-effective MIG deployments.

Misconfigured Health Checks

Using generic checks instead of application-specific ones can lead to unhealthy instances serving traffic or healthy ones being replaced.

Overly Aggressive Auto-scaling

Short cool-down periods or sensitive thresholds can cause 'thrashing' (rapid scaling up/down), increasing costs and instability.

Best Practice

Always test your auto-scaling and health check configurations thoroughly under simulated load to fine-tune parameters and ensure stable, cost-effective behavior.

Key Takeaway

Proactive testing and careful configuration of health checks and auto-scaling policies are essential to avoid common MIG misconfigurations and ensure stable, cost-efficient operations.
