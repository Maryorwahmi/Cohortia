Coldline, Archive, and Object Lifecycle Management

Automating cost savings for infrequently accessed and archival data.

Coldline Storage

  • Access Frequency: Less than once a quarter.
  • Cost: Lower storage, higher retrieval, 90-day minimum.
Use Case

Archives, long-term backups with occasional access.

Archive Storage

  • Access Frequency: Less than once a year.
  • Cost: Lowest storage, highest retrieval, 365-day minimum.
Use Case

Regulatory archives, digital preservation, disaster recovery.

Object Lifecycle Management

Policies to automatically transition objects between storage classes based on age or other criteria, optimizing costs over time.

Example

Move data from Standard to Nearline after 30 days, then to Coldline after 90 days, and to Archive after a year.

Key Takeaway

Leveraging Coldline and Archive storage with automated lifecycle management is essential for cost-effectively managing long-term and rarely accessed data.
