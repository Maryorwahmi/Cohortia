Hierarchy & Inheritance

IAM Policy Inheritance in the Resource Hierarchy

Understanding how policies flow down from Organization to Resources.

Hierarchical Structure

GCP resources are organized in a hierarchy: Organization > Folders > Projects > Resources.

Policy Flow

Policies defined at a higher level are automatically inherited by all child resources below them.

Security Implications

Overly permissive roles at high levels can lead to unintended broad access, a common security pitfall.

Key Takeaway

Always consider the cascading effect of IAM policies when applying them at any level of the resource hierarchy.
