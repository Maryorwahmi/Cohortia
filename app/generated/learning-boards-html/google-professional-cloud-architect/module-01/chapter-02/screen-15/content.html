Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential takeaways from Identity and Access Management.

Primitive Roles

Broad, legacy roles (Owner, Editor, Viewer); generally discouraged.

Predefined Roles

Granular, GCP-managed roles tailored to specific services; recommended.

Custom Roles

User-defined roles with specific permissions, for when predefined roles are insufficient.

Service Account

A special Google account used by applications or VMs for authorized API calls.

Principle of Least Privilege

The security principle of granting only the minimum necessary permissions.

Summary Insight

Effective IAM involves choosing the right role type, securing service accounts, and consistently applying least privilege.
