Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential takeaways from Identity and Access Management.

Member

An identity (user, service account, group) that can be granted access.

Role

A collection of permissions that defines what actions a member can perform.

Resource

Any GCP asset (project, bucket, VM) that can have an IAM policy applied.

IAM Policy

A collection of role bindings defining who has what access on a resource.

Inheritance

Policies set at higher levels in the hierarchy apply to all resources below.

Summary Insight

IAM's core components and hierarchical inheritance are fundamental to defining and enforcing access control in GCP.
