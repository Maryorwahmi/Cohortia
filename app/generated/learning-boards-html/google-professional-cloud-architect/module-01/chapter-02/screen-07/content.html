Role Types

Primitive Roles: Broad Access, High Risk

Understanding the legacy roles and why they are generally discouraged.

Extensive Permissions

Grant very broad access across an entire GCP project, often including billing and IAM management.

Legacy Roles

These roles (Owner, Editor, Viewer) are older and less granular compared to modern predefined roles.

Violate Least Privilege

They grant more permissions than typically needed, increasing the attack surface and security risk.

Key Takeaway

Avoid primitive roles for routine operations; prioritize granular roles to enforce the principle of least privilege.

Primitive Roles Overview
Owner

Full control over all resources, billing, and IAM policies.

Editor

Can deploy and manage resources, but cannot manage billing or IAM.

Viewer

Can view all resources and data, but cannot make any changes.

Common Mistake:

Using 'Editor' for a service account that only needs to read data. This grants unnecessary write permissions.
