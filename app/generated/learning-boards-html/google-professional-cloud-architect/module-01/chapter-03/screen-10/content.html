Understanding Network Routes

Guiding Data Flow Within Your VPC Network

What are Routes?

Rules that determine how traffic flows within your VPC network and to external destinations.

Default Route

Automatically directs traffic to the internet, enabling external communication.

Custom Static Routes

Direct traffic to specific 'next hops' like VPN tunnels, virtual appliances, or other instances.

Key Takeaway

Routes are essential for controlling traffic flow, using default and custom rules to guide data.

Route Example: Traffic to a NAT Gateway

A custom route directs private VM traffic through a NAT Gateway to reach the internet.
