Controlling Traffic with Firewall Rules

Ingress and Egress Protection for Your Instances

What are Firewall Rules?

Rules that control which traffic is allowed to enter (ingress) or leave (egress) your VM instances.

VPC Network Level

Defined at the VPC network level, applying to all instances by default, but can be scoped.

Granular Control

Use target tags or service accounts to apply rules to specific sets of instances.

Key Takeaway

Firewall rules are critical for securing your VPC, managing ingress/egress traffic with precise control.

Firewall Rule in Action

Firewall rules act as a barrier, allowing specific ingress traffic while blocking unwanted egress.
