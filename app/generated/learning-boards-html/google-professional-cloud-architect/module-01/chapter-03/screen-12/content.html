Pitfalls to Avoid

Avoiding Common VPC Networking Mistakes

Ensuring Robust and Secure Network Design

Misconfigured Firewall Rules

Either too permissive (security vulnerability) or too restrictive (blocking legitimate traffic).

Overlapping IP Ranges

Causes routing conflicts between VPCs or with on-premises networks.

Lack of Network Segmentation

Increases security risk by allowing easy lateral movement in case of a breach.

Key Takeaway

Proactive design and regular review prevent common networking mistakes, enhancing security and reliability.

Impact of Networking Mistakes

Overlapping IP ranges can lead to routing conflicts, preventing VMs from communicating.
