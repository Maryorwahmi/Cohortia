Expanding Your Database Toolkit

Brief overview of BigQuery and Cloud Bigtable for specific big data needs.

BigQuery

A fully managed, serverless data warehouse for large-scale analytics. Ideal for business intelligence, reporting, and machine learning on petabytes of data.

  • SQL-based queries over massive datasets.
  • Optimized for analytical workloads.
  • Serverless and highly scalable.

Cloud Bigtable

A high-performance NoSQL wide-column database for massive-scale operational analytics and IoT data.

  • Millions of reads/writes per second.
  • Low-latency access to large datasets.
  • Ideal for time-series, financial, and IoT data.
Key Takeaway

BigQuery and Cloud Bigtable are powerful specialized databases for analytics and high-throughput NoSQL workloads, complementing the core transactional services.
