GCP Zones: High Availability within Regions

Achieving fault tolerance through isolated data centers.

1

Definition: An isolated location within a region, representing a distinct data center or cluster of data centers.

2

Independence: Each zone has independent power, cooling, and networking, ensuring isolation from other zones.

3

High Availability: Multi-zone deployments within a region are fundamental for achieving fault tolerance and minimizing downtime.

Key Takeaway

Zones provide the granular isolation needed to build highly available applications within a single GCP region.
