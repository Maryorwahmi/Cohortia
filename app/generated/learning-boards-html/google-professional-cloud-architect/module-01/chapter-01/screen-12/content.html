High Availability (HA) and Disaster Recovery (DR)

Designing resilient solutions with GCP's global infrastructure.

1

High Availability (HA): Ensures continuous operation by distributing workloads across multiple zones within a single region.

2

Disaster Recovery (DR): Recovers from widespread outages by deploying resources across two or more distinct regions.

3

Strategy: HA minimizes downtime from localized failures; DR ensures business continuity from regional disasters.

Key Takeaway

Leveraging GCP's multi-zone and multi-region capabilities is essential for building highly available and disaster-resilient applications.
