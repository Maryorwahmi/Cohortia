Key Concepts: Resource Hierarchy & Principles

Essential terms for managing and designing on GCP.

Edge Location (PoP)

Distributed network locations closer to end-users for caching and network ingress/egress.

Organization

The top-level node in the GCP resource hierarchy, representing a company or entity.

Project

The fundamental container for all GCP resources, acting as a trust, billing, and logical boundary.

Summary Takeaway

Effective GCP architecture relies on understanding the resource hierarchy for governance and leveraging infrastructure for high availability and performance.
