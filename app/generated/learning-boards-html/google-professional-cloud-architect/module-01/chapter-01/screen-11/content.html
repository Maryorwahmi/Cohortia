GCP Resource Hierarchy: Individual Resources

The building blocks within your projects and their management implications.

1

Definition: Any service or component deployed within a GCP project (e.g., VM, database, storage bucket).

2

Project Scope: Every resource must belong to a project, which acts as its logical and billing container.

3

Management Implications: Enables granular control over access (IAM), billing, and resource quotas at the project level.

Key Takeaway

Individual resources are the operational units within GCP projects, managed and governed by the overarching hierarchy.
