Data Residency and Compliance

Meeting regulatory requirements with GCP's regional choices.

1

Definition: Requirement that data must be stored and processed within specific geographic boundaries.

2

Regulatory Driver: Often mandated by laws like GDPR, HIPAA, or national data sovereignty acts.

3

GCP Solution: Architects select specific GCP regions to ensure data physically resides within required geographic boundaries.

Key Takeaway

GCP's regional choices are fundamental for meeting strict data residency and regulatory compliance requirements.

Compliance Example: GDPR

European Union

Data must reside within EU

GCP Region: europe-west1

Ensures data stays in Belgium
