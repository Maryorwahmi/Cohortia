GCP Resource Hierarchy: Folders and Projects

Organizing resources for granular control and policy enforcement.

1

Folders: Group projects for hierarchical management and policy enforcement (e.g., by department or environment).

2

Projects: Fundamental containers for all GCP resources, acting as trust, billing, and logical boundaries.

3

Unique Identifiers: Each project has a unique Project ID and Project Number for identification and API interactions.

Key Takeaway

Folders and Projects provide essential layers for organizing, managing, and applying policies to your GCP resources effectively.
