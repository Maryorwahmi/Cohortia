GCP Resource Hierarchy: The Organization Node

Structuring your cloud environment from the top down.

1

Top-Level Container: Represents your company or entity, acting as the root for all GCP resources.

2

Centralized Control: Enables unified management of IAM, billing, and organizational policies across your entire cloud footprint.

3

Policy Enforcement: Policies defined at this level automatically inherit down to all child resources (folders, projects, resources).

Key Takeaway

The Organization node is the foundational element for enterprise-level governance and consistent policy application in GCP.
