GCP Regions: Geographical Footprint

Understanding where Google Cloud hosts its data centers.

1

Definition: A specific geographical location where Google hosts its data centers.

2

Independence: Each region is an independent geographic area, isolated from other regions to minimize impact of outages.

3

Key Considerations: Choice of region impacts latency for users, data residency compliance, and service costs.

Key Takeaway

Regions are the primary geographical divisions of GCP infrastructure, crucial for global deployment strategies.
