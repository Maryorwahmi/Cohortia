Key Concepts: Global Infrastructure

Essential terms for GCP's physical layout.

Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

A suite of cloud computing services running on Google's internal infrastructure.

Region

A specific geographical location where Google hosts its data centers, composed of multiple zones.

Zone

An isolated location within a region, representing a distinct data center with independent infrastructure.

Summary Takeaway

GCP's global infrastructure, built on Regions and Zones, provides the foundation for scalable, highly available, and geographically compliant cloud solutions.
