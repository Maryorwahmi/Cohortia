Rolling Updates for Safe Deployments

Rolling updates allow safe deployment of new application versions or configurations across a MIG with minimal downtime and reduced risk.

How it Works
  • Updates instances gradually, not all at once.
  • Maintains service availability during updates.
  • Allows for early detection and rollback of issues.
Update Strategies
  • **Proactive:** Gradually replaces instances with new configuration.
  • **Opportunistic:** Replaces instances only during auto-healing or manual deletion.
