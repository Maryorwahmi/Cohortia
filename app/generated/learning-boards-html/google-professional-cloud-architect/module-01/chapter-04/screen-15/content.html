Architectural Best Practices

Avoiding Common Mistakes

Understanding common pitfalls helps in designing robust and secure Compute Engine deployments, optimizing for performance, cost, and reliability.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • **Under-provisioning:** Leads to performance bottlenecks.
  • **Ignoring MIGs:** Not leveraging for scalability and resilience.
  • **Misusing Preemptible VMs:** Using for critical or stateful workloads.
  • **Over-provisioning:** Wasting resources and increasing costs.
Security Best Practices
  • **IAM Roles:** Ensure proper, least-privilege access for VM management.
  • **OS Patching:** Regularly update operating systems to mitigate vulnerabilities.
  • **Private IPs:** Use for internal communication to enhance network security.
  • **Firewall Rules:** Configure strictly to control inbound/outbound traffic.

Designing for Success

Right-size VMs

Adhering to best practices ensures your Compute Engine deployments are secure, efficient, and resilient.

Key Takeaway

Proactive management, leveraging MIGs, and strong security practices are essential for successful and sustainable Compute Engine operations.
