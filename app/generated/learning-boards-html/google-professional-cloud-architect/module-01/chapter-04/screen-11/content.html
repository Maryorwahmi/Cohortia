Auto-healing for Reliability

Auto-healing continuously monitors instance health and automatically replaces unresponsive or unhealthy VMs, ensuring application resilience.

How it Works
  • **Health Checks:** Periodically verify instance responsiveness.
  • **Detection:** Identifies unhealthy instances (e.g., failed health checks).
  • **Replacement:** Deletes unhealthy instances and provisions new, healthy ones.
Benefits
  • **High Availability:** Minimizes downtime from individual VM failures.
  • **Automated Recovery:** No manual intervention needed for instance recovery.
  • **Increased Resilience:** Applications can withstand unexpected issues.
