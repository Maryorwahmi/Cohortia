Instance Groups: Managing VMs

Instance Groups allow you to manage a collection of VM instances as a single entity, moving beyond individual VM management for complex applications.

Why Group VMs?
  • **Simplified Management:** Apply policies to many VMs at once.
  • **Load Distribution:** Easily integrate with load balancers.
  • **High Availability:** Distribute workloads to avoid single points of failure.
  • **Scalability:** Prepare for automatic scaling based on demand.
Real-World Scenario

Instead of managing 10 individual web servers, an Instance Group allows you to manage them as one logical unit, simplifying updates, scaling, and monitoring.
