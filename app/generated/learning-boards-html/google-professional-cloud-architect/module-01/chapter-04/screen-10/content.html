MIG Feature Highlight

Auto-scaling for Dynamic Demand

Auto-scaling automatically adds or removes instances from a MIG based on metrics, adapting to fluctuating application demand.

How it Works
  • Monitors metrics (CPU utilization, HTTP load, custom metrics).
  • Scales out (adds instances) when demand increases.
  • Scales in (removes instances) when demand decreases.
Benefits
  • **Optimized Performance:** Handles traffic spikes without degradation.
  • **Cost Efficiency:** Avoids paying for idle resources during low demand.
  • **Reduced Manual Effort:** Eliminates the need for manual scaling adjustments.

Key Takeaway

Auto-scaling is vital for creating elastic applications that can efficiently handle varying workloads, ensuring performance and cost-effectiveness.
