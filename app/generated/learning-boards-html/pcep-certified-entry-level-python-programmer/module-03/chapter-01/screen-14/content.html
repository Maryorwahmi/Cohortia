What to Remember Key Concepts — Part 2
Bounds check
Use len(list) to confirm indexes before access.
Removal intent
Decide whether to remove by index, by first matching value, or to capture the removed item.
Reference safety
Copy lists when you need independent containers: use slicing or list().
Final takeaway

Practice with small scripts, print intermediate states, and ask the three checks—bounds, removal intent, and references—before changing lists.
