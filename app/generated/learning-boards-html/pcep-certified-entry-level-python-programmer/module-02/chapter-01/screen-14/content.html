Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What to Remember
Read Conditions Aloud
Saying conditions in plain language helps ensure they do what you intend.
Order Checks Carefully
Put specific tests before general ones to prevent earlier matches from blocking correct branches.
Provide Safe Defaults
Use else or explicit validation to keep your program predictable when inputs are unexpected.
Final Takeaway

Small habits — clear conditions, ordered checks, and explicit defaults — dramatically reduce logic errors and improve maintainability.
