Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › format() and legacy styles

format()

Templates with placeholders and an explicit format call. Useful for templates coming from external sources.

name = "Alice"
age = 30
print("My name is {} and I am {} years old.".format(name, age))
print("Pi to two decimal places: {:.2f}".format(3.14159))

Percent (%) style

Legacy printf-style formatting. Works but is generally less readable and more fragile for complex templates.

print("My name is %s and I am %d years old." % (name, age))
Prefer f-strings for clarity. Use format() when templates are external or must be constructed at runtime without direct expression evaluation.
