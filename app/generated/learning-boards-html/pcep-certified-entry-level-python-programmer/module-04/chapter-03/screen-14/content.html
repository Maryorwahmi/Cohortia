Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Dictionary
A mutable mapping of unique keys to values for labeled access.
Access
Square brackets for required keys; get for safe defaults when keys might be missing.
Mutation
Assign to add or update, pop to remove and return, del to remove without return.
Summary

Dictionaries are versatile for modeling labeled data; apply safe access patterns, respect key immutability, and choose iteration methods that fit your task.
