Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › What to Remember
Keys & Values
Keys are immutable identifiers; values hold the data and can be nested.
Access Safety
Use square brackets for required keys and get for safe defaults when keys may be missing.
Iteration & Mutation
Loop with keys, values, or items. Assign, pop, del, and clear to change contents safely.
Final takeaway

Dictionaries are a powerful way to represent labeled data; practice safe access, proper key choices, and clear iteration to use them effectively.
