Learning Objectives

Progress from recognizing tuples to applying packing, unpacking, and safe use-cases.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Recognize immutability
Explain what immutability means for tuples

You will describe the safety and integrity advantages of a read-only sequence and why changing a tuple's item raises an error.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Create tuples
Use parentheses and comma-packed forms

You will produce normal and single-element tuples and avoid the trailing-comma mistake with a quick mnemonic.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Pack & unpack
Assign multiple variables in one statement

You will practice unpacking returned values and using tuple swaps for concise code.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Methods & use-cases
Count, index, and hashable uses

You will use count and index for inspections and recognize when tuples make better keys than lists.
