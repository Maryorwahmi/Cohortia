Resource Records

Defining Name Servers with NS Records

Declaring which servers are authoritative for your domain.

Purpose of NS Records

  • •**Delegation:** Tell the internet which servers are responsible for your domain.
  • •**Authoritative Source:** Point to your primary and any secondary DNS servers.
  • •**Redundancy:** Essential to list multiple NS records for high availability.
  • •**Accessibility:** Without correct NS records, your domain cannot be resolved.
Key Takeaway

NS records are crucial for delegating authority for your domain, directing queries to your designated name servers for resolution.

Code Walkthrough: `db.example.com` (NS Records)
; Name Servers
        IN      NS      ns1.example.com.
        IN      NS      ns2.example.com.

; Note: 'ns1' and 'ns2' must also have A records defined later.
Common Mistake

Forgetting the trailing dot `.` on the FQDN (e.g., `ns1.example.com.`). Without it, BIND might append the current origin, leading to incorrect resolution.
