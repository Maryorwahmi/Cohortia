Resource Records

Directing Email with MX Records

Specifying which servers handle email for your domain.

MX Record Structure & Priority

  • •**Purpose:** Specifies mail servers responsible for receiving email for your domain.
  • •**Preference Value:** A numerical priority (lower numbers are preferred).
  • •**Redundancy:** Multiple MX records with different preferences provide failover.
  • •**Requirement:** The mail server hostname (e.g., `mail.example.com`) must have a corresponding A/AAAA record.
Key Takeaway

MX records are essential for email delivery, guiding sending mail servers to the correct destination with a preference system for reliability.

Code Walkthrough: `db.example.com` (MX Records)
; MX Records (Mail Exchanger)
        IN      MX  10  mail.example.com.
        IN      MX  20  backupmail.example.com.

; Note: 'mail' and 'backupmail' must have A records.
Common Mistake

Not providing an A or AAAA record for the hostname specified in the MX record. The mail server needs an IP address to be reachable.
