Best Practices

LVM Best Practices and Common Pitfalls

Ensuring robust and error-free LVM deployments

  • •**Monitor VG Free Space:** Regularly check sudo vgs to anticipate storage needs.
  • •**Filesystem Resize Order (Extend):** Always lvextend then resize2fs/xfs_growfs.
  • •**Filesystem Resize Order (Reduce):** Unmount, resize2fs, then lvreduce.
  • •**Use Snapshots:** Leverage LVM snapshots for safe backups and testing.
  • •**Clear Naming:** Use descriptive names for PVs, VGs, and LVs.
Key Takeaway

Adhering to correct operational sequences, proactive monitoring, and leveraging LVM features like snapshots are vital for successful and safe LVM management.

Common Pitfalls

Mistakes to Avoid

  • •**No Free Space:** Trying to extend an LV when its VG has no free space.
  • •**Filesystem Not Resized:** Extending LV but forgetting to grow the filesystem.
  • •**LV Shrunk First:** Shrinking the LV before the filesystem (data loss risk).
  • •**Unmounted LV Removal:** Attempting to remove a mounted Logical Volume.
Pro Tip

Automate Monitoring

Integrate LVM free space monitoring into your system's alerting tools (e.g., Nagios, Prometheus). Set thresholds to warn you when VG free space drops below a certain percentage, allowing you to proactively add capacity before it becomes critical.
