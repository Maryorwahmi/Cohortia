LVM Cleanup: Removing Components Safely

Decommissioning Logical Volumes, Volume Groups, and Physical Volumes

01

Unmount LV

Before removal, ensure the Logical Volume is unmounted to prevent data corruption.

sudo umount /mnt/data_lv
02

Remove LV

Once unmounted, remove the Logical Volume from its Volume Group.

sudo lvremove /dev/my_vg/data_lv
03

Remove VG

After all LVs are removed, the Volume Group can be safely decommissioned.

sudo vgremove my_vg
04

Remove PV

Finally, remove LVM metadata from the Physical Volume once it's no longer part of any VG.

sudo pvremove /dev/sdb1
Key Takeaway

LVM component removal must follow a strict reverse order (LV, then VG, then PV) to ensure data integrity and avoid errors.
