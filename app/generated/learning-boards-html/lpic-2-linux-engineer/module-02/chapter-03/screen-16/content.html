Key Concepts: LVM Operations

Commands for managing LVM components

Logical Volume (LV)

A virtual partition carved out of a Volume Group, used by the OS.

lvcreate / lvextend

Commands to create a new LV and increase its size, respectively.

lvreduce / lvremove

Commands to decrease an LV's size (carefully!) and remove an LV.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering LVM operations involves creating, resizing, and safely removing Logical Volumes, Volume Groups, and Physical Volumes using their dedicated commands.
