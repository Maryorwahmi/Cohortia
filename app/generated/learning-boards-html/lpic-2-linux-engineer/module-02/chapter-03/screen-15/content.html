Key Concepts: LVM Fundamentals

Essential terms and commands for LVM

Logical Volume Management (LVM)

A system providing an abstraction layer over physical storage for flexible disk space management.

Physical Volume (PV)

A raw disk partition or entire disk initialized for use by LVM.

Volume Group (VG)

A collection of one or more PVs, acting as a pool of storage space.

Summary Takeaway

LVM provides a flexible storage hierarchy built from Physical Volumes, aggregated into Volume Groups, and then carved into Logical Volumes for dynamic management.
