The LVM Layered Abstraction

Understanding Physical Volumes, Volume Groups, and Logical Volumes

Physical Volumes (PVs)

Raw disk partitions or entire disks initialized for LVM. These are the foundational building blocks.

Raw Storage

Volume Groups (VGs)

A collection of one or more PVs, forming a single, flexible pool of storage capacity.

Pooled Capacity

Logical Volumes (LVs)

Virtual partitions carved out of a VG's free space, used by the OS like regular partitions.

Virtual Partitions
PVs form VGs
VGs yield LVs
Key Takeaway

LVM's layered structure—PVs, VGs, and LVs—provides a powerful abstraction that decouples logical storage from physical hardware, enabling dynamic management.
