Learning Objectives: Part 1

What you'll achieve in this chapter

Learn
Understand

01

Understand Quota Purpose

Grasp why disk quotas are vital for resource management and system stability in multi-user environments. This skill ensures fair resource distribution.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Configure `ext4` Quotas

Learn to enable and configure disk quotas specifically on `ext4` filesystems. This involves modifying `/etc/fstab` and remounting filesystems.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Manage User/Group Quotas

Master the use of `edquota`, `setquota`, `repquota`, and `quotaon`/`quotaoff` for comprehensive quota management. This ensures efficient resource allocation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Troubleshoot Quota Issues

Identify and resolve common problems related to disk quotas, such as incorrect `fstab` entries or aggressive limits. This ensures smooth system operation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
