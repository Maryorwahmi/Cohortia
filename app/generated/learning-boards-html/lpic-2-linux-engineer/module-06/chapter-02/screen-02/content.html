Skills You'll Master in This Chapter

01

Apply Advanced NFS Export Options

Learn to use options like `sync`, `async`, `root_squash`, and `anonuid` in `/etc/exports` to fine-tune share behavior. This skill is vital for optimizing performance and enforcing security policies.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Differentiate NFSv3 and NFSv4

Understand the key differences between NFSv3 and NFSv4, including stateful operations, firewall traversal, and security implications. This knowledge is crucial for selecting the appropriate protocol for your environment.
