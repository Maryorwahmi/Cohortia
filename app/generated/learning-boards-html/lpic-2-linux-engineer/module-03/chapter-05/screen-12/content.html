What's on the Wire? (`tcpdump`)

09

Review Logs

Clues from System/App Logs (`journalctl`)

Common Troubleshooting Mistakes

  • Jumping to conclusions without full diagnosis.
  • Not checking the most basic things first (e.g., cables).
  • Overlooking firewall rules as a cause of blocked traffic.
  • Failing to verify if a service is actually listening on its port.

Safety Note: Data Exposure

Always ensure proper authorization before using tools like `tcpdump` on production networks, as they can capture sensitive information. Understand the implications for data privacy and security.

Key Takeaway

A methodical, step-by-step approach is paramount for effective network troubleshooting. Combine tool usage with a systematic mindset to diagnose and resolve issues efficiently and safely.
