Key Concepts: Firewalld Essentials

A quick recap of the most important terms and ideas related to Firewalld.

Firewalld

A dynamic firewall management solution that uses zones to manage firewall rules, commonly found on RHEL-based systems.

Zones

Predefined sets of rules in firewalld that can be assigned to network interfaces, allowing different security policies based on network context.

firewall-cmd

The command-line utility for managing firewalld rules, used to query, add, and remove services, ports, and zones.

Chapter Takeaway

Firewalld simplifies complex network security by abstracting rules into manageable zones, making it the preferred choice for modern Linux systems.
