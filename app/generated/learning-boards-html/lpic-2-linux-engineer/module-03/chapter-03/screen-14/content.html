Key Concepts: iptables and Advanced Rules

A quick recap of the most important terms and ideas related to iptables and advanced Firewalld rules.

iptables

The traditional Linux firewall utility that directly interacts with the Netfilter framework, using chains and tables to filter packets.

Chains

Lists of rules within iptables (e.g., INPUT, OUTPUT, FORWARD) that packets traverse sequentially.

Targets

Actions taken by an iptables rule when a packet matches its criteria (e.g., ACCEPT, DROP, REJECT).

Chapter Takeaway

While Firewalld offers modern abstraction, iptables provides direct, low-level control, making both essential tools for comprehensive Linux network security.
