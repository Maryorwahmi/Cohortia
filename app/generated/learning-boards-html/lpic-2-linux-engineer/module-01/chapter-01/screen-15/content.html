Key Concepts: System Initialization & Management

A quick review of the most important terms from this chapter.

Kernel

The core of the operating system, responsible for managing system resources and acting as a bridge between hardware and software.

systemd

The modern init system and service manager that takes over after initramfs and manages the rest of the boot process and ongoing services.

systemd Units

Configuration files (e.g., .service, .mount, .target) that systemd uses to manage system resources.

systemd Targets

Groups of systemd units that define a specific system state or runlevel, like multi-user.target or graphical.target.

Summary Takeaway

The kernel is the heart of the OS, while systemd, with its units and targets, provides the modern framework for managing system initialization and ongoing services.
