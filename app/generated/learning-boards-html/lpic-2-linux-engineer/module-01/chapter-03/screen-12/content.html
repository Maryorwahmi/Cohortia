Bootloader Integration

Integrating with GRUB2 and Safety Practices

Ensuring your new kernel is bootable and stable

GRUB2 Update

After installation, run sudo update-grub to ensure GRUB2 recognizes and lists your new custom kernel in the boot menu.

Test Before Default

Boot into the new kernel once and thoroughly test its functionality and stability before making it your default boot option.

Key Takeaway

Always verify GRUB2 integration and maintain a known-working kernel as a fallback to ensure system resilience.

Fallback Kernel

Always keep at least one known-working kernel available in your GRUB menu as a safety net for unexpected issues.

Example: Updating GRUB
sudo update-grub
