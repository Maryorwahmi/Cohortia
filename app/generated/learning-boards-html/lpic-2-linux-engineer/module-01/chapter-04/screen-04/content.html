Understanding Boot Failure Causes

Recognizing the Symptoms of a Non-Booting System

GRUB2 Misconfiguration

Incorrect root or kernel paths prevent the bootloader from finding essential files, leading to a grub rescue> prompt.

Kernel Panic

A critical error where the kernel detects an unrecoverable problem, often due to missing or corrupted initramfs or incompatible modules.

Filesystem Corruption

Dirty unmounts or power failures can lead to partitions becoming unmountable, read-only, or showing data integrity issues.

/etc/fstab Errors

Incorrect mount points or options in the filesystem table can prevent partitions from mounting, causing boot delays or failures.

systemd Unit Failures

Critical services failing to start during the boot process can prevent the system from reaching a usable state, often requiring manual intervention.
