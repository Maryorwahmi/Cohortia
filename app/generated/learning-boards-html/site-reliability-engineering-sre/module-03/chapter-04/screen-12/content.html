Chapter Summary

Key Concepts

Essential ideas to remember from this chapter.

On-Call Rotation

A schedule ensuring a designated engineer is available to respond to critical incidents outside of normal working hours.

Alert Fatigue

Desensitization to alerts caused by excessive non-actionable notifications, leading to missed critical alerts.

Actionable Alert

An alert that clearly indicates a problem requiring immediate human intervention with enough context for diagnosis.

Summary Takeaway

Effective on-call and alerting are about balancing system reliability with engineer well-being, ensuring critical issues are addressed without causing burnout.
