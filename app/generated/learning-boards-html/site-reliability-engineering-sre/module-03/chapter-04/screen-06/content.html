Operational Workflow

Establishing Clear Escalation Policies

Guiding incident resolution when primary support needs help.

Why Escalation is Crucial

  • Prevents Stalling: Ensures incidents don't get stuck with one person.
  • Reduces Downtime: Faster resolution by involving appropriate expertise.
  • Distributes Load: Spreads the burden of complex issues.
  • Clear Chain of Command: Defines roles and responsibilities during an incident.
Key Tools

PagerDuty, Opsgenie, and VictorOps are invaluable for automating rotations, scheduling, and escalation paths.

Example: A Tiered Escalation Path

01

Primary On-Call: Initial responder, attempts resolution.

02

Secondary On-Call: Engaged if primary needs help or doesn't respond within 15 min.

03

Team Lead/Manager: Engaged if secondary cannot resolve within 30 min.

04

Incident Response Team: For major incidents, engaged by team lead.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined escalation policy is a critical safety net, ensuring no incident is left unaddressed and expertise is brought in efficiently.
