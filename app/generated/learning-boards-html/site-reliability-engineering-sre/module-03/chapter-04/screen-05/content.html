Crafting Sustainable On-Call Rotations

Balancing incident response with engineer well-being.

Key Elements of Sustainability

  • Reasonable Shifts: Typically one week on-call, followed by several weeks off.
  • Adequate Coverage: Primary and secondary responders ensure backup.
  • Manageable Workload: Incident volume and severity should not overwhelm.
Common Mistake

Putting too few people on call or having rotations that are too long leads directly to engineer fatigue and burnout.

Example: A Balanced Rotation

Shift Duration: 1 week primary, 1 week secondary, 4 weeks off-call.

Team Size: Minimum 4-6 engineers to support rotation.

Incident Review: Regular post-mortems to reduce toil.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize engineer well-being in rotation design; a rested team is a reliable team.
