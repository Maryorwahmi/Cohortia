Core Concept

The Core of On-Call in SRE

Ensuring prompt incident response while safeguarding team well-being.

What is On-Call?

On-call is a designated schedule where an engineer is available to respond to critical incidents that automated systems cannot resolve. It's about ensuring human intervention when necessary.

Why it Matters for SRE

  • Minimizes User Impact: Ensures critical issues are addressed promptly.
  • Maintains Service Reliability: Prevents prolonged outages and degradation.
  • Supports Team Health: A well-designed system prevents burnout.
Common Pitfall

Poorly designed rotations lead to burnout, high turnover, and ultimately, less reliable systems due to fatigued engineers.

The On-Call Imperative

Availability: Always a designated engineer ready to respond.

Promptness: Critical issues addressed quickly to minimize user impact.

Sustainability: Designed to protect engineer well-being and prevent burnout.

Key Takeaway

On-call is essential for reliability, but its design directly impacts both system health and the human engineers behind it.
