Core Principle

Balancing Reliability and Innovation

How error budgets foster healthy tension between stability and progress.

Without Error Budgets

  • •Pressure for 100% reliability, often unfeasible.
  • •Stifles feature development and innovation.
  • •Risk of introducing too much instability without clear targets.
Consequence

Teams struggle with subjective reliability goals, leading to either stagnation or instability.

With Error Budgets

  • •Provides a quantifiable boundary for acceptable unreliability.
  • •Allows flexibility for risk-taking and faster feature deployment.
  • •Ensures reliability remains a first-class concern, driven by data.
Benefit

Teams can make data-driven decisions, balancing innovation with agreed-upon reliability targets.
