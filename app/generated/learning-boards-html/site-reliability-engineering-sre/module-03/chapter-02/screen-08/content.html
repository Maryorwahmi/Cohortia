When the Error Budget is Healthy

Leveraging a healthy budget for innovation and strategic risk-taking.

Budget Status: Healthy

  • •Well within the allowable unreliability.
  • •Indicates strong service performance against SLOs.
  • •Provides a buffer for potential issues.
Key Takeaway

A healthy budget empowers teams to innovate and take calculated risks, knowing they have a reliability buffer.

Actions to Consider

  • •Deploy new features faster, even with minor risks.
  • •Conduct experiments that might introduce temporary instability.
  • •Perform risky database migrations or scheduled maintenance.
Example Scenario

Rolling out a new feature to a small percentage of users, anticipating minor, temporary instability.
