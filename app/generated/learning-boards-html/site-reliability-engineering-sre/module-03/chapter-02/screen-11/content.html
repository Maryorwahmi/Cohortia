Decision Point

Scenario: Risky Deployment Decision

Using error budgets to guide critical engineering choices.

Scenario: Healthy Budget

  • •Service has 30 minutes of error budget remaining.
  • •New feature deployment carries a small risk of 5-10 minutes downtime.
SRE Recommendation

Proceed with careful planning and rollback strategy, leveraging the budget buffer.

Scenario: Depleted Budget

  • •Service has only 2 minutes of error budget remaining.
  • •Same new feature deployment with 5-10 minutes risk.
SRE Recommendation

Strongly advise against deployment; prioritize testing, phased rollout, or delay until next budget cycle.
