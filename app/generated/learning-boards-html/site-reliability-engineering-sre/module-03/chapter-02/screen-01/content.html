Mastering Error Budgets

Balancing Reliability with Innovation in SRE

Error budgets are a powerful, practical tool for managing reliability. They transform abstract reliability targets into a concrete, finite resource that teams can consciously 'spend,' fostering a healthy tension between perfect reliability and rapid innovation.

Key Idea

The allowable amount of unreliability over a given period, derived directly from your Service Level Objective (SLO).
