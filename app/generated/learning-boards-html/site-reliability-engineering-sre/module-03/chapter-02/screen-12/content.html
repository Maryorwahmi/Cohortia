Avoiding Common Error Budget Mistakes

Ensuring your error budget remains an effective tool.

Mistake 1: Treating as a Target

  • •Viewing the budget as an allowance to be used up.
  • •The goal is to provide the best service, staying well within the budget.
Correction

The error budget is a ceiling on unreliability, not a target to reach.

Mistake 2: Lack of Consequences

  • •No change in priorities when the budget is depleted.
  • •Budget becomes a meaningless metric without organizational buy-in.
Correction

Ensure clear, agreed-upon actions and shifts in focus when the budget is consumed.
