Understanding the Error Budget

The allowable amount of unreliability derived from your SLOs.

Error Budget Defined

An error budget is simply 1 minus your Service Level Objective (SLO). It represents the maximum acceptable amount of unreliability or downtime over a defined period.

  • SLO: 99.9% uptime
  • Error Budget: 0.1% (100% - 99.9%)
  • This 0.1% is the 'budget' for acceptable failures.
Key Takeaway

Error budgets provide a clear, data-driven mechanism to manage the trade-off between reliability and the pace of innovation.

Using the Budget

  • If budget is healthy: Consider more aggressive feature development or tightening SLOs.
  • If budget is depleted: Prioritize reliability work, potentially delaying new features.
  • Planned downtime: Can 'spend' from the error budget for maintenance.
Example

If your SLO is 99.99% availability over 30 days, your error budget is 0.01%, which translates to roughly 4 minutes and 20 seconds of downtime per month.
