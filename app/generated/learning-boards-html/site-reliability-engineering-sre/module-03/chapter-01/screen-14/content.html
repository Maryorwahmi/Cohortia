Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms to remember from this chapter.

Service Level Indicator (SLI)

A quantitative measure of some aspect of the service provided, directly reflecting user experience (e.g., latency, error rate).

Service Level Objective (SLO)

A target value or range for an SLI over a specific period, representing a commitment to a certain level of service (e.g., "99.9% of requests within 300ms").

Error Budget

The allowable amount of unreliability or downtime over a period, derived from 1 minus the SLO. It balances reliability with feature development.

Summary Takeaway

SLIs, SLOs, and Error Budgets are foundational SRE tools for defining, measuring, and managing service reliability from a user-centric perspective.
