Core Concept

Defining Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

Setting target values for your chosen SLIs over a specific period.

SLO Definition

An SLO is a target value or range for an SLI, defined over a specific period. It's a commitment to your users about the level of service they can expect from your system.

  • Combines an SLI with a **target percentage** and a **time window**.
  • Represents a **service commitment** to users.
  • Provides a **quantifiable goal** for reliability.
Key Takeaway

SLOs transform raw metrics (SLIs) into actionable goals, guiding teams to deliver consistent service quality.

SLO Example

"99.9% of user requests must complete within 300ms over a 30-day rolling window."

  • SLI: Requests completing within 300ms.
  • Objective: 99.9% of these requests.
  • Period: Over a 30-day rolling window.
Pro-Tip

SLOs should be challenging but achievable. Too ambitious leads to demoralization; too lax provides no incentive for improvement.
