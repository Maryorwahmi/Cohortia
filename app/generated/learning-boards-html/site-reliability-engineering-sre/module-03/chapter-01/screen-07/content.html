Strategic Alignment

SLOs, User Experience, and Business Impact

Aligning reliability targets with critical user journeys and business value.

Critical User Journeys

These are the sequences of actions users take that are essential for the core functionality or business value of your service. SLOs should prioritize these journeys.

  • Login: Essential for accessing personalized content.
  • Checkout: Direct impact on revenue for e-commerce.
  • Content Upload: Core functionality for media platforms.
Key Takeaway

Focus SLOs on what truly matters to users and the business; avoid setting too many, which can dilute focus.

The Golden Signals

These are four key metrics for monitoring any user-facing system, providing a holistic view of its health:

  • Latency: Time taken to serve a request.
  • Traffic: How much demand is being placed on your system.
  • Errors: Rate of requests that fail.
  • Saturation: How 'full' your service is (e.g., CPU, memory, network I/O).
Example

For a video conferencing app, 'join meeting latency' and 'audio/video stream error rate' are critical, directly impacting user experience and business value.
