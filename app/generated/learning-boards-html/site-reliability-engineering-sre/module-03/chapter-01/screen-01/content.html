Defining Reliability: SLIs and SLOs

Quantifying Service Performance from a User's Perspective

In this chapter, you'll learn how to establish clear, measurable targets for your service's performance and reliability. We'll explore how to identify key metrics that truly reflect user experience and set objectives that drive meaningful improvements.

Key Takeaway

SLIs and SLOs provide concrete, measurable ways to define and track service reliability from a user's perspective, moving beyond vague notions of 'uptime'.
