Implementation Process

The SLO Definition Workflow

A step-by-step approach to defining and tracking SLIs and SLOs.

1. Understand User Needs

Start by identifying user expectations and the business impact of service failures. What's critical for your users?

2. Define SLIs & SLOs

Translate user needs into measurable SLIs (e.g., login latency) and set specific SLO targets (e.g., 99.9% within 1s).

3. Instrument & Monitor

Implement monitoring to collect metrics for your SLIs. Ensure your tools can accurately track and report against your SLOs.

4. Review & Refine

Continuously review your SLI/SLO performance. Adjust targets or monitoring as your service evolves and user needs change.

5. Manage Error Budget

Use the error budget (derived from SLOs) to make data-driven decisions about risk, feature development, and reliability work.

Key Takeaway

A robust SLO workflow integrates user understanding, clear targets, effective monitoring, and continuous improvement.
