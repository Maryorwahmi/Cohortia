Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 2

Additional essential terms for SRE practice.

Critical User Journey

A sequence of actions a user takes that is essential for the core functionality or business value of the service (e.g., logging in, completing a purchase).

Golden Signals

Four key metrics for monitoring any user-facing system: Latency, Traffic, Errors, and Saturation, providing a holistic view of health.

Proactive Management

The combined use of SLIs, SLOs, error budgets, critical user journeys, and golden signals enables proactive reliability management.

Summary Takeaway

By focusing on critical user journeys and monitoring golden signals, SRE teams can proactively ensure service health and user satisfaction.
