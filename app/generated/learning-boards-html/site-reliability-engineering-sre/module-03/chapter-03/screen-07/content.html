Response Actions

From Triage to Resolution

Rapid assessment and restoration of service functionality.

1. Triage

Assess impact and urgency.

2. Mitigation

Restore service functionality (temporary fix).

3. Resolution

Implement permanent fixes.

Example Scenario

A critical API is returning 500 errors. Triage identifies high user impact. Mitigation involves rolling back the last deployment. Resolution then focuses on debugging the root cause and deploying a fixed version.

Focus during Mitigation

The priority is always speed and impact reduction. Finding the root cause is secondary to restoring service functionality.

Key Takeaway

A clear progression from triage to mitigation to resolution ensures rapid service restoration and systematic problem-solving.
