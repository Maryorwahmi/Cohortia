Core Concept

The Incident Management Lifecycle

A structured approach to handling service disruptions.

1. Detection

Identify the issue via alerts or user reports.

2. Response

Triage, mitigate, and resolve the incident.

3. Learning

Conduct postmortems to prevent recurrence.

Why it matters

A structured process prevents chaos, minimizes downtime, and builds trust with users and stakeholders.

Concrete Example

A sudden database overload triggers alerts (Detection). The team quickly rolls back a recent change (Response: Mitigation). Later, they analyze why the change caused the overload (Learning: Postmortem).

Key Takeaway

Incidents are inevitable; a well-defined management lifecycle is essential for resilience and continuous improvement.
