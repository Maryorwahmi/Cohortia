Postmortem Best Practices

Anatomy of a Postmortem Document

Key sections for comprehensive incident analysis.

1. Incident Summary

A high-level overview of what happened, when, and its impact. Provides quick context for readers.

2. Timeline of Events

A detailed, chronological account of detection, diagnosis, mitigation, and resolution steps. Helps identify delays and critical decision points.

3. Root Cause Analysis

Investigation into the underlying factors that contributed to the incident, often using techniques like the '5 Whys'.

4. Lessons Learned & Action Items

What was learned and what specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) actions will be taken to prevent recurrence or improve response.

Why Structure Matters

A consistent structure ensures all critical aspects of an incident are covered, making postmortems easier to conduct, review, and learn from.

Avoid

Skipping sections or providing vague details. This undermines the learning process and makes it difficult to implement effective changes.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive postmortem document is a vital tool for organizational learning and building more resilient systems over time.
