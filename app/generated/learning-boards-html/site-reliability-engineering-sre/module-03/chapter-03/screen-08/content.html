Critical Skill

Communication During Incidents

Keeping stakeholders informed, internally and externally.

Internal Communication

  • IC coordinates responders.
  • Ensures information flow among technical teams.
  • Prevents duplicated efforts and miscommunication.

External Communication

  • Comms Lead informs affected users and stakeholders.
  • Provides timely updates on status and expected resolution.
  • Utilizes status pages for transparency.
Why it matters

Timely and transparent communication builds and maintains trust with users and internal stakeholders, even during outages.

Common Mistake

Delaying communication or providing vague updates, which can lead to increased frustration and erosion of trust.

Key Takeaway

Effective communication is the cornerstone of a successful incident response, ensuring coordination and maintaining confidence.
