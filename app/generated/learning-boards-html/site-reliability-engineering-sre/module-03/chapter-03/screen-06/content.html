Phase 2: Response

Defining Incident Response Roles

Clear responsibilities for effective incident coordination.

Incident Commander (IC)

Leads the overall response, focuses on coordination, communication, and high-level decision-making. Not involved in technical execution.

Communications Lead

Manages all internal and external communications, keeping stakeholders informed without distracting technical responders.

Technical Responders

Engineers actively diagnosing, debugging, and mitigating the issue. They report findings and actions to the IC.

Why it matters

Clear roles prevent confusion, reduce cognitive load, and ensure efficient, parallel work streams during high-pressure situations.

Common Mistake

Lack of a single Incident Commander or having technical responders also manage communications, leading to fragmented efforts and delays.

Key Takeaway

Well-defined roles are the backbone of an effective incident response, ensuring clarity and accountability.
