Essential Concepts in Postmortems

Key ideas for learning and continuous improvement.

Postmortem (Blameless)

A detailed, non-judgmental analysis of an incident to understand causes and derive actionable improvements.

Root Cause Analysis

The process of identifying the fundamental reasons for an incident, often using techniques like the '5 Whys'.

Action Items

Specific, measurable, and time-bound tasks derived from a postmortem to prevent recurrence or improve future response.

Summary Takeaway

Blameless postmortems, root cause analysis, and actionable items are critical for continuous learning and building resilient SRE systems.
