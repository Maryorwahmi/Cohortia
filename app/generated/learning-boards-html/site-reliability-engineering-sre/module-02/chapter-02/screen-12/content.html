Module 2›Chapter 2.2›Precision in SLI Definition
1

What is being measured?

Be specific: all requests, specific types (e.g., read-only), or critical business transactions?

2

Where is it being measured?

Client-side, load balancer, or within the application? Client-side often best reflects user experience.

3

How is it aggregated?

Time window (e.g., 5-min rolling), averages, sums, or percentiles (p90, p99 for latency).

Key Takeaway

Precision in SLI definition, especially regarding measurement scope, location, and aggregation (favoring percentiles for latency), ensures meaningful reliability insights.

Pro-Tip

Always use percentiles (e.g., p99) for latency SLIs to capture the experience of the slowest users, not just the average.
