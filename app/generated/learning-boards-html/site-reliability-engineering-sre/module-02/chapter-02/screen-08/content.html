What is an SLI?

A Service Level Indicator (SLI) is a carefully chosen, quantifiable measure of some aspect of the level of service that is provided, directly reflecting user satisfaction.

Quantifiable

Must be measurable and expressed numerically.

User-Centric

Directly reflects the experience and satisfaction of the end-user.

Role of SLIs in SRE

  • •Foundation for SLOs/SLAs: SLIs are the raw data points used to define Service Level Objectives and Agreements.
  • •Objective Reliability: Provides an unbiased view of service performance.
  • •Prioritization: Helps SREs focus on issues that truly impact users.

A good SLI must be measurable, understandable, and directly reflect user experience.

Key Takeaway

SLIs translate raw metrics into meaningful indicators of service reliability, always with the user's perspective at the forefront.

Common Misconception

Confusing any metric with an SLI. An SLI is a carefully selected metric that directly impacts user satisfaction.
