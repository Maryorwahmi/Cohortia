What are Metrics?

Metrics are numerical measurements collected over time, providing a quantifiable way to track the behavior and performance of your systems and applications.

Numerical Data

Metrics are always numbers, allowing for aggregation, trending, and thresholding.

Time-Series

Collected at regular intervals, enabling us to observe changes and patterns over time.

Why Metrics Matter for SREs

  • •Early Anomaly Detection: Spot deviations from normal behavior before they impact users.
  • •Performance Tracking: Monitor service speed, efficiency, and resource consumption.
  • •Capacity Planning: Understand resource needs and predict future scaling requirements.
  • •Post-Mortem Analysis: Diagnose root causes of incidents using historical data.
Key Takeaway

Metrics are the essential raw data that provide objective insights into system health, enabling SREs to proactively manage and improve service reliability.

Common Misconception

Metrics alone don't tell the whole story. They need context and interpretation to become actionable insights.
