Error Rate SLI

Measures the proportion of requests that result in an error, indicating service instability.

Calculation Example

(error requests / total requests) * 100%

Includes HTTP 5xx, application-specific errors, or failed jobs.

A low error rate is critical for maintaining user trust and ensuring service functionality.

Why it matters

Directly impacts user experience and the ability to complete tasks.

Throughput / Traffic

Measures the number of requests processed per unit of time, indicating load and capacity.

Key Role

Requests per second (RPS)

While not always an SLI, it's crucial for understanding service load and capacity planning.

Monitoring throughput helps ensure your service can handle expected and unexpected traffic spikes.

Why it matters

Essential for capacity planning and detecting service degradation under load.

Common Mistake

Ignoring throughput when other metrics look good. A sudden drop can indicate a silent failure or user abandonment.
