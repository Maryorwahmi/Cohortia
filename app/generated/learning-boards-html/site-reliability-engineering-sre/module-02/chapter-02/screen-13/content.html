Module 2›Chapter 2.2›Common SLI Pitfalls

Too Many SLIs

Focusing on too many metrics dilutes attention and makes it harder to identify critical issues. Stick to a few impactful ones.

Unmeasurable SLIs

Defining an SLI for something you cannot reliably collect data for is pointless. Ensure your tools support the measurement.

Averaging Away Problems

Relying solely on averages for latency or error rates can hide significant issues for a subset of users. Use percentiles!

Ignoring Context

An SLI might look bad during a scheduled maintenance window, which is expected. Always interpret data with operational context.

Key Takeaway

Proactively identify and avoid common SLI pitfalls to ensure your reliability measurements are accurate, actionable, and truly reflect user experience.
