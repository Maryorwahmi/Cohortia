Key Concepts: SLI Types & Best Practices

Critical SLI types and effective measurement techniques

Availability SLI

Measures the proportion of time a service is accessible and operational.

Latency SLI

Measures the time it takes for a service to respond, often expressed as a percentile.

Error Rate SLI

Measures the proportion of requests that result in an error.

Percentiles (p90, p99)

Statistical measures for latency SLIs to capture the experience of a large percentage of users.

PromQL

Prometheus Query Language, used for querying and aggregating metrics in Prometheus.

Chapter Summary

Specific SLI types like availability, latency, and error rate are crucial. Using percentiles for latency and PromQL for querying ensures precise and actionable reliability measurements.
