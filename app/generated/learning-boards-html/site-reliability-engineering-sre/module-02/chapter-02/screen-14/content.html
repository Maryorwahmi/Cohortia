Key Concepts: Metrics Foundation

Essential terms for understanding service health

System Metrics

Low-level measurements of infrastructure components (e.g., CPU, memory, disk I/O).

Application Metrics

Measurements emitted by application code, reflecting internal behavior and performance (e.g., latency, error rates).

Business Metrics

Measurements reflecting the business impact and usage of the application (e.g., daily active users, conversion rates).

Service Level Indicator (SLI)

A quantifiable measure of service level, directly reflecting user experience.

Chapter Summary

Metrics provide the raw data, categorized by their source and purpose. SLIs are carefully selected metrics that directly quantify user-centric service reliability.
