Availability SLI

Measures the proportion of time a service is accessible and operational.

Calculation Example

(successful requests / total requests) * 100%

Or (uptime / total time) * 100%

A high availability SLI ensures users can consistently reach and interact with your service.

Why it matters

Directly impacts user trust and ability to use the service.

Latency SLI

Measures the time it takes for a service to respond to a request, typically in milliseconds.

Key Consideration: Percentiles

p99 latency (99% of requests complete within this time)

Averages can mask slow outliers; percentiles capture the experience of the majority.

A low latency SLI ensures a fast and responsive user experience.

Why it matters

Crucial for user satisfaction and engagement; slow services lead to abandonment.

Common Mistake

Using only average latency. This can hide a terrible experience for a small but significant percentage of users. Always consider percentiles!
