Understanding Business Metrics

These metrics track the success and usage of your application from a business perspective, reflecting its real-world impact.

Daily Active Users (DAU)

Number of unique users engaging with the service daily.

Conversion Rates

Percentage of users completing a desired action (e.g., purchase).

Revenue Generated

Total income from sales or subscriptions.

Successful Transactions

Count of completed business operations.

Why SREs Care About Business Metrics

While not directly technical, business metrics are crucial because:

  • •Direct Impact: System reliability directly affects business outcomes.
  • •Early Warning: A drop in conversion rate can signal subtle user experience issues not caught by technical metrics.
  • •Prioritization: Helps SREs prioritize work that delivers the most business value.
Key Takeaway

Business metrics connect technical performance to real-world value, guiding SREs to focus on what truly matters for the organization.

Misconception

Believing business metrics are solely for product teams. SREs need them to understand the full impact of their work.
