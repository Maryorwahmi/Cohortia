Configuring Prometheus to Scrape

Updating prometheus.yml for Target Discovery

The Central Configuration

prometheus.yml is where you define how Prometheus discovers and collects metrics from your monitored targets. It's the blueprint for your monitoring.

scrape_interval

Defines how frequently Prometheus pulls metrics.

job_name

Logical grouping for related monitoring targets.

YAML Configuration: `prometheus.yml`
# prometheus.yml
global:
  scrape_interval: 15s # How frequently Prometheus scrapes targets

scrape_configs:
  - job_name: 'node_exporter'
    static_configs:
      - targets: ['<YOUR_SERVER_IP>:9100'] # Replace with your server's IP
Common Mistake

Forgetting to restart or reload Prometheus after modifying prometheus.yml will prevent changes from taking effect.

Key Takeaway

Properly configuring prometheus.yml with scrape_configs and target definitions is crucial for Prometheus to discover and collect metrics from your monitored systems.
