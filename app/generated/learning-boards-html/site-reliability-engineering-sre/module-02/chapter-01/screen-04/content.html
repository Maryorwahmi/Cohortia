CORE CONCEPT

Monitoring & Observability: The SRE Toolkit

Ensuring Reliability in Complex Systems

Monitoring

Understanding the known unknowns. Collecting predefined data to answer specific questions.

Observability

Understanding the unknown unknowns. Inferring internal state from external data.

Why it matters

Essential for maintaining system health and performance in complex, distributed environments.

Common Misconception

Often used interchangeably, but they have distinct purposes and capabilities.

Key Takeaway

Both are critical for SREs, providing different lenses through which to view and understand system behavior.
