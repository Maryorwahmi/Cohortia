COMPARISON

Monitoring vs. Observability: Distinct but Complementary

Known Unknowns vs. Unknown Unknowns

Monitoring

  • • Focuses on known unknowns (predefined issues).
  • • Answers: What is broken?
  • • Relies on predefined alerts and dashboards.
  • • Example: CPU usage exceeds 90%, error rate spikes.
  • • Analogy: Car dashboard.

Observability

  • • Focuses on unknown unknowns (novel problems).
  • • Answers: Why did it break?
  • • Enables arbitrary querying of rich data.
  • • Example: Intermittent slowdowns with no alerts.
  • • Analogy: Mechanic's diagnostic computer.
Key Takeaway

Both are critical and complementary: monitoring for proactive alerts, observability for deep investigative debugging.
