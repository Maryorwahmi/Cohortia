Key Concepts: Reinforcing Your Understanding

Essential Terms for SRE Success

Metrics

Numerical measurements collected over time, aggregated for trends, alerts, and dashboards.

Logs

Immutable, timestamped records of discrete events, providing granular detail for debugging and auditing.

Traces

Records the end-to-end journey of a single request through a distributed system, showing operations across services.

Chapter Summary

Mastering telemetry types is crucial for effective monitoring, observability, and proactive reliability engineering.
