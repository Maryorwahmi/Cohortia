CORE CONCEPT

Telemetry Data: The Pillars of Insight

Metrics, Logs, and Traces Explained

Metrics

Numerical measurements collected over time, aggregated for trends and alerts.

Logs

Immutable, timestamped records of discrete events, providing granular detail for debugging.

Traces

End-to-end journey of a single request through a distributed system, showing operations across services.

Key Takeaway

Each telemetry type offers unique insights; combining them provides a holistic view of system health and performance.
