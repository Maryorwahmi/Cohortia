Golden Signal 3

Errors: Measuring Failures

The rate of requests that are failing

Definition

The proportion of requests that explicitly or implicitly fail, indicating service instability.

Example Measurement

http_requests_total{status_code=~"5xx"} (Prometheus) or log.level=error (log query).

Common Mistake

Only counting total errors. The rate is more informative. Also, not distinguishing between client (4xx) and server (5xx) errors.

Error Rate vs. Count
Scenario 1: High Traffic1000 req/sec, 10 errors
Error Rate: 1%
Scenario 2: Low Traffic100 req/sec, 10 errors
Error Rate: 10%
Key Takeaway

A small number of errors can be a critical problem if the traffic volume is also low, indicating a high error rate.
