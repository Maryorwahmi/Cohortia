Golden Signals: Interconnections & Scenarios

How signals influence each other and inform diagnosis

Latency + Saturation

High latency and high saturation often indicate a capacity issue or resource bottleneck.

Latency (High) + Traffic (Normal) + Saturation (Low)

Suggests a specific dependency issue or inefficient code path, not a general overload.

Traffic (Drop) + Errors (Spike)

Could indicate an upstream dependency failure or a cascading failure within the system.

Scenario-Based Diagnosis

Scenario: Slow Image Uploads

  • Latency: High (8s p99)
  • Traffic: 2x higher than usual
  • Errors: 5% (5xx)
  • Saturation: Disk I/O 95-100%
Initial Hypothesis

The system is experiencing high disk I/O saturation due to increased traffic, leading to increased latency and errors. The bottleneck is likely the disk subsystem.

Key Takeaway

Correlating signals helps pinpoint the root cause, moving beyond symptoms to actionable insights.
