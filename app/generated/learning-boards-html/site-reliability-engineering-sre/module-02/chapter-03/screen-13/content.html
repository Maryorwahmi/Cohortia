Key Concepts: Part 2

Continuing our essential takeaways

Errors

The rate of requests that are failing, focusing on server-side (e.g., HTTP 5xx) vs. client-side (4xx).

Saturation

A measure of how 'full' a service is, indicating proximity to capacity limits (e.g., CPU, memory, I/O).

Percentile Latency

Using p90, p99, etc., to measure latency, capturing the experience of the majority of users, including those at the slower end.

Summary Takeaway

By understanding Errors, Saturation, and the nuances of Percentile Latency, SREs can proactively manage system reliability and make informed decisions for scaling and optimization.
