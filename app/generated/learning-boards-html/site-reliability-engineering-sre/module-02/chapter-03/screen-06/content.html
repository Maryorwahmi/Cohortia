Traffic: Measuring Demand

How much demand is being placed on your system

Definition

The rate of requests or activity being processed by your service, indicating the load it's under.

Example Measurement

http_requests_total (Prometheus counter) for web services, or messages per second for queues.

Common Mistake

Not normalizing traffic by service or endpoint. A high total might be fine, but a spike on a specific endpoint could be an issue.

Traffic Patterns
TimeRequests/Sec
Normal Load
Sudden Spike!
Key Takeaway

Traffic metrics are vital for capacity planning and detecting unusual demand patterns, whether high or low.
