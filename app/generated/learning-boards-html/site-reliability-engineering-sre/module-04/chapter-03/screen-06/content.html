SRE's Role in CI/CD

SREs are crucial for both the reliability of the pipeline itself and the integration of reliability practices into the delivery process.

Pipeline Reliability

Ensure CI/CD infrastructure (runners, agents) is highly available, performant, and correctly configured to prevent delivery blockers.

Integrate Reliability Practices

Define and implement automated tests for SLOs, performance, chaos engineering, and security scans within the pipeline.

Deployment Gates

Establish automated checks (e.g., error rates) that a release must pass before progressing to the next stage, like production.

Key Takeaway

SREs are the guardians of the delivery factory, ensuring both its operational health and the quality of its output.
