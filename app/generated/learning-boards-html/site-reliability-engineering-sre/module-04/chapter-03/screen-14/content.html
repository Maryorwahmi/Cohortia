Key Concepts: SRE Practices & Deployment Types

Ensuring Reliability and Strategic Releases

Deployment Gate

An automated or manual check that must pass before a release can proceed to the next stage of the pipeline.

Rolling Deployment

Gradually replacing instances of an old application version with new ones to minimize downtime.

Blue/Green Deployment

Maintaining two identical production environments and switching traffic between them for new releases.

Chapter Summary

SREs integrate deployment gates for quality control and leverage strategies like rolling, blue/green, and canary deployments to manage risk and ensure high availability during releases. Each strategy offers distinct benefits and trade-offs for different operational needs.
