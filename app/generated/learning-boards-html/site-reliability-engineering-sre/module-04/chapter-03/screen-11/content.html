Module 4›Chapter 3›Rolling vs. Blue/Green Deployments

Rolling Deployments

Gradually replaces old instances with new ones, minimizing downtime but potentially leading to mixed versions.

Mechanism

One by one, old application instances are shut down and replaced by new ones.

SRE Benefit

Minimizes downtime, as the service remains available throughout the update process.

Consideration

Can result in mixed versions running concurrently, complicating debugging if issues arise.

Key Takeaway

Good for minimizing downtime, but requires careful management of version compatibility during rollout.

Blue/Green Deployments

Maintains two identical production environments, switching traffic from 'Blue' (old) to 'Green' (new) once validated.

Mechanism

New version deployed to 'Green' environment; traffic is switched from 'Blue' to 'Green' after testing.

SRE Benefit

Offers near-zero downtime and instant rollback by switching traffic back to the 'Blue' environment.

Consideration

Requires double the infrastructure, increasing costs, but provides high confidence and safety.

Key Takeaway

Ideal for critical systems needing high availability and rapid rollback, despite higher infrastructure costs.
