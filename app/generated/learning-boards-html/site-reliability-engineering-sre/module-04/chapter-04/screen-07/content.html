Runbooks and Playbooks: Your Incident Guides

Structured documentation for effective incident management

Runbook

  • •Detailed, step-by-step guide.
  • •For routine operational tasks.
  • •Responds to common, well-understood incidents.
  • •Includes exact commands, checks, and expected outputs.
  • •Highly prescriptive and linear.
Example

"How to restart Service X" or "Clear the cache for Application Y".

Playbook

  • •Higher-level, adaptive guide.
  • •For complex or novel incidents.
  • •Provides strategies and decision trees.
  • •Links to relevant runbooks or automated tools.
  • •More flexible and strategic.
Example

"Major Outage Response Strategy" or "Database Corruption Recovery Plan".

Key Takeaway

Runbooks offer precise instructions for known issues, while playbooks provide strategic guidance for the unknown, together forming a robust incident response framework.
