Key Concepts

What to Remember

Automated Diagnostics

Scripts or tools that automatically collect relevant system information upon an alert to aid in incident triage.

Automated Remediation

Pre-defined, automated actions triggered by specific alert conditions to resolve common, well-understood issues.

Runbook

A detailed, step-by-step guide for performing routine operational tasks or responding to common incidents.

Summary Takeaway

Automated diagnostics, remediation, and structured runbooks are essential tools that empower SREs to respond to incidents with speed, consistency, and reduced manual effort.
