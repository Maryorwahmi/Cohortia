Key Concepts

Essential ideas from this chapter

Toil

Manual, repetitive, automatable operational work that scales linearly with service size and lacks enduring value.

Automation

Using technology to perform tasks with minimal human intervention, aiming for consistency, speed, and reduced errors.

Configuration Management

Maintaining consistent settings across all servers and services in an infrastructure, often using tools like Ansible or Chef.

Chapter Summary

Automation is fundamental to SRE, enabling the reduction of 'toil' through consistent, rapid, and error-free operations like configuration management, freeing SREs for strategic engineering.
