Rapid Recovery

Accelerating Incident Response and Remediation

Automated Actions for Faster MTTR

Initial Diagnostics

Automated scripts can quickly gather logs, metrics, and system status to provide immediate context.

Automated Remediation

Scripts can perform actions like restarting services, scaling resources, or triggering failovers to mitigate issues.

Reduced MTTR

By automating initial steps, the time to detect and resolve incidents is drastically cut, minimizing impact.

Automated Incident Response Flow
1. Alert Triggered
2. Automated Diagnostics
3. Automated Remediation (if applicable)
4. Human SRE Review & Complex Actions
5. Incident Resolution
Key Takeaway

Automated runbooks and scripts can perform initial incident diagnostics and remediation, drastically reducing MTTR and allowing SREs to focus on higher-level problem-solving.
