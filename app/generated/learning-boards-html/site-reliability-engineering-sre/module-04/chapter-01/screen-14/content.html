Automation Toolkit

Risks and Best Practices in SRE Automation

Automate Wisely, Test Thoroughly, and Maintain Oversight

Automating Broken Processes

Automating a flawed manual process will only make it fail faster and at a larger scale. Refine manually first.

Over-Automation & Complexity

Creating overly complex automation can be difficult to maintain and debug. Start simple and iterate.

Best Practices for Safe Automation

Test in Non-Prod

Always validate automation in a safe environment first.

Error Handling & Logging

Implement robust mechanisms for debugging and monitoring automation.

Least Privilege

Automated actions should only have necessary permissions.

Human Oversight

Automation augments, not replaces, human SREs.

Key Takeaway

Approach SRE automation cautiously by refining processes first, keeping solutions simple, testing rigorously, and ensuring human oversight remains for complex scenarios.
