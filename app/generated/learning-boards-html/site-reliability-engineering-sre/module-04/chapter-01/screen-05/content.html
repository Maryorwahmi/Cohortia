Toil vs. Engineering Work: The SRE Distinction

Eliminating Repetitive Tasks to Focus on Value

What is Toil?

Manual, repetitive, automatable, tactical operational work that scales linearly with service size and lacks enduring value.

What is Engineering Work?

Designing and implementing solutions that improve system reliability, reduce future toil, and provide lasting value.

Toil vs. Engineering: Examples

Toil Example:

Manually restarting a failed service instance after an alert. This is reactive and doesn't prevent future failures.

Engineering Work Example:

Designing and implementing a self-healing system that automatically detects and restarts failed services. This is proactive and reduces future toil.

Key Takeaway

SREs aim to spend no more than 50% of their time on toil, dedicating the rest to engineering projects that improve system reliability and reduce future toil through automation.
