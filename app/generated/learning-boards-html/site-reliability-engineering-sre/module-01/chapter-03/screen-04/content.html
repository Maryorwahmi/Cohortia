What is Toil in SRE?

Understanding the Characteristics of Undesirable Work

Defining Toil

In Site Reliability Engineering, toil refers to the manual, repetitive, automatable, tactical work that scales linearly with service growth and lacks lasting value.

Manual & Repetitive

Automatable & Tactical

Scales Linearly

Lacks Lasting Value

Common Examples of Toil

  • Manually restarting failed services or servers.
  • Responding to common alerts with manual steps.
  • Manually deploying code or configuration changes.
  • Creating ad-hoc reports by manually querying logs.
Why it Matters

Identifying toil is the first step towards reclaiming engineering time and improving system stability. It allows teams to focus on innovation rather than maintenance.
