Beyond Incidents: Automation and Proactive Planning

Building Resilient Systems and Learning from Failures

Strategic SRE Focus Areas

  • Automation: Eliminating 'toil' (manual, repetitive work) through code.
  • Capacity Planning: Ensuring systems can handle future growth and demand.
  • Disaster Recovery: Designing and testing plans for system resilience.
  • Performance Tuning: Optimizing systems for speed and efficiency.
  • SLO Compliance: Meeting defined Service Level Objectives.

Blameless Post-Mortems

  • Learning Culture: Incidents are opportunities for systemic improvement.
  • Focus: Understanding system failures, not assigning individual blame.
  • Outcome: Stronger, more resilient infrastructure over time.
Key Takeaway

SREs proactively enhance system resilience through automation, strategic planning, and a culture of continuous learning from incidents.
