SRE in Practice: A Case Study

Transforming Reactive Fixes into Proactive Solutions

Scenario: E-commerce Checkout Failures

  • Intermittent failures during checkout process.
  • Impact: Lost sales, frustrated customers.

Traditional Ops Response

  • Manually restart servers.
  • Immediate goal: Restore service, often without deep root cause analysis.
  • Approach: Reactive, temporary fixes.
Key Takeaway

SRE transforms reactive incident response into a data-driven, engineering-focused process aimed at long-term prevention and automation.
