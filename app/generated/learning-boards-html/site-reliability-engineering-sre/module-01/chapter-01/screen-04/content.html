The Genesis of SRE

From Reactive Firefighting to Proactive Engineering at Google

Google's Operational Challenges

  • Rapid growth of services led to unsustainable manual operations.
  • Traditional ops teams were constantly reacting to incidents, not preventing them.
  • Manual interventions became a bottleneck for scalability and reliability.

The SRE Vision

  • Ben Treynor Sloss advocated applying software engineering to operations.
  • Shift from 'fixing' to 'building systems that don't break'.
  • Proactive, engineering-driven approach to system health.
Key Takeaway

SRE was born out of necessity at Google to manage complex systems at scale by transforming operations into an engineering discipline.
