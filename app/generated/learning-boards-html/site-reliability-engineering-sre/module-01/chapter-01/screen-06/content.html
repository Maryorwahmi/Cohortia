The SRE Role: A Blend of Dev and Ops

Engineering Solutions for Operational Excellence

Who is an SRE?

  • Software Engineers: Deep coding skills are fundamental.
  • Systems Experts: Proficient in networking, distributed computing, and infrastructure.
  • Problem Solvers: Focused on engineering solutions to operational challenges.

Typical Time Allocation

  • 50%+ Coding: Automation, tooling, monitoring, architecture design.
  • Remaining Time: Incident response, on-call duties, capacity planning.
  • Goal: Minimize 'toil' (manual, repetitive work) through automation.
Key Takeaway

SREs are software engineers who apply their development skills to operational problems, dedicating significant time to automation and proactive system improvement.
