What is Site Reliability Engineering?

Explore how SRE transforms traditional operations into a proactive, engineering-driven discipline focused on reliability, scalability, and efficiency.

Key Idea

Site Reliability Engineering applies software engineering principles to operational challenges, aiming to build inherently reliable and scalable systems.

In this lesson, you will understand the origins of SRE, its core philosophy, and how it differs from and complements DevOps. We'll also cover the key responsibilities of an SRE and common misconceptions about the role.
