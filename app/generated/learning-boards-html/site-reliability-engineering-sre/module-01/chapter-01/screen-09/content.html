Ensuring System Health: Monitoring and Incident Response

Proactive Visibility and Efficient Problem Resolution

Monitoring & Alerting

  • Deep Visibility: Beyond basic uptime checks to performance and user experience.
  • Metric Definition: Identifying key SLIs (e.g., latency, error rates) and setting thresholds.
  • Proactive Detection: Alerts trigger before user impact or widespread failure.

Incident Management

  • Detection & Triage: Rapid identification and assessment of issues.
  • Root Cause Analysis: Understanding 'why' an incident occurred, not just 'what' happened.
  • Preventative Measures: Implementing changes to avoid recurrence.
Key Takeaway

SREs are responsible for building robust monitoring systems and leading incident response, focusing on understanding and preventing future issues.
