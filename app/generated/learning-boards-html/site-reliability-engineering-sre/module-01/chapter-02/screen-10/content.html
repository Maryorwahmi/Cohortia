Balancing Velocity and Stability with Error Budgets

How error budgets guide engineering priorities

Healthy Error Budget

When the error budget is largely unused, teams have the freedom to take more risks and prioritize new feature development.

  • Faster Feature Deployment: More frequent releases.
  • Experimentation: Freedom to try new approaches.
  • Technical Debt Repayment: Can allocate time for refactoring.

Depleted Error Budget

If the error budget is nearly or fully consumed, the team must shift focus to reliability work.

  • Pause New Features: Halt deployments of non-critical features.
  • Prioritize Reliability: Dedicate resources to bug fixes, testing, and infrastructure improvements.
  • Root Cause Analysis: Investigate and resolve underlying issues causing unreliability.
Key Takeaway

Error budgets provide an objective mechanism to resolve the tension between development velocity and operational stability, guiding teams to prioritize work based on real-time reliability data.
