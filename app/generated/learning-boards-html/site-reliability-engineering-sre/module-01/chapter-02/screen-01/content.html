The SRE Pillars: SLIs, SLOs, and Error Budgets

Quantifying Reliability for Better User Experience

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts of Service Level Indicators (SLIs), Service Level Objectives (SLOs), and Error Budgets, explaining their definitions, relationships, and how they form the quantitative framework for managing service reliability in Site Reliability Engineering.

Key Takeaway

SRE uses data-driven metrics to define, measure, and manage service reliability, ensuring a consistent user experience.
