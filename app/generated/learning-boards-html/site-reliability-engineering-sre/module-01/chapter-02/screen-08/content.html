Definition

Setting Targets: Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

Transforming metrics into measurable goals

An SLO is a target value or range for an SLI that defines the desired level of service. It converts abstract notions of 'good service' into concrete, measurable targets for engineering teams.

Key Takeaway

SLOs are target values for SLIs, transforming abstract service quality into concrete, measurable goals that align teams and manage expectations realistically.

Common Pitfall

Aiming for 100% availability is unrealistic and prohibitively expensive; focus on user needs.

SLO Examples & Considerations

Latency SLO

99% of requests complete within 300ms.

Availability SLO

99.9% availability over a 30-day period.

Realistic Targets

Balance user needs with engineering cost and effort.

Clarity & Alignment

Provide clear goals that all teams can work towards.
