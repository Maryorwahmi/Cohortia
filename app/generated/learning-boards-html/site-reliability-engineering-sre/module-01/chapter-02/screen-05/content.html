Definition

What are Service Level Indicators (SLIs)?

Quantitative measures of service performance

An SLI is a carefully chosen quantitative measure of some aspect of the level of service that is provided. These are the raw data points you track to understand how your service is performing from a user's perspective.

Key Takeaway

SLIs are direct, measurable, quantitative metrics that reflect user experience, providing raw data to understand service health.

Misconception

Internal metrics like CPU utilization are not direct SLIs; focus on user-facing impact.

Characteristics of Good SLIs

Directly Reflect User Experience

Measures what users actually care about, like page load time.

Quantifiable and Measurable

Can be expressed as a number or percentage, e.g., milliseconds, requests/second.

Actionable

A change in the SLI should prompt a clear response or investigation.

Understandable

Easy for all stakeholders, technical or not, to comprehend.
