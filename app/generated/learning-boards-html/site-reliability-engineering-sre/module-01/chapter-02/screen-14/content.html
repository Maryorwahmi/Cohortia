Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 2

Understanding the actionable aspects of reliability

Error Budget

The allowed amount of unreliability for a service over a given period, calculated as (100% - SLO).

Availability

The percentage of time a service is operational and accessible, a key measure of uptime.

Latency

The time delay between a request and its response, directly impacting user experience speed.

Summary Takeaway

The Error Budget provides an actionable framework for balancing reliability and innovation, while Availability and Latency are critical SLIs for measuring and managing service health.
