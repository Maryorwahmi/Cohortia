Core Concept

The Foundation of SRE Reliability

A data-driven approach to service management

Site Reliability Engineering relies on a quantitative framework to manage service reliability, moving beyond subjective assessments to objective, measurable goals. This framework ensures clarity and alignment across teams.

Key Takeaway

SLIs, SLOs, and Error Budgets form a data-driven framework to measure, understand, and improve service reliability from a user's perspective.

Common Misconception

Reliability is not just about 'fixing things when they break'; it's about proactive, measurable management.

Service Level Indicators (SLIs)

Raw data points measuring service performance.

Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

Target values for SLIs, defining desired service level.

Error Budgets

Allowed unreliability, derived from SLOs, driving decisions.
