Core Concept

The Error Budget: Embracing Unreliability

The allowed amount of 'badness' derived from your SLO

The error budget is the allowed amount of unreliability for a service over a given period, calculated directly from your Service Level Objective (SLO). It provides a quantitative way to balance reliability with innovation.

Key Takeaway

The Error Budget is the allowed amount of unreliability for a service, derived directly from its SLO, providing a quantitative balance between reliability and innovation.

Pro Tip

Error budgets acknowledge that 100% reliability is often impossible and too costly to achieve.

Calculating Your Error Budget

Start with your SLO

Example: 99.9% Availability

Calculate Allowed Unreliability

100% - SLO = Error Budget Percentage

Example: 100% - 99.9% = 0.1% allowed downtime.

Translate to Time/Events

Example: 0.1% of 30 days = ~43 minutes of downtime.
