Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Part 1

Recap of foundational SRE terms

Service Level Indicator (SLI)

A quantitative measure of some aspect of the level of service that is provided (e.g., latency, error rate).

Service Level Objective (SLO)

A target value or range for an SLI, defining the desired level of service (e.g., 99.9% availability).

Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A formal contract between a service provider and a customer specifying the expected level of service.

Summary Takeaway

SLIs measure, SLOs target, and SLAs contractually define the expected level of service, forming the core language of reliability in SRE.
