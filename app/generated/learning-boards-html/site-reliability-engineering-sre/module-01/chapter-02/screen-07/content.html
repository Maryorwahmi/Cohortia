Measuring Reliability: Error Rate and Availability

Quantifying service failures and uptime

Error Rate

The proportion of requests that result in an error. A high error rate directly impacts user satisfaction.

  • HTTP 5xx Errors: Server-side errors for web services.
  • Failed Database Transactions: Operations that don't complete successfully.
  • Application-Specific Errors: Any failure within the application logic.
Example

Less than 0.1% of requests result in a 5xx error.

Availability

The proportion of time a service is accessible and working correctly. Often expressed as an uptime percentage.

  • Uptime Percentage: Total operational time divided by total time.
  • Service Reachability: Can users connect to the service?
  • Functional Correctness: Is the service not just up, but also working as expected?
Example

99.99% availability over a 30-day period.

Key Takeaway

Error Rate and Availability are critical SLIs for measuring service reliability, directly impacting user trust. Always prioritize user-facing metrics over internal system health indicators.
