SRE Framework

Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

Setting internal targets for desired service quality based on SLIs

Definition

An SLO is a target value or range for an SLI, defining the desired level of service that guides engineering efforts.

Internal Commitments

SLOs are internal targets that help SRE teams understand if they are meeting user expectations and guide their work.

Key Takeaway

SLOs transform raw SLI data into actionable goals, providing clear targets for maintaining and improving service reliability.

SLI to SLO: Setting a Target
SLI Example

P99 latency for /api/v1/data requests.

Corresponding SLO

P99 latency for /api/v1/data requests must be less than 200ms over a 30-day rolling window.

Why it Matters

This specific SLO provides a clear, measurable goal for the engineering team to ensure the API is consistently responsive for 99% of users.
