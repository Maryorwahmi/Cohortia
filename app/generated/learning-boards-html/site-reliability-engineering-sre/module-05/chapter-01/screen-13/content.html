Best Practices

Crafting SMART Performance SLOs

Making objectives Measurable, Actionable, and User-Centric

SMART Criteria

SLOs should be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound to be effective and actionable.

Common Pitfalls

Avoid overly ambitious or lax SLOs, and always ensure you are measuring the right thing from the user's perspective.

Key Takeaway

Well-defined, SMART SLOs are essential for driving meaningful improvements and ensuring your service consistently meets user expectations.

Example: SMART SLO for an API
Vague Goal

"The API should be fast."

SMART SLO

"99% of user-facing API requests must complete within 300ms, measured from the load balancer, over a 7-day period."

Why it's SMART

It's specific (99%, 300ms, load balancer), measurable, achievable (with effort), relevant (user-facing), and time-bound (7-day period).
