Resource Monitoring

Saturation: The Bottleneck Indicator

When resources have more work queued up than they can immediately process

Definition

Saturation measures the amount of work a resource has queued up that it cannot immediately process, indicating a bottleneck.

Key Distinction

Unlike utilization, which is simply how busy a resource is, saturation indicates that the resource is overwhelmed and cannot keep up, leading to delays.

Key Takeaway

Saturation is a critical indicator of performance bottlenecks, directly impacting latency and user experience due to queued work.

Utilization vs. Saturation
High Utilization, Low Saturation

CPU is 100% busy but has a low run queue. It's working hard and keeping up.

High Utilization, High Saturation

CPU is 100% busy with a high run queue. It's overwhelmed, and requests are delayed.

Example Scenario

A network interface at 80% utilization with a full transmit queue is saturated, meaning packets are being dropped despite not being 100% utilized.
