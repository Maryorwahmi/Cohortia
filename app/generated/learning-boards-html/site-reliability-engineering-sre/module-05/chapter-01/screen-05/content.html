Performance Metric

Latency: The Time Delay

Measuring the responsiveness of your system from request to response

Definition

Latency is the time it takes for a request to travel from its origin to its destination and for a response to return. It's a measure of delay.

Why Percentiles Matter

Percentiles (p50, p90, p99) provide a nuanced view of user experience, showing typical vs. slowest request times. A high p99 latency indicates a poor experience for a segment of users.

Key Takeaway

Latency, especially at higher percentiles, is a direct indicator of user experience; managing it ensures responsiveness for all users.

Latency Percentiles Explained
P50 Latency (Median)

50% of requests complete within this time. Represents the typical user experience.

P90 Latency

90% of requests complete within this time. Captures the experience of most users, excluding some outliers.

P99 Latency (Tail Latency)

99% of requests complete within this time. Crucial for understanding the experience of the slowest 1% of users, which can significantly impact satisfaction.

Example

If p99 latency is 500ms, 99% of requests finish in under 500ms, but 1% take longer, potentially causing noticeable delays for those users.
