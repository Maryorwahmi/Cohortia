Combining Tracing and Profiling

A Comprehensive Approach to Performance Troubleshooting

The Two-Stage Diagnostic Process

  • •Stage 1 (Tracing): Identify *which* service or operation is slow or failing across the distributed system.
  • •Stage 2 (Profiling): Pinpoint *why* that specific service is slow by analyzing its internal resource consumption (CPU, memory, I/O).
Real-World Scenario

A trace shows your 'RecommendationService' is slow. Profiling reveals an O(N^2) algorithm in its data processing function. You now know exactly what to optimize.

Key Takeaway

Tracing and profiling are complementary; use tracing for system-wide visibility and profiling for deep, service-level code optimization.
