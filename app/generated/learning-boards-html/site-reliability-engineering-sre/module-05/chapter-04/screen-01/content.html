Mastering Distributed Tracing and Profiling

Unraveling Performance Bottlenecks in Complex Systems

In modern microservices, diagnosing performance issues is complex. This chapter introduces distributed tracing for end-to-end request visibility and profiling for pinpointing service-level bottlenecks, empowering SREs to troubleshoot effectively.

Key Takeaway

Distributed tracing and profiling are critical for understanding and optimizing the performance of microservices architectures.
