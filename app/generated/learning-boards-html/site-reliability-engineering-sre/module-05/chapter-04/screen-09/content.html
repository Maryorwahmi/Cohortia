Interpreting Trace Data

Spotting Bottlenecks with Waterfall Diagrams

  • •Waterfall Diagrams: Visual representation of spans over time, showing sequence and duration.
  • •Identify Long Spans: Quickly spot operations taking an unusually long time, indicating bottlenecks.
  • •Error Propagation: Observe how errors in one service affect downstream services.
  • •Unexpected Calls: Discover unnecessary or duplicate service calls.
Example

A trace shows 'RecommendationService' taking 500ms. Within it, a 'Database Query' span is 450ms. This immediately points to the database as the bottleneck.

Key Takeaway

Trace UIs provide a powerful visual narrative, making it intuitive to diagnose performance issues and understand system behavior.
