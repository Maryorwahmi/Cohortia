Factors Influencing Growth Projections

Business Initiatives

New product launches, feature rollouts, or market expansions can significantly increase load.

Marketing Campaigns

Large-scale advertising, promotions, or viral content can drive sudden, massive traffic spikes.

Seasonal Events

Predictable surges during holidays (e.g., Black Friday, Christmas) or specific industry events.

Organic Growth

The baseline growth identified through historical data analysis, which continues steadily.

Key Takeaway

Accurate capacity planning requires combining historical trends with forward-looking business and seasonal projections to anticipate peak demands.

Scenario: Holiday Sale Impact

A retail platform typically handles 5,000 requests per second (RPS). During a major holiday sale, marketing projects a 5x increase in traffic, reaching 25,000 RPS. This surge is far beyond organic growth and requires specific planning.

Planning for Peaks
  • •Identify peak event dates and durations.
  • •Estimate peak traffic multiplier.
  • •Provision additional resources well in advance.
  • •Conduct pre-event load testing at projected peak levels.
Common Pitfall

Failing to account for peak events or underestimating their impact can lead to severe service degradation or outages.
