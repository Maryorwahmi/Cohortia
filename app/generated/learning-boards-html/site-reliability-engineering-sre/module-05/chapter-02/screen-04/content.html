Core Concept

What is Capacity Planning?

Capacity planning is the process of determining the resources (CPU, memory, storage, network bandwidth, database connections, etc.) needed to meet future demand while maintaining acceptable performance levels.

Why it Matters

Prevents service degradation, outages, and poor user experience during traffic spikes.

Key Benefit

Allows SRE teams to scale infrastructure gracefully and optimize costs.

The Capacity Planning Cycle

1. Collect Data

Monitor current usage.

2. Analyze & Predict

Forecast future needs.

3. Provision & Test

Allocate and validate resources.

Key Takeaway

Proactive capacity planning is crucial for system stability, cost optimization, and a superior user experience, preventing reactive firefighting.
