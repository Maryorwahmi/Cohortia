Historical Data Analysis Process
01

Identify Key Metrics

Focus on traffic, resource utilization (CPU, memory), and business growth (users, transactions).

02

Collect Long-Term Data

Use monitoring systems like Prometheus and Grafana for historical records.

03

Analyze Patterns & Trends

Look for daily, weekly, monthly, and seasonal patterns, as well as year-over-year growth.

04

Establish Baselines

Define normal operating ranges and identify deviations for future projections.

Key Takeaway

Historical data provides the essential foundation for capacity planning, revealing past usage patterns and growth trends to inform future predictions.

Example: E-commerce Traffic Growth

An e-commerce site observes a consistent 10% month-over-month increase in traffic over the last year. This trend can be extrapolated to project future organic growth.

Traffic Trend

Month 1: 10,000 RPS

Month 2: 11,000 RPS (+10%)

Month 3: 12,100 RPS (+10%)

...projected...

Month 12: ~28,500 RPS

Common Pitfall

Relying solely on historical data without considering external factors can lead to underestimation.
