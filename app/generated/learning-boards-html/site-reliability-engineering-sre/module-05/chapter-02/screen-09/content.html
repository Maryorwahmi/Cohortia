Core Concept

What is Load Balancing?

Load balancing is the process of distributing incoming network traffic across multiple servers to optimize resource utilization, maximize throughput, minimize response time, and avoid overload of any single resource.

Role of a Load Balancer

Acts as a 'traffic cop' directing client requests to healthy backend servers.

Key Benefits

Improves performance, enhances reliability, enables graceful degradation and failover.
