Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Core Definitions

Essential terms in Chaos Engineering

Chaos Engineering

The discipline of experimenting on a system in production to build confidence in its capability to withstand turbulent conditions.

Steady State Hypothesis

A measurable output that indicates the normal functioning of a system, against which the impact of chaos experiments is measured.

Blast Radius

The potential impact or scope of a chaos experiment, which should be minimized to prevent widespread disruption.

Summary Takeaway

Chaos Engineering is a proactive, scientific approach to building resilient systems by understanding and minimizing the impact of failures through controlled experimentation against a defined steady state.
