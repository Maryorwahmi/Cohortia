Module 5.5›Chapter 5.5›What is Chaos Engineering?

Definition

Chaos Engineering is the discipline of experimenting on a system in production in order to build confidence in the system's capability to withstand turbulent conditions.

Why It Matters

  • Proactively finds weaknesses before they cause real outages.
  • Builds confidence in system resilience under turbulent conditions.
  • Reduces the impact of unexpected failures by preparing for them.
  • Validates that systems meet performance SLOs even when degraded.
