Service & Database Failures

Testing resilience to critical component outages

Service Failure/Crash

Randomly terminate instances or hosts to validate load balancer health checks and failover mechanisms.

Database Latency/Failure

Inject delays or simulate unavailability to test application resilience to database issues and cache effectiveness.

Key Takeaway

Simulating direct component failures is essential for validating the robustness of your system's recovery, failover, and caching strategies under critical conditions.
