Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › CSPM
What CSPM does
  • Discover cloud resources and configurations across accounts and regions.
  • Detect misconfigurations and compliance violations using rules and benchmarks.
  • Prioritize findings and provide remediation recommendations.
  • Integrate with SIEM, ticketing, and automation to close the loop.
Example alert
CSPM finds a bucket without default encryption and triggers a workflow: notify owners, create a ticket, and optionally apply an automated remediation to enable encryption.
Integration
Feed findings into SIEM for correlation with detection data and into incident response playbooks for prioritized action.
Why it matters
CSPM reduces manual discovery burden and helps prevent simple configuration errors from persisting in production.
