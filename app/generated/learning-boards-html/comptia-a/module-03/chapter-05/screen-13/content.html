Module 3 › Chapter 3.5 › Operational Pitfalls
Avoid These Mistakes
Anticipate operational impact and plan pilots, rollbacks, and approvals.
Configuration drift
Use automation and regular scans to detect and repair divergence from baselines.
Insufficient testing
Start in Audit mode and pilot cohorts to avoid breaking applications in production.
Missing services
Remember supporting services like Application Identity for AppLocker and correct TPM provisioning for BitLocker.
Approval bottlenecks
Define fast approval workflows for legitimate exceptions so security does not become a blocker.
Key Takeaway

Operational readiness — pilots, automation, and documented rollback — makes strong baselines and whitelists sustainable without disrupting users.
