Deployment steps
  1. Inventory workloads and required flows.
  2. Define least-privilege policies per tier.
  3. Roll out policies in monitoring mode, then enforce.
  4. Maintain rollback and change-audit processes.
Controller hardening
Protect the SDN controller with multi-factor access, limited admin roles, logging, and secure channels for policy distribution. A compromised controller can alter policies network-wide, so treat it as a high-value asset.
