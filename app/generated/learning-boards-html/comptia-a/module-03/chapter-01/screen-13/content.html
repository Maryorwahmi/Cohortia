Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Operational Checklist
Checklist
  • Inventory assets and classify by trust level.
  • Document VLAN, PVLAN and VRF plans and mappings.
  • Apply ACLs and firewall rules at routing points.
  • Harden switches and controllers, disable DTP on user ports.
  • Test and monitor continuously; keep rollbacks ready.
Avoid these mistakes
Do not assume VLANs are enough, do not leave native VLANs for users, and do not permit automatic trunk negotiation. Verify PVLAN mappings and VRF route-targets carefully.
