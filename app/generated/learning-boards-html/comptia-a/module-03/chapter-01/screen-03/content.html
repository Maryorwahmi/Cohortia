Verify and Demonstrate
Concrete lab milestones for each objective.
Bloom: Apply → Master
Design verification
Create a VLAN/PVLAN plan and map switch ports to isolated, community, and promiscuous roles. Confirm isolation with pings or simulated traffic logs.
Configuration verification
Implement PVLAN commands in a simulator, validate that servers reach only the firewall and not each other, and document the exact switchport commands used.
Controller and NFV checks
Show policy distribution, list controller access rules, and describe how you would detect tampering or misdistribution of policies.
Routing and ACL tests
Apply ACLs to inter-VLAN routes and verify allowed flows and blocked flows. Demonstrate trunk hardening and native VLAN handling.
