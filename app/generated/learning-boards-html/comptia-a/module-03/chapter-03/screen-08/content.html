Protecting time services
Hardening steps
  • Enable NTP authentication to prevent unauthorized time sources.
  • Use multiple trusted NTP servers for redundancy and diversity.
  • Prefer internal NTP servers that obtain time from reliable stratum sources.
Example configuration
Commands such as ntp authenticate and ntp trusted-key are used to require authenticated NTP peers on routers. Verify peers are configured and authenticated.
Misconception
Believing NTP is non-critical. Incorrect time can invalidate logs and disrupt certificate-based authentication.
Check your setup
Compare timestamps from multiple devices and confirm NTP authentication status to ensure integrity.
