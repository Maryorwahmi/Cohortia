Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Device Hardening Fundamentals
Fundamental hardening steps
  • ● Change default credentials and enable MFA for admin access when available.
  • ● Disable Telnet and HTTP; prefer SSH and HTTPS with properly configured certificates.
  • ● Shutdown unused switch interfaces and remove them from active VLANs.
  • ● Document each disabled service and maintain an inventory for audits.
Example
On a Cisco switch run shutdown on unused interfaces and confirm they are removed from active VLANs to stop unauthorized patch-panel access.
Common mistake
Assuming a firewall alone protects management protocols. Local access or misapplied ACLs can still expose services.
Checklist for verification
  1. Inventory default accounts and confirm they've been removed or renamed.
  2. Scan for open management ports and verify transport uses encryption.
  3. Confirm unused interfaces are administratively down and documented.
  4. Verify change control records for service disabling to avoid accidental outages.
Key Takeaway

Start hardening with small, verifiable changes: remove defaults, close unused ports, and stop insecure services to immediately lower risk.
