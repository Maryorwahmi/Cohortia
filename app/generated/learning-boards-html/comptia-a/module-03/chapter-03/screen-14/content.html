Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Control Plane Policing (CoPP)
Rate-limit traffic to the CPU to maintain routing and management service continuity during floods.
Management Plane Protection (MPP)
Restrict administrative access to specific interfaces or addresses so management remains secure and auditable.
Secure Protocols
Use SSHv2 for admin access and SNMPv3 for monitoring; disable Telnet and SNMPv2c/v1 to avoid plaintext credentials.
Takeaway

Hardening is layered: secure management, secure telemetry, secure time and DNS, and plane protections together preserve device reliability and forensic integrity.
