Using NetFlow and IPFIX for detection
Why flows matter
  • Flows summarize connections, enabling fast detection of unusual volume or destinations.
  • They are efficient to store and useful for long-term trend analysis and incident timelines.
  • They complement logs and packet capture rather than replace them.
Security considerations
  • Protect flow export transport when crossing untrusted networks, for example using encrypted tunnels.
  • Harden and limit access to the flow collector and SIEM to prevent tampering.
  • Validate that flows are parsed and retained long enough for investigation.
Example
Export NetFlow to a dedicated collector and confirm the SIEM shows interface-level flows and unusual destination counts.
Check
Verify the collector accepts only authorized exporters and that exported data is encrypted or carried over trusted links.
