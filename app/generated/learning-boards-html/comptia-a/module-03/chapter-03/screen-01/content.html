Hardening Network Devices and Services
Practical steps to protect administrative access, monitoring, time, and name services so the network stays reliable and auditable.
Secure Management
SSHv2 and SNMPv3 replace insecure services to protect credentials and integrity.
Monitoring
NetFlow/IPFIX provide flow context for detection and response when securely transported to a collector.
Time & DNS
NTP authentication and DNSSEC protect timestamps and name resolution integrity.
Why this chapter

Compromised devices can expose networks and erase evidence. Hardening reduces the paths an attacker can use and helps preserve logs and availability for incident response.

What you will practice

You will configure SSH keys, enable SNMPv3, prepare NetFlow exports, secure NTP, sign DNS zones, and apply CoPP and MPP policies on routers and switches.

SSH & SNMPv3: Replace plaintext management with authenticated, encrypted access to prevent credential theft.
NTP & DNSSEC: Keep clocks correct and DNS responses verifiable so logs and name resolution remain trustworthy.
CoPP & MPP: Limit and restrict traffic to the device CPU and management interfaces to maintain availability under attack.
