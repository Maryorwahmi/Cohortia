Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Learning Objectives — Outcomes
Practical Outcomes
SSHv2 Enforcement
Enforce SSHv2, generate RSA keys, and bind to VTY lines so remote admins use encrypted access only. Lab check: verify transport input is ssh.
SNMPv3 Hardening
Create SNMPv3 users with auth and priv, restrict views to required OIDs, and verify collectors authenticate successfully.
NetFlow/IPFIX Export
Send flow data to a secured collector, or tunnel exports, and confirm flow logs appear in your SIEM or collector.
NTP Redundancy
Configure authenticated NTP and multiple trusted sources so time remains correct for logs and certificates.
DNSSEC Readiness
Prepare zone signing and DS records; ensure resolvers validate signatures to prevent cache poisoning.
CoPP & MPP
Apply rate limits for CPU-bound traffic and restrict management plane access to authorized interfaces or addresses.
