DNSSEC essentials
What DNSSEC does
It signs DNS records cryptographically so a resolver can verify the response came from the zone owner and was not altered in transit.
Deployment notes
  • Sign zones and publish DS records in the parent zone to establish delegation of trust.
  • Test on a non-critical zone first and validate resolver behavior before wide rollout.
  • Keep DNS servers patched and restrict recursion; DNSSEC does not replace server hardening.
Example
Sign a test zone and confirm a validating resolver rejects manipulated records but accepts properly signed responses.
Common mistake
Assuming DNSSEC alone prevents all DNS abuse. You still need patched, well-configured authoritative and recursive servers.
