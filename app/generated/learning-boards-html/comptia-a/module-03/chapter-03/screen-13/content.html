Staged deployment checklist
Prepare
  1. Inventory devices and management paths.
  2. Identify high-value zones and critical devices for phased rollout.
Pilot
  1. Apply SSH, SNMPv3, and NTP auth to a pilot group.
  2. Test access, monitoring, and failover behavior.
Rollout
  1. Apply CoPP and MPP conservatively, then tune policies.
  2. Document changes, update runbooks, and schedule reviews.
Key Takeaway

A staged, documented approach with verification and rollback plans ensures hardening improves security without risking management or routing availability.
