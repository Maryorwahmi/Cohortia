Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Management Plane Protection
MPP design pattern
Core rules
  • Allow management only from specific interfaces or a management VLAN.
  • Restrict management access to trusted IP addresses with ACLs.
  • Combine with authentication, encryption, and logging for complete protection.
Operational checklist
  1. Identify all authorized admin hosts and interfaces.
  2. Apply MPP settings and test administrative access from allowed hosts.
  3. Document procedures for emergency access to avoid lockout.
Example
Configure the device to accept SSH and SNMP only on a management interface and block those protocols elsewhere to limit exposure.
Common mistake
Failing to document emergency or out-of-band access can lead to accidental lockout during maintenance — always plan recovery paths.
