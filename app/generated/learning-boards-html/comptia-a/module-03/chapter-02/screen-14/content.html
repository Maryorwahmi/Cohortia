What to Remember Practical Concepts
MDM
Device-level management enforcing encryption, PINs, apps, and remote actions.
MAM
App-level controls allowing containerization and selective corporate data wipe for BYOD.
WIDS / Rogue AP
Use detection to find suspicious radios, then remediate with port security, audits, and policies.
Summary

Layer authentication, device/app controls, and detection to build a resilient wireless and mobile security program that scales with BYOD and cloud services.
