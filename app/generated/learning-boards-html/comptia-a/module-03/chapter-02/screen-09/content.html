Definition Mobile Device Management
Capabilities
  • Enforce PIN/password complexity and auto-lock.
  • Require device encryption and control OS updates.
  • Deploy Wi‑Fi profiles, certificates, and VPN settings.
  • Remote actions: lock, locate, or wipe devices.
Pro tip
Use staged enrollment and test policies on pilot groups before broad rollout to avoid blocking legitimate employee workflows.
Policy example summary
Device enrollment: All corporate devices must enroll in MDM.
Password: Minimum 6-digit PIN, auto-lock 5 minutes.
Encryption: Full device encryption required.
Remote actions: Admins may lock or wipe on loss/theft.
Key Takeaway

MDM secures the device as a whole; balance control with clear privacy and support policies when enforcing device-wide rules.
