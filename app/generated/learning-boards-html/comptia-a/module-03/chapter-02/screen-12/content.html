Corporate Device Security Policy (MDM)

1. Device Enrollment:
   All corporate-issued mobile devices must be enrolled in the corporate MDM solution prior to receiving access to corporate resources.

2. Password/PIN Policy:
   - Minimum length: 6 digits
   - Complexity: numeric PIN or alphanumeric if device supports
   - Auto-lock timeout: 5 minutes
   - Failed attempts lockout: 10 attempts then remote wipe

3. Device Encryption:
   Full device encryption must be enabled and enforced by MDM.

4. Application Management:
   - Only applications from approved app stores are permitted.
   - Installation from unknown sources is prohibited.

5. Remote Management:
   Administrators may remotely lock, locate, or wipe devices in case of loss or theft.
Policy guidance
  • Define enrollment lifecycle and ownership rules.
  • Test lock and wipe functions on pilot devices before rollout.
  • Provide transparent communications to users about privacy and actions.
Common pitfall
Applying overly strict controls without user communication causes resistance; balance security with clear support and privacy statements.
Key Takeaway

Draft policy, pilot it, and instrument monitoring so enforcement is effective and transparent to users.
