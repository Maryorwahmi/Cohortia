Comparison MAM vs MDM
MDM (Device-level)
Controls entire device: enforce PINs, encryption, remote wipe of device, app installation restrictions, and inventory reporting.
When to use
Corporate-owned devices where full control and compliance reporting are required.
MAM (App-level)
Controls corporate applications and data: containerization, selective wipe, and DLP between corporate and personal apps.
When to use
BYOD scenarios where privacy is important and only corporate data needs protection.
Key Takeaway

Combine MDM for corporate devices and MAM for BYOD to protect assets while respecting user privacy.
