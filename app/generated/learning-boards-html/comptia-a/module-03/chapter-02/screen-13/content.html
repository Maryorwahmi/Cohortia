What to Remember Core Concepts
WPA3 Enterprise
Stronger key exchange and mandatory 802.1X for per-user auth and forward secrecy.
802.1X
Port-based access control that uses RADIUS to authenticate devices before granting network access.
EAP
Authentication framework supporting methods like EAP-TLS and PEAP used with 802.1X.
Summary

These three concepts form the authentication foundation; pick EAP methods and certificate practices that match your operational capacity and risk tolerance.
