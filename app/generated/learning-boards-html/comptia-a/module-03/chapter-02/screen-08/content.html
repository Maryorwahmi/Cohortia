Process Flow Rogue AP Detection
Detection techniques
  • WIDS: Monitor RF for unknown SSIDs, signal anomalies, and known attack signatures.
  • Active scans: Scheduled scans that map BSSIDs to physical locations.
  • Inventory comparison: Compare observed radios to authorized AP inventory.
Investigation steps
Alert → Map BSSID → Physical check → Remediate (port disable, remove device).
Prevention controls
Port security: limit MAC addresses and disable unused ports.
Physical security: lock network closets and secure wall jacks.
Policy: forbid unauthorized APs and enforce employee reporting.
Common pitfall
Assuming detection equals prevention. Detection must be followed by physical remediation and policy enforcement to close the gap.
Key Takeaway

Combine automated detection with physical controls and clear employee rules to prevent rogue APs from creating network backdoors.
