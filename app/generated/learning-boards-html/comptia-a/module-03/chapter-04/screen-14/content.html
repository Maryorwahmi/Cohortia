Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Next‑Generation Firewall
DPI, application and user awareness, and integrated IPS give precise control beyond ports.
IPS vs IDS
IDS alerts passively; IPS blocks inline. Tune in detection before enabling prevention.
SIEM & Feeds
SIEM correlates alerts; threat feeds add proactive indicators. Score and dedupe before automatic blocking.
Summary Takeaway

Layered controls, phased rollouts, careful tuning, and centralized correlation are the operational practices that make NGFW and IPS/IDS deployments effective and sustainable.
