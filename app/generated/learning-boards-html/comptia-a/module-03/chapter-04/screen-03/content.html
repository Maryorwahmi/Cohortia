Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Objective Deep Dive
NGFW
Configure DPI, application and user awareness, and URL filtering. Pitfall: enabling without performance testing.
Mnemonic: TEST (Tune, Evaluate, Scale, Track)
IPS/IDS
Place NIPS at choke points, deploy HIPS on critical hosts, and tune signatures. Pitfall: scattered sensors with low visibility.
Mnemonic: CENTRAL (Choke, Evaluate, Narrow, Tune)
SIEM
Forward normalized logs and create correlation rules to find multi-step attacks. Pitfall: log overload without prioritization.
Mnemonic: NORMALIZE
Threat Feeds
Integrate curated feeds for IPs, domains, and hashes. Pitfall: blocking without context leads to business impact.
Mnemonic: CURATE
