Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Threat Intelligence
Process

Integrate and prioritize threat indicators

Ingest curated feeds, score indicator confidence, and apply actions based on thresholds: block, quarantine, or alert for review.

Automate Blocking
High-confidence items: confirmed C2 IPs, known malware hashes — block automatically at NGFW and IPS.
Analyst Review
Lower-confidence signals are routed to SIEM for human review and additional context before blocking.
Operational Note
Automate feed updates and avoid overlapping noisy feeds. Prefer curated commercial or verified community feeds for blocking policies.
Key Takeaway
Treat feeds as a signal pipeline: score, dedupe, and apply actions based on confidence to avoid business disruption.
