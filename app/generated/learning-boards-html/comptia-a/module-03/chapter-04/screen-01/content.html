Advanced Firewall and IPS/IDS Deployments

Understand modern NGFW capabilities, how to deploy IPS/IDS, and how to integrate logs and threat feeds for actionable security.

In this lesson
Plan NGFW/IPS deployments, tune signatures, integrate SIEM, and use threat feeds.
Hands-on

Outcomes

Concept Map
NGFW features → IPS/IDS placement → SIEM correlation → Threat feed integration. Each step tightens detection or improves context for alerts.
NGFW

DPI, app and user awareness, URL filtering, sandboxing, ATP.

IPS/IDS

Passive monitoring or inline prevention; requires placement and tuning to avoid false alerts.

Quick Start

Begin with conservative NGFW policies, place NIPS at choke points, forward logs to a SIEM, and ingest a high‑quality threat feed. Tune signatures iteratively based on logs and business application behavior.
