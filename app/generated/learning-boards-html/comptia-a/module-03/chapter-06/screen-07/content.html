Core Concept

VM Escape

Define the threat and the controls that reduce the likelihood of escape.

What it is
An attacker leverages a vulnerability in the guest or hypervisor to move from guest to host or other guests.
Mitigations
  • Patch hypervisor and guest tools.
  • Disable unnecessary virtual hardware and passthrough.
  • Isolate management networks.
Example
Remove USB passthrough and disable shared folders in VM templates to reduce host-guest bridge vectors.
Common Misconception
Believing guest OS hardening alone is sufficient. Protect the hypervisor and management plane first.
Key Takeaway

Treat virtual hardware and management interfaces as high-value attack surfaces and remove or isolate them when not required.
