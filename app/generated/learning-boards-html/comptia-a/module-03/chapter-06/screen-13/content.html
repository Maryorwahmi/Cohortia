Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Pod Security Standards and Policies
Hands-on

Prevent privileged pods with admission controls

Use Pod Security Standards to enforce read-only roots, disallow hostPath mounts, and block privileged containers.

Policy Goals
  • Prevent privileged containers.
  • Restrict hostPath and node-level mounts.
  • Require read-only root when possible.
Operational Tip
Apply strict policies per namespace and route exceptions through an approval workflow. Reject non-compliant pods at admission rather than rely on post-deployment scanning.
Common Mistake
Allowing privileged pods for convenience. Privileged pods increase the risk of node-level compromise and should be rare and audited.
Key Takeaway

Enforce pod-level restrictions by default and handle exceptions through controlled approvals, not ad-hoc configuration drift.
