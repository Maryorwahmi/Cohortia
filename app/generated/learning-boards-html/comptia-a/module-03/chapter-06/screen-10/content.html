Module 3 › Chapter 3.6 › Container Registries and Image Scanning
Definition

Registry and Image Security

Protect image provenance and scan every build before deploying.

Registry Controls
Require authentication and authorization for push and pull, restrict who can promote images.
Image Scanning
Scan images for vulnerabilities at build time and block promotions with critical results.
Provenance
Sign and version images so consumers can validate source and integrity before deployment.
Common Misconception
Assuming a public image is safe because it's popular. Always scan and validate external images before use.
Key Takeaway

Integrate registry authentication, scanning, and promotion policies into your CI pipeline to secure your image supply chain.
