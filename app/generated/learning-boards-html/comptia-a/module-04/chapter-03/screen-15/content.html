RBAC & MFA
Protect management accounts with least privilege and multi-factor authentication to reduce compromise risk.
IaC & Templates
Enforce consistent, hardened VM deployments and prevent ad-hoc provisioning that leads to sprawl.
EDR & Logging
Install EDR on guests and centralize hypervisor and guest logs for correlation and fast response.
Final takeaway

Combine host and guest hardening with segmentation and monitoring. Enforce these controls through automation and governance to scale security across your virtual estate.
