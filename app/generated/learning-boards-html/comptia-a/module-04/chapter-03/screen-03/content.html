Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Learning Objectives
Operational Connections
Translate technical controls into repeatable operational steps and audits for your environment.
Management Access
Enforce MFA, RBAC, and network restrictions on hypervisor management interfaces so a single compromised credential cannot control hosts.
Patching and Baselines
Schedule hypervisor and guest patches, maintain configuration baselines, and verify compliance with automated scans.
Infrastructure-as-Code
Use IaC to standardize VM provisioning, avoid ad-hoc VMs, and reduce VM sprawl risk through templates and policy enforcement.
Monitoring and Response
Collect logs from hypervisors and guests into a SIEM to detect anomalies and enable rapid containment of incidents.
