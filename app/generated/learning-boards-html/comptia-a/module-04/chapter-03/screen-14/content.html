Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Hypervisor
Software or firmware that creates and runs VMs; secure it to protect all hosted workloads.
Type 1 vs Type 2
Type 1 runs on bare metal and is preferred for production; Type 2 runs on a host OS and is riskier for production workloads.
VM Escape
An attacker breaks guest isolation to reach the hypervisor; mitigated by patching and management controls.
Summary Takeaway

Focus on hypervisor hardening, enforce governance to prevent sprawl, and combine segmentation with monitoring to detect and limit compromises.
