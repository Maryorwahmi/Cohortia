Problem Pattern
VM Sprawl
Unmanaged or forgotten VMs increase attack surface and operational burden.
Why it matters
  • Unpatched VMs are easy targets for attackers.
  • Resource exhaustion affects availability and performance.
  • Incident response is slowed when assets are untracked.
Mitigation pattern
  1. Require IaC templates for provisioning.
  2. Automate inventory and tag VMs with owner and purpose.
  3. Run scheduled audits and remove dormant instances.
Key Takeaway

Governance and automation are the most effective long-term defenses against VM sprawl.

Concrete Example
Require every VM to be provisioned from an IaC template that includes tagging, EDR, a baseline patch policy, and a retirement date to avoid forgotten instances.
Operational checklist
  • Automate discovery and include VM metadata in CMDB.
  • Enforce template-based provisioning with required agents.
  • Schedule quarterly audits and automatic decommissioning rules.
Misconception
Manual monthly cleanups are sufficient — no, automation is necessary to keep pace with rapid provisioning.
