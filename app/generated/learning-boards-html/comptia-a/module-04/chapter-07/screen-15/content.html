Summary › Key Concepts — Part 2
Serverless Functions
Ephemeral, event-driven compute that needs strict IAM, secrets management, and logging.
Microservices
Loosely coupled services requiring per-service identity, API controls, and secure communication like mTLS.
IaC Security
Scan templates before provisioning to prevent public exposure and enforce encryption and least-privilege.
Takeaway

Design your controls so prevention, detection, and response are integrated and measurable across pipeline and runtime.
