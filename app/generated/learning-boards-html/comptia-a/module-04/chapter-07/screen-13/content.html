End-to-end Flow
DevSecOps: SAST, image & IaC scanning block risky artifacts.
CSPM: Continuous checks detect post-deploy drift and compliance gaps.
CWPP: Runtime detection, isolation, and forensic data collection.
IR Playbooks: Automated containment, snapshots, and escalations.
Practical Checklist
  • Protect IaC with scanners and policy gates.
  • Tune CSPM rules for critical assets.
  • Deploy CWPP coverage where workloads run.
  • Automate containment and centralize immutable logs.
