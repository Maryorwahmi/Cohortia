Process › Policy Enforcement Architecture
Components
Detectors: SAST, IaC, Image, DAST
Policy Engine: map findings to severity, compliance rules
Enforcement Gates: CI block, orchestration admission, network policy
Design gates with tiers: block critical issues, warn for low severity, and track trend metrics.
Example Flows
Build: Image scan fails on critical CVE → CI blocks push.
Provision: IaC scan finds public bucket → merge blocked and policy ticket created.
Runtime: CWPP detects anomaly → pod isolated and admin alerted.
Key Takeaway

Automated enforcement turns detection into consistent action and reduces human latency during incidents.
