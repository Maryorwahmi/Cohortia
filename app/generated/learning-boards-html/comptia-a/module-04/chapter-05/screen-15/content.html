What to Remember › Controls & Practices
MFA
Require additional verification for sensitive accounts to prevent credential misuse.
Federation
Use SAML or OIDC to authenticate users with enterprise credentials and enable SSO.
Policies & Logs
Express permissions in JSON and centralize CloudTrail or Activity Log for auditing and alerting.
Final Summary

Combine MFA, federation, scoped policies, and robust logging to build a resilient cloud identity posture that limits unauthorized access and supports fast detection.
