Logging strategy
Ship CloudTrail or Activity Log to a secure, immutable store and integrate with a SIEM for alerts and investigations.
Example event: {"eventName":"CreateUser","userIdentity":{...},"eventTime":"202x-xx-xxTxx:xx:xxZ"}
Monitor important IAM events: new admin creation, policy changes, failed logins, and unusual geographic access. Automate alerting to reduce detection time and ensure logs have controlled access and appropriate retention.
Checklist
  • Enable CloudTrail/Activity Log for all regions/subscriptions.
  • Deliver logs to an immutable central store.
  • Integrate with SIEM and create alerts for critical IAM changes.
Pro tip
Test alerts by simulating a policy change in a safe environment so you confirm your pipeline and notifications work as expected.
