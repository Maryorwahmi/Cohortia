Definition › Identities, Roles, Permissions
Model components
Explain each piece and how they relate in a stable architecture.
Identity
Human users, groups, service principals, and roles assumed by compute resources.
Role
A bundle of permissions assigned to identities to simplify management and auditing.
Permission
Actions allowed on specific resources, expressed in provider policy syntax.
Example
Create a role named "app-readonly" that has s3:GetObject on a particular bucket. Assign that role to the application service principal so it can read objects without possession of long-term credentials.

Avoid assigning permissions to dozens of users manually. Instead, create role definitions, attach a small set of permissions, and assign the role to the required identities. This reduces drift and makes periodic reviews tractable.
Pro tip
Prefer temporary credentials for compute and services—roles with short-lived tokens are safer than embedded long-term keys.
