Learning Objectives
Progress from understanding to applying cloud IAM controls.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain IAM principles
Define identities, permissions, and enforcement and why identity is the perimeter.
Mnemonic: Venn of identity, permissions, and resources.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Implement RBAC & PoLP
Create roles with scoped permissions and apply least privilege to users and services.
Mnemonic: Role = permission container.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Configure MFA & federation
Enable MFA for critical accounts and set up identity federation for SSO with on-prem directories.
Mnemonic: One account, many factors.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Audit and monitor IAM
Use CloudTrail or Activity Log, centralize logs, and alert on critical IAM changes.
Mnemonic: Logs are your identity audit trail.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
