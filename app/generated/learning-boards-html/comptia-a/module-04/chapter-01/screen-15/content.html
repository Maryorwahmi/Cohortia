What to Remember
Three compact concept cards and a closing strip.
Assign Responsibility
State clearly which party secures infrastructure versus workloads for every service you use.
Classify Data
Place data where compliance and risk appetite align with controls and residency needs.
Secure Integrations
Use dedicated interconnects, federated identity, and centralized logs to eliminate integration blind spots.
Final Quick Takeaway

Model choice encodes responsibility. Make that mapping explicit, and build layered controls for visibility and compliance.
