Private Cloud: control and responsibility
Exclusive resources with full organizational control.
Core Definition
Private cloud resources are dedicated to a single organization. They may be located on-premises or hosted by a vendor under exclusive tenancy.
Security Benefits
Full control over patching, hardware security, and environmental safeguards.
Operational Cost
Requires sustained investment in security teams, automation, and monitoring tools to remain secure.
Concrete Example
Intrusion Detection in Private Cloud
Implementing IDS requires choosing sensors, tuning alerts, and staffing analysts. The organization must own forensics, patching cadence, and network controls rather than relying on provider-managed services.
Key Takeaway

Private cloud enables control but shifts all security operations and costs to your organization.
