Risk Assessment for Model Selection
Choose models based on data sensitivity, compliance, cost, and expertise.
01 Classify data
Label data by sensitivity and legal constraints to guide placement decisions.
02 Map compliance
Identify regulatory drivers like GDPR, HIPAA, or PCI and their residency and audit needs.
03 Assess ops
Determine in-house capacity for patching, monitoring, and incident response.
04 Recommend model
Produce a model choice with explicit controls: encryption, segmentation, logging, and identity.
Concrete Example
For PHI, recommend a hybrid architecture keeping PHI on-premises while anonymized workloads run in public cloud. Include dedicated interconnect, unified SIEM, and strict IAM to ensure compliance and traceability.
Key Takeaway

A defensible architecture arises from data-driven risk assessment plus clear operational plans for controls and monitoring.
