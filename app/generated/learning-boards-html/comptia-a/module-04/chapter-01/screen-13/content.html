Common Security Risks and Mitigations
Misconfiguration, inconsistent policies, and governance gaps.
Misconfiguration
Cause: overly permissive ACLs or security groups. Mitigation: automated scanning, hardened templates, and policy-as-code.
Integration Gaps
Cause: inconsistent IAM, logging or network rules across environments. Mitigation: federation, centralized SIEM, and IaC to standardize configs.
Governance Gaps
Cause: unclear roles in community clouds. Mitigation: trust frameworks, shared audits, and clear ownership matrices.
Concrete Example

An exposed public storage container could have been prevented with enforced block-public policies, role-based access, and encryption. Detection alone is insufficient without automated policy enforcement and practiced incident response.
