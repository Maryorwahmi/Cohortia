Cloud Deployment Models and Security Considerations
Understand trade-offs, responsibilities, and controls across public, private, hybrid, and community clouds.
In this lesson
Models, responsibilities, and security controls
Why it matters
Security design must align with where responsibility lives.
Overview
This chapter focuses on the four deployment models and the security implications each creates. Use this overview to keep decisions traceable: who owns the infrastructure, who configures identity, where data may be stored, and which controls are essential.
Public Cloud
Shared providers, CSP manages physical layer; customer manages data and configuration.
Private Cloud
Exclusive resources and full organizational control; greater operational burden.
Hybrid Cloud
Mix of both with secure connectivity, identity federation, and consistent policies.
Community Cloud
Shared among organizations with common compliance or jurisdiction needs.
