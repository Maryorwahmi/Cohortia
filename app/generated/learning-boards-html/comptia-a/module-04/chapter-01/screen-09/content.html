Community Cloud: shared concerns and governance
A cooperative model for organizations with aligned compliance or jurisdiction needs.
What it is
Infrastructure shared by several organizations that trust each other to support common goals such as compliance, security posture, or data residency.
Benefits
Shared costs, focused compliance controls, and combined expertise for specialized security needs.
Challenges
Coordinating governance, access policies, and incident response across independent organizations.
Concrete Example
Healthcare community cloud
A HIPAA-focused community cloud can provide common compliance controls and audits, but each provider remains responsible for securing their specific applications and PHI within the shared environment.
Governance Tip
Define clear trust and roles, and standardize logging and audit procedures across members to enable accountable operations.
