Security Controls and Architectural Patterns
Segmentation, IAM, encryption, and centralized monitoring.
Core Patterns
  • Network segmentation: VPCs/VNets, subnets, security groups
  • Strong IAM: least privilege, role separation, MFA
  • Encryption: at rest and in transit, consider customer-managed keys
Monitoring & Response
Centralize logs from all environments into a unified SIEM to enable correlation across public and private systems and speed incident response.
Pattern Example
Segment sensitive services into private subnets, require MFA, use customer-managed keys for critical datasets, and forward logs to a central SIEM that covers both cloud and on-premises infrastructure.
Key Takeaway

Treat controls as a system: segmentation, IAM, encryption, and monitoring together produce a resilient architecture across deployment models.
