Key Concepts
Three cards summarize core takeaways.
Public vs Private
Public: provider-managed infra, customer-managed data. Private: exclusive resources and full customer control and costs.
Hybrid and Integration
Hybrid requires secure connectivity and unified identity; inconsistent policies at integration points are high-risk.
Controls to Prioritize
Segment networks, enforce least-privilege IAM, encrypt data, and centralize logging for detection and response.
Final Takeaway

Treat deployment model selection as a security decision: map responsibilities and design layered, auditable controls.
