Hybrid Connectivity and Identity
Dedicated links, VPNs, and federated identity keep hybrid environments coherent.
Connectivity Options
  • VPN tunnels for encrypted connections over the internet
  • Dedicated interconnects (Direct Connect, ExpressRoute) for private, high-bandwidth links
  • Strict perimeter and route controls to enforce segmentation
Why dedicated links matter
They reduce exposure, improve throughput, and simplify network controls — but they do not replace unified identity and logging.
Identity Patterns
Use federation (SAML, OAuth) or directory sync to provide single sign-on and consistent IAM across private and public domains.
Concrete Example
Using Azure AD Connect syncs on-premises Active Directory with Azure AD, enabling centralized policies, MFA enforcement, and cross-environment auditing.
Common Mistake
Treating connectivity as the only control — failing to harmonize identity, authorization, and logging leaves gaps.
