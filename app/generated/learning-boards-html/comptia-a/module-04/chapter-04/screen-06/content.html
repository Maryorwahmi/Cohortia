Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Container registries and provenance
Registry supply chain

Registries store tagged images and should enforce policies: authenticated pushes, scanning gates, and signatures to attest integrity.

Step 1
Build in CI
Produce an immutable image and tag with a specific version to avoid 'latest' ambiguity.
Step 2
Scan and sign
Run image scanning and if it passes, sign the image to prove provenance and integrity.
Step 3
Push to registry
Push only scanned, signed images to production registries that enforce access controls and immutability.
Example policy

Require successful image scan, a valid signature, and a pinned tag before the registry accepts a promotion to production. This prevents accidental or malicious deployments of unverified images.

Watchout

Public registries can host untrusted images; do not rely on name or 'official' labels without verification.
