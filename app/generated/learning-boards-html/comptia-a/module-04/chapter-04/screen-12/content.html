Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Secrets management for containers
Runtime secrets pattern

Store secrets in a dedicated manager and inject them at runtime. Rotate and audit access; do not bake secrets into images or source control.

Concrete approaches
  • Use Kubernetes Secrets or Vault to store credentials.
  • Inject secrets as mounted files or environment variables at runtime.
  • Enable encryption at rest for secret stores and apply RBAC.
Example scenario

Instead of hardcoding an API key into a Dockerfile, store it in Vault and grant only the application service account permission to access it. Inject the key at runtime and rotate it regularly to reduce exposure time.

Watchout

Do not assume Kubernetes Secrets are encrypted; confirm your cluster's encryption at rest and RBAC protections.
