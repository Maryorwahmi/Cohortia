Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Image scanning and signing
Scan → Sign → Push

Automate scanning in CI, enforce pass/fail thresholds, then sign successful images as a proof point before registry push.

Scan

Run tools like Trivy or Clair to detect CVEs and misconfigurations.

Fail/Remediate

Treat critical CVEs as build-fail criteria and fix in source or dependencies before proceeding.

Sign

Use image signing so consumers can verify the image origin and that it passed your pipeline checks.

Concrete example

CI runs Trivy; pipeline fails on critical CVEs. After fixes, a signing step stores an attestation before pushing to a protected registry.

Misconception

Scanning without signing leaves room for tampering or accidental re-tagging of unvetted images.
