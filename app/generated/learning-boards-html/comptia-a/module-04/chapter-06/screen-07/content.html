Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Encryption at Rest
Compare modes
A
Provider-managed (SSE-S3)
Simplest, provider controls keys and rotation. Low operational overhead but minimal key control.
B
KMS-managed (SSE-KMS)
You manage keys in KMS. Better auditability and access control; integrates with provider services like object storage and databases.
C
Client-side encryption
Encrypts before upload and keeps keys off the cloud provider. Highest control, highest complexity in key distribution and integration.
Example decision
Payment card data: prefer client-side or KMS-managed keys to meet stringent key-control requirements. Public assets: provider-managed may be sufficient.
Common mistake
Assuming server-side encryption implies provider never sees keys; understand whether keys are customer-managed.
Key Takeaway

Pick the mode that balances control and operational needs while satisfying regulatory requirements.
