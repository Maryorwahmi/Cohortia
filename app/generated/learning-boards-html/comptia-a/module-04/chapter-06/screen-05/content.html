Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Operationalizing Classification
Process steps
A reliable pipeline turns labels into enforceable controls.
1
Discover & Inventory
Scan storage, databases, and object stores to find data locations and schemas.
2
Assess Sensitivity
Evaluate business value, legal requirements, and impact for each dataset.
3
Tag & Enforce
Apply tags that trigger encryption, access policies, and retention rules.
4
Monitor & Audit
Log access, track policy failures, and detect classification drift.
Example scenario
An upload service accepts images and documents. Discovery flags two kinds: images only, and attachments with metadata. Assess labels attachments with PII as Restricted. Tagging triggers a bucket policy to require KMS-backed encryption and ACLs deny public access. Monitoring triggers an alert if a Restricted object lacks the tag.
Key Takeaway

Automate tagging and enforcement to prevent misconfigurations as systems scale.
