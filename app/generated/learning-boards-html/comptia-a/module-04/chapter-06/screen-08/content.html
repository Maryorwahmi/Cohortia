Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Data in Transit
Patterns & protocols
TLS for HTTP, mutual TLS for service authentication, VPNs for private connections.
User to cloud
Enforce HTTPS with strong TLS configurations and HSTS where appropriate.
Service to service
Use mutual TLS or service mesh to authenticate and encrypt internal traffic.
On-prem to cloud
Use VPNs or private links and ensure tunnel ciphers meet policy.
Management & control planes
Ensure administrative APIs and telemetry are encrypted and authenticated.
Concrete example
Web tier calls an internal billing service. Configure mutual TLS between the two, restrict TLS client certificates to authorized services, and log TLS session information for audit and forensics.
Misconception
Treating only external traffic as requiring encryption. Internal traffic and service meshes also need encryption and auth to avoid lateral exposure.
Key Takeaway

Encrypt every network hop that moves sensitive data and know where encryption terminates.
