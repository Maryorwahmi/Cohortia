Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Cloud Compliance
Framework mapping
GDPR
Data residency, subject rights, protection by design; choose regions and logging that support EU controls.
HIPAA
PHI protections, encryption, audit logging, and BAAs with cloud providers.
PCI DSS
Network segmentation, encryption, strict access, and continuous monitoring for cardholder data.
Practical controls
Region selection, VPC/dedicated isolation, KMS key control, strict IAM policies, and comprehensive audit trails are common controls that support all three frameworks in different ways.
Pitfall
Assuming provider certifications replace your responsibility to configure services; certifications help but do not obviate proper configuration and evidence collection.
Key Takeaway

Compliance requires mapping legal obligations to concrete architecture and operational controls, then documenting evidence.
