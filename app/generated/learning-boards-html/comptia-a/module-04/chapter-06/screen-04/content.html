Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Data Classification
Definition & purpose
Assign sensitivity labels so security controls align with business and legal risk.
Public: No restrictions, low controls.
Internal: Business-use only, baseline encryption and access controls.
Confidential/Restricted: Strong encryption, restricted roles, audit logging.
Common mistake
Relying solely on default provider settings without ensuring key control and retention policies match classification.
Operational flow
Inventory
Catalog data sources and flows.
Assess
Evaluate sensitivity, legal obligations, and impact.
Label
Apply tags used by automation and policy engines.
Example: Customer orders
Classify order records containing payment data as Restricted; images without PII can be Internal or Public.
Key Takeaway

Labels must drive technical policies: encryption, retention, and access control.
