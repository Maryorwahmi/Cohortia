Key Concepts
Three compact reminders
Identity Federation
Trust-based assertions from an IdP enable cross-domain access without duplicate accounts.
SSO & MFA
SSO centralizes login and must be protected by MFA and careful session controls.
PAM & Governance
PAM protects privileged accounts; IGA automates lifecycle and access reviews to prevent privilege creep.
Summary

Design integrations with correct metadata, enforce MFA and PAM for high-value accounts, and use governance tools to keep access appropriate over time.
