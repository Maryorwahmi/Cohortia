Quick Reference
Three short reminders for deployment
SAML / OIDC
Match protocol to app type and verify audience, issuer, and claims during testing.
PAM
Onboard privileged accounts, enable session brokering and recording, and enforce rotation.
Directory Sync
Plan attribute mapping, pilot syncs, and ensure clean deprovisioning across systems.
Final Tip

Protect the identity layer with MFA and monitoring; use governance to keep access appropriate as systems change.
