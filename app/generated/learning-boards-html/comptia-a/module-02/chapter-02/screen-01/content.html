Advanced IAM Integration
Design secure federation, SSO, MFA, PAM, and directory sync strategies for hybrid and cloud systems.

This chapter shows how to connect identity providers and service providers securely, choose appropriate authentication factors, and protect high-value administrative access. Expect checklists, integration steps, and common pitfalls to avoid.

We emphasize practical configuration points, attribute mapping, and governance that keep access consistent as systems move to the cloud.
