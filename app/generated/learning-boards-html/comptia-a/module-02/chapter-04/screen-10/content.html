Module 02 › Chapter 2.4 › Securing Hybrid Connectivity
Connectivity options
  • IPsec or SSL VPN for encrypted site-to-cloud tunnels.
  • Dedicated direct links for high-bandwidth, low-latency connections.
  • ExpressRoute or Direct Connect equivalents for private connectivity.
Ensure monitoring across the link and test failover regularly.
Operational checklist
  1. Choose connection mode by bandwidth and latency needs.
  2. Replicate segmentation and access policies on both sides.
  3. Forward logs into a central SIEM for unified monitoring.
  4. Validate failover and DR for cross-site traffic paths.
Key Takeaway
Connectivity and policy parity are both required to avoid blind spots in hybrid environments.
