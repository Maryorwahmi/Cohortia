Module 02 › Chapter 2.4 › CSPM: Continuous Scanning
What CSPM finds
  • Publicly accessible storage buckets or services.
  • Unencrypted databases or object stores at rest.
  • Overly permissive IAM policies or open network ports.
Integration checklist
  1. Grant minimal scan permissions.
  2. Tune rules and suppress false positives.
  3. Define triage and remediation workflows.
Concrete examples
Public S3 bucket
Risk: data exposure and compliance violations. Remediation: set private ACLs and enable encryption.
Unencrypted DB
Risk: if storage or snapshots leak, data is readable. Remediation: enable server-side encryption and rotate keys.
Key Takeaway
Treat CSPM alerts as inputs to prioritized remediation and IaC fixes to prevent recurrence.
