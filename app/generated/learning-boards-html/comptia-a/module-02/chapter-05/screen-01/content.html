Endpoint Detection & Response and MDM/UEM Integration
Understand telemetry, agent deployment, threat hunting, and how device management integrates with identity and network controls.

EDR continuously collects rich telemetry so analysts can hunt and investigate. MDM/UEM enrolls and enforces device posture and separates personal from corporate data for BYOD scenarios.

Integration with SIEM and IAM enables correlation across endpoints, network, and identity, reducing detection time and improving response accuracy.
