Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Assessment
Scenario
1,200 endpoints, mixed corporate and BYOD, SOC of six, alert noise, and need for safe containment without disrupting production servers.
Recommended approach
Pilot, measure telemetry rates, use 30-day hot retention, staged automation requiring multi-indicator confirmation, containerize BYOD, and map EDR fields into SIEM for correlation with IAM logs.
Why this helps
Reduces false positives, prevents inadvertent disruption, and improves signal-to-noise by correlating endpoint, network, and identity events.
Guided questions
  • Which endpoints should be excluded from automatic isolation and why?
  • How will you measure event rates during the pilot?
  • What fields must be present for reliable SIEM correlation?
Key Takeaway

Design choices should be guided by pilot data, analyst capacity, and the criticality of endpoints to minimize disruption and maximize detection fidelity.
