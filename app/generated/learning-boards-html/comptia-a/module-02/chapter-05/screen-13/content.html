Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › Best Practices
Practice hierarchy
Pilot and validate
Run a pilot to measure compatibility and telemetry volume.
Tune collection
Capture forensic details but reduce noisy events.
Document playbooks
Map alerts to triage steps and owner responsibilities.
Rehearse responses
Test automation in controlled drills and ensure rollback plans.
Common mistakes
  • Full rollout without pilot testing.
  • Broad automated isolation on critical systems.
  • Ignoring telemetry field mapping for SIEM correlation.
Key Takeaway

Operationalize with pilots, tuning, documentation, and rehearsal to reduce risk and improve detection quality.
