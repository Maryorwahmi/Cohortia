Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › MDM / UEM
Enroll and enforce
Enrollment: Device Enrollment Program, Android Enterprise, or manual methods to register devices.
Policies: Strong passcodes, encryption, app whitelisting/blacklisting, secure Wi‑Fi and VPN profiles.
Remote actions: Selective wipe, full wipe, and device configuration updates.
BYOD and containerization
Use work profiles or containers to isolate corporate apps and data. This enables selective wipe and policy enforcement without accessing personal user data.
Key Takeaway

Containerization preserves user privacy while securing corporate assets, making BYOD policies feasible and less intrusive.

Common Mistake
Applying corporate-level device restrictions on BYOD devices causes user pushback and leads to policy workarounds.
