Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › BYOD Trade-offs
Policy comparison
Strict control
Full device management gives strong security but impacts privacy and user satisfaction.
Work profile / container
Isolates corporate data and allows selective wipe while preserving user privacy.
Minimal enforcement
Less intrusive, but increases risk of data leakage unless paired with app-level controls.
Design guidance
  • Simplify enrollment to encourage adoption.
  • Apply least privilege to corporate containers.
  • Monitor for policy bypass and enforce selective wipe when needed.
Key Takeaway

A policy that secures corporate data without heavy intrusion gets better long-term adoption and reduces bypass attempts.
