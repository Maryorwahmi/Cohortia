Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Integrate Threat Intelligence Feeds
Feed Types & Sources
  • Commercial vendor feeds
  • Open-source feeds and community sources
  • Industry sharing groups and internal intelligence
Integration Steps
  1. Ingest feed into SIEM with metadata and freshness indicators.
  2. Map IOC fields to normalized event fields.
  3. Score or whitelist feed entries to reduce noise.
Validation

Run discovery to see how often a feed generates true matches and tune or remove feeds that add little signal.

Concrete Example

When a successful login matches an IP on a validated threat feed, the SIEM increases severity and triggers an enrichment step to collect endpoint telemetry and initiate analyst notification for rapid triage.

Key Takeaway

Threat intelligence is powerful but requires validation and tuning; integrate feeds that demonstrably improve detection quality.
