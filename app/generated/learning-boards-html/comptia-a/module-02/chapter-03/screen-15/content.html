Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
SIEM
Aggregates and normalizes logs, enabling real-time monitoring and correlation across systems.
Correlation Rules
Logic that identifies sequences or patterns indicating potential incidents.
Threat Intelligence
IOCs enrich events so the SOC can prioritize confirmed malicious activity.
Summary Takeaway

Consistent data, well-designed correlations, validated intelligence, and gated automation make SIEM integration effective and safe.
