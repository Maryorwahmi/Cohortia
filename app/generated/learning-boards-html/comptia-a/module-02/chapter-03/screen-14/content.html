Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Avoiding Pitfalls in SIEM Integration
Top Mistakes
  • Not collecting from critical systems.
  • Agent misconfiguration or insufficient SIEM capacity.
  • Over-automating SOAR without validation.
  • Integrating noisy threat feeds without tuning.
Best Practices

Phased onboarding, monitoring ingestion, sandbox testing, and periodic reviews keep the system reliable and responsive.

Check Your Understanding

If an automated playbook isolated a host that turned out to be a critical database node, what step would you add to prevent this? The recommended pattern is to require human approval or whitelist critical systems, and test the playbook in a sandbox first.

Key Takeaway

Operational hygiene — testing, monitoring, and gated automation — protects availability while enabling faster response.
