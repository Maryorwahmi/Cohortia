Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Create a Brute-Force → Successful Login Rule
Rule Conditions — Conceptual
  • Condition 1: Failed Login events, group by source_ip and target_username, threshold > 10 within 5 minutes.
  • Condition 2: Successful Login for same target_username within 10 minutes, source_ip different or unusual geo for that user.
  • Logic: IF Condition 1 AND Condition 2 THEN trigger alert.
Actions

Enrich with threat intelligence, notify SOC, and optionally trigger a SOAR playbook to block or isolate after human approval for critical actions.

Conceptual Rule Example
// Pseudocode for conceptual SIEM rule
IF count(event.type == "failed_login" AND event.source_ip == X
       AND event.target_username == Y) > 10
   WITHIN 5 minutes
AND exists(event.type == "successful_login" AND event.target_username == Y
       AND (event.source_ip != X OR event.geo != baseline_geo(Y)))
   WITHIN 10 minutes
THEN generate alert severity=high; enrich(source_ip); notify_SOC(); trigger_SOAR_playbook()
Key Takeaway

Express rules as clear conditions and actions, test with historical data, and include enrichment before automated containment where possible.
