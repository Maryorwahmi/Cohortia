Architecture › Zero Trust Network Access
Principles
Never trust implicitly, always verify identity and device posture, and grant least-privilege access to specific resources.
Identity-centric policies — access by user and group, not network location.
Device posture checks — verify patching and antivirus before access.
Micro-segmentation — limit resource exposure and lateral movement.
Pilot ZTNA on non-critical apps first and ensure integration with IAM and logging for troubleshooting.
Components
Policy Enforcement Point (PEP) or ZTNA gateway: authenticates and enforces access.
Identity Provider (IdP): provides authentication and group membership claims.
Posture Service: verifies device health before granting access.
Common mistake
Overly broad policies that grant access to multiple resources break the least-privilege model.
