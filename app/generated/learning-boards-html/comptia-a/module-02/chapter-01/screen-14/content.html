Micro-segmentation
Small trust zones with minimal allowed flows reduce attack surface and help containment.
802.1X
Port-based network access control requiring device authentication before granting VLAN access.
Policy Enforcement Point (PEP)
Component that enforces ZTNA decisions after authenticating identity and verifying posture.
Final takeaway
Combine segmentation, verification, logging, and cautious automation to build an integrated, resilient security posture that preserves availability.
