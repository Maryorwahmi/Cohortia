Module 3 › Chapter 2.6 › Monitoring & Pipeline Security
Centralized monitoring
Aggregate logs from WAF, RASP, app telemetry, and CI into a SIEM for correlation and alerting.
Correlation example
Combine WAF blocks, failed auth events, and DAST findings to detect coordinated attacks that single signals miss.
Incident playbooks
Define who triages high-severity alerts, how to quarantine affected services, and how to roll back or patch quickly.
Protect CI/CD
  • Use role-based access and short-lived credentials for pipeline agents.
  • Audit pipeline changes and require multi-party approvals for sensitive steps.
  • Rotate tokens and secrets that pipelines use to push artifacts or deploy.
Key Takeaway

Monitoring plus protected pipelines reduce the risk of unnoticed exploitation and ensure rapid response when incidents occur.
