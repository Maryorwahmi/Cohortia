Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Commercial vs ISAC
Commercial feeds
Curated, analyst-validated intelligence

Commercial providers deliver context-rich reports, validated IoCs, and actor profiles. They are valuable for depth and timeliness but come at a cost and must be integrated carefully to avoid overload.

ISACs / ISAOs
Sector-focused sharing and collective defense

Industry sharing centers provide anonymized, practical bulletins tailored to specific sectors. They are excellent for detecting sector trends and recommended mitigations from peers.

Scenario
A healthcare provider receives an H-ISAC bulletin about an ongoing ransomware campaign. Use the bulletin to adjust incident response priorities. Then consult a commercial feed for validated IoCs and an actor profile to support longer-term defenses and hunting.
Common mistake
Blindly ingesting all commercial IoCs without filters leads to alert fatigue and blocking legitimate traffic.
Pro tip
Apply relevance rules: geography, sector targets, and asset criticality before enforcement.
