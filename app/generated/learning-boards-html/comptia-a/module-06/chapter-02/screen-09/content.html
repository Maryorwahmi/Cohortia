Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › TTPs
Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures

TTPs describe how attackers achieve objectives and are organized in frameworks like MITRE ATT&CK. They support behavior-based detection and strategic planning because they are less ephemeral than single indicators.

Example technique
Credential dumping that follows lateral authentication attempts is a classic pattern that maps to ATT&CK techniques and should trigger detection rules targeting the behavior chain.
Operational use
Map events to ATT&CK, build detections that watch for the sequence of actions, and use TTPs to inform long-term controls and threat hunting.
Misconception
Believing TTPs are only for strategic use. In practice they directly shape detection logic and incident response playbooks.
Key Takeaway

Use TTP mappings to create behavior-focused detections that remain useful as specific IoCs change.
