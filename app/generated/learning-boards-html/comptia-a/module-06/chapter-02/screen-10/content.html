Threat Actor Profiles

Profiles synthesize motives, capabilities, targets, and TTP history. They are strategic inputs used to drive prioritization and long-term defensive decisions rather than immediate blocking actions.

Use case
If an actor targets healthcare for ransom, prioritize backups, segmentation, and detection of lateral movement.
Caveat
Attribution is probabilistic; use profiles to influence risk, not to claim definitive blame without evidence.
Analyst tip
Combine historical TTPs, infrastructure overlap, and intent indicators to assign confidence levels to actor attributions.
Key Takeaway

Use actor profiles to prioritize defenses and design hunting hypotheses, but treat attribution as a confidence-based input.
