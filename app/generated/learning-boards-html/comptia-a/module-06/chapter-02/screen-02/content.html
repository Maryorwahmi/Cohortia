Learning Objectives
Skills to identify, evaluate, and share threat intelligence.
01
Identify internal and external sources
Recognize logs, NetFlow, OSINT, commercial feeds and ISACs and why each is useful to your environment.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Evaluate relevance and reliability
Assess timeliness, provenance, and false positive risk before integrating feeds into controls.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Explain open vs commercial feeds
Contrast free OSINT with curated commercial feeds, including cost, depth, and analyst validation.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Describe sharing formats
Describe STIX/TAXII and OpenIOC and why machine-readable exchange matters for automation.
Verified Competency Milestone
Bloom taxonomy
Learn
Understand
Apply
Master
These objectives balance identification, evaluation, and practical application so you can move from noisy alerts to prioritized, validated intelligence that protects critical assets.
