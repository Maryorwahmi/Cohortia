Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
Internal Sources
SIEM, EDR, NetFlow and human reports provide contextual evidence tied to your assets.
External Sources
OSINT, commercial feeds, and ISACs broaden awareness but vary in trustworthiness.
IoCs
Atomic indicators for immediate detection; enrich and expire them to avoid stale blocking.
Summary
Combine internal and external sources, validate evidence, and prefer behavior-based detection driven by TTPs for resilient defenses.
