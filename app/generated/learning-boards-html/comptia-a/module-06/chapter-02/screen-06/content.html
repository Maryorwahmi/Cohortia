Open-Source Intelligence

OSINT covers publicly available threat information such as blogs, advisories, public IoC repositories, research papers, and social feeds. It can alert you to emerging threats quickly and at low cost, but quality varies and verification is required.

Strengths
  • Timely community reporting
  • Low cost and broad coverage
Limitations
  • Variable accuracy
  • Potential for misinformation
Example
A blog post describes a new ransomware sample and shares a file hash and C2 domain. Validate by cross-referencing the hash against another trusted feed and checking whether your environment has the relevant file or processes.
Key Takeaway

Treat OSINT as an early warning that requires verification and enrichment before enforcement in your environment.
