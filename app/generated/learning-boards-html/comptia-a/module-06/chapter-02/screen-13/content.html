Criteria for Evaluation

Use a checklist of provenance, accuracy, sector relevance, and integration effort to score potential feeds. This helps you allocate budget and automation safely.

Provenance & accuracy
Does the source state how it collected the data and show past reliability?
Relevance & scope
Is the intelligence targeted to your industry, geography, and asset types?
Simple scoring
Assign 0–3 for provenance, accuracy, relevance, and integration effort. Prioritize sources with high total scores and manageable integration cost.
Key Takeaway

A scored portfolio of sources prevents over-reliance on any single feed and helps balance cost, coverage, and trustworthiness.
