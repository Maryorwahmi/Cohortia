Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)

IoCs are atomic data points — IPs, domains, URLs, hashes — used for tactical detection. They are effective for immediate action but often short-lived and need enrichment.

IP address: 192.0.2.1
Domain: malicious-c2.com
Hash: SHA256:a1b2c3d4...
Operational note
Tag IoCs with source, confidence, and expiry. Automate safe actions but require analyst approval for broad blocking on high-value assets.
Example scenario
An internal server connects to a public blocklisted IP. Treat the IP as a tactical IoC, block temporarily, and investigate host activity and related process hashes before permanent remediation.
Key Takeaway

Use IoCs for quick detection and blocking, but enrich and corroborate before treating them as decisive evidence of compromise.
