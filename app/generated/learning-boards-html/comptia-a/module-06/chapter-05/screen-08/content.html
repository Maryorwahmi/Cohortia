Tool comparison
Packet capture (tcpdump / Wireshark)
Full packet contents for protocol inspection and payload validation. Use for targeted, time-bound captures to avoid storing large volumes.
Flow data (NetFlow / IPFIX)
Metadata about conversations: source, destination, port, duration, and size. Good for long-term anomaly detection and prioritization.
Zeek
Performs deep inspection and emits structured logs for DNS, HTTP, and connections that are easy to search in a SIEM.
Practical workflow
  1. Use NetFlow to find unusual long-lived or low-volume external connections.
  2. Identify the internal host and external IP from the flow record.
  3. Trigger a targeted packet capture for that host and IP to confirm payload or protocol misuse.
Key takeaway
Flows help you prioritize. Packets and Zeek logs give the evidence you need to confirm exfiltration or C2.
