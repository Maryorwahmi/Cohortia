Method comparison

Select based on intelligence & data
Hypothesis-driven
Start with a testable TTP. Efficient when you have operational intelligence.
Known Indicator
Search for IoCs from feeds. Quick but limited by indicator freshness.
Behavioral
Look for anomalies and patterns. Useful against polymorphic or zero-day threats.
Combine methods when appropriate: a behavioral anomaly may reveal new indicators, which you then validate with a hypothesis-driven hunt and convert into detections.
Practical scenario
If intelligence reports PowerShell Empire activity in your sector, begin with hypothesis-driven hunts for encoded PowerShell. Supplement with indicator searches against known C2 hosts and behavioral checks for process spawning anomalies.
