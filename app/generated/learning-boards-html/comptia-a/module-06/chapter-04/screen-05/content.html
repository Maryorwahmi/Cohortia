Hunting Loop — Phases

01
Formulate Hypothesis
02
Collect Data
03
Analyze
04
Uncover / Refine
05
Enrich & Automate
Collect: prioritize EDR process events, SIEM logs with command-line capture, network flows, and endpoint forensic artifacts.
Analyze: use statistics, visual timelines, pivoting, and manual inspection to validate or reject a hypothesis.
Enrich: convert confirmed indicators and TTP patterns into TIP entries, SIEM rules, or automated hunts to reduce manual work.
Common mistake
Skipping documentation or failing to feed results back into intelligence. Every hunt should produce artifacts that improve detection coverage.
Key Takeaway
Treat the loop as iterative: even negative results refine your baselines and inform future hypotheses.
