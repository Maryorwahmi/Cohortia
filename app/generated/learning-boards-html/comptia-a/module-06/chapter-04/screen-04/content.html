Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › Hunting vs IR
Reactive: Incident Response
  • Triggered by alerts or confirmed incidents.
  • Focuses on containment, eradication, recovery.
  • Follows playbooks and escalation paths.
Common misconception
Believing hunting replaces IR. Hunters escalate confirmed findings to IR for containment.
Proactive: Threat Hunting
  • Assumes possible compromise and searches without an alert.
  • Uses hypotheses, telemetry queries, and analytics.
  • Reduces dwell time and uncovers stealthy threats.
Key takeaway
Use both: hunting finds what alerts miss; IR restores safety when hunts uncover confirmed threats.
Concrete example
A hunter queries SIEM for process creation events showing powershell.exe with "-EncodedCommand" or unusually long command lines. If confirmed as malicious, the finding triggers IR to isolate hosts and remediate; if no threat is found, the rule is tuned or documented to improve detection in the future.
