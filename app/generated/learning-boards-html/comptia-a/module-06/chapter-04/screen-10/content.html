Statistical & Visual techniques

  • Outlier detection on command-line length, frequency, and login times.
  • Timelines and process trees to reconstruct sequences of actions.
  • Heat maps for unusual network connections or account activity.
Validation rule
Always confirm anomalies against asset roles, scheduled tasks, and known automation accounts before escalating.

Pivoting workflow

  1. Start with suspicious event (e.g., encoded PowerShell).
  2. Pivot to parent/child processes and associated network connections.
  3. Search for similar patterns on other hosts and users.
  4. Document timeline and escalate to IR if confirmed.
Key Tip
Keep a reproducible query and record the pivots so others can validate your findings rapidly.
