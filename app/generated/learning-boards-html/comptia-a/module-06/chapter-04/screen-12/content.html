Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › Safety & Mistakes

Top mistakes

  • Hunting without a clear objective or hypothesis.
  • Running broad queries that produce excessive noise or system load.
  • Not documenting negative results or missing data visibility gaps.

Safety checklist

  • Confirm authorization and legal review for data access.
  • Limit query scope, sample when necessary, and avoid heavy aggregation on production systems.
  • Coordinate with system owners if hunts might affect services.
Why documentation matters
Document both findings and the telemetry gaps you encounter. Negative results narrow the attack surface and inform future hunts and SIEM tuning. This is how hunting matures into a reliable capability.
