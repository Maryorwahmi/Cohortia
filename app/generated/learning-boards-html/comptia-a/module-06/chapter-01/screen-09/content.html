Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Dissemination & Feedback
Dissemination formats
  • Automated tactical feeds for controls with metadata and confidence scoring.
  • Operational reports for SOCs with TTP mappings and recommended detections.
  • Strategic summaries for leadership that link threats to business risk.
Designing feedback
Ask recipients measurable questions: Did the intelligence reduce investigation time? Were false positives unacceptable? Use answers to update IRs and collection filters.
Scenario follow-up
After providing a detection feed, the SOC reports many blocked sessions were benign. Feedback leads to enriching indicators with time windows or behavior patterns to reduce false positives.
Key takeaway
Make dissemination audience-specific and build measurable feedback into the cycle.
