Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Hands-on Activity
Scenario
Organization: mid-sized e-commerce platform. Recent incidents: credential stuffing and periodic DDoS attempts affecting checkout services.
Starter IRs (examples)
  • IR 1: Which IP ranges and user-agent patterns correspond to credential stuffing against our auth endpoints? Potential sources: authentication logs, WAF telemetry, commercial botnet feeds.
  • IR 2: What patterns indicate early-stage DDoS reconnaissance against our public order endpoints? Potential sources: upstream provider telemetry, IDS logs, community alerts.
  • IR 3: Are there compromised credential lists or credential stuffing tools referencing our domain on OSINT forums? Potential sources: OSINT, dark web forums, password-leak feeds.
Mapping to actions
Each IR should map to a measurable action: adjust rate limits, block or monitor IP ranges during activity windows, update SIEM detection rules, or engage CDN/ISP for DDoS mitigation.
Key takeaway
Good IRs are specific, source-mapped, and tied to actions you can measure.
