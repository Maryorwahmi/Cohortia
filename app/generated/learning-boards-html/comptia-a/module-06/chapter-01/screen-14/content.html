Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Threat Intelligence (TI)
Processed and contextualized data that yields actionable insights about threats.
Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)
Atomic signals like IPs, domains, and hashes; useful but often need context.
TTPs
Tactics, Techniques and Procedures describe the methods used by actors and link indicators into campaigns.
Summary takeaway

Intelligence adds context to signals so you can prioritize actions, plan defenses, and measure impact.
