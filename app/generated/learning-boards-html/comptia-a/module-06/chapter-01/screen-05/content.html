Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Intelligence Levels
Strategic
Long-term trends & motivations
Informs executives on investments and risk appetite by describing big-picture threats and geopolitical drivers.
Example: A rise in state-sponsored attacks driving a decision to invest in threat hunting and supply-chain reviews.
Operational
TTPs and campaign context
Used by SOCs to tune detection, update playbooks and guide incident response with actor methods and behavior patterns.
Example: Malware families, exploit chains, and lateral movement techniques that explain how attacks are executed.
Tactical
Indicators for immediate use
IoCs like IPs, domains, and hashes that feed security controls for detection and blocking right now.
Example: Blocklist updates for a SIEM rule to drop known malicious connections during a campaign.
Comparison notes
  • Complementary roles: strategic sets priorities, operational translates into playbooks, tactical provides immediate controls.
  • Misconception: Tactical feeds alone do not inform long-term investments.
  • Delivery must be tailored: executive briefs, SOC reports, or automated feeds based on audience.
