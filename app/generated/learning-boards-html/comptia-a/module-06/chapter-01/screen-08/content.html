Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Processing & Analysis
Processing tasks
  • Normalize formats, timestamps, and schema.
  • De-duplicate repeated events to reduce noise.
  • Enrich with context: ASNs, registrant data, historical activity.
Analysis activities
  • Correlate IoCs across sources to reveal campaigns.
  • Identify TTPs and map to known actor profiles.
  • Assess impact and produce prioritized recommendations.
Concrete example
Multiple IPs seen probing login endpoints are enriched with ASN and historical behavior. Analysis reveals they belong to a known credential stuffing botnet active during specific hours and using common user-agent patterns. Result: a recommended detection rule and temporary blocking profile for observed windows, plus a note to investigate compromised credentials.
Pro tip
Track source confidence levels to weigh evidence during analysis.
Mistake
Relying solely on automated matches without analyst validation can cause misattribution.
