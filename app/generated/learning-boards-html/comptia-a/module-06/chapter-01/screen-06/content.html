Purpose of each phase
  • Planning: set focused questions tied to assets and risks to avoid noise.
  • Collection: gather targeted raw data from the most relevant sources.
  • Processing: normalize, deduplicate and enrich to prepare for analysis.
  • Analysis: correlate, attribute and draw implications for action.
  • Dissemination: deliver tailored outputs for the intended audience.
  • Feedback: improve future cycles by measuring usefulness and accuracy.
Why iterate?
Threats and business context change. Iteration ensures intelligence stays aligned with priorities and maintains operational value.
Concrete scenario
A financial services team plans to focus on fraud. Collection pulls transaction anomalies and OSINT on fraud toolkits. Processing enriches events with customer metadata. Analysis links tools to a campaign. Dissemination gives SOC detection rules and an executive risk brief. Feedback refines the detection window and adds new log sources.
Key takeaway
Treat intelligence as a cycle, not a one-time product.
