Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › IoC Enrichment
Enrichment layers
  • Technical: ASN, geolocation, reputation scores.
  • Historical: past activity windows, prior campaigns.
  • Contextual: targeted assets and industry focus.
Correlation and attribution
Link enriched IoCs to TTPs and known actor profiles to form a narrative and recommend mitigations with confidence levels.
Concrete example
A feed of scanning IPs is enriched with ASN and seen-to-target data. Correlation shows the IPs are used by a botnet linked to credential stuffing. Analysts assign confidence and recommend rate limiting during observed windows, deploy monitoring rules, and add curated indicators to prevention controls.
Key takeaway
Enrichment plus correlation creates meaning; document confidence to avoid false attribution.
