Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Assessment: IoCs vs Intelligence
Evaluation checklist
  • Does the product include attribution or actor context?
  • Are TTPs and impact assessed?
  • Are recommendations and confidence levels provided?
  • Is the format tailored to the intended audience?
Example transformation
Raw IP feed + enrichment (ASN, historical activity) + correlation to credential stuffing behavior + suggested detection rules = actionable intelligence for SOC and risk decisions for leadership.
Key takeaway
Ask whether the product changes a decision — if not, it's still raw data.
Common mistake
Assuming labeling alone converts IoCs into intelligence without analysis or recommendations.
