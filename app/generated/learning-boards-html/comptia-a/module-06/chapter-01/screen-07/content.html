Formulating Intelligence Requirements (IRs)
IRs should be specific questions tied to critical assets and recent incidents. They direct collection and help prioritize sources.
Example IR: "What new credential stuffing techniques are emerging and what IoCs are associated with them?"
Collection sources
  • Internal logs: authentication, WAF, and application logs for targeted behaviors.
  • OSINT and forums: for actor chatter and emerging tools.
  • Commercial feeds and ISACs: for curated intelligence and community indicators.
Collection checklist
  • Map IRs to sources before collecting.
  • Record source metadata: reliability, timeliness, and scope.
  • Avoid dumping unfiltered feeds into analysis without enrichment.
Key takeaway
Collect intentionally: answer the IRs, not every available signal.
