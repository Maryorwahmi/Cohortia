Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Common Pitfalls
Top mistakes
  1. Treating IoC feeds as finished intelligence.
  2. Unfocused collection without IRs.
  3. Delivering the wrong format to stakeholders.
  4. Overstating attribution without confidence.
  5. Skipping feedback and iteration.
Mitigations
  • Define IRs tied to assets before collecting.
  • Enrich and weigh indicators; document confidence.
  • Tailor dissemination and require measurable feedback.
Common misconception
Assuming more data equals better intelligence. It does not without focus and context.
