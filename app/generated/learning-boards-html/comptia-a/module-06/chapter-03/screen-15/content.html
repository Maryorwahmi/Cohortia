Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Key Concepts
Threat Intelligence Platform
Central hub that aggregates, normalizes, enriches, analyzes, and shares intelligence.
Normalization
Map diverse formats to a canonical schema so indicators can be correlated reliably.
Enrichment
Add WHOIS, GeoIP, TTPs and telemetry so indicators are actionable.
Correlation & Analysis
Identify relationships and emerging campaigns from linked indicators.
Scoring
Assign risk and map score bands to actions to prioritize responses.
Integration Points
SIEM, SOAR, EDR, and firewalls are the primary consumers for actionable intelligence.
Takeaway
Preserve provenance, vet sources, and map scores to reversible, auditable actions to make TIPs effective operational tools.
