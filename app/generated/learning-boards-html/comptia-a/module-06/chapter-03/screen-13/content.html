Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Deployment & Best Practices

Common challenges

  • Ingesting irrelevant sources without defined requirements
  • Legacy systems lacking modern connectors
  • Poor data quality and inconsistent provenance
  • Over-automation without analyst oversight
Common mistake
Deploying many feeds at once and expecting immediate accuracy is a recipe for alert fatigue.

Best practices

Start with intelligence requirements aligned to your threat profile
Vet sources and sample payloads before enabling ingestion
Tune correlation and scoring iteratively with analyst feedback
Log provenance and make dissemination actions reversible
Key Takeaway
A TIP succeeds when process and governance guide gradual, monitored integration and tuning.
