Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Analysis & Correlation
Process

Find relationships and patterns

Use shared attributes, time windows, and graph links to group indicators into campaigns or clusters.
  • Attribute matching: shared ASN, WHOIS, or hosting patterns.
  • Temporal links: indicators seen within the same timeframe.
  • Behavioral clusters: shared TTP signatures and payload similarities.
Example
Multiple phishing domains resolving to one IP and sharing registrant patterns—elevate to campaign.
Misconception
Linked indicators do not equal confirmed attacker attribution; they indicate a relationship to investigate.
