Process Flow

Ingestion pipeline

  1. Connect feeders: APIs, TAXII, CSV, SIEM/EDR exports.
  2. Validate and timestamp incoming payloads for auditing.
  3. Deduplicate and archive raw inputs for traceability.
  4. Queue normalized records for enrichment and correlation.
Concrete example
An API feed, a passive DNS CSV and a SIEM export are validated and queued together to preserve arrival order and provenance.
Common mistake
Turning on many feeds at once without vetting causes noise and alert fatigue.
