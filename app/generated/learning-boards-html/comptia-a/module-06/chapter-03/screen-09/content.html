Comparison

High score vs Low score

High score
Multiple recent sightings, reputable sources, correlation to known campaign, near-critical asset.
Low score
Single historical sighting, low-reputation source, no correlation to assets.
Key Takeaway
Scoring directs automation and analyst attention; tune it to your environment and document weights.
Scoring factors
  • Source reputation and vendor confidence
  • Recency and frequency of sightings
  • Correlation with other indicators or campaigns
  • Relevance to critical assets or business units
Common mistake
Using a static threshold for all assets instead of asset-aware scoring increases false positives or missed threats.
