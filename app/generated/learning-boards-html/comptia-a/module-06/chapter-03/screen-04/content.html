Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › TIP Definition
Core Concept

Threat Intelligence Platform

A centralized system that turns raw, disparate intelligence into contextualized, prioritized insights.

Ingest many sources and connectors
Normalize formats to a common schema
Enrich indicators with contextual data
Example
Upload a suspicious URL; the TIP enriches it with WHOIS and DNS history and links it to prior TTPs.
Key Takeaway
A TIP is an operational engine, not a passive feed store.
