Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › SOAR, Firewall, EDR
SOAR
Use TIP context to automate investigation steps, ticketing, and reversible remediation actions.
Example: automatic enrichment and playbook run on medium-score indicators for analyst review.
Firewall/NGFW
Push curated blocklists and ensure rules are reversible and time-limited to avoid business impact.
Example: high-score IPs are added to a time-bound blocklist with rollback capability.
EDR
Feed hashes and behavioral indicators for scanning, quarantining, and containment actions at endpoints.
Example: push YARA rules or file hashes from TIP to EDR for targeted hunts.
Best practice
Blend automated enforcement with review gates and maintain an audit trail to revert erroneous actions quickly.
