Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Financial Institution

Task mapping

SIEM
Push high-confidence phishing domains and C2 IPs as a lookup table for DNS and connection correlation.
NGFW
Push reversible, time-limited blocklists for confirmed malicious IPs to reduce attack surface.
SOAR
Automate enrichment, asset lookup, and EDR scans for medium-score indicators with human-in-the-loop gates.
EDR
Deploy hashes and behavior rules for endpoint scanning and quarantine actions tied to TIP confidence.

Design notes

  • Define intelligence requirements: phishing, credential theft, C2 infrastructure.
  • Map score bands to actions: high→block, medium→SOAR review, low→log.
  • Ensure reversible actions and maintain dissemination logs.
  • Train analysts on TIP provenance and scoring interpretation.
Key Takeaway
A clear mapping from score to action and incremental deployment reduces fraud and phishing risk without disrupting business operations.
