Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Quick Reference
Verify Backups
Ensure backups are immutable and pre-date compromise before restoring.
Rotate Credentials
Reset passwords and keys for any possibly compromised accounts before rejoin.
Assign Remediation Owners
Every recommended change should have an owner and a deadline to ensure completion.
Reminder
Good recovery restores service; great recovery prevents the same incident from happening again.
