Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Key Concepts
Eradication
Remove malware, persistence, and backdoors, and fix the root cause so threats cannot return.
Recovery
Restore from known-good backups or rebuild, then harden and validate before returning to production.
Post-Incident Activities
Lessons learned, incident reports, and control updates form the feedback loop for continuous improvement.
Summary
Eradicate completely, recover securely, and document lessons to drive measurable improvements and reduce recurrence.
