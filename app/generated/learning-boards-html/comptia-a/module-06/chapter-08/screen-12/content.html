Scenario
WEB_PROD_01 compromised via unpatched web app
Use the checklist below to create eradication and recovery steps, form lesson questions, and recommend control improvements.
Eradication & Recovery Steps (examples)
  • Snapshot system state, preserve logs and file hashes
  • Search for modified configs, other web shells, and scheduled tasks
  • Identify and remove unauthorized accounts and SSH keys
  • Validate backups and restore or rebuild, followed by hardening
  • Rotate service and admin credentials before rejoin
Lessons Learned Questions & Controls
Questions:
  • Was the vulnerability detected in a timely manner?
  • Were containment decisions and communications effective?
  • Are backups and logging adequate to prevent or detect this earlier?
Recommended controls:
  • Immutable offsite backups and verification processes
  • WAF tuning, additional IDS rules, and improved logging
  • Strengthened patch management with prioritized web-app fixes
Key Takeaway

Use this scenario as a repeatable template: document actions, verify cleans before restore, and convert findings into prioritized remediation and policy updates.
