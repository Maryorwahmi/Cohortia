Post-Incident
Conducting a lessons learned review

Assemble a timeline, capture impact metrics and costs, and evaluate the effectiveness of detection, containment, eradication, and recovery steps.

Key questions
  • Was detection timely and accurate?
  • Were containment and eradication effective and documented?
  • Are procedures and tools sufficient for similar incidents?
Incident report components
  • Executive summary and scope
  • Technical timeline and root cause analysis
  • Actions taken and evidence preserved
  • Recommendations, owners, deadlines, and estimated costs
Common mistake

Producing vague recommendations without assigned owners or deadlines prevents real improvement.
