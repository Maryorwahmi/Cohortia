Eradication, Recovery & Post-Incident
Understand how to remove threats, restore systems, and convert incidents into lasting improvements.

Eradication means removing all traces of an attacker and fixing the root cause so they cannot return. Recovery means restoring services using trusted sources and stronger configurations. Post-incident activities capture what happened and how to prevent it next time.

For an advanced practitioner, these phases demand precise forensic work, careful credential rotation, rapid but verified restoration, and a disciplined lessons learned process that produces actionable policy and control changes.

In this lesson
A practical walkthrough of removing threats and rebuilding systems, plus a structured post-incident review.
Eradication
Remove malware, persistence, and backdoors. Identify root cause.
Recovery
Restore from clean backups or rebuild and harden systems before rejoin.
Post-Incident
Document, review, update playbooks and controls; track costs and legal needs.
Continuous Improvement
Feed lessons into controls, policies, and training for better future detection and response.
