Quick Reference

Three short reminders to check when you build or revise your IR program.

Actionable Playbooks
Include verification and ownership for each step.
Tested Preparation
Run frequent tabletop and full-scale drills.
BC/DR Alignment
Coordinate cleanup and restore priorities with business needs.
Parting Thought

A plan that is practiced, focused, and integrated with business recovery will save time, money, and reputation when incidents occur.
