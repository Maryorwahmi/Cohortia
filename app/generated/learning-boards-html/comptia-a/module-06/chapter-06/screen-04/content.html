Module 06 › Chapter 6.6 › What is an IR Plan?
Core Concept

Incident Response Plan — Definition

A documented, repeatable approach that guides detection, analysis, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident learning.

Purpose
Reduce damage, preserve evidence, and speed safe restoration.
Common Components
  • Scope and objectives
  • Roles and escalation criteria
  • Playbooks and communications
Misconception
A plan alone is sufficient. Without exercises and updates, it will fail under real stress.
Concrete Example — Ransomware Playbook
# Ransomware playbook (excerpt)
1. Detect signs of encryption and isolate affected hosts.
2. Preserve memory and disk images for forensics.
3. Verify backups are intact and offline copies exist.
4. Notify legal and executive stakeholders using template.
5. Eradicate malware and validate system integrity before restore.
Key Takeaway

Plans must be actionable, specific to your environment, and exercised regularly to be effective.
