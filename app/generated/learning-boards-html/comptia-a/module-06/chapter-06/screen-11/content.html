Comparison

Bad vs Good Preparation

Bad: Generic Policy
No runbooks or verification steps; responders must improvise.
Good: Policy + Runbooks
Actionable steps and sign-off points, tested in drills.
Bad: Untested Backups
Assumes backups are available and intact during recovery.
Good: Immutable Verification
Air-gapped or immutable backups with documented restore checks during exercises.
Bad: Tools Unprepared
Purchased but not configured; inaccessible during incidents.
Good: Golden Images
Pre-configured forensic and analysis images validated during drills.
Action Steps
  • Create runbooks for prioritized incidents and schedule their validation
  • Verify backups are immutable and test restorations on a schedule
  • Include legal and PR in policy drafting and exercise planning
  • Maintain golden images and verify licensing/access in exercises
Key Takeaway

Avoid theoretical plans. Make small, testable improvements and validate them frequently.
