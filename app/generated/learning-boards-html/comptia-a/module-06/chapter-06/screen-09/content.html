Process Flow

Designing Effective Exercises

Tabletop Exercises
Discussion-based scenarios to validate decisions, contacts, and escalation steps without technical execution.
Full-Scale Simulations
Operational tests that exercise tools, containment actions, and recovery procedures under time pressure.
After-Action Reporting
Document findings, assign owners for fixes, and schedule follow-up validation exercises.
Exercise Checklist
  • Realistic scenario tied to mission-critical assets
  • Clear objectives and success criteria
  • Observers to capture gaps and decisions
  • Actionable after-action report with owners
Pitfall

Running exercises without objective metrics or follow-up actions wastes time. Always track remediation progress.
