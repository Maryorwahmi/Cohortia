Learning Objectives

Each objective links a skill to a practical outcome you can apply immediately.

Bloom
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Design a Comprehensive IR Plan

You will explain policies, escalation points, and documented procedures so responses are consistent and auditable. This matters because clarity reduces error when stress is high.

Mnemonic: Map → Author → Test
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe the NIST IR Lifecycle

You will recount Preparation, Detection & Analysis, Containment/Eradication/Recovery, and Post-Incident Activity and why each phase matters. Practically, this helps prioritize resources and tests.

Mnemonic: Prep → Find → Fix → Learn
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Define IRT Roles and Responsibilities

You will identify core roles—technical lead, forensics, legal, PR, and management—and describe their primary contributions during incidents and why cross-functional membership prevents single-point failure.

Mnemonic: Right People, Right Time
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Plan Preparation Activities

You will outline tool purchases, secure configurations, training schedules, and tabletop exercises so the team can execute under pressure. This reduces friction and restores services faster.

Mnemonic: Tools • Train • Test
