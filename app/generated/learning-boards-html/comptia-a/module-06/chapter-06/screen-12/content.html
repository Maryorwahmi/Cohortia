Architecture

Aligning Security Cleanup with Recovery Priorities

Shared Objectives
Both teams aim to restore mission-essential services while preventing reinfection or data loss.
Decision Inputs
IR supplies forensic validation and contamination risk; BC/DR supplies recovery priorities and alternate infrastructure plans.
Validation Steps
Define checks to confirm restored systems are clean before rejoining production.
Practical Example
If a critical customer database is affected, IR validates backups and malware absence, while BC/DR ramps up an isolated recovery environment. Both teams coordinate cutover timing to balance availability and safety.
Key Takeaway

Treat IR and BC/DR as coordinated functions with shared exercises and documented validation steps to avoid recovery mistakes.
