Key Concepts

Three compact cards to anchor your learning.

Incident Response Plan
A living, actionable document that standardizes response and preserves evidence.
NIST Lifecycle
Preparation; Detection & Analysis; Contain/Eradicate/Recover; Post-Incident Activity.
Cross-Functional IRT
Include technical, legal, PR, and executive roles and integrate IR with BC/DR for safe recovery.
Final Takeaway

Invest early in preparation, test often, and coordinate recovery so incidents are contained, restored safely, and learned from.
