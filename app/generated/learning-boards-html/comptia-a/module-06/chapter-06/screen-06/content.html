Module 06 › Chapter 6.6 › Preparation Activities
Process

Preparation Phase Checklist

Policy & Procedures
Define authority, escalation criteria, and which incident types trigger which playbooks.
IRT Formation
Document roles, contact methods, and decision rights for technical, legal, and PR team members.
Tool Acquisition & Config
Pre-configured EDR, SIEM, forensic images, and secure comms reduce setup time in an incident.
Training, Playbooks & Backups
Training & Exercises
Run tabletop and red/blue drills to discover assumptions and gaps before real incidents.
Playbooks & Runbooks
Actionable checklists for specific scenarios standardize responses under pressure.
Backups
Store backups immutable or air-gapped and document verification steps for restoration.
Common Mistake

Purchasing tools without deployment plans or expecting staff to configure them during a crisis undermines response speed.
