Execution & Resilience Objectives

A plan is only as good as its practice. These objectives target the actions that prove readiness.

Assessment
Validated milestones: playbook test, communication drill, BC/DR alignment
05
Create Actionable Playbooks

You will author step sequences that are specific to your environment so responders avoid guesswork. Playbooks accelerate containment and preserve evidence integrity.

Mnemonic: Step • Verify • Record
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06
Design Communication Plans

You will define who must be notified and the information each party needs immediately to act, reducing delays and legal exposure.

Mnemonic: Who • What • When
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Plan Tabletop Exercises

You will schedule and run simulations that surface gaps in contacts, tools, and assumptions before a real incident occurs.

Mnemonic: Simulate • Observe • Improve
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Integrate IR with BC/DR

You will align incident priorities with business recovery objectives so cleanup and restoration decisions support continuity goals.

Mnemonic: Secure • Restore • Validate
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Use these objectives as a checklist while you design your IRT structure and communication plans in the hands-on activity.
