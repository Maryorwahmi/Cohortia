Definition

Incident Response Team — Core Roles

Technical Lead
Makes containment decisions and coordinates technical response steps.
Forensic Analyst
Preserves evidence, performs imaging, and supports root cause analysis.
Legal Counsel
Advises on notification laws, evidence handling, and regulatory exposure.
Other Core Roles
Public Relations
Manages external messaging and stakeholder communications to protect reputation.
Executive Sponsor / Incident Commander
Authorizes cross-department actions and allocates resources rapidly.
Support Roles
HR for insider issues, business owners for prioritization, and procurement for tool access when needed.
Key Takeaway

Cross-functional teams reduce blind spots and ensure decisions balance technical, legal, and business needs.
