Scenario

Design an IRT & Communication Plan

Step 1 — Core IRT Roles (5–7)
  • Technical Lead — containment and technical coordination (IT / Security)
  • Forensic Analyst — preserve and analyze evidence (Security Operations)
  • Legal Counsel — regulatory and notification guidance (Legal)
  • Public Relations — external messaging and reputation (Communications)
  • Incident Commander — resource allocation and executive decisions (Executive)
  • Product Owner — business impact and prioritization (Product)
Step 2 — Internal Communication Plan
Notify CISO/Incident Commander, Legal, and Product Owner immediately. Provide an initial summary: what was detected, affected systems, confidence level, and immediate containment actions. Explain why: the incident commander allocates resources, legal assesses notification requirements, and product owners prioritize recovery.
Step 3 — External Communication Plan
Notify regulators when required and prepare a customer notification if data exposure is confirmed. Initial external info should include confirmed facts, mitigations in place, and next steps. Legal will approve language to meet compliance obligations.
Practical Templates
Provide a short incident summary template and a verification checklist: evidence preserved, backups checked, communications logged, and next exercise date scheduled.
Key Takeaway

Design practical roles and notifications, then validate them in a tabletop to ensure contacts, tools, and backups are actually available.
