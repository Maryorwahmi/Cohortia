Process Flow

NIST Incident Response Lifecycle

1. Preparation
Policies, tools, training, IRT formation, and playbooks ready in advance.
2. Detection & Analysis
Identify incidents, analyze scope and severity, and collect initial evidence.
3. Contain, Eradicate, Recover
Limit spread, remove the cause, and restore services from validated sources.
4. Post-Incident Activity
Perform lessons learned, update playbooks, and improve detection rules.
Concrete Example
In a simulated malware outbreak, an EDR alert triggers analysis, containment isolates affected hosts, backups are validated, and post-incident updates address the detection gap.
Common Contrast
Containment is immediate limitation of impact; recovery restores services deliberately after ensuring systems are clean.
Why Preparation Matters

Preparation reduces detection time, ensures tools are available during containment, and makes post-incident learning actionable. Well-prepared teams contain incidents faster and make more defensible legal choices.

Key Takeaway

Each lifecycle phase depends on pre-planned choices made during Preparation. Invest there first.
