Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Triage — Quick, Accurate Assessment
Core Concept
Rapidly determine incident validity and priority
Triage yields a fast, evidence-backed decision that directs containment and forensics.
Checklist: validate timestamps, source credibility, corroborate with EDR/network, estimate impact.
Pitfall: mislabeling severity due to incomplete context.
Scenario example
Alert: POS server outbound to known skimmer IP. Triage actions: verify flow logs, pull EDR process tree, check transaction integrity, and identify criticality of the host.
Documentation: record what was checked, initial severity, and the justification for containment actions.
Key Takeaway: A structured checklist speeds triage and reduces misclassification.
