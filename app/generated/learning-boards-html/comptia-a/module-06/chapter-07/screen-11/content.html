Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Severity Assessment and the CIA Triad
Definition
Use CIA to determine impact and priority
Translate technical findings into business risk using confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
Confidentiality: was sensitive data exposed or exfiltrated?
Integrity: was data or system state modified maliciously?
Availability: are critical services degraded or unavailable?
Concrete mapping
Example: POS server contacting a skimmer IP is high confidentiality risk. If transactions are failing, availability also becomes critical. Severity drives whether you isolate the host immediately or perform a more measured containment.
Document the evidence used to assign severity so decisions are auditable.
Key Takeaway: Assess incidents across CIA to prioritize response and communications correctly.
