Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Containment Strategies
Short-Term Containment
Network segmentation: isolate subnets or hosts via firewall/VLAN changes.
Host isolation: disconnect a compromised host entirely; effective but disruptive.
Service shutdown & account disablement: stop vectors quickly with less network change.
Long-Term Containment
Actions to prevent re-emergence and strengthen detection.
Re-architecting segments, deploying additional EDR coverage, and improving logging retention.
Pros and cons: long-term steps reduce recurrence but require planning and downtime windows.
Key Takeaway: Combine short-term measures that stop spread with long-term fixes to prevent re-entry.
