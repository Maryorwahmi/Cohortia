Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Key Concepts — Short Reminders
Corroborate
Match SIEM, EDR, and network data before escalating.
Preserve evidence
Capture memory, PCAPs, and images before disruptive steps when possible.
Balance containment
Prefer reversible, documented steps that reduce impact and prevent re-entry.
Final takeaway:
A methodical, evidence-first approach reduces mistakes and ensures both containment and useful analysis after an incident.
