Incident Detection, Analysis, and Containment
Understand where alerts come from, how to investigate them, and how to limit damage without losing evidence.
Why this matters

Detection reduces dwell time. Analysis focuses effort on the true incident. Containment prevents spread while you gather facts for a complete remediation.
