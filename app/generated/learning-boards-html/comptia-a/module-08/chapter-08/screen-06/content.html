Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Compliance Frameworks
NIST CSF
Voluntary guidance with five functions to organize cybersecurity programs and prioritize activities.
ISO/IEC 27001
International standard for an auditable ISMS; used for certification to show implemented governance.
GDPR / HIPAA / PCI DSS
Legal and industry controls: GDPR for EU data privacy, HIPAA for protected health information, PCI DSS for payment card data.
Practical use
Map your controls to framework requirements and collect evidence that the control operates over time. Use frameworks together to cover governance, operational controls, and legal obligations.
