Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › POAM
POAM Components
  • Finding description and risk impact
  • Assigned owner and responsible team
  • Target completion dates and milestones
  • Verification steps and evidence references
Example entry
Unpatched web server: apply security patch, owner: web-ops, target: 10 days, verification: patch scan and evidence file, status updates logged weekly.
Why auditors care
A POAM demonstrates that findings were acknowledged, prioritized, and tracked to completion, turning audit output into program action.
