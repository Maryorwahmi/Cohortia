Security Audit — Definition & Purpose
A structured review to compare actual control implementation against requirements, and to produce verifiable evidence.
Scope the review: systems, controls, timeframe.
Collect evidence: configs, logs, policies, interview notes.
Evaluate controls and report findings with risk ratings.
Concrete example
Verify SSH configuration and password policy: gather /etc/ssh/sshd_config and /etc/login.defs, compare values to baseline, and capture outputs as evidence.
Common misconception
Passing an audit snapshot does not mean continuous security. Audits are periodic checks; controls must be maintained and verified over time.
Key Takeaway

Audits produce traceable evidence that controls exist and operate, and they should drive remediation and continuous improvement.
