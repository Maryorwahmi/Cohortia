Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Key Concepts
Security Audit
Systematic evaluation of controls, producing evidence for improvement and compliance.
Compliance Testing
Verification that requirements from frameworks or law are implemented and evidenced.
Frameworks
NIST, ISO, GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS provide structure for controls and legal obligations.
Summary

Use frameworks to guide controls, collect reproducible evidence, and treat audits as ongoing programs that produce measurable remediation.
