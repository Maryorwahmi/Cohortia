Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Internal vs External
Internal Audit
  • Performed by internal teams for continuous improvement
  • Flexible scope and higher cadence
  • Focus: operational efficiency and policy adherence
Example
Quarterly configuration scans and control effectiveness checks by the security team.
External Audit
  • Conducted by independent third parties for objective assessment
  • Used for certifications and regulatory proof
  • Deliverable: formal audit report and possible certification
Example
Annual ISO 27001 certification assessment or HIPAA compliance review by a qualified assessor.
Note: Use internal audits to prepare and remediate before external assessment so findings are minimized and evidence is organized.
