Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Logs & Audit Trails
What to collect
  • OS and application logs with username and source IP
  • Network device logs and firewall events
  • Security tool events and SIEM alerts
Retention & integrity
Ensure retention meets policy and that logs are time-synchronized and protected from tampering to remain valid audit evidence.
Example validation
Confirm failed admin login entries include username and source IP and that the retention window covers the period required by policy.
Common mistake
Assuming logs exist everywhere. Validate central collection, correlation rules, and alert tuning before an audit arrives.
