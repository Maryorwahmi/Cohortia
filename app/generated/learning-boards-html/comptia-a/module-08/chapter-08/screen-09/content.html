Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Automation vs Manual
Manual Checks
  • Readable and simple for one-off fixes
  • Prone to human error and inconsistency
  • Hard to scale and to provide continuous evidence
Automated Management
  • Enforces desired state across many systems
  • Produces execution logs and history for audits
  • Enables periodic scanning and continuous compliance reporting
For auditors, combine automation logs, policy definitions, and evidence snapshots to demonstrate not just a single fix but ongoing control operation.
