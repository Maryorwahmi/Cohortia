Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Managing Findings
Triage & Prioritization
Classify findings by risk and compliance impact; escalate critical items for immediate remediation.
Ownership & Tracking
Assign an owner with clear milestones and weekly status updates recorded in the POAM.
Verification
Require verification steps and evidence prior to closing a finding, such as rescans or log exports showing the fix.
Key Takeaway

Treat audit findings as prioritized projects: owner, milestone, verification, and documented evidence until closure.
