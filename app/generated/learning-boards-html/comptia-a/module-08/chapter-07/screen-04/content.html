Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Pen test vs Red Teaming
Penetration Testing
Scope-based technical assessment
  • Often white-box or grey-box with specific targets and timeboxed tests.
  • Focuses on vulnerabilities, exploitability, and remediation steps.
  • Tools: scanners, exploit frameworks; reports list findings with severity.
Example
Scan a web app, exploit SQL injection, and provide a patch recommendation.
Red Teaming
Goal-oriented adversary emulation
  • Black-box style operations that simulate real adversaries with objectives.
  • Chains multiple TTPs: social engineering, lateral movement, persistence, exfiltration.
  • Measures defender detection, response, and containment, not just vulnerabilities.
Example
Spearphish to gain credentials, move laterally to a data store, and exfiltrate files—reporting on what was detected and how quickly.
Common Mistake
Running noisy scans in a red team reduces realism and undermines measurement of detection capability.
Pro Tip
Define high-level objectives and rules of engagement early to align red and blue expectations.
Key Takeaway
Shift reports from vulnerability lists to resilience metrics tied to adversary objectives.
