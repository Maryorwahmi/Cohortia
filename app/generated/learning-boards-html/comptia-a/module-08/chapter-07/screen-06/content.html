Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Mapping TTPs: A Short Walkthrough
Process
Stepwise mapping
  1. Decompose the operation into clear phases and actions.
  2. Select the ATT&CK technique that best matches observable behaviour.
  3. Record timestamps, evidence, and telemetry sources for each mapped technique.
  4. Share mappings with blue team to tune detections and playbooks.
Common Mistake
Over-mapping to multiple techniques creates noise; prefer the best-fit technique and note alternatives.

Key Takeaway

Present concise mappings with telemetry links so defenders can validate and tune detections quickly.
