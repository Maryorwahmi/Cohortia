Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Operational Security (OPSEC)
OPSEC Checklist
Core measures to protect an engagement and maintain realism.
  • Use dedicated C2 servers, domains, and VMs separate from client infrastructure.
  • Mimic legitimate traffic using Malleable C2 rather than raw, anomalous patterns.
  • Avoid noisy scanning; choose stealthier discovery methods when possible.
  • Prefer in-memory or reflective techniques to reduce disk forensic artefacts.
Operational Notes
Coordinate with blue-team leads on response thresholds and safe containment steps to prevent accidental operational disruption.
Pitfall
Excessive secrecy that prevents the blue team from safely handling incidents can cause business impact; define transparency boundaries in advance.
Key Takeaway

OPSEC is a balance: protect realism while giving enough context and safe handling guidance to defenders.
