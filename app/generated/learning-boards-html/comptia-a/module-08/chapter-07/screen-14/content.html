Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Key Concepts
Red Teaming
Goal-oriented simulation of sophisticated adversaries to test full security posture.
MITRE ATT&CK
A knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques used to plan, describe, and measure emulations.
C2 & OPSEC
Frameworks and operational security practices that enable stealthy, realistic adversary emulation.
Summary Takeaway

Use ATT&CK to plan and report, protect realism with OPSEC, and convert red-team findings into defensive improvements through purple teaming.
