Red Teaming and Adversary Emulation
Simulate determined attackers to measure people, processes, and technology.
Orientation

This chapter teaches why red teams operate with objectives, how ATT&CK guides realistic emulation, how C2 frameworks enable persistence, and why OPSEC and purple teaming are essential for learning.

Why it matters

A successful red team exercise reveals gaps that scans miss — social engineering failures, detection blind spots, and process weaknesses that allow adversaries to persist.

How ideas connect
Red Teaming
Goal-oriented simulation of adversaries to test resilience across people, processes, and technology.
MITRE ATT&CK
A matrix of observed adversary TTPs used to plan, describe, and measure emulation exercises.
C2 Frameworks
Infrastructure and agents used to manage compromised hosts and maintain persistence during emulation.
OPSEC & Purple Teaming
Operational security to protect engagements and collaborative debriefs to turn findings into detection improvements.
