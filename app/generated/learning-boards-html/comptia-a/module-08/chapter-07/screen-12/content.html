Module 8 › Chapter 8.7 › Purple Teaming
Cycle
Share, Validate, Improve
  1. Red team shares mapped ATT&CK steps with evidence and expected telemetry.
  2. Blue team demonstrates which detections fired and which telemetry captured the activity.
  3. Teams agree on detection rules, telemetry to add, and playbook updates; then retest.
Deliverables
  • Detection rule mappings to ATT&CK IDs.
  • Telemetry enhancement list and instrumentation tasks.
  • Updated incident response playbooks with validated steps.
Key Takeaway

Make purple-team outputs specific, measurable, and tied to ATT&CK techniques so improvements are trackable.
