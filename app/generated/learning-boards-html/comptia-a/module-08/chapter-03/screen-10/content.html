Process

Seven phases: plan to report

Use these phases to plan legal, thorough, and repeatable tests.

1. Pre-engagement
Authorize scope, RoE, and legal terms to avoid misunderstandings and liability.
2. Intelligence Gathering
Collect OSINT and active scan data to map attack surface.
3. Threat Modeling
Prioritize likely attack paths given business context.
4. Vulnerability Analysis
Use scanners and manual checks to list specific issues.
5. Exploitation
Attempt to exploit selected findings to show real impact.
6. Post-Exploitation
Maintain access, escalate privileges, and pivot as allowed to reach objectives.
7. Reporting
Translate technical evidence into prioritized, actionable remediation and business impact statements for stakeholders.
Key Takeaway

Following PTES reduces legal risk, ensures test completeness, and makes results useful to technical teams and managers alike.
