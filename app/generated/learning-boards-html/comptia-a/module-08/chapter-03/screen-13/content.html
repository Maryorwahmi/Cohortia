Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Reporting
Definition

From evidence to action

Reports must be understandable by technical teams and business leaders to drive remediation and risk reduction.

Executive summary
High-level impact and prioritized recommendations for decision makers.
Technical details
Reproducible steps, module choices, payloads, and evidence for remediation verification.
Prioritization guidance
Combine exploitability, impact, and remediation effort to help teams prioritize patches and compensating controls.
Key Takeaway

A test is only useful if its findings are presented as clear, prioritized actions that reduce real business risk.
