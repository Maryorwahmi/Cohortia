Module 8 › Chapter 8.3 › Ethics & Legal
Definition

Rules of Engagement

A formal RoE document defines scope, authorized techniques, targets, timing, and emergency procedures to keep tests lawful and safe.

Common legal error
Testing without written authorization or exceeding agreed scope can be treated as illegal activity.
Practical control
Confirm IP ranges and maintain an emergency communication channel in case an exploit causes instability.
Checklist
  • Written permission from asset owner with explicit dates and scope.
  • Clear statement about allowed activities (social engineering, DoS, data exfiltration).
  • Lab testing for exploitation when production risk is high.
  • Emergency contact and rollback plan.
Key Takeaway

Authorization and clear RoE are the minimum ethical and legal safeguards before conducting any exploitation activity.
