Layered Mitigations
Awareness + Technical + Physical
Awareness training
Continuous, role-specific training and non-punitive reporting channels.
Technical controls
Email filtering, MFA, endpoint detection and response, and logging for analysis.
Physical controls
Access gates, mantraps, CCTV, secure shredding, and visitor vetting.
From test to remediation
Map each finding to a control layer, assign ownership, and set timelines so fixes are measurable.
Final takeaway
A layered approach reduces overall risk; no single control is sufficient against social engineering.
