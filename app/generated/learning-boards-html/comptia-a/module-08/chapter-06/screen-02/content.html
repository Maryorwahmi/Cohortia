Learning Objectives
What you will be able to do and why it matters
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify social engineering tactics
List and recognize phishing, spear phishing, whaling, baiting, pretexting, vishing, and tailgating and why they work.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain the human attack surface
Describe cognitive biases, trust, authority, and stress as vulnerabilities and their implications for architecture and policy.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design ethical simulations
Set written scope, notify stakeholders, ensure harmless payloads, and plan debriefs that teach, not punish.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Assess physical security gaps
Recognize weak locks, surveillance blind spots, unsecured devices, dumpster risks, and visitor management issues.
