Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Interaction Tactics
Pretexting
Fabricated story to elicit information or action.
Defense: Always verify identity through an independent channel before sharing sensitive information.
Example: Caller claims to be IT and asks for credentials to 'fix an urgent issue'.
Baiting / Quid pro quo / Vishing
Offer, exchange, or voice-based persuasion to obtain access or data.
Defenses: No plugging unknown USBs, follow formal service processes, verify phone requests using directory numbers.
Baiting example: USB dropped in parking lot labeled 'Payroll Data'.
Quid pro quo example: 'I'll fix your computer if you tell me your password.'
