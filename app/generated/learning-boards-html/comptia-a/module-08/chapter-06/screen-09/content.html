Conceptual phishing email (safe template)
This example is for authorized simulations only; it logs clicks and does not collect real credentials.
# Subject: Urgent: Your Password Expires Today - Action Required!
# From: IT Support <support@yourcompany.com> (spoofed)
# To: [Employee Name]

Dear [Employee Name],

Your Cohortia account password is set to expire today. To avoid service interruption and ensure continued access to company resources, please update your password immediately by clicking the link below:

[Simulated Link to a harmless landing page that logs clicks]

Failure to update your password within 24 hours will result in account lockout.

Thank you,
Cohortia IT Security Team
Call to action
Clicking the link should log an event and redirect to training content.
IoCs to teach
Generic greeting, mismatched domain, spoofed sender. Use these in the debrief.
