Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Human Element
Cognitive biases
Authority, urgency, scarcity, reciprocity — common levers attackers use.
Example: An employee receives a 'manager' request to approve an expense immediately. Under time pressure they skip verification and comply.
Mitigation: A single-line verification script employees can follow in under 15 seconds.
Mitigation: A non-punitive reporting channel for suspected phishing.
Design changes that help
  • Auto-verified processes for sensitive actions.
  • Role-based scenarios in training with real examples.
  • Visible cues for high-risk messages, like banners or warnings.
Key Takeaway

Reduce cognitive load and give employees simple, trusted verification steps so attackers cannot rely on rushed decisions.
