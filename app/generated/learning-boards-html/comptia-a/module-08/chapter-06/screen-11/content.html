Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Physical Pen Testing
Typical tests
  • Tailgating into buildings
  • Inspecting CCTV coverage and blind spots
  • Checking badge reader robustness and mantrap efficacy
  • Searching for unsecured laptops, documents, or trash with sensitive data
Example scenario
A tester observes an unlocked server room door and leaves a harmless validation device that logs network presence. The device is recovered after authorization and used as evidence of a control gap. The remediation plan includes a lock upgrade, CCTV repositioning, and staff reminders about locking doors.
Key Takeaway
Physical vulnerabilities often precede or enable cyber incidents; test findings should map to concrete fixes and ownership.
