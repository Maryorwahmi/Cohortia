Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Ethical Simulations
Simulation process
Plan → Authorize → Execute → Debrief
Plan
Define theme, targets, and harmless payload.
Authorize
Get explicit written approval and notify HR and Legal.
Execute
Monitor live, abort on unexpected harm, log outcomes securely.
Debrief
Aggregate results, train, and avoid punitive actions.
Include a clear reporting mechanism and immediate resources for employees who inadvertently engaged with the simulated content.
