Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Phishing Types
Phishing
Mass, low-specificity messages aimed at many recipients.
Mitigations: email filtering, user training, link analysis, and MFA to limit credential misuse.
Example: Bulk email claiming account issue with a generic greeting.
Misconception: If only a few people click, the risk is low. In reality, any click can seed later attacks.
Spear phishing & Whaling
Highly targeted emails that use personal context; whaling targets executives.
Mitigations: role-based training, verification protocols for sensitive actions, monitoring for anomalous requests.
Example: Email referencing a specific project and inviting a click to a fake document.
Takeaway: High-value targets need stricter out-of-band checks and policies for authorization.
