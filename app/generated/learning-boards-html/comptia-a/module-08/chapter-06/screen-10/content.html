Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Safety & Legal
Authorization essentials
  • Explicit written approval and rules of engagement
  • Stakeholder notification: HR, Legal, C-suite
  • Defined scope and excluded systems
Physical test safeguards
  • Testers carry authorization letters identifying them as security personnel
  • Abort criteria and safe recovery plans
  • Avoid scenarios that risk injury, panic, or property damage
Pro tip

Coordinate a dry run with stakeholders when possible and always have a clear chain of custody for any recovered devices.
