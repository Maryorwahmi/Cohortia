Module 08 › Chapter 8.6 › Physical Vulnerabilities
Common vulnerabilities
Weak access controls
Easily bypassed locks, propped doors, ineffective badge readers.
Surveillance gaps
Blind spots, unplugged cameras, unmonitored feeds.
Unsecured equipment
Laptops left unlocked, servers in unlocked racks.
Dumpster diving
Sensitive documents or media thrown away without shredding.
Visitor management
Inadequate vetting or escorting of visitors.
Perimeter issues
Climbable fences, broken gates, poor lighting.
Simple remediation steps
  • Fix locks and gates
  • Repair or reposition cameras
  • Enforce secure disposal and device policies
Key Takeaway
Prioritize fixes by impact; small physical controls often prevent larger cyber incidents.
