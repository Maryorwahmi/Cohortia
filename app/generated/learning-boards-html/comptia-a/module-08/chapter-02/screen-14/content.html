Nessus / OpenVAS
Plugin-based scanners that require tuned policies and credential configuration for accurate results.
CI/CD Integration
Scan images and dependencies in build pipelines to block critical vulnerabilities before deployment.
False Positives
Common in unauthenticated scans or default policies; verify with credentials and exposure data before remediation.
Final Takeaway
Use the right tool, scan early in pipelines, validate findings, and apply least-privilege credentials for safe, effective scanning.
