Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Interpreting Scan Reports
Step-by-step verification
  1. Identify informational plugins and mark them for context.
  2. Cross-check authenticated evidence or service versions.
  3. Assess exposure: is the service reachable from attacker vantage points?
  4. Prioritize based on exploitability, exposure, and asset criticality.
Common Mistake
Blindly trusting unverified scan output leads to wasted effort and missed true risks.
Concrete Example
In a Nessus excerpt, an ETag header disclosure is low priority and informational; an outdated Apache version listed as critical with known public CVEs and public exposure should be remediated first. Use authenticated checks to confirm patch level before scheduling emergency updates.
Key Takeaway

Use evidence and exposure to rank findings. Validate alerts to reduce false positives and focus remediation where it matters most.
