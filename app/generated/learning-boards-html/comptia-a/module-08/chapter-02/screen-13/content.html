Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Key Concepts — Part 1
Authenticated Scan
Performs checks with valid credentials to inspect installed software and configurations.
Unauthenticated Scan
Simulates external attackers and maps exposed services and ports without credentials.
Network Scan
Targets operating systems and network services to identify CVEs, weak protocols, and default credentials.
Summary
Combine these scan types to reduce blind spots: network and unauthenticated scans show exposure, authenticated scans confirm internal risk.
