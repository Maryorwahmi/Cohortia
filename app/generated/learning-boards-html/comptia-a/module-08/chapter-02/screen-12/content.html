Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Safety & Operational Notes
Authorization
Obtain explicit permission in writing for any scans, especially against production environments, to avoid legal and operational risk.
Scheduling
Run intrusive scans during off-peak windows and gradually increase scan intensity during a pilot phase to monitor impact.
Credential Handling
Use least-privilege accounts for scanners, store credentials securely, and audit their use frequently.
Testing
Validate scan policies in staging before running them in production to avoid surprises and system disruption.
Rollback Plan
Prepare a rollback and monitoring plan in case a scan unexpectedly affects service behavior or performance.
Common Misconception
Believing broader credentials are safer; they actually increase risk if the scanner or secrets are compromised.
Key Takeaway

Authorize, scope, test, schedule, and secure. These operational steps keep scanning effective and safe.
