Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Key Concepts
Security Assessment Report
Document findings and provide separate executive and technical sections to enable decisions and fixes.
CVSS
A standardized technical severity score that must be adjusted by asset importance and threat context to set priorities.
Remediation & POAM
Use POAM-style tracking with owners, milestones, and verification to ensure fixes are implemented and validated.
Summary
Make findings actionable: communicate appropriately, prioritize with risk context, and verify remediation to sustain improvement.
