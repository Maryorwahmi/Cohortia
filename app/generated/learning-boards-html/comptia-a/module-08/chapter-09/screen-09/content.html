Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Compensating Controls
Definition

Temporary mitigations while planning permanent fixes

Use compensating controls to reduce exposure when immediate remediation is impossible, and document timelines and ownership.

Examples
  • WAF rules blocking exploit payloads
  • Network segmentation isolating vulnerable hosts
  • Access restrictions and increased monitoring
Key Takeaway

Compensating controls must be tracked with owners and removal dates; they are temporary bridges, not endpoints.

Scenario
Legacy application cannot be immediately patched. Implement segmentation and strict ACLs, add targeted WAF rules, and schedule deprecation or vendor coordination. Record the compensating controls in the POAM with owner and removal date.
Common Mistake
Allowing compensating controls to remain indefinitely without a plan for permanent remediation increases long-term risk and operational debt.
