Applied Objectives

Skills focused on coordination, tracking, and measurable improvement.

05
Coordinate Vulnerability Disclosure
Notify vendors privately, provide reproducible details, and coordinate timelines for public disclosure after fixes are available.
Mnemonic: disclose responsibly
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06
Use POAM for Tracking
Assign owners, set milestones, and include verification steps to demonstrate that remediation is complete.
Mnemonic: assign, track, verify
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Choose Compensating Controls
When immediate fixes are impossible, apply alternative controls like segmentation or a WAF while tracking permanent remediation.
Mnemonic: mitigate now, fix later
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Define KPIs and Metrics
Track metrics such as time to patch critical vulnerabilities and the count of unpatched critical systems to show progress to leadership.
Mnemonic: measure what matters
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
