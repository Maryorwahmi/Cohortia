Comparison

CVSS, Asset Criticality, and Threat Intelligence

Treat prioritization as a three-factor decision and document the rationale for remediation order in your POAM or ticketing system.

High CVSS + High Criticality + Active Exploitation
Immediate remediation and increased monitoring.
High CVSS + Low Criticality + No Exploitation
Schedule remediation based on risk appetite; consider containment.
Key Takeaway

Document decisions using all three factors so remediation reduces the most meaningful risk first.

Concrete Scenario
Compare two RCEs with identical CVSS 9.8 scores. The public-facing portal hosting payment data is prioritized because of asset criticality and exposure; the isolated Jenkins server is deprioritized while monitored and isolated.
Common Mistake
Assigning priority solely on CVSS causes teams to spend time on low-impact systems instead of protecting customer-facing assets.
Check
If two vulnerabilities are equal in CVSS, what other questions will you ask? Consider exposure, data sensitivity, and whether exploit code exists.
