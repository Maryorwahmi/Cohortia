Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Monitoring & Feedback
Process

Monitor, measure, and feed lessons back into change

Use SIEM, EDR, and cloud security posture tools to detect issues and feed that intelligence back to operations, development, and policy owners.

Key KPIs
  • Average time to patch critical vulnerabilities
  • Number of unpatched critical systems
  • Mean time to detect exploitation
Key Takeaway

Make monitoring actionable by assigning owners to KPIs and feeding findings into development and training cycles.

Real-world connection
After discovering repeated misconfigurations, teams automated checks in CI/CD and added a policy gate that prevents deployment until configuration checks pass. This reduced drift and the number of repeated findings.
Common Mistake
Publishing KPIs without assigning owners or actions results in dashboards that don't change behavior; metrics must drive decisions.
