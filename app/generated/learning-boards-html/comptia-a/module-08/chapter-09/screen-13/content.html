Assessment Idea

Practical tasks that reflect real decisions

Ask learners to prioritize vulnerabilities, draft an executive summary for the board, and create POAM entries including verification steps and evidence requirements.
Sample question
A critical legacy app cannot be patched. What compensating controls do you apply immediately, and what long-term strategy do you propose? Expected answer: network isolation, WAF, access controls, monitoring, POAM entry, and a plan for replacement or vendor coordination.
Key Takeaway

Assessments should validate both technical steps and the business rationale behind prioritization and remediation choices.

Rubric hints
  • Clarity of executive summary
  • Completeness of technical remediation steps
  • Evidence and verification criteria in POAM
