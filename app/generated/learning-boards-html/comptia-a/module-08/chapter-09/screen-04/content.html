Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Assessment Reports
Core Concept

Tailor reports for decision-makers and implementers

Reports must both inform leaders about risk and give engineers the evidence and steps they need to remediate effectively.

Executive Summary
Top risks, business impact, recommended investments, and concise timelines.
Technical Appendix
CVE identifiers, PoC steps, affected hosts, remediation commands, and evidence.
Example Scenario
Provide the CISO with a one-page table showing top three risks and potential business impact. Provide developers with exact remediation steps and verification commands to close the issue.
Key Takeaway

Design reports to answer decisions and enable fixes: one clear summary, one detailed appendix.

Common Mistake
Mixing technical detail into the executive summary or omitting evidence from the technical appendix reduces actionability for both audiences.
Checklist for a good report
  • One-page executive summary with top risks and business impact.
  • Technical appendix: CVEs, PoC, affected assets, remediation steps, and evidence.
  • Clear risk ratings and recommended next steps with owners and timelines.
Check your understanding
If a report lists a critical CVE on a dev server, what should the executive summary emphasize? Answer: the business impact and whether customer data could be affected, not the technical exploit steps.
