Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Key Concepts
Vulnerability Management Lifecycle
A continuous process that reduces risk through repeatable stages.
Asset Inventory
Automated, multi-source inventories are required to bring assets under management.
Contextual Prioritization
CVSS + exposure, criticality, exploitability, and controls determine true priority.
Summary takeaway

Make the lifecycle repeatable, ensure assets are discovered, and prioritize with context to turn findings into defensible action.
