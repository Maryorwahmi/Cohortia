Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Vulnerability Management Lifecycle
Process overview
A recurring loop that turns discovery into risk reduction and audit evidence.
Supporting bullets
  • Discovery must be frequent and include cloud APIs and endpoints.
  • Use scanners to identify known weaknesses, but validate findings.
  • Prioritize using CVSS plus asset context and exploitability.
  • Verification closes the loop and avoids recurring findings.
Key Takeaway

Treat vulnerability management as an operational program with measurable steps and ownership, not a one-off technical task.

Example
Weekly cloud inventory finds a new VM; an authenticated scan reports missing patches; the team schedules a patch and re-scans to verify closure.
Common mistake
Treating all high severities the same without checking exposure and business impact.
Pro tip
Automate inventory and authenticated scanning where possible to reduce false positives and speed verification.
