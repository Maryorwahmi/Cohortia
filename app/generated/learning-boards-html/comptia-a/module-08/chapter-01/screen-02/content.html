Learning Objectives
Skills to apply: lifecycle design, assessment selection, policy drafting, and risk integration.
01
Design the Lifecycle
Explain and document the continuous stages: discovery, identification, prioritization, remediation, verification, and monitoring. This matters because a defined lifecycle turns scans into repeatable, auditable work.
Mnemonic: circle of stages
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Choose Assessment Types
Differentiate authenticated, unauthenticated, host, network, and cloud inventory methods and choose the right mix for coverage and risk. Knowing when to authenticate reduces false positives and finds deeper issues.
Mnemonic: map scan type → depth
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Draft Policies & SOPs
Create policy statements and SOPs that set scope, timelines, reporting, and escalation so remediation happens consistently and on schedule.
Mnemonic: policy = scope + timeline + owners
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Integrate with Risk & Roles
Translate technical findings into risk register entries and map responsibilities so vulnerabilities are prioritized by business impact and remediated by the right teams.
Mnemonic: triage by context
