Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Verification
Verification methods
Use matching scan types and targeted tests to confirm closures.
Re-scan
Run the same or a deeper scan to confirm vulnerability no longer appears.
Manual testing
Targeted checks or exploit verification where appropriate and safe.
Evidence capture
Keep pre/post outputs for audit trails and to demonstrate remediation effectiveness.
Operational rules
Treat verification as a required closing step for high-severity findings.
Common mistake
Closing tickets without documented verification leads to recurring vulnerabilities in reports.
Example
After patching a service, run the authenticated scan again and attach both before and after evidence to the remediation ticket.
Check your understanding
Why might a re-scan still show the same vulnerability and how would you respond? Consider rollout issues, caching, or failed patches.
