Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Roles & Responsibilities
Typical role hierarchy
Define who owns the program, who performs technical actions, and who accepts business risk.
Vulnerability Manager
Oversees the program, defines policy, reports metrics, and coordinates stakeholders.
Security Analysts
Perform scans, analyze results, and recommend prioritization and mitigations.
System Owners / Developers
Implement fixes, provide testing, and confirm remediation.
Operational practices
Ensure ownership is recorded on each finding and integrate responsibilities into ticketing systems.
Common mistake
Failing to assign an owner or assuming a team will act without explicit assignment leads to prolonged risk exposure.
Example
Attach remediation owners to tickets and require verification evidence before closure to ensure accountability.
Check your understanding
How would you change ownership if remediation requires a third-party vendor? Consider escalation and verification responsibilities.
