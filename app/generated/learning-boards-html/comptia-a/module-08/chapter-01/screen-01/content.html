Foundations of Vulnerability Management Programs
A practical, continuous lifecycle to reduce attack surface and prioritize fixes where they matter most.
In this lesson
Understand the lifecycle, policy essentials, tooling choices, and team roles required for an effective program.
Why it matters
A structured program turns scan output into prioritized action and audit evidence instead of noisy, untrusted reports.
