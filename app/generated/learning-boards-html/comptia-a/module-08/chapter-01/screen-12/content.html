Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Risk & Compliance Integration
What to send to risk
Deliver prioritized findings with asset context and remediation status.
  • Scanner finding identifier and description.
  • CVSS score and contextual adjustments.
  • Asset criticality, owner, and exposure details.
  • Remediation plan, owner, and verification evidence.
Compliance evidence
Auditors expect documented policy, scan reports, remediation records, and verification outputs.
Example
Provide weekly scan summaries and remediation logs when demonstrating compliance to frameworks like ISO or NIST.
Common mistake
Sharing raw scan output without contextual metadata makes it hard for risk owners and auditors to assess business impact.
Key Takeaway
Structure vulnerability data so risk and compliance stakeholders can make decisions and evidence due diligence to auditors.
