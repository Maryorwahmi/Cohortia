Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Monitoring & Continuous Improvement
What to measure
Metrics that reflect program health and identify recurring problems.
  • Mean time to remediate (MTTR) by severity.
  • Scan coverage and inventory reconciliation rates.
  • Count of outstanding critical/high findings over time.
  • False positive rate and scanner tuning effectiveness.
Example
If a class of misconfiguration recurs, create a remediation playbook or change the deployment pipeline to prevent reintroduction.
Governance & reviews
Periodic reviews and stakeholder alignment keep the program funded and effective.
Operational rule
Assign owners for each metric and require program reviews that include security, operations, and risk stakeholders.
Pro tip
Turn recurring vulnerabilities into systemic fixes by updating CI/CD pipelines, hardening templates, and SOPs.
