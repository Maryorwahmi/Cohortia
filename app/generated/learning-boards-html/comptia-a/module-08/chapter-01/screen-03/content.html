Common misconception
Scanner severity alone does not equal business risk. Use CVSS as a baseline, then apply context like exposure, criticality, and controls.
Misprioritization risk
Fixing every high-severity item first can waste effort if those systems are isolated or noncritical. Prioritize by impact and exploitability.
Verification matters
Always re-scan or test after remediation to confirm the issue is closed and nothing new was introduced.
