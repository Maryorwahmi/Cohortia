Practical outcomes and safety
How to gather evidence and avoid collateral impact
Device work
Use emulators or dedicated test devices. Never extract data from personal phones.
Cloud work
Run checks in test accounts, avoid destructive changes, and keep provider policies in mind.
Reporting
Provide reproducible steps, impact, and an actionable remediation recommendation.
Safety reminder

Authorization and isolation reduce legal and operational risk. Document approvals before exercises and use non-production environments whenever possible.
