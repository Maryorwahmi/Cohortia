Module 08 › Chapter 8.5 › IAM Misconfigurations
Why IAM matters
Access control defines what principals can do. Excess permissions escalate risk quickly.
Risk: broad roles
Roles with wildcard actions allow unintended operations across services.
Detection
Use Prowler or ScoutSuite to list policies, then validate manually.
Remediation
Apply least-privilege, require MFA, rotate keys, and remove unused roles.
Concrete scenario
EC2 role with s3:*
This allows attached workloads to perform any S3 action. Attackers who compromise the instance can exfiltrate or delete buckets.
Key takeaway

Treat role permissions as the primary control. Narrow policies and require best practices for keys and MFA.
