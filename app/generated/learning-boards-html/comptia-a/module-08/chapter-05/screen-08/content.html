Safe testing practices
Protect users, maintain legal compliance, and enable reproducible remediations.
Authorization
Get written permission that covers the target, scope, and allowed techniques.
Isolation
Use emulators, test accounts, and dedicated devices with no personal data.
Non-destructive first
Collect evidence without changing production state, then escalate in a controlled lab.
Documentation
Log commands, device state, and artifacts so fixes are reproducible.
Warning

Unauthorized reverse engineering or tests against production accounts may violate law or provider policy. Always confirm scope and provider rules.
