Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Remediation & Safety
Effective Remediations
  • SQLi → Parameterized queries and least-privilege DB accounts.
  • XSS → Context-aware output encoding; CSP for additional protection.
  • Broken Access Control → Server-side object-level authorization checks.
Safe Testing Practices
  • Prefer non-production environments and read-only probes.
  • Avoid payloads that cause data loss or account lockouts.
  • Coordinate with owners for scheduled tests requiring production access.
