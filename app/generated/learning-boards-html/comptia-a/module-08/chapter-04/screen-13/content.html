Module 8 › Chapter 8.4 › Scanner vs Manual
Automated Scanner
  • Fast coverage across many endpoints.
  • Identifies common patterns and misconfigurations.
  • Good for baseline scans and regression checks.
Manual Techniques
  • Human-guided logic testing, chaining steps, and proof-of-concept crafting.
  • Use Repeater and Intruder to manipulate requests and automate controlled sequences.
  • Essential for business-logic vulnerabilities and complex flows.
Key Takeaway

Combine automated tools for breadth with manual testing for depth to produce actionable findings.
