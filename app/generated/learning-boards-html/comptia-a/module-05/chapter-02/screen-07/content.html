Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › AES-GCM in TLS
Why TLS prefers AEAD
AEAD reduces the risk of incorrect MAC/encryption ordering and gives a single primitive to audit and configure.
Operational guideline
Require AES-GCM suites where possible and restrict legacy CBC suites to controlled exceptions with compensating controls.
Concrete example
A policy item: permit TLS_AES_256_GCM_SHA384 and disallow TLS_RSA_WITH_AES_128_CBC_SHA unless specific legacy compatibility is required and documented.
Pro tip
Test client support during a staged rollout to avoid unexpected outages when deprecating legacy ciphers.
