Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › AEAD / AES-GCM
What AEAD does
Provides confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity in one operation, reducing risk from incorrect composition of primitives.
Associated Data (AD)
Include headers or metadata in the integrity check without encrypting them, preventing undetected tampering.
Key Takeaway
Use AEAD to combine encryption and authentication and to protect associated metadata in a single, well-tested primitive.
Concrete example
A TLS cipher suite using AES-GCM ensures payload confidentiality while its tag and associated data protect headers and handshake integrity.
Misconception
Don't assume any encryption mode gives integrity. AEAD is specifically designed to do both safely.
