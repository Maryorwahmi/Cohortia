Initialization Vector (IV)
Random or pseudorandom value that ensures distinct ciphertexts for identical plaintexts under the same key.
Modes of Operation
Describe how block ciphers handle multi-block data; choice affects security properties and integrity requirements.
CSPRNG
Cryptographically secure generator required for keys and IVs to prevent predictability and compromise.
