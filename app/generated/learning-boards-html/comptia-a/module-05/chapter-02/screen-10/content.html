Distribution strategies
Use asymmetric channels or HSM-backed APIs to distribute symmetric keys. Avoid transmitting raw keys in logs or unprotected channels.
Storage and access control
Prefer hardware-backed storage, enforce least privilege, and maintain audit trails for key access and use.
