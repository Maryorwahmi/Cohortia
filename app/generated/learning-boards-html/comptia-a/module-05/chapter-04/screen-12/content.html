Key Concepts
Three compact concepts to remember
Two-tier / Three-tier
Models that separate the offline Root CA from online Issuing CAs to limit risk.
Active Directory Certificate Services
Microsoft's PKI integration for automating certificate issuance in Windows domains.
Certificate Template
A policy blueprint specifying key usage, SANs, and validity for issued certificates.
Summary

Map these concepts onto your environment: choose a hierarchy, plan templates, and use AD CS or equivalent to automate securely.
