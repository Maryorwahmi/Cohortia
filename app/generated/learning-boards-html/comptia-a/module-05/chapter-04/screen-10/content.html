Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Hardware Security Modules
Definition
What an HSM does
Generates, stores, and performs cryptographic operations inside a tamper-resistant hardware boundary so private keys never leave the device.
Key protection
Keys are non-exportable and used only for operations inside the HSM.
Operational design
Plan for redundancy, maintenance, and secure access control to HSMs.
Compliance
HSMs help meet regulatory FIPS levels and provide stronger assurance for CA keys.
Concrete scenario
Place Root and issuing CA private keys in HSMs. For online issuing CAs, ensure HSM access is automated and auditable so signing operations continue during normal operation without exposing keys.
Common mistake
Treating an HSM as a silver bullet and neglecting physical security, access controls, or logging. HSMs must be combined with good operational controls.
Key Takeaway

HSMs significantly reduce key exposure risk. Integrate them with CA software, define physical and access controls, and plan for redundancy and audits.
