Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Two-tier vs Three-tier
Comparison
Two-tier model
Offline Root CA signs online Issuing CA(s). Simpler to operate, smaller certificate chain overhead.
Security
Root key kept offline and protected, reducing catastrophic compromise risk.
Operations
Fewer CA certificates to manage, simpler CRL and AIA configuration.
Recovery
Compromise of an issuing CA is recoverable by rebuilding and reissuing under the root.
Comparison
Three-tier model
Adds a middle intermediate layer to separate functions: internal servers, users, devices. Offers stronger compartmentalization.
Segmentation
Limit blast radius by assigning function-specific intermediates.
Complexity
More certificates to manage and more CRL/AIA endpoints to publish and monitor.
Control
Fine-grained policy and template assignment by CA role.
Key Takeaway

Choose the simplest hierarchy that achieves required segmentation. Keep the root offline and documented signing procedures in a secure runbook.
