Secure Key Exchange and Management
Establishing secrets, protecting keys, rotating safely, and planning recovery.
This lesson explains Diffie-Hellman and ECDH for shared secret derivation, covers the role of KDFs and Perfect Forward Secrecy, compares storage options including HSMs and TPMs, and outlines rotation and escrow choices used in enterprise architectures.

Lifecycle
Rotation, archival, and cautious escrow reduce risk and preserve recoverability.
