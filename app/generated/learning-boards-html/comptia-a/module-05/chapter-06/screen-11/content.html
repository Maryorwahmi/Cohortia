Escrow risks
Escrow creates a centralized target; protect it with strong separation, encryption, and auditability.
Multi-party control
Require multiple approvals and custodians to recover a key.
Split keys
Divide key into shares held by distinct custodians; reassemble only with quorum.
Strict audit
Log all recovery operations and review logs regularly for anomalies.
Consider alternatives such as robust application-level recovery, backups with access governance, or delegated access models before choosing escrow. If you must escrow, limit the keys, define retention windows, and require legal or multi-stakeholder authorization for recovery events.
Scenario
An enterprise needs to recover employee-encrypted files if an employee leaves. Escrowing data encryption keys under split custody allows recovery while avoiding a single point of failure. Document policies, log every access, and rotate escrow components periodically.
Common misconception
Escrow eliminates all risk. It does not — it shifts risk to the escrow mechanism, which must be hardened and controlled.
