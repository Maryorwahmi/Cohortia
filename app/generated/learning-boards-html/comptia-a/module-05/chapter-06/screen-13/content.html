Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Operational Key Management
Centralized KMS and HSM integration
Use KMS APIs to request operations; keep master material in HSMs and use KMS to orchestrate access and lifecycle events.
Automation
Automate rotation, staged rollovers, and rekey distribution to reduce manual errors.
Backups & Recovery
Encrypt backups and limit access; test recovery procedures periodically.
Audit & Monitoring
Log all key operations and review for anomalies; enforce separation of duties.
Testing and change control
Run periodic drills for rotation and recovery, stage changes in non-production, and validate that all dependent services accept new keys before retiring old ones.
Operational takeaway
Centralized policy, automation, and testing are the foundation of reliable key management and minimize risk from human error.
