Secure software updates

Choose the primitive and explain why it fits the distribution model.

Recommended primitive

Digital signatures — provide integrity, authenticity, and non-repudiation to many recipients without shared secrets.

Operational controls

Protect signing private keys in hardware modules and provision public keys via trusted configuration management to clients.

Audit focus

Verify signing key custody, revocation procedures, and whether the verification code accepts algorithm upgrades.

Key Takeaway

For wide distribution choose signatures and secure the entire signing pipeline; a signature is only as good as the key management surrounding it.
