Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Remember
Hashes = Integrity
Good for detecting changes; not for proving origin.
MACs = Shared-secret auth
Use for efficient, point-to-point authenticity when a secret can be protected.
Signatures = Public verification
Provide integrity, authenticity, and non-repudiation at scale when public keys are trusted.
Final action: inventory weak hashes, protect signing keys, and implement algorithm agility so your systems remain secure as standards evolve.
