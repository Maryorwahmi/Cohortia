Design Consideration

Trust models: hierarchical, mesh, and bridge

Choose a trust model that balances centralized control, federation needs, and incident coordination.

Hierarchical

Single root CA signs intermediates which issue end certificates. Easier governance and clear trust path.

Mesh (Cross-certification)

CAs cross-certify each other to form mutual trust—useful for federations but operationally complex.

Bridge

A central bridge CA links separate hierarchies. Good for policy mapping but requires careful coordination.

Which to choose?
Choose hierarchical for internal enterprise PKI to simplify governance.
Use bridge models when integrating distinct organizational PKIs with clear policy mapping.
Prefer mesh only when mutual cross-domain trust and heavy coordination are acceptable.
