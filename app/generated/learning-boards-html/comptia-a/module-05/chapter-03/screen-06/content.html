Process Flow

Registration Authority (RA) and enrollment workflow

A procedural chain that validates identity and prepares CSRs for CA signing.

Enrollment Steps
1
Key generation & CSR: Subscriber generates a key pair and submits a CSR containing identifying fields.
2
Identity proofing: RA verifies domain ownership or identity documents according to policy.
3
Policy checks: RA ensures requested Key Usage and EKU align with allowed templates.
4
Forward to CA: Approved CSRs are sent to the CA for signing; logs are stored for audit.
Practical Controls & Pitfalls
  • Keep detailed audit logs for each enrollment decision to support incident analysis.
  • Enforce MFA and second-factor checks for high-sensitivity certificate enrollments.
  • Avoid letting the CA handle enrollment and signing on the same machine to reduce compromise risk.
Analogy: The RA is like passport control that verifies identity before a passport (certificate) is issued by the government (CA).
