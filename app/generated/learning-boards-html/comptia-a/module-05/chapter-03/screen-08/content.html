CRL vs OCSP — revocation mechanisms

Understand the operational trade-offs between batch revocation lists and real-time queries.

Certificate Revocation List (CRL)
  • Signed list of revoked certificate serial numbers
  • Published periodically; clients download and check locally
  • Simple but can be stale between publications
Good for offline environments and bulk validation.
Online Certificate Status Protocol (OCSP)
  • Real-time query for specific certificate status
  • OCSP stapling reduces client latency by letting servers present a recent response
  • Requires responsive, secure OCSP responders and operational SLAs
Preferred when timely revocation is crucial.
Practical Guidance
Use OCSP stapling for TLS to reduce latency and dependency on client queries.
Keep CRLs published for environments that require offline checks or as a backup.
Ensure responder availability; a failure can create blocking checks or unsafe fallbacks.
