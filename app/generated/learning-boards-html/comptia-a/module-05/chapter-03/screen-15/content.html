What to Remember

Key Concepts — Part 1

Certificate Authority (CA)
Trusted issuer that signs certificates and anchors trust chains.
X.509 Certificate
Contains Subject, Issuer, public key, validity, SAN, Key Usage and EKU.
Revocation (CRL & OCSP)
CRLs and OCSP communicate revoked status; OCSP is timelier while CRLs provide bulk compatibility.
Summary takeaway

Protect trust anchors, verify certificate details, and ensure revocation and renewal are operationalized to reduce risk.
