Key Concepts — Part 2

A short checklist and common pitfalls to spot during inspections and design reviews.

SAN Validation
Confirm SAN includes all hostnames to prevent name-mismatch errors.
Scoped Intermediates
Segment issuance by purpose to reduce the blast radius of compromises.
Revocation Availability
Ensure CRL and OCSP endpoints are published, signed, and redundant to communicate revocations reliably.
Final takeaway

A secure PKI blends well-defined policies, protected keys, and robust revocation and renewal operations. Use the checklist to evaluate and improve your PKI posture.
