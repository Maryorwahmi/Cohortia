Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) Fundamentals

Understand how certificates, CAs, revocation, and repositories work together to enable secure TLS, email, and code signing across an enterprise.

PKI at a glance
Root CA
Highest trust anchor. Often kept offline to limit risk.
Intermediates & RAs
Issue end certificates or verify requests on behalf of the CA.
Revocation / Status
CRL and OCSP provide revocation information relied on by clients.
