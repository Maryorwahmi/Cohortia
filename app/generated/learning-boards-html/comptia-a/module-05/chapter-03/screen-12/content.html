Revocation operations and response playbook

A step-by-step incident playbook ensures compromised certificates are revoked and replaced effectively.

Immediate Steps
  1. Confirm compromise and scope.
  2. Identify all services using the certificate.
  3. Issue replacements with new keys and install quickly.
  4. Revoke the compromised certificate and publish CRL/OCSP updates.
Operational Advice
  • Automate distribution of replacement certificates to minimize downtime.
  • Ensure OCSP responders and CRL repositories are updated and reachable.
  • Run post-incident reviews to tighten key protection, RA checks, and template restrictions.
Key Takeaway

Speed and thoroughness in revocation and replacement limit attacker windows and restore trust.
