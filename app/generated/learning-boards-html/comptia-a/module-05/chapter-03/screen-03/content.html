Practical Competencies

These objectives are measurable tasks you will perform during an audit or incident response.

Inspect a Live Certificate

Retrieve and parse a TLS certificate chain and report Subject, Issuer, Validity, SAN, Key Usage and EKU.

Compare Revocation Channels

Explain CRL vs OCSP, timeliness trade-offs, and when to require OCSP stapling for latency-sensitive services.

Design Trust Placement

Argue for offline root CA and intermediate issuance model that balances security and operational agility.

Manage Certificate Lifecycles

Define renewal windows, private key protections, and revocation procedures to minimize outages and compromise windows.
