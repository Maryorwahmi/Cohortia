Definition

Repository and publication

Repositories publish certificates and revocation data so relying parties can retrieve and validate them.

What a repository holds
  • Issued certificates and chains
  • CRLs with timestamps and signatures
  • Access information such as CRL Distribution Points and OCSP responder URLs
Best Practice
Provide redundant, authenticated endpoints and automate publication to avoid stale or missing revocation data.
Operational Checks
  1. Verify CRLs are signed and timestamped by the issuing CA.
  2. Confirm CRL Distribution Points in certificates match published repositories.
  3. Monitor repository availability and integrity checks to detect tampering.
Key Takeaway

A trustworthy repository ensures clients can fetch certificate chains and revocation data reliably and securely.
