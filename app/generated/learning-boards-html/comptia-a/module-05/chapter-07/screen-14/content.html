Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Key Concepts
Brute-Force
Exhaustive guessing; solve with large keys and key-stretching.
Side-Channel
Physical leakage like timing or power; requires implementation hardening.
Birthday Attack
Collision search costs ~2^(N/2); prefer large hash outputs.
Downgrade & HSTS
Prevent forced fallback by disabling weak protocols and enabling HSTS.
Cryptographic Agility
Make algorithms configurable and replaceable for rapid migration.
Post-Quantum
Plan migration for asymmetric primitives; inventory long-lived secrets now.
Summary Takeaway

Effective cryptographic defense combines correct algorithm selection, secure implementation, rigorous configuration, and architectural agility to respond when the threat landscape changes.
