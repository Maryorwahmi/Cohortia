Practical Skills

Checklist for assessments and hardening

TLS & Cipher Audits
  • Identify weak TLS versions and remove them.
  • Ensure cipher suites provide AE and PFS.
Side-Channel Testing
  • Measure timing variance and power traces on implementations.
  • Recommend constant-time or masked algorithms as needed.
Key & Password Hardening
  • Verify sufficient key sizes and apply key-stretching for passwords.
  • Audit entropy sources and key lifecycle management.
Inventory & PQC Planning
  • Catalog long-lived secrets and plan migration priorities.
  • Design cryptographic agility into procurement and development.
