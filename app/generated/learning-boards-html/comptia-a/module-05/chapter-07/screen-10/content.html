Definition

Post-quantum algorithms and planning

PQC aims to provide primitives that remain secure even if attackers have access to large quantum computers. The primary challenge is migrating complex ecosystems that rely on asymmetric cryptography.

Planning actions
  • Inventory asymmetric dependencies and long-lived secrets.
  • Prioritize migration for archives, certificates, and key-exchange endpoints.
  • Test hybrid schemes and maintain agility for algorithm replacement.
Concrete Example

Data that must remain confidential for decades — such as intellectual property or sensitive archives — may be at risk if adversaries harvest encrypted data today for future quantum decryption. Start by identifying these assets and planning replacements or hybrid protections now.

Why this matters
Proactive migration reduces future emergency rework and preserves long-term confidentiality.
