Concept Map

Primitives and Relationships

Symmetric
Fast bulk encryption. Example: AES for databases and traffic.
Asymmetric
Secure key distribution. Example: RSA or ECC for handshakes.
Hashing
One-way integrity checks; use SHA-256 or SHA-3.
Key Stretching & PFS
PBKDF2 and ephemeral key exchange protect passwords and session history.
Use this map to choose the right primitive: confidentiality, integrity, and long-term safety are separate goals that often require combining techniques.
Key takeaway
Cryptographic choices are architectural decisions: match speed and scale needs with secure key management and enable PFS for sensitive environments.
