Warning › Operational Risks

Common misconfigurations

  • Low PBKDF2 or bcrypt iterations that are easy to brute-force.
  • Server TLS suites that do not prefer ephemeral key exchange.
  • Storing unsalted or un-stretched password hashes.
Remediation steps
Enforce iteration baselines, update TLS configs to prefer ECDHE, and require unique salts and stretching for credentials.
Pro tip

Include cryptographic configuration checks in CI/CD and security audits to catch regressions early.

Checklist for audits

TLS cipher review
Ensure cipher suites support PFS and prefer ECDHE over static key exchange options.
Credential storage
Verify unique salts and adequate iteration counts for password hashing algorithms.
Key lifecycle
Document generation, storage, rotation, and compromise procedures for long-term keys.
