Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Symmetric Encryption
A single secret key encrypts and decrypts data; use for high-throughput bulk encryption.
Asymmetric Encryption
Public and private keys solve secure key exchange and provide authentication for handshakes.
Cryptographic Hashing
One-way digests confirm integrity and support signatures; combine with salt for password protection.
Key Stretching
PBKDF2 and bcrypt make password cracking computationally expensive by using salt and many iterations.
Perfect Forward Secrecy
Ephemeral session keys prevent past sessions from being decrypted after long-term key compromise.
Hybrid Cryptography
Combine asymmetric key exchange with symmetric bulk encryption for both secure distribution and performance.
These concepts form the foundation for secure enterprise communications and credential storage. Use them together to meet confidentiality, integrity, and resilience requirements.
