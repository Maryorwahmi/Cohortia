Chapter 5.1 › What to Remember
1. Right primitive
Match symmetric for speed and asymmetric for secure exchange; use hybrid designs for real protocols.
2. Salt and stretch
Store passwords with unique salts and an appropriate PBKDF2 or bcrypt work factor to deter offline attacks.
3. Enable PFS
Prefer ephemeral Diffie-Hellman exchanges so past sessions remain safe even after key compromise.
Applying these three reminders during design and configuration reviews will significantly reduce the risk of retroactive data exposure and credential compromise.
