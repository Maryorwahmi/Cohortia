From objective to architecture

Each learning goal is paired here with the typical architectural action an engineer would take during design or review.

Design decision: Symmetric for bulk
Use AES for databases and bulk TLS payloads to conserve CPU and reduce latency.
Configuration: Use asymmetric for key exchange
Use RSA or ECC to protect the exchange of a session key, then switch to a symmetric cipher for data.
Policy: Salt and stretch passwords
Mandate PBKDF2 or bcrypt with institutional iteration counts to defend against offline attacks.

Assessment readiness

Be ready to explain the security implications and trade-offs for compliance and operational needs.

Scenario
A messaging system needs confidentiality and integrity at scale. Recommend a hybrid approach: ECDHE for handshake, AES-GCM for bulk encryption, and salted, stretched credential storage.
Verification
You should be able to justify iteration counts, cipher suite choices, and why enabling PFS is necessary for recorded traffic protection.
