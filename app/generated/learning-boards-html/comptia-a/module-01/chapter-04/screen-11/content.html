Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › CSPM & CWPP
CSPM
Continuously monitor cloud accounts for misconfigurations and compliance violations. Use CSPM for guardrails and automated policy enforcement at the account and service level.
Examples
Identify open buckets, default encryption off, or overly permissive security groups across multiple accounts.
CWPP
Protect workloads with vulnerability scanning, runtime detection, and host-level telemetry. CWPP addresses exploitation and anomalous behavior inside workloads.
Examples
Detect a process spawning a reverse shell inside a container or a high-severity CVE on a VM image.
Integration Pattern
Use CSPM to maintain posture and prevent misconfigurations, and use CWPP to detect and block runtime attacks. Route findings from both into your SOC pipeline for prioritized remediation.
Key Takeaway

CSPM and CWPP are complementary: posture for configuration issues, workload protection for runtime threats. Use both to reduce risk across the cloud stack.
