Module 1 › Chapter 1.4 › Learning Objectives
CSPM & CWPP
Learn how CSPM continuously finds misconfigurations and policy violations, and how CWPP protects workloads at the host, container, or function level. Use CSPM for account-wide posture and CWPP for runtime defenses and vulnerability management.
CSPM
Continuous config and compliance checks across cloud accounts.
CWPP
Workload-focused runtime protection, vulnerability scanning and host-level telemetry.
IAM Controls
Practice least privilege, prefer roles over long-lived user keys, require MFA on privileged accounts, and use explicit deny and service control policies to enforce guardrails at scale.
Roles vs Users
Roles are assumed and yield temporary credentials; users often have longer-lived keys and require stricter audit.
Audit & Remediate
Regularly review policies and remove overly permissive statements to reduce blast radius.
