Core IGA capabilities
  • User provisioning and de‑provisioning
  • Access request and approval workflows
  • Access certifications and periodic review
  • Segregation of duties analysis
Operational example
A role change in AD triggers SCIM connectors to update SaaS entitlements; privileged access is included in quarterly certifications to validate necessity.
Key Takeaway
IGA ties lifecycle automation to regular review processes, preventing privilege creep and supporting compliance.
