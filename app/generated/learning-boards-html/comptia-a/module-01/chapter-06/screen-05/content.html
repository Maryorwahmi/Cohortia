Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Centralized vs Decentralized
Centralized IAM
  • Single source of truth and unified policy enforcement.
  • Simpler provisioning, consistent auditing, and easier compliance.
  • Requires high availability and robust integration layers.
Key Takeaway

Prefer centralization when policy consistency and audit evidence are priorities.

Decentralized IAM + Federation
  • Local control for apps and services needing autonomy or performance.
  • Federation validates identities across domains without duplicating accounts.
  • Requires attribute mapping, trust agreements, and monitoring to avoid drift.
Concrete example
A SaaS system keeps local profiles but accepts SAML assertions from the enterprise IdP for authentication and role claims.
Misconception
Federation removes the need for governance. In reality, it requires attribute policy and monitoring.
Pro tip
Document attribute mappings and apply least privilege to mapped roles to avoid over-permissive access after federation.
