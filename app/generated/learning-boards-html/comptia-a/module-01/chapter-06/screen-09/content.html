PAM components and flows
Vault, JIT access, session recording, and rotation form the operational pillars.
Credential vault
Securely store privileged secrets and issue on approved requests.
Just‑in‑time access
Grant time-limited elevation to reduce standing privileges.
Session recording
Monitor and record privileged sessions for audit and forensics.
Automated rotation
Rotate passwords and keys after use to prevent reuse by attackers.
Concrete scenario
An admin requests server root access through the PAM portal, receives a temporary credential, conducts work while the session is recorded, and the credential rotates when the session ends.
Common mistake
Ignoring service accounts. Include service accounts in vaulting and rotation to avoid persistent privileged tokens.
