Design process for MFA
Choose factors, apply controls, and design secure recovery and emergency procedures.
Factors
Hardware tokens, authenticator apps, biometrics, and one‑time passcodes.
Deployment points
IdP, VPN, PAM elevation, and sensitive SaaS operations.
Hardening
Avoid SMS for admins, secure recovery flows, and audit break‑glass usage.
Concrete scenario
Administrators use hardware keys and authenticator apps for sign‑on; general staff use authenticator push. Break‑glass accounts are offline, stored in a sealed vault and require multi‑person approval for use.
Common mistake
Making recovery too easy. Weak recovery undermines MFA strength; require documented, auditable steps.
