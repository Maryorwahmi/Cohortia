Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Weaknesses & Mitigations
Overly permissive policies
Mitigate with role mining, entitlement reviews, and least‑privilege mappings.
Lack of MFA
Enforce MFA for admin and external access; prefer stronger factors than SMS.
Unmanaged service accounts
Include service accounts in PAM and automated rotation to avoid persistent credentials.
Broken auth/authorization
Validate claims mapping and test authorization logic to prevent bypasses.
Shadow IT identities
Detect and onboard rogue accounts using inventory and enforce connector-based provisioning.
Key Takeaway
Prioritize audits and automation for privileged and long-lived credentials; those yield the biggest security gains.
