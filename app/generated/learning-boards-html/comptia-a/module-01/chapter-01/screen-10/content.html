Core architectural principles

Apply principles together to design resilient, practical architectures that reflect risk appetite.

Zero Trust

Continuous verification of identity, device posture, and context for every access request to reduce implicit trust.

Least Privilege

Grant the minimum permissions required and use just-in-time elevation to limit lateral movement.

Defense in Depth

Layer multiple, diverse controls so the failure of one control does not lead to systemic compromise.

Example

Protecting a database: use RBAC and least privilege, require MFA and device checks from Zero Trust, and enforce network segmentation and host hardening as defense in depth.

Key takeaway

Combine these patterns and tailor them to the organization’s risk appetite and operational capacity for practical security gains.
