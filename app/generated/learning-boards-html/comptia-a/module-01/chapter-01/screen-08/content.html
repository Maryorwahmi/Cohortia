NIST CSF Functions

Use the CSF to prioritize capabilities and communicate risk to non-technical stakeholders.

Identify

Assets, risks, governance

Protect

Controls, access, encryption

Detect

Monitoring, alerts

Respond

Incident response, containment

Recover

Restore services, lessons learned

Mapping example

Map encryption and access control to Protect, centralized logging to Detect, incident playbooks to Respond, and backup strategies to Recover. Use this mapping to show leadership which functions need investment to reduce prioritized risks.
