Risk management in architectural decisions

Assess assets, threats, and impacts, then map prioritized risks to architecture choices and budgets.

Steps
  1. Determine risk appetite with stakeholders
  2. Identify critical assets and threats
  3. Assess likelihood and impact; prioritize
  4. Map controls and metrics to reduce prioritized risk
Example

A payments team sets low tolerance for data loss. The architect prioritizes full-disk and database encryption, role-based access, and continuous monitoring. The metric: percent of sensitive data encrypted and number of unauthorized access attempts detected and blocked.

Misconception

Treating risk management as purely technical misses business tradeoffs. Always include business leaders in defining appetite and acceptable outcomes.

Key takeaway

Architecture succeeds when risk decisions are measurable, tied to business objectives, and reviewed as conditions change.
