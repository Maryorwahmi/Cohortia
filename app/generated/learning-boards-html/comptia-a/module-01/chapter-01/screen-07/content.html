SABSA: from business to operations

Use a matrix-based approach to derive security requirements from business attributes and carry them through design layers.

Layered traceability
  • Business attributes → Conceptual requirements
  • Logical and physical architectures → implementable controls
  • Operational architecture → runbooks and metrics
Example

A service-level business attribute for 99.99% uptime yields redundancy requirements at the component layer and monitoring and failover playbooks at the operational layer. Those requirements are then mapped to engineering tasks and test plans.

Misconception

SABSA is not merely documentation; it is a requirements discipline that ensures controls are defensible and traceable to business risk.

Key takeaway

Use SABSA to justify controls by business need, making design decisions auditable and aligned to organizational priorities.
