GlobalTech Innovations — hybrid analysis

Use a simple template to identify evident and missing principles and propose prioritized integrations.

Evident principles
  • Network segmentation on-prem
  • Role-based IAM roles for EC2 instances
  • Logging enabled through CloudTrail
Missing principles
  • Data classification and encryption at rest for cloud storage
  • Just-in-time privileged access and separation of duties
  • Consistent cross-environment policy enforcement
Integration suggestions
  • Implement data classification tags and enforce encryption-by-default for S3 with KMS
  • Introduce just-in-time access and logging for administrative accounts
  • Automate policy checks in CI/CD and centralize identity across cloud and on-prem
Key takeaway

Prioritize controls that reduce the highest business risk and can be automated for consistency across environments.

Analysis template
---
**GlobalTech Innovations - Hybrid Security Architecture Analysis**

1. Evident Principles:
    * Defense in Depth: perimeter firewall and zoning justify layered controls.
    * Segmentation: production, development, administrative zones reduce lateral spread.
    * Role-based access: IAM roles for EC2 reflect principle of least privilege for services.

2. Missing Principles & Integration Suggestions:
    * Data Classification:
        * Missing: No controls for classifying or protecting sensitive data in S3.
        * Suggestion: Implement automated classification tags and encryption-by-default via KMS and S3 bucket policies.
    * Privileged Access Control:
        * Missing: DBAs have full access without just-in-time controls.
        * Suggestion: Implement just-in-time privileged access and enforce separation of duties with audit logging.
    * Cross-environment Policy Enforcement:
        * Missing: Disparate controls between on-prem and cloud.
        * Suggestion: Centralize identity and enforce policies via CI/CD and policy-as-code for consistent enforcement.
