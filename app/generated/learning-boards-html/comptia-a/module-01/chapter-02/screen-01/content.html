Integrating Security into the Enterprise Architecture Lifecycle
Make security an architected, continuous capability from strategy through decommissioning.
Why this matters

Addressing security early is cheaper and more effective than retrofitting controls later. Architects reduce risk and support compliance by embedding security into decisions.

What you will practice

Threat modeling at multiple lifecycle stages, mapping controls to design decisions, and applying change management for continuous improvement.

Key Takeaway

Design decisions determine security outcomes. Built-in security saves time, cost, and risk across the lifecycle.
