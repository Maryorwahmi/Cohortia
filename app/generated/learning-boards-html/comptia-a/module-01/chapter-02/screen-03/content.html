Hands-on artifacts
Threat Model
A prioritized list of threats, maps to STRIDE categories, and suggested architectural countermeasures.
Security Diagram
Diagram annotated with trust boundaries, data flow, and control locations for verification.
Config Checklist
Concrete secure configuration items and expected settings to prevent drift during implementation.
Decommission Plan
Steps for sanitization, access revocation, and documentation required for compliance and audit trails.
Collaboration mnemonic
Use a simple RACI mapping: who reviews threat models, who implements controls, who operates monitoring, and who is accountable for decommissioning.
Key Takeaway

Skill work centers on producing reviewable artifacts and ensuring teams own the decisions across the lifecycle.
