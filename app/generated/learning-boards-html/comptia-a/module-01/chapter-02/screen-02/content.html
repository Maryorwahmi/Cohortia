Module Enterprise Architecture › Chapter Integrating Security › Learning Objectives
01
Explain early integration
Describe the business and cost rationale for shifting security left.
You will learn to present why addressing security during planning saves rework and aligns architecture with risk appetite. This matters when stakeholders evaluate project tradeoffs.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Describe threat modeling
Explain STRIDE and iterative modeling across stages.
You'll practice framing threats and countermeasures, and learn how models evolve from high-level to component-level analysis as design matures.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Identify phase activities
Map deliverables to planning, design, implementation, operations, and decommissioning.
You will catalogue specific controls and documents needed at each phase so design decisions translate into verifiable outputs such as specs, diagrams, and test plans.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Apply secure by design
Translate principles into designs and change processes for continuous improvement.
You'll practice recommending authentication, data protection, and configuration controls and learn how to embed reviews into change management and operations feedback loops.
Verified Competency Milestone

Each objective aligns to a milestone: knowledge, analysis, applied design, and ongoing operational mastery.
Key Takeaway

These objectives move you from explaining why security matters to producing secure design artifacts and sustaining them over time.
