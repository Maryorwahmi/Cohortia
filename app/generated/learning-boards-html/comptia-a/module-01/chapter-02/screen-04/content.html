Core Concept › Shift Left
What shift left means
Shift left embeds security into early lifecycle activities so architectural choices reflect desired risk posture and controls become integral rather than add-ons.
Example: Identity design
Choosing federation and token-based access in planning avoids storing credentials and simplifies compliance later.
Common mistake
Treating shift left as optional or as documentation only leads to superficial compliance without engineering changes.
Key Takeaway

Early security decisions reduce rework. Capture them as required deliverables to ensure follow-through.

Definition & analogy
Think of architecture like a house plan: adding a safe after construction is more expensive than designing a secure room from the start. Security shaped by the plan integrates plumbing, wiring, and load-bearing decisions correctly.
Practical check: list the three security choices that cannot be changed later without major rework and ensure they are approved during planning.
