STRIDE categories
Spoofing
Attacker impersonates an identity. Countermeasure: strong authentication and mTLS.
Tampering
Unauthorized modification of data. Countermeasure: integrity checks and signed payloads.
Repudiation
Inability to prove action provenance. Countermeasure: auditable logs and non-repudiation mechanisms.
Information Disclosure
Sensitive data exposure. Countermeasure: encryption at rest and in transit, least privilege.
Denial of Service
Service resource exhaustion. Countermeasure: rate limiting and resilience patterns.
Elevation of Privilege
Unauthorized privilege gains. Countermeasure: least privilege and role separation.
Practical prompt
Pick an API and run one STRIDE question for each category. Capture a single control recommendation per threat and rank them by risk and implementation effort.
Key Takeaway

STRIDE helps ensure comprehensive coverage of threat types; it guides control selection and documentation.
