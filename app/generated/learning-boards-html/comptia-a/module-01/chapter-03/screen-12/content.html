Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Common Mistakes
Skip baseline
Result: outages. Fix: monitor-only mode and collect flow logs first.
Overly permissive rules
Result: large attack surface. Fix: use least privilege and group-referencing rules.
Unsecured control plane
Result: controller compromise. Fix: strong auth, RBAC, and logging for SDN/NFV controllers.
Untuned prevention
Run IDS in detect-only, tune signatures, and then enable IPS or WAF prevention carefully.
No rollback plan
Always prepare rollback scripts and document maintenance windows for policy enforcement.
