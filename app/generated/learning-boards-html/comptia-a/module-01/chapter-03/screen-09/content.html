What is SDN?
Centralized control plane that programs forwarding behavior on data plane devices. Enables dynamic policy updates and automation.
What is NFV?
Virtualized network functions like firewalls and load balancers running on commodity servers for flexibility and scale.
Security implications
  • SDN controller is a critical asset; secure with strong authentication and audit logging.
  • Validate and sign VNF images and secure orchestration APIs.
  • Segment management and data planes to reduce exposure.
Misconception check: automation does not replace access controls. Treat controllers and orchestrators as high-value targets and protect them accordingly.
