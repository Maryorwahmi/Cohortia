Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › NGFW, WAF, IDS/IPS
Definitions
NGFW: Application-aware firewall with DPI and IPS capabilities.
WAF: Protects web apps from Layer 7 attacks like SQL injection.
IDS/IPS: IDS alerts on suspicious activity; IPS blocks or prevents it when safe.
Placement & tuning
Place NGFWs at perimeter and between trust zones. Put WAFs in front of web tiers. Deploy IDS sensors at east-west chokepoints. Begin in detect-only mode and tune to reduce false positives before enabling prevention.
Analogy: enforcement is like locks and detection is like motion sensors. Locks stop unauthorized access; sensors tell you when someone tried.
Common mistake
Deploying prevention without tuning causes outages. Always run detection first, validate alerts, and prepare rollback and escalation procedures before blocking traffic.
