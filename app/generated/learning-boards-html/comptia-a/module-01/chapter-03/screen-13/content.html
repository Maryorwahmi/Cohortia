Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Micro-segmentation
Isolate workloads with least-privilege network rules to limit lateral movement and shrink blast radius.
Cloud controls
VPC for isolation; Security Groups for instance-level stateful rules; NACLs for subnet-level stateless filters.
Connectivity & detection
Choose IPsec, SSL/TLS, or ZTNA appropriately and layer NGFW/WAF and IDS/IPS for enforcement and visibility.
Final takeaway

Start small, observe traffic, and iterate: a monitored rollout prevents outages and gives you confidence to enforce micro-segmentation and layered defenses.
