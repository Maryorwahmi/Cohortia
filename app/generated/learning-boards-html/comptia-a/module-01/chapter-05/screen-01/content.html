Data Security Architecture and Privacy Controls
Design policies and controls that protect data across its lifecycle and meet regulatory requirements.
In this lesson

You will learn to classify data, select encryption options for rest and transit, design key management, apply DLP and database protections, and integrate privacy-enhancing technologies to meet regulatory controls.

Why it matters

Poor classification or missing key rotation often leaves sensitive records exposed. Architects reduce risk by translating policy into enforceable controls and auditable processes.
