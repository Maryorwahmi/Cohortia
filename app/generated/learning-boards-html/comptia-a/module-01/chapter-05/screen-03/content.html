Assessment Artifacts
  • Data flow diagram: Annotate where classification, encryption, tokenization, DLP, and retention occur.
  • Encryption design: Specify at-rest and in-transit methods and the KMS/HSM strategy.
  • DLP & DB rules: Show detection patterns and response actions tied to classification.
  • Privacy mapping: Demonstrate how PETs and policies meet GDPR/HIPAA/CCPA controls.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Common Pitfalls
  • Overly complex classification that users ignore.
  • Encrypting data without key lifecycle controls.
  • Assuming tokenization removes all compliance obligations without proof.
  • Adding PETs without validating analytic utility or privacy budgets.
