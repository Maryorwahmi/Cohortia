PETs Quick Reference
Differential Privacy
Adds statistical noise to protect individuals while preserving overall trends. Track privacy budgets when publishing results.
Secure Multi-Party Computation (SMC)
Enables joint computation without sharing raw inputs. Use for collaborative analytics across organisations.
Federated Learning
Train models locally and aggregate updates centrally, keeping raw data on devices.
Concrete Example
A mobile keyboard app trains language models using federated learning to avoid collecting typed text. Aggregated model updates are combined centrally. For published trend statistics, differential privacy is applied before sharing to external analysts. If multiple hospitals need a shared statistic without sharing raw patient records, SMC enables the computation of the result while keeping inputs private.
Misconception
Believing PETs are plug-and-play. They require careful design, measurement of utility loss, and operational controls such as privacy budgets and secure aggregation.
