Definition & Scope

NSM collects network-derived telemetry to detect, analyze, and escalate suspicious activity. It focuses on communications between hosts, offering both immediate alerts and historical evidence for investigations.

  • Flow data: scalable summaries for pattern detection.
  • IDS/IPS alerts: signatures and behavioral detections.
  • Full packet capture: byte-level forensic detail.
Example & Misconception
Example: Flow records flag a sudden outbound spike from a database server. A targeted FPC reveals large POST payloads to an external host — evidence of exfiltration. Misconception: "More capture everywhere" is better. In fact, indiscriminate FPC creates storage and privacy problems and slows analysis.
Key Takeaway

Use layered telemetry: flow for scale, IDS/IPS for alerts, and targeted FPC for forensic detail.
