Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Flow vs Packet
NetFlow / IPFIX
Summarized records of conversations: source/destination, ports, protocol, bytes, and timestamps. Low storage cost, broad coverage, ideal for spotting spikes, scanning behavior, and unusual peers.
Full Packet Capture (FPC)
Records entire packet contents for forensic reconstruction. High storage cost, used for payload analysis and extracting evidence like uploaded files or C2 commands.
Example Workflow
Use flow collectors to find indicators like beaconing or large outbound transfers. When a host and time-window are identified, perform a targeted FPC to reconstruct payloads and confirm exfiltration. This preserves storage while enabling forensic proof when needed.
