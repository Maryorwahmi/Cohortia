Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › IDS vs IPS
Side-by-side
IDS (Passive)
Monitors and alerts. Best for visibility and investigation without disrupting traffic.
IPS (Active)
Blocks or drops traffic that matches rules. Best for immediate mitigation when rules are reliable.
Example & Best Practice
Example: IDS flags DNS tunneling; after investigation you create a high-confidence IPS rule blocking the specific C2 domain. Best practice: stage change, start in detection mode, tune thresholds, document whitelist and maintenance windows to avoid disrupting critical services.
Common Mistake

Deploying IPS without tuning leads to blocked services and disabled protections. Tune before enforcing.
