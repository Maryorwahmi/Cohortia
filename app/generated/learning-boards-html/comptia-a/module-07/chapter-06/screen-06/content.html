Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Identification
Triage steps
  1. Collect logs and alert details from SIEM and EDR.
  2. Correlate IoCs across endpoints, network, and identity providers.
  3. Interview affected users and inspect browser or proxy history.
  4. Classify severity and scope; escalate if lateral movement is suspected.
Pro tip
Use behavioral analytics and threat intelligence to reduce false positives and speed validation.

Common mistake
Escalating on every alert wastes time. Validate with context before invoking containment actions.
