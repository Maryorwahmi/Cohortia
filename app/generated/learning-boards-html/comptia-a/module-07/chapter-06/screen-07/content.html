Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Containment
Short-term containment
Rapid steps to halt spread: isolate endpoints, block IPs, force password resets, and quarantine emails.
Long-term containment
Controls that prevent recurrence: firewall reconfiguration, segmentation, and temporary access restrictions.
Practical guideline
Always capture volatile evidence and EDR telemetry before making irreversible changes to systems.
Scenario — Ransomware
Short-term: isolate affected hosts and block lateral spread. Long-term: reimage, patch vulnerable services, and restore from tested backups.
Common mistake
Immediately wiping or reimaging without capturing evidence may destroy critical forensic data and harm investigations.
