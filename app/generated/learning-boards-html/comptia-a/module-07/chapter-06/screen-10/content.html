Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Lessons Learned
Post-mortem agenda
  1. Reconstruct the timeline and actions taken.
  2. Analyze detection, containment, and eradication effectiveness.
  3. Identify policy, tooling, or training gaps.
  4. Assign remediation tasks with owners and deadlines.
Outcome examples
Updates might include new SIEM correlation rules, revised playbooks, additional training, or procurement of missing forensic tools.
Pro tip
Document decisions and timeline carefully so lessons are actionable and measurable over time.
Key Takeaway

A robust Lessons Learned process turns incidents into measurable improvements and reduces future impact.
