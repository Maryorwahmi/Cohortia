Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Legal & Regulatory
Reporting obligations
Identify applicable mandatory reporting rules early and keep legal counsel involved for notification timelines and privilege issues.
Cross-border constraints
Be aware that collecting logs or images across jurisdictions can trigger data transfer and privacy rules that affect how evidence is handled.
Evidence admissibility
Proper chain of custody and validated tools help ensure evidence is admissible in court or regulatory reviews.
Common mistake
Delaying legal involvement or failing to document preserved evidence can create legal and compliance exposure after the incident.
