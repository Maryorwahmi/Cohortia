Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Chain of Custody
What to record
  • Collector name, date/time, and method used.
  • Source device identifiers and asset tags.
  • Hash values and storage location of the copy.
  • Every transfer with purpose and recipient name.
Practical rule
Always take a verified image and compute a cryptographic hash before analysis; store the image securely and document every access.
Chain of custody example
Collector: T. Rivera
Date: 2026-06-01 10:20 UTC
Device: Laptop-42 (SN: 12345)
Action: Forensic image created with ImgTool v2.1
Hash: SHA256: e3b0c44... (stored)
Stored: Vault01/CaseA/Laptop42.img
Notes: User powered off; image taken from powered-down device.
Common mistake
Altering originals or failing to record handlers undermines admissibility and can derail investigations.
