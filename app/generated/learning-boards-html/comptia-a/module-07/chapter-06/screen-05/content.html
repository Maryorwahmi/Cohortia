Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Preparation
What belongs in preparation
Team definitions, playbooks, contact lists, validated forensic tools, logging, asset inventories, and tested backups.
Playbooks: Step-by-step guides for likely incidents like phishing or ransomware.
Validated tools: Use forensic workstations and imaging tools you’ve tested for accuracy.
Backups: Regular, tested restores with integrity checks to ensure recovery works.
Key Takeaway

Preparation is not optional. It shortens response time, preserves evidence, and reduces business impact.

Preparation Checklist
  • Define IRT roles and escalation paths.
  • Maintain current asset and network inventories.
  • Validate forensic tools and imaging processes.
  • Enable logs and SIEM feeds across critical systems.
  • Test backup restores and document procedures.
Common mistake
Relying on untested backups or undocumented tools; always validate and document before an incident occurs.
