What mastery looks like
Concrete artifacts and behaviors that show you can lead DFIR.
  • Documented playbooks and tested backups
    You can produce an IRP with roles, escalation, and validated restore procedures.
  • Alert triage and IoC capture
    You can validate alerts using logs and capture forensic indicators for investigation.
  • Containment with evidence preservation
    You can isolate systems while preserving copies for analysis and avoid modifying originals.
