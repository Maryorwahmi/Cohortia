# Incident Type: Successful Phishing Attack (Credential Compromise)

1) Preparation
 - IRT roles: SOC L1/L2, Forensic Investigator, IT Admin, Communications, Legal
 - Tools: SIEM, EDR, Email Gateway, Identity Provider, Forensic workstation

2) Identification
 - Analyze email headers and sandbox links
 - Check identity provider logs for unusual logins
 - Inspect EDR for suspicious processes

3) Containment
 - Force password reset and terminate sessions
 - Block phishing URL/IP and quarantine email
 - Isolate endpoint if compromise suspected (image before changes)

4) Eradication
 - Clean endpoints or reimage
 - Remove persistence and patch vulnerabilities
 - Threat hunt for related IoCs
Scenario notes
Include communication templates and legal checklists for reporting obligations. Tailor commands and tool steps to your environment.
Key Takeaway

A clear playbook reduces decision friction during incidents and ensures consistent, legally sound handling of evidence.
