DFIR Lifecycle
Six phases that form a continuous improvement loop for incident response.
Chain of Custody
Record every handling of evidence and always work from verified copies.
Preparation & Tests
Validated tools, tested backups, and tabletop exercises are the best investment to reduce incident impact.
Summary Takeaway

Treat DFIR as a planned, repeatable cycle. Prioritize preparation and lessons learned, preserve evidence with a strict chain of custody, and verify eradication before recovery.
