Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Eradication
Eradication checklist
Identify and remove malware, close backdoors, remove persistence artifacts, and patch exploited vulnerabilities.
Verification practices
  • Threat hunt across endpoints for related IoCs.
  • Deep log review and EDR behavior analysis.
  • Rescan and re-interview affected users if needed.
Analogy
Think of eradication like removing invasive roots from a garden: you must find every root and repair the soil to prevent regrowth.
Key Takeaway

Eradication is incomplete without verification. Never move to recovery until you’ve hunted for residual access paths.
