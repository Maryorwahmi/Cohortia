Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Key Concepts
Acquisition Strategy
Decide live vs dead acquisition based on volatility. Use trusted tools and minimize in-place changes when collecting evidence.
Integrity & Hashing
Compute SHA256 or MD5 before and after imaging. Record values in the chain of custody to prove identical copies.
Correlated Analysis
Combine memory, file system, and network evidence to reconstruct attacker timelines and identify hidden or in-memory threats.
One-Line Takeaway
Preserve evidence, prove its integrity, and correlate multiple artifact sources—memory, disk, and network—to build a defensible investigation.
