Forensic Data Acquisition and Analysis

Practical techniques to collect volatile and persistent evidence while preserving integrity and chain of custody.

In this lesson
Live vs Dead acquisition, disk imaging, memory forensics, file system and network analysis.
Why it matters
Admissible evidence requires preserving originals, minimizing modification, and proving integrity with hashes and documentation.
Explore Key Topics
Live Acquisition
Capture RAM, running processes, and open connections before shutdown.
Disk Imaging
Bit-for-bit copies with dd, FTK Imager, or EnCase and verify with hashes.
Memory & Network
Use Volatility for memory and Wireshark/Zeek for packet capture analysis.
This chapter gives you the workflow and concrete command examples to perform acquisitions safely and analyze volatile and persistent artifacts with standard forensic tools.
Visual Concept
Acquisition Types
Live acquisition captures volatile RAM and sessions. Dead acquisition images storage with write-blockers to prevent writes.
Verification
Hash original and image (MD5 or SHA256). Document every step to maintain chain of custody.
Example commands
sudo dd if=/dev/sda of=/media/forensics/disk_image.dd bs=4M status=progress
sha256sum /media/forensics/disk_image.dd
Key Takeaway

Preserve originals, prioritize volatile data when necessary, and verify all copies with cryptographic hashes to support legal admissibility.
