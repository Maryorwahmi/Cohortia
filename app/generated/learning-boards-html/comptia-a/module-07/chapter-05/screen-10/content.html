Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Log Pipelines
Pipeline stages
Collect
Provider services, agents, and sidecars gather raw telemetry.
Transport
Use encrypted transport like Kinesis Firehose or Event Hubs to stream logs to your SIEM.
Normalize
Map provider fields to common event schema for cross-cloud correlation.
Enrich
Add identity, geolocation, and threat intel so analysts can prioritize alerts.
Store
Raw logs in secure, encrypted buckets and indexed data in the SIEM for fast search.
Alert
Create correlation rules and notify responders with context and playbooks.
Implementation tips
  • Encrypt in transit and at rest for every transport.
  • Test end-to-end delivery and retention periodically.
  • Limit SIEM access and log-archive access to authorized roles only.
Key takeaway
Normalization is the single most important step to enable cross-cloud detection and reduce analyst cognitive load.
