Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › CloudGuard Inc. Lab
Lab tasks
Complete these three tasks in your AWS environment or a sandbox account.
1. Configure CloudTrail
Enable a multi-region trail. Enable management and S3 data events for targeted buckets. Send logs to an encrypted S3 bucket and CloudWatch Logs.
2. Apply S3 bucket policy
Ensure only the CloudTrail service principal can put objects and deny unencrypted uploads. Confirm bucket ownership controls are set.
3. Create CloudWatch Alarm
Add a metric filter that matches IAM policy changes and attach an alarm to an SNS topic used by your SOC.
Validation
Perform a benign IAM policy modification and confirm the log file is written, CloudWatch metric increases, and the alarm sends a notification.
