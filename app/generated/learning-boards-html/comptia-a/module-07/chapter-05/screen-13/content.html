Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Key Concepts
Cloud-Native Logging
Provider logs like CloudTrail and Activity Logs are the primary evidence for cloud incidents.
CSPM & CASB
CSPM finds misconfigurations; CASB monitors SaaS access. Both should feed your detection pipelines.
Containers & Serverless
Ephemeral workloads need sidecars, agents, and metadata tagging for reliable collection and correlation.
Final takeaway

A secure cloud monitoring posture depends on comprehensive collection, secure storage, and centralized analysis. Design pipelines that protect log integrity and enable cross-environment correlation.
