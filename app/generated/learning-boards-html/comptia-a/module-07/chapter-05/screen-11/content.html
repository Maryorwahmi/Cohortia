Investigation timeline
A quick timeline to guide analysts when events span cloud and on-prem.
T0 — Initial
Suspicious sign-in or API call recorded in CloudTrail or Activity Logs.
T+minutes — Escalation
Privilege changes, role assumptions, or unusual resource creation events.
T+hours — Movement
VPC Flow Logs or network sensors show outbound connections; EDR alerts register lateral activity.
T+days — Clean-up
Deletion of snapshots, disabling of logging, or evidence removal attempts appear in provider logs.
Practical checklist
  • Verify consistent timestamps and timezone settings.
  • Map cloud identities to on-prem users for correlation.
  • Ensure SIEM receives VPC Flow Logs and EDR alerts.
Key takeaway
A unified timeline across sources is essential to determine scope and remediation steps quickly.
