Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › Overview
Advanced SIEM Deployment and Management
Build resilient, scalable SIEMs that ingest diverse telemetry, detect complex attacks, and automate response.
In this lesson
Design distributed architectures, normalize logs, craft correlation rules, optimize performance, and integrate with SOAR.
Why it matters
High-volume, hybrid environments require architecture and tuning to produce reliable detections without overloading analysts.
Explore Key Topics
Distributed Architecture
Regional collectors, indexers, and search heads for scale and resilience.
Data Normalization
Parsing diverse formats into a common schema for accurate correlation.
SOAR Integration
Automated playbooks that act on high-fidelity alerts to reduce response time.
Visual Concept
Cards show components and flows
Collectors
Lightweight forwarders or syslog collectors near sources to reduce bandwidth and ensure sovereignty.
Indexers
High-performance storage and search nodes that normalize and index events for correlation.
Search Heads
Analytics and query nodes that serve analysts and dashboards.
SOAR
Orchestrates automated playbooks when the SIEM emits high-fidelity alerts.
