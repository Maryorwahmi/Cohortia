Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › SOAR Integration
What SOAR adds
Automation, orchestration, and consistent execution of response tasks triggered by SIEM alerts.
Enrichment
Add threat intel, geo-IP, and asset risk to increase alert context.
Orchestration
Execute multi-step actions across tools: firewall, endpoint, ticketing.
Response
Isolate affected systems and document remediation steps automatically.
Concrete playbook example
Trigger: high-confidence credential theft alert. Steps: enrich with threat intel, check asset risk, isolate endpoint if risk is high, block IP on perimeter, and create incident ticket with evidence attachments.
Best practices
  • Start by automating enrichment and low-risk containment
  • Gate high-impact actions behind analyst approval
  • Test playbooks in staging with simulated alerts
Key Takeaway

Automate repeatable, low-risk tasks first and validate playbooks to avoid accidental impact while reducing mean time to contain.
