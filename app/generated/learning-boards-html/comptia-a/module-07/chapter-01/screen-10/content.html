Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › Storage & Search
Tiered storage model
Hot (fast SSD) → Warm (HDD) → Cold (object storage, compressed)
Hot tier
Recent, frequently queried data stored on high-performance disks for quick search.
Cold tier
Archived data compressed in object stores for forensics and compliance.
Indexing strategies
Index common fields and use summary indexes for frequent analyses to speed queries.
Concrete example
Keep 90 days of hot indexes on SSD for fast incident response. Move 1–2 years of historical logs to cold object storage with compression for compliance purposes.
Monitoring
  • Indexing lag and queue sizes
  • Search response time percentiles
  • Storage capacity and growth trends
Key Takeaway

Make storage and indexing decisions based on typical analyst queries and legal retention needs, not only cost.
