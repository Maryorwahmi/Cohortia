Module 07 › Chapter 7.1 › Rule Tuning
Strategies to reduce alert fatigue
Baselining
Profile typical behavior per account, host, or asset class and detect deviations from those baselines.
Exceptions and Allow Lists
Exclude known, legitimate automation or service accounts from noisy rules.
Statistical Thresholds
Use percentiles or deviation from mean instead of a single absolute number.
Concrete example
Set failed-login alerts based on a user’s historical rate. If an account normally experiences one failed login per day, a sudden burst of ten within five minutes is more meaningful than applying a global threshold of five.
Common mistake
Disabling low-severity rules wholesale removes precursors that, when correlated, reveal complex attacks.
Pro tip
Track precision and recall for each rule and use those metrics to prioritize tuning work.
Key takeaway
Tune with data: baselines, metrics, and iterative adjustments beat blunt rule disablement.
