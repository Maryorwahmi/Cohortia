Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Key Concepts
EDR
Continuous endpoint monitoring, detection, and response focused on behavior.
Endpoint Telemetry
Process, file, registry, network, and user signals used for detection and hunting.
Behavioral Analytics
Baselining and anomaly detection to reveal stealthy or novel attacks.
Automated Response
Containment actions like isolation, process kill, and snapshotting to reduce MTTR.
Living‑off‑the‑Land
Attackers use legitimate tools; detection relies on context rather than blocking binaries.
SIEM Integration
Forward enriched endpoint fields for correlation while keeping raw data for forensics.
Summary

Use agents and behavioral analytics to detect stealthy threats, tune telemetry to avoid noise, and integrate with SIEM to get a complete incident picture.
