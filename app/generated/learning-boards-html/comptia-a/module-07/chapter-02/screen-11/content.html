Threat hunting workflow
  1. Form hypothesis (e.g., suspicious outbound connection followed by new process on host).
  2. Query across endpoint, network, and identity logs for related artifacts.
  3. Enrich and pivot results to build a timeline and identify affected assets.
Forensics similarly relies on breadth of collection and retention. Keep both indexed summaries and immutable raw archives when possible to support complete investigations.
Scenario
Unusual process + outbound IP
Search for process creation events on endpoints, then join on source IPs to identify external connections and asset owners for response.
Key Takeaway

Broad collection, enrichment, and retention turn logs into a primary investigative source for hunters and responders.
