Module 07 › Chapter 7.2 › Investigation Enrichment
Which enrichments help most?
  • Geo-location of external IP to determine destination risk.
  • Internal asset owner and criticality to know data sensitivity and contacts.
  • Normalized timestamp to align events across sources.
Why not hardware specs?
Hardware details rarely change the immediate investigative actions for exfiltration; owner and destination context are more actionable.
Key Takeaway

Append geo and owner tags first during investigations; they answer the critical who and where questions quickly.
