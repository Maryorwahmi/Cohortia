Module 07 › Chapter 7.2 › Alert Fatigue
Baselining
Collect historical metrics to determine normal ranges for users, groups, and assets. Use those baselines to detect true anomalies rather than static thresholds.
Dynamic Thresholding
Adjust thresholds by time of day, user role, or observed patterns so rules adapt to expected variations and reduce false alarms.
Example: failed login rule uses a moving baseline per user group; sales team gets a higher working-hour threshold while executives have a wider geographic baseline. Combine with prioritization by asset criticality to ensure high-impact alerts surface first.
Key Takeaway

Tune rules with baselines and dynamic thresholds instead of disabling them; this preserves detection while lowering noise.
