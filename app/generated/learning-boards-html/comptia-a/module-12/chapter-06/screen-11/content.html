Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Legal & Ethics
Regulatory Checklist
  • Identify applicable breach notification laws.
  • Clarify timelines for regulator and customer notification.
  • Coordinate legal review before detailed disclosures.
Ethical Considerations
  • Be truthful while avoiding speculation.
  • Express empathy and accountability.
  • Keep affected parties updated at scheduled intervals.
Documentation

Record approvals, message drafts, timestamps, and monitoring data to support compliance and after-action reviews.
