Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Designing a Plan
Step-by-step Plan
1
Designated Spokespeople
Keep the list small and trained; define media vs regulator roles.
2
Channels & Secure Internal Methods
Use secure messaging and out-of-band contacts when systems may be compromised.
3
Templates & Approval
Pre-approved language with a rapid approval path reduces delay.
4
Monitoring & Rapid Response
Assign a small team to watch media and social draws and escalate misinformation immediately.
Practical Example
Imagine a service outage affecting payments. Your plan already has a template acknowledging an outage, a spokesperson listed for customer statements, a trigger that notifies the executive team if payment throughput drops by 50%, and an approval chain that lets the PR lead send the initial customer message within 30 minutes. That coordination prevents contradictory statements and gives customers clear expectations.
Common Mistake
Expanding the approval chain during an incident; keep the chain efficient and documented in advance.
Key Takeaway

Document and rehearse workflows: simplicity and speed matter more than perfect wording under pressure.
