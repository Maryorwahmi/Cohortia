Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Media Relations
Spokesperson Protocol
  • Designate a single media contact or small team.
  • Provide rehearsed Q&A and approved talking points.
  • Direct all media inquiries to the spokesperson.
  • Avoid "no comment"; state facts and promise updates.
Training
Conduct mock interviews and coordinate with legal and security to ensure accuracy under pressure.
Media Q&A Example
Q: What data was affected? A: We are investigating and will provide verified details to affected customers and regulators as required. Next update: [time].
Pro Tip

Prepare brief, repeatable phrases that convey control and empathy while avoiding speculation.
