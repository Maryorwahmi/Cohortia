Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Message Structure
Five-part message template
  1. Acknowledge: "We are aware of an issue affecting [service]."
  2. Impact: Factually state what is affected and to what extent.
  3. Actions: Explain steps being taken to investigate and remediate.
  4. Next update: Provide an estimated time for the next status report.
  5. Empathy: Apologize and give customer instructions if needed.
Example Statement
We are aware of a payments outage affecting a subset of customers. Our teams are investigating and working to restore service. We will provide an update by 2:00 PM. We apologize for the inconvenience and recommend checking your account summary for unusual activity.
Checklist
  • Keep language plain; avoid technical jargon.
  • Coordinate messages across channels and spokespeople.
  • When in doubt, state what you know and promise the next update time.
Pitfall

Over-sharing attack specifics early. Verify technical details and consult legal before describing attack vectors.
