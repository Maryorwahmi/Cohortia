Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Quick Reminders
Short Templates
Immediate messages should be brief, clear, and include a next-update time.
Lean Approvals
Enable a fast publishing path for approved templates and a review path for sensitive details.
Active Monitoring
Assign a rapid-response team to monitor social and media for 24–72 hours after announcements.
Final Thought

Practice these patterns in drills so they become reliable under real pressure: clarity, speed, and documented compliance win back trust faster.
