Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Internal vs External
Internal Communication (Employees, Leadership)
  • Purpose: operational instructions and reassurance.
  • Content: roles, actions required, escalation paths, and talking points.
  • Channel: secure messaging, internal portal, emergency hotlines.
Example
A call-center script that tells agents to confirm customers’ identity and escalate any account-impact questions to the security team.
External Communication (Customers, Regulators, Media)
  • Purpose: explain impact, what you are doing, and next steps.
  • Content: concise impact statement, actions, next-update time, and empathy.
  • Channel: official website, verified social accounts, press release, regulator filings.
Example
A public post that acknowledges a service issue, indicates affected services, and promises an update at a specific time.
Common Mistake

Using identical technical messages for all groups. Tailor the detail level and tone to the audience.
