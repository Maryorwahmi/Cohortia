Initial Customer Notification (Example)
We have discovered unauthorized access that may have exposed a subset of customer account numbers and email addresses. Our teams contained the incident and are investigating the scope. While we have no evidence at this time that passwords or full payment card numbers were exposed, our investigation is ongoing. We recommend you review recent account activity and report any suspicious communications to our security line. We will provide an update within 48 hours. We apologize for the inconvenience and are committed to keeping you informed.
Internal Briefing Items
  • Customer-facing script with escalation path.
  • List of verified facts and what remains under investigation.
  • Legal notification tasks and regulator timelines.
Checklist
  • Confirm affected data types with security before naming them.
  • Include a next-update time even if details are limited.
  • Provide clear customer actions and contact info for suspicious activity.
Key Takeaway

An effective initial notice is brief, factual, empathetic, and linked to a follow-up plan coordinated with legal and security.
