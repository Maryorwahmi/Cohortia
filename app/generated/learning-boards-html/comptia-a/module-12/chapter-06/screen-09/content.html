Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Social Monitoring
Monitoring Steps
  1. Define keywords: brand, service names, "breach", "outage", and likely misspellings.
  2. Assign tools and a small triage team for 24–48 hour coverage.
  3. Prioritize responses to misinformation that risks customer safety or regulatory complications.
  4. Document actions and timelines for post-incident reviews.
Scenario: False Password Leak
If social posts claim passwords were leaked, the rapid-response team posts an official correction linking to the incident page, explains what data is under investigation, and directs customers to verify communications through official channels. This corrects misinformation and reduces phishing risk.
Common Mistake
Replying to every mention. Focus on high-impact misinformation and signals that affect customer safety or regulatory exposure.
