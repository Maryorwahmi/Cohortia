What crisis communication is

A governance-driven practice that ensures accurate, timely information reaches stakeholders to limit damage and enable recovery.

Concrete Example
During a ransomware event, an internal advisory about password resets and external customer notice about affected services prevents confusion and potential exploitation.
Common Misconception
Silence reduces liability. In regulated environments, delayed disclosure can increase fines and damage trust.
Key Takeaway

Treat communication as part of your recovery plan: accurate messaging speeds restoration and reduces reputational loss.

Components of a Crisis Communication Plan
  • Designated spokespeople and media authority rules.
  • Communication channels and secure internal methods.
  • Pre-approved message templates for common scenarios.
  • Notification triggers and approval workflows.
  • Monitoring strategy for media and social sentiment.
