Module 12 › Chapter 12.6 › Post-Incident Review
Review Steps
  1. Gather logs, messages, timestamps, and approvals.
  2. Interview PR, Legal, Security, Ops, and frontline staff.
  3. Identify root causes for communication failures.
  4. Assign owners and deadlines for corrective actions.
From Findings to Practice
Convert findings into concrete updates: simplified templates, updated approval flows, expanded spokespeople training, and refreshed monitoring rules. Add the changes to tabletop exercises and verify effectiveness in future drills.
Key Takeaway

An after-action review with assigned follow-ups ensures lessons are implemented and not forgotten.
