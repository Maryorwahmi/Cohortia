Crisis Communication and Stakeholder Management

Prepare structured plans, choose spokespeople, and tailor messages so your organization communicates accurately and ethically under pressure.

In this lesson
Build a crisis plan, identify stakeholders, craft clear messages, and coordinate media and social monitoring with legal oversight.
Why it matters
Communication decisions influence trust, regulatory compliance, and the speed of recovery — not just technical outcomes.
Explore Key Topics
Designated Spokespersons
A small trained group authorized to speak prevents mixed messages and legal risk.
Message Templates
Pre-approved language for scenarios saves time and keeps statements consistent and compliant.
Monitoring & Legal
Social listening plus legal review ensures timely corrections and required disclosures.
