What to Remember

Key Concepts — Part 1

Backup Types
Full, incremental, differential — choose by restore needs and storage.
3‑2‑1 Rule
Three copies, two media types, one off‑site reduces correlated failures.
Replication & Snapshots
Lower RPO and enable fast recovery; still pair with off‑site backups for durability.
Summary

Start with objectives, choose layered controls, and verify restores regularly to maintain trust in your backups.
