Snapshots and Point‑in‑Time Recovery

Snapshots provide rapid rollback capability but are not a substitute for off‑site backups.

How snapshots fit in a layered approach
Use snapshots for immediate rollback after configuration changes or to recover from recent logical errors. Combine snapshots with replication for high availability and with off‑site backups for long term durability.
Concrete use

Take an application‑consistent snapshot before upgrades. If the upgrade fails, rollback to the snapshot for rapid recovery.

Operational note

Snapshots often live on the same storage; copy critical snapshots off‑site or export them to cloud storage for long term retention.

Quick checklist
  • Use application‑consistent snapshots when possible.
  • Keep short snapshot retention to limit storage and impact.
  • Export or copy critical snapshots off‑site for durability.
  • Test snapshot restores periodically.
