Key Concepts — Part 2

Encryption
Use strong algorithms and separate key management for backup confidentiality.
Integrity Verification
Checksums and regular restore tests confirm backups are recoverable and intact.
Immutable & Air‑gapped
Protect availability against tampering and ransomware with immutability or physical isolation.
Final takeaway

Combine layered protection, documented recovery procedures, and recurring verification to ensure backups deliver real recoverability.
