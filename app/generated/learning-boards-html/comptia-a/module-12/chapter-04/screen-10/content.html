Core Concept Secure Backups

Encryption, Access Controls, and Integrity

Protect backup confidentiality, integrity, and availability against threats including ransomware.

Security Checklist
  • Encrypt at rest and in transit (use strong algorithms and managed keys).
  • Apply least privilege and MFA for backup admin accounts.
  • Isolate backup networks and limit direct access from production systems.
  • Use immutable storage or air‑gapped copies to defend against ransomware.
Example: enable AES‑256 at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit, enforce MFA on backup console and store keys in a hardware security module or managed KMS with limited access.
Integrity & Verification
Generate checksums or hashes when backups complete and store them separately. Periodically perform restores to an isolated environment to confirm data usability and run application‑level checks.
Key Takeaway

Encryption protects confidentiality; immutability and isolation protect availability in the face of ransomware.

Pitfall

Keeping backup keys with the same privileges as production keys can allow attackers to decrypt backup data.
