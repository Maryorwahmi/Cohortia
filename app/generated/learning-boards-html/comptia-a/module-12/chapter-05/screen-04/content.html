Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Integration Rationale
What each discipline does

• Incident Response: detect, contain, eradicate, collect evidence.

• Business Continuity: keep critical functions running via alternate processes.

• Disaster Recovery: restore IT systems, data, and backups.

Concrete example
A ransomware event encrypts production servers and backups. IR identifies the strain and IOCs. DRP must avoid restoring infected backups; BCP must route work to alternate systems until clean restores are available.
Misconception
Speed alone fixes incidents. Fast but uninformed restores can reintroduce the threat, cause repeat outages, and complicate legal evidence.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Integration ties detection intelligence to continuity and recovery actions so restores are safe and auditable.
