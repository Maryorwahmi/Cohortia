Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Snapshots vs Imaging
Snapshot
Fast point-in-time copy for VMs and cloud volumes. Minimal downtime and good for initial preservation.
Full Disk Image
Complete forensic capture preserving slack space and exact disk structure. Slower and resource intensive.
Guiding questions
  • Is the host virtualized or physical?
  • What is the legal requirement for evidence?
  • How long can the service remain offline?
Practical pattern

Snapshot quickly for all virtual hosts, then image the highest-value systems based on priority.
