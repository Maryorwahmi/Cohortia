Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Crisis Communications
Key channels
  • Dedicated secure chat or conference bridge (isolated).
  • Shared incident management platform as single source of truth.
  • Immutable logging system for forensic exports.
Information roles
IR posts IOCs and forensic actions. BCP posts business impacts. DRP posts restore and backup validity. Communications crafts internal and external messaging coordinated with Legal.
Example ticket updates
• IR: "IOC set posted; systems A-C imaged; backups pending verification."
• DRP: "Primary backups verified clean; restore of database cluster scheduled at 04:00 UTC."
• BCP: "Alternate order-entry process active; estimated 60% throughput."
Pitfall

Do not use compromised internal channels as your primary crisis communications path.
