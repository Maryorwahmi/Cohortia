Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › IOCs & Handoff
Handoff packet contents
  • Confirmed IOCs (hashes, IPs, domains, TTP notes).
  • List of affected systems and last known good states.
  • Forensic actions taken (images, snapshots, log exports).
  • Backup verification status and recommended restore sources.
Example summary
Compromised hosts: DB1-DB3. Malware family: encryptor-X. Snapshots taken at 2024-06-01T03:30Z. Primary backups indicated corrupted. Recommended restore: golden image from vault, then apply verified clean backups tested offline.
Checklist

Ensure each handoff includes a named owner for verification and a timestamped evidence list.
