Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Escalation Criteria
Activation Triggers (examples)
  1. Critical business function unavailable for > 2 hours.
  2. > X% of production servers are compromised/unavailable.
  3. Primary and secondary backups are confirmed compromised.
  4. Regulatory notification requirements triggered.
Tie each condition to an observable metric and the BIA so the Incident Commander can verify quickly.
Activation steps (protocol)

1. Incident Commander assesses impact vs. BIA.

2. Notify executive leadership and C-level stakeholders.

3. Activate BCP/DRP leads and establish a joint command center.

4. IR hands over IOCs, affected-systems list, and forensic status.

Common mistake

Avoid ambiguous language like "major" or "critical" without numeric or time-based qualifiers.
