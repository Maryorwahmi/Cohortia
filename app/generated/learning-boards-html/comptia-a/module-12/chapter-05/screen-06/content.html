Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › War Room Design
Roles and responsibilities
Incident Commander
Assess triggers, declare disaster, convene command.
IR Lead
Contain, collect IOCs, preserve evidence status.
BCP Lead
Activate alternate workflows, report business impacts.
DRP Lead
Validate backups, coordinate restores, use golden images.
War room checklist
  • Establish secure, isolated communication channels.
  • Define briefing cadence and decision windows.
  • Assign a single incident log and authoritative status source.
  • Confirm forensic preservation priorities before restores.
