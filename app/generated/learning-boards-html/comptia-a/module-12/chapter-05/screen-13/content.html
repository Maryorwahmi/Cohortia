Packet sections
  • Incident summary and timestamps.
  • Confirmed IOCs and brief TTP notes.
  • List of affected systems and last-known-good states.
  • Forensic actions taken and custody notes.
  • Backup verification and recommended restore sources.

Incident: Ransomware encryptor-X
Timestamp: 2024-06-01T03:22Z
IOCs: hash-abc123, C2-ip-1.2.3.4
Affected: DB1, DB2 (last-known-good: 2024-05-31T23:50Z)
Forensics: VM snapshots taken at 2024-06-01T03:30Z; disk images queued for DB1
Backups: Primary backups flagged corrupted; golden image in vault verified
Recommended action: DRP restore DB cluster from golden image; hold snapshots for analysis
Owner signatures: IR lead / DRP lead / Incident Commander
