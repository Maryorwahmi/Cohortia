Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Common Mistakes
Top pitfalls
  • Siloed exercises that miss handoffs.
  • Ambiguous escalation thresholds.
  • Using compromised communication channels.
  • Imaging everything or nothing — no prioritization.
Mitigations
Run integrated tabletop exercises, bind triggers to BIA metrics, provision an out-of-band crisis channel, and maintain a prioritized imaging and snapshot policy.
