Module 12 › Chapter 12.5 › Tabletop Exercises
Design elements
  • Use multi-domain scenarios that require IR, BCP, and DRP coordination.
  • Define objectives: test handoffs, communications, and decision authority.
  • Assign a facilitator and note-taker for action items.
Post-exercise outputs
A prioritized list of gaps, updated handoff templates, clarified escalation thresholds, and scheduled follow-up trainings to close the loop.
Common mistake

Running exercises in silos misses cross-team friction. Always include all relevant roles.
