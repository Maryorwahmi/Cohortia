Pre-incident decisions
  • Which systems are RTO-critical?
  • Which systems require full imaging for legal purposes?
  • Which hosts will use snapshot-first patterns?
Operational pattern
Snapshot or export logs immediately for high-priority systems, restore from golden images to meet RTO, and assign forensic resources to analyze preserved artifacts offline.
