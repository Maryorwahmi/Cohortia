Definition
Evidence Preservation
Capture forensically sound images and immutable logs before restoration when incidents may lead to legal action.
  • Imaging: full disk or VM images stored with integrity checks.
  • Log retention: export and write-once storage for system, application, and security logs.
  • Immutability: use storage that prevents alteration of preserved artifacts.
Chain of custody example
Document who created the image, when it was transferred, where it is stored, and who accessed it. This record must be maintained for legal admissibility.
Step 1: Identify compromised assets and list them.
Step 2: Create forensically sound images and calculate hashes.
Step 3: Store images in immutable storage and record chain-of-custody entries.
Key Takeaway

Define imaging and custody procedures in the DRP so that preservation is routine, not ad hoc during a crisis.
