Comparison
PCI DSS vs SOX
PCI DSS
Requires formal incident response and business continuity planning for cardholder data; testing at least annually.
SOX
Mandates internal controls over financial reporting, which implies DRP steps to preserve availability and integrity of financial systems and records.
Scenario
A payment gateway outage: PCI requires documented incident response and annual exercise results showing recovery. A public company relying on that gateway must also show SOX-compliant controls that ensure financial statements remain accurate despite the outage.
Checklist snippet
  • Annual test documentation (PCI)
  • Evidence of control effectiveness post-recovery (SOX)
  • Retention of test logs and approvals
Key Takeaway

Align test frequency and audit records to the strictest applicable requirement for the system in scope.
