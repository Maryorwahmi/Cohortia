Definition & Timeline
GDPR: Resilience and Notification
Article 32 requires measures to ensure resilience and restoration; Articles 33 and 34 define notification duties and timelines.
Incident timeline (example)
0–6 hours
Detect and classify incident; determine if personal data is impacted.
6–24 hours
In-depth impact assessment; collect evidence and export logs.
24–72 hours
Prepare and send supervisory authority notification if required; legal review included.
Example
When 100,000 EU customers' data is exposed, use the DRP to produce the supervisory authority notice within 72 hours and prepare direct notices to affected individuals if high risk.
Common mistake
Delaying assessment or legal review until after restoration can miss notification windows and increase fines.
Key Takeaway

Embed impact assessment and notification checkpoints into your DRP so you can meet statutory timelines while preserving evidence.
