Process Flow
Notification Sequence for Compliance
1. Detect & Classify
Establish whether regulated data is involved.
2. Impact Assessment
Measure scale, affected data types, and risk to individuals.
3. Preserve Evidence
Image systems and export logs before restoration.
4. Notify & Communicate
Prepare notifications to authorities and individuals with legal approval.
Example checkpoint: "Within 24 hours of detection, complete impact assessment form and upload to the secure audit folder. Legal review scheduled and logged."
Key Takeaway

Time-boxed, auditable steps ensure notification obligations are met without sacrificing evidence preservation.
