Risk & Consequence
Consequences of Non-Compliance
  • Regulatory fines: administrative penalties and enforcement actions.
  • Civil suits: affected individuals may seek damages for harm caused by the incident.
  • Operational loss: revoked licenses or contract bans can end lines of business.
Practical mitigation
Maintain auditable records of DRP actions, preserve evidence, and use legal-reviewed notification templates. This reduces both regulatory risk and the chance of successful litigation.
Common mistake
Prioritizing immediate restoration without preserving evidence can increase legal liability and destroy the ability to defend decisions.
Key Takeaway

Treat compliance activities as risk controls and document them to reduce both fines and legal exposure.
