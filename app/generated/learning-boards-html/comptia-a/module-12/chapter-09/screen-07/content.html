Definition
Data Retention & Recovery Mapping
Define what data must be kept, for how long, and how restoration will occur within regulatory timelines.
  • Retention rule: identify regulated classes (financial, patient, cardholder) and their retention windows.
  • Storage: map hot, warm, and cold storage locations, and ensure immutability where regulation requires it.
  • Recovery test: verify backup restores for each retention tier.
Example mapping
Financial records: retain seven years in immutable archive and test quarterly restores for samples. Patient records: retain per healthcare policy and verify restores annually with integrity checks.
Common mistake
Archiving without restoration tests assumes recoverability; verification is required to meet legal obligations.
Key Takeaway

Pair retention rules with restoration tests and clear RTO/RPO targets for each data class.
