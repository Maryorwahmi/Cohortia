Process Flow
Designing Compliance Tests
Translate regulatory requirements into specific test objectives and artifacts.
  • Define objective (e.g., notify supervisory authority within 72 hours).
  • Build scenario that forces the objective (e.g., simulate data exfiltration of EU data).
  • Capture artifacts (logs, timestamps, legal sign-off) and retain them for audit.
Key Takeaway

Tests should create the exact auditable records a regulator would request.

Example scenario
Simulate a breach of EU customer data affecting 50,000 accounts. Execute detection, impact assessment, forensic imaging, and prepare a 72-hour supervisory authority draft. Record timestamps for every action and collect legal sign-off during the tabletop.
Artifact: Impact assessment form with timestamps and verifier name
Artifact: Forensic image manifest and chain of custody log
Artifact: Notification draft and legal approval record
