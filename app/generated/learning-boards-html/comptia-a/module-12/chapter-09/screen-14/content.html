Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Key Concepts
HIPAA
Requires DRP and backup plans for ePHI availability and integrity.
GDPR
Mandates resilience, restoration ability, and timely breach notification (e.g., supervisory authority timelines).
PCI DSS
Requires incident response and business continuity controls for cardholder data, with testing requirements.
Summary
Map each regulation to concrete DRP steps, tests, and artifacts so compliance is auditable.
