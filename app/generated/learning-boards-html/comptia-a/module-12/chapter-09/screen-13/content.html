Scenario Exercise
GDPR Breach Notification Integration
Step 1: Identify
Determine supervisory authority and whether affected individuals must be notified.
Step 2: Integrate
Add assessment, preservation, and communication checkpoints to the DRP runbook.
Step 3: Preserve
Before restoration, image systems, export logs, and record chain of custody.
Sample runbook checkpoint:
"Forensic Evidence Preservation for GDPR: Before any restoration, DRP Lead must confirm with IR Lead that images and log exports are completed, hashes recorded, artifacts saved to immutable storage, and chain-of-custody entries made; IR Lead sign-off required."
Key Takeaway

Practice this scenario in tabletop exercises and keep the artifacts to prove compliance under time pressure.
