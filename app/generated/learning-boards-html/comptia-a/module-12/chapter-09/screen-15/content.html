Module 12 › Chapter 12.9 › Key Concepts
Data Retention
Laws specify retention windows and secure destruction rules; map them to backup and archive policies.
Evidence Preservation
Capture images and logs with integrity checks and store them immutably for forensics.
Chain of Custody
Document each handling step so digital evidence remains admissible and verifiable.
Final takeaway
Make evidence capture and retention part of normal recovery operations so compliance is proven, not hoped for.
