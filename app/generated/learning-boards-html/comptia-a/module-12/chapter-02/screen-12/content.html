Common challenges
How to keep BIA accurate, objective, and secure.
Bias & accuracy
Cross-check stakeholder answers with logs and incident histories.
Prioritization disputes
Use validated metrics and executive sponsorship to resolve claims of criticality.
Data sensitivity
Protect BIA outputs to avoid exposing organizational vulnerabilities.
Best practices
Standardize questionnaires, hold cross-functional workshops, and schedule regular BIA reviews tied to change management.
Key Takeaway
Treat the BIA as a living document that must be updated, validated, and secured.
