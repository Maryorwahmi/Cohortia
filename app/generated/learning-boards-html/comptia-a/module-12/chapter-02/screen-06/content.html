Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Financial vs Non-Financial
Comparing impacts
Present both monetary loss estimates and non-financial severity with examples.
Financial
  • Lost revenue per hour
  • Overtime and temporary staffing costs
  • Fines, SLA penalties, recovery hardware costs
Present as an accumulated cost curve over time.
Non-Financial
  • Reputational damage and customer churn risk
  • Legal exposure and compliance penalties
  • Operational disruption and employee morale
Use a documented severity scale and examples to support judgments.
Table example
Hourly loss: $50k | Reputational: High | Legal risk: Medium
Example scenario
E-commerce outage during peak shopping can produce immediate large financial losses and long-term customer erosion.
Key Takeaway
Combine numbers and severity ratings for a complete impact picture.
