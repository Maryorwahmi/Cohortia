Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › RTO & RPO
Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
Maximum acceptable downtime before recovery must be completed.
• Drives infrastructure choices: hot site for minutes, warm or cold sites for longer RTOs.
Example
An online payments engine might require an RTO of minutes to avoid major revenue loss during peak hours.
Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
Maximum tolerable amount of data loss measured backward in time.
• Guides backup frequency and replication. Near-zero RPO requires continuous replication or synchronous replication.
Example
A customer order system might require an RPO of 15 minutes to avoid lost orders and reconciliation complexity.
Misconception
RTO and RPO are not interchangeable; one measures downtime, the other measures data-loss window.
Check
Validate both metrics with business owners and operations to avoid misaligned expectations.
Key Takeaway
Set RTO and RPO together to define acceptable downtime and data loss, then design recovery accordingly.
