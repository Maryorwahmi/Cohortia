Key Concepts — Quick Reference
Business Impact Analysis
A systematic process to determine effects of interruptions on operations.
RTO & RPO
RTO = downtime tolerance. RPO = data-loss tolerance.
MTD & WRT
MTD is the absolute outage limit; WRT is post-restoration work to resume normal operations.
Final takeaway

Use a validated, repeatable BIA to align recovery spending with measurable business risk and to produce actionable DR plans.
