Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Gathering Data
Collecting reliable inputs
Structured interviews, workshops, and validation are essential to avoid bias.
Interviews
Open-ended but anchored to recent incidents and numeric estimates.
Workshops
Cross-team sessions to reveal hidden dependencies and confirm priorities.
Questionnaires
Standard templates ensure consistent data collection across units.
Bias & pitfalls
Stakeholders may over-claim criticality or miss dependencies; mitigate by cross-checking logs and incident history.
Validation
Present quantified results back to owners and executives for sign-off and funding alignment.
