Activity: BIA for Secure Customer Portal
Draft questions, propose RTO/RPO, and identify dependencies.
1. Interview question examples
  • Can you describe the primary business functions supported by the Secure Customer Portal?
  • What is the estimated financial loss per hour if the portal is unavailable during peak times?
  • What non-financial impacts (customer trust, regulatory risk) occur if the portal is down for extended periods?
  • Which internal and external systems does the portal rely on, and what is the impact if they fail?
  • What is the maximum time the business could tolerate a full outage before severe consequences?
  • What is the maximum amount of data loss tolerable without causing significant issues?
  • Are there regulatory or contractual uptime obligations specific to the portal?
2. Suggested RTO & RPO
Proposed RTO: 4 hours — justification: customer self-service and compliance require rapid restoration to avoid heavy support load and regulatory notices. Proposed RPO: 15 minutes — justification: transactional and profile changes mean data loss beyond this window would create reconciliation complexity and customer impact.
3. Likely dependencies
  • Centralized Identity Provider (IdP) for authentication
  • Customer Database (e.g., PostgreSQL) for profile and transaction data
  • Payment Gateway API for billing functions
  • Logging and monitoring systems for incident detection and audit
Note
Include WRT in total recovery timeline to account for reconciliation and testing.
Pitfall
Avoid optimistic estimates without cross-checking incident logs and vendor SLAs.
Key Takeaway
Ask for concrete examples and metrics; translate answers into defensible RTO/RPO and dependency mappings.
