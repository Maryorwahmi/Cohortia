Applied Skills for BIA
Techniques to collect reliable BIA inputs and validate outputs with stakeholders.
Interview Guides
Structured open-ended questions reduce bias and capture dependencies.
Workshops
Group sessions reveal cross-team dependencies and hidden single points of failure.
Validation & Sign-off
Present quantified impacts to management for approval and funding decisions.
Common facilitation tip
Ask for concrete examples of recent outages to anchor impact estimates and avoid hypothetical inflation.
Why it matters
Validated inputs make recovery investments defensible and aligned to business risk.
