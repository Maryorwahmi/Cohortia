MTD and WRT
MTD is the absolute outage limit; WRT covers all recovery steps after restoration.
Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)
The point at which continued outage causes unacceptable business consequences.
MTD is used to set escalation thresholds and long-term continuity decisions.
Work Recovery Time (WRT)
Time required after meeting RTO to perform data validation, configuration, and testing needed for normal operations.
Always add WRT to RTO when communicating true time-to-business-readiness.
Example
A system with RTO=4h and WRT=2h requires six hours to return to full operation; ensure planning reflects that total.
Misstep
Ignoring WRT leads to early handoff and operational errors during recovery.
Key Takeaway
Report both technical recovery time and the additional work required for true business readiness.
