Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Prioritizing CBFs
Priority tiers
Rank functions to match investment to the true cost of failure.
Mission-Critical
Shortest RTO/RPO, high availability, active-active or hot sites.
Essential
Longer tolerances; warm sites, manual workarounds acceptable temporarily.
Non-Essential
Lowest priority; cold site or deferred restoration is acceptable.
Concrete example
Banking: transaction engine (mission-critical), HR portal (essential), archive (non-essential).
Key Takeaway
Use validated impact numbers and executive alignment to prevent priority inflation.
