Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › Trading Platform Scenario
Financial trading scenario
Apply component-level RTO/RPO to guide recovery investments.
Order matching engine
RTO: minutes | RPO: near-zero | Requires active-active replication.
Trade ledger
RTO: short hours | RPO: low minutes | May use synchronous replication for critical tables.
Historical reporting
RTO: days | RPO: hours | Restorable from backups; lower-cost recovery acceptable.
Example calculation
Estimate revenue per minute lost for order matching and multiply by probable outage duration to inform the cost of high-availability architecture.
Key Takeaway
Apply RTO/RPO at component granularity to avoid over-spending on non-critical parts of the system.
