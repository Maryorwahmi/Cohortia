BIA Methodology
A step-by-step approach that produces validated recovery requirements.
1. Scope
Define boundaries and objectives for the analysis.
2. Gather
Interviews, questionnaires, and workshops with owners and IT.
3. Analyze
Quantify financial and non-financial impacts over time.
4. Identify
Map Critical Business Functions and dependencies.
5. Document
Validate results and present for management approval.
Example
A retail site's BIA scoped payment, inventory, and customer profile systems separately to produce different RTOs for each.
Pitfall
Collecting only high-level opinions without numeric impact estimates leads to weak prioritization.
Pro tip
Use recent outage incidents to anchor estimates and reduce hypothetical inflation.
