Module 12 › Chapter 12.2 › What is a Business Impact Analysis?
Definition & purpose
A systematic process to quantify the effects of service interruptions.
• Identifies Critical Business Functions and their dependencies.
• Quantifies financial and non-financial impacts over time.
• Produces recovery metrics that inform BCP and DRP investments.
Analogy
Like medical triage: triage the most critical functions first so limited recovery resources have the largest effect.
Security role
Security practitioners ensure recovery methods preserve confidentiality and integrity, and identify cascading risks from compromised systems during recovery.
Concrete example
When restoring a customer database, security must ensure PHI is never exposed to an unsecured recovery environment even if that speeds recovery.
Common misconception
BIA is not only an IT activity; it depends on business owners to quantify impact and set priorities.
Key Takeaway

BIA outputs are the foundation of recovery planning: objective metrics drive architecture and investment priorities.
