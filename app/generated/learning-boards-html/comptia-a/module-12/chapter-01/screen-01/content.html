Understand the strategic role of BCP in enterprise security architecture and how to align recovery objectives with business priorities.

BCP Scope
Processes • People • Technology • Suppliers
A whole-organization view ensures recovery enables business activities, not only system restoration.
DRP (subset)
Systems • Data • Infrastructure
DRP restores IT capability that supports the broader continuity objectives.
Concentric view: BCP is the outer layer; DRP sits inside to restore IT.
Key Takeaway

BCP defines what must keep working for the organization; DRP restores the systems that make those processes possible.
