Practical BCP capabilities

Turn policy into measurable, testable continuity actions that protect operations and compliance.

Governance
Executive sponsorship, BCP team and roles
Stakeholders
Business owners, operations, legal, vendors
Testing
Tabletops, simulations, and full exercises with measurable outcomes
Common Pitfall
Treating BCP as a checkbox
When BCP lacks testing and governance, documented plans rarely perform under stress.
Pro tip
Secure offsite copies and vendor inclusion
Maintain offline, protected copies of plans and ensure third parties are part of exercises.
