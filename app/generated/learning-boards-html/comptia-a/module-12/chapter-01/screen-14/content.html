Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Key Concepts
BCP is holistic
Continuity spans people, process, technology and external partners to keep business functions alive.
BIA drives objectives
RTO and RPO should be set by business impact, not technical convenience or guesswork.
Test and govern
Regular exercises and leadership ownership are required to keep plans effective and auditable.
Summary
Keep continuity holistic, base priorities on impact, and validate plans through repeatable testing and governance.
