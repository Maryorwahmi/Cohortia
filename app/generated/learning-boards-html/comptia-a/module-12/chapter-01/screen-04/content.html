Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › What is BCP?
Definition

Business Continuity Planning

A management process to identify threats and ensure essential activities continue during disruptions.

Example
E-commerce order processing maintains alternate staff and payment routing when the primary site fails.
Misconception
BCP is more than backups; it coordinates people and processes as well as technology.
Key Takeaway

BCP aligns organizational activities to keep the business operating, not just to restore systems.

Analogy
Think of BCP as a city's emergency services plan. It names essential services, routes resources, and keeps life-sustaining operations running when infrastructure is damaged.
Covers
People, Processes, Tech, Partners
Doesn't equal
Only IT backups or facilities work
