Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Plan Development

Plan components

  • Roles and responsibilities with escalation matrix
  • Step-by-step technical runbooks and checklists
  • Communication templates for customers, regulators, and staff
  • Secure offsite copies and access procedures
Example notification
A short customer message: what happened, expected impact, recommended actions, and a contact method. Keep it concise and approved in advance.
Usability tip
Use short, bolded checklists that can be followed under stress rather than long narrative procedures.
