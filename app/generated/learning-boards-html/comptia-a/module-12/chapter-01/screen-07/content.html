Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Business Impact Analysis

What BIA delivers

  • Identification of Critical Business Functions and owners
  • Dependency maps across people, systems, locations, and vendors
  • RTO and RPO per function and measurable impact estimates
Example
A trading engine: RTO = minutes; RPO = near-zero. Back-office reports: RTO = hours; RPO = daily.

Common mistakes

Avoid arbitrary objectives or technical-only targets. Validate recovery needs with business owners and test assumptions through tabletop exercises.
Why it matters

Accurate BIA ensures resources are invested where downtime would cause unacceptable harm to operations or compliance.
