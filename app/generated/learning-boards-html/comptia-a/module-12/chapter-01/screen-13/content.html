Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Common Pitfalls

Top mistakes

  • No executive sponsorship or insufficient resourcing
  • Confusing DRP with full BCP requirements
  • Infrequent or unrealistic testing
  • Omitting third parties and supply chain risks
Mitigations
  1. Secure leadership commitment and define KPIs
  2. Align DRP targets with BIA-derived RTO/RPO
  3. Test regularly and involve vendors in exercises
Practical action
Start with a tabletop that includes a critical vendor and one senior exec to demonstrate value and reveal gaps.
