Regulatory examples

  • Healthcare rules requiring data restoration procedures
  • Financial regulations demanding operational resilience and reporting
  • Industry-specific critical infrastructure requirements for availability

Stakeholder expectations

  • Customers expect continuity for critical services
  • Investors expect demonstrated risk management
  • Employees and partners require clarity during incidents
Process: identify stakeholders → map applicable regulations → perform risk and dependency analysis → prioritize critical business functions for BIA. This alignment ensures BCP decisions meet legal obligations and stakeholder expectations while focusing limited resources where they matter most.
