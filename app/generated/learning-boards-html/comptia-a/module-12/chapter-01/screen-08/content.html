Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Initiation & Governance

Key steps

  1. Secure executive sponsorship and assign a program lead
  2. Form a cross-functional team with business and technical owners
  3. Define scope, objectives, and success metrics
  4. Set governance, decision authorities, and review cadence
Scope example
This BCP covers the TradeExecutionEngine process, order routing, staff continuity, and related third-party gateways. It does not cover physical building security, which is managed by facilities.
Misconception check: sponsorship means a budget exists. Sponsorship must be accompanied by committed time, clear accountabilities, and measurable milestones.
