Maintenance activities

  • Schedule periodic BIA and plan reviews
  • Update runbooks during system changes via change control
  • Securely store current plan versions offsite
Audit readiness
Maintain evidence of tests, approvals, and version history to satisfy regulatory and stakeholder inquiries.
Misconception check: maintenance is not only for documentation. It includes retraining, validating access, and aligning with infrastructure changes.
