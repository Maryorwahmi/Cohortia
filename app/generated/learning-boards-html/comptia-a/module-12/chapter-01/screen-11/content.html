Types of exercises

  • Tabletop — validate decisions and communications
  • Simulation — test technical steps and process handoffs
  • Full exercise — failover and operational continuity in real conditions
Measurement
Set clear success criteria based on the RTO and RPO, and verify both technical recovery and business process continuity.
After-action
Document findings, update runbooks, retrain teams, and retest to confirm improvements.
