Module 12 › Chapter 12.1 › Strategy Development

Strategy options

  • Active-active regional replication for fastest recovery
  • Cold standby with manual switchover for cost savings
  • Alternate work locations and manual procedures for degraded operations
Example
Trading engine: primary = replication across regions; fallback = manual routing and order limits with notification to stakeholders.
Checklist
  1. Map resources needed for each strategy
  2. Estimate cost, lead time, and security controls
  3. Document decision criteria and roll-back conditions
