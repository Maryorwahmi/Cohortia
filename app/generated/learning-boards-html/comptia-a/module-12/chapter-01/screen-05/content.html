Business Continuity Planning

Organization-wide continuity

Focuses on keeping critical business functions operational, including people, processes, communications, and suppliers.

  • Prioritizes Critical Business Functions and dependencies
  • Sets RTO/RPO at the process level
  • Includes communications, alternate work arrangements, and vendor management
Disaster Recovery Planning

IT systems and data recovery

Technical plans for restoring infrastructure, applications, and data, often including backups, failover, and restoration procedures.

  • Defines technical recovery steps and restoration order
  • Specifies backup frequency, restore verification, and secure recovery environments
  • Works to meet the RTO/RPO required by business processes
Integration note: Ensure DRP's RTO and RPO targets are derived from the Business Impact Analysis so that IT recovery supports the business need rather than technical convenience.
