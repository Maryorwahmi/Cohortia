Vendor-inclusive Tabletop Exercises
Design realistic scenarios, collect evidence, and assign follow-up actions to vendors and internal teams.
Exercise checklist
  • Define a clear scenario and objectives
  • Include vendor notification and escalation steps
  • Require tangible evidence of restore or mitigation
Example scenario
Simulate a major outage at a cloud provider that affects database services for a high-volume region. Test failover to secondary provider and validate data consistency.
Post‑exercise actions
Document lessons learned, update contracts or technical designs, and assign remediation tasks to responsible parties.
Key takeaway
Make exercises evidence‑focused and treat vendor cooperation as a measurable outcome to support future audits.
