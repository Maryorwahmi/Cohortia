Legal and Regulatory Implications
Ensure vendor oversight is documented to satisfy regulators and reduce post‑incident exposure.
Controller vs Processor
As a controller, you are responsible for ensuring processors handle data according to legal obligations.
Required evidence
Contracts with data handling clauses, audit reports, test results, and vendor assessment artifacts.
Common error
Treating certification as proof of ongoing compliance; regulators expect active oversight and records.
Example
Under data protection rules, controllers must ensure processors notify breaches promptly and follow agreed controls; contracts must reflect this.
Key takeaway
Documented oversight and auditable vendor evidence protect your organization from regulatory and reputational harm.
