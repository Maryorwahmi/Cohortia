Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Mitigation Controls
Diversify, Redund, Monitor
Choose cost-effective controls that protect the most critical services first.
Diversification
Use alternate vendors for critical services to reduce vendor concentration risk.
Redundancy
Implement redundant connections, cross‑region resources, or secondary providers with clear failover triggers.
Monitoring
Continuously track vendor SLAs, incident feeds, and performance to detect problems early.
Contingency plans
Document manual workarounds and temporary procedures to maintain critical functions while full recovery occurs.
Example: database RPO gap
If a provider’s RPO is too long, use application‑level replication or continuous data protection to meet your RPO independently.
Key takeaway
Apply mitigation according to impact; expensive redundancy belongs where failure cost is highest.
