Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › What is Supply Chain Resilience?
Definition
Supply chain resilience explained

Supply chain resilience means anticipatory planning, contractual controls, and operational readiness to keep critical services available when vendors fail.

Concrete example
If a payment processor goes down, a prearranged alternate processor or manual process preserves sales and reconciliation.
Common misconception
Assuming a vendor's compliance certificate equals operational recovery capability; testing is required.
Why it matters
Link to BCP/DRP
  • Include vendor RTO/RPO in your BIA and recovery objectives.
  • Map vendor services to critical business functions to reveal single points of failure.
  • Plan compensating controls when vendor recovery targets don’t match yours.
Key Takeaway

A resilient supply chain is an explicit part of your continuity plan, not an afterthought.
