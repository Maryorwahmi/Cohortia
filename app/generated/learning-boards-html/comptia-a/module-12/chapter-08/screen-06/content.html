Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Vendor Risk Assessment
Assess Vendor Risk
Compare attributes to determine continuity impact and actions.
Compare: High impact vs Low impact
High-impact vendors support multiple critical functions; require strict RTO/RPO alignment and audit rights.
Low-impact vendors support non-critical functions and can be lower priority for immediate remediation.
Assessment checklist
  • Confirm vendor RTO and RPO and compare to your BIA.
  • Verify geographic redundancy and blast‑radius controls.
  • Request evidence of recovery tests and review financial stability.
  • Identify fourth‑party dependencies and require transparency.
Example: fourth‑party risk
A software supplier uses an open‑source library maintained by a small project. If that library is compromised, your supplier may deliver vulnerable code. Request an SBOM and update procedures to mitigate exposure.
Key takeaway
Use a combined evidence approach: documented RTO/RPO, tested recoveries, financial checks, and SBOMs when software is involved.
