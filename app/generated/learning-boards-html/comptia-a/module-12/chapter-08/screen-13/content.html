Common Mistakes and Safety Notes
Recognize and correct practices that create false assurance or increased exposure.
Top mistakes
  1. Incomplete vendor inventory tied to BIA
  2. Relying only on certifications, not tests
  3. Vague contractual language for recovery
  4. Excluding vendors from exercises
Safety notes
  • Segregate and encrypt shared data with vendors
  • Limit and audit vendor access to systems
  • Keep test evidence and audit artifacts for compliance
Remediation actions
Prioritize high‑impact vendors for audits, demand SBOMs for critical software, and negotiate measurable SLAs with test obligations.
Key takeaway
Address the riskiest gap first—inventory, contract, or test evidence—to get the most immediate risk reduction.
