Key Concepts
A compact reminder of the chapter’s most important terms.
Supply Chain Resilience
Ability to anticipate, prepare for and respond to supplier disruptions.
Third‑Party Risk
Risk introduced by reliance on external vendors and services.
Fourth‑Party Risk
Risk coming from a vendor's own suppliers or subcontractors.
Summary takeaway
Integrate these concepts into inventories, contracts and tests so vendor resilience becomes measurable and auditable.
