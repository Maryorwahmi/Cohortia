Inventory Critical Vendors
A four-step, repeatable process to prioritize vendor risk for continuity planning.
Step 1 — Identify vendors
List cloud, SaaS, MSPs, hardware, logistics and niche suppliers tied to operations.
Step 2 — Map dependencies
Connect each vendor to specific business functions and required recovery times.
Step 3 — Classify criticality
Score impact and likelihood against organizational RTO/RPO to prioritize follow-up.
Step 4 — Tag risk attributes
Record vendor location, financial stability, certifications and use of sub‑contractors.
Example: CRM provider mapping
  • Dependencies: customer support, billing, sales analytics
  • RTO requirement: 4 hours for sales process continuity
  • Assessment action: verify provider RTO or prepare manual billing workaround
Pro tip

Keep the inventory lean by focusing on vendors that support your top business processes rather than every small contractor.
