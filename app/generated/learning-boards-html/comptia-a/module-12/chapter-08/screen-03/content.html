Practical assessment and compliance goals
Prepare the right questions and contractual language to verify vendor resilience.
Key assessment areas
  • Data backup and recovery practices
  • Regional redundancy and failover
  • Incident response and notification
Compliance evidence
Request SOC 2 or ISO reports and test results for recovery exercises. Certifications aid but don’t replace testing.
Practical mnemonic
Use a simple scorecard: Availability, Recovery targets, Audit rights, Testing frequency.
