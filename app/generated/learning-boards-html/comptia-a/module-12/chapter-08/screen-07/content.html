A concrete example clause documents recovery commitments and testing.
Mock SLA clause (illustrative)
Service Level Agreement - Disaster Recovery Clause

1. Recovery Time Objective (RTO): Vendor commits to restoring the critical service within 4 hours of a declared disaster.
2. Recovery Point Objective (RPO): Vendor commits to a maximum data loss of 1 hour for the critical service.
3. Backup & Recovery: Vendor shall maintain daily full backups and hourly incremental backups, stored encrypted and off-site. Test restores shall be performed quarterly.
4. Incident Notification: Vendor shall notify Customer within 30 minutes of detecting any incident impacting service availability or data integrity.
5. Audit Rights: Customer reserves the right to review Vendor's BCP/DRP documentation and audit its testing results annually.
Explanation and negotiation tips
  • Translate your BIA values into numeric RTO/RPO in the SLA.
  • Require encrypted off‑site backups with specified restore test frequency.
  • Insist on short incident notification windows and documented escalation paths.
  • Preserve audit rights or accept third‑party attestations with accessible reports.
Common mistake

Using vague language leaves your organization exposed; always require measurable commitments and evidence.
