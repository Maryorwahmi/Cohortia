Module 12 › Chapter 12.8 › Vendor Assessments
Questionnaires, Audits and Certifications
Collect both attestations and operational evidence to verify vendor resilience.
Questionnaire sample areas
  • Backup frequency and retention
  • Failover and multi‑region architecture
  • Incident response and notification timelines
Certifications vs evidence
Certifications show that controls exist; test reports and restore artifacts show those controls work in practice.
Pen testing & scans
For vendors with direct access to your systems, negotiate vulnerability assessments or review independent test results.
Example request
"Please provide the most recent quarterly restore test report for region X and details of backup encryption in transit and at rest."
Common error
Relying solely on a certification without reviewing recent test outcomes and the vendor's recovery timeline.
Key takeaway
Combine standardized questionnaires with artifacts and, where critical, independent verification.
