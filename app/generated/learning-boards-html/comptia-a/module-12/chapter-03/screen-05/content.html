Classification

Types of disasters

Natural disasters
Cause physical damage and connectivity loss; plan for relocation, hardware replacement, and secure transport of backups.
Accidental outages
Power loss or human error; emphasize restore automation and configuration management to reduce human recovery time.
Malicious incidents
Ransomware and breaches require forensic preservation, validation of backups, and careful reintroduction of systems.
Impact & response matrix
Natural — Example
A datacenter flood: secure backups offsite, arrange alternative hosting, verify restored hardware components before bringing services online.
Malicious — Example
Ransomware: preserve forensic copies, scan backups for malicious changes, and apply security patches before reconnecting to networks.
Accidental — Example
Accidental data deletion: follow documented restore steps and confirm data integrity and application compatibility after restore.
Pro tip
Include decision gates in your DRP that force forensic checks after suspected compromise before restoration.
