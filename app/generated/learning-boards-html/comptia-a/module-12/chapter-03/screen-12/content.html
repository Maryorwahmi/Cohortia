Process Flow

Network reconfiguration steps

  1. Establish secure admin connectivity and monitoring
  2. Apply minimal firewall rules for verification
  3. Restore backend services and validate internal connectivity
  4. Swap DNS and load balancers after successful verification
Example issue
Cutting over DNS too early exposed users to unverified services and increased outage impact.
Communications playbook
Internal: Roles, status cadence, escalation contacts.
Vendors: Recovery SLAs, support contact, and verification steps.
Customers: Impact notices, estimated timelines, and final restore confirmation.
Pro tip
Use templated messages and pre-authorized sign-offs for DNS and network cutovers to prevent miscoordination.
