Recovery site strategies

Hot Site
Fully equipped and mirrored, minimal failover time, highest cost.
Warm Site
Essential systems ready, requires configuration and data sync, moderate cost.
Cold Site
Facility with power/network only; hardware and install take time, lowest cost.
Cloud-based Recovery
Cloud can be configured for snapshot restore, pilot light, warm standby, or active-active depending on RTO/RPO and budget.
Example decision
Choose warm standby for a customer-facing application that needs a 4-hour RTO and 1-hour RPO to balance cost and speed.
Common mistake
Picking a strategy without service-level mapping leads to overspend or missed recovery targets.
Decision checklist
  • Map RTO/RPO per service
  • Estimate cost vs downtime impact
  • Confirm staff and automation for activation
  • Document secure connectivity and IAM
Key Takeaway
Match strategy to measured service needs, not to a single organizational preference.
