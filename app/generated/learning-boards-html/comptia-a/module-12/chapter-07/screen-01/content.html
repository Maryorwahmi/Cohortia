BCP / DRP Testing and Validation
Make plans operational: choose tests, measure results, and close the improvement loop.
In this lesson

You will learn test types, how to build a testing schedule aligned to RTO/RPO, define KPIs, run post-test analysis, and avoid common pitfalls that negate testing value.

Why it matters

Regular, realistic testing converts documents into reliable recovery capabilities and trains personnel to act under pressure.

Explore Key Topics
Test Types
Walkthroughs to full interruption tests, each with trade-offs in realism and risk.
KPIs
Metrics like RTO and RPO achievement measure whether recovery meets expectations.
Post-Test
Debrief, assign corrective actions, update plans, and retrain staff for continuous improvement.
Concept map
How tests, metrics, and post-test actions connect
Walkthrough
Discussion-based review for familiarity and obvious gaps.
Simulation
Partial system activation in test environments to validate procedures.
Parallel
Full recovery site activation while production continues to run.
Full Cutover
Highest realism and risk: primary systems intentionally shifted to recovery site.
Tip: Align test frequency and rigor with risk tolerance, regulatory needs, and available resources.
