Definition
KPIs for BCP/DRP tests
Specific metrics that prove whether recovery met targets and where improvements are needed.
• RTO Achievement: measured time from DRP declaration to verified service readiness.
• RPO Achievement: actual data loss measured against accepted RPO window.
• Documentation Accuracy: count of incorrect or missing steps in runbooks discovered during tests.
• Communication Effectiveness: time to notify stakeholders and percent confirmations received.
• Team Performance: task completion rates, coordination quality, and observer ratings.
Concrete example
If RTO is 4 hours and the measured recovery time is 6 hours, record ART=6h and assign corrective actions to reduce restore time to meet RTO.
