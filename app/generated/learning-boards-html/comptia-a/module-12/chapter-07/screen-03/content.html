Hands-on Objectives
Design scenarios, assign roles, secure resources, and avoid common pitfalls.
05 — Scenario Design
Build realistic, challenging scenarios tied to likely failures like power loss or storage faults. A good scenario reveals process and technical gaps.
Mnemonic: "Real, Specific, Measurable"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06 — Roles & Resources
Define facilitators, observers, participants, and list required environments, backups, and tools so a test runs smoothly and safely.
Small visual: timeline with Roles → Actions → Review
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07 — Communication KPIs
Measure notification speed and accuracy, escalation effectiveness, and stakeholder message clarity to ensure communications don't block recovery.
Mnemonic: Notify → Confirm → Act
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08 — Avoid Pitfalls
Guard against testing in isolation, unrealistic scenarios, and failing to act on findings. Executive buy-in prevents resource shortfalls.
