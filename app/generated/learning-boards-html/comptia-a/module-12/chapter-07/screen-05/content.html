Comparison
Walkthrough vs Tabletop
Low-cost review versus scenario-driven decision exercise.
• Walkthrough: document review, low setup, catches clerical and contact errors.
• Tabletop: scenario conversation, tests communication and command decisions under a plausible incident.
• Misconception: tabletop validates technical restores. It does not; simulations do that.
Example sequence
Start with a walkthrough to clean the plan. Run a tabletop to exercise roles and notifications. Then schedule a simulation for technical restores revealed as unclear.
Key Takeaway

Choose the exercise that reveals the class of gaps you care about: documents, decisions, or technical procedures.

Scenario card
Tabletop scenario: Regional power outage
Simulate loss of a primary data center for 8 hours. Participants run notification templates, escalate orders, and decide on partial service degradation. Record decisions and time-to-notify as communications KPIs.
What to measure
Notification latency, decision clarity, and documented owner responsibilities.
Follow-up
Use outcomes to update runbooks and schedule targeted simulations for any procedural gaps discovered.
