Common pitfalls
Why tests sometimes fail to improve readiness, and practical mitigations.
• Lack of realism — Mitigation: craft layered scenarios that force trade-offs.
• Check-the-box mentality — Mitigation: tie tests to KPIs and corrective actions.
• Insufficient scope — Mitigation: include business process and communications owners.
• No executive buy-in — Mitigation: present risk-aligned cadence and resource needs to leadership.
• Failure to act — Mitigation: assign owners and deadlines; verify fixes in follow-up tests.
Key Takeaway

Realistic, integrated tests with assigned corrective actions and leadership support produce lasting improvements.

Mitigation cards
Realism
Add realistic constraints and secondary failures to scenarios to force real decisions.
Integration
Include IR, BCP, DRP, and business teams so interdependencies are exercised.
Action
Track corrective actions with owners and verify them in follow-up tests.
No-blame culture: encourage honest reporting in hotwashes so corrective actions target root causes, not individuals.
