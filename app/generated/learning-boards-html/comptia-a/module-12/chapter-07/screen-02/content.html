Learning Objectives
Four competency milestones that map to testing, measurement, and improvement
01 — Differentiate Test Types
Recognize walkthroughs, tabletops, simulations, parallel tests, and full interruptions and when to use each. This matters because the wrong test wastes time or risks disruption.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Design a Testing Program
Create schedules, objectives, roles, and resources that align with risk tolerance and compliance. This ensures tests are repeatable and defensible to auditors and executives.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Define KPIs & Metrics
Select measurable indicators like RTO and RPO achievement, accuracy of documentation, and time to restore critical functions so tests yield objective evidence.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Post-Test Analysis & Improvement
Conduct hotwashes, document root causes, assign corrective actions with owners and deadlines, and update plans and training to close the loop.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
