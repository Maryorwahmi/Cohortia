Process
Hotwash and corrective action
Immediate debrief, root cause analysis, assigned actions, and verification steps.
• Hold a hotwash with all participants and observers immediately after the test.
• Document observations, categorize issues by severity, and analyze root causes.
• Create corrective actions with owner, priority, deadline, and verification method.
• Update runbooks and schedule retraining as required.
Example corrective action
Issue: DNS changes delayed recovery by 40 minutes. Action: create pre-staged DNS entries and automated TTL reduction. Owner: Network team. Target: 30 days. Verification: follow-up simulation.
Corrective action tracker (sample)
Action: Automate DB restore
Owner: DB team • Priority: High • Due: 30 days • Verify: Simulation
Action: Update notification templates
Owner: Comms • Priority: Medium • Due: 14 days • Verify: Tabletop
Avoid: long, ambiguous recommendations. Prefer small, verifiable actions with clear owners and dates.
