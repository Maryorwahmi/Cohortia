Container image security: scanning, trusted registries, and signing
Build, scan, sign, and enforce with admission policies.
Eyebrow: Comparison
Scanning
Use tools to detect CVEs during CI and fail builds with critical findings.
Trusted registries
Restrict who can push images, enforce mutability rules, and integrate with IAM for auditability.
Signing
Sign images in CI so clusters can verify provenance before admission.
Practical enforcement
Use an admission controller to deny unsigned images and integrate scanner results into your CI gating to stop vulnerable images from reaching production.
