Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › Core Capabilities
Process Flow
1
Asset discovery
Inventory across accounts and regions, with resource metadata and owners.
2
Policy evaluation
Continuous checks against CIS, NIST, PCI, GDPR policies and custom rules.
3
Identity checks
Detect over-privileged identities and unused entitlements for remediation.
Prioritization
Risk scoring and contextual data focus attention on what matters most, avoiding alert fatigue.
Explanation & Example

Imagine an environment where a new S3 bucket is created without encryption. The CSPM detects the missing encryption setting, links the resource to the creating identity, notes the affected policy, and assigns a risk score. That finding can feed into a remediation workflow or be routed to a developer for correction.

Takeaway
A CSPM's power is turning inventory and policy checks into prioritized actions and audit evidence.
Pitfall
Ignoring identity context leads to many low-value alerts about permissions that are actually unused or expected.
