Risk Scoring
Risk scoring weights findings by asset value, exploitability, and exposure. This reduces alert fatigue by highlighting the findings with the biggest potential business impact.
Example
A public database is scored higher than a dev-only public bucket because it contains sensitive data and is more attractive to attackers.
Attack Path Analysis
This models possible lateral movement across services and identities. It shows chained weaknesses an attacker could exploit to reach critical targets, helping prioritize fixes that break the chain.
Takeaway
Combine scoring and path analysis to prioritize remediation that most reduces real-world risk.
