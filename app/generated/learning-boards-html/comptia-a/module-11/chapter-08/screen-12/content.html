Module 11 › Chapter 11.8 › CIEM
What CIEM Does
  • Detects over-privileged and unused identities.
  • Provides permission reduction recommendations.
  • Helps enforce least privilege across accounts.
Operational Example
CIEM finds a machine identity with admin rights but low activity. It recommends downgrading the role and automates a review ticket. This reduces blast radius if the machine identity is compromised.
Pair CIEM with CSPM to reduce identity-related attack paths and improve prioritization.
