Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Key Concepts
Federation & SSO
Use IdP assertions to centralize authentication and reduce credential sprawl.
Least Privilege
Specify actions, resources, and conditions to minimize access scope.
Managed Identities
Use provider-managed roles for resources to avoid static keys and enable rotation.
Takeaway

Combine federation, least privilege, MFA, and managed identities with auditing to create a resilient IAM posture.
