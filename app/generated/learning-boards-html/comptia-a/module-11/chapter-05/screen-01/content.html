Cloud Identity and Access Management (IAM)
Secure who can access cloud resources, how they authenticate, and the conditions under which access is granted.
In this lesson

Goal
Secure programmatic access
Roles, managed identities, and rotation
Why it matters
A single misconfigured role or long‑lived key can expose data across services. Tying access to identity, MFA, and short-lived credentials reduces risk.
Start-up tip
Prioritize MFA on privileged accounts and convert static keys to managed identities first.
