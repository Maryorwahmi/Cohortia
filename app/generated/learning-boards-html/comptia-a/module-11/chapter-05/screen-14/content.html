Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Quick Reference
IAM
Controls identities, roles, policies, and access decisions.
MFA
Second factor enforcement for sensitive sessions and actions.
Audit Logs
Enable and review logs to detect policy changes and anomalous activity.
Study tip

When preparing for an assessment, map each scenario to these three concepts: who (IAM), how they authenticate (MFA/federation), and how you would detect misuse (audit logs).
