Multi-Factor Authentication
Add a second verification factor for privileged accounts to reduce the chance of account takeover.
Types
Virtual MFA apps, hardware tokens, and FIDO U2F keys.
Enforcement
Use IAM conditions to require MFA for sensitive actions or roles.
Pitfall
Do not block automation: use roles and managed identities for services instead of forcing human MFA where automation runs.
Key Takeaway

MFA should be mandatory for privileged accounts, enforced in policy, and combined with least privilege and monitoring.
