Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Least Privilege
Principle of Least Privilege
Limit permissions to necessary actions and resources and use conditions to tighten access.
Practical rules
  • Prefer specific actions over wildcards.
  • Scope resources by ARN or tags, not "*".
  • Use conditions for MFA, IP, or tag checks.
Analogy
Give someone a key to one drawer, not the whole filing cabinet.
Example
Grant ec2:StartInstances and ec2:StopInstances only on instances tagged Environment:Development; deny other EC2 actions.
Common mistake
Giving broad * permissions for expedience and never revisiting them later.
Key Takeaway

Iteratively narrow permissions using action, resource, and condition constraints to minimize exposure while preserving required functionality.
