Summary Key Concepts
What to Remember
Three compact concept cards for quick review.
Shared Responsibility
Provider secures runtime and infrastructure; you secure code, IAM, data, and configuration.
Input Validation
Assume all event data is hostile. Validate early and bind parameters to prevent injection.
Secrets Manager
Store secrets centrally, grant scoped access, and avoid embedding keys in code.
Summary

When you secure inputs, restrict access, and manage secrets, you dramatically reduce the attack surface of serverless applications.
