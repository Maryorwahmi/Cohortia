Shared responsibility in serverless
A clear boundary defines who secures what.
Provider responsibilities
  • Physical hosts, hypervisor, and runtime patching.
  • Scaling, sandboxing, and underlying OS isolation.
  • Network virtualization and provider-managed networking.
Customer responsibilities
  • Function code, dependencies, and secure configuration.
  • IAM policies, secrets retrieval, and input validation.
  • Logging, monitoring, and incident response instrumentation.
Analogy and why it matters
Think of the provider as the apartment manager who locks the building and provides utilities. You are the tenant responsible for what you keep inside your unit. If you leave a window open, the manager will not fix it.
Key Takeaway

Design defense at the function boundary: validate inputs, restrict permissions, and centralize logs for short-lived compute.
