Quick Reference Operational checklist
Three compact reminders
Use this list during design and review.
Centralized Logs
Export invocation logs and traces to a central store for detection and forensics.
Scoped IAM
Grant only required actions and specific resource ARNs to function roles.
Protect the API
Enable WAF, schema validation, throttling, and authorizers at the gateway.
Final question

For each function you manage, can you point to its secret store, its least-privilege policy, and the logs you'd use to investigate a compromise?
