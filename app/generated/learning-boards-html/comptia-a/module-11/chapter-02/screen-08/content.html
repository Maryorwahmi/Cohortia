Customer duties for SaaS

Your responsibilities typically focus on identities, authentication, data governance, and per-tenant configuration inside the SaaS application.

Identity & Access
Configure MFA, role assignments, and least privilege for all users.
Data Governance
Classify and enforce retention, sharing, and encryption policies for data within the app.
Concrete example

Migrating email to Microsoft 365 means Microsoft maintains servers and patches; you must enforce MFA, configure mailbox permissions, and set retention policies to meet compliance.

Common Mistake

Assuming provider oversight removes the need for strong tenant-level authentication and permission management.

Key Takeaway

Even with SaaS, the customer must own identity hygiene and data governance to prevent account-based breaches.
