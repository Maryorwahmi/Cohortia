Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Definition
Security of the cloud

The CSP secures physical facilities, host hardware, networking, hypervisor, and service-level controls. This is the provider's operational perimeter to keep the cloud resilient and available.

Examples
Data center physical access controls, hypervisor patching, network isolation between tenants.
Misconception check
Do not assume the provider configures tenant policies for you; those remain your job unless explicitly managed.
Security in the cloud

Customer responsibility for data, OS, applications, network configuration, IAM policies, encryption keys, and logging. These are the controls you must own and operate for your workload.

Concrete example
If an S3 bucket is publicly writable due to bucket policies, the customer is responsible for correcting the policy and auditing permissions.
Key controls
  • OS patching and hardening
  • Application security testing
  • IAM, MFA, and least privilege
  • Encryption and data classification
Key Takeaway

Always read the provider's shared responsibility details for each service you use and treat the 'in cloud' items as your operational controls to manage and prove.
