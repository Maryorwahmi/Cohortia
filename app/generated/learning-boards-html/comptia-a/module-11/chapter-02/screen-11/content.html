Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Audit & Compliance
Data classification and controls

Identify sensitive datasets, apply encryption and retention policies, and restrict access according to classification.

Steps
  1. Inventory data locations and sensitivity
  2. Apply encryption and access policies based on classification
  3. Periodically review and remediate exceptions
Logging & evidence

Enable tenant-level access logs, centralize them, and preserve integrity for audit and incident response.

Practical Tip
Automate log collection into an immutable store and link logs to change tickets and alerts for traceability.
Key Takeaway

To pass audits, show your data classification, the tenant-level logs you retain, and your incident response actions tied to those logs.
