Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › IAM
Principles
  • Least privilege for users and roles
  • MFA for privileged and user accounts
  • Use ephemeral credentials and service roles
  • Automated permission reviews
Example incident

A SaaS administrator account without MFA was phished and used to download sensitive reports. The account had broad export permissions. Responsibility for enforcing MFA and limiting that account's scope rests with the customer.

Mitigation
Require MFA, limit export permissions, and create a delegated role for administrative tasks with session duration limits.
Audit
Enable and retain access logs to track who assumed roles and what actions were performed for forensic analysis and compliance.
Key Takeaway

Treat IAM as a primary control: enforce MFA, minimize privilege, and log all privileged actions to reduce compromise impact.
