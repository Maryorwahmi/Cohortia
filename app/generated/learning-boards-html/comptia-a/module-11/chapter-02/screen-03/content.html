Assessment Focus & Pitfalls
Practical evidence, common errors, and remedial steps.
Bloom: Apply → Master
Audit S3 and storage

Check bucket policies, block public access, and run automated checks to detect public objects.

Verify network rules

Inspect security groups and NACLs to ensure no production ports are exposed to 0.0.0.0/0 without a business justification.

Confirm patching and SDDLC

Ensure OS patching schedules, vulnerability scans, and SAST/DAST pipelines protect customer code in cloud-hosted services.

Common Mistake

Assuming the CSP handles guest OS patching or application-level access within managed services.

Pro Tip

Document responsibilities in contracts and map controls to owners during onboarding to prevent gaps.
