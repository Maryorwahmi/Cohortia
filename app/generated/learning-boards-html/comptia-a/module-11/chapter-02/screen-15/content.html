Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › What to Remember
Rule 1: Own configurations
If you configure it, you must secure and document it.
Rule 2: Automate defaults
Block public resources and schedule patching across accounts.
Rule 3: Verify providers
Request shared responsibility matrices and confirm SLAs and notification procedures.
Final takeaway

These practical rules reduce misconfiguration risk and make it easier to prove that customer-side controls are in place and effective.
