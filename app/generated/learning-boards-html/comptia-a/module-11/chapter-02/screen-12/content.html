Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Incident Response
Incident playbook essentials
  • Define owner for tenant-level remediation
  • Preserve evidence and capture logs
  • Communicate with CSP per contract procedures
  • Run post-incident reviews and remediation
Vendor due diligence checklist
Ask for the shared responsibility matrix, incident notification timelines, log access, and proof of relevant certifications. Confirm remediation responsibilities in writing.
Key Takeaway
Contracts and playbooks must make responsibilities and notification procedures explicit so you can act quickly when incidents occur.
