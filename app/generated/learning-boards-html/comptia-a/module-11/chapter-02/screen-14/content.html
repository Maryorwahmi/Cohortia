Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Key Concepts
Shared Responsibility
Clear division: provider secures infrastructure; customer secures deployments.
Service Model Shift
Responsibility decreases for the customer as you move from IaaS → PaaS → SaaS.
Auditability
Inventory, automate, log, and document ownership to support audits and incidents.
Summary

Keep the model visible. Know which controls you must own, automate them, and prove them with logs and documentation.
