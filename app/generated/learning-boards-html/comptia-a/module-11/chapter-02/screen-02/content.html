Learning Objectives
From explanation to validated controls
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain the model across service types

Be able to state what CSPs secure and what customers secure for IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS, and why those boundaries matter.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify owner of specific controls

Map tasks like OS patching, bucket policies, and network rules to CSP or customer ownership using concrete justifications.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Analyze shifts by service model

Understand why responsibility moves from customer to provider as you adopt higher-level services and how to adapt controls accordingly.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design customer-side strategies

Plan IAM, patching, audit logs, and incident response so customer obligations are demonstrably met during audits and incidents.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
