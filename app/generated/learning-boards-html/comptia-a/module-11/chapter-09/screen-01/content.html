Cloud Incident Response & Forensics
Adapting IR workflows to ephemeral, API-driven cloud systems and protecting evidence at scale.

Why this matters

Incidents in cloud environments can escalate quickly because APIs and automation enable broad access. Planning ahead reduces impact and preserves evidence.

Who owns what

The shared responsibility model changes what data you can collect. Know provider vs customer boundaries before you need to investigate.
