Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › Eradication & Recovery
Recovery pattern
Snapshot and preserve: retain evidence before rebuilding.
Rebuild from image: deploy a new instance from hardened images under automated deployment.
Validate: perform integrity and security checks before returning to production.
Image provenance
Maintain signed, versioned images in a trusted registry. If the supply chain is in doubt, rebuild images from source and re-run your CI/CD validation steps.
Key Takeaway
Immutable recovery reduces uncertainty and accelerates safe restoration of services.
