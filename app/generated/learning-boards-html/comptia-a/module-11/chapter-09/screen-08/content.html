Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › Playbooks & Break Glass
Playbook skeleton
Incident trigger: triggered alert or manual report with severity and affected resources.
Immediate actions: isolate resource, revoke credentials, create snapshots.
Evidence steps: export logs, record snapshot IDs, tag artifacts with incident metadata.
Escalation: provider support contact, legal, and classified reporting.
Break‑glass controls
  • Store emergency credentials in a hardened vault with multi-person approval.
  • Make credentials time-limited and rotate immediately after use.
  • Audit every action performed using break‑glass credentials and attach audit IDs to the incident record.
Avoid
Using shared, un-audited emergency accounts. That destroys accountability and complicates investigations.
