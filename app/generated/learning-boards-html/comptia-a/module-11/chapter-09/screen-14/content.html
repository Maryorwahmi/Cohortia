Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › Mistakes & Safeguards
Common mistakes
  • Insufficient or scattered logging.
  • Making live changes before snapshotting.
  • Unclear shared responsibility assumptions.
  • Untested emergency access and playbooks.
  • Unprotected forensic artifacts.
Practical safeguards
Automate snapshotting on high-risk alerts.
Use encrypted, versioned storage and tag artifacts consistently.
Run regular tabletop exercises and break‑glass drills to validate your playbooks.
