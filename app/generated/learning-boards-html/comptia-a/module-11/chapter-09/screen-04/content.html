Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › Shared Responsibility
What it means

The provider secures the cloud; you secure in the cloud. Access to low-level host artifacts is restricted, so investigations must use exposed APIs and managed logging services.

Provider responsibilities
Physical hosts, hypervisor security, and managed services' underlying platform health.
Customer responsibilities
Guest OS, applications, IAM, data, and logging configurations needed for forensics.
Key Takeaway

Don’t assume host access. Predefine what logs and artifacts you can collect and whom to contact for provider-side evidence.

Practical mapping
Audit services
CloudTrail, Activity Logs, Audit Logs are your primary provider-facing sources for API-level events.
Network flows
VPC Flow Logs and equivalents give network context when you cannot access the host.
Coordination example
If you need host-level logs the provider may require a support engagement. Document these escalation paths in your playbook so evidence access is fast.
Common misconception: "I can get hypervisor memory or host forensic images." In most public clouds that is not available to customers; plan accordingly.
