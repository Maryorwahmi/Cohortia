Module 12 › Chapter 11.9 › Cloud Forensics
Artifact collection
APIs and CLIs: use official APIs to export logs and create snapshots so collection is auditable.
Metadata: record snapshot IDs, hashes, collector identity, and timestamps.
Storage: keep artifacts in encrypted, versioned buckets with restricted access.
Legal note
Be aware of jurisdiction and provider policy limits. Engage legal early if evidence may cross regions or accounts.
Chain of custody record
Keep a chronological record that includes who collected each artifact, the method, storage location, and hash. This makes forensic results defensible in internal reviews or legal processes.
Key Takeaway
Treat cloud artifacts like physical evidence: collect, record, protect, and never alter the original copy.
