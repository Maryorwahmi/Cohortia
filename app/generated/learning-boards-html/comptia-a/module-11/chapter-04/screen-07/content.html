Definition

Messaging & Queue Security

Queues must be treated as sensitive resources: control who writes and reads messages, encrypt data, and manage message lifecycle.

Controls checklist
  • Strict IAM or queue-level ACLs for producers and consumers.
  • Encryption at rest and TLS for transport.
  • Short retention windows for sensitive data, and application-layer encryption where necessary.
Key Takeaway

Protect queues both at the platform level and in your application logic to prevent unintended disclosure or abuse.

Scenario

An SQS queue receives messages containing order data. Producers have a policy allowing only s3:PutObject to a specific bucket and sqs:SendMessage to this queue. Consumers have a narrow policy allowing only sqs:ReceiveMessage and sqs:DeleteMessage on the queue ARN. Retention is set to the minimum required by business policy.

Common mistake

Granting broad 'sqs:*' or making queues publicly readable. Even with encryption, overly broad permissions let attackers manipulate or read messages through impersonation.
