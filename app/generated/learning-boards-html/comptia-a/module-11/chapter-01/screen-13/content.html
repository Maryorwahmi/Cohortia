Common Cloud Risks and Mitigation Strategies

Identify repeatable cloud failures and apply prioritized automated mitigations.
Top risks
  • Over-permissioned IAM and service accounts.
  • Open or misconfigured network security groups and endpoints.
  • Lack of centralized logging and visibility across clouds.
Concrete Example
An app role granted broad storage permissions led to accidental exposure; remedy: apply scoped roles and automated policy checks.
Common Mistake
Relying on manual inspections rather than automated posture management as environments scale.
Mitigation playbook
- Define secure IaC modules and enforce them via pipeline gates.
- Implement least-privilege IAM and regularly review service principals.
- Centralize logs, use automated detection rules, and remediate drift automatically.
Key Takeaway
Prioritize automation, least-privilege, segmentation, and centralized telemetry to reduce cloud risk at scale.
