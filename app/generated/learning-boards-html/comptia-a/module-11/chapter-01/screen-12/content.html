Service Model Trade-offs: Control vs. Operational Overhead

Decide which model aligns with your need for control, compliance, and ops capacity.
IaaS
High control, high responsibility: you manage OS and network.
PaaS
Less infra work, focus on app security and config.
SaaS
Vendor manages almost everything; you control identity and data policies.
FaaS
Automatic scaling, but require secure code and fine-grained permissions.
Decision guidance
  • Pick IaaS for legacy or deep-control needs.
  • Choose PaaS for developer velocity with app-layer security controls.
  • Use SaaS for standard business functions with strong vendor governance.
  • Adopt FaaS for event-driven scale when you can secure functions and APIs.
Key Takeaway
Service model selection shifts where security work happens. Align your choice with control needs and operational capacity.
