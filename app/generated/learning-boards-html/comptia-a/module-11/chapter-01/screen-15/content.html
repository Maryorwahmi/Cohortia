Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Key Concepts
Public Cloud
Shared, provider-managed infrastructure; customer secures data and configuration.
Private Cloud
Dedicated infrastructure for one organization with greater control and operational responsibility.
Hybrid Cloud
Interoperable private and public clouds with unified security policies and secure connectivity.
What to Remember
Match deployment and service models to data sensitivity, compliance, and your operational capacity; automate policy enforcement for consistency.
