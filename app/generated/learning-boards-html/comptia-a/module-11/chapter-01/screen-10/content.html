Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › SaaS
Definition

SaaS — vendor-managed applications and customer responsibilities

Provider manages app and infra; customer focuses on identity, permissions, data governance, and vendor assurance.
Customer focus areas
  • Strong identity and access controls, including MFA.
  • Data classification and in-app permissions.
  • Vendor risk assessments and contractual compliance guarantees.
Concrete Example
Using hosted email: enable enterprise SSO with MFA and set DLP policies to prevent exfiltration of sensitive attachments.
Common Mistake
Assuming provider handles tenant-level permissions and not auditing or limiting internal user access appropriately.
Vendor evaluation checklist
- Verify provider certifications and data residency options.
- Confirm backup, retention, and incident response commitments contractually.
- Enforce SSO, MFA, and least-privilege roles for users.
Key Takeaway
SaaS reduces operational burden but requires strong identity and vendor risk controls to protect data and access.
