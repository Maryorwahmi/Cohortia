Definition › Compliance Monitoring

Compliance Dashboard

Dashboards show aggregate compliance across providers, broken down by standard, account, and resource type. They display pass/fail counts and offer drilldowns to affected assets and remediation steps.
Exportable Evidence
Reports should include timestamps, account context, and policy versions for audit reproducibility.
Custom Frameworks
Ability to map internal policies and regulatory controls, not just vendor presets.

Example: Audit Ready

A CSPM report shows 95 percent compliance with a CIS benchmark. The remediation column lists resources, owner contacts, and suggested fixes. Security and compliance teams use the report to prepare an audit package with evidence attachments and timestamps.
Gotcha
Treat CSPM output as evidence, not the only control. Maintain change control and validate that remediations were applied.
Key Takeaway

Use CSPM reports to accelerate audits, but complement them with validated operational controls and retention policies.
