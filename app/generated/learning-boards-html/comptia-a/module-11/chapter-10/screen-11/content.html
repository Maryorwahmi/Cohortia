Definition › Containers & Serverless

Container Protections

  • Image scanning in registries and CI/CD.
  • Admission control to block insecure images or misconfigurations.
  • Runtime monitoring for process and network anomalies in pods.

Serverless Protections

  • Dependency scanning to detect supply-chain vulnerabilities.
  • Execution monitoring to detect anomalous function behavior.
  • Role and permission auditing to enforce least privilege.
Key Takeaway

Cover the full lifecycle: scan images and dependencies before deployment, enforce admission controls, and monitor runtime behavior for ephemeral cloud-native workloads.
