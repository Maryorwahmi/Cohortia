Core Concept › What is CSPM?

CSPM Definition

CSPM tools continuously ingest cloud configurations and validate them against known best practices and compliance frameworks.

Configuration Auditing
Detects public storage, weak IAM policies, open security groups, and unencrypted resources.
Compliance Mapping
Maps findings to CIS, PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, and other frameworks for reporting.
Prioritization
Integrates asset criticality and threat context to rank issues.

Concrete Example

A CSPM scan finds three buckets in an AWS account marked public that contain backups. The CSPM maps this to CIS controls and flags the assets as high risk because they contain customer data. The report lists accounts, owners, and suggested remediation steps such as disabling public access and enabling bucket encryption.
Common Misconception
CSPM is not a runtime defender — it won't stop a process from executing. It reduces risk by fixing misconfigurations before they are abused.
Key Takeaway

Use CSPM for continuous configuration assurance and compliance evidence, and pair it with runtime protections for full coverage.
