Securing Cloud Infrastructure (IaaS)
Practical defenses for compute, storage, and network layers you control
What you'll understand

How to harden instances, enforce patching and least privilege, secure object and block storage with encryption and policies, and design VPCs, subnets, and controls that limit lateral movement.

Who benefits

Security architects, operations engineers, and CASP+ practitioners who design and maintain secure IaaS environments.

Explore Key Topics
Compute Hardening
Choose hardened images, remove services, enforce SSH/RDP controls and automated patching.
Storage Controls
Encrypt at rest and in transit, block public access, use KMS and lifecycle policies.
Network Security
Design VPCs and subnets, secure with security groups and NACLs, and use NAT for private instances.
