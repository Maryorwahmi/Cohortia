Module 11 › Chapter 11.3 › Patch Management
Patch Process
1. Inventory & Assess
Identify instances and criticality of installed software.
2. Test in Staging
Apply patches to a mirror environment and run health checks.
3. Rollout & Monitor
Staged deployment with canaries and alerting for regressions.
4. Verify & Audit
Confirm success, record outcomes, and update baselines.
Concrete Example

Use a cloud patch manager to create a patch baseline, test it on a staging instance, then approve it for production. Collect patch reports and feed them into your centralized logging for verification.

Common Mistake

Scheduling broad, mandatory reboots during business hours without canaries or health checks can cause outages.

Key Takeaway

Treat patching as a lifecycle that includes testing, staged rollout, and verification to keep systems secure and available.
