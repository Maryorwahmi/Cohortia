Instance Hardening
Secure base images, disable services, enforce SSH/RDP best practices.
Encryption & Storage Policy
Require encryption at rest and in transit; block public access and scope IAM policies.
Network Segmentation
Use VPCs, subnets, security groups, and NAT to isolate tiers and limit exposure.
Summary
Combine hardened instances, encrypted and access-controlled storage, and layered network controls. Validate designs through testing and logging to maintain a secure IaaS posture.
