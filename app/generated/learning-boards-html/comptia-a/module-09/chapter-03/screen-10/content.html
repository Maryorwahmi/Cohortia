Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Prioritizing Findings
Three lenses
Severity
Tool-assigned rating and technical scope.
Exploitability
How easily an attacker can use the flaw.
Business Impact
Which assets or users are affected and potential consequences.
Triage checklist
  • Reproduce the finding and collect evidence for the ticket.
  • Estimate exploit complexity and likely attacker motivation.
  • Assign priority and remediation owner; include retest criteria.
Concrete prioritization example
Two alerts: a medium-severity static issue in a rarely used utility, and a low-severity DAST-authenticated injection affecting transaction processing. Prioritize the DAST finding because it is exploitable in a critical business flow despite the lower static severity score.
Common pitfall
Avoid blind reliance on tool scores; add context and human judgment to avoid wasting engineering time on low-impact findings.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Prioritize fixes by exploitability and business impact, not by scanner score alone, and always attach evidence for verification.
