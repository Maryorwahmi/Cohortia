Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Common Mistakes
Top mistakes
  • Relying on one testing approach for all threats.
  • Failing to retest after remediation or deploy fixes without verification.
  • Poor triage that treats all findings equally and wastes engineering time.
Remediation loop
  1. Detect (automated + manual)
  2. Validate (reproduce, collect evidence)
  3. Prioritize (exploitability + impact)
  4. Remediate (owner, tests, timeline)
  5. Verify (retest and close)
Operational fixes
  • Tag and track false positives; tune rules rather than ignoring them.
  • Automate regression tests for fixed issues to prevent recurrence.
  • Use a clear SLA for high-priority remediation to maintain momentum.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Triage and verification steps are as important as detection—build processes that turn findings into verified, tested fixes.
