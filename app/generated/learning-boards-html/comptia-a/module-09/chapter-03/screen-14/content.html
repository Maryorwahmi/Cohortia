Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Key Concepts
AST is multi-method
Combine SAST, DAST, IAST, RASP, fuzzing, and pen tests to find diverse vulnerabilities.
Shift left and schedule
Run SAST on commit, IAST in QA, and DAST post-build to embed security in CI/CD.
Actionable remediation
Validate, prioritize, assign, and verify fixes; automated tests prevent regressions.
Summary takeaway
Tools find issues; processes and human judgment turn those findings into lasting reductions in risk.
