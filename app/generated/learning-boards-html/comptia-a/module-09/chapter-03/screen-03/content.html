Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Skill Outcomes & Practice

What you will be able to do

  • Translate tool findings into prioritized remediation tickets based on exploitability and impact.
  • Design a CI/CD test flow that runs SAST on commit, IAST during QA, and DAST after staging builds.
  • Run a basic DAST scan, interpret alerts, and verify fixes with follow-up scans.
  • Decide when to escalate to manual penetration testing or specialized fuzzing campaigns.

Practice checkpoints

Checkpoint 1
Run SAST locally and interpret one true positive and one false positive.
Checkpoint 2
Perform an automated DAST scan, inspect the alerts, and plan remediation for one critical finding.
Checkpoint 3
Integrate a SAST check into a commit hook or CI job and confirm it blocks a known vulnerable commit.
