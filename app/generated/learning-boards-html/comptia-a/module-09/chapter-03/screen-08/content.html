Penetration Testing Workflow

  1. Scoping and rules of engagement: define targets and permitted tests.
  2. Reconnaissance: gather surface area and possible entry points.
  3. Exploit development: chain issues and attempt to prove impact safely.
  4. Reporting: evidence, remediation suggestions, and impact analysis.
Example scenario
Tester finds a logic flaw in a role-assignment API, chains it with an unvalidated redirect, and demonstrates data access beyond intended privileges. Result: prioritized fixes and re-testing.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Pen testing provides realistic exploit proofs and impact context—use it to validate defenses and inform remediation priorities.
