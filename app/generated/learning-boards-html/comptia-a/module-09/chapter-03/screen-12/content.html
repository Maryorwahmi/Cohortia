Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › OWASP ZAP Lab
Step-by-step
  1. Start your local app and confirm the base URL and port.
  2. Open OWASP ZAP and choose Automated Scan; enter the app URL and attack.
  3. Watch the Sites tree populate; review Alerts for detected issues.
  4. Use Spider or Ajax Spider to discover more routes, then re-run Active Scan on new URLs.
  5. For authenticated flows, configure credentials so ZAP can exercise protected pages.
Inspecting results
  • Open an alert to view description, evidence, and remediation guidance.
  • Capture request/response pairs as evidence for tickets.
  • Prioritize findings by exploitability and affected features, not just risk score.
Practice goal
Find a reflected XSS or insecure header, collect the alert details, and plan a fix and retest.
