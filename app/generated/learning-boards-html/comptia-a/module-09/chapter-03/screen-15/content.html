Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Quick Checklist
Instrument & Test
Enable SAST, run IAST during tests, and DAST post-build for continuous coverage.
Verify & Prioritize
Reproduce alerts and weigh exploitability and business impact before fixing.
Automate Prevention
Convert verified fixes into CI checks to prevent regressions.
Keep a single question in mind
Will this change reduce real, demonstrable risk now and prevent it from coming back later?
