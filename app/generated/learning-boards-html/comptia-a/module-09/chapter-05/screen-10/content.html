Module 09 › Chapter 9.5 › Access Controls & Auditing
Principle of least privilege
Grant only necessary permissions and revoke them when no longer needed.
Role-Based Access Control
Assign permissions to roles to simplify management and limit accidental exposure.
Multi-factor authentication
Add strong authentication for sensitive roles and administrative actions.
Auditing and logging
Record access events, retain logs according to policy, and protect logs from tampering for incident response and compliance.
Example
Payroll service account with minimal DB roles; all access logged to immutable storage and reviewed monthly.
Key Takeaway
Combine least privilege, RBAC, strong authentication, and robust logging to control and verify access to sensitive data.
