Module 09 › Chapter 9.5 › Categories & Handling
Public
Freely distributed information
Example: marketing collateral or press releases. Protect availability and integrity; strong confidentiality is not required.
Internal / General Use
Internal team documents
Example: meeting notes or project plans with low sensitivity. Use standard access controls and periodic review.
Confidential
Business-critical or sensitive
Example: financial reports or customer lists. Require encryption, tighter RBAC, and audit logging.
Restricted / Highly Confidential
PII, PHI, trade secrets
Example: patient medical history or social security numbers. These require the highest protections: strong encryption, strict logging, minimal access, and often regional storage constraints.
Controls
  • Encryption at rest & in transit
  • Granular RBAC and MFA
  • Strong auditing and retention rules
Pitfall
Over-classifying everything as restricted wastes budget and complicates operations.
Key Takeaway
Use labels to simplify decisions: each label must have documented handling rules so teams apply protections consistently.
