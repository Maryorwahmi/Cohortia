Module 09 › Chapter 9.5 › Key Concepts
Data Classification
Organize data into labels that determine handling and controls.
State-based Protections
At rest, in transit, and in use each require different controls like TDE, TLS, and TEEs.
Residency & Sovereignty
Where data is stored determines legal obligations; design regions and replication accordingly.
Summary
Classify, pick state-appropriate protections, and respect regional rules. Layered controls and clear policies are the path to compliance and risk reduction.
