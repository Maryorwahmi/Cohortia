Module 09 › Chapter 9.5 › Cloud Storage & Regions
Key Management Options
  • Provider-managed keys: simple, less operational overhead
  • Customer-managed keys: more control, separation of duties
Region & replication
Choose storage regions that align with legal constraints and verify that replication, snapshots, and backups do not cross prohibited borders.
Example
Store restricted health data in compliant regions, use customer-managed keys, and restrict replication to the same jurisdiction.
Key Takeaway
Cloud storage must be chosen with region and key ownership in mind to meet residency and sovereignty obligations.
