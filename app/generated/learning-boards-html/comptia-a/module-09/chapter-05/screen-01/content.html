Module 09 › Chapter 9.5 › Overview
Data Classifications and Protection Strategies
A practical guide to labeling data, choosing controls for different data states, and meeting residency requirements.
In this lesson

Learn classification categories, protections for data at rest, in transit, and in use, and how residency shapes storage decisions.

Why it matters

Without classification, security spending is inefficient and risky. Proper labels reduce risk and ensure compliance.

Key takeaway
Label your data first. Then apply controls appropriate to each label and state — that sequence prevents misapplied effort and compliance gaps.
