Data Residency and Sovereignty
Where you place data can change the legal obligations you must follow. Design architectures with jurisdictional constraints in mind.
Operational steps
  • Choose compliant regions for restricted data
  • Limit cross-region replication
  • Audit storage and backups for location
Legal steps
  • Document legal drivers for region choices
  • Engage legal counsel for cross-border transfers
Key Takeaway
Treat residency and sovereignty as requirements when classifying and storing data; verify replication and backups do not violate regional rules.
