Module 9 › Chapter 9.4 › Assessment Scenario: SBOM in Incident Response
Assessment Idea

Scenario walk-through

When a critical zero-day is disclosed in a dependency, an SBOM lets you perform precise queries to identify affected artifacts by name, version, and hash. Use automation to map SBOM entries to deployed services and prioritize remediation. Signed artifacts and CI logs help confirm whether a build included the vulnerable component and who initiated it.
Two limitations
SBOMs can be stale if not generated per build, and they may miss transitive data if tools are misconfigured. Always generate SBOMs in CI and validate their completeness.
Key Takeaway
An SBOM paired with artifact signatures and CI logs enables fast, auditable incident response and targeted remediation.
