Common Mistakes

Where teams typically fail

  • Only scanning direct dependencies and ignoring transitive chains.
  • No SBOM or outdated inventory, causing slow incident response.
  • Poorly managed signing keys and long-lived build credentials.
  • Relying on a single control rather than layered defenses.
Mini exercise
Check one recent production build for an SBOM, a signature, and a CI audit log. If any are missing, list the steps required to add them.
Mitigation checklist
  1. Generate SBOMs for every build.
  2. Enable transitive dependency scanning.
  3. Limit and rotate pipeline credentials.
  4. Sign artifacts and verify on deploy.
Key Takeaway
A single missed control can lead to widespread compromise; make your defenses layered and auditable.
