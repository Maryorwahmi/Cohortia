What to remember
Data Classification
Categorize data, assign handling rules, and integrate labels into enforcement systems.
Encryption & Key Mgmt
Protect data at rest and in motion; keys must be securely managed and auditable.
DLP & PETs
Prevent exfiltration and use PETs where analytics demand privacy-preserving computation.
Summary Takeaway
Classify first, then apply layered controls and governance. Align technical measures with legal requirements and operational needs for effective and sustainable data protection.
