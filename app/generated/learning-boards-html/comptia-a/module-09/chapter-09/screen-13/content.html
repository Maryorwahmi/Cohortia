Process Flow Data Privacy Compliance
Turning regulation into technical requirements
GDPR — Rights & DPIAs
Support erasure, portability, access rights; run DPIAs for high-risk processing and document legal bases for processing.
CCPA — Consumer Controls
Maintain opt-out mechanisms, transparency on data uses, and record disclosures to third parties; support deletion requests.
HIPAA — Healthcare Safeguards
Implement access controls, encryption where appropriate, audit logging, and breach notification procedures for PHI.
Practical Steps for Erasure & Portability
  • Inventory all storage locations, including backups and logs.
  • Implement secure deletion or cryptographic erasure procedures and document timelines.
  • Provide secure export endpoints that deliver data in machine-readable formats with authentication and integrity checks.
