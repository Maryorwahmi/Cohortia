Architecture In-Transit Encryption
TLS, IPsec, and VPN patterns
TLS Best Practices
  • Prefer TLS 1.3 and disable legacy protocol versions and weak ciphers.
  • Automate certificate issuance and renewal to prevent outages and risk.
  • Use mutual TLS for strong service-to-service authentication where needed.
  • Example: enforce HTTPS with HSTS and monitor for certificate changes or downgrade attempts.
IPsec and VPNs
IPsec secures site-to-site communications at the network layer. VPNs protect remote users; use client certificates, MFA, and split-tunnelling policies carefully to reduce risk.
Misconceptions & Checks
  • Network segmentation alone is not encryption; assume packets may traverse untrusted paths.
  • VPN without application-layer encryption is insufficient against endpoint compromise.
  • Certificate pinning adds protection but increases operational overhead; test rollouts carefully.
Key Takeaway

Encrypt and authenticate all transport paths for sensitive data; manage certificates and cipher suites centrally and proactively.
