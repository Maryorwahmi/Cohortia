Process Flow Classification Policy Steps
A pragmatic process to scale classification
Follow an inventory-to-automation approach and keep policy review cycles short.
Step 1 — Discover & Inventory
Find all data stores, logs, backups and endpoints. Record owners and formats.
Step 2 — Map to Impact & Law
Decide which data types need stricter controls based on business and regulatory risk.
Step 3 — Define Handling Rules
Specify storage, encryption, access controls, monitoring, and retention for each class.
Step 4 — Integrate & Automate
Enforce labels via DLP, IAM, cloud policies and backup rules; automate classification where feasible.
Step 5 — Test & Review
Run scenarios, tune policies to reduce false positives, and schedule periodic audits.
Example Policy Snippet (Restricted)
"Restricted data must be stored exclusively on approved encrypted storage. Access requires MFA and least privilege. Transmission outside corporate network is allowed only via approved end-to-end encrypted channels. Retention limited to legal requirement; disposal follows secure media sanitization guidance."
Check Your Understanding
Where would you place the following: customer PII, marketing lists, internal build artifacts? Draft handling rules for the one you labeled 'Restricted'.
Key Takeaway

Make classification actionable: labels must directly trigger enforcement and review processes.
