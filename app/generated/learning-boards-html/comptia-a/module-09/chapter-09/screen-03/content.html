Learning Objectives — Continued
Decisions and trade-offs that make controls usable and enforceable.
Practical Milestone
Produce a short policy snippet for 'Restricted' data covering storage, access, transmission, and retention aligned with technical controls.
Avoid Over-Classification
Over-labeling creates friction; keep classes meaningful and documented with examples to guide users.
Tune DLP
Start with high-value patterns and iteratively refine rules to reduce false positives and align with incident workflows.
Key Management Trade-offs
Customer-managed keys give control; provider-managed simplify operations. Document threat model and SLA expectations.
PETs for Analytics
Choose PETs that match data utility needs: pseudonymization for reversible uses, anonymization or differential privacy for published results.
