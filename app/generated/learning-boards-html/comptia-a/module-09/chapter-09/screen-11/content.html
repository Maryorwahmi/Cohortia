Process Flow DLP Strategy
Design and tune Data Loss Prevention
Stepwise Rollout
  1. Discovery: identify egress points and common sensitive patterns.
  2. Monitoring: enable detection in monitor mode to collect telemetry and tune rules.
  3. Enforcement: enable blocking for high-confidence cases and integrate alerts with incident response.
Example: begin by logging every outbound upload with a PII match, tune pattern rules for your formats, then block only high-confidence transmissions of restricted data.
Integration Points
  • Connect DLP alerts to SIEM and IR playbooks for triage.
  • Use classification labels to scope DLP policies and reduce false positives.
  • Train users on safe handling and upload alternatives to reduce risky behavior.
Common Mistake

Switching directly to blocking without monitoring causes business disruption and encourages users to circumvent controls.
