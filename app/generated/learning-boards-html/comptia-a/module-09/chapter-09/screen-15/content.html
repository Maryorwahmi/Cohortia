What to Remember Quick Reference
Three checks before you sign off
1. Classified?
Can you point to the classification level and handling rules for the data?
2. Keys Protected?
Are keys managed by KMS/HSM with rotation, limited access, and audit logs?
3. DLP & PETs?
Are DLP policies tuned and have you evaluated PETs for analytics or sharing scenarios?
Final Prompt
Pick one high-value dataset and run these three checks. Document missing items and propose a prioritized remediation plan.
