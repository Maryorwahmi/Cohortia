DLP Definition
Tools and processes that detect and prevent unauthorized data exfiltration.
Content Inspection
Detects sensitive info using patterns, fingerprints, keywords, and ML.
Policy & Context
Policies must consider user, destination, application, and classification.
Summary Takeaway

DLP is most effective when it is precise, context-aware, integrated with other controls, and continuously tuned using incident feedback and metrics.
