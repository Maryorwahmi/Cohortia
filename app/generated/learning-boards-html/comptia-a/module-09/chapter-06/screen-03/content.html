Practical examples to practice
Each objective maps to a concrete scenario you can configure and measure.
Finance — Credit Card Protection
Use regex detection of card patterns and block external email sends with attached spreadsheets containing more than one card number.
HR — PII in Payroll
Fingerprint known payroll exports and prevent uploads to unapproved cloud storage while allowing secure, audited transfers.
Legal — Confidential Contracts
Tag contract templates as confidential and stop external sharing unless encrypted and approved by legal.
Operations — Logs and IP
Monitor logs for accidental PII exposure before they are sent to third-party analytics services.
Measure progress
Track blocked incidents, false-positive rate, and mean time to triage to prove value and tune policies.
