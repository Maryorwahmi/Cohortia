01
Define DLP and its role
Explain DLP as inspection and enforcement that complements classification and encryption. This matters because it prevents accidental or malicious exfiltration of regulated data.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify deployment models
Map endpoint, network, and cloud DLP to use cases such as USB use, email egress, and cloud sharing. Knowing where to place controls reduces blind spots.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design classification-driven policies
Write policies tied to classification labels and context to reduce false positives and support business exceptions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Manage DLP operations
Plan for incident response, tuning, and stakeholder communication to handle alerts and minimize disruption.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
