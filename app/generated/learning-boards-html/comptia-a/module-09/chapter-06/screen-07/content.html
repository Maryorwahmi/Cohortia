Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Network DLP
What network DLP inspects
Email gateways, web proxies, firewall egress, and file transfer points. Inspection can include headers, bodies, and attachments.
Protocols
SMTP, HTTP(S), FTP, SFTP, and API-based uploads. HTTPS often requires TLS interception or metadata inspection.
Actions at egress
Block, quarantine, strip attachments, force encryption, or alert SOC teams.
Performance & privacy
Deep inspection can add latency and raise privacy concerns; balance inspection depth with legal requirements.
Example scenario
An employee attempts to upload a customer database to a consumer cloud service. Network DLP detects multiple PII matches and blocks the upload while generating an alert and logging the event for investigation.
Rollout pattern
  1. Monitor and log egress traffic.
  2. Tune detection rules based on real traffic.
  3. Enable blocking for high-confidence violations.
Key Takeaway

Use staged inspection and coordination with legal/network teams to manage TLS inspection, latency, and compliance concerns.
