Mitigation patterns
  • Layer detection methods to lower false matches.
  • Deploy in monitor mode and review metrics before enforcing.
  • Centralize policy templates and use role-based exceptions.
  • Plan infrastructure capacity and offload heavy inspection when possible.
  • Train users and publish exception workflows to maintain trust.
Real example: Reducing false positives
A global HR team initially saw high false positives for address fields. By adding SSN regex checks and excluding trusted HR systems from external-upload rules, the false positive rate dropped and legitimate work flows resumed without risk.
