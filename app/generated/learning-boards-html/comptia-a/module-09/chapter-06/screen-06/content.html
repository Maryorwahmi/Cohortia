Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Endpoint DLP
What endpoint DLP does
Monitors removable media, clipboard, local printing, file writes, and application exports to prevent data leakage at the source.
Monitored channels
USB, printers, clipboard, local apps, screen capture tools, and uploads to uncontrolled cloud services.
Operational concerns
Agent performance, privacy, patching, and support for multiple OS platforms.
Pro tip
Begin in monitor mode and involve helpdesk and business owners before enabling blocking to ensure continuity of operations.
Example scenario
A payroll spreadsheet labelled Restricted is blocked from being copied to USB. The file is quarantined, HR and security are notified, and a documented approval process allows a secure, audited transfer when appropriate.
Common mistake
Applying the same global policy across all users causes operational friction and increases helpdesk load. Instead, scope policies by role and department.
Key Takeaway

Endpoint agents are essential for stopping local exfiltration but must be deployed gradually, with monitoring and stakeholder communication.
