Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Technologies
Detecting, classifying, and enforcing policy to stop sensitive data from leaving your control.

DLP inspects content using rules, patterns, and context, then applies actions to block or flag risky transmissions. It complements classification and encryption to provide a practical, enforceable control over data in use, in motion, and at rest.

  • Content Inspection
  • Policy Enforcement
  • Incident Response
In this lesson
You will map DLP capabilities to endpoint, network, and cloud scenarios, and learn how policy design ties directly to classification and operations.
