Module 9 › Chapter 9.6 › Incident Response
Triage checklist
Fast, consistent decisions reduce impact
  • Confirm detection source and match confidence.
  • Identify user role and intended recipient.
  • Check data classification and business justification.
  • Decide: false positive, allowed exception, or incident.
Example incident
An employee attempts to upload payroll to a personal cloud drive. Detection shows payroll fingerprint and external destination. Containment revokes network upload and creates an incident for forensic analysis.
Key Takeaway

Design response playbooks tied to DLP alerts and ensure all alerts are logged, triaged, and fed back into policy tuning and training.

Workflow Diagram (step cards)
Structured steps from alert to remediation
1. Alert
Automatic detection triggers an alert with context.
2. Enrich
Attach user info, device, file fingerprint, destination.
3. Triage
Use checklist to categorize: false positive, exception, incident.
4. Contain
Revoke access, block transfers, collect logs.
5. Remediate
Notify stakeholders, restore clean copies, and close ticket.
6. Tune & Train
Adjust rules and train users to prevent recurrence.
