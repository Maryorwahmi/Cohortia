Definition

Threat modeling explained

Identify potential attackers, assets, trust boundaries, and probable attack vectors to guide architecture and controls.

STRIDE categories
  • Spoofing
  • Tampering
  • Repudiation
  • Information disclosure
  • Denial of service
  • Elevation of privilege
Method & Output
Use data flow diagrams to inspect components and flows, mark trust boundaries, and apply STRIDE questions at each point to create a list of threats.
Prioritization (DREAD)
Score threats by damage, reproducibility, exploitability, affected users, discoverability to prioritize fixes.
Example: Photo upload
STRIDE finds information disclosure (EXIF data) and denial of service (large files). Mitigate by stripping metadata and enforcing size limits.
Key Takeaway
Threat modeling is collaborative analysis that creates prioritized mitigation work for design and implementation.
