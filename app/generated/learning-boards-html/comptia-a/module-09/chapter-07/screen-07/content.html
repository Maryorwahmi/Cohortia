Module 9 › Chapter 9.7 › STRIDE vs DREAD

STRIDE — Categorize threats

Ask these questions for each component and data flow to ensure full coverage of threat types.
Spoofing
Tampering
Repudiation
Information Disclosure
Denial of Service
Elevation of Privilege

DREAD — Prioritize findings

Score each identified threat on five dimensions to rank mitigation effort.
  • Damage potential
  • Reproducibility
  • Exploitability
  • Affected users
  • Discoverability
Example
STRIDE finds metadata disclosure on uploads. DREAD shows high discoverability and wide user impact, making it a high priority to remediate.
