Designing security gates

Define measurable gate criteria that reflect prioritized threats and balance velocity with safety.

Automated checks: SAST/DAST thresholds
Triage: false positives and compensating controls
Escalation: clear remediation ownership and SLAs
Example gate
No open critical vulnerabilities; high vulnerabilities require owner and 7-day SLA to patch or accept with compensating controls.
Misconception
Gates are not punitive—they are quality controls that require collaboration to resolve issues.
Key Takeaway
Make gates practical, measurable, and linked to prioritized risks to keep releases safe and predictable.
