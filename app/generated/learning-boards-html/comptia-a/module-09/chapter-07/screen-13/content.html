Sustain and adapt

Monitoring, incident response, and audits keep the application secure as the environment and threats change.

Monitoring: Auth failures, file upload anomalies, access patterns
Incident response: roles, playbooks, and communications
Audits: periodic checks and pen testing to validate assumptions
Misconception
Passing gates once does not guarantee perpetual safety; systems and threats evolve.
Pro tip
Tune alerts to meaningful signals and review false positives to keep teams focused.
Key Takeaway
Operate with continuous detection and clear response plans so security remains effective after deployment.
