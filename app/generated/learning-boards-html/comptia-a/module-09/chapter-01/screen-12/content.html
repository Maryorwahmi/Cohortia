Module 9 › Chapter 9.1 › Common Mistakes
Anti-patterns
  • Checkbox Security: One audit and no follow-up.
  • Late Security Involvement: Design flaws found during testing.
  • No Developer Training: Repeatable coding mistakes persist.
Corrective Practices
Embed security in requirements and acceptance criteria
Policy-gate critical checks in CI/CD
Deliver targeted secure-coding workshops
Metric-driven Improvement

Track time to remediate critical vulnerabilities, percent of builds blocked by security policy, and frequency of repeat findings. Use those metrics in retrospectives to prioritize tooling, training, and design reviews.

Playbook Item

Run an SSDLC retrospective after each incident: update threat models, close tooling gaps, and schedule developer training.

Key Takeaway

Anti-patterns are fixable through process, measurement, and education. Make small, measurable commitments each sprint.
