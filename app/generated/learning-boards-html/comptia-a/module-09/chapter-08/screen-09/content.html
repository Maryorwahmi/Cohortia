Penetration Testing

A human-led simulated attack focusing on real exploitability and business impact.

Engagement scope
Define targets, permitted techniques, timing, and reporting expectations to prevent disruption.
Deliverables
Repro steps, impact assessment, suggested mitigations, and re-test criteria.
When to use pen testing
  • Before major releases or after significant architecture changes.
  • To validate business logic and complex exploit paths.
Misconception
Passing a pen test is not permanent assurance; it is a snapshot that must be followed by fixes and continuous testing.
