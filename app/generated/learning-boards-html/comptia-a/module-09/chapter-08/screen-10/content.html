Bug Bounty Programs

Crowdsourced vulnerability discovery with rewards and disclosure management.

Operational needs
Clear scope, triage processes, payment workflows, and secure channels for reports.
Program pitfalls
High volume of reports, duplicates, and potential unauthorized testing if scope is unclear.
Benefit
Continuous external testing that can find creative, real-world attack paths.
Integration
Feed validated reports into remediation trackers, SAST tuning, and developer training to prevent recurrence.
