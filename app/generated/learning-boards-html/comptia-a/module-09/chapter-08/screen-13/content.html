Analyzing Reports

Reproduce, prioritize, and transform findings into developer tasks with clear acceptance criteria.

Checklist for each alert
  • Reproduction steps and proof of concept
  • Exploitability and business impact
  • Suggested remediation and re-test plan
Example: Username enumeration
Include the request/response differences, severity, and a recommended fix such as consistent responses and rate limiting.
Scaling triage
Group similar findings into a single remediation ticket to address systemic issues efficiently.
