Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Key Concepts
SAST
Analyze code early to catch vulnerabilities before deployment.
DAST
Test a running app to find runtime issues and misconfigurations.
IAST & RASP
IAST instruments runtime for precise findings; RASP protects inline when immediate mitigation is needed.
Pen testing & Bug Bounty
Human testers and crowdsourced researchers find creative and complex vulnerabilities not caught by tools.
Summary

Build a layered approach, prioritize by risk, fix root causes, and verify fixes through re-testing and process improvement.
