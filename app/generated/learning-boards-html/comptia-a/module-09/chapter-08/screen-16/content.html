1. Place tests
SAST early, IAST in tests, DAST in staging, RASP selectively in prod.
2. Prioritize fixes
Assess exploitability and impact, then remediate root causes and re-test.
3. Combine testing
Automated tools plus pen tests and bounties provide complementary coverage.
Final takeaway

Build a repeatable, layered program that reduces noise, shortens remediation time, and improves long-term security.
