Definition Software Composition Analysis
How SCA works
  • Identify direct and transitive dependencies from lockfiles and build artifacts.
  • Match package versions to vulnerability databases and license metadata.
  • Produce prioritized findings and suggested upgrades or patches.
Example
CI runs an SCA scan; a transitive dependency is flagged with a critical CVE. The build fails or a ticket is opened based on policy.
Policy & Practice
  • Fail builds on critical or high CVEs; create remediation tasks for medium/low.
  • Maintain an allowlist for approved packages and track license compatibility.
  • Automate dependency updates and retest to reduce exposure window.
Common Mistake

Ignoring transitive dependencies and only scanning top-level packages leaves serious exposure unnoticed.
