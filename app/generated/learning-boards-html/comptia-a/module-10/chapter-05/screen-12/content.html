Architecture SOAR in DevSecOps
SOAR playbook example
  1. Receive CI vulnerability alert from SCA/SAST.
  2. Enrich alert with contextual data and assign severity.
  3. Create a remediation ticket and notify the owning team.
  4. Trigger verification scan after fix and close the ticket on success.
When to use SOAR
  • High volume of alerts that follow repeatable workflows.
  • Need to automate ticketing, enrichment, and verification steps.
  • Desire for consistent, auditable response processes across tools.
Key Takeaway

Use SOAR to automate repetitive remediation steps and free teams to focus on complex incidents.
