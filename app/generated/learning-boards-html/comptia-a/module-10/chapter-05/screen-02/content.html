Learning Objectives
These objectives guide focused practice: explain, identify, evaluate, design, and analyze DevSecOps concepts and tooling.
01
Explain DevSecOps principles
Describe automation, integration, collaboration, feedback, and continuous improvement and why they matter for secure delivery.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify automatable security activities
Learn which scans and checks belong in CI/CD: SAST, SCA, DAST schedules, IaC scans, image checks, and secrets workflows.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Evaluate DevSecOps tools
Compare SAST, SCA, DAST, IaC scanners, container scanners, secrets managers, and SOAR for fit and integration points.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Design a secured pipeline
Place automated gates and feedback loops and choose gate criteria that balance security and delivery speed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
