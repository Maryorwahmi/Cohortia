Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › RBAC & Service Accounts
Core Concept
Design Principles
  • • Grant the minimum verbs and resource scope required by a workload.
  • • Use namespaced roles rather than cluster roles when possible.
  • • Regularly audit bindings and rotate credentials for service accounts.
Example
A backend service should receive read access to a specific ConfigMap and write access to its own Deployment annotations, not broad pod or node privileges.
Operational Steps
  1. 1. Inventory service accounts and their current permissions.
  2. 2. Define narrowly scoped Roles and RoleBindings per application.
  3. 3. Automate checks into CI to prevent overly broad changes.
Common Mistake
Reusing a single service account with broad permissions across many apps multiplies blast radius when compromised.
Key Takeaway

RBAC and scoped service accounts are essential to prevent a compromised pod from altering cluster state or accessing unrelated resources.
