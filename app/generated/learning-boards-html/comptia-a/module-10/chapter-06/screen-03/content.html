Learning Objectives — Continued

These objectives emphasize automation, verification, and secrets handling for production readiness.

05
Integrate image scanning and signing

Detect known CVEs early and assert image provenance through signing. This prevents deploying vulnerable or tampered artifacts to clusters.

Verified Competency Milestone
06
Design least-privilege RBAC and service accounts

Assign narrow rights to service accounts so a compromised pod cannot manipulate cluster resources beyond its need. This containment reduces blast radius.

Verified Competency Milestone
07
Secure serverless with IAM and validation

Create granular IAM roles and validate all inputs to functions. This prevents unauthorized access and reduces the chance of injection or data leakage.

Verified Competency Milestone
08
Manage secrets outside images and code

Use secret managers for credentials and retrieve them at runtime. Hardcoding secrets widens the exposure if images or repos are leaked.
