Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Image Scanning & Signing
Definition
Image Scanning
Detect known vulnerabilities before deployment
  • • Integrate Trivy, Clair, or Anchore into CI to fail on high-risk CVEs.
  • • Scan both OS packages and language dependencies to find transitive issues.
  • • Store reports with the build metadata for audits and triage.
Example
Run a scan after building an image; block promotion on high-severity matches and open a ticket for remediation.
Image Signing
Assert integrity and provenance of images
  • • Use signing tools to create verifiable metadata for each promoted image.
  • • Enforce verification in deploy pipelines or admission controllers in clusters.
  • • Treat signatures as part of your SBOM and audit trail.
Common Mistake
Relying on scanning alone without signing allows an attacker to swap images in registries after scans pass.
Key Takeaway

Combine automated scanning with signing and verification to detect vulnerabilities and ensure only trusted images reach runtime.
