Authorization Gaps
Don't stop at authentication. Enforce per-resource and per-action checks to prevent BOLA.
Excessive Exposure
Return only necessary fields. Avoid verbose errors and internal identifiers in responses.
Secrets & Keys
Use secret stores, rotate keys, and avoid embedding secrets in clients or source control.
Rate Limiting
Protect sensitive endpoints and place broad limits at the gateway to preserve availability.
CORS & Headers
Be specific in CORS policies and set security headers such as HSTS and CSP where appropriate.
Monitoring
Centralize logs, alert on anomalies, and integrate with SIEM for incident detection and response.
