API Security Best Practices

Secure APIs protect data, control access, and ensure service availability across microservices, mobile, and third-party integrations. This lesson focuses on practical patterns and common pitfalls you can act on today.

Why this matters
Attackers use APIs to reach data and business workflows. A few server-side checks stop many common breaches.
