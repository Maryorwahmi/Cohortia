Signed vs Encrypted JWTs

Signed JWTs prove the token hasn't been tampered with. Encrypted JWTs also hide sensitive claims. Base64 is encoding, not encryption, so treat contents as readable unless encrypted.
Always verify signature, issuer, audience, and expiry.
Avoid putting secrets in token payloads unless encrypted.
Practical Checks
  • Verify signature with public keys or shared secret.
  • Check exp and nbf claims to enforce time validity.
  • Confirm aud and iss to ensure token was issued for this API.
Common mistake: trusting claims without validating the signature or issuer.
Key Takeaway

Use signed tokens for integrity; encrypt or avoid sensitive claims and enforce claim checks.
