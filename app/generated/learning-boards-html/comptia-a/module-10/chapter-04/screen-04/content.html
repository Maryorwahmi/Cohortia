Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Supply Chain Security
Definition & context

Software supply chain security protects the integrity of components and processes from development to deployment. It treats third-party libraries, build tools, and delivery scripts as assets that must be inventoried and validated.

Why supply chain thinking matters
A single vulnerable or malicious component can affect many applications simultaneously, increasing blast radius dramatically.
Common misconception
This is not only a perimeter or network problem. It is a provenance and integrity problem: who supplied the component, and can we verify it?
Key Takeaway

Inventory and verify components; treat supply chain items as first-class security assets.
