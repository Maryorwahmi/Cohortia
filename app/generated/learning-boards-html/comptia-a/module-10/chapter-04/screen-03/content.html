Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Applied Objectives
Four applied objectives
  • • Interpret SCA reports and map CVEs to remediation actions.
  • • Generate and query SBOMs to find affected applications quickly.
  • • Draft component governance: acceptable licenses, approval flows, and scan policies.
  • • Automate SCA in CI to fail builds on critical risk and notify owners for remediation.
Mnemonic & evidence
Track evidence: a saved SCA report, a CI job that runs SCA, and an SBOM artifact for each release. These three items prove your process works in an audit or incident.
Example
Scan output flags log4j-core 2.14.1 as vulnerable. The triage step decides to upgrade to 2.17.1 or apply compensating controls if immediate upgrade is impossible.
Why this matters
Applied skills shorten the window between disclosure and mitigation, reducing exposure across many services.
