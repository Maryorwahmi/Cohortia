Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Final Checklist
Automate
Run SCA in CI and fail builds on critical issues to catch risks early.
Inventory
Generate SBOMs for every artifact so you can query affected systems instantly after disclosure.
Govern
Create and enforce policies for licenses, allowed dependencies, and update cadence.
Ask: Do builds produce SBOMs? Are critical CVEs blocked in CI? If not, prioritize those improvements to reduce response time during incidents.
