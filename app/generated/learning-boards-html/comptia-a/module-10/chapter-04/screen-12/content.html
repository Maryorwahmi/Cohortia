Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › SBOM Practice
Tooling
Use scanners that support standard SBOM formats. Integrate them into your build pipeline so each artifact has an accompanying SBOM that is archived with the release.
Important fields
Name, version, supplier, purl or cpe, license, hash, and dependency list are minimum viable fields for effective incident queries.
Governance fit
Define retention, access controls, and the authority that approves SBOM exceptions. Make SBOMs discoverable so incident responders can query them by component identifier quickly.
Takeaway
Automated SBOMs plus a searchable inventory dramatically reduce time to scope and remediate supply chain incidents.
