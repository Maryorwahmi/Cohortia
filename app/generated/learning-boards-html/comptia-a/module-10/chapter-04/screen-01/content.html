Software Composition Analysis and Supply Chain Security
Managing risks from third-party components, vulnerabilities, and SBOMs
In this lesson

You will learn why third-party and open-source components create supply chain risk, how SCA tools and SBOMs provide visibility, and concrete strategies to govern and remediate dependency issues.

Why it matters

A vulnerability or malicious component can propagate quickly across many applications. Detecting and inventorying components early saves time and reduces incident impact.

Who should care

Developers, DevOps, and security architects who must maintain resilient and compliant enterprise software under continuous delivery.
