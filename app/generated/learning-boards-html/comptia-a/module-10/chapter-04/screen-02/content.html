Mnemonic & checks
Use the mnemonic S.A.F.E.: Scan continuously, Apply governance, Fix prioritized issues, Enforce SBOMs. This helps turn awareness into repeatable practice.
Practical check
Verify that CI produces an SBOM and fails builds for critical vulnerabilities based on policy thresholds.
Real world example
When Log4Shell was disclosed, teams with SBOMs quickly found affected services and prioritized upgrades. That speed matters.
