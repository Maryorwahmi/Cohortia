What a CVE tells you
  • Identifier and description of the flaw.
  • Affected versions and sometimes remediation advice.
  • Severity score, but not context on exploitability in your app.
Example
SCA flags log4j-core 2.14.1 with a critical CVE. The remediation step may be to upgrade to 2.17.1 after testing.
Common mistake

Treating severity alone as the only prioritization factor. Always include exploitability and code-path relevance.

SCA report snapshot
When SCA reports list CVEs, use these action steps: identify instances of the component, evaluate whether the vulnerable functionality is used, estimate remediation effort, and schedule upgrade or mitigation. Document decisions for audit trails.
Takeaway
Use SCA to find CVEs and to inform prioritized, contextual remediation—not only automated version upgrades.
Check your understanding
If a CVE is high severity but the vulnerable code is never called by your app, how would you prioritize the fix? Consider risk and cost before immediate upgrade.
