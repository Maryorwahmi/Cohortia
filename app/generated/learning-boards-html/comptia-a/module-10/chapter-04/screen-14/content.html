Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Key Concepts
Supply Chain Security
Protect the integrity of all components and processes that deliver software.
SCA
Scans code and artifacts to find known vulnerabilities and license issues and maps dependency trees.
SBOM
A machine-readable inventory of components, versions, suppliers, hashes and transitive dependencies for rapid response.
Summary Takeaway
Combine SCA for detection, SBOMs for inventory, and governance for enforcement to reduce supply chain risk systematically.
