Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Licensing & Compliance
Two license families
Permissive (MIT, Apache)
Fewer distribution requirements; often acceptable for commercial use with attribution.
Copyleft (GPLv3)
May require derived works to be licensed under the same terms; raises distribution concerns for proprietary software.
Practical action
Use SCA to detect licenses and route components with restrictive licenses to legal review before inclusion in distributables.
Governance card
A governance policy should list approved licenses, describe approval steps for exceptions, and specify when a legal review is required. Integrate this policy into your SCA tool policy engine so checks happen automatically in CI.
Common mistake

Ignoring licenses until release time, which can force costly rewrites or legal exposure.

Takeaway
Make license checks part of the development lifecycle, not an end-stage compliance task.
