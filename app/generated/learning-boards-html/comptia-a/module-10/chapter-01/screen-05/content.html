Module 10 › Requirements
What to capture
  • Functional security: authentication, authorization, session management.
  • Non-functional: confidentiality, integrity, availability, resilience under attack.
  • Compliance mappings for relevant standards and regulations.
Abuse case example

Use case: "User transfers funds." Abuse case: "Attacker attempts to transfer funds from another user's account."

From abuse case to acceptance

Turn abuse cases into acceptance criteria that are specific and testable. Avoid vague requirements such as "secure data" without measurable controls.

Practice artifact
Abuse case: Brute-force login
Acceptance: Account locks after 5 failed attempts for 30 minutes; failed attempts logged to audit stream.
Key Takeaway

Make security requirements measurable by linking abuse cases to acceptance tests and compliance checks.
