Module 10 › Challenges
Top pitfalls
  • Treating security as an end-of-line gate.
  • Relying solely on one testing technique.
  • Lack of ownership for remediation tasks.
Addressing them
  1. Create small, enforceable security checkpoints per phase.
  2. Integrate tools into developer workflows and CI/CD.
  3. Assign owners and timelines for remediation of findings.
Key Takeaway

Start small, measure outcomes, and expand practices that show clear risk reduction and team acceptance.
