Module 10 › Maintenance
Monitoring and detection

Aggregate logs, alerts, and telemetry into a central system to detect anomalies and suspicious behavior across components.

Example

Alert on repeated failed logins and unusual data exfiltration patterns; route to on-call and incident response playbooks.

Patch and feedback loop
  • Regular vulnerability scans and prioritized patching for OS, libs, and apps.
  • Feed incidents and findings back into requirements and design for continuous improvement.
Key Takeaway

Monitoring plus a formal feedback loop ensures your SDLC evolves to address real-world threats and incidents.
