PASTA Steps
  1. Define business and technical objectives.
  2. Define technical scope and boundaries.
  3. Analyze application and infrastructure.
  4. Threat analysis.
  5. Vulnerability analysis.
  6. Attack simulation and analysis.
  7. Risk and impact analysis.
Applied Example
For an enterprise service, PASTA begins by scoring business impact for data exfiltration and then runs attack simulations to estimate likelihood and mitigations.
If business criticality is high, PASTA supports more detailed simulation and validation steps to ensure controls meet required risk tolerances.
Key Takeaway

PASTA aligns technical threats with business impact so teams can choose appropriate depth of analysis and mitigation effort.
