Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Least Privilege
Implementation Patterns
  • Create service-specific accounts with tightly scoped permissions.
  • Use short-lived credentials and token scopes for inter-service calls.
  • Automate policy reviews and least-privilege audits.
Concrete Example
UserProfileService DB user: only SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE on user tables. Inter-service token: scoped to specific endpoints and data fields.
Avoid granting administrative privileges to application accounts or sharing credentials between services.
Key Takeaway

Least privilege is practical: design roles and scopes that match real application needs and automate verification.
