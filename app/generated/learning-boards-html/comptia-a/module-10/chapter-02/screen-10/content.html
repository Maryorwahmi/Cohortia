Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Applying PASTA
Practical Steps
  • Define business goals and risk tolerance.
  • Scope the technical surface and list assets.
  • Perform threat and vulnerability analysis focused on high-value assets.
  • Simulate realistic attacks for prioritized paths.
  • Produce risk and mitigation recommendations with residual risk estimates.
Example Focus
If customer PII is critical, simulate attacks to extract records and estimate exploit complexity, then recommend encryption, access controls, and monitoring as mitigations.
Document assumptions about attacker capabilities and business impact to keep stakeholders aligned.
