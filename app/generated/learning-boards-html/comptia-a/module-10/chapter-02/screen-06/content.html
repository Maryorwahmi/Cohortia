STRIDE Categories
Spoofing — impersonation risks; check authentication and identity proofing.
Tampering — unauthorized modification; ensure integrity protections and signatures.
Repudiation — lack of proof; ensure logging, audit trails, and non-repudiation controls.
Information Disclosure — exposure of sensitive data; apply least privilege and encryption.
Denial of Service — availability attacks; design rate limiting and capacity controls.
Elevation of Privilege — privilege escalation; ensure proper authorization checks and separation of duties.
Applied Example
Authentication service: Spoofing via stolen tokens; Tampering if tokens are accepted without integrity checks; Repudiation if actions aren't logged.
After identification, map each finding to controls: stronger token handling, signed payloads, audit logs, and role separation to prevent privilege escalation.
Misconception Check

STRIDE enumerates threats; it does not rank them. Use DREAD or another scoring approach to prioritize fixes.
