Principles List
  • Least Privilege
  • Defense in Depth
  • Secure Defaults
  • Separation of Concerns / Compartmentalization
  • Fail Securely and Minimize Attack Surface
Example Patterns
Use service-specific database accounts with minimal permissions. Enforce TLS and scoped tokens. Put an API gateway to limit external exposure.
Avoid superficial application; map each principle to measurable controls and tests.
Misconception

Principles are not prescriptive code; they are constraints that guide design choices and measurable controls.
