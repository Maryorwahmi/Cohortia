Scoring Factors
  • Damage potential — impact if exploited.
  • Reproducibility — how easily the attack can be repeated.
  • Exploitability — required tools or skills.
  • Affected users — scope of impact.
  • Discoverability — how easy it is to find the vulnerability.
Sum scores for relative prioritization; always record scoring rationale.
Example
An information disclosure bug that exposes records: Damage 9, Reproducibility 8, Exploitability 6, Affected users 8, Discoverability 7 → high overall score.
If compensating controls reduce impact or scope, adjust scores accordingly and document why.
Misuse Alert

Treat scores as comparative. Cross-functional reviews reduce scoring bias and improve prioritization quality.
