Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Key Concepts
Threat Modeling
A structured process to identify and prioritize threats in system design.
STRIDE
Categorizes threats as Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Info Disclosure, DoS, Elevation of Privilege.
DREAD & PASTA
DREAD scores risk factors; PASTA aligns simulation with business impact for complex systems.
Takeaway
Pair a modeling method with design principles and document decisions so threat models remain useful throughout the system lifecycle.
