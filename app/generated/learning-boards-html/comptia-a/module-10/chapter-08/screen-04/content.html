Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Unique Security Challenges
Definition

What makes IoT different

Resource Constraints
Limited CPU, memory, and power restrict strong logging and heavy cryptography, forcing tradeoffs.
Physical Exposure
Fielded devices may be tampered with or have components probed; hardware protections reduce this risk.
Long Lifespan
Devices remain in operation for years, so update strategy and lifecycle planning are crucial.
Examples & Takeaway
Consider a smart sensor without OTA: a discovered flaw stays exploitable for the device's operational life. If the device is also reachable physically, an attacker can extract credentials or alter firmware. Secure design anticipates these constraints and builds layered mitigations.
Common mistake
Relying only on perimeter defenses like firewalls and expecting devices to remain safe is a false comfort.
Pro tip
Prioritize hardware-backed identity and update mechanisms during procurement to reduce future risk.
