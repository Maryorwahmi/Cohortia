Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Lifecycle — Design & Provisioning
Design Phase
Perform threat modeling and choose secure hardware, then design firmware with least privilege and minimal services.
Choose parts with crypto accelerators and secure memory.
Design for updateability and logging to the extent resources allow.
Specify secure manufacturing and supply chain checks.
Provisioning Phase
Inject unique identities, keys, and certificates during manufacturing or use zero-touch provisioning for secure onboarding.
Use secure key-injection processes and record an SBOM and device manifest.
Automate onboarding with mutual TLS and device-specific certificates for strong identity.
