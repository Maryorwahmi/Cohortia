Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Secure Communication & FOTA
Guidance

Encrypt, authenticate, monitor

Use TLS for MQTT and DTLS for CoAP; enforce mutual authentication where possible.
Ensure FOTA images are signed and verified before install; add rollback protection.
Forward logs and telemetry to a SIEM for anomaly detection and incident response.
Example config
# Illustrative settings
protocol: mqtt
transport: tls
auth: x509-certs
fota:
  signed: true
  rollback-protection: enabled
logging: forward-to-siem
If a device cannot do mutual TLS, place a gateway that enforces identity and performs verification.
Do not assume encryption alone prevents compromise without authentication and update validation.
