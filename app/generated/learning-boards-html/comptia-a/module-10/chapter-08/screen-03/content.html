Evaluation checkpoints

01
Secure provisioning
Confirm unique device identity and secure key injection at manufacturing.
02
Supply chain integrity
Assess supplier controls and signed components to reduce pre-deployment compromise.
03
Operational checks
Verify logging, monitoring, and firmware update paths to enable incident response.
04
Decommissioning
Ensure secure erase and key destruction before disposal or repurposing.

Assessment mnemonic

Use the checklist in the hands-on activity: for each item assign importance and justify it relative to an industrial sensor's exposure and constraints.
Deliverable
A completed checklist rating controls as High/Medium/Low with justification.
Outcome
Actionable remediation recommendations for procurement and operations.
