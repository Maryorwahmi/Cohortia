Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Attack Vectors & Mitigations

Top attack vectors

Default/weak credentials — Mitigate with unique certs and disable defaults.
Unpatched vulnerabilities — Mitigate with signed FOTA and SBOM tracking.
Physical tampering — Mitigate with secure boot, tamper-evident seals, and HSM/TPM protections.

Network & supply risks

Insecure communication — Mitigate with TLS/DTLS and mutual auth.
Supply chain compromise — Mitigate by vetting suppliers and signing firmware.
DDoS from compromised devices — Mitigate via segmentation and rate limiting.
Action step: prioritize fixes that reduce immediate risk, like eliminating default credentials and enabling signed updates for high-impact devices.
