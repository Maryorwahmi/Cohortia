Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Decommissioning
Process

Secure erase and revoke

Execute hardware secure-erase or overwrite keys in secure elements.
Revoke certificates and remove device records from PKI and device registries.
Update asset inventory and confirm physical disposal or re-provisioning steps.
Concrete checklist
1) Trigger secure erase on device. 2) Revoke certificates in PKI. 3) Delete device entry from cloud registries. 4) Confirm with asset management.
Pitfall: relying on a simple factory-reset without key destruction or certificate revocation can leave a device impersonable.
