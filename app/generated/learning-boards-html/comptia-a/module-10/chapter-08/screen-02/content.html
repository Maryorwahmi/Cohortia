Learning Objectives

Focus and outcomes

These objectives guide hands-on assessment and decision-making for device security.

01
Identify constraints
Recognize resource, physical, and lifecycle constraints that shape secure design choices.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain HRoT & Secure Boot
Articulate how immutable hardware roots and signed boot chains prevent unauthorized firmware.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Design lifecycle controls
Plan secure provisioning, OTA updates, and safe decommissioning in device programs.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Choose secure comms
Select TLS/DTLS and certificate management appropriate for constrained devices.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
A practical mnemonic
Use the mnemonic "HARD LIFE": Hardware trust, Authentication, Resilience, Deployment, Lifecycle, Identity, Firmware, Encryption. Each letter maps to a lifecycle control you will evaluate in the hands-on checklist.
Why it matters
Devices are long-lived and exposed; early design choices reduce future risk.
How to verify
Check for signed firmware, unique IDs, OTA support, and encrypted channels during procurement.
