Module 10 › Chapter 10.3 › Security Misconfiguration
Common misconfigurations
  • Leaving debug or verbose error output enabled in production
  • Using default credentials for components
  • Exposing management interfaces without restriction
Hardening checklist
  • Establish secure configuration baselines in IaC and audit them.
  • Restrict access to sensitive files and remove unused features.
  • Log internally; show generic messages externally to avoid information leakage.
Why this matters

Configuration issues are often low-effort routes for attackers. Baselines and automation reduce human error.
