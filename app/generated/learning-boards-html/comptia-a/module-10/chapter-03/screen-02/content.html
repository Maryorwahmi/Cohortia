Your practical targets
In addition to reading and coding, you will run simple SAST scans, inspect DAST reports, and produce small secure patches. These tasks build muscle memory for safe defaults that scale across projects.
Why these objectives?
They target the highest-return skills for preventing breaches: pattern recognition, secure coding, and automated checks.
Ready to measure?
Evidence includes corrected code commits, SAST results triaged, and a short report explaining fixes.
