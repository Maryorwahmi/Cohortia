Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Key Concepts
Redundancy
Duplicate critical components so another can take over on failure.
Circuit Breaker
Stop calling a failing service and return a safe fallback while it recovers.
Chaos Engineering
Controlled failure injection to discover weaknesses before they cause outages.
Final takeaway
Design to detect, isolate, and recover. Test those designs with small, repeatable experiments and learn from every run.
