Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Failover
Failover steps
1
Detect: health checks, latency or error thresholds trigger alerts.
2
Isolate: remove the unhealthy instance from service routing.
3
Recover: restart, replace, or route to redundant instances.
4
Verify: confirm steady-state metrics and resume normal operations.
Concrete scenario
A database replica is marked unhealthy due to replication lag. The orchestrator removes it from reads, promotes a healthy replica for failover, and re-syncs the unhealthy node later. During this time, the application switches to cached reads for non-critical queries.
Misconception
Automated recovery always reduces downtime. It helps, but without resilient state handling and load-shedding it can prolong instability.
