Bulkhead patterns
Separate critical workloads into isolated compartments to prevent resource starvation. Examples include separate thread pools, isolated queues for payments, or different database instances for critical and non-critical data.
Thread pools
Prevent blocking one workload from consuming all threads.
Namespaces
Logical separation in orchestration platforms to limit scope of failures.
Load balancing essentials
Distribute traffic across healthy instances with health checks and weighted routing. Combine with rate limits to avoid overload during recovery.
Check
If a service is slow, does your load balancer remove it from rotation quickly? If not, load balancer configuration is a likely weak point.
