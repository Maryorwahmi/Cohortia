Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Chaos Engineering
Principles
  • Hypothesize about steady state metrics (latency, error rates).
  • Introduce realistic failures that simulate real-world events.
  • Minimize blast radius, automate experiments, and observe outcomes.
Example hypothesis
If InventoryService is unreachable, PaymentService will use cached stock data for in-stock items and prevent purchases for out-of-stock items without increasing error rates.
Safety controls
Start in staging when possible, limit blast radius in production, and have automated rollback and alerts ready.
