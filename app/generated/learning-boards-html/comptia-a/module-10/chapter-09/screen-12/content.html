Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Security & Response
Practical integration steps
  • Secure failover and replication with access controls and encryption.
  • Validate incident response with controlled experiments and Game Days.
  • Design DoS resilience with load shedding, rate limits, and graceful degradation.
  • Protect backups and ensure integrity during recovery operations.
Compliance note
Documented resilience controls and tested recovery processes support regulatory requirements for availability and disaster recovery.
Operator checklist
Restrict who can trigger failover, log all automated recovery actions, ensure backups are encrypted, and periodically run small blast-radius chaos experiments to validate the end-to-end process.
