Common modifiers
  • padding(), font(), foregroundColor()
  • background(), cornerRadius(), shadow()
  • frame(width:height:), resizable() for images
Practical tip
Apply modifiers deliberately: decide whether you want the effect inside or outside other modifiers and order accordingly.
Debugging layout
Add temporary background colors and frames to visualize bounds. Remove them once layout is correct.
Common mistake
Applying padding after background when you intended the padding inside the colored area causes a different visual result.
