Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Continuous Delivery vs Deployment
Definitions
  • Continuous Delivery: Build is always in a releasable state. Use archives and distribution for testers.
  • Continuous Deployment: Every passing change is released automatically; used when safe and permitted.
Example: Uploading a .xcarchive to TestFlight after a successful pipeline is a Delivery step that speeds QA feedback.
When to choose which
Choose Delivery when you want builds ready for humans to approve. Choose Deployment only when tests and monitoring are mature and legal or platform constraints allow automatic releases.
Misconception: "Always deploy automatically" — not all apps can due to review, compliance, or staged rollout needs.
Pro-tip: Use TestFlight distribution for fast internal testing and save public App Store submissions for scheduled releases.
