Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Apple Developer Program
Core Concept

What enrollment gives you

Membership gives the rights to create certificates, register App IDs, enable features, and publish through App Store Connect. It ties your identity and team to the distribution pipeline.

Certificates

Issued by Apple to prove developer identity for signing.

App IDs & Capabilities

Register names and enable services like Push or In-App Purchase.

App Store Connect

Create app records, manage metadata, and upload builds.

Team Roles

Admin and App Manager roles affect who can create App IDs or upload builds.

Example
Company App Ownership

When a company enrolls, apps are listed under the organization. That means certificates and app records are owned by the company account and managed by team members with appropriate roles.

Common Mistake

Assuming Xcode can sign without membership. If the account has no active membership, distribution certificates are unavailable, and uploads will fail.

Key Takeaway

Enroll early and verify the team selected in Xcode matches the account that will own the app.
