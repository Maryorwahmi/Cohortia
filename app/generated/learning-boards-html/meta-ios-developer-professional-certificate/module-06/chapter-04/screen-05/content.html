Definition

Development and Distribution Certificates

Certificates are issued by Apple to confirm the identity of the developer or organization creating the app. They are used during signing so Apple can validate the origin of the binary.

Development Certificate
Used to sign builds that run on registered test devices during development.
Distribution Certificate
Used to sign archives you upload to App Store Connect for public distribution or ad hoc testing.
Practical Check
Inspect Certificates

Open the developer portal or Keychain and confirm the distribution certificate expiry date and that it is present for the team used in Xcode.

Common Mistake

Using an expired certificate or assuming automatic signing will silently regenerate a missing distribution certificate; it often requires manual regeneration and profile updates.

Key Takeaway

Keep distribution certificates healthy and coordinate regeneration when team members or CI systems rely on them.
