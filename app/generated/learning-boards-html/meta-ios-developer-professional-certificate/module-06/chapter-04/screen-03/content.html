Troubleshooting & Readiness

Checks and fixes you will use when signing or review fails.

Bloom: Understand → Apply
Detect Bundle ID Mismatch

Confirm that the Bundle Identifier in Xcode matches the App ID chosen when creating the App Store Connect record. A mismatch prevents the build from associating with the app record.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Certificates Health Check

Inspect expiration dates in your developer account. If automatic signing fails, be prepared to revoke and regenerate certificates and profiles.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Archive Validation

Use Xcode's Organizer validation step before upload to catch missing assets or incorrect architectures that trigger immediate rejection.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Review-ready Metadata

Prepare accurate screenshots, demo accounts if needed, and a valid privacy policy URL to avoid review delays or rejections.
