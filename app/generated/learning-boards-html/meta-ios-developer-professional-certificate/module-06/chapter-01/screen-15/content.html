Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Final Reminders
Separate concerns
Keep data, presentation logic, and UI rendering in distinct layers.
Use reactive bindings
ObservableObject and @Published keep Views synchronized with state changes.
Refactor incrementally
Move one piece of state at a time and test after each change.
Keep these practical rules in your workflow and you'll find refactors safer and architecture decisions clearer as your project grows.
