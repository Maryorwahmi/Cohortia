Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Checklist
Pre-merge checklist
  • No persistence or networking code in Views
  • ViewModel exposes clear, intention-named methods
  • @Published used for reactive properties, ObservableObject for ViewModels
  • Views that create ViewModels use @StateObject
  • Services abstracted behind protocols and unit-tested
Key Takeaway
A short checklist prevents drift and preserves the value of your chosen architecture.
