Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Scaling MVVM
When to escalate
If the app requires formal routing, many independent teams, or strict unit tests across layers, stricter patterns may help. Otherwise favor service abstractions and protocol-based designs within MVVM.
Introduce protocol-based services for networking and persistence
Write unit tests for ViewModel methods and domain services
Tradeoffs
  • VIPER/Clean: stronger guarantees, more files and wiring
  • MVVM + services: lower overhead, faster iteration
Key Takeaway

Start with MVVM and strengthen it with service abstractions and tests. Move to stricter patterns only when the team gains clear benefits.
