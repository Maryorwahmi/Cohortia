Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › MVVM vs Other Patterns
MVVM

Natural fit for SwiftUI: View reactivity and ViewModel publishing complement each other. Lower boilerplate than enterprise patterns.

Strength: easier testing of presentation logic
Use when: app complexity grows beyond a few screens
MVC, VIPER, Clean

MVC can be fine for simple apps but risks controller bloat. VIPER/Clean add strong separation and testability but increase boilerplate and coordination overhead.

Tradeoff: more files, more structure, easier to reason in large teams
Pitfall: over-engineering for small projects
Key Takeaway

Match the pattern to your project's scale: MVVM is a strong default with SwiftUI; escalate to stricter patterns only when team size and complexity demand it.
