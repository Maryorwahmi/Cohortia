What to Remember
Key Concepts — part 2
@Published
Marks properties that notify observers when values change.
Memory safety
Avoid strong captures in closures; prefer [weak self] to prevent leaks.
Migration
Follow extraction, @Published, @StateObject creation, and @ObservedObject injection steps when converting state.
Final takeaway

Ask who owns the data, make ownership explicit with @StateObject, and let children observe with @ObservedObject to keep state consistent and predictable.
