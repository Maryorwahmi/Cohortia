What to Remember
Key Concepts — part 1
ObservableObject
Protocol for classes to notify SwiftUI about changes.
@StateObject
Use when the view creates and must preserve the model instance.
@ObservedObject
Observe an externally owned ObservableObject without managing its lifecycle.
Summary takeaway

Ownership decides persistence: let an owner hold the model with @StateObject and let other views observe it with @ObservedObject.
