Hierarchy
Navigation, tabs, and conditional views
Place models where their lifecycle matches the container behavior to avoid unexpected reinitialization.
NavigationView
Pushed views own models persist while pushed. Pop destroys them.
TabView
Tabs may keep views alive; put shared models in a parent to guarantee persistence across tabs.
Conditional branches
Toggling branches recreates views; owning models should live above transient branches.
Concrete scenario
ContentView owns GameManager with @StateObject. Toggling a child view or switching tabs won't reset the manager because the owner persists at the right level.
Key takeaway
Match model lifetime to a stable parent; that parent should use @StateObject to guarantee persistence.
