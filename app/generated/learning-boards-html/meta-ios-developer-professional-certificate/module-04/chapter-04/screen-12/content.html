Assessment Idea

Concept + Practical

Combine a multiple-choice concept question with a hands-on implementation to verify understanding and application.

Concept Question

Which approach lets a control respond to both a normal tap and a long-press in the most idiomatic way?

Answer: Use Button for the tap and .onLongPressGesture(minimumDuration:) for the long press.
Rationale: Button handles the default tap while onLongPressGesture triggers only when the duration is met, avoiding complex gesture composition.
