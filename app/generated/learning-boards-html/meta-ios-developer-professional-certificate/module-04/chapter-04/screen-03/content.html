Practice Flow

A stepwise approach to mastering gesture-driven UI.

Step 1
Single Gesture

Implement a TapGesture or LongPress and observe onChanged/onEnded states.

Step 2
State Handling

Use @State for offsets and scale so SwiftUI updates the view during gestures.

Step 3
Compose

Try simultaneousGesture and sequenced gestures to control multi-step interactions.
