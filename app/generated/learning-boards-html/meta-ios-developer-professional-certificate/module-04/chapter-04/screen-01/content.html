User Interaction and Gestures

Learn how SwiftUI recognizes touch sequences and how to implement Tap, Long Press, Drag, Magnification, and Rotation gestures to build intuitive, touch-first UIs. Practical examples show how to update state during gestures and finalize changes when they end.

What we'll use and why it matters in apps

Tap & Press
Tap and long-press let you trigger quick actions and context menus without complex state management.
Drag & Drop
Drag gestures provide translation values to move views fluidly and finalize positions on end.
Pinch & Rotate
Magnification and rotation update scale and angle during onChanged and persist in onEnded.
