Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Safety & Comparison
Explicit passing

Pros

  • Compiler ensures parameters are present.
  • Ownership is explicit in initializers.

Cons

  • Boilerplate across many forwarders.
  • Refactors require updates in many places.
Environment sharing

Pros

  • No forwarding boilerplate in intermediates.
  • Reactive: @Published changes update consumers automatically.

Cons

  • Missing provider causes runtime crash.
  • Overuse can obscure where data comes from.
Practical tip

Keep a single owner with @StateObject at the app root and call .environmentObject there. For test or preview flows, attach a provider to ensure consumers don’t crash. Use environment sharing for truly global models, and keep local models owned and passed explicitly when they scope to a specific subtree.
