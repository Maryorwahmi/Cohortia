Common Mistakes
  • Not wrapping the whole navigable area in NavigationView, causing broken back behavior.
  • Nesting multiple NavigationViews unnecessarily, which creates separate stacks and odd toolbar placements.
  • Putting TabView inside a NavigationLink destination without clear intent; plan architecture first.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Best Practices
  • Give each TabView tab its own NavigationView to isolate stacks.
  • Use toolbar + ToolbarItem for bar buttons and placements like navigationBarLeading/trailing.
  • Keep titles clear and use displayMode (.inline or .large) to match context.
