Rubber duck technique

Explaining the code aloud forces you to state assumptions and expected values. Often the act of explanation reveals the mismatch causing the bug.

Pair explanation with a printed trace: say what you expect and then print the actual values to confirm or refute the hypothesis.
Mindset tips
  • The computer follows instructions exactly; check your instructions.
  • Form a hypothesis and test it deliberately.
  • Take a short break when stuck; returning often reveals the issue.
