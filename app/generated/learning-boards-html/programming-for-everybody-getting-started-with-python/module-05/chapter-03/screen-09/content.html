Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Finding pairs
Code
numbers = [2, 4, 6, 8]
for i in range(len(numbers)):
    for j in range(i + 1, len(numbers)):
        print(f"Pair: ({numbers[i]}, {numbers[j]})")
Starting j at i plus one avoids self-pairs and avoids generating both orders of the same pair. Verify with a small input.
Why this matters
Avoiding duplicates reduces unnecessary work and simplifies reasoning about which relationships are reported.
Use cases
Checking pair sums, comparing every two items, or generating combinations without order mattering.
Common mistake

Starting the inner loop at zero or at i leads to duplicates or self-comparisons; choose i + 1 when you want unique unordered pairs.
