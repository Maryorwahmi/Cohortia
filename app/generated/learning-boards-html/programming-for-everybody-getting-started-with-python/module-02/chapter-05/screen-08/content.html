Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Unary operators
Examples and rules
+5       # unary plus, result 5
-3       # unary minus, result -3
~x       # bitwise NOT of x
Unary operators are higher precedence than addition and subtraction but are below exponentiation in binding order. Parentheses prevent ambiguity and make intention clear.
Common confusion
Reading -2**2 as negative of two squared can be unclear; add parentheses to show whether you mean -(2**2) or (-2)**2.
Takeaway
Verify whether a minus sign is unary or binary in context. Use parentheses to avoid subtle bugs.
