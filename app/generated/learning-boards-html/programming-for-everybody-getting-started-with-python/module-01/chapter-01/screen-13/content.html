Hands-on Activity Write and test an everyday algorithm

Practice precision by having a partner follow your steps literally

Activity steps: pick a simple task, write each step in plain English, exchange with a partner, observe where they ask for details, and refine until the partner completes the task without guessing.
Example: making tea (first draft)
  1. Boil water.
  2. Place tea in cup.
  3. Pour water and wait.
When followed literally, the partner may ask: how much tea, how hot is 'boil', and how long to wait? Use those questions to improve the algorithm.
Peer test
Swap algorithms and have partners follow them exactly to find ambiguity.
Reflection
Each missing step you find is an opportunity to make code more reliable.
