Introduction to Design Patterns

Understand patterns, their categories, and a focused study of the Singleton

Design patterns are templates you adapt to recurring problems. They help teams communicate architecture, improve reusability, and encourage robust designs. Singleton is a creational pattern that guarantees one instance and a single access point.
