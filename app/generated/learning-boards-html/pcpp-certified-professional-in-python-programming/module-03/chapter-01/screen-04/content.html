Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › What is a Pattern?
Definition & benefits
A pattern captures intent, structure, and consequences. It standardizes solutions so teams can reason quickly and maintain designs across systems.
Reusability
Apply proven approaches to avoid reinventing the wheel.
Communication
Name a solution and share intent across the team.
Structure
Guide object relationships and responsibilities.
Maintainability
Promote separation of concerns for easier changes.
Metaphor: Recipe book
Think of a design pattern as a recipe: it describes ingredients, steps, and when to use the dish. You still adapt quantities to your taste and tools.
Key Takeaway

Use patterns to communicate intent, but adapt implementations to your codebase and domain needs.
