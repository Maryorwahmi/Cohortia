Numba with PyPy: The Possibility

It is technically possible to use Numba within a PyPy environment, but the benefits are not always straightforward.

Numba's Role: Numba can still compile decorated functions to machine code, even when running under PyPy.

PyPy's JIT: PyPy's own JIT compiler is highly optimized and may already accelerate many of the numerical patterns Numba targets.

Considerations for Combined Use

  • Diminishing Returns: The additional performance gain from Numba on top of PyPy's JIT might be minimal, requiring careful benchmarking.

  • Compatibility Layers: Numba's CPython-specific integrations might introduce complexities or limitations when running under PyPy.

  • Ecosystem Choice: For scientific computing, Numba within CPython is often preferred due to its deep NumPy integration and compatibility with other C-optimized libraries.

Key Takeaway

While possible, combining Numba and PyPy typically requires careful evaluation. Often, choosing one primary JIT solution based on your application's needs is more effective.
