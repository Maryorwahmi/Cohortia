Deployment Strategy

Blue/Green Deployment

Achieving zero-downtime releases and easy rollbacks

Blue Environment (Old)

Currently serving live traffic.

Switch Traffic

Green Environment (New)

New version deployed, ready to receive traffic.

Deploy to inactive environment, test, then switch traffic for zero-downtime release.
Key Takeaway

Blue/Green deployment enables zero-downtime releases and instant rollbacks by switching traffic between two identical environments.

Benefit

Minimizes risk during deployments by providing a quick and easy way to revert to the previous stable version.
