Key Distinction

Continuous Delivery vs. Continuous Deployment

Understanding the nuances of automated releases

Continuous Delivery

  • •Code is always in a deployable state.
  • •Automated build, test, and staging.
  • •Human approval for production release.
  • •Focus on readiness and reliability.

Continuous Deployment

  • •Every change automatically deploys to production.
  • •No human intervention for release.
  • •Requires high confidence in automation.
  • •Focus on speed and full automation.
Key Takeaway

Continuous Delivery makes releases possible at any time, while Continuous Deployment makes them happen automatically.
