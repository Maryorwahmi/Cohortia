Core Concept

The Challenge of Dependency Drift

Why `pip` and `requirements.txt` fall short in complex projects

Limitations of `requirements.txt`

  • Only lists direct dependencies, not transitive ones.
  • Doesn't guarantee exact versions across installations.
  • Vulnerable to breaking changes from upstream dependency updates.
  • Leads to inconsistent development and production environments.
Common Pitfall

Relying solely on `requirements.txt` can lead to 'it works on my machine' issues due to unmanaged transitive dependencies.

Key Takeaway

For robust projects, a more sophisticated approach is needed to manage both direct and transitive dependencies deterministically.
