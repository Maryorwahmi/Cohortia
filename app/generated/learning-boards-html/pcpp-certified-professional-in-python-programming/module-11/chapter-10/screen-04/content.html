The Pillars of Observability

Monitoring, Logging, and Alerting for Production Systems

What is Observability?

Observability is the ability to understand the internal state of a system by examining its external outputs. It's crucial for diagnosing issues, understanding performance, and ensuring reliability in production environments.

Why it Matters

Without observability, debugging production issues becomes a blind hunt, leading to extended downtime and frustrated users.

Real-world Impact

Quickly diagnose performance bottlenecks or errors in complex distributed systems.
