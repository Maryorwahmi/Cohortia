Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Observability

The ability to understand a system's internal state by examining its external outputs (logs, metrics, traces).

Logging

The practice of recording events that occur in a software system, providing a historical record of its execution.

Structured Logging

Logging data in a machine-readable format (e.g., JSON) with key-value pairs for easier parsing and analysis.

Chapter Summary

Observability, logging, and structured logging are fundamental practices for gaining deep insights into your application's behavior and ensuring its reliability in production.
