Understanding Metrics and Monitoring Systems

What is Monitoring?

Monitoring is the continuous process of collecting and analyzing data (metrics) about a system's performance and health over time. It provides aggregate views and trends, unlike granular logs.

  • Metrics: Numerical values collected at regular intervals.
  • Trends: Observe changes and patterns over time.
  • Health: Assess the overall well-being of the application.
Logs vs. Metrics

Logs tell you individual events; metrics show aggregate behavior and trends.
