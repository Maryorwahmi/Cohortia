Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter

Monitoring

The continuous process of collecting and analyzing data (metrics) about a system's performance and health.

Metrics

Numerical measurements collected over time, representing specific aspects of a system's behavior (e.g., CPU usage, request latency).

Alerting

The process of notifying personnel when predefined conditions are met, indicating a potential issue.

Chapter Summary

Monitoring with metrics provides a holistic view of system health, while intelligent alerting ensures proactive notification of critical issues, minimizing downtime and impact.
