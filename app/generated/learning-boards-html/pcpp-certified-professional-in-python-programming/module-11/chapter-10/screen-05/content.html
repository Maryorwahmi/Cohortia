Understanding Python's logging Module

Why Logs are Essential

  • Execution Path: Trace the flow of your application.
  • State Changes: Understand how data transforms over time.
  • Error Diagnosis: Pinpoint the exact moment and context of failures.
  • Auditing: Maintain a record of critical operations.
Beyond print()

print() statements lack context, levels, and flexible output destinations, making them unsuitable for production.
