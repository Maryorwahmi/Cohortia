Designing Effective Alerting

Strategies for Actionable Alerts

What is Alerting?

Alerting is the process of notifying relevant personnel when predefined conditions (based on monitored metrics or log patterns) are met, indicating a potential issue.

  • Alert Conditions: Rules based on metrics (e.g., CPU > 90%).
  • Notifications: Via email, Slack, PagerDuty, etc.
  • Proactive: Informs you before users are severely impacted.
Common Mistake

**Alert Fatigue:** Too many non-actionable alerts lead developers to ignore important notifications.
