Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts — Sockets & Best Practices
Constructor mapping
socket.socket(address_family, socket_type) combines address format and transport semantics into an endpoint.
Families & types
AF_INET/AF_INET6/AF_UNIX choose addresses; SOCK_STREAM/SOCK_DGRAM choose TCP/UDP behavior.
Safety & cleanup
Close sockets, handle bind errors, and avoid exposing services unintentionally by binding to all interfaces without safeguards.
Quick checklist

Choose protocol by guarantees, verify s.family and s.type after creation, close sockets reliably, and secure any binding to 0.0.0.0.
