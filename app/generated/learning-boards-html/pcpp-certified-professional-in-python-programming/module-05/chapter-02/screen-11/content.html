Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Common mistakes and best practices
Top mistakes
  • Not closing sockets, causing resource leaks.
  • Confusing AF_* with SOCK_* responsibilities.
  • Binding to 0.0.0.0 without understanding exposure.
  • Assuming IPv6 is always available.
Best practices
Close resources
Use try/finally or structured cleanup. Explicitly call close to free ports and file descriptors.
Verify settings
Print s.family and s.type after creation to confirm AF_INET/AF_INET6 and SOCK_STREAM/SOCK_DGRAM mappings.
Binding caution
Binding to 0.0.0.0 exposes services on all interfaces; use firewall rules and consider binding to specific addresses in production.
