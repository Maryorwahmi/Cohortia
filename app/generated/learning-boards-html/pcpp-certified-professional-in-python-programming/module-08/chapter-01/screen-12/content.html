Socket

An endpoint for program-to-program byte exchange identified by IP and port.

TCP vs UDP

TCP: reliable stream. UDP: fast datagrams. Choose by required guarantees.

Resource safety

Use with to ensure sockets close and handle exceptions to avoid leaks and port collisions.
