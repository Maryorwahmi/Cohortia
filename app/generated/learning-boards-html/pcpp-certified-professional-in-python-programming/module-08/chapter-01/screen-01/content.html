Foundations of Network Programming with Sockets

Learn how Python's socket module gives you low-level access to TCP and UDP. This chapter teaches the practical steps to create simple servers and clients, manage resources safely, and choose the right protocol for your application.

In this lesson
Socket basics, TCP vs UDP, example code, and safe patterns for resource and error handling.
Coming up
Echo servers and chat starter code, blocking vs non-blocking operations, and common pitfalls.
Why it matters
Networked programs power services, automation, and real-time systems — sockets are the letting point.
Concept map
Socket
Endpoint abstraction for sending and receiving bytes.
TCP
Connection-oriented stream with delivery guarantees.
UDP
Connectionless datagrams for low-latency, unordered traffic.
