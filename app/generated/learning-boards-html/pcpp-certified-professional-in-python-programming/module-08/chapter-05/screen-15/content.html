Final Notes › Three Reminders
Prefer async libraries
aiohttp and asyncpg avoid blocking the loop and scale better than thread-based workarounds.
Offload blocking work
Use run_in_executor or to_thread for unavoidable blocking operations.
Clean up resources
Always close writers and await wait_closed to avoid socket leaks and ensure graceful shutdown.
Closing thought
Asyncio is powerful for I/O-bound network work when you respect await boundaries and plan for blocking cases.
