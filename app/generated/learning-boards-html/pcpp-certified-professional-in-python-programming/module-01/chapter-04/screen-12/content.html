Question recap
Given an ABC Shape with abstract method area, which subclasses will not raise a TypeError at definition time? Analyze each case by checking implementations or new abstract declarations.
Circle (no area)
Does not implement area and is concrete — will raise TypeError.
Square (perimeter only)
Also missing area — will raise TypeError unless declared abstract.
Triangle (implements area)
Implements area — concrete and valid; will not raise TypeError.
Polygon (declares new abstract)
Adds its own abstract method; remains abstract and will not raise TypeError at definition time.
Key Takeaway

When checking for TypeError at definition, determine whether each subclass is concrete and complete or intentionally abstract.
