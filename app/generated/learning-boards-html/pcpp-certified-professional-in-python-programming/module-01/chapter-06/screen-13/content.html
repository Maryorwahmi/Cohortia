Module 2 › Chapter 1.6 › Pitfalls & debugging
Stateful iterator
Returning self from __iter__ causes the iterator to advance and not reset for a second loop. Test by iterating twice.
Exhausted generator
Generators are single-use. Converting to list exhausts it; recreate the generator to iterate again.
Leaking internals
Avoid returning references to internal lists unless you intend callers to modify them. Prefer returning new objects or read-only views.
Debugging checklist
  • Iterate twice to check reset behavior.
  • Convert generator to list and then again to confirm exhaustion.
  • Raise an exception inside with to confirm cleanup runs.
