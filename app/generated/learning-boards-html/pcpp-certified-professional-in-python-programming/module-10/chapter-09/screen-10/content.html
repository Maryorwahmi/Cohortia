Module 11 › Chapter 10.9 › Rotation & Safety
Rotation options
  • RotatingFileHandler — rotate by file size.
  • TimedRotatingFileHandler — rotate at time boundaries.
Decide retention based on how far back you need to debug incidents. Combine rotation with compression and log shipping for long-term storage.
Operational concerns
  • Avoid blocking the main thread with heavy synchronous logging.
  • Archive and protect rotated logs to preserve privacy and audit trails.
  • Monitor disk usage and alert before space becomes critical.
Key Takeaway
Treat logs as an operational artifact: manage volume, retention, and access to keep the system healthy.
