Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › What to Remember
tottime
Exclusive time in the function; optimize local heavy work here.
cumtime
Time for the function and all its callees; reveals expensive call chains.
Bottleneck
The part of the system limiting throughput or latency; optimize based on measured evidence.
Final takeaway: measure realistically, profile to find real hot spots, and validate every optimization with the same measurement before and after.
