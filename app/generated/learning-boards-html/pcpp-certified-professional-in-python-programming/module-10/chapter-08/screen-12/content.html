Module 10 › Chapter 10.8 › Key Concepts
Performance Testing
Measure speed and stability under representative workloads.
Profiling
Collect function-level stats to find hot spots by tottime and cumtime.
timeit
Run many iterations of a snippet to compare implementations reliably.
Summary: combine these tools. Use timeit for microbenchmarks, cProfile for whole programs, and the measurement workflow to validate any optimization.
