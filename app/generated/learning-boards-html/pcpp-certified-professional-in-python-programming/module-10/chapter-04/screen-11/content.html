Common assertions
  • assert_called_once_with(args) — single expected call.
  • assert_called_with(args) — verify a specific call occurred.
  • call_count and call_args_list — inspect sequences.
Best practice
Assert the contract the unit expects, not every internal detail of the collaborator.
Example
service.get_user_details(1)
MockRepo.return_value.get_user_by_id.assert_called_once_with(1)
For retry behavior assert call_count and examine call_args_list to ensure the same argument was retried.
