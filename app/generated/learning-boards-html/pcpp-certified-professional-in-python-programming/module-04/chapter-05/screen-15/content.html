Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › What to Remember
Handle Task results
Await tasks or attach done callbacks to surface exceptions and results.
gather vs wait
gather for ordered batches; wait for reactive first-completed or partial processing.
Use Semaphore
Limit concurrent I/O with async with semaphore to prevent overloads and rate-limit breaches.
Final takeaway
Combine these patterns intentionally: schedule work with Tasks, collect with gather or wait, and protect systems with Semaphores for reliable async programs.
