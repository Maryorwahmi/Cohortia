Definition
Deadlock, Livelock, Starvation
Common concurrency failures and simple mitigations you can apply.
Deadlock
Two or more participants block forever waiting for each other. Mitigate by consistent lock ordering, timeouts, and avoiding circular waits.
Livelock
Participants change state in response to others without progress. Mitigate with randomized back-off or stronger coordination.
Starvation
A participant never acquires a needed resource due to scheduling or contention. Use fairness strategies and supervisory policies to prevent long-term starvation.
Practical checks
  • Audit lock acquisition order across code paths to find cycles.
  • Add timeouts during development to reveal stuck waits.
  • Introduce monitoring to detect long waits and trigger recovery.
Warning

Notification and retries alone cannot fix structural deadlocks. You must change resource ordering or add recovery paths.
