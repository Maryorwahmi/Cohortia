Core Pattern

asyncio.gather

Gather schedules multiple awaitables and returns their results as a list or tuple when all complete.

Use-case: launching many independent fetches and waiting until they all return.
Behavior note: results are returned in the order of the awaitables you passed, not in completion order.
Example and tips
  • Call coroutine functions to obtain coroutine objects before passing to gather.
  • Gather raises the first exception by default; use return_exceptions to collect errors as results.
  • For fine control over cancellation and progress, use create_task and inspect Task states.
Pro tip

If you need results as they complete, combine create_task with asyncio.as_completed for streaming results.
