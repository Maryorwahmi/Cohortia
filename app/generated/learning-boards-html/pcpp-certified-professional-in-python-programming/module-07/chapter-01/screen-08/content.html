Definition › Why closing matters

Risks of not closing

  • Buffered writes may not be flushed, causing data loss.
  • Open file descriptor limits can be reached, causing errors.
  • Other programs may be blocked from modifying or removing the file.
Misconception check
Relying on finalizers or garbage collection to close files is brittle; behavior varies across interpreters and execution timing.
Key Takeaway

Always close files deterministically using close() or the with statement to ensure data integrity and resource cleanup.
