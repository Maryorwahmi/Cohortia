Comparison Why split fails
str.split vs csv.reader
See how each approach handles quoted commas and newlines.
Naive split example
line = 'Alice,New York,"A vibrant city, known for its culture."'
parts = line.split(',')
# parts becomes ['Alice','New York','"A vibrant city',' known for its culture."']
Symptom: wrong number of fields and split inside quoted text. This corrupts downstream processing and may raise index errors or bad imports.
Common mistake
Assuming commas only separate fields. Quoted fields are allowed by the format; splitting ignores quotes.
csv.reader solution
import csv
row = 'Alice,New York,"A vibrant city, known for its culture."'
for parsed in csv.reader([row]):
  print(parsed)
# prints ['Alice', 'New York', 'A vibrant city, known for its culture.']
csv.reader respects quoting and yields the correct field list. It also supports custom delimiters and quoting parameters for different formats.
Key Takeaway
Always use a CSV-aware parser for files that may include quoted separators or newlines.
