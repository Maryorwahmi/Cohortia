Ethical Challenge

Navigating Out-of-Scope Discoveries

Responding to Unexpected and Sensitive Findings

Scenario: Egregious Out-of-Scope Content

During a routine penetration test, you uncover evidence of illegal activity (e.g., child exploitation material) on a client's server, which is explicitly outside the agreed-upon scope of your engagement.

Ethical Dilemma

Your duty to the client vs. your moral and legal obligation to report a serious crime.

What NOT to Do

Do NOT include the finding in your pentest report. Do NOT exploit the vulnerability further. Do NOT confront the client directly without legal guidance.

Professional & Ethical Response

  • Document Securely: Record the finding for your own legal protection, but keep it separate from the pentest report.
  • Seek Legal Counsel: Consult with your own legal team immediately for guidance on reporting obligations.
  • Report via Legal Channels: If advised, report to appropriate law enforcement agencies, not as part of your pentest deliverable.
Key Takeaway

Extreme out-of-scope findings require a legal, not technical, reporting pathway to ensure proper handling and protection.
