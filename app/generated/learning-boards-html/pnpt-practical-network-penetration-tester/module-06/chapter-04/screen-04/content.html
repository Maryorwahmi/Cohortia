Core Process

The Critical Debriefing Meeting

Presenting Findings and Planning Remediation

Key Stages of a Debriefing

1

Present Findings

Walk through the report, highlighting key vulnerabilities and risks.

2

Clarify & Answer

Address client questions and clarify any ambiguities in the report.

3

Discuss Remediation

Collaborate on a roadmap for fixing identified vulnerabilities.

4

Gather Feedback

Solicit client feedback on the engagement process and report.

Pro-Tip

Prepare for diverse audiences; tailor your presentation to both technical and executive stakeholders, focusing on impact and business risk.

Effective Communication Principles

  • Clarity: Use plain language, avoid jargon where possible.
  • Active Listening: Pay attention to client concerns and questions.
  • Collaboration: Frame discussions around joint problem-solving.
  • Professionalism: Maintain composure, even when challenged.
Key Takeaway

A well-executed debriefing solidifies client trust and ensures findings translate into actionable security improvements.
