Ethical Scope Management

Responding to Out-of-Scope Vulnerabilities

Question:

During a penetration test, you discover a severe vulnerability in a system that was explicitly out of scope for the engagement. What is the most ethical and professional course of action?

A)

Exploit the vulnerability immediately to demonstrate its severity and include it in your report.

B)

Ignore the vulnerability entirely since it's out of scope.

C)

Inform the client immediately about the out-of-scope finding, without exploiting it, and await their written instructions.

D)

Document the finding but only mention it verbally during the debriefing.

Correct Answer & Explanation:

C)

Inform the client immediately about the out-of-scope finding, without exploiting it, and await their written instructions.

Operating strictly within the agreed-upon scope is a fundamental ethical principle. While discovering an out-of-scope vulnerability is valuable, exploiting it without permission is unethical and potentially illegal. The correct approach is to inform the client and let them decide how to proceed, potentially amending the scope with written consent.

Key Takeaway

Always respect the scope of engagement; report out-of-scope findings without exploitation and await client instructions.
