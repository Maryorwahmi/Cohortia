Key Concepts

Upholding Professional Standards

Scope Creep

Expanding testing activities beyond the agreed-upon scope without client authorization.

Client Trust

The reliance a client places on the penetration tester's integrity, professionalism, and expertise.

Conflict of Interest

A situation where competing professional or personal interests could make it difficult to fulfill duties impartially.

Chapter Summary

Upholding professional standards, managing scope, building trust, and avoiding conflicts of interest are paramount for ethical and successful penetration testing.
