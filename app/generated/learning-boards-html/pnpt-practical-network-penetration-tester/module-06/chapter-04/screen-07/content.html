Ethical Foundation

The Bedrock of Professional Ethics

Operating Within Scope and Responsibility

Core Ethical Principles

  • Scope Adherence: Never deviate without explicit written permission.
  • Do No Harm: Avoid disrupting critical business operations unless agreed.
  • Data Minimization: Only exfiltrate/retain data necessary for reporting.
Warning

Unauthorized actions, even if well-intentioned, can be considered illegal and unethical, leading to severe consequences.

Unethical Actions to Avoid

  • Scope Creep: Expanding testing activities beyond agreed scope.
  • Exploiting for Curiosity: Testing vulnerabilities purely for personal interest.
  • Unauthorized Data Retention: Keeping sensitive data longer than necessary.
Key Takeaway

Your ethical compass is your most important tool; always prioritize scope, permission, and the principle of 'do no harm'.
