Common Misconception

Simply having a strong password on your pentest machine is not enough; data itself must be encrypted and access controlled.

Secure Handling Principles

  • Encryption: Encrypt all stored evidence at rest.
  • Restricted Access: Implement strict access controls (least privilege).
  • Audit Trails: Maintain logs of who accessed what and when.
  • Policy Adherence: Follow client's data retention and security policies.
Key Takeaway

Treat all client data as highly sensitive; secure storage and access controls are non-negotiable ethical and legal requirements.
