Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Post-Engagement

Debriefing Meeting

A post-engagement session with the client to present findings, clarify the report, and discuss remediation.

Secure Data Destruction

The process of permanently erasing sensitive client data and evidence to prevent recovery.

Ethical Hacking

Conducting penetration tests with explicit permission, within scope, and adhering to legal and moral guidelines.

Chapter Summary

Post-engagement activities and professional ethics are critical for ensuring client satisfaction, legal compliance, and building a trusted reputation in cybersecurity.
