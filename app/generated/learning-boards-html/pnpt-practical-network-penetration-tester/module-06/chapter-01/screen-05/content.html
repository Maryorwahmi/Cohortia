Tailoring Your Message for Diverse Stakeholders

Communicating Effectively with Technical Teams, Management, and Executives

Technical Teams

Require granular details, command outputs, specific remediation steps, and technical explanations. Focus on 'how to fix it'.

Deep Dive

Management

Need to understand operational impact, resource implications, project timelines, and risk prioritization. Focus on 'what it means for operations'.

Operational View

Executives

Require a high-level overview of overall risk posture, key findings, and strategic recommendations. Focus on 'what it means for the business'.

Strategic Outlook
Pro Tip

Always start with the executive summary, then drill down into technical details. This ensures leadership gets the critical overview first.
