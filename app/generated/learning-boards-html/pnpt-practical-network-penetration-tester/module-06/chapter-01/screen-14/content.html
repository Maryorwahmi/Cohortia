Key Concepts: Report Essentials

Fundamental Elements for Effective Penetration Testing Reports

Executive Summary

A high-level overview of the engagement, key findings, and overall risk, tailored for non-technical leadership.

Scope and Methodology

Defines the boundaries of the test, what was included/excluded, and the techniques used during the assessment.

Technical Findings

Detailed descriptions of each vulnerability, including its description, impact, evidence, and remediation.

Summary Takeaway

These core sections ensure your report is comprehensive, credible, and effectively communicates critical security information to all stakeholders.
