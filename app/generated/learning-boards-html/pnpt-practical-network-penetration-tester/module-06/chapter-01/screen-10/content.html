Crafting Clear and Concise Technical Narratives

Avoiding Jargon and Using Active Voice for Effective Communication

Clarity Over Complexity

Use simple language and explain technical terms when necessary to ensure broad understanding.

Active Voice & Strong Verbs

Make your writing direct and authoritative. Example: "We identified" instead of "It was observed."

Logical Structure

Guide the reader through your findings with coherent sentences and paragraphs.

Key Takeaway

Effective technical writing prioritizes clarity, conciseness, and active voice to ensure your report is understood and acted upon.

Good vs. Bad Writing

❌ Passive & Vague

"It was observed that the server was vulnerable to a known exploit."

✅ Active & Direct

"We identified the server as vulnerable to a known exploit."

❌ Jargon-Heavy

"The XSS vulnerability allowed for arbitrary script execution via DOM manipulation."

✅ Clear & Explained

"The Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) vulnerability allowed an attacker to execute malicious code in a user's browser."
