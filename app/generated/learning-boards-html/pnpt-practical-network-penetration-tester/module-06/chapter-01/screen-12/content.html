Ensuring Credibility: Common Mistakes and How to Prevent Them

Insufficient Evidence, Lack of Business Context, and Unclear Language

Insufficient Evidence

Making claims without verifiable proof (screenshots, command outputs, logs). Always back your findings.

Lack of Proof

No Business Impact

Presenting technical issues without explaining their real-world consequences for the client's operations or finances.

Missing Context

Unclear Language

Using excessive jargon, passive voice, or convoluted sentences that make the report difficult to read and understand.

Confusing Terms
Key Takeaway

Proactively avoiding common reporting mistakes ensures your report is credible, persuasive, and drives effective security improvements.
