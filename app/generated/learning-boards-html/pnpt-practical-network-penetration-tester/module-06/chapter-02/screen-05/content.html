Best Practices

Crafting an Impactful Executive Summary

Focus on key elements and business implications to ensure your summary drives action.

  • •State the purpose of the test clearly.
  • •Summarize overall findings and most significant risks.
  • •Provide high-level, strategic recommendations.
  • •Avoid technical jargon; focus on the 'so what?' for the business.
Key Takeaway

An effective Executive Summary translates technical risks into tangible business consequences, guiding strategic decision-making and resource allocation.

Business Impact Examples
  • •Data exfiltration leading to regulatory fines.
  • •Disruption of critical business services.
  • •Reputational damage and loss of customer trust.
  • •Non-compliance with industry standards.
High-Level Recommendations
  • •Implement a comprehensive patch management program.
  • •Enhance network segmentation.
  • •Conduct regular security awareness training.
