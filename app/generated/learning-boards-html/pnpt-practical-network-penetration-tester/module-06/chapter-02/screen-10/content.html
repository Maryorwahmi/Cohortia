Categorizing Vulnerabilities: Severity Ratings

Assigning severity levels helps clients prioritize remediation based on exploitability and potential impact.

  • •Based on exploitability and potential impact.
  • •Guides clients on immediate vs. later attention.
  • •Requires consistency and clear definitions.
Key Takeaway

Consistent severity ratings empower clients to make informed decisions about security investments and resource allocation.

CCritical

Easily exploitable, leads to severe consequences (e.g., RCE, data loss).

HHigh

Exploitable, significant impact (e.g., privilege escalation, sensitive data access).

MMedium

Requires more effort to exploit, moderate impact (e.g., information disclosure, minor misconfiguration).

LLow

Difficult to exploit, minimal impact (e.g., outdated software version without known exploits).

IInformational

Observations that don't pose direct risk but could be best practices (e.g., verbose error messages).
