Core Concept

The Executive Summary: Your Report's Elevator Pitch

The Executive Summary is the most crucial section, providing a high-level overview for non-technical stakeholders.

  • •Often the only part read by high-level executives and board members.
  • •Must be concise, impactful, and articulate critical findings.
  • •Focuses on business implications, not technical jargon.
Key Takeaway

A well-crafted Executive Summary translates complex security risks into understandable business impacts, motivating decision-makers.

Audience Focus

Primarily for high-level executives, board members, and non-technical stakeholders who need a quick, clear understanding.

Business Impact

Translates technical risks into tangible consequences for business operations, data, reputation, and compliance.

Call to Action

Motivates decision-makers to understand the urgency and allocate resources for remediation.
