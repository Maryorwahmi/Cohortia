Risk Assessment

Quantifying Risk: The Impact Statement

Connects the technical flaw to potential business consequences, helping stakeholders understand the 'so what?'.

  • •Translates technical flaws into business risks.
  • •Uses language that resonates with business concerns (e.g., financial, reputational).
  • •Articulates potential consequences if exploited.
Key Takeaway

Impact statements are crucial for translating technical vulnerabilities into business risks, enabling clients to prioritize remediation based on potential harm to operations, data, and reputation.

Common Business Impacts
  • •Full system compromise leading to data exfiltration.
  • •Unauthorized access to sensitive customer data.
  • •Disruption of critical business services.
  • •Regulatory fines and reputational damage.
Quantifying Risk

Use specific terms like 'breach of PII,' 'incurring regulatory fines,' or 'disruption of critical services' to make the impact concrete.
