Prioritizing Vulnerability Remediation

Guiding Clients with Limited Resources to Address Critical Risks First

Why Prioritize?

Clients have limited resources. Prioritization ensures the most severe and exploitable vulnerabilities are addressed first, maximizing risk reduction.

Severity as a Guide

Use your Critical, High, Medium, Low ratings as a primary guide, but refine based on exploitability and business impact.

Phased Approach

Suggest phases (e.g., Phase 1: Critical/High within 30 days; Phase 2: Medium within 90 days) to manage client efforts.

Key Takeaway

Effective prioritization transforms a list of problems into a strategic, manageable plan for security improvement.

Prioritization Hierarchy
Critical

Immediate, easily exploitable, full compromise.

High

Significant impact, moderate exploitability.

Medium

Moderate impact, requires specific conditions.

Low

Minimal impact, difficult to exploit.
