Pitfall 2: Ignoring Root Causes and New Risks

Holistic Solutions for Sustainable Security Improvement

Address Root Causes

Don't just fix symptoms; identify and address the underlying reason for the vulnerability to prevent recurrence.

Avoid New Risks

Ensure proposed solutions don't introduce new vulnerabilities or become overly complex for the client to manage.

Practicality & Maintainability

Always consider if the client can realistically implement and maintain the solution long-term.

Key Takeaway

Sustainable security comes from holistic solutions that address root causes and are practical for the client to maintain.

Root Cause vs. Symptom
Symptom: Weak Passwords

Users are choosing simple, easily guessable passwords.

Root Cause: No Policy Enforcement

Lack of robust password policy or MFA implementation.

Holistic Solution: Policy + MFA

Implement strong password policy AND deploy MFA for all critical accounts.
