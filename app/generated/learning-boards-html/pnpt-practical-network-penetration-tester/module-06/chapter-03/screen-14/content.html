Essential Remediation Concepts

Core Principles for Effective Vulnerability Management

Remediation Strategies

Actionable plans and steps to fix identified vulnerabilities, transforming findings into solutions.

Mitigation

Short-term measures to reduce the impact or likelihood of a vulnerability being exploited, often temporary.

Long-Term Solutions

Permanent fixes that address the root cause of vulnerabilities, often involving architectural or policy changes.

Summary Takeaway

Effective remediation requires a clear understanding of both immediate risk reduction and sustainable, root-cause fixes.
