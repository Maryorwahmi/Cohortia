Applying Remediation Principles

Key Ideas for Actionable and Sustainable Security Improvements

Prioritization

Ranking recommendations based on severity, exploitability, and business impact to guide client efforts.

Actionable Advice

Recommendations that are specific, clear, and practical for the client to implement, avoiding vagueness.

Root Cause Analysis

Identifying the underlying reason for a vulnerability to ensure remediation addresses the fundamental problem, not just symptoms.

Summary Takeaway

By applying these principles, you can provide impactful, sustainable security guidance that truly enhances client defenses.
