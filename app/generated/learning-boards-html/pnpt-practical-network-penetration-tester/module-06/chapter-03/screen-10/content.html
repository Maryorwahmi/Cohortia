Your Role: Expert Guidance, Not Dictation

Empowering Clients to Make Informed Security Decisions

Provide Expert Guidance

Offer advice based on your findings, empowering clients to make informed decisions for their unique environment.

  • •Be prepared to discuss and clarify your advice.
  • •Explain potential trade-offs and implications.

Avoid Dictating Solutions

Do not impose solutions; the client retains ultimate responsibility and knowledge of their operations.

  • •Respect client autonomy in decision-making.
  • •Focus on collaboration, not command.
Key Takeaway

A well-crafted remediation section transforms your report into a valuable roadmap for security enhancement, fostering client ownership.
