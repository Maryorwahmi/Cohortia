Pitfall 1: Vague or Unrealistic Advice

Ensuring Your Advice is Both Practical and Implementable

Vague or Insufficient Advice

Recommendations that lack specificity or context are unhelpful and difficult to act upon.

Example of Poor Advice

"Fix all vulnerabilities." or "Use strong passwords."

  • •Lacks specific steps for implementation.
  • •Doesn't consider client's current capabilities.

Specific & Holistic Advice

Recommendations that are clear, actionable, and address the root cause comprehensively.

Example of Better Advice

"Implement a robust password policy (14+ chars, complexity, rotation) and deploy MFA for critical systems."

  • •Provides clear, actionable steps.
  • •Addresses the problem holistically.
Consider Client Context

Always consider the client's operational context, budget, and existing infrastructure when formulating recommendations.
