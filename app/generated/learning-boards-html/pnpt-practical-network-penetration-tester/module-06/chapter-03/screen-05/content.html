Short-Term Mitigations vs. Long-Term Fixes

Balancing Immediate Risk Reduction with Sustainable Security

Immediate Mitigations

Quick, temporary fixes to reduce immediate risk. They buy time for permanent solutions.

  • •Disabling a vulnerable service temporarily.
  • •Blocking specific IP addresses at the firewall.
  • •Implementing a temporary workaround to bypass a flaw.
Example

Block external access to an exposed management port at the perimeter firewall.

Long-Term Solutions

Permanent fixes that address the root cause of vulnerabilities for sustainable security.

  • •Changing default credentials and enforcing strong policies.
  • •Implementing multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • •Placing services behind a VPN or internal network segment.
Example

Change default credentials, implement MFA, and place the service behind a VPN.

Pro-Tip: SMART Recommendations

Ensure recommendations are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound for maximum effectiveness.
