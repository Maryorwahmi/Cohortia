Roadmap Deep Dive

Resources and Community Engagement

Leverage industry resources and active participation to stay updated and connected.

Industry Blogs & News Feeds

Regularly read SANS Internet Storm Center, BleepingComputer, and PortSwigger for threat intelligence.

CTF Platforms & Home Labs

Practice new skills on Hack The Box, TryHackMe, and build your own vulnerable lab environments.

Online Forums & Social Media

Engage with communities like Reddit's r/netsec and follow leading security researchers.

Local Meetups & Conferences

Network with peers, mentors, and potential employers at industry events.

Key Takeaway

A multi-faceted approach to learning and networking is crucial for sustained growth.
