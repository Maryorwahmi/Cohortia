Your Journey Beyond PNPT: Next Steps

Continue building your skills and career in the dynamic field of cybersecurity.

Explore OSCP, eWPT, or CRTP to specialize and deepen your practical exploitation skills.

Join online communities and attend conferences to network, share knowledge, and stay informed.

Dive into cloud, mobile, or IoT security, and develop personal scripts or research new attack vectors.

Key Takeaway

Your journey as a penetration tester is ongoing; embrace curiosity, persistence, and ethical conduct.
