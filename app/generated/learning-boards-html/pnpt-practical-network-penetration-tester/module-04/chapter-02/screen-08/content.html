Kerberos Attack

The Power of Kerberoasting

Harvesting NTLM hashes from Service Principal Names (SPNs) for high-value service accounts.

Understanding SPNs

  • •**Service Principal Name (SPN):** Unique identifier for a service instance using Kerberos.
  • •**Service Ticket Encryption:** KDC encrypts service tickets with the *service account's* NTLM hash.
  • •**Offline Cracking:** Attacker extracts encrypted ticket and cracks the hash offline.
Key Takeaway

Kerberoasting is a potent attack for privilege escalation, targeting service accounts whose NTLM hashes can be extracted from SPN service tickets and cracked offline.

Why Service Accounts are Valuable

Key reasons why compromising service accounts is a high-impact objective.
