Key Concepts: Advanced Credential Harvesting

Understanding Kerberoasting, SPNs, Responder, and NTLM hashes.

Kerberoasting

A Kerberos attack targeting Service Principal Names (SPNs) to obtain service account NTLM hashes.

Service Principal Name (SPN)

A unique identifier for a service instance that uses Kerberos authentication.

Responder

A tool used to poison LLMNR, NBT-NS, and MDNS requests to capture NTLMv1/v2 hashes.

Summary Takeaway

These advanced techniques, including understanding SPNs and NTLM hashes, are crucial for comprehensive credential harvesting and privilege escalation in Active Directory.
