Core Service

DNS: The Navigator of Active Directory

Absolutely vital for clients to locate domain controllers and other services.

DNS's Role in AD
  • Clients use DNS to find Domain Controllers (DCs).
  • Locates other AD services (e.g., Global Catalog).
  • Essential for name resolution within the domain.
Impact of DNS Issues

Misconfigured or compromised DNS can lead to AD instability, authentication failures, and significant security vulnerabilities, such as service impersonation.

Key Takeaway

DNS is a foundational service for Active Directory; its proper functioning is critical for AD's stability and security, making it a prime target for reconnaissance.
