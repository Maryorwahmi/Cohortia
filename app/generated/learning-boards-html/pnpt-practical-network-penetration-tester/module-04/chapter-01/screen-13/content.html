Reconnaissance Tools

Scanning AD Ports with Nmap

Identifying open ports and services on discovered Domain Controllers.

Common AD Ports
  • 53/TCP/UDP: DNS
  • 88/TCP/UDP: Kerberos
  • 135/TCP: RPC
  • 389/TCP/UDP: LDAP
  • 445/TCP: SMB
  • 636/TCP: LDAPS
  • 3268/TCP: Global Catalog LDAP
Nmap Scan Benefits

Reveals running services, operating system details, and potential vulnerabilities, helping to map the DC's attack surface.

Key Takeaway

Targeted Nmap scans on common AD ports provide crucial insights into the services running on a Domain Controller, guiding further enumeration.

# Basic Nmap scan for common AD ports nmap -p 53,88,135,139,389,445,464,636,3268,3269 -sV -sC <DC_IP>
