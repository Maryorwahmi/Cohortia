AD Architecture

Trees and Forests: Scaling AD

Hierarchical organization for multiple domains and shared infrastructure.

What is a Tree?

A grouping of one or more domains that share a common, contiguous DNS namespace (e.g., corp.local and sales.corp.local).

What is a Forest?

The highest logical boundary in AD, consisting of one or more trees. All domains in a forest share a common schema, configuration, and global catalog, with implicit trust relationships.

Key Takeaway

Forests establish the ultimate security boundary and trust relationships across an entire Active Directory deployment.
