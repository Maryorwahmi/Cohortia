AD Architecture

Organizational Units (OUs): Delegated Control

Containers for granular administration and Group Policy application.

What is an OU?

A container within a domain used to organize objects (users, computers, groups) and delegate administrative tasks.

Key Benefits
  • Delegate specific administrative permissions.
  • Apply Group Policy Objects (GPOs) to specific sets of objects.
  • Mirror organizational structure for easier management.
Key Takeaway

OUs provide granular control and policy application, making them valuable for understanding administrative boundaries during reconnaissance.
