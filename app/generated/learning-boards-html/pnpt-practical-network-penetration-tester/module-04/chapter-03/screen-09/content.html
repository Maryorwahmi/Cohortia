Delegation Abuse

Constrained Delegation and Resource-Based Constrained Delegation (RBCD)

Exploring more nuanced delegation attacks and their exploitation scenarios.

Constrained Delegation

Restricts delegation to specific services. Compromised service account can impersonate users only for those services.

Resource-Based Constrained Delegation (RBCD)

Target service specifies who can delegate to it. Attacker controlling a computer object can configure RBCD to grant admin access.

  • •**Constrained:** Safer than unconstrained, but still abusable for specific services.
  • •**RBCD Control:** The *target service* dictates delegation permissions.
  • •**RBCD Attack:** Compromise a computer object, configure RBCD to grant a compromised service account admin access to that computer.
Key Takeaway

Constrained Delegation allows impersonation for specific services, while RBCD enables a compromised account to gain administrative access to a controlled computer.
