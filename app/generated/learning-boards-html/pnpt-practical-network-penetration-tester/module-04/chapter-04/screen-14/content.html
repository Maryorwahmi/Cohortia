Key Concepts: Core Principles

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Persistence

Techniques used to maintain access to a compromised system or network, even after initial access is detected or remediated.

Lateral Movement

The process of moving from one compromised system to another within a network to expand control or reach high-value targets.

Golden Ticket

A forged Kerberos Ticket Granting Ticket (TGT) that grants an attacker arbitrary privileges in an Active Directory domain.

Chapter Summary

Persistence and lateral movement are critical post-exploitation phases. Golden Tickets represent a powerful Kerberos-based persistence mechanism, offering domain-wide arbitrary privileges.
