Key Concepts: Mechanisms & Tools

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Silver Ticket

A forged Kerberos Service Ticket (ST) that grants an attacker access to a specific service on a specific server.

krbtgt

The Kerberos service account in Active Directory, whose hash is critical for forging Golden Tickets.

Mimikatz

A post-exploitation tool that extracts plaintext passwords, hashes, and Kerberos tickets from Windows memory, and can forge tickets.

Chapter Summary

Silver Tickets offer targeted service access, while the krbtgt hash is fundamental for Golden Tickets. Mimikatz is a key tool for both extracting credentials and forging tickets.
