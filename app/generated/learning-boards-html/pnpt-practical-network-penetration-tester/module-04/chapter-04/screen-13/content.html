Cleanup and Detection Avoidance

Minimizing forensic traces during post-exploitation activities

Cleanup Best Practices

  • Remove temporary files and tools.
  • Delete any created scheduled tasks.
  • Remove rogue accounts or group memberships.
  • Revert any modified configurations or GPOs.

Detection Avoidance Strategies

  • Be mindful of logging on target systems.
  • Use noisy tools judiciously.
  • Plan movements carefully to avoid triggering alerts.
  • Blend in with normal network traffic patterns.
Key Takeaway

Effective cleanup and detection avoidance are crucial for realistic penetration testing, minimizing forensic traces and simulating advanced attacker stealth.

Common Mistakes

  • Leaving behind easily detectable artifacts.
  • Failing to verify persistence mechanisms.
  • Moving laterally without a clear objective or plan.
  • Not understanding the logging capabilities of target systems.

Pro-Tip: Verify & Document

Always verify that your persistence mechanisms work before concluding your test. Document all changes made for proper cleanup and reporting, ensuring no lingering access points.
