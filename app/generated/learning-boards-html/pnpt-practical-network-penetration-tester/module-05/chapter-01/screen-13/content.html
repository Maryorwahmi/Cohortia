The Ethical Compass: Legal & Scope Boundaries
Ensuring responsible and authorized web application penetration testing.
1
Get Written Permission

Always obtain explicit, written authorization from the client before starting any test.

2
Define Scope Clearly

Specify exact URLs, IP ranges, allowed test types, and data handling policies.

3
Handle Data Responsibly

Protect any sensitive data discovered and adhere to client's privacy policies.

4
Document & Communicate

Maintain detailed records of findings and communicate regularly with the client.

5
Avoid Harm

Your primary goal is to identify vulnerabilities, not to cause system disruption or data loss.

Key Takeaway

Ethical and legal compliance is paramount; always operate with explicit permission, a clear scope, and a commitment to responsible disclosure.
