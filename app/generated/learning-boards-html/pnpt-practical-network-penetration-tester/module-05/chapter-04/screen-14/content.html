Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Authentication

The process of verifying the identity of a user, typically through credentials like username and password.

Authorization

The process of determining what an authenticated user is permitted to do or access within an application.

Broken Authentication

Vulnerabilities allowing attackers to bypass login mechanisms, impersonate users, or gain unauthorized access.

Credential Stuffing

Using leaked username/password pairs from one breach to attempt logins on other applications.

Summary Takeaway

Authentication and authorization are distinct but equally vital security controls, and their weaknesses are frequently exploited.
