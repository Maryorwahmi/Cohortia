Core Concept

Broken Authorization

When Access Restrictions Fail

What is Broken Authorization?

  • Application fails to enforce proper access restrictions.
  • Authenticated users access resources or actions they shouldn't.
  • Often leads to privilege escalation and unauthorized data access.
Key Takeaway

Broken Authorization compromises access control, allowing users to exceed their intended permissions.

Privilege Escalation

  • A low-privileged user gains access to higher-privileged functions.
  • Example: A regular user accessing administrative settings.
  • Directly violates the principle of least privilege.
Impact Scenario

A customer views or modifies another customer's order details on an e-commerce platform.
