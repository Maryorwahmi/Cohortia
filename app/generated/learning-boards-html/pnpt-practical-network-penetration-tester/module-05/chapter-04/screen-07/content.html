Authentication Flaw

Insecure Session Management

Hijacking User Sessions for Unauthorized Access

Session Tokens & Their Role

  • Session tokens (cookies) maintain a user's logged-in state.
  • They act as temporary keys, avoiding repeated login prompts.
  • Crucial for a seamless user experience after authentication.
Common Mistake

Not setting `HttpOnly` and `Secure` flags on session cookies, making them vulnerable to XSS and insecure transmission.

Session Hijacking

  • Attackers steal valid session tokens to impersonate legitimate users.
  • Occurs if tokens are predictable, not invalidated, or transmitted insecurely.
  • Allows full access to the victim's account without their password.
Key Takeaway

Secure session management is paramount; tokens must be unpredictable, properly invalidated, and transmitted securely (HTTPS).
