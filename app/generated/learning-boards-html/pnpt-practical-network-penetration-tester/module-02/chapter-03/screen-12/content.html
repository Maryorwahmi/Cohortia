Ethical Hacking

Ethical Boundaries and Impact Assessment

Prioritizing integrity and availability before any exploitation attempt.

Scope of Engagement: Ensure all actions are strictly within the agreed-upon scope and authorized targets.

Impact Assessment: Evaluate potential side effects of exploits, including system instability or service disruption.

Written Authorization: Never proceed without explicit, written permission from the target organization.

Key Takeaway

Ethical penetration testing demands strict adherence to scope, impact assessment, and explicit written authorization before any exploitation.

Ethical Considerations

Questions to Ask Before Exploitation

Is this action within the scope of my engagement?

What are the potential side effects if the exploit fails?

Could this disrupt critical services or cause system instability?

Do I have explicit, written authorization for this specific action?

Real-World Connection

The Importance of a 'Rules of Engagement' Document

A clear 'Rules of Engagement' document, signed by all parties, is your legal and ethical shield. It defines what you can and cannot do, protecting both you and the client.
