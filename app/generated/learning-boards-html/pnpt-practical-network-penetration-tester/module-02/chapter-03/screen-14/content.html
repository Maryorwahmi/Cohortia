Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from Initial Access: Exploitation Fundamentals.

Initial Access

The phase in a penetration test where an attacker gains a first foothold or access to a target system or network.

Weak Credentials

Easily guessable, default, or reused usernames and passwords vulnerable to brute-force or dictionary attacks.

Unpatched Software

Software with known security vulnerabilities that have not been fixed by applying updates or patches.

Chapter Summary

Initial access is the critical step of gaining a foothold by exploiting vulnerabilities like weak credentials and unpatched software, forming the foundation of a penetration test.
