Key Concepts

Foundational Elements of Active Reconnaissance

Active Reconnaissance

Gathering information by directly interacting with target systems, sending packets and analyzing responses.

Nmap

A powerful open-source network scanner for host discovery, port scanning, and service detection.

TCP SYN Scan (`-sS`)

A stealthy 'half-open' port scan that avoids completing the TCP handshake.

TCP Connect Scan (`-sT`)

A full TCP handshake port scan, less stealthy but reliable when SYN scans are blocked.

UDP Scan (`-sU`)

A port scan specifically for UDP services, often slower and less reliable than TCP scans.

Summary Takeaway

Active reconnaissance leverages Nmap's diverse scanning capabilities, from stealthy SYN scans to comprehensive UDP scans, to map target networks effectively.
