TCP Connect (`-sT`) and UDP (`-sU`) Scans

Balancing Stealth with Reliability for Comprehensive Port Discovery

TCP Connect (`-sT`)

Completes full TCP handshake; less stealthy but reliable when SYN scans are blocked.

UDP Scan (`-sU`)

Discovers UDP services (DNS, SNMP, DHCP); slower and less reliable due to stateless nature.

When to Use Each

Strategic Scan Selection

  • •TCP Connect: Use when SYN scans are blocked by firewalls, accepting higher detection risk.
  • •UDP Scan: Essential for identifying critical services that communicate over UDP.
  • •Consider combining scans for a complete picture, starting with stealthier options.
Key Takeaway

Choosing between TCP Connect and UDP scans depends on the target's defenses and the type of services you aim to discover, balancing stealth with thoroughness.
