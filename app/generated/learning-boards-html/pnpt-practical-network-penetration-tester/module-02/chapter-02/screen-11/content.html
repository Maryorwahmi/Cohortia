Common Mistakes in Active Reconnaissance (Part 1)

Avoiding Detection and Unintended Consequences

Too Aggressive Scans

Launching overly aggressive scans can trigger IDS/firewalls, leading to IP blocking or alerts.

Start Less Intrusive

Always begin with stealthier scans and progressively increase aggression as permitted by scope.

Misconception

Aggression Equals Speed

A common mistake is believing that more aggressive scans will always yield faster or better results. In reality, they often lead to early detection and a blocked assessment, hindering overall progress. Stealth and a phased approach are more effective for long-term success.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize stealth and a gradual approach in active reconnaissance to avoid detection and ensure a successful, uninterrupted penetration test.
