Active Reconnaissance: Direct Engagement

Transitioning from Passive to Proactive Information Gathering

Direct Interaction

Involves sending packets to target systems to elicit responses, revealing network details.

Higher Risk of Detection

Requires precision and stealth to avoid triggering intrusion detection systems or firewalls.

Key Distinction

Passive vs. Active Reconnaissance

Passive

No direct interaction; relies on public data.

Active

Direct interaction; sends packets to target.

Key Takeaway

Active reconnaissance provides deeper insights but demands careful execution to balance information gathering with detection avoidance.
