Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential takeaways from this chapter

Google Dorking

Using advanced search engine operators to find specific, often sensitive, information exposed on public websites.

Shodan

A search engine for internet-connected devices that indexes banners and other metadata to identify exposed services and their versions.

OSINT

The practice of collecting and analyzing information from publicly available sources to produce actionable intelligence.

Summary Takeaway

Advanced search techniques like Google Dorking and Shodan, underpinned by OSINT principles, are powerful tools for comprehensive and ethical passive reconnaissance.
