What is Shodan?

Shodan is a search engine that indexes internet-connected devices and services, rather than just websites. It collects banner information and metadata from various devices, providing insights into their configurations and potential vulnerabilities.

  • Indexes devices like routers, servers, webcams, and IoT devices.
  • Collects data on open ports, service versions, and operating systems.
  • Reveals a target's external attack surface without direct scanning.
  • Powerful for identifying exposed services and their associated risks.
Key Information Provided

Device types, open ports, service banners, geographical location, and potential vulnerabilities associated with specific software versions.

Shodan Search Examples
# Find devices belonging to 'Example Corp' with port 22 (SSH) open
org:"Example Corp" port:22

# Search for webcams in a specific city
webcam city:"London"

# Find Apache web servers running on port 80 with specific version
apache port:80 version:2.4.41

# Discover databases exposed to the internet
product:"MongoDB" port:27017
Key Takeaway

Shodan provides a unique, passive view of a target's internet-facing devices and services, helping identify potential entry points and vulnerabilities.
