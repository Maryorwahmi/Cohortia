Ethical Conduct in Reconnaissance

Passive reconnaissance must always be conducted within strict ethical and legal boundaries. The primary goal is to gather intelligence, not to engage in unauthorized access or violate privacy.

  • Only gather publicly available information.
  • Do not attempt to bypass security controls or access private data.
  • Respect privacy laws and the scope of your engagement.
  • Focus on improving security, not causing harm.
Misconception

'Publicly available' does not mean 'free to exploit'. Ethical boundaries still apply to how information is used.
