What is Passive Reconnaissance?

It's the process of gathering information about a target without directly interacting with their systems. This means no network scans, no direct probes, and no actions that could leave a trace on the target's logs.

  • Relies on publicly available information (OSINT).
  • Minimizes risk of detection by the target's security team.
  • Builds a comprehensive profile of the target's digital footprint.
  • Informs subsequent, more active stages of a penetration test.
Why it Matters

Stealthy information gathering allows you to uncover vulnerabilities without alerting the target, preserving the element of surprise for deeper assessment.

Key Data Sources

DNS Records

IPs, subdomains, mail servers

WHOIS Databases

Registrant info, contacts

Public Search Engines

Exposed documents, directories

Specialized Platforms

Shodan for IoT/services

Common Misconception

Passive recon is NOT about guessing or making assumptions. It's about verifiable, publicly available facts.
