What is Google Dorking?

Google Dorking, or Google Hacking, is the practice of using advanced search operators in search engines to find specific, often sensitive, information that is publicly available but not easily discoverable through standard searches.

  • Leverages search engine indexing capabilities.
  • Uncovers misconfigurations, exposed files, and sensitive data.
  • Requires no direct interaction with the target's systems.
  • A powerful OSINT technique for penetration testers.
Why it's Effective

Organizations often inadvertently expose sensitive data or misconfigured services that search engines index, making them discoverable with precise queries.

Google Dorking Examples
# Find PDF documents on example.com containing 'confidential'
site:example.com filetype:pdf confidential

# Find publicly accessible directory listings with 'backup' in title
intitle:"index of" "backup"

# Find login pages for a specific domain
site:example.com inurl:login

# Find SQL error messages on a site
site:example.com intext:"sql syntax error"
Key Takeaway

Mastering Google Dorking allows you to discover sensitive information and misconfigurations that could be critical for your penetration test.
