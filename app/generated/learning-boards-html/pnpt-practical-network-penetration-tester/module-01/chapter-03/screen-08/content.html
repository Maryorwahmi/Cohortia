The Search Engine for Connected Devices

Discovering Exposed Services and Vulnerabilities

What is Shodan?

A specialized search engine that indexes internet-connected devices based on banners, open ports, and other metadata, rather than web content. It's often called the 'search engine for the Internet of Things'.

Key Capabilities

Search for specific services, software versions, default credentials, and device types exposed to the public internet.

Key Takeaway

Shodan provides a unique, device-centric view of the internet, enabling the discovery of exposed services and potential vulnerabilities without direct target interaction.
