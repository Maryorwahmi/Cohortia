Stealth vs. Interaction

Understanding the Two Approaches to Information Gathering

Passive Reconnaissance

  • No Direct Interaction: Gathers information without sending packets to the target.
  • Undetectable: Leaves no trace on the target's network.
  • Examples: Browsing public websites, WHOIS lookups, Shodan searches, social media analysis.
Benefit

Maximizes stealth and allows extensive intelligence gathering without alerting the target.

Active Reconnaissance

  • Direct Interaction: Sends network packets to the target's systems.
  • Detectable: Carries a risk of being logged or blocked by security systems.
  • Examples: Port scanning (Nmap), sending ICMP pings, banner grabbing directly from servers.
Risk

Can trigger alerts and lead to detection, potentially compromising the penetration test.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize passive OSINT methods first to maximize stealth and gather initial intelligence before resorting to detectable active reconnaissance techniques.
