Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Your OSINT Toolkit

WHOIS Lookup

A query to a public database that provides information about domain registrants and registration details.

Shodan

A search engine for internet-connected devices, allowing discovery of open ports, services, and banners.

Passive Reconnaissance

Gathering information about a target without directly interacting with its systems, thus leaving no trace.

Summary Takeaway

WHOIS and Shodan are powerful OSINT tools, while understanding the distinction between passive (undetectable) and active (detectable) reconnaissance is crucial for strategic information gathering.
