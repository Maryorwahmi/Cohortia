The "Do No Harm" Principle

Operating Within Legal and Ethical Frameworks

Ethical Guidelines for OSINT
  • Stay Legal: Ensure all activities comply with local and international laws.
  • Respect Privacy: Focus on professional, public information relevant to the engagement.
  • Legitimate Purpose: Gather intelligence solely for authorized security assessments.
  • Do No Harm: Avoid harassment, stalking, or any malicious use of information.
Common Misconception

Finding sensitive public data (e.g., API keys on GitHub) does NOT automatically grant permission to exploit it. Report, don't exploit, without explicit authorization.

Ethical Flow

Discover Public Information

Assess Sensitivity & Risk

Is it critical? Does it violate privacy?
