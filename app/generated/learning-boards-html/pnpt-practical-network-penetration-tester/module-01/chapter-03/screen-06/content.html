Advanced Search Engine Queries

Uncovering Hidden Information with Google Dorks

What is Google Dorking?

Leveraging Google's advanced search operators to find specific types of files, directories, or sensitive information that isn't immediately obvious through standard searches.

Pro Tip

Combine multiple operators for highly specific and effective searches. For example, site:target.com filetype:xls intitle:"salaries".

Google Dork Examples
# Find confidential PDFs on example.comsite:example.com filetype:pdf confidential# Find login pages for a targetsite:target.com inurl:login OR inurl:admin# Find pages with 'password' in the titleintitle:"password"# Find pages linking to a specific domainlink:old-domain.com# View Google's cached version of a pagecache:example.com/sensitive-page
Key Takeaway

Mastering Google Dorking allows you to transform a general search engine into a powerful, precise intelligence-gathering tool for uncovering sensitive information.
