Definition

Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)

The practice of collecting and analyzing information from publicly available sources to produce actionable intelligence. It's about finding and understanding data that anyone can access.

Role in Penetration Testing
  • Crucial First Step: Initiates the reconnaissance phase.
  • Builds Target Profile: Gathers data on organization, employees, tech stack, digital footprint.
  • Passive Approach: No direct interaction with target systems.
  • Undetectable: Leaves no trace, maximizing stealth.
Key Process

Collect Public Data

Websites, social media, records

Analyze & Correlate

Identify patterns, connections

Produce Intelligence

Actionable insights for testing

Analogy

Think of OSINT as being a detective gathering clues from publicly visible sources before ever knocking on the suspect's door, ensuring stealth and comprehensive understanding.
