Diverse Information Streams

Where to Find Publicly Available Data

Public Records

  • Company registrations
  • Domain registrations (WHOIS)
  • Legal filings, property records

Corporate Websites

  • "About Us" pages, press releases
  • Job postings (reveals tech)
  • Investor relations, annual reports

Social Media

  • LinkedIn (employee roles, structure)
  • Twitter (announcements, discussions)
  • Facebook (public pages, events)

News & Press

  • Company mergers, new products
  • Security incidents, executive changes
  • Industry reports, financial news

Technical Information

  • Shodan, Censys (internet devices)
  • Public code repositories (GitHub)
  • DNS records, IP ranges

Geo-location Data

  • Public images with metadata
  • Google Maps, satellite imagery
  • Open-source mapping services
Key Takeaway

Effective OSINT leverages a wide array of public sources, each offering unique insights into a target's operations, infrastructure, and personnel.
