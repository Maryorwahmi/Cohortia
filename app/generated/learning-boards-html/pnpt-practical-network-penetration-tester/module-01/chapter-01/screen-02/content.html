Distinguish Security Practices

Clearly differentiate between ethical hacking, penetration testing, and vulnerability assessment. This skill is vital for understanding the scope and intent of various security engagements.

Understand Test Goals & Phases

Grasp the primary objectives and the systematic phases of a professional penetration test. This knowledge ensures you approach engagements with a clear methodology and purpose.

Recognize Scope & Authorization

Identify the critical importance of defining scope and obtaining explicit authorization. This is fundamental for conducting legal and ethical penetration tests.

Identify Test Types

Identify the key differences between various types of penetration tests, such as black-box, white-box, and gray-box. This helps in selecting the appropriate testing strategy for different scenarios.
