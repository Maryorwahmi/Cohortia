Key Concepts: Scope, Authorization & PTES

Recapping critical aspects of professional engagements.

Scope

The clearly defined boundaries and targets for a penetration test, outlining what is permitted and forbidden.

Authorization

Explicit, written permission from the target organization to legally conduct a penetration test.

PTES

The Penetration Testing Execution Standard, a comprehensive framework outlining the phases of a professional penetration test.

Summary Takeaway

Adhering to scope, securing authorization, and following a structured methodology are fundamental for ethical and effective penetration testing.
