Defining Boundaries: The Scope of Engagement

Meticulously outlining what is in and out of bounds for testing.

What is Scope?

The clearly defined boundaries and targets for a penetration test, specifying systems, networks, applications, and permitted activities.

Why it's Critical

Prevents legal issues, protects professional reputation, and ensures the test focuses on agreed-upon objectives without causing unintended harm.

Key Takeaway

The scope is your contract and ethical guide; never test anything not explicitly included.

Penetration Test Scope

Client: SecureShop

In-Scope Targets

  • •Web application: https://www.secureshop.com
  • •Public IP range: 203.0.113.0/29
  • •Permitted: Web app exploitation, vulnerability scanning.

Out-of-Scope Items

  • •Internal network systems.
  • •Social engineering attempts.
  • •Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks.

Scenario: Unauthorized System

Your scope allows testing of app.example.com. You discover dev.example.com. Without explicit authorization, testing dev.example.com is a violation, even if it's related.
