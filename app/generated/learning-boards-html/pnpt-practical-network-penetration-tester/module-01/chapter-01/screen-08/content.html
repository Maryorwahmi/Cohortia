Professional Conduct

Strict Adherence: If It's Not In Scope, It's Out

Understanding the severe repercussions of violating agreed-upon testing parameters.

1. Discovery

You find a system or asset during reconnaissance.

2. Check Scope Document

Verify if the discovered asset is explicitly listed in your signed scope.

3. If In Scope: Proceed

Continue testing as per the agreed-upon rules.

4. If Out of Scope: Report & Clarify

Immediately inform the client and await explicit written authorization before any action.

Consequence: Legal & Reputational Damage

Testing out-of-scope systems can lead to legal charges (unauthorized access) and severely harm your professional credibility.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize strict adherence to scope; when in doubt, clarify with the client before proceeding.
