Essential Terms to Remember

Reinforcing foundational definitions for penetration testing.

Penetration Testing

A simulated cyberattack to identify and exploit vulnerabilities, demonstrating real-world impact.

Ethical Hacking

A broad term for hacking activities performed with explicit permission to improve security.

Vulnerability Assessment

The process of identifying and quantifying security weaknesses without attempting to exploit them.

Summary Takeaway

Distinguishing these core terms is fundamental for understanding the scope and intent of various cybersecurity practices.
