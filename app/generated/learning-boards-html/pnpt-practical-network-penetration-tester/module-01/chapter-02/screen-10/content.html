Ethical Obligation

Data Privacy: Safeguarding Sensitive Information

You are ethically and legally bound to protect sensitive data encountered during a penetration test.

Minimize Collection

Only collect data absolutely necessary for the test objectives to reduce risk.

Secure Storage

Store any collected data securely, often encrypted, and only for the specified duration.

Non-Disclosure

Never disclose client information or vulnerabilities to third parties without explicit permission.

The Data Protection Mandate

Protecting sensitive data is a core ethical and legal mandate, requiring careful handling and strict confidentiality.

Collect Only What's Needed
Encrypt & Limit Access
Strict Confidentiality
Key Takeaway

Always adhere to strict data privacy principles, minimizing collection, ensuring secure storage, and maintaining absolute confidentiality of client information.
