Key Concepts: Ethical Responsibilities

Review the core ethical duties of a professional penetration tester.

Do No Harm

A core ethical principle requiring testers to minimize disruption, avoid data corruption, and respect operational continuity.

Data Privacy

The ethical and legal obligation to protect sensitive information encountered during a test through minimal collection, secure storage, and non-disclosure.

Responsible Disclosure

The ethical practice of reporting vulnerabilities to affected parties (e.g., vendors) privately before publicizing them, coordinating for a fix.

Summary Takeaway

Ethical penetration testing is guided by the principles of 'do no harm,' strict data privacy, and responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities to protect all stakeholders.
