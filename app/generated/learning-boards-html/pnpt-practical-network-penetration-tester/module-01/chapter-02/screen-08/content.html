Legal Authorization: The Power of Explicit, Written Consent

Only explicit, written consent from an authorized representative is acceptable for penetration testing.

Explicit, Written Consent

The only legally defensible form of authorization, clearly documented and signed.

Avoid Verbal/Implied Consent

These forms are difficult to prove and carry significant legal risks for the tester.

Verify Authority

Ensure the signatory has the legal authority to grant permission for the entire organization.

The Consent Spectrum

Understanding the different forms of consent and why only explicit, written authorization provides adequate legal protection.

Verbal Consent (Risky)
Implied Consent (Unacceptable)

Key Takeaway

Always obtain explicit, written consent from a legally authorized individual to protect yourself and ensure the legitimacy of your penetration test.
