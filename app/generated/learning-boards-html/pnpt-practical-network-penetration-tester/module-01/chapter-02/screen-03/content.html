By the end of this chapter, you will differentiate forms of consent and understand ethical duties.

Differentiate Consent Forms

Distinguish between verbal, implied, and explicit written consent, emphasizing why only the latter is acceptable for penetration testing. This ensures all your engagements are legally defensible and clearly authorized.
