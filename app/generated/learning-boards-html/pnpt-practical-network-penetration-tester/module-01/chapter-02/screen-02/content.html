Learning Objectives: Legal Principles & RoE

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to articulate key legal principles and the purpose of a Rules of Engagement document.

Articulate Legal Principles

Identify and explain the core legal principles governing cybersecurity, particularly in penetration testing. This skill ensures you operate within legal boundaries, protecting both yourself and your client from unauthorized access claims.

Describe the function of a Rules of Engagement (RoE) document and its essential sections. Understanding the RoE is crucial for defining scope, managing expectations, and establishing a clear, legally sound contract for your testing activities.
