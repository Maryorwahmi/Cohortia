Key Concepts: Legal Foundations

Review the essential legal principles and documents governing penetration testing.

Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)

U.S. federal law prohibiting unauthorized access to computer systems, with severe penalties.

Rules of Engagement (RoE)

A legally binding document outlining the scope, permitted activities, timing, communication, and authorization for a penetration test.

Explicit, Written Consent

The only acceptable form of authorization for penetration testing, clearly documented and signed by an authorized representative.

Summary Takeaway

Operating legally requires understanding the CFAA, utilizing a comprehensive RoE, and always securing explicit, written consent from authorized parties.
