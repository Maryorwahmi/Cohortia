Core Concept

The Legal Imperative: Never Test Without Permission

Unauthorized access is a criminal offense with severe penalties, making explicit consent non-negotiable.

Foundational Principle

Always obtain explicit, written permission from the asset owner before any security testing.

Legal Consequences

Laws like the CFAA criminalize unauthorized access, leading to fines and imprisonment.

Ignorance is No Excuse

Lack of awareness about legal frameworks does not absolve you of responsibility.

The Unauthorized Access Trap

Even with good intentions, testing without explicit, written consent can lead to severe legal penalties under laws like the CFAA.

Verbal 'Go-Ahead'
Implied Permission
Explicit Written Consent
Key Takeaway

Always secure a formal, written agreement before initiating any penetration testing activities to avoid legal liability.
