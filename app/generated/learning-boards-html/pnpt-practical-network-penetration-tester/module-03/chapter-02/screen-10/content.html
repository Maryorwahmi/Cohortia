Choosing the Right Payload and Evading Detection

Tailoring Your Post-Exploitation Access

Payload Selection Factors

  • Network Egress: Reverse shells (target to attacker) vs. Bind shells (attacker to target).
  • Target Architecture: Match payload (x86/x64) to the target system.
  • Desired Functionality: Simple shell vs. advanced Meterpreter capabilities.
  • Evasion: Encoding to bypass basic antivirus or IDS.
Key Takeaway

Careful payload selection based on target environment and desired outcome, combined with strategic encoding, enhances exploitation success and stealth.

Reverse Shell

Target connects back to attacker. Good for bypassing firewalls.

Bind Shell

Target opens a listener. Attacker connects to target.

Payload Encoding

Obfuscates payload to evade basic antivirus/IDS. Use `set Encoder` or `set EvasionOptions`.
