Learning Objectives

What you'll master in this chapter to become a proficient penetration tester.

Bloom's Taxonomy:LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
01

Identify Common Vulnerabilities

Learn to recognize exploitable weaknesses in internal network services based on enumeration results, such as outdated software or misconfigurations. This skill is crucial for pinpointing high-value targets.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Utilize Metasploit Framework

Master the Metasploit Framework to select, configure, and execute exploits against identified vulnerabilities. This includes setting target and listener options for successful compromise.
