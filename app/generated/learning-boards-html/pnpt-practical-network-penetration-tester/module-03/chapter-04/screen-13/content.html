Ethical Hacking

Responsible Lateral Movement

Lateral movement and persistence activities are highly intrusive. Always ensure explicit authorization and strict adherence to the scope of engagement.

Explicit Authorization

Never perform intrusive actions without clear, written permission from the client, detailing allowed techniques and targets.

Adhere to Scope

Strictly follow the defined scope of your penetration test to avoid unintended impact or legal repercussions.

Minimize Impact

Prioritize techniques that are less disruptive and ensure all actions are reversible and well-documented.

Risks of Uncontrolled Movement
Widespread Disruption

Uncontrolled actions can lead to network instability, service outages, or data corruption across the domain.

Early Detection

Aggressive or noisy lateral movement significantly increases the chances of being detected by security monitoring systems.

Key Takeaway

Ethical penetration testing demands explicit authorization and strict adherence to the scope of engagement for all lateral movement and persistence activities to prevent disruption and ensure responsible security assessment.
