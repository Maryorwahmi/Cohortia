NTLM Relay: Intercepting Authentication

NTLM Relay attacks intercept authentication attempts and relay them to another server, allowing authentication as the victim.

How it Works

An attacker's machine acts as a rogue server, listening for NTLM authentication requests from clients.

Role of Responder.py

Responder.py is a tool that captures NTLM hashes or facilitates NTLM relay by listening for authentication requests.

Key Advantage

Bypasses the need for plaintext passwords, leveraging existing authentication mechanisms for lateral movement.

NTLM Relay Attack Flow
Key Takeaway

NTLM Relay attacks leverage Responder.py to intercept NTLM authentication attempts and relay them to another server, enabling authentication as the victim without needing their password.
