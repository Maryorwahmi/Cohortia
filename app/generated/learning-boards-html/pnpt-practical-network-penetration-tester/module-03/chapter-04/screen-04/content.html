Core Concept

The Path to Domain Administrator

Once local admin access is achieved, the next critical step is lateral movement to gain control over other systems and ultimately the entire domain.

What is Lateral Movement?

The process of gaining access to other systems on the network from an initially compromised machine.

The Ultimate Goal

Achieving Domain Administrator privileges, granting full control over all Active Directory resources.

Why it Matters

Active Directory's centralized design, while efficient, presents a single point of failure that attackers can exploit for widespread impact.

Attack Flow Overview
Key Takeaway

Lateral movement is a structured approach to expand access within an AD network, with the ultimate goal of achieving Domain Administrator privileges by exploiting AD's inherent design and misconfigurations.
