Harvesting Credentials for Lateral Movement

Lateral movement often starts with credential harvesting from compromised machines, leading to powerful attacks like Pass-the-Hash (PtH).

Credential Harvesting

Tools like Mimikatz dump NTLM password hashes and cleartext credentials from memory on compromised systems.

What is Pass-the-Hash (PtH)?

A technique allowing authentication to other Windows machines or services directly using a stolen NTLM hash, bypassing the need for the plaintext password.

Why PtH is Effective

It avoids the time-consuming process of cracking hashes, enabling faster lateral movement and reducing the risk of detection.

Pass-the-Hash Flow
Key Takeaway

Pass-the-Hash is a highly effective lateral movement technique that uses stolen NTLM hashes for direct authentication, bypassing the need for plaintext passwords.
