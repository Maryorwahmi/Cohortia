Hands-on Technique

Interacting with SMB Shares

Listing and accessing shared resources to gather sensitive information.

SMB Enumeration Workflow

1

Enumerate with enum4linux

Use enum4linux -a <target_ip> to discover users, groups, and shares.

2

List Shares with smbclient

Use smbclient -L //<target_ip>/ -N (for anonymous) or -U username to list shares.

3

Connect & Interact

Connect to specific shares (e.g., smbclient //<target_ip>/sharename -N) to browse and download files.

Terminal Demo: SMB Interaction
# Enumerate all info with enum4linux
enum4linux -a 192.168.1.105

# List shares (anonymous access attempt)
smbclient -L //192.168.1.105/ -N

# Connect to a specific share (e.g., 'temp')
smbclient //192.168.1.105/temp -N

Key Takeaway

Always attempt anonymous access to SMB shares and thoroughly examine their contents, as they often contain critical information or misconfigurations that can be leveraged for deeper access.
