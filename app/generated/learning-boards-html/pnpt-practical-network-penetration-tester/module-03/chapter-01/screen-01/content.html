Chapter 3.1

Internal Network Reconnaissance and Enumeration

Uncovering the hidden landscape behind the firewall to identify vulnerabilities and attack paths.

Key Idea

Internal reconnaissance focuses on assets behind the firewall, often less hardened and more trusting than external systems. The goal is to build a comprehensive picture of the network topology, identify key servers, discover user accounts, and uncover misconfigurations.
