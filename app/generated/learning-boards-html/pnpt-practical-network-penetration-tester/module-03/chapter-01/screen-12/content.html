Strategic Analysis

Analyzing BloodHound Graphs and Operational Security

Pinpointing attack paths and staying undetected during reconnaissance.

Interpreting BloodHound Results

  • Querying Paths: Use built-in queries like 'Shortest Path to Domain Admins' to visualize attack chains.
  • Identifying Relationships: Look for 'GenericAll' rights on sensitive objects or high-privileged user sessions on specific computers.
  • Actionable Intelligence: Translate visual graphs into concrete steps for privilege escalation and lateral movement.

Operational Security (OpSec) Considerations

!

Careful Tool Transfer

Use stealthy methods like certutil or PowerShell's Invoke-WebRequest to transfer tools to avoid detection.

!

Adhere to Scope

Always ensure all reconnaissance activities are within the explicit scope of your engagement to prevent disruption or legal issues.

Key Takeaway

BloodHound provides unparalleled visualization for AD attack paths, but effective reconnaissance demands strict operational security, including careful tool transfer and unwavering adherence to the engagement's defined scope.
