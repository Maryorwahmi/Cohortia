Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Skills and assessment focus
Practical skills
  • Implement GET and SETEX for Cache-Aside fetch and confirm cache hit behavior.
  • Invalidate cache after updates with DEL and validate fresh reads.
  • Simulate TTL expiry and observe cache miss recovery.
  • Use locking or SETNX to prevent simultaneous rebuilds.
Evaluation cues
  • Explain trade-offs between Write-Through and Write-Back for consistency and latency.
  • Choose an eviction policy based on access patterns and TTL usage.
  • Describe mitigations for stampede and penetration and when to apply them.
Competency checklist

You can consider yourself competent when you can implement and test Cache-Aside with TTL, choose a suitable eviction policy for your dataset, and demonstrate a mitigation for cache stampede or penetration in a small demo.
