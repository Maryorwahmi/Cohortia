Implementing Caching Patterns with Redis

Learn practical caching patterns, TTL and eviction choices, and strategies to avoid stale data, cache stampede, and cache penetration.

In this lesson

Understand patterns like Cache-Aside, Write-Through, and Write-Back; implement Cache-Aside with TTL in Python; and learn how to pick eviction policies and mitigate common issues.

Why it matters

A well-designed cache reduces latency and backend load. Choosing the right pattern and TTL prevents inconsistencies and keeps memory bounded under load.

Quick takeaway

Redis is fast, but effective caching needs explicit patterns, TTLs, and eviction policy choices to remain correct and stable under load.

Concept map
Cache Patterns

Lazy load on miss; app controls populate cache.
Write-Through
Writes go to cache and database synchronously.
