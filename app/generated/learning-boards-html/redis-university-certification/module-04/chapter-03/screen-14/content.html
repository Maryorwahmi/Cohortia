Caching

Storing copies of frequently used data in fast storage to reduce latency and backend load.

Cache-Aside

Application checks cache, on miss reads DB and populates cache; simple and demand-driven.

Write-Through / Write-Back

Write-Through syncs cache and DB for immediate consistency; Write-Back acknowledges cache writes and persists asynchronously at risk of loss.

Takeaway

These three concepts are central to deciding how to use Redis for caching in your application architecture.
