Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Redis Eviction Policies
Common policies
  • noeviction: writes fail when full.
  • allkeys-lru: evict least recently used across all keys.
  • volatile-lru: evict LRU among keys with TTL.
  • allkeys-lfu: evict least frequently used across all keys.
  • volatile-ttl: evict keys with smallest remaining TTL.
Choosing a policy

Match the policy to your workload: use allkeys-lru for general-purpose caches with mixed keys, volatile-lru if only TTL keys should be removed, or LFU when hot items are identified by frequency over time.

Example scenario
For an e-commerce catalog where some product pages are highly popular, allkeys-lru preserves recently viewed products under memory pressure, keeping user experience smooth.
Performance note

Eviction decisions have runtime cost. Test your choice under realistic load to ensure eviction behavior matches expectations.
