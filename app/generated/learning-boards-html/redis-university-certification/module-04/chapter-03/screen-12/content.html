Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Invalidation and stale data
Immediate invalidation

After writing to the database, call DEL on the cache key to force subsequent reads to reload fresh data. This is the most direct way to prevent staleness.

Event-driven invalidation
Emit an event when data changes and have a cache worker listen and delete or refresh affected keys across instances.
Hybrid strategy

Use short TTLs for general freshness and explicit invalidation for critical updates. This reduces reliance on manual deletion and gives predictable expiry.

Key Takeaway

Invalidate immediately after updates for strong freshness, or use short TTLs plus events for pragmatic consistency guarantees.
