Safety checklist
Don't assume rollback
Handle per-command errors from EXEC; validate before queueing.
Keep WATCH short
Avoid heavy work between WATCH and EXEC to reduce aborts.
Watch minimally
Only monitor keys that affect transaction correctness.
Retry on EXEC None or WatchError with backoff to limit contention.
Call UNWATCH or reset the pipeline when you abort intentionally to release the optimistic lock.
Key Takeaway

Follow simple rules: validate early, watch narrowly, keep windows short, and implement robust retry strategies to avoid subtle concurrency bugs.
