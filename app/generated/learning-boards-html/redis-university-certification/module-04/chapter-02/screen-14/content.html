Pre-flight checklist
Validate data types to avoid EXEC-time errors.
Watch only essential keys to lower abort probability.
Keep the WATCH to EXEC window short and local.
Implement retry loops that re-read state after aborts.
Call UNWATCH or reset when you intentionally abort.
Consider Lua for complex atomic operations.

Try this: review a transaction in your codebase against this checklist and refactor to validate and reduce watched keys before deploying.
