Common mistakes to avoid
Checklist before shipping leaderboards and rate limiters.
Leaderboard
  • Paginate top-N results.
  • Seed with ZADD and use ZINCRBY for events.
  • Convert 0-based ranks for users.
Rate limiting
  • Use pipelines or Lua to avoid missing EXPIRE.
  • Choose key granularity carefully.
  • Return 429 and Retry-After on blocks.
Testing
Simulate concurrent updates and burst traffic; monitor Redis metrics and tune accordingly.
Final takeaway
Balance simplicity and correctness: pipelines are pragmatic, Lua scripts provide full atomic guarantees when required.
