What to remember
Client Library
Use a well-supported client that offers pooling and pipelines.
Connection Pooling
Create one pool at startup and reuse it across handlers.
Cache-Aside & SETEX
Check cache, fetch on miss, and use atomic set-with-expire for safety.
Summary Takeaway

Combine the right client, a shared pool, cache-aside flow with atomic expirations, and herd mitigation to integrate Redis safely into frameworks and ORMs.
