Three compact reminders
1. Pick the client
Choose the standard, well-maintained client for your language.
2. Pool connections
Create a single ConnectionPool at startup and reuse it across handlers.
3. Use atomic TTLs
Set values with expiration atomically and plan mitigations for simultaneous misses.
Final thought

These three rules will guide safe, maintainable Redis integrations: client, pool, and atomic expirations. Use them as a checklist before deploying.
