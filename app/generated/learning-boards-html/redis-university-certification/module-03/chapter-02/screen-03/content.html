Objective verification cards

Quick hands-on checks and mnemonics to confirm each skill.

Channels connect, not couple
Sketch a chat flow and explain how publishers never need to know subscribers' identities.
PUBLISH/SUBSCRIBE check
Open two redis-cli sessions. Subscribe in one, publish in the other, and observe immediate delivery.
PSUBSCRIBE star
Use PSUBSCRIBE news:* and publish to news:sports to verify pattern matching.
No memory, no replay
Publish while no subscribers are active to see the message dropped and understand the implication.
