UNSUBSCRIBE

Stopping subscriptions and cleaning up

UNSUBSCRIBE channel1 channel2 or UNSUBSCRIBE with no args to end all subscriptions. When subscription count hits zero the connection accepts normal commands again.

Shutdown pattern
On service stop, UNSUBSCRIBE to gracefully release sockets and avoid lingering listeners.
No recovery
UNSUBSCRIBE does not let you recover messages that were published while you were offline.
Monitoring
Track active subscription counts for operational visibility and to detect clients that re-subscribe frequently.
Key Takeaway

Treat subscriptions as resource-bound connections and manage lifecycle explicitly to avoid surprises.
