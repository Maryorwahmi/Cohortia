AOF Maintenance
Compact and back up the append-only file

Trigger BGREWRITEAOF to create a compact append-only file and copy appendonly.aof off-server after completion.

# Compact the AOF in the background
redis-cli BGREWRITEAOF
# After success copy the optimized AOF
cp /var/lib/redis/appendonly.aof /tmp/redis_backup/appendonly-2023-09-15.aof
Monitoring & Capacity
  • Alert on rapid AOF growth and low disk space.
  • Schedule BGREWRITEAOF during low traffic windows to reduce impact.
  • Keep off-site copies and record rewrite timestamps for auditing.
Key Takeaway

AOF needs periodic compaction; BGREWRITEAOF plus off-site copies make AOF backups reliable and faster to restore.
