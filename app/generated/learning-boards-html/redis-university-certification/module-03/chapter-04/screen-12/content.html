Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Backup
Create copies of dump.rdb and appendonly.aof and store them off-site; verify restores regularly.
Restore
Stop Redis, replace persistence files in the configured dir, restart, and verify data integrity.
High Availability
Sentinel supplies monitoring and automatic failover; Cluster provides sharding and scale across nodes.
Takeaway

Backups, tested restores, Sentinel for availability, and Cluster for scale form a layered resilience strategy for Redis.
