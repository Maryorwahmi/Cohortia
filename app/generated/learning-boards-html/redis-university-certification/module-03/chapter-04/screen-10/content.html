Sentinel Process
Automatic failover steps
  1. Subjective down: a Sentinel detects master unresponsiveness.
  2. Quorum: Sentinels communicate; if quorum agrees, mark master objectively down.
  3. Leader election: Sentinels elect a leader to coordinate the failover.
  4. Replica promotion: Leader selects and promotes the best replica to master.
  5. Reconfiguration: Other replicas are pointed to the new master and clients are informed.
Example sentinel.conf lines:
# sentinel monitor    
sentinel monitor mymaster 127.0.0.1 6379 2
sentinel down-after-milliseconds mymaster 5000
sentinel failover-timeout mymaster 60000
sentinel parallel-syncs mymaster 1
Key Takeaway

Quorum and timeouts determine how quickly and reliably failover happens; tune them to your environment and test failovers regularly.
