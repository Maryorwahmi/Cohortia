Comparison › RDB vs AOF
When to choose RDB

Backups, fast restarts, and scenarios where occasional data loss is acceptable.

When to choose AOF

Workloads requiring minimal data loss or strict durability guarantees.

Combined approach

Enable both RDB and AOF to get compact backups and low data-loss recovery. Redis will prefer AOF on restart if both exist.

Scenario

A streaming metrics pipeline may accept RDB. Transaction logs for billing should use AOF with a strict fsync policy.

Misconfiguration to avoid

Do not set appendfsync to no for critical data, and do not rely solely on frequent RDB snapshots when minimal loss is required.
