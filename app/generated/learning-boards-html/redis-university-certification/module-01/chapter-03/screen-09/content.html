Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Choosing Persistence
RDB Only
Good for caches and datasets where losing recent writes is acceptable. Fast restarts and compact backups.
Example: Non-critical session cache
AOF Only
Use when every write matters and you can accept longer restarts. Tune appendfsync for durability.
Example: Financial ledger where writes must persist
RDB + AOF
Recommended for critical applications: compact backups plus near-real-time durability and fallback options.
Example: Production app backend requiring fast recovery and minimal data loss
Check your thinking

List your application's acceptable data-loss window and restart SLA, then pick RDB, AOF, or both to meet those limits. Test by performing saves and restarts.
