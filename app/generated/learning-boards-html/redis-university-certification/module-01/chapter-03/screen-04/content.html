Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › What is Persistence?
Definition

Persistence means writing Redis's in-memory data to durable storage so it survives restarts and crashes. Redis offers two mechanisms: RDB snapshots and AOF logging. Each has distinct durability and performance trade-offs that affect restart time and disk usage.

Concrete example
If you enable AOF with every-second fsync, a write completed at time T will be safe on disk within one second. If you use RDB every 5 minutes, any writes after the last snapshot may be lost on crash.
Key takeaway
Persistence is a choice: prioritize durability or restart speed based on your app's needs.
Common mistake
Assuming snapshots are continuous backups. Snapshots are periodic and can miss recent writes.
Analogy
Think of RDB as taking photos of a ledger at intervals, and AOF as keeping a running diary of every entry. A photo is compact and quick to review, a diary contains everything but takes longer to replay.
