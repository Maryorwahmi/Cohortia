Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Safety & Best Practices
Disk space and permissions
Confirm the dir setting points to a volume with plenty of free space and correct ownership so saves and rewrites won't fail.
Backups
Regularly copy RDB and AOF files off-site. Test restores to ensure backups are valid.
Monitoring
Watch disk I/O and server load during BGSAVE and BGREWRITEAOF. Schedule heavy operations during lower traffic.
Operational checklist
  • Ensure dir has sufficient space and permissions.
  • Prefer BGSAVE over SAVE in production.
  • Schedule BGREWRITEAOF to manage AOF size and reduce restart time.
  • Test restores from both RDB and AOF regularly.
