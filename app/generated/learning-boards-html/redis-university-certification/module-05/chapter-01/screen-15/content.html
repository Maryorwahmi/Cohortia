Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Master-Replica
Master handles writes and streams them to replicas which serve reads and provide redundancy.
Asynchronous
The master does not wait for replicas, enabling high write throughput at the cost of potential small lag.
Full & Partial Sync
Initial full sync uses RDB + buffered commands; partial resync uses a backlog when possible to reduce overhead.
Summary takeaway

Use REPLICAOF to configure replicas, verify with INFO replication and offsets, keep replicas read-only, and enable master persistence for durable, reliable deployments.
