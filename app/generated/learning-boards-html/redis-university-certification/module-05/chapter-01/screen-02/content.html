Explain Master-Replica

Describe the roles of master and replica and why replication provides read scaling and redundancy. Understanding roles prevents misconfiguration and data loss.

Configure Replication

Use the REPLICAOF command or redis.conf to point replicas to a master and keep replica-read-only set by default to avoid divergence.

Mnemonic: configuration line = one source of truth.

03
Monitor Health

Read INFO replication to check role, connected replicas, master link status, and offsets to detect lag or disconnection.

Mnemonic: INFO is your replication dashboard.

Understand Trade-offs
