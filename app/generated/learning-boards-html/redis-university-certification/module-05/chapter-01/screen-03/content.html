Objectives — Continued

Gain hands-on ability to observe full and partial sync behavior and make safe scaling choices.

05
Describe Full Sync

Explain the RDB snapshot transfer, buffering of writes, and the replica catch-up step. This helps plan for network and disk costs during syncs.

Mnemonic: snapshot then stream.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06
Recognize Partial Resync

Understand how a replication backlog allows reconnecting replicas to request only missed commands and avoid full snapshot transfers when possible.

Mnemonic: backlog saves bandwidth.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07
Plan for Read Scaling

Apply replicas to distribute read load across instances. This reduces master CPU usage and increases throughput for read-heavy workloads.

Mnemonic: many readers, one writer.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08
Prepare for Recovery

Ensure master persistence is enabled so a full restart doesn't lose data when replication alone cannot recover state.

Mnemonic: persistence plus replication.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
