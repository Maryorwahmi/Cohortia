Frequent causes of replication failure

Missing replicaof
An instance without replicaof starts as a master. Confirm configuration to avoid accidental masters.
Writing to a replica
Replicas are read-only by default. Attempts to write will fail. Making them writable risks inconsistency.
Network interruptions
Connectivity issues may cause replicas to fall behind or disconnect, possibly forcing expensive full syncs.
Checks to run
  • Confirm replicaof or REPLICAOF is set on replicas.
  • Verify open ports and firewall rules between master and replicas.
  • Use INFO replication to check master_link_status and offsets.
  • Ensure master persistence (AOF or RDB) is enabled.
Reminder
Replication provides redundancy and read scaling, but it is not equivalent to failover. Combine replication with Sentinel or other tools for automated promotion and complete availability plans.
