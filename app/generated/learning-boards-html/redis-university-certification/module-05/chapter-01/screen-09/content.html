Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Master-Replica Topology
Architecture

Scale reads with replicas

Route writes to the master and distribute reads across replicas. This pattern reduces the master’s read load and improves throughput for read-heavy applications.

Placement tip
Put replicas near consumers for lower read latency, but expect more replication lag across regions.
Redundancy benefit
Replicas provide copies of data for fault tolerance, but they depend on master persistence to survive a full restart without data loss.
Example routing
Application writes go to master at 10.0.0.1:6379. Read requests are balanced across replicas at 10.0.0.2:6379 and 10.0.0.3:6379 to increase throughput and reduce master load.
Operational checklist
  1. Enable persistence on the master (AOF or RDB) so data survives a full restart.
  2. Monitor replication offsets and link status with INFO replication.
  3. Test read-after-write needs and route sensitive reads to the master when necessary.
