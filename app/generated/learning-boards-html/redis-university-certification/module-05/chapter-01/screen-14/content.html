Durability versus redundancy

Replicas provide redundancy and read scaling, but persistence ensures data survives full outages and restarts.

Operational rule
Always enable RDB or AOF on the master even if you have replicas to ensure durability across full system outages.
Example risk
A master without persistence that crashes loses its in-memory dataset. Replicas cannot recover writes that were only in master's memory.
Checklist
  • Enable appropriate persistence mode on the master.
  • Test full restart scenarios to ensure data survives and replicas can resync.
  • Combine replication with monitoring and failover tools for full availability plans.
Takeaway
Replication improves availability and scale; persistence guarantees durability. Use both together for production-grade resilience.
