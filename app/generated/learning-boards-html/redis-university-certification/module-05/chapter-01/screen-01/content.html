Redis Replication

Master-replica replication is the most direct path to read scaling and redundancy in Redis. This lesson combines concept, configuration, and a short lab to try on your machine.

Why it matters

Replicas let you serve more read traffic and provide copies of data for recovery, while keeping the master fast by streaming writes asynchronously.

In this lesson
Master-replica mechanics, REPLICAOF, INFO checks, and a local lab.
Coming up
Full sync, partial resync, and replication offset interpretation.
Master
Handles all writes and streams changes to replicas. Keep persistence enabled on the master for safety.
Replica
Serves reads and applies streamed write commands. Read-only by default to avoid divergence.
Initial Sync
RDB snapshot + buffered commands until the replica catches up.
Asynchronous
Writes are streamed without waiting for replica acknowledgement. Faster master, possible small lag.
Explore Key Topics
Configuration
Use REPLICAOF or redis.conf to point replicas at a master and keep replicas read-only by default.
Synchronization
Initial RDB snapshot, buffered commands, then continuous streaming.
Monitoring
Use INFO replication to read role, connected replicas, and replication offset.
