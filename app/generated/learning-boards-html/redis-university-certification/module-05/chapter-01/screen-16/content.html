Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › What to Remember
Configure & Verify
Use REPLICAOF or redis.conf and confirm with INFO replication and link status checks.
Enable Persistence
Replication is redundancy, persistence provides durability. Keep RDB or AOF on the master.
Monitor Offsets
Watch replication offsets and master_link_status to detect lag or disconnection early.
Final takeaway

Replication is simple and powerful. Configure carefully, persist the authoritative source, and monitor continuously to use it safely in production.
