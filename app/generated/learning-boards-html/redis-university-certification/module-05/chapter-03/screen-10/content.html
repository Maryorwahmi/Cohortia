Failover in Redis Cluster
What happens on failure
  • • The cluster detects an unreachable master.
  • • Eligible replicas are considered and one is promoted to master.
  • • Slot ownership is updated so clients can send commands to the new master.
Risk: replication lag means recent writes may not be present on the promoted replica.
Operational checklist
  • • Place replicas in separate failure domains from their masters.
  • • Monitor replication lag and replica health continuously.
  • • Expect brief transient errors during leader election and slot updates.
Key Takeaway

Automatic failover preserves availability but plan for short disruption and potential loss of the very latest writes if lag exists.
