Topology checklist
  • Deploy at least three Sentinel processes on different hosts.
  • Prefer odd numbers to simplify majority voting.
  • Ensure Sentinels and Redis instances can communicate across firewall rules.
  • Avoid co-locating all Sentinels on a single physical host.
Deployment tips
Spread Sentinels across zones and configure monitoring thresholds appropriate to network latency to avoid flapping promotions.
Scenario
Three Sentinels on separate machines observe a master. One Sentinel's host crashes. Remaining two can still reach quorum and safely elect a leader to perform failover.
Common mistake

Running a single Sentinel defeats the point of high availability for the monitoring system. It creates a single point of failure for failover decisions.
