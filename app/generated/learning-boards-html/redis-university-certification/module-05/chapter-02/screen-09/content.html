Simulation › Master failure › SHUTDOWN and observe
Master shutdown
# On master
redis-cli -p 6379
SHUTDOWN

# On Sentinel loop
while true; do redis-cli -p 26379 SENTINEL masters; sleep 1; done
Look for flags containing sdown then odown, and finally the new master ip:port in the masters output.
Verification
After promotion, connect to the new master (for example, redis-cli -p 6380) and run INFO replication. Confirm role: master, and that the other replica follows the new master.
Key Takeaway
A controlled SHUTDOWN and observation of Sentinel outputs is the fastest way to learn failover behavior and to tune your timeouts.
