Process Flow › Failover life cycle › SDOWN to Promotion
Step sequence
  1. Sentinel marks SDOWN after down-after-milliseconds.
  2. Other Sentinels report similar SDOWN; quorum reached → ODOWN.
  3. Sentinels elect a leader to coordinate failover.
  4. Leader selects best replica and promotes it with REPLICAOF NO ONE.
  5. Leader reconfigures remaining replicas to follow the new master.
  6. Clients are informed and reconnect to the new master.
Pro tip
Configure parallel-syncs and replication priorities so the chosen replica is ready to be promoted with minimal data loss risk.
Concrete example
A master at 6379 stops responding. After 5 seconds a Sentinel marks SDOWN. Two Sentinels report SDOWN, meeting quorum=2, so the state becomes ODOWN. A leader is elected and promotes replica at 6380, then updates other replicas to follow 6380.
Key Takeaway

Failover is a coordinated multi-Sentinel decision, designed to reduce mistakes by requiring multiple confirmations and a leader to act.
