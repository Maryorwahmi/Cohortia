Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Backup & Restore
Persistence choices
  • RDB: snapshots at intervals, low runtime overhead, coarser durability.
  • AOF: logs writes, can be fsync everysec for stronger durability at higher cost.
Practical tip

Take backups from a replica to reduce master impact during snapshot creation.

Hands-on snippet (AOF conf)
port 6379
daemonize yes
pidfile /var/run/redis_6379.pid
logfile "/var/log/redis_6379.log"
dir ./data/aof
appendonly yes
appendfsync everysec
Start Redis with this config, write test keys, simulate SHUTDOWN NOSAVE, and then restart to validate that AOF recovers your data. Document and automate the restore steps so recovery is repeatable.
