Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Monitoring Metrics
Essential metrics
  • used_memory_rss and used_memory_peak
  • CPU utilization
  • connected_clients, blocked_clients
  • replication offsets for lag
  • persistence status: AOF/RDB health
Key takeaway

Monitor masters, replicas, Sentinels and cluster nodes; alerts should cover high memory, master-down, and replication lag.

Alert examples
used_memory_rss > 85% of RAM for 2 minutes
Replication lag > threshold or replica offset trailing master consistently
Master not reachable by majority of Sentinels
Frequent AOF rewrites indicating write pressure
Use Prometheus with Grafana, Datadog, or scripted INFO checks for baseline dashboards and threshold alerts. Don’t forget to monitor replicas and Sentinels, not just the master.
