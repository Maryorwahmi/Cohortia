Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Client Interactions
Client requirements
  • Use Sentinel-aware or Cluster-aware libraries for discovery.
  • Avoid hardcoded addresses; let the client discover masters.
  • Implement connection pooling and retry strategies.
Read-scaling patterns
Route read-only queries to replicas when eventual consistency is acceptable.
In Cluster, distribute reads across masters to spread load.
If strong consistency needed, read from master only and accept reduced read scaling.
Check

Simulate failovers and verify your client reconnects, retries correctly, and routes reads as intended without generating excessive reconnection traffic.
