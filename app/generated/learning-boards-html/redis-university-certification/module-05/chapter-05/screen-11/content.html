Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Persistence Comparison
RDB
Point-in-time snapshots. Low runtime cost, possible data loss since last snapshot, fast restore for moderate datasets.
Good when data can be recomputed or snapshots frequent.
Lower CPU and storage overhead during normal operation.
AOF
Append-only log of writes. Stronger durability when fsync configured; larger storage and periodic rewrites can consume CPU and memory.
Good for workloads where losing a second of writes is unacceptable.
Combine with RDB snapshots for faster recovery and durable log replay.
Make the choice

Decide based on durability needs, write volume, and operational capacity. Test restores for your chosen strategy.
