Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Replication
Core idea
Redundancy and read scaling with asynchronous replication
Replicas receive a stream of updates from the master and can serve reads. Replication is asynchronous unless configured otherwise.
Example

A master handles writes for users, replicas serve read-heavy product pages to reduce master load and latency for readers.

Details & cautions
  • Replicas may lag; this is eventual consistency.
  • Replication does not provide automatic failover by itself.
  • Corrupt data or accidental deletes on master replicate to replicas unless detected and recovered via backups.
Misconception

Assuming replicas are instant backups. They can lag and reflect the master’s mistakes.

Pro tip

Take snapshots from a replica to avoid load on the master during backups.
