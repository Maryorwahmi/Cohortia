Learning Objectives

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master

Bloom's ladder
01
Identify minimum cluster requirements

Explain why at least three masters are needed for quorum and how replicas provide high availability. Verified competency: name topology and failure scenarios.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Create a cluster with redis-cli

Run redis-cli --cluster create with the IP:port list and --cluster-replicas. Verified competency: form and accept a proposed layout during creation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Add masters and replicas

Use add-node and add-node with --cluster-slave and --cluster-master-id. Verified competency: join a new node and assign it as replica to a chosen master.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Reshard and monitor safely

Use redis-cli --cluster reshard and redis-cli --cluster check to rebalance slots and verify health. Verified competency: move slots and confirm cluster status after maintenance.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
