Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Minimum Cluster Size
Core Concept

Three masters, paired replicas

Redis Cluster requires a minimum of three master nodes for quorum. Each master should have at least one replica for high availability. This pairing preserves failover capability and allows promotion when a master fails.

Concrete example

Test on one host using ports 7000–7005. Assign masters to 7000, 7001, 7002 and replicas to 7003, 7004, 7005.

Common misconception

Replicas do not participate in quorum voting. They hold copies but cannot elect a master unless promoted as a master during maintenance.

Key Takeaway

Design clusters with at least three masters and place replicas on separate hosts for true resilience.

Why separate hosts?

Placing a master and its replica on different hosts ensures a single host failure doesn't remove both copies. This reduces correlated failures and keeps the cluster operational.
