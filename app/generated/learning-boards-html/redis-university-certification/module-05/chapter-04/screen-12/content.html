Common operational mistakes and recovery

Recognize frequent errors and the safe steps to fix them without causing data loss or extended downtime.

Mistake: too few masters

Running fewer than three masters prevents quorum and reliable failover. Recovery: add masters and reshard slots to reach three or more masters.

Mistake: shared config file

Using the same cluster-config-file across instances breaks node identity. Recovery: stop misconfigured instances, correct redis.conf to unique files, restart and rejoin nodes properly.

Mistake: del-node too soon

Attempting to delete a master with slots causes failure. Recovery: reshard its slots away, confirm zero slots, then run del-node.

Mistake: reshard without monitoring

Resharding during peak load can cause lag. Recovery: perform during off-peak times and watch replication lag metrics while moving slots.

Key Takeaway

Sequence and monitoring are how you prevent and recover from cluster operational issues.
