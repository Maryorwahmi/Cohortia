Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Masters vs Replicas
Master

Owns hash slots

Masters accept writes for keys mapped to their assigned slots. They participate in quorum and can be the target of replica promotion or failover voting.

Assigned slots: yes
Votes for failover: yes
Replica promotion target: yes
Replica

Holds a copy

Replicas store copies of a master's data and can serve reads. They do not vote in quorum but can be promoted to master if needed.

Assigned slots: no
Votes for failover: no
Promotion to master: possible
Operational example

If master 7001 fails, its replica (for example 7004) is eligible for promotion. Verify with CLUSTER NODES to see the new role and slot ownership after election.
