Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Remove Nodes
Process

Reshard away slots, then del-node

Do not attempt to delete a master before migrating its slots. The del-node operation will fail if slots remain.

# reshard all slots away from master <master_id>
redis-cli --cluster reshard 127.0.0.1:7000
# follow prompts to move all slots to other masters

# once the master has zero slots
redis-cli --cluster del-node 127.0.0.1:7000 <master_id>
Operational tip

Confirm slot-less state with CLUSTER NODES before del-node to avoid failed removal attempts.

Example: replica removal

For a replica, you can remove it with add-node and del-node without moving slots because replicas do not own slots. Still, ensure you are not removing the only replica of a master if you require redundancy.

Key Takeaway

Always migrate slots from a master first; then del-node will succeed and the cluster remains consistent.
