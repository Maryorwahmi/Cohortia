Key Commands & Final Takeaway
A compact reference to keep while you practice.
Strings
SET, GET, MSET, MGET, SETEX, APPEND, GETRANGE, SETRANGE, INCR, INCRBY
Hashes
HSET, HGET, HMGET, HGETALL, HDEL, HINCRBY
Final design question
Before you choose a type, ask: will I read and write the whole object always, or change fields frequently? Your answer determines whether a String or a Hash is the right tool.
