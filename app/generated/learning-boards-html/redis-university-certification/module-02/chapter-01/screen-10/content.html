Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Hash commands
Field-level operations
HSET and friends let you work with individual fields without rewriting a whole object. Use HINCRBY for numeric updates and HDEL to remove fields safely.
Field-level caveat
Remember EXPIRE applies to the whole key, not single fields. Model expirations with separate keys if you need TTL per field.
CLI examples
redis-cli> HSET user:456 name "John Doe" email "john.doe@example.com" age 30 city "New York"
(integer) 4
redis-cli> HGET user:456 name
"John Doe"
redis-cli> HMGET user:456 email city
1) "john.doe@example.com"
2) "New York"
redis-cli> HINCRBY user:456 login_count 1
(integer) 1
redis-cli> HDEL user:456 city
(integer) 1
redis-cli> HGETALL user:456
1) "name"
2) "John Doe"
...
Use HGETALL only when you need the whole object. For frequent small reads, prefer targeted HGET or HMGET calls to limit bandwidth and parsing cost.
