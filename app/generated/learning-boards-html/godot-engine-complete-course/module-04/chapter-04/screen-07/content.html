Container Nodes — Quick Comparison
Choose the container that matches the layout pattern you need.
HBoxContainer
Arranges children horizontally. Use for toolbars and horizontal lists.
VBoxContainer
Stacks children vertically. Ideal for menus and stacked controls.
MarginContainer
Adds padding around a single child to create consistent spacing.
GridContainer
Creates a uniform grid; children sized by cell with Expand to distribute space.
PanelContainer
MarginContainer with a stylebox background; good for panels and dialogs.
Practical pattern
Compose containers: a root Control with nested VBox and HBox containers makes complex UIs manageable.
