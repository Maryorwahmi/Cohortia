Common Pitfalls
Performance & Cleanup
CPUParticles2D runs on the CPU. Reduce particle counts, use small textures, and avoid expensive per-particle materials to keep frame rates healthy.
Cleanup
Connect finished to queue_free or hide for one-shot systems to prevent lingering nodes.
Pooling
Reuse nodes by hiding them to avoid frequent allocation, or implement a small pool for repeated effects.
Checklist
  • Test particles on target hardware to validate performance.
  • Reduce texture resolution where possible and use ramps/curves to simulate complexity.
  • Balance Amount and Lifetime to limit active particle count.
  • Use finished signal to clean up one-shot effects.
Warning

Avoid leaving one-shot nodes in the scene. They may appear harmless but can accumulate and increase CPU load over time.
