Definition
Curves and ColorRamp
Use these resources to animate particle attributes relative to their lifetime. Curves are numeric; ColorRamp handles color and alpha.
Scale Amount Curve
Define size across life: start, peak, and end values control perceived growth and fade.
ColorRamp
Map life to color and alpha so particles shift or fade naturally.
Applied example
Explosion: ColorRamp from bright yellow at 0.0 to deep red at 0.6 to transparent at 1.0. Scale Curve 1.0 → 1.8 at 0.3 → 0.0 at 1.0 to create a fast growth then fade.
Smoke example
Slow scale growth with alpha decreasing slowly to simulate dissipating smoke.
Pro tip
Combine subtle per-particle hue or alpha variation with ramps to avoid uniform appearance.
Key Takeaway

Curves and ramps are powerful for timing visual transitions; tweak control points to match the feel and tempo of your effect.
