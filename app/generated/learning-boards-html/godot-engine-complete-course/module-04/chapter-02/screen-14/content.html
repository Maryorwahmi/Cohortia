Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Particle System
Many short-lived sprites coordinated to form effects like smoke or explosions.
Texture & ColorRamp
Texture makes the particle readable; ColorRamp animates color and alpha over lifetime.
GDScript Control
Use restart(), emitting, and finished to play effects and free or hide them when done.
Summary
Design what the particles should feel like, animate their look over their lifetime, and script lifecycle events to keep effects responsive and memory-safe.
