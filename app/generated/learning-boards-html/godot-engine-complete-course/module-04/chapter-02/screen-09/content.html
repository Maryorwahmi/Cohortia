Process Flow
Velocity & Forces
Initial Velocity Min/Max
Add per-particle speed variation so not every particle feels identical.
Direction & Spread
Direction is an average vector; Spread defines a cone around it in degrees.
Gravity (Vector2)
Constant force applied to particles; use to simulate weight or wind.
Linear & Angular Accel
Change velocities across the particle lifetime to create acceleration effects.
Concrete scenarios
Explosion spit
High initial velocity, Spread 180, small gravity so particles arc outward.
Smoke plume
Low initial velocity, negative or low gravity, slow Linear Accel upward.
Falling debris
Moderate velocity and strong gravity to make pieces fall quickly off-screen.
Common mistake

High velocities with many particles can push most particles off-screen quickly; balance Amount, Lifetime, and velocities for visible impact.
