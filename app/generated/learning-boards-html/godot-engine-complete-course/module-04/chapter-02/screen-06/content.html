Comparison
Emission Shapes
Point
Single origin. Best for muzzle flashes, small explosions, or sparks coming from one spot.
Sphere
Circular disk. Good for radial bursts and dust puffs with soft edges.
Box
Area emission. Use for fog patches, broad environmental particles, or large swaths of rain.
Directed Box
Emits from a side or edge. Ideal for waterfalls, curtains of rain, or edge-origin effects.
Practical scenarios
Explosion
Sphere with Spread 180 to send particles outward in all directions.
Rain from a roof
Directed Box placed at the roof edge with Direction pointing downward and Gravity set to pull drops.
Ground fog
Box emission with low initial velocity and long lifetime to create a lingering layer.
Pro tip

Use small shape sizes for tight bursts, larger sizes for soft, spread-out effects. Shape choice affects both origin and perceived scale.
