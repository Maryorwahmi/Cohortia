Process Flow
Particle Lifecycle Steps
  1. Emit: Emission Shape, Amount, Direction, Spread.
  2. Move: Initial Velocity, Gravity, Linear and Angular Accel.
  3. Evolve: ColorRamp and Scale Amount Curve animate appearance.
  4. Die: Lifetime ends and the particle disappears.
Misconception: lifetime controls only visibility. It also impacts perceived speed and density when combined with velocity and amount.
Visual controls
ColorRamp
Define a gradient for color and alpha across the particle’s lifetime to fade or shift hues.
Scale Amount Curve
A curve controlling size from birth (0.0) to death (1.0) so particles grow or shrink naturally.
Initial Velocity
Set min and max to add variation in speed among particles.
Gravity & Accel
Gravity Vector2 pulls particles; linear and angular acceleration modify velocities over time.
Example: Explosion
One Shot true, Amount 50–100, Lifetime 0.5–1.0, Sphere emission, Spread 180 to send particles in all directions.
Check your understanding
If particles fall too slowly, should you increase lifetime or gravity? Consider perceived speed first.
