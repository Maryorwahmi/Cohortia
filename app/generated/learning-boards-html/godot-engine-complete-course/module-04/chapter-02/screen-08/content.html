Definition
Texture and Material
Texture determines the particle sprite; Material and Color settings refine shading and animation.
Small, soft textures
Radial gradients make good smoke and dust sprites without an external image.
GradientTexture2D
Built-in gradients can create soft particles and reduce art dependency.
Variation settings
Hue, saturation, and alpha variation add randomness so particles don't look identical.
Example: Dust Puff
Use a soft radial GradientTexture2D or a small cloud sprite. One Shot true, Amount 20, Lifetime 0.8, Scale Curve grows then fades, ColorRamp from light grey to transparent.
Avoid
Large detailed textures for many particles; these cost CPU and can blur on screen.
Pro tip
Use ColorRamp alpha to fade particles instead of per-frame sprite animation to reduce texture needs.
Key Takeaway

Pick small readable textures and rely on ColorRamp and curves to animate look over lifetime for efficient, clear effects.
