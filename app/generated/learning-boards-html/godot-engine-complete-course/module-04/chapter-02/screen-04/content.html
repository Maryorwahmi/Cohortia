Core Concept
What is a Particle System?
Small actors, big motion. A coordinated set of sprites that follow rules to appear as smoke, sparks, or rain.
Definition
Generates and animates many small images that together form a visual effect.
CPUParticles2D
Godot’s 2D particle node that runs on the CPU and is straightforward to configure for typical 2D effects.
Common misconception
Particles are not single animated sprites; they are many small sprites each with its own lifecycle and properties.
Concrete examples
Explosion (one-shot)
Many particles, short lifetime, Sphere emission, ColorRamp from bright to dark, Scale Curve to grow then fade.
Rain (continuous)
One Shot off, steady Amount and Lifetime, Directed Box or Box emission, Gravity to pull droplets down.
Dust puff (triggered)
One Shot true, small Amount, low initial velocity, light ColorRamp fading to transparent.
Key Takeaway

Think of particles as many tiny actors; choose CPUParticles2D for straightforward 2D effects and tune properties to match intent.
