Core Concept What is Parallax Scrolling?

Parallax: a perceptual trick

Layers move at different rates so the brain infers depth. This is purely visual and works with flat textures.

Example: Car window metaphor — close objects pass quickly, distant ones move slowly.
Misconception: Parallax needs 3D assets. Reality: 2D layers and relative motion are enough.
Why it matters: Adds scale and movement cues without extra geometry.
Key Takeaway

Parallax is a relative motion technique that reads as depth; tune speed contrast to match your scene's scale.

Layer examples and motion_scale suggestions
Distant mountains
motion_scale = Vector2(0.1, 0.1)
Almost stationary
Mid-ground skyline
motion_scale = Vector2(0.4, 0.4)
Moderate speed
Close bushes
motion_scale = Vector2(0.7, 0.7)
