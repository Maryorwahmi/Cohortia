Key Concepts What to Remember
Parallax Scrolling
Layered motion where closer layers move faster than distant ones.
ParallaxBackground
Container node that manages scroll_offset to drive layers.
ParallaxLayer
Holds visuals and uses motion_scale, motion_offset, and mirroring for effect.
Summary
Tune motion_scale for depth, set mirroring to texture widths for seamless tiling, and update scroll_offset from Camera2D for smooth motion.
