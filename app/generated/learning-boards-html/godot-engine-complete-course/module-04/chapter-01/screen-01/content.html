Parallax Scrolling in Godot

Create layered backgrounds that move at different rates to suggest distance and scale. This chapter shows how to set the nodes, tune motion, tile textures, and script camera sync.

In this lesson
Parallax nodes, motion_scale, mirroring, GDScript camera sync.
Outcome
A working multi-layer background that tiles and follows your Camera2D.
Why it matters
Adds perceived scale without extra geometry or 3D complexity.
What to watch
Texture tiling and motion_scale values are the most impactful settings.
Visual concept
Layer speeds produce the depth illusion. Tune motion_scale per layer.
Explore Key Topics
motion_scale
Controls layer speed. Smaller values appear further away.
mirroring
Repeat textures to create infinite horizontal scrolling.
Camera sync
Update ParallaxBackground.scroll_offset from Camera2D for consistent motion.
Key Takeaway

Parallax uses different motion scales to imply distance. Mirroring makes repeatable backgrounds seamless while syncing to the camera keeps movement coherent.
