Comparison
Node categories
Visual — Sprite2D
Draws images. Keep rendering separate from logic.
Transform — Node2D
Provides position and grouping without additional behavior.
Physics — CharacterBody2D
Handles movement and collision for characters.
Audio — AudioStreamPlayer
Play sound effects or music tied to a node.
Concrete example
Player composition with responsibilities
Player.tscn
Root node: CharacterBody2D — movement and collision. Child: Sprite2D — visuals. Child: CollisionShape2D — hitbox. Child: AudioStreamPlayer — footsteps.
Key Takeaway

Keep behavior in nodes that provide relevant systems, and visuals in render nodes to keep scenes modular.
