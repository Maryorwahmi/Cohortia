Shapes and their effects
  • CircleShape2D: best for round coins; consistent radial detection.
  • RectangleShape2D: suitable for square or rectangular items and panels.
  • Size shapes to match visuals to avoid early or late triggers.
Concrete example
If a coin sprite has visible padding, reduce the CollisionShape2D radius so the player touches the visible coin before detection triggers.
Testing checklist
  1. Place coin and player in scene.
  2. Move player through coin and observe when body_entered fires.
  3. Adjust shape until detection timing feels correct.
Pitfall
Using overly large masks or leaving shapes misaligned causes coins to trigger off-screen or through walls. Always test in the running scene.
