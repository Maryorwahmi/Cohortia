Enemies and Basic Combat
Practical patterns for enemy scenes, patrolling AI, hit detection, and health-driven defeat.
In this lesson
Build an Enemy.tscn with CharacterBody2D, add Area2D hitboxes, and implement a health system that queues freed enemies.
Why it matters
Reliable hit detection and safe node removal keep gameplay predictable and prevent runtime errors.
Concept map
Enemy Scene
CharacterBody2D + Sprite2D + CollisionShape2D + Area2D
AI
Patrol via direction variable and patrol_distance
Combat
Area2D signals for attack and damage; _take_damage handles health
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Quick topics to explore
Patrol AI
Use a direction variable, patrol_distance, and move_and_slide to loop movement between waypoints.
Damage Detection
Area2D body_entered and area_entered signals let you trigger damage only when intended.
Health & Removal
Implement _take_damage to reduce health, then queue_free to remove defeated enemies safely.
