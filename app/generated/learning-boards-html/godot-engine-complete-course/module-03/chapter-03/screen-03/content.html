Module 03 › Chapter 3.3 › Learning Objectives — Signals
Signal wiring
Practice connecting your DamageArea body_entered signal to the enemy handler and verify body checks for the Player node before calling _take_damage. This prevents non-player bodies from triggering damage.
Test case
Move the player through an enemy DamageArea and confirm the player health decreases by the configured amount, and the enemy remains until its health reaches zero.
Misconception check
Signals are not magic: verify the node's name or group membership before calling its methods to avoid calling on null or unrelated nodes.
Integration & testing
Instance multiple enemies in your main scene and walk through interactions: enemy patrols, player damage, enemy defeat, and scene restart on player death. Confirm queue_free is used to remove enemies safely.
Playtest checklist
  • Player health decrements on contact
  • Enemy health decrements when hit by player attack
  • Defeated enemy calls queue_free and disappears
  • Player death triggers scene reload
