Module 03 › Chapter 3.3 › CharacterBody2D vs RigidBody2D
CharacterBody2D
  • Scripted movement via velocity and move_and_slide
  • Deterministic patrols and chase behaviors
  • Good for platformer enemies and NPCs
Example
Patrolling guard that reverses direction at waypoints.
RigidBody2D
  • Physics-driven via forces and impulses
  • Natural collisions, momentum, and realistic reactions
  • Good for hazards like rolling boulders or debris
Misconception
Don’t set position each frame on a RigidBody2D to simulate AI — it fights the physics solver and creates jitter.
Key Takeaway

Pick CharacterBody2D for controlled AI movement and RigidBody2D for physics-first interactions; structure your enemies accordingly.
