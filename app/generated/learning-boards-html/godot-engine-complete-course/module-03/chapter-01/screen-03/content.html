Learning Objectives — Testing & Pitfalls
How to verify and avoid common mistakes
Collision alignment
Adjust the CollisionShape2D to match the sprite. Visible testing helps find edge clipping and stuck conditions.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Frame-rate safety
Use _physics_process and multiply accelerations by delta for stable physics across devices.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Smooth stopping
Use move_toward to decelerate velocity.x so stops feel natural and avoid snapping to zero.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Avoid direct position changes
Directly changing position bypasses collisions and leads to tunneling. Always use move_and_slide or move_and_collide for physics bodies.
