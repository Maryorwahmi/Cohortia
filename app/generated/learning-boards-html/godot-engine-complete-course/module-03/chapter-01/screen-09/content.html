Gravity pattern
Apply gravity as acceleration multiplied by delta for consistent falling behavior.
  • Only apply gravity if the character is airborne to prevent buildup while grounded.
  • Multiply gravity by delta to keep behavior independent from frame rate.
  • Tune gravity and jump velocity together for desired hang time and responsiveness.
Example
velocity.y += gravity * delta when not is_on_floor(); set velocity.y = jump_velocity to jump upward.
Tuning guide
If your jumps feel floaty, increase gravity or lower jump_velocity. If jumps feel too short, decrease gravity or increase negative jump_velocity.
Key Takeaway

Gravity is an acceleration applied each physics frame; always multiply by delta and tune alongside jump_velocity.
