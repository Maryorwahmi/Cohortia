Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Testing & Pitfalls
Quick test setup
Add a StaticBody2D floor with a CollisionShape2D, instance the player, and run the scene to observe behavior.
  • Toggle collision shape visibility to inspect alignment during runtime.
  • Walk the player to edges and slopes to ensure sliding and landing behave correctly.
  • Adjust exported speed, gravity, and jump_velocity until the feel is right.
Common mistakes
Direct position changes, missing CollisionShape2D, or using _process for physics cause most early bugs.
Scenario
If your player sometimes passes through thin platforms, first verify the CollisionShape2D size and that movement happens in _physics_process. If the problem persists, reduce movement per frame or investigate collision layers and masks.
Key Takeaway

Systematic testing with visible collision shapes and physics-frame updates will catch most movement and collision issues early.
