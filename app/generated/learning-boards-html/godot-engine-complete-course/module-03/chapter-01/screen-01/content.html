Setting Up the Player and Movement
Create a script-driven player that responds to input, gravity, and collisions reliably.
In this lesson

Scene setup, GDScript for physics, input mapping, gravity, and move_and_slide for collision-aware motion.

Why this matters
Collision-aware movement prevents players from passing through geometry and gives consistent behavior across machines.
Quick start

Create a CharacterBody2D scene, add Sprite2D and CollisionShape2D, attach Player.gd, then instance in a main scene with a StaticBody2D floor.
