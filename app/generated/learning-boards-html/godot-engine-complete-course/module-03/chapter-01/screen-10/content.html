Grounded jump pattern
Only allow a jump when the engine reports the body is on the floor and the jump action is just pressed.
  • Check is_on_floor to ensure the player is grounded.
  • Use Input.is_action_just_pressed("ui_accept") to apply a single jump impulse.
  • Set velocity.y = jump_velocity, then gravity will act on future frames.
Debug tip
Toggle CollisionShape2D visibility to confirm the collision bottom overlaps the floor and that is_on_floor returns true when expected.
Jump snippet
if Input.is_action_just_pressed("ui_accept") and is_on_floor():
  velocity.y = jump_velocity
Key Takeaway

Gate jumps with a floor check and trigger with a just-pressed action to avoid repeated impulses.
