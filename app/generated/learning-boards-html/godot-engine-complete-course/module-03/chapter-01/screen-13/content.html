Collision-aware movement
Use move_and_slide to let Godot resolve collisions, sliding along surfaces rather than stopping abruptly.
  • move_and_slide takes your velocity and moves the body with collision resolution.
  • It enables reliable contact checks such as is_on_floor after the move.
  • Avoid changing position directly for bodies that must collide.
Behavior check
When hitting a wall, the engine will slide the body along the surface if velocity directs it that way, producing smooth gameplay motion.
Usage
# After velocity updates
move_and_slide()
Key Takeaway

Call move_and_slide each physics frame after updating velocity so the engine can perform collision resolution and sliding.
