Comparison › Loop mistakes
Off-by-one
Confirm whether the range end should be inclusive or exclusive and adjust arguments accordingly.
Infinite loop
Add counters, maximum iterations, or break conditions to ensure loops terminate in all cases.
Mutating collections
Iterate over a copy or collect removals to apply after the loop to avoid skipping elements.
Quick checklist

Verify termination, check range bounds, avoid in-loop mutation, and test with small inputs first.

Pro tip

Use clear names like charge_progress, iteration_limit, or items_to_remove so intent is obvious when reviewing code.
