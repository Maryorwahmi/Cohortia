What to Remember › Short Reference
Loops
For known counts use for(range). While is for dynamic termination but must be guarded.
Conditionals
if/elif/else for ordered checks; ensure branches cover all cases and use else as a fallback.
Safety
Avoid infinite loops, watch indentation, and do not mutate a collection while iterating it.
Final Tip
Write small functions, add short comments about termination, and test edge cases—these habits keep control flow correct and maintainable.
