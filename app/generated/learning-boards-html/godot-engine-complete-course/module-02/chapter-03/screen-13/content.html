Layered responsibilities
Player
Owns health, movement, and emits signals like player_health_changed and player_died.
UI
Listens for health updates and renders labels without changing player state.
GameManager
Coordinates game flow, listens for death events, and handles level transitions.
Quick guideline
Design each module to do one thing well and announce changes rather than directly mutating other modules when possible.
