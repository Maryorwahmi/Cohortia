Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Print vs Assert vs push_error
print()
Exploratory checks
Good for quick visibility; remove or convert to debug-only prints later.
assert()
Fail fast
Use for invariants you want to enforce during development with clear messages.
push_error()
Report without halting
Log recoverable problems while continuing execution for resilience.
Decision rule
If a broken assumption makes further execution meaningless or dangerous, assert. If you want to record but continue, push_error. For temporary exploration, use print_debug and remove it once solved.
