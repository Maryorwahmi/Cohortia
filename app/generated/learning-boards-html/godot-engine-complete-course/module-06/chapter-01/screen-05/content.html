Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Folder Patterns
Type-based
Organize by Scenes, Scripts, and Assets. Simple to navigate when you know what you want.
Feature-based
Group files by feature, such as Player, Enemy, or UI, useful when features are self-contained.
Hybrid
Combine both: maintain top-level Scenes and Scripts while allowing feature folders for complex areas.
Concrete example
Assets/ Sprites/ player_idle.png Player/ player_run_sheet.png Scenes/ player.tscn Scripts/ player.gd
When the player has many specialized assets, move them into a Player subfolder under Assets while keeping scenes and core scripts at a predictable top level.
