Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Optimization Workflow
Step-by-step
1. Profile the slow scenario and record baseline numbers.
2. Identify highest-cost systems from the profiler (Script, Draw, Physics).
3. Implement one focused fix (e.g., instancing, caching nodes, simplifying shapes).
4. Re-run profiler, compare numbers, and confirm the change improved the target metric.
Check your understanding
If the Draw time drops but physics time rises after a change, consider whether the new structure introduced extra physics bodies or per-instance computations.
Key Takeaway

Optimizations must be measurable. If a fix can't be shown to help in the profiler, reassess its cost and benefit.
