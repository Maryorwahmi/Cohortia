Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
Culling
Skip rendering objects not visible to the camera to save GPU cycles.
Batching & Instancing
Group draws using MultiMeshInstance2D or shared materials to reduce draw calls.
Atlases & LOD
Texture atlases reduce texture swaps; LOD lowers detail for distant objects to save GPU and memory.
Final takeaway

Combine profiling evidence with targeted techniques like culling, batching, and asynchronous loading to achieve measurable performance gains without sacrificing gameplay quality.
