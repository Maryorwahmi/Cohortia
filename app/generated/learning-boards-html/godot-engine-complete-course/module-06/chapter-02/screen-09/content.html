Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Physics & Audio
Physics checklist
  • Use StaticBody for immovable objects.
  • Use simple shapes: Box or Circle rather than complex meshes.
  • Reduce active bodies by disabling physics for distant or off-screen objects.
Common Mistake

Using ConcavePolygon shapes for many objects when convex approximations would suffice.

Audio checklist
  • Pool AudioStreamPlayer nodes for repetitive short sounds.
  • Limit simultaneous sounds; silence or duck distant sources.
  • Choose compressed formats and preload or stream large music files appropriately.
Key Takeaway

Match physics and audio fidelity to gameplay needs; avoid defaulting to maximum precision when cheaper options suffice.
