Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Mistakes to Avoid
Top mistakes
  • Premature optimization: changing code without profiler evidence.
  • Assuming full automatic batching: material and atlas design matter.
  • Replacing logic with instancing: MultiMesh handles visuals, not AI or physics.
  • Raising physics_fps unnecessarily: increases CPU load.
  • Synchronous loading in gameplay: causes frame hitches.
Cures and checks
Collect profiler data and prioritize the largest contributors first.
Group identical textures into atlases to aid batching.
Use pooling for behavior and instancing for rendering where appropriate.
Key Takeaway

Trust the profiler, not intuition. Small, measured changes beat broad rewrites without data.
