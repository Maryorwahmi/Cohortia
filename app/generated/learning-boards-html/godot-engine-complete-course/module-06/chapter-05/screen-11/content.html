Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Business & Taxes
Why it matters

Revenue becomes reportable income

Whether you sell one copy or thousands, platforms report activity. You must keep records and comply with local tax obligations.

Considerations
  • Decide individual vs company registration based on liability and tax needs.
  • Separate business banking for clear bookkeeping.
  • Understand VAT, sales tax, or digital services taxes for regions you sell into.
Platform reports

Stores provide revenue and transaction reports; use them to reconcile payouts, fees, refunds, and chargebacks before filing taxes.

Example workflow
  1. Export monthly sales and payout reports from each platform.
  2. Reconcile platform fees and refunds against gross sales.
  3. Record net revenue in your bookkeeping tool and allocate for tax obligations.
  4. Consult an accountant for local filing advice.
Misconception

Don't assume low revenue means no reporting. Many jurisdictions require reporting even for small businesses; thresholds and rules vary.

Takeaway
Plan bookkeeping before launch, centralize reports, and consult tax guidance for your country to avoid surprises.
