Publishing and Distribution Considerations
Make your Godot game available, visible, and supported across platforms.
In this lesson

Learn platform options and submission steps, prepare assets reviewers expect, plan marketing, and cover essential legal items like IP and privacy policies.

Why it matters

A well-prepared release cuts review delays, improves discoverability, and reduces legal risk. Treat publishing as part of development, not an afterthought.
