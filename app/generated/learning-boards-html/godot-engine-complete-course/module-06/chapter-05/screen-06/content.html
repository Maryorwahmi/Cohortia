Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Promotional Assets
What to prepare
  • 4–8 screenshots showing core mechanics and variety
  • 30–90 second gameplay trailer focused on moments players care about
  • App icon and store-sized key art with clear composition
  • Press kit with logo, short description, and contact information
Naming & Organization
Use consistent filenames and a clear folder structure so reviewers and press can find the right images and videos quickly.
Example press kit layout
/presskit/logo.png — high-res logo
/presskit/screenshots/01_hub.png
/presskit/trailer.mp4 — short gameplay trailer
/presskit/onepage.txt — short description and contact
Avoid misleading thumbnails and over-edited screenshots that do not reflect actual gameplay.
