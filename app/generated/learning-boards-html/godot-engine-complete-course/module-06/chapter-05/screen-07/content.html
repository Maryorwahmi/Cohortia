Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Marketing & Promotion
Core Strategies
  • Start devlogs and share progress early to build a following.
  • Create a press kit and prepare review keys for journalists and streamers.
  • Post short gameplay clips and GIFs on social channels that fit your audience.
  • Engage with targeted communities rather than broadcasting everywhere.
Launch Timeline (Example)
- 8–12 weeks out: Build devlog cadence, gather press contacts.
- 4 weeks out: Share trailer and key screenshots, open wishlists.
- Launch week: Announce release, send review keys, monitor feedback.
- Post-launch: Patch quick fixes, follow up with new content or discounts.
