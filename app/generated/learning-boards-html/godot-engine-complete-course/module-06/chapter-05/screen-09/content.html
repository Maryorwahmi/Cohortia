Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Age Ratings
Definition

What an age rating is

Ratings classify content for regions and storefronts. They are used by platforms and laws to determine who can access your game and whether additional warnings or restrictions are required.

IARC
Questionnaire-based system used by many app stores to map answers to regional ratings.
Regional Boards
ESRB, PEGI and other boards apply regional rules and may be required for certain storefronts.
Process

Typical steps to obtain a rating

  1. Review a platform's rating requirements and regional rules.
  2. Prepare representative screenshots or clips showing the most mature content.
  3. Complete the rating questionnaire accurately; be conservative on questionable content.
  4. Submit and retain confirmation records; plan for review time in your schedule.
Misconception

Some developers assume ratings are optional for indie PC and mobile games. In reality many storefronts and laws require ratings, and missing them can delay or prevent release in some regions.

Example

How a small studio handled ratings

A small studio completed the IARC questionnaire early, discovered one mechanic that increased the likely rating, and adjusted a non-essential effect to keep the rating broader. This change expanded their potential audience and avoided a formal regional review for that content.

Takeaway
Plan for ratings early. They influence content and market access.
Checklist
  • Gather clips
  • Answer questionnaires honestly
  • Log the certificate
Why this matters

Proper ratings ensure your game reaches the right audience, comply with store policies and law, and avoid unexpected removal or restricted availability after release.
