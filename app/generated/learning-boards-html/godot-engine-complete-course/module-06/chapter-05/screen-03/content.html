Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Practical Skills
01 • Accounts & Packaging
Learn the steps to create developer accounts and pick the correct build format for each platform. For example, prepare .apk for Android and .ipa for iOS, and desktop builds like .exe with accompanying data files.
Checklist
  • Register developer account and verify identity
  • Choose target OS and export templates
  • Test builds on target devices
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 • Press Kit & Trailers
Prepare high-quality screenshots, a short trailer, and a one-paragraph description that clearly communicates gameplay and hooks the audience.
Kit Elements
  • 4–8 screenshots showing core mechanics
  • 30–90 second trailer highlighting unique moments
  • Logo, icon, and short description
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 • Privacy & Ratings
Prepare a privacy policy if you collect analytics or any user data, and be ready to request age ratings when required by a platform.
Key Items
  • Privacy policy text covering data collected and retention
  • Plan for ESRB/PEGI/IARC where applicable
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 • Post-launch Plan
Draft a simple schedule for patches, community channels, and how you will collect and respond to player feedback after release.
Plan Steps
  • Set up a feedback channel (Discord, forum, or email)
  • Schedule first patch window and bug triage process
