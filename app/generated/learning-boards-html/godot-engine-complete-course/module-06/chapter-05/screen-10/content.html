Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Developer Accounts
Core Idea

Account setup is preparation

Developer accounts couple identity, tax, and payments. Fill them carefully; errors lead to payouts or submission blocks.

Steps
  1. Gather identity or company documents for verification.
  2. Prepare bank/merchant details for payouts.
  3. Review platform-specific fees; Steam has a per-game fee as noted in platform docs.
  4. Set up tax or VAT information where required.
Misconception

Many assume developer accounts are instant. Verification and payment setup can require identity checks, bank confirmations, or tax forms that take time.

Example

Preparing a payment profile

In one example, a solo developer set up accounts for two storefronts: a PC storefront and a mobile app store. They prepared scanned ID, their tax number, and bank details in advance. When the mobile store requested additional VAT details for a region, they could respond quickly. Because their payment name matched tax records, payouts cleared without holds.
Verification
ID, company docs, sometimes bank proofs.
Payouts
Set payout currency and thresholds early.
Fees
Check platform fees and revenue share terms.
Practical tip

Create a secure credential store, enable two-factor authentication, and keep a changelog of which bank or tax info you supplied to each platform. This saves time during audits or tax season.
