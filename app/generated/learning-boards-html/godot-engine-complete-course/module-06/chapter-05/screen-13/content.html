Pre-Launch Checklist
  • Developer account & payments: Verified and ready.
  • Build testing: Final build passes basic smoke tests and platform checks.
  • Legal: Privacy policy, EULA, and IP clearance completed.
  • Assets: Screenshots, trailer, key art, and press kit prepared.
  • Marketing: Launch posts scheduled, reviewers contacted, and community channels set up.
  • Support plan: Patch schedule and bug triage ready.
Common Mistakes
Neglecting marketing, ignoring asset licenses, missing legal documents, and abandoning the game after release. Each of these reduces reach or creates legal risk.
Example impact

A missing privacy policy prevented one developer from publishing the mobile build. Preparing this document and re-submitting cost them valuable launch momentum.

Key Takeaway

Build a short pre-launch checklist and verify each item two weeks before your release date to avoid last-minute delays.

Quick prompt

Which single missing item would block your release today? Fix that first.
