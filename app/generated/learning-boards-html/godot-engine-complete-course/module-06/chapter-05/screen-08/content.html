Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Legal Considerations
Intellectual Property
Verify ownership and licenses for all art, audio, code, and fonts. Keep a simple record of sources and license terms for third-party assets.
EULA & Privacy
  • Draft EULA to state permitted uses and liability limits
  • Create a privacy policy if you collect data, even anonymous analytics
Ratings & Taxes
Plan for ESRB/PEGI/IARC when required. If selling, set up proper tax and payment details to receive revenue from stores.
Common Mistake
Skipping a privacy policy or mis-licensing an asset can delay or block your submission. Resolve licenses early.
