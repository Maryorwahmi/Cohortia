Platform Snapshot
Itch.io
Low barrier, flexible pricing, direct community interaction—great for early releases and experimental work.
Steam
Large audience and discovery tools, but a submission fee, review steps, and a crowded marketplace.
Mobile Stores
Huge reach on Android and iOS, but require store packaging, privacy policy, and potentially age ratings.
Quick comparison
Barrier: Low (Itch)
Reach: High (Steam/Mobile)
Fees: Minimal (Itch) vs Fee/Revenue Share (Stores)
Review: Light (Itch) vs Strict (Stores)
Decision Checklist
  • Who is your primary player? Desktop, mobile, or niche communities?
  • Do you need flexible pricing or discoverability tools?
  • Can you meet store policies and review timelines?
  • Are you prepared for post-launch support on that platform?
Common Mistake

Choosing a platform based on prestige rather than audience fit often reduces discoverability and wastes effort on unsupported features.
