Definition

What post-launch support looks like

Support combines technical work—patching bugs and releasing updates—with community actions—responding to feedback, moderating channels, and publishing devlogs.

Triage
Reproduce reports, prioritize by severity, assign fixes to sprints.
Communication
Release notes, changelogs, and progress updates build trust.
Example schedule
  • Week 1: Patch critical bugs and publish hotfix notes.
  • Weeks 2–4: Address high-priority balance and quality-of-life items.
  • Monthly: Publish a devlog summarizing progress and roadmap.
Channels
Choose one or two primary platforms for community interaction—Discord, a forum, or a store page—and direct players there from your store listing.
Moderation
Set clear rules and a path to report abuse so the community remains welcoming.
Feedback loop
Collect reproducible bug reports and prioritize by player impact.
Takeaway
Consistent patches and respectful engagement turn players into long-term supporters.
