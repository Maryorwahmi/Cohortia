Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Practice Milestones
01 — Viewport Navigation
Orbit, pan, and zoom to inspect objects. Confirm transform values update in the Inspector as you move objects.
Why it matters: precise placement and inspection speed up prototyping.
02 — Create a Primitive
Add a MeshInstance3D, choose New BoxMesh, and place it slightly above the PlaneMesh floor.
Why it matters: visible geometry is required to iterate on level shapes.
03 — Parenting and Transforms
Create a parent Node3D, give the child a local offset, then move the parent to observe global movement.
Why it matters: predictable hierarchies prevent transform surprises.
04 — Camera + Light
Place a Camera3D, make it current, and add a DirectionalLight3D with shadows to reveal form.
Why it matters: visibility and lighting are essential for readable scenes.
