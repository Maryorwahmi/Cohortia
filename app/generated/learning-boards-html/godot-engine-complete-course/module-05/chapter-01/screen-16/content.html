Transforms & Hierarchy
Local transforms are relative; global transforms are absolute. Parent movements affect children automatically.
Visibility Essentials
Ensure MeshInstance3D has a mesh, Camera3D is current, and you have at least one Light3D so objects render properly.
Lighting & Performance
Use directional lights for broad illumination; limit shadow-casting lights to maintain performance.
Final thought

These three checkpoints will help you set up stable, visible, and performant 3D scenes in Godot as you prototype and iterate.
