Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Metallic vs Roughness
Metallic in practice
Metals reflect their environment and have little or no diffuse color. Use metallic maps to localize metal vs non-metal areas on a mesh.
Example
Polished chrome: metallic = 1, roughness = 0.05. A painted surface: metallic = 0, roughness varies.
Roughness in practice
Roughness controls microfacet scattering and reflection blur. Small roughness gives sharp reflections, large roughness gives diffuse appearance.
Pro tip
Combine maps: use a roughness map to vary smooth and worn areas, and a metallic map to define which parts behave like metal.
