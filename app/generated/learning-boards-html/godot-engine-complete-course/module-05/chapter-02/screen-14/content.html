File Formats
GLTF is recommended for PBR; FBX, OBJ, and DAE have trade-offs.
PBR Properties
Albedo, Metallic, Roughness, Normal, Emission and AO each serve specific roles.
Optimization
Right-size textures, use compression, and pack channels when appropriate.
Summary takeaway
Correct format choice, precise import checks, correct texture placement, and optimization keep your models both realistic and performant.
