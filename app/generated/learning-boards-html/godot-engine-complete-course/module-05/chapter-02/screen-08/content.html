Albedo explained

Albedo is the material’s inherent color. It should not include baked lighting or shadows. Use sRGB color space for albedo textures to display colors accurately under scene lighting.

Correct: color texture with clean material color, no shadows.
Incorrect: albedo with baked shadows or lighting baked into the image.
Practical checks
  • Verify texture color space and import flags.
  • Make sure normal, roughness, and metallic maps are not placed in the albedo slot.
  • Preview the model under neutral lighting to ensure albedo looks correct.
Misconception
Baked shadows in albedo are acceptable. They are not — they limit lighting flexibility and realism.
