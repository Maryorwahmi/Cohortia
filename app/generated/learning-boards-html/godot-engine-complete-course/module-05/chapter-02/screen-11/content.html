Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Texture Optimization
Sizing and compression
Match resolution to visual importance and enable compression in the import dock. Smaller textures save VRAM and improve rendering performance.
Hero props: 2048 or 4096 depending on camera proximity.
Distant props: 512–1024 to avoid wasted memory.
Packing & flags
Consider packing metallic and roughness into a single texture’s channels. Set normal maps to linear and enable filtering for better results.
Performance tip
Profile on target devices and reduce texture sizes until performance targets are met without noticeable quality loss.
