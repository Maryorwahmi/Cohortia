PBR property quick-reference
Albedo
Base color or texture defining surface color and pattern.
Metallic
Defines metalness. Metals reflect environment and have no diffuse color contribution.
Roughness
Controls smoothness. Low roughness = sharp reflections; high roughness = diffuse reflection.
Detail maps and extras
Normal Map
Fakes surface detail via per-pixel normals without extra geometry.
Emission
Makes surfaces appear self-lit for screens and glowing elements.
Ambient Occlusion (AO)
Adds soft shadowing in crevices for perceived depth.
Key Takeaway
PBR properties work together; tweak metallic and roughness together for believable reflections.
