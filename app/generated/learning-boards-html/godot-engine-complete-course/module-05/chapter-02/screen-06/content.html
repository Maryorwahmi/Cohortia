Common problems and fixes
Unit scale mismatch
Adjust Unit Scale in the Import dock or re-export at correct units from your modeling tool.
Incorrect origin/pivot
Fix the pivot in the modeling tool or translate mesh geometry so the origin behaves as expected in Godot.
Flipped normals and UV issues
Check normals in your modeling tool and ensure UVs are not overlapping; normal maps require correct UVs to look right.
Quick validation scene
  1. Instance the model in a new scene.
  2. Add Camera3D and DirectionalLight3D.
  3. Rotate camera around model, inspect silhouettes and shading.
  4. Fix scale, pivot, or normals and reimport until correct.
Common mistake
Assuming the model's transform in the authoring tool will match Godot’s; always re-check after import.
