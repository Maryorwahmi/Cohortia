Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › DirectionalLight3D
Core idea

Parallel rays, rotation controls angle

Directional lights simulate distant sources. Position is ignored; rotation and intensity determine shadows and highlights across the entire scene.

Properties to tune
  • Color — tint of the light.
  • Energy — overall intensity.
  • Specular — contribution to reflective highlights.
  • shadow_enabled — whether the light casts shadows.
Key Takeaway
Use DirectionalLight3D to set global sun/moon lighting and animate rotation to create day-night cycles.
Concrete example
Add one DirectionalLight3D and rotate its X to 45 degrees for midday, 15 degrees for sunrise/sunset. Enable shadows for a dramatic silhouette effect; reduce energy to avoid blown highlights at sunset.
Common Mistake
Moving the directional light's position to try to change lighting. Rotation only matters; repositioning has no visual impact.
