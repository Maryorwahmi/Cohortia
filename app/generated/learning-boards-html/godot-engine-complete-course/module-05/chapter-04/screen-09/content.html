Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Performance Strategies for Lighting
Decision checklist
  • Identify main lights that affect composition and give them priority.
  • Disable shadows for decorative lights or use cheaper shadow modes.
  • Consider baking static lighting where possible for complex static geometry.
  • Limit how many shadow-casting lights affect the same object.
Concrete workflow
Primary directional: high-quality shadows. Secondary lamp: no shadows or low-cost shadow. Static props: bake shadows into lightmaps.
Testing
Profile on target hardware and iterate. Visual quality must be weighed against framerate goals.
