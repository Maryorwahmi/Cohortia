Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › SpotLight3D
Core concept

Cone-shaped, directional illumination

Spot lights create focused beams that can be softened at the edges. Adjust inner/outer cone and range for desired effect.

Use cases
Flashlights, car headlights, stage spotlights, and any directed beam effect.
Performance tip
Narrow cones and short ranges reduce the number of lit fragments and shadow cost.
Concrete example
Attach a SpotLight3D to a vehicle's headlamp with a tight cone and limited range. Add a soft outer cone to avoid harsh edges while keeping the scene readable at night.
Common Mistake
Widening cone indiscriminately to light a large area. This increases shading and shadowing costs quickly; prefer multiple local lights or ambient tweaks instead.
