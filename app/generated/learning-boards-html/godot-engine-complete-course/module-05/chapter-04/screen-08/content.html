Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Avoiding Shadow Artifacts
Iterative tuning process
Step 1
Enable shadows for the main light first and observe artifacts.
Step 2
Incrementally adjust Bias until acne reduces—but watch for peter-panning.
Step 3
If detachment appears, lower Bias and try a softer PCF mode or geometry fixes.
Quick fixes
  • Adjust normal bias where available.
  • Avoid extremely thin geometry that self-intersects.
Testing tip
Test on target hardware with multiple lights enabled to measure real performance impact.
