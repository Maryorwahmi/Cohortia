Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › PhysicalSkyMaterial
What it models
  • Sun Disk and sun angle.
  • Rayleigh and Mie scattering parameters for sky color.
  • Dynamic ambient contribution as sun moves.
When to choose
Use when you need evolving skies for day-night cycles and believable atmospheric color changes.
Concrete example
Rotate your DirectionalLight3D to a low sun angle and observe warm tones in the PhysicalSkyMaterial. Adjust Rayleigh for bluer mid-day skies and Mie for haze during sunrise.
Key Takeaway
PhysicalSkyMaterial is your tool for procedural, physically based sky effects that respond to sun position.
