Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › WorldEnvironment & Environment
Role of WorldEnvironment
One global node that provides an Environment resource containing global rendering settings. Use it to ensure consistent sky, fog, and ambient parameters across the scene.
Background
Solid color, Sky, or CanvasTexture. Sky choices significantly influence ambient contribution.
Ambient Light
Non-directional base light to avoid black occluded areas. Tune color and energy carefully.
Fog & Glow
Fog adds atmospheric depth with depth and height controls. Glow (bloom) makes bright areas feel luminous and can improve perceived brightness for emissive materials.
Key Takeaway
Configure Environment early to set a baseline for all lights; incorrect defaults often make scenes unexpectedly dark or flat.
