Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What to Remember
Directional = rotation
Use rotation to simulate sun angles; animate rotation for day-night cycles.
Prioritize shadows
Give high-quality shadows to main lights, use cheaper options elsewhere.
Configure WorldEnvironment
Set sky, ambient, fog, and glow early to ensure consistent lighting behavior.
Final quick tip

Iterate visually and on hardware. Small environment or light tweaks often have the largest perceptual effect.
