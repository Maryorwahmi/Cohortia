Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Light Properties & Shadows
Shared light properties
  • Color — tint that mixes with materials.
  • Energy — intensity of illumination.
  • Specular — amount of reflective highlights.
Shadow controls
Enable shadow_enabled to cast shadows. Choose shadow Mode (e.g., basic ShadowMapping or PCF variants) and adjust Bias to reduce self-shadowing artifacts.
Example tuning
For a primary sun, use PCF7 or PCF13 for softer shadows and tune bias to avoid acne. For decorative lamps, consider disabling shadows or using a low-cost shadow mode.
Common Issues
  • Shadow acne — visible self-shadowing artifacts; fix with bias.
  • Peter-panning — too much bias separates shadows from casters; balance is key.
Key Takeaway
Quality shadows for main lights, cheaper or no shadows for fill lights keeps visuals and performance balanced.
