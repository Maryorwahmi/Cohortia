Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › PanoramaSkyMaterial
What it provides
A 360° HDR or EXR image as a skybox that contributes realistic ambient lighting and reflections when used as the Environment background.
When to choose
Use for photorealistic scenes or when you need an exact photographic backdrop.
Concrete example
Import an HDRI panorama, assign to PanoramaSkyMaterial, and test how it changes ambient and reflections on materials. Swap panoramas to change environment instantly.
Limitations
Static by nature—does not simulate changing sun position or scattering unless you replace or blend images at runtime.
