Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
CharacterBody3D
Script-driven movement with collision handling via move_and_slide().
CollisionShape3D
Defines the collision boundary used by physics — keep it simple for performance.
RigidBody3D
Fully simulated body responding to forces, gravity, and collisions automatically.
Takeaway
Choose nodes that match expected interaction: control, simulation, or static collision. Keep collision shapes simple and test regularly.
