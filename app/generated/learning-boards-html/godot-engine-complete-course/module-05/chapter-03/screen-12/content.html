Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Debugging
Step checks
  1. Is the object a physics body with a CollisionShape3D child? If not, add one.
  2. Are collision layers and masks configured so the two bodies can interact?
  3. After move_and_slide(), call get_slide_collision_count() to see reported collisions.
  4. For signals, temporarily print from body_entered to verify detection.
  5. Visualize shapes in the editor and test at different speeds for tunneling.
Pro Tip

Use small debug prints and the editor's shape visualization to quickly locate missing or misaligned collision geometry.
