When to use Area3D
  • Pickups and coins that should trigger collection.
  • Zone-based checks, like entering an enemy detection radius.
  • Damage or healing volumes that apply effects without collision response.
Areas rely on signals such as body_entered and area_exited so your game logic can respond to overlaps cleanly.
Example: coin pickup

Create an Area3D with a small CollisionShape3D. Connect body_entered to a script that checks for the player and awards points, then removes the coin. This avoids physics pushes and keeps collection logic separate from collisions used for blocking.

Debug tip
Use collision layers and masks to limit which bodies the Area3D will detect.
