Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › CharacterBody3D
Definition

A node you move by setting velocity in code; collisions are resolved by the physics engine after your movement call.

Key properties
  • velocity: Vector3 representing movement
  • is_on_floor(): detects when grounded
  • move_and_slide(): performs movement with collision handling
Key Takeaway

Use CharacterBody3D when you need precise, script-driven player movement with reliable collision response.

Analogy & Example

Think of CharacterBody3D as a car you steer directly while the road provides friction and prevents you from leaving the pavement. Example: a first-person player where you set velocity.x and velocity.z from input and let move_and_slide() keep the player from clipping into walls.

Common mistake
Trying to simulate physics forces on a CharacterBody3D; it won't behave like a RigidBody3D.
Pro tip
Keep horizontal and vertical motion separate: update velocity.y for gravity and use input for x/z components.
Check your understanding: If your player keeps sliding after input stops, ensure you apply deceleration using move_toward on horizontal velocity components.
