Common Shape Resources
  • CapsuleShape3D — great for humanoid players; smooth sliding at edges.
  • BoxShape3D — fast and predictable for crates and platforms.
  • SphereShape3D — ideal for round objects like balls.
If you need highly detailed static collision, consider CollisionPolygon3D or a simplified mesh collider, but profile performance first.
Scenario & Analogy

Imagine wrapping a simple capsule around the player's mesh instead of matching every visual vertex. That capsule is cheaper to collide against and still prevents the player from passing through walls.

Common mistake
Using a high-poly visual mesh directly as collision leads to slow physics and unexpected behavior.
Check
Confirm every physics body has at least one CollisionShape3D child, otherwise collisions won't register.
Key Takeaway

Simpler collision shapes mean faster, more stable physics. Keep visuals and collision separate.
