Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Practical Objectives
01 — Scene Setup
Create a 3D scene with floor, light, and player node.

This environment lets you validate floor collision, jumping, and sliding interactions during development.

02 — Collision Testing
Add StaticBody3D obstacles and verify collisions.

Confirm objects have CollisionShape3D so the player can't pass through them and collisions report correctly.

03 — Signals & Areas
Use Area3D for pickups and triggers.

Areas detect overlaps without applying physical forces, making them ideal for pickups and zone checks.

04 — Debugging
Watch for missing shapes and scaling issues.

If a mesh isn't colliding, the likely cause is a missing StaticBody3D or CollisionShape3D; avoid non-uniform scale on collision geometry.
