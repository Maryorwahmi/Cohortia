Quick practice checklist
Projection toggle
Observe grid and object scale when switching between Perspective and Orthogonal. Verify parallel lines.
Mouse-look test
Capture mouse, rotate horizontally and vertically, and clamp vertical rotation to avoid flipping.
SpringArm clipping test
Add obstacles and watch the arm shorten or reposition rather than letting the camera move inside geometry.
Viewport sanity check
Render a SubViewport to a texture and confirm the rendered view matches the camera placement and is set to current within its viewport.
Mnemonic: P - Projection, F - Field of view, V - Viewport, P - Performance. Repeat these checks after major camera changes.
