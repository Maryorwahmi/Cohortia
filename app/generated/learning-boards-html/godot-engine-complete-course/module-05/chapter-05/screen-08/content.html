Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Third-Person Setup
Step-by-step setup
1. Add SpringArm3D as child of player
2. Add Camera3D as child of SpringArm3D
3. Set SpringArm length to 4–6 units and rotate X -20°
4. Set Camera3D current and wire mouse look with clamped vertical rotation
Why SpringArm3D helps
It shortens or repositions the camera when obstacles lie between the player and the desired camera position, reducing clipping into geometry and maintaining visibility of the player.
Concrete test
Add static bodies around the player and walk into them. Observe the SpringArm shorten smoothly rather than letting the camera pass through walls.
Key Takeaway

SpringArm3D simplifies third-person framing and reduces clipping, but tune length and collision behavior for tight spaces.
