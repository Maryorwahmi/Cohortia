Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Projection: Perspective vs Orthogonal
Perspective
Simulates human vision: distant objects shrink, lines converge, and scenes feel immersive.
Example
Use perspective for first-person or third-person action to communicate depth and movement speed.
Misconception
Higher field of view always improves gameplay. In fact, increasing fov can distort the scene and change perceived speed, so tune to your design goals.
Orthogonal
Renders without perspective distortion: size remains constant with distance and parallel lines stay parallel.
Example
Use orthogonal for isometric strategy or architectural views where consistent scale and grid alignment are important.
Design note
Without perspective depth cues, rely on layering, shadows, and parallax to convey depth information to players.
