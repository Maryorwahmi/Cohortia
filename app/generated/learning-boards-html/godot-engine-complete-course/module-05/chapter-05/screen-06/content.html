Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › fov & clip planes
Field of View (fov)
Angular extent visible to a perspective camera. Larger fov widens view and can exaggerate motion; smaller fov zooms in.
Example: A racing game may use a wider fov to emphasize speed.
Misconception: Wider fov always improves awareness — it can also distort objects and make aiming harder.
Near & Far clip planes
Define the minimum and maximum distances for rendered geometry. Objects outside this range are not drawn.
Example: Set near small enough to show weapon models, and far to just beyond visible environment.
Common mistake: setting near too large causing nearby objects to disappear.
Key Takeaway

Adjust fov for game feel and clip planes for correctness and performance. Balance precision and cost.
