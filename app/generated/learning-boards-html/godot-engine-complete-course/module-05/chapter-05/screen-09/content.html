Module 6 › Chapter 5.5 › Fixed Cameras
When to use fixed cameras
Fixed cameras provide stable composition and can clarify spatial relationships in puzzles or create cinematic angles for storytelling.
Example
A platforming arena with a high fixed camera gives players full visibility of obstacles and jump distances.
Transition advice
Avoid sudden camera cuts. Use eased motion, fades, or guided camera moves so the player retains orientation.
Common mistake
Relying on fixed cameras for all scenes removes player agency and can hide important interactive elements.
Key Takeaway

Use fixed cameras for clarity and drama, but combine with interactive views where player control matters.
