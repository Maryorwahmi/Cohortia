Comparison

Trusted vs Untrusted

Trusted (allowed relay)
127.0.0.0/8 [::1]/128 192.168.1.0/24 — clients here may relay to any destination.
Untrusted (blocked relay)
Any client not listed in mynetworks will be subject to recipient restrictions and typically rejected for external destinations.
# /etc/postfix/main.cf mynetworks = 127.0.0.0/8 [::1]/128 192.168.1.0/24
Pitfall example
Adding an overly broad range such as 0.0.0.0/0 results in an open relay and immediate abuse, often ending in blacklisting. Always prefer the narrowest range that meets operational needs.
Testing strategy
# internal test
echo "Test" | mail -s "Internal Relay Test" external@example.com
# external test should be blocked from untrusted host
Pro Tip

When in doubt, remove a range and re-add it narrowly after verifying which hosts need relay access.
