Assessment & Lab

Final checklist

  • Set myhostname and mydomain and verify with postconf -n.
  • Configure inet_interfaces and inet_protocols to match your network plan.
  • Define a minimal mynetworks and set smtpd_recipient_restrictions appropriately.
  • Ensure MX and A records point to your mail host and verify using dig.
  • Reload Postfix, run internal and external relay tests, and inspect logs.
Assessment question
If mynetworks = 127.0.0.0/8 and an internal client 192.168.1.50 tries to send mail to externaluser@anotherdomain.com, will it relay?
Answer: No. 192.168.1.50 is not in mynetworks, so permit_mynetworks won't apply and reject_unauth_destination will block relay.
Key Takeaway

Use the assessment to confirm you understand how mynetworks and recipient restrictions interact; test to prove your conclusion.
