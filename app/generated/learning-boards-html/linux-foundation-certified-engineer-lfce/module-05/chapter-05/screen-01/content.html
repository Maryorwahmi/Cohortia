Integrating Postfix with Dovecot
Make local delivery reliable: use Dovecot’s LDA for Maildir placement and virtual user support.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
What you will do
Configure mailbox_command, test delivery to Maildir, and set up simple virtual aliases.
Why it matters
Correct handoff prevents lost mail, keeps IMAP/POP access consistent, and enables scalable virtual hosting.
In this lesson
Step-by-step edits to Dovecot LDA and Postfix main.cf, plus commands to test delivery and check logs.
Coming up
Virtual alias mapping and postmap workflow for alias management.
Delivery Flow
Postfix accepts SMTP
Receives incoming mail for your domain
Dovecot LDA delivers
Deliver or dovecot-lda writes to Maildir or virtual mailbox
Tip: confirm the path to dovecot-lda on your distribution before editing mailbox_command.
