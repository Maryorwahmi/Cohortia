Security & Practical Checks

Risk: Open Relay

An open relay allows anyone to send mail through your server. Learn how to confirm relaying is restricted to trusted clients only.

Ports to consider

Understand common ports for mail: 25 for SMTP, 587 for submission, and 465 for SMTPS, and why firewall rules should limit access.

DNS and Hostname

Verify that your server’s hostname resolves and that an MX record points to the mail host before expecting reliable delivery.

Postconf habit

Use postconf -n to list only non-default settings after edits so you can audit what you intentionally changed.
