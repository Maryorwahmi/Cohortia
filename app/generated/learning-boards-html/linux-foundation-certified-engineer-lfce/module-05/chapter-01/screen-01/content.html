Introduction to MTAs & MDAs

Understand how mail flows across servers and into local mailboxes, and learn the basic Postfix checks you’ll use in initial setup and troubleshooting.

Concept map
MTAs route mail by DNS MX → MDAs deliver locally → MUAs present to users
MTA (Postfix)
Routes mail, queues undelivered messages, consults MX records
MDA (Dovecot)
Places messages in local mailboxes and provides IMAP/POP3 access
MUA (Thunderbird)
Composes and reads mail; talks to MTA for send and MDA for retrieval
