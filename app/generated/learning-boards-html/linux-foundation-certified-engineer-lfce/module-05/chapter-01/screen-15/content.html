Confirm DNS
Ensure myhostname and MX records resolve correctly before testing mail delivery.
Audit Config
Run postconf -n to see the effective non-default settings and confirm your edits are active.
Harden Relay & Ports
Restrict relaying, open only required ports, and apply least-privilege for processes and users.
Final Thought

A small set of deliberate checks—DNS, postconf, and relay controls—prevents most initial deployment issues with mail servers.
