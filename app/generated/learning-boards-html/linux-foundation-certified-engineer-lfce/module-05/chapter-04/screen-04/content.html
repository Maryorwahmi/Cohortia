What is Dovecot and the MDA role?
Understand why Dovecot, not the MTA, manages user mailboxes and client access.
Definition and responsibilities
  • Accept local delivery from the MTA and place messages into mailboxes.
  • Serve mail to clients via IMAP and POP3 protocols.
  • Manage indexing, mailbox format, and access permissions.
Analogy
Think of Postfix as the postal service moving letters between offices; Dovecot is the local post office that stores mail for recipients and lets them retrieve it securely.
Example scenario
An incoming email is routed by Postfix to the local delivery agent. Dovecot stores it in the user's Maildir and allows the user's IMAP client to mark it read and move it to a folder. Those changes are visible from other clients because Dovecot manages server-side state.
Common misconception
Do not assume the MTA provides mailbox browsing. The MTA delivers; the MDA stores and serves mail to MUAs. Confusing these leads to configuration errors and broken client access.
Key Takeaway

Dovecot is the mailbox authority; configure it for storage format, access protocols, and authentication to ensure reliable client access.
