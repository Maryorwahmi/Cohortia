Dovecot configuration files
Locate settings quickly by editing the appropriate 10-*.conf file.
Key files
  • /etc/dovecot/dovecot.conf (main include)
  • /etc/dovecot/conf.d/10-mail.conf (mail_location)
  • /etc/dovecot/conf.d/10-auth.conf (authentication)
  • /etc/dovecot/conf.d/10-master.conf (listeners/processes)
Why modularity helps
Modular files let you change a single responsibility without touching unrelated settings. For example, set mail_location in 10-mail.conf and authentication methods in 10-auth.conf. This reduces the blast radius of edits and simplifies audits and rollbacks.
Practical tip
Use grep to confirm your change: grep -E "mail_location|disable_plaintext_auth|auth_mechanisms" /etc/dovecot/conf.d/*
