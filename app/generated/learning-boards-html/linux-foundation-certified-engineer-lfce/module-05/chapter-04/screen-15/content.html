Compact Review
Three quick checks before you begin the lab.
Check Dovecot
sudo systemctl status dovecot — ensure active before editing configs.
Set Maildir
Configure mail_location = maildir:~/Maildir and reload Dovecot.
Auth & TLS
Use PAM in lab, but enforce TLS and disable plaintext auth in production.
Final takeaway

Follow the lab steps in order: install, configure Maildir, enable auth for testing, open ports, then verify with a test user.
