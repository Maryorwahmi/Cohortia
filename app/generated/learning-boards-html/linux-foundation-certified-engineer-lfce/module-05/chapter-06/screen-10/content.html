Module 05 › Chapter 5.6 › Policy Decisions
Virus actions (recommended)
Set $final_virus_lovers to D_REJECT to refuse infected messages at SMTP time. This prevents harmful files from ever reaching mailboxes or quarantine storage that might be accidentally reintroduced.
Spam actions (options)
Lower scores: tag headers and deliver with notice. Medium scores: quarantine for admin review. High scores: discard or reject depending on tolerance for false positives.
Risks
  • Aggressive thresholds produce false positives and user complaints.
  • Overuse of whitelists can allow malicious mail through.
  • Poor quarantine notification confuses administrators.
Key takeaway
Document and test your policy. Use GTUBE and EICAR to verify actions, and ensure logs show the intended disposition.
