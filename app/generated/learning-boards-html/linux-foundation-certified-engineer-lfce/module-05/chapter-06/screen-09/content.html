GTUBE spam test
echo "X5O!P%@AP[4\\PZX54(P^)7CC)7}$EICAR-STANDARD-ANTIVIRUS-TEST-FILE!$H+H*" | mail -s "GTUBE Test" mailuser1@your_domain.com
This should trigger SpamAssassin and either tag or quarantine the message depending on your amavisd.conf settings.
EICAR virus test
echo 'X5O!P%@AP[4\\PZX54(P^)7CC)7}$EICAR-STANDARD-ANTIVIRUS-TEST-FILE!$H+H*' > eicar.com
# send with mutt or your mail client attaching eicar.com
echo "EICAR test" | mutt -s "EICAR Test" -a eicar.com -- mailuser1@your_domain.com
Inspect logs: sudo journalctl -u amavisd or tail -n 200 /var/log/maillog to find ClamAV detections and the resulting action.
What to look for in logs
  • X-Spam-Flag or X-Spam-Score headers added by Amavisd-new/SpamAssassin.
  • ClamAV FOUND lines appearing and amavisd actions such as quarantine or reject.
  • Connection and socket errors that indicate misconfiguration.
