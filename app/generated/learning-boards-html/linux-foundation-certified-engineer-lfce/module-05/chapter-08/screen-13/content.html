Definition

SPF, DKIM, DMARC explained

  • SPF — DNS TXT listing authorized sending hosts for a domain.
  • DKIM — cryptographic signing of messages plus a DNS-published public key selector.
  • DMARC — a policy combining SPF and DKIM results with handling instructions and reporting.
Checks
dig TXT example.com
dig MX example.com
# For DKIM, check selector._domainkey.example.com
dig TXT selector._domainkey.example.com
Common misconception: assuming MX and A are sufficient. Missing SPF/DKIM/DMARC often causes downstream filtering even when SMTP succeeds.
Key Takeaway

DNS-based authentication records are critical for mail reputation and acceptance; include them in any delivery checklist.
