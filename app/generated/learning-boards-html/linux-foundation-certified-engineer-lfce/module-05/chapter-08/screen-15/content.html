Correlate logs

Minimal changes first
Edit configuration only after evidence points to the specific setting to change.
Verify filters & services
Ensure amavisd, SpamAssassin, ClamAV, and their transports are active and updated.
Incident checklist: capture queue ID, tail relevant journals, reproduce with telnet/openssl, inspect queue and config, verify DNS and filtering services, then apply a targeted fix and re-test.
