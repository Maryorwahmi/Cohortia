Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › TLS Policy Choices: may vs encrypt
'may' — permissive
Offers TLS but allows fallback to plaintext. Use when you need broad compatibility during rollout.
When to use
Staged deployments and mixed peer support.
'encrypt' — strict
Rejects connections that cannot negotiate TLS. Suitable when confidentiality is mandatory and peers support TLS.
When to use
Closed environments or after careful compatibility testing.
Deployment tip
Start with 'may', collect metrics on non-TLS peers, then selectively apply 'encrypt' once you confirm peer support to avoid delivery failures.
