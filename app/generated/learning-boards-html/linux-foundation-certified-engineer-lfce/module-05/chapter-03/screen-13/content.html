Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Self-signed vs CA-signed Certificates
Self-signed
Quick to create, useful for testing or internal services. Not trusted by external peers unless explicitly added to trust stores.
Use case
Internal labs and isolated environments.
CA-signed
Trusted by clients and remote MTAs. Required for public-facing mail servers to avoid trust warnings and rejections.
Use case
Production servers and cross-organization mail exchange.
Hostname requirement
Regardless of signer, the certificate must include the server's FQDN as the Common Name or Subject Alternative Name to avoid hostname mismatches.
