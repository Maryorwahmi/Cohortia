UID / GID
Numeric identifiers used by the kernel for ownership and access control.
Primary vs Secondary
Primary group sets default ownership; secondary groups enable shared access.
useradd flags
Use -r, -s, -d, -c, and -G to match account purpose and security requirements.
Summary Takeaway

Think numerically, act deliberately, and always verify: consistent IDs, correct shells, and disciplined lifecycle management keep systems secure and predictable.
