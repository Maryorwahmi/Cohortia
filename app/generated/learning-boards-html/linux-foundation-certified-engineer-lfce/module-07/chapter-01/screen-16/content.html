Verify
Use id, grep, and chage to confirm attributes and aging without guessing.
Use tools
Prefer useradd/usermod/gpasswd to manual edits to avoid inconsistencies.
Stage deletions
Lock, locate external files by UID, reassign or archive, then delete the account.
Final Thought

Small habits—verification, documented UID policies, and staged retirements—scale to reliable, secure administration across many systems.
