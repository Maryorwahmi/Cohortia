Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Mistakes & Best Practices
Top mistakes
  • Not aligning UIDs/GIDs across hosts serving shared storage.
  • Giving service accounts interactive shells or forgetting -r.
  • Deleting users without reassigning files outside home.
  • Using usermod -G without -a and unintentionally removing memberships.
Best practices
  • Document critical service UIDs/GIDs and sync them across systems.
  • Use /sbin/nologin and system UID ranges for non-interactive accounts.
  • Stage user retirement: lock, reassign/archive files, then delete.
  • Prefer useradd/usermod/gpasswd over manual file edits; verify with id and grep.
Key Takeaway

Policies and verification are your strongest tools. Prevent issues by documenting and confirming identity mappings and account attributes.
