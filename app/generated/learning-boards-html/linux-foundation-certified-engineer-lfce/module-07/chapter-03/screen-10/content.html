pam_faillock Example
Recommended additions
# In /etc/pam.d/system-auth (or equivalent)
auth    required      pam_faillock.so preauth audit deny=3 unlock_time=600
auth    sufficient    pam_unix.so try_first_pass
auth    [default=die] pam_faillock.so authfail audit deny=3 unlock_time=600

account required      pam_faillock.so
Why this ordering works
Preauth logs attempts before verification, authfail logs failures after verification, and the account line enforces the lock based on recorded counters.
Misconception
Failing to add faillock to account leaves a recorded failure counter without enforcement in some flows.
Test step
After editing, try three failed logins and run sudo faillock --user testuser to confirm lock state before restarting services.
Key Takeaway
Record failures, enforce locks, and verify with faillock to ensure the desired protection is in place.
