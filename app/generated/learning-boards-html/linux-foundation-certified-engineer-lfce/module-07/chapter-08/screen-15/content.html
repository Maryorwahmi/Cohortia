Module 8 › Chapter 7.8 › Key Concepts
auditd
A kernel-level framework producing structured, security-focused event records for forensics and compliance.
Targeted Rules
Use file, syscall, exe, and UID filters and tag rules with keys to reduce noise and make searches efficient.
Query & Summarize
Use ausearch for detailed events and aureport for quick summaries during triage.
Summary
Combine auditd data with system logs, protect and rotate logs, and centralize archives to maintain integrity and availability for investigations.
