Operational Notes
Protections and common mistakes
Tune rules
Prefer executable, UID, or syscall filters to avoid excessive logging.
Protect logs
Set strict permissions on /var/log/audit and ensure auditd starts at boot.
Centralize
Ship logs to a SIEM or remote store to prevent local tampering and preserve long-term evidence.
Other critical points: configure NTP so timestamps are reliable, test postrotate hooks to ensure loggers reopen files, and monitor disk usage to avoid lost logs from full partitions. Avoid a false confidence in default rules; validate coverage against realistic threat actions and tune accordingly.
Pro Tip

Start with a small set of high-value rules, verify they produce useful events, then expand coverage iteratively while monitoring volume and disk impact.
