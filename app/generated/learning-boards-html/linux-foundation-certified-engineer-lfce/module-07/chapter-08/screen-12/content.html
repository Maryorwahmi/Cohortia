Sample logrotate entries
Two examples: auth logs and audit logs rotation blocks.
Auth log rotation
/var/log/auth.log /var/log/secure {
    weekly
    rotate 4
    compress
    delaycompress
    missingok
    notifempty
    create 0640 root adm
    postrotate
        /usr/lib/rsyslog/rsyslog-rotate
    endscript
}
Rotates weekly and keeps four compressed backups.
Audit log rotation
/var/log/audit/audit.log {
    monthly
    rotate 12
    compress
    delaycompress
    missingok
    notifempty
    postrotate
        /usr/sbin/augenrules --load >/dev/null 2>&1 || :
    endscript
}
Monthly rotation with a year of archives for longer-term retention.
Force rotation to test: sudo logrotate -f /etc/logrotate.d/authlog and then list rotated files to confirm creation and permissions.
