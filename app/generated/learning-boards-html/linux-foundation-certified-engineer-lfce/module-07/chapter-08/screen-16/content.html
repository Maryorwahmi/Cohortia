Final Checklist
Enable & Protect
Ensure auditd starts at boot and protect /var/log/audit permissions.
Rotate & Archive
Configure logrotate and test postrotate hooks to reload logging services.
Centralize
Ship logs to a SIEM or remote archive for long-term retention and tamper resistance.
One-liner takeaway

Start small with high-value rules, verify they produce useful events, and then expand coverage while protecting and centralizing logs.
