Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Manual & Inactivity Locks
Manual lock: passwd -l / -u
sudo passwd -l testuser   # lock
sudo passwd -u testuser   # unlock
passwd -l sets a '!' in /etc/shadow and is quick to apply and revert. It is ideal for immediate suspensions when you want to avoid deletion.
Scheduled expiration: chage -E / inactivity -I
sudo chage -E 2025-01-01 temp_user
sudo chage -I 30 john_doe
chage -E sets an auditable expiration date; chage -I controls how many days after expiry the account becomes inactive.
Dormant account strategy
  • Detect inactivity with lastlog or chage -l reports.
  • Decide between immediate lock or scheduled expiry based on business needs.
  • Record lock rationale and reactivation process for audits.
