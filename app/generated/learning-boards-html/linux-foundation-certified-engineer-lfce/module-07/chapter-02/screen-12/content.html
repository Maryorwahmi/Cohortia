Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
rwx → octal
Read=4, Write=2, Execute=1; sum per principal to form modes like 644 and 755.
umask
Masks bits from 666 (files) or 777 (dirs). 0022 → 644/755. 0002 → 664/775 for shared groups.
Special bits
SUID for owner identity on exec, SGID for group inheritance on dirs, sticky to protect user-owned files in shared dirs.
ACLs
Named user/group entries and a mask that limits their effective permissions; default ACLs for inheritance.
Common mistakes
Avoid 777/666, careless SUID, forgetting umask, and mask confusion when using ACLs.
Verification
Use ls -l, getfacl, and sudo -u to inspect and test permissions as other users.
Summary takeaway
Precision, verification, and least-privilege: combine umask, special bits, and ACLs to meet team needs without exposing risk.
