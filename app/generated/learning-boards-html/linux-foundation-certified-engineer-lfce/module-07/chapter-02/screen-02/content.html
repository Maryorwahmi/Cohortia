Learning Objectives

Master practical permissions, defaults, and ACL tooling for secure file-system access.

Learn
Understand
Apply
Master
01
Deepen rwx and special bits
Understand owner, group, others and SUID, SGID, sticky behavior for both files and directories.
Mnemonic: Think "identity, inheritance, and protection" — who runs it, who inherits, who can delete.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain and calculate umask
Compute resulting modes from a umask like 0022 or 0002 for files and directories and choose safe defaults for shared systems.
Mnemonic: mask subtracts from max — 666 for files, 777 for directories.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Master ACLs with getfacl/setfacl
Create, inspect, and remove ACL entries and default ACLs so specific users and groups get the access they need.
Mnemonic: ACL = named user/group rules plus a mask that caps them.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify mistakes and apply best practices
Avoid overly permissive modes, misuse of SUID, mask confusion, and forgetting default ACLs on shared directories.
Mnemonic: Test access from other users to validate settings.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
