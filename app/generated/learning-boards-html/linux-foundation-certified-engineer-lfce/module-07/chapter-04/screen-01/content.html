Centralized Authentication with LDAP and Kerberos
Connect Linux clients to enterprise identity services using SSSD and Kerberos for secure, repeatable authentication.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson
What you will configure and why it matters
  • Use SSSD to proxy NSS and PAM to remote LDAP and Kerberos providers and cache credentials for offline use.
  • Configure krb5.conf and obtain TGTs with kinit to avoid sending passwords over the network.
  • Validate integration with id, getent passwd, klist, and system logs, then enable SSSD via authselect to adjust NSS and PAM.
LDAP
Directory of users and groups. Stores UIDs, shells, and home directories.
Kerberos
Ticket-based authentication via a KDC. Prevents password replay.
SSSD
Local proxy and cache for identity and auth services.
