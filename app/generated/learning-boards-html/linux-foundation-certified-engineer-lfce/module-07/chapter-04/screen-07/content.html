How authentication works
  1. Client authenticates with KDC and receives a TGT after verifying credentials.
  2. Client uses TGT to request service tickets from the Ticket Granting Service.
  3. Client presents service tickets to network services to authenticate without sending the password.
Analogy
Think of the KDC as a ticket booth and the TGT as a season pass that lets you obtain single-entry tickets for multiple attractions without re-proving who you are.
Testing Kerberos
kinit: obtain a TGT for a principal, for example kinit kerberosuser@EXAMPLE.COM.
klist: list current tickets and their expiry.
Common-mistake: If you get "Cannot contact any KDC for realm", verify time, DNS, and KDC entries in krb5.conf.
Key Takeaway

Kerberos issues time-limited tickets via a KDC so passwords are not repeatedly transmitted, but proper DNS and clock sync are essential.
