What to Remember
Short operational checklist for deployments and fixes.
Operational best-practices
Protect secrets
Set mode 0600 on sssd.conf and avoid plaintext tokens in production.
Enable SSSD
Use authselect or pam-auth-update so NSS and PAM consult SSSD for lookups and auth.
Check time & DNS
Kerberos requires clock sync; DNS issues frequently block discovery of KDCs or LDAP servers.
Final take

Use precise configuration, small reproducible tests, and SSSD/ system logs to make LDAP and Kerberos integrations reliable and maintainable.
