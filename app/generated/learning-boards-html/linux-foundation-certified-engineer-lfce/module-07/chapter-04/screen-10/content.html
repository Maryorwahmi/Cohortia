Symptoms vs Likely causes
Cannot contact LDAP/KDC
Check network ports, firewalls, and DNS resolution with nc or telnet and dig/nslookup.
Lookups return nothing
Validate ldap_search_base and schema; ensure sssd.conf has correct domain and id_provider settings.
Authentication fails but lookups succeed
Check krb5 realm and KDC entries, time synchronization, and auth_provider selection in SSSD.
Direct checks and commands
  • Network: nc -vz ldap.example.com 389 or nc -vz kdc.example.com 88
  • DNS: dig kdc.example.com and dig SRV _kerberos._tcp.EXAMPLE.COM
  • Time: sudo systemctl status chronyd or ntpd and compare date output.
  • Logs: tail /var/log/sssd/sssd_example.com.log and journalctl -u sssd
Pro tip
Reproducing the failure with id, getent, kinit, and klist lets you gather precise error messages to feed into logs or admin requests.
