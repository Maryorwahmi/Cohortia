Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Key Concepts
Performance Baseline
Representative historical metrics used as the reference for future comparisons.
Bottleneck Types
CPU, memory, disk I/O, and network each have distinct symptoms and remedies.
Methodical Troubleshooting
Observe, form a hypothesis, change one variable, and verify results against the baseline.
Summary takeaway
Use data, not guesswork. Baselines and disciplined change management turn incidents into opportunities for predictable improvement.
