Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Common mistakes and documentation
Avoid these pitfalls
Making multiple simultaneous changes, lacking a baseline, and poor notes are primary sources of repeated incidents.
Error: multiple changes
You won’t know which action fixed or worsened performance when changes are bundled.
Error: no baseline
Without historical context, current metrics lack meaning and comparisons fail.
Documenting your fix
  • Record the observed metric values and timestamp.
  • Note the hypothesis and the single change you applied.
  • Capture the after metrics and whether the change helped.
Key Takeaway

Good notes and one-change-at-a-time testing let you evaluate effectiveness and hand a clear remediation plan to others.
