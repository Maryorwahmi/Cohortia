Foundations of Linux Performance Analysis
A practical, data-first approach to identifying bottlenecks, building baselines, and choosing the right fix for CPU, memory, disk I/O, and network issues.
In this lesson
Learn to identify resource saturation, collect baseline metrics, and follow a methodical troubleshooting flow that avoids guesswork.
Coming up
Hands-on baseline steps and practical examples of CPU-bound, memory-bound, disk-bound and network-bound symptoms.

CPU
High utilization and runaway processes often point to CPU-bound issues requiring profiling.
I/O
Slow reads/writes and high wait times cause disk-bound performance loss impacting responsiveness.
Baseline
Collect normal metrics so deviations are meaningful when troubleshooting later.
