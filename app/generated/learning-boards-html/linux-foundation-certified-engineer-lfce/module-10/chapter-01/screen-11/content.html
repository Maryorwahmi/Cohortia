Module 10 › Chapter 10.1 › Troubleshooting strategy
Top-down vs Bottom-up
Choose top-down when users report a problem; choose bottom-up when metrics show anomalies without direct reports.
Observe
Collect current metrics and symptoms.
Hypothesize
Form a single explanation tying symptom to resource.
Test
Change one variable and measure.
Documentation and validation
Log each change, the expected effect, and capture before/after baseline comparisons so you can undo or iterate confidently.
Common mistakes
Making multiple changes at once, relying on intuition without data, or skipping baseline comparison turn fixes into guesswork.
Key Takeaway

Use a repeatable observe-hypothesize-change-verify loop, change one thing at a time, and document outcomes for reliable troubleshooting.
