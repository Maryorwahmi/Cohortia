Common mistakes
Long traces in production
They add overhead and may mask or create timing issues. Use short sessions or staging.
Frequency ≠ cost
A function called often may still be cheap; check inclusive time before optimization.
No debug symbols
perf without symbols yields addresses only and is difficult to interpret.
Best practice
Triangulate with multiple tools: strace/ltrace for clues, perf to confirm CPU hotspots, and reproduce in staging when possible.
Check your understanding
After profiling, can you explain why the resource was used and which change will reduce that use?
