Key Concepts

Three compact reminders to take into your troubleshooting toolkit.

Cache is normal
High buff/cache usually speeds the system; it is reclaimable when needed.
Watch si/so
Sustained swap-in/swap-out signals swapping and possible thrashing.
Use combined tools
Combine free, vmstat and sar -r plus top to make confident decisions.
Summary

Measure first, confirm swapping with si/so, locate the process, and apply reversible fixes before committing to capacity changes.
