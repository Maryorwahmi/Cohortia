What to Remember

A compact cheat sheet for incident response and routine checks.

Cache vs Free
High buff/cache is expected; check available to know usable memory.
Swap indicators
Sustained si/so values with rising swpd indicate swapping and performance risk.
Toolchain
Free for snapshots, vmstat for live traces, sar -r for history, top to find processes.
Final Tip

Measure then act: gather free/vmstat/sar data, confirm swapping, and apply reversible mitigations first.
