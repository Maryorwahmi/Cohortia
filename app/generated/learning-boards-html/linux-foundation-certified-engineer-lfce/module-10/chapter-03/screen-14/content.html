Best Practice

Incident triage timeline

0–5 min
Run free -h and vmstat 1 10 to capture immediate state.
5–15 min
Use top/htop to locate high RES processes and observe growth.
15–30 min
Apply reversible fixes: restart safe services, adjust swappiness temporarily.
30–60 min
Plan follow-up investigations: profiling, log analysis, or capacity changes.
This timeline helps preserve evidence, minimize impact, and gives you measurable checkpoints to evaluate whether an action improved memory behavior.
Key Takeaway

Follow a short, observable triage sequence before committing to disruptive or permanent changes.
