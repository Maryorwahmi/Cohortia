Advanced System Monitoring with sysstat and atop
Historical trends plus detailed process deltas let you find intermittent and root-cause issues faster.
In this lesson
Commands, logging configuration, and a reproducible investigative workflow combining sar, pidstat, and atop.
Why it matters
Historical logs let you analyze incidents you missed; process-level deltas reveal the real actor that produced the spike.
Explore Key Topics
Sysstat & sar
Collects periodic snapshots and stores them for later analysis.
pidstat
Per-process CPU, memory and I/O that pinpoints offenders.
atop
Real-time deltas and replayable logs for detailed post-mortem.
Visual Concept
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Combined workflow
1. sar
Historic trends, daily binary files, useful for spotting when a spike occurred across CPU, memory, disk, and network.
2. atop
Real-time deltas and process I/O in one view. Replay logs to step through time and see which process produced the spike.
