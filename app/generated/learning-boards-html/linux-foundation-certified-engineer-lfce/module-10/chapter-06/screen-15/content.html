Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Remember
Verify
Confirm sysstat and atop logging are active and files are written.
Correlate
Use sar for when, atop and pidstat for who, then correlate timestamps across outputs.
Interval
Choose an interval that captures bursts without creating excessive logs; ten minutes is a common starting point.
Final takeaway

Combining historical records with process-level deltas turns intermittent problems into resolvable incidents.
