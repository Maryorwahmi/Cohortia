Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Troubleshooting
Mistake: No logging
Verify cron/timer or systemd service, and force a sample to confirm file creation before relying on data for incident analysis.
Mistake: Misreading averages
Averages can hide short spikes. Use an appropriate collection interval and inspect consecutive samples for bursts.
Mistake: Missing replay files
Check LOGPATH, service status, and permissions if atop -r cannot find expected log files.
Key Takeaway

Validate collection and retention policies, choose intervals that capture bursts, and use pidstat and atop to attribute spikes accurately.
