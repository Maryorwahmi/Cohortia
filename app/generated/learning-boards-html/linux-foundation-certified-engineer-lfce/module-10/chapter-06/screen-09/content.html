Replay commands
atop -r /var/log/atop/atop_$(date +%Y%m%d)
# In replay: use t/T to move forward/back and c/m/d/n to sort
During replay, look for the interval where a metric spikes, then sort processes to see which produced the activity. Record timestamps and process names for correlation with sar logs.
Example forensic step
  1. Replay the log and jump to the spike interval.
  2. Sort by d to surface heavy disk writers at that moment.
  3. Note PID and process name, and cross-check with sar for system-level context.
