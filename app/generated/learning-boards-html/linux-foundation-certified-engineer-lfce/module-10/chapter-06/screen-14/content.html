Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Key Concepts
sysstat / sar
Periodic binary samples for long-term trend analysis.
pidstat
Per-process samples for CPU, memory and I/O across intervals.
atop
Real-time deltas and replayable logs to attribute spikes to processes.
Takeaway
Enable logging, choose intervals wisely, and use sar for when and atop/pidstat for who. Correlate outputs to build a precise incident narrative.
