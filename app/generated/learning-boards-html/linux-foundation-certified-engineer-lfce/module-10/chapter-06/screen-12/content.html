Module 10 › Chapter 10.6 › Lab: Simulate & Analyze
Quick command checklist
sudo /usr/lib/sysstat/sa1      # force sysstat sample
sudo systemctl enable --now atop
# generate a short CPU load
stress --cpu 2 --timeout 60s &
Analyze sar
After the interval, run sar -u -f /var/log/sysstat/sa$(date +%d) to find the time of the spike. Note the timestamp for deeper examination.
Replay atop
Use atop -r /var/log/atop/atop_$(date +%Y%m%d) and jump to that timestamp. Sort by the metric to reveal the offending process.
Confirm with pidstat
Run pidstat -u -d 1 10 while reproducing the load to confirm the process keeps consuming resources across samples.
Pro tip

Align collection intervals between sysstat and atop when possible to make timeline correlation simpler.
