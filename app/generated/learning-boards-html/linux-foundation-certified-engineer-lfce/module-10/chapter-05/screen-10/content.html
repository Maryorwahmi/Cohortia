Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Bottlenecks
Insufficient Bandwidth
Sustained high utilization reported by sar or iftop suggests the link cannot carry demand.
High Latency
Measured by ping/traceroute; affects responsiveness even with healthy throughput.
Packet Loss
Causes retransmits and reduced effective throughput; visible in ping and transfer retries.
Interface Errors
RX/TX errors indicate physical or driver problems; check ip -s link and ethtool.
Firewall/Security
Misconfigured rules can block, delay, or drop legitimate traffic; verify port rules and logs.
Protocol/Config
TCP window sizes and NIC offload settings can limit throughput on particular paths.
Key Takeaway

Collect evidence from multiple tools to determine whether the bottleneck is physical, network-path, or protocol/configuration related before applying fixes.
