Module 10 › Chapter 10.5 › Metrics
Definitions
Bandwidth
Maximum theoretical data rate of a link, e.g., 1 Gbps.
Throughput
Actual data rate observed during transfers; affected by overhead and loss.
Latency
Time delay for packets; high latency degrades responsiveness.
Packet loss
Packets not delivered; causes retransmits, higher latency, and lower throughput.
Analogy
Think of bandwidth as the width of a pipe, throughput as the water you get out, latency as the delay before water arrives, and packet loss as holes in the pipe that leak water.
Example & Misconception
Concrete example
# Link advertises 1 Gbps, iperf3 shows 200 Mbps
iperf3 -c 10.0.0.2 -t 10
If iperf3 shows 200 Mbps despite a 1 Gbps NIC, investigate loss, latency, and device errors rather than assuming the NIC is broken.
Key Takeaway

Measure throughput and counters to confirm whether a perceived capacity issue is actually bandwidth, loss, or latency-driven.
