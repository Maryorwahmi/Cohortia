Bandwidth vs Throughput
Advertised capacity vs observed rate during transfers.
Latency & Packet Loss
Latency affects interactivity; loss triggers retransmits and reduces throughput.
Inspect & Test
Use ss/netstat/ip for state, sar/iftop for monitoring, ping/traceroute and iperf3 for tests.
Summary

Use the right measurement for the symptom, collect multiple samples, and correlate findings across tools before changing configuration.
