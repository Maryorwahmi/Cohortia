Module 10 › Chapter 10.9 › Kernel & Network
Socket backlog
net.core.somaxconn and net.ipv4.tcp_max_syn_backlog increase the kernel's TCP accept backlog to handle bursts of new connections.
Network buffers
Tune net.core.rmem_max, net.core.wmem_max, and the tcp_rmem/tcp_wmem ranges for high-throughput links or large transfers.
Swappiness
vm.swappiness controls swap behavior. Lower values reduce swapping for database servers and help maintain low latency.
Key Takeaway
Global sysctls can relieve or create bottlenecks across services. Make conservative changes, test under load, and monitor the metrics most likely affected by the change.
