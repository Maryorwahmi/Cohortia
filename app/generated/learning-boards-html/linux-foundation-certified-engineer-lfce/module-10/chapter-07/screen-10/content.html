Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › somaxconn & SYN backlog
net.core.somaxconn
Maximum number of pending connections a listening socket can queue. Increase for busy servers to prevent connection refusals.
Practical example
Set a higher backlog for high-traffic web servers and ensure the application uses a sufficient accept backlog to consume queued connections.
tcp_max_syn_backlog
Controls how many SYN requests the kernel queues. Raising it can help during bursts of new connection attempts, including during SYN floods when paired with other protections.
Misconception

Raising backlog protects only against brief bursts; it does not replace proper DDoS mitigation or application-level rate limiting.
