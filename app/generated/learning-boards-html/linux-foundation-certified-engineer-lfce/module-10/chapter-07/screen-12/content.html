Module 10 › Chapter 10.7 › Prioritizing parameters
Web servers
Focus on somaxconn, tcp_max_syn_backlog, and the accept/listen backlog in the application. Verify accept rate and worker pool sizing.
Databases
Prioritize swappiness and dirty writeback ratios to shape I/O patterns and reduce latency for reads and writes.
High-concurrency services
Check scheduler settings and profiling access. Ensure kernel tuning aligns with application scheduling and thread models.
Workflow
  1. Identify the symptom and relevant subsystem.
  2. Tune the smallest, most relevant parameter set.
  3. Test under representative load and monitor.
  4. Document and persist only if validated.
