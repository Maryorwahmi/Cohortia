Kernel Tuning for Performance

Use sysctl and the /proc/sys interface to inspect and tune kernel parameters that affect memory, networking, and scheduling.

In this lesson

View, test, and persist kernel settings safely for real workloads.

Why it matters

Correct tuning prevents connection drops, reduces latency, and optimizes write patterns for databases and web servers.

Coming up

Examples for vm.swappiness, net.core.somaxconn, and persistent sysctl configuration.
