Module 11 › Chapter 10.10 › Key Concepts
Proactive Monitoring
Collect live and historical metrics to spot slow degradations before they impact users.
Alerting
Set thresholds with sustained duration and route alerts to the right team with clear remediation steps.
Capacity Planning
Use trend analysis and peak-load modeling to choose between scaling up, scaling out, or optimizing resources.
Bottom line

Combine monitoring, alerts, and planning into a continuous cycle so systems remain stable and predictable as usage grows.
