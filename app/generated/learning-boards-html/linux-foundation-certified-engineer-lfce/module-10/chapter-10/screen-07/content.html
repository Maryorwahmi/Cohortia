Retention decisions
Choose retention based on how far ahead you plan: 3 months to 1 year are common. Use downsampling for older data to save space while preserving trends.
Three-month
Good for immediate trend detection and short-term capacity planning.
One-year
Helpful for seasonality and long-term projections.
Dashboard practices
Correlated panels
Overlay CPU and network to detect linked spikes and identify workload types.
Peak-load panels
Show daily/weekly peaks so you know required capacity during busiest times.
Contextual labels
Include host and service labels so panels can filter and compare similar targets.
Downsampling
Store high-resolution recent data and aggregated older data to balance cost and insight.
Key Takeaway

Retention plus correlated visualization turns raw metrics into actionable forecasts and root-cause signals.
