Module 11 › Chapter 10.10 › Assessment & Practice
Assessment scenario
CPU grows 5% monthly for six months and now hits 70% at peak. This indicates a capacity trend needing planning: project when you will hit 90% and decide between optimization, scaling up, or scaling out.
Immediate actions
Profile processes and check for inefficient workloads.
Planning
Project time to critical threshold and schedule capacity actions ahead of that date.
Avoid this
Don’t apply short-term fixes without updating metrics and alerts.
Key Takeaway

Translate trends into concrete plans: profile, model, then choose scaling or optimization before users are impacted.
