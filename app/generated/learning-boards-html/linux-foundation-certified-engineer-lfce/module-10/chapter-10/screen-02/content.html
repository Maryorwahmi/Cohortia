Mnemonic & visualization
Think of the cycle: Collect → Inspect → Alert → Plan. Visualize metrics over time, then convert trends into capacity actions. Small, repeatable checks keep the system healthy.
Venn: Alerts ∩ Trends ∩ Plans
When all three overlap you have proactive management rather than reactive firefighting.
Timeline: Retention windows
Short-term alerts and long-term retention together reveal seasonal patterns and growth rates.
