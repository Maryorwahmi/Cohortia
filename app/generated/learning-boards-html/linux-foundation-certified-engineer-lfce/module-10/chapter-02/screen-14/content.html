Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Key Concepts
CPU States
User, system, idle, and iowait map to application, kernel, idle capacity, and I/O respectively.
Per-core Analysis
mpstat reveals whether load is balanced or concentrated on a single core.
Historical Trends
sar shows whether CPU behavior is transient or recurring and helps plan remediation.
Summary
Use top/htop for live diagnosis, mpstat for per-core detail, and sar for historical context. Match the dominant CPU state to the subsystem you will investigate next.
