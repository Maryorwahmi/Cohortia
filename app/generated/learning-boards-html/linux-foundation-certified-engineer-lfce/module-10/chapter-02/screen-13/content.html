Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Interpret sample output
Sample
%Cpu(s):  5.0 us,  2.0 sy,  0.0 ni, 80.0 id, 13.0 wa,  0.0 hi,  0.0 si,  0.0 st
Interpretation: high iowait suggests a disk I/O bottleneck. Investigate disks and I/O-heavy processes before tuning CPU.
Decision guide
  • High wa → run iostat or sar -b to find disk hotspots.
  • High sy → consider strace or perf for kernel-level actions.
  • One core at 100% → use mpstat and profile that thread.
Key takeaway
Use the highest category as your starting hypothesis and test it with evidence-specific tools.
