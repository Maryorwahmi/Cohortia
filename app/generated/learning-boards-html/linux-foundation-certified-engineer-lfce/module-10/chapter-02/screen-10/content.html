Module 10 › Chapter 10.2 › Troubleshooting workflow
Steps
Observe
top / htop
Inspect
mpstat -P ALL
Trend
sar -u
Act
profile, tune, or scale
Example flow
Observe heavy iowait in top, confirm disk saturation with iostat, identify processes issuing I/O, then either tune I/O concurrency or move heavy jobs to off-peak hours.
Key takeaway
Follow the data. Actions without evidence can be disruptive and ineffective.
