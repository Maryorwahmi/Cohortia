Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Metric Comparison
Pattern A

High svctm, High %util

Device service time is long; device may be slow or overloaded. Consider faster media or look for hardware saturation.

Example: svctm 50ms, %util 95% — device itself is slow.
Pattern B

await ≫ svctm, High avgqu-sz

Requests spend significant time waiting in queue; many concurrent small I/O operations or dispatcher issues.

Example: await 40ms, svctm 10ms, avgqu-sz 20 — heavy queuing.
Misconception

Do not treat svctm alone as full latency. Always compare await to svctm to see queue contribution.
