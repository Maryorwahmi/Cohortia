Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Common Bottlenecks
Hardware

HDDs struggle with random I/O; SSDs/NVMe give better IOPS and lower svctm.

Workload

Many small random reads/writes overwhelm devices designed for throughput over IOPS.

Caching

Insufficient RAM reduces page cache effectiveness, increasing physical disk reads.

RAID & Filesystem

Wrong RAID level or stripe size and suboptimal mount options can degrade write performance.

Application behaviour

Excessive logging, unbatched writes, or inefficient queries can generate heavy I/O.

Scheduling

Backups or batch jobs scheduled during peak hours can push devices into contention.
