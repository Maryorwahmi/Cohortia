Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › sar -b / sar -d
Global stats
sar -b 1 5  # Global block device stats
sar -d 1 5  # Per-device stats

tps, rd_sec/s, wr_sec/s and avgrq-sz are useful to understand transfer counts and request sizes over time.

When to use

If an intermittent spike occurred outside a live window, check sar logs to confirm duration and devices affected before acting.

Example: use sar to compare overnight backups to daytime traffic to determine scheduling changes.
