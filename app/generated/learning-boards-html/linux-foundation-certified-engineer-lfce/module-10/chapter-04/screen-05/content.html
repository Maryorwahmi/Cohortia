Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › iostat -x Walkthrough
Command
iostat -x 1 5  # Extended stats, 1s interval, 5 samples
Key columns to watch:
  • %util: device busy percentage; sustained near 100% = saturation.
  • svctm: service time in ms for issued requests.
  • await: queue wait plus service time; use this for end-to-end latency.
  • avgqu-sz: average outstanding requests; large values mean queueing.
Interpretation
If await is much higher than svctm, requests are waiting in the queue. If svctm is high, the device service time itself is slow. Both combined with high %util point to urgent attention.
Key Takeaway

Use await for total latency and svctm to understand device service time; compare both to find queueing.
