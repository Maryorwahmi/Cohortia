Disk I/O Performance Monitoring and Analysis

Practical commands and metrics to detect saturation, identify processes, and begin mitigation.

In this lesson
Read iostat and sar, find processes with iotop, run a safe dd load, and triage.
Key metrics
%util, await, svctm, avgqu-sz, r/s, w/s, rkB/s, wkB/s.
When to act
Sustained %util near 100% or high await versus svctm means start investigating.
Coming up

Terminal demo and lab checklist

We’ll simulate load, watch live stats, and find the process responsible.

iostat
Device and per-device extended stats.
iotop
Per-process I/O usage in real time.
sar
Historical and per-device statistics.
Why this matters
I/O issues cause wait time that hides as slow applications; measuring accurately lets you fix the right layer.
Quick visual
%util: near 100% -> saturated device
await vs svctm: large gap -> queuing
iotop shows process-level bandwidth and I/O
