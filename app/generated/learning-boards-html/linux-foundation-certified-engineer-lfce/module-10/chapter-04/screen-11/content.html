Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Troubleshooting Workflow
Steps
  1. Measure: iostat -x, sar -b/-d.
  2. Attribute: sudo iotop to find process.
  3. Inspect: logs, configs, query plans.
  4. Mitigate: schedule changes, batching, mount options, or hardware.
Example
A backup runs and device %util hits 100%. iotop shows the backup process; reschedule to off-peak and re-measure to validate improvement.
Validation: confirm await decreases and avgqu-sz returns to normal after mitigation.
