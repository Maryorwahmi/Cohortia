Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Disk I/O-bound
Claim

Disk I/O limits throughput

When storage is the slowest component, processes wait and the OS reports iowait. This is the right signal to investigate device metrics.

Example
A database performing many small writes can saturate an HDD; even a lightly loaded CPU will show high iowait.
Common misconception
Low CPU usage means healthy system. Not always—iowait hides blocked progress caused by slow storage.
Key Takeaway

High iowait and device saturation point you to storage; measure with iostat before changing CPU or memory settings.

Visual cues
Look for iowait in top or mpstat
Check %util and await with iostat -x
Use sar -b for global block stats
Pinpoint process with sudo iotop
