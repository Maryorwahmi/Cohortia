Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Key Concepts
Device metrics

%util, await, svctm, avgqu-sz reveal saturation and queueing.

Process attribution

iotop identifies the process causing high read/write bandwidth so you can inspect it.

Mitigation path

Try scheduling, mount options, scheduler changes, then hardware, always validating with metrics.

Summary Takeaway

Use iostat and sar to measure device health, iotop to find the process, then apply minimal mitigation and re-check metrics.
