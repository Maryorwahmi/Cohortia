Module 10 › Chapter 10.4 › Hardware & RAID
When to upgrade

Sustained high svctm and %util after tuning; legitimate application I/O that cannot be reduced by batching or scheduling.

RAID considerations

Choose RAID level and stripe size appropriate to write/read patterns. RAID 5 can hurt write-heavy workloads; consider RAID-level trade-offs.

Example: After measuring, you discover high svctm on a database host. You decide to move the data directory to NVMe for lower latency and higher IOPS, validate with iostat, and observe await drop significantly.
