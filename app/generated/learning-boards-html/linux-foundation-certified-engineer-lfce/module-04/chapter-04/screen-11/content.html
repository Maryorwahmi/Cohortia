Deployment
Deployment considerations and common mistakes
Network reachability
Open ports on load balancer (e.g., 80, 8080 for stats) and allow backend ports (e.g., 5000 or 8080) from the balancer host.
SELinux
On RHEL setsebool -P haproxy_connect_any=1 if HAProxy cannot connect to backend ports, or adjust port contexts appropriately.
High availability
Use two load balancers with Keepalived and a virtual IP for active/passive failover, or active‑active clusters for higher throughput.
Logging & monitoring
Enable HAProxy logging and integrate with your monitoring tools to detect patterns, not just single failures.
Common Mistakes

Misconfigured health checks, blocked firewall rules, and SELinux are common causes of perceived balancer failure. Check these first.
