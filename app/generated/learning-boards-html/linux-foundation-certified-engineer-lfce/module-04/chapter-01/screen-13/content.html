Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Logs & Overlaps
Per-site logging

Configure ErrorLog and CustomLog per VirtualHost to isolate errors and access records for each site.

Example: 
ErrorLog /var/log/httpd/site1_error.log
CustomLog /var/log/httpd/site1_access.log combined
Prevent overlaps

Use explicit ServerName values and avoid broad ServerAlias entries that could match multiple hostnames unexpectedly.

Key Takeaway

Per-site logs and unique hostnames are essential for clear diagnostics and preventing requests from resolving to the wrong VirtualHost.
