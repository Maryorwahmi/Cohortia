Why these objectives matter

A hardened server reduces the chance of data loss and service interruption. Precise firewall rules prevent lateral movement, MAC controls stop processes doing unintended actions, and logs provide evidence when things go wrong.

Use the verified milestones to confirm hands-on competency in a lab environment.
