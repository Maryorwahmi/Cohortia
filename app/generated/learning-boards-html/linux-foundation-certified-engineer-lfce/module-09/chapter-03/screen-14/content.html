Module 9 › Chapter 9.3 › Key Concepts
cron
A long-standing scheduler using crontabs; use absolute paths and handle minimal environments.
systemd timers
Timers pair .timer and .service units; OnCalendar and Persistent=true enable flexible, persistent scheduling.
Least privilege & logging
Run jobs with minimal permissions and send output to logs or journalctl for observability and auditing.
Final takeaway

Choose the scheduler that fits your platform and reliability needs, then apply explicit paths, logging, and least privilege to make scheduled tasks dependable.
