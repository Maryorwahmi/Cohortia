Module 9 › Chapter 9.8 › Secrets management and credential safety
Definition

Protect credentials and secrets

Prefer environment variables or dedicated secrets services. Avoid hardcoded keys and limit credential lifetime and scope.

  • Do not commit private keys or tokens to version control.
  • Use managed identities or short-lived tokens when possible.
  • Audit and rotate secrets regularly.
Key takeaway

Treat secrets as first-class infrastructure: store them securely, grant least privilege, and rotate regularly.
