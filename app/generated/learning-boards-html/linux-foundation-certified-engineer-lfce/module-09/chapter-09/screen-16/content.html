Module 10 › Chapter 9.9 › Memory Aids
.gitignore
Exclude virtualenvs, caches, logs, and local configs from tracking.
Commits
Make commits small and message them with both what changed and why.
Branches
Use feature branches for experiments and merge after review and testing.
Closing Thought
Version control is an operational discipline. Apply it consistently and your automation becomes an accountable, maintainable asset.
