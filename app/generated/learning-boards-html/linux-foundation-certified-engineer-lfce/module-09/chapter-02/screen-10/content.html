Definition
awk: field-aware processing and reporting
Powerful for extracting fields, calculating sums, and formatting reports.
Examples
# Print first and third fields from CSV
awk -F',' '{print $1, $3}' data.csv

# Sum second column
awk '{sum+=$2} END {print sum}' numbers.txt

# Extract timestamp and message after grep
grep -i "ERROR" app.log | awk '{print $1, $2, $0}'
Why use awk
  • Field splitting simplifies extraction of timestamps and individual columns.
  • BEGIN and END blocks let you set headers and print aggregates.
  • Inline arithmetic makes quick summaries straightforward without extra tools.
Key Takeaway

Prefer awk for structured extraction and aggregation after filtering with grep when needed.
