Dependencies ensure required setup runs first. Test each role in isolation and then with declared dependencies to confirm correct behavior.
Practical note
Avoid deep dependency chains. Prefer explicit role lists in playbooks when coupling would lead to hidden side effects. Document expected variables and their precedence for each dependency.
Why this matters
Clear dependencies reduce runtime surprises and make roles composable for larger deployments.
