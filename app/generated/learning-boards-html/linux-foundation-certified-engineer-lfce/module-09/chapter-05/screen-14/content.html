Module 9 › Chapter 9.5 › Key Concepts
Roles
Reusable, directory-based units that group tasks, templates, handlers, and variables.
Templates & Handlers
Jinja2 templates generate configs. Notify handlers to restart services only when a change occurs.
Ansible Vault
Encrypt secrets and include them at runtime. Avoid committing vault passwords or unencrypted secrets.
Summary Takeaway

Structure automation with roles, render configs with templates, protect secrets with Vault, and coordinate changes with handlers to build scalable, secure playbooks.
