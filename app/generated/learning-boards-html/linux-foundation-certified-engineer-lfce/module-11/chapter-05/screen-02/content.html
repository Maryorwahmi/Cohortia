Learning Objectives
Progression: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Identify resource symptoms
Recognize signs of CPU, memory, and I/O exhaustion and why they matter for responsiveness.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use live monitoring tools
Run and interpret top, htop, vmstat, and iostat for immediate diagnosis during incidents.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Analyze per-process consumption
Pinpoint offending processes using pidstat, iotop, and lsof to avoid killing critical services.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Mitigate bottlenecks safely
Choose immediate and long-term fixes: stop processes, tune configs, or scale hardware appropriately.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
