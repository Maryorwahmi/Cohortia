Correlate metrics to avoid misdiagnosis
Collect aligned time-series from multiple tools while the issue is active.
Symptom set: high wa
top shows high wa; check iostat %util and await. If %util ≈ 100% and await is high, storage is the bottleneck.
Symptom set: high si/so
vmstat shows heavy swap; look for increased disk I/O as a side effect and identify memory-hungry processes.
Symptom set: high us
Top shows high user CPU while iostat remains quiet; consider CPU-bound code, parallelism, or inefficient algorithms.
Key Takeaway

Align short time-series from top, iostat, and vmstat to form a consistent diagnosis before taking corrective action.

Action
Capture outputs for 30–60 seconds while reproducing the issue if possible; store logs for postmortem analysis.
Mistake
Relying on a single instantaneous reading often leads to the wrong root cause and ineffective remediation.
