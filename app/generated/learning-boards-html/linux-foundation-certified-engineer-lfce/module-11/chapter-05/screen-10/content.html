Module 11 › Chapter 11.5 › Mitigation strategies
Actions: immediate → short-term → long-term
Choose the least disruptive but effective remedy and verify with the same metrics used to diagnose the issue.
Immediate
  • Renice process or pause noncritical jobs.
  • Graceful restart of misbehaving services after verifying PID.
Short-term
  • Tune app caches, reduce connection limits, adjust vm.swappiness.
  • Throttle backup jobs with ionice or tool-specific settings.
Long-term
  • Add CPU, RAM, or faster storage where justified by capacity planning.
  • Introduce monitoring alerts tied to the metrics that reflect the bottleneck.
Key Takeaway

Mitigate safely: confirm process roles, prefer non-disruptive steps first, and monitor to confirm resolution before restorative actions.

Checklist
PID verified, logs checked, action logged, metrics rechecked.
Mistake
Killing critical daemons without a rollback plan can cause larger outages than the original problem.
