Module 11 › Chapter 11.1 › Key Concepts
GRUB
Bootloader that selects and loads the kernel and initramfs.
systemd
Init system that manages units and their dependencies after kernel boot.
Kernel Panic
A fatal kernel error; use older kernels and diagnostics to isolate cause.
Summary
Locate the failing stage, collect logs, and apply targeted repairs. Use live-boot and chroot for bootloader repairs and journalctl for service diagnostics.
