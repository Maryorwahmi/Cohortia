Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Incident Checklist
Stepwise triage
  1. Record symptoms and collect logs (dmesg, syslog).
  2. Make a forensic image or snapshot if feasible.
  3. Identify layer: device, RAID, LVM, or filesystem.
  4. Replace failed hardware and rebuild RAID first.
  5. Reactivate LVM and repair filesystems from rescue mode.
  6. Verify integrity and restore from backup if needed.
Communication

Report the expected impact and recovery plan. Conserving recoverable data should take precedence over a rushed restore attempt.

Key Takeaway

Follow a documented checklist that preserves data first, repairs hardware second, and restores services last.
