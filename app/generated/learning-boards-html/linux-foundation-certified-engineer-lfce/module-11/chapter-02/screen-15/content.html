Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › What to Remember
Isolate & Image
Preserve the current on-disk state before performing repairs.
Diagnose Bottom-Up
Check SMART, then RAID, then LVM, then filesystem repairs.
Backups & Monitoring
RAID is not a backup; monitor SMART and test restores regularly.
Final thought

Implement these three rules in your on-call checklist to reduce the risk of irreversible data loss during incidents.
