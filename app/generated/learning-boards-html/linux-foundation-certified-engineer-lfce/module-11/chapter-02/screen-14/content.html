Module 11 › Chapter 11.2 › Key Concepts
Filesystem Corruption
Damage to metadata or data structures that breaks access.
fsck & xfs_repair
Tools to check and repair; run unmounted or from rescue mode.
Bad Blocks & SMART
Detect physical defects with badblocks and smartctl; replace failing drives.
Summary takeaway

Preserve data first, diagnose bottom-up, repair hardware then logical layers, and keep monitoring and backups in place.
