SMART checks
sudo smartctl -a /dev/sda
# Important attributes: Reallocated_Sector_Ct, Current_Pending_Sector
Schedule smartctl or smartd checks and alert on rising reallocated or pending counts. Early replacement is cheaper than data recovery.
Backup & test
  • Automate regular backups and store offsite or on different hardware.
  • Perform periodic restores to validate backups are usable.
  • Document RAID rebuild and LVM restore procedures and test them in a lab.
Key Takeaway

Monitoring plus tested backups reduces downtime and prevents desperate, high-risk repairs during incidents.
