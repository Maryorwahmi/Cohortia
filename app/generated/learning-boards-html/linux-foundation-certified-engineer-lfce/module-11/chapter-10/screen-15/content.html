Module 11 › Chapter 11.10 › Key Concepts
systemtap
Scripted kernel/user probes; flexible but must be validated to avoid impact on system stability.
eBPF / bpftrace
Low-overhead, in-kernel tracing for production observability; safe but kernel-dependent.
Documentation
Record hypotheses, commands, and outputs. Even failed attempts are useful for future debugging.
Final takeaway

Combine the right tools, respect safety limits, and follow a repeatable workflow. That combination is what makes an expert troubleshooter.
