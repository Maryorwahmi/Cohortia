Module 11 › Chapter 11.3 › Link-layer and NIC verification
Definition

Confirm physical and link health

Interpret interface state and counters so you can confidently rule in or out layer-1/2 faults.

# Inspect link state and flags
ip link show eth0

# View counters and errors
cat /sys/class/net/eth0/statistics/rx_errors
cat /sys/class/net/eth0/statistics/tx_dropped

# Watch statistics over time (example)
sar -n DEV 1 5
Concrete scenario
A VM shows no carrier: check hypervisor vNIC mapping. Reattach or restart the virtual NIC rather than reconfiguring IPs.
Misconception
A green LED is not proof of correct settings; duplex or driver issues still cause packet errors.
Quick checks and visuals
Interpretation tips
  • LOWER_UP indicates physical carrier; absence means no link.
  • Rising error counters suggest physical or NIC driver issues.
  • Consistent drops during bursts suggest duplex mismatch or faulty cable.
Check your understanding
If ip link shows UP and carrier but you still see Rx errors, what is the likely next check? (Answer: driver and duplex/speed mismatch or faulty switch port.)
Key Takeaway
Physical validation prevents wasted application-level debugging. Always confirm link and counters first.
