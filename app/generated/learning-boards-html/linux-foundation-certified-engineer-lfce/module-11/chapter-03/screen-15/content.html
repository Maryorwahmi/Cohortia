What to Remember

ss
Inspect sockets and map ports to processes to confirm service binding.
tcpdump
Capture with filters to observe TCP flags, retransmits, and handshake failures.
iptables / nftables
List rules and counters to identify dropped packets and confirm rule matches.
Final takeaway
Combine layer-ordered checks with focused tool usage and document each change. Re-test the original failure to confirm resolution.
