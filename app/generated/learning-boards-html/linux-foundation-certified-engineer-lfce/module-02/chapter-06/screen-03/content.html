Learning Objectives (continued)
Practical command fluency and verification steps
LUKS command fluency
Run cryptsetup luksFormat, luksOpen, mkfs on the mapped device, and luksClose safely.
Header and keyfile safety
Back up the LUKS header and keep keyfiles off the encrypted device to avoid lockouts.
ACL auditing
Use getfacl to confirm effective permissions and avoid conflicting ownership rules.
Quota testing
Simulate soft warnings and hard-limit failures to validate quota behaviour before rollout.
