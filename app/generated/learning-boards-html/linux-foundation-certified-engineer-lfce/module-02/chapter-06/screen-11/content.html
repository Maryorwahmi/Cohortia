Comparison
I/O schedulers at a glance
noop
Simple FIFO. Low overhead; good for SSDs and hypervisor-managed storage.
deadline / mq-deadline
Prioritizes reads, reduces latency; useful for databases and latency-sensitive services.
cfq / bfq
Fairness-oriented; cfq is deprecated on many kernels, bfq targets fairness on certain devices.
Commands
cat /sys/block/sda/queue/scheduler
echo deadline | sudo tee /sys/block/sda/queue/scheduler
# Persistent: add elevator=deadline to GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX and update-grub
Misconception
Assuming the kernel scheduler always improves performance; on SSDs, minimal scheduling often wins because the device handles queuing internally.
Key Takeaway
Benchmark scheduler changes with realistic workloads and prefer noop or mq-deadline for modern SSD environments unless specific latency or fairness needs exist.
