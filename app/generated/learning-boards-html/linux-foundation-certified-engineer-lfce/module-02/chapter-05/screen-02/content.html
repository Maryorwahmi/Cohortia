Learning Objectives
Bloom goals: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Bloom
Learn Understand Apply Master
01
Understand XFS and Btrfs features
Explain feature trade-offs and use cases. Why it matters: matching filesystem to workload reduces downtime and maximizes throughput.
Verified competency milestone
02
Perform XFS admin tasks
Grow a mounted XFS filesystem and run online defragmentation. Why it matters: maintain uptime for production services.
Verified competency milestone
03
Manage Btrfs volumes and snapshots
Create subvolumes and snapshots; verify read-only snapshots. Why it matters: fast recovery and flexible development environments.
Verified competency milestone
04
Select and maintain filesystems
Apply best practices: backups, scrubs, repair tools, and careful RAID planning. Why it matters: protects data and reduces operational risk.
Verified competency milestone
