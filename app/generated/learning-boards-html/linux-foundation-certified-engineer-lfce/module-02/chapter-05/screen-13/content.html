Module 2 › Chapter 2.5 › XFS vs Btrfs
XFS — Strengths
  • High throughput for large files and parallel I/O
  • Stable online growth with xfs_growfs
  • Mature tooling and production history
Btrfs — Strengths
  • Copy-on-write snapshots and writable branching
  • Subvolumes for flexible dataset management
  • Checksums, integrated RAID, and compression options
Best practice
Document your choice, automate snaps and scrubs, schedule maintenance, and keep backups. Re-evaluate RAID and snapshot strategies as your workload evolves.
