Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Commands & Safety
Enable libvirtd
systemctl enable --now libvirtd to ensure the daemon is running.
Back up disks
Verify disk paths with virsh dumpxml and back up before deleting images.
Use logs & XML
journalctl -u libvirtd and virsh dumpxml are the first places to look when things fail.
Final note

Operational discipline—confirming services, verifying paths, and reading logs—keeps your virtualization environment reliable and recoverable.
