Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › What to Remember
Type 1 vs Type 2
Type 1 is bare-metal and better for production; Type 2 is hosted and convenient for desktops.
Resource discipline
Measure, allocate conservatively, and monitor to prevent contention across VMs.
Operational safety
Use snapshots and clones for agility, but retain backups and test migration procedures.
Keep these anchors visible while you design or operate virtual hosts: choose the hypervisor for your workload, plan resources, and verify operational recovery paths before relying on VMs in production.
