Common bottlenecks
  • CPU: high utilization and CPU steal time
  • Memory: ballooning and swap activity
  • Storage: high latency and queue depth
  • Network: throughput limits and drops
Pro tip

Compare guest-level metrics with host-level counters to locate which layer is the bottleneck before making allocation changes.

Debugging checklist
Collect metrics, review snapshot chains, verify bridge and NIC settings, test with workloads scaled to expected production levels.
Mistake to avoid: Hasty scaling of vCPUs for perceived CPU shortages without checking host capacity can worsen contention.
