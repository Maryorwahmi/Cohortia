Module 9 › Chapter 8.9 › Assessment
Question 1

Explain a scenario where containers-on-VMs is preferable to bare-metal containers. Focus on two distinct advantages: enhanced security isolation for multi-tenant workloads, and mature hypervisor resource management and portability.

Question 2

Describe three levels to collect CPU and memory metrics and provide a tool example for each: Host (atop or sar), VM (virt-top or virsh domstats), Container (docker stats or podman stats). Explain how you will correlate findings across these levels.

Scoring tip

Correct answers must identify advantages with operational implications and name one tool per monitoring level plus a brief correlation plan.

Reflection

When presenting your plan, include a short incident example showing how you would trace a container CPU spike to a host-level limit.
