Virtualization vs. Containerization

Understand trade-offs, pick the right environment, and design hybrid deployments that combine isolation with agility.

In this lesson

Compare isolation, resource usage and portability. Learn hybrid containers-on-VMs patterns and monitoring strategies for host, hypervisor, VM and container layers.

Why it matters

Design choices affect security boundaries, cost, and operational procedures. Choosing the wrong layer for isolation or monitoring can cause outages or exposure.

Quick preview

We’ll finish with a short hands-on activity: launching nginx inside a KVM VM and measuring resources from host, VM and container.
