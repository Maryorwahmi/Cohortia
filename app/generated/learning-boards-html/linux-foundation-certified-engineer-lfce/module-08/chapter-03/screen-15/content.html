Key Concepts: Networking & Storage Foundations

Essential Terms for KVM Infrastructure

Bridged Networking

A virtual networking mode connecting VMs directly to the physical network, making them fully accessible and integrated.

LVM (Logical Volume Manager)

A system for managing disk space, providing flexible resizing and snapshotting of logical volumes for VM disks.

Storage Pools

A libvirt abstraction defining a source of storage from which VM disk volumes can be created, centralizing management.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering bridged networking, LVM, and libvirt storage pools provides the foundational knowledge for building robust, scalable, and efficiently managed KVM virtual infrastructures.
