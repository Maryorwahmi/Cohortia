What to Remember
Short, practical reminders for daily use.
Explicit runs
Use -d, --name, -p, -v, and -e to make container behavior predictable and scriptable.
Named volumes
Prefer named volumes for database and service data to ensure portability and lifecycle control.
Compose & networks
Use user-defined bridges for DNS-based discovery and docker-compose to codify multi-service setups.
Closing takeaway
Small, consistent patterns for run flags, volumes, and networking lead to reliable, repeatable container deployments.
