Core Concept › Benefits of Containerization
Portability

An image runs the same on any host with Docker, removing environment drift.

Efficiency

Sharing the host kernel reduces overhead and enables faster startup and higher density.

Isolation

Namespaces and cgroups isolate processes and control resources to limit interference.

Scalability & Productivity

Quick startup improves scaling, and consistent images streamline development and CI workflows.

Key Takeaway

Use containers to deliver consistent environments that boot quickly and scale, and pair them with limits and monitoring for predictable operations.
