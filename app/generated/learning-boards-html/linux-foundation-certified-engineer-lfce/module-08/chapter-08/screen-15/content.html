Module 8 › Chapter 8.8 › Quick Reminders
Daemonless
No central dockerd means smaller attack surface and per-container resilience.
Rootless verification
Ensure /etc/subuid and /etc/subgid entries exist for your user and verify with podman info --debug.
Reuse workflows
podman build and podman-compose support existing Dockerfiles and Compose files for a smooth migration.
Final thought

Use rootless Podman to reduce privileged surfaces while continuing to enforce image and runtime best practices.
