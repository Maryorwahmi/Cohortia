conmon and the runtime

Podman launches a small monitor process named conmon for each container. Conmon handles logging, exit codes, and lifecycle supervision without a central daemon.

Per-container supervisor: conmon
Direct kernel interactions: namespaces and cgroups
No single privileged daemon to manage all containers
Why this matters
Isolated supervision reduces blast radius when a single container has runtime problems and makes per-user lifecycle management straightforward.
Common Misunderstanding
Conmon does not add privileges to containers; it simply supervises processes and collects logs and exit codes.
Key Takeaway
Small supervisory processes plus kernel namespaces give Podman resilience without a central privileged service.
