Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
Exclude noise
Avoid monitoring volatile directories that create predictable but unhelpful alerts.
Protect baselines
Keep the AIDE database off-host or on read-only storage to prevent tampering.
Tune and correlate
Use thresholds and multi-source correlation to reduce false positives and highlight true incidents.
Final takeaway

Detection is most effective when host integrity checks, log analysis, and network context are combined, baselines are protected, and alerts are tuned to be actionable.
