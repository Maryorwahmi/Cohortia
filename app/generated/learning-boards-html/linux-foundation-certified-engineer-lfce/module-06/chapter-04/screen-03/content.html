Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Learning Objectives
This page expands the operational objectives into judgement calls and best practices. Four compact cards below summarize specific skills and why they matter.
Select attributes
Choose permissions, time, and strong hashes
Monitoring p+i+u+g+s+m and md5 or sha256 gives reliable change detection; choose hashes that meet policy.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Exclude noise
Exclude /var/log, /tmp, /proc
Excluding volatile directories reduces false positives and keeps reports actionable.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Protect baseline
Store AIDE DB off-host or read-only
An attacker with root on the host can modify the baseline if it is kept on the same writable filesystem.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Tune alerts
Use correlation and thresholds
Aggregate repeated events (failed logins) into a single alert to avoid fatigue and highlight real incidents.
