Practical tuning checklist
  • Monitor high-value directories and exclude expected-change paths.
  • Use thresholds to aggregate repeated events by source.
  • Whitelist scheduled maintenance and known benign processes.
  • Adjust severity levels so only correlated multi-source events escalate automatically.
Measure alert volume before and after tuning. Tuning requires iteration and collaboration with operations teams to avoid blind spots.
