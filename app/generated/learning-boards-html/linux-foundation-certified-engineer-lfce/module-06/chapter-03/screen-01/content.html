Why this matters

Modern Linux services run on mixed IPv4/IPv6 stacks and require consistent, maintainable firewall policies. nftables reduces duplication, improves performance with sets and maps, and lets you express NAT and filtering in one framework.

Common risk

A misapplied rule can block access to a server. Always test rules safely and keep a rollback mechanism ready.
