Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › What to Remember
Authorization first
Get written permission and define scope before scanning or testing.
Prioritize by context
Combine CVSS with exposure and business impact to decide what to fix now.
Verify every fix
Re-scan after remediation to prove the vulnerability is closed and keep the audit trail.
Closing thought
Responsible vulnerability management balances automation, human judgment, and clear rules so security improves without causing harm.
