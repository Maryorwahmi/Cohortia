Module 7 › Chapter 6.8 › Key Concepts
Vulnerability Scanning
Automated checks for known weaknesses; broad coverage and repeatable.
Penetration Testing
Simulated attacks to validate exploitability and measure impact.
Nmap
Essential for discovery: use -sS, -sV, -O, and -A appropriately with permission.
Takeaway
Combine automated scanning with verification and clear Rules of Engagement to maintain security without causing harm.
