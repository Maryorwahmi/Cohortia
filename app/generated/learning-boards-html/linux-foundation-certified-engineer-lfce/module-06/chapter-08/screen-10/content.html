Authorization and Rules of Engagement

Protect operations and legal boundaries by formalizing scope and responsibilities before any test.

What to include in ROE
  • Authorized targets and networks.
  • Allowed test types and timing.
  • Contact persons and incident handling instructions.
Practical example
Before scanning production, obtain written permission specifying the IP range, accepted scan templates, and a point of contact for emergency stop commands.
Key Takeaway

Rules of Engagement and written authorization are essential to prevent legal exposure and operational harm from security testing.

Quick do / don't
Do: Obtain written authorization and define scope.
Don't: Scan unknown third-party systems or exceed agreed test types.
Do: Communicate risk and schedule scans to limit disruption.
