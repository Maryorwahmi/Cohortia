Reading CVSS with context

A numerical score helps triage, but do not treat it as the sole decision factor.

Three contextual checks
  1. Exposure: Is the service reachable from untrusted networks?
  2. Exploitability: Is a public exploit known and easy to run?
  3. Impact: Would exploitation affect critical business functions?
Example
SSH weak MACs (CVSS 7.5) on a public host is prioritized higher than an internal version disclosure with similar score because the public host has direct exposure and potential for data compromise.
Key Takeaway

Use CVSS to triage, then apply exposure and business impact to finalize remediation priority and schedule.

Prioritization matrix
High CVSS + Public = Urgent
Medium CVSS + Internal = Scheduled
Low CVSS + Isolated = Monitor
