sudo and visudo
Grant minimal command-level privileges and edit safely with visudo to avoid syntax errors.
System users
Run services as dedicated, non-login accounts and verify ownership and shells in /etc/passwd and /etc/shadow.
pam_faillock & fail2ban
Combine per-account locks with log-driven IP bans to stop both targeted and noisy brute-force attempts.
Final takeaway
Apply configuration, then verify with test users and log checks. Conservative defaults and regular audits keep these controls effective.
