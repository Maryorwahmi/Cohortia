Final Summary
What to Remember
Three practical reminders to carry forward into your lab and production practices.
1. Protect startup
Secure Boot enforces signed code at boot and helps prevent persistent compromise.
2. Protect storage
LUKS encrypts data; manage slots and backups to balance security and recoverability.
3. Protect keys
Use TPMs for device binding and HSMs for enterprise key custody to reduce exposure of secrets.
Final tip: test your recovery plan. Security without recoverability can turn protection into permanent loss.
