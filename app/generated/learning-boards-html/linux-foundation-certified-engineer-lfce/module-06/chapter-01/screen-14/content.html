Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Access Control Lists (ACLs)
Define per-user or per-group permissions beyond owner/group/others for targeted access control.
File Attributes (chattr)
Immutable (+i) and append-only (+a) protect files at a behavioral level; test impacts on system processes.
Mount Options & tmpfs
Use noexec,nosuid,nodev and tmpfs for temporary directories to limit execution, privilege escalation, and device creation.
Summary

Combine ACLs, attributes, and mount hardening with scheduled audits to maintain a secure filesystem baseline and detect drift early.
