Module 06 › Chapter 6.1 › What to Remember
Prefer groups
Use groups for broad access and ACLs for targeted exceptions to reduce complexity.
Inspect the mask
When permissions don't behave as expected, check the ACL mask; it may be silently limiting access.
Document & automate
Record attribute and ACL changes and automate audits to keep your security posture consistent.
Final takeaway

Treat filesystem security as a living process: apply precise controls, test, document, and audit regularly to keep systems safe and predictable.
