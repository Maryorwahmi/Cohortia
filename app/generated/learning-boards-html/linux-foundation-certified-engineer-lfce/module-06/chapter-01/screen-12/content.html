Best Practice

Baselines and scheduled audits

Record the outputs of your permission and attribute scans, store them versioned, and compare regularly to detect drift.

Baseline commands
find / -perm /4000 -type f > baseline_suid.txt
find / -type f -exec getfacl {} + > baseline_acls.txt
lsattr -R /etc > baseline_attrs.txt
Audit cadence
  • Weekly: SUID/SGID scan and quick ACL diff for high-change dirs.
  • Monthly: Full ACL collection and attribute inventory.
  • On change: Run targeted audits after major deployments or user provisioning.
Automation & Enforcement
Use configuration management to enforce ownership, modes, and ACLs where appropriate. Automation reduces human error and keeps your baseline intact.
Key Takeaway

Audits must be repeatable, scheduled, and tied to incident response. Make baselines and automate enforcement to prevent drift and detect unauthorized changes quickly.
