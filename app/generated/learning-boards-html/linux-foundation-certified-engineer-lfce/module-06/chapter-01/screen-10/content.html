Process Flow

Audit: find SUID/SGID and inspect ACLs

# Find SUID files
find / -perm /4000 -type f 2>/dev/null
# Find SGID files
find / -perm /2000 -type f 2>/dev/null
# Collect ACLs for critical directories
find /etc /opt/app -type f -exec getfacl {} + 2>/dev/null
Best practices
  • Record a baseline of SUID/SGID and ACL outputs for comparison.
  • Schedule weekly or monthly audits depending on change rate.
  • Investigate any newly discovered SUID files immediately; assess their need and provenance.
Common mistake

Only scanning traditional permissions misses ACL entries that grant unexpected access. Include getfacl outputs in your audits.
