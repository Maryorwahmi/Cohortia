Auditd steps
  1. Backup /etc/audit/audit.rules to a safe location.
  2. Add scoped rules in /etc/audit/rules.d/ and reload auditd.
  3. Perform test changes and confirm with ausearch on the rule key.
Rsyslog & rotation
  1. On client, add a forwarding line for authpriv to the collector and restart rsyslog.
  2. On collector, enable UDP/TCP reception and write incoming auth messages to a dedicated file.
  3. Create a logrotate entry for your app log and force rotation to validate postrotate behavior.
Verification checklist
Confirm: audit entries appear for your key, collector receives forwarded logs, rotated files are present and readable, and timestamps align across hosts.
Reflection prompt
