Module 6 › Chapter 6.7 › Forensic readiness
Core elements
  • Time synchronization across systems (NTP)
  • Secure or authenticated transport to the collector
  • Access controls and audit on the central store
  • Long-term archival and compression policies
Practical check
Confirm timestamps from client and collector events align, and that archived logs are readable and indexed for search.
Why this matters
Without consistent times and secure transport, correlating events across systems becomes unreliable and reduces the legal and compliance value of your logs.
Takeaway
Build your logging architecture with an eye to integrity: synchronized clocks, reliable transport, controlled access, and documented retention.
