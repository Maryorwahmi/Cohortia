Auditd, rsyslog, and logrotate — a practical pairing

Learn kernel-level event capture with auditd, reliable collection and forwarding with rsyslog, and safe retention with logrotate. This combo gives you detectability, central visibility, and archival.

In this lesson
Hands-on terminal demo
Audit Rules
Monitor passwd, shadow, sudoers and SUID execution.
Central Logging
Forward auth logs to a remote syslog for correlation and tamper resistance.
Rotation & Archive
Rotate, compress, and postrotate safely to preserve data and service continuity.
Key Takeaway

A focused auditd rule set plus centralized rsyslog collection and a tested logrotate policy form the baseline for forensic readiness.

Concept Map
Events → Collector → Forward → Rotate → Archive
auditd
Kernel-level monitoring for system calls and security events. Use rules to target critical assets.
rsyslog
Collects and forwards logs. Use TCP for reliability and templates/filters for routing.
logrotate
Rotate and compress logs, run safe postrotate scripts, and keep permissions correct.
Forensics
Time sync, transport integrity, and centralized storage reduce risk of tampered evidence.
