logrotate
Rotate, compress, and run safe postrotate scripts to protect log availability and disk use.
Centralized Logging
Ship logs to a central collector to prevent tampering and enable correlation across systems.
Forensic controls
Ensure NTP sync, transport integrity, access controls, and documented retention to preserve evidence value.
Final takeaway

Logging and auditing are systems engineering tasks: design, test, and maintain them to keep your environment forensic-ready and compliant.
