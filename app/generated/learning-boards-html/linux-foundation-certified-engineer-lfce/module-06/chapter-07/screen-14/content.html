Linux Auditing System
Kernel-level logging of system calls and security events managed by auditd.
ausearch & aureport
Command-line tools to search audit logs by key and to produce summary reports for triage.
rsyslog
Collects, filters, and forwards syslog messages; use TCP for critical forwarding.
Summary

Combine targeted audit rules, reliable forwarding, and validated rotation to produce trustworthy logs for compliance and forensics.
