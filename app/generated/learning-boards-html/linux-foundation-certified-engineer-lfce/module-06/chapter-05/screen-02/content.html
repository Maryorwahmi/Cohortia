Learning Objectives
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Understand MAC
Define Mandatory Access Control and contrast it with DAC. Understand why MAC reduces the impact of compromised accounts.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Administer SELinux
Use sestatus, ls -Z, ps -Z, semanage, chcon, and restorecon to inspect and maintain correct contexts for services.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Generate Policy Modules
Analyze AVC denials with audit2allow and create a minimal .te/.pp module; install it with semodule -i to enable app behavior safely.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Compare SELinux & AppArmor
Understand label-based vs path-based enforcement and decide which matches a given distribution and operational policy goals.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
