Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › ntpd vs chronyd
ntpd
  • Traditional daemon; uses /etc/ntp.conf.
  • Better for long-running physical servers with stable connectivity.
  • Inspect with ntpq -p.
chronyd
  • Fast initial sync; designed for VMs and intermittent networks.
  • Uses /etc/chrony.conf and chronyc commands.
  • Handles large offsets and suspend/resume more effectively.
Decision checklist
Match the daemon to your environment, verify firewall and access rules, and measure sync quality with offsets and stratum numbers. Remember: secure configuration is independent of the chosen daemon.
