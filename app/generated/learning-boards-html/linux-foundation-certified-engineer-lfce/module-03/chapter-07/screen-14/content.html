Accurate time
Essential for logging, TLS validation, and distributed correctness.
Tool choice
ntpd for steady hosts, chronyd for VMs and intermittent networks.
Security & verification
Open UDP 123 carefully, use restrict/allow, and verify with ntpq/chronyc; use keys when needed.
Summary: Keep clocks accurate, pick the right daemon for your environment, and secure and verify NTP/chrony services to maintain infrastructure integrity.
