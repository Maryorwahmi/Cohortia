Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Practical Skills
Tools you'll use
  • chronyd / chronyc — modern client/server; resilient to suspends and disconnections.
  • ntpd / ntpq — traditional daemon and peer query tool.
  • firewall-cmd and ss — verify UDP 123 and open services as needed.
  • date and ntpdate — manual corrections when offsets are huge.
Assessment focus
You will be checked on configuration correctness, service state, listening ports, access controls, and the ability to read ntpq or chronyc output to confirm synchronization.
