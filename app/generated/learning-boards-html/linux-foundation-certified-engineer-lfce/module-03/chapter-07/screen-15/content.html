Module 4 › Chapter 3.7 › Commands & Files
Configuration files
/etc/ntp.conf and /etc/chrony.conf are your primary edit points for daemon behavior and access control.
Verification
ntpq -p, chronyc sources, chronyc tracking, and ss -lnu | grep 123 are core commands to validate sync and listening sockets.
Service & firewall
systemctl enable --now chronyd|ntpd and firewall-cmd --add-service=ntp --permanent; firewall-cmd --reload.
Keep this cheat sheet close when configuring NTP/chrony. Correct configuration, verification, and firewall rules are the fastest path to reliable synchronization.
