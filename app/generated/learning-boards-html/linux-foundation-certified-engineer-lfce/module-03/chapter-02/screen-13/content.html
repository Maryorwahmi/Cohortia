Deployment timeline (quick)
Step 1
Validate dhcpd.conf and restart server
Step 2
Confirm interface binding and open UDP 67/68
Step 3
Verify client lease and options
Step 4
Inspect dhcpd.leases and server logs
Key Takeaway

A short, repeatable verification process prevents common deployment mistakes and catches relay, firewall, or reservation errors early.
