Module 3 › Chapter 3.5 › Mail system roles
MTA · MDA · MUA

Clear definitions prevent configuration mistakes and misplaced expectations, especially about who handles relay and who serves mail to clients.

MTA (Postfix) — transports mail and applies relay policy.
MDA (Dovecot) — delivers mail to Maildir and provides IMAP/POP3 access.
MUA — client apps used by users to send and retrieve messages.
Key Takeaway

Keep transport and delivery separate: Postfix moves mail, Dovecot stores and serves it.

Concrete Example

User sends mail from an MUA. Postfix accepts it on port 587 or 25. If the recipient is local, Postfix hands the message to Dovecot or delivers it into /home/user/Maildir. Dovecot then permits the user to read messages over IMAP with TLS.

Common misconception
IMAP/POP3 servers do not relay mail to remote domains; that is the MTA's job. Confusing these roles leads to misconfigured MX or relay settings.
