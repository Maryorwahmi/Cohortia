A / AAAA
Map names to IPv4 and IPv6 addresses respectively.
MX
Directs mail to mail servers and includes priority values.
CNAME / PTR
CNAME creates aliases; PTR provides reverse mapping from IP to name.
Final takeaway

Keep a short operations checklist and validate every change. The combination of correct zone files, restricted transfers, and regular validation keeps DNS reliable and secure.
