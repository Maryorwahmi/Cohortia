Advanced Firewall Management with nftables

Learn the nftables architecture, write compact rulesets with sets and maps, and configure NAT to safely forward and masquerade traffic for internal clients.

In this lesson
Architecture, rules, sets/maps, NAT, troubleshooting and a 15-minute lab-style walkthrough.
Why it matters
Unified, expressive rules simplify multi-protocol firewalls and improve performance in mixed IPv4/IPv6 environments.
