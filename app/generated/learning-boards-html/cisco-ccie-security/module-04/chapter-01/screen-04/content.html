Core Concept

What is a Virtual Private Network (VPN)?

A VPN creates a secure, encrypted 'tunnel' through an untrusted network, like the internet, allowing remote users or entire branch offices to access private network resources as if they were directly connected.

Key Takeaway

VPNs are essential for establishing secure, private communication channels over public networks, protecting data from eavesdropping and unauthorized access.

Analogy

Think of a VPN as building a private, armored road directly between two points across a vast, public highway. Only those with the right keys can travel on your private road, and all cargo is securely locked away.
