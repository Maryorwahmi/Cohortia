Core Components

Encryption: Data Confidentiality

Encryption scrambles the data payload within the VPN tunnel, making it unintelligible to unauthorized parties.

Algorithms: AES, 3DES

These algorithms convert readable data into ciphertext, ensuring privacy even if intercepted.

Core Components

Authentication: Identity Verification

Authentication verifies the identity of communicating parties, preventing unauthorized access to the VPN tunnel.

Methods: Pre-shared Keys, Digital Certificates, User/Pass

Ensures only trusted devices and users can establish a secure connection.

Key Takeaway

Encryption and authentication are vital for VPNs, ensuring data privacy through scrambling and trusted access through identity verification, respectively.
