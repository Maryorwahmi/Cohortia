Why VPNs Matter

The Critical Need for VPNs

VPNs address fundamental security requirements by ensuring data confidentiality, integrity, and authentication across untrusted networks.

Confidentiality

Protecting data from unauthorized access via encryption.

Integrity

Ensuring data remains unaltered during transit.

Authentication

Verifying identities of communicating parties.

Key Takeaway

VPNs are indispensable for securing data in transit by combining encryption for privacy, integrity checks for data accuracy, and authentication for trusted access.
