Key Concepts: VPN Operation & Authentication

Understanding traffic, control, and authentication methods

Interesting Traffic

Network traffic identified by an access list that should be encrypted and sent through the VPN tunnel.

Crypto Map

Ties together IKE policy, IPsec transform set, interesting traffic ACL, and remote peer, applied to an interface.

Digital Certificate

An electronic document used for stronger authentication than PSKs, issued by a Certificate Authority (CA).

Summary Takeaway

Effective IPsec VPN deployment requires understanding traffic selection, the crypto map's role, choosing appropriate authentication methods, and using verification commands.
