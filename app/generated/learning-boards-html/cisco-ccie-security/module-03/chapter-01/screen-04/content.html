Core Concept

Firewalls: The Network's Security Guard

Controlling traffic and enforcing security policies at network boundaries.

What is a Firewall?

A network security device that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic based on predefined security rules.

Why it Matters

Firewalls are the first line of defense, preventing unauthorized access, blocking malicious attacks, and ensuring legitimate traffic flow to protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

Common Misconception

Many beginners assume firewalls are 'set it and forget it' devices. In reality, they require continuous monitoring, policy updates, and tuning to remain effective against evolving threats.

Firewalls are dynamic, rule-based systems crucial for network defense, requiring continuous management to adapt to new threats.
