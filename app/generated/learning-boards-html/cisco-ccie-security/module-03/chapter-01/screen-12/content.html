Deployment Models

Common Firewall Deployment Models

Strategically positioning firewalls for optimal network protection.

Perimeter Firewall

Positioned at the network edge (e.g., between internal network and the internet) to protect the entire internal infrastructure from external threats. Defines 'inside', 'outside', and 'DMZ' zones.

Internal Segmentation Firewall

Deployed within the internal network to segment different departments, sensitive data centers, or critical application environments. Prevents lateral movement of threats.

Defense-in-Depth Strategy

Utilizing multiple layers of security controls, including both perimeter and internal firewalls, to provide comprehensive protection against various attack vectors.
