01

Explain Packet Filtering Principles

Understand how packet filtering firewalls operate at the network and transport layers, making decisions based on individual packet headers. Recognize their inherent limitations, such as statelessness and vulnerability to spoofing, which are critical for appreciating advanced firewall solutions.

02

Describe ACL Implementation

Articulate how Access Control Lists (ACLs) are utilized to configure packet filtering rules on network devices. This includes understanding the criteria used for filtering, the sequential processing of rules, and the impact of rule order on traffic flow decisions.
