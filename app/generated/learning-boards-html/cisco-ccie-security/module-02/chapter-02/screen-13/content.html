Proactive Defense with Talos and SecureX

Leverage global threat intelligence and automated orchestration for faster, more effective incident response.

Integrated Threat Response

Cisco SNA is powered by continuous threat intelligence and automation, enabling proactive detection and rapid incident response.

  • • Cisco Talos: Feeds global threat intelligence to Cisco security products for proactive blocking.
  • • Cisco SecureX: XDR platform unifying visibility, automation, and orchestration across the security portfolio.
  • • Automation: Playbooks within SecureX reduce manual effort and improve consistency in incident response.
Core Benefit

Faster Incident Response

Unified visibility and automation allow security teams to respond to threats more quickly and effectively.

Scenario Example

Automated Threat Containment

Talos identifies a new phishing campaign. SecureX automatically updates Umbrella to block related domains and pushes new IPS signatures to FTD devices.

Key Takeaway

Threat intelligence and automation are the backbone of Cisco SNA, enabling a truly adaptive and resilient security posture.
