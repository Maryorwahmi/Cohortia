Advanced Data Center Micro-segmentation

Preventing Lateral Movement with Cisco Secure Workload

Cisco Secure Workload (formerly Tetration)

Provides pervasive visibility into application dependencies and enables micro-segmentation at the workload level, ensuring applications only communicate with authorized components.

Why Micro-segmentation is Vital

This granular control is crucial for preventing lateral movement of threats within the data center, significantly reducing the 'blast radius' if a workload is compromised.

Safety Note

Misconfiguring data center firewalls or access controls can lead to catastrophic data breaches or service outages. Always follow strict change management and testing protocols.
