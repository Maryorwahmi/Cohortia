The IR Lifecycle: Preparing for the Inevitable

Establishing Readiness and Detecting Incidents

Why a Lifecycle?

A structured approach ensures that security incidents are managed effectively, from initial detection to post-incident analysis and improvement.

  • •Preparation: Policies, procedures, tools, and training *before* an incident.
  • •Identification: Detecting and confirming the occurrence of a security incident.
  • •Cisco tools like Firepower, Stealthwatch, and Secure Endpoint aid in identification.
Key Takeaway

Solid preparation is the foundation for effective incident response, enabling rapid and accurate identification of threats.
