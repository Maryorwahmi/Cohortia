Recovery and Continuous Improvement

Restoring Operations and Learning from Incidents

The Final Steps

These phases ensure systems are fully restored and that valuable lessons are learned to strengthen future security defenses.

  • •Recovery: Restore systems and data to normal operation, often from clean backups.
  • •Post-Incident Activity: Analyze the incident to identify weaknesses and improve processes.
  • •This feedback loop is essential for continuous security posture improvement.
Key Takeaway

Recovery ensures business continuity, while post-incident analysis drives long-term security resilience and adaptation.
