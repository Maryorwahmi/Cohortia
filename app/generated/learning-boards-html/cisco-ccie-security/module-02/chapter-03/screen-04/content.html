Core Concept

What is Threat Intelligence?

Knowledge for Proactive Security

Definition

Threat intelligence is actionable knowledge about existing or emerging threats, including their capabilities, infrastructure, and motivations. It provides context for informed security decisions.

Reactive vs. Proactive

Without intelligence, security teams react to attacks after they occur. With it, they can proactively block known malicious indicators.

Why it Matters

It's the 'eyes and ears' of your security posture, enabling foresight to reduce attack surface and impact.
