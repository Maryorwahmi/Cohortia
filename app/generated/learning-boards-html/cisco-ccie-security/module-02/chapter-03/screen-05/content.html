Diverse Feeds for Comprehensive Insight

Open-Source, Commercial, and Proprietary Intelligence

Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)

  • •Publicly available information (blogs, forums, advisories).
  • •Free to access, but requires significant curation and validation effort.
  • •Examples: CISA alerts, security research blogs, open-source projects.
Consideration

While cost-effective, OSINT can be time-consuming to process and may lack the depth of commercial feeds.

Commercial Threat Intelligence

  • •Provided by vendors, offering curated and high-fidelity data.
  • •Often includes additional context, analysis, and integration capabilities.
  • •Examples: Recorded Future, CrowdStrike Falcon Intelligence.
Key Takeaway

Commercial feeds offer higher fidelity and reduced operational overhead, but come with a subscription cost.
