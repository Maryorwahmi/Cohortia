Critical Phases

Containing and Eliminating the Threat

Limiting Impact and Removing Root Causes

Immediate Action

These phases focus on stopping the spread of an incident and thoroughly removing all traces of the threat from affected systems.

  • •Containment: Isolate compromised systems, block malicious IPs, disable accounts.
  • •Eradication: Patch vulnerabilities, remove malware, reset compromised credentials.
  • •Cisco tools like ISE, ASA/FTD, Umbrella, and Secure Endpoint are vital here.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize containment to prevent spread, then meticulously eradicate the threat to ensure it's completely gone.
