What is a SOC?

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a centralized unit responsible for monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cybersecurity incidents.

  • •Leverages integrated Cisco tools for comprehensive visibility and control.
  • •Shifts security from reactive to proactive through continuous vigilance.
  • •Ensures a coordinated and effective defense strategy against evolving threats.
Key Takeaway

The SOC is the nerve center, translating threat intelligence into actionable defense and response.
