Key Concepts: Foundational Principles

Reinforcing Core Security Understanding

Confidentiality

The principle of preventing unauthorized disclosure of information to maintain its secrecy.

Integrity

Ensuring information has not been altered or destroyed in an unauthorized manner, guaranteeing accuracy.

Availability

Ensuring timely and reliable access to information and resources for authorized users.

Summary Takeaway

The CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) represents the three core pillars of information security, guiding all efforts to protect data and systems.
