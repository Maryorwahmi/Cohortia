Core Principle

Ensuring System Availability

Reliable Access to Information and Resources

What is Availability?

Availability ensures that authorized users can access information and resources when and where they need them, keeping systems operational and responsive.

  • Redundancy: Duplicates critical components to prevent single points of failure.
  • High Availability (HA): Provides automatic failover for critical systems.
  • Load Balancing: Distributes traffic to prevent overload and ensure responsiveness.
Common Pitfall

Neglecting disaster recovery planning. Even with high availability, a catastrophic event can take systems offline if a proper recovery plan isn't in place.

Cisco Implementation Example

Cisco technologies are key to maintaining availability:

  • Redundant Hardware: Dual power supplies, supervisor engines in switches.
  • Cisco ASA Active/Standby HA: Ensures firewall continuity during failures.
  • DDoS Protection (e.g., Cisco Umbrella): Mitigates denial-of-service attacks to keep services online.
Key Takeaway

Availability is critical for business continuity. Cisco solutions like HA, redundancy, and DDoS protection ensure reliable access to network resources for authorized users.
