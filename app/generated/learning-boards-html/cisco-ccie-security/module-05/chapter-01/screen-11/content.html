Key Factors

Considerations for Deployment

  • •**Risk Tolerance:** How critical is immediate prevention versus avoiding service disruption?
  • •**Budget & Resources:** Cost of deployment, management, and staffing for alert review.
  • •**Specific Threats:** Are you targeting known exploits, internal threats, or sophisticated attacks?
  • •**Network Uptime:** Is preserving network availability paramount, even at the cost of immediate blocking?
Pro-Tip

Consider deploying an IPS initially in 'monitor-only' (IDS) mode to fine-tune rules and minimize false positives before enabling active prevention.

Deployment Scenarios

When to Choose Which

Choose IDS When...
  • •Uptime is critical.
  • •Human review is feasible.
  • •Need forensic data.
Choose IPS When...
  • •Immediate prevention is vital.
  • •High risk tolerance for false positives.
  • •Automated response is preferred.
Key Takeaway

The optimal IDS/IPS strategy is a balance between detection, prevention, performance, and risk, often involving a hybrid approach or careful tuning of IPS in monitor-only mode.
