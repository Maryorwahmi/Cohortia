Anomaly vs. Signature:
Two Detection Philosophies

Explore how anomaly-based detection differs from traditional signature-based methods in identifying threats.

Signature-Based

Known Threat Patterns

  • •Relies on databases of known attack signatures.
  • •Effective for identifying previously documented malware and exploits.
  • •Low false positive rate for known threats.
  • •Cannot detect novel or zero-day attacks.
Analogy

Like a security guard checking IDs against a list of known suspects.

Anomaly-Based

Deviations from Normal

  • •Identifies activity significantly outside an established baseline.
  • •Capable of detecting novel, zero-day, and polymorphic attacks.
  • •Higher potential for false positives.
  • •Requires a learning phase to establish 'normal'.
Key Takeaway

Anomaly detection complements signature-based systems by providing a crucial layer of defense against unknown and evolving threats.
