Navigating the Downsides:
False Positives & Evasion

Understand the significant challenges of anomaly-based detection, including alert fatigue and sophisticated evasion techniques.

Key Disadvantages

  • •High propensity for **false positives**, leading to alert fatigue.
  • •**Dynamic 'normal' behavior** makes tuning complex and continuous.
  • •Vulnerable to **'slow and low' attacks** that blend into the baseline.
  • •Susceptible to **'mimicry attacks'** where malicious activity imitates normal.
  • •Initial learning phase is **time-consuming** and leaves the system vulnerable.
Example Challenge

A legitimate software update causes a server to download large files from new sources, triggering numerous false positive alerts and distracting security analysts.

Evasion Techniques

Slow & Low Attacks
Gradual Adaptation to Baseline
Mimicry Attacks
Blends Malicious Activity with Normal
Key Takeaway

Managing false positives and defending against sophisticated evasion tactics are critical operational challenges for anomaly detection systems.
