The Power of Anomaly Detection:
Catching the Unknown

Explore the significant advantages of anomaly-based detection, particularly its ability to identify novel and zero-day attacks.

Key Advantages

  • •Detects **novel or zero-day attacks** that lack known signatures.
  • •Effective against **polymorphic malware** and constantly evolving attack vectors.
  • •Identifies **internal threats** like misuse of resources or unauthorized access.
  • •Provides a **proactive defense** layer against unpredictable threats.
Real-World Impact

A new ransomware variant, never seen before, attempts to encrypt files. Its unusual file access patterns trigger an anomaly alert, preventing widespread damage.

Anomaly Detection vs. Zero-Day

Zero-Day Exploit Occurs
Causes Unusual System/Network Behavior
Anomaly Detection Flags Deviation
Alert Generated for Investigation
Key Takeaway

Anomaly detection provides a critical defense against unknown threats, offering a proactive security posture where signature-based systems fall short.
