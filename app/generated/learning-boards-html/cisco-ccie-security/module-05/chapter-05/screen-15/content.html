Key Concepts

Essential terms for IDS/IPS management.

Alert Fatigue

A state where security analysts are overwhelmed by a large volume of alerts, making it difficult to identify and respond to real threats.

Rule Management

The process of enabling, disabling, customizing, and updating the rules or signatures used by an IDS/IPS.

Whitelisting/Exceptions

Configuring the IDS/IPS to ignore specific legitimate traffic patterns or sources that would otherwise trigger alerts.

Summary Takeaway

Effective IDS/IPS management hinges on understanding and mitigating alert fatigue, diligently managing rules, and implementing whitelisting to ensure accurate and efficient threat detection.
