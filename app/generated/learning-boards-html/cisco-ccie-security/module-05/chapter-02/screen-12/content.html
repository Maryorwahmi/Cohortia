Signature-Based Detection in Cisco Firepower

Leveraging Talos intelligence for robust threat defense.

Cisco Firepower Threat Defense (FTD)

FTD devices integrate firewall and IPS capabilities, with signature-based detection as a core mechanism for identifying known threats.

Cisco Talos Threat Intelligence

Talos is Cisco's leading threat intelligence organization, continuously researching and developing new signatures for Firepower products.

IPS Policy & Actions

IPS policies on Firepower devices activate specific Talos signatures and define actions (alert, drop, reset) upon detection, ensuring tailored protection.

Integrated Threat Defense

Cisco Firepower leverages Talos's extensive signature database to provide robust, integrated threat detection and prevention capabilities.

Key Takeaway

Cisco Firepower Threat Defense relies on Cisco Talos's continuously updated signature database to provide integrated and effective signature-based threat detection.
