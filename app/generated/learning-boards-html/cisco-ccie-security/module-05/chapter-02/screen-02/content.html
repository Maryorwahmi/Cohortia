Explain Principles

Articulate the core concepts of signature-based detection, including how pattern matching works to identify threats. This skill is foundational for understanding IDS/IPS operations.

02

Identify Signature Types

Recognize and differentiate between common signature types, such as string, header, port/protocol, and stateful signatures. This helps in understanding diverse threat detection methods.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Describe Pros & Cons

Outline the key advantages and disadvantages of signature-based detection, including its effectiveness against known threats versus its limitations with zero-day exploits.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Understand Database Updates

Grasp the importance of maintaining and regularly updating signature databases to ensure protection against the latest threats and vulnerabilities.
