Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Promiscuous Mode

NIDS deployment where the sensor passively monitors a copy of network traffic without being in the direct data path.

Inline Mode

NIPS deployment where the device is placed directly in the path of network traffic, inspecting and potentially blocking traffic in real-time.

High Availability (HA)

Deploying redundant devices (e.g., active/standby pairs) to ensure continuous operation and minimize downtime in case of failure.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding these core deployment models and resilience strategies is fundamental for designing effective and robust IDS/IPS solutions.
