Optimal Sensor Placement: Perimeter and DMZ

Protecting Entry Points and Public-Facing Assets

Why These Locations Matter

The perimeter and DMZ are high-risk areas that require robust IDS/IPS coverage to prevent external attacks and monitor exposed services.

  • Perimeter: Intercepts threats before they reach the internal network.
  • DMZ: Protects public-facing servers from direct internet attacks.
  • Early Detection: Catches malicious activity at the earliest possible stage.
Key Takeaway

Prioritizing IDS/IPS deployment at the perimeter and DMZ establishes a strong initial defense against the most common external threats.

Example Scenario

An organization places an inline NIPS behind its perimeter firewall to block known malware. It also deploys a promiscuous NIDS in the DMZ to monitor web server traffic for unusual patterns, providing visibility without disrupting service.

Perimeter & DMZ Placement

Perimeter Placement

NIPS behind firewall to block external threats before they reach internal systems.

DMZ Placement
