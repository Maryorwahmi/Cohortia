Optimal Sensor Placement: Internal and Core Networks

Detecting Lateral Movement and Protecting Critical Assets

Why Internal Monitoring is Essential

Even with strong perimeter defenses, internal threats and lateral movement by attackers necessitate deep network visibility.

  • Internal Network Segments: Protects critical assets and detects lateral movement.
  • Core Network: Monitors high-traffic backbone links for widespread threats.
  • Layered Defense: Complements perimeter security for comprehensive coverage.
Key Takeaway

Internal and core network IDS/IPS deployments are crucial for detecting sophisticated attacks that bypass initial defenses and for protecting an organization's most valuable data.

Pro Tip

When monitoring internal segments, consider using a combination of NIDS for network-wide visibility and HIDS for granular protection on critical servers.

Internal & Core Network Placement

Internal Network Segments

Monitors traffic between VLANs/subnets to detect lateral movement and protect critical assets.

Core Network

Monitors high-traffic backbone links, requiring high-performance sensors for broad visibility.
