Cisco Firepower: Passive Mode, High Availability, and Bypass

Ensuring Resilience and Flexibility in Deployment

Key Features for Robust FTD Deployment

These features provide operational flexibility and ensure continuous security enforcement even in challenging scenarios.

  • Passive Mode: Detection-only monitoring via SPAN port.
  • High Availability (HA): Active/Standby pairs for continuous operation.
  • Bypass Mechanism: Ensures network connectivity during failure or maintenance.
Key Takeaway

Implementing passive mode for tuning, HA for uptime, and bypass for resilience are essential practices for a production-grade Cisco Firepower deployment.

Common Mistake

Under-provisioning hardware for an inline IPS can lead to performance bottlenecks. Always ensure adequate resources and a robust bypass mechanism.

FTD Advanced Deployment Concepts

Passive Mode

FTD monitors SPAN traffic for detection only, without blocking. Useful for tuning and non-blocking environments.

High Availability (HA)

Active/Standby pairs ensure continuous protection and minimize downtime in case of device failure.

Bypass Mechanism

Allows traffic to flow around a failed or offline inline IPS, preventing network disruption.
