Traditional Network Architecture

Perimeter-Based Security

Securing the boundary between internal and external networks

Core Concept

This model focuses on securing the boundary between an organization's internal network and the outside world (the internet), typically with a strong firewall at the edge.

Key Assumption

Everything inside the network perimeter is trusted; everything outside is untrusted.

Limitations

Vulnerable to insider threats and lateral movement once the perimeter is breached; struggles with cloud and mobile workforces.

Key Takeaway

While foundational, perimeter-based security alone is insufficient for modern threats due to its inherent trust in internal networks.
