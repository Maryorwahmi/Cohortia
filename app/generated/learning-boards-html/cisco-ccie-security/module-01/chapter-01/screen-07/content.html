Integrity: Preserving Data Accuracy

Guaranteeing Information Remains Unaltered and Complete

What is Integrity?

Integrity ensures that information remains accurate, complete, and unaltered throughout its lifecycle, preventing unauthorized or accidental modification.

Example Scenario

A bank transaction amount being changed mid-transfer by an attacker.

Key Technologies

Hashing algorithms and digital signatures verify data authenticity.

Practical Application

Verifying software updates haven't been tampered with before installation.

Impact of Failure

Leads to incorrect data, system instability, and loss of trust.

Detection & Prevention

Mechanisms like checksums, digital signatures, and access controls are used to detect and prevent unauthorized modifications. Regular audits and version control systems also play a crucial role in maintaining data integrity over time.

Key Takeaway

Integrity is fundamental for trustworthy data, ensuring its accuracy and preventing unauthorized changes through cryptographic and access control measures.
