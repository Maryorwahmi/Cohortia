Navigating the Threat Landscape

Categorizing Common Attacks and Vulnerabilities

Understanding Threats

Network threats are constantly evolving attempts to compromise the CIA Triad. Categorizing them helps in developing targeted and effective defense strategies.

Malware

Malicious software like viruses, worms, and ransomware.

Phishing

Deceptive attempts to trick users into revealing sensitive info.

DoS/DDoS

Overwhelming systems to make them unavailable.

MITM

Intercepting and potentially altering communication.

Evolving Risks

The threat landscape is dynamic, with new vulnerabilities and attack methods emerging constantly. Staying informed about zero-day exploits and recognizing the unique challenges posed by insider threats are crucial for maintaining a strong security posture.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive understanding of diverse network threats is fundamental for designing and implementing effective, adaptive security measures.
