Deceptive Attacks: Malware and Phishing

Understanding Software-Based and Social Engineering Threats

Malware

Malicious software designed to infiltrate and damage computer systems or networks. Includes viruses, worms, Trojans, ransomware, and spyware.

  • **Impact:** Data corruption, system control, information theft.
  • **Defense:** Antivirus, endpoint detection, regular patching.
Example

Ransomware encrypts files, demanding payment, impacting data availability and potentially confidentiality.

Phishing

An attack attempting to trick users into revealing sensitive information, often through deceptive emails or websites mimicking legitimate sources.

  • **Impact:** Credential theft, financial fraud, system access.
  • **Defense:** User awareness training, email filters, multi-factor authentication.
Key Takeaway

Both malware and phishing are primary vectors for initial compromise, requiring a combination of technical controls and strong user education.
