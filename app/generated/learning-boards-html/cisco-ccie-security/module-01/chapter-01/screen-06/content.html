Confidentiality: Keeping Secrets Safe

Ensuring Data Access for Authorized Users Only

What is Confidentiality?

Confidentiality ensures that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized individuals, preventing unauthorized disclosure or eavesdropping.

Example Scenario

Customer credit card numbers transmitted during an e-commerce transaction.

Key Technology

Encryption scrambles data, making it unreadable without the correct key.

Common Misconception

Assuming data is confidential just because it's behind a firewall.

Why it Matters

Prevents financial fraud, identity theft, and protects intellectual property.

Access Control & Data Protection

Implementing strong access controls, such as authentication and authorization mechanisms, is critical. Data classification helps identify sensitive information, allowing for appropriate protection measures like encryption, secure storage, and strict handling policies.

Key Takeaway

Confidentiality is achieved through a combination of encryption, robust access controls, and careful data handling practices.
