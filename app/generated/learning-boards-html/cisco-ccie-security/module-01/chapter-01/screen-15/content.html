Key Concepts: Threats and Defense

Understanding Adversaries and Strategic Protection

CIA Triad

The three core principles of information security: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.

Malware

Malicious software designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to computer systems.

Defense-in-Depth

A layered security strategy that employs multiple security mechanisms to protect assets.

Chapter Summary

Understanding the CIA Triad, common threats like Malware, and strategic defenses like Defense-in-Depth are crucial for building robust network security.
