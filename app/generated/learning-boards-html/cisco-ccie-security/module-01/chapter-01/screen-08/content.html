Availability: Always On, Always Accessible

Ensuring Reliable Access to Systems and Data

What is Availability?

Availability ensures that authorized users can access systems and data when needed, focusing on uptime, reliable access, and resilience against disruptions.

Example Scenario

A critical Cisco router providing internet access to an entire campus.

Key Strategies

Redundant systems, load balancing, and robust disaster recovery plans.

Common Mistakes

Neglecting redundancy or insufficient bandwidth for peak loads.

Threats Addressed

Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks and hardware failures.

Ensuring Continuous Operation

High availability is achieved by designing systems with no single point of failure. This involves using redundant hardware, implementing failover mechanisms, and distributing traffic across multiple servers or network paths to ensure continuous service delivery.

Key Takeaway

Availability is critical for business operations, requiring robust design, redundancy, and protection against disruptions to ensure continuous access.
