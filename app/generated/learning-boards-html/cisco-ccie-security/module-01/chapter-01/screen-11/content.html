Disrupting Communications: DoS, DDoS, and MITM

Attacks Aimed at Service Disruption and Interception

DoS & DDoS Attacks

Denial-of-Service (DoS) and Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks aim to overwhelm a system or network resource, making it unavailable to legitimate users.

  • **Impact:** Service outage, financial loss, reputational damage.
  • **Defense:** Traffic filtering, rate limiting, CDN services, robust infrastructure.
Example

A website flooded with traffic from a botnet, rendering it inaccessible to customers.

Man-in-the-Middle (MITM)

An attack where an attacker intercepts communication between two parties without their knowledge, potentially eavesdropping or altering the data.

  • **Impact:** Data theft, credential compromise, data manipulation.
  • **Defense:** HTTPS, VPNs, strong authentication, certificate pinning.
Key Takeaway

DoS/DDoS attacks target availability, while MITM attacks compromise confidentiality and integrity, both requiring robust network and communication security.
