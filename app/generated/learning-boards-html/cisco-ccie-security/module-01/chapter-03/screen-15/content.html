Essential Cryptography Terms

Core Ideas to Remember

Hashing

A one-way function that transforms data into a fixed-size string (hash value) to verify data integrity (e.g., SHA-256).

Digital Signature

A cryptographic mechanism used to verify the authenticity and integrity of a message or document, providing non-repudiation.

PKI & Digital Certificate

PKI is a system for managing digital certificates, which bind public keys to identities, issued by a Certificate Authority (CA).

Summary Takeaway

Hashing ensures data integrity, digital signatures provide authenticity and non-repudiation, and PKI with digital certificates establishes a trusted framework for public key management.
