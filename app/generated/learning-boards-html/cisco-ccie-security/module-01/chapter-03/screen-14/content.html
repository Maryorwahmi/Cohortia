Essential Cryptography Terms

Core Ideas to Remember

Encryption

The process of converting readable information into a coded format to prevent unauthorized access.

Symmetric Encryption

An encryption method using a single, shared secret key for both encryption and decryption (e.g., AES).

Asymmetric Encryption

Uses a pair of mathematically linked keys (public and private) for encryption and decryption (e.g., RSA).

Summary Takeaway

Encryption is the core of data confidentiality, with symmetric methods offering speed and asymmetric methods providing secure key exchange and digital signatures.
