Digital Signatures: Proving Identity and Integrity

Combining Hashing and Asymmetric Encryption for Trust

Digital Signature Workflow

Sender's Process

1

Hash the Message: Sender computes a hash (message digest) of the original message.

2

Encrypt Hash with Private Key: Sender encrypts the hash with their own private key to create the digital signature.

3

Send Message + Signature: Sender transmits the original message along with the digital signature.

Receiver's Process

4

Decrypt Signature with Public Key: Receiver uses sender's public key to decrypt the digital signature, revealing the original hash.

5

Hash Received Message: Receiver independently computes a hash of the received message.

6

Compare Hashes: If the two hash values match, integrity and authenticity are verified.

Key Takeaway

Digital signatures provide robust integrity, authentication, and non-repudiation by combining hashing with asymmetric encryption.
