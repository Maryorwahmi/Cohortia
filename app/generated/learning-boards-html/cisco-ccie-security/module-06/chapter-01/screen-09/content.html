Defining Separation of Duties

Separation of Duties (SoD) is a security principle designed to prevent a single individual from having too much control over a critical process.

Core Idea

Requires multiple individuals to complete a critical task, preventing single points of failure.

Benefit

Helps prevent fraud, errors, and malicious activities by requiring collusion for illicit actions.

Key Takeaway

SoD enhances internal controls and resilience by distributing responsibilities across different roles.

SoD in Practice: Firewall Rule Change

Approver

Network Manager approves the change request.

Implementer

Network Engineer implements the approved rule.

Auditor

Security Auditor verifies the rule's effectiveness and compliance.

Risk of Consolidation

Combining these roles into one person creates a significant risk of error, fraud, or malicious activity.
