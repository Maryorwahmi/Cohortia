Key Concepts: Accounting & Principles

Tracking Actions and Guiding Security Design

Accounting (AuthC)

The process of tracking and logging user activities and resource consumption.

Principle of Least Privilege

Granting only the minimum necessary permissions to perform required tasks.

Separation of Duties

Preventing a single individual from having too much control over a critical process.

Summary Takeaway

Accounting ensures accountability, while Least Privilege and Separation of Duties are vital for designing robust, secure, and resilient IAM systems.
