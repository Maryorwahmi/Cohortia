Key Concepts: AAA Framework

The Pillars of Identity and Access Management

Identity and Access Management (IAM)

A framework of policies and technologies for ensuring appropriate access to technology resources.

Authentication (AuthN)

The process of verifying the identity of a user, device, or service.

Authorization (AuthZ)

The process of determining what an authenticated user or device is permitted to do or access.

Summary Takeaway

IAM, built on Authentication and Authorization, forms the essential framework for controlling and securing access to all network resources.
