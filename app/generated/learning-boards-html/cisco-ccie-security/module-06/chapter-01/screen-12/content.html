Challenges of Local Authentication

While simple for single devices, local authentication presents significant challenges in larger network environments, primarily due to scalability and management overhead.

Scalability Issue

Manual configuration on hundreds of devices is inefficient and error-prone.

Security Risk

Difficult to enforce consistent password policies or quickly revoke access across many devices.

Key Takeaway

For enterprise networks, centralized IAM solutions are essential to overcome the limitations of local authentication.

Why Centralized IAM is Preferred

Centralized Management

Single point of control for all identities and access policies (e.g., RADIUS/TACACS+).

Enhanced Auditing

Comprehensive logging and reporting across the entire network from a central location.

Fallback Use Case

Local authentication is best reserved for isolated devices or as a critical fallback mechanism when centralized systems are unavailable.
