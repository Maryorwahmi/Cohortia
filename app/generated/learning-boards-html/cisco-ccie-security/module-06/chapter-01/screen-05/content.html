What is Authentication?

Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user, device, or service. It confirms that an entity is who or what it claims to be.

Core Question

"Who are you?"

Example Methods

Passwords, digital certificates, biometrics, multi-factor authentication (MFA).

Key Takeaway

Authentication is the essential first step in securing access, preventing unauthorized entities from even entering the system.

Authentication in a Cisco Network

User Login

Verifying credentials for router/switch access.

Device Connection

Authenticating a device attempting to join the network.

Common Pitfall

Weak or easily guessed passwords undermine the entire authentication process, making it vulnerable to brute-force attacks.
