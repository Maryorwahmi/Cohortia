Identity & Access Management

Securing Access in Modern Networks

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts and principles of Identity and Access Management (IAM), a critical component for securing any network infrastructure. We'll explore how IAM ensures that only authorized users and devices can access specific resources, protecting against threats and enabling efficient operations.

Key Takeaway

IAM is the strategic framework that underpins all network security, ensuring controlled and accountable access to resources.
