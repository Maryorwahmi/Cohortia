Key Concepts

Core Ideas for Authorization

Authorization

The process of determining what an authenticated user or device is permitted to do or access.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

An access control model where permissions are associated with roles, and users are assigned to roles.

Role

A collection of permissions that describes a job function or responsibility.

Summary Takeaway

Authorization and RBAC are foundational for secure network operations, enabling precise control over who can do what, where, and when.
