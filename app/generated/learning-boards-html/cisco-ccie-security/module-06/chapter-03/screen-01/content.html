Authorization & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Controlling Access: What Authenticated Users Can Do

After verifying identity, authorization determines precisely what an entity is permitted to do or access. This chapter explores how RBAC simplifies this critical security function, ensuring granular control and policy enforcement across your network.

Key Idea

Authorization ensures that even legitimate users operate within their defined scope, preventing unauthorized actions and maintaining network integrity.
