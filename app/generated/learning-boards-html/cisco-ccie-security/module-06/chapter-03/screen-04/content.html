What is Authorization?

The Gatekeeper of Network Access

Definition

Authorization is the process of determining what an authenticated entity is permitted to do or access within a network. It's the enforcement of security policies.

Analogy: The Key Card System

Your key card authenticates you at the main entrance, but authorization determines which specific floors or rooms you can enter. It's about 'what you can do', not just 'who you are'.

  • •Enforces security policies after authentication.
  • •Prevents unauthorized actions by legitimate users.
  • •Crucial for data confidentiality and network integrity.
Key Takeaway

Authorization is the critical step that defines and enforces access privileges, ensuring users only interact with resources they are explicitly allowed to.
