Access Control Model

Introducing Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Simplifying Access Management at Scale

Core Principle

Instead of assigning permissions directly to individual users, RBAC assigns permissions to specific roles, and users are then assigned to these roles.

Analogy: Job Titles

Just as a 'Network Engineer' job title comes with specific responsibilities, an RBAC 'Network_Engineer' role comes with predefined access permissions.

  • •Abstracts permissions from individual users.
  • •Highly scalable for large organizations.
  • •Reduces administrative overhead.
Key Takeaway

RBAC is a powerful model that uses roles as an intermediary to manage permissions, making access control more efficient and less error-prone in complex environments.
