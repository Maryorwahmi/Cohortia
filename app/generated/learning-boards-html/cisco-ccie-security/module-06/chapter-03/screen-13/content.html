Common Authorization Pitfalls

Avoiding Security Vulnerabilities

Overly Broad Permissions

Violates least privilege, increases attack surface.

Stale Roles & Permissions

Leads to 'privilege creep' as job functions change.

Principle of Least Privilege

Always grant only the minimum necessary access for a user or role to perform its function.

Regular Audits

Periodically review all roles and their assigned permissions to ensure they remain relevant and secure.

Key Takeaway

Avoiding overly permissive access and actively managing role lifecycles are crucial for preventing security vulnerabilities and maintaining a strong authorization posture.
