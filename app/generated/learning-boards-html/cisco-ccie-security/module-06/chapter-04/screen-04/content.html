Understanding Accounting in AAA

The final pillar for tracking network activities and resource usage.

What is Accounting?

Accounting is the process of collecting and recording information about user and device activities, resource consumption, and network events. It answers the crucial question, "What did you do?"

Key Benefit

Provides forensic data for security incident investigations.

Compliance Aid

Helps demonstrate adherence to regulatory standards (e.g., PCI DSS, HIPAA).

Operational Insight

Supports capacity planning and resource utilization analysis.

Accountability

Ensures users and devices are held responsible for their actions.

Key Takeaway

Accounting is essential for comprehensive network visibility, enabling security, compliance, and effective incident response by tracking all activities.
