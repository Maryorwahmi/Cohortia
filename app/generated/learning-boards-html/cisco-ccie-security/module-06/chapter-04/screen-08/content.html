Security Assurance

The Critical Role of Auditing

Systematically examining records to ensure compliance and identify vulnerabilities.

Auditing's Purpose

  • •Evaluate Security Posture: Assess the effectiveness of existing security controls.
  • •Verify Compliance: Confirm adherence to regulatory requirements (e.g., PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR).
  • •Identify Vulnerabilities: Uncover weaknesses or misconfigurations that could lead to breaches.
  • •Support Incident Response: Provide independent verification of events during investigations.
Key Takeaway

Auditing is the systematic review of accounting records and security controls, essential for validating security effectiveness, ensuring compliance, and proactively identifying risks.
