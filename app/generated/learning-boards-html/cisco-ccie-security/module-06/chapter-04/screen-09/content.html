Avoiding Mistakes

Preventing "Log Noise" and Data Disasters

Strategies for effective logging and audit readiness.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • •Log Noise: Generating excessive, unanalyzed data that obscures real threats.
  • •Insecure Storage: Logs vulnerable to tampering or deletion, compromising forensic evidence.
  • •Lack of Analysis: Collecting data without tools (like SIEMs) to make it actionable.
  • •No NTP Sync: Inconsistent timestamps hindering event correlation across devices.
Key Takeaway

A strategic approach to logging, including secure storage, analysis tools, and NTP synchronization, is vital to prevent log noise and ensure actionable security intelligence.
