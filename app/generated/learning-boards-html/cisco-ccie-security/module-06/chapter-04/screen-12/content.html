Synchronizing Clocks with NTP for Accurate Logs

Verifying accounting records on the TACACS+ server.

Why NTP is Essential

  • •Accurate Timestamps: Ensures all log entries have precise time data.
  • •Event Correlation: Critical for piecing together events across multiple devices during investigations.
  • •Forensic Integrity: Reliable timestamps are non-negotiable for legal and compliance purposes.
Key Takeaway

NTP synchronization is paramount for accurate log correlation and forensic analysis; always verify your accounting logs on the centralized server.
