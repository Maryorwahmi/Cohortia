Avoiding Common ISE Configuration Pitfalls

Ensuring Smooth and Secure Operations

Common Mistakes

  • Mismatched Shared Secret: Leading cause of RADIUS authentication failures between NADs and ISE.
  • Incorrect Policy Order: Rules processed top-down; specific rules must be above general ones to avoid unintended matches.
  • Incomplete NAD Configuration: Forgetting to enable AAA on the NAD or pointing it to the wrong ISE PSN.
  • Certificate Issues: Invalid or expired certificates causing secure communication failures.

Best Practices for ISE Deployment

  • Thorough Testing: Always test policies with a small group before wider deployment.
  • Granular Rules: Design specific rules for specific access needs, following the principle of least privilege.
  • Secure ISE Nodes: Protect administrative access to ISE with strong authentication and regularly patch nodes.
  • Monitor Logs: Regularly check ISE live logs and reports for authentication failures and policy hits.
Key Takeaway

Successful ISE implementation requires meticulous attention to detail, especially with shared secrets and policy order. Adhering to best practices and thorough testing are crucial for a secure and reliable deployment.
