Implementing WAF Effectively

A Step-by-Step Approach to Robust WAF Deployment

01

Understand Application Traffic

Analyze your application's traffic patterns and identify potential vulnerabilities to inform your WAF configuration.

02

Deploy in Detection Mode

Start by deploying WAF in Detection Mode with an enabled managed rule set (e.g., OWASP Top 10) to monitor without blocking.

03

Monitor WAF Logs Closely

Regularly review WAF logs (Azure Monitor, Log Analytics) for alerts and identify any false positives that may occur.

04

Fine-tune Rules & Exclusions

Investigate false positives and create exclusions or fine-tune rule parameters to ensure legitimate traffic is not blocked.

05

Switch to Prevention Mode

Once confident in your WAF configuration, switch the policy to Prevention Mode to actively block detected threats.

06

Regularly Review & Update

Continuously review WAF logs and update rules as your application evolves or new threats emerge to maintain optimal security.

Key Takeaway

Effective WAF implementation is an iterative process involving careful planning, phased deployment (detection then prevention), continuous monitoring, and regular rule updates to adapt to evolving threats.
