Reviewing Risk Detections and Risky Sign-ins

Observing the impact of your new report-only policies.

1

Allow Processing Time

Wait a few minutes for the newly configured policies to process any recent activity.

2

Review Risk Detections Report

Navigate to the 'Risk detections' report to see individual suspicious events flagged by your policies.

3

Examine Risky Sign-ins Report

Check the 'Risky sign-ins' report to see which sign-in attempts would have been blocked.

4

Fine-Tune & Enforce

Use the gathered data to adjust policies and eventually switch them to 'On' (enforced) mode.

Key Takeaway

Reviewing reports in report-only mode is crucial for validating policy effectiveness and preventing unintended disruptions before full enforcement.
