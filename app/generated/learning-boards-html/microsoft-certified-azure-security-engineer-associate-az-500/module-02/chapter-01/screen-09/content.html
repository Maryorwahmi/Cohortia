Preparing for Identity Protection Policies

Navigating the Azure portal to begin configuration.

1

Understand the Scenario

Configure policies for 'High' user risk (password change) and 'High' sign-in risk (block access), both in report-only mode.

2

Navigate to Azure Portal

Log in to the Azure portal (portal.azure.com) with appropriate administrative credentials.

3

Search for Identity Protection

Use the search bar at the top of the portal to find and select 'Azure AD Identity Protection'.

Key Takeaway

The initial steps involve understanding the lab's objectives and correctly navigating to the Azure AD Identity Protection service in the portal.
