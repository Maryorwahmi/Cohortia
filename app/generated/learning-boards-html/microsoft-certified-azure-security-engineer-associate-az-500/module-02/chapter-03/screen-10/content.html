Tracking PIM Activities and Break-Glass Accounts

Maintaining an audit trail and securing emergency access.

PIM Audit Logs

Detailed records of all PIM activities, providing a comprehensive trail for security investigations and compliance requirements.

  • What's logged: Role assignments, activations, access review decisions.
  • Purpose: Accountability, compliance, security forensics.
  • Benefit: Unalterable record of privileged access usage.

Example Use:

Reviewing logs to identify who activated the 'Global Administrator' role during a suspected security incident.

Break-Glass Accounts

Permanently assigned Global Administrator accounts excluded from PIM, serving as an emergency access mechanism.

  • Purpose: Prevent complete lockout in emergencies.
  • Configuration: At least two accounts, highly secured, outside PIM.
  • Caution: Bypasses JIT; requires extreme vigilance and monitoring.

Security Best Practice:

Treat break-glass accounts with the highest level of security, including physical access controls and strict monitoring.

Key Takeaway

Audit logs provide essential accountability and compliance data, while carefully managed break-glass accounts ensure resilience against lockout scenarios.
