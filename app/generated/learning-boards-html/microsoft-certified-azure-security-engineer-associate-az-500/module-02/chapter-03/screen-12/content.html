Lab: Activating and Auditing a PIM Role

Experiencing the JIT activation process and reviewing audit history.

Scenario: Activate Role and Review Audit Logs

  1. Now, to activate the role:
    • Navigate back to the PIM blade and select "My roles" -> "Azure AD roles."
    • Find the "User Administrator" role listed under "Eligible assignments."
    • Click "Activate."
    • Provide a justification (e.g., "Performing user management tasks").
    • Click "Activate."
  2. After activation is complete (it might take a minute), navigate to "Azure AD Privileged Identity Management" -> "Azure AD roles" -> "Audit history" to view your role activation event.

Checkpoint: Activation & Audit

Upon successful activation, you will temporarily hold the 'User Administrator' permissions. In the audit history, you should find a record of your activation request, including the justification provided.

Why this matters:

This demonstrates the full JIT access workflow and the critical auditability PIM provides for compliance and security investigations.

Key Takeaway

Activating an eligible role provides temporary, justified access, and the audit logs ensure full accountability for all privileged operations within PIM.
