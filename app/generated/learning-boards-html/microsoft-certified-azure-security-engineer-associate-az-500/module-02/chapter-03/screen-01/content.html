Manage Azure AD Privileged Identity Management (PIM)

Secure your Azure AD roles with Just-in-Time access and robust governance.

This chapter will guide you through implementing Azure AD PIM to minimize standing access, enforce time-bound permissions, and enhance the security posture of your cloud environment. You'll learn how to configure, activate, and review privileged roles effectively.

Key Idea

PIM reduces the time privileged accounts are active, significantly lowering the risk of exploitation.
