Eligible vs. Active: The PIM Distinction

Differentiating between potential and current role permissions.

Eligible Assignment

A user is qualified to activate a role but does not have its permissions until they explicitly request and complete the activation process.

  • Status: Inactive permissions.
  • Requirement: Requires activation to gain access.
  • Benefit: Reduces standing access and attack surface.

Analogy:

Having a driver's license, but not currently driving a car. You have the qualification, but not the active state of driving.

Active Assignment

A user currently holds the permissions of a role, typically for a limited duration after successfully completing the role activation process.

  • Status: Active permissions.
  • Requirement: Time-bound; automatically deactivates.
  • Benefit: Enables JIT access for specific tasks.

Analogy:

Actively driving a car. You have the immediate control and permissions for a specific journey.

Key Takeaway

Understanding the difference between eligible and active assignments is fundamental to implementing and managing PIM effectively, ensuring permissions are granted only when and for as long as needed.
