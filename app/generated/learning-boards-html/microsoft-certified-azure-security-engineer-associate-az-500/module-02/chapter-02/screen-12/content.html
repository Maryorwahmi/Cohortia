Hands-on: Creating an MFA Policy (Part 1)

Navigating to Conditional Access and defining policy scope.

Scenario Overview

Require MFA for all users accessing Azure management portal from outside the corporate network, excluding an 'Emergency Access' group.

  • Target: Azure management portal.
  • Condition: Outside corporate network.
  • Control: Require MFA.
  • Exclusion: Emergency Access group.
Checkpoint

Ensure you have at least two emergency access accounts configured and ready for exclusion.

Step-by-Step: Policy Initialization

1

Navigate to Azure portal and search for "Conditional Access".

2

Click "New policy" and name it: "MFA for Azure Portal - External Access".

3

Under "Assignments" > "Users or workload identities": Include "All users".

4

Exclude: Select "Users and groups" and choose your "Emergency Access" group.

Key Takeaway

Properly defining the policy's name and user scope, including critical exclusions, is the foundational step for any Conditional Access policy.
