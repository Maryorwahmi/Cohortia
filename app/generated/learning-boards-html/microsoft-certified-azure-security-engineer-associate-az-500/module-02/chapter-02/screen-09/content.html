Planning & Implementation: Avoiding Common Pitfalls

Strategic considerations for successful policy deployment.

Key Planning Steps

  • Define Scope: Clearly identify target users, groups, and applications.
  • Identify Conditions: Determine relevant device, location, and risk factors.
  • Choose Controls: Select appropriate grant and session controls.
  • Document Policies: Maintain clear records of policy purpose and configuration.
Pro Tip

Always start new policies in 'Report-only' mode to monitor their impact without enforcing them, preventing unexpected access issues.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Widespread Lockouts: Targeting 'All users' without proper exclusions.
  • Overlapping Policies: Conflicting policies leading to unpredictable access outcomes.
  • Insufficient Testing: Deploying policies without validating their impact on various scenarios.
  • Ignoring Emergency Accounts: Not having excluded accounts for break-glass scenarios.
Misconception

Thinking more policies automatically means more security. Complex, overlapping policies can create vulnerabilities or block legitimate users.

Key Takeaway

Effective Conditional Access implementation relies on meticulous planning, strategic exclusions, and rigorous testing to ensure security without disrupting legitimate access.
