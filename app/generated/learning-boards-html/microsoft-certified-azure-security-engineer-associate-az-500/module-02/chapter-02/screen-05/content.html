Policy Structure

Building Blocks: Assignments - Who & What

Defining the scope of your Conditional Access policy.

1. Users and Groups (Who)

Specify which users or groups the policy applies to. This is the primary target for your access controls.

  • Include: Select 'All users', specific users, or groups.
  • Exclude: Crucial for emergency access accounts to prevent lockouts.
Important Note

Always exclude at least two emergency access accounts from all Conditional Access policies.

2. Cloud Apps or Actions (What)

Define the specific cloud applications or user actions that the policy will protect.

  • Cloud Apps: Target specific applications like Microsoft 365, Azure portal, or SaaS apps.
  • User Actions: Apply policies to actions like 'Register security information' or 'Register or join devices'.
Example Scenario

Target administrators accessing the Azure management portal to enforce stricter controls.

Key Takeaway

Careful definition of users, groups, and target applications/actions is the foundation of an effective Conditional Access policy, requiring precision and strategic exclusions.
