Definition

What is Data Governance?

Data governance is a comprehensive approach that encompasses the entire lifecycle of data, from its creation and storage to its use, sharing, and eventual deletion. It defines policies, roles, and processes to ensure data quality, usability, integrity, and security.

  • Policies: Rules for data handling, access, and retention.
  • Roles: Clear responsibilities for data ownership and management.
  • Processes: Structured workflows for data lifecycle events.
  • Security: Protecting data from unauthorized access and breaches.
  • Compliance: Adhering to legal and regulatory requirements.
Why it Matters

Ensures sensitive data is identified, classified, protected, and retained only as long as necessary, minimizing risk and ensuring regulatory compliance.

Key Takeaway

Data governance is a strategic framework that integrates security, compliance, and operational efficiency across the data lifecycle.
