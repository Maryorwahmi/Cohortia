DLP Policy Enforcement

Detecting and acting on sensitive information types.

DLP Policy Workflow
Detect Sensitive Info
Apply Action (Block/Audit/Notify)
Prevent Data Loss
Example Scenario

A user attempts to upload a document containing patient health information from OneDrive to an unapproved external sharing site. A DLP policy blocks the upload and alerts the security team.

Common Mistake

Overlooking the integration of DLP with Azure-connected services, leaving potential gaps for data exfiltration from VMs or other Azure resources.

Key Takeaway

DLP policies are essential for proactively identifying and preventing sensitive data from leaving your organization, integrating with Microsoft 365 and Azure-connected services.
