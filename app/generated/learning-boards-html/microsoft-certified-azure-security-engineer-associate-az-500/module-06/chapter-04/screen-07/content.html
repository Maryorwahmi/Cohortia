Definition

What is Data Loss Prevention?

Data Loss Prevention (DLP) solutions aim to prevent sensitive information from leaving the organization's control. Primarily found within Microsoft 365, DLP policies identify, monitor, and protect sensitive data across various services.

  • Identify: Detects sensitive information types (e.g., PII, financial data).
  • Monitor: Tracks data movement and sharing activities.
  • Protect: Blocks, audits, or notifies administrators of policy violations.
  • Scope: Covers Microsoft 365 services and endpoints, impacting Azure-connected data.
Common Misconception

DLP is not just about blocking; it also provides auditing and notification capabilities to understand data flow and potential risks.

Key Takeaway

DLP acts as a critical safeguard, preventing sensitive data from being unintentionally or maliciously exfiltrated from your organization's control.
