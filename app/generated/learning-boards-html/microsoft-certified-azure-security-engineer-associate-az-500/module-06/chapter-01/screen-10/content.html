Preventing Accidental Changes

Resource Locks: Safeguarding Critical Azure Assets

Protecting against unintended deletion or modification.

What it is

An additional layer of protection against accidental deletion or modification of critical Azure resources.

CanNotDelete

Prevents anyone from deleting the resource, even administrators, without first removing the lock.

ReadOnly

Prevents any modifications or deletions to the resource, ideal for static configurations.

Example Scenario

Applying a `CanNotDelete` lock to a production database resource group prevents anyone from accidentally deleting the database or any other resource within that group, providing a crucial safety net.

Key Takeaway

Resource Locks are a simple yet powerful mechanism to prevent accidental deletion or modification of critical Azure resources, enhancing resilience and security.
