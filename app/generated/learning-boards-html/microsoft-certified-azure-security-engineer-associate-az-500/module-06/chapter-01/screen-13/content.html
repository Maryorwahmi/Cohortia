Essential Governance Concepts

Reinforcing core ideas for secure Azure management.

Azure Policy

Service for defining and enforcing organizational standards and assessing compliance at scale.

Policy Definition

A rule specifying conditions for resource evaluation and the effect to take if conditions are met.

Policy Assignment

Applying a policy definition or initiative to a specific scope (management group, subscription, or resource group).

Summary Takeaway

Azure Policy, its definitions, and assignments form the foundational pillars for automated compliance and governance in your cloud environment.
