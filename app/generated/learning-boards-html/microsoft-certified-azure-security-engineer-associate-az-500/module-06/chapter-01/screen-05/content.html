Process Flow

Defining and Assigning Azure Policies

Crafting rules and applying them to your Azure scope.

Step 1

Create Policy Definition

Define the desired state or behavior for Azure resources (e.g., 'all storage accounts must use HTTPS').

Step 2

Assign Policy to Scope

Apply the definition to a management group, subscription, or resource group, determining its enforcement area.

Step 3

Continuous Evaluation

Azure Policy continuously monitors resources within the assigned scope for compliance against the defined rules.

Example Scenario

An organization wants to ensure all new virtual machines have a 'CostCenter' tag. A policy definition is created for this rule, then assigned to the 'Development' management group. All new VMs within any subscription under 'Development' will now be evaluated for this tag.

Key Takeaway

Policy definitions establish the rules, and policy assignments determine the scope, ensuring targeted and effective governance across your Azure environment.
