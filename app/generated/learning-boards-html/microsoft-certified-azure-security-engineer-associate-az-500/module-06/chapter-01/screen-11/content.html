Management Groups: Hierarchical Governance at Scale

Applying policies and RBAC across multiple subscriptions.

What it is

A hierarchical container that allows you to organize subscriptions.

Key Benefit

Apply governance policies and RBAC roles at a broader scope, inherited by all contained resources.

Why it matters

Essential for scaling governance in organizations with many subscriptions, ensuring consistency.
