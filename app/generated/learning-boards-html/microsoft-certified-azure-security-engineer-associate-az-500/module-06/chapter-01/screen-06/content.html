Key Mechanism

Azure Policy Effects: Audit, Deny, DeployIfNotExists, Modify

Controlling how non-compliance is handled.

Audit

Logs non-compliance without blocking operations. Ideal for initial assessment.

Deny

Prevents creation or modification of non-compliant resources. Strict enforcement.

DeployIfNotExists

Automatically deploys a compliant resource or configuration if missing.

Modify

Alters a resource's properties to bring it into compliance.

Common Mistake

Starting with `Deny` effects too broadly can inadvertently block legitimate operations. It's often better to begin with `Audit` to understand the impact, then gradually move to `Deny` for critical controls.

Key Takeaway

Choosing the right policy effect is crucial for balancing security enforcement with operational flexibility. Start with `Audit` to assess, then escalate to `Deny` or `DeployIfNotExists` as needed.
