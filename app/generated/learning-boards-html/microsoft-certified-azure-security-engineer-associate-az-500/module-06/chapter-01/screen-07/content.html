Grouping Policies with Initiatives

Simplifying complex compliance requirements.

What it is

A collection of policy definitions grouped together into a single assignment.

How it helps

Simplifies management of complex compliance requirements by applying multiple policies simultaneously.

Example

A 'PCI DSS Compliance' initiative can group policies for data encryption, network segmentation, and access control.
