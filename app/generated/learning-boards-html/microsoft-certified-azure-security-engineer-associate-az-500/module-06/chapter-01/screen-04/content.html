Core Concept

Azure Policy: Your Automated Compliance Officer

Enforcing organizational standards and assessing compliance at scale.

What it is

A service that helps you enforce organizational standards and assess compliance across your Azure environment.

How it works

You define rules (policy definitions) that describe the desired state of resources, and Azure Policy continuously evaluates them.

Why it matters

Ensures consistent security, compliance, and cost management across your entire cloud environment, preventing misconfigurations.
