Lifecycle Phase 3 & 4

Containment and Eradication

Limiting damage and removing the threat.

03

Containment

Actions taken to limit the scope and impact of an incident, preventing further damage or spread. This is a critical immediate response.

  • •Isolate compromised systems
  • •Segment affected networks
  • •Disable compromised accounts
Common Mistake

Rushing to eradication without proper containment can allow attackers to pivot to other systems or cause further damage.

04

Eradication

The process of removing the root cause of the incident and eliminating the threat from the environment. This ensures the incident does not recur.

  • •Clean infected systems
  • •Patch exploited vulnerabilities
  • •Remove malicious software/backdoors
Example Scenario

After containing a malware outbreak, eradication involves deploying updated antivirus definitions and patching the system vulnerability that allowed the initial infection.

Key Takeaway

Containment and eradication are sequential and critical steps to neutralize threats and prevent their recurrence.
