Azure Private Endpoints for Inbound Access

Provide private access to your App Service from within a Virtual Network.

What it Does
  • Creates a private IP address for your App Service in your VNet.
  • Makes the app accessible only from within that VNet or connected networks.
  • Completely removes public internet exposure.
Key Benefits
  • Enhanced security for internal applications.
  • Reduced attack surface.
  • Simplified network architecture.
  • Compliance with private access requirements.
Best Practice

When using private endpoints, always disable public access to the App Service to ensure complete network isolation.

Inbound Flow

Traffic from the VNet accesses the App Service via its private IP.

Key Takeaway

Azure Private Endpoints create a private IP for your App Service within your VNet, ensuring inbound access is restricted to private networks and removing public internet exposure.
