Deep Dive into Azure Private Endpoints

Bringing Storage into Your Private VNet

Private IP in VNet

Private Endpoints assign a private IP address to your storage account directly within your Azure Virtual Network.

No Public Internet Exposure

Enables private and secure connectivity from your VNet and on-premises networks without traversing the public internet.

Common Mistake

Leaving public access enabled on the storage account while using private endpoints can inadvertently expose your data.

Key Takeaway

Private Endpoints offer superior network isolation by integrating storage directly into your VNet's private IP space; always disable public access.
