Core Concept

The Need for External Identities

Extending collaboration beyond your organizational boundaries

Modern Business Reality

Collaboration with partners, vendors, and contractors is essential for growth and innovation.

Security Imperative

Sharing resources securely without compromising internal data or systems is paramount.

Efficiency & Simplicity

Avoid managing duplicate accounts; leverage existing external identities for streamlined access.

Concrete Example

Your marketing team needs to share campaign assets with an external advertising agency. Instead of creating new accounts, external identities allow agency members to use their existing corporate logins for secure access to your SharePoint site.

Key Takeaway

External identities are crucial for enabling secure, efficient, and compliant collaboration with external entities, extending your digital perimeter safely.
