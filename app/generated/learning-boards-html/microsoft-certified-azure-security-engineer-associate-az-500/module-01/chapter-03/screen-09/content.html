B2B vs. B2C: Choosing the Right Solution

Understanding the fundamental differences and use cases

Azure AD B2B

  • Target Audience: Known external users (partners, vendors, contractors).
  • Purpose: Collaboration on internal resources and applications.
  • Identity Source: Users authenticate with their existing corporate or social identities.
  • Tenant Model: Guest users are represented in your corporate Azure AD tenant.
Example Scenario

Inviting a consultant to a Microsoft Teams channel to collaborate on a project.

Azure AD B2C

  • Target Audience: Unknown consumers for public-facing applications.
  • Purpose: Customer sign-up, sign-in, and profile management.
  • Identity Source: Local accounts (email/password) or social identity providers.
  • Tenant Model: A separate, dedicated B2C tenant for customer identities.
Example Scenario

Allowing customers to create accounts and log in to your company's mobile app.

Key Takeaway

The choice between B2B and B2C hinges on whether you're collaborating with known business partners or managing identities for unknown consumers.
