Implementing Administrative Units (AUs)

Delegating granular administrative permissions for specific identity subsets.

What are AUs?

Administrative Units are Azure AD resources that logically group users and groups, allowing for scoped delegation of administrative permissions.

Key Benefits
  • Enforces Principle of Least Privilege at scale.
  • Limits the 'blast radius' of compromised admin accounts.
  • Enables decentralized administration for large organizations.
Key Takeaway

AUs are essential for granular administrative delegation, allowing specific teams to manage only their relevant users and groups.

Example Scenario

A 'Europe IT' team can manage 'Europe Users' AU, but not 'Asia Users' AU, preventing over-privileging.
