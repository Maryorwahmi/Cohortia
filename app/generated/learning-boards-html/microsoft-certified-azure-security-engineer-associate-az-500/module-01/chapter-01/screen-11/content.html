Scenario: Delegating Departmental User Management

Setting up an Administrative Unit for granular control.

The Challenge

Your organization, Cohortia, needs to delegate password reset capabilities for the new 'Cloud Operations' department to a specific 'CloudOps Admin' account, without granting broader permissions.

Why Administrative Units?

AUs allow you to scope administrative roles to a specific subset of users, ensuring the 'CloudOps Admin' can only manage users within their department, adhering to the principle of least privilege.

Key Takeaway

This scenario demonstrates how AUs provide precise, secure delegation, minimizing the risk of over-privileged accounts in complex organizations.
