Definition

Federation with AD FS

Azure AD trusts your on-premises AD FS infrastructure for user authentication, redirecting sign-in requests to AD FS which then authenticates against on-premises AD and issues a security token.

Considerations
  • Most Control: Allows custom authentication policies and third-party MFA.
  • High Infrastructure: Requires AD FS servers and Web Application Proxy (WAP) servers.
  • Increased Complexity: More challenging to deploy and manage.
  • Specific Use Cases: Best for organizations with existing AD FS or unique requirements.
How AD FS Federation Works
Key Takeaway

AD FS Federation offers maximum control over authentication policies and integration, but it demands significant on-premises infrastructure and is more complex to manage than PHS or PTA.
