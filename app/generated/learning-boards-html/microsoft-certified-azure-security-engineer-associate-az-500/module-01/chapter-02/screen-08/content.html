Authentication Method Comparison

Selecting the right Azure AD Connect authentication method is crucial. Each method offers distinct advantages and considerations based on your organization's security, infrastructure, and compliance needs.

PHS (Password Hash Sync)

Pros: Simple, high availability, minimal infrastructure, Seamless SSO. Passwords never sent.

Cons: Password hashes stored in Azure AD.

PTA (Pass-through Auth)

Pros: Passwords stay on-premises, strong security control.

Cons: Dependency on on-premises agents, requires agent management.

Federation (AD FS)

Pros: Most control, custom policies, third-party MFA integration.

Cons: High infrastructure, complex to deploy and manage.

Key Takeaway

Evaluate your organization's specific needs for security, infrastructure, and compliance to choose between PHS for simplicity, PTA for on-premises password validation, or AD FS for maximum control.
