Post-Incident Activity: Learning from Experience

Driving Security Enhancements Through Review

The 'Lessons Learned' Review

  • Understand what happened, why it happened, and how to prevent recurrence.
  • Identify gaps in security controls, processes, and tools.
  • Findings feed back into the 'Preparation' phase.
  • Leads to updates in policies, monitoring, baselines, and playbooks.
Common Mistakes

Failing to document incident details, skipping the post-incident review, or not implementing identified improvements, leaving the organization vulnerable to repeat attacks.

Why Post-Incident Review is Vital

This phase transforms a reactive incident into a proactive opportunity. It ensures that your security posture continuously evolves and strengthens based on real-world threats and experiences, making your organization more resilient over time.

Drives continuous security improvement

Example: Policy Update

A review reveals an incident was caused by weak password policies. The 'lessons learned' lead to an update requiring stronger passwords and mandatory multi-factor authentication for all users, feeding back into the 'Preparation' phase.

Key Takeaway

The post-incident review is essential for continuous improvement, transforming security incidents into valuable learning experiences that strengthen your defenses.
