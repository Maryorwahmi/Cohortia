Investigation, Triage, and Visualization

Sentinel Incident Blade Capabilities

  • Triage incidents: assess severity and priority.
  • Assign incidents to security analysts or teams.
  • Change incident status (e.g., New, Active, Closed).
  • Add comments for documentation and collaboration.
  • Manages the entire incident workflow from detection to resolution.
Pro-Tip

Integrate Sentinel with your existing ticketing system to streamline incident handoffs and tracking across platforms.

The Incident Graph: Visualizing Relationships

The incident graph is a powerful visual tool that displays the relationships between entities (users, IPs, hosts) and events within an incident. It helps analysts understand the scope and impact of an attack, identify lateral movement, and uncover hidden connections.

Quickly grasp attack scope and impact

Example: Visualizing Lateral Movement

An incident graph shows a compromised user account logging into a critical server, then attempting to access a database. This visual flow helps confirm lateral movement and prioritize containment actions.

Key Takeaway

Microsoft Sentinel's incident management features, especially the incident graph, are essential for efficient investigation, triage, and understanding complex attack patterns.
