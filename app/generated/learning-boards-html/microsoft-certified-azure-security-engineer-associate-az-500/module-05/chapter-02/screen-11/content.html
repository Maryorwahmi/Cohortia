Incident Management Lifecycle in Sentinel

The 'Incidents' blade in the Sentinel portal provides a centralized view for managing and responding to security threats.

  • •Review Details: Examine related alerts, entities (users, hosts, IPs), and raw events.
  • •Change Status: Update incident status (New, Active, Closed) to track progress.
  • •Assign & Comment: Assign incidents to team members and add notes for collaboration.
  • •Investigation Graph: Visualize relationships between entities and events to understand attack scope.
Goal of Incident Management

Efficiently triage, investigate, and resolve security incidents to contain and eradicate threats quickly.
