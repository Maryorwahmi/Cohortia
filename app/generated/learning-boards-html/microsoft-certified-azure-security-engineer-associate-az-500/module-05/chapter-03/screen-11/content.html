Grouping Policies

Organizing Policies with Initiatives

Combining multiple policy definitions for comprehensive baselines.

What are Policy Initiatives?

Policy initiatives (also known as policy sets) are collections of policy definitions, both built-in and custom, grouped together to achieve a larger goal or compliance standard.

Benefits of Initiatives

  • **Simplified Management:** Assign multiple policies as a single unit.
  • **Consistent Application:** Ensures all related policies are applied together.
  • **Clearer Compliance View:** Provides a consolidated view against a specific standard (e.g., PCI DSS).
  • **Prevents Policy Sprawl:** Reduces the number of individual assignments to track.

Example: PCI DSS Baseline Initiative

An initiative named 'PCI DSS Baseline' could include policies for network segmentation, data encryption, logging, and access control. Instead of assigning each of these policies individually, you assign the 'PCI DSS Baseline' initiative once to your subscription, ensuring all relevant controls are in place.

Key Takeaway

Policy initiatives are crucial for organizing and managing complex sets of security policies, providing a streamlined approach to comprehensive compliance.
