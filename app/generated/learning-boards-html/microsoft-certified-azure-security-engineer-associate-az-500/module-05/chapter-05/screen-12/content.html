Compliance & Governance

Regulatory Compliance Dashboard

Monitoring adherence to industry standards and benchmarks within Azure Defender for Cloud.

What it Does
  • •Maps recommendations to industry standards (ISO 27001, PCI DSS).
  • •Shows compliance status and identifies gaps.
  • •Allows adding custom compliance standards.
  • •Invaluable for audit preparedness.
Key Benefit

Provides a unified view of your compliance posture, simplifying the process of demonstrating adherence to internal policies and external regulations.

Key Takeaway

The Regulatory Compliance Dashboard is a powerful tool for monitoring and demonstrating adherence to industry standards, crucial for audits and governance.

Example: PCI DSS Compliance

If PCI DSS requires all data at rest to be encrypted, the dashboard will highlight any unencrypted storage accounts and link to the Defender for Cloud recommendation to remediate them.

Operational Importance

Regularly reviewing this dashboard is crucial for security engineers to proactively identify and address compliance gaps, ensuring continuous adherence to required standards.

Why it Matters

The dashboard simplifies complex compliance requirements, making it easier to demonstrate due diligence and pass audits, which is vital for business reputation and legal standing.
