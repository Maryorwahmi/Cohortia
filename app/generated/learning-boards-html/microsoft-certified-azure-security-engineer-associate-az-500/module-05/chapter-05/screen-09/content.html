Operational Task

Interpreting Security Recommendations and Secure Score

Prioritizing actionable advice to continuously improve your security posture.

Recommendations Overview
  • •Grouped by security control (e.g., 'Enable MFA').
  • •Each has a severity level (low, medium, high).
  • •Impacts your overall Secure Score.
Secure Score Impact
  • •Numerical representation of your security posture.
  • •Higher score indicates better security.
  • •Prioritize high-impact recommendations.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize high-severity recommendations with significant Secure Score impact, but always consider business context and potential disruption before implementation.

Example: MFA Recommendation

A recommendation to 'Enable MFA for all accounts with owner permissions' has high severity and high Secure Score impact, significantly reducing credential compromise risk.

Consider Business Impact

Implementing a recommendation like 'Restrict network access to storage accounts' might require careful planning to avoid disrupting applications that rely on that storage.

Why it Matters

Regularly addressing recommendations is key to maintaining a strong security posture, reducing your attack surface, and demonstrating continuous improvement to auditors and stakeholders.
