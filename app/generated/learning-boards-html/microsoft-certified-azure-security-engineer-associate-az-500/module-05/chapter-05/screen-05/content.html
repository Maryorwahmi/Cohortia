Azure Defender Plans: Tailored Workload Protection

Activating specific plans for comprehensive threat detection across your Azure resources.

Resource-Specific

Each plan is designed to protect a unique type of Azure resource, recognizing diverse threat vectors.

Advanced Protection

Goes beyond basic CSPM to offer real-time threat detection and response capabilities.

Comprehensive Coverage

Ensures all critical workloads are protected against their most relevant and sophisticated threats.

Key Takeaway

Activating specific Azure Defender plans is essential for comprehensive CWPP, providing tailored, advanced threat protection for each resource type.

Pro-Tip: Don't Underestimate

While the free tier offers basic CSPM, it lacks the advanced threat detection of paid Defender plans. For critical workloads, the enhanced security often justifies the cost.
