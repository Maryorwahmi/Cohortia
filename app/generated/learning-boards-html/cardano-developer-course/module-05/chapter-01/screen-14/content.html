Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
DApp
Frontend that interacts with Plutus, relying on wallets for signing and providers for network access.
Mesh & Lucid
Libraries that abstract transaction building, wallet integration, and UTXO management for frontends.
EUTXO
Transactions consume specific UTXOs and produce new ones; selection and change are central design concerns.
Remember: design your UI to present fees, change, and collateral before requesting wallet signatures. That clarity prevents surprises and reduces rejected transactions.
