Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › What is a Cardano DApp?
Core Concept

DApp = Frontend + On-chain Logic

A DApp coordinates user intent, transaction building, wallet signing, and on-chain submission without holding private keys. The frontend is responsible for UX and preparing transactions; the wallet signs locally.

Concrete example: a lottery deposit UI builds a transaction to lock ADA at a Plutus address, requests a signature from the user’s wallet, and submits the signed transaction.
Common mistake: assuming the frontend signs transactions. Wallets must always sign locally to keep keys safe.
Metaphor
Think of the DApp as a cashier preparing a deposit slip. The wallet is the customer's signature; without it the slip is not valid and cannot be processed by the bank (the blockchain).
Key Takeaway

Design DApps so that the frontend constructs intent and transactions, and wallets retain signing authority to protect users' keys.
