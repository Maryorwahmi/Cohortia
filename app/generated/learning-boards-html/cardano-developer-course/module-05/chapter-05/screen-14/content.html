Key Concepts: Part 2

Review the fundamental terms and ideas from this chapter.

Script Address

A unique Cardano address derived from a Plutus smart contract's compiled script, where UTxOs can be locked.

Datum

A piece of arbitrary data attached to a UTXO locked at a script address, serving as the 'state' or condition for the Plutus script.

Redeemer

A piece of arbitrary data provided when attempting to spend a UTXO locked by a Plutus script, used for validation.

Chapter Insight

The interplay between script addresses, datums, and redeemers defines how Plutus smart contracts manage and secure assets on Cardano.
