Funding Your Testnet Transactions with Faucet ADA

Obtaining free test ADA for DApp development and testing.

What is a Faucet?

A service that dispenses free, valueless test ADA or other test tokens on a testnet for development purposes.

How to Use

Visit the official Cardano Testnet Faucet (e.g., for Preprod), paste your testnet wallet's receiving address, and receive test ADA.

Key Takeaway

Testnet faucets are invaluable resources, providing free test ADA to enable comprehensive and risk-free DApp development and testing.

Faucet Flow

You provide your testnet wallet address to the faucet, which then sends free test ADA to your wallet, enabling transactions on the testnet.
