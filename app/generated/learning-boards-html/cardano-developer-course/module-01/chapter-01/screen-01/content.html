Visual Concept
Ledger

An append-only history of transactions linked cryptographically.

Consensus

A protocol that decides which entries are final and valid.

Stake

Stake secures participation and selects block producers.

Research

Peer-reviewed design for long-term correctness and safety.

Why this matters

Trust without a central gatekeeper

Systems that move trust away from single organizations enable more resilient services, lower censorship risk, and new economic models for applications that require verifiable history.

Key Takeaway
Cardano matches blockchain foundations with formal engineering and a PoS protocol to offer a sustainable platform for value and computation.
