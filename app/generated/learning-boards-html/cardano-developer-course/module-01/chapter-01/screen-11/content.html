Proof-of-Work vs Proof-of-Stake

An explicit look at energy, security assumptions, and decentralization impacts.

Proof-of-Work
Relies on computational cost and mining rewards; energy intensive and can centralize around large miners.
Proof-of-Stake (Ouroboros)
Uses stake-weighted selection to reduce energy needs and encourage broader participation through delegation.
Security assumptions
PoW: cost of acquiring hashing power. PoS: economic majority of honest stake and proper incentive alignment.
Decentralization implications
PoW can centralize around mining economies; PoS can decentralize through many delegators and pools if incentives are set well.
Key Takeaway

Both mechanisms secure the ledger but rely on different economic and technical assumptions; choose based on your project's sustainability and trust model.
