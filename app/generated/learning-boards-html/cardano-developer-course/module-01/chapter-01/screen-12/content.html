Common Misconceptions About Cardano

Clarifying why Cardano's design choices exist and what they mean for developers.

Not just an Ethereum copy
Cardano emphasizes peer review and formal verification, which shapes its architecture differently than earlier platforms.
Research pace is deliberate
A research-first approach slows feature rollout but aims to avoid costly security mistakes in the long run.
Decentralization ≠ anonymity
Networks can be decentralized while still maintaining transparent, auditable histories and accountable validators.
Key Takeaway

Recognize the trade-offs: Cardano trades rapid feature churn for provable design and sustainability, which influences your project's risk profile.
