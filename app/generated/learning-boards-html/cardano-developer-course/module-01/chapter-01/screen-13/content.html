Core Concept

ADA — Currency, Fees, and Governance

The multiple functions ADA serves within Cardano's protocol and economy.

Payments & fees
ADA pays transaction fees, incentivizing validators and making operations economically accountable.
Staking
Holding or delegating ADA participates in securing the network and earning rewards through stake pools.
Governance
ADA holders influence protocol parameters and upgrades through stake-weighted participation mechanisms.
Key Takeaway

ADA is more than a token: it is integral to fee economics, network security, and governance, shaping application behavior and incentives.
