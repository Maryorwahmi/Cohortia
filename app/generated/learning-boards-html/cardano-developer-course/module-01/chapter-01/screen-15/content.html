Key Concepts — What to Remember

A compact reference of the chapter's most important ideas.

Blockchain
A distributed, immutable ledger secured by cryptography.
Decentralization
Distributes control to reduce single points of failure and censorship risk.
Ouroboros PoS
A stake-weighted, epoch-and-slot protocol for energy-efficient consensus.
Summary takeaway
Use immutability, decentralization, and protocol assumptions as lenses when architecting or evaluating blockchain solutions.
