Hands-on Activity: Compare PoW and Ouroboros

A guided checklist to produce a focused 200–300 word comparison.

Research checklist
  • Energy: note operations that consume resources and whether specialized hardware is required.
  • Security: list the main economic security assumptions and typical attack vectors.
  • Decentralization: observe validator access models and delegation mechanics.
  • Throughput: collect statements about transaction finality and protocol cadence.
Write-up tips
Tie each claim to a protocol property. Prefer short paragraphs and one clear recommendation: which mechanism fits sustainable global finance and why.
Example outline
1. Intro sentence linking energy and scalability
2. Energy comparison and impact on deployment
3. Security assumptions and decentralization effects
4. Recommendation and closing rationale
Key Takeaway

A clear, protocol-grounded comparison helps you choose consensus mechanisms that match your application's priorities: sustainability, security, and decentralization.
