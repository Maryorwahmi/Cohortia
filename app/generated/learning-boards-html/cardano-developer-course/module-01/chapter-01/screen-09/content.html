Ouroboros Proof-of-Stake — Overview

How Ouroboros organizes time and selects block producers using stake-weighted randomness.

Epoch
A fixed period during which stake accounting and leader selection are applied.
Slot
A small time unit; each slot may have a selected slot leader who can create a block.
Slot Leader
A participant chosen probabilistically based on stake to mint the block for a slot.
Example epoch (10 slots)
Before the epoch, eligible leaders are computed. As slots occur, leaders produce blocks if online; otherwise the slot is skipped.
Stake Pools
Pools combine stake from many holders to increase practical uptime and selection chances for block production.
Key Takeaway

Epochs and slots provide predictable rhythm; stake-weighted selection reduces energy needs while enabling decentralization when participation is broad.
