Slot Leader Selection and Stake Pools

How stake determines selection probability and the operational role of pools.

Stake-weighted randomness
Probability of selection is proportional to stake; randomness prevents absolute control by large holders.
Stake pools
Pools aggregate stake, increase uptime, and handle block production on behalf of delegators.
Example: delegating
A small holder delegates to a pool run by an operator with reliable infrastructure; rewards are distributed proportionally to delegators.
Common mistake
Assuming large stake equals irreversible control. Protocol parameters and broad delegation patterns moderate concentration effects.
Key Takeaway

Pools make participation practical and help decentralize block production by enabling many holders to participate through delegation.
