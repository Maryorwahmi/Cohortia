Cardano's Research-Driven Philosophy and Stewards

Who builds Cardano and why separating responsibilities matters for reliability.

IOHK
Research and engineering: protocol design, peer-reviewed work, and implementations.
Emurgo
Commercial adoption: supporting businesses integrating Cardano technology.
Cardano Foundation
Community and governance: ecosystem growth, standards, and outreach.
Why separation helps
Independent roles reduce concentration of control and make governance and research outcomes more transparent.
Key Takeaway

Understanding the stewards helps you find the right resources: technical specs, commercial support, or community governance channels.
