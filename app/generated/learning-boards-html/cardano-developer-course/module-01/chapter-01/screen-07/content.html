Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Cardano — Different Paths

A brief comparison highlighting consensus and development philosophy.

Bitcoin
PoW ledger focused on simple, robust cash transfers and censorship resistance.
Ethereum
Introduced smart contracts and richer programmability for decentralized applications.
Cardano
A research-driven platform with PoS and a layered architecture designed for correctness and sustainability.
Contrast: Energy model
PoW requires extensive computation; PoS selects validators by stake, avoiding energy-intensive puzzles.
Contrast: Development style
Cardano emphasizes peer review and formal methods to reduce the chance of costly vulnerabilities over time.
Key Takeaway

Platform choices reflect trade-offs between speed of features, assurance of correctness, and operational costs like energy.
