Definition

Decentralization and Trust

What decentralization means for failure modes, censorship, and system design.

Spectrum
Decentralization ranges from permissioned validators to open public participation; evaluate where a design sits on this spectrum.
Incentives matter
Economic incentives like staking determine who runs validators and how decentralized participation becomes in practice.
Concrete scenario
Payment network example

A centralized payment processor can freeze funds or be a single outage. In a decentralized ledger, replicated validators and consensus rules protect users from unilateral changes.

Common misconception
Decentralization is not equivalent to privacy or anonymity. Systems can be decentralized but transparent, with known validators accountable for behavior.
Key Takeaway

Design decisions about decentralization affect resilience, governance, and how you reason about trust boundaries.
