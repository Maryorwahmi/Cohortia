Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Setting Up a New Wallet Securely
Process Flow

Create, Back Up, Verify, Protect

01
Create wallet in the app
Follow the app prompts to generate your seed phrase and local keys.
02
Write down the seed phrase
Record the 12 or 24 words on paper. Do not store it digitally.
03
Verify selected words
The wallet will ask you to confirm certain words to ensure accuracy.
04
Set a spending password
This protects daily actions while your seed remains the ultimate recovery key.
Key Takeaway

A correct physical backup and verification are the single most important steps in wallet setup.

Example
When creating Nami, you will receive 24 words. Write them down, then confirm some words when prompted. After setup, switch to Testnet and request tADA to confirm receipt before sending anything important.
Danger
Do not photograph or copy the seed phrase to cloud storage, and do not share it in chat or email.
Try this
After setup, copy one public address and paste it into a testnet faucet to request tADA and confirm your address works.
