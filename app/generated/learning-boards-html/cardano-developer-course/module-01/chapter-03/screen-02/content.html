Learning Objectives

Progress from identification to hands-on verification.

01 Wallet Types
Identify full-node, light, and hardware wallets.
Why it matters: choose security or convenience for your use case.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Secure Setup
Create a new wallet and protect the seed phrase.
Why it matters: seed phrase loss equals permanent loss of funds.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Transactions
Send and receive ADA, understand fees and metadata.
Why it matters: fees and metadata affect cost and traceability.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Verify On-chain
Use a block explorer to confirm transaction hashes and details.
Why it matters: public verification prevents errors and fraud.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
