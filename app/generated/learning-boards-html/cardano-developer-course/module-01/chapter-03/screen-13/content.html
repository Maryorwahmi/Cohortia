Hardware Wallet Architecture

Keys held in a physical device; signing requires physical confirmation.

Signing flow
Transaction constructed in the UI is sent to the device, you review and approve on the device, and the signed transaction is returned for broadcast.
Practical pairing
Pair a hardware device with a light client so you get usability without exposing keys to the browser environment.
Do's and Don'ts
  • Do verify details on the device screen before approving.
  • Do keep the device firmware updated from official sources only.
  • Don't store the recovery phrase on your computer or online.
Scenario
For high-value storage, use a hardware wallet and multiple physical backups for the seed phrase in secure locations.
