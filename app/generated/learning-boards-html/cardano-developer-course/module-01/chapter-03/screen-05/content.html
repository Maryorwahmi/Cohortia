Wallet Type Trade-offs

Compare full-node, light client, and hardware wallets to match your threat model.

Full-node (Daedalus)
  • Downloads and verifies the entire blockchain.
  • Maximum trust and decentralization.
  • Requires storage and time to sync.
Light client (Yoroi, Lace)
  • Connects to trusted nodes for quick access.
  • Low resource use and fast setup.
  • Great for daily use and dApp interaction.
Hardware (Ledger, Trezor)
  • Stores private keys offline; requires physical confirmation.
  • Strong protection against online compromise.
  • Recommended for large or long-term holdings.
Misconception
Assuming light clients are unsafe. When paired with good seed protection, they are practical and secure for many users.
Pro tip
Use a hardware wallet for significant funds and a light client for daily interactions.
Scenario
If your primary goal is to validate chain state yourself, pick Daedalus. If you want quick access with low overhead, choose Yoroi or Lace. If you store large sums, pair a hardware device with a light client UI.
