Inspecting On-chain Evidence

Use the transaction hash to view confirmation status, fees, and UTXO details.

What you can see
  • Transaction hash and confirmation count.
  • Inputs and outputs with amounts and addresses.
  • The fee paid and any metadata attached.
Verification example
If someone claims they sent funds, ask for the transaction hash and confirm it appears in an explorer and that the outputs match your address.
Misunderstanding
Don’t rely solely on wallet UI states for finality; explorers show on-chain confirmations which are the authoritative view.
Quick Steps
  1. Send transaction and copy the hash from your wallet.
  2. Paste the hash into a block explorer search field.
  3. Confirm outputs, fee, and confirmation count.
Practice
Use a testnet explorer to inspect transactions you perform in the hands-on activity to build confidence in verification steps.
