Process

Share and Verify Public Addresses

Copy an address from your wallet and share it. Verify receipts via a block explorer.

Practical tip
Always copy the address string to avoid typos.
Testnet example
Paste your addr_test1... into a testnet faucet form and wait for tADA.
Confirmation awareness
A received transaction is visible quickly; consider several block confirmations before treating funds as final.
