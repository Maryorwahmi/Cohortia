Common Errors and Fixes

Adopt simple habits to avoid large losses.

Address typos
Always copy the recipient address or scan a QR. Double-check the first and last segments of the address if you must verify visually.
Seed exposure
Never store seed phrases digitally or share them. Treat the phrase as a master key and protect it offline.
Confirmation misunderstanding
Use explorers to check confirmation counts rather than relying solely on the wallet's pending indicator.
Checklist
  • Copy recipient address
  • Keep seed offline
  • Check transaction hash on explorer
  • Test on Testnet first
