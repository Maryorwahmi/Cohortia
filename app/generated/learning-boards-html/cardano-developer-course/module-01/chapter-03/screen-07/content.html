What the Seed Phrase Means

It is the master key from which all private keys and addresses are derived.

Why it matters
Lose the phrase and you lose access to funds. Expose the phrase and others can take your funds.
Best practices
  • Write it down physically and store offline.
  • Keep multiple secure copies in separate places.
  • Use metal backup for long-term durability if available.
What not to do
Do not store the seed phrase in cloud storage, screenshots, or share it with anyone.
Verification Tip
When prompted to confirm words, read your written copy carefully. This step prevents typos that would lock you out later.
Reflection
If you lost access to a device, how would you restore the wallet using the seed phrase? Practice the mental steps now.
