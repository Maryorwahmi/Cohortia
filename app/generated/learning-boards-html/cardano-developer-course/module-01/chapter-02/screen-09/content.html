Role

Policy ID enforces mint/burn rules

Choose a policy that matches your token lifecycle: single mint, scheduled minting, or governance-controlled minting. Simpler policies reduce risk.

  • Single key policy: one signer can mint then lock the policy.
  • Time-locked policy: allow minting only before a block/time threshold.
  • Multi-signer policy: require signatures from multiple keys.
Common mistake
Confusing metadata with mint policy. Metadata is descriptive data attached to transactions. Policies enforce who may change supply. Both matter, but they are distinct concerns.
Key Takeaway

Design the simplest policy that fulfills your supply needs; complex minting rules may justify using Plutus instead of policy scripts.
