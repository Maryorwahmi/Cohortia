The Cardano Architecture: Layers and Components

A modular approach that separates secure value transfer from programmable smart contract execution.

In this lesson
Understand CSL, CCL, EUTXO, tokens, Plutus & Marlowe

You will see how roles split across two layers and why that separation supports safer, upgradeable development.

Why it matters
Safer contracts and cheaper, native token transfers

Separation reduces attack surface and keeps simple transfers efficient while enabling powerful on-chain logic.
