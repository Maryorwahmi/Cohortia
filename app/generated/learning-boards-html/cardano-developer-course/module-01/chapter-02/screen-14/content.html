Hands-on Activity

Examine a live transaction and map UTXO flows

Step 1
Open a block explorer and choose a recent transaction on the homepage.
Step 2
Identify Inputs and Outputs and note asset types and quantities in each UTXO.
Step 3
Look for Metadata, fees, and the block confirmation status; reflect on how inputs became outputs.
Reflection

Describe briefly how observing inputs and outputs helped you understand the EUTXO model and how it differs from an account-based mental model.
