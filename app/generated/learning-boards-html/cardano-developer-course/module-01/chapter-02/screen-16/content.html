Two-Layer Design

Keep settlement simple and computation flexible to reduce risk and support upgrades.

EUTXO

Design with discrete outputs. Predictability comes from explicit inputs listed in transactions.

Tokens & Contracts

Use native tokens for simple needs; use Plutus or Marlowe when programmable behavior is required.

Final Takeaway
Think in lanes and parcels: lanes for settlement vs computation, parcels for UTXOs. This mental model will guide safer, more effective Cardano development.
