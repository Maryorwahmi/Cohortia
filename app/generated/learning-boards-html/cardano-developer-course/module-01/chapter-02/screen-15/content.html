CSL & CCL

Separate settlement from computation so ledger upgrades and contract innovation proceed independently and safely.

EUTXO

Transactions consume specific UTXOs and produce new ones, enabling deterministic validation and parallelism.

Tokens & Tools

Prefer native tokens for simple issuance; choose Plutus or Marlowe when programmable, domain-specific behavior is required.

Summary
Use the two-layer model, think in UTXOs, and pick the simplest secure tool for tokens and contracts. Test actual transactions to validate your design choices.
