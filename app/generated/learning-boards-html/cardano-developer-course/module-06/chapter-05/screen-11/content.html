Development Best Practices

Minimizing Script Complexity & Thorough Testing

Building robust contracts through simplicity and rigorous validation.

Why Simplicity Matters

  • Easier to understand and reason about.
  • Fewer lines of code, fewer potential bugs.
  • Faster to audit and verify correctness.
  • Reduced execution costs (less likely to hit limits).

Strategies for Simplicity

  • Modularize logic into small, focused functions.
  • Avoid unnecessary features or over-engineering.
  • Use clear, concise variable names and comments.
  • Refactor complex sections into simpler patterns.

The Importance of Testing

Thorough testing is the bedrock of smart contract security. It's the process of systematically verifying that your code behaves as expected under all conditions, not just the ideal ones.

Test-Driven Security

Combine simple, modular code with exhaustive unit, integration, and property-based testing to catch bugs and vulnerabilities early.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize simplicity in script design and implement comprehensive testing strategies to build secure and reliable Plutus contracts.
