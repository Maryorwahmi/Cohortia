Why Testnets?

Testnets provide a safe, realistic environment to validate smart contract logic and interactions without risking real ADA, mimicking mainnet behavior.

The `cardano-cli` Role

It's the essential command-line tool for building, signing, submitting transactions, and querying the blockchain, central to all deployment steps.

Key Stages

The deployment process involves compiling the script, generating its address, funding it with Datum, and spending from it with a Redeemer.

Key Takeaway

Testnet deployment is a critical bridge, allowing developers to thoroughly test smart contracts in a live environment before mainnet deployment, ensuring reliability and security.
