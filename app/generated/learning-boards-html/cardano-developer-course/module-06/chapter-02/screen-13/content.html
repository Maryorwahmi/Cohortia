Troubleshooting Testnet Deployments

Identifying and Resolving Common Errors

Incorrect Datum/Redeemer

Ensure your datum.json and redeemer.json values precisely match what your Plutus script expects. Mismatches lead to script failure.

Wrong Testnet Magic

Verify that the --testnet-magic value in your cardano-cli commands corresponds to the specific testnet you are targeting (e.g., 1 for preprod).

Missing Collateral

When spending from a script, always include a collateral UTXO from a regular address. Forgetting it will cause the transaction to be rejected.

Fee/Change Miscalculation

Always use cardano-cli calculate-min-fee and adjust your transaction outputs to precisely account for the fee. Incorrect fees invalidate transactions.

Pro Tip: Use a Block Explorer

Utilize block explorers like preprod.cardanoscan.io to inspect transaction details, UTXOs, and script execution results. This is invaluable for diagnosing issues.

Key Takeaway

Effective troubleshooting involves meticulous checking of script data, command parameters, and on-chain state, with block explorers being your best friend in debugging testnet deployments.
