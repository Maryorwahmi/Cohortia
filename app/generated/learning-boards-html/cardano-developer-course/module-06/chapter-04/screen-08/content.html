Block Explorers: Monitoring On-Chain Activity

Your window into the Cardano blockchain's real-time data.

What Block Explorers Do

Block explorers are web-based tools that provide a searchable interface to view all data recorded on the Cardano blockchain.

  • Transaction Monitoring: Track the status and details of any transaction.
  • UTXO Inspection: View the contents and state of Unspent Transaction Outputs.
  • Smart Contract Analysis: Examine script addresses and interaction details.

Why They Are Crucial for Developers

For developers, block explorers are vital for debugging and understanding the behavior of DApps and smart contracts.

  • Debugging: Identify issues in transaction construction or script execution.
  • Data Verification: Confirm that `Datum` and `Redeemer` values are correctly placed and consumed.
  • Performance Analysis: Observe execution units and fees consumed by scripts.
Key Takeaway

Block explorers are essential for real-time monitoring and debugging of all on-chain activity, providing transparency and critical insights for developers.
