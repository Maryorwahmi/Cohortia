Developer Platforms

Demeter.run and Plutus Playground: Streamlining Development

Tools for managed infrastructure and smart contract prototyping.

Demeter.run: Managed Infrastructure

A cloud-based development platform offering managed Cardano infrastructure, simplifying setup and deployment.

  • Simplified Setup: Access to managed nodes, Blockfrost, Maestro.
  • Reduced Overhead: Focus on DApp logic, not infrastructure management.

Plutus Playground: Online Simulator

An online simulator for Plutus smart contracts, allowing developers to write, compile, and simulate contract behavior.

  • Rapid Prototyping: Test contract logic quickly without local setup.
  • Learning Tool: Excellent for understanding Plutus behavior and initial development.
Key Takeaway

Demeter.run simplifies infrastructure management, while Plutus Playground offers an accessible environment for prototyping and learning smart contract logic.
