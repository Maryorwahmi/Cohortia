Chapter Summary

Key Concepts to Remember (Part 2)

Reinforcing your understanding of advanced Cardano tools.

Oracles

Services that securely bring off-chain data onto the blockchain for use by smart contracts, overcoming determinism limitations.

UTXO Indexer

Tools like Kupo that index and allow efficient querying of UTXOs, critical for DApps to find relevant transaction inputs.

Full vs. Light Wallets

Distinction between full-node wallets (max security, resource-heavy) and light wallets (convenience, third-party reliance).

Summary Takeaway

Oracles bridge off-chain data, UTXO indexers enable efficient data access, and understanding wallet types is crucial for balancing security and convenience in Cardano development.
