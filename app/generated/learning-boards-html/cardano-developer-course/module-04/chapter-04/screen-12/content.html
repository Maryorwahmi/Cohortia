Patterns to avoid contention
Patterns
Sharding: split state into multiple UTXOs so parallel operations rarely collide.
Batching/Queueing: collect many requests off-chain or in request UTXOs and process them in a single transaction.
State Machine: define explicit transitions, and have an operator create the transition transactions.
Trade-offs & checklist
  • Sharding reduces contention but increases coordination complexity.
  • Batching improves throughput but requires an off-chain coordinator or operator.
  • State machines are auditable but require careful transition validation to avoid stuck states.
Ask yourself: where will users collide on a single UTXO, and which pattern balances throughput, trust, and implementational complexity for your dApp?
