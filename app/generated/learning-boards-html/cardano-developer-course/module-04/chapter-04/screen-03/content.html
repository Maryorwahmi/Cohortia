Practice checks and design tasks
What you'll practice
Script-context checks
Identify signers and validity windows in the provided transaction information and reason how a validator would use them.
Output verification
Ensure outputs include specific payments or updated datums required by the validator's logic.
Datum propagation
Detect missing or incorrect datums on new UTXOs that would break state continuity.
Transaction flow design
Sketch deposit and withdrawal flows for a funding pool: which UTXOs, datums and redeemers are used.
