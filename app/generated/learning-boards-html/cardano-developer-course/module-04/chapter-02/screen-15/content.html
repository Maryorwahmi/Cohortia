Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › What to Remember
Confirm shell
Run ghc --version inside nix-shell to ensure the pinned compiler is active before building.
Be patient
The first nix-shell can compile large dependencies. Subsequent entries are faster due to caching.
Manage storage
Run nix-collect-garbage -d when disk usage grows to reclaim unreferenced artifacts.
Final thought

Keep a short pre-build checklist: verify the shell, confirm tool versions, and ensure sufficient disk space before large builds.
