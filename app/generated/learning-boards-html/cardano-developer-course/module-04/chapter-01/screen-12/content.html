What to Remember
Three compact concept cards summarize the chapter.
Smart Contract
A validator script that enforces conditions for spending a UTXO.
EUTXO
UTXOs hold datums and are consumed/created by transactions, enabling explicit state transitions.
Plutus
Cardano's Haskell-based language for writing validator scripts with emphasis on purity and types.
Summary: Map state to datums, make transitions explicit, and design UTXO boundaries to minimize contention.
