Sync timeline

Connect to peers
Node contacts peers listed in topology.json to start fetching blocks.
Download & validate
Blocks are downloaded, signatures and headers are validated, and entries are written to the database.
Catch up to tip
The node reaches the latest block and syncProgress becomes 100.00.

What affects duration

  • Available disk space and disk write speed.
  • Number and health of peers in topology.json.
  • Internet bandwidth and latency.
Common Mistake

Assuming mainnet and testnet sync times are similar. Mainnet has a much larger history and requires more disk and time.

Key Takeaway

Syncing is essential and may take significant time; plan for disk space, stable connectivity, and good peers.
