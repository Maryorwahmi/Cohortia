Quick Reference
Three short reminders to prevent errors.
Include Change
Always send leftover ADA back as a change output to avoid burning funds.
Calculate Fee
Run calculate-min-fee with the correct tx counts and fresh protocol.json.
Correct Signing Key
Use payment.skey for spending inputs; stake keys do not authorize spends.
Pin these reminders to your workflow while practicing until they become second nature.
