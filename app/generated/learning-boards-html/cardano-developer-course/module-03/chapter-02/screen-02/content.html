Learning Objectives
Master the practical sequence for minting and sending native tokens using cardano-cli on testnet
01
Construct a minting policy script
Write a time-locked or signature-based policy that controls mint and burn rules, so you limit who can mint tokens and when.
Mnemonic: Policy = Rules
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Generate policy signing keys
Produce the key hash from your payment verification key and prepare .skey files that will sign minting operations when required.
Mnemonic: Hash the vkey
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Build and mint using cardano-cli
Use build-raw with --mint and --minting-script-file, calculate min fee and min ADA, then sign and submit the transaction on testnet.
Mnemonic: Draft → Fee → Sign → Submit
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Send and verify minted tokens
Prepare a tx-out that includes ADA plus the native asset, submit, and inspect your UTXO set to confirm token arrival and ADA minima.
Mnemonic: UTXO check
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
