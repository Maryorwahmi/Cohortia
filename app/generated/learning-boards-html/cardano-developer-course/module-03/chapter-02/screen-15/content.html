Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › What to Remember
Design your policy
Choose time-lock or signatures depending on whether you want fixed supply or controlled future minting.
Compute, don’t guess
Always run calculate-min-required-utxo and calculate-min-fee using up-to-date protocol.json.
Secure keys & verify
Protect your .skey files and confirm minted assets by querying your UTXO after submission.
One-line summary
Policy + accurate calculations + secure signing = predictable minting outcomes on Cardano.
