Time-locked
Enforces minting only within a slot range. Good for fixed issuance windows without ongoing key custody.
Pros
  • Automatic enforcement after expiry
  • No ongoing key custody needed after lock
Cons
  • Less flexible post-expiry
Signature-based
Requires one or more key signatures for minting. Good for controlled or limited future minting when keys are securely managed.
Pros
  • Flexible, can mint later if keys retained
Cons
  • Requires secure key management
Design Tip
Combine signature and time-lock conditions when you want dual control: signatures to authorize minting and a slot-based expiry to enforce finality later.
