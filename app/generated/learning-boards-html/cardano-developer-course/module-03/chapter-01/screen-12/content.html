Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Native Assets
Tokens handled by the ledger with the same transfer safety as ADA.
Policy ID & Asset Name
Policy ID is a script hash; Asset Name is up to 32 bytes. Together they are the canonical identifier.
Minting Policies
Scripts that control minting/burning. Their design and key custody have long-term consequences.
Final Takeaway

Use ledger-native tokens for simple transfers, verify policy identifiers, and treat policy scripts and keys with careful design and testing.
