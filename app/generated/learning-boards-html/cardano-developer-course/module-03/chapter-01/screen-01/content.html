Understanding Native Assets on Cardano

Explore how tokens are built into the ledger, how the EUTXO model carries multiple assets, and why Policy IDs and Asset Names together make each asset unique.

Orientation

This chapter focuses on three things: ledger-level token handling, the EUTXO mechanics that let UTXOs carry bundles of assets, and the minting policy rules that produce a Policy ID. You’ll practice identifying Policy IDs and Asset Names using a block explorer in the hands-on activity.

Concept Map
Native Asset
Ledger-handled token with the same transfer rules as ADA.
Policy ID
Hash of the minting policy script that governs minting and burning.
Asset Name
Up to 32 bytes of user-defined data that names the token in that policy.
Quick Example
A token might be identified as abcdef123... .MyAwesomeToken. The left part is the Policy ID, the right part is the Asset Name. Their combination is globally unique.
