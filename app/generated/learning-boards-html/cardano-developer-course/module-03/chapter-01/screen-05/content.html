Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › EUTXO Flow
Process: Mint → Hold → Transfer
1
Minting
A transaction with a minting action creates UTXOs that contain the new token quantity alongside ADA. The minting policy yields the Policy ID.
2
Holding
UTXOs store bundles: ADA plus zero or more native assets. These outputs remain spendable until consumed by a transaction.
3
Transfer
Spending a UTXO consumes assets and produces new outputs for recipients and change, all enforced by ledger validation.
Concrete Scenario
A mint transaction creates 1,000 MyToken and an ADA dust amount in the same UTXO. Later, a user spends that UTXO to send 200 MyToken to Bob and receives a change UTXO with 800 MyToken. Ledger rules ensure totals remain consistent with the policy script.
Annotated UTXO Cards
UTXO: Mint Output
ADA: 2.0 | MyToken: 1,000
UTXO: Recipient
ADA: 1.5 | MyToken: 200
UTXO: Change
ADA: 0.48 | MyToken: 800
Common Mistake

Assuming tokens must be wrapped by contracts to move. On Cardano, simple transfers use standard ledger UTXO consumption and production.
