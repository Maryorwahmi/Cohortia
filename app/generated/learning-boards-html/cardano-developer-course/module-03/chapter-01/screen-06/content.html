Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Policy ID & Asset Name
Policy ID

Derived by hashing the minting policy script. It represents the origin and minting rules of the asset. The policy script may require signatures or time conditions.

Security note
Loss of keys required by the policy or an unintended infinite-mint rule are irreversible for that Policy ID.
Asset Name

A user-defined byte string up to 32 bytes. Wallets may display a friendly version, but the on-chain value is raw bytes that combine with the Policy ID to form the token’s identifier.

Misconception
Do not rely on a wallet’s ticker as the authoritative ID. Always verify the Policy ID and raw Asset Name.
