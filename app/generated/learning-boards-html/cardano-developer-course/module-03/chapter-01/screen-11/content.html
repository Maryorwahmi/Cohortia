Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Pitfalls & Tips
Mistake: Trusting tickers
Always check Policy ID + Asset Name; wallets may show friendly labels that are not authoritative.
Mistake: Infinite minting
Design policies intentionally. If you wanted fixed supply, enforce that in the policy script and test it.
Best practice: Key custody
Use multisig or documented custody for keys that control minting to reduce single-point failures.
Checklist
  • Verify Policy ID and Asset Name on an explorer.
  • Test the policy script on testnet to confirm intended constraints.
  • Plan key management and consider multisig/time-locks where needed.
