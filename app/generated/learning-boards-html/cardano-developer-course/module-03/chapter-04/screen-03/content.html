Learning Objectives — Apply & Verify
Practice hex encoding, min-ADA, fees, verification, and policy safety.
01 Hex Encoding
Convert your human-readable asset name to its hex form and use it both in --mint and inside the 721 JSON under YOUR_HEX_ASSET_NAME.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 Min ADA & Fees
Calculate minimum ADA for the NFT UTXO and compute fee so the change output balances correctly before signing.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 Verification
Use a testnet explorer to confirm metadata parsing: name, image, files, and attributes must match your JSON.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 Policy Safety
Plan a locked policy for fixed supply or be aware that retaining signing keys allows additional mints. Consider time-locks or burning keys to lock supply.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
