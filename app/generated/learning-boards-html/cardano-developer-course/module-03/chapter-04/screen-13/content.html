Common Mistakes & Safety
Avoid these pitfalls before minting
Metadata Mismatch
Policy id or hex asset name in metadata differs from the minted asset.
Incorrect Quantity
Minting more than one unit makes the token fungible, not an NFT.
Policy Locking
Use time-locks or destroy signing keys to lock supply if fixed supply is desired.
IPFS Pinning
Pin your media so explorers can fetch the image; unpinned content may disappear.
Testnet First
Always mint and verify on testnet before mainnet to catch errors safely.
Wallet Compatibility
Follow CIP-25 fields and include mediaType and files for broader wallet support.
Key Takeaway
A small verification checklist—policy & hex match, min ADA, fee, IPFS pinning, and testnet validation—prevents the majority of minting and display failures.
