Minting and Managing NFTs with Cardano CLI
Prepare CIP-25 metadata, mint a single non-fungible native asset with cardano-cli, verify its metadata, and use the same transaction flow to send and receive NFTs.
In this lesson

You will create CIP-25 JSON, mint an NFT with quantity 1, attach metadata, and verify on a block explorer. You will also practice sending the NFT using standard UTXO transactions.

Why it matters

Correct metadata ensures wallets and explorers recognize your token as a unique collectible. Policy design and IPFS pinning protect rarity and media availability.
