Question Example
Doctor and Patient both can be linked to many of the other; which relationship is this and how would you represent it?
Answer
Many-to-many; implement with an intermediary Treatments table with doctor_id and patient_id foreign keys.
Checkpoint: draw the Treatments table and decide whether a composite or surrogate PK suits your scenario; justify your choice.
