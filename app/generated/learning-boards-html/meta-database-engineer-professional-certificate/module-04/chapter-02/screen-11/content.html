Starter ERD Sketch (text)
Book(ISBN PK, title, author, publication_year)
Copy(copy_id PK, isbn FK, location, status)
Member(member_id PK, name, address)
Loan(loan_id PK, copy_id FK, member_id FK, borrowed_date, returned_date)
Design Notes
  • Track physical copies separately from book editions to model availability.
  • Use Loan to preserve history and record borrow/return timestamps.
  • Enforce foreign keys from Loan to Copy and Member to preserve integrity.
Check Your Understanding

Can a Copy be loaned to two Members at the same exact time? If your model prevents that, you are modeling availability correctly.
