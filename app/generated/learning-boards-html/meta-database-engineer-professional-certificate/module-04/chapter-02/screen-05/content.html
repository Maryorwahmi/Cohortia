Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Requirements → Entities
Step-by-Step
  1. Highlight nouns and noun phrases in requirements.
  2. Mark verbs that suggest relationships between nouns.
  3. List attributes required for each noun.
  4. Choose primary keys and decide on surrogate keys when needed.
  5. Sketch relationships and annotate cardinality and optionality.
  6. Validate with stakeholders and run the ER checklist.
Worked Example: Library
Entities:
Book(ISBN PK, title, author), Member(member_id PK, name, address), Loan(loan_id PK, member_id FK, isbn FK, borrowed_date, returned_date)
Why Loan Entity?

Loan resolves the many-to-many history between members and books and records when items were borrowed and returned.
