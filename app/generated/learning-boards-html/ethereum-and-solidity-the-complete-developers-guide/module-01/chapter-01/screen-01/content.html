Introduction to Blockchain Technology

Learn the fundamental architecture: a distributed ledger of chained blocks secured by cryptography, the core properties that give blockchain trust, and practical trade-offs across network types.

Concept Map
Ledger • Blocks • Hash • Consensus
Blocks hold transactions and include the previous block's hash.
Consensus lets many nodes agree on the next block without a central authority.
Explore Key Topics
Decentralization
Many nodes maintain copies of the ledger, removing single points of failure and censorship risk.
Immutability
Cryptographic hashes link blocks so earlier edits become evident and break the chain.
Applications
Smart contracts, supply chain provenance, and digital identity illustrate blockchain uses beyond money.
