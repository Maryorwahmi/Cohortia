Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Blockchain
A distributed ledger that records transactions in linked blocks to provide a tamper-evident history.
Decentralization
Control and data are spread across many nodes rather than a single authority, reducing single-point failure.
Immutability
Linked hashes and consensus make altering past records detectable and practically infeasible.
Summary takeaway

These three concepts form the foundation for evaluating blockchain designs: how trust is distributed, how history is protected, and how visible records are to participants.
