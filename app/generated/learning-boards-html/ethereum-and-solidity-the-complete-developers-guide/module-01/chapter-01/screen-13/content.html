Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Cryptographic Hashing
A one-way function that produces a fixed fingerprint used to verify block integrity and link blocks.
Consensus Mechanism
The protocol nodes use to agree on the ledger’s next state, e.g., Proof of Work or Proof of Stake.
Network Types
Public, private, and consortium blockchains define who can participate and who can read or write data.
Final takeaway

These technical concepts are the levers you’ll adjust when evaluating or building blockchain solutions. Keep them front and center while designing systems.
