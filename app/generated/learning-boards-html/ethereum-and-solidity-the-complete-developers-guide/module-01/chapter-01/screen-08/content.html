Core Concept

Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake

Both aim to make it costly to mislead the network, but they rely on different costly resources: computation versus stake.

Proof of Work (PoW)
Miners compete using computational work. Security comes from the cost of hardware and energy.
Proof of Stake (PoS)
Validators are chosen based on locked stake. Misbehavior can result in losing stake, making attacks economically risky.
Concrete Example
In PoW, an attacker needs massive compute power to rewrite history. In PoS, an attacker must control a large percentage of stake, risking financial loss if detected and penalized.
Design Consideration
Consider energy cost, economic incentives, validator distribution, and governance when deciding on consensus mechanisms.
Misconception
PoS is not inherently immune to centralization risks; stake concentration and governance rules shape its security profile.
