Cryptography Fundamentals for Blockchain
Hashes, keys, and signatures that make decentralized systems verifiable and secure.
In this lesson
You will explore cryptographic hash functions, how blocks link with hashes, public-key cryptography, and how digital signatures authenticate transactions.
Why it matters
These primitives are the backbone of immutability and authorization on blockchain networks. Understanding them prevents costly mistakes with keys and transactions.
Explore Key Topics
Hash Functions
Fixed-size fingerprints of arbitrary data that reveal tampering.
Key Pairs
A private secret and a derived public key used for signing and verification.
Signatures & Safety
Signatures prove origin and integrity; private keys must remain confidential.
