Misconceptions
Common incorrect beliefs
1) Hashes are reversible. 2) Collision resistance guarantees impossibility. 3) Hardware wallets remove all risk. Each of these is an over-simplification and leads to unsafe choices.
Reversibility myth
Hashes are not reversible; they are verifiers only.
Overtrusting hardware
Hardware reduces remote risk but users must still secure seed phrases and verify addresses.
Attack vectors
Malware, phishing for seed phrases, and insecure backups are common causes of irreversible asset loss. Map each to a concrete mitigation you can adopt today.
Practical defenses
Use hardware wallets for important keys, store seed phrases offline in multiple secure locations, compartmentalize keys, and avoid pasting secrets into web pages.
Key Takeaway

Adopt specific defenses for each likely threat and keep recovery procedures simple and rehearsed so human error does not lead to permanent loss.
