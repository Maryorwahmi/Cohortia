Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Practical Safety
Separate environments
Use distinct keys for testing and production so experiments cannot affect real assets.
Hardware & backups
For significant funds, use hardware wallets and offline seed phrase backups stored securely in multiple locations.
Verify before signing
Signatures confirm content, so always verify the destination address and transaction details before approving a signature.
Final takeaway

Combine cryptographic understanding with disciplined key management to use blockchain securely and responsibly.
