Definition

Ether: Currency and Fuel

ETH is both the transferable native asset and the payment for executing operations on the network.

Paying Fees
Gas fees are denominated in Ether; cost is gasUsed times gasPrice.
Balance Needs
Senders must hold enough ETH to both transfer value and cover possible gas costs.
Design Impact
Frequent on-chain interactions increase ETH costs for users; optimize call frequency and storage use.
Concrete Example
Deploying a contract can consume significant Ether because of storage and bytecode that must be written during creation.
Misconception
Ether and Ethereum are not interchangeable terms: one is the network, the other is the currency used within it.
Consideration
Think about who pays for on-chain costs in your dApp: user, developer, or meta-transaction relayer patterns.
Key Takeaway

ETH is required to use the network and pay for computation; this economic link drives security and usage patterns.
