Learning Objectives — Skills & Tasks

Practical actions that follow from understanding.

Apply → Master
Inspect Transactions
Locate nonce, gas used, gas price, input data and infer what the contract call did.
Estimate Fees
Multiply gas used by gas price and reason about refunds and failed transactions.
Deploy & Identify Contracts
Understand how contract deployment creates a Contract Account that stores bytecode on-chain.
Avoid Common Mistakes
Recognize why contracts cannot send transactions autonomously and how that affects design.
