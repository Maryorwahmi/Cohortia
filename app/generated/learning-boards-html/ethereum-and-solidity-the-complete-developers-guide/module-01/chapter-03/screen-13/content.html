Best Practice

Designing for Gas and the EVM

Let gas costs and EVM constraints guide what stays on-chain versus off-chain.

Minimize Storage
Store only authoritative state and keep large data off-chain where possible.
Off-chain Computation
Compute heavy work off-chain and submit proofs or results to the contract.
Triggering Actions
Use external relayers or user-triggered patterns for scheduled or delayed tasks.
Concrete Pattern
Use pull payments to avoid loops that might revert if one transfer fails; let recipients withdraw funds at their convenience.
Pitfall
Trying to replicate complex server logic on-chain can be prohibitively expensive and error-prone.
Checklist
Ask whether a guarantee requires on-chain enforcement, how gas affects UX, and who pays fees in typical flows.
Key Takeaway

Design with gas and EVM boundaries in mind: keep critical guarantees on-chain and push non-essential work off-chain to save cost and complexity.
