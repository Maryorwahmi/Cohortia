Gas Limit, Gas Price, and Execution

Choose limits and prices to balance successful execution, cost, and inclusion speed.

Estimate: learn typical gasUsed for similar calls before sending.
GasLimit: caps computation; underestimating causes reversion and consumed gas is still charged.
GasPrice: influences fee and priority but does not affect gasUsed.
Refunds: unused gas is returned to the sender after execution completes.
Concrete Scenario
Calling a complex contract that writes multiple storage slots may need a high gasLimit. If you set gasLimit too low the transaction will revert but you still pay for the work done up to failure.
Tip
Use gasUsed from past, similar transactions as a baseline and add a safety margin to the limit.
Misunderstanding
Gas price affects cost and priority but not how much gas an operation consumes.
Key Takeaway

Pick gasLimit to safely cover expected computation and gasPrice to trade off cost versus inclusion speed; inspect similar transactions for guidance.
