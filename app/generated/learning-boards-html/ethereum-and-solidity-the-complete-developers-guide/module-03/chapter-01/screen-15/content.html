Visibility
Confirm public/internal for inherited members. Private members are not available to children.
Overrides
Use virtual on bases and override(BaseA, BaseB) on children to be explicit and satisfy the compiler.
Constructors
Forward parent constructor arguments or list them in the inheritance clause; test owner/state after deployment.
Final Tip

Run unit tests that capture ownership, override behavior, and constructor initialization to prevent regression and ensure predictable contract behavior.
