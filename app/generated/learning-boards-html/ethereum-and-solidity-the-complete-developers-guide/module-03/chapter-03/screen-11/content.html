Module 3›Chapter 3.3›Storage Slot Packing
Why packing matters
Fewer storage slots reduce gas when writing state. Group small types together to fit in 32-byte slots.
Example
uint128 a; uint128 b; uint16 c; // likely fit in one slot if declared consecutively
Best practice
Review and reorder struct fields when adding new variables to keep packing efficient.
Tooling note
Use compiler output and storage layout reports to verify packing after changes.
Key Takeaway
Careful field ordering reduces storage slots and yields meaningful gas savings over many writes.
