Comparison

onlyOwner vs role-based systems

onlyOwner
Single administrative key; simple but a single point of failure if compromised.
Role-based
Assign specific permissions for minting, pausing, or upgrading. Better separation of duties.
Practical guidance
  • Use the least privilege principle: restrict each function to the minimal role needed.
  • Protect admin keys with multisignature wallets for critical actions like upgrades or unpausing.
  • Document role assignments and enable role rotation or revocation processes.
Common mistake

Leaving public functions that can be called by anyone or assuming a private key will never be compromised.
