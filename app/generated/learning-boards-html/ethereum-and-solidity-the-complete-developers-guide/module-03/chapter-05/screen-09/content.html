Denial of Service by gas exhaustion

Unbounded loops used for payouts can be pushed past the block gas limit, preventing execution and freezing the function.

Example risk
A payout loop that iterates all recipients is vulnerable if the recipient list grows without bound.
Pull payment
Record owed amounts and let recipients call claim(). This avoids synchronous mass transfers and reduces reentrancy exposure.
Practical tips
  • Use pull payments for user claims to eliminate large synchronous transfers.
  • Avoid loops over unbounded arrays; if needed, implement pagination or batching.
  • Combine pull payments with CEI and reentrancy guards for defense in depth.
Common misconception

Assuming push payments are safe because they feel simpler. They can open reentrancy and DoS risks when recipients are contracts.
