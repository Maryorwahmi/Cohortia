Practice target

Complete the hands-on activity: pick a bridge, research its architecture, mechanism, and security posture, then write a 2–3 paragraph summary assessing trust.

Skill
Research
Find chains, mechanism, audits, incidents.
Skill
Assess
Map trust assumptions and risks.

Assessment idea

Describe the transfer steps for a lock-and-mint bridge and identify two potential failure points. Explain mitigation ideas suitable for a developer or a careful user.

Mnemonic
LOCK → MINT → BURN → UNLOCK
Remember the life cycle of assets moving across chains and where verification matters.
Verified competency
Produce a short report naming a bridge, its connected chains, its mechanism, its trust model, and any audits or incidents you found.
