Learning Objectives

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master

Verified competency milestones
Each objective includes a short assessment task you can use to verify understanding.
01
Explain interoperability
Describe why separate chains need cross-chain messaging and asset flow, and give a concrete user example such as moving ETH to a cheaper chain for a DApp.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe lock/burn mint patterns
Explain the lock-and-mint and burn-and-mint flows and how they preserve total supply across chains with a short on‑chain step list.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify bridge types
Classify bridges as centralized, federated, or decentralized and explain the trust assumptions of each class with a short mnemonic.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Recognize security risks
List concrete risks such as smart contract bugs, operator compromise, and oracle manipulation, and suggest two practical checks to reduce exposure.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
