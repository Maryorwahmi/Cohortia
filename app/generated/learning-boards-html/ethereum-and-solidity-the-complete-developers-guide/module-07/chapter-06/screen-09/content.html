Architecture

Multi‑signer custody & quorum rules

Signers collectively approve transfers by providing signatures to a multi-signature wallet or an off-chain coordinator. Transfers require a threshold to execute, reducing single-key risk but keeping governance and collusion as primary concerns.

- Compromise difficulty grows with required quorum but so does operational friction.
- Key rotation and transparent signer policies are critical security controls.
- Audits should include signer procedures and access controls.
Concrete scenario
A 5-of-7 multi-sig means five keys must sign to move funds. Attackers must compromise the majority, which raises the bar but does not remove risk.
Best practice
Prefer federated bridges with public signer identities, regular audits, and clear rotation policies to reduce hidden centralization risk.
