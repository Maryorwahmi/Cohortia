Concept

What is a wrapped token?

A wrapped token is a token contract minted on a destination chain to represent an asset from a source chain. Its trustworthiness is tied to the bridge’s ability to back or recreate the original asset on demand.

Check
Verify one-to-one backing and the redemption path before trusting a wrapped token.
Example & warning
If wETH is minted on a destination chain while the original ETH is locked on the source, the wrapped token should be exchangeable for the original only when the bridge correctly enforces burns or unlocks. If a bridge’s lock contract is buggy or its validators collude, the wrapped token can lose its backing and its peg.
Mnemonic
Representation, not native
Misconception
Wrapped tokens are not the native asset; they depend on bridge guarantees.
