Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Lock‑and‑Mint
Process

Lock → Mint → Burn → Unlock

1
Lock: user sends asset to a locking contract on the source chain and waits for confirmation.
2
Mint: bridge mints an equivalent wrapped token on the destination and credits the user.
3
Burn: to return, the user burns the wrapped token on the destination chain.
4
Unlock: bridge verifies burn and unlocks the original asset to the user on the source chain.
Concrete example
Moving 100 ETH to a rollup: 100 ETH locked on L1, 100 wETH minted on L2. Burn wETH and unlock to reverse.
Common mistake
Assuming the destination mint is final before the source lock has strong finality. Confirm the lock and the bridge verification method before trusting minted tokens.
Key Takeaway
Lock-and-mint preserves supply by holding the original asset locked; verification of lock and mint events is the security pivot.
