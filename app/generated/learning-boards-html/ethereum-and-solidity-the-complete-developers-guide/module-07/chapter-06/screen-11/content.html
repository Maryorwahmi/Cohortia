Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Security Risks
Risk categories
Smart contract bugs
Code flaws that allow state manipulation or fund drains.
Centralized points of failure
Operator or signer key compromise leading to theft.
Oracle manipulation
Tampering with data feeds that inform cross-chain actions.
Economic exploits
Attacks that exploit collateral, pricing, or incentive rules.
Practical checks
  • Confirm audits and recent fixes.
  • Limit funds per bridge and per transfer.
  • Prefer bridges with decentralized verification.
Warning
Bridges have historically been a high-risk component; treat them like a critical custody decision rather than a convenience feature.
