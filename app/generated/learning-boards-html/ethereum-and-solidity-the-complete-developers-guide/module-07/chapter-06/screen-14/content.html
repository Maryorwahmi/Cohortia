Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Key Concepts
Interoperability
Different chains exchanging assets and data with preserved guarantees.
Lock‑and‑Mint & Burn‑and‑Mint
Core mechanisms that preserve or shift supply via locking or burning.
Security Risks
Smart contract bugs, operator compromise, oracle attacks, and economic exploits.
Takeaway

Before bridging, identify the mechanism and trust model, check audits and governance, and limit exposure until you trust the design.
