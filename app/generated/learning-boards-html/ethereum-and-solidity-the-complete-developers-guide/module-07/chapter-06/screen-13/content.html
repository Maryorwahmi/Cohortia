Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Practical Guidance
User checklist
  • Confirm bridge trust model and audit history.
  • Limit funds per transfer and overall exposure.
  • Verify wrapped token contract addresses and redemption path.
Developer checklist
  • Minimize trusted operators and document signer governance.
  • Design robust verification with redundancy and audits.
  • Plan key rotation and emergency procedures.
Hands-on activity
Choose a bridge such as Hop Protocol, Synapse Protocol, Polygon Bridge, Wormhole, or LayerZero. Identify chains, mechanism, trust model, audits, and any incidents. Summarize your assessment in two to three paragraphs.
