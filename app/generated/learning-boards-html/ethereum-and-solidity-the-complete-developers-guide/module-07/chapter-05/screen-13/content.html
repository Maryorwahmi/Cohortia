Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Choosing an L2
Decision matrix
Instant finality: prefer ZK-Rollups.
Tooling & compatibility: prefer Optimistic Rollups.
Max throughput & low fees with different trust: consider Sidechains.
Pattern

Hybrid approaches are common: keep high-value settlement on rollups that derive L1 security and use sidechains for lower-value, high-throughput tasks. Always pilot and measure costs and latency before production rollout.
