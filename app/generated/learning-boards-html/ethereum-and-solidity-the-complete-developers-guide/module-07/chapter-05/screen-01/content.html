Understanding Layer 2 Scaling Solutions
Rollups, sidechains, and practical trade-offs for secure scaling

Ethereum’s base layer secures value and consensus, but cannot scale infinitely without high costs. Layer 2s process activity off-chain and rely on L1 for settlement or verification, improving throughput and lowering fees for DApps. Knowing the differences between Optimistic and ZK designs, and when sidechains are acceptable, helps you choose the right path for your application.
