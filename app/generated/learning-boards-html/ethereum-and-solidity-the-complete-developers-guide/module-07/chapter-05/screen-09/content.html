Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Optimistic vs ZK
Optimistic
  • Assume validity, allow challenges
  • Lower immediate verification cost
  • Withdrawal delays due to challenge window
ZK-Rollup
  • Generate validity proofs for each batch
  • Faster withdrawals and immediate finality
  • Higher prover complexity and cost
Decision guidance
Choose ZK when instant L1 finality is crucial, choose Optimistic when compatibility and lower verification cost matter. Model costs and user flows before committing.
