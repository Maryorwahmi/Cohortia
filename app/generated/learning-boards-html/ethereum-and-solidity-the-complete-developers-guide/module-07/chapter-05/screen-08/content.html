Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › ZK-Rollups
Definition

A ZK-Rollup produces a cryptographic proof that a batch of off-chain transactions leads to the claimed new state. L1 verifies the proof before accepting the batch.

Advantages
  • Instant verification on L1, no challenge window.
  • Faster withdrawals and stronger immediate finality.
Key Takeaway

ZK-Rollups trade prover complexity and cost for instant, cryptographic settlement on L1.

Concrete example

When a zkSync batch is posted, the L1 contract verifies the validity proof. If the proof passes, the batch is final and funds can be withdrawn quickly. The heavy work happens off-chain in the prover that constructs the proof.

Common-mistake
Believing proof generation is trivial. Proofing requires specialized compute and development effort, and it’s a major engineering consideration when choosing ZK approaches.
