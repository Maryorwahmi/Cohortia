Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Optimistic trade-offs
UX impact
  • Lower verifier cost on L1
  • Challenge window causes withdrawal delays
  • Better tooling compatibility with existing EVM stacks
Developer options
  • Accept delayed withdrawals for security.
  • Use liquidity providers for instant UX at cost and trust.
  • Implement bonds to economically discourage fraud.
Example decision

A marketplace for rare NFTs might accept a short withdrawal delay to keep fees low. A trading bot or arbitrage system that needs quick movement of funds likely cannot tolerate multi-day claims windows.

Pro tip

Model user flows and costs under peak load to decide if the challenge period breaks your UX requirements.
