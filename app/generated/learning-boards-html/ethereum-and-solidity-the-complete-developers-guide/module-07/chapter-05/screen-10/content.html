How sidechains work

A sidechain runs its own consensus and validator set. Assets are bridged by locking on L1 and minting equivalents on the sidechain. Security depends on the sidechain validators, not directly on Ethereum.

When to use
Use sidechains when throughput and low fees are priorities and when you can accept the sidechain's trust model for validator security.
Concrete example

Polygon PoS offers EVM compatibility and low fees. Developers can deploy Solidity contracts easily, but users must trust Polygon’s validator set and the bridge mechanics that move tokens between networks.

Reflect: does your app accept a separate validator trust model for faster performance and lower fees?
