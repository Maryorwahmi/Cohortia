Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Key Concepts Part 1
Layer 1 (L1)
The base chain where final settlement occurs and security anchors are trusted.
Rollups
Execute off-chain and post a batch summary or proof to L1 to reduce fees and increase throughput.
Sidechains
Independent EVM-compatible chains with their own validators and a separate trust model.
Summary
Use these concepts to choose an L2 based on required finality, costs, and acceptable trust assumptions.
