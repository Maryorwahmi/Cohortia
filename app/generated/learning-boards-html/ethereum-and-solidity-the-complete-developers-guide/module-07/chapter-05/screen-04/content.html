Module 7 › Chapter 7.5 › Why Layer 1 struggles
Definition

Layer 1 networks secure consensus and final settlement. Their design choices trade throughput for decentralization and security, limiting transaction capacity and making fees variable under load.

Symptom
High gas fees make micro-transactions uneconomical.
Misconception
Scaling always means sacrificing security. L2s can retain key L1 guarantees in different ways.
Key Takeaway

Identify which constraint—cost, throughput, or finality—is most critical before choosing an L2.

Concrete example

A decentralized game that records every move would require many small writes. On L1 this becomes expensive and slow. An L2 can record moves off-chain, compress them, and commit a summary to L1, enabling responsive gameplay with low cost.

Checklist to evaluate L1 limits
  • Average gas per transaction under normal and peak load
  • Acceptable withdrawal delay for users
  • Criticality of L1-native security for funds
