Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Operational Checklist
Verify addresses
Use environment configs or network maps to avoid using the wrong feed or coordinator address.
Enforce freshness
Require recent timestamps and matching round IDs before critical on-chain decisions.
Fund VRF
Always fund subscriptions with LINK and track request IDs for callback handling.
Final takeaway
Combine network verification, freshness checks, and subscription funding to make oracle-dependent contracts reliable and secure.
