Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Centralized vs Decentralized
Centralized API
Fast and simple but introduces a single point of trust and failure.
Pros
Low integration effort, predictable latency.
Cons
Single point of compromise, offline risk, and no native on-chain audit trail.
Chainlink Price Feeds
Aggregated answers from many nodes, posted on-chain and accessible via AggregatorV3Interface.
Pros
Tamper resistance, auditable history, reduced trust in any single node.
Notes
Feeds update on a cadence; always check timestamp and answeredInRound to decide if data is fresh enough for your logic.
Concrete example
A liquidation mechanism should prefer aggregated Chainlink prices to avoid manipulation that can occur when relying on a single exchange API.
Key Takeaway
Pick the model that matches your required integrity and acceptable latency.
