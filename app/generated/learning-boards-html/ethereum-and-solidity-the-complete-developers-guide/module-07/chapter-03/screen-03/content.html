Practical Objectives
Apply the concepts to a Hardhat workflow and secure contract code.
Import and instantiate feeds
Find the right feed address for your network and convert the 8-decimal returned price to readable units.
Write deployment scripts
Deploy the PriceConsumer contract and call the getter to verify the live price in your console output.
Handle freshness checks
Use answeredInRound and timestamp to avoid stale data; add require checks suitable for your app.
Implement VRF flow
Subscribe, fund with LINK, request randomness, and write the callback to receive the verified number.
