Interacting with Deployed Contracts on Testnets

Learn the exact pieces you need — address and ABI — then use Ethers.js or Web3.js to read data and send transactions, and verify results with a public block explorer.

In this lesson
Store the contract address and ABI, create a contract instance, perform read and write calls, and verify transactions on a block explorer.
Why it matters
Reliable client interactions power DApps. Properly handling Providers and Signers prevents errors and gives users a clear audit trail via transaction hashes.
Explore Key Topics
Address & ABI
Where the contract lives and how to call its functions.
Provider vs Signer
Read-only calls need a Provider; writes require a Signer to sign transactions.
Verification
Wait for tx confirmations and inspect the transaction hash on a public explorer for transparency.
