Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Quick Reference
Check ABI
Confirm ABI source matches the deployed build artifact before instantiating the contract.
Choose Provider/Signer
Use Provider for reads and Signer for writes to prevent missing-signer errors.
Await & Verify
Await tx.wait() and inspect the transaction hash on a block explorer to confirm the result.
Keep this checklist handy when moving from deployment to interaction to ensure predictable and auditable contract calls on testnets.
