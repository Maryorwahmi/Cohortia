Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
Contract Address
The unique on-chain location used to target your deployed contract.
ABI
A JSON blueprint describing functions and events so your client can encode and decode calls.
Provider vs Signer
Provider for reads, Signer for writes. Choose the right one to avoid missing-signer errors.
Summary Takeaway
Pair the correct address and ABI, use a Provider for free reads, attach a Signer for state changes, await confirmations with tx.wait(), and verify with a transaction hash on a public explorer to maintain transparency and reliability.
