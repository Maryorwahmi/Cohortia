Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Verify on Explorer
What to check
  • Transaction status and number of confirmations.
  • Gas used and gas price information.
  • Logs and events emitted by your contract.
If a transaction reverted, the explorer may display the failure. Use the hash to share details with users or teammates for quick diagnosis.
Practical example
Copy the printed hash from your interaction script and paste it into the testnet explorer to confirm the increment or transfer took place and to view any emitted events.
Key Takeaway

Explorers make transactions transparent and are the first stop for debugging and user support.
