Process › Getting Test Ether
Step-by-step
  1. Derive your wallet address from the PRIVATE_KEY and copy it.
  2. Visit a Sepolia faucet and paste the address, complete any required checks, and request funds.
  3. Wait for the faucet transaction and verify the balance via RPC or Sepolia Etherscan.
Pitfall
Using a faucet for a different testnet will leave your Sepolia wallet unfunded; double-check the network name.
Check
After requesting test Ether, confirm the balance with an RPC balance call or by entering the address into Sepolia Etherscan.
Practical note
If a faucet is rate-limited, schedule your request earlier or use multiple faucets to aggregate enough test Ether for your deployment gas costs.
Key Takeaway

Fund your deployer in advance and verify balance to avoid deployment interruptions caused by out-of-gas errors.
