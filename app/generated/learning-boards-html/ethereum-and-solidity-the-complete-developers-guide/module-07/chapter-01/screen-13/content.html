Core Concept › Troubleshooting
Out of Gas
Increase gasLimit or add test Ether from a faucet and retry.
RPC/API Key Errors
Confirm the provider URL, that the API key is active, and that it targets Sepolia.
Verification Failures
Re-check constructor args, compiler version, and optimization flags used during deployment.
Practical debugging checklist
  1. Check deployer balance on Sepolia.
  2. Confirm SEPOLIA_RPC_URL and provider key are correct.
  3. Increase gasLimit if needed and retry the transaction.
  4. Wait for mining and check Sepolia Etherscan for indexing delays.
