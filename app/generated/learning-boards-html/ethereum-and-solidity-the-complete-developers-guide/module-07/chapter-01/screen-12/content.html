Definition › Contract Verification
What verification does
It uploads your source code and compiler settings to the block explorer, which then attempts to match the deployed bytecode to your sources and publish a human-readable contract page.
Example command
npx hardhat verify --network sepolia  "ConstructorArg1"
Include the constructor arguments and ensure the compiler version and optimization settings match those used for deployment.
Troubleshooting
If verification fails, check the constructor arguments, compiler version, and optimization flags you used during compilation.
Key Takeaway

Verification completes the deployment cycle and makes your contract discoverable and auditable on Sepolia Etherscan.
