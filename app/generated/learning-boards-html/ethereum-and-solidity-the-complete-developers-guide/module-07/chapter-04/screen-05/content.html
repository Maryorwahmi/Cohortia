Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › DAO Principles
Core Principles
Transparency, decentralization, and community ownership guide every DAO decision.
Transparency
On-chain proposals and votes build trust and auditability. Consider privacy trade-offs for sensitive actions.
Decentralization
Reduces single points of control but requires mechanisms to prevent power concentration.
Ownership
Token allocation and delegation patterns determine who benefits and who governs.
Trade-off example
Short voting period enables fast decisions but risks low participation and rushed mistakes.
Warning
Concentrated token distribution with token-weighted voting can create plutocracy.
Progressive Decentralization
Start with guarded, centralized controls while the protocol matures. Gradually hand over roles and permissions as the community grows and audits confirm safety.
Key Takeaway
Balance responsiveness and safety: design voting periods, quorum, and token distribution to match the DAO's maturity and threat model.
