Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Three Quick Reminders
Delegate
Make sure token holders delegate to themselves or delegates to activate voting power.
Wire roles
Grant Governor proposer rights, timelock executor rights, and remove deployer admin keys.
Conservative params
Choose longer voting periods, reasonable quorum, and a sensible timelock during early stages.
Closing
These practical actions reduce the most common risks when moving from prototype to community governance.
