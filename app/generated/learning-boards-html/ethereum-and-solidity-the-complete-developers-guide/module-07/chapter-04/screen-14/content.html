Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Key Concepts
DAO
An organization governed by smart contracts with member voting instead of a central authority.
OpenZeppelin Modules
ERC20Votes, TimelockController, and Governor compose a standard DAO architecture for proposals, voting, and delayed execution.
Parameters Matter
Voting delay, voting period, and quorum determine responsiveness and legitimacy; choose conservative defaults and test them.
Final takeaway
Build with tested modules, wire roles carefully, and validate governance flows locally before decentralizing control.
