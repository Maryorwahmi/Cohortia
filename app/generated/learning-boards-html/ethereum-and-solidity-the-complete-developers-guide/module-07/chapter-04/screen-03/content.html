Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Skills & Assessment
Practical Skills
Concrete tasks you will perform to demonstrate applied understanding.
  • Deploy an ERC20Votes token and delegate votes to addresses for governance participation.
  • Configure a TimelockController with a non-trivial delay to protect treasury actions.
  • Deploy a Governor, tune voting delay, voting period, and quorum fraction.
  • Create, vote on, queue, and execute a proposal on a Hardhat local network.
Why practice this
Hands-on experience reveals timing, role, and parameter pitfalls that are invisible in theory. Testing locally lets you iterate safely before public deployment.
Assessment Checklist
A quick checklist for the deploy-and-propose exercise.
Deploy
Token, Timelock, Governor
Propose
Encode function call and create a proposal
Vote
Advance blocks and cast votes
Execute
Queue and execute after timelock
Small, intentional exercises build confidence. Your final check is being able to explain why a timelock prevents immediate execution of a passed proposal and how quorum affects legitimacy.
