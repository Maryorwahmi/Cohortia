Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Deploy & Test
Script steps
  1. Deploy MyToken and delegate votes for deployer and test voters.
  2. Transfer tokens to voter accounts for test participation.
  3. Deploy MyTimelock with minDelay, proposers, and executors.
  4. Deploy MyGovernor and grant it proposer and admin roles on the timelock.
  5. Create and propose an encoded mint transaction, advance blocks, cast votes, queue, advance time, and execute.
Simulation tips
In a local network you can advance blocks quickly with provider calls. Use this to simulate voting delays and periods deterministically. Always inspect logs for proposalId and state transitions, and check token balances after execute to confirm the payload ran.

Key Takeaway
Testing the full lifecycle locally is essential to validate role wiring, encoding of calldata, and correct timelock behaviour before mainnet deployment.
