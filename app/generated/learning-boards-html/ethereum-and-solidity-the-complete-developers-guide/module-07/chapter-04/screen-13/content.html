Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Mistakes & Best Practices
Common mistakes
  • Voting period too short → low participation and rushed decisions.
  • Quorum set too low → decisions by a small minority lack legitimacy.
  • Deployer retains timelock admin rights → centralization of execution control.
  • Incorrect role wiring prevents the Governor from queueing or executing proposals.
Best practices
Use conservative parameters initially, perform audits, and adopt progressive decentralization to hand over control gradually. Configure the timelock so the Governor is the proposer and remove unnecessary deployer admin roles. Document governance rules and quorum expectations to encourage informed participation.
Pro-tip
Run the full deploy and proposal test on a local network multiple times and validate logs and balances to confirm the end-to-end flow before any mainnet deployment.
