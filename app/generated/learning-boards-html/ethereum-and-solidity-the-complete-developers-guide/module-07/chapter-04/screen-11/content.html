Module 07 › Chapter 7.4 › Proposal Lifecycle
Stages
Propose
Create an action with targets and calldata.
Vote
Token holders cast votes during the voting period.
Queue
If passed, schedule on the timelock.
Execute
After the delay, the operation is executed on-chain.
Example: Proposal to mint 1,000 tokens to a test address. Propose the mint, wait voting delay, cast votes, confirm quorum and tally, queue the proposal to the timelock, wait minDelay, then execute to mint tokens. This demonstrates end-to-end governance control flow.
Checklist
  • Encode calldata correctly for target contract functions.
  • Advance blocks to reach voting period in tests.
  • Confirm delegates and voting power before voting.
  • Queue only passed proposals and respect timelock delay.
Key Takeaway
Execution is deliberately delayed. Use the timelock to buy time for detection and reaction to harmful proposals.
