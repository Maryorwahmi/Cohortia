Orientation

You will learn DAO principles, compare governance mechanisms like token-weighted and delegated voting, and assemble a minimal DAO using OpenZeppelin's ERC20Votes, TimelockController, and Governor contracts. We'll close with a practical deployment and a simulated proposal lifecycle so you can observe propose → vote → queue → execute in a reproducible environment.

Why this matters
DAOs transfer trust from individuals to code. When designed well, they enable collective control, transparent decision-making, and safer upgrades. Poor design risks plutocracy or catastrophic exploits.
