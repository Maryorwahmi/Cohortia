Process

From CID to on-chain reference

Automate publishing, pinning, and recording the CID so your contract always points to a verified asset.

Step
Publish files, capture the root CID, pin it, and then call the contract setter with that exact CID string.
Example check
After storing the CID, call tokenURI and resolve the returned pointer to confirm metadata loads from IPFS as expected.
Script snippet (visual)
// deploy contract, then interact to setCID
// call setTokenCID(1, 'YOUR_ROOT_CID') and then tokenURI(1) to verify
Pitfall
Recording a CID before pinning can lead to an on-chain pointer that later resolves to nothing if the CID gets garbage collected.
Key Takeaway

Automate the chain: publish, pin, then record the exact CID to guarantee the contract points to persistent content.
