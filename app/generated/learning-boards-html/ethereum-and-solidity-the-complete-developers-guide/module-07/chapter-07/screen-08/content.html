Definition

Pinning & pinning services

Pinning instructs nodes to keep specific CIDs indefinitely. Pinning services host pinned content across multiple nodes so your data remains available.

How to use
After you receive a CID, submit it to a pinning service to ensure it is stored by reliable nodes that remain online.
Examples
Pinning services maintain copies of your content so the CID resolves even if your original node is offline.
Concrete workflow
Add a build directory, receive a root CID, then use a pinning service to pin that root CID. Confirm the service reports the CID as pinned.
Why it matters
Without pinning, content can be garbage collected and cease to exist on the network, even if you previously published it.
Common mistake
Relying on a single personal node to keep content alive for production use. Use a pinning service for reliability.
