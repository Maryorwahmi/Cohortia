Assessment

Practice questions

Question 1
Explain why using a centralized URL in a smart contract threatens immutability and how IPFS/CIDs address that risk.
Question 2
Your DApp CID no longer resolves after weeks. Outline the most likely cause and a step-by-step repair plan.
Rubric hints
  • Mention single point of failure and mutable hosts for Q1.
  • Mention garbage collection and pinning for Q2.
  • Include verification steps like cat and cross-node retrieval.
Reflection prompt
Which asset in your project would cause the most damage if lost, and how would you protect it using IPFS and pinning?
Key Takeaway

Use assessments to rehearse both the conceptual justification for IPFS and the operational steps for keeping content available.
