Decentralized Storage
Storage not reliant on a single server, improving censorship resistance and availability.
IPFS
A peer-to-peer protocol for content-addressed file storage and retrieval.
Content Addressing
Identifying files by a cryptographic hash of their content rather than a location URL.
Quick summary

Use IPFS CIDs as immutable references for assets, pin them for persistence, and use gateways only for browser compatibility.
