Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Quick Reference
Instantiate
Address + ABI + Provider = usable contract instance for reads.
Read-only calls
Use provider-only calls for view and pure functions to avoid gas and signing.
Handle errors
Try/catch calls, map raw errors to helpful messages, and check address → ABI → provider when debugging.
Final thought
Master the pairing of ABI and address, and your DApp will reliably reflect on-chain truth without unnecessary gas costs.
