Assessment walkthrough
Why wrapping window.ethereum with Ethers.js is the right sequence after a user clicks Connect.
Correct flow
eth_requestAccounts → new ethers.providers.Web3Provider(window.ethereum) → provider.getSigner()
Why other options fail
  • JsonRpcProvider to localhost ignores wallet signing session.
  • Constructing a Wallet from an address lacks a private key and cannot sign.
Final reflection

This pattern keeps private keys inside the wallet while letting your DApp request signatures from the user's active account. It preserves security and aligns with EIP-1193 expectations.
