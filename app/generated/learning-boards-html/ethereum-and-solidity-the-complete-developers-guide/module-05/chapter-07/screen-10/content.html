Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Provider vs Signer
Provider (reads)
  • Calls view functions, no gas required.
  • No signing prompt necessary.
  • Safe for initial state loads and refreshes.
Signer (writes)
  • Used for state-changing methods; requires signature.
  • Always builds a transaction that consumes gas.
  • Attach signer to Contract for setGreeting and similar calls.
Practical pattern
Maintain two contract references: providerContract for reads and signerContract for writes. This reduces unnecessary wallet prompts and clarifies intent in code.
