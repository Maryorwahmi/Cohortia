Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › What to Remember
Consent & Reinit
Request permission and reinitialize on account or chain changes.
Gas & UX Safeguards
Estimate gas, add buffer, and disable controls during pending operations.
Actionable Errors
Parse revert reasons and display short, helpful next steps to users.
These simple rules reduce confusion and make asynchronous blockchain interactions predictable for users. Keep them as a checklist during development and testing.
