Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Estimate Gas
Estimate pattern
Call the RPC estimate methods to get a suggested gas amount, then add a buffer. Example: estimated 80,000 → submit 100,000.
Web3.js: methods.myFunction().estimateGas({ from, value })
Ethers.js: myContract.estimateGas.myFunction(args)
Key Takeaway

Never guess gas: estimate programmatically and add a margin to avoid wasted reverts.

Practical checklist
  • Estimate gas for the exact call and parameters.
  • Add a small buffer to the estimate to tolerate variability.
  • Verify the sender’s balance covers gasLimit * gasPrice + value.
  • Log gasUsed from receipts to refine future estimates.
Common mistake
Setting gas too low to save cost often results in reverts and wasted fees; estimate instead of guessing.
