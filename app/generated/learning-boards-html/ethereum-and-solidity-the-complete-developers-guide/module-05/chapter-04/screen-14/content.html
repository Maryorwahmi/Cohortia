Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Transaction
A signed message that modifies blockchain state and requires gas to execute.
Gas
A unit of computation; used to measure how much work the network performs for a transaction.
Gas Limit & Price
Gas limit caps units consumed; gas price sets Wei paid per unit and influences inclusion speed.
Summary
Use estimates, show pending status, await receipts, and surface clear errors to users to build robust DApps.
