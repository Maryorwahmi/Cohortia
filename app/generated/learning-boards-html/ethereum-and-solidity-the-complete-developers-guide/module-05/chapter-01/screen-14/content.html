Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Libraries
Ethers.js and Web3.js provide the helpers to call contracts, parse data, and integrate wallets.
Provider
Connects your app to a node: browser wallet, hosted endpoint, or local node.
Signer
Authorizes transactions with a private key, usually via a wallet; providers do not sign.
Summary

Use a maintained library, choose the provider type to match your needs, and always protect signing keys. With these three principles you can build safe, interactive dApps.
