Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Key Concepts
Access Control
Restrict functions to intended callers using msg.sender and centralized modifiers.
Front-running
Mempool visibility allows ordering attacks; use commit-reveal, batching, or slippage to mitigate.
Denial-of-Service
Avoid unbounded loops and push payments; prefer pull-over-push and pagination to keep functions usable.
Final takeaway

Design for the caller you trust, hide sensitive data where necessary, and prove your defenses with attacker-style tests. Small design choices protect both funds and availability.
