Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Objectives — Practice
Demonstrable skills
Authorize
Use msg.sender-based checks and explicit modifiers instead of tx.origin. Verify the check with tests that attempt unauthorized calls.
Secret
Protect time-sensitive inputs with commit-reveal to stop mempool observation and front-running.
Availability
Avoid unbounded loops and push payments; prefer pagination and pull-withdraw patterns.
Tests
Write attacker-style tests: malicious relays, unauthorized signers, and large datasets that stress gas limits.
Mnemonic
Remember: Caller = msg.sender, Origin = tx.origin. Authorize the caller you intend to trust.
