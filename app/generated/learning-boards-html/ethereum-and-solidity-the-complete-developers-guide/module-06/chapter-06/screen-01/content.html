Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Overview
Access Control, Front-running, Tx.origin
A focused, practical tour of caller identity mistakes, mempool races, and DoS loops with code-first examples and testing advice.
In this lesson

You’ll learn the difference between msg.sender and tx.origin, see a phishing relay exploit, patch it using msg.sender or onlyOwner, study front-running mitigations like commit-reveal and batching, and identify DoS vectors such as unbounded loops and blocking external calls.

Why it matters

These vulnerabilities can lead to stolen funds, unfair market advantage, or a contract becoming permanently unusable. Small design choices have large security consequences.

Risk
Trusting the wrong caller or exposing time-sensitive info creates easy exploit paths.
Fix
Use msg.sender-based checks, explicit modifiers, commit-reveal for secrecy, and pull-over-push for payments.
