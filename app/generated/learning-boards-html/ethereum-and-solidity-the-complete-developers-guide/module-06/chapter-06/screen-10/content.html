Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Commit-Reveal
Process steps
1. Commit
User submits a hash of their secret plus nonce. The contract stores the commit without revealing the secret.
2. Wait / Window
A short waiting period or batch ensures commits are collected before reveals.
3. Reveal
User sends the clear value and nonce. The contract verifies the hash and then executes the intended action.
Notes & pitfalls
  • Handle missing reveals: decide whether to cancel commits or penalize non-reveals.
  • Ensure reveal windows are long enough for honest users but short enough to limit latency.
  • Remember this adds complexity and gas overhead; evaluate cost vs benefit.
Misconception: commit-reveal eliminates all front-running. It obscures values but needs correct phase management to be effective.
