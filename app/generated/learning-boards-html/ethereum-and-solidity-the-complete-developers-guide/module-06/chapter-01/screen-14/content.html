Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Immutability
Code cannot be changed after mainnet deployment, so pre-deploy tests must be exhaustive.
Gas Costs
Measure and assert gas usage as part of tests to ensure economic viability.
Adversarial Testing
Write negative tests, fuzz inputs, and assert invariants to anticipate attacks.
Summary

Combine unit, integration, end-to-end, and adversarial tests with gas analysis to reduce the risk of irreversible issues after deployment.
