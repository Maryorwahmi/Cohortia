Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Specialized Tests: Fuzzing & Property-Based
Fuzzing
Randomized inputs aim to find crashes, reverts, or invariant violations you didn't expect.
Automate thousands of random calls and inspect any failures found.
Reproduce failures deterministically and add regression tests.
Property-Based Testing
Declare invariants and let the test runner try to find inputs that break them.
Example property: total supply unchanged after valid transfers.
If violated, capture counterexample and write deterministic test to lock it down.
Practical Tip

Integrate fuzzing into CI so new commits are exercised with randomized inputs regularly.
