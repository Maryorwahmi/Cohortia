The Imperative of Smart Contract Testing
Why immutability and economic risk make testing non-negotiable

Smart contracts cannot be patched on mainnet and often control value. Testing must therefore verify correctness, gas efficiency, and resistance to attacks. This lesson sets the framework for unit, integration, and end-to-end approaches and highlights adversarial testing methods.
