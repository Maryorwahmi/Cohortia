Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › CEI & Payment Patterns
Checks → Effects → Interactions
Order operations to reduce reentrancy risk.
Pattern
Checks: validate inputs and permissions. Effects: update state. Interactions: perform external calls last.
Example
Withdraw function reduces balance before transferring funds to the user, preventing reentry into the unchanged state.
Misconception
Reentrancy guards help but should not replace correct ordering and cautious external-call handling.
Pull over Push
Prefer user-initiated withdrawals to forced transfers.
Why Pull?
Pull reduces the impact of failed or malicious external receivers and isolates payment logic from state updates.
Testing
Write tests that simulate failed external calls and assert that internal invariants hold and funds remain withdrawable.
Key Takeaway
Ordering and pull payments are practical defenses with low overhead and high audit value.
