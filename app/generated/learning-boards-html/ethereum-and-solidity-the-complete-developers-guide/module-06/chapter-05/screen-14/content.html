Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Reentrancy

An external call can reenter your contract and repeat operations if state is not updated first.

Checks-Effects-Interactions

Perform checks, then update state, and finally make external interactions to minimize reentrancy windows.

Reentrancy Guard

A mutex-like guard blocks nested calls and makes reentry explicit and auditable.

Summary

Combine ordering, guards, and tests to form a provable defense against nested-call attacks.
