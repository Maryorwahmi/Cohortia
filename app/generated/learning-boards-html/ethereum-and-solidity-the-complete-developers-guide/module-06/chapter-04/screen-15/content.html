Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › Remember
Mine after time change
evm_increaseTime only sets the next block timestamp; evm_mine applies it.
Account for gas
Balance assertions should consider gas costs and use tolerant comparisons like closeTo.
Snapshot heavy setups
Use snapshots to avoid slow redeploys when the initial state is expensive to create.
Final thought
Adopt one pattern at a time, measure its effect on test speed and flakiness, and expand your toolkit gradually.
