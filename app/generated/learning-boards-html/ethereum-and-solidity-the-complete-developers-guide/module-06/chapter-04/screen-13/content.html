Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › Common Mistakes
Top causes of flaky tests
  • Forgetting evm_mine after evm_increaseTime so timestamps don't apply.
  • Making exact balance assertions without accounting for gas.
  • Shared mutable state between tests due to missing snapshots or redeploys.
  • Mismatched behavior between mocks and real contracts.
Checklist to fix a flaky test
1) Re-check whether the test needs a mined block after time change. 2) Convert balance equality to a closeTo with gas accounted. 3) Isolate state by snapshotting or redeploying. 4) Replace or augment mocks with a thin real implementation if behavior differs.
Quick win
Switch a fragile exact balance assertion to a closeTo check that deducts gas. Many failing tests recover with this single change.
