Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › Snapshot vs Redeploy
Redeploy (beforeEach)
  • Simple and explicit; each test gets fresh contracts.
  • Best when deployments are cheap or tests require different configs.
  • Less chance of accidental state carryover.
Snapshot (before + snapshot)
  • Faster for suites with an expensive identical setup.
  • Requires careful snapshot id handling and understanding of revert behavior.
  • Pairs well with mocks and time manipulation done after the snapshot.
Decision checklist
Is setup expensive?
Are configs identical?
Do tests need fresh deployments?
