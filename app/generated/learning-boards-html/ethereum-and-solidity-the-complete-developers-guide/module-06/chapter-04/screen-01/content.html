Advanced Testing Patterns & Strategies
Use Hardhat's EVM controls, snapshots, gas checks, and mocks to make tests accurate and fast.
In this lesson
Simulate time, snapshot the EVM, analyze gas, and mock dependencies so your smart contract tests reflect production behavior without becoming slow or brittle.
Coming up
Hands-on examples include a TimeLockVault test that fast-forwards time and an Auction activity that demonstrates a full time-based flow with assertions and event checks.
Starter takeaway

These patterns reduce flakiness and speed tests: time control for temporal logic, snapshotting for expensive setups, gas checks for regressions, and mocks for external dependencies.
