Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Key Concepts
Control Structures
If/else direct execution and enforce simple rules in functions.
Loops
For and while repeat work; bound them or move computation off-chain.
Error Handling
Require/revert refund gas; assert signals bugs and consumes gas.
Takeaway
Write clear guards, design bounded loops, and choose error primitives that match intent to protect contracts and users.
