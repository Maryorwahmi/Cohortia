Hands-on Activity
VotingContract steps
  1. Paste the VotingContract starter into Remix and compile.
  2. Deploy with a small candidate array like ["Alice","Bob","Charlie"].
  3. Call vote before toggling voting — observe VotingNotActive revert.
  4. toggleVoting as owner, then vote successfully.
  5. Try duplicate vote and invalid candidate and inspect reverts.
What to observe
  • Revert reasons appear in the console for require strings and custom errors appear as structured reverts.
  • State is unchanged when a revert occurs after partial work.
  • Constructor loops are executed at deployment and are acceptable for small initial data.
Challenge
Replace require messages with custom errors in the vote function and compare gas in Remix.
Key Takeaway
Testing the contract in Remix reveals how control flow and error handling interact in live transactions.
