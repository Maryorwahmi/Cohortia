Quick Reminders

Pick sizes
Use the smallest integer width that fits your data domain.
Mark payable
Use address payable for transfer recipients to make intent explicit.
Test locations
Write unit tests that assert whether changes persist or are local copies.
Final takeaway

Types and data locations are not just syntax; they are essential design choices that affect gas, security, and correctness. Make them explicit and test them.
