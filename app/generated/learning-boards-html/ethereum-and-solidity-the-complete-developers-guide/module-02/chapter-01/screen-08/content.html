Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Compilation in Remix
Step-by-step
  1. Open the Solidity Compiler plugin.
  2. Select a compiler version that satisfies the pragma, typically 0.8.x.
  3. Click Compile and watch editor highlights for errors.
  4. Read console messages and address syntax or semantic issues.
  5. Confirm ABI and bytecode appear on successful compilation.
Compiler outputs
ABI and Bytecode
ABI lets external apps call your functions; bytecode is what you deploy to the virtual machine.
Common mistakes
Selecting a compiler outside the pragma range, or ignoring warnings, often leads to unexpected runtime behavior or deployment failures.
Key Takeaway

Compiler messages are diagnostic tools; learn their structure to fix errors quickly and produce correct ABIs and bytecode.
