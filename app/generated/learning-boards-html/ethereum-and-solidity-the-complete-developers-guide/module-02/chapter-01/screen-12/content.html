Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Common Mistakes
Pragma mismatch
Pick a compiler inside the pragma range
If the pragma uses 0.8.0 caret range, select a 0.8.x compiler. Mismatches lead to compile or semantic errors.
Syntax errors
Missing semicolons, braces, or parentheses
Compiler messages point to exact lines; use them to navigate directly to the problem in the editor.
Visibility mistakes
Public vs internal vs private
Choosing incorrect visibility can expose functions or prevent intended access. Be intentional about visibility for safety.
Troubleshooting steps
  1. Compile after small edits.
  2. Click error links to jump to lines in the editor.
  3. Read warnings; they often suggest improvements.
