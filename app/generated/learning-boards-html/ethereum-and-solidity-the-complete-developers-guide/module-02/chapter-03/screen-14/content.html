Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Visibility
Control who calls a function; default to the most restrictive visibility that fits your needs.
Mutability
Use view/pure for reads and pure computations; payable allows Ether into a function; no specifier means state may change.
State & Access Control
State lives on-chain and is costly to change. Use msg.sender and require to guard privileged actions and follow checks-effects-interactions.
Takeaway
Make access, mutability, and state changes explicit in your code. These choices determine security, cost, and correctness.
