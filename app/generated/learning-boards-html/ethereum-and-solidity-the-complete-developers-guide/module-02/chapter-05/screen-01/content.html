Events and Logging

Events are compact, gas-efficient logs your contract emits to communicate with the off-chain world. They record what happened without adding on-chain state.

Why events matter

Events connect contracts to UIs, explorers, and analytic systems. They are permanent, cheaper than state, and optimized for search when properly indexed. You will practice declaring, emitting, and filtering events.

Use case
Real-time UI updates
Listen for Deposit events to update balances without polling.

Analytics

Off-chain triggers
Emit events to trigger external services like notifications.
