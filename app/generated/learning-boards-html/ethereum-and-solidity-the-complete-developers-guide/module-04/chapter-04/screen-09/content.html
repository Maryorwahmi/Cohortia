Process Flow

Execute migrations and read output

truffle migrate compiles, runs pending migrations, and logs deployment details you can verify.

Starting migrations...
> Network name: 'development'
1_initial_migration.js
  Deploying 'Migrations' ...
  > transaction hash: 0x...
  > contract address: 0x...
2_deploy_simplestorage.js
  Deploying 'SimpleStorage' ...
  > transaction hash: 0x...
  > contract address: 0x...
Look for the contract address line — that is where your deployed contract lives on the local chain.
Troubleshooting
If you see a "Could not connect to the network" error, confirm Ganache is running and truffle-config points to the right host and port.
Tip

Use truffle migrate --reset to force re-running all migrations if you need to redeploy changed contracts.
