Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Verify in Ganache
Comparison

Terminal output vs Ganache UI

Terminal
Shows transaction hashes, gas used, and deployed addresses that Truffle recorded.
Ganache UI
Shows blocks, transaction entries, and updated account balances to confirm the chain state.
Quick checklist
  • Did the terminal show a contract address for SimpleStorage?
  • Is there a new block in Ganache corresponding to that transaction?
  • Did the deploying account’s balance decrease by the gas cost?
Key Takeaway

Cross-verify terminal output with Ganache to ensure deployments actually changed chain state.
