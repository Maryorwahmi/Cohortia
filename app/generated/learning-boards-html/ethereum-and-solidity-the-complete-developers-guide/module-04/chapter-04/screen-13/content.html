Key Concepts — Part 1

Three short reminders to carry forward.

Migrations
Ordered scripts that Truffle executes and tracks.
artifacts.require
Loads the compiled contract abstraction (ABI + bytecode).
deployer
Orchestrates sending creation transactions and records addresses.
Summary
These components bridge compile time to runtime and let you verify deployments on Ganache.
