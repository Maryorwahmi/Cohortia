Key Concepts — Part 2

Three practical reminders and a final verification step.

Start Ganache
Ensure Ganache is running before migrating to avoid connection errors.
Use --reset
Force re-running migrations after contract changes to deploy updated bytecode.
Verify addresses
Match the contract address from the terminal to the one shown in Ganache.
Final takeaway
These checks make deployments reliable and your local development environment consistent.
