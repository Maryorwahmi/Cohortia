Diagnosing failures
Look at the failing test message and stack trace, map the assertion to your test file, reproduce the failing case in isolation, then fix the contract or the test accordingly.
Negative-case advice
If a test expects a revert, assert that the revert occurs rather than treating any revert as a failure. Explicit expectations clarify intent and prevent false negatives.
Quick takeaway

Run tests often and read failures carefully; the terminal output is your fastest route to resolving issues before deployment.
