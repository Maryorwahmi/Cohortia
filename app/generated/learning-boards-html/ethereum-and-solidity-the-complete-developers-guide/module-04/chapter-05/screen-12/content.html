Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Truffle, Ganache, Console
Ganache
Local node
Provides accounts, mining, and a deterministic chain for local testing.
Truffle
Tooling & artifacts
Compiles, migrates, and stores contract ABIs and addresses used by deployed().
Console
Interactive runtime
Exposes contract abstractions, web3, and accounts for direct interaction and testing.
Why this matters
If any component is misconfigured or out of sync — for example, Ganache restarted without re-running migrations — deployed() will not locate instances. Knowing this mapping helps you quickly identify and fix connection issues.
