Local Ethereum Development

Ganache
A personal blockchain providing pre-funded accounts, instant transactions, and transaction logs for debugging.
Network ID & RPC
Always confirm the RPC server address and network ID shown by Ganache and mirror them in your project.
Summary takeaway

Ganache accelerates development by giving you a local, controllable chain with accounts, logs, and reset capability—use it to iterate quickly and debug confidently.
