Local Ethereum Development
Testing contracts in a private, simulated environment to avoid cost and delay.
Ganache
Tool that runs a personal blockchain and shows accounts, logs, and an RPC endpoint.
Network ID
Numeric chain identifier; must match between Ganache and your project's configuration.
Final takeaway

Keep Ganache running, confirm RPC and network ID, and use pre-funded accounts and logs to iterate safely and quickly.
