Best Practice

Turning Practice into Proof: Documenting Your Labs

Detailed write-ups transform experience into portfolio assets.

Why Document?

  • Verifiable Proof: Provides concrete evidence of your technical skills.
  • Showcases Problem-Solving: Details your approach to challenges.
  • Communication Skills: Demonstrates ability to explain complex technical processes.
Pro Tip

Include screenshots and command outputs to visually validate each step of your process.

Essential Documentation Sections

Project Objective

Clearly state what you aim to achieve.

Environment Setup

Details on VMs, hypervisor, network.

Exploitation Steps

Detailed procedure, commands, and observations.

Remediation & Lessons

How to fix vulnerabilities and key learnings.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive documentation is as important as the technical work itself, showcasing your full understanding of the security lifecycle.
