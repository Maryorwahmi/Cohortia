Incident Response Analyst: Crisis Commander

Containing, eradicating, and recovering from security breaches.

  • Containment: Isolate affected systems to prevent further spread.
  • Eradication: Remove the threat from the environment.
  • Recovery: Restore systems and data to normal operations.
  • Post-Incident Analysis: Learn from the incident to prevent future occurrences.
Example Scenario

An IR analyst leads the response to a ransomware attack, isolating infected servers, eradicating the malware, and restoring data from secure backups.

Key Takeaway

IR Analysts are critical during security crises, minimizing damage and ensuring business continuity through swift and methodical response.
