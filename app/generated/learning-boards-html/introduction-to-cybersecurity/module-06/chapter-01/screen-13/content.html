Key Concepts: Core Roles

Essential cybersecurity roles and their primary functions.

SOC Analyst

Frontline defenders who monitor security systems, analyze alerts, and respond to incidents.

Cybersecurity Engineer

Designs, builds, and maintains secure systems, implementing security controls and best practices.

Cybersecurity Architect

Develops high-level security strategies, frameworks, and policies for an organization's entire infrastructure.

Summary Takeaway

These roles are fundamental to establishing and maintaining a strong, resilient cybersecurity posture within any organization.
