APT Lifecycle: Gaining Initial Access
From Information Gathering to System Breach
Phase 1

Reconnaissance

Attackers gather extensive information about the target, including network topology, employee details, and existing security measures. This phase is about understanding the target's defenses and identifying vulnerabilities.

  • Gathering public information (OSINT)
  • Mapping network infrastructure
  • Identifying key personnel for targeting
Phase 2

Initial Compromise

Attackers gain initial unauthorized access to the target system or network. This is the first breach of the perimeter, often achieved through highly targeted methods.

  • Spear-phishing emails targeting specific individuals
  • Exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities
  • Compromising supply chain partners
Key Takeaway

The initial stages of an APT involve extensive information gathering (reconnaissance) and a targeted breach (initial compromise), often leveraging social engineering or unknown vulnerabilities.
