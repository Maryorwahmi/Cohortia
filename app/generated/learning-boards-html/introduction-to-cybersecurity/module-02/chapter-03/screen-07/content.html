APT Lifecycle: Persistence & Exfiltration
Establishing Long-Term Presence and Data Theft
Phase 5

Establishing Persistence

Attackers ensure they can maintain access to the compromised system even if initial vulnerabilities are patched or systems are rebooted. This guarantees long-term control.

  • Installing backdoors or rootkits
  • Modifying system startup scripts
  • Creating hidden user accounts
Phase 6

Data Exfiltration

The ultimate goal for many APTs: silently siphoning off sensitive information from the compromised environment. This is often the final strategic objective.

  • Encrypting and sending data over covert channels
  • Breaking data into small packets to evade detection
  • Using legitimate cloud services as staging grounds
Key Takeaway

Establishing persistence ensures long-term access, while data exfiltration is the stealthy removal of sensitive information, often the final objective of an APT.
