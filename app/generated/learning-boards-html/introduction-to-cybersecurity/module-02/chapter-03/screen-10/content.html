Threat Definition

Unintentional Insider

An insider who poses a threat due to carelessness, lack of awareness, or human error, rather than malicious intent. This is a common and often underestimated risk.

Common Examples
  • Falling for phishing scams and revealing credentials.
  • Losing an unencrypted laptop with sensitive data.
  • Accidentally misconfiguring a server or cloud service.
Impact & Prevention

Severe Consequences

While not malicious, the impact of unintentional insider threats can be just as severe as malicious ones, leading to data breaches, financial losses, and reputational damage.

Prevention Strategies

Comprehensive security awareness training, strong security policies, and Data Loss Prevention (DLP) systems are crucial for mitigating unintentional insider risks.

Key Takeaway

Unintentional insider threats, though not malicious, can cause significant harm through error or negligence, emphasizing the need for robust training and preventative controls.
