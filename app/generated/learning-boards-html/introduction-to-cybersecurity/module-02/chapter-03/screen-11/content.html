Core Concept

Data Exfiltration Defined

Data exfiltration is the unauthorized transfer of data from a computer or network. It is often the ultimate goal of advanced persistent threats (APTs) and malicious insider activities, aiming to steal sensitive information.

Common Methods
  • Encrypting data and sending it over encrypted channels.
  • Breaking data into small packets to evade detection.
  • Using legitimate cloud storage services as a staging ground.
Impact & Prevention

Severe Consequences

Data exfiltration can lead to massive financial losses, regulatory fines, severe reputational damage, and loss of competitive advantage for an organization.

Prevention Strategies

Network segmentation, strict access controls, robust logging, and anomaly detection systems are crucial for preventing and detecting data exfiltration.

Key Takeaway

Data exfiltration is the unauthorized removal of data, a critical objective for advanced threats, necessitating comprehensive monitoring and layered defenses to protect sensitive information.
