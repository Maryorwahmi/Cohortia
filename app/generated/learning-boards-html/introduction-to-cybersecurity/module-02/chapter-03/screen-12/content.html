Key Concepts Revisited
Essential Terms from Advanced Threats

Advanced Persistent Threat (APT)

A stealthy, continuous attack by nation-states or organized groups, characterized by sophisticated tools, long-term access, and specific targeting for espionage or strategic advantage.

Reconnaissance

The initial phase of an APT attack where attackers gather extensive information about the target, including network topology, employee details, and security measures.

Data Exfiltration

The unauthorized transfer of data from a computer or network, often the ultimate goal of APTs and malicious insiders, leading to significant organizational damage.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding APTs, their reconnaissance phase, and the critical objective of data exfiltration is fundamental to recognizing and defending against sophisticated cyber threats.
