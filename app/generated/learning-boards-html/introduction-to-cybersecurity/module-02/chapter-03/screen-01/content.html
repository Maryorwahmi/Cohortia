Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs) and Insider Threats
Unmasking the Stealthiest Cyber Adversaries

This chapter will equip you with the knowledge to identify, understand, and strategize against highly sophisticated and often long-term cyber attacks, originating from both external and internal sources. You'll learn to differentiate these threats from more common attacks and recognize their unique characteristics.

Key Idea

APTs and Insider Threats represent advanced, often long-term risks from both external and internal sources, demanding sophisticated detection and prevention strategies.
