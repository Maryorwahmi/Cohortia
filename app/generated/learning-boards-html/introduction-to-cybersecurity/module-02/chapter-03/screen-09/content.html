Threat Definition

Malicious Insider

An insider who deliberately uses their authorized access to steal data, sabotage systems, or otherwise harm the organization. Their actions are intentional and driven by specific motives.

Common Motivations
  • Financial Gain: Selling data or access for money.
  • Revenge: Disgruntled employees seeking to harm the company.
  • Corporate Espionage: Stealing secrets for competitors.
Example & Detection

Employee Data Theft

An employee about to leave for a competitor downloads sensitive customer lists or intellectual property onto a personal USB drive.

Detection Strategies

Technical monitoring (unusual data access, large transfers) and behavioral analysis (changes in conduct) are key to identifying malicious insiders.

Key Takeaway

Malicious insiders are driven by intent to harm, leveraging their access for personal gain or revenge, requiring vigilant monitoring of both technical and behavioral indicators.
