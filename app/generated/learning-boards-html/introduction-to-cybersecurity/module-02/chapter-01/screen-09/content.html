Malware Mechanics

Trojans and Ransomware

Disguise, Deception, and Digital Hostage-Taking

Trojans (Trojan Horse)

Malware in Disguise

  • •Disguises itself as legitimate software.
  • •Carries a hidden malicious payload (e.g., backdoor, data theft).
  • •Relies on user deception to be installed and executed.
Example

Downloading a 'free' game that secretly installs a keylogger to steal your passwords in the background.

Ransomware

Encrypts Files, Demands Payment

  • •Encrypts a victim's files, making them inaccessible.
  • •Demands a ransom (often cryptocurrency) for the decryption key.
  • •Can cause significant data loss and operational disruption.
Example

The WannaCry attack encrypted files on hundreds of thousands of computers worldwide, demanding Bitcoin for their release.

Key Takeaway

Trojans rely on deception for installation, while ransomware directly impacts data availability by encrypting files and demanding payment.
