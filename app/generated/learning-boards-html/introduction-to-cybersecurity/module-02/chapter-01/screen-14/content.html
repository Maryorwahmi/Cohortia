Key Concepts: Part 1

Reinforcing Core Understanding

Attack Vector

The path or method used by an attacker to gain unauthorized access to a system, network, or data.

Phishing

Fraudulent communications disguised as legitimate sources to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information.

Malware

Malicious software designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to computer systems.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding attack vectors, phishing, and malware is foundational to recognizing and defending against the most common cyber threats.
