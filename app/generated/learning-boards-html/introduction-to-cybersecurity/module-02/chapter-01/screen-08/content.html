Malware Mechanics

Viruses vs. Worms

Understanding Their Spread and Impact

Computer Viruses

Requires a Host & User Action

  • •Attaches to legitimate programs or documents.
  • •Needs user interaction (e.g., opening a file) to activate and spread.
  • •Cannot self-replicate independently across networks.
Example

An infected Word document is opened, and the virus then spreads to other executable files on the local machine.

Computer Worms

Self-Replicating & Autonomous

  • •Standalone malicious programs.
  • •Can self-replicate and spread across networks without user intervention.
  • •Often exploit software vulnerabilities to propagate.
Example

A worm exploits a vulnerability in an operating system, spreading rapidly to other unpatched computers on the network.

Key Takeaway

Viruses require a host and user action to spread, while worms are self-contained and can propagate autonomously across networks.
