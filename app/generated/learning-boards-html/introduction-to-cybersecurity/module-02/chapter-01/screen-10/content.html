Social Engineering: Exploiting Human Psychology

The Art of Manipulation, Not Technical Hacking

What is Social Engineering?

The psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information.

Core Principle

It exploits human psychology (trust, fear, urgency, curiosity) rather than technical vulnerabilities to bypass security measures.

Key Takeaway

Social engineering targets human behavior, making people the weakest link in security by manipulating them into compromising systems.

Psychological Triggers

How Attackers Manipulate

  • •Creating a false sense of urgency or crisis.
  • •Impersonating authority figures (e.g., IT support, CEO).
  • •Exploiting natural human curiosity or helpfulness.
Example Scenario

An attacker calls an employee, pretending to be from the IT department, claiming a critical system issue requires their password to 'fix it immediately.'
