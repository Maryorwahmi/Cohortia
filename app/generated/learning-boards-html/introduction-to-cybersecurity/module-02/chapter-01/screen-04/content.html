Core Concept

What is an Attack Vector?

The Entry Point for Digital Intruders

Definition

An attack vector is the path or method used by an attacker to gain unauthorized access to a system, network, or data.

Analogy: The Burglar's Entry

Think of it like a burglar finding an unlocked window, a weak door, or even using a disguise to enter a house. These are all 'entry points' or vectors.

Key Takeaway

Identifying potential attack vectors is the foundational step in building effective cybersecurity defenses.

Visualizing Entry Points

Understanding the Paths

  • •Attackers exploit various vulnerabilities to gain access.
  • •These paths can be technical (software flaws) or human (deception).
  • •Proactive defense involves identifying and securing these entry points.
Example Scenario

An attacker sends a malicious email (phishing) to trick an employee into clicking a link, which then installs malware. Here, the email is the attack vector.
