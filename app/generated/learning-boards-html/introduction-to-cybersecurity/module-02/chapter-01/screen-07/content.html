Malware: A Broad Threat Category

Software Designed to Disrupt, Damage, or Access Systems

What is Malware?

Malicious software designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to computer systems.

Primary Goals

  • •Data Theft: Stealing personal or financial information.
  • •System Disruption: Making systems unusable or slow.
  • •Unauthorized Access: Creating backdoors for future entry.
Key Takeaway

Malware is a diverse category of malicious software with various destructive or intrusive capabilities, requiring varied defense strategies.

Common Malware Types
Viruses
Worms
Trojans
Ransomware
Common Mistake

Failing to regularly update software, clicking suspicious links, or downloading files from untrusted sources are common entry points for malware.
