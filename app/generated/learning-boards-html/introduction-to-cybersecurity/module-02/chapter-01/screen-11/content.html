Social Engineering Methods

Pretexting and Baiting

Crafting Scenarios and Enticements

Pretexting

Inventing a Believable Scenario

  • •Attacker creates a fabricated story to gain trust.
  • •Often involves impersonating authority (e.g., IT, HR).
  • •Goal is to extract specific information or access.
Example

An attacker calls an employee, pretending to be a new IT technician, asking for their login details to 'test the system.'

Baiting

Offering Something Enticing

  • •Involves offering a tempting item (e.g., free download, infected USB).
  • •Relies on the victim's curiosity or desire for the 'bait.'
  • •Leads to malware infection or system compromise.
Example

A USB drive labeled 'Company Payroll' is left in a public place, hoping an employee will plug it in, infecting their computer.

Key Takeaway

Pretexting uses fabricated stories to build trust, while baiting uses tempting offers to trick victims into compromising their systems.
