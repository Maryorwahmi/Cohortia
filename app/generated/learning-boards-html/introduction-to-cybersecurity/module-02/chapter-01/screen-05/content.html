Phishing: A Social Engineering Tactic

Fraudulent Communications Designed to Deceive

What is Phishing?

Attackers send fraudulent communications (emails, texts, calls) appearing from reputable sources to trick recipients.

Common Forms

  • •Email Phishing: Most common, fake emails.
  • •Smishing: Phishing via SMS text messages.
  • •Vishing: Phishing conducted over voice calls.
Key Takeaway

Phishing leverages human trust and urgency through deceptive messages to steal sensitive information or deploy malware.

Anatomy of a Phishing Attempt
From: support@mybank-update.co
Subject: Urgent Account Verification Required

Dear Valued Customer,

We detected unusual activity on your account. For your security, we have temporarily suspended your online banking access.

To reactivate your account and avoid further interruption, please click the link below and verify your details immediately:

Click Here to Verify Your Account

Failure to do so will result in permanent account closure.

Thank you for your cooperation,

MyBank Security Team

FRAUDULENT SENDER
SUSPICIOUS LINK

How Phishing Works

  • •Attackers impersonate trusted entities.
  • •They create a sense of urgency or fear.
  • •The goal is to trick you into clicking malicious links or divulging info.
Example Scenario

You receive an email from 'PayPal' asking you to 'confirm your billing information' due to a 'security breach.' The link provided leads to a fake login page designed to steal your credentials.
