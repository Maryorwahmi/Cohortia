Attack Vector Deep Dive

Password Attacks and Secure Practices

Protecting Your Digital Gateways

Password Attack Methods

How Attackers Compromise Passwords

  • •Brute-Force: Systematically trying every possible character combination.
  • •Dictionary Attack: Using lists of common words and leaked passwords.
  • •Credential Stuffing: Reusing leaked credentials from one breach on other sites.
Common Mistake

Reusing the same simple password across multiple accounts makes you highly vulnerable to credential stuffing attacks.

Secure Password Practices

Fortifying Your Digital Access

  • •Strong, Unique Passwords: Long, complex, and different for every account.
  • •Password Manager: Use a reputable tool to generate and store complex passwords.
  • •Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Adds a second verification step (e.g., code from phone).
Pro-Tip

Enabling MFA is one of the most effective ways to protect your accounts, even if your password is compromised.

Key Takeaway

Combining strong, unique passwords with Multi-Factor Authentication creates a robust defense against most password-related attacks.
