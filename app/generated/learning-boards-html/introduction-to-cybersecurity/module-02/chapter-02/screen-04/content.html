Core Concept

Denial of Service (DoS) Attacks

Overwhelming Systems to Disrupt Availability

Single Source Overload

A DoS attack typically originates from a single attacker or system, directing a flood of traffic at a target.

Resource Exhaustion

The goal is to consume all available resources (bandwidth, CPU, memory) of the target system.

Service Disruption

The ultimate impact is that legitimate users cannot access the targeted service, leading to outages and financial losses.

Key Takeaway

DoS attacks are about overwhelming a single target from one source to cause service unavailability.

Real-World Example

Imagine a single person repeatedly calling a customer service line, tying up the phone lines so real customers can't get through. This is analogous to a DoS attack.

Common Misconception

DoS attacks are often confused with DDoS. Remember, DoS involves a single source, making it easier to trace and block.
