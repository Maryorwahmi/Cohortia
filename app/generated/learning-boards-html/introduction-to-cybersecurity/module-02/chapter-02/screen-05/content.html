Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) Attacks

The Power of a Botnet

Multiple Sources (Botnet)

DDoS attacks use a network of compromised systems ('botnet') to launch a coordinated attack, making it harder to block.

Difficult to Mitigate

The sheer volume and distributed nature of traffic make it challenging to filter out malicious requests from legitimate ones.

Severe Impact

Can lead to significant financial losses, reputational damage, and disruption of critical services due to prolonged unavailability.

Key Takeaway

DDoS attacks leverage botnets for a distributed assault, making them more powerful and harder to defend against than single-source DoS attacks.

DoS vs. DDoS Comparison

DoS Attack

Single attacker

Easier to block

DDoS Attack

Multiple attackers (Botnet)

Harder to mitigate

DDoS attacks are a more sophisticated form of DoS, using a network of compromised machines (a botnet) to amplify the attack.

Pro-Tip

DDoS mitigation often involves specialized services that can absorb and filter massive amounts of malicious traffic before it reaches the target server.

Misconception

A botnet is not a single supercomputer; it's a collection of ordinary computers, often belonging to unsuspecting users, controlled by an attacker.
