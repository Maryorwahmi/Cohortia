Key Concepts: Network Attacks

Essential Terms to Remember

Denial of Service (DoS)

An attack designed to make a resource unavailable by overwhelming it with traffic from a single source.

Distributed DoS (DDoS)

A DoS attack from multiple compromised systems (a botnet), making it harder to mitigate.

Man-in-the-Middle (MITM)

An attack where the attacker secretly intercepts and relays messages between two communicating parties.

Summary Insight

Understanding DoS, DDoS, and MITM attacks is fundamental to protecting network availability, data confidentiality, and communication integrity in any digital environment.
