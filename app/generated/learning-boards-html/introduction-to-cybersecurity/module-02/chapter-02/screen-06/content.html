Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) Attacks

Secretly Intercepting and Manipulating Communication

Interception Point

The attacker positions themselves between two communicating parties, acting as a secret relay.

Data Manipulation

The attacker can read, insert, or modify messages without either party being aware of the interference.

Compromised Security

Leads to compromise of confidentiality (eavesdropping) and integrity (data alteration) of communication.

Key Takeaway

MITM attacks secretly intercept and manipulate communication, posing a severe threat to data confidentiality and integrity.

MITM Attack Flow
User A

Initiates comms

Server B

Receives comms

Attacker
Intercept & Modify

The attacker secretly relays messages between User A and Server B, reading or altering them without detection.

Example Scenario

On an unsecured public Wi-Fi network, an attacker can use ARP spoofing to trick your device into sending all its traffic through their laptop, allowing them to see your unencrypted data.

Misconception

MITM attacks are not just about eavesdropping; they can actively modify data, leading to much more severe consequences than simple surveillance.
