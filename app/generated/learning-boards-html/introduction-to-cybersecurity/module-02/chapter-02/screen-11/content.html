Port Scanning: Tool for Attackers and Defenders

Identifying Open Ports and Services

Service Discovery

Identifies open ports and the services running on them (e.g., HTTP on port 80, SSH on port 22).

Dual Use Tool

Used by attackers for reconnaissance and by network administrators for security auditing and vulnerability assessment.

Ethical Implications

Unauthorized scanning is often a precursor to attacks, while authorized scanning is crucial for proactive defense.

Key Takeaway

Port scanning is a neutral tool for identifying open services, used ethically for auditing and maliciously for reconnaissance.

Pro-Tip

Regularly scan your own network and systems to identify and close unnecessary open ports, reducing your attack surface.

Ethical Consideration

Always obtain explicit permission before performing port scans on any network or system you do not own or manage.
