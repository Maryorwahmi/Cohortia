Core Idea

Tunneling creates a virtual, private pathway through a public network by wrapping data packets.

Analogy

Like putting a letter in a new, unmarked box to hide its true sender and recipient during transit.

Benefit

Hides the original source and destination of your traffic from external observers on the public network.

Key Takeaway

Tunneling wraps original data packets in new ones, creating a hidden path through public networks and obscuring traffic details.
