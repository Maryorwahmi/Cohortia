Connecting Networks: Site-to-Site VPNs
Establishing secure links between entire offices
Definition

Connects two or more entire networks (e.g., two branch offices) over a public network, creating a secure link between them.

Key Characteristic

Routers or firewalls at each site establish a permanent, encrypted tunnel, allowing all devices on connected networks to communicate securely.

Contrast

Unlike remote access VPNs, individual clients are not needed; the connection is between the networks themselves.

Key Takeaway

Site-to-site VPNs create permanent, encrypted tunnels between entire networks, allowing seamless and secure communication between offices or data centers.
