Application-Layer Gateways: Deep Content Inspection

Inspecting actual traffic content at the application layer

Application-Layer Gateway

Operates at the application layer (Layer 7 OSI model), inspecting the actual content of network traffic.

Deep Packet Inspection
Granular Policies

Enforce rules based on specific applications, user identities, and commands within protocols.

Superior Protection

Effective against application-specific attacks, even on standard ports.

Next-Gen Firewalls (NGFW)

Often incorporate application-layer inspection with other advanced security features.

Potential Drawback

Deep inspection requires more processing, potentially introducing network latency.

Key Takeaway

Application-layer gateways offer the most comprehensive traffic inspection, providing granular control and robust defense against sophisticated threats, at the cost of increased processing overhead.
