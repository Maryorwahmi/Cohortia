Demilitarized Zones (DMZs): The Buffer Network

Protecting public-facing services with layered firewalls

What is a DMZ?

A physical or logical subnetwork that acts as a buffer zone between the internal network and the untrusted external network (Internet).

  • Hosts public-facing services (web, email, DNS servers).
  • Provides a layered defense with firewalls on both sides.
Purpose

Allows external access to public services without granting direct access to the internal corporate network, significantly reducing risk.

Key Takeaway

DMZs provide a critical buffer zone for public-facing services, using layered firewalls to protect the internal network from direct external threats.
