Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Anomaly-Based Detection

Identifies threats by detecting deviations from a baseline of normal behavior.

Network-based IDS/IPS (NIDS/NIPS)

Monitors network traffic on a specific network segment for suspicious activity.

Host-based IDS/IPS (HIDS/HIPS)

Monitors the internal activities of a specific host for suspicious behavior.

Summary Takeaway

Anomaly-based detection catches new threats, while NIDS/NIPS and HIDS/HIPS provide distinct network and host-level monitoring capabilities.
