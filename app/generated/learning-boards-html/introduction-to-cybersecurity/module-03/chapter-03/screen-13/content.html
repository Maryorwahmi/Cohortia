Common Mistakes & Safety Notes

Avoiding Pitfalls in IDPS Deployment

Common Mistakes

  • •Improper Tuning: Leads to excessive alerts and alert fatigue.
  • •'Set It and Forget It': Neglecting continuous monitoring and updates.
  • •Ignoring Baselines: Failing to adapt anomaly detection to network evolution.
Consequence

Untuned IDPS can generate so many false positives that real threats are missed, or legitimate traffic is blocked.

Safety Notes for IPS Deployment

  • •Start in IDS Mode: Begin with detection-only, then gradually enable prevention.
  • •Careful Tuning: Overly aggressive IPS can block legitimate traffic.
  • •Resource Allocation: Ensure sufficient processing power and bandwidth to avoid bottlenecks.
Pro Tip

Integrate IDPS with a SIEM for centralized logging and correlation, providing better context for alerts and reducing false positives.

Key Takeaway

Effective IDPS deployment requires continuous tuning, careful configuration, and a phased approach for IPS to prevent service disruption and maximize security.
