Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Endpoint

Any device connected to a network, such as laptops, desktops, mobile devices, or servers.

Antivirus (AV)

Software designed to detect, prevent, and remove malicious software, traditionally signature-based.

Signature-based Detection

Method used by AV to identify malware by comparing files against a database of known signatures.

Summary Takeaway

Endpoints are critical network access points, and traditional antivirus uses signature-based detection as a foundational defense against known threats.
