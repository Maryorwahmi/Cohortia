Core Concept

Traditional Antivirus: Signature-Based Defense

The Foundation of Endpoint Protection

Key Takeaway

Antivirus relies on a database of known malware signatures to identify and neutralize threats, providing essential protection against common attacks.

How it Works
  • Maintains a database of known malware signatures.
  • Scans files and compares their signatures against the database.
  • Quarantines or deletes files if a match is found.
  • Effective against well-known and established threats.

Signature-Based Detection Process

1. Malware Signature Database
2. File Scan & Comparison
3. Threat Quarantined/Deleted
