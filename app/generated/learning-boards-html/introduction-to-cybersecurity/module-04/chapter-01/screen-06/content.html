Common Vulnerabilities

The Gaps in Signature-Based Protection

When Traditional Antivirus Falls Short

Key Takeaway

Traditional AV is limited by its reliance on known signatures, making it vulnerable to new and evolving threats like zero-day exploits and polymorphic malware.

Common Mistake

Assuming traditional antivirus provides complete protection. It's a necessary but insufficient defense in today's threat landscape.

Why AV Falls Short

Zero-Day Exploits

Attacks that target unknown software vulnerabilities, for which no signature or patch exists yet. AV cannot detect what it doesn't know.

Polymorphic Malware

Malware that constantly changes its code signature to evade detection. Traditional AV struggles to keep up with these mutations.
