Understanding Endpoints

The Crucial Devices at the Edge of Your Network

Key Takeaway

Endpoints are the 'front doors' to your network; securing them is paramount to preventing breaches and protecting sensitive data.

Why it Matters

A single vulnerable endpoint can compromise an entire network, making robust device-level protection a foundational element of cybersecurity.

What is an Endpoint?

An endpoint is any device connected to a network that can send or receive data. These devices are often the initial point of compromise for cyber attackers.
