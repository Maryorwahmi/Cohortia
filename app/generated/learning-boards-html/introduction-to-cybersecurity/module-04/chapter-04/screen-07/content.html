Process Step 2

Assessment: Prioritizing Risks

The severity of identified vulnerabilities is assessed to prioritize which patches are most critical. This involves evaluating factors like exploitability, impact, and the existence of public exploits.

  • Severity assessed using CVSS scores.
  • Factors: exploitability, impact, public exploits.
  • Prioritizes critical vulnerabilities for immediate action.
Key Takeaway

Prioritizing vulnerabilities through assessment and carefully testing patches before deployment are crucial for effective and safe remediation.

Process Step 3

Remediation: Applying the Fix

This phase involves the vendor releasing a patch and the organization applying it. It includes downloading, testing in a non-production environment, and then deploying to production systems.

  • Vendor releases the patch.
  • Download and test in a staging environment.
  • Deploy to production systems after successful testing.
  • Prevents regressions and ensures compatibility.
Example: Testing Patches

Staging Environment Use

Before deploying a critical OS patch to all employee workstations, an IT team first applies it to a small group of test machines. They monitor for any software conflicts or performance issues for a week. Only after confirming stability do they roll it out to the entire organization.
