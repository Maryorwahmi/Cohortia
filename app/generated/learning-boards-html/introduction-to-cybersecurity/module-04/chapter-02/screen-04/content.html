Core Concept

User Authentication: Verifying Identity

The first line of defense for your digital assets.

  • Identity Confirmation: The process of verifying that a user is who they claim to be.
  • Gatekeeper Role: Acts as the initial barrier, preventing unauthorized access to systems and data.
  • Common Method: Passwords are the most widespread form, relying on 'something you know'.
  • Foundation of Trust: Essential for establishing secure digital interactions and protecting sensitive information.
Key Takeaway

User authentication is the critical first step in cybersecurity, ensuring only verified identities can proceed to access resources.

Authentication Process

Verify user identity before granting access.

1. User provides credentials
2. System verifies identity
3. Access granted/denied

Example: Email Login

When you enter your email and password, the system authenticates you by checking if the 'something you know' matches its records. If it does, you gain access to your inbox.
