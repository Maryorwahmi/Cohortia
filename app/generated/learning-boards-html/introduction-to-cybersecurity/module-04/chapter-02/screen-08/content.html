MFA Deep Dive

Exploring MFA Factors and Common Methods

Understanding 'something you know, have, or are.'

  • Something You Know: Passwords or PINs, the most common but vulnerable factor.
  • Something You Have: Physical tokens like smartphones (TOTP, push notifications) or hardware security keys (YubiKey).
  • Something You Are: Biometric data such as fingerprints, face scans, or iris scans.
  • Strongest Protection: FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware keys offer phishing-resistant security.
Key Takeaway

Combining factors from different categories significantly enhances security, making it much harder for attackers to bypass authentication.

Misconception: SMS is Always Secure

While convenient, SMS-based MFA is less secure than TOTP apps or hardware keys due to risks like SIM swapping, where attackers can port your phone number to their device.
