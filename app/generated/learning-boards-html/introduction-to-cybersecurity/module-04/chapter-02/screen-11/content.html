Access Control Models

Discretionary and Role-Based Access Control

Different approaches to managing permissions.

  • DAC (Discretionary Access Control): Resource owners define access permissions.
  • RBAC (Role-Based Access Control): Permissions are assigned to roles, and users are assigned to roles.
  • Flexibility vs. Scalability: DAC offers granular control, while RBAC simplifies management in large environments.
  • PoLP Enforcement: Both models can enforce PoLP, but RBAC often does so more efficiently at scale.
Key Takeaway

Choosing the right access control model, like DAC for personal systems or RBAC for organizations, is vital for efficient and secure permission management.
