Critical Component

The Importance of Key Management

Safeguarding your encryption keys for robust security.

Core Principle

Encryption is only as strong as its keys. Securely managing keys is paramount to maintaining data confidentiality.

Key Lifecycle

Involves secure generation, storage, distribution, and timely revocation of encryption keys.

Common Mistakes

Using weak keys, storing keys with encrypted data, or failing to rotate keys regularly can severely compromise security.
