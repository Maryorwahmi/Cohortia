Key Concepts

Essential ideas to remember from this chapter

Data Encryption

The process of transforming data into an unreadable format to protect it from unauthorized access.

Encryption at Rest

Encryption applied to data stored on a physical storage medium (e.g., hard drive, cloud storage).

Encryption in Transit

Encryption applied to data as it is transmitted over a network, protecting it from interception.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding data encryption and its application to data at rest and in transit is foundational for securing information in any digital environment.
