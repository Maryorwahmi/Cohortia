Key Concepts (Continued)

More essential ideas to remember from this chapter

Full Disk Encryption (FDE)

A method of encrypting an entire hard drive, making all data on it inaccessible without the correct decryption key.

Transport Layer Security (TLS)

Cryptographic protocols providing communication security over networks, commonly used for HTTPS.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Tools and processes ensuring sensitive data is not lost, misused, or accessed by unauthorized users.

Summary Takeaway

FDE, TLS, and DLP are powerful, complementary strategies that provide comprehensive protection for data across its lifecycle and states.
