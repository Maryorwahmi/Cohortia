Core Concept

What is Data Encryption?

Transforming data into an unreadable format for security.

Definition

The process of converting information into a code to prevent unauthorized access, making it unintelligible without the correct decryption key.

Purpose

Ensures data confidentiality and integrity, protecting sensitive information even if it falls into the wrong hands.

Analogy: Locked Safe

Imagine sensitive documents in a locked safe. Even if someone enters your office, they can't read the documents without the key. Encryption acts as that digital safe.
