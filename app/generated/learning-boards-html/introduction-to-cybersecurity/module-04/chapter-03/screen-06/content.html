How it works

Deep Dive

Full Disk Encryption (FDE)

Comprehensive protection for your entire storage device.

What it is

Encrypts the entire storage device, including the operating system, applications, and all user data.

Why it matters

Provides a strong defense against data theft from lost or stolen devices, as data remains unreadable without the decryption key.

Key Takeaway

FDE offers a robust, all-encompassing security layer for data at rest, making it a critical tool for device security.
