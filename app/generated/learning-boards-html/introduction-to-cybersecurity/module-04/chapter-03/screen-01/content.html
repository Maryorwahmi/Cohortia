Chapter 4.3

Data Encryption and Loss Prevention (DLP)

Safeguarding Sensitive Information in the Digital Age

This chapter explores how encryption transforms data into an unreadable format and how Data Loss Prevention (DLP) systems prevent sensitive information from leaving controlled environments. Together, these strategies form a robust defense against unauthorized access and data exfiltration.

Key Takeaway

Data encryption and DLP are critical for protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access and accidental or malicious data exfiltration.
