Deep Dive

Transport Layer Security (TLS)

How HTTPS keeps your online interactions private.

What it is

A cryptographic protocol that secures communication over computer networks, commonly used for web traffic (HTTPS) and email (SMTPS).

Why it matters

Prevents eavesdropping and tampering with data during transmission, ensuring confidentiality and integrity for online activities.

Key Takeaway

TLS is the standard for secure internet communication, encrypting data between your browser and websites to protect your privacy and information.
