Core Concept

Understanding Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Preventing sensitive data exfiltration from controlled environments.

What it is

A set of tools and processes designed to ensure sensitive data is not lost, misused, or accessed by unauthorized users.

Purpose

To prevent sensitive information from leaving an organization's control, whether accidentally or maliciously.

Key Takeaway

DLP acts as a digital guardian, proactively stopping sensitive data from leaving your organization's secure perimeter, complementing encryption efforts.

How DLP Works

DLP systems scan data for sensitive patterns, analyze usage context, and enforce policies to block, quarantine, or encrypt suspicious transfers.
