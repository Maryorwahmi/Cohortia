Crisis Communication: Informing Stakeholders

Strategic Updates During an Ongoing Security Incident

Effective communication is paramount during an incident to manage expectations, maintain trust, and ensure informed decision-making across the organization.

  • •Timely Updates: Provide accurate information without causing panic.
  • •Stakeholder Identification: Know who needs to be informed (management, legal, PR, customers).
  • •Pre-approved Templates: Use standardized messages for consistency and speed.
  • •Strategic Disclosure: Avoid prematurely releasing sensitive details that could harm the investigation or reputation.
Key Takeaway

A well-executed communication plan is as critical as technical response in mitigating the overall impact of a security incident.

Key Communication Audiences

Internal Teams

Technical updates, operational impact, task assignments.

Executive Leadership

High-level summaries, business impact, strategic decisions.

Legal & PR

Compliance, public statements, reputational management.

Affected Customers

Impact, protective measures, support channels (if necessary).

Tailor messages to each audience's needs and concerns.

Example: Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach, a pre-approved communication plan would dictate that legal counsel reviews all external statements, PR crafts public announcements, and a dedicated customer support channel is established to address affected individuals, all while keeping executive management informed of the overall situation.

Common Pitfall

Lack of a predefined communication plan can lead to inconsistent messaging, panic, and further damage to reputation.
