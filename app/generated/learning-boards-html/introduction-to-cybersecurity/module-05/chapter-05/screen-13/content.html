Essential Incident Handling Terms

Core Concepts for Effective Response

Incident Handling

The practical execution of the incident response plan, covering containment, eradication, and recovery.

Triage

The initial process of assessing an incident to determine its nature, scope, and priority.

Forensic Readiness

The state of systems and processes enabling efficient collection and preservation of digital evidence.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering these core concepts provides the foundation for a proactive and effective cybersecurity incident response strategy.
