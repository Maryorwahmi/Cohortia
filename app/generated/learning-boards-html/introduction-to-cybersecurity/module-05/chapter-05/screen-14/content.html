Essential Incident Handling Terms

Core Concepts for Effective Response

Evidence Preservation

Collecting and protecting digital evidence to maintain its integrity for investigations.

Root Cause Analysis

Identifying the fundamental reason for an incident, not just addressing symptoms.

Continuous Improvement

Ongoing refinement of security controls and plans based on lessons learned.

Communication Plan

A predefined strategy for informing stakeholders during a security incident.

Summary Takeaway

These advanced concepts ensure that incident response is not just reactive, but a strategic driver for long-term security resilience.
