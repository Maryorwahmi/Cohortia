Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Adding Layers of Security Beyond Just Passwords

Why MFA is Crucial

  • •Significantly enhances security beyond single-factor passwords.
  • •Requires multiple distinct forms of verification.
  • •Protects against credential theft even if a password is compromised.
  • •A fundamental requirement for modern cybersecurity.
Key Takeaway

MFA is a critical defense against unauthorized access, providing a robust second line of defense when passwords alone are insufficient.

The Three Factors of Authentication

Something You Know

Password, PIN, security question.

Something You Have

Phone, hardware token, smart card.

Something You Are

Fingerprint, face scan, voice recognition.

Example: Online Banking Login

You enter your password (something you know). Then, you receive a one-time code on your registered mobile phone (something you have) which you must enter to complete the login. This combination significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access.
