Access Control Model

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Streamlining Permissions Through Job Functions

Key Characteristics of RBAC

  • •Permissions assigned to roles, not individual users.
  • •Users are assigned to one or more roles.
  • •Simplifies management in large, dynamic organizations.
  • •Naturally enforces the principle of least privilege.
Key Takeaway

RBAC is highly scalable and efficient, making it the preferred model for managing access in most modern enterprise environments.
