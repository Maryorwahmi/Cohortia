System Architecture

Centralized Identity Management Systems

The Backbone for Scalable Access Control

Key Functions

  • •User Provisioning: Creating and managing user accounts.
  • •Access Management: Assigning and revoking permissions.
  • •Authentication: Verifying user identities.
  • •Auditing: Tracking access events for compliance and incident response.
Key Takeaway

Identity management systems centralize user and access control, providing a scalable and auditable foundation for organizational security.

Example: Employee Onboarding/Offboarding

New Employee

Account created, role assigned.

Applications

Email, CRM, HR system access.

Automated Access Provisioning

Based on the assigned role, the identity management system automatically grants access to necessary resources.

Offboarding: Instant Revocation

When an employee leaves, their account is disabled in the central system, instantly revoking access across all integrated applications, preventing insider threats.
