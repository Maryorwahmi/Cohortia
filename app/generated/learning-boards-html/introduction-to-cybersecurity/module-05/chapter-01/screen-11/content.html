A Strategic Approach to Patch Management

The continuous cycle of identification, assessment, deployment, and verification.

1. Identification

Identify all software/hardware assets and subscribe to vendor security advisories.

2. Assessment

Assess patch criticality and potential impact; test in non-production environments.

3. Deployment

Deploy patches across the organization, often using automated tools for efficiency.

4. Verification

Ensure patches are successfully applied and vulnerabilities are mitigated.

Key Takeaway

A structured, continuous patch management strategy is vital for proactively defending against vulnerabilities and maintaining a strong security posture.
