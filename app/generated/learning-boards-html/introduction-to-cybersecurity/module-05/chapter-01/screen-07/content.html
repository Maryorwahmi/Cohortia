Hardening a Linux Server: Identifying Services

Using netstat and systemctl to discover open ports and running services.

Why Identify Services?

Many Linux distributions enable various services by default. Identifying these helps you determine which are essential and which can be safely disabled to reduce potential attack vectors.

Key Commands for Discovery

  • netstat -tulnp: Lists all listening TCP/UDP ports and associated processes.
  • systemctl list-units --type=service --state=running: Shows active services managed by systemd.
Key Takeaway

Thorough identification of running services and open ports is the foundational step before implementing any hardening measures on a server.

Pro-Tip

Always cross-reference identified services with your application's actual requirements. If it's not needed, it's a risk.
