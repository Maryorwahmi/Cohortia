Framework

The NIST Incident Response Life Cycle

A structured, six-phase approach to managing cybersecurity incidents.

1. Preparation

Before the incident

2. Identification

Detecting the incident

3. Containment

Limiting the damage

4. Eradication

Removing the threat

5. Recovery

Restoring operations

6. Post-Incident Analysis

Learning and improving

Key Takeaway

The NIST framework provides a systematic, cyclical approach to incident response, ensuring comprehensive management and continuous improvement across all phases.
