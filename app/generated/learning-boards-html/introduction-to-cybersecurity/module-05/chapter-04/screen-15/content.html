Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter to reinforce your understanding.

Cybersecurity Incident

An event that constitutes a violation or imminent threat of violation of computer security policies, acceptable use policies, or standard security practices.

Cybersecurity Event

Any observable occurrence in a system or network, most of which are benign and do not indicate a security breach.

Incident Response (IR)

A structured approach an organization takes to prepare for, detect, contain, eradicate, recover from, and learn from cybersecurity incidents.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the distinction between events and incidents, and having a structured Incident Response framework, is fundamental to effective cybersecurity management.
