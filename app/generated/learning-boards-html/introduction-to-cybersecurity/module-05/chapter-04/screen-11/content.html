NIST Phase 5

Recovery: Restoring Operations Securely

Bringing systems and services back online in a verified and secure manner.

Key Recovery Activities

  • •Restore data and systems from clean, verified backups.
  • •Thoroughly test systems to ensure full functionality and integrity.
  • •Implement enhanced security controls before going live.
  • •Monitor closely for any signs of recurrence or new threats.
  • •Gradually bring systems back online, often in phases.

Example: Database Restoration

After a database breach, the IRT restores the database from a backup taken before the compromise, then applies all necessary patches and security hardening before reconnecting it to the application servers.

Primary Goals of Recovery

  • •Restore affected systems and services to normal operation.
  • •Ensure the restored environment is secure and stable.
  • •Minimize business disruption and downtime.
Key Takeaway

Recovery is about more than just restoring functionality; it's about ensuring the environment is brought back online securely, with thorough testing and continuous vigilance.
