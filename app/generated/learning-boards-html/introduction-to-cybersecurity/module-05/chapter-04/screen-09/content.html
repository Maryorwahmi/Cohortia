NIST Phase 3

Containment: Stopping the Spread and Limiting Damage

Isolating affected systems to prevent further compromise and preserve evidence.

Key Containment Strategies

  • •**Short-Term:** Isolate affected systems (e.g., unplug, firewall block).
  • •**Long-Term:** Implement temporary fixes to allow business continuity while planning full eradication.
  • •Disable compromised accounts and services.
  • •Block malicious IP addresses at network perimeter.

Example: Ransomware Attack

Upon detecting ransomware, the IRT immediately disconnects affected servers from the network and isolates the infected subnet to prevent the malware from encrypting more data or spreading to other systems.

Primary Goals of Containment

  • •Prevent further compromise of systems and data.
  • •Limit the overall damage and impact of the incident.
  • •Preserve forensic evidence for root cause analysis and legal action.
Key Takeaway

Containment is a critical, time-sensitive phase focused on stopping the immediate threat and preventing escalation, while carefully balancing damage control with evidence preservation.
