NIST Phase 1

Preparation: Building Your Defense Before the Attack

Laying the groundwork for effective incident handling and rapid response.

Key Preparation Areas

  • •Develop a comprehensive Incident Response Plan (IRP).
  • •Form a dedicated Incident Response Team (IRT) with defined roles.
  • •Establish clear communication channels and contact lists.
  • •Acquire necessary forensic and secure communication tools.
  • •Conduct regular training and simulated incident drills.

Example: IRT Training

A company conducts quarterly tabletop exercises where the IRT simulates responding to a ransomware attack, practicing communication, containment, and recovery steps without impacting live systems.

Essential Documentation

  • •Up-to-date asset inventories (servers, workstations, applications).
  • •Detailed network diagrams and data flow maps.
  • •Baseline configurations for critical systems.
  • •Contact information for internal and external stakeholders (legal, PR, law enforcement).
Key Takeaway

Effective preparation is the bedrock of incident response, ensuring that an organization can react swiftly and systematically when a security incident inevitably occurs.
