NIST Phase 6

Post-Incident Analysis: Learning and Improving

Reviewing incidents to identify areas for improvement and prevent recurrence.

Key Activities

  • •Conduct a thorough review of the entire incident.
  • •Document what happened, how it was handled, and the outcomes.
  • •Identify what worked well and what didn't during the response.
  • •Determine the root cause and contributing factors.

Example: Ransomware Post-Mortem

After resolving a ransomware attack, the IRT holds a meeting to discuss the incident. They identify that a lack of clear communication protocols delayed containment, leading to a new objective to refine their communication plan.

Primary Goals of Post-Incident Analysis

  • •Strengthen the organization's overall security posture.
  • •Improve the existing incident response plan.
  • •Enhance the skills and readiness of the IRT.
  • •Prevent similar incidents from occurring in the future.
Key Takeaway

Post-incident analysis is a vital feedback loop, transforming past challenges into opportunities for continuous improvement and enhanced organizational resilience.
