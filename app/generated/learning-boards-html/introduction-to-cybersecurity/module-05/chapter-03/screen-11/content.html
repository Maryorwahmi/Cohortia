Human Psychology

Why We Fall For It: Psychological Exploitation

Understanding the human tendencies attackers leverage.

Authority

Compliance with perceived figures of power (e.g., 'IT Support').

Urgency/Scarcity

Pressure to act quickly due to immediate need or limited opportunity.

Trust/Liking

Cooperation gained by building rapport or impersonating trusted individuals.

Curiosity/Fear

Enticing offers or threats that lead to impulsive actions.

Key Takeaway

Recognizing these psychological levers helps you pause and critically evaluate suspicious requests, rather than reacting impulsively.

The 'Pause Button'

Develop the habit of pausing and questioning requests that trigger strong emotional responses.

Example: Urgent IT Request

An email from 'IT' demanding immediate password change due to a 'security breach' exploits urgency and fear.
