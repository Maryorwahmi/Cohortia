Key Concepts to Remember

Reinforcing your understanding of social engineering defense.

Social Engineering

Psychological manipulation of people to perform actions or divulge confidential information.

Phishing

Fraudulent communications (typically emails) disguised as reputable sources to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information.

Pretexting

An attacker creates a fabricated scenario ('pretext') to gain trust and extract information, often via phone.

Chapter Summary

Understanding these core social engineering tactics is the first step in building a robust defense against human-centric cyberattacks.
