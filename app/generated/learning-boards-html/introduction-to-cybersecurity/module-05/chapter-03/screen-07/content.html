Spear Phishing

Highly Personalized Attacks

Attackers research victims to craft believable, tailored messages. These messages often reference specific projects, colleagues, or events to appear legitimate.

  • Targets specific individuals or groups.
  • Uses personalized information to build trust.
Whaling

Targeting High-Profile Individuals

A type of spear phishing specifically aimed at senior executives (e.g., CEOs, CFOs). The goal is often large financial transfers or sensitive data.

  • Focuses on individuals with significant authority.
  • High-value targets, high-impact outcomes.
Example Scenario

An email to a CFO, seemingly from the CEO, requesting an urgent, confidential wire transfer for an acquisition.

Key Takeaway

Always verify unexpected requests, especially those from perceived authorities or with high stakes, through an alternative, trusted channel.
