Key Indicators

How to Identify Phishing Emails

  • 1**Suspicious Sender:** Inspect the actual email address, not just the display name. Look for unusual domains or misspellings.
  • 2**Malicious Links:** Hover over links (without clicking!) to reveal the true URL. It often differs from the displayed text.
  • 3**Poor Grammar/Spelling:** Unprofessional language, grammatical errors, or awkward phrasing are red flags.
  • 4**Urgency/Threats:** Emails demanding immediate action, threatening account closure, or creating panic are highly suspicious.

Common Mistake: Trusting Display Names

Attackers can easily spoof the 'From' name. Always verify the full email address.

Pro Tip

If an email seems suspicious, do not click any links or open attachments. Instead, navigate directly to the official website or contact the sender via a known, verified phone number.

Key Takeaway

Vigilance and critical inspection of email details are crucial for detecting and preventing phishing attacks.
