Core Concept

The Human Element: A Critical Link

Why people are often the target of cyberattacks.

  • 1Even advanced technical defenses can be bypassed if human users are manipulated.
  • 2Attackers often find it easier to exploit human psychology than to discover complex system vulnerabilities.
  • 3A strong cybersecurity strategy must integrate human awareness and training alongside technological safeguards.
Key Takeaway

Ignoring the human factor leaves organizations vulnerable, regardless of their technical security investments.

The Human Factor

Individuals are often the primary target, making them the most critical link in the security chain.

Psychological Exploitation

Attackers leverage human tendencies like trust, fear, and curiosity to gain unauthorized access or information.
