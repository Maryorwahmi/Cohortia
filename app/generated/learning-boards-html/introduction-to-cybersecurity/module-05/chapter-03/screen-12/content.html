Proactive Defense

Building Resilience: The Power of Training

Ongoing education as the first line of defense.

Key Components of Effective Training:

  • **Interactive Modules:** Engaging content that teaches about current threats.
  • **Simulated Phishing:** Practical exercises to identify and report fake phishing attempts.
  • **Clear Reporting Guidelines:** Easy-to-use mechanisms for reporting suspicious activities.
Key Takeaway

Continuous, interactive training empowers employees to be the first and most effective line of defense against social engineering.

Example: 'Report Phish' Button

A dedicated button in email clients allows employees to quickly and safely report suspicious emails to the security team.

Common Mistake: One-Time Training

Security threats evolve constantly; training must be ongoing to remain effective and relevant.
