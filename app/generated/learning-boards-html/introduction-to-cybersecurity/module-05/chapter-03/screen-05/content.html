Definition

Social Engineering: The Art of Manipulation

Exploiting human psychology, not technical flaws.

Key Aspects:

  • Manipulating people into divulging confidential information.
  • Tricking individuals into performing actions they wouldn't normally do.
  • Bypassing sophisticated technical controls by exploiting human trust.
Key Takeaway

Social engineering leverages human nature to circumvent security, making awareness a critical defense.

Human Tendencies Exploited

Trust, helpfulness, fear, and curiosity are common psychological levers used by attackers.

Common Misconception

It's not just about technical skill; anyone can be a target, regardless of their cybersecurity knowledge.
