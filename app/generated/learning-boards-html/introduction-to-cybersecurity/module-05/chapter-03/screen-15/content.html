Key Concepts to Remember

Final takeaways for a security-conscious mindset.

Baiting

Luring victims with an enticing offer (e.g., free software, 'lost' USB drive) to compromise their systems.

Tailgating

A physical social engineering tactic where an unauthorized person gains entry to a restricted area by following an authorized person.

Security Awareness Training

Ongoing education for employees about cybersecurity threats, best practices, and how to recognize and report suspicious activities.

Chapter Summary

By understanding these tactics and embracing continuous training, you can significantly reduce vulnerability to social engineering.
