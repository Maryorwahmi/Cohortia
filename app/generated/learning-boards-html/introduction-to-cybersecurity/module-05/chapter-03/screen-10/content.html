Baiting

Luring Victims with Enticing Offers

Attackers offer something desirable (e.g., free software, a 'lost' USB drive) to trick victims into compromising their systems.

  • Exploits curiosity or desire for freebies.
  • Often results in malware installation.
Tailgating (Piggybacking)

Unauthorized Physical Entry

An unauthorized person follows an authorized individual into a restricted area, often by feigning a forgotten badge or needing help.

  • Exploits human politeness or helpfulness.
  • Gains access to secure physical locations.
Example Scenario

Plugging in a 'found' USB drive labeled 'Q4 Financials' into your work computer, or holding a door for someone without a badge.

Key Takeaway

Always be wary of unsolicited items or requests for physical access; verify before you trust or assist.
