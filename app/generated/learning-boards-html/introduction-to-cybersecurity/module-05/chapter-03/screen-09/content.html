Pretexting: Building a Fake Story

Creating believable scenarios to gain trust and information.

Key Characteristics:

  • Involves creating a fabricated scenario or 'pretext'.
  • Aims to gain trust and extract sensitive information.
  • Often involves direct, interactive communication, like phone calls.
Key Takeaway

Pretexting relies on a convincing fake story to manipulate victims; always verify identities through independent channels.

Example: Fake IT Support Call

An attacker calls, pretending to be IT, claiming an urgent account issue and asking for your password to 'fix' it.

Misconception: Trusting Professionalism

Attackers can sound very convincing and have some details about you. Don't assume legitimacy based on their demeanor.
