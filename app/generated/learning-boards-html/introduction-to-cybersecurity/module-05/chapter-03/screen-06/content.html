Phishing: The Widespread Threat

Fraudulent communications disguised as reputable sources.

How Phishing Works:

  • Sends fraudulent emails appearing from trusted entities (banks, services).
  • Aims to trick recipients into revealing sensitive data (passwords, credit cards).
  • Often creates urgency, fear, or excitement to prompt immediate action.
Key Takeaway

Phishing relies on deception and emotional triggers to bypass rational thought and steal information.

Example: Fake Bank Alert

An email claiming your bank account is compromised, urging you to click a link to 'verify' your details.

Common Mistake

Only checking the sender's display name, not the actual email address or link destination.
