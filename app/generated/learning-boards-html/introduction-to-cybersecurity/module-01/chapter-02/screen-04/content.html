Malware: Malicious Software Defined

Understanding the Foundation of Digital Threats

What is Malware?

Malware is a portmanteau for "malicious software." It's a broad term encompassing any software intentionally designed to cause harm, disrupt operations, or gain unauthorized access to a computer system, network, or data.

  • •Purpose: To disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access.
  • •Scope: A general term covering many specific types of threats.
  • •Evolution: Constantly evolving, requiring continuous vigilance.
Concrete Example

Imagine a program that secretly deletes your files or sends your private emails to an attacker. Both are examples of malware, even though their methods and impacts differ significantly.

Malware's Objectives

Disrupt Operations

Damage Systems

Unauthorized Access

Malware aims to compromise the integrity, availability, or confidentiality of data and systems.
Key Takeaway

Malware is a broad category of malicious software designed to harm or exploit computer systems, serving as the foundation for many cyber attacks.
