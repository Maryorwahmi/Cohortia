Social Engineering: Exploiting Human Psychology

The Art of Manipulation in Cybersecurity

What is Social Engineering?

Social engineering is a non-technical attack method that relies on manipulating individuals to trick them into revealing sensitive information or performing actions that compromise security. It exploits human trust, curiosity, and fear.

  • •Target: Human psychology, not technical vulnerabilities.
  • •Goal: Gain information or access through deception.
  • •Weakest Link: Often exploits the human element in security.
Concrete Example

An attacker calls an employee, pretending to be from IT support, and convinces them to reveal their password to 'fix' a critical issue.

Psychological Triggers

Urgency

Authority

Trust

Attackers exploit these human tendencies to bypass technical security controls.
Key Takeaway

Social engineering exploits human psychology to bypass security, making human awareness a critical defense layer.
