Key Concepts: Vulnerabilities & Attacks

Essential Terms for Cybersecurity Defense

Phishing

Fraudulent communications impersonating a reputable source to trick recipients into revealing sensitive data.

Vulnerability

A weakness in a system, design, or implementation that can be exploited by an attacker.

DoS Attack

An attack designed to make a machine or network resource unavailable to its intended users.

Summary Takeaway

Effective cybersecurity requires understanding specific attack methods like phishing, identifying system and human vulnerabilities, and recognizing denial-of-service attacks.
