Key Concepts: Malware & Social Engineering

Reinforcing Core Cybersecurity Definitions

Malware

Malicious software designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to computer systems.

Ransomware

Malware that encrypts files and demands a ransom for decryption, primarily impacting data availability.

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into performing actions or divulging confidential information through psychological tactics.

Summary Takeaway

Cybersecurity threats encompass both malicious software that targets systems and social engineering tactics that exploit human psychology.
