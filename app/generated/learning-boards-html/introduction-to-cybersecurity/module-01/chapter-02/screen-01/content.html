Common Cybersecurity Threats and Vulnerabilities

Understanding the Adversaries and Their Methods

In this chapter, you will learn to identify and differentiate between various types of malware, explain the mechanisms of social engineering attacks, recognize common system and human vulnerabilities, and describe Denial-of-Service attacks. This foundational knowledge is essential for building robust cybersecurity defenses.

Explore Key Topics
Malware

Malicious software designed to disrupt or gain unauthorized access.

Social Engineering

Exploiting human psychology to trick individuals into compromising security.

Vulnerabilities

Weaknesses in systems or human behavior that can be exploited by attackers.
