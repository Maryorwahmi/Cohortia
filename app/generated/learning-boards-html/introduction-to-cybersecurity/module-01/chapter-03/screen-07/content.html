The Three Pillars of MFA

Something You Know, Have, or Are

Something You Know

This is information only you should know, like a password or a PIN. It's the most common first factor.

Example: Your Account Password

Something You Have

This is a physical item in your possession, like a smartphone or a hardware token.

Example: SMS Code, Authenticator App, YubiKey

Something You Are

This involves unique biological characteristics, such as your fingerprint or facial scan.

Example: Fingerprint, Facial Recognition
Key Takeaway

Combining factors like 'something you know' (password), 'something you have' (phone), and 'something you are' (biometrics) creates a robust, multi-layered defense for your accounts.
