Understanding Strong, Unique Passwords

What Makes a Password Strong?

  • Length: At least 12-16 characters is a good baseline.
  • Complexity: Mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
  • Uniqueness: Never reuse passwords across different accounts.
  • Passphrases: A sequence of unrelated words is often strong and memorable.
Key Takeaway

A strong, unique password is your foundational defense against unauthorized access to your digital accounts.

Common Mistake

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts creates a single point of failure, making all accounts vulnerable if one is compromised.
