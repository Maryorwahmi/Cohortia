On-the-Go Protection

Navigating Public Networks Safely

Caution and VPNs for Public Wi-Fi

Unsecured Public Wi-Fi

Data can be easily intercepted by others on the same network, risking sensitive information.

  • Risk of data eavesdropping.
  • Avoid sensitive transactions.
  • Often no encryption.

Secure with a VPN

Encrypts your internet traffic and routes it through a secure server, protecting your data.

  • Encrypts all data traffic.
  • Masks your IP address.
  • Creates a secure tunnel.
Key Takeaway

Public Wi-Fi networks are often unsecured; always exercise caution and use a Virtual Private Network (VPN) to encrypt your traffic for sensitive activities.
