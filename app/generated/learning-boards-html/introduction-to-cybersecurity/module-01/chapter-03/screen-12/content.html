The Golden Rule of Data Protection

Implementing the 3-2-1 Backup Strategy

3

Copies of Your Data

Keep your primary data plus at least two additional backup copies.

Example: Original + External Drive + Cloud
2

Different Media Types

Store your backups on at least two distinct types of storage media.

Example: Hard Drive & Cloud Storage
1

Copy Offsite

At least one of your backup copies should be stored in a separate physical location.

Example: Cloud Storage or Remote Drive
Key Takeaway

The 3-2-1 backup rule ensures robust data protection: 3 copies of your data, on 2 different media types, with 1 copy stored offsite.
