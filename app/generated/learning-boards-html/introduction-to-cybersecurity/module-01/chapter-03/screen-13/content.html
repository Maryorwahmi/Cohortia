Everyday Vigilance

Navigating the Web with Confidence

Cultivating Smart Online Habits

Verify Links & Attachments

Be cautious of suspicious links and attachments, even from known senders. Always verify legitimacy.

Use Updated Antivirus

Install and regularly update reputable antivirus software to protect against malware and viruses.

Mind Your Shared Info

Be cautious about personal information shared online, especially on social media, to prevent social engineering.

Think Before You Click

If something seems too good to be true or unusual, it likely is. Exercise critical thinking online.

Key Takeaway

Cultivating smart online habits, such as verifying links, using updated antivirus, and being mindful of shared information, significantly enhances your personal cybersecurity.
