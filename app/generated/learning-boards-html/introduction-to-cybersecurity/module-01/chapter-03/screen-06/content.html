Beyond the Password

Adding a Second Layer of Verification with MFA

1. Enter Password

Provide your 'something you know' factor.

2. Provide Second Factor

Verify with 'something you have' or 'something you are'.

3. Gain Secure Access

Key Takeaway

MFA creates a crucial second barrier, protecting your accounts even if your password is stolen, by requiring additional verification factors.
