CIA Triad in Action

The Interplay of Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability

How Breaches in One Principle Affect the Others

Scenario: Ransomware Attack

Attackers encrypt an organization's data, making it inaccessible, and often exfiltrate sensitive information before encryption.

Availability Compromised

Data becomes inaccessible to authorized users.

Integrity Compromised

Backups may be corrupted, data altered.

Confidentiality Compromised

Sensitive data exfiltrated by attackers.

Key Takeaway

A single cyberattack can simultaneously undermine all three pillars of the CIA Triad, emphasizing the need for a holistic and integrated security approach.
