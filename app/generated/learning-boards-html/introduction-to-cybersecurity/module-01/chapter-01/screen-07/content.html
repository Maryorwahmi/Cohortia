Confidentiality: Keeping Secrets Safe

Ensuring Information is Accessed Only by Authorized Individuals

Definition

Ensures sensitive information is accessed only by authorized individuals, preventing unauthorized disclosure.

Key Tools & Techniques

  • Encryption
  • Access Controls
  • Strong Authentication (MFA)

Analogy: Personal Diary

Keeping your personal diary locked away ensures only you (or those you trust) can read its contents.

Concrete Example: Online Banking

Your username, password, and encrypted data transmission ensure only you access your account details.

Key Takeaway

Confidentiality is paramount for protecting sensitive information, relying on encryption, access controls, and strong authentication to prevent unauthorized disclosure.
