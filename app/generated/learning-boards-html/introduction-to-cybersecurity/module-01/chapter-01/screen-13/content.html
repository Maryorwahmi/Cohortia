Key Concepts: Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Revisiting Core Definitions and Principles

Cybersecurity

Protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks aimed at data access, modification, or disruption.

Information Security (InfoSec)

Broad practice of protecting all information (digital or physical) from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or destruction.

Network Security

Subset of cybersecurity focused on protecting network infrastructure and traffic from unauthorized access or misuse.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the distinct yet interconnected roles of Cybersecurity, Information Security, and Network Security is fundamental for building comprehensive digital defenses.
