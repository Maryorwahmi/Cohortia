Key Concepts: The CIA Triad

The Bedrock of Information Security

Confidentiality

Ensuring sensitive information is accessed only by authorized individuals, preventing unauthorized disclosure.

Integrity

Guaranteeing information remains accurate, complete, and authentic, preventing unauthorized modification or deletion.

Availability

Ensuring authorized users can access information and systems when needed, maintaining uptime and resilience.

Summary Takeaway

The CIA Triad is the foundational model for information security, providing the essential principles for designing and evaluating all security measures.
