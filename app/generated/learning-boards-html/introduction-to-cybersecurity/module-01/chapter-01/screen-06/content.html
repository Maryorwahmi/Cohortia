The CIA Triad: Core of Information Security

Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability

CIA Triad

Confidentiality

Protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access.

Integrity

Ensuring data accuracy, completeness, and authenticity.

Availability

Providing reliable access to information and systems when needed.

Key Takeaway

The CIA Triad provides a universal framework for assessing and designing robust security solutions, guiding comprehensive digital protection.
