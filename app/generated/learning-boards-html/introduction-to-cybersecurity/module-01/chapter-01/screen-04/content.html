What is Cybersecurity?

Protecting Digital Systems, Networks, and Programs

Definition

The practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.

Primary Purpose

To defend against attacks aimed at accessing, changing, or destroying sensitive information, extorting money, or interrupting business processes.

Common Attack Goals

  • Accessing sensitive data
  • Changing or destroying information
  • Extorting money
  • Interrupting operations

Analogy: Digital Home Security

Just as you lock your doors and install an alarm for your physical home, cybersecurity provides layered defenses for your data, devices, and online interactions.

Key Takeaway

Cybersecurity is a proactive and reactive defense mechanism against the ever-present threats in our digital landscape, ensuring safety and continuity.
