Key Distinctions

Information Security vs. Cybersecurity vs. Network Security

Understanding Their Scopes and Relationships

Information Security (InfoSec)

Broadest term, protecting all information (digital or physical) from unauthorized access, use, or destruction.

Cybersecurity

Subset of InfoSec, focusing on protecting information within the digital realm (systems, networks, electronic data).

Network Security

Subset of Cybersecurity, protecting network infrastructure and traffic from unauthorized access, misuse, or denial.

Key Takeaway

Understanding the distinct scopes of InfoSec, Cybersecurity, and Network Security is crucial for implementing a layered and comprehensive protection strategy.
