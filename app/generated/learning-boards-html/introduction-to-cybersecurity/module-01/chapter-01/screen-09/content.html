CIA Triad Principle

Availability: Ensuring Access When Needed

Providing Reliable Access to Information and Systems

Definition

Ensures authorized users can access information and systems when needed, maintaining uptime and resilience.

Key Strategies

  • Redundant Systems
  • Disaster Recovery Plans
  • Robust Network Infrastructure

Example Scenario: E-commerce Website

A website going offline due to a cyberattack or server malfunction compromises its availability, preventing user access.

Common Threat: DDoS Attacks

Denial-of-Service (DoS) and Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks specifically target availability by overwhelming systems.

Key Takeaway

Availability is critical for continuous operations and user satisfaction, requiring robust infrastructure and recovery plans to ensure reliable access.
