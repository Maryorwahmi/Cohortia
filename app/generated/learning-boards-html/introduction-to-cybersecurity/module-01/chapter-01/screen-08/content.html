Integrity: Maintaining Data Accuracy

Guaranteeing Information Remains Accurate and Authentic

Definition

Guarantees information remains accurate, complete, and authentic, preventing unauthorized modification or deletion.

Key Tools & Techniques

  • Hashing Algorithms
  • Digital Signatures
  • Version Control Systems

Example Scenario: Medical Records

Unauthorized modification of a patient's prescription dosage could lead to severe medical errors.

Misconception: Only Malicious Changes

Integrity protects against both malicious tampering and accidental corruption of data.

Key Takeaway

Integrity is vital for ensuring data trustworthiness, preventing any unauthorized or accidental alteration that could compromise its accuracy and authenticity.
