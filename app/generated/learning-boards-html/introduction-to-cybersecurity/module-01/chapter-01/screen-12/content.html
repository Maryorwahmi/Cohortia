Consequences

Real-World Impact of Cyber Breaches

From Individual Harm to Organizational Devastation

Financial Damages

Direct theft, recovery costs, legal fees, regulatory fines.

Reputational Harm

Loss of customer trust, damage to brand image, reduced market share.

Legal Liabilities

Regulatory fines, lawsuits from affected parties, compliance penalties.

Operational Shutdowns

Interruption of critical business processes, loss of revenue, potential job losses.

Example: E-commerce Credit Card Theft

Compromises confidentiality, leading to financial loss and identity theft for individuals.

Example: Database Server Crash

Compromises availability, making critical customer records inaccessible for an extended period.

Key Takeaway

The far-reaching and severe consequences of cyber breaches underscore why robust cybersecurity is not just good practice, but an absolute necessity for all.
