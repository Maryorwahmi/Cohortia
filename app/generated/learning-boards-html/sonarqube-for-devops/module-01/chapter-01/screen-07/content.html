Tangible Business Risks

From bugs and outages to reputational damage and lost revenue.

System Outages

Bugs can lead to critical system failures, making applications unavailable to users.

Lost Revenue & Trust

Downtime and poor user experience directly impact sales and damage brand reputation.

Increased Support Costs

More bugs mean more customer complaints and a higher burden on support teams.

Key Takeaway

The financial and reputational costs of poor code quality far outweigh the investment in proactive quality assurance.
