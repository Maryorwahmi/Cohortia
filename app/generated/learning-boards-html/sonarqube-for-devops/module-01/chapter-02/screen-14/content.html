Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember.

Technical Debt

The implied cost of additional rework caused by choosing an easy solution now instead of a better approach that would take longer.

Deliberate Technical Debt

Technical debt incurred consciously, often for short-term gains like meeting a deadline, with an intention to repay it later.

Inadvertent Technical Debt

Technical debt that accumulates unknowingly, often due to a lack of experience, poor design choices, or evolving requirements.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding the different types of technical debt is crucial for effective diagnosis and strategic management in software projects.
