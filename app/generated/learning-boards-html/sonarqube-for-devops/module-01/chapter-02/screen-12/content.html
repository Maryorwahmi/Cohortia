Measuring and Prioritizing Debt

Quantifying and strategizing repayment.

Strategic Debt Management

After identification, technical debt must be measured and prioritized to ensure that repayment efforts are focused on the most impactful areas.

  • •Quantify Effort: Use metrics like 'debt ratio' or 'remediation cost' to understand the scale of work.
  • •Prioritize by Impact: Focus on debt that poses high risk or significantly hinders development.
  • •Continuous Process: Debt management is ongoing, not a one-time fix.

Concrete Example

A team uses SonarQube's 'debt ratio' to identify the module with the highest technical debt, then prioritizes refactoring based on its criticality to the application.

Key Takeaway

Effective technical debt management requires quantifying the debt and strategically prioritizing repayment based on its potential impact and risk.
