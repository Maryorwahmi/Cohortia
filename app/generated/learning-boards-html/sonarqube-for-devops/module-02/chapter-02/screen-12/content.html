Practical Scenario
Responding to a Quality Gate Failure
A step-by-step guide for issue resolution.
Step 1
Identify Failed Quality Gate

Upon pipeline failure, go to the SonarQube project dashboard to see the failed Quality Gate status.

Step 2
Filter Issues in 'Issues' Tab

Navigate to the 'Issues' tab and filter for 'Vulnerabilities' and 'Critical' severity within the 'New Code' period.

Step 3
Locate & Understand Issue

Click on the specific issue to view details, remediation steps, and the exact line of code where it was found.

Step 4
Address & Re-run Pipeline

Fix the issue in your code, commit the changes, and trigger a new CI/CD pipeline run to re-evaluate.

Key Takeaway

A Quality Gate failure is a direct call to action, initiating a focused workflow to quickly identify and resolve critical issues in new code, ensuring continuous quality.
