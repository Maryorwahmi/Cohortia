Dashboard Overview

The SonarQube project dashboard is the first place you'll land after an analysis. It provides a comprehensive, at-a-glance view of your codebase's health.

  • •High-level ratings (A-E) for Reliability, Security, and Maintainability.
  • •Instant snapshot of project health.
  • •Quickly identify areas needing attention.
Key Takeaway

The dashboard is your initial guide, providing a quick health check and highlighting critical areas for deeper investigation.
