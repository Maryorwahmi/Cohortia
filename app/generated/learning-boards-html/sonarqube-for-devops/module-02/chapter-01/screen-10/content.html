Customizing Your Quality Profiles

Tailoring static analysis rules to fit specific project and team needs.

Activate Rules

Enable specific rules that align with your team's coding standards or compliance requirements.

Deactivate Rules

Disable rules that are irrelevant or too strict for your project, reducing noise in analysis reports.

Adjust Severity

Change the impact level of a rule (e.g., from Minor to Critical) to reflect its importance for your project.

Example Customization

For a Java project, you might activate rule 'S116: Class names should comply with a naming convention' and set its severity to 'Critical' to enforce strict coding standards. Conversely, you might deactivate 'S1118: Utility classes should not have public constructors' if your team frequently uses such patterns.

Benefit

Customization ensures that SonarQube's analysis is highly relevant to your project, reducing false positives and focusing developer attention on truly important issues.

Key Takeaway

Customizing Quality Profiles allows you to fine-tune SonarQube's static analysis rules, ensuring they perfectly match your project's unique quality requirements.
