Implementing Role-Based Access Control

A practical example of assigning permissions

Global Roles Sparingly

Limit 'Administer System' to very few individuals. Most users need only 'Browse' globally.

Project Roles for Granular Control

Assign specific permissions (e.g., 'Administer', 'Code Viewer') at the project level.

Leverage Groups

Assign permissions to groups rather than individual users for easier management.

Key Takeaway

Adopt a 'least privilege' model: grant minimal global access and use project-level roles and groups for specific responsibilities.

Example: DevOps Team Permissions
1
Identify Group

LDAP group: devops-team

2
Global Permissions

Grant Create Projects and Administer Quality Gates & Profiles.

3
Project-Level Result

Members automatically get Administer for projects they create.

Scenario: A member of devops-team creates a new project. They can now manage its specific quality gate and profile, but cannot change global SonarQube system settings.
