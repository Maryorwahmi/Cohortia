Assigning Quality Profiles to Projects

Ensure your projects use the correct rule sets for analysis.

Key Considerations

  • •**Explicit Assignment:** Each project needs a profile per language.
  • •**Default Fallback:** Without assignment, 'Sonar Way' is used.
  • •**Changes Not Immediate:** Profile modifications require re-analysis.
  • •**Regular Review:** Ensure profiles remain relevant and effective.
Key Takeaway

Always explicitly assign Quality Profiles to projects and re-run analyses after changes to ensure your custom rules are applied effectively.

Common Pitfall: Unassigned Profiles

A frequent mistake is creating a custom Quality Profile but forgetting to assign it to the target project. Even if you've meticulously configured your rules, the project will continue to use the default 'Sonar Way' profile until you explicitly go to 'Project Settings > Quality Profiles' and select your custom profile for the relevant language. This oversight can lead to confusion when expected issues aren't reported.

Process: Assigning a Quality Profile

1

Navigate to your **project's dashboard** in SonarQube.

2

Go to **Project Settings > Quality Profiles**.

3

Select your **custom profile** for each relevant language and click **'Apply'**.

4

**Re-run an analysis** for the project to apply the new profile changes.
