Key Concepts: Tuning & Resilience
Essential terms for optimizing and securing your SonarQube instance.

JVM Memory Tuning

Adjusting Java Virtual Machine heap size for SonarQube's processes to optimize performance.

High Availability (HA)

Designing a system to remain operational and accessible even if some components fail.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Policies and procedures to recover vital infrastructure and systems after a disaster.

Summary Takeaway

Effective JVM tuning, implementing high availability, and planning for disaster recovery are crucial for ensuring SonarQube's performance, stability, and continuous operation in production environments.
