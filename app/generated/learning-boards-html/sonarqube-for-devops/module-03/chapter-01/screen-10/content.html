Avoiding Common Integration Mistakes

Timing and Configuration of SonarQube Analysis

Analysis Too Late

  • •Running analysis during nightly builds or post-deployment.
  • •Slow feedback loop negates 'shift left' benefits.
  • •Increases remediation cost and time.
Best Practice

Execute immediately after successful build and unit tests.

Incorrect Quality Gate

  • •Overly strict gates cause frustration and false failures.
  • •Overly lenient gates defeat the purpose of quality enforcement.
  • •Hinders adoption and trust in the tool.
Best Practice

Start with reasonable gates and iterate based on team feedback.

Key Takeaway

Optimal timing and balanced Quality Gate configuration are crucial for SonarQube's effectiveness and team adoption.
