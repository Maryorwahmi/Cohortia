Core Concept

Shifting Left: Early Quality Assurance

Detecting and Addressing Issues at the Source

Early Detection

Integrate quality checks at the earliest stages of the development lifecycle, immediately after code commit.

Reduced Cost

Fixing issues early is significantly less expensive and time-consuming than discovering them later in the pipeline or production.

Proactive Prevention

Shifting left transforms quality from a reactive bottleneck into a proactive, continuous process, preventing defects at the source.

Key Takeaway

Embracing 'shift left' with SonarQube ensures that quality is built into the software from the start, not just tested in at the end.
