Granular Quality Gates: The Concept

Tailoring quality checks to development stages

Definition

Granular Quality Gates involve using distinct sets of quality criteria for different development contexts, such as feature branches, development branches, or release branches.

Why It Matters

This approach prevents unnecessary blockers in early development while ensuring rigorous standards for production-ready code, balancing speed with quality.

Key Benefit

Enables context-aware quality checks, allowing for flexibility during experimentation and strictness for deployment.

Key Takeaway

Granular Quality Gates optimize the development workflow by applying appropriate quality standards at each stage, from lenient feature development to strict release preparation.
