Implementing Effective Quality Gates

Balancing aspiration with practicality for sustainable quality

01

Start Achievable

Begin with a baseline that is manageable and realistic for your team's current maturity level. Avoid setting overly strict conditions initially.

02

Gradually Tighten

As your team improves and adapts, incrementally increase the strictness of your Quality Gate conditions. This fosters continuous improvement.

03

Focus on New Code

Prioritize conditions on 'new code' to prevent the introduction of new issues, rather than trying to fix all legacy debt at once.

04

Integrate Early

Embed Quality Gates into your CI/CD pipeline so feedback is immediate, allowing developers to address issues quickly.

05

Communicate & Educate

Ensure developers understand the purpose of the gates and how to meet the conditions. Foster a culture of shared ownership.

Key Takeaway

Effective Quality Gates evolve with your team, balancing strictness with practicality to drive continuous improvement without causing frustration.
