Deployment Strategy

Single vs. Multiple SonarQube Instances

Weighing the benefits and challenges of different SonarQube deployment models.

Single Scaled Instance
  • Simpler administration & centralized reporting.
  • Easier cross-project analysis.
  • Potential single point of failure if HA is weak.
  • Performance bottlenecks if not adequately resourced.
Multiple Instances
  • Isolation for decentralized organizations/departments.
  • Allows different security/compliance requirements.
  • Increased administrative overhead & difficult cross-instance reporting.
  • Each instance needs its own database.
Key Takeaway

The choice between single and multiple SonarQube instances depends on your organization's structure, compliance needs, and willingness to manage administrative overhead.

Decision Factors for Instance Strategy

Organizational Structure

Centralized vs. decentralized teams and departments.

Compliance & Security

Varying regulatory or security requirements across business units.

Administrative Overhead

Capacity to manage multiple deployments, upgrades, and configurations.

Reporting Needs

Requirement for unified cross-project reporting vs. isolated views.

Scenario

A large conglomerate with independent subsidiaries, each having unique quality gates and development practices, would likely benefit from multiple SonarQube instances for isolation.
