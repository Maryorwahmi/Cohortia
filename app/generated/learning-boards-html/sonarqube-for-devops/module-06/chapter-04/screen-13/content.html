Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

JVM Tuning

Optimizing JVM settings (heap size, garbage collector) for SonarQube's performance and stability.

High Availability (HA)

System design ensuring high operational continuity for SonarQube, minimizing downtime.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Policies and procedures for recovering vital systems like SonarQube after a disaster.

Summary Takeaway

Effective SonarQube scaling relies on a deep understanding of JVM optimization, high availability, and robust disaster recovery strategies to ensure continuous operation and data integrity.
