Configuration Best Practices

Common Project Misconfigurations

Correcting sonar-project.properties, Quality Gates, and Profiles.

01

sonar-project.properties

Ensure correct paths for sonar.sources, sonar.tests, and especially sonar.java.binaries (must point to compiled .class files).

02

Quality Gate Assignment

Verify that the correct Quality Gate is assigned to your project. An incorrect gate can lead to misleading pass/fail statuses.

03

Quality Profile Assignment

Ensure the correct Quality Profile is active for your project's language. The wrong profile means rules won't be applied as expected.

Key Takeaway

Meticulous configuration of project properties, Quality Gates, and Profiles ensures accurate and effective code analysis.
