Understanding SonarQube Upgrades: Minor vs. Major

Differentiating upgrade types for effective planning

Minor Upgrades (e.g., 9.x to 9.y)

Generally straightforward, focusing on bug fixes and small enhancements within the same major version. Database migrations are usually minimal.

Major Upgrades (e.g., 8.x to 9.x)

Involve significant changes, including database schema updates, deprecated features, and potential plugin incompatibilities. Require extensive planning.

Key Takeaway

Always consult the official SonarQube upgrade guide for your specific version path before initiating any upgrade, especially major ones.

Upgrade Impact Comparison
Minor Upgrade
  • Minimal downtime
  • Small DB changes
  • Low plugin risk
Major Upgrade
  • Planned downtime
  • Significant DB schema changes
  • High plugin incompatibility risk
