Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › Network segmentation and VLANs
Comparison: Segmentation vs No Segmentation
Segmentation confines threats; lack of segmentation allows lateral movement to sensitive systems.
With Segmentation
Guest, corporate, IoT, and POS VLANs with strict ACLs and firewall rules limit access between groups.
Without Segmentation
Devices on the same flat network can access sensitive systems, increasing breach impact.
Concrete Steps
  • Define VLANs for each trust level and document allowed flows.
  • Enforce segmentation with firewalls and ACLs, not just SSID separation.
  • Maintain diagrams and firewall rule sets for auditors.
Misconception

SSID isolation without network-layer enforcement is insufficient; enforce segment boundaries at routers and firewalls.
