Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › Logging, auditing, and breach notification
Logging that matters
  • RADIUS authentication and accounting events
  • AP association and roaming logs
  • Controller administrative changes and syslogs
  • Firewall and ACL flow logs showing segment enforcement
Concrete Example
After a suspected PHI access, RADIUS and AP logs can show which user and device connected and when, enabling targeted investigation.
Process Tip
Define retention windows aligned to policy and ensure secure storage of logs to prevent tampering.
Breach Notification
Know your internal contacts, the evidence to provide, and statutory timelines for notification. Prepare templates and an evidence pack for speed during incidents.
Common Misconception

Storing logs without review does not reduce risk. Regular review and alerting are necessary to detect and act on incidents.
