Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › GDPR essentials for WLANs
GDPR Principles Applied
Focus on three principles: minimize collection, limit purpose, and secure data.
Data Minimization
Only collect identifiers you need. Avoid raw MAC address logs if an aggregated count suffices.
Purpose Limitation
Document why you collect guest emails or connection times and do not reuse them without consent.
Security
Encrypt wireless traffic and secure stored identifiers with access controls and logging.
Concrete Example
A hotel collects email addresses for guest Wi‑Fi. To align with GDPR they must: record consent, protect the email store, limit retention, and document the purpose. If breached, they must follow notification rules.
Common Misconception

Deleting a log file from one system does not eliminate responsibility; you must account for copies, backups, and correlated data that can re-identify users.

Key Takeaway

Treat any collected identifier as regulated until you can show it cannot be linked to an individual.
