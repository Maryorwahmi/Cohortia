Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › Key Concepts
Encryption
Use WPA2/WPA3 Enterprise for sensitive wireless links.
Authentication
802.1X with RADIUS for per-user/device accountability.
Segmentation
VLANs and firewalls to isolate guests, staff, IoT, and POS.
Logging
Collect RADIUS, AP, controller, and firewall logs and retain per policy.
Vulnerability Mgmt
Scan, patch, and test wireless infrastructure regularly.
Physical Security
Prevent tampering with APs and controllers to avoid local compromise.
Summary
Regulatory compliance for WLANs is achieved by implementing, documenting, and testing these controls across technology and policies.
