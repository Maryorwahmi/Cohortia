Orientation

This chapter treats regulations as design constraints. Where data that can identify individuals, patient records, or cardholder information flows over Wi‑Fi, the network must use appropriate technical and organizational measures to reduce risk and enable compliance.

What you'll practice
  • Mapping regulation requirements to WLAN controls
