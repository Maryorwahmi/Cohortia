Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › What to Remember
Encrypt & Authenticate
Always use enterprise-grade methods for sensitive traffic.
Segment & Restrict
VLANs and firewalls reduce blast radius for sensitive data.
Log & Maintain
Collect evidence, patch, and document to show due diligence.
Final Thought
Focus on controls you can document and test; that is how technical choices become defensible in audits and incidents.
