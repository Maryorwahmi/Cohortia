Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › Designing a compliant WLAN for a small clinic
Step-by-step Design
  1. Identify categories: staff (PHI), guests, IoT.
  2. Create separate VLANs and document allowed flows.
  3. Require 802.1X with RADIUS for staff; enable accounting.
  4. Use WPA3 Enterprise for staff; captive portal for guests with no internal access.
  5. Enforce firewall ACLs to prevent guest-to-staff access.
  6. Include APs in patching and physical security checks; retain logs for audits.
Key Takeaway

Combine segmentation, strong auth, encryption, and logging with documented policies to meet HIPAA obligations for wireless access.

Concrete Example
Staff VLAN: EHR access, WPA3 Enterprise, 802.1X, RADIUS logs. Guest VLAN: captive portal, internet-only, no route to internal servers. IoT VLAN: restricted outbound only.
Misconception
Believing captive portal alone protects PHI. Without VLAN and firewall enforcement, guests could still reach internal services.
