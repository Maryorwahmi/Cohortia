Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › Learning Objectives (continued)
Apply and Demonstrate
Plan and document technical controls so they can be validated by auditors and incident responders.
Design VLANs & Firewall Rules
Isolate staff, guests, IoT, and payment devices with strict ACLs and minimal routing between them.
Logging & Retention
Keep access and authentication logs for audit windows required by policies and incident response needs.
Device Hardening
Change defaults, restrict admin access, and apply firmware updates as part of vulnerability management.
Document Evidence
Prepare configuration snapshots, policy documents, and patch records to demonstrate compliance.
