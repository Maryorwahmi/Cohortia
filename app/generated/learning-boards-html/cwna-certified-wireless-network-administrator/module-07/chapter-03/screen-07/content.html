Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › PCI DSS and wireless payment systems
PCI DSS Applied to WLANs

Focus on encryption, segmentation, firewalling, and avoiding default credentials when wireless is used for cardholder data.

Firewall & Segmentation
Protect the CDE with firewall rules and place POS devices on an isolated VLAN to limit access.
Change Defaults
Do not use vendor-supplied admin passwords for APs or controllers; these are common breach points.
Encrypt Transmission
Use WPA2/WPA3 Enterprise for any wireless segment that touches cardholder data.
Retail POS Example
Wireless POS terminals must be on a segmented VLAN with ACLs to backend processors. Devices should authenticate to RADIUS and be covered by scheduled vulnerability scans.
Common Misconception

Assuming that installing encryption is enough. Encryption helps, but segmentation, patching, and monitoring complete the required controls.

Key Takeaway

Demonstrable segmentation and encryption, plus evidence of scans and changed defaults, are essential to meet PCI expectations.
