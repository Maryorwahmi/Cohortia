Module 07 › Chapter 7.3 › Vulnerability management and physical security
Vulnerability Management
Include APs, controllers, and authentication servers in scans and remediation plans.
Scans & Pen Tests
Schedule regular wireless scans and penetration tests to find misconfigurations and exposures.
Patch Management
Maintain a tested patch schedule and document updates for auditors.
Physical Security
Secure APs and controllers against tampering and theft to prevent local compromise.
Concrete Example
Wall-mounted APs in public areas should be anchored and monitored; controllers should be in locked equipment rooms with restricted access.
Key Takeaway

Combine digital vulnerability programs with physical controls to reduce the likelihood of wireless compromise.
