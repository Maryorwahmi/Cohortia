Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Conflict of Interest
Core Concept

Recognize and manage conflicts

Ask whether your recommendation serves organizational goals. If not, disclose and recuse.

Example: disclose personal incentives when suggesting a vendor and include a comparative analysis.
Safeguard: involve procurement and document decision criteria.
Objective checklist
Use measurable criteria: performance, compatibility, security, total cost of ownership.
If incentives exist, disclose in writing and step back from the final decision if appropriate.
Misconception: small gifts are harmless. In reality they can bias decisions and harm trust.
