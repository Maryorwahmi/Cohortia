Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Protecting privacy
Process Flow

Responsible monitoring steps

1. Confirm documented authorization and legal approvals.
2. Narrow scope: capture only what is necessary and for a short window.
3. Secure storage and access control for any captured data.
4. Document reasoning, steps taken, and who was notified.
Common Mistake

Collecting broad packet captures without authorization or retention limits exposes users and increases legal risk.

Scenario
You suspect data exfiltration from a device. Follow the process: get approval, capture targeted metadata first, avoid full payloads unless critical, and store artifacts securely for incident response.
Pro Tip: Use role-based access logs so only authorized investigators can access captures.
Key Takeaway: Authorization and minimal collection make monitoring ethical and legally defensible.
