How to use this chapter

Apply the framework in scenarios, document choices, and consult stakeholders when needed.

Study Habit
Work through the hands-on dilemma. Write the steps you would take and the stakeholders you would notify.
Document your decision and the authorization status.
If unsure, consult legal counsel or HR before acting.
Prioritize fixes that protect confidentiality and integrity even if they cause short downtime.
Assessment Tips
Explain the ethical reason for each choice, show who you would notify, and list practical safeguards you would implement.
Example: prioritize patching a guest portal vulnerability to prevent unauthorized access to internal systems.
Counterexample: do not adopt a weak shared password for convenience; explain the risk to network integrity.
