Question Example

Fixing a critical vulnerability vs avoiding outage

Explain why fixing now is ethically required: it reduces risk to confidentiality and integrity despite short-term disruption. Describe communication steps and scheduling to limit impact.
Answer Structure
  1. State the primary ethical priority (privacy, integrity, availability).
  2. Describe potential harms if action is delayed.
  3. Propose authorized remediation and communication.
  4. Note documentation and follow-up checks.
Key Takeaway
Frame answers around risk and authorization; document decisions and communicate clearly.
