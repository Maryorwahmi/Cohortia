Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Reminders
Confidentiality
Treat vulnerabilities and user data as sensitive and restrict disclosure to authorized parties.
Consult & Document
When unsure, consult HR, legal, or ethics committees and keep written records of requests and approvals.
Codes & Learning
Follow professional codes of conduct and update practices as laws and threats evolve.
Summary Takeaway

Good ethics are a combination of policy, controls, transparent communication, and professional judgement — practice them regularly.
