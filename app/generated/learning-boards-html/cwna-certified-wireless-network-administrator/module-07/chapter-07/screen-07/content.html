Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Integrity & Availability
Definition

Integrity and availability as duty

Maintain accurate configurations and ensure services are accessible to authorized users when needed.

Design for redundancy and recovery to minimize the ethical cost of outages.
Do not sacrifice availability for convenience or personal preference.
Key Takeaway
Treat uptime and accuracy as ethical priorities: plan, test, and document trade-offs.
Scenario
An administrator considers delaying a firmware upgrade to avoid downtime during business hours. Ethical practice requires evaluating the security risk of delaying versus the disruption of applying the patch and choosing the option that protects confidentiality and system integrity while communicating impacts to stakeholders.
Pro Tip: schedule critical patches during agreed maintenance windows with clear notifications.
Misconception: avoiding small disruptions is always best. Reality: delaying critical fixes can result in much larger harm.
