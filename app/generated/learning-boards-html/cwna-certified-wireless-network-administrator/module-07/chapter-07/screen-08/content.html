Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Transparency
Core Concept

Be factual, timely, and protective

Report issues with relevant facts, protect confidential details, and outline mitigation steps.

Example: after an outage, provide a timeline and remediation plan to stakeholders rather than a vague explanation.
Avoid: withholding information that prevents leadership from assessing risk or making decisions.
Why it matters
Honesty preserves trust, speeds response, and creates a record for learning and prevention.
Practical communication checklist
  • Summarize the issue in plain language for stakeholders.
  • State known impact and affected services.
  • Describe immediate mitigation and next steps.
  • Avoid exposing user data in public updates.
Key Takeaway
Transparent, factual updates reduce harm and help stakeholders act responsibly.
