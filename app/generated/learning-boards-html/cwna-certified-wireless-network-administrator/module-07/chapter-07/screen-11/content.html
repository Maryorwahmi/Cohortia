Scenario

Manager requests covert monitoring

Your manager asks for monitoring an employee's internet activity without HR approval. This request raises privacy, legality, and professional-ethics concerns.

Step 1: Identify the dilemma and stakeholders: employee, manager, HR, legal, and the organization.
Step 2: List consequences of complying: illegal surveillance, damaged trust, and exposure to liability.
Step 3: Ethical response: refuse covert action, document the request, consult HR/legal, and propose formal investigation steps.
Deliverable
Write 3–4 paragraphs outlining the dilemma, three negative consequences, and your ethical course of action: refusal, consultation, and documented alternatives.
Check your work: Did you prioritize privacy and document your steps? Did you propose a lawful alternative?
