Learning Objectives

01
Understand ethical responsibilities
Describe duties that come with WLAN access, and why administrators must safeguard trust and privacy.
Mnemonic: Duty-first

Identify ethical dilemmas
Recognize scenarios like unauthorized monitoring, misuse of tools, or conflicts of interest.
Mnemonic: Spot the fork
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain privacy, confidentiality, integrity
Explain how these principles apply to wireless data, logs, and configurations.
Mnemonic: PIC (Privacy, Integrity, Confidentiality)
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Develop an ethical decision framework
Use codes of conduct, seek counsel, document actions, and prioritize security and privacy.
Mnemonic: Ask, Consult, Document, Act
