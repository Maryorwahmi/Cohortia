Process Flow

Four steps to decide

  1. Identify the issue and stakeholders.
  2. Consult policies and codes of conduct.
  3. Seek advice from HR, legal, or supervisors.
  4. Act to protect privacy, integrity, and availability; document everything.
Concrete example
Manager requests covert monitoring. Follow the four steps: identify privacy risk, consult policy, seek HR/legal guidance, and refuse unauthorized monitoring while proposing alternatives.
Support cards
Document: keep a written timeline of requests, approvals, and actions.
Consult: use established professional codes as a baseline for action.
Fallback: if still unsure, decline covert or intrusive requests until formal authorization is provided.
