Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Key Concepts
Data Privacy
Individuals can control their personal data; admins must avoid unauthorized monitoring and protect captured data.
Integrity & Availability
Keep the network accurate and accessible; design for redundancy and recoverability.
Responsible Tools
Use sniffers, injectors, and other tools only with authorization, limited scope, and proper documentation.
Takeaway

Ethical administration combines policy, technical controls, transparent communication, and sound judgement to protect people and systems.
