Module 7 › Chapter 7.7 › Tool Use
Comparison

Authorized troubleshooting

Used with permission, documented, and scoped. Minimizes data collection and impact while enabling diagnosis.
Example: approved packet capture to trace authentication failures for 30 minutes.
Safeguards: encryption of stored captures, role-based access, and retention limits.
Comparison

Misuse

Running intrusive tests or continuous monitoring without authorization can disrupt services and violate privacy or policy.
Counterexample: executing a deauthentication attack on production to test resilience without permission.
Consequence: service disruption, loss of trust, and possible legal ramifications.
Pro Tip
When testing, use a sandbox, lab, or scheduled maintenance window and secure approvals beforehand.
