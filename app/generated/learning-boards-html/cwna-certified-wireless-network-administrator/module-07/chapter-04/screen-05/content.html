Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › MPE vs SAR vs EIRP
MPE
Regulatory exposure limits, often power density thresholds.
Use these limits to judge whether an installation exposes the public or workers to unsafe RF levels.
SAR
Rate of RF energy absorption by body tissue, W/kg.
SAR is used to understand internal absorption, commonly in device safety statements.
EIRP
Effective output accounting for transmitter and antenna gain.
EIRP determines how concentrated RF energy will be and therefore how far safe distances must be.
Practical example
An AP with 20 dBm transmitter power and an antenna with 12 dBi gain results in greater EIRP than the AP alone. That higher EIRP can reduce the required safe distance or create a danger zone where MPE would be exceeded.
Mistake: Only reading transmitter watts.
Fix: Calculate EIRP and consult MPE tables.
Check: Manufacturer exclusion distances.
