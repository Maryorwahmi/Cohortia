Learning Objectives
Understand, apply, and verify safe WLAN installations.
01
Explain RF exposure and limits
Define MPE and SAR, and why they matter for public safety. Recognize when measured power densities could exceed limits and require mitigation.
Mnemonic: Think "Power density, Absorption"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Apply antenna placement rules
Select mounting height and exclusion zones from manufacturer guidance to keep public exposure below MPE thresholds and improve coverage.
Mnemonic: "Height, Restrict, Aim"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Implement grounding and surge protection
Create a verified ground plan and choose lightning arrestors and surge protectors for outdoor feedlines and PoE runs.
Mnemonic: "Earth first"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Manage power and cabling safely
Match PoE standards, use rated outdoor cable, and ensure secure routing to avoid overheating and physical hazards.
Mnemonic: "Rated, Routed, Reviewed"
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
