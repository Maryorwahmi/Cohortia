Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Rooftop Scenario
Installation checklist
  1. Confirm EIRP and manufacturer exclusion distance.
  2. Map the courtyard footprint and intersection with any beam paths.
  3. Specify grounding conductor and earth electrode; bond all metal parts.
  4. Install coax arrestor and bond to the same ground.
  5. Use outdoor-rated PoE cable, match PSE, and add Ethernet surge protection.
  6. Document mitigations and test grounding continuity after install.
Concrete mitigation examples
If exclusion distances intersect the courtyard, options include raising to roof level, changing antenna pattern, or restricting courtyard access during operation. For lightning protection, pick arrestors rated for the cable type and bond them to the same ground rod as the mast.
Key Takeaway

A site checklist ensures safety and compliance. Require documented measurements and bonding before sign-off.
