Environmental and Safety Regulations
Protect personnel and equipment by following RF exposure limits and electrical safety practices.

This lesson explains why RF exposure limits and grounding practices matter in WLAN deployments. You will learn the meaning of MPE and SAR, how EIRP relates to exposure, and the practical steps needed for safe antenna installs. We'll use an outdoor rooftop scenario to show typical mistakes and fixes.
