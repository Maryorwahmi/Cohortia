Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Surge Protection
Where to install
  • Coax lightning arrestor at enclosure entry, bonded to earth.
  • PoE surge protectors near the point where cable transitions from outdoor to indoor.
Bond arrestors to the same grounding electrode used for the mast for best protection.
Example
A surge on an outdoor coax will be diverted through the arrestor into ground, reducing energy reaching the AP and downstream switches.
Remember: rating and correct bonding determine effectiveness.
Common Mistake
Installing arrestors but not bonding them to the same earth electrode can leave equipment vulnerable due to potential differences.
Key Takeaway
Arrestors work only when correctly chosen, located, and bonded; include them in the grounding plan and test regularly.
