Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Antenna Placement
Safe placement process
1. Gather specs
EIRP, antenna pattern, manufacturer exclusion distances.
2. Map human traffic
Identify walkways, maintenance access, and work zones that could intersect beams.
3. Choose mount & orientation
Avoid pointing high-gain beams across public spaces; raise height or change location when needed.
4. Document and restrict
Record exclusion zones and post signage or restrict access if necessary.
Concrete example
An outdoor omnidirectional antenna planned at 10 feet over a courtyard may create an exclusion zone at ground level. Mitigations include raising the mount to roof level, selecting a lower-gain antenna, or adding restricted access around the courtyard.
Common Mistake

Applying indoor mounting rules outdoors without recalculating EIRP and exclusion distances can create unexpected danger zones.
