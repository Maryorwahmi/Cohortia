Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Inspection & Maintenance
Maintenance schedule
Create routine checks: visual inspections after storms, annual grounding resistance tests, and periodic replacement of surge protectors.
  • Inspect for corrosion or loose bonds.
  • Verify cable jackets and conduit integrity.
  • Test ground continuity and record results.
Documentation
Keep inspection records, test readings, and corrective actions. This protects people and supports future troubleshooting.
Key Takeaway

Ongoing inspections and clear records are essential to maintain safety and respond to weather-related events.

Check

After any storm, re-test grounding continuity and visually inspect arrestors and cable runs before declaring the network stable.
