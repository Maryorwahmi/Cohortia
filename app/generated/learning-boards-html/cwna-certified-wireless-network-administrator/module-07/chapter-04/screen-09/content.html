Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › PoE & Cable Safety
PoE matching
Confirm AP power class and ensure the PSE supports the required wattage plus headroom for peak load.
  • Check PSE capability and cable length limits.
  • Use higher-spec cabling for higher power classes.
Cabling and routing
Use outdoor-rated, UV-resistant jacketed cable for outside runs and conduit for mechanical protection. Secure runs and avoid sharp bends or tension that stress connectors.
Example
An outdoor AP fed by an underspecified PoE switch and long cable run may experience thermal stress. Use a PSE rated for the worst-case power draw and choose appropriate cable to reduce voltage drop and heat.
Warning

Incorrect PoE matching or poor cable routing can cause overheating and failure; always verify power budgets and cable ratings before deployment.
