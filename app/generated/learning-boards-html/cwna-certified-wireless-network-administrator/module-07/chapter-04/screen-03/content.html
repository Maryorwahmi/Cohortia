Objective Contexts
Situations where each skill matters
RF Exposure — When assessing outdoor APs
  • Compare EIRP and antenna gain to manufacturer exclusion distances.
  • Decide if a public area requires restricted access or a different mounting location.
Antenna Placement — Coverage and safety
  • Mount at heights reducing human exposure while keeping signal paths clear of obstructions.
  • Avoid pointing high-gain beams across walkways.
Grounding & Arrestors — Protect during storms
  • Install lightning arrestors on coax and surge protectors on PoE lines.
  • Verify earth connections with a continuity tester after installation.
PoE & Cabling — Match standards and rating
  • Ensure the PSE and AP use compatible PoE standards and cables are outdoor-rated where needed.
  • Secure cables to prevent tripping and physical damage.
