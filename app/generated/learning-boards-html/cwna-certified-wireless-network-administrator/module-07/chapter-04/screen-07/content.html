Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Grounding Basics
Why ground outdoor equipment?
Grounding provides a low-resistance path to earth for lightning or fault currents, reducing the chance of shock and equipment destruction.
Bond antenna mounts, feedline shields, and enclosures to a verified earth electrode.
Use manufacturer-specified clamps and ensure conductor continuity.
Record resistance or continuity test results for maintenance.
Architecture overview
Show a simple plan: antenna mast bonded to ground conductor, ground rod/earth electrode, arrestor on feedline tied to the same ground.
Ensure short bonds between antenna and the ground electrode and avoid multiple unbonded grounds that create loops.
Pro Tip

Use a continuity tester to confirm bonding and record resistance values; a single documented test is better than a guess.
