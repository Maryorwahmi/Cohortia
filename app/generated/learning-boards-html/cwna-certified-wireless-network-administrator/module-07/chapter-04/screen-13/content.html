Activity: Review the rooftop plan
Identify three safety concerns and propose mitigations based on the scenario: an AP 10 feet above a public courtyard with a high-gain omnidirectional antenna.
Concern 1
RF exposure to courtyard users if exclusion distance is exceeded.
Mitigation: raise mount, lower antenna gain, or restrict access.
Concern 2
No grounding or arrestor specified for the coax feedline.
Mitigation: add a bonded lightning arrestor and short, tested ground conductor.
Concern 3
Use of indoor cable or insufficient PoE capacity.
Mitigation: use outdoor-rated cable, conduit, and verify PSE power budget.
Deliverable

Create a bulleted list of concerns and mitigations and two questions to ask the installer: example questions include asking for EIRP/exclusion data and grounding test records.
