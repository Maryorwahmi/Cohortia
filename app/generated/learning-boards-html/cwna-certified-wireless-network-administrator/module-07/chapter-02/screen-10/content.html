Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Regulatory Domains
Architecture

AP regulatory domain settings

Vendors map country codes to allowed channels and power. Set the code to match your physical region to align AP behavior with local rules.

Consequences of misconfiguration
  • Illegal operation or lower-than-expected spectrum availability.
  • Poor performance due to unavailable channels or incorrect power limits.
AP features

Most enterprise APs auto-adjust settings when the correct country is set; verify during commissioning.

Concrete task

Change the regulatory domain in an AP GUI and observe channel lists and maximum power values update for both 2.4 and 5 GHz.

Pitfall

Deploying devices with a mismatched domain can lead to legal and operational problems; never assume defaults are correct.

Key Takeaway

Verify and set the country/regulatory domain on each AP to match its deployment location before service activation.
