Concept

Transmit Power Control

Adjust transmit power to the minimum required to maintain reliable links and reduce interference.

Benefits
  • Reduces co‑channel interference.
  • Improves spatial reuse and network capacity.
Example

AP reduces from 23 dBm to 10 dBm when clients are nearby to limit interference footprint.

Misconception

Higher transmit power always improves user experience — not true. If clients roam to far APs, uplink may fail even when downlink seems fine.

Practical tip

Use TPC with careful RSSI goals: lower power to improve reuse in dense areas; raise power only where coverage gaps exist.

Key Takeaway

TPC reduces interference and supports higher overall capacity when applied with coverage-aware planning.
