Practice tasks and verification

Each objective connects to a hands‑on check you can run on an enterprise AP management interface or simulator.

Inspect regulatory domain
Open the AP settings and confirm country code; observe available channels and max power values listed.
Detect DFS events
Check AP logs for radar detection and channel switches; note the non‑occupancy time applied.
Apply TPC reasoning
Measure RSSI and reduce power if clients are close to improve spatial reuse and reduce interference.
Verification

Show a screenshot or note of the AP's regulatory page for two different regions and a short explanation of observed differences.
