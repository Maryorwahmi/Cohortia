2.4 GHz
Longer range, fewer non‑overlapping channels, heavy interference sources.
5 GHz
More channels and capacity; plan for DFS behavior and regional variations.
6 GHz
Large contiguous spectrum and wide channels with shorter range and stricter indoor/power rules.
Memory aid

Range decreases as frequency increases; capacity generally increases—always check DFS and TPC rules for your region.
