Definition

Licensed vs Unlicensed

Licensed spectrum provides exclusive rights; unlicensed is shared but governed by rules.

Licensed - practical view
  • Exclusive rights reduce unexpected interference.
  • Often used by carriers and critical services.
Unlicensed - practical view
  • Open to all devices that follow technical rules.
  • Requires coexistence mechanisms and planning.
Analogy

Think of licensed spectrum like a private road and unlicensed like a public highway: the public highway is open, but rules and traffic management are essential for safe travel.

Concrete example

Cellular carriers use licensed bands so their networks do not contend with arbitrary devices, while Wi‑Fi operates in unlicensed bands and must plan for many coexisting devices.

Common misconception

Unlicensed does not mean 'unregulated' — devices must obey power, channel, and coexistence rules.

Key Takeaway

Design for sharing: expect interference and use regulatory tools to reduce its impact.
