Mistakes and mitigations

Recognize frequent errors and the practical steps to avoid them.

Avoiding DFS

Consequence: reduced usable channels and increased congestion. Mitigation: include DFS channels, plan for occasional channel moves.

Wrong regulatory domain

Consequence: illegal operation or limited performance. Mitigation: verify country code on deployment.

Over‑powering APs

Consequence: interference and roaming issues. Mitigation: use TPC and plan AP density instead of raising power.

Key Takeaway

Fix the root cause—use regulatory tools and planning instead of disabling protections or increasing power without analysis.
