Hands‑On

Inspect and compare regulatory domains

Follow these steps in a lab or maintenance window to avoid impacting production clients.

Step 1: Locate setting

Open the AP management GUI or CLI and find the Country Code or Regulatory Domain field.

Step 2: Record channels & power

Note available channels and maximum transmit power for each band; take screenshots if possible.

Step 3: Change domain

Switch to a different country and repeat the observations, noting channels removed or power reduced.

Step 4: Compare

Prepare a short note explaining the practical differences and any actions you would take as a result.

Deliverable

A screenshot or notes showing regulatory settings for two regions and a two‑sentence summary of observed channel or power differences.
