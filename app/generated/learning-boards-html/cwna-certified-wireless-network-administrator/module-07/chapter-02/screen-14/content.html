Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Troubleshooting DFS

Diagnose and mitigate DFS interruptions

Follow a log-driven process to confirm radar detection and make measured mitigation choices.

Step 1: Check logs

Look for radar detection and channel switch entries with timestamps and affected channels.

Step 2: Short mitigation

Temporarily move critical SSIDs to reliable non‑DFS channels while you plan a long‑term fix.

Step 3: Long term

Perform a site survey and adjust channel plan to reduce DFS exposure while maintaining capacity.

Practical note

Communicate with stakeholders about temporary interruptions and document any temporary channel restrictions you apply.
