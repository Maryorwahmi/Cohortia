Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › 2.4 GHz Band
Characteristics

Range vs Congestion

  • Range and wall penetration are superior to higher bands.
  • Typically only three non‑overlapping 20 MHz channels: 1, 6, 11.
  • Shared with many non‑Wi‑Fi devices causing non‑Wi‑Fi interference.
When to use

Provide broad coverage for legacy devices or low bandwidth connectivity where capacity is not the primary concern.

Concrete deployment scenario

A warehouse uses 2.4 GHz APs to ensure devices across large open areas remain connected through shelving and equipment, accepting lower per‑user throughput in exchange for coverage.

Common mistake

Overusing 2.4 GHz for high capacity needs. When many users demand throughput, 5 GHz or 6 GHz are better choices.

Key Takeaway

Use 2.4 GHz for reach and legacy support; avoid expecting it to provide modern multi‑user capacity.
