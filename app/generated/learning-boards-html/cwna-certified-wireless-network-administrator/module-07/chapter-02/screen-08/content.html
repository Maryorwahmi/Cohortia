Module 7 › Chapter 7.2 › Dynamic Frequency Selection
Process

DFS: listen, detect, vacate, avoid

1. Channel availability check
AP listens for existing radar signatures before transmitting on a DFS channel.
2. Continuous monitoring
After starting, the AP must keep listening for radar and other primary users.
3. Channel vacate and non‑occupancy
If radar is detected, the AP ceases transmissions and avoids the channel for the regulatory non‑occupancy period.
Concrete scenario

Near an airport, periodic radar signals cause APs to detect and vacate DFS channels. Clients may see brief reconnections as the network moves to a clear channel.

Administrator pitfall

Avoiding DFS channels entirely limits usable spectrum and can worsen congestion on remaining channels.

Key Takeaway

Include DFS channels in your plan and design for occasional channel moves rather than avoiding them completely.
