Activity Guide

Build your quarter-by-quarter plan

Create concrete checkpoints and deliverables so progress is visible and repeatable.

Quarter 1
Study foundation

Complete the official CWSP study guide and assemble lab tools.

Quarter 3
Protocol mastery

Practice packet analysis and timed practice exams.

Quarter 2
Hands-on labs

Run an 802.1X lab with RADIUS and reproduce common troubleshooting scenarios.

Quarter 4
Consolidate & exam

Review weak areas, repeat lab tasks, and schedule the exam.

Deliverables
  • Lab report documenting 802.1X setup and test results
  • Packet capture analysis showing EAP exchange diagnosis
  • Practice exam score improvement log
Resources
Suggested resource types
  • Official CWNP study guide and practice tests
  • Vendor documentation or cloud guides for managed Wi‑Fi
  • Community forums and recorded conference sessions
Misconception
Reading the guide without repeating lab tasks will not produce practical troubleshooting skill. Practice and documentation matter more than reading alone.
Key Takeaway

Turn study time into demonstrable outputs: reports, captures, and repeatable lab steps.
