Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Developing a PD Plan
Step 01 — Define Goal
Choose a role or certification target.

Example: aim for CWSP within 12 months to specialize in wireless security.

Step 02 — Map Milestones
  • Study guide completion
  • Two lab projects (802.1X lab, packet capture practice)
  • Exam registration and practice tests
Accountability

Share the plan with a mentor or study buddy and schedule regular check-ins.

Key Takeaway

A small set of measurable milestones keeps learning steady and relevant.

Process Flow

Turn goals into steps and checkpoints

A repeatable cycle: choose → learn → practice → assess → adjust.

Estimated effort: 3–8 hours/week
Plan Element
Resource Mix

Combine official CWNP guides, vendor docs, labs, and community resources for depth and breadth.

Timing
Milestone Cadence

Use 8–12 week blocks for study segments and 2–4 week blocks for lab practice and review.

Concrete Example
Sample 6-month sprint
  • Months 1–2: CWSP study guide
  • Months 3–4: 802.1X lab + packet captures
  • Months 5–6: practice tests & exam
Common Mistake

Packing too many outcomes into one month leads to burnout. Space study and labs for retention.
