Module 7 › Chapter 7.8 › Certification Specializations
CWSP
Focus on advanced WLAN security, 802.1X/EAP, WIPS, and intrusion prevention techniques.
CWDP
Centers on WLAN design: capacity planning, RF propagation, and multi-vendor deployments.
CWAP
Focus on deep protocol analysis and packet-level troubleshooting for complex WLAN issues.
Summary

Security, design, and analysis are distinct but complementary specializations; pick the path that matches the problems you most enjoy solving.
