Definition

CWNP Program: Foundation and Pathway

A vendor-neutral certification ladder that starts with CWNA and branches into specialized tracks and an expert-level capstone.

CWNA — Foundation
Validates RF fundamentals, 802.11 standards, antenna theory, site surveying, and basic security knowledge.
How to use this pathway
  • Pick a specialization that solves a recurring problem in your role.
  • Pair study with labs that recreate real troubleshooting or design scenarios.
Key Takeaway

Use CWNA as baseline knowledge and choose the next certification to close a specific skill gap you face on the job.

Pathway flow
CWSP
Advanced WLAN security, 802.1X, intrusion prevention, and rogue detection — ideal for security-focused roles.
CWDP
Advanced design: capacity planning, RF propagation, and multi-vendor architecture for high-density sites.
CWAP
Protocol analysis, packet captures, and deep MAC-layer troubleshooting skills.
CWNE
Expert capstone that requires mastery of all domains and proven real-world projects.
Concrete example: after CWNA, choose CWSP if you’re responsible for compliance and incident response; pair study with a lab implementing 802.1X and testing rogue AP detection.
