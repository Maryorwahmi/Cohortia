Definition Physical Diagram & Site Survey
Where devices and signals live
Map placement and measure performance.
Physical diagram checklist
  • AP locations with labels and heights.
  • Antenna types and orientations.
  • Nearby metallic objects, pillars, and major obstructions.
  • Cable runs and closet locations with switch ports noted.
Site survey essentials
Include heat maps, RSSI samples, SNR readings, channel utilization, and noted interference sources. Keep raw data and annotated conclusions.
Concrete example
A survey captured high noise near a new coffee machine and showed required AP power adjustments. When a problem recurred months later, the historical heat map pointed to an increase in noise from a nearby tenant's device.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Keep dated survey artifacts and physical diagrams together so field teams can validate coverage and trace physical changes over time.
