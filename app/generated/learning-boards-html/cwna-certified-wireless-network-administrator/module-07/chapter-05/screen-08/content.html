Process Flow Site Surveys & Baselines
Measure first, change later
Baseline data prevents guesswork and validates changes.
Report contents
  • Heat maps with date and firmware versions.
  • Sampled RSSI and SNR values across key locations.
  • Channel usage and adjacent-channel interference readings.
  • Identified interference sources and remediation notes.
Archive raw survey files and attach them to the site record so changes are reproducible and verifiable.
Concrete scenario
After adding a temporary partition, Wi‑Fi throughput in a corridor dropped. The survey baseline showed the corridor previously had strong SNR; the new survey confirmed the partition's impact and guided AP repositioning.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Keep dated survey artifacts to validate and quantify changes in RF performance over time.
