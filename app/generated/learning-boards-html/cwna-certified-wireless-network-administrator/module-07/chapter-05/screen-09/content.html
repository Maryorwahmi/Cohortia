Core Concept Inventory & Asset Tracking
Know what you have and where it lives
Link inventory to configs and floor plans.
Inventory fields
  • Device model and serial number.
  • Purchase date, warranty expiry, and vendor details.
  • Current physical location and owner.
  • Linked configuration record and floor plan reference.
Operational example
A failed AP was replaced in under 30 minutes because the inventory showed the closet, spare part number, and warranty status. The technician scanned a QR tag to confirm the replacement matched the recorded model.
Key takeaway
Key Takeaway

Use consistent fields and link inventory items to other records so replacements are fast and correct.
