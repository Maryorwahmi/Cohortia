Core Concept Configuration Records
The single source of truth for device state
Capture settings required to rebuild or audit devices.
Minimal configuration record
  • Hostname, model, serial number, management IP.
  • SSID names and security (WPA2/WPA3, 802.1X parameters).
  • VLAN assignment per SSID and DHCP scope.
  • Channel plan, transmit power, regulatory domain.
  • Firmware version and change history notes.
  • Any custom scripts and their location.
Operational example
During a controller upgrade, a device failed to rejoin because a manual change to its regulatory domain was undocumented. With good config records you would have detected and reversed that change faster.
Check your process
Use a version-controlled repository or a device-management platform to track configurations and store a change log with ticket references.
