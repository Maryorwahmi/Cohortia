Comparison WLAN Policy Types
Separate documents, aligned outcomes
Map policy to enforcement and ownership.
Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
Defines permitted user activity and ensures users understand responsibilities. Keep it concise and enforceable.
Security Policy
Specifies encryption, authentication, and posture requirements. Tie it to technical controls like WPA3 or 802.1X configuration settings.
Access Control Policy
Maps users and groups to SSIDs and VLANs, separating guest and corporate traffic and limiting lateral movement.
Device Onboarding
Describes requirements and steps for BYOD, such as antivirus, device management or enrollment, and authentication method used.
Practical tip
Assign a single owner for each policy, include a review cadence, and map each policy requirement to a technical control and enforcement process.
