WLAN Documentation and Policy Best Practices
Build records and rules that keep your wireless network secure, auditable, and recoverable. This lesson shows the artifacts and policies every administrator should produce.

  • Policy focus: acceptable use, security, access control, device onboarding, incident response, and change management.

Documentation shortens outages and removes single points of failure.
