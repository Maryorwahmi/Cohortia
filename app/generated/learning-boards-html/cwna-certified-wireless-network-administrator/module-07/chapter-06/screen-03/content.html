Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Skills & Assessment

Demonstrable skills

Scope & Authorization
Write a rules of engagement document that lists targets, time windows, and approval signatures.
Evidence Collection
Capture handshakes, logs, and packet traces; timestamp and preserve integrity for reporting.
Impact Control
Decide when passive scanning is required versus when active testing is safe.
Reporting
Convert findings into prioritized remediation with clear technical steps and risk scores.

Assessment prompt

Simulate passive reconnaissance, record SSIDs and channels, and write a short findings note identifying one high-priority risk.

Key Takeaway

Skillful tests pair technical ability with clear approvals and responsible reporting.
