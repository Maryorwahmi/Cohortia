Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Key Concepts
Permission & Intent
Always obtain written authorization and act to improve security.
Right tools for the job
Kismet, Aircrack-ng, Wireshark, Kali—match tool to task and verify hardware support.
Plan & Report
Scope, impact controls, evidence, and prioritized remediation make tests valuable.
Summary takeaway

Ethical wireless pentesting combines legal authorization, appropriate tools, careful planning, and clear reporting to reduce real-world risk. Make tests repeatable and remediation-focused.
