Permission
Obtain written authorization, define scope and emergency stop procedures.
Discover
Start passive reconnaissance, then move to controlled active tests if allowed.
Remediate
Provide prioritized, verifiable remediation steps and follow-up verification scans.
Final takeaway

Ethical wireless pentesting protects users by finding and fixing real issues. Keep legal and safety practices at the center of every assessment.
