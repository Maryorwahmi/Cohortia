Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Learning Objectives

Learning Objectives

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate ethical, penetration, and malicious hacking

Explain how permission, intent, and authorization change a test from illegal to legitimate. You will be able to give an example of each case and why authorization matters.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify common tools and techniques

Map tools like Aircrack-ng, Kismet, Wireshark, and Kali to tasks such as capture, sniffing, analysis, and exploitation. Provide a short scenario where each tool is the right choice.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Understand legal and ethical considerations

Describe the required written permission, scope, start and stop times, and privacy safeguards. Recognize the legal risks of unauthorized testing and how to avoid them.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Explain how pentesting improves WLAN security

Turn test findings into prioritized remediation steps. You will be able to explain why a weak PSK or rogue AP is a higher priority than a legacy protocol in many environments.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
