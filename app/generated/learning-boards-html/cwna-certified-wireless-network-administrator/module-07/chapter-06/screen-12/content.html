What to test in enterprise auth

  • RADIUS policies and attribute checks
  • Certificate validation and chain checks
  • Fallback mechanisms that allow weaker auth
Example

A RADIUS setup allowed machine auth without requiring client certs. An unauthorized device presented expected attributes and gained access. Fix: require mutual certificate checks and tighten policies.

Testing precautions

Coordinate with identity and network teams, test in lab or maintenance windows, and monitor authentication services to avoid unintended lockouts.

Reporting

Explain the misconfiguration, exploit method, business impact, and corrective changes such as policy adjustments or certificate renewals.

Key Takeaway

Enterprise auth tests must be deliberate, coordinated, and focused on configuration fixes rather than exploitation.
