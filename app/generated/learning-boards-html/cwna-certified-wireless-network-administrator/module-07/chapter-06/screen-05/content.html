Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Planning a Pentest
Process

Plan: scope → rules → control

Scope
List SSIDs, APs, locations, and devices that are in and out of scope.
Rules of engagement
Allowed tools, permitted active techniques, test windows, and signatures.
Impact controls
Emergency stop, rollback plans, and communication channels for incidents.
Concrete scenario

You plan a guest SSID assessment. The scope allows passive scans and targeted handshake captures only during after-hours. Deauthentication is permitted in a controlled window. You prepare an emergency contact and a rollback procedure to restore AP configurations if needed.

Checklist
  • Signed authorization
  • Detailed scope and exclusions
  • Allowed techniques and time windows
  • Emergency stop and contacts
  • Evidence handling and reporting format
Pro Tip

Define technical and business owners for each asset in scope to speed remediation.
