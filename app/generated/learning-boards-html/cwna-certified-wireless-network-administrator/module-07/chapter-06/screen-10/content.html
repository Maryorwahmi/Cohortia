Module 7 › Chapter 7.6 › Rogue APs & Evil Twin

Rogue APs

Unauthorized devices connected to the network or broadcasting within the facility. They can be accidental or malicious and may bridge traffic to internal systems.

How they appear
  • SSIDs not in asset inventory
  • AP MACs on unexpected switch ports
  • Unknown DHCP leases tied to APs

Evil Twin

An AP that mimics a legitimate SSID to capture credentials or traffic. Clients that auto-join are particularly vulnerable.

Detection hints
  • Multiple APs advertising the same SSID with different BSSIDs
  • APs on unexpected channels or signal sources
  • Clients associating to weaker security settings
Scenario

A pentest identifies a rogue AP plugged into a meeting room switch. It broadcasts the corporate SSID but connects to a home router. The immediate remediation is to remove the device and audit switch port security, then educate staff about unauthorized devices.
