Definition

What makes a test ethical?

Permission and intent together change identical technical actions from illegal to lawful. Written scope, objectives, and evidence rules are required.

Permission
Letter of authorization, defined scope, and approved time windows.
Intent
Improve security and protect privacy; no exploitation for gain.
Concrete example

A contracted pentest runs a deauthentication to capture a handshake. The tester immediately hands over captures to the client and recommends stronger PSKs. That same deauth on an unapproved network is a criminal act.

Document scope
Preserve evidence
Common misconceptions
  • Tools alone make you ethical — false: legal agreements and intent are critical.
  • Passive scans need no permission — false: organizations may restrict any monitoring.
  • Successful exploit means security failure — not necessarily; it reveals a fixable gap.
Key Takeaway

Verify written authorization and purpose before any wireless test.
