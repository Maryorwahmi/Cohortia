Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Competency Milestones
How you will demonstrate competency
Actionable checks you can do after studying this chapter.
01 Regulator ID
Produce a short list of global and national bodies and state their primary duties in one paragraph each.
02 IEEE Standards
Explain how 802.11 amendments define PHY and MAC behaviors and why vendors reference them.
03 Certification
Identify certification programs and explain the practical benefits of buying certified equipment.
Example deliverable
A two‑paragraph report comparing allowable EIRP and DFS channels between two regions, and a recommendation for equipment selection and power settings.
Common mistake to avoid
Assuming a device legal in one region is automatically legal in another; always verify local rules before deployment.
