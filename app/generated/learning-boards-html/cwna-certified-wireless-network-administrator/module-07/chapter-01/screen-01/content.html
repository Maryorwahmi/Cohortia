Device journey — simplified
Imagine a new Wi‑Fi chip. The ITU confirms the band exists internationally. The national regulator sets allowed power and channel rules. The IEEE defines how frames, rates, and channels operate. The Wi‑Fi Alliance certifies that the chip interoperates and meets security tests. Each step reduces risk and increases predictability for administrators.
