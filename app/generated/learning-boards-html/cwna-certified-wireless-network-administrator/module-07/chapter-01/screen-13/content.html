Assessment and reflection
Sample questions and practical checks to evaluate your learning.
Sample question 1
If an outdoor 5 GHz AP is operated at 36 dBm in a European country, which regulator's rules are likely violated and why?
Sample question 2
What is the primary functional difference between IEEE and the Wi‑Fi Alliance?
Reflection prompt
How would a regulatory change in your region impact your current WLAN design, power settings, and procurement choices?
Practical checklist
  • Document region-specific EIRP and channel rules.
  • Record device certification marks and supported standards.
  • Plan monitoring for unexpected transmissions and DFS events.
Key takeaway
Use scenario questions and checklists to move from knowledge to verified practice before wide deployment.
