How regulation shapes WLAN design
Translate rules into placement, power, antenna, and channel choices.
Power and coverage
EIRP limits constrain single‑AP coverage; design density must compensate when power ceilings are low.
Channels and DFS
Some channels mandate radar detection and avoidance, affecting channel availability and failover behavior.
Indoor vs outdoor
Regionally distinct outdoor rules may require different equipment and site approvals.
Antenna selection
Antenna gain multiplies transmitter power; check EIRP calculations to remain compliant.
Concrete tool
Create a design matrix mapping site, required coverage, allowed EIRP, allowed channels, and preferred antenna gain to standardize compliant designs across regions.
Common mistake
Designing coverage first and then trying to fit regulatory constraints; this leads to costly redesigns or regulatory violations.
Key takeaway
Bring regulation into the design phase and document power and channel decisions by jurisdiction to avoid surprises during deployment.
