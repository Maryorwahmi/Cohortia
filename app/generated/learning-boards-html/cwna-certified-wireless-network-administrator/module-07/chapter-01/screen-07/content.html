Wi‑Fi Alliance
Industry association that certifies products for interoperability and promotes Wi‑Fi technology.
What certification does
Runs test programs against IEEE standards and issues certification marks that indicate interoperability and certain security features.
Practical benefits
  • Reduces interoperability risk across vendors.
  • Provides assurance on security protocols and performance baselines.
  • Makes procurement decisions easier with standard marks.
Concrete example
Selecting devices marked as Wi‑Fi CERTIFIED 6 and WPA3 gives administrators a better chance that clients and APs will interoperate and meet security expectations.
Common misconception
Certification reduces risk but does not guarantee optimal network performance; real deployments still require design verification and testing.
Key takeaway
Use certification as a procurement filter and then validate performance in your environment before wide rollout.
