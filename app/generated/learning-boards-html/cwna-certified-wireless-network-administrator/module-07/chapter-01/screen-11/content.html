Interplay between ITU, Regulators, IEEE, and Wi‑Fi Alliance
Each organization contributes a different kind of authority or assurance for wireless systems.
ITU
International allocation and coordination of spectrum.
Regulators
Enforceable national rules for power, channels, and deployments.
IEEE
Technical standards that vendors implement for interoperability.
Wi‑Fi Alliance
Certification programs that validate interoperability and security profiles.
Practical rule
Consult ITU for band context, regulators for legal limits, IEEE for technical behavior, and Wi‑Fi Alliance for interoperability assurances.
Concrete example
A product may be IEEE‑compliant and Wi‑Fi Alliance certified, but still need configuration to meet a national regulator's power limits before deployment.
Check
For each procurement risk, ask which organization's guidance or assurance answers it: legal, technical, or interoperability?
