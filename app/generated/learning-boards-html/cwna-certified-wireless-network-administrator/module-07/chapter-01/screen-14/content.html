Key Concepts — Part 1
ITU
Allocates spectrum globally and issues recommendations that guide national rules.
FCC / ETSI
Regional regulators that set enforceable power, channel, and deployment rules.
IEEE 802.11
Defines the technical rules for PHY and MAC that vendors implement.
Bottom line
Treat these organizations as complementary: allocation, regulation, and standards together determine legal and operational constraints for WLANs.
