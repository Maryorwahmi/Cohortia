Activity: Research local regulations
Collect EIRP limits, DFS channel lists, and outdoor restrictions for the US (FCC) and Germany/Europe (ETSI).
Task steps
  1. Find the published EIRP limits for indoor 5 GHz devices in each region.
  2. Identify two 5 GHz channels that require DFS in both regions.
  3. Note any special outdoor deployment rules that would affect AP placement.
Deliverable
A two to three paragraph report summarizing differences, with clear recommendations for equipment selection and configuration profiles.
Practical tip
Record findings in a project checklist and flag any channels and power levels that require different profiles per country.
Common mistake
Assuming vendor defaults are compliant across all regions; verify and adapt defaults to fit local rules before deployment.
Key takeaway
Local research is essential: translate public rules into configuration checklists and procurement filters.
