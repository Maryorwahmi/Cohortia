Device journey: stepwise
Six checkpoints that convert a band and a design into a deployable product.
1. ITU: Spectrum allocation
International allocation of bands that informs national policy.
2. National regulator
Defines local EIRP, channelization, and DFS obligations.
3. IEEE standards
Technical specifications for PHY and MAC that vendors implement.
4. Vendor implementation
Hardware and firmware built against standards and regulatory constraints.
5. Wi‑Fi Alliance certification
Interoperability and security testing that issues certification marks.
6. Deployment
Configure devices to meet local rules and verify performance in the field.
Concrete scenario
A vendor builds a device to an IEEE amendment, tests for Wi‑Fi Alliance certification, but the deployment team must still set the local EIRP and verify DFS behavior in the target country to be compliant.
Misconception check
Certification does not remove the need to configure devices to meet local regulatory limits.
Key takeaway
Treat the lifecycle as checkpoints where documentation and tests reduce legal and operational risk before deployment.
