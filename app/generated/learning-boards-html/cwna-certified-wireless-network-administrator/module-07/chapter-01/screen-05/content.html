Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › FCC vs ETSI
Federal Communications Commission (FCC)
United States regulator that sets enforceable rules for radio, including Wi‑Fi power and channel restrictions.
Regulatory scope
Sets EIRP limits, channel use, and DFS requirements in specified bands; enforces rules through fines and other actions.
Practical checklist
  • Document maximum EIRP for target band and use case.
  • Identify channels that trigger DFS and plan for radar avoidance.
  • Check outdoor vs indoor distinctions before outdoor installs.
European Telecommunications Standards Institute (ETSI)
Sets European rules informed by regional policy; requirements are comparable but can differ in specifics from the FCC.
Regulatory scope
Defines allowable bands, power limits, and requirements such as DFS; national authorities implement ETSI guidance.
Practical checklist
  • Confirm national limits derived from ETSI recommendations.
  • Record any lower EIRP ceilings for outdoor installations.
  • Plan for regional DFS channel requirements and testing.
Concrete example
When a company deploys APs across the US and Europe, separate power and channel profiles are required to remain compliant with FCC and ETSI rules.
Common mistake
Assuming equipment default settings meet local rules without auditing power and DFS settings for the target region.
Key takeaway
Always collect regional EIRP and DFS requirements early in the design process and maintain separate configuration profiles when necessary.
