Key Concepts — Part 2
EIRP
Equivalent Isotropically Radiated Power is the regulated metric; calculate it when choosing power and antennas.
DFS
Channels that require radar sensing and avoidance; include DFS in channel planning and testing.
Certification
Look for Wi‑Fi Alliance marks for interoperability and security; still validate configurability for local rules.
Final reflection
Add EIRP checks, DFS channel lists, and required certification marks to your procurement checklist to ensure devices are both compliant and interoperable in the target regions.
