Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Troubleshooting with Management Frames
Checklist for captures
  • Confirm Beacons exist and check their interval and IEs.
  • Compare Probe Requests and Responses for matching SSIDs and capability fields.
  • Inspect Authentication and Association status codes for explicit rejections.
  • Look for repeated deauth/disassoc frames and note their source MACs and reason codes.
Example
A client that keeps sending Probe Requests but never associates may be probing the wrong channel or the AP may be configured with incompatible rates or security flags.
Attack indicators
  • Bursts of deauthentication coming from one non-AP MAC.
  • Repeated association failures followed by immediate deauths.
  • Probe requests targeting many SSIDs in short timeframes.
Key Takeaway

Follow the sequence of management frames and examine IEs and reason/status codes to find the earliest indicator of misconfiguration or attack.
