Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Beacon Frame
Periodic announcement with IEs such as SSID, rates, channel, and security.
Probe Req / Resp
Active discovery pair; directed probes name an SSID, broadcast probes solicit any APs.
Authentication
MAC-layer confirmation (typically Open System) that allows a client to proceed to association.
Association & AID
Association establishes the logical link; AID tracks the station for buffering and power save.
Disassociation
Graceful termination of association; authentication state may remain intact.
Deauthentication
Severs MAC-layer authentication and association; often targeted in spoofing attacks if unprotected.
Summary Takeaway
Use management frame captures to read IEs and reason codes. Enable protection where supported and use consistent AP configurations to reduce failures.
