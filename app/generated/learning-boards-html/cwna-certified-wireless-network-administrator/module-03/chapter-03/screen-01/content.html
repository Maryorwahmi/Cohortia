802.11 Management Frames
Control-plane messages that enable discovery, connection setup, roaming, and disconnection.
Why this matters

These frames carry the metadata and control required before data can flow. Troubleshooting and security analysis rely on understanding their contents and timing.

What you'll learn
Identify frame types, scan methods, authentication and association, roaming, and security implications.

Note: Management frames are unencrypted unless management frame protection is used. Understanding them is the first step in troubleshooting and securing a WLAN.
