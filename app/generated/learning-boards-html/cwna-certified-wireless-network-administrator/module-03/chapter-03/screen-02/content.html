How these objectives map to practice
  • Identify a problematic AP by reading Beacon IEs and frequency of management frames.
  • Use active scanning to find hidden SSIDs or confirm probe behavior on clients.
  • Inspect association responses to locate AID assignments and capability mismatches.
  • Recognize suspicious bursts of deauthentication frames as a possible attack vector.

Key Takeaway

These objectives focus on recognition, explanation, hands-on capture, and analysis of management frames for troubleshooting and security.
