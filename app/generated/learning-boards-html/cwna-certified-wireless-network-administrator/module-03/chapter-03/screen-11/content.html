Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Management Frame Protection
Why management frames are risky
Because they are unencrypted by default, an observer can read their contents and an attacker can forge frames to cause disruptions.
Common attack: forged deauthentication frames used to drop clients repeatedly.
Mitigation

Enable management frame protection (802.11w) where supported, and combine with monitoring to detect anomalous patterns.

Example observation
In a protected network, capture traces show protected management frames with indicators in IEs. In unprotected networks deauth spikes are visible and easier to spoof.
Practical reminder
MFP reduces risk but requires coordinated support on both APs and clients to be effective across an ESS.
