Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Operational Best Practices
Avoid SSID hiding
Hiding SSIDs causes more problems than security; active probes can still reveal the network.
Enable MFP carefully
Enable management frame protection after validating client compatibility to reduce spoofing risk.
Consistent AP configs
Match security IEs, supported rates, and QoS settings across APs to ease roaming and association.
Channel planning
Reduce overlapping interference and unnecessary probe traffic with deliberate channel assignment.
Watch deauth patterns
Frequent deauths may indicate a misconfigured AP or an attack; correlate sources and reason codes in captures.
Key Takeaway

Operational consistency and enabling protection where supported will reduce connection failures and security exposure tied to management frames.
