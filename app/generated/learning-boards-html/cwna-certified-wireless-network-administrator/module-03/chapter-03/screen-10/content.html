Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Disassoc vs Deauth
Disassociation
Terminates association but preserves MAC authentication. Useful for graceful departures or inactivity timeouts.
Client may reassociate without redoing authentication if the AP allows.
Key Takeaway

Disassociation is a polite leave; it ends the logical connection without clearing authentication state.

Deauthentication
Severs MAC layer authentication and association. Requires full reauthentication and reassociation to reconnect. Frequently used administratively or in attacks.
Security note: unprotected deauth frames can be forged to launch denial-of-service attacks.
Diagnostic example
Repeated deauth frames from a non-AP MAC suggest spoofing. Check reason codes and correlate timing with client disconnects.
