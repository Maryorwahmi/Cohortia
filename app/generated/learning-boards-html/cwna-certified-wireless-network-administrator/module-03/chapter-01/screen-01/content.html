In this lesson
What the MAC does

Encapsulates MSDU into MPDU, enforces medium access rules, and implements retransmission and sequencing.

Frame categories

Management, Control, and Data frames each play distinct roles in discovery, control, and payload delivery.

Header anatomy

Frame Control, Duration/ID, Addresses, Sequence, Body, and FCS form the standard MAC header and trailer.

Why it matters

Reading these fields helps you troubleshoot association, collisions, hidden nodes, and retransmissions.

MAC frames are the units Wi‑Fi uses to carry payloads and control medium access; understanding header fields unlocks capture analysis and troubleshooting.
