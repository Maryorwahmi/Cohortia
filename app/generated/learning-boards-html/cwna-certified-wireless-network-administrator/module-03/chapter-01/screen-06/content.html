Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Frame Categories
Management
Discovery & Association

Used to find networks, exchange capabilities, authenticate clients, and maintain association state between clients and APs.

Examples: Beacon, Probe Request, Probe Response, Authentication, Association Request/Response.
Control
Medium access helpers

Enable reliable delivery and coordinate access to the medium. Small and timing-critical.

Examples: RTS, CTS, ACK, Block Ack.
Data
User payload transport

Carry MSDUs from higher layers. May include QoS fields and can be fragmented or aggregated by the MAC.

Examples: Data, QoS Data, Null Function frames.
Concrete scenario

A client probes for networks with a Probe Request (management). An AP replies with a Probe Response. After association, the client sends data frames and the AP responds with ACK control frames to confirm receipt.

Misconception check

Control frames do not carry large payloads, but they are required for reliability. Missing or blocked control frames cause retransmissions and poor performance.

Key Takeaway

Classify captured packets by category to set your expectations about content and behavior; type determines what to inspect and why.
