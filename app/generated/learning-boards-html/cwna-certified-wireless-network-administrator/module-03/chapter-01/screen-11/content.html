Frame Body Roles
MSDU content or management information elements
Management frames embed Information Elements (IEs) in the frame body. IEs are TLV-style tags that convey the SSID, supported rates, channel information, RSN or security capabilities, HT/VHT capabilities, and vendor-specific data. Beacon frames periodically advertise these IEs so clients can discover and interpret what the AP supports. Probe Responses mirror that information on demand. Data frames instead carry the upper-layer packet as the MSDU within the frame body, often preceded by an LLC header that indicates the encapsulated protocol.
SSID IE
Network name advertised in Beacon/Probe.
Supported Rates
Rates the AP and clients can use.
RSN IE
Security suite and cipher suites advertised.
Concrete Wireshark Tip
Select a Beacon or Probe Response and expand the 802.11 management body. Look for SSID, Supported Rates, DS Parameter Set, and RSN. These tell you what the AP advertises and help diagnose association or security mismatches.
Key Takeaway

Management IEs are the authoritative list of an AP’s advertised capabilities. Compare capture IEs with AP configuration when troubleshooting connections.
