Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Power Save Poll (PS‑Poll)
PS‑Poll explained
Client wakes, sends PS‑Poll, AP responds with buffered frames or indicator.
  • • Designed for battery conservation by letting clients sleep between polls.
  • • AP buffers unicast frames when the client is in power‑save state.
  • • Misconfiguration of buffer timers can cause delayed delivery.
Practical note
When troubleshooting delayed notifications, inspect PS‑Poll sequences and AP buffering behavior in captures.
Concrete example
A tablet sleeps with a 5‑second wake interval. Email arrives at the AP while the tablet is sleeping; the AP buffers it. When the tablet PS‑Polls, the AP sends the buffered email frame, the tablet acknowledges, and returns to sleep if no more data is indicated.
Misconception
Assuming PS‑Poll eliminates latency. It reduces power use but introduces polling latency; tuning wake intervals is the balancing act.
