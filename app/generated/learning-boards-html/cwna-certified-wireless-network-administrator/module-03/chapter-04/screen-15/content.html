Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › What to Remember
Inspect control frames
RTS/CTS, ACKs, PS‑Poll reveal medium behavior and buffering issues.
Tune thresholds by evidence
Adjust RTS Threshold when captures show repeated large‑frame retransmits or hidden‑node patterns.
Use aggregation wisely
Block ACK and aggregation reduce overhead on fast links; ensure negotiation succeeds in captures.
Closing takeaway
Control frames are short but powerful: read them first when diagnosing wireless medium problems and tune selectively based on measurable behavior.
