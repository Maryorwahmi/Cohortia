Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Diagnosing Retransmissions
Checklist
  • • Confirm missing ACKs for retransmitted data frames.
  • • Check RSSI and SNR at the receiver to rule out weak signal.
  • • Look for frequent RTS/CTS exchanges indicating hidden nodes or large-frame transfers.
  • • Inspect Block ACK negotiations and timers in high-throughput captures.
Common-mistake: immediately changing transmit power or enabling RTS globally without capture evidence — this can worsen performance.
Concrete example
Sequence: data frame → no ACK → retransmit. If receiver's RSSI is low, moving the client closer or improving coverage reduces retries. If ACKs are missing despite good RSSI, check for interference sources or hidden nodes and enable RTS selectively.
Key Takeaway: Control frames reveal the failure point — inspect them first to guide targeted fixes rather than broad changes.
