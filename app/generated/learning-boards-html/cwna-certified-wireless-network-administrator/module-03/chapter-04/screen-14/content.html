Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
RTS/CTS
Handshake that reserves the medium and sets NAV to avoid collisions.
ACK / Block ACK
ACK confirms single frames; Block ACK aggregates acknowledgements for efficiency.
PS‑Poll
Power‑save clients poll the AP for buffered frames to conserve battery life.
Summary
Reserve, Confirm, Save — small control frames steer medium access and reliability across Wi‑Fi networks.
