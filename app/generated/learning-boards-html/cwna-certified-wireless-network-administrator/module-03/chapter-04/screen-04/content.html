Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › What are Control Frames?
Control frames — the coordinator
Short messages that manage who transmits and confirm reception.
  • • They are high priority and occupy minimal airtime to reduce interference with data traffic.
  • • Their roles include medium reservation, acknowledgements, power-save polling, and control of contention-free periods.
  • • They include RTS, CTS, ACK, PS-Poll, CF-End, and Block ACK among others.
Analogy: traffic signals that prevent collisions on a shared road
Key Takeaway: Without control frames, MAC reliability and coordinated access collapse into higher retransmissions and wasted airtime.
Quick comparison
Management: associations, beacons
Data: payloads and higher layer traffic
Common-mistake
Treating control frames as optional. They are essential for timing, and misconfiguring them (for example disabling RTS/CTS or mishandling buffering for power save) degrades reliability.
