RTS Threshold explained
Threshold sets the minimum frame size for which RTS/CTS will be used.
• Low threshold: more RTS/CTS handshakes, fewer collisions for large frames, increased short‑frame overhead.
• High threshold: fewer handshakes, more collision risk when large frames and hidden nodes exist.
Practical rule
Tune based on capture evidence: repeated large‑frame retransmits or RTS frames indicate lowering the threshold may help.
Scenario comparison
Dense office: many clients and hidden nodes — lower threshold helps avoid costly collisions.
Home network: light traffic and small frames — raise threshold to reduce unnecessary handshakes.
Check your understanding
If captures show many retransmissions for large frames and few RTS frames, lowering the threshold could reduce wasted airtime due to retransmits.
