PCF and CF‑End
PCF grants contention‑free access via a point coordinator; CF‑End ends that period.
Most modern deployments use DCF. PCF and CF‑End are useful to understand historically and when reading legacy captures, but DCF with control frames provides the practical coordination for today's networks.
Why it matters
Knowing CF‑End helps you interpret captures that include legacy coordination, and clarifies why NAV and control frames are central to most troubleshooting.
Key Takeaway
Focus troubleshooting on DCF, RTS/CTS, ACK, and PS‑Poll; treat CF‑End as a legacy artifact when encountered.
