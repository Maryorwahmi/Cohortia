Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Hidden Node Problem
Hidden nodes: description
Two nodes that cannot hear each other but can reach a common AP cause collisions at that AP unless coordinated.
Why physical CS fails
Physical carrier sense relies on hearing transmissions. If a station is out of range, it cannot see the other's transmission and will not defer.
How CTS helps
AP's CTS is heard by both sides and carries a duration that causes the hidden node to set NAV and wait.
Scenario card
A and B are hidden from each other but both see the AP. A sends RTS; AP responds with CTS; B hears CTS and defers. If CTS is lost, A retries and collision risk remains.
Common-mistake
Assuming RTS/CTS will fix all collisions. It helps where the AP is central to both nodes, but it cannot prevent collisions caused by interference or when the AP itself cannot reach all nodes reliably.
Check your understanding: In a capture, look for RTS without corresponding CTS and repeated retransmits — that indicates a failed handshake or lost CTS.
