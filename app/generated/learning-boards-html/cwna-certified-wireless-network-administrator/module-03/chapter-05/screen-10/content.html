Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Misclassification
Problem

Why QoS seems to fail

  • Client or app doesn't mark traffic, so TID is missing or wrong.
  • AP and client have incompatible WMM settings or one lacks support.
  • Excessive high-priority traffic overloads available airtime.
Diagnostic steps
1. Confirm WMM enabled on AP and client. 2. Capture and read QoS Control TID values. 3. Measure channel utilization and queue saturation.
Remedy
If misclassification is found, correct application or client markings, update drivers, or apply QoS policies at the wired edge. If overload persists, plan capacity increases.
Key Takeaway

QoS depends on correct marking and sufficient channel capacity; inspect the QoS Control TID to confirm classification and avoid assuming policy alone fixes congestion.
