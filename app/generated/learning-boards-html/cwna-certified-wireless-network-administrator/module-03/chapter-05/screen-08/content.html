Concept

Transmission Opportunity explained

A TXOP is a bounded time window granted to a station to transmit multiple frames after winning contention, reducing per-frame overhead.

Why it helps
By sending several small packets back-to-back, the station avoids repeated AIFS and backoff, improving latency and throughput for streams like voice.
Concrete capture sign
Look for a burst of QoS Data frames from the same source with few or no intervening frames; that pattern commonly indicates a TXOP in use.
Misconception
TXOP does not increase total channel bandwidth — it simply reduces contention overhead for the station holding the opportunity.
Key Takeaway

TXOP bundles transmissions after a successful contention win, improving efficiency for prioritized traffic without creating new capacity.
