Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Assessment

Scenario

Choppy VoIP, normal web traffic. WMM enabled on devices. Capture and reason to a root cause.

  • If voice packets show Best Effort TIDs → misclassification at the client or app.
  • If voice shows AC_VO but channel is saturated → capacity or too many high-priority streams.
  • Observe TXOP bursts to confirm the station is obtaining opportunities to reduce overhead.
Guided reasoning
Collect evidence, then map each finding to remedy: re-marking, driver updates, capacity increases, or EDCA tuning.
Key Takeaway

Use captures to decide between misclassification and capacity limits; each requires a different corrective action.
