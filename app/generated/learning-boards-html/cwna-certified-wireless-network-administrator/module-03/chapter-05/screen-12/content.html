Module 4 › Chapter 3.5 › Lab Activity

Step-by-step

  1. Enable monitor mode and capture the AP channel.
  2. Generate mixed traffic: video call, web browsing, and a file download.
  3. Capture for 2–3 minutes and stop.
  4. Filter: wlan.fc.type_subtype == 0x28, inspect QoS Control and map TID→AC.
  5. Look for bursts from the same source to identify TXOP behavior.
Hints
Capture near the client or AP, and ensure your adapter supports monitor mode. If you cannot see bursts, try moving closer or disabling other heavy traffic.
Key Takeaway

Hands‑on capture and inspection are the most reliable ways to confirm QoS behavior and observe real TXOP usage.
