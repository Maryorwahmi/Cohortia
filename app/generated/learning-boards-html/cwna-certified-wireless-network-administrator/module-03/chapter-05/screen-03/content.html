Practical Objectives

Steps and checks you'll perform when diagnosing QoS on a live network.

Step
Capture QoS Data Frames

Use a monitor mode capture and filter for QoS frames to inspect QoS Control fields.

Step
Map TID to AC

Confirm that voice and video frames map to AC_VO or AC_VI respectively.

Step
Check WMM and Capacity

Verify WMM enabled on both AP and clients and ensure the channel has enough bandwidth.
