Architecture

End-to-end QoS planning

  • Estimate the number of concurrent high-priority streams and plan AP density and channel width accordingly.
  • Ensure marking at the application/host and preserve tags through switches and AP configuration.
  • Tune TXOP and per-AC parameters for the deployment’s latency vs throughput goals.
Example tradeoff
Longer TXOP helps video throughput but can delay other stations momentarily. Choose limits that favor the most critical application mix.
Misconception
AP-side settings alone cannot guarantee QoS; host marking and network capacity matter just as much.
Key Takeaway

Design QoS as an end‑to‑end set of choices: marking, AP queues, EDCA tuning, and capacity planning together determine perceived performance.
