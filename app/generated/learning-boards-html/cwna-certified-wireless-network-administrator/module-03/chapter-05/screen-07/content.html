Process

EDCA step-by-step

  1. Frame arrives and is assigned to an AC queue based on TID.
  2. The AC waits its specific AIFS before enabling backoff.
  3. The AC selects a random backoff from its CWmin–CWmax range.
  4. When the backoff reaches zero, the station transmits; TXOP allows bursts within a limit.
Practical note
Shorter AIFS and smaller CW reduce average wait and collision probability for high-priority ACs.

Key Takeaway

EDCA biases medium access by tuning AIFS and contention windows per AC; TXOP reduces overhead by bundling frames once access is won.
