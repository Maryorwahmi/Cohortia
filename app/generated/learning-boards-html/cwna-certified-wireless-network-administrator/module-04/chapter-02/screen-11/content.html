Definition
Passphrase strength and offline cracking
Key ideas:
  • Captured handshake enables offline PSK testing.
  • Entropy and length determine resistance to brute force.
  • Avoid dictionary words and short common phrases.
Concrete example
A 10‑character passphrase of common words is often cracked quickly. A 20‑character mixed passphrase raises the cost dramatically, making offline cracking infeasible for casual attackers.
Misconception
AES protects session confidentiality, but it does not stop an attacker who captured the handshake from testing passphrases offline; PSK strength is your defense.
Key Takeaway

Choose long, random passphrases and rotate them when exposure is suspected to reduce offline cracking risk.
