Process
PSK management workflow
  1. Generate a long, random passphrase and store it securely.
  2. Set router to WPA2‑PSK with AES‑CCMP; avoid TKIP or mixed modes.
  3. Document devices and schedule rotation or rotate when needed.
  4. After rotation, update all trusted devices and validate connectivity.
Concrete scenario
After a contractor leaves, rotate the PSK to prevent unauthorized access. Update each device with the new passphrase and confirm they reconnect with AES‑CCMP enabled.
Common mistake
Keeping default admin credentials or using short PSKs for convenience negates protocol-level protections.
Key Takeaway

Operational controls like secure storage and timely PSK rotation are essential complements to cryptographic protocols.
