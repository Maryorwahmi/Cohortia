Module 04 › Chapter 4.2 › What to Remember
Avoid WEP
WEP’s IV and integrity issues make it unusable for secure networks.
WPA was transitional
TKIP/MIC improved deployment security but did not equal AES‑CCMP strength.
PSK matters
Even with AES‑CCMP, a weak PSK enables offline attacks; manage and rotate passphrases.
Final takeaway

Choose WPA2‑PSK with AES‑CCMP and enforce long, random passphrases plus disciplined rotation to secure personal Wi‑Fi networks.
