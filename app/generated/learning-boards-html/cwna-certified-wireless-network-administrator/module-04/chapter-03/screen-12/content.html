Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Mistakes & Best Practices
Common mistakes
  • Not distributing trusted root CAs and disabling server validation.
  • Neglecting certificate expiry and revocation processes.
  • Mixing incompatible inner methods without testing clients.
Example failure
An expired RADIUS certificate causes mass authentication failures unless renewal is automated and scheduled.
Best practices
  • Automate certificate issuance and renewal via PKI tooling.
  • Deploy trusted root CAs and EAP profiles centrally to clients.
  • Monitor RADIUS logs and certificate status to detect issues early.
Key Takeaway

Operational discipline around certificates and client configuration is as important as choosing the right EAP type.
