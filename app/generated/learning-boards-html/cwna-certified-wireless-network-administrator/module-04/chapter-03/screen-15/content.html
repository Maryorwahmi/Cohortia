Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Quick Reference
Authenticator role
Relays EAP and enforces RADIUS decisions, but does not perform credential validation itself.
EAP choice
PEAP simplifies deployment without client certificates; EAP-TLS requires PKI but offers the strongest mutual authentication.
Server validation
Provision trusted root CAs and enable server certificate validation on clients to prevent credential harvesting.
Final takeaway

Design for trust: pick an EAP type that fits your operational capacity and make certificate trust and lifecycle automation a planning priority.
