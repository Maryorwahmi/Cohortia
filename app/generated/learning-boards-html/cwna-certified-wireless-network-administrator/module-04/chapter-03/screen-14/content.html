Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
802.1X
Port-based access control enabling centralized per-user authentication and dynamic session keys.
EAP
Extensible Authentication Protocol framework; variants include PEAP, EAP-TLS, and EAP-TTLS.
Certificate Management
Issuance, renewal, and revocation of certificates and CA trust are essential for TLS-based EAP security.
Summary

Use 802.1X and the appropriate EAP type with correct certificate handling to secure enterprise wireless and enable per-user access control.
