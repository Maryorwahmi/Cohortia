Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Governance & Ethics
Policy elements
Clear authorization, escalation, and evidence requirements keep containment safe.
  • Define when automated containment is allowed and who can authorize it.
  • Require confirmation signals before disruption.
  • Document all actions and preserve captures for forensics.
Operational controls
Training and rollback plans reduce risk when containment affects legitimate services.
Training
Ensure operators can interpret signals and perform targeted containment safely.
Rollback
Have procedures to restore service quickly when legitimate clients are impacted.
Key Takeaway

Embed WIPS into governance so containment is safe, auditable, and aligned with legal and business requirements.
