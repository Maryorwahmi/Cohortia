Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Forensics & Response
Evidence types
Logs, packet captures, and wired-port correlations are central to wireless forensics.
Event logs
Record detections, timestamps, confidence levels, and containment actions.
Packet captures
Store sensor captures to review frames and reconstruct attacker behavior.
Wired correlation
Map MACs to switch ports to locate physical devices for remediation.
Incident response steps
Contain, collect, correlate, eradicate, recover, and learn.
  1. Contain the immediate threat with minimal disruption.
  2. Collect WIPS logs and sensor packet captures; preserve timestamps and chain of custody.
  3. Correlate wireless events with wired infrastructure and other security logs.
  4. Eradicate the rogue device and recover services.
  5. Update rules and controls based on lessons learned.
Key Takeaway

Forensics turns alerts into actionable evidence; preserve captures and logs to understand scope and prevent recurrence.
