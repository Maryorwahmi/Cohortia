Interpretation
The first line is suspicious: corporate SSID on an unexpected channel and tied to an unauthorized wired port. That suggests a rogue AP unless further confirmation disproves it.
Supporting steps
What to do next with this telemetry.
  • Confirm wired-port mapping and inspect Port 5 physically if possible.
  • Capture packets for RF fingerprinting and to preserve evidence.
  • Consider medium-confidence containment only if multiple signals agree; otherwise log and monitor.
Pro tip
Keep a current inventory of legitimate AP MACs and usual channels to speed decision-making.
