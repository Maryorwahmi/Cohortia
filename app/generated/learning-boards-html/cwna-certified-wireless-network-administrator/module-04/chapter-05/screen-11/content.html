Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Sensor Placement
Coverage planning checklist
Identify high-risk locations and test empirically.
  • Map wired jacks in public or shared spaces as high-priority monitoring points.
  • Place sensors to cover lobbies, conference rooms, and employee shared desks.
  • Account for walls, reflective surfaces, and multi-floor bleed when planning placement.
Validation and tuning
Use trial scans and adjust sensor positions based on real RF readings.
Test
Perform controlled broadcasts and verify sensors detect them with expected signal strength.
Tune
Move or add sensors to eliminate blind spots and balance cost with coverage.
Document
Keep a placement map and update it as spaces change to maintain detection fidelity.
Key Takeaway

Plan placement around high-risk touchpoints, validate with tests, and document sensor maps for effective coverage.
