Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Overlay vs Integrated
Overlay WIPS
Dedicated sensors placed throughout the environment, independent of production APs.
Strengths
  • 24/7 scanning across all channels
  • No impact on client-serving APs
  • Better forensic capture coverage
Trade-offs
Additional hardware and management overhead; placement planning required for full coverage.
Integrated WIPS
Sensing built into APs or WLCs so existing hardware performs double duty.
Strengths
  • Lower hardware cost
  • Simplified management under the WLAN controller
Trade-offs
Possible reduced visibility and slight performance impact when scanning; may not scan all channels continuously.
Key Takeaway

Choose overlay for maximal, continuous visibility and minimal production impact; choose integrated for lower cost and simpler operations where visibility trade-offs are acceptable.
