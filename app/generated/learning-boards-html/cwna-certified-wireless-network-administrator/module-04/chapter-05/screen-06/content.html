Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Rogue AP Detection
Detection checklist
Require multiple signals before containment.
  • Wired-side detection: match MAC to switch port activity.
  • RF fingerprinting: radio traits that suggest vendor/model.
  • SSID matching and channel scanning: reveal cloned names and odd channel use.
Example
An AP broadcasts the corporate SSID but appears on an unusual channel and is seen on an unexpected switch port — this combination is a strong indicator of a rogue device.
Decision flow
Collect signals, increase confidence, then choose containment level.
Low confidence
Log the event and schedule physical inspection; no disruptive action.
Medium confidence
Send alerts to admins, gather packet captures, and consider localized containment.
High confidence
Authorized deauthentication to affected clients and immediate admin notification for removal.
Key Takeaway

Detection should be confidence-based and rely on multiple signals before any containment that might disrupt service.
