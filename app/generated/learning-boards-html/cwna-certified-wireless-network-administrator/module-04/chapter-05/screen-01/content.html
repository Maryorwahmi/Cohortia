Wireless Intrusion Prevention Systems
Active RF monitoring, rogue AP detection, containment, and forensic visibility.

A WIPS continuously scans the radio spectrum, identifies unauthorized activity, and can take automated actions to neutralize threats. It fills gaps that encryption and authentication cannot cover, such as devices physically connected without authorization or evil twin hotspots aimed at tricking users.

In this lesson
  • Compare overlay and integrated WIPS deployments and their trade-offs.
  • Learn detection techniques: wired correlation, RF fingerprinting, SSID and channel analysis.
  • Explore containment options and safe incident response steps.
