Scenario data
Use the following to reason about detection and containment.
Legit SSID: CorpNet
Legit MACs: 00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5E, 00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5F
Allowed channels: 1, 6, 11 (2.4 GHz); 36,40,44 (5 GHz)
Wired ports for legit APs: Port 1, Port 2
Sensor report:
  BSSID: AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF
  SSID: CorpNet
  Channel: 7 (2.4 GHz)
  Wired port: 5
Analysis
Mismatch on MAC and channel plus wired port presence makes this a strong rogue candidate.
Recommended next steps
Gather RF fingerprint, packet capture, verify port 5 physically, then consider targeted containment.
Decision guide
Use this checklist before containment.
  1. Confirm port mapping and inventory mismatch.
  2. Collect RF fingerprint and packet capture evidence.
  3. Notify administrators and obtain any required authorization.
Key Takeaway

Multiple corroborating signals plus documented evidence are required before taking disruptive containment actions.
