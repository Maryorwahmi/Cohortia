Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › Learning Objectives
01
Explain WIPS necessity
Describe why an active RF monitoring layer is needed beyond encryption and authentication.
You will learn to justify WIPS to stakeholders by showing how it detects threats that bypass upper-layer controls. Mnemonic: "Airwatch" — the air itself needs watching.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Describe deployment models
Compare overlay and integrated WIPS architectures and their trade-offs.
You will map costs and visibility needs to the right model. Mnemonic: "Separate sensors vs shared duty" helps you remember the trade-off.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Detect and contain rogue APs
Explain detection techniques and safe containment methods such as deauthentication with caution.
You will practice reasoning about which detection signals are strong enough to act on. Mnemonic: "Match, fingerprint, confirm" summarizes the steps.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Forensics and incident response
Understand what logs and captures a WIPS provides and how to use them in a response plan.
You will learn what evidence to collect and how to tie wireless events back to wired infrastructure. Mnemonic: "Log, capture, correlate."
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
