Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › SAE vs WPA2
WPA2‑PSK

Characteristics

  • Authentication via shared pre‑shared key and four‑way handshake.
  • Handshake captures enable offline dictionary attacks.
  • No forward secrecy by default; compromise of PSK affects past sessions.
WPA3‑SAE

Characteristics

  • Uses a PAKE-style exchange so passphrases are never exposed for offline testing.
  • Provides forward secrecy with ephemeral session keys.
  • Requires supported client and AP implementations.
Deployment considerations
Security: SAE improves resilience against offline attacks.
Compatibility: mixed mode may be needed for older clients.
Best practice: maintain strong passphrases and firmware updates.
