Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › WPA3‑Enterprise
Definition

192‑bit security suite requirements

Mandates stronger cryptographic primitives and key lengths, such as AES‑256 in robust modes and SHA‑384 for key derivation.
Retains 802.1X/EAP for authentication but requires higher‑assurance algorithms beneath that framework.
Targeted at environments with stringent cryptographic requirements, like government and defense.
Why it matters
Longer keys and stronger hashes reduce the feasibility of cryptanalytic attacks and align with high‑assurance policies.
Example
A university research lab or government office handling classified research may require WPA3‑Enterprise 192‑bit mode to comply with internal crypto standards and external regulations.
Misconception check:
Stronger algorithms raise computational costs and require compatible supplicants and authentication servers; they are not a drop‑in replacement without planning.
Key Takeaway

Use WPA3‑Enterprise 192‑bit mode when policy or regulation demands higher cryptographic strength; plan for compatibility and performance impacts.
