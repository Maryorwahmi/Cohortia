Process

How SAE works at a high level

Both client and AP combine the passphrase with fresh randomness and compute exchange values.
If computations match, each side derives a unique ephemeral session key — no reusable handshake data exists for offline testing.
Forward secrecy follows because session keys are ephemeral and not recoverable from the long‑term PSK alone.
Analogy
Think of SAE as two people proving a shared secret by performing matching secret math that cannot be rechecked later from a recording.
Example scenario
When a client enters a passphrase, SAE produces ephemeral session material. An eavesdropper capturing that exchange cannot use it later to verify guesses because each attempt would require live protocol interaction. SAE stops offline verification so brute force must be online and is observable.
Contrast: WPA2 allowed offline verification from handshake captures; SAE does not.
Misconception: SAE alone makes poor passphrases safe — it reduces risk but strong passphrases remain best practice.
Key Takeaway

SAE replaces the four‑way handshake to stop offline dictionary attacks and provide forward secrecy for WPA3‑Personal.
