Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › What to Remember
WPA3
Enhanced authentication and encryption across Personal, Open, and Enterprise modes.
SAE
Password‑authenticated key exchange that blocks offline attacks and gives forward secrecy.
OWE
Provides per‑client encryption on open networks to protect passive privacy threats.
Summary takeaway
Adopt WPA3 features thoughtfully: verify support, test in a lab, use mixed mode for transition, and move to WPA3‑only when your device inventory allows.
