Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Skills & Checks
Practical checks
  • Verify device support: OS and driver reports for WPA3 or WPA3‑Personal/Enterprise indicators.
  • Inspect router settings: look for WPA3‑Personal, WPA3‑Enterprise, or Mixed Mode options.
  • Create an inventory: list clients, OS versions, and firmware to plan a migration window.
Quick checklist
Device support, firmware updates, user notification, fallback plan for legacy clients.
Decision guide
Choose mixed mode when many legacy clients exist and you need continuity.
Move to WPA3‑only when inventory shows broad client support and you need strongest protections.
Document risks: mixed mode inherits vulnerabilities from WPA2 clients, so schedule upgrades.
Reflective prompt
Which devices matter most in your network and when can they be updated? Use that to sequence migration.
