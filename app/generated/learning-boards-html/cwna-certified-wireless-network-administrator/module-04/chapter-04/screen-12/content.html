Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Mistakes & Tips
Common mistakes
  • Relying on mixed mode for security without upgrading legacy clients.
  • Assuming vendor labels always mean complete WPA3 support.
  • Not updating firmware or supplicants before enabling WPA3 features.
Pro tips
  • Inventory and prioritize devices for upgrades based on risk and usage.
  • Segment legacy clients and use test SSIDs before production changes.
  • Prepare rollback steps and notify users to reduce disruption.
Key Takeaway

Plan migrations deliberately: testing and inventory reduce surprises, and segmented rollouts lower operational risk while improving security over time.
