Module 4 › Chapter 4.4 › Assessments
Sample questions
  • MCQ: What is SAE's primary improvement over WPA2‑PSK? (Answer: forward secrecy and resistance to offline dictionary attacks.)
  • Scenario: Which feature best protects cafe patrons from passive sniffing? (Answer: OWE.)
  • Short task: Run a device check and report whether WPA3 is available and why.
Grading guidance
Look for clear explanation of forward secrecy, an accurate description of OWE's privacy goal, and evidence of verified device checks.
Penalty for answers that conflate encryption with authentication or that assume weak passwords are rendered safe by SAE alone.
Key Takeaway

Assess both theory and hands‑on verification to ensure learners can plan and execute secure WPA3 migrations.
