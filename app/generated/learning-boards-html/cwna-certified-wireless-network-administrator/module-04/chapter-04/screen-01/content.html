Visual concept
Connected cards show Personal, Open, Enterprise protections
Personal — SAE
Password Authenticated Key Exchange gives forward secrecy and blocks offline attacks.
Open — OWE
Automatic Diffie‑Hellman-like exchange creates per-client encryption without authentication.
Enterprise — 192‑bit
Mandates AES‑256 and SHA‑384 for environments with high assurance needs.
Note
Transition modes let old and new devices coexist, but mixed mode is limited by the weakest client.
