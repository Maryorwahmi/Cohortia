Practical milestones
  • 1
    Run a scan and interpret results
    Identify SSIDs, BSSIDs, channels, and signal strength anomalies.
  • 2
    Classify suspicious APs
    Decide whether an AP is a misconfigured guest device, a rogue, or an evil twin requiring immediate action.
  • 3
    Recommend layered mitigations
    Choose controls that address confidentiality, integrity, and availability together.
Mnemonic & common mistakes
Mnemonic

Listen, Imitate, Force, Inject — then defend with CIA and layers.

Common mistakes

Open SSIDs, weak/default passwords, hiding SSIDs, and lacking segmentation are recurring issues that enable attackers.

Verification

A short practical check is to run a scan, list anomalies, and map each anomaly to a risk and mitigation step.
