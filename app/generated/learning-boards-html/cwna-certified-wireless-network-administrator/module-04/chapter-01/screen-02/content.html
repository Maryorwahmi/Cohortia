Objective details
Why these objectives matter

Knowing attack categories helps you detect and respond faster. Mapping attacks to confidentiality, integrity, and availability lets you choose controls that directly reduce business risk.

How you'll prove competency

Run a local scan, identify anomalies such as duplicate SSIDs, and explain the likely risk and mitigation plan in a short report.

Mnemonic

Think: Listen (eavesdrop), Imitate (evil twin), Force (deauth/jam), Inject (packets) — then apply CIA and layers.
