Introduction to Wireless Security Threats

Understand common wireless attack vectors and the principles that defend against them.

In this lesson

Why wireless is uniquely exposed, the most important attack patterns you will see in the wild, and a simple, layered approach to reduce risk.

Who this helps

Network administrators, security analysts, and anyone responsible for protecting Wi‑Fi in office or home environments.

What you'll use

A laptop with a Wi‑Fi adapter, a basic scanning command or GUI tool, and network configuration access to inspect AP settings.

Why it matters

Attacks against Wi‑Fi can expose credentials, allow lateral access, or disrupt operations. Small misconfigurations have large real-world consequences.

Explore Key Topics
Eavesdropping
Passive capture of unencrypted frames; solved primarily with strong encryption.
Rogue & Evil Twin
Unauthorized APs that bypass controls or impersonate legitimate SSIDs to intercept traffic.
Availability Attacks
Deauthentication and jamming attacks that disrupt services and require layered detection.
Visual Summary
Treat the air as public
Assume anyone can listen unless encryption prevents it.
Watch for anomalies
Multiple identical SSIDs or unfamiliar open networks are red flags.
Layer controls
Combine encryption, segmentation, monitoring, and physical security.
Coming up

We will examine each attack type in depth, show simple detection steps you can run locally, and highlight common configuration mistakes to avoid.
