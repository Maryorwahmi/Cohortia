Detection

Monitor for sudden spikes in deauth or disassociation frames and correlate with user reports of disconnections.

Example & response

If many clients drop simultaneously, check AP logs and WIPS alerts for bursts of management frames. If MFP (Management Frame Protection) is available, enable it to prevent spoofed management frames from being accepted.

Mitigation checklist
  • Enable management frame protection where supported.
  • Alert on unusual frame patterns and mass disconnects.
  • Physically locate and neutralize the source if the attack persists.
Common mistake

Relying solely on client-side patches — detection and network-level protections are necessary to stop ongoing attacks.
