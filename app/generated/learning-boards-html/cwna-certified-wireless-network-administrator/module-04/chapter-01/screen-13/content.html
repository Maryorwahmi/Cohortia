Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › What to Remember
Verify encryption

Strong WPA2/WPA3 and application TLS are essential to prevent captured frames from revealing data.

Maintain AP inventory

Track BSSIDs, channels, and locations so unexpected devices are found quickly and investigated.

Monitor availability

Alert on deauth storms, high noise, or mass disconnects to catch jamming or protocol abuse early.

Final takeaway

Small routine checks — scan, compare, monitor — are powerful. Build those habits to prevent common wireless incidents.
