Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts
Wireless is observable

Assume transmissions can be sniffed. Rely on strong layer-2 or application encryption to preserve confidentiality.

Unauthorized AP risks

Rogue APs bridge to internal networks; evil twins impersonate SSIDs to intercept traffic. Verify BSSIDs and inventory.

Map to CIA triad

Eavesdropping hits confidentiality; injection harms integrity; deauth and jamming reduce availability. Choose defenses accordingly.

Summary takeaway

Defend wireless networks with layered controls: encryption, segmentation, monitoring, and physical/port security. Small misconfigurations create large risks.
