Definition

Guest Network Isolation

Segregating guest traffic so visitors have internet-only access and cannot reach internal systems.

VLAN assignment
Map guest SSID to a dedicated VLAN ID and ensure routing enforces separation.
Separate DHCP
A separate DHCP scope avoids address overlap and visibility with corporate ranges.
Firewall rules
Allow only necessary outbound ports; explicitly block all internal subnets.
Scenario
Guest SSID: MyCompany-Guest VLAN ID: 10 DHCP: 10.10.10.0/24 Firewall: Permit 80,443,53 outbound; deny 10.0.0.0/8 and 192.168.0.0/16 internal ranges
Common mistake
Using a captive portal without VLAN separation or sharing DHCP with internal networks undermines isolation.
Check your work:
A guest device must receive a guest-scoped IP, fail to ping internal addresses, and only access internet ports allowed by firewall rules.
