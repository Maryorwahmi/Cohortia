Process Flow

Security-focused RF Survey

  1. Passive scan: enumerate BSSIDs and channels across the site.
  2. Active measurements: take RSSI and SNR readings at sample points.
  3. Spectrum analysis: identify non-Wi-Fi interference sources.
  4. Locate unknown APs and validate their network attachment.
  5. Review results and adjust placement, power, or policies as needed.
Example finding
Survey result: Strong AP seen in office floor 2 with same SSID as corporate but unknown switch port. Action: locate device, disable port, reconfigure with 802.1X.
Common mistake
Assuming coverage never changes. Furniture, new devices, and relocated APs alter RF and require re-checks.
Why it matters:
Routine surveys are how you detect unauthorized devices and environmental changes before they become security incidents.
