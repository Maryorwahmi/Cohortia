Pro-Tip

Operational Controls

  • Inventory devices and firmware versions centrally.
  • Automate config backups and run periodic drift checks.
  • Centralize logging and monitor for anomalous authentication patterns.
  • Use switch port security and limit management plane exposure.
Common pitfalls
Leaving controllers on general VLANs, skipping firmware updates, and not auditing after physical moves are simple lapses that create real vulnerabilities.
Key Takeaway
Operational rigor often prevents misconfigurations that are more likely causes of breaches than exotic attacks.
