Process Flow

Audit Checklist

  • Firmware up-to-date on APs and controllers.
  • Authentication methods reviewed (802.1X, PSK usage).
  • VLAN mappings and DHCP scopes validated.
  • Firewall rules enforce segregation.
  • Logging and alerting configured for anomalies.
Pen Test example
Scope: Guest isolation and corporate SSID. Test actions: try to join guest VLAN with spoofed MAC, attempt to access corporate services, and test WPA2-PSK cracking attempts on a captured handshake.
Operational note
Schedule and authorize tests to avoid unintended outages; have rollback and emergency contacts ready.
Result expectation:
Pen tests should yield prioritized remediation items tied to audit findings and risk levels.
