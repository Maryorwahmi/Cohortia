Definition

Vulnerability Management Steps

1) Inventory devices and firmware versions.
2) Monitor advisories and alerts from vendors.
3) Assess risk and schedule tested patches.
4) Verify remediation with scans and audits.
Example prioritization
High: Remote config manipulation on deployed APs — patch immediately after testing. Medium: Information disclosure in logs — plan patch within standard cycle. Low: Cosmetic UI flaw — schedule with regular maintenance.
Key Takeaway
Include wireless devices in your vulnerability program and close tickets with verification steps recorded.
