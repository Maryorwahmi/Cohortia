Comparison

MAC Filtering vs SSID Hiding

MAC Filtering
Restricts allowed devices by MAC. Easily bypassed by sniffing and spoofing; high admin overhead.
SSID Hiding
Removes SSID from beacons but clients still reveal it on association; discoverable with radio tools.
Example: Attack flow
1) Attacker passively captures frames and notes a legitimate client's MAC and SSID.
2) Attacker sets their interface to the observed MAC (MAC spoofing).
3) Attacker attempts to authenticate; with PSK systems a captured MAC gives a usable identity to connect.
Result: MAC filter and hidden SSID offer no real barrier to a determined attacker.
Misconception
Believing these measures replace encryption or 802.1X is risky. They are cosmetic at best.
Practical rule:
Use strong crypto and authentication; treat MAC filtering and SSID hiding only as optional deterrents.
