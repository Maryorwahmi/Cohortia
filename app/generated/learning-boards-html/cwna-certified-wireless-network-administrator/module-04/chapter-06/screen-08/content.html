Passive capture example

BSSID            PWR  Beacons  #Data  CH  MB  ENC  CIPHER  AUTH  ESSID
00:11:22:33:44:55  -40   120      10     6   54  WPA2  CCMP   PSK   MyCompany-Guest
(Hidden SSID)     -65    50       2     11  54  WPA2  CCMP   PSK   
Client assoc: 66:77:88:99:AA:BB -> MyCompany-Guest
The captured association reveals both the SSID and client MAC even for networks that do not broadcast beacons.
Implication
Once an attacker observes these frames they can spoof the client MAC and attempt to join. Hiding the SSID only delays casual discovery.
Defense
Use per-user strong authentication and network segmentation so that stolen identifiers alone do not grant access to sensitive systems.
Reflective question:
If an attacker spoofs a MAC, what additional control would prevent them from accessing corporate services?
