Process Flow

AP Placement Checklist

  1. Map coverage requirements and exclusion zones on the floor plan.
  2. Select AP count and approximate locations for overlap using manufacturer guidelines.
  3. Choose antennas: omnidirectional indoors, directional in corridors or outdoors.
  4. Adjust transmit power to limit external signal bleed; aim for minimal usable signal outside perimeter.
  5. Perform a walk-test and RF survey; iterate placement and power as needed.
Common mistake
Maximizing power for coverage increases interference and external exposure. Tune for containment, not brute force.
Example Walk-test
RSSI readings (sample):
Room A interior: -55 dBm
Exterior near window: -64 dBm
Parking lot 3m away: -78 dBm
Target: < -70 dBm usable outside perimeter
Adjust: lower AP power by 3 dB or relocate closer to interior center
Key Takeaway
Measure, then adjust. Walk-tests and simple RSSI checks are often enough to confirm containment.
