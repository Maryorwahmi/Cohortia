Module 5 › Chapter 4.6 › Key Concepts
Secure WLAN Design
Placement, power, and segmentation reduce exposure and limit impact from breaches.
Guest Isolation
Separate VLAN, DHCP scope, and firewall denies are required for true isolation.
Continuous Testing
RF surveys, audits, and pen testing find drift and validate defenses.
Takeaway

Apply layered controls: design to limit exposure, isolate to contain risk, and test to keep defenses current.
