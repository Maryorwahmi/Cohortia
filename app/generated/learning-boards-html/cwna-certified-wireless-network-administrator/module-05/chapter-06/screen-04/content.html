Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Consolidate Data
Process

Merge layers and preserve traceability

1. Import predictive plan and baseline AP placements.
2. Overlay passive walk samples: RSSI, SNR, noise floor, channel observations.
3. Add active traces: throughput, retransmissions, and roaming latency.
4. Tag data with timestamps, device IDs and channel info for auditing.
Supporting explanation
  • Cross‑check: a finding should be visible in at least two data layers when possible.
  • Discrepancy example: good RSSI but high noise floor suggests interference, not weak coverage.
  • Active vs passive: throughput problems with good RSSI often indicate capacity limits or high retransmissions.
Example scenario
A predictive map showed -65 dBm coverage in a conference area. Passive walk found -60 dBm RSSI but an elevated noise floor. Active tests measured throughput collapse under load. Consolidation revealed interference during business hours causing low SNR and high retransmissions.
Key Takeaway
Do not act on one measurement alone. Consolidate layers so recommendations solve root causes.
