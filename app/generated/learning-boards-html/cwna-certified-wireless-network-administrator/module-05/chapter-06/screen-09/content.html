Report structure

1. Executive Summary — high level findings and recommended next steps for decision makers.
2. Introduction — scope and methodology so readers know how data was produced.
3. Requirements Review — restate business and technical goals to align solutions.
4. Survey Findings — heatmaps and annotated problem areas with data citations.
5. Recommendations & Proposed Design — prioritized, actionable steps and final AP map.
Bill of Materials (BoM)
List all hardware and software by quantity and model, and include notes for alternatives. This helps procurement and budgeting and prevents delays during implementation.
Appendices
Attach raw data, additional heatmaps and photos for auditors and engineers who need detail beyond the main report.
Key Takeaway
Make the report readable at two levels: clear summary for decision makers and detailed evidence for implementers.
