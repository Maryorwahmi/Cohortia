Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Capacity & Interference
Capacity heatmap
Maps throughput and client density so you can see whether the network supports expected loads. Use active tests that simulate concurrent clients where possible.
Example: Large meeting room throughput drops to 20–30 Mbps with 10+ devices — capacity shortfall that needs design changes.
Action prompt
If capacity fails, quantify whether additional APs or airtime management are the correct response after checking interference.
Interference heatmap
Shows CCI/ACI and non‑Wi‑Fi noise. High interference in a zone suggests changing channels, adjusting power, or mitigating non‑Wi‑Fi devices.
Example: Three APs in a meeting room all on the same 5 GHz channel at -60 dBm — adjust channel plan and consider power or antenna changes.
Key Takeaway
Combine capacity and interference maps to choose whether to add APs, change power, or reassign channels; document which map motivated the decision.
