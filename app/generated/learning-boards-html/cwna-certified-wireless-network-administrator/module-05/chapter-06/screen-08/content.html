Recommendation pattern

Action: Clear, specific change to make.
Justification: Which heatmap or measurement motivates this action.
Expected outcome: Measurable improvement and risk notes.
Examples
  • AP Relocation: move AP X 2 m to avoid concrete column — expect RSSI +5 dB in affected area.
  • Channel Plan: reassign three meeting room APs to non‑overlapping 5 GHz channels to reduce CCI.
  • Power Tuning: lower transmit power in open office to reduce cell overlap and improve capacity.
Prioritization
Rank fixes by impact and effort. Quick low‑effort wins first, then planned changes requiring procurement.
Key Takeaway
Make each recommendation actionable, measurable, and tied to survey evidence so implementation succeeds.
