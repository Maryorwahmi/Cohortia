Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Activity: Recommendations

Scenario recap

Predictive design: 15 APs with -65 dBm target. Passive: good noise floor overall, but three meeting rooms show multiple APs on the same 5 GHz channel. Neighbor strong on 2.4 GHz channels 1 and 6. Active: meeting rooms throughput drops to 20–30 Mbps with 10+ devices and retransmissions spike to 25–30%.
Recommended actions (example)
  1. Meeting rooms: Reassign the three APs to non‑overlapping 5 GHz channels, lower transmit power to reduce overlap, and consider adding capacity APs or directional antennas to isolate each room. Expected result: throughput recovery and lower retransmissions.
  2. Perimeter 2.4 GHz interference: Move capable clients to 5 GHz via band steering, change 2.4 GHz channels away from neighbor overlap, and investigate neighbor coordination or shielding. Expected result: reduced intermittent connectivity.
  3. General optimization: Review whether 15 APs cause over‑design. Remove or power‑tune APs in over‑lapped zones to reduce co‑channel interference and improve overall capacity distribution.
Checklist
Attach heatmaps showing channel overlap, SNR and active throughput; list AP IDs and channels; include BoM changes if new APs or antennas are required.
Key Takeaway
Make each recommendation measurable and reference the data that motivated it so validation is straightforward after implementation.
