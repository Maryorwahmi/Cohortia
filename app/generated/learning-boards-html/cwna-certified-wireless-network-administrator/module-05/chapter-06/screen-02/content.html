Learning Objectives

Skills to consolidate data, interpret heatmaps, recommend changes, and report clearly.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Consolidate survey data
Combine predictive, passive and active measurements on a single floor plan to see correlations and discrepancies. This matters because only a complete view reveals where measurements disagree and why.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Generate and interpret heatmaps
Produce coverage, SNR, capacity and interference heatmaps and learn what each metric reveals. Being able to read these visuals lets you prioritize fixes where they matter most.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Formulate actionable recommendations
Translate heatmap findings into specific actions such as AP moves, power adjustments, and channel changes. Clear, justified steps enable efficient implementation and reduce risk of repeated re-work.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Produce a comprehensive report and BoM
Assemble an executive summary, findings, recommendations, proposed design and Bill of Materials so stakeholders can make informed decisions. This builds trust and ensures your design is executed correctly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
