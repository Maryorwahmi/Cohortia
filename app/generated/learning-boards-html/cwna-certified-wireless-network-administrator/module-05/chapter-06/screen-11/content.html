Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Monitoring & Re‑survey

Monitoring triggers

- Persistent user complaints in a previously healthy area.
- Spike in retransmission or retry rates.
- Sudden throughput degradation or roaming failures.
Re‑survey approach
Begin with targeted passive and spectrum scans, then expand to active tests. Document changes and update the floor plan and BoM as needed.
Operations note
Include monitoring cadence and re‑survey criteria in the report to hand over to operations staff.
Key Takeaway
Treat surveys as part of a lifecycle: monitor, trigger, investigate, and re‑survey when evidence supports it.
