Module 5 › Chapter 5.6 › Reporting Mistakes

Frequent errors

  • Present raw data without interpretation for stakeholders.
  • Recommend actions without citing the supporting measurement.
  • Bury the BoM where procurement cannot find it easily.
Corrective checklist
- Pair symptom with the heatmap and the active test evidence.
- State expected improvement and implementation risk.
- Prioritize and estimate effort so stakeholders can decide quickly.
Example
Rather than saying 'optimize channels', show the interference heatmap, list specific AP IDs and their current channels, and recommend the new channels with expected reduction in CCI.
Key Takeaway
Clarity and traceability in reports reduce implementation risk and speed approvals.
