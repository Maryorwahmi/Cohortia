Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Survey workflow and checklist
Process Flow

Plan → Execute → Validate → Report

A consistent checklist reduces surprises and yields reproducible results stakeholders can act on.

Plan
Collect floor plans, note materials, schedule access, select survey types and tools, and plan the walking route.
Execute
Run predictive models, passive scans, spectrum sweeps, and active throughput tests as required by objectives.
Validate
Compare measured heatmaps to predictive output, adjust model inputs, and re‑test critical locations.
Report
Deliver heatmaps, spectrum captures, AP placement recommendations, cabling notes, and assumptions log.
Field metadata to collect
  • Time of day, survey device model, and adapter orientation.
  • Temporary AP locations, PoE source, and client device types used for active tests.
  • Observed non‑Wi‑Fi sources and their locations.
Safety & setup
Secure temporary APs, watch for trip hazards, and never disrupt critical infrastructure during measurements.
Common mistake

Relying solely on predictive output without verifying floor plan accuracy and materials can lead to incorrect AP counts and placements.
