Troubleshooting

Frequent mistakes and fixes

Be aware of measurement errors and include spectrum analysis and active validation to avoid incorrect remediation.

Outdated plans
Verify current wall positions and materials; measure key distances when plans are missing or old.
Inconsistent adapters
Standardize external adapters and document orientation to make measurements reproducible.
Skipping spectrum checks
Use spectrum captures for intermittent or unexplained degradation and correlate with passive/active data.
Remediation log
Include a short remediation table: issue observed, evidence (heatmap or spectrum), and recommended action to guide installers.
Example actions
Move AP 2 to corridor, change channel plan to avoid CCI, shield or relocate the interfering device identified by spectrum capture.
Pro tip

Correlate passive RSSI drops with spectrum peaks and active throughput to identify whether issues are Wi‑Fi congestion or external noise.
