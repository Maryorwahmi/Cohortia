Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Predictive vs Passive vs Active surveys
Comparison

Choose by goal and context

Each survey type answers a different question: where to place APs, what the RF baseline looks like, or whether users get the performance they need.

Predictive
Simulation driven by floor plans and material types; good for early planning and budgeting.
Passive
Listen to the air and map RSSI, SNR, and channel use; detects existing Wi‑Fi and rogue APs.
Active
Associate a client and measure throughput, roaming, latency, and jitter to validate application performance.
Supporting notes
  • Predictive accuracy depends on floor plan fidelity and correct material choices.
  • Passive surveys cannot directly measure throughput or roaming; they show potential quality issues via SNR and channel congestion.
  • Active surveys are the only way to confirm application-level behavior under real association conditions.
  • Use all three in sequence for new deployments: predictive → passive → active.
Scenario example
For a 5‑story building under construction, start with a predictive model using CAD plans, then verify with passive and active tests once walls and APs are available.
Key Takeaway

No single survey type answers every question; combine them to move from design to validated deployment.
