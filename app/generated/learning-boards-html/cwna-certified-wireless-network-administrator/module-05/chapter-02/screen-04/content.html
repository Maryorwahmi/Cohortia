Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › What is a Wi‑Fi site survey?
Core Concept

A measured approach to WLAN design

A site survey replaces assumptions with data. It produces the evidence needed to place APs, set channels, and verify capacity.

Why it matters
Avoids blind AP placement that leads to dead zones, interference, and costly rework.
Common misconception
Consumer apps and a single smartphone pass do not replace full survey tools and spectrum analysis.
Example
Predictive modeling suggested APs in a corridor, but a measured scan showed a metal duct causing a shadowed area that required an additional AP.
Deliverables
Heatmaps
Color‑graded overlays showing RSSI or SNR across a floor plan to justify AP positions.
Spectrum captures
Visual evidence of non‑Wi‑Fi energy and interference patterns for troubleshooting and remediation.
Reports & recommendations
AP count and placement, cabling notes, and material‑assumption documentation to guide installers.
Key Takeaway

A survey turns planning into evidence; skip it and you risk unpredictable coverage and performance.
