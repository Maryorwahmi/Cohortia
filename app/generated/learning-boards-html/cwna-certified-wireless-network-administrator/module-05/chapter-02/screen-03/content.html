Applied Objectives

Practice selecting survey types, assembling the toolset, and performing validation so your deliverables are actionable.

Scenario Practice
Map survey type to requirements
E.g., predictive for a building under construction; passive plus spectrum for an interference investigation.
Tool Checklist
Assemble minimal and escalation gear
Laptop, external adapters, temporary APs, PoE injectors, and a spectrum analyzer as needed.
Reporting
Deliver heatmaps and notes
Include floor plan sources, material assumptions, and spectrum captures for auditability.
