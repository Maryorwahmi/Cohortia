Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Spectrum analyzers: what they do and why they matter
Tool Focus

Visualizing all RF energy

Spectrum analyzers show amplitude over frequency so you can identify non‑Wi‑Fi interferers that reduce SNR and throughput.

What it shows
Peaks and noise floors across the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands, including intermittent bursts and continuous carriers.
Why it's necessary
Wi‑Fi adapters only report 802.11 signals; spectrum analysis is required to find non‑802.11 energy disrupting performance.
Example
A warehouse with forklift radios produced wideband noise visible on the spectrum capture but invisible to a standard Wi‑Fi scan.
Practical tips
  • Always capture the spectrum at the location where users report issues and include screenshots in reports.
  • Look for patterns: periodic bursts may indicate rotating equipment; continuous carriers may be a transmitter or security camera.
  • Correlate spectrum findings with passive and active metrics before deciding on remediation.
Misconception

Believing Wi‑Fi analyzers can detect all interference is risky; only spectrum tools show non‑Wi‑Fi energy signatures.
