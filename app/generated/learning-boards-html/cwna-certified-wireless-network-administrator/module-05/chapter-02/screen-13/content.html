Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Activity: selecting survey tools for scenarios

Scenario mapping exercise

Choose the primary survey type and at least two tools for each scenario, explaining why they are needed.

Scenario 1 — Greenfield Office
Primary: Predictive survey. Tools: survey software (to import CAD and model APs), accurate CAD floor plans (to feed the model).
Scenario 2 — Existing Warehouse
Primary: Passive + spectrum analysis. Tools: external Wi‑Fi adapters (accurate RSSI), spectrum analyzer (identify non‑Wi‑Fi interference).
Scenario 3 — School Post‑Deployment
Primary: Active survey. Tools: temporary APs and PoE (simulate production), survey software (record throughput and roaming metrics).
How to justify tools
Tie each tool to the measurement it enables: simulation, baseline mapping, spectrum evidence, or application validation.
Instructor prompt

Write a short tools & justification note for each scenario to practice communicating your plan to a client.
