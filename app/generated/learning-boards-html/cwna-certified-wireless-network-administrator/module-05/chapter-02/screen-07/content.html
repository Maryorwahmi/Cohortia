Hardware

Assemble reliable field equipment

Hardware affects data fidelity. Choose devices that minimize measurement error and represent the production environment where possible.

Survey laptop/tablet
Runs survey software, stores captures, and should have enough battery and processing headroom for long sessions.
External Wi‑Fi adapters
Provide consistent antenna orientation, better sensitivity, and chipset compatibility for survey tools.
Temporary APs & PoE
Simulate production APs; use PoE injectors or batteries to position APs where power is not available.
Measuring & orientation
Measuring tools
Laser distance measurers and tape measures verify floor plan dimensions and AP placement distances.
Compass
Orient heatmaps accurately to building axes so installers place APs in the correct physical direction.
Spectrum analyzer (hardware)
A dedicated RF tool that visualizes all energy in a band, essential for identifying non‑Wi‑Fi interference sources invisible to Wi‑Fi adapters.
Key Takeaway

Choose hardware that preserves measurement fidelity; field choices determine how trustworthy your recommendations will be.
