Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Importance of accurate floor plans
Planning

Floor plans determine predictive reliability

Predictive tools are only as accurate as their inputs. Verify walls, materials, and large objects before trusting simulation outputs.

Preferred inputs
CAD or SVG floor plans with annotated wall materials and dimensions.
When plans are missing
Walk the site with a laser measurer and record wall locations, metal objects, and large obstructions.
Document assumptions
Include plan provenance, material choices, and any unknowns in the report for transparency.
Example
A predictive run using outdated plans missed a new mechanical room; measured passive scans later revealed a persistent shadow behind the room that required an extra AP.
Common mistake

Not verifying wall materials and renovations before trusting predictive outputs leads to incorrect AP counts and placements.
