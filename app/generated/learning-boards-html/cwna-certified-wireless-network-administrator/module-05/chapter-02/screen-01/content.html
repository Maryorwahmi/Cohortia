Site Survey Fundamentals and Tools

Understand survey types, essential tools, and how measured RF data prevents poor placement and costly rework.

In this lesson
Survey types, hardware, software, and planning essentials to produce accurate heatmaps and reports.
Why it matters
Avoid guessing AP locations, find interference sources, and validate roaming and throughput before users are impacted.
Coming up
Hands‑on toolset, spectrum basics, and activity scenarios for typical client sites.
Visual Concept
Predictive
Blueprint simulation using floor plans and material data to estimate AP count and placement.
Passive
Listening to real signals to map RSSI, SNR and detect rogue or co‑channel problems.
Active
Associates a client to measure real throughput, roaming, and application performance.
Toolkit Highlight
Spectrum Analyzer
Visualizes non‑Wi‑Fi RF energy across bands to find microwave, Bluetooth, or cordless phone interference.
Survey Software
Import plans, draw wall types, simulate APs, and overlay measured heatmaps for reporting.
Explore Key Topics
Choosing the right survey
Match predictive, passive, or active surveys to planning, baseline, and validation needs.
Spectrum analysis
Detect non‑Wi‑Fi energy that degrades SNR and throughput but is invisible to a Wi‑Fi adapter.
Floor plan accuracy
Accurate walls and materials make predictive models useful; verify with a walkthrough when plans are missing.
Key Takeaway

A thorough site survey combines simulation, measurement, and spectrum inspection so AP placement and capacity decisions are based on reality, not guesswork.
