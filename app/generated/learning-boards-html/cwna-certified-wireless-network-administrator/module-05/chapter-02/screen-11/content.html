Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Setting up temporary APs and power
Practical Setup

Simulate production radios and mounting

Matching AP models and mounting conditions gives the most realistic passive and active survey results.

Mounting
Tripods or poles at planned heights replicate coverage and handoff conditions for roaming tests.
Powering
Use PoE injectors or battery packs to power temporary APs where AC is unavailable; record PoE source in metadata.
Safety
Secure mounts, route cables to avoid trip hazards, and avoid interference with critical infrastructure.
Notes on validation
When testing roaming, place overlapping APs and perform active tests with client devices representative of real users to measure handoff latency and throughput during roaming.
Common mistake
Using a different AP model for temporary testing can mislead you about coverage and antenna behavior; match models when possible.
Key Takeaway

Proper temporary AP mounting and power recreate production conditions and make passive and active measurements meaningful.
