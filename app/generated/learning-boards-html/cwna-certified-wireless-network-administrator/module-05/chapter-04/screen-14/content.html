Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Passive survey
Listen without associating to capture real RF activity.
RSSI & SNR
RSSI shows power; SNR (RSSI − noise) shows usable quality.
Interference & rogues
Detect co‑channel/adjacent interference and unauthorized APs for remediation.
Summary takeaway

Treat passive surveys as both a performance and security assessment: they provide concrete, repeatable evidence to guide AP placement, power, and channel choices.
