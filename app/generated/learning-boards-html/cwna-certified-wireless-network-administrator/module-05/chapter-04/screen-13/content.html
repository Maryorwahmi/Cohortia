Checklist
Avoid these common mistakes
  • Rushing the walk and not collecting enough samples for stable heatmaps.
  • Using internal Wi‑Fi or mismatched adapters that change sensitivity between runs.
  • Interpreting RSSI without considering noise floor, SNR, or channel utilization.
  • Failing to record survey metadata or environmental context that explains anomalies.
Safety note: secure power and cables, be aware of surroundings, and prevent equipment theft.
