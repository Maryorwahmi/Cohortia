Comparison
Co‑channel interference (CCI)

CCI appears when multiple APs use the same channel and their coverage overlaps. The result is airtime contention and reduced throughput. A passive survey shows multiple APs on one channel with strong RSSI in the same area.

Remedy: channel reassignment, reduce transmit power, or physical separation.
Adjacent‑channel interference (ACI)

ACI occurs when APs operate on overlapping channels. It causes spectral bleed and degrades performance even when channels differ slightly. Passive surveys show overlapping channel energy rather than identical channel listings.

Remedy: choose non‑overlapping channels, reduce channel width, or reposition APs.
Key Takeaway

Both CCI and ACI reduce performance but require different diagnostics and fixes; passive surveys make them visible.
