Hardware essentials
External Wi‑Fi adapters
Offer better sensitivity, consistency, and sometimes support for wide channel scanning across 2.4 and 5 GHz.
Laptop configuration
Disable internal card associations and ensure survey software is linked to external adapters only.
Power and ergonomics
Charge batteries or use a power bank and secure cables to avoid trips during the walk.
Software and checks

Use survey software that records continuous RF samples and produces heatmaps for RSSI and SNR. Verify adapters appear in the device list and that the software’s channel view updates in real time.

Quick check: disable internal Wi‑Fi, start software, confirm external adapter is selected.
Practical tip: standardize how you carry the laptop and adapter so orientation is consistent between surveys.
Key Takeaway

Reliable hardware and consistent configuration eliminate many sources of survey error.
