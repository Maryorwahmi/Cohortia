How you'll prove mastery
Four compact verification tasks map directly to the learning objectives and are easy to grade in a lab session.
Task 1
Adapter & laptop setup

Confirm external adapter recognition, disable internal Wi‑Fi associations, and load the floor plan in survey software.

Check: adapter appears in software list and does not associate
Task 2
Repeatable walking & data capture

Take consistent sample points with steady pace and orientation so heatmaps are comparable across runs.

Check: heatmap patterns stable between two short walks
Task 3
Calculate and interpret SNR

From a data point, subtract noise floor from RSSI to compute SNR and interpret the result for the application in question.

Check: compute SNR and explain whether voice or data performance is at risk
Task 4
Identify interference & rogue APs

Use channel maps and SSID lists to find co‑channel and adjacent‑channel contention and unauthorized SSIDs inside the floor plan.

Check: mark a rogue SSID and suggest remediation
