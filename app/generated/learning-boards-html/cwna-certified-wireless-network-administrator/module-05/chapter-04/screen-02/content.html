Learning Objectives
Learn what to measure, how to collect it, and how to act on passive survey results.
01
Explain passive survey methodology
Describe why listening without associating provides a true snapshot of the RF environment and when to use a passive survey vs. predictive models.
Venn: prediction, reality, verification
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Prepare hardware and software
Select external adapters, configure the laptop to avoid associations, and choose survey software that records continuous RF samples.
Mnemonic: adapters → isolation → software
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Collect and interpret RF metrics
Measure RSSI, SNR, noise floor, and channel utilization and use thresholds to identify coverage or capacity problems.
Timeline: collect → analyze → recommend
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Identify interference and rogue APs
Detect co‑channel, adjacent‑channel interference, and unauthorized access points that create security risks or performance loss.
Mnemonic: channel map + SSID inventory
