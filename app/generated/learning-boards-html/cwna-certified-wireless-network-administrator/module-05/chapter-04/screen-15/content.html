Standardize your kit
External adapters, disabled internal Wi‑Fi, consistent orientation and pace.
Combine metrics
Use RSSI, noise floor, SNR, and utilization together to diagnose problems accurately.
Prioritize rogues
Map and remove unauthorized on‑premises SSIDs quickly; they are high‑risk security issues.
Final takeaway

A disciplined passive survey produces reliable evidence that turns assumptions into actionable network and security improvements.
