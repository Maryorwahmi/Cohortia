Data point interpretation

Calculate SNR for each location and then reason about coverage, interference, and capacity. Present a one‑line recommendation for each point.

# Location A: Open Office
RSSI = -55 dBm
Noise floor = -92 dBm
SNR = -55 - (-92) = 37 dB
Interpretation: strong SNR, low noise, low utilization → good coverage and capacity

# Location B: Conference Room
RSSI = -68 dBm
Noise floor = -75 dBm
SNR = -68 - (-75) = 7 dB
Interpretation: low SNR, high channel utilization → capacity and interference problems

# Location C: Corner Office
RSSI = -82 dBm
Noise floor = -90 dBm
SNR = -82 - (-90) = 8 dB
Interpretation: poor RSSI and low SNR; likely coverage gap and neighbor AP influence
Example conclusions & actions
Action for A

No change required; verify across a second walk and monitor utilization if density increases.

Action for B

Investigate neighbor AP on same channel and high utilization; consider channel change or additional capacity for meetings.

Action for C

Address coverage gap by moving AP closer or adding an AP; check client orientation and obstacles.
