Process Flow
Planning and walking
1. Load floor plan and mark route.
2. Attach external adapters; disable internal Wi‑Fi association.
3. Maintain consistent pace and device orientation while sampling.
4. Record metadata and repeat for verification.
Why repeatability matters

Consistent walking produces heatmaps that can be compared across time or after network changes. Inconsistent speed or orientation causes sample clustering or gaps that obscure real problems.

Example
Click vs continuous sampling
Clicking to mark points can help create precise locations on a floor plan, while continuous sampling is quicker but depends on steady motion to map correctly.
Key Takeaway

A repeatable walk is as important as the hardware — data without consistency is unreliable.
