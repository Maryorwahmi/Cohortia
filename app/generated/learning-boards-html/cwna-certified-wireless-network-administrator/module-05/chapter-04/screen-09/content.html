Core Metric
Noise floor

Noise floor is ambient RF energy. A higher noise floor reduces SNR and hurts performance even if RSSI is moderate. Identify noisy areas to reduce interference sources or adjust AP placement.

Cause: other networks, non‑Wi‑Fi devices, or environment
Action: identify sources and mitigate where possible.
Channel utilization

Utilization is the percentage of time a channel is occupied. High utilization — for example sustained levels above 50–60% — indicates congestion that reduces effective throughput even with strong signals.

Interpretation: high utilization → consider channel reassignment or additional APs for capacity
Key Takeaway

Noise affects quality, utilization affects airtime and capacity. Both explain different symptoms.
