Detecting rogue APs

Passive surveys reveal all broadcasting SSIDs and their signal strengths. If an unauthorized SSID appears inside your mapped floor plan, treat it as a rogue device requiring investigation and remediation.

Record: SSID, BSSID, channel, approximate location on the floor plan.
Security risk: unauthorized APs can bypass network controls and provide access to internal resources.
Scenario

If you find an SSID like "FREE_WIFI_GUEST" broadcasting from inside a server room, this strongly suggests a rogue AP and requires immediate action.

Action steps: document details, notify security, locate and remove or reconfigure the device.
Key Takeaway

Treat on‑premises unauthorized SSIDs as high‑priority security issues and coordinate removal immediately.
