Reliability: redundancy, interference & testing
Plan for failure and reduce interference to keep services predictable.
Redundancy
Overlap coverage, redundant controllers, and failover plans to maintain availability.
Channel Planning
Limit co-channel and adjacent-channel interference; in 2.4 GHz use non-overlapping channels thoughtfully.
Adaptive Controls
Use transmit power control and DFS in higher bands to adapt to the environment.
Process
Create a channel plan, build redundancy, run pilot tests, and validate failover under load.
Misconception
More APs alone do not equal greater reliability; without planning they increase interference.
