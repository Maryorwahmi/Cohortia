Coverage: RSSI, SNR, Attenuation
Define minimum signal strength and quality required for each application.
RSSI
Received Signal Strength Indicator. Measured in dBm; less negative is stronger.
SNR
Signal-to-noise ratio. High SNR means cleaner, more reliable links.
Attenuation
Materials like concrete and glass reduce signal; human bodies also absorb RF energy and must be considered in dense spaces.
Example: VoWi‑Fi in exam rooms
Set a minimum RSSI of -67 dBm and target SNR of 25 dB for every critical exam room to support voice quality. Verify during site survey under typical occupancy conditions.
Key Takeaway
Coverage must be expressed as a measurable threshold tied to the application.
Common Mistake
Assuming any detected signal is sufficient; weak signals can cause poor experience despite presence of coverage.
