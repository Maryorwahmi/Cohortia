Capacity Planning
From application mix to AP density and transmit power.
Estimate
Concurrent active users × per-user Mbps = aggregate throughput requirement for an area.
Example
50 users × 5 Mbps = 250 Mbps aggregate. That informs number of APs and uplink capacity.
Common Pitfall
Designing for coverage only produces APs overloaded with clients despite good RSSI.
High-density strategy
Use more APs with reduced transmit power to create smaller cells and avoid co-channel collisions. Plan channels carefully and prioritize critical traffic.
Pro Tip
Reserve QoS and airtime for latency-sensitive apps like VoWi‑Fi instead of increasing raw bandwidth only.
Check
Do your per-area capacity calculations include a realistic concurrency factor for peak hours?
