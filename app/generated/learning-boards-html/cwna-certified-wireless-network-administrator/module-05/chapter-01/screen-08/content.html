Requirements: Who, What, Where, When, Why, How
Use structured interviews and audits to collect measurable inputs.
Who
Users and device types: staff, guests, IoT, medical devices.
What
Applications and performance needs: EHR, VoWi‑Fi, video, file transfers.
Where
Critical spaces, outdoors, high-density zones that require special planning.
When
Peak hours, 24/7 needs, and maintenance windows that affect capacity and availability.
Why
Business drivers and compliance factors that drive stricter requirements.
How
Device onboarding model: managed, BYOD, or specialized medical equipment.
Mitigation
Avoid missing stakeholders by validating interviews with site walks and pilot tests that confirm usage assumptions.
