Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Clinic Scenario
Scenario Requirements
Convert scenario elements into at least three measurable requirements per category.
Coverage
  • RSSI ≥ -67 dBm in all exam rooms for VoWi‑Fi.
  • RSSI ≥ -70 dBm in waiting areas for video.
  • Seamless roaming with <50 ms handover where medical devices move between APs.
Capacity
  • Support 50 concurrent devices at peak with aggregate throughput documented.
  • Reserve QoS for EHR and VoWi‑Fi to guarantee low latency.
  • Design uplink capacity to match aggregate area throughput plus margin.
Reliability & Security
Define redundancy and access controls to support clinical operations.
Reliability
  • Controller redundancy with automatic failover.
  • Overlapping AP coverage in corridors and exam rooms.
  • Pilot tests to validate roaming and failover behavior.
Security
  • WPA3‑Enterprise or equivalent 802.1X for staff devices.
  • VLAN segmentation for medical devices, staff, and guests.
  • Guest SSID isolated with captive portal.
