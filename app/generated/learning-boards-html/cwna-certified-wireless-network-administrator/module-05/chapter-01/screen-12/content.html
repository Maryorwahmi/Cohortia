Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Validation
Validation timeline
Model → site survey → pilot → acceptance → phased rollout.
Model
Predictive planning and AP placement mockups.
Survey
Measure RSSI, SNR, and interference under real conditions.
Pilot
Test acceptance criteria under representative load and roaming scenarios.
Accept
Stakeholder sign-off when pilot meets documented metrics.
Key Advice
Document evidence from surveys and pilots and require acceptance criteria to be met before wide rollout.
