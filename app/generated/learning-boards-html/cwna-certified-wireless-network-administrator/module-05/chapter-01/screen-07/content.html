Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Security
Security built into the design
Authentication, encryption, segmentation, and management-plane controls.
Authentication
Enterprise authentication for staff devices provides stronger control over access and policy enforcement.
Segmentation
Use VLANs to separate guest, staff, and IoT traffic and define ACLs for resource access.
Guest Access
Provide a captive portal and isolate guest traffic from internal resources as a policy requirement.
Practical acceptance tests
  • Successful 802.1X login for staff devices and denied access for invalid credentials.
  • Guest SSID reaches internet but cannot reach internal resources.
  • Management interfaces protected and device firmware signed or controlled.
Key Takeaway
Write security requirements into the design and include tests to verify them during the pilot.
