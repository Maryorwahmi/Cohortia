Common Pitfalls and How to Mitigate Them
Practical checks to avoid rework and surprises.
Coverage-only design
Symptom: good RSSI but poor throughput in high-density areas. Mitigation: capacity calculations and more APs with reduced power.
Missing stakeholders
Symptom: unexpected device types after deployment. Mitigation: structured interviews and device inventories early on.
Undocumented assumptions
Symptom: disagreement on success criteria. Mitigation: include measurable acceptance criteria in the SOW.
Ignoring materials and density
Symptom: coverage gaps in concrete-walled areas. Mitigation: site surveys and attenuation modeling.
Action
Run a pilot and iterate until acceptance metrics are met under representative load and occupancy conditions.
