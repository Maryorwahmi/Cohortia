Environmental factors

  • IP-rating for dust and water resistance.
  • Temperature range and UV exposure.
  • Wind loading and mechanical stability.
Safety
Grounding, surge protection, and safe mounting procedures reduce risk of damage and injury.

Power options

  • PoE is standard; account for voltage drop over long cables.
  • Solar may be viable for remote sites but requires planning for availability and battery capacity.
Practical note

Treat outdoor deployments as multidisciplinary projects: RF, mechanical, and electrical considerations are all required for success.
