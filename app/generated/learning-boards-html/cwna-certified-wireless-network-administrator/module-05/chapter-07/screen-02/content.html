These objectives move from recognizing unique design constraints to applying calculations and producing safe, reliable outdoor links. Each milestone links to a competency you can demonstrate in real planning documents.

  • Translate a capacity need into AP counts and power settings.
  • Choose a deployment type that matches bandwidth and backhaul needs.
