High Density & Outdoor WLAN Design

Focus on capacity, RF boundaries, and environment-driven decisions. These screens give practical calculations, antenna guidance, and safety considerations used in real deployments.

In this lesson
Learn strategies for sizing cells, choosing antennas, and planning channels so many clients can be served simultaneously without catastrophic contention.
Coming up
Practical examples: a 300-seat lecture hall and a 500 meter point-to-point campus link, plus safety and mounting guidance for outdoor APs.
Key Idea

Design for capacity in dense crowds and for environment and safety outdoors; both demand different priorities than basic coverage.
