Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › What is a High-Density WLAN?
Core Concept

Capacity over coverage

High-density focuses on supporting many active clients by creating small, manageable cells and enabling channel reuse.

Explanation
  • Capacity is limited by airtime and management frames, not only signal strength.
  • Many clients increase retransmissions and contention without careful planning.
  • Human bodies absorb signal; crowds reduce effective coverage.
Key Takeaway

Design for spatial reuse: smaller cells and lower power improve overall capacity in crowded venues.

Concrete Example
Auditorium estimate
If 500 attendees each need 2 Mbps, plan for roughly 1 Gbps aggregate. This drives AP and backhaul choices, not just coverage.
Common Misconception

Stronger transmit power solves coverage, but in dense settings it increases co-channel interference and reduces capacity. Power control and more APs are the proper response.
