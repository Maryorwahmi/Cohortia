Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Cell Sizing & Power
Process Flow

Sizing steps

1. Estimate active clients
Count peak users and devices per user in the area you plan to serve.
2. Determine per-client throughput
Decide expected Mbps per active client to derive aggregate throughput.
3. Choose AP density and power
Pick AP counts and reduce transmit power to create smaller cells aligned with client groups.
4. Select antennas & mount
Apply directional or down-tilt antennas to limit overlap and improve isolation.
Pro Tip
Pair lower power with more APs and tight channel planning. Don’t add APs alone.
Example scenario
Section planning
If a section has 300 people and you allocate 10 active clients per AP, you need about 30 APs for that section. Adjust for antenna gain and mounting so coverage patterns match seating.
Common mistake

Adding APs without reducing power increases overlap and co-channel interference. Always pair density changes with power control.

Key takeaway

Design cells to carry a predictable number of clients by controlling power, antenna pattern, and mounting height.
