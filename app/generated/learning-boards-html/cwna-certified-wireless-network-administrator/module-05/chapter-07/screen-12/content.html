Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Outdoor Deployment Types
Point-to-Point
Dedicated bridge between two sites using highly directional antennas for long-distance, high-bandwidth links.
Point-to-Multipoint
A central hub with a sector antenna connecting multiple remote locations; good for aggregating remote clients to a single hub.
Mesh
Self-forming mesh nodes extend coverage without wired backhaul at every site but may reduce throughput due to wireless backhaul overhead.
Example decision

For two buildings 500 meters apart with clear sight, a point-to-point link with a directional dish is recommended to achieve high bandwidth and reliability without running fiber.
