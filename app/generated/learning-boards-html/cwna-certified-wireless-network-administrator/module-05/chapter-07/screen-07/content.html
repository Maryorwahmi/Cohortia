Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › AP Placement & Antennas
Placement patterns
  • Ceiling down-tilt to focus coverage into seating areas and reduce lateral bleed.
  • Under-seat APs to confine coverage for rows and mitigate reflection and crowd absorption.
  • Section-targeted directional antennas to aim into specific seating blocks.
Antenna choices
Omni for open areas, sector for targeted hub coverage, directional for long links or tight sections.
Concrete scenario
Use directional panels for seating sections and down-tilt ceiling APs for aisles. Under-seat APs are effective when the crowd absorbs signal and you want to reduce vertical propagation.
Common mistake

Ignoring antenna pattern lets signals bleed into adjacent cells, reducing channel reuse. Select patterns that confine energy where clients are located.

Key Takeaway

Placement and antennas are your tools to shape cells and preserve capacity in dense venues.
