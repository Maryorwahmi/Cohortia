Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Antennas, Deployments & Safety
Directional vs Omni
Directional antennas focus energy for links; omnidirectional deliver broad client access.
Deployment types
Pick PtP for bridges, PtMP for hubs, and mesh where wired backhaul is impractical.
Safety & grounding
Specify IP-rated hardware, grounding, surge protection, and safe mounting procedures.
Use these three checks when finalizing outdoor designs: antenna fit, topology fit, and safety fit. All three must pass before deployment.
