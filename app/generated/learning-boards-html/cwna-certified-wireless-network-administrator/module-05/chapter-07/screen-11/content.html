What is the Fresnel zone?

An elliptical region around the visual line-of-sight that radio energy uses. Obstructions within this region cause signal degradation.

  • Calculate Fresnel radius at midpoint for your frequency and distance.
  • Ensure a significant portion of the zone is free of vegetation or structures.
  • Adjust mounting heights to achieve clearance where needed.

Practical advice

Even when you have visual LoS, run a Fresnel clearance check. For mid-range links like a 500 meter campus bridge, small obstructions can significantly affect throughput if they intersect the Fresnel zone.
Common Mistake
Relying only on visual sight; failing to clear the Fresnel zone leads to degraded link performance.
