Band comparison

5 GHz: More non-overlapping channels and better isolation than 2.4 GHz.
6 GHz: Even more channels where available, ideal for dense reuse and reduced interference.
DFS: Adds channels but requires planning due to radar events and potential move operations.

Practical tips

  • Favor higher bands for capacity and reuse.
  • Combine band steering with power control to distribute clients.
  • Plan for DFS behavior and have fallback channel patterns.
Key Takeaway
Channels + power + antenna patterns together create effective reuse; channels alone are insufficient in dense spaces.
