Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Key Concepts
High-Density WLAN
A design that supports many clients in a small area by prioritizing capacity and reuse.
Cell Sizing
Reduce AP power to create smaller cells and enable spatial channel reuse.
Client Density
Number of active clients per area; used to calculate aggregate throughput needs.
Keep these concepts as quick checks when you make design decisions: capacity, reuse, Fresnel clearance, and outdoor safety are your primary drivers.
