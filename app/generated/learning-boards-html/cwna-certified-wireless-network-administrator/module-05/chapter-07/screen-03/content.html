On-the-job skills

These objectives become design decisions you communicate to stakeholders and implement in site surveys and RF plans.

Capacity estimates
Turn active clients and per-user Mbps into aggregate throughput and AP counts.
Hardware selection
Recommend AP models, IP ratings, and antenna types suited to the environment.
Safety & compliance
Document grounding, surge protection, and safe mounting for outdoor installations.

Assessment-ready

These outcomes prepare you for scenario-based questions and design tasks used in real assessments and projects.

  • Translate a scenario into AP counts and power settings.
  • Choose a deployment type and antenna for a specified distance and LoS.
  • Identify common mistakes and how to avoid them in the field.
