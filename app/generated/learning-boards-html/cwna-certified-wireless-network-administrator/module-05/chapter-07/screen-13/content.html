Antenna types

  • Omnidirectional: 360-degree access coverage for open spaces.
  • Directional: Yagi, panel, dish for focused long-range links.
  • Sector: Wider directional hub coverage for PtMP.

Mounting & safety

  • Choose height to achieve LoS and Fresnel clearance.
  • Design for wind loading and physical stability.
  • Implement grounding and surge protection per local codes.
Installer safety
Require fall protection and avoid installing near power lines. Document grounding and surge protection in the design deliverable.
