Module 6 › Chapter 5.7 › Common Mistakes

High-density errors

  • Adding APs without lowering power creates interference.
  • Designing to PHY rates rather than usable throughput under-provisions capacity.
  • Ignoring human-body loss when planning dense seating.

Outdoor errors

  • Failing to clear the Fresnel zone for long links.
  • Skipping IP-rating, grounding, or lightning protection.
  • Improper mounting height or ignoring wind loading and safety.
Correction
Use power control, conservative throughput numbers, site surveys for Fresnel, and proper outdoor protection to avoid these pitfalls.
