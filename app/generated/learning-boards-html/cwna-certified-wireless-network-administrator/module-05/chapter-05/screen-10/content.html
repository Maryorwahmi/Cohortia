Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Common Mistakes and Safety Notes
Common mistakes
  • No wired server for throughput testing.
  • Testing with non-production SSID or security.
  • Only testing download direction and missing upload problems.
  • Insufficient sample density or rushed walks.
Safety & logistics
Active testing consumes power. Bring a spare battery or portable power. Stay aware of your surroundings while walking and tracking live metrics.
Pro tip
Document AP settings, client configuration, and server addresses so you can reproduce tests after adjustments.
