Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Translating KPIs into Remediation Steps
Symptom → Likely fix
  • High retransmissions: investigate interference, reassign channels, or adjust AP placement.
  • Low throughput, low retransmissions: check AP capacity and wired network bottlenecks.
  • Sticky clients / high roaming latency: reduce AP transmit power or enable client steering.
  • High-density poor throughput: consider more APs, better antenna patterns, and cellular offload if appropriate.
Practical process
  1. Document baseline KPIs.
  2. Propose a single targeted change.
  3. Re-run active tests and compare results.
  4. Iterate until KPIs meet pass criteria.
Reminder
One change at a time preserves the ability to attribute improvements to a specific action.
