Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Retransmissions and Their Impact
What retransmissions tell you

Retransmissions indicate frames are being lost or corrupted on the air. They reduce usable bandwidth and increase jitter and latency for real-time apps.

Thresholds
Rates above 5–10% are significant and warrant investigation.
Causes
  • Interference from other devices
  • Incorrect channel assignment
  • High client density and contention
Example scenario

Collaboration pods showing 10–30 Mbps with retransmissions at 15–20% likely suffer from RF interference or too much contention. Users will experience stalls and poor video quality despite some measured signal strength.

Recommended actions
Investigate channels, reduce overlapping transmit power, increase AP density, or identify non‑Wi‑Fi interferers.
