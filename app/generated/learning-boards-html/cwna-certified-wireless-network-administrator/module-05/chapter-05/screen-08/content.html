Roaming latency

Time for a client to disassociate and reassociate. For VoIP, latency must be low to avoid audio drops during handoff.

Good: roaming within tens of milliseconds for seamless calls.
Problem: roaming in hundreds of milliseconds causing call glitches.
Sticky client behavior

Clients sometimes cling to a distant AP until the signal is very weak. That increases retransmissions and latency before roaming occurs.

Mitigations
  • Reduce AP transmit power to shrink cell size.
  • Enable controller-based client steering where available.
  • Verify placement to avoid large overlapping cells.
