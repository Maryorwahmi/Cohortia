Zone 1 — Main Work Area (Observed)
  • Download: 80–100 Mbps

Zone 2 — Collaboration Pods (Observed)
  • Download: 10–30 Mbps
  • Upload: 5–15 Mbps
  • Retransmission: 15–20%
  • Roaming latency: 150–200 ms
  • Client roaming: often sticky
Assessment
Fails high-density expectations. Requires interference analysis, AP tuning, and targeted re-tests.
