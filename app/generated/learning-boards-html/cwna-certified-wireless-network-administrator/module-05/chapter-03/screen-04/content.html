Core Concept

Predictive Site Survey

A model-based survey that predicts RF propagation using scaled floor plans and material attenuation.

Why use it?
  • Saves time by reducing physical trial-and-error.
  • Helps estimate AP count, placement, and power settings.
  • Highlights potential coverage gaps before installation.
Common misconception

The model isn't absolute truth; it reflects the inputs. Incorrect scaling or wrong material losses make results unreliable.

Concrete example

Using a scaled CAD plan and assigning a 12 dB loss for a concrete exterior wall, you can predict a coverage gap behind that wall and plan a second AP rather than increasing transmit power on the first.

Key Takeaway

Predictive surveys guide design decisions and reduce on-site rework, but only when scale and material inputs are accurate.
