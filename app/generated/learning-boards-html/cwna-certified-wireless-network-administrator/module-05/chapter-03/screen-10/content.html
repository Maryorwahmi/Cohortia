Core Concept

Channel planning essentials

Assign channels to minimize interference and preserve capacity in dense environments.

2.4 GHz

Prefer the three non-overlapping channels 1, 6 and 11 to avoid adjacent channel overlap in denser AP deployments.

5 GHz and 6 GHz

More channels exist, but ensure separation and be mindful of DFS channels which may require special handling.

Power & cells

When creating smaller cells for capacity, lower AP power and careful channel assignment help reduce co-channel interference.

Practical note

Let the tool suggest initial channels, then review channel reuse and DFS constraints before finalizing the design.

Key Takeaway

Channel planning is a necessary complement to AP placement; misconfigured channels can undo careful placement choices.
