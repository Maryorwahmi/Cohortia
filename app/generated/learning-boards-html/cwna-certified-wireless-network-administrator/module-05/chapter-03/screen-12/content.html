Process Flow

Iterative refinement of AP layout

Converge on a design by repeating placement, simulation, and adjustments.

Loop
  1. Place APs and set power.
  2. Generate RSSI, SNR and throughput heatmaps.
  3. Identify gaps, overlaps, and low-quality zones.
  4. Adjust placement, power, or add APs and repeat.
Practical rule

Prefer adding APs to cover blocked areas rather than solely raising power, and watch for over-provisioning that increases interference.

Documentation

Record final AP locations, power, and channel plan, and mark areas for physical verification after deployment.

Example

If an area behind a concrete wall shows red on the RSSI map, add a virtual AP on that side and re-run heatmaps to ensure coverage and acceptable SNR.

Key Takeaway

Iterate deliberately: small changes, re-simulate, and avoid the temptation to over-power or over-deploy APs.
