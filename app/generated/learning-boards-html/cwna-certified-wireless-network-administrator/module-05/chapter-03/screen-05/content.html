Process Flow

Import & scale the plan accurately

A reliable base map is essential for meaningful heatmaps and AP placement.

Step-by-step
  1. Obtain CAD or high-resolution image of the floor.
  2. Import into the survey tool and choose drawing units.
  3. Set scale by measuring a known dimension and entering its real length.
  4. Verify scale by checking at least two independent distances.
  5. Label important rooms and features for later capacity mapping.
Common mistakes
  • Assuming a drawing is already scaled without verification.
  • Using low-resolution images that hide small but important features.
Example check

If the plan shows an exterior wall labeled 20 meters, set that segment to 20 m in the tool. Then verify an interior room width or corridor length to confirm scaling consistency.

Key Takeaway

Scaling errors propagate to everything else. Verify at least two known distances before modeling materials or placing APs.
