Comparison

Coverage-driven vs Capacity-driven

Choose the strategy depending on whether your priority is area coverage or number of supported users and throughput.

Coverage-driven
  • Goal: minimum RSSI across the service area.
  • Fewer APs, higher power may suffice in open areas.
  • Validate with RSSI heatmap and SNR checks.
Capacity-driven
  • Goal: serve high client density with acceptable throughput per user.
  • Place APs closer together and reduce power to form smaller cells.
  • Use client density inputs and channel planning to avoid interference.
Example

A conference room expected to host 50 users should be capacity-designed: several APs, lower power, and careful channel planning rather than one high-power AP that only meets RSSI.

Key Takeaway

Decide whether coverage or capacity drives each area, then tailor AP count, placement, and power to that need.
