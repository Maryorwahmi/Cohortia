What to Remember

Placement Strategy

Choose coverage or capacity based on area needs and client density.

Heatmap Multiview

Always cross-check RSSI, SNR and throughput before declaring success.

Avoid Over-design

Adding APs without channel coordination can reduce overall performance; plan and verify.

Closing takeaway

Translate predictive outputs into an actionable deployment plan, then validate on-site to confirm the model's predictions.
