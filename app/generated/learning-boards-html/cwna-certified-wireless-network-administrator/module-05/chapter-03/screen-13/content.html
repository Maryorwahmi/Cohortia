Predictive Survey Plan for Small Office

Follow the steps to practice import, material definition, and virtual AP placement for a 20m × 10m office.

1. Floor Plan Import & Scaling
  1. Import CAD or image of the 20×10 office.
  2. Set scale using a 20 m exterior wall and verify using a 10 m room width.
2. Material Definition
  • Mark exterior walls as concrete, attenuation = 12 dB.
  • Mark interior partitions as drywall, attenuation = 4 dB.
3. Initial AP Placement

Place a 5 GHz 802.11ax AP with an omnidirectional pattern in each 10×10 room center. Generate RSSI heatmap and check for areas below -65 dBm.

4. Refine & Validate

If a red zone appears near the concrete wall, add an AP on that side or move the AP closer instead of increasing power. Run SNR and throughput maps to confirm performance.

Key Takeaway

Follow the import-scale-define-place-iterate cycle to turn the scenario into a validated predictive design you can test on-site.
