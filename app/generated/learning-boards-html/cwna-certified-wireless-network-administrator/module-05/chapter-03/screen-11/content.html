Core Concept

Heatmaps: RSSI, SNR, Throughput

Read multiple heatmap types together to understand predicted performance and user experience.

RSSI (Signal)

Shows predicted signal level; used to find coverage gaps relative to your RSSI floor.

SNR (Quality)

Indicates signal-to-noise ratio; low SNR means performance will suffer even if RSSI is acceptable.

Throughput

Estimates achievable data rates and validates capacity assumptions against client density targets.

Interpretation tip

Look for red zones showing poor RSSI or SNR, check whether they align with high-loss materials, and decide whether to move APs or add new ones to fix the problem.

Key Takeaway

Use heatmaps together — RSSI, SNR and throughput — to make balanced design decisions and avoid false confidence in signal strength alone.
