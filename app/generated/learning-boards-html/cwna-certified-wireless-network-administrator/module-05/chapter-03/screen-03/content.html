Practice & Interpretation

From placement strategy to reading heatmaps

Apply → Test → Refine
Coverage-driven
Meet a minimum RSSI target across space

Place APs to ensure the required signal floor, then confirm with RSSI heatmap. Useful for general-area coverage.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Capacity-driven
Design for user density and throughput

Place multiple APs with reduced power to create smaller cells and distribute client load in high-density rooms.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Channel planning
Avoid co-channel interference

Use non-overlapping 2.4 GHz channels and ensure separation and DFS awareness in 5 GHz to reduce interference impact.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Heatmap interpretation
Evaluate RSSI, SNR, and throughput together

Don’t rely on RSSI alone — low SNR or poor throughput indicate issues even when signal strength looks adequate.
