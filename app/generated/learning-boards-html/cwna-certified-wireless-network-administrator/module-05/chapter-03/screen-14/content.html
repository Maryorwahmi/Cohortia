Assessment & Pitfalls

Avoid these common errors

Use the following quick checks and the sample questions to validate your approach.

Top mistakes
  • Failing to scale the floor plan before placing APs.
  • Relying on RSSI alone and ignoring SNR or throughput.
  • Over-designing with too many APs and causing interference.
Quick assessments
  1. If a red zone appears behind a concrete wall, add another AP in the red zone rather than increasing transmit power.
  2. After importing a floor plan, the first critical step is to scale it using a known dimension so all distances are accurate.
Example explanation

Increasing transmit power to overcome a high-loss wall often increases interference and rarely solves the root cause. Adding a localized AP is the recommended solution in simulation and practice.

Key Takeaway

Use assessment items to test designs: scale first, then model materials, and prefer AP placement fixes over power increases for blocked zones.
