Key Concepts

Predictive Site Survey

A simulation using scaled plans and material losses to optimize AP placement before installation.

Floor Plan Scaling

Set scale with a known real-world dimension and verify with at least one other measurement.

Material Attenuation

Assign dB losses for drywall, concrete and metallic objects so the model reflects the built environment.

Summary

Accurate inputs lead to reliable predictive outputs; treat scaling and material mapping as the most important steps.
