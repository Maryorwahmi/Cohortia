Performing a Predictive Site Survey

Plan RF with confidence by importing plans, assigning material attenuation, placing virtual APs, and reading heatmaps to validate coverage and capacity.

Estimated time: 15 min demo
In this lesson
Floor plan scaling
Set accurate distances so heatmaps map to real meters.
Key topic
Material attenuation
Assign dB losses for drywall, concrete, glass and metallic obstructions.
Coming up
Virtual AP placement & heatmaps
Balance coverage and capacity with transmit power and cell size control.
Visual concept
Floor plan
Scaled CAD or image with walls and doors defined.
Materials
Assign attenuation values so simulations reflect reality.
Heatmaps
RSSI, SNR and throughput predict performance and guide tweaks.
Start by checking two known distances on your floor plan, then define walls with realistic attenuation and place virtual APs to meet RSSI and SNR targets. Iterate until coverage and capacity match requirements.
Quick checklist
  • Scale plan using known dimension(s).
  • Map materials and assign attenuation.
  • Place virtual APs for coverage and capacity.
  • Inspect RSSI, SNR, throughput heatmaps.
Why this matters

Accurate predictive surveys reduce costly physical rework and help you budget AP count and placement before procurement or installation.
