Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Drawing Walls and Obstacles
Process Flow

Draw structural elements and assign materials

Make the plan an RF-aware model by tracing walls and tagging materials.

Steps to follow
  1. Trace exterior walls and assign concrete where appropriate.
  2. Trace interior partitions and assign drywall or other material types.
  3. Add doors, windows, elevator shafts and large metallic objects.
  4. Label rooms for capacity planning and user expectations.
Validation

Run an initial simulation and look for sensible signal shadows and coverage patterns. Implausible results often point to scaling or material assignment errors.

Example

If a heavy metal shelving unit sits in a storage room, add it as a high-attenuation object rather than leaving it blank. The heatmap will then show a localized shadow where signal is reduced.

Key Takeaway

Careful drawing of obstacles translates directly to realistic heatmaps and correct AP placement decisions.
