Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › 2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz
2.4 GHz
Longer range, fewer non‑overlapping channels, more prone to co‑channel interference.
Use case
Simple coverage where clients are stationary and density is low.
Pitfall
Overloading 2.4 GHz with many APs and devices causes contention and poor performance.
5 GHz
More channels, higher capacity, less interference, but shorter range and weaker penetration.
Use case
Dense deployments, high throughput applications, and environments needing many non‑overlapping channels.
Design note
Expect to deploy more APs to achieve the same coverage as 2.4 GHz when using 5 GHz.
Key Takeaway

Select band strategy based on density, capacity needs, and physical environment; prefer 5 GHz when channels and capacity matter.
