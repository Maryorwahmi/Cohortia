Roaming steps
Walkthrough of the small sequence that preserves sessions across APs.
1. Client decision and scanning
Device measures signal and looks for a stronger AP or better channel.
2. Authentication and association
Device authenticates (if needed) and associates to the new AP, exchanging necessary management frames.
3. DS forwarding and session continuation
AP and DS forward traffic so the client's IP and application sessions continue with minimal interruption.
Key Takeaway

Roaming speed depends on authentication method, controller features, and correctly tuned overlap between APs.

Concrete example
During a call a phone weakens on AP‑A, scans, authenticates to AP‑B, and resumes voice packets—if the DS and controller preserved the session context, the user notices little to no audio gap.
Misconception
Same SSID equals roaming. In practice, correct DS topology and mobility settings are required to make roaming seamless.
