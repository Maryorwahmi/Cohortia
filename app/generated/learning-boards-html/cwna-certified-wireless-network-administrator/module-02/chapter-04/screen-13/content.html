Key Concepts — Part 1
BSS & BSSID
The basic 802.11 grouping; BSSID uniquely identifies each AP's BSS.
IBSS vs Infrastructure
IBSS is ad‑hoc peer mode; infrastructure relies on an AP for management and wired access.
ESS & DS
Multiple BSSs connected by a Distribution System to enable roaming and network access.
Takeaway
These core terms map directly to deployment choices and troubleshooting checks for mobility and scale.
