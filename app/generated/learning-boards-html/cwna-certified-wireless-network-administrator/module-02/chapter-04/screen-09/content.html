Placement & overlap
Balance overlap for roaming and minimize co‑channel interference.
Rule of thumb
Design enough overlap for a smooth handoff, but tune transmit power to avoid excessive contention.
Channel planning
Use available non‑overlapping channels and prioritize 5 GHz where capacity and channels are needed.
Common mistake
Using too few channels in 2.4 GHz leads to high co‑channel interference even with many APs.
Key Takeaway

Plan AP density, channel reuse, and power holistically to support capacity and roaming goals.

Concrete scenarios
Small office
Fewer users, lower mobility: fewer APs, focus on coverage and throughput to desks.
Large campus
High mobility: more APs, planned overlap, 5 GHz usage, and capable DS to support inter‑AP signaling.
