Module 2 › Chapter 2.4 › Design Scenario
Scenario A — Small Office
5–10 users, desks, internet and printer access, low mobility.
BSS Type
Single infrastructure BSS with one AP is suitable.
ESS Needed?
Not required unless coverage expands; an ESS becomes beneficial when more APs are added.
Roaming
Low priority; simple AP placement and single SSID are sufficient.
Scenario B — Large Campus
Thousands of users, high mobility, multiple buildings and services.
BSS Type
Multiple infrastructure BSSs, each served by APs wired to the DS.
ESS Necessity
Yes. ESS with robust DS supports roaming and central policy enforcement.
Roaming Importance
High. Prioritize overlapping coverage, 5 GHz capacity, and DS/controller performance.
