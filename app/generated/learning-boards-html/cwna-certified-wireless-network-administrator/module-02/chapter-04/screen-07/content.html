Distribution System — how frames travel
The DS is the backbone that transfers frames between APs and to the wired network.
Example walkthrough
  1. Client on AP‑A sends a frame to a client on AP‑B while roaming.
  2. AP‑A forwards the frame across the DS to AP‑B.
  3. AP‑B transmits the frame to the receiving client.
Design considerations
Capacity, VLAN consistency, QoS for voice/video, and preserving authentication state during handoffs.
Misconception
Assuming air carries inter‑AP traffic; the DS normally handles inter‑AP frames to keep the wireless medium for local access.
Topology cards
Wired DS
Most common. Ethernet switches and VLANs carry inter‑AP traffic.
Wireless mesh DS
Used where wiring is impractical; requires careful capacity planning.
Check

Can your planned DS carry peak inter‑AP traffic and preserve mobility and security contexts?
