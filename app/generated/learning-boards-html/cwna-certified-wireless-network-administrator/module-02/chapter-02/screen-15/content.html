Spread Spectrum
FHSS, DSSS, OFDM — choose by interference and multipath conditions.
MIMO & Beamforming
Multiple antennas improve throughput or reliability; beamforming focuses energy to clients.
Bands
2.4 GHz for range; 5/6 GHz for capacity and more channels.
Final takeaway

The PHY is a set of interlocking choices. Match modulation, spectrum method, antenna techniques, and band selection to client needs and the RF environment for reliable WLAN design.
