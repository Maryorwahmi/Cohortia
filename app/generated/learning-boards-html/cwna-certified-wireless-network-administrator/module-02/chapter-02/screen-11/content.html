Band comparison table
Key practical differences
2.4 GHz
Pros: better range and penetration. Cons: few non-overlapping channels and heavy interference from other devices.
5 GHz
Pros: many channels and less congestion. Cons: shorter range and poorer wall penetration than 2.4 GHz.
6 GHz (Wi‑Fi 6E)
Pros: very large clean spectrum, supports wide channels for high capacity. Cons: similar range limitations as 5 GHz and regulatory constraints.
Design guidance

Choose bands based on environment and client capabilities: use 2.4 for range or legacy devices, 5/6 GHz for capacity. Plan more APs when you rely on higher frequencies to cover an area.
