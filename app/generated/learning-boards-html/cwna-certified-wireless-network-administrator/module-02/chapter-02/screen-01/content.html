802.11 Physical Layer
Orientation: what the PHY does and why it controls throughput, range, and robustness.

The PHY encodes digital data into radio waves using modulation, spreads or subdivides spectrum to resist interference, and leverages antenna techniques to increase capacity. Understanding it helps you choose channels, shape coverage, and match client capabilities to network design.

Common misunderstandings include assuming higher modulation always improves real-world speed, and thinking more frequency always gives better range. Both are trade-offs you will learn to reason about.
