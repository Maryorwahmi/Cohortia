2.4 GHz scenario
Neighbors and RSSI readings
AP_A (neighbor)
Channel 1, RSSI -60 dBm (strong)
AP_B (neighbor)
Channel 6, RSSI -75 dBm
AP_C (neighbor)
Channel 11, RSSI -80 dBm (weak)
Evaluation
AP_D on channel 6 shares with AP_B, creating co-channel contention. AP_A on channel 1 is strong and would cause adjacent-channel interference if you pick 1. Channel 11 has the weakest neighbor energy and is the best choice to reduce interference.
Recommended Channel
Channel 11 — minimizes interference from stronger neighbors in this environment.
5 GHz advantage
More non-overlapping channels and less congestion, improving capacity and simplifying planning, at the cost of shorter range.
