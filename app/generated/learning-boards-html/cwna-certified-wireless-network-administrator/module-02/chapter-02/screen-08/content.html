How OFDMA improves efficiency
Partitioning subcarriers to serve multiple clients simultaneously
Benefit
Lower latency and better aggregate throughput in dense environments with many small flows.
Example
In a conference room many clients upload short telemetry; OFDMA packs those small transmissions efficiently into a single channel use.
Misconception
OFDMA is not primarily for peak single-client speed; it optimizes multi-user capacity and latency.
Check your understanding

When would OFDMA help most? Consider many clients with small packets versus a few clients with continuous large streams.
