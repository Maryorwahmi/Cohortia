Spatial streams vs. diversity
Two modes with different goals
Spatial multiplexing: multiple independent streams for higher throughput when channel supports it.
Spatial diversity: send the same data across antennas to reduce fading and increase reliability.
AP and client must support stream counts; a 3x3 AP with a 1x1 client cannot deliver three streams to that client.
Example

2x2 MIMO can yield near-double throughput in rich multipath. If the room has little multipath, the practical gain will be smaller and diversity may be favored.

Practical checklist
  • Confirm AP and client supported spatial streams.
  • Assess environment: multipath favors multiplexing.
  • Design placement to encourage favorable path differences for multiplexing gains.
Key Takeaway

MIMO can multiply throughput or improve reliability depending on whether you use spatial streams or diversity.
