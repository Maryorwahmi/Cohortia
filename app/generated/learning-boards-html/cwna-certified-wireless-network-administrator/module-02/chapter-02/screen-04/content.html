Definition and responsibilities
How the PHY shapes wireless behavior
  • Encodes bits using modulation and symbols.
  • Defines channel width, subcarrier structure, and timing.
  • Specifies frequency bands and regulatory considerations.
Key Takeaway

PHY decisions determine the realistic rate and range a client will experience under given RF conditions.

Concrete example
A client sees strong interference and its SNR drops. The PHY drops from 64-QAM to QPSK, which halves bits per symbol and reduces throughput even though RSSI looks acceptable.
Misconception
Higher modulation always means better user experience. Not true when SNR is low—higher modulation becomes unusable and throughput falls.
Pro tip
When troubleshooting, compare modulation rates and actual throughput rather than RSSI alone to find PHY limitations.
Check your understanding
If a client is close to an AP but shows low modulation, what PHY conditions should you inspect? Consider SNR, interference, and channel width.
