Practical Outcomes
How you will apply PHY knowledge in the field
Troubleshoot RF vs. Rates
Use PHY indicators like modulation and SNR to find whether to change AP placement or channel.
Infer Capacity from Modulation
Higher QAM means higher theoretical throughput but requires cleaner signal; watch for fallbacks.
Choose Bands & Channels
Balance range and capacity: 2.4 for reach, 5/6 for performance and channel availability.
Plan Antenna Use
Decide when to prioritize spatial streams for throughput or diversity for reliability.
Interpret Client Capabilities
Match AP features like MU‑MIMO or 256‑QAM to client support before assuming performance gains.
Regulatory Awareness
Check local power and channel rules when selecting 5 or 6 GHz channels to stay compliant.
