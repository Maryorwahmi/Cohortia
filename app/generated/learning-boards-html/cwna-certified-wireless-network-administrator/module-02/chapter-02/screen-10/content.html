Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Beamforming
What beamforming does
Focus energy to a client by controlling antenna phases
Explicit beamforming uses feedback from the client to compute optimal antenna weights.
Implicit beamforming estimates channel characteristics without explicit feedback, less precise but sometimes useful.
Beamforming improves SNR for targeted clients, enabling higher modulation or better range for them.
Operational notes
Confirm client and AP support. Expect focused benefit rather than network-wide improvements.
Example
A distant client gains usable 256‑QAM when beamforming raises its SNR, while nearby clients see no change.
Misconception
Beamforming is not a universal capacity multiplier; it improves targeted links when channel feedback or estimation is available.
