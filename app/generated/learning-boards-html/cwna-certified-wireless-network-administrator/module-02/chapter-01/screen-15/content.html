MIMO
Multiple antennas for spatial streams or diversity; introduced in 802.11n to increase capacity and reliability.
MU‑MIMO
AP can send to multiple clients simultaneously (downlink). Client support is required for benefits.
OFDMA & TWT
OFDMA improves multi-user efficiency; Target Wake Time saves client power. Both are key to Wi‑Fi 6’s value in dense environments.
Final takeaway

Match standard features to client capability and deployment density. The right feature in the wrong environment can reduce, not improve, performance.
