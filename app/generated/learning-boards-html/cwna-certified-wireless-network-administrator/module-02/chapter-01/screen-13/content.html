Diagnostic checklist
  • Inventory client capabilities and radio bands.
  • Check airtime utilization and protection frames.
  • Validate channel width and neighboring AP activity.
Example
A single 802.11b client can cause high airtime use; removing or isolating it often restores throughput for others.
Practical fixes
Segment legacy devices, tune channel widths for spatial reuse, and ensure AP features match client support before enabling advanced modes.
Key Takeaway
Targeted configuration and client strategy often yield better ROI than wholesale replacements.
