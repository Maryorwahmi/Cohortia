Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › 802.11ac — MU‑MIMO & Wide Channels
Key technologies
  • Wider channels up to 160 MHz
  • More spatial streams (up to 8)
  • MU‑MIMO for downlink to multiple clients
Use case
Streaming and large file transfers in environments with clean 5 GHz spectrum and MU‑MIMO capable clients.
Design notes
Confirm client MU‑MIMO support and avoid unnecessary 160 MHz bonding unless interference is minimal. Aggregate throughput improves when multiple clients can be served simultaneously.
Example
An AP sending three simultaneous downlink streams reduces per-client wait time compared with sequential single‑user transmissions.
Misconception
Assuming MU‑MIMO always helps; without client support the AP falls back to single-user behavior.
