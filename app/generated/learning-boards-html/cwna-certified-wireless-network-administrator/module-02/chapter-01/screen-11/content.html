Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Channel Planning Process
Step-by-step
  1. Survey spectrum and identify neighboring APs and interference.
  2. Inventory client bands and channel-width support.
  3. Select band (2.4/5/6) and suitable channel widths for each area.
  4. Validate with traffic tests and adjust for airtime and contention.
Example
In dense office floors, prefer multiple 20 MHz channels with good spatial reuse rather than a few 80 MHz channels that crowd neighbors.
Checks & Tools
  • Verify airtime utilization, not just throughput.
  • Look for non‑Wi‑Fi interference in 2.4 GHz like microwaves and Bluetooth.
  • Segment legacy clients when necessary to preserve modern network performance.
Key Takeaway
Channel choices must reflect density and client capabilities; measure under load to confirm success.
