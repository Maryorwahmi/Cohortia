MIMO explained
Multiple antennas enable spatial multiplexing (more bits per transmission) or diversity (better range/reliability). Client and AP antenna counts determine spatial streams.
Example
A 2x2 MIMO link with two spatial streams on 40 MHz can outperform a single-stream 20 MHz link by a large margin when clients support it.
Caution
40 MHz bonding in 2.4 GHz is rarely practical due to limited non-overlapping channels and increased interference risk.
Planning checklist
  • Inventory client antenna capabilities.
  • Map available channels and nearby networks before bonding.
  • Prefer 40 MHz bonding in 5 GHz where spectrum is freer.
Key Takeaway
MIMO and channel bonding raise capacity only when client support and spectrum conditions allow; otherwise they can backfire.
