Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › PHY & MAC Roles
Definition
Physical layer (PHY)
Defines modulation, subcarriers, channel widths, and radio band selection. Shapes raw achievable rates and interference characteristics.
Medium Access Control (MAC)
Coordinates who transmits and when, manages acknowledgements, and applies protection mechanisms so mixed clients can coexist.
Example
An OFDM PHY increases peak throughput, but an active 802.11b client will cause MAC protections that reduce overall throughput for everyone.
Annotated Diagram
PHY concepts
  • Modulation: DSSS vs OFDM
  • Channel width: 20/40/80/160 MHz
  • Frequency bands: 2.4, 5, 6 GHz
MAC concepts
  • Contention and backoff
  • Protection: RTS/CTS, CTS‑to‑self
  • Aggregation and ACK policies
Common mistake
Blaming peak PHY rates for low throughput without checking MAC-level protection and legacy clients is common. Always inspect client capabilities and MAC protection behavior.
Key Takeaway
Diagnose both layers: PHY sets potential, MAC sets realized performance.
