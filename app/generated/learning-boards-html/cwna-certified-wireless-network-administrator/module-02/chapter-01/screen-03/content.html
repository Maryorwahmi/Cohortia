Objective 5: Scenario analysis
Predict the user-visible impact of mixing legacy and modern clients, and propose configuration changes that reduce performance penalties.
06
Plan an upgrade
Prioritize capacity and client capability rather than maximum theoretical throughput.
07
Design channels
Choose channel widths and bands with interference and coexistence in mind.
Practical checks
  • Verify client support for new features before relying on them for capacity gains.
  • Avoid unnecessary 40/80/160 MHz bonding where it creates more interference than benefit.
  • Remember protection mechanisms can slow entire BSS when legacy clients are present.
Verified Competency Milestone
Write a short upgrade recommendation that lists three immediate benefits and two deployment considerations for moving from 802.11g to 802.11ax.
