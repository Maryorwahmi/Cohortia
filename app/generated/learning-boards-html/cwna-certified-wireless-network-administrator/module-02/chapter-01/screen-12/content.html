Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Upgrade Scenario: g → ax
Immediate benefits
  • Increased efficiency and capacity via OFDMA.
  • Better multi-client throughput with MU‑MIMO and resource allocation.
  • Improved battery life for mobile devices via Target Wake Time.
Example Result
Reduced latency and fewer retransmissions for video calls when clients support ax features.
Challenges & recommendations
  • Legacy clients may force protection mechanisms; consider separate SSIDs/VLANs.
  • Avoid blind channel bonding; plan spectrum for your environment.
Client Recommendation
Phase in Wi‑Fi 6 capable devices for core users, and move legacy devices to a separate network during transition to preserve performance.
