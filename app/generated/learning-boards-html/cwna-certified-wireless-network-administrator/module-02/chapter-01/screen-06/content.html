Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › 802.11g — OFDM in 2.4 GHz
What changed
802.11g combined the 2.4 GHz band with OFDM modulation, increasing theoretical speeds while allowing 802.11b devices to remain on the network.
Benefit
Better throughput with existing 2.4 GHz deployments and easy adoption due to backward compatibility.
Cost
Protection mechanisms add protocol overhead when legacy clients are present, reducing effective throughput.
Scenario
A mixed network with a few 802.11b devices will suffer slower average speeds for all 802.11g clients due to protection collisions and extra airtime.
Quick checks
  • Inventory client radios before upgrade.
  • Consider SSID segmentation for legacy devices.
  • Use 5 GHz where possible to avoid 2.4 GHz interference.
Key Takeaway
Backward compatibility can preserve devices but often lowers overall performance; plan accordingly.
