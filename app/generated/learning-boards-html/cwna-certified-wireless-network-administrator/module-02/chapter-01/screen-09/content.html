Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › 802.11ax — Efficiency Tools
OFDMA
Breaks a channel into resource units so multiple users can transmit simultaneously on different subcarriers. Reduces contention and improves latency under load.
BSS Coloring
Labels overlapping BSS frames so devices can reduce unnecessary deferral, improving spatial reuse.
Target Wake Time
Schedules when clients wake to send or receive, saving client battery life and reducing contention.
Design implications
  • Best gains appear in dense deployments with many active clients.
  • Client support for OFDMA and TWT is required to realize benefits.
  • 6 GHz band (Wi‑Fi 6E) extends capacity where available.
Key Takeaway
Wi‑Fi 6 improves how many users share spectrum; it is about efficiency under load, not simply raw peak speed.
