IEEE 802.11
The family of WLAN standards defining PHY and MAC operations across amendments.
802.11a
First major adopter of OFDM in 5 GHz, up to 54 Mbps under ideal conditions.
802.11b
Used DSSS in 2.4 GHz, up to 11 Mbps, widely adopted for coverage and low cost.
Summary

Keep amendment names tied to their defining technologies and expected trade-offs. That context yields better design choices than relying on peak numbers alone.
