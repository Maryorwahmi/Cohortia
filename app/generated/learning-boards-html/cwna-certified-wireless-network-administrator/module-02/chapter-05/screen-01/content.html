Interactive Concept
Topology snapshot
A quick view: ad-hoc is peer-to-peer, infrastructure uses APs to bridge clients to wired networks, mesh forwards across APs until a wired gateway is reached.
Design tip
Plan for centralized management when scale or roaming is required. Use mesh for hard-to-wire areas and expect careful backhaul planning.
Starter takeaway
Match topology to scale and physical constraints; select AP type to match management needs.
