Wireless Clients & Extension Devices
Understand client responsibilities and devices that extend or link networks.
Wireless Clients
Devices with wireless NICs that associate, authenticate, and exchange data with APs. Ensure NIC standards match AP capabilities.
Wireless Gateway
Combines AP, routing, NAT, and often a modem. Ideal for small offices and homes as a primary internet access point.
Wireless Bridge
Connects wired network segments over a wireless link at Layer 2; used for building-to-building connectivity in point-to-point or point-to-multipoint modes.
Key takeaway
Match device role to need: gateways for routing/NAT, bridges for transparent links, and ensure clients support required standards and security.
Use case example
Point-to-point bridge between two buildings to avoid new cabling while keeping the same VLANs and addressing across sites.
Misconception check
A gateway performs routing and address translation; a bridge simply forwards frames at Layer 2.
Quick checklist
  • Does the client NIC support required 802.11 standards?
  • Will a bridge preserve VLANs and Layer 2 design?
