Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Key Concepts
Access Point (AP)
Bridges wireless clients to wired networks; manages SSIDs, associations, and frame forwarding.
Wireless LAN Controller (WLC)
Central device that configures APs, enforces policies, and coordinates RF and roaming behavior.
Repeaters / Extenders
Extend wireless range but often reduce effective bandwidth because of retransmission on the same radio.
Final takeaway
Use the right component for the problem: coverage, backhaul, scale, or simplicity. Always consider security and management implications before deploying.
