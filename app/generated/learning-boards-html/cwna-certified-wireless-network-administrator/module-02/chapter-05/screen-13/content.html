Module 3 › Chapter 2.5 › Key Concepts
Infrastructure Topology
Clients connect via APs to the wired network; supports roaming and centralized policies.
Mesh Topology
APs forward traffic among themselves until reaching a wired gateway; use when cabling is difficult.
Ad-hoc (IBSS)
Direct peer-to-peer links with no AP; fast for temporary sharing, poor for scale or centralized security.
Summary takeaway
Match topology to physical and operational constraints. Use infrastructure for manageability, mesh for flexibility, and ad-hoc for quick peer tasks.
