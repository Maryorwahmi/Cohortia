Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
MAC Layer
Manages access and fairness on the shared wireless medium.
CSMA/CA
Avoids collisions using sensing, timing, and backoff rather than detection.
NAV
Virtual carrier sense that reserves airtime via duration fields.
RTS/CTS
Reserves the medium to mitigate hidden nodes, at the cost of extra frames.
MAC Overhead
Headers, ACKs, SIFS/DIFS, backoff, and control frames consume airtime and reduce throughput.
Access Methods
DCF is the mandatory contention method; PCF is optional and rarely used.
Final summary

Use CSMA/CA, NAV, and selective RTS/CTS to coordinate access. Always measure airtime and throughput before changing MAC parameters.
