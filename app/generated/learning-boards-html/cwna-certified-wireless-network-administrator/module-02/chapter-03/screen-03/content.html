Demonstration and hands‑on goals

These skills support capture analysis and safe configuration changes related to MAC behavior.

01
Identify RTS/CTS and NAV patterns

Locate control frames and duration fields in captures and explain their role in reserving airtime.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Estimate airtime from durations

Use duration fields and known SIFS values to approximate how long a sequence will hold the medium.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use RTS/CTS selectively

Enable RTS/CTS where hidden nodes or large frames justify the overhead; avoid universal activation without evidence.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Measure before and after

Change one MAC parameter at a time and measure impact to avoid unintended performance loss.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
