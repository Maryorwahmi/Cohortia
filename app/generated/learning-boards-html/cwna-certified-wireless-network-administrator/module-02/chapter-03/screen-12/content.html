Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › Frame Capture Analysis
Observed sequence
RTS → SIFS → CTS → SIFS → Data → SIFS → ACK
Interpretation
RTS/CTS indicates the medium was deliberately reserved; NAV values on listeners reflect the duration and prevent other transmissions.
Simplified duration calculation

Given RTS duration = 500 µs and SIFS = 10 µs, the sequence includes three SIFS gaps. Subtract 3 × 10 µs = 30 µs from 500 µs to reserve time for CTS + Data + ACK ≈ 470 µs. Removing small CTS and ACK portions leaves the Data frame as the primary airtime consumer.

Check your understanding
Did the RTS/CTS exchange prevent hidden node collisions here? If NAV timers were respected, yes — surrounding stations would defer for the listed duration.
