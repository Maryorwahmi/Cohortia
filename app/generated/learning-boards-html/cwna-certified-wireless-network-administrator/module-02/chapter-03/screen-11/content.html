Module 2 › Chapter 2.3 › MAC Overheads
Common MAC overheads
  • MAC headers and control fields.
  • Interframe spaces (SIFS, DIFS) and random backoff times.
  • ACK frames and control exchanges like RTS/CTS.
  • Retransmissions due to collisions or errors.
Measured effect
A theoretical 54 Mbps PHY can yield roughly 20–25 Mbps to applications once MAC overheads and timing are considered.
Optimizing airtime

Avoid universal RTS/CTS, tune fragmentation and RTS thresholds only with evidence, reduce retransmits by improving signal quality, and measure airtime usage before and after changes.

Key Takeaway

Throughput depends on airtime used by non‑data frames; balance protection mechanisms against their airtime cost.
