Process: How traffic flows
From client association to network delivery via AP and DS.
1.
Client associates to SSID and authenticates if required.
2.
AP handles radio, applies VLAN or security policy, and forwards frames to DS.
3.
DS transports traffic to servers or internet gateway; controller may log or optimize flows.
Key Takeaway

Understanding the flow lets you isolate whether issues are radio, AP, DS, or controller related.

Component cards
Wireless LAN Controller
Central policy, firmware, and coordinated RF optimization for many APs.
Distribution System (DS)
Wired network that interconnects APs and carries client traffic to services.
Bridge / Mesh / Extender
Optional components for linking or extending coverage with performance tradeoffs.
Scenario example
A client on "OfficeWiFi" authenticates, the AP tags its traffic with a VLAN, and the DS routes it to the corporate server farm or internet gateway; if a controller exists it records the association and applies RF policies.
