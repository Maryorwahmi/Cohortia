Wireless LAN Topologies and Components
How devices, identifiers, and AP architectures form practical Wi‑Fi networks
In this lesson
  • Contrast Ad‑Hoc and Infrastructure modes and their tradeoffs.
  • Define BSS, ESS, SSID, and BSSID with concrete examples.
  • Compare standalone, controller, and cloud AP architectures for scale and management.
Why this matters

Design choices determine coverage, roaming behavior, and operational workload. Good topology and component selection prevents slow Wi‑Fi and frequent drops.
