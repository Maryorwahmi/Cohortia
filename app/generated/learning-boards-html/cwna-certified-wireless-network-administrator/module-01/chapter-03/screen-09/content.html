Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Bridges, Repeaters, Mesh
Wireless Bridge
Connects two wired network segments wirelessly for a dedicated link.
Example: linking two buildings across an outdoor span when cabling is impractical.
Repeater / Extender
Receives and retransmits Wi‑Fi to extend range, often at performance cost.
Note: extenders can halve effective throughput and increase latency; avoid for performance‑critical traffic.
Wireless Mesh
Self‑configuring APs that route traffic among themselves to extend coverage without wired backhaul.
Mesh is useful in difficult cabling environments but requires planning for hop count and overall throughput.
Rule of thumb

Prefer wired backhaul for production traffic; use wireless bridging or mesh when cabling is impossible.
