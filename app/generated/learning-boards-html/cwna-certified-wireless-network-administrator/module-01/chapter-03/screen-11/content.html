Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Architecture Comparison
Standalone
Best for 1–5 APs with low management needs.
Pros: simple, low upfront. Cons: manual upgrades, inconsistent policies at scale.
Controller‑Based
Best for large campus or enterprise deployments.
Pros: centralized control, RF optimization. Cons: controller hardware or redundancy planning.
Cloud‑Managed
Best for distributed sites needing central policy without on‑prem controllers.
Pros: unified management across locations. Cons: dependence on cloud service and internet connectivity for management.
Practical checklist
Count APs, estimate management effort, list required features like guest access or RF coordination, and evaluate whether a cloud provider fits your compliance and connectivity needs.
