Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Ad‑Hoc (IBSS)
Peer‑to‑peer wireless between devices without an AP.
BSS & BSSID
A BSS is one AP plus clients; BSSID is the AP radio MAC that identifies it.
ESS & Roaming
Multiple BSSs sharing an SSID on a common wired DS to enable roaming.
Takeaway

Match topology and AP architecture to scale and management needs, and plan placement and channels to avoid poor performance.
