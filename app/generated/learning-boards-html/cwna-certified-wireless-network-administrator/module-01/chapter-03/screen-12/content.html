Common mistakes
  • Too few APs for client density, causing contention and slow speeds.
  • Poor AP placement that creates dead zones or excessive reflections.
  • Neglecting channel planning in 2.4 GHz, leading to co‑channel interference.
Practical steps
Plan density based on expected users and applications, prefer 5 GHz where possible, and perform site surveys or simple walk tests to validate placement.
Visual: Planning checklist
Assess clients
Estimate client density and application needs like voice or video.
Plan channels
Assign non‑overlapping channels and prefer 5 GHz for capacity.
Validate
Walk the space to check coverage, client experience, and adjust power and placement.
Misconception check

Don't assume boosting transmit power fixes coverage; it often increases interference and worsens roaming.
