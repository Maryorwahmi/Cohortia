What is an SSID?
A broadcast name clients use to select a wireless network.

An AP can advertise multiple SSIDs. Each SSID may be mapped to a different VLAN and security policy.

Example: "CorpNet" → internal VLAN with strong authentication; "Guest" → isolated guest VLAN with captive portal.

Misconception: SSID does not uniquely identify physical location; use BSSID for that purpose.

Why it matters

SSIDs control user experience and security boundaries; mapping them carefully prevents accidental access to internal resources.

Visual: SSID mapping examples
CorpNet
WPA3, mapped to internal VLAN, restricted access to servers.
Guest
Open or captive portal, isolated VLAN, internet‑only access.
IoT
Separate SSID for devices with weaker security, limited to specific services.
Key Takeaway

Use SSIDs to shape access and policy, but rely on BSSID and inventory for physical troubleshooting.
