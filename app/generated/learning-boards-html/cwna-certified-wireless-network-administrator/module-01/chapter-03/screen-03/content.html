Verification card: Identify topology
Quick checks and hints to confirm Ad‑Hoc or Infrastructure mode.
  • Look for an SSID broadcast and an AP MAC (BSSID). If present, you are in Infrastructure mode.
  • Ad‑Hoc networks often appear as peer connections without an AP managing DHCP or routing.
  • Example: Two laptops directly sharing files without a router is Ad‑Hoc.
Verification card: Classify AP
How to tell standalone from controller or cloud-managed APs.
  • Check the AP’s admin UI for controller or cloud settings; standalone APs are configured locally.
  • Controller‑based deployments show a central controller managing multiple APs.
  • Cloud‑managed solutions show a cloud service URL or management portal.
Reflection
Record what you find: SSID, BSSID, whether multiple APs share the SSID, and the AP management model. These observations tie directly back to topology and architecture choices.
