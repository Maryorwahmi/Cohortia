Definition: BSS
A Basic Service Set is a single AP and its associated clients; the BSSID identifies that BSS.

Remember that BSSID is normally the MAC address of the AP radio. Use it to identify the serving AP during troubleshooting.

Example: Seeing a particular BSSID lets you map a client to a physical AP and check signal strength and load on that radio.

Pro tip

When several APs share the same SSID, the BSSID is the reliable identifier to locate a client.
