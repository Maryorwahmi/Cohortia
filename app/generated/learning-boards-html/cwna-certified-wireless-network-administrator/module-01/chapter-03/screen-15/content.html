Module 1 › Chapter 1.3 › Final Concepts
Access Point
The radio bridge connecting clients to the wired network; architecture affects management.
SSID vs BSSID
SSID is the network name; BSSID is the AP radio MAC used to identify the serving BSS.
Extensions & Backhaul
Prefer wired backhaul; use bridges for dedicated links and mesh only when cabling is impractical.
Quick checklist

Identify SSID/BSSID, classify AP architecture, and check AP placement and channel planning before recommending changes.
