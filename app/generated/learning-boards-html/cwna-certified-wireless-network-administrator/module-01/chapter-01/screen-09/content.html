MIMO explained
Multiple antennas form multiple spatial streams; under the right conditions each stream carries independent data, increasing throughput.
Spatial streams
More antennas can produce up to N× throughput, but real gains depend on environment.
Channel bonding
Combining adjacent channels (20 MHz → 40 MHz) increases capacity when spectrum allows.
Concrete example
A 2x2 MIMO AP with channel bonding can deliver much higher nominal throughput than single‑antenna designs, but in a crowded 2.4 GHz band bonding may cause interference to neighbors.
Misconception
Assuming maximum spatial streams will always be used—clients and environment determine effective streams.
Key Takeaway
MIMO and bonding can greatly increase throughput, but enable them thoughtfully based on spectrum and client capabilities.
