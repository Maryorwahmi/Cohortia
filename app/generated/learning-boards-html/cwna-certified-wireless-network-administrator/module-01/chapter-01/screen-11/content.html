Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › 802.11ax (Wi‑Fi 6)
Core features
  • OFDMA for subcarrier resource allocation across many clients.
  • Improved MU‑MIMO, both uplink and downlink.
  • Target Wake Time for power savings and 1024‑QAM for higher rates.
Practical example
In a dense office where dozens of clients exchange small bursts of data, OFDMA allows the AP to allocate small resource units to many clients at once, reducing contention and improving latency compared to serving clients serially.
Misconception
Thinking Wi‑Fi 6 always yields higher single‑client throughput; its primary benefit is capacity and efficiency in multi‑user situations.
Key Takeaway
Use Wi‑Fi 6 where many clients demand responsive, efficient airtime management; it improves real‑world capacity more than raw peak speed for one device.
