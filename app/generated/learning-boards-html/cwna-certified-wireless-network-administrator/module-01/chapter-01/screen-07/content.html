Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › 802.11a vs 802.11b
802.11a (5 GHz, OFDM)
  • Up to 54 Mbps using OFDM.
  • Higher frequency → shorter wavelength → reduced penetration and range.
  • Better in environments with many channels and fewer obstacles.
802.11b (2.4 GHz, DSSS)
  • Up to 11 Mbps using DSSS.
  • Lower frequency → longer wavelength → better penetration and range.
  • Often preferred where coverage through obstacles is important.
Example
In a multiroom building, 2.4 GHz equipment may give fewer dead zones. In a dense open office, 5 GHz devices can exploit more channels for higher throughput.
Key Takeaway
Choose by environment: penetration (2.4 GHz) vs available spectrum and channel width (5 GHz).
