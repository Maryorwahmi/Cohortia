Timeline: Key milestones
1997 — 802.11
Initial standard, 1–2 Mbps, set foundation for future amendments.
1999 — 802.11a & 11b
a: 5 GHz, OFDM; b: 2.4 GHz, DSSS. Different tradeoffs in range and speed.
2003 — 802.11g
Combined OFDM speed with 2.4 GHz compatibility.
2009 — 802.11n
Introduced MIMO and channel bonding across 2.4 & 5 GHz.
2014 — 802.11ac
Wider channels, more spatial streams, MU‑MIMO downlink.
2019 — 802.11ax
Efficiency for dense environments: OFDMA, improved MU‑MIMO, TWT, higher QAM.
Practical mapping
Map OFDM to 802.11a/g/n/ac/ax, MIMO to n and beyond, MU‑MIMO to ac/ax, and OFDMA to ax. This mapping explains how standards addressed specific challenges.
