Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › 802.11ac
What 802.11ac added
  • Wider channels up to 160 MHz.
  • More spatial streams for higher throughput.
  • MU‑MIMO for downlink multi‑client transmission.
Operational note
160 MHz channels require contiguous spectrum and are rare. MU‑MIMO needs client support and mainly benefits aggregate capacity, not a single client’s peak rate.
Misconception
Believing MU‑MIMO increases a single client’s maximum throughput rather than serving multiple clients at once.
Key Takeaway
802.11ac raises aggregate capacity with wider channels and MU‑MIMO, but spectrum and client support determine real gains.
