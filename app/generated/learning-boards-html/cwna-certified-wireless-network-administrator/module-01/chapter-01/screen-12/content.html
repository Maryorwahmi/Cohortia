Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Regulatory Bodies
Who does what
  • FCC: US spectrum allocation and device certification.
  • ETSI: European technical standards and regional rules.
  • ITU: Global coordination to harmonize spectrum use.
Deployment checklist
Confirm device certification, set regulatory domain in APs, and verify allowed channel and power settings for the region.
Misconception
Assuming devices will always auto‑detect the correct regulatory domain—administrators must verify settings and certifications.
Key Takeaway
Always verify regional certification and configure regulatory domain to avoid interference and legal issues.
