Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Upgrade Decision
Decision steps
1. Define the problem
Coverage, peak throughput, or capacity?
2. Check spectrum
Are wide contiguous channels available in 5 GHz or 6 GHz?
3. Verify clients
Do clients support ac or ax features needed to benefit?
Example: dense office
Choose 802.11ax for OFDMA and improved MU‑MIMO to reduce contention and improve latency across many clients.
Example: small studio
If a few clients need very high throughput, 802.11ac with wide channels and many spatial streams may suffice if spectrum is available.
Key Takeaway
A systematic decision flow—goal, spectrum, clients, compliance—prevents costly misconfiguration and ensures upgrades deliver real benefits.
