Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › 802.11g
What 802.11g did
Brought OFDM speed to the 2.4 GHz band, enabling up to 54 Mbps while supporting 802.11b devices.
Benefit
Faster rates without abandoning existing 2.4 GHz devices.
Tradeoff
Legacy clients can force protection mechanisms that add overhead.
Deployment note
In mixed networks, monitor airtime use and consider separate SSIDs or 5 GHz encouragement to preserve performance for capable clients.
Misconception
Assuming new clients automatically get full benefit regardless of legacy traffic on the same channel.
Key Takeaway
Compatibility helps adoption but can reduce efficiency; manage legacy clients to protect capacity.
