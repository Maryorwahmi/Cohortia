Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Quick Reference
Fresnel
Keep at least 60% of the first Fresnel Zone clear for dependable links.
Pattern & Gain
Pick pattern and dBi to match client spread; higher dBi narrows the beam.
Polarization
Match polarization or use dual-polarized antennas to avoid large signal loss.
Final quick tip
Measure after install. Adjust alignment and height based on real signal measurements at intended client positions to confirm the theoretical choices.
