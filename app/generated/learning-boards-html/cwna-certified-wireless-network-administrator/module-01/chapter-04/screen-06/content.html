Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Directional Antennas
Definition

Yagi, Patch, Sector

These directional types concentrate RF energy differently: narrow beam, flat wide beam, or fixed angular sector. Each has deployment patterns and trade-offs worth knowing.

Yagi
Very narrow beam, high gain, used for long point‑to‑point links.
Patch / Panel
Flat panel with wider directional beam, good for covering a corridor or section.
Sector
Covers a specified angular sector; used in point‑to‑multipoint deployments.
Scenario Match
Select antenna type by listing coverage angle and required reach. Example: two buildings 1 km apart with narrow corridor-like sightlines → Yagi. Stadium outer seating sections → sector or multiple patch panels.
Common mistake
Using a single antenna type as a one-size-fits-all solution rather than matching shape to client distribution.
Key Takeaway

Identify your desired coverage angle and range first; that drives whether you choose Yagi, patch, or sector antennas.
