Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Polarization
Definition

Polarization orientation

Orientation of the electric field affects how well antennas couple. Matching polarization maximizes received energy; mismatch creates loss.

Concrete example
A vertically polarized AP paired with a horizontally mounted client antenna will suffer significant loss even with LOS.
Mitigation
Use dual-polarized antennas or ensure orientation matches during installation.
Practical checklist
  • Confirm antenna orientation before finalizing mounts.
  • Prefer dual-polarized antennas where client orientation may change.
  • Remember that reflections can alter polarization; match where possible and test on-site.
Common misconception
Some assume polarization only matters for long links — it matters for any link where coupling efficiency affects throughput.
