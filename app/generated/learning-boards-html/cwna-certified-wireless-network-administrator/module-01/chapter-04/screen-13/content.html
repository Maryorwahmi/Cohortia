Scenario

2 km bridge with small intrusions

Visual LOS is present but trees and a small hill intrude around the direct path. The key concept is the Fresnel Zone, which can be obstructed even when LOS looks clear.

Recommended actions
Raise antenna height to clear Fresnel, reroute the path, or consider site alternatives rather than simply increasing transmit power.
Common mistake
Assuming more transmit power fixes Fresnel diffraction — it does not correct obstruction-induced diffraction and may violate regulations.
Practical checklist
  • Measure or estimate Fresnel Zone intrusion at mid-path.
  • Plan to restore at least 60% clearance; 80% if possible.
  • Document proposed physical changes and test link metrics after adjustments.
Key Takeaway
Fresnel analysis and physical mitigation trump raw power increases for restoring long-distance link reliability.
