Learning Objectives

Develop practical skills to choose and place antennas and to reason about RF propagation in outdoor and indoor Wi‑Fi links.

Bloom Progress
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain Antenna Function
Describe how an antenna converts electrical energy and why placement affects efficiency.
Verified competency milestone
02
Differentiate Antenna Types
Match omni, Yagi, patch, and sector antennas to deployment scenarios with rationale.
Verified competency milestone
03
Understand Antenna Gain
Explain dBi and dBd as pattern measurements and how gain affects effective range.
Verified competency milestone
04
Analyze Propagation Effects
Assess LOS, Fresnel clearance, polarization, and multipath; recommend mitigations.
Verified competency milestone
Mnemonic
Think: CONVERT → PATTERN → GAIN → PROPAGATE. Convert energy, choose a pattern, read gain numbers, then check propagation obstacles like Fresnel and multipath.
Why these skills matter
Accurate antenna selection and placement prevent coverage gaps, reduce rework, and make outdoor bridges reliable. These skills save time and protect equipment.
Quick check
Can you sketch a donut-shaped pattern and label where a sector antenna would be preferred? If so, you are on track.
