Process

Plan → Install → Verify

  1. Map user locations and link endpoints to choose antenna pattern.
  2. Verify LOS and Fresnel clearance; plan for seasonal obstructions.
  3. Install antenna with proper mounts, grounding, and lightning protection.
  4. Measure signal at real client positions and adjust alignment as needed.
Safety and operational notes
Always include grounding and surge protection for outdoor antennas. During alignment avoid standing in the beam of a high-gain directional antenna for prolonged periods. After storms, validate mounting hardware and connections.
Common mistake
Skipping surge protection to save cost often leads to equipment loss and larger replacement expense after a lightning event.
