Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Key Concepts
Antenna
A transducer that converts between electrical signals and RF waves; design and placement determine how energy is radiated.
Gain (dBi/dBd)
Gain describes pattern focus relative to a reference. Higher gain narrows beamwidth and increases effective range in that direction.
Propagation
LOS, Fresnel clearance, polarization, and multipath determine link quality; account for them in planning and testing.
Final summary
Select antennas by matching pattern to users or link geometry, read gain as beam focus, and always verify propagation factors such as Fresnel clearance and polarization during site surveys.
