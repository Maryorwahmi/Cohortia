Core Concept

Multipath explained

Reflections create multiple signal copies that may interfere constructively or destructively. The timing differences determine impact on the receiver.

Concrete example
An indoor AP with glass and metal surfaces will see reflections that create dead spots at certain locations due to destructive interference.
Opportunity
MIMO uses multiple antennas to receive distinct paths and combine them to improve data rates and reliability.
Mitigation checklist
  • Relocate or tilt antennas to reduce strong reflections toward reflective surfaces.
  • Choose antenna types that limit energy toward large reflectors.
  • Use MIMO-capable equipment to exploit beneficial multipath when available.
Key Takeaway

Multipath is both a challenge and a tool: control the environment and pick equipment that turns reflections into improved reliability where possible.
