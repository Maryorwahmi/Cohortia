Core Concept

Antenna as a transducer

An antenna converts electrical energy into electromagnetic waves and vice versa. Its design, resonance, and placement determine conversion efficiency.

Example
A dipole at resonance converts energy efficiently; near-metal placement detunes it and reduces efficiency.
Common misconception
Antennas do not amplify power. High gain means a shaped pattern, not added energy.
Key Takeaway

An antenna's role is to shape and transfer energy between guided circuits and free space; choose and place antennas to match coverage goals.

Scenario
A router placed in a corner wastes radiated energy into walls. Centering the device improves usable coverage because the transducer's pattern then reaches users more evenly.
Pro tip
Check for nearby metal or large objects that can detune an antenna and change its effective pattern before assuming the antenna itself is faulty.
