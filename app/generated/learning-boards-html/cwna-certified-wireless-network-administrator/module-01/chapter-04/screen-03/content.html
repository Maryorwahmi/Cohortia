Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Learning Objectives
Objective 05
Sketch and Compare Patterns
Reproduce a donut pattern for omni, a flattened wide beam for patch, and a narrow focused beam for Yagi, then explain coverage trade-offs for each scenario.
Mnemonic
Donut, Plate, Arrow — Omni, Patch, Yagi.
Objective 06
Evaluate LOS and Fresnel
Assess visible LOS and check for Fresnel Zone intrusions; recommend raising antennas or changing route for better clearance.
Milestone
You can propose one corrective action when Fresnel intrusion is identified.
Objective 07
Align Polarization
Explain the effect of vertical vs horizontal polarization and why matching orientation is required to avoid large losses.
Objective 08
Mitigate Multipath
Identify multipath causes and recommend mitigations: antenna repositioning, type changes, or leveraging MIMO equipment.
Verification
Each objective links to a small hands-on task in the activity: sketches, site checks, and scenario matching to demonstrate competency.
