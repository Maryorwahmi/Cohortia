Comparison

Omnidirectional

Donut-shaped horizontal coverage, useful where clients may be anywhere around the AP. Energy is spread, so per-direction range is modest.

Example: Home router antennas provide 360° coverage in a living area.
Common mistake: Using omni for a narrow corridor wastes energy and causes weak signal at ends.
Comparison

Directional

Focuses energy into a beam for longer range or targeted coverage. Use Yagi for long point-to-point links, patch for area sections, and sector for defined angular coverage.

Example: A Yagi provides long-range connectivity between two buildings.
Common mistake: Using a narrow beam where clients are distributed around the AP causes coverage holes.
Pattern cards
Donut (Omni)
Wide horizontal coverage, mild gain.
Flat Beam (Patch)
Wider directional coverage for area sections.
Narrow Beam (Yagi)
High directionality for long-distance links.
Key Takeaway

Match the antenna pattern to the spatial distribution of clients or the link orientation to avoid wasted energy or coverage holes.
