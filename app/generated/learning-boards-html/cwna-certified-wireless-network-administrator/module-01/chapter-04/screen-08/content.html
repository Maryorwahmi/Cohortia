Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › LOS & Fresnel Zone
Process Flow

Survey → Identify → Adjust

  1. Survey visual Line of Sight along the intended link path.
  2. Identify intrusions into the first Fresnel Zone such as trees or terrain.
  3. Adjust antenna height, route, or choose different frequencies to increase clearance.
  4. Re-check with seasonal foliage and plan for maintenance access.
Example
A 2 km bridge with small hill intruding into the path may suffer diffraction loss even with visual LOS. Raising antenna height often restores necessary Fresnel clearance.
Common errors & mitigations
  • Assuming visual LOS equals good RF path — check Fresnel instead.
  • Ignoring seasonal foliage — leaves can intrude into Fresnel and reduce link quality.
  • Mitigation: raise antenna height, re-route the path, or reduce frequency to change Fresnel size.
Pro tip
Always plan for at least 60% Fresnel clearance; 80% is ideal for reliable throughput on outdoor links.
