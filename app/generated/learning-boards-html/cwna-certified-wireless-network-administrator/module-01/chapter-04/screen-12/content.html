Module 2 › Chapter 1.4 › Activity
Instructions
  1. Draw an omnidirectional donut, a patch flattened beam, and a Yagi narrow beam.
  2. For each scenario, pick the antenna type and justify your choice concisely.
  3. For the point‑to‑point bridge, identify potential Fresnel intrusions and propose one corrective action.
  4. State polarization choice for each scenario and whether dual polarization is preferred.
Scenarios
- Open-plan office: omnidirectional to cover users around an AP.
- Long corridor: patch or panel to shape coverage along the corridor.
- 1 km bridge: Yagi for narrow, long-range beam; check Fresnel clearance and raise height if intrusions exist.
- Outdoor seating: patch for area coverage with moderate range.
- Stadium section: sector antennas combined to cover angular slices.
Reflection
After sketching, compare your choices with actual antenna datasheets to confirm beamwidth and gain match your scenario assumptions.
