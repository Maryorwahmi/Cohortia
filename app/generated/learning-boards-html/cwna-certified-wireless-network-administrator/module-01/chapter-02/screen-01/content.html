Radio Frequency (RF) Fundamentals

Measure power, read antenna gain, predict coverage, and identify interference sources that affect Wi‑Fi.

In this lesson

Understand dB/dBm, dBi/dBd, ISM bands, propagation effects (absorption, reflection, diffraction), interference types and a hands‑on link budget example.

Why it matters

These fundamentals let you predict coverage gaps, choose channels, and diagnose problems without guessing.
