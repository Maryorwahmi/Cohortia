Decibel (dB)
Logarithmic unit expressing a ratio of power or gain.
dBi
Antenna gain relative to an ideal isotropic radiator.
dBm
Absolute power referenced to 1 milliwatt.
Keep these three terms clear: ratio versus absolute, and antenna reference points. They are essential for accurate link budgets and equipment comparison.
