Process Flow › Mitigation Strategies
Measure → Analyze → Plan → Implement → Verify
  • Measure: RSSI, retry rates, spectrum scans.
  • Analyze: classify as CCI, ACI, or non‑Wi‑Fi.
  • Plan: channel shifts, placement, power tuning, AP density changes.
  • Implement: apply changes and monitor.
  • Verify: remeasure to confirm improvement.
Common mistakes
Blanket power increase

Often worsens noise floor and contention; only increase power after analysis and within regulatory limits.

Channel mismanagement

Using overlapping channels creates ACI; choose non‑overlapping channels and account for neighboring networks.

Ignoring materials

Expect attenuation through concrete or water and use AP placement or additional APs to cover absorptive zones.

Key Takeaway

Follow a measured workflow and prefer targeted fixes over blunt changes to transmit power or uncoordinated channel choices.
