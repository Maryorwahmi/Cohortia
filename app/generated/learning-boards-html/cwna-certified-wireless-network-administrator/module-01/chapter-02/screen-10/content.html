Definition › Reflection, Refraction & Diffraction
Reflection

Signals bounce off large surfaces and create multipath. Multipath can cause destructive interference and timing differences at the receiver.

Example

A metal wall near an AP can produce delayed echoes at clients, reducing throughput unless mitigated.

Refraction & Diffraction

Refraction bends signal paths when crossing media. Diffraction lets signals bend around edges into shadowed areas, though at reduced strength.

Practical note

Diffraction can provide connectivity behind corners but expect lower RSSI; reflection needs care to avoid destructive multipath.

Key Takeaway

Recognize each wave behavior and use measurement to decide whether to reposition APs, change antennas, or leverage diversity features.
