Propagation Effects
Absorption, reflection, refraction, diffraction and scattering change signal strength and timing.
Interference Types
Co‑channel, adjacent‑channel and non‑Wi‑Fi interference disrupt channel access and frame integrity.
Measurement-first
Measure, analyze, and then apply targeted mitigation; avoid blanket power increases.
A short summary: identify what is absorbing, reflecting, or interfering with your signals, quantify it with dBm and dB arithmetic, and apply targeted fixes based on measurements.
