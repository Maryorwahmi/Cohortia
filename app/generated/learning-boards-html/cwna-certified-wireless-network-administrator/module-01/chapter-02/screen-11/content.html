Co‑channel vs Adjacent‑channel

Co‑channel: same channel, increased contention but Wi‑Fi shares the medium. Adjacent‑channel: overlapping channels, often worse due to corrupted frames.

Practical sign

High retransmits with low visible Wi‑Fi client counts points to non‑Wi‑Fi interference; slow throughput across many clients often indicates CCI.

Non‑Wi‑Fi interference

Devices that do not follow Wi‑Fi protocols create noise that Wi‑Fi cannot avoid using CSMA/CA. Microwave ovens are a classic example in the 2.4 GHz band.

Common sources

Microwave ovens, Bluetooth, cordless phones, some cameras, and defective electronics.

Mitigation checklist
  • Channel planning to reduce overlap
  • AP placement to reduce CCI
  • Spectrum analysis to locate non‑Wi‑Fi sources
  • Avoid indiscriminate power increases
Key Takeaway

Diagnose interference type first and apply targeted fixes; use spectrum tools rather than guessing.
