Definition › Absorption & Scattering
Absorption

When RF passes through materials, energy is lost and converted to heat. Water, concrete and thick walls are strong absorbers and cause attenuation.

Real example

Human bodies and concrete reduce signal strength indoors, creating coverage gaps near large walls or crowded areas.

Scattering

Irregular surfaces and many small objects disperse RF into multiple weak paths, degrading coherent signal and increasing complexity.

Indoor clutter

Cubicle walls, foliage and suspended ceilings commonly scatter signals and increase multipath components.

Key Takeaway

Map absorbers and scatterers in your environment and use placement, frequency selection, and additional APs to reduce their impact.
