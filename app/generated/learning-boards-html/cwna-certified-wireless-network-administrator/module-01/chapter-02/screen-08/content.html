Comparison › ISM Bands
2.4 GHz

Range and penetration advantages, but limited non‑overlapping channels and heavy congestion from many device types.

Key facts

2.400–2.4835 GHz; typically channels 1, 6, 11 are non‑overlapping.

Common interferers

Bluetooth, microwave ovens, cordless phones, and many IoT devices.

5 GHz and 6 GHz

5 GHz supplies many more 20 MHz channels and higher throughput but reduced range and building penetration. 6 GHz adds contiguous spectrum for Wi‑Fi 6E and more non‑DFS channels in many regions.

5 GHz notes

Roughly 5.150–5.875 GHz depending on region; includes DFS channels in some ranges.

6 GHz notes

Opened for Wi‑Fi 6E to provide additional contiguous spectrum and reduce congestion.

Key Takeaway

Choose lower frequency for coverage and higher frequency for capacity and more channels; check regional rules.
