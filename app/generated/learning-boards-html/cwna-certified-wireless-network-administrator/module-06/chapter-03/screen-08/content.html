Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Hidden Node
Definition

Hidden node explained

A client that hears the AP but cannot hear other clients creates collisions at the AP when transmissions overlap.

Detection signs
  • High retransmission rates on affected clients
  • ACK timeouts observed in captures
  • Geographic or obstruction-based separation between clients
Mitigation

Enable RTS/CTS selectively or lower RTS threshold for large frames; confirm effect using retransmit metrics.

Example scenario
Two clients in separate rooms can both reach the AP but cannot hear each other. The AP reports many retransmissions and poor throughput for both. Enabling RTS for large frames reduces collisions and improves throughput.
Check
Capture from AP and clients to confirm that clients' transmissions overlap unseen by peers.
Pitfall
Assuming interference is the cause before confirming lack of hearability between clients.
