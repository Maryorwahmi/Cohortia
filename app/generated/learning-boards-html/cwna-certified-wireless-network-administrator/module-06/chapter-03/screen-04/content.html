Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Role of Layer 2
Definition

What Layer 2 controls

Layer 2 handles discovery, association, authentication, and medium access arbitration. If Layer 1 is healthy, begin your troubleshooting here.

Why it matters
A failed association or authentication leaves the client unable to send or receive data, even when radios work.
Common causes
  • Incorrect SSID or hidden SSID
  • AP capacity limits or MAC filtering
  • Incorrect PSK or 802.1X supplicant settings
Key takeaway
Confirm management-frame exchange and status codes before guessing hardware failure.
Annotated Flow
Typical association sequence to watch for in captures
Discovery
Client sees beacons or probe responses from APs advertising SSIDs.
Association Request / Response
Look for the response status code. Non‑zero indicates association failure reasons.
Authentication
For WPA2/Psk expect EAPOL exchange; for 802.1X expect RADIUS traffic after EAP encapsulation.
Example
A client sends association request and never receives a response. Check SSID, AP capacity, and MAC whitelist.
Common mistake
Assuming authentication failed when an association never completed. Verify the association step first.
