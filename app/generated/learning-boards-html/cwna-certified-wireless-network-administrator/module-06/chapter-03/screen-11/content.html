Analysis

Interpreting retransmit metrics

Not all retransmits are created equal. Look at where retransmits are concentrated, compare with RSSI, and decide if collisions or radio corruption are responsible.
Workflow
  1. Capture on channel with timestamps
  2. Compute retransmit percentage per client
  3. Correlate with RSSI and hearability
  4. Decide: Spectrum cleanup, RTS tuning, or driver/firmware update
Example
Client A has 40% retransmits and low RSSI — address radio conditions. Client B has 40% retransmits but strong RSSI and cannot hear other clients — suspect hidden node.
Common mistake
Replacing hardware without comparing retransmit patterns and RSSI across devices and captures.
Check Your Understanding

If retransmits are high but RSSI and spectrum look clean, what is a likely cause? Consider hidden nodes or driver issues as the next checks.
