Filters and capture checklist

Recommended Wireshark filters
wlan.fc.type_subtype == 0x0004    // Association Request
wlan.fc.type_subtype == 0x0005    // Association Response
wlan.fc.type_subtype == 0x000b    // Authentication
eapol                              // EAP over LAN frames (EAPOL)
Apply these filters to find the exact management frames and the EAP exchanges you need to inspect.
Example result
Capture shows association success then missing EAPOL-Key 3/4, indicating an interrupted four-way handshake.
Capture tips
Record exact timestamps and channel, and capture management frames not just data frames.
Common mistakes
Misinterpreting retransmissions as authentication failure or missing management frames because of wrong capture channel.
Check Your Understanding

If a capture shows an association response with status non‑zero, what should you check next? Look at AP logs, MAC filters, and AP client limits.
