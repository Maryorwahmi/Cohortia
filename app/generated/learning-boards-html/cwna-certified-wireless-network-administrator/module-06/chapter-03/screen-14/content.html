Association & Authentication
Association builds the link; authentication verifies identity or key before granting access.
Hidden vs Exposed Nodes
Hidden nodes cause collisions at the AP; exposed nodes cause unnecessary deferral by clients.
RTS/CTS
A reservation mechanism to prevent hidden-node collisions; use selectively to avoid overhead.
Takeaway

Use captures and AP/server logs together. Identify the failing frame and the responsible component before changing access or protocol settings.
