Frame Loss / Retransmits
High retransmits indicate collisions, interference, or device faults; correlate with RSSI and hearability.
MAC Address Filtering
A simple access control that can block devices; verify allow lists when clients fail to connect.
Protocol Analyzer
Capture management, EAPOL, and RADIUS frames to find the failing handshake step quickly.
Final Takeaway

Use evidence-driven checks: captures, AP logs, and retransmit metrics will direct you to the right corrective action faster than guesswork.
