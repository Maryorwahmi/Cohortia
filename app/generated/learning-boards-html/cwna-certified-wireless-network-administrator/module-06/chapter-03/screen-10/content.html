Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › RTS/CTS & Fragmentation
Process

When and how to apply RTS/CTS

Enable RTS/CTS selectively when hidden-node evidence shows collisions on large frames. Adjust RTS threshold rather than enabling globally.
Fragmentation role
Fragmentation reduces the retransmit cost by limiting corrupted frame size, but it raises overhead. Use only when large-frame loss is frequent.
Example application
You detect frequent collisions for frames larger than 2000 bytes. Lower the RTS threshold to 2000 and monitor retransmit percentage for improvement.
Measure impact
Track retransmissions before and after adjusting RTS or fragmentation to confirm benefit.
Caution
Enabling RTS/CTS everywhere will add overhead and may reduce overall throughput if no hidden nodes exist.
