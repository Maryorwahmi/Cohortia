Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Exposed Node
Definition

Exposed node behavior

A client hears another client's transmission and defers, even though its own transmission would not interfere at the AP. This reduces usable airtime.

Detection
  • Observe frequent deferrals with little collision evidence
  • Capture NAV/CTS patterns that show unnecessary waiting
Example
A device defers because it hears another client on the same channel communicating with a different AP. Throughput drops even though no collision occurs.
Consideration
Reposition clients or change channel plan to reduce unnecessary carrier sensing among non‑interfering clients.
Misconception
Turning on RTS everywhere will not solve exposed node inefficiency and will create overhead.
Key Takeaway

Exposed nodes reduce airtime; solve with planning rather than blanket protocol changes.
