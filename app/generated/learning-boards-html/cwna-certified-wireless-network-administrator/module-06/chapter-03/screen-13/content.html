Layer 2 Troubleshooting Checklist

A concise repeatable flow to isolate common client connectivity issues.

Step 1 — Verify Layer 1
Check channels, RSSI, and interference before Layer 2 work.
Step 2 — Association
Capture association requests and responses and inspect status codes.
Step 3 — Authentication
Capture EAPOL frames and check RADIUS logs for 802.1X networks.
Step 4 — Retransmits
Compute retransmit percent and correlate with RSSI and hearability.
Step 5 — Policies
Verify MAC filters and AP client limits before deeper changes.
Step 6 — Targeted fixes
Apply RTS/CTS or fragmentation only where evidence supports it and remeasure.
Final Tip

Always correlate captures with AP and server logs using timestamps. That correlation is often the quickest path to root cause.
