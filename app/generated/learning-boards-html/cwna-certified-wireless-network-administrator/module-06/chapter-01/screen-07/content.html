Process Flow

Association and authentication checks

  • Confirm the client can see and select the correct SSID.
  • Check association status and timestamps in the controller or AP logs.
  • Verify authentication: RADIUS success, credential validity, or PSK match.
  • Confirm VLAN tagging and switch port configuration for assigned VLAN.
  • Inspect MAC filters or NAC policies that may prevent access.
Concrete example
Client associates but cannot reach internal servers. Controller logs show association time but RADIUS returned an access‑reject due to expired certificate. After renewing credentials, authentication succeeds and access is restored.
Common mistake
Assuming association alone equals connectivity. Always verify authentication and VLAN mapping before moving up the stack.
Key Takeaway

Association must be followed by successful authentication and correct VLAN placement to result in usable network access.
