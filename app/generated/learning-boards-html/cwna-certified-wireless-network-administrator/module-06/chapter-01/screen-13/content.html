Checklist

Safety and security rules

  • Follow proper PoE and rack safety before touching equipment.
  • Capture logs before restarting services or devices.
  • Avoid broad firewall/ACL relaxations without approval and rollback plans.
  • Document changes and who approved them.
Concrete instruction
Before adjusting firewall rules to get an application working, record the rule you will add, who approved it, and how you will remove it. If possible, test in a lab or maintenance window.
Common mistake
Making broad changes to restore service quickly without documenting or planning rollback, which can cause security exposure or cascading outages.
Key Takeaway

Treat safety and security as core steps: capture evidence first, make controlled changes, and always document approval and rollback plans.
