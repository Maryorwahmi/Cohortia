Learning Objectives

Skills to practice: methodology selection, OSI framing, and disciplined documentation.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Apply structured methodologies
Learn to use OSI, top‑down, bottom‑up and divide‑and‑conquer to isolate issues reliably.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Use the OSI model as a diagnostic map
Map symptoms to layers so your checks are targeted and efficient.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Gather evidence before acting
Ask who, what, when, and change history to focus your tests on likely causes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Document and establish baselines
Record tests and maintain baselines for signal, noise, and utilization to spot deviations.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
