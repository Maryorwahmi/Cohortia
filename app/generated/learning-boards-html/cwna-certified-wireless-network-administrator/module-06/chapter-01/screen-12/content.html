Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Baselines & Metrics
Hierarchy

Key baseline metrics

  • Signal strength (RSSI) typical ranges per location.
  • Noise floor and signal‑to‑noise ratios.
  • Channel utilization and airtime consumption.
  • Average client counts and peak patterns by time of day.
Concrete use
When a complaint arises, compare current measurements to baseline. If utilization or noise is beyond expected variance, prioritize RF or scheduling fixes over client configuration changes.
Misconception
Baselines are not single numbers; they are expected ranges and patterns that help you spot meaningful deviations.
Key Takeaway

Keep per‑location baselines for signal, noise, utilization, and client counts so anomalies jump out during troubleshooting.
