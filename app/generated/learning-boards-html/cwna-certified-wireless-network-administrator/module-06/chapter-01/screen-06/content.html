Process Flow

Physical checks checklist

  1. Verify AP power and link light; confirm switch port is enabled.
  2. Confirm PoE safety and correct voltage for the device.
  3. Measure RSSI and noise from the client and compare to baseline.
  4. Check cabling, connectors, and visible damage.
  5. Scan for interference or new RF sources in the area.
Concrete scenario
New AP reports no uplink. Check the switch port: it is administratively down. Enabling the port restores connectivity. Root cause: physical link disabled, not radio tuning.
Safety note
Always verify power sources and follow electrical safety procedures before touching rack or PoE equipment.
Key Takeaway

Physical faults are fast to validate. Confirm power and link before making RF or config changes.
