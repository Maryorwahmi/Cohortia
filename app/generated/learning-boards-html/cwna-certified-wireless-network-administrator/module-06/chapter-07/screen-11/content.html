Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Proactive Monitoring
Process

Detect → Triage → Investigate → Confirm

Automated alerts anchored to baselines let you spend time on real problems and not false positives.

  1. Alert triggers on deviation from baseline (e.g., retransmit rate spike).
  2. Quickly compare current metrics to baseline to estimate impact.
  3. Gather evidence: Wireshark captures, site survey results, WIPS logs.
  4. Decide mitigation or escalate with documented findings.
Example scenario
An alert reports channel utilization jumped above baseline. You check graphs: utilization and retransmissions increased simultaneously. You run a passive spot survey and see a non‑Wi‑Fi spectrum signal. Now you can recommend mitigation: locate and remove the interferer or change channel planning for affected APs.
Common mistake

Chasing transient spikes without confirming persistence wastes time; use trend windows to validate severity before major changes.
