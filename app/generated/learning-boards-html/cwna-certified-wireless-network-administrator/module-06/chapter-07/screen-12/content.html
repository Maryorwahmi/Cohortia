Best Practices

Document, plan, test, and learn

Adopt a clear change process: scope, authorization, rollback, and post-change validation. Keep a troubleshooting log tied to incidents and baselines.
  • Record symptoms, timestamps, and reproducible steps.
  • Change one item at a time and observe results against baseline.
  • Test in controlled environments when possible before production changes.
  • After resolution, update runbooks and baselines with lessons learned.
Scenario
You plan to change channel allocations to reduce co‑channel interference. Document the planned change, the expected outcome, a rollback plan, and schedule the change during a maintenance window. After the change, compare utilization and retransmission metrics to the baseline to confirm success.
Common mistake

Making multiple simultaneous changes hides the cause and slows recovery; change one thing at a time.
