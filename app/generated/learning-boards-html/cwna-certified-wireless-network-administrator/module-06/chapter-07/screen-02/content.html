Learning Objectives

Skills you will practice and why they matter for operational reliability.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Advanced Wireshark Filters

Create precise filters to isolate retransmissions, specific MAC traffic, or problematic information elements. This lets you focus analysis on relevant frames and save time.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Site Survey Techniques

Run passive surveys to detect coverage and interference, and active surveys to validate client throughput and roaming performance. Choose the right method for the question you need answered.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Network Baselines

Collect RF, throughput, retransmit, and client distribution baselines. Baselines let you identify abnormal behavior quickly and prioritize fixes where they matter most.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Operational Best Practices

Document findings, use controlled change management, reproduce issues when possible, and learn continuously to prevent repeat problems and escalate effectively when needed.
