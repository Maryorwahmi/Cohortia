Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Network Baselines
Hierarchy

Key baseline categories

  • RF: RSSI, SNR, noise floor, channel utilization per AP and channel.
  • Performance: Throughput, latency, jitter, retransmission rates.
  • Clients: Associated client counts, distribution, roaming behavior.
  • Network: DHCP lease times, DNS resolution times, gateway pings.
Collection and usage
Collect baseline data with monitoring tools and refresh periodically. When a metric deviates significantly from baseline, use that deviation to focus captures and surveys.
Example comparison

If baseline retransmission is 3% and current is 15%, prioritize an RF survey and retries inspection for affected clients and APs.

Pro tip

Store baseline snapshots and annotate them with environment conditions so you know when and how they were collected.

Key Takeaway

Baselines turn measurement into context; without them, you cannot judge whether a metric is normal or a sign of regression.
