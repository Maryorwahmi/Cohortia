Module 7 › Chapter 6.7 › Decryption Considerations
Definition

When and how decryption helps

Decrypt when payload visibility is required for application-layer troubleshooting and you have authorized key material.

WPA2‑Personal

You can decrypt if you provide SSID and PSK. Useful for single-SSID test environments or labs where keys are controlled.

WPA2‑Enterprise

Decryption is often impractical due to key management; enterprise keys are not normally available for routine troubleshooting.

Ethical note

Only decrypt traffic when you have authorization and document the reason and time window for the capture and analysis.

When decryption is not possible
Use metadata and RF evidence: look at retry counts, I/O graphs, RSSI and SNR from surveys, and WIPS logs to diagnose performance without payload inspection.
Key Takeaway

Decryption is a targeted tool for specific scenarios; most operational troubleshooting depends on headers, flags, and RF data rather than decrypted payloads.
