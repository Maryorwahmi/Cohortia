Definition

Expert Information: prioritized capture insights

A central summary of warnings, errors, and notes that helps you quickly identify recurring or severe events in a capture.

What it shows
  • Retransmissions and duplicate ACKs
  • Malformed packets and protocol errors
  • Frequency of repeated events
How to act
  • Prioritize frequent or severe warnings
  • Create filters to inspect flagged frames
  • Correlate with RF and performance data
Analogy

Think of expert information as a checklist that points you to the most suspicious parts of a long capture, similar to a triage nurse listing urgent conditions first.

Concrete workflow
  1. Open the Expert Information panel to see flagged events.
  2. Note the most frequent or severe warnings as leads.
  3. Create display filters for the flagged frame types and addresses.
  4. Collect corroborating RF or performance data if retransmissions are present.
Key Takeaway

Expert Information reduces noise by turning raw frames into prioritized investigative leads; always corroborate leads with additional data.
