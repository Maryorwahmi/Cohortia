Advanced Features

Heatmaps, utilization, data rates, and spectrum

Combine multiple visual layers to understand coverage, capacity, and interference at the physical location of complaints.

RSSI / SNR layer

Highlights areas with weak signal or poor signal-to-noise ratios that often cause retransmissions or low rates.

Channel utilization

Shows airtime saturation; high utilization can explain slow speeds even when RSSI looks acceptable.

Data-rate overlay

Reveals where clients are negotiating low rates and consuming disproportionate airtime.

Spectrum integration

Identifies non‑Wi‑Fi interferers; essential when retransmissions and noise don’t align with visible Wi‑Fi networks.

Spot survey workflow
  1. Run a passive spot survey and record heatmap layers for RSSI and utilization.
  2. If usage is high, perform an active throughput test in the same spot.
  3. Use spectrum analysis if retransmits appear without clear Wi‑Fi culprits.
  4. Compare current heatmap to design baselines to identify changes.
Key Takeaway

Layered heatmaps give fast spatial insight, but always validate visual findings with captured frames and baseline comparisons before you act.
