Inspect and renew DHCP leases

Run the appropriate command for the OS, read the lease details, and if necessary force a release and renew to observe errors and timeouts.

Windows example
C:\> ipconfig /all
Ethernet adapter Wi-Fi:
   DHCP Enabled . . . . . . . . : Yes
   Autoconfiguration Enabled . . : Yes
   IPv4 Address. . . . . . . . . : 169.254.23.18(Preferred)
   Default Gateway . . . . . . . : 

C:\> ipconfig /renew
Windows IP Configuration
   An error occurred while renewing interface Wi-Fi: The operation timed out.
Interpretation
The APIPA address and renew timeout confirm DHCP did not complete. Next steps: check DHCP server reachability, relay configuration, and UDP port access.
Linux/macOS example
$ ip a
3: wlp2s0: <BROADCAST,MULTICAST,UP,LOWER_UP> mtu 1500
    inet 192.168.1.45/24 brd 192.168.1.255 scope global dynamic wlp2s0

$ ip route
default via 192.168.1.1 dev wlp2s0 proto dhcp metric 600
If ip a shows an address and no default route appears in ip route, there may be a gateway misconfiguration. Confirm that the gateway IP is in the same subnet and reachable with ping.
Check your assumption

Do not assume a displayed IP is sufficient; confirm mask and gateway to ensure proper routing beyond the local segment.
