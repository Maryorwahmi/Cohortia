DHCP & APIPA
When DHCP fails, Windows may self-assign 169.254.x.x — a clear sign to check DHCP reachability and scopes.
DNS Resolution
Name resolution problems stop browsing even when IP connectivity exists; use nslookup to test servers directly.
Routing & Firewalls
Ping and traceroute reveal where packets stop; firewalls and ACLs often block ports or entire subnets.
