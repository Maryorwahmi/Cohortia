Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Transmit Power Optimization
Cell size design
Aim for clients to hear their serving AP noticeably stronger than other APs on the same channel. Smaller cells enable higher spatial reuse and capacity.
Balanced power checklist
  • Set AP power to create intended cell radius.
  • Consider client transmit power and antenna gain.
  • Use TPC/ATPC features where available to adapt dynamically.
Example strategy
In an open office with many APs, set APs to medium/low power so cells overlap minimally and allow channel planning to be effective. After changes, verify client SNR and uplink acknowledgements.
Common mistake
Setting all APs to maximum power to ‘cover more’ increases CCI and often reduces effective capacity.
Pro tip
After reducing AP power, check that clients can still transmit successfully by measuring uplink frames, retries, and association stability. If uplink problems appear, raise power or adjust AP placement.
