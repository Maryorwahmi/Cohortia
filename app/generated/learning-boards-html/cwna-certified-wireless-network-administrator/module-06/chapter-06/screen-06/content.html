Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › 2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz Planning
2.4 GHz — Characteristics
  • Only three non‑overlapping channels: 1, 6, 11.
  • Longer range and better penetration, but higher risk of CCI.
  • Useful for legacy clients or where single AP coverage is needed.
Misconception: more power fixes coverage. In 2.4 GHz, increasing power often worsens interference.
5 GHz — Characteristics
  • Many non‑overlapping channels at 20 MHz; fewer as widths grow.
  • Smaller cells, higher capacity, better channel reuse.
  • DFS channels add capacity but can force channel moves when radar is detected.
Practical tip: use 5 GHz in dense deployments and manage power to create small cells for better reuse.
Example decision
A busy auditorium with modern devices: prefer many 5 GHz APs at medium/low power and 20–40 MHz channels rather than a few high‑power 2.4 GHz APs. Measure client support first and keep 2.4 GHz only if legacy clients must be served.
