Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Data Rate Optimization
Key ideas
  • Slower rates use more airtime per byte and can dominate the medium.
  • Disabling lowest rates forces clients to use more efficient rates or roam.
  • Set appropriate mandatory and supported rates to match client mix.
Misconception: keeping low rates helps clients. It helps a few edge clients but often reduces overall capacity.
Example policy
Set mandatory minimum to 12 or 24 Mbps in an office environment where most clients support those rates. Monitor coverage edges and add APs if necessary.
Key Takeaway
A rate policy improves airtime efficiency but may require more APs for edge coverage—plan and measure accordingly.
