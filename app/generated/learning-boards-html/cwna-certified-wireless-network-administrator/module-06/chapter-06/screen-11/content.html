Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Capacity Planning & Surveys
Sizing steps
  1. Estimate bandwidth per user by application mix.
  2. Multiply by expected peak concurrent users.
  3. Determine AP count and placement to meet airtime budgets.
  4. Predictive model, then perform active site survey to validate.
Survey focus areas
  • Coverage: RSSI and SNR across the space.
  • Capacity: client counts and airtime consumption measurements.
  • Interference: external sources and adjacent networks.
After any major change or growth, re‑survey to ensure your assumptions still hold.
