Capacity planning objectives
  • Estimate per‑user throughput for typical apps (web, video, voice) and aggregate for peak loads.
  • Decide AP count and placement for expected client density.
  • Choose site survey method: predictive to model, active to validate.
  • Document baseline metrics and re‑survey after changes.
Mnemonic: Plan → Model → Baseline → Tune → Validate.
Dynamic channel choices
Understand when Automatic Channel Selection helps and when DFS use can cause interruptions. In sensitive environments, prefer a reviewed manual plan; in dynamic RF conditions, ACS can adapt but should be monitored.
Milestone: produce a capacity worksheet listing client counts, expected bandwidth per client, and suggested AP settings.
