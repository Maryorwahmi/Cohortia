Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Key Concepts
Channel Planning
Assign channels to minimize CCI/ACI and choose widths carefully in 5 GHz.
Transmit Power Control
Adjust AP power to create appropriate cell sizes and improve reuse.
Data Rates
Higher minimum rates reduce airtime used by slow clients and improve efficiency.
Summary
Channel planning, power tuning, and rate policy are the most effective levers for improving WLAN performance while aggregation and WMM provide protocol and QoS improvements.
