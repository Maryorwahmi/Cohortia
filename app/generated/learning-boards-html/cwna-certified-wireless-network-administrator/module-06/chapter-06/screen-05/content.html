2.4 GHz rule
Use channels 1, 6, and 11 only. Intermediate channels overlap and create adjacent channel interference that reduces throughput more than strict reuse.
Example placement
[AP1] --- [AP2]
  |         |
[AP4] --- [AP3]

AP1: Ch 1
AP2: Ch 6
AP3: Ch 11
AP4: Ch 1
Rationale: spatially separate identical channels to minimize CCI and allow predictable reuse.
5 GHz considerations
  • 20 MHz channels give many non‑overlapping choices. Wider channels reduce the count of non‑overlap options.
  • DFS channels increase capacity but may force temporary channel moves if radar is detected.
  • ACS can automate selection but review decisions in critical environments.
Common mistake
Random channel assignment without a plan leads to adjacent APs sharing channels and causing CCI.
