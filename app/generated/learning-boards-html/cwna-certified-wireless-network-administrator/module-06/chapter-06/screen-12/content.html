Module 6 › Chapter 6.6 › Common Mistakes
More power is better
Reality: high power increases interference. Mitigation: reduce power and remeasure.
Ignoring 2.4 GHz limits
Reality: non‑standard channels overlap. Mitigation: use 1,6,11 only in enterprise deployments.
Not disabling low rates
Reality: slow clients hog airtime. Mitigation: raise minimum rates and add APs at the edge.
Neglecting WMM
Reality: voice suffers. Mitigation: enable WMM and map traffic categories appropriately.
Failing to re‑survey
Reality: new problems go unnoticed. Mitigation: re‑survey after changes or growth.
DFS / ACS surprises
Reality: channel moves can interrupt service. Mitigation: review ACS behavior and carefully consider DFS in sensitive environments.
