A‑MPDU / A‑MSDU
Aggregate frames to reduce overhead and raise throughput for many small packets.
WMM / QoS
Prioritize voice and video using WMM; pair with capacity to be effective.
Measure & Re‑survey
Document settings, measure before and after, and re‑survey after changes or growth.
Final takeaway
Optimization is iterative. Use measurement, small controlled changes, and planning to make predictable WLAN improvements.
