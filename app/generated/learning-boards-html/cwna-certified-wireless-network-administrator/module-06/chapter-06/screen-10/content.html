WMM access categories
  • Voice: highest priority, shortest contention windows.
  • Video: higher than best effort for streaming flows.
  • Best Effort and Background: standard and lowest priority.
Ensure both AP and client support WMM to enforce priorities across the link.
Example: voice quality
Without WMM, voice competes with bulk downloads and can experience high jitter. Enable WMM and mark voice flows to get reduced contention and improved consistency.
Common mistake
Assuming WMM solves capacity issues. It prioritizes access but cannot create bandwidth where none exists.
