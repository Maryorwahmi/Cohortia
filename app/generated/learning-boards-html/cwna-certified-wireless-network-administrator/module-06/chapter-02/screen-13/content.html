Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Key Concepts
RSSI
Received signal power; higher (less negative) is stronger, but requires noise context.
Noise Floor
Background RF energy; a lower (more negative) value is better for Wi‑Fi performance.
SNR
RSSI minus noise floor. Higher SNR means better signal quality and fewer errors.
Summary Takeaway
Always measure RSSI and noise floor, compute SNR, classify the type of interference, and choose mitigations based on that evidence.
