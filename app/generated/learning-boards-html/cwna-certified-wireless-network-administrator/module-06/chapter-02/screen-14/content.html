Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Quick Reference
Measure
Collect RSSI and noise floor, then compute SNR to know whether a link is usable.
Classify
Decide if interference is co‑channel, adjacent‑channel, or non‑Wi‑Fi; each requires a different response.
Inspect
Check antennas, connectors, and Ethernet cabling as early steps; many outages are physical.
Closing
Practice the measurement and classification steps in a small area to build a reliable diagnostic routine.
