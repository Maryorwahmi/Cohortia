Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Noise Floor
Definition
Noise Floor
The total background RF and thermal noise in a frequency band. Lower (more negative) is better.
Ideal: -95 to -100 dBm
High noise floor: -80 dBm or higher — problematic
Measurement
Use a spectrum analyzer to visualize baseline energy and to distinguish wideband noise from narrowband carriers.
Example
A microwave oven produces wide, intermittent spikes in 2.4 GHz raising the noise floor in bursts and causing packet errors during those bursts.
Why it matters
High noise floor lowers SNR and reduces achievable data rates even when RSSI looks good.
