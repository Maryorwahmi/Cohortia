Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Learning Objectives
01
Identify and diagnose Layer 1 issues
You will learn practical checks and symptoms for intermittent connectivity, poor throughput, and no‑connect cases using observable RF metrics and simple inspections.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Detect and mitigate RF interference
Understand co‑channel, adjacent‑channel, and non‑Wi‑Fi sources and how to use simple mitigations like channel changes or device relocation.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Interpret RSSI, noise floor, and SNR
You'll calculate SNR from RSSI and noise floor and use thresholds to decide if a link is usable or needs remediation.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Troubleshoot antennas, cabling, and power
Inspect antenna type, orientation, Ethernet cable quality, and PoE behavior to eliminate physical failures before deeper analysis.
