Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › RF Interference Types
Co‑channel
Same channel contention
Many APs on one channel increase airtime contention and retransmissions; devices take turns but throughput suffers.
Mitigation: channel planning, power tuning.
Adjacent‑channel
Overlapping channels corrupt frames
Using overlapping channels in 2.4 GHz creates collisions that reduce throughput for both APs.
Mitigation: use non‑overlapping channels, avoid channel 2–5 or 7–10 in 2.4 GHz.
Non‑Wi‑Fi
Appliances and other RF sources
Microwaves, cordless phones, Bluetooth, and faulty electrical gear create signals the Wi‑Fi protocol cannot negotiate with.
Mitigation: spectrum analysis, relocation, shielding, or moving to 5 GHz when possible.
Example scenario
If a spectrum analyzer shows wide, strong intermittent spikes coinciding with disconnections on Channel 6 and adjacent channels, the source is likely a microwave or other wideband non‑Wi‑Fi device rather than another AP.
