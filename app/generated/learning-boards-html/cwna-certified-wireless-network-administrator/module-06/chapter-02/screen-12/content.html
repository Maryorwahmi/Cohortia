Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Hands‑on Activity
Given Data
Client RSSI: -72 dBm. Noise Floor: -85 dBm. Channel: 2.4 GHz Channel 6. Spectrum: wide intermittent spike on Channel 6 and adjacent channels.
Step 1
SNR = -72 − (-85) = 13 dB.
Step 2
Signal quality: marginal — expect retransmissions and low throughput.
Step 3
Likely source: wideband non‑Wi‑Fi (microwave) because spikes are wide and intermittent.
Immediate Actions
Power off or relocate the interfering device, move clients/APs to a different channel or to 5 GHz, then remeasure SNR.
Follow up
Log the environment and recheck at peak times to confirm the mitigation lowered the noise floor.
