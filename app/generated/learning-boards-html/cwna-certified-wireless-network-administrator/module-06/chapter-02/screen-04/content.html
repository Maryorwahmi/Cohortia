Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Physical Layer
Definition
The radio foundation that carries all data
  • Radio waves propagate and interact with materials; distance and obstacles reduce power.
  • Layer 1 issues manifest as retransmits, low throughput, and disconnects.
  • Start troubleshooting by measuring: RSSI, noise floor, and SNR, then inspect hardware.
Example
An HVAC motor creates periodic RF energy; measuring the spectrum shows bursts that correlate with drops in throughput.
Key Takeaway
Confirm physical measurements before altering higher‑layer settings.
Supporting Details
Why distance matters
Signal power drops with distance; walls and metal further reduce usable RSSI.
Hardware checks
Verify antenna orientation, secure connectors, and cable quality to rule out simple failures.
Common Mistake
Changing encryption or roaming settings before checking RF wastes time and rarely fixes Layer 1 issues.
