Antenna checks
Type, placement, and orientation
Confirm the antenna type matches coverage goals. Ensure connectors are tight and antennas are not blocked by metal or heavy obstructions.
Omni vs directional selection
Avoid shielding antennas behind structures
Cabling & PoE
Use appropriate cable and test physical links
Damaged, low‑quality, or miswired Ethernet can cause poor throughput or loss of PoE power, appearing as random AP failures.
Example
An AP rebooting during peak load was traced to a kinked Cat5e cable causing intermittent PoE loss; replacing the cable resolved the issue.
Misconception
Cranking transmit power will not fix a bad antenna or damaged cable and may increase interference for neighboring APs.
