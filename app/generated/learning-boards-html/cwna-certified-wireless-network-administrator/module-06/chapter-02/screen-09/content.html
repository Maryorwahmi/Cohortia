Module 6 › Chapter 6.2 › Spectrum Analyzer Process
Step‑by‑step
1
Scan the band and note baseline
Record noise floor and any persistent peaks before you change anything.
2
Correlate with client reports
Time the scan to match the interference and watch for coincident spikes.
3
Localize the source
Move the analyzer and note amplitude changes to pinpoint the device location.
4
Mitigate appropriately
Choose relocation, shielding, channel changes, or hardware repair based on the signature.
Example
Wide intermittent spikes on Channel 6 correlate with lunch breaks. Localizing the spikes near the kitchen identifies the microwave as the culprit.
Common Mistake
Using a Wi‑Fi frame analyzer only — it cannot see non‑Wi‑Fi energy and will miss the true source.
Key Takeaway
Spectrum analyzers visualize what clients and APs cannot, enabling precise interventions.
