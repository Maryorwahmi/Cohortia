Wireless Security Troubleshooting

A compact, practice-first lesson that teaches how to isolate authentication and encryption failures across clients, APs, and RADIUS servers. Learn checks, logs, and safe mitigation steps.

In this lesson
Diagnose PSK and 802.1X failures and use logs and captures to find the root cause.
Coming up
RADIUS checks, shared secret validation, and cert/time issues.
Also
Rogue AP detection and safe response steps.
Follow a simple checklist: verify client, check AP, check RADIUS, capture if needed, then remediate safely.

This compact chain represents the three places you must check when security fails. Often the symptom appears at the client but the decisive evidence is in server logs or a packet capture.
Start checklist
  • Confirm SSID and PSK or 802.1X profile on the client.
  • Check AP reachability to RADIUS and shared secret match.
  • Inspect RADIUS logs and use captures for EAPOL failure points.
