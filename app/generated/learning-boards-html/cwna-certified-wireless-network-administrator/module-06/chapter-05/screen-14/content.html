Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › What to Remember
Verify values
Exact PSK and shared secrets matter; small differences break authentication.
Follow evidence
Collect client, AP, and RADIUS logs; capture EAPOL when needed.
Respond safely
Don’t disable security in production; coordinate and document remediation.
Final takeaway
A disciplined troubleshooting approach saves time and keeps networks secure—trust logs, validate values, and act with caution.
