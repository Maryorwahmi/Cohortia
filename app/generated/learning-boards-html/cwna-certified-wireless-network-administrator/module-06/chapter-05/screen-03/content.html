Skill Outcomes & Bloom

Move from understanding to mastering practical troubleshooting techniques.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Read RADIUS logs

Map denial codes to fixes like shared secret, policy, or certificate issues. Logs are usually decisive.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Test connectivity

Ping, traceroute, and confirm UDP 1812/1813 across firewalls to validate AP-to-RADIUS reachability.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Capture EAPOL

Use a packet capture to follow the EAP exchange and 4‑way handshake to find the failure point.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Rogue AP hunt

Detect with WIPS or surveys, then locate physically with directional antenna and signal metrics before removing.
