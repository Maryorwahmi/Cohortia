Module 06 › Chapter 6.5 › Supplicant & Profiles
Definition

Supplicant configuration and profile health

The client supplicant must be configured for the correct EAP type, inner method, and certificate validation settings. Corrupt profiles or drivers cause many avoidable failures.

First-aid steps
  • Update wireless drivers
  • Delete and re-create the SSID profile
  • Verify CA trust and certificate validity
Analogy & scenario
Think of a supplicant profile as the client's instruction sheet for authentication. If the sheet is torn or has the wrong steps, the client follows incorrect procedures and fails. Rewriting the sheet — recreating the profile — often restores correct behavior.
Check your understanding

If one client fails and many succeed, which steps should you try first? Update drivers, recreate profile, collect supplicant logs, then escalate to AP/RADIUS checks.
