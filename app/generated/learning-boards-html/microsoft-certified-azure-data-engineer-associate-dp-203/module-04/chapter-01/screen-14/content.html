Key Concepts

Three compact cards summarize the most important ideas to carry forward.

Pipelines & Activities
Group and run steps that move and transform data.
Linked Services & Datasets
Connections and data shape that activities reference and reuse.
Integration Runtimes
Choose Azure IR, Self-Hosted IR, or Azure-SSIS IR based on where work must run.
Summary Takeaway

ADF organizes data workflows: define connections and shapes, orchestrate steps with pipelines and activities, and pick the right runtime for your environment.
