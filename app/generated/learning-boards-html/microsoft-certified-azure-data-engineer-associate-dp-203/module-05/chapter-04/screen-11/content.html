Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › OPTIMIZE & VACUUM
OPTIMIZE

Compacts small files into larger ones to reduce read overhead. Use ZORDER BY on frequent filter keys to improve data locality.

spark.sql(f"OPTIMIZE delta.`{delta_path}` ZORDER BY (DeviceID)")
VACUUM

Removes unreferenced files older than the retention threshold to reclaim storage. Be cautious: aggressive retention breaks time travel.

# Disable safety check only for demos
spark.sql(f"SET spark.databricks.delta.retentionDurationCheck.enabled = false")
spark.sql(f"VACUUM delta.`{delta_path}` RETAIN 0 HOURS")
spark.sql(f"SET spark.databricks.delta.retentionDurationCheck.enabled = true")
Operational guidance

Schedule OPTIMIZE for query-critical tables and use VACUUM with a conservative retention aligned to your audit and recovery policies.
