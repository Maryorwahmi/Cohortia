Core Concept Troubleshooting
Problem: Unbatched sends
Symptoms: High RPC rate, low throughput. Fix: Aggregate events and send in batches to reduce overhead.
Problem: No checkpointing
Symptoms: Duplicate processing after restart. Fix: Use BlobCheckpointStore and checkpoint after safe commits.
Problem: Consumer group misuse
Symptoms: Contention and reduced throughput. Fix: Create separate consumer groups for independent consumers.
Debugging checklist
  • Inspect metrics for RPC calls, incoming events, and outgoing bytes.
  • Verify checkpoint blobs in your Storage container to confirm progress.
  • Confirm each logical consumer uses its own consumer group.
