Developing Stream Analytics Queries (SAQL)
Write continuous queries that filter, window, aggregate, and join streaming events.
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
In this lesson
Learn SAQL basics, four window types, aggregation, joins, type handling, and best practices for performant streaming jobs.
Why it matters
Correct windowing and timestamps ensure accurate analytics and timely alerts from live streams.
Who benefits
Data engineers, real-time analysts, and developers who build telemetry and alerting systems.
Common risk
Mis-specified timestamps or window sizes lead to missing or duplicated insights.
