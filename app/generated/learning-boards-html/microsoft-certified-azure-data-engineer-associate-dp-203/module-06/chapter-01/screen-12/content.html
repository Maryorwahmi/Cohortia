Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Throughput Units
What they control
Throughput or processing units set the maximum ingress and egress rates. They are the capacity resource you allocate to avoid throttling under load.
Planning tips
Size units to expected peak traffic, test under load, and scale up as needed. Monitor metrics for throttling and partition imbalance.
Monitoring
  • Watch ingress/egress metrics and throttling alerts.
  • Track partition-level throughput to detect hot spots.
  • Adjust partition count and throughput units together for balanced scaling.
Key takeaway
Capacity units and partitioning together determine how much throughput your Event Hubs deployment can support without throttling.
