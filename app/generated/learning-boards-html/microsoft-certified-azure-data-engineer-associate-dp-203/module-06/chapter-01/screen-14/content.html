Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Hands-on: Provisioning
Steps
  1. Create Event Hubs Namespace in the Azure portal; choose Standard tier and set throughput units to 1 for testing.
  2. Inside the namespace, add an Event Hub named telemetry-events, set Partition Count to 4 and retention to 1 day.
  3. Open Consumer groups, inspect $Default, and add analytics-app-group.
Check
Confirm the Event Hub is active, view partition metrics, and verify the new consumer group appears in the list.
Why each step matters
Choosing partitions and consumer groups upfront ensures your consumers can scale and read independently. The Standard tier and throughput units determine capacity during testing.
Reflection
After completing the steps, consider whether four partitions match your target parallelism and whether analytics-app-group isolates analytics reads from operational consumers.
