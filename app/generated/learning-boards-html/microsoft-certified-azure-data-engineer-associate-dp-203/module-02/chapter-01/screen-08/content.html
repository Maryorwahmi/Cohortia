Comparison
DTU
A blended resource unit for CPU, memory, and I/O useful for simpler sizing.
When to pick DTU
Small to medium workloads with predictable performance needs and simpler cost estimation.
Concrete example
A small reporting database with stable usage may choose a DTU tier to simplify management and cost.
vCore
Separate compute and storage for flexible scaling and clearer performance trade-offs.
When to pick vCore
Workloads requiring independent compute scaling or reserved capacity options for cost savings at scale.
Concrete example
A data warehouse with heavy compute and growing storage needs benefits from vCore combined with Hyperscale storage.
Misconception
Assuming DTU is always cheaper — pricing depends on workload profile and required scale.
