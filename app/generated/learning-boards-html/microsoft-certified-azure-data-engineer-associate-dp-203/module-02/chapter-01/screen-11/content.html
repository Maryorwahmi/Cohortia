Core Concept
Service Tiers
Match availability and scale needs to the right tier.
General Purpose
Balanced cost with remote storage and suitable SLAs for general workloads.
Business Critical
Local SSD storage for low latency and higher availability for OLTP systems.
Hyperscale
Architecture for very large databases and rapid, independent scaling of storage and compute.
Concrete example
An OLTP system with strict latency needs uses Business Critical. A growing analytical store with many terabytes chooses Hyperscale for faster scaling.
Checklist
  • Define latency and recovery objectives.
  • Estimate expected storage growth and peak compute.
  • Choose the tier that matches both performance and scale needs.
