Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Normalization vs Denormalization
Normalized (OLTP)
  • Reduces redundancy and update anomalies.
  • Good for many small transactions and consistent updates.
  • May require many joins for complex queries.
Mnemonic: Normalize to maintain single source of truth.
Denormalized / Star Schema (OLAP)
  • Redundancy reduces join complexity and speeds reads.
  • Fact table with surrogate keys links to small dimension tables.
  • Requires ETL discipline to keep dimension attributes consistent.
Mnemonic: Denormalize to optimize read-heavy reporting.
Scenario and guidance
If your workload is analytics and reporting over very large row counts, prefer a star schema and selective denormalization. If your workload is high-volume transactional processing with many concurrent writes, prefer normalized designs to ensure correct updates and smaller transactional scope.
