Module 3 › Chapter 2.4 › Backups & Restore
Backup essentials
Confirm automatic backups are enabled and check the retention window. Decide if the default retention meets your recovery objectives.
Test plan
Schedule periodic restores to a sandbox server, validate data integrity, and time the recovery to estimate your recovery time objective.
Common misconception
Assuming backups guarantee recoverability without testing. Only a restore verifies that backups and procedures work as expected.
Key Takeaway
Regular restore drills are required to trust your backups and estimate realistic recovery timelines.
