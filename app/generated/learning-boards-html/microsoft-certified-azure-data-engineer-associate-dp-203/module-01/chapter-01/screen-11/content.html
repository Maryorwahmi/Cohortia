Security & Governance
Identity, secrets, and encryption patterns to protect data
Azure Active Directory
Control identities and role-based access to resources and services.
Key Vault
Store secrets and keys centrally for managed rotation and access control.
Encryption
Use encryption at rest and in transit to meet compliance needs and protect data.
Concrete Example
Grant Databricks a managed identity to read a single ADLS container; store connection secrets in Key Vault; rotate keys as part of release cycles.
Key Takeaway

Adopt least-privilege access, centralize secrets, and design for audit and rotation from the start.
