Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Choosing Redundancy
Step 1: Identify critical data
List datasets by business impact if lost or delayed.
Step 2: Define RPO and RTO
Decide acceptable data loss and recovery time per dataset.
Step 3: Map to redundancy
Choose LRS, ZRS, GRS or GZRS based on protection needs and budget.
Example allocation
Production configs: GZRS. Logs and diagnostics: LRS or Cool/Archive depending on retention and access.
Key Takeaway

Use a checklist to align redundancy to dataset criticality. Avoid one-size-fits-all redundancy; optimize cost and resilience per dataset.
