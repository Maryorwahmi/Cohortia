Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Redundancy Options Overview
LRS
Three copies within a single data center. Protects against hardware failures but not region or zone outages.
ZRS
Synchronous replication across availability zones in a region. Protects against datacenter outages within the region.
GRS / RA-GRS
Async replication to a secondary region. RA-GRS provides read access to the secondary for certain scenarios.
GZRS / RA-GZRS
Zone replication in the primary region plus async replication to a secondary region. RA variant offers read access in the secondary region.
Key Takeaway

Match redundancy to your RPO/RTO: higher redundancy increases resilience and cost. Test failover and read-access needs before choosing.
