Process Flow

Move to Cool after 30 days → Archive after 180 days

Automate tiering and deletion to lower costs and reduce manual maintenance.

Rule: If blob not modified for 30 days → move to Cool.
Rule: If blob not modified for 180 days → move to Archive or delete after required retention.
Target by container, prefix, or blob index tags for granularity.
Example policy
Apply to raw-data-container: move to Cool after 30 days, then move to Archive after 180 days. Use the Portal's Lifecycle management blade or author JSON rules for automation.
Why test first
Archive rehydration takes time and cost. Validate rules in a test account to avoid surprises.
Common mistake
Assuming tier changes affect permissions or immediate availability. Tiering affects cost and retrieval time.
Key Takeaway

Use lifecycle policies to align storage tiers with access patterns and retention requirements, and test them before broad rollout.
