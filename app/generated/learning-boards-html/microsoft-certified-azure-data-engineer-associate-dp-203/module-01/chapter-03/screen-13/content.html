Scenario-based assessment

Two short scenarios to test lifecycle and access design choices.

Scenario 1 — Long-term logs
Logs are written frequently, rarely read after 90 days, retained for five years. Expected answer: move to Cool after 30 days and Archive after 90 days (or 60 days after moving to Cool), with retention policy to keep for five years.
Scenario 2 — Vendor read-only access
Vendor needs read-only access for 48 hours. Expected answer: create a user delegation SAS scoped to the container with read permissions and 48-hour expiry. Avoid sharing account keys.
When grading, check for use of least privilege, correct scope and expiry, and awareness of Archive rehydration when applicable.
