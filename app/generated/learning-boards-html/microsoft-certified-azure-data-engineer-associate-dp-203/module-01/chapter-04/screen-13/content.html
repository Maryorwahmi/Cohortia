Common Mistakes & Governance
Small configuration choices have large security and cost impacts.
Common Mistakes
  • Over-permissioning via account-level RBAC roles that override ACLs.
  • Omitting execute permission on directories, blocking traversal.
  • Not validating HDFS-compatible tool behavior before migration.
Governance Tips
  • Use RBAC for services, ACLs for teams and users.
  • Apply least-privilege and regularly audit permissions.
  • Follow consistent naming and medallion layering for lineage.
Reflection

Who in your organization needs write access to Bronze, and who should only have read access to Gold? Answering that guides RBAC and ACL design.
