Essential Compute Concepts

IaaS, PaaS, and FaaS Defined

Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)

Cloud model where provider hosts virtualized resources; users manage OS, apps, and data.

Google Compute Engine (GCE)

Google Cloud's IaaS offering, providing customizable VMs with full control over the software stack.

Platform as a Service (PaaS)

Cloud model providing a complete development and deployment environment; provider manages infrastructure.

Summary Takeaway

IaaS, PaaS, and FaaS define the fundamental levels of abstraction and management in cloud computing, with Google Compute Engine representing IaaS.
