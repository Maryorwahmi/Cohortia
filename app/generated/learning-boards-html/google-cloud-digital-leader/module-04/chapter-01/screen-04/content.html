The Cloud Compute Spectrum

Google Cloud offers a range of compute services, each balancing control and operational overhead.

Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)

Provides virtualized computing resources. You manage OS, applications, and data. Highest control, most responsibility.

Platform as a Service (PaaS)

Offers a complete development and deployment environment. Provider manages infrastructure. Focus on code.

Function as a Service (FaaS)

Serverless execution model for event-driven functions. Provider manages all servers. Pay-per-use.

Key Characteristics
Control

High (IaaS) to Low (FaaS)

Management

User (IaaS) to Provider (FaaS)

Flexibility

High (IaaS) to Specific (FaaS)

Focus

Infrastructure to Code

Key Takeaway

Choosing the right compute model depends on your desired level of control, operational overhead tolerance, and application-specific requirements.
