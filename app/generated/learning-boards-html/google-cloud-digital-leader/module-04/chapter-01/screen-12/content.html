Strategic Factors for Compute Selection

Beyond technical specs, consider these business-driven factors when choosing a Google Cloud compute service.

Development Velocity

How quickly can your team build, deploy, and iterate? Managed services reduce operational burden, speeding up development.

Operational Cost

Evaluate billing models (pay-per-use vs. always-on) and the cost of managing infrastructure versus managed services.

Scalability Requirements

Does your application need to scale elastically with demand, or handle predictable, consistent loads?

Team Skill Sets

Leverage your team's existing expertise. If they are strong in containers, Cloud Run might be a better fit than App Engine Standard.

Regulatory Compliance

Certain regulations may require specific control over infrastructure, influencing the choice towards IaaS.

Key Takeaway

A holistic approach, considering both technical and strategic business factors, leads to optimal compute service selection.
