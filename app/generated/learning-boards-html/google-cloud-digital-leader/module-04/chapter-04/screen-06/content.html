Migration Strategies

The 5 Rs: Refactor, Repurchase, Retain/Retire

Transforming applications for cloud-native benefits or strategic disposition.

3 Refactor/Re-architect

Fundamentally change the application's architecture to fully embrace cloud-native capabilities, often breaking a monolith into microservices (e.g., GKE, Cloud Run). Most complex but highest long-term benefits.

4 Repurchase

Move to a different product, typically a SaaS solution, when an existing cloud offering meets business needs better than migration.

5 Retain/Retire

Keep the application on-premises if not suitable for cloud, or decommission it if it no longer provides business value.

Decision Factors for Each 'R'
Business Value

How critical is the application? What are the benefits of moving it?

Technical Feasibility

How complex is the application? Can it be easily refactored or replatformed?

Cost & Time

What are the migration costs, and how long will the transformation take?

Choosing the right strategy depends on a careful assessment of these factors.

Key Takeaway

Refactor offers deep cloud integration, Repurchase leverages SaaS, and Retain/Retire manage non-cloud-suitable applications.

Pro Tip

A portfolio approach is best; different applications may require different 'R' strategies based on their unique characteristics.
