Databases: Structured Data Storage

Databases are specialized systems for efficient data storage, organization, and retrieval, crucial for modern applications.

Key Characteristics
  • Structured Storage: Data is organized in a defined manner.
  • Efficient Retrieval: Optimized for quick data access.
  • Data Integrity: Mechanisms to ensure data accuracy and consistency.
  • Relationship Management: Ability to link related pieces of data.
Architectural Decision

Choosing between relational (SQL) and non-relational (NoSQL) models is a critical architectural decision, driven by data structure, access patterns, scalability, and consistency needs.

Key Takeaway

Databases are essential for managing application data effectively, and the choice of database model is fundamental to system design.

Relational (SQL)

Structured, fixed schema, strong consistency.

Non-Relational (NoSQL)

Flexible schema, varied models, high scalability.

Example Scenario

An online store needs a database for customer orders (structured, consistent) and another for product reviews (flexible, high volume). This highlights the need for diverse database solutions.
