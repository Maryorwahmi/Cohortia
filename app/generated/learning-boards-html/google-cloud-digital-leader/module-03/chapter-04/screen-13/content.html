Key Concepts

Essential ideas to remember from this chapter.

Machine Learning (ML)

A subset of AI enabling systems to learn from data and make predictions or decisions.

Vertex AI

Google Cloud's unified platform for building, deploying, and managing ML models throughout their lifecycle.

Pre-trained APIs

ML services (e.g., Vision AI) offering pre-built models for common tasks, allowing immediate integration.

Summary Takeaway

Google Cloud's ML offerings, from the comprehensive Vertex AI platform to specialized pre-trained APIs, democratize AI and enable organizations to build intelligent solutions efficiently.
