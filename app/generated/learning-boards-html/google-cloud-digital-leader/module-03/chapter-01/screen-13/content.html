Making Informed Storage Decisions

Balancing Cost, Performance, and Durability

Key Takeaway

A holistic approach considering application needs, cost, performance, durability, and regional factors leads to optimal storage choices.

Pro Tip

Regularly review your storage strategy as application requirements evolve to maintain efficiency and cost-effectiveness.

Key Decision Factors for Storage

Cost Efficiency

Align storage class and type with data access frequency and budget constraints.

Performance Needs

Consider latency, IOPS, and throughput requirements for your application workloads.

Durability & Availability

Ensure data resilience against loss and consistent accessibility for critical operations.

Regional Considerations

Choose regions/zones to minimize latency and meet data residency compliance.
