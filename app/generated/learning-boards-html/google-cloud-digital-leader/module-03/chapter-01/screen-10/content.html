Sizing Persistent Disk for Efficiency

Avoiding Bottlenecks and Unnecessary Costs

Key Takeaway

Regularly review and adjust Persistent Disk sizing to match actual workload demands, optimizing both cost and performance.

Common Mistake

Over-provisioning disks 'just in case' leads to wasted resources; under-provisioning causes performance bottlenecks.

Steps for Efficient Disk Sizing

1

Understand Application Needs

Assess both storage capacity and I/O requirements (IOPS, throughput) of your application.

2

Monitor Performance Metrics

Use Cloud Monitoring to track disk utilization, IOPS, and throughput over time.

3

Adjust Disk Size and Type

Scale up or down, or change disk types, based on observed performance and cost data.
