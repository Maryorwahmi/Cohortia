Matching Disk Types to Workload Needs

Standard, SSD, Balanced, and Extreme Persistent Disks

Key Takeaway

Align Persistent Disk types with your VM's I/O requirements to optimize both performance and cost.

Common Mistake

Using Standard disks for high-transaction databases, leading to severe performance bottlenecks.

Persistent Disk Type Comparison

Type Performance Cost Ideal Workload
Standard Balanced IOPS/throughput Lowest Boot disks, general purpose
Balanced Good balance of IOPS/throughput Medium Most enterprise applications
SSD High IOPS/throughput Higher Databases, analytics, HPC
Extreme Highest IOPS/throughput Highest Large in-memory databases
