What are Persistent Disks?

Persistent disks are durable block storage devices that attach to your VM instances. They are designed to be independent of your VM's lifecycle.

  • Durable: Data persists even if the VM is deleted or stopped.
  • Block Storage: Functions like a traditional hard drive.
  • Flexible: Can be resized and moved between VMs.
Key Feature

The independence of persistent disks from VMs ensures data durability and allows for flexible management of your storage resources.

Types of Persistent Disks

Google Cloud offers various persistent disk types, each optimized for different performance and cost requirements.

  • Standard Persistent Disk (HDD): Cost-effective for infrequent access, boot disks.
  • Balanced Persistent Disk (SSD): Good balance of performance and cost for general workloads.
  • SSD Persistent Disk: High performance, ideal for databases and high-throughput applications.
Key Takeaway

Choosing the right persistent disk type, from cost-effective HDD to high-performance SSD, is crucial for optimizing both application performance and storage costs.
