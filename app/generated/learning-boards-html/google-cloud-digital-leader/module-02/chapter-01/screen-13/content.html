Designing for Resilience: Multi-Region vs. Multi-Zone

Choosing the right strategy for high availability and disaster recovery

Deployment Strategies
  • **Multi-Zone:** High availability within a single region.
  • **Multi-Region:** Disaster recovery across geographically separate regions.
  • **Cost vs. Resilience:** Higher resilience often means higher cost and complexity.
  • **RTO/RPO:** Align strategy with Recovery Time/Point Objectives.
Scenario: Critical Financial App

A banking application requires near-zero downtime. It would use a multi-zone deployment for daily operations and a multi-region setup for protection against a rare, catastrophic regional outage.

Key Takeaway

Select multi-zone for intra-region fault tolerance and multi-region for inter-region disaster recovery, based on your application's criticality and budget.
