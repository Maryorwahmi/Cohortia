Definition

Diving Deeper: Google Cloud Zones

Isolated locations within a region for enhanced resilience

Zonal Characteristics
  • **Distinct Data Centers:** Physically separate facilities.
  • **Independent Infrastructure:** Own power, cooling, networking.
  • **Fault Isolation:** An outage in one zone won't affect others.
  • **High Availability:** Enables multi-zone deployments for resilience.
Zone Naming Convention

Zones are named by appending a letter to the region name, e.g., `us-central1-a`, `us-central1-b`, `us-central1-c`. This clearly indicates their regional association.

Key Takeaway

Zones provide a granular level of fault isolation within a region, essential for building highly available and resilient applications.
