Cost and Service Availability Considerations

Additional factors influencing region and zone choices

Secondary Considerations
  • **Cost Variations:** Differences in infrastructure, energy, and tax.
  • **Service Availability:** Not all GCP services are in all regions.
  • **Feature Parity:** Some features might be region-specific.
  • **Pricing Models:** Review regional pricing for specific services.
Service Availability Example

A new AI/ML service might launch first in `us-central1` and `europe-west4`. If your application heavily relies on this service, you must deploy in one of these regions, even if another region is closer to your users.

Key Takeaway

Always verify regional pricing and ensure all necessary Google Cloud services are available in your chosen deployment location to avoid unexpected costs or limitations.
