Decision Factors

Key Factors for Region and Zone Selection

Optimizing for latency and data residency

Primary Considerations
  • **Latency:** Direct impact on user experience and application responsiveness.
  • **Data Residency:** Strict legal and regulatory requirements for data storage.
  • **User Location:** Proximity to the majority of your application's users.
  • **Regulatory Landscape:** Understanding local and international data laws.
Latency Example

A real-time multiplayer game needs minimal latency. Deploying game servers in `us-east1` for East Coast players ensures quick response times, crucial for competitive gameplay.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize user proximity for low latency and ensure strict adherence to data residency laws when selecting your deployment location.
