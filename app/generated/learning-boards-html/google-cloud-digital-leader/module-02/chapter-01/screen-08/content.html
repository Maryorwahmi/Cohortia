High Availability

Achieving High Availability with Multi-Zone Deployments

Minimizing downtime through distributed resources

Benefits of Multi-Zone
  • **Fault Tolerance:** Resilient against single zone failures.
  • **Automatic Failover:** Traffic redirects to healthy zones.
  • **Reduced Downtime:** Ensures continuous application operation.
  • **Business Continuity:** Critical for mission-critical applications.
Common Mistake

Deploying all resources into a single zone creates a single point of failure. If that zone goes down, your entire application becomes unavailable, leading to significant business impact.

Key Takeaway

For critical applications, always distribute resources across multiple zones within a region to ensure high availability and resilience.
