Strategic Region Selection

Balancing compliance, performance, and cost

Key Decision Factors
  • **Data Residency:** Legal/regulatory requirements for data storage.
  • **Latency:** Proximity to users for faster response times.
  • **Cost:** Regional variations in infrastructure and energy expenses.
  • **Service Availability:** Specific GCP services might differ by region.
Compliance Scenario

A European e-commerce platform must store customer data within the EU to comply with GDPR. Choosing `europe-west1` (Belgium) or `europe-west3` (Frankfurt) is mandatory to avoid legal issues.

Key Takeaway

Region choice is a critical architectural decision impacting legal compliance, user experience, and operational budget.
