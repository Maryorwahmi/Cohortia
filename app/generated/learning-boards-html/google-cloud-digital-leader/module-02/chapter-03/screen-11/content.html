Principle of Least Privilege in Networking

Granting Only Necessary Access

What is Least Privilege?

  • Minimum Access: Grant only the permissions absolutely required for a function.
  • Reduced Attack Surface: Limits potential entry points for attackers.
  • Enhanced Security: Prevents unauthorized access and exploitation.

Common Misconception: Overly Permissive Rules

Allowing 0.0.0.0/0 on all ports is a major security risk, exposing your resources to the entire internet.

This violates the Principle of Least Privilege and significantly increases the attack surface, making your instances vulnerable to various attacks.

Key Takeaway

Always apply the Principle of Least Privilege by configuring firewall rules to allow only the minimum necessary traffic, thereby enhancing your network's security posture.
