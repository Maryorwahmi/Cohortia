Key Concepts

What to Remember

Ingress

Inbound network traffic entering your VPC network, typically from external sources.

Egress

Outbound network traffic leaving your VPC network, originating from your cloud resources.

Principle of Least Privilege

A security principle advocating for granting only the minimum necessary permissions or access required.

Applying these concepts ensures precise traffic control and a strong security posture for your Google Cloud deployments.
