Understanding Traffic Direction

Ingress Traffic

  • Definition: Inbound traffic entering your VPC network.
  • Example: A user accessing your web server.
  • Rule Action: Allows or denies connections to your resources.

Egress Traffic

  • Definition: Outbound traffic leaving your VPC network.
  • Example: Your VM downloading software updates.
  • Rule Action: Allows or denies connections from your resources.

Key Takeaway

Ingress rules control traffic entering your network, while egress rules control traffic leaving it, providing comprehensive directional security.
