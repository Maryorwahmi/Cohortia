Why Global VPC Matters

  • Simplified Design: No complex cross-region peering needed.
  • Enhanced Security: Traffic stays on Google's private network.
  • Improved Performance: Low-latency communication across regions.
Key Takeaway

The global nature of VPCs allows resources in different regions to communicate privately and efficiently, simplifying distributed application architectures.
