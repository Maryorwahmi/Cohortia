Designing Secure Cloud Networks

Combining VPC, Subnets, and Firewalls

Elements of Secure Network Design

  • VPC Segmentation: Use custom VPCs and subnets to logically separate environments.
  • Granular Firewall Rules: Control traffic precisely between segments and external networks.
  • Principle of Least Privilege: Only allow necessary access to minimize attack surface.
