Chapter Recap

Key Concepts to Remember

Reinforce your understanding of cloud computing fundamentals.

Cloud Computing

On-demand delivery of IT resources and applications over the Internet with pay-as-you-go pricing.

On-demand Self-service

Users can provision computing resources without human interaction from the service provider.

Rapid Elasticity

Resources can be scaled up or down quickly and automatically to meet fluctuating demands.

Summary Takeaway

Cloud computing's core characteristics enable unparalleled agility, efficiency, and responsiveness for modern businesses.
