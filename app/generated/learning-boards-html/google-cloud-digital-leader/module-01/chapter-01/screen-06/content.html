Essential Characteristics

Resource Pooling & Rapid Elasticity

Efficiently sharing and dynamically scaling computing capabilities.

Resource Pooling

Provider's computing resources are pooled to serve multiple consumers using a multi-tenant model, ensuring efficient utilization.

Rapid Elasticity

Capabilities can be rapidly and elastically provisioned and released to scale quickly outward and inward based on demand.

Key Takeaway

Pooling optimizes resource use, while elasticity ensures applications can handle fluctuating demands seamlessly.
