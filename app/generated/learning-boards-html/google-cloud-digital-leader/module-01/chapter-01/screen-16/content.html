Chapter Recap

Key Concepts to Remember

Solidify your knowledge of cloud service and deployment models.

Public Cloud

Cloud services offered over the public internet and available to anyone who wants to purchase them.

IaaS

Cloud service model providing virtualized computing resources over the internet (e.g., VMs, storage, networks).

SaaS

Cloud service model delivering software applications over the internet, on demand, typically on a subscription basis.

Summary Takeaway

Choosing the right cloud model depends on your organization's control requirements, flexibility needs, and management preferences.
