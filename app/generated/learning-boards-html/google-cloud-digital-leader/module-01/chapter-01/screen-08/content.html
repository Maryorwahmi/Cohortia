Deployment Models

Public Cloud: Shared & Scalable

Leveraging third-party infrastructure over the internet.

Definition

Owned and operated by a third-party cloud service provider, delivering computing resources over the Internet to the general public.

Characteristics

  • Provider manages all infrastructure.
  • Resources shared among multiple tenants.
  • High scalability and reliability.
Key Takeaway

Public clouds offer cost-effective, highly scalable, and readily available resources managed entirely by the provider.
