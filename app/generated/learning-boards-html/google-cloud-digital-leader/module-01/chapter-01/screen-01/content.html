Understanding Cloud Computing Fundamentals

Explore the core concepts, models, and benefits of modern cloud infrastructure.

Cloud computing is about delivering on-demand computing services—including servers, storage, databases, networking, software, analytics, and intelligence—over the Internet. Instead of owning your computing infrastructure, you access services from a cloud provider, paying only for what you use. This model fundamentally transforms how businesses acquire and use IT resources, moving from capital expenditure to operational expenditure.

Key Idea

Cloud computing transforms IT by delivering on-demand services over the internet, shifting from capital to operational expenses.
