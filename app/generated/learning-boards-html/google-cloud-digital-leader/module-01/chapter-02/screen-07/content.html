Zones: Fault Tolerance and Resilience

Isolated locations within a region for high availability.

Key Takeaway

GCP Zones are isolated locations within a region, providing independent infrastructure to enable high availability and fault tolerance through multi-zone deployments.

Common Mistake

Relying on a single zone for critical applications. Always distribute resources across multiple zones for true high availability.

Isolated Locations

Physically separate within a region.

Independent Infrastructure

Own power, cooling, and networking.

High Availability

Ensures services remain operational during localized failures.

Multi-Zone Deployment

Standard practice for critical applications to enhance resilience.
