Understanding GCP's Global Infrastructure

A hierarchical structure for resilient and performant applications.

Key Takeaway

GCP's global infrastructure uses regions, zones, and edge locations to provide resilience, high availability, and low latency for applications worldwide.

Common Mistake

Deploying all resources in a single zone creates a single point of failure. Always consider multi-zone or multi-region deployments for critical applications.

Regions

Independent geographic areas containing multiple isolated locations (zones). Designed for isolation to prevent widespread outages.

Zones

Isolated locations within a region, with independent power, cooling, and networking. Provide high availability and fault tolerance within a region.

Edge Locations (PoPs)

Globally distributed points of presence used to cache content closer to users, reducing latency for services like Cloud CDN.
