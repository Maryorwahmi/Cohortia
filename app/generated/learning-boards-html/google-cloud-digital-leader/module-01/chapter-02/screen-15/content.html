Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

A suite of cloud computing services running on Google's internal infrastructure.

Global Network

Google's high-performance fiber network, providing low latency and high availability worldwide.

Region

An independent geographic area containing multiple zones, designed for isolation.

Summary Takeaway

GCP's robust global infrastructure and powerful services provide a scalable and resilient foundation for modern cloud applications.
