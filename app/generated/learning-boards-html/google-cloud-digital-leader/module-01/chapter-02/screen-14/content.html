Mapping GCP Services to Cloud Models

Understanding control vs. management overhead.

Key Takeaway

GCP services align with IaaS (Compute Engine), PaaS (App Engine, GKE, Cloud SQL, Cloud Firestore), and specialized PaaS/SaaS (BigQuery) models, guiding optimal service selection based on control and management needs.

Common Mistake

Trying to force-fit an application into a service model it's not suited for, leading to inefficiencies or increased complexity.

IaaS: Compute Engine

Provides virtual machines with full control over OS and software. You manage the infrastructure.

PaaS: App Engine, GKE, Cloud SQL, Cloud Firestore

Managed platforms that abstract infrastructure, allowing focus on application development and data.

Specialized PaaS/SaaS: BigQuery

Highly managed, serverless data warehouse that offers a near-SaaS experience for data analysts.
