Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Zone

An isolated location within a region, providing high availability.

Edge Location (PoP)

Globally distributed points to cache content closer to users, reducing latency.

Compute Engine (IaaS)

GCP's offering for running virtual machines with full control.

App Engine (PaaS)

Fully managed platform for developing and hosting web applications.

BigQuery (Serverless DW)

Serverless, highly scalable enterprise data warehouse for analytics.

Summary Takeaway

GCP offers a spectrum of services from IaaS (Compute Engine) to PaaS (App Engine) and specialized analytics (BigQuery), all leveraging its global infrastructure.
