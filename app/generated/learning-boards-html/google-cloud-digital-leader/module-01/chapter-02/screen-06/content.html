Regions: Independent Geographic Areas

Ensuring isolation and preventing widespread outages.

Key Takeaway

GCP Regions are independent geographic areas designed to isolate failures and prevent widespread outages, crucial for disaster recovery and global service availability.

Example

us-central1 (Iowa) and europe-west1 (Belgium) are examples of distinct GCP regions, each designed to operate independently.

Geographic Area

Independent and physically separate from other regions.

Contains Zones

Each region hosts multiple isolated zones.

Failure Isolation

Designed to prevent widespread outages across the globe.

Disaster Recovery

Key for building highly resilient, geographically distributed applications.
