Billing Accounts: Who Pays for What?

Linking projects to a billing account for cost allocation and payment.

Definition

A billing account defines who pays for Google Cloud resources and is linked to one or more projects.

Necessity

Essential for provisioning most GCP resources; without it, you cannot deploy services that incur costs.

Key Function

Ensures all spending is properly attributed, managed, and provides a financial backbone for cloud operations.

Example

An organization might use separate billing accounts for different departments (e.g., Marketing, Engineering) to track costs independently.
