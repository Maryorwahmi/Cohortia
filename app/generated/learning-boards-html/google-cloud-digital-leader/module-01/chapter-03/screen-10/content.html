Proactive Cost Management

Setting Budgets and Alerts to Prevent Surprises

Tools to control spending and receive timely notifications.

1

Define Budget Scope

Choose a project or billing account.

2

Set Target Amount

Specify your spending limit (e.g., $100/month).

3

Configure Alert Thresholds

Set percentages for notifications (e.g., 50%, 90%, 100%).

4

Receive Notifications

Get alerts via email or other channels.

Example: Low Threshold Budget

For learning or experimentation, set a budget of $1.00 with alerts at 50% and 100%. This provides immediate notification of any unexpected charges, preventing bill shock.
