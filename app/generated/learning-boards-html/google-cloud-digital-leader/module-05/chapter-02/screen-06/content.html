Module 5›Chapter 5.2›Primitive Roles

Owner Role

Grants full access to all resources within a project, including managing billing and IAM policies.

  • Highest level of access
  • Rarely suitable for daily operations

Editor Role

Allows creation, modification, and deletion of resources within a project, but typically excludes billing and IAM management.

  • Broad operational access
  • Still generally too permissive for specific tasks

Viewer Role

Provides read-only access to all resources within a project. Cannot create, modify, or delete anything.

  • Read-only access
  • Safer than Owner/Editor, but still broad
Key Takeaway

Primitive roles are powerful but generally too broad for most specific tasks; prefer more granular roles to enhance security.

Misconception

Many assume 'Editor' is safe for developers, but it grants far more permissions than typically needed for application deployment.
