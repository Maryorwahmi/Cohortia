Service-Specific Permissions

Predefined roles are tailored to specific Google Cloud services, offering fine-grained control over actions within that service.

  • Example: roles/compute.instanceAdmin for Compute Engine
  • Example: roles/storage.objectViewer for Cloud Storage

Adherence to Least Privilege

By granting only the necessary permissions for a specific task, predefined roles significantly reduce the attack surface and enhance security.

  • Minimizes potential damage from compromised accounts
  • Simplifies security audits

Recommended for Most Use Cases

Predefined roles are the preferred choice for managing access for both human users and service accounts in most operational scenarios.

  • Easier to manage than individual permissions
  • More secure than primitive roles
Key Takeaway

Always prioritize predefined roles to grant precise, service-specific permissions, aligning with security best practices.

Contrast

Unlike primitive roles, predefined roles prevent over-privileging by limiting access to only what's necessary for a specific service or task.
