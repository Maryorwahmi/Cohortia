Dedicated Service Accounts

Create a unique service account for each application or service to ensure clear separation of concerns and limit impact if compromised.

  • Improves auditability
  • Reduces blast radius of security incidents

Least Privilege for Service Accounts

Grant only the minimum necessary roles to service accounts, avoiding broad primitive roles like 'Editor' or 'Owner'.

  • Use predefined or custom roles
  • Scope permissions to specific resources

Secure Key Management

Prefer Google-managed keys or Workload Identity Federation over user-managed keys to reduce key exposure and management overhead.

  • Workload Identity for GKE
  • Workload Identity Federation for external providers
Key Takeaway

Implement dedicated service accounts, apply least privilege, and use secure key management strategies to protect your automated workloads.

Risk Alert

Reusing service accounts or granting excessive permissions can lead to widespread security vulnerabilities if compromised.
