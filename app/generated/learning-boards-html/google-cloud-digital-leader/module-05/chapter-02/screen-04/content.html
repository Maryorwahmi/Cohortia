Member: The 'Who'

An identity that can be granted access. This includes:

  • Google Accounts (individual users)
  • Google Groups (collections of users)
  • Service Accounts (applications/VMs)
  • Authenticated Domains

Role: The 'What'

A collection of permissions that defines what actions a member can perform. Roles simplify access management by bundling related permissions.

  • Examples: roles/viewer, roles/storage.objectViewer
  • Granted instead of individual permissions

Resource: The 'Which'

Any Google Cloud asset that a member can interact with. Resources are organized hierarchically.

  • Examples: Compute Engine VM, Cloud Storage bucket, BigQuery dataset, entire project
  • Access is granted at various levels of the hierarchy
Key Takeaway

Understanding members, roles, and resources is fundamental to constructing effective IAM policies and securing your cloud environment.

Common Mistake

Granting overly broad roles to members, especially at high levels of the resource hierarchy, can lead to unintended security vulnerabilities.
