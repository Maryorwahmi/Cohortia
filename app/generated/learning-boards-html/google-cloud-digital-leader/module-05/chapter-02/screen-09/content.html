Module 5›Chapter 5.2›Least Privilege

Definition: Minimum Necessary Access

Grant users and services only the permissions absolutely required to perform their specific job functions, and nothing more.

  • Applies to human users and automated systems
  • A core principle of secure system design

Why It Matters: Reduced Risk

Minimizes the potential damage if an account is compromised, by limiting the scope of actions an attacker could perform.

  • Reduces the attack surface
  • Contains the 'blast radius' of security incidents

Common Pitfall: Over-Privileging

Granting more permissions than necessary is a significant security risk, often leading to vulnerabilities and compliance issues.

  • Avoid primitive roles for daily tasks
  • Scope roles to specific resources
Key Takeaway

Always adhere to the principle of least privilege by granting only the minimum necessary permissions to all identities.

Pro-Tip

Regularly audit your IAM policies to identify and remove any outdated or overly permissive roles.
