Key Concepts: IAM Fundamentals

Essential terms to remember

Member

An identity (Google Account, Google Group, service account, or domain) that can be granted access to a resource.

Role

A collection of permissions that can be granted to a member, defining what actions they can perform on specific resources.

Resource Hierarchy

The organizational structure of Google Cloud resources (Organization > Folders > Projects > Resources), which dictates how IAM policies are inherited.

Key Takeaway

Understanding these fundamental components is essential for effectively defining and managing access control within your Google Cloud environment.
