Core Concept

What is Cloud Governance?

Ensuring secure, cost-effective, and compliant cloud operations.

Definition

A set of rules, processes, and tools to manage your cloud environment securely, cost-effectively, and compliantly.

Purpose

Prevents security vulnerabilities, uncontrolled spending, and compliance gaps by providing a structured approach.

Key Foundation

The Google Cloud resource hierarchy is the bedrock for applying governance consistently.

Governance Pillars

Security: Protect data and infrastructure.

Cost Management: Optimize spending and resource usage.

Compliance: Meet regulatory and industry standards.

Operational Consistency: Standardize deployments and configurations.

Key Takeaway

Cloud governance is crucial for maintaining control, security, and efficiency as your organization scales its cloud adoption.
