How Policies Flow: Understanding Inheritance

Applying consistent rules across your cloud environment.

Inheritance Flow
Organization Policy

Defined at the top

Folder

Inherits policy

Project

Inherits policy

Simplified Management

Apply policies once at a high level, and they automatically propagate to all descendant resources.

Ensured Consistency

Guarantees that all resources adhere to corporate standards, preventing individual teams from deviating.

Common Mistake

Not using folders or flattening the hierarchy makes consistent policy enforcement difficult and leads to sprawl.

Key Takeaway

Policy inheritance is fundamental for scalable governance, ensuring that security and operational rules are consistently applied across your entire cloud footprint.
