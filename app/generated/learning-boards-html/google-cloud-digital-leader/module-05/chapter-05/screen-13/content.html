Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Policy Inheritance

Policies applied at higher levels of the hierarchy are automatically applied to lower-level resources.

Organization Policies

Service to programmatically enforce constraints on GCP resource configurations across an Organization or Folder.

Compliance

Adherence to specific regulatory standards and legal requirements (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR, PCI DSS).

Summary Takeaway

Leveraging policy inheritance and Organization Policies, alongside understanding compliance, is crucial for building a well-governed and secure cloud environment.
