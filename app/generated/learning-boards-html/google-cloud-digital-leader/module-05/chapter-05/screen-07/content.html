Governance Tool

Enforcing Constraints with Organization Policies

Defining what can be done to your GCP resources.

What they are

A service that allows administrators to programmatically enforce constraints on GCP resource configurations.

Key Difference from IAM

IAM defines who can do what. Organization Policies define what can be done to resources.

Inheritance

Set at the Organization or folder level, they apply to all descendant projects, providing strong guardrails.

Policy Enforcement
IAM Policy

Who can create a VM?

Organization Policy

Where can the VM be created? Can it have an external IP?

Key Takeaway

Organization Policies are essential for enforcing specific configurations and behaviors across your cloud resources, complementing IAM for comprehensive governance.
