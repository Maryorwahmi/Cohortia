Core Concept

Organizing Your Cloud with the Resource Hierarchy

A structured approach from Organization to Projects.

Organization Node

The root of your Google Cloud presence, representing your entire company or entity.

Folders

Containers that group projects, enabling logical organization by department, environment, or application.

Projects

The fundamental deployment unit where all your specific Google Cloud resources reside.

Key Takeaway

A well-defined resource hierarchy simplifies management and provides a clear structure for applying policies across your cloud environment.
