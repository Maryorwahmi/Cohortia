Regulatory Adherence

Meeting Regulatory Requirements with Google Cloud

Understanding shared responsibility and GCP's compliance offerings.

What is Compliance?

Adherence to specific regulatory standards and legal requirements (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR, PCI DSS).

GCP's Role

Google Cloud is designed with compliance in mind, offering various certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3).

Shared Responsibility Model
Google Cloud

Compliance of the cloud (infrastructure, services)

Your Organization

Compliance in the cloud (configurations, data, apps)

Your Responsibilities

Configure services, handle data, and design applications to align with regulatory standards (e.g., Cloud DLP, CMEK).

Key Takeaway

Achieving compliance in the cloud is a shared responsibility, requiring both Google Cloud's compliant infrastructure and your organization's diligent configuration and data handling.
