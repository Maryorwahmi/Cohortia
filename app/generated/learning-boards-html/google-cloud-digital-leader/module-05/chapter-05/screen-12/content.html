Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember from this chapter

Resource Hierarchy

The structured organization of Google Cloud resources: Organization > Folders > Projects > Resources.

Organization Node

The root node in the resource hierarchy, representing an entire company or entity.

Folders

Organizational units within an Organization that group projects, enabling policy enforcement.

Summary Takeaway

The Google Cloud resource hierarchy provides the essential structure for organizing and managing all your cloud assets effectively.
