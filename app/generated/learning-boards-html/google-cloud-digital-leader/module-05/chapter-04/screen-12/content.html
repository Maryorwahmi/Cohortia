Responding to Service Incidents

A structured approach to detection, investigation, and resolution.

Incident Management Workflow

Incident management is the process of responding to and resolving service incidents. A structured workflow ensures efficient resolution and continuous improvement.

  • •Relies heavily on observability tools (Logging, Monitoring, Trace).
  • •Aims to minimize impact and prevent recurrence.
  • •Essential for maintaining service reliability.
Key Takeaway

A well-defined incident management process, supported by robust observability, is crucial for quickly restoring service and learning from disruptions.

The 6 Steps of Incident Response

1. Detection

Alerts or user reports signal an issue.

2. Triage

Assess impact and severity.

3. Investigation

Use logs, metrics, traces to find root cause.

4. Mitigation

Implement temporary fixes to reduce impact.

5. Resolution

Apply permanent fix and verify stability.

6. Post-mortem

Analyze, learn, and prevent recurrence.
