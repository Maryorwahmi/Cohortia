The CIA Triad: Pillars of Information Security

Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability form the bedrock of secure systems.

Confidentiality

Ensures sensitive data is accessible only to authorized individuals and systems, preventing unauthorized disclosure. This is about keeping secrets secret.

Integrity

Guarantees that data remains accurate and unaltered, protecting against unauthorized modification or deletion. This maintains the trustworthiness and reliability of data.

Availability

Ensures that systems and data are accessible and operational when needed, guarding against service disruptions. This means users can access resources reliably.

Concrete Example

A patient's medical records must be confidential (only doctors can see them), have integrity (no one can change diagnoses without authorization), and be available (doctors can access them immediately in an emergency).

Common Misconception

Many believe security is solely about confidentiality. However, if data lacks integrity (is corrupted) or availability (cannot be accessed), its confidentiality becomes irrelevant.

Key Takeaway

The CIA Triad provides a comprehensive framework for evaluating and implementing security measures, ensuring a balanced approach to protecting information assets.
