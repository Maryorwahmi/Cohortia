Understanding the Shared Responsibility Model

Clarifying who secures what in the cloud.

The Model Defined

A framework that delineates security responsibilities between the cloud provider (Google) and the customer. It's a partnership, not a full handover of security.

Google's Responsibility

Security of the Cloud: Google secures the underlying infrastructure, including physical facilities, network, hardware, and hypervisor.

Customer's Responsibility

Security in the Cloud: You are accountable for your data, applications, operating systems, network configurations, and IAM within your projects.

Key Takeaway

The Shared Responsibility Model is crucial for defining clear security boundaries and ensuring all aspects of cloud security are addressed by either the provider or the customer.
