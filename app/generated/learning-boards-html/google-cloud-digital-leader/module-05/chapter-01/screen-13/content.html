Key Concepts

Reinforcing Your Cloud Security Knowledge

CIA Triad

Fundamental principles of information security ensuring data privacy, accuracy, and accessibility.

Shared Responsibility Model

Framework defining security responsibilities between Google (security of the cloud) and customers (security in the cloud).

Defense-in-Depth

A security strategy employing multiple layers of controls to protect against various threats, ensuring resilience.

Summary Takeaway

Mastering these core principles is essential for any digital leader to effectively navigate and secure their cloud environment, ensuring robust protection for all digital assets.
