The Principle of Least Privilege

Granting only the minimum necessary permissions to users and services.

Core Definition

Users, applications, and services should only be granted the minimum permissions required to perform their specific tasks, and no more.

Why it Matters
  • Reduces the attack surface by limiting potential entry points.
  • Minimizes the 'blast radius' of a compromised account.
  • Enhances overall security posture and compliance.
Concrete Example

A data analyst needs to read data from a BigQuery dataset. They should be granted a 'BigQuery Data Viewer' role, not 'BigQuery Admin,' which would allow them to delete tables.

Common Mistake

Over-provisioning permissions, such as granting 'Owner' or 'Editor' roles unnecessarily, which can lead to severe consequences if the account is compromised.

Key Takeaway

Always adhere to the principle of least privilege by regularly reviewing and auditing IAM policies to ensure permissions are strictly limited to what is essential.
