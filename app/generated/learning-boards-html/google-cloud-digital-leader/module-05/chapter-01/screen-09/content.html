Identifying Common Cloud Security Threats

Understanding the vectors that can compromise your cloud environment.

Misconfiguration of Services

Incorrectly configured cloud resources (e.g., public storage buckets, overly permissive IAM roles) are a leading cause of breaches.

Insecure APIs & Interfaces

Exploitable if not properly secured, authenticated, and managed, providing potential entry points for attackers.

Account Hijacking & Insider Threats

Compromised credentials or misuse of authorized access by internal users can lead to significant data loss or system compromise.

Example Threat

A developer accidentally grants a public IAM role to a Cloud Storage bucket containing customer PII, leading to unauthorized data exposure.

Other Threats
  • Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks
  • Data breaches and exfiltration
  • Malware and ransomware
  • Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs)
Key Takeaway

Proactive identification and mitigation of both cloud-specific and traditional security threats are essential for maintaining a secure cloud environment.
