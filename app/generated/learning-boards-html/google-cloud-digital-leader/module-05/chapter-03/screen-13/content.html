Key Considerations for Data Protection

  • •Prioritize Key Management: Regularly rotate keys, use IAM for access control to KMS, and never hardcode keys in applications.
  • •Regularly Audit Configurations: Continuously review encryption settings and DLP policies to ensure alignment with compliance and governance.
  • •Understand Key Implications: Be aware of the operational and recovery implications of GMEK, CMEK, and CSEK before implementation.
  • •Combine Services: Leverage default encryption, customer-managed keys, and DLP for a multi-layered defense.

Warning: Losing a CSEK key means permanent data loss. Ensure robust key management processes are in place.
