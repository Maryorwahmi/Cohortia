Key Concepts: Part 2

Advanced key management and data loss prevention

Customer-Managed Keys (CMEK)

Customers use Google Cloud KMS to generate and manage encryption keys.

Customer-Supplied Keys (CSEK)

Customers provide their own encryption keys, which Google uses and discards.

Cloud Key Management Service (KMS)

Google Cloud's centralized service for managing cryptographic keys.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Service for discovering, classifying, and redacting sensitive data.

Summary Takeaway

CMEK, CSEK, KMS, and DLP provide advanced tools for granular key control and sensitive data protection in GCP.
