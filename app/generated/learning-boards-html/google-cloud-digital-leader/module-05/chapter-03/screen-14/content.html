Key Concepts: Part 1

Essential terms and definitions to remember

Encryption at Rest

Protecting data when it is stored on persistent storage devices.

Encryption in Transit

Protecting data as it moves across networks between systems or services.

Google-Managed Keys (GMEK)

Default encryption where Google manages keys transparently for the customer.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding encryption at rest, in transit, and Google-managed keys forms the bedrock of data protection in GCP.
