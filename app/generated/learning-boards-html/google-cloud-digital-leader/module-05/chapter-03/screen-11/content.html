Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Google Cloud DLP identifies, classifies, and protects sensitive data across various GCP sources, preventing accidental exposure.

  • •Sensitive Data Types: Detects credit card numbers, SSNs, PII, and more.
  • •Scan Data At Rest & In Transit: Works across Cloud Storage, BigQuery, text streams, and other services.
  • •Compliance Aid: Essential for meeting regulations like GDPR and HIPAA.

Example: DLP can scan Cloud Storage buckets to find unencrypted files containing Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
