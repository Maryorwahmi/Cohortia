DLP: Redaction and Policy Enforcement

DLP extends beyond discovery to actively protect sensitive data through automated redaction and policy-driven controls.

  • •Automated Redaction: Replaces or masks sensitive information (e.g., credit card numbers) with placeholders.
  • •Policy Enforcement: Helps enforce rules to prevent accidental exposure or exfiltration of data.
  • •Real-time Protection: Can scan data in transit to prevent sensitive information from reaching unauthorized destinations.

Example: Automatically redacting PII from customer support logs before they are stored or analyzed.
