Customer-Supplied Encryption Keys (CSEK)

CSEK offers the highest level of key control, where you provide your own encryption key to Google Cloud, which is then discarded after use.

  • •No Key Storage by Google: Google uses the key for encryption/decryption and then discards it.
  • •Full Customer Responsibility: You are solely responsible for key storage, rotation, and recovery.
  • •High Risk: Losing a key means permanent data loss.

Common Mistake: Choosing CSEK without fully understanding the significant operational overhead and irreversible risks involved.

CSEK: Key Flow

You Supply Key
Google Uses Key (Temporarily)
Key Discarded / Data Encrypted
Key Takeaway

CSEK offers maximum key control but demands full customer responsibility for key management, making it suitable for highly specialized, high-risk scenarios.
