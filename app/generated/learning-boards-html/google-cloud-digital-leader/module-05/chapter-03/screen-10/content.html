Encryption Key Management Options

GMEK

Google-Managed Encryption Keys: Default, automatic, and transparent encryption. Google manages all key aspects.

  • •Control: Low (Google manages)
  • •Complexity: Low
  • •Use Case: General purpose, default security

CMEK

Customer-Managed Encryption Keys: You manage keys via Cloud KMS. Google performs encryption/decryption.

  • •Control: Medium (You manage lifecycle)
  • •Complexity: Medium
  • •Use Case: Compliance, enhanced governance

CSEK

Customer-Supplied Encryption Keys: You provide the key, Google uses it temporarily and discards it.

  • •Control: High (You supply & manage)
  • •Complexity: High
  • •Use Case: Extreme key isolation, specialized needs
Key Takeaway

The choice of encryption key management depends on your organization's specific control requirements, compliance needs, and operational capacity.
