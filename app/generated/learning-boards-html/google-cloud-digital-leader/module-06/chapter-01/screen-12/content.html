COMMON PITFALLS

Navigating the Challenges of Digital Transformation

Beyond technology: leadership, culture, and legacy systems.

Common Mistakes

  • Viewing DX solely as an IT project.
  • Lack of strong leadership buy-in.
  • Resistance to cultural change.
  • Struggling with legacy systems and data silos.
  • Insufficient skilled personnel.

Impact of Mistakes

  • Failure to deliver full potential of new tech.
  • Limited adoption and resistance from employees.
  • Inability to integrate new solutions effectively.
  • Slow progress and wasted investment.
Key Takeaway

Digital transformation requires a holistic approach, addressing organizational, cultural, and technical challenges beyond just technology adoption.

Challenges & Barriers

IT-Centric View

Not a business initiative.

Legacy Systems
