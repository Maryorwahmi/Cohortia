Key Concepts

Essential Terms for Digital Transformation

Digital Transformation (DX)

The strategic adoption of digital technology to improve processes, culture, and customer experiences to meet changing business and market requirements.

Agility

The ability of an organization to respond quickly and effectively to changes in the market or business environment, often enabled by cloud infrastructure.

Scalability

The capability of a system to handle a growing amount of work or its potential to be enlarged to accommodate that growth, a core benefit of cloud computing.

Summary Takeaway

Digital transformation is a strategic, agile, and scalable journey, fundamentally reshaping how businesses operate and deliver value.
