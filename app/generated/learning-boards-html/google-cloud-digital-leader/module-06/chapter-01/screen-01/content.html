Understanding Digital Transformation with Google Cloud

Leveraging Cloud to Reshape Business and Customer Value

Digital transformation is a fundamental shift in how an organization operates and delivers value. It involves leveraging digital technologies to create new or modify existing business processes, culture, and customer experiences to meet changing market requirements. This chapter explores its core pillars and how Google Cloud acts as a critical enabler.

Key Idea

Digital transformation is a holistic shift, not just tech adoption, driven by strategic vision and cloud capabilities.
