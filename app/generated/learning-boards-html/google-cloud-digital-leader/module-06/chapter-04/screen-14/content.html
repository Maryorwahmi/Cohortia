Key Concepts: Operational Excellence & Improvement

Critical tools and principles for ongoing cloud optimization.

Committed Use Discounts (CUDs)

Discounts offered by Google Cloud for committing to a specific level of resource usage over a 1-year or 3-year term.

Cloud Monitoring

Google Cloud's service for collecting, visualizing, and alerting on metrics and events from cloud resources.

Continuous Improvement

An ongoing effort to improve products, services, or processes within a cloud environment.

Summary Takeaway

Leverage CUDs for cost savings, utilize Cloud Monitoring for deep operational visibility, and embrace continuous improvement as an ongoing cycle to ensure sustained cloud success.
