Example Scenario

A company uses labels like team:frontend and environment:prod on their Compute Engine instances. This allows them to generate billing reports showing the exact cloud spend for the frontend team's production resources, enabling accurate chargebacks.

Key Benefits
  • •Granular Insights: Understand costs by service, project, and custom tags.
  • •Accountability: Enable chargebacks or showbacks to teams.
  • •Informed Decisions: Support better budgeting and resource planning.
  • •Security: Control access to billing information with IAM policies.
Key Takeaway

Leverage Cloud Billing reports, labels, and projects to gain deep cost visibility and allocation, fostering financial accountability across your organization.
