Embracing Continuous Improvement

An ongoing iterative process for optimizing your cloud environment.

The Continuous Improvement Cycle
Review
Optimize
Learn
Iterate
Why Continuous Improvement?
  • •Dynamic Cloud: Adapts to evolving services and pricing.
  • •Sustained Value: Ensures cloud investments continue to deliver benefits.
  • •Risk Mitigation: Identifies and addresses security or performance issues early.
  • •Business Alignment: Keeps cloud strategy aligned with changing business needs.
Key Takeaway

Continuous improvement is an essential, ongoing cycle of reviewing, optimizing, and learning to ensure your cloud environment remains efficient, secure, and aligned with business objectives.
