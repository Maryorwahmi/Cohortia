FinOps Strategy

Optimizing Resource Allocation and Commitments

Leveraging right-sizing, CUDs, and SUDs for cost efficiency on Google Cloud.

Right-sizing Resources

Provisioning Compute Engine VMs, Cloud SQL instances, and other services with appropriate CPU, memory, and storage for actual workload, avoiding over-provisioning.

  • •Identify underutilized resources with Cloud Monitoring.
  • •Adjust machine types to match demand.

Committed Use Discounts (CUDs)

Committing to a certain level of resource usage (e.g., vCPUs, memory) for 1-year or 3-year terms in exchange for significant discounts.

  • •Requires careful forecasting of stable workloads.
  • •Best for predictable, long-running services.

Sustained Use Discounts (SUDs)

Automatic discounts applied to Compute Engine resources that run for a significant portion of a billing month, without requiring a commitment.

  • •No upfront commitment needed.
  • •Ideal for consistent, non-committed workloads.
Example Scenario

A development team identifies that their staging environment VMs are consistently underutilized. By right-sizing them, they reduce monthly costs by 15%. For their production database, which runs 24/7, they purchase a 3-year CUD, saving 50% compared to on-demand pricing.

Key Takeaway

Leverage right-sizing to match resources to actual demand, and utilize CUDs for predictable workloads and SUDs for consistent, non-committed usage to significantly reduce cloud costs.
