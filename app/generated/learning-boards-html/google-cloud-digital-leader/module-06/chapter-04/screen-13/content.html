Key Concepts: Measuring & Optimizing Cloud

Essential terms and practices for cloud success.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Measurable values that demonstrate how effectively a company is achieving key business objectives through cloud adoption.

FinOps

A cultural practice combining finance, operations, and development to maximize business value from cloud spend.

Right-sizing

Adjusting cloud resource allocation to match actual workload requirements, avoiding over-provisioning.

Summary Takeaway

Effective cloud management hinges on defining clear KPIs, adopting FinOps for financial accountability, and continuously right-sizing resources to optimize costs and performance.
