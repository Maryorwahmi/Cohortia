Core Concept

Defining Cloud Success KPIs

Measuring cloud adoption beyond technical metrics to capture true business value.

Cost Savings

Reduced infrastructure costs compared to on-premises or previous cloud spend.

Operational Efficiency

Reduced deployment times, fewer incidents, and streamlined workflows.

Application Performance

Improved response times, higher availability, and enhanced reliability.

Time-to-Market

Faster delivery of new features and products to end-users.

Example Scenario

A successful cloud migration might be measured by a 20% reduction in infrastructure costs, a 50% decrease in application downtime, or a 30% acceleration in product release cycles.

Why Business KPIs?
  • •Align with Goals: Connect cloud investments directly to organizational objectives.
  • •Demonstrate Value: Show stakeholders the tangible benefits beyond technical metrics.
  • •Drive Decisions: Provide data-driven insights for strategic planning and resource allocation.
  • •Avoid Cloud Sprawl: Prevent uncontrolled growth and unexpected cost escalations.
Key Takeaway

Focus on business-centric KPIs to truly measure cloud success, ensuring alignment with organizational goals and demonstrating tangible value.
