Retain and Retire: Strategic Decisions

Managing Applications That Stay or Go

05

Retain (Revisit)

Keep certain applications on-premises due to strict regulatory requirements, specific hardware dependencies, or current unsuitability for cloud migration.

Why Retain?

Ensures compliance and stability for applications with unique constraints, allowing for future re-evaluation.

06

Retire

Decommissioning applications that are no longer needed or used, reducing overall migration scope and saving resources.

Why Retire?

Reduces complexity, eliminates maintenance costs, and frees up resources for more strategic initiatives.

Key Takeaway

Retain keeps applications on-premises due to specific constraints, while Retire decommissions unused applications to optimize resources and reduce costs.
