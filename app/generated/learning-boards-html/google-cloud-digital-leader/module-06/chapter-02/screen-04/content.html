What is a CAF?

A Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) is a comprehensive set of guidelines, best practices, and tools designed to help organizations plan, execute, and manage their transition to cloud computing.

Key Areas Covered

Typically addresses strategy, people, processes, and technology, providing a holistic approach to cloud transformation.

Benefits of a CAF

Mitigates risks, accelerates migration, ensures alignment with business objectives, and maximizes cloud investment value.
