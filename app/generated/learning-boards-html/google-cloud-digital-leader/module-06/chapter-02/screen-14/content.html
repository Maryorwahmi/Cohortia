Key Concepts

Reinforcing Your Migration Knowledge

Replatform (Lift and Tinker)

Making minor cloud-specific optimizations to an application during migration, such as using managed services.

Refactor (Rearchitect)

Fundamentally redesigning an application to leverage cloud-native services and architectures for maximum benefits.

Application Assessment

The process of inventorying, analyzing, and categorizing existing applications to determine their suitability for cloud migration.

Summary Takeaway

Replatform offers optimization, Refactor enables cloud-native transformation, and thorough Application Assessment is crucial for strategic migration planning.
