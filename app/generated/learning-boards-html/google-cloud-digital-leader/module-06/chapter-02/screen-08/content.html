Refactor: Embracing Cloud-Native Architectures

Redesigning for Maximum Cloud Benefits

Definition

Significantly modifying an application's architecture to fully leverage cloud-native capabilities, such as microservices or serverless functions.

Key Benefit

Offers the most long-term benefits in scalability, resilience, cost optimization, and developer agility, but requires significant effort.

GCP Examples

Breaking a monolith into microservices on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), or using Cloud Functions for event-driven logic.
