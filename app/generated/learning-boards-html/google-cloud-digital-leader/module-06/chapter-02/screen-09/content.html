Repurchase: Replacing with SaaS Solutions

Adopting New Cloud-Native Applications

Definition

Replacing an existing application with a new, cloud-native Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) solution.

Key Benefit

Offloads operational burden, provides access to modern features, and often results in cost savings and improved security.

GCP Examples

Moving from an on-premises email server to Google Workspace, or replacing a custom CRM with a leading SaaS CRM.
