Wi-Fi Generation

802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6/6E): Designed for High-Density Environments

Optimizing efficiency and capacity with OFDMA and BSS Coloring

OFDMA (Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiple Access)

Divides a channel into smaller resource units, allowing an AP to serve multiple clients simultaneously within a single channel, greatly improving efficiency.

Target Wake Time (TWT)

Allows devices to schedule specific times to wake up and send/receive data, extending battery life for client devices.

BSS Coloring

Reduces co-channel interference by allowing devices to identify and ignore traffic from neighboring BSSs operating on the same channel.

MU-MIMO (Multi-User MIMO)

Enhanced for both uplink and downlink, enabling the AP to communicate with multiple clients simultaneously, both sending and receiving data.

Example: OFDMA vs. OFDM

Think of a delivery truck. With OFDM (older Wi-Fi), the truck delivers one package at a time. With OFDMA (Wi-Fi 6), the truck can carry and deliver multiple packages to different recipients on a single trip, making it far more efficient.

Key Takeaway

Wi-Fi 6 focuses on efficiency and capacity in dense environments through innovations like OFDMA, TWT, and BSS Coloring, significantly improving overall network performance.
