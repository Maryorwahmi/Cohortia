Key Concepts: Wi-Fi Standards and Generations

Recap of the evolutionary journey of 802.11

IEEE 802.11

The foundational set of standards for Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN) communication.

Wi-Fi Generations

Common names (e.g., Wi-Fi 4, 5, 6, 7) for major 802.11 standards, indicating significant advancements.

802.11n (Wi-Fi 4)

Introduced MIMO and channel bonding, operating in both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands for higher speeds.

Summary Takeaway

The continuous evolution of 802.11 standards, marked by distinct Wi-Fi generations, has consistently pushed the boundaries of wireless speed, capacity, and efficiency, making Wi-Fi a cornerstone of modern connectivity.
