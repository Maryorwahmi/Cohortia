Wi-Fi Generation

802.11ac (Wi-Fi 5): Focusing on 5 GHz for Gigabit Speeds

Achieving unprecedented speeds through wider channels and advanced modulation

802.11n (Wi-Fi 4)

  • •2.4 GHz & 5 GHz bands
  • •Up to 4 spatial streams
  • •Max 40 MHz channels
  • •64-QAM modulation
  • •Theoretical max 600 Mbps

Key Enhancement: 5 GHz Focus

By operating exclusively in the 5 GHz band, 802.11ac leveraged cleaner spectrum and more available channels for higher performance.

802.11ac (Wi-Fi 5)

  • •5 GHz band ONLY
  • •Up to 8 spatial streams
  • •Max 160 MHz channels
  • •256-QAM modulation
  • •Theoretical multi-gigabit speeds
Key Takeaway

Wi-Fi 5 significantly boosted speeds for demanding applications by leveraging the 5 GHz band with wider channels and advanced modulation, setting a new benchmark for wireless throughput.
