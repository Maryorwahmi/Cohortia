Spectrum Overview

  • Definition: Entire range of electromagnetic radiation.
  • Ordered by: Frequency or wavelength.
  • Components: Radio, microwave, infrared, visible light, UV, X-rays, gamma rays.
  • Wi-Fi Focus: Primarily Radio Frequency (RF) and Microwave bands.

Why it Matters for Wi-Fi

Different frequencies have distinct characteristics affecting range, penetration, and data capacity. Understanding this helps optimize network performance.

Key Takeaway

The EM spectrum is the foundation of wireless communication, with RF and microwave bands being crucial for Wi-Fi due to their unique propagation characteristics.
