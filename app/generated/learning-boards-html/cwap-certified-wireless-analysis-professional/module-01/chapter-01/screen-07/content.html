Wi-Fi Bands

The 2.4 GHz Band: Range vs. Congestion

A widely used band with good reach but significant interference challenges.

Advantages

  • Good Range: Signals travel further.
  • Better Penetration: Can pass through walls and obstacles more easily.
  • Widespread Compatibility: Supported by almost all Wi-Fi devices.

Disadvantages

  • High Congestion: Shared with many other devices.
  • Limited Channels: Only 3 non-overlapping channels (1, 6, 11).
  • Lower Data Rates: Generally slower speeds due to interference.

Common Interference Sources

Bluetooth devices, cordless phones, baby monitors, and microwave ovens all operate in the 2.4 GHz band, contributing to significant signal interference and reduced Wi-Fi performance.

Band Details

  • Frequency Range: 2.400-2.4835 GHz (most regions).
  • IEEE Standard: Primarily 802.11b/g/n.
  • Typical Use: Basic internet browsing, general connectivity.
Key Takeaway

The 2.4 GHz band offers range and penetration but is highly susceptible to congestion and interference, limiting its performance in dense environments.
