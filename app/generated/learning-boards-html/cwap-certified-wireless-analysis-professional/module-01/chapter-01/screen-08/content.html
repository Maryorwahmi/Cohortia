Wi-Fi Bands

The 5 GHz Band: Speed and Capacity

Offering higher data rates and more channels, but with shorter range.

Advantages

  • Higher Data Rates: Supports faster speeds and throughput.
  • More Channels: Many non-overlapping channels reduce interference.
  • Less Congestion: Fewer non-Wi-Fi devices operate in this band.

Disadvantages

  • Shorter Range: Signals don't travel as far as 2.4 GHz.
  • Easily Obstructed: More susceptible to absorption by walls and objects.
  • Requires More APs: Often needs more access points for full coverage.

Ideal Use Cases

Best for high-bandwidth applications like 4K video streaming, online gaming, and large file transfers, especially in environments with many Wi-Fi devices.

Band Details

  • Frequency Range: 5.150-5.850 GHz (regional variations).
  • IEEE Standard: Primarily 802.11a/n/ac/ax.
  • Typical Use: High-performance, high-density Wi-Fi.
Key Takeaway

The 5 GHz band offers superior speed and capacity due to more bandwidth and channels, but its shorter range and susceptibility to obstructions require careful network planning.
