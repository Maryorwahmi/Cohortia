Discovering Your Local Wi-Fi Environment

Using system tools to identify active Wi-Fi bands and channels.

Windows Users

Use netsh wlan show networks mode=bssid in Command Prompt to see radio type and channels.

macOS Users

Option-click Wi-Fi icon > Wireless Diagnostics > Window > Scan to view bands and channels.

Linux Users

Use iwlist <interface> scan or sudo iw dev <interface> scan in terminal.

Reflection: What to Observe

Pay attention to the number of networks on 2.4 GHz vs. 5 GHz, and look for overlapping channels (e.g., 2.4 GHz channels 1, 6, 11 are non-overlapping). This reveals local spectrum utilization.

Activity Purpose

  • Identify active Wi-Fi bands (2.4, 5, 6 GHz).
  • Discover channels in use by nearby networks.
  • Observe channel overlap and potential interference.
  • Gain practical insight into spectrum utilization.
Key Takeaway

Hands-on scanning of your local Wi-Fi environment provides invaluable practical experience in identifying active bands and channels, revealing real-world spectrum utilization and potential interference issues.
