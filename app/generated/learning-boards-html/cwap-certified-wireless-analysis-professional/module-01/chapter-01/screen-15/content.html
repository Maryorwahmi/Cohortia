Key Concepts

Essential ideas from this chapter (continued)

2.4 GHz Band

Common Wi-Fi band offering good range but prone to congestion.

5 GHz Band

Wi-Fi band offering higher data rates and more channels but shorter range.

Regulatory Bodies

Organizations (e.g., FCC, ETSI) that manage spectrum allocation and wireless device regulations.

Summary Takeaway

Effective wireless analysis requires understanding the characteristics and trade-offs of different Wi-Fi bands, alongside the crucial role of regulatory bodies in governing spectrum usage.
