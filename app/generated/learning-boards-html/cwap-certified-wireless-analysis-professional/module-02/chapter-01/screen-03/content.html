Applying Your Knowledge: Practical 802.11 Skills

Develop the ability to analyze and interpret wireless traffic effectively.

Bloom's Taxonomy:LearnUnderstandApplyMaster
05

Interpret Frame Control Flags

Accurately interpret the various flags within the Frame Control field, such as ToDS and FromDS, to determine frame direction and context. This is critical for understanding traffic flow.

Contextual AwarenessVERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
06

Differentiate Address Fields

Differentiate between the up to four address fields in 802.11 frames, understanding their roles as receiver, transmitter, source, and destination. This prevents common misinterpretations in analysis.

Accurate AddressingVERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07

Analyze Sequence Control

Analyze the Sequence Control field to understand frame ordering and fragmentation. This helps in reconstructing fragmented data and ensuring reliable delivery in challenging environments.

Reliable DeliveryVERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
08

Perform Wireshark Dissection

Perform detailed dissection of 802.11 frames in Wireshark, identifying and interpreting all key fields to understand frame purpose and context. This is the ultimate practical skill for wireless analysis.

Advanced Packet AnalysisVERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
