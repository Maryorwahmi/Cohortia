Sequence Control Field: Ordering and Fragmentation

Ensuring reliable and ordered delivery of 802.11 frames through sequence and fragment numbers.

Sequence Number (12 bits)

Identifies the order of frames transmitted by a station, detecting duplicates and ensuring correct processing order.

Fragment Number (4 bits)

Used when a frame is broken into smaller fragments; each fragment has the same sequence number but a unique fragment number.

Reassembly & Reliability

These numbers enable the receiver to reassemble fragmented frames and ensure overall reliable data delivery.

Key Takeaway

The Sequence Control field is vital for managing frame order and fragmentation, ensuring data integrity and reliable communication.

Sequence Control in Action
Original Frame (Seq: 100)Too large for single transmission
Fragment 1 (Seq: 100, Frag: 0)First part of the frame
Fragment 2 (Seq: 100, Frag: 1)Second part of the frame
Fragment 3 (Seq: 100, Frag: 2)Last part of the frame
Example: Large File Transfer

When transferring a large file, it might be fragmented. The sequence and fragment numbers ensure all pieces are received and reassembled correctly.
