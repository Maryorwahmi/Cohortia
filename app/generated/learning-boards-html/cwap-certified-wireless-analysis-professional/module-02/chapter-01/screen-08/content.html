Frame Type Deep Dive

Management Frames: The Wireless Connection Lifecycle

Exploring how devices discover, authenticate, and associate with Wi-Fi networks.

Connection Establishment

These frames initiate and manage the process of a client joining a wireless network.

Key Examples

Beacons (AP advertisement), Probe Requests/Responses (client discovery), Authentication, Association.

Connection Termination

Disassociation and Deauthentication frames are used to gracefully end a wireless connection.

Key Takeaway

Management frames are essential for every stage of a wireless client's interaction with an access point, from discovery to disconnection.

Wireless Connection Process (Management Frames)
1. DiscoveryBeacons, Probe Requests/Responses
2. AuthenticationVerifying Client Identity
3. AssociationJoining the BSS
4. Data ExchangeUser Traffic (via Data Frames)
5. DisconnectionDisassociation, Deauthentication
Example: Roaming

When a client roams between APs, management frames facilitate the re-association process to maintain connectivity.
