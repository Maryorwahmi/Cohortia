Understanding 802.11 Address Fields: Beyond Source and Destination

Navigating the up to four MAC address fields and their roles in wireless communication.

Up to Four Addresses

802.11 frames can contain up to four MAC address fields, unlike simpler Ethernet frames.

Receiver (RA) & Transmitter (TA)

Address 1 is the Receiver Address (RA), and Address 2 is the Transmitter Address (TA) of the immediate hop.

Source (SA) & Destination (DA)

Address 3 is typically the original Source Address (SA) or Destination Address (DA) of the IP packet.

Key Takeaway

The multiple address fields in 802.11 frames are crucial for tracking frame flow in complex wireless environments.

802.11 Address Field Roles
Address 1 (RA)Receiver Address (Next Hop)
Address 2 (TA)Transmitter Address (Current Sender)
Address 3 (SA/DA)Source/Destination Address (Original Packet)
Address 4 (WDS)Used in Wireless Distribution Systems
