Key Concepts: 802.11 Foundations

Essential terms and definitions for understanding wireless communication layers and frames.

OSI Model

A conceptual framework describing networking system functions in seven layers.

802.11 Protocol Stack

The specific implementation of PHY and MAC layers for wireless local area networks.

MAC Layer

Manages media access control, addressing, and frame formatting in 802.11 networks.

PHY Layer

The lowest layer, responsible for actual transmission and reception of radio signals.

802.11 Frame

The basic unit of data exchanged over an 802.11 wireless network.

Management Frame

Used for establishing, maintaining, and terminating wireless connections.

Summary Takeaway

The 802.11 standard builds upon the OSI model, with its MAC and PHY layers defining how wireless frames are structured and managed for all network operations.
