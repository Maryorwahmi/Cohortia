Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Beacon Frame

An 802.11 Management frame broadcast periodically by an AP to advertise its presence, SSID, and network capabilities.

Probe Request Frame

An 802.11 Management frame sent by a client to actively discover available networks, either for a specific SSID or as a wildcard.

Probe Response Frame

An 802.11 Management frame sent by an AP in response to a Probe Request, providing network information.

Summary Takeaway

Beacon and Probe frames are critical for network discovery, while Authentication frames initiate the client's identity verification with the AP.
