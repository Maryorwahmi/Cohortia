The 802.11 Connection Sequence

A Step-by-Step Journey from Discovery to Data Exchange

STEP 1

Discovery

Client finds available networks via Beacons or Probes.

STEP 2

Authentication

Client proves its identity to the Access Point.

STEP 3

Association

Establishes a logical link for data exchange.

Key Takeaway

The 802.11 connection sequence is a precise, multi-step process involving discovery, authentication, and association, each critical for establishing a functional wireless link.
