Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Association Frame

802.11 Management frames exchanged between a client and an AP to establish a logical link for data exchange, assigning an Association ID (AID).

Deauthentication Frame

An 802.11 Management frame sent by a client or AP to definitively terminate the 802.11 connection.

Deauth Attack

A type of denial-of-service attack where an attacker sends spoofed deauthentication frames to disconnect legitimate clients from a Wi-Fi network.

Summary Takeaway

Association frames establish the data link, while Disassociation and Deauthentication frames manage connection termination, with the latter being a significant security vulnerability.
