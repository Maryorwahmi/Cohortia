Skills You'll Master in This Chapter

01

Identify Frame Subtypes

Learn to recognize and distinguish between common 802.11 Management frame subtypes, such as Beacons, Probes, Authentication, and Association frames. This skill is fundamental for accurate packet analysis and understanding wireless communication flows.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Explain Frame Roles

Articulate the specific function and purpose of each Management frame within the 802.11 connection establishment process. This includes understanding the sequence and interaction of frames from discovery to association.
