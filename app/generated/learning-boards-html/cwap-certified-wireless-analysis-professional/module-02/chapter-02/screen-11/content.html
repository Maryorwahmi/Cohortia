Deauthentication Attacks: Exploiting Management Frames

Understanding a Critical Wireless Denial-of-Service Vulnerability

Denial-of-Service (DoS)

Attackers forcibly disconnect legitimate users from a Wi-Fi network.

MAC Address Spoofing

Attackers send deauthentication frames pretending to be the AP or client.

Lack of Protection

Management frames in 802.11 lack cryptographic protection, making them vulnerable.

ATTACK MECHANISM

Attacker Spoofs MAC

Impersonates AP or client.

Sends Deauth Frames

Targets legitimate clients.

Clients Disconnect

Forced off the network.

Key Takeaway

Deauthentication attacks exploit a fundamental vulnerability in 802.11 management frames, allowing attackers to perform denial-of-service by forcing clients offline.
