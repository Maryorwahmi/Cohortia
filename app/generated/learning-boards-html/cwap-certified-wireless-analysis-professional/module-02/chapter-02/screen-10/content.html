Graceful Exits: Disassociation and Deauthentication

Voluntarily Leaving or Forcibly Terminating a Connection

Disassociation Frame

  • Client voluntarily leaves the BSS.
  • Indicates a temporary departure; client might return soon.
  • AP removes the client's association entry.
  • Less severe than deauthentication.
Key Insight

Disassociation is a polite way to temporarily step away from the network, maintaining the possibility of a quick return without full re-establishment.

Deauthentication Frame

  • Definitive termination of the 802.11 connection.
  • Requires full re-authentication and re-association to rejoin.
  • Can be sent by either client or AP.
  • Often used in denial-of-service attacks.
Security Risk

Deauthentication frames can be exploited in 'deauth attacks' to forcibly disconnect legitimate users from a Wi-Fi network.
