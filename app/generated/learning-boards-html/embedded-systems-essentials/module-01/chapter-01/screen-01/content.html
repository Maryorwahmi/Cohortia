What Are Embedded Systems?
Focused computers that run a single, dedicated task inside another device.

Embedded systems are everywhere: they sense the world, run a program optimized for one job, and act through outputs. They differ from general-purpose computers by their constraints and the fact they are often hidden inside a product. In this chapter you will identify components, compare platforms, and learn how real-time and resource limits change design choices.
