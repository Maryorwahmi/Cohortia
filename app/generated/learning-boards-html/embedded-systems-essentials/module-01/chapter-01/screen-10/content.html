Common interfaces
  • GPIO: digital read/write for buttons and LEDs.
  • ADC: converts analog voltages to digital values.
  • UART: simple serial communication for logs and modules.
  • SPI: fast synchronous bus for short distance modules.
  • I2C: two-wire bus that supports many devices on the same lines.
Concrete example
A temperature sensor may use I2C to provide temperature data. Your firmware polls or receives data, then updates an LCD over SPI and controls fans via GPIO-driven relays or PWM outputs.
Misconception
Not all devices support all buses; choosing a sensor because it's cheapest can force complex wiring or slow updates if the interface doesn't meet timing or voltage requirements.
Key Takeaway

Match the sensor or actuator bus to your timing, wiring, and voltage requirements to avoid integration issues.
