How we will practice
  • Inspect three devices at home and list inputs, outputs, and the likely processor.
  • Compare a Raspberry Pi, an Arduino, and the TI LaunchPad for a simple sensor project.
  • Explain a trade-off between power use and response time in one paragraph.
These activities link the vocabulary to real hardware choices.
Assessment idea
Match five device descriptions to the correct platform type.
