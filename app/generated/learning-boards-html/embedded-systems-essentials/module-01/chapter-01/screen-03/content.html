Platform selection checklist
Timing: Does the task require millisecond responses?
Power: Will it run on battery for long intervals?
Interface: Need for display, keyboard, or networking?
Complexity: Does the app need multiple programs or an OS?
Use this checklist when you inspect a device or design a prototype.
Common misconception
A small single-board computer is not always the best choice. It may be overpowered, draw too much power, and complicate simple real-time tasks. Microcontrollers often provide a simpler, more reliable solution for dedicated control.
Practice: pick a device and argue for a microcontroller or single-board computer in one paragraph.
