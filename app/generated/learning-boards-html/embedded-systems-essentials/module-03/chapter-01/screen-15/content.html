Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Persistent main loop
main runs initialization once and keeps the device operating in an infinite loop until power is removed.
Fixed-width types
Use uint8_t, int16_t, uint32_t to guarantee sizes and avoid cross-platform surprises.
Bitwise control and masking
Use masks and bitwise operators to manipulate hardware registers safely and clearly.
Summary takeaway
Design firmware with explicit types, clear structure, and careful bit operations. Verify behavior in a debugger and document hardware assumptions.
