Comparison

Heap vs static allocation

Compare risks and benefits and see practical alternatives to malloc.

Heap (malloc)
Flexible, but risks fragmentation and unpredictable failures on small RAM.
Static pools / globals
Deterministic, predictable memory use, and easier to reason about for long-running devices.
Key Takeaway

Choose deterministic allocation strategies in embedded systems; reserve heap for noncritical, well-tested subsystems only.

Concrete scenario
Packet buffer pool

Instead of malloc per packet, reserve a fixed pool of buffers and hand them out and reclaim them deterministically to prevent fragmentation.

Misconception check

Avoid assuming heap errors are rare; on constrained devices a single leak or fragmentation pattern can eventually starve the system.
