Assessment Idea

Why avoid dynamic allocation?

The slide presents a sample answer and a safe alternative design to explain to an assessor.

Risks of malloc
  • Heap fragmentation
  • Small heap size, leaks exhaust memory
  • Unpredictable allocation failures
Safe alternative

Use a statically allocated pool of fixed-size buffers with a free list. Allocate at startup and reuse buffers deterministically.

Key Takeaway

Explain both the risks of heap use and a concrete deterministic alternative to earn full credit.
