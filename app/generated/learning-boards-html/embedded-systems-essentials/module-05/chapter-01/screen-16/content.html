Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › What to Remember
Use constants
Define BUFFER_SIZE and reuse it for declarations and loops to avoid mismatch errors.
Validate
Check pointers and sizes in functions and ensure indexes stay within bounds.
Document policy
State whether the buffer overwrites or blocks and whether overwrites are signaled to the system.
Parting thought

Small design choices about types and buffer policy have outsized impact in embedded systems—be deliberate and document assumptions.
