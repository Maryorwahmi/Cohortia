Process Flow
Measurement guards
  1. 1. Choose a reload comfortably larger than expected measurement.
  2. 2. Sample start and end; if end > start, account for wrap by adding reload+1.
  3. 3. Repeat the measurement several times and take the median.
  4. 4. Correlate cycle counts with inputs to detect scaling patterns.
Example correction
If start is 1000 and end is 0x00FFFF00 (larger numerically), detect the wrap and compute elapsed as (start + (reload - end) + 1) or use a standard wrap formula.
Pro Tip

For short functions, pick a full reload and keep the measured window small; for longer tasks, measure in chunks and accumulate.

Noise reduction
Run 11 or more iterations and choose the median; remove outliers caused by interrupts or cache misses to see consistent cost.
Plot cycles for inputs 1,5,10,15 to identify whether cost grows linearly, quadratically, or shows plateaus due to cache or peripheral waits.
Check your understanding
If your measured cycles increase irregularly, consider whether interrupts or hardware involvement, not just algorithmic complexity, are causing variation.
