From cycles to decisions
Collect cycle counts for representative inputs, display them, and use the pattern to choose optimizations.
  • Record input, cycles, and median across runs.
  • Plot cycles vs input to spot scaling behavior.
  • Prioritize functions with the highest cycles per call or worst scaling.
Practical tip
Capture environment metadata with your measurements: CPU frequency, interrupts enabled, and whether caches or peripherals were involved for reproducibility.
Key Takeaway

Use cycle counts as evidence to prioritize optimizations and verify timing requirements against real hardware behavior.
