Module 07 › Chapter 7.2 › Final Summary
Interrupt
Hardware event that triggers a short handler for immediate response.
Flag clearing
ISRs must clear peripheral flags explicitly to avoid repeated triggers.
ISR caution
Keep ISRs short, non-blocking, and use volatile for shared simple state but handle atomicity carefully.
Parting thought
Plan interrupt priorities and handler behavior early in your design to ensure reliable, real-time responses.
