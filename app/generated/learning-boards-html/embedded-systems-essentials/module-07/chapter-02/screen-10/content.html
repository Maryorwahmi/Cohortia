Module 07 › Chapter 7.2 › Clearing Interrupt Flags
Why clear flags?
Clearing the hardware flag tells the peripheral the event has been handled; otherwise the interrupt remains pending and will retrigger.
Example action
Write GPIO_PORTF_ICR_R |= 0x10 to clear PF4 in the ISR before returning.
Misconception
Returning from the ISR does not clear peripheral flags automatically; explicit clearing is required.
Danger
If you forget to clear a flag, the CPU may become stuck servicing the same interrupt repeatedly.
Tip
Clear flags early in the ISR, then perform minimal state updates, and always keep handlers short.
