Module 07 › Chapter 7.2 › Key Concepts
Interrupts vs Polling
Interrupts are efficient for rare or urgent events; polling is simpler for frequent predictable tasks.
NVIC & Priority
NVIC enables and ranks interrupts; lower numeric value often means higher priority and allows nesting.
ISR behavior
ISRs must clear flags, be short and non-blocking, and update volatile state safely.
Takeaway
Plan priorities, follow the enable/configure/clear sequence, and keep ISRs minimal to build reliable real-time responses.
