Module 07 › Chapter 7.2 › When to Use Interrupts
Practical examples
  • Use interrupts for user buttons, emergency stops, and rare asynchronous events.
  • Use polling for steady, frequent ticks or when overhead of context switching is too high.
  • Prefer interrupts to enable low-power sleep modes and wake-on-event behavior.
Debounce note
Mechanical switches can create multiple transitions; even with interrupts you may need software debounce or a short disable period after the first event.
Decision aid
Ask these questions
  1. How often does the event occur?
  2. How urgent is the response?
  3. Does power consumption benefit from sleeping until the event?
