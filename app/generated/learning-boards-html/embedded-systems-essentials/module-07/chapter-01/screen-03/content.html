Verify Each Objective
Concrete checks for the hands-on activity
UART1 Configuration Check
Confirm PB0 is receiving and PB1 is transmitting. Confirm UART0 remains for PC logs so you can debug while testing the link.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Send/Receive Check
Transmit a known sequence and verify exact bytes are received in order with no dropped bytes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Checksum Verification
Send a sample packet such as 0xAA,0x01,0x01 and verify the checksum equals their sum and the slave processes only valid packets.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Error Detection
Induce a baud mismatch or drop a byte and confirm the receiver flags a frame or overrun error and requests retransmission.
