UART Communication Between Devices
Configure UART1, wire two LaunchPads, and exchange verified packets with checksum-based integrity checks.
In this lesson
UART1 setup, wiring, send/receive code, packet format, and error handling.
Why it matters
Reliable board-to-board links let sensors and controllers coordinate without a PC intermediary.

Error Handling
Detect frame and overrun errors with the error register and request retransmission.
