Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Wiring two LaunchPads for UART1
Wiring Checklist
Follow these steps to build the null modem connection.
1
Identify pins
Use PB0 as RX and PB1 as TX on UART1 for each LaunchPad.
2
Cross TX and RX
Connect PB1 of A → PB0 of B and PB1 of B → PB0 of A. This is the null modem crossover.
3
Connect ground
Tie the grounds together so the receiver interprets voltage levels correctly.
4
Keep UART0 for debug
Do not repurpose UART0; keeping it connected to the PC simplifies debugging.
Quick verification
After wiring, send a single known byte from board A and run a receive routine on board B. If the byte appears, wiring is correct. If not, check ground and crossing before troubleshooting code.
Common mistake
Connecting TX to TX because pins look similar. This yields no received data even though lines appear connected.
