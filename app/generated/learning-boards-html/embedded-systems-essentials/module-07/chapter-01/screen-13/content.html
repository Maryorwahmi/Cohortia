Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Common causes when nothing is received
Three likely causes
Baud mismatch
Frame errors or garbled bytes indicate timing mismatch; confirm both sides share the same baud and framing.
Wiring / Ground
TX/RX wires must be crossed and grounds connected. Check continuity and pin labeling carefully.
Configuration
Ensure your code initializes UART1 and configures PB0/PB1; mistakenly targeting UART0 or wrong pins yields no data.
Isolation order: wiring and ground first, then baud and UART initialization, and finally read error flags to determine whether the issue is timing, buffer overrun, or parity. This ordered approach narrows the search space quickly and prevents wasted debugging effort.
