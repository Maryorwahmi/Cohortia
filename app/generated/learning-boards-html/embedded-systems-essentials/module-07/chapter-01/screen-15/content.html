UART Errors
Frame, overrun, parity — read the error register after reads.
Error Handling
Discard bad packets and request retransmission; avoid uncontrolled retries.
Lab Verification
Confirm LED toggle with the sample packet and review UART0 logs for success and errors.
Final takeaway
Detect and log errors, discard corrupted data, and verify with a small, observable action like toggling an LED to confirm system health.
