Module 7 › Chapter 7.1 › Lab walkthrough — toggle LED over UART1
Step-by-step
  1. Wire PB1(A) → PB0(B), PB1(B) → PB0(A), and connect ground.
  2. Configure UART1 on both boards to the same baud and enable TX/RX.
  3. Implement blocking send and receive routines to transmit bytes reliably.
  4. On receiver, parse start, command, data, compute checksum, and verify.
  5. On checksum match, toggle LED and optionally send an acknowledgment.
Testing checklist
Send the packet 0xAA,0x01,0x01,0xAC and confirm the slave toggles the LED and logs success on UART0. If failure occurs, re-check wiring and baud first.
Key Takeaway
Work incrementally: confirm wiring, then UART config, then basic send/receive, then protocol and checksum.
