Module 7 › Chapter 7.3 › Submission Checklist
Functionality
All modes behave as required and transitions are reliable under button presses.
Evidence
Attach UART logs, screenshots, or a short demo video showing the modes and an intrusion event.
Documentation
Include requirement list, block diagram, state diagram, and a brief note on debugging and known issues.
Final Tip
A concise demo that is fully repeatable and well-documented earns higher marks than an over-ambitious but flaky system.
