Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › GPIO vs Alternate Functions
GPIO (direct control)
Best for simple on/off signals like LEDs and buttons. Keep AFSEL cleared and use DIR, DEN, and DATA registers.
When to use
Direct toggling, debounced button reads, status indicators, or simple logic-level outputs.
Alternate function (AFSEL)
Enable AFSEL when a pin must serve UART, I2C, PWM, or other peripheral roles. Then configure the peripheral module registers.
Important
Reserve pins for peripheral use early to avoid conflict with GPIO code that assumes control of those pins.
Check your understanding

If a pin needs to act as UART transmit, which register do you set? Answer: AFSEL and configure the UART peripheral, not just DEN and DIR for GPIO.
