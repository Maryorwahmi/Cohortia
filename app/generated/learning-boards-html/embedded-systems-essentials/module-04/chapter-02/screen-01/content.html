Interfacing Switches and LEDs
Reliable inputs, safe outputs, and practical wiring on the LaunchPad
Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Orientation: Define pull-ups, debouncing, and LED wiring. Build a breadboard circuit that toggles LEDs from switches without ghost presses.
What you will understand
  • How floating inputs produce random readings and how pull resistors fix that.
  • How to enable internal pull-ups on the TM4C123G and when to place external resistors on a breadboard.
  • Two software debounce patterns and the wiring pattern for LEDs with current-limiting resistors.
