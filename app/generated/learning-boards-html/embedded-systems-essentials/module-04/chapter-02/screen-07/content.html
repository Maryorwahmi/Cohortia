Breadboard Wiring Patterns
Place pull resistors and LED resistors correctly to avoid floating pins and overcurrent.
Pull resistor wiring
  1. For pull-up: resistor between 3.3V rail and the input pin; switch between pin and ground.
  2. For pull-down: resistor between pin and ground; switch between pin and 3.3V.
  3. Use 10 kΩ for most cases; stronger pull means less noise but more idle current.
LED wiring
Connect GPIO → 220 Ω resistor → LED anode (long leg); LED cathode (short leg) → ground. When the pin is driven HIGH the LED lights; when LOW it is off.
Pro tip
Label wires and resistor values on your breadboard so you don't confuse pull resistors with LED resistors during debugging.
Example build
One switch on PF0 with internal pull-up enabled; one switch on PE0 with external 10 kΩ pull-down. LEDs on PB0 and PB1 each with 220 Ω in series. This mirrors the hands-on activity in the chapter.
