Active-Low Switches & RGB Pins
Match software logic to hardware active levels
Switches
SW1 and SW2 connect to PF0 and PF4 and are active-low. The board enables internal pull-ups so the pin reads HIGH when untouched and LOW when pressed.
Quick check: read the pin at rest and while pressing to confirm the expected HIGH then LOW pattern.
RGB LED
The RGB LED maps three channels to pins 1, 2, and 3. Driving each channel individually lets you mix colors; for external LEDs always include a 220 Ω resistor.
Key Takeaway
Confirm active level before writing logic: an active-low switch inverts the natural meaning of a HIGH/LOW read for your code.
