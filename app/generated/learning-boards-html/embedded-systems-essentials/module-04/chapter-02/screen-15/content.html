Key Concepts
Three compact cards to remember
Pull resistors
Prevent floating inputs; choose pull-up or pull-down by wiring convention.
Debouncing
Use counter-based or double-read methods to accept only stable transitions.
Safe outputs
Always use a series resistor for LEDs and avoid driving high currents from a single pin.
Summary takeaway
Defined inputs, debounced signals, and current-limited outputs make embedded systems predictable and safe.
