Switch Bounce Over Time
A simple timeline shows initial contact, bouncing, then steady contact
Phase 1
Initial contact: the switch first touches and may briefly close the circuit.
Phase 2
Bounce: contacts oscillate for a few milliseconds causing multiple HIGH/LOW transitions.
Phase 3
Steady: contacts settle and the pin remains in the pressed state until release.
Practical example
If your code samples faster than the bounce interval, each oscillation may trigger your press detection. Debounce by waiting a short interval for the signal to remain stable.
Key Takeaway
Treat switches as noisy for a few milliseconds after transitions and verify stability before acting.
