UART0 on LaunchPad

UART0 connects to the USB debugger and appears as a virtual COM port on the PC.

Baud divisors

Compute BRD = SystemClock / (16 × Baud). IBRD = integer(BRD), FBRD = round((BRD - IBRD) × 64).

Send & verify

Wait for the transmit-not-full flag, write to the data register, and verify output with a terminal set to the correct framing.

Summary takeaway

Follow the initialization checklist, compute divisors from your system clock, and build small send helpers. Use the terminal to validate each step before adding more complex debug prints.
