Circuit → Expression → Table
Stepwise evaluation is the reliable path to understanding combined gates.

Identify intermediate signals and compute them first. Then apply the final operation.

Example formula: Y = (A AND B) OR (NOT C). Compute A AND B, compute NOT C, then OR the results.

Common mistake: evaluating in the wrong order and missing intermediate inversion effects.

Worked walkthrough
Take inputs A=1, B=0, C=0. A AND B=0. NOT C=1. Final OR yields 1. Record this row in the truth table. Repeat for all rows to validate.
Key Takeaway

Always compute intermediates first and use a truth table to validate the full function.
