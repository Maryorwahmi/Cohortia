Module 3›Chapter 2.2›Breadboard Activity
Build an AND and OR circuit
Practice wiring to match truth tables and observe real hardware behavior.
Step 1: AND wiring
Wire switches in series so current flows only when both are closed. Label switches A and B. Test 00, 01, 10, 11 and record LED states.
Step 2: OR wiring
Rewire switches in parallel so current flows when either is closed. Test and record the truth table.
Verification
Compare recorded tables to the expected tables. If they differ, recheck wiring for active‑low or LED orientation issues.
Key Takeaway

Hands‑on wiring cements the mapping from switches to logical functions and truth tables.
