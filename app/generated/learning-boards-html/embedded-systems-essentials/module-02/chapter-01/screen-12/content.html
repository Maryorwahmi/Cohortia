Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Key Concepts
Ohm's Law
V = I × R links voltage, current, and resistance and lets you compute unknowns.
Resistors for protection
Use series resistors for LEDs and divider pairs for level shifting; pick standard values that keep current safe.
Measure to verify
Assemble on a breadboard, measure voltage drops, and compute current to confirm your design before prolonged operation.
Summary takeaway

Apply the workflow: compute expected values with Ohm's Law, protect components with appropriately chosen resistors, and verify results with a simple breadboard and a multimeter.
