Step-by-step

Assemble and measure the LED circuit

Step 1: Place parts
LED and 220 Ω resistor in series on the breadboard. Ensure leads are in separate connected rows.
Step 2: Wire rails
Tie LaunchPad GND to negative rail and 3.3 V to positive rail if using power pin. Use red/black wire conventions.
Step 3: Power and observe
Briefly apply power to confirm LED lights before measuring voltages.
Measure and compute
Measure voltage across resistor, then compute I = Vr / R. Example: Vr ≈ 1.3 V across 220 Ω gives about 5.9 mA.
If current too high
Power down, increase resistor value, and re-measure. Never rely on guesswork for safe currents.
Try this
After building, connect the LED to a GPIO pin such as PF1 and write a blink program. Measure the resistor voltage while blinking to confirm dynamic behavior.
