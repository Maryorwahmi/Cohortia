Hands-on Objectives

Convert theory into breadboard-tested results.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Compute LED resistor values
Use the forward voltage and desired current to compute R. Example: 3.3 minus 2 volts over 10 milliamps gives about 130 ohms.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Build a voltage divider
Pick resistor pair values to scale 5 volts to 3.3 volts and verify the result with a meter before connecting to a microcontroller input.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Breadboard wiring conventions
Use color conventions and connect ground to the negative rail. Verify pin groups on the board so signals land where you expect them.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Measure and confirm current
Measure voltage across a resistor and compute current by dividing that voltage by the resistor value to confirm safe operation.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
