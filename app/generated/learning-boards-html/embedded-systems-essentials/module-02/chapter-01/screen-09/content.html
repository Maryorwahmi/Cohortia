Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Voltage Divider
Definition

Two resistors scale an input voltage

Formula: Vout = Vin × (R2 / (R1 + R2)). Useful for level shifting signals from 5 V sensors to 3.3 V inputs.
Worked example
Choose R1 = 10 kΩ, R2 = 20 kΩ with Vin = 5 V. Vout = 5 × (20 / 30) = 3.33 V, suitable for a 3.3 V input. Measure the result before connecting the microcontroller.
When to buffer
If the downstream input has low impedance or you need precise timing, add a buffer to avoid loading the divider and altering the voltage.
Do
Use dividers for signal scaling and measure before connecting.
Don't
Don't use a divider to power devices that draw current.
Key Takeaway
Voltage dividers are simple and effective for level shifting, but consider bufferization for loaded circuits.
