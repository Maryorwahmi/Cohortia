Learning Objectives

Build the skills to calculate, protect, and measure circuits safely.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define V, I, and R
Explain voltage as electrical pressure, current as flow, and resistance as opposition. Understanding these definitions stops common confusions between voltage and current when troubleshooting.
Mnemonic: Pressure, Flow, Narrow pipe
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Compute with Ohm's Law
Use V = I × R and its rearrangements to calculate voltage, current, or resistance. This skill lets you choose parts and avoid unsafe currents.
Mnemonic: V equals I times R
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Use resistors correctly
Design current-limiting resistors for LEDs and create voltage dividers to interface different logic levels safely.
Mnemonic: Series limits, pair divides
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Build and measure circuits
Assemble a simple LED-and-resistor circuit on a breadboard, then verify voltages with a multimeter to confirm calculations.
Mnemonic: Wire, Measure, Confirm
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
