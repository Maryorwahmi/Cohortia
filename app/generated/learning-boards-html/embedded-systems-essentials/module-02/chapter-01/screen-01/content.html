Voltage, Current, Resistance

Understand the electrical quantities that control current flow and component safety.

Interactive 16:9 Masterclass
Voltage
Electrical pressure (volts)
Current
Flow of charge (amps)
Resistance
Opposition to flow (ohms)
In this lesson
Calculate with Ohm's Law, choose resistors for LEDs, build on a breadboard, and measure voltages safely.
Why it matters
Avoid damage to microcontroller pins and ensure predictable behavior when you power real hardware.
Explore Key Topics
Ohm's Law
Use V = I × R to compute unknowns and check units to avoid errors.
Resistors & LEDs
Pick a current-limiting resistor so an LED doesn't draw too much from a GPIO pin.
Breadboard Practice
Wiring conventions and measurement techniques let you verify the circuit with a multimeter.
Visual Concept
Water-pipe analogy
Pressure = voltage, flow = current, narrow pipe = resistance.
Quick numbers
AA = 1.5 V, LaunchPad GPIO = 3.3 V, USB = 5 V.
Starter Circuit
Connect a 220 ohm resistor in series with an LED from 3.3V to ground. Use a GPIO pin later to blink it and measure the voltage across the resistor with a multimeter to confirm current.
