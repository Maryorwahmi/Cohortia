Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Quick Reference
Common voltages
AA ≈ 1.5 V, LaunchPad GPIO = 3.3 V, USB = 5 V.
Current guidance
LED ≈ 10 mA; treat GPIO pins as about 8 mA safe for the LaunchPad family unless the datasheet says otherwise.
Resistor rule of thumb
If computed R is borderline, pick the next larger standard resistor value (for safety and simplicity).
Final reminder

When prototyping, compute expected currents, protect components with resistors, and always verify by measuring voltages and recalculating current using I = V / R.
