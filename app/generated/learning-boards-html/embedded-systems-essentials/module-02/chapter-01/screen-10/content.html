Overview

How rows and rails connect

Power rails run horizontally along edges. The central grid has vertical groups of five holes that are electrically common. Component leads and jumper wires share the same row when connected.

Practical wiring rules
  • Connect LaunchPad GND to the negative rail.
  • Connect 3.3 V or 5 V to the positive rail as needed.
  • Use red wires for power, black or blue for ground.
Assembly checklist
Confirm rail polarities, ensure resistor is in series with LED, and look for accidental shorts between rows before applying power.
Example: LED circuit layout
Place the LED so its anode connects to the resistor, the resistor connects to the 3.3 V rail or to a GPIO pin, and the cathode connects to ground rail. Verify continuity visually and with a meter if unsure.
Key Takeaway
Consistent placement and color conventions reduce wiring errors and make debugging faster.
