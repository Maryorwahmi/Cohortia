Process Flow

Solve V, I, or R in four steps

Step 1: Identify knowns
Decide which two of V, I, R you have.
Step 2: Choose formula
Use V = I × R, or rearrange to I = V / R or R = V / I.
Step 3: Convert units
Convert milliamps to amps and kiloohms to ohms before calculating.
Step 4: Verify limits
Compare computed current to pin and component limits; adjust resistors if needed.
Worked calculation
With V = 3.3 V and R = 220 Ω, I = 3.3 / 220 = 0.015 A = 15 mA. If your GPIO safe limit is 8 mA, this resistor is too small for direct connection.
Misconception
Don’t mix units. 0.01 A is 10 mA. Unit errors are common and can lead to selecting resistors that are far too small or too large.
Key Takeaway
Follow a method: pick formula, convert units, compute, and confirm limits to prevent hardware mistakes.
