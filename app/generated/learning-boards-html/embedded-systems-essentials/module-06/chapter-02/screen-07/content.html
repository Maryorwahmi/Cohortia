Module 7 › Chapter 6.2 › Vending Machine FSM
States
  • Idle — waiting for coins
  • AcceptingCoins — accumulate credit
  • SelectionMade — validate and prepare dispense
  • Dispensing — open dispenser until ItemDispensed
  • ReturningChange — issue coins when needed
Inputs & Outputs
Inputs: CoinInserted, ButtonPressed, CancelPressed, ItemDispensed.
Outputs: DisplayMessage, OpenDispenser, ReturnCoins.
