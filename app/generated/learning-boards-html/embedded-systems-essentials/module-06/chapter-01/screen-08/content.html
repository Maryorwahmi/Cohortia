Comparison Moore vs Mealy
Where outputs come from matters
Trade timing and compactness against clarity and testability.
Moore Machine
Outputs depend only on the current state. Observers can read outputs to infer state. Easier to test and reason about.
When to use
When predictable outputs and testability are priorities.
Misconception
Thinking Moore is always slower; it's often clearer which improves reliability.
Mealy Machine
Outputs depend on state and current inputs. Can respond immediately to events and reduce state count, but outputs may be transient and timing-sensitive.
When to use
When you need immediate reactions tied to specific inputs and accept more complex testing.
Pro Tip
Document whether outputs are state-driven or input-driven so testers know what to assert.
Key Takeaway

Choose Moore for clarity and testability; choose Mealy for compact, immediate reactions and when transient outputs are acceptable.
