Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Pitfalls & Tips
Save habit
Save the scene immediately after creating it and before major edits to avoid lost work.
Invisible models
Check Mesh Renderer and material assignment in the Inspector rather than reimporting assets.
Navigation fixes
Double-click a Hierarchy item to focus it in Scene view or use right-click + WASD for fly navigation.
Key Takeaway

Small habits like saving and using Inspector checks prevent most early problems and keep your project stable as it grows.
