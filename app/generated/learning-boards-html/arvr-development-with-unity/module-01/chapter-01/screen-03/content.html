Bloom's Taxonomy Mini-Bar
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Learn

Memorize basic definitions: AR overlays, VR immerses, MR integrates. This is the foundation for later design choices.

Understand

Explain core components such as stereoscopic displays, head tracking, and spatial mapping, and why they matter for presence and interaction fidelity.

Apply

Choose hardware and design simple interaction flows for a use case, accounting for safety and performance limits.

Master

Justify trade-offs across devices and designs, and map an industry problem to AR, VR, or MR with clear reasoning.
