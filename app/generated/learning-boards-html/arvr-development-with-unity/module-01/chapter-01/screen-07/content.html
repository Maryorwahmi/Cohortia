Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Immersion vs Presence
Immersion
System qualities that enable convincing worlds
  • Stereoscopic displays and wide field of view.
  • High and stable frame rates.
  • Accurate head and controller tracking.
Misconception
High-resolution graphics alone guarantee presence. They do not if tracking or latency are poor.
Presence
The user's feeling of being there

Presence arises when sensory cues, interactivity, and system performance align. It is measured by users' subjective sense of engagement and realism.

Key Takeaway

Presence requires both capable hardware and careful UX to avoid discomfort and preserve realism.
