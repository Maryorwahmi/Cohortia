Activity: Explore AR/VR Use Cases

Choose an industry and research two distinct examples. For each example document: type (AR, VR, MR), problem solved, typical hardware, and how the technology functions in context.

Example template
Industry: Healthcare Example 1: VR for Surgical Training  • Type: VR  • Problem/Value: Risk-free practice for trainees  • Hardware: PC-tethered headsets with controllers  • How it works: Users enter a simulated operating room and interact with virtual instruments while receiving feedback
Guidance
  • Identify why AR/VR is the right tool for that problem.
  • Note hardware constraints like battery, compute, and tracking needs.
  • Write one paragraph justifying your hardware choice per example.
Key Takeaway
Key Takeaway

This activity trains your ability to translate a real problem into an appropriate AR, VR, or MR solution with clear hardware reasoning.
