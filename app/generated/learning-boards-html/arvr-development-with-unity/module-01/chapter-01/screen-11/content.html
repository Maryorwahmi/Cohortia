Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › AR & VR Use Cases
Industry snapshots
Healthcare
VR for surgical training; AR overlays for in-surgery patient data.
Education
VR field trips and AR-enhanced textbooks for interactive learning.
Retail
AR try-before-you-buy applications that place 3D products in customers' homes.
Manufacturing
AR maintenance guides, remote assistance; VR for design reviews and prototyping.
Concrete example
AR for remote field service

A field technician uses an AR tablet to view annotations from a remote expert who highlights components and overlays step-by-step instructions, reducing downtime and travel costs.

Key Takeaway
Key Takeaway

Use cases determine the technology: training and simulation lean to VR; live context and augmentation lean to AR.
