Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What is AR?
Definition
Augmented Reality

An interactive experience where digital objects are overlaid onto your view of the world, often using device cameras or transparent optics, preserving awareness of surroundings.

Forms
  • 2D overlays and filters.
  • 3D models anchored to surfaces.
  • Wearable visual overlays via glasses.
Design note
Maintain scale, occlusion, and lighting to make augmentations believable; otherwise users may distrust the experience.
Concrete example
Try-before-you-buy retail AR

A shopper points a phone at their living room and sees a 3D sofa placed to scale. This reduces returns and increases buyer confidence by showing products in context.

Key Takeaway
Key Takeaway

AR augments awareness. Choose AR when the physical context matters and when overlays improve a real-world task.
