Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What is Mixed Reality (MR)?
Definition
Mixed Reality

MR blends physical and virtual elements so they interact in real time, respecting occlusion, scale, and lighting. It typically relies on spatial mapping to integrate content convincingly into the environment.

Spatial mapping
Scanning the environment to locate surfaces and obstacles for correct placement and occlusion.
Occlusion & shadows
Digital objects hide behind real objects and cast realistic shadows to increase believability.
Interaction fidelity
MR supports more natural interactions because digital objects behave like physical ones in the same space.
Concrete example
Architectural review with occlusion

An architect places a 3D model in a real room; team members can walk around and see virtual features correctly occlude and cast shadows, enabling better spatial judgment.
