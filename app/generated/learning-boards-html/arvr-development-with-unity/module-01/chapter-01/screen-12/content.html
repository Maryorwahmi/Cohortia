Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Designing for Comfort and Safety
Best Practices
Clear play space
Use virtual boundaries and instruct users to remove hazards before starting VR sessions.
Performance stability
Prioritize consistent frame rates and avoid sudden camera movements to reduce motion sickness.
Adjustable comfort options
Provide locomotion alternatives and adjustable settings for users sensitive to motion.
Concrete checklist
  • Define minimum safe play area before session start.
  • Include a guardian or boundary system for VR.
  • Test at target frame rate with representative content.
Common mistake
Neglecting safety testing until late—this increases risk and can force costly redesigns.
