Platforms
Smartphones & Tablets

Most AR experiences run on phone cameras and are widely accessible. They are suitable for visual overlays and spatial placement but face battery and tracking variability.

Design note
Optimize for varying lighting, camera quality, and performance across devices when targeting phones and tablets.
Platforms
AR Glasses

Glasses enable hands-free workflows and persistent overlays, useful for remote assistance and industrial maintenance, though they require different interaction models and testing approaches.

Concrete example
AR remote assistance with glasses—an expert annotates the technician's real-time view to guide repairs.
