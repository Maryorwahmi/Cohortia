Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Virtual Reality (VR)
A simulated experience that replaces the user's perception of the real world.
Augmented Reality (AR)
Digital information overlaid on the real world, preserving awareness of surroundings.
Mixed Reality (MR)
Digital and physical objects coexist and interact in real time, with occlusion and spatial awareness.
Summary
Use these concise definitions when planning projects. If the user must remain aware of the environment, favor AR; if training or full immersion is needed, choose VR; if deep interaction with space is required, plan for MR-level mapping and sensors.
