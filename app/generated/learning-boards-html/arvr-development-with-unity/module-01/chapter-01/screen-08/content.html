Comparison
Standalone vs PC-tethered
Standalone
Self-contained, portable, easier to deploy but lower max fidelity and compute for complex simulations.
PC-tethered
Requires a connected computer, supports higher fidelity rendering, physics, and precision—better for high-stakes training.
Design question
Does your experience require top visual fidelity and physics accuracy, or portability and ease of use in the real world?
Concrete example
Surgical training uses PC-tethered systems

High-resolution visuals and realistic instrument behavior often need the extra compute of a PC to deliver valid training outcomes.

Key Takeaway
Key Takeaway

Choose hardware based on fidelity needs and deployment constraints, not just availability.
