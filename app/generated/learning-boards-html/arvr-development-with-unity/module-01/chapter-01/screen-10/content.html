Components
Displays
Stereoscopic headsets for VR; camera feeds or transparent optics for AR.
Tracking
Head and controller tracking to update viewpoint and interaction in real time.
Spatial mapping
Environment scanning to place and occlude digital objects accurately for AR and MR.
Input
Controllers, hand tracking, and gaze-based input provide interactivity with virtual elements.
Concrete scenario
Tablet-based AR for field service

A tablet camera captures the machine, spatial mapping anchors step-by-step instructions to components, and the technician taps onscreen markers to advance the procedure while a remote expert annotates the live view.

Key Takeaway
Key Takeaway

Specify component fidelity early and test end-to-end with target hardware to avoid late surprises.
