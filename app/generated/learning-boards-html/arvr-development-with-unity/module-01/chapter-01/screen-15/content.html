Immersion & Presence
Immersion is technical; presence is the user's psychological reaction.
System Components
Displays, tracking, spatial mapping, and input must be integrated and tested together.
Hardware trade-offs
Balance portability and fidelity; choose devices that match your interaction and performance needs.
Final takeaway
Design with the problem first, then the technology. Test early on target hardware, and always include safety and comfort checks as core requirements.
