Learning Objectives
Skills to practice and verify in this chapter
01
Differentiate AR, VR, and MR

Explain how AR overlays digital content on reality, VR fully replaces the user's world, and MR integrates digital objects to interact with the physical environment. This skill prevents term confusion and guides correct project scope.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify core components

Name and describe displays, head tracking, spatial mapping and input methods that enable presence and immersion. This helps you plan technical requirements ahead of design decisions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Recognize hardware platforms

Map typical devices—smartphones, standalone headsets, PC-tethered headsets, AR glasses—to their capabilities and limitations so you can choose the right target platform for a project.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Discuss applications and safety

Describe cross-industry examples and list safety considerations like play-space boundaries and user comfort. This helps you design responsibly from the start.
