AR Session vs AR Session Origin
Understand lifecycle and world mapping so placed objects remain aligned with the physical environment.
AR Session
Manages starting and stopping tracking and connects to the device AR runtime. Think of it as the session controller that enables AR features.
AR Session Origin
Transforms AR coordinate space into Unity world space and usually contains the AR Camera, AR Plane Manager, and AR Raycast Manager.
Concrete example
Add AR Session and AR Session Origin, then add AR Plane Manager and AR Raycast Manager to the origin. Instantiate a prefab at hit poses returned by raycast to place a cube on a detected plane.

Common mistake
Putting plane and raycast managers on a separate root not aligned with the origin can lead to mismatched coordinates and objects appearing offset from detected surfaces.
Key Takeaway

Use AR Session to manage runtime and AR Session Origin to host camera and spatial managers so world mapping is consistent.
