Common mistakes
A short checklist to find configuration errors quickly.
Quick checklist
  • Did you switch platform to Android or Standalone before toggling providers?
  • Is the provider enabled under the correct platform tab in XR Plugin Management?
  • Are Player Settings correct: Package Name, Minimum API Level, IL2CPP, ARM64?
  • Is Android Build Support installed in the Editor for APK builds?
  • Is "Initialize XR on Startup" checked for the target platform?
Runtime log hints
Use the Editor log or Android logcat to find missing native binding errors or exceptions that point to disabled providers or initialization failures.
Concrete example
If Quest shows a blank scene after build, verify the Android tab has Oculus or OpenXR checked and that Initialize XR on Startup is enabled. Also confirm package name and ARM64 are set.
Safety reminder
In AR be aware of your surroundings; in VR clear a play area and use comfort locomotion to reduce motion sickness during testing.
Pro tip

When in doubt, rebuild after toggling provider settings and restart the Editor to ensure native libraries load correctly.
