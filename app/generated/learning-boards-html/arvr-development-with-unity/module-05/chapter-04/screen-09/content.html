Definition
Shader Optimization and URP
Prefer optimized shaders and a lighter pipeline to reduce GPU time in VR.
When to simplify
Distant objects, peripheral elements, and repeated instances are good candidates for simplified shaders or unlit variants.
Use URP
URP supplies mobile-friendly shaders and streamlined rendering paths that are suited for VR performance targets.
Concrete example
Replace a complex water shader on a distant pond with a simpler animated texture or unlit variant to cut pixel cost substantially.
Checks and pitfalls
  • Profile pixel and shader cost in the Profiler to target expensive materials.
  • Avoid many overlapping transparent objects; they increase overdraw.
  • Consider baked lighting where dynamic lights are not necessary.
Common misconception
Assuming advanced real-time effects are acceptable in VR without profiling. Always measure pixel cost and overdraw first.
Key Takeaway

Favor URP-friendly and unlit shaders for non-critical surfaces to reduce GPU workload and keep frame rate stable.
