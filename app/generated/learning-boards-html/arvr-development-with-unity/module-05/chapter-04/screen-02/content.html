Learning Objectives
Understand, apply, and measure optimizations for VR comfort.
01
Measure FPS and Latency
You will learn to interpret frame rate and latency, why a stable 72–120 FPS matters, and how to spot stalls in the Profiler.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Apply Occlusion & LOD
Mark static objects, bake occlusion culling, and create LOD Groups to reduce draw and vertex cost in scenes with many meshes.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Optimize Assets
Choose texture sizes and compression, limit shader complexity, and prefer simple colliders to lower GPU and CPU load.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Design for Comfort
Provide locomotion options and clear feedback, offer teleportation and snap turning, and use vignette for smooth-motion comfort.
Verified Competency Milestone
