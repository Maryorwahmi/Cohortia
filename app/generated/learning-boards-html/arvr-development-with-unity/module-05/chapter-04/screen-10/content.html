Physics Optimization and Colliders
Reduce physics cost by simplifying colliders and limiting dynamic objects.
Step 1 — Audit dynamic objects
Locate all dynamic rigidbodies and assess whether they must be simulated every frame.
Step 2 — Replace mesh colliders
Use Box, Sphere, Capsule, or compound colliders instead of expensive Mesh Colliders where possible.
Step 3 — Reduce active simulation
Put objects to sleep when idle and limit high-frequency physics queries.
Concrete example
Replace a Mesh Collider on a chair with a compound of Box Colliders approximating legs and seat. The user collision feels identical, but CPU physics cost drops.
Validation
Check Fixed Timestep and Physics sections in the Profiler. If physics time falls, you know the change helped.
Common misconception
Assuming visual fidelity of collision must match render mesh. Approximate colliders often suffice for user interaction and save CPU cost.
Key Takeaway

Simpler colliders and fewer dynamic rigidbodies reduce CPU physics time and lower the risk of frame spikes.
