Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Frame Rate (FPS)
Target stable frame rates like 72, 90, or 120 for comfort and immersion.
Latency
Low input-to-visual delay is essential to avoid sensory conflict and discomfort.
Occlusion Culling
Bake occlusion to skip rendering objects hidden from the camera's view.
LOD (Level of Detail)
Swap simpler meshes at distance to reduce vertex and fragment cost.
Batching
Static and dynamic batching reduce draw calls when materials are shared.
Profiler & Frame Debugger
Measure CPU, GPU, and memory to target the biggest bottlenecks first.
Summary
Combine measurement, scene optimizations, asset simplification, and comfort-focused UX to build performant and comfortable VR experiences.
