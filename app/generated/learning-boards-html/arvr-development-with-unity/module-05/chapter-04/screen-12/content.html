UX Design
Locomotion & Comfort
Options and design patterns that reduce motion sickness and improve accessibility.
Teleportation
Instant movement that avoids continuous optic flow and greatly reduces motion sickness for many users.
Smooth locomotion + Vignette
Higher immersion but use vignette to reduce peripheral motion during movement and offer speed limits.
Snap Turning
Discrete rotation steps reduce disorientation compared to smooth yaw rotation.
Concrete scenario
Provide a comfort menu: Teleportation toggle, Smooth locomotion with vignette slider, Snap turn angle selection. Let users choose and persist preferences.
Validation
Collect subjective comfort feedback and prefer the mode that yields the fewest discomfort reports while preserving performance targets.
Common mistake
Forcing one locomotion style on all users. Preferences vary; offering options increases accessibility and retention.
Key Takeaway

Offer locomotion choices and comfort aids, and test with real users to find settings that balance immersion and well-being.
