Process Flow
Occlusion Culling: Bake and Validate
A step-by-step workflow to prevent hidden geometry from being rendered.
Step 1 — Prepare static objects
Select large, non-moving geometry and mark them Static in the Inspector so they can be occluders.
Step 2 — Open Occlusion Culling window
Use Window → Rendering → Occlusion Culling, then go to the Bake tab to prepare the baking process.
Step 3 — Bake and test
Click Bake. Enter Play Mode, move the XR Origin, and confirm occluded objects are no longer rendered.
Pro tip
Use Scene view's Occlusion Culling visualization to inspect which areas are blocked and tune parameters if needed.
Example: Furnished room
Mark walls and furniture as static. Bake occlusion so props in adjacent rooms are not drawn when unseen. Observe draw call reductions in the Profiler when moving between rooms.
Validation
Use the Frame Debugger to inspect draw calls and confirm occluded meshes are skipped during rendering.
Common mistake
Do not mark moving objects as static. They won't update correctly and occlusion results will be incorrect.
Check your understanding
After baking occlusion, which areas of your scene show the largest reduction in rendered objects? If none, revisit static flags and occlusion parameters.
