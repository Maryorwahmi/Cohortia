Setting Up Your VR Development Environment

Prepare Unity so your headset, controllers, and interaction toolkit work together. This lesson gives a practical checklist and hands-on steps to get a simple scene running.

What you will understand

Which Unity packages and prefabs to install, how to assign input actions, and how to validate tracking and grabbing with a basic cube.

Why it matters

A stable setup reduces time wasted on configuration issues and prevents false assumptions about controller or headset failures during development.
