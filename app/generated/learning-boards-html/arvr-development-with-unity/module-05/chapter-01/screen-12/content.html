Why scale matters

Realistic scale helps users judge distances and reduces motion discomfort. Incorrect scale alters perceived speed and reach for interactions.

Practical example

Add a Plane at 0,0,0 scaled to 10,1,10 to serve as a ground reference during early tests.

Test loop
  1. Add XR Origin and ground plane.
  2. Enter Play and check perceived height and horizon in headset.
  3. Adjust model scales if the world feels oversized or miniature.
Quick Tip

Rescale models instead of shifting the XR Origin to preserve consistent tracking behavior.
