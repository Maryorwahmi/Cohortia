Custom Logic & Feedback
Combine toolkit components with scripts to refine behavior and visuals.
Example 1 — Dual activation
A cube responds to gaze by showing info and responds to grab to move. Use OnGazeActivate and XR grab callbacks together, ensuring they don’t conflict by gating one while the other is active.
Example 2 — Reticle context
Change reticle color or shape depending on whether the ray points at a Teleportation Area or an interactable, giving immediate context to the user’s action.
Design rule
Add short grace periods after teleportation and use progress indicators so activations are intentional and predictable.
Concrete pattern
Create a small manager script that listens to interactor hover and select events. On hover, call a highlight method on the target. On select, perform the action and optionally disable gaze activation for a short period to avoid accidental re-triggering immediately after teleport or a grab release.
Key Takeaway
Small scripts that coordinate feedback and gating are what make interactions feel polished; the toolkit provides hooks to do this cleanly.
