Practical Reminders
Keep these quick checks in your toolkit as you build and test.
Visual Feedback
Reticles, highlights, and progress indicators make interactions discoverable and reduce frustration.
Test with Headset
Editor behavior may differ from headset experience; test early to catch comfort and visibility issues.
Colliders & Layers
Most interaction failures trace back to missing colliders or incorrect Interaction Layer Mask settings.
Final Tip
Start with the toolkit defaults, validate visibility and collisions, then add custom scripts for feedback and gating to polish the UX.
