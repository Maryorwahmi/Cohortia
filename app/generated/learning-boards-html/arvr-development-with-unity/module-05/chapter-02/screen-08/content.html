Teleportation Components
How Provider, Area, and Anchor coordinate to move the XR Origin.
Teleportation Provider
Placed on XR Origin; executes the teleport and manages orientation when landing.
Teleportation Area
Attach to ground planes with an enabled Collider to allow area teleportation.
Teleportation Anchor
Place on specific points for fixed destinations; can define orientation on arrival.
Example Setup
Add Teleportation Provider to XR Origin, add Teleportation Area to the Ground plane with a Box Collider, and place Teleportation Anchors at fixed scenic viewpoints. Ensure the controllers' XR Ray Interactors include the ground's layer in their Interaction Layer Mask so the reticle appears when pointing at valid spots.
Troubleshooting
If teleport reticle does not appear while rays hit other objects, check the Interaction Layer Mask and confirm the Teleportation Area's Collider is enabled and not obscured by other geometry.
Key Takeaway

Teleportation requires both destination definition on geometry and an active Teleportation Provider on XR Origin to move the user.
