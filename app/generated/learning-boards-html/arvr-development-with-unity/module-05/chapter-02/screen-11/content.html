Troubleshooting
No Hits or No Reticle
A quick checklist to find the root cause and fix it fast.
Missing Collider
Ensure Collider exists and is enabled on the interactable object or its child.
Interaction Layer Mask
Confirm the interactor mask includes the target object's layer; mismatches are common.
Teleport Provider Missing
If teleport activation does not move the user, check for a Teleportation Provider on the XR Origin.
Visual Assets
Validate Line Renderer material and reticle prefab so users see the pointer and target markers.
Diagnostic Steps
  1. Enable Gizmos and inspect Colliders in Scene view.
  2. While in Play, toggle Interaction Layer Mask to find a working combination.
  3. Confirm Teleportation Provider exists and is enabled on XR Origin.
  4. Check for other geometry occluding the teleport area or blocking the ray.
Quick Fix
Adding or adjusting a collider and including the object's layer in the interactor mask resolves most “no hit” reports.
