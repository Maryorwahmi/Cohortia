Assessment Idea
Diagnostics and Fixes
Question 1
Ray visible but cannot grab objects with XR Grab Interactable. What to check?
  1. Is there an enabled Collider on the object?
  2. Is the object’s layer included in the Interaction Layer Mask of the ray interactor?
  3. Is the XR Grab Interactable attached to the active GameObject or a child with the collider?
Question 2
Teleport reticle not appearing while grabbing still works. Likely causes and fixes.
  1. Verify Teleportation Area exists on the ground and its Collider is enabled.
  2. Confirm the Teleportation Provider is present and active on XR Origin.
  3. Ensure the interactor mask includes the teleport layer.
Instructor tip
When you fix a reported issue, document the root cause and the exact inspector change you made. This habit speeds future debugging and teaches you the mappings between components and behavior.
