Key Concepts
Compact reminders of the chapter’s essential points.
Gaze Interaction
Look-to-select with a dwell timer and visible progress prevents accidental activations.
Raycasting
XR Ray Interactor projects a pointer from a controller; add Line Renderer and reticle for clarity.
Teleportation
Teleportation Provider moves XR Origin; Areas and Anchors mark valid destinations with colliders.
Summary
Remember the role mapping: Interactable = behavior, Collider = detection, Layer Mask = filtering. Verify these three to make interactions work reliably.
