Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Input Comparison
Controller-Based Input
  • Provides buttons, triggers, sticks and haptics for discrete and precise input.
  • Tracked in 3D for pointing and accurate position-based grabs.
  • Ideal for fast-paced or physics-dependent interactions.
Common misconception
Controllers feel less natural, but they often provide the precision and feedback needed for reliable gameplay.
Direct Hand Tracking
  • Uses headset cameras to detect hand joints and finger poses.
  • Enables natural gestures like pinches and open-hand grabs without controllers.
  • Less precise in occlusion or fast motion; needs strong visual feedback.
Design tip
Add highlights and pose changes to indicate when a grab or pinch is recognized since there is no haptic feedback.
Practical example

Offer both methods: allow controller grip to pick up a tool, but support a pinch gesture for toggling UI or inspection. Fall back to controllers when hand joints are not reliably tracked.
