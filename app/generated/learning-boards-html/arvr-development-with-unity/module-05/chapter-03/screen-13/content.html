Quick steps
  1. Add Collider and Rigidbody to Interactable Cube.
  2. Add XR Grab Interactable to the cube.
  3. Give controllers XR Direct Interactor and XR Ray Interactor components.
  4. Create Input Action Asset with Grab bindings and assign it to Input Action Manager.
  5. Link Grab Action in XR Controller inspector and test grabbing in Play mode.
Prepare hand tracking

Enable Oculus under XR Plug-in Management → Android. In Meta XR SDK settings set Tracking Origin Mode to Floor and enable Hand Tracking Support as Supported or Required. For visualization, add the OVRHandPrefab and attach interactors to child fingertips or palm.
