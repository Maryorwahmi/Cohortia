Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
XR Grab Interactable

Makes objects grabbable; requires Collider and Rigidbody and supports attachment modes.

Interactors on Hand Prefabs

Add XR Direct Interactor or XR Ray Interactor to fingertip/palm children so visual hands can actually select objects.

Gesture Recognition

Measure joint positions and apply thresholds, smoothing, and hysteresis to detect pinches and grabs reliably.

Summary

Follow the checks above during development: assign assets, add required components, and provide feedback to users so interactions feel reliable and natural.
