Process Flow

SLAM: Map while locating

A step-by-step view of how SLAM enables markerless AR tracking on mobile devices.

1. Feature detection
Find trackable points like corners and texture patches in camera frames.
2. Motion estimation
Match features across frames to infer device movement and update pose estimates.
3. Mapping
Accumulate those tracked features into a spatial map used for anchoring.
4. Loop closure & correction
Recognize previously seen areas to correct drift and improve stability.
Illustrative callout
Concrete example

Walk around a room while holding your phone. SLAM tracks the phone's position by tracking feature points on walls, tables, and objects, and uses that motion to place and hold a virtual lamp on a table as you change view angle.

Common misconception

SLAM does not always produce dense 3D reconstructions; sparse point maps are often enough for stable tracking and plane detection.

Key Takeaway

SLAM’s strength is combining mapping and localization so virtual content remains anchored as the device moves.
