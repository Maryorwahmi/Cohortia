Core Concept

AR vs VR

A practical comparison that highlights why AR needs environmental sensing while VR focuses on immersion.

Definition
AR overlays digital content on real-world views; VR creates a fully simulated environment.
Example
Place a virtual couch on a real floor (AR) versus rearrange furniture inside a virtual room (VR).
Misconception
AR is not just VR with a camera; AR must sense surfaces, light, and device pose continuously.
Key Takeaway

AR demands environmental awareness; design for sensing, permissions, and stability on device.

Comparison
Augmented Reality
  • Overlays on live camera
  • Needs SLAM, plane detection, light estimation
  • Runs widely on phones
Virtual Reality
  • Fully simulated view
  • Focus on graphics and input within scene
  • Often uses headsets and dedicated sensors
Reflection
Which constraints—sensing or rendering—are most important for your project idea?
