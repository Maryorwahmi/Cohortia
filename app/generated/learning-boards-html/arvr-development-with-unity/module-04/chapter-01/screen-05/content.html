Marker-based vs Markerless AR

Understand when to use fixed markers and when to rely on environment mapping and SLAM.

Marker-based AR
Relies on detecting a known image or object. Offers precise alignment and repeatable placement.
Markerless AR
Uses SLAM and plane detection to place content without special markers, enabling flexible placement in varied spaces.
Common mistake
Assuming markerless always wins; sometimes repeatable markers are the right tool for production workflows.
Concrete example
Use cases
  • Marker-based: Product packaging overlays and museum labels with exact alignment.
  • Markerless: Furniture placement apps and location-based AR experiences without special markers.
Pro tip

Prototype both approaches early: markers simplify tracking while markerless reveals real-world edge cases.
