Key Concepts — Part 1

Three compact concept cards to reinforce core definitions and choices.

Augmented Reality (AR)
Overlays digital content on the real world to enhance perception without replacing it.
SLAM
Simultaneous Localization and Mapping builds a map while tracking device pose for stable placement.
Plane Detection
Identifies flat surfaces so virtual objects can rest realistically on floors and tables.
Summary

These fundamentals determine how you will anchor, render, and test AR experiences. Use the right anchoring strategy and validate tracking on device.
