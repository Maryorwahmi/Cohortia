Key Concepts — Part 2

Three compact cards that summarize the toolchain and essential settings.

ARCore & ARKit
Native platforms that implement SLAM, plane finding, feature tracking, and light probes for each OS.
AR Foundation & XR Plugins
Unity's AR Foundation abstracts ARCore and ARKit; XR Plugin Management chooses the provider at runtime.
Player Settings & Scene Components
Set Android API ≥24, IL2CPP, ARM64, add iOS camera usage text, and include AR Session and AR Session Origin in scenes.
Final takeaway

Combining the right tooling, settings, and scene components, plus frequent device testing, is the minimum recipe for reliable mobile AR experiences in Unity.
