Architecture

AR Session & AR Session Origin

These GameObjects manage the AR lifecycle and the mapping between real and virtual space.

AR Session
Starts and stops the AR system and coordinates platform-specific resources.
AR Session Origin
Controls scale and transforms so tracked anchors align with Unity world coordinates; usually parent of AR Camera.
Practical check
If anchors drift or the camera behaves oddly, verify these components are added and configured correctly.
Step-by-step
  1. Create a new scene and remove the default Main Camera if the AR Camera will replace it.
  2. Right-click Hierarchy → XR → AR Session to add the session GameObject.
  3. Right-click Hierarchy → XR → AR Session Origin to add the origin and AR Camera.
  4. Confirm the AR Camera is child of AR Session Origin and that tracking components are present.
Key Takeaway

AR Session and AR Session Origin are required scene foundations; add them before placing anchored content.
