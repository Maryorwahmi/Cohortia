Permissions & Safety
Explain camera access, encourage situational awareness, and avoid interactions that distract users from obstacles.
Prompt with rationale before requesting camera permission.
Provide visual reminders to watch surroundings during active AR sessions.
Avoid UX that forces continuous downward focus in hazardous environments.
Testing checklist
  • Run in bright sunlight, dim rooms, and high-reflectivity areas.
  • Log Pose and tracking state in debug builds for postmortem analysis.
  • Perform user trials to validate gesture discoverability and placement expectations.
Key Takeaway

Real-world testing across conditions is essential; permissions and safety prompts protect users and reduce support issues.
