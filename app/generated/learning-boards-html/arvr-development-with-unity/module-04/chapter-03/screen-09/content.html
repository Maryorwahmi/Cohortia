Process
Pinch & Twist Gestures
Detect two touches, store initial distance and angle, then apply relative transforms during move phases.
Step 1: On two-touch begin store initialDistance and initialScale.
Step 2: On moved compute currentDistance and scaleFactor = current / initial.
Step 3: Apply rotation delta from the change in angle between touch vectors.
Step 4: Clamp localScale to a safe min/max range to protect UX.
High-level pseudocode
// On two-finger begin
initialDistance = Distance(touch0.pos, touch1.pos)
initialScale = placedObject.transform.localScale
initialAngle = AngleBetween(touch0, touch1)

// On moved
currentDistance = Distance(touch0.pos, touch1.pos)
scaleFactor = currentDistance / initialDistance
placedObject.transform.localScale = initialScale * scaleFactor

currentAngle = AngleBetween(touch0, touch1)
angleDelta = currentAngle - initialAngle
placedObject.transform.Rotate(0, -angleDelta, 0)
UX note: tune sensitivity so small finger jitter doesn't trigger large changes; use smoothing or dead zones as needed.
