Object Placement and Manipulation in AR
From raycasts to gestures: make virtual objects land, move, and behave reliably in the real world.

This lesson explains how to convert a touch point into a 3D Pose with ARRaycastManager, spawn prefabs at that Pose, and add touch-driven translation, scaling, and rotation so objects behave predictably. You will also learn common failure modes like drift and how to design cues that help users judge scale.

In this lesson: raycasting, spawning, manipulation, drift diagnosis, scale cues.
