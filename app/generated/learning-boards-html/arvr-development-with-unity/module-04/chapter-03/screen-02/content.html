Learning Objectives
Skills to show you can place, spawn, and manipulate objects with confidence.
01
Implement Raycasting
Use ARRaycastManager to convert touches into Pose hits on detected planes. Verified competency milestone available.
02
Dynamically Spawn Prefabs
Instantiate prefabs at hit Pose positions and manage references for later manipulation.
03
Touch Manipulation
Implement one-finger drag, two-finger pinch, and twist gestures to translate, scale, and rotate objects.
04
Diagnose Drift & Perception
Recognize causes of object drift and add simple visual cues to improve scale perception and testing reliability.
