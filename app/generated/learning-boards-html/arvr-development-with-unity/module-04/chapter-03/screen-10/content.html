Drift Causes vs Symptoms
Cause: Poor visual features (plain floors, low texture)
Cause: Rapid, jerky device motion that breaks SLAM tracking
Symptom: Object slowly slides despite the device being held still
Symptom: Sudden jumps when relocalization occurs
Diagnosis & Mitigation
  • Reproduce drift by walking around the object and varying lighting.
  • Log positions over time to quantify movement and find correlations.
  • Mitigate with anchors, UX resets, or by instructing users to move slowly.
  • Design tests in different environments to understand limits.
Common mistake

Assuming drift is always code-related; often it is environmental or tracking-limit related. Test before refactoring placement logic.
