Implementation Milestones
How to confirm each objective on a real device.
01
Confirm Raycast Hits
Tap visible planes, log the returned Pose, and ensure position and rotation match the surface normal.
02
Verify Instantiation
Instantiate a prefab at hit Pose and check that it rests without sinking or floating.
03
Test Touch Controls
Place an object and move, pinch, and twist to confirm responsive manipulation without jumps.
04
Reproduce Drift
Walk around the scene and vary lighting to observe drift; record conditions that increase drift.
