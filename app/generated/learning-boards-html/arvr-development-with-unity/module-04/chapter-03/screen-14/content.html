Key Concepts
Three compact concept cards to keep at hand.
Raycasting
Project a screen point into AR to get a Pose for placement.
Dynamic Spawning
Instantiate prefabs at runtime using hit Pose position and rotation.
Object Manipulation
Translation, scaling, rotation controlled by touch gestures.
Summary

Keep the flow Raycast → Pose → Instantiate → Manipulate as your mental model when implementing placement features.
