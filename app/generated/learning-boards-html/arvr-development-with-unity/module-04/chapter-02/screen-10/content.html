Comparison

Trackable types for raycasts

PlaneWithinPolygon
Hits only inside detected plane boundaries. Best for precise anchored placement.
PlaneEstimated
Returns an estimated plane even if full polygon isn't available. Useful when immediate placement is required but less precise.
FeaturePoints
Hits on detected feature points allow placement in textured but non‑planar regions; stability varies.
Decision guide
  • Use PlaneWithinPolygon for furniture, product previews, or anything needing a firm anchor.
  • Use estimated or feature hits only when immediate availability is more important than long-term stability.
  • Test placements in low‑texture and reflective environments to validate chosen trackable types.
Example
A floor‑placing app uses PlaneWithinPolygon for stable furniture placement. An art app that spawns ephemeral particles may use feature points for playful placement even if they jitter slightly later.
Key Takeaway

Match raycast types to your UX: prefer stability for anchored content and looser types when availability or immediacy is more important.
