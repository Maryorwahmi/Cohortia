Troubleshooting

Why planes aren't visible

  • Plane Prefab slot is empty or prefab lacks AR Plane component.
  • Material is opaque and blocking the camera feed.
  • Poor lighting or textureless surfaces prevent detection.
Fix
Assign a translucent prefab with AR Plane component, test in textured, well-lit areas, and confirm AR managers are on the AR Session Origin.
Why objects drift
Drift often signals tracking issues or a misconfigured origin. Confirm the AR Camera is a child of AR Session Origin and that the origin hasn't been transformed after tracking starts. Also test on-device movement patterns to ensure consistent tracking quality.
Check list
Plane Prefab, AR managers placement, lighting, and device motion when testing.
Testing tip
Walk slowly and vary viewpoints to help the system build a more complete map during testing.
Key Takeaway

Start with prefab and hierarchy checks, then validate environment and device motion before debugging placement code.
