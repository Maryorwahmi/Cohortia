Architecture

AR Session & AR Session Origin

These components work together so tracking data aligns correctly with Unity objects and camera rendering.
AR Session
Manages the AR lifecycle and interfaces with the underlying platform for tracking state.
AR Session Origin
Translates platform coordinates into Unity's world space; AR components should live on it or its children.
AR Camera
Usually a child of AR Session Origin and renders the camera feed with overlays for virtual objects.
Practical checks
  • Ensure AR Plane Manager and ARRaycastManager are on AR Session Origin or its child to keep trackable coordinates consistent.
  • Avoid transforming AR Session Origin manually after tracking starts; unexpected translations or rotations lead to misalignment.
  • If objects drift, check tracking quality and whether AR Session has been paused or restarted during runtime.
Scenario
If a placed object appears offset when you walk around, examine whether the AR Session Origin was moved or scaled at author time, or whether the AR Camera is not correctly parented.
Key Takeaway

Keep AR managers on AR Session Origin. Treat the origin as your reference frame to avoid placement and tracking errors.
