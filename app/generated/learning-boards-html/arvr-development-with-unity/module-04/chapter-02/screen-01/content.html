Plane Detection and Environment Understanding

Understand how AR Foundation finds flat real‑world surfaces, how Unity represents them, and how raycasts let users place virtual content reliably on those surfaces.

Visual Overview
At a glance
Detect
AR SDK scans camera frames for planar geometry and produces plane trackables with pose and boundary.
Visualize
AR Plane Manager instantiates a translucent prefab to represent each detected plane in Unity.
Why this matters: Anchored surfaces let virtual objects obey gravity and look grounded, improving believability.
