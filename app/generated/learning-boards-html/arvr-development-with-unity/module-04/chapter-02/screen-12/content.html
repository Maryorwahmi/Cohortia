Hands-on Activity

Visualize planes and place a cube

  1. Select AR Session Origin and add AR Plane Manager and ARRaycastManager components.
  2. Create a Quad named ARPlaneVisualizer and a material set to Fade/Transparent. Tint it light blue and set alpha to ~40%.
  3. Add the AR Plane component to the visual and make it a prefab in Assets. Assign to Plane Prefab on AR Plane Manager.
  4. Create a small cube, make it ARCubePrefab, and scale it down to fit AR world scale.
  5. Create or paste the ARPlacementManager script into your project and attach it to AR Session Origin. Assign ARCubePrefab to objectToPlace.
  6. Build to a device, scan a textured, well-lit area, and tap a detected plane to place your cube.
Checks and tips
  • If no planes appear, confirm the Plane Prefab is assigned and has AR Plane component.
  • Test in varied lighting and textured areas to improve detection reliability.
  • Adjust material alpha to balance visibility and realism for the user.
Reflection
After placing the cube, scan more of the scene. Watch plane polygons change and think about whether you need to reanchor objects after merges or updates.
Key Takeaway

Follow the exact setup steps and test on a real device. Environment quality has more impact than code in many plane detection issues.
