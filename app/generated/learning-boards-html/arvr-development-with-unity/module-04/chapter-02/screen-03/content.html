Practice tasks and quick checks

Short actionable checks to confirm each skill during development and testing.

Observe plane refinement
Walk slowly while watching plane polygons expand and merge; this confirms continuous SLAM updates.
Mnemonic: Scan → See → Grow
Verify visualization prefab
Use a semi‑transparent material so planes are visible but don't hide the real scene underneath.
Mnemonic: Clear overlay
Test raycast placement
Tap near edges and centers to ensure TrackableType.PlaneWithinPolygon returns valid poses only inside plane boundaries.
Mnemonic: Tap → Ray → Pose
Check session/origin alignment
Move the device and ensure virtual objects remain fixed to the real world; instability points to tracking or origin setup problems.
Mnemonic: Move → Hold → Verify
