Process Flow

Plane lifecycle

Create: AR SDK reports a new trackable when features cohere. Update: boundary polygon and pose refine. Merge: overlapping planes combine into single trackable. Remove: low confidence or out‑of‑view planes may be discarded.
Create
When initial features indicate a flat surface.
Update
Polygon grows or refines with more observations.
Merge
Two trackables representing the same surface combine into one.
Remove
Planes may be removed if tracking confidence drops.
Key Takeaway

Expect plane geometry to change. Allow your app to reanchor, relocate, or allow user adjustments rather than assume fixed boundaries.

Concrete scenario
You place a vase near the edge between two detected patches. Later the engine merges those patches into a single larger plane. If your app depended on the earlier small polygon for placement validation, it might need to update the object's allowed interactions after the merge.
Design suggestion
Provide a brief scanning state UI and allow users to confirm placement or to move objects after initial placement. This reduces surprises when planes change.
