Device testing checklist
Lighting observation
Confirm ambient tint and intensity adjust as you move through lit and dim areas.
Anchor stability
Place objects, walk around, and verify they remain aligned to the same real-world spot.
Edge cases
Test sudden lighting changes, occlusion, and rapid motion to reveal weak points.
Add a small on-screen debug panel that prints reported averageIntensityInLumens and averageColorTemperature so you can tune mapping factors quickly while observing the scene. Device testing will guide practical choices like update frequency and smoothing time constants.
Key Takeaway

Real device testing is mandatory; visual improvements and anchor reliability are visible only under real lighting and motion conditions.
