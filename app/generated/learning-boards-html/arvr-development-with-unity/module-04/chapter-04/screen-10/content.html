Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Hands-on Activity
Activity checklist
  1. Add ARCameraManager to AR Session Origin and confirm frame events are available.
  2. Create and attach ARLightEstimator to read lightEstimation and update RenderSettings and Directional Light.
  3. Add ARAnchorManager to AR Session Origin to enable anchor APIs.
  4. Modify ARPlacementManager to create anchors at hit poses and parent placed objects to ARAnchor transforms; remove old anchors on move.
  5. Build and run on a device; test lighting changes and placement stability across motion.
Common mistakes
  • Not checking HasValue on light fields.
  • Applying unscaled lumens directly to Unity lights.
  • Failing to null-check AddAnchor return values.
Quick test
Place a cube, walk to a brighter area, and confirm the cube appears brighter and stays in place when you walk around it.
Key Takeaway

Work iteratively on-device: lighting and tracking behavior are best tuned by observation under real-world conditions.
