Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › What are AR Anchors?
Anchor definition

An anchor is a fixed real-world point tracked by the AR system. Parent your virtual object to an ARAnchor so the system maintains its alignment as tracking updates.

Why use anchors
They significantly reduce drift, improving perceived stability for placed content during an active session.
When to prefer anchors
When objects must remain at a specific real-world location or when multi-touch positioning requires reliable transforms.
Limitations & considerations
  • Anchors are generally session-scoped unless you implement persistence externally.
  • Anchors depend on underlying tracking quality; full loss of tracking can invalidate them.
  • Create anchors only when tracking confidence is good to avoid bad saved poses.
Common misconception
Anchors do not guarantee perfect absolute world alignment across app restarts without additional persistence steps.
Key Takeaway

Use anchors to improve placement stability during sessions; treat cross-session persistence as an advanced follow-up task.
