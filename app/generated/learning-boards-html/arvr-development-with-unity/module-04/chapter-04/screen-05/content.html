Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › AR Camera Manager
Component flow
AR Camera Manager → frameReceived event → your estimator script → update lights and probes.
Event data
  • averageIntensityInLumens
  • averageColorTemperature or averageColor
  • mainLightDirection and mainLightIntensity (optional)
  • sphericalHarmonics (for advanced lighting)
Best practice
Smooth changes over frames, clamp extreme values, and test on real devices under varying conditions to tune scale factors.
Visualization
Scene mapping
Use directional light for the main source and RenderSettings.ambientLight for general tone. Optionally update custom probes with spherical harmonics for reflections.
Common mistake
Applying raw intensity without scaling makes objects too bright or too dark. Use a divisor or curve to map lumens to Unity intensity nicely.
Key Takeaway

AR Camera Manager supplies the raw lighting inputs; your script interprets and smooths them for scene use.
