GameObjects & Components

Understand how empty GameObjects become visible, physical, and interactive through attached Components. This composition model is the basis of all Unity scenes, from simple AR overlays to complex VR simulations.

In this lesson
What a GameObject is and how Components add behavior.
Coming up
Common Components: Transform, Mesh, Collider, Rigidbody.
Hands-on
Build a simple physics scene to see components in action.
Why this matters

Component composition lets you reuse, iterate, and combine capabilities. In AR/VR, that speed and flexibility translates directly into prototyping interactive experiences and aligning virtual content with the real world.

Visual Concept
Composable parts make the object
GameObject (empty)
A blank container that holds Components. No visual or physical behavior by itself.
Components
Transform, Mesh Filter, Mesh Renderer, Collider, Rigidbody, and scripts attach to add capability.
Combine components to convert the empty container into a visible, interactive, or physical object.
