Rigidbody properties
Mass — how heavy the object behaves.
Drag — simulates air resistance.
Use Gravity checkbox and constraints to control motion axes.
Example test
Place Cube at Y=5, Plane at Y=0, add Rigidbody to Cube. Play the scene and observe the Cube fall and rest on the Plane. Modify Mass to feel the difference when hitting another object.
Misuse: Changing Transform.position every frame for a Rigidbody object can cause non-physical behavior. Use forces or Rigidbody.MovePosition for physics-driven motion.
Key Takeaway

Use Rigidbody to let physics govern movement and collisions; tune properties for realistic responses.
