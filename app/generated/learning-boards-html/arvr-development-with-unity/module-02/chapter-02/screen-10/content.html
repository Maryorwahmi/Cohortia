Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Add / Configure / Remove
Step-by-step
1
Select the GameObject and inspect current Components.
2
Click Add Component, search the Component name, and add it to the GameObject.
3
Adjust properties in the Inspector (e.g., mass, collider size) and test in Play mode.
4
To remove, open the Component gear and choose Remove Component.
Example: Add Rigidbody
Select Cube → Add Component → Rigidbody → set Mass to 1 → Play to observe gravity. If Cube lacks a collider, add Box Collider first.
Remember: changes made only during Play are not saved. Make required adjustments outside Play or reapply them after testing.
Key Takeaway

The Inspector is your control panel: add, tune, and remove Components, then verify behavior in Play mode.
