Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Physics Demo
Steps
  1. Create Plane → position (0,0,0) as ground.
  2. Create Cube → set position (0,5,0). Confirm Box Collider exists.
  3. Add Rigidbody to Cube. Play to observe gravity and collision.
  4. Create Sphere → add Rigidbody. Make Physics Material with high Bounciness and assign it to Sphere's Collider.
  5. Test different Mass and Collider settings and save the scene.
Troubleshoot
If objects pass through the floor, confirm both objects have Colliders and at least one has a Rigidbody. Also ensure Is Trigger is unchecked for solid collisions.
Try: change Rigidbody Mass on Cube to 10 and on Sphere to 0.1 to see the effect on collisions and feel of weight.
Key Takeaway

This simple scene demonstrates how Components combine to create visible, collidable, and physics-driven objects. Adjust settings to learn their effects.
