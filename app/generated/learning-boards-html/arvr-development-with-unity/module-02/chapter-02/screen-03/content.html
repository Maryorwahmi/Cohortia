Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Learning Objectives
Editor tasks
Create & Inspect
Make primitives and empty GameObjects; identify Transform and default components.
Add Physics
Attach Rigidbody to observe gravity and tune mass to change behavior.
Configure
Use the Inspector to change collider size, check trigger flags, and remove unwanted components.
Quick mnemonic
Think: Container (GameObject) + Parts (Components) = Function. The Transform is always attached — it anchors the object in space.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Demonstrate a Cube that falls under gravity and collides with a Plane by attaching a Rigidbody to the Cube and ensuring both objects have appropriate colliders.
