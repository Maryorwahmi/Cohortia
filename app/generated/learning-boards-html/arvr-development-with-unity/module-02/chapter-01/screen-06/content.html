Hierarchy & Inspector Windows

Managing objects and their properties

Hierarchy Window

  • •Lists all GameObjects in the active scene.
  • •Shows parent-child relationships, crucial for organization.
  • •Helps manage complex scene structures.
Example

A virtual character (parent) with a sword (child) attached to its hand.

Inspector Window

  • •Displays detailed properties and components of selected objects.
  • •Allows modification of Transform (position, rotation, scale) and other components.
  • •Your control panel for fine-tuning object behavior.
Pro Tip

Use the Inspector to quickly reset an object's transform to default values.

Key Takeaway

The Hierarchy structures your scene's objects, while the Inspector provides granular control over their properties and components.
