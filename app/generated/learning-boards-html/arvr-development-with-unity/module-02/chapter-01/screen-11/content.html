Understanding Coordinate Systems

Defining position and orientation in 3D space

Left-Handed System

  • •Unity uses a left-handed coordinate system.
  • •Thumb: Positive X-axis (Right).
  • •Index Finger: Positive Y-axis (Up).
  • •Middle Finger: Positive Z-axis (Forward).
Visual Aid

Form a 'gun' shape with your left hand to visualize the axes.

Impact on Transforms

  • •Object position, rotation, and scale are relative to these axes.
  • •Inspector window displays and allows direct input of these values.
  • •Crucial for precise placement and orientation in AR/VR.
Why it Matters

A clear understanding prevents misaligned objects and ensures accurate spatial relationships in your immersive experiences.

Key Takeaway

The left-handed coordinate system in Unity defines how all objects are positioned and oriented, making its understanding fundamental for precise 3D manipulation.
