Scene View vs. Game View

Understanding your workspace and player's perspective

Scene View

  • •Your interactive 3D workspace for building and arranging objects.
  • •Offers a free-moving perspective camera to navigate your environment.
  • •Primary area for visual construction and editing.
Analogy

Like a film director's set, where you physically place and adjust all elements.

Game View

  • •Displays what the player sees during runtime.
  • •Simulates AR camera feed or VR headset view.
  • •Used for testing and previewing the final user experience.
Key Distinction

You edit in Scene view, but you experience and test in Game view.

Key Takeaway

The Scene view is your construction zone, while the Game view is your live preview, both essential for iterative AR/VR development.
