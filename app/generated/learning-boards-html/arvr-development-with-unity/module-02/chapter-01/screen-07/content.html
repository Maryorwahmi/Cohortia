Scene Management

Understanding Unity Scenes

The blueprint for your AR/VR environments

What is a Scene?

  • •A single level or environment in your application.
  • •Contains all GameObjects, cameras, lights, and scripts for that specific part.
  • •Acts as a blueprint for a distinct user experience.
Analogy

Like a chapter in a book, each scene tells a specific part of your application's story.

Why Use Multiple Scenes?

  • •Optimizes performance by loading only necessary assets.
  • •Provides clear organizational separation for content.
  • •Facilitates collaboration and modular development.
Common Use Cases

Main menus, different levels, training modules, or distinct AR/VR experiences.

Key Takeaway

Scenes are fundamental for structuring your Unity project, allowing for modular development, optimized performance, and clear organization of distinct environments.
