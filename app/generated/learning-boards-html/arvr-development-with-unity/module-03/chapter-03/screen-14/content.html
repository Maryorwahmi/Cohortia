Design for comfort
A comfortable UI reduces fatigue and increases task success in XR.
Readability
Use larger fonts, strong contrast, and generous spacing. Test text at intended viewing distance inside the headset.
Interaction distance
Place common controls within comfortable reach or enable ray interaction. Avoid requiring extended arm holds.
Chunking & density
Group related controls and avoid cramming many small targets. Larger targets improve hit success for pointer-based input.
Testing tip
Always validate UI in an HMD using the exact scale and placement to catch readability and reachability issues early.
Key Takeaway
Human comfort drives good XR UI — prioritize readable text, reachable controls, and minimal sustained effort.
