What is Gaze Interaction?
A camera-originated ray detects look-based hover and, after a dwell timer, triggers selection. It is ideal for hands-free or head-tracked flows.
When to use: menus, selection at distance, quick look-based choices in VR and AR.
Misconception: Gaze is not always more precise — head jitter and small targets reduce reliability.
Good practice: add a progress indicator for dwell and tune dwell time for comfort.
Key Takeaway

Gaze works when hands aren’t available, but only if you give clear progress feedback and tune dwell time for your users.

Concrete Example
A gaze-activated button: add XRSimpleInteractable to a cube, set Gaze Dwell Time to 2 seconds, then subscribe to selectEntered to open a panel or play a sound.
Visual cues to add
  • Change material on firstHoverEntered.
  • Show a radial progress indicator during dwell.
  • Reset visuals on lastHoverExited.
