Gaze Interaction
Select by sustained looking; use dwell timers and progress indicators.
Pointer Interaction
Use XR Ray Interactor and controllers for precise aiming and selection.
XR Interactor Line Visual
Draws the ray and reticle but does not affect hit logic; it communicates intent to the user.
Summary Takeaway

Match interactor masks and colliders, subscribe to hover/select events for feedback, and test incrementally so interaction feels reliable and discoverable.
