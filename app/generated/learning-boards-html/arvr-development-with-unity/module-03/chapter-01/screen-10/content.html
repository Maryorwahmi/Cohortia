Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › XR Origin Setup
Create XR Origin
Right-click Hierarchy → XR → XR Origin (VR/AR). Expand Camera Offset to find LeftHand and RightHand children.
Verify
  • Main Camera exists and represents the HMD.
  • LeftHand and RightHand have XR Controller (Action-based) components.
  • XR Interaction Manager is present in the scene.
Practical check
Enter Play Mode with headset attached and observe that the camera follows head movement and controllers update their transforms when tracked.
Common oversight
Not assigning an Input Action Asset to XR Controller (Action-based) components will prevent logical actions from firing even if tracking is active.
