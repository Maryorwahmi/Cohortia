XRI — definition and role
A high-level toolkit that connects logical actions to physical inputs across XR devices through components like Interactors and Interactables.
Interactors
Tools on the controller or gaze origin that initiate interactions, such as rays or direct overlap spheres.
Interactables
Components on objects that define responses: grab, hover, select, or socket behavior.
Analogy
Think of Interactors as tools like a virtual hand or pointer, and Interactables as objects that have rules for how those tools affect them.
Common mistake
Assuming XRI auto-maps all devices without assigning or enabling the correct Input Action Assets; always verify mappings and action map activation.
