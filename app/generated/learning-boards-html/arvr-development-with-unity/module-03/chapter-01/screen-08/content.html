Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Interactable Types
XRGrabInteractable
Enables picking up and holding objects, with attach transforms and physics options.
XRSocketInteractor
Defines slots that accept compatible Interactables and snap them into a defined pose.
XRSimpleInteractable
Lightweight responses for buttons, toggles, or activations without full grab semantics.
Practical tip
Always provide a small attach transform and test release behavior; misaligned attaches cause visual jumps during grabs.
