Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Haptic Design
Principles
  • Subtle by default: favor short, low-intensity pulses for non-critical feedback.
  • Vary intensity and duration to convey different event types.
  • Contextual use: only vibrate when the feedback matches the user’s action.
  • Accessibility: avoid rapid repeated pulses and provide visual/auditory redundancy.
Key Takeaway

A small set of distinct haptic patterns, used consistently, communicates events effectively without causing fatigue.

Practical palette
Hover
Intensity 0.2, duration 0.05s.
Select
Intensity 0.4, duration 0.08s.
Impact
Intensity 0.7, duration 0.1–0.15s.
Iterate in Play Mode and test with different controllers to find comfortable defaults for your target hardware.
