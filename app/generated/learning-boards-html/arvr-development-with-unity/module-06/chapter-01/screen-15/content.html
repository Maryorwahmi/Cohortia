Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What to Remember
Toolchain Ready
Unity Hub modules and external SDK/NDK/JDK are installed and verified.
Config Correct
Active platform, Player Settings, XR providers, and scenes are set for your target.
Signed & Allowed
Keystore or provisioning is secure and manifest permissions like camera access are declared.
Final takeaway

A short pre-build checklist saves hours of debugging. Verify these three areas before packaging your XR app for distribution.
