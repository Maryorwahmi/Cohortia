How you'll practice
A guided lab will have you deploy an ARCore build, connect the Profiler, and add custom markers to observe and fix a hotspot.
Mnemonic
P-M-D: Profile, Measure, Deploy
Assessment
Identify a GC-related hiccup and state the mitigation strategy; add a custom marker and show the measured improvement.
