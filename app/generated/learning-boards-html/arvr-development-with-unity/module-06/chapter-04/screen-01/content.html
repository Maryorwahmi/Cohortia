Performance Optimization Techniques for XR
Keep frame rates high, reduce latency, and protect immersion across AR and VR platforms.
In this lesson
You will learn to identify rendering and CPU bottlenecks, apply batching and occlusion culling, optimize assets and lighting, and use the Unity Profiler to measure results.
Why it matters
Low or inconsistent frame rates break immersion and can cause motion sickness in VR or tracking instability in AR. Optimizations protect both UX and device constraints.
Explore Key Topics
Draw Calls & Batching
Combine static objects and reduce batches to lower CPU overhead during rendering.
Occlusion Culling
Bake visibility data so the GPU skips objects hidden from view at runtime.
Profiler-Driven Workflow
Measure first, fix the biggest bottleneck, then re-measure to confirm gains.
