Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Occlusion Culling
Bake workflow
  1. Mark large static geometry as "Occluder Static" and potential hidden objects as "Occludee Static".
  2. Open Window → Rendering → Occlusion Culling and review bake settings.
  3. Adjust Smallest Occluder and Smallest Hole if needed, then click Bake to precompute visibility data.
  4. Play and move the camera; verify objects are not rendered when fully occluded and check Profiler counts.
When it helps
Indoor or cluttered scenes with many objects that can be blocked by walls or large props benefit most from baked occlusion culling.
Example Scenario
A museum interior with many exhibits: walls and large display cases occlude distant objects, so baking visibility reduces GPU work as the viewer moves through rooms.
Misconception
Occlusion culling is not a cure-all for open outdoor scenes; it gives most value in environments with clear occluders.
Action
Mark occluders and occludees, bake, then verify Batches drop when objects become hidden during camera movement.
