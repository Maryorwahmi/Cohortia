Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Texture optimization
Practical guidance
  • Use the smallest resolution that looks acceptable at the object's screen size.
  • Enable texture compression appropriate to the target device.
  • Remove unused channels and consider disabling mipmaps for UI atlases where not needed.
Examples
Replace a 4K prop texture with 512 or 1024 where the prop appears small in frame; choose ASTC for many mobile XR targets to balance quality and compression.
Common mistake
Assuming a single compression format works for all devices. Compression artifacts differ by format; always check results on your target hardware.
What to profile
  • GPU memory usage after texture changes.
  • Frame time improvements when reducing texture size or resolution scale.
