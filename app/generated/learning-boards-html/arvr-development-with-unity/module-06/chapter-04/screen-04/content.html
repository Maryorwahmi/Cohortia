Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Why performance is critical in XR
Definition
Performance in XR is the combination of frame rate, frame-time consistency, and low latency between input and rendered output. All three affect user comfort and the accuracy of tracking.
Concrete Example
A VR scene targeting 90 FPS that briefly drops to 45 FPS produces a large frame-time spike that users perceive as stutter. That stutter can cause disorientation and break immersion.
Common Misconception
Randomly lowering texture resolution without profiling can waste time. Always profile to see whether CPU draw calls, GPU shaders, or scripts are the primary cost.
Key Takeaway

Protect frame rate and frame-time stability first; use profiling to choose the correct optimization path.

Why users notice spikes
  • Human vestibular system expects smooth updates; spikes create sensory mismatch.
  • AR tracking can lose alignment when rendering lags, causing jitter in overlays.
  • Small, consistent compromises are sometimes better than large intermittent drops.
What to monitor
  • Frame rate target for the device (platform-specific).
  • Frame-time spikes in the Profiler showing main thread or GPU stalls.
  • SetPass Calls and Batches as indicators of draw call cost.
