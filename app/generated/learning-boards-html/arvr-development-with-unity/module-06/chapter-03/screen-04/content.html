Core Concept
Apple Developer Program
Paid annual membership that enables app signing, device provisioning, and App Store distribution.
• Certificates verify the developer’s identity to Apple and devices.
• App IDs (tied to bundle identifiers) determine which services an app can use.
• Provisioning profiles tie certificates and App IDs to specific devices for testing.
Key Takeaway

Without an active Apple Developer membership, you cannot reliably sign and run ARKit apps on physical devices or submit them to the App Store.

Concept Cards
Certificates
Digital proof of developer identity used in signing.
App ID
Registered identifier that maps to your bundle identifier and entitlements.
Provisioning Profile
Links certificate, App ID, and allowed devices for testing.
Common Mistake
Expired membership or mismatched bundle identifier causes signing failures.
