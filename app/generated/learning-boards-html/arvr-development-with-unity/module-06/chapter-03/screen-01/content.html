Building and Deploying to iOS (ARKit)
Take a Unity AR scene through Player Settings, build an Xcode project, and sign & deploy to a physical device for accurate AR testing.
What this chapter covers
Prerequisites, Unity iOS settings, Xcode project, signing & provisioning, deploy to device.
Prerequisites
Mac with Xcode, Apple Developer membership, iOS Build Support in Unity.
Unity Setup
Player Settings: bundle id, IL2CPP, ARM64, camera usage string.
Xcode & Signing
Open generated Xcode project, select Team, let Xcode manage provisioning, deploy to device.
