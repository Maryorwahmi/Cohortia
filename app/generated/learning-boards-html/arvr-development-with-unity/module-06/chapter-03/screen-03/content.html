Deploy & Debug Objectives
What you will be able to do on the Mac and device after this chapter.
01
Manage Signing & Team
Select your Apple Developer Team in Xcode and let it provision certificates and profiles automatically.
Verified Competency Milestone
02
Bundle ID Matching
Ensure the bundle identifier matches the App ID in your developer portal to avoid signing errors.
Verified Competency Milestone
03
Deploy and Trust
Run the app on a connected device and follow the trust flow if it is the first installation from your account.
Verified Competency Milestone
04
Device Debugging
Use Xcode console and Console.app to view device logs for runtime ARKit issues like camera permission failures.
Verified Competency Milestone
