Definition
Camera Usage Description
This human-readable string tells users why your app needs camera access and appears in the system permission prompt.
When missing, attempts to access the camera can crash the app or cause App Store review rejections. Keep the message simple and relevant to the AR experience.
Info.plist excerpt
<key>NSCameraUsageDescription</key>
<string>Used for augmented reality experiences to place virtual objects in your environment.</string>
Pro Tip
Author the message for clarity. It is what users see when they first allow camera access.
