Build and Sideload an ARCore APK

Follow these steps in order to complete the lab successfully.

Step 1
Enable Developer Options and USB Debugging on your phone.
Step 2
Create a keystore and alias in Unity's Publishing Settings.
Step 3
Build the APK and save it to Builds/Android as ARPlaneDetector.apk.
Step 4
Connect device and verify with adb devices, then run adb install.
Step 5
If install fails, use adb uninstall com.yourcompany.yourapp and retry.
Step 6
Run adb logcat -s Unity ActivityManager if the app crashes to capture logs for diagnosis.
Expected Outcome

A signed APK installed on your phone that launches and performs AR plane detection and placement as in the Editor.
