Process

Enable Developer Options & USB Debugging

Follow a small sequence to make your device accessible to adb and Unity.

  1. 1. Go to Settings > About phone and tap Build number seven times to enable Developer Options.
  2. 2. Open Settings > System > Developer options and toggle on USB debugging.
  3. 3. Connect device via USB, accept the computer's RSA key when prompted on the device.
  4. 4. Verify with adb devices.
Key Takeaway

If adb does not list your device, USB Debugging or the RSA confirmation is usually the issue. Check cable and drivers next.

Common Mistake
Treating a phone as USB storage is not enough. adb requires explicit developer access via USB Debugging and an accepted RSA key.
Quick check
adb devices
# Expected: a device id and 'device' state listed
Pro tip
If your device appears as 'unauthorized' in adb, unlock the phone and accept the RSA fingerprint popup.
