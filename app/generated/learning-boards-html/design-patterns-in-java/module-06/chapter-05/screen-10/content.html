Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Strategy vs State
Definition Contrast
Strategy

Client chooses an algorithm. Context delegates to strategy; strategies are interchangeable and independent.

State

Object behavior changes with its internal state. States may alter transitions and behavior autonomously.

Example: Strategy

Checkout selects PercentageDiscountStrategy or FixedAmountStrategy based on a promotion code.

Example: State

An Order object moves from Pending to Shipped to Delivered and its allowed actions differ per state.

Misconceptions
  • They are interchangeable terms — they are not. Intent and who drives the change differ.
  • State objects often know about transitions; Strategy objects typically do not change the context’s state machine.
Reflection

Ask: who selects the behavior — the client or the object itself? Use that to guide your pattern choice.
