Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › External vs Internal Iterators
External Iterator (explicit)
  • Client calls hasNext() and next() to control stepping.
  • Good for complex traversal, early exit, or conditional steps.
  • Slightly more verbose; method-call overhead per element.
Internal Iterator (callback)
  • Aggregate applies a callback or exposes forEach/stream operations.
  • Simpler for mapping, filtering, and aggregation; aggregate can optimize.
  • Less control for client; not ideal for irregular stepping or complex stateful traversal.
Scenario guide
Use external iterator when you need to pause, rewind, or take different actions based on element position.
Use internal iteration for simple pipelines like print, map, or sum where the aggregate can control traversal efficiently.
Hybrid: provide both APIs so clients choose the most appropriate one.
Check your understanding

Can the client stop early? If yes, prefer external iteration; if not and operations are uniform, internal is fine.
