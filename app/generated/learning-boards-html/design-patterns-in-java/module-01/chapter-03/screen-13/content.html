Safety note
Patterns are not magic. If a simple, local solution suffices, prefer it. Overuse leads to indirection and harder debugging.
Introduce patterns incrementally and where you can measure benefits such as reduced duplication or clearer responsibilities.
Document why you chose a pattern and what trade-offs were accepted to help future maintenance.
Use tests to preserve behavior while refactoring toward a pattern; this reduces the risk of regressions.
