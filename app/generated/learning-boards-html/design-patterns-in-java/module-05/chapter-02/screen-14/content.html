Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts
Observer Pattern
One-to-many dependency where Subjects notify Observers of state changes.
Subjects & Observers
Subject manages registration and notifies; Observer implements update to react.
Safety & Scale
Manage unsubscription, thread-safety, and batching to avoid leaks and performance issues.
Takeaway

Use Observer for decoupled, event-driven systems but design lifecycle, concurrency, and scale strategies before production use.
