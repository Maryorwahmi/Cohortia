Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Subject & Observer
Subject responsibilities
  • Maintain a registry of Observers.
  • Provide add and remove operations for Observers.
  • Decide when to call notifyObservers and what data to send.
  • Consider holding weak references if observers are short-lived.
Pro-tip: keep notifyObservers lightweight and non-blocking.
Observer responsibilities
  • Implement update to react to notifications.
  • Register and unregister at appropriate lifecycle points.
  • Keep update idempotent or tolerant to repeated calls.
  • Avoid heavy blocking work on the update call; delegate long tasks.
Common-mistake: Observers that do expensive work synchronously inside update can slow down the Subject's notification loop.
