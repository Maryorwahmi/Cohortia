Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Strategy vs Conditionals
If-else / switch
  • Low upfront complexity for small tasks.
  • Becomes hard to extend and test as cases grow.
  • Violates open/closed principle when modified frequently.
Good for single, stable decision points.
Strategy pattern
  • Improves extensibility and testability by isolating algorithms.
  • Encourages small, focused classes and adherence to single responsibility.
  • Adds indirection and more classes; weigh cost vs benefit.
Good when behavior varies and you expect changes or need isolated tests.
Concrete example
DiscountCalculator: switch requires edits for each new customer type; Strategy adds a new class and registers it.
Key Takeaway
Choose Strategy when extensibility and testability outweigh additional classes and indirection.
