Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
Over-engineering
Applying patterns when a simple constructor would suffice increases maintenance cost.
Anti-pattern: God Object Singleton
Centralizing many responsibilities in a singleton hides dependencies and hurts tests.
Loose Coupling & Immutability
Factories and Builders promote decoupling and enable immutable, well-tested objects.
Summary
Choose patterns to reduce future maintenance and improve testability. Prefer DI for lifecycle and avoid premature abstraction.
