Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Refactor or Adapt?
When to adapt
  • Adaptee is third-party or legacy and cannot be changed.
  • Adaptation reduces risk and time to integrate.
  • Differences are interface-level and convertible with clear rules.
When to refactor
  • You control both components and a unified interface reduces ongoing work.
  • Adapters would create layered complexity and extra testing burden.
  • Performance concerns make delegation overhead unacceptable in critical paths.
Practical guidance
Use adapters to minimize change and risk. Use refactoring to build long-term cohesion when you can safely change both sides. When in doubt, prototype an adapter and measure the maintenance cost before committing to large refactors.
