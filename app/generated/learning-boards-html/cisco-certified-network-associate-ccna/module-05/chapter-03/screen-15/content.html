IPsec
Framework of protocols for securing IP communications via SAs negotiated in two phases.
IKE (Phase 1)
Authenticates peers and establishes the secure channel used to negotiate IPsec SAs.
IPsec SA (Phase 2)
Negotiates the protocols and algorithms (AH/ESP, encryption, hash) used to protect user data.
Takeaway

Match parameters exactly on both peers and verify Phase 1 before expecting Phase 2 SAs; prefer ESP for encryption and use tunnel mode for site-to-site.
