Integrity Controls
Detect and prevent unauthorized modification with hashes and signatures.
Hashing
Create a data fingerprint so any change becomes detectable during verification.
Digital signatures
Combine hashing with an authentication mechanism to prove both integrity and origin.
Verification processes
Regular checks on backups and transfers ensure restored data hasn't been tampered with.
Concrete example
Before restoring a customer database, compare stored hashes against a trusted baseline. If hashes differ, investigate to avoid restoring corrupted or modified data.
Misconception
Integrity checks are not optional for live systems. They help detect silent corruption or unauthorized changes early.
Key Takeaway

Integrate hashing and signatures into backups and transfers, and verify before trusting restored data.
