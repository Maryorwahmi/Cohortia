Learning Objectives — Applied Skills
Practice mapping threats to controls and planning incident steps.
Learn → Apply
01
Differentiate security controls
Classify controls as technical or administrative, and choose the right mix to reduce specific risks. Controls enforce policies and reduce exposure.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Map threats to CIA principles
Decide which principle is primarily threatened and prioritize controls that restore that property. This clarifies trade-offs when resources are limited.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Write basic policies
Create clear rules such as acceptable use and patching cadence to close common vulnerabilities. Policies guide consistent behavior across an organization.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Plan incident response steps
Prepare to detect, contain, eradicate, recover, and learn from incidents so damage and downtime remain limited. Practice reduces panic and speeds recovery.
