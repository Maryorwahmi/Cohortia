Learning Objectives
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
Bloom progression
01
Identify threats and vulnerabilities
Recognize malware, DoS, and common weak points such as unpatched systems or default credentials. This skill helps you triage risk and prioritize fixes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Explain the CIA triad
Define Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability and map threats to each principle to choose suitable controls. This enables precise risk responses.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Describe malware and social engineering
Differentiate viruses, worms, Trojans, ransomware, and spyware, and identify phishing, pretexting, and baiting tactics. This knowledge informs detection and training measures.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Apply policies and controls
Match technical controls like firewalls and IDS/IPS with administrative controls such as awareness training and incident response plans to reduce risk.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
