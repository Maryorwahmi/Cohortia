Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Point-to-point links
Definition

/30 blocks for two hosts

/30 provides four addresses; two are usable. This is compact and predictable for links between two routers.

Example
192.168.10.0/30 → range 192.168.10.0–192.168.10.3. Usable: 192.168.10.1 and 192.168.10.2. Broadcast: 192.168.10.3.
Allocation tip
When including many point‑to‑point links in a VLSM plan, place /30 blocks in a dedicated range and increment by four addresses for each link to maintain clarity and avoid overlap with larger LAN subnets.
Misconception check

Do not assume every two-host need must be a /30; use the smallest standard block appropriate to your routing and design constraints. In classic subnetting /30 is standard.
