Why this ordering works
  • Largest block first reduces fragmentation and ensures contiguous free space.
  • Picking the smallest mask that fits minimizes wasted addresses.
  • Tracking the next available address prevents accidental overlaps.
Key Takeaway

A structured allocation order turns VLSM planning into a repeatable, verifiable process.
