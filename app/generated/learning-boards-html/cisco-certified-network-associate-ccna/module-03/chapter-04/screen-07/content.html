Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Protocol Comparison
RIP
Distance-vector, hop count

Simple to configure but slow to converge and limited to 15 hops. Use only for very small or legacy networks.

EIGRP
Cisco hybrid protocol

Fast convergence with composite metric. Effective in Cisco-managed environments but proprietary.

OSPF
Link-state, area-based

Builds an LSDB, uses Dijkstra for shortest path, and supports areas for scalability. Ideal for enterprise networks.

Practical note

For CCNA and enterprise designs, focus on OSPF because it combines scalability, standardization, and hierarchical tools like areas and Area 0 backbone design.
