Process Flow

Area design and Area 0

Areas divide the OSPF domain. Area 0 is the backbone and must connect all other areas for inter-area routing.

Step 1
Assign networks to areas

Use area IDs to limit LSDB scope and group related networks.

Step 2
Connect areas to Area 0

Ensure ABRs or routers link non-backbone areas to the backbone for inter-area communication.

Step 3
Contain topology change

A change in one area should not require full-domain recalculation if areas are used properly.

Key Takeaway

Areas increase scalability. Always ensure non-backbone areas connect to Area 0 to preserve routing reachability across the domain.
