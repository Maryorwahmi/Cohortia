Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › IGP vs EGP

Interior Gateway Protocols (IGP)

Used inside a single AS. Examples: RIP, EIGRP, OSPF. Focus on reachability and quick convergence.

When to use

On networks you control where you need fast internal rerouting and topology awareness.

Exterior Gateway Protocols (EGP)

Used between autonomous systems. BGP is the standard for Internet-scale routing and supports policy controls.

Concrete example

Your company runs OSPF internally to build reachability. When peering with an ISP, you use BGP to exchange and filter prefixes.

Misconception

IGPs are not suitable for interdomain policy. BGP provides policy controls and path attributes necessary for multi-AS routing decisions.
