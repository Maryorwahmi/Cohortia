Wide Area Network (WAN)

A telecommunications network that spans a large geographic area to connect LANs and systems.

Why it matters
WAN choice affects application performance, security, and cost. Design to match traffic needs.
Common misconception
An encrypted VPN over the internet secures data but does not provide guaranteed bandwidth or latency like a leased line.
Key Takeaway

Choose WAN technology by matching application SLAs to the connectivity option's guarantees.

Concrete example

A company with a VoIP deployment may pay for leased lines or MPLS between major sites to ensure low latency, while remote branches use VPNs to access cloud applications.
