How MPLS works
Service providers attach short labels to packets so intermediate routers forward based on labels. This enables faster forwarding and traffic engineering.
Label switching speeds packet forwarding
MPLS VPNs provide logical separation for multiple customers
Enables QoS and traffic engineering
Does not provide encryption by default
Key Takeaway

Use MPLS for provider-managed performance and logical VPNs when you need QoS and traffic engineering.

Concrete example

A multi-site enterprise uses an MPLS VPN so voice receives priority across the provider backbone, and branches remain logically separated.

Design note

Coordinate label distribution and QoS policies with the provider during design and testing.
