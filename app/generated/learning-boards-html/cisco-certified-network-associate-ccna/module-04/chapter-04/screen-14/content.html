Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Wide Area Network (WAN)

A network spanning large geography; choose technology based on SLA and application requirements.

MPLS

Label-based forwarding used by providers for fast forwarding and VPN/traffic engineering.

Leased Line

Dedicated, point-to-point circuit offering guaranteed bandwidth and low latency.

VPN

Secure tunnel over the internet. Site-to-site for networks, remote-access for users.

HDLC / PPP

HDLC is Cisco default; PPP is standard with authentication (PAP/CHAP), LQM, and multiplexing.

SD-WAN

Separates control from data plane to centrally manage multiple transport links and route traffic by application needs.

Summary

Match technology to need: leased lines for strict SLAs, MPLS for provider-managed VPNs and traffic engineering, VPNs for encrypted internet transport, and PPP for authenticated serial links.
