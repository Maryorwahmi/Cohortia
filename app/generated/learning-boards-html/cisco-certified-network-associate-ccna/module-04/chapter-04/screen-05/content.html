Leased Line Characteristics
  • Private, point-to-point circuit with exclusive bandwidth.
  • Predictable latency and consistent performance.
  • Often the most expensive WAN option.
Example

A data center and a headquarters connected by a dedicated fiber leased line to meet strict application SLAs.

Key Takeaway

Reserve leased lines for traffic that requires SLAs and predictable performance.

Design checklist
  1. Confirm bandwidth, latency, and SLA terms with the carrier.
  2. Plan redundancy: geometric diversity or secondary circuits.
  3. Verify whether encryption is still required by policy.
Common mistake

Assuming private equals secure; regulatory or compliance requirements may still necessitate encryption over leased circuits.
