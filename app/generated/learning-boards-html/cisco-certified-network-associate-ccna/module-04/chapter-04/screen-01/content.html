WAN Concepts and Technologies

Compare WAN services, learn HDLC and PPP encapsulation, configure a serial link, and explore SD-WAN benefits for modern enterprise connectivity.

In this lesson • Coming up: WAN options • Serial config • SD-WAN
Orientation

We'll develop an operational understanding: what each WAN option gives you, how PPP adds authentication and link checks to serial links, and why SD-WAN changes the decision process for routing traffic across multiple links.

Leased Line
Private, point-to-point circuit with guaranteed bandwidth and low latency.
MPLS
Provider backbone using labels for fast forwarding and VPN separation.
VPN/Internet
Encrypted tunnels over the internet; cost-effective but no inherent QoS.
Concept Map
WAN types → leased, MPLS, Internet+VPN, SD-WAN
Serial links → HDLC or PPP
Security → VPN with IPsec, and PPP authentication (CHAP)
