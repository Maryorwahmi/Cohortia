What SD-WAN does

Centralizes control, monitors link quality, and routes traffic across multiple WAN links based on policy and application requirements.

Dynamic path selection based on performance metrics
Centralized visibility and simplified management
Cost optimization by steering low-priority traffic to cheaper links
Key Takeaway

SD-WAN lets you orchestrate multiple transports to meet application SLAs while optimizing cost and visibility.

Concrete example

Send voice over an MPLS link and bulk backup over internet links, using SD-WAN policies to steer traffic based on realtime metrics.

Design caution

SD-WAN improves path choice but cannot create provider SLAs on an unmanaged internet link; important traffic still needs appropriate transport guarantees.
