Common Mistakes
Prevent misconfigurations
Forgotten preempt
A recovered higher-priority router won't reclaim active status unless preempt is set.
Mismatched group/IP
Different group numbers or virtual IPs prevent routers from forming the same HSRP group.
No tracking configured
Router with dead uplink remains active and black-holes traffic if tracking is not used.
Quick verification checklist
  • Match group and virtual IP across routers.
  • Confirm priorities and preempt settings.
  • Enable tracking for critical links and test failover.
  • Validate with show standby brief and host ARP checks.
