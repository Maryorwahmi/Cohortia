Comparison
HSRP • VRRP • GLBP
HSRP

Cisco proprietary. Active and standby roles. Primary focus for CCNA examples.

Use when all devices are Cisco and you want simple active/standby redundancy.
VRRP

Open standard. Master and backup roles. Vendor interoperable for multi-vendor networks.

Use when routers from different vendors must share a virtual gateway.
GLBP

Cisco proprietary. Load-sharing plus redundancy. Multiple routers forward for one virtual IP.

Use when you want active-active forwarding across multiple routers for bandwidth or redundancy.
Concrete contrast
HSRP and VRRP provide single active forwarder behavior; GLBP can distribute forwarding. Choose GLBP only if load distribution and multiple active forwarders are required.
