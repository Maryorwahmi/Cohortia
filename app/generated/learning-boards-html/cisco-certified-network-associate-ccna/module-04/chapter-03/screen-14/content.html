Design Consideration
Make failover operationally effective
Beyond the virtual IP

Ensure routing, NAT, firewall rules, and ACLs are consistent so the standby can forward traffic properly when it becomes active.

  • Verify routing protocol adjacency and route distribution on all participants.
  • Synchronize NAT and firewall rules used for outbound traffic.
  • Include failover testing in maintenance windows to catch policy issues early.
Checklist
Routing parity, ACL/NAT parity, tracking for uplinks, and test failovers regularly.
Key Takeaway

Address-level redundancy must be paired with functional configuration parity to maintain service after failover.
