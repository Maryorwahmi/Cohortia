NAT Planning & Maintenance
Practical checklist for assigning and maintaining NAT mappings.
Document mappings
Record static NAT entries and pool assignments with owner and purpose to avoid conflicts.
Capacity planning
Estimate concurrent sessions to size pools. Use PAT if address resources are limited.
Operational tips
Schedule maintenance for major NAT changes, clear translations during tests, and use show ip nat statistics to monitor usage. Keep debug usage minimal and collect logs during controlled tests only.
Key Takeaway

Treat NAT mappings as part of IPAM and change management; good documentation prevents outages and conflicts.
