Advanced Public DNS Record Types

Configuring email and verification with MX and TXT records

MX Record (Mail Exchange)

Specifies the mail servers responsible for receiving email messages for your domain. Crucial for email delivery.

Example: yourcompany.com → mail.yourcompany.com (priority 10)
TXT Record (Text Record)

Holds arbitrary text strings, often used for email validation (SPF, DKIM) or domain ownership verification.

Example: yourcompany.com → "v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all"
Key Takeaway

MX records are essential for email routing, while TXT records provide flexible text storage for verification and security protocols.

Other Important Records
NS Record (Name Server)

Specifies authoritative DNS servers. Automatically created by Azure DNS.

SOA Record (Start of Authority)

Provides authoritative info about the DNS zone. Automatically created by Azure DNS.
