Chapter Review

Key Concepts: Part 2

Essential terms and definitions from this chapter

MX Record

Specifies mail servers responsible for receiving email messages on behalf of your domain.

TXT Record

Stores arbitrary text strings, often used for email validation (SPF, DKIM) or domain ownership verification.

VNet Link

Connects an Azure Virtual Network to a Private DNS zone, enabling name resolution and/or automatic registration of VMs.

Registration VNet Link

Enables automatic registration of VM hostnames and private IPs in a private DNS zone.

Resolution VNet Link

Enables resources in a VNet to resolve names in a private DNS zone.

Summary Takeaway

MX and TXT records manage email and verification, while VNet links are essential for connecting virtual networks to private DNS zones for seamless internal name resolution and automatic VM registration.
