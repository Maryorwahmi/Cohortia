Optimal Subnet Sizing and Security Posture

Balancing capacity, granularity, and security in your VNet subnets.

Subnet Sizing Mistakes

Incorrect subnet sizing can lead to significant issues:

  • Too Small: Leads to IP address exhaustion as resources scale.
  • Too Large: Reduces network segmentation granularity, impacting security.
Best Practice

Start with reasonable sizes (e.g., /24 or /22) and reserve larger blocks for future expansion or specialized services.

Security Considerations

Subnet design is integral to your security posture:

  • Align with Policies: Ensure VNet design meets organizational security requirements.
  • Protect Sensitive Resources: Never expose databases or critical services directly to the internet.
  • Understand Traffic Flow: Know how traffic moves between subnets and external networks.
Key Takeaway

Optimal subnet sizing balances capacity and security. Avoid extremes, plan for growth, and always align your VNet design with robust security policies to protect sensitive resources.
