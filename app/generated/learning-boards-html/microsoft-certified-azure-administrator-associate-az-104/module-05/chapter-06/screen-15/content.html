Key Concepts

A quick review of traffic control mechanisms and service tiers.

Health Probe

A mechanism used by the load balancer to monitor the health and availability of instances in the backend pool.

Load Balancing Rule

Defines how incoming traffic on a specific frontend IP and port is distributed to the backend pool.

Standard SKU

A feature-rich Load Balancer SKU recommended for production, supporting Availability Zones and outbound rules.

Summary Takeaway

Health probes and load balancing rules are crucial for traffic management, with the Standard SKU offering advanced capabilities for robust deployments.
