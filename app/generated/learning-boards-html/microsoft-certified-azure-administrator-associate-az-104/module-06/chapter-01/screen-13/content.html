Avoiding Common Diagnostic Setting Mistakes

Be aware of common pitfalls when configuring diagnostic settings to ensure effective and cost-efficient monitoring without security risks.

Forgetting to Configure

Many services default to minimal data collection, leading to a lack of visibility when issues arise.

No Retention Policy for Storage

Sending all logs to storage without a clear retention policy can lead to unexpected storage costs.

Ignoring Sensitive Data

Be mindful of sensitive data in logs and ensure appropriate access controls for destinations.

Infrequent Review

Monitoring needs evolve; regularly review and update diagnostic settings to match current requirements.

Key Takeaway

Proactive and thoughtful management of diagnostic settings is crucial to avoid visibility gaps, control costs, and maintain security in your Azure environment.
