Recovery Plans

Group VMs, define startup order, and include custom scripts for pre/post-actions during failover. Automates complex application recovery sequences.

Concrete Example

After a regional outage, you fail over your application to West US. Once East US recovers, you initiate failback to return your application to its original location.

Key Takeaway

Failback returns workloads to the primary region, while Recovery Plans orchestrate complex failover sequences with custom actions for reliable application recovery.
