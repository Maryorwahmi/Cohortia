Definition

What is Disaster Recovery (DR)?

DR is the strategy and processes an organization uses to recover and resume critical technology infrastructure and systems after a natural or human-induced disaster.

Importance
  • Minimizes downtime and data loss.
  • Ensures business continuity and operational resilience.
  • Protects against financial losses and reputational damage.
  • A business imperative in today's 'always-on' world.
DR Process Flow
Primary SiteLive Operations
Disaster EventUnforeseen Disruption
Recovery SiteResumed Operations
Concrete Example

A retail company's e-commerce platform needs DR to ensure customers can continue shopping even if their primary data center goes offline, preventing lost sales and customer dissatisfaction.

Key Takeaway

DR is a holistic strategy to restore entire IT services after a disaster, ensuring business continuity and minimizing impact, distinct from data backup.
