Azure Backup

Data Recovery & Point-in-Time Restore

  • Primary Goal: Data recovery, restoring individual files, folders, or VMs to a previous state.
  • RTO (Recovery Time Objective): Typically higher (hours to days), as focus is on data integrity.
  • RPO (Recovery Point Objective): Can vary, often point-in-time snapshots.
  • Scope: Granular data restoration.
Azure Site Recovery

Workload Availability & Business Continuity

  • Primary Goal: Workload availability, restoring entire services and applications.
  • RTO (Recovery Time Objective): Low (minutes), critical for business continuity.
  • RPO (Recovery Point Objective): Low (seconds/minutes), continuous replication.
  • Scope: Full application stack recovery and orchestration.
Concrete Example

Backup: Restoring a corrupted database file from yesterday's backup. DR with ASR: Failing over an entire e-commerce application to a secondary region after a primary data center outage.

Key Takeaway

Azure Backup focuses on data recovery (higher RTO), while ASR targets workload availability (low RTO/RPO) by orchestrating full application recovery.
