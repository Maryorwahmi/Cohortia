Concrete Example

A company replicates its on-premises VMware servers to Azure. In case of a local data center failure, these servers can be failed over to Azure, running there until the primary site is restored.

Key Takeaway

ASR extends disaster recovery to on-premises VMware, Hyper-V, and physical servers by replicating them to Azure, eliminating the need for a secondary datacenter.
