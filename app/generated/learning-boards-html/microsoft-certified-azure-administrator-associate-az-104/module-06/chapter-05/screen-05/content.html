The Recovery Services Vault
A centralized hub for managing all your backup data.
Centralized Management

A single hub for all backups, recovery points, and policies across various workloads.

Data Security

Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, ensuring protection against unauthorized access.

Redundancy Options

Defaults to GRS (Geo-Redundant Storage) but supports LRS and ZRS for flexibility and compliance.
