Lab: Configuring VM Backup Policy
Defining backup rules for your Azure Virtual Machines.
STEP 1

Navigate to Vault

Go to `myRSVault01` and click "Backup" under "Getting Started".

STEP 2

Select Workload

Choose "Azure" for workload location and "Virtual machine" for what to back up. Click "Backup".

STEP 3

Create Backup Policy

Select "Create new policy". Name it `DailyVMBackupPolicy`. Set frequency to Daily, time to 03:00 AM, and retention for 30 days (daily) and 2 days (snapshot).

STEP 4

Add Virtual Machine

On the "Virtual Machines" tab, click "Add Virtual Machines" and select `myVM01`. Click "OK" and then "Enable backup".

Checkpoint

Verify that `myVM01` is listed under the selected virtual machines and the new policy is applied.

VM Backup Policy Configured

Your VM is now associated with a defined backup schedule and retention.

Key Takeaway

Configuring a backup policy for your Azure VMs ensures automated, consistent data protection aligned with your recovery objectives.
