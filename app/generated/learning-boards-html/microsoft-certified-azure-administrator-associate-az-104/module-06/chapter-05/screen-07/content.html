Policy Definition

Crafting Your Backup Policy

A backup policy is a set of rules that governs when backups are taken and how long they are retained. It's crucial for automated and compliant data protection.

  • Defines the frequency of backup operations.
  • Specifies the duration for which recovery points are kept.
  • Can be applied to multiple protected items for simplified management.
  • Ensures compliance with regulatory and business requirements.
Example Schedule

A daily backup policy for VMs might run at 03:00 AM, retaining daily backups for 30 days, weekly for 52 weeks, and monthly for 12 months.

Key Policy Elements

Backup Schedule

Defines how often backups occur (e.g., daily, weekly, hourly).

Retention Range

Specifies how long recovery points are kept (e.g., 30 days, 52 weeks, 10 years).

Snapshot Retention

For VMs, how long the instant recovery snapshot is kept on disk (e.g., 1-5 days).

Key Takeaway

Backup policies are essential for defining backup frequency and retention, ensuring data availability and compliance while optimizing storage costs.
