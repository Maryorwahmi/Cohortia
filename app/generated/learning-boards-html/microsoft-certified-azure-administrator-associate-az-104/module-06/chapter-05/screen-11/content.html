Lab: Creating a Recovery Services Vault
Setting up the central hub for your Azure backups.
STEP 1

Access Azure Portal

Open the Azure portal and search for "Recovery Services vaults".

STEP 2

Create New Vault

Click "+ Create" to start the vault creation process.

STEP 3

Configure Vault Details

Provide `Resource group`: `myMonitorRG`, `Vault name`: `myRSVault01`, and `Region`: East US (or your VM's region).

STEP 4

Review and Create

Review the settings and click "Create". Wait for deployment to complete.

Checkpoint

Ensure your `myVM01` (from Chapter 6.2) is running and in the same region as your new vault for optimal performance and cost.

Recovery Services Vault Created

Your central hub for backups is now ready to protect your resources.

Key Takeaway

Creating a Recovery Services vault is the foundational step for centralizing and securing all your Azure backup data and policies.
