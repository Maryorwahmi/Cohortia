Action Group Details

Configuring Diverse Alert Actions

From simple notifications to complex automation workflows.

Notification Actions

  • •Email/SMS/Push/Voice: Direct communication to individuals or groups.
  • •ITSM: Create work items in your IT Service Management provider.
Purpose

Ensure human operators are immediately aware of critical issues through various communication channels.

Automation Actions

  • •Webhook: Call a URL to trigger external systems (e.g., custom scripts, ServiceNow).
  • •Azure Function/Logic App: Trigger serverless functions for complex automation (e.g., scale out VMSS, restart service).
  • •Automation Runbook: Execute an Azure Automation runbook for predefined tasks.
  • •Event Hub: Send alert notifications to an Event Hub for further processing.
Purpose

Enable automated remediation, integration with third-party tools, and advanced workflow orchestration.

Key Takeaway

Action groups provide a comprehensive toolkit for responding to alerts, combining direct human notification with powerful automation capabilities to ensure efficient incident management.
