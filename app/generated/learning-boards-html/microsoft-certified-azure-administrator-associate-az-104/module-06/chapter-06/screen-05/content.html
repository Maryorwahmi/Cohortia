Keeping an Eye on Your Backups: The Monitoring Dashboard

Centralized visibility into all backup and restore operations.

Accessing Backup Jobs

  • Navigate to your Recovery Services vault in the Azure portal.
  • Under the "Monitoring" section, select the "Backup jobs" blade.
  • This provides a consolidated view of all backup and restore activities.
Key Takeaway

Regularly review backup job statuses and configure alerts for failures. This proactive approach ensures your data is consistently protected and allows for immediate troubleshooting.

Understanding Job Statuses

  • In progress: Operation is currently running.
  • Completed: Operation finished successfully.
  • Failed: Operation encountered an error; click for details.
  • Completed with warnings: Operation finished, but with minor issues that need review.
Pro Tip: Detailed error messages provide specific troubleshooting guidance for failed jobs.

Best Practice: Configure Alerts

Don't wait to discover a failed backup. Configure alerts (e.g., email, SMS) for failed backup jobs to receive immediate notifications. This allows you to address issues promptly and maintain your RPO.

Common Mistake: Assuming backups are working without verification. Always monitor and test!
