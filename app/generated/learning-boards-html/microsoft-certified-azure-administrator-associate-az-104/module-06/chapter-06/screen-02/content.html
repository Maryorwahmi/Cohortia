01

Monitor Backup Jobs

Learn to track the status of all backup and restore operations within the Recovery Services vault. This skill ensures you can proactively identify and troubleshoot any issues, maintaining consistent data protection.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02

Perform Full VM Restore

Master the process of restoring an entire Virtual Machine from a selected recovery point. This is crucial for recovering from catastrophic failures or corruption, ensuring rapid system recovery.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03

Execute File-Level Restore

Learn to recover individual files or folders from a VM backup without restoring the entire VM. This granular approach is efficient for recovering accidentally deleted data or specific configurations.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04

Understand Restore Options

Gain insight into the various considerations and choices when restoring different types of Azure resources, optimizing your recovery strategy for diverse scenarios.
