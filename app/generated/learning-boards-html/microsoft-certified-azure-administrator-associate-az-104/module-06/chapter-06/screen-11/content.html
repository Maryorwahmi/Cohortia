Data Resilience

Soft Delete: A Safety Net for Your Backups

Protecting against accidental or malicious deletion of backup data.

What is Soft Delete?

  • An extra layer of protection for backup data in Recovery Services vaults.
  • When a backup item or vault is deleted, it's retained in a soft-delete state.
  • This retention period is configurable (default 14 days, up to 180 days).
  • During this period, the data can be recovered, preventing permanent loss.
Key Takeaway

Soft delete is a crucial safety net, providing a window to recover accidentally or maliciously deleted backup data, thus preventing permanent data loss and enhancing your overall data protection strategy.

Protection Against Accidental Deletion

Soft delete is enabled by default, offering immediate protection. This means even if an administrator mistakenly deletes a backup, there's a grace period to undo the action before the data is permanently lost.

Scenario: An admin deletes a VM backup item, thinking it's obsolete. With soft delete, they can recover it within the retention period.

Configurable Retention Period

You can adjust the soft delete retention period to align with your organization's compliance requirements and risk tolerance, providing flexibility in how long deleted data remains recoverable.

Consideration: While soft delete protects, it's not a substitute for proper backup policies and access controls.
