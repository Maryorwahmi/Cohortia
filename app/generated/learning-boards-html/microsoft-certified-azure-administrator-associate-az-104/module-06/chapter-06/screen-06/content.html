Recovery Strategies

Choosing Your Path: Azure VM Restore Options

Flexible recovery tailored to different data loss scenarios.

1. Restore New VM

Creates a completely new VM from a recovery point, including all disks, network interfaces, and configuration. Ideal for disaster recovery where the original VM is lost or corrupted.

Use Case: Full system rebuild after a catastrophic failure.

2. Restore Disks

Restores only the disks from a recovery point to a specified storage account. These disks can then be used to create a new VM manually or attached to an existing VM.

Use Case: Recovering specific data from disks or custom VM creation.

3. File-Level Restore

A highly granular option to restore individual files or folders from a VM backup without restoring the entire VM, minimizing downtime and resource usage.

Use Case: Recovering accidentally deleted files or specific application configurations.
Pro Tip

Always consider your Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) when choosing a restore option. The most granular option that meets your needs will often be the most efficient.
