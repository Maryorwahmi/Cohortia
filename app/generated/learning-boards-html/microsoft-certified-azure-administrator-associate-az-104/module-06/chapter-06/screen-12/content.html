Immutability: Unbreakable Backup Data

Making backup data non-erasable and non-modifiable for enhanced protection.

What is Immutability?

  • A security feature for Recovery Services vaults.
  • Makes backup data non-erasable and non-modifiable for a specified duration.
  • Protects against ransomware attacks and insider threats.
  • Ensures recovery points remain pristine and trustworthy.
Key Takeaway

Immutability provides the highest level of data protection by making backups tamper-proof. It's essential for defending against ransomware and meeting stringent data governance and compliance requirements.

Defense Against Ransomware

Ransomware often targets backups to prevent recovery. Immutability ensures that even if your production systems are compromised, your backup data remains untouched and available for restoration.

Benefit: Guarantees that your recovery points are always trustworthy and uncorrupted.

Data Governance and Compliance

For organizations with strict regulatory requirements, immutability provides verifiable proof of data integrity and non-alteration, which is crucial for audits and compliance mandates.

Consideration: Once configured, the immutability period cannot be shortened, so plan carefully.
