Module 4›Chapter 3›High Availability vs. Disaster Recovery
High Availability (HA)

Continuous Operation

HA focuses on keeping systems running continuously, minimizing downtime from localized failures within a single datacenter.

  • Protects against hardware failures (server, network).
  • Mitigates planned maintenance downtime.
  • Focuses on uptime within a single geographic area.
Analogy

Like a backup power generator for a building, ensuring local services continue during outages.

Key Takeaway

HA is about preventing downtime from common, localized failures to maintain continuous service.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

Major Incident Recovery

DR focuses on recovering from catastrophic incidents that could take down an entire region or multiple datacenters.

  • Recovers from regional outages (natural disasters).
  • Involves data replication to a secondary site.
  • Focuses on restoring services after a major event.
Analogy

Like having a second home in a different city, providing a fallback if your primary residence is uninhabitable.

Common Misconception

DR is not a substitute for HA; they are complementary strategies for comprehensive resilience.
