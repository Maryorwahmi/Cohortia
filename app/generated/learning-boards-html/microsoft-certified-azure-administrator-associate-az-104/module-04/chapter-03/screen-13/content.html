Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember for Azure VM availability.

High Availability (HA)

A system's ability to remain operational and accessible despite failures, minimizing downtime.

Disaster Recovery (DR)

The process of recovering from a major incident that could take down an entire region or datacenter.

Availability Set

A logical grouping of VMs distributed across different physical hardware (fault domains) and update groups (update domains).

Summary Takeaway

HA, DR, and Availability Sets are fundamental for building resilient Azure VM infrastructure, protecting against various failure scenarios.
