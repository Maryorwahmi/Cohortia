Key Concepts

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)

A managed Kubernetes offering that simplifies the deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications.

Kubernetes

An open-source container orchestration platform that automates the deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications.

Azure Container Registry (ACR)

A managed, private Docker registry service in Azure for storing and managing your custom container images securely.

Chapter Insight

AKS and Kubernetes provide the robust orchestration needed for complex applications, while ACR ensures secure and efficient management of your container images.
