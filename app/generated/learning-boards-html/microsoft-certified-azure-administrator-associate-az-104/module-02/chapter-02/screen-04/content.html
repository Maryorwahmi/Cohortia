Users and Groups: The Foundation of Identity

Understanding their Role in Microsoft Entra ID

What are Users?

Individual identities (employees, partners) requiring access to resources. Each user has a unique User Principal Name (UPN).

What are Groups?

Collections of users (or other groups) used to simplify permission management and apply access policies at scale.

Key Takeaway

Groups enable efficient, scalable access control, supporting the principle of least privilege by managing permissions collectively.

Common Mistake

Assigning permissions directly to individual users instead of groups, leading to complex and error-prone access management.
