Core Components: Users and Groups

The building blocks of access control within your tenant.

Users

Individual identities representing people who need access to resources. Each user has unique credentials and attributes.

Groups

Collections of users or other groups, used to simplify permission management and streamline access assignments.

Key Takeaway

Users are individual identities; groups simplify access by bundling permissions for multiple users.
