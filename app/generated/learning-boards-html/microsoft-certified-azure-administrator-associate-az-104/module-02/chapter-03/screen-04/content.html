Core Concept

External Identities and Azure B2B Collaboration

Extending your organizational boundaries securely.

What are External Identities?

Users from outside your organization's Microsoft Entra ID tenant who need access to your resources.

Why are they Important?

Enable seamless collaboration with partners, vendors, and customers, fostering business growth and efficiency.

Key Takeaway

Azure B2B simplifies external user management by allowing them to use their existing credentials, reducing administrative burden and improving user experience.

What is Azure B2B Collaboration?

A Microsoft Entra ID feature to invite external users to your tenant, granting access to resources while they use their own identity provider.

Common Misconception

External users don't create new accounts in your tenant; a representation is created, linked to their existing identity provider.

Example Scenario

A consulting firm needs access to your project management application. Instead of creating new credentials, they use their existing corporate Microsoft Entra ID account.
