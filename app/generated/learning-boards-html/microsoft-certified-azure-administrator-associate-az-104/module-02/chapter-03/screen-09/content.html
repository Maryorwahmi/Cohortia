Restricting Guest Access: Principle of Least Privilege

Ensuring secure collaboration by default.

Default Guest Limitations

Guest users typically cannot enumerate the full list of users or groups in your directory, preventing unnecessary information disclosure.

Principle of Least Privilege

Grant guest users only the minimum permissions required to perform their specific tasks, minimizing potential security risks.

Modifying Guest Permissions

Adjust default guest user permissions in the Microsoft Entra admin center under 'External identities' > 'External collaboration settings'.

Key Takeaway

Always adhere to the principle of least privilege for guest users, keeping their permissions as restrictive as possible to maintain a strong security posture.
