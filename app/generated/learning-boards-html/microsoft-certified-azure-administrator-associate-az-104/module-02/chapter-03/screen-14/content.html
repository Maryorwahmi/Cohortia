Key Concepts

Reinforcing your understanding.

Invitation Redemption

The process by which a guest user accepts an invitation and gains access to your tenant.

Principle of Least Privilege

Granting users only the minimum permissions necessary to perform their tasks.

Access Reviews

Periodic evaluation of user access to ensure permissions are still appropriate and necessary.

Summary Takeaway

Secure external collaboration relies on understanding invitation redemption, applying the principle of least privilege, and conducting regular access reviews.
