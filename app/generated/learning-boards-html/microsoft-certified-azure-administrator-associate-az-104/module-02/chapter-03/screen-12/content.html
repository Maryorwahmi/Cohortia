Azure B2B vs. Azure AD B2C: Choosing the Right Solution

Understanding the difference for external identity management.

Azure B2B Collaboration

  • **Purpose:** Business-to-business collaboration.
  • **Users:** Partners, vendors, consultants from other organizations.
  • **Identity:** Use existing corporate or social identities.
  • **Scenario:** Granting access to internal applications/resources.

Azure AD B2C

  • **Purpose:** Business-to-consumer identity management.
  • **Users:** Customers of your applications.
  • **Identity:** Social accounts (Facebook, Google) or local accounts.
  • **Scenario:** Managing sign-up/sign-in for consumer-facing apps.
Key Takeaway

Choose Azure B2B for organizational collaboration with known external entities, and Azure AD B2C for managing customer identities in consumer-facing applications.
