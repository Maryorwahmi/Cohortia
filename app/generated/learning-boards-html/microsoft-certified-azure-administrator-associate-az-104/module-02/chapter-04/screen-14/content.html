Avoiding RBAC Pitfalls and Applying Best Practices

Ensuring secure and manageable access control.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • •Assigning roles at too broad a scope: E.g., subscription level when resource group suffices.
  • •Unnecessarily assigning 'Owner' or 'Contributor' roles: These grant extensive permissions.
  • •Not using security groups: Leads to complex individual user management.

Misconception: More Permissions = Easier

Granting broad permissions might seem simpler initially, but it significantly increases your attack surface and the risk of unauthorized access or accidental changes. Always prioritize least privilege.

RBAC Best Practices

  • •Apply Principle of Least Privilege: Grant only necessary permissions.
  • •Use Groups for Assignments: Simplifies management and scalability.
  • •Regularly Review Assignments: Ensure permissions remain appropriate.
  • •Use Narrowest Scope: Limit potential damage from compromised credentials.
  • •Consider PIM for High Privileges: Just-in-time access for sensitive roles.
Key Takeaway

Avoid common RBAC mistakes by applying the principle of least privilege, using groups for assignments, and regularly reviewing permissions to maintain a secure and manageable access control strategy.
