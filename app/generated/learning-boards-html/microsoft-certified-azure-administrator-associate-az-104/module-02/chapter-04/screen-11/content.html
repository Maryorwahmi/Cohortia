Performing RBAC Role Assignments: Azure Portal

A step-by-step guide to granting access visually.

Scenario: Assign 'Reader' Role

We will assign the 'Reader' role to our TestSecurityGroup at the resource group scope (e.g., my-resource-group). This grants read-only access to all resources within that specific resource group.

Why the Portal?

The Azure portal provides a user-friendly graphical interface, making it ideal for visual learners and for performing individual or infrequent role assignments. It guides you through each step clearly.

01

Navigate to the specific Resource Group (e.g., my-resource-group).

02

In the left-hand menu, click "Access control (IAM)".

03

Click "Add" > "Add role assignment".

04

On the "Role" tab, search for and select "Reader".

05

On the "Members" tab, select "User, group, or service principal". Click "+ Select members" and search for TestSecurityGroup. Select it and click "Select".

06

Click "Review + assign".

Key Takeaway

The Azure portal provides an intuitive, step-by-step interface for assigning RBAC roles, making it easy to grant specific permissions at defined scopes for individual or infrequent assignments.
