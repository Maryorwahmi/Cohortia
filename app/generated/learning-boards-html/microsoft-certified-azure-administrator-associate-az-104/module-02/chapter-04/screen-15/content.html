Removing RBAC Role Assignments

Revoking access when it's no longer needed.

Why Remove Role Assignments?

Timely removal of role assignments is crucial for security. It ensures that individuals or services no longer requiring access cannot interact with your Azure resources, preventing potential vulnerabilities and adhering to least privilege.

  • •Prevents unauthorized access.
  • •Reduces attack surface.
  • •Maintains compliance.

Portal Removal Steps

1. Navigate to "Access control (IAM)" at the relevant scope.
2. Go to the "Role assignments" tab.
3. Find the assignment you wish to remove.
4. Click "Remove".

Azure CLI Removal Command
# Get the object ID of the TestSecurityGroup (if not already set)
GROUP_ID=$(az ad group show --group "TestSecurityGroup" --query id -o tsv)
# Get the resource ID of your resource group (if not already set)
RESOURCE_GROUP_ID=$(az group show --name "my-resource-group" --query id -o tsv)
# Remove the Reader role assignment from the group at the resource group scope
az role assignment delete --assignee $GROUP_ID --role "Reader" --scope $RESOURCE_GROUP_ID
Key Takeaway

Timely removal of RBAC role assignments via the portal or CLI is vital for maintaining security, ensuring that access is revoked when no longer needed and adhering to the principle of least privilege.
