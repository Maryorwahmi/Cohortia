Configuring an Access Review

Step-by-step guide to setting up access reviews in Entra ID.

Step 1

Navigate and Initiate

Go to 'Identity Governance' > 'Access reviews' in the Microsoft Entra admin center and click 'New access review'.

Step 2

Define Scope and Reviewers

Specify what to review (e.g., group members, application users) and who the reviewers will be.

Step 3

Set Frequency and Actions

Determine the review frequency (e.g., monthly) and configure actions for non-responses and auto-apply results.

Safety Note

Always start with a 'take no action' or 'recommendation only' approach for initial access reviews to understand the impact before enabling automatic access removal.
