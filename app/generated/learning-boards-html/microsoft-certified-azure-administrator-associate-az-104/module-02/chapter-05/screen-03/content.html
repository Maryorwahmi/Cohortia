Module 2 › Implementing and Managing Identity and Access › Learning Objectives

Learning Objectives

Deepening your understanding of identity governance

01

Purpose and Benefits

Explain the core purpose of Entra ID Governance: managing identity lifecycle, securing privileged access, and ensuring compliance.

02

Access Reviews Implementation

Learn to set up and manage periodic reviews for user, group, and application access to minimize stale permissions.

03

Privileged Identity Management (PIM)

Understand PIM's role in providing just-in-time (JIT) access to privileged roles, reducing the attack surface.

04

PIM Activation & Terms of Use

Configure eligible assignments for roles and grasp how Terms of Use enforce policies, especially for guest users.
