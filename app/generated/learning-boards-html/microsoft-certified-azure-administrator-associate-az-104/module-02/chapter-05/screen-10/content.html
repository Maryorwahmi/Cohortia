Terms of Use for Guest Users

Enforcing organizational policies and agreements.

Key Features
  • Present terms and conditions to users.
  • Require explicit acceptance, especially for guest users.
  • Prompt users to re-accept upon policy updates.
  • Provides an auditable record of acceptance.
  • Configurable for specific users, groups, or all guests.
