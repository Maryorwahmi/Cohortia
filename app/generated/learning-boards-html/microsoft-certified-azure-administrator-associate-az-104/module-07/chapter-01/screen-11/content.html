Securing SQL Databases and Understanding Costs

Advanced protection for SQL and plan pricing considerations.

Defender for SQL

  • Vulnerability Assessment: Scans for and identifies database vulnerabilities.
  • Threat Detection: Detects anomalous activities, SQL injection attacks, and potential brute-force attempts.
  • Sensitive Data Discovery: Helps locate and protect sensitive data within your databases.
  • Alerts & Remediation: Provides security alerts and actionable steps to address threats.
Key Takeaway

While Defender for SQL provides critical database protection, it's essential to understand the cost implications of all Azure Defender plans to ensure a balanced security budget and strategy.

Cost Implications of Defender Plans

  • Basic Features (Secure Score, Recommendations): Generally free for all Azure subscriptions.
  • Advanced Defender Plans: Incur additional costs.
  • Pricing Model: Typically on a per-resource or per-transaction basis (e.g., per VM, per GB of storage).
  • Budget Planning: Crucial to review pricing details before enabling advanced plans to align with budget.
Common Mistake

Enabling various Azure Defender plans without fully understanding their cost implications can lead to unexpected billing. Always review pricing before activating advanced features.
