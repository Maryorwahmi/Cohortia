Turning Insights into Action: Security Recommendations

Specific, actionable steps to enhance the security of your resources.

01

Identify Vulnerabilities

Defender for Cloud continuously scans your resources to identify potential security weaknesses and misconfigurations.

02

Receive Recommendations

You receive specific, actionable recommendations, each with a severity level and estimated effort, to guide your remediation.

03

Implement & Improve

By implementing these recommendations, you directly improve your Secure Score and strengthen your overall security posture.

Example Recommendation

Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Recommendation: Enable MFA on accounts with owner permissions. This significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if passwords are compromised.

Key Takeaway

Regularly reviewing and remediating security recommendations is paramount for proactively reducing your attack surface and maintaining a strong cloud security posture.
