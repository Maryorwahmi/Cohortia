Introducing Azure Defender Plans: Tailored Threat Protection

Extending security capabilities for specific resource types with advanced features.

What are Defender Plans?

Specialized, advanced threat protection capabilities for specific types of Azure resources. They go beyond basic security posture management to offer deeper, intelligent protection.

Why use them?

They provide enhanced protection against advanced threats, moving from reactive security to proactive threat detection and response for your most critical assets.

Key Takeaway

Azure Defender plans are crucial for organizations requiring advanced, resource-specific threat protection beyond basic security posture management, enabling a robust, layered defense.
