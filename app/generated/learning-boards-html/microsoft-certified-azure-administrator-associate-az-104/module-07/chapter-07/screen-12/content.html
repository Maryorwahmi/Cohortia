Avoiding Common Cost Management Pitfalls

Learn to identify and prevent common mistakes to balance cost savings with operational needs.

Common Mistakes

  • •Ignoring unused resources: Leaving VMs or services running when not needed.
  • •Not leveraging reservations: Missing out on discounts for stable workloads.
  • •Lack of tagging: Inability to accurately attribute costs to projects/departments.
  • •Setting and forgetting budgets: Not actively monitoring and reacting to alerts.

These can lead to significant, avoidable overspending.

Safety Notes & Balance

  • •Performance: Ensure cost-cutting doesn't degrade application speed.
  • •Reliability: Avoid measures that compromise service uptime or data integrity.
  • •Security: Do not reduce security controls for cost savings.

Balance cost optimization with critical operational requirements.

Key Takeaway

Effective cost management requires vigilance against common pitfalls and a balanced approach that prioritizes operational integrity alongside financial savings.
