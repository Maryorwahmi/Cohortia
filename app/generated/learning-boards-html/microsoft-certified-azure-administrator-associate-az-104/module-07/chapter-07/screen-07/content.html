Implementing Budgets for Cost Control

Define financial thresholds and track spending at various scopes to prevent overruns.

Budget Scopes

  • •Subscription: Overall spending for an entire subscription.
  • •Resource Group: Specific spending for a collection of resources.
  • •Management Group: Aggregate costs across multiple subscriptions.

Align budgets with your organizational structure.

Budget Periods & Types

  • •Reset Period: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
  • •Cost Type: Track Actual costs or Forecasted costs.
  • •Thresholds: Define percentages (e.g., 80%, 100%) for alerts.

Gain foresight and control over your spending.

Key Takeaway

Budgets are critical for proactive cost control, enabling administrators to set financial limits, track spending, and prevent unexpected overruns across their Azure environment.
