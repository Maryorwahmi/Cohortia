Key Concepts

Essential terms and tools for Azure cost optimization.

Azure Cost Management and Billing

A suite of tools to help analyze, manage, and optimize your Azure costs effectively.

Cost Analysis

A feature to explore and visualize spending by various dimensions like resource, service, and tags.

Budgets

Financial thresholds set at a specific scope to track spending and trigger alerts.

Summary Takeaway

Azure Cost Management, Cost Analysis, and Budgets are foundational for gaining visibility, controlling spending, and ensuring financial accountability in your cloud environment.
