Visualizing Spending with Cost Analysis

Explore your Azure costs across various dimensions and timeframes to identify trends.

Explore by Dimensions

  • •Resource Group: See costs per logical grouping.
  • •Resource Type: Understand spending on VMs, Storage, etc.
  • •Service Name: Identify costs by Azure service (e.g., Compute, Networking).
  • •Location: Analyze costs by Azure region.

Gain granular insights into where your cloud budget is being allocated.

Visualize Trends & Drivers

  • •Spending Trends: Track costs over daily, monthly, or yearly periods.
  • •Compare Periods: Benchmark current costs against previous months.
  • •Identify Spikes: Quickly spot unexpected increases in spending.
  • •Top Cost Drivers: Pinpoint the resources or services consuming the most budget.

Understand historical patterns and predict future expenditures.

Key Takeaway

Cost Analysis is indispensable for granular visibility into Azure spending, enabling administrators to identify cost drivers and make data-driven optimization decisions.
