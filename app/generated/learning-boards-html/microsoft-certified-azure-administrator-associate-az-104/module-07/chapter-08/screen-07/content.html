Recommendation Category

Building Resilient Applications with Advisor

Recommendations to ensure business continuity and high availability for your critical workloads.

Key Reliability Enhancements

Deploy in Availability Sets/Zones

Distributes VMs across isolated hardware or data centers for enhanced fault tolerance.

Enable Backup for VMs

Ensures data recoverability and business continuity in case of accidental deletion or corruption.

Configure Geo-Redundant Storage

Replicates data to a secondary Azure region, protecting against regional outages.

Advisor's Role in Reliability

  • Identifies single points of failure in application architecture.
  • Suggests configurations to improve fault tolerance and disaster recovery.
  • Helps ensure business continuity for critical applications.
  • Provides insights for data protection and recoverability.

Example: Using Availability Zones

Advisor might recommend, "Use Availability Zones to improve application resiliency," for VMs not currently deployed with zone redundancy, providing steps to re-deploy or configure.

Key Takeaway

Reliability recommendations from Azure Advisor are crucial for building highly available and resilient applications, protecting against various failure scenarios from hardware issues to regional disasters.
