Recommendation Category

Streamlining Operations for Efficiency

Achieve process and workflow efficiency, resource manageability, and compliance in Azure.

Key Operational Improvements

Enable Diagnostic Logs

Configures logging for Azure resources to aid in monitoring, troubleshooting, and auditing.

Configure Azure Service Health Alerts

Notifies you of service issues, planned maintenance, and health advisories impacting your resources.

Use Azure Policy to Enforce Standards

Helps enforce organizational standards and compliance, such as requiring specific tags on resources.

Advisor's Role in Operational Excellence

  • Improves process and workflow efficiency.
  • Enhances resource manageability and troubleshooting capabilities.
  • Aids in maintaining compliance with organizational standards.
  • Reduces manual effort through proactive insights and alerts.

Example: Enabling Diagnostic Logs

Advisor might suggest, "Enable diagnostic log settings for Azure resources," listing resources like Network Security Groups or Key Vaults that lack proper logging, with steps to configure them.

Key Takeaway

Operational Excellence recommendations from Azure Advisor are key to streamlining cloud management, improving visibility through logging, and enforcing governance policies for a well-managed Azure environment.
