Continuous Optimization

Best Practices for Continuous Optimization & Avoiding Common Mistakes

Maximize Advisor's value while understanding potential pitfalls.

Regular Review & Prioritization

Schedule consistent reviews and focus on high-impact recommendations first (cost, security, reliability).

Tagging & Automation

Use consistent resource tagging for better filtering and automate remediation where possible with Azure tools.

Avoid Blind Application & Ignoring Alerts

Always understand implications before applying changes; don't ignore dismissed recommendations or neglect alert configuration.

⚙️

Optimize Smart, Not Hard

Combine best practices with caution to continuously refine your Azure environment for peak performance and efficiency.

Key Takeaway

Effective Azure Advisor utilization requires consistent review, smart prioritization, automation, and a cautious approach to applying changes, always testing in non-production environments first.
