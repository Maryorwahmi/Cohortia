Recommendation Management

Acting on Recommendations: Postpone, Dismiss, and Implement

Effectively manage Advisor recommendations to maintain an optimized Azure environment.

Direct Implementation

Apply recommendations directly from Advisor or follow links to the relevant Azure service in the portal.

Postpone Recommendations

Temporarily hide recommendations that are not immediately actionable, with an option to revisit them later.

Dismiss Irrelevant Insights

Permanently remove recommendations that do not apply to your specific environment or operational strategy.

✅

Take Action, Stay Optimized

Regularly review, implement, postpone, or dismiss recommendations to keep your Azure environment aligned with best practices.

Key Takeaway

Effectively managing Azure Advisor recommendations through implementation, postponement, or dismissal is crucial for continuous optimization and maintaining a clean, actionable dashboard.
