Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Advisor Pillars & Tools

Essential terms and ideas from this chapter.

Reliability Recommendations

Advice to ensure business continuity, such as using availability sets/zones or enabling backups.

Operational Excellence Recommendations

Guidance for process efficiency, resource manageability, and compliance, like enabling diagnostic logs.

Performance Recommendations

Tips to improve application speed and responsiveness, such as upgrading VM SKUs or optimizing databases.

Summary Takeaway

Azure Advisor provides comprehensive guidance across all optimization pillars, with the Advisor Score offering a quick overview and Advisor Alerts ensuring proactive notification of critical insights.
