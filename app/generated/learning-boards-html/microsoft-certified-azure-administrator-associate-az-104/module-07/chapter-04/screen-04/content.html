What is a DDoS Attack?

A Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack is a malicious attempt to disrupt the normal traffic of a targeted server, service, or network by overwhelming the target or its surrounding infrastructure with a flood of Internet traffic from multiple compromised sources.

Attack Goal

Make a service unavailable to legitimate users by exhausting its resources.

Attack Vectors

Volumetric (traffic flood), Protocol (resource exhaustion), Application-layer (L7 attacks).

Key Takeaway

DDoS attacks are diverse and aim to disrupt service availability, necessitating robust defense strategies.
