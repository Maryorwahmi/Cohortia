Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

DDoS Attack

A malicious attempt to disrupt a service by overwhelming it with a flood of Internet traffic.

DDoS Protection Basic

Automatic, always-on, free protection for the Azure platform infrastructure against common network-layer DDoS attacks.

DDoS Protection Standard

A paid, enhanced service with adaptive tuning, attack analytics, and rapid response support for public IPs within protected VNets.

Summary Takeaway

Azure provides two tiers of DDoS protection, Basic for platform infrastructure and Standard for enhanced, resource-specific defense of your applications.
