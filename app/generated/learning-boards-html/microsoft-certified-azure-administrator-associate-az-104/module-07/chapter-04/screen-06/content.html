Why Choose Standard?

Azure DDoS Protection Standard provides enhanced capabilities tailored to protect your specific Azure resources within a virtual network, offering enterprise-grade defense.

Defends public IP addresses associated with VMs, Load Balancers, and Application Gateways.

Advanced Capabilities

Includes adaptive tuning, attack analytics, and rapid response support.

Key Takeaway

Standard protection is crucial for critical applications, offering intelligent, resource-aware defense beyond basic platform safeguards.
