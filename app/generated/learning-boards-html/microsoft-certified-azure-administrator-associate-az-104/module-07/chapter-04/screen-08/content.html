Operational and Financial Benefits

Azure DDoS Protection Standard extends beyond mitigation to provide critical insights, expert support, and financial safeguards.

Attack Analytics & Metrics

Provides rich telemetry during a DDoS attack, including duration, mitigated traffic volume, and attack vector insights for post-attack analysis.

Example

Reviewing attack data in Azure Monitor to understand traffic sources and types, informing future security improvements.

Rapid Response & Cost Guarantee

Access to Microsoft's DDoS Rapid Response team during an active attack and credit for resource costs incurred due to a documented attack.

Example

Receiving expert guidance from Microsoft's team to fine-tune mitigation during a severe attack, with financial protection for unexpected resource consumption.

Key Takeaway

DDoS Protection Standard provides comprehensive support, including detailed analytics, expert assistance, and financial assurance, throughout the attack lifecycle.
