Common Mistakes and Safety Notes

Ensure your DDoS protection strategy is effective by avoiding these common pitfalls and understanding critical safety considerations.

Confusing Basic and Standard

Relying solely on Basic protection for critical, public-facing applications is a mistake; Basic protects Azure infrastructure, not your specific application's availability.

Not Onboarding VNets

Creating a DDoS protection plan is insufficient; you must explicitly link your target virtual networks to the plan for protection to activate.

Ignoring Application-Layer Attacks

DDoS Protection Standard primarily mitigates Layer 3/4 attacks; Layer 7 attacks require additional defenses like Web Application Firewalls (WAFs).

Lack of Monitoring and Alerting

Without proper monitoring and alerts, you may not be aware of an ongoing attack or the effectiveness of mitigation, hindering rapid response.

Key Takeaway

A comprehensive security strategy combines DDoS Protection Standard with resilient application design, WAFs, and other network security controls, along with robust monitoring.
