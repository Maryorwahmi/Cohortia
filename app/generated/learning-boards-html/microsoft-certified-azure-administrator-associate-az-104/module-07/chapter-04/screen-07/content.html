Core Capabilities of DDoS Standard

Azure DDoS Protection Standard provides intelligent, dynamic defense mechanisms to safeguard your applications.

Adaptive Tuning

Learns your application's normal traffic patterns to accurately detect and mitigate attacks without impacting legitimate traffic. This prevents false positives during peak legitimate usage.

Example

Distinguishes a legitimate traffic surge during a flash sale from a malicious volumetric attack.

Always-on Monitoring

Provides continuous monitoring of your public IP addresses within protected virtual networks, ensuring immediate detection and mitigation.

Example

Continuously scans for anomalous traffic patterns indicative of a DDoS attack on your web server's public IP.

Key Takeaway

Adaptive Tuning and Always-on Monitoring provide intelligent, continuous protection tailored to your application's traffic, minimizing false positives.
