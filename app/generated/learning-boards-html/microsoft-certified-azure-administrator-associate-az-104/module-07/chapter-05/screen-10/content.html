Defining Permissions

Key Vault Access Policy Components

Who (Principal)

Users, groups, or applications (via service principals or managed identities).

What (Permissions)

Specific operations like Get, List, Set, Delete for secrets, keys, or certificates.

On What (Object Type)

Secrets, Keys, or Certificates. Permissions are scoped to these types.

Common Mistake

Granting `Set` or `Delete` permissions to an application that only needs to `Get` secrets. Always follow the principle of least privilege.

Key Takeaway

Access policies are crucial for Key Vault security, defining precise permissions for principals on specific object types, and must adhere to the principle of least privilege.
