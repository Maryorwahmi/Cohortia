Compliance Management

Compliance Reports and Remediation Tasks

Monitoring policy adherence and automatically fixing non-compliant resources.

Understanding Compliance Reports

The Azure Policy compliance report provides a dashboard view of your resources' adherence to assigned policies. It categorizes resources as:

  • Compliant: Resources that meet all policy rules.
  • Non-Compliant: Resources that violate one or more policy rules.
  • Exempt: Resources explicitly excluded from policy evaluation.
Key Takeaway

Regularly review compliance reports and utilize remediation tasks to maintain a compliant and secure Azure environment.

Initiating Remediation Tasks

Remediation tasks bring non-compliant resources into compliance. Their behavior depends on the policy effect:

  • DeployIfNotExists / Modify: Remediation can automatically deploy missing resources or modify properties.
  • Audit: Remediation typically involves manual intervention or a separate policy with an automated effect.

Example: Automated Remediation

If a policy requires all VMs to have a specific diagnostic setting, and a VM is found without it:

  • A DeployIfNotExists policy identifies the non-compliant VM.
  • A remediation task is initiated to automatically deploy the missing diagnostic setting.
  • The VM is then brought into compliance without manual intervention.
