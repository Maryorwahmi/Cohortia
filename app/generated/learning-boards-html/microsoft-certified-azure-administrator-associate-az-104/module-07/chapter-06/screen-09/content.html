Policy Assignments: Applying Rules to Scope

Defining where and how policies are enforced in Azure.

What is an Assignment?

An assignment is the act of applying a policy definition or an initiative to a specific scope within your Azure hierarchy. This activates the policy's rules for resources within that scope.

  • Target Scope: Can be a management group, subscription, or resource group.
  • Parameter Values: Customize policy behavior for specific assignments.
  • Continuous Evaluation: Resources are checked against rules at creation/update and periodically.
Key Takeaway

Assignments are the activation point for your policies, defining their reach and impact within your Azure hierarchy.

Understanding Scope Hierarchy

Policies inherit down the Azure resource hierarchy. Be mindful of:

  • Management Group: Policies apply to all subscriptions and resource groups within it.
  • Subscription: Policies apply to all resource groups and resources within that subscription.
  • Resource Group: Policies apply to all resources within that specific resource group.

Practical Example

To ensure all storage accounts in your 'Production' subscription enforce HTTPS, you would:

  • Create or select a policy definition for HTTPS enforcement.
  • Assign this policy to the 'Production' subscription.
  • All new and existing storage accounts in that subscription will be evaluated.
