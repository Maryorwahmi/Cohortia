Key Concepts: Foundations of Azure Policy

Essential terms for enforcing cloud governance.

Azure Policy

A service to create, assign, and manage policies for enforcing organizational standards and assessing compliance at scale.

Policy Definition

A JSON-based rule describing conditions for evaluation and the effect to take if conditions are met.

Initiative

A collection of policy definitions grouped together to achieve a single, larger governance goal.

Summary Takeaway

Azure Policy provides the framework, definitions are the rules, and initiatives organize those rules for effective cloud governance.
