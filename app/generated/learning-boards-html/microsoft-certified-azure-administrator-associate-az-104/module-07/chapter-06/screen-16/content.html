Key Concepts: Policy Implementation & Monitoring

Understanding how policies are applied and managed.

Policy Assignment

The act of applying a policy definition or initiative to a specific scope (management group, subscription, resource group).

Policy Effect

The action taken when a policy rule is met (e.g., Deny, Audit, DeployIfNotExists, Modify).

Compliance Report & Remediation

A dashboard showing resource compliance status and actions to bring non-compliant resources into adherence.

Summary Takeaway

Assignments activate policies, effects dictate actions, and compliance reports with remediation ensure ongoing adherence to governance standards.
