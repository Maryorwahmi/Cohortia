The Three Pillars of Azure Policy

Policy Definitions, Initiatives, and Assignments.

1

Policy Definitions

JSON-based rules describing what to evaluate and what action to take (e.g., Deny, Audit).

Example

"Deny virtual machines without a specific tag."

2

Initiatives

Collections of policy definitions grouped to achieve a larger goal, simplifying management.

Example

"Azure Security Baseline" initiative includes multiple security policies.

3

Assignments

Applying a policy definition or initiative to a specific scope (e.g., subscription, resource group).

Example

Assigning a policy to your 'Production' resource group.

Key Takeaway

Definitions are the rules, Initiatives group them for scale, and Assignments apply them to your Azure environment.
