Advanced Geo-Redundancy: GZRS & RA-GZRS

Combining zone and geo-redundancy for maximum resilience.

Geo-Zone-Redundant Storage (GZRS)
  • ZRS in primary region, async replicated to secondary
  • Protects against datacenter and regional outages
  • Highest durability and availability
Read-Access Geo-Zone-Redundant Storage (RA-GZRS)
  • Same as GZRS, plus read access to secondary region
  • Most robust and highly available option
  • Maximum resilience for mission-critical workloads
Key Takeaway

GZRS and RA-GZRS offer the pinnacle of data redundancy, combining intra-regional zone protection with cross-regional disaster recovery for ultimate resilience.
