Account Types

General-Purpose v2: The Recommended Choice

The most flexible and cost-effective storage account type.

GPv2 Account Benefits
  • Supports all latest features
  • Lowest per-gigabyte pricing
  • Supports Blob, File, Table, and Queue
  • Recommended for new deployments
Why GPv1 is Outdated

GPv1 accounts are older, lack many modern features, and are generally less cost-efficient compared to GPv2. Avoid for new projects.

Key Takeaway

Always choose General-purpose v2 for new Azure Storage accounts to leverage the best features, performance, and cost efficiencies.

Comparison

GPv2 vs. GPv1

General-purpose v2
  • All services (Blob, File, Table, Queue)
  • Latest features
  • Lowest cost/GB
  • Recommended
General-purpose v1
  • Limited services
  • Older features
  • Higher cost/GB
  • Not recommended
GPv2 offers superior features and cost-efficiency for modern cloud deployments.
