Key Concepts

Essential terms to remember

Locally Redundant Storage (LRS)

Data copied 3x within a single physical location, protecting against component failures.

Zone-Redundant Storage (ZRS)

Data copied 3x across Availability Zones within a region, protecting against datacenter failures.

Geo-Redundant Storage (GRS)

LRS in primary region, asynchronously replicated to a secondary region for disaster recovery.

Read-Access GRS (RA-GRS)

GRS with read access to the secondary region, improving availability during primary region outages.

Geo-Zone-Redundant Storage (GZRS)

Combines ZRS in primary region with async replication to a secondary region for maximum durability.

Read-Access GZRS (RA-GZRS)

GZRS with read access to the secondary region, offering the most robust and highly available option.

Summary Takeaway

Azure offers a spectrum of redundancy options to meet diverse durability and availability needs, from LRS for basic protection to RA-GZRS for ultimate resilience.
