Consistent Tagging Strategy

Define clear tag names and values, and use Azure Policy to enforce consistent tagging across all resources for better management and cost allocation.

Avoid Over-tagging

Focus on creating tags that provide real, actionable value for cost, operations, or security, rather than creating too many complex tags.

Cautious Use of ReadOnly Locks

Be extremely careful when applying ReadOnly locks, especially at higher scopes, to avoid inadvertently blocking necessary administrative actions.

Review Resource Organization

Regularly audit your resource groups and tags to ensure they remain aligned with your organizational structure and cost management goals.

Key Takeaway

Proactive and thoughtful resource organization, combined with careful use of governance tools, is fundamental for a well-managed and cost-optimized Azure environment.
