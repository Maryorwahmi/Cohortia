Understanding Resource Locks

Resource locks are a critical safety mechanism in Azure, designed to prevent unintended changes to your cloud infrastructure.

  • Prevent Accidental Actions: Protects resources from deletion or modification, even by authorized users.
  • Inheritance: Locks applied at a parent scope (e.g., resource group) are inherited by all child resources.
  • Overrides RBAC: Locks apply to all users, regardless of their Role-Based Access Control permissions.
Key Takeaway

Resource locks are a vital layer of protection against human error, ensuring critical Azure resources are not accidentally deleted or modified.
