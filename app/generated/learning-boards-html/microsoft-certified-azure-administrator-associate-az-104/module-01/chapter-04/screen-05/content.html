Key Aspects of Management Groups

Management Groups provide a crucial layer for organizing and governing Azure subscriptions, especially in complex enterprise environments.

  • Scope Above Subscriptions: They are the highest level of organization, allowing you to group multiple subscriptions.
  • Policy and Compliance: Policies and access controls applied at this level are inherited by all child subscriptions and their resources.
  • Scalability: Ideal for large organizations to manage governance across many departments or projects efficiently.
Key Takeaway

Management Groups are essential for enforcing consistent governance, policies, and access control across multiple Azure subscriptions in an enterprise environment.
