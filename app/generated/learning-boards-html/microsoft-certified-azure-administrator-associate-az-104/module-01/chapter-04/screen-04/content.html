Azure's Organizational Layers

Azure's resource hierarchy provides a scalable framework for managing cloud assets, ensuring consistent governance and simplified administration across diverse environments.

  • 1
    Management Groups: Top-level containers for subscriptions, enabling policy and access management across multiple subscriptions.
  • 2
    Subscriptions: Billing and administrative boundaries, linking to an Azure account and containing all deployed resources.
  • 3
    Resource Groups: Logical containers for related resources that share the same lifecycle, simplifying deployment and management.
Key Takeaway

A well-defined Azure hierarchy is crucial for scalable governance, cost management, and consistent policy enforcement across your cloud estate.
