Deploying a Storage Account (Part 1)

Initiating your first Azure resource deployment through the portal.

Initial Storage Account Configuration

  1. 1

    Access: From your Resource Group, click "+ Create" or search for "Storage accounts" and click "+ Create".

  2. 2

    Subscription & RG: Select your subscription and the target Resource Group.

  3. 3

    Unique Name: Provide a globally unique name (e.g., myfirstaz104storage).

  4. 4

    Region: Choose a region (e.g., East US) where the storage account data will reside.

Key Takeaway

A globally unique name is essential for Storage Accounts; if it's taken, you must choose another.

Common Mistake
