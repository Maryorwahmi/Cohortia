Creating Resource Groups in the Portal

A step-by-step guide to organizing your Azure assets.

Steps to Create a Resource Group

  1. 1

    Navigate: Click "Resource groups" in the navigation pane or search for it.

  2. 2

    Initiate: Click the "+ Create" button.

  3. 3

    Configure: Provide a subscription, a unique name (e.g., my-first-rg), and a region (e.g., East US).

  4. 4

    Confirm: Review your settings and click "Create".

Key Takeaway

Resource Groups are logical containers; their creation is a foundational step for organized Azure resource management.

Common Mistake

Forgetting to assign a resource group to a subscription can lead to deployment errors or unorganized resources.
