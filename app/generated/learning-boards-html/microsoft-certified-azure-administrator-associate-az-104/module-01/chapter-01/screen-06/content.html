Management Groups: Enterprise Governance

Logical containers for managing access, policy, and compliance across subscriptions

What are Management Groups?

Containers that help you manage access, policy, and compliance for multiple subscriptions.

  • Apply policies and access controls at a higher level.
  • Policies inherit down to all contained subscriptions and resources.
  • Simplifies governance for large organizations.
Key Takeaway

Management Groups enable centralized governance and consistent policy enforcement across your entire Azure estate, simplifying management for complex organizations.

Common Misconception

Management Groups are not for resource deployment; they are for organizational policy and access management.

Hierarchy of Management
Root Management Group

Enterprise-wide policies

Department Management Group

Department-specific policies

Subscription A, B, C

Billing and resource containers

Policies flow down the hierarchy, ensuring consistent governance.
