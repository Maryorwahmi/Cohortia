sum(rate(node_network_receive_bytes_total[1m])) by (instance)

Sum rates across interfaces or hosts to get totals. Convert bytes to megabytes by dividing by 1024*1024 if desired.

Tip

Choose rate window wisely: one minute for responsive dashboards, five minutes for smoothing regular traffic. Longer windows mask short spikes.

Convert to megabytes per second by dividing the rate result by 1,048,576 and format your dashboard units accordingly.
