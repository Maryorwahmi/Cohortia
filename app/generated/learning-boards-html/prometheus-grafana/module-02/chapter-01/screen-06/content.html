Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Structure of prometheus.yml
Core sections explained
  • global: default scrape_interval and evaluation_interval that apply to all jobs unless overridden.
  • scrape_configs: a list of jobs that tell Prometheus which endpoints to scrape.
  • job_name: a label applied to metrics from that job to identify origin.
  • static_configs: explicit target addresses such as localhost:9090 for each job.
Misconception check: you do not need a separate job per endpoint; group related targets in one job unless different scrape settings are required.
Visual quick-reference
global:
- scrape_interval: 15s - evaluation_interval: 15s
scrape_configs:
- job_name: 'prometheus' static_configs: - targets: ['localhost:9090']
Key Takeaway
Understand global defaults and structure scrape_configs logically so your jobs and targets reflect how you want metrics grouped and collected.
