Configuration › prometheus.yml & Verify

prometheus.yml example

global:
  scrape_interval: 15s

scrape_configs:
  - job_name: 'prometheus'
    static_configs:
      - targets: ['localhost:9090']

  - job_name: 'node_exporter'
    static_configs:
      - targets: ['localhost:9100']
After editing, restart Prometheus or send SIGHUP to reload. Then check the Targets page in the Prometheus UI; the node_exporter target should be listed and show an UP status when reachable.
Verify with curl
From the Prometheus host: curl http://localhost:9100/metrics — if successful, Prometheus should also be able to scrape that endpoint.
Troubleshooting
If the target is DOWN, check firewall rules, verify the exporter process is running, and confirm the configured port matches the exporter's listening port.
Key takeaway
Confirm reachability with curl and the Prometheus Targets page to ensure your exporter is being scraped successfully.
