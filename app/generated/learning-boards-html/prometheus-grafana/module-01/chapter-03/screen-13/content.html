Common pitfalls

Prevent avoidable operational problems

Alert storms
Group alerts in Alertmanager and route by team and severity to prevent overwhelming responders.
High-cardinality labels
Avoid labels with many unique values unless you need them for aggregation; they increase series and storage cost.
Role clarity
Prometheus collects and queries metrics; Grafana visualizes data from Prometheus and other sources. Keep responsibilities distinct.
Quick checklist
  • Define alert severity and routing.
  • Review label sets for cardinality.
  • Confirm which system is the source of truth for each signal.
Key Takeaway

Operational hygiene around alerts, labels, and roles keeps the monitoring stack effective and manageable.
