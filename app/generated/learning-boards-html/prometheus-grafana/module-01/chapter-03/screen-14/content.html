Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Key Concepts
Prometheus
Open-source monitoring and TSDB with a label-driven model.
Grafana
Visualization platform that queries data from Prometheus and other sources.
PromQL
Query language for selecting and aggregating labeled time-series.
Summary

Keep collection, querying, and visualization roles distinct: Prometheus for metrics and queries; Grafana for dashboards and investigation. Use exporters and Alertmanager to complete the monitoring workflow.
