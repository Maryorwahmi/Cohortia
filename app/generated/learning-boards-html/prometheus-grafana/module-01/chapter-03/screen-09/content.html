Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Prometheus vs Grafana
Prometheus

Collect, store, query

Collection
Pulls metrics by scraping targets and integrates with service discovery.
Query
PromQL selects and aggregates labeled series for alerts and analysis.
Grafana

Visualize and correlate

Visualization
Build dashboards from queries against Prometheus and other data sources.
Templating
Use variables to make dashboards reusable across services or environments.
Concrete workflow
When an alert triggers from Prometheus, engineers inspect Grafana dashboards to visualize the affected metrics, correlate with other signals, and begin remediation.
