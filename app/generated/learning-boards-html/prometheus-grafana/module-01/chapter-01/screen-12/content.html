Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Instrumentation Pitfalls
Common trade-offs
Choose instrumentation depth based on usefulness and cost.
Excessive logging
Produces storage and noise issues; prefer targeted structured logs.
High-cardinality metrics
Labels that change per request or user can overwhelm a metrics store.
Trace sampling
Sampling reduces cost but risks missing rare failures; tune for critical flows.
Mitigation strategies
  • Standardize log format and key names across services.
  • Limit metric labels to operationally meaningful keys.
  • Apply trace sampling with higher rates for critical paths.
Key takeaway
Instrumentation design is iterative: observe incidents, then adjust what you collect to improve diagnosability while controlling cost.
