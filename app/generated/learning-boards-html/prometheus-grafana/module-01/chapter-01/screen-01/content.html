The Pillars of Observability
Understand why logs, metrics, and traces are complementary tools for modern systems.

Observability lets engineers ask ad-hoc questions about system behavior without changing code. It emphasizes inference from outputs.

When combined, the three pillars let you detect problems, inspect details, and follow a request across services to find the root cause.

Logs
Narrative records of events, rich context, high volume.
Metrics
Numeric time-series, efficient for trends and alerts.
Traces
End-to-end request paths broken into spans.
