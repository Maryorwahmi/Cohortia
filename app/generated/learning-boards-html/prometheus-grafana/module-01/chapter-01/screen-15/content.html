Correlation ID
An opaque request ID that links logs, metrics, and traces for one transaction.
Structured Logs
Use JSON or similar formats to make parsing and querying reliable.
Metric Labels
Choose labels that enable operational slicing but avoid high cardinality.
Final takeaway
Instrumentation that balances detail, cost, and privacy is the foundation of reliable observability. Use the pillars together to detect, investigate, and confirm root causes efficiently.
