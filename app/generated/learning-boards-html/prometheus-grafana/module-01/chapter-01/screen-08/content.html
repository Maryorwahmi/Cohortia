How they fit together
A coordinated workflow moves you from alert to root cause.
Metrics
Efficient trends and alerts. Use labels for slicing and dashboards for impact.
Logs
Detailed events with messages and context. Use structured formats to enable filtering.
Traces
Request-level paths and spans for causal analysis and latency attribution.
Detect
Use metrics and alerts to signal when something needs attention.
Investigate
Use logs to find messages and stack traces tied to the alert window.
Confirm
Use traces to see the end-to-end impact and confirm the root cause.
