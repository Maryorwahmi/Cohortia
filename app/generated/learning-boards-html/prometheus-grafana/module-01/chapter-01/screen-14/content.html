Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Key Concepts
Observability
Infer internal state from external outputs to understand why systems fail.
Logs
Timestamped event records; use structured formats and correlation IDs for searchability.
Metrics & Traces
Metrics for alerts and trends; traces for span-level causal paths across services.
Bottom line
Design instrumentation so that metrics detect, logs explain, and traces map the user-facing transaction to its root cause.
