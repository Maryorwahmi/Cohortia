Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Scalability and High Availability
Design for scale
Sharding, replication, and multi-instance deployments reduce bottlenecks.
Sharding
Spread write load across multiple TSDB shards to accommodate high cardinality and volume.
Replication
Replicate critical time-series to ensure availability and faster failover for alerting.
Monitor the stack
Collect metrics about ingestion lag, disk usage, and evaluation durations to detect degradation early.
Operational checklist
  • Estimate peak write rate and label cardinality.
  • Plan retention tiers and downsampling for older data.
  • Run redundant alerting engines with failover paths.
  • Test monitoring recovery procedures periodically.
Key Takeaway

Scale the pipeline deliberately: plan for cardinality, protect alerting availability, and instrument the stack itself.
