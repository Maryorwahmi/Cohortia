Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Alerting and Notification
From rule to page
Evaluate rules continuously and deliver alerts to channels used by your responders.
Rule evaluation
Engine runs queries and checks conditions like sustained CPU or error-rate thresholds.
Notification systems
Integrations deliver alerts to Slack, email, or incident platforms to ensure visibility and escalation.
Design tip
Prefer alerts that indicate a clear remediation step and map to a single responsible team to reduce confusion.
Checklist
  • Require condition persistence to avoid flapping alerts.
  • Group related signals into a single incident when possible.
  • Document playbooks and runbooks for common alerts.
  • Monitor the alerting engine for health and delays.
Key Takeaway

Well-designed alerts reduce mean time to resolution by focusing attention on actionable, high-impact issues.
