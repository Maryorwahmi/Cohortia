Design connecting collectors, storage, processing, alerts, and dashboards.
Data Collection
Gathering metrics, logs, and traces from apps and infrastructure.
Agent / Exporter
Program running on targets that exposes metrics for scraping or pushing.
Summary: Keep these simple definitions in mind while mapping tools to roles, and use them to check that your architecture covers collection, storage, processing, alerting, and visualization.
