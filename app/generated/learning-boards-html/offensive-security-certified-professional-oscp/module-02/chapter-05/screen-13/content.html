The Common Thread: Information Leakage

Identifying Critical Data from Misconfigured Services

Why Information Leakage Matters

  • Directly leads to system compromise.
  • Provides intelligence for targeted attacks.
  • Often overlooked by administrators.
  • Forms the basis for exploitation strategies.
Key Takeaway

Every piece of leaked information, no matter how small, is a potential puzzle piece that can be correlated to reveal critical attack vectors. Document everything.

Key Information to Look For

Data points that reveal vulnerabilities

Usernames & Groups
System Information
Network Shares
Domain Information

Misconception Check

Don't assume non-web services are secure. Many 'background' protocols like SMB, SNMP, and LDAP are frequently overlooked but offer rich attack surfaces.
