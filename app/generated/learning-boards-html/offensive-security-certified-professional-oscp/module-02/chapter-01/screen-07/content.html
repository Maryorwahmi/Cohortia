Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)

Leveraging Publicly Available Information

Our primary focus in passive reconnaissance will be on Open Source Intelligence, commonly known as OSINT. OSINT refers to any information that is publicly available and legally accessible to anyone.

This is a vast domain, encompassing everything from a company's official website and its social media presence to publicly filed government records, news articles, press releases, and even job postings. The beauty of OSINT is that it allows you to gather significant intelligence without ever directly touching the target's systems, making it incredibly stealthy.

By analyzing these public sources, you can build a comprehensive profile of the target organization, understanding its business, its key personnel, its technological footprint, and potential areas of interest or vulnerability. Tools like standard web search engines, social media analysis tools, and specialized OSINT frameworks are invaluable for this process.

The key is to know where to look and how to connect the dots between seemingly disparate pieces of information to form a coherent picture of the target's landscape.
