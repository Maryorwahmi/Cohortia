Generic Wordlists

Broad lists of common names, often found in tools like dirb or gobuster default installations.

  • •**Pros:** Quick to use, covers basic common paths.
  • •**Cons:** Misses many hidden resources, less effective for specific technologies.

Targeted Wordlists

Curated lists specific to certain technologies (e.g., WordPress, Apache) or contexts (e.g., admin panels).

  • •**Pros:** High success rate, efficient for specific targets.
  • •**Cons:** Requires prior reconnaissance to identify target technology.

Wordlist Selection Factors

  • •**Target Technology:** Match wordlist to identified CMS or web server.
  • •**Context:** Are you looking for admin panels, backup files, or specific scripts?
  • •**Size vs. Time:** Balance thoroughness with scan duration and risk of detection.
Common Mistakes

Using a tiny, irrelevant wordlist or forgetting to include common file extensions (e.g., .php, .bak).

Kali Wordlist Paths

Explore /usr/share/wordlists/ for a variety of pre-installed wordlists, including specialized ones.

Key Takeaway

The strategic selection of a wordlist, tailored to the target's technology and context, is paramount for effective and efficient directory brute-forcing.
