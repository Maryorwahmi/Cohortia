Specialized Search

Shodan.io: The IoT Search Engine

Indexing internet-connected devices and services, not just websites

  • •Indexes devices and services, not just web pages.
  • •Scans for open ports, banners, and metadata across the internet.
  • •Identifies exposed services, misconfigured devices, and vulnerabilities.
  • •Offers a unique perspective on an organization's network perimeter.
Analogy

If Google is a library for books, Shodan is a blueprint for all the machines and infrastructure connected to the internet.

Key Takeaway

Shodan provides a critical view of internet-facing infrastructure, revealing potential attack surfaces that traditional web searches miss.

Device Discovery

Find specific types of devices like webcams, routers, or industrial control systems.

Service Identification

Locate specific services (e.g., Apache, Nginx, FTP) and their versions.

Vulnerability Exposure

Identify devices running outdated software or known vulnerabilities.
