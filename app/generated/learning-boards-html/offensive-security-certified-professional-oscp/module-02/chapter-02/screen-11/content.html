Data Breach Sites: Compromised Credentials

Checking for exposed email addresses and informing attack strategies

  • •Check if target email addresses appear in known data breaches.
  • •Inform social engineering efforts and prioritize password guessing.
  • •Highlights the importance of strong password hygiene for employees.
  • •Provides insight into the target's security posture.

Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) is a widely used service to check for email address exposure in data breaches.

Key Takeaway

Data breach sites offer crucial intelligence on compromised credentials, guiding attack strategies and revealing security weaknesses.
