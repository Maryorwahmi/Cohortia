Google Dorking: Uncovering Hidden Paths

Crafting combined queries for sensitive data discovery

Example Scenario: Finding Sensitive PDFs

Imagine you're targeting 'example.com'. You want to find any PDF documents that might contain the words 'confidential' or 'internal'.

Pro Tip

Experiment with different combinations of operators and keywords. The most effective dorks are often custom-tailored to the specific target and information sought.

Key Takeaway

The true power of Google Dorking lies in combining operators to create highly specific queries that reveal otherwise hidden information.

# Advanced Google Dorking Examples
site:target.com filetype:pdf "confidential" OR "internal"
# Finds sensitive PDFs on a domain
site:target.com inurl:admin | inurl:login
# Locates potential administrative login pages
site:target.com intitle:"index of"
# Discovers directory listings that may expose files
site:target.com intext:"password"
# Identifies pages containing the word 'password'
