Correlating OSINT: Building a Target Profile

Connecting disparate pieces of information for actionable intelligence

  • •Combine seemingly insignificant data points for critical insights.
  • •Move from data collection to active intelligence synthesis.
  • •Meticulously document findings and cross-reference connections.
  • •Transform raw data into actionable intelligence for penetration testing.
Analogy

Think of it like solving a puzzle: each OSINT finding is a piece, and correlation is how you fit them together to reveal the full picture.

Key Takeaway

Correlation is the ultimate skill in OSINT, turning isolated facts into a powerful, comprehensive target profile for strategic advantage.
