Key Concepts

Essential takeaways from this chapter

Social Engineering Reconnaissance

Gathering information from social media to understand individuals and organizational structures.

Data Breach Sites

Websites that aggregate information about known data breaches for email exposure checks.

Correlation

Connecting disparate pieces of information from various sources to build a comprehensive target profile.

Summary Takeaway

By combining human intelligence, breach data, and the art of correlation, you can build a truly actionable and comprehensive target profile.
