Social Media OSINT: Profiling Personnel

Leveraging public profiles for employee information and connections

  • •LinkedIn reveals professional details: roles, skills, projects.
  • •Twitter/Facebook expose personal interests, travel, and connections.
  • •Trivial details can form comprehensive personnel profiles.
  • •Information can be leveraged for social engineering efforts.
Ethical Boundary

Gathering public information is acceptable, but interacting under false pretenses or harassment is unethical and illegal.

Key Takeaway

Social media is a rich source for profiling, but always respect privacy and ethical guidelines to avoid legal repercussions.

LinkedIn Insights

Discover employee roles, skills, and organizational hierarchy.

Personal Details

Uncover interests, travel plans, or connections from public profiles.

Social Engineering Prep

Use gathered information to craft believable phishing or pretexting scenarios.
