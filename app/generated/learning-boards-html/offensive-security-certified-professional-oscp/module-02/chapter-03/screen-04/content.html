The Shift to Active

Active information gathering involves direct interaction with target systems to collect real-time, precise data. This contrasts with passive methods that rely on publicly available information without direct engagement.

Benefits of Active Recon
  • Confirm host existence and reachability.
  • Identify open ports and listening services.
  • Determine operating systems and versions.
  • Gather current and precise network data.
Inherent Risks
  • Detection by firewalls or IDS.
  • Activities can be logged by target systems.
  • Potential legal repercussions without authorization.
  • Increased footprint on the target network.
