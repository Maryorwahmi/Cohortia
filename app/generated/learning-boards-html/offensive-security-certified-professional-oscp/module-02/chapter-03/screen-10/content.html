What is Port Scanning?

The process of determining which ports on a network host are open, closed, or filtered, indicating active services.

TCP SYN Scan (-sS)
  • Default and most popular Nmap scan type.
  • Often called a "half-open" scan.
  • Sends SYN, receives SYN/ACK, then sends RST (resets connection).
  • Faster and stealthier than a full connect scan.
Why it's Stealthy

The connection is not fully established, meaning the target's operating system often won't log the attempt, making detection less likely.

Command: nmap -sS 192.168.1.100

Key Takeaway

TCP SYN scan (-sS) is a fast, stealthy "half-open" scan that identifies open ports without completing the TCP handshake.
