Key Concepts

Understanding Nmap Scan Types

Port Scanning

The process of determining which ports on a network host are open, closed, or filtered.

TCP SYN Scan (-sS)

A "half-open" port scan that sends SYN and immediately RSTs upon SYN/ACK, making it stealthier.

TCP Connect Scan (-sT)

A full-connect port scan that completes the TCP three-way handshake, less stealthy but reliable.

Summary Takeaway

Choosing between TCP SYN and TCP Connect scans involves a trade-off between stealth and reliability, both crucial for effective port enumeration.
