Two Powerful Credential Attacks

Understanding Kerberoasting and AS-REPRoasting in Active Directory.

Core Concepts
  • •Both attacks exploit specific aspects of the Kerberos authentication protocol.
  • •They aim to obtain password hashes that can be cracked offline.
  • •Often do not require administrative privileges, making them ideal for privilege escalation.
  • •Relatively stealthy as they involve legitimate Kerberos requests.
Key Distinction

While both harvest credentials, they target different vulnerabilities and yield different types of hashes.

Kerberoasting Overview

Targets Service Principal Names (SPNs) associated with user accounts. Obtains Kerberos service tickets (TGS-REP) encrypted with the service account's NTLM hash.

Targets: Service Accounts
AS-REPRoasting Overview

Targets user accounts with the 'Do not require Kerberos preauthentication' flag enabled. Obtains Ticket Granting Tickets (TGTs) directly, encrypted with the user's NTLM hash.

Targets: User Accounts (Preauth Disabled)
Key Takeaway

Both attacks are valuable for privilege escalation, but understanding their distinct mechanisms is crucial for successful execution and defense.
