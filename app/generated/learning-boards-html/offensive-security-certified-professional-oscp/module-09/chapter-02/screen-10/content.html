STRATEGIC APPROACH

A Systematic Approach to AD Enumeration (Part 1)

From Broad Scans to Targeted User and Group Discovery

01

Identify Domain Controllers

Locate the core servers managing the AD environment using Nmap or DNS queries.

02

Enumerate Users & Groups

Discover accounts, naming conventions, disabled accounts, and privileged groups.

03

Enumerate Computers

Identify potential targets for lateral movement or specific services running on machines.

Key Takeaway

A systematic approach, starting with identifying Domain Controllers and then enumerating users, groups, and computers, forms the essential foundation for comprehensive AD reconnaissance.
