Common Mistakes in AD Enumeration

Pitfalls to Avoid for Effective Reconnaissance

Stopping Too Early

Prematurely ending enumeration can lead to missed vulnerabilities and attack paths.

Being Too Noisy

Aggressive scanning can trigger alerts and reveal your presence to defenders.

Lack of Authorization

Never perform enumeration on systems without explicit, written permission.

BEST PRACTICES

Best Practices for AD Enumeration

Maximizing Effectiveness While Minimizing Risk

Be Systematic & Thorough

Follow a structured approach from broad scans to targeted queries to ensure comprehensive data collection.

Understand the Output

Don't just run tools; interpret the results and correlate information to build a mental model of the AD.

Practice in Labs

Hone your skills in controlled lab environments to avoid unintended consequences in real engagements.

Key Takeaway

Effective AD enumeration balances thoroughness with discretion, requiring a deep understanding of the environment and strict adherence to ethical guidelines and authorization.
