Key Concepts to Remember
Foundational Terms in AD Reconnaissance

Reconnaissance

The initial phase of gathering information about a target, often passively, to understand its landscape.

Enumeration

The active process of extracting detailed information about a target, such as users, groups, shares, and services.

RID Cycling

A technique used to guess valid user accounts by iterating through Relative Identifiers (RIDs) in Windows.

Summary Takeaway

Reconnaissance and enumeration are distinct but complementary phases, with techniques like RID Cycling providing specific methods for intelligence gathering.
