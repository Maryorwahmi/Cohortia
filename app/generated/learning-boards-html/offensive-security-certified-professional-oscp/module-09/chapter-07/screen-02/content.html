Understand DC Attacks

Explain Unconstrained Delegation

Explain the concepts of unconstrained delegation and its exploitation for privilege escalation. This involves understanding how a service can capture and reuse a client's TGT.

Execute DCSync attacks using Mimikatz or Impacket/secretsdump.py to extract NTLM hashes from a Domain Controller. This practical skill is key for credential harvesting.
