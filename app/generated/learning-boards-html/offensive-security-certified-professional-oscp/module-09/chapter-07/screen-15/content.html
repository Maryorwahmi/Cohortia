Key Concepts: Supporting Elements

Essential takeaways from Domain Controller and Trust Exploitation.

`krbtgt` hash

The NTLM hash of the Kerberos Ticket Granting Ticket service account, critical for forging Golden Tickets.

`DS-Replication-Get Changes All`

A permission required for performing DCSync attacks, typically held by `Domain Admins`.

Domain Trust

A relationship between two AD domains allowing users in one to access resources in the other.

Summary Takeaway

These foundational elements are crucial for understanding the mechanisms behind advanced Active Directory attacks and for developing effective defensive strategies.
