Key Concepts: GPO Fundamentals

Essential terms to remember

Group Policy Object (GPO)

A collection of settings that control the working environment of user and computer accounts within an Active Directory domain.

GPO Link

The association of a GPO with a site, domain, or Organizational Unit (OU), determining where its settings are applied.

Enforced GPO

A GPO that has precedence over all other GPOs linked at a lower level in the AD hierarchy, preventing them from being overridden.

Restricted Groups

A GPO setting that allows administrators to specify which users or groups should be members of local groups on target machines; a common abuse vector.

Summary Takeaway

Understanding GPO definitions, linking, enforcement, and specific settings like 'Restricted Groups' is foundational for identifying and exploiting misconfigurations.
