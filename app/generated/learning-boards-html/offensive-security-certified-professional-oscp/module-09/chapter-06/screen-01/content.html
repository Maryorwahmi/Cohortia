Unmasking GPO Abuse

Exploiting Group Policy Objects for Privilege Escalation

Group Policy Objects are the backbone of Active Directory management, but they are also a prime target for attackers. This chapter will guide you through understanding GPO mechanics, identifying misconfigurations, and leveraging them for privilege escalation and persistence in enterprise environments.

Key Insight

GPOs offer centralized control, but their complexity and hierarchical application often lead to misconfigurations that attackers can exploit for significant gains.
