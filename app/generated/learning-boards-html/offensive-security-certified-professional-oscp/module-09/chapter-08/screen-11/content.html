Core Defense Evasion Strategies

Techniques attackers use to bypass security tools and remain undetected.

Strategy 1: Blend In

Use naming conventions similar to legitimate accounts or objects to avoid standing out in security logs and audits.

Strategy 2: Time-Based Attacks

Execute malicious actions during off-hours or periods of low vigilance to reduce the likelihood of immediate detection.

Strategy 3: Living off the Land (LotL)

Utilize legitimate system tools (e.g., PowerShell, wmic, schtasks) instead of custom malware, making activities harder to distinguish from normal operations.

Other Evasion Tactics
  • Obfuscation: Obfuscate scripts or executables to bypass antivirus.
  • Memory-only execution: Avoid writing malicious files to disk.
  • Stealthy network traffic: Use encrypted channels or legitimate protocols.
  • Clear logs: Remove relevant event logs (though this can be detected).
Key Takeaway

Effective defense evasion combines blending in, strategic timing, and leveraging native system tools to operate covertly within a compromised environment.
