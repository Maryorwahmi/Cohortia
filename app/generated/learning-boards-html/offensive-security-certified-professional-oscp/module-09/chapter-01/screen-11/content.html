Penetration Testing Focus

Identifying Weaknesses in Active Directory

What penetration testers look for to compromise an AD environment.

Misconfigured OUs & GPOs

Look for excessive permissions or weak policy settings that can be exploited.

Insecure Trust Relationships

Identify weak trusts between domains or forests for lateral movement opportunities.

Service Vulnerabilities

Exploit weaknesses in DNS, LDAP, or Kerberos for access or privilege escalation.

Key Takeaway

A systematic approach to identifying AD misconfigurations and service vulnerabilities is key to successful penetration testing.

Common AD Weaknesses
Weak GPOs

Policies granting excessive rights.

Kerberoasting

Cracking service account hashes.

Weak User Passwords

Easy targets for brute-force.

LDAP Enum. Exposure

Sensitive data via anonymous queries.
