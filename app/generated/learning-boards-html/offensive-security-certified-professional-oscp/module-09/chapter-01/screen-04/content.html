Active Directory: The Network's Central Hub

Understanding its fundamental role in modern corporate environments.

Centralized Database

AD is a hierarchical database storing information about network objects like users, groups, and computers.

Unified Management

Allows administrators to manage permissions and access to network resources from a single, central location.

Critical for Pen Testers

Compromising AD often means compromising the entire organization, making it a primary target.
