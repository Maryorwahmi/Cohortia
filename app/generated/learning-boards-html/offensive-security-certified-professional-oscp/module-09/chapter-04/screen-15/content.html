Key Concepts: Advanced Ticket Attacks

A quick recap of the essential ideas from this chapter.

Golden Ticket

A forged Kerberos TGT that grants an attacker indefinite access to any service in the domain as any user, requiring the `krbtgt` account's NTLM hash.

Silver Ticket

A forged Kerberos ST that grants an attacker access to a specific service on a specific server, requiring the target service account's NTLM hash.

`krbtgt` Account

The special service account in Active Directory responsible for encrypting and signing all Kerberos tickets. Its NTLM hash is critical for Golden Ticket attacks.

Summary Takeaway

Golden Tickets offer domain-wide compromise via the `krbtgt` hash, while Silver Tickets provide targeted service access using specific service account hashes, both bypassing traditional Kerberos authentication.
