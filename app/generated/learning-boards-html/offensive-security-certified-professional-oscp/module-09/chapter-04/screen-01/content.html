Pass-the-Hash & Pass-the-Ticket Attacks

Master advanced post-exploitation techniques to leverage stolen credentials for lateral movement and privilege escalation in Active Directory environments.

Key Idea

These attacks exploit authentication protocols to bypass plaintext password requirements, enabling attackers to impersonate users with just their NTLM hashes or Kerberos tickets.

In this lesson, you will learn to exploit NTLM hashes and Kerberos tickets.
