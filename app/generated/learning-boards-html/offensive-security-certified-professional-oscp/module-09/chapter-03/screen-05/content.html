Password Spraying Fundamentals

Trying one password against many accounts to bypass lockouts

How Password Spraying Works

  • •Single Password, Many Accounts: Instead of many passwords for one user, it's one password for many users.
  • •Bypasses Lockout Policies: Distributes failed attempts across multiple accounts, avoiding individual account lockouts.
  • •Leverages Weak Passwords: Exploits the common practice of users choosing simple or default passwords.
  • •High Success Rate: Often effective in large environments where a small percentage of users have weak credentials.
Key Takeaway

Password spraying is an efficient, low-risk method to identify weak credentials across an organization by distributing login attempts.

Password Spraying vs. Brute Force

Password Spraying

One password against many users. Low risk of account lockout. Effective for common passwords.

Brute Force

Many passwords against one user. High risk of account lockout. Effective for complex passwords (if no lockout).

Common Misconception

Many believe brute-forcing is always the first step. However, password spraying is often more stealthy and effective against modern lockout policies.
