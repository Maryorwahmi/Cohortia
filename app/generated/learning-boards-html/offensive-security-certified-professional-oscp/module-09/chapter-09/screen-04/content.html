Core Concept

Active Directory Certificate Services (AD CS) Abuse

Exploiting PKI Misconfigurations for Privilege Escalation

What is AD CS?

AD CS is Microsoft's Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) implementation, enabling organizations to issue and manage digital certificates for secure authentication (e.g., smart cards, VPNs).

Why is it Vulnerable?

Its inherent complexity often leads to misconfigurations in certificate templates, permissions, and enrollment policies, creating attack surfaces.

Impact of Abuse

Successful exploitation can lead to privilege escalation, often resulting in Domain Admin compromise, allowing attackers to impersonate any user.

AD CS: Essential but Risky

While critical for secure authentication and digital trust, AD CS's complexity makes it a prime target for misconfiguration-based attacks.

Key Takeaway

AD CS misconfigurations are a common and highly effective vector for privilege escalation in Active Directory environments.
