Remediation Strategy

AD CS Hardening & Endpoint Security

Auditing PKI and Enhancing System-Level Defenses

AD CS Hardening

Audit certificate templates for misconfigurations (ESC1, ESC8, 'Supply in request'). Secure template ACLs and restrict sensitive certificate enrollment.

Endpoint Security & Logging

Deploy EDR solutions. Enable comprehensive logging (PowerShell script block, process creation) and forward logs to a SIEM for centralized analysis.

Key Takeaway

Securing AD CS at the template level and implementing robust endpoint monitoring are critical for detecting and preventing advanced attacks.
