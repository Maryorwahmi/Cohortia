Remediation Strategy

Principle of Least Privilege & Secure Service Accounts

Minimizing Attack Surface and Automating Credential Management

Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP)

Grant users and service accounts only the minimum necessary permissions to perform their functions. Regularly audit group memberships and ACLs.

Secure Service Accounts

Utilize Managed Service Accounts (MSAs) or Group Managed Service Accounts (gMSAs) for automated password management. For traditional accounts, enforce strong, unique, and rotated passwords.

Key Takeaway

Strict adherence to PoLP and robust service account management are critical for reducing the attack surface and preventing credential abuse.

Key Areas of Focus

Audit Group Memberships

Regularly review and remove unnecessary accounts from privileged groups.

Review ACLs

Ensure Access Control Lists are tightly controlled and follow PoLP.

Implement MSAs/gMSAs

Automate password management and SPN registration for service accounts.

Rotate Passwords

Regularly change strong, unique passwords for traditional service accounts.
