Cross-Forest Attacks

Pivoting Between Trusted Active Directory Forests

Leveraging Trust Relationships

Attackers exploit trust keys, SID filtering bypasses, or specific trust configurations to move from a compromised forest to a trusted one.

Golden Ticket Implications

A forged Golden Ticket in one forest can potentially grant access to resources in a trusted, two-way transitive forest.

Key Takeaway

Cross-forest attacks highlight the critical need to secure trust relationships and understand Kerberos across enterprise boundaries.
