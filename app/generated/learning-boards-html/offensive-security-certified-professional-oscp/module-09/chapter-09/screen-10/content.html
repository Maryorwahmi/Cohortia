Remediation Strategy

Tiered Administration & Strong Password Policies

Segregating Privileges and Enforcing Credential Hygiene

Tiered Administration Model

Segregates administrative accounts into different security tiers (e.g., ESAE/Red Forest) to prevent high-privilege accounts from logging into low-security workstations.

Strong Password Policies & MFA

Enforce complex, unique passwords for all accounts and implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all privileged accounts and critical systems.

Key Takeaway

Limiting credential exposure and enforcing strong authentication are fundamental to preventing lateral movement and privilege escalation.

Secure vs. Insecure Practices

Secure Practice

Domain Admins only log into hardened DCs.

Insecure Practice

Domain Admins log into user workstations.

Secure Practice

MFA for all privileged accounts.

Insecure Practice

Weak or reused passwords for service accounts.
