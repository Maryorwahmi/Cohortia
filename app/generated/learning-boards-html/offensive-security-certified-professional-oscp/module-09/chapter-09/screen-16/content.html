Key Concepts

Critical defense strategies and tools

Cross-Forest Attack

An attack leveraging trust relationships between different AD forests to gain access or escalate privileges.

Tiered Administration

A security model segregating administrative accounts and resources into different tiers to limit compromise impact.

Managed Service Accounts (MSAs/gMSAs)

Special AD service accounts that automate password management and SPN registration, enhancing security.

Summary Takeaway

Effective AD defense relies on understanding cross-forest threats, implementing tiered administration, securing service accounts, and robust logging.
