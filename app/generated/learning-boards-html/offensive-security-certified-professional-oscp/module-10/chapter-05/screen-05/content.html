Fileless vs. Traditional Security
Traditional AV

Scans files on disk for known signatures. Relies on persistent artifacts.

Effective against known disk-based threats.
Fileless Malware

Operates in RAM, no disk footprint. Leverages trusted processes.

Bypasses traditional AV, challenges EDR.
Evasion Advantage

Why Fileless Attacks Are Potent

Bypassing traditional security controls designed for disk-based threats.

Challenges for Defenders
  • No file for signature-based AV to scan.
  • Difficult for EDR to baseline and quarantine.
  • Malicious activity blends with legitimate processes.
  • Minimal forensic artifacts complicate investigation.
Key Takeaway

Fileless attacks leverage memory-only operations to evade detection, making them a significant challenge for traditional and even some advanced security solutions.
