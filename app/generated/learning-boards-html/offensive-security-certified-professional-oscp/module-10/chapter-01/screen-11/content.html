Intrusion Detection System (IDS)

  • Passive Monitoring: Observes network traffic without interfering.
  • Alert Generation: Notifies administrators of suspicious activity or known attack patterns.
  • Analogy: A security camera for your network.

Intrusion Prevention System (IPS)

  • Active Defense: Detects and actively blocks or prevents threats in real-time.
  • Actions: Drops malicious packets, resets connections, blocks source IPs.
  • Analogy: A security guard who can intervene and stop threats.
Key Takeaway

IDS alerts on threats, while IPS actively prevents them, both using signature and anomaly detection.

Detection Methods

How IDS/IPS identify malicious activity.

Signature Matching
  • Known attack patterns
  • Specific byte sequences
  • Reactive to known threats
Anomaly Detection
  • Unusual behavior
  • Deviations from baseline
  • Proactive against unknown threats

Attackers use fragmentation, encryption, or custom protocols to bypass these systems.
