Chapter 10.1

Understanding Common Defenses

Essential Knowledge for Penetration Testers

As penetration testers, our goal is to simulate real-world attacks. This means encountering and overcoming various defensive mechanisms. Before we can bypass them, we must understand how they work, what they protect, and their inherent limitations. This foundational knowledge is crucial for developing sophisticated evasion strategies.

Key Takeaway

To bypass defenses, you must first understand their mechanisms and limitations.
