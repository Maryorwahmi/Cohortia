The Attacker's Mindset

To effectively simulate real-world attacks, penetration testers must anticipate and overcome defensive mechanisms. This requires a deep understanding of how these defenses operate.

Crucial Insights

  • Operational Principles: How each defense fundamentally works.
  • Protection Goals: What assets or activities each defense aims to secure.
  • Inherent Limitations: The specific weaknesses or blind spots of each technology.
Key Takeaway

Understanding defenses reveals their weaknesses, enabling effective bypass strategies.

The Defensive Landscape

Various layers of security protect modern systems and networks.

Endpoint Protection (AV/EDR)
Network Security (Firewalls/IDS/IPS)
Application Control (AWL)
Vulnerability Management

Each layer presents unique challenges and opportunities for an attacker.
