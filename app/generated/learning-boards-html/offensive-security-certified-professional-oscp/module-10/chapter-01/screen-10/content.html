Attacker's Approach to Firewalls

Our primary goal is to identify and exploit weaknesses in firewall configurations to gain unauthorized access or establish covert communication channels.

Common Bypass Strategies

  • Find Open Ports: Identify services explicitly allowed by firewall rules.
  • Exploit Misconfigurations: Leverage overly permissive or incorrectly applied rules.
  • Tunnel Over Allowed Protocols: Encapsulate malicious traffic within legitimate protocols.
  • IP Fragmentation: Split packets to evade simple inspection rules.
Key Takeaway

Firewall evasion relies on reconnaissance, exploiting configuration flaws, and disguising malicious traffic as legitimate.

Tunneling Example: DNS C2

Using DNS queries to establish a covert command and control channel.

1. Firewall allows DNS (Port 53)
2. Attacker uses `dnscat2`
