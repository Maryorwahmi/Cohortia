Key Concepts: Advanced Evasion & Tools

Techniques and utilities for bypassing AV

Polymorphism

Malware's ability to change its internal structure and appearance with each execution or infection, while preserving its original functionality.

Metamorphism

An advanced evasion technique where malware completely rewrites its own code, changing instructions, registers, and control flow.

msfvenom

A command-line utility within the Metasploit Framework used to generate and encode various types of payloads.

Chapter Summary

Polymorphism and metamorphism represent advanced forms of dynamic evasion, while msfvenom serves as a practical tool for generating and testing encoded payloads.
