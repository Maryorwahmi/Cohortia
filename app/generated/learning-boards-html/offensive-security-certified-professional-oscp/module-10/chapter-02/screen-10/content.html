Custom Encoding & Encryption for Evasion

Moving beyond known msfvenom encoders

Why Custom is Better

Instead of relying on well-known encoders, custom encryption schemes offer a higher chance of bypassing modern AV. This involves unique algorithms and stealthy loaders.

  • •Unique Signatures: Your custom code won't match known AV patterns.
  • •Stealthy Loaders: Custom C/C++ loaders decrypt and execute payloads in memory.
  • •Advanced Techniques: Often combined with process injection or reflective DLL loading.
Key Takeaway

Custom encoding and encryption, coupled with in-memory execution, provide a more robust and stealthy approach to bypass modern signature-based AV.
