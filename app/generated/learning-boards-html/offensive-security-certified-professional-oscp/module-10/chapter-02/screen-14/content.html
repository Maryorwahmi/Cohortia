Key Concepts: Detection & Obfuscation

Core ideas for signature-based AV evasion

Signature-based Detection

Antivirus method that identifies malware by matching known malicious patterns or byte sequences from a database.

Encoding

Transforming data into a different format (e.g., Base64, XOR) to obscure its original content, often used to bypass AV signatures.

Decoder Stub

A small piece of code embedded with an encoded payload, responsible for decoding the payload back to its original form at runtime.

Chapter Summary

Signature-based AV is reactive, making encoding and the use of a decoder stub fundamental techniques to bypass static detection by altering payload appearance.
