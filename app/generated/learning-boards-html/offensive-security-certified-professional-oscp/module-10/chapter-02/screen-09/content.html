Metamorphism: Complete Code Rewriting

The ultimate form of structural evasion

What is Metamorphism?

Metamorphism is an advanced technique where malware completely rewrites its own code, changing instructions, registers, and control flow, while preserving the original logic. It's like rewriting an entire essay with different words and structures.

  • •Code Transformation: Involves a 'metamorphic engine' that disassembles, analyzes, and reassembles code.
  • •Structural Uniqueness: Creates functionally identical but structurally unique variants.
  • •High Complexity: Extremely difficult to develop and typically found in sophisticated malware.
Key Takeaway

Metamorphic malware completely rewrites its code, making it structurally unique with each iteration, posing the highest challenge to signature-based AV.
