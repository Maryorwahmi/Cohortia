Evasion Mistakes: Decoder Logic & Artifacts

Ensuring functional payloads and clean execution

Critical Considerations

Beyond initial bypass, ensure your payload functions correctly and leaves no traces.

Mistake 3: Forgetting the decoder

If you encode or encrypt, a corresponding decoder must run before execution. This decoder itself needs to be stealthy to avoid detection.

Mistake 4: Leaving artifacts

Ensure your loader doesn't leave suspicious files on disk or create obvious registry entries that could be flagged by EDR or forensic analysis.

Key Takeaway

A functional, stealthy decoder is essential for encoded payloads, and avoiding suspicious artifacts ensures long-term evasion and operational security.

Operational Security
Pro-Tip: Stealthy Decoders

Design your decoder stub to be as small and generic as possible. Avoid unique strings or patterns that AV could easily signature. Consider using system calls directly to reduce imported library functions.

Real-World Connection

Advanced persistent threats (APTs) often employ custom loaders and in-memory execution to minimize disk artifacts, making their operations harder to detect and attribute.
