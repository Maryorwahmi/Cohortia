Core Concept

The Art of Stealth

Minimizing Traces and Blending In

What is Stealth?

Stealth is the practice of minimizing detectable traces and blending malicious activity with legitimate system operations to avoid detection.

  • •Minimize forensic artifacts like unusual file timestamps or logs.
  • •Blend in with legitimate system activity, processes, and network traffic.
  • •Evade detection by security tools, analysts, and automated monitoring.
Key Takeaway

Effective stealth allows for prolonged access and deeper system compromise without triggering alarms, crucial for advanced penetration testing.
