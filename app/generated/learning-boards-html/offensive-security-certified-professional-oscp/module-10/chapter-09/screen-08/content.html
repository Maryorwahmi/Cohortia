Establishing Persistence

Ensuring Continuous Access to Compromised Systems

What is Persistence?

Persistence is the ability to maintain access to a compromised system across reboots, user logoffs, or other system events.

  • •Guarantees continued access even after system disruptions.
  • •Crucial for long-term objectives and simulating real-world attacks.
  • •Involves embedding malicious code into legitimate autostart mechanisms.
Key Takeaway

Persistence is vital for converting a temporary compromise into a lasting foothold, allowing for sustained operations and data exfiltration.

Why it Matters

Without persistence, a system reboot could wipe out your access, forcing you to re-exploit the target. Persistence ensures your foothold remains.
