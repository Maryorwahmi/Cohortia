Systemd Services & Startup Scripts

Modern and Traditional Linux Autostart

# Example /etc/systemd/system/myservice.service content
[Unit]
Description=My Legitimate Service
After=network.target

[Service]
ExecStart=/usr/bin/python3 /tmp/.hidden_backdoor.py
Restart=always

[Install]
WantedBy=multi-user.target

# Enable and start the service
systemctl enable myservice.service
systemctl start myservice.service

# Add your command before 'exit 0'
# /usr/bin/python3 /tmp/.hidden_backdoor.py &
  • •**Systemd Services:** Modern, robust, and flexible for boot-time execution.
  • •**Startup Scripts:** Traditional methods like /etc/rc.local or /etc/init.d/.
Modern vs. Traditional

Systemd is preferred on most modern Linux systems, offering better control and logging. Traditional scripts are useful for older systems or specific scenarios.

Key Takeaway

Systemd services provide a powerful and resilient way to achieve Linux persistence, while traditional startup scripts offer compatibility with older environments.
