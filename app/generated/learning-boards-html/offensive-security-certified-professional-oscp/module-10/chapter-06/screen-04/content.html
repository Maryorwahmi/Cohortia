Core Concept

Network Defense Mechanisms

Network firewalls and IDS/IPS are critical security components. Firewalls control traffic based on rules, while IDS/IPS monitor for malicious activity.

  • •Firewalls: Act as gatekeepers, enforcing rules on IP addresses, ports, and protocols.
  • •IDS/IPS: Actively monitor traffic for known attack signatures or anomalous behavior.
  • •Egress Filtering: A common firewall configuration blocking most outbound connections, allowing only specific protocols (e.g., HTTP/HTTPS, DNS).
Key Takeaway

Understanding how firewalls and IDS/IPS operate, especially egress filtering, is the first step in developing effective bypass strategies.

Analogy Card
