Custom Protocols & Encryption

Using custom algorithms or non-standard protocols for C2 traffic, especially when encrypted, makes it appear as unintelligible data to signature-based IDS/IPS, preventing detection.

  • •Custom Protocols: Design a unique communication method that doesn't conform to known standards.
  • •Encryption: Encrypt the payload with a custom algorithm or key, rendering it unreadable to IDS/IPS.
  • •Requirement: Requires custom client and server components to handle the unique protocol and encryption.
Key Takeaway

Custom protocols and strong encryption are highly effective against signature-based IDS/IPS, as they transform malicious traffic into unrecognizable data, forcing systems to either block all unknown traffic or allow it.

Pro Tip

When designing custom protocols, avoid common patterns or headers that could still be fingerprinted by advanced behavioral analysis systems. Randomness is key.
