Advanced Port Forwarding

Socat: The Swiss Army Knife for Network Relays

socat is a powerful command-line utility for establishing bidirectional byte streams and transferring data between them. It's highly versatile for complex port forwarding, relaying, and creating various network connections.

# Listen on local 8080, forward to 192.168.1.10:80
socat TCP-LISTEN:8080,fork TCP:192.168.1.10:80

Critical Safety Notes

  • •Layered Defenses: Bypassing one firewall doesn't mean all defenses are down. Assume layered security.
  • •Stealthy C2: Avoid loud, default C2 profiles. Customize user-agents and beaconing frequency.
  • •Encrypt Tunnels: Always encrypt sensitive C2 traffic, even within an allowed tunnel, to prevent DPI.
  • •Legal Implications: Always operate strictly within the scope of your authorized engagement.
Key Takeaway

socat offers unparalleled flexibility for network manipulation, but its power necessitates a strong understanding of network segmentation, stealth, encryption, and legal boundaries to ensure responsible and effective penetration testing.
