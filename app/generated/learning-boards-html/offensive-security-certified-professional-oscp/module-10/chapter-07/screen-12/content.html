Discovering New LOLBAS & Safety Notes

Expanding Your Toolkit Responsibly

Strategies for Discovery

  • Leverage the LOLBAS Project (lolbas-project.github.io) as your primary resource.
  • Analyze System Binaries in `C:\Windows\System32` and `C:\Windows\SysWOW64` for legitimate functions.
  • Monitor Process Execution with tools like Sysmon in a lab to spot unusual command-line arguments.
  • Test with AppLocker in a lab environment to experiment with what's allowed/blocked.
Pro Tip

Focus on binaries that can interact with files, network, or execute other processes.

Common Mistakes & Safety Notes

  • AWL policies vary: What works against one might not work against another.
  • Clean up artifacts: Malicious files fed to LOLBAS binaries remain on disk.
  • Command-line logging: EDRs often log arguments, even for whitelisted binaries.
  • Authorization is key: Never test on production systems without explicit, written permission.
  • Don't overlook scripts: PowerShell, JScript, VBScript can also be LOLBAS candidates.
Warning

Misuse can lead to system instability or security breaches. Always act ethically.

Key Takeaway

Mastering AWL bypass requires continuous research, responsible testing, and an understanding that effectiveness varies based on specific policy implementations and EDR capabilities.
