Base64: Binary to Text Transformation

Embedding Shellcode in Text-Based Formats

Why Use Base64?

  • Text Compatibility: Makes binary data safe for text-only environments.
  • Bypass Filters: Evades simple content filters looking for raw binary.
  • Stealthy Delivery: Embeds payloads in scripts or web requests.
Pro-Tip

Combine Base64 with other obfuscation techniques for a multi-layered evasion strategy, as Base64 alone is easily decoded.

Key Takeaway

Base64 encoding is a simple yet effective method to transform binary shellcode into a text string, making it suitable for embedding in various text-based formats and bypassing basic content filters.
