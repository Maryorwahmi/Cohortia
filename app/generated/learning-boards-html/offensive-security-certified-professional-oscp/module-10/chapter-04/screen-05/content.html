Encoding Fundamentals

Beyond Standard Encoders

Mastering Base64 and XOR for Payload Obscurity

Why Manual Encoding?

  • Evade Signatures: Standard encoders are often fingerprinted by AV/EDR.
  • Greater Control: Custom implementations offer unique outputs.
  • Adaptability: Tailor encoding to specific target environments and filters.

Key Encoding Schemes

Base64 Encoding

Converts binary data to an ASCII string. Ideal for embedding binary payloads in text-based formats (e.g., PowerShell, web requests) or bypassing simple content filters.

XOR Encoding

A simple bitwise operation where each byte is XORed with a key. Effective for short shellcode snippets, especially when combined with a custom C/C++ loader.

Key Takeaway

Understanding and implementing manual encoding techniques like Base64 and XOR is crucial for creating unique, evasive payloads that bypass common signature-based detections.
