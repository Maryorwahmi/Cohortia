Droppers and Layered Evasion Strategies

Combining obfuscation with custom delivery for maximum stealth.

Custom Loader

  • Executes obfuscated/encrypted payload directly in memory.
  • Avoids writing malicious content to disk.
  • Offers high stealth against disk-based scans.

Dropper (Fileless Preferred)

  • Installs other malicious programs, sometimes to disk.
  • Fileless droppers execute directly in memory.
  • Can be part of a multi-stage attack chain.
Key Takeaway

A layered approach, combining obfuscated payloads with custom loaders or fileless droppers, significantly enhances evasion by forcing security products into deeper, more resource-intensive analysis.
