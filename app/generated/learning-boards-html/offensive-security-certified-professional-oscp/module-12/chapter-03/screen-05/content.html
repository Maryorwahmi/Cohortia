The Art of the Vulnerability Description

Starting with a clear title and explaining the flaw's nature precisely.

Crafting a Strong Title

Your title is the first impression. It should be specific, informative, and immediately convey the essence of the vulnerability.

Avoid (Vague)

"Web Vulnerability"

Prefer (Specific)

"SQL Injection via 'username' Parameter on Login Page"

Key Takeaway

A precise title and clear explanation of the flaw's nature are fundamental for effective vulnerability reporting and remediation.

Explaining the Nature of the Flaw

Detail the underlying cause and mechanism of the vulnerability. Avoid jargon where simpler terms suffice, but maintain technical accuracy.

Example: SQL Injection

User-supplied input is not properly sanitized or parameterized, allowing an attacker to manipulate SQL queries and potentially access or modify database contents.

Example: Buffer Overflow

An oversized input overwrites critical memory regions, leading to unexpected program behavior, crashes, or even arbitrary code execution.

Pro Tip

Always assume your reader has a moderate technical understanding, not necessarily your expert-level knowledge. Explain complex terms concisely.
