Key Concepts

Reinforcing core principles of vulnerability reporting

Vulnerability Description

A detailed, technically accurate explanation of a security flaw, including its nature, location, and underlying cause.

Technical Impact

The direct consequences of a vulnerability's exploitation in terms of system access, data manipulation, or service disruption.

Business Impact

The real-world organizational consequences of a technical impact, such as financial loss, reputational damage, or legal penalties.

Summary Takeaway

A comprehensive report clearly articulates the vulnerability, its direct technical consequences, and its broader business implications.
