Two Sides of the Coin: Technical vs. Business Impact

Differentiating direct system consequences from organizational repercussions.

Technical Impact

Describes the direct control or access an attacker gains on a system or application.

  • •Focuses on system-level consequences.
  • •Answers: "What can the attacker *do*?"
  • •Examples: Arbitrary code execution, data exfiltration.
Example

Gaining a SYSTEM shell on a critical server.

Business Impact

Explains how the technical impact translates into real-world consequences for the organization.

  • •Focuses on organizational repercussions.
  • •Answers: "How does this affect the *business*?"
  • •Examples: Financial loss, reputational damage.
Example

Complete compromise of Active Directory, leading to data theft and operational disruption.

Key Takeaway

Distinguishing between technical and business impact allows for comprehensive risk communication to both technical and executive audiences.
