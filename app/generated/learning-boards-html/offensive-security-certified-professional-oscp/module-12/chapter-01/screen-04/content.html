Core Concept

The Report: A Comprehensive Record

Documenting scope, methodology, and findings for compliance and due diligence.

Key Functions

Provides a detailed account of all penetration test activities and findings.

Legal & Compliance

Demonstrates due diligence and adherence to regulatory requirements and agreed-upon testing parameters.

Transparency & Accountability

Ensures clear communication and responsibility for both the testing team and the client organization.

Elements of a Comprehensive Record
Scope Definition

Clearly outlines what was tested and what was excluded.

Methodologies Used

Details the tools, techniques, and procedures followed.

Detailed Findings

All identified vulnerabilities, their severity, and impact.

Evidence Collection

Screenshots, logs, and other proof of concept.
