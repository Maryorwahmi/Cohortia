OSCP Requirement

The OSCP Exam: Reporting as Your Final Hurdle

Proving not just technical skill, but professional documentation ability.

OSCP Reporting Expectations
Professional Standard

Document findings and process to a high professional level.

Clear Evidence

Provide screenshots and logs for every step of your exploit chain.

Detailed Exploit Chain

Explain each step from initial access to privilege escalation.

Structured Approach

Organize your report logically and adhere to OffSec's template.

Key Takeaway

A compliant OSCP report is non-negotiable for exam success, demonstrating your ability to document the full pentesting lifecycle.

Exam Failure Risk

Even with successful exploitation, a non-compliant report can lead to failing the OSCP exam.

Pro-Tip

Practice documenting your labs meticulously. Treat every lab report as if it were your final exam submission.
