Audience Focus

Tailoring Communication for Impact

Adapting your report's language and detail for diverse stakeholders.

Report Sections & Target Audiences
Executive Summary

For C-suite, Board. Focus: Business impact, high-level risk.

Technical Findings

For Engineers, Dev Teams. Focus: Specifics, PoC, exploit chain.

Recommendations

For Security Teams, IT Ops. Focus: Prioritized remediation steps.

Key Takeaway

Tailoring your report to each audience ensures clarity, relevance, and ultimately, action on security improvements.

Misconception

A 'one-size-fits-all' report will not effectively communicate critical findings to all stakeholders.

Pro-Tip

Start with an executive summary that highlights business impact, then progressively add technical detail.
