Structured Writing

Structured Writing for OSCP Reports

Adhering to OffSec's template for clarity and compliance.

Key Sections of an OSCP Report
Executive Summary

High-level overview of findings and business impact.

Scope & Methodology

Details of the engagement boundaries and techniques used.

Technical Findings

Detailed vulnerability descriptions, PoC, and impact.

Recommendations

Prioritized, actionable steps for remediation.

Key Takeaway

Following OffSec's structured report template is essential for clarity, compliance, and successful verification of your exam findings.

Risk of Disorganization

A poorly structured report can obscure critical findings and make it difficult for examiners to follow your exploit chain.

Pro-Tip

Familiarize yourself with the official OSCP reporting template well in advance of your exam to streamline your documentation process.
