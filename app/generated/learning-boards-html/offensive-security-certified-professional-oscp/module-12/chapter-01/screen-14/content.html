Chapter Summary

Key Concepts: Reporting Foundations

Essential ideas to remember from this chapter

Penetration Test Report

A formal document detailing the scope, methodology, findings, and recommendations of an engagement.

Audience Tailoring

Adjusting the language, depth, and focus of a report to suit different stakeholders (e.g., executive vs. technical).

Ethical Reporting

The responsibility to present accurate, unbiased, and constructive findings, adhering to professional standards.

Summary Takeaway

Effective penetration testing relies on professional, audience-tailored, and ethical reporting to drive meaningful security improvements.
