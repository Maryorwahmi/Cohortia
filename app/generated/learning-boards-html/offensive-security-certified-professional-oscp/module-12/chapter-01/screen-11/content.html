Evidence Collection for OSCP Success

Gathering and presenting proof of concept for every vulnerability.

Types of Evidence for OSCP
Command-Line Output

Screenshots of successful commands, shell access.

Configuration Files

Proof of sensitive data, misconfigurations.

Network Traffic Captures

Wireshark or similar captures showing exploit traffic.

Proof of Access/Impact

Screenshots of flags, sensitive data, or system control.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive and clear evidence is non-negotiable for OSCP, allowing examiners to verify your findings and exploit chain.

Common Mistake

Insufficient or unclear evidence, making it impossible for examiners to validate your claims.

Pro-Tip

Organize your evidence logically within your report, clearly labeling each screenshot and explaining its relevance.
