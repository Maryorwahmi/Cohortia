Key Concepts: Report Structure Essentials

Core ideas for building effective penetration test reports.

Executive Summary

A high-level, non-technical overview of the report's key findings, impact, and recommendations, typically for management.

Scope and Methodology

Defines the boundaries of the penetration test, including what was tested, excluded, and the techniques used.

Technical Findings

Detailed descriptions of each discovered vulnerability, including its nature, impact, proof of concept (PoC), and steps to reproduce.
