"Show, Don't Just Tell": OSCP Evidence Best Practices

Ensuring your findings are backed by clear, unedited proof.

Essential Evidence

  • Clear Screenshots

    Unedited images showing commands and their full output (e.g., `whoami`, `id`, `ipconfig`, `ifconfig`, successful shell access).

  • Avoid Common Mistakes

    No blurry images, no cropped details, and never just state a shell was obtained without visual proof.

Example: Shell Access Evidence

# Attacker machine
kali@kali:~$ nc -lvnp 4444
# Listening on 0.0.0.0 4444

# Victim machine (after exploit)
C:\Users\Public\> whoami
nt authority\system

C:\Users\Public\> ipconfig
Windows IP Configuration
...
IPv4 Address. . . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.105
...
OSCP CRITICAL
Key Takeaway

Always provide clear, unedited, and contextual screenshots for every successful exploit to meet OSCP evidence requirements and maintain report credibility.
