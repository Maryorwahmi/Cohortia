Executive Summary: Bridging Technical Gaps

A concise, non-technical overview for high-level stakeholders.

Key Elements

  • Concise & Non-Technical

    Summarizes scope, overall security posture, critical findings, and top-level recommendations, typically one page, free of jargon.

  • Business Impact Focused

    Translates technical risks into organizational consequences (e.g., regulatory, reputational, financial) to prompt action.

Good vs. Bad Example

Good Example:

"Critical vulnerability allowing unauthorized access to sensitive customer data, posing significant regulatory and reputational risks."

Bad Example:

"CVE-2023-12345 found, enabling ROP chains and EIP overwrite via buffer overflow."

Key Takeaway

The Executive Summary must be a jargon-free, business-focused snapshot that drives management decisions, not a technical deep dive.
