Proof of Concept: The OSCP's Critical Evidence

Providing concrete, reproducible steps and crucial evidence.

PoC Essentials

  • Steps to Reproduce

    Exact, detailed steps an auditor can follow to replicate your findings, including specific commands, code snippets, and URLs.

  • Screenshots as Evidence

    Visual proof of successful exploitation, such as fuzzer output, debugger states, and shell access (e.g., `whoami`, `id`).

OSCP Requirement

For the OSCP exam, the PoC is paramount. Every claim of a vulnerability or successful exploit *must* be backed by clear, unedited evidence. This demonstrates your ability to not only find but also prove the impact of a vulnerability.

Verifiable Exploitation
Key Takeaway

A robust Proof of Concept with clear, reproducible steps and unedited screenshots is essential for validating findings and is a critical component of the OSCP report.
