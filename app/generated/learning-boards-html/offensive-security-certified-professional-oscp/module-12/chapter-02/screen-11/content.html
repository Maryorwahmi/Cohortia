Leveraging Appendices for Comprehensive Documentation

Including detailed raw data and supporting information.

Purpose of Appendices

  • Supplementary Information

    Houses detailed data that is too extensive for the main body but still relevant, such as full scan results or raw tool outputs.

  • Avoid Clutter

    Keeps the main report concise and focused on key findings and recommendations, while still providing technical depth.

OSCP Application

For the OSCP, appendices are ideal for including full Nmap scan results, detailed `enum4linux` output, or `BloodHound` analysis that supports your findings without disrupting the main narrative flow.

Thoroughness & Detail
Key Takeaway

Appendices are essential for providing comprehensive technical detail and raw data, supporting your findings without overwhelming the main report's narrative.
