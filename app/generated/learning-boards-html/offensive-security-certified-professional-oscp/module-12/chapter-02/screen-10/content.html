Actionable Remediation Recommendations

Providing practical, prioritized advice for vulnerability resolution.

Key Principles

  • Specific & Actionable

    Recommendations must be precise, telling the client exactly how to fix the vulnerability, not just what needs fixing.

  • Prioritized List

    Offer a categorized list of remediation actions, often by severity, to help clients focus on the most critical fixes first.

Practical Examples

SQL Injection:

"Implement parameterized queries or prepared statements for all database interactions."

Vulnerable Service:

"Upgrade [Service Name] to version X.Y.Z or higher, and implement network segmentation to restrict access to port Z."

Key Takeaway

Effective recommendations are specific, practical, and prioritized, guiding clients directly to solutions and maximizing the impact of your penetration test.
