Defining Boundaries: Scope and Methodology

Establishing the 'what,' 'how,' and 'when' of your assessment.

Key Definitions

  • Scope

    Clearly defines what systems, applications, or networks were tested and what was explicitly out of bounds. This prevents misunderstandings.

  • Methodology

    Details the approach taken (e.g., black-box, white-box, specific frameworks like OWASP Top 10) and the duration of the test.

OSCP Relevance

For the OSCP exam, this section is where you specify the lab environment, the particular machines targeted, and the general strategy you employed to achieve your objectives. It demonstrates your planning and execution.

Transparency & Context
Key Takeaway

A well-defined Scope and Methodology section provides crucial context for your findings and ensures transparency throughout the penetration test engagement.
