Key Concepts: Evidence and Action

Reinforcing the critical elements for report credibility and remediation.

Proof of Concept (PoC)

Concrete evidence (e.g., commands, code, screenshots) demonstrating the existence and exploitability of a vulnerability.

Remediation Recommendations

Specific, actionable advice provided to the client for fixing identified vulnerabilities.

Appendices

Supplementary sections containing detailed technical data, raw tool output, or other supporting documentation.
