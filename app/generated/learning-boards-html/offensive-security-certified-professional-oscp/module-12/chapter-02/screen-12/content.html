The Holistic Report: A Logical Narrative

Ensuring a logical flow from executive overview to technical detail.

Report Progression
  • 1.

    Start Broad: Executive Summary

    High-level overview for management, focusing on business impact.

  • 2.

    Contextualize: Scope & Methodology

    Define the boundaries and techniques of the assessment.

  • 3.

    Deep Dive: Technical Findings

    Detailed vulnerabilities with descriptions, impact, and Proof of Concept.

  • 4.

    Action: Recommendations

    Specific, prioritized advice for remediation.

  • 5.

    Support: Appendices

    Supplementary technical data and raw tool outputs.

Why a Narrative Matters

A logical narrative ensures that your report is not just a collection of facts, but a compelling story that educates, informs, and drives action. This structured approach is key to demonstrating professionalism and maximizing the impact of your work, especially in high-stakes scenarios like the OSCP exam.

Impact & Professionalism
Key Takeaway

A well-structured report creates a logical narrative, guiding readers from high-level summaries to detailed findings and actionable recommendations, ensuring maximum impact.
