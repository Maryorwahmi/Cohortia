Workflow Strategy

Managing Your Report Draft: Iterative Approach

Start drafting during the exam, not after

Key Takeaway

Begin drafting your report as soon as you find a vulnerability or gain access to a machine to capture details accurately and reduce end-of-exam pressure.

Pro-Tip

Create a dedicated 'scratchpad' section in your report template to quickly dump raw commands, notes, and observations as you work, then organize them later.

Iterative Reporting Steps
1

Initial Access: Start a new section in your draft immediately.

2

Copy-Paste Commands: Log all commands used for enumeration and exploitation.

3

Take Screenshots: Capture evidence as soon as it appears, with descriptive filenames.

4

Note Observations: Jot down key insights, errors, and thought processes.
