Mandatory Requirement

The OffSec Reporting Template

Understanding its critical structure and sections

Key Takeaway

The OffSec template is a mandatory framework; download it early and adhere strictly to its predefined sections and formatting requirements.

Common Mistake

Submitting a report that deviates from the OffSec template or misses critical sections is a common reason for report rejection, even if the technical work is sound.

Typical Template Sections

Executive Summary: High-level overview of findings.

Scope: Defines the boundaries of the test.

Technical Findings: Detailed vulnerabilities and exploits.

Buffer Overflow: Dedicated section for BOF machine details.
