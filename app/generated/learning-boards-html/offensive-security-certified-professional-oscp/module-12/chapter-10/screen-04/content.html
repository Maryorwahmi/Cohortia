Exam Logistics

Understanding Your OSCP Exam Results

The waiting period and initial feedback from OffSec.

The Result Process
01

Submission & Waiting Period

After submitting your exam report, expect a review period of up to 10 business days from OffSec.

02

Email Notification

You will receive an email with your official results once the review is complete.

03

Passing Outcome

If you pass, congratulations! Instructions to claim your certification will be provided.

04

Non-Passing Outcome

Don't be discouraged. Many successful OSCPs failed their first attempt. OffSec provides general feedback.

Understanding Feedback
  • •OffSec provides general indications like "insufficient points" or "reporting issues."
  • •No specific hints about exam machines are given to encourage genuine learning.
  • •This feedback helps you identify broad areas for improvement, not specific solutions.
Key Takeaway

The exam result is a data point for growth. Use the feedback to refine your approach, not to feel defeated.

Common Misconception

Failing the OSCP on the first try means you're not cut out for offensive security. In reality, it's a common experience and a valuable learning opportunity.
