Deep Dive Areas

Exploring Specific Cybersecurity Domains

After the OSCP, you might find certain areas particularly challenging or fascinating. Dedicate time to specialize and become an expert.

Active Directory

Master advanced enumeration, exploitation, and lateral movement techniques within AD environments.

Web Application Exploitation

Go beyond OWASP Top 10 to explore complex RCE, deserialization, and API vulnerabilities.

Cloud Security

Learn to assess and secure environments in AWS, Azure, and GCP, identifying unique attack vectors.

Reverse Engineering

Dissect binaries, understand malware, and develop custom exploits by analyzing machine code.

Example

Mastering a New Tool: BloodHound

After the OSCP, you might discover BloodHound, a powerful Active Directory reconnaissance tool. Dedicating time to learn its queries, data ingestion, and visualization capabilities can dramatically improve your ability to identify attack paths in complex AD environments, a skill not explicitly covered in depth by the OSCP but highly valuable in real-world engagements.

Key Takeaway

Don't just learn more; learn smarter. Focus on mastering tools and diving deep into areas that align with your interests and career goals.
