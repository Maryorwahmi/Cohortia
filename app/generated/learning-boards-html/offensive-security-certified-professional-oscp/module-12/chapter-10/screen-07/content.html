Deep Dive Analysis

Learning from Setbacks: Analyzing Your Activity Log

Pinpointing where you got stuck and what was missed during the exam.

Steps for Activity Log Analysis
01

Review Chronologically

Go through your log machine by machine, command by command, to retrace your steps.

02

Identify Stuck Points

Mark where you spent excessive time without progress or hit dead ends.

03

Missed Enumeration

Did you overlook crucial information from initial scans or service enumeration?

04

Privilege Escalation Vectors

Did you try all common PE techniques for the given OS? What did you miss?

Example Questions to Ask
  • •For Windows: Did I check for unquoted service paths, vulnerable kernel drivers, or misconfigured scheduled tasks?
  • •For Linux: Did I look for SUID/SGID binaries, cron jobs, or weak file permissions?
  • •Was my initial port scan comprehensive enough? Did I miss any unusual open ports?
  • •Did I thoroughly enumerate web applications for common vulnerabilities like SQLi or LFI?
Key Takeaway

The activity log is your most objective source of truth for identifying precise technical and methodological shortcomings.
