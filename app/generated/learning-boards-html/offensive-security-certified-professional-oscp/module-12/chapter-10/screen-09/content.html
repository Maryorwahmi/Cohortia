Re-evaluate Your Approach

Optimizing Your Exam-Day Strategy

A critical part of post-exam review is to re-evaluate your overall strategy. This goes beyond technical skills and focuses on your approach to the exam itself.

Time Management

Did you allocate time effectively across machines? Avoid getting stuck on one target for too long.

Break Strategy

Regular breaks prevent tunnel vision and mental fatigue. Ensure you step away and clear your head.

Prioritization

Did you prioritize high-point machines or the BOF correctly? Focus on maximizing your score efficiently.

Tunnel Vision

Avoid getting fixated on one path. If stuck, pivot to another machine or enumeration technique.

Scenario

Exam Day Reflection

Imagine you spent 10 hours on a 10-point machine, neglecting the 20-point targets. This is a strategic misstep.

  • Did you stick to your initial plan, or did you adapt?
  • Were you too rigid, or did you pivot effectively when needed?
  • Did external factors (environment, stress) impact your focus?
Key Takeaway

A failed attempt is a blueprint for success. Use it to refine not just your technical skills, but your entire exam-day strategy.

Pro-Tip

Simulate & Practice

Before your retake, practice full 24-hour exam simulations. This helps you test and refine your new strategy under pressure, identifying any remaining weaknesses in your approach.
