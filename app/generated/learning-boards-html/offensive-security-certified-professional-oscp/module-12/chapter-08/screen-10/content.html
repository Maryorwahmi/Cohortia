Strategies for Sustained Focus

Regular Short Breaks

Step away for 5-10 minutes every 1-2 hours to stretch and clear your head.

Planned Meal Breaks

Eat proper meals, not just snacks, for sustained energy and cognitive function.

Strategic Power Naps

A 20-30 minute power nap can be incredibly rejuvenating if you hit a wall.

Avoid Burnout

Staring at the same terminal for hours leads to tunnel vision. Switch machines or take a break to reset your brain.

Pro-Tip

Integrate breaks into your exam day plan. Treat them as essential components of your strategy, not optional luxuries.

Key Takeaway

Actively manage your stress and schedule regular breaks to maintain mental clarity and sustained performance throughout the 24-hour exam.
