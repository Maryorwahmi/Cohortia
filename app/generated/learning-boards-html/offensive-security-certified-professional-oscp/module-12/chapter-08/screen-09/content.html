The Power of an Activity Log

Report Writing Aid

Primary source for reconstructing your attack path for the final exam report.

Troubleshooting

Review your exact steps to identify errors or missed opportunities if something goes wrong.

Context Switching

Easily pick up where you left off when switching between different target machines.

What to Log
  • Every command executed
  • All discoveries (e.g., open ports, user accounts)
  • Exploit attempts and their outcomes
  • Screenshots of shells, flags, and critical steps
Consequence of Neglect

Poor documentation can lead to significant time loss, frustration, and difficulty in completing the required exam report.

Key Takeaway

Maintain a detailed activity log throughout the exam; it's your most valuable asset for reporting, troubleshooting, and efficient progress.
