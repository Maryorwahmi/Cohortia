Essential Tunneling Tools for OSCP

SSH Local Forwarding

ssh -L: Forwards a local port to a remote port on the pivot machine, making internal services accessible locally.

Chisel

A fast TCP/UDP tunnel over HTTP, often used for creating SOCKS proxies through compromised hosts.

Proxychains

Allows you to force any TCP client through a SOCKS or HTTP proxy, routing all traffic through your pivot.

Practice is Key

Extensively practice these tools in the labs to understand their nuances and quickly configure them during the exam.

Internal Recon

Once tunneling is established, re-run your reconnaissance tools (e.g., Nmap) on the newly accessible internal network segment.

Key Takeaway

Mastering tunneling tools is crucial for effectively routing traffic through pivot machines and accessing isolated internal network services.
