Core Concept

The Mental Game

The OSCP exam is as much a mental challenge as it is a technical one. Your ability to strategize, manage time, and stay calm under pressure significantly impacts your success.

Why it Matters

Without a solid plan, it's easy to get lost in rabbit holes, burn out, or miss critical opportunities. A strategic approach maximizes your points and preserves your sanity.

Key Takeaway

Technical skills are foundational, but strategic planning and mental resilience are the true differentiators for OSCP success.

OSCP Success Factors
Strategy
Time Management
Resilience
Technical Skills

A balanced approach integrating strategic planning, effective time management, mental resilience, and strong technical skills leads to OSCP success.
