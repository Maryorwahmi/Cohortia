Key Concepts

Essential Takeaways for Exam Success

Time Management

Strategically allocate time across the 24-hour exam, prioritizing the Buffer Overflow and other targets.

Prioritization

Ranking targets based on point value, perceived difficulty, and familiarity with potential vulnerabilities.

Activity Log

A chronological record of all actions, commands, and observations during the exam for later report writing.

Summary Takeaway

A structured approach to time, targets, and documentation forms the bedrock of a successful OSCP exam strategy.
