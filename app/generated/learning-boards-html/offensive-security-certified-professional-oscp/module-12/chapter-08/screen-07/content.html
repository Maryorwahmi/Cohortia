Prioritization vs. Getting Stuck

Prioritize Smartly

  • Focus on services you know.
  • Target known vulnerabilities.
  • Consider point values.
  • Build momentum with easier wins.

Avoid Rabbit Holes

  • Don't spend too long on one machine.
  • Recognize when you're stuck.
  • Tunnel vision is a common pitfall.
  • Burnout reduces effectiveness.
The 2-3 Hour Rule

If you've spent 2-3 hours on a machine with no progress, take a break and switch. Return with fresh eyes later.

Example Scenario

You're stuck on a complex web app. Switch to an SMB share you're familiar with to gain user access, then revisit the web app.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize targets based on your strengths and the likelihood of success, and don't hesitate to switch machines if you hit a prolonged roadblock.
