Key Concepts

Sustaining Performance and Expanding Reach

Stress Management

Techniques like taking breaks, power naps, and switching tasks to avoid burnout and maintain focus.

Pivoting & Tunneling

Using a compromised machine as a jump point to access internal networks, often with tools like ssh -L or chisel.

"Try Harder"

OffSec's philosophy emphasizing persistence, creativity, and resilience, coupled with knowing when to adapt your approach.

Summary Takeaway

Sustaining performance through stress management, expanding reach via pivoting, and embracing adaptive persistence are crucial for advanced exam success.
