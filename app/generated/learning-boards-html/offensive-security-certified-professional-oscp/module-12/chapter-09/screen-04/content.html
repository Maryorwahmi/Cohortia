Exam Overview

The Three Pillars of OSCP Pitfalls

Understanding where candidates most commonly stumble.

Key Takeaway

OSCP success requires overcoming technical, psychological, and reporting hurdles, not just technical skill.

Why These Pillars Matter

  • Comprehensive Challenge: The exam tests more than just technical skills; it assesses your overall approach.
  • Interconnectedness: A psychological pitfall like burnout can lead to technical mistakes or reporting errors.
  • Strategic Advantage: Proactively addressing all three areas significantly boosts your chances of success.
  • Real-World Relevance: These challenges mirror real-world penetration testing engagements.
Categories of Common Pitfalls

Technical

Exploitation, enumeration, privilege escalation.

Psychological

Burnout, frustration, confidence.

Reporting

Proof, evidence, template adherence.
