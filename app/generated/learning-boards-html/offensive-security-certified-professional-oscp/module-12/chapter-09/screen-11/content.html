Psychological Pitfall

Building Confidence and Managing Information

Trusting your training and meticulous note-taking.

Key Takeaway

Trust your preparation and use a detailed activity log to manage information and reinforce confidence.

The Power of Documentation

  • Reduces Cognitive Load: Frees up mental resources by externalizing information.
  • Reliable Reference: Provides a consistent source of truth for all your findings.
  • Progress Tracking: Visually demonstrates your efforts, boosting morale.
  • Report Foundation: Serves as the primary source for your exam report.
Strategies for Confidence & Information Management

Trust Your Training

You've prepared for this; focus on the process and small wins.

Maintain a Meticulous Activity Log

Document every command, output, and observation to offload your brain.

Focus on the Process

Concentrate on executing steps correctly, not just the final outcome.
