Psychological Pitfall

Combating Burnout and Frustration

Maintaining mental resilience during the 24-hour exam.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize self-care and strategic breaks to manage mental fatigue and emotional responses during the exam.

Impact of Mental Fatigue

  • Poor Decision-Making: Exhaustion leads to errors and missed opportunities.
  • Reduced Focus: Difficulty concentrating on complex problems.
  • Increased Errors: Simple mistakes become more frequent.
  • Giving Up: Mental drain can lead to premature surrender.
Strategies for Mental Resilience

Schedule Regular Breaks

Step away from the screen, eat, hydrate, and even take a power nap.

Recognize Frustration Cues

When feeling stuck, take a deliberate step back to clear your mind.

Change Your Environment

Even a short walk can provide a fresh perspective and reduce stress.
