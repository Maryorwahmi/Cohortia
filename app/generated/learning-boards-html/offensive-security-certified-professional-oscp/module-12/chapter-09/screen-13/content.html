Reporting Pitfall

Adhering to the Report Template and Remediation

Structuring your findings and providing actionable advice.

Key Takeaway

Strictly follow the official report template and provide specific, actionable remediation advice for every vulnerability.

Importance of Report Structure

  • Clarity for Reviewers: A consistent format makes reports easier to evaluate.
  • Completeness: Ensures all required sections and information are included.
  • Professionalism: Reflects your attention to detail and adherence to standards.
  • Actionable Advice: Specific remediation helps clients fix vulnerabilities effectively.
Reporting Best Practices

Use Official OffSec Template

Download and adhere to the provided report structure and headings.

Specific Remediation

Provide actionable, detailed recommendations for each vulnerability.

Thoroughly document every step of your Buffer Overflow process.
