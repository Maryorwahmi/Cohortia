Reporting Pitfall

Critical Reporting: Proofs and Evidence

Ensuring every claim is backed by clear, unedited documentation.

Key Takeaway

Always include `proof.txt` and provide clear, contextual, and unedited screenshots for every step of your exploit chain.

Why Evidence is Non-Negotiable

  • Verification: OffSec needs to verify your claims of compromise.
  • Integrity: Unedited evidence ensures the authenticity of your work.
  • Point Allocation: Missing proofs lead to zero points for that machine.
  • Clarity: Clear screenshots help the reviewer understand your methodology.
Essential Reporting Evidence

proof.txt / local.txt

Mandatory unique strings from compromised machines.

Clear Screenshots

Unedited images of every significant command and its output.

Contextual Command Output

Show the commands you ran and their direct results.
