Avoiding Tunnel Vision: When to Pivot

Don't get stuck on a single vulnerability or machine for too long.

Key Takeaway

Set strict time limits for each target and be prepared to switch if no significant progress is made.

Why Tunnel Vision is Dangerous

  • Wasted Time: Hours spent on a dead end could be used to compromise other machines.
  • Missed Opportunities: Fixation prevents you from seeing easier paths or other targets.
  • Increased Frustration: Lack of progress leads to mental fatigue and poor decision-making.
  • Exam Failure: Not accumulating enough points across targets due to over-focus.
Strategies to Avoid Tunnel Vision

Set Time Limits

Allocate 2-3 hours per target. If no progress, move on.

Take Breaks & Review

Step away, clear your mind, and review your notes with fresh eyes.

Switch Targets

Move to another machine or vulnerability if you're stalled.
