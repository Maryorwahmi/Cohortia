Chapter Summary

Key Concepts to Remember

Essential takeaways for avoiding common OSCP pitfalls.

Tunnel Vision

Excessive focus on a single problem, leading to missed opportunities and wasted time.

Incomplete Enumeration

Failing to thoroughly explore all potential attack vectors and information sources.

Burnout

Mental and physical exhaustion from prolonged effort without adequate rest.

Summary Takeaway

By understanding and actively mitigating these core pitfalls, you can significantly enhance your OSCP exam preparation and performance.
