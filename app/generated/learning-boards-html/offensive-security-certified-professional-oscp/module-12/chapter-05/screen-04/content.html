Core Principle

Evidence: Show, Don't Just Tell

Every claim you make about a vulnerability or exploit must be backed by verifiable proof.

The OSCP Requirement

Meticulous documentation is a non-negotiable requirement for the OSCP exam. Your report is scrutinized for both findings and proof.

Common Failure Point

Failing to provide sufficient, clear, and contextual evidence is a primary reason for exam report failure, even with successful roots.

Screenshots are Key

The most common and crucial form of evidence is screenshots, providing visual proof of commands, outputs, and exploitation.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize clear, contextual evidence. Your report's strength lies in its verifiable proofs, not just your claims.
