Screenshot Guidelines

Full View & Unedited Proof

Capture entire windows and never manipulate your evidence.

Full Screen/Window

Capture the entire terminal or browser view to provide maximum context, including hostname, username, and directory.

Strictly Unedited

Never crop, blur, or manipulate screenshots. OffSec has a zero-tolerance policy for altered evidence.

Re-capture if Needed

If a screenshot is unclear, re-run the command or exploit to capture a fresh, clear image rather than attempting edits.

Key Takeaway

Always capture full, unedited screenshots to provide maximum context and maintain the integrity of your exam evidence.

Good Screenshot
kali@kali:~
whoami
www-data
[Full Window]

Shows full terminal, command, and output clearly.

Bad Screenshot
www-data
[Cropped]

Only shows output, cropped, missing context.
