Key Concepts: Part 1

Reinforcing the fundamentals of OSCP evidence collection.

Evidence Collection

The systematic gathering of data (screenshots, command output, logs) to prove findings in your report.

Screenshots

Visual proof of commands executed, outputs received, and successful exploitation, requiring clear context and no editing.

Command Output

Textual records of terminal commands and their results, often copied directly from the shell for detailed analysis.

Summary Takeaway

Effective evidence collection hinges on systematically gathering both visual (screenshots) and textual (command output) proofs, ensuring clarity, context, and unedited integrity for your OSCP report.
