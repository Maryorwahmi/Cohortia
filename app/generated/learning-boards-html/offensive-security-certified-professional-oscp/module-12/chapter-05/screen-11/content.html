Activity Logging

Maintaining a Detailed Activity Log

A chronological record of all your actions is vital for reconstructing your attack path.

Exam Memory Aid

During the 24-hour exam, it's impossible to remember every action. A log acts as your reliable memory.

Logging Tools

Use tmux or screen with logging enabled, or a simple text file to copy-paste commands and notes.

Reconstruct Attack Path

A chronological log helps you accurately reconstruct your steps for the report, ensuring no crucial details are missed.

Key Takeaway

Maintain a continuous, timestamped activity log to accurately recall and document every significant action during your exam.
