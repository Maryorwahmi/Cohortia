Report Submission Process

Transforming Your Findings into a Passing Grade

Upload Report

Submit your detailed PDF report to the OffSec portal within 24 hours.

Official Template

Your report MUST follow the official OffSec reporting template exactly.

Include Proofs

Include `proof.txt` and `local.txt` strings, plus detailed BOF steps and screenshots.

Common Mistake

Submitting a report that doesn't follow the template, misses proof files, or lacks sufficient evidence can lead to failure.

Key Takeaway

Meticulous reporting, strict adherence to the OffSec template, and complete evidence are paramount for converting your technical work into a passing grade.
