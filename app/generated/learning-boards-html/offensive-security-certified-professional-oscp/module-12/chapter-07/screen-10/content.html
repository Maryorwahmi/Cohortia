Monitoring and Safety During the Exam

Understanding Proctor Behavior and Environment Preparation

Proctor Monitoring

Your webcam and screen(s) are actively monitored for the entire 24-hour lab exam to ensure compliance.

Suspicious Behavior

Looking away frequently, using a phone, or unauthorized persons in the room can lead to exam termination.

Environment Safety Note

Test equipment, clear desk, and inform housemates of your exam schedule to prevent interruptions and rule violations.

Key Takeaway

Strict adherence to proctoring rules and proactive environment preparation are essential to avoid exam termination and ensure focus.
