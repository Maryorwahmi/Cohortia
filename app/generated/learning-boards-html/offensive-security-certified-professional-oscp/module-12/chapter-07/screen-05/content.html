Buffer Overflow Machine

Guaranteed 20-25 points. Often considered a 'must-pass' due to its predictable nature and high value. Requires full exploit from fuzzing to SYSTEM shell.

Individual Target Machines

Remaining points (75-80) distributed among 3-4 machines. Each typically 20-25 points for full compromise (root/SYSTEM). Partial points possible for initial access.

Prioritization Example

Focus on the BOF machine early to secure significant points, then move to other high-value targets. Avoid getting stuck on low-point machines.

Key Takeaway

Strategic point allocation, especially prioritizing the Buffer Overflow, is essential for reaching the 70-point passing threshold efficiently.
