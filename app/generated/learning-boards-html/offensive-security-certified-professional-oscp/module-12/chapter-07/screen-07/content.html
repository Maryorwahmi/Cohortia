Target Variety

Individual Target Machines

Navigating Diverse Systems for Remaining Points

Machine Types & Count

Typically 3-4 machines, a mix of Linux and Windows operating systems. Each presents unique challenges.

Full Compromise Points

Each machine offers 20-25 points for achieving root/SYSTEM privileges. This is the primary objective.

Partial Points (Rare)

Sometimes, 10 points may be awarded for initial user-level access. However, always aim for full compromise.

Diverse Skillset

Requires a broad range of skills: web exploitation, privilege escalation, network services, and more.

Example Strategy

After BOF, quickly enumerate all remaining machines. Prioritize based on perceived difficulty and potential for full compromise.

Key Takeaway

Successfully compromising individual target machines requires adaptability, a broad skillset, and strategic prioritization to maximize points.
