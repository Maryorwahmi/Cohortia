Technical Issues and Support

What to Do When Things Go Wrong During the Exam

Immediate Contact

If a lab machine crashes, VPN fails, or proctoring software has issues, immediately contact the proctor or OffSec support.

No Self-Fixing

Do NOT attempt to fix lab environment issues yourself; this could be seen as tampering and lead to exam termination.

Document Everything

Document any issues, including timestamps, in your activity log for potential review.

Key Takeaway

In case of technical issues, always report to OffSec support immediately and document the incident; never attempt to self-remediate lab problems.
