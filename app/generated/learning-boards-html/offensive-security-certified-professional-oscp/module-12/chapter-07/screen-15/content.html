Key Concepts Continued

Reinforcing Your OSCP Exam Knowledge

Proctoring

Live monitoring of candidates via webcam and screen sharing to ensure exam integrity and compliance.

`proof.txt` / `local.txt`

Unique strings found on compromised machines that serve as proof of root/SYSTEM or user-level access.

OffSec Reporting Template

The mandatory template that must be used for submitting the official OSCP exam report.

Summary Takeaway

Adherence to proctoring rules, diligent collection of proof files, and strict use of the official reporting template are non-negotiable for OSCP exam success.
