OSCP Proctoring Requirements

Ensuring a Compliant and Secure Exam Setup

Technical Setup

Stable internet, working webcam, and microphone are mandatory for live proctoring and screen sharing.

Physical Environment

A quiet, private room with a clean desk (no unauthorized materials) is required to prevent distractions and rule violations.

Identification

A valid government-issued ID must be presented for verification at the start of the exam.

Key Takeaway

Thoroughly prepare your technical and physical environment in advance to ensure compliance and a distraction-free OSCP exam experience.
