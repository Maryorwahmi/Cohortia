Proof of Compromise

The `proof.txt` and `local.txt` Files

Your Essential Evidence for Exam Success

`local.txt`

Found upon initial user-level access. Proves you gained a low-privilege shell. Essential for partial points if applicable.

`proof.txt`

Found upon achieving root/SYSTEM privileges. The ultimate evidence of full compromise, critical for full points.

Why They Matter

These unique strings are the only way OffSec verifies your compromise. Without them, no points are awarded for the machine.

Key Takeaway

Always prioritize locating and securely documenting the contents of `local.txt` and `proof.txt` as soon as you compromise a machine.

proof.txt

Root/SYSTEM Access

local.txt

Initial User Access

This is your unique proof string: 0xDEADBEEF
