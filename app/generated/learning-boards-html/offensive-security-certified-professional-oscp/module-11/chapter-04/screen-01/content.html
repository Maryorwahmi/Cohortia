Lateral Movement Techniques

Expanding Your Foothold Across the Network

Discover how attackers extend their reach from an initial compromise to high-value targets within a network. Learn to identify and leverage critical vulnerabilities for strategic access.

Key Takeaway

Lateral movement is a critical post-exploitation phase, enabling attackers to reach sensitive assets by leveraging compromised credentials and trust relationships.

Credential Access

Obtaining hashes or passwords from compromised systems.
