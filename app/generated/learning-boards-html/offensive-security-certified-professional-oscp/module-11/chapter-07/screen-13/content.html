CHAPTER SUMMARY

Essential Takeaways

Core ideas from Steganography & Covert Channels.

Steganography

The art and science of hiding a message within another message or file, making its presence undetectable.

Cryptography

The practice of scrambling a message to make it unreadable, focusing on confidentiality rather than concealment.

Least Significant Bit (LSB)

The lowest-order bit in a binary number, often used in steganography to embed data with minimal visual or auditory impact.

Chapter Summary

Steganography and cryptography are distinct but complementary techniques for data protection. Steganography hides the existence of data, while cryptography makes it unreadable. The LSB method is a core technique for embedding data subtly within digital media.
