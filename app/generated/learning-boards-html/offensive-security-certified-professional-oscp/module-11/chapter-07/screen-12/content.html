DEFENSIVE MEASURES

The Challenge of Detection

Identifying hidden communication in a sea of legitimate traffic.

Detecting Steganography

  • Steganalysis: Statistical analysis of media files to identify subtle anomalies.
  • File Comparison: Comparing suspicious files against known originals or baselines.
  • Entropy Analysis: Looking for unusual randomness or patterns in data that suggest hidden content.
  • Digital Forensics: Specialized tools and techniques to uncover embedded data.
Challenge

Subtle changes are hard to spot without a baseline or advanced analytical tools.

Detecting Covert Channels

  • Deep Packet Inspection (DPI): Analyzing protocol fields for unusual or unexpected data.
  • Behavioral Analysis: Monitoring network traffic for deviations in timing, packet sizes, or frequency.
  • Baseline Comparisons: Establishing normal traffic patterns and flagging significant anomalies.
  • Protocol Anomaly Detection: Identifying non-standard usage of protocol fields or sequences.
Challenge

Requires deep understanding of protocol specifications and extensive network baselining.

Key Takeaway

Effective detection of steganography and covert channels demands a multi-layered approach, combining statistical analysis, deep inspection, and behavioral monitoring to identify subtle deviations from normal patterns.
