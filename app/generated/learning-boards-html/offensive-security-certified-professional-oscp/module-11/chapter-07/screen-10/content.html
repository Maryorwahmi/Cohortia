COVERT CHANNEL TYPES

Exploiting Time for Secrecy

Conveying information through the modulation of event delays.

Information can be encoded by varying the time intervals between legitimate network events, such as ICMP pings.

How it Works

Data is conveyed by modulating the timing of events. For example, a short delay might represent a '0' bit, and a long delay a '1' bit, within a series of otherwise normal network packets.

Low Bandwidth, High Stealth

Timing channels typically offer very low data transfer rates but are extremely difficult to detect because they don't alter packet content, only the intervals between them.

Detection Difficulty

Identifying timing channels requires sophisticated behavioral analysis, baselining normal network traffic patterns, and looking for subtle, consistent deviations in timing.

Key Takeaway

Timing channels leverage the temporal aspects of network communication to hide data, making them a highly stealthy but low-bandwidth covert method.
