Key Takeaway

`netstat -ano` is crucial for linking network connections to specific processes via their PIDs, aiding in service analysis.

# Command: ipconfig /all
ipconfig /all
# Output (excerpt):
Ethernet adapter Ethernet0:   Connection-specific DNS Suffix  . : localdomain   IPv4 Address. . . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.150   Subnet Mask . . . . . . . . . . . : 255.255.255.0   Default Gateway . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.1

Why it Matters

  • Privilege Mapping: `whoami /priv` reveals potential escalation vectors.
  • User Account Discovery: Identify local users and administrative groups.
  • Network Topology: Understand IP addresses, subnets, and default gateways.
  • Active Connections: Pinpoint open ports and associated processes.
Common Mistake

Overlooking the detailed output of `ipconfig /all` for critical network segment or DNS configuration clues.
