Enumerating the Compromised Host

Uncovering Hidden Clues for Post-Exploitation Success

Key Idea

Systematic information gathering is crucial for understanding a compromised host and planning further actions.

Once you've established a stable foothold on a target system, the next crucial step is thorough enumeration. This process is like a detective gathering clues at a crime scene, revealing opportunities for privilege escalation, lateral movement, or data exfiltration. It forms the foundation for all subsequent post-exploitation activities.
