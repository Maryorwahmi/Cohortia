Data Exfiltration Fundamentals

Stealthily Extracting Valuable Information from Compromised Systems

In this chapter, you will learn how adversaries covertly transfer sensitive data from compromised networks, understand the critical techniques for detection evasion, and grasp the ethical responsibilities involved in simulating such attacks.

Key Idea

Data exfiltration is the unauthorized, stealthy, and reliable transfer of sensitive data from a target network to an attacker, demonstrating the full impact of a security compromise.
