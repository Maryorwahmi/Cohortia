Exfiltration Strategy

Strategic Approach: Execution & Cleanup

Ensuring Successful Data Transfer and Maintaining OpSec

Why Cleanup Matters

Removing traces prevents detection, preserves operational security, and ensures the longevity of your access or the stealth of your operation.

Key Takeaway

Execution and verification ensure data is successfully extracted, while meticulous cleanup is critical for maintaining stealth and avoiding forensic detection.

Step-by-Step Cards
01

Execute Exfiltration

Initiate the data transfer using the chosen channel (e.g., curl for HTTP POST, DNS tunneling tool).

02

Verify Receipt

Confirm that the data has been successfully received on your attacker machine and check its integrity (e.g., file size, hash, content preview).

03

Clean Up Traces

Remove any temporary files, tools, or logs generated during the exfiltration process from the compromised system to avoid detection.

Example: Verifying a Tarball

After receiving secret_data.tar.gz, use tar -tzvf secret_data.tar.gz to list its contents and ensure it's not corrupted.
