Key Concepts

Essential terms for understanding post-exploitation and maintaining access.

Post-Exploitation

The phase after initial access, focusing on achieving objectives like privilege escalation, data exfiltration, and maintaining access.

Persistence

Techniques used to maintain access to a compromised system across reboots, service restarts, or dropped connections.

Reverse Shell

A shell initiated from the target system that connects back to a listener on the attacker's machine.

Summary Takeaway

Post-exploitation is the strategic phase where you leverage initial access, with persistence being key to maintaining control, often achieved through reverse shells that bypass common network defenses.
