Define Post-Exploitation

Understand what post-exploitation entails and its critical role in the penetration testing lifecycle. This skill helps you frame your actions within a strategic context.

Identify Initial Steps

Learn the immediate actions to take after gaining a foothold, such as stabilizing your shell and performing initial reconnaissance. This ensures you maximize your initial access.

Verified Competency Milestone

Understand Persistence

Grasp the various techniques for establishing and maintaining persistent access to a compromised host. This is crucial for long-term control and avoiding loss of access.

Verified Competency Milestone

Implement Persistence

Apply basic reverse and bind shell persistence mechanisms on both Linux and Windows systems. This practical skill allows you to secure your access effectively.
