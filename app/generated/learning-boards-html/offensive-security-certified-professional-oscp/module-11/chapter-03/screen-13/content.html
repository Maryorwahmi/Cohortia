Troubleshooting

Common Challenges & Best Practices

Identify and overcome obstacles to ensure stable and secure network pivots.

Frequent Mistakes

  • Incorrect Port Numbers: Mismatched local/remote ports.
  • Firewall Blocking: Ingress/egress rules preventing connections.
  • Missing Tools/Permissions: Required binaries or root access unavailable.
  • Network Reachability: Jump host cannot reach the internal target.

Best Practices

  • Verify Connectivity: Use ping or nc -zv from jump host.
  • Check Permissions: Ensure necessary privileges for tools.
  • Clean Up: Always terminate tunnels and proxies after use.
  • Security Awareness: Avoid exposing new attack vectors.
Key Takeaway

Proactive verification and careful cleanup are critical for successful and responsible pivoting.

# 1. Verify Connectivity from JumpHost to Internal Target
ping 192.168.1.100
nc -zv 192.168.1.100 80
Confirm the jump host can reach the internal target on the desired port.
# 2. Common Error: Firewall Blocking
ssh: connect to host 10.0.0.5 port 22: Connection refused
This often indicates a firewall blocking SSH or the service port.
# 3. Cleanup Example (after SSH tunnel)
killall ssh
Ensure all tunnel processes are terminated on both machines.
