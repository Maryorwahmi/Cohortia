Local Port Forwarding (`-L`)

Access a service on a remote network from your local machine.

How it Works

  • You connect to the jump host via SSH.
  • SSH creates a tunnel from your local port.
  • Traffic is forwarded through the tunnel to the jump host.
  • Jump host then forwards to the target IP:Port on its network.

Scenario Example

Your AttackerMachine cannot directly reach InternalWeb (192.168.1.100:80). However, JumpHost (10.0.0.5) in the DMZ can. You want to browse InternalWeb from your local browser.

Key Takeaway

Local forwarding is ideal for accessing services behind a firewall as if they were on your localhost.
