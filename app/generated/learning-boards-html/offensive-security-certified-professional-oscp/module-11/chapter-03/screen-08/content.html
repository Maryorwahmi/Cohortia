SSH Tunneling

Remote Port Forwarding (`-R`)

Expose an internal service to your local machine, initiated from the jump host.

How it Works

  • Jump host initiates an SSH connection to your attacker machine.
  • SSH creates a tunnel from a port on your attacker machine.
  • Traffic to this port is forwarded through the tunnel to the jump host.
  • Jump host then forwards to the target IP:Port on its network.

Scenario Example

InternalDB (192.168.1.200:3306) is reachable by JumpHost (10.0.0.5). You want to access InternalDB from your AttackerMachine, but JumpHost must initiate the connection to you.

Key Takeaway

Remote forwarding is crucial when the jump host can initiate outbound connections to you, but you cannot initiate inbound connections to it.
