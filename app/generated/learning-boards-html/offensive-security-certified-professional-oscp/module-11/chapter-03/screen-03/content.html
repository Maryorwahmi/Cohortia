Utilize Chisel for SOCKS Proxying

Master the use of `chisel` to establish fast TCP/UDP tunnels and SOCKS proxies, enabling advanced port forwarding scenarios even when traditional SSH methods are not feasible. This tool offers significant flexibility.

Understand Socat & Netcat

Grasp the fundamental capabilities of `socat` and `netcat` for basic port redirection and creating simple data tunnels, providing essential alternatives for network manipulation in diverse environments. These are versatile command-line utilities.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
07

Identify Common Challenges

Recognize and troubleshoot common issues and mistakes encountered when setting up network pivots, such as firewall blocks, incorrect port numbers, or permission problems. This ensures successful and reliable tunneling operations.
