Socat & Netcat: Basic Port Redirection

Versatile command-line utilities for data stream manipulation and simple tunneling.

Socat: The Stream Relay

  • Establishes bidirectional data streams.
  • Supports TCP, UDP, stdio, pipe, exec, proxy, socks.
  • Highly flexible for complex tunneling.
  • Often used for advanced port forwarding.

Netcat: The TCP/IP Swiss Army Knife

  • Reads from and writes to network connections.
  • Simple for basic port redirection and listening.
  • Often pre-installed, making it readily available.
  • Less sophisticated than socat for complex tunnels.
Key Takeaway

Socat and Netcat are essential for lightweight, flexible port redirection and tunneling when SSH or Chisel are not viable.

# 1. Socat: Simple TCP Port Forwarding (from JumpHost)
socat TCP-LISTEN:8080,fork TCP:192.168.1.100:80
Listens on port 8080 on JumpHost, forwards to InternalWeb:80.
# 2. Netcat: Simple TCP Port Forwarding (from JumpHost)
mkfifo /tmp/f
nc -l -p 8080 < /tmp/f | nc 192.168.1.100 80 > /tmp/f
rm /tmp/f
A classic Netcat pipe for forwarding traffic, though more complex.
