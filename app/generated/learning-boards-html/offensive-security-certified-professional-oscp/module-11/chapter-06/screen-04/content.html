Core Concept

Choosing the Right Exfiltration Channel

Factors influencing channel selection for covert data movement

Network Egress Filtering

Identify allowed outbound ports and protocols (e.g., 80, 443, 53). Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) can detect unusual traffic patterns, even on allowed ports.

Available Tools on Target

Determine if `curl`, `wget`, `Invoke-WebRequest`, or other utilities are present. This dictates the commands and scripts you can execute for data transfer.

Key Takeaway

A thorough reconnaissance of network egress, available tools, data volume, and stealth requirements is essential for successful and undetected data exfiltration.

Data Volume & Type

Consider the size and sensitivity of the data. Large files may require chunking or faster channels, while sensitive data demands encryption or more covert methods.

Desired Level of Stealth

Determine how covert the operation needs to be. High stealth might mean slower, less common protocols, while lower stealth allows for faster, more direct methods.

Pro Tip

Always test your chosen exfiltration channel in a controlled environment to understand its behavior and detectability before attempting it on a live target.
