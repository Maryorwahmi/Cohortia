Avoiding Common Exfiltration Mistakes

Understanding pitfalls to ensure reliable and stealthy data exfiltration.

Ignoring Egress Filtering

Failing to check what outbound ports and protocols are allowed. This can lead to immediate blocking and detection.

Using Unencrypted Channels

Transferring sensitive data over plain HTTP or FTP exposes it to interception. Always encrypt data or use secure protocols.

Generating Excessive Traffic

Sending large volumes of data, especially over covert channels like DNS, creates unusual traffic patterns that trigger alerts.

Key Takeaway

Careful reconnaissance, encryption, and traffic management are crucial to minimize detection risk during data exfiltration operations.
