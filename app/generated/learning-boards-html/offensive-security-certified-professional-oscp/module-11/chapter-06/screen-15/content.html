Key Concepts: Covert & Traditional Channels

Understanding diverse exfiltration pathways.

ICMP Tunneling

A covert channel that encapsulates data within ICMP (ping) packets, allowing exfiltration when other channels are restricted.

FTP/SFTP

File Transfer Protocol and SSH File Transfer Protocol, standard protocols for transferring files, often used if outbound ports 20/21/22 are open.

Email Exfiltration

Sending data as attachments or in the body of an email via SMTP, often easily detected by email security gateways.

Summary Takeaway

The choice of exfiltration channel depends on network restrictions, desired stealth, and data volume, requiring a strategic approach to bypass defenses.
