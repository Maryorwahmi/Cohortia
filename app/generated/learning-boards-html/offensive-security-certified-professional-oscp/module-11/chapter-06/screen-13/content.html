Post-Exfiltration Cleanup and Stealth

The critical steps to remove artifacts and maintain a low profile after data exfiltration.

01

Remove Temporary Files

Delete any temporary files created on the target, such as credential dumps, encoded data chunks, or logs.

02

Delete Scripts & Binaries

Ensure all transferred or executed scripts and binaries (e.g., `icmpshell` client, `dnscat2` client) are removed from the target system.

03

Terminate Listeners

Stop all listener processes on your attacker machine (e.g., web servers, `dnscat2` server) to prevent further activity or detection.

04

Clear Command History

Attempt to clear command history on the target system to remove traces of executed commands, if feasible and safe to do so.

05

Verify No New Vulnerabilities

Confirm that your actions haven't inadvertently created new vulnerabilities or backdoors on the target system.

Key Takeaway

Thorough cleanup is paramount for operational security, preventing detection, and ensuring no lingering artifacts compromise future operations.
