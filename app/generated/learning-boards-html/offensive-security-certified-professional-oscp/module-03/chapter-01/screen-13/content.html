Strategic Prioritization

Contextual Prioritization of Vulnerabilities

Aligning risk with business impact and network placement.

Network Placement

Is the system internet-facing or internal? Publicly exposed systems carry higher risk.

Business Criticality

How vital is the system to business operations? Does it handle sensitive data or critical services?

Potential Impact

What would be the consequences of a successful exploit? Data breach, service disruption, reputational damage?

Scenario Card

A 'medium' severity vulnerability on an internet-facing web server handling customer data is often more critical than a 'high' severity vulnerability on an isolated internal development machine.

Key Takeaway

Effective prioritization requires considering a vulnerability's severity in conjunction with its context, including network exposure, business criticality, and potential impact.
