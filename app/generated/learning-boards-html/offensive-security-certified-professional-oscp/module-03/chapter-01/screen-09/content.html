Interpreting Vulnerability Scan Reports

Extracting actionable insights from automated findings.

Understand Report Structure

Reports typically include a summary, detailed findings, severity levels, and sometimes remediation suggestions. Familiarize yourself with these sections to navigate efficiently.

Identify Key Findings

Focus on critical and high-severity vulnerabilities first. These represent the most significant risks and potential entry points for exploitation.

Contextualize Risks

Consider the target system's role, network placement, and potential impact. A 'medium' vulnerability on a critical internet-facing server can be more severe than a 'high' one on an isolated internal machine.

Key Takeaway

Vulnerability scan reports are essential guides, but require careful analysis and critical thinking to differentiate between actual risks and potential noise.
