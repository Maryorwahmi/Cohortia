Risk Assessment

Severity Levels and Remediation Advice

Prioritizing risks based on impact and exploitability.

Critical

Immediate Threat

Directly exploitable, leading to full system compromise, remote code execution, or significant data loss. Requires urgent attention.

High

Significant Impact

Could lead to significant data exposure, denial of service, or privilege escalation. Requires prompt action.

Medium

Moderate Risk

May lead to information disclosure or minor privilege escalation under specific conditions. Address in a timely manner.

Low

Minor Impact

Often minor misconfigurations or informational findings with limited direct security impact. Address as resources allow.

Remediation Guidance

  • Scanners often suggest specific patches or configuration changes.
  • Use this advice as a starting point for your verification and mitigation strategy.
  • Always cross-reference with official vendor documentation.
  • Prioritize remediation based on severity and contextual risk.
Common Mistake

Blindly trusting remediation advice without manual verification can lead to unnecessary work or even system instability if the vulnerability is a false positive.

Key Takeaway

Severity levels provide a crucial framework for prioritizing vulnerabilities, but remediation advice must always be validated through manual verification.
