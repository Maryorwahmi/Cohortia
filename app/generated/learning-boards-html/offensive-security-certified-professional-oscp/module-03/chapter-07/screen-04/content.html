Defining Post-Exploitation

The critical phase after initial system access

What is Post-Exploitation?

It's the phase following initial access to a target system, where the attacker aims to deepen their control and understanding of the compromised environment. It's about moving beyond simply 'getting in'.

Primary Objectives

  • Explore: Understand system configuration, data, and network topology.
  • Escalate: Gain higher privileges (root/Administrator).
  • Persist: Maintain access over time, even after reboots.
  • Pivot: Use the host to access other network segments.

Why it Matters

Post-exploitation is crucial for demonstrating the true impact of a vulnerability. It shows what an attacker could realistically achieve, fulfilling engagement objectives beyond just initial compromise. Without it, the full risk is often underestimated.

Key Takeaway

Post-exploitation is the strategic phase where initial access is leveraged to achieve deeper control, broader network reach, and a comprehensive understanding of the target's security posture.
