The Art of Social Engineering

Successful delivery of client-side exploits relies heavily on social engineering to trick users into interacting with malicious content.

  • •Craft convincing phishing emails or messages.
  • •Host files on seemingly legitimate web servers.
  • •Leverage urgency, authority, or curiosity to bypass suspicion.
Key Takeaway

The human element is the weakest link; a well-crafted social engineering lure is often more critical than the technical sophistication of the exploit.
