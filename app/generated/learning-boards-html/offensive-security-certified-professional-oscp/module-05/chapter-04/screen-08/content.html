Burp Suite Intruder: Executing & Analyzing Attacks (Part 2)

Loading wordlists and interpreting results for successful exploitation

After setting up payload positions, the next steps involve loading your wordlists and carefully analyzing the attack results to identify successful login attempts or other vulnerabilities.

  • Load Payloads: Set `Payload type` to `Simple list` and load your wordlist (e.g., `rockyou.txt`).
  • Start Attack: Initiate the attack and observe the results in the Intruder window.
  • Analyze Responses: Look for differences in response length, status codes, or error messages to identify success.
Key Takeaway

Successful brute-forcing with Burp Intruder relies on careful payload selection and astute analysis of HTTP responses to pinpoint valid credentials.

Response Analysis Cues
Response Length: Significant difference often indicates a different page or outcome.
Status Codes: Look for 200 (success), 302 (redirect), or 401 (unauthorized).
Error Messages: Absence of 'Login failed' or presence of 'Welcome, user!'
Pro-Tip

Use the 'Grep - Match' feature in Intruder options to automatically flag responses containing specific strings (e.g., 'Login failed') for easier filtering.
