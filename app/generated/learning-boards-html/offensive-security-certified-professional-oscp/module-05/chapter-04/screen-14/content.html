Key Concepts: Authentication & Session Flaws

Essential terms and ideas to remember

Authentication

The process of verifying a user's identity.

Session Management

Maintaining a user's state and identity across multiple requests after authentication.

Brute-Force Attack

Systematically trying all possible combinations of credentials until the correct one is found.

Burp Suite Intruder

A Burp Suite tool for automating customized attacks, including brute-forcing and fuzzing.

Hydra

A command-line parallelized login cracker that supports numerous protocols.

Summary Takeaway

Authentication and session management are critical security components, and understanding their common flaws and exploitation techniques is fundamental for any penetration tester.
