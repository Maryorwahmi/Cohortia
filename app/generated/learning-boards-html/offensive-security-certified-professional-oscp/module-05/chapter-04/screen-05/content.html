Authentication Flaws

Weak Credentials: The Low-Hanging Fruit

How easily guessable passwords compromise security

Weak credentials are a primary entry point for attackers. When applications don't enforce strong password policies, users often choose simple, easily guessable passwords, making them vulnerable to automated attacks.

  • No Policy Enforcement: Applications fail to require strong passwords (length, complexity, uniqueness).
  • Susceptible to Attacks: Easily exploited by dictionary attacks and brute-forcing.
  • Impact: Unauthorized access, account takeover, and further system compromise.
Key Takeaway

Strong password policies are a fundamental defense against credential-based attacks and must be enforced by the application, not just relied upon by users.
