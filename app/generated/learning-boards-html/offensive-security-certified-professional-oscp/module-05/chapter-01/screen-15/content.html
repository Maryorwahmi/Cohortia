Key Concepts: The Pentesting Lifecycle

Phases and Ethical Practice

Information Gathering

The initial phase of collecting data about the target web application, including technologies, directories, and parameters.

Vulnerability Analysis & Exploitation

Systematically testing for weaknesses and then crafting payloads to leverage identified vulnerabilities and achieve objectives.

Ethical Hacking

Conducting security assessments with explicit permission, adhering to legal and ethical guidelines to ensure responsible practice.

Summary Takeaway

A comprehensive web application penetration test follows a systematic methodology, from information gathering to exploitation, always underpinned by strict ethical and legal adherence.
