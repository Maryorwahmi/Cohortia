Web Proxies: Intercepting and Manipulating Traffic

Burp Suite as the Industry Standard

Role of a Web Proxy

Acts as an intermediary between your browser and the web application, allowing for real-time inspection and modification of HTTP/S traffic.

  • Intercept Requests: Stop outgoing requests before they reach the server.
  • Modify Traffic: Change parameters, headers, or body content.
  • Analyze Responses: Inspect server replies for clues or vulnerabilities.
  • Observe Behavior: Understand how the application reacts to different inputs.

Example: Modifying a Price Parameter

When buying an item, a request might send price=100. Intercepting this with Burp, a tester could change it to price=1 and forward it, testing for price manipulation vulnerabilities.
