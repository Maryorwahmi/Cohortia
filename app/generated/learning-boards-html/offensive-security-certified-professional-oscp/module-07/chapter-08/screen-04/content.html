Why Credential Exploitation Matters

  • Common & Effective: Often more successful than exploiting complex system vulnerabilities.

  • Bridges Access: Transforms low-privileged shells into higher-privileged or root access.

  • Direct Control: Leads directly to full system control, bypassing traditional exploit chains.

Scenario Card

You've gained a shell as 'www-data'. Instead of finding a kernel exploit, you discover a user's SSH private key with weak permissions. This key could grant you direct access as that user, potentially leading to root.

Key Takeaway

Prioritize credential-based attacks as they are often the most efficient path to privilege escalation in real-world scenarios.
