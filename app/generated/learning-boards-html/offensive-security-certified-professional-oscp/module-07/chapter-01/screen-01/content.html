Linux Privilege Escalation & Enumeration Fundamentals

Your First Steps Towards Root Access

In the world of offensive security, gaining initial access is just the beginning. The real challenge, and often the ultimate goal, is to elevate your privileges on a compromised system. For Linux, this typically means moving from a low-privileged user, like one obtained through a web shell, all the way up to the powerful 'root' user. This isn't just about running a magic exploit; it's about understanding the system's inner workings, its configurations, and its potential weaknesses. Think of it as becoming a detective, meticulously gathering clues to unlock the system's deepest secrets. This chapter will equip you with the essential knowledge and commands to start this crucial process, laying the groundwork for more advanced techniques you'll encounter later. Let's begin by understanding why this process is so vital and what initial steps you need to take.

Key Idea
Privilege escalation is the process of gaining higher-level access on a system, often from a standard user to the administrator ('root') account, which is a primary objective in penetration testing.
