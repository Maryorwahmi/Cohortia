Learning Objectives

What You Will Master

01

Explain Privilege Escalation

Articulate the fundamental concept and importance of privilege escalation in penetration testing, recognizing its role as a key objective.

02

Identify Enumeration Commands

Recognize and utilize essential Linux commands for gathering system information during initial reconnaissance.

03

Differentiate User Contexts

Understand various user contexts and privilege levels on a Linux system, from standard users to root.

04

Establish Reconnaissance Approach

Develop a systematic approach to initial reconnaissance for effective privilege escalation.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Mastering foundational Linux enumeration and privilege escalation concepts.
