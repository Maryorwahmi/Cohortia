The 'Why' of Privilege Escalation

Beyond Just Gaining Access

01

Define Privilege Escalation

Understand that privilege escalation is the process of gaining elevated access, moving from a low-privileged user to a higher-privileged one, typically 'root' on Linux.

02

Recognize Root's Power

Grasping that 'root' access grants complete administrative control, enabling actions like data exfiltration and persistence.

03

Focus on Systemic Flaws

Appreciate that escalation exploits underlying misconfigurations or vulnerabilities, not just isolated exploits.

04

Embrace a Holistic View

Understand that effective privilege escalation requires a comprehensive understanding of the system, not just a single exploit.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

Understanding the strategic importance and goals of privilege escalation.
