Key Concepts

What to Remember

Privilege Escalation

Gaining elevated access, typically to the root user, is a primary objective.

Enumeration

Systematic information gathering is the crucial first step.

User Contexts

UIDs and GIDs define access; group memberships are key clues.

SUMMARY TAKEAWAY

Mastering enumeration and understanding user contexts are fundamental to successful Linux privilege escalation.
