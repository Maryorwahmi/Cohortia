Defensive Measures

Further Defenses and Troubleshooting

Addressing mount issues, permissions, and firewalls.

Incorrect Mount Options

Forgetting -o nolock or using incompatible NFS versions can lead to mounting failures. Always verify the correct options for your specific setup.

Permissions on Mount Point

Ensure the local mount point on your attacking machine has appropriate write permissions. Without them, you cannot create or modify files on the mounted share.

Firewall Rules

Firewalls can block NFS traffic (ports 111, 2049, and others for RPC). Verify that necessary ports are open on both the client and server to allow communication.

Cleanup After Exploitation

Always remove your malicious SUID binary and any other artifacts from the NFS share after a successful exploit to maintain stealth and clean up your tracks.

Key Takeaway

A robust NFS security posture requires attention to mount options, local permissions, firewall configurations, and diligent post-exploitation cleanup.
