External Reconnaissance

Querying NFS Exports with showmount

Identifying accessible shares from an attacking machine.

The showmount -e Command

This utility queries an NFS server to list its exported directories. It's a crucial tool for initial reconnaissance from an attacker's perspective, revealing potential targets.

Interpreting Output

  • Export List: Directories shared by the target.
  • Client Access: IP addresses, network ranges, or * (all clients).
  • Red Flag: An asterisk (*) indicates broad access, often a sign of misconfiguration.
Key Takeaway

showmount -e is your first line of defense and offense for identifying NFS shares and their accessibility from an external network.

Terminal: Attacking Machine (Kali)
kali@kali:~$ showmount -e 192.168.1.100
Export list for 192.168.1.100:
/home/shared_data 192.168.1.0/24
/var/www/html *
/opt/data 192.168.1.10(rw,no_root_squash)

Analysis:

  • /home/shared_data is restricted to a specific subnet.
  • /var/www/html is exported to all clients (*), making it widely accessible.
  • /opt/data is exported to a single IP with rw and the critical no_root_squash option.
