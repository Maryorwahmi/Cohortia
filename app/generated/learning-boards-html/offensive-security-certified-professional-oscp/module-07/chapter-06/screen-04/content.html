What are Weak Permissions?

Permissions that allow unauthorized users or groups to modify, delete, or execute critical system files or directories.

Why are they Overlooked?

Often overshadowed by more direct SUID/SGID or sudo misconfigurations, but equally potent for escalation.

Impact: Root Access

Modifying a critical file can lead to executing commands as a higher-privileged user, ultimately granting root access.

Key Takeaway

Thorough enumeration of file system permissions is the crucial first step in identifying these often-missed privilege escalation vectors.
