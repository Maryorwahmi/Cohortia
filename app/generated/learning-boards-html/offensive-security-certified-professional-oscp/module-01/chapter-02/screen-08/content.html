Navigating Virtual Network Modes

Controlling VM Communication and Isolation

Key Takeaway

Proper configuration of virtual network modes is essential for controlling VM communication and ensuring critical isolation of your penetration testing lab.

Why Network Isolation Matters

Accidentally exposing vulnerable VMs to your home network or the internet can lead to real-world compromises. Network modes are your primary defense.

Connectivity

How VMs interact with each other, the host, and external networks like the internet.

Isolation

The degree to which a VM is separated from other VMs, the host, and the physical network.

Network Address Translation (NAT)

VMs share the host's IP, accessing the internet but isolated from the local network.

Bridged Adapter

VMs act as full participants on the physical network, getting their own IP address.
