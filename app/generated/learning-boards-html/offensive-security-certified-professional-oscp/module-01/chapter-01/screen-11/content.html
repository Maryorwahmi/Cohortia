Penetration Test Phases

Phases 6 & 7: Covering Tracks and Reporting

Post-Exploitation Actions and Comprehensive Documentation

Covering Tracks

Simulating evidence removal (e.g., clearing logs) to assess a system's detection and logging capabilities, rather than hiding activity.

Reporting

Documenting all findings, vulnerabilities, exploitation steps, and actionable recommendations in a comprehensive report for the client.

The Importance of Reporting

A well-structured report translates complex technical findings into clear, actionable insights for the client. It should include an executive summary for management, detailed technical findings for engineers, and specific remediation steps. Effective communication ensures the client can improve their security posture based on your assessment.

Key Takeaway

The final report translates technical findings into actionable security improvements, making it the most critical phase for client value.
