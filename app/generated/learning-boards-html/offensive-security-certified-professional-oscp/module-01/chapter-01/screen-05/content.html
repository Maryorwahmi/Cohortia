Core Concept

Ethical Hacking vs. Malicious Hacking

The Critical Distinction of Permission and Intent

Ethical Hacking

Authorized simulation of cyberattacks to identify vulnerabilities and improve security, always with explicit permission.

Malicious Hacking

Unauthorized access to computer systems with harmful intent, such as data theft, damage, or service disruption.

The Role of Permission

Imagine a security professional hired by a bank to test its online banking system. They have a signed contract explicitly outlining what they can test. This is ethical hacking. In contrast, a criminal attempting to steal customer data from the same bank without any authorization is engaging in malicious hacking. The presence or absence of explicit permission is the fundamental legal and ethical differentiator.

Key Takeaway

Permission and intent define the legality and ethics of hacking; always operate within authorized boundaries.
