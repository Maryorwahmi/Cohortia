Execution Steps

Payload Construction

Concatenate junk, RETN, NOP sled, and shellcode into a single buffer.

Socket Connection

Establish a TCP connection to the target service using Python's socket module.

Send Payload

Transmit the crafted payload over the established socket connection.

Netcat Listener

Crucially, ensure your netcat listener is active on your attacking machine to catch the reverse shell.

Key Takeaway

The exploit script automates the delivery of the payload, but requires a pre-configured listener to receive the shell.
