Module 4›Chapter 7›Diagnosing Exploit Issues: Offset, Bad Chars, JMP ESP

Incorrect Offset

Even a single byte off can cause the exploit to crash without reaching your shellcode. Use pattern_offset.rb meticulously.

Troubleshooting Tip

Re-verify the exact offset to EIP using your pattern generation tool and debugger.

Bad Characters

A single bad character can prematurely terminate or corrupt your payload, preventing shellcode execution.

Troubleshooting Tip

Re-run your bad character analysis and ensure all identified bad bytes are excluded from your shellcode.

Wrong JMP ESP Address

The address must be a valid JMP ESP in a reliable module (no ASLR/rebase). Incorrect addresses lead to crashes.

Troubleshooting Tip

Verify the address in Immunity Debugger and ensure it's correctly converted to little-endian.

Key Takeaway

Systematic verification of these fundamental exploit components is the first step in effective debugging.
