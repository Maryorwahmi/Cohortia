Understanding Bind Shells

Target Listens for Attacker's Incoming Connection

How it Works

  • Shellcode on target opens a listening port.
  • Attacker connects to this port on the target.
  • A command channel is established.

When to Use

Direct Access

Target is directly reachable by attacker.

Internal Networks

Useful post-compromise for internal pivoting.

Attacker Connects to Target

The attacker initiates the connection to the target's open port, requiring direct network access to the target.

Key Takeaway

Bind shells are effective when the target is directly accessible, but they are often blocked by perimeter firewalls due to their inbound nature.
