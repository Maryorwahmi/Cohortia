NOP Sleds: Creating a Landing Zone

Increasing Exploit Robustness with No-Operation Instructions

What is a NOP Sled?

  • A sequence of No-Operation (NOP) instructions.
  • In x86, a NOP is `\x90`.
  • Placed before the actual shellcode.

Purpose & Benefit

Creates a 'landing zone' for EIP. If EIP jumps into the sled, it slides down to the shellcode, providing a margin of error and increasing exploit reliability.

Guiding EIP to Success

The NOP sled acts as a buffer, ensuring that even slightly misaligned jumps still lead to shellcode execution.

Key Takeaway

NOP sleds are sequences of `\x90` instructions that create a buffer before shellcode, significantly increasing exploit reliability by providing a larger target area for EIP.
