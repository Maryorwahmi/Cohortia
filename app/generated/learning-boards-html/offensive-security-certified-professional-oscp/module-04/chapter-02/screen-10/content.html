Fuzzing Safety and Ethics

Performing fuzzing responsibly in a lab environment.

1. Isolated Environment: Always conduct fuzzing within a controlled lab setup (e.g., VMs) disconnected from production networks.

2. Explicit Permission: Never fuzz systems you do not own or have explicit, written authorization to test.

3. Potential Disruptions: Be aware that fuzzing can cause denial of service, data corruption, or system instability.

4. Preparedness: Be ready to restart target applications and debuggers frequently.
