Mistake 1: Over-Reliance on Single Control

Believing one strong security product (e.g., AV) is sufficient, neglecting a layered approach.

Impact: Leaves critical gaps in defense, allowing attackers to bypass the single point of failure.

Mistake 2: Neglecting Patching & Updates

Failing to regularly update client applications and operating systems, leaving known vulnerabilities unaddressed.

Impact: Exposes systems to easily exploitable, well-documented vulnerabilities.

Best Practices for Robust Defense

  • Implement Defense-in-Depth: Combine technical controls (firewalls, AV/EDR, CSP) with user education.
  • Regular Patch Management: Establish a consistent schedule for applying security updates to all software and systems.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Use EDR and SIEM solutions to detect and respond to suspicious activity in real-time.
  • User Training & Testing: Conduct ongoing awareness programs and simulated phishing campaigns.
Key Takeaway

A strong defense against client-side attacks requires a holistic, continuously updated, and layered security strategy that addresses both technical and human vulnerabilities.
