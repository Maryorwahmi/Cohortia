What is Defense-in-Depth?

A security strategy employing multiple, overlapping controls to protect assets. It assumes no single defense is foolproof.

Why it Matters

Client-side attacks are diverse. Layered defenses increase the complexity and cost for attackers, making successful breaches less likely.

Key Layers of Defense

Browser Security: XSS filters, SOP, CSP to control web content.

Email & Endpoint: Filters, AV/EDR to block malicious files and links.

User Awareness: Training to identify phishing and suspicious activity.

Network Security: Firewalls, IDS/IPS to filter malicious traffic.

Key Takeaway

A robust defense-in-depth strategy integrates multiple security layers to create a resilient barrier against client-side attacks, making it harder for attackers to succeed.
