Email Filters & Gateways

  • Purpose: Detect and block phishing emails, malicious attachments, and suspicious links.
  • Techniques: Sender reputation checks, content analysis, sandboxing attachments.
  • Example: Blocking an email with a `.exe` attachment or a link to a known phishing site.

Antivirus (AV) & EDR

  • Antivirus (AV): Detects and prevents execution of known malicious files using signature-based detection.
  • Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR): Monitors endpoint activity for suspicious behavior, identifies threats, and enables rapid response.
  • Example: AV might block a virus, while EDR detects a legitimate tool being abused (LOLBin).

Defense Flow: Email to Endpoint

1. Email Arrives: Filter scans for threats.

2. User Interaction: If malicious content reaches user, AV/EDR monitors.

3. Threat Neutralized: AV/EDR prevents execution or alerts security team.

Key Takeaway

Email and endpoint security solutions provide critical layers of defense, acting as gatekeepers and monitors to prevent and detect malicious activity on user devices.
