XSS Assessment: Identifying Types and Evasion

Applying Your Knowledge to Real-World Scenarios

Scenario 1: Stored XSS & Evasion

An attacker injects <img src=x onerror=alert(1)> into a comment section, and the alert appears for other users.

  • Question: What type of XSS is this, and why is onerror used?
  • Answer: This is Stored XSS. The payload is saved and executed for multiple users. onerror is used because it triggers JavaScript on error (e.g., broken image), often bypassing <script> tag filters.

Scenario 2: CSP Impact on BeEF Hook

A reflected XSS is found on a login page with CSP: script-src 'self' cdn.example.com;

  • Question: How does CSP impact a BeEF hook, and what's a bypass strategy?
  • Answer: CSP prevents the BeEF hook from loading from your Kali IP (not whitelisted). An advanced bypass involves finding XSS on cdn.example.com or uploading hook.js there if possible.
Key Takeaway

Effective XSS assessment requires understanding the different types of XSS, common evasion techniques, and the impact of security mechanisms like Content Security Policy on exploitation.
