Chapter Review

Key Concepts

Essential Terms to Remember

Social Engineering

The psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information, often used to facilitate client-side attacks.

Ethical Hacking

Performing penetration tests with explicit permission and within defined legal and ethical boundaries, ensuring responsible and authorized security assessments.

Vulnerable Client Software

Applications like web browsers, email clients, and document readers that contain exploitable flaws, serving as primary targets for client-side attacks.

Summary Takeaway

Effective client-side security requires understanding social engineering tactics, adhering to ethical hacking principles, and recognizing vulnerabilities in client software.
