Ethical Hacking

Ethical Boundaries: Client-Side Penetration Testing

Responsibility and Professional Conduct

  • •

    Explicit Consent: Always obtain clear, written authorization from the target organization.

  • •

    Defined Scope: Clearly outline attack vectors, target individuals/groups, and expected outcomes.

  • •

    No Harm: Primary goal is vulnerability identification, not causing damage or violating privacy.

Key Takeaway

Ethical client-side penetration testing demands explicit, written consent and a clearly defined scope to ensure legal compliance and responsible practice.

Ethical Testing Framework
Obtain Written Consent

Legal authorization for testing activities.

Define Clear Scope

Specify targets, attack types, and expected outcomes.

Prioritize No Harm

Avoid unintended damage or privacy violations.

Example

Before a phishing test, get written approval specifying the email content, target employee group, and the exact time window for the campaign.
