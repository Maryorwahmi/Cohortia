Manipulating Trust: Common Social Engineering Tactics

Exploiting Curiosity, Urgency, and Fear

  • •

    Urgency/Fear: Creating a sense of immediate danger or missed opportunity to prompt quick action.

  • •

    Curiosity/Reward: Enticing users with promises of gain or intriguing content to click malicious links.

  • •

    Impersonation: Pretending to be a trusted entity (e.g., IT, bank) to gain credibility and bypass scrutiny.

Key Takeaway

Social engineering leverages human psychology to bypass technical controls, making users unwitting participants in their own compromise.
