Core Concept

The Credential Harvesting Lifecycle

From Reconnaissance to System Access

1. Reconnaissance
2. Phishing Campaign
3. Credential Capture
4. Impersonation

Key Stages

  • Identify targets and services.
  • Craft convincing phishing lures.
  • Deploy fake login pages.
  • Capture submitted credentials.
  • Use credentials for unauthorized access.

Why it Matters

Understanding the full lifecycle allows for more effective attack planning and robust defensive strategies. Each stage presents opportunities for both offense and defense.

Concrete Example

An attacker researches a company's VPN provider, clones its login page, and sends a phishing email to employees. When an employee enters their credentials, they are captured, and the attacker then uses them to access the corporate network.

Key Takeaway

Credential harvesting is a structured, multi-phase attack. Success hinges on meticulous planning and execution across all stages, from initial reconnaissance to the final act of impersonation.
