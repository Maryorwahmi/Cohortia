Attack Impact

Real-World Impact of Impersonation

Unlocking Sensitive Data and Expanding Attack Surface

Email Access

Gain access to internal communications, sensitive documents, and potentially other credentials. Can be used for Business Email Compromise (BEC).

Cloud Services (OneDrive/SharePoint)

Access, download, or modify sensitive company files, leading to data breaches or intellectual property theft.

Internal Applications

Gain access to HR systems, customer databases, or financial platforms, depending on the compromised user's privileges.

Social Media / Public Platforms

Used for further social engineering, spreading misinformation, or damaging the victim's reputation and trust.

Concrete Example

An attacker impersonates a finance department employee to send a fraudulent invoice to a vendor, leveraging the trusted identity to trick the vendor into making an unauthorized payment.

Key Takeaway

Impersonation significantly amplifies an attacker's capabilities, granting access to sensitive data and systems. This can lead to severe consequences, including data breaches, financial fraud, and reputational damage.
