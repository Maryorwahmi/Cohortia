Core Concept

The Art of Human Manipulation

Understanding advanced social engineering beyond basic phishing

What is Social Engineering?

It's the psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information. It exploits human trust, curiosity, and helpfulness.

Beyond Basic Phishing

Advanced tactics go deeper than mass emails, focusing on targeted individuals and exploiting specific psychological triggers for higher success rates.

OSCP Relevance

Crucial for simulating realistic attacks and designing comprehensive defense strategies, often combined with technical exploits to create potent attack chains.

Key Takeaway

Human vulnerabilities are often the weakest link; understanding social engineering is paramount for both offense and defense.

Scenario: Blended Attack

An attacker uses a convincing phone call (social engineering) to trick an employee into clicking a malicious link, which then deploys malware (technical exploit) to gain access to the internal network. This shows how human and technical vulnerabilities combine.

Exploiting the Human-Tech Interface

Common Misconception

Social engineering only targets 'unaware' users. In reality, even security-conscious individuals can be manipulated under specific conditions like urgency or perceived authority.
