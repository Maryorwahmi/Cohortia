Key Concepts to Remember

Essential definitions from this chapter

Pretexting

Creating a fabricated scenario to trick a target into divulging information or performing an action.

Baiting

Luring targets with a tempting offer (e.g., a malicious USB drive) to initiate an attack.

Quid Pro Quo

Offering a benefit or service in exchange for information or access.

Chapter Summary

Advanced social engineering tactics exploit human psychology through fabricated scenarios, tempting lures, and deceptive exchanges, making awareness and verification critical defenses.
