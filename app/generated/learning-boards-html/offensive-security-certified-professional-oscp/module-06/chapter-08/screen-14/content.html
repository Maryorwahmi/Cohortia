Key Concepts to Remember

Essential definitions from this chapter

Water Holing

Compromising a website frequently visited by a target group to infect them when they visit.

Tailgating

Gaining unauthorized access to a restricted area by following an authorized person.

Psychological Principles

Fundamental human behavioral traits exploited by social engineers (e.g., authority, urgency, liking).

Chapter Summary

Advanced social engineering leverages indirect attacks like water holing and tailgating, fundamentally relying on psychological principles to manipulate human behavior.
