Defense Strategy

Countermeasures: Physical Security & Incident Response

Securing the physical perimeter and preparing for attacks

Strong Physical Security

Implement access control systems, visitor logs, and clear policies for challenging unauthorized individuals to counter tailgating and other physical threats.

Incident Response Plan

A well-defined plan for social engineering attacks, including clear reporting mechanisms, ensures prompt investigation and mitigation of suspicious activities.

Example: Challenging Unknowns

An employee notices an unfamiliar person without a badge in a restricted area. Following company policy, they politely approach and ask, 'Can I help you find someone, or are you lost?' This simple act can deter an attacker.

Empowering Employees

Common Mistake

Failing to integrate physical and digital security strategies. Attackers will exploit the weakest link, whether it's a software vulnerability or an unlocked door.

Key Takeaway

Comprehensive security requires a holistic approach, integrating physical and digital defenses with a proactive incident response plan.
