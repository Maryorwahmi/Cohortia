Key Aspects of Credential Harvesting
  • Real-time Capture: SEToolkit displays submitted credentials instantly in the terminal.
  • Victim Redirection: After submission, the victim is often redirected to the actual legitimate site to avoid suspicion.
  • Ethical Boundaries: Always ensure explicit, written permission for all testing activities.
Common Mistake

Failing to ensure your Kali machine's IP address is publicly accessible or that necessary ports (80/443) are open, preventing external targets from reaching your hosted phishing page.

Key Takeaway

Successful credential harvesting relies on both the deceptive setup and ensuring network accessibility, with careful attention to ethical considerations and common technical pitfalls.
