Crafting a Deceptive Email

1

Spoof Sender Address

Falsify the 'From' field to appear as a trusted entity.

2

Enticing Subject Line

Use urgency or curiosity to compel the recipient to open.

3

Malicious Link/Attachment

Embed a link to a fake site or an infected file.

4

Call to Action

Direct the victim to click the link or open the attachment.

Key Takeaway

Effective phishing emails combine spoofing, psychological triggers, and malicious payloads to trick recipients into compromising their information.
