Key Concepts

Foundational Terms in Phishing Attacks

Phishing

A social engineering attack where attackers impersonate a trustworthy entity to trick victims into revealing sensitive information.

Spear Phishing

A highly targeted phishing attack customized for a specific individual or organization, leveraging personal information.

Email Spoofing

Falsifying the sender address of an email to make it appear as if it originated from a legitimate source.

Summary Takeaway

Phishing and spear phishing are distinct but related social engineering attacks, both relying on deception and often employing email spoofing to initiate their malicious campaigns.
