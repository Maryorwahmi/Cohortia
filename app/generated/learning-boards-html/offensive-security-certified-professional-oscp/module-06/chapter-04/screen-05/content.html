Phishing (Broad)

Generic, High-Volume Attacks

  • Target: Large, undifferentiated groups.
  • Personalization: Minimal or none; generic greetings.
  • Reconnaissance: Limited, focuses on common vulnerabilities.
  • Lures: General alerts (e.g., 'Account suspended,' 'Password reset').
  • Success Rate: Lower percentage, relies on volume.
Example

An email sent to thousands of users, claiming to be from a popular social media platform, asking everyone to 'verify their account activity.'

Spear Phishing (Targeted)

Personalized, High-Impact Threats

  • Target: Specific individuals or organizations.
  • Personalization: Extensive; uses job roles, interests, internal jargon.
  • Reconnaissance: Deep, gathers personal and organizational data.
  • Lures: Highly relevant to target (e.g., 'Project X update,' 'CEO request').
  • Success Rate: Significantly higher due to authenticity.
Example

An email to a finance manager, appearing from their CEO, referencing a specific, ongoing company acquisition and requesting an urgent wire transfer.

Key Takeaway

Spear phishing leverages deep personalization to bypass defenses, making it a more potent threat than generic phishing due to its tailored approach.
