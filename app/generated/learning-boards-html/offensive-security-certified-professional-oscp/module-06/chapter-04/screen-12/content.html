Delivering the Phishing Link

SEToolkit URL

Obtain the hosted phishing page URL from SEToolkit.

Craft Email

Design a compelling email body with psychological triggers.

Send to Target

Deliver the email with the embedded malicious link.

Example Email Snippet

"Your Microsoft 365 account requires immediate verification. Click here: http://YOUR_KALI_IP/ to avoid service interruption."

Key Takeaway

The delivery phase connects the technical setup of the cloned site with the social engineering aspect, using a crafted email to entice the victim to click the malicious link.
