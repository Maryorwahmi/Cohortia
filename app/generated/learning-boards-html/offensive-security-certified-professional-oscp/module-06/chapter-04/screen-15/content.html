Key Concepts

Tools and Techniques for Credential Harvesting

Credential Harvesting

The act of collecting usernames and passwords, often through fake login pages.

SEToolkit

An open-source penetration testing framework designed for social engineering attacks, including phishing.

Cloned Website

A replica of a legitimate website created by an attacker to trick users into believing they are on the authentic site.

Summary Takeaway

Credential harvesting is the goal, achieved through tools like SEToolkit that facilitate the creation of convincing cloned websites to deceive users.
