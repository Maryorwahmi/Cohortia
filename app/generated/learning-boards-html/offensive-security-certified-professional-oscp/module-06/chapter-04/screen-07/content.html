How Credential Harvesting Works

Phishing Email

Contains a malicious link.

Malicious Link

Redirects to a cloned website.

Cloned Login Page

Mimics a legitimate service to capture credentials.

Example Scenario

A user receives an email about 'urgent account verification' for their Microsoft 365. Clicking the link takes them to a pixel-perfect fake login page, where they enter their credentials, which are then stolen.

Key Takeaway

Cloned websites are critical for credential harvesting, as they provide a convincing facade that tricks users into voluntarily submitting their sensitive information to attackers.
