Key Concepts

Advanced Credential Harvesting Terminology

LSA Secrets

Encrypted secrets in the Registry containing cached domain credentials and service passwords.

hashdump

A technique or tool (e.g., Metasploit module) to extract password hashes from SAM and LSA secrets.

secretsdump.py

An Impacket tool used to dump hashes from SAM and LSA secrets, often requiring extracted Registry hives.

Credential Harvesting

The process of collecting authentication material from a compromised system for lateral movement or privilege escalation.

Summary Takeaway

These concepts represent advanced tools and techniques crucial for comprehensive credential harvesting and post-exploitation analysis.
