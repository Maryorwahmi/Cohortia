Core Concept

Credential Harvesting: The Post-Exploitation Goal

Extracting authentication material for lateral movement and privilege escalation.

What is it? The process of collecting usernames, passwords, hashes, and other authentication material from a compromised system.

Why it matters: Enables lateral movement to other machines and privilege escalation on the current system.

What Windows stores: Password hashes, cleartext passwords (in specific scenarios), and authentication tokens.

Key Takeaway

Credential harvesting is the strategic collection of authentication data from a compromised system, crucial for expanding access and control within a target environment.
