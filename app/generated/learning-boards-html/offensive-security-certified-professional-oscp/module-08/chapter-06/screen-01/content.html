Module 8.6

Stored Credentials & Password Mining

Uncovering Authentication Secrets on Windows Systems

This chapter will guide you through the critical process of identifying and extracting sensitive authentication material from various locations on Windows systems, a key step in post-exploitation.

Key Takeaway

The ability to discover and extract stored credentials is a fundamental skill in post-exploitation, enabling lateral movement and privilege escalation within a compromised network.

Credential Discovery

Locate hashes, cleartext passwords, and tokens across the system.

Tool Application

Utilize specialized tools like Mimikatz and secretsdump.py for extraction.

Lateral Movement

Leverage extracted credentials to gain access to other systems or elevate privileges.
