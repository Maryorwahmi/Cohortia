Ethical Handling of Sensitive Data

Best practices for managing and disposing of extracted credentials during an engagement.

  • •Handle extracted credentials with extreme care and adhere to strict ethical guidelines.
  • •Store sensitive data in encrypted, access-controlled environments.
  • •Delete all extracted credentials immediately and securely after the engagement, or as per client policy.
  • •Misuse or improper handling can lead to severe legal and ethical repercussions.
Key Takeaway

Ethical penetration testing demands extreme care in handling extracted credentials, ensuring they are secured, used only as authorized, and promptly deleted post-engagement.
