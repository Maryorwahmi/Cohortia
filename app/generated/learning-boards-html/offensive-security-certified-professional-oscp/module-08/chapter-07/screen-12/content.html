Executing the DLL Hijack

Transferring and Triggering Your Malicious Payload

The Three-Step Exploitation

  • •Create Malicious DLL: Develop your custom DLL with the desired payload (covered in previous screens).
  • •Transfer DLL: Place your crafted DLL into the identified vulnerable directory on the target system.
  • •Trigger Process: Start or restart the high-privileged application that will load your malicious DLL.
Key Takeaway

Successful DLL hijacking relies on precise placement of the malicious DLL and correctly triggering the vulnerable high-privileged process.

Example Scenario

After compiling missing.dll, you transfer it to C:\VulnerableApp\. You then restart the legit.exe service, which loads your DLL, granting you a SYSTEM shell on your Kali listener.
