Unmasking Service Exploitation

Elevating Privileges Through Windows Service Vulnerabilities

Windows services are background processes crucial for system operations. Misconfigurations in these services often create critical vulnerabilities that attackers can exploit to gain elevated privileges, making them a prime target in penetration testing.

Key Idea: Privilege Escalation

Exploiting misconfigured Windows services is a classic and highly effective method for privilege escalation, often leading to SYSTEM-level access.
