Chaining Exploits for Multi-Stage Attacks

Connecting Vulnerabilities for Deeper Compromise

The Reality of Escalation

Rarely does a single, direct exploit lead from initial low-privilege access to SYSTEM. Most real-world scenarios require a sequence of steps.

Why Chain?

Chaining allows you to:

  • Overcome patched vulnerabilities.
  • Progressively gain higher access.
  • Navigate complex, segmented environments.
  • Increase reliability and stealth.
Key Takeaway

Chaining exploits is essential for advanced privilege escalation, as it allows for a progressive and adaptable approach to system compromise.

The Chaining Mindset

1. Initial Access

Gain a low-privileged shell.

2. Local Enumeration

Identify vulnerabilities (e.g., weak service, UAC bypass).

3. Intermediate Escalation

Elevate to Administrator (e.g., UAC bypass).

4. Final SYSTEM Access

Achieve ultimate control (e.g., psexec, Mimikatz).
