Key Concepts: Tools & Advanced Chaining

Practical Applications and Strategic Thinking

fodhelper.exe

A legitimate Windows utility that can be abused for UAC bypass by manipulating specific Registry keys it consults.

psexec

A Sysinternals utility that allows execution of commands on local or remote systems, often used to launch processes as SYSTEM.

Chaining Exploits

Combining multiple vulnerabilities and techniques in a sequence to achieve a more significant impact, such as escalating from low-privilege to SYSTEM.

Summary Takeaway

Effective privilege escalation often involves leveraging specific tools like fodhelper.exe and psexec within a strategic chain of exploits.
