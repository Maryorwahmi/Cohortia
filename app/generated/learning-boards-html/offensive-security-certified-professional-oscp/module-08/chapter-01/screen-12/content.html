Identifying Running Processes and Services: tasklist /svc

Uncovering services running with high privileges or unusual configurations.

C:\Users\User>tasklist /svc

What to Look For

  • Services running as SYSTEM: High-privilege services are prime targets for misconfiguration or exploit.
  • Unusual Service Names: Custom or third-party services might have known vulnerabilities.
  • Services with Writable Paths: If a service executable path is writable by a low-privileged user, it's a potential hijack.
  • Outdated Services: Services from old software versions might have unpatched flaws.

Pro Tip: Service Permissions

After identifying interesting services, investigate their permissions using sc qc <service_name> or tools like AccessChk to find writable configurations.

Key Takeaway

tasklist /svc helps identify high-privileged or vulnerable services that can be leveraged for privilege escalation.
