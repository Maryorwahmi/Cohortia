Your Current Context: whoami /priv

Understanding your user, groups, and assigned privileges.

C:\Users\User>whoami /priv

Key Privileges to Watch For

  • SeDebugPrivilege: Allows debugging processes, potentially leading to SYSTEM access.
  • SeImpersonatePrivilege: Crucial for token impersonation attacks (e.g., Potato attacks).
  • SeAssignPrimaryTokenPrivilege: Allows assigning a primary token to a process.

Why it's Important

These privileges can be directly abused or leveraged in conjunction with other vulnerabilities to escalate privileges to Administrator or SYSTEM. Identifying them early can save significant time.

Key Takeaway

Always start by checking your current privileges with whoami /priv to identify immediate privilege escalation opportunities.
