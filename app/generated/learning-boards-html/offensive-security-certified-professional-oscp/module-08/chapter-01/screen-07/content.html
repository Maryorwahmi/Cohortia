Methodology

The Critical Role of Enumeration

Systematically collecting data to identify vulnerabilities.

OS Version & Build

Crucial for identifying kernel exploits.

Installed Patches

Missing updates indicate known vulnerabilities.

Running Services

Identify services with unusual privileges or configurations.

User Accounts

Identify local users, groups, and their memberships.

Network Configs

Look for unusual ports, open shares, or misconfigured firewalls.

Installed Applications

Identify outdated software with known vulnerabilities.

Key Takeaway

Thorough enumeration is the most critical phase, providing the necessary intelligence to identify and exploit privilege escalation vulnerabilities.
