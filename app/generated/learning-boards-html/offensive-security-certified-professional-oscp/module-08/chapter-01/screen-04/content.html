Core Concept

What is Privilege Escalation?

The methodical process of gaining elevated access rights on a system.

1

Initial Foothold

Gain basic, often low-privileged, access to a target system.

2

Identify Weaknesses

Uncover misconfigurations, vulnerable software, or OS flaws that can be exploited.

3

Elevate Privileges

Exploit identified weaknesses to move from a low-privileged user to Administrator or SYSTEM.

Why it Matters

  • Achieve full control over the target system, enabling deeper reconnaissance and impact.
  • Access sensitive data, install persistent backdoors, or pivot to other systems.
  • Often the most challenging and rewarding phase of a penetration test.

Common Weaknesses

Misconfigured Services
Vulnerable Software
Unpatched OS Flaws
Weak Permissions
Key Takeaway

Privilege escalation is a systematic process to gain higher access, crucial for achieving full control over a compromised system.
