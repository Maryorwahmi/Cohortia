Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › What to Remember
Resource baseline
Assign at least 2GB RAM, 2 CPUs, and 20–25GB disk for a usable RHEL lab VM.
Snapshots
Snapshot after a clean install and before risky changes to recover quickly.
Network modes
Use NAT for outbound access, Bridged for inbound reachability, and Host-Only for isolated labs.
Final takeaway
Apply these three practices to ensure your RHEL lab is reliable, recoverable, and configured for the RHCSA tasks you will practice and be tested on.
