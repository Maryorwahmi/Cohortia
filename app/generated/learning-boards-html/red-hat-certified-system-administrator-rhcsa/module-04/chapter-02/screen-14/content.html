Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
NTP
Protocol for clock synchronization across systems to maintain consistent timestamps.
chronyd
Daemon that synchronizes system clock on RHEL and is resilient to intermittent networks.
chronyc
Command-line utility to monitor chronyd: use sources and tracking to understand synchronization state.
Final takeaway
Configure services through their management tools, then verify with diagnostic commands. That pattern prevents persistent misconfiguration and makes troubleshooting predictable.
