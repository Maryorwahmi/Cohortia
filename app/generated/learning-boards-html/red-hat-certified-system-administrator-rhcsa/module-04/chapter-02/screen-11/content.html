Core Concept

Why NTP matters

Authentication, certificate validation, logging, and distributed systems depend on accurate and consistent clocks across hosts.

Concrete example
If clocks drift, Kerberos tickets may be rejected and SSL certificates may appear invalid.
Misconception
Manually setting the clock is a temporary fix. A synchronized NTP client maintains alignment automatically.
Recommended client on RHEL
chronyd — configured via /etc/chrony.conf and controlled with systemctl.
Key Takeaway

Treat time as foundational. Use chronyd to keep clocks aligned so security and logging work reliably.
