Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Key Concepts — part 1
NetworkManager
Daemon that manages devices and connection profiles on RHEL.
nmcli
Scriptable CLI for creating and controlling connection profiles.
nmtui
Curses-based interactive editor for quick terminal edits of connections.
Summary

Choose nmcli for automation, nmtui for guided interactive setup, and remember NetworkManager is the persistent controller.
