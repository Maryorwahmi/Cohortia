Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › firewalld — zones and services
Zones explained
  • public: for untrusted networks; only explicitly allowed services are accessible.
  • internal: for trusted networks where more services may be allowed.
  • dmz: for servers exposed to the internet but isolated from internal networks.
Service management
To allow a service in a zone, use firewall-cmd with --add-service and consider --permanent followed by --reload to apply persistent changes.
Concrete example
To allow HTTP permanently in the public zone: add the http service with --permanent and then reload firewalld to apply changes.
Common mistake
Forgetting --permanent or forgetting to reload after a permanent change are the most common sources of confusion.
Key takeaway
Always decide if a change should be temporary or permanent and confirm with firewall-cmd --list-services for the zone.
