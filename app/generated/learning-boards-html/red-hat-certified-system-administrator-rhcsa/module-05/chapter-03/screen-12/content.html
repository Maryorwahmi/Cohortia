Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Troubleshooting Patterns
Checklist
  • Run systemctl status for the service and read embedded journal lines.
  • List processes with ps aux and confirm PID and owner before signaling.
  • Watch resource trends with top or htop to distinguish spikes from leaks.
  • Use journalctl -u <unit> --since or -f when reproducing issues.
  • If the unit manages the process, prefer systemctl stop/restart over kill.
Avoid
Reaching immediately for kill -9 or killall without checking service ownership and logs first.
Do
Follow Observe → Request → Verify → Persist and confirm effects with status and journal entries.
