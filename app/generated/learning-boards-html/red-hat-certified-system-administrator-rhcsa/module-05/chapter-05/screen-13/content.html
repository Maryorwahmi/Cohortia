Assessment Reasoning & Diagnosis
Translate outputs into actions
State the evidence, the hypothesis it supports, and your remediation and follow-up plan.
Example assessments
High Swap
Evidence: free -h shows large swap used, available low. Hypothesis: RAM shortage. Fix: increase RAM or reduce app memory; follow up with monitoring and memory profiling.
Cannot create files
Evidence: df -h shows free blocks, but df -i shows 100% inode usage. Hypothesis: inode exhaustion. Fix: delete many small files or reformat with more inodes; follow up with cleanup automation.
Key Takeaway

Assessment requires listing evidence, proposing a hypothesis, and giving a remediation plus a monitoring or prevention plan.
