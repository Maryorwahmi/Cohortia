Disk I/O iostat -x
Assess device utilization
Look for %util near 100% and long await times to identify saturated storage.
Sample iostat -x 1 output
Device            r/s    w/s   rkB/s  wkB/s  await  %util
sda               5.0   40.0  1024.0 8192.0 35.2   98.3
sdb               0.1    0.2    10.0   12.0  2.1    1.2
Here sda is nearly saturated at 98% util with high await; sdb is mostly idle. This suggests moving workload off sda or investigating high-write behavior.
Interpretation checklist
  • %util ~100% → device busy, probable I/O bottleneck.
  • High await → long I/O latency, user-facing slowdown likely.
  • High w/s with low %util → device handles throughput well; check IOPS vs block size.
Pro Tip

Sample repeatedly and compare devices; one busy disk can dominate a server's performance even if others are idle.
