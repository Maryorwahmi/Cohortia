What to Remember
Actionable checklist
Privilege

Always use sudo for installs, updates, and removals to ensure proper access and auditability.

Review

Read DNF’s transaction summary before accepting changes to avoid accidental removals or undesirable upgrades.

Repositories

Enable third-party repos only when needed and disable them afterward to prevent long-term instability.

Keep this checklist near your terminal while you practice the hands-on activity: search, install, verify, and clean up.
