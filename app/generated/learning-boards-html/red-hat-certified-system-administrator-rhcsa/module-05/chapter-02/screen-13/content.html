Diagnostic commands
rpm -qa
rpm -qi package_name
rpm -ql package_name
rpm -V package_name
rpm -q --whatrequires package_name
These commands provide the evidence you need to decide whether to reinstall, restore, or remove packages safely.
Recovery checklist
  1. Confirm database state: rpm -qa and rpm -qi.
  2. Locate files: rpm -ql and inspect missing or altered files.
  3. Verify integrity: rpm -V and interpret flags.
  4. Remove or reinstall with careful attention to dependencies.
Pro tip: Collect rpm outputs and logs before making changes — they help you reverse actions if needed.
