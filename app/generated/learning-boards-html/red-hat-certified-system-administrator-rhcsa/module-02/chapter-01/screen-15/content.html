Key Concepts

Physical Volume (PV)
A disk or partition initialized for LVM with pvcreate.
Volume Group (VG)
A pool of storage combining one or more PVs for flexible allocation.
Logical Volume (LV)
A virtual block device carved from a VG; formatted and mounted like a partition.
Summary: Use LVM to build flexible, resizable storage. Verify free space, back up data, and test fstab changes. These practices keep systems resilient as they grow.
