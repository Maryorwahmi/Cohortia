Key Concepts

Partitioning
Dividing a disk into logical sections for filesystems or LVM.
MBR & GPT
MBR is legacy with limits; GPT is modern and supports large disks and many partitions.
File System
ext4 and XFS are common; choose by workload characteristics and growth needs.
Summary: Choose GPT for large disks, create a suitable filesystem, use UUIDs in fstab, and test before reboot. These habits prevent downtime.
