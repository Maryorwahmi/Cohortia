Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › What is logrotate?
Definition
Log rotation and retention
logrotate schedules rotations, compresses old logs, and can remove logs older than a retention target. It helps prevent disks from filling up and automates housekeeping tasks.
Key directives include daily, rotate N, compress, missingok, notifempty, create, and postrotate/endscript blocks for service signaling.
Why it matters
Without rotation, large text logs can exhaust disk space, cause outages, and make forensic analysis harder. logrotate provides predictable retention and compressed archives for long-term storage.
Key Takeaway

Define rotation frequency and retention to match application needs and disk capacity; always test postrotate hooks.
