Module 06 › Chapter 6.4 › journald vs rsyslog
Definition
systemd-journald
Collects structured, binary logs from kernel and services. Enables filtering and querying by fields and metadata.
Strength
Rich filtering and boot-based queries.
Caveat
Binary storage may be volatile unless persistence is enabled.
Key Takeaway

Use journalctl for fast, field-aware queries and transient debugging.

Definition
rsyslog
A traditional syslog daemon that writes text files, applies selectors, and forwards to remote servers for aggregation or SIEM integration.
Strength
Persistent text logs and flexible forwarding rules.
Caveat
Misconfiguration can drop messages; always test rules safely.
Key Takeaway

Use rsyslog when you need plain-text archives or to forward logs off-host.
