Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › What is firewalld?
Definition and purpose

firewalld manages packet filtering policies with a higher-level, zone-based abstraction. It supports runtime changes, named services, rich rules, and an optional low-level direct interface.

Zone = trust profile
Services = named rules
Runtime vs permanent control
Key Takeaway

Choose zones by network trust to apply consistent policies and avoid editing low-level rules directly unless necessary.

Concrete example
Suppose eth0 faces the internet and eth1 connects to an internal network. Put eth0 in the public zone and eth1 in the internal zone. Then allow only necessary services on public while permitting more traffic on internal.
Common misconception
People sometimes believe firewalld is only a wrapper that adds little value. In practice, its zones and service names reduce human error and speed up safe changes during operations.
