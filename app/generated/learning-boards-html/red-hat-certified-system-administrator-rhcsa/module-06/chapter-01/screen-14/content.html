Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Key Concepts
Zones
Trust profiles assigned to interfaces.
Services & Ports
Use services for common apps; ports for custom endpoints.
Runtime vs Permanent
Test at runtime; use --permanent and reload to persist.
Rich Rules
Readable, source-aware policies for fine control.
Direct Rules
Low-level iptables access; use sparingly.
Verification
Always verify listeners and firewall listings after changes.
Summary: Apply zone-based policies, prefer services and rich rules for clarity, persist changes deliberately, and use a safe verification workflow to maintain availability and security.
