Tuning cycle
1. Monitor
Collect baseline metrics from top, vmstat, iostat, ss and logs.
2. Identify
Pinpoint the limited resource: CPU, memory, disk, or network.
3. Change
Make a minimal, reversible configuration change or restart a service if safe.
4. Re-monitor
Check the same metrics to confirm improvement and document results.
Example: high disk %util
  1. Observe iostat -x 1 and note %util and avgqu-sz spikes.
  2. Investigate application logging and query patterns that cause heavy writes.
  3. Reduce fsync frequency or rotate logs more often as a config change.
  4. Re-run iostat to confirm reduced %util before adding hardware.
Safe termination
Prefer sending SIGTERM (kill PID) to allow cleanup. Use SIGKILL (kill -9 PID) only when the process is unresponsive after graceful attempts, and after capturing strace or logs.
Avoid this
Don't reboot or use kill -9 before gathering logs; you may lose the evidence needed to find the root cause.
Key Takeaway
One small change at a time, measure impact, and document timestamps and outcomes for future tuning and audits.
