Definition iostat — disk metrics

Understand device utilization and queues

iostat is the right tool when you suspect storage is delaying I/O and slowing the whole system.

Sample iostat -x 1 output
Device:         r/s     w/s    rkB/s   wkB/s  avgrq-sz  avgqu-sz  await  r_await  w_await  svctm  %util
sda              0.5   100.0   16.0    800.0     8.16     12.34    120.0   120.0    120.0    1.00   98.5
Here %util is near 100% and avgqu-sz is non-trivial, indicating the device is busy and requests are queuing — a disk I/O bottleneck.
Investigation steps
  1. Run iostat -x 1 during peak activity to identify the device with highest %util and avgqu-sz.
  2. Correlate with application logs and consider whether logging or database writes are responsible.
  3. Test remediation: reduce logging, optimize queries, or move workload to faster storage, then re-run iostat.
Pro Tip

Small, frequent writes create high IOPS with poor throughput; consider batching or buffering in the application to reduce load on the device.
