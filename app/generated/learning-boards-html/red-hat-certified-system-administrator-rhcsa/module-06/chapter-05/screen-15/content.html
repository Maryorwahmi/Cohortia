Metrics to trust
Three concise concept cards connecting metric to likely cause
Performance Bottleneck
A constrained resource (CPU, memory, disk, network) that limits overall system throughput and requires focused remediation.
Load Average
Average number of processes waiting to run. High sustained values suggest CPU contention or blocked I/O.
si / so (swap activity)
Swap-in and swap-out rates from vmstat. Persistent activity here points to memory pressure and potential performance degradation.
Takeaway
Monitor the right metric for the resource you believe is constrained, then make one change and re-measure to verify improvement.
