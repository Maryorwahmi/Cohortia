What to remember
Three compact concept cards to keep nearby during incidents
dmesg
Volatile kernel ring buffer; first place to check for hardware and driver errors.
strace
Traces system calls and signals. Use to find failing open/connect calls and errno values.
lsof
Lists open files and sockets. Perfect for finding deleted-but-held files or open network sockets.
Summary
Use these three tools together with monitoring output to build an accurate incident timeline and targeted fixes.
