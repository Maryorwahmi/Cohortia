Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Key Concepts
mdadm
Tool for building and managing software RAID devices and for monitoring rebuilds.
fsck / xfs_repair
Filesystem repair tools; must be run on unmounted devices to avoid further damage.
Diagnostics
Use df, du, dmesg, and journalctl together to locate space issues and mount-time errors.
Final takeaway
Practice the lab checklist and verification steps until the workflow is familiar: RAID build, quota init, repair tools, and diagnostics form the core of reliable storage operations.
