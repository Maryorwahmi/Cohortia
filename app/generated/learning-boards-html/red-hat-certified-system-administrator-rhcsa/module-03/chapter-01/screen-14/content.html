Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Pitfalls & Best Practices
Before writing

Review partition edits and back up data. Use q to quit without saving if unsure.

Mount & fstab

Create mount points, mount manually, then add UUID-based /etc/fstab entries and test with mount -a.

Unmounting safely

Resolve busy mounts with lsof or fuser to identify processes before attempting to unmount.

Final takeaway

Adopt safe, repeatable steps: inspect, plan, apply, verify; prefer UUIDs and validate fstab to keep systems reliable.
