Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Shrinking LVs
Why shrinking is riskier than growing
Shrinking requires filesystem reductions and often offline steps; errors can cause data loss.
Risk checklist
• Backup data before any shrink operation. • Unmount or use rescue mode to shrink filesystems safely. • XFS cannot be shrunk; plan a rebuild if necessary.
Safer alternatives
Add new LVs, migrate services, or grow the VG with pvcreate and vgextend rather than shrinking existing LVs.
Common misconception
Shrinking is simply the reverse of growing. In practice it often requires offline filesystem operations and carries risk.
