Logical Volume Management (LVM)
Pool disks, carve resizable volumes, and grow filesystems safely

LVM introduces three layers: Physical Volumes (PVs), Volume Groups (VGs), and Logical Volumes (LVs). Use pvcreate to initialize disks, vgcreate to pool them, and lvcreate to allocate space. For many filesystems you can extend an LV and then expand the filesystem without downtime. This model is especially useful on servers where storage needs change often.

Why it matters
Scale filesystems without repartitioning and reduce planned downtime for growth events.

Physical Volumes
Disks or partitions initialized with pvcreate, e.g. /dev/sdb, /dev/sdc.
Volume Group
A pooled container that spans one or many PVs and supplies extents for LVs.
Logical Volume
A resizable block device you format and mount, for example /dev/vg_data/lv_web.
