LVM Layers
PVs supply storage to VGs; LVs are carved from VGs and host filesystems.
Commands & Order
pvcreate → vgcreate/vgextend → lvcreate → lvextend → filesystem grow (xfs_growfs/resize2fs).
Verify & Backups
Always verify with pvs/vgs/lvs and df -h; back up before risky operations like shrinking.
Summary Takeaway

LVM gives you flexible pools and resizable volumes. Follow the correct command sequence, verify each change, and prefer growth or migration over shrinking to protect data.
