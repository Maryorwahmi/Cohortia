The LVM stack
How PV, VG, LV and extents relate and why each layer exists.
Physical Volume (PV)
A disk or partition prepared with pvcreate. It contributes physical space to a VG.
Volume Group (VG)
A pool of storage made from one or more PVs. It tracks free extents for allocation.
Logical Volume (LV)
Allocations taken from the VG. Format with a filesystem to make them usable.
Extent concept
Extents are the smallest LVM allocation units inside a VG. They determine how finely you can grow or shrink LVs. Remember: LVs consume extents and cannot exceed the VG's free extents.
Misconception
PVs are not filesystems and adding a PV does not automatically change mounted filesystems — you must create or extend LVs and filesystems explicitly.
Check your understanding
If a VG has 5GB free and you need a 6GB LV, what step must you take before creating the LV? (Answer: add another PV to the VG to supply the extra extents.)
