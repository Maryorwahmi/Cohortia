Comparison
ss vs netstat
Both show sockets; ss is usually faster and preferred on modern systems, netstat remains common on older systems.
Goal: find listening ports, protocol, and owning pid.
Cross-check: link pid to process owner with ps aux.
Misconception: absence of listeners doesn’t mean no network activity; check outbound connections too.
Terminal examples
# show listening sockets with pid/program
ss -tulnp
# or on older systems
netstat -tulnp
# cross-reference pid with process details
ps -p 2345 -o pid,user,cmd
Key Takeaway

Map sockets to processes and prioritize hosts with unexpected listeners or unknown outbound destinations.
