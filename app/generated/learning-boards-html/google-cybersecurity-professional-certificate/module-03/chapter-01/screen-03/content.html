From command to action
  • Find evidence: Use pwd, cd, ls to reach directories with logs and binaries you need to inspect.
  • Reduce exposure: Use chmod and chown to close access to sensitive files following least privilege.
  • Detect anomalies: Use ps/top and netstat/ss to find strange processes or open ports for containment.
Quick mnemonic
Locate → Inspect → Contain → Log
