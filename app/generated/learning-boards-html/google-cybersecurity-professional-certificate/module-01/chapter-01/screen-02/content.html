Learning Objectives
Skills and milestones you'll reach in this chapter.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define Cybersecurity
Explain what cybersecurity protects and common attack goals.
Knowing the definition helps you recognize assets, threats, and where to apply controls.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify Threats
Categorize malware, phishing, DoS, MitM, and zero-day exploits.
Classification makes it easier to anticipate attacker goals and required defenses.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Explain the CIA Triad
Describe Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability with examples.
The triad guides how you prioritize controls and measure incident impact.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Account for the Human Element
Recognize social engineering and the role of training and policies.
Human behavior shapes risk; awareness programs and clear processes reduce human-driven breaches.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
