Scenario Analysis: Identify Threats and CIA Violations
Use observation to name threats and map them to confidentiality, integrity, or availability impacts.
Scenario Summary
Employees got fake CEO emails. One employee submitted credentials. The storefront went down after a traffic surge. Inventory prices briefly changed before an automated correction.
Guided Analysis
  • Threat 1: Phishing leading to credential theft — CIA: Confidentiality.
  • Threat 2: DoS / DDoS causing unavailability — CIA: Availability.
  • Threat 3: Tampering or misuse of credentials causing incorrect product prices — CIA: Integrity.
Immediate Mitigations
  1. Reset and enforce MFA for affected accounts.
  2. Isolate systems and analyze logs for lateral movement.
  3. Enable traffic protections and restore service from safe backups if needed.
