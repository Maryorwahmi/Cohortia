Comparison
MitM vs Zero-day
Two stealthy risks with different mitigation approaches.
Man-in-the-Middle
Intercepts or modifies in-transit communications. Defend with TLS, certificate checks, and network monitoring.
Zero-day Exploit
Exploits unknown vulnerabilities. Defend by segmentation, least privilege, detection, and rapid patching when available.
Concrete Example
A MitM might alter a transaction in transit; a zero-day could give an attacker silent access to a server before a patch exists.
Detection Signals
Unexpected certificates, traffic patterns, or unexplained process behavior suggest interception or unknown exploits.
Response Priorities
Containment, forensic logging, and minimizing exposure while fixes or patches are prepared.
Misconception
Relying solely on patching ignores the need for detection; a holistic posture balances prevention and rapid containment.
