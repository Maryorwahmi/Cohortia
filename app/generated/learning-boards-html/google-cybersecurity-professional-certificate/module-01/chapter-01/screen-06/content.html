Common Types of Cyber Threats
Match each threat to the attacker's likely goal.
Malware
Software that disrupts systems, steals or encrypts data. Includes viruses, worms, Trojans, ransomware, spyware.
Goal: access, extortion, data theft.
Phishing
Social engineering to trick people into revealing secrets or clicking malicious links.
Goal: credential theft, initial access.
DoS / DDoS
Flooding systems with traffic or requests to make services unavailable to legitimate users.
Goal: deny availability.
Man-in-the-Middle
Intercepting communications between two parties to eavesdrop or tamper with data.
Goal: data interception, tampering.
Zero-day Exploit
Attack that uses a software vulnerability unknown to the vendor; no patch exists yet.
Goal: stealthy, high-impact access.
Human-driven Attacks
Social engineering and policy weaknesses exploited to bypass technical controls.
Goal: exploit people to reach technical assets.
Key Takeaway

Categorize incidents by attacker goal to speed triage and choose focused mitigations.

Pro Tip
When in doubt, ask: did the attacker want data, control, or to stop service? That question narrows your path forward quickly.
