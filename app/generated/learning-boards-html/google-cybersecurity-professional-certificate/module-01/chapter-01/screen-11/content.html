Core Concept
The CIA Triad
A simple model to judge security goals and impacts.
Confidentiality
Ensure access only to authorized users via encryption and access controls.
Integrity
Ensure information is accurate and unchanged except by authorized actions using hashing and signatures.
Availability
Ensure authorized users can access systems and data when needed through redundancy and recovery planning.
Practical Classification
Not all assets require equal emphasis on every property. Prioritize based on impact if an asset is compromised.
Payment Details
High confidentiality, high integrity, moderate availability.
Product Catalog
Moderate confidentiality, high integrity, high availability.
Status Page
Low confidentiality, low integrity, high availability.
Internal Logs
Moderate confidentiality, high integrity, moderate availability.
Key Takeaway

Use the triad to map incident impact and choose the highest-value controls for each asset.
