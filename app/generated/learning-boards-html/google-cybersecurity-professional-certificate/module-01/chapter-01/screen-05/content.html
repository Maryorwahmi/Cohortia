Example: Retailer lifecycle
Identify: customer data and store uptime. Assess: impact of data loss vs downtime. Implement: encryption and redundancy. Monitor: logs and traffic spikes. Respond: incident plan and backups.
Common Mistake
Focusing only on prevention. Detection and response shorten attacker dwell time and reduce damage; plan and practice them.
Key Takeaway

Preventing attacks is important, but fast detection and practiced response are what limit real damage.
