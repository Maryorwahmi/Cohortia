Summary
Key Concepts — What to Remember
Three compact concept cards to keep handy.
Cybersecurity
Protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.
Threat Types
Malware, phishing, DoS, MitM, and zero-day attacks each have distinct goals and indicators.
CIA Triad
Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability — use them to assess impact and prioritize controls.
Summary Takeaway
When triaging or designing defenses ask: what asset is at risk, what was the attacker trying to do, and which control reduces the most risk given people and processes?
