Objectives — Applied Focus
Translate definition and concepts into incident triage skills.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define Cybersecurity (Apply)
Explain protection goals and give a real example like securing online banking or e-commerce platforms.
Being able to name assets and plausible attacker goals helps during initial triage.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify Threats (Apply)
Quickly classify a reported issue as malware, phishing, DoS, MitM, or zero-day to choose next steps.
Classification speeds decision-making and points to the right tools.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
CIA Triad (Apply)
Map an incident to confidentiality, integrity, or availability violations and recommend controls accordingly.
The triad is a simple checklist to guide impact assessment and response.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Human Element (Apply)
Identify when human behavior enabled an attack and suggest awareness or policy changes.
Human-driven vulnerabilities are often the simplest to fix with training and process changes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
