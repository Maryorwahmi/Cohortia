Key Concepts — Short Definitions
Three one-line definitions to use in notes and reports.
Cybersecurity
Protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.
Malware
Software designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to systems.
Phishing
Deceptive messages that trick people into revealing sensitive information or taking harmful actions.
Final Thought
Use these definitions as shorthand, and always expand them with triad impact and the human element when you act.
