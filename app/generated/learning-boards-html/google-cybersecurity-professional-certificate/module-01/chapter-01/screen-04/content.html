Core Concept
What Is Cybersecurity?
Protecting systems, data, and services from digital attacks.

Cybersecurity spans technology, processes, and people. It focuses on preventing unauthorized access, stopping unauthorized changes, and keeping services available.

Technical Controls
Encryption, firewalls, logging, and monitoring.
Organizational Controls
Access policies, incident response, and training.
Common misconception
Security is not only a product you buy. It is a continuous practice of assessing risk and applying appropriate defenses across people and systems.
Key Takeaway

Think of cybersecurity as ongoing risk management that protects confidentiality, integrity, and availability of assets.

Practical Example
Online retailer protecting payments, inventory, and uptime.
Payment Data — Confidentiality
Encryption and strict access controls prevent unauthorized disclosure of payment details.
Inventory — Integrity
Checks, version control, and logging ensure product data is accurate and tamper-evident.
Storefront — Availability
Redundancy and DDoS mitigation keep the site reachable for customers.
Why this matters: protecting these three areas preserves trust, revenue, and legal compliance.
