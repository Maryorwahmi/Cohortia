Applying the CIA Triad — Controls and Examples
Map controls directly to the property you need to protect.
Confidentiality
Encryption, access controls, multi-factor authentication, and data-handling policies.
Integrity
Hashing, digital signatures, audit logs, version control, and change management.
Availability
Redundancy, backups, DDoS protection, and disaster recovery planning.
Concrete Example: Payment Data
Encrypt storage, require MFA for admin access, log changes, and keep offline backups to recover from ransomware.
Control Integration
Controls must be tested together; automation and monitoring ensure they do not conflict and remain effective.
Key Takeaway

Prioritize controls by the triad property most relevant to an asset, and verify effectiveness through monitoring and exercises.
