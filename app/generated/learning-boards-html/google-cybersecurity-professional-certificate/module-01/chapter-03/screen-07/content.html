Ethics & Professional Responsibility
Principles that guide trustworthy practice and protect people.
Confidentiality
Only access and share sensitive data when authorized. Preserve user privacy in investigations.
Integrity
Avoid altering evidence, logs, or audit trails during analysis or testing.
Accountability
Document actions, decisions, and escalate when uncertain to maintain transparency.
Concrete example
An analyst preserves logs by making cryptographic copies and logs chain-of-custody, preventing accidental tampering and ensuring valid evidence for later investigation.
Misconception
High technical skill does not replace written consent, proper reporting, or conflict-of-interest disclosure.
