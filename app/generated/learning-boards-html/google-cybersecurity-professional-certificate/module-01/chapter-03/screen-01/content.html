Roles, Responsibilities, and Ethics
An introduction to common cybersecurity careers, ethical boundaries, and the legal context that shapes your work.

This chapter helps you connect a daily task to a named role, understand how similar techniques change meaning with intent, and recognize the legal limits you must follow. We emphasize practical examples you can carry into interviews and early projects.

Why it matters: organizations depend on trustworthy practitioners. Your decisions affect confidentiality, availability, and privacy for real people.
