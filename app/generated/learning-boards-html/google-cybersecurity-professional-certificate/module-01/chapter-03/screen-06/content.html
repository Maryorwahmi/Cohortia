Responsible Penetration Test
A five-step process to keep tests ethical and effective.
01 Authorization
Signed scope and consent.
02 Plan
Choose safe methods for production.
03 Execute
Capture evidence and avoid destructive payloads.
04 Report
Prioritized, actionable results.
05 Verify
Confirm remediations and close the loop.
Concrete Example
An authorized scan on staging avoids destructive SQL payloads, findings are documented with reproduction steps and a remediation checklist for developers.
Common mistake
Skipping written authorization or failing to set scope can expose you and your organization to legal risk.
