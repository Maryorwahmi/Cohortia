How to approach typical assessment items for this chapter.
MCQ Example
Simulating attacks with permission and scope = Penetration Tester. Look for keywords: permission and scope.
Policy Question
Updating data handling to protect customers = Legal & Regulatory Compliance. Explain why regulations constrain technical choices.
When answering short responses, include role name, a short example from the prompt, and one sentence linking to ethics or law. This structured approach shows both technical understanding and professional judgment.
