Auditing and Forensics
How assurance and investigation roles support security and compliance.
Security Auditor
Assesses policies and controls, documents gaps, and prioritizes fixes for compliance needs.
Example: reviewing access control logs and verifying multi-factor authentication deployment.
Forensics Analyst
Collects and analyzes evidence, preserves chain-of-custody, and produces defensible reports for investigations.
Example: imaging a compromised system and extracting malware artifacts for timeline analysis.
Misconception: audits are only bureaucratic. In truth, they provide prioritized evidence for improvement. Forensics is not only for prosecution; it informs remediation and reduces repeat incidents.
