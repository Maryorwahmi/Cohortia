Career Path Exploration
Use the three professional descriptions to identify roles and ethical alignment.
Professional A
Monitors SIEM alerts, investigates login attempts, and helps users recover from phishing. Likely role: Security Analyst. Ethical alignment: preserves evidence and escalates confirmed incidents.
Professional B
Writes automation scripts, hardens Linux servers, and evaluates new tools. Likely role: Security Engineer. Ethical alignment: tests changes in staging, documents impact, and avoids breaking production.
Professional C
Performs authorized attacks under scope, documents vulnerabilities, and gives remediation recommendations. Likely role: Penetration Tester. Ethical alignment: operates with permission and reports responsibly.
Reflection: when you describe your own experience, always include the authorization, scope, and how you handled sensitive data or legal constraints.
