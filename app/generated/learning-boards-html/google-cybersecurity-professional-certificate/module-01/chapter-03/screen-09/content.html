Incident Response Steps
A practical checklist to contain and recover from security events.
Detect
Identify alerts and collect initial data.
Contain
Isolate hosts or block malicious channels.
Eradicate
Remove malware, revoke credentials.
Recover
Restore from verified backups and validate integrity.
Concrete example
In ransomware response, preserving disk images for forensics before restoring ensures evidence is available while services are safely restored from backups.
Common mistake
Rushing to restore without evidence preservation can destroy crucial forensic data and hinder root cause analysis.
