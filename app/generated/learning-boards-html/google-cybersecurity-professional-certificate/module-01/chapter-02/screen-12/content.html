Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Access Control Architecture
Design pattern
Roles, permissions, approvals, and audit logs
Step 1: Define roles
List responsibilities and map minimal permissions to each role.
Step 2: Enforce approvals
Require multiple approvals or separate accounts for high-risk operations.
Step 3: Audit and review
Log actions and regularly review permissions and sudo policies.
Checks
  • Are roles narrowly scoped and documented?
  • Are elevated actions logged and approved?
  • Do audits detect stale privileges or shared accounts?
Key Takeaway

Use roles and approvals to make privilege predictable, auditable, and minimal.
