Availability strategies
Redundancy, backups, and recovery planning
Use redundant servers and geographically diverse hosting to reduce single points of failure.
Maintain regular backups and test restores to validate recovery procedures.
Create a disaster recovery and business continuity plan with clear roles and timelines.
Concrete example
Host application replicas in two regions and automate failover, while backing up databases daily and testing restores monthly.
Checks and misconceptions
  • Don’t just rely on backups—test restores under realistic conditions.
  • Define acceptable downtime and recovery objectives before designing redundancy.
  • Consider both technical and human processes in recovery plans.
Key Takeaway

Design for failure: redundant components and tested recovery plans maintain availability when things go wrong.
