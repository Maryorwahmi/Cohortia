Principle of least privilege
Grant minimal permissions to users and services.
Limits the blast radius of a compromised account or a software vulnerability.
Use groups and role-based access to give capabilities, not blanket rights.
Regularly review and revoke unnecessary permissions.
Example
Set web app files to be owned by the deployment user and avoid running the app as an all-powerful system user.
Practical steps
  • Inventory privileges and remove ones not required for daily tasks.
  • Use separate accounts for elevated tasks and require approval or auditing.
  • Avoid shared privileged accounts; prefer audited escalation mechanisms.
Common mistake
Granting broad permissions to simplify operations instead of designing minimal privileged workflows.
