NIST functions
A simple structure to organize controls
Identify: inventory assets and assess risk priorities.
Protect: encryption, access controls, patching, and training.
Detect: logging, monitoring, and IDS to spot anomalies.
Respond: containment, remediation, and communication plans.
Recover: backups, restore testing, and lessons learned.
How to start
Inventory critical assets and map existing controls to the five functions to find gaps.
Key Takeaway

A framework helps you organize controls, prioritize gaps, and measure progress over time.
