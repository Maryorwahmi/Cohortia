Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Common Mistakes
Frequent failures
How small errors become major incidents
Weak passwords and reused credentials undermine confidentiality and let attackers bypass controls.
Excessive permissions let a single compromised account make large-scale changes.
Backups without restore tests fail to provide availability during incidents.
Remediation checklist
  • Enable MFA on all privileged accounts.
  • Replace shared accounts with role-based access and logging.
  • Run permission audits and revoke unused rights monthly.
  • Test backup restores quarterly and document the process.
Key Takeaway

Simple, regular checks prevent many common security failures; schedule them and verify results.
