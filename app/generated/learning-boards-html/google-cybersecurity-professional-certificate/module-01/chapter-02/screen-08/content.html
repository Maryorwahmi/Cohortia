Layered controls example
Perimeter to host-level protections
1. Perimeter firewall: block known bad traffic and restrict exposed services.
2. Internal segmentation: limit lateral movement by separating critical resources.
3. IDS/monitoring: detect suspicious patterns and generate alerts for investigation.
4. EDR: stop malicious processes on endpoints and remediate compromised hosts.
5. Organizational controls: training, policies, and access reviews reduce risky behavior.
Checks
  • Are firewall rules minimal and reviewed?
  • Is segmentation enforced with internal firewalls or VLANs?
  • Does monitoring generate actionable alerts and not just noise?
Common mistake
Over-reliance on perimeter controls while ignoring endpoint and identity protections.
