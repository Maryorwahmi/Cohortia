Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › What to Remember
Encryption
Converts data into code to prevent unauthorized access and protect confidentiality.
Hashing
Produces a fingerprint of data to detect tampering and support integrity checks.
MFA & Redundancy
Multi-factor authentication increases account security; redundancy ensures availability during failures.
Final takeaway

Apply these mechanisms together and use a framework to measure and improve your security program over time.
