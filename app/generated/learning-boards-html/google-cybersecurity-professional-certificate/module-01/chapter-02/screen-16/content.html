Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Defense-in-Depth
Multiple, diverse layers of control reduce the chance of undetected compromise.
Least Privilege
Grant only necessary permissions to minimize blast radius of compromise.
Separation of Duties
Split critical tasks across people to create checks, prevent fraud, and improve detection.
Summary

Combine these concepts with monitoring and tested recovery to build resilient, auditable systems.
