Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Separation of Duties
Why separate duties?
Prevent a single person from fully controlling critical processes.
Creates checks and balances to reduce fraud and errors.
Requires different roles for approvals, execution, and auditing.
Use immutable logs and multiple reviewers for critical changes.
Example
A deployment requires code review by a lead, approval by a manager, and execution by a separate operations engineer.
Design tips
  • Identify critical processes and split initiation, approval, and execution.
  • Automate where possible, but keep human reviews for high-impact changes.
  • Maintain clear audit trails so actions can be reviewed later.
Common mistake
Assuming separation requires many staff; small teams can use role separation and approvals to gain similar benefits.
