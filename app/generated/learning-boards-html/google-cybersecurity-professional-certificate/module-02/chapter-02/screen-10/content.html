Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Common Threats
DDoS

A distributed flood of traffic from many sources intended to exhaust resources and deny service to legitimate users.

Packet Sniffing

Interception and analysis of packets; effective against unencrypted traffic such as plain HTTP.

IP Spoofing

Falsifying source IP addresses to impersonate trusted hosts or to hide origin; can be used in other attacks like DoS.

Man-in-the-Middle

Intercepting and optionally modifying communications between two parties, often via ARP or DNS manipulation or rogue Wi-Fi.

Why it matters

Different attacks exploit different weaknesses: capacity limits, lack of encryption, weak filters or name resolution. Defend accordingly.
