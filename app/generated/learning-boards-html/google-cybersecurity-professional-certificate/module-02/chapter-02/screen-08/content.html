IP: addressing and routing

IP assigns addresses and determines how packets move between networks. Use addressing to locate hosts and to apply network-layer controls.

TCP: reliable delivery

TCP ensures ordered, error-checked streams by sequencing segments and requiring acknowledgements and retransmissions when needed.

Investigator example
# When analyzing a connection, note: source IP, dest IP, source port, dest port # Unusual destination ports suggest non-standard services # Sudden increase in incomplete handshakes may indicate a scan or DoS
Key Takeaway

Combine addressing and transport behavior to triage traffic: ports suggest service, TCP state reveals intent or anomaly.
