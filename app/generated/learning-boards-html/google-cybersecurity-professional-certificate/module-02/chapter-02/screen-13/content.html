Name: cohortia.com

Interpretation: confirm the DNS server used and the resolved IP. Unexpected addresses or unknown resolvers may indicate DNS manipulation or misconfiguration.
Key Takeaway

Collect these outputs as first-step evidence, compare them to expected configurations, and correlate with logs and running processes before making containment decisions.
