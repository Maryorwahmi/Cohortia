Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › DDoS
1. Botnet assembly
Attacker compromises many hosts to create a distributed set of traffic sources.
2. Attack launch
Compromised hosts flood the target with requests or malformed packets.
3. Service impact
Resources are exhausted and legitimate users are denied service.
Immediate mitigations
  • Apply rate limiting and temporary ACLs.
  • Use upstream scrubbing or CDN services to absorb traffic.
  • Monitor baselines and collect flow logs to identify vectors.
Investigator prompt

Collect flow logs, syslogs and application metrics to separate legitimate spikes from attack traffic and to determine whether source IPs are spoofed.
