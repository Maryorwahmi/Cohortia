Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › What to Remember
Think in layers
Place controls where they can actually inspect or protect the relevant data.
Encrypt sensitive traffic
Prefer HTTPS and secure DNS to reduce the value of captured packets.
Gather evidence first
Use ip addr, netstat, ping, traceroute and nslookup to form a defensible response plan.
Final takeaway

Combine layered controls, encryption and evidence-driven investigation to reduce exposure and speed effective response to network threats.
