01 — Firewalls
Task: Label a rule as stateless or stateful and justify whether it will allow an inbound reply.
Mnemonic: "State tracks sessions"
02 — IDS vs IPS
Task: Given a detection, state whether the system should alert only or block traffic immediately and why.
Mnemonic: "Alert versus Act"
03 — VPNs
Task: Explain how a remote user’s session is protected from eavesdropping and name common tunnel protocols.
Mnemonic: "Tunnel = Encrypt"
04 — Segmentation & Hardening
Task: Sketch a short plan to isolate public servers in a DMZ and place IoT on a separate VLAN; list two hardening steps.
Mnemonic: "Isolate to Limit"
