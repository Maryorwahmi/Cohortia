Hierarchy › Network Segmentation
Why segment?
Reduce blast radius and limit lateral movement if a segment is compromised.
DMZ
A small, isolated segment for public-facing servers that sits between internet and internal network.
VLANs
Logical subdivisions to isolate departments or device classes on the same physical switch.
Concrete Example
Public web servers in a DMZ, HR on one VLAN, Finance on another, guest Wi‑Fi and IoT isolated from internal resources.
Implementation considerations
  • Combine VLANs with ACLs and firewall rules for effective isolation.
  • Monitor inter-segment traffic to detect suspicious lateral movement.
  • Plan segmentation to minimize operational disruption while enforcing least privilege.
Common mistake
Creating too many segments without clear policy or monitoring increases complexity and maintenance burden.
Key Takeaway

Segmentation contains damage and enforces least privilege; combine DMZs, VLANs, ACLs, and monitoring for best effect.
