Definition › Secure Configuration
Authentication & SSH
Use strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication. Prefer SSH keys and disable root login.
Example sshd_config entries
PermitRootLogin no AllowUsers alice bob PasswordAuthentication no
Why it matters
Reducing exposed services and tightening access controls stops many common attacks before they start.
Patching & Least Privilege
Keep OS, firmware, and applications updated. Grant only necessary permissions to users and services.
  • Disable unused services and close unnecessary ports.
  • Audit accounts and remove defaults.
  • Schedule regular updates and configuration reviews.
Key Takeaway
Harden devices with strict auth, minimal services, and prompt patching to close common attack paths.
