Implementing Network Security Controls
Understand and apply layered defenses that monitor, filter, and protect network traffic.
In this lesson
Firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPNs, segmentation, and hardening.
Why it matters
Prevent breaches and limit lateral movement to protect critical assets.
Hands-on
Follow the UFW example to safely lock down a web server.
Assessment
Identify the correct placement for firewalls, IDS, and DMZs in a network.
Explore Key Topics
Firewalls
Packet, stateful, and application-layer inspection.
IDS & IPS
Alerting vs active prevention and placement tradeoffs.
Segmentation
DMZ & VLAN patterns to limit attacker movement.
Coming up: a hands-on UFW demo and decision guide for choosing controls based on risk.
Visual Concept
Perimeter defenses and segmentation
Public services sit in a DMZ, internal systems behind stateful firewalls, deeper inspection at application layer where needed, monitoring from IDS/IPS, and secure remote tunnels via VPN.
Think in layers: filter, inspect, detect, respond.
Quick Example
Block SSH from the internet while allowing web traffic, and restrict SSH to a single internal IP.
Key Takeaway
Layered controls reduce single points of failure and slow attackers.
Note
As you progress, keep the principle "deny all, permit by exception" in mind when you design rules and segmentation.
