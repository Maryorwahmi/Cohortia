Key Concepts › What to Remember
Firewall
Monitors and controls traffic based on rules at network boundaries.
Packet-Filtering
Inspects IPs and ports per packet; fast but stateless.
Stateful Inspection
Tracks active connections and allows legitimate responses.
Application-Layer (NGFW)
Inspects payloads to block application-specific attacks.
IDS / IPS
IDS alerts; IPS blocks inline. Both use signatures and anomalies.
VPN
Creates encrypted tunnels to protect data in transit over untrusted networks.
Summary
Layer controls appropriately: filtering, state tracking, content inspection, detection, prevention, and encrypted transit all play distinct roles.
