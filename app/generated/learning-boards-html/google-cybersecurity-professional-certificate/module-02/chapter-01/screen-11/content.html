Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Prioritization
How to prioritize
Use likelihood and impact, then consider ALE when financial comparison is needed.
Sequence
High likelihood × high impact for critical assets first; group common vulnerabilities for programmatic fixes.
Cost comparison
If ALE reduction exceeds control cost, the control typically pays for itself. Consider non-financial obligations too.
Avoid diffusing effort across many low-priority items. Apply centralized controls like automated patching or password policy enforcement to reduce many similar risks efficiently.
Decision record
Document why a risk is mitigated, transferred, avoided, or accepted and who owns the decision. This record prevents implicit acceptance and supports audits.
Key Takeaway

Prioritization should guide resource allocation and be transparently recorded so business leaders see the rationale.
