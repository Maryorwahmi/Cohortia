Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Assessment & Mistakes
Sample assessment items
  1. Identify the vulnerability in a scenario of outdated web server software.
  2. Name the treatment strategy when an organization purchases cyber insurance.
  3. Explain why documenting acceptance is necessary.
Common mistakes
  • Not recording accepted risks or owners.
  • Labeling everything as critical and diluting focus.
  • Delaying action while chasing exact quantitative numbers.
Key Takeaway

Answering targeted questions and avoiding common errors strengthens both assessments and real-world risk decisions.
