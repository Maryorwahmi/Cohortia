Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Assets, Threats, Vulnerabilities
Core definitions
Clear, practical definitions and examples you can use in a register.
Asset
Anything of value: cloud database, customer records, applications, or people with privileged access.
Threat
Potential danger: malicious actors, malware, accidental deletion, or natural disasters.
Vulnerability
A weakness: unpatched OS, default credentials, misconfiguration, or lack of training.
Common misconception
Calling exposure a risk without a valuable asset. If the asset has no value, prioritize lower.
Scenario example
Cloud-hosted Customer Database
Asset
Customer Database with names, addresses, payment details. High business value.
Vulnerability
Unpatched operating system and weak default admin passwords.
Threat
Malicious actors actively targeting e-commerce sites for data theft.
Key Takeaway

List assets first, then map vulnerabilities and threats. The explicit mapping turns guesses into actionable risks.
