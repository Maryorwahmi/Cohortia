Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Identifying Assets
Asset inventory steps
  1. List hardware and software, cloud services, and databases.
  2. Include data categories and business processes that rely on the asset.
  3. Record an owner and business value statement for each asset.
  4. Tier assets: critical, important, non-critical to focus effort.
Owners make treatment choices accountable. Tiers guide whether you accept some risks or must mitigate immediately.
Tiering example
Critical
Customer database with payment data — high regulatory and reputational impact.
Important
Internal reporting server — operational impact if down.
Non-critical
Public marketing site with no sensitive data.
Action
Use tiers to set review frequency and acceptable residual risk levels.
Avoid
Avoid labeling everything as critical; use simple, repeatable criteria.
Key Takeaway

A short, owned inventory with tiers makes prioritization fast and defensible when incidents occur.
