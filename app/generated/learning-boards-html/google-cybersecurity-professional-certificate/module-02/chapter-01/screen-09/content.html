Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Risk Treatment Strategies
Four strategies
Mitigation
Reduce likelihood or impact with controls: patching, encryption, MFA, training.
Avoidance
Eliminate the risk by stopping the risky activity or selecting an alternative.
Transfer
Shift financial/operational burden via insurance or outsourcing.
Acceptance
Consciously accept residual risk when treatment cost outweighs impact.
Document the chosen strategy, why it was selected, and who owns the decision. Avoid implicit acceptance by recording rationale and review dates.
Example mapping
Unpatched OS on cloud DB
Treatment: Mitigation — schedule immediate patching and enable vulnerability scanning. Owner: Ops team.
Use of a risky third-party tool
Treatment: Avoidance — select an alternative or remove the integration.
Residual financial exposure
Treatment: Transfer — cyber insurance to cover breach costs.
Key Takeaway

Match strategy to value and cost. Mitigate common technical risks; avoid or transfer when mitigation is impractical or too costly.
