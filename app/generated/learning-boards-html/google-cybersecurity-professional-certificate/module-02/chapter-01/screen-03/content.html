Practice tasks
Concrete exercises you'll do to apply the concepts.
Create a risk register
List assets, threats, vulnerabilities, risk descriptions, likelihood and impact, and treatment plans.
Score risks qualitatively
Use low/medium/high when numerical data is unavailable to prioritize quickly.
Estimate ALE
Practice a simple quantitative calculation to connect risk to financial impact.
Document treatment
Record when you mitigate, avoid, transfer, or accept a risk and why.
Practice tip
When unsure, use conservative estimates and re-assess after monitoring data is available.
