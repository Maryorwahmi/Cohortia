A practical framework to find, measure, and treat risks so controls align with business priorities.

Why it matters

Core elements
  • Asset — anything valuable that needs protection.
  • Threat — a potential danger, intentional or accidental.
  • Vulnerability — a weakness that a threat can exploit.
  • Risk — the potential loss when these align.
Quick example

A cloud customer database (asset) with unpatched OS (vulnerability) and active hackers scanning the internet (threat) creates a high-priority risk of data theft.

Key Takeaway

Start by cataloging assets, then map threats and vulnerabilities. That mapping creates actionable risks you can assess and treat.
