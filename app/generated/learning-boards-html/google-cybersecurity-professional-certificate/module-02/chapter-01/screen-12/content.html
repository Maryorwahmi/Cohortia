Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Continuous Monitoring
Monitoring sources
  • Vulnerability scans and patch reports
  • Penetration test findings and remediation verification
  • Security logs, alerts, and incident post-mortems
  • Changes in business systems or third-party services
Review cadence guidance
Critical assets — weekly to monthly reviews
Important assets — monthly to quarterly
Non-critical — semi-annual or annual
Key Takeaway

Make monitoring data feed the risk register. Reviews keep priorities current and demonstrate due diligence.
