When to use qualitative assessment
  • Quick triage across many assets when numerical data is unavailable.
  • When you need stakeholder alignment and fast prioritization.
  • To identify candidates for later quantitative analysis.
Reduce subjectivity
Agree on what each category means in business terms, for example defining ‘high impact’ as regulatory fines or loss of critical services for 24+ hours.
Risk matrix (3x3)

High
Example: Cloud DB risks
  • Unpatched OS exploited by external attackers — Likelihood: High; Impact: High → Priority: High.
  • Accidental data deletion by employee — Likelihood: Medium; Impact: Medium → Priority: Medium.
Key Takeaway

Qualitative scoring gives quick prioritization. If a decision requires dollars, follow up with a quantitative estimate.
