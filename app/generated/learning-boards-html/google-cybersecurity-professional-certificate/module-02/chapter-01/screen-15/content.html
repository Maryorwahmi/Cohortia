Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › What to Remember
Map the three
Always identify the asset, the threat, and the vulnerability that together create a risk.
Choose assessment
Use qualitative triage for speed; use ALE when leadership needs dollar-based justification.
Treat and document
Mitigate, avoid, transfer, or accept — and always record the owner and review date.
Final Thought

A short, repeatable process and clear records are the difference between reactive firefighting and strategic risk management.
