Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Rollback Plans
What to include
  • Versioned and immutable artifacts to redeploy
  • Automated scripts or pipeline steps for rollback
  • Predefined health checks and rollback triggers
  • Communication and runbook steps for responders
Common mistake
Relying on undocumented or manual rollback steps during incidents, increasing time to recovery.
Example rollback paths
Blue-green: switch load balancer to previous environment. Canary: stop canary and route users back to stable version. Rolling: redeploy previous image to updated nodes or reconfigure routing to stable nodes.
Key Takeaway

Automate rollback steps, version artifacts, and rehearse procedures so recovery is fast and reliable.
