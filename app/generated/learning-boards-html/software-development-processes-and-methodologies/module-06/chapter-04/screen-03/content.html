Operational Objectives

Turn patterns into procedures you can execute under pressure.

Rollback Plans
Create automated and tested reversal steps that restore the previous stable artifact and configuration quickly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Monitoring & Alerting
Define health metrics, error budgets, and automated alerts that trigger remediation steps or rollbacks.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Tool Fit
Match CI/CD tool capabilities to pipeline stages and the team's automation maturity before committing to a platform.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Pattern Selection
Choose rolling, blue-green, or canary based on downtime sensitivity, impact radius, and rollback speed.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
Operational Mnemonic
Automate: Build, Test, Stage, Deploy, Observe. Tie alerts to rollback and rehearse the sequence so teams can act quickly when incidents occur.
Example
For a payment microservice, automated rollback and alerting on error rates limit financial exposure. Configure a canary to reach 2% of users, monitor payments per second and error rate, and automatically revert if thresholds are breached.
