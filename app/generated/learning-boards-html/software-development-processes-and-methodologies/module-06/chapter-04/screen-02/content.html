Learning Objectives

Explain Deployment

Compare Release Models

CI → CD Relationship

Design Deployments

You will move from conceptual knowledge to applied design: first knowing what deployment is, then choosing strategies and operationalizing safety for production systems.
Mnemonic
Think: Package, Verify, Release, Observe. Packaging makes releases reproducible, verification prevents regressions, release patterns control risk, and observation drives rollback decisions.
