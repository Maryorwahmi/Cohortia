Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Monitoring & Alerting
Key metrics
  • Request latency and error rates
  • Throughput and saturation
  • Business metrics such as transaction success
Design alerts that trigger action, map them to runbooks, and ensure they notify both humans and automation when appropriate.
Misconception
More alerts are not better. Prioritize signals that indicate real service degradation and pair them with clear remediation steps.
Example: Canary trigger
If error rate for canary traffic rises above a threshold, trigger: stop rollout, notify on-call, and execute automated rollback to previous stable artifact for the canary group.
Key Takeaway

Monitoring converts deployment from a blind release into a controlled experiment with clear pass/fail criteria and automated reactions.
