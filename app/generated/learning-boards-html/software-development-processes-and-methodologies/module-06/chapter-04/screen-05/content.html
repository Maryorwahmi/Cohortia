Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Traditional vs Modern
Traditional Release Train
  • Infrequent, large bundles of changes
  • Manual coordination and long downtimes
  • Large rollback surface and complex recovery
Common pitfall: batching hides defects and delays feedback.
Why teams changed
Faster feedback, smaller blast radius, and shorter recovery time motivate automation and frequent release models.
Modern Frequent Delivery
  • Small, automated changes validated by CI pipelines
  • Faster feedback loops and safer incremental releases
  • Rollback and monitoring integrated into the pipeline
Benefit: issues are smaller, detected faster, and reversible with lower customer impact.
Example
A team moving to daily deploys reduced mean time to detect by enabling rapid rollback of single changes instead of reverting an entire monthly release.
