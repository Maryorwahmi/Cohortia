Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts
Software Deployment
Making an application available by packaging, configuring, installing, and activating it for users.
CI and CD
CI verifies changes through automated builds and tests; CD makes verified changes deployable with a manual gate before production.
Deployment Strategies
Rolling, blue-green, and canary are common patterns to manage risk and downtime during releases.
Takeaway

Combine automation, a chosen release pattern, rollback planning, and monitoring to deliver frequent, low-risk releases.
