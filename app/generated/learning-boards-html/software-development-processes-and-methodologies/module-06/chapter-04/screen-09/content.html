Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Canary Deployment
Pattern
Start by routing a small percentage of production traffic to the new version, then monitor and progressively increase the percentage until full rollout. If metrics break, halt and roll back to the stable version.
Steps
  1. Define initial canary percentage and monitoring criteria.
  2. Deploy and route canary traffic.
  3. Observe metrics and expand or rollback.
Pitfall
Relying on small canaries without good observability — you must measure the right metrics to make the canary useful.
Example
For the payment service, the canary might be 2% of production users for 30 minutes while monitoring transaction success rate and latency. If thresholds hold, expand to 10% then to 50% and finally 100%.
Key Takeaway

Canaries provide real-user validation with limited impact, but only when paired with clear metrics and automated rollback paths.
