Definition Knowledge Sharing

Why documentation matters

Capture what you learned so teams don’t repeat the same problems and new members come up to speed faster.

Artifacts to maintain
  • Post‑mortem summaries with action items.
  • Pre‑deployment checklists and runbooks.
  • Architecture notes and ADRs.
Practical habit
Make documentation an explicit step in action items: assign an owner to update the knowledge base when an action is complete.

Transfer methods

Pair programming
Real‑time knowledge transfer that complements written notes.
Internal demos & lunch‑and‑learns
Brief presentations to share context, decisions, and how‑tos.
Avoiding the trap
Documentation must be concise and actionable; avoid long, stale documents that nobody reads.
Key Takeaway

Make updating documentation part of the action, and use demos and pairing to make knowledge practical and durable.
