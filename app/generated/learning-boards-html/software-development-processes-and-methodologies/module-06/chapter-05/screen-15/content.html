Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Feedback Loop
A system where outputs inform future planning and improvements.
Mean Time To Recovery
Average time to restore service after an outage; use it to track operational health.
Defect Escape Rate
Percentage of defects found by customers after release; use it to evaluate tests and QA.
Quick Reminder

Make improvements measurable, assign owners, and document outcomes. Small, repeated steps create durable progress.
