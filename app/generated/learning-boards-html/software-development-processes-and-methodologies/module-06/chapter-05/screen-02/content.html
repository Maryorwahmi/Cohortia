Learning Objectives

Learn, Understand, Apply, Master the practices that turn releases into improvements.
Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Explain continuous improvement

Define continuous improvement in software and explain why repeated small changes beat rare large rewrites. A clear habit reduces recurring failures.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Run a blameless post‑mortem

Describe retrospective structure, facilitation practices, and how psychological safety leads to honest lessons and better actions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Choose and interpret metrics

Identify product and process metrics such as uptime, lead time, MTTR, and defect escape rate, and explain what each reveals.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Write actionable improvements

Translate metrics and retrospective findings into SMART actions, assign owners, and track outcomes to close the loop.
