Comparison Product vs Process Metrics

Product metrics

Uptime & Performance
Availability and response times that directly affect users.
User engagement & satisfaction
Active users, session duration, and survey feedback indicate value.
Bug density & defect escape rate
How many defects reach customers versus being caught internally.

Process metrics

Lead time & Cycle time
How long requests take to reach users and how long active work takes.
Deployment frequency
How often you ship changes; useful only with quality metrics.
Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR)
How quickly the team restores service after a failure.
Example scenario
If defect escape rate rises after a release, product metrics show customer impact. Pair that with MTTR and deployment frequency to decide whether to focus on testing, environment parity, or incident response improvements.
Tip
Track a small balanced set: one product metric and two process metrics to explain causes.
Misuse
Avoid optimizing a single metric without checking its effect on user experience or team health.
Key Takeaway

Use product metrics to see impact and process metrics to explain and improve delivery capability.
