Process Flow Post‑Mortem / Retrospective

Retrospective structure

A short, facilitated flow that surfaces facts, feelings, causes, and actions.

Steps
  1. Set context and invite psychological safety.
  2. Collect facts and timeline of what happened.
  3. Discuss what went well and what could improve.
  4. Create SMART, assigned actions and agree on follow‑up.
Concrete example
For a launch with config mismatches, actions might include a pre‑deployment checklist and a schema validation tool, each assigned to an owner with a deadline.
Common mistake
Writing vague items like 'improve deployments' without owner, measure, or date. Such items do not produce change.
Key Takeaway

Facilitate safety, capture facts, and convert lessons into SMART, assigned tasks with follow‑up.

Mini template
Project / Feature:
Describe the work or incident and date.
What went well?
  • Two positive observations.
What could improve?
  • Two improvement items with context.
Actionable items
  • At least two SMART tasks with owner and deadline.
Psychological safety
Make it explicit: this is blameless. Focus on systems and decisions, not people. That yields honest learning and better actions.
