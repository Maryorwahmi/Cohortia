Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Continuous Improvement
Ongoing efforts to refine products, processes, and practices driven by feedback.
Post-Mortem / Retrospective
Structured reflection to identify what went well, what to improve, and specific commitments.
Actionable Item
A specific, assigned task with measurable success criteria and a deadline.
Chapter Takeaway

Turn facts into root causes, convert those into a few SMART actions, measure outcomes, and document what you learn so the organization improves over time.
