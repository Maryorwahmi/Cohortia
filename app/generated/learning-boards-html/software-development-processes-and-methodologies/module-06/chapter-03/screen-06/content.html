Definition

User Acceptance Testing

Performed by real users or client reps to validate that the system meets business needs and is acceptable for release.

Why UAT matters

It ensures that the software delivers the expected value, not just correct operations. UAT reduces the risk of delivering a technically functioning product that users find unusable or misaligned with their workflows.

Pro Tip
Define acceptance criteria with users up front and use those criteria to structure UAT sessions and sign-offs.
UAT Scenario Example
Ask representative users to complete realistic tasks, such as placing an order and applying a discount, while you observe issues and gather subjective feedback about ease and clarity. Capture acceptance or requested changes as part of scope decisions.
Alpha vs Beta
Alpha is internal, Beta engages external users. Both validate user fit but differ in audience and environment.
Success signal
Users complete critical tasks with defined acceptance rates and minimal workarounds.
