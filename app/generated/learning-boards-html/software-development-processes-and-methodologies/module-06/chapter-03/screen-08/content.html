Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › UAT Best Practices
Plan & Recruit
  • Define business scenarios and acceptance criteria with stakeholders.
  • Recruit users who match real personas and environments.
  • Create realistic data sets for meaningful tests.
Pro Tip
Use observers to collect both objective task completion metrics and subjective user feedback for prioritization.
Run & Evaluate
  • Structure sessions with clear tasks and timeboxes.
  • Record issues, classify severity, and map to acceptance criteria.
  • Collect sign-offs tied to defined success signals for go/no-go decisions.
Watchout
Treating UAT as purely a bug-finding phase misses the point. Focus on whether the software delivers the intended business value.
