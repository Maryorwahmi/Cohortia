Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Alpha vs Beta
Alpha Testing
Internal testing by employees or QA teams. Use it to find early usability issues and validate feature completeness before exposing external users.
Who
Internal staff, product and QA representatives.
Environment
Controlled, development or staging environments.
Beta Testing
External testing by a selected group of customers in real-world environments. Use it to gather diverse feedback and detect issues that only appear under varied usage.
Who
Selected external users or customers.
Environment
Real-world conditions outside the development site.
Example
Alpha: product managers run end-to-end flows to confirm feature readiness. Beta: a subset of customers use the feature for a month and report usability and performance issues encountered under their daily use.
