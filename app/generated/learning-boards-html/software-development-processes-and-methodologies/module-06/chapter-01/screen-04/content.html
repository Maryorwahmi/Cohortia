Core Concept
What is SQA?
SQA is a proactive, system-wide approach that prevents defects and ensures products meet requirements.
Why it matters
  • Saves time and cost by finding issues early.
  • Reduces rework through standards and reviews.
  • Aligns teams on acceptance criteria and practices.
Key Takeaway

Treat quality as a continuous process: prevention, verification, validation, and maintenance.

Concrete examples & analogy
Building a house analogy
Inspect foundation and framing during construction to avoid structural failure later.
Process example
Requirements review prevents ambiguous acceptance criteria that cause rework.
Developer practice
Unit tests and coding standards reduce integration defects.
Common misconception
Quality is not just final testing. Effective SQA prevents many defects before code reaches testing phases.
