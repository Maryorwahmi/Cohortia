Quick Reference
Three short reminders to apply now.
Prevent Early
Use reviews and early tests to avoid expensive fixes later.
Write Clear Cases
Include preconditions, inputs, expected results, and post-conditions.
Balance V & V
Verify correctness and validate user fit to deliver real value.
Final note

Embed testing into everyday work: small, early checks compound into reliable releases.
