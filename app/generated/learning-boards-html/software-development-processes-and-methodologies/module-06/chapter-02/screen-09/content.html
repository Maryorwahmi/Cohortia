Strategies

Big Bang vs Incremental

Big Bang: Integrate everything and test. Pro: simple coordination. Con: failures hard to isolate.
Top-Down Incremental: Start with high-level modules and use stubs for lower-level parts to test interfaces early.
Bottom-Up Incremental: Start with low-level modules and use drivers to simulate higher-level callers.
Sandwich: Combine top-down and bottom-up to integrate in parallel for layered systems.
Trade-offs
  • Big Bang: slower debugging, may delay feedback.
  • Incremental: faster isolation, needs drivers/stubs for missing pieces.
  • Choice depends on architecture, team parallelism, and testing resources.
Practical Note

For microservices, prioritize integration tests for critical APIs and use contract tests to ensure compatibility.

Key Takeaway

Incremental integration gives earlier feedback and easier fault isolation; choose stubs or drivers when full components are unavailable.
