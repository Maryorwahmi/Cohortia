Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › The Sprint
Definition

Sprint — time-box of one month or less

A Sprint is a consistent time-box where the Scrum Team produces a usable, potentially releasable Increment aligned to a Sprint Goal.

Concrete example
Two-week Sprint delivering a tested feature that can be released if the Product Owner chooses.
Why keep Sprint length steady?
Steady length improves planning accuracy and provides regular opportunities to inspect and adapt.

Key Takeaway

Treat the Sprint as a learning cycle: keep the duration stable, produce a usable Increment, then inspect and adapt.
