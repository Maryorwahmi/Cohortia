Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Sprint Retrospective
Purpose

Inspect how we work and improve

A time for the Scrum Team to reflect on the Sprint and decide one or two concrete improvements to try next Sprint.

Practical prompt
One thing that worked, one thing to change, one experiment to try next Sprint.
Risk of skipping
Without the Retrospective the team loses its improvement loop and technical or process debt can accumulate.
Outcome
A short list of actions the team commits to try next Sprint and a way to measure whether the change improved things.
Key Takeaway

Make Retrospectives concrete: leave each meeting with one measurable experiment to try in the next Sprint.
