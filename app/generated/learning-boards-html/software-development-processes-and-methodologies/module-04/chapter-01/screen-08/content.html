Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Sprint Planning
Purpose

Decide what and how

Agree a Sprint Goal and build the Sprint Backlog from prioritized Product Backlog items with Development Team commitment.

Concrete rehearsal
Select two user stories, estimate capacity, and create a short plan for delivering them in the Sprint.
Anti-pattern
Product Owner dictating the Sprint Backlog without Development Team input reduces buy-in.
Planning checklist
  • Confirm Sprint Goal.
  • Select Product Backlog items and create the Sprint Backlog.
  • Estimate work and define the plan to deliver selected items.
Key Takeaway

Planning is a team commitment: the Development Team selects what it can deliver and defines how to achieve the Sprint Goal.

Check your understanding
From your backlog, pick two items and describe a proposed Sprint Goal that ties them together.
