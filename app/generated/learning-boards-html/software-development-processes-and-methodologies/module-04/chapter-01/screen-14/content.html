Roles
Product Owner, Scrum Master, Development Team — clear accountabilities avoid confusion.
Events
Sprint, Planning, Daily, Review, Retrospective — time-boxed for inspection and adaptation.
Artifacts
Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Increment — visible work and releasable results.
Final takeaway

Use clear roles, steady time-boxes, and living artifacts so inspection and adaptation become routine and your team continuously improves.
