Sprint Backlog

Plan for the Sprint

A visible picture of the work selected for the Sprint and the plan to deliver it; it evolves as the team learns.

Practical view
A board or list showing selected stories, their tasks, and current status updated daily by the Development Team.
Increment & DoD

Releasable result and quality standard

The Increment is the sum of done backlog items; the Definition of Done is the team's shared agreement about what 'done' requires.

Example DoD items
Automated tests passing, code reviewed, documented, and deployable to staging.
Key Takeaway

A clear Definition of Done prevents partial work and reduces technical debt by ensuring each Increment is potentially releasable.
