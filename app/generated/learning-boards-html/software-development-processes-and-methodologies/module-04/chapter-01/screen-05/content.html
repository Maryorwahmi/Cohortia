Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Scrum Master
Definition

Scrum Master — enable and protect

A servant-leader who coaches Scrum, removes impediments, and helps the organization adopt Scrum without commanding the team.

Concrete example
Facilitating a discussion that helps the team decide how to split a complex backlog item rather than assigning subtasks.
Common mistake
Taking over decision-making or assigning tasks, which reduces team ownership and learning.
Role actions
  • Coach the team in self-management and cross-functionality.
  • Facilitate Scrum events as a servant and ensure they produce the intended outcomes.
  • Work with the organization to remove systemic impediments preventing flow.
Pro tip

Measure whether team decisions increase ownership; if decisions come from the Scrum Master, coach toward more team-led choices.
