Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Sprint Review
Definition

Review and adapt

Present the Increment to stakeholders, gather feedback, and adapt the Product Backlog accordingly.

Concrete example
A stakeholder demo reveals a missing edge case; the team creates a backlog item and reorders priority based on impact.
Why it matters
Stakeholder feedback closes the loop between delivery and real user needs, ensuring the backlog evolves with real information.
Review checklist
  • Demonstrate the "Done" Increment.
  • Collect stakeholder feedback and discuss market or product changes.
  • Adapt the Product Backlog based on new insights.
Key Takeaway

Use the Sprint Review to gather meaningful feedback that directly informs backlog priorities and product direction.
