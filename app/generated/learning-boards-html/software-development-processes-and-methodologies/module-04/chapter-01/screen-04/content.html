Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Product Owner
Definition

Product Owner — maximize value

The Product Owner orders and refines the Product Backlog, represents customers and business stakeholders, and makes product decisions.

Concrete example
Choosing to prioritize secure login before a new homepage when user analytics show access failures.
Common mistake
Acting as a scribe instead of an empowered decision maker leads to unclear priorities and delays.
Why it matters
Clear ownership ensures the team builds outcomes that deliver measurable value, not just activity.
Backlog example
As a: Returning user
I want to: log in securely with two-factor authentication
So that I can: protect my personal fitness data
Acceptance Criteria:
- Valid credentials grant access
- Two-factor prompt appears after password
- Error messages show for invalid inputs
Key Takeaway

The Product Owner must balance stakeholder input and product strategy, and provide a clear, ordered backlog for the team to plan from.
