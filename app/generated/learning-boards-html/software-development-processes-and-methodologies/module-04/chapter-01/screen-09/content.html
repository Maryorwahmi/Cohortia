Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Daily Scrum
Definition

Daily Scrum — 15-minute sync

A daily inspection and planning moment for the Development Team focused on progress toward the Sprint Goal.

Good pattern
Each member states progress toward the Sprint Goal, plan for the next 24 hours, and any impediments.
Anti-pattern
Turning it into a detailed status meeting or problem-solving session reduces value and wastes time.
Micro-format
  1. Inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal.
  2. Create the immediate plan for the next 24 hours.
  3. Identify impediments and agree next steps for removal outside the meeting.
Key Takeaway

Keep the Daily Scrum short, focused on the Sprint Goal, and move problem-solving offline to maintain flow.
