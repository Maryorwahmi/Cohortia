Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Development Team
Definition

Self-organizing, cross-functional

The Development Team decides how to turn backlog items into a usable Increment and contains the skills needed to do so.

Concrete example
Developers, a tester, and a designer collaborate to deliver a complete checkout flow rather than passing tasks across silos.
Common mistake
Splitting the team into sub-teams with handoffs reduces ownership and increases delays.
Practices
  • Collaborative estimation during Sprint Planning.
  • Ownership of Sprint Backlog and daily inspection of progress.
  • Delivering a "Done" Increment that meets the Definition of Done.
Key Takeaway

A Development Team that is self-organizing and cross-functional is better able to deliver releasable value each Sprint.
