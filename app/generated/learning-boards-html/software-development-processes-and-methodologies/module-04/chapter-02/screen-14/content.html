Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Product Backlog Refinement
Ongoing activity to ready items for planning by adding detail and estimates.
Sprint Goal
A single concise objective that guides selection and trade-offs during the Sprint.
Sprint Backlog
Selected items plus the plan and task breakdown owned by the Development Team.
Daily Scrum
A 15-minute team sync to inspect progress and plan the next 24 hours.
Definition of Done
Team-agreed checklist ensuring consistent quality for every completed item.
Increment
A usable, potentially releasable result of completed Product Backlog items in a Sprint.
Summary

A continuous loop of refinement, planning, execution, review, and retrospective delivers value reliably and enables learning.
