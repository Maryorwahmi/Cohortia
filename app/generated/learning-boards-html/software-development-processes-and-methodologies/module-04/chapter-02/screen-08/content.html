Daily Scrum — 15 minutes

A tactical sync where the Development Team inspects progress to the Sprint Goal and adapts plans for the day.

Question 1

What did I do yesterday to help meet the Sprint Goal?

Question 2

What will I do today to help meet the Sprint Goal?

Question 3

Do I see any impediments that stop me or the team?

Tip

If a detailed problem arises, take it offline with the relevant teammates after the Daily Scrum to keep the meeting brief.

Key Takeaway

A focused Daily Scrum keeps the team aligned and surfaces blockers early.
