Definition

Product Backlog & Refinement

Continuous activity to clarify, estimate, and split backlog items so they are ready for Sprint Planning.

Top-of-backlog items are the most detailed and ready for Sprint selection.
Signals of readiness
  • Clear acceptance criteria
  • Small enough for a Sprint
  • Estimate or relative size
Key Takeaway

Regular refinement prevents surprise work and makes planning predictable.

Process steps
1. Identify large or unclear items
Highlight unknowns and dependencies.
2. Discuss acceptance criteria
Agree how success will be demonstrated and tested.
3. Split and estimate
Break big items into smaller stories that fit a Sprint.
Example

"Improve authentication" is split into smaller deliverables with clear tests like password reset and rate limiting.

Common mistake

Treating refinement as a single meeting; it should be ongoing and collaborative.
