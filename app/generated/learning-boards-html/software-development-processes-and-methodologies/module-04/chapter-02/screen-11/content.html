Increment and Sprint Review

Present the Done Increment to stakeholders, gather feedback, and update the Product Backlog accordingly.

What to show

Functional, tested features that meet the Definition of Done and deliver user value.

Stakeholder engagement

Use feedback to create, re-order, or clarify Product Backlog items for future Sprints.

Example
Stakeholders ask for a heat-map visualization of daily steps after seeing the Increment. The Product Owner adds a new backlog item with acceptance criteria for future refinement.
Anti-pattern

Delivering a demo without discussion misses the feedback loop that informs product direction.

Key Takeaway

Use the Sprint Review to validate the product and update priorities for the next cycle.
