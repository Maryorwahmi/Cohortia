Scrum process
Sprints and ceremonies
  1. Sprint Planning: set a Sprint Goal and select backlog items.
  2. Daily Scrum: short sync for progress and impediments.
  3. Sprint Review and Retrospective: inspect product and process.
Why it helps
Creates a predictable cycle for planning and stakeholder feedback, improving forecasting through velocity.
Kanban flow
Continuous pull-based delivery
Work items are pulled when capacity permits; there are no fixed-length iterations. Focus is on reducing lead time and smoothing flow.
Concrete example
A support team triages tickets and pulls the next highest priority ticket when ready. Urgent incidents can be addressed immediately rather than waiting for a Sprint boundary.
