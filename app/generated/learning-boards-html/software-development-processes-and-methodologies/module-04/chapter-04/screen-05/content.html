Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Roles and Responsibilities
Scrum
Prescribed roles
  • Product Owner: prioritizes backlog and represents stakeholders.
  • Scrum Master: coaches the team and clears impediments.
  • Development Team: delivers the increment and self-organizes.
When to prefer this
Stable, cross-functional teams that can commit to Sprint goals.
Kanban
Respect existing roles
Kanban overlays a workflow without prescribing new job titles. It focuses on how work flows rather than who has what title.
Concrete example
An IT support group keeps current shift leads and adopts a Kanban board to visualize tickets, set WIP limits, and measure lead time without role changes.
Common mistake
Adding Kanban visuals but not enforcing WIP limits or flow metrics removes the benefit while adding noise.
