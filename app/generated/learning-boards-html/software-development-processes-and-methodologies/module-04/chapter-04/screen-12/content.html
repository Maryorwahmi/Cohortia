Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Scrumban: Blending Structure and Flow
Common Scrumban pattern
  • Keep Scrum roles (Product Owner, Scrum Master) and Sprint cadence for planning and review.
  • Use a Kanban board inside the Sprint with WIP limits to manage flow and expose blockers.
  • Allow controlled interrupts with a defined policy for expediting urgent work.
Transition steps
Run a pilot for two sprints: set goals, measure velocity and lead time, and review whether flow controls improve delivery without harming stakeholder feedback.
Check your understanding
If interrupts are frequent but you value Sprint reviews, could Scrumban reduce disruption while keeping planning discipline?
