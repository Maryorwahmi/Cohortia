Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Key Concepts
Scrum vs. Kanban
Distinct frameworks: Scrum structures cadence and roles; Kanban optimizes continuous flow and WIP.
Time-boxing
Fixing activity duration creates predictable feedback cycles and commitments in Scrum.
Continuous Flow
Kanban delivers items as capacity permits, focusing on reducing lead time and smoothing flow.
Summary takeaway
Use predictability, interruption frequency, and organizational readiness to choose Scrum, Kanban, or Scrumban. Test with a short pilot and measure the right metrics.
