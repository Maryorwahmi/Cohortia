Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › What to Remember
Predictability
Stable backlog and team → Scrum for cadence and commitment.
Interrupts
Frequent urgent work → Kanban for flow and responsiveness.
Both
Need both → Scrumban: keep sprint rhythm and add WIP limits to improve flow.
Final takeaway
Treat selection as an experiment: pilot your choice, collect the right metrics, and adapt based on evidence.
