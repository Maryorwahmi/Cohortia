What is a WIP limit?
A numeric cap on how many items can be in a workflow state at once, used to control flow and surface bottlenecks.
Practical effect
Reduces multitasking, exposes blocked work, and encourages finishing before starting new items.
Key Takeaway

Use WIP limits experimentally to find a balance between flow and capacity.

Concrete example
Team sets In Progress WIP=3. Instead of starting more tasks, they focus on completing the three, which reduces handoffs and decreases average lead time.
Common mistake
Setting arbitrary WIP numbers without observing impact. Tune by measuring lead time and throughput after changes.
