Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › When to Choose Kanban
Decision cues
  • Unpredictable incoming work such as incidents or support tickets.
  • Need to minimize response and resolution time.
  • Desire to add flow discipline without changing roles or structure.
Example scenario
An IT operations team uses Kanban to visualize tickets, set WIP limits, and measure lead time so urgent incidents are addressed promptly.
Pro tip
Start with a visible board and one or two WIP limits, then iterate based on measured lead times.
