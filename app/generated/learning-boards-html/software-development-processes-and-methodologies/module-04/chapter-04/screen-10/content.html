Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › When to Choose Scrum
Decision cues
  • Dedicated, cross-functional team that can commit to a Sprint Goal.
  • Predictable backlog and need for regular stakeholder reviews.
  • Desire for a rhythm of planning, delivery, and retrospection.
Example scenario
A team building an analytics dashboard uses two-week Sprints to deliver small, testable increments and gather user feedback during Sprint Reviews.
Misconception
Don’t assume Scrum fixes all coordination problems; it requires commitment and role clarity to work as intended.
Try this
If you choose Scrum, run a two-sprint pilot with clear success criteria such as improved stakeholder feedback cadence and predictable increments.
