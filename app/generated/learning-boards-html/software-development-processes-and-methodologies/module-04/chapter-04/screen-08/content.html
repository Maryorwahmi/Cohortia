Scrum metrics
Velocity & Burndown
Velocity measures how much the team completes each Sprint; burndown tracks remaining Sprint work over time and helps forecast delivery.
When helpful
Planning and forecasting for new product development with stable teams.
Kanban metrics
Lead time, cycle time, throughput
These flow metrics tell you how long items take and how much you finish, guiding flow improvements.
Concrete use
Track average lead time and run experiments like lowering WIP limits to see if lead time decreases.
Measurement advice
Only track metrics that will change your decisions. If a metric does not lead to process experiments or planning actions, it’s noise.
