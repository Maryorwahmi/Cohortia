Scrum: Protecting the Sprint
Scrum discourages changing the Sprint Backlog during a Sprint so teams can focus on the Sprint Goal and deliver a usable increment.
Use when
You need reliable delivery windows and predictable stakeholder reviews.
Misconception
Protection is not inflexibility; it creates focused learning cycles and predictable increments.
Kanban: Embracing Change
Kanban allows high-priority items to enter the workflow as capacity allows, supporting fast response to urgent needs.
Use when
Work arrives unpredictably and responsiveness is critical, such as incident management.
Healthy constraint
Kanban requires explicit policies for urgent work; without those, flow becomes chaotic.
