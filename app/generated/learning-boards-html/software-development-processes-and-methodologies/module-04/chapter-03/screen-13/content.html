Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Key Concepts
Kanban
An Agile method focused on visualizing work, limiting WIP, and maximizing flow.
Kanban Board
Columns represent stages and cards represent work items moving left to right.
WIP Limit
An explicit maximum for how many items are allowed in a column at once.
Summary

Use these core concepts to explain why visualization and limits produce faster, more predictable delivery.
