Respect roles

Respect current roles, responsibilities, and titles

Kanban minimizes organizational disruption by allowing people to keep their jobs as you add visual workflow and policies.

Example
A content team keeps editors and writers, but adds explicit handover criteria between writing and editing.
Why it helps
Fewer political obstacles to adoption mean quicker gains from visibility and limits.
Encourage leadership

Encourage acts of leadership at all levels

Invite anyone to notice bottlenecks and propose experiments. Leadership is measured by action, not title.

Concrete case
A junior editor suggests a checklist to reduce rework; the team trials it and measures reduced cycle time in Review.
Misconception
Leadership acts are not extra tasks — they're small, focused improvements that improve team flow.
Key Takeaway

Keep people in place and create permission for everyone to propose and test small improvements that improve flow.
