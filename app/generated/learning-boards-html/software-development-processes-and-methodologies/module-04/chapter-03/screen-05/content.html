Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Four Foundational Principles — Part A
Principle

Start with what you do now

Map your current workflow and keep existing roles while adding visibility and small process rules.

Why this matters
It reduces resistance and allows immediate transparency without restructuring teams.
Example
A support team keeps existing escalations but adds columns for Triage and In Progress to see backlog shape.
Principle

Agree to pursue incremental, evolutionary change

Choose small, measurable experiments and observe their effect on flow rather than doing a large transformation.

How to experiment
Set a hypothesis, change one policy or limit, measure lead time and review results in a feedback loop.
Misconception
Incremental doesn't mean aimless. Experiments are small, measurable, and timeboxed for review.
Key Takeaway

Keep roles intact and change the process through small, measured experiments to improve flow safely.
