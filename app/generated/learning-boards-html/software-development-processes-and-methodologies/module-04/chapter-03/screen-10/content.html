Practice

Make Policies Explicit

Write short, testable rules that define when work may enter or exit a column to remove ambiguity.

Policy example
In Testing entry: passed developer tests and deployed to staging. In Testing exit: all acceptance criteria validated and no critical bugs.
Pitfall
Avoid long, theoretical policies — prefer concise checklists people can follow daily.
Practice

Implement Feedback Loops

Use daily flow-focused stand-ups and periodic service reviews to inspect and adapt based on metrics and observations.

Key Takeaway
Short, frequent reviews and concise policies create a feedback loop that powers continuous improvement.
Try this
Write a one-sentence entry and exit policy for two columns and review them after two iterations.
