What is Software Development?
An introduction to the full lifecycle: idea, design, build, verify, deploy, maintain.
Orientation

This lesson defines the software development process as a sequence of collaborative activities that produce reliable, user-focused software. Expect practical distinctions and team role descriptions you can use on day one.

House-building analogy
Imagine software like a house: plan, foundation, frame, finishes, and maintenance. That structure keeps complexity manageable.
