Practice Task

Choose three products you use and categorize each as application, web, mobile, system, embedded, or AI/ML. For each, write a short justification explaining where the software runs and why that classification fits.

Example: A navigation app on your phone — Mobile application; runs on device, interacts with GPS hardware.
Example: Your laptop operating system — System software; manages hardware and provides platform for apps.
Assessment Ideas
  • Multiple-choice checking embedded vs AI/ML distinctions.
  • Short-answer asking consequences of skipping documentation.
  • Role-description task for a hypothetical product team.
