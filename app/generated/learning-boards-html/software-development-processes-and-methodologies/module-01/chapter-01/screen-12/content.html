Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Hands-on Activity
Activity Steps
  1. Research: pick three products you use regularly.
  2. Categorize: assign each to a software type and justify with platform and constraints.
  3. Roles: for one product, list three essential roles and their contributions.
Tip: be explicit about where the software runs and what hardware or data it depends on.
Example Template
Product: Transit app — Type: Mobile application. Justification: installed from app store, uses GPS and push notifications; requires testing across devices. Roles: UI/UX Designer for mobile flows, Backend Developer for route APIs, QA for device compatibility.
Key Takeaway

Concrete categorization plus role planning demonstrates applied understanding and readies you for team discussions.
