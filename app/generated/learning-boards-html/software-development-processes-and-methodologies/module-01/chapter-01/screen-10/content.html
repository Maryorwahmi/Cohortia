Collaboration Practices
  • Shared acceptance criteria to align expectations.
  • Design reviews to catch usability issues early.
  • Automated tests as executable documentation.
  • Regular focused demos to gather feedback quickly.
Concrete Example
A team that defines acceptance criteria for a search feature will measure success by specific behavior like result ordering and performance, making testing precise and reducing back-and-forth after implementation.
Common Mistake

Mistaking meeting volume for clarity. Focused artifacts and clear responsibilities beat unfocused discussions.
