Classify Early
Decide whether the project is system, application, web, mobile, embedded, or AI/ML to guide engineering choices.
Document Clearly
Create minimal, clear acceptance criteria and specs so future work is efficient and predictable.
Define Roles
Name who owns product decisions, development, QA, design, and operations to avoid overlaps and gaps.
Closing Prompt

Before your next task, answer: what is the software type, what one artifact will reduce ambiguity, and who owns it?
