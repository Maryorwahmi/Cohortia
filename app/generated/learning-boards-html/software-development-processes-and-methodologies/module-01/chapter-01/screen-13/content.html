Top Misconceptions
  • "It's just coding." — omits planning and testing.
  • "Test later." — leads to expensive defect fixes.
  • "One person can do it all." — ignores specialized skills and scale.
Concrete Example
A team that skipped design shipped a confusing interface, causing customer complaints and a costly redesign. The initial speed was lost in the rework cycle.
Pro Tip

When pressured for speed, produce minimal but clear artifacts: one-page requirements, a short acceptance test, and a prototype to validate assumptions before coding.
