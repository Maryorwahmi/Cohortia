Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Who builds software?
Role Cards
Product Owner / Business Analyst — defines vision, writes requirements, prioritizes features.
Software Engineer — writes and maintains code; implements design and features.
QA Engineer — creates tests and ensures the product meets quality criteria.
Supporting Roles
UI/UX Designer — designs interfaces and user journeys to reduce friction.
DevOps Engineer — manages infrastructure, CI/CD, monitoring, and deployment.
Project Manager — coordinates schedules, stakeholders, and communication.
Concrete Scenario

E-commerce checkout: product owner defines flow, designer prototypes UI, engineers integrate payments, QA tests edge cases, DevOps ensures reliability under load.
