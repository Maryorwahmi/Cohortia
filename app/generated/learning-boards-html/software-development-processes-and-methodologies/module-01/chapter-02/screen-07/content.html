What is scope creep?

An uncontrolled expansion of requirements beyond the original plan. It increases work, disrupts schedules, and magnifies risk if not managed with formal change controls.

Example
A CEO requests multiple user-facing features midway through development, pushing out release dates and forcing rushed fixes.
Controls to prevent creep
  • Formal change requests with impact assessment.
  • Prioritization and trade-off discussions with stakeholders.
  • Clear acceptance criteria and a definition of done.
  • Reserve buffer time in planning for approved change.
Check your understanding
When a new feature is asked for, list who decides whether it replaces or delays existing work and how the decision will be recorded.
