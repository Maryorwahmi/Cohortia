Roles and Responsibility

Define who decides scope, who implements, and who verifies. This prevents blame games and unclear ownership that cause slowdowns.

Example
A product owner approves acceptance criteria so developers know exactly when a feature is complete.
Artifacts and feedback
  • Requirements and acceptance tests reduce ambiguity.
  • Demos and reviews create frequent alignment with stakeholders.
  • Single sources of truth (diagrams, specs) prevent duplicated work.
Misconception

Artifacts don't replace conversation; they focus it so teams spend time deciding, not guessing.
