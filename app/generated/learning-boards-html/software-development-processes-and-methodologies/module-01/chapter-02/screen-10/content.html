Visible uncertainty

Document assumptions and unknowns early. Use spikes or prototypes to verify technical feasibility before committing to a design.

Example
Before committing to an email integration, build a tiny prototype to verify throughput and authentication behavior, avoiding late surprises.
Checkpoints and CI
  • Regular stakeholder checkpoints make trade-offs explicit.
  • Continuous integration detects integration problems early.
  • Small, frequent releases reduce blast radius of errors.
Key takeaway
Reducing uncertainty early costs little and prevents large downstream failures.
