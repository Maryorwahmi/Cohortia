Module 1 › Chapter 1.2 › Key Concepts
Software Crisis
When complexity outpaced informal practices, causing missed deadlines and low reliability.
Technical Debt
Shortcuts that produce extra future work; manageable if tracked and repaid.
Scope Creep
Uncontrolled requirement growth that undermines schedules and budgets.
Takeaway
Adopt a small number of visible processes that align teams and prevent repeating costly mistakes.
