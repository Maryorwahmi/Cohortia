The V-Model: Verification and Validation in Tandem
Link development phases directly to testing phases so test planning begins early and quality is traceable.

Why this matters: when tests are drafted alongside requirements and designs, defects surface earlier and fixes cost less. For safety-critical systems, that discipline is essential.
