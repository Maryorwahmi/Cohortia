Module 02 › Chapter 2.2 › Advantages & Limits
Advantages
  • Early test planning reduces defect costs.
  • Clear mapping improves communication and traceability.
  • Well-suited to regulated, safety-critical projects.
Limitations
  • Sequential nature makes late changes costly.
  • Documentation overhead can slow development in fluid contexts.
  • Limited continuous customer feedback compared to iterative models.
Pro tip: assess project volatility and compliance needs before choosing the V-Model.
