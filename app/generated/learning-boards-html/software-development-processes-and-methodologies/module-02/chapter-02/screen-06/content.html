Module 02 › Chapter 2.2 › V-Model vs Waterfall
Shared traits
  • Sequential progression through defined phases.
  • Emphasis on documentation and plan-driven work.
  • Customer involvement mainly at start and acceptance.
Misconception check: the V-Model is not a fully iterative process; it organizes tests, but it still expects phase completion before moving forward.
What the V-Model adds
  • Explicit mapping from design artifacts to test cases.
  • Test planning begins during the corresponding design phase.
  • Improved traceability from requirements through tests.
When to prefer the V-Model

When requirements are stable and audits or safety standards demand strict traceability between requirements and test cases.
