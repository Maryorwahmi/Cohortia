Three-iteration plan (recipe platform)
Iteration 1 — Goal
Establish user access and basic viewing. Increment: registration, login, and recipe viewer. Feedback: registration usability and whether users can find recipes.
Iteration 2 — Goal
Enable user content. Increment: recipe submission and personal dashboard. Feedback: submission flow clarity and required fields.
Iteration 3 — Goal
Improve discovery and engagement. Increment: search by ingredient, ratings and comments. Feedback: search relevance and user engagement patterns.
Guiding questions
  • What is the riskiest assumption for this increment?
  • Can this increment be tested end-to-end without full infrastructure?
  • What specific feedback will change our backlog priorities?
Key takeaway
Structure each iteration around one testable increment and collect the feedback needed to inform the next cycle.
