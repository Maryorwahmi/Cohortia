Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Early feedback & risk
Early and continuous feedback
  • Short cycles increase opportunities to validate assumptions with real users.
  • Feedback guides prioritization and reduces wasted work on low-value features.
  • Usability issues are found sooner and fixed in later iterations.
Reduced risk
  • Small increments limit the blast radius of bad design choices.
  • Technical risks can be explored in early iterations.
  • Costly rework is minimized because problems surface sooner.
Concrete example
Releasing login early reveals authentication UX problems that are cheaper to fix before transfers and payments are built.
