Iterative and Incremental Development
How short cycles and staged delivery reduce risk and speed learning
Orientation

This chapter explains the difference between an iteration — a short cycle of plan, build, test — and an increment — a deployable, usable piece of functionality. You'll see how combining both yields frequent learning and usable releases.
