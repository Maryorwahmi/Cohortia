How these ideas feed Agile
Iterative cycles and incremental delivery are the operational heart of Agile. Scrum adds explicit roles and ceremonies to make those cycles predictable. Kanban applies incremental flow control to continuous delivery. Both rely on the same principle: shorten the feedback loop and deliver value early.
Scrum
Timeboxed iterations with reviews and retrospectives.
Kanban
Flow-based incremental delivery with continuous improvement.
Practical note
Mastering iteration and increment planning will make you more effective on Agile teams because you can create clear goals and testable increments each cycle.
Key takeaway
Iteration + increment = the practical mechanism Agile uses to deliver value and learn continuously.
