Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Iterative Development
Repeating cycles that refine the product and close the feedback loop.
Incremental Development
Delivering small, usable slices that add tangible capability over time.
Feedback Loop
Early review of working software guides the next cycles and reduces risk.
Summary takeaway
Use short cycles and small increments together to deliver value early and learn fast. This is the practical foundation for Agile.
