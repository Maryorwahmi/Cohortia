What is an iteration?

A single, complete cycle of planning, design, implementation, and testing that produces a working, improved version of the product.

Plan
Design
Build
Test & Review
Example
Iteration 1 builds login and balance view. Iteration 2 adds transfers and refines the login flow based on user feedback.
Misconceptions & pitfalls
  • Not a free-for-all: Iterations still require planning and definition.
  • Too large iterations: If cycles are long they delay feedback and learning.
  • Scope creep: Trying to add too much in one iteration can leave work unfinished.
Key takeaway
Iterations make product improvement systematic by limiting work to short cycles with a defined review at the end.
