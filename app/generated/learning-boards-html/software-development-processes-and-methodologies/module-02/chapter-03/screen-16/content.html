Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Key Concepts
Iteration
A repeated cycle that improves the product through review and refinement.
Increment
A deployable, user-valuable slice of functionality.
Feedback
The evidence you collect from users to guide the next iteration.
Final summary
Plan increments that are end-to-end, set short iteration timeboxes, gather the right feedback, and avoid scope creep. These habits make iterative and incremental work practical and effective.
