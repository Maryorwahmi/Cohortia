Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Advantages
Advantages
  • Predictable schedules and budgets driven by upfront planning.
  • Comprehensive documentation that supports audits and compliance.
  • Clear roles and formal signoff for governance and procurement.
Concrete example
In a regulatory payroll deployment, Waterfall's documentation and traceability reduce legal and compliance risk during audits.
Key Takeaway

Leverage Waterfall when documentation and governance outweigh the need for rapid iteration.
