Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Disadvantages
Risks
  • High cost to make changes after a phase is signed off.
  • Late discovery of requirement or design defects during testing.
  • Limited early stakeholder feedback and potential misalignment with users.
Mitigation
If partial uncertainty exists, use prototypes or contract phases to validate high-risk assumptions before committing the full project to Waterfall.
Common mistake

Applying Waterfall to rapidly changing product contexts where market feedback matters is likely to fail.
