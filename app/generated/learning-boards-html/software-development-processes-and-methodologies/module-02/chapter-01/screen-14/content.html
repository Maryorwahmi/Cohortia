Module 2 › Chapter 2.1 › Summary
Waterfall Model
A linear, sequential process where each phase must finish before the next begins.
Strengths
Predictability and thorough documentation support compliance and governance.
Risks
Late discoveries are costly and delayed feedback can lead to mismatch with user needs.
Takeaway

Use Waterfall when stability and traceability matter. Choose iterative methods when adaptability and early feedback are essential.
