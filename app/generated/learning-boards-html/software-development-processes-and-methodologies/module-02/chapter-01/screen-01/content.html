The Waterfall Model

A clear, sequential process where each phase must be completed before the next begins. Best for stable, well-known requirements.

In this lesson
We will study each Waterfall phase and when to use it.
Why it matters
Clear deliverables reduce ambiguity in regulated or safety-critical projects.
