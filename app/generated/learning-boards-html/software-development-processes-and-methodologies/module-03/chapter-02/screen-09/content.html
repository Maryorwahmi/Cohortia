Module 4 › Chapter 3.2 › Principle 6
Definition
Face-to-face conversation carries nuance and immediate feedback that written artifacts cannot reproduce.
For distributed teams, replicate richness with video, pairing, and collaborative work sessions.
Practical Examples
  • Synchronous story grooming for ambiguous acceptance criteria.
  • Paired debugging with screen sharing for tricky integration bugs.
  • Short co-working sessions for design alignment.
