Motivated Individuals
Teams do their best work when given trust, clarity, and the resources they need. Support and remove blockers.
Self-organization lets teams decide task allocation and architectures while leaders provide goals and constraints.
Example & Pitfall
A team chooses a lightweight architecture to meet a tight deadline. Pitfall: leaders override technical choices without cause, eroding trust and motivation.
Action: set a clear outcome and let the team propose multiple approaches; evaluate trade-offs together.
