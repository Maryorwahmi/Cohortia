The 12 Principles That Put Agile into Practice
Concrete guidance rooted in the four Agile values to help teams deliver value continuously and sustainably.

These principles show how to be Agile, not just do Agile. They guide decisions about delivery cadence, collaboration, design quality, and improvement cycles. Throughout this chapter you will see short scenarios and practical checks to apply each principle to your team’s work.
