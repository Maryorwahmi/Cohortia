Module 4 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
Welcome Change
Evaluate and prioritize late changes for customer advantage rather than accepting every request uncritically.
Sustainable Pace
Plan within capacity and avoid recurring overtime to preserve quality and morale.
Reflect & Adapt
Run small improvement experiments from retrospectives and measure their effect in the next cycle.
Final Takeaway
Combine evaluation of change, sustainable pace, and continuous reflection to build a resilient, adaptive team.
