Module 4 › Chapter 3.2 › Principle 4
Definition
Business stakeholders and developers collaborate daily to keep work focused on delivering value that meets real needs.
  • Make product decisions with engineering context.
  • Keep acceptance criteria current and testable.
  • Resolve ambiguities quickly to avoid rework.
Contrast: Documents vs Daily Conversation
Documents record decisions but cannot replace the nuance and immediate clarification afforded by ongoing collaboration.
Pro tip: designate one product contact per sprint who is reachable for clarifications and acceptance decisions.
