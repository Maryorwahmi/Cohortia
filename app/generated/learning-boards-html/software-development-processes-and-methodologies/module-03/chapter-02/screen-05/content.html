Module 4 › Chapter 3.2 › Principle 2
Definition
Welcome changing requirements and harness them for the customer's competitive advantage.
  • Expect requirements to evolve as you learn.
  • Evaluate changes for value and cost before integrating.
  • Design for change by limiting up-front assumptions and refactoring frequently.
Scenario — Startup adapts roadmap
User feedback points to demand for live video. The team reprioritizes the backlog, builds a small MVP, and measures uptake, giving them a market advantage.
Pitfall
Accepting changes without cost assessment or creating churn by abandoning short-term focus.
Pro tip: keep a lightweight impact assessment for late changes: value estimate, engineering cost, and risk score.
