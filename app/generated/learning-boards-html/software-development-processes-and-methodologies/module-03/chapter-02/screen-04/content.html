Module 4 › Chapter 3.2 › Principle 1
Definition
Early and continuous delivery means breaking work into usable slices that customers can interact with and from which teams learn.
  • Deliver features that are usable and testable by real users.
  • Use feedback to reshape priorities and reduce risk.
  • Measure value: usage, satisfaction, or business metric for each increment.
Key Takeaway

Plan for shippable slices with acceptance criteria that deliver observable value and invite customer feedback.

Concrete Example — E-commerce
Sprint 1: product catalog and search. Sprint 2: shopping cart. Sprint 3: payments. Each increment is usable and informs the next priority.
Common Mistake
Delivering prototypes that aren’t shippable or failing to measure user response after delivery.
Try this: identify a Minimum Viable Slice for your next sprint and write three acceptance criteria that show value.
