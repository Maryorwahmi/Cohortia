Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Responding to Change
Principle in practice

Short iterations and frequent feedback let teams adapt priorities based on new evidence rather than rigidly sticking to an initial plan.

Example

Market research reveals a different priority. The team reprioritizes the backlog for the next sprint and delivers a focused increment within weeks.

Avoiding chaos
  • Keep time‑boxed iterations to limit scope change impact.
  • Use a clear backlog and Sprint Goal to focus adaptation.
  • Document decisions that affect release commitments.
Key Takeaway

Adaptation is disciplined: short plans, clear goals, and frequent inspection keep change manageable and valuable.
