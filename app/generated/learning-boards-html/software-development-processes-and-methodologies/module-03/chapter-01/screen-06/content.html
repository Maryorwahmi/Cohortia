Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Individuals & Interactions
Claim

Effective communication, trust, and direct collaboration reduce delays and misunderstandings more than strict process adherence when the two conflict.

Example

Pair programming and frequent demos help identify incorrect assumptions immediately, preventing wasted work.

Pro tip

Keep one source of truth for status and use short, regular face‑to‑face or virtual syncs to preserve context.

Common mistake

Treating this value as an excuse to skip necessary documentation or to reject any process. The right balance keeps minimal documentation that enables interaction.

Check your understanding
  • What single process could you simplify to improve team conversation?
  • How would you measure if interactions produced faster decisions?
