Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Twelve Principles
Delivery & Change
  • Deliver valuable software frequently.
  • Welcome changing requirements, even late.
Collaboration & Teams
  • Business people and developers work together daily.
  • Build around motivated individuals; trust them to self‑organise.
Quality & Improvement
  • Continuous attention to technical excellence and good design.
  • Regular reflection and adjustment to become more effective.
Practical mapping
Action

Choose one principle and define a measurable action for this sprint, such as a demo cadence or a retrospective improvement.

Example

Deliver a small usable increment in two weeks, and collect usage feedback to reprioritize the backlog.
