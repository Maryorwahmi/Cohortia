Understanding the Agile Mindset

A concise orientation to why Agile matters and what you will master

This chapter replaces rigid, multi‑month plans with short cycles, early feedback, and customer collaboration. You'll learn the four values of the Agile Manifesto and the supporting twelve principles that guide iterative and incremental delivery. Expect practical examples and a short exercise to apply values to real scenarios.

In this lesson

Understand origins, values, principles, common mistakes, and how Scrum fits as a lightweight framework to practice Agile.

Why it matters

Teams that adopt an Agile mindset reduce wasted effort, get usable software earlier, and adapt to real customer feedback.
