Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Customer Collaboration
Principle

Collaborate with customers continuously to ensure the product adapts to real business needs as they change.

Scenario

Coffee shop owner asks for a loyalty program after market changes. Collaborate to create a minimal, testable feature and refine from usage.

Contract vs collaboration
  • Fixed contracts limit learning and slow adaptation.
  • Adaptive agreements or iterative deliverables preserve continuity and trust.
  • Use demos and short reviews to keep customers engaged and aligned.
