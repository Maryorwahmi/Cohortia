Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Key Concepts
Kanban
An Agile approach focused on visual signals, WIP limits, and flow improvement.
Kanban Board
Columns represent stages and cards represent work to visualize flow and queues.
WIP Limits
Caps on items per stage to improve focus and reveal bottlenecks.
Summary

Keep these concepts visible while you experiment: they will guide your choices and make results measurable.
