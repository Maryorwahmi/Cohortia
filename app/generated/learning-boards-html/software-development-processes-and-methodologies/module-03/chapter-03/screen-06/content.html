Respect current roles

Kanban does not create mandatory new roles. Retain responsibilities while changing system rules to improve flow.
Example
A tester keeps their testing responsibilities while the team adds a testing WIP limit to balance throughput.
Misconception
Respecting roles doesn't prevent process change; it simply avoids forced organizational restructuring.

Encourage leadership at all levels

Leadership in Kanban is about initiative: spotting bottlenecks, suggesting experiments, and documenting policies regardless of title.
Practical idea
Anyone can propose a one-week experiment such as changing the 'Definition of Ready' to reduce rework.
Why it matters
Distributed leadership creates faster experiments and continuous improvement because change does not wait for permission.
Key Takeaway

Preserve roles to reduce friction and empower everyone to propose small, measurable improvements that enhance flow.
