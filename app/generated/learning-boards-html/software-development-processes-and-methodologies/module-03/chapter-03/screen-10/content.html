Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Manage Flow

Identify bottlenecks

Look for columns with persistent queues and cards that age. These are signals a stage lacks capacity or has inefficient work.
Metrics
Lead time: total time from request to delivery. Throughput: items completed per period. Use these to measure the effect of experiments.
Example
If Testing has many queued cards, consider cross-training or automation, or lower upstream WIP to balance flow.

From diagnosis to action

Investigate root causes, propose a small change, run it for a fixed period, and then measure lead time and throughput.
Common mistake
Blaming people instead of testing system changes leads to resentment and hides systemic fixes.
Key Takeaway

Use flow data to guide improvements. Remove bottlenecks with small experiments and measure their impact.
