Feedback loops and experiments

Use short, focused meetings and measurable experiments to evolve your process.
Standups
Daily, flow-focused check-ins to unblock work and keep movement steady.
Service delivery reviews
Discuss outcomes with stakeholders and align expectations on delivery.
Operations reviews
Look at system-level metrics and strategic improvements.
Example experiment
Cross-train a developer to assist testing for two weeks and measure throughput changes.
Common mistake
Running experiments without clear metrics makes it impossible to judge their value. Always measure lead time or throughput.
