Kanban Overview

Understand how visual signals and flow-based practices help teams deliver value steadily. This chapter focuses on actionable practices you can apply without reassigning roles or imposing timeboxes.
Why this matters
Kanban reduces overload, makes problems visible, and leads to faster, predictable delivery.
Who benefits
Teams with continuous incoming work, operations teams, and individuals managing multiple small tasks.
