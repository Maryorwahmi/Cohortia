Process

Scrumban & other hybrids

Scrumban pattern
Use sprints for planning and reviews; use WIP limits and pull for flow management.
Kanban + ceremonies
Keep Kanban flow but add retrospectives and reviews for feedback and improvement.
Concrete scenario
Two-week sprints for feature delivery plus a Kanban buffer of two slots for urgent customer fixes, with rules for pulling and prioritizing buffer work.
Pitfall
Combining practices without explicit rules leads to confusion and lost accountability.
Pro tip
Define clear policies for how and when change is allowed, then use retrospectives to refine those policies.
