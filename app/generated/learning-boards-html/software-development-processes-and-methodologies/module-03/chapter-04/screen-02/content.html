Learning Objectives

Bloom goals from understanding to application

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Compare Scrum and Kanban
Explain how cadence, roles, and WIP influence delivery. You’ll be able to name specific trade-offs and use cases.
Venn mnemonic: cadence vs flow
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Identify other Agile frameworks
Recognize XP, Lean, and SAFe by their emphasis: technical practices, waste reduction, or scaling needs.
Icon mnemonic: wrench, leaf, tower
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Choose based on context
Use project predictability, team size, culture, and compliance to guide framework selection.
Timeline mnemonic: decide as late as possible
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Recognize hybrid options
Identify when Scrumban or Kanban-with-sprints helps teams balance predictability and flow.
Venn mnemonic: overlap of roles and flow
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
