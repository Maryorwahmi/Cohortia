Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Anti-patterns
Water-Scrum-Fall
Ceremonies exist but upstream and downstream processes stay waterfall, so sprints do not shorten delivery or reduce handoffs.
Fix
Extend inspection and automation: include stakeholders in reviews and automate release steps to reduce gate delays.
Kanban without WIP
A board without limits hides bottlenecks and fails to reduce cycle time.
Fix
Set explicit WIP limits and observe when columns pile up; use that signal to improve flow.
Over-adopting SAFe
Scaling without intent or training can shift focus to process compliance rather than delivery.
Remedy: pilot at program level, invest in training, and measure whether coordination overhead decreases inter-team friction.
