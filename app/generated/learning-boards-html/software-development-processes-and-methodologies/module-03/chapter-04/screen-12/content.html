Module 3 › Chapter 3.4 › Activity: Team Recommendations
Team A — Feature Innovators
Project: new, evolving mobile feature. Team size: 9. Delivery goal: usable increment every 2–3 weeks. Management: needs predictable reporting.
Recommended
Scrum with XP practices for engineering excellence: two-week sprints, sprint reviews, TDD, and pair programming.
Justification
  • Predictable cadence meets management reporting needs.
  • XP reduces technical risk during rapid iteration.
Team B — System Maintainers
Project: maintain large enterprise system. Team size: 5. Delivery goal: rapid fixes and continuous deployment. Work: unpredictable and interrupt-driven.
Recommended
Kanban with WIP limits and a clear SLA for urgent work to preserve flow and responsiveness.
Justification
  • Kanban supports unpredictable arrivals with pull-based capacity.
  • WIP limits prevent overload and reduce cycle time for fixes.
Hybrid option
Combine sprint-based feature work with a Kanban buffer for urgent tickets; define clear rules for what interrupts a sprint and how the buffer is prioritized.
