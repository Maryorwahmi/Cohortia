Definition

Scrum essentials

Scrum organizes work into Sprints and prescribes roles, events, and artifacts to support iterative delivery and continuous improvement.

Roles
Product Owner, Scrum Master, Development Team
Events
Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Review, Retrospective
Artifacts
Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Increment
Cadence
Fixed-length Sprints 1–4 weeks
Concrete example
A seven-person team uses two-week Sprints. They set a Sprint Goal, commit to backlog items, hold a daily synchronization, and demonstrate a working increment to stakeholders at the Review.
Key Takeaway

Scrum adds structure and predictability through a protected sprint and defined roles; it’s best when you can stabilize short-term scope.

Common misconception
Scrum does not prevent change — it provides controlled points for inspection and re-planning.
