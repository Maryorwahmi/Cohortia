Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Requirements Engineering
Discover, document, analyze, validate, manage requirements.
Functional Requirements
Describe observable system behaviour to be tested.
Non-Functional Requirements
Qualities like performance, security, and usability with measurable targets.
Stakeholders
Include users, owners, developers, testers, admins, and compliance roles.
Cost of Change
Fixing requirement errors becomes more expensive later in the lifecycle.
Validation & Management
Validate assumptions and keep requirements traceable as they evolve.
Summary

Clear, measurable requirements and early stakeholder validation keep projects aligned, reduce rework, and improve product fit.
