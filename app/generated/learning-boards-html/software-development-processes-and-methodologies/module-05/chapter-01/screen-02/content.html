Learning Objectives
Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Define requirements engineering

Explain the systematic activities used to discover, document, analyze, validate, and manage requirements. This skill helps you anchor projects in clear scope and reduce late changes.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Differentiate functional and non-functional

Write examples that separate what the system does from how well it does it. This distinction guides design, testing, and architecture decisions.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Identify and engage stakeholders

Recognize the groups affected by the system and gather diverse perspectives to prevent blind spots and biased requirements.

VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Understand consequences of poor requirements

Explain how ambiguity and omissions drive cost increases and project failures, and how early validation lowers this risk.
