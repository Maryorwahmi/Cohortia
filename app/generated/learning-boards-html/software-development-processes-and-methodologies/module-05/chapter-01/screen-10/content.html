Stakeholder mapping
Identify who matters and why
End users
Prioritise usability and feature fit. Example: students need fast searching and clear availability info.
Business owners
Care about value, market fit, and strategic priorities.
Operations & Admin
Focus on deployment, maintainability, and reliability.
Legal & Compliance
May impose constraints such as data retention or encryption requirements.
Concrete practice
Create a short stakeholder list, record each group's primary concerns, and invite at least one representative to validation sessions.
Common mistake
Relying on one champion to represent all perspectives; this leads to missing requirements and unexpected constraints.
Key Takeaway

Map stakeholders early, include diverse voices during discovery, and validate requirements with representatives from each group.
