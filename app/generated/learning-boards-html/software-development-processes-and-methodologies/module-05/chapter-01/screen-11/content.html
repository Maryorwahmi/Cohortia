Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Validation and Managing Requirements
Validation methods
Prove requirements reflect real needs
Walkthroughs
Review requirements with stakeholders step-by-step to catch misinterpretations.
Prototypes
A quick interactive example reveals usability issues and hidden expectations.
Acceptance criteria
Define how a tester or stakeholder will confirm the requirement is satisfied.
Requirements management
Track changes, maintain traceability
Change log
Record who requested a change, why, and which tests or designs it affects.
Traceability
Link requirements to acceptance tests and design artifacts to measure impact when changes occur.
Practical tip
Keep artifacts lightweight and focused; use prototypes or short test scripts rather than overly long documents.
Key Takeaway

Validation shows a requirement works for users; management keeps the team aligned as requirements evolve.
