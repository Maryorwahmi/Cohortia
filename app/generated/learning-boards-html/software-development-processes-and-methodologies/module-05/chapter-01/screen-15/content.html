Make it testable
Define acceptance criteria for each requirement.
Separate what vs how well
Functional = actions; non-functional = measurable qualities.
Validate early
Use prototypes or walkthroughs to confirm assumptions with stakeholders.
One-line reminder

Clear, measurable requirements plus early validation are the simplest way to reduce rework and deliver value.
