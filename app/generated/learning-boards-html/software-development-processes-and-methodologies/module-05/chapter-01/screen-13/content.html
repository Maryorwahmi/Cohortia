Consequences of poor requirements
Scope risk, cost, and missed needs
Common outcomes
  • Late rework and higher cost
  • Feature gaps or unusable features
  • Schedule slippage and lost trust
Assessment examples
Question: Which is a non-functional requirement? Correct: Encryption during transmission — it describes a quality (security).
Quick audit
For three requirements, ask: is it testable? who verifies it? what is the acceptance criterion?
Key Takeaway

Use small assessments and audits to catch ambiguity early and keep stakeholder trust by reducing late surprises.
