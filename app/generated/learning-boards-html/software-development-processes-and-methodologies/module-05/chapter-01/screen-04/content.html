Requirements engineering defined
Discover, document, analyze, validate, manage
Key activities
  • Discover: interviews, observations, stakeholder workshops
  • Document: clear, testable statements
  • Analyze: find conflicts and gaps
  • Validate: reviews, prototypes, acceptance criteria
  • Manage: versioning and traceability
Example — Online library

Discovery uncovered search needs. A documented requirement: "The system shall allow users to search books by title, author, or ISBN."

Misconception

Requirements are not a final static list; they change and need governance and validation.

Key Takeaway

Treat requirements as living, verifiable specifications that guide design and reduce costly rework.
