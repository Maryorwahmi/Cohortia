What is a Requirement?

A concise statement of a need or constraint the system must satisfy. It can describe a capability or a quality.

Why it matters

Clear requirements reduce ambiguity, lower late fixes, and guide design and testing decisions from day one.

Outcome

Requirements that are testable, agreed by stakeholders, and traceable through development.

Common mistake

Assuming stakeholders share the same understanding; that leads to late surprises and rework.
