Why run a workshop?
• Builds shared mental models across roles.
• Resolves conflicting priorities in the room.
• Produces rapid, collective decisions and artifacts.
Facilitation checklist
  • • Clear agenda and intended outputs.
  • • Short framing example or prototype.
  • • Breakout activities to include quieter voices.
  • • Defined next steps and owners at the end.
Example session
A workshop for short-video features: opening 10 minutes of user scenarios, 30 minutes of sketching flows in small groups, and 20 minutes to produce prioritized user stories and assignments for follow-up prototypes.
Common mistake
Assuming a workshop replaces interviews or observation. Workshops work best when informed by earlier individual research.
