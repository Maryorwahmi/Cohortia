Structured vs Unstructured
• Structured: consistent questions, easier to compare answers across stakeholders.
• Unstructured: conversational, reveals unexpected insights, harder to aggregate.
• Combine both when possible: structured core with time for open discussion.
Good question examples
  • • "Tell me about the last time you completed this task."
  • • "What part of the process causes the most delay?"
  • • "Can you show me an example or explain in detail?"
Practical walkthrough
Prepare 3 open-ended questions, run a 10-minute interview, capture direct quotes and the tasks described, then list possible follow-ups to observe or prototype.
Common mistake
Leading the interviewee toward a specific solution or using loaded terms. Stop and restate what you heard to confirm accuracy.
