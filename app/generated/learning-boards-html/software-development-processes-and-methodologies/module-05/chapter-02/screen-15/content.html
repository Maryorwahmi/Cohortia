Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Concrete examples
Always ask for a recent example to ground requirements in reality.
Validate quickly
Use a quick prototype or observation to test the riskiest assumption before specifying it.
Acceptance criteria
Attach observable conditions to stories so teams know when a story is done.
Final takeaway

Small, repeatable habits — ask for examples, validate assumptions, keep documentation proportional — make requirements reliable and reduce rework.
