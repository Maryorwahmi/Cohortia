Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Surveys & Observation
Surveys
• Good for measuring trends across many users.
• Use clear, short questions and avoid leading language.
• Best for prioritization after qualitative discovery.
Observation
• Reveals actual steps, workarounds, and context-specific problems.
• Often shows what users do, not what they think they do.
• Short sessions can reveal high-impact usability gaps.
Combined workflow
  1. Observe or interview a small set of users to form hypotheses.
  2. Create survey questions to measure those hypotheses at scale.
  3. Use results to prioritize features and follow-up research.
Practical example
Observation finds manual exports. A short survey asks how often users export data and why. If many users export daily, prioritize a built-in report feature and validate with a prototype.
