Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Prototyping
Purpose of prototypes
• Make abstract requirements concrete and testable.
• Reveal missing acceptance criteria and hidden flows.
• Prioritize learning: low-fidelity is often enough early on.
Practical tip
Prototype one interaction at a time and ask specific questions during validation, for example: "Can you complete this task? What surprised you?"
Concrete example
A clickable mock of the video trim UI reveals users want a clearer undo action. The team updates the user story and the prototype before finalizing acceptance criteria.
Avoid this mistake
Expecting a prototype to capture every use case. Prototypes validate assumptions, not specify every edge case.
