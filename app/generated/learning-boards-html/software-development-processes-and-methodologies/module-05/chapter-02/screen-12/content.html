Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Common Pitfalls
Leading questions
Avoid questions that suggest a desired answer. Instead, ask for examples of recent tasks and frustrations to learn concrete needs.
Over-specification
Recording too many UI details early makes requirements hard to change. Capture intent and outcomes, not fixed UI arrangements.
Single-method reliance
Relying on surveys or interviews alone misses behavior; triangulate with observation and prototypes.
No validation
Failing to prototype or test assumptions leads to rework. Validate the riskiest assumptions quickly.
