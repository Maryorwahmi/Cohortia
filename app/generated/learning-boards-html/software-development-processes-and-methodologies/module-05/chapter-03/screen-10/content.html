Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Iterative validation
Timeline

Prototype → Test → Refine

Short cycles focused on the riskiest assumptions yield fast learning about usability and feasibility.

Prototype
Low- or high-fidelity UI to explore interaction.
Test
Observe users or stakeholders and measure task completion metrics.
Refine
Update requirements and acceptance criteria based on evidence.
Metric examples
Completion rate, time-on-task, error rate, and perceived effort help convert subjective terms into measurable goals.
Prototype checklist
  • Define the question the prototype should answer.
  • Keep scope small; prototype only the risky parts.
  • Choose measurable metrics and collect evidence.
Scenario
For "intuitive task creation," prototype the form and observe whether new users complete task creation in fewer than five clicks on average.
