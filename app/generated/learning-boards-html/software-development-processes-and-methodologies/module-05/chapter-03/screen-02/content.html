Learning Objectives

Clear outcomes to practice writing and validating requirements.

Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Recognize high-quality requirements

You will identify SMART and other quality traits and explain why each trait reduces ambiguity and risk.

Mnemonic: SMART
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Analyze and resolve conflicts

You will use dependency mapping and stakeholder negotiation to resolve contradictions and prioritize needs.

Mnemonic: Dependencies map impact
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Validate with stakeholders

You will run walkthroughs, inspections, and prototyping sessions to collect actionable feedback.

Mnemonic: Prototype to reveal assumptions
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Make requirements testable and traceable

You will create simple test cases from requirements and map each requirement to design and tests using traceability links.

Mnemonic: Trace to reduce regression risk
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
