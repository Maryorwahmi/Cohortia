Process

Run focused walkthroughs and structured inspections

Preparation, facilitation, and clear outcomes make reviews productive.

Prepare: Share context and examples
Facilitate: Time-box and ask targeted questions
Capture: Record decisions and owners
Inspection roles
Moderator, reader, recorder, and reviewers — use a checklist to find defects systematically and produce traceable results.
Walkthrough checklist
  • Check SMART criteria and acceptance criteria.
  • Request concrete examples or prototypes to test assumptions.
  • Identify dependencies and impacted artifacts.
  • Assign owners to resolve each identified issue.
Pro tip
Invite at least one representative from the end-user or product side to ensure real-world concerns surface during the session.
