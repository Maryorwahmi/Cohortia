Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Validation techniques
Walkthroughs

Informal meetings to step through requirements. Good for eliciting immediate feedback and clarifying intent.

Inspections

Structured reviews using a checklist and roles to systematically find defects. Useful when traceable evidence is needed.

Prototyping

Build low- or high-fidelity interactions to validate UI/UX and to make subjective terms measurable through observation.

Test-case generation

Derive acceptance tests from requirements to ensure they are verifiable and to reveal ambiguities.

Comparison table
Walkthroughs — speed and consensus
Inspections — rigor and traceability
Prototyping — usability and clarity
Test cases — verifiability and regression safety
Scenario
For "task creation must be intuitive," prototype the flow with target users and measure completion rate; pair that insight with test cases for success criteria.
Pitfall
Sending a document and waiting for comments often yields insufficient feedback. Facilitate sessions instead.
