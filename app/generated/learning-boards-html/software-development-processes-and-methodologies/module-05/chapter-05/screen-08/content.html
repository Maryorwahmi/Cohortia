Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Risk Response Planning
Strategies
Avoid · Mitigate · Transfer · Accept
Document the selected strategy, assign an owner, and specify triggers and contingency actions for implementation and monitoring.
Avoidance
Change the plan to remove risk, e.g., choose proven tech over unproven research tools.
Mitigation
Reduce probability or impact: tests, cross-training, backups, or incremental delivery.
Transfer
Shift responsibility to a vendor or insurer, e.g., cloud provider for infrastructure.
Acceptance
Accept and prepare a contingency plan when cost of mitigation outweighs benefit.
Practice
For a prioritized risk, list the chosen strategy, the owner, triggers, and a one-sentence contingency plan. Review in the next status meeting.
