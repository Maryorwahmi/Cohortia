Learning Objectives
Master the practical skills to manage uncertainty and govern change in software projects.
01
Define risk management
Explain the process of identifying, analyzing, planning, and monitoring risks. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE

03
Plan risk responses

04
Operate change control
Run CR submission, impact analysis, CCB decision, implementation, and verification. VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
