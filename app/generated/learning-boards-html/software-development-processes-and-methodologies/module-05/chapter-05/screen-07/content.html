Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Risk Analysis
Method
Qualitative vs Quantitative
Use qualitative labels for speed and quantitative values when decisions require cost or schedule trade-offs. Capture assumptions for each probability estimate.
3x3 Matrix (sample)
High Impact
Medium Impact
Low Impact
Place risks into cells by estimated probability and impact to focus response efforts on high-high cells first.
Example
A data migration risk: probability medium, impact high. Quantitative estimate: 60% chance of a 2-week schedule delay; expected exposure equals probability times impact, which informs contingency budgeting.
Misconception
Ignoring many low-impact but likely risks. Aggregate frequent small failures to avoid surprise escalation.
Action
Pick three risks this week and assign quantitative or qualitative values, then decide which to mitigate first based on exposure and cost-benefit.
