Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Avoid vs Mitigate
Avoidance (example)
Choose mature tech instead of a risky prototype. Pros: eliminates the root risk. Cons: may reduce flexibility or add cost.
Mitigation (example)
Add integration tests and cross-training. Pros: keeps functionality, lowers impact. Cons: residual risk remains and costs testing effort.
Decision checklist
  • Estimate cost of avoidance vs mitigation
  • Estimate residual exposure after mitigation
  • Consider secondary risks introduced by avoidance
  • Document rationale and contingency plans
