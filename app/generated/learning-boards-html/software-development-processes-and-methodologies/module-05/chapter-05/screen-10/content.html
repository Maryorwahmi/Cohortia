Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Transfer & Acceptance
Transfer (example)
Use a cloud provider for infrastructure to shift hardware failure and maintenance risk. Include SLAs and monitoring in contracts.
Acceptance (example)
Accept a low-impact bug, but prepare a one-day hotfix plan and monitoring so you can act if reports spike.
Checklist
When transferring, record contract terms and monitoring. When accepting, record triggers and a concise contingency plan with assigned resources.
