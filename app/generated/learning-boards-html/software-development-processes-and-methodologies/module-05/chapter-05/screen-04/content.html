Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › What is a Risk?
Definition
Uncertain event or condition affecting project objectives
Treat risks as recorded items with an owner, trigger, and potential impact on scope, schedule, budget, or quality.
Example
A mobile app depends on a third-party API. Risk: the API may change and break compatibility, affecting release timelines and functionality. Owner: integration lead. Trigger: provider announces breaking changes.
Common misconception
A risk is not the same as a discovered defect. Risks are about uncertainty; defects are realized problems to fix.
Key Takeaway
Name and document risks early to convert them into actionable items with owners and triggers.
