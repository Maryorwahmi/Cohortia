Risk Management & Change Control
Understand, plan for, and govern uncertainty so changes become controlled improvements rather than sources of failure.
In this lesson
You will learn the cycle: identify risks, analyze probability and impact, plan responses, and monitor. You will also master a five-step change control workflow that preserves quality while adapting to new requirements.
Why this matters
Poor risk and change control leads to scope creep, rework, and missed deadlines. Structured approaches reduce surprises and protect quality.
Explore key topics
Risk Identification
Techniques like brainstorming and checklists find hazards early.
Risk Response
Avoid, mitigate, transfer, or accept risks with planned actions.
Change Control
Formal steps for CR submission, impact analysis, approval, and verification.
Visual Concept
R
Risk Register
A living list of identified risks with owners and triggers.
C
Change Requests
Formal CRs that enter a review and approval cycle.
Connecting lines indicate that approved changes update baselines and the risk register is reviewed after changes.
Quick takeaway
Treat risk management and change control as continuous, team-wide activities tied to configuration management and verification.
