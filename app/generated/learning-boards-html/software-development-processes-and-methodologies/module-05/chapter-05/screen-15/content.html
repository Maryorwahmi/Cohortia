Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Key Concepts
Risk
An uncertain event or condition that may affect objectives.
Risk Management
Identify, analyze, plan responses, and monitor risks throughout the lifecycle.
Change Control
Formal process to evaluate, approve, and implement changes with verification.
Takeaway
Keep risk items live, document responses, use CM for traceability, and run a formal change process to avoid hidden rework and scope creep.
