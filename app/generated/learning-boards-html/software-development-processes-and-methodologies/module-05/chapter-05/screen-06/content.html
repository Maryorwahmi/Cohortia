Module 5 › Chapter 5.5 › Common Software Risks
Scope Creep
Uncontrolled requirements growth. Owner: Product lead. Response: Strict CR process and scope baselines.
Technical Debt
Shortcuts causing future rework. Owner: Architecture lead. Response: Schedule refactor sprints and track debt items.
Key Personnel
Loss or unavailability of critical staff. Owner: Project manager. Response: Cross-training and documentation.
Unrealistic Estimates
Underestimated effort leads to delay. Owner: Planning lead. Response: Re-estimate with historical data and buffer time.
Technology Obsolescence
Platform or library becomes unsupported. Owner: Tech lead. Response: Monitor vendor roadmaps and choose mature tech.
Integration Failures
Components fail to integrate. Owner: Integration lead. Response: Automated integration tests and early end-to-end checks.
Practice
List two risks from each category for your project and mark their current probability and impact. Assign owners so the team knows who watches triggers.
