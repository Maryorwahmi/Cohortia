Definition

Scope Creep

The uncontrolled expansion of project scope without adjusting time, cost, or resources. It creates schedule risk and budget overruns.

Illustrative Scenario
Agree to a website redesign, later accept requests for an analytics dashboard and live chat without changing the deadline. Result: missed delivery and team overload.
Best Practice

Use a formal change control log: request, impact estimate, decision, and updated plan.

Change Control Checklist
  • Log each new requirement with a date and requester.
  • Estimate impact on time, cost and resources.
  • Discuss trade-offs with stakeholders and decide.
  • Update the baseline and communicate changes to the team.
Common Mistake
Accepting changes informally and relying on goodwill. This hides real impacts and erodes trust.
Key Takeaway

A change control process protects delivery and makes trade-offs explicit.
