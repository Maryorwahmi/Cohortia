Resource Allocation Workflow

  1. Map each WBS work package to required roles and tools.
  2. Check calendars and license inventories for availability.
  3. Identify conflicts and evaluate options: shift, add capacity, or reduce scope.
  4. Agree decisions with stakeholders and update schedule and budget.
Resource Matrix (example)
Columns: Weeks. Rows: Roles. Cells: percent allocation. Use this to find over-allocations visually.
Conflict Resolution Options
Shift Dates
Delay lower-priority tasks to free key people for critical work.
Add Capacity
Hire, contract, or borrow resources to shorten critical tasks at a cost.
Reduce Scope
Remove or defer non-essential deliverables to meet deadlines within budget.
Key Takeaway

Resolve resource conflicts by balancing time, cost and scope while documenting decisions and assumptions.
