Core Concept

Project Triangle

Scope, Time, and Cost are linked. Change one and at least one other will change too.

Use as a negotiation tool
When a stakeholder requests an addition, present the options: extend time, add cost, or reduce other scope items.
Misconception
Believing scope can expand without impact. Every addition has cost or schedule implications.
Practical Example
If stakeholders want an extra reporting dashboard late in the project, show them three options: delay delivery by X weeks, add Y dollars to hire two contractors, or drop another feature of similar effort. This frame helps make decisions explicit.
Key Takeaway

Explicitly present trade-offs and get stakeholder agreement on which constraint to move when changes occur.
