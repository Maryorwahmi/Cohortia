Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Learning Objectives

Master practical Graph Editor skills

These objectives combine navigation, analysis, and edit techniques you will use to refine timing and spacing.

01
Navigate and interpret the Graph Editor

Open the editor, inspect the attribute list, and identify keys and curves so you can select the right channels to edit. This matters because accurate selection prevents accidental edits to other attributes.

Verified competency milestone
02
Identify curve shapes and motion impact

Link curve steepness and flatness to speed and holds so you can predict how an attribute will move between keys. This helps you diagnose unwanted motion like spikes or pops.

Verified competency milestone
03
Apply tangent types to shape motion

Choose Linear, Spline, Flat, Stepped, Auto, or Clamped tangents to achieve specific behaviors, from mechanical motion to organic arcs and holds.

Verified competency milestone
04
Break and manipulate tangents for nuance

Break tangents to control incoming and outgoing slopes independently, enabling slow-ins, quick-outs, and precise impacts without altering the other side of the key.

Verified competency milestone
