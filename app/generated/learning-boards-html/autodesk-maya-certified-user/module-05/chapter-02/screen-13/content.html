Module 5 › Chapter 5.2 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Tangent
Handle that controls the curve slope into and out of a keyframe.
Break Tangents
Function to separate incoming and outgoing handles for independent shaping.
Overshoot / Undershoot
When curves exceed or fall short of targets; use clamped tangents or handle edits to control it.
Summary
Use tangents and tangent types with purpose, break tangents when you need asymmetry, and clean curves to produce predictable, polished motion.
