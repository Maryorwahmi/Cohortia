Objective checks and common misconceptions

Each card lists a practical action and a short misconception to watch for during edits.

Verify selection

Action: Confirm attribute channel before editing. Misconception: "Auto selected the right curve" is risky — check it yourself.

Mnemonic: tiny checkbox of attention
Read slope for speed

Action: Judge steepness to predict velocity. Misconception: A smooth curve is not always correct; it may overshoot.

Mnemonic: slope = speed
Pick tangent types intentionally

Action: Use Linear for mechanical moves, Flat for hang, Stepped for blocking. Misconception: Don’t leave everything on Auto.

Mnemonic: choose purpose over default
Break tangents when needed

Action: Use Break Tangents to separate incoming and outgoing influence. Misconception: Symmetric handles are not always desirable.

Mnemonic: break to specialize
