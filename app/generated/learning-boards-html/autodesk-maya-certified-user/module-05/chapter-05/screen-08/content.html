Definition › Skin Weights
What a weight does
Each vertex may be influenced by several joints. Weights specify the contribution of each joint to the vertex’s final position during deformation.
  • 0 means no influence; 1 means full influence.
  • Weights across joints are usually normalized so the sum equals 1.
  • Typical organic models use 2–4 influences per vertex for smooth control.
Example: Elbow Area
Vertices at the crease get blended weights from upper and lower arm joints to form a smooth bend rather than a hard hinge.
Pitfall
Stray distant joint influences cause unexpected pulls; remove them via painting and normalization checks.
Key Takeaway

Weights control how joints blend to move the mesh. Inspect visualized weights to identify and fix problematic influence areas.
