Assessment Idea › Diagnose a Knee Pinch
Diagnosis Steps
  1. Reproduce the pinching with the knee pose.
  2. Open Paint Skin Weights and inspect joint influences around the knee.
  3. Smooth abrupt boundaries and remove stray distant influences.
  4. Normalize weights and retest across the full motion.
Concrete Fix
Use the Smooth brush to blend the elbow/knee crease area, then remove any joint influence that causes a distant pull. Re-verify normalization to prevent scaling defects.
Success Criteria
Knee bends through walk cycle without severe pinching and with natural volume preservation.
Reflection Question
Which came first in your fix: removing stray influence or smoothing the crease, and why?
