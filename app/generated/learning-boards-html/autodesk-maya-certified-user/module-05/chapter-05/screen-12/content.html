Best Practices › Reliable Skinning
Guiding Rules
  • 2–4 influences per vertex is a good balance for organic meshes.
  • Use low-opacity strokes and Smooth after each paint pass.
  • Rotate joints regularly to validate changes across the motion range.
Pipeline Tip
Name your root joint and maintain a consistent hierarchy for later export to the game engine. Clear naming reduces integration errors.
Misconception
More joints equals better results — not always. Aim for necessary joints and good weights.
Check
After final painting, do common poses deform cleanly without pinching?
Reminder
Iterative tests win: small adjustments and re-testing produce predictable results.
Why it matters
Stable deformation keeps animations looking natural and portable to engines.
