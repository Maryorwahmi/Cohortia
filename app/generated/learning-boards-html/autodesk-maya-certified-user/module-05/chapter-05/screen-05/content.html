Process Flow › Create Joint Chain
Maya steps
1. Switch to Rigging menu set
2. Skeleton > Create Joints
3. Click to place shoulder, elbow, wrist, fingertip
4. Press Enter to finish
5. Adjust with Move (W) and Rotate (E) in Joint mode
After creation, rotate the parent joint to confirm children follow. Correct orientation early to avoid rework later.
Verification Checklist
  • Joint placed at anatomical axis.
  • Parent-child relationship behaves when rotated.
  • No unexpected offsets or flipped orientations.
Common Mistake
Placing joints off the rotation axis leads to unnatural deformation that is hard to correct with weights alone.
Key Takeaway

Strategic joint placement and correct parent-child setup are the foundation for reliable deformation.
