Hands-on Activity › Simple Arm Rig
Starter Steps
1. Create Polygon Cylinder, scale Y to 3
2. Duplicate twice and position for upper arm, forearm, hand
3. Mesh > Combine to merge segments
4. Skeleton > Create Joints: shoulder, elbow, wrist, fingertip
5. Press Enter to finalize
These steps give you a single mesh and a 4-joint chain ready for binding and painting.
Bind & Paint
Select mesh → Shift-select root joint → Skin > Bind Skin (Smooth)
Then open Paint Skin Weights Tool, select elbow joint, paint Add with low opacity and use Smooth. Rotate to test.
Outcome
A working arm rig that bends at the elbow with blended vertex influence and reduced pinching.
Reflection Prompt
After your first pass of painting weights, which area still shows pinching and what painting mode helped most?
