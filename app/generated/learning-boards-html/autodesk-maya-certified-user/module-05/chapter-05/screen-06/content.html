How joints transmit motion
Each joint is a pivot with position and orientation. Child joints inherit transforms from their parents, so a parent rotation affects the entire sub-chain.
  • Parent transforms are applied to children.
  • Correct local orientation prevents twisting artifacts.
  • Visual bones are guides; joint objects and their orientations are the functional elements.
Axis Alignment
Align local axes to expected rotation directions to avoid unexpected rotation coupling.
Debugging Tip
If a child moves oddly, check parenting and local orientation first before adjusting weights.
Example
Rotate the shoulder joint and observe the elbow and wrist follow due to hierarchy.
Common Mistake
Ignoring joint orientation leads to twisting during animation.
