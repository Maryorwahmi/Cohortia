Comparison › Skinning Issues
Pinching
Sharp creases at joints due to abrupt weight transitions or too few influences. Fix by smoothing and adding nearby joint influence.
Unwanted Influences
A distant joint pulls geometry. Visualize influence and remove weight via painting for that joint.
Normalization Issues
Weights do not sum to one, producing unexpected scaling. Use normalization options or manually rebalance weights.
Topology vs Weights
Don’t assume mesh topology is the cause; often rebalancing weights solves deformation without remeshing.
Workflow
Reproduce issue → visualize influences → paint corrective weights → retest.
Test Poses
Use expected motion ranges to validate fixes, not just neutral poses.
Reflection
When you see strange deformation, ask whether it’s joint placement, weights, or normalization first.
