Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Follow Through & Overlapping Action
Definition
Secondary motion that continues after the primary action and moves with timing offsets.
• Follow Through: continuation and settling after the main movement ends.
• Overlapping Action: parts move at different times; some lead, others lag.
• Use offsets and softened tangents for natural cascades.
Key Takeaway

Stagger timing for secondary parts so motion reads as continuous and physically plausible.

Concrete example — ponytail or flag
Ponytail
Animate root movement, offset keyframes for each segment down the chain so the tip lags and then settles with damping.
Flag
Different parts of the fabric move with varying delay and amplitude to mimic wind and inertia.
Misconception: staggering keys equals sloppy animation. In fact, staggered timing makes motion appear deliberate and physically grounded.
