Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Key Concepts
Squash & Stretch
Deform while preserving apparent volume to show material and impact.
Anticipation
A short preparatory motion that cues and strengthens the main action.
Follow Through
Secondary parts continue and settle after the main movement, adding realism.
Summary takeaway
Use scale and timing adjustments intentionally; small, well-timed changes make animations feel alive.
