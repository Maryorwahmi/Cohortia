01 — Bouncing Ball
Create a 48-frame bounce with scale keys at impact and peak. Preserve volume by compensating other axes.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02 — Jump with Anticipation
Add a 2–4 frame crouch before a jump to signal force; tune timing so it feels neither too long nor too abrupt.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03 — Secondary Motion
Animate a ponytail or flag by offsetting keyframes down the chain so motion cascades and settles.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04 — Combine All Three
Produce a short playblast showing anticipation, squash on impact, and follow-through for a polished loop.
