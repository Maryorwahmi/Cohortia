Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Animating Follow Through
Step-by-step
1. Block main body motion and key primary poses.
2. Select secondary joints or controllers and offset their keys progressively later in time.
3. Use Graph Editor to soften tangents and add decaying oscillation to simulate damping.
4. Playblast and adjust offsets until secondary motion reads as a smooth cascade.
Key Takeaway

Small, progressive time offsets plus softened tangents create believable follow-through.

Concrete example — ponytail chain
• Root follows body at frames 10, 20.
• Mid joint keys at frames 12, 22 (offset +2).
• Tip keys at frames 14, 24 (offset +4) with softer tangents and small oscillation keys after stop.
Use copy/paste offset in the Graph Editor to apply consistent delays and then tweak amplitude per segment for natural variation.
