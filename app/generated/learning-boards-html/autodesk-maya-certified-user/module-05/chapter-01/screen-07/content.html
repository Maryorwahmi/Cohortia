Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Auto Keyframe vs Manual
Comparison
Auto Keyframe
Automatically creates keys when you change attributes. Fast during blocking but risky during modeling.
Speed: High
Control: Lower
Risk: Accidental keys if left on
Pro tip

Enable Auto Key for early blocking, then disable it before modeling or detailed edits to prevent unwanted key creation.

Manual Keying
Use 's' or Key Selected to record keys intentionally. Slower but precise, essential for polishing and final timing work.
Speed: Moderate
Control: High
Safety: Safer for long scenes
Key Takeaway

Use Auto Key for rapid blocking; switch to manual keying for refinement to avoid accidental keys.
