Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Key Concepts
Keyframe
A recorded attribute value at a specific frame; interpolation makes motion between keys.
Time & Range Slider
Time Slider navigates frames; Range Slider restricts playback visibility and looping window.
Auto Keyframe
Automatically keys modified attributes; use for fast blocking but toggle off for careful edits.
Summary Takeaway

Record intentionally, inspect visual cues, and use Playblast to validate timing before sharing or finalizing work.
