Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Key Concepts
Hierarchy
Parent-child chains let children inherit parent transforms for predictable motion flow.
Pivot Point
The local origin for rotation and scale; align it to physical joints like elbows or hinges.
Constraint
Non-destructive relationships — Parent, Point, Orient, Aim, Scale — for flexible control and blending.
Summary
Design hierarchies intentionally, reposition pivots at articulation points, and choose constraints to keep rigs flexible and safe to iterate.
