Why use the Outliner?
  • Visualize nested hierarchies clearly.
  • Verify parenting, constraints, and transform nodes.
  • Rename and group objects for cleaner scenes.
Practical tips
Naming
Use clear names like Base, Arm1, Arm2 for easier selection and debugging.
Re-parent
Drag items in the Outliner to re-parent without risking viewport selection mistakes.
Check transforms
Expand transform nodes to see shape nodes and hidden children.
Pre-animation pass
Do an Outliner pass to catch hierarchy or pivot mistakes before animating.
