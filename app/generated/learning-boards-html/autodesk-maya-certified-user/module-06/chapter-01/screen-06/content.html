Architecture › Parent-child

Why hierarchies matter

Transforms applied to a parent propagate to children. This is how a shoulder rotation drives the entire arm chain in forward kinematics.

Outliner usage
Open Window → Outliner to view scene structure. Middle-mouse drag to re-parent nodes and tidy the hierarchy.
Practical pattern
Parent skeleton under a named root or global control so you can move the whole character without disturbing local joint rotations.
Scenario
If geometry is not parented to joints, the mesh won’t follow the rig during animation. Use the Outliner to ensure geometry is bound or parented correctly.
Common mistake
A messy Outliner with scattered nodes makes it difficult to select and keyframe controls. Regularly group and parent items to maintain clarity.
Key Takeaway

Use the Outliner to enforce a single root and clear parent-child flows so transforms propagate predictably and selection is reliable.
