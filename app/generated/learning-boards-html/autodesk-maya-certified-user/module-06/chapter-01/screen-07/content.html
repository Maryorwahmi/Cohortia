Forward Kinematics (FK)

Rotations applied to parent joints propagate to children. This is the default behavior of joint chains and is ideal for predictable, animator-driven keyframing.

Concrete example
Rotate shoulder joint to raise the arm, then rotate elbow to bend the forearm. The wrist follows elbow rotations as its child.
Contrast: IK
Inverse kinematics moves the end effector and solves parent rotations; use it for grounded foot placement or reaching tasks.
Misconception
FK is not old-fashioned. It provides deliberate animator control and is often combined with IK in advanced rigs for flexibility.
Key Takeaway

Use FK when you want sequential, predictable rotations and precise control over joint-by-joint motion.
