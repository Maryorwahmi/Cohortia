Module 6 › Chapter 6.1 › Practice Checks
Quick checklist
  • Place joints at pivot points. Test by rotating a single joint to watch natural bending.
  • Rename joints consistently. Example convention: L_shoulder_JNT.
  • Orient joints so X points down the bone and secondary axis is consistent across the chain.
  • Parent skeleton under a single root and test transform propagation.
Practice activity
Create a 3-joint arm, rename joints in the Outliner, show local rotation axes, orient joints with X as primary, rotate shoulder to confirm children follow, and re-parent under a root node. Repeat until axes behave predictably.
