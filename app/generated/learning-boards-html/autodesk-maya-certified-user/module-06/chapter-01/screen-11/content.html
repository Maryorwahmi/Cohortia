Build a simple arm

Follow these steps in Maya and verify at each stage to catch problems early.

  1. 1. New scene → Rigging workspace → Joint Tool.
  2. 2. In Side view, click shoulder, click elbow, click wrist → press Enter.
  3. 3. Rename in Outliner: shoulder_JNT, elbow_JNT, wrist_JNT.
  4. 4. Display Local Rotation Axes and Orient Joint with Primary = X, Secondary = Y.
  5. 5. Rotate shoulder and elbow to confirm FK propagation and check for skewed behavior.
Scenario
If the wrist does not follow elbow rotations correctly, inspect the joint orientations before changing skin weights or adding constraints.
Pro tip
Keep the Outliner visible during setup so you can rename and re-parent immediately; it saves time and prevents misparenting mistakes.
Key Takeaway

This short cycle — create, rename, orient, test — is the most efficient way to build reliable joint chains.
