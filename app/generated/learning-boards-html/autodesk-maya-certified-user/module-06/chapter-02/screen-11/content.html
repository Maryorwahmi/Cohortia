Definition › Artifacts & Fixes
Identify & correct
Use testing and painting to solve common artifacts rather than immediately remeshing.
Collapsing geometry
Cause: wrong joint influence reaching into adjacent regions. Fix: select the incorrect joint, Replace with 0 on affected vertices, then add influence to the correct joint and smooth.
Bulges & pinching
Cause: abrupt weight transitions. Fix: apply Smooth brush and refine local weights, check topology if smoothing doesn't help.
Quick diagnostic
Rotate the offending joint slowly. If far geometry moves, that joint's influence extends too far and must be reduced with painting.
Mirror weights
For symmetrical characters, use Mirror Skin Weights to copy corrections across axes and maintain parity between sides.
