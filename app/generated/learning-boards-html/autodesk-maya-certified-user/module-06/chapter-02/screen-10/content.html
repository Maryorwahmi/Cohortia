Core Setting › Max Influences & Dropoff
Max Influences
Restrict how many joints can influence any single vertex to keep painting manageable.
  • 3–4 is a common starting range for characters.
  • Lower values reduce complexity but may need extra painting in transitions.
  • Higher values increase smoothness potential but complicate painting and calculation time.
Dropoff Rate
Controls how rapidly influence decays with distance from a joint.
  • Steep dropoff confines influence to nearby vertices.
  • Shallow dropoff spreads influence and can smooth large deformations.
  • Adjust to mesh scale and test rotations to find the right feel.
Quick guideline

Start with Max Influences = 3 and a moderate Dropoff Rate. Increase or decrease based on test rotations and painting effort.

Example
An arm with Max Influences = 3 typically has shoulder, upper arm, and elbow influence at many vertices, giving a controllable and natural bend without distant joints interfering.
