Process › Performing a Smooth Bind
Step-by-step bind
Follow these checked steps to produce a clean initial bind.
1. Prepare geometry
Freeze transforms, delete history, and ensure even topology so weights interpolate predictably.
2. Select joints and mesh
Select root joint or joint set, then Shift-select the mesh to bind to those joints.
3. Set Bind Options
Choose Bind To, Bind Method (Geodesic Voxel recommended for complex meshes), Max Influences (3–4), and Dropoff Rate.
4. Bind and test
Click Bind Skin and immediately rotate joints to surface-test deformations and locate problem areas.
Key Takeaway

A careful bind with the right options reduces weight painting time and yields more natural deformations.

Options explained
Bind To: Selected Joints or Joint Hierarchy — choose based on selection scope.
Bind Method: Closest Distance vs Geodesic Voxel — Geodesic Voxel often improves initial weights on complex volumes.
Max Influences: Limit joint count per vertex to balance deformation quality and painting complexity.
Quick test
Rotate shoulder, elbow, and wrist joints after binding. Note pinching or collapsing and target those areas in the Paint Skin Weights Tool.
