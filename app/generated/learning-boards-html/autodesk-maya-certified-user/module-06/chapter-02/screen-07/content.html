Skin Weights explained
Weights determine how vertex positions are computed as a blend of joint transforms.
Invariant: weights for a vertex sum to 1.0, ensuring consistent blending.
Example: Vertex V = 0.7*UpperArm + 0.3*Shoulder produces a softer elbow curve than a single-joint influence.
Practice: Limit Max Influences to simplify painting; use Smooth brush to average neighbors for stable blends.
Common misconception
Automatic weights equal final weights. They are a starting point; high-deformation areas require careful painting.
Concrete number example
Vertex: shoulder 0.2 | upperArm 0.8 | sum = 1.0
Key Takeaway

Treat weights as normalized blending factors; paint to redistribute influence and keep the sum consistent.
