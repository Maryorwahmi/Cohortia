Comparison › Bind Method
Closest Distance
Surface-based distance; fast and effective on simple surfaces but can misassign across thin geometry.
  • Fast calculation.
  • May transfer influence across thin gaps.
  • Good for simple meshes and when performance matters.
Geodesic Voxel
Voxel-based volume approach; better for complex or thick meshes and often reduces manual fixes.
  • Respects mesh volume for more accurate initial weights.
  • Slightly slower but often saves time in painting.
  • Recommended for limbs, torsos, and characters with thickness.
Example
An arm inside a sleeve: Closest Distance may let shoulder influence show inside the sleeve; Geodesic Voxel typically respects the sleeve's volume and assigns cleaner weights.
Takeaway
Choose the method based on mesh complexity; test and then paint to refine.
