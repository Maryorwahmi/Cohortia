Competency Milestones
Inspect Weights
Learn to spot incorrect influence and test by rotating problem joints.
Mirror Strategy
Use symmetry and Mirror Skin Weights to speed up corrections on bilateral models.
Repeatable Workflow
Prepare geometry, bind, paint, test, and save versions to avoid repeating work.
Common Pitfalls
  • Too many influences per vertex makes painting hard and slow.
  • Unfrozen transforms or leftover history cause unstable binds.
  • Ignoring topology leads to unpredictable pinching during deformation.
Tip

Set Max Influences to 3–4 as a starting rule and refine by painting only where needed.
