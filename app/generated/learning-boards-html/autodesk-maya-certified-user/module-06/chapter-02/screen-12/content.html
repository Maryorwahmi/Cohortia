Pre-bind Checklist
Complete these steps before binding to avoid costly rework.
Freeze Transformations
Reset translate/rotate/scale so the bind uses neutral transforms.
Delete History
Remove construction history that can alter deformation behavior unexpectedly.
Confirm Joint Orientation
Correct orientation keeps rotations predictable and reduces odd deformations at bends.
Topology checks
Ensure even edge loops around joints and adequate subdivisions where deformation must be smooth.
Why it matters

Proper preparation reduces time spent re-binding and painting, and produces more predictable animation-ready rigs.
