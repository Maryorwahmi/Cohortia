Module 6 › Chapter 6.4 › Key Concepts — Part 2
Orient Constraint
Copies rotation only; use when translation is inherited elsewhere.
Point Constraint
Copies translation only; pair with orient if needed.
Circular Dependency
A loop of constraints that must be broken to restore stability.
Outliner Best Practice
Group controls and keep constraint nodes visible for faster debugging.
Freeze & Delete
Zero transforms and clear history before constraining to avoid snapping and hidden nodes.
Test Early
After each constraint, move and reset controls to validate expected behavior and catch errors early.
Final Takeaway

Small, consistent rigging practices — the right constraint, frozen transforms, and organized Outliner — prevent most animator headaches and keep your rigs robust.
