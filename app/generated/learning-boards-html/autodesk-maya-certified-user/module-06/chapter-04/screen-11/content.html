Common Mistakes & Troubleshooting

Recognize snapping vs. drive loops and the solid fixes for each.

Symptom: Control snaps on reset
Likely cause: transforms not frozen or parent transforms still active. Fix: unparent if necessary, freeze transforms with control in place, reapply constraints.
Symptom: Rig instability or Maya slowdown
Likely cause: circular dependency. Fix: trace constraint nodes in the Outliner and remove or reassign drivers to restore a one-way flow.
Prevention
Build and test constraints incrementally. Keep constraint nodes visible and use clear naming so tracing is straightforward.
When deleting constraints isn't enough
Sometimes you must fix parenting or reassign which object is the true driver rather than simply removing constraint nodes.
Warning

Circular dependencies can crash or freeze Maya. Resolve them promptly by breaking the loop and restoring a single direction of control.
