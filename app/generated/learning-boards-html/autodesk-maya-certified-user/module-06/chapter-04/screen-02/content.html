Learning Objectives

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master

Bloom's Bar
01
Design and create NURBS controls
Shape and color controls to match body parts so animators find them quickly. A circle for head, square for hips, arrows for feet improves readability.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Apply Parent, Orient, Point
Choose the correct constraint to transfer position, rotation, or both. This prevents unwanted overrides of upstream parenting.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Zero transforms and delete history
Freeze Transformations creates a predictable default pose so reset operations won't snap controls unexpectedly.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Organize and troubleshoot in the Outliner
Group controls, check constraint nodes, and prevent circular dependencies to keep rigs stable and editable.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
