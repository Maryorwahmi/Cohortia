Objectives — Practical Skills

These objectives emphasize organization, testing, and troubleshooting.

Outliner Mnemonic
Group controls into _CONTROLS_GRP
Keep all animator handles in one place. Makes selecting and exporting simpler for animation passes.
Testing
Move, rotate, reset
Verify that freeze transforms prevented snaps and constraints behave as intended under typical animator actions.
Troubleshoot
Detect circular dependencies
If constraints form loops, break the chain by reassigning drivers or removing redundant constraints.
