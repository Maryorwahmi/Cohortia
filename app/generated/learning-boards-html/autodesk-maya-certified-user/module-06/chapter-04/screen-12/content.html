Hands-on Activity

Creating Controls for the IK Arm

Follow the numbered steps and test after each to confirm expected behavior.

Preparation
Ensure shoulder_JNT, elbow_JNT, wrist_JNT, L_arm_IK_HNDL, L_hand_CTRL, and L_elbow_PV_CTRL exist. Freeze transforms and delete history on the two controls.
Step 1 — Hand Parent Constraint
Select L_hand_CTRL, Shift-select L_arm_IK_HNDL, Rigging > Constrain > Parent. Toggle Maintain Offset based on whether you want an immediate snap.
Step 2 — Pole Vector
Select L_elbow_PV_CTRL, apply Pole Vector constraint to L_arm_IK_HNDL so the elbow bend direction follows the PV control.
Step 3 — Organize
Create L_arm_CONTROLS_GRP and middle-mouse drag L_hand_CTRL and L_elbow_PV_CTRL into it for Outliner clarity.
Key Takeaway

Work step-by-step: freeze, constrain, test, and organize. Testing after each step prevents compounded errors.
