Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Activity: Cleanup & Export
Step-by-step Guided Tasks
01
Freeze Transforms
Ensure controls read zero rotation/translation and scale is one.
02
Apply Naming
Rename using side prefix and type suffix, e.g., L_shoulder_JNT, L_hand_CTRL.
03
Delete History
Select controls only; do NOT delete history on skinned geometry after binding.
04
Hide Helpers
Place joints/IK on RIG_HELPERS layer and turn visibility off for animators.
05
Lock/Hide Attributes
Lock scale and unwanted rotations on animator controls via Channel Box.
06
Simulate Export
Select root + skinned geo, choose FBX, ensure Skins and Smooth Mesh are enabled, and review joint orientations.
Keep a short log after each item noting what changed and why. This makes rollbacks and reviews far easier when handing off to animators or engines.
Concrete Caution
Never delete history on a skinned mesh after skinning. Doing so can remove the skinCluster and break deformations.
Why it matters

Clean files load faster, are easier to troubleshoot, and integrate smoothly into an engine or team pipeline.

Takeaway

Work methodically through the checklist; each small cleanup step reduces downstream risk.
