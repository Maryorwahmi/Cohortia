Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Quick Reference
01 • Naming
Use L_/R_/C_ prefix and _JNT/_CTRL/_HNDL suffixes for clarity and script compatibility.
02 • History & Transforms
Delete history on controls only; freeze transforms on animator controls; never delete skin history after binding.
03 • Blend Shapes & Nodes
Combine blend shapes with joint deformation and use utility nodes for deterministic behaviors that are easy to inspect.
Final Note
Small, consistent practices in naming, cleanup, and node-based design compound into a rig that is robust, fast to iterate, and friendly for animators and engines.
