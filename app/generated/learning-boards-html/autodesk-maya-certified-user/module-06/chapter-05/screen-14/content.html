Module 6 › Chapter 6.5 › Key Concepts
Rigging Workflow
A stepwise process from model prep to testing ensures consistent, animator-friendly rigs.
Naming Conventions
Use side prefixes and type suffixes such as L_shoulder_JNT and C_global_GRP for clarity and scripts.
Cleanup & Export
Freeze transforms, delete history on controls, hide helpers, check FBX options and joint orientations before export.
Summary Takeaway
A predictable, documented rigging process and disciplined cleanup prevents most production issues and makes your rig useful to animators and engines alike.
