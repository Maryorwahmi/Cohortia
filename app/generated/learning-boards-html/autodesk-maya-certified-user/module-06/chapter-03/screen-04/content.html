Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › FK vs IK
Forward Kinematics

Rotate joints to pose

FK is ideal for sweeping arcs and fine rotational timing because you animate each joint explicitly.

Example
Animating a shoulder-led wave using coordinated rotations down the arm.
Misconception
FK is not always easier — precise position tasks become tedious with many joints.
Inverse Kinematics

Move the end effector; joints follow

IK lets you place a hand or foot directly; Maya computes the required joint rotations to reach that target point.

Example
Placing a foot on a small platform by moving the foot control instead of rotating hip and knee manually.
Misconception
IK does not remove the need for a pole vector or proper joint orientation to avoid flipping.
Key Takeaway

Choose FK for rotational nuance and IK for target-driven placement; many rigs support both via a blend attribute.
