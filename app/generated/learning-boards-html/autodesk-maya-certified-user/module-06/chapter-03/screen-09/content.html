Module 6 › Chapter 6.3 › Activity: IK Arm — Setup
Step-by-step
  1. Confirm joint chain: shoulder_JNT, elbow_JNT, wrist_JNT and joint orientation.
  2. Create a NURBS circle at the wrist, rename L_hand_CTRL, freeze transforms, delete history.
  3. Create a NURBS circle or square in front of the elbow, rename L_elbow_PV_CTRL, freeze transforms, delete history.
  4. Group controls and tidy names in the Outliner so the rig stays organized.
Why freeze transforms?
Freezing makes zeroed transforms so parenting and constraints don't introduce unexpected offsets when you attach the IK handle later.
Example names
L_hand_CTRL, L_elbow_PV_CTRL, L_arm_IK_HNDL — include side, limb, and type.
Pitfall
Skipping joint orientation checks can lead to surprising rotations when the IK handle is created.
Key Takeaway

Proper setup and naming are small efforts that prevent large debugging time later on.
