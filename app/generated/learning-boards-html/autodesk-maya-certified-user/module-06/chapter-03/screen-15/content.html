Check orientation
Joint orientation affects solver results; confirm before creating IK handles.
Use pole vectors
Pole vectors define the bend plane for RP solvers and prevent flipping.
Provide controls
Animator-facing NURBS controls with clean naming and parenting make rigs usable and maintainable.

A robust IK rig balances solver choice, pole vector placement, and clear animator controls to produce predictable, production-ready motion.
