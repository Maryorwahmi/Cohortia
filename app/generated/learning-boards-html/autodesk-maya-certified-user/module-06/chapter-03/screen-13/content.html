Hierarchy

Names, groups, and parenting practices

Naming convention
Include side prefix, element, and suffix: L_arm_IK_HNDL, L_hand_CTRL, L_elbow_PV_CTRL. This reveals function at a glance.
Group logically
Place controls under a single rig group and isolate technical nodes in a hidden hierarchy for animator clarity.
Freeze before parenting
Freeze transforms to avoid offsets when you parent IK handles under controls or move hierarchy nodes.
Example Outliner
rig_GRP > controls_GRP > L_hand_CTRL, L_elbow_PV_CTRL; tech_GRP > L_arm_IK_HNDL
Key Takeaway

Consistent naming and grouping reduce confusion and speed up animator and pipeline work.
