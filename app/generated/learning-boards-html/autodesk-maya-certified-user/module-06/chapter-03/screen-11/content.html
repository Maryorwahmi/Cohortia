Symptoms

Identify and fix flipping and knee pop

Flipping
Often caused by a missing or poorly placed pole vector. Connect or reposition the pole control and test across poses.
Knee pop
Can occur when the IK handle passes a singular configuration; adjusting pole distance and joint orientation typically resolves it.
Naming issues
Ambiguous names slow workflows. Use clear prefixes and suffixes to find nodes quickly and avoid accidental selection of hidden handles.
Quick fix
If bending is unstable, disconnect the pole vector, reposition it far from the chain, freeze transforms, reconnect, then test again.
Remember
IK does not fix deformations; good joint placement and skin weights are still required for pleasing results.
Key Takeaway

Diagnose flips by checking pole connection and joint orientation, and standardize naming to make fixes faster.
