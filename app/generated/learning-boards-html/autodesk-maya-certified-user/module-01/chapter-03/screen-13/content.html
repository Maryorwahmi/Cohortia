Module 2 › Chapter 1.3 › Freeze & Delete History
Freeze Transformations
Resets Translate/Rotate channels to zero and Scale to one while keeping the object visually unchanged. This sets a clean local default for export.
When to use
After final modeling, pivot edits, and orientation checks — before rigging or export.
Check
Verify Channel Box values change to 0/0/0 and 1/1/1 but your geometry stays in place.
Delete History
Removes construction history nodes accumulated during modeling to reduce scene complexity and prevent export issues.
When to use
After modeling is finalized and before rigging, animating, or exporting.
Common Mistake
Deleting history too early and losing non-destructive editability during iteration.
Key Takeaway

Freeze transforms, then delete history as a final handoff ritual to create predictable, optimized exports.
