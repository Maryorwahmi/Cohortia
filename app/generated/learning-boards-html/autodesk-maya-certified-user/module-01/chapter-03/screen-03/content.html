Hotkeys & Mnemonics
W
Move — Think "Where" to place geometry.
E
Rotate — "Edge" pivot rotation or orient props.
R
Scale — Resize uniformly or per axis.
Pivot editing
Press D to enter pivot edit mode, position the pivot using the manipulator, then press D to lock. Mnemonic: D for "drop and drag" the pivot where it must rotate from.
Snapping & Cleanup Checklist
  • X — Grid snap to intersection points for modular layouts.
  • V — Point snap to vertices for perfect corners and seams.
  • C — Curve snap to follow paths or rails when placing assets.
Key Takeaway

Practice these hotkeys and cleanup steps as a pre-export ritual for predictable engine imports.
