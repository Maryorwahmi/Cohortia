Viewport layouts & display modes
Perspective view
Immersive camera view used for composition and final checks.
Quad view
Top, front, side and perspective combined for accurate modeling and alignment.
Wireframe
Inspect topology and edge flow without shading distractions.
Shaded/Textured
Evaluate form, silhouette, and materials for lighting and rendering decisions.
Example: modeling check
While creating a head model, use the quad view to adjust profile and frontal proportions simultaneously. Switch to wireframe to verify clean topology and back to shaded mode to assess the overall silhouette against a reference.
Key Takeaway

Different viewport layouts and modes reveal different problems; use them intentionally to catch errors early.
