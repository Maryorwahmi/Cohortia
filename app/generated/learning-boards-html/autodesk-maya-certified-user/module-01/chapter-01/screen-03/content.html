Five Short Drills
  1. Survey the main menu headings and note where modeling commands appear.
  2. Switch shelves and add a sphere tool to your custom shelf with the modifier click.
  3. Create a cube and practice orbiting, panning, and zooming until smooth.
  4. Cycle Q, W, E, R until tool changes are instant and muscle-memory driven.
  5. Arrange panels and save the workspace with a unique name.
Quick Metrics
Time each drill and aim to reduce by 25% after three repeats. Note which drill required a menu lookup; that area needs another short practice round.
