Time & Range Sliders
Use the time slider to move through frames and the range slider to define the playback window. When testing animation, loop the range you are refining to focus on a short sequence.
Example: set a key at frame 1 and frame 24, then scrub to observe the interpolation and adjust easing or tangents as needed.
Command Line & Script Editor
Watch the commands emitted when you perform actions to learn the scripting equivalents. This accelerates repetitive tasks when you convert common sequences into small scripts.
