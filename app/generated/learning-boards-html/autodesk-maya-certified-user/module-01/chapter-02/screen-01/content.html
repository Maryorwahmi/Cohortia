Understanding Scene Organization and Basic Objects
Create primitives, learn node roles, and organize scenes so assets remain manageable and export-ready.
In this lesson
How primitives, nodes, Outliner, grouping, and parenting form the foundation of scene structure.
Why it matters
Properly organized scenes make animation, iteration, and game-ready export far more reliable. Small habits like renaming and grouping scale quickly as scenes grow.
Visual Concept
Primitives: cube, sphere, cylinder — building blocks to model from.
Transform node: controls position, rotation, scale — interacts with tools.
Shape node: actual geometry and material info — separate from transform.
