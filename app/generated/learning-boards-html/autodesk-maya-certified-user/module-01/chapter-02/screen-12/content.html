Naming conventions and scene hygiene
Why naming matters
Consistent names enable easy selection, scripting, and engine integration. They also reduce errors during export because tools often rely on predictable names.
Practical patterns
  • Use Asset_Part format like Car_Body or House_Window_01
  • Use underscores or camelCase consistently
  • Include version or instance numbers when appropriate
Practice task
Rename Body, Head, Neck to Body_v01, Head_v01, Neck_v01 and group them into SimpleCharacter_GRP. This makes it obvious which items belong to the asset when preparing exports.
Key Takeaway

Naming and tidying the Outliner early prevents confusion later and enables robust pipelines for animation and game export.
