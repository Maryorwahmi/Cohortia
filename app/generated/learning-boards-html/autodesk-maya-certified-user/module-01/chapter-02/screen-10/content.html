Comparison: Parenting vs Grouping
Parenting
  • Creates inheritance of transforms
  • Used for mechanical or anatomical relationships
  • Children keep local transforms while following parent
Grouping
  • Creates a neutral parent for collective operations
  • Useful for packaging and export
  • Pivot often needs adjustment after grouping
Example workflow
Parent logical components (wheels → axle → chassis). Then group the entire assembly under Car_GRP for export. This maintains functional relationships internally while packaging the whole asset for transfer.
Key Takeaway

Choose parenting when behavior is important. Choose grouping when you need a packaging container without implying internal dependence.
