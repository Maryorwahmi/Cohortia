Assessment prompts
Scenario 1
You need a spaceship to move and rotate as one object in your game. Which two techniques do you use and why? Answer: parent internal parts to preserve local relationships, then group the top-level parent as Ship_GRP for export.
Scenario 2
You see two nodes for a new sphere in the Outliner. Name them and their primary roles. Answer: transform node manages position/rotation/scale; shape node stores geometry and materials.
Self-check
After completing the lab, answer these prompts and confirm behavior by testing move, rotate, and local child edits.
Key Takeaway

Use assessment scenarios to validate both knowledge and practical scene behavior before moving to animation or export.
