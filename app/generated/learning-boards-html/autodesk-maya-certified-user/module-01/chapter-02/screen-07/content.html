Outliner — hierarchy and control
What it shows
A tree of transforms, shapes, cameras, lights, and groups. You can expand nodes to inspect child items and their shape nodes.
Key actions
  • Double-click a name to rename
  • Drag items to change parent-child relationships
  • Select items for precise transforms and edits
Practical example
After creating Body, Neck, Head, drag Head onto Neck and Neck onto Body in the Outliner. The resulting tree shows Body → Neck → Head and confirms how transforms will propagate when the Body moves.
Common mistake
Relying only on viewport selection can cause hidden items to remain unselected or mis-parented; the Outliner gives an authoritative view of scene structure.
Key Takeaway

Use the Outliner continuously as you build. It keeps your scene readable and makes hierarchy changes predictable and reversible.
