Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Lambert vs Blinn vs Phong
Lambert

Matte, diffuse

No specular highlights. Ideal for dusty, rough, or non-reflective surfaces and simple game placeholders.

Example: old wooden barrel or clay pot
Blinn

Soft rounded highlights

Controls highlight size and intensity via Specular Roll Off and Eccentricity. Flexible for skin, plastic, and painted surfaces.

Example: character skin or glossy paint
Phong

Sharp, intense highlights

Produces focused specular highlights controlled by Cosine Power. Use for polished metal and mirror-like plastics.

Example: chrome trim or glossy plastic
Misconception
Blinn and Phong are not identical; they differ in falloff and highlight focus. Use the visual cue of highlight softness to choose.
Check your understanding
If a surface looks too flat but should be shiny, try a Blinn first; switch to Phong if the highlight needs to be crisper.
