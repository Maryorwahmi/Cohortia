Definition

Why highlights appear

Highlights occur where the view direction aligns with reflected light from a surface. They are shaped by material specular controls, surface normals, and the light source.

Blinn controls
Specular Roll Off and Eccentricity affect intensity and spread.
Phong control
Cosine Power tightens highlights for mirror-like responses.
Concrete example
Set Cosine Power high on a Phong to produce a tiny, very bright highlight like polished chrome; lower it for broader shiny surfaces.
Misconception check
Shininess is not achieved by diffuse maps alone; specular parameters and lighting produce gloss and highlights.
Key Takeaway

Match specular attributes to your reference first, then add textures for surface detail and realism.
