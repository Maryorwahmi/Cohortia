Practice routine

Rotate, compare, adjust

  1. Fix a directional light and camera start position.
  2. Assign a shader and rotate the camera slowly to inspect highlights.
  3. Tweak one attribute at a time, observe changes, and compare to reference.
Concrete tip
When matching reference, zoom into the highlight area and compare size and softness to determine whether to tweak eccentricity or cosine power.
Key Takeaway

One change at a time plus consistent lighting yields predictable, learnable results when refining materials.
