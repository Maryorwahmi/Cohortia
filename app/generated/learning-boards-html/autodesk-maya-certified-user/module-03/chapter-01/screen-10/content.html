Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Inspecting Specular Behavior
Observation routine
  1. Place a directional light at an angle that produces a clear specular reflection.
  2. Assign a Blinn or Phong to a sphere to compare highlight shapes.
  3. Rotate the camera slowly and observe how highlights move and change.
Interpretation: wide soft highlights imply rougher micro-surface; tight bright highlights imply very smooth surface.
Concrete test
Assign Blinn to sphere, increase Eccentricity then decrease it. Observe highlight widening and narrowing to learn the effect.
Troubleshooting
If highlight does not appear, confirm the shader type supports specular and that hardware texturing is enabled.
Key Takeaway

Use highlight behavior to infer surface roughness and then adjust Eccentricity or Cosine Power to reach the desired look.
