Module 3 › Chapter 3.1 › Key Concepts
Shading
How light interacts with a surface to produce diffuse, specular, and transmission effects.
Material (Shader)
Node that defines surface properties such as color, specular and transparency.
Hypershade
Maya’s node-based editor for creating, renaming, and connecting materials and textures.
Summary Takeaway

Use these concise definitions as a quick reference while creating materials: they represent the essentials you will build on in later shading and texturing lessons.
