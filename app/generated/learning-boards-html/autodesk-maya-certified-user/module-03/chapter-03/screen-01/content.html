Understanding UV Mapping Fundamentals

Why UVs matter: they let your detailed 2D textures wrap on complex 3D shapes without distortion. Learn how to unwrap, project, and arrange UVs for clean, game-ready assets.

Duration: ~10 min
In this lesson
UV basics, projections, editor, and common pitfalls.
Skills
Pick projections and spot stretching using a checker texture.
Outcome
Produce UV shells that fit the 0-1 space without overlap.
Explore Key Topics
Planar, cylindrical, spherical projections; 0-1 UV packing; avoiding stretching and overlap.
Visual Concept
0-1 UV space shown as a single square. UV shells must fit here for standard textures.
Why this matters

Proper UVs ensure consistent texel density, prevent overlaps that confuse baked maps, and let game engines sample textures efficiently.

Quick analogy

Unwrapping is like cutting cloth patterns for a garment. Each piece must be cut and arranged so printed details land where the designer intends.
