Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › UV Editor Workflow
Selection Modes
  • UV: move individual coordinates.
  • Edge: select island borders to cut or sew.
  • Face: project specific faces with mapping tools.
Workflow

Project → adjust with move/rotate/scale → relax/unfold → verify with checker → pack into 0-1.

Visual Diagnostic
Checker on model

Detect stretched or squashed areas immediately by looking at tile shapes on the 3D surface.

Projection manipulator

Align and scale the initial projection to minimize distortion before relaxing or unfolding.

Example

After cylindrical mapping a bottle, adjust the rectangular side shell so the checker squares are uniform, then rotate to align the seam where appropriate.
