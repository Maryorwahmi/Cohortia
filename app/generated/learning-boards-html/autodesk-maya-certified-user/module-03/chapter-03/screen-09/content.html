Round forms and pole issues

Spherical maps suit broadly rounded objects but create stretching near poles that you must manage with seams and shells.

Example

Project a character’s cranium with spherical mapping, then cut out the ears and face into separate shells for detail control.

Common misconception

Expecting a perfect unwrap from spherical projection alone. It’s usually a starting point that needs refinement.

Practical checks
  • Look for pinching at top and bottom poles with a checker.
  • Consider splitting high-detail areas into their own shells.
Key Takeaway

Spherical mapping is useful but not final; refine seams to control distortion where detail matters.
