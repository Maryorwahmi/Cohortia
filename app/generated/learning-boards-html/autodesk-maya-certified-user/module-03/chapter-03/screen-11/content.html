Module 3 › Chapter 3.3 › Stretching & Overlap

Diagnose and fix UV problems

Stretching distorts texels; overlap causes texture conflicts. Both are visible with a checker and require targeted fixes.

Stretching symptoms

Elongated or squashed checker tiles. Fix by reprojecting, cutting seams, or relaxing the UVs.

Overlap symptoms

Two parts sample the same texture region. Fix by separating shells and repacking within 0-1.

Example

A brick texture appears squashed because that wall’s UVs were compressed horizontally; scaling the UVs restores correct brick proportions.

Key Takeaway

Use the checker as a diagnostic, then choose between reprojecting, cutting seams, or relaxing to fix stretching. Separate and repack to remove overlaps.
