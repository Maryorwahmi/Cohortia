Module 3 › Chapter 3.2 › Key Concepts
What to Remember
Three compact concept cards to recall quickly.
Reflectivity
How much environment the surface mirrors.
Roughness
Microfacet scatter that blurs or sharpens reflections.
Incandescence
A visual glow; may not emit real light unless renderer supports it.
Summary
Tune these attributes together and preview under realistic lighting to judge material fidelity.
