Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Engine Preparation
Key export considerations
  • Decide atlas vs standalone: atlases require careful non-overlap packing and shared density planning.
  • Ensure unique UVs for baked maps so lighting and normal information are mapped correctly.
  • Maintain padding for mipmaps and compression to avoid edge artifacts in-game.
Scene hygiene
  • Delete history after UV edits to reduce scene issues.
  • Freeze transforms and verify UVs in the exported format if your pipeline requires it.
  • Document texel density and packing choices for downstream artists and engineers.
Examples & decisions
Atlas: pack multiple small props together with the same target density to save draw calls.
Standalone: give a hero character higher density and unique maps for facial detail.
Baking: ensure no overlaps if you plan on baking AO or lightmaps into textures.
