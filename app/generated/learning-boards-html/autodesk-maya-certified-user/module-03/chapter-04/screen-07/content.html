Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Texel Density
Definition & importance
Texel density is the ratio of texture pixels to physical surface area. It determines perceived sharpness and consistency of textures across a model. Mismatched density causes some parts to look blurry compared to others.
When to prioritize
  • Prioritize faces, hands, or other high-visibility areas that the player will inspect closely.
  • Lower density for distant or hidden surfaces to conserve texture space.
  • For atlases, ensure related assets share compatible density to avoid visible scale differences.
Practical checks
Use the UV Toolkit’s Texel Density tools or apply a checker texture and scale shells until squares match your target size.
Misconception: uniform maximum density wastes texels; instead, budget density by importance.
Outcome: consistent visual detail and predictable texture memory usage in-game.
