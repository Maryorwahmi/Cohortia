Advanced UV Editing and Workflow for Game Assets
A stepwise, production-minded approach to seams, unfolding, texel density, and packing for game-ready textures.
In this lesson
Plan seams, Cut & Sew, Unfold/Optimize, match texel density, and pack shells into 0–1 space for engines.
Why it matters
Prepares assets for painting, baking, and prevents lighting or sampling artifacts in-game.
Coming up

Packing
Layout, padding, and avoiding overlaps.
Visual concept
Seams create separate UV shells
Think of seams as fabric cuts; place them where they’re least visible or along natural creases.
Unfold and Optimize
Relax shells to reduce stretching and aim for a uniform checker pattern.
Pack and export
Maximize 0–1 space, add padding, and avoid overlaps for baking and atlases.
Key Takeaway

Intentional seams, careful unfolding, consistent texel density, and thoughtful packing are the steps that convert an unwrapped model into a game-ready, paintable asset.
