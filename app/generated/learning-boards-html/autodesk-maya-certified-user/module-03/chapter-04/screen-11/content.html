Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Common Mistakes
Frequent errors
  • Too many small shells: increases painting and packing complexity.
  • Unintentional overlaps: breaks baking and unique texturing.
  • Inconsistent texel density: causes uneven visual detail across the model.
  • Not deleting history: can cause unexpected scene behavior later.
Mitigation checklist
Before export: run a checker test, verify texel density, ensure no overlaps, and delete history.
During layout: favor larger shells that still unwrap without severe distortion.
For atlases: plan shared densities and avoid accidental overlaps between different objects packed together.
