Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Final Checklist
Quick production checklist
  • • Seam placement verified and hidden where possible.
  • • Unfold and Optimize applied; checker looks uniform.
  • • Texel density measured and matched across priority shells.
  • • Packed with shell padding and no accidental overlaps.
  • • Delete history and freeze transforms as needed.
  • • Mipmap or engine preview tested for bleeding.
Key Takeaway

A short, repeatable checklist prevents most UV-related export problems and keeps assets engine-ready.
