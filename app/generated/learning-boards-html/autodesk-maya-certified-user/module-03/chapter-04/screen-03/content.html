Practice checklist
  • Set up a polygon pipe and apply a checker material to visualize stretching.
  • Plan seams along less-visible areas and natural creases for minimal visible joins.
  • Unfold each shell and apply Optimize to remove minor distortion.
  • Measure texel density and scale shells until uniform across key surfaces.
Verification visuals
Uniform checkerboard across shells confirms minimal stretch.
No overlapping shells and proper padding prevent bleeding and baking artifacts.
Delete history after major UV edits to keep the scene stable for export.
