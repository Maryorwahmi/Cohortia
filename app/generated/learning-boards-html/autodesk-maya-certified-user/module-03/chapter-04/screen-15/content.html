Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Delete History
Why clean history?
History can preserve outdated operation nodes that later interfere with tools or export. Deleting history stabilizes your scene and reduces hidden dependencies that cause unexpected behavior.
When to delete
  • After finalizing seam cuts, unfolding, and packing.
  • Before big exports, baking, or sending assets to another artist.
  • Always save a versioned copy before deletion to preserve rollback capability.
Key Takeaway
Delete history to maintain a clean, reliable scene state; version first so you can revert if needed.
