Module 4 › Chapter 3.4 › Packing UVs
Layout usage
Run UV > Layout to automatically scale and position shells. Set shell and tile padding to a small positive value to prevent bleeding. Use the result as a starting point, then manually refine orientations and positions to fill empty regions.
Manual adjustments
  • Rotate shells to nest shapes into gaps and increase packing efficiency.
  • Group visually related shells so their density and orientation match when painted.
  • Avoid overlaps unless mirroring or texture sharing is explicitly intended.
Practical cards
Example: Fit three circular cap shells around a long cylindrical body by rotating and scaling to maximize the surrounding free space.
Warning: Insufficient padding leads to texture bleeding, especially after mipmap generation in engines.
Check: Reapply checker after packing to ensure scales and padding survived the layout process.
