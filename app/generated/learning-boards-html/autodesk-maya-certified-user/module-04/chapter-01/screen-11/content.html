Naming Conventions

Use clear prefixes and roles in names. Example: key_light_main, fill_light_front, back_light_rim. This helps selection, scripting and versioning.

Group lights into a single Outliner folder.
Store presets or scripts for repeatable setups.
Animate attributes on existing lights rather than creating new ones per frame.

Practical Example

Create a lights group and parent all lights to it. Use short descriptive notes in the scene to record typical intensity ranges and color choices for your project’s look.
Why it matters

Good organization prevents mistakes and speeds iteration during look development and production handoff.
