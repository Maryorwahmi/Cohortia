Light Type Comparison

Ambient
Uniform fill, flattens detail
Use sparingly
Directional
Parallel rays, sun-like
Outdoor lighting
Point
Omnidirectional, bulb-like
Localized
Spot
Cone with cone/penumbra control
Focused beam
Area
Planar emitter, soft shadows
Studio lighting
Volume
3D shape emitter for fog/rays
Atmospheric effects

Concrete Examples

Directional: Scene lit by a distant sun for an exterior daytime render.
Spot: A flashlight or stage spotlight with a focused cone and adjustable penumbra.
Area: A window or softbox to produce soft, realistic shadows and highlights.
Common Mistake

Relying on ambient light to simulate global illumination will remove directional cues; prefer area lights or proper GI for realism.
