Common Problems & Fixes

Overexposed: Reduce intensity, enable decay, or lower specular on materials.
Underexposed: Raise key intensity or add subtle fill rather than globally increasing lights.
Flat: Reduce ambient, reposition key to create directional contrast.
Harsh Shadows: Use area lights or increase penumbra for spots to soften edges.

Step-by-Step Diagnosis

  1. Mute all lights. Enable only the key and render.
  2. Add fill and render to compare shadow contrast.
  3. Toggle back light and check separation. Adjust intensities and decay as needed.
Pitfall

Fixing brightness by adding more lights often removes shape. Instead, adjust one source or its decay to restore form.
