Introduction to Lighting Principles in Maya

Explore the light types and attributes you'll use to shape form, mood, and depth in your 3D scenes. This chapter focuses on practical control rather than theory.

In this lesson
Identify lights, adjust attributes, and set up a three-point lighting scene to render a subject with depth.
Coming up
Understand shadow types, decay behavior and common mistakes to avoid flat or overexposed renders.
Practice
Follow a step-by-step activity to place directional, point, and spot lights and verify results in Render View.
Why this matters

Lighting reveals material detail and spatial relationships. Intentional lighting improves readability of your assets and helps sell the story behind a shot.
