Module 4 › Chapter 4.1 › Skills You’ll Practice
01 — Identify

Match scenarios to light types. Practice: label three lights in a new scene and explain your choice.

Mnemonic: Sun → Directional, Bulb → Point
02 — Adjust Attributes

Tune intensity, color and decay. Practice: create warm key, cool fill and observe render adjustments.

Mnemonic: Intensity = Brightness, Color = Mood
03 — Compose

Assemble a three-point setup and iterate positions. Practice: render three passes with only key, key+fill, and key+fill+back.

Mnemonic: Build in passes to isolate impact
04 — Troubleshoot

Diagnose exposure and shadow errors. Practice: reduce intensity and enable quadratic decay to recover realistic falloff.

Mnemonic: Reduce, Reposition, Re-render
Practice Tip

Save iterative renders as versioned frames to compare changes. This makes small lighting decisions visible and repeatable.
