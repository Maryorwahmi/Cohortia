Roles & Placement

Key Light — Primary source, strongest intensity, sets main shadows. Position: 30–45° angle, slightly above.
Fill Light — Softer, lower intensity (25–50% of key), often with shadows disabled to reduce clutter.
Back/Rim Light — Behind subject, creates separation and rim highlights to define edges.
Key: Directional/Spot
Fill: Point/Area
Back: Spot/Area

Workflow

  1. Create key and find a direction that sculpts form.
  2. Add fill with lower intensity to control shadow contrast.
  3. Add back light to separate the subject from the background.
  4. Render passes: key, key+fill, full. Iterate intensities and color.
Pro Tip

Name lights in the Outliner like key_light_main so you can mute and solo them during iterative renders.
