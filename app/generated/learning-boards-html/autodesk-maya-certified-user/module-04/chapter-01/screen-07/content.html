Shadow Types

Depth Map Shadows — Fast previews, adjustable resolution, may show aliasing at low settings.
Raytraced Shadows — Accurate contact and penumbra, higher render cost, preferred for final frames.
Speed: Depth map
Quality: Raytraced

Practical Controls

  • Shadow Density: reduces darkness without changing direction.
  • Shadow Color: tint shadows slightly for stylized looks or ambient bleed.
  • Resolution (depth maps): increase to avoid blocky edges; costs memory.
Example

Use raytraced shadows on the key light for crisp contact, and disable shadows on the soft fill light to avoid competing silhouettes.
