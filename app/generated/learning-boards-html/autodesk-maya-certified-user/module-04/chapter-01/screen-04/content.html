Lighting shapes perception

Light creates highlights and shadows that reveal curvature, texture, and depth. Thoughtful light placement draws the viewer’s eye and supports narrative choices.

Control attention
Use stronger key light or warmer color to emphasize important areas.
Create depth
Introduce rim/back lights to separate subject from background and define edges.
Avoid over-lighting
Multiple bright lights remove contrast and flatten forms — reduce intensity instead of adding more lights.
Key Takeaway

Think of lighting as sculpting; change direction and intensity to reveal shape, not just brightness.

Hands-on Visual Exercise
Place a single key light and rotate it around a sphere. Observe highlights and shadow shapes.
Note how a light directly above creates flat top lighting, while a 45-degree angle sculpts form.
Common mistake: adding a bright fill when the issue is poor key placement. Reposition key first.
