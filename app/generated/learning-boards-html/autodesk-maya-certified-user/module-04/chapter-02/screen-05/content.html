Process Flow

Create a Camera and Aim

A Camera and Aim rig keeps a target centered without complex rotation work.

1
Create › Cameras › Camera and Aim
Maya creates camera and aim transforms together.
2
Select camera › Panels › Look Through Selected
Now the viewport shows exactly what the camera sees.
3
Move aim to subject
The camera will rotate to face the aim automatically.
4
Adjust focal length & save
Set lens values and create a bookmark or keyframe if needed.
Example scenario

Create Camera and Aim, place aim on a cube at Z=-3, set focal length to 50mm for balanced perspective, and bookmark the shot as "Initial Shot".

Common mistake

Moving the shape node instead of the transform will produce unexpected results; always move the camera transform or the aim transform, not the shape.

Pro tip

Use the Outliner to select camera transforms and aim locators explicitly before editing.

Key Takeaway

Camera and Aim rigs simplify targeting so you can position the camera and let the aim locator keep the subject centered.
