Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Focal Length: Wide vs Telephoto
Comparison

Focal Length

Short values widen and distort; long values compress and isolate.

18mm — Wide Angle

Wide field of view, foreground exaggeration, suitable for establishing shots and dramatic perspective.

50mm — Standard

Balanced perspective that approximates a natural look, good for neutral framing and mid-shots.

200mm — Telephoto

Compressed depth, isolates subject from background, ideal for close-ups and dramatic pulls.

Practical test

Render the same scene at 18mm, 50mm and 200mm. Observe how foreground size, background compression, and perceived scale change. Use this comparison to choose the lens that supports your story.

Common misconception

Don't assume focal length alone controls perspective — camera distance and framing also affect scale and distortion.

Key Takeaway

Use focal length deliberately: wide for scale and drama, telephoto for isolation and compression.
