Composition

Frame with intent

Composition determines what the viewer sees first and how they move through the image. Use it with lens choices to tell your story.

Rule of Thirds

Place focal points along grid lines or intersections for balanced emphasis.

Leading Lines

Use scene geometry to guide the eye toward the subject; lens choice affects how those lines feel.

Negative Space

Use empty space intentionally to suggest scale or isolation, especially with wide lenses.

Concrete example

For a hero-in-environment shot, use a short focal length to exaggerate scale, place the hero on an intersection, and use leading lines to pull the eye into the scene.

Misconception

Lens effects don't replace composition. Always frame deliberately before relying on DOF or focal compression to rescue a weak composition.

Key Takeaway

Compose first. Use focal length and DOF to reinforce the composition and guide viewer attention.
