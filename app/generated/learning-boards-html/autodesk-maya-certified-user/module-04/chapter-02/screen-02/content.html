Learning Objectives

Learn, Understand, Apply, Master

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Differentiate Default & Custom

Identify Maya's default cameras like persp and create custom cameras for final renders or shot-specific setups. Verified competency milestone

02
Create Camera & Aim

Use Create › Cameras › Camera and Aim to build a rig where the camera points at a dedicated aim locator for stable targeting. Verified competency milestone

03
Manipulate Transforms & Aim

Move, rotate, and aim cameras directly or navigate through their view to craft precise compositions. Verified competency milestone

04
Adjust Focal Length & DOF

Tweak focal length, focus distance and aperture size to shape perspective and depth of field. Verified competency milestone
