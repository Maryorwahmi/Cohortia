Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Key Concepts
Default Cameras
Pre-existing navigation views like persp, top, front and side.
Custom Cameras
User-created cameras for controlled framing, lens and DOF adjustments.
Camera and Aim
A rig with a separate aim locator that keeps the camera pointed at a subject.
Focal Length
Short for wide-angle distortion; long for telephoto compression.
Depth of Field
Enable DOF, set focus distance and aperture to control blur and emphasis.
Bookmarks & Keyframes
Save camera views and animate Translate/Rotate over time for motion.
Summary

Treat cameras as creative tools: combine lens, DOF and composition, save shots with bookmarks, and animate with keyframes to tell visual stories.
