Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Sampling in Arnold
Definition

How Arnold samples light per pixel

Sampling adds repeated light samples per pixel so the renderer can estimate lighting, reflections, and shadows with less noise.

Camera (AA)
Primary anti-aliasing control affecting sharpness and overall sampling quality.
Diffuse / Specular
Diffuse for soft-shadow and GI noise; Specular for reflections and highlights.
Transmission
Controls quality of refracted and transparent surfaces.
Strategy
Increase specific samples for noisy areas instead of raising all values together.
Example & Tip
If reflections show grain, increase Specular samples. If soft shadows are noisy, increase Diffuse. Test with a single frame crop to save time.
Common mistake
Raising all sample types at once increases time unnecessarily; target the source of noise.
