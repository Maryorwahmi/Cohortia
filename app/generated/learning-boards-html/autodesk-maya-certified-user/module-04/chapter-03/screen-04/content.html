Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › What is rendering?
Definition

Rendering turns scene data into pixels

Rendering computes light paths, shading, and camera sampling to produce the final image or animation sequence.

Concrete example
A sphere on a plane with a directional light: rendering computes highlights, soft shadows, and surface response to the light which you then save as a PNG or EXR file.
Common misconception
Lighting alone won’t remove noise; sampling controls and resolution determine whether details are clean or grainy.
Key takeaway
Rendering is necessary to see and deliver your work; tune settings to match your quality and time constraints.
Visual checklist
  • Scene: geometry, materials, lights, camera
  • Render engine: chooses algorithm and performance characteristics
  • Settings: format, resolution, samples, frame range
