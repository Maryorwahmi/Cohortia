Purpose and workflow

Render Layers let you override specific objects or lights to produce separate renders for compositing. Render Passes (AOVs) break the final image into components like diffuse, specular, reflections, and Z-depth.

• Use a character layer and a background layer to adjust each independently.
• Export AOVs so you can tweak reflections or shadows without re-rendering.
• Passes are especially helpful for color grading and selective adjustments in compositing tools.
Analogy

Think of render passes as color separations in printing: each pass carries controllable information you recombine later.

Misconception

Passes do not magically fix bad lighting setups; they let you fine-tune components, but extreme problems may require re-rendering the scene or layer adjustments.

Key Takeaway

Use layers to isolate elements and AOVs to export components for non-destructive compositing and faster iteration between artist and compositor.
