Module 5 › Chapter 4.4 › Optimization
Lower Camera (AA)
Primary control for anti-aliasing. Drop for test renders, raise for finals.
Reduce Diffuse / Specular Samples
Lowering these reduces noise in indirect lighting and reflections during tests.
Render Region
Render a selected portion of the image to focus computation on areas under review.
Practical example

When lighting a character's face use a region render around the head, set Camera AA to 2 or 3 and reduce diffuse/specular samples temporarily. This gives quick feedback on lighting direction and material response without a full-frame cost.

Misconception

Turning every setting to minimum may hide real issues. Instead, selectively lower the most expensive channels for the test you’re doing.

Key Takeaway

Optimize selectively: lower Camera AA and specific samples, use render regions, and simplify geometry/textures where appropriate to speed iteration.
