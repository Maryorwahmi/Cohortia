What rendering computes
Render engines evaluate light, shading, and camera sampling to output pixels.
  • • Geometry and UVs provide surface position and mapping.
  • • Materials respond to lights; shaders produce color and specular highlights.
  • • Camera and sampling control anti-aliasing and noise behavior.
Concrete example

Render a lit sphere: the renderer calculates diffuse shading, specular highlights, and shadowing based on lights. A higher Camera AA reduces jagged edges but increases render time.

Common misconception

A viewport screenshot equals a render. In reality, viewport previews approximate lighting; full renders compute sampling and AOVs accurately.

Key Takeaway

Rendering produces final pixels; choose test or final settings intentionally to balance speed and image quality.
