Module 2 › Chapter 2.2 › Good vs Bad
Good topology
  • Loops follow contours and deformation lines.
  • Supporting loops near bevels preserve shape under subdivision.
  • Quad-dominant topology around joints.
Visual test: rotate with a matcap. Highlights will flow smoothly along the surface without abrupt changes.
Bad topology
  • Spoke patterns and poles in deformation areas.
  • Missing supporting loops near bevels causing pinching.
  • Excess triangles or n-gons near joints.
Fix: use Multi-Cut or Connect to reroute loops and Merge carefully to close seams.
Practice prompt

Make two versions of a beveled panel: one with supporting loops, one without. Compare deformation and highlights to see the difference in real time.
