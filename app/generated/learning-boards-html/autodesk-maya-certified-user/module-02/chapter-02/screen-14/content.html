Efficient habits
Small decisions add up to robust, export-ready models.
Plan loops first
Use Multi-Cut to place support loops before extruding or beveling.
Choose parameters
Match Fraction and Segments to the target platform: low for games, higher for still renders.
Cleanup intentionally
Delete history and Freeze transforms after finalizing shape; keep a pre-clean save.
Common mistakes

Extruding without moving faces, over-beveling, and using large merge thresholds are frequent sources of trouble. Small topology edits usually fix shading and deformation problems better than adding more polygons.
