Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Edge Flow
Definition

Edge flow is the routing of edge loops to follow major forms and joints so the mesh deforms smoothly.

Why it matters

Reduces stretching, makes skin weighting predictable, and produces clean UV islands for texturing.

Misconception

Adding polygons without planning loops rarely fixes deformation; you must route loops around joints.

Key Takeaway

Plan edge loops where the mesh bends. Concentrate detail at joints and reduce elsewhere.

Practical cards
Shoulder loop planning

Route circular loops around the shoulder and connect them to the chest and arm loops to support rotation.

Knee and elbow

Create tighter loops where you expect bending; keep quads evenly spaced to avoid pinching.

UV benefit

Consistent quad regions unwrap predictably and reduce distortion during texture painting.

Example

On a lumpy sphere, route loops around the largest bulge to keep deformation smooth when animating.
