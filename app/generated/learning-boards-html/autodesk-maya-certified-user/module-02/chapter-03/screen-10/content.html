Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Reduce Tool
What Reduce does

Automatically simplifies geometry by removing elements while attempting to preserve overall shape.

Good use: rough LODs for background props.
Bad use: final character topology without cleanup.
Workflow
  1. Duplicate the mesh before reducing so you keep an original copy.
  2. Apply Reduce and review silhouette and loop integrity.
  3. Retopologize or fix broken loops on areas that will deform.
Pro tip

Treat Reduce as a helper, not a final solution for animated assets.
