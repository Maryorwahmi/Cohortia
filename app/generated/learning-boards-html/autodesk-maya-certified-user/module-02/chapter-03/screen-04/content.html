What is Retopology?

Retopology means drawing a new polygon surface over a sculpt so you end up with a low-polygon mesh that deforms predictably and bakes well.

Why it matters

High-res sculpts carry detail but are too expensive for real-time. Retopology balances silhouette and performance, enabling animation and efficient UVs.

Common mistake

Relying solely on automatic reduction often produces poor edge flow. Manual retopology gives control for deformation and UV layout.

Key Takeaway

Good retopology keeps silhouette and provides predictable loops for animation and clean UV seams.
