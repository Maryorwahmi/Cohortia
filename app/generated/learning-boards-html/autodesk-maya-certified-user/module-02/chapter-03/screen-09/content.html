Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Polygon Optimization
Optimization checklist
  • Remove unnecessary edge loops that don't change silhouette.
  • Merge vertices that are too close together.
  • Convert selected quads to triangles in flat, non-deforming regions.
  • Create LODs for camera-distance-based rendering.
Guideline

Always validate optimizations against poses and typical camera distances to avoid visible popping or deformation issues.

Example scenario
A detailed weapon model can reduce mid-body edge loops away from the handle while keeping tight loops on the grip for silhouette. Farther parts become lower LODs.
Common mistake

Applying heavy automatic reduction and exporting without cleaning topology produces artifacts and unpredictable deformation.
