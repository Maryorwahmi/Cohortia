Module 3 › Chapter 2.3 › Subdivision vs Hard Surface
Subdivision Surfaces

Model a low-poly cage, subdivide for a smooth result, and use the high-res for baking onto a low-poly game mesh.

Good for characters and organic shapes.
Requires baking to keep runtime geometry low.
Hard Surface Modeling

Use extrudes, bevels, and cuts to create sharp forms and control loops for mechanical objects like weapons or props.

Good for precise silhouettes and real-world mechanical detail.
Often requires careful bevels and support loops to look right at low poly budgets.
