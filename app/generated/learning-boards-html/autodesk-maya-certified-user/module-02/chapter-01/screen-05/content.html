Comparison
Quads vs Triangles vs N-gons
Understand trade-offs for deformation, rendering, and UVs.
4
Quad (4 sides)
Preferred for modeling and deformation. Keeps clean loops and subdivides predictably.
3
Triangle (3 sides)
Efficient for render engines. Use in flat or non-deforming regions or for final export.
N
N-gon (>4 sides)
Prone to unpredictable triangulation and deformation issues. Avoid in deforming areas.
Practical tip
Keep quads while modeling and only triangulate as a controlled final step if required.
Concrete Example
Character shoulder topology vs crate faces
Example 1: Shoulder — quads maintain loop flow around the joint, enabling smooth bending without pinching. Example 2: Crate — faces are flat and non-deforming, so triangles can be used for optimization but quads are fine for easy editing.
Common Mistake
Leaving n-gons in areas that will deform. They may triangulate badly and make skinning fail.
Key Takeaway

Model with quads for deformation, use triangles for optimization, avoid n-gons in animated areas.
