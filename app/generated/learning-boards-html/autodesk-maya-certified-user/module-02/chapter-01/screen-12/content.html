Common Mistakes and Pro Tips
Simple checks that save hours later in the pipeline.
Over-density
Add vertices only where needed. Start low and refine.
N-gons in joints
Avoid n-gons where deformation occurs; convert to quads or triangles strategically.
Transforms
Freeze after scale and rotate to keep data engine-friendly.
Pro Tip
Plan support loops before smoothing to hold edges cleanly.
Quick Check
Rotate joint-like regions to verify smooth deformation before finalizing topology.
Key Takeaway

Small, consistent habits—low density, quad-first modeling, and freezing transforms—prevent the majority of downstream problems.
