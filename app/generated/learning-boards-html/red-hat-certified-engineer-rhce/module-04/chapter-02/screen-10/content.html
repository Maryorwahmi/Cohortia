Module 4 › Chapter 4.2 › Troubleshooting
Common issues
  • Overlap error: Double-check start/end ranges with lsblk and sfdisk before applying.
  • Wrong device: Confirm the disk device and narrow inventory target to avoid /dev/sda mistakes.
  • Resizing risks: Avoid changing part_end on partitions with filesystems without backups and proper resize steps.
Debugging steps
Capture the module register, review parted_result.changed and parted_result.msg, and correlate with kernel output from lsblk and sfdisk. If necessary, boot into rescue mode and use backups to recover.
Tip: Run in check mode first to see what parted would change. Check mode is your safety net for catching device or range mistakes.
