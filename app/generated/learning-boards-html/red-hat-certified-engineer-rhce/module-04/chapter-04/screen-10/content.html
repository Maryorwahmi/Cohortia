Module 04 › Chapter 4.4 › Hands-on
Checklist
  1. Verify disks on the managed node with lsblk.
  2. Create a playbook using community.general.lvg with vg and pvs.
  3. Run ansible-playbook with become privileges and register results.
  4. Verify with pvdisplay and vgdisplay after the run.
Starter playbook
---
- name: Create an LVM Volume Group
  hosts: your_target_host
  become: yes
  tasks:
    - name: Ensure 'app_vg' Volume Group exists and includes /dev/sdb and /dev/sdc
      community.general.lvg:
        vg: app_vg
        pvs:
          - /dev/sdb
          - /dev/sdc
        state: present
      register: vg_status

    - name: Display Volume Group status
      ansible.builtin.debug:
        var: vg_status
Verify
After the playbook runs, confirm with ansible ad-hoc commands or login and run sudo pvdisplay and sudo vgdisplay app_vg to validate that the PVs and VG exist and the capacity is correct.
