Logical Volume Management with Ansible — Part 1
Automate Physical Volumes and Volume Groups so you can allocate flexible, resizable storage programmatically.
In this lesson
Definitions of PVs and VGs, an Ansible playbook walkthrough for creating a VG, verification commands, and safety best practices to avoid data loss.
Coming up
A reproducible task using community.general.lvg that will initialize disks as PVs when necessary and create or extend the named VG.
Quick topics
Physical Volume (PV)
A raw disk or partition initialized for LVM use.
Volume Group (VG)
A pool of PVs providing a single storage space to create LVs.
Ansible lvg
community.general.lvg initializes devices and manages VG creation or extension idempotently.
Visual map
/dev/sdb, /dev/sdc
raw disks to initialize
Physical Volumes (PV)
initialized by lvg when needed
Volume Group (VG)
a pooled block device for Logical Volumes
Start tip
Confirm device names with lsblk and ensure disks are unmounted and empty. Initializing a PV will overwrite headers and may destroy data.
