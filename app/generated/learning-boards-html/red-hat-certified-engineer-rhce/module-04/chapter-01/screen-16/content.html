What to Remember › Key Concepts — Part 2
Ansible Modules
Use community.general.parted, filesystem, ansible.posix.mount, lvg and lvol for storage tasks.
Logical Volume Management
LVM pools physical volumes into volume groups and provides flexible logical volumes for filesystems.
Idempotency
Running playbooks repeatedly should not change resources that already match the desired state.
Final takeaway
Plan, discover, test, and declare. Use the right Ansible modules and idempotent patterns to make storage changes safe and repeatable.
