What to Remember › Key Concepts — Part 1
Physical Disk
The raw storage device, for example /dev/sda.
Partition
A logical division of a disk that defines a range to format and mount.
Filesystem
The structure written to a partition that stores files and directories, such as ext4 or XFS.
Summary
Distinguish device, partition, and filesystem. Confirm these when creating playbooks to avoid destructive surprises.
