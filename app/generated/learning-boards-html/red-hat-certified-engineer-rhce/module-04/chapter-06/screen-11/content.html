Operational checklist for safe automation
Firewall
Open only required ports and automate with firewalld module.
Root squashing
Avoid no_root_squash for broad networks; prefer host-specific entries.
Device naming
Use /dev/disk/by-path or UUIDs to mount iSCSI devices reliably.
Idempotency
Ensure repeated playbook runs do not duplicate targets or exports.
Check your understanding

Name one verification command you would run on a server and one on a client after a playbook completes.
