Advanced Storage: NFS and iSCSI with Ansible

Explore automated NFS exports and persistent mounts for file-level sharing, then learn how to present block devices over the network with iSCSI. We focus on the correct Ansible modules and safe, idempotent playbook patterns.

Concept map
NFS — File-level
Exports directories. Good for shared files and home directories.
iSCSI — Block-level
Presents remote disks. Used for VM or database storage that needs raw block devices.
Automation: use ansible.builtin.yum, ansible.posix.mount, community.general.targetcli and community.general.iscsi for repeatable setups.
Explore Key Topics
NFS Server & Client
Playbooks to install nfs-utils, export directories, and persist mounts with _netdev.
iSCSI Target
Configure IQNs, LUNs and portals with targetcli and ensure idempotency in playbooks.
Client Initiator
Discover and login to targets, wait for devices, and use by-path paths or UUIDs for reliability.
