Comparison

File-level (NFS)

  • Clients access files managed by the server.
  • Good for shared directories, home folders, and web content.
  • Configure exports in /etc/exports and protect with host-based rules.
Use case: multiple web servers reading and writing the same document tree.
Comparison

Block-level (iSCSI)

  • Clients see a raw disk device; the client manages partitions and filesystems.
  • Good for VM disks, databases, and workloads needing raw block performance.
  • Access controlled by IQN-based ACLs; LUNs identify logical units presented by targets.
Use case: a hypervisor storing VM images on remote LUNs for consolidation.
