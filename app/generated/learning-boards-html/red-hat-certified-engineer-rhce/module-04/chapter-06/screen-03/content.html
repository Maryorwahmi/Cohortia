Module 4 › Chapter 4.6 › Safety Checks

Security and operational checks to include in playbooks

Firewall
Open specific ports: NFS (111, 2049) and iSCSI (3260). Automate with ansible.posix.firewalld for consistency.
Access Controls
Use host-specific exports and iSCSI ACLs instead of wildcards or no_root_squash unless you understand the risk.
Idempotency
Ensure targetcli and export edits are written so repeated runs do not create duplicates.
