Verification commands
Server: list exports
exportfs -v shows exported paths and options.
Client: check mounts
df -h or mount shows the mounted NFS share and its source.
iSCSI: verify device
lsblk and /dev/disk/by-path show the correct device to format and mount.
Quick checklist
  • Run server playbook first.
  • Confirm exports with exportfs -v.
  • Run client playbook and confirm df -h.
  • For iSCSI, verify by-path device before formatting.
