Module 4 › Chapter 4.5 › What to Remember
Verify LV
Confirm lvdisplay and lsblk to ensure the LV exists and the device path is correct.
Resize FS
After LV changes, run xfs_growfs or resize2fs and verify with df -h.
Snapshot life
Size snapshots correctly, mount for backup, and remove them to reclaim VG space.
Final takeaway

Embed verification and filesystem commands into your playbooks so storage automation remains safe and auditable.
