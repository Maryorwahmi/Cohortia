Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Device names vs UUIDs
Device names
Pros: simple to write. Cons: can change across reboots or when hardware is added. Risk: wrong device mounted or mount fails.
UUIDs
Pros: stable metadata tied to the filesystem. Cons: require an extra command to read and register in playbooks. Benefit: robust persistent mounts.
Practical checklist
Format device
Read UUID with blkid
Use UUID in mount src
