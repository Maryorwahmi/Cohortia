Module 4 › Chapter 4.3 › Verification
Commands to verify
  • df -h /path to confirm mount is active.
  • blkid or lsblk -f to list UUIDs and fstype metadata.
  • cat /etc/fstab to inspect the persistent entry and options.
Practical checks
After mounting, confirm file ownership and that applications can write. If you plan to reboot, test persistence by restarting the host and re-checking df and fstab.
Key takeaway
Verification closes the loop on automation and prevents surprises in production environments.
