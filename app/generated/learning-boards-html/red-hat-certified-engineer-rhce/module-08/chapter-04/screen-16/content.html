Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › What to Remember
Inspect first
Collect df, du, lsblk, pvscan outputs before acting.
Backup & dry-run
Always have backups and perform dry runs of repair tools first.
Automate carefully
Use Ansible for diagnostics; require human approval for destructive tasks.
Last thought

A careful, documented, and backup-first approach converts risky repairs into reliable recovery steps.
