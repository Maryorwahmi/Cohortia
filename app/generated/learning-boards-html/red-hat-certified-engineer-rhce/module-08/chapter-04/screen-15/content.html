Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Key Concepts
df vs du
df measures allocation; du measures visible file sizes.
Device mapping
Use lsblk, fdisk, parted to confirm device-to-mount relationships.
Repairs & backups
Always back up and unmount before running xfs_repair or fsck.
Summary takeaway

Combine correct diagnosis, careful mapping of devices, and conservative repair steps with backups to recover storage safely.
