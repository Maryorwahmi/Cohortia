Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Troubleshooting LVM
Missing PVs
Symptoms: vgs shows missing PVs or reduced VG size. Command: pvscan, pvdisplay.
If the PV is readable, migrate extents with pvmove. If not, restore from backup or replace the disk and restore the VG metadata carefully.
Inactive LVs
Run vgscan and lvscan. Activate a VG with vgchange -ay <vgname> to bring LVs online.
Insufficient free space
Check vgs and lvs to see free extents. Add a PV to the VG or resize other LVs with care.
Plan before you change
Collect pvdisplay, vgdisplay, lvdisplay and verify backups. Structural LVM changes are destructive if misapplied.
