Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Safety & Backups
Backup mandate
Always have a current backup or snapshot before filesystem or LVM repairs. Repairs can be destructive.
Documentation and dry-runs
Save df/du/lsblk/ pvdisplay outputs with timestamps. Use xfs_repair -n or fsck -n for non-destructive checks where supported.
Human oversight
Include manual approval gates in automation for unmounts and repairs. Test procedures in a lab until repeatable.
