Learning Objectives

Practical skills for diagnosis, repair, and automation.

Bloom: Learn → Understand → Apply → Master
01
Diagnose disk space issues
Use df and du to find full filesystems and large directories. Essential for restoring services blocked by no space.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
02
Inspect devices and partitions
Read lsblk, fdisk, and parted output to map disks, partitions and LVM layers before any change.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
03
Repair filesystems safely
Understand unmounting and run xfs_repair or fsck only on unmounted filesystems, with backups in place.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
04
Troubleshoot LVM and automate checks
Use pvscan, vgscan, lvscan and simple Ansible playbooks to collect status and reduce manual steps.
VERIFIED COMPETENCY MILESTONE
