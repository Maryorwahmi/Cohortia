Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › LVM hierarchy
Hierarchical cards
Physical Volume (PV)
A disk or partition used by LVM. Command: pvscan, pvdisplay.
Volume Group (VG)
A pool of storage made from one or more PVs. Command: vgscan, vgs.
Logical Volume (LV)
A virtual block device created from a VG. Command: lvscan, lvs, lvdisplay.
Common commands
pvscan · vgscan · lvscan · vgs · lvs · vgchange -ay
Troubleshooting tip
If an LV is missing, check pvscan and vgscan first. Activate VGs with vgchange -ay before searching for LVs.
