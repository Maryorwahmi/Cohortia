Module 08 › Chapter 8.4 › Health check scenario
Step 1 — Disk usage
Run: df -h
Step 2 — Top directories
Run: du -h --max-depth=1 /var | sort -rh | head -n 5
Step 3 — Active LVs
Run: lvscan --active
Step 4 — Inactive VGs
Run: vgscan --inactive
Verification
After any cleanup or activation, re-run df and lvscan, then mount read-only and inspect critical services. Always confirm backups before structural changes.
