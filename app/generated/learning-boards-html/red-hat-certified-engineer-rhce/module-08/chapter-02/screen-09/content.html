Process Flow
Establish baselines
Collect metrics routinely and compare anomalies to normal behavior.
  1. Choose representative times and intervals (e.g., vmstat 1 300 for 5 minutes).
  2. Collect CPU, memory, disk and network samples and tag them with host and timestamp.
  3. Store outputs centrally for trend comparison and alerting thresholds.
Key Takeaway
A one-off sample is rarely enough; baselines turn noisy numbers into meaningful signals.
Example schedule
Run vmstat 1 300 and iostat -x 1 300 during peak and off-peak hours, store files with host and timestamp and compare averages and percentiles across days.
Pitfall
Avoid immediate hardware changes based on a single spike; confirm with baseline data and repeated samples first.
