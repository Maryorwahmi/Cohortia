Module 8 › Chapter 8.2 › Key Concepts
top / htop
Quick live view of processes, CPU, mem, swap and wa.
vmstat / free
Memory trends and swapping indicators (si/so).
iostat / sar
Device-level I/O, %util, await and historical sampling for baselines.
Final takeaway
Measure consistently, collect baselines, automate collection, change one thing, and remeasure to confirm real improvement.
