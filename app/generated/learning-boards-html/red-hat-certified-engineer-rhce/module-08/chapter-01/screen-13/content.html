Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Scenario
Guided terminal steps
1. Confirm unit state
systemctl status httpd
2. Inspect unit logs
journalctl -u httpd -xe
3. Check kernel boot messages
dmesg -T | tail -n 30
Next: collect across hosts
ansible webservers -m shell -a "journalctl -u httpd --since '1 hour ago' -n 50 --no-pager"
This collects the same journal slice from all webservers so you can spot trends or differences quickly.
Check your assumption

If journal entries show missing mounts, fixing the service alone won't help. Confirm that mounts succeed and storage is present before restarting services.

Common mistake

Treating a successful restart as a permanent fix without verifying the root cause leads to recurring incidents.

Key Takeaway

Follow the define-hypothesize-test-analyze loop, collect data across hosts, and verify that the root cause is addressed with a rollback plan ready.
