Module 8 › Chapter 8.1 › Root Cause Analysis
Root Cause Checklist
  • Confirm the failing flow and capture logs before any change.
  • Test hypotheses and document commands and outputs for each test.
  • Ensure the chosen fix addresses the evidence collected.
  • Prepare rollback: backups, version pinning, and undo steps.
Verification

After applying a fix, re-run the original diagnostic commands to confirm the symptom no longer occurs and that no new errors appear in journal or dmesg.

Analogy

Treat symptoms like a fever: lowering the temperature is useful, but you should find the infection and treat it to prevent recurrence.

Common mistake

Applying quick fixes without documenting tests and rollback steps often makes later debugging harder and hides recurring root causes.

Key Takeaway

Document evidence, validate fixes, and always have a rollback plan to avoid making incidents worse.
