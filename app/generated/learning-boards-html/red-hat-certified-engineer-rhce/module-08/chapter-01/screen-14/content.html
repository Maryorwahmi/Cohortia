Systematic Troubleshooting
An iterative define-hypothesize-test-analyze loop minimizes guesswork.
journalctl
Centralized systemd journal; filter by unit, time, and priority for targeted logs.
dmesg
Kernel ring buffer; useful for boot and driver diagnostics with timestamps.
Summary takeaway
Collect consistent, timestamped evidence before changing systems and use automation to scale diagnostics across hosts.
