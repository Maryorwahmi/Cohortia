Essential Troubleshooting Methodologies
A practical, evidence-driven workflow for diagnosing services, kernels, processes, and networks on Red Hat systems.
In this lesson

You will practice a repeatable approach: define the issue clearly, form the most likely cause, test using journalctl, dmesg, ps, lsof, and strace, then collect multi-host data with Ansible.

Why it matters

A structured method reduces time spent guessing, prevents collateral changes, and produces evidence you can share with teammates or for postmortem analysis.
