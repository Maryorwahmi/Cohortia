Module 8 › Chapter 8.5 › Key Concepts
systemctl & journalctl
Unit-filtered journal entries are the first source of truth when a systemd service fails.
Application logs
Follow and filter error_log files; capture timestamped snippets for correlation.
SELinux & firewall
Security layers often block access; check SELinux contexts and firewalld rules before changing app code.
Takeaway
Collect evidence first, then act. Automate data collection with Ansible so your team can diagnose issues faster and consistently.
