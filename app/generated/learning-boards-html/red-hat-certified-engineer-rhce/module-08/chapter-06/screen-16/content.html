Quick Reference › Three concept cards
sysctl
Change kernel behavior at runtime and persist validated settings under /etc/sysctl.d/.
ulimit & systemd
Use limits.conf or pam_limits for sessions and LimitNOFILE in unit files for services.
Ansible
Use sysctl, pam_limits, cron and lineinfile modules to apply consistent, auditable changes across hosts.
Summary Takeaway

Treat optimization as an iterative, measured process: choose the right scope, test, persist what helps, automate, and document.
