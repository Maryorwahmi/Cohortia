Core Concept › Ansible modules for optimization
Key modules
  • ansible.posix.sysctl — manage kernel parameters and persist them.
  • community.general.pam_limits — manage /etc/security/limits.conf entries.
  • ansible.builtin.cron — schedule cron jobs.
Practical tips
Use notify handlers for systemd daemon reloads and service restarts. Keep playbooks idempotent and test with check mode and in staging to confirm effects before production rollout.
Key Takeaway

Automate what you have validated manually. Ansible modules mirror the manual steps and make them repeatable across hosts.
