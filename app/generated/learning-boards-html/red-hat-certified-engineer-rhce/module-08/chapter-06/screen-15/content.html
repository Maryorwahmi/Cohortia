Key Concepts › What to Remember
Baseline & Verify
Measure before and after every tuning change so you can attribute effects accurately.
sysctl & Persistence
Use sysctl for kernel tunables; persist validated settings in /etc/sysctl.d/ and reload with sysctl --system.
ulimit & systemd
Use limits.conf for sessions and systemd unit directives for services to set reliable resource limits.
Final Takeaway

Tuning is an iterative, measured process. Validate each change, automate via Ansible for consistency, and always maintain a rollback path.
