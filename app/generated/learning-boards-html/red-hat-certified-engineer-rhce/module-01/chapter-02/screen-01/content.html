Agentless automation, inventories, installation, and ad-hoc commands.

What you will understand

This chapter explains Ansible's design goals: simplicity, agentless operation, and declarative automation. You will see how a control node manages many hosts via an inventory and why ad-hoc commands let you test connectivity quickly without writing a playbook.

In this lesson
Install Ansible on RHEL, create a basic inventory, and run ad-hoc commands to verify connectivity.
Why it matters
Agentless automation reduces maintenance and speeds team onboarding when managing servers at scale.
