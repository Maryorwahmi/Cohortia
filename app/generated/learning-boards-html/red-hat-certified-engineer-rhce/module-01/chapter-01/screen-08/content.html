Module 1 › Chapter 1.1 › Time Management
A Practical Process
A repeatable cycle to maximize points under time limits.
Step 1: Scan & Prioritize
Quickly review all tasks and identify straightforward items to secure early points.
Step 2: Plan
Sketch the steps, required modules, and verification checks before changing systems.
Step 3: Implement
Write concise playbooks or commands and run them with become when needed.
Step 4: Verify
Check services, ports, users, and files exactly as the task requires.
Practice Techniques
  • Time-box practice runs to mimic the exam three to four hour window.
  • Secure easy tasks first, then iterate on complex automation items.
  • Validate immediately after each change to avoid cascading errors.
Common Mistake
Rushing without reading: missed details like ports or exact permission settings often cause point loss.
