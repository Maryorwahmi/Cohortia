Objective Details
  • Format: Practice solving real tasks in a live RHEL system to demonstrate real-world skills.
  • Ansible focus: Author playbooks that are idempotent and clear, and understand modules and collections.
  • Lab: Use at least two VMs to simulate control and managed nodes; validate SSH and sudo before running playbooks.
  • Timing: Track how long typical tasks take and rehearse under similar time limits to the exam.
