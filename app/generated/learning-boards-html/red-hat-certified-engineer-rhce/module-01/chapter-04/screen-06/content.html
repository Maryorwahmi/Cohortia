YAML Example
# YAML format example: ~/ansible_projects/inventory.yml
all:
  children:
    webservers:
      hosts:
        web1.example.com:
        web2.example.com:
    databases:
      hosts:
        db1.example.com:
          ansible_port: 2222
        db2.example.com:
  vars:
    ansible_user: devops
    ansible_ssh_private_key_file: /home/devops/.ssh/id_rsa
YAML groups and vars read naturally, and nested host mappings let you attach dictionaries or lists easily.
Why choose YAML
  • Clear hierarchy for children and vars.
  • Better for structured variables and complex data.
  • Easier to maintain for larger inventories with nested definitions.
Common mistake

Incorrect indentation or mixing tabs and spaces will cause YAML parsing errors; validate files before use.

Key Takeaway

YAML gives you a readable, hierarchical inventory structure that's preferable for complex variables and nested data.
