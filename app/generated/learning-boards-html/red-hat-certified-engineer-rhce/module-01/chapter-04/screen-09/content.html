# ~/ansible_projects/my_advanced_inventory.ini
[webservers]
web1.example.com
web2.example.com

[databases]
db1.example.com

[all:vars]
ansible_user=devops
ansible_ssh_private_key_file=/home/devops/.ssh/id_rsa
default_message="Hello from Ansible!"
Replace example hostnames with your targets. Use inventory-relative group_vars and host_vars.
