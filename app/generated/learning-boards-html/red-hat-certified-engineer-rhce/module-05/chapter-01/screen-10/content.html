Module 5 › Chapter 5.1 › Device vs Profile
Device (kernel interface)
Names like enp0s3, eth0. Represents hardware or virtual interfaces. Inspected with ip a. Not the primary target for nmcli automation.
Connection profile (NetworkManager)
Named configuration with IP, DNS, and routes. Managed by NetworkManager and targeted by conn_name or UUID in automation.
Practical checklist
  • Run nmcli connection show to find conn_name and UUID.
  • Prefer conn_name or UUID in your playbooks.
  • Verify application with nmcli connection show --active and ip a.
Misconception check: If you edit device-specific files directly on a NetworkManager-managed host, your changes may not take effect until NetworkManager re-reads or reactivates the connection.
Key Takeaway

Target the profile. Use UUIDs or exact connection names to ensure predictable automation.
