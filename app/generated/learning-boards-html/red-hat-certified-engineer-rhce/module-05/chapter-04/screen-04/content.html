Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › What is firewalld and why zones matter
Definition

firewalld and zones

Firewalld groups rules into zones that reflect trust levels. Zones are applied to network interfaces to determine allowed services and ports.

Example
Assign external NIC to public zone and internal NIC to internal zone so policies differ by trust.
Common misconception
Zones are not interfaces. They are policy sets applied to interfaces. Assignments determine which policy controls traffic.
Key Takeaway

Design zones around trust, then assign interfaces. This reduces repeated rule edits and prevents surprises.

Annotated policy cards
public
Minimal services, untrusted networks
internal / home
More services allowed for trusted networks
drop
Drop all incoming packets
trusted
Allow almost all traffic
Why this helps
  • Change a policy once, affect many interfaces.
  • Reduce duplication and human error.
  • Map zones to real-world trust boundaries for clarity.
