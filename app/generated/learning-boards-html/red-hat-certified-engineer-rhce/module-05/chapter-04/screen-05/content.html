Module 5 › Chapter 5.4 › Assigning interfaces and choosing zones
Process

Four-step decision flow

  1. Inventory interfaces and what networks they connect to.
  2. Choose the least-privilege zone that supports needed services.
  3. Assign the interface to the chosen zone.
  4. Verify with firewall-cmd --get-active-zones and --zone=<zone> --list-all.
Common mistake
Not assigning the interface to the intended zone, so rules appear present but do not affect traffic on that interface.
Example scenario

You have eth0 connected to the internet and eth1 on a management VLAN. Assign eth0 to public and eth1 to internal. Then allow SSH only from your controller on the internal zone.

# Verify active zones
firewall-cmd --get-active-zones

# Show details for internal zone
firewall-cmd --zone=internal --list-all
Key Takeaway

Assign interfaces based on trust, then verify rules apply to traffic by checking the assigned zone contents.
