Explore Key Topics
CIDR & Subnets
CIDR combines address and prefix length, e.g., 192.168.1.100/24 maps to 255.255.255.0.
nmcli Module
community.general.nmcli configures NetworkManager connection profiles from Ansible playbooks.
Safety & Verify
Always plan console access or a fallback IP, and include verification tasks in your playbook.
