Common Troubleshooting Mistakes and Best Practices

Avoid false assumptions, collect evidence first, and be mindful of production impact
Mistake: Act before facts
Change configuration without gathering facts and you may mask the real issue. Gather ansible_facts first.
Mistake: Ignore rc
Relying on stdout alone can miss command failures. Inspect the registered rc field to detect errors reliably.
Mistake: Unbounded captures
Running tcpdump without filters or timeouts risks resource exhaustion and triggers alerts. Timebox and filter captures.
Best practice checklist
  • Gather facts first, then run read-only diagnostics.
  • Register outputs, debug them, and use rc values in when conditions.
  • Limit the scope and duration of scans and captures in production.
Key Takeaway

Make troubleshooting repeatable and safe: collect evidence, use conditional logic, and limit operational impact.
