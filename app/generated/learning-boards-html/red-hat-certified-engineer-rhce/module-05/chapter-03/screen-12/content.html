Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Common Mistakes & Safety
Common mistakes
  • Enabling 802.3ad without switch LACP configuration.
  • Failing to remove existing per-NIC connections so slaves keep IPs.
  • Not activating the master connection after creation.
Safety checklist
  1. Test in a lab first.
  2. Have console or IPMI access before changes.
  3. Include automated verification and readable debug output.
Warning
Misapplied network automation can cause lockouts. Always prepare a fallback path.
