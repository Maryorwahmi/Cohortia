Module 5 › Chapter 5.3 › Creating a Bond Master
Task structure

Define a connection of type bond with conn_name and ifname matching the master name. Include ip4, gw4, dns4, and the desired mode. Use state: present and autoconnect: yes to make it persistent and startable.

---
- name: Ensure bond master connection exists and is configured
  community.general.nmcli:
    conn_name: "{{ bond_name }}"
    type: bond
    ifname: "{{ bond_name }}"
    ip4: "{{ bond_ip }}"
    gw4: "{{ bond_gw }}"
    dns4: "{{ bond_dns }}"
    mode: "{{ bond_mode }}"
    state: present
    autoconnect: yes
Validation steps
  • Activate the connection with state: activated to bring it up immediately.
  • Run ip -4 addr show dev bond0 to confirm the IP appears on the master.
  • Use cat /proc/net/bonding/bond0 to inspect mode and active slave.
Tip: Place variables for bond name, mode, and IP in vars to reuse tasks across hosts and networks.
