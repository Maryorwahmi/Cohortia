Managing Network Bonds and Teams

Learn the practical differences between kernel bonding and teamd, explore common modes such as active-backup and 802.3ad, and automate master and slave configuration using community.general.nmcli in Ansible.

Orientation

You will practice creating both bond and team master interfaces, assign physical slaves, and verify results using standard RHEL diagnostic commands. Remember to test in a lab and keep console access available for production changes.

Explore Key Topics
Bonding Modes
Compare active-backup, balance-rr, and 802.3ad and learn their operational trade-offs and switch requirements.
Teaming and Runners
Understand teamd runners, runtime flexibility, and why teaming can offer finer control over link monitoring and recovery.
Ansible Automation
Automate creation of master and slave connections with community.general.nmcli to ensure repeatable deployments and safe rollouts.
