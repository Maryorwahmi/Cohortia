Module 8 › Chapter 7.7 › Deployment Strategies
Rolling updates
Update hosts in batches to minimize impact and allow quick rollback of a subset if issues arise.
Blue/green
Provision a parallel environment and switch traffic when the new environment is verified to reduce downtime risk.
Canary releases
Deploy to a small subset first, monitor, then expand if healthy. Useful for catching regressions in production-like conditions.
Practical design tip
Decide the strategy early and design inventories and playbooks to select target hosts and batch behavior accordingly. Document approval gates for production switches.
