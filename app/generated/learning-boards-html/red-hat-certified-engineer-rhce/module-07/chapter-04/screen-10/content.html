Module 7 › Chapter 7.4 › Ansible Debugger
What the debugger gives you
  • Access to registered variables and their full structure at failure time.
  • Ability to re-run or retry tasks after inspecting or modifying context.
  • Direct, live exploration of why failed_when or other conditions tripped.
Enable the debugger
# Example environment usage
ANSIBLE_DEBUGGER=on_failed ansible-playbook your_playbook.yml
Quick debugger commands
  • p result — print the last registered variable.
  • n — step to the next command.
  • r — retry the task after editing context.
  • q — quit the debugger and stop playbook execution.
Safety
Use in staging or trusted terminals and avoid leaving debug sessions open where they could expose secrets.
