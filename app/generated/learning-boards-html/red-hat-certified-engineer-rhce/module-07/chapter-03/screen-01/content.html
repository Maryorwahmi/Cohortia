Orientation

This chapter shows how to apply filters to transform values, write conditionals and tests to choose rendered blocks, loop over lists or dictionaries for repeated config, and use Ansible facts to make templates host-specific and safe.

In this lesson
Practical Jinja2 patterns: sequential filters, defined checks, loop iteration variables, and fact-driven conditions to reduce boilerplate.
Coming up
Hands-on role example that generates users and firewall rules from variables and templates.

Filters

Conditionals & Tests
If, elif, else and tests such as is defined or is empty to control output paths.
Loops & Facts
For-loops render repeating blocks; use ansible facts for host-aware values.
