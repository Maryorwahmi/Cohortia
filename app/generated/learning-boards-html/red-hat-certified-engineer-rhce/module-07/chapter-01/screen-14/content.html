Module 7›Chapter 7.1›Key Concepts — Vars & Idempotency
defaults/main.yml
Lowest precedence defaults suitable for environment overrides.
vars/main.yml
Role-specific values with higher precedence than defaults but lower than extra vars.
Idempotency
Ensure tasks and handlers run only when necessary so repeated runs are safe.
Final thought

Structure, consistent defaults, and idempotent design together keep automation predictable and maintainable across environments.
